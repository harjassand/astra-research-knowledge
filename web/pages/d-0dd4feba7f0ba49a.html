## Exact rule and acceptance interface

Start from a cleaned state satisfying the cycle11 invariant, on its current
minimal ORIGINAL face F and fixed cost slice c.x=gamma. Keep eta, every
retained coefficient, scale, row classification and completed-row tracker
fixed throughout a trial movement. Use a rational candidate h with
1/2<=||h||<=1, exact original-face tangent and cost residual zero, zero
movement on frozen coordinates, and b_i.h=0 for every large row. Require
the current protected-barrier gradient equations exactly as well. A
candidate module and its encoding costs belong to the implemented
algorithm; they are not a free supplied trajectory oracle.
Attempt at most one such candidate per iteration. Its interval evaluation
has a prescribed polynomial precision budget; an unresolved comparison
rejects the event and invokes the usual fallback. A search for an exact
transcendental equality must not become an uncharged infinite trial loop.

Find an exact ORIGINAL face endpoint s>0 in the selected sign. Boundedness
and nonzero h ensure this hits a proper original subface. Its whole segment
is feasible by convexity, and c.h=0 preserves cost exactly. Original-face
facets or the previously gated exact strong-oracle endpoint construction
can certify maximality. This is an original-face hit, not just a boundary
of an artificially imposed search region.

For every medium row/sign, the fixed-state barrier is the exact concave
quadratic

`u_sigma(t)=u_sigma(0)+t g_sigma.h`

`              -[R/(2r_i^2)] t^2 sum_j p_ij h_j^2`.

The verifier computes its maximum over [0,s] from the two endpoints and
its rational stationary point, if present, and requires that maximum<=0.
It additionally certifies L(t)<=9 throughout the segment with a sound
upper bound for the retained Gram plus large diagonal and eta11^T.
Examples are exact rational exp enclosures plus a diagonal domination,
or an explicitly proved rank-one/rank-two spectral bound. No approximate
eigenvalue test is accepted as that certificate.

Let d_i(t)=d_i(0)+t b_i.h for retained rows, with large d remaining zero
and completed d fixed. The endpoint certificate must prove

`Psi_eta(x+s h,d(s))<=Psi_eta(x,d(0))`,

where `Psi=(L-8)_+^4+eta(n-||x_all||^2)+lambda sum_all d_i^2`.
Use certified lower/upper potential intervals with a nonnegative gap, or
a verified algebraic monotonicity identity. The charge includes completed
rows. A decreasing coordinate term alone is insufficient.

On acceptance, move to that exact endpoint, update retained trackers by
the actual feature displacement, recover the NEW minimal original face,
clean, reset within its same cost slice using displacement below the
fixed cycle11 delta, update/round medium trackers, increment e by4delta,
and clean again. Perform the specified eta-epoch switches when required.
On rejection, discard the trial and use the independently proved cycle11
flat-direction/tau/full-or-short rule. Trial evaluation costs are charged.

The event acceptance rule needs neither approximate-gradient orthogonality
nor the fourth-order curvature/flatness contracts: its direct segment and
potential certificates replace those reasons for the prescribed local

does. It must never reuse a failed event candidate as the fallback local

## Why the expanded whole run retains its proof

By exact segment feasibility and the barrier checks, all signed trackers
remain within their allowed barriers during the long movement. The large
trackers have no movement. The explicit cap establishes the spectral
domain without invoking the quarter-length local Taylor proof. Endpoint
potential is nonincreasing before representation changes.

Cleanup/entry deletion and freezing do not increase the top Perron value;
entry deletion uses entrywise domination, not a false Loewner claim.
Scale and large-to-medium transitions use the already proved admitted
parameter inequalities. Stored/completed d remains fixed through these
representation changes, and newly frozen D summands are zero.

The exact new-face/cost reset costs at most64n delta. Cycle11 fixes

`delta<=Delta_min/(1024n)`, `Delta_min=eta0^3/2^42`.

Thus this event's move/reset/cleanup cost is at most

`Delta_min/16=eta0^3/2^46 <eta_j/2^24`

at its epoch eta_j>=eta0. It can be charged by exactly the same face-hit
allowance as a prescribed short step. Each accepted event strictly drops
the ORIGINAL face dimension. Exact reset preserves the entire new face;
it cannot reopen a prior facet. Including prescribed short steps, total
face-hit updates are at most n. Within epoch j the count is at most its
entry face dimension<=N_entry<=S_j, including rank facets with every
coordinate fractional.

All cycle11 short-hit, epoch-switch, full-descent and hard-cap inequalities
therefore remain valid after replacing any number of short-hit slots with
accepted long events. The bound is still fewer than
