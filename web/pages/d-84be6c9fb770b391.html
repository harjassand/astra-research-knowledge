6. FAILURES, PRECISE REPAIRS, AND WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN

F3: Non-star-adjacent holes can be two physical steps apart: singleton sources

F4: Local all-set inclusion alone cannot imply the global hull tail. Take a
deterministic square bad ring well outside the eligible buffer around Q0.
Every local nonempty-set inclusion probability is zero, but its filled hull
