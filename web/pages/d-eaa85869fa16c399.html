# Result card: aging/regeneration theory search

**Status:** no new theory or biological capability survived; retain as three scoped source cards and one descriptive source-data reanalysis.

## Best current mechanism

Sereda et al. (Nature Aging, 2026; DOI `10.1038/s43587-026-01240-w`) report that macrophage CMA regulates lysosomal/endocytic turnover of the inhibitory receptor SIRPα, associated SHP-1, phagocytosis/efferocytosis, and wound senescence resolution. CMA-deficient senescent-cell secretomes also suppress macrophage activity/survival. CA77.1 has systemic aged-mouse effects, but does not isolate which compartment mediates the outcome.

## Source-data check

Reanalysis of Fig. 4 source data shows that the Fig. 4h bead-uptake mean gap between control and CMA-deficient secretome narrows by 31.4% under CA and retains 68.6% of its original magnitude. Fig. 4e efferocytosis means and the source-reported ANOVA are strongly macrophage-genotype dominated in that exact assay. Fig. 4h has fields nested in three independent experiments; no inferential analysis of those field-level values is made here. The bead and corpse assays are not direct living senescent-cell clearance rates.

## Novelty boundary

The CD47/SIRPα/QPCT-L bypass is directly preempted by Schloesser et al. (2022). Generic production/clearance feedback is preempted by prior turnover and spatial wound models. Mitochondrial neutral-drift and Paneth niche directions are also standard conditional math atop published mechanisms. Muscle-stem-cell pathway restoration lacks measured repeated-injury reserve transitions. No direction produced a new theorem that removes a biological obstruction with acquired source parameters.

## Decisive next observation

Measure, under compartment-restricted CMA manipulation, macrophage survival and per-cell uptake of lineage-labelled senescent targets together with source-cell birth/transition and tissue recovery in the same age/sex/tissue/time window. A lower senescence-marker burden without a corresponding labelled-target removal trajectory does not identify physical clearance. Sample size, specialized allele/delivery costs, assay throughput and in-vivo pharmacodynamics need experimental acquisition before quantitative prediction.
