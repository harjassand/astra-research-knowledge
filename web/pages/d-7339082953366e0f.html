# Kernel check of the all-dimensional affine approximation lemma

## Result

The original arbitrary-tolerance affine approximation lemma is now proved in Lean 4.28 in `project/N33Affine/GlobalAffineComposition.lean`, declaration `N33Affine.exists_full_affine_family`. It is a kernel-checked theorem, not only a feasibility result or a collection of helper lemmas.

Its interface says: for every finite dimension `m`, reals `a ≤ b`, and arbitrary function `E : (Fin m → ℝ) → ℝ` that is positive at every point of the cube `[a,b]^m`, there exists a finite nonempty family with slopes in `[a,b]^m`, whose offsets are `LittleO a`, such that every label that is eventually a weak minimizer along `hₙ → 0+`, `pₙ → p ∈ [a,b]^m` satisfies the strict coordinatewise conclusion `|p_i − slope_i| < E(slope)`. The approximants `pₙ` are unrestricted outside the cube; the tolerance need not be continuous; and weak activity includes every tied minimizer.

This matches and strengthens the source lemma at `work/agents/permanence_hostile/endotactic_permanence.tex:161`: the source assumes `m ≥ 1`, `a < b`, and activity at every sequence index. The Lean theorem also covers `m = 0` and `a = b`, and only assumes eventual activity, so its activity premise is weaker. `LittleO a` encodes the source's `bump(h)=o(h^a)` as `h→0+`.

## What the proof composes

The induction derives each lower-dimensional slice input from the same theorem at smaller dimension, using the exact arbitrary tolerance `s ↦ E(liftPoint t e s)`. It constructs the raised-coordinate finite family and terminal archive, proves the local-slice estimate for every label, and turns exact-slice success into a minimum-coordinate neighborhood by closed active-limit sets and a compact bad set.

The outer step extracts a finite open-interval cover of `[a,b]`, selects a minimum-cardinality subcover to remove contained intervals, orders its centers, and invokes the checked cut construction. The finite tagged union uses the common prefix shifts. The separate slab-placement proof forces a globally active label's limiting minimum into its tag's slab; weak activity then restricts to the corresponding local family. Finally, finite sums of little-o offsets give the global `o(h^a)` bound. The full public theorem therefore composes the formerly separate slice and gluing components without assuming a local approximation property as an oracle.

## Pointwise finite-minimum derivative identity

`dynamics/FiniteMinimum.lean` formalizes the all-ties derivative identity used inside the source's finite-minimum chain rule (`endotactic_permanence.tex:585–616`). At any point where a nonempty finite minimum and its branches are differentiable, every active branch has the same derivative as the minimum. The proof uses Fermat's theorem on the nonnegative difference between an active branch and the lower envelope. The exact declarations and source/verification limits are recorded in `dynamics/FINITE_MINIMUM_REPORT.md`; `verify_finite_minimum.sh` passes and the declarations use only `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

This does not yet prove the full chain rule for Carathéodory trajectories. Absolute continuity of the finite minimum, almost-everywhere differentiability of the branch and minimum functions, and the trajectory's differential inclusion remain outside this component.

## Claim boundary and remaining gates

The lemma is an internal reconstruction of the affine argument attributed in the source to OpenAI release 149; this formal proof is not a novelty or priority claim. The formal artifacts verify the affine lemma, its fixed-family uniform-activity corollary, pointwise and a.e. finite-minimum calculus components, scalar AC barrier/entry principles, and conditional capped-scale/entry-time composition. They do not by themselves certify the complete all-dimensional permanence candidate in the larger TeX manuscript. The remaining formal gates include the actual measurable-rate weighted drift construction/replay, deriving the transition premise from the network vector field, global Carathéodory continuation/positivity, and the physical-time change for rational rates. That candidate remains distinct from the source-reported two-species result and established stronger-endotactic multidimensional results; independent external review and post-2014 priority audit remain open.

The new formal theorem also gives no practical algorithm when `E` is an opaque positive function. Its existence proof uses classical choices and has no useful bound on the number of labels, thresholds, cover size, or numerical cutoff.
