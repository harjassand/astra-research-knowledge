1. FIXED-d IID-MIXTURE SUPPORT IS POLYNOMIAL, NOT THE LOOSE d^(2N) CAP

Root pointed out the fixed-d permutation commutant algorithm being pursued
by c01_s01. The following support-bound correction is independently proved;
the algorithm itself is not my contribution and is not imported here.

Use matrix units E_ij in End(C^d). Tensor words of length N in the d^2
letters E_ij form a basis of End((C^d)^tensorN). Permutations of the tensor
factors act by permuting these words. Their orbits are indexed by the d^2
nonnegative letter counts summing to N. Each orbit sum has disjoint tensor
basis support, so the orbit sums are linearly independent and span the
permutation-invariant operators. Their complex dimension is exactly

 D_N=binom(N+d^2-1,d^2-1).

The invariant subspace is closed under adjoint, and its Hermitian part has
real dimension D_N. Every rho^tensorN is in this part and has trace one.
The compact convex hull of these states lies in an affine subspace of
dimension at most D_N-1. Therefore every normalized stopped iid-mixture
sigma_N has an exact representation using at most D_N iid atoms, by
Caratheodory. This is polynomial in N for fixed d and improves the valid
but loose d^(2N) cap recorded earlier.

The proof does not find the atoms or bound their algebraic degrees/bit
precision. Polynomial support existence alone is not a compiler. Full
separability of the exact target after ball absorption does not imply an
exact iid representation; its general-product decomposition has a different
atom class and is not covered by this iid cap. A separate SDP/Schur-block
approximation algorithm belongs to the worker constructing/auditing it.

2. A SHARPER FUNDAMENTAL SU(d) COVARIANCE FACTORIZATION

c04_s01 supplied its intrinsic compact-Lie filter/rotation relation. Here
is an independent eigenbasis reconstruction for the full traceless matrix
space, needing no compact-complexification or polar-decomposition import.

In the eigenbasis rho=diag(p_i), define the real-linear map K_rho on
traceless Hermitian T by
 (K_rho T)_ij=+i(p_i-p_j)/(p_i+p_j) T_ij for i!=j,
 with diagonal entries zero. It maps Hermitian matrices to Hermitian
matrices and is skew-adjoint in the HS metric. The plus sign is required
by r_T=-i[T,rho]; my earlier coordination message had an incorrect minus
sign, now corrected. The covariance positivity conclusions do not depend
on replacing K by -K, but the exact rotation relation does.

Since Tr rho K_rhoT=0, direct entrywise substitution gives

 r_T=v_(K_rhoT),
 D_C=V[C-K_rho C K_rho*]V*.

Here V sends T-coefficients in the Tr T_aT_b=delta_ab/2 basis to v_T.
If rho>=eta I, p_i+p_j<=1 and p_i,p_j>=eta imply
 |(p_i-p_j)/(p_i+p_j)|<=1-2eta.
Thus ||K_rho||<=1-2eta and
 I-K_rho K_rho*>=4eta(1-eta)I>=2eta I, since eta<=1/2.
For C=betaI+E with ||E||<=a,

 C-KCK* >=(2beta eta-2a)I.

On the traceless HS space the filter map is positive selfadjoint and
 <T,v_T>=2 Var_rho(T)>=2eta Tr(T-Tr rhoT I)^2>=2eta||T||_HS^2.
Accounting for the basis norm1/sqrt2 gives VV*>=2eta^2 I. Therefore if
a<=beta eta/2, D_C>=2beta eta^3 I, a strictly positive known margin.

For weak anisotropy E=A_N/N^delta, choose
 k=max(1,4M/beta), eta_N=kN^-delta.
Then M/N^delta<=beta eta_N/4, so the bracket has a margin even on
rho>=eta_N I/2. The exact logdet/Duhamel proof is unchanged, except
L_N now equals delta logN-log k-dlogd-D0. This doubles the leading
logdet cutoff drop compared to the earlier sqrt-anisotropy cutoff and
strengthens its superexponential coefficient. No new probability or
normalization assumption is used.

For either fixed-cutoff addendum, the original sufficient radius
beta eta^2/10 may be replaced by beta eta/4 with this factorization.
On rho>=eta I/2 the principal bracket is >=beta eta/2 I, ensuring a
smooth positive extension around the stopping boundary. C remains positive
and <=2betaI. All fixed logdet/error/full-separability bounds in the
original addenda therefore hold on this larger radius.

This fundamental reconstruction is an audit/refinement of c04_s01's
broader compact-Lie candidate. The general Lie-manifold/polar import in
that candidate is not independently validated by this full-matrix argument.
Covariance square roots, stopping precision and a finite-bit sampler remain
unimplemented. No novelty or efficient algorithm claim is made.
