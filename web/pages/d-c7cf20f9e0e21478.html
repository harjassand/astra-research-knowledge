projector with eta I/16 produces a full-rank stationary rho.

states with ancillary Hamming weight1 or2. It annihilates psi. Hence
||P_boundary rho||_1=3eta/4 and p_A=(1-eta)p+eta/8. Measuring a1 after the

amplitude from a0 is2(1-p)/(2-p). At p=1/16 and eta=p^3, the lower bound
