0. Exact stronger interface

For every desired fixed alpha>0, there is a decoder on the source-reported
GGJ binary CSS family with fixed positive linear data/syndrome tolerances and

 |e+f(syn(e)+m)|_stab-red <= alpha |e|_stab-red + beta |m|.

It is implemented, after O(N) fixed-code preprocessing, by a classical
circuit of O(N) gates, constant depth, and bounded fanout. Local matrix
conversion, fault-free classical computation, and code adjacency wiring are
the circuit model; no geometrically local embedding in Euclidean hardware is
claimed. For each fixed alpha and code parameter set, every constant is
independent of the block-length index. This meets the deterministic decoder
invariant needed by the separate coherent-noise investigation, while its
noise model remains a separate hypothesis there.

1. Arbitrary-cochain gain version of the support recursion

For b in C^d(G), 1<=d<t, define at each vertex v

 g_v(b) = |b|_{X_{>=v}(d)}
          - min_{w in C^{d-1}, supp w subset X_{>=v}(d-1)}
                     |b+delta^{d-1}w|_{X_{>=v}(d)}.

This is the largest global measured-syndrome-weight decrease from a local
flip at v, because delta w is supported entirely above v. It is an integer
>=0. If g_v=0, choose w=0. If g_v>0, choose a maximizing w deterministically.

At an ell-face u define the analogous local decrease q_u. For ell=d there
is no degree-(d-1) cochain above u, so q_u=0. For ell<d, a local correction
above u is admissible at every vertex v<=u and has exactly the same global
weight decrease; therefore q_u <= g_v for any such vertex. Fix one vertex
a(u)<=u, and use q_u<=g_{a(u)}.

The local filling inequality (v1 (1.1)) now reads

 r_{d,ell} (|b|_{X_{>=u}(d)}-q_u) <= |delta_u b_u|.

The same external-facet/global-syndrome decomposition used in v1 §2 gives

 r_{d,ell}|b|_{X_{>=u}(d)}
 <= O_u+N_u+S_u+r_{d,ell}q_u.

Unwind the neighbor recurrence exactly as v1 (2.4). For a fixed vertex v,
the number of pairs (u,f) with dim u=ell, dim f=d, a(u)=v, u<=f is at most

 N_{d,ell} = binom(t,ell)binom(t-ell,d-ell)n_+^d.

Indeed v<=u gives at most binom(t,ell)n_+^ell choices for u; each such u
has at most binom(t-ell,d-ell)n_+^{d-ell} upper d-faces. This bound ignores
the restriction a(u)=v and hence is safe. Define

 J_d = sum_{ell=0}^{d-1}
       (d-ell)! N_{d,ell}
       / prod_{h=ell+1}^d r_{d,h}.                      (1.1)

All r_{d,h}=n_- kappa_{t-h,d-h}>0. The ell=d term is absent because q=0.
The r_{d,ell} multiplying q cancels its factor in the unwound denominator.
The result for every b is

 (1-lambda L_d)|b|
 <= Q_d|b|^2/Z_d + M_d|delta^d b| + J_d sum_v g_v(b).   (1.2)

In particular, if |b|<=a_dZ_d/2 and c_d=(1-lambda L_d)/2, then

 c_d|b| <= M_d|delta^d b| + J_d sum_v g_v(b).           (1.3)

This is an exact positive bound on aggregate available repair gains, not a
claim that a nonzero individual symbol has a useful one-symbol flip.

2. Fixed color sweep and charging of invalidated gains

Build a conflict graph on vertices: v and v' are adjacent if their full
syndrome stars X_{>=v}(d) and X_{>=v'}(d) intersect. Every d-face has 2^d
vertices; a vertex belongs to at most ell_d=binom(t,d)n_+^d d-faces. Thus

 max degree <= (2^d-1)ell_d.

Greedy-color this graph once using at most Q_col=1+(2^d-1)ell_d colors in
O(N) preprocessing. A macro sweep processes these colors in fixed order.
For one color, compute every local optimal gain/correction from the current
syndrome and apply all its corrections simultaneously. Their entire
syndrome footprints are disjoint, so the decrease is exactly the sum of
the gains. No global sum, maximum color selection, or communication is used.

Let B_0 be the measured syndrome before one macro sweep and let
Delta_B=|B_0|-|B_final| be its total decrease. Write tau(v) for the time
v's color is processed. Then

 Delta_B = sum_v g_v(B_{tau(v)}).                       (2.1)

The gain function is 2-Lipschitz in block Hamming distance restricted to its
star: its first weight is 1-Lipschitz, and the minimum of all translated
weights is also 1-Lipschitz. Consequently

 g_v(B_0) <= g_v(B_{tau(v)})
            +2 |B_0-B_{tau(v)}|_{X_{>=v}(d)}.           (2.2)

Each accepted positive-gain update changes at most ell_d syndrome blocks.
Its gain is an integer >=1, so changed-block count is <=ell_d times its
gain. Each changed syndrome block belongs to only 2^d vertex stars.
Triangle inequality and summation over all earlier updates thus give

 sum_v |B_0-B_{tau(v)}|_{X_{>=v}(d)}
 <= 2^d ell_d Delta_B.

Combining with (2.1)-(2.2),

 sum_v g_v(B_0) <= C_gain,d Delta_B,
 C_gain,d = 1+2^{d+1}ell_d.                            (2.3)

No correction is performed at gain zero; otherwise support could change
without a chargeable potential decrease. The fixed-color ordering itself
does not need to find the color containing most initial gains: all lost
gains are charged to actual earlier measured-syndrome changes by (2.3).

3. Geometric syndrome contraction under arbitrary measurement faults

Take d=j+1, b=delta^j e+m plus all previous correction coboundaries.
It always obeys delta^d b=delta^d m, so |delta^d b|<=D_d M.
Its weight decreases throughout every sweep. If initially

 D_j E+M <= a_d Z_d/2,                                (3.1)

then (1.3) applies before every sweep. Let

 C_B,j = M_d D_d/c_d = A_d D_d,
 tau_j = min{1/2, c_d/(J_d C_gain,d)}, rho_j=1-tau_j.   (3.2)

Both tau_j>0 and rho_j in [1/2,1) are constants. Equations (1.3),(2.3)
give Delta_B >= [c_d |b|-M_dD_dM]/(J_d C_gain,d).
When |b|>=C_B,j M, replacing the positive coefficient by tau_j can only
weaken this bound; when |b|<C_B,j M, nonnegative Delta_B already implies
the weaker bound. Thus universally one macro sweep gives

 |b_after| <= rho_j |b_before| + tau_j C_B,j M.

After q macro sweeps,

 |b_q| <= rho_j^q(D_jE+M)+(1-rho_j^q)C_B,j M
         <= rho_j^q D_jE+(rho_j^q+C_B,j)M.              (3.3)

Every positive update has gain >=1 and correction support <=ell_j.
Total gains telescope to at most the initial syndrome weight, regardless of
how many fixed sweeps are run. Therefore the accumulated correction f_q
has support <=ell_j(D_jE+M), exactly the raw-support estimate of v1 §3.

Use eps_j and a_j from v1 (3.1). Soundness supplies u with the same
syndrome as r=e+f_q and

 |u| <= alpha_j(q) E + beta_j(q) M,
 alpha_j(q)=D_j rho_j^q/eps_j,
 beta_j(q)=(rho_j^q+C_B,j+1)/eps_j
            <= beta_j^par=(C_B,j+2)/eps_j.              (3.4)

Since |r|<=R_j E+ell_jM, its nearest cocycle is a stabilizer whenever

 (R_j+alpha_j(q))E+(ell_j+beta_j(q))M < a_j Z_j.        (3.5)

Consequently

 |e+f_q|_stab-red <= alpha_j(q)E+beta_j(q)M.            (3.6)

For any desired fixed alpha_*>0, choose a fixed integer q with

 rho_j^q <= alpha_* eps_j/D_j.                        (3.7)

If the right side >=1, q=0 already suffices; otherwise the ceiling of
log[D_j/(alpha_*eps_j)]/(-log rho_j) suffices. Promises (3.1) and

 (R_j+alpha_*)E+(ell_j+beta_j^par)M < a_jZ_j            (3.8)

are explicit positive linear data/syndrome thresholds independent of N.

4. Classical circuit size, depth, fanout, and locality

At fixed code parameters, one upward star has fixed many syndrome/data
bits over the fixed field. Its gain/optimal-correction function is a finite
Boolean function and can be implemented by a fixed-size fixed-depth circuit
with bounded fan-in and fanout. Exhaustive enumeration can equivalently be
hardwired into this circuit; the constant is enormous but independent of N.
Each color applies O(N) independent local circuits. Summing the disjoint
updates is local. If data-star overlap were present then the vertices would
share a j-face; because j<t every j-face has an upper (j+1)-face, this would
give a shared syndrome footprint and hence a conflict. Thus data supports
are disjoint as well within one color. Across colors, updates are sequential.

There are fixed Q_col colors and fixed q sweeps. Every input block feeds at
most a constant number of local circuits. Splitting a fixed fanout into
fanout-two replication adds constant depth. The full classical decoder has
O(N) gates and constant depth, bounded fanout, and only fixed-size stars as
its communication neighborhoods. It needs no global reduction or adaptive
unbounded loop. Code preprocessing/coloring is O(N) sequential and is done
once for each fixed member, not in the repeated syndrome circuit.

5. Constant-depth transpose contraction and full physical Pauli interface

Run v1's supplied-syndrome preprocessing/postprocessing (5.1)-(5.2), but
replace the sequential K decoder by the q-sweep contraction decoder of §3
in degree L=t-k. Its residual is

 c+c_hat=delta_K q0+w,
 |w|<=alpha_K P_data E+beta_K^par P_noise M.            (5.1)

The exact homology chase v1 §6 is unchanged. Its noisy reverse comparison
now gives

 |e+f|_stab-red <= alpha_trans E+beta_trans M,
 alpha_trans=G H^L P_data alpha_K,
 beta_trans=G c_m [sum_{r=0}^{L-1}(HD)^r
                     +(HD)^L(1+beta_K^par D)].        (5.2)

Choose alpha_K <= alpha_trans,desired/(G H^L P_data), a fixed positive
number. The K promise is explicitly

 D_L P_data E+P_noise M <= a_{L+1}Z_{L+1}/2,
 (R_L+alpha_K)P_data E
   +(ell_L+beta_K^par)P_noise M < a_L Z_L.             (5.3)

The forward linear maps, K projection, local cube H, and final J each have
fixed finite local support/fanout, fixed field, and a fixed t-stage ladder.
They are fixed-size bounded-fanout circuits repeated O(N) times. Thus the
transpose decoder also has O(N) gates, constant depth, and bounded fanout.

For t=4,k=2 all required source degrees are 2 and 3, and the GGJ parameter
selection already gives lambda L<1/2 for F and K. No stronger spectral
inequality or enlargement beyond the source choice is needed. N,Z_2,Z_3
are proportional with family-independent constants, so all promises above
are fixed positive fractions of N.

Binary trace-dual conversion and physical X/Z conventions remain those of
v1 §8. For a desired whole-Pauli physical contraction alpha in (0,1), let
B=max_f s dim_F F(f). Choose the block contraction of EACH sector to be
at most alpha/(2B). Given a Pauli representative of minimum physical
support, each sector's reduced block weight is at most that physical weight.
The output support is at most the sum of the sector output bit supports.
Thus the resulting full decoder has

 |e+f(syn(e)+m)|_physical,stab-red
 <= alpha |e|_physical,stab-red + beta_phys |m|_bits,
 beta_phys=B max{beta_F^par,beta_trans}.               (5.4)

The sum of faulty syndrome-block supports is at most total faulty syndrome
bit support. Choose the common data/syndrome promises by taking a positive
minimum of the sufficient F-sector and transpose-sector linear budgets,
and shrink each by one half to avoid strict-boundary equality. This defines
finite positive eta_data,eta_syndrome and the exact two-sector interface.

6. Gauge covariance, source status, and scope

Under an actual stalk basis change, upper-face components of every array
transform contragrediently. These transformations commute with Delta,
intertwine T, carry K to its changed-coordinate copy, and transport the
chosen S/P/Pi maps. H and J act only on cube/face indices. Transporting all
choices makes the construction equivariant, and all support bounds are
unchanged. Independently rechoosing local splittings in new bases can change
the decoder's tie-breaking/correction but still satisfies the same theorem.
No minimum-weight inverse needs to be invariant under an anchor permutation.

The exact code's constant rate, relative distance, check degree, and local
universal product expansion remain source-reported GGJ hypotheses, not
independently reconstructed in this campaign. Their published preprint is
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20780v1. NP's earlier almost-good family decoder
and dual reduction remain prior work (https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03632v2).
The present result is the source-dependent applicability proof and stronger
explicit local gain analysis, not a claim that single-shot or parallel
decoding itself is new.

This decoder may be supplied as a deterministic good-path component to the
separate Sol3 common-bath theorem only with all these source and circuit
dependencies retained. It does not validate that theorem's aggregate
coherent-noise bound or establish a physical bath mechanism. No empirical
or realistic-resource conclusion follows from astronomically large fixed
constants. Exact external novelty and independent verification remain open.
