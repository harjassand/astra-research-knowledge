## Decision and precise interface

All four acquired centroid/correction/reset trajectories and both conditional frozen-preconditioner continuations pass. Every acceptance uses exact rational equalities, unit/distinct checks, normalized harmonic mean and a positive-definiteness certificate. The interface is: given integer T, acquire N distinct rational unit vectors x_i on S² with equal weights, nonconstant harmonic mean through degree T at most

    beta_T = 1 / [2048 (T+1)^5],

and normalized empirical harmonic Gram matrix greater than I/2. M=T(T+2). Use probability surface measure, rational orthogonal solid harmonics q_j with squared norms nu_j>0, and e_j=q_j/sqrt(nu_j). The precise rational tests are

    mean_norm² = sum_j mu_j²/nu_j <= beta_T²,
    H - (1/2) diag(nu) positive definite,
    H_jk = (1/N) sum_i q_j(x_i)q_k(x_i).

The producer's basis is checked and transported into an independently constructed basis; its moment/Gram scalars are never accepted on assertion alone.

The executed cases are (T,q,N)=(2,3,18) and (3,4,32), with q=T+1. The previously gated universal centroid theorem uses q=600(T+1), N=720000(T+1)², hence N=6,480,000 and 11,520,000 here. Both executions are outside that theorem's cardinality regime by a factor of 360,000. The accepted output satisfies the target interface directly. No universal q=T+1 theorem follows, and no enormous-cardinality execution is attributed to this gate. The source theorem's exact polynomial-time LP primitive also remains distinct from this bounded HiGHS-proposal/repair implementation.

## Independently reconstructed basis and final certificates

For exponents (2a,2b,2c), the normalized sphere integral is

    integral x^(2a)y^(2b)z^(2c)
       = (2a-1)!! (2b-1)!! (2c-1)!! / [2(a+b+c)+1]!!,

with (-1)!!=1; any odd exponent gives zero. This follows by comparing the product Gaussian integral in Cartesian and polar coordinates. It supplies the entire rational inner product without an orthonormalization oracle. Homogeneous degree-l harmonic polynomials have dimension 2l+1: the Laplacian onto degree l-2 polynomials is surjective, since multiplication by |x|² followed by the Laplacian has a nonzero scalar diagonal plus lower Laplacian powers and therefore a finite triangular inverse. This yields the difference of the two homogeneous-polynomial dimensions. The implemented nullspaces and integrations check the required dimensions, harmonicity, orthogonality and zero means directly.

Let q^p be the producer basis and q^o our basis. We reconstruct the rational invertible S satisfying q^p=S q^o by inner products, and compare the actual polynomials, not sampled values. Thus mu^p=S mu^o, J^p=S J^o and H^p=S H^o S^t. Weighted residual norms are invariant under this change, as are positive-definiteness tests with the norm diagonal. The independent final Gram certificate clears all rational denominators and records the leading principal integer minors using fraction-free elimination. Their positivity is Sylvester's criterion. This acceptance does not use numerical eigenvalues or the producer's LDL factorization.

The mutation/control file also supplies a useful failure distinction: the octahedron has mean zero through degree2, yet its degree2 Gram fails the required positive-definiteness condition. A small harmonic mean alone is insufficient for the radial/design interface.

## Acquired rational centroid initializer

The cells are equal-area northern rings and longitude wedges, reflected south. For ring j=1,...,q,

    r0=(j-1)/q, r1=j/q, m=2j-1,
    z=1-r², phi_k=(2k+1)pi/m, alpha=pi/m.

Under probability area measure, d area=r dr d phi/(2pi); each cell has area (r1²-r0²)/(2m)=1/(2q²). Its northern centroid has

    mz = 1-(r0²+r1²)/2,
    A = (2q²/m) integral_(r0)^(r1) r² sqrt(2-r²) dr,
    (mx,my) = A sinc(alpha) (cos(phi_k),sin(phi_k)).

The polar m=1 cell has transverse centroid zero. The normalized centroid has stereographic parameters (mx,my)/(sqrt(mx²+my²+mz²)+mz). The hemisphere sign is applied by the rational sphere chart. The exporter deliberately stores positive template mz on both hemispheres; treating it as signed mz would be a schema error.

Our independent enclosure uses the binomial expansion of sqrt(2-r²), a geometric rational tail, Machin's alternating arctangent formula for pi, Taylor/factorial sin/cos bounds, outward dyadic interval operations and integer-square-root bounds. It uses more precision and terms than the supplied chart boxes, proves its enclosure is contained in them, and checks that the emitted midpoint is within delta/16 in parameter norm. No decimal centroid or numerical integration oracle is accepted. Every required hemisphere/ring/wedge label occurs exactly once.

Development failures were retained: an initial exact-power interval prototype became too slow and was terminated; a first fixed-precision Horner scheme produced a wide interval. It was corrected by adding six guard bits per Horner level before any acceptance. The resulting independent interval acquisition passes both q3/q4 cases. ## Original equations, optimality, tangent and reset

At current exact unit points, J is the averaged tangential derivative of the rational features, and mu their averaged value. The original box problem is

    minimize d,
    Jw=-mu,
    -d <= w_a <= d, 0<=d<=1.

The exported equation matrix uses A=N J and b=-N mu. The checker rebuilds all A,b, the two signed box inequalities for each coordinate, the bound list and objective. Numerical row scaling and a solver's success string cannot replace these original equations. Float.hex vectors are converted to their exact binary rationals; all original equality residuals and every box margin are recomputed and retained.

For an exact primal w,d, an exported dual consists of lambda and mu_a>=0 with signs s_a, satisfying

    A^t lambda + s*mu = 0,
    sum_a mu_a=1,
    d=-lambda dot b.

For any feasible v,t, -lambda dot b=sum_a s_a mu_a v_a<=t. Equality with the feasible primal d proves its box optimum, including the redundant d<=1 restriction. The checker also evaluates a dual when the associated primal is infeasible; dual feasibility does not rescue that primal.

The exact Euclidean comparator satisfies Aw=b and w=A^t lambda. For any other feasible w+z, Az=0, so w dot z=lambda dot Az=0 and ||w+z||²=||w||²+||z||². This proves the unique Euclidean minimum. The derivative columns are already tangent, so this vector is tangent; the checker nonetheless verifies the projection and derivative equations directly. Its coordinate box radius is only a feasible bound, and is not labeled a box optimum.

Projection v_i=(I-x_i x_i^t)w_i preserves the harmonic derivative equation. The exact rational Cayley update is

## Independently derived global feature/Cayley Hessian bound

This is a mathematical statement for every T, separate from the finite runs. Put lambda_l=l(l+1) and

    A² = sum_(l=1)^T lambda_l(2l+1)/2,
    B² = sum_(l=1)^T lambda_l(3lambda_l-2)(2l+1)/8.

The addition kernel of the degree-l orthonormal feature vector along one great circle is (2l+1)P_l(cos(t-s)). P_l'(1)=lambda_l/2 and P_l''(1)=lambda_l(lambda_l-2)/8. Differentiating once in each variable gives the squared first directional derivative; twice in each gives (2l+1)[P_l'(1)+3P_l''(1)]. Summing degrees proves the displayed A,B. The second derivative along a unit-speed great circle is the covariant feature Hessian on its velocity twice. Polarization and ||u+v||²+||u-v||²=4 show its mixed operator norm is at most B as well. Direct exact derivatives at a pole independently check these constants through degree4. In particular T2 gives A²=18,B²=63; T3 gives A²=60,B²=420.

In fixed tangent Cayley coordinates u, write d=1+||u||²/4. The induced metric is d^-2 I, and the speed of the image of a coordinate velocity w is ||w||/d. Along u+t w, the Christoffel vector is

    Gamma(w,w) = [||w||² u - 2(u dot w)w]/(2d).

Its Euclidean norm is ||u||||w||²/(2d), because the numerator is a reflection of u multiplied by ||w||². The intrinsic acceleration of the curve has norm ||u||||w||²/(2d²). Hence

    ||D²(e compose Q)(u)[w,w]||
      <= [B+(||u||/2)A] ||w||²/d²
      <= K(r)||w||²,  K(r)=B+(r/2)A,

for ||u||<=r. Polarization again yields the mixed bound. This confirms the load-bearing coefficient r/2 in the proposed basin proof; a crude extra factor3 is unnecessary.

## Conditional a-posteriori basin and finite reset continuation

This general lemma requires supplied, explicitly checked rational data. It does not automatically acquire an acceptable preconditioner at moderate cardinality. For tangent preconditioner R from harmonic residual coordinates to node velocities, the exact quantities

    C² = max_i sum_(a,j) R_(ia,j)² nu_j,
    a² = sum_(j,k) (I-JR)_(jk)² nu_k/nu_j

bound, respectively, the maximum-node map and the normalized residual-space defect by their Frobenius norms. Under a basis change, use R^o=R^p S. The checker reconstructs these quantities from every exported candidate matrix and the independent derivative, checks tangent columns and rational upper bounds a,C,A,B,E (E>=sqrt(M)), and proves each claimed Gram lower bound independently.

Let rho>0, r=C rho<=1. Define F(z) to be the averaged rational feature vector at Q_(x_i)((Rz)_i); use the norm ||z||_nu²=sum z_j²/nu_j. If

    ||F(0)||_nu <= rho/4,
    a+K(r) C² rho <= 1/2,

then Phi(z)=z-F(z) has derivative norm at most1/2 on the rho ball and maps it into the 3rho/4 ball. Indeed DF(0)=JR, and the Hessian bound gives ||DF(z)-JR||<=K(r)C²||z||_nu. Banach's theorem supplies a true mean-zero point within this chart ball. A numerical residual alone would not supply this assertion.

For retention of Gram and a final distinctness perturbation delta_f, it suffices that the initial Gram lower bound lambda0 obey

    lambda0 > 1/2 + 2 E A (r+delta_f),
    A delta_f <= beta_T/16.

The feature vector has constant norm sqrt(M), and its change along geodesic length d is at most A d. Thus each outer product moves by at most2 sqrt(M)A d. Cayley geodesic travel is 2 atan(||u||/2)<=||u||, yielding the stated bound for the averaged Gram.

With coordinate mesh chi<=sigma/(2MV), V=max(1,max_j 1/nu_j), and sigma<=min(beta_T/128,rho/16), every reset has normalized norm at most sigma and the rho ball stays invariant. The distance to the fixed point after k steps is at most2^-k rho+2sigma. If2^-I rho<=beta_T/128, F is at most3/2 Lipschitz and the final pre-distinctness residual is at most9beta_T/256<beta_T/4. The final distinct reset adds at most beta_T/16. All coordinates have a fixed mesh before each new action; the certificate is compatible with finite-bit regeneration.

We checked all four candidate R matrices for each degree: centroid and each of the three LP outputs.
