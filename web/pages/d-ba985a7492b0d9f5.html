STRONGEST EXACT CLAIM
Let F be an explicit n by n rational-complex matrix and lambda_i>0 rational.
Suppose all but t rows are monomial relative to one permutation p: for each
regular i, F[i,j]=0 unless j=p(i). The t exceptional rows are arbitrary, dense,
and have arbitrary phases. Define
 c_k(F,lambda)=sum_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k}
                 (product_{i in I}lambda_i)|det F[I,J]|^2.
All c_k, all row/column occupation-prefix partitions, exact zero tests, and
conditional samples can be ACQUIRED in
 O(n^2 (t+1)^2 5^t) exact arithmetic operations per partition computation,
with polynomial bit lengths. Hence t<=C log n for fixed public C admits a
polynomial-bit exact counting algorithm and expected-polynomial-time exact
sampler, or a fixed-time epsilon-TV random-bit sampler at charged precision.
This strictly admits dense arbitrary-phase rows beyond the monomial subclass.
No coefficient/partition oracle or imported FPRAS is used.

For the corresponding fixed-k full-basis homogenization with d=n-2k unit
old-to-dummy coordinate lines, an acquired two-replica kernel redraws the
FIRST full-basis configuration independently from this law. It is reversible
for pi tensor pi, and for EVERY real f1,f2,
 E_P[f1(A)+f2(B)] = Var_pi f1.
Thus the requested additive energy bound holds with L=1 in this admitted
subclass. This is not a construction for arbitrary F or arbitrary calibrated
coordinate auxiliaries. The unrestricted OA113 transfer remains unresolved.

FULL DERIVATION
1. Expand the determinant on regular rows.
Fix selected exceptional rows A=I intersect H. Let R=I minus H. A nonzero
minor requires p(R) subset J. Since p is injective, expansion along every
regular row gives
 det F[I,J] = sign(I,J) (product_{i in R}F[i,p(i)]) det F[A,B],
 B=J minus p(R), |B|=|A|.
The sign disappears on taking the squared modulus. Conversely every choice
of R and B with |B|=|A| and disjoint I,J yields precisely this term; there is
no expansion multiplicity. Therefore the desired partition sums over
regular row choices and exceptional free-column choices, weighted by
 product_{i in R}lambda_i |F[i,p(i)]|^2,
 product_{a in A}lambda_a,
 |det F[A,B]|^2.

2. Encode the exceptional squared determinant by a double exterior algebra.
For fixed A in H, let E_A have ordered basis e_a, a in A. For each column j
put h_j=sum_{a in A}F[a,j] e_a. If B is processed in ANY fixed order,
 h_{j1} wedge ... wedge h_{j|A|}
   =det F[A,B] e_A,
with the column-order sign understood. In a second copy use conjugate h_j.
The top coefficient of the product of both wedge strings is exactly
|det F[A,B]|^2. Process a free-column choice b_j=1 by applying
 C_j: u tensor v -> (u wedge h_j) tensor (v wedge conjugate(h_j)).
Process b_j=0 by the identity. This keeps the full mismatch registers P,Q;
it never projects ket/bra, replicas, or common occupied cores onto one cut.
Intermediate coefficients can have either sign and can be complex. This
is an EXACT arithmetic DP, not a positive sampling rule for those registers.
Only completed nonnegative prefix partitions are used as probabilities.

Explicit matrix element: for a notin P and b notin Q,
 (P,Q) -> (P union {a}, Q union {b})
 has multiplier F[a,j] conjugate(F[b,j]) times
 (-1)^(|{x in P:x>a}|+|{x in Q:x>b}|).
The two exterior degrees always agree. There are
 sum_{q=0}^{|A|}binom(|A|,q)^2=binom(2|A|,|A|)<=4^|A|
possible (P,Q) registers.

3. Process the monomial permutation cycles.
At site i let r_i indicate that row i is selected, let m_i indicate incoming
monomial column occupancy, and let b_i indicate selection of this column by
the exceptional determinant. The hard disjoint condition is exactly
 r_i+m_i+b_i<=1.
The actual column occupation is c_i=m_i+b_i. If i is regular, outgoing
occupancy on p(i) is r_i, and choosing r_i=1 contributes
lambda_i |F[i,p(i)]|^2 and one row-count degree. If i is exceptional,
r_i=1 iff i in A, choosing it contributes lambda_i and one row degree, and
its outgoing monomial occupancy is zero. A free-column choice b_i=1 applies
C_i. Prefix constraints simply prohibit the incompatible r_i or c_i.

Write each cycle in p order. Enumerate its incoming boundary bit 0 or1
(only0 if the last row is exceptional), process all sites, and retain terms
whose last outgoing bit equals the initial bit. This traces precisely the
circular occupancy condition, with each valid configuration counted once.
Carry the exterior registers across cycles, initially empty. At the end
retain only P=Q=A; the row-degree-k coefficient is the desired contribution.
Summing over all A subset H gives c_k and the same formula for each prefix.

4. Costs and numerical representation.
For fixed A, carry at most O(n 4^|A|) register/count/boundary states. Each
site update has O((|A|+1)^2) branches. There are n sites and at most two
boundary choices per cycle; their contributions are summed, not multiplied
into a separate exponential. Summing over A costs
 sum_A (|A|+1)^2 4^|A| <=(t+1)^2 5^t.
This proves the displayed O(n^2(t+1)^2 5^t) arithmetic bound.

Rational-complex entries can be converted to Gaussian integers with one
common denominator D of polynomial bit length. Each c_k then has the factor
D^(-2k). For lambda_i=a_i/b_i, multiply every partition by Q=product b_i;
at each site use factor b_i when r_i=0 and a_i when r_i=1, in place of1 and
lambda_i. The common denominator QD^(2k) is independent of the occupied
sets, including under prefix constraints. All DP operations are now exact
Gaussian-integer operations. The log of the sum of absolute intermediate
path contributions is polynomial in n and input length: there are at most
3^n occupation choices, at most (t!)^2 exterior expansion terms, and at most
2n matrix/activity factors in each contribution. Thus cancellation does not
require exponentially many precision bits; exact integer bit lengths are
polynomial. The output can be exponentially small as a real number without
causing inverse-probability runtime. When t grows linearly, 5^t is explicitly
exponential and is not hidden as an oracle.

5. Acquire prefix samples.
At site i compute the three completed partitions for (r_i,c_i) equal to
(0,0),(1,0),(0,1), conditioned on all earlier outcomes. Choose a branch in
proportion to those nonnegative exact weights. The chain rule gives the
specified determinant-squared law. Zero branches are recognized exactly.
At most3n partition calls are made, for
 O(n^3(t+1)^2 5^t) arithmetic operations per draw, plus bit arithmetic.
An exact integer categorical draw by rejection uses expected at most two
attempts. For a fixed-time epsilon-TV version, approximate each 3-category
choice by a dyadic grid with denominator2^b, b=ceil(log2(3n/epsilon))+1;
per-call TV<=3/2^b and sequential coupling charges the sum over n choices.
Random bits, exact rational threshold comparisons, and the charged b are
part of the algorithm. This is not a fixed bounded-time exact non-dyadic
random-bit claim.

6. Actual full-basis point law, rather than an aggregate-hole oracle.
Let d=n-2k. Add d private dummy coordinates and all coordinate pairs
(e_j,e_dummy), activity1, to the original pairs (e_i,f_i), activity lambda_i.
A full basis must choose exactly d coordinate pairs, covering every dummy
and distinct old coordinates. For every I,J in c_k, the d remaining old
coordinates can be bijected to the d dummies in d! ways. Private-row
expansion gives determinant modulus |det F[I,J]| in each case. Thus the
full-paired partition is d! c_k. Sample I,J by the DP and an independent
uniform bijection; this samples the exact microscopic full-basis line law.
All point weights are direct rational determinant values, and all required
partitions are computed by the DP above. No f(U) hole coefficient is supplied.

7. Explicit two-replica kernel and additive energy.
On (A,B), draw A' independently from the acquired full-basis law pi and keep
B. For positive-law states,
 pi(A)pi(B) P((A,B),(A',B))=pi(A)pi(B)pi(A')
 is symmetric in A,A', proving reversibility. For Fpair=f1(A)+f2(B),
 E_P(Fpair)=1/2 E_(A,A' iid pi)[(f1(A)-f1(A'))^2]=Var_pi f1.
It holds for arbitrary f2 without a full pair gap, exactly the OA113/L07
additive-function interface. If a symmetric choice of refreshed replica is
preferred, its energy is (Var f1+Var f2)/2 and L=2. Arbitrary positive rational
row activities lambda along annealing preserve the DP and the same energy
constant. A certified finite-time approximate draw is charged separately;
it does not turn an approximate transition into an exactly stationary kernel.

8. Acquire the structural certificate rather than supplying it silently.
Inspect each row by exact zero tests. A row with more than one nonzero must
be exceptional. Among singleton rows sharing a nonzero column, retain one
regular row per column and mark all others exceptional. Zero rows are
regular and can be assigned distinct unused permutation images. Extend the
resulting partial injection to a permutation by assigning the remaining
unused columns to exceptional rows. This finds a valid certificate with the
minimum number of exceptional rows: every multi-entry row is forced out,
and at most one singleton row in each column group can remain regular.
All operations are polynomial-bit. A supplied permutation is unnecessary.
The current diagnostic implementation accepts a certificate and verifies it;
this straightforward certificate-acquisition routine is not yet coded.
