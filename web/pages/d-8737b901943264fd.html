The decoder reconstructs the full n-qutrit density. Error is supremum half

  ell=(d/(sr)) integral Tr[P0 F_v(I-P0)F_v P0] dv.

For the last bound, each complex root is T_x+iT_y and costs at most twice

integral sum_a [T_a,F_v]^dag[T_a,F_v]dv=(E(F)/d)I.

the frame was produced by the instrument, not supplied after localization.

5. INTERNAL HOT-SPIN CHANNEL: EXACT BINOMIAL FORMULA

Set A_v=P0 F_v P0. The normalized P0 restriction

  T0(sigma)=(d/(s c0)) integral A_v sigma A_v dv               (5.1)

is unital, TP and SU(2)-covariant on H_k. c0>0 since the integral contains
nonzero projected filters. Missing components in (2.1) merely cost ell.

Let u=sin^2(theta) describe the canonical tilt mixing e1 and e3. CP^2 Haar
measure has radial density 2u du; its first-plane direction is uniform.
On the N=0 spin basis q=0,...,k, the E22 weight is lambda2+q. That basis
vector is highest for SU(2)_(13) with highest root weight M_q=m+k-q.
Consequently A_v has, up to an internal SU(2) rotation, diagonal entries

  a_q(u)=E f_(Bin(M_q,u)).                                  (5.2)

Adding one Bernoulli trial proves exactly

  |a_q(u)-a_(q-1)(u)|
     =u/(K+1) Pr[Bin(m+k-q,u)<=K].                          (5.3)

The internal Dirichlet form is
E_2(A)=sum_(q=1..k) q(k-q+1)(a_q-a_(q-1))^2.
Using probability squared <=probability and the beta integral

  integral_0^1 u^3 Pr[Bin(M,u)<=K]du
    =6 binom(K+4,4)/[(M+1)(M+2)(M+3)(M+4)],                 (5.4)

gives

  I_2:=integral E_2(A_v)dv
   <= k(k+1)(k+2)(K+2)(K+3)(K+4)
       /[12(K+1)(m+1)(m+2)(m+3)(m+4)].                     (5.5)

For j=k/2>0, covariance and the Hermitian-Kraus commutator identity give
the exact dipole contraction gap

  1-lambda1(T0)=d I_2/[2s c0 (k+1)j(j+1)].

Combining (3.2),(5.5),

  c0(1-lambda1)<= B_mK
   :=(m+k+2)(K+4)/[(K+2)(m+2)(m+3)(m+4)]<=4/m^2            (5.6)

when k<=m. The k=0 channel is trivial. This is substantially sharper than

  T(T0 sigma_gamma,sigma_gamma)

  T(Phi_F sigma_gamma,sigma_gamma)

7. EXACT POSITIVE PARTIAL-FLAG REPRESENTATION OF THE FULL GIBBS DENSITY

The latent coherent planes in this section are a proof representation, not
data supplied to the code. Let A=diag(p_n)>0 and W be an orthonormal two-frame.
Its normal is v, and P_W is the corresponding top-fiber projector. Resolution
of identity gives I=(d/r) integral P_W dW. Therefore

  pi_lambda(A)=(d/r) integral pi(A^(1/2))P_W pi(A^(1/2))dW.   (7.1)

The top fiber has the concrete Cartan-product realization
Sym^k(W) tensor (w1 wedge w2)^tensor m, with a determinant^lambda3 scalar.
Polar QR of A^(1/2)W gives W' G_W^(1/2), G_W=W^dag A W. Hence the integrand
is a positive fiber operator on W', with scalar det(G_W)^m and internal
operator Sym^k(G_W), times the same irrelevant det(A)^lambda3. Normalizing
(7.1) is an EXACT positive mixture of the fiber states in (6.2).

Here are uniform quantitative bounds on its mixing distribution. Put
u=1-|v3|^2, qbar=(1+b/a)/2<1 and g0=1-qbar. For large n,

  det G_W/(p1 p2)
      =1-(1-p3/p1)|v1|^2-(1-p3/p2)|v2|^2 <=e^(-g0 u).

Compression interlacing gives eigenvalues mu1<=p1, mu2<=p2, hence
Tr Sym^k G_W<=Tr Sym^k diag(p1,p2). For u<=1/m, a principal-frame
comparison of the compression with diag(p1,p2) has norm <=4p1 u.
Thus, for m large and k<=m, its symmetric-power trace is bounded below by
a fixed positive multiple of Tr Sym^k diag(p1,p2); the determinant factor
is >=(1-1/m)^m. CP^2 volume of u<=1/m is 1/m^2. Dividing by this lower
normalization, the mixture expectation is dominated by

  C_a m^2 * 2u exp(-g0 m u)du.                              (7.2)

The fiber inverse temperature is gamma_W=log(mu1/mu2). Rayleigh gives
mu2>=p2-(p2-b)u, so, for a fixed C_a,

  0<=gamma_W<=gamma_c+C_a u, gamma_c=log(p1/p2)=O(n^(-1/2)).  (7.3)

Since k=O(sqrt(n log n))=o(m), the exponential e^(3j C_a u) is absorbed
in (7.2) for large n. Direct gamma integrals imply

  E gamma_W <=gamma_c+C/m,
  E[gamma_W^2 e^(3j gamma_W)]
       <=C e^(3j gamma_c)(gamma_c^2+1/m^2).                  (7.4)

Average (6.2) over (7.1), using convexity and covariance. This proves

  e_lambda<=12/(K+2)
     +C/m^2 [j(gamma_c+1/m)
              +j^2 e^(3j gamma_c)(gamma_c^2+1/m^2)].         (7.5)

On typical collision carriers this is exactly (1.2). The positive mixture
does not classicalize the spin: all its internal quantum density is retained
in every filtered branch. No free estimated or latent frame is decoded.

8. EXACT FINITE FRAME ALPHABET, CHANNELS AND PRECISION

Set L=k+m. F_v is a polynomial of degree <=L in the projector vv^dag:
interpolate f on the integer spectrum N=0,...,L, then substitute
N_v=sum_ij v_i conj(v_j)E_ij-lambda3 I. Thus the instrument completeness
and composed channel are polynomials of degree <=2L in vv^dag.

An explicit positive CP^2 cubature for this degree uses two relative phases
on (2L+1)-point uniform grids and two probability stick variables
x1=t1, x2=(1-t1)t2, x3=(1-t1)(1-t2). t1 has weight 2(1-t1)dt1;
t2 has weight dt2. Use L+1-node Gauss-Legendre quadrature for each,
reweighting the first coordinate by 2(1-t1). The first reweighted rule is
positive and integrates degree <=2L against its linear weight exactly,
since the unweighted polynomial then has degree <=2L+1.
The phase grids kill all nonzero modes of magnitude <=2L. The surviving
balanced monomials have stick degrees <=2L, integrated exactly by these
rules. Positive Gauss-Jacobi for the first coordinate is an alternative.
The number of public nodes is

  C_frame=[(2L+1)(L+1)]^2=O(n^4).                            (8.1)

With positive normalized weights w_v, replace (2.1) by the exact finite sum.
Choose a public unitary U_v taking e3 to v and I_K:Q_K->V_lambda. The
encoder Kraus to its q_K-dimensional branch is

  A_v=sqrt(d w_v/s) I_K^dag F pi(U_v)^dag.

It stores the discrete node label v and A_v X A_v^dag. Completeness is
sum A_v^dag A_v=I. The decoder embeds by pi(U_v) I_K. Their composition
is precisely the covariant channel Phi_F, so the bound is uniform on the
entire unknown U(3) orbit. There is no retained continuous frame register.

The phase indices and quadrature-node indices are finite labels. Their public
coordinates and weights have explicit algebraic descriptions. One stable
completion, for v=(w,s) with s=v3>=0 real, is
U_v=[[I_2-ww^dag/(1+s), w],[-w^dag,s]], with denominator >=1.
Frame coordinates are not supplied at input: the label is produced by the
instrument and fully charged. Exact quadrature/synthesis is ideal here.
Finite approximations need separate channel-error budgeting; pi_lambda(U)
has generator norms O(n), and weight/filter/normalization errors accumulate.
Efficient root computation, matrix realization, conditioning, finite gate
counts and runtime are not proved. An infinite-precision physical instrument
or an efficient hardware implementation is not inferred from the finite
information-theoretic CPTP construction.
