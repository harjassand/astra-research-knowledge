1. EXACT MATCHED LOCAL-FISHER REFORMULATION: PROMISING PROOF INTERFACE

This part is a direct finite-dimensional derivation from the gate, not a
quoted theorem. Let A_1,...,A_m be any finite traceless-Hermitian frame for Q
and define the local model

  rho_theta = (I + sum_i theta_i A_i)/d

for theta in a sufficiently small neighborhood of 0. At theta=0 the state is
I/d, its SLDs are exactly A_i, and the trace of its quantum Fisher matrix is
V(Q)=sum_i tau(A_i^2). For a rank-one POVM E_y=d p_y pi_y (so
sum_y p_y pi_y=I/d), the classical Fisher-information trace at 0 is

  sum_i,y p_y [Tr(pi_y A_i)]^2.

Thus k(Q) is exactly the largest local classical Fisher-information trace
over one POVM, with the exact tracial reference required by the target. Any
finite POVM can be refined into rank-one effects without decreasing Fisher
information, so this is the unrestricted POVM optimum. An arbitrary pure-state
ensemble with a different barycenter does not represent this k(Q) problem.

For a legal symmetric broadcaster B with marginal Phi, the joint states
Omega_theta=B(rho_theta) have marginals Phi(rho_theta). At theta=0 those
marginals are again I/d. The output marginal SLDs are Phi(A_i), hence their
quantum-Fisher trace is s_{Phi^2}(Q)=sum_i tau[A_i Phi^2(A_i)]. On the
positive-HS-spectrum subcase, 0<=lambda(Phi)<=1 and therefore

  V-s_Phi <= V-s_{Phi^2} <= 2(V-s_Phi).

This gives the desired linear scale for the *quantum* marginal-Fisher loss.
The exact missing conversion is a dimension- and frame-rank-independent
quantitative no-broadcasting lemma producing one POVM M_Q on the two-output
state Omega_theta such that its classical Fisher-information trace at 0 is
at least V(Q)-C_0[V(Q)-s_Phi(Q)]. The POVM may depend on Q. Pulling it back
through B gives a legal input POVM, hence a lower bound on k(Q); if this holds
for all Q, the gate's cone-separation argument yields one Psi for all Q.
The critical gap is one measurement for the whole weighted frame: scalar SLD
measurements can be direction-specific, and a large marginal QFI alone does
not give a common classical Fisher-information trace. Extending from Phi>=0
uses the gate's Phi^2 positive-spectrum reduction and changes constants.

Primary boundary results:
* Lu, Sun, Wang, Luo, Oh, “Broadcasting Quantum Fisher Information,”
  arXiv:1206.2821v3, HTML lines 56–62: at a fixed parameter value QFI can be
  broadcast by broadcasting outcomes of an optimal measurement that may
  depend on that parameter. Their Theorem 2 says infinite broadcasting of a
  whole one-parameter family's QFI is equivalent to a single measurement
  optimal throughout the family. HTML lines 94–100 gives a finite-broadcast
  nonuniform-QFI example. This marks the common-measurement issue; it is not a
  weighted multi-parameter deficit theorem for the present channel interface.
* Lemm–Wilde, “Information-theoretic limitations on approximate quantum
  cloning and broadcasting,” arXiv:1608.07569v2, Theorem 4, HTML lines 86–97:
  the same channel approximately broadcasts one fixed mixed state and
  approximately clones a second to a product output, then relative-entropy
  loss is at least (n-1) times the retained relative entropy and at least
  (n-1)||tilde_sigma_1-tilde_sigma_2||_1^2/2. A supplied B only promises
  equal marginals; it does not promise product output on a second state.
  Moreover, the theorem is for a fixed state pair and does not directly
  compare k(Q) with the channel's Dirichlet loss. It cannot be transferred
  without those missing conversions.

Supplied information/cost: the model is constructed from the queried finite
frame Q and the supplied Phi/B; there is no acquired sample or chosen seed
ensemble. The new lemma must be uniform in d and m, and its proof must preserve
the I/d barycenter and the single-Psi-after-separation quantifiers.

2. COMMUTING DILATIONS / HS ROUNDING: STRONG EXISTING RESULTS, WRONG SCALE OR
   MISSING INPUT

Helton, Klep, McCullough, Schweighofer, “Dilations, Linear Matrix Inequalities,
the Matrix Cube Problem and Beta Distributions,” arXiv:1412.1481v4 (2016),
Theorem 1.1, HTML lines 145–153: for matrix size d there is a fixed Hilbert
space, a fixed commuting family C_d of self-adjoint contractions, and an
isometry V such that every d-by-d symmetric contraction X has some T_X in
C_d with X/theta(d)=V*T_XV; theta(d) is optimal. Proposition 13.3(3), HTML
lines 2584–2611, proves theta(d)/sqrt(d) -> sqrt(pi)/2. Thus the common
commutative dilation has an unavoidable sqrt(d) scale for this full operator
norm cube problem. The chosen T_X are per-X witnesses, not a demonstrated
linear, unital, CP measure-and-prepare map. The result has no relative-loss
term and does not encode the fixed tracial Choi marginals. It limits a generic
operator-norm commuting-dilation route; it does not refute the target.

Bansil–Kachkovskiy, “On almost commuting matrices with respect to the
normalized Hilbert–Schmidt norm,” arXiv:2608.31000v1 (2026), Theorem 1.1,
HTML lines 65–70: for self-adjoint contractions X,Y, commuting contractions
X',Y' exist with [X',X]=0 and

  ||X-X'||_(2,d)^2 + ||Y-Y'||_(2,d)^2 <= 24 ||[X,Y]||_(2,d)^(2/3).

Theorem 1.2, lines 72–87, makes this exponent order-sharp under the theorem's
constraint. Theorem 1.3 gives a reference X and N further matrices with a
sqrt(N) factor; Corollary 1.4, lines 92–111, gives arbitrary fixed N with
constant C(N) and exponent 2/3^(N-1). Dimension is absent for a pair, but the
error is not linear in a small loss delta unless a separate estimate gives
commutator O(delta^(3/2)); for a growing full frame, the stated N-dependence
also deteriorates. The broadcaster premise supplies joint measurability, not
the required small normalized-HS commutators.

Exact compatibility check: for a,b in {+1,-1},
G_ab=(I+(a sigma_x+b sigma_z)/sqrt(2))/4 is PSD and sums to I. Its marginals
are E^x_a=(I+a sigma_x/sqrt(2))/2 and E^z_b=(I+b sigma_z/sqrt(2))/2, so the
binary POVMs are jointly measurable; nevertheless
[E^x_+,E^z_+]=-i sigma_y/4. Compatibility alone therefore gives no small
commutator. Any use of the HS theorem needs a new estimate derived from the
full legal B constraints, followed by a proof that the commuting approximation
induces one normalized EB map and preserves the linear deficit scale.

3. JOINT MEASURABILITY DOES NOT ITSELF SUPPLY EB PREPARATION DATA

Heinosaari, Kiukas, Reitzner, Schultz, “Incompatibility breaking quantum
channels,” arXiv:1504.05768v2 (2015), Proposition 8, HTML lines 287–299,
constructs a common parent for any n noisy observables after white-noise
depolarization with t <= (n+d)/(n(d+1)); the threshold depends on family size
n and dimension d. Section 4.2, lines 300–311, exhibits 2-IBC but not 3-IBC
qubit channels. Proposition 7, lines 253–260, relates all finite levels to
compatibility of the whole observable family. Theorem 2, lines 404–442,
proves EB is a proper subset of IBC and gives depolarizing examples in the
gap. In an EB channel, the response to every input POVM outcome is a Born
probability against fixed preparation states; a parent for compatible image
POVMs only supplies arbitrary postprocessing kernels. The parent does not
automatically define these fixed states. Hence even universal joint
measurability is weaker than the required EB factorization. This is a
counterexample to that *transfer*, not to the target: the target additionally
has the supplied symmetric broadcaster and a relative quadratic-form bound.

EARLY DIAGNOSTIC / FALSIFICATION BAR
For the sharp C=2 conjecture, the qubit Pauli frame Q=I and the universal
1-to-2 qubit cloner are a useful exact check: V=3, k=1 (unit Bloch vectors),
and the marginal channel shrinks Pauli matrices by 2/3, so s_Phi=2 and the
ratio (V-k)/(V-s_Phi)=2. This checks the boundary but does not prove C=2.
For any proposed finite C, a genuine refutation needs exact legal B/Phi and Q
with a rigorous bound on unrestricted k; a finite numerical fit or a failure
of a chosen seed ensemble does not suffice. For the Fisher route, the first
useful analytic check is all weighted Pauli frames under an exact qubit
broadcaster, with the same local multi-parameter POVM optimizing every
direction accounted for.
