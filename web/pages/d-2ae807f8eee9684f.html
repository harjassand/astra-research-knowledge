# Cycle 3 source continuation

The recent primary starting source remains Eilertsen–Romanovski–Schnell–Walcher, arXiv:2606.28895v2, whose July 2026 version and fixed-map generic/critical quantifiers were verified in cycle 2. This cycle pursues its older moment/reconstruction prerequisites. Nothing below is attributed as a new paper result to our investigation.

Paweł Pietrzycki and Jan Stochel, *Two-moment characterization of spectral measures on the real line*, arXiv:2103.09964v2, revised 7 June 2021. Read abstract, introduction and Theorem 1.7, with the source's operator-valued/self-adjoint hypotheses. Its result is about when semispectral operator moments imply spectral structure; it is not an empirical assurance that an unknown chemical mechanism is reversible. Our scalar variance/Taylor certificate is independently reconstructed elementary spectral calculus.

Bernard Mourrain, *Polynomial-exponential decomposition from moments*, arXiv:1609.05720v3, revised 20 October 2017. Read Section 1.1's univariate Prony/recurrence and Hankel-pencil mechanism, with introductory flat-extension context. Finite-rank support recovery and recurrence extraction are extensive established prior art. Our four-moment residual construction explicitly uses standard two-node Gaussian-quadrature ideas; no general algorithmic or novelty claim is based on renaming them.
