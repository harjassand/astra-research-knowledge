# Higher-power modifications with polynomial cylinders

Status: new deduction from the fully read September23 and October5 source mechanisms. Complete universal derivation below; independently gated adoption PENDING. This is a parameterized construction and a reusable extraction lemma, not a claim that the parameters distinguish isomorphism classes or a historical priority claim.

## Exact interface

For integers q≥2 and odd m≥3, define

  P=C[p,s,u,F,J], X=s²+u^m+p^qF,
  H=X²F−(1+2sX)J−p^qJ²−p^(q−1)u,
  A_(q,m)=P/(H).                                           (M1)

The intended output is a finitely generated smooth complex domain of dimension four whose one-variable cylinder has acquired polynomial coordinates, while A_(q,m) itself is not C^[4]. The source September23 manuscript uses only q=2,m=3. Its transfer calculation and its LND argument combine technically at all q≥2 and odd m≥3. None of the universal claims below are inferred from a parameter sweep.

## 1. Basic presentation and fibres

Set Y=s+X(X−u^m), Z=sX+p^qJ. Literal expansion gives

  XY−Z(Z+1)=p^q(H+p^(q−1)u).                                (M2)

After inverting p, (p,X,Y,Z,u) are coordinates on P:

  s=Y−X(X−u^m), F=(X−s²−u^m)/p^q, J=(Z−sX)/p^q.

On H=0, u=[XY−Z(Z+1)]/p^(2q−1), so

  A_(q,m)[p^-1]=C[p±1,X,Y,Z].                              (M3)

H modulo p is a nonzero linear form in F,J. Thus H is not divisible by p; the irreducibility after p-localization implies H is prime in the UFD P. A is a domain. The same argument applies to Htop=H+p^(q−1)u.

Let X0=s²+u^m and use the determinant-one substitution

  L=X0²F−(1+2sX0)J, N=(1−2sX0)F+4s²J,
  F=4s²L+(1+2sX0)N, J=−(1−2sX0)L+X0²N.                   (M4)

At p=0 the equation is L=0, hence A/(p)=C[s,u,N]. At every p=λ≠0, (M3) gives A/(p−λ)=C[X,Y,Z]. The morphism p:Spec A→A^1 is a smooth A^3-fibration: A is torsion-free and hence flat over the PID C[p], is finitely presented, and all geometric fibres are smooth affine spaces. Directly, at p=0 the F,J coefficient pair is unimodular, and away from p=0 the localization is polynomial. No local-triviality theorem is used here.

## 2. Acquired stabilization at every pair of parameters

On P[p^-1], Δ=−p^q∂u fixes p,X,Y,Z. It restricts to P:

  Δu=−p^q, Δs=−m p^q X u^(m−1),
  ΔF=m(2sX+1)u^(m−1), ΔJ=m X²u^(m−1).

It is locally nilpotent and (M2) gives ΔH=p^(2q−1). Its finite polynomial flow is acquired without a division. For invariant time t, let

  a=u−p^q t,
  B=−t Σ_(j=0)^(m−1) a^(m−1−j)u^j.

The difference-of-powers identity gives p^q B=a^m−u^m. Thus exp(tΔ) is

  u'=a, s'=s+p^qXB,
  F'=F−(2sX+1)B−p^qX²B²,
  J'=J−X²B.                                                (M5)

Replacing t by −t is its inverse. It sends H to H+p^(2q−1)t, and hence identifies A[w] with

  T=P[w]/(H+p^(2q−1)w).

Use (M4) and put

  Q(L)=2X0F(L)²−2sF(L)J(L)−J(L)²,
  C0=Q(0), Q1(L)=[Q(L)−C0]/L.

Q is quadratic in L, so Q1 is a polynomial, including at L=0. The relation becomes

  H=L−p^(q−1)u+p^qQ(L)+p^(2q)F(L)³.                       (M6)

Define

  L*=p^(q−1)u−p^qC0,
  h=u−pQ(L)−p^(q+1)F(L)³−p^q w,
  e0=−w−pF(L)³−Q1(L)h.

The exact universal transfer identity is

  p^(2q−1)e0−(L−L*)
      =(p^qQ1(L)−1)(H+p^(2q−1)w).                        (M7)

This identity can be checked with independent symbols p,t0 and substitutions p^(q−1)=t0, p^q=pt0, p^(2q−1)=pt0²; it does not require a finite q enumeration.

Consequently e=e0 satisfies L=L*+p^(2q−1)e on T. Substitution gives the polynomial inverse, because

  H(L*)/p^(2q−1)
      =(u−pC0)Q1(L*)+pF(L*)³.                             (M8)

For L=L*+p^(2q−1)e, the extra difference H(L)−H(L*) is also divisible by p^(2q−1) as a polynomial. Thus w=−H(L)/p^(2q−1) is a polynomial in p,s,u,N,e, not a rational chart. Explicitly, if Q=q2L²+q1L+C0, a=4s² and F*=F(L*), then

  w=−e−(u−pC0)Q1(L*)−pF*³
     −p^q e[q1+q2(L+L*)]
     −p^(2q)a e[F(L)²+F(L)F*+F*²].                        (M9)

Equation (M7) then recovers e from that polynomial w. T is a domain (it is isomorphic to A[w]), so no p-torsion can hide a further relation. Algebraic independence also follows from its transcendence degree five. Hence

  A_(q,m)[w]=C[p,s_minus,u_minus,N_minus,e0_minus]≅C^[5],    (M10)

where “minus” means applying exp(−wΔ) to the respective expression. The coordinate p is unchanged.

This is a finite constructive interface: forward and inverse maps are given by (M4),(M5),(M7),(M9), with no p-division. The difference-of-powers sum can be acquired in O(m) elementary operations, or O(log m) circuit operations by the recurrences D_(2n)=D_n(a,u)(a^n+u^n), D_(2n+1)=aD_(2n)+u^(2n). Repeated-squaring acquisition of p^q uses O(log q) operations.

The explicit degree ledger charges what the small circuit can output. For the six forward inputs of degree one, set d=max(m,q+1), h=m(q+1). Conservative output degree bounds are

  (p,s_minus,u_minus,N_minus,e_minus):
  (1,d+h,q+1,5(d+h)−q,18(d+h)−3q+1).

For the five inverse inputs of degree one, set

  E=max(q+4m+2,2q),
  W=max(3q+12m+13, q+E+2m+3, 2q+2E+7),
  Xdeg=q+E+2, Bdeg=mW+(m−1)q.

Bounds in source order (p,s,u,F,J,w) are

  (1,q+Xdeg+Bdeg,q+W,q+2Xdeg+2Bdeg,2Xdeg+Bdeg,W).

They follow successively from (M4),(M9),(M5); the highest F term is p^qX²B². In particular the inverse bound is O((q+m)^2), while the forward bound is O(mq+m+q). The nodewise coefficient one-norm bound used for the fixed stable chart also applies. A coarse universal coefficient bound suffices: with s arithmetic nodes, the maximum log2(max(1,C)) increases by at most L→2L+1 at each node, starting from bounded integer constants. Thus it is O(2^s). Here s=O(log q+log m), with absolute constants, so this is polynomial in q+m. Combined with the displayed degree bounds and the fixed-chart denominator/numerator ledger, rational output/intermediate height is polynomial in q+m and input height b. Exact evaluation charges ordinary multiplication/gcd costs on those integers. Small binary-parameter circuit size is not a unit-cost computation claim.

## 3. The common graded modification

Give (p,s,u,F,J) weights (−1,0,0,q,q). The top relation is Htop=H+p^(q−1)u of degree q. The quotient filtration is separated: F_-r A⊂p^rA; localizing at the prime (p), which is a smooth height-one point, bounds the p-order of every nonzero element. Since Htop is prime as above,

  gr A=P/(Htop)
      =G_q=B[τ,I/τ^q],                                    (M11)

where B=C[X,Y,Z,u]/(XY−Z(Z+1)),

  sB=Y−X(X−u^m),
  I=(f0=X−sB²−u^m, g0=Z−sB X), deg τ=−1, deg B=0.

The mutually inverse substitutions are p→τ, s→sB, F→f0/τ^q, J→g0/τ^q and (M2). The graded ring is smooth: away from τ=0 it is the smooth quadric times the τ-torus and u-line; at τ=0 its F,J partial derivatives are X0² and −(1+2sX0), a unimodular pair. This also shows grA/(τ)=C[s,u,N].

Pull back the line bundle C=C[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac−bd−1)→B with X=ab,Y=dc,Z=db. Set

  v=a²(X−u^m)−d²=a³b−a²u^m−d².

For h0=X−u^m, define

  C1=c²−b²h0, α=a²(1−2bd),
  β=d²(3+2bd)+αh0.

The exact identities modulo ac−bd−1 are

  f0=C1v, g0=b²v, v=αf0+βg0.

Thus IC=(v), and the pullback bundle ring is

  Gtilde_q=C[τ,V]/(τ^qV−v),                                (M12)

with modification degree deg τ=−1, deg V=q, deg C=0. Its separate fibre weights are (a,d,b,c,u,τ,V)=(1,1,−1,−1,0,0,2).

## 4. Reusable higher-power extraction lemma

Suppose G_q has a nonzero homogeneous LND D0 killing a nonzero positive-degree element. This is exactly what a polynomial presentation A≅C^[4] would force: some polynomial coordinate has positive filtration degree because F has degree q>0; differentiation in a different coordinate kills it and induces a nonzero homogeneous LND by the finite generator-shift argument in NONCOORDINATE_RECONSTRUCTION.md. Here D0 is NOT assumed to fix τ.

Every Ga-action lifts uniquely after normalization to a line bundle over a regular affine integral base. To make the prerequisite explicit: Pic(R)→Pic(R[T]) is an isomorphism for regular noetherian R. Indeed, localize a divisor in Frac(R)[T], a PID; subtract a principal divisor to remove its horizontal part. Every remaining height-one prime is q[T] for a height-one prime q of R. Injectivity follows because a rational function with vertical divisor only is in Frac(R)^*. Regularity identifies Cartier and Weil divisor classes. Normalize the isomorphism of the two pulled-back line bundles at T=0; its group-law discrepancy is a unit of R[T,S] and therefore constant in T,S and one. This is the same normalization mechanism reconstructed in the special October5 audit.

Apply this to Spec G_q and the pullback bundle (M12). Uniqueness also preserves D0's grading homogeneity: conjugate the action by the degree scaling and rescale the time; the normalized lifts coincide. The lifted LND δ is nonzero, homogeneous of some modification degree j, and of fibre degree zero.

The normal homogeneous pieces of (M12) are

  (Gtilde_q)_r = τ^a V^b C,
  r=qb−a, 0≤a≤q−1, b≥0,                                (M13)

with the obvious pure τ powers for negative degrees. This is an equality of modules (not an assertion that the displayed summands are free over an unrelated polynomial ring). Every positive-degree piece has a factor V. Factorial closure of the LND kernel therefore gives δV=0.

If δτ=0, write δr=τ^-jE(r) on C in the localization. E is a nonzero LND of fibre degree zero and E(v)=0.

If δτ≠0, an LND cannot have δτ divisible by τ: its additive order ν would imply ν(δτ)=ν(τ)−1≥ν(τ), a contradiction. Thus (M13) forces

  j−1=qℓ for an integer ℓ≥0,
  δr=τ^(q−1)V^(ℓ+1)E(r), r∈C.                            (M14)

The same factor for every r makes E a derivation of C. Its fibre degree is e=−2(ℓ+1)<0. Put k=ν(τ)≥1. Since V is invariant,

  ν(E(r))=ν(r)−1−(q−1)k

whenever E(r)≠0. Thus E is locally nilpotent. Since v=τ^qV, the corresponding equation gives ν(Ev)=k−1. If E²v≠0 its order would be

  k−2−(q−1)k=(2−q)k−2<0,

which is impossible. E is nonzero, since otherwise δv=0 would imply δτ=0. Both cases therefore force

  a nonzero homogeneous-fibre LND E of degree e≤0 with E²v=0. (M15)

The important technical combination is that the second-order conclusion is independent of the exponent q≥2. Replacing τ²V−v by τ^qV−v does not require an E^q rigidity theorem.

## 5. Uniform odd-power rigidity

Give C the auxiliary degrees (a,d,b,c,u)=(0,m,−m,0,2). The top part of v is −N, where N=d²+a²u^m has degree 2m and the remaining term a³b has degree −m. The top nonzero homogeneous part E' of E is a nonzero LND, has the same fibre degree e≤0, and satisfies (E')²N=0.

Over K=Frac C=C(a,d,b,u), exponentiate E' with time T. Let a(T),d(T),u(T) be the resulting polynomials. Their squared expression equals N+T E'N, a nonzero polynomial of degree at most one.

We use the polynomial abc estimate, with its proof charged. For pairwise coprime nonzero A+B=C in characteristic-zero K[T], not all constant, W=A'B−AB' is nonzero. For every root multiplicity r of A,B or C, its factor contributes at least r−1 to W; for C use W=A'C−AC'. Thus ABC/rad(ABC) divides W. Comparing degrees for all three equivalent Wronskians gives max(deg A,deg B,deg C)≤deg rad(ABC)−1.

Take A=d(T)², B=a(T)²u(T)^m and C=N+T E'N. A common root of A,B would have multiplicity at least two in C, impossible. Thus the estimate applies if any orbit polynomial is nonconstant. Their leading terms must cancel, giving

  M=2deg d=2deg a+m deg u,
  M≤deg d+deg a+deg u.

Consequently (m−2)deg u≤0, so deg u=0 for m≥3. Then deg d=deg a>0, and leading cancellation makes −u^m a square in K. This is impossible for odd m: the u-adic valuation of −u^m in C(a,d,b)(u) is odd. Therefore E' fixes a,d,u.

As in the October5 audit, set Q=cE'(b)−bE'(c). The determinant relation gives E'(b)=aQ,E'(c)=dQ. The LND on K0[b], K0=C(a,d,u), forces Q∈K0. The two a/d charts give C∩K0=C[a,d,u]. But Q has fibre weight e−2<0, while this polynomial ring has only nonnegative weights. Q=0 contradicts E'≠0.

This excludes (M15), and hence excludes A_(q,m)≅C^[4].

## Scope, prior and next gate

The q=2,m=3 construction and all basic ingredients are source-author prior, fully read in September23 Sections2–6. The all-q extraction and odd-m combination above are our deductions; wider priority is UNKNOWN. We make no assertion that distinct (q,m) give nonisomorphic A, no Makar-Limanov-invariant calculation, and no affine-cancellation claim in dimension three.

The q=1 case does not pass the additive-order descent: (M14) loses its τ factor. Even m does not pass the nonsquare valuation step. Neither boundary is an impossibility or a positive result about its corresponding rings.

Concrete use: this supplies acquired polynomial-cylinder examples with controllable input degree 2max(m,q+1)+1, a smooth affine-space-fibre map and a common proof certificate. The lemma (M13)–(M15) can test other affine modifications by an order-two orbit condition instead of a growing order q. A further allocation could vary the principal generator v while retaining its nonpositive-weight rigidity, rather than redo bounded coordinate searches.

Next falsifiable step: independently audit (M7)–(M9), the bundle homogeneity/normal form (M13), and the q-independent order descent (M14)–(M15). A finite parameter replay cannot substitute for these three gates. Only after that gate should root adopt the parameterized nonpolynomial family.
