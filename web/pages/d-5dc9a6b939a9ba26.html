## Determinant twist and residual tensor realization

Writing m=lambda_d and mu=lambda-m(1,...,1), the residual partition has K=N-dm boxes. Tensoring its polynomial representation by det^m raises every highest-weight coordinate by m. Its U(d) equivalence can be chosen unitary. The blind proof supplies the complex extension explicitly: differentiate the unitary intertwining identity, extend complex-linearly on the Lie algebra, exponentiate positive Hermitian matrices, and use polar decomposition for every invertible complex matrix. This is sufficient for its Cauchy circles, where ||zX||<=1/2 makes I+zX invertible.

For a general singular matrix, polynomial continuity extends the identity from the dense invertible set. det^0 is one; a positive determinant exponent makes the action zero if the determinant is zero. The originator writes this case directly. There is no use of a normal-matrix eigenvalue estimate for a nonnormal matrix. The residual representation has an invariant isometric copy in K tensor factors, so its norm is <=||A||^K; the unitary intertwiner does not alter that bound.

## The one-body identity is a density identity

For every Hermitian H, the derivative of the determinant twist is m Tr(H), while the residual generator is the sum of K copies of H. Transporting a sector density matrix into its isometric tensor realization gives a positive normalized state; averaging its K one-site marginals gives a genuine d-dimensional density matrix gamma_lambda. Thus

    N tau_lambda = m I + K gamma_lambda.

This is an equality for all Hermitian H and therefore an exact density-matrix equality, not only a traceless mean bound. K=0 is harmless because gamma is multiplied by zero. Averaging gives tau=(1-EK/N)I/d+(EK/N)gamma for some density matrix gamma.

The originator bounds EK/sqrt(N) by sqrt(A exp(B+C)); the blind proof obtains the stronger simple Jensen bound log(A)+B+C. Their respective finite N thresholds imply EK/N<=1/2, so tau>=I/(2d). The originator additionally bounds its normalized traceless Bloch length by one half. All constants are independent of N for fixed d,A,B,C.

## Noncommuting weighted Parseval and normalization

The inverse conditioning factor is 2^w on support size w.

The blind proof instead chooses a complete operator basis A_a orthonormal for Tr(tau A^dagger B), with A_0=I. It need not be Hermitian.
