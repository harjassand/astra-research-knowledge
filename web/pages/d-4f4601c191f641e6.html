FULL DERIVATION

A. Actual channel and actual broadcaster.

The canonical grouping V:Sym^(2s)(C^d) -> H_s tensor H_s is an isometry:
a globally symmetric tensor is separately symmetric on its two halves.
The partial traces of V X V* are both T_Ns(X) on every input X. Schur
irreducibility, or the occupation-basis formula below, gives
 T_Ns(I_N/D_N)=I_s/D_s.
The trace adjoint T_Ns* is P_N(X tensor I)P_N and sends I_s to I_N.
Consequently R_sN=(D_s/D_N) T_Ns* is CP, TP, and sends I_s/D_s to I_N/D_N.
The full-domain CPTP map

 B(X)=(R_sN tensor R_sN)(V X V*)                           (3)

has both physical marginals Phi_Ns. It is not a promised-only channel.
On L2(tau_N), Phi_Ns=(D_s/D_N) T_Ns* T_Ns is positive selfadjoint.
Reference preservation makes it bistochastic and a positive contraction.

Explicit finite V in normalized occupation bases, n=k+l:
 V_(k,l),n = sqrt[ product_i binomial(n_i,k_i) / binomial(N,s) ].
Its isometry identity is the multivariate Vandermonde identity. This also
specifies the acquired finite matrices without an oracle.

B. EB channel and the simultaneous spectral decomposition.

Normalized Haar measure dz on pure z in C^d satisfies
 integral |z^N><z^N| dz = I_N/D_N.
Hence the displayed Psi_N is measure/prepare, UCP, TP and reference preserving.
The quadratic form tau_N(A* Psi_N A)=integral |<z^N|A|z^N>|^2 dz is nonnegative;
Psi_N is selfadjoint and 0<=Psi_N<=I by scalar Schwarz.

For clarity the representation step used below is stated precisely. The
coherent symbol map A -> <z^N|A|z^N> identifies End(H_N) with bihomogeneous
polynomials of bidegree (N,N) restricted to the unit sphere. Occupation
monomials give injectivity: a vanishing symbol has every coefficient zero.
Fischer decomposition by the complex Laplacian splits this space into the
harmonic bidegrees (ell,ell), 0<=ell<=N. These are the multiplicity-one U(d)
modules with highest weight (ell,0,...,0,-ell); each is generated by
(bar z_1 z_d)^ell and has dimension
 m_(d,ell)=binomial(ell+d-1,d-1)^2-binomial(ell+d-2,d-1)^2,
where the second term is zero for ell=0. The dimensions telescope to D_N^2.
The highest-weight generator statement also follows directly by solving the
raising-operator differential equations on harmonic polynomials; lowering
operators generate its orbit module. This is the usual polynomial harmonic
representation, not a numerical diagonalization premise. Covariant maps
therefore act by scalars on these orthogonal modules.

Put (n)_ell=n(n-1)...(n-ell+1), and for ell<=N choose
 O_(N,ell)=(a_1^dagger)^ell (a_d)^ell on H_N.
Its coherent symbol is (N)_ell (bar z_1 z_d)^ell. It spans the highest-weight
line (the choice of raising convention only exchanges first/last coordinates).
The Haar moment is elementary Dirichlet integration:

 integral product_i |z_i|^(2r_i) dz
   = (d-1)! product_i r_i! / (sum_i r_i+d-1)!.

Evaluate the Psi_N O matrix element between occupations
 (ell,0,...,0,N-ell) and (0,...,0,N). The prefactors are
 sqrt[binomial(N,ell)] and sqrt[ell!(N)_ell]. Dividing gives

 mu_(N,ell)
   = (N)_ell (N+d-1)!/(N+d+ell-1)!
   = product_(j=0)^(ell-1) (N-j)/(N+d+j).                  (4)

Thus Psi_N acts as mu_(N,ell) on the complete ell module, including mu_(N,0)=1.
No large-N asymptotic is used.

For the partial trace, coherent vectors give exactly

 T_Ns* O_(s,ell) = [(s)_ell/(N)_ell] O_(N,ell), ell<=s.     (5)

Indeed both sides have the same coherent symbol, and the coherent symbol
map is injective. For ell>s the smaller operator space has no such module.
Let Q_Ns=(D_s/D_N)T_Ns map likelihood operators from H_N to H_s. Its adjoint
for the two normalized trace inner products is exactly T_Ns*. Since

 integral |<z^N|O_(N,ell)|z^N>|^2 dz
   = mu_(N,ell) tau_N(O_(N,ell)* O_(N,ell)),

the norm equals (N)_ell^2 times a common ell,d Haar moment divided by mu_N.
Combining this norm formula with (5) yields the singular square of Q_Ns:

 lambda_(N,s,ell) = mu_(s,ell)/mu_(N,ell), ell<=s,
 lambda_(N,s,ell) = 0, ell>s.                              (6)

These are precisely the eigenvalues of Phi_Ns=T_Ns* Q_Ns.

C. Exact scalar deficit comparison.

For N=2s and j=0,...,s-1 set
 a_j=(2s-j)/(2s+d+j),
 l_j=[(s-j)/(s+d+j)]/a_j.
All lie in [0,1], and on ell<=s,
 mu_(N,ell)=product_(j<ell) a_j,
 lambda_(N,s,ell)=product_(j<ell) l_j.
Direct rational subtraction gives

 (1-a_j)/(1-l_j)
   =(2s-j)(s+d+j)/[s(2s+d+j)] <= c_(s,d),                 (7)

because the difference from its j=0 value c_(s,d) is

 j[d^2+d j+3d s+2j s] / [s(d+2s)(d+j+2s)] >=0.

To pass from factors to products WITHOUT assuming a_j<=l_j, let
 A=1-1/c, B=1/c. The factor inequality gives l_j<=A+B a_j.
For u,v in [0,1],

 A+Buv-(A+Bu)(A+Bv)=AB(1-u)(1-v)>=0.

Induction yields product l_j <= A+B product a_j, exactly
 1-mu_(N,ell)<=c(1-lambda_(N,s,ell)).
For ell>s, lambda=0 and 1-mu<=1<c. ell=0 has zero deficits.
The complete orthogonal decomposition proves (1), with no missing directions.

D. Optimality among EB maps.

Take traceless Hermitian t_a on C^d with Tr(t_a t_b)=delta_ab,
a=1,...,d^2-1, and J_a=sum_(i=1)^N t_a^(i) restricted to H_N.
The matrix completeness identities are
 sum_a t_a tensor t_a = Swap-I/d,
 sum_a t_a^2=(d^2-1)I/d.
Every swap is identity on H_N, hence

 sum_a J_a^2 = N(d-1)(N+d) I_N/d,
 V=sum_a tau_N(J_a^2)=N(d-1)(N+d)/d.                       (8)

For every state eta on H_N with one-particle marginal eta_1,
 sum_a [Tr(eta J_a)]^2=N^2[Tr(eta_1^2)-1/d]
   <=N^2(d-1)/d.                                         (9)

Write ANY physical EB endomorphism E as sum_y Tr(M_y X) sigma_y.
Put p_y=Tr(M_y)/D_N, eta_y=M_y/Tr(M_y). Then

 sum_a tau_N(J_a E(J_a))
   =sum_y p_y sum_a Tr(eta_y J_a)Tr(sigma_y J_a)
   <=N^2(d-1)/d = [N/(N+d)] V,                            (10)

by (9) and scalar Cauchy--Schwarz; sum p_y=1. E need not be unital,
selfadjoint or canonical for this bound. All J_a belong to ell=1 and
Phi_Ns(J_a)=lambda_(N,s,1)J_a. Testing I-E<=c(I-Phi_Ns) on this whole frame
therefore forces

 c >= (1-N/(N+d))/(1-lambda_(N,s,1))
   =2(s+d)/(2s+d).                                        (11)

Psi_N attains it. This proves the stated optimality. The tests are Hermitian
quadratic forms; their sum suffices even if an arbitrary E is nonselfadjoint.
