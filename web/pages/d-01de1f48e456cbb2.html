## Provisional mathematical/interface observations

- `ReactionDiagram` is a finite labelled edge list with arbitrary real source and target vectors. Repeated edges remain distinct. `EssentialEdge` takes the maximum source level only among edges with nonzero projected reaction, and keeps all tied edges by universal quantification. `AllEndotactic` imposes the negative sign on every such essential edge, matching the manuscript's weak all-ties essential-source definition (`endotactic_permanence.tex:66–86`).
- `exists_minimum_reacting_edge` chooses a minimum among all edges with nonzero projection. The sign flip to direction `-r` proves the chosen edge is essential and hence has positive `r·ν`; `negative_edge_source_above_minimum` proves every negative-projection edge is strictly above the minimum tier. No strong-endotactic or global-maximum condition appeared in the draft.
- The pointwise rate vector `k : Fin m → ℝ` is arbitrary subject to `κ ≤ k e ≤ K`, with `κ>0` and `κ≤K`. The drift inequality is pointwise in rates and therefore does not need a measurability assumption in this algebraic lemma; measurable rate paths can be instantiated pointwise downstream. It does not itself connect to a time-dependent ODE.
- The draft's `StoichPerp` is exactly the annihilator condition `∀ e, r·ν_e=0`. It separates perpendicular equality/nonnegativity from the nonperpendicular strict lower bound.
- The listed comparison vectors are coordinate vectors, every labelled reaction vector, and all ordered differences of edge-source vectors. The TeX list additionally uses all differences of vertices in `C`; the edge-source list is enough for source-order comparisons used by this proof. It includes duplicates/zero vectors harmlessly, which only add zero or conservative terms.
- The draft's positive point tolerance is `1/(2 + sum_z 2||v_z||₁/|r·v_z|)` over nonzero comparisons, rather than the manuscript's finite minimum. Its defining sum makes it no larger than the half-margin for each represented comparison. A potential affine family can be obtained by composing the imported global affine theorem with this positive tolerance; `exists_activity_margin` itself is conditional on `F` and `hFull`, so the wrapper is derivable but not exported in this file.
- The fixed-family margin `H` is another positive conservative margin (`1/(2 + sum_{label,comparison} 2/|r·v|)` over nonzero products). It is proved at most `|r·v|/2` for every represented comparison. The sum budgets `L=Σ||ν||₁` and `M=Σ||source||₁` replace the manuscript's `L` and maximum source norm. The summed `M` is a larger exponent than the manuscript maximum and gives a weaker but still positive lower bound for `0<h<1`.
- `monomial_source_ratio_bound` retains the scalar gap by deriving `p·(source e-source f) ≥ H` from the active-slope error bound, then uses `0<h<1` to get the required `h^H` ratio. The stated gate is exactly `K*L*h^H ≤ κ*H`; the positive edge supplies `2κH` and the negative budget subtracts at most `KLh^H`, leaving `κH` before the source monomial floor. No factor `κ`, `H`, or exponent was visibly dropped in this draft.
- The formal strict theorem is conditional on a pointwise activity margin, `h<hAct<1`, the small-scale gate, and a fixed active label. The companion perpendicular theorem gives equality zero for all coefficients. The source's general nonnegative assertion follows by cases from these two results, though no combined case-split theorem was visible in this draft.
- Real source/target exponents are represented directly by `ℝ` vectors and monomials on positive `x`, so negative exponents are included. `Real.rpow` is only evaluated at positive coordinates generated from `h>0`.

## Failed terminology challenge (resolved)

I initially suspected the manuscript's phrase “every edge with `r·nu_e < 0` is essential” (source line 491) was false because I read “essential” as meaning that the source lies on the maximal essential support. That challenge was incorrect. I checked the cited primary source, Gopalkrishnan–Miller–Shiu, *A geometric approach to the Global Attractor Conjecture*, arXiv:1305.5303v2: Definition 3.9.3 calls a reaction `w`-essential exactly when its reaction vector has nonzero `w`-projection, and defines the `w`-support separately as the maximal source set of such reactions; Definition 3.14 imposes the sign condition only on essential reactions whose source lies on that support ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/1305.5303v2)). Thus every negative-projection edge is essential in the intended sense and belongs to `R_{-r}`, though it is not necessarily sourced on the essential support. The 0↔1 reversible-pair example does not falsify the manuscript sentence. The Lean predicate `EssentialEdge` packages `InR` together with source-on-support, so it is narrower in name, but the all-endotactic predicate expresses the same mathematical condition. No source wording defect is claimed after this check.
