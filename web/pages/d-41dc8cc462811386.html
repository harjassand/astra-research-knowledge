1. PRECISE RECONSTRUCTED THEOREM

Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint. Supply rational 0<delta<=1. Let ell denote
the TOTAL Choi input bitlength, and h_delta the rational delta input
bitlength. The structural properties are promises; their exact rational
checks can also be charged polynomially if desired. Phi is computed by
partial trace. No black-box tomography, compatible dilation acquisition,
instrument oracle or spectral-gap promise is implied.

There is an exact classical Las Vegas algorithm returning at most
 T+d rank-one rational-complex effects, T=ceil(64 d^2/delta^2),
whose sum is I, and whose canonical map
 Psi(A)=sum_j M_j Tr(M_j A)/Tr(M_j)
obeys, simultaneously on the ENTIRE real Hermitian HS space,
 I-Psi <= 4(I-Phi)+delta I.
Zero effects are omitted. Each return includes exact rational PSD
certificates. The compiler terminates almost surely; expected batches <=2.
Expected bit time/random bits and deterministic returned output length are
polynomial in d,1/delta,ell,h_delta. This does not assert a worst-case bound
on the number of randomized batches or integer-rejection trials.

For rational implementation tolerance epsilon>0, the returned ONE ideal
channel can be implemented to diamond error <=epsilon with classical and
quantum per-shot resources polynomial in d,1/delta,ell,h_delta and
log(1/epsilon), under a named fixed universal gate model below. Its actual
finite gate approximation is EB but need not be exactly canonical, unital
or HS-self-adjoint. All simultaneous inequalities above refer to the ideal
exact rational map. This distinction is substantive, not a free-precision
convention. The comparator does not depend on a subsequently chosen state,
observable, frame or ensemble.

If epsilon is serialized as an arbitrary rational, its full input length
is additionally charged during gate preprocessing, even when epsilon is

2. FIXED LEAF AND FINITE ANALYTIC CERTIFICATE

for every j<k form R=dr pure projectors pi_z with sum pi_z=rI. F_z=pi_z/r
is a rational-complex rank-one POVM. On matrices its canonical channel is
 S0(A)_jj=((d-1)A_jj+Tr A)/r,
 S0(A)_jk=A_jk/r (j!=k).
Therefore it fixes I and has traceless eigenvalues (d-1)/r on diagonals and
1/r on off-diagonals. In any normalized-HS traceless basis its posterior
coordinate covariance is S0; the unbiased dual estimator has covariance
N0=S0^{-1}<=rI. The estimator is proof data, never acquired or evaluated
by the compiler.

After rational output-swap symmetrization of B, write rho_BC=B(I/d).
For selected proof-only Phi eigenmodes lambda>3/4, define
 C_ab=Tr rho_BC(E_a tensor E_b).
For every real vector x, positivity of
 (sum_a x_a E_a tensor I-I tensor sum_a x_a E_a)^2
and both tracial marginals gives x^T Cx<=||x||^2. C is symmetric by the
swap. The exact estimator recursion is
 N_m=(1/2)Lambda^{-1}(N_{m-1}+C)Lambda^{-1}.
The cross moment equals C because product SUBTREE ADJOINT first moments
are E_a tensor E_b. This uses CP composition on correlated siblings, not
conditional independence or independent clone assumptions.

Induction with N0<=rI and C<=I gives diagonal upper bound
 D_m,aa=r t_a+(1-t_a)/a_a,
 a_a=2lambda_a^2-1, t_a=(2lambda_a^2)^{-m}<=(8/9)^m.
Its inverse is >=a_a-r(8/9)^m. For the full posterior covariance S_m,
the joint covariance block [[S_m,J],[J^T,N_m]] is PSD, with J the embedding
of selected modes into ALL traceless modes. Injective J forces N_m>0;
Schur complementation gives S_m>=J D_m^{-1}J^T. Any fine-record refinement
retaining the same estimator preserves this full-space bound. Thus selected
and unselected cross terms are controlled, not merely separate scores.
Since a_a-(4lambda_a-3)=2(1-lambda_a)^2, omitted modes lambda<=3/4 have

3. RATIONAL PURE REFINEMENT AND EXACT PROBABILITY LAW
Rational pivoted LDL* of the d^3-by-d^3 Choi matrix gives positive rational
q_j and Gaussian-rational W_j with
 B(X)=sum_j q_j W_j X W_j*, sum_j q_j W_j* W_j=I,
and s<=d^3. PSD zero pivots have zero residual rows/columns, so they are
discarded exactly. Rational elimination quantities are ratios of minors;
their bit lengths are polynomial in d,ell. The square roots needed for a
physical Kraus operator never enter the CLASSICAL CP branch coefficients.

Retain every internal label j and every rank-one leaf outcome z. A full
record w has effect M_w=t_w v_w* v_w, t_w>0 rational, with rational row
 v_leaf=u_z*, v_node=(v_left tensor v_right)W_j.
Positive scalar leaf/Kraus coefficients are tracked separately. The rank
one identity follows by induction, and no environment is silently traced
out. Completeness gives sum_w M_w=I. At reference I/d the law is
 p_w=Tr M_w/d, pi_w=M_w/Tr M_w,
so E pi_w=I/d and Psi_m=d E |pi_w><pi_w| in ordinary HS convention.
Every nonzero pi_w is a rational pure projector. No eigenvector or matrix
square-root oracle is used to get it.

For ANY partial record h, unassigned leaves have effect I, assigned leaves
F_z; an unassigned internal node uses B*, an assigned node the specified
branch q_j W_j*(. )W_j. Bottom-up messages yield EXACT partial input effect
M_h and P(h)=Tr M_h/d. Assigning the next variable to each of its possible
values gives nonnegative probabilities that sum to P(h). Hence the usual
conditional chain rule produces exactly p_w, in arbitrary variable order.
Sibling states are never factored. All messages are d-by-d; a full d^K
density matrix is never stored. Even a fresh O(K)-node contraction for every
candidate at every O(K) variable is polynomial; caching is an improvement,
not a premise. Local domains are at most max(d^3,2d^2).

4. EXPLICIT BITBOUND, INCLUDING RARE PREFIXES
Precompute rational coefficients of each branch adjoint and of each leaf
effect. Clear ONE common positive denominator D across their real and
imaginary parts; use D>=2. The number of these coefficients and log D are
polynomial in d,ell. A message for a subtree with n vertices has denominator
dividing D^n: tensoring adds the child exponents and each local adjoint adds
one. Unassigned identity leaves can be padded to the same denominator.
Thus ALL partial probabilities use common Q=d D^(2K-1).

Each partial subtree effect satisfies 0<=M_h<=I: leaf effects do, and both
the unassigned unital B* and assigned subunital CP branch preserve this
bound on child tensor effects. Each real/imaginary entry has absolute value
<=1, so its integer numerator has O(K log D) bits. Trace probabilities have
integer counts 0<=c_h<=Q, bitlength O(K log D+log d). Intermediate local
sums/products have the same polynomial exponent plus polynomial local
summation overhead. This is an exact bit bound; it does not posit likely
outcomes, a numerical probability oracle, or precision independent of data.

For a prefix already selected, c_h>0. Its candidate conditional probabilities
are c_a/c_h with sum c_a=c_h. Sample a uniform integer in [0,c_h) by drawing
ceil(log2 c_h) fair bits and rejecting integers >=c_h; success probability
>1/2 (or 1 when c_h is a power of two). Cumulative integer counts select
the next value. Expected trials <=2; zero-probability values are never
selected. Rare prefixes merely have different polynomial-bit counts.
The complete root M_w and its normalization divide polynomial-bit rational
numbers, so the posterior and its rational direction have polynomial bit
length. No minimum p_w promise or posterior rejection loop is needed.
The compiler samples records, never enumerates s^(K-1)R^K possibilities.

6. FINITE-GATE IMPLEMENTATION — PRIMARY-SOURCE OBLIGATION CLOSED
Write returned rank-one effects as M_j=t_j v_j* v_j, rational t_j>0 and
rational nonzero rows v_j. The rows sqrt(t_j)v_j form an isometry V because
V*V=sum_j M_j=I. The newly exposed cost ledger supplies the particularly
clean (s+d)-dimensional unitary
 U=[[I_s-VV*,V],[V*,0_d]].
Independently multiplying its blocks gives U*=U and U^2=I: VV* is a
projection, (I-VV*)V=0, V*V=I. Its action on the bottom d-dimensional code
space is exactly V into the top s-dimensional outcome space. Pad the full
dimension to a power of two <2(s+d)<=4s. A basis permutation embeds the
input code. No ill-conditioned arbitrary basis completion is needed.
Each square root of a positive rational and each U entry is computable
to P bits in polynomial bit time; P includes rational output encoding
length plus O(log(s/epsilon)). The final ledger gives a stronger explicit
stable complex-Householder decomposition. For a norm-one remaining column
tail x, choose sigma in {1,-1} opposite the sign of Re x_1, put
 v=x-sigma e_1, c=v*x, H=I-vv*/c.
Direct multiplication gives Hx=sigma e_1 and H*H=I since ||v||^2=c+conj(c).
Re c>=1 and ||v||<=2; the normalized v first entry has modulus >=1/2.
Two-level rotations mapping v/||v|| to e_1 therefore have pair norms >=1/2,
and H is their conjugate of diag(-conj(c)/c,1,...). Approximate sign choice
still has Re c>=1/2, with all needed denominators bounded below by constants
or inverse polynomials. A loose product Lipschitz bound over O(D^3) scalar
operations gives working precision O(D^3 log(2D)+bout+log(G0/epsilon)) bits.
Thus even conservative amplification accounting is polynomial; no exact
algebraic sign oracle or minimum pivot premise is supplied.

Measure the ENTIRE instrument output register to obtain j, DISCARD all
original-input and measurement ancilla registers, and pass only classical
j to preparation of v_j*/||v_j|| on a fresh output register. This architecture
is exactly measure-and-prepare even with approximate gates, hence exactly
EB. An approximate generic Stinespring circuit for an EB map would not
provide that guarantee. Each direction is known classical data of
polynomial length; the input state is never assumed classically known.

For generic dense unitary decomposition, Barenco et al., Elementary gates
for quantum computation, quant-ph/9503016, pp.26-27, give a construction
using O(n^3 4^n) elementary operations for an n-qubit unitary. Here 2^n is
