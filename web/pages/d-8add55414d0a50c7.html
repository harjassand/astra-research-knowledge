I. FULL DERIVATION OF THE PROJECTIVE IDENTITY

Write r=|X|, e=X/r and N=e^perp, P=I-e tensor e. The quotient-normal
cocycle is Q_t=P_(phi_t x) D phi_t|N_x. It is invertible and composes
because D phi_t maps the X-line onto itself. If w=Q_t v, the moving-normal
calculation gives P nabla_X w=P nabla_w X. Consequently

 d/dt log|w| = <B w,w>/|w|^2,   B=P S P|N.                     (4)

This retains the moving-normal connection; no frozen ambient plane is
substituted. Put q=<S e,e>=X log r and b=P S e. Since div X=0,
tr B=-q. Set B_0=B+(q/2)I_N, with eigenvalues +/-s, s>=0. Direct block
algebra gives

 |S|^2=2s^2+(3/2)q^2+2|b|^2.                                  (5)

All one-step full derivative and inverse norms are bounded on compact M;
the same holds for Q_1 and Q_1^(-1). Since r is bounded above and below,
the X-line exponent is zero, and |det Q_t|=r(x)/r(phi_t x) has sublinear
logarithm. Oseledets thus gives full exponents chi,0,-chi, chi>=0, and

 lambda=integral_M chi dmu,
 lim_(T->infinity) (1/T) integral_M log||Q_T|| dmu=lambda.         (6)

The continuous-time limit follows from integer time and the uniformly
bounded derivative for the remaining time in [0,1]. Dominated convergence
uses |log||Q_T||/T| bounded for T>=1. Ruelle's Theorem2(b), applied to
phi_1 and mu, supplies H<=lambda. No Pesin equality is required.

Here is a construction of the invariant lift eta, avoiding an assumed
measurable invariant unstable line on neutral components. Let nu_0 have
base marginal mu and, in each fiber PN_x, the uniform angular measure
induced by g. Let Phi_t be the projective flow of Q_t. Define

 eta_T=(1/T) integral_0^T (Phi_t)_* nu_0 dt.

Each eta_T projects to mu. PN is compact. Any weak convergent subsequence
T_j->infinity has a probability limit eta, still with marginal mu.
For each fixed u, the total variation norm of
(Phi_u)_* eta_T-eta_T is at most 2|u|/T, so eta is Phi_u-invariant.

Let A(x,l)=<S v,v> for any unit v in l. It is continuous on PN, and (4)
gives

 integral A d eta_T=(1/T) integral_PN log|Q_T v| dnu_0.

For the two singular values alpha>=beta>0 of Q_T at a fixed x, angular
averaging gives
 log alpha-log 2 <= E_angle log|Q_T v| <=log alpha.
Indeed the lower bound follows from |Q_T v|>=alpha |cos(theta)| and
E log|cos(theta)|=-log 2. Thus (6) implies integral A d eta=lambda.
The construction is an EXISTENCE limit, with no uniform rate or acquired
projective sampler asserted.

Because q=X log r and mu is invariant, integral q dmu=0. Define the
trace-free ideal strain J_l=H(2 Pi_l-P), whose eigenvalues are 0,H,-H.
Its norm squared is 2H^2. The block decomposition gives

 <S,J_l>=H(2<S v,v>-tr B)=H(2A+q).

Integrating over eta yields integral <S,J_l>=2H lambda. Expansion of the
squared difference therefore gives

 (1/2) integral |S-J_l|^2 d eta
 = (1/2) integral |S|^2 dmu+H^2-2H lambda
 = delta-2H(lambda-H),

which proves (1). The stationary work identity W=2 nu integral |S|^2 is
obtained by pairing the force law with X; advection and pressure integrate
to zero, and integration by parts gives the positive viscous term.

If theta is the angle of l with the +s eigenline of B_0, the same identity
has pointwise integrand

 (1/2)|S-J_l|^2=(s-H)^2+4Hs sin^2(theta)+(3/4)q^2+|b|^2.          (7)

Thus the strain-amplitude and transported-line errors share ONE deficit.
For t=s/H>=0 and z=sin^2(theta) in [0,1],

if 4t>=1 subtracting z is nonnegative; if 4t<1, its minimum in z is at

III. FULL POSITIVE-VOLUME CONFIGURATION OBSTRUCTION

Let T be an invertible probability-measure-preserving transformation.
Let C_s be pairwise disjoint measurable decoding cells. Suppose a
specified computation has distinct labels s_0,...,s_N. Define
 A_N=intersection_(j=0)^N T^(-j) C_(s_j).
Then T^j A_N lies inside C_(s_j); these N+1 translates are disjoint and
each has measure mu(A_N). Their union lies in the unit-volume space, so
(N+1)mu(A_N)<=1. This proves (3). The infinite-run set is contained in
every A_N, hence has measure zero by letting N->infinity. For initial
states uniform on C_(s_0) of mass alpha, divide (3) by alpha. The bound
is sharp in the finite-horizon abstract interface: a cyclic permutation
of N+1 equal-volume cells has mu(A_N)=1/(N+1).

Complete configuration labels are essential. Outputs alone may repeat
while hidden state changes. For a physical apparatus with an environment,
the transformation, measure and cell capacity must include that
environment; ignoring it can invalidate the finite-volume hypothesis.
For continuous flows the same argument applies at a fixed clock step.
