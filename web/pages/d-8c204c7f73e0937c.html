SETUP

Let D_j=2^j+1, j=0,...,K-1, D_H=2^K+K, delta=w/K, 0<w<1.
The nonfaithful family is
rho_psi=(1-w)vac directsum delta sum_j P_(psi,2^j),
on the exact finite degree-2m algebraic design of the frozen main proof.
Let 0<eta<1, u=eta/D_H, and
rho'_psi=(1-eta)rho_psi+eta I_H/D_H.
Write
v=(1-eta)(1-w)+u,
c=(1-eta)delta+u,
q_j=(1-eta)delta/D_j+u.
The design mean sigma' has vacuum eigenvalue v and every sector-j
eigenvalue q_j. Each rho'_psi has vacuum eigenvalue v, coherent-ray
eigenvalue c in every active sector, and eigenvalue u on its remaining
D_j-1 directions. Its entropy is independent of psi.

EXACT OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS

For every K and eta in this setup,

R(E')=max_(r=0,...,K-1)
 (K-r)c ln[(K-r)c /
 ((1-eta)delta sum_(j=r)^(K-1)1/D_j + u(K-r))].       (F1)

Proof of the upper at the design mean. The two states commute in the
coherent-ray occupation decomposition, so an arbitrary effect can be
replaced by its diagonal in that basis without changing probabilities.
Its coherent-ray coefficients are f_j in[0,1]. In the vacuum the target
and center probability densities are equal. On every orthogonal active
direction target density u is <=center density q_j.

Any maximizing effect with positive divergence has p/q>1. Adding a
piece with target density a<=center density b has derivative
a[ln(p/q)+1]-b(p/q)<=b[ln(p/q)+1-p/q]<=0.
Removing such a piece preserves p>q, since it removes a nonpositive
contribution to p-q, and can only improve the value. Thus a maximum
can be taken to have zero vacuum and orthogonal-direction coefficients.
Effects of nonpositive divergence cannot maximize, because any coherent
projection has positive divergence (c>q_j).

We are left with p=c sum_j f_j, q=sum_j q_j f_j. Joint convexity of
p ln(p/q) gives a maximum at a cube vertex. For a fixed number n of
selected rays p=nc is constant, and the q_j strictly decrease with j.
Selecting the highest n flags minimizes q. This proves exactly the
right side of (F1) as the centered worst divergence.

Proof for every convex center. Every sigma' in conv(E') is
(1-eta)sigma+eta I_H/D_H for a sigma in conv(E). Every active block of
sigma has trace delta. For each tail r use the effect sum_(j>=r)P_(psi,N_j).
Its target probability is exactly (K-r)c, independent of psi. Averaging
its center probability over the same finite design gives
(1-eta)delta sum_(j>=r)1/D_j+u(K-r).
Jensen for -ln q therefore implies a worst state at least the r-th term
of (F1), for every r and every center. The mean attains the upper. Finite
families have closed convex hulls here, so no separate closure issue arises.
This proves the exact convex minimax radius, not merely one centered value.

The unperturbed formula is recovered by eta=0:
R(E)=w max_r[(K-r)/K]ln[(K-r)/(sum_(j>=r)1/D_j)].
For eta>0 the formulas are generally different. In fact joint convexity
gives R(E')<=(1-eta)R(E)<R(E), since R(E)>0, so identifying the faithful
radius literally with the unperturbed value is false.

EXACT MAX-PRIOR HOLEVO CAPACITY

Each state's fixed block eigenvalues give
S(rho')=-v ln v-K c ln c-u sum_j(D_j-1) ln u.
Every mean retains sector-j trace c+(D_j-1)u=D_j q_j and vacuum trace v.
Its entropy is maximized by the scalar block q_j I_j, simultaneously
attained by the finite design prior. Thus the max-prior capacity is

C(E')=K c ln c+u sum_j(D_j-1)ln u
                         -sum_j D_j q_j ln q_j.     (F2)

This has no vacuum term because the equal vacuum entropies cancel.
Formula(F2) is distinct from the unperturbed binomial/log-dimension sum.
The origin's bounds (1-eta)C-h2(eta)<=C(E')<=(1-eta)C are also valid:
the tagged binary mixture has Holevo information (1-eta)C, and discarding
that tag loses at most its entropy h2(eta). They are not claimed to be
equalities.

ETA=DELTA^2 AND THE TWO REGIMES

In RegimeA w=1/K, delta=K^-2, eta=K^-4. At r=floor(K/2), n=K-r,
put S_r=sum_(j>=r)1/D_j. The geometric bound S_r>=2^(-r)/2 gives

eta n/(D_H delta S_r)=delta n/(D_H S_r)
                                      <=2delta n 2^(r-K)->0.

Also c/delta=1-eta+delta/D_H->1. The r term of(F1) therefore has the
same leading n delta[r ln2+ln n+O(1)] as the unperturbed radius and tends
to ln2/4. The upper R(E')<=(1-eta)R(E), with R(E)->ln2/4, gives

R(E')->ln2/4, C(E')->ln2/2.

The capacity limit also follows immediately from the binary-tag bounds.
Hence the fully faithful RegimeA still has a uniformly bounded nonzero
convex radius, in addition to the origin's stated error orders.

In RegimeB w=2^-K, delta=w/K, eta=delta^2, the same central-tail argument
gives R(E')=Theta(K2^-K) and R(E')/R(E)->1. The tagged-capacity entropy
loss h2(delta^2)=o(K2^-K) gives C(E')/C(E)->1. Both quantities vanish.
