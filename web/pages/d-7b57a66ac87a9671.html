1. Exact states and legal generator
----------------------------------

Thus rho has eigenvalues 729/730,1/730 and is faithful; sigma is faithful.

KMS transform H=Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2); H is HS selfadjoint because V and B
are Hermitian. This explicitly retains the Hamiltonian correction Q/2.
