ENTROPY / SUSTAINING POWER: INDEPENDENT AUDIT AND TWO EXTENSIONS
7 October 2026. Worker entropy_power_audit. Internal analytic results;
external correctness and historical priority remain unverified.

VERDICT
The cycle-1 inequality, its factor 4, force sign, deficit, and Sol equality
example survive this audit. I found no gap in their stated smooth, steady,
closed-manifold, normalized-volume interface. This is a sharp geometric
kinematic inequality plus the classical stationary energy identity; it does
not by itself resolve computation costs, entropy acquisition, or a fluid
regularity problem. It is not presently evidence of a field-breaking result.

Two further internally proved candidates are useful:
(A) Smooth zeros do not invalidate the inequality. With
    alpha=mu({X != 0})>0,
       ||Def X||_2^2 >= 2 V H^2 / alpha,
       W >= 4 nu V H^2 / alpha.
    If alpha=0, X=0 and H=W=0. No integrability of log|X| is assumed.
(B) For nowhere-zero X on connected M and H>0, exact equality forces a
    locally homogeneous frame: on a finite cover, |X|=r is constant and
    there are orthonormal normal fields U+,U- satisfying
       [X,U+]=-H U+,  [X,U-]=H U-,  [U+,U-]=c X
    for a constant c. Its Lie algebra is Sol for c=0 and sl(2,R) for c!=0.
    This is an exact-equality conclusion, not stability near equality.

INPUTS / EXACT INTERFACE
M is a smooth closed Riemannian 3-manifold, V=Vol_g(M), X is smooth and
divergence-free, phi_t is its autonomous particle flow, mu=dVol_g/V, and
H=h_mu(phi_1), with natural logarithms. S=Def X=(nabla X+(nabla X)^*)/2,
and |S| is the Hilbert-Schmidt tensor norm. Time-one means one specified
unit of physical time; equivalently replace H with h_mu(phi_T)/T.
The physical constitutive model audited is
    nabla_X X - 2 nu div S + grad p = F,   nu>0,
where F is force per unit mass and nu is kinematic viscosity. W=integral
g(F,X)dVol is integrated power per unit density. The power identity applies
to every sustaining F,p in this model; it requires no assumption that F is
divergence-free. Changing pressure changes F by a gradient and cannot
change W. Smooth sustaining F always exists by defining it as the residual;
an actuator, force acquisition, and a specified digital interface are absent.

PROOF AUDIT: THE LOAD-BEARING POINTS
1. Moving normal cocycle is correct. Put r=|X|, e=X/r, P=I-e tensor e.
For v=Dphi_t v0 write v=w+a e, w=Pv. Torsion-freeness gives
    nabla_X v=nabla_v X.
Projection of its decomposition yields
    P nabla_X w + a nabla_X e = P nabla_w X + a P nabla_e X.
Since nabla_X e=nabla_e X-e X(log r), and the left side is orthogonal to e,
    nabla_X e=P nabla_e X.
The a terms cancel exactly; there is no omitted moving-frame term:
    P nabla_X w=P nabla_w X.
Q_t=P_(phi_t x) Dphi_t|_(e(x)^perp) is an invertible cocycle because the
flow transports the X-line to itself. Inserting an intermediate P changes
the input by that line only, whose image is killed by the final projection.

2. Normal trace and strain algebra are correct. q=<Se,e>=e(r)=X(log r),
B=PSP restricted to the normal plane, b=PSe. Incompressibility gives
tr B=-q. Write B=B0-(q/2)I, with B0 eigenvalues +/-s. Then
    |S|^2=2s^2+(3/2)q^2+2|b|^2,
    d/dt log|w|=<Bw,w>/|w|^2 <= s-q/2.
Thus
    log||Q_t|| <= integral_0^t s(phi_tau x)d tau
                    -(1/2)log(r(phi_t x)/r(x)).
For nowhere-zero smooth X on compact M the last term is uniformly bounded.

3. Exponent and measure claims are licensed. The line has exponent zero,
because Dphi_t X(x)=X(phi_t x). The normal determinant in absolute value is
r(x)/r(phi_t x): the other diagonal block has determinant r(phi_t x)/r(x)
and the full Jacobian preserves volume. The two normal exponents sum to
zero. The standard invariant-subbundle / quotient consequence of Oseledets
gives the full spectrum chi,0,-chi, chi>=0. This applies without ergodicity.
All one-step derivative and inverse norms are bounded on the compact
manifold. The normal estimate and Birkhoff imply
    integral chi dmu <= integral s dmu.
To apply discrete Birkhoff correctly, use the bounded observable
    s1(x)=integral_0^1 s(phi_tau x)d tau,
whose integral is integral s dmu by invariance of volume under every phi_t.

4. Ruelle and Cauchy constants are correct. Ruelle's inequality gives
H <= integral chi dmu <= integral s dmu. Consequently
    H^2 <= (integral s dmu)^2 <= integral s^2 dmu
         <= (1/2) integral |S|^2 dmu.
The exact discarded quadratic terms yield
    W/(4nuV)-H^2 >= Var_mu(s)+(3/4)E_mu q^2+E_mu |b|^2.
The weaker estimate from a generic trace-free 3x3 strain is W>=3nuVH^2;
the autonomous neutral direction is what improves 3 to 4 here.

5. The full stationary power calculation has the right sign and factor.
Advection pairs to integral X(|X|^2/2)=0 and pressure pairs to
-integral p div X=0. Integration by parts gives
    integral <2 div S,X> = -2 integral <S,nabla X>
                         = -2 integral |S|^2.
Hence W=2nu||S||_2^2. No rough/Hodge Laplacian may be substituted in this
identity without its different curvature contribution. Samavaki-Tuomela's
symbol S_u is twice the S used here; their Lemma 3.4 therefore agrees,
rather than giving an extra factor 2.

6. Sol sharpness survives independently. For A=[[2,1],[1,1]], lambda=
(3+sqrt(5))/2, a=log lambda, the metric
    g=e^(2at)dx^2+e^(-2at)dy^2+dt^2
is preserved by (v,t)->(Av,t-1) in the eigenbasis of A; the transverse
lattice must be the original integer lattice expressed in that eigenbasis.
X=partial_t descends, has constant unit speed, preserves the constant
volume density, and its time-one map is (A,id) in the measurable domain
t in [0,1). It is not an ergodic time-one map; ergodicity is unnecessary.
The cylinder-area argument in cycle 1 is valid: small same-atom lift
differences satisfy delta_(k+1)=A delta_k because a nonzero lattice error
would violate the shortest-lattice-vector choice. Every n-cylinder has
area <=C_eta lambda^(-n), so Shannon entropy/n is >=a in the limit.
Ruelle with exponents a,0,-a supplies the matching upper bound. Therefore
H=a. Directly, |S|^2=2a^2, nabla_X X=0, 2 div S=-4a^2 X, and
F=4nu a^2 X,p=0 yield W=4nuVa^2. dt is a global harmonic 1-form, so Hodge
viscosity annihilates X. The two constitutive models really have different
sustaining work on precisely this same field and metric.

EXTENSION A: ZEROS AND MOVING VOLUME, COMPLETE ARGUMENT
Let Z={X=0}, U=M\Z and alpha=mu(U). Both sets are invariant. The map phi_1
is the identity on Z. For alpha>0 let mu_U=mu|U/alpha, an invariant
probability measure on the original compact manifold. Entropy satisfies
    H=alpha h_mu_U(phi_1).
This can be checked directly by adjoining the finite invariant partition
{U,Z}: its own binary entropy contributes O(1), while the n-fold partition
entropy is the weighted sum of the entropies of its restrictions. Divide
by n and take suprema; the identity restriction contributes zero.

At every Oseledets regular point x in U, the exponent of the nonzero
vector X(x) exists. It equals
    lim_n [log r(phi_n x)-log r(x)]/n.
The upper limit is <=0 because r is bounded above. Almost every x is
Poincare recurrent; along a recurrent subsequence phi_(n_j)x -> x,
r(phi_(n_j)x)->r(x)>0, so the same expression tends to 0. Its existing
limit is therefore 0. This avoids the unjustified assumption that log r is
integrable: a smooth field can vanish to infinite order, so that assumption
should not be silently added.

The normal bundle and Q are smooth on U, which need not be compact.
Nevertheless, ||Q_1|| and ||Q_1^-1|| are bounded by the corresponding
full derivative norms on M, since Q_1^-1=Q_-1 at the appropriate basepoint.
Hence its Oseledets log-integrability hypotheses hold. The bounded
observable s (defined only on U) admits Birkhoff time averages under mu_U.
The same pointwise estimate, with the endpoint log r term just proved
sublinear almost everywhere, gives chi <= time-average s. The full
spectrum remains chi,0,-chi. Apply Ruelle to mu_U to obtain
    H <= integral_U s dmu.
Now weighted Cauchy gives
    H^2 <= alpha integral_U s^2 dmu
         <= (alpha/2) integral_M |S|^2 dmu.
This proves the stated extension and then its power version. If alpha=0,
smoothness and full-support volume imply X=0 everywhere. The alpha factor
is sharp if disconnected closed manifolds are allowed: put the Sol field
on a component of volume alpha V and the zero field on the remainder.
No sharpness assertion for positive-volume zeros on connected M is made.

EXTENSION B: EXACT EQUALITY FORCES A HOMOGENEOUS FRAME
Assume now M connected, X nowhere-zero, H>0 and ||S||_2^2=2VH^2.

(i) All nonnegative losses vanish. From the deficit and continuity,
    q=0, b=0, s=H everywhere.
Thus S X=0 and B has the two smooth simple eigenline bundles E+,E- with
eigenvalues H,-H. q=0 also says X(r)=0. As an identity of vector fields,
    2 S X = nabla_X X + grad(|X|^2/2)=0.
For an advected vector v, d/dt <v,X>=2<Sv,X>=0. Therefore the actual
derivative flow, not just its quotient, preserves N=X^perp. Its normal
top exponent chi is <=H almost everywhere. Since Ruelle gives
    H <= integral chi dmu <= H,
we have chi=H almost everywhere.

(ii) Saturation makes E+ invariant. Let L_x be the measurable normal
Oseledets unstable line (exponent H). It is invariant under each phi_t
almost everywhere: commutation with phi_1 preserves the simple exponent,
and Dphi_t and its inverse have bounded norms for every fixed t. Set
    A(x)=<S v,v>/|v|^2 for 0!=v in L_x.
This is measurable, bounded by H in absolute value, and its integral is
invariant under every phi_t. The exact norm derivative on L gives
    integral [log|Dphi_n v|/|v|]/n dmu = integral A dmu.
Dominated convergence is legal because these normalized logarithms lie
in [-H,H]. Their almost-sure limit is H, hence integral A=H. Since A<=H,
A=H almost everywhere, forcing L_x=E+_x. Invariance of E+ holds everywhere
for each t by continuity and full support of mu.

(iii) The other strain line is invariant too. If v+ starts in E+ and v-
starts in its orthogonal normal line, both remain normal, and
    d/dt <Dphi_t v+,Dphi_t v-> = 2H <Dphi_t v+,Dphi_t v->.
The initial pairing is zero. Thus E- is invariant, and directly
    |Dphi_t v+|=e^(Ht)|v+|,  |Dphi_t v-|=e^(-Ht)|v-|.
Together with the X-line this is a uniform Anosov splitting. Every smooth
first integral f is constant: df annihilates E+ by taking t->-infinity in
df_x(v+)=df_(phi_t x)(Dphi_t v+), and annihilates E- by t->+infinity; it
annihilates X by invariance. These directions span TM. In particular r is
constant, so nabla_X X=0.

(iv) Orient the two eigenline bundles by passing to their joint orientation
cover (degree at most 4; take a connected component). Choose global smooth
unit sections U+,U-. Exact norm growth and invariance imply
    [X,U+]=-H U+, [X,U-]=H U-.
Write [U+,U-]=cX+dU+ +eU-. Jacobi gives [X,[U+,U-]]=0, hence
    Xc=0, Xd=Hd, Xe=-He.
On this compact cover d and e are bounded; their exponential evolution
along complete trajectories forces d=e=0. The first-integral argument
forces c constant. The metric coefficients in the frame X/r,U+,U- and
all its structure constants are constant. Its Maurer-Cartan equations
are therefore those of a left-invariant metric on the corresponding Lie
group. This proves local homogeneity: Sol if c=0; sl(2,R) if c!=0 (rescale
X,U+,U- to obtain the usual three sl2 brackets). Global identification as
a compact quotient additionally uses the classical integration of complete
constant-bracket frames, not audited here; the local-frame statement is
the fully reconstructed conclusion. This argument proves neither a
quantitative gap nor closeness of almost-minimizers to these metrics.

An independent saturating sl2 example is the geodesic field on the unit
tangent bundle of a closed curvature -1 surface with its Sasaki metric.
The standard frame (X,H0,V0) has
    [X,H0]=-V0, [X,V0]=-H0, [H0,V0]=X.
Then U+=(H0+V0)/sqrt(2), U-=(H0-V0)/sqrt(2) have c=-1 above, |S|^2=2,
and 2 div S=-4X. The stable/unstable norms grow at rates +/-1 by the
explicit Jacobi equation J''-J=0. The established smooth-volume Pesin
entropy formula gives H=1; therefore F=4nu X,p=0 sustain equality. This
extra example imports Pesin (Theorem 5.1 inspected), whose full proof was
not replayed; it is not needed for the Sol sharpness or the rigidity proof.

PRIOR ART / NOVELTY DECISION
The most relevant retrieved comparator is Brué-Colombo-Johansson,
"Lyapunov exponents, entropy and mixing for DiPerna-Lions flows," 2025,
arXiv:2510.02921v1. For 1-periodic incompressible W^(1,p) fields on T^d,
p>1, their Theorem 1.2 / 5.2 gives a low-regularity Ruelle inequality,
and Corollary 5.3 bounds volume entropy by a dimension-dependent constant
times the time-space L1 norm of the full velocity gradient. Equation 1.12
bounds the averaged top exponent by the same gradient integral. Their
time-periodic, rough, flat-torus interface differs from the steady,
smooth, sharp normal-strain statement here. The sections/theorems were
inspected; their full proof was not independently validated or imported.
This comparator makes the general entropy/velocity-gradient connection
old, and it supplies a concrete priority contact/route for the constant.

Other nearby primary works were inspected to avoid entropy conflation:
- Samavaki-Tuomela 1812.09015v2: the strain operator, energy balance,
  harmonic/strain distinction and unforced Killing conclusion are prior art.
- Hoeijmakers et al., PRE 81,066302 (2010): entropy tracking and dissipation
  in a model stirring/control problem. Abstract inspected; full text was
  authorization-protected. Its hydrodynamic entropy is not adopted as the
  KS entropy of the present particle flow.
- Manoharan-Subramanian-Joy 2412.08996 / PRE112,015106 (2025): Eulerian
  strain statistics and line-stretching entropy for statistically stationary
  2D turbulence. The derivation includes distribution/decorrelation and
  angular sampling assumptions. It is not a general steady 3D metric-
  entropy theorem and does not subsume the present sharp bound.

Searches for metric entropy with strain, symmetric gradient, enstrophy,
dissipation, Lie derivative, Sol, and sharp flow inequalities did not
locate the exact coefficient-4 statement or this equality argument. This
is a limited negative search result, not priority clearance. The basic
inequality is elementary once the normal cocycle is used; it could readily
be known under different terminology. Equality rigidity resembles classical
algebraic Anosov rigidity, which needs a dedicated comparison before any
novelty claim. My decision: CORRECT WITHIN THE AUDITED INTERFACE; NOVELTY
UNKNOWN; modest analytic/geometric significance; no field-breaking claim.

PHYSICAL CONSEQUENCES AND FAILED UPGRADES
There is a sharp sustaining-power floor at a supplied positive normalized-
volume entropy rate; exact optimizers have very special geometry. A
Sol/SL2 homogeneous example supplies a reproducible continuum benchmark.
Entropy, geometry, and actuation are additional inputs to practical use.
Saying that chaos requires dissipation is not new by itself, and this H is
not thermodynamic entropy or the entropy of the Navier-Stokes state-space
semigroup. On curved manifolds enstrophy cannot simply replace ||S||_2^2
without curvature terms. Closed Riemannian-fluid geometry is the exact
constitutive setting; Euclidean apparatus design is a separate transfer.

Universal computation can live on a null-volume coding set, while positive
volume entropy need not be computationally universal. Therefore no minimum
work per instruction follows. Under X->cX, H scales as c and sustaining
power as c^2. Any fixed-duration or throughput claim must specify its clock.
Topological entropy cannot replace volume entropy in this integral bound.
Walls add boundary work; time dependence removes the automatic neutral
direction; none of those stronger interfaces is proved here. Positive
volume entropy also does not imply mixing: the constant-roof Sol example
retains a periodic clock and its time-one map has an identity factor.
