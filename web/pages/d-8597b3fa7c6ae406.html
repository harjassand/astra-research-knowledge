# Capacitor topology projection: valid model, no advantage established

Candidate physical operation: independently thermalize every capacitor, then
connect capacitor plates into computational nodes through local switches.
This is a proposed hardware interpretation, not a claim of new mathematics,
priority, a built circuit, or a frontier technology.

## Exact law

Let C0>0 be the Maxwell capacitance matrix on expanded plate nodes, including
all grounded parasitics. Let J map computational-node potentials to plate
potentials: each row has one 1. Assembled capacitance is C=J^T C0 J.
Independent initial capacitor thermalization gives q~N(0,theta C0).
Plate merging conserves Q=J^T q. Thus Q~N(0,theta C), and the ideal final
potential V*=C^-1 Q is N(0,theta C^-1). This uses no acquired inverse.
The capacitor topology represents only capacitance-realizable (SDD M-matrix)
precisions. A dense arbitrary SPD matrix is not supplied for free.

For arbitrary conserved deterministic charge h injected after assembly,
V*=C^-1h+N(0,theta C^-1). This does not distinguish the candidate: a conventional
capacitive sampler can equilibrate, disconnect its bath, and inject h too.

## Finite resistance and finite-temperature switches

Take merger conductance G>=0, ker G=range J, and physical bath theta_b=kT.
The circuit SDE is

    C0 dz = -Gz dt + sqrt(2 theta_b G) dW.

The conserved mode is J V*. H=C0^-1/2 G C0^-1/2 has nonzero eigenvalues at
least lambda_min+(G)/lambda_max(C0), by comparing the positive eigenvalues
with those of G^1/2 C0^-1 G^1/2. This local assembly bound can be independent
of condition(C). It is NOT by itself the sampling/readout time.

At theta=theta_b, fast residual voltage r=z-JV* has stationary covariance

    theta [C0^-1 - J C^-1 J^T].

Instantaneous central-node readout therefore is not the desired assembled
sample. Readout must average fast modes or physically eliminate their
degrees of freedom. Ignoring switch Johnson noise is an invalid shortcut.

## Relative readout bound for star mergers

Each edge capacitor has two leaf plates, each linked through R to its node's
central grounded capacitor. P reads the centers, so PJ=I. C0 is a central
grounded diagonal block plus mutually independent leaf-capacitor blocks.
Let cblock bound the largest eigenvalue of any leaf block (2ce+cp for equal
edge ce and leaf-to-ground parasitic cp). For any scalar center observable a,
let v=C^-1 a and b=P^T a-C0Jv. Then J^T b=0 and

    b^T G^dagger b = R sum_leaf b_leaf^2
                     <= R cblock v^T C v
                     = R cblock a^T C^-1 a.

The identity uses the central star node as voltage reference in G^dagger;
changing this gauge does not change the quadratic form. Spectral integration
of the stationary fast covariance gives precisely this quadratic form.
Consequently, for a W-long boxcar average Y,

    Cov(Y) = theta C^-1 + E,
    0 <= E <= (2 theta R cblock/W) C^-1.

This controls covariance relative to the desired law without a condition-
number factor, but adds a 1/delta readout-window cost for covariance error
delta. It also presumes leakage negligible throughout readout and includes
readout capacitance in C0 before initialization. It excludes amplifier,
ADC, clock injection, wire inductance, and nonlinear transistor corrections.

## Strong counterbaseline

The established capacitive-precision RC sampler is

    C dV = -g V dt + sqrt(2 theta g) dW.

It already has stationary covariance theta C^-1 and slowest correlation
time lambda_max(C)/g, not 1/lambda_min(C). Ground bath switches can be opened
to hold an instantaneous sample before readout, preserving its law. Giving
this baseline the same total switch conductance as all expanded plate-reset
switches makes it faster in all replay fixtures. In the 5x5-grid fixture,
1% worst canonical lag correlation needs 8.09 normalized RC time units;
quench reset alone needs 9.26, followed by approximately 40 for a 10% worst
relative excess covariance. This is a negative finite-model comparison,
not a universal circuit lower bound. No measured power advantage exists.

## Rank-one update interpretation and limitation

If V~N(C^-1h,theta C^-1), independently initialize an incoming capacitor
charge eta~N(cy,theta c) and merge it at u=e_i or e_i-e_j. Then

    Cnew=C+c uu^T,
    Vnew=Cnew^-1(CV+u eta)
         ~N(Cnew^-1(h+cyu),theta Cnew^-1).

This is physical Matheron conditioning / Gaussian precision-weighted charge
sharing, not a new conditioning formula. It yields a correct marginal for
fixed or exogenous observations. Adaptive observations depending on previous
sample-generated actions can invalidate the required conditional prior law.
One persistent sample per hardware replica is not an independent sample
stream. Repeated positive updates consume capacity unless a lawful reset or
factor deletion is implemented. No end-to-end advantage over Gaussian
filtering, factor updates, or a matched analog sample-and-hold is established.

## Falsifiable experiment

Build 8 nodes with explicit grounded parasitics, 12 edge capacitors, switched
plate reset, star merger, and high-impedance front ends. Compare to the same
capacitance network with grounded RC bath and sample-and-hold. Match total
switch width/conductance, total capacitor area, noise temperature, ADC
precision, clock distribution and output sample tolerance. Preregister
relative covariance eigenvalue error <=10%, canonical intersample lag
correlation <=1%, and total measured joules and wall time per accepted
8-vector. At least 100000 cycles distinguish covariance from mean artifacts.
The current model predicts the quench architecture will lose; a measured
gain would require identifying a physical cost omitted from this comparison.
