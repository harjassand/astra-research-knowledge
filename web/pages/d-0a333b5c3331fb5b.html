1. INDEPENDENT LOGDET COEFFICIENT CHECK

For a traceless Hermitian axis T and density rho, write x=Tr(rho T),
V={T,rho}/2-x rho and R=-i[T,rho] (the c04_s01 normalization).
The local identities are V logdet rho=-d x and R logdet rho=0. The Hessian
is Hess(logdet)[U,W]=-Tr(rho^-1 U rho^-1 W). Directly, with
P=rho^-1 T rho and Q=T,

 rho^-1 V=(P+Q)/2-xI,       rho^-1 R=-i(P-Q).

Using Tr(P+Q)=0, Tr(P^2)=Tr(Q^2)=Tr(T^2), and cancellation of the mixed
Tr(PQ) terms gives

 Hess(logdet)[V,V] - (1/4)Hess(logdet)[R,R]
       = -Tr(T^2)-d x^2.                                      (A1)

Directional differentiation gives
 Vx=Tr(rho T^2)-x^2 and Rx=0. Applying these identities to the exact drift
in FULL_RANK_STOPPING (T3), and adding the Hessian term D:Hess/N, yields

 L logdet rho=-d(2-1/N)x^2-(d/N)Tr(rho T^2)-d b_T x

for one unit coefficient axis plus its field contribution; polarization
replaces x^2 and Tr(rho T^2) by x^T C x and Tr(rho Z_C). The martingale
quadratic variation is the gradient contraction

 Gamma(logdet)= (2d^2/N) x^T C x.

This reproduces c04_s01 (T8)-(T9). The apparently missing constant in the
Hessian is the -Tr(T^2) term in (A1); it cancels against the derivative of
V in the drift and leaves exactly -(d/N)Tr(rho Z_C). Omitting this term would
be a real factor error, but the written formulas retain it.

For c04_s02's basis Tr(T_aT_b)=delta_ab/2, v={T,rho}-2x rho=2V and its
rotation field is the same R. Its covariance D_I is twice c04_s01's
normalized D_I. Hence D_I=2 Tr[rho(Y-yI)rho(Y-yI)] and |D_A|<=10M in
c04_s02 correspond to D_I=Tr[rho Y rho Y] and |D_A|<=5M in c04_s01.
This accounts for the different cutoff/radius constants; the two versions
are consistent under basis rescaling.

For c05_s01's normalization, v=2V and z=i[T,rho] (the sign does not affect
its square). The same Hessian calculation, or two derivatives of the
normalized filter rho(s)=e^(sT)rho e^(sT)/Tr(e^(2sT)rho), gives

 L logdet rho=-alpha q[(2N-1)mu^T C mu+Tr(rho Z_C)]-u q mu_b,
 Gamma(logdet)=2alpha q^2 mu^T C mu.

These match (G8). The lower-drift and QV bounds in (G9) follow from
|mu|^2<=1/2, C<=Lc I, Z_C<=Lc c_F I, |mu_b|<=B/sqrt(2), and
N^(kappa-1)>=q Lc(1+c_F). Since u<=1, the bounded unscaled field costs
at most qB/sqrt(2); it is not omitted.

2. CUTOFF POSITIVITY AND EXIT ARITHMETIC

The full-SU principal identities are positive with the stated margins:

* c04_s01: D_I>=epsilon^2 I on rho>=epsilon I and
  |D_A|<=5M I. With k0^2=32M/alpha, epsilon=k0 N^(-gamma/2), the margin

* c04_s02: D_I>=2epsilon^2 I and |D_A|<=10M I. With
  k^2=max(1,10M/beta), epsilon=kN^(-delta/2), the margin is at least
  beta k^2 N^-delta at the cutoff when N^delta>=2M/beta. A positive margin

* c05_s01: D_I>=2epsilon^2 I and |D_A|<=10M I. Its choice
  Lambda=(20M+2)/eta^2 gives margin at least 1 on rho>=eta I/2, so the
  square root is smooth on an explicit neighborhood.

For a first hit tau, logdet at the boundary is at most log epsilon because
all other eigenvalues are at most one. The starting lower bounds are
-d log d for the direct full-SU SDE and q log a for the truncated heat-seed
SDE. Decomposing ell_t=ell_0+integral Lell+M_t, the uniform lower drift
bounds force the displayed one-sided martingale drops. The standard maximal
exponential bound exp(-s^2/(2v)) with the stated stopped QV gives exactly
(T11), (13), and (G9). No inverse-rho factor is hidden in either drift or QV.

The direct Duhamel exponent also checks. On tau<=t, the product of the
quantum semigroup bound exp((1-t)h) and stopped weight exp(tau h) is at most
exp(h), since tau<=t. Thus c04's h exp(h)P bound is valid. c05's
h exp(2h)P bound is looser and valid. The target normalized-trace lower
bound follows from C>=0 (or the isotropic heat seed's zero mean for the
field) and scalar Jensen. This is needed before converting the unnormalized
trace error to normalized trace distance.

3. SHIFTED SU(q) HEAT SEED CHECK AND COMPACT-LIE GATE

I rederived the coefficient normalization in c05_s01 G2. On the trace-zero
Cartan, Weyl integration contributes Delta(x)^2; the positive factor
j_+(x)=product sinh((x_i-x_j)/2)/((x_i-x_j)/2) reduces the character
integrand to exp(-|x|^2/(2t)) Delta(x) A_(lambda+rho)(x). The Weyl alternant
terms have equal integral. Completing the Gaussian square and using that
Delta is harmonic gives the ratio
exp{t(|lambda+rho|^2-|rho|^2)/2}. With roots of length squared 2 this is
the Casimir for Tr(T_aT_b)=delta_ab/2 (fundamental eigenvalue
(q^2-1)/(2q)). The integral is positive and finite. Thus the heat seed
normalization is consistent. One short proof-presentation repair: when
passing from a character trace to the operator identity on tensor powers,
mention both simultaneous SU(q) invariance and permutation invariance (or
that each representation summand is acted on identically on equivalent
copies); SU invariance alone plus Schur's lemma does not rule out arbitrary
multiplicity-space operators.

The primary source inspected was Maher, Wrapping Brownian motion and heat
kernels I (arXiv:1005.4746). It develops Kirillov/wrapping heat machinery;
it is relevant classical context, not by itself the stated positive split-real
j_+ identity. c05's Weyl/Gaussian calculation is the needed derivation. It
also means the character formula and Frobenius/root metric normalization
must remain explicit theorem inputs.

For the compact-Lie extension, COMPACT_LIE_EXTENSION's relation
R_T/2=V_(K_H T), K_H=i tanh(ad_H/2), is correct in a rho-eigenbasis:
K_H T_ij=i(p_i-p_j)/(p_i+p_j)T_ij, so filtering gives
(p_i+p_j)(K_HT)_ij/2=i(p_i-p_j)T_ij/2=R_T,ij/2. Hence
D_C=V[C-K_H C K_H*]V*. Since ||K_H||<1 on rho>=epsilon I,
I-K_HK_H*>=4epsilon/(1+epsilon)^2 I>=epsilon I; perturbing C by A costs
at most 2||A||N^-gamma. The chosen cutoffs and half-cutoff margins follow.
INTRINSIC_LOG_CHART's derivative formula is also consistent: the divided
difference of exp is (p_i-p_j)/(h_i-h_j), and multiplication by
(z/2)coth(z/2) changes it to (p_i+p_j)/2. Diagonal entries become
p_i(T_ii-Tr(rho T)), exactly V_T. Lie closure makes the even commutator
functional calculus preserve g; rotations are tangent by closure. This
provides the required invariant manifold and retains curvature terms in the
Ito chart. Keep the declared supplied Lie-closure condition: arbitrary local
lists are not covered.

4. FIXED-NEIGHBORHOOD / EXACT FULL-SEPARABILITY CONSEQUENCE

The new fixed-anisotropy constants in c04_s02 FIXED_ANISOTROPY_NEIGHBORHOOD
and c04_s01 COMPACT_LIE_EXTENSION check: a fixed cutoff eta gives exit rate
exp[-(H+2)N] (or exp[-(H_g+3)N]); the h=HN Duhamel weight leaves e^-2N
(or stronger). The radius choices make C positive and leave the stated
full-rank covariance margin. These are very small but positive radii,
not claims about arbitrary fixed anisotropy.

I also checked the arithmetic of the added white-noise absorption in
c04_s01 EXACT_FULL_SEPARABILITY and c04_s02 EXACT_FULL_SEPARABILITY. For an
HS-orthonormal tensor basis, the parity-conditioned local states give
(I +/- P_v)/d^N fully separable; Cauchy-Schwarz yields the sufficient
trace-norm ball d^(-2N). Each stopped product with rho>=eta I contains a
common white term (d eta)^N I/d^N, leaving a fully separable remainder. Hence
an unnormalized error ||R-sigma||_1<=(eta/d)^N implies exact full
separability. The factor two from half-trace distance is retained in their
thresholds. c04_s01's all-N equation (E6) pays the ball and white weight
with slack e^-2N; c04_s02's explicit weak-anisotropy test (S8)-(S9) solves
the same inequality. This proves full separability of the target in those
stated regimes, not an exact identical-product representation; the final
decomposition and stochastic sampler are not efficiently constructed.

5. REMAINING GATES AND COSTS

These are representation/existence results. c04_s01/c04_s02 do not provide
a certified finite-bit Brownian sampler, stopping-time resolver, weight
normalizer, or polynomial-time compiler; their weights can be exp(O(N)).
c05_s01 adds a finite exhaustive computable fallback under computable
parameters, but it uses the q^N-by-q^N target matrix, exponentially fine
local meshes, up to q^(2N)+1 Caratheodory atoms, and combinatorial search.
It is finite, not efficient. Matrix log/tanh/coth/square-root precision and
physical local-state emission remain charged. The exact-separability
consequence likewise gives no efficient decomposition. No algorithmic or
resource advantage follows.

The only material proof-presentation gates I found are: (i) state the
SU(q) multiplicity/permutation argument in G2 as above; (ii) give an explicit
larger-region margin if a quantitative SDE extension neighborhood is
required in c04_s02 (the theorem existence follows from the stated strict
margin and compactness); and (iii) in INTRINSIC_LOG_CHART, make explicit
that functional calculus is for the ordinary commutator operator
[H, .] on the complexification, so the factor i and real-Hermitian image are
unambiguous. None changes the stated mathematical bounds. External proof
validation, full literature priority (including Fannes--Kossakowski--Verbeure
1991), and executable costs remain UNKNOWN.
