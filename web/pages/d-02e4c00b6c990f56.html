# Cycle 3: trace-controlled gradient quadratics, including approximate centers

2026-10-06. Mathematical reconstruction by Team C; independently reconstructed direct primitive by c_gaussian and cost transfer by r10. This is a structural-promise result, not acquisition of that promise and not a proof of the source's faster cloud recursion.

## Exact interface

Supply known q>0,h>=0,tau>=1 and a gradient oracle for V(x)=q|x|²/2+U(x), with U convex C² and 0<=H_U<=hI, tr H_U<=tau h everywhere. U itself need not have integrable exp(-U). The residual gradient is legally grad U(x)=grad V(x)-qx. No function values, Hessians, proximal oracle, eigenvectors or hidden mean are supplied. For an explicit RGO center y and eta>0 put gamma=eta/(1+q eta), z=y/(1+q eta). Completing the known quadratic gives R^V_{eta,y}=R^U_{gamma,z} exactly.

## The missing oddness is not a problem for this primitive

The cycle2 determinant lemma only used oddness to establish E|S(G)|²<=rho a². More generally let S be C¹, op DS<=a, nuclear DS<=tau a, HS² DS<=tau a², and separately E|S(G)|²<=tau a². The same proof gives

    E exp[t G.S(G)] <= exp[|t|tau a+3tau t²a²], |t|a<=1/4,
    ||G.S(G)||_p <= C a[tau+sqrt(tau p)+p], p>=2.

For completeness: R=|S| is a-Lipschitz and ER<=sqrt(tau)a. Gaussian concentration plus independent scalar Gaussian integration gives

    E exp(lambda R²) <= (1-2lambda a²)^(-1/2)
      exp[lambda(ER)²/(1-2lambda a²)].

The map F_t(x)=x-tS(x) is a global diffeomorphism by contraction, with positive determinant by homotopy. Its inverse obeys |S(F_t^-1(y))|<=|S(y)|/(1-|t|a). The exact Gaussian change of variables gives

    E exp[t G.S(G)] = E_y exp[t²|S(F_t^-1(y))|²/2]
                         /det(I-t DS(F_t^-1(y))).

The trace-log estimate is -logdet(I-tD)<=|t|tau a+tau t²a²/[2(1-|t|a)]; it follows from |tr D|<=||D||_* and |tr D^k|<=||D||_F² a^(k-2) for k>=2. Applying the norm-square bound with lambda=t²/[2(1-|t|a)²] proves the displayed MGF and its two-sided Chernoff/moment consequence. This is the classical finite-dimensional Gaussian/Ramer identity, not new measure-change theory; cycle2 source metadata records Ustunel Chapter10.

Now fix a center c and s in [0,1], and set

    T_s(g)=sqrt(gamma)[grad U(c+s sqrt(gamma)g)-grad U(c)].

Its derivative is s gamma H_U, hence has the required budgets with a_s=s gamma h. Convex h-smooth co-coercivity gives

    |grad U(c+tG)-grad U(c)|²
       <=h t G.[grad U(c+tG)-grad U(c)].

Taking expectations and Gaussian integration by parts yields

    E|grad U(c+tG)-grad U(c)|²
       <=h t² E tr H_U(c+tG)<=tau h²t².

Consequently E|T_s(G)|²<=tau a_s² even though T_s is generally NOT odd. The bounds are uniform in c and s. With S~Uniform[0,1],

    R_S(G)=sqrt(gamma) G.[grad U(c+S sqrt(gamma)G)-grad U(c)]

is a lawful unbiased estimator of the nonnegative Bregman remainder

    R(G)=U(c+sqrt(gamma)G)-U(c)-sqrt(gamma)grad U(c).G,

and ||R_S(G)||_p<=C gamma h[tau+sqrt(tau p)+p]. No Hessian modulus or fixed derivative subspace was used.

## Approximate mode is accounted for exactly

Let e=grad U(c)+(c-z)/gamma and zeta=sqrt(gamma)|e|. Under proposal X=c+sqrt(gamma)G the exact target tilt is exp(-H(G)), where

    H(G)=R(G)+sqrt(gamma)e.G.

Thus a finite approximate mode creates no omitted target bias if this computable linear residual is included. For independent G', S,T, the source-centered estimator

    W=R_S(G')-R_T(G)+sqrt(gamma)e.(G'-G)

satisfies E[W|G]=EH-H(G). Constants in a log tilt do not change the normalized target. Under the proposal,

    ||W||_p<=C{gamma h[tau+sqrt(tau p)+p]+zeta sqrt(p)}.

The same scale holds under the true target: Gaussian interpolation bounds ER<=gamma tau h/2, and Jensen gives E exp(-H)>=exp(-ER). Since R>=0,

    dP/dQ <= exp(gamma tau h/2-sqrt(gamma)e.G),
    ||dP/dQ||_2 <= exp(gamma tau h/2+zeta²).

Hölder transfers the bound using 2p in place of p; independent G' retains its proposal law. If gamma h tau+zeta²<=c this is a universal constant. Choose p=C log(1/delta), universal clipping B<=1, gamma h(tau+p)<=cB and zeta sqrt(p)<=cB. Source clipping/FORS then gives TV<=delta, with O(1) expected attempts and O(B) estimator calls, including failures. c_gaussian supplies the independently reconstructed clipping proof and precise conservative four-gradient ledger in its cycle3 artifact.

To compute c, minimize F(x)=U(x)+|x-z|²/(2gamma) by gradient descent with step gamma/(1+gamma h), starting at z. Hessian eigenvalues lie in [gamma^-1,gamma^-1+h]. The gradient update contracts by alpha=gamma h/(1+gamma h): use the integrated Hessian on each update. Therefore |grad F(c_k)|<=alpha^k|grad U(z)|, and

    k >= log(sqrt(gamma)|grad U(z)|/zeta)/log(1/alpha)

is sufficient, with nonpositive numerator interpreted as zero. This is logarithmic numerical optimization, not an exact proximal query. When h=0, the potential is affine and the RGO is sampled exactly by known Gaussian completion after one residual-gradient query.

## Consequence and boundary

This primitive permits a slower complete proximal sampler with eta of order 1/[h(tau+log(1/delta))], rather than replacing d by tau in the source's faster Picard forest. Whole-chain mixing, reference/gradient norms, precision/caps and query complexity are audited separately by r10. Source Algorithm3.8 already fails at U=0 without known-quadratic removal, and the trace promise does not control the actual correlated H(W)B G high moments in Proposition5.4. Both are concrete failures of the attempted fast-architecture transfer.

Nearest baseline: the source AppendixA.1 direct FORS RGO and Section6.3 proximal wrapper; AppendixA.2 already integrates a known quadratic. Our supplied trace promise strengthens the direct primitive's admissible variance using a trace-leading bound. It is a complete scoped extension if the outer ledger closes, not a claimed new rejection-sampling principle or an oracle lower bound. All gradients produce d coordinates, so query savings do not remove O(d) vector work per query/proposal.
