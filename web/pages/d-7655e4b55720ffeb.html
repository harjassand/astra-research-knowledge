# Phase 2: prior-art challenge for minimum explosive endotactic order

**Cutoff:** 2026-10-08 (arXiv API latest-record snapshot 2026-10-08 10:14:51 UTC).  
**Scope:** source-level challenge only. I did not inspect or assess the proposed five-order network's derivation.

## Finding

I did not locate a verified fully endotactic stochastic mass-action explosive network of source order 3, 4, or 5, nor a theorem excluding all such higher-dimensional networks. The strongest current primary-source statement remains Xu, arXiv:2409.05340v2, last revised 2026-05-09: Remark 4.8 identifies the known explosive strongly endotactic example as order 10 and says the minimum explosive order for endotactic stochastic mass-action systems remains open. The arXiv record still lists v2 as current on the audit date. This is a current-source check, not priority clearance.

The search did recover lower-order explosion examples that could be mistaken for a counterexample if one checked order and explosion but not full endotacticity:

  * **Source order 4, almost-sure explosion, not endotactic.** Anderson–Cappelletti–Koyama–Kurtz, arXiv:1708.09356v2, Example 3, pp. 8–9, gives the one-species chain
  \[
  A\rightleftarrows 2A\rightleftarrows 3A\rightleftarrows 4A\to5A,
  \]
  with the displayed positive rates. The maximum *source* is 4A, so the source-order reconstruction is 4. The paper proves explosion from the birth/death-chain criterion: its birth and death rates are \(x^4\) and \(x^2(x-1)^2\), and \(\sum_{n\ge2}\prod_{x=2}^n q(x,x-1)/q(x,x+1)=\sum_{n\ge2}n^{-2}<\infty\), while a stationary distribution exists. It is not endotactic: for direction \(u=1\), 4A is a maximal source and 4A\(\to\)5A points outward. Thus this is a genuine order-4 stochastic explosion but not a full-endotactic one.
* **Source order 3, explosion only under a weaker directional condition.** Xu v2, Example 4.11, pp. 9–10, reports \(0\rightleftarrows S_1,\;2S_1\to3S_1,\;3S_2\to2S_2\) as strongly \((1,1)\)-endotactic and explosive on its stated closed classes. The source-order reconstruction is 3. It is not fully endotactic: for \(u=e_1\), the reaction \(3S_2\to2S_2\) is orthogonal and omitted; among the remaining sources, \(2S_1\) is maximal and \(2S_1\to3S_1\) points outward. This near miss does not lower the full-endotactic minimum.
* **Source order 2, parameter-dependent explosion, explicitly not 1-endotactic.** Xu v2, Example 4.10, p. 9, reports \(S_1\rightleftarrows2S_1\to3S_1\) and says it is regular iff \(\kappa_2\le\kappa_{-2}\). Its maximum source is 2S\(_1\); the outward reaction at that maximal source violates 1-endotacticity. Again, it does not conflict with Theorem 4.6.

There is also a **post-Xu-v2, source-order-2 mass-action network that the source reports as explosive** in Anderson–Howells–Rojas La Luz, current arXiv:2511.10223v4 (2026-06-10), Proposition 3.10, pp. 10–11. The four reactions and displayed rates are
\[
2Y\xrightarrow{2}Y,\qquad S\xrightarrow{1}S+Y,\qquad Y\xrightarrow{1}Y+E,\qquad E+S\xrightarrow{1}E+2S.
\]
The source reports explosion by constructing a positive function \(g(e,s,y)=(\log(e+2))^{-1}+(\log(s+2))^{-1}+(y+1)^{-1}\) whose generator is at most \(-1\) outside a finite set, then invoking its Corollary A.5. The maximum source-complex size is 2, reconstructed from \(2Y\) and \(E+S\). The source's rate convention is \(\kappa\prod_j\binom{x_j}{\nu_j}\) (§2.1, eq. (1)); its first rate 2 therefore gives the displayed generator term \(y(y-1)\). This is **not endotactic** (reconstructed): for \(u=e_S\), the only reaction with nonzero projection is \(E+S\to E+2S\); its source is maximal among projected reactions and its reaction vector has positive \(u\)-projection. I did not independently verify the explosion claim: the proof invokes Corollary A.5, whose printed hypothesis requires a state in a closed irreducible component. On the paper's restricted state space with \(s\geq1\), the displayed generator has nondecreasing \(e,s\), and from every state an increase in \(e\) or \(s\) is reachable, so no such closed irreducible component appears to exist. This is a source-proof concern, not a claim that the network is nonexplosive. In any event it does not contradict Xu’s order-2 theorem or lower the fully endotactic minimum. In the same paper, a different order-3 underlying chemistry in Example 3.11 is also called explosive, but the proposition above is the smallest source-order example I found in the later-source screen.

The known *exclusion* results remain compatible with an order-5 candidate. Xu v2, Theorem 4.6 and proof, p. 9, establish regularity/non-explosion for every endotactic stochastic mass-action system of order at most 2 (and also state the lower-/strong-endotactic subcases). Wiuf–Xu, arXiv:2012.07954v3, Theorem 4.8, p. 12, additionally gives non-explosion on each compatibility class for one-dimensional endotactic mass-action SRNs under H2–H5: one-dimensional stoichiometric subspace; nonempty positive- and negative-direction reaction sets; positive integer gcd direction; and every species changes in some reaction. The latter is a one-dimensional exclusion, not a dimension-free order bound.

## Later-source challenge

I checked the current arXiv history for Xu’s paper, its technical open-question passage, targeted exact-term searches for endotactic/explosive low-order systems, the arXiv query `all:endotactic AND (all:explosion OR all:explosive)`, and the available citation lists. The Xu record has only v1 (2024-09-09) and v2 (2026-05-09); the v2 page and HTML retain the open-minimum statement. Semantic Scholar’s citation endpoint for Xu returned one citing primary preprint, Xu’s 2026 first-order ergodicity paper. Its stated result is first-order essentiality/exponential ergodicity, not an order-3–5 explosion construction.

The citation graph around the 2018 tier-structure paper surfaced several later papers. I screened the current versions that could plausibly touch the model class: arXiv:2604.00756v2 (updated 2026-09-29) is a general stochastic-ordering/coupling paper; arXiv:2511.10223v4 (updated 2026-06-10) studies compartment models and, crucially, reports the order-2 non-endotactic CRN described above as explosive (with the proof-criterion caveat); arXiv:2507.09421v2 concerns randomly switching networks with asymptotically linear rates; and arXiv:2506.12163v1 proves positive recurrence for a network whose deterministic ODE blows up. These do not supply a finite, fixed, fully endotactic order-3–5 explosive mass-action network. The compartment paper’s additional explosive compartment result depends on content-dependent fragmentation and is outside the fixed mass-action CRN question. The 2510.03621v3 flux/toric paper and 2506.01606v1 boundary-reproduction paper are deterministic reaction-system analyses, not stochastic explosion results.

These checks can miss uncited, unindexed, unpublished, or not-yet-posted work. No novelty or priority claim follows from a search non-hit. I did not validate the proposed candidate network or any candidate proof.

## Deliverable boundary

This phase challenges only the historical-priority claim that no prior lower-order fully endotactic explosion is known. The evidence found strengthens confidence that the 3–10 documented bounds were not accidentally invalidated by the checked sources, but does not certify a candidate, prove it, establish its novelty, or clear priority. Proposition 3.10 of arXiv:2511.10223v4 is recorded as a source-reported non-endotactic order-2 explosion claim, with the Corollary A.5 hypothesis concern above.
