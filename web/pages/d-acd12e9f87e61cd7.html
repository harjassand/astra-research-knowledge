# Cycle 6 source and provenance notes

1. **Universal symmetric cloning construction.** Keyl and Werner, “Optimal Cloning of Pure States, Judging Single Clones,” [arXiv:quant-ph/9807010](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9807010), gives the universal (N\to M) symmetric cloner and one-site shrink factor. The present construction uses (N=2,M=4,d=2), with coefficient (D_2/D_4=3/5), then traces out two outputs. The qutrit marginal spectrum is independently computed exactly in `scripts/spin1_star_certificate.py`.

2. **Prior structure of covariant spin channels.** Cîrstoiu, Korzekwa and Jennings, “Robustness of Noether's Principle,” [Phys. Rev. X 10, 041035 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.10.041035), characterize extremal SU(2)-irreducibly covariant channels and their Choi representation via irreducible tensor operators (see Sec. V and Theorem 8). That structure supports the multiplicity-free sector reduction used here. It does not give a common canonical-EB Loewner comparator for self-compatible channels. The universal cloner and spin-coherent covariant measure/prepare channels are standard constructions; no novelty is claimed for either channel family.

3. **Star spectrum.** The (27\times27) characteristic polynomial and both output-swap blocks (18 and 9 dimensions) in `proofs/STAR_HAMILTONIAN.md` are produced from the exact spin-1 matrices and double-commutator projectors. They are internal exact-arithmetic certificates; no external theorem or SDP is used for this diagonalization. SymPy is only a symbolic arithmetic dependency.

The pure-state identity Σ_a⟨A_a⟩²=2 for a τ-orthonormal traceless qutrit basis is Parseval. Spin-coherent moments and the τ-orthonormal normalization are derived in the proof. No source-reported optimization is imported.
