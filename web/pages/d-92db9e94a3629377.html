## 3. Cell sign and frozen-radius smoothing

    v_sigma=(S_sigma K_cell)/c,
    alpha_sigma=(S_sigma A_cell)/c,

with the zero extension on the inner harmonic collar. These are smooth pi-periodic functions, with

    mean(v_sigma)=0,
    m_r(sigma)=mean(alpha_sigma)
      ->[-pi+2delta+cot(delta)-delta csc(delta)^2]/pi
      <=-1+2delta/pi<-0.93.                             (7)

There is a stronger pointwise sign, avoiding reliance on this limit. At points outside the sigma-enlarged stripes, every convolution source lies in the regular region A_cell=-c; radial harmonic averaging then gives alpha_sigma=-1 exactly. Consequently

    m_r(sigma)<=-1+2(delta+sigma)/pi<-0.92<-1/2.         (7a)

This holds for EVERY positive radial source kernel and fixed sigma<delta/4, in addition to its original zero-safe restrictions. It includes rather than discards the endpoint measures. No j-dependent mollifier or further sigma limit is required.

## 4. The variable radius changes (8) by only O(mu)
