PRIMARY SOURCE COMPARISON (checked 2026-10-08)

1. C. Fronsdal and T. Hirai, “A remark on the tensor product of irreducible
representations of a compact Lie group,” Japan. J. Math. 7(1) (1981),
201-211, DOI 10.4099/math1924.7.201.
Publisher scan: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/math1924/7/1/7_1_201/_pdf

Their Theorem 1 gives the stronger representation inclusion
End(U) embeds into Ind_T^G(1)=L^2(G/T) for connected compact G. Frobenius
reciprocity yields mult(V_nu,End(U))<=dim V_nu[0]. Their stabilizer form
uses the coadjoint stabilizer and can be sharper for singular weights.
The internal highest-vector coefficient injection is recorded in
multiplicity_injection.txt; this result is not claimed as new.

2. B. Kostant, “A formula for the multiplicity of a weight,” Trans. Amer.
Math. Soc. 93 (1959), 53-73, DOI
https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1959-0109192-6.

Used for the exact alternating Weyl sum underlying both stable PBW shells
and the uniform zero-weight multiplicity bound. The PBW partition estimate
is an upper bound; it is not mistaken for an equality to the alternating
Kostant sum.

3. S. Billey, V. Guillemin, E. Rassart, “A vector partition function for
the multiplicities of sl_k C,” J. Algebra 278(1) (2004), 251-293, DOI
10.1016/j.jalgebra.2003.12.005; arXiv:math/0307227v1 (2003-07-16).
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0307227

Their type-A chamber-polynomial result identifies the regular zero-weight
degree binom(m-1,2)=p-(m-1), consistent with the coarse PBW upper used
here. The profile proof does not require their sharper chamber formulas.

4. G. Alagic and A. Russell, “Uncertainty Principles for Compact Groups,”
Illinois J. Math. 52(4) (2008), 1315-1324, arXiv:math/0608702v2.
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608702

Their compact-group operator identity and support/Fourier-rank inequality
are related in method but constrain spatial support or Fourier matrix
ranks; they do not state the present rank-Q Casimir variational profile
on a single irreducible carrier.

5. PRV caveat. Parthasarathy-Ranga Rao-Varadarajan (1967) and Kumar (1988)
prove distinguished tensor-product component occurrence. Such an
occurrence theorem alone does not upper-bound total multiplicities. The
proof here uses the injection / Fronsdal-Hirai inclusion instead.

This is a bounded source comparison, not an absence or novelty proof.
