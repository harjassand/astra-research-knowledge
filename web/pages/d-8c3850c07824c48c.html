QUASI-GALOIS POINTS FOR QUARTIC SURFACES
KEI MIURA AND SHINGO TAKI
Abstract.We study quasi-Galois points, which are a generalization of Galois
points. We characterize smooth quartic surfaces admitting a quasi-Galois point
in terms ofK3 surfaces with a certain involution, and provide a criterion for
quasi-Galois points for quartic surfaces. Moreover, we also determine all quasi-
Galois points of the Fermat quartic surface and show that it has exactly 28
quasi-Galois points.
1.Introduction
We will work overC, the field of complex numbers, throughout this paper. In
algebraic geometry, hypersurfaces of a projective space are one of the most funda-
mental objects. H. Yoshihara introduced a remarkable new notion, that is, a Galois
point, in 1996. Galois points have been studied and generalized by many mathe-
maticians. For example, S. Fukasawa, the first-named author, and T. Takahashi
introduced the concept of quasi-Galois points [1] [2].
LetVbe a (smooth) hypersurface inP n andC(V) the function field ofV. For a
pointP∈P n, we consider a projectionπ P :V99KP n−1. Note that the projection
πP induces the extensionC(V)/π ∗
P C(Pn−1) of function fields. If the extension is
Galois then the pointPis called aGalois pointforV. It is known that an element
of its Galois group acts onVas a birational automorphism, and preservesπ P .
We consider the subsetG[P] :={τ∈Bir(V)|π P ◦τ=π P }of all birational
automorphisms ofV. If the order ofG[P] satisfies|G[P]| ≥2 thenPis called
quasi-Galois. In particular, if|G[P]|= degπ P holds thenPis Galois. In this sense,
quasi-Galois points are a generalization of Galois points.
Here is a fundamental problem about (quasi-)Galois points:
•Find the equation of a quartic surface with (quasi-)Galois points.
•Find the number of (quasi-)Galois points of a given hypersurface.
•Characterize hypersurfaces with (quasi-)Galois points.
This problem has been solved for quartic surfaces. Yoshihara [10] [11] determined
equations of quartic surfaces with Galois points, and the number of Galois points.
Recently, the authors [5] [6] obtained a characterization of quartic surfaces with a
Galois point in terms ofK3 surfaces with an automorphism.
In this paper, we consider this problem in the case of quasi-Galois points for
quartic surfaces. The following is the main theorem of this paper. (For detailed
terminology, refer to Section 2 and beyond.)
