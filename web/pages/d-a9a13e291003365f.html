1. AN EXACT, UNIFORM RACAH-TO-BESSEL ERROR BOUND

The earlier file proves the terminating exact series

  eta=eta_l(l)=sum_(r=0..l)(-1)^r t_r,
  t_r=(n+1)(n-r)!/(n+r+1)!
          *[(l+r)!/((l-r)!r!)]^2,
  t_(r+1)/t_r=(L-r(r+1))^2/[(r+1)^2(N-r(r+2))].          (1)

Its all-n ratio inequality is t_(r+1)/t_r<=z^2/(r+1)^2.
Define u_r=z^(2r)/(r!)^2 for EVERY r>=0, and t_r=0 for r>l.
For r<=l,

  t_r/u_r=product_(s=0..r-1) a_s,
  a_s=[1-s(s+1)/L]^2/[1-s(s+2)/N].                       (2)

The earlier ratio proof gives 0<=a_s<=1 for s<=l-1. Since its denominator
is <=1 and the numerator base is nonnegative,

  a_s>=[1-s(s+1)/L]^2>=1-2s(s+1)/L.

The elementary product union bound now gives

  0<=1-t_r/u_r<=2 sum_(s=0..r-1)s(s+1)/L
                 =2r(r^2-1)/(3L).                       (3)

For r>l, the left side is exactly1. The same right side is >=1:
at r=l+1 it equals 2(l+2)/3>=2, and it increases thereafter.
For r=0,1 the terms agree exactly. Thus (3) is valid for ALL r>=0.

The Bessel series is J_0(2z)=sum_(r>=0)(-1)^r u_r. Absolute convergence
and (3) imply the EXPLICIT all-n, all-l error estimate

       =2(z^2+3z)exp(2z)/(3sqrt(N)).                     (4)

For completeness, r(r^2-1)=r(r-1)(r-2)+3r(r-1).
