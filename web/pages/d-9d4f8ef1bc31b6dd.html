# Counterpart splitting gives constant-time old-decoder error

7 October2026. Root derivation, independently reconstructed in cycle17 by
pde_spectrum9 and pde_transport9; written peer gates requested. A finite-family
Davies result with generic WEIGHTED check penalties and vanishing total field.
No fixed-intensive or exactly unit-weight theorem, microscopic bath theorem,
or obstruction to every possible encoding/recovery is asserted.

## 1. Interface and acquired gap premise

Use the actual admitted CSS family with one logical qubit, bounded Z-check
degree g<=7 and penalty weights in a fixed positive open interval bounded by
hmax. Let H0 be its commuting check Hamiltonian. Choose independently weighted
Z-check penalties so the distinct valid Z-syndrome energies in each fixed
X-check sector have minimum spacing Delta_N>0. The weight selection is an
existence/genericity premise with no uniform spacing or cheap selector claimed.
Let V=-sum_i epsilon_i X_i with all epsilon_i>0 and nu=||V||<=sum_i epsilon_i.
Take nu<=min(Delta_N/64,1/2), as well as the earlier admitted activity threshold.

Choose a pure-X logical circuit. It can be selected without minimum-distance
search using a column basis of H_Z: fundamental circuits span ker H_Z, so at
least one lies outside the X-stabilizer row space. The root companion proof
LOGICAL_FLUX_COUNTERPART_SPLITTING.md shows at least two physical sites q on
such a circuit for which every counterpart transition gap splits generically
between the logical +/- blocks. The nonzero squarefree coefficient persists
when all other fields are positive. For each finite code choose positive
weighted penalties and arbitrarily small positive fields avoiding these
finitely many analytic zero sets. This is not a statement at the degenerate
exactly equal penalty vector. Selection costs/heights remain unacquired.

Let O be the original syndrome-dressed logical Z: a Hermitian involution
commuting with all old checks and anticommuting with the bare logical X.
In the unperturbed basis, O maps each syndrome state phi_(a,+) to a phase times
phi_(a,-). The bare physical A=X_q maps a to b=a+v_q with unit modulus.
Keep this ONE original physical bath, its current-frequency Davies components,
and the fixed rate gamma(omega)=1/(1+exp(-beta omega)), 0<gamma<=Gamma=1.
The proof below also permits an upper Gamma and a positive lower gamma_min
on |omega|<=W=g hmax+1. No Lamb shift is included.

## 2. Other-pair resonances do not rescue this old decoder

Elementary eigenvector perturbation suffices. For a normalized eigenvector
psi_a of D+V with eigenvalue within nu of E_a, projecting its equation away
from phi_a bounds the perpendicular norm by nu/(Delta_N-nu). Choose its phase
so the phi_a overlap is positive. Then

    ||psi_a-phi_a|| <= 2nu/(Delta_N-nu)=:r <= 1/16.

This estimate holds in every fixed X-check/logical block. In particular
||O psi_(a,+)-u_a psi_(a,-)||<=2r, and the physical counterpart amplitude
|<psi_(b,+),A psi_(a,+)>|>=1-2r.
At the actual plus counterpart frequency omega, simplicity within the plus
block makes A_omega psi_(a,+) its b component alone. Counterpart gap mismatch
makes the minus a-to-b matrix entry EXACTLY zero. Taking the b,- coefficient
of [O,A_omega]psi_(a,+), the first term has magnitude at least (1-2r)^2;
the remaining minus term is at most 2r. A frequency component has norm at
most ||A||=1, since its distinct energy input/output blocks are orthogonal.
Thus this commutator coefficient is at least 1-6r >=1/2.
The same argument applies with logical signs reversed, to ALL eigenstates.
Other-pair frequency coincidences enter only through the bounded 2r term.

The selected frequencies have |omega|<=g hmax+2nu<=W. Summing with the FULL
current Gibbs weights, whose total is one, yields

    k_O := sum_omega gamma(omega) Tr rho [O,A_omega]^*[O,A_omega]
          >= gamma_min/4,
    gamma_min = 1/(1+exp(beta W)).                              (1)

There is no exponentially small selected-sector probability here. Conversely,
this is for the fixed old decoder; an arbitrary O mixing far-separated old
syndromes need not have the near-diagonal intertwiner used above.

## 3. From a Dirichlet lower bound to actual constant-time channel error

Write the physical generator L=-i[H,.]+D. In Heisenberg form

    D*(X)=sum_omega gamma(omega)
       [A_omega^* X A_omega - {A_omega^*A_omega,X}/2].

Because A=X_q is a Hermitian unitary, sum A_omega^*A_omega=I. Hence the gain
map norm is <=Gamma, the anticommutator norm is <=Gamma, and
||D*||_(infinity->infinity)<=2Gamma. The paired KMS frequencies make D*
self-adjoint and nonpositive for <X,Y>_rho=Tr rho X*Y. One can verify this
term by term using rho A_omega=exp(beta omega) A_omega rho and
A_-omega=A_omega^*, with gamma(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma(omega).
The loss operator commutes with rho. Its finite-dimensional eigenvalues are
therefore -lambda with 0<=lambda<=2Gamma. This is a GNS spectral argument,
not a false bound on right multiplication in a Gibbs-weighted norm.

For O^2=I the spectral measure of O has total mass one, and stationarity plus
the GKLS product identity gives its mean lambda=k_O/2. The chord inequality
on [0,2Gamma] now implies

    1-C_D(t) >= k_O(1-exp(-2Gamma t))/(4Gamma),
    C_D(t)=<O,exp(tD*)O>_rho.                                  (2)

Davies covariance [ad_H,D*]=0 allows the Hamiltonian rotation to act on the
initial O before the unital dissipative contraction. Since [H0,O]=0,
||exp(itH)O exp(-itH)-O||<=2nu t. Thus the full correlation differs from
C_D(t) by at most 2nu t. No global norm ||H|| or N factor is needed here.

Let rho_bar be the exact full logical Pauli twirl of rho. Under its acquired
logical factorization, rho_bar=I/2 tensor rho_aux; the actual thermal encoder
sends the logical +/- Z states to sigma_+/-=rho_bar +/- O rho_bar.
The original logical decoder reads O. Its average label-error probability is

    p_err(t)=[1-Re Tr O exp(tL)(O rho_bar)]/2.

Replacing rho_bar by rho costs at most delta/2, where delta=||rho_bar-rho||1.
For the positive-X field, the inherited Pauli Gibbs-twirl bound gives

    delta<=beta nu.                                           (3)

An independent alternative proof uses entropy: the Pauli conditional
expectation T fixes H0 and log(T rho), so
D(rho||T rho)+D(T rho||rho)=beta Tr V(T rho-rho).
Two Pinsker bounds give a lower bound delta^2, whereas the right side is at
most beta nu delta. This proves (3) directly, without assuming [O,rho]=0.
Combining (1)-(3),

    p_err(t) >= k_O(1-exp(-2Gamma t))/(8Gamma)
                    -nu t-beta nu/2.                         (4)

For t0=1/(2Gamma), let a=gamma_min(1-exp(-1))/(16Gamma). In addition to the
spacing/activity conditions above choose nu<=a/[2(1/Gamma+beta)]. Then

    p_err(t0) >= a/4 = gamma_min(1-exp(-1))/(64Gamma) >0.        (5)

All constants in (5) are independent of N at fixed beta, local degree and
weight range. At least one logical Z input has that much error, so half the
diamond distance of this encoded/decoded logical channel from the identity
is also at least the displayed constant. Preparation of its thermal auxiliary
is a named resource, not an algorithm supplied by this negative result.

## 4. Scientific scope and next question

This rejects a uniform vanishing-error theorem for THIS old thermal encoder
and decoder over the stated generic weighted, arbitrarily weak all-positive
perturbations in the exact current-frequency Davies model. It does not require
complete cross-sector frequency-set disjointness. It does not rule out a
better final recovery or a different logical encoding. Fields may have to
shrink far below the finite-system syndrome spacing; no polynomial-bit
parameter-construction or fixed-intensity extension is inferred.

The exact unit-weight model has degenerate syndrome energies and is NOT
covered by the eigenvector-label argument. The next consequential alternatives
are: resolve those degenerate blocks without replacing the original model;
acquire an encoding robust to the current-frequency bath; or establish an
actual finite-resolution/product-reservoir theorem with a uniform time budget.
The engineered stationary Gaussian result remains valid at its different
interface. Discontinuity of secular weak-coupling limits is an established
phenomenon; historical novelty of this code-specific construction is UNKNOWN.
