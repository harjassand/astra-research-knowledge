CYCLE 5 FOLLOW-ON — HIGHER-ORDER CO-CATALYST TOGGLES
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact grammar and two finite
certificate margins below. Exact checker is finite evidence. External review
and novelty/priority remain UNKNOWN.

1. NETWORK
----------
Fix integers r>=1 and d>=2. Species are A, B, C_1,...,C_r. Include positive
finite stochastic mass-action pairs

  0 <-> A                       alpha, delta a
  0 <-> C_i                     gamma_i, mu_i c_i
  A <-> A+B                     p a, q a b
  A+d C_i <-> d C_i              eta_i a (c_i)_d, zeta_i (c_i)_d,
                                  i=1,...,r,

where (n)_d=n(n-1)...(n-d+1), zero for n<d. Each pair is reversible, so
the network is weakly reversible. The last pair has catalyst source order
d+1 and preserves every C_i and B count.

Write

  phi_i(c)=(c_i)_d,  Q(c)=sum_i zeta_i phi_i(c),
  E(c)=sum_i eta_i phi_i(c),  u(c)=1/(1+Q(c)),
  Delta_i(c)=Q(c)-Q(c-e_i),
  H(c)=sum_{i:c_i>0} mu_i c_i Delta_i(c)/[(1+Q(c)-Delta_i(c))(1+Q(c))].

For the positive C_i immigration jump define Delta_i^+(c)=Q(c+e_i)-Q(c),
and let

  J_+(c)=sum_i gamma_i Delta_i^+(c)/[(1+Q(c))(1+Q(c)+Delta_i^+(c))].

The exact inactive-correction and A-weight margins are

  theta(c)=[alpha+Q(c)]u(c)+J_+(c)-H(c),
  kappa_A(c)=[delta+E(c)]u(c)-H(c).

Assume

  theta_*:=inf_{c in N^r} theta(c)>0,
  kappa_*:=inf_{c in N^r} kappa_A(c)>0.                 (1)

Choose positive weights w_i>0. Define

  G_R(n)=[p(R-1)-q(1-R^{-1})n]R^n,
  g_+(R)=sup_{n in Z_{>=0}} max(G_R(n),0).

Choose R>1 such that g_+(R)<kappa_*/2. Such an R exists: G_R(n)>0
only for n<pR/q, so the positive maximum is over a uniformly finite set
when R is near 1, and tends to zero as R down to 1. For arbitrary epsilon>0
and K>=1, set

  V(a,c,b)=1+sum_i w_i c_i+a u(c)
                +R^b[1+epsilon 1_{a=0}u(c)]
                +K 1_{a+sum_i c_i=0}.                    (2)

2. THEOREM
----------
Under (1) and the R condition, V is positive and norm-like. There exist
finite C,c>0 with

  LV <= C-cV.                                             (3)

The minimal continuous-time chain is nonexplosive in physical time and is
positive recurrent on its irreducible class. All states communicate because
the immigration/death pairs adjust A and each C_i, and, while A>0, the
A<->A+B pair adjusts B; an A death can then leave any chosen B count.

This is an all-rate theorem inside a higher-order coupled-toggle class once
the exact margins (1) are certified. It is not a blanket weak-reversibility
claim.

3. GENERATOR IDENTITIES
-----------------------
The B-edge contribution to R^b is a G_R(b). The A u(c) part receives

  u(c)[alpha-delta a+Q(c)-aE(c)]
  +a sum_i gamma_i[u(c+e_i)-u(c)]
  +a sum_i mu_i c_i[u(c-e_i)-u(c)].                    (4)

The last sum is aH(c). The immigration sum is nonpositive. Thus (4) is at
most

  alpha+1-kappa_* a.                                      (5)

On a=0 the correction epsilon R^b u(c) has generator exactly
epsilon R^b F(c), where

  F(c)=-[alpha+Q(c)]u(c)
       +sum_i gamma_i[u(c+e_i)-u(c)]
       +sum_i mu_i c_i[u(c-e_i)-u(c)]
      =-theta(c).

On a>=1 this correction vanishes except when a=1 and A death or a forward
A+dC_i->dC_i toggle fires. Its positive generator contribution is at most

  epsilon R^b u(c)[delta+E(c)]
       <= epsilon[delta+eta_max/zeta_min]R^b,             (6)

where eta_max=max_i eta_i and zeta_min=min_i zeta_i.

The weighted C immigration/death term is
sum_i w_i gamma_i-sum_i w_i mu_i c_i <= C_C-kappa_C sum_i c_i,
with kappa_C=min_i w_i mu_i>0. Toggle reactions do not change C.

The K-indicator generator is at most K max(delta,mu_max): it can increase
only on death of the sole catalyst, and all catalyst immigration at the
origin lowers the indicator. A full C_i-immigration jump at the origin can
still be positive because of w_i; its positive part is bounded by
(w_i-K)^+ and contributes at most sum_i gamma_i(w_i-K)^+ to C.

4. PROOF OF THE ALL-STATE DRIFT
-------------------------------
As b grows, G_R(b)/(bR^b)->-q(1-R^{-1}). Choose N so for every b>=N,

  G_R(b)<=-[q(1-R^{-1})/2]bR^b.

If a>=1 and b>=N, this negative term absorbs the O(R^b) correction gain
(6), while (5) controls a and the weighted C drift controls sum c_i. If
a=0 and b>=N, the inactive correction is at most
-epsilon theta_* R^b; (5)'s C counterpart controls the C coordinates and
all remaining terms are bounded above by a constant minus a positive
multiple of sum c_i.

For b<N, R^b is bounded. Equation (5) controls a uniformly through
-kappa_* a, and aG_R(b)<=g_+(R)a with g_+<kappa_*/2. The active correction
gain is bounded on this region. The inactive correction is nonpositive by
theta_*>0. The weighted C drift controls sum c_i. These cases yield

  LV<=C_0-c_a a-c_C sum_i c_i-c_B R^b

for positive constants (decrease c_B or enlarge C_0 on b<N). Since
V<=1+sum_i w_i c_i+a+(1+epsilon)R^b+K, (3) follows.

The inequalities in (1) also imply a finite rational-rate certificate.
Let M=max_i c_i and rho=min_i eta_i/zeta_i, lambda=min(delta,rho),
zeta_min=min_i zeta_i, zeta_max=max_i zeta_i, and mu_max=max_i mu_i.
For M>=4d, every coordinate vector c-e_i with c_i>0 still has a
coordinate at least M-1. Each factor in its falling factorial, and in
that of c, is therefore at least M/2. Thus both Q(c) and Q(c-e_i) are at
least zeta_min(M/2)^d. Also Delta_i(c)<=zeta_max d c_i^(d-1), so direct
falling-factorial bounds give

  H(c) <= r C_d / M^d,
  C_d = 2^(2d) mu_max zeta_max d / zeta_min^2.

Also Q(c)>=zeta_min(M/2)^d and E(c)>=rho Q(c). Choose an integer M0
satisfying M0>=4d and

  zeta_min(M0/2)^d>=1,
  r C_d/M0^d <= min(1/4,lambda/2).

Then for every max_i c_i>=M0,

  theta(c)>=1/4,   kappa_A(c)>=lambda/2.

Consequently (1) is exactly decidable on rational rates by enumerating the
M0^r vectors with 0<=c_i<M0 and checking theta(c)>0 and kappa_A(c)>0 by
exact rational arithmetic. The minimum of these checked values and the
analytic tail bounds give positive rational lower certificates theta_cert
and kappa_cert. Search R=1+1/m, m=1,2,..., checking the finite positive
support of G_R, until g_+(R)<kappa_cert/2; this search terminates because
g_+(R)->0 as R down to one. Thus the stated grammar has a terminating exact
certificate compiler on inputs satisfying the two margins. This finite
certificate is not claimed to run in polynomial time in the rate encoding
or variable r; the margin enumeration uses M0^r states.

5. EXPLICIT NON-COMPLEX-BALANCED INSTANCE
-----------------------------------------
Take r=2,d=2, alpha=2, delta=p=q=1, gamma_1=gamma_2=1,
mu_1=mu_2=1/100, eta_1=eta_2=1, zeta_1=1,zeta_2=2,
w_1=w_2=1, R=11/10, epsilon=K=1. Here

  Q(c)=(c_1)_2+2(c_2)_2, E(c)=(c_1)_2+(c_2)_2,
  rho=1/2, lambda=1/2, g_+(R)=1/10.

The analytic tail cutoff above is M0=64. An exact finite enumeration of
0<=c_1,c_2<64 verifies (1); the checker reports the exact minima. The
complex-balance equations at complexes A+2C_1 and A+2C_2 would require
the A activity to equal zeta_1/eta_1=1 and zeta_2/eta_2=2 simultaneously.
Thus this all-rate-recurrent example is weakly reversible but not complex
balanced or detailed balanced.

6. LIMITS AND SOURCES
---------------------
The theorem uses identical cofactor degree d on separate C_i coordinates,
independent C_i immigration/death, and toggles that leave C and B unchanged.
It does not address cofactor mixtures, C conversions, or arbitrary
weakly-reversible networks. The margins (1) are explicit sufficient
conditions, not necessary for recurrence. No deterministic permanence
implication is used.

The recurrence interface is Anderson–Kim, Theorem 3.1, which applies once
LV<=-1 outside a finite set; the current stronger drift gives this condition.
The nonexplosion argument is separate: V is norm-like, LV is bounded above,
and localized stopped Dynkin on finite V-sublevels rules out escape to
infinity in finite time. Primary source:
https://arxiv.org/html/1710.11263v3. The exact source theorem and scope are
recorded at that link. No external validation or novelty claim is made.
