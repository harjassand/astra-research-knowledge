# Pivot-moving repair realization and the all-level Schur condition

Cycle15; 7 October2026. Worker hessian_bridge12, root-verified GPT-6.1 Sol/max. No nested workers. Derivations below are ours; the starting potential and toric normalization are published/pinned prior. Independent internal reconstruction by hessian_check12 is not external validation.

**Outcome.** Every polynomial repair of the selected nonzero-level four-variable envelope is realizable by a global polynomial source automorphism and a global polynomial critical section. Thus the previous residual-gradient ideal gate cannot be imposed on all pivot-moving charts. The unchanged zero-level envelope nevertheless obstructs *every* pivot translation from restoring full five-variable constant Hessian determinant for our fixed nonlinear residual chart. No HC4 counterexample is constructed.

## 1. Exact interface and realization theorem

Work over \(\mathbb C\), with base \(b=(x,y)\), residual variables \(R=(\rho,\eta)\), and \(u=(b,R)\). Let \(L\in\mathbb C^\times\), \(a\in\mathbb C[b]\), and \(c=(c_1,c_2)\in\mathbb C[b]^2\). Start with
\[
 f_0(b,t,R_0)=Lt^2+a(b)t+c(b)R_0.
\]
Let \(\Theta_b:\mathbb A^2\to\mathbb A^2\) be a polynomial automorphism over \(\mathbb C[b]\), independent of \(t\), and set \(V(u)=c(b)\Theta_b(R)\).

For **arbitrary** \(H\in\mathbb C[u]\), the source chart
\[
 \Phi_H(b,\tau,R)=(b,\ t=\tau+H(u),\ R_0=\Theta_b(R))
\]
has global polynomial inverse
\[
 R=\Theta_b^{-1}(R_0),\qquad
 \tau=t-H(b,\Theta_b^{-1}(R_0)).
\]
Its Jacobian is the nonzero constant Jacobian of \(\Theta\). This is actual global source legality, not a formal local change.

For any constant \(\sigma\), \(f_H=f_0\circ\Phi_H\) has
\[
 (f_H)_{\tau\tau}=2L,\qquad
 \tau=s_\sigma(u)=\frac{\sigma-a(b)}{2L}-H(u)
\]
as its unique global polynomial solution of \((f_H)_\tau=\sigma\). Its critical-value envelope is
\[
 \psi_\sigma(u)=(f_H-\sigma\tau)|_{\tau=s_\sigma}
 =V(u)-\frac{(a(b)-\sigma)^2}{4L}+\sigma H(u). \tag{1}
\]
The chain rule gives \(\nabla_u\psi_\sigma=\nabla_u f_H|_{\tau=s_\sigma}\). The section derivative terms cancel because \((f_H)_\tau=\sigma\).

Consequently, if \(\sigma\ne0\), **every** polynomial repair \(R_{\rm rep}(u)\) of \(V-(a-\sigma)^2/(4L)\) is realized by \(H=R_{\rm rep}/\sigma\). No residual-gradient ideal, degree bound or origin vanishing is required for this selected-level realization.

If the unmodified envelope has a collision at distinct \(q_+,q_-\), the repaired envelope retains that pair exactly when
\[
 \nabla R_{\rm rep}(q_+)=\nabla R_{\rm rep}(q_-). \tag{2}
\]
The corresponding full source points are \((s_\sigma(q_\pm),q_\pm)\); their \(\tau\)-coordinates need not coincide. Their full gradient equality is equivalent to (2), with common first component \(\sigma\). The source inverse sends them back to the same old critical \(t=(\sigma-a(q_\pm))/(2L)\) when \(a(q_+)=a(q_-)\). Equal source gradients under a nonlinear chart are verified by (1), not assumed invariant.

## 2. Exact full-source determinant criterion

Write
\[
 A=2LH+a,\quad P=LH^2+aH+V,\quad
 W=P-\frac{A^2}{4L}=V-\frac{a^2}{4L},\quad
 Z=\frac{A}{2L}=H+\frac{a}{2L}.
\]
Then
\[
 f_H=L\tau^2+A(u)\tau+P(u),\qquad
 \Sigma=2L\tau+A(u).
\]
Its Hessian in coordinates \((\tau,u)\) is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
 2L&(\nabla A)^T\\
 \nabla A&H_P+\tau H_A
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
The Schur complement is exactly
\[
 H_P+\tau H_A-\frac{\nabla A(\nabla A)^T}{2L}
 =H_W+\Sigma H_Z.
\]
Therefore the **global** polynomial identity is
\[
 \det H_{f_H}=2L\det(H_W+\Sigma H_Z). \tag{3}
\]
The polynomial change \((\tau,u)\mapsto(\Sigma,u)\) is invertible because \(2L\) is a constant unit. Hence full five-variable constant determinant means constancy of the entire pencil in \(\Sigma\), not just at one selected critical graph.

Specifically, full determinant constancy at a nonzero value is equivalent to
\[
 \det H_W=d\in\mathbb C^\times,\qquad
 K=(H_W)^{-1}H_Z\text{ is nilpotent}. \tag{4}
\]
Here \(K\) has polynomial entries because \(\det H_W\) is a constant unit. For four variables, nilpotence is equivalently \(K^4=0\).

Proof: evaluate (3) at \(\Sigma=0\) to obtain \(d\), then divide by it. The pencil condition is \(\det(I+\Sigma K)=1\). Its four coefficients are the signed characteristic-polynomial coefficients of \(K\), so its characteristic polynomial is \(z^4\); Cayley–Hamilton gives \(K^4=0\). Conversely nilpotence over the rational-function field gives the same characteristic polynomial and pencil identity, which hold over the polynomial ring. This elementary argument does **not** supply one constant triangularizing flag, strong nilpotence, or a polynomial gradient inverse.

At the selected level,
\[
 \psi_\sigma=W+\sigma Z-\frac{\sigma^2}{4L},\qquad
 H_{\psi_\sigma}=H_W+\sigma H_Z. \tag{5}
\]
If (4) is not established, its nonzero constant determinant must be checked directly for the actual \(\sigma\). Equation (1) alone provides no determinant conclusion.

The exact symbolic checker verifies the full nonlinear Hessian chain rule. For the pivot translation alone, set \(\widetilde f(t,u)=Lt^2+a t+V(u)\), \(\Lambda_H(\tau,u)=(\tau+H(u),u)\). Then
\[
 H(\widetilde f\circ\Lambda_H)
 =J_{\Lambda_H}^{T}(H\widetilde f\circ\Lambda_H)J_{\Lambda_H}
 +(2L(\tau+H)+a)\,H_H
\]
in the four-dimensional lower-right block. For the full original chart \(\Phi_H=(\tau+H,b,\Theta)\), its correction is
\[
 (2L(\tau+H)+a)H_H+c_1H_{\Theta_1}+c_2H_{\Theta_2}
\]
in that block. The residual correction is already included in \(H_V\) in the Schur proof above. All25 entries of the full chain are also checked with arbitrary coordinate functions. Omitting either correction would falsely treat arbitrary polynomial coordinate changes as Hessian-determinant preserving.

## 3. Actual diagnostic chart and ideal-bypass counterexample

Use the previously verified/pinned toric normalization of Meng–Yang v2,
\[
 L=1,\quad a=13+2\beta,\quad c=2(y,2x),\qquad
 \beta=y^2(6x^2y^2+15xy+4).
\]
The normalized starting source really has constant full Hessian determinant128: in the general \(L\) version it is \(2L\det D_b(2y,4x)^2=128L\), independently of \(a(x,y)\). This is separately verified in the fresh checker. The later nonlinear residual chart need not preserve it.
For \(h=(1-x)/2\), define
\[
 D(x)=\left[
 \begin{pmatrix}1&h\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-h&1\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}1&h\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
 \right]^2,\qquad
 T=(\rho+\eta^2,\ \eta+(\rho+\eta^2)^2).
\]
Then \(\det D=1\), \(D(1)=I\), \(D(-1)=-I\), and \(\Theta=D T\) is a global polynomial automorphism. Its inverse is \(T=D^{-1}R_0\), followed by \(\eta=T_2-T_1^2,\rho=T_1-\eta^2\).

At \(\sigma_*=-19/2\), the baseline envelope
\[
 \psi_* =c\Theta-(\beta+45/4)^2
\]
has the actual reduced collision
\[
 q_\pm=(\pm1,\mp3/2,0,0),\qquad
 \nabla\psi_*(q_\pm)=(0,0,-3,4).
\]
The affine repair \(R_{\rm rep}=\rho\), with \(H=\rho/\sigma_*\), is legal by Section1, preserves the collision with gradient \((0,0,-2,4)\), and gives
\[
 (\psi_*+\rho)_R|_{x=y=0}=(1,0).
\]
Thus its residual-gradient ideal is not \((x,y)\). This proves that legal source transformations **moving the old pivot** need not satisfy the fixed-old-pivot source ideal gate. It does not supply constant determinant: the baseline and affine-repaired pair determinants are \(429397/4\) and \(-339587/4\).

The cycle14 all-\(\alpha\) minimal degree9 repair is also globally realizable by this construction and retains its collision. Its pair determinants are \(228311/2\) and \(-684619/8\). Its failure of the fixed-old-pivot ideal no longer prevents selected-level source realization; its actual determinant failure still prevents a HC4 result. Unrestricted degree9 tails remain unresolved.

The stronger full-source interface is impossible for **every** \(H\) with this fixed \(\Theta\), at any degree. Its \(W=c\Theta-a^2/4\) is independent of \(H\), and exact evaluation yields
\[
 \det H_W(q_+)=92073161/4,\qquad
 \det H_W(q_-)=92043809/4.
\]
Already these differ. Its degree34 term also persists:
\[
 (\det H_W)_{34}=-57024x^{16}y^{16}\eta^2.
\]
One can certify this leading term using the degree12 base top \(-36x^4y^8\), whose binary Hessian determinant is \(-456192x^6y^{14}\), times the degree14 fibre-minor top \(x^{10}y^2\eta^2/8\); all other top-degree possibilities were bounded in the cycle13 proof. The fresh checker verifies the two factors and the decisive differing point values; it does not rerun a full determinant expansion.

## 4. Published inheritance versus direct target construction

Meng–Yang v2 AppendixA lemma "Schur descent" (label lem:schur) starts from an old-pivot-affine potential \(tA+B\), a full bordered Hessian determinant \(c\ne0\), and the polynomial identity \(\det(H_B+sH_A)=0\) for all \(s,w\). These hypotheses make their quadratic corner repair preserve the full determinant; then the Schur identity transfers constancy to the lower-dimensional envelope. Their proposition "Odd-dimensional Schur-descent bridge" (label prop:bridge) additionally uses a Keller map affine in the named original source coordinate and explicitly says “no coordinate change is applied”.

These are essential hypotheses of that automatic inheritance theorem. An arbitrary nonlinear source automorphism need not preserve them. Our target-only realization is a separate construction with the target determinant still an obligation. Full five-variable constancy is a stronger search interface; it is necessary to invoke the published inheritance argument, but it is not logically necessary for a directly verified four-variable constant-Hessian polynomial with a collision.

**Scope correction.** The cycle14 residual-gradient theorem assumed the old base and **old pivot** were fixed. It remains true, as does charges_design9's degree≤6 theorem with its explicit origin-fibre membership premise. Any earlier shorthand extending its ideal to *all* legal source-coordinate charts is false by the affine repair above. No old proof or conditional result is retroactively erased.

## 5. Prior, frontier and downstream use

The critical-value/Schur identities and characteristic-polynomial nilpotence argument are standard algebra; no novelty is claimed for them in isolation. The useful programme deduction is the exact selected-level realization/strong-interface split for the actual collision chart, its concrete legal ideal-bypass counterexample, and the all-degree unchanged-zero-level obstruction. No exact prior statement of this combined interface has been located.

The target-only missing construction is explicit: find \(R_{\rm rep}\) with equal gradients at \(q_\pm\) and \(\det H(\psi_*+R_{\rm rep})\in\mathbb C^\times\). Legality and a polynomial critical section are then automatic at \(\sigma_*\ne0\). This is not a proof by renaming a conjecture; determinant compatibility remains unsolved. Searching repairs solely for source ideal membership would now discard legitimate target-only charts.

For the full-source interface, choose a different residual chart with \(\det H_W\) already a nonzero constant, then solve the additional integrable relative-nilpotence equation (4) while checking the actual collision. The present \(\Theta\) cannot be rescued at any degree by moving the pivot alone. Three-or-more residual shears and genuinely base-moving charts are outside this obstruction.
