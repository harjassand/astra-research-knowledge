## 3. A general quantum concentration principle

Fix local dimension d≥2 and constants κ>0, A≥1, B≥0. Let ρ_N be permutation invariant, with actual one-site marginal τ_N≥κI. Assume for every Hermitian one-site H with operator norm at most one and every real t,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}\rho_N\exp\!\left[t\sum_{i=1}^N(H_i-\operatorname{Tr}(\tau_NH)I)\right]
\le A e^{BNt^2}.
\]

Set L=e(B+2)(d²+1)/κ, q=r/N and y=Lq/(1−q). The [fresh theorem](../concentration_blind/CONCENTRATION_FROZEN_REPORT.txt) gives, for 2≤r<N and y<1,

\[
\chi_P^2(\rho_r\Vert\tau_N^{\otimes r})
=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_r^2\tau_N^{-\otimes r})-1
\le \frac{A^2y^2}{1-y}.
\]

For q≤1/(2L+1), both Petz chi-square and quantum relative entropy are at most 8A²L²q². At r=1 both are exactly zero. Thus every sublinear sequence has quadratic local divergence for uniform constants and fixed d. A separable mixture of two product states with opposite O(N^−1/2) biases attains quadratic Petz order; concentration does not imply entanglement.

The [originator proof](../collective_concentration/CONCENTRATION_PROOF_FROZEN.md) and the independently frozen fresh proof survive [separate exposed audits](collective_concentration_dual_audit_v1.md). The main mechanism is an entire scalar generating function along each Hermitian direction, Cauchy coefficient bounds, real symmetric tensor contraction, and the full complex local Gram matrix. Actual marginal centering removes weight one exactly. The fresh spherical contraction improves the finite bound over coordinate polarization. Replacing the complex Gram by a real covariance matrix is invalid in noncommuting cases.

The hypothesis concerns **all directions**, all real t and a faithful actual marginal. A finite list of measured variances or a mean Casimir does not supply it. It is model information unless acquired by a charged procedure. If B_N=V_N/N varies, the sufficient local window is r=o(N²/(N+V_N)); the baseline N cannot be dropped.

An independently derived, [dually audited](qudit_dual_audit_v1.md) sufficient Schur-law condition covers arbitrary sector states in fixed dimension: with λ a Young partition and K=N−dλ_d, a uniform subGaussian K/√N tail implies the same centered quadratic conclusion. ## 5. Critical Curie–Weiss local window

The model is the full-tensor Gibbs state ρ_N proportional to exp(2J²/N), with τ_N=I/2. The temperature normalization is fixed. Let

\[
\mu_2=\sqrt{12}\,\Gamma(5/4)/\Gamma(3/4).
\]

The [originator theorem](../curie_weiss/CRITICAL_PROOF_FROZEN.md) and [independent report](../critical_window_blind/CRITICAL_WINDOW_FROZEN_REPORT.txt) prove that centered Petz divergence, relative entropy and trace distance vanish exactly when r=o(√N). At r=1 they vanish identically. For 2≤r=o(√N),

\[
\chi_P^2\sim \mu_2^2r(r-1)/(6N),\quad
\tfrac12\|\rho_r-I/2^r\|_1=\Theta(r/\sqrt N),\quad
D=\Theta(r^2/N).
\]

At a nonzero critical r/√N, bounded cosine measurements give a strictly positive separation. When r/√N→∞, half-trace tends to one; the originator also proves D=Θ(r/√N), through r=N. Its stronger entropy upper uses permutation invariance and strong subadditivity to compare subsystem and global entropy deficits. Its lower uses a fixed scaled magnetization tail and a white Hoeffding bound. Integration explicitly checked these arguments in the [first audit](critical_window_originator_first_audit_v1.md). The fresh proof instead gives a logarithmic above-window KL lower; agreement on the window is not an independent proof of the stronger linear rate. The [dual audit](critical_window_dual_audit_v1.md) preserves that distinction. A further [independent exposed quantitative reconstruction](../critical_quantitative_audit/report.md), frozen before integration comments or the fresh critical report were read, also passes all the stronger regimes.

The concentration substitution retains the baseline N. Bounded witnesses, rather than a chi-square lower alone, establish trace/KL nonvanishing. Above the window the exact hypergeometric law avoids an invalid r=o(N) approximation.

The primary [Björnberg–Fröhlich–Ueltschi model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12834) supplies this Hamiltonian normalization and critical β=2, but its opened global limit is not the growing-local-marginal theorem.
