Q09 — Exact qubit Schur–Weyl orbit decomposition

For ρ_v=(I+δ v·σ)/2, let p±=(1±δ)/2 and q=p_-/p_+. The exact n-qubit decomposition is

  (C^2)^⊗n = ⊕_{L=n,n-2,...,n mod 2} V_L ⊗ C^{m_{n,L}},
  m_{n,L}=C(n,k)-C(n,k-1),  k=(n-L)/2,
  dim V_L=L+1,  dim(isotypic L)=(L+1)m_{n,L}.

Thus L has the parity of n; spin j=L/2 is integer for even n and half-integer for odd n. With J_v eigenvalues L/2-r, r=0,...,L, define

  Z_L(q)=∑_{r=0}^L q^r,
  τ_{Lv}=Z_L(q)^-1 q^(L/2-J_v),
  P_L=m_{n,L} p_+^n q^((n-L)/2) Z_L(q).

Then exactly ρ_v^⊗n=⊕_L P_L[τ_{Lv}⊗I_{m_{n,L}}/m_{n,L}]. For δ>0,

  P_L=m_{n,L}(p_+p_-)^((n-L)/2)(p_+^(L+1)-p_-^(L+1)) /δ;

at δ=0, P_L=m_{n,L}(L+1)2^-n and τ_{Lv}=I/(L+1). At δ=1, only L=n remains and its spin state is pure highest weight. The weights are independent of v and sum to one.

If a blockwise approximation preserves P_L and the mixed multiplicity register, orthogonal direct-sum additivity gives the exact global error

  T(ρ_v^⊗n,σ_v)=∑_L P_L T(τ_{Lv},\tilde{τ}_{Lv}).

If weights change to Q_L, add TV(P,Q); if only typical sectors are controlled, add the omitted P-mass. The full derivation and a general inequality are in [the work note](/Users/harjas/Documents/Codex/2026-10-08/use-the-astra-research-knowledge-repository-3/work/programmes/combinatorics_optimization/cycle3/qubit_compression/q09_spin_representation/RESULT.txt).

These formulas are finite-n identities. At the critical scale δ_n=a/√n, the exact spin-label law has the weak limit L/√n ⇒ noncentral-χ with 3 degrees of freedom and noncentrality a; this is a radial LAN limit, not by itself a quantum channel or whole-state trace-norm approximation. In this regime each τ_{Lv} remains mixed across O(√n) magnetic levels.

N07's Gaussian memory exponent uses affine dimension s=3 for a nonzero-radius qubit sphere in the three-dimensional traceless-Hermitian space, despite its two-dimensional manifold; using s=2 to claim Θ(ε^-1) is invalid. N07 applies only after a valid Gaussian comparison. N08's separate critical full-orbit transfer is conditional on its inherited tracial bridge, with remainder O(δ_n+n^-1/2), and requires ε_n√n→∞. Fixed δ>0 makes the local Gaussian radius √nδ diverge, outside the fixed-bounded-mean premise; N08's restricted block-algebra result does not itself imply the full-orbit transfer or unrestricted partial-collision LAN.

Primary LAN reference: [Guță–Kahn, *Local asymptotic normality for qubit states* (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.73.052108). Inherited exact and Gaussian records: `work/astra_prior/cards/P-qubit.txt`, `N07-gaussian-memory.txt`, and `N08-block-critical-gaussian.txt`.
