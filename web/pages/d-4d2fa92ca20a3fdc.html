COMPLETE CONSTANT-4 REGULARITY CLASSIFIES MODULAR COVARIANCE
Separate addendum to the immutable qubit baseline; baseline unchanged.
Origin: independently reconstructed from root's ancillary-frequency steering.
Status: exact finite-dimensional necessity proof; sufficiency uses the exact

1. Precise theorem

Let sigma>0 be a density matrix on a finite-dimensional B, and let G_H be a
bounded Heisenberg quantum Markov generator: exp(t G_H) is unital completely
positive, its Schrödinger adjoint G_S preserves sigma, and
 Gamma_sigma G_H = G_S Gamma_sigma,
where Gamma_sigma(X)=sigma^(1/2) X sigma^(1/2). No primitivity is required.
Put L_sigma = −Gamma_sigma^(-1/2) G_S Gamma_sigma^(1/2), a self-adjoint
nonnegative Hilbert-Schmidt operator fixing sqrt(sigma) at zero. For states rho,
 E_G,sigma(rho)=Tr[sqrt(rho) L_sigma(sqrt(rho))],
 J_G,sigma(rho)=−Tr[G_S(rho)(log(rho)−log(sigma))].

The following are equivalent, within the stated KMS-symmetric class:
 (a) G_H commutes with X -> sigma^(it) X sigma^(-it) for all real t.
 (b) G_H is GNS self-adjoint: Tr[sigma X^* G_H(Y)]
       = Tr[sigma G_H(X)^* Y] for all X,Y.
 (c) For every faithful qubit state tau and every faithful density rho_AB,
       J_{id_A tensor G,Sigma}(rho_AB) >= 4 E_{id_A tensor G,Sigma}(rho_AB),
       where Sigma=tau tensor sigma.
 (d) The inequality in (c) holds for every finite-dimensional faithful tau.

For the original channel question take G_H=Phi*−I. Then the definitions above
are exactly its genuine Poisson entropy production and weighted root energy.
No EB, positive channel spectrum, or compatibility premise is used here.

2. The exact Hessian obstruction at any faithful reference

Diagonalize a reference Sigma=diag(s_i)>0. For Hermitian h with
 Tr[sqrt(Sigma)h]=0, use the physical density perturbation
 rho_e=Sigma+e Gamma_Sigma^(1/2)(h), faithful for sufficiently small |e|.
Define matrix-unit diagonal superoperators R,M by
 R_ii=1/2, R_ij=(s_i s_j)^(1/4)/(sqrt(s_i)+sqrt(s_j)),
 M_ii=1, M_ij=sqrt(s_i s_j)(log(s_i)−log(s_j))/(s_i−s_j),
with continuous limits when s_i=s_j. The baseline Fréchet calculation gives
 J(rho_e)−4E(rho_e)=e^2 <h,D4 h>+O(e^3),
 D4=(LM+ML)/2−4RLR.

Let C={X:[X,Sigma]=0} be the full commutant, including degenerate blocks.
On C, R=I/2 and M=I, so the C-to-C block of D4 is identically zero, regardless
of whether L preserves C. D4 sqrt(Sigma)=0 as well. Therefore the hypothesized
inequality for all faithful rho implies D4>=0 on all Hermitian matrices:
the trace-tangent inequality extends by the zero direction sqrt(Sigma).

A positive semidefinite self-adjoint operator with zero C-to-C block must have
zero C-to-C-perpendicular block. This follows, for example, by testing u+t v
for arbitrary u in C and real t of either sign: its quadratic form has zero
constant term, so a nonzero linear term is impossible.

For any output matrix unit E_ij outside C, the multiplier relating this block
of D4 to the same block of L is
 a_ij=(1+M_ij)/2−2R_ij.
Write z=sqrt(s_i/s_j)>0. Then
 R_ij=sqrt(z)/(1+z), M_ij=2z log(z)/(z^2−1),
 M_ij/(4R_ij^2)=(z+1)log(z)/(2(z−1))>=1.
The last inequality is the elementary logarithmic-mean bound
 log(z)>=2(z−1)/(z+1) for z>=1, with its symmetric version for z<=1;
differentiate its difference or integrate its nonnegative derivative.
For s_i!=s_j, R_ij<1/2, and hence
 a_ij >= 2(R_ij−1/2)^2 >0.
Thus D4>=0 forces L(C) subset C. Degenerate matrix units belong to C, so no
nonzero-frequency assumption is smuggled in for a degenerate reference.

Consequence at one reference: constant4 requires commutant invariance at its
entropy Hessian. For a qubit this is also sufficient for the Hessian inequality:
all noncommutant units have the same R=r and M=m, and the remaining defect
block is (m−4r^2)L >=0. In dimension >=3, commutant invariance alone does not
resolve possible mixing between different nonzero frequencies; no such
unamplified sufficiency is claimed.

3. A faithful qubit reference detects every modular violation

Work in an eigenbasis sigma=diag(s_i). Failure of modular covariance means
there are matrix units E_ij,E_kl with distinct ratios s_i/s_j != s_k/s_l and
coefficient (L_sigma(E_ij))_kl !=0. The weighted conjugation uses nonzero
diagonal matrix-unit scalings and commutes with the modular operator; hence
the same criterion applies to G_H or G_S. This criterion remains valid with
degeneracies: ratios label entire modular eigenspaces, not single units.

Choose a faithful two-level diagonal tau with tau_a/tau_b=s_j/s_i and a!=b.
The unit U=E_ab tensor E_ij then commutes with Sigma=tau tensor sigma, since
 tau_a s_i = tau_b s_j.
Its Hermitian real and imaginary parts are both in C_Sigma and perpendicular
to sqrt(Sigma); the ancilla off-diagonal block ensures zero trace tangent.
The amplified weighted operator is exactly I_A tensor L_sigma, because the
Gamma_tau factors cancel through the untouched identity map. Its action on
U has (a,b)-block coefficient at E_kl outside C_Sigma, since
 tau_a s_k/(tau_b s_l) !=1.
The adjoint term U^* has ancilla block (b,a), distinct from (a,b), so it cannot
cancel this component. Consequently the real Hermitian input U+U^* already
has a nonzero output in C_Sigma-perpendicular (and the imaginary input is
available as an independent control if desired).

Section 2 says such a cross block is impossible under (c). This proves
 (c) => (a), using only dimension-two faithful untouched references.
Conversely, for every nonmodular KMS generator this construction gives a
strict C4 violation: choose the commuting input u and a Hermitian output
test v with <v,D4u>!=0; a sufficiently small signed t makes
 <u+t v,D4(u+t v)> <0. Then a sufficiently small physical density perturbation
 e gives J(rho_e)<4E(rho_e). This is a constructive exact existence proof.

4. GNS equivalence and primitive regularization

On matrix units the GNS metric has weight s_j and the KMS metric has weight
sqrt(s_i s_j). Their ratio is sqrt(s_j/s_i), a strictly monotone function of
the modular eigenvalue s_i/s_j. KMS self-adjointness plus modular covariance
therefore gives GNS self-adjointness. Conversely, simultaneous self-adjointness
for the two metrics implies commutation with their ratio operator, hence with
the modular group. This proves (a) <=> (b) without a source convention guess.

The violation is not confined to a nonprimitive product generator. Fix the
faithful rho_AB giving a strict negative J−4E. Let R_Sigma,H(X)=Tr(Sigma X)I
be the replacement map in the Heisenberg picture and add
 delta (R_Sigma,H−I), delta>0, to the amplified generator. This is a genuine
GNS/KMS-symmetric Markov generator with the same reference Sigma. Its weighted
Dirichlet operator adds delta (I−|sqrt(Sigma)><sqrt(Sigma)|), making the total
fixed space one-dimensional and the reference faithful, hence primitive.
Both J and E at the fixed rho are linear in the generator. By strictness,
every sufficiently small delta preserves J−4E<0. Thus every nonmodular finite
KMS generator produces a primitive KMS counterexample after an untouched
faithful qubit extension and arbitrarily small reference replacement.

6. Scope

This classifies the complete p=1 constant4 property, not the entire strong
Lp family. Within KMS generators, GNS implies the entire known strong Lp
family; necessity here uses only its p=1 endpoint. The qubit baseline violates
constant4 without any ancilla. A modular-but-non-GNS distinction does not
arise within the KMS class by Section 4. The theorem does not settle complete
or unamplified constant2, or existence of any universal smaller c>0. Neither
weighted C4 broadcasting comparison nor an existential EB conclusion is proved
or refuted by this classification alone.
