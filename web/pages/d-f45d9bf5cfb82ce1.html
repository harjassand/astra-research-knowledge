Banked results
--------------

1. `regular_su3_unrestricted_eb.txt`: exact P_27 symmetric SU(3) broadcaster
   on the 8-dimensional adjoint carrier. The coherent root-orbit EB channel
   attains `I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi_27)` and `(I-Psi)^2 <= 2(I-Phi_27)` on the
   entire normalized-trace operator space. Twirled-separable-Choi bounds
   show that no full-domain EB channel can use a smaller linear coefficient;
   equality occurs on 8_a. `../cycle4/multiplicity_blocks_check.py` and
   `su3_full_space_check.py` passed; `../cycle4/multiplicity_blocks.txt`
   §6 contains an independent audit of the unrestricted twirl and sharp seed
   bound. This is one fixed channel, not a vanishing-defect sequence or a
   resolution of the global bounded-Holevo implication.

2. `common_measurement_gate.txt`: exact matched commuting and Clifford score
   frames in the same matrix algebra have the same information center,
   Holevo and D_max radii, and full covariance spectrum, but common-POVM
   deficiencies 0 and a^2(1-1/m). This refutes entropy-center/covariance-only
   truncation. The Clifford frame has a strictly positive actual-compatible
   residual lower bound, so it is not a target counterexample. The exact
   d=16 replay is `common_measurement_check.py`; it checks only that finite
   fixture.

3. `acquired_complexity_transfer.txt`: a supplied rational eps-net and
   full-domain broadcaster yield

     e_EB(E)<=min{1,2 epsbar+inf_K[phi(K) beta_R+sqrt(Gamma(R)/(2K))]},

   with Gamma(R)=max_p (sum sqrt(p_i))^2 chi_p, directly measured net error
   beta_R, and full acquisition/precision/decoder costs. It gives the
   sufficient net gate epsbar->0 and beta_R log N->0 but does not remove the
   metric-complexity term.
