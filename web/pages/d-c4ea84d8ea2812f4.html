# Cycle 2 initial mathematical audit

## Effective versus existential finite realization

The previous compact-minor argument selects finite current and voltage spaces by an open cover of the compact normalized operator secant/tangent set. It supplies existence only. No bound on their dimensions, coefficient magnitudes, conditioning, optimization cost, acquisition precision, or Galerkin/electrode discretization error follows from that cover. A computation must define and charge its actual spaces; it cannot call them the spaces supplied effectively by this theorem.

## Independent variational matrix identities

For a fixed conforming trial basis with constants removed, stiffness matrices `K(θ)=K0+Σθj Kj` are symmetric positive definite on an admitted parameter rectangle. If the boundary-current load matrix is B and the same basis is used to test voltages, the finite response is `A(θ)=BᵀK(θ)^(-1)B`. Input and output bases may differ: then use `CᵀK(θ)^(-1)B` with independently declared C. Reciprocal symmetry applies only to matched bases.

Differentiating `K^(-1)K=I` gives

`∂j A(θ)=-BᵀK(θ)^(-1)Kj K(θ)^(-1)B`.

The exact resolvent identity gives

`A(θ)-A(η)=BᵀK(θ)^(-1)[K(η)-K(θ)]K(η)^(-1)B`.

The two inverses differ in a secant; replacing both by the same inverse gives a derivative approximation only. This is a decisive algebraic test for any nonradial implementation.

## Secant diagnostics are not global certificates

For a fixed design D define `κ(D)=inf_{θ≠η in K} ||Y_D(θ)-Y_D(η)||/||θ-η||`. A finite list of tested pairs computes an upper bound on κ, because it minimizes over a subset. A positive tested minimum establishes no lower bound on unseen secants. A finite-grid minimum Jacobian singular value is similarly an upper bound on the continuous local minimum.

Even an exact uniform local singular-value lower bound alone does not certify global inverse Lipschitz behavior. For example `Y(t)=(cos t,sin t)` has derivative norm one everywhere but identical endpoint values on `[0,2π]`. Its restriction to `[0,2π-δ]` is injective yet its endpoint secant ratio tends to zero as δ→0. Local conditioning and long secants must be reported separately. A global lower certificate needs an additional mechanism, such as proved monotonicity of a sufficient statistic, a global secant factorization with certified bounds, or rigorous covering plus continuity estimates on a normalized secant compactification.

## Equal-cost nonradial controls

For currents G with columns g_j and output tests Q, the forward experiment is `Y_j(θ)=Q A(θ)g_j`. Total current energy is `||G||_F²`. The number of acquired scalars is the sum of declared output-test counts across physical experiments. With independent N(0,σ²) scalar noise its Gaussian Fisher information for θ is

`I_{kl}(θ)=σ^(-2)Σ_j <Q ∂kA(θ)g_j,Q ∂lA(θ)g_j>`.

The radial per-mode sufficient-statistic collapse uses a common diagonalizing basis. It does not extend automatically to noncommuting response matrices. An optimized multi-current comparator therefore remains necessary in the nonradial case. If its output tests differ by experiment, the actual Q_j must appear in this formula and in detector accounting. Merely equalizing input energy is insufficient to equalize the statistical experiment.

With common fixed Q and equal independent noise, the local information depends on G only through `S=GGᵀ`:

`I_{kl}(θ)=σ^(-2)tr[S (∂kA(θ))ᵀQᵀQ∂lA(θ)]`.

Here `S≥0`, `tr S=1` fixes energy, and `rank S≤J` restricts the number of currents. Single-current designs have rank one. For a declared finite training set, maximizing the minimum eigenvalue of I over S is a semidefinite problem if the rank restriction is absent or automatically feasible. A factorization of the optimized S realizes the corresponding nonadaptive multi-current design. This is a competent training-only comparator, but must retain detector/current charges and does not turn the local information objective into a global stability proof. Changing Q between experiments invalidates this single-S reduction.

At the whole-experiment level, common-Q designs G and H with the same GGᵀ=HHᵀ are related, after zero-column padding if needed, by a right orthogonal transformation. Right-rotating the matrix data preserves independent equal-variance Gaussian noise and rotates the mean accordingly. Padded zero columns carry ancillary noise. Thus S characterizes this ideal Gaussian experiment's information, subject to the retained acquisition and physical-current costs. This broader covariance reduction does not generally yield a rank-one representative; the radial model's stronger collapse relied on simultaneous diagonalization.

Freezing must declare which distinct output modes each current observes, whether repeated readouts are permitted, and whether readout noise is independent per acquisition. Otherwise repeated measurements can alter variance without a stated cost. Charge boundary input modes, output modes, scalar observations, number/duration of currents, forward/derivative evaluations, design-search budget, and reconstruction evaluations separately. Both optimized controls may use the same known model/range and training grid, never heldout truth/noise.

## Discretization consequence

Suppose an independently certified uniform measurement error `||Y_exact(θ)-Y_h(θ)||≤δ_h` is available. Then, for every pair,

`||Y_exact(θ)-Y_exact(η)||≥||Y_h(θ)-Y_h(η)||-2δ_h`.

After division by `||θ-η||`, this alone loses control as parameters approach one another. A uniform map approximation does not transfer a global inverse Lipschitz constant by simple subtraction. Transferring small-secant stability requires a derivative/normalized-secant approximation bound, or a separate local analysis. Mesh agreement gives an empirical difference between approximations; without a tail/error theorem it is not a bound on the exact PDE discrepancy.

For reconstruction only, a rigorous uniform δ_h can be charged as extra model error. If exact data are fitted to Y_h and the exact or approximate admitted map has proved κ, the triangle argument includes `2δ_h` in addition to detector noise and optimizer slack. The orientation and choice of κ must be explicit. Quadrature and roundoff errors are additional terms unless certified negligible by an independent bound.
