SUPPLEMENT — SHARPENED POINTWISE MIXED H BOUND
==============================================
Outcome: the proposed h(M) bound is valid, including M=0 and M=1, for
arbitrary mixed cofactor vectors with overlapping supports. No counterexample
found.

Let M=M_i=max_j nu_{ji}, c=c_i>0, Q_i=Q(c-e_i), and
Delta_i=Q(c)-Q_i. Write the unweighted i-contribution as

  h_i(c)=c*Delta_i/[(1+Q_i)(1+Q)].

All coefficients zeta_j and all falling-factorial monomials are
nonnegative. Partition the active channel sum by its i-th order; overlap in
the other coordinates does not alter this disjoint partition.

If M=0, every channel has nu_{ji}=0. Then Q(c)=Q_i and h_i=0, so h(0)=0.

If 1<=c<=M, let Q_< be the sum of channels with nu_{ji}<c and Q_= the
sum with nu_{ji}=c. Channels with nu_{ji}>c vanish both before and after
the death. The threshold channels have zero post-death propensity, hence

  Q_i=Q_<,  Q=Q_<+Q_=.

For nu_{ji}<c, the falling-factorial identity gives

  phi_j(c)-phi_j(c-e_i)
    = [nu_{ji}/(c-nu_{ji})]phi_j(c-e_i)
    <= M phi_j(c-e_i).

Consequently the surviving-channel part of Delta_i is at most M Q_i.
The threshold-channel part is exactly Q_=, so

  h_i(c)
   <= c*M*Q_i/[(1+Q_i)(1+Q)]
      +c*Q_=/[(1+Q_i)(1+Q)]
   <= c*M/4+c.

For the first term, Q>=Q_i implies
Q_i/[(1+Q_i)(1+Q)]<=Q_i/(1+Q_i)^2<=1/4. For the second,
Q_<=Q and the denominator is at least 1+Q, so its normalized fraction is
at most 1. Maximizing over 1<=c<=M gives

  h_i(c)<=M^2/4+M.

If c>M, every active channel has nu_{ji}<=M<c. For each such term,

  phi_j(c)-phi_j(c-e_i)
    = [nu_{ji}/(c-nu_{ji})]phi_j(c-e_i)
    <= [M/(c-M)]phi_j(c-e_i).

After summing, Delta_i<=M Q_i/(c-M). Again Q>=Q_i, so

  h_i(c)
   <= [cM/(c-M)] Q_i/[(1+Q_i)(1+Q)]
   <= cM/[4(c-M)]
   <= M(M+1)/4
   <= M^2/4+M.

The penultimate bound uses integer c>=M+1, for which c/(c-M) is largest
at c=M+1.

The special case M=1 improves the generic formula. At c=1, channels with
nu_{ji}=0 have zero death difference and channels with nu_{ji}=1 are the
threshold part; hence h_i(1)<=1. At c>=2, the safe bound above gives
h_i(c)<=c/[4(c-1)]<=1/2. Thus h(1)=1 is valid. Combining cases proves

  h_i(c)<=h(M_i),
  h(0)=0, h(1)=1, h(M)=M+M^2/4 for M>=2,

and therefore H(c)<=sum_i mu_i*h(M_i).

The overlap check is structural: all grouping is by the single coordinate
order nu_{ji}; every channel belongs to exactly one of nu<c, nu=c, nu>c.
Other-coordinate zeros only remove nonnegative summands, and shared support
or repeated cofactor monomials cannot increase the bound. The sharpened
constant is a valid uniform bound; the proof does not claim it is always
attained for a fixed rate vector or fixed cofactor list.
