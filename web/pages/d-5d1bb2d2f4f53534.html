SECOND COVARIANT THEOREM: COMPACT SYMPLECTIC CHANNELS
c04_s01 independent derivation, 7 October 2026.
No cycle6 peer result used. Not part of frozen INITIAL.

For even d>=2, fix a real orthogonal skew matrix J with J^2=-I and let
Theta(A)=J A^T J*. Consider a bistochastic UCP channel commuting with
conjugation by the compact symplectic group Sp(d/2). The traceless Hermitian
space splits into the Theta-minus Lie-algebra irrep and the Theta-plus
complement, with ranks
 r_minus=d(d+1)/2,
 r_plus=d(d-1)/2-1=(d-2)(d+1)/2.
For d>=4 these are irreducible distinct components; the channel's HS
eigenvalues are lambda_minus,lambda_plus. For d2 the plus component is
absent and this is simply the qubit depolarizing case.

If the channel is selfcompatible, then BOTH eigenvalues satisfy
        lambda_± <= (d+2)/[2(d+1)].                       (S1)
In particular positive HS channels in this class admit the ordinary Haar
canonical EB channel E(A)=[A+Tr(A)I]/(d+1) as a c=2 comparator:
        I-E <=2(I-Phi).
No search for a POVM is required to describe E; its finite implementation
cost is the established projective-design/finite-POVM cost, not dimension
independent. This result does not retain slow nonclassical eigenmodes,
since (S1) bounds all traceless eigenvalues away from one. It is therefore
less decisive for the unrestricted slow-space problem than the signed-
permutation theorem, but a separate all-dimensional exact subclass.

PROOF OF (S1)
The selected-basis star witnesses, before a unitary conjugation on the
input, are
 K_minus=(d(P_RB+P_RC)-(F_J,RB+F_J,RC))/2,
 K_plus =(d(P_RB+P_RC)+(F_J,RB+F_J,RC))/2-(2/d)I,
where F_J=(I tensor J)Swap(I tensor J*) is a Hermitian involution. Conjugate
the input R by J. This sends both maximally entangled projectors to the
antisymmetric normalized singlet projectors Q_RB,Q_RC and sends both F_J
operators to ordinary swaps. Thus the witnesses become
 K_minus=(dQ-S)/2,
 K_plus =(dQ+S)/2-(2/d)I.
The Q contractions are represented by x1(v)=J_RB tensor v_C,
x2(v)=J_RC tensor v_B, x3(v)=v_R tensor J_BC. In the ordered basis their
Gram matrix has diagonal d and off-diagonal (12,13,23)=(1,-1,1), positive
for d>=4. Direct index contraction gives
 dQ=[[d,1,-1],[1,d,1],[0,0,0]],
 S =[[-1,0,-1],[0,-1,1],[-1,1,0]].                       (S2)
The contraction span and its orthogonal complement are invariant; on the
complement Q vanishes and ||S||<=2.

K_minus on the contraction span has eigenvalues (d+2)/2,d/2,0, so
 K_minus <=(d+2)/2 I globally.
For K_plus, x1+x2 has eigenvalue d/2-2/d. Its remaining two eigenvalues are
 [d-2 +/-sqrt((d-2)^2+16)]/4-2/d.
The upper one is <=d/2-2/d since
 (d+2)^2-[(d-2)^2+16]=8(d-2)>=0.
On the complement K_plus<=1-2/d<=d/2-2/d. Consequently
 K_plus<= (d/2-2/d)I.
Taking expectations in the normalized compatible Choi state yields
 2r_minus lambda_minus/d <=(d+2)/2,
 2r_plus lambda_plus/d <=d/2-2/d,
which simplify to (S1). d2 is handled by the absent-plus depolarizing
calculation (or ordinary universal cloner witness).
Finally E has every traceless eigenvalue 1/(d+1); hence
 1-1/(d+1)<=2(1-lambda_±), proving the comparison. Positivity and exact
selfadjointness of E give the usual squared-displacement trace modulus.
Tensor and mixture closure from the baseline still require positive Phi.
