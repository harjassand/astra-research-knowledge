# Cycle 8 source and provenance notes

Date: 2026-10-08. This is a scoped proof/prior audit, not an exhaustive literature review. The universal all-Q claim remains open in this work package; no novelty or priority claim is made.

## Almost-commuting normalized-HS results

- Bansil and Kachkovskiy, [*On almost commuting matrices with respect to the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm*, arXiv:2608.31000v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31000), Theorems 1.1 and 1.3 and Corollary 1.4. Their pair theorem has a dimension-free modulus. The many-observable estimates retain explicit tuple-size dependence (including a \(\sqrt N\) term and an all-N modulus whose exponent deteriorates with N). This does not furnish a modulus uniform for a channel image with up to \(d^2-1\) directions.
- Glebsky, [*Almost commuting matrices with respect to normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm*, arXiv:1002.3082](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.3082), Theorem 4. It gives stability for each fixed finite self-adjoint tuple size; the recursively defined modulus depends on that size.
- Houdayer and Ioana, [tracial-ultraproduct lifting result, arXiv:2309.15029](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15029), Theorem 5.1. The stated lifting concerns separable abelian subalgebras in a 2-independent pair. It is not a uniform finite-coordinate measurement for a growing, potentially nonseparable channel range.

These are positive fixed-family results. Their stated scopes neither prove nor disprove a stronger uniform-range theorem tailored to channels. The missing step is explicitly a quantitative finite reconstruction controlling the *whole* relevant range or every whole weighted frame with one POVM.

## Quantum channel / EB representation sources

- Horodecki, Shor, and Ruskai, [*Entanglement Breaking Channels*, arXiv:quant-ph/0302031](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0302031). Standard Holevo measure-and-prepare and separable-Choi representations are used in the C7 full-domain canonical reduction. The present PSD Gram identity and identity-slack unitalization are internally derived; this source is not cited as proving those order steps.
- Haapasalo, [*Compatibility of covariant quantum channels with emphasis on Weyl symmetry*, arXiv:1901.06113](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.06113), finite-phase-space Corollary 2 and Eq. (22). Supplies the Weyl-channel compatibility-kernel criterion used by the already-known exact d=5 compatible channel. It does not establish the EB domination target.
- Chen, Ji, Kribs, Lütkenhaus, and Zeng, [*Symmetric extension of two-qubit states*, arXiv:1310.3530](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.3530). Context for symmetric-extension tests. No solver relaxation of their state criteria is treated here as an all-EB theorem.
- Keyl and Werner, [*Optimal cloning of pure states, judging single clones*, arXiv:quant-ph/9807010](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9807010). Source context for the universal cloner and the four-MUB depolarizing comparator boundary; the current qutrit inequalities are checked directly.
