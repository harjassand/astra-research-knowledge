QUANTUM CAPABILITY — CYCLE 4 QUANTITATIVE TENSOR-HAAR GATE
2026-10-08

Result and status

The finite U(N) construction supports a conditional pairwise
minimum-output-entropy violation for Phi_N and its complex-conjugate channel
bar(Phi_N). Bordenave–Collins Lemma 9.3 is an expectation bound and cannot
itself be union-bounded over the coefficient net by p-Markov. Their separate
Haar Lipschitz-concentration lemma (Lemma 7.4) supplies the required
exponential upper tail around that expectation. After degree-2 linearization
(Lemma 8.1), the score polynomial is additive over 130 independent generator
matrices, so the concentration step has a direct product-group Lipschitz
proof.

The earlier claim applying the 2026 paper's real-orthogonal Bell quotient to
Phi_N tensor Phi_N with complex Haar U(N) branches is withdrawn: that
transfer is false as an exact collision argument. The precise repair uses
Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N), whose Bell branch vectors are
vec(W_s W_t*)/sqrt(N^2) and are indexed by the same relative-operation
fibres. The finite trace and entropy transfer are recorded in
evidence/PRODUCT_CONJUGATE_REPAIR.txt. A standard flagged direct-sum channel
then gives a self-tensor witness with one extra input flag qubit per use.

The reconstructed constant c_E<=64 exp(5/2)<780 and an explicit even p in
evidence/rough_gate.py give score-net failure at most 1/3. The product-
conjugate Bell entropy bound is exact for every finite representation, so
no Bell-trace approximation is required. At d=65, K=260, score slack
s=0.165, and N=N_0 p^836 from the joint parameter replay, the finite Haar
sample has the score property, and hence the pairwise nonadditivity witness,
with probability at least 2/3 conditional on the free-radius bound. The
resulting von Neumann gap is at least 0.0010070146 nats. This is finite
probabilistic existence, not an explicit seed, efficient finite-precision sampler,
circuit, or practical channel. The free-radius bound C_r^2=16r^2-7r=50 at
r=2 is an upper bound, not an exact-radius claim. The source's sector proof
has been reconstructed for the cycle4 interface in
evidence/FREE_RADIUS_AUDIT.txt; it remains internally audited rather than
externally validated. Novelty is unknown.

Quantitative upper-tail argument

Fix a finite eta-net N_eta in the real unit sphere of traceless Hermitian
K-by-K matrices. Its real dimension is K^2-1=67,599. For each A in the net,
Lemma 8.1 of Bordenave–Collins converts P_A (degree at most 2) to a
degree-1 polynomial Q_A with coefficient matrices in M_522 and a scalar
theta_A >= 0 such that, in every group representation,

  ||P_A|| = ||Q_A||^2 - theta_A,
  theta_A <= 261 ||P_A,free||,
  ||Q_A,free||^2 = ||P_A,free|| + theta_A <= 262 C.

The coefficient dimension is 522=2|B_1|, since |B_1|=1+2*65*2=261 and
the original coefficients of P_A are scalar.

Bordenave–Collins Lemma 9.3's product-leg interpolation, re-run from
Theorem 5.1's ell_0 moment gate, gives for d=65, k=2 and coefficient
dimension n=522, for even p within that gate and N>=65^70,

  E ||Q_A,N|| <= R(N,p) ||Q_A,free||,
  R(N,p) = 1044^(3/p) N^(3/p) p^(9/p) (1+c_E/sqrt(N))^2,

The source theorem does not print c_E, but the specialized unitary
Weingarten/path proof gives c_E <= 64 exp(5/2) < 780; see

For the concentration step, each coefficient b_(i,j) of Q_A obeys
||b_(i,j)|| <= ||Q_A,free|| by Fourier coefficient extraction using the
canonical trace. The function (U_(i,j)) -> ||Q_A,N|| is therefore
L-Lipschitz for the L2-sum Hilbert–Schmidt metric with
L <= 2 sqrt(dk) ||Q_A,free||. Meckes Theorem 5.17 gives the one-sided bound

  P(||Q_A,N|| >= E||Q_A,N|| + t ||Q_A,free||)
    <= exp(-(N-2)t^2/(96dk)).

This is a real tail inequality; it does not infer a p-th moment from
Lemma 9.3. Haar U(N) is directly covered by both the Weingarten estimate
and product-group concentration.

For a total score slack s=0.165 nats, choose

  eta = s/(2K),
  e = s/(6*262*C).

If R(N,p) <= 1+e/2 and the concentration event has deviation at most e/2,
then ||Q_A,N|| <= (1+e)||Q_A,free||. Since e<1,

  ||P_A,N|| <= ||P_A,free|| + (2e+e^2)262C

||P_A,N-P_B,N|| <= sum_st |A_st-B_st| <= K||A-B||_F.
Thus the eta-net extends the bound to every A with

  Gamma_K,N <= C+s.

The net size obeys log |N_eta| <= (K^2-1) log(1+2/eta). At the selected

The right side has log2 N=66.86071... at the selected parameters. It is

Product-conjugate Bell-entropy transfer and gap

For the maximally entangled input to Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N), the
complementary Stinespring branch vector is
vec(W_s W_t*)/sqrt(N^2)=vec(pi_N(s t^-1))/sqrt(N^2). Grouping branch pairs
by g=s t^-1 gives an exact quotient Gram matrix B_N=D_q^(1/2)R_ND_q^(1/2),
where R_N is a Hermitian positive semidefinite correlation matrix and
diag(B_N)=q. The output is unitarily equivalent up to zero padding to B_N.
Schur-Horn implies q is majorized by the eigenvalue vector of B_N; therefore
its entropy is at most H(q), for every finite representation. The fibre map
has the source's profile after inverting both branch labels. No Haar trace
estimate or Bell continuity bound is needed. This exact argument does not
establish the same quotient for Phi_N tensor Phi_N itself.

At score bound C+s, the one-copy entropy lower bound is
5.377204724658078... nats. Since the Bell entropy is at most H(q)=
10.753402434708715... nats for every finite representation, the pairwise
gap is at least 0.001007014607441300... nats, with no continuity debit.

Uniform-net removal check

One can put all Q_A blocks into a block-diagonal polynomial and apply one
concentration inequality to the maximum. To retain the relative estimate
needed after subtracting theta_A, each block must be normalized by
||Q_A,free||. The coefficient dimension then becomes 522*|N_eta|, so the
expectation factor pays 3 log |N_eta|/p instead of paying the net only in
the concentration tail. This removes a probability union bound but does
not remove the coefficient net or improve the leading dimension scale;
the direct union-tail route is smaller. No operator-valued/SOS identity
controlling the product-vector radius without a net was found.

Cycle-4 structural obstruction checks (2026-10-08)

Two proposed routes toward an explicit or net-free strong-radius certificate
were tested and closed only in their stated architectures. First, no PSD/SOS
majorant on the entire traceless Hilbert-Schmidt sphere can certify the selected
purity target: the local branch sum has exact finite-N norm at least
130(1-1/(N^2-1))>=260/3 on a one-leg traceless sector, against the required
full-space sufficient bound below 17.674. This does not refute the pure-input
score gate, because those high-rank directions do not carry the same weight on
the rank-one input variety; a Schmidt-aware/product-vector certificate remains
open. Second, the left regular representation of any nontrivial finite quotient
of F_65 x F_65, after deleting only its global constant, has score radius at
least sqrt(130*129), far above the selected target. The proof uses a fixed
mean-zero vector when one factor image is proper, and a central-irrep phase
witness otherwise. Thus increasing quotient size or girth cannot repair this
regular-minus-constants construction. Mixed augmentation, nonregular
representations, and independently designed algebraic unitaries remain open.
Exact derivations, scope, and costs are in evidence/common_score_certificate_route.txt
and evidence/deterministic_representation_route.txt; claim statuses are added
to CLAIMS.json. In particular, the regular group adjacency estimate
4*sqrt(k-1) does not by itself bound the full doubled adjoint representation
when only the global identity is removed, because local-identity tensor
traceless sectors remain.

Pure-input reduction, mixed augmentation, and significance calibration

The pure-input identity and Schmidt-sector split are in
evidence/pure_input_sector_route.txt. For every pure input, each local
identity-tensor-traceless sector has relative squared weight at most
1/(N+1), with post-branch local contribution at most
8*130^2(P-1/N)/N. Thus the full-HS local-sector obstruction cannot be used
with unit weight against a pure input. The remaining correlation sector
C=(Pi_0 tensor Pi_0)rho can have norm near one, so the exact reduction does
not itself close the score gate; a sharp bound on its rank-one-constrained
quadratic form is still missing.

The straightforward mixed-augmentation quotient repair is also falsified in
a matched-factor architecture. For q_1=q and q_2=q composed with the free
generator-inversion automorphism, a diagonal mean-zero Bell vector is fixed
by 130 matched cross-factor relative operators. A normalized 260-entry
coefficient witness gives Gamma_260>=sqrt(260), independent of quotient size
or girth; see evidence/mixed_augmentation_route.txt. This does not rule out
independent quotient pairs or selected nonregular representations.

Significance is limited by the published frontier. Zhen et al.,
arXiv:2609.18747v1, already prove an almost-one-bit same-channel Bell gap
simultaneously at all Renyi orders in a real-orthogonal model, with K=O(eps^-3)
and a K=320 example. Zhen, Chen, and Wang, arXiv:2610.06533v1, already give
an explicit real-algebraic p=1 Bell witness with output dimension 256 and
input exponent 37,608,898 qubits. This cycle does not discover additivity
violation or the free-product Bell-collision mechanism. Its narrower
conditional/internal increment is a complex-U product-conjugate quotient and
a numerical finite Haar-U p=1 threshold, without a materialized matrix seed
or practical implementation. The bounded source comparison is in
evidence/significance_comparison.txt; novelty and priority remain unknown.

The quotient obstruction has a broader nonregular form. If two factor carriers
share a matched conjugate-equivalent invariant constituent on m cross-factor
branch pairs, a maximally entangled vector on that constituent yields
Gamma>=sqrt(2m); m>=27 already exceeds the selected target. See
evidence/shared_constituent_extension.txt. Thus a candidate mixed tensor
representation must avoid enough synchronized common constituents and still
prove the full coefficient-sphere upper bound. This necessary condition does
not provide a positive construction.
