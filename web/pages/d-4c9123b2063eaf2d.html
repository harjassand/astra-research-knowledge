## A1. Interface and the question left by the source

The actual register has N qubits, one logical qubit, and original unsigned commuting CSS checks C_a. Write

    H0=sum_(a=1)^m h_a(I-C_a)/2,
    0<hmin<=h_a<=hmax,
    H=H0-sum_i J_i X_i, 0<J_i<=J,
    v=sum_i J_i<=NJ.                                      (A1)

Every qubit meets both check colors. Check arity and incidence are at most7. The actual qubit graph is connected and has degree at most42. These are the admitted source-template conclusions, not properties asserted for every CSS code. The unperturbed ground projector P0 has rank2. Actual check matrices and coefficient encodings are input; geometric existence alone does not provide a fabricated physical register.

The acquisition target is a state within trace norm epsilon of the **actual new Gibbs state** rho=e^(-beta H)/Z_H, and ultimately the old-decoder thermal encoder D^*(tau_L tensor rho_A)D, where rho_A=Tr_L D rho D^*. Approximate preparation must preserve any reference entangled with tau_L. The bath during storage stays the originally specified physical bath, with actual-H spectral components and paid rate envelope. Preparation is a separate active task.

BDL2605.10943v1, Outlook (source484--485), explicitly leaves efficient passive initialization open. Its suggested within-sector heating/cooling is not a proved mixing bound. Its footnote cites Bergamaschi--Gheissari--Liu's SODA2026 theorem, whose recoverable arXiv2507.10976v1 is dated15July2025. That theorem requires a bounded-degree metacheck syndrome network, individually erasable small syndrome components, local erasure, and amenability; the paper's general block implementation may be quasipolynomial even when these hypotheses hold. BDL explicitly says its metachecks fail the required LDPC hypothesis. No theorem applying it directly here is claimed.

## A2. A specific fixed-temperature obstruction to the old-code cold start

Greedily select an independent set I of the actual qubit graph. Its cardinality l satisfies l>=N/43. No original check meets two selected qubits. For each i in I, X_i anticommutes with a nonempty set s_i of original Z checks because both CSS colors occur. These sets are disjoint, and each has total penalty

    e_i=sum_(a in s_i) h_a <=g:=7hmax.

Their incidence vectors are therefore linearly independent. For every subset S of I, X_S maps the rank2 code space to a distinct legal syndrome eigenspace of H0 with energy sum_(i in S)e_i. This is a construction of actual valid syndromes; it does not substitute independent Bernoulli defects for the whole constrained syndrome law. Consequently

    Z0>=2 product_(i in I)(1+e^(-beta e_i))
       >=2(1+e^(-beta g))^l.                            (A2)

Each X_i has zero expectation in the unperturbed Gibbs state: it anticommutes with an original Z check commuting with rho0. Thus the convex function F(t)=log Tr exp[-beta(H0-t sum_i J_i X_i)] has F'(0)=0, and Z_H>=Z0 for t=1. This trace convexity is not a noncommuting operator-exponential order assertion.

Since H>=-v I, finite-dimensional spectral order gives exp(-beta H)<=e^(beta v)I. This last comparison is with a scalar; no comparison with exp(-beta H0) is used. Hence

    p_code,new:=Tr(P0 rho)
        <=2e^(beta v)/Z_H
        <=exp[beta NJ-l log(1+e^(-beta g))]
        <=exp[-N c(beta,J)],
    c(beta,J)=(1/43)log(1+e^(-7beta hmax))-beta J.         (A3)

When c(beta,J)>0, every state sigma supported in the old code space obeys

    ||sigma-rho||_1>=2[1-exp(-N c(beta,J))].             (A4)

The two-outcome measurement {P0,I-P0} proves(A4). It applies to any old-code mixture, not just one logical codeword. All fields may be strictly positive, and J may simultaneously satisfy the full-density tiny-activity witness condition. The conclusion is asymptotic in N at **fixed beta,J**. Its enormous constants do not prove a failure at the source's first admitted finite recursion level.

For the cycle15 canonical unit-penalty choice beta=253250 and J=2^(-5318278), c>0 follows using e<4, beta<2^18 and log(1+u)>=u/2 for0<=u<=1:

    (1/43)log(1+e^(-7beta)) >=2^(-3545507),
    beta J <2^(-5318260).

Thus even that literal positive-field parameter choice has(A4) for sufficiently large recursion depth. This is an obstruction to **this initializer**, not quantum preparation hardness, slow mixing for every channel, a physical-memory no-go, or a claim that all Gibbs samplers are inefficient.

## A3. A fully specified active cold window

Every valid syndrome of H0 has rank2 degeneracy. Parameterize them by r=N-1 independent generator signs. For syndrome s, energy is at least hmin|s| when counted among all m original checks. Therefore

    Z0/2=sum_(valid s)e^(-beta E(s))
         <=(1+e^(-beta hmin))^m,
    ||rho0-P0/2||_1
         =2(1-2/Z0)
         <=2[(1+e^(-beta hmin))^m-1].                    (A5)

The upper sum over all check bitstrings is legal here because it is only an upper bound; no sampler of those bitstrings is asserted.

For any finite-dimensional Hermitian perturbation V, Gibbs differentiation gives

    d rho_t/dt=-beta integral_0^1
       rho_t^u[V-Tr(rho_t V)]rho_t^(1-u)du.

Schatten Holder, ||rho_t^u||_(1/u)=1, and |Tr(rho_t V)|<=||V|| imply

    ||rho(H0+V)-rho0||_1<=2beta||V||.                   (A6)

This proof needs neither commutation nor an invalid exponential Loewner comparison. Apply it to V=-sum_i J_i X_i. For0<epsilon<=1, choose

    beta hmin>=log(16m/epsilon),
    v<=epsilon/(8beta), equivalently J<=epsilon/(8beta N).

Then m e^(-beta hmin)<=epsilon/16, and e^x-1<=2x for0<=x<=1/16. Equations(A5)--(A6) give

    ||rho-P0/2||_1<=epsilon/2.                          (A7)

Preparing P0/2 now requires no new-Gibbs service: exact symplectic Gaussian elimination of the supplied stabilizer rows produces a Clifford D0 taking the code space to one logical qubit tensor |0>^(N-1). Initialize the logical qubit maximally mixed and the other N-1 qubits in |0>, then apply D0^*. Conservative costs are O(N^3) binary operations, O(N^2) unrestricted Clifford gates, and O(N^3) two-qubit gates routed serially on any supplied connected degree42 graph (a path of length at mostN-1 suffices per gate). No quantum postselection, classical Gibbs draw, or protective mixing step occurs.

The output P0/2 is an epsilon/2 approximation of the NEW global Gibbs state in this parameter window. It is exact only as a preparation of P0/2; finite-temperature full Gibbs equality is not claimed.

Choose additionally beta hmin>=2b0 and J no larger than the cycle15 activity threshold gamma exp(-b0/gamma)/(e beta q_c). This places the cold window inside the proved new-Gibbs witness regime. It remains a different asymptotic regime from fixed-temperature/fixed-intensive initialization. In particular beta grows at least logarithmically with m/epsilon, and the total field norm vanishes with epsilon/beta. The uniform bath rate envelope and any control-temperature dependence still have to be paid.

For unit penalties, epsilon=2^(-B), and fixed admitted b0,gamma,q_c, a rational integer beta=O(b0+B+log(N+1)) can satisfy both temperature inequalities. Taking every J_i=2^(-L) with

    L>=B+3+ceil(log2(beta N))

and increasing L by the fixed source activity offset realizes all-positive fields exactly. For example use e<3 and ln2>2/3 to bound the additional threshold by

    L>=ceil(3b0/(2gamma))+ceil(log2(3beta q_c/gamma))+1.

The coefficient height is O(B+log N+log beta+b0/gamma) bits, including the enormous fixed activity constant. The input contains N such coefficients. No underflow or rounding to zero is permitted. Classical preprocessing and the ideal Clifford acquisition are polynomial in this input size, even though the fixed family constants are not practical.

## A4. Unknown input/reference and recovery

Let D be the old full-Hilbert decoder frame, with its ground-syndrome correction fixed to identity; if a fixed logical Pauli convention differs, pay that one Pauli. Then D=D0 on P0 up to that convention, and

    D(P0/2)D^*=I_L/2 tensor |0><0|_A.

For an arbitrary input tau_LR, an exact active code encoding is

    Enc_cold(tau_LR)=(D0^* tensor I_R)
          [tau_LR tensor |0><0|_A](D0 tensor I_R).        (A8)

No measurement is made on the unknown logical input. It preserves the reference exactly. The desired new-Gibbs auxiliary satisfies

    ||rho_A-|0><0|_A||_1<=||rho-P0/2||_1<=epsilon/2

by unitary invariance and partial-trace contraction. Because D0 and D agree on the ground support,

    ||Enc_cold-Enc_new||_diamond<=epsilon/2.             (A9)

The reference-safe inequality follows by tensoring the auxiliary difference with tau_LR and conjugating, not by separately checking unreferenced pure inputs. If an independently proved physical-storage theorem applies to Enc_new, CPTP contraction charges the initialization mismatch once; it does not grow like t. This does not acquire the missing full-frequency Davies leakage theorem or justify a different bath.

Recovery is the named old decoder channel: measure the original commuting checks at the END, compute the source correction with the repaired fixed base lookup, apply it, then use D0 to extract the logical qubit. Syndrome measurement at recovery implements the partial trace over the decoder auxiliary; it does not assert that measuring the NEW Gibbs state at initialization preserves its quantum auxiliary. The deterministic RG correction algorithm and giant fixed bottom lookup, workspace, routing and imperfect gates must be charged. If their polynomial cost is not supplied, the fallback is the explicit O(N2^N) syndrome-sign table used in cycle16. No efficient recovery service is inferred from the existence of the logical factor.

The preparation used for a Gibbs SAMPLE can be completed before exposing the unknown input. Direct(A8) inserts the input before encoding and requires a controlled noise budget throughout that interval. These alternatives have different operational debts.

## A5. Physical control cost is not a free Gibbs service

The acquisition above is a specified ideal circuit algorithm using resettable pure qubits, classical unbiased randomness, and exact Clifford gates on the supplied physical graph. It does not claim that these operations are automatic under the passive Hamiltonian and bath. A local gate duration tau and serial gate count G cost time G tau. Native Hamiltonian cancellation or toggling requires an explicit control implementation with original local check intensity and field intensity paid. A known during-preparation noise bound nu(t) in diamond norm contributes at most integral nu(t)dt, by time-dependent Duhamel and complete contraction. Gate synthesis errors contribute their diamond sum. The full budget must fit the remaining epsilon/2 in(A7).

For a SPECIFIED Markovian control model retaining independent couplings A_alpha and a rate envelope Gamma_alpha at every controlled Hamiltonian, a conservative bound is nu<=2 R_b, R_b=sum_alpha Gamma_alpha||A_alpha||^2. Thus merely using G tau polynomial with bounded gate strength and an always-on extensive bath does NOT give a small error. The sufficient condition 2R_b G tau<=epsilon/4 explicitly demands fast control, noise suppression, or a separately acquired fault-tolerant implementation. At fixed bounded gate speed this condition is generally unproved. Strong driving does not itself license a microscopic Davies approximation; its control/bath model has to be specified independently.

No exact unknown-input protocol is claimed from the scalar Gibbs estimate when this physical control debt is absent. The positive result is an acquired finite circuit and its quantitative state/encoding error in a cold parameter window. The fixed intensive, fixed-temperature physical initialization task remains OPEN.

## A6. Exact normalization bottleneck behind the proposed classical shortcuts

For the commuting starting Hamiltonian, let d_a=e^(-beta h_a) and P_a=(I+C_a)/2. The exact positive identity is

    e^(-beta H0)=product_a[d_a I+(1-d_a)P_a]
              =E_(independent F) P_F,
    P_F=product_(a in F)P_a,
    Tr P_F=2^(N-rank F).                               (A10)

The independent proposal retains check a with probability1-d_a. Every normalized P_F is a efficiently describable stabilizer mixed state, but the correct Gibbs mixture draws F from

    mu(F)=Pr_ind(F) 2^(N-rank F)/Z0.                   (A11)

Sampling the independent proposal and then normalizing EACH P_F gives E[P_F/Tr P_F], which is generally a different state. Binary rank queries are polynomial; they do not acquire the normalizing random-subset distribution. Its single-check conditional inclusion probability is exactly

    1-d_a                 if C_a lies in span(F\{a}),
    (1-d_a)/(1+d_a)       otherwise.                   (A12)

These formulas define a computable heat-bath chain, but no family-specific mixing bound at fixed beta is proved here. Similarly the valid-syndrome law is e^(-beta E(s)) restricted to the image of the actual check matrix. Gaussian elimination tests validity and gives a preimage; it does not supply the weighted conditional partition functions needed for sequential sampling. A positive expansion and an efficient test of one configuration are not a normalization algorithm.

The very recent Putterman--Zlokapa--Cotler2607.28536v2 (5October2026) proves an unnormalized stabilizer-cone representation when the near-commuting activity Delta(beta)<=1/72. Its separate efficient **normalized** preparation theorem is for beta<=1/(4096 e s k). We read the literal theorem, the near-commuting pinning invariant and the normalized high-temperature partition-ratio proof. The latter relies on high-temperature pinned zero-freeness and computable ratios; these premises are not obtained simply by making the perturbative activity tiny while beta s k is large. The near-commuting invariant E sigma_chi=e^(-beta H) normalizes to a trace-weighted law Pr(chi)Tr sigma_chi/Z_H. That law, together with efficient preparation of its terminal normalized commuting/stabilizer components, is the missing fixed-temperature acquisition step here. No statement that the paper is false or that its stabilizer proof is nonconstructive follows.
