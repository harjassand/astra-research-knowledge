# Quantum coding / Hamiltonian-complexity report

Date: 2026-10-07. Agent: quantum_coding.

## Result and status

The strongest usable opening is an intermediate lemma in release family 265, **distributed density compression**, rather than the full area-law claim. That lemma has no Hamiltonian, spectral-gap, commutativity, or ground-state hypothesis. I reconstructed its Gaussian covariance and dimension-free trace-norm argument from exact TeX. Tracking arbitrary absolute accuracy in the **exact-expansion** version yields a new explicit deduction:

> A bounded-lifetime distributed contraction network with polynomial gate-expansion budgets cannot create a maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank D across a cut with h crossing gates at yield exceeding poly(size) times D^{-1/(A h)}. All supplied normalized pair states and all discarded private spaces may have arbitrarily large finite dimensions.

For an L-by-L nearest-neighbor network whose output is a volume-law pairing of qubits across the middle cut, this gives yield at most poly(L) exp(-c L), with c>0 depending on the fixed interaction/expansion parameters. This does not use the unverified area-law analytics or transfer a commuting game to a local Hamiltonian.

This is an internally reconstructed deduction, not externally reviewed mathematics or cleared priority. The source compression proof appears self-contained and the decisive estimates below were reconstructed, but the entire OA265 area-law/PEPS proof was not certified. The exponential conclusion requires exact expansions, or an explicitly quantitative approximation interface at arbitrarily small accuracy. With only the source's fixed inverse-polynomial approximation interface, the retained conclusion is superpolynomially small yield, not an exponential rate.

A second, shorter deduction is a collision-entropy lower bound for the successful Pauli correction record of a QAC0 cat-state factory. It is conditional on the released OA274 parity/felinity theorem, whose full proof was not independently certified. An exact edge-parity factory demonstrates the feedback loophole and saturates the algebraic collision bound.

## Exact distributed interface

Fix finitely many parties and M nonprivate gates. Initial vectors are normalized and product **by party**. Every private or aggregate nonprivate gate is a linear contraction. Each party may have unbounded finite private memory throughout; at the end its intended physical output has dimension at most d, and all remaining registers are discarded locally.

Let b>=2 bound both the number of parties in a nonprivate gate and the total number of nonprivate gates in which any one party participates over its entire lifetime. Each gate has an **exact** expansion into at most K monomials. A monomial is a composition of local contractions, supplied normalized bipartite vectors, normalized bipartite bras, and explicitly small messages. Its coefficient sum is at most W>=1. It contains at most r normalized pair effects. Polynomial-dimensional messages are first expanded into matrix units; charge their dimension in the resulting K and W. r may harmlessly be enlarged to the source's original constant bound on all resource occurrences.

Pair-state Schmidt ranks, coordinates, and private dimensions are not bounded. Pair sources may only join participants of their gate. A change in ownership of a large register is an interaction and must obey this same expansion interface; it is not a free relabeling.

Choose a bipartition of parties. Let h count the original nonprivate gates having participants on both sides. Suppose the physical output density is

    rho = P |Omega><Omega|,  0<P<=1.

P is the operational yield / retained norm squared. Conditional on success, Omega is normalized. A constant trace-distance error from a pure target can be admitted with adjusted constants; the clean theorem below uses exact purity.

M,K,W,d are charged resources. For an asymptotic family they must be polynomial in the size parameter to obtain a useful asymptotic yield bound. b,r must be fixed. A promise that an expansion exists, without supplying/acquiring it, is not an efficient construction algorithm.

## Reconstructing the useful compression lemma

### 1. Convert effects to sources without a dimension charge

For a normalized pair eta, append m-1 copies and average the m local cyclic shifts of the pair registers. The map T_m satisfies

    T_m eta = eta^{tensor m},
    ||T_m - |eta^{tensor m}><eta||| = m^{-1/2}.

The second assertion follows because, for v perpendicular to eta, the m insertion positions are orthogonal. Use a separate garbage inventory for every effect occurrence in every monomial. Active branches use T_m; inactive branches append the same eta^{tensor m}. Thus all ideal branches have the same garbage vector. Telescoping bounds a gate's operator error by r W / sqrt(m), increases its branch count to at most K m^r, and does not increase its coefficient sum.

For absolute density accuracy 0<epsilon<=1 choose

    m = max{1, ceil[(8 M r W / epsilon)^2]}.

Rescale each approximating gate by its 1+epsilon/(8M) norm bound. The entire source-only circuit has output density rho' with

    ||rho'-rho||_1 <= epsilon/2.

This argument is finite and quantitative for every epsilon, including exponentially small epsilon, **provided the original expansions are exact**.

### 2. Sample one pair density as product operators

For ket source eta=sum_a sqrt(lambda_a) u_a tensor v_a and bra source eta_tilde=sum_c sqrt(mu_c) u_tilde_c tensor v_tilde_c, define independent circular complex Gaussians g_ac and

    U = sum_ac (lambda_a mu_c)^{1/4} g_ac |u_a><u_tilde_c|,
    V = sum_bd (lambda_b mu_d)^{1/4} conjugate(g_bd) |v_b><v_tilde_d|.

The average of k independent U tensor V is unbiased for |eta><eta_tilde|. Its centered coefficient covariance is diagonal, with entry

    k^{-1} sqrt(lambda_a lambda_b mu_c mu_d).

This follows from the two complex Gaussian fourth-moment pairings; centering removes the mean pairing. Neither sampled matrix norm is controlled. Positivity of the sampled network is not asserted or needed.

### 3. Charge only the parties touched by the correction

Expand the density error by a set S of corrected source positions, with |S|=s. At most 2s endpoint parties are affected. Lifetime participation bounds the number of gates touching those parties by 2bs. Opening the ket/bra monomial labels only at those gates costs at most W^{4bs}. Fixing the affected physical matrix entries costs at most d^{4s}. Set

    Q = W^{4b} d^4.

For fixed labels and physical entries, separating the affected parties from the exterior leaves exact normalized crossing sources. Their ket/bra Schmidt probability matrices tau,tau_tilde have trace one. The affected maps are contractions, giving a block operator O of norm at most one. If z_il are the centered sample coefficients, their covariance is

    E[z_il conjugate(z_i'l')] = k^{-s} sqrt(p_i p_tilde_l) delta_ii' delta_ll'.

The exterior-input operator has the form

    Z = sum_il z_il tau^{1/2} O_li^T tau_tilde^{1/2}.

Schatten Holder with exponents (4,2,4) gives

    E ||Z||_1 <= k^{-s/2} ||R_tilde^{1/4} O R^{1/4}||_2 <= k^{-s/2},

where R=diag(p) tensor tau and R_tilde=diag(p_tilde) tensor tau_tilde are trace-one positive matrices. The final inequality uses ||O||<=1 and the trace-one quarter-power norms. Contracting the exterior and tracing its garbage cannot increase trace norm: pull a norm-one dual observable through the two contraction maps. This remains true for rectangular, non-Hermitian cross operators.

This is the load-bearing dimension-free estimate. It contains no pair-source rank or private-memory dimension. It is not a Hilbert–Schmidt-to-trace inequality with an omitted dimension factor; the two density weights supply the missing control explicitly.

### 4. Sum corrections and count cut indices

Let N be the number of source slots, at most M binom(b,2). Independent sampling and the triangle inequality give

    E ||rho_hat-rho'||_1 <= (1+Q/sqrt(k))^N - 1.

Choose

    k = max{1, ceil[(8 N Q / epsilon)^2]}.

A realization exists with sampling error <=epsilon/2, so an operator network sigma obeys ||sigma-rho||_1<=epsilon.

Across the chosen cut, one common ket/bra branch label per crossing gate has at most (K m^r)^2 values. Each crossing source slot adds a k-valued sample label. At most q_b=floor(b^2/4) party pairs cross in a gate. Grouping all tensors on each side therefore gives the **operator Schmidt-rank** bound

    OSR_cut(sigma) <= [(K m^r)^2 k^{q_b}]^h.                 (1)

Counting a common branch label once per crossing gate is valid: expand that label globally at the gate, and then factor left/right tensors. Counting each star edge separately gives a weaker safe bound, but is unnecessary.

This proves the quantitative version needed here, not just the source theorem's fixed inverse-polynomial accuracy statement.

## New deduction: separator versus postselection yield

First reconstruct the column lemma. If ||sigma-|Omega><Omega|||_1<=eta<1, write E=sigma-|Omega><Omega|. In any product basis,

    sum_z ||E|z>||^2 = ||E||_2^2 <= eta^2,
    sum_z |<Omega|z>|^2 = 1.

Some z with nonzero overlap has ||E|z>|| <= eta |<Omega|z>|. Dividing that column by the overlap and normalizing gives a unit vector phi with distance at most 2eta from Omega, up to phase. Selecting a product column cannot increase the cut Schmidt rank beyond OSR_cut(sigma). There is no lower bound on the selected overlap and no positivity requirement.

Apply the compression estimate at epsilon=P/8 to sigma/P. Its error from |Omega><Omega| is at most 1/8. Thus there is a unit vector phi of Schmidt rank at most the right side of (1), with ||phi-Omega||<=1/4 up to phase.

If Omega is maximally entangled of Schmidt rank D across the cut, then

    |<phi|Omega>|^2 >= (31/32)^2,
    |<phi|Omega>|^2 <= SchmidtRank(phi)/D.

The second inequality is Cauchy–Schwarz applied to the D equal Schmidt coefficients. Put f0=(31/32)^2 and A=4r+2q_b. Define

    S=max{1,M r W},   T=max{1,N W^{4b} d^4},
    C0=K^2 2^{2r+q_b} 64^A S^{4r} T^{2q_b}.

For 0<P<=1 the chosen sample/inventory sizes obey

    (K m^r)^2 k^{q_b} <= C0 P^{-A}.

Consequently, whenever h>=1,

    f0 D <= (C0 P^{-A})^h,
    P <= C0^{1/A} (f0 D)^{-1/(A h)}.                     (2)

This is the claimed yield/separator theorem. It is dimension-independent in the supplied pair sources and discarded private spaces. In the source-only r=0, b=2 case, A=2. Constants were deliberately rounded upward.

### Square-grid consequence

Put one fixed-dimensional output at every site of an L-by-L grid. Let nonprivate gates have fixed geometric diameter, fixed arity/lifetime b, and exact polynomial M,K,W budgets. A middle vertical cut has h=O(L). A target containing a separate Bell pair from each left-half site to a right-half site has D=2^{Theta(L^2)}. Equation (2) gives

    P <= poly(L) exp[-Omega(L)].

Thus arbitrarily large supplied *neighboring* pair resources do not remove the preparation-yield cost under this contraction/expansion interface. This is stronger than simply assuming polynomial bond dimensions initially. It is not a theorem about arbitrary LOCC networks, arbitrary classical feedback, unrestricted physical output dimensions, or uncharged register transfers.

For a general pure target with volume-law entropy, use entropy continuity after selecting a sufficiently accurate column. The Schmidt-rank bound then gives the same qualitative exponential-in-linear-size conclusion, with constants depending on the entropy density.

If gates have only fixed inverse-polynomial approximation interfaces, assume for contradiction P>=L^{-B} on an unbounded subsequence, choose accuracy substantially below P times a fixed target error, and obtain polynomial bond dimensions. The cut rank is exp[O(L log L)], which cannot approximate the volume-law Bell pairing. This proves P=L^{-omega(1)}. Exponential P requires the stronger accuracy interface above.

## Second deduction: cat factories require a large correction record

OA274's released corollary asserts, for fixed-depth QAC0 preparations on polynomially many total qubits with arbitrary discarded registers,

    F(rho)=2 sum_y p_y p_complement(y) < n^{-B}

eventually for every fixed B>0. Its gate model uses arbitrary one-qubit unitaries and unbounded-arity Toffoli gates, disjoint supports within each layer, zero-initialized ancillas, and no intermediate measurement/feedback primitive. The reduction to parity with arbitrary garbage was inspected; full OA274 correctness remains unverified here.

Let an arbitrary measurement of the environment produce flags s and subnormalized physical states rho_s. Select successful flags, with total probability P. Suppose each successful conditional state is at trace distance <=delta<1/2 from a cat state after a Pauli frame. Let a=1/2-delta. Group successful flags by their unordered computational pair {y,complement(y)} and let Q_j be the total probability in group j. Then both raw diagonal probabilities in pair j are at least a Q_j. Nonnegativity and the definition of F give the exact inequality

    F(rho) >= 4 a^2 sum_j Q_j^2
           = (1-2delta)^2 P^2 exp[-H2(J|success)].       (3)

For P>=n^{-B0} and fixed delta<1/2, the released felinity result therefore implies

    H2(J|success) / log n -> infinity.

In particular, polynomially many Pauli correction frames cannot produce inverse-polynomial success. A single fixed frame/postselection choice has superpolynomially small yield. This remains true even for an arbitrary environment measurement because discarding its result leaves the same raw physical rho.

For arbitrary constant-depth QAC0 correction circuits, rather than Pauli frames, fix any one correction branch and append that fixed circuit to the raw preparation. The same source corollary implies each successful branch probability is superpolynomially small. This gives a min-entropy / number-of-branches obstruction, but (3)'s stronger summed collision inequality is specifically justified for the common Pauli-pair basis.

With a constant number of adaptive single-qubit measurement rounds, freeze a full transcript and defer its measurements coherently, using fresh record qubits and fixed branch-dependent gates. The resulting fixed circuit is QAC0 at constant overhead. Hence each successful full-transcript atom is superpolynomially small; inverse-polynomial aggregate success requires superlogarithmic conditional min entropy of the full transcript. This does not claim the same lower bound for the compressed final correction label of an arbitrary adaptive protocol.

### Decisive escape construction and exact check

Prepare |+>^n, extract the n-1 neighboring ZZ parities into fresh edge ancillas in four disjoint CNOT layers, and measure those ancillas. For syndrome s, the data state is

    (|y_s>+|complement(y_s)>)/sqrt(2),
    y_1=0, y_{i+1}=y_i XOR s_i.

Every syndrome is uniform with probability 2^{-(n-1)}. Classical prefix parity followed by one layer of local X corrections makes GHZ with probability one. The quantum unitary depth before measurement is five and total qubits are 2n-1. The classical record has n-1 bits of collision entropy and 2^{n-1} correction pairs. Its raw felinity is 2^{1-n}, exactly equal to the right side of (3) at P=1 and delta=0.

`cat_factory_exact.py` checks disjointness of all four extraction layers, enumerates the exact basis action and every syndrome branch, verifies the prefix correction, and verifies this equality using rational arithmetic for n=2,...,12. Results: `cat_factory_exact_results.json`. This fixture validates the stated finite construction and falsifies any claim forbidding unrestricted classical feedback; it does not verify OA274's asymptotic lower bound.

## Failures and boundaries retained

1. **No general QAC0 high-distance-code impossibility was proved.** The source's low-degree propagation can produce operators of norm exp(m^K), whereas pruning approximates the encoded state only to inverse-polynomial error. Transferring exact Knill–Laflamme identities through that approximation multiplies the error by this large norm. This is a load-bearing gap, not a harmless technicality. The cat/repetition-code argument does not establish a barrier for arbitrary good quantum codes.
2. **Polynomial PEPS existence does not put the ground-energy problem in NP.** Tensor acquisition, bit precision, contraction, and certification are separate. The source explicitly supplies arbitrary state-dependent tensors and does not promise an efficient algorithm.
3. **No constant-locality PCP Hamiltonian was constructed.** Neither OA265 compression nor OA274 parity yields a positive common encoding of the actual amplified noncommuting hard family. No commuting/tensor/finite-dimensional value substitution was made.
4. **No unrestricted LOCC or adaptive preparation bound follows from (2).** A feedforward channel can require exponentially many monomial branches or a large coefficient budget when forced into the exact contraction interface. These resources must be charged, rather than silently converted into a normalized pair effect.
5. **The geometric area-law and PEPS analytics remain unaudited.** Only the independent compression mechanism and column extraction support the main deduction. No assertion of external correctness or priority follows from matching calculations or downloading the source.

## Costs and next decisive observation

The proofs are finite-dimensional existence arguments, with no efficient Schmidt-decomposition acquisition, Gaussian sampling implementation, tensor-contraction algorithm, or bit-size guarantee. Arbitrarily large source ranks/private spaces are explicitly allowed and would be retained resource costs in any implementation. The derived yield bound is meaningful precisely because it already permits those supplied resources, while separately charging M,K,W,d,b,r and the separator h.

Local execution downloaded about 600 kB of selected TeX/bibliography material and ran a small exact combinatorial fixture. There was no remote publication, memory modification, Lean build, or whole-corpus replay. Sources and exact checks are retained under this agent directory.
