# Exact structural-rule criterion for the unit-leaf potential

Status: universal mathematical derivation; explicit rational certificates and
counterweights below. This is an exact local criterion for one fixed potential,
not a classification of logics, admissible rules, or all possible tree lower bounds.
Priority is UNKNOWN: the convex support-function argument is elementary old
mathematics, and no literature-wide novelty claim is made.

## Interface

Extend base FL_e by a fixed finite set of schematic structural rules of the
kind in Definition2 of arXiv:2609.00422v1, pp8–9. Formula schemata are uniformly
instantiated by arbitrary L_u formulas; contexts are multisets; there are no
literal atomicity/freshness restrictions. Separate the finitely many allowed
succedent cases (empty, or the one active singleton succedent variable).

For each such case, take independent integer coordinates x for the weights of
the schematic formula slots, each free antecedent context, and the active
succedent slot. Each sequent deficit from TREE_RESOURCE_BOUNDARY.md is then a
homogeneous integer linear form. Write the premises as v1·x,...,vm·x and the
conclusion as c·x. Repeated schematic atoms and contexts have repeated
coefficients: they are never erased or treated as free contraction.

If a presentation literally restricts an atom slot to ground atoms, its weight
is forced0 here and must be removed from x. Arbitrary formula substitution is
essential for the necessity statement with unrestricted integer coordinates.
No side-condition-preservation theorem is silently assumed.

## The exact criterion

The rule preserves the induction

    d(premise_i)≤R_i, R_i≥0  implies d(conclusion)≤Σ_i R_i

for every weight assignment and budget **iff**

    c = Σ_i λ_i v_i  for some rational 0≤λ_i≤1.       (Z)

Equivalently, c belongs to the zonotope Z=Σ_i[0,v_i].

Sufficiency is direct: λ_i d_i≤λ_i R_i≤R_i, so summing yields the required bound.
No contraction of any formula is involved; budgets add over actual premise
occurrences of the proof tree.

For necessity, the smallest permitted R_i is max(v_i·x,0), so preservation is
equivalent to

    c·x≤Σ_i max(v_i·x,0)=max_{z in Z} z·x  for every x.     (H)

If c is outside the compact convex Z, choose a closest point z0 of Z and
x=c−z0. Minimality gives x·(z−z0)≤0 for every z in Z (differentiate the squared
distance along the segment from z0 to z). Hence

    c·x > z0·x ≥ z·x for every z in Z,

contradicting (H). Conversely membership gives (H). Strict failure persists
under a sufficiently close rational approximation of x; scale it to an integer
counterweight vector. The feasible rational coefficient system (Z), when
nonempty, has a rational solution (finite linear equations and rational box
inequalities; Gaussian elimination on a face gives one).

Every integer formula weight is realizable: weight0 by an atom, positive k by
a fusion of k copies of1, and negative −k by (that fusion)→0. Each free context
weight is realized by a singleton carrying that formula. Thus these are genuine
allowed weight assignments, not a real-number relaxation with unrealizable
source syntax. Failure of (Z) obstructs *this local induction*. It does not by
itself provide closed proofs realizing the chosen minimum premise budgets.

Here r=w(Δ)−w(Γ). For wc take context total q and atom weight a. Its deficits
are a−q and −a−q, and conclusion −q, so the averaging certificate is exact for
all signs. This is the literal *sequent wc rule* in the recent primary's Example3;
it is not identified with any other axiom called weak contraction, including the
previous cycle's CWC axiom. No source equivalence between those interfaces is
claimed.

The failures for W/C/expansion also have zero-unit closed premise derivations:

The resulting deficits are positive although their trees contain no ⇒1 leaf.
Thus unrestricted W/C genuinely invalidate this potential, not merely its
syntactic certificate format.

## Consequence for tree versus DAG proofs

For any finite structural extension whose every allowed rule case passes (Z),
the universal unit-leaf induction extends rule by rule. Its occurrence-tree
proofs of ⇒Un therefore charge at least2^n formula occurrences, whereas its DAG
system still has the n+1-line pure FL_e proof. The presentation's finite maximum
arity r gives a DAG deficit bound r^(i−1) (take r≥2), so the minimum DAG line
count remains Θ(n) whenever every row carries a formula. The exponential
tree/DAG separation follows even without that last condition, from the displayed
DAG upper bound and the charged unit leaves.

For **FL_e+wc**, and also with mix, the rules are binary and Boolean-sound. Thus
empty⇒empty remains impossible and the sharp minima remain exactly

    DAG n+1 versus occurrence-tree 2^(n+1)−1.

This extends the resource obstruction beyond the superficial criterion "no
contraction-looking rule". A displayed contraction can average complementary
premise deficits and preserve the tree resource measure; unrestricted contraction
does not. This offers a useful structural taxonomy and a small exact admission
certificate for future lower-bound arguments. It is not a largest-logic claim.

Costs: for a fixed presentation, precomputed rational vectors/λ certificates
are constant overhead. Checking a certificate is exact rational matrix-vector
arithmetic. Formula weights and deficits are evaluated on a common syntax DAG
by one operation per constructor/antecedent occurrence, with integer bit length
charged. The unit family uses n+1 common nodes and weight2^n of n+1 bits. No
uncounted exponential formula unfolding is required to verify the obstruction.
