Each attempt: take fresh PAID classifier samples, obtain k, propose c~H_k, and accept with probability W_k(c)/(7eta H_k(c)). On a rejection repeat the WHOLE attempt, including new physical classification. Under true i the accepted submass for c in ONE attempt is

    sum_k A_R(i,k)H_k(c) W_k(c)/(7eta H_k(c))
        = T_i(c)/(7eta).

Its total acceptance probability is delta_i/(7eta). Independent attempts consequently produce T_i/delta_i exactly without evaluating delta_i. Expected attempts are7eta/delta_i<=7. This cancellation is why reclassification is both correct and affordable. Holding the classifier output fixed over retries would normalize W_k row by row and generally break the desired mixture.
