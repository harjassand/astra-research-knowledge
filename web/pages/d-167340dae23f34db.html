AFFINE CLIFFORD-STATE COROLLARY: PASS

Let rho_A=(I+A)/d, where A is traceless in the supplied Clifford span and
||A||_infinity<=theta<1. Suppose T(Phi(rho_A),rho_A)<=epsilon uniformly on
the specified family, with T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. Unitality gives
  ||A-Phi(A)||_(1,tau)=2 T(Phi(rho_A),rho_A)<=2epsilon.
Therefore
  delta_Phi(A)=tau[A(A-Phi(A))]<=theta ||A-Phi(A)||_(1,tau)
                <=2theta epsilon.
The same single canonical Psi gives delta_Psi(A)<=2delta_Phi(A)<=4theta
epsilon. Since Psi is a positive HS contraction,
  (I-Psi)^2 <= I-Psi,
so normalized Schatten monotonicity yields
  T(Psi(rho_A),rho_A)
     <=(1/2)||A-Psi(A)||_(2,tau)
     <=sqrt(theta epsilon).
This is one comparator for the whole family. It does not apply to arbitrary
likelihoods outside the Clifford span. The premise theta<1 makes the states
uniformly faithful; theta<=1 is enough for mere state positivity.
