# Dense negative rank-one contrasts: an exact cyclicity witness

7 October 2026, cycle13, worker `metric_acquisition`. **Full independent mathematical gate PASS:** `notes/metric_geometry10/cycle13/DENSE_RANK_ONE_PENCIL_INDEPENDENT_GATE.md`. This is a new promised-family guarantee for the existing normalized pencil, not a claim of universal dense-QPD cyclicity, historical priority or practical superiority.

## 1. Original interface and theorem

The learner receives only the expanded rational homogeneous quartic

    f(x)=((Lx)^2)^T Q ((Lx)^2),

with unknown rational invertible L and unknown rational symmetric Q>0. Assume that an unknown permutation makes Q a direct sum of blocks, each of which either has dimension at most3 (with arbitrary PD signs, zeros and degeneracies), or has an unknown representation

    Q_b=D_b [diag(a_1,...,a_b)+b_0 J_b] D_b,
    D_b positive real diagonal,
    a_i>0 pairwise distinct,
    1+b_0 sum_i(1/a_i)>0.                         (1)

The last condition is exactly positive definiteness of the bracketed matrix. All blocks, the representation, D_b, a_i, b_0, hidden coefficient heights, axes, metrics and condition numbers are unsupplied. In the proof of a single block below, its dimension is called n and its rank-one coefficient is called b.

Define from the observed coefficients, on h!=0,

    H=Hess f=12T(x,x,.,.), h=det H,
    V=H^-1 grad log h,
    L_u=12H^-1 T(x,u,.,.),
    N_tau=Jac(V)+tau L_V.

Then for all but at most n(n+1)/2 real scalar values tau, the full-block-composed matrix N_tau has a generically cyclic initial vector x. Its polynomial clearing

    P_tau=h^3 N_tau
         =h Jac(F)-2F(grad h)^T+12tau adj(H)T(x,F,.,.),
    F=adj(H)grad h,
    K_tau=det[x,P_tau x,...,P_tau^(n-1)x]           (2)

is nonzero and divisible by every hidden coordinate hyperplane. Its degree remains

    d=n+(6n-4)n(n-1)/2.

Fresh parameter and rational-line trials, followed by the existing exact reflection, rational invariant-metric, axis-selection and positive weighted-square verification, give expected polynomial-bit coefficient-only acquisition on this complete promised family. Repeated residual a_i in a larger block are not included by the new proof; no limiting argument is used. Arbitrary dense QPD remains open for this detector.

## 2. Normalize only for the existence proof

It suffices to prove one block with D=I: put z=D^(1/2)y. This is a real proof-only GL change. The original input f and its rational hidden hyperplanes remain rational, and no square-root or latent-parameter arithmetic is performed by the learner. The rational normalized matrices transform by similarity, and (2) is a polynomial relative covariant, as proved in the gated cycle12 note.

Thus consider

    f(y)=sum_i a_i y_i^4+b (sum_i y_i^2)^2,
    A=sum_i 1/a_i,
    d_0=4(3+bA), rho=d_0+8bA=12(1+bA).

Both d_0 and rho are positive by (1). Choose a positive real witness

    y_i=1/sqrt(a_i), m_i=y_i^2=1/a_i.            (3)

This witness is not acquired or supplied. Its only purpose is to prove that an intrinsic rational determinant is not identically zero.

For every y,

    H=12diag(a_i y_i^2)+4b(sum_i y_i^2)I+8b yy^T.

At (3), the Sherman-Morrison identity gives exactly

    H=d_0 I+8b yy^T,
    X=H^-1=d_0^-1 I-beta yy^T,
    beta=8b/(d_0 rho),
    tr X=(n-1)/d_0+1/rho, Xy=y/rho.             (4)

In particular h!=0 at the witness, including the negative-b PD boundary arbitrarily close to 1+bA=0.

## 3. The logarithmic Hessian field is affine in the residual diagonal

Write alpha=grad log h. Directly differentiate the displayed H and use alpha_i=tr(X partial_i H):

    alpha_i/y_i=24a_i X_ii+8b tr X+16b (Xy)_i/y_i
               =24a_i/d_0+B,
    B=8b(n-1)/d_0+24b/rho-192b/(d_0 rho).        (5)

The last term follows from a_i y_i^2=1, not from a generic-diagonal approximation. Multiplying (5) by (4) yields

    V_i/y_i=c a_i+e,
    c=24/d_0^2>0,
    e=B/d_0-beta(24n/d_0+BA).                   (6)

Thus, with A_d=diag(a_i),

    V=c A_d y+e y.                              (7)

The common coefficient e may have any sign; the proof never assumes that V has full support, that the witness attracts a flow, or that any axis is attracting.

## 4. Tensor multiplication has a two-dimensional perturbation image

The symmetric polarization of the rank-one term gives, for every u,

    12T(y,u,.,.)=12diag(a_i y_i u_i)
                  +4b[(y^T u)I+yu^T+uy^T].     (8)

At u=V and (3), equations (6)-(7) imply

    12T(y,V,.,.)=12c A_d+(12e+4bW)I
                            +4b(yV^T+Vy^T),
    W=y^T V=cn+eA.

Multiply on the left by X. The diagonal and scalar terms have principal part d_0^-1 I; the remaining term of X has image span{y}. Also Xy is proportional to y and XV belongs to span{y,A_d y}. Therefore

    L_V=gamma A_d+delta I+E,
    gamma=12c/d_0=288/d_0^3>0,
    delta=(12e+4bW)/d_0,
    im E subset span{y,A_d y}.                  (9)

No rank hypothesis is imposed on E. Its rank is at most2, and its coefficients need not be positive or symmetric in the ordinary Euclidean inner product. Independently, the unital identity L_u y=u gives exactly

    L_V y=V=c A_d y+e y.                        (10)

The proof uses a unital commutative Hessian product. It does not assume associativity, power associativity or a Jordan identity.

## 5. Explicit Vandermonde cyclicity

Put w_k=A_d^k y. Equations (9)-(10) imply inductively, for k>=1,

    L_V^k y=c gamma^(k-1) w_k
                       +span{w_0,...,w_(k-1)}. (11)

For k=1 this is (10). If it holds at k, multiplication of its leading w_k term by L_V yields gamma w_(k+1)+delta w_k+span{w_0,w_1}; multiplication of every lower w_j increases its index by at most1. This proves (11), including k=1 where the exceptional perturbation image already lies in the required lower span after the next step.

Consequently the change between the columns y,L_Vy,...,L_V^(n-1)y and w_0,...,w_(n-1) is triangular, and

    det[y,L_Vy,...,L_V^(n-1)y]
       =c^(n-1) gamma^[(n-1)(n-2)/2]
          (product_i y_i) product_(i<j)(a_j-a_i) !=0.   (12)

All residual parameters are strictly positive and distinct, so no factor vanishes. This is an exact identity for a continuum of parameters, not a conclusion from finite fixtures.

For fixed y the polynomial det[y,N_tau y,...,N_tau^(n-1)y] has tau degree at most s=n(n-1)/2. Its coefficient of tau^s is (12). Hence it is nonzero. Any tau for which the same intrinsic determinant is identically zero as a rational function of y must be one of at most s zeros of this witness polynomial. This proves single-block generic cyclicity for all but at most s parameters, without computing the witness or its heights.

## 6. Unknown block composition and parameter accounting

For a direct sum, normalized V, Jac(V) and L_V split exactly; global h introduces no coupling in N_tau. The cycle12 universal invertibility primitive says that det L_V is not identically zero for every positive homogeneous quartic: at an interior maximum of h on an H-positive component of f=1, V=(b_dim/6)y and L_V=(b_dim/6)I. That existence proof neither computes the point nor requires isolation/full support.

Therefore a block of dimension b_dim has at most b_dim bad tau for det N_tau and at most b_dim(b_dim-1)/2 for cyclicity. Larger blocks use (12); blocks of sizes<=3 use the independently gated flow/Jacobian identities from cycle12, including repeated, signed and zero PD degeneracies. Summing gives

    bad parameters <=sum_blocks b_dim(b_dim+1)/2
                   <=n(n+1)/2.                 (13)

For every good tau, choose points where each block is invertible and cyclic and h!=0. The open conditions intersect. Positive rational block input scalings multiply N_tau by the inverse fourth power of the scale. Since each spectrum is finite and has no zero, choose these proof-only scales to avoid all cross-block eigenvalue collisions. The minimal polynomials have their full block degrees and are pairwise coprime, so the Chinese remainder theorem makes the full initial vector cyclic. Repeated/non-diagonalizable within-block spectra are allowed. The proof finds no blocks, points, spectra or scales algorithmically.

Use a fresh uniform tau from {0,...,R} on every complete trial, where

    R=max(8n,2n(n+1)).                          (14)

Then R+1>4 times the bound in (13), so the probability of a good parameter is at least3/4. Parameter bits are O(log n). Reuse the fresh charged rational-line grid from cycle12, whose conditional good-line probability is at least3/4; success per complete trial is at least9/16, expected complete trials at most16/9. Holding one unlucky tau fixed forever is not an acquiring algorithm. A bounded/forced run is only an implementation discriminator.

The intrinsic matrix degree6n-4, determinant degree d, second-ambient-dual line circuit, multiplicity-aware univariate normal extraction, rational reflection checks, rational invariant-SPD projection, axis selection and exact positive weighted-square terminal certificate remain unchanged. ## 7. A strictly newly covered dense case

Let n=4, D=I, a=(1,10,20,30), b=-4/5. Then

    A=71/60, 1+bA=4/75>0,
    Q_ii=(1/5,46/5,96/5,146/5), Q_ij=-4/5 (i!=j).

Its leading principal minors are 1/5,6/5,16,320. It is one dense block of size4. Every off-diagonal triangle has negative sign product, so it is not sign-balanced. Unequal residual a_i and equal nonzero off-diagonal entries exclude a positively rescaled exchangeable matrix when n>=3. The independently gated hidden-axis identifiability statement excludes restoring exchangeability by a different real even-PD frame.
