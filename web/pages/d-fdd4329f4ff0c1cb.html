nonexplosive and positive recurrent for every fixed finite positive rate

Key mathematical choices
------------------------
For any positive lambda_j, choose rational R_i>1 close enough to one that
chi_j=lambda_j delta_j-sum_i p_ji(R_i-1)>0 for all j. Set
K>(sum_j alpha_j lambda_j+1)/sum_j alpha_j. Use
V=sum_j lambda_j a_j+sum_i R_i^b_i+K 1_{sum_j a_j=0}.
Each substrate drift has a negative q_ji b_i R_i^b_i term at large b_i;
each unbounded catalyst is controlled either by strict death margin when all
adjacent b_i are zero, or by the matched reverse factorial term when some
adjacent b_i is positive. Nonexplosion is established in physical time
before applying the CTMC Foster criterion.

Exact fixture
-------------
r=1,s=2,k_1i=1; alpha=delta=p_i=1, q_i=2, u_i=1, v_i=2. The strict LP
would require lambda>w1+w2 and 2w_i>lambda for both i, a contradiction.
The fixture is also not complex balanced. Yet the exponential Foster
function with lambda=1,R_i=6/5,K=3 proves positive recurrence outside
F={(a,b1,b2):1<=a<=3,0<=b1,b2<=9}. No closed-form stationary law is given;
irreducibility and positive recurrence imply a unique invariant probability.
