# Result card: aged Paneth-cell niche renewal

**Status:** internally reconstructed conditional model and exact aggregation counterexample; no new biological finding, no priority claim, and no nature-level rejuvenation claim.

## Target mechanism

Andersson et al. (2025) report that a subset of mouse intestinal stem cells (ISCs) inherits old mitochondria, has higher α-ketoglutarate (αKG), and is biased toward Paneth-cell (PC) emergence. In aged mice, systemic dimethyl-αKG (dm-αKG) increased the EdU-positive PC fraction, and newly made EdU-positive PCs had lower Notum expression. A separate cohort pretreated with dm-αKG had reduced weight loss and restored villus cell density after 5-fluorouracil (5-FU).

The high-ceiling question is whether the intervention creates *spatially distributed, functionally competent replacement of aged Paneth niches* in enough crypts to explain repair, while preserving stem-cell support. It is distinct from DNA-damage partition and IFNγ/chromatin-selection routes.

## Quantitative obstruction

The paper reports about 9% of Lgr5-high ISCs enriched for old mitochondria (usually 1–2 cells per crypt), and age-selective asymmetric mitochondrial apportioning in about 15% of the observed divisions, localized to crypt positions +3/+4. Its own rate argument is that a deterministic mapping from these events to PC fate would overproduce PCs relative to their slow homeostatic turnover. The correct minimal event budget is

\[
  0.15 D\,p c \leq P/\tau_P,
  \qquad p \leq \frac{P}{0.15 D c\tau_P},
\]

where (D) is the daily number of relevant +3/+4 ISC divisions per crypt, (P) is the Paneth-cell pool per crypt, \(\tau_P\) is the mean Paneth residence time under the assumed steady-state model, (p) is the probability that a mitochondria-age-biased daughter generates a mature PC, and (c) is its expected PC output conditional on that fate. These denominators are not jointly measured in the paper, so the probability is not numerically identified. This makes the source's verbal deterministic-overproduction point explicit without filling its missing rates.

There is a second exact obstruction. Let (R_i\in[0,1]) be the fraction of Paneth cells renewed in crypt (i), and suppose a crypt has a local functional threshold (R_i\geq\tau). From a pooled mean \(\bar R\) alone, the fraction \(\pi=\Pr(R_i\geq\tau)\) obeys only

\[
  \max\!\left(0,\frac{\bar R-\tau}{1-\tau}\right)
  \leq \pi \leq \min\!\left(1,\frac{\bar R}{\tau}\right),
\]

with endpoint qualifications when the threshold includes equality. At \(\bar R=\tau=1/2\), the closure of the identified interval is \([0,1]\). An exact 100-crypt toy example has the same pooled EdU fraction (1/2), same pooled Notum mean, and same Notum means within the EdU-positive and EdU-negative pools, but 1/2 versus 1 of crypts passing an illustrative local Notum threshold. The threshold and cell-expression values are synthetic, not biological estimates.

Even under a constant-hazard replacement assumption, a pooled EdU fraction only fixes \(\mathbb E[1-e^{-\lambda T}]\), not \(\mathbb E[\lambda]\) or the crypt-level rate distribution. A 21-day synthetic example gives the same pooled EdU fraction 0.5 with mean replacement rates 0.0330/day and 0.0512/day (1.551-fold apart).

## Interface, falsifier, and next gate

EdU, Lgr5-EGFP, mitochondrial SNAP age labels, Paneth markers, and Notum qPCR are reporters or molecular proxies. The reported acute insult is a 100 mg/kg 5-FU challenge; body weight and villus-cell density are tissue-level outcomes. The paper does not report a per-crypt joint trajectory from old/new PC status through local Wnt/stem-cell activity to the subsequent physical epithelial deficit and repair.

The decisive next experiment is a matched aged-mouse factorial study that (i) measures renewal, Notum, and a functional stem-cell readout per crypt before injury; (ii) co-registers crypt-level epithelial loss and recovery after the same 5-FU challenge; and (iii) selectively prevents the proposed ISC-to-PC/Notum change or clamps Notum in newly made PCs while retaining matched dm-αKG exposure. The equal-information comparator is dm-αKG versus vehicle under identical reporter, injury, handling, and sampling schedules. A direct epithelial or systemic dm-αKG benefit that persists when PC renewal/Notum change is blocked would falsify the claim that this route is necessary for the recovery effect. Conversely, per-crypt Notum reduction that fails to predict local Wnt/stem activity or repair rejects the simple threshold model.

The protocol costs are substantial and not priced here: the published work used aged (≥18-month) mice, repeated intraperitoneal dm-αKG, EdU exposure, flow/qPCR or tissue readouts, and a 5-FU injury cohort. Cell-level lineage and spatial assays, mediator blockade, injury quantification, and enough animals for crypt-within-mouse clustering are additional costs. Throughput, variance components, dose exposure, and required sample size remain unknown.

## Primary boundary

The broad Paneth–Notum–Wnt aging mechanism and the ability of Notum inhibition to improve aged intestinal regeneration were already reported by Pentinmikko et al. (2019). The 2025 organelle-age/αKG mechanism is also itself a published primary result. The contribution here is a scoped model-identifiability obstruction and a source-faithful quantitative gate, not discovery or priority.
