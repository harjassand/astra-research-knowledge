## Adopted statement

Let P=conv(V)⊂[0,1]^n be a nonempty Boolean polytope, y∈P rational, and A rational with every column having Euclidean norm at most one. Supply an exact strong optimizer returning an optimizing Boolean vertex of P for every rational cost. Let the supplied integer 1≤k≤n bound the support size of every edge of P. A singleton is handled immediately. The algorithm does not silently acquire the minimum valid k.

Set K=64k and R=floor(sqrt(K)). There is a deterministic polynomial-bit, oracle-polynomial construction of an explicit rational probability law on V with

    E[b]=y,   ||A(b−y)||∞ < B(k)=16R²+2K+8k+1 ≤1161k

on every positive-probability atom. At most dim(F_P(y))+1 atoms are needed. For matroid base polytopes k=2, ordinary independence-oracle greedy optimization realizes the supplied strong optimizer, and B(2)=2209. Rational inputs and exact oracle answers are essential to this algorithmic statement. Real-input existence can follow separately by compactness, without an algorithmic bit claim.

The longest-edge midpoint construction in `notes/root_handoff_20261007/EDGE_BOUNDARY.md` gives a worst-case lower bound k(P)/2 for any exact-mean law, with a single row and unit-norm columns. Thus the dependence on edge support is optimal up to constants in this general interface. This does not assert that the constant or the running time is useful in practice.
