For independent G' standard and T uniform [0,1], theta=pi T/2, use

Gamma=mu+sqrt(a)[sin(theta)G+cos(theta)G'],
dotGamma=(pi/2)sqrt(a)[cos(theta)G-sin(theta)G'],

The endpoints are the independent reference X'=mu+sqrt(a)G' and proposal X. Integration over T gives F(X)-F(X') exactly. Hence E[W|X]=F(X)-E_QF, and E_QW=0. The centered tilt normalizer is Z=E_Q exp(E[W|X])>=1 by Jensen. Approximate c is accounted for by the shifted proposal mean; no residual likelihood bias is omitted. One fresh gradient implements W, using the cached g_c.

## Independently reconstructed centered MGF

For every fixed T, the rotation of (G,G') is orthogonal. Thus Gamma=mu+sqrt(a)Z and dotGamma=(pi/2)sqrt(a)H, with independent standard Z,H. Their joint law does not depend on T; mixing over T preserves independence. It is important to center both Gaussian vectors at mu before rotating: rotating the nonzero physical means directly would not yield this identity.

Set R(Z)=sqrt(a)||grad U(mu+sqrt(a)Z)-g_c||. Conditional on Z, W is centered Gaussian with variance pi²R(Z)²/4. Convex co-coercivity and Stein, at center mu, yield

E a||grad U(mu+sqrt(a)Z)-grad U(mu)||²<=a²tau h².

Since ||mu-c||=sqrt(a)zeta and grad U is h-Lipschitz,

ER²<=2(ah)²(tau+zeta²)<=2(ah)²(tau+1).

R is ah-Lipschitz. Gaussian norm concentration and the auxiliary-scalar-Gaussian identity give

E exp(k R²) <= (1-2k(ah)²)^-1/2 exp[k(ER)²/(1-2k(ah)²)].

Substituting k=pi²lambda²/8 proves

E exp(lambda W)<=exp[C lambda²(ah)²tau], |lambda|ah<=c,
||W||p<=C ah[sqrt(tau p)+p], p>=2.

Both signs of lambda are controlled. tau>=1 absorbs the norm-MGF prefactor. No third-derivative bound or realized Hessian action is required. The leading tau term in the older independent-time secant estimator disappears because the rotated velocity is independent of the position gradient and has conditional mean zero.
