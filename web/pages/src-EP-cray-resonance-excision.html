Research decision
Stop the strongly gapped independent estimator as a breakthrough direction. It removes the previous engineered quartics and prohibitive time-stepping, but the comparator and parameter tests do not show new scientific capability.

Preserve the rank-local defect bound and exact conditional-resonance operation. The genuine local result is that exact integration removes a logarithmic sampling-variance singularity while retaining the original answer. Its ingredients—conditional expectation, Schur complements, and Fourier heat evolution—are not themselves claimed as historically new.

The strongest remaining mathematical opening is therefore a globally affordable, coefficient-acquired treatment of coupled resonances and their residual phase. That theorem is unresolved. Without it, this investigation has made local calculations reliable, not established a new affordable solution to a consequential many-body sign problem.
