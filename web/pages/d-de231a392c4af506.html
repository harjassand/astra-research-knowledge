MODEWISE-PRECONDITIONED CONDITIONAL GAUSSIAN SAMPLER
Date: 8 October 2026
Status: internally proved candidate; exact-rational fixture checked; sibling-leaf adversarial audit found no fatal gap; external review and priority open

1. Extension target
===================

The PC-16/PC-17 sampler uses a single scalar variance b for all m positive-
count herald modes. Replace that scalar covariance by a rational
mode-diagonal preconditioner

    D0 = direct_sum_(j in H_+) b_j I_2,   b_j>0 rational,

and assume a supplied rational relative Loewner certificate

    0 < s_- <= s_+,       s_- D0 <= S <= s_+ D0.                 (MW)

The scales b_j may differ by an arbitrarily large factor. The certificate
controls the covariance after standardizing each positive-count herald mode
by its own variance; it permits heterogeneous local energies and nonzero
cross-mode correlations. It is stronger than positivity of S and does not
cover arbitrary cross-mode condition number.

Use the PC-16 interface and conventions: C is a rational strictly positive
centered P covariance, H is a PNR herald with target counts k_j, H_+ contains
the m positive-count modes, kappa=sum_j k_j is charged numerically, U is the
unheralded output set, and

    C°=(C^(-1)+P_H)^(-1),
    S=(C°)_(H_+,H_+),
    K=(C°)_(U,H_+)S^(-1),
    V=(C°)_(U,U)-KSK^T.

All matrices and certificate checks are rational. The common zero-count
herald exponential has already been integrated into C°. The positive-core
likelihood is

    W(x)=prod_(j in H_+)(||x_j||^2/2)^(k_j)/k_j!,
    pi(x)=phi_S(x)W(x)/Z_S.

2. Exact-real theorem
=====================

Let K0=m+kappa and rho=s_+/s_-. For m>0 use

    q(x)=phi_(s_+ D0)(x) W(x)/Z_q,
    Z_q=prod_(j in H_+)(s_+ b_j)^(k_j),
    Delta=S^(-1)-(s_+ D0)^(-1).

By (MW), Delta is positive semidefinite. The target/proposal ratio is a
constant times exp(-x^T Delta x/2), so accepting a q draw with probability

    a(x)=exp(-x^T Delta x/2)

produces pi exactly. The unknown constant, Z_S, and physical herald mass are
not needed by the accept coin.

To bound the acceptance, whiten with Y=D0^(-1/2)X and
S'=D0^(-1/2) S D0^(-1/2). This is an analysis change of variables only;
the input certificate itself is checked using rational PSD tests. The
eigenvalues of S' lie in [s_-,s_+], and

    Delta'=D0^(1/2) Delta D0^(1/2)=S'^(-1)-s_+^(-1)I >=0,
    ||Delta'|| <= s_-^(-1)-s_+^(-1).

Under q, each Y_j has a uniform phase and
||Y_j||^2/2 distributed as Gamma(k_j+1, scale s_+), independently across
modes. Hence E_q||Y||^2=2s_+ K0. Jensen gives

    alpha=E_q a(X)
      >= exp[-s_+ K0(s_-^(-1)-s_+^(-1))]
       = exp[-K0(rho-1)].

Thus the exact-real expected number of proposals is at most
exp[K0(rho-1)]. A computable dyadic envelope is

    Lambda=2^ceil(2 K0(rho-1)),

because ln(2)>1/2, so alpha>=1/Lambda. The cost depends on the numeric
heterogeneity/correlation parameter K0(rho-1), not on d, the number of
zero-target herald modes, the number of output modes, or 1/p_H. For positive
heralds, m<=kappa and K0<=2kappa. The finite sampler is polynomial in Lambda;
in particular, polynomial-in-L bit time follows when numeric kappa, Lambda,
the output-rate cap, and accuracy costs are polynomially bounded.

The PC-16 certificate is recovered by taking D0=bI,
s_-=1-eps, s_+=1+eps. Then

    K0(rho-1)=2 K0 eps/(1-eps)
             =2(1+eps)eps K0/(1-eps^2),

which is exactly the PC-16 Jensen exponent. Therefore this extension does
not weaken the existing scalar-certificate bound.

3. Finite-bit implementation and explicit costs
================================================

The PC-17 finite-bit construction carries over with modewise radial scales.
For clarity, the changed proposal and core-coupling parameters are listed
here; the conditional-output Gaussian, normal-CDF, and Poisson routines are
the same as PC-17, with the revised output cap below.

Let b_max=max_j b_j and J=ceil(log_2(256 Lambda/delta)). In whitened
coordinates the proposal has

    T_Y=8 s_+(K0+J),
    P_q(||Y||^2>T_Y)<=delta/(256 Lambda).

The finite proposal in original coordinates is clipped to
[-B_X,B_X]^(2m), where

    B_X=ceil(sqrt(b_max T_Y)).

Let

    Lbar_Delta=1+sum_(i,j)|Delta_ij| >= sqrt(||Delta||/e),
    K_* >= ||K||_2,
    tau=delta/[512 Lambda(1+Lbar_Delta+K_*)],
    zeta=delta/[256 Lambda K0],
    eta_e=tau^2/[128 s_+ b_max K0].

Use dyadic uniforms with mesh

    h <= min(zeta/2, zeta eta_e/4),
    2(22/7)h <= tau/[32(B_X+1)].

The latter makes the coupled phase-angle error at most tau/[32(B_X+1)].
Approximate each -ln(U_mid) to absolute error eta_e/2 and project the numerical value onto
[0,infinity) before summation; this cannot increase the absolute error.
The uniform-cell log error is at most 2h/zeta, so the total exponential
error is at most eta_e. The squared-radius error for mode j is at most

    2 s_+ b_j (k_j+1) eta_e.

Summing radial errors in Euclidean norm gives at most
sqrt(2 s_+ b_max K0 eta_e)=tau/8. Approximate each original-coordinate mode
radius R_j=||X_j|| to absolute error tau/(32 ceil(sqrt(m))); their combined Euclidean error is at most
tau/32. Evaluate sine and cosine to absolute error at most
tau/[32(B_X+1)]. On the proposal-tail complement, the phase-angle
contribution is at most tau/32 because the total core norm is at most B_X;
the sine/cosine contribution is at most
sqrt(2)B_X tau/[32(B_X+1)]<tau/16. These radial, square-root, phase, and
trigonometric contributions sum to less than tau/4. Clipping is
nonexpansive and leaves the exact core inside the box on that event. The
resulting bad-distance probability is at most
delta/(128 Lambda), exactly as in PC-17.

The acceptance function has Euclidean Lipschitz constant
sqrt(||Delta||/e)<=Lbar_Delta. Approximate exp(-x^T Delta x/2) to additive
delta/(512 Lambda) and use a dyadic acceptance coin with mesh at most the
same. The one-trial disagreement is at most delta/(64 Lambda); its finite
success probability is at least 1/(2 Lambda). The normalized rejection
coupling therefore gives accepted-core Euclidean error tau except with
probability at most delta/16. The finite uncapped expected proposal count
is at most 2 Lambda; the exact-real count is at most Lambda. A cap

    N=ceil(4 Lambda ceil(log_2(2/xi)))

makes timeout probability at most xi/2.

For output modes, factor V=R diag(v)R^T by exact rational LDL and approximate
sqrt(v_i) to absolute precision as in PC-17. Define

    r=2|U|, cr=ceil(sqrt(r)), Rz=2ceil(log_2(512r/delta)),
    L_*>=sqrt(tr V),
    gamma_L=delta/[512(1+cr Rz)],
    gamma_Z=delta/[512(1+L_*+gamma_L)].

Use the PC-17 rational Taylor/Machin inverse-normal routine and Poisson
Hellinger coupling. Its finite output amplitude has total rate at most

    M_out=1/2[K_* ceil(sqrt(2m)) B_X
                 +(L_*+gamma_L) cr(Rz+1)]^2.

The Poisson cutoff is Q=ceil(16(M_out+ceil(log_2(64/delta)))). Thus bit time
and random-bit use are polynomial in d, rational input length L, numeric
kappa, Lambda, M_out, delta^(-1), log(xi^(-1)), and exact certificate/LDL
bit lengths. b_max and s_+ enter core precision and coordinate magnitudes
only through their rational bit lengths; the algorithm does not iterate
proportionally to their numeric values. Polynomiality in L alone still
requires numeric kappa, Lambda, M_out, and delta^(-1) polynomially bounded.
No p_H or log(1/p_H) is used by the conditional sampler. Physical
repeat-until-herald generation still costs 1/p_H.

4. Output-energy bound
======================

Since the accepted-core density is q(x)a(x)/alpha and a<=1,

    E_pi ||D0^(-1/2) X||^2 <= 2 s_+ K0 Lambda.

Regression gives X_U|X=x~N(Kx,V). Therefore the conditional expected total
output photon count obeys the explicit rational bound

    E[N_U|H]
      <= mu_* = [tr(V)+2 s_+ K0 Lambda tr(K D0 K^T)]/2.

This conditional mean-energy bound is distinct from M_out, the deterministic
finite-bit cap used to bound Poisson inversion work.

5. Scope and falsifiers
=======================

This is a sampler for a centered, rational, strictly positive-P Gaussian
with a supplied relative diagonal-scale certificate for the tilted
positive-count herald covariance. It handles arbitrary heterogeneity among
the local scalar quadrature scales b_j and standardized inter-mode
correlations certified by (MW); it does not cover arbitrary anisotropic
S at polynomial cost. A simple candidate D0 is obtained from the rational
per-mode average variance

    b_j=(S_(2j,2j)+S_(2j+1,2j+1))/2,

provided the exact rational sandwich (MW) passes. Certificate acquisition
and validation are charged; a failed certificate gives no sampler claim.

The claim fails if the likelihood tilt has a different sign, the proposal
normalizer is not the product shown, the whitened precision gap is not PSD,
the Gamma radial law is mis-scaled, or the finite output coupling loses a
factor depending on 1/p_H. A rational fixture verifies one heteroskedastic
two-herald covariance and the acceptance/Jensen and output-energy identities,
but this is finite evidence only. A distinct sibling-leaf adversarial review
found no fatal gap; see `conditional_gaussian_modewise_audit.txt`. The
derivation and this audit are internal checks, not external validation or
priority review. The all-zero herald uses the PC-17
exact Gaussian tilt with no rejection. Extension to displaced/non-positive-P
or general anisotropic covariances remains open.
