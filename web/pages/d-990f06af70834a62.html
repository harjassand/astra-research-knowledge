## Exact interface and the first-jet gate

Let f be a polynomial, Phi a global polynomial source automorphism,
p_i=Phi(q_i), and grad f(p_1)=grad f(p_2)=lambda. The chain rule gives

`grad(f o Phi)(q_i)=J_Phi(q_i)^T lambda`.

The original collision survives exactly when
`[J_Phi(q_1)-J_Phi(q_2)]^T lambda=0`. Determinant-one coordinates do
not by themselves supply this covector equality.

If a polynomial critical graph t=s(u) solves partial_t(f o Phi)=sigma
and `psi=(f o Phi-sigma t)|_(t=s(u))`, then
`grad_u psi=partial_u(f o Phi)|_(t=s(u))` because the critical equation
cancels all derivatives of s. To retain a collision after this
elimination, both source points must lie on this same critical graph
and the full transformed covectors must agree.

For the normalized form

`f0=t²+a(x,y)t+2(y rho0+2x eta0)`,

the actual v2 source pair, in normalized coordinates, is
`(x,y,t,rho0,eta0)=(1,-3/2,0,0,0),(-1,3/2,0,0,0)`.
Its residual covectors are c+=(-3,4)^T and c-=-c+, while both
t-components equal sigma=-19/2. They are therefore not a collision
of grad f0. A fibre automorphism Theta(x,y,t,R) fixing R=0 identically
in the base has zero base and t derivatives there. With residual
Jacobians J+ and J-, it retains the full collision exactly when

`(J+ + J-)^T c+=0`.

In particular, the same invertible first jet at both points is
impossible. This excludes base-independent origin-fixing fibre
automorphisms and the larger class with a constant invertible linear
part at the origin, regardless of their higher fibre terms. It only
excludes this retained witness, not other possible collisions.

The fibre Jacobian determinant of a global polynomial automorphism is
a common nonzero constant. Consequently R*=J- J+^-1 has determinant
one, and the criterion becomes R*^T c+=-c+. Thus tr(R*)=-2 is a
necessary scalar filter. Trace alone is insufficient: the specified
covector must be a -1 eigenvector. For t-dependent Theta a polynomial
critical graph remains an additional gate.

More generally, for

`f'=L t²+a(u)t+N h(u)K(u,R)`, L!=0,
`psi=N hK-(sigma-a)²/(4L)`,

at points satisfying K=0 and a=sigma the gradient row is
`N(h D_u K, h D_R K)`. Equal rows give the exact collision criterion.
If K(u,0)=0 identically, the first block vanishes. This statement uses
the actual first derivatives and is independent of any Hessian claim.

`(det Hess_(x,y) h0)_20=-456192 x^6 y^14`,
`(det Hess_(rho,eta) F)_14=x^10 y² eta²/8`.

`-57024 x^16 y^16 eta²`. The exact Hessian entry degrees are


`deg(det(Hpsi+HR)-det Hpsi)<=max(28+k,20+2k,10+3k,4k)`.
