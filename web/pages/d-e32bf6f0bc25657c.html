1. VERIFY THE DELIVERED N32/N64 MODELS WITHOUT RECOMPILING

library and its own neighbouring independent_posterior_verifier.py, included.

Expected scope: independent true-target joint posterior and every actual
rounded conditional-block bridge, 673 blocks, 1,378 joint entries. Read the
new receipt; neither a solver status nor the earlier stored PASS is a replay.
The original replay took 7.366 seconds; other machines need not match it.
Original end-to-end N64 generation exceeded its planned60s cap. This check
does not repair that benchmark failure or rerun model acquisition.

For generation commands, format contracts, dependencies and current hard caps,
read SAMPLER_REPLAY_README.txt or the implementation/README.txt in the archive.
The implementation uses |0>=spin-down, |1>=spin-up and Jz=k-N/2.
To use standard computational sigma_z=diag(+1,-1) with the same ordered
density matrices, flip the input field sign (or globally exchange basis labels).
The universal separability theorem itself is invariant under that convention.
The practical compiler is a bounded proposer with exact posterior acceptance;
universal success of its numerical optimizer is not claimed. Its tested
delta<=1 domain is narrower than the mathematical compiler's domain.

3. WHAT COMPUTATION DOES NOT CHECK AUTOMATICALLY

The endpoint proof also needs the analytic N>=200 contour/correction argument,
the explicit singular N2 measure and the full-space coherent-subset transfer.
Read OPTIMAL_JOIN.txt, OPTIMAL_ANALYTIC_TAIL.txt,
QUANTITATIVE_VERTICAL_GAP.txt and the independent analytic audits. Replaying
finite signs alone does not prove these mathematical dependencies.

The compiler complexity proof, unequal-spin projection proof, and moment
regularization proof are mathematical algorithms; bounded fixtures test their
specified identities and implementations. No fixture establishes external
priority, physical hardware preparation, a causal model comparison, or a
general-purpose optimizer's practical success rate.
