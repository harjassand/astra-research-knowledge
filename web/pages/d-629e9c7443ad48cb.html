## S1 — constrained adaptive LQR, chance constraints

- Source: Spencer Hutchinson, Nanfei Jiang, Mahnoosh Alizadeh, *Rate-Optimal Regret for the Safe Learning-based Control of the Constrained Linear Quadratic Regulator*, [arXiv:2604.22158v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22158v2), dated 2026-05-07.
- Read depth: arXiv HTML primary full text. Sections 2–3.2, theorem statement and full theorem in Appendix B, plus Lemma 1/covariance-tracking discussion.
- Interface: (x_{t+1}=A_*x_t+B_*u_t+w_t), i.i.d. (w_t\sim N(0,W)); full states; unknown ([A_*\ B_*]); known cost, chance constraints, and norm bound.
- Assumptions: (i) zero input is strongly stable and strictly chance-feasible; (ii) (W\succeq\sigma I), (\sigma>0); (iii) (|[A_*\ B_*]|\le S).
- Result: per-time chance constraints and high-probability (widetilde O(\sqrt T)) regret relative to the optimal strongly stable linear controller obeying the same chance constraints. Constants are polynomial in (sigma^{-1}), the strict-feasibility margin inverse, and other problem parameters.
- Acquisition/cost boundary: algorithm begins with uniform input dither on a sphere, then zero control, computes LS, and uses covariance SDPs with delayed updates. Dither radius is proportional to the safety slack. The full-rank (W) assumption appears beyond regression: it sets ((\widetilde\kappa,\widetilde\gamma)) in the covariance-tracking lemma, with (widetilde\gamma=\sigma/(4\nu)) in the full theorem. Thus a replacement must recover multi-step covariance forgetting and transient constraint control.
- Source-specific internal note: the 2026 algorithm already has a safe excitation input; the candidate only weakens the environmental-noise/rank argument if its reachability geometry suffices. It is not a new dither method.

## S2 — robust adaptive discrete-time CBF, bounded disturbances

- Source: Changrui Liu, Anil Alan, Shengling Shi, Bart De Schutter, *Robust Adaptive Discrete-Time Control Barrier Certificate*, [arXiv:2508.08153v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08153v3), dated 2026-09-08; arXiv says to appear in Automatica.
- Read depth: arXiv HTML primary full text. System model and estimator assumptions in §§2.2–2.4; robust adaptive certificate and invariance in §3.2/Theorem 2; RLS/set-membership instantiation in §4; explicit excitation boundary in Remark 4.2.
- Interface: compact state set; input-affine model with known (f_d,\phi,g) and unknown constant (\theta\) in a known compact polytope; bounded polytope disturbances. A set estimator must maintain (\theta_*\in\Theta_t) and bounded point-estimate increments. A valid parameterized robust-adaptive DT-CBF is supplied.
- Result: the online admissible input set is nonempty and any policy taking admissible inputs preserves the time-varying robust safe sets against all allowed disturbances and parameter mismatch.
- Boundary: the guarantee does not need parameter convergence or persistent excitation. The paper explicitly leaves excitation/convergence outside scope; it supplies no general acquisition sample complexity or regret result.

## S3 — earlier robust constrained-LQR regret

- Source: Yingying Li, Subhro Das, Jeff Shamma, Na Li, *Safe Adaptive Learning-based Control for Constrained Linear Quadratic Regulators with Regret Guarantees*, [arXiv:2111.00411](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00411).
- Read depth: primary arXiv abstract and the 2026 paper's related-work comparison.
- Role: earlier single-trajectory robust-constraint benchmark with (widetilde O(T^{2/3})) regret under its assumptions. It is not the same chance-constrained interface as S1.
