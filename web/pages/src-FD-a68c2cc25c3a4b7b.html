1. Exact interface and scope

Let V=F_2^n. Q:V->V is a bijection ordered lexicographically and the ONLY
source port is Cmp(x,y)=1[Q(x)<Q(y)]. Supply n,r,d,beta, h=n-r, d>=2,
beta in (0,1/2]. The first h output coordinates q_0,...,q_(h-1) have degree

Delta_(v_i)q_i=1 and Delta_(v_j)q_i=0 for i<j<h. Neither q, the v_i, their
inverse, source ranks, a prefix sampler nor a period oracle is supplied.

Prefix-only output through m<=h requires the degree/toggle promises only for
those acquired coordinates, with later hidden flag directions through h
retained by them. The remaining source order inside each acquired prefix
fiber may be arbitrary. Full rank and inverse additionally require ALL n
output coordinates degree<=d; only then does the tail coefficient fit apply.
No full rank/inverse statement is made for arbitrary suffixes.

witness_compiler.acquire sees only dimensions/promises, seed and comparator.
Test-only truth and source data live in run_witness.py. Copied old code is

2. Independent audit of the witness geometry

At a correct stage k let S be the exact acquired-prefix stabilizer. Then
h-k<=dim S<=n-k. For the true next polynomial q put
 C={s in S:Delta_s q is constant}, lambda(s)=q(s)+q(0),
 F_s(x)=q(x+s)+q(x)+q(s)+q(0).
The derivative cocycle proves C is a subspace and lambda linear on C.
The hidden suffix flag spans an (h-k)-dimensional subspace of C and one
of its vectors has response one. A genuine selected own-toggle v lies in C
and has lambda(v)=1.

For any s notin C, F_s is nonzero, has degree<=d-1 and is invariant under
C+span(s), whose dimension is >=h-k+1. Thus F_s descends to <=k+r-1 bits.
Restricting to a linear section preserves degree; elementary Reed-Muller
distance gives
 Pr_uniform x[F_s(x)=1]>=mu_k=2^(-min(d-1,k+r-1)).
If k+r<=1, its quotient has dimension zero and F_s(0)=0, a contradiction:
C=S. For W containing C and W!=C, uniform s in W escapes C with probability
>=1/2. Independent uniform x therefore finds F_s(x)=1 with probability
>=mu_k/2. No hidden polynomial support is supplied to this test.

Given a detection (s*,x*), P(s)=F_s(x*) on W is C-invariant, P(0)=0,
P(s*)=1. Its period space D contains C and excludes s*, and
codim_W D<=dim W-(h-k). Every nonperiod t has Delta_t P nonzero and invariant
under D+span(t), so it descends to at most codim_W D-1 bits. Consequently
 Pr_uniform z in W[Delta_t P(z)=1]>=nu=2^(-(cap-1)),
where cap=dim W-(h-k). This assertion does NOT require degree bounds on P.
It does require the genuine semantic invariant C subset W.

The dictionary scans a known W basis e_j. For each represented quotient
class u, it tests e_j+sum u_i b_i for being a period. Genuine periods never
reject. A false all-zero pass has probability <=(1-nu)^Lp. On the event of
no false passes, induction identifies all quotient columns correctly and
their GF(2) kernel is exactly D. At most dim(W)*2^cap candidates are tested.
The label identity uses FOUR actual q labels per period probe:
 q(z)+q(z+x*)+q(z+t)+q(z+t+x*).
Each q label is one actual comparator after genuine own-toggle selection.

Exact cap-one shortcut (v3): if cap=1, nonzero P with P(0)=0 is exactly a
nonzero linear form. Acquire its row from P(e_j) using two q labels per W
basis vector, reusing cached q(0),q(x*). Its kernel is D exactly conditional
on the preceding semantic promises; no period-sampling miss remains in this
case. The general dictionary remains available when cap>=2.

3. Acquisition, interpolation and exact local update

Start W=S and cache q(0). While dim W>h-k, search for a witness using L fresh
(s,x) tests. A whole zero epoch closes W as an estimated C. A detection learns
D and sets W=D. Required guards are: D is a subspace of old W; dim D>=h-k;
selected v in D; detected s* notin D; and strict dimension drop. At final W,
choose a linear complement U. Then dim U<=k+r. Acquire q on every degree<=d
ANF anchor in U, plus q(w)+q(0) on a W basis. On semantic success,
 q(y+z)=q_U(y)+lambda(z), y in U,z in W,
so this reconstructs q. Locally expand the raw ANF at M_d(n) coefficient
anchors using no source queries. Check its own-toggle and old invariances.
This implementation retains raw expansion work; compact-update acquisition
without that work is not claimed. Source-free static execution is compact.

Recompute the candidate's exact full constant-direction space and stabilizer
by descending derivative-degree filtration. At a filtration level ell, the
degree-ell coefficient map of Delta_s q is linear on the subspace where all
derivatives have degree<=ell; its kernel reduces the maximum derivative
degree. Repeating to ell=1 gives the full C, then the response kernel gives
the full period space. This is exact local ANF algebra, not sampled recognition.

For a genuine stage with a_k=codim_(S_k) C_k, nonzero lambda gives
 dim S_(k+1)=dim S_k-a_k-1,
 d_(k+1)=d_k+a_k, d_k=n-k-dim S_k.
Thus sum a_k<=r over the whole prefix. Every correct witness drop consumes
at least one unit of this total defect. There are at most r costly witnesses
and at most m+r outer fixed-W epochs on correct histories.

4. All-history guards and the independently found cap correction

After the first semantic error, candidate exact stabilizers may refund
MEASURED defect. The learner therefore keeps a separate persistent cumulative
global_drop AND paid_dictionaries counter across all stages. Once a detection
occurs, it returns UNKNOWN BEFORE starting a dictionary if either counter is
already >=r. After a returned dictionary it checks global_drop+drop<=r before
updating. An explicit persistent outer_epochs counter caps epochs at m+r.

The pre-invocation check is necessary: a post-drop-only check could pay one
(r+1)st dictionary before rejecting a later bad-history refund. The initial
s09b pseudocode was ambiguous on this order; independent inspection raised
it and s09b confirmed/corrected the requirement. Current code enforces both
counters before any paid dictionary labels. A dictionary that aborts after
payment is still counted, and terminates the learner. Hence even aborted
histories pay at most r dictionaries, each with <=n*2^r*Lp probes. This is
also why a budget statement cannot condition away failure paths.

Independent geometric guards do not certify truth. A false period pass can
retain the own-toggle, remove the detected direction, strictly drop dimension
and yet return a wrong space. The forced semantic-miss/refund fixture in the
independent l01 audit exercises this distinction and the pre-spend guard.
No failed-history probability theorem is applied after a first wrong step.

5. Raw label acquisition, exact tournament and failure accounting

For nonzero v in exact S_k, define g_v(x)=1+Cmp(x,x+v). If Delta_v q=1,
g_v=q exactly. Otherwise g_v error against q is
 e=(1/2)Pr[Delta_v q=0]>=2^-d,
because 1+Delta_v q is a nonzero degree<=d-1 polynomial. The preserved
paired-cut validator draws a uniform acquired prefix and two independent
uniform acquired-prefix-fiber points, folds them to g_v=0 and g_v=1, then
compares them. It pays three comparisons and rejects a nongood v with
probability >=gamma=2^(-2d). Good directions never reject. At least a
fraction p=2^(-r-1) of uniform S_k directions are good. Zero draws are skipped.

When dim S_k=h-k, the true q has constant derivative on all of S_k. The
preserved exact-on-completion basis tournament chooses a response-one
direction for any unacquired suffix order. Its match probes agree with
conditional probability>=1/2 under any strict order. With
 P=sum_(k<m)(n-k-1), H=ceil(4P+8 log(4/beta)),
all histories pay <=2H+P source comparisons. Exhaustion before P matches has
probability<=beta/4. Expected tournament comparisons<=5P. This uses a single
global cap, not a logarithmic cap per basis-pair match.

For m>0 use
 R=ceil(log(4m/beta)/p), delta_raw=beta/(4mR^2),
 t=ceil(log(1/delta_raw)/[-log(1-gamma)]),
 L=ceil(log(8R(m+r)/beta)/[-log(1-2^-d)]),
 eta=beta/(8rR),
 Lp=ceil(log(n2^r/eta)/[-log(1-2^(-(r-1)))]) for r>=2;
 Lp=1 for r=1; no witness acquisition for r=0.
The code uses these uniform conservative caps, while recording the stronger
stage mu_k and local cap-based dictionary Lp. Exact rational checks verify
all nontrivial geometric failure inequalities in the saved finite runs.

Before first wrong semantics: raw candidate-cap failures total<=beta/4;
tournament cap failure<=beta/4; first false raw pass<=mR delta_raw=beta/(4R);
first outer false closure<=(m+r)(1-2^-d)^L<=beta/(8R);
first period false pass<=r eta=beta/(8R). The cap-one shortcut adds no error.
So first wrong semantics<=beta/(2R), and total UNKNOWN-or-wrong probability
<=beta/2+beta/(2R)<=beta. Guards do not reject any fully semantically correct
history that avoids cap failures. The probability theorem assumes independent
ideal conditional fair draws; seeded Python PRNGs are fixture reproducibility,
not physical entropy acquisition.

6. Comparator, bit-work, storage and execution bounds

Write M_d(t)=sum_(j=0)^min(d,t) binom(t,j),
 A_(d,m)=sum_(k=0)^(m-1) M_d(k+r),
 B=A_(d,m)+m(n+1)+3(m+r)L+4r n2^r Lp.
Every history pays <=B reconstruction comparisons, including all failed
histories: m cached origins, at most mn affine responses, A interpolation
anchors, 3 per outer probe, and at most r paid dictionary invocations. The
cap-one row cost 2n is smaller than its conservative dictionary allowance.
For full output let Tail=M_d(n)(2^r-1); for prefix-only Tail=0. Then
 Q_max<=B+3mRt+2H+P+Tail,
 E Q<=B+3m[(1+beta/2)t+gamma^-1(1-p)/p]+5P+Tail.
The expected raw term couples pre-first-error acquisition to the first good
direction and early rejection. A stage entered after first wrong semantics
has probability<=beta/(2R) and raw validation costs<=3Rt. The inverse-R
semantic allocation cancels R in this charge. Do not transfer this sharper
expected formula to the preserved old fixed-fraction raw/member budgets.

For fixed d,r and beta^-1 polynomial in n, B=A_(d,m)+O(n^2+n log n), raw
cap cost O(n log^2 n), tournament cost O(n^2). Thus prefix comparisons are
A_(d,m)+O(n^2). Full output additionally pays the existing Tail=O(n^d).
Nothing here removes the full-output tail or proves novelty/query optimality.
