Title:Separability criteria for genuine multiparticle entanglement

Authors:Otfried Gühne, Michael Seevinck
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Separability criteria for genuine multiparticle entanglement, by Otfried G\"uhne and 1 other authors

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:  We present a method to derive separability criteria for the different classes of multiparticle entanglement, especially genuine multiparticle entanglement. The resulting criteria are necessary and sufficient for certain families of states. Further, the criteria are superior to all known entanglement criteria for many other families; also they allow the detection of bound entanglement. We next demonstrate that they are easily implementable in experiments and discuss applications to the decoherence of multiparticle entangled states.
