PURE-INPUT SCHMIDT-SECTOR SCORE AND THE LOCAL-IDENTITY OBSTRUCTION
2026-10-08

Question and verdict

For the selected r=2,d=65 channel, I derive the exact pure-input expression for Gamma_K^2 and resolve how the high-norm local-identity tensor traceless sector interacts with pure inputs. The obstruction is suppressed for every pure input by its Schmidt weights: each local sector occupies at most 1/(N+1) of the traceless input's squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm, and its contribution after the branch sum is at most 4 kappa^2(P-1/N)/N, where P is the common marginal purity and kappa=130 branches per leg. This eliminates the specific local-sector witness as a pure-input counterexample.

It does not close the selected gate. The correlation sector M_N^0 tensor M_N^0 has squared input norm 1-2P/N+1/N^2, which is close to one for every pure input, including maximally entangled inputs where the local sectors vanish. A sector-split sufficient inequality is given below. Its unresolved term is the branch-sum quadratic form on the pure Schmidt correlation variety; marginal weights alone cannot control it. The global purity score remains UNKNOWN without that bound or the established coefficient-net route.

Exact pure-input score identity

Let H_in=C^N tensor C^N, n=N^2, and let W_s, s in S, be the K=260 unitary branches. Write rho=|psi><psi| and

    S(X)=sum_s W_s X W_s^*.

For the equal-weight complementary channel, let G_psi be the K-by-K Gram matrix of the branch vectors W_s psi. Its diagonal is one, Tr G_psi=K, and Phi(rho) is G_psi/K up to transpose/conjugation convention. The score convention in the cycle4 gate is exactly

    Gamma_K^2 = max_{||psi||=1} ||G_psi-I_K||_HS^2
               = max_{||psi||=1} sum_{s!=t} |<psi,W_s^* W_t psi>|^2.

Equivalently, since Tr(S(rho)^2)=sum_{s,t}|<psi,W_s^*W_t psi>|^2 and S(I_n)=K I_n,

    Gamma_K^2 = max_psi [ ||S(rho-I_n/n)||_HS^2 + K^2/n - K ].

Thus Gamma_K<=C+s is implied by, and in this decomposition equivalent to, the pure-state inequality

    ||S(rho-I_n/n)||_HS^2 <= Lambda_N,
    Lambda_N = K+(C+s)^2-K^2/N^2,

with the selected C^2=50 and s=0.165. This is a restriction to rank-one rho, not a norm claim for arbitrary traceless operators.

Schmidt-sector decomposition

There are kappa=2d=130 inverse-closed branches on each leg, so K=2 kappa. Define the self-adjoint local superoperators

    T_j(X)=sum_(i=1)^65 [U_(j,i) X U_(j,i)^* + U_(j,i)^* X U_(j,i)],
    S = T_1 tensor id + id tensor T_2.

Each T_j preserves trace, fixes I_N, and has HS norm at most kappa. Let Pi_0(X)=X-Tr(X)I_N/N. For a pure bipartite input, put

    rho_A=Tr_B rho, rho_B=Tr_A rho,
    A=Pi_0(rho_A), B=Pi_0(rho_B),
    C=(Pi_0 tensor Pi_0)(rho).

Then the orthogonal decomposition of the global traceless input is

    rho-I_(N^2)/N^2 = A tensor I_N/N + I_N/N tensor B + C,

where C is traceless on both legs. The three sectors are invariant and mutually orthogonal under S, and

    S(rho-I_(N^2)/N^2)
      = (T_1+kappa id)(A) tensor I_N/N
        + I_N/N tensor (T_2+kappa id)(B)
        + (T_1 tensor id + id tensor T_2)(C).

Consequently the exact Schmidt-sector expression is

    Gamma_K^2(psi)
      = (1/N)|| (T_1+kappa id)(rho_A-I/N) ||_HS^2
        + (1/N)|| (T_2+kappa id)(rho_B-I/N) ||_HS^2
        + || (T_1 tensor id + id tensor T_2)(C) ||_HS^2
        - K + K^2/N^2.

For an explicit Schmidt form, write psi=sum_(a=1)^N sqrt(lambda_a)|a>_A|a>_B, allowing zero lambda_a, and let E_ab=|a><b| and F_ab=|a>_B<b|. Then

    rho_A-I/N = sum_a (lambda_a-1/N) E_aa,
    rho_B-I/N = sum_a (lambda_a-1/N) F_aa,
    C = sum_(a,b) sqrt(lambda_a lambda_b) E_ab tensor F_ab
        - sum_a lambda_a E_aa tensor I_N/N
        - sum_a lambda_a I_N/N tensor F_aa + I_N tensor I_N/N^2.

This is the requested pure-input/Schmidt expression: the maximum ranges over every probability vector lambda and every pair of local Schmidt frames. The reduced states have the same nonzero Schmidt weights.

Exact Schmidt-weight suppression of the local sectors

Set P=Tr(rho_A^2)=Tr(rho_B^2)=sum_a lambda_a^2. Then

    ||A||_HS^2=||B||_HS^2=P-1/N,
    ||A tensor I_N/N||_HS^2=||I_N/N tensor B||_HS^2=(P-1/N)/N,
    ||rho-I_(N^2)/N^2||_HS^2=1-1/N^2,
    ||C||_HS^2=1-2P/N+1/N^2.

The relative weight of either local sector in rho-I_(N^2)/N^2 is

    (P-1/N)/(N(1-1/N^2)) = (N P-1)/(N^2-1) <= 1/(N+1),

because 1/N<=P<=1. Together the two local sectors occupy at most 2/(N+1) of the total squared norm. The bound is exact at product pure inputs; at maximally entangled inputs their weight is zero.

This suppression also survives the branch sum quantitatively. Each local T_j is a sum of kappa HS isometries, hence ||T_j+kappa id||_(2->2)<=2 kappa. The two local output sectors therefore obey

    L_A+L_B <= 8 kappa^2 (P-1/N)/N <= 8 kappa^2 (1-1/N)/N.

At kappa=130 the exact prefactor is 135200/N. In particular, the fixed local-eigenvector obstruction from the full-HS no-go cannot be inserted as a pure-input witness with unit weight: every pure input scales its local component by the above Schmidt-purity factor.

Sector-split certificate and precise remaining obstruction

Let

    R_11 = (T_1 tensor id + id tensor T_2)|_(M_N^0 tensor M_N^0),
    Lambda_11 = ||R_11||_(2->2).

A valid sector-aware sufficient bound, for every pure input, is

    ||S(rho-I_(N^2)/N^2)||_HS^2
       <= 8 kappa^2(P-1/N)/N
          + Lambda_11^2(1-2P/N+1/N^2).

Thus a full-HS bound on the whole traceless space can be replaced by a bound only on the correlation sector plus the explicit Schmidt-weighted local debit. For the selected gate it would suffice to show the right side is <=Lambda_N for all P in [1/N,1]. As N grows, the local term is O(kappa^2/N), while the correlation factor is near one; so this architecture still needs Lambda_11^2 approximately no larger than K+(sqrt(50)+0.165)^2. The trivial triangle bound Lambda_11<=2 kappa is much too weak. No claim that the needed correlation-sector bound holds is made here.

The exact correlation quadratic form is

    ||R_11 C||_HS^2
      = <C,(T_1^2 tensor id + id tensor T_2^2
              + 2 T_1 tensor T_2) C>_HS,

since T_1 and T_2 are self-adjoint on their local HS spaces. A genuinely rank-one-aware improvement would bound this form over the explicit C_(lambda,frames) above, rather than over all M_N^0 tensor M_N^0. The Schmidt weights alone cannot do that: for a maximally entangled input P=1/N, C=rho-I_(N^2)/N^2 and ||C||^2=1-1/N^2, while both local sectors vanish. For a product pure input, ||C||^2=(1-1/N)^2, also near one. Hence there is no small Schmidt-weight multiplier on the correlation sector. Any certificate must use its operator-Schmidt structure, the equal marginal Schmidt spectra, purity/idempotence constraints, or properties of the concrete U_(j,i); local-sector suppression by itself is insufficient.
