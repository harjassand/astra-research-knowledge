Root proposed this extension after the quarter compiler proof. The algebraic
margin below is reconstructed here. This file does NOT audit the separate
c07_s02 delta1 analytic/finite PSD certificates, whose validity is a premise.

The quadrature proof only needs a positive measure mu>=c dp with an explicit
rational c>0 and polynomial log(1/c). A continuous density for all of mu is
not required. Grams, cardinal-polynomial weights, gaps and all bit bounds
remain valid if the remainder is atomic.

Let unnormalized q_k(delta)=exp[-delta(k-N/2)^2/N]/C(N,k), 0<=delta<=1.
Given U~Normal(0,2(1-delta)/N), exactly
q_k(delta)=E[e^(U(k-N/2))]q_k(1).
For legal block s0,1,
H_s(delta)=E[e^((s-N/2)U)D(U) H_s(1) D(U)], D_ii=e^(iU).
Here i is the nonnegative INTEGER index, not the imaginary unit.
If lambda_s is a certified positive lower eigenvalue bound for H_s(1),
v^T H_s(delta)v>=lambda_s E[e^((s-N/2)U)||D(U)v||^2].
On |U|<=2, all exponents (s-N/2+2i)U are at least -N, since the legal i
ranges put s-N/2+2i in[-N/2,N/2]. Var U<=1 for N>=2; Chebyshev gives
P(|U|<=2)>=3/4. Hence H_s(delta)>=(3/4)e^-N lambda_s I uniformly in delta.
The normalized coefficients divide by Z_delta<=N+1, so their block margin
is at least beta=(3/4)4^-N min_s lambda_s/(N+1), using e<4.

For mu_uniform=dp, q_uniform,k=1/[(N+1)C(N,k)]. Its Hankel entries are at
most1/(N+1); their dimensions are <=N+1, so ||H_s,uniform||<=1.

normalized homogeneous coefficient vector leaves both Hankel blocks PSD.
The compact Bernstein-curve / half-line SOS sufficiency gives a positive
representing remainder nu. Thus mu_delta=c dp+nu exactly, for every delta
in[0,1], with this SAME positive c for the fixed N.

Extracting lambda_s from a rational outward interval LDL certificate needs
no floating eigenvalue optimization. If H=L diag(d) L^T, unit lower L,
all interval pivots have lower bound d_min>0, and |L_ij|<=B, then
||L^-1||<=d(1+dB)^d by its finite nilpotent inverse series. Consequently
lambda_s>=d_min/[d^2(1+dB)^(2d)], an explicit rational lower bound.
All interval arithmetic and the certificate bit lengths remain charged.
For a finite prefix N2..39, finite constant tables suffice; a uniform
polynomial bit statement follows if their supplied bit lengths are bounded
by the reported finite certificate budget and every entry is independently
checked. None is silently promoted from an optimizer's "positive" flag.
