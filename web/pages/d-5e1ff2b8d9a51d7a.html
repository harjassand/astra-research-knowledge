# Independent hostile gate: original-generator filling with a linear buffer

7 October 2026. pde_spectrum9, verified GPT-6.1 Sol/max, no nested workers.
Analytic verdict: **PASS with the exact realization hypotheses below**.
No physical code at the source's enormous scale, patch matrix, or thermal

## Claim, source, and adoption boundary

Primary source: Shankar Balasubramanian, Margarita Davydova, Ting-Chun
Lin, *A passive self-correcting quantum memory in three dimensions*,
arXiv:2605.10943v1, submitted 11 May 2026. The official v1 record and
recoverable TeX archive are pinned in this worker's sources. I read the
listed decisive excerpts and actual figures; I did not read or audit all
102 pages, the full lifetime proof, or its entire prior history.

Fix the source's finite seed and alternating R_X/R_Z replacements with
one fixed odd ell. Fix the **X–Z auxiliary diagonal** in every parent
square. This is the diagonal drawn in replacement-fold. It affects the
piecewise affine realization and is removed from the final Tanner
complex; it is never promoted to a new stabilizer or a new gluing. Every
triangle of an X-star contains X and every triangle of a Z-star contains
Z. No assertion covers arbitrary Q–Q diagonal choices.

The physical positions must satisfy, at every perturbation step,

    L <= 2002lambda, l >= (135/2)lambda,
    w >= (2/5)lambda, lambda >= 10^24,
    ell is the least odd integer >2001lambda.          (1)

Here L bounds side lengths above, l bounds them below, and w is the
minimum vertex-to-opposite-closed-side distance. Fine Tanner edges have
length at most one. The original separated-cap parameters with
alpha=1000,alpha2=139/2 imply (1), paying old auxiliary length two;
rounding_spectral's separate alpha2=80 readmission gives stronger bounds.
Any existence/density admission for these positions is a separate
premise until that argument is adopted. The present local proof does
not use a density value. The literal illustrative source pair
lambda≈5.4e24,t≈6.6e10 is not certified by this audit.

For these hypotheses there is a constant C independent of recursion
depth such that every global X or Z stabilizer supported on qubits in
B(c,r), r>=1, is a product of the **original checks** whose complete
qubit supports lie in B(c,Cr). For all r>=0 replace Cr by C max(r,1).
At each fine level the small-ball anchor proves the stronger statement
for every closed error. At the finite seed only global stabilizers are
fillable. Components of a global stabilizer are not assumed to be global
stabilizers; this is essential to the anchor proof.

This is proportional-buffer generator localization. It is not a
fixed-collar TQO-2 theorem, generic perturbation stability, a thermal
lifetime theorem under perturbed Davies dynamics, or efficient thermal
encoding. Any later graph enlargement or stability transfer needs its
own proof. The source authors claim the recursive code and unperturbed
memory theorem; proportional-buffer localization is the scoped deduction
being gated here. Priority for it is UNKNOWN.

## 1. Reachable combinatorial invariant, including all cap layers

Use the old cochain convention A0 --d0--> A1 --d1--> A2 over F2.
For every reachable old code:

1. Every qubit has at least one X and one Z neighbor.
2. Every X/Z check pair has zero or exactly two shared qubits.
3. Each check link is connected and has an edge. Its vertices are the
   neighboring qubits; an opposite check joins its two shared qubits.

The actual primary Tanner-base.png, visually inspected here, gives in
qubit order NW,NE,C,SW,SE

    HX=[11100;00111], HZ=[10110;01101].                 (2)

All four X/Z pairs overlap in two qubits and each link is a three-vertex
path. The Tanner graph is connected. For example X=(1,0,0,1,0) and
Z=(1,1,0,0,0) are closed nonboundary logical errors. A claim that every
closed seed error is fillable would be false; that strengthened claim
is not used.

For R_X the actual local complexes are

    C_x = cellular cochains of Y_x,
    C_q = cellular cochains of Y_q shifted by one,
    C_z = the two separate point copies z0,z1 shifted by two.

Y_x is two subdivided copies of the parent X-star joined by its boundary
cylinder. Y_q is two subdivided copies of the Q–Z star joined at their
Z endpoints by cylinder edges. Gluing is restriction Y_q→Y_x and
Y_z→Y_q, exactly paper.tex lines1558–1674. All fine X checks are Y_x
vertices. The fine Z checks have precisely three types:

* A Y_x face and a Y_x vertex have two common edge qubits when incident,
  and none otherwise. Fine cells are regular: parent square corners,
  the two layer labels, and a cell's four corners are distinct abstractly.
* A Y_q edge and a Y_x vertex share its Y_x edge qubit and its one
  matching Y_q endpoint qubit if incident, hence two; otherwise zero.
  This includes an endpoint-cylinder edge z0–z1. Its two layers are
  distinct vertices even when their physical positions coincide.
* An inherited cap z_sigma and a Y_x boundary vertex at that old Z
  corner have the old X/Z overlap, zero or two. The qubits are the
  matching Y_q endpoints in layer sigma. This calculation is made
  **separately for sigma=0 and sigma=1**. An interior point of a cone
  edge cannot see a cap; restriction to a cap is at its corner only.

All other block pairs give zero. Thus no unexamined overlap-four case
is hidden at a corner. The source's integer subdivision ell>=5 prevents
two different small face corners from becoming one abstract vertex.
Physical flattening identifies images, not vectors or abstract cells.

The link induction also needs all branch and seam types, not just H1:

* At each old X apex the fine vertex link is the subdivided old connected
  check link.
* An interior face vertex has the usual circle link. On an interior
  radial X–Q edge with d incident sectors its link is a subdivision of
  K_(2,d); d>=1, so it is connected even in the boundary d=1 case.
* At an equatorial old Q vertex the cone contributes a star on its d
  boundary directions and the cylinder contributes a second center
  joining those same directions. The link is K_(2,d), not two disjoint
  copies of a star. At an equatorial old Z corner the link has degree
  two because a nonzero X/Z pair has exactly one square; the cylinder
  closes its two incident sectors by a path. Interior boundary-edge
  vertices give the same cone-sector/cylinder link after subdivision.
* Every extra shifted-Y_q vertex qubit in a fine X check is attached to
  this connected face link by an incident Y_q edge check and its
  corresponding Y_x edge qubit. Y_q has no isolated vertex.
* A fine Z face link is its boundary cycle. A fine Z Y_q-edge link has
  its two endpoint qubits joined through the matching Y_x edge qubit
  for every old X neighbor of q, a connected K_(2,m), m>=1. A cap
  z_sigma has the actual old Z link, independently in each layer.

There are no further gluing types in replacement-Cx, replacement-Cq,
replacement-full and the quotient specification. A corner connects
identified boundary cells; it never identifies the two layer labels.
Every old X–Q/Q–Z edge belongs to a square: choose an opposite-color
neighbor of its qubit; overlap two supplies the second qubit. This
justifies all nonempty d,m sets above. Every Y_x edge has endpoint X
checks and an incident face check; every shifted Y_q vertex has an X
restriction check and an incident Y_q edge check. Nonisolation follows.
R_Z is the literal color-swapped/transposed template, including the
two X caps. Applying the same classified cases proves the invariant
in both alternating steps. This is a universal induction on the
reachable template, not a finite test on an arbitrary CSS matrix.

## 2. Controlled maps and why the second transfer term vanishes

Inside each finite local block split C^j=B^j⊕H^j⊕L^j with
d:L^j→B^(j+1) an isomorphism. Inclusion i, projection p, and inverse h
on B give pi=id, ip=id+dh+hd and hi=ph=h^2=0. Matrices are local to
their tags; arbitrary Gaussian elimination choices do not become a
support-free global oracle. Gluing g moves tags X→Q→Z, whereas h
preserves them, so (gh)^3=(hg)^3=0. The finite HPL formulas are

    i'=i+hgi+hghgi, p'=p+pgh+pghgh,
    h'=h+hgh+hghgh, i'p'=id+Dh'+h'D.                  (3)

Nilpotence alone does not delete the transferred pghgi term. In R_X,
the degree-zero two-hop source H0(C_X) would require h_Q landing in
C_Q0=0; the degree-one source H1(C_Q) stops at C_Z1=0. In R_Z,
the possible two-hop degree-zero source ends after the second g in
C_Z1, whose cohomology projection is zero because H1(C_Z)=0. The
degree-one possibilities stop. Hence pghgi=0 by the actual degrees.
The induced complexes are consequently

    RX: E0=A0,E1=A1,E2=(A2⊕A2)⊕M2,
        dE0=d0, dE1=(d1,d1,0);
    RZ: E0=(A0⊕A0)⊕M0,E1=A1,E2=A2,
        dE0(u,v,m)=d0(u+v), dE1=d1.                  (4)

Selectors J:A→E and Q:E→A choose J2z=(z,z,0),Q2(z,w,m)=z
for RX, and J0x=(x,0,0),Q0(x,y,m)=x+y for RZ, with identity
selectors in the remaining degrees. They are chain maps, QJ=id and
J1Q1=id. They retain the incoming boundary map; mere H1 isomorphism
would not suffice.

Set P1=Q1p'^1,P0=Q0p'^0,I1=i'^1J1,I0=i'^0J0,K=h'^1.
For a closed fine X error e,

    e+I1P1e=D0Ke, P1D0=d0P0, D0I0=I1d0.              (5)

For Z chains use P1Z=J1^T(i'^1)^T, P2Z=J2^T(i'^2)^T,
I1Z=(p'^1)^TQ1^T,I2Z=(p'^2)^TQ2^T,KZ=(h'^2)^T.
Transposing gives the corresponding incoming D1^T identity and
closedness d0^TP1Z e=0. Both sectors use original fine generators.

A block lies within 2ell of its perturbed parent tag, via two parent
Tanner sides. At most two tag hops occur in (3). Paying whole-check
completion gives valid one-level bounds

    P: fine radius r -> old radius r/lambda+a,
    K: whole fine generator radius r+b,
    I: old whole-check radius s -> fine radius lambda s+b,
    a=1000+2ell/lambda+2, b=6ell+2.                    (6)

The injection comparison includes the additional one old Tanner step
from an old qubit support to its check center: the offset is at most
(1000+3)lambda+2ell+1<=b. Omitting that lambda is not legal. Tag
reversal under transposition preserves the bounds. A naive local
contraction costs O(ell^6) binary operations for O(ell^2) dimensions;
it was not executed here and is not practical at these constants.

## 3. Exact small-ball geometric carrier classification

For B(c,s) set R=(1+2L/w)s. Every triangle angle has sine at least
w/L: take its longest side, whose line altitude is at least w;
all other line altitudes are no smaller. If the ball meets two sides
sharing v, choose encounter points y,z in it. Then
|y-v|sin(angle)<=|y-z|<=2s and |v-c|<=R.

In the graph of formal triangles meeting B(c,s), join only across an
**identified abstract** common side or vertex that meets the ball.
When 2R<l, a component is a single triangle, one edge-star, or one
vertex-star with its vertex within R. A path switching side labels
first finds their shared vertex v within R. Continuing through a side
not containing v would place the adjacent endpoint u within R too,
contradicting |u-v|>=l>2R. This inductive path argument proves the
classification, including vertex crossings. Unrelated physical
triangle crossings give no graph edge.

Join supported fine qubits through shared syndrome checks. The joining
Tanner edges lie in B(c,r+1). Their intrinsic parent carriers, split
at the formal diagonal, make exactly such abstract incidence paths;
the source boundary/corner quotient is their only transition between
parent faces. Each interaction component of a closed error is closed:
a syndrome check contributing to two parts would join them. This does
not make either part a global boundary.

Write

    r*=a lambda/(lambda-1), r0=r*+1,
    s=r+128, R=10011s, M=R+8ell+100, r<=r0.            (7)

Then r0<5006,s<5134,R<51,396,474 and

    2(R+65)<l-16, w>R+s.                              (8)

Thus the classification applies with radius r+1 and all margins used
below are uniform in depth. The enormous scale gap is explicit.

## 4. Actual inset mesh and its unique far complement component

In RX, let Gamma=boundary of the parent X-star. The overlap/link
invariant makes it a connected graph (each old Z has degree two in
this link and subdivides a link edge). Y_x consists of two subdivided
cones on Gamma joined by the Gamma cylinder. H1(Y_x)=0 over F2:
both cones are contractible and the connected intersection has reduced
H0 zero. This is a property of the **full** complex, not yet a relative
filling claim.

Compare the actual positions with an ideal map of the same abstract
Y_x onto the full parent X-star, collapsing only its cylinder image.
In the normalized primary flat grid put N=(ell-1)/2. Ideal blue
coordinate tau=i/N and actual blue coordinate (1+2i)/ell differ by
(1-tau)/ell, between zero and 1/ell. The two coordinate derivatives
of the affine X–Z triangle chart are parent Tanner sides, not its
auxiliary diagonal. Hence actual/ideal vertex difference is bounded
by two, and the looser eight is safe. Shifted boundary endpoints and
the mirrored RZ grid have the same bounded offset. The transverse
flattened cells retain their abstract endpoints and cylinder edges.

Fine face vertices are at most four apart through their fine check,
and graph-edge endpoints at most two apart through their qubit.
Adding two vertex offsets gives the safe ideal cell diameter 32 and
actual edge-qubit offset from its ideal cell at most 64. This includes
cells cut by the formal diagonal and collapsed cylinder cells.

Here is the continuum step with the precise cap exception. The planar
intersection of B(c,s) with a parent triangle is a disk. It meets at
most two sides, since three meetings would put adjacent vertices
within R. Every point outside the disk can reach a triangle vertex
outside the disk: if O is the disk center, choose a vertex v with
(p-O)·(v-p)>=0, possible by the barycentric average; distance to O
increases along [p,v]. If the point was outside B(c,R), this vertex
is also outside B(c,R). If two sides are met, their shared vertex is
within R. The possibly separated corner component is bounded by that
vertex and the first encounters on those sides, so its triangle cap
is inside B(c,R). All far vertices and points in that triangle join
through the other component while avoiding B(c,s).

If the cone apex X lies within R, its entire link avoids B(c,s): each
link edge is an opposite Q–Z side at distance at least w from X, so
has distance at least w-R>s from c. Far endpoints alone would not
justify this. The connected link joins the far components on both
sheets, and its cylinder joins the sheets. If X is outside R, every
triangle has a far link vertex because its two link endpoints cannot
both be within R. Each triangle's outer component joins that vertex
to X. A cylinder at any far link vertex joins the two apices. Thus
all ideal points outside R meet one common far component. A cut-off
apex cap may remain inside R and is allowed.

Lift each of these paths through the actual fine cells it meets.
An original cell boundary is connected, even if its auxiliary diagonal
is removed; choose a boundary edge path through it. Every ideal cell
is within 32 of the continuous path and its actual edge qubits within
an additional 64. The clearance s=r+128 exceeds r+96, so the chosen
edge qubits are outside B(c,r). This is an abstract path lift, not a
jump between overlapping images. A collapsed cylinder path is lifted
using its original vertical edge. Consequently all actual Y_x vertices
outside B(c,R+64) lie in **one** unaffected graph component. The graph
is not asserted connected outside B(c,r) itself. Existence of a far
vertex follows from a link edge of length at least l.

## 5. RX X-sector anchor via the localized potential

For D1e=0 its local part e_x satisfies d_x1 e_x=0 independently in
every Y_x block. Use H1(Y_x)=0 to write e_x=d_x0 f_x. On graph
edges whose qubits are outside the input ball, f_x has equal endpoint
values. Gauge the constant potential to zero on the common far
component of Section4. Then f_x is supported on original X vertices
in B(c,R+64), and their complete checks lie in B(c,R+65). If e_x=0
take f_x=0.

After subtracting those checks the residual lies only in C_Q1. Its
local closedness says it is constant on each connected Y_q. A nonzero
constant occupies all Y_q vertex qubits; that graph contains a parent
Q–Z segment whose actual endpoint distance is at least l-16. By (8)
it cannot fit in B(c,R+65). Hence the residual is zero. This proves
the RX X anchor for all closed errors, with original generators.

## 6. RX Z-sector anchor: parity, the diagonal exception, both caps

The dual degree-one projection is literally

    b_q = sum of e on the Y_q vertex qubits mod two.   (9)

Primal i'^1 on H1(C_Q) is the all-ones Y_q vector. Its possible
HPL correction first lands in C_Z2 and h_Z lands in C_Z1=0,
so it has no Y_x part. Thus (9) is the actual map, not an assumed
coarse matrix. Closedness gives d0^T b=0.

Apply Section3 separately to each closed interaction component. Shifted
Y_q vertex qubits have Q–Z side carriers. A triangle has one Q label;
an X–Q or Q–Z edge-star has that same Q label. A Q-vertex star also
has one Q label. An X-vertex star contains none of these qubits, since
every Q–Z side is opposite X and its distance exceeds w-R>r+1.
A Z-vertex star permits only labels in N(z).

The auxiliary X–Z edge is a genuine remaining case. The old pair
(x,z) has exactly two shared qubits, so its edge-star is precisely the
two halves xqz and xzq' of one original square. Two geometrically
nearby Q–Z branches do not alone imply a near Z. Instead, if a
**connected intrinsic** fine component contains both Q labels, its
carrier path crosses the identified X–Z seam within B(c,r+1).
The half reached from its Q–Z supported qubit has both its Q–Z side
and X–Z side meeting that ball. Their shared Z is within
10011(r+1)<=R by the angle calculation. Reclassify that component
as the near Z-star. There is no second parent square with the same
X/Z pair and no gluing through an unrelated physical intersection.

Thus b is either supported on one q, or contained in N(z) of a single
near old Z. The first case gives b=0 since q has an X neighbor. In the
second, each old X equation d0^T b=0 is zero or the equality between
the two shared neighbors of that X and Z. These pairs form the
connected old Z link. Hence

    b=t d1^T z, t in F2.                              (10)

Both inherited cap equations were retained in the overlap/link proof;
they cannot be replaced by their sum. The one original fine layer-zero
cap check D1^T z0 has (9)-projection d1^T z and its complete support
lies within one of the old Z position. If t=1 subtract this check.
The residual u is closed, has P1Z u=0, and is supported in B(c,R+1).
The exact dual identity (5) now gives u=D1^T KZ u; by (6) its original
generator supports lie in B(c,R+1+b)⊂B(c,M).

Equivalently, the even Y_q vertex chains fill by complete Y_q edge
checks; the remaining Y_x cycles fill by Y_x face checks, using
H1(Y_x)=0. This direct cellular construction and the typed homotopy
give the same support interface. The homotopy version avoids an
unquantified choice of a remote fine filling. No component was assumed
to be a global boundary. Summing fillings of separate components can
overlap without increasing their common support ball.

R_Z exchanges these two proofs: the doubled Z-star potential fills Z
errors and the X-cap parity/link argument fills X errors. It includes
the same mirrored inset/cylinder types and both inherited X caps.

## 7. Uniform anchor, recursion, and the seed promise

Let M0=10011(r0+128)+8ell+100, enlarged if needed to absorb the fixed
seed diameter D_seed. Every closed fine error of radius at most r0
has the just-proved original-check filling of radius M0. At the seed
only global boundaries do, by using its finite full check set; its
logical errors in (2) are not assigned a filling.

The chain maps preserve the global-boundary promise under projection.
For such errors the two sectors obey

    f_(i+1)(r)<=max(r+b,lambda f_i(r/lambda+a)+b).     (11)

Stop after j=ceil(log_lambda max(r,1)) projections or at the seed
if earlier. The projected radius is

    r_j=r/lambda^j+r*(1-lambda^(-j))<=r0.              (12)

Lift the anchor and all earlier homotopy terms. Since
lambda^j<=lambda max(r,1), the safe uniform constant

    C=2+lambda[M0+b+(a lambda+2b)/(lambda-1)]           (13)

bounds their whole supports by C max(r,1). For r>=1 this is Cr.
At r=0 coincident images can contain distinct qubits, so literal Cr
is not silently extended there. A mixed CSS stabilizer has pure X
and Z components contained in its support, and their original-check
fillings yield its product with the usual fixed stabilizer signs.

## 8. Actual connectivity, size, distance, and bounded padding

The source degree bounds z_v<=7,z_e<=4 can also be checked in the
above cases. At a fine nonapex vertex branching sectors give at most
d+2<=6 neighbors; an old Z corner has three Y_x edge qubits and two
Y_q endpoint qubits. Old apex/cap degrees are inherited, at most seven.
A Y_q edge check has its two endpoints plus at most four old X
neighbors, at most six. Fine face checks have four. A Y_x edge qubit
has two X endpoints and at most four Z faces/restrictions (a cylinder
Z corner contributes two faces and two Y_q restrictions); a shifted
Y_q vertex qubit is either the inherited old Q apex degree or at most
four restrictions plus two incident graph edges. Thus qubit degree
is at most seven and its X and Z neighbor counts at most four.
The overlap-two invariant then bounds each Tanner edge's face degree
by its opposite-color neighbor count, at most four. The seed satisfies
these inequalities, so they propagate in both colors.

Every old Tanner edge lies in a square as proved in Section1.
Connected seed Tanner graph therefore has a connected parent-face
incidence graph. Each literal U_X/U_Z has a connected Tanner
one-skeleton; its shared boundary/corner interfaces are nonempty.
The quotient union stays connected at every depth. Contract check
vertices along Tanner paths to obtain a connected qubit adjacency
graph. Every adjacency has physical length at most two. A spanning
tree path uses at most N-1 adjacencies, so

    physical qubit diameter <=2(N-1).                (14)

Any fixed-padding lattice box around the code consequently contains
O((N+1)^3) sites, with constants allowed to depend on the fixed padding.
No recursive physical-coordinate magnitude bound is needed for that
volume statement. Efficient discovery/rounding of coordinates remains
a separate computational interface.

There are at most E<=7N parent Tanner edges. Counting four edge
incidences per parent square and z_e<=4 gives F_parent<=E<=7N.
The two ell×ell layers and fixed thickness-two strip of one template
contain at most 8(ell+1)^2 vertices. Quotient identifications only reduce
that number. Hence

    N_(i+1)<=56(ell+1)^2 N_i,
    N_i<=5B^i, B=64(ell+1)^2.                        (15)

The factor five from the actual seed is retained.

A distinct consequence uses the stronger fine anchor: at level i>=1
every closed error in a radius-lambda^(i-1) ball is a boundary. Project
i-1 times, stopping at level one; (12) gives radius at most r0, where
all closed errors are fillable. Lift by (5), without projecting to the
logical seed. A nontrivial logical has at least one nontrivial closed
syndrome-interaction component. If it has w qubits, its connected
qubit graph puts it in a radius-2(w-1) ball. Therefore

    d_i>=floor(lambda^(i-1)/2)+2.                    (16)

The X/Z parts of a mixed Pauli only shrink support, so (16) covers
mixed logicals. Combining (15) with (16) supplies a power distance
bound with exponent log(lambda)/log(B)>0. This is a scoped code/geometry
consequence, not a spectral or dynamical stability conclusion.

## 9. Density and thermal parameter compatibility

width is [1000(1-cos(139/2000))-2]lambda>(2/5)lambda,
whose exact lower Taylor expression is 159034698959/384000000000.
Using the source's -1 width and its literal numerical t/lambda after

fixed fine-to-parent density inflation F=2^26 for the literal layers,

delta>=27/(2*2003^2) and bounded longitudinal arrays; it does not
inherit the source's 64pi coefficient. rounding_spectral's separate

finite LLL dependency estimate with alpha2=80, finite colors, and an

That admission is separately auditable; (1) alone is not a random
geometric acquisition algorithm.

Changing these fixed geometric constants and enlarging odd ell does
not change the abstract RX/RZ generators, degree bounds, incoming
boundary maps, or the constant-in-depth character of the source's
unperturbed theorem. Once its density/other hypotheses are satisfied,
it remains the appropriate primary thermal theorem. I have not
independently reproved its Peierls/Davies/preparation analysis. The
continuous cap sampling and bad-event oracle still require precision
