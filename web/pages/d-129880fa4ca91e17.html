costs1, miss costs k1,k2≥1, independent success probabilities p1,p2, the

 \qquad w_1=1-(p_1/q_1)\mathbb E[m_1\mid\text{finish}].
\]

A useful universal companion was reconstructed in the subsequent message.
For L fixed successes costing1 and a common iid chronological miss-cost
sequence X_j≥1 independent of the Bernoulli trials, set
M=max{m:sum_{j≤m}X_j≤N−L}. It is independent of p. A trace with m
misses costs exactly L+sum_{j≤m}X_j, so conditional on M completion is
exactly unit-retry completion within L+M trials. Hence
`J_N=sum_m P(M=m) Jhat_(L+m)`. The log-gradient is the **posterior**
mixture with weights `P(M=m)Jhat_(L+m)/J_N`, not the prior average.

The proof is conditioning on an independent renewal capacity. Stage-specific
costs can make capacity depend on the success path; global restart can
make the number of charged successes differ from L. Those variants need
different arguments. No source theorem, global research advance, or whole
search experiment is inferred from this scoped check.
