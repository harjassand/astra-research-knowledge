# Weyl-compatible lift: d=5 line-cover obstruction and EB repair

## Result

For a Weyl-diagonal channel with eigenvalues \(\lambda_x\), the factor-two Hilbert--Schmidt order
\[
 I-\Psi\preceq 2(I-\Phi)
\]
is equivalent to the mode inequalities \(\mu_x\ge 2\lambda_x-1\) for every nonidentity Weyl mode, where \(\mu_x\) are the eigenvalues of \(\Psi\). For the comparator constructed below, \(\mu_x\ge0\), so it suffices to dominate
\(t_x=(2\lambda_x-1)_+\).

In prime dimension, mixing the \(d+1\) maximal-abelian dephasing channels (one for each projective phase-space line) with the completely depolarizing channel is feasible exactly when
\[
 B(\lambda):=\sum_{L}\max_{x\in L\setminus\{0\}} t_x\le1.
\]
Indeed, a line-dephasing channel has eigenvalue one on its own line and zero on all other nonidentity modes. A mixture with line weights \(w_L\) therefore has \(\mu_x=w_L\) on \(L\), and the mode constraints are precisely \(w_L\ge\max_{x\in L\setminus\{0\}}t_x\), \(\sum_Lw_L\le1\). This is necessary and sufficient for this restricted mixture. It is **not** necessary for a general EB comparator.

The exact qutrit search was a useful calibration: the 81 active-mode selections gave an SCS maximum \(B\simeq1.000000515\), at the isotropic qutrit-cloner spectrum \(\lambda_x\simeq5/8\). This is a floating-point diagnostic only; it does not prove a d=3 upper bound. A finite d=5 SDP search found a clear line-cover violation, and a separate exact Q(\(\sqrt5\)) construction now certifies one. The exact channel has \(B=1.336943720657926\ldots\), yet an explicit EB comparator satisfies the **full** factor-two order with rigorously positive interval margins. Thus this is an obstruction to proving the general result by a maximal-abelian-line mixture, not a counterexample to factor-two EB domination.

## Exact d=5 self-compatible channel

Index phase space by \((u,v)\in\mathbb F_5^2\), with symplectic form \(S((a,b),(u,v))=av-bu\), and let \(\omega=e^{2\pi i/5}\). Set
\[
x_0=\frac{23-2\sqrt5}{25},\quad
x_1=\frac{75-16\sqrt5}{500},\quad
x_2=\frac3{20},\quad x_3=\frac9{100},\quad x_4=\frac14,
\]
\[
N=x_0^2+10x_1^2+10x_2^2+2x_3^2+2x_4^2
 =\frac{1901}{1250}-\frac{152\sqrt5}{625}.
\]
Define a real vector \(f\in\mathbb R^{25}\) by assigning values \(x_0\) at \((0,0)\), \(x_1\) at all modes with second coordinate \(v=\pm1\), \(x_2\) at all modes with \(v=\pm2\), \(x_3\) at \((u,v)=(\pm1,0)\), and \(x_4\) at \((\pm2,0)\). The exact identities in the audit script establish that the normalized symplectic Fourier transform fixes this vector:
\[
\widehat f(r)=\frac15\sum_{s\in\mathbb F_5^2}\omega^{S(s,r)}f_s=f_r.
\]
For an explicit check, put \(c_1=(\sqrt5-1)/4\), \(c_2=-(\sqrt5+1)/4\), and \(y=x_0+2x_3+2x_4\). The five class equations are
\[
\frac{x_0+2x_3c_1+2x_4c_2}{5}=x_1,\quad
\frac{x_0+2x_3c_2+2x_4c_1}{5}=x_2,
\]
\[
\frac y5+2x_1+2x_2=x_0,\quad
\frac y5+2x_1c_1+2x_2c_2=x_3,\quad
\frac y5+2x_1c_2+2x_2c_1=x_4.
\]
Substitution of the displayed \(x_i\) makes each identity exact in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\).
Now let \(q_r=f_r^2/N\) and \(X_{mn}=f_mf_n/N\). Then \(q\) is a probability vector, \(X\succeq0\), \(\operatorname{diag}X=q\), and the Weyl-compatibility equation is
\[
q'_r=\frac1{25}\sum_{m,n}\omega^{S(m-n,r)}X_{mn}
 =\frac{|\sum_m\omega^{S(m,r)}f_m|^2}{25N}
 =\frac{f_r^2}{N}=q_r.
\]
This is an exact rank-one compatibility-kernel certificate. By Haapasalo's finite-Weyl compatibility criterion, \(q\) is the error law of a unital CPTP Weyl channel with a two-output CPTP extension and equal marginals. The vector is invariant under \(r\mapsto-r\), so the channel is Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint. The four nonidentity multiplier classes are exactly
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{class}&\lambda&\text{decimal}\\\hline
v=\pm1&\frac{13376451}{25213768}-\frac{94040\sqrt5}{3151721}&0.4638026469969644\\
v=\pm2&\frac{14203331}{25213768}+\frac{35228\sqrt5}{3151721}&0.5883098718799547\\
(u,v)=(\pm1,0)&\frac{13041363}{25213768}+\frac{31896\sqrt5}{3151721}&0.5398612374668739\\
(u,v)=(\pm2,0)&\frac{17214643}{25213768}+\frac{62264\sqrt5}{3151721}&0.7269225009291898
\end{array}
\]
Every class is positive, so this example is also positive as a Hilbert--Schmidt operator. The horizontal line \(v=0\) peaks at \((\pm2,0)\); each of the other five projective lines peaks at a mode with second coordinate \(\pm2\). Therefore
\[
B=\left(2\lambda_{(2,0)}-1\right)+5\left(2\lambda_{(0,2)}-1\right)
 =\frac{6294997}{6303442}+\frac{476808\sqrt5}{3151721}
 =1.336943720657926\ldots>1.
\]
The exact excess is \((953616\sqrt5-4445)/6303442>0\). Hence no convex mixture of the six projective-line dephasers and the depolarizer can be a factor-two comparator for this channel.

## A general EB comparator for the same channel

The d=5 fixture `d5_eb_comparator.json` contains six complex vectors \(z_j\) and six positive decimal weights. The exact audit interprets all finite decimals as rationals, sets \(\psi_j=z_j/\|z_j\|\), and renormalizes the weights to positive rationals \(w_j\) summing exactly to one. Form the single Choi state
\[
J_\Psi=\sum_{j=1}^6w_j\,\mathcal T_W\!\left(
 |\psi_j\rangle\langle\psi_j|\otimes
 |\overline{\psi_j}\rangle\langle\overline{\psi_j}|
 \right),
\]
where \(\mathcal T_W\) is the Weyl group twirl. Every twirled term is a mixture of local-unitary conjugates of product states, hence separable; their convex sum is the Choi state of one EB comparator channel. Its multipliers are
\[
\mu_{u,v}=\sum_{j=1}^6 w_j\left|
 \frac{\sum_{r=0}^4\overline{z_{j,r+u}}\,\omega^{-vr}z_{j,r}}
 {\sum_{r=0}^4|z_{j,r}|^2}\right|^2.
\]
The compatibility target \(\lambda_{u,v}\) above is exact over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\); the comparator inputs are exact rationals after the stated parsing and renormalization. Outward-rounded 80-digit interval evaluation of the algebraic/trigonometric expressions certifies all 24 nonidentity inequalities. The minimum direct gap \(\mu_x-(2\lambda_x-1)\) is greater than \(0.0236367126398862\), and the minimum clipped gap \(\mu_x-t_x\) is greater than \(0.0139661206985528\). Since the maps are Weyl diagonal, these modewise inequalities prove \(I-\Psi\preceq2(I-\Phi)\) on the entire Hilbert--Schmidt space.

This also answers the separability-witness check for this candidate: no entanglement witness for non-domination is available or needed, because the explicit separable comparator succeeds. PPT alone would not certify separability in dimension five; the product-state decomposition does.

## Quantifiers, twirling, and boundaries

The group twirl is legitimate for the **operator-level, channel-specific** statement. If a comparator \(\Psi\) satisfies the order for a Weyl-covariant \(\Phi\), conjugating the inequality by each Weyl action and averaging preserves the order and EB property; it yields one Weyl-covariant EB comparator for that fixed \(\Phi\). In the displayed construction the comparator is already explicit: six product seeds, each averaged over 25 Weyl conjugations (at most 150 product Choi terms before combining). Twirling does not supply one common measurement/comparator across a varying state family \(E_n\); that stronger familywise quantifier remains outside this calculation.

The primary compatibility source is Haapasalo, *Compatibility of covariant quantum channels with emphasis on Weyl symmetry*, arXiv:1901.06113, finite phase space Corollary 2, Eq. (22): [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.06113). The EB/separable-Choi and measure-and-prepare characterizations are reviewed in Horodecki, Shor, and Ruskai, *General Entanglement-Breaking Channels*, arXiv:quant-ph/0302031: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0302031). The earlier exact line-constant proof is retained at [`quantum_physics/proofs/weyl_line_comparator.md`](../quantum_physics/proofs/weyl_line_comparator.md); it depends on the spectrum being constant within each projective line, a condition this d=5 channel violates.

## Evidence and costs

- `scripts/weyl_sdp.py` implements the Haapasalo PSD-kernel compatibility constraints. The d=3 exhaustive run evaluates 81 line-mode selections (about 0.52 seconds, SCS, tolerance \(2\times10^{-7}\)); it is a diagnostic rather than a proof of the d=3 optimum.
- The exploratory d=5 outer search evaluated 1,000 random choices of one candidate active mode per line, using one 25-by-25 complex PSD SDP per choice (about 101 seconds total, SCS, tolerance \(2\times10^{-7}\)). It supplied a discovery lead, not a d=5 theorem.
- `scripts/reconstruct_sdp_primal.py` rebuilt that lead with 1% depolarizing mixing. Residuals were at most \(1.53\times10^{-15}\), the reconstructed kernel's minimum eigenvalue was about \(4.0\times10^{-4}\), and the line-cover budget remained 1.26433. This floating-point point is superseded for the main claim by the exact rank-one Q(\(\sqrt5\)) certificate above.
- The EB LP searched 5,000 Haar-random product-state seeds and returned a six-state support. The comparator's rational-decimal interpretation and all-mode inequalities were then checked in `quantum_physics/broadcast_lift/scripts/d5_exact_repair_audit.py`; this run took about 3 seconds and produced strictly positive 80-digit interval lower bounds. The interval script and the exact self-compatibility algebra have not been formalized in a proof assistant or independently reviewed.
- No external quantum hardware, experiment, family-specific instrument, or sent communication was used. No broad d>5 sweep was run.

## Status

**Established in this finite example:** an exact d=5, positive-spectrum, unital, TP, HS-self-adjoint, Weyl-covariant, self-compatible channel has line-cover budget \(>1\), and the same channel has an explicitly certified factor-two EB comparator. Thus the maximal-abelian-line-mixture sufficiency criterion cannot be promoted to a necessity statement for all EB comparators. The unrestricted dimension-free operator lemma and the bounded-Holevo two-broadcast-to-one-common-EB-family theorem remain **OPEN / UNKNOWN**; this computation neither proves nor refutes them.
