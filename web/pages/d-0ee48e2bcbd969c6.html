# Why the rational-base fiber shortcut does not extend to every curve

Question: can the family142 norm solver's global-order/fiber argument be used over an arbitrary smooth projective base once the generic algebra is promised split and local maximal orders are available?

No for this rank-independent route. There are split generic matrix algebras with local maximal endomorphism orders whose global section algebra, and every rational-point evaluation image, has no nontrivial idempotent. This is an obstruction to the proposed generalization, not a counterexample to family142, whose base is P1 and whose norm cover degree is an odd prime.

## Complete construction

Let C be a smooth projective geometrically connected genus-one curve over a field k, with a rational point. For a concrete possible base use y²=x³−x over F5 with its point at infinity; the polynomial is separable. We use H0(C,O)=k and H1(C,O)=k.

Choose nonzero ξ∈H1(C,O)=Ext1(O,O), and the corresponding nonsplit vector bundle

    0 → O --i--> V --π--> O → 0.

The connecting map H0(O)→H1(O) is multiplication by ξ, hence an isomorphism. Therefore H0(V)=k and all maps O→V are scalar multiples of i. Every global endomorphism a of V preserves i(O), acting by a scalar α there and inducing a scalar β on the quotient. Compatibility with the extension class gives αξ=βξ, hence α=β.

Then a−αI is zero on the subbundle and on the quotient. It factors through π as a map O→V whose image is in i(O), so it equals c iπ for some c∈k. Conversely I and N=iπ are endomorphisms, N≠0 and N²=0. Thus

    H0(C,EndV)=kI⊕kN ≅ k[ε]/(ε²).

At every rational-point fiber, i is injective and π surjective, so N remains a nonzero rank-one nilpotent. The evaluation image is exactly the dual-number algebra.

For αI+βN to be idempotent requires α²=α and (2α−1)β=0. Over any field α is0 or1, and the latter coefficient is±1, so β=0. Only0 and I are idempotents. In particular no rank-one idempotent exists.

Nevertheless End(V_generic)≅M2(k(C)); at every closed point the local order End(V_local) is a full matrix algebra over a DVR and is maximal. Their intersection is precisely the stated global endomorphism algebra. Hence generic splitting plus maximal local orders does not imply a full-parabolic fiber or acquire the required idempotent. Extending the constants preserves the nonzero extension class.

## Exact mechanism boundary and checks

Family142's decisive implication uses splitting of bundles over P1 into line bundles over the original field. It thereby obtains a full block upper triangular evaluation image; genus-one indecomposability removes that implication. The cyclic cover Y^q=F can have positive genus without any contradiction: the orders and bundle used by the norm solver live on the rational base k(X).

The separate no-import checker tests the dual-number fiber in characteristics2,5,127. In characteristic2 its entire trace pairing vanishes and the common kernel is zero; in the other tested characteristics the proposed rank-one matrix lies outside the algebra. It returns UNKNOWN in every case. Enumeration confirms only0/I idempotents for these finite fixtures. The universal obstruction is the extension proof above, not the enumeration. This is a rank2 obstruction to a rank-independent all-curve assertion; it does not establish a failure for the source's odd-prime cyclic class.

## Prior and research consequence

This is the classical indecomposable unipotent bundle phenomenon, closely associated with Atiyah's *Vector bundles over an elliptic curve*, Proc.London Math.Soc.7(1957),414–452, DOI10.1112/plms/s3-7.1.414. Only publisher bibliographic metadata was accessed; the paper was not read in full. The proof here is self-contained and uses elementary extension/cohomology identities. No discovery of the underlying bundle phenomenon is claimed.

The consequence for future acquisition is precise: a general-curve method must acquire more than the evaluation of global integral endomorphisms. Allowing controlled poles could enlarge the section algebra, but then a fiber rank-one value no longer forces constant characteristic-polynomial coefficients, so the lifting step must be replaced or certified differently. An unproved “same fiber method on every curve” is not an enabling primitive.

Next falsifiable step: specify an allowed-pole divisor, exhibit a bound from the input representation, and test whether a rank-one fiber can be lifted to a genuine generic idempotent with charged pole degree. The present obstruction preserves this alternative but does not prove it.
