off-diagonal supports are HS-orthogonal; X and Y on one support are orthogonal;
diagonal terms are orthogonal to off-diagonal terms. For l<k,
Tr(H_l H_k)=l/(lk)-1/k=0. Their squared norms are

All nonidentity norms lie in[1,2]. X,Y have eigenvalues +/-1 on their two-site
local subspace, and H_l has l eigenvalues1/l, one-1 and other zeros. Hence
every nonidentity T has op norm<=1. In particular (I+sT)/d is EXACTLY a
positive trace-one local density for s=+/-1, even when it has zero eigenvalues.

For a count vector m with sum m_a=N, let S_m be the SUM, not average, over
distinct ordered tensor words of those local labels. Its orbit multiplicity is

different orbits, so
