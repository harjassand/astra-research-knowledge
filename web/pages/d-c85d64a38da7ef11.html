1. EXACT REFERENCE TERM AND PHYSICAL DRIFT

Since L(I)=0, Tr_B[(id_R tensor L_*)rho]=0 as an operator on R. Therefore
 Tr[(id_R tensor L_*)rho (log tau_R tensor I)]=0.
The reference term cancels for EVERY faithful tau_R and every rho,
including correlated states with a different R marginal. The local
stationary term log sigma does not generally cancel and must be kept.

P1 gives C+C^*=2K^*K from the Lyapunov equation. Write
C=K^*K+iQ with Q Hermitian. Then
 L_B,*(rho)={K^*K,rho}/2-KrhoK^*+(i/2)[Q,rho].
The skew term has zero trace against log rho, but can contribute against
the local log sigma. Discarding it in J is not licensed. The block
formulas below retain its full original-picture contribution.

2. ONE-NOISE QUADRATIC GATE ON GENERAL REFERENCE BLOCKS

Identity additions to B cancel H_B and L_B,*: V_(B+tI)=V_B+2tB+t^2I.
Diagonalize sigma=(I+tanh(alpha)Z)/2 with alpha>=0. A local diagonal
unitary commuting with sigma makes the noise off-diagonal entry real;
it also rotates rho and does NOT make its reference blocks real.
Up to scaling, non-diagonal Hermitian noises reduce to B=X+aZ, a real.
Pure Z noise has the complete strong4 inequality proved below.
Set c=cosh(alpha), h=sinh(alpha), A=cosh(alpha/2), k=tanh(alpha/2).
Let rho_ij be arbitrary reference-operator blocks in the local B basis,
q=sqrt(rho) and ell=log rho the GENUINE joint functions. Then

 G0(rho)=[[e^-alpha rho00-e^alpha rho11, c rho01-rho10],
         [c rho10-rho01, e^alpha rho11-e^-alpha rho00]],
 G1(rho)=A^-1[[-rho01-rho10, e^alpha rho11-e^-alpha rho00],
             [e^alpha rho11-e^-alpha rho00, rho01+rho10]],
 G2(rho)=rho-ZrhoZ,
 L_(X+aZ),*(rho)=G0(rho)+aG1(rho)+a^2G2(rho).         (1)
All local operators are tensored with I_R; tensor factors may be permuted
to display these blocks. Each G_i has trace zero.

The coefficients are
 j_i=Tr[G_i(rho)(ell-alpha Z)],
 e0=Tr[(cI-hZ)rho]-Tr[XqXq],
 e1=2kTr[Xrho]-2Tr[XqZq],
 e2=1-Tr[ZqZq]=(1/2)||[Z,q]||_HS^2.                 (2)
These are real for arbitrary complex joint rho; no simultaneous block
diagonalization is assumed. Thus with f_i=j_i-pi e_i,
 J-pi E=f0+a f1+a^2f2.                             (3)

3. STRONG Z AND THE DEGENERATE ENDPOINT

Diagonalizing joint rho and using
 (x-y)(log x-log y)>=4(sqrt(x)-sqrt(y))^2
in its commutator matrix elements gives j2>=4e2. Hence
 f2>=(4-pi)e2>=0.
If f2=0, e2=0, so rho and ell commute with Z, and (1)-(2) give f1=0.
Write rho=diag(A0,A1) with faithful positive reference blocks. Set
 a0=e^-alpha A0, a1=e^alpha A1. Direct substitution gives
 j0=Tr[(a0-a1)(log a0-log a1)],
 e0=||sqrt(a0)-sqrt(a1)||_HS^2.
These matrices need not commute. Expanding in their separate eigenbases
reduces the trace comparison to the same scalar inequality with positive
overlap weights, so j0>=4e0. Thus f0>=0 and the degenerate case closes.

If f2>0, the full nonlinear one-noise theorem is EXACTLY equivalent to
 4f0f2-f1^2>=0                                    (4)
for every faithful joint rho and alpha>=0. Completing the real square
proves this equivalence and gives the potential violating noise
a=-f1/(2f2). No proof of (4) is supplied here. Scalar-qubit angular
relations do not hold for noncommuting reference blocks. The all-real
Hardy kernel premise requires a new explicit factorization into this
joint criterion; all-point positivity by itself is not that factorization.

4. EXACT ARBITRARY-SPECTRUM X-NOISE FORMULA

For X noise, diagonalize rho with eigenvalues lambda_i>0 and eigenvectors
v_i. Put r_i=sqrt(lambda_i), d_ij=log(r_i/r_j), S_+=I_R tensor |0><1|,
a_ij=<v_j|S_+|v_i>, and
 g_lambda(x)=x sinh(x)-lambda(cosh(x)-1), lambda=pi/2.
Expanding K=e^(alpha/2)S_++e^(-alpha/2)S_- in the physical entropy
formula, keeping the local log sigma term, and using S_+^2=0 gives
 j0-pi e0
 =4 sum_ij r_i r_j [g_lambda(d_ij+alpha)|a_ij|^2
                   +g_lambda(d_ij)Re(a_ij a_ji)].     (5)
The nilpotent relation sum_j a_ij a_ji=0 removes the constant terms in
the root-energy expansion. This exact identity was also checked on
the SAME 81 preregistered states; maximum residual 8.44e-15.
