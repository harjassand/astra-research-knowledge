## Exact real parity/X-state reduction

For a real state commuting Z1Z2, reorder the Hilbert space as even parity(00,11), odd parity(01,10). Write its positive square root in two SPD blocks

    R_plus=[[a,b],[b,c]], R_minus=[[d,e],[e,f]],
    D_plus=ac-b²>0, D_minus=df-e²>0.

Filtered Z1,Z2 have blocks diag(K_plus,+/-K_minus), with K_plus and K_minus nonzero traceless2x2 matrices. Their commutant therefore splits into these two parity blocks and is diagonal in each K eigenbasis. Filtered X1,X2 connect the blocks by maps `Y_X` and `Y_I`; the rectangular filter solves

    R_plus² Y+Y R_minus²=2 R_plus B R_minus, B=X or I.

The rectangular filter sends a2x2 unitary B to an invertible matrix: in the two root eigenbases the entries are multiplied by positive factors c_ij and its determinant is a nonzero phase times `c11 c22 |a|²+c12 c21 |b|²`. Therefore the support graph can disconnect only into a common matching. In the real case this would force the two filtered X operators to commute.

Independent symbolic Sylvester inversion produces the two off-diagonal commutator numerators, up to a common strictly positive denominator:

    4(d-f)(d+f)D_plus(D_plus-D_minus)(D_plus+D_minus),
    -4(a-c)(a+c)D_minus(D_plus-D_minus)(D_plus+D_minus).

Positivity implies a possible collision must therefore lie in one of these two branches:

1. D_plus=D_minus; the exceptional equal-determinant family remains unexcluded by this calculation.
2. a=c and d=f; this is a Bell-diagonal family, excluded by the separately derived stabilizer-diagonal theorem.

This is a necessary restriction, not a proof of n2 identifiability. Script: `work/b_compute/cycle2/parity_symbolic.py`. Complete rational expressions: `results/b_compute/cycle2/parity_commuting_conditions.txt`. The six-qubit exact construction does not settle the equal-determinant n2 branch or general n2/n3 states.
