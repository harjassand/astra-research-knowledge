## 1. Explicit dynamics and logical preparation

Let H=H0+V, ||V||=v, and let O be a Hermitian old logical Pauli satisfying
O^2=I and [O,H0]=0. Use the SAME unitary physical bath A=X_q, its full-H
components A_omega, and 0<=gamma(omega)<=Gamma with KMS rates. Let

    D^*(F)=sum_omega gamma(omega)
            (A_omega^* F A_omega-{A_omega^*A_omega,F}/2),
    L^*=i[H,.]+D^*,       rho=exp(-beta H)/Z,
    k=sum_omega gamma(omega)Tr rho[O,A_omega]^*[O,A_omega].        (1)

No Lamb shift is silently discarded from a claimed physical model: (1)
defines the omitted-shift interface. An actual nonzero shift would need
its logical commutator charged separately. The bath is one fixed physical
channel before and after adding fields. Adding an extensive collection of
channels would change the total norm budget and the consequence below.

For the full old decoder Pauli algebra define its twirl T and

    bar_rho=T(rho)=I_L/2 tensor rho_A,
    Enc_old(tau)=tau tensor rho_A,
    Dec_old=Tr_A,      delta=||bar_rho-rho||_1.                   (2)

These are in the acquired OLD factorization. The encoder uses the actual

Its Gibbs sampling, coherent decoder implementation and precision remain

## 2. Davies self-adjointness and the norm interval

The spectral identity for a Hermitian unitary A is

    sum_omega A_omega^* A_omega=pinch_H(A^2)=I.                  (3)

Thus the CP gain has infinity norm at most Gamma and the anticommutator
has infinity norm at most Gamma. Hence ||D^*||_(infty->infty)<=2Gamma.
KMS reindexing gives GNS self-adjointness in

    <F,G>_rho=Tr rho F^*G,

and full-H frequency covariance gives [D^*,ad_H]=0. These are the primary
Davies properties in Alicki et al. 0811.0033v1, Sec II, source lines 317-396.
The finite-temperature state is full rank. A stationary unital CP semigroup
contracts this GNS norm by Schwarz, so its self-adjoint generator has
eigenvalues -lambda with 0<=lambda<=2Gamma. The upper bound is spectral
radius <= any operator norm, not an unjustified transfer of norms.

The carré-du-champ identity and stationarity give

    -<O,D^*O>_rho=k/2,       <O,O>_rho=1.                       (4)

No [O,H]=0 or [O,rho]=0 is required for (4). Expand O in the orthonormal
GNS eigenbasis of D^*, with weights w_l>=0 summing to one. Then

    C_D(t)=<O,exp(tD^*)O>_rho=sum_l w_l exp(-lambda_l t),
    sum_l w_l lambda_l=k/2.

Concavity of 1-exp(-lambda t) on [0,2Gamma] gives the CHORD lower bound

    1-C_D(t)>= k(1-exp(-2Gamma t))/(4Gamma).                     (5)

This is not Jensen applied to a coherent generator. The Hamiltonian is
handled separately, using covariance and unital contraction:

    |C_full(t)-C_D(t)|
      <=||exp(it ad_H)O-O|| <=t||[H,O]||<=2vt.                  (6)

Here C_full(t)=Tr rho O exp(tL^*)O. Covariance permits the coherent
difference to act on O before the dissipative contraction. Without that
property the stated norm estimate would need a separate proof.

## 3. A sharper exact Gibbs-twirl bound

The earlier weighted criterion gives delta<=(beta/4)sum_j c_j. Root's
suggested delta<=beta v is also correct, by an independent entropy argument.
Because log(bar_rho) lies in the fixed algebra of the trace-preserving
twirl, Tr rho log(bar_rho)=Tr bar_rho log(bar_rho). Therefore

    D(rho||bar_rho)+D(bar_rho||rho)
       =beta Tr H(bar_rho-rho)
       =beta Tr V(bar_rho-rho) <=beta v delta.                  (7)

The H0 term vanishes since the twirl fixes H0. Both states are full rank;
relative entropies use natural logarithms. Quantum Pinsker gives EACH
term >=delta^2/2. Thus delta^2<=beta v delta and

    delta<=beta v.                                              (8)

This is stronger than applying three independent operator commutator
bounds. It uses the exact full-space twirl, not a ground-band projection.

## 4. An actual old-channel error, without an all-encoding inference

Encode the two O eigenstates. Their physical outputs are
sigma_+/-=bar_rho(I+/-O). Read O in the OLD final decoder. The average
wrong-label probability is exactly

    e_old(t)=[1-Re Tr O exp(tL)(bar_rho O)]/2.                   (9)

The CP semigroup contracts trace norm also on complex inputs; its adjoint
is a unital CP infinity contraction. Consequently replacing bar_rho O
by rho O changes the correlation by at most delta. Combining (5)-(9),

    e_old(t)>=k(1-exp(-2Gamma t))/(8Gamma)-vt-delta/2.            (10)

This proves root's proposed constants and retains the encoder conversion.
If the matched-gap theorem gives k>=gamma_min/4, take t_*=1/(2Gamma).
Since 1-exp(-1)>1/2, the sufficient calibration

    v<=gamma_min/[128(1+beta Gamma)]

gives

    e_old(t_*)>=gamma_min/(128Gamma)>0.                         (11)

The constant is independent of N at fixed temperature, check range and
rate function, although it may be unusably tiny numerically. At least one
of the two OLD encoded labels has error >=(11). Its final decoded trace
distance from the desired state is at least 2e_old for that input, and
therefore the old encoded-and-decoded channel has a nonvanishing error.
The statement is not that every finite-time recovery or another logical
algebra must fail. A different encoder may intertwine the sector atoms.

If additional channels are included, the same proof uses their TOTAL
Gamma-budget in (3)-(11). Keeping only one lower bound k can then give a
polynomially small error rather than the displayed constant. No monotonic
correlation comparison between noncommuting dissipative generators is
assumed. If rates have no positive lower bound on the selected interval,
the decisive premise also disappears.
