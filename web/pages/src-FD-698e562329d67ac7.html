Scope and conclusion
--------------------
I read p_s01c_witness_compiler/PROOF.txt and the current witness_compiler.py,
including its pinned fixed-degree and adaptive-compiler helpers. I found no

checking the all-history guards, raw-label interface, exact interpolation,

I initially reported an apparent counterexample to the certified-zero
tournament because I reversed its loop condition. That report is retracted.
The helper stops on label agreement (a == b), not disagreement. The actual
helper was run on the two-bit identity order in both basis orders and 16 seeds

selector_square_check.json. The proof below gives the order-independent

1. Exact dimension and defect accounting
----------------------------------------
Write h=n-r. On a correct prefix history of length k, let S_k be the exact
common translation stabilizer of q_0,...,q_(k-1). The remaining flag vectors
v_k,...,v_(h-1) all lie in S_k by triangularity, so dim S_k >= h-k. Each
acquired q_i has a direction v_i in the previous S_i with Delta_(v_i)q_i=1.
The response-one direction is not in the full period kernel, so adding q_i
reduces the exact stabilizer dimension by at least one. Thus

    h-k <= dim S_k <= n-k.

For the next true bit q=q_k, put C={s in S_k: Delta_s q is constant}. C is a
subspace: the derivative cocycle makes the constant response lambda(s)
additive on C. The suffix flag vectors v_k,...,v_(h-1) lie in C, with
lambda(v_k)=1 and lambda(v_j)=0 for j>k. Hence lambda is nonzero. If
a_k=codim_(S_k) C, then a_k <= dim S_k-(h-k) = r-d_k, where
d_k=n-k-dim S_k is the measured defect.

For good directions G={s in C:lambda(s)=1}, |G|=2^(dim C-1), and
|G|/|S_k|=2^(-a_k-1) >= 2^(-r-1). Zero is never good, so skipping zero raw
draws does not invalidate this lower bound. For a correct transition,

    dim S_(k+1)=dim S_k-a_k-1,
    d_(k+1)=d_k+a_k.

Summing gives sum a_k <= r over the acquired prefix. This is the geometric
budget that correct dictionary drops consume.

2. Raw own-toggle selection under an arbitrary suffix order
------------------------------------------------------------
For nonzero v in S_k, define g_v(x)=1+Cmp(x,x+v). If Delta_v q=1, the
lexicographic leading-bit property gives g_v=q on every x. Otherwise
1+Delta_v q is a nonzero Boolean polynomial of degree at most d-1, so
Pr[Delta_v q=0] >= 2^(-(d-1)) by Reed-Muller minimum distance. On every
acquired-prefix fiber, q is balanced because the hidden v_k toggle preserves
that fiber; g_v is balanced because translation by nonzero v swaps g_v and
1-g_v. The two off-diagonal cells in the (g_v,q) table therefore have equal
mass. If e_f is the conditional error rate on fiber f, then the folded
g_v=0 and g_v=1 samples in _pair_test independently land on wrong q sides
with probability e_f each. When both are wrong, the source order must put
the nominal q=0 point after the nominal q=1 point, so the test rejects.
Since E_f[e_f] = Pr[g_v!=q] = (1/2)Pr[Delta_v q=0] >= 2^-d,
Jensen gives rejection probability at least E_f[e_f^2] >= 2^-2d.
Good directions never reject. This proves the raw false-pass bound for the
actual arbitrary within-fiber source order; it does not assume suffix order
is uniform or random.

There is a separate exact-on-completion tournament when dim S_k=h-k. Then
the flag directions span S_k, and the response lambda on S_k is nonzero.
The selector compares basis directions u,w, drawing a uniform x until
g_u(x)=g_w(x). For distinct independent u,w, partition V into cosets of
span{u,w}. On each four-point square, each g flips across its own direction.
The number of disagreements between g_u and g_w on the square is even.
It cannot be four: assuming all four are opposite gives a strict-order cycle
around the square. Hence at least two of four points agree, and each attempt
matches with conditional probability at least 1/2. (For u=w, agreement is
certain; distinct basis elements are independent.)

When the match occurs, the selector's comparison of x+u and x+w has a
one-bit consequence. If both directions have response one, either winner is
good. If both have response zero, either is bad. If u has response one and
w zero, agreement means g_u=g_w=q(x); the endpoint comparison equals that
common bit, so u replaces w. If u has response zero and w one, the endpoint
comparison is the complement of the common bit, so the good incumbent w
stays. Thus a good winner is absorbing, and the first good basis direction
replaces a bad winner whenever that match completes. Some basis direction
is good because lambda is nonzero. Therefore a completed tournament returns
a true own-toggle; cap exhaustion returns UNKNOWN.

Across all stages there are at most P=sum_(k<m)(n-k-1) matches. A match's
attempt count is conditionally dominated by Geom(1/2); H=ceil(4P+8 log(4/beta))
gives the stated beta/4 cap failure by the geometric-sum Chernoff bound.
Each attempt uses two labels, and each completed match one extra comparison,
so expected tournament comparisons are at most 5P. These claims hold even
after a wrong prefix for the cap and cost: the square/order argument uses
only a strict total order. Correct-prefix semantics are used only to prove
that the completed tournament's winner toggles q_k.

3. Witness-space geometry and interpolation
--------------------------------------------
For s in S_k define F_s(x)=q(x+s)+q(x)+q(s)+q(0). It vanishes identically
exactly when s is in C. If s is not in C, F_s is nonzero, has degree at most
d-1, and is invariant under C+span{s}. Since dim C>=h-k, it descends to at
most k+r-1 variables. Its uniform weight is therefore at least
2^(-min(d-1,k+r-1)). A uniform s in W containing C escapes C with
probability at least 1/2 when W>C. Thus a witness probe detects F_s(x)=1
with probability at least 2^-d; the code uses this conservative uniform
bound in outer_L. When k+r<=1, a nonzero such F_s would descend to zero
variables and vanish at zero, impossible, so C=S and skipping the witness
loop is sound.

For a detected (s*,x*), P(s)=F_s(x*) on W has P(0)=0 and P(s*)=1. Its true
period space D contains C, excludes s*, and has codimension at most
cap=dim W-(h-k). For t not in D, Delta_t P is nonzero and invariant under
D+span{t}; it is a nonzero function on at most cap-1 quotient bits, so its
weight is at least 2^(-(cap-1)). This needs no degree bound on P. The
dictionary tests the quotient columns using the actual second derivative
label identity

    Delta_t P(z)=q(z)+q(z+x*)+q(z+t)+q(z+t+x*).

Each label is obtained by one chosen-pair comparison through the selected
genuine own-toggle v. True periods never reject. Conditional on no false
period pass, the basis scan identifies the quotient columns and returns
exactly ker(P); adaptive candidate choices are covered by a union bound over
at most dim(W)*2^cap probes, each with fresh samples. The cap-one shortcut
is exact: a detected P with P(0)=0 cannot be constant, and period codimension
at most one makes P the nonzero linear form on W/D. Two cached-label calls
per W basis vector recover that row and its kernel.

On semantic success, final W=C. Choose a complement U. For y in U,z in W,
q(y+z)=q(y)+lambda(z). Degree-d interpolation of q|U from M_d(dim U)
anchors and one response per basis vector of W therefore reconstructs q
exactly. The raw degree-d ANF expansion is source-free local work; it is not
being counted as compact computation. Its dimension is bounded by
dim U<=k+r. The full-raw polynomial expansion and its bit-work are disclosed
in the source proof; the comparison bound does not pretend to bound Python
wall time or total physical bit cost.

4. Bad-history scope and hard caps
----------------------------------
The current code carries global_drop, paid_dictionaries, and outer_epochs
outside the stage loop. After a witness is found it checks
global_drop>=r OR paid_dictionaries>=r before calling period_dictionary.
Each returned dictionary must pass subspace, minimum-dimension,
own-toggle, detected-direction exclusion, strict-drop, and
global_drop+drop<=r guards before the candidate W is updated. A dictionary
that aborts is already counted as paid and terminates. Hence no history can
pay more than r dictionaries, including paid-then-abort histories. Each
dictionary makes at most n*2^r*Lp probes, and the cap-one shortcut's 2n
labels are below the same conservative comparison allowance. The outer
epoch counter is incremented before testing and capped at m+r. On a correct
history, at most r strict drops plus one closing epoch per stage are needed;
after a bad history, the explicit counter still enforces the hard limit.

The B reconstruction-query bound includes A=sum_k M_d(k+r) quotient
anchors, at most m(n+1) cached origins/affine responses, at most
3(m+r)L outer labels, and at most 4r*n*2^r*Lp period labels. The current
code returns UNKNOWN before a further dictionary if either persistent cap
has been used. This is stronger than a post-drop-only check and closes the
refund loophole. All claimed probability bounds are first-error bounds under
fresh ideal conditional fair randomness. Seeded Python randomness only
replays finite fixtures and is not an entropy-source claim.

5. Forced semantic-miss/refund fixture
---------------------------------------
forced_refund_cap.py runs the actual p_s01c reconstruct and period_dictionary
through the counted ac.Source comparator, with a forced positive-probability
transcript. The teacher is the 7-bit bijection

    (a+yH, z1+yH, z2+yH, z3+yH, y, s1, s2), H=s1+s1*s2,

with four cubic first coordinates and flag directions e_a,e_z1,e_z2,e_z3.
The controlled first three dictionaries accept e_s2 after probing at zero,
although it is not a period of the actual restricted P: P(0)=P(e_s2)=0,
but P(e_s1)=1 and P(e_s1+e_s2)=0. Each detected direction is e_s1. Their
geometric guards all pass. The accepted candidate bodies are a,z1,z2, each of which
omits the common cubic yH contribution. After each stage, the candidate's
measured defect is zero while the exact true stabilizer defect is three; the
three units are refunded by semantic misses. Nonetheless, after three paid
dictionaries and cumulative drop three, the next detected witness returns
UNKNOWN at global_witness_budget_before_dictionary, before paying a fourth.
The saved receipt reports 3,132 actual comparisons, below the conditional
reconstruction cap 29,733 and whole cap 521,833; seven outer epochs are at
most m+r=7. It also checks the flag promise on every input, the teacher's
bijection, false-period containment, detected-direction exclusion, exact
stabilizer dimensions, and actual comparator-only label access.

The fixture is one forced positive-probability path, not a fair-random
frequency estimate. It demonstrates why geometric guards alone do not
recognize the true C and why the separate global accounting is necessary.

6. Failure-budget audit and remaining limits
---------------------------------------------
The proof allocates beta/4 to raw cap exhaustion, beta/4 to the global
tournament cap, beta/(4R) to first false raw selection, beta/(8R) to first
false outer closure, and beta/(8R) to first false period pass. The latter
three sum to beta/(2R); total UNKNOWN-or-wrong probability is at most
beta/2+beta/(2R)<=beta. Raw false passes union-bound mR candidates using
delta_raw=beta/(4mR^2). Outer closure union-bounds at most m+r epochs. Period
misses union-bound at most r dictionaries using eta=beta/(8rR), with no
failure term for cap one. These counts are all-history safe because failed
dictionary calls are charged before execution and the explicit invocation
counter persists across stages.

I found no mismatch in these budget compositions. The proof remains
conditional on the supplied low-degree/flag promises and exact source
comparator; it does not prove promise recognition, novelty, a physical
randomness interface, or a practical wall-time advantage. The existing
matched-control evidence is finite and the source proof itself correctly
retains the strongest generalized-junta/value-oracle caveat.
