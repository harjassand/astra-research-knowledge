C01_S01 — TWO INDEPENDENT EXTENSIONS OF THE FROZEN INITIAL
7 October2026. No peer exposure. Standard algebra; novelty unverified.

A. SINGLETON BACKGROUND WITH COLUMN COLLISIONS
Let t be the number of rows of F with more than one nonzero. All other rows
have zero or one nonzero; no distinct-column promise is needed. For a
selected exceptional set A and regular selected rows R, a nonzero minor
requires the forced columns p(R) to be distinct. Laplace expansion gives
 product_(i in R)F[i,p(i)] times det F[A,B], B=J minus p(R), up to one sign.
Squared norm removes the sign. There is exactly one forced background
matching; no amplitude sum is replaced by a sum of squares.

The regular arrows i->p(i) form an outdegree<=1 digraph. Each component has
one directed cycle or a root, and trees feed into it. At site i let r_i be
selected row, m_i the number of selected incoming regular rows, b_i an
exceptional free column. Legality is r_i+m_i+b_i<=1, with c_i=m_i+b_i.
Incoming count is only0 or1; a collision contributes zero automatically.
Tree messages condition on r_i and multiply child messages while keeping
at most one selected incoming child. Cycle messages are2 by2 transfer
matrices, traced over predecessor row bits. Roots are summed directly.

Carry the double-exterior coefficient (P,Q) from INITIAL. Its product is
 (e_P tensor e_Q)(e_R tensor e_S)
 =sign(P,R)sign(Q,S)e_(P union R) tensor e_(Q union S)
if P,R and Q,S are disjoint, otherwise0. Both exterior degrees agree, so
these products commute: reversing two factors gives the same two signs.
Consequently subtrees/components can be merged in arbitrary fixed order.
Selecting b_i=1 multiplies by
 C_i=(sum_(a in A)F[a,i]e_a) tensor
                  (sum_(a in A)conjugate(F[a,i])e_a).
The top (A,A) coefficient is exactly |det F[A,B]|^2.

Dictionary multiplication has at most16^|A| register-pair cases and O(n^2)
particle-degree convolution, at O(n) graph merges. A conservative bound is
O(n^3 17^t) ring operations after summing all A. Bit heights are polynomial
in input,n and17^t, exactly as in INITIAL. Thus t=O(log n) is polynomial.
The parameter t and the graph are acquired by inspecting F. Zero rows,
self-loops, repeated columns and arbitrary phases are admitted. Prefixes
and full-basis sampling use the same local restrictions and chain rule.

B. MONOMIAL PLUS RANK-s CORRECTION
Suppose explicit F0[i,p(i)]=d_i is monomial for a permutation p, and
F=F0+UV^T, with s columns in U,V. The certificate F0 is supplied or chosen
explicitly; the minimum-rank monomial approximation is NOT acquired for free.
Given F0, exact rational elimination computes and verifies U,V and s.
The acquisition is implemented in rank_certificate_sampler.py.

Let alpha_a^dagger=sum_i U_ia a_i^dagger,
 beta_a^dagger=sum_i V_ia b_i^dagger.
All creator pairs commute and square to zero, hence exactly
 |G_F>=product_(a=1)^s(1+alpha_a^dagger beta_a^dagger)|G_F0>.
A Jordan--Wigner linear creator has a bond2 MPO with local operator matrix
 [[Z_i,U_ia c_a^dagger],[0,I]],
where Z_i=(-1)^(n_ai+n_bi), and c_b^dagger includes the local(-1)^n_ai.
Multiplying the alpha and beta MPOs gives bond4; adding an identity channel
represents1+alpha beta with bond5. Composing s factors gives bond5^s,
with at most10^s local virtual transitions and at most4^s destinations per
fixed virtual source. This construction retains all signs exactly.

For each p-cycle, the monomial Gaussian has a bond2 supertrace MPS. Let
x_i indicate its chosen pair i->p(i), with physical occupations
(a_i,b_i)=(x_i,x_previous). The local tensor factor is
 d_i^x_i (-1)^(x_previous x_i),
and the circular starting bit contributes an additional(-1)^x_start.
Sorting creator words into physical site order gives exactly this sign;
for a1-cycle the two signs cancel. Cycles are processed successively,
carrying the global rank-MPO virtual registers between them.

Contract ket and bra tensors with equal physical outcomes, omit11, and
attach lambda_i z to10. The final coefficient of z^k is c_k. Prefixes delete
incompatible outputs. Keeping both MPO virtual registers uses25^s states;
a conservative per-site scan has400^s virtual source/destination cases.
Thus O(poly(n,s)400^s) arithmetic and polynomial bit heights suffice, which
is polynomial for s=O(log n). Finite-time approximate conditional draws and
full-basis dummy lift again acquire the L=1 additive energy kernel.

Generic rank s=Theta(n) retains explicitly exponential complexity.
