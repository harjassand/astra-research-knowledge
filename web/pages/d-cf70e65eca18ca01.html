Important formulas
------------------
For S>=1, the inactive correction drift coefficient is

  F(c)=[-alpha-sum_k zeta_k c_k-Gamma/(S+2)+mu_bar(c)]/(S+1)=-theta(c),

where Gamma=sum gamma_k and mu_bar=sum mu_k c_k/S. At S=0,
F=-alpha-Gamma/2. For rational rates, theta_*>0 reduces to a finite exact
check using the tail lower bound

  theta(c)>=[alpha-mu_max+zeta_min S]/(S+1)

and cutoff S>=ceil(1+2*max(mu_max-alpha,0)/zeta_min), after which the lower
bound is at least zeta_min/2. The simpler alpha>mu_max condition implies
theta_*>0 but is not necessary for this sufficient condition. If
S0=ceil(1+2*max(mu_max-alpha,0)/zeta_min), the exact finite check evaluates
binomial(S0+r-1,r) count vectors; no polynomial-time guarantee is claimed.
