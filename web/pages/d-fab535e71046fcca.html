Main retained advance
---------------------
Every finite closed critical EPR* gate network has an exact positive auxiliary expectation on a polynomial-size skew matrix. It retains all cycle parity and applies to arbitrary physical graphs, repeated gates and interleavings. Positivity is acquired globally, but polynomial relative variance/mixing is not. The determinant-estimator mechanism is closely related to established Godsil-Gutman/Barvinok ideas; the EPR* specialization and tuned gauge require separate priority review.

THEOREM D: exact global positive expectation
------------------------------------------
Let m four-port gates have nonnegative weights a_v,c_v,d_v, a_v>0, b_v=a_v+c_v+d_v, and uncomplemented matrix
 F_v=[[a,0,0,d],[0,b,c,0],[0,c,b,0],[d,0,0,a]].
Ports are (out_i,out_j,in_i,in_j). The physical closed circuit pairs output and subsequent input ports of every worldline with EQUALITY sewing. Idle qubits give a separate factor2 each.

Define a real skew matrix A_v by its upper entries
 A12=A34=sqrt(d_v/a_v),
 A13=A24=sqrt(b_v/a_v),
 A14=A23=sqrt(c_v/a_v).
Then Pf(A_v)=(d_v-b_v+c_v)/a_v=-1. For every subset S of the four ports,
 F_v(S)=a_v Pf((A_v)_S)^2,
with Pf(empty)=1 and odd principal Pfaffians0. Explicitly: empty/full coefficient a; 12/34 coefficient d; 13/24 coefficient b; 14/23 coefficient c. This is the local squared-Pfaffian identity.

Let E be the external wire matching on all4m ports (2m wire edges). Choose any fixed orientation of each external edge. For z in C^E let K(z) be blockdiag(A_1,...,A_m), plus z_e and -z_e in the two oriented positions of wire e. Overlapping internal and external entries are added.

For independent Rademacher xi_e and arbitrary fixed phases eta_e with |eta_e|=1,
 Z_circuit = (prod_v a_v) E_xi[ |Pf K(eta*xi)|^2 ]
           = (prod_v a_v) E_xi[ |det K(eta*xi)| ].              (D)
When every eta_e=1, K is real skew, detK=PfK^2>=0, so absolute values on det are unnecessary.

Proof. P(z)=PfK(z) is multiaffine in the external matching variables. For T subset E, its coefficient at z^T is epsilon_T times the product of local principal Pfaffians on the ports not covered by T; epsilon_T is a fixed sign from the Pfaffian expansion. Fixing T leaves exactly the disjoint local blocks, so the coefficient identity is exact even on nonplanar graphs. Equivalently, choose external edge e iff both its incident spin ports are0; remaining ports are1. Squaring a coefficient removes epsilon_T and gives the physical configuration weight divided by prod a_v. Rademacher characters are orthogonal: E xi^T xi^U=0 for T!=U and1 for T=U. Unit phases do not affect diagonal magnitudes. Thus Parseval gives (D). Finally detK=PfK^2 for every complex skew matrix, so |detK|=|PfK|^2.

No fermionic crossing signs were silently erased: they occur as epsilon_T and disappear only AFTER coefficient orthogonality. Equality sewing is essential to this statement. The initial COMPLEMENTED h polynomial has constant d, but its physical wires are disequalities. Using its A_v with equality sewing would be incorrect. This repair was sent to root before any composition inference. Our final matrix and scripts use the uncomplemented tensor and normalization prod a_v throughout.

Acquired size and per-draw cost
------------------------------
The matrix has dimension4m, 2m independent sign bits, six local square roots per gate, and a directly constructed matching. One determinant evaluation uses O(m^3) arithmetic operations; this is polynomial PER DRAW, not a total counting guarantee. All-zero-spin contribution gives Z/(prod a_v)>=1 (indeed the all-identity connection word gives2^{n_active}), so the expectation has an acquired positive floor. For rational input of B-bit ratios, the matrix norm is bounded by R with logR=O(B+log m). Rounding local roots to entry error delta changes the determinant by at most O(N^2 delta (R+1)^{N-1}), N=4m, by column multilinearity and Hadamard. Thus absolute accuracy epsilon per draw needs only O(m(B+log m)+log(1/epsilon)) precision bits. Exact rational/complex determinant computation on rounded entries followed by magnitude evaluation gives polynomial finite-bit per-draw work. The exact theorem is over algebraic entries; no inefficient exact global algebraic-field construction is required for an absolute-accuracy implementation. Total sample cost remains unbounded by a polynomial because of variance.

THEOREM V: raw determinant estimator has exponential variance
------------------------------------------------------------
For one gate at the rational-root weights above, external wires connect ports13 and24. With real signs xi1,xi2,
 PfK=-1-(3/2)(xi1+xi2)-xi1xi2.
Its squared values are25,0,0,1, each sign pair probability1/4. Thus E Y=13/2, E Y^2=313/2 and E Y^2/(E Y)^2=626/169. On k disjoint gates the matrix and phases factor and the relative second moment is exactly (626/169)^k. Averaging this raw positive estimator therefore lacks a uniform polynomial relative-variance bound. Component factorization can handle this easy family; it does not prove failure of every estimator or algorithm.

THEOREM G: tuned phase removes zero-time variance, but has a remaining exact obstruction
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At identity gates a=b1,c=d0, K consists of disjoint alternating internal/wire cycles, one per physical worldline. Each component has two perfect matchings, giving a Pfaffian factor 1+/-product_e z_e up to a harmless overall sign. Choose the unit wire phases so product_e eta_e=i on each physical worldline (one chosen edge phasei and others1 suffices). Each factor has squared magnitude |1+/-i product xi|^2=2. Therefore Y=|PfK|^2=2^{n_active} pointwise: zero variance. The phases are directly acquired, use only1,i, and need no bipartite graph or parity rejection.

For one nonidentity critical gate, choose both external phasesi. Then
 PfK=-1-i sqrt(b/a)(xi1+xi2)+xi1xi2.
Y takes4b/a for equal signs and4 for unequal signs, each with probability1/2. Its relative variance is exactly ((b-a)/(b+a))^2. At a4,b9 this is25/169. For k independent gates the relative second moment is (194/169)^k, still exponential. For a1,b1+t it is t^2/(2+t)^2, so the one-gate high-temperature behavior is quadratic in t; this alone does not control repeated interacting cooling circuits at fixed total beta.

Local LC interpretation and its limit
------------------------------------
The local matrix [I|A_v] gives a rank4 basis-volume polynomial with coefficients |det|. Its log-concavity follows from the volume polynomial of a zonotope and Brunn-Minkowski. Restricting to one column from each dual-rail pair produces det(A_S)=Pf(A_S)^2 and hence the local gate. This supplies a positive local matroid-volume lift, but sewing two ports to equal spin values requires paired column choices. Their allowed family {00,11} is not itself a matroid basis family or LC factor. Treating it as an ordinary partition constraint introduces invalid dual-rail choices. No construction reducing that sewing to only two global LC factors was acquired. The initial single-LC selector obstruction remains compatible with this two-factor local lift.
