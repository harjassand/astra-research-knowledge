## New derivations and exact checks

- New analytic derivation in `REPORT.md`: joint normal regression for a regular multi-quadric slice, followed by the conditional all-affine fourth-moment estimate under an explicit local multi-Finsler decomposition and simple transverse spectral gaps. It is a proof under stated assumptions, not a priority claim and not a universal quasiconvexity theorem.
- New exact counterexamples in `REPORT.md`: (i) a singular constraint Jacobian for which \(|z|/v\to\infty\), and (ii) a five-cycle quadratic nonnegative on a multi-quadric null cone with no PSD-plus-constraint-multipliers representation.
- Source tangent-law counterexample: the construction is from the incoming adaptive source; this report separates it as source-derived and independently recomputes its finite algebraic claims.
- `verify_examples.py` ran with `work/FE-hidden-gate/venv/bin/python` and passed the three listed groups. `check_results.json` records only exact symbolic checks of those finite examples. It does not certify the analytic inequalities, external correctness, novelty, or full quasiconvexity.
