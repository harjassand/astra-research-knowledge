1. THE SINGLE LOG-COST STEP IN THE PINNED TREE PROOF
LA001 assigns a different receiver to each frame direction and retains
only its marginal score. At depth m, the receiver sees Phi^m. The
conversion I-Phi^m<=m(I-Phi) is where log frame-rank becomes a Dirichlet
factor. The full joint record contains additional regression information,
but that one-leaf estimate discards it. Compact-convex separation is not
the source of logarithmic loss.

The exact weighted-tree note already treats every prefix assignment and
energy-weighted frame decomposition. Its entropy lower bound follows
from Kraft: for leaf-private lengths l_i and energy weights p_i,
sum_i p_i l_i>=H_2(p). Any pure decomposition of the normalized energy
matrix G^(1/2)QG^(1/2)/Tr(QG) has weight entropy at least its von Neumann
entropy; the flat rank-r case therefore charges at least log_2 r. Merely
changing code weights, stopping depths, or decompositions cannot make
that particular certificate dimension-free. This is an architecture
obstruction, not a legal-channel counterexample.

The new estimator avoids the hypothesis of that obstruction: each leaf
uses the SAME randomized vector measurement, and all leaf observations
are aggregated for EVERY selected spectral mode. The matrix Schur bound
handles all directions at once. There is no private output per direction.

2. CENTERING, MARTINGALES, AND WHAT IS NOT ASSUMED
Every estimator has zero reference mean because its exact pullback is a
traceless HS eigenmode. The level recursion concerns centered second
moments. The quantum measurements at different depths need not have a
common classical filtration. No Doob optional-stopping theorem or
assertion that the different-depth estimators are a joint classical
martingale is used. Calling the quantum construction a martingale without
an additional dilation/filtration proof would overstate it.

All sibling correlations are retained in C. The proof never substitutes
E[X_left X_right]=E[X_left]E[X_right]. Product measurement FIRST MOMENTS
pull back as E_a tensor E_b even when the joint quantum state is entangled.
The bound C<=I is the nonnegative square of A_B-A_C with tracial marginals.
This is the entire correlation input to the variance upper bound.

The first-freeze proof's off-diagonal/full-domain step is essential. A list
of individual-mode scores would not suffice. The unbiased first moment
gives E[a_full X_slow^T]=J on the ENTIRE traceless HS space. A PSD block
covariance then gives S_full>=J N^-1 J^T, so no uncontrolled fast/slow
cross term remains. This is an operator-order claim, not an average-frame
score or pointwise reconstruction only.

4. PRIMARY LITERATURE: AVERAGING IS ESTABLISHED CLASSICAL MACHINERY
Yuval Peres and Sebastien Roch, Reconstruction on Trees: Exponential
Moment Bounds for Linear Estimators, arXiv:0908.2056v1 (14 Aug 2009),
https://arxiv.org/html/0908.2056v1, Eq.1 and introduction lines54-66,
explicitly use the unbiased estimator (b lambda)^(-n) times the sum of
leaf eigenvector values, with bounded variance when b lambda^2>1. They
attribute this estimator to Mossel-Peres (2003). This matches the binary
squared-eigenvalue threshold and linear averaging underlying this route.
No novelty is claimed for that classical mechanism.

Svante Janson and Elchanan Mossel, Robust reconstruction on trees is
determined by the second eigenvalue, Ann. Probab.32 (2004),2630-2649,
https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~mossel/publications/robust.pdf,
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406447, abstract/introduction, prove the robust
classical reconstruction threshold based on branching times lambda^2.
Their model uses independent Markov transmissions; that is not imported
as an independence assumption for quantum sibling outputs here.

Yui Kuramochi, Entanglement-breaking channels with general outcome
operator algebras, arXiv:1806.05854v2,
https://arxiv.org/html/1806.05854v2, Theorem2, gives equivalence between EB,
measure-and-prepare form, and compatibility of arbitrarily many copies.
This is an established infinite-compatibility/EB characterization, not
the quantitative finite-two-output Dirichlet inequality proved by the
candidate. Our compactness argument is finite-dimensional and elementary.

Shiv Akshar Yadavalli and Iman Marvian, Noisy Quantum Trees: Infinite
Protection Without Correction, https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14294,
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-025-00961-x, is relevant overlapping quantum
tree literature. The inspected abstract concerns stabilizer encoders,
fresh ancillas, Pauli edge noise, and classical/entanglement propagation.
Its full quantitative overlap with the present comparator has NOT been
exhaustively checked. It is a priority-review lead, not a theorem premise.
