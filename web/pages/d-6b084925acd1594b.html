# Algebraic algorithms: a smaller characteristic-uniform matrix detector

Date: 2026-10-07. Status: complete internal derivation and scoped exact executions; external proof review and historical priority remain open. This report does not upgrade the release as a whole. The new argument uses the October-4 release's multiplicative-shift word encoding, reconstructs its finite-word faithfulness, and replaces its first-column multiplicity bound by a full-matrix kernel argument.

The strongest result is an explicit integer tuple, constructed without reading the target, that detects every nonzero polynomial with an acyclic m-state representation over **every field** at dimension O(n³m³). A nilpotent length marker extends it to arbitrary cyclic recognizable series at dimension O(n³m⁴). This gives an exact realization learner with a supplied full-matrix evaluation oracle in every characteristic. The cost improvement is real within that interface; practical acquisition of the oracle remains missing.

## 1. Exact interface and construction

Fix n,m ≥ 1. A scalar recognizable formal series over a field F has coefficients

\[
 f_w=uB_wv,\qquad B_w=B_{w_1}\cdots B_{w_k},\qquad B_\epsilon=I_m.
\]

The matrices B_i, row u, and column v are hidden; only an upper state bound m is supplied. No positivity or probabilistic meaning is assumed. On a jointly nilpotent tuple X, evaluation means the finite sum \(f(X)=\sum_w f_wX_w\), equivalently evaluation of its linear star representation. This oracle is stronger than scalar membership queries, passive data, or a generic rational-expression oracle with an unknown inverse domain.

Set

\[
 N=2nm,\qquad E=1+\binom N2+n(N-1).
\]

For 0 ≤ q < r < N define a truncated polynomial

\[
 a_{i,r,q}(t)=
 -(1+t)^{i(r-q-1)}\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor(E-1)/r\rfloor}t^{rj}
 \pmod {t^E};
\]

put all entries with r ≤ q equal to zero. Replace each entry by its multiplication matrix on \(\mathbb Z[t]/(t^E)\), in basis 1,t,…,t^(E−1). This gives integer matrices V_i of dimension NE. Let S_m be the nilpotent one-step shift on m coordinates and put

\[
 T_i=S_m\otimes V_i,\qquad d=mNE=O(n^3m^4).
\]

The tuple depends only on n,m. Every word of length m in the T_i is zero. Every nonzero entry of V_i is a negative sum of distinct binomial coefficients from a row of \((1+t)^k\), where k ≤ n(N−1). Its absolute value is at most 2^k, so entry bit length is O(1+nN)=O(n²m). These are ordinary integer matrices; reduction in the characteristic of F is automatic. There is no field extension, characteristic lower bound, factorization oracle, or analytic hypothesis.

**Derived theorem.** For every field F and every recognizable series with a representation of dimension at most m,

\[
 f\not\equiv0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ f(T_1,\ldots,T_n)\ne0.
\]

**Derived acyclic theorem.** If the B_i are jointly strictly upper triangular, then V_i alone detect every nonzero f, at dimension NE=O(n³m³). A division-free formula of tree size s has such a representation with m≤2s, as established in the chosen release's `systems.tex`; consequently the new dimension is O(n³s³), versus the source's O(n³s⁶). Full output, rather than only the first output column, is essential to the proof.

## 2. Reconstructed source lemma: finite words remain faithful

Introduce fresh transcendental t over F, K=F(t), and let σ act on K((z)) by z↦tz while fixing K. Put

\[
 b_i(z)=\frac{z}{1-(1+t)^iz},\qquad
 (\sigma-1)J_i(a)=b_i a,\qquad J_i(a)(0)=0.
\]

Coefficient r≥1 is divided by t^r−1, which is nonzero in every characteristic. Therefore J_i raises z-order. Define h_ε=1 and h_iw=J_i(h_w).

The finite family h_w is linearly independent over K(z). Here is the load-bearing argument. Each b_i has its sole pole at α_i=(1+t)^−i. Its shift orbit {t^jα_i:j∈Z} is infinite, and the orbits are disjoint: an intersection would give (1+t)^(i−i′)=t^a, whose valuations at t=0 and t=−1 force a=0 and i=i′. A rational difference σR−R with poles in one such orbit has two uncancelled endpoint poles, so it cannot equal a nonzero K-linear combination of the b_i, which has at most one pole in each orbit.

For a finite relation among h_w, minimize first the maximum word length and then the number of coefficients at that length; normalize one top coefficient to 1. Applying σ and subtracting removes that coefficient. Minimality forces every new coefficient to vanish. The remaining top coefficients are σ-fixed, hence belong to K. At the next word length, the vanished new coefficient expresses a combination of b_i as a rational difference. The pole argument kills every top coefficient, a contradiction. This reconstructs the exact source lemma without using analytic continuation or characteristic-zero differentiation.

Source: [October-4 positive-characteristic manuscript, pinned TeX](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Uniform-Matrix-Hitting-Points-in-Every-Positive-Characteristic-October-4-2026/build/sections/words.tex). All seven selected source files were fetched at this commit; hashes and byte agreement with the live main copies are in `source_manifest.json`.

## 3. New full-matrix kernel deduction

For cyclic systems introduce another fresh scalar indeterminate λ and work over F(t,λ). Define

\[
 H(z)=\sum_w\lambda^{|w|}B_w h_w(z),\quad H(0)=I,
 \quad A(z)=I+\lambda\sum_iB_i b_i(z).
\]

Every z coefficient is a finite sum, so this is a valid invertible formal matrix, even when the B_i have cycles. The word recursion gives

\[
 \sigma H=A H.
\]

Write A=P/Q, where \(Q=\prod_i(1-(1+t)^iz)\). Then Q(0)=1, deg_z P,deg_z Q≤n, and deg_λ P≤1. If f≠0, one nonzero finite homogeneous part f_h has nonzero h_fh by the source lemma; its coefficient λ^h makes uH(z)v nonzero. Define

\[
 \mathcal K(z,w)=uH(z)H(w)^{-1}v.
\]

Thus \(\mathcal K\ne0\), since its restriction at w=0 is uH(z)v. A nonzero constant coefficient uv is already detected on the output diagonal, so below assume uv=0.

### 3.1 A nonzero diagonal shift occurs before m

Define rational rows

\[
 R_0=u,\qquad R_{j+1}=\sigma(R_j)A.
\]

They satisfy \(R_jH=\sigma^j(uH)\) and therefore

\[
 R_j(z)v=\mathcal K(t^jz,z).
\]

Let k≤m be the first dependence among R_0,…,R_k over F(t,λ,z). The preceding span is invariant under the semilinear map θ(R)=σ(R)A: applying θ to a dependence only shifts its scalar coefficients, and sends R_j to R_(j+1). If R_jv=0 for all j<k, it follows that R_jv=0 for every j≥0.

But then \(\mathcal K(t^jz,z)=0\) for every j. For every total degree ℓ, write the homogeneous part as \(\sum_{a+b=\ell}c_{a,b}z^aw^b\). Substitution gives the polynomial \(\sum c_{a,b}X^a\) vanishing at every X=t^j. These are infinitely many distinct elements of F(t,λ), so all its coefficients vanish. This would force \(\mathcal K=0\), a contradiction. Hence some j<m has R_jv≠0.

Moreover

\[
 R_j=\frac{Z_j}{\prod_{e=0}^{j-1}Q(t^ez)},\qquad
 \deg_z Z_j\le nj,\qquad \deg_\lambda Z_j\le j.
\]

Induction follows from Z_(j+1)=σ(Z_j)P. Choose a nonzero λ^h coefficient of Z_jv; h≤j<m. Its z-order after division by the denominator is at most nj, since that denominator is a unit at zero and independent of λ. Therefore some λ^h coefficient of a nonzero homogeneous part of \(\mathcal K\) has total degree

\[
 1\le\ell\le nj\le n(m-1).
\]

No dependence coefficient is specialized or reduced modulo λ; only the polynomial numerator of the actual R_j is used. Poles in a rational dependence therefore cause no hidden specialization gap.

### 3.2 A difference analogue of Cauchy moments

Let a(z) be an arbitrary input series and let

\[
 Y_a=\sum_w\lambda^{|w|}B_wv\,J_w a.
\]

Then Y_a=va+λ∑_i B_iJ_i(Y_a), so

\[
 \sigma Y_a=A Y_a+v(\sigma-1)a.
\]

Put Z_a=H^−1Y_a. Direct substitution, with the factor H(tz)^−1 in the correct order, gives

\[
 (\sigma-1)Z_a=H(tz)^{-1}v(\sigma-1)a,
 \qquad Z_a(0)=v a(0).
\]

Fix the λ^h coefficient and total degree ℓ selected above, and write it in the kernel as \(\sum_{b=0}^{\ell}c_bz^{\ell-b}w^b\). For a=1, the coefficient z^ℓ in uY_a is c_0. For a=z^q with q≥1, the coefficient z^(q+ℓ) is

\[
 (t^q-1)\sum_{b=0}^{\ell}
       \frac{c_b t^b}{t^{q+b}-1}.
\]

If c_0≠0, the first input detects it. Otherwise use q=1,…,ℓ. The ℓ×ℓ matrix on b=1,…,ℓ has entries

\[
 \frac{t^b}{t^{q+b}-1}=\frac1{t^q-t^{-b}}.
\]

This is an invertible Cauchy matrix: the row parameters t^q are distinct, the column parameters t^−b are distinct, and the two sets are disjoint. The factors t^q−1 are nonzero. This holds in every characteristic. Consequently at least one of the tested coefficients is nonzero. Its largest row index is 2ℓ≤2n(m−1)<N.

On truncating z modulo z^N, the J_i become rational matrices

\[
 (U_i)_{r,q}=\frac{(1+t)^{i(r-q-1)}}{t^r-1}\quad(q<r),
 \qquad (U_i)_{r,q}=0\quad(q\ge r).
\]

The preceding calculation proves that f_h(U)≠0 for at least one h<m. Therefore

\[
 f(S_m\otimes U)=\sum_{h<m}S_m^h\otimes f_h(U)\ne0.
\]

For an acyclic representation, H is a finite word sum and word faithfulness makes \(\mathcal K\ne0\) without λ. The same row and moment argument directly proves f(U)≠0; there is no length-marker factor m.

### 3.3 Finite-ring descent and integer entries

Put \(D(t)=\prod_{r=1}^{N-1}(t^r-1)\). Every nonzero summand of an entry of U_w follows a strict chain of row indices. Its denominators use distinct factors of D, and its numerator is a power of 1+t of exponent at most n(N−1). Thus every entry of D f_h(U) is a polynomial of degree at most

\[
 \binom N2+n(N-1)=E-1.
\]

This remains true after any F-linear combination; no coefficient-height promise is needed for the algebraic statement. D(0)=±1 is a unit in every characteristic. A nonzero entry of D f_h(U) survives reduction modulo t^E. Since D remains a unit, f_h(U) also survives. Multiplication on F[t]/t^E is injective, because a multiplication operator applied to 1 recovers its defining element. The matrix regular representation therefore preserves nonvanishing. Its entries are exactly the integer construction in Section 1 after reduction to F. This completes both derived theorems.

## 4. Exact realization acquisition and full costs

For every prefix p, let f_p(w)=f(pw). All linear combinations of these residuals share the same hidden transitions B_i, changing only their initial row. Hence they still have state bound m, rather than one bound per hypothesis. The common feature \(\Phi(p)=\mathrm{vec}\,f_p(T)\) is injective on the residual space by the cyclic theorem.

Prefix features can be acquired from the original full-output oracle: use |p| prefix layers of width d, put an I_d edge for its successive letter at each layer, and put T_i in the final diagonal block. The (first,last) block of f evaluated on this tuple is exactly f_p(T). The entire tuple is jointly nilpotent; a sufficient vanishing length is m+|p|. Its dimension is (|p|+1)d. This is a legal query to the supplied formal-series service.

Start with ε, add a residual pi whenever its feature is independent of the current basis, and close under all n letters. There are r≤m basis rows, at most 1+nr exact queries, and prefix length at most r. Coefficients expressing Φ(pi) in the feature basis form the transition matrices. The output vector is f_p(ε), readable on the diagonal of f_p(T); the initial row selects ε. Closure proves every word coefficient is reproduced. Injectivity shows the resulting r equals the Hankel rank, so the realization is minimal. Comparing two unrelated m-state hypotheses instead requires the detector parameter 2m.

Resources must be charged as follows. For d=mNE=O(n³m⁴):

* Maximum query dimension: (r+1)d.
* Full responses: at most (1+nr)(r+1)²d² field entries, O(n⁷m¹¹) when r=m.
* Full dense input descriptions: n times that entry count. Reusing an encoded common tuple does not erase an oracle's input or computation charge.
* Over F_p with p in binary, each output entry uses O(log p) bits; all local construction and elimination use polynomial bit work. The dense universal integer tuple has O(n d²(1+nN)) bits, O(n⁹m⁹).
* Over an unspecified field this is a field-arithmetic theorem. For rational targets, coefficient heights and oracle response lengths must be added; m alone cannot bound them. A supplied coefficient-height bound makes the bounded-length evaluations and elimination polynomial in that bound, but acquiring that bound or the oracle is separate.
* During acquisition, count retained features, query matrices, elimination workspace, and any retained transcripts. Only after discarding those records can storage be described by the final O(nr²+r) field entries.

The theorem does not turn passive trajectory samples into matrix evaluations, does not yield a positive stochastic realization, and does not test arbitrary commutative circuits by simply lifting their syntax. It also does not apply to arbitrary rational formulas undefined at the nilpotent query. The released rational hitting lists could provide a matrix base point, but generalized-series reconstruction and reverse specialization require additional explicit domain work; no such transfer is adopted here.

## 5. Comparators, priorities, and failed shortcuts

The chosen October-4 source gives dimension O(n³m⁶) for an acyclic m-vertex path program. Its general shifted Casoratian order bound is O(nm²); after t descent, dimension becomes cubic in that z length. The new full-matrix argument gives z length O(nm), hence dimension O(n³m³). All-field universality of the integer entries is also directly recoverable from the source's coefficient loops; that fact alone is not a novelty claim.

For cyclic series, source plus the standard short-coefficient bound, m-layer unrolling into at most m² states, and an m-dimensional length marker already gives a polynomial detector of dimension O(n³m¹³). Thus **first polynomial learnability is not claimed**. The new deduction replaces that bound by O(n³m⁴) and extends the characteristic-zero compact-kernel programme to the shift setting. The earlier characteristic-zero L01 construction remains much smaller, O(nm²), in its applicable field class.

The first direct cyclic use of the source's order theorem would give O(n⁴m⁸), by retaining grading through the full O(nm²) z truncation. It is valid but superseded by the kernel proof. Finite-field scalar grids cannot simply reuse the characteristic-zero argument: a prime field may lack m distinct nonzero scalars, and a nonzero grading polynomial such as λ^p−λ vanishes at every scalar in F_p. The nilpotent marker preserves grades algebraically. No necessity or optimality claim is made for this marker or the final dimensions.

Primary older comparators:

* [Raz–Shpilka 2005](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~shpilka/publications/RazShpilka_PIT.pdf) give deterministic polynomial white-box PIT for noncommutative formulas and ABPs. Their described method uses the circuit structure, so this is not an output-only comparator.
* [Forbes–Shpilka, arXiv:1209.2408v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.2408v2), Theorem 1.5 and Corollary 4.7, provide membership-plus-equivalence-query learning and quasipolynomial deterministic simulation of those queries under the stated field-cardinality conditions. Standard truncation already makes this relevant to proper cyclic series.
* [Lakhani–Mukhopadhyay, TR26-120 revision 2](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/120/) give one integer hitting point for sparsity-bounded noncommutative polynomials. Sparsity and hidden-state bounds differ; a small acyclic representation can have exponentially many words.
* [Arvind–Chatterjee–Mukhopadhyay, arXiv:2309.15647v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15647v4) give quasipolynomial black-box rational identity testing. That interface includes inverse-domain work absent from the nilpotent series theorem here.

The bounded search and inspected source do not establish historical priority for the q-shift full-matrix kernel bound. It is an internal candidate deduction requiring independent proof review. The release's matrix-multiplication exponents were not repackaged as a new bit algorithm. Family 142's deterministic factorization was screened: its finite-field table and analytic conversion are meaningful, but the all-extension-field corollary is classical and the displayed overall exponent is about 400 million. The number-theory worker is auditing its companion and no stronger factorization claim is adopted here.

## 6. Executed evidence and decisive falsification

`verify_shift_detector.py` performs exact F_p arithmetic for nine fixtures, including dense cyclic systems in characteristics 2,3,5; a nonminimal cancellation zero; x²−x over F_2; a last-permitted-grade word; and a characteristic-two commutator. It simulates a lossless structured subvector of the full oracle outputs. The resulting realization is checked by an independent all-word reachable-space certificate on the difference automaton and by an independently formed finite Hankel matrix. Every scoped check passed; `verification.json` records the resources. The 1.48-second execution is **not** an external-oracle or dense algorithm benchmark. Some nominal dense response counts exceed 40 billion field entries.

`verify_dense_prefix.py` expands the n=2,m=2,p=2 detector (N=8,E=43,d=688), evaluates the actual prefix matrices by exact sparse word multiplication, and materializes every dense full response. Five queries with dimensions 688,1376,1376,2064,2064 return 12,780,288 field entries. Prefix extraction agrees with a separately computed coefficient model, and all-word equivalence and rank two pass. This remains an internally simulated oracle; inputs were stored sparsely and network/serialization costs were not exercised. Details are in `dense_prefix_verification.json`.

The universal mathematical claim is falsifiable by a nonzero m-state realization for which every grade h<m has f_h(U)=0 at N=2nm, or by a flaw in the semilinear-row closure, the difference Green-function identity, or the Cauchy recovery. Its most sensitive formula is the H(tz)^−1 factor in Section 3.2; replacing it by H(z)^−1 changes the moment identity. A practical capability claim would additionally require a useful target whose exact matrix oracle is acquired and timed with full input/output and coefficient costs. That acquisition has not occurred.

The independent-review target is therefore precise: check the q-shift full-matrix kernel proof, especially the passage from R_jv to a bounded grading coefficient and the input-monomial formula. There is no identified unresolved lemma inside the displayed derivation, but independent mathematical and novelty review are still required before treating this as an established new result.
