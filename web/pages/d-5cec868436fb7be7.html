VERDICT

The two assigned independent audits found no substantive gap in their
disjoint proof blocks. Under the stated measurable-instrument model, the
candidate inequality

  omega_Q(X)/(148 kappa^2) <= e_Q(X) <= 2 sqrt(omega_Q(X))

survives the operator/rank argument (§§2–4) and the covariance dual / growing
coherent-ball argument (§§6–7). The upper finite-label construction in §5
was not independently audited by these two leaves. Root/local-chart audits
remain separate. No result is transferred to mixed iid states.

AUDIT RECEIPTS

- operator_rank.txt: composite Kraus rank <=Q after arbitrary classical
  outcome and decoder; half-trace error controls the aggregate output
  off-diagonal vector; TP on input |x><y| gives the scalar-coefficient
  quadratic; an anchor-weighted mixture of Kraus-range projectors has trace
  <=Q and yields the claimed 148 kappa^2 factor. Exact orthogonality sharpens
  an intermediate bound to 37 kappa^2 epsilon, so source constants are safe.
  A common measurable Kraus field is needed for outcome integrals.

- width_dual.txt: weak-* compactness of the positive trace-constrained
  contraction set, minimax, exact Ky Fan covariance-spectrum dual and
  attainment all check. The two-mode coherent-ball resolution and volume
  give sigma<=2/R^4 I; the overlap chain costs kappa<=sqrt(e)(R+1); for
  R>=1, epsilon R^2<=1/(1184 e) implies Q>=ceil(R^4/4). The actual experiment
  must contain this growing pure-state subball.

HARMLESS PRESENTATION CLARIFICATIONS

1. In finite H with Q>dim H, pad the covariance eigenvalue list by zeros
   and use the identity/top available spectral projection; a literal
   rank-Q projection does not exist.
2. Distinguish Euler's constant e from the error e_Q in §7 notation.
3. In integral Kraus formulations, specify the measurable instrument/Kraus
   field, as already signaled by the source's measurable-alphabet caveat.
4. The width theorem is an existence/variational statement: optimizer
   acquisition, finite-precision eigensystems, gates, runtime and synthesis
   costs are not provided by the inequalities.

CONCRETE CROSS-FIELD CONSEQUENCE

The companion file CROSS_FIELD_COHERENT_PHASE_SPACE.txt derives a matched
capacity law for p fixed complex modes. On a growing radius
R=c epsilon^(-1/2) coherent ball, with c^2<=1/(1184 e), the pure-width lower
and the total-photon cutoff upper give

  Q_min(epsilon; R)=Theta_p(epsilon^(-p)).

The lower converts phase-space volume into a worst-case maximum-branch
dimension bound despite unlimited classical labels; the upper uses a single
Q-dimensional branch of rank binom(M+p,p), with an explicit Poisson-tail
cutoff M=ceil(R^2+sqrt(2 R^2 log(4/epsilon^2))+(2/3)log(4/epsilon^2)).
This joins convex approximation / covariance spectra with coherent-state
phase-space geometry and quantum memory scaling. It differs from the fixed-
radius chart's polylogarithmic precision law. The extension is conditional on
the candidate pure-hybrid width inequality; no external correctness,
algorithmic implementation or novelty/priority is established.

A second compact-domain example is in HARDY_SZEGO_PHASE_FAMILY.txt: the
geometric Fock-tail phase circle has exact width omega_Q=q^Q and hence
Q_min=Theta_q(log(1/epsilon)); fixed p-fold phase products give
Theta_(p,q)((log(1/epsilon))^p). This uses an exact covariance spectrum and
a matching phase-independent Fock cutoff, and is also only a conditional
mathematical corollary, not a priority claim.
