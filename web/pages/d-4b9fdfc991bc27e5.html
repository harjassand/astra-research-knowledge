## Exact interface and source claims

Let rho,sigma be faithful trace-one d-dimensional matrices, A_a known Hermitian generators, partial_a X=-i[A_a,X], I_{q,lambda}=(1-lambda)L_q+lambda R_q and S_{lambda,a}(q)=I_{q,lambda}^{-1}(partial_a q). Here scores may be non-Hermitian. Candidate matrix/evolution access is distinct from copy access to sigma; the target eigenbasis, modular operator and scores are not supplied. Define

F_lambda(rho||sigma)=1/2 sum_a Tr[(S_lambda(rho)-S_lambda(sigma))^dagger I_{sigma,lambda}(S_lambda(rho)-S_lambda(sigma))].

## Properness is already proved; strict properness is the scientific question

Reconstructing the trace integration by parts gives, for every lambda in[0,1],

Tr[sigma(O_lambda(rho)-O_lambda(sigma))]=F_lambda(rho||sigma)>=0.

Indeed the cross term is Re Tr[S_lambda(rho)^dagger partial_a sigma]=-1/2 Tr[sigma partial_a(S_lambda(rho)+S_lambda(rho)^dagger)]. There are no boundary integrals in finite matrices. Thus every published loss is proper. Strict propriety requires its score fiber to be trivial on the admitted model. Existing interior-frame collisions refute strict propriety of particular unrestricted losses, not this regret identity.

For nonsymmetric lambda, Hermiticity of an ambiguity matrix requires the adjoint-completed dressed commutant. Read notes/b_prior/prior_and_fiber.md for its independently reconstructed exact cone-slice fiber. The source unstarred scalar-commutant hypothesis is sufficient, and not claimed necessary. Full physical XYZ and a single asymmetric weight with the fixed physical local XZ frame remain unresolved here.

## Complementary weights are exactly redundant

In a q-eigenbasis, (S_lambda)_ij=-i A_ij(p_j-p_i)/[(1-lambda)p_i+lambda p_j]. Consequently

S_lambda(q)^dagger=S_{1-lambda}(q),
F_lambda(rho||sigma)=F_{1-lambda}(rho||sigma),
O_lambda(rho)=O_{1-lambda}(rho).

The last identity follows by swapping the two quadratic summands; the derivative summand is unchanged. It holds as an operator, not just at zero regret. A repair that adds complementary weights provides the same measurement observable and the same objective. An asymmetric weight can differ from SLD, but lambda and1-lambda do not supply distinct constraints.

## A convex-potential interpretation: older general proper-score theory applies

Let G_lambda(q)=1/2 sum_a <partial_a q,I_{q,lambda}^{-1}(partial_a q)>_F. The variational identity

G_lambda(q)=sup_{T_a} sum_a [Re Tr(T_a^dagger partial_a q)-1/2 Tr(T_a^dagger I_{q,lambda}(T_a))]

shows convexity: each trial expression is real affine in q, and the unique maximizing T_a is S_lambda(q). Differentiation on the faithful cone gives dG_lambda(q)[delta]=-Tr(delta O_lambda(q)). Positive homogeneity gives -Tr(q O_lambda(q))=G_lambda(q). Therefore the reward -Tr(sigma O_lambda(rho)) is precisely the supporting affine function of convex G_lambda at rho, and F_lambda is its Bregman regret. No new statistical properness principle is involved.

Primary older theorem: Frongillo, Quantum Information Elicitation2203.07469v1, submitted14Mar2022 20:07:47UTC, Theorem3.5 Eq8 (PDFp8) characterizes copy-measurement truthful scores through convex potentials. Theorem3.7 says fixed-measurement strict truthfulness on the entire state space requires a tomographically complete POVM. Example3.4 already supplies strictly proper quantum Brier reward2Tr(rho sigma)-Tr(rho^2) using candidate-dependent binary POVM{rho,I-rho}; Proposition3.10 implements arbitrary score observables by report-dependent projective measurement. Those abstract results charge neither efficient construction of that POVM nor local many-body evolution. They exclude novelty claims for generic one-copy proper scoring or candidate-dependent experimental conversion, while leaving efficient partition-free local mechanisms meaningful.

The fixed-measurement statement cannot be applied to a restricted Gibbs model without restricting the injectivity domain. Also a local generator is not a local measurement: O_lambda(rho) can be dense and candidate-dependent.

## Exact repair by a mixture over weights

Theorem (our elementary deduction). Suppose the ordinary frame has scalar commutant. Let mu be any probability distribution with density positive almost everywhere on a nonempty interval inside(0,1). Then

F_mu(rho||sigma)=integral F_lambda(rho||sigma)dmu(lambda)

is strictly positive for every distinct faithful pair. Its copy-expectation objective is integral O_lambda(rho)dmu(lambda), or equivalently choose lambda using classical randomness before measuring the candidate-dependent observable.

Proof. Nonnegativity implies zero mixed regret gives equality of all scores for mu-almost every lambda on the interval. For fixed faithful q each matrix entry of S_lambda(q) is a rational function of lambda with no pole on[0,1], by its eigenbasis formula. Equality on a set with an interior accumulation point therefore gives equality for every lambda. Direct scalar integration shows

integral_0^1 S_lambda(q)dlambda=partial_a log q:
integral_0^1 dlambda/[(1-lambda)p_i+lambda p_j]=(log p_j-log p_i)/(p_j-p_i),

with the continuous equal-population interpretation. Thus [A_a,log rho-log sigma]=0 for all a. Scalar ordinary commutant forces log rho-log sigma=cI, and normalization forces rho=sigma. All steps hold for arbitrary finite d; no numerical test is the proof.

In particular any such mixture with fixed physical local X/Z identifies all faithful states at all temperatures. This does not prove the same claim for any single fixed interior weight or for SLD plus physicalY. It also does not refute the existing all-interior arbitrary-frame example: its frame changes with lambda, whereas this theorem fixes one frame while mixing lambda.

Finite repair. For d>=2, any M=2d(d-1) distinct interior weights with positive mixture coefficients also suffice. Each S_lambda(q) has at most d(d-1) nonzero eigenbasis rational terms, with linear denominators (equal-eigenvalue terms vanish). A matrix entry of the score difference has a common denominator of degree at most M and numerator degree at most M-1. M distinct zeros force that numerator identically zero. The preceding proof finishes. This overestimate costs O(4^n) weights for n qubits and is not scalable. Complementary-weight redundancies can improve the count; no optimal count is asserted.

## Uniform mixture controls a BKM Dirichlet form

Let Omega_sigma(X)=integral_0^1 sigma^t X sigma^{1-t}dt, the logarithmic-mean superoperator. Petz–Ghinea1008.2417v1, submitted14Aug2010 05:38:58UTC, Example3 Eq13 (PDFp9) and Eq25 (p14) explicitly give the logarithmic mean and generalized inverse-metric scores. Spectral entry integration yields

integral_0^1 I_{sigma,lambda}^{-1}dlambda=Omega_sigma^{-1}.

For X_lambda=S_lambda(rho)-S_lambda(sigma), define z=integral X_lambda dlambda=partial_a(log rho-log sigma). Completing a square with Y=Omega_sigma(z) gives

integral <X_lambda,I_{sigma,lambda}X_lambda>_F dlambda
>=<z,Omega_sigma(z)>_F.

Explicitly integrate the nonnegative norm of X_lambda-I_{sigma,lambda}^{-1}Y. Its cross term is2Re<z,Y>; its final term is<Y,Omega_sigma^{-1}Y>=<z,Omega_sigma z>. Therefore

F_uniform(rho||sigma)>=1/2 sum_a Tr[(partial_a(log rho-log sigma))^dagger Omega_sigma(partial_a(log rho-log sigma))].

This is a useful exact bridge: the left objective is linear in the target and the right is the target-dependent BKM log-score Dirichlet form. The right need not be separately acquired to optimize the left. A quantitative entropy/trace-distance comparison needs a correctly matched functional inequality. Existing detailed-balance log-Sobolev results cannot be borrowed without checking the directions and weighting; no such comparison is proved here. For a mixture clipped to a strict interior interval, the log-integral identity no longer applies directly, so this full-uniform lower bound is not inherited automatically.

## Costs, decisive baselines and selection verdict

Mixed-weight strict propriety is elementary loss mixing plus analytic resolvent calculus. Endpoint strict propriety is already in two2026 papers. Thus the repair is a usable audit lemma, not a claimed foundational discovery. Random lambda selection is cheap, but exact construction and measurement of O_lambda(rho), truncation bias, variance, optimization and low-temperature stability are unresolved costs. Copy-estimability in principle does not mean polynomial resources.

Generic clipped acquisition remains exponential under a simple bound: for lambda in[epsilon,1-epsilon], each score entry satisfies |S_ij|<=|A_ij|/epsilon, hence ||S||_op<=||A||_F/epsilon<=sqrt(d)||A||_op/epsilon. The resulting norm bound for O is O(sum_a d||A_a||_op^2/epsilon^2) when epsilon<=1/2. A spectral measurement with that range gives an unbiased estimate, but neither constructing its basis nor its copy variance is controlled independently of d. Better locality-specific representations must be proved, not inferred from bounded scalar multipliers.

Fair baselines: endpoint operator score matching (same copies and candidate access), Dong–Leng's proved high-temperature local learner, classical ratio matching for diagonal states, and existing constant-temperature Hamiltonian-learning algorithms rather than generic tomography alone. Full quantum Brier has exact strict propriety but can demand candidate state/POVM construction; its access cost must remain visible.

Strongest current selected quantum result remains the independently certified fixed physical X/Z SLD collision. FullXYZ is a genuinely sharper unresolved algebraic problem; this worker found no primary older theorem resolving it. Absence of a search hit is not novelty evidence. The broad claim that proper losses or exact experiment-to-report conversions alone create a new research engine is weakened by the2022 characterization. Consequential progress would need an efficient accessible stability theorem, or a certified physicalXYZ/asymmetric-fixed-frame collision.
