SPIN-ONE ALL-WEIGHT STAR BOUND
2026-10-09. Internally derived exact algebraic theorem/certificate.
External validation and historical priority are not established.

THEOREM
Let Ji=-i epsilon_ijk be the vector spin-one representation on C^3,
Ai=sqrt(wi)Ji with arbitrary wi>=0, and
 W=sum_i wi Ji^T_A (Ji_B+Ji_C),
 L=sum_i wi Ji^2,
 V=Tr(L)/3=2 sum_i wi/3.
Let k be the unrestricted pure-state tracial support
 max_{sum_y py |psi_y><psi_y|=I/3} sum_y py sum_i
       <psi_y|Ai|psi_y>^2.
Choose the exact support optimizer z from
 k=(1/3) min sum_i zi^2,
 zi>=0, zj+zk>=2 sqrt(wi),
and put G=diag(zi^2), K=L+G. Then the OPERATOR inequality
  W <= (3/5)K_A+(1/5)(K_B+K_C)                    (S1)
holds on (C^3)^tensor3 for every weight triple.

Consequently every state R with all three single marginals I/3 obeys
  Tr(R W)<=Tr(K)/3=V+k.                          (S2)
In particular, every legal tracial unital self-compatible channel Phi
satisfies 2 s_Phi<=V+k for this full weighted spin-one frame. The Choi
self-adjointness and BC-swap conditions are not needed for (S2).
The isotropic and single-observable controls attain equality, so the
factor two cannot be reduced for this class. This does not close the
general arbitrary-frame/dimension universal-constant question.

SIMULTANEOUS CHANNEL CONSEQUENCE
For EVERY unital CPTP channel Phi on M3 admitting a CPTP two-output
broadcaster with both marginals Phi, there exists ONE canonical unital
HS-self-adjoint entanglement-breaking channel Psi on M3 such that
  tau(A^2)-tau(A Psi(A))
     <=2[tau(A^2)-tau(A Phi(A))]                  (S3)
for EVERY Hermitian A in span_R{I,J1,J2,J3}, where tau=Tr/3.
Phi need not be HS-self-adjoint or rotation covariant. The statement
controls this four-dimensional observable subspace only; it supplies no
comparison on the five remaining traceless quadrupole directions.

Proof of the consequence. A positive frame on the three-dimensional
real vector span is a real PSD 3x3 matrix Q. Choose an SO3 eigenbasis;
an orthogonal diagonalization can always be made proper by changing the
sign of an eigenvector. The real spin-one representation implements
these rotations unitarily. Conjugating the operator inequality (S1) on
all three registers proves it for that frame. Alternatively rotate the
channel and Choi state accordingly; legal tracial marginals are
preserved. No covariance of the originally supplied channel is assumed.
Thus for every Q>=0, k(Q)>=2s_Phi(Q)-V(Q).

Define the compact convex covariance set C of all matrices
 M_ij=sum_y py Tr(pi_y Ji)Tr(pi_y Jj)
over pure ensembles with sum_y py pi_y=I/3. Compactness follows from the
compact convex hull of the joint moment map pi -> (pi,m m^T), in affine
dimension at most 8+6=14. Every such covariance has a pure finite-ensemble
representation with at most 15 states by Caratheodory; refinement from
mixed states is unnecessary because the original set already uses pure
states. Put T_ij=tau(Ji Phi(Jj)) and H=2(T+T^T)/2-(2/3)I3.
The all-frame inequalities say sup_{M in C} Tr(QM)>=Tr(QH) for every
Q>=0. The downward closed convex set C-PSD is closed because C is compact.
If H were outside it, strict separation would produce a functional Q;
its supremum on C-PSD is finite only for Q>=0, contradicting the displayed
support inequality. Hence some M in C satisfies M>=H.

Choose its single finite ensemble and define
 Psi(X)=3 sum_y py pi_y Tr(pi_y X).
The effects 3py pi_y sum to I, so Psi is CPTP, unital, canonical EB, and
HS-self-adjoint. Its restricted quadratic form is x^T M x for
A=sum_i xi Ji, while tau(A^2)=(2/3)||x||^2 and the channel quadratic form
is x^T[(T+T^T)/2]x. The matrix domination gives (S3) simultaneously for
every such A. Unitality and trace preservation cancel all identity
cross terms, extending (S3) to aI+A. Psi depends on Phi, and is fixed
before choosing A. This is an existence theorem; no efficient algorithm
for extracting that ensemble is claimed.

INDEPENDENT SUPPORT PROOF
Write a pure vector, after a global phase, as p+i q with real orthogonal
p,q. Its spin expectation m has magnitude 2||p||||q|| and direction
normal to their plane. The D2 rotations preserve the objective, so the
convex-roof dual can be averaged to Y=diag(y1,y2,y3). Testing coordinate
pure vectors gives yi>=0; write yi=zi^2.
For any plane n-perp let lambda1,lambda2 be the eigenvalues of Y on that
plane. Minimizing over the two lengths and their orthogonal directions
shows that pure-state domination sum_i wi mi^2<=<psi|Y|psi> is equivalent
to
  (sqrt(lambda1)+sqrt(lambda2))^2 >= 4 sum_i wi ni^2
for every real unit normal n. Its left side equals
 sum_i (zj^2+zk^2)ni^2+2sqrt(sum_i zj^2 zk^2 ni^2).
Concavity of sqrt makes this at least sum_i (zj+zk)^2 ni^2. Thus the
three coordinate-plane constraints zj+zk>=2sqrt(wi) are sufficient;
testing coordinate normals makes them necessary. Compact convex-roof
duality at the interior reference I/3 gives the exact stated minimum.
There is also a direct primal attainment check. When all zi>0, let
lambda_i>=0 be the pair-constraint KKT multipliers for minimizing
(1/2)sum zi^2, so zj=lambda_i+lambda_k. On each active pair i, take the
conjugate pure states
 psi_i^+/-=sqrt(zk/(zj+zk)) |j> +/- i sqrt(zj/(zj+zk)) |k>
with combined probability mu_i=lambda_i(zj+zk)/(3zj zk). Their diagonal
barycenter is I/3 and each saturates the dual. In regime C, take the
circular conjugate states on coordinates 2,3 with combined weight 2/3
and |1> with weight 1/3. At the origin k=0. Boundaries follow by either
the corresponding explicit construction or compact limits.
