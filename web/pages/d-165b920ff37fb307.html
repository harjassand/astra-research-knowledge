LEAD SUPPLEMENT — TWO CERTIFIED ONE-SIDED REGIMES IN THE MARGINAL WINDOW
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE. The sharp thermal transition and the
speed-L free-energy limit remain UNKNOWN.

INTERFACE
---------
Let S=L-1, J,tau>0, |lambda|<=B, and define the coupling parameter
  gamma_L=L(4J+V_L).
The marginal negative scaling is gamma_L=-kappa/L, equivalently
  V_L=-4J-kappa/L^2,
so the Gibbs exponent is the scaled operator
  tau G_L=tau[L^2 Hcrit-kappa W-lambda N].
On the positive side gamma_L>=0, the scaled operator is
  tau[L^2 Hcrit+gamma_L L W-lambda N].
Here Hcrit is the endpoint-inclusive positive form in
../negative_scaled_coupling/RESULT.txt (1)-(2).

A. COHERENT PROFILE LOWER RATE AND MACROSCOPIC NUMBER
-----------------------------------------------------
For theta in C^2([0,1]), theta(0)=theta(1)=0, and 0<=theta<=pi, put
p=sin^2(theta/2) and sample the product spin coherent state with angles
 theta_i=theta(i/L), i=1,...,L-1.
Its exact local expectations are
  <J(I-sigma_i.sigma_{i+1})>=J[1-cos(theta_{i+1}-theta_i)],
  <J(I-Z_1)>=J[1-cos(theta_1)],
  <J(I-Z_S)>=J[1-cos(theta_S)],
  <N>=sum_i p(i/L), <W>=sum_i p(i/L)p((i+1)/L).
The endpoint terms are O(L^-2); Riemann sums yield
  <Hcrit>=J/(2L) integral |theta'|^2+o(L^-1),
  <N>=L integral p+o(L), <W>=L integral p^2+o(L).
Gibbs variational principle (the trial state is pure) gives

 liminf L^-1 log Z_L >= tau Gamma(theta),
 Gamma(theta)=kappa integral p^2+lambda integral p
                         -(J/2) integral |theta'|^2.       (1)

Let Phi_coh(kappa,lambda)=sup_theta Gamma(theta), with the H^1_0,
0<=theta<=pi extension. Smooth approximation preserves the lower bound.
If Phi_coh>0, then Z_L>=exp[tau Phi_coh L+o(L)].

There is a uniform-in-sector heat bound that turns this into a thermal
statement. In the m-particle sector the exact graph forms satisfy
Hcrit,m>=Hneu,m, where Hneu,m is the reflecting symmetric-exclusion
Laplacian without endpoint killing, and 0<=W<=m. The scaled sector
operators therefore obey, for the negative marginal and bounded lambda,
  L^2 Hcrit,m-kappa W-lambda m
       >=L^2 Hneu,m-(kappa+|lambda|)m I.
The elementary exclusion return-kernel comparison gives, for every
0<=m<=L-1,
  Tr_m exp(-tau L^2 Hneu,m)<=(e C_tau)^m,
  C_tau=1+2 sum_{r>=1} exp(-8J tau r^2),
independently of L,m. A proof is in
outputs/research/sol_attractive_critical/proof.txt, (5.1)-(5.6): domination
of the m-point return kernel by products of one-particle reflecting kernels,
p_t(i,j)<=C_tau/S, followed by binomial(S,m)<=S^m/m! and m!>=(m/e)^m.
Min-max, not operator monotonicity of the exponential, transfers the bound.
Thus
  Z_{L,m}<=A^m, A=e C_tau exp(tau(kappa+|lambda|)).       (2)
For any epsilon>0,
  Z_{N<=epsilon L}<=(L+1)A^(epsilon L).
If Phi_coh>0, choose epsilon with epsilon log A<tau Phi_coh. Then
  P_L(N>=epsilon L)->1
with an exponential-in-L failure bound. This proves macroscopic occupation
whenever the coherent lower functional is positive. It does not prove that
the coherent functional is the exact free-energy rate or that its zero set
is the true no-macroscopic-occupation phase.

The coherent variational threshold is
  lambda_coh(kappa)=inf_{theta !=0}
    [(J/2) integral |theta'|^2-kappa integral p^2]/integral p.
It satisfies
  2J pi^2-kappa <= lambda_coh(kappa)<=2J pi^2.           (3)
Indeed p<=theta^2/4, p^2<=p, and Dirichlet Poincare give the lower bound;
small multiples of sin(pi x) give the upper bound. For kappa=0 the value is
exactly 2J pi^2. For kappa>0 no closed form or true-phase interpretation
is claimed. This is the threshold for the lower-bound witness only.

B. A CONSERVATIVE TIGHTNESS REGION, INCLUDING ALL POSITIVE CONTACTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For negative marginal coupling, keep the sign of lambda instead of taking
its absolute value in the operator comparison:
  L^2 Hcrit,m-kappa W-lambda m
       >=L^2 Hneu,m-(kappa+lambda)m I.
Hence
  Z_{L,m} <= r_-^m,
  r_-=e C_tau exp(tau(kappa+lambda)).                     (4)
If r_-<1, then Z_L>=1 from the vacuum and, for every K,
  P_L(N>K)<=r_-^(K+1)/(1-r_-),
uniformly in L. Thus the number is uniformly tight when
  lambda < -kappa-tau^-1 log(e C_tau).
This is a sufficient low-lambda condition, not a sharp boundary.

For any nonnegative contact parameter gamma_L>=0 (including every sequence
gamma_L down to zero), positivity of gamma_L L W gives
  L^2 Hcrit,m+gamma_L L W-lambda m
       >=L^2 Hneu,m-lambda m I,
so the same proof yields uniform tightness whenever
  lambda < -tau^-1 log(e C_tau).                           (5)
The bound is independent of the rate gamma_L->0. It proves a nonempty
universal positive-side tightness regime but is conservative; the sharpened
positive-side route and low-energy archive are being separately audited.

COSTS, LIMITS, AND FALSIFIERS
-----------------------------
The coherent lower test uses L-1 local rotations, O(L) profile/observable
evaluations, and O(log L) bits per angle for a total state error O(L^-3)
that keeps the variational exponent error O(1). It is not a thermal sampler.
The number-tail proof is exact analytic work. C_tau can be evaluated to
additive precision delta by truncating after O(sqrt(log(1/delta))) terms;
no floating-point evaluation is required in the theorem.
The bounds distinguish macro-occupation from full-Fock tightness and do not
turn a physical postselection probability into a complexity lower bound.

Falsifiers: an error in the endpoint-inclusive Hcrit form; failure of
Hcrit,m>=Hneu,m; or failure of the product return-kernel heat estimate.
External proof validation and historical priority remain UNKNOWN. The
primary context and prior-art boundary are recorded in

matching upper at speed L, controlling the low-energy ferromagnetic density
of states and the entropy of sectors m=Theta(L); the sitewise spin-half
Berezin upper symbol is explicitly too loose in
luna_berezin/RESULT.txt §4.
