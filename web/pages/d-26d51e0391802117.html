## 1. Exact interface and block invariant

Work on S2 with probability area sigma. Let X_N be a distinct rational exact-unit set, supplied with a disjoint K-block decomposition A_1,...,A_K satisfying, for all x and 0<=r<=pi,

    # (A_nu intersect B(x,r)) <= C(1+N r^2),              (C1)

where C=15000. This bound counts actual nodes; no literal within-block separation is required. Every arbitrary prescribed X_N has the legal decomposition into N singleton blocks. Every block acquired by the weighted primitive has (C1) with its own size in place of N. Increasing the total N preserves the inequality, so (C1) persists forever with the same C. One can use a larger certified C with an adjusted constant below, but it is unnecessary for arbitrary prescribed-node completion.

Put

    H=sum_nu sqrt(|A_nu|),
    U=sum_nu ceil(sqrt(|A_nu|)),   H<=U<=2H.              (C2)

U is computed exactly by integer square roots; H is only an analysis quantity. Fix an integer S>=2. The construction works simultaneously for every real r in (1,S], with error constants uniform on each compact [r0,S], r0>1. Set

    L=ceil(sqrt(U^2 K^(S-2))),   A=2^90,
    t=min {integer v>=1: 200000000 v^2>=A K L^2},
    T=t-1,   m=200000000 t^2,
    B=S ceil(log2(m+1))+4.                              (C3)

All choices are exact integer operations. They satisfy

    L^2>=N,
    H^2 K^(S-2)<=L^2<=16H^2 K^(S-2),
    A K L^2<=m<=4A K L^2,  T>=t/2>=1,  T>=2L.        (C4)

For the last assertion, 200000000<2^28 and A=2^90 give t>=sqrt(A K/(200000000))L>2^31 L. In particular the actual acquired primitive corrects every harmonic in the entire filtered target, including the plateau through L.

The same order is available for any fixed rational S=a/b>=2: define L as the least integer satisfying L^(2b)>=U^(2b)K^(a-2b) and use ceil(S) in B. Complexity is for a fixed rational endpoint (or charges its numerical exponents), not polynomial only in their compressed bit length. For an arbitrary fixed real desired smoothness one can choose an integer or rational upper endpoint; no noncomputable real parameter is supplied as an algorithmic oracle.

## 2. Explicit rational filter and checked kernel hypothesis

Define h(u)=1 for u<=1 and h(u)=0 for u>=2. For 1<u<2 put v=u-1 and

    h(u)=1-12012 sum_(j=0)^6 (-1)^j binom(6,j) v^(7+j)/(7+j).
                                                               (C5)

Equivalently this is the normalized tail integral of v^6(1-v)^6 on [0,1]. Its beta integral is1/12012. Thus 0<=h<=1, its derivatives of orders1,...,6 vanish at both transition endpoints, and h is C6. Every h(l/L) is rational and exactly acquired. C-infinity is not assumed or needed here.

For any L>=1 set kappa_L(u)=h(|u|-1/(2L)), with the value one where the shifted argument is below one. It is even, C6, supported in[-5/2,5/2] and flat near zero. Their hypothesis is even kappa in C^k, k>=max(2,n-1), and derivatives through k bounded by C_kappa(1+|u|)^(q-alpha), alpha>n+k. Compact support is explicitly sufficient. Their kernel uses the shifted spectral parameter l+(n-1)/2, which is why the shift in kappa_L is present. We use n=2,k=6,alpha=9.

Here is an explicit safe bound, so positivity is not based on an uncharged existential constant. The coefficient absolute sum of the transition polynomial is <=1+12012*64/7<2^17. ## 3. Actual density positivity and exact cancellation target

The occupancy hypothesis and L^2>=N give, for each block and fixed y,

    sum_(x in A_nu) 1/[1+(L dist(x,y))^6] <8C.          (C7)

Indeed the ball of radius1/L contains at most2C nodes. The jth annulus [2^j/L,2^(j+1)/L), j>=0, contains at most C(1+4^(j+1))<=5C4^j nodes, each weighted at most2^(-6j). Their sum is at most2C+5C/(1-1/16)<8C. Truncation at pi only improves this bound.

Define the actual rational spherical polynomial

    g_L(y)=sum_(x in X_N)Phi_L(x,y)-N,
    w_L(y)=1-g_L(y)/m.                                  (C8)

The addition theorem and the constant l=0 coefficient give integral g_L d sigma=0 exactly. Equations(C6)–(C7), N<=L^2 and K>=1 give

    ||g_L||_infinity <=2^68 C K L^2.

Since m>=2^90 K L^2 and 2^90>2^69*15000, we have ||g_L/m||_infinity<1/2. Thus w_L has mean one and lies between1/2 and3/2, with an explicit strict margin. These are proved facts about the constructed polynomial, not supplied oracle promises.

In any sigma-orthonormal harmonic basis, the nonconstant moment of g_L is

    <g_L,e_lk>=h(l/L)sum_(x in X_N)e_lk(x).

Consequently the new weighted target is exactly its negative divided by m. Because h=1 through L, new weighted moments compensate all inherited moments through L. The encoded target is computed in a rational unnormalized basis. Legendre coefficients, rational h(l/L), old coordinates, and exact rational sphere monomial integrals suffice.

## 4. Old filtered high tail from cap occupancy

Write D_X=sum_(x in X)delta_x-|X|sigma. In the norm

    ||mu||_(H^-r)^2=sum_(l>=0,k)(1+l(l+1))^(-r)|<mu,e_lk>|^2,

the discrepancy D_(A_nu)-g_(L,nu)sigma has coefficients (1-h(l/L))sum_(x in A_nu)e_lk(x) for l>=1. Its squared norm is the sum over all actual ordered pairs in the block of the kernel

    Psi_(L,r)(x,y)=sum_(l>=1)(1-h(l/L))^2
                         (1+l(l+1))^(-r)(2l+1)P_l(x dot y).
                                                               (C9)

The series converges absolutely for r>1 by |P_l|<=1. We reconstruct the localization step with only six derivatives. Set R_j=2^j L. Use the nonnegative partition chi_0(u)=h(u/(2L)), and chi_j(u)=h(u/(2^(j+1)L))-h(u/(2^jL)) for j>=1. Their sum is one. After multiplication by(1-h(u/L))^2, the jth multiplier is supported in[R_j,4R_j]. Derivatives of orders q<=6 of

    a_j(u)=chi_j(u)(1-h(u/L))^2(1+u(u+1))^(-r)

are bounded by C_(r0,S) R_j^(-2r-q), uniformly in j,L and r in[r0,S]. The finite transition bands have bounded R_j/L; later ones have h(u/L)=0. Product rule and the positive lower bound1+u(u+1)>=u^2 prove this bound explicitly. Derivatives of (1+u(u+1))^(-r) are finite sums with coefficients bounded in terms of q and S. A fixed rational upper bound for any declared compact interval can therefore be computed.

The even shifted functions R_j^(2r)a_j(R_j|v|-1/2), extended as zero at the origin, are C6, have uniformly bounded compact support and derivatives, and satisfy the same published equation(10). Theorem3.5 consequently gives

    |Psi_j(x,y)| <= C_(r0,S) R_j^(2-2r)
                              /[1+(R_j dist(x,y))^6].   (C10)

The factor R_j^(2-2r) includes both the multiplier amplitude and sphere dimension; neither is omitted. Since R_j^2>=L^2>=N, the same annular bound(C7) applies at every scale R_j. Therefore

    ||D_(A_nu)-g_(L,nu)sigma||_(H^-r)^2
      <=8C C_(r0,S)|A_nu| sum_j R_j^(2-2r)
      <=C'_(r0,S)|A_nu| L^(2-2r).

The geometric sum converges uniformly for r>=r0>1. Taking square roots and triangle inequality yields

    ||D_X-g_L sigma||_(H^-r)<=C''_(r0,S) H L^(1-r).     (C11)

This is Wu–Zhuang Lemma3.2's load-bearing high-tail estimate with local multiplicity replacing separation. The full pair kernel, including diagonal terms, was used. No continuum quadrature is substituted for the inherited point discrepancy.

## 6. Nested cardinalities and all charged bit work

Start from one fixed rational unit point and one block. At stage j use K=j+1 in(C3). Let U_j be the exact integer sum in(C2). Since m_j>=A U_j^2 and m_j>=A,

    U_(j+1)=U_j+ceil(sqrt(m_j))

So U_j^2<=4N_j after the first stage (and initially U_0^2=N_0=1). Combining with(C3)–(C4) gives the uniform refinement schedule

    N_(j+1)<=65A (j+1)^(S-1)N_j,                      (C15)

while N_(j+1)>A N_j. Each finite prefix is nested, keeps all earlier nodes exactly fixed, and satisfies(C14) for every fixed real1<r<=S. This closes the critical and supercritical gap for the acquired interface. The source-level conceptual completion mechanism and exponent are prior.
