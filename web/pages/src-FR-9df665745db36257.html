# Scope correction: deterministic finite-error bound versus exact heralded theorem

Date: 15 October 2026. Applies to Section 4 of the frozen `REPORT.md`, specifically the paragraph beginning “An elementary finite-error obstruction is also available.” The original report, checks, and inventory are preserved unchanged.

The displayed finite-error bound

\[
 \epsilon\ge\frac{(2m)!}{2^{2m}(2m)^{2m}},\qquad N=2m-1,
\]

is proved here **for deterministic CPTP outputs only**, with uniform trace-distance error

\[
 \frac12\|\Lambda(\rho_t^{\otimes N})-
       (\rho_A(t)\otimes\rho_E(t))^{\otimes m}\|_1\le\epsilon
 \quad\text{for every }t\in[0,1].
\]

Under this normalization, the error of every effect probability is at most `epsilon`. A deterministic output effect probability is a degree-at-most-`N` polynomial in `t`; its order-`2m` finite difference vanishes. This is the premise used in the original finite-error calculation.

For a heralded trace-nonincreasing CP map, the unnormalized all-zero effect probability `f(t)` and success probability `q(t)` are polynomials of degree at most `N`. The normalized successful output has effect probability `f(t)/q(t)`, which is generally rational, not polynomial. Its order-`N+1` finite difference need not vanish. The displayed approximate-output bound has therefore **not** been established for normalized heralded outputs. No approximate heralded lower bound is supplied by this correction.

For example, with one classical correlated-bit preparation, accept input bit zero with probability one and bit one with probability `alpha`, where the fixed parameter `0 < alpha < 1` is independent of the unknown `t`. On success, output the original classical pair. Then

\[
 q(t)=t+\alpha(1-t),\qquad f(t)=t,
 \qquad\Pr(00\mid\mathrm{success})
 =\frac{t}{\alpha+(1-\alpha)t}.
\]

At `alpha=1/2`, the normalized all-zero probabilities on the grid `t=0,1/2,1` are `0,2/3,1`, whose second finite difference is `-1/3`. This explicitly shows why a degree-one input does not force the normalized successful probability to have zero second finite difference. It is a rationality example, not a protocol that achieves the decorrelated target or a counterexample to a stated approximate bound.

The **exact universal heralded theorem `N >= 2m` remains valid**. Exact normalized target equality on an interval of nonzero success gives the polynomial identity

\[
 f(t)=q(t)t^{2m}.
\]

For nonzero polynomial `q`, the right-hand side has degree `deg(q)+2m`, whereas `deg(f) <= N`. Hence `N >= 2m`, as argued in the frozen report. Bounded adaptive budgets still reduce to a fixed CP instrument; unbounded stopping times and expected-copy budgets remain outside that proof.

This correction narrows the finite-error statement and leaves the exact deterministic/heralded lower bound, routing structure, estimator proof, and physical limitations unchanged. It records a proof-scope clarification identified by the independent adversarial review; no external correctness certification or priority claim is implied.
