# Geometry / PDE / topology source-to-problem scout

Date: 2026-10-09. Scope: Phase A scout only; source trees remained read-only. No theorem here is independently certified. ASTRA source pin: `39cdea532f19217f633f70315d92c314a6ce5e3e`. OpenAI Math pin: `fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb`.

## Lead: a fixed three-manifold with recurrent harmonic-dimension excess

**Proposed theorem.** For every `4/9 < v < 1` and `1 < c < 9v/4`, there is one complete smooth metric `g` on `R^3`, Euclidean near the origin, with `Ric_g >= 0` and `AVR(g)=v`, for which

`limsup_{k -> infinity, k integer} dim H_k(R^3,g)/(k+1)^2 > c`.

The same `g` must work along an unbounded sequence of integer degrees. The metric may depend on `v,c`; the claim gives neither endpoint attainment nor a full limit.

This is the best geometry lead because it attacks a real quantifier obstruction in the pinned construction. The OpenAI Math 3D source states a metric `g_k` for each sufficiently large `k` and expressly allows it to depend on `k`; it leaves open whether one fixed manifold recurs infinitely often and whether `9v/4` is optimal. ASTRA N175 proposes the common-metric limsup. If N175 and the separate N240 upper are both verified, their combination makes `9v/4` the supremal limsup coefficient at fixed `v` in the stated 3D polar-end class for `v>4/9`. That is a sharpness conclusion inside this class, not a solution of a new all-manifolds upper-bound problem.

**Historical boundary.** Yau's sharp Euclidean comparison question is stated explicitly in Cai–Lai's 2026 primary preprint; they prove it under local conformal flatness. Colding–Minicozzi already establish finite dimensionality and optimal-order dimension estimates, which do not give the exact Euclidean count. Donnelly's 2001 examples violate comparison at suitable noninteger growth between one and two in dimensions at least five, not at integer degree. Huang proves the exact leading coefficient from normalized AVR under a unique tangent cone at infinity; N175's cycling links give nonunique cones, so that theorem is not contradicted. Lin–Wang–Xu's 2026 preprint proves the 3D sharp Euclidean bound under nonnegative sectional curvature and positive AVR; the pinned OpenAI `g_k` construction explicitly has negative radial sectional planes arbitrarily far out, outside that hypothesis. The 3D OpenAI release is itself a preprint premise, not independent validation.

**Mechanism.** The finite-degree 3D construction uses exact isolated double crossings of adjacent ordered eigenvalues of area-form-fixed metrics on `S^2`. Continuing eigenlines through the doubles cycles a band, so each mode averages the band frequencies, even when some instantaneous frequencies exceed the target growth degree. Whole-ball harmonic transfer then controls mode leakage and realizes compatible entire harmonic functions. N175's additional step is a countable hierarchy of disjoint degree bands `I_n=(B(M_{n-1}),B(M_n)]`, with `M_{n-1}/M_n -> 0`; increasingly late stages add new bands to one slow polar metric. The fixed-area angular family and a concave radial tail retain `Ric >= 0` and prescribed `AVR`. A countable compact-product fixed point matches born-band lines, and all-radius estimates place those functions in `H_{k_n}`. The band count and mean-frequency formula yield the `9v/4` limiting coefficient.

**Load-bearing missing lemma / proof gate.** Independently reconstruct the countable whole-ball matching lemma: after the finite spectral library is fixed, one schedule must make every new band's exact transfer recurrences solvable through all later stages, with one control per mismatch, no duplicated equations for old bands, and all-radius pointwise growth. The essential local estimate must bound the **entire** finite transfer-error matrix by `O_n(a_nu)` at each crossing, where `a_nu=eta(t_nu)^(3/4)`, uniformly over future controls; a bound only on the selected off-diagonal entry is false (the retained 3x3 counterexample flips its sign through another column). Separately reconstruct the S1–S3 all-adjacent fixed-area exact-crossing library; countable transfer cannot compensate for a missing crossing input.

**Early falsifier.** Before auditing the full countable argument, take one fixed born band and one crossing, derive the graph-corrected full local matrix expansion from the actual whole-ball maps uniformly over all other controls, and check that the mismatch sign remains opposite at the two control endpoints after every off-diagonal term is charged. An error term controlled only by `O(a_nu^(3/4))` or `O(eta)` rather than `O(a_nu)` falsifies the fixed coordinate box argument. Then check one stage transition with the stated support-comparability and stage-start inequalities; if no finite `T_m` satisfies them simultaneously, the schedule is circular/infeasible. These are decisive gates; another finite frequency plot is not.

**Compatibility and novelty limits.** The candidate fits the N240 3D upper hypotheses if the metric has the stated compact fixed-area polar links; that upper is attributed to published Li–Wang energy-Gram machinery in ASTRA, but its source PDF is absent from the pinned packet and its exact statement still needs checking. No claim follows for every Ricci-nonnegative manifold, for unique-cone manifolds, for locally conformally flat or nonnegative-sectional metrics, or at `v<=4/9`. No endpoint metric, all-degree lower bound, effective construction cost, or finite metric has been materialized. N175's reported internal source/transfer/uniformity audits do not establish external correctness or priority.

## Secondary technical lead: averaged polar capacity when link area varies

**Proposed theorem.** Let `n>=3`, `d=n-1`; suppose a complete manifold with compact core has global smooth polar end `g=dr^2+f(r)^2 h_(log r)` on `S^d`, with proper `r`, `f(r)/r -> a>0`, `h_t` precompact in `C^infinity`, and `||dot h_t||_(C^0(h_t)) -> 0`. Write `A(t)=Vol(S^d,h_t)`, `K(t)=omega_d a^d A(t)/(2pi)^d`, and `L=liminf_(T->infinity) T^(-1) int_(t0)^T K(t)^(-1/d)dt`. Then every `H_k` is finite and `limsup h_k/k^d <= ((d+1)/(dL))^d`. There is no pointwise `rf'/f -> 1`, no fixed area form, no limit of `A(t)`, and no Ricci-sign assumption.

This is a plausible standalone analytic theorem, but it is a reserve item, not a second foundational headline. Its mechanism integrates the scalar density derivative exactly as `d log(f(e^t)/e^t)`, an endpoint-bounded term, and charges the slowly varying angular density by `o(T)`. A uniform frozen-cone free-Neumann-collar comparison supplies a lower bound for high Dirichlet-to-Neumann eigenvalues; a Gram determinant and Ky Fan then count harmonic modes. It removes assumptions in narrower polar-end estimates, but no named longstanding conjecture is thereby resolved.

**Load-bearing lemma.** Prove a multiplicative Dirichlet-energy and boundary-norm comparison for every boundary test function between the actual outer sphere and a frozen-cone collar, uniform in the logarithmic radius, then combine it with a compact-family uniform Weyl law. Pointwise Weyl asymptotics are insufficient because the radius and index limits cannot be exchanged.

**Early falsifier.** Check the claimed law on a radial oscillation with `f(r)/r -> a` but `rf'/f` not converging, and on a slowly varying, nonconvergent `A(t)`; derive the exact Gram identity with the moving boundary measure. Any uncancelled bulk term involving `f'` or a non-`o(T)` angular-measure term invalidates the theorem. This path needs specialist proof and priority review; N240 already gives a broader 3D fixed-area upper by attributed published machinery, and N263 contains a neighboring all-dimensional birth-degree result.
