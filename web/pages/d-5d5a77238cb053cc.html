# Controlled target catalogues: independent root derivation

Cycle2, 6 October 2026. Status: candidate theorem with independently developed team-D proof under review. Root's initial finite-batch argument gives a weaker logarithmic remainder; team D's random deficit repair may restore the square-root remainder. No novelty claim. All channels and terminal policies are supplied, not learned, and exact-probability computation is outside the sample-cost model.

## Interface and geometry

Finite hidden states i=1,...,s. Finite known source actions a, costs c_a>0, full-support finite observation laws P_i^a. The source menu jointly distinguishes all state pairs. Finite known target actions b, costs β_b>0, full-support finite observation laws Q_i^b. Individual target actions may leave pairs indistinguishable. Policies adapt actions to past observations, stop in finite expected time under every state, and produce a finite terminal report. Source simulation preserves the complete statewise terminal report law exactly; it need not reproduce target timing or online interaction.

Let d_ij^a=KL(P_i^a||P_j^a), g_ij^b=KL(Q_i^b||Q_j^b). For v∈R_+^(s−1), define the source row gauge

 φ_i(v)=min{Σ_a c_aw_a : w≥0, Σ_a d_ij^a w_a≥v_j for every j≠i}.

Its compact dual is Λ_i={λ≥0: Σ_j λ_j d_ij^a≤c_a for every a}, and φ_i(v)=max_{λ∈Λ_i}λ·v. Compactness follows from any source allocation w^0 with D_iw^0≥1: Σ_jλ_j≤c·w^0. Thus φ_i is monotone, positively homogeneous, subadditive and globally Lipschitz on the nonnegative orthant. Define

 γ_i=max_b φ_i(g_i^b)/β_b.

Conjectured sharp uniform leading coefficient: every integrable target-menu policy Π admits exact source reproduction with

 E_i C_source ≤ E_i Σ_{t≤T}φ_i(g_i^{B_t}) + o(E_i C_target),

uniformly over target policies, and hence coefficient γ_i. The remainder we derive below is C sqrt(μ_i log(e+μ_i)), μ_i=E_i C_target. Team D's adaptive deficit repair aims at C sqrt(μ_i).

Geometric form: the source unit-cost information region
 R_i={v≥0: v≤Σ_a w_a d_i^a for some w≥0,c·w≤1}
is the downward closure of the convex hull of the per-cost source vectors. γ_i≤1 iff every per-cost target action vector g_i^b/β_b lies in R_i. The inclusion is ROW-SPECIFIC: the simulator cannot know i, so a proof must realize all rows using a single admissible source policy. Candidate classification/proposal/verification is what supplies that missing step.

## Necessary leading coefficients

Fix target action b and return the full transcript of m observations. Under any controlled source policy of finite expected cost, KL of its stopped path in states i,j equals Σ_a E_i N_a d_ij^a. The action-selection probabilities cancel between states because the policy is a state-independent function of its observed history. Exact terminal output and data processing imply D_i(E_iN)≥m g_i^b. Thus E_i C_source≥m φ_i(g_i^b). Target cost is m β_b. No policy-uniform coefficient smaller than γ_i can hold in state i. This converse uses only pure target actions; adaptation must be handled in sufficiency.

## Bounded target and a finite-batch verifier

Suppose T≤m, m≥1. Write H_i(y) for target report probabilities, retaining its common support. Full support of the target channels implies all retained H_i(y)>0 and |log(H_i(y)/H_j(y))|≤L_Qm with a menu-dependent L_Q. Let λ_ij(y)=log(H_i(y)/H_j(y)), η=η_0/(m+1), g=log(1/η).

Classify a candidate k from O(log(m+1)) samples of a fixed identifying source mixture, with statewise error≤η. Propose y~H_k using independent state-independent randomization. Given (k,y), choose an LP allocation satisfying

 D_kw≥[λ_k(y)]_+ +(g+u_m)1,
 u_m=A sqrt(m log(e m)).

Round each action count upward and acquire that deterministic batch. Its cost is at most φ_k([λ_k(y)]_+)+O(g+u_m), and its length at most C_A m, uniformly in H,y (constants depend only on catalogues/costs). All source log increments are bounded. Choose fixed A large enough that A²/(C_0+C_1A) exceeds the Hoeffding constant; then under true k, all batch log likelihoods exceed λ_kj(y)+g with probability≥1−η. This avoids any lower-bound assumption on the output-specific optimal mixture's individual drift.

Accept only if these likelihood inequalities all hold. Under wrong true i, fixed-history change of measure gives
 P_i(accept|k,y) ≤η H_i(y)/H_k(y) P_k(accept|k,y).

The deterministic action batch depends on k,y, but these are observed/proposed data and its same conditional policy applies under every state. No action is chosen using true i. Wrong-candidate batch cost is O(m), multiplied by candidate error≤η, hence O(1).

Thin acceptance by a suitable state-independent O(η) factor, making accepted submass Q_i(y)≤(1−η)H_i(y), with total missing mass δ_i between η and Cη. The normalized residual R_i=(H_i−Q_i)/δ_i has log-ratio diameter O(m+log(m+1)). The existing exact fixed-length classifier-and-inverse-kernel primitive reproduces it with O(m+log(m+1)) source samples. It is needed only on an event of probability O(η), so adds O(1) expected cost. This yields

 E_i C_source ≤ E_{Y~H_i} φ_i([λ_i(Y)]_+) + C sqrt(m log(e m)).        (A)

This construction is mathematically finite for bounded targets, but computing H, verifier probabilities, residuals and exact stochastic inverse kernels is not assumed efficient.

## Target adaptivity does not change the information gauge bound

Let S_ij(T) be the stopped target log likelihood, including action labels. Conditional Jensen gives
 λ_ij(Y)≤E_i[S_ij(T)|Y].
Write S_i(T)=A_i(T)+M_i(T), where A_i(T)=Σ_{t≤T}g_i^{B_t}≥0 coordinatewise and M is a martingale. The finite menu bounds conditional increment variances uniformly, so E_i||M_i(T)||²≤C E_iT.

Using componentwise positive parts, monotonicity, convexity and subadditivity,
 E_i φ_i([λ_i(Y)]_+)
 ≤E_i φ_i([S_i(T)]_+)
 ≤E_i φ_i(A_i(T))+E_i φ_i(|M_i(T)|)
 ≤E_i Σ_{t≤T}φ_i(g_i^{B_t}) + C sqrt(E_iT).

For the first step, [λ]_+≤E[[S]_+|Y] and Jensen applies to φ. The compact dual supplies the last Lipschitz constant. These bounds are uniform in policy and terminal report alphabet. Combining with(A) gives the claimed bounded-target guarantee.

## Arbitrary integrable target policy

Use exact dyadic continuation reports: at each target boundary return either a terminal report or the next finite target history, including action labels. Source restarts use fresh source randomness and the bounded converter for that specific continuation. Exactness inductively preserves target histories and their probabilities. Leading gauge costs telescope/sum to EΣφ_i(g_i^{B_t}).

For μ=E_iT, the remainder is controlled by
 Σ_{k≥0}P_i(T>2^(k−1)) sqrt(2^k log(e2^k)) ≤C sqrt(μ log(e+μ)).
Split at 2^k≈max(1,μ); before it use probability≤1, afterward Markov≤μ/2^(k−1). For μ<1 preserve the initial state-independent continue decision and multiply by its probability≤μ, giving O(μ)≤O(sqrt(μ log(e+μ))). Positive bounded target costs convert μ to expected target cost with menu-dependent constants. Almost-sure termination and finite expected source cost follow from the finite target mean and the summed bound.

Consequently, the leading coefficients γ_i are sharp, and strict inclusion γ_i<1 for all i gives eventual uniform same-budget reproduction of every target-menu terminal policy. Equality is not decided by the sublinear remainder. The row-wise information polytope is a scientific comparison tool only after the model class and laws have been supplied or acquired with their own costs.

## Stronger team-D route to check

Use a rounded LP batch with only g1 slack. Under the proposed true state, random deficits relative to success thresholds have expected size O(sqrt(batch length)). A fresh fixed identifying baseline repairs those observed deficits; reject whenever a reverse log likelihood reaches g. Ville bounds own rejection by(s−1)η without requiring deterministic high-probability slack. Under a wrong state, the reverse pair has positive drift and the extra rejection cost is O(batch length+g). This may replace the logarithmic remainder by sqrt(μ). It must be checked for likelihood exactness, reverse-start positions and finite expected hitting times. Root's weaker construction remains an independent fallback if that repair fails.

## Prior art and unresolved discovery value

The fixed-target exact converter is Li–Liu arXiv2608.19897v2. Statewise controlled testing and KL allocations are classical Chernoff/Nitinawarat–Atia–Veeravalli. The present whole-catalogue arbitrary-output comparison goes beyond testing, but novelty has not been established. Strongest potential use is choosing or replacing calibrated experimental apparatus while preserving every later adaptive finite decision law. It does not identify a model family, compute unknown laws, reproduce physical interventions that change the hidden state, or provide an efficient implementation by itself.
# Root audit update: sharper theorem adopted internally

The independently checked deterministic-batch/observed-deficit continuation in
`notes/team_d_experiments/cycle2/sharp_target_menu.md` improves this note's
square-root-log remainder to a square-root remainder. The decisive point is to
bound deficits on a hypothetical full batch and then drop the survival indicator;
one must not condition a mean-zero batch fluctuation on survival. The baseline
continuation repairs only the observed deficits, while reverse-likelihood
rejection bounds wrong-candidate cost. See
`notes/check_selection/cycle2/strong_uniformity_audit.md` for the independent audit.

Root read the full proof and accepts the mathematical known-channel, finite-report
claim with those scopes. The converter depends on the requested target policy.
It is not a record acquired once before all future questions, and the finite
rational implementation has not demonstrated a same-budget advantage. Prior-art
and computational acquisition gaps remain explicit.
