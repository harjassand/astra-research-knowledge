## Strongest claim

The joint-variance minimax in the supplied broadcasting report correctly encodes one measurement shared by every state label. In each fixed finite-dimensional faithful tracial algebra, for a bounded likelihood family \(E=\{H_x\}\), its quantity

\[
q(E)=\inf_M\sup_x\left[\tau(H_x^2)-\sum_y\frac{\tau(M_yH_x)^2}{\tau(M_y)}\right]
\]

is exactly

\[
q(E)=\sup_{p\in\mathcal P(E)}\inf_{\mathcal F\,:,\sum_y w_y|\psi_y\rangle\langle\psi_y|=\sigma}
\sum_y w_y\sum_xp_x\operatorname{Var}_{\psi_y}(H_x).
\]

Here the infimum ranges over a *single* rank-one measurement / reference ensemble for the whole prior-weighted family. The equality is a finite-dimensional minimax theorem, not an exchange of independent label optimizers. I independently reconstructed the algebra and the weighted-block interpretation below. This validates the quantifier placement of the proposed collective-variance target, but it does not prove that selfcompatibility makes \(q(E)\) small or establish the proposed universal Dirichlet comparison.

## Derivation and exact common-map witness

Let \(\mathcal A=\bigoplus_b M_{d_b}\), with faithful normalized trace
\(\tau(A)=\sum_b w_b\operatorname{Tr}(A_b)/d_b\), where every \(w_b>0\) and \(\sum_b w_b=1\). The corresponding physical reference is central, \(\sigma_b=(w_b/d_b)I_b\); states are \(\rho_H=\sigma(I+H)\), with \(H=H^*\), \(\tau(H)=0\), and \(\|H\|_\infty\le\theta<1\).

For a POVM \(M_y\in\mathcal A\), put \(p_y=\tau(M_y)>0\) and \(a_y(H)=\tau(M_yH)\). The canonical measure-and-prepare likelihood map is \(\Psi_M(A)=\sum_y \tau(M_yA)M_y/p_y\), and the physical map is \(\rho\mapsto\sum_y\operatorname{Tr}(M_y\rho)\,\sigma M_y/p_y\). Its outcome distribution on \(\rho_H\) is \(p_y+a_y(H)\), so the measured \(\chi^2\) score is \(\sum_y a_y(H)^2/p_y\). Its loss is the displayed \(\tau(H^2)\) minus that score.

Refining an effect into positive effects within its matrix block can only increase the score for every \(H\): for \(M=\sum_jM_j\), weighted Cauchy–Schwarz gives \(\sum_j\tau(M_jH)^2/\tau(M_j)\ge\tau(MH)^2/\tau(M)\). Thus rank-one refinement is valid simultaneously for all labels. A rank-one effect \(M_y=t_y|\psi_y\rangle\langle\psi_y|\) in block \(b\) has \(p_y=w_bt_y/d_b\), and its prepared physical state \(\sigma M_y/p_y\) is exactly \(|\psi_y\rangle\langle\psi_y|\). Completeness becomes the fixed-weight ensemble identity \(\sum_{y\in b}p_y|\psi_y\rangle\langle\psi_y|=(w_b/d_b)I_b\). Thus the pure ensembles in the variance formula must represent the actual faithful reference, including each block's mass; they are not arbitrary ensembles with uniform weights across blocks.

For finite \(E\), the set of POVM score vectors is convex when the coin selecting a POVM is retained as an outcome, and compact after rank-one refinement with a finite outcome bound in each fixed dimension. The loss is affine in this retained-outcome measurement and affine in the prior \(p\). Sion's finite-dimensional minimax therefore yields \(\inf_M\max_x\ell_M(H_x)=\max_p\inf_M\sum_xp_x\ell_M(H_x)\). For each prior, subtracting the maximum score over reference ensembles from \(V(p)=\sum_xp_x\tau(H_x^2)\) gives the average pure-state variance. In finite dimension the same statement extends to a compact infinite label family by taking its compact closure and approximating probability measures by finitely supported priors. No label-count factor appears in this identity.

An exact qubit example shows why the common-map quantifier matters. Under \(\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/2\), take \(E=\{X,Z\}\). For a pure state with Bloch vector \(r\), \(\operatorname{Var}(X)=1-r_x^2\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1-r_z^2\). For prior \((p,1-p)\), the largest ensemble capture is \(\max(p,1-p)\), because \(p r_x^2+(1-p)r_z^2\le\max(p,1-p)\), with equality from the corresponding antipodal eigenstate ensemble. Hence the prior's minimum collective variance is \(\min(p,1-p)\), and \(q(E)=1/2\). One common POVM attains it: \(M_{\pm X}=(I\pm X)/4\), \(M_{\pm Z}=(I\pm Z)/4\), giving loss \(1/2\) for both labels. Each label separately has loss zero under its own projective eigenbasis. Therefore \(\max_x\inf_M\ell_M(H_x)=0\) while \(\inf_M\max_x\ell_M(H_x)=1/2\); replacing one common measurement with per-state choices is invalid.

The same calculation audits faithful weighted traces. Embed \(X,Z\) into one qubit block of weight \(w\in(0,1)\), with zero on the other blocks, and multiply both likelihoods by \(\theta\). The collective variance is exactly \(w\theta^2/2\). The matching four-outcome measurement has score and variance weighted by \(w\), with no factor \(1/w\). Thus the estimates are naturally in the faithful physical trace; they require no uniform lower bound on block weights. This does not license replacing the weighted \(L^2(\tau)\) order by an unweighted blockwise norm.

A second exact benchmark recovers the factor-two threshold already scoped in the supplied report. For \(E=\{X,Y,Z\}\) with uniform prior, \(\sum_i r_i^2=1\) for every pure qubit state, so the best ensemble capture is \(1/3\) and the collective variance is \(2/3\). The six-outcome Pauli POVM \(M_{\pm i}=(I\pm\sigma_i)/6\) has loss \(2/3\) for each label. The marginal of the primary-source optimal 1-to-2 universal qubit cloner is the depolarizing channel \(\Phi(\sigma_i)=2\sigma_i/3\), so its Dirichlet energy averaged over this prior is \(1/3\). The ratio is exactly 2. This proves that any universal \(c\) in \(I-\Psi\le c(I-\Phi)\) must be at least 2; it does not prove the candidate inequality for general \(\Phi\). A rational-arithmetic script checks the POVM score identities and block-weight scaling.

## Growing-label boundary and next gate

The minimax identity itself is per algebra and includes the entire label family, even when its size grows with dimension. Its dual witnesses may nevertheless use a number of labels growing with the real dimension of the Hilbert–Schmidt space: the finite-dimensional positive-cone separation only bounds a witness frame by a dimension-dependent Carathéodory/rank bound. Consequently, proving a statement for every fixed number of labels and then letting dimension grow does not prove \(q_n\to0\) for families with \(|E_n|\to\infty\). A dimension-free Dirichlet inequality for all Hermitian tests would avoid this gap; a weaker sequential theorem must state a uniform modulus over the growing families. The supplied N46 route gives a separate sufficient condition \(b_n\log N_n(a_n)\to0\), with \(a_n\to0\), and must retain that entropy condition rather than silently assume bounded covering numbers. The supplied N61 copy-ladder counterexample is a warning about growing complexity for a different, non-sandwiched experiment interface; it is not a counterexample to the current fixed-sandwich target.

The exact next test is still: prove a dimension- and trace-weight-independent bound on the one shared \(q(E)\) from the selfcompatible channel's Dirichlet energies, or give a sequence of faithful tracial algebras, positive selfadjoint selfcompatible \(\Phi_n\), and growing bounded families \(E_n\) with \(\sup_{H\in E_n}\|\Phi_n(H)-H\|_{1,\tau_n}\to0\) but \(q(E_n)\not\to0\). Failure of the proposed constant 2 alone would not refute a weaker vanishing-modulus theorem.
