C09_L01 REVISION 02 — INVERSE-COVARIANCE LOCALIZATION AUDIT
UTC: 2026-10-07T13:40:21Z. INITIAL remains immutable.

I derived a finite-horizon inverse-covariance estimate that addresses the
main regularity concern raised in INITIAL. It uses the same third-cumulant
input already needed for the BKL induction. This is an internal argument, not
external validation of the cited third-cumulant theorem.

LEMMA (FINITE-HORIZON MOMENTS OF THE INVERSE POSTERIOR COVARIANCE)

Let the initial law be full-dimensional, compactly supported in B(0,R), with
isotropic covariance A_0=I. Along Eldan localization, before any possible
singularity,
  dA_t = sum_i H_{i,t} dB_{t,i} - A_t dt.
Let C_{i,t}=A_t^(-1/2) H_{i,t} A_t^(-1/2). The whitened third-cumulant bound
R_3^{nu_t}<=8I gives the matrix inequality used explicitly in BKL Lemma 5.1,
  sum_i H_{i,t} A_t^(-1) H_{i,t} <= 8 A_t,
and hence
  sum_i C_{i,t}^2 <= 8I.
Then for every p>0 and finite T,
  sup_{0<=t<=T} E[(det A_t)^(-p)] <= exp(5pnT+4p^2 n^2 T),
and consequently
  sup_{0<=t<=T} E[lambda_min(A_t)^(-p)]
       <= R^(2p(n-1)) exp(5pnT+4p^2n^2T).
The displayed constants are deliberately crude; they depend on n,R,T,
which is harmless for justifying a fixed-dimensional Ito computation. They do
not enter the universal induction constant K.

PROOF

Apply matrix Ito to log det A, first stopped where lambda_min(A) is bounded
away from zero and the coefficients are bounded:
  d log det A
   = sum_i tr(C_i) dB_i
     + [-n - (1/2)sum_i tr(C_i^2)]dt.
Since C_i are symmetric and sum C_i^2<=8I,
  0 <= sum_i tr(C_i^2) <= 8n,
so the drift lies in [-5n,-n]. Also
  sum_i tr(C_i)^2 <= n sum_i tr(C_i^2) <= 8n^2.
The log-determinant martingale therefore has quadratic variation at most
8n^2 t. Exponential-martingale control gives
  E exp(-p M_t) <= exp(4p^2n^2t),
while log det A_t >= log det A_0 -5nt+M_t. This gives the determinant moment
bound. The compact support gives lambda_max(A_t)<=R^2, since every directional
variance is at most the second moment of a variable supported in B(0,R). Thus
det A_t<=lambda_min(A_t) R^(2(n-1)), giving the inverse-minimum bound.
The same stopped estimate rules out finite-time covariance collapse: if
lambda_min reached zero before finite T, log det would tend to -infinity,
contradicting the bounded drift and finite quadratic variation of its
martingale. The estimate passes from stopped processes by Fatou. Once A^-1 is
locally finite pathwise, the localization coefficients for c_t and Q_t are
locally bounded (posterior means stay in the compact support); ordinary local
SDE continuation excludes finite-time explosion. This closes the covariance
part of the compact-support localization argument.

CONSEQUENCE FOR ITO AND THE T-INFINITY LIMIT

For fixed compact support radius, raw centered posterior moments and cumulants
of each fixed order are bounded by constants depending on R and the order.
The inverse-covariance estimate supplies every finite moment of the negative
powers introduced by whitening. Thus on every bounded time interval the
cumulant-energy process, its finite-order drift terms, and its Ito martingale
have finite moments sufficient for the finite-T expectation identity (or its
stopped limit). Dimension-dependent constants are acceptable here because
this step only licenses the computation; the subsequent drift inequalities
retain the dimension-free coefficient C and induction constant K.

In Lemma 5.2, the paper's statement “integrability ... allows T to infinity”
should be read along a sequence. If f(t)=E E_m(t)>=0 and integral f<infinity,
choose increasing T_j with f(T_j)<=2^-j. Apply the justified finite-T
identity at each T_j. The terminal term tends to zero, the integrable drift
terms converge by Cauchy–Schwarz, and the nonnegative E_{m+1} integral passes
by monotone convergence. This repairs the stated limit without needing
E E_m(T)->0 for all T.

SCOPE / CAVEAT

The estimate relies on R_3^{nu_t}<=8I, i.e. the imported Letwin/Chen–Klartag
third-moment result, and on compact support. It supplies a plausible explicit
finite-horizon integrability argument for the inverse covariance; it does not
independently reprove the imported third-cumulant theorem, the Song–Zhang
criterion, the suspension argument, or BKL's approximation pass. It also does
not certify historical novelty or external review. Subject to those imported
results, the inverse-covariance objection in INITIAL is materially narrowed:
there is a dimension-dependent moment bound sufficient for finite-horizon Ito
regularity, with no dimension-dependent loss in the claimed cumulant
induction constants.
