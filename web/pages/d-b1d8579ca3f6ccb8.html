## Exact claim to check

Fix the classical affine linear Frege system in the source's Tables 6–8
(TeX labels `Fig: FL_e`, `Fig: linear`, `Fig: contraction-free logics`):
the FL_e Frege axioms, double-negation elimination, bounded constants,
linear-exponential axioms/rules and unrestricted weakening, but no
unrestricted contraction. Proofs are DAG-like sequences. A formula is a
finite acyclic syntax DAG whose unfolding is an ordinary formula.
Shared formula nodes are **syntax references**, not a logical copying rule.
Let V be the number of nodes in a common syntax DAG for all proof lines
and annotated axiom/rule substitutions, and L the number of proof steps.
Wire IDs and proof references are charged O((V+L) log(V+L)) bits.

Candidate C1: there is an ordinary intuitionistic affine-linear sequent
proof of the unfolded Chu t-translation of the last line, with at most
O(V+L) sequent rows (and O(V+L) formula occurrences), when D=N=bottom.
Its explicit symbol length may be exponential. The proposed compiler
maintains a shared formula DAG and a legal proof DAG; it must never
interpret shared antecedent occurrences as one available linear resource.

Candidate C2: a fresh, acyclic definitional EF proof of an original-language
target can be flattened to such a circuit Frege proof in polynomial size.
An extension introduces (e -> F) AND (F -> e), with e absent from all earlier
lines and F, and absent from the final target. After simultaneous acyclic
definition expansion this becomes (F_exp -> F_exp) AND (F_exp -> F_exp),
provable with a constant number of ordinary Frege steps. All other axioms
remain substitution instances; modus ponens, adjunction and necessitation
remain their original rules. Uniform substitution of an already proved
formula is NOT being added. The common expanded syntax DAG has at most
the original ordinary syntax-node count plus O(L) nodes: extension atoms
are redirected to earlier definition roots, never recursively copied.

If C1 and C2 pass, the source's conservative hard formulas Theta*_n still
require exponential **total circuit/definitional-EF certificate size**:
Chu compilation plus its conservative-target repair gives an ordinary
intuitionistic proof with polynomially many lines, contradicting the
older intuitionistic line lower bound. This is not automatically a lower
bound on the number of affine Frege lines with arbitrarily large,
uncompressed formulas. It also does not resolve classical EF lower bounds.

## Minimal legal template and possible obstruction

For every source syntax node A, cache the exact sequent

    Pair(A): A^t, A^s => bottom.

For A=B -> C, source Definition11 (§4.1) gives

    A^t=(B^t -> C^t) AND (C^s -> B^s), A^s=B^t * C^s.

Starting from Pair(B) and identity C^s=>C^s, apply left implication to
derive B^t,C^s,(C^s -> B^s)=>bottom, then left AND (second projection),
then left fusion. This proves Pair(A). It uses four additional rows;
it never contracts B^t or C^s. Reusing the *same proved sequent* Pair(B)
in different later inferences is legal under the source's DAG-like proof
definition. Copying an antecedent within a single sequent remains illegal.
AND/OR use the two cached child pairs and legal additive branching;
fusion uses a child pair and identity; ! uses identity and left implication.
Constants and atoms are bounded-constant axiom cases when D=N=bottom.

The source's Section 7 translates each of its finitely many Frege axiom
schemes using constant templates plus cached Pair(A) or its two constant
cost negation-duality repairs. No template traverses unfolded syntax
except to build Pair(A). This assertion is now being checked rule by rule;
an unbounded distinct-substitution family or a rule requiring an unproved
context-duplication operation would refute C1.

A useful discriminator is F_0=p, F_(i+1)=F_i -> p. Its input tree has
2i+1 symbols, but unsimplified translated tree sizes satisfy

    T_(i+1)=T_i+S_i+5, S_(i+1)=T_i+2, T_0=S_0=1.

Thus output tree size grows at the golden-ratio rate. A shared translation
needs four constructor nodes per input implication, and Pair(F_i) uses
only constant new proof rows per i. An exact independent rule checker
is being implemented for this template; a merely small expression DAG
will not be treated as a checked proof.

## Prior and adoption boundary

The recent source explicitly notes translation expansion and proves the
number of translated lines against the *tree-symbol size* of the input
proof, not its shared syntax-node size. Its conclusion discusses affine
EF/line robustness as future work. Older Jeřábek 2009 and Jalali 2021
provide the relevant intuitionistic lower bounds; primary versions and
precise EF conventions are being checked, not inferred from that discussion.
Chu's categorical construction and generic sharing/extension equivalence
are older ideas. Historical novelty of the combined strengthening is
UNKNOWN. An independent worker is auditing C1/C2 and the precise lower-bound
transfer. Finite template checks alone cannot establish the universal claim.
