THE FALSIFIED ROUTE

Replacing the false KMS log-likelihood form by a genuine relative-entropy
DPI loss does not by itself close the power route. Even with fixed dimension
3, a faithful reference IN the family, uniformly bounded observational
divergence to that reference, and a supplied full-domain broadcaster of
vanishing worst HALF trace residual, the genuine entropy loss can stay
bounded below by a positive constant. Thus no bound of the form

  D(rho||sigma)-D(Phi(rho)||Phi(sigma))
       <=g(Dobs(rho||sigma),b), g(C,b)->0 for fixed C,   (R1)

is available in this scope. This is NOT a counterexample to the requested
e(E)^2 <=K(R(E)+1)b(E)^alpha. All these states commute and e(E)=b(E)=0.

EXPLICIT RATIONAL DATA AND FULL-DOMAIN CHANNEL

For integers n>=2, let w=1/n, q=2^(-2n), h=2^(-n), and supply

  rho_n=diag(1-w,w,0),
  sigma_n=diag(1-q-h,q,h),
  E_n={rho_n,sigma_n}.

Sigma is faithful. Put f(0)=0, f(1)=f(2)=1 and define on ALL of M_3

  B_n(X)=sum_(i=0,1,2) <i|X|i> |f(i),f(i)><f(i),f(i)|,
  Phi_n(X)=sum_i <i|X|i> |f(i)><f(i)|.

The supplied broadcaster is CPTP and its two marginals are Phi_n. The
channel is EB and no part of its input domain is omitted. Exactly,

  Phi_n(rho_n)=rho_n,
  tau_n=Phi_n(sigma_n)=diag(1-q-h,q+h,0),
  sup_(x in E_n) T(Phi_n(x),x)=h=2^(-n).               (R2)

OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS AND FIXED-REFERENCE COST

For any effect F, only its diagonal entries matter. Set a_i=<i|F|i> in
[0,1]. The scalar log-sum inequality gives

  p ln(p/Q) <=sum_(i=0,1) rho_i a_i ln(rho_i/sigma_i),
  p=sum_i rho_i a_i, Q=sum_i sigma_i a_i.

Including the nonnegative sigma_2 a_2 in Q can only decrease the left side.
For n>=2, q+h<=w, so the i=0 summand is nonpositive. The remaining term is
at most w ln(w/q). Therefore

  Dobs(rho_n||sigma_n)<=w ln(w/q)
                     =2ln2-(ln n)/n<=2ln2.            (R3)

Sigma itself has divergence zero. This is a bound to the particular
faithful reference sigma_n in conv(E_n), not merely an optimized-radius
claim. Independently the average center (rho_n+sigma_n)/2 lies in conv(E_n)
and dominates each state by factor 2, so

  R(E_n)<=ln2.                                        (R4)

TRUE ENTROPY DEFICIT STAYS POSITIVE

All matrices commute, so genuine quantum relative entropies reduce to
their scalar formulas. The i=0 term cancels between input and output.
Thus

  D(rho_n||sigma_n)-D(Phi_n(rho_n)||tau_n)
     =w ln((q+h)/q)=(1/n)ln(1+2^n)>=ln2.              (R5)

Equations (R2),(R3),(R5) falsify (R1), including any proposed positive
power estimate for this entropy deficit. The example is exact, with a
single common broadcaster, worst-case HALF error, and no average prior,
local measurement norm or hidden dimensional conversion.

WHAT HAPPENS TO THE PETZ-CORRECTED CANONICAL MAP

The actual Petz correction at sigma_n recovers the two merged classical
coordinates only in their sigma proportions q:h. Its corrected marginal
Theta_n=R_n Phi_n is the sigma-preserving conditional expectation

  Theta_n(X)=<0|X|0>|0><0|
       +( <1|X|1>+<2|X|2>) diag(0,q,h)/(q+h).

It is KMS positive/self-adjoint, compatible and already EB. Its actual
error on rho_n is

  T(Theta_n(rho_n),rho_n)=w h/(q+h)
                       =1/[n(1+2^(-n))].             (R6)

This decays only as 1/n, although the raw residual h is exponentially
small and (R3) is uniformly bounded. Thus the COARSE posterior canonical
map generated by the merged measurement is not a power reconstruction
for this supplied broadcaster/reference. Positive-depth measurement
records produced only from this corrected broadcaster cannot distinguish
input coordinates 1 and 2; their coarse effects have equal entries there.

This does NOT obstruct pure refinement or an arbitrary EB comparator.
Refining the coarse effect |1><1|+|2><2| into its two rank-one parts adds
a valid measurement of the original input and yields the full dephasing
channel, which fixes BOTH supplied states exactly. Consequently

  e(E_n)=b(E_n)=0.

The C4 inequality permits this stronger comparator; it does not force the
coarse posterior map. A proof of the main target must retain such extra
information, rather than upper-bound the unrefined Petz entropy/energy
loss and assume that bound controls the best classical reconstruction.
