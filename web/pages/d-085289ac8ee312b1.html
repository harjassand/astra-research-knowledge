ADDENDUM TO REV2 — COMPLETE ATTAINABILITY INTERVAL
c04_l09 | 2026-10-07 | internal mathematical correction

This addendum repairs the final attainability sentence of REV2. The sentence
there says that the canonical class's range [0,1/4] is contained in the
interval reached by the tetrahedral construction and then concludes that
every smaller target is attained. That containment alone is insufficient:
it does not cover targets between 1/4 and the stated cap.

The tetrahedral family itself covers this missing interval. Its midpoint
shrink is

  s(a)=(1+2 sqrt(1-a^2)+a)/6,   0<=a<=1.

On 0<=a<=1/sqrt(5), this function is continuous and

  s'(a)=(1-2a/sqrt(1-a^2))/6 >= 0,

with strict inequality before the right endpoint. Indeed, on this interval
both sides of sqrt(1-a^2)>=2a are nonnegative, and squaring reduces the
inequality to a^2<=1/5. The endpoint values are

  s(0)=1/2,
  s(1/sqrt(5))=(1+sqrt(5))/6.

Thus s(a)^2 continuously and strictly increasingly covers
[1/4,(3+sqrt(5))/18]. The canonical Pauli random-unitary construction in
REV1 covers [0,1/4]. Together, these constructions prove attainability for
every 0<=lambda<=(3+sqrt(5))/18; the universal upper bound in REV2 proves
the converse. The iff classification is therefore complete after this
correction.

the derivative identity and endpoint values symbolically. The interval claim
uses the analytic monotonicity and the intermediate value theorem above;
