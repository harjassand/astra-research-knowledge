1. Metric and Cartan-coordinate normalization

For each orthogonal Cartan block h_i=i[[0,1],[-1,0]], h_i is Hermitian,
Tr(h_i^2)=2, and (1/2)Tr(h_i^2)=1. The extra B_l zero coordinate has no
effect. For the symplectic Cartan h_i=diag(0,...,1,...,0;0,...,-1,...,0),
again Tr(h_i^2)=2 and norm squared one. Distinct h_i are orthogonal in
both cases. Thus the coordinates z_i are orthonormal coordinates for the
stated real metric; no hidden factor 2 belongs in the Cartan Gram.

For SO, X=iA with A real skew is Hermitian and transpose-skew. For Sp,
X^T J=-JX; induction gives (X^r)^T J=(-1)^r JX^r. Hence odd powers
remain in the indicated compact real Lie algebra in every type. The listed
roots are consistent with these coordinates: B has e_i and e_i±e_k; C has
2e_i and e_i±e_k; D has e_i±e_k. Therefore B/C regularity requires
z_i!=0 and z_i^2!=z_k^2, while D regularity requires only z_i^2!=z_k^2.
In particular D permits exactly one zero coordinate at a regular point.

2. Odd-power gradient and determinant orientation

With gradient defined by <grad f,Y>=(1/2)Tr(grad f Y), cyclicity gives
D[Tr(X^(2j))/(4j)](X)[Y]=(1/2)Tr(X^(2j-1)Y). Thus Z_j=X^(2j-1)
with no scale factor. On an SO block, X^2=z_i^2 I and the odd power has
Cartan coordinate +z_i^(2j-1), not an alternating-sign variant; the Sp
diagonal model gives the same coordinate. Factoring z_i from row i leaves
the ordinary Vandermonde in u_i=z_i^2, so with rows i and columns j in
the stated increasing order,

det[v_1,...,v_l]=(product_i z_i) product_(i<k)(z_k^2-z_i^2).

Exact Fraction fixture: B_l/C_l rank 3, z=(3,2,1) (regular dominant).
The odd-power frame has det M=-720 and det(M^T M)=518400, exactly equal
to the displayed product and its square. This also checks the sign for

3. Pfaffian orientation, derivative, and the D_l zero boundary

With the displayed ordered 2x2 blocks and the standard ordered-coordinate
Pfaffian, A=-iX has blocks [[0,z_i],[-z_i,0]], so Pf(A)=product_i z_i.
This is real and SO(2l)-invariant; under an orthogonal determinant-minus-one
change of basis it changes sign. A global reversal of the chosen ambient
orientation flips Pf and its gradient together, hence flips det M_D but
leaves the Gram and all conditioning unchanged.

Since (1/2)Tr gives unit norm to each h_i, the gradient of the Cartan
restriction product_i z_i has coordinates w_i=product_(k!=i)z_k, with no
extra factor. Invariance makes the full gradient torus-fixed, hence
Cartan-valued; its Cartan derivatives therefore give the complete gradient.

For nonzero z_i, write u_i=z_i^2 and P=product_i z_i. Factoring z_i from
each row of M_D leaves columns 1,u,...,u^(l-2),P/u. Multiplying each row
by u_i and moving the last column to the first gives sign (-1)^(l-1);
factoring P and cancelling product_i u_i=(product_i z_i)^2 yields

det M_D=(-1)^(l-1) product_(i<k)(u_k-u_i).

Both sides are polynomials in z, so equality on the dense set product z_i
!=0 proves the identity also when a coordinate is zero. Direct block
verification at z_l=0 makes the mechanism transparent: the first l-1 odd
columns vanish in the last row, w_i=0 for i<l, and
w_l=product_(i<l)z_i!=0. The top-left odd-power Vandermonde is invertible
exactly when the nonzero squares are distinct. Thus the Pfaffian's value
being zero does not remove its gradient frame direction.

4. Classical frame conditioning

For compact K contained in the open dominant chamber at fixed rank, every
root evaluation has a positive minimum in absolute value. Thus some finite
R and epsilon>0 simultaneously bound |z_i| above, all |z_i-z_k| and
|z_i+z_k| below by epsilon, and in B/C also |z_i| below by epsilon.
No coordinate lower bound is needed in D. For B/C, each factor
|z_k^2-z_i^2|=|z_k-z_i||z_k+z_i|>=epsilon^2; including l factors
|z_i| gives |det M|>=epsilon^(l^2), hence det H>=epsilon^(2l^2).
For D there are l(l-1)/2 such pair factors and no single-coordinate
factor, so |det M_D|>=epsilon^(l(l-1)) and det H>=epsilon^(2l(l-1)).

Because H=M^T M, Tr H is the sum of squared column norms. The B/C bound
l sum_(j=1)^l R^(4j-2) and D bound
l[sum_(j=1)^(l-1)R^(4j-2)+R^(2l-2)] follow entrywise; the latter bounds
w_i by R^(l-1). For positive l-by-l H, det H=lambda_min(H) times the
product of the other l-1 eigenvalues, and each is at most Tr H. Therefore
lambda_min(H)>=det H/(Tr H)^(l-1), including exponent zero at l=1.
All displayed powers of epsilon, R, and the trace-to-eigenvalue step are
consistent. The constants depend on the fixed rank and the distance of K
from the root hyperplanes; they are not uniform as K approaches a wall or
as rank grows.
