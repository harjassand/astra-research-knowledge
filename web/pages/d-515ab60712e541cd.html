CANONICAL REDUCTION FOR THE EXACT FULL-DOMAIN DIRICHLET INTERFACE
Independent reconstruction, 2026-10-09. Status: elementary proof supplied;
external correctness/priority not established. No universal constant claimed.

Let H_d be the real Hilbert space of Hermitian d-by-d matrices with
<A,B>_tau=Tr(AB)/d. Let Phi be a unital, trace-preserving channel, with
Phi HS-self-adjoint. Let Lambda be any CPTP entanglement-breaking map,
not initially assumed unital or HS-self-adjoint. Interpret its quadratic
form through S=(Lambda+Lambda*)/2.

Suppose <A,(I-S)A> <= C <A,(I-Phi)A> for every Hermitian A, with C finite.
Put D=C(I-Phi)-(I-S). D is a positive semidefinite real HS operator.
Trace preservation implies Lambda*(I)=I and Tr Lambda(I)=d. Thus
<I,D I>=0. Positivity implies D I=0: for any A the quadratic polynomial
<I+tA,D(I+tA)> has vanishing constant term, so its linear term must vanish.
Since Phi(I)=I, D I=(S-I)I, hence S(I)=I. But
S(I)=(Lambda(I)+Lambda*(I))/2=(Lambda(I)+I)/2.
Therefore Lambda(I)=I. Full-domain form domination forces unitality.

Choose a finite EB representation
Lambda(X)=sum_y Tr(E_y X) sigma_y,
E_y>=0, sum_y E_y=I, sigma_y>=0, Tr sigma_y=1.
Discard E_y=0. Write p_y=Tr E_y/d and rho_y=E_y/(d p_y). Then
sum_y p_y rho_y=I/d, and unitality implies sum_y p_y sigma_y=I/d.

Use the two reference ensembles, each with half its original weight:
  {(p_y/2,rho_y)} union {(p_y/2,sigma_y)}.
This is a single ensemble with barycenter I/d. It defines one POVM with
effects M_(y,0)=d p_y rho_y/2=E_y/2 and
M_(y,1)=d p_y sigma_y/2. The effects sum to I.
Its canonical EB map is
Psi(X)=(d/2) sum_y p_y[Tr(rho_y X)rho_y+Tr(sigma_y X)sigma_y].
It is CPTP, unital, HS-self-adjoint, and positive as a real HS operator.
For all Hermitian A,
  <A,(Psi-S)A>_tau
     = (1/2) sum_y p_y[Tr(rho_y A)-Tr(sigma_y A)]^2 >=0.
Thus I-Psi <= I-S <= C(I-Phi).

Consequences:
1. Existence of any CPTP EB comparator is equivalent to existence of a
   canonical comparator for this exact full-domain inequality, with the
   SAME C. If Lambda was already self-adjoint, S=Lambda.
2. Mixed ensemble states may be refined to pure states. For any mixed
   rho=sum_j q_j pi_j, scalar Jensen gives
   sum_j q_j Tr(pi_j A)^2 >= Tr(rho A)^2 simultaneously for every A.
   Refinement preserves the barycenter and further improves Psi's form.
3. Failure against the unrestricted canonical POVM cone DOES refute all
   EB comparators under this full-domain criterion. Finite seed hull
   failures still do not refute the unrestricted canonical cone.
4. The reduction does not apply automatically to a restricted test
   subspace omitting I and cross terms, nor to trace-error approximation
   that does not fix the reference exactly. Those interfaces remain
   distinct.
5. The proof is existential given an EB decomposition. It acquires no
   broadcaster, decomposition, finite design, or efficient compilation.
   From a supplied n-outcome EB representation it uses at most 2n mixed
   canonical effects; pure refinement can increase this to at most 2nd.
