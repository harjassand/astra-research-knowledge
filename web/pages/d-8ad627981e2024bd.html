# Independent semantic audit of the formalized affine lemma

## Verdict

Within the requested source-to-interface audit, I found no counterexample or missing quantifier. `N33Affine.exists_full_affine_family` has the same sequential content as the affine approximation lemma in the pinned local OA149 source for its stated range `m ≥ 1, a < b`; its formal interface is stronger in two harmless ways: it also covers `m = 0` and `a = b`, and it assumes only eventual weak activity. The proof contract retains all ties, pointwise-only positivity of an arbitrary tolerance, unrestricted approximating vectors, arbitrary (not necessarily monotone) scale sequences tending to zero from above, and the strict error bound.

The formal core at `work/agents/c4_rational_hostile/formalization/project/N33Affine/AffineCore.lean:11–46` uses:

- `InCube a b p := ∀ i, a ≤ p i ∧ p i ≤ b`;
- a fixed `Label` with slope `Fin m → ℝ` and total offset function `ℝ → ℝ`;
- affine value `∑ i, slope i * Real.rpow h (p i) + bump h`;
- `Active` as weak comparison `value j ≤ value j'` for every label `j'`;
- `LittleO bump a` as `bump h / h^a → 0` in the positive-side neighborhood filter at zero.

`GlobalAffineComposition.lean:14–22` defines `FullApproximation`. The exported theorem at lines 12 onward states, for every `m`, `a ≤ b`, and `E : (Fin m → ℝ) → ℝ` positive at every cube point, that there is a `BoxFamily` (a finite, nonempty indexed family with every slope in the cube) whose every bump is `LittleO` at exponent `a`, and for every fixed label, every `hSeq → 0+`, every `pSeq → p` with only `p` required to lie in the cube, eventual activity implies

```lean
∀ i, |p i - label.slope i| < E label.slope
```

This is exactly the coordinate form of the source's infinity-norm conclusion: in finite dimension, `‖p-r‖∞ < ε` iff every coordinate satisfies `|p_i-r_i| < ε`. The formal `Real.rpow` agrees with `h^p` on the eventually positive scale values required by `hSeq → 0+`. There is no monotonicity assumption on `hSeq` or `pSeq`.

The source says a fixed label is active at every index; formal `FullApproximation` needs it only eventually. Every formal sequence can be shifted to an active tail with the same limits, so this is a stronger conclusion, not a lost hypothesis. Although formal bump functions are total on `ℝ` while source offsets are only needed for sufficiently small positive `h`, behavior outside a punctured neighborhood is irrelevant to either the little-o premise or the active sequence tail.

## Adversarial checks and edge cases

- **Ties / weak versus exact activity.** Source activity means equality with the finite minimum. The formal definition `∀ j', value j ≤ value j'` is equivalent because the family is nonempty and finite. It includes every tied minimizer; it does not choose one minimizer or replace a weak comparison by a strict one. The conclusion is quantified per fixed label, so all tied active labels are covered.
- **Discontinuous tolerance.** `E` is a total function, but the only hypothesis is `0 < E r` when `r` lies in the cube. In the local bad set, `E (family j).slope` is a fixed real scalar. The closedness argument therefore never needs continuity of `E`, nor a positive lower bound over the whole cube. This matches OA149 lines 175–189 and its explanation at lines 307–308.
- **Outside-cube approximants and boundary limits.** Neither `FullApproximation` nor `ActiveLimit` imposes cube membership on `pSeq n`; only the limit `p` is in the cube. This preserves the induction's necessary face cases `p_i = β_k` and `p_i = b` (OA149: lines 119–132). A test strengthening to in-cube approximants would be a genuine weakening of the source interface and is not present.
- **Scale and exponent extremes.** `hSeq → 0+` implies eventually `0 < hSeq n < 1`, which is all real-power order comparisons need, even for negative exponents. The sequence need not be decreasing and may have arbitrary finite-prefix values. No exponent-sequence cube bound is assumed; only its limit is boxed. The formal varying-exponent helper supplies strict-gap comparison at limits without imposing such a bound.
- **No uniform tolerance floor.** A hypothetical discontinuous `E` with infimum zero on the cube does not obstruct the argument. Threshold selection uses finitely many already-constructed slopes at a time, while the closed bad set uses the fixed value at each of finitely many labels.
- **`m = 0`.** The original lemma assumes `m ≥ 1`; the formal theorem handles zero dimension directly before taking a minimum coordinate. The output family can be a singleton with the unique empty slope and zero bump. The coordinatewise conclusion is vacuous, matching the zero norm on the zero-dimensional vector space. No `AtMinSlice` construction is used in this branch.
- **`a = b`.** The source assumes `a < b`; the formal theorem allows equality. For positive dimension, the cube has the single slope/limit vector `(a,…,a)`. The local-window proof takes its `t=b` singleton-baseline branch; all coordinate errors are zero and `E` is positive at that point. In the global cover the interval subtype `Icc a a` also has a single point. No strict threshold `β < b` is needed in this branch.
- **Strict tolerance output.** The source and Lean both conclude a strict bound. The compact bad set is defined by the complementary weak violation `E(r) ≤ |p_i-r_i|`; it excludes the exact minimum slice and, being compact, stays a positive distance away in the minimum-coordinate parameter. Thus points at the chosen neighborhood radius are not needed; the neighborhood premise is strict, as in the source.

These attacks did not produce a counterexample. In particular, I did not find a way to use an active tie, a discontinuous `E`, a face approached from outside, a negative exponent, a nonmonotone scale sequence, or either degenerate extension to defeat the stated implication.

## What the theorem does and does not unlock

The affine lemma is a load-bearing gate for the **proposed proof route**. In the internal candidate, the tolerance is chosen from finitely many coordinate, source-difference, and reaction-vector comparisons (`endotactic_permanence.tex:399–414`). The source then uses the affine approximation to obtain a uniform-in-`p` active-slope bound and preserve signs with a finite margin `H` (`:414–450`). That sign margin is the input to the weighted essential-source drift estimate (`:461–508`). In this argument, without a finite family whose every active slope tracks the exponent vector at the arbitrary pointwise tolerance, the later finite minimum `H > 0` and monomial source-ratio separation do not follow. This is a gate for this proof, not a claim that no other proof of permanence is possible.

The formal statement is sequential rather than a named uniform-cutoff corollary. The fixed-family uniform activity statement at internal lines 369–385 follows from it by compactness: from any violating sequence, use finiteness to fix one label, compactness to converge the points, and pass the violation to the limit using the fixed scalar `E(r)`. That derived corollary is what the sign-margin application uses.

The formal module proves no part of the downstream reaction-network or ODE argument. Still outside this formal theorem are:

1. construction and validation of the application-specific tolerance and uniform comparison margin, followed by the weighted essential-source domination using endotacticity and the rate bounds (`endotactic_permanence.tex:399–508`);
2. the least-constant compact plateau, exclusion of neutral active slopes, and the finite-minimum chain rule along absolutely continuous trajectories (`:519–616`);
3. uniform increase, plateau invariance, global continuation, and the waiting-time/finite-entry estimate (`:618–676`);
4. iteration across nonnested scales to the common classwise absorber (`:678–737`);
5. common-span measurable switching/mixing, invariant sets for different-span mixtures, common-species transport, and equilibrium/periodic consequences (`:739–939`);
6. the Carathéodory existence, uniqueness, positivity and class-preservation steps used by the manuscript's ODE theorem (`:88–101`, `:649–664`).

The internal candidate itself distinguishes the inherited affine construction from its claimed weighted-drift extension (`endotactic_permanence.tex:127–140`). The Lean result therefore removes an important formalization gate for the candidate but does not establish the all-dimensional measurable-rate permanence theorem, its novelty, or any biological application. The source attribution is to OA149's weakly reversible mass-action manuscript; the internal all-endotactic variable-rate claim is a separate downstream deduction.
