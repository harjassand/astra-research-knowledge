## Exact kinetic interface

In dimension d >= 2 let epsilon be sphere diameter and
mu = epsilon^{-(d-1)}. Let f >= 0 be a supplied bounded integrable phase density,
normalized to integral one, and let r(x) = integral f(x,v) dv be bounded. Assume
a supplied sampler/envelope and support information if an effective sampling
claim is desired. For nonnegative activity a, define

    P_a(N,dZ_N) = Z_a^{-1} mu^N/N! 1(no distances <= epsilon)
                  product_i a(z_i) dZ_N.

The activity need not integrate to one. Its normalization is incorporated in
the fugacity; treating an activity correction as a normalized probability
density without also correcting fugacity would lose the desired density shift.
Let g_a be the normalized first factorial intensity, so E pi(phi)=integral g_a phi
for pi=mu^{-1} sum_i delta_{z_i}. Define

    K r(x) = mu integral_{|x-y| <= epsilon} r(y) dy,
    J[r](x) = mu^2/2 integral_{|x-y|,|x-z|,|y-z| <= epsilon} r(y)r(z) dy dz.

The packing parameter is delta = mu v_d epsilon^d ||r||_infinity
= v_d epsilon ||r||_infinity. These assertions are for fixed dimension and a
fixed supplied density bound. Growing dimension, unknown source density, and
adaptive acquisition costs are not waived.

## Independently reconstructed calibration lemma

For sufficiently small delta and the corresponding activity bounds,

    a_1(z) = f(z) exp(Kr(x))             => g_{a_1} = f[1 - J[r] + O(delta^3)],
    a_2(z) = f(z) exp(Kr(x) + J[r](x))   => g_{a_2} = f[1 + O(delta^3)].

The weaker conclusion g_{a_1}=f[1+O(delta^2)] also follows. The error is a
uniform multiplicative bound, interpreted as zero where f=0. In particular it
implies the corresponding L1 bound. No spatial derivative assumption is
needed when the actual ball and triangle convolutions are supplied or computed.

Proof. The hard-core Mayer factor is -chi_ij, chi_ij=1(|x_i-x_j|<=epsilon).
The initial-ensemble source proves the connected-graph identity and the Penrose
tree bound; the following finite-order argument reconstructs it directly. For
one fixed root, the n auxiliary-vertex term is bounded by

    (n+1)^(n-1) delta_a^n/n! <= e^(n+1) delta_a^n.

Consequently the tail n>=3 is bounded by
e^4 delta_a^3/(1-e delta_a). This supplies a universal third-order remainder
when e delta_a<1. The one-edge term is -Kr_a. The three-vertex connected
polynomial is

    chi_01 chi_02 + chi_01 chi_12 + chi_02 chi_12 - chi_01 chi_02 chi_12.

Integration, including the factor mu^2/2, gives

    g_a/a = 1 - q_a + q_a^2/2 + K(r_a q_a) - J[r_a] + O(delta_a^3),
    q_a = Kr_a,
    log(g_a/a) = -q_a + K(r_a q_a) - J[r_a] + O(delta_a^3).

For a_2, r_a = r[1+q+J+q^2/2+O(delta^3)], whence
q_a=q+K(rq)+O(delta^3), K(r_a q_a)=K(rq)+O(delta^3), and
J[r_a]=J[r]+O(delta^3). Adding log(a_2/f)=q+J cancels all displayed
terms. For a_1 the uncancelled term is -J. Bounds q<=delta,
J<=delta^2/2 and delta_a<=delta exp(delta+delta^2/2) justify every expansion.

In a uniform three-dimensional region away from boundaries, if z=Kr then
J=(15/64)z^2. Indeed the unit-ball triangle volume is

    integral_{|y|<=1} |B_1(0) intersect B_1(y)| dy
      = 4pi integral_0^1 s^2 [pi(4+s)(2-s)^2/12] ds
      = 5pi^2/6,

and its ratio to (4pi/3)^2 is 15/32. The corresponding local chemical-potential
correction is z+(15/64)z^2. This coefficient is a reconstructed virial
coefficient, not a new equation of state.

## Sharp centering threshold and consequence

The exact family-364 initial source has, in d=3,

    epsilon^{-1}(E pi_0(phi) - integral f phi)
      -> -(4pi/3) integral f(x,v) phi(x,v) r(x) dx dv.

Thus kinetic-centered fluctuation confidence bands are shifted even at time
zero. This is an exact decisive falsification of automatically replacing the
microscopic mean by the kinetic mean. It is already explicitly recorded in the
released source; it is not a new counterexample to the source.

After correcting intensity to error O(epsilon^{k+1}), centering at f is negligible
on the CLT scale iff k+1 > (d-1)/2. Without correction, the leading shift scales
as epsilon^{(3-d)/2}: it vanishes in d=2, stays finite in d=3 and diverges for
d>=4 on positive tests. First-order correction suffices in d=3,4; second-order
correction suffices in d=5,6. First-order correction is insufficient in d=5:
a smooth density with a positive uniform interior plateau and an observation
supported there has the strictly negative triangle term at order epsilon^2,
which survives after multiplication by sqrt(mu)=epsilon^-2. This also shows
why the second-order term is load bearing.

The initial CLT itself follows from the same absolutely convergent connected
expansion. For bounded test phi, ordinary cumulants of S_phi=sum_i phi(z_i) obey

    kappa_k(S_phi) = mu integral a phi^k + O_k(mu delta_a).

After exact centering and division by sqrt(mu), covariance tends to integral
f phi psi and all higher cumulants vanish. The calibrated mean estimate gives
the kinetic-centered CLT in the stated dimensions. This is weak convergence,
not TV convergence of lattice-valued counts to a continuous Gaussian.

For the conserved global charge list (mass, momentum, energy), corrected
initial centering persists at **every microscopic time** by exact hard-sphere
conservation. With compact initial velocity support, these are bounded initial
observations and the argument above is literal. No long-time PDE result is
needed for that statement. For local observables at t>0, initial calibration
alone does not remove the full microscopic/kinetic bias: a separate finite-size
bias equation remains to be proved.

## Effective acquisition rather than free initial-law oracle

The exact ensemble is a hard-core Gibbs measure for positions with activity
lambda(x)=mu r_a(x); velocities are independent conditional marks of a/r_a.
It is invariant under proposal births at lambda(x), rejection of births
overlapping accepted centers, and unit death rate for each accepted center.
Detailed balance proves this stationary law directly.

Use one stationary marked Poisson proposal process in space-time, with Exp(1)
lifetimes. A birth's parents are older overlapping proposals alive at that
birth time. Put

    A = integral lambda(x) dx,
    delta_a = sup_x integral_{B_epsilon(x)} lambda(y) dy < 1.

The expected number of parents per proposal is <=delta_a, because the lifetime
factor integrates as integral_0^infinity e^-s ds=1. Repeated Campbell integration
bounds the number of length-n ancestral paths from the time-zero roots by
A delta_a^n. Even though ancestors can be shared and parent sets are correlated,
the number of distinct ancestors is bounded by the number of paths. Hence

    E(total queried ancestors) <= A/(1-delta_a).

The clan is finite a.s. Replay it in birth order, rejecting an overlap exactly
when an older accepted parent is alive. This yields the stationary target law;
finite clans make it independent of the unknown remote past. This is the
Fernandez--Ferrari--Garcia mechanism, not a new sampling invention.

An implementation must generate a **single common Poisson environment**, reuse
already queried regions/cylinders, and sample new regions conditional on those
queries. Generating independent parent trees and combining them would produce
the wrong law. With a known compact support Omega and spatial envelope M, one
may instead use uniform proposal rate mu M on Omega and thin by r_a/M, with
A_bar=mu M|Omega| and delta_bar=mu M v_d epsilon^d. The bound is then
A_bar/(1-delta_bar). Conditional velocity sampling, local Poisson sampling,
data-structure work, density evaluations, and adaptive finite-bit comparisons
are charged separately. The result is a candidate-birth count, not a universal
bit-time bound or a claim that evolving hard-sphere trajectories is cheap.

This removes a real acquisition bottleneck. Global rejection of an iid Poisson
configuration has, for a fixed regular r,

    log P(no exclusions) = -(v_3/(2epsilon)) integral r(x)^2 dx + O(1),

so its expected number of full trials is exponential in 1/epsilon. The n=2
Mayer term gives this exponent and n>=3 contributes O(mu delta^2)=O(1).
The local ancestor construction costs O(mu) expected proposals for a fixed
bounded envelope and small epsilon, matching the order of the output size.
It does not turn a known kinetic density into an uncharged oracle.

## Fluid-computation primitive and falsification

The exact `geometry.tex` of family 376 proves the following directly. For
separated moving boxes K_i(t)=c_i(t)+D_i(t)K_i(0), positive diagonal D_i with
determinant one, and a supplied separation margin eta, let L_i=D_i' D_i^-1.
On a plateau where cutoff chi_i=1, the curl of

    chi_i [ (c_i' cross y)/2 + ((L_i y) cross y)/3 ],  y=x-c_i,

is c_i'+L_i y because tr L_i=0. Disjoint supports allow addition. The exact
time-one map holds on full closed boxes and a quantified neighborhood.
Parking all sources before delivering targets resolves cross-family overlap;
rational detours and cutoffs supply effective finite instructions. The field
U produces a Navier--Stokes solution by prescribing
f=U_t+(U dot grad)U-nu Delta U. This force must be acquired; the PDE does not
discover the computation.

The balanced recorder uses base B and prefix codes
e(word)=sum_j digit_j B^-j, with reciprocal scales and blank infinite tails.
Take the explicit machine that scans right, continues on blank, and halts at
a chosen nonblank letter. The all-blank initialized code never halts. Changing
only the m-th right-tape letter gives another legal code that halts, at distance
at most (B-1)B^-m. Every positive neighborhood therefore contains a false halt
relative to the intended blank input. Full-box robustness of one instruction
does not prevent this after an unbounded number of prefix pops. Reliability
through m inspected letters needs at least order m log2 B bits of initial
coordinate precision. Viscosity does not remove this requirement because the
externally prescribed residual force sustains the chosen exact velocity.

No hardware acceleration, fixed-noise universal computer, or decidable
all-time reachability oracle follows.
