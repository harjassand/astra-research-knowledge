1. Unrestricted-EB factor two on the full regular-SU(3) algebra
---------------------------------------------------------------

Let V=V_(1,1), dim V=8, and let P_27 be the projector onto the 27 in
Sym^2(V). The physical channel

  Gamma_27(X)=(4/27)P_27[(X tensor I)+(I tensor X)]P_27

is a symmetric CPTP broadcaster. Its marginal Phi_27 is bistochastic,
SU(3)-covariant, and self-adjoint on normalized-trace L2. On
End(V_8)=1+8_s+8_a+10+10bar+27, its exact multipliers are respectively
1, 14/25, 2/3, 28/75, 28/75, and 218/675.

For every full-domain EB channel E, the sharp full-space inequality

  I-E <= C(I-Phi_27) on L2(Tr/8)

requires C>=2, interpreting the order through the symmetric part when E is
not Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint. One common full-domain orbit EB channel
attains C=2 on all of L2: its multipliers on nontrivial sectors are
1/5, 1/3, 1/15 and 7/135, with the 8-copy matrix diag(1/5,1/3).

Proof route. Haar twirling any EB comparator preserves its full-space
quadratic-form inequality, keeps it EB and CPTP, and makes it
bistochastic by irreducibility. Taking its Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint and
averaging also preserves EB and the symmetric-part inequality. Every
covariant separable Choi state is a convex mixture of twirled pure product
seeds. For each pure seed psi, the anti-adjoint moment vector q_a obeys
||q_a(psi)||^2<=1/24; this is sharp at
(|1>+i|2>)/sqrt(2). Thus every covariant EB comparator has anti-copy
coefficient M_aa<=8/24=1/3. Since Phi_27 has coefficient 2/3 there,
testing I-E<=C(I-Phi_27) forces C>=2. The complex root seed has block
diag(1/5,1/3), which verifies attainability on both copies.

The unrestricted-EB lower-bound proof and independent audit are in
`regular_su3_unrestricted_eb.txt`; exact symbolic replays are
`../cycle4/multiplicity_blocks_check.py` and `su3_full_space_check.py`.
Both passed their exact projector, channel, sector, and seed assertions.
`fixed_domain_geometry` independently audited the unrestricted twirl,
Choi convention, EB symmetrization and sharp seed bound. The alternate
commutator proof appears in `../cycle4/multiplicity_blocks.txt`, §6.

The same comparator gives, for tracial likelihood scores H_x in the full
traceless operator space with
||H_x||_infinity<=theta and r=sup_x||Phi_27(H_x)-H_x||_(1,tau),

  sup_x T(Psi(rho_Hx),rho_Hx) <= (1/2)sqrt(2 theta r),
  rho_H=(I+H_x)/8.

The conclusion covers the full fixed eight-dimensional tracial algebra, but
only for this fixed channel. Its defects are nonzero, so this is not a
sequence with b_2->0 and e_EB bounded below. The continuous orbit POVM is
exact; no efficient finite cubature compiler or numerical precision budget
is claimed. Slansky Table 24 is used only as a representation-label

2. Exact failure of center-only and covariance-spectrum truncation
------------------------------------------------------------------

For k=2^s>=4, set m=2k and d=2^k. In M_d construct two uniform-prior
families of centered Hermitian scores, each with operator norm a in (0,1),
variance a^2, exact information center I/d, Holevo capacity

  c(a)=[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2,

and max-relative-entropy radius ln(1+a). Both score covariance operators
Q_p on traceless normalized-trace L2 have the same entire spectrum:
eigenvalue a^2/m with multiplicity m and zero on its orthogonal complement.

For the commuting family, the scores are H_x^D=a sum_i x_i Z_i, where
Z_i is supported on one of m equal basis blocks and has balanced +/-1
diagonal entries there. One common basis measurement captures all the
variance, so the common-POVM deficiency Delta_D=0; the family is exactly
broadcastable and EB-reconstructible.

For the Clifford family, choose m=2k pairwise anticommuting Hermitian Pauli
strings Gamma_i on C^(2^k) and set
H_x^C=(a/sqrt(m))sum_i x_i Gamma_i. For any pure vector psi,
sum_i <psi,Gamma_i psi>^2<=1, with equality attainable in a Gamma_1
eigenbasis. Thus the best one common pure-state measurement captures only
a^2/m of prior score variance and

  Delta_C=a^2(1-1/m).

This proves that the information center, its relative-entropy and D_max
radii, and even the full score-covariance spectrum cannot by themselves
bound Delta or justify fixed-rank spectral truncation. It is not a
compatible-channel counterexample: the established compatible-commutator
estimate forces the Clifford family residual r to obey

  r >= a(1-1/m)/(2+sqrt(2))^2,

while the commuting frame has an exact broadcaster. Thus the missing
ingredient is compatibility-sensitive and operator-algebraic. Full proof
and scope are in `common_measurement_gate.txt`. The exact d=16 replay in
`common_measurement_check.py` passes; it is a finite sanity check only and
does not verify the asymptotic proof or imported compatibility estimate. No
external priority claim is made.

3. Supplied-net operational reconstruction with full costs
----------------------------------------------------------

Let E be any state family and let R={r_1,...,r_N} be a supplied finite list
of rational density matrices with a certified trace-distance cover of
radius epsbar. Supply one full-domain CPTP broadcaster B, with arbitrary
correlated outputs, and compute

  beta_R=max_(i,j=1,2) T(B_j(r_i),r_i).

For a prior p on R let chi_p be its Holevo information and define

  Gamma(R)=max_p (sum_i sqrt(p_i))^2 chi_p.

If 2^s<=K<=2^(s+1), put phi(K)=s+2-2^(s+1)/K and phi(1)=0. Then

  e_EB(E) <= min{1, 2 epsbar + inf_(K>=1)
                         [phi(K) beta_R + sqrt(Gamma(R)/(2K))]}.

Also Gamma(R)<=N C(R)<=N ln N. The proof builds a balanced-depth K-leaf
tree using K-1 calls to B, symmetrizes its receivers, sequentially measures
history-conditioned Helstrom tests, and uses the information chain and
finite-dimensional minimax to obtain one common full-domain EB channel.
The decoder is compiled only from supplied rational net states and the
supplied physical broadcaster; its measured beta_R avoids assuming a
separate uniform error certificate on E. Trace contraction extends the net
bound to E.

This is operationally stronger than merely having an abstract small b_2:
it says exactly what finite cover, channel data, and numerical certificates
are needed and charges the channel/state storage, net verification, K-1
calls, output dimensions, adaptive Helstrom branches, minimax/LP grid and
precision slacks. The generic compiler is exponential in N and K. Bounded
Holevo capacity still does not control N: the balanced-sign commuting
family has constant capacity, exponential fixed-radius packing, exact
broadcasting and zero EB error. Hence the condition epsbar->0 and
beta_R ln N->0 is sufficient, not necessary; it does not settle the target.
The full derivation and cost ledger are in `acquired_complexity_transfer.txt`.
