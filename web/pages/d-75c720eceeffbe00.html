## Decisive result and scope

There is an exact obstruction criterion for allowing contraction on a fixed
source domain under the unchanged Chu translation into intuitionistic affine
linear logic. In addition to the expected diagonal, a negative-completion
endpoint is necessary. The second obligation is not repaired by granting
ordinary contraction to the target.

A useful sufficient subcase is substantially cheaper: a closed proof of the
positive translation of the domain compiles arbitrary codomain contraction
without any target contraction. It admits the ordinary atomic par domain
`(p->linear0)->q`, which is not uniformly contractible in unchanged source
affine logic. Thus this is a proper strengthening of the permitted source
proof profile, rather than a restatement of already admissible bang contraction.
The admitted axioms still preserve the actual conservative hard-family lower
bound when the guard is maintained under compression and definition expansion.

    c(A,B) = (A->(A->B))->(A->B).

There is no unrestricted uniform-substitution inference on previously proved

## 2. Sharp domain criterion, including necessity

Put `x=T_A,u=S_A,y=T_B,v=S_B`, and abbreviate

    H=(x->y)&(v->u),  K=x*v,
    J=(x->H)&(K->u).

Literal Chu expansion gives

    T_c = (J->H) & (K->(x*K)).

For fixed A, in the ordinary affine target, the following are equivalent:

1. `=>T_(c(A,B))` is derivable for every source formula B.
2. `=>T_(c(A,linear0))` is derivable.
3. Both `D_A: x=>x*x` and `U_A: (x->bottom)=>u` are derivable.

Sufficiency also uses the independently proved Pair endpoint `x,u=>bottom`.
Necessity does not require Pair. Thus this is an exact maximal proof-witnessed
domain class for arbitrary codomains under this unchanged translation. It is
not a complete efficient decision procedure for membership. The witnesses may
be difficult or large, and their acquisition and cost remain part of the input.

### Sufficiency: no free contraction

Select `x->H` in J and `x->y` in H. Two left-implication applications give
`J,x,x=>y`. Left fusion groups the two x occurrences into `x*x`; a cut against
D_A yields `J,x=>y`. Discharge x to obtain `J=>x->y`.

Separately use `K->u` in J on `K=x*v`, and Pair on a second x, to derive
`J,v,x,x=>bottom`. Group the two x occurrences and cut D_A as above, then
discharge x: `J,v=>x->bottom`. Cut U_A to obtain `J,v=>u`; discharge v. Right
additive conjunction uses the same J context to give `J=>H`, then discharge J.

For the other conjunct, identities and right fusion give
`x,x,v=>x*(x*v)`. Group the two x occurrences, cut D_A, group x,v into K and
discharge K. Combine the two closed conjuncts by right additive conjunction.

Every duplicated resource is supplied by the charged D_A cut. The template
has30rows/84occurrences including its three obligation input rows; maximum
antecedent multiplicity is4. No ordinary or bang contraction, or general
weakening, appears in this template.

### Necessity from one codomain

At B=linear0, set

    H0=(x->bottom)&(1->u), K0=x*1,
    J0=(x->H0)&(K0->u).

Project the second conjunct `K0->x*K0` of the supplied closed translation.
Explicit tensor-unit maps `x=>x*1` and `x*(x*1)=>x*x`, with cut, yield D_A.

Let `negx=x->bottom`. Ex falso derives `negx,x=>H0` and
`negx,x*1=>u`, using the legal left-unit rule for1. Discharge x and K0, then
right additive conjunction: `negx=>J0`. Apply the first supplied conjunct to
obtain `negx=>H0`; project `1->u` and apply closed `=>1`. This gives U_A.
The complete two-endpoint certificate has42rows/91occurrences including the
one supplied closed-theorem row, maximum antecedent3.

The necessity/sufficiency bases are schematic proof certificates. Arbitrary
compound substitutions preserve every exact sequent rule and its resource
multiplicity. The universal argument is this rule induction, not an
extrapolation from examples.

If the target instead has ordinary contraction (`LJ_!`), D_A is automatic.
The same proof therefore gives an exact iff with just U_A in that target.
Codomain-restricted profiles can avoid B=linear0 and need not satisfy this
domain-only criterion.

Two sound Łukasiewicz pair controls separate the obligations at the schematic
interface: `(x,u,y,v)=(0,0,0,1)` satisfies Pair and D but fails U and T_c;
`(1/2,1/2,0,1)` satisfies Pair and U but fails D and T_c. These are abstract
compatible-pair controls, not a claim that every such pair is realized by a
source formula. Actual source countermodels appear below.

## 3. Closed-positive domain: a9row affine proof

A supplied closed endpoint `=>x` alone suffices for arbitrary x,u,y,v. Select
`x->H` from J and apply the closed endpoint: `J=>H`. Discharge J. Independently,
right fusion of the same closed endpoint with identity `K=>K` gives
`K=>x*K`; discharge K and combine by right conjunction.

This costs9rows/14formula occurrences including the sole input row, maximum
antecedent1. It uses no Pair, weakening, contraction or bang rule. Reusing a
closed theorem at two proof nodes is legal proof sharing; no antecedent resource
has been duplicated for free. This is smaller than deriving D_A and U_A first.

### A proper source extension beyond bang

Let `A=(F->linear0)->G` and suppose legal cached endpoints
`S_F=>bottom` and `S_G=>bottom` are supplied. Write

    Z=T_(F->linear0)=(T_F->bottom)&(1->S_F),
    W=S_(F->linear0)=T_F*1.

Project `1->S_F` from Z and apply closed1, then the first cached null endpoint:
`Z=>bottom`. Ex falso yields `Z=>T_G`. The second null endpoint yields
`S_G=>W` by ex falso. Discharge and combine to obtain
`=>T_A=(Z->T_G)&(S_G->W)`, then use the9row template.

supplied null rows. For ordinary atoms p,q their S components are bottom and

## 4. Decisive barriers and controlled alternatives

Bang domains already meet the exact criterion: D_!A follows from native bang
contraction, and U_!A is identity because S_!A=(!T_A->bottom). This observation
alone would not be a new extension.

Even additive conjunction of two such domains is unsafe. Take A=!p & !q and
B=linear0. Use the Heyting algebra of upsets of the fork r<a,r<b, fusion=meet
and bang=identity. Put p={a},q={b}. Then T_A is empty and S_A={a,b}. D_A is
valid, but `(T_A->bottom)->S_A` omits r; T_(c(A,0))={a,b} likewise omits r.
This model permits ordinary target contraction. It therefore isolates the
negative-completion obstruction and refutes the proposed additive closure even
if target contraction is supplied.

The standard CWC weak contraction/divisibility schema from Arthan--Oliva is
`A*(A->B)->B*(B->A)`. Set A=p->linear0, B=q in the3element Heyting chain,
with p=q=middle, fusion=meet and bang=identity. The exact Chu pairs are

| formula | T | S |
|---|---:|---:|
| A |0|middle|
| B |middle|0|
| A->B |top|0|
| B->A |0|middle|
| A*(A->B) |0|middle|
| B*(B->A) |0|top|
| CWC |middle|0|

Thus the translated theorem is false at the root. The target model validates
CWC itself, because `a meet (a->b)=a meet b` in a Heyting algebra, as well as
ordinary structural contraction. Adding those target principles does not
repair the unchanged Chu transfer. No lower bound for Łukasiewicz proof systems
is deduced from this obstruction.

Finally t(c(p,linear0)) has Boolean value p. At p=false it is false, even in a
full classical target. A different translation would need a new hard-family
preservation or repair theorem; unrestricted target contraction alone cannot
be that repair.

All three sound-model refutations were independently calculated by
`semantic_barriers.py` using finite-poset upset implication. They are exact
counterexamples. Successful valuations are not used to assert validity.
