10. ADDITIONAL PROVED HIGH-FREQUENCY SPLITTING; STRONGER GATE

For any 0<rho,gamma<1 there is eta_0>0, depending on n,p,N,C,rho,gamma,
with the following local alternative (all balls have common interior
room, and all stated physical radii are <2). Suppose
  sup_{z in Z intersect B_{3r}(x)} P(z,3r)<=E.
For eta<=eta_0, either every z in Z intersect B_r(x) satisfies
P(z,gamma r)>=E-gamma, or
  {z in Z intersect B_r(x):P(z,3eta r)>=E-eta}
is contained in a rho r-neighborhood of an affine (n-2)-plane.     (10.1)
For E<=1/2+gamma the first alternative is immediate. If the high set
is empty the second is automatic.

Proof of the remaining case is by normalized compactness, not harmonic
representation. If (10.1) fails as eta down to zero, choose n high
centers which are quantitatively affine independent in n-1 directions.
Translate to the first, normalize a kernel at radius r/2, and dilate
by r. Uniform frequency bounds give local tuple compactness. The high
center frequencies lie between E-eta and E on EVERY fixed interval
between 3eta and 3; in the limit each is constant E. Its degree
n_0=2E>1. PSD equality therefore annihilates (-n_0,y-z_i) on each
ball B3(z_i); all contain a common ball around the first center.
Subtract those full matrix identities. The n-1 independent center
differences force f to be normal to their affine plane and beta to have
only the corresponding normal-normal entry d.

Let t be that normal coordinate. Grad nu=2f and the zero-center radial
identity t f_n=n_0 nu give the local profiles
  nu=c_+ t^{2n_0}dy (t>0), nu=c_- |t|^{2n_0}dy (t<0),
with no mass on t=0. Tangential constancy is distributional; the normal
ODE also rules out a plane atom. At least one coefficient is positive
by normalized positive height in the common ball. Div f=d gives
  d=n_0(2n_0-1)c_+/- |t|^{2n_0-2}dy.
The stress equation with rank-one beta forces mu-d to be a constant
Lebesgue density. Because n_0>1, d tends to zero at t=0. Domination
|mu|<=C d forces that constant to vanish. Thus mu=d, and 2mu<=d
forces d=0, contradicting positive height. This rules out the selected
simplex and proves (10.1). No same-frequency assumption beyond the
explicit high-set pinching to E is made.

This sharper alternative, together with (1.3), matches the usual
critical quantitative-stratification framework. Nick Edelen's primary
notes on the measure-theoretic Naber--Valtorta theorem were inspected
as a route guide. Their packing/rectifiability construction uses center
measures supported on the singular set, but their displayed beta
assumption is formally phrased for ANY measure. Our proved (1.3) is
ONLY for measures supported on Z. An explicit restriction of that
framework to its actually used center measures, plus a checked
stopping-cover implementation, is the remaining gate before asserting
ONE whole-Z critical Minkowski constant from that framework. The notes
also display a polynomial-looking Lambda bound while their final
iteration gives an exponential-in-Lambda bound; no polynomial frequency
dependence is imported here.

Accordingly, the main freeze claims the fully proved sigma-finite
critical measure, countable rectifiability, arbitrary center-measure
L2 estimate, and uniform packing of each small-tail piece. It does
not silently upgrade those conclusions to globally uniform critical
packing or locally finite measure for all Z. These stronger claims
remain a specific general-cover gate, not a counterexample or a
generic unexamined harmonic-map invocation.
