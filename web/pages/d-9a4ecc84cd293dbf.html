1. Definitions
---------------
Put lambda=kappa/2 and define, for real x,y,

 k_lambda(x)=x sinh(x)-lambda(cosh(x)-1),
 K_lambda(x,y)=[x sinh(y)+y sinh(x)
                  -4lambda sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)]/[2cosh((x-y)/2)].

Thus K_lambda(x,x)=k_lambda(x) and K_lambda(0,y)=0. Let

 A=cosh(alpha/2), B=sinh(alpha/2), C=cosh(alpha), S=sinh(alpha),
 H_beta=cosh(beta),
 m_beta=2beta tanh(beta), n_beta=1-sech(beta),
 d_kappa(x)=2x tanh(x)-kappa(1-sech(x))
           =2k_lambda(x)/cosh(x),
 P=S beta+(alpha C-lambda S)tanh(beta),
 Q=2B beta+[(alpha-2lambda B)/A]tanh(beta).

The authorized matrix is

 M_kappa=
 [[A^2 m_beta-kappa n_beta+B^2 d_kappa(alpha), -P,
                                            A m_beta-kappa n_beta],
  [-P, B^2 m_beta+A^2 d_kappa(alpha),         -Q],
  [A m_beta-kappa n_beta, -Q,                d_kappa(beta)]].

Set the three nodes and constant invertible matrix

 u=beta+alpha, v=beta-alpha, w=beta,
 T=[[1/2,-1/2,0],
    [1/2, 1/2,0],
    [0,   0,  1]].

The determinant of T is 1/2. Write G_lambda for the three-node Gram matrix
with entries K_lambda(node_i,node_j), in the order (u,v,w).

2. Exact congruence
-------------------
The identity is

     (cosh(beta)/2) M_kappa(alpha,beta)=T^t G_lambda T.       (C)

Every entry is verified below by elementary hyperbolic addition formulas.
No division by alpha,beta,lambda or d_kappa occurs.

Upper two-by-two block: the diagonal-node sum and difference are

 [k_lambda(u)+k_lambda(v)]/2
    =C beta sinh(beta)+alpha S cosh(beta)
                                  -lambda C cosh(beta)+lambda,
 [k_lambda(u)-k_lambda(v)]/2
    =S beta cosh(beta)+(alpha C-lambda S)sinh(beta)
    =cosh(beta) P.                                         (1)

The off-diagonal-node identity is

 K_lambda(u,v)
    =beta sinh(beta)-alpha tanh(alpha)cosh(beta)
                          -lambda cosh(beta)/C+lambda
    =k_lambda(beta)-cosh(beta)k_lambda(alpha)/C.            (2)

For example, its numerator before division by 2C is
 2beta C sinh(beta)-2alpha S cosh(beta)
                                    -2lambda[cosh(beta)-C].

For g=(cosh(beta)/2)M_kappa, use A^2+B^2=C, A^2-B^2=1 and
d_kappa(alpha)=2k_lambda(alpha)/C to get

 g_11+g_22=[k_lambda(u)+k_lambda(v)]/2,
 g_11-g_22=K_lambda(u,v),
 g_12=-[k_lambda(u)-k_lambda(v)]/4.                         (3)

Equivalently

 g_11=[k_lambda(u)+k_lambda(v)+2K_lambda(u,v)]/4,
 g_22=[k_lambda(u)+k_lambda(v)-2K_lambda(u,v)]/4.

These are precisely the entries of the upper block of T^t G_lambda T.

Third row: directly g_33=k_lambda(beta). Put

 R=A beta sinh(beta)-lambda[cosh(beta)-1],
 U=B beta cosh(beta)+(alpha-2lambda B)sinh(beta)/(2A)
   =cosh(beta)Q/2.

The two mixed Hardy entries are

 K_lambda(u,w)=R+U,
 K_lambda(v,w)=R-U.                                       (4)

For the first, expand sinh(beta+alpha) and
sinh((beta+alpha)/2). Its numerator divided by 2A is
 A beta sinh(beta)+B beta cosh(beta)
  +alpha sinh(beta)/(2A)-lambda[cosh(beta)-1]
  -lambda(B/A)sinh(beta), giving R+U. Replacing alpha by
-alpha yields R-U, since A is even and B is odd.

The displayed M has g_13=R and g_23=-U. Hence

 g_13=[K_lambda(u,w)+K_lambda(v,w)]/2,
 g_23=[-K_lambda(u,w)+K_lambda(v,w)]/2,
 g_33=K_lambda(w,w).                                     (5)

Equations (3) and (5) prove the complete matrix identity (C).

3. Full Hardy PSD premise and conclusion
----------------------------------------
The worker's own frozen Cycle06 Hardy proof, together with the root-origin

t=tanh(x/2),z=tanh(y/2),

 C_lambda(t,z)=[a(t)+a(z)-lambda b(t)b(z)]/(1-tz),
 a(t)=(1-t^2)atanh(t)/t, a(0)=1,

On Hardy space the full analytic kernel represents

 D_lambda=(pi/2)M_b^*M_b-lambda M_bM_b^*
          =(pi/2-lambda)M_b^*M_b
               +lambda(M_b^*M_b-M_bM_b^*)>=0.

The boundary identity is 2Re a=(pi/2)|b|^2. Multiplier hyponormality follows
from ||M_b^* f||=||P_+(conj(b)f)||<=||conj(b)f||=||M_b f||. The signed
real-node congruence preserves PSD even if one or more nodes are negative.
Zero nodes have an identically zero row/column. Thus G_lambda>=0 on
(beta+alpha,beta-alpha,beta) and (C) proves M_kappa>=0 for 0<=kappa<=pi.
