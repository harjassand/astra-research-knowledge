## 2. A precise reusable procedure: repair the law, then transfer surplus

**Question/interface.** Given a sampler for a probability law P on the binary
cube, exact rational expectations of a supplied list of nonconstant characters,
and a bounded certificate whose unconditional surplus is known, can we repair
those moments while preserving positivity and retaining enough surplus? Moment
acquisition is a separate charged input service. No teacher, target-label, exact
moment, or legal-support oracle is inferred from samples.

Let U be uniform on {0,1}^n. For a list F of distinct nonconstant binary
characters chi_S, set

    c_S = E_P chi_S,  H(x) = sum_{S in F} c_S chi_S(x),
    q = sum_{S in F} |c_S|.

Define the probability measure

    Q = (P + (q-H)U)/(1+q).                         (1)

If q=0, Q=P. Otherwise R=(1-H/q)U is a probability law: H has U-mean zero and
|H|<=q pointwise. Thus Q=(P+qR)/(1+q), a mixture of two probability laws.
Orthogonality gives E_U H chi_S=c_S, so

    E_Q chi_S = (c_S-c_S)/(1+q)=0,
    TV(P,Q) = [q/(1+q)] TV(P,R) <= q/(1+q).         (2)

Every selected moment is repaired at once, with no clipping. This reconstructs
the correction used in **007, Lemma `q:finite-law`**, lines following
`q:small-fourier-mass`. The manuscript uses a>=q and writes a density
(f-H+a)/(1+a), with a stated upper bound 3a/2 on TV. The mixture proof above
gives the sharper a/(1+a). This constant refinement is elementary and incremental.

**Primary prior art is decisive.** Alon–Goldreich–Mansour,
*Almost k-wise independence versus k-wise independence*, 31 July 2002 author
snapshot, later published in Information Processing Letters 88(3), 107–110
(2003), Theorem 2.1 and §3.1, already augment the original law by a uniform
opposite-parity distribution to kill each selected bias. At stage j the remaining
bias c_j is divided by 1+sum_{i<j}|c_i|. The normalization after repairing j is
therefore 1+sum_{i<=j}|c_i|. Flattening all these mixtures gives exactly (1).
The released correction is the same prior construction in Fourier notation.
Neither the repair nor its sampler is a new discovery of this worker or release.

**Sampler and complete costs.** One can sample (1) without enumerating the cube:
with probability 1/(1+q), invoke the supplied P-sampler; otherwise choose S with
probability |c_S|/q and sample a uniform string conditioned on
chi_S=-sign(c_S). Choose all but one bit of S uniformly and force that last bit.
This directly samples R, since

    R = sum_S (|c_S|/q) U(1-sign(c_S) chi_S).

The implemented rejection alternative accepts a uniform proposal x with
probability (q-H(x))/(2q). Its acceptance is exactly 1/2, so it uses expected two
uniform proposals when this mixture branch is chosen. The prior parity sampler
is a stronger same-information sampling control: it avoids that rejection loop.

If M characters and their exact rational coefficients have total input length B,
building q and a rational categorical sampler costs polynomial bit time in B,
and evaluating H costs O(M) character evaluations plus charged rational
arithmetic. Exact rational Bernoulli sampling uses ordinary fair bits and
integer comparisons. Its expected bit cost is polynomial in the rational
encoding. An explicit table of P or Q would have 2^n entries; that cost is not
included in the sampler-only interface. M=sum_{j<=t} binomial(n,j) for t-wise
independence; treating this as a constant-size interface would be wrong.

### 2.1 Transfer a signed unconditional certificate

Let J be a witness event, M a success event, and a in [0,1). Put

    Delta_P = P(J intersect M) - a P(J).

The payoff h=1_{J intersect M}-a 1_J has values -a, 0, 1-a. Its range has
length at most one. For any probability laws P,Q,

    |Delta_P-Delta_Q| <= TV(P,Q).                   (3)

Proof: h+a lies in [0,1], and the added constant cancels between the two
expectations. The standard event definition of TV, or integration of level sets,
then proves (3). Together, (1)–(3) give

    Delta_Q >= Delta_P-q/(1+q).

The source uses this method in **102, Lemma `lem:projected-witness`**:
transfer the probabilities of the chosen slice and its matches before
conditioning on the joint public data D. Its displayed triangle bound is
(1+alpha/4)TV; nesting improves it to TV by (3).

After transfer, write p_D=Q(J|D) and r_D=Q(M|J,D), setting the latter to zero
when p_D=0. If Delta_Q>=epsilon>0, then

    E_D[p_D(r_D-a)] >= epsilon.

The integrand is nonpositive unless p_D>0 and r_D>a, and it is everywhere at
most 1-a. Hence those useful public data have probability at least
epsilon/(1-a). This proves a positive-mass conclusion without asserting that
conditional distributions were close. A chosen witness must be a fixed function
of data present in the compared laws. Adaptive selection on unrecorded private
success information is an extra dependence, not covered by (3).

**Rare-conditioning obstruction.** For rho=1/1024, take
P=(1-rho,rho,0), Q=(1-rho,0,rho), J={second,third atom}. Then TV(P,Q)=rho,
but TV(P(.|J),Q(.|J))=1. In general, if P(J),Q(J)>0, overlap of the two
unnormalized restrictions proves conditional TV <=
min(1,TV(P,Q)/max(P(J),Q(J))). Thus a small unconditional error alone is
insufficient for a rare witness. Signed surplus pays its actual mass.

### 2.2 Exactly what the frozen experiment establishes

`positive_moment_transfer.py` checks eight fixed rational laws on cubes of
dimension three/four. Its strong control minimizes TV subject to exactly the
same moments and nonnegativity, using the same full input table. Numerical LP
output is only a proposal: rational primal/dual feasibility and matching objectives
are checked using Fraction independently of the solver.

Every repair is a probability law with all target moments exactly zero. The
minimum-TV control is strictly better in **five of eight** cases and ties in
three. Example: a three-bit point mass repaired for singleton characters costs
TV=3/4, while the exact optimum is 1/2. One four-bit irregular law costs
945/8296 versus the exact optimum 9/122. Subtraction followed by clipping and
normalization loses target moments in **two of eight** cases: defects 1/11 and
3/11. A single-character tilt gives equality with the optimum at TV=1/8.

The 324 exact nested-event checks verify the bound (3) on the frozen four-atom
fixture; this count is scoped evidence, not its proof. Sampling 4096 outputs is
a smoke test only; distributional exactness follows from the sampler derivation.
The first SymPy control proposal violated the original constraints and was
rejected. It is preserved, not counted as a valid optimum. SciPy 1.18.1 proposals
subsequently yielded exact independently checked certificates for all eight cases.
The first control time includes about seven seconds of dependency import; later
controls take roughly 1–2ms, repairs about 0.1–0.4ms. These tiny-input timings
are not an asymptotic or practical-discovery advantage claim.

### 2.3 Acquisition and legal-support obstructions

If c_S is replaced by an estimate chat_S, (1) has residual moment
(c_S-chat_S)/(1+qhat); it does not become exactly independent. With only iid
samples, exact expectation acquisition cannot be uniformly guaranteed in a
finite run: distinct rational Bernoulli laws give every common finite sample
sequence positive probability. The same output cannot be the exact expectation
of both laws.

Even approximate acquisition has a charged accuracy cost. Distinguish Bernoulli
parameters 1/2+delta and 1/2-delta. Their one-sample chi-squared divergence is
16delta^2/(1-4delta^2). For N samples, it is (1+kappa)^N-1. Cauchy–Schwarz
gives TV<=sqrt((1+kappa)^N-1)/2. An estimator that distinguishes the two laws
with success at least 3/4 requires TV>=1/2, and therefore
N>=log(2)/log(1+kappa)=Omega(delta^-2). This is an elementary information
bound under this explicit sampler interface, not a general oracle lower bound.

The correction also adds support from U. It can violate the legal configuration
set or an outcome-wise discrepancy bound. A point mass at the zero string cannot
retain its singleton support while all singleton characters become mean zero.
For a general legal set, a reference distribution supported on that set and a
bounded dual frame could replace U, but acquiring that reference and frame is a
new required service. If the desired support already consists of all
low-discrepancy exact-marginal outcomes, a reference law on that support supplies
the hard object sought; that would be circular in the imported rounding problem.

Correcting a normalized progression-free density need not preserve its support.
For k independent samples the product-law TV is at most
1-(1-v)^k<=kv, where v=TV(P,Q), by coordinatewise maximal coupling. Thus a
positive configuration signal under Q transfers only if it exceeds the actual
kv error. It does not fill the sparse holes identified in family159's narrative
at zero cost. The exact-marginal, teacher-free HMM, and selective-output tensor
routes are not reopened by this repair alone.

## 3. A stronger source strategy: choose the potential to cancel the hazard

The distinctive move in **102** is the latent gadget, not repeated prompting or
an undocumented search routine. Its quadratic block is

    Q(x1,x2,x3)=(x2*x3,x1*x3,x1*x2), C(x,y)=y+Q(x)

over F_{2^d}. For a field line A=Fv, let D_A={w:v.w=0} and
U_A={(a,Q(a)+w):a in A,w in D_A}. The polar expression
D(x,a)=Q(x+a)+Q(x)+Q(a) lies in D_A; Q restricted to A is binary additive.
Hence U_A is a binary-linear space of dimension 3d. On uniform aggregate input
and a uniform nonzero update in U_A, the change probability is

    (1-q_field^-2)/(1-q_field^-3)
       =1-1/(q_field^2+q_field+1)=1-theta.

These are universal algebraic calculations, independently reconstructed here;
the code exhausts F2 and F4 as adversarial local checks. It does not validate
unbounded-rank genericity or the full hardness reduction.

At a generic rank r, the source bounds loss by at most one, with conditional
loss probability <=3r*theta. The potential H_r=sum_{j=1}^r1/j makes one loss
cost 1/r, cancelling the r in that hazard. Thus the expected harmonic loss per
level is <=3theta. The source's random isomorphisms make the next character
space uniformly oriented conditional on its rank; genericity bounds every
possible extension map, including one depending on the earlier transcript.
This is the exact reason the adaptive choice does not defeat the potential.

**Reusable conditional theorem.** Let 0<=r_t<=R decrease along a public
filtration. On good states, it loses at most one rank and has conditional loss
probability <=a*r_t*theta. Suppose bad-state probability is at most epsilon,
and on bad states the conditional expected harmonic loss is <=btheta. Then

    E[H_R-H_{r_T}] <= Ttheta(a+b*epsilon),
    Pr[r_T>0] >= 1-Ttheta(a+b*epsilon)/H_R.          (4)

The first inequality follows by conditioning and summing; extinction costs H_R,
so Markov proves the second. If nonlinear failure is p0(1-theta)^T, choosing
T proportional to H_R/theta gives a constant survival certificate and nonlinear
failure polynomially small in R. A raw-rank potential with the same conditional
hazard only certifies survival >=1-aTtheta, a constant rather than logarithmic
horizon in units of theta^-1. This is a theorem under the stated hazards;
acquiring such hazards in a new problem is the actual new mathematical task.

**Equally informed numerical control.** The pure-death chain losing one rank
with probability r/256 has a fully explicit exact transition law. For R=4,8,16
and T=floor(128H_R), exact dynamic programming gives survival probabilities
approximately .8261, .9089, .9632. The harmonic lower bound remains above 1/2;
the raw-rank lower bound is zero in all three cases. The exact chain control is
stronger than the harmonic bound. The contribution is a scalable symbolic
certificate with no enumeration of all adaptive future transcripts, not better
prediction than an equally informed complete finite-chain computation.

**Prior/novelty/cost boundary.** Harmonic potentials are the familiar
coupon-collector/pure-death calculation. No novelty is asserted for (4). The
source-specific combination of equivariant quadratic blocks, adaptive orientation,
uniform extension control, quotient shifts, and later alphabet enlargement is
potentially substantive new research capital; its historical novelty and all
global gates remain unchecked here. Its effective construction is enormous:
the source requires H_R about 8log(1/p), hence roughly R at least p^-8, then
enumerates field subspaces, extension maps, every isomorphism child, and all
injections. Termination is not a useful polynomial-in-1/p runtime bound.
It remains polynomial in the later SAT input only because every such parameter
is fixed first. No efficient local-learning or low-memory capability follows.

## 4. Separate persistent size from temporary proof precision

**159**, setup Theorem `ap:increment`, and iteration Propositions
`ap:iteration:dimensions`, `ap:iteration:widths`, isolate a precise proposed
invariant: output width-log at layer i increases by a polynomial of strictly
higher-layer width logs, while the complete temporary computation record may
depend on its own current precision. The recurrence itself is valid:

    x_i(t+1) <= x_i(t)+P_i(p,x_{>i}(t)),
    B_D=x_D(0)+T P_D(p),
    B_i=x_i(0)+T P_i(p,B_{>i}).

For nondecreasing fixed-degree P_i and a fixed number D of layers, descending
induction bounds every x_i(t)<=B_i. If T and inputs are polynomial in p,
all B_i are polynomial in p. Same-layer feedback is materially different:
x(t+1)=x(t)+x(t)^2 from x(0)=2 has bit lengths 2,3,6,...,5540 by t=12.
Fixed three-layer checks pass the explicit polynomial envelope, but do not
verify the manuscript's sampling/lifting ingredients.

The validated research procedure is therefore a dependency gate: identify the
state retained across rounds; separately record discarded computation budgets;
prove, rather than declare, that the retained output does not depend on its own
late precision. A procedure that reads every old precision to choose the next
one fails this gate even if each individual call is polynomial.

This points toward the cycle18 rounded-purification bottleneck: controlled rounding
could replace inherited exact-denominator growth with an independently set
accuracy budget. The budget principle does not prove the rounding error lemma,
repair an insufficient optimizer gap, or bound rational hidden-frame height. The
root already identified those mathematical gates; the release supplies no audited
missing service here. The actual `ap:width-rule` still depends on unadopted
relative detection, scalar transfer, and backward-lifting assertions.
