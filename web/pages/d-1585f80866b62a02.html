q08 representation-cost audit — critical full-rank qubit orbit
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally proved candidate for a sector-truncation upper; not an optimality or novelty claim.

For rho_(r,v)=(I+r v·sigma)/2, 0<r<1 and c0<=r sqrt(n)<=c1, set p±=(1±r)/2. The spectral gap is p+−p−=r. Schur-Weyl gives sectors indexed by doubled spin L=2j in I_n={L:0<=L<=n, L≡n (mod 2)} (spacing 2), with dim V_L=L+1 and multiplicity m_(n,L)=C(n,(n−L)/2)−C(n,(n−L)/2−1). The sector law is

    P_(n,L)=m_(n,L)(p+p−)^((n−L)/2) sum_(a=0)^L p+^(L−a)p−^a,

and the normalized spin block is tau_(L,r,v)=Z_L(q)^−1 sum_(a=0)^L q^a |L,a;v><L,a;v|, q=(1−r)/(1+r). P_(n,L) is independent of v. At r=0 its continuous limit is m_(n,L)(L+1)2^(−n).

Keep every spin block L<=J and map the tail to a one-dimensional overflow branch. The memory is M_1 ⊕ ⊕_(L<=J) M_(L+1), and its encoded state is

    eta_J |overflow><overflow| ⊕ ⊕_(L<=J) P_(n,L) tau_(L,r,v),
    eta_J=sum_(L>J) P_(n,L).

The L label is carried by the direct-sum summand, so it is charged: D=1+sum_(L<=J)(L+1), not max_L(L+1). For J=pi+2s, pi=n mod 2, this equals 1+(s+1)(s+pi+1); explicitly it is 1+(s+1)^2 for even n and 1+(s+1)(s+2) for odd n. If J=n, omit the empty overflow branch. In all cases D<=1+(J+2)^2/4. The maximally mixed multiplicity I/m_(n,L) can be discarded and recreated conditioned on L; no multiplicity register is retained. This uses the iid density-only promise and does not preserve a purification or arbitrary multiplicity correlations.

The decoder inserts I/m_(n,L) in each retained sector, applies inverse Schur transform, and maps overflow to any fixed n-qubit state omega. Thus the exact whole-output difference is eta_J(sigma_tail,v−omega), so

    (1/2)||rho_(r,v)^(⊗n)−rhohat_(r,v)||_1 <= eta_J,

uniformly for every v. No factor n or square root of the discarded mass appears.

An independent tail derivation gives P_(n,L)(r)<=C_(c1)(L+1)^2 n^(−3/2) exp(−(L−nr)^2/(2n)); it follows from P_L(r)/P_L(0)<=e^(rL), the binomial-difference multiplicity, and a local binomial bound. Summing on the parity-2 lattice yields eta_J<=C0(c1)(1+t)e^(−t²/2), t=(J−nr)/sqrt(n). Choose A=A(c1) large and J=min{n, ceil[nr+2 sqrt(n ln(A/epsilon))]}, where the ceiling is the least L congruent to n modulo 2 above its argument. If the threshold reaches n, the code is exact; otherwise parity rounding adds at most 2, t>=2sqrt(ln(A/epsilon)), and eta_J<=epsilon for 0<epsilon<=1/4. Hence D=O_(c1)(min{n²,n[1+ln(1/epsilon)]}) in this cutoff scheme. If r is unknown but c1 is supplied, center at c1 sqrt(n) instead.

This cutoff is valid but wasteful: it retains entire V_L blocks and gives O(n log(1/epsilon)) in the unsaturated range, so it does not establish N08’s conditional D=Theta(epsilon^(−3/2)) critical-precision candidate. The missing sharper mechanism is a finite joint sector/orientation codec with an end-to-end whole-state trace bound. N08/N07 are only comparison/interface guardrails here; N39 is a lower obstruction, not an upper encoder.

Costs and prior art: Schur transform, sector dephasing, multiplicity trace/preparation, inverse transform, output-state generation, and finite input/threshold precision are operational costs outside persistent D. No compiled runtime or gate count is claimed. Yang–Chiribella–Hayashi, arXiv:1606.02893v3, Eqs. (1)–(8), establish the Schur representation, multiplicity discard/repreparation, and a universal-cloning compressor that maps variable spin sectors to a fixed spin without retaining L. That is an alternate established mechanism, but its displayed concentration/error proof is not uniform for the moving critical sequence r=Theta(n^(−1/2)) without a separate audit. Plesch–Bužek, arXiv:0907.1764v2, give exact pure symmetric-subspace compression, not this full-rank orbit.
