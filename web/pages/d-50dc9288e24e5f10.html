FINDING

I found no substantive gap in the stated theorem or in the handling of
rectangular source/output Schur blocks. The proof does not identify the
rank-(k+1) sandwich spectrum with a scalar Berezin/frame spectrum. Instead,
it proves the needed rank-Q bound from a chain-of-projectors domination and
uses the scalar CP2 kernel only after that domination. This distinction is
valid. The final lower bound is scoped to the positive-critical qutrit iid
orbit and its stated epsilon window; it does not establish a product lower
below the critical scale or optimal total memory.

1. FIBER OVERLAP AND REDUCIBLE SANDWICH

For the harmonic realization

  V_(k,m)=ker(sum_i partial_xi partial_yi)
       subset Sym^k(C^3) tensor Sym^m((C^3)^*),

the map W_v:x -> x tensor bar(v)^tensor m is isometric on Sym^k(v^perp),
and its range is harmonic because every contraction contains
<x,v>=0. The operator between two fiber spaces factors as the normal-vector
overlap to power m times Sym^k of a plane projection. That projection is a
contraction, so

  ||W_v^dag W_w|| <= |<v,w>|^m,
  ||P_v P_w|| <= |<v,w>|^m.

The rank r=k+1 is retained; the shared one-dimensional intersection of the
two planes does not invalidate the bound because the independent normal
factor supplies |<v,w>|^m. Irreducibility and trace give
int P_v dv=(r/d)I=I/a, with a=d/r=(m+1)(m+k+2)/2.

For the reducible direct sum, F_v=direct_sum_lambda sqrt(a_lambda)P_(lambda,v)
satisfies int F_v^2=I. Thus S(B)=int F_v B F_v is trace-preserving and unital;
it is HS self-adjoint and positive since
<B,F_v B F_v>=||F_v^(1/2) B F_v^(1/2)||_2^2. Its spectrum is in [0,1].
On a lambda-by-mu rectangle its exact action is

  S_(lambda,mu)(B)=sqrt(a_lambda a_mu) int P_(lambda,v)B P_(mu,v)dv.

No irreducible-to-irreducible matrix block is omitted.

For unit x,y in the direct sum, the t-fold Kraus product obeys

 |<x,F_(v1)...F_(vt)y>|^2
   <=a_max^(t-2) f_x(v1)f_y(vt)
       product_i |<v_i,v_(i+1)>|^(2m_*),

where f_x(v)=||F_vx||^2 and int f_x=1. To check the absence of a sector
factor, first apply the fiber-overlap bound in each lambda; then use
a_lambda^(t/2)<=a_lambda a_max^((t-2)/2), followed by Cauchy--Schwarz in
lambda with weights a_lambda. This produces exactly f_x and f_y, not a sum
over the number of carriers. Cross-sector terms in x,y are handled by the
same inequality.

2. SCALAR WALK BOUND AND ITS LEGAL ROLE

For normalized Haar measure on CP2, h_m(v,w)=|<v,w>|^(2m) has integral
1/d_m, d_m=binom(m+2,2). The normalized scalar kernel K_m=d_m h_m has
multiplicity-free harmonics V_(l,l), l=0,...,m, with dimensions (l+1)^3
and eigenvalues

  beta_l=m!(m+2)!/[(m-l)!(m+l+2)!].

The coherent-symbol calculation in the candidate gives these eigenvalues:
for X_l=(a_1^dag)^l a_3^l, the symbol is
[m!/(m-l)!](bar(v_1)v_3)^l, and the normalized Dirichlet moment divided
by ||X_l||_2^2=(l!)^2 binom(m+l+2,2l+2), then multiplied by d_m, gives
beta_l. Equivariance makes the eigenvalue constant on V_(l,l). The standard
decomposition End(Sym^m C^3)=direct_sum_(l=0)^m V_(l,l) is the needed
multiplicity-free representation fact; the dimension sum alone would not
prove that decomposition, so it should remain explicitly named as a
representation input. Since beta_l>=0 and normalized zonal coefficients
have absolute value at most their diagonal value,

  ||K_m^s||_infinity=sum_(l=0)^m (l+1)^3 beta_l^s.

The product formula implies beta_l<=exp[-l(l+2)/(m+l+2)]. For
1<=s<=m+1, set u=s/[2(m+1)]<=1/2 and j=l+1. Then

  sum (l+1)^3 beta_l^s
   <= e^u int_0^infinity (x+1)^3 e^(-u x^2) dx
   <= 16/u^2 <=64(m+1)^2/s^2,

and division by d_m gives ||K_m^s||_infinity/d_m<=128/s^2. The integral
bound is conservative and valid for u<=1/2.

Combining the endpoint-weighted chain inequality with convolution yields

  ||S^t||_(1->infinity)
   <=(a_max/d_(m_*))^(t-2)||K_(m_*)^(t-1)||_infinity/d_(m_*).

For positive trace-one inputs, the rank-one matrix element is an integral
of the chain kernel against two probability densities f_x,f_y, hence bounded
by the kernel supremum. For a complex rank-one input |u><v|, Kraus
Cauchy--Schwarz bounds each output matrix element by the geometric mean of
the corresponding two positive-input bounds. SVD and the triangle inequality
then give the same 1->infinity bound for arbitrary complex inputs; positivity
of a minimizer is not assumed.

Since 0<=S<=I on HS space, for unit-HS B,
<B,S^t B> >=1-t E_S(B), E_S(B)=1-<B,S B>. If rank(B)<=Q, then
||B||_1^2<=Q and the preceding norm bound gives <B,S^tB><=Q||S^t||_(1->infinity).
Taking t=ceil(32 sqrt(Q))+1 makes this product below 1/2 whenever
t-1<=m_*+1 and log(a_max/d_(m_*))(t-2)<=1. Thus

  Lambda_S(Q)>=1/(128 sqrt(Q))

in the explicitly stated window. The l=0 contribution is retained in the
finite norm sum; the ratio a_max/d_(m_*) is not silently set to one. This
checks the key arbitrary-rank and arbitrary-rectangle step.

3. IID REDUCTION, WINDOW, AND ERROR ACCOUNTING

The Schur--Weyl input is exactly direct_sum_lambda p_lambda R_(lambda,U)
tensor I_(M_lambda)/f_lambda. Preparing the known maximally mixed
multiplicity at the encoder and tracing it after decoding maps any full
archive to a carrier-only archive with the same maximum branch Q and no
larger trace error. Conversely, the multiplicity state can be recreated.
This is a parameter-independent CPTP simulation, not free information about
U. The full carrier reduction is valid for a lower bound.

The tail estimate uses
p_lambda=f_lambda s_lambda(p)<=[(n+1)^5]exp[-nD(lambda/n||p_n)]
after summing over at most (n+1)^2 partitions. Pinsker gives
D>=2 max_i|lambda_i/n-p_(n,i)|^2. Hence the chosen h_n has discarded mass
at most delta exactly as claimed. Conditioning changes the target by at
most delta in half trace; applying the original channel changes its output
by at most delta more. The CPTP projection/replacement onto H_J fixes the
conditioned target and cannot increase error. Therefore the conditioned
archive error is at most epsilon+2delta, even though the original decoder
may move between sectors. This is the correct use of typical conditioning;
no inference from per-sector errors is made.

For the conditioned right-U2-mixture, the Haar mean is
sigma=direct_sum_(J)(p_lambda/p_J)I/d_lambda and the whitened likelihood is
L_U=direct_sum_(J)d_lambda barR_(lambda,U). Its envelope M=t_max is finite.
The exact physical-number cutoff identity gives
w_(0,lambda)=w_*/Tr(P_m Omega_product),
with w_*=(1-q1)(1-q2) and
1<=1/Tr(P_m Omega_product)<=1/(1-eta),
eta=(m_*+2)qbar^(m_*+1). This follows from the two geometric thermal tails;
the independent transverse-mode cutoff is paid, not replaced by an
untruncated oscillator.

On vacuum P_(lambda,U), L has eigenvalue t_lambda=a_lambda w_(0,lambda);
on its complement, the exact U2-branching character ratio is at most
q2 t_lambda. For every rectangular B_(lambda,mu), matrix entries crossing
vacuum/complement therefore have gap at least Delta=t_min-qbar t_max,
which proves the commutator inequality (7.5). The vacuum-vacuum terms
cancel from [P,B] and all additional likelihood terms are nonnegative.
The identity

 int||P_lambda B-BP_mu||^2
  =2/sqrt(a_lambda a_mu) E_(S,lambda,mu)(B)
    +(1/sqrt(a_lambda)-1/sqrt(a_mu))^2||B||_2^2

then proves (7.6) with 2/a_max. This is the main all-sector transport
check; it is an exact rectangle calculation, not a source-sector argument.

The reference-weighted self-score identity applies on H_J to arbitrary TP
Kraus channels. For B_i=A_i sqrt(sigma), ranks stay <=Q and
sum_i||B_i||_2^2=1. With C=int L_U^2, operator Jensen gives I<=C<=MI; the
two effects L_U/M and (C-I)/(M-1), together with convexity of mean-output
trace distance, give e_J>=D/(3M-1). Combining with the rectangular gap and
(7.6) yields

  e_J >= [2 Delta^2/(a_max(3M-1))] Lambda_S(Q).

Under the displayed typical-window conditions, the coefficient is bounded
below by a positive constant depending only on the fixed spectral gap
1-qbar. Thus the theorem's Q^-1/2 term and Q-window saturation follow.

4. STATUS, LIMITS, AND SMALL EDITS

No fatal algebraic, representation-normalization, sector-conditioning, or
rectangular-transport defect found in Sections 2--8. The lower is an
INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE. It establishes the stated
Q>=c min{epsilon^-2,n/log((n+1)/epsilon)} for positive-critical qutrit iid
orbits in the stated log/n range, allowing unlimited labels and arbitrary
output sectors. It does not prove the proposed product lower below the
critical scale, a general collision law, an optimal total-memory law, or an
efficient implementation. Matching Q upper for epsilon=n^-alpha,
0<alpha<1/2, is a separate JOINT_CAPTURE claim and should not be merged into
this lower audit without its separate validation. External correctness and
priority remain UNKNOWN.

Minor clarity edits recommended in the candidate:
1. State End(Sym^m C^3)=direct_sum_(l=0)^m V_(l,l) with multiplicity one
before using the coherent eigenvalue computation; dimension-sum alone is
not a decomposition proof.
2. In the CP 1->infinity extension, display the Kraus Cauchy--Schwarz step
for |u><v|; the claimed step is valid and avoids any hidden positivity
assumption on B.
3. Make explicit in the Schur reduction that a full-state code induces a
carrier code by preparing I_(M_lambda)/f_lambda based on the orthogonal
Schur label, then running the encoder and tracing multiplicity after
decoding. This is a legal simulation with unchanged Q.

These are proof-presentation clarifications, not counterexamples. No finite
checker can establish the asymptotic kernel bound or the analytic theorem.
