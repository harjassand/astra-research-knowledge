## 1. A genuine all-time Davies approximation must be preserved

Marco Merkli, Quantum Markovian master equations: resonance theory shows validity for all time scales, arXiv1908.01984v2, revised 16 December 2021, identifies an Annals of Physics2020 published version (DOI10.1016/j.aop.2019.167996). The title here follows the literal retained arXiv metadata and TeX header; no different Merkli paper is being imported. Own primary substantive reading: intro, assumptions(A)/(H), Result1/2 main source392–480, and full Result2 proof1030–1090; not the entire resonance-analytic proof of Result1 or unread Result3.

For a fixed finite-dimensional system coupled linearly to a free boson thermal field, thermal form factors must admit a translation-analytic strip, with width theta0<2pi/beta, and coupling lambda small enough (in particular lambda²<=c theta0). With the Fermi golden rule minimum second-order decay gamma_FGR>0 and the source's spectral perturbation hypotheses, Result2/Eq24,13.01 gives

    sup_(t>=0)||V_t-exp[t(L_H+lambda²K_Davies)]|| <=C lambda².

It is the standard Davies weak-coupling semigroup, not merely a modified Gibbs sampler. This is a real all-time result and rebuts any blanket claim that microscopic-to-Davies accuracy is only known on compact times. The constant C and threshold lambda0 are independent of t and lambda, but not supplied uniformly over system dimension, Hamiltonian degeneracies, resonance separation, temperature or bath form factor.

The source proof explicitly controls the resonance-truncation term

    O(lambda^4 t exp[-lambda²t(gamma_FGR+O(lambda²))]).

If lambda² corrections are <=gamma_FGR/2, its supremum is at most a constant times lambda²/gamma_FGR. Resonance contour and matrix-projection bounds introduce further spectrum/dimension dependence. Thus a code family with tiny decay or nearby Bohr resonances cannot inherit a fixed lambda0,C by holding the finite-N theorem statement unchanged. For each acquired N the theorem may still apply at an acquired smaller coupling. This is an acquisition debt, not a proof that physical memory necessarily fails.

An arbitrary-reference or diamond upgrade is also not free: the source works with its finite-dimensional map norm. An equivalent reference norm may have a dimension-dependent equivalence constant. Retain that cost before using a channel approximation for encoded coherence.
