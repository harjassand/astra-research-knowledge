## 1. Schur complement of the actual quartic Hessian

Work in the normalized negative real star. Let r=y_0^2 be the center mass and m_i=y_i^2 the leaf masses. Direct quartic differentiation gives

    H_00=12r-4sum_i t_i m_i,
    H_ii=d_i=12m_i-4t_i r,
    H_0i=-8t_i sqrt(r m_i), H_ij=0 for distinct leaves.

At positive masses, H>0 precisely when d_i>0 and its center Schur complement is positive. Use the elementary identity m_i=(d_i+4t_i r)/12 to rewrite that complement as

    v=H_00-sum_i 64t_i^2 r m_i/d_i
     =(12-16q/3)r-4sum_i t_i m_i
       -(64/3)sum_i t_i^3 r^2/d_i.                    (3)

Thus

    h=(product_i d_i)v.                                (4)

There is no rank-one-coupling assumption in this computation; only the absence of leaf-leaf edges is used.

## 3. Determinant-root concavity and the exact maximum
