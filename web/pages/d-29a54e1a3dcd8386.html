N113 — Exact local two-qubit log-concavity cone

N113 | Exact local two-qubit log-concavity cone
status=source_derived_unreviewed;COMPLETE supplied Z evidence package;branch-specific internal proof/audit status below,external correctness/priority unresolved;checks SOURCE-REPORTED unless marked otherwise.
Notation local.

For real-symmetric 4x4 generators indexed 00,01,10,11 with nonnegative off-diagonals, the specified input-complemented polynomial symbol is infinitesimally log-concave iff pair creation A00,11=0, neighbor-independent single-flip rates g0=g1 and h0=h1, and |A00-A01-A10+A11|<=2*A01,10. Admitted products/exponentials preserve real stability. This classifies one local representation only; it is not a hardness/all-algorithm theorem and excludes no ancillas, nonlocal encodings, signed gadgets or other methods. Uses the sufficient algebraic-symbol preserver theorem; priority/external audit unresolved.
