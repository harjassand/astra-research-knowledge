# The critical O(r^(-1/2)) amplitude is already excluded

7 October 2026. **Author theorem plus our endpoint reconstruction.** Cruz-Sampedro1999 Theorem1 pp143–144 explicitly permits ε=1/2, giving δ=0 in 3δ=max(0,1−2ε). A solution of −Δu+Vu=Eu with real E, |V|=O(r^(−1/2)), and exp(τr)u∈L² for every τ>0 has compact support. Complex V is allowed. For our smooth bounded real class, local UCP then excludes nonzero u. Do not call this endpoint open.

Rendered primary PDF Lemma2.1 explicitly assumes δ≥0 and permits −3δ<κ≤2−δ. The extracted text misread ≥ as >; r07 caught this by rendering p144, which I independently viewed. Thus the endpoint is directly within the printed lemma. Its displayed integration-by-parts identity can also be reconstructed at the endpoint, as root first observed and I independently substituted. Let α=1+δ=1 and choose the lemma's auxiliary κ=1. (This κ is not the construction's potential envelope.) Let U=exp(τr)v, with smooth v compactly supported in r>ρ. In the identity, radial derivative terms have coefficient

    4τ²r+2τ(3−2n),

which is nonnegative for τ≥(2n−3)/(2ρ). The angular contribution is −2τr^(−2)UΔ_(S^(n−1))U, hence its integral is nonnegative. The troublesome second zeroth-order coefficient vanishes because 2α−3+κ=0. The remaining zeroth-order coefficient is 2τ³−2τE, positive and at least τ³ once τ²≥2|E|. Thus, with a fixed harmless constant,

    ∫ r^(1−n) exp(2τr)|v|² dx
       ≤Cτ^(−3) ∫ r^(2−n) exp(2τr)|(Δ+E)v|² dx.       (1)

The weights are checked by polar coordinates: dx=r^(n−1)drdω, so the left side is ∫|U|²drdω and the right side is ∫r|(A_τ+E)U|²drdω. No δ→0 limit is taken.

If |V|≤Mr^(−1/2) outside ρ, then the potential contribution on the right of (1) is at most CM²τ^(−3) times the left. Absorb it for sufficiently large τ. For a solution, use an inner cutoff h equal to one outside ρ+1 and zero inside ρ+1/2, and an outer cutoff tending to one. The assumed all-exponential L² decay, plus local H² estimates, lets the outer cutoff errors tend to zero for every fixed τ. The resulting right side is supported in the fixed inner annulus. Let τ→∞: the weight strictly exceeds the annular weight beyond ρ+1, forcing u to vanish there. This reconstructs compact support at ε=1/2, E real, without requiring smoothness of V in the Carleman step.

Our arbitrary-loss proposal therefore has a strict boundary: any prescribed unbounded loss above r^(−1/2) may be attainable by the real repair mechanism, but a bounded loss is ruled out by an established theorem and the explicit estimate (1). Neither this endpoint nor the proposed above-endpoint examples determines the number of negative eigenvalues.
