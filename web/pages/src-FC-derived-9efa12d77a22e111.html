H=D+T and inverse temperature beta>0. Let

    rho=e^(-beta H)/Z_H,
    sigma=sum_i P_i rho P_i,

## 2. Proof by an exact entropy identity

Finite-temperature rho and sigma are strictly positive. Pinching makes
log sigma block diagonal, and Tr(rho log sigma)=Tr(sigma log sigma). Hence

    D(rho||sigma)=S(sigma)-S(rho).

Also Tr(sigma D)=Tr(rho D), Tr(sigma T)=0, and

    D(sigma||rho_D)=-S(sigma)+beta Tr(sigma D)+log Z_D.

Using S(rho)=beta Tr(rho H)+log Z_H gives the exact identity

    log(Z_H/Z_D)
      =beta a-D(rho||sigma)-D(sigma||rho_D).              (2)

Both relative entropies are nonnegative. The binary POVM

    E_+=(I+T/K)/2, E_-=(I-T/K)/2

has probabilities (1-a/K,1+a/K)/2 on rho and (1/2,1/2) on sigma.
Relative-entropy data processing therefore yields

    D(rho||sigma)>=f(a/K).

Dropping the last nonnegative term in (2) proves the upper bound in (1).
The lower bound is the submitted Peierls/block-trace inequality. QED.

The binary POVM is a mathematical witness, not an extra required experiment.
Its mean is already supplied by the kinetic expectation. In particular no
nonlocal implementation of the POVM is charged as if it had been performed.
