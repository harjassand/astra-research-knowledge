# Exact obstruction to eigenvector-motion stress at the minimal reserve

7 October 2026, `rounding_spectral`. This is a structural deduction, not a
simulation or a failure of the charged quartic theorem. It explains what
the proposed cycle12 high-L trace can and cannot exercise.

Suppose a cleaned retained row is on exactly N=K active coordinates and
obeys p_j<=q/K, q=sum p_j>0. Its largest entry is at least its average
q/K. Equality of the maximum and average forces **every** active p_j=q/K,
including coordinates where the row might otherwise have been zero.
Thus every nonzero retained p is a scalar multiple of the all-ones vector.

In the k2 specialization K=R^2=64, column energy gives every q<=N=64,
so no row is large (large means q>R^2). All remaining rows are medium
and every p p^T is a scalar multiple of11^T. Therefore the entire
fixed-mask potential matrix is

`M(t)=c(t)11^T`, `c(t)>0`,

for every legal fixed-state segment. The normalized Perron vector is
exactly1/sqrt(N) and its derivative is identically zero, regardless of
how the scalar weights change. The exact projector is11^T/N. No
rounded Perron approximation is needed and no accepted direction can
have nonzero eigenvector-motion charge in such a state.

This conclusion relies on N=K and K=R^2. For nonsquare K, a large row
may be possible because R^2<K, and its diagonal contribution can break
the rank-one identity. For N>K, the spread condition allows heterogeneous
p vectors. The cycle12 input reaches exactly64 active heavy coordinates,
so its ordinary high-L step exercises genuine nonzero quartic spectral
curvature while its eigenvector-motion term is zero. It validates neither
the generic rounded filter/Perron implementation nor a nonzero reduced-
resolvent motion computation.

A discriminating next construction should keep N>K and at least two
nonuniform tracked rows. One promising symmetric layout uses two feature
rows exchanged across equally sized coordinate groups, equal d and G,
and a direction producing opposite row displacements. Then L'=0 and
the charge gradient can cancel by symmetry while M'v is nonzero, hence
v' is nonzero. Unit-column normalization, d=0 reachability, spread,
barriers and full charged curvature still require exact proof and actual
execution. Merely supplying such a high-L matrix would not establish
reachability. No such extended trajectory was executed here.
