## High-y-degree obstruction for every fibre part affine in y

The following proof does not assume a selected short word. Suppose the actual reduced potential is

    psi=h0+u(x,y,rho,eta), deg_y u<=1,
    C=Hess_(rho,eta)u is not identically singular.     (C5)

Then its four-dimensional Hessian determinant is nonconstant. Indeed h0 has y-leading term -36x^4y^8. Its base Hessian has y-degree pattern

    [[8,7],[7,6]],
    [y^14] det Hess_b h0=-456192x^6.                 (C6)

Write B=Hess_b(h0+u), L=Hess_(b,R)u. Since u is affine in y,

    (Hess_b u)_yy=0, deg_y (Hess_b u)_xy<=0,
    deg_y (Hess_b u)_xx<=1,
    deg_y L_x<=1, deg_y L_y<=0,
    deg_y C<=1, deg_y adj C<=1, deg_y det C<=2.

The exact 2+2 block identity is

    det Hess psi=det B det C
      -tr(adj B L adj C L^T)+(det L)^2.              (C7)

Here det B-det Hess_b h0 has y-degree at most7. Thus its product with det C has degree at most9. The three entries of L adj C L^T have degrees at most3,2,1 respectively, so the trace term has degree at most9. The last term has degree at most2. In contrast, (det Hess_b h0)det C has degree 14+deg_y(det C)>=14 with nonzero leading coefficient. It cannot be cancelled by the other terms. This proves (C5), including all lower coefficients of h0 and arbitrary affine-y fibre parts.

## Two-letter words with arbitrary base coefficients: outer degree at most three fails

A two-letter word ending in a rho-update has final coefficient G1, hence fails the noncylindrical filter. Consider the remaining orientation

    X=rho+F(x,y,eta), Y=eta+H(x,y,X),
    H=sum_(j=2..d) a_j(x,y) X^j, d>=2.              (C11)

The leading filter forces a_d in C[x], because E=p a_d is cylindrical and p has distinct parallel x-level factors. The second coefficient filter (W14)–(W17) then forces F_y=0 and Q_yy=0, where Q=[s^(d-1)]V after undoing F.

If d=2, Q=G1, and H=a_2(x)s^2. The entire fibre part is affine in y. If d=3, Q=p a_2(x,y), so a_2 is affine in y; again the entire fibre part is affine in y. Equation (C9) gives det C nonzero, so (C5) excludes both cases.

Therefore a surviving two-letter completion after this actual changed jet would require outer fibre degree at least four and some lower outer coefficient genuinely nonlinear in the transverse base coordinate y. This is a necessary frontier, not existence or sufficiency.
