P14 — postselection continuity audit

For an instrument with success outputs A=E(rho), B=E(sigma), probabilities p=Tr A and q=Tr B, and input trace distance delta, completing the success map with a failure outcome and using trace-norm contractivity gives

  ||A-B||_1 + |p-q| <= 2 delta.

Therefore |p-q|<=delta, and

  D(A/p,B/q)
    <= (||A-B||_1+|p-q|)/(2p)
    <= delta/p
    <= delta/(q-delta),       q>delta.

For TMSV amplitude lambda, D(TMSV,vacuum)=lambda exactly. The common fanout, isotropic additive noise and loss channel, followed by the herald instrument, contracts this to delta<=lambda. A vacuum input gives a product thermal reference with mean t=eta*nu; the R-fold one-photon herald has

  p_0=[t/(1+t)^2]^R.

Thus p_H>=p_0-lambda and, when p_0>lambda,

  D(noisy TMSV conditional signal, thermal(t))
     <= lambda/(p_0-lambda).

The reference conditional signal is thermal(t), with distance t/(1+t) from vacuum.

For eta=1/R, nu=R^(-a), t=R^(-(a+1)), and lambda=2^(-R^b),

  log_2(lambda/p_0)
    = -R^b +(a+1)R log_2 R + 2R log_2(1+R^(-(a+1))).

It tends to minus infinity iff b>1. In that regime the noisy conditional signal tends to vacuum. The ideal conditional law tends to Poisson(1): its excess source pair number has exact negative-binomial tail at most (R+1)lambda^2(1-eta), and its retained count is within 1/R TV of Binomial(R,1/R). Hence the ideal/noisy conditional TV gap tends to 1-e^(-1). For b<=1, p_0>lambda eventually is not certified; this method gives no continuity conclusion. This extends the original b=2 regime analytically to fixed b>1 (finite rational lambda for integer b>=2). No eta-exponent extension is asserted.

This is a postselection instability result only. It establishes no sampling hardness, inverse-herald lower bound, or N81 hardness transfer. N81's binary-herald interface does not cover this R-fold high-count herald.
