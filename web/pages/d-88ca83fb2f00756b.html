# A repaired two-wave macro transition: exact flux cocycle and transverse second jet

7 October 2026, cycle 9. **A construction-specific local calculation, not a global conductivity realization.** On a legally specified member of the arbitrary-loss family, a phase-safe trajectory crosses an entire two-wave macro transition. Its conductivity cocycle tends to zero and its transported transverse second jet tends to zero. The very large transverse flow Jacobian cancels the exponentially small normal flux exactly. This removes a proposed ridge obstruction; it leaves the repaired nodal collars and global compatibility unresolved. No result on the original potential's negative index follows.

The existing theorem specifies a family of covers, directions, partitions and frequencies rather than a numerical global F. We now select legal choices in that family. All following estimates refer to its literal final function

    F(x)=∫ψ(u)[E q+W](x−ε(x)u)du,
    Ω={|q|<τ},  supp W⊂closure Ω,  ε=σ/a.

We do not replace F by a potential-only fixture or by a raw nodal quotient. The repair vanishes in the selected averaging balls by an exact inclusion certificate. It generally remains nonzero outside this tube.

## 1. A legal plateau and local partition

Let S be one fixed smooth nondecreasing step, S=0 on (−∞,0], S=1 on [1,∞). For example use S(t)=B(t)/(B(t)+B(1−t)) on (0,1), B(t)=exp(−1/t). Set

    T_j=e^j,  a(R)=1+Σ_{j≥1} S(log R−T_j).

This is an admissible nondecreasing unbounded subpower frequency with |a^(m)|≤C_m a/R^m. On log R∈[T_j+1,T_(j+1)] it is exactly a_j=j+1. Choose

    R_j=exp((T_j+1+T_(j+1))/2),
    r_j=(R_j²−K²)^(1/2),   L_j=(R_j/a_j)^(1/2).

Every fixed multiple of L_j about r_j e_1 lies in that plateau for all sufficiently large j. Put s=x_1, y=x_2, z=x_3 and v=(x_2,…,x_n). Reserve two radial-axis centres r_j e_1±c L_j e_1 and tangent directions e_2,e_3. In a cylinder containing s∈[r_j−L_j,r_j+2L_j], |v|≤c L_j, prescribe

    q(s,v)=χ(s)cos(a_j y)+(1−χ(s))cos(a_j z),
    χ(s)=S((s−r_j)/L_j).                         (1)

Here and below fixed geometric constants may be enlarged once.

This prescription is legal in the audited construction. To see this without assuming an exclusive core, take disjoint enlarged cylinders on the escaping subsequence and bumps β_j equal to one on the displayed cylinders, supported on slightly larger macro clusters, with |D^mβ_j|≤C_m L_j^(−m). Add the two partition entries β_jχ and β_j(1−χ). Multiply an ordinary macro partition ξ_i by 1−Σβ_j. All entries are nonnegative, sum to one, have the required derivative bounds and macro-sized supports. The original bounded-overlap packing bounds, with two additional entries per separated cluster, remain bounded. Preassign e_2,e_3, then greedily choose the other tangent directions outside the finitely many forbidden caps; their separation remains positive because n≥3. The two prescribed frequencies are identical in magnitude and their directions have separation sqrt(2). Thus the compact separated-frequency sparsity proof, global Dirichlet repair and smoothing apply unchanged, with once-fixed constants. This modifies legal construction choices; it does not assert that an arbitrary previously chosen partition already contains this transition.

## 2. Exact repair inclusion and literal final F

Choose a fixed d>0 and the construction's fixed σ so that d+σ<π/3. In the tube

    s∈[r_j−σ/a_j,r_j+L_j+σ/a_j],  |v|≤d/a_j,

and in every averaging ball about it, both cos(a_j y) and cos(a_j z) exceed cos(d+σ)>1/2. Hence q>1/2>τ after decreasing the original threshold to τ<1/2 if necessary before fixing parameters. The macro prescription also holds throughout those balls. They are disjoint from closure Ω. Consequently

    W=0 on every averaging ball,  H=∫K_x W=0,
    F=G=T_(σ/a_j)(E q) throughout this tube.     (2)

No small-W estimate is substituted for (2), and no derivative of W is assumed. Since a is constant throughout the larger macro cluster, ε is constant there. There are no variable-radius commutator terms in this local calculation. The original global smoothing and repair remain in place.

On the plateau E=C_j exp(−a_j R), R=(K²+|x|²)^(1/2). Curvature and the regularized origin K remain present in this exact formula.

## 3. What radial smoothing does to the principal transition

Write a=a_j, L=L_j, A=aL=sqrt(aR_j). For the flat principal function

    f_flat=e^(−as)[χ(s)cos(ay)+(1−χ(s))cos(az)],

radial smoothing gives exactly

    T_(σ/a)f_flat=e^(−as)[θ(s)cos(ay)+(1−θ(s))cos(az)],
    θ(s)=∫ψ(u)e^(σu_1)cos(σu_2)χ(s−σu_1/a)du.   (3)

The sine averages vanish by reflection. The weights in (3) are nonnegative because σ<π/2, and their integral is exactly one: the function e^(σu_1)cos(σu_2) is harmonic, so radial averaging preserves its value at zero. Symmetry interchanging u_2,u_3 supplies the second coefficient. Therefore θ is a smooth nondecreasing step, 0≤θ≤1, |θ^(m)|≤C_m L^(−m), constant outside an interval of length L+2σ/a.

Equation (3) is used only to acquire the leading part of the literal F in (2). The error from the actual radial envelope is retained. On |v|≤(d+σ)/a define

    δ_j=1/(aR_j)+K²/R_j².

Taylor expansion of R in v, with |s−r_j|≤C L and fixed K, gives the following local jet ledger for m≤4:

    D^mF = D^m{E_0(s)[θ cos(ay)+(1−θ)cos(az)]}
               +O_m(δ_j a^m E_0(s)),
    D^kΔF = D^k{E_0(s)(θ''−2aθ')[cos(ay)−cos(az)]}
               +O_k(δ_j a^(k+2) E_0(s)), k≤2,       (4)
    E_0(s)=C_j exp(−a sqrt(K²+s²)).

For completeness, at an averaging point the transverse contribution to R−sqrt(K²+s²) is O(1/(a²R_j)); multiplication by a costs 1/(aR_j). Also ∂s log E_0=−a+O(K²a/R_j²), and each extra fixed derivative costs at most a. The ratio E_0(s−σu_1/a)/E_0(s) differs from e^(σu_1) by O(K²/R_j²); transverse differentiation of the radial envelope contributes a/R_j or its a-multiples. Differentiating under the compact integral proves the first ledger. Applying Δ, retaining the exact partition term θ''−2aθ', and using E_0''−a²E_0=O(δ_j a²E_0) proves the second. Thus (4) retains the scales a/R_j and K²a²/R_j² rather than absorbing them into the much larger generic κ.

## 4. Exact axis cocycle: expansion cancels normal flux

The literal F is even in every transverse coordinate in this tube. Let

    F_0(s)=F(s,0),  p=F_0',
    B_i(s)=∂_(v_i)^2F(s,0),  d(s)=ΔF(s,0).

Swapping y,z in the averaging integral shows that F_0 is EXACTLY independent of χ. Therefore p and d=p'+Σ_iB_i are also independent of the transition. Their defining integrals are the single-wave radial-envelope integrals, not a repair-free approximation elsewhere. By (4), uniformly through the transition,

    p=−aE_0(1+O(δ_j))≠0,
    d=O(δ_j a²E_0),
    B_y=−a²E_0 θ+O(δ_j a²E_0),
    B_z=−a²E_0(1−θ)+O(δ_j a²E_0),
    B_i=O(δ_j a²E_0) for the other directions.     (5)

Prescribe h=0 on a small transverse section at s=s_−, and transport across the axis; finite-interval flow-box continuation supplies a smooth h on a neighborhood of that trajectory. Its axis value obeys

    h_0'=−d/p,
    C_j:=h_0(s_+)−h_0(s_−)=−∫_(s_−)^(s_+) d/p ds,
    |C_j|≤C[ L/R_j+K²aL/R_j² ]→0.                (6)

For an exact geometric evaluation, let P be the variable-time section-to-section map obtained by parametrizing the same gradient trajectories by increasing s. Along the axis its transverse derivative is diagonal with entries

    D_iP=exp(∫_(s_−)^(s_+) B_i/p ds).

The exact cocycle is

    C_j=−log(|p(s_+)|/|p(s_−)|)−log det DP.       (7)

This follows by d=p'+Σ_iB_i, and is precisely the normal-flux factor absent from an uncorrected return-map determinant. Equivalently e^(h_0)|p| det DP is conserved. In the flat principal transition,

    log D_yP=a∫θ ds,  log D_zP=a∫(1−θ)ds,
    log det DP=a(s_+−s_−),
    log(|p(s_+)|/|p(s_−)|)=−a(s_+−s_−).

Each nontrivial transverse multiplier can be exp(cA), yet C_j=0 exactly. In the literal curved F the error in each logarithmic multiplier is O(δ_j aL)→0 and (6) remains valid. Large linearized flow norm alone is therefore not a rejection mechanism on this transition.

## 5. Nontrivial transverse derivative calculation

Evenness and zero initial transverse data give ∂_(v_i)h(s,0)=0 exactly. A first derivative alone would miss the nontrivial effect. Put k_i(s)=∂_(v_i)^2h(s,0) and d_i(s)=∂_(v_i)^2ΔF(s,0). Differentiating the literal transport equation twice gives the exact ODE

    p k_i'+2B_i k_i=−d_i−B_i' h_0',  k_i(s_−)=0. (8)

This retains the radial h_0 contribution. Using (4)–(5),

    k_y'+[2aθ+e_y]k_y= 2a²θ'−aθ''+r_y,
    k_z'+[2a(1−θ)+e_z]k_z=−2a²θ'+aθ''+r_z,
    |e_i|≤Cδ_j a,  |r_i|≤Cδ_j a³.               (9)

Other directions have coefficient O(δ_j a), forcing O(δ_j a³). The curvature term B_i'h_0'/p has size O(δ_j a³) and is included in r_i. All mixed transverse second derivatives vanish on the axis by reflection.

There is a uniform estimate in (9) considerably better than multiplying forcing by the macro transit time. For v=θ or 1−θ, nonnegativity and ||v''||∞≤C/L² imply the elementary Glaeser bound

    |θ'|≤C L^(−1) sqrt(v).                       (10)

Proof: Taylor's upper bound v(s+t)≤v(s)+v'(s)t+Mt²/2, M=||v''||∞, at t=−v'/M gives |v'|²≤2Mv. This uses θ's exact positive-kernel monotonicity.

Let J_y(s,t)=exp(−2a∫_t^sθ), J_z(s,t)=exp(−2a∫_t^s(1−θ)). Change variables to v in the monotone parts and use (10). For activation,

    ∫_t^sθ ≥ cL[θ(s)^(3/2)−θ(t)^(3/2)].

For deactivation use the reversed difference of (1−θ)^(3/2). The inequality (u+w)^(3/2)≥u^(3/2)+w^(3/2) then gives, for either direction,

    sup_s ∫_(s_−)^s J_i(s,t)θ'(t)dt ≤ C A^(−2/3). (11)

Indeed each integral is bounded by ∫_0^∞exp(−cA v^(3/2))dv. Variation of constants in (9), with ∫|e_i|≤Cδ_j aL=o(1), and ∫|θ''|≤C/L yields

    sup_s |k_i(s)| ≤ C[ a²A^(−2/3)+a/L+δ_j a³L ]
       = C[ a^(5/3)R_j^(−1/3)
              +κ_j(1+K²a/R_j) ]→0.             (12)

The other directions satisfy the smaller last term. Also h_0'=O(δ_j a)→0 and h_0''=O(δ_j a²)→0 by differentiating the exact quotient in (6). Hence on the whole selected axis trajectory

    |Dh(s,0)|→0,   |Δh(s,0)|→0.                 (13)

For this statement a is subpower; no quantitative divergence rate of a is used. These are transported jets of a genuine local solution. They are not uniform bounds for h on a full macro cross-section. The width of the flow neighborhood supplied by continuation has not been lower-bounded uniformly.
