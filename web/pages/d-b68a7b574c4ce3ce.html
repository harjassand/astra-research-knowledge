# Fixed local Pauli SLD scores do not identify all faithful states

Date: 2026-10-06. Status: exact finite arithmetic certificate plus independently audited analytic construction. This establishes an existence theorem, including a fully specified conservative positive parameter. No literature-priority claim is adopted.

## Exact question and result

On n qubits use the fixed physical frame A={X_i,Z_i}_{i=1}^n, and define its unitary-orbit symmetric scores by -i[A,rho]={rho,S_A(rho)}/2. Does equality of all these scores identify every faithful density operator, or every Gibbs state in a bounded-locality/bounded-interaction-degree Pauli model at every finite temperature?

**Theorem.** Already on six qubits there are distinct faithful trace-one states sigma and rho with S_A(sigma)=S_A(rho) for every physical local X_i and Z_i. They lie in the same known full six-qubit Pauli Hamiltonian model with coefficients in [-1,1] at one common finite inverse temperature. Tensoring blocks gives a bounded-locality/bounded-degree counterexample for arbitrarily many qubits, with constants independent of the number of blocks.

This is stronger than the cycle1 arbitrary-reflection obstruction: the frame here is exactly the prescribed local Pauli frame. It does not contradict Dong–Leng2609.28391v1 Theorems9–11, which impose a uniformly high-temperature regime. It shows that the frame alone cannot provide unrestricted all-temperature Fisher consistency.

These 64 real orthogonal 8-by-8 matrices are Frobenius-orthogonal: Tr(P^T Q)=8 delta_PQ. Their column vectorizations divided by sqrt(8) form a real orthogonal maximally entangled Bell basis B across the physical 3|3 bipartition.

## Real analytic continuation to a faithful state

For a real parameter s near0, define a symmetric matrix C_s by

C_s,ii=1,
C_s,ij=2 s^|i-j|/(1+s^(2|i-j|)) for i!=j.

It is real analytic at0, with C_0=I. For every positive s<1 it is exactly the SLD filter of the faithful populations

p_i(s)=s^(2i)/(sum_{j=0}^{63}s^(2j)).

Define F_a(x,s) as the first63 coordinates of

(C_s Hadamard B_a(x))1 - [1^T(C_s Hadamard B_a(x))1/d]1.

Use only the 756 skew coordinates selected by the exact minor. At (x,s)=(0,0), F=0, because C_0 keeps only the zero diagonal. The derivative D_xF is exactly the certified nonsingular minor; its mean correction vanishes because the diagonal derivative has trace zero. The real analytic implicit-function theorem therefore gives x(s) near0 with F(x(s),s)=0. No imaginary equations have been discarded: U, A, C and F are real throughout. The earlier complex four-qubit trial did not have this property and was not adopted.

For any sufficiently small positive s, set

sigma(s)=U(x(s)) diag(p_i(s)) U(x(s))^T.

It is faithful. In its eigenbasis, all dressed generators are K_a=C_s Hadamard B_a(x(s)). Their row sums are constant, so the all-ones vector is a common eigenvector. Their symmetry makes P_1=11^T/d commute with every K_a.

## An explicit finite positive parameter, beyond an unquantified IFT

The following bounds are deliberately conservative. They make the counterexample fully specified even though the resulting spectrum is impractical numerically.

Let J_0 be the certified 756-square minor. Each of its entries has absolute value<=2 and denominator dividing Q. The nonzero rational determinant has magnitude>=Q^-m; a permutation cofactor bound yields

||J_0^-1||_infinity <= L=m! 2^(m-1) Q^m.

For |s|<=1/2, |C_ij|<=1 and |partial_s C_ij|<=2. For each skew coordinate, ||partial_x U||_op<=1 and ||partial_x partial_y U||_op<=1 by the integral derivatives of orthogonal matrix exponentials; consequently |partial_x B_ij|<=2 and |partial_x partial_y B_ij|<=4. Thus

||F_s||_infinity<=4d,

||D_xF(x,s)-J_0||_infinity<=8dm(m||x||_infinity+|s|).

Define the positive rational numbers

r=min{1/(4m),1/(32dLm^2)},
epsilon=min{1/2,1/(32dLm),r/(8dL)},
s_star=epsilon/2.

For every |s|<=epsilon, G_s(x)=x-J_0^-1F(x,s) contracts the closed infinity-norm ball of radiusr with factor<=1/2. Indeed its derivative norm is at most1/2 and ||G_s(0)||<=4dL|s|<=r/2. Banach iteration from0 therefore defines the unique exact root x(s) in that ball. In particular the rational positive s_star specifies one faithful counterexample, with no appeal to a singular limiting density operator. A numerical continuation to larger s could supply a more usable witness, but is not needed for this existence theorem.

For these constants the minima simplify to s_star=1/(512 d^2 L^2 m^2). ## Distinct state with exactly equal scores

In the eigenbasis of sigma let

R=(1-1/d)I+P_1,

rho=U diag(sqrt(p_i)) R diag(sqrt(p_i)) U^T.

R has eigenvalues2-1/d on1 and1-1/d on its complement, so rho>0. Its diagonal in this eigenbasis equals p_i; hence Tr rho=1. Offdiagonal entries sqrt(p_i p_j)/d are nonzero, so rho!=sigma. R commutes with every dressed K_a.

The exact SLD fiber identity proves equality of all scores: fixing S_a(sigma), the score equation for rho is equivalent to [sigma^-1/2 rho sigma^-1/2,K_a]=0. The displayed R satisfies it, and faithfulness makes the SLD Lyapunov solution unique. There is in fact an open interval of collisions R_t=I+t(P_1-I/d), -d/(d-1)<t<d, not only one competing state.

## Same bounded-local Gibbs model and arbitrarily large systems

Let h_tau=-log(tau) with its scalar identity coefficient removed, for tau=sigma,rho. Expand each in the same known 4095 nonidentity Pauli strings on six qubits. Choose one finite beta>=1+max_{tau,j}|h_tau,j| and set H_tau=sum_j(h_tau,j/beta)P_j. Both coefficient vectors lie in [-1,1], and exp(-beta H_tau)/Tr exp(-beta H_tau)=tau.

Tensor k copies of these six-qubit blocks. A physical local derivation acts on one block, and its SLD is the block score tensor identities: substituting into the defining Lyapunov equation verifies this directly. Therefore sigma^tensor k and rho^tensor k have identical scores for all local X_i,Z_i, yet remain distinct faithful states. The Hamiltonian is a sum of the block Hamiltonians, each term has locality<=6, and each term intersects at most4094 others. All constants, including beta, are independent of k. Additional maximally mixed spectator qubits extend the example to every n>=6.

Thus bounded locality and degree alone do not restore all-temperature population consistency for this fixed local frame. The construction's conservative beta is enormous, so it establishes a low-temperature boundary; it does not falsify the published high-temperature theorem or its measurement/sample guarantees.

## Maximal state separation after tensor amplification

The same-diagonal collision can become maximally separated in global state distance while every local score remains equal. Let M=U(E_01+E_10)U^T, a Hermitian norm1 block observable. Its expectation is0 under sigma and Delta=2sqrt(p_0p_1)/d>0 under rho. Measuring M independently on k blocks of the tensor product gives outcomes in[-1,1]. Threshold their empirical mean at Delta/2. Hoeffding bounds each decision error by exp(-k Delta^2/8). Therefore

||rho^tensor k-sigma^tensor k||_1 >=2[1-2exp(-k Delta^2/8)] ->2.

This is an explicit state-separation proof, not a practical learning claim: its k cost is O(Delta^-2 log(1/error)), and Delta is extraordinarily small for the conservative certified s_star. The constructed six-qubit M also requires the witness basis U; it is not automatically a supplied target-observable interface. Nevertheless exact score blindness is not restricted to a small global trace-distance discrepancy.

**Reusable abstract lemma.** The same argument applies to any q real symmetric trace-zero matrices on R^d for which there is an orthogonal simultaneous zero-diagonal basis U_0 and the diagonal-rotation map has full row rank q(d-1).
