# Causal-mediation alias for systemic dm-αKG

The primary paper reports the renewal/Notum readouts and the 5-FU recovery readouts in different cohorts and does not selectively block the proposed Paneth-renewal mediator during the recovery experiment. The reported treatment contrast supports an association/effect under that mouse protocol, subject to its allocation design; it does not by itself identify how much of that effect is mediated by PC replacement or Notum reduction.

Let (T\in\{0,1\}) denote vehicle versus dm-αKG. Let (M) summarize the measured mediator group mean (EdU-positive Paneth fraction and/or Notum), and (Y) the recovery outcome. Suppose the observed group means are (E[M\mid T]=m_0+\delta T) and (E[Y\mid T]=y_0+\gamma T), with \(\delta\ne0\).

Two structural models produce the same observed group means:

1. **Full mediation:** (M=m_0+\delta T+U), (Y=y_0+(\gamma/\delta)(M-m_0)+V).
2. **No mediation:** (M=m_0+\delta T+U), (Y=y_0+\gamma T+V), with no arrow from (M) to (Y).

With mediator and outcome measured in separate cohorts, the observed design does not even supply an individual-level (M,Y) covariance that might constrain these alternatives. Even co-measuring (M) and (Y) would not by itself identify mediation in the presence of common causes or a direct dm-αKG path.

This is a generic causal-identification obstruction, not evidence that the published mechanism is false. The prior Notum study independently establishes that manipulating Notum/Wnt can affect aged intestinal regeneration. What remains unestablished is whether the 2025 dm-αKG recovery benefit requires the specific chain ISC αKG/TET activity → new low-Notum Paneth cells → local Wnt/stem-cell restoration.

## Intervention that changes the identification state

A decisive test needs a randomized mechanism intervention in addition to dm-αKG/vehicle, such as a validated inducible ISC-specific block of the relevant TET-dependent PC-commitment route (with selectivity, baseline crypt effects, and dm-αKG exposure checked), or a Paneth-specific Notum clamp in newly made cells. It should measure EdU and the relevant target manipulation in each crypt before the same 5-FU challenge, then quantify per-crypt epithelial injury and recovery. If a broad perturbation changes the injury response on its own, interaction effects must be compared against its own matched control. Costs include additional aged animals, lineage/tissue assays, and adequate power for mouse-level clustering; no sample size is estimable until the relevant variances are acquired.
