1. CONVENTIONS AND STATEMENT
sigma_-=(sigma_x-i sigma_y)/2, J_-=sum_i sigma_-^(i)=J_x-iJ_y,
J_z=sum_i sigma_z^(i)/2, E=J_z+N/2, R_op=J_+J_-.
D[L]rho=LrhoL^dagger-{L^dagger L,rho}/2. For dot rho=Gamma D[J_-]rho,
Gamma>0, use scaled time s=Gamma*t. All times below are in s unless stated.
Using twice this dissipator or a normalized jump requires the corresponding
rescaling; a zero rate Gamma0 would invalidate a decay conclusion.

For EVERY N>=2 and every full-rank initially fully separable state, including
every admitted finite-parameter axial Gibbs state from the frozen optimal
certificate, this evolution is entangled at every sufficiently late FINITE
time. The argument permits initial coherences and requires no postselection.
It proves failure of full separability, not genuine N-partite entanglement.

2. FULL-SEPARABILITY WITNESS, INCLUDING MIXED PRODUCTS
For a product of arbitrary one-qubit states let p_i=<sigma_+sigma_->_i and
c_i=<sigma_->_i. Local positivity gives |c_i|^2<=p_i(1-p_i). Direct expansion
gives
 R=sum_i(p_i-|c_i|^2)+|sum_i c_i|^2
   >=sum_i p_i^2 >=(sum_i p_i)^2/N =<E>^2/N.
For a convex product mixture, linearity of R and Jensen's inequality for the
square give the SAME R>=<E>^2/N. This covers the entire full-SEP cone.

For any fixed beta>=0 it also supplies the linear entanglement witness
 W_beta=R_op-(2beta/N)E+(beta^2/N)I,
since every fully separable expectation is >=(<E>-beta)^2/N>=0. Thus the
later violation can be expressed by a single ordinary linear witness rather
than only a nonlinear inequality.

3. CONSERVED SPIN WEIGHTS, MULTIPLICITIES AND COHERENCES
Decompose H_N=direct sum_j (V_j tensor M_j), j=N/2-b,
b=0,...,floor(N/2), with multiplicity
d_(N,b)=binomial(N,b)-binomial(N,b-1)>0.
The jump is J_-^(j) tensor I_M. Consequently each spin-sector weight q_b is
conserved. For a full-rank initial state q_b>0 for every nonempty sector.
In particular q_1>0 when N>=2.

Define the conserved positive operator
 B=(N+1)I/2-sqrt(J^2+I/4).
Its value in spin j is b=N/2-j. Therefore
 beta=<B>_0=sum_b b*q_b>0
for every full-rank initial state at N>=2. Also E-B has eigenvalues n=j+m
in {0,...,2j}, so E>=B as an operator and <E>_s>=beta at every time.
This lower bound does not require the state to commute with J_z.

The populations p_(j,n)=Tr[rho (|j,m><j,m| tensor I_M)] obey a classical
pure-death equation even with initial spin/coherence/multiplicity correlations.
Neither coherences nor off-diagonal multiplicity entries enter these traces.
The rate out of n=j+m is
 lambda_(j,n)=n(2j-n+1).
It follows directly from the matrix element of J_-. The operator R_op is
diagonal in these same populations, so this population equation suffices for
all witness estimates. Initial finite axial fields alter positive sector and
magnetic weights but not the conserved-sector argument or rates.

For an axial Gibbs initial state the multiplicity block is scalar and magnetic
populations are strictly positive. Its limit is explicitly
 direct sum_b q_b |j,-j><j,-j| tensor I_(d_b)/d_b.
Hence R_infinity0 and E_infinity=beta>0. For arbitrary coherences the final
dark-space coherences need not have this scalar formula, but the same R and E
limits hold. The finite-time argument below does not assume their absence.

4. ORIGINAL COARSE BOUND IS VALID
Every positive rate is an integer >=1, and at most N deaths occur. The sum
of its independent holding times is stochastically dominated by Gamma(N,1).
At Chernoff parameter1/2, E exp(T/2)<=2^N, so survival<=2^N exp(-s/2).
Since ||R_op||<= (N+1)^2/4,
 R(s)<=(N+1)^2*2^(N-2)*exp(-s/2).
Thus s>2log[N(N+1)^2*2^(N-2)/beta^2] suffices for entanglement. This
coarse derivation is retained as valid, rather than silently discarded.

5. STRONGER SECTOR-UNIFORM BOUND
Put K=2j. For K>=1 and1<=n<=K,
 lambda_n-K=(n-1)(K-n)>=0.
There are at most K steps, each with rate at least K, so absorption is
dominated by Gamma(K,K). At parameter1/2 its exponential moment is at most
 (1-1/(2K))^(-K)<=2.
The last inequality is the integer Bernoulli inequality
(1-1/(2K))^K>=1-K/(2K)=1/2. K0 is already dark and is excluded from division.
This proves survival<=2exp(-s/2) independently of N and of the initial level.

The maximum ladder intensity is j(j+1) for integer j and j(j+1)+1/4 for
half-integer j. All allowed spins have the parity of N/2. Averaging the sector
bound therefore yields
 R(s)<=2C exp(-s/2),
 C=<J^2>_0+(N mod2)/4,
with C conserved. For a full-rank N>=2 state C>0. Consequently
 s>2log(2NC/beta^2)
suffices for R(s)<beta^2/N<=<E(s)>^2/N and also <W_beta>_s<0.
The physical time is t>2log(2NC/beta^2)/Gamma. There is no universal such
time over all full-rank Gibbs parameters: beta can become arbitrarily small
as the state approaches exclusive maximum-spin support.

6. MAXIMALLY MIXED INITIAL STATE AND A UNIVERSAL TIME
For rho0=I/2^N, C=(3N+(N mod2))/4. Spin addition to a maximally mixed extra
qubit takes j to j+1/2 with probability(j+1)/(2j+1), or to j-1/2 with
probability j/(2j+1). Its mean-spin increment is1/[2(2j+1)]. Thus
 beta_(N+1)-beta_N=1/2-(1/2) E_N[1/(2j+1)].
The expectation is sum_j d_(N,j)/2^N=binomial(N,floor(N/2))/2^N by telescoping.
It is <=1/2 for every N>=1: the central-binomial probabilities decrease on
an even-to-odd step and are unchanged on the following odd-to-even step,
starting from1/2 at N1. With beta_1=0 this proves beta_N>=(N-1)/4.

Let beta0=(N-1)/4, a known lower bound usable directly in W_beta0. Then
 2NC/beta0^2=8N(3N+(N mod2))/(N-1)^2<=96 for every N>=2.
At N2 it equals96. For N>=3, N-1>=2N/3 and N mod2<=1<=N/3 give even the
stronger upper60. The finite positive Taylor sum gives

Therefore scaled time s10 guarantees entanglement for EVERY N>=2, with
 <W_beta0>_(s10)<-[2465/16289]*beta0^2/N<0.
This is an analytic all-N bound, not a numerical scan or a claim about the
earliest possible onset. In physical units t10/Gamma suffices.
