## Exact question and claimed interface

Does a certificate for every centered linear marginal's subgaussian tail imply a dimension-free LSI for any log-concave target? Theorem 1.1 claims one finite universal C such that, in any Euclidean dimension,

\[
\sup_{|\theta|=1}E e^{\langle X,\theta\rangle^2/a^2}\le2,
\qquad
\operatorname{Ent}_\mu(f^2)\le Ca^2\int|\nabla f|^2d\mu.
\]

The target is a centered log-concave Lebesgue density; nonsmooth potentials and uniform convex bodies are admitted. Isotropy and positive curvature are unnecessary. With `Ent<=2R energy`, this is R<=Ca²/2. A Gaussian's exact squared parameter is a²=(8/3) times its largest variance. Covariance boundedness alone is insufficient. The theorem supplies no numerical C, target-sample oracle, or query/bit bound.



## Independent adversarial results and computational evidence

Full derivations are in [ADVERSARIAL_INTERFACES.md](</Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/Literature Digestion/notes/frontier_check_lsi/cycle19/ADVERSARIAL_INTERFACES.md>).

* The smooth symmetric two-Gaussian mixture has uniformly bounded linear ψ₂ parameter but an exponentially growing Poincaré/LSI bottleneck. At component standard deviation 1/8, the certified Poincaré lower bound exceeds 3.58×10¹⁰ while a²<=65/24. Its missing assumption is log-concavity.
* That family gives a direct analytic failure of Lemma 4.2 after deleting base log-concavity: at standard deviation 1/4, twice entropy loss over unit Gaussian variance is at least 0.28268, whereas the entire predicted-original-score integral is at most 0.02691. Coarse rational inequalities also separate them, independently of numerical quadrature. The whole Gaussian positive-control family satisfies the lemma by a pointwise Fisher comparison.
* The legal log-concave density proportional to e^{-Rx} on [0,1] has centered ψ₂² of order 1/R, while external field +R produces uniform variance 1/12. Thus the supplied initial tail scale does not certify all tilted posteriors.
* A diffuse commuting matrix block preserves the exact whole-scale information integral but sends every fixed finite scale window's contribution to zero. This tests why the source needs its full-axis spectral Jacobian; the static block is not asserted to be a critical stationary law.
* The legal Gaussian `N(0,diag(epsilon²,1))` has fixed exact R and ψ₂, but ordinary Euler Langevin requires a step of order epsilon². At epsilon=0.001 the displayed stable step needs 23,025,850 updates for a tenfold slow-coordinate mean reduction. Preconditioning can avoid this, so it is an obstruction to that inference, not a universal sampler lower bound.

## Strongest supported consequence and competent comparison

Conditional on 093, linear ψ₂ certification becomes dimension-free concentration for fixed nonlinear Lipschitz observables, including norms, distances, and suprema of unit-slope affine fields. A stationary Dirichlet diffusion contracts KL at rate `4/(Ca²)`. Fixed-dimension weak limits retain the inequality, bounded density perturbations cost at most their ratio, and convex conditioning of mass p costs at most `4log(2/p)/log2` in the entropy coefficient. Convex-body targets require the appropriate reflected/Dirichlet process. These are mathematical certificates; sampling access and data-dependent observable complexity remain charged.
