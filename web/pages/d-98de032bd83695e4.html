# Primary-source notes

1. **Robust orthogonal tensor perturbation.** Anandkumar, Ge, Hsu, Kakade, and Telgarsky, “Tensor Decompositions for Learning Latent Variable Models,” *Journal of Machine Learning Research* 15 (2014), 2773–2832, [paper and PDF](https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/anandkumar14b.html). Theorem 5.1 assumes a symmetric orthogonally decomposable tensor \(T=\sum_i\lambda_i v_i^{\otimes3}\), positive \(\lambda_i\), and a symmetric operator-norm perturbation \(\|\widehat T-T\|\le\xi\). Under \(\xi\le C_1\lambda_{\min}/K\) and finite restart/iteration conditions, the robust tensor-power procedure returns, up to permutation, vector error at most \(8\xi/\lambda_i\), eigenvalue error at most \(5\xi\), with prescribed high probability. This is the perturbation result used after whitening. The paper's theorem also charges a polynomial restart count and an iteration count depending logarithmically and log-logarithmically on the tensor parameters; it does not itself analyze adversarially corrupted HMM read blocks.

2. **Three-view latent-class identifiability.** Allman, Matias, and Rhodes, “Identifiability of Parameters in Latent Structure Models with Many Observed Variables,” *Annals of Statistics* 37(6A) (2009), 3099–3132, [DOI and paper](https://doi.org/10.1214/09-AOS689) and [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.5032). The paper uses Kruskal's theorem for conditionally independent observed views and gives a three-view identifiability route. Here, full-rank \(E\) makes all three pre-view factor matrices full column rank, while \(\pi_i>0\), so the same label-permutation boundary is expected. The proof in this report also exhibits a constructive whitening reduction to an orthogonal tensor.

3. **Finite-state HMM identifiability (context, not the theorem used for the pre block).** Gassiat, Cleynen, and Robin, “Finite State Space Non Parametric Hidden Markov Models are in General Identifiable,” *Statistics and Computing* 26 (2016), 61–71, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11222-014-9523-8) and [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4657). Theorem 2.1 identifies a known-order stationary HMM up to state permutation from three consecutive observations when emissions are linearly independent and the hidden transition matrix is full rank. Our pre-read latent-class design avoids assuming stationarity or full-rank \(P\); it pays for three conditionally iid reads before the transition and uses the separate transition pair moment.

4. **Categorical \(L^1\) concentration.** Weissman, Ordentlich, Seroussi, Verdú, and Weinberger, “Inequalities for the \(L_1\) Deviation of the Empirical Distribution,” HP Labs Technical Report HPL-2003-97(R.1), [report PDF](https://shiftleft.com/mirrors/www.hpl.hp.com/techreports/2003/HPL-2003-97R1.pdf). The finite-alphabet bound used here is
   \[
   \Pr\{\|\widehat q-q\|_1\ge t\}\le(2^d-2)e^{-nt^2/2}.
   \]
   It is applied to independent episode-level categorical records. It is not applied to the four reads as if they were four independent episodes.

5. **Contamination threshold.** The midpoint condition in the report is a direct total-variation-ball calculation: two radius-\(\varepsilon_{\rm pair}\) neighborhoods intersect iff their centers are at distance at most \(2\varepsilon_{\rm pair}\). It is included as a derivation rather than attributed as a new robust-testing theorem. For classical context see Huber, “Robust Estimation of a Location Parameter,” *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 35(1) (1964), 73–101, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177703732). The post-endpoint-dependent witness and its rational enumeration are local to this report.

## Novelty and applicability boundary

The tensor theorem, whitening, HMM/latent-class identifiability, and empirical \(L^1\) concentration are prior art. The present result composes them for a specified pre-read/transition/post-read design and tracks arbitrary episode or separate-block replacement through the joint transition table. No external source located in this bounded search states this exact contamination-aware paired inversion with the same interface; this is not a priority or novelty certification.

All assumptions remain mathematical premises: conditional independence and invariance of pre reads, shared \(E\), the \(p_*,\gamma\) floors, independent starts, and the calibrated corruption count. No cited paper validates those conditions in any physical assay.
