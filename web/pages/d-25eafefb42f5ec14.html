1. PRECISE FINITE-CHANNEL THEOREM

Let sigma>0 be a trace-one matrix in M_d and Phi:M_d->M_d be CPTP with
Phi(sigma)=sigma. Let H=Phi* be self-adjoint for
 <A,B>_KMS=Tr[A* sigma^(1/2) B sigma^(1/2)].
Set Gamma(A)=sigma^(1/2)A sigma^(1/2), S=sigma^(1/4),
 T=Gamma^(-1/2)Phi Gamma^(1/2), D=I-T.
For a faithful state rho on M_d define
 J_Phi,sigma(rho)=Tr[(rho-Phi(rho))(log rho-log sigma)],
 E_Phi,sigma(rho)=Tr[sqrt(rho) D(sqrt(rho))].
On any ancilla use Phi tensor id, the PRODUCT reference sigma tensor tau,
and the same definitions with the full state rho, which may be entangled.

The following assertions are equivalent:
 (A) For EVERY faithful trace-one tau in M_2 and EVERY faithful trace-one
     rho in M_d tensor M_2, J>=4E.
 (B) The actual state-Hessian of J-4E at sigma tensor tau is nonnegative
     on every Hermitian trace-zero state direction for every such tau.
 (C) Phi commutes with all modular conjugations
     M_t(A)=sigma^(it)A sigma^(-it), t real.
 (D) H is also self-adjoint for the GNS inner product
     <A,B>_GNS=Tr[sigma A*B].
 (E) J>=4E for EVERY finite ancilla dimension, EVERY faithful product
     reference, and EVERY faithful full state on that product algebra.

No primitiveness, self-compatibility, EB, or Hilbert-space positive spectrum
of T is required for this classification. A faithful stationary state and
the stated KMS symmetry are required. It does NOT say that the scalar
unamplified FOUR test alone is equivalent to modular covariance.

2. BASIC WEIGHTED OPERATORS

KMS symmetry gives Gamma H=Phi Gamma, so
 T=Gamma^(1/2)H Gamma^(-1/2)=T* in ordinary HS space.
H is UCP, hence contracts operator norm. Its eigenvalues, identical to T's,
therefore lie in [-1,1]. Thus D>=0, D(R)=0 for R=sqrt(sigma).
The transform T is CP because its input/output transforms are positive
congruences. It need not be unital or trace preserving. For the ancilla
the transformed map is exactly T tensor id, with R_product=sqrt(sigma)
tensor sqrt(tau). In particular the full marginal/reference conventions
are retained under amplification.

3. LOCAL NECESSITY, RECONSTRUCTED

For reference eta=diag(e_a)>0, an actual trace-zero state direction A has
weighted coordinate C=eta^(-1/4)A eta^(-1/4), satisfying <sqrt(eta),C>=0.
For D_eta=I-T_eta, the exact state-Hessian coefficients are
 J2=<C,(D_eta F+F D_eta)C/2>, E2=<L C,D_eta L C>,
with matrix-unit multipliers
 F_ab=sqrt(e_a e_b)(log e_a-log e_b)/(e_a-e_b),
 L_ab=(e_a e_b)^(1/4)/(sqrt(e_a)+sqrt(e_b)).
At equal eigenvalues F=1,L=1/2; at unequal eigenvalues, with
u=|log(e_a/e_b)|/2, F=u/sinh u,L=1/(2cosh(u/2)).
These formulas follow by divided differences for log/sqrt and D_eta R=0.

If the Hessian Q=(D_eta F+F D_eta)/2-4L D_eta L is PSD on R^perp,
it vanishes quadratically on the centered commutant of eta. Its cross
entry from a centered commutant vector to an unequal-eigenvalue coherence
is [ (1+u/sinh u)/2-sech(u/2)] times the corresponding D_eta entry.
The bracket is strictly positive: multiply by 2cosh(w)sinh(w), w=u/2,
to get g(w)=(1/2)sinh(2w)-2sinh(w)+w, with
g(0)=0,g'(w)=2cosh(w)(cosh(w)-1)>0.
A PSD form annihilates all cross entries from a zero quadratic vector.
D_eta R=0 handles the reference direction. Therefore T_eta, equivalently
D_eta, preserves the ENTIRE complex commutant of eta. Hermitian vectors
give this first on the real commutant, and complex linearity gives the
complex commutant. This proves the needed local implication, including
degenerate references and general D_eta>=0.

Now diagonalize sigma=diag(s_i). For EACH ordered i,j choose the faithful
two-level ancilla
 tau=diag(s_j,s_i)/(s_i+s_j).
The matrix E_ij tensor E_01 commutes with eta=sigma tensor tau because
s_i tau_0=s_j tau_1. Its Hermitian real/imaginary parts have zero physical
trace constraint: the ancilla off-diagonal matrix has weighted trace zero.
Assertion (B) therefore forces
 T(E_ij) tensor E_01 in the commutant of eta.
If the E_kl coefficient of T(E_ij) is nonzero, necessarily
 s_k tau_0=s_l tau_1, so s_k/s_l=s_i/s_j.
Hence T preserves EVERY modular frequency sector, including frequency
zero and repeated eigenvalues. This is precisely T M_t=M_t T. Congruence
by sigma^(1/4) commutes with M_t, so Phi is modular covariant: (B)=>(C).
Only the finite set of the above d^2 ancilla ratios is actually needed for
this implication. There is no scalar-support or unconstrained-state
replacement of the full physical Hessian.

Thus every nonmodular KMS channel fails FOUR on SOME two-level product
reference and an ACTUAL faithful state sufficiently near that reference.
The local indefiniteness gives a negative quadratic coefficient; analyticity
then makes the sign negative at sufficiently small nonzero perturbation.
This is an existence consequence. A rational fixed-state certificate for
an arbitrary input channel is not claimed.

4. PAIRED MODULAR KRAUS LEMMA

Assume (C). Then T is CP, HS-self-adjoint and modular covariant. It admits
finite Kraus matrices V_alpha satisfying simultaneously
 T(A)=sum_alpha V_alpha A V_alpha*,
 sigma^(it)V_alpha sigma^(-it)=exp(it omega_alpha)V_alpha,
and an index involution alpha->bar(alpha) with
 V_bar(alpha)=V_alpha*, omega_bar(alpha)=-omega_alpha.
Indices fixed by the involution have omega=0 and Hermitian V_alpha.

Proof from Choi geometry: vectorize matrices as |V>>=sum_i |i> tensor V|i>.
The positive Choi matrix J_T is invariant under the antiunitary involution
R|V>>=|V*>>, because J_(T*)=R J_T R and T=T*. Modular covariance says
J_T commutes with W_t|V>>=|sigma^(it)V sigma^(-it)>>.
Its frequency eigenspaces are orthogonal and preserved by J_T. R sends
frequency omega to -omega, and commutes with J_T. In every nonzero
frequency pair, diagonalize the positive block of J_T, take Kraus vectors
sqrt(lambda)v on one side, and their R-images on the other. At frequency
zero, J_T is a positive operator commuting with a conjugation; each real
eigenspace has an orthonormal R-fixed basis. Those Kraus matrices are
Hermitian. This constructs the stated involution with equal Choi weights.
It does not assert that arbitrary KMS maps have modular eigen-Kraus.

Physical Kraus matrices for Phi are
 K_alpha=S V_alpha S^(-1)=exp(omega_alpha/4)V_alpha.
Trace preservation gives the EXACT completeness relation
 sum_alpha exp(omega_alpha/2)V_alpha*V_alpha=I.             (1)
The modular eigen-relation implies
 log(sigma)V_alpha-V_alpha log(sigma)=omega_alpha V_alpha. (2)
Thus V_alpha*V_alpha commutes with sigma, and
 H(log sigma)=log sigma+sum_alpha omega_alpha
                   exp(omega_alpha/2)V_alpha*V_alpha.      (3)

5. FULL SCALAR SUFFICIENCY WITHOUT COMMUTING rho

Diagonalize the ARBITRARY faithful rho with eigenvalues r_j>0. In that
rho eigenbasis let v_alpha,ij be V_alpha's matrix entries. Define
 x_alpha,ij=exp(omega_alpha/2)r_j,
 y_alpha,ij=exp(-omega_alpha/2)r_i.
Use (1) to expand Tr rho log rho, and (3) for the log sigma term. Directly,
 J=sum_alpha,ij |v_alpha,ij|^2 x_alpha,ij log(x_alpha,ij/y_alpha,ij).
Also, since Tr rho=1 and T has the stated Kraus expansion,
 E=sum_alpha,ij |v_alpha,ij|^2[x_alpha,ij-sqrt(r_i r_j)].
The involution (alpha,i,j)->(bar(alpha),j,i) preserves |v|^2 and swaps x,y.
Symmetrizing these finite sums gives the EXACT identities
 J=(1/2)sum_alpha,ij |v_alpha,ij|^2 (x-y)log(x/y),
 E=(1/2)sum_alpha,ij |v_alpha,ij|^2 (sqrt(x)-sqrt(y))^2.    (4)
These are identities for the actual full physical state, with no assumption
that rho commutes with sigma. Degenerate rho eigenvalues cause no problem.
For all x,y>0, the elementary scalar inequality
 (x-y)log(x/y)>=4(sqrt(x)-sqrt(y))^2
follows by setting z=sqrt(x/y), using log z>=2(z-1)/(z+1) for z>=1,
and exchanging x,y when needed. Applying it term by term in (4) gives
J>=4E. The matrices V_alpha tensor I are a paired modular Kraus family
for Phi tensor id relative to ANY product reference sigma tensor tau.
Thus the same proof covers any ancilla and entangled rho: (C)=>(E).
(E)=>(A)=>(B) is immediate from restriction and second differentiation.

6. GNS EQUIVALENCE IN THE STATED KMS INTERFACE

Let Delta(A)=sigma A sigma^(-1), and let R_sigma be right multiplication
by sigma. The GNS metric operator is R_sigma=Gamma Delta^(-1/2).
KMS symmetry is Gamma H=H*Gamma. If H commutes with Delta, it therefore
is self-adjoint for R_sigma. Conversely, simultaneous KMS and GNS symmetry
give Delta^(-1/2)H=H Delta^(-1/2), hence modular covariance by finite positive
spectral calculus. Phi and H have the same covariance property because the
modular conjugations are unitary in ordinary HS space and because they
commute with Gamma. This proves (C)<=>(D) within the theorem's assumptions.
No conclusion follows here by replacing KMS with GNS silently.

7. QMS VERSION

Let G be the Heisenberg generator of a finite-dimensional UCP QMS with
faithful stationary sigma, and assume G is KMS-self-adjoint. For its
Schrodinger generator G* define
 J_G(rho)=-Tr[G*(rho)(log rho-log sigma)],
 L_G=-Gamma^(-1/2)G*Gamma^(1/2),
 E_G(rho)=Tr[sqrt(rho)L_G(sqrt(rho))].
Then complete J_G>=4E_G for every faithful two-level product-reference
ancilla is equivalent to G commuting with all modular conjugations, and
equivalent within KMS symmetry to GNS symmetry. It is also equivalent to
complete FOUR for every finite ancilla and faithful product reference.

Necessity uses the exact same local Hessian with D replaced by L_G>=0;
positivity follows because its weighted self-adjoint negative generator
has nonpositive spectrum, as every exp(tG) is a UCP contraction. The
product-reference transform is L_G tensor id. The ancilla frequency
cancellation proof above then forces every modular sector to be invariant.
For sufficiency use Phi_t=exp(tG*) for t>0. Each Phi_t is CPTP, stationary,
KMS symmetric and modular covariant, hence satisfies the finite-channel
inequality. Divide it by t and let t down to zero to obtain the displayed
generator inequality. No GKSL structure assumption or primitive limit is
needed. The old primitive counterexamples are included as special cases.

8. STATUS, COSTS, AND FALSIFIERS

This classification goes beyond the original witness's commutant condition:
two-level product-reference tests detect every off-frequency mixing.
It gives the exact boundary of COMPLETE FOUR in this finite KMS interface;
it does not resolve unamplified FOUR for commutant-preserving nonmodular
channels, weak TWO, or previous weighted comparator/logarithmic gates.
No source is used as a proof premise for the classification. The source
normalization of p=1 remains as established in the frozen scalar proof.
No numeric sweep, SDP, or descendants were used. The finite set of ancilla
ratios is explicit; no efficient channel acquisition algorithm is claimed.

Decisive falsifiers: a failure of the full physical state-Hessian; a trace
constraint excluding the ancilla off-diagonal real/imaginary directions;
incorrect Choi antiunitary orientation; unequal paired Kraus weights;
the sign of omega in physical Kraus or (3); an incorrect scalar identity
in (4); or an unwarranted interchange of the t->0 QMS limit.
The proof is an internal analytic reconstruction, not formal verification
or external validation, and no novelty/priority claim is made.
