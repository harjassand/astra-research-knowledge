# An explicit parameter acquisition for the weighted recursive obstruction

7 October 2026. pde_transport9, verified Sol/max; no nested workers.
Status: independent analytic reconstruction of root's logical-flux lemma,
plus a conservative finite rational selector and precision ledger. Root,
pde_spectrum9 and pde_gate10 independently reconstructed the complete
quantitative selector as analytic PASS in their cycle17 gates. These internal
gates are not external validation. This is a theorem of
finite computability with severe costs, not an executed recursive-family
diagonalization or practical amplitude-acquisition claim.

## 1. Precise output and quantifiers

Given explicit binary check matrices of a finite one-logical-qubit actual
CSS member, and its original decoder frame, the construction below supplies

* a nontrivial pure-X logical circuit S of size d>=2;
* a physical q in S;
* rational weights 1<=h_a<2, with all valid syndrome energies separated by
  Delta>=2^(-(m+3)), where m is the number of original checks;
* strictly positive dyadic X fields on ALL N qubits, possibly using only
  two different extremely small amplitudes;
* exact counterpart-gap splitting at q for EVERY old syndrome, and total
  field norm small enough for the old-frame leakage theorem and channel gate.

All N, h_a and field parameters refer to the SAME finite actual code. The
weights and amplitudes may depend on N. They are a weighted-check extension
of the unit source and do not establish a result for equal weights or a
fixed intensive field. The algorithm uses only free local exact arithmetic
but can require exponential time/memory and exponentially many precision
bits. Actual acquisition of the full source matrices, enormous base decoder,
thermal samples and physical amplitude accuracy are additional inputs/costs.

## 2. The logical circuit is obtainable by elimination

Let B be a maximal independent set of columns of H_Z. For each nonbasis
column j, express it as a binary combination of B and take the kernel
vector f_j with j-coordinate one and those basis coefficients. The f_j
form a basis of ker H_Z. Each nonzero f_j has circuit support: the unique
relation in B union {j} leaves no nonempty proper dependent subset.

Since k=1, row(H_X) is a codimension-one subspace of ker H_Z. At least one
f_j is outside row(H_X). Select the first such vector with fixed ordering.
Its pure-X operator L=X^f_j is a nontrivial logical and its columns v_i
have exactly one nonempty dependence, sum_(i in S)v_i=0. Actual all-color
qubits and distance>1 exclude d=1. The circuit has d<=rank(H_Z)+1<=N.

This requires polynomial GF(2) elimination and membership work, conservatively
O(N^3) bit operations for bounded-degree explicit matrices. It avoids a
minimum-distance search. Selecting a different circuit changes which q and
how high an order d the flux proof uses; it does not reduce the source's
actual distance lower bound.

## 3. Independent reconstruction of the squarefree flux coefficient

Fix original X-check labels and L eigenvalue eta. On a circuit syndrome
orbit s+W, W=span{v_i}, dim W=d-1, the unperturbed energies are E_w and
the field translations T_i commute with product T_i=eta I. Every proper
nonempty subset of field labels has nonzero syndrome. Hence the only closed
squarefree walk uses all d labels. Intermediate resolvents exist for generic
independent check weights. The coefficient is

    [prod_(i in S) epsilon_i] lambda_(eta,s)
        =(-1)^d eta t_s,
    t_s=sum_(pi in permutations(S)) prod_(j=1)^(d-1)
          (E_s-E_(s+sum_(k<=j)v_pi(k)))^(-1).                    (1)

This can also be derived by expanding the simple eigenvalue resolvent with
its s-coordinate normalized to one. Lower squarefree eigenvalue corrections
would need a proper closed walk and vanish. Normalization corrections cannot
enter (1) for the same reason. The product of the physical Xi is L, not an
assumed geometric path winding. Fixed X-check energies are additive and
cancel from every denominator.

For positive h, every denominator in t_0 is negative and all d! terms have
the same sign, so t_0 is not zero. With D_s h=((-1)^(s_a)h_a)_a,

    E_(s+w)(h)-E_s(h)=E_w(D_s h),
    t_s(h)=t_0(D_s h).                                          (2)

These are rational identities; sign-flipped weights need not be physically
positive. The orbit trace is independent of the fields and eta, hence

    sum_(w in W)t_w=0.                                          (3)

Define K={w:t_0(D_w h)=t_0(h) identically}. This is a subgroup of W. If
K=W, equations (2)-(3) would force t_0=0, contradicting its positive-weight
sign. At least one generator lies outside K; in fact at least two do, since
sum_i v_i=0 forbids exactly one outside. Fix such a q. For EVERY ambient
valid syndrome s,

    Delta_s(h)=t_s-t_(s+v_q)
              =[t_0-t_(v_q)](D_s h)                             (4)

is a nonzero rational function. Thus the same q is visible for all s as an
algebraic statement, not just for the ground label. This independently
reconstructs root's trace/sign-covariance lemma. No claim that every q is
visible follows: spectrum's path circuit has exactly two visible endpoints.

When all circuit amplitudes are one common scalar z, the first eta-dependent
term is degree d and its coefficient in the counterpart-gap difference is
2(-1)^d Delta_s. Any eta-dependent field word must have every circuit label
odd; total degree d then forces the squarefree word. Lower-degree repeated
closed words have product I and are eta-independent. Thus specializing the
amplitudes to a common z does not silently cancel this first coefficient.

## 4. A bounded rational grid with a guaranteed energy spacing

There are R=2^(N-1) valid old joint syndromes. Some original checks can be
redundant; independent variables h_a are still assigned to their actual
positive penalties. Nonempty supports and total degree seven give m<=7N.
Take the explicit base

    b_a=1+2^(-(a+2)),  a=1,...,m,
    delta0=2^(-(m+2)),   xi=delta0/(8m).                         (5)

Every binary syndrome energy at b is distinct. If the integer violation
counts differ, their fractional correction differs by less than 1/4; if
the counts agree, finite binary fractional sums differ by at least delta0.
Therefore all valid energy gaps have magnitude at least delta0.

Let Kc=2^(d-1)-1. A circuit prefix visits distinct nonzero W elements, so
every summand in (1) has d-1 DISTINCT denominators. A common denominator
for t_s is

    D_s(h)=prod_(u in W excluding 0)(E_s-E_(s+u)),

of degree Kc. Its numerator has degree Kc-d+1. Hence each nonzero (4) has
a numerator polynomial P_s of degree at most 2Kc-d+1, using denominator
D_s D_(s+v_q). The product of all P_s is a nonzero polynomial of degree
at most R*2Kc<2^(N+d-1). No polynomial characteristic of a 2^N-dimensional
perturbed matrix is needed for this selector.

Set Lg=2^(N+d+2). Choose each h_a on the Lg-point grid

    h_a=b_a+xi j_a/Lg,   0<=j_a<Lg.                             (6)

A nonzero multivariate polynomial of degree less than Lg cannot vanish on
every point of this Cartesian grid (successive univariate interpolation).
Thus a point where ALL P_s are nonzero exists for a fixed visible q. At
every point, an energy difference changes by at most m xi=delta0/8, so

    Delta>=delta0/2=2^(-(m+3)),   1<h_a<2.                     (7)

An exact deterministic algorithm enumerates grid points and circuit sites q,
computes t_s-t_(s+v_q) for every valid s, and stops only when they are ALL
nonzero for one q. No need to know K symbolically. At least one such pair
is guaranteed. This finite search is very expensive. A randomized grid
selector could use Schwartz-Zippel, but no probabilistic success is invoked
by the deterministic theorem.

All weights have one common integer denominator

    D=2^(m+5) m Lg,

so their individual bit lengths are O(m+N+d+log m)=O(N), and the full
weight list is O(N^2) bits. This is a DESCRIPTION bound, not a claim that
their values can be found with polynomial work.

## 5. Nonzero coefficient size and computation costs

At the selected grid point let T=2mD. Each energy denominator equals a
nonzero integer n/D with |n|<=T. Bringing (1) to its common denominator gives

    t_s=D^(d-1) J_s/prod_(u!=0)n_(s,u),    J_s integer.

Consequently every nonzero difference (4) satisfies the conservative bound

    |Delta_s|>=T^(-2Kc)=:alpha.                                (8)

The common numerator cannot be silently assumed not to cancel; its exact
nonzero test is the stopping predicate in Section 4. Once nonzero, its
integer magnitude is at least one, which proves (8).

The d! permutations can be summed in O(d 2^d) rational operations per
syndrome by the subset recurrence

    F(empty)=1,
    F(P)=[sum_(i in P) F(P without i)]/(E_s-E_(s+v(P)))
                                     for 0<|P|<d,
    t_s=sum_(i in S)F(S without i).                             (9)

The denominator statement above bounds reduced rational bit sizes by
O(Kc(log T)+d log d). Using at most R syndromes, one grid point costs at
most O(d 2^(N+d)) such exact operations, plus polynomial enumeration work.
The conservative deterministic search has up to Lg^m=2^O(N^2) grid points.
These are exponential costs, not executed or feasible-large-N estimates.

## 6. A uniform analytic remainder over all finite syndrome branches

Keep only the circuit fields initially, all equal to z. Their perturbation
norm is at most d|z|. Put

    Rc=Delta/(8d).

For complex |z|<=Rc, the normal unperturbed matrix and a resolvent argument
put every perturbed eigenvalue within d|z| of some E_s. These disks are
disjoint. Riesz projections on surrounding circles have rank one in each
eta sector, so every continuing branch is analytic throughout this disk.
Its displacement from E_s is at most d|z|. Thus the counterpart difference

    F_s(z)=lambda_(s,+)-lambda_(s+v_q,+)
             -lambda_(s,-)+lambda_(s+v_q,-)

obeys |F_s(z)|<=4dRc=Delta/2 on the boundary, has no terms below degree d,
and has leading coefficient of magnitude at least 2alpha. Cauchy's bound
on the analytic remainder gives, for z<=Rc/2,

    |F_s(z)-a_(s,d)z^d|
       <=Delta (z/Rc)^(d+1).                                  (10)

Therefore a common positive rational z satisfying

    z<=alpha Rc^(d+1)/(4Delta)

has |F_s(z)|>=alpha z^d for EVERY s. This is a uniform finite-code margin
acquired from the rational coefficient, rather than an unquantified
analytic-zero avoidance argument. Complex spectral closeness is used only
inside the small normal-matrix resolvent disks; no nonnormal Weyl theorem
is presumed.

## 7. Positive fields on every remaining site and explicit dyadic bits

Choose fixed rational activity cap J_* within the independently gated
weighted Gibbs witness. Choose a rational norm cap v_* sufficient for the
old channel gate. At beta=253250, h_min=1,h_max=2 one safe, deliberately
very loose choice from b0<=126625 and gamma_shape=1/42 is

    J_*=2^(-10636530).

Indeed e<4 and 294 e beta<2^29 give
J_*<exp(-42b0)/(294 e beta). This retains the enormous base-family constant,
k>=228 and all repaired decoder/network hypotheses. The rate in the owned
leakage theorem is explicitly the NUMERATOR-TWO logistic rate
gamma(omega)=2/(1+exp(-beta omega)), with Gamma=2. Since h_max<2 and
v<=1/2, its window is below15 and gamma_min>=exp(-15 beta)>

has gamma_min>=(1/2)exp(-15 beta)>gamma_0 at this declared beta; the latter

    v_*=2^(-(30beta+27)),

which is below gamma_0/[128(1+2beta)] at this beta. These fixed constants
are not represented as good practical rates or amplitudes. Arbitrary other

A finite-Gaussian rate option needs no new proof or new parameter selector:

Let nD=m+3 so Delta=2^(-nD), let bT be the integer bit length of T, and set

    a_*=2^(-2Kc bT)<=alpha,
    cR=nD+3+ceil(log2 d),      Rc>=2^(-cR),
    Bz=max{2Kc bT+(d+1)cR+3,
           nD+7+ceil(log2 d), cR+1,
           bit_exponent(J_*)+1, bit_exponent(v_*)+2+ceil(log2 d)},
    z=2^(-Bz),             sigma_*=a_* z^d.                    (11)

These integer formulas ensure (10), z<=Rc/2, dz<=Delta/128,
dz<=v_*/4, and z<=J_*/2. The leading/remainder argument with a_* then gives
|F_s(z)|>=sigma_* for every s.

If N>d, give every outside site the common positive amplitude zeta with

    B_out=max{Bz+1, bit_exponent(J_*)+1,
              bit_exponent(sigma_*)+4+ceil(log2 N),
              nD+7+ceil(log2 N),
              bit_exponent(v_*)+2+ceil(log2 N)},
    zeta=2^(-B_out).                                           (12)

The outside perturbation norm w<=Nzeta is at most sigma_*/16,
Delta/128 and v_*/4. Real eigenvalue Weyl bounds change any counterpart
gap difference by at most 4w. Consequently all exact gaps still split,
with margin at least 3sigma_*/4; the total v<=Delta/64 and v<=v_*/2.
Every field is positive and below the admitted activity cap. If S already
contains all sites, the outside step is omitted.

The ACTIVE dyadic precision Bz is O(2^d(N+log N)+N^2+B_fixed). The outside
precision is O(d Bz+2^d(N+log N)); the extra factor d is important. Writing
binary denominators literally needs that many bits. A shared two-amplitude
description costs O(N^2+B_out); an expanded N-coefficient list can cost
O(N B_out+N^2). Writing a compact exponent does not acquire physical
control of a number with denominator 2^B_out. Neither the enormous bit
cost nor the selector time is suppressed as an uncharged family constant.

## 8. Exact downstream consequence and limit order

The resulting actual weighted recursive family has all-positive fields,
arbitrarily small total perturbation norm, and the proved small NEW Gibbs
witness mass at the fixed admitted temperature. Its original decoder's
bare protection/coherent commutator can still be exponentially small. Yet
the unchanged X_q bath's exact full-H Davies leakage is at least
gamma_0/4, uniformly in N. The old thermal encoder and old final decoder
then incur the strictly positive N-independent scaled-time error in the
companion channel gate. Other cross-pair resonances are irrelevant to that
old-frame theorem, because the individual eigenvector error is paid.

The exact splitter is at least of order sigma_*=2^(-2Kc bT-dBz). Resolving
it with an infinite secular projector is a different interface from a
finite observation window. A microscopic weak-coupling construction must
place its coupling below the relevant resonance scale and account for
physical time; those N-dependent constants are not supplied here. The
finite-system exact Davies limit is taken FIRST at each positive set of
parameters. No physical exponential-time lifetime or failure is inferred.

There is still no conclusion for unit check weights, a fixed nonvanishing
intensive field, all logical frames, or all recoveries. Those are decisive
next falsifiers. Root's trace lemma and our matrix/selector/channel use are
deductions, not BDL author claims. Prior art is the primary CSS/decoder and
Davies/GNS framework, with standard analytic perturbation and polynomial
grid avoidance; historical novelty of the combination is UNKNOWN.
