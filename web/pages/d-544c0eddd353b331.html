SU3 WALL: EXACT THREE-ROOT JOINT FILTERS AND CONSTRUCTIVE GIBBS UPPERS
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; independent audit requested.
These are upper comparators, NOT a claimed matching all-matrix profile.

Main result. For a>=b>=1,N=a+b, the actual principal-height positive
filter truncates three root directions jointly. For R<=b it has rank
Theta(R^3), and its full Gibbs risk is at most C_(H,h1)/(NR), uniformly
in a/b. Thus a concrete legal code gives e*_Q<=C/(NQ^(1/3)) through
Q~(b+1)^3. The earlier whole-fiber filter gives the succeeding comparator
C sqrt(b+1)/(N sqrtQ) on its exact range. Joint and whole-fiber holes
also give rigorous complementary near-full-rank upper bounds below.
No exponent or order is asserted as optimal without an all-matrix lower.

This proof uses exact root commutators, adjoint covariance, weight-shell
counts and the already proved ordered full-exponential estimate. It
does not assume a classical flag-volume limit, a coupled-spin tensor
factorization or iid transport.

1. OPERATIONAL CONTRACT AND THE GENERAL MIXED UPPER

All notation and exact SU3 tensor identities are as in the preceding
wall notes. Supply V_(a,b), a>=b>=1,N=a+b and fixed nonzero fundamental
H=sum x_A t_A, r=||x||. Consider tau_g=exp[(h/N)T_H,g]/Z for h in any
fixed positive compact interval. Full density recovery is on the same
carrier, with half-trace risk; maximum quantum branch Q is charged and
classical labels are free. No assistance, g-oracle or sector transport.

Write E_T,E_S,E_TS, S=(D-alphaT)/N, C_S=Delta/N^2, S_F=TrF^2 and
D_F=int[F_g,[F_g,.]]dg. The complete two-adjoint block gives

 ||D_F(T_H/N)||1 <=2r E_T(F)/N^2
                         +r |E_TS(F)|/(N sqrt(C_S)).       (1.1)

For a positive F the actual covariant branch-rank filter code satisfies

 e(Phi_F;h) <= exp(4rh)/(4S_F)
  {h||D_F(T_H/N)||1+(r^2/8)h^2 E_T(F)/N^2}.               (1.2)

Equation(1.2) is the full ordered-exponential bound, not an inference
from the first generator alone. Let gamma=r^2/8. For explicit matrix
F, exact finite positive Choi cubature exists. Its efficient acquisition
and circuit precision are separate from this ideal existence result.

2. EXACT PRINCIPAL-HEIGHT SHELLS

Let J=pi(diag(1,0,-1)), whose largest eigenvalue in V_(a,b) is N.
Let K_t be its entire eigenspace with eigenvalue N-t. The defect t is
an integer from0 to2N. The simple roots12,23 lower J by1, and root13
lowers it by2. The Weyl character at this principal specialization is

 sum_t g_t z^t =
   (1-z^(a+1))(1-z^(b+1))(1-z^(N+2))
                     /[(1-z)^2(1-z^2)].                 (2.1)

This follows by substituting the three diagonal arguments z,1,z^-1
into the U(3) Weyl-character alternant and removing the leading z^-N.
Equivalently its stable coefficients are the ordered PBW monomials
in the three lowering roots of heights1,1,2; the exact alternant
accounts for the first highest-weight subtractions at a+1,b+1.
In particular, for 0<=t<=b<=a,

  g_t=dim K_t=sum_(j=0)^floor(t/2)(t-2j+1)
             =floor((t+2)^2/4),
  (t+1)^2/4 <= g_t <= (t+1)^2.                            (2.2)

These are dimensions of actual carrier eigenspaces. Only this exact
stable interval is used below; no large-t continuation is assumed.

3. ROOT CROSSING RECURRENCES, INCLUDING THE SECOND TENSOR

For a positive root alpha=(i,j),i<j, let l_alpha be1 for12,23 and2
for13. Set T_alpha=E_ij in the U(3) lift and S_alpha the complex
adjoint tensor with the same fundamental matrix argument. These obey
T_alpha^dagger=T_-alpha,S_alpha^dagger=S_-alpha and

 [T_alpha,T_-alpha]=T_Halpha,
 [S_alpha,T_-alpha]=S_Halpha,
 Halpha=E_ii-E_jj.                                       (3.1)

The second equality is exact adjoint covariance. Both root operators
raise J by l_alpha, so neither maps into a negative-defect shell.
Define for t>=0

 w_alpha(t)=Tr_(K_t)(T_alpha T_-alpha)>=0,
 c_alpha(t)=Re Tr_(K_t)(S_alpha T_-alpha),                 (3.2)

and set them zero for t<0. Taking shell trace of (3.1), and using
cyclicity between the two shell spaces, gives exact recurrences

 w_alpha(t)-w_alpha(t-l_alpha)=Tr_(K_t) T_Halpha,
 c_alpha(t)-c_alpha(t-l_alpha)=Tr_(K_t) S_Halpha.           (3.3)

For example the opposite crossing in the second line is
ReTr_(K_t)(T_-alpha S_alpha); their difference is the commutator
in(3.1). This orientation is necessary; replacing it by a conjugate
within the same shell would lose the recursion.

The fundamental coefficient norm of Halpha is2. Scalar sums of
generator/tensor squares and C2<=4N^2/3 therefore imply

 |Tr_(K_t)T_Halpha| <=4N g_t,
 |Tr_(K_t)S_Halpha| <=2 sqrt(C_S) g_t.                     (3.4)

For t<=b, summing (3.3) along its residue class and using(2.2) gives

 w_alpha(t) <=4N sum_(j>=0,t-jl>=0) g_(t-jl)
             <=4N(t+1)^3,
 |c_alpha(t)| <=2 sqrt(C_S)(t+1)^3.                        (3.5)

The inequality sum_(s=0)^t(s+1)^2<=(t+1)^3 proves the final bounds.
No assumption of a uniformly conditioned second-adjoint Gram is used.

For a real function F=f(t)I on K_t, Cartan components of T and S
commute with F, because they preserve J. The two Hermitian generators
of each root pair contribute one (real mixed) weight per undirected
edge. Consequently the exact energies are

 E_T(F)=sum_(alpha>0,t>=0) w_alpha(t)[f_t-f_(t+l_alpha)]^2,
 E_TS(F)=sum_(alpha>0,t>=0) c_alpha(t)[f_t-f_(t+l_alpha)]^2. (3.6)

All varying-edge starting shells in the constructions below lie in
the stable interval. Endpoints may be just outside it; (3.3) already
computes their total crossing weight from the starting shell and does
not require an unproved endpoint count.

4. ACTUAL POSITIVE JOINT TAPER: RANK AND TWO ENERGIES

For integer 1<=R<=b set F_R=f_R(J) with

  f_t=R-t for0<=t<R, f_t=0 for t>=R.                      (4.1)

This is an explicitly supplied positive matrix filter, with exact rank
Q_R=sum_(t=0)^(R-1)g_t. Its HS norm is
S_F=sum_(t=0)^(R-1)g_t(R-t)^2. Equation(2.2) gives

  R^3/12 <=Q_R<=R^3,
  S_F >=(1/4)sum_(t=0)^(R-1)(t+1)^2(R-t)^2 >=R^5/120.    (4.2)

For the last inequality the exact polynomial sum is
R(R+1)(R+2)(R^2+2R+2)/30. Each edge difference is at most l_alpha.
Since sum_(alpha>0)l_alpha^2=6, (3.5)--(3.6) imply

 E_T(F_R)<=6N R^2(R+1)^2<=24N R^4,
 |E_TS(F_R)|<=3sqrt(C_S)R^2(R+1)^2<=12sqrt(C_S)R^4,
 E_T/S_F<=2880N/R,
 |E_TS|/S_F<=1440sqrt(C_S)/R.                             (4.3)

These estimates include the actual spin-fiber commutators. They do not
preserve the entire b-fiber or ignore the roots23. The mixed bound
comes from its own exact recurrence, not Cauchy followed by a
degenerating energy/Gram constant.

Equations(1.1)--(1.2),(4.3) give the full density upper

 e(Phi_FR;h) <= C_joint(H,h)/[N R],
 C_joint=exp(4rh)[7200rh+2880gamma h^2]/4.                 (4.4)

Every constant is fixed before a,b,N,Q. It is deliberately loose.
For any1<=Q<=min(d,(b+1)^3), choose
R=min(b,floor(Q^(1/3))). Then Q_R<=Q and R>=Q^(1/3)/2, so

  e*_Q(h)<=2C_joint(H,h)/(NQ^(1/3)).                      (4.5)

This is a rigorous constructive upper. The exponent1/3 is not
asserted as a lower bound or optimal law from the shell dimension.

5. WHOLE-FIBER SATURATION: THE SECOND CONSTRUCTIVE RANGE

The exact top-E11 filter in FIBER_PRESERVING_FILTERS.txt has
rank(b+1)R(R+1)/2 and error<=C_fiber/(N(R+1)) forR<=floor(a/2).
If a>=8(b+1), the range fromQ~(b+1)^3 up to

  Q_cap=(b+1)floor(a/2)(floor(a/2)+1)/2                    (5.1)

is nonempty. Choosing the largest admitted R gives

  e*_Q(h)<=C_(H,h1)sqrt(b+1)/(N sqrtQ),
                        (b+1)^3<=Q<=Q_cap.              (5.2)

At the crossover Q~(b+1)^3, the two ACTUAL upper comparators agree
up to constants. ForQ>Q_cap the largest whole-fiber taper still
gives O(1/N^2), but does not capture near-full precision. No rank
allocation optimum or independent tensor-factor model is inferred.

6. JOINT NEAR-FULL-RANK HOLE COMPARATOR

For integer1<=R<=floor(b/4), define positive F by

 f_t=0 for0<=t<R,
 f_t=(t-R+1)/R forR<=t<2R,
 f_t=1 for t>=2R.                                        (6.1)

Its nullity is exactly Q_R from(4.2), so its rank is d-Q_R. Every
edge difference is at most l_alpha/R, and all varying-edge starting
shells have t<2R<=b. Thus (3.5)--(3.6) give

 E_T(F)<=24N(2R+1)^2<=216N R^2,
 |E_TS(F)|<=12sqrt(C_S)(2R+1)^2<=108sqrt(C_S)R^2.           (6.2)

There is no small denominator in the norm: d>=(b+1)^3 and the total
dimension of shells t<2R is at most (2R)^3<=d/8. Hence
S_F>=7d/8>=d/2. Equations(1.1)--(1.2) yield

 e(Phi_F;h)<=C_hole(H,h) R^2/(N d),
 C_hole=exp(4rh)[1080rh+432gamma h^2]/4.                   (6.3)

For b>=32 and any1<=k<=floor(b^3/32768), choose the leastR withQ_R>=k.
The rank is then<=d-k. Minimality and(4.2) give
R<=1+(12k)^(1/3)<=b/4, and R^2<=4(12k)^(2/3). Therefore

  e*_(d-k)(h)<=C_(H,h1) k^(2/3)/(N d).                   (6.4)

These cutoffs are conservative finite choices. In particular this
gives a concrete single-hole code for sufficiently large positive b.
It is NOT a proof that coherent holes optimize any all-matrix minimum.

7. WHOLE-FIBER NEAR-FULL-RANK HOLE COMPARATOR

Apply the same plateau/ramp profile(6.1) to TOP E11 shells instead,
with1<=R<=floor(a/4). Its exact nullity is
k_R=(b+1)R(R+1)/2. The varying edges lie at t<2R<=a/2, so the mixed
cancellation of the whole-fiber note applies. Its ordinary conductance
is bounded by(3a/2)(b+1)(t+1)(t+2). Therefore

 E_T(F)<=12a(b+1)R,
 S_F>=d/2,
 e(Phi_F;h)<=C_(H,h1)(b+1)R/(N d).                       (7.1)

For the norm use d>=(b+1)a^2/2 and dimension of shells t<2R at most
3(b+1)R^2<=3d/8. For the energy sum use
sum_(t=0)^(2R-1)(t+1)(t+2)=2R(2R+1)(2R+2)/3<=8R^3.

For a prescribed nullity1<=k<=k_floor(a/4), choose the leastR withk_R>=k.
If k>=b+1, its minimality gives (b+1)R<=C sqrt((b+1)k), with a universal
constant. Thus throughout that exact range,

  e*_(d-k)(h)<=C_(H,h1)sqrt((b+1)k)/(N d).                (7.2)

For k<b+1 the same construction has an overshoot of b+1 and instead
gives C(b+1)/(Nd); do not replace that by(7.2). The joint construction
handles the smaller holes on its own scope. The high-/low-hole upper
comparators agree at k~b^3 when both are admitted. For larger k, or
near the endpoints of these finite cutoffs, use the admitted actual
construction or the identity code; no extrapolated all-Q profile.

8. WHAT REMAINS PROOF-GATED

The wall weighted arbitrary-code converse is already proved in
WEIGHTED_POSTERIOR_CONVERSE.txt for regular H and sufficiently small
fixed supplied h. Its all-matrix rank functional is

  Lambda_wall(Q)=inf_rankQ [E_T/N^2+E_S/(N sqrt(C_S))]/||A||^2.

To turn any upper comparator above into an optimal law one must still
prove the matching ALL-matrix/Kraus lower, for example a uniform
spectral projector count for the weighted operator. The ordinary
SU3 Casimir character profile controls E_T, not E_S's action inside
the growing multiplicity spaces. Scalar covariance does not supply
that weighted spectral count. A joint-filter rank of R^3 is an upper
construction only. Neither an anisotropic classical volume nor the
coupled spin/transverse local picture establishes this missing gate.

No efficient all-Q optimizer or finite cubature compiler, formal or
external validation, historical priority, unknown-g acquisition, or
weak-to-polarized/iid collision transport is asserted. All preceding
results are preserved. verify_joint_height.py supplies exact character,
root-trace, energy and independent matrix diagnostics on this scope.
