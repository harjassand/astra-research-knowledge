C04_S01 -- EXACT FUNDAMENTAL SU(d>=3) CENTRAL-SEED OBSTRUCTION
7 October 2026 UTC. Initial phase-two derivation, no compiler or asymptotic
source theorem imported. This is a route obstruction, not entanglement evidence.

Fix an integer d>=3 and any Tr-orthonormal traceless Hermitian basis T_a,
a=1,...,d^2-1. On (C^d)^tensorN define F_a=sum_i T_a^(i), C=sum F_a^2,
Z_N(alpha)=Tr exp(alpha C/N), and rho_N(alpha)=exp(alpha C/N)/Z_N(alpha).
The susceptibility-critical isotropic value is alpha_c=d/2 (quadratic entropy
cancellation at I/d). This label does not assert that alpha_c is the global
thermodynamic phase-transition point in higher dimension.

Take the local density eta with spectrum ((d-1)/d,1/[d(d-1)],...,1/[d(d-1)]).
Direct calculation gives
 S(eta)=logd-(d-2)log(d-1)/d,
 q_*^2=Tr(eta-I/d)^2=(d-2)^2/[d(d-1)].
Completeness sum T_a^2=(d^2-1)I/d implies, for c0=(d^2-1)/d,
 Tr eta^tensorN C=Nc0+N(N-1)q_*^2.
The Gibbs variational principle therefore yields

 logZ_N(alpha_c)-Nlogd
 >=N Delta_d+alpha_c(c0-q_*^2)>=N Delta_d,
 Delta_d=(d-2)^2/[2(d-1)]-(d-2)log(d-1)/d>0.             (C1)

To see strict positivity put x=d-1>1. The bracket inequality is
x-1/x>2logx; its difference vanishes at x=1 and has derivative
(x-1)^2/x^2>0. Numerically Delta_3=1/4-log2/3, about0.01895; acceptance
does not rely on that decimal.

Convexity of logZ_N in alpha, and logZ_N(0)=Nlogd, gives

 <C>_(rho_N(alpha_c))/N = (d/dalpha)logZ_N(alpha_c)
 >=[logZ_N(alpha_c)-logZ_N(0)]/alpha_c
 >=N Delta_d/alpha_c.

Hence <C>>=N^2 Delta_d/alpha_c, a finite-N inequality. Suppose ANY positive
identical-product representation of this state is supplied:
 rho_N(alpha_c)=integral theta^tensorN dmu_N(theta).
Taking this observable gives exactly

 E_mu Tr(theta-I/d)^2
 >=[N^2 Delta_d/alpha_c-Nc0]/[N(N-1)],                 (C2)

for N>=2. If no such representation is supplied, the claim about every
representation is simply conditional on its existence; the separate global
isotropic diffusion in FULL_RANK_STOPPING.txt also gives one.

Set q0^2=Delta_d/(2alpha_c)=Delta_d/d. For
 N>=max(2,ceil[4alpha_c c0/Delta_d]), (C2) is >=3Delta_d/(4alpha_c).
Every local density has Tr(theta-I/d)^2<=(d-1)/d. Therefore

 P_mu(||theta-I/d||_2^2>q0^2)
 >= [3Delta_d/(4alpha_c)-q0^2]/[(d-1)/d-q0^2]
 >= Delta_d/[2(d-1)]>0.                                (C3)

The second inequality deliberately enlarges the denominator to (d-1)/d.
Thus a fixed positive fraction lies outside a fixed HS ball, for every exact
positive iid representation of the seed and all sufficiently large N. No
shrinking-radius concentration at I/d, and in particular no concentration
at an N^-1/4 critical scale, is possible. The obstruction survives changing
the particular mixture law; it is witnessed by the actual state's Casimir.

The qubit quartic-critical entropy has no such cubic direction. For d>=3,
the local entropy expansion is
 S(I/d+q)=logd-(d/2)Trq^2+(d^2/6)Trq^3+O(||q||^4),
which explains the route failure but is not used in (C1)-(C3). A full
classification of the higher-dimensional transition is not claimed.

The direct full-rank stopping construction does not require a central seed,
so (C3) is fully compatible with its exp[-cNlog^2N] positive-product
approximation and exact full-separability absorption. This result proves no
entanglement lower bound, no absence of a different critical decoder, and no
generic failure of compact-Lie models. Priority and external audit UNKNOWN.
