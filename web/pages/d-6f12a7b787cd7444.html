Claim: For finite-dimensional H, any pure-state family F, and one full-domain channel C:H->H tensor H whose marginals approximate every family member within half-trace error b, the report constructs one EB channel with uniform half-trace error at most kappa(b)+(1-kappa(b))14b for 0<=b<=1/4096. Here delta=4 sqrt(b), eta=(3/2)((1-4delta)^(-1/2)-1), a=delta+eta, kappa=(sqrt(a eta)+sqrt(2b))^2, and f(b)=(208+16 sqrt(6))b+O(b^(3/2)); linear order is optimal.
Interface: Finite-dimensional input, arbitrary pure-state family, one full-domain channel, and a uniform two-marginal broadcasting promise for every family member. The conclusion is statewise half-trace reconstruction, not diamond closeness.
Proof spine: A Stinespring dilation yields an approximate Schur idempotence relation. A convergent binomial correction gives an idempotent Schur mask; its norm forces an equivalence-relation partition. One common POVM distinguishes blocks, and representative-state preparation gives the EB channel and modulus.
Costs: For an explicitly supplied rational list of n states in dimension d, overlap formation costs O(n^2 d^2); the SDP has O(k d^2) real variables and k PSD blocks of order d. Unknown-state acquisition and device costs are excluded.
Failure: Does not cover general mixed families or establish dimension-free runtime or experimental access. Supplied finite SDP checks are not proof validation or external review.
Closest comparator: The premise differs from the stronger complete near-idempotence condition of N533. Standard broadcasting SDPs predate this candidate.
