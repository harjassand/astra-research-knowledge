# What the filling anchor supplies to a graph-stability interface

7 October 2026, pde_transport9; independent input conversion only.
**Conditional conversion PASS; enlarged-graph theorem legality is not
gated here.** Root and metric_acquisition own that separate proof audit.

Primary prior: De Roeck, Khemani, Li, O'Dea and Rakovszky,
arXiv2411.02384v3,12August2026. Sections2.1–2.4 were read from the
versioned primary PDF/text. Its check graph is defined by actual
shared Pauli support; its TQO-II uses a graph buffer proportional to
the cardinality of a connected check set; its perturbation norm uses
a minimum connected qubit set. A geometrically nearby but originally
disconnected pair does not satisfy these graph hypotheses by proximity.
Our filling gate alone is therefore not an invocation of that theorem.

The following makes the required bridge precise. Suppose an admissible
extension uses declared supports of physical diameter at mostD, with
bounded declared check and qubit weights. Identity padding may be used
only if the theorem's entire word/norm/commutator proof remains valid
for that extension; this is a separate required premise. Suppose also
its check graph has a routing bound: a check at physical distance t
from a starting check can be reached within L(t+1) graph steps in the
region in question. Neither routing nor declared-support legality
follows from bounded upper density.

A connected check set S has its union of declared supports inside a
ball of radius at most2D|S| around a site of a starting check: traverse
a spanning tree, paying at most2D per edge. A global stabilizer p
supported in that union is therefore generated by original memory
checks inside the radius2DC|S| ball, by our ambient proportional
filling gate. Declared padding of those filler checks costs at mostD
more physical distance, and their centers at most1 more. Routing then
places every selected check in the graph buffer of S of radius

    ell_graph |S|, with ell_graph=O(LDC+LD+L).       (1)

This verifies the required local/global stabilizer inclusion UNDER
those explicit declared-support and routing premises. It does not
verify the whole theorem after changing its graph definition.

Decoupled single-qubit Z ancillas on a bounded-density ambient grid
can supply routing if declared supports connect appropriate nearby
grid sites and memory checks. For a stabilizer p of the product code,
factor the memory stabilizer and the selected ancilla Z's. Each factor
has support contained in p's original support; the ancilla factors
already fill at their sites. Thus the same inclusion(1) is available.
The grid changes the physical Hamiltonian to a product extension; its
unperturbed logical space and distance equal those of the memory, but
this is not a claim about the original Tanner adjacency unchanged.

The all-closed anchor also supplies the distance input independently
of the source's asserted exponent: d_i>=floor(lambda^(i-1)/2)+2.
All coordinates lie in a box of diameter O(lambda^i), since each
perturbation adds at most alpha lambda and every fine cell lies in a
parent hull. A full unit grid in that box has O(lambda^(3i)) sites.
The number of memory qubits is at most K(c ell²)^i by the bounded
degree and local-block dimensions. Therefore an augmented N'_i
satisfies log N'_i<=K0+K1 i log ell, with fixed constants. Since
lambda>=2 gives lambda^(i-1)>=i, the distance bound implies
d_i>=c log N'_i for some positive depth-independent c. The seed,
if included, is a finite separate adjustment to c.

Constants C,L,D,c may be extremely unfavorable and are not discarded.
For fixed-range perturbation terms, norm conversion to a Pauli sum
also pays the number of qubits in each physical ball and the connected
cover's graph size. General operator-norm exponential spatial decay
is not automatically the stringent exponential connected-weight norm
of this primary. Those costs and all KAM uses remain in the separate
metric_acquisition audit.

Exact outstanding question: can the primary's graph-support proof be
extended to the declared identity supports with these routing premises,
including its word bounds, ghost terms and commutator locality? Until
that is answered, the scoped actual anchor plus(1) is a promising
downstream interface, not a generic spectral stability theorem.
