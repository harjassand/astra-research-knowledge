## Exact ultrapower/bidual compatibility

- Stefan Heinrich, “Ultraproducts in Banach space theory,” *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* 313 (1980), 72–104, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1980.313.72), [digitized article](https://www.digizeitschriften.de/download/pdf/243919689_0313/log9.pdf).
