## Analytic-map reconstruction

Let d=64, w=(1,...,1)^T and let A_a run over the 12 real symmetric local Pauli generators. Choose real orthogonal 8x8 matrices V,W from the rational Cayley transforms of the explicit integer skew matrices in the witness JSON. Form U_0=(V tensor W)U_Bell, where the columns of U_Bell are vec(P)/sqrt(8), P in {I,X,Z,J}^{tensor3}, J=[[0,1],[-1,0]]. These 64 real orthogonal matrices P are orthogonal in the Frobenius inner product. Each column is maximally entangled across the 3|3 cut. Thus every column has reduced state I/8 on each half, and diag(U_0^T A_a U_0)=0 exactly for every local generator.

For real s near zero define C_s,ii=1 and C_s,ij=2s^{|i-j|}/(1+s^{2|i-j|}) for i!=j, indexing i=0,...,63. This is real analytic at s=0, where it is the identity-entry Schur filter (diagonal projection). For 0<s<1 it is precisely the physical SLD filter associated to p_i=s^{2i}/sum_j s^{2j}. The use of s=0 as an analytic basepoint does not require a faithful physical state there; the eventual witness has strictly positive s.

Let B_a(U)=U^T A_a U and K_a(U,s)=C_s Hadamard B_a(U). Define r_a=K_aw and F_a=r_a-(w^T r_a/d)w. Then sum_i F_ai=0 for every U,s. Keep only entries i=0,...,62 for each generator, obtaining 12*63=756 real constraints. Deleting one entry is valid for every s, not just the limiting diagonal map. F(U_0,0)=0.

Use local orthogonal coordinates U=U_0 exp(Omega), Omega^T=-Omega. There are 64*63/2=2016 real coordinates. At s=0, for coordinate Omega=E_ij-E_ji,

d B_a /dt=[B_a,Omega], and its diagonal derivative is -2(B_a)_ij at i, +2(B_a)_ij at j.

The derivative of the subtracted mean is zero at s=0 because tr(B_a)=tr(A_a)=0 throughout this orthogonal conjugation. This is the exact 756x2016 rational Jacobian used for the minor. No missing imaginary constraints arise: U, A_a and C_s are real, so K_a is real symmetric and common-w eigenvector equations are real. The unused local Y_i generators would introduce a different interface and are not covered by this construction.

## IFT and equal-score state

Fix all unselected Omega coordinates at zero. The 756 selected coordinates give an invertible derivative of F with respect to these variables. The real analytic implicit function theorem therefore supplies a real analytic Omega(s) near zero with Omega(0)=0 and F(U_0 exp(Omega(s)),s)=0. There is a nonzero interval of positive s values. For these s, all dressed K_a have w as an eigenvector. Symmetry then implies P_w=ww^T/d commutes with every K_a.

In the eigenbasis set R=(1-1/d)I+P_w, D=diag(sqrt(p_i)), sigma=U D^2 U^T and rho=U D R D U^T. R is strictly positive, its diagonal entries are one, so tr(rho)=sum_i p_i=1. rho is faithful and distinct from sigma because D R D has nonzero offdiagonal entries sqrt(p_i p_j)/d. The exact SLD fiber theorem then gives equal scores for all fixed physical local generators. Their ordinary commutant remains CI because the generators themselves have never changed. No frame-irreducibility inference under a Schur filter is used.

This proves existence, not an explicit numerical radius for s or a practically well-conditioned witness. Large spectral separation is built in. H(s)=-log(sigma(s)) is a dense six-qubit block Hamiltonian, not generally a two-local Hamiltonian. To state bounded coefficients at a finite inverse temperature, shift/rescale H and compensate with beta; any claimed fixed-H all-temperature family would require a separate argument since U(s) varies along the IFT branch.
