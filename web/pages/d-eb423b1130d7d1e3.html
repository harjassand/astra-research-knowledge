Cycle07 primary-source interface memo — 2026-10-09

Question and disposition
------------------------
Two transfers were checked: whether a universal weak p=1 entropy-production
bound yields weak Lp regularity for every p, and whether known KMS-twisted
derivation formulas compare entropy production to the root Dirichlet energy.
The first implication is UNKNOWN in the checked primary literature. The
second yields a concrete proof interface, but no checked theorem closes it.
This memo makes no priority or novelty claim.

1. The p=1 endpoint does not come with an all-p interpolation theorem
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kastoryano–Temme (KT), arXiv:1207.3261v2, use a primitive finite-dimensional
Liouvillian L with stationary faithful sigma and T_t=e^{tL}. Their convention
is Gamma_sigma(X)=sigma^(1/2) X sigma^(1/2),
<A,B>_sigma=Tr[Gamma_sigma(A) B], and detailed balance is
Gamma_sigma L=L^* Gamma_sigma. Write I_{p,q} for their weighted power map.
Definition 7 / Proposition 8 give

 E_p(f)= -p/(2(p-1)) <I_{q,p}(f),L(f)>_sigma,
 E_1(f)= -1/2 Tr[Gamma_sigma(L(f)) (log Gamma_sigma(f)-log sigma)],
 E_2(f)= -<f,L(f)>_sigma,  with 1/p+1/q=1.

Their Definition 9 says “weakly Lp-regular” only when, for every p>=1 and
every f,

 E_p(f) >= E_2(I_{2,p}(f))                         (1<=p<=2),
 E_p(f) >= (p-1) E_2(I_{2,p}(f))                   (p>=2).

Thus a p=1 endpoint establishes only the p=1 instance of the first line.
After the target's rho=Gamma_sigma(f) change of variables and consistent
Schrodinger/Heisenberg conjugation, the proposed J>=2E is the relevant
endpoint-shaped inequality; the identity E_1=J/2 and E_2(I_{2,1}f)=E must
be checked under that exact conjugation, not by identifying raw HS and KMS
forms. This memo does not claim that conversion as a new theorem.

KT Lemma 18 provides a sufficient all-p route, but with a materially stronger
premise. For every positive g and t>=0 require convexity on the entire interval
s in [0,2] of

 h(s)=Tr[sigma^(s/4) g^(2-s) sigma^(s/4)
          T_t(sigma^(-s/4) g^s sigma^(-s/4))].

With p=2/(2-s), its two secants from h(0)=h(2)=||f||_(2,sigma)^2 yield the
weak regularity inequalities for p in [1,2] (s in [0,1]) and p in [2,infty)
(s in [1,2)); every finite p is covered. KT explicitly state that this
convexity condition is sufficient but not necessary (after Lemma 18), and
Conjecture 19 proposes weak Lp regularity for every primitive Liouvillian.
Neither result says that the p=1 inequality alone implies convexity of h or
the p>1 inequalities. No counterexample to that endpoint-to-all-p implication
was found in this bounded check, so its status remains UNKNOWN.

2. Exact KMS forms and the missing entropy comparison
----------------------------------------------------
For the target's finite-dimensional physical channel, take the Heisenberg
adjoint and its positive generator L=I-G (continuous-time convention:
T_t=e^{-tL}). Vernooij–Wirth (VW), arXiv:2303.15949v2, assume a faithful
reference state rho and a KMS-symmetric QMS on M_n. The KMS inner product is
<A,B>_rho=Tr[A* rho^(1/2) B rho^(1/2)]; their modular automorphism is
sigma_z(A)=rho^(iz) A rho^(-iz). Theorem 2.4 gives a Hilbert bimodule and
twisted derivation delta with

 delta(AB)=pi_l(sigma_(-i/4)(A)) delta(B)
           +pi_r(sigma_(i/4)(B)) delta(A),
 <A,L(B)>_rho=<delta(A),delta(B)>.

Theorem 2.5 specializes this, for finite M_n, to a finite family with
{V_j}={V_j*} and

 <A,L(B)>_rho = sum_j <[V_j,A],[V_j,B]>_rho.       (VW23, Thm. 2.5)

This is the exact KMS Dirichlet-form mechanism. It does not state a chain rule
for log rho or sqrt rho and does not compare entropy production with the
root's square-root energy.

VW's 2026 preprint, arXiv:2610.09678v1 (submitted 7 Oct 2026), sharpens the
structural distinction. Theorem 3.9 represents a quantum Dirichlet form by a
modularly covariant derivation; its analytic half-step V_(-i/2) is accretive.
It is positive self-adjoint iff the generator strongly commutes with the
modular operator (the GNS-symmetric subclass). Theorem 4.4 gives, for bounded
completely Dirichlet forms on S^2(H) relative to rho^(1/2), the exact form

 E(A)=sum_j integral_R ||V_j rho^(it) A rho^(-it)
                         -rho^(it) A rho^(-it) V_j*||_2^2
                          2 dt/cosh(2 pi t),

where V_j is in dom(sigma_(i/2)) and sigma_(i/2)(V_j)=V_j*. In finite
dimension the analytic-domain and convergence requirements are automatic
for bounded V_j. This is prior art for the root's finite modular-orbit
representation, subject to identifying its K_j family with a family
implementing the same bounded Dirichlet form. In root notation s=sqrt(sigma),
K_j=s^(1/2)B_j s^(-1/2) with B_j=B_j* satisfies
sigma_(i/2)(K_j)=K_j*. Setting the source integration variable u=-t/2 gives
rho^(iu)q rho^(-iu)=s^(-it)q s^(it)=q_t and transforms
2 du/cosh(2 pi u) into sech(pi t) dt. Thus the source's coefficient-one
form is exactly twice the parent-supplied E(q) normalization; equivalently,
rescaling each source jump by 1/sqrt(2) gives the parent normalization.
The orbit-form representation itself is therefore not a new opportunity.
Theorem 4.4 still supplies no entropy-resolvent estimate.

The precise remaining interface is therefore one of the following equivalent
proof tasks in the supplied target coordinates: (a) compare the known KMS
modular-orbit commutator form for the square-root input with the weighted
entropy-resolvent integral below, with a universal constant and exact
Schrodinger/Heisenberg conjugation; or (b) prove positivity of the supplied
Hermitian superoperator M in the B-representation for every faithful sigma
and rho. Neither cited source asserts either result.

For context only, the parent supplied this candidate entropy representation
from a separate ongoing derivation (not a theorem verified by this literature
memo):

 X_lambda=(L_rho+lambda R_sigma)^(-1)(rho-sigma),
 J=integral_0^infty lambda/(1+lambda)^2 I_lambda d lambda,
 I_lambda=Tr rho Gamma_L(X_lambda*,X_lambda*)
          +lambda Tr sigma Gamma_L(X_lambda,X_lambda).

Here Gamma_L and this identity are parent-supplied candidate interface. The
concrete opportunity is to insert VW's KMS modular-orbit representation into
these two Gamma_L terms and seek a pointwise or integrated lower bound by E.
The missing lemma is exactly that comparison; merely knowing each I_lambda
is nonnegative does not compare it to E. Equivalently, in the supplied root
gate, the missing sign is M>=0 for

 M=(L_s R_W+L_W R_s-L_r R_z-L_z R_r)/2 + 2 L_q R_q,

with s=sqrt(sigma), q=sqrt(rho), r=s^(-1/2)q^2s^(-1/2),
z=s^(1/2)(log(q^2)-2log(s))s^(1/2), and
W=2 Lyap_s^(-1)(({r,z}/2)-2q^2), Lyap_s(X)=sX+Xs. This target expression
and its claimed identity J-2E=<B,MB> were supplied by the parent, not derived
or validated here. The available primary papers do not establish its sign.

3. Why the ordinary/GNS chain rule cannot fill the gap
------------------------------------------------------
Carlen–Maas, arXiv:1609.01254v3, use inner products
<A,B>_s=Tr[sigma^s A* sigma^(1-s) B]; s=1 is GNS and s=1/2 is KMS.
Their detailed-balance/gradient-flow results assume sigma-DBC, which is GNS
symmetry and entails modular covariance. For modular-frequency jumps V with
Delta_sigma V=e^(-omega)V, Remark 5.6, Eqs. (5.5)–(5.7), give

 f_omega(t)=integral_0^1 e^(omega(s-1/2)) t^s ds
           =e^(omega/2)(t-e^(-omega))/(log(t)+omega),
 R_rho f_omega(Delta_rho)
    (V log(e^(-omega/2)rho)-log(e^(omega/2)rho)V)
      =e^(-omega/2)V rho-e^(omega/2)rho V,

and at omega=0, R_rho f_0(Delta_rho)([V,log rho])=[V,rho]. This is an
actual logarithmic chain rule, but only with the modular-eigenoperator
structure of the GNS-symmetric detailed-balance representation. It is not a
general KMS formula: KMS symmetry alone need not give modular covariance or
such eigenoperators. Therefore it cannot be inserted into the arbitrary-KMS
target without an additional theorem.

There is a primary counterexample to replacing the VW twisted derivation by
an ordinary Hilbert-bimodule derivation. Vernooij, arXiv:2203.12307v3,
Example 5.3, gives a three-level faithful state proportional to
diag(1,pi^2,e^2) and jumps E_23,E_32. The paper proves no derivation-square
factorization exists for its KMS inner product: every feasible matrix has a
strictly negative tested quadratic form (the displayed value is
-[e^2+2e(pi-1)+pi(pi-2)]/(1+pi^2+e^2)). This is a counterexample to the
untwisted factorization route, not to J>=2E, not to the VW23 twisted theorem,
and not to a modular-orbit comparison. It is a decisive reason to retain the
KMS twist in the missing lemma.

Result and next mathematical gate
---------------------------------
The strongest checked transfer is: global h(s)-convexity implies weak Lp
for all finite p (KT Lemma 18); p=1 alone has no checked all-p lift. For
arbitrary finite KMS symmetry, VW23 Theorems 2.4–2.5 and VW26 Theorems 3.9
and 4.4 provide exact twisted derivation and modular-orbit energy
representations, but not an entropy chain rule or the required comparison.
The modular-orbit representation itself is prior art. The next falsifiable
task is to derive the Gamma_L terms in the supplied resolvent representation
using the VW twisted module and test whether their integral dominates the
known square-root Dirichlet form. If that fails, the equivalent M>=0 gate
remains explicit. Historical novelty remains UNKNOWN.
