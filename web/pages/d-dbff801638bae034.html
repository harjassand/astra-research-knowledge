STRONGEST EXACT CLAIM

For the full d-dimensional tracial algebra, the collective variance value in the cycle-4 broadcasting reduction has an exact symmetric-separable two-copy representation and an exact one-matrix dual. This is a reformulation, not a proof of a dimension-free rounding theorem.

Let tau=Tr/d, let H_x=H_x* be a finite bounded likelihood family (centering is allowed but not needed for the identity), and let p_x be a prior. Write P_psi=|psi><psi| and define the compact frame-measure set

  M_d = { probability measures mu on pure projectors P : integral P dmu(P)=I/d }.

For rank-one POVM effects M_y=d w_y P_y, completeness is exactly sum_y w_y P_y=I/d. The canonical measure-prepare channel has normalized Choi state

  omega_mu = sum_y w_y P_y^T tensor P_y,

and its quadratic capture is

  tau(H Psi_mu(H)) = sum_y w_y (Tr P_y H)^2
                     = Tr[omega_mu (H^T tensor H)].

Put eta_mu=omega_mu^{T_1}=sum_y w_y P_y tensor P_y and

  K_p = (1/2) sum_x p_x (H_x tensor I - I tensor H_x)^2.

Both marginals of eta_mu are I/d. Direct expansion gives

  sum_x p_x [tau(H_x^2)-tau(H_x Psi_mu(H_x))]
       = Tr(eta_mu K_p)
       = sum_y w_y sum_x p_x Var_{psi_y}(H_x).

Rank-one refinement and finite-dimensional minimax therefore give the exact identities

  q_p = min_{mu in M_d} Tr(eta_mu K_p)
      = min_{mu in M_d} integral sum_x p_x Var_psi(H_x) dmu(psi),
  q(E) = sup_p q_p.

Equivalently, q_p is the minimum energy of K_p over the convex hull of P tensor P with prescribed marginal I/d. The associated normalized Choi states omega_mu are separable and have maximally mixed marginals. This is a structured separable-Choi optimization, not optimization over all separable states: the same pure state appears on both factors after partial transpose.

Its exact LP dual is

  q_p = sup_{Y=Y*} tau(Y)
        subject to <psi|Y|psi> <= sum_x p_x Var_psi(H_x) for every unit psi.

The primal is a compact probability-measure problem with fixed first moment. Strong duality follows by finite-dimensional convex separation; Caratheodory reduces a primal optimizer to finitely many outcomes. The dual constraint is a quartic inequality only on the diagonal coherent vectors psi tensor psi, so it is not the PSD condition K_p >= Y tensor I or a generic Choi SDP.

LOAD-BEARING REDUCTION / NEXT GATE

For a positive selfadjoint bistochastic selfcompatible channel Phi and a PSD operator-frame G=sum_x p_x |H_x><H_x| on the real Hermitian Hilbert space, a universal c Dirichlet rounding is equivalent to

  max_{mu in M_d} sum_y w_y sum_x p_x (Tr P_y H_x)^2
       >= 2 Tr_HS(G Phi) - Tr_HS(G)       (candidate c=2),

for every G >= 0. More generally the right side becomes c Tr_HS(G Phi) - (c-1)Tr_HS(G), after rearrangement of q_p <= c Tr_HS(G(I-Phi)). The exact c=2 form is h_{M_d}(G) >= 2<G,Phi>-Tr G. This is the concrete remaining inequality for an attributed Choi-extension/SOS proof. The existing cycle-4 reports prove it for POVM/Luders and supplied bistochastic-instrument/Petz classes, and for the qubit/depolarizing/tensor-product classes, but do not establish it for every compatible channel.

A useful qubit specialization is exact: if H_x=a_x dot sigma and K=sum_x p_x a_x a_x^T, then q_p=Tr K-lambda_max(K), attained by the antipodal eigenstate ensemble on a top eigenaxis. For a diagonal compatible channel Phi with eigenvalues lambda_i, c=2 reduces to lambda_max(K) >= sum_i (2lambda_i-1)_+ K_ii. It follows whenever sum_i(2lambda_i-1)_+ <=1, matching the known qubit spectral constraints. This is a consistency reduction, not a higher-dimensional proof.

COSTS, INPUT BOUNDARIES, AND STATUS

The finite-dimensional primal is over a compact continuum of pure states; Caratheodory gives a finite support bound depending on d, not an efficient compiler. Exact separation over all psi is an infinite family of quartic constraints; a finite SOS certificate or computationally tractable hierarchy is not provided. No unknown POVM, ensemble, channel dilation, or separability witness is assumed to be supplied for free by this reformulation. All Hilbert-space traces use tau=Tr/d; the mean constraint fixes the reference to I/d. Nontracial references and the cycle-3 centralization reduction require their separate hypotheses and costs.

Status: exact useful representation; unrestricted structural comparison remains OPEN. The key obstruction is that the dual variance condition is only nonnegative on |psi> tensor |psi>, whereas a general symmetric Choi extension is a quantum state, not a mixture of these coherent products. The next decisive attack is to use the full channel-extension SDP and selfadjoint/positive Liouville structure to prove the frame support inequality, or produce a finite-dimensional compatible-channel counterexample to c=2 and then test whether a weaker uniform modulus survives. A failure of c=2 alone would not refute the qualitative broadcasting-to-EB implication.
