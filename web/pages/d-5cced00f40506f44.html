## Main proof development and current authority

RESULTS.md is the authoritative statement and proof in this package. It supersedes the checkpoints where the same mechanism was narrower or only conjectural.

The key final strengthening was to vary an off-diagonal coherent block while keeping both diagonal blocks fixed. This preserves the reference marginal exactly. A rare reference sector makes the BKM divided difference converge to a rectangular inverse-matrix quadratic form. It replaces a longer varying-marginal entropy expansion and proves equality of fixed-reference complete and conditional coefficients, not only a formula for the latter.

The signed-lift comparison technique has a primary-literature precedent in Zhu--Wang, arXiv:2607.24693v1. The independent mathematical content claimed here is the exact finite optimization and its converse, not invention of the entire less-noisy or BKM framework.

A direct extension would need a common adjoint-preserving lift satisfying both weighted square inequalities. This is not asserted to follow from the one-sided quadratic comparison. - A channel with zero unassisted private/quantum capacity is not thereby an NPT-undistillable state. No NPT undistillability conclusion was obtained.
- No statement about the quantum Mrs Gerber conjecture or a universal log-Sobolev constant is claimed.

## Final strengthening: input marginal and correlation-only witnesses

The affine coherent-flag perturbation also has zero partial trace over the reference. Therefore its two states have exactly equal input marginals, and its binary auxiliary is independent of each local subsystem separately. A further orthogonal reference flag can impose any prescribed faithful input marginal without changing the relative-entropy ratio. The input-dependent complete coefficient consequently depends only on the support of that prescribed marginal, with no statement made about comparison against a fixed product reference state.

For classical channels, dephasing the minimax state in the input alphabet preserves the matrix objective; its weighted projection and coherent-flag witness remain classical on the input. This verifies that the classical-channel formulas do not depend on allowing input coherences.
