Our first deduction is exact hidden-index addition and multiplication from
the same bounded-error height interface. For positive canonical point codes

`A=max(W(Q+R),W(Q−R))=W_(k+l)`.

Then addition is `W(S)=A`. For multiplication use

`L=A W(S) W1`,
`D=W(Q) W(R) max(W(S+P),W(S−P))`.

The exact identity is `log(L/D)=2a(kl−r)+E`, with `|E|≤6c`.

`L≤κD` and `D≤κL` hold exactly when r=kl. Every auxiliary is uniquely
fixed after the output; zero and inactive branches are explicitly gated and
zeroed. The interface is an arithmetic graph on natural-coded points, not a
Diophantine model over Q or an acquired graph from an ordinary integer index
to its representative. Complete derivation and charged representation sizes
are in `notes/logic_gate14/cycle19/HEIGHT_WINDOW_ARITHMETIC.md`.

The six-height method needs bounded additive error. The monotone integer
sequence `W_j=2^(8j²+floor(j^(3/2)))` has quadratic leading growth and vanishing
relative error, yet rejects true products for every fixed multiplicative
