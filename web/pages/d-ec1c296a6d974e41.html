## 1. Two nested invariant syndrome projectors

Let P(X) denote all4^m Pauli labels on X, ignoring scalar phases,
and define the commuting-projector intersections

    Q_X=intersection_(F in P(X)) F*P1F,
    P_XB=intersection_(E in union_alpha P(Y_alpha)) E*Q_X E.

Include identity in the second inventory; then P_XB<=Q_X<=P1.
All factors are original syndrome functions. Pauli conjugations of
such functions are syndrome translations and commute even when
the underlying physical Paulis anticommute. Conjugating Q_X by any
R in P(X) permutes its full inventory. The same is true for P_XB:
commuting the two Pauli conjugations only discards a scalar phase.
Hence Q_X and P_XB commute with every operator supported in X.
They reduce H0,V,H, and commute with the old logical algebra.

For F in P(X), telescope [Oi,F] over its one-site factors. Every
partial product maps Q_X into P1, because partial products also
belong to P(X). Single-error protection at each partial state
therefore gives [Oi,F]Q_X=0. By linearity,

    [Oi,V]Q_X=0, [Oi,H]Q_X=0.                        (I1)

For any E in P(Y_alpha), E P_XB lies in Q_X. All partial products
on Y_alpha also lie in its FULL inventory, so the same telescope

    A_alpha P_XB = Q_X A_alpha P_XB,
    [Oi,A_alpha]P_XB=0.                              (I2)

This uses the full bath-support inventory, not merely P_XB<=P1.
