Abstract

We show that any symmetric positive definite matrix can be symmetrically scaled
by a positive diagonal matrix, or by a diagonal matrix with arbitrary
signs, to
have arbitrary positive rows sums.  The scaling can be constructed by
solving an
ordinary differential equation.
