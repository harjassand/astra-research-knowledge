INDEPENDENT AUDIT: SOL HISTORY-IMAGE GAP BOUND
2026-10-08

Status: INTERNALLY CHECKED CANDIDATE. This note audits the scoped Chebyshev
projection-row obstruction in outputs/research/sol_history_gap/RESULT.txt;
it is not an external review or a novelty claim.

1. Chebyshev lemma and constants

For a finite 0<=C<=I with kernel projector P and first positive eigenvalue
gamma in (0,1), define

 p_l(x)=T_l((1+gamma-2x)/(1-gamma)) /
        T_l((1+gamma)/(1-gamma)).

Then p_l(0)=1 and, on [gamma,1],

 ||p_l(C)-P|| <= 2 exp(-2 l sqrt(gamma)).

If the maximum row support of C is s, every degree-l polynomial in C has row
support at most 1+s+...+s^l <= (s+1)^l. For a row i with projection mass at
least m on J and entries bounded by |P_ij|^2<=a, a polynomial approximant A
must have

 ||A-P||^2 >= m-a(s+1)^l,

because all target mass on J outside the support of A_i remains in the row of
A-P. Taking l=ceil(log(8/m)/(4 sqrt(gamma))) gives squared approximation
error <=m/2 and forces (s+1)^l>=m/(2a). With
D=log(m/(2a))/log(s+1)>1, use l<=log(8/m)/(4 sqrt(gamma))+1 to obtain

 gamma <= [log(8/m)/(4(D-1))]^2.

I checked the ceiling, factor 2 in the Chebyshev estimate, and rearrangement.
For gamma=1, C=I-P and the direct support bound m<=a(s+1) makes D>1
impossible, so the endpoint does not create an exception. The argument uses
only positive contraction, exact kernel, and maximum row support.

2. Seed-tail interface and directional convention

The exact seed fixture's c_phi,c_psi are preparation circuits:
 c_j |0^5> = |j>.
The diagonalizers in h_j=U_j^*D_jU_j are U_j=c_j^*, so
 U_j^*|0^5>=|j>.
For the history isometry V=K^(-1/2) sum_u |u>U_u, its projector block is

 P_(u,z),(v,z')=K^(-1) [U_u U_v^*]_(z,z').

For u in the phi tail and v in the psi tail, the required relative decoder is
 U_phi U_psi^*=c_phi^*c_psi.
An exact Q(sqrt(2)) multiplication using
outputs/research/games_hamiltonians/cycle2/bmvz_bjsw_seed_pair_test.py
gives exactly 8 nonzeros in every row, each with squared magnitude 1/8.
The two seed projectors are singleton BMVZ layers of weight 1/4 each; after
aggregating their identical repeated clauses, each tail has q=Q/4 clock
positions. Thus a selected phi-tail row has

 |J|=q*8^T,  a=1/(K^2*8^T),  m=q/K^2,

into the psi-tail columns. The q/K clock mass is greater than 1/16 since
Q<=K<4Q. Other layers have distinct clock columns and cannot cancel this
selected mass. This verifies the support and mass used in RESULT.txt A/C.

3. Correct scope of the consequence

Substituting these m,a gives

 gamma <= [log(8K^2/q) log(s+1) /
            (4[log(q*8^T/2)-log(s+1)])]^2

when the denominator is positive. For K,s polynomial in input size and T
dominating log K+log s, this is O((log K log(s+1)/T)^2). It tends to zero
when T>>log K log(s+1); in particular it vanishes for K,s<=n^b and T>=n^c
with fixed b,c>0. Thus it excludes a constant all-state gap for every
polynomial-row positive enforcer of this exact full-list uniform-history
image in that regime.

The invariant also survives basis-injective monomial workspace recodings,
including classical reversible computations, because such an embedding only
permutes and phases the projector row. It does not cover coherent quantum
workspace maps or a redesigned isometry V. It does not give a lower bound on
every positive encoding of the hard Hamiltonian, does not constrain B, and
does not imply a quantum PCP theorem. The separate Clifford stabilizer
construction is a valid positive control for C alone; its missing logical
comparison B is the relevant gate.

4. Factorwise arbitrary-decoder extension

I independently checked Sol's additional C.2 extension. For any two
factorwise seed diagonalizers with diagonal rank-one tests |r_phi><r_phi|
and |r_psi><r_psi|, W=U_phi U_psi* has

 |W_(r_phi,r_psi)|^2=|<phi,psi>|^2=1/8.

The r_phi row is normalized, so every other entry in it has squared
magnitude at most the remaining mass 7/8; the distinguished entry also
satisfies this bound. The T-fold product row therefore has every entry
square at most (7/8)^T. Across q psi-tail clock values the total projector
row mass remains q/K^2, by unitarity of W. Substitution in the general lemma
gives

 D=log[q(8/7)^T/2]/log(s+1),
 gamma <= [log(8K^2/q)log(s+1) /
            (4[log(q(8/7)^T/2)-log(s+1)])]^2,

when D>1. It retains the same qualitative vanishing-gap conclusion when
T>>log K log(s+1), including polynomial K,s and T>=n^c, now for arbitrary
factorwise decoders and not only the selected Clifford decoders.

For decoders acting on an entire base data copy, choose a row z in the phi
diagonal support. Then U_phi*|z> lies in ran(P_phi tensor I), and
P_phi P_psi P_phi=(1/8)P_phi shows the row has exactly 1/8 squared mass in
the psi diagonal support, leaving 7/8 outside. Thus each individual entry
of this row is still at most 7/8 in square. Tensoring over independent copies
is valid. The proof still requires factorization across T copies and fixed
uniform-history endpoints; a global decoder of the amplified OR projector,
or a new coherent active-workspace isometry, is outside scope.

No defect found in C.2. Its weaker exponent log(8/7) is essential to the
general statement; the exact 8^T estimate is specific to the chosen Clifford
decoder relative matrix. This remains an internal audit, not external proof
validation.
