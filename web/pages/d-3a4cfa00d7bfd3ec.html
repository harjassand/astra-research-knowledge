C08_L08 FINAL — FINITE-TRAJECTORY ACQUISITION LOWER BOUND
7 October 2026. Requested gpt-6-luna/max; observed model UNKNOWN.

RESULT

There is no finite trajectory-start budget that uniformly estimates or distinguishes zero from fixed positive normalized-volume entropy over all smooth divergence-free fields on one fixed flat T^3 when no global regularity, speed, or energy envelope is supplied. This remains true when the admissible fields are required to be nowhere zero, which repairs the zero-field mismatch in my immutable INITIAL relative to the baseline interface.

For any fixed target eta>0, an imported smooth area-preserving disk diffeomorphism f, identity near its boundary and with normalized-area metric entropy h0>0, yields on T^2 x S^1 a steady suspension field Y_j supported as a perturbation of a vertical field only in one disk tube D_j x S^1. Put M=n^2 disjoint equal disks in an n-by-n grid, choose q>4eta/(pi h0), set alpha=eta/(qMh0), and scale the common vertical speed by a_M=qM. The null X_0^(M)=a_M partial_t and each alternative X_j^(M)=a_M Y_j are smooth, divergence-free, and nowhere zero. Their time-one entropies are 0 and eta, respectively. Outside its hidden tube, every alternative agrees with its null field for all time.

For any randomized adaptive protocol with m chosen trajectory starts, arbitrarily long exact paths, and a common measurement-noise channel, the null transcript can visit at most m disks. Coupling gives

    TV(P_0, M^(-1) sum_j P_j) <= m/M.

Thus if m<M/3, the equal-prior zero-versus-eta test has error greater than 1/3. Uniform eta/3-accurate estimation with success at least 2/3 is impossible. The noise channel and temporal precision are immaterial on unvisited tubes; the proof already grants noiseless full paths of unbounded duration.

The same family has an explicit strain-energy cost. For fixed isotopy generator v_s, define A0=integral |sym Dv_s|^2 dz ds>0 and B0=(1/2) integral |partial_s v_s|^2 dz ds. Then

    integral |Def X_j|^2 dmu
      = [q A0/(pi h0)] M eta + [B0/(pi^2 h0^2)] eta^2.

For fixed eta and gadget constants, a normalized strain-energy cap B therefore still permits M=Theta(B/eta) hidden alternatives and forces m=Omega(B/eta) for chosen-start protocols. The strain-viscosity power is W=2nu V integral|Def X|^2. If starts are instead required to be independent uniform-volume draws, the coupling improves to TV<=m alpha, giving m=Omega(B/eta^2). If zeros are permitted, the immutable compactly supported construction gives the stronger chosen-start lower bound Omega(B/eta^2). These are lower bounds, not optimality claims; constants depend on the imported map/isotopy and, for fixed eta, the packing choice.
