## Weighted-tracial centralization audit

Let (S_0=\sqrt{\sigma}), (X_\rho=S_0^{-1}(S_0\rho S_0)^{1/2}S_0^{-1}), and

\[
\Delta(\sigma,\rho)=\|\sqrt{\sigma}\sqrt{\rho}\|_1-\operatorname{Tr}(\sqrt{\sigma}\sqrt{\rho}),\quad
\Xi=\sup_{\rho\in E}\|[X_\rho,S_0]\|_2.
\]

The canonical likelihood identities are (X_\rho\sigma X_\rho=\rho), (0\le X_\rho\le M I), and \(\|X_\rho S_0\|_2=1\). The supplied polar-decomposition estimate is

\[
\Xi\le 2\sqrt{2M\sup_{\rho\in E}\Delta(\sigma,\rho)}.
\]

Restricting the same global broadcaster to each pair \((\sigma,\rho)\) gives \(\sup_\rho\Delta(\sigma,\rho)\le v(b)\). This uses no net and no union bound: the broadcaster error is already a supremum over the full experiment, and N46 is a two-state statement. If (b) is only an infimum, choose broadcasters within an arbitrarily small additive slack before applying the pair bound.

For (0<t\le1), let (D_u) pinch eigenvectors of (S_0) into log-eigenvalue bins \([u+kt,u+(k+1)t)\), with (u\) uniform on \([0,t)\). The random-bin calculation gives

\[
\mathbb E_u\|(X_\rho-D_uX_\rho)S_0\|_2^2\le 3M\Xi/t,
\qquad
\mathbb E_u\|D_u\rho-\rho\|_1\le4\sqrt{3M\Xi/t}.
\]

The first inequality follows by bounding the probability that a pair of eigenvalues is separated by a random bin boundary, then applying Cauchy--Schwarz to the commutator matrix entries. The second uses (\rho=X_\rho\sigma X_\rho), the Hilbert--Schmidt amplitude difference, and contractivity of pinching. It is a per-state expectation with the same distribution of (u); retaining (u) as a classical flag is what avoids selecting one shift that works for all labels.

Flatten (S_0)'s eigenvalues to lower bin endpoints, normalize to Hilbert--Schmidt norm one to obtain (S'_u), and set (\sigma'_u=(S'_u)^2), (A_u=e^{-t}S'_uS_0^{-1}). Then

\[
e^{-t}S_0\le S'_u\le e^tS_0,\quad e^{-2t}I\le A_u\le I,\quad A_u\sigma A_u=e^{-2t}\sigma'_u.
\]

The channel

\[
C_u(Z)=A_uD_u(Z)A_u+\operatorname{Tr}[(I-A_u^2)Z]\sigma'_u
\]

is CP and TP: (A_u^2) is fixed by the pinching, so the first term has trace \(\operatorname{Tr}(A_u^2Z)\); the replacement term supplies the remaining trace. It sends \(\sigma\) to \(\sigma'_u\), preserves the fixed likelihood sandwich, and changes any input state from (D_u\rho) by at most (8t). Thus

\[
\mathbb E_u\|C_u(\rho)-\rho\|_1\le4\sqrt{3M\Xi/t}+8t.
\]

The maps are piecewise constant in (u\): changes occur only at the at most (d) residues \(\log s_a\pmod t\). Their intervals give a finite flagged CPTP encoder with at most (d+1) branches; the decoder discards the flag. The encoder depends on \(\sigma,t\), not on \(\rho\). The reference \(\sigma_F\) is scalar on each output bin, so for \(\mathcal A=\bigoplus_{(\ell,\mathrm{bin})}B(H_{\ell,\mathrm{bin}})\),

\[
\tau(X)=\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_FX)
\]

is a faithful normalized trace. Family size, minimum eigenvalue, spectral gap, and bin separation do not enter the reconstruction error. The number of branches is dimension-dependent, and no finite-precision spectral routine, circuit bound, or efficient encoder is established.

The broadcaster transfers through encoder/decoder with half-trace error at most (b+\varepsilon/2), and an EB reconstruction on the flagged experiment transfers back with error at most its flagged error plus (\varepsilon/2). Pinching onto \(\mathcal A\) fixes the promised states. The physical/likelihood conversion is exact because \(\sigma_F\) is central: \(\|\sigma_F X\|_1=\tau(|X|)\). Petz correction then yields a positive, selfadjoint, selfcompatible UCP marginal on the tracial algebra, with no comparison between KMS and GNS norms.

## Collective variance and CP-range audit

The tracial report's quantity

\[
q(E)=\inf_M\sup_{H\in E}\langle H,(I-\Psi_M)H\rangle_\tau,
\qquad
\Psi_M(A)=\sum_y\frac{\tau(M_yA)}{\tau(M_y)}M_y,
\]

uses one canonical EB map for all labels. Its stated dimension-free conversions are

\[
e_{EB}(E)\le\tfrac12\sqrt{q(E)},\qquad
q(E)\le8\theta e+6\theta^2\sqrt{2e}
\]

for likelihood radius \(\theta\) and an EB error (e). For finite experiments, minimax is finite-dimensional; for arbitrary bounded experiments the finite-dimensional closure is compact. Neither conversion contains the number of labels. The unresolved collective step is to force (q(E)\to0) from small broadcast residual; pairwise commutators and accessible information do not supply it.

For positive selfadjoint selfcompatible \(\Phi\), normalized trace \(\tau\), and Hermitian contractions (A,C), put (A_m=\Phi^m(A)), (C_m=\Phi^m(C)), and

\[
d_m=\frac{m^m}{(m+1)^{m+1}}\le\frac1{m+1}.
\]

Spectral calculus for the positive selfadjoint (L^2(\tau)) contraction gives

\[
\|\Phi(A_m)-A_m\|_2\le d_m,
\qquad
\tau(\Phi(A_m^2)-\Phi(A_m)^2)\le d_m.
\]

For a joint UCP splitter (J:\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal A\to\mathcal A), the receiver inputs (A_m\otimes I) and (I\otimes C_m) commute. A Stinespring isometry bounds the two multiplicative defects by the square roots of the two Schwarz defects, giving

\[
\|[\Phi(A_m),\Phi(C_m)]\|_2\le2\sqrt{d_m}.
\]

Replacing \(\Phi(A_m),\Phi(C_m)\) by (A_m,C_m) costs at most (4d_m), since all four operators are contractions. Therefore

\[
\sup_{\|A\|,\|C\|\le1}\|[\Phi^m(A),\Phi^m(C)]\|_{2,\tau}
\le2/\sqrt{m+1}+4/(m+1).
\]

This calculation is uniform in dimension and trace-block weights, and uses no spectral gap. Iteration of the splitter makes \(\Phi^m\) (2^m)-selfcompatible. The commutator result is a statement about the whole range, not only a selected finite family.

The still-open lift must be a single EB map uniformly close to \(\Phi^m\) on the entire operator-norm unit ball in normalized \(L^1(\tau)\), uniformly over dimensions and channels. An ultraproduct's pointwise abelian limit does not furnish this finite-dimensional \(\infty\to1\) map approximation. No such lift or growing-dimensional counterexample to it was obtained here. A sufficient gate is \(\beta_m\to0\), where

\[
\beta_m=\sup_{(\mathcal A,\tau,\Phi)}\inf_{E\in EB_\tau}
\sup_{A=A^*,\|A\|\le1}\|E(A)-\Phi^m(A)\|_{1,\tau}.
\]

For an experiment with residual (r=\sup_H\|\Phi(H)-H\|_{1,\tau}\), this would imply

\[
T(E(\sigma(I+H)),\sigma(I+H))
\le\tfrac12[(1+\theta)\beta_m+mr],
\]

and a slow choice (m\to\infty), (mr\to0) would close the fixed-sandwich target. This sufficient route is not a proof.

## Exact instrument subclass and closure

Let (K_y\in\mathcal A) be a supplied block-internal Kraus family with \(\sum_yK_y^*K_y=\sum_yK_yK_y^*=I\), and define (L(A)=\sum_yK_yAK_y^*\), (M_y=K_y^*K_y), (p_y=\tau(M_y)), and

\[
\Psi(A)=\sum_{p_y>0}\frac{\tau(M_yA)}{p_y}M_y,\quad C=(L+\Psi)/2,\quad\Phi=C^\dagger C.
\]

The instrument-append map \(\rho\mapsto\sum_yK_y\rho K_y^*\otimes[\sigma M_y/p_y]\), averaged with its swap, has common physical marginal \(C\). Applying its tracial Petz recovery to both outputs gives the positive selfadjoint compatible marginal \(C^\dagger C\). The exact map identity is

\[
2(I-\Phi)-(I-\Psi)
=(I-L^\dagger L)+(\Psi-\Psi^2)+(L-\Psi)^\dagger(L-\Psi)/2\succeq0.
\]

Here (L^\dagger L\preceq I), (0\preceq\Psi\preceq I); expanding the square reproduces both cross terms. Thus \(I-\Psi\preceq2(I-\Phi)\), and one EB channel reconstructs all bounded likelihood states with half-trace error at most \(\frac12\sqrt{2\theta r}\). No assumption (\Psi\preceq\Phi\), (L=L^\dagger), or positivity of (L) is needed. The all-POVM/Luders class has the simpler \(0\preceq\Psi\preceq L\) comparison. Tensorization with a general positive comparator is exact:

\[
[aI+b(\Psi_1\otimes\Psi_2)]-[(aI+b\Psi_1)\otimes(aI+b\Psi_2)]
=ab(I-\Psi_1)\otimes(I-\Psi_2)\succeq0,
\quad a=1-1/c,\ b=1/c.
\]

It preserves the same (c\ge1\) without local \(\Psi_j\preceq\Phi_j\) or commutation. Weighted tensor traces are product Hilbert spaces. Mixtures need one common trace; direct sums need block-preserving maps. The instrument extension requires block-internal supplied Kraus operators; arbitrary block-changing instruments and a representation of every compatible channel are outside its scope.

These examples identify boundary conditions; none is a valid counterexample to the tracial reconstruction problem.
