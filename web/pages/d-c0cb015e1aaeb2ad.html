# Facet-local finite-volume safety from an acquired strict-collar certificate

**Status.** Internally derived refinement of the P/N35 finite-horizon safety argument. Conditional theorem; no priority or novelty claim. It is independent of N33 if a strict-collar certificate is supplied. A proposed N33-to-certificate bridge is conditional on N33's candidate proof and is non-effective.

## 1. Model and inputs

Let a finite reaction list have integer source and product complexes (y_r,y'_r\in\mathbb N^d), jump vectors \(\nu_r=y'_r-y_r\), and stoichiometric span (S=\mathrm{span}_{\mathbb R}\{\nu_r\}). At volume (V\), the molecule-count process has concentration (X^V=Z^V/V), jump (\nu_r/V), and predictable intensity

\[
V k_r(t,\mathcal F_{t-})\,\phi^V_{y_r}(X^V_{t-}),\qquad
\phi^V_y(x)=V^{-|y|}\prod_j(Vx_j)_{y_j},
\]

where all controls satisfy (0\le k_r\le K). The stopped argument below permits arbitrary predictable feedback; no Markov controller is needed.

Supply a compact positive relative polytope

\[
Q=\{x\in c+S:g_i(x)=a_i\cdot x+b_i\ge0,\ i=1,\dots,m\}
\]

with its proper relative facets, positive collar widths \(\ell_i\), and margins \(\gamma_i>0\). The deterministic requirement is, for every (x\in Q) with (0\le g_i(x)\le\ell_i),

\[
a_i\cdot f_k(x)\ge\gamma_i,\qquad f_k(x)=\sum_r k_r x^{y_r}\nu_r,
\tag{1}
\]

for every admissible instantaneous rate vector (k\). Supply rational coordinate upper bounds (U^i_j\ge\sup_{x\in L_i}x_j) on each collar (L_i=Q\cap\{0\le g_i\le\ell_i\}), and global bounds (U_j\ge\sup_{x\in Q}x_j). The initial count must be lattice-compatible and have deterministic gaps (g_i(X^V_0)\ge d_i>0\).

## 2. Facet-local constants

For a source complex (y\), define

\[
E_y(U)=\sum_{j:y_j\ge2}\binom{y_j}{2}U_j^{y_j-1}\prod_{l\ne j}U_l^{y_l}.
\]

The falling-factorial correction satisfies, on any coordinate box bounded by (U\),

\[
0\le x^y-\phi_y^V(x)\le E_y(U)/V.
\tag{2}
\]

For facet (i), use its own collar box (U^i) and set

\[
D_i=K\sum_r|a_i\cdot\nu_r|E_{y_r}(U^i),\quad
J_i=\max_r|a_i\cdot\nu_r|,\quad
Q_i=K\sum_r(a_i\cdot\nu_r)^2(U^i)^{y_r}.
\]

For the stopped total hazard, retain the global constant

\[
\Lambda=K\sum_r U^{y_r}.
\]

Let

\[
V_0=\max\left(1,\max_i\left\lceil\frac{2D_i}{\gamma_i}\right\rceil\right),\quad
\mu_i=\gamma_i/2,\quad
\theta_i=\min\{J_i^{-1},\mu_i/(3Q_i)\},\quad
c=\min_i\theta_i\ell_i.
\]

Zero-projection facet inequalities are removed as non-facets in the relative class. For (V\ge V_0), the finite-volume projected drift in collar (i) is at least \(\mu_i\), by (1)–(2).

## 3. Proof of the bound

Define

\[
\Phi_i(x)=e^{-\theta_i Vg_i(x)},\qquad \Phi(x)=\sum_i\Phi_i(x).
\]

Inside collar (i), the predictable generator obeys

\[
\frac{\mathcal L_t\Phi_i(x)}{\Phi_i(x)}
=V\sum_r k_r\phi^V_{y_r}(x)
(e^{-\theta_i a_i\cdot\nu_r}-1).
\]

Using (e^z-1\le z+z^2e^{|z|}/2), (\theta_iJ_i\le1), (e<3), the local drift and quadratic bound give

\[
\mathcal L_t\Phi_i/\Phi_i
\le -V\theta_i\mu_i+(3/2)V\theta_i^2Q_i
\le -V\theta_i\mu_i/2\le0.
\tag{3}
\]

Outside collar (i), while (x\in Q), (g_i(x)>\ell_i) and (\Phi_i(x)\le e^{-\theta_iV\ell_i}). The positive part of each exponential jump increment is at most (e^{\theta_iJ_i}-1\le e-1<2); the total reaction intensity is at most (V\Lambda). Thus

\[
\mathcal L_t\Phi_i(x)\le2V\Lambda e^{-\theta_iV\ell_i}
\]

outside its collar. Summing (3) and these bounds gives

\[
\mathcal L_t\Phi(x)\le2mV\Lambda e^{-cV},\qquad x\in Q.
\tag{4}
\]

Let \(\tau_Q\) be the first jump outside (Q\). Before the stopped jump, all intensities are bounded by (V\Lambda\); each projected overshoot is at most (J_i/V\), so \(\Phi(X^V_{\tau_Q})\le\sum_i e^{\theta_iJ_i}\le3m\). The stopped process is therefore nonexplosive on finite intervals and Dynkin's formula applies. At exit some gap is negative, so \(\Phi(X^V_{\tau_Q})\ge1\). Applying Dynkin to \(t\wedge\tau_Q\), using nonnegativity, yields

\[
\boxed{\Pr(\tau_Q\le T)\le
\sum_i e^{-\theta_iVg_i(X^V_0)}+2mV\Lambda T e^{-cV}.}
\tag{5}
\]

This holds for every finite (T\), uniformly over the supplied predictable-control class. It is not an infinite-time survival, recurrence, stationary-tightness, or permanence theorem.

## 4. Why collar-local envelopes are valid

The factorial error (2) and projected quadratic term are used only at points in the corresponding collar (L_i). Replacing a global coordinate bound (U) by any certified collar bound (U^i) therefore preserves each step of (3). Outside the collar the proof uses only the global total-hazard bound and (g_i>\ell_i). This is the sole change to N35's proof; no locality assumption is added to reaction propensities, and no cross-facet factorization is used.

A simpler conservative implementation can use the exact coordinate maxima on each compact (L_i), rounded upward to rationals. Obtaining them is a separate certificate-acquisition task and is not included in the arithmetic complexity of evaluating (5).

## 5. Conditional bridge from an N33 plateau

Assume N33's all-endotactic candidate theorem is correct for a fixed finite integer-complex network and a fixed positive stoichiometric class (P=(c+S)\cap\mathbb R^d_{>0}), with all rates in a common compact positive box. Its constructed absorber is a compact plateau

\[
K_P=\{x:F_h(x)=F_h(c)\}
= (c+S)\cap\bigcap_{\lambda}\{L_{\lambda,h}(x)\ge F_h(c)\},
\]

where the affine family is finite and (K_P\) lies strictly inside the positive orthant. It is therefore a compact relative polytope.

Take any proper relative facet (F_i\). Some defining affine function (L_{\lambda,h}-F_h(c)) gives its supporting halfspace on the relative interior; write its inward gap as (g_i\). The corresponding active slope is not in (S^\perp), because a slope in (S^\perp) is constant on the class and cannot define a proper facet. N33's weighted essential-source lemma gives

\[
r_\lambda\cdot f_k(x)\ge\delta_h>0
\]

at every point of (F_i\), uniformly over the rate box. The face and rate box are compact, and mass-action (f_k(x)) is jointly continuous in (x,k). If no positive collar width preserved, say, half this margin, a sequence (x_n\in K_P) with (g_i(x_n)\downarrow0) and admissible (k_n) would have a convergent subsequence to (F_i\times[\kappa,K]^R), contradicting the face margin. Hence some \(\ell_i>0\) and \(\gamma_i=\delta_h/2\) satisfy (1). There are finitely many facets, so N35 applies to this class.

This bridge is conditional twice: N33 is a source-derived candidate whose external correctness is unresolved, and its affine slopes/scales provide no useful numeric bounds on \(\delta_h\), the collar widths, bit heights, or acquisition time. Integer complexes are also required for the molecular CTMC; N33 allows broader real kinetic orders. Finally, N35 still needs a buffered count in the lattice/coset at the selected volume.

## 6. Synthetic calculation used in the report

The executable input and exact rational arithmetic are in `../calculations/facet_local_calculation.py`; generated values are in `../calculations/facet_local_results.json`. The network is

\[
\varnothing\xrightarrow{k_1}A,\quad 2A\xrightarrow{k_2}A,\quad
\varnothing\xrightarrow{k_3}B,\quad B\xrightarrow{k_4}\varnothing,
\]

with all (k_i\in[0.9,1.1]\), (Q=[1/2,2]^2\), four collars of width (1/20\), and (X_0=(1,1)\). This is a synthetic model with exactly stipulated parameters, not a parameter fit or a measured biochemical system.

The deterministic margins can be checked at the collar endpoints:

- (A\)-lower: (0.9-1.1(0.55)^2=0.56725>0.56\).
- (A\)-upper: (0.9(1.95)^2-1.1=2.32225>2.3\).
- (B\)-lower: (0.9-1.1(0.55)=0.295>0.29\).
- (B\)-upper: (0.9(1.95)-1.1=0.655>0.65\).

Global bounds use (U=(2,2)\), giving (V_0=8\), (c=29/39600\approx0.000732323\), and (Lambda=44/5\). Collar-local bounds use (U^{A-}=(11/20,2)\), (U^{B-}=(2,11/20)\), and (U=(2,2)\) for upper facets. They give (V_0=3\), (c=29/20460\approx0.00141740\), and the same global (Lambda=44/5\). The exact exponent ratio is (39600/20460\approx1.93548\).

At (V=20{,}000\), (T=e^{cV/4}\approx1{,}196.31\): the local certificate gives exit probability at most (8.2237319967\times10^{-4}\). At that same horizon, the original global-box right-hand side is about (733.78\), hence vacuous. For 200 independent replicates with fixed admissible rates, the probability bound on at least 3 exits is the binomial tail at (p=0.0008223732\), about (6.47192014\times10^{-4}\). This is a pre-specified model falsifier, not an observed experiment. Direct event simulation is costly: even the global candidate bound is (V\Lambda T\approx2.11\times10^8\) candidate events per replicate, about (4.21\times10^{10}\) for 200 replicates. The physical feed and molecular turnover must also be budgeted.
