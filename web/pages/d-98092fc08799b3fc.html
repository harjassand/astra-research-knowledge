1. PHYSICAL INTERFACE AND LEGAL DISTINCT-NOISE COMPLETION

ell=log sigma-log rho. The original fixed Hermitian weighted noise B has

Put Psi_s(X)=s^(1/2)Xs^(1/2), L_s=Psi_s^(-1)H_sPsi_s and
L_s,*=Psi_s H_s Psi_s^(-1). The target nonlinear forms are

The blind Cycle07 proof used the opposite Lindblad generator; no older
bytes are changed, and that convention is not used in this baseline.

A legal HS self-adjoint weighted H is PSD: it is similar to the positive

2. EXACT ENERGY QUADRATIC AND ITS SCHUR COMPLEMENT

On a cross block,
 H_cross(X)=(V_s(B)X+XV_q(C))/2-BXC.
The two Hermitian mirror blocks of Y give
 E_ext(C)=E2_ext
         =A+Tr[R_q C q C]-Tr[D B D C],
 A=Tr[D^2 V_s(B)]/2,
 R_q=Lyap_q^(-1)(D^2)>=0.
The inverse is positive by R_q=integral_0^infinity exp(-tq)D^2exp(-tq)dt.
All terms and normalization factors follow directly from the cross
action; the linear term is real since DBD and C are Hermitian.

On the real HS space of Hermitian matrices define
 M(C)=q C R_q+R_q C q, N=DBD.
It is a positive self-adjoint operator, since
 <C,M(C)>=2Tr[R_q C q C]>=0.
The exact completion of the square is
 E_ext(C)=A-(1/2)<N,M^+N>
       +(1/2)<C-M^+N,M(C-M^+N)>.
Here M^+ is the finite-dimensional Moore-Penrose inverse. N lies in
the range of M: otherwise the universally nonnegative legal energy
quadratic would have a nonzero linear term along ker M and become
negative for one sign of an arbitrarily large coefficient. Thus this
identity is valid even when D is singular. If D is invertible then R_q
and M are positive definite, the minimizer is unique and obeys
 q C_opt R_q+R_q C_opt q=DBD.
The exact minimum is A-(1/2)<N,M^+N>>=0.

For multiple fixed first noises B_a, the energies add and the same M
applies to every C_a. Therefore the second noises optimize independently.
This is an actual finite convex optimization formula, not an assumed
physical Schur complement. The first original generator is unchanged.

At C=B the formula reduces to the old average
E_ext=(E_s(rho)+E_q(sigma))/2. At other C there is no such reverse-energy
floor. In particular the exact minimizer need not preserve any constant
fraction of E_s(rho), as Section4 proves with a fixed primitive original.

5. RIGIDITY OF TRACE-PRESERVING CROSS ACTIONS

Named class: flag-preserving GKSL completions with common block-diagonal
jumps diag(K_(1,a),K_(2,a)) and block-diagonal Hamiltonians. Allow ANY
finite jump list, extra block-diagonal components and identity gauges.
Use physical positive drifts C_j satisfying
(C_j+C_j^*)/2=sum_a K_(j,a)^*K_(j,a). The diagonal generators are
L_j,*(X)=(C_jX+XC_j^*)/2-sum_a K_(j,a)XK_(j,a)^*.
The cross generator is
M(X)=(C_1X+XC_2^*)/2-sum_a K_(1,a)XK_(2,a)^*.

Its trace is Tr[M(X)]=Tr[F X], where
 F=(C_1+C_2^*)/2-sum_a K_(2,a)^*K_(1,a),
 F+F^*=sum_a (K_(1,a)-K_(2,a))^*(K_(1,a)-K_(2,a))>=0.
This is an exact cross leakage identity. If the cross action is trace
preserving on EVERY input matrix, i.e. Tr M(X)=0 for all X, then F=0.
Each jump pair must coincide, and then the skew drift parts also coincide:
K_(1,a)=K_(2,a), C_1=C_2. Therefore M=L_1,*=L_2,*.

If the extension is stationary at Omega, rho is consequently stationary
for the original first generator. If that generator is primitive, this
forces rho=sigma. In particular an exact original cross action on ALL
matrices is impossible for any nonstationary rho, even when the second

This is broader than assuming autonomous original dynamics on the rho

6. ZERO-SECANT-ENERGY RIGIDITY FOR A BROADER FLAG CLASS

Named class: ANY faithful Omega-stationary KMS QMS fixing both flag
projections, whose autonomous first flag semigroup is the primitive
original sigma-stationary semigroup. This statement needs no particular
noise representation or trace preservation of the cross action.
Claim: E_ext=0 for this physical secant Y forces rho=sigma.

Proof. H_ext>=0, so E_ext=0 implies H_extY=0. In Heisenberg coordinates
F=Psi_S^(-1)Y=[[0,T],[T^*,0]],
 T=s^(-1/2)(s-q)q^(-1/2),
is fixed by every P_t. Unital CP Schwarz gives P_t(F^2)>=F^2.
Faithful Omega stationarity makes the positive difference have zero
Omega trace, hence it vanishes. Thus F is in the multiplicative domain
of every P_t. To see the product identity directly, write a finite
Stinespring representation P_t(A)=V^*pi(A)V. Schwarz equality makes
(I-VV^*)pi(F)V=0; since F is Hermitian this yields
P_t(F A F)=P_t(F)P_t(A)P_t(F). Its squared upper block TT^* is fixed by the original
primitive semigroup, so TT^*=alpha I. If alpha=0 then D=0 and rho=sigma.

If alpha>0, T=sqrt(alpha)U for a unitary U. Multiplicative-domain
identities applied to F diag(0,A) F give
P_(1,t)(U A U^*)=U P_(2,t)(A)U^*.
The second flag semigroup is therefore unitarily equivalent to the
primitive first. Since rho is its stationary state, rho=U^*sigma U
and q=U^*sU. The defining equation for T now gives
D=sqrt(alpha)s^(1/2)U q^(1/2)=sqrt(alpha)sU.
D is Hermitian, so D^2=alpha s^2. Therefore s commutes with D and
U=s^(-1)D/sqrt(alpha) is Hermitian and commutes with s. This implies
q=U^*sU=s, contradicting alpha>0. Only rho=sigma remains. QED.

The flag-fixed property guarantees the diagonal block actions used
here: a fixed projection lies in the multiplicative domain of a unital
CP map. Primitivity is used only to identify the fixed first-block
algebra as scalar and its stationary state as unique. The collapse
family in Section4 has positive secant energy for every t>0 and tends
to zero relative to the original energy; it is fully consistent with
this zero-energy rigidity.
