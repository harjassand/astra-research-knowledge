# Frozen discriminator: positive moment repair and witness transfer

Date: 7 October 2026. Worker: frontier_methods, GPT-6.1 Sol/max.
Source version: openai/math commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a.

This protocol precedes the first experiment run. It tests an elementary reusable
procedure extracted from family 007 and a transfer rule extracted from family 102.
It does not test either family's headline theorem or a general discovery capability.

## Interface and procedures

Input: an exact rational probability table on the binary cube, the complete
chosen set of degree-at-most-t nonconstant characters, and exact supplied
character expectations. Both tested procedures receive the same table and tests.
The correction uses their Fourier coefficients, H=sum c_S chi_S, and
q=sum |c_S|. Its output is (P + (q-H)U)/(1+q), with U uniform. q=0 returns P.

The equally informed strong control minimizes total variation distance to P over
all nonnegative distributions satisfying the same character constraints. It is
an exact rational LP. We require a separately checked matching primal/dual pair;
a solver's status by itself is insufficient. A second, weaker control subtracts
H, clips negative masses, and normalizes; it illustrates the positivity trap,
but is not the sole comparator.

## Frozen cases and outcomes

Use eight cases: three binary-cube distributions with n=3 and t=1, three with
n=3 and t=2, two with n=4 and t=1. Integer weights are frozen explicitly in the
script, along with one point mass and one single-character tilt. No optimization
of correction against these cases is permitted. Report exact nonnegativity,
normalization, all target moments, actual TV, q/(1+q), LP optimum, exact dual
verification, and elapsed time. Compare all methods at their actual information
and computation costs. Failure of the clipping control is expected in some cases;
the strong control should weakly dominate the proposed correction on TV.

Separately test (i) a rare-event swap with mass 1/1024, demonstrating that TV
1/1024 can become conditional TV 1; (ii) signed-surplus transfer with all nested
event pairs on a fixed four-atom example; (iii) the obstruction to support
preservation on a point mass; and (iv) a fixed three-layer triangular resource
recurrence versus a same-layer polynomial-feedback recurrence. These are exact
finite checks of proved elementary statements, not evidence for the full source
construction or a universal research improvement.

## Acceptance and limits

The correction is accepted only if its exact constraints and proved TV bound
hold in every case. The LP comparison must have matching independently checked
exact dual witnesses. No advantage in minimum TV is hypothesized. The intended
benefit is a closed-form repair and a direct exact sampler when the defect
coefficients and original sampler are supplied. Charge acquisition of those
coefficients, support restrictions, and rare conditioning separately.

The method's suggested next application is transfer of a bounded scientific or
proof certificate between two explicitly acquired laws. It cannot replace the
missing structural lemma, establish a legal target-support sampler, or confer
an efficient moment oracle.
