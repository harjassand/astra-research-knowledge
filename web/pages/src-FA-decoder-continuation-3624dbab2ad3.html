6. ADVERSARIAL FALSIFICATION AND THE MISSING GENERAL STEP

Independent block controllers cannot be applied separately to components of a
direction that touches multiple blocks: the hardware supplies ONE common sign.
Exact example: the coordinate-box invariant has halfwidth3/2 at gamma1/2. At
s=(3/2,-3/2), take v=(3/5,4/5). If epsilon=+1, postdecision zeta=+1/2 makes
the first coordinate12/5>3/2. If epsilon=-1, zeta=-1/2 makes the second
coordinate-27/10<-3/2. Thus neither sign preserves this naive product invariant.
This rejects the proposed extension at that corner; it does not prove that
another schedule-aware controller cannot avoid the corner or obtain the bound.

A second proposed compact representation was a redundant coefficient box:
s=B_t c_t, ||c_t||_infinity<=1+gamma, a current direction column v_t=B_t e_j,
and rational affine transports T_t with B_(t+1)T_t=B_t and
||T_t||_(infinity->infinity)<=1. Coordinate sigma-delta followed by T_t is a
valid efficient controller with radius (1+gamma)||B_t||_(infinity->2). However,
Z_t=B_t[-1,1]^q obeys Z_t subset Z_(t+1). This monotone-tube representation
cannot give a universal constant radius, even in the plane:

Take a periodic schedule with m distinct recurring unit rays. If all nested
Z_t lie in a fixed radiusR disk, their closure-union Z_infinity is compact and
Z_t tends to it in Hausdorff distance. Along rounds activating ray v_j,
Z_t=[-v_j,v_j]+Y_t, with Y_t compact symmetric and bounded. A convergent
subsequence of Y_t gives Z_infinity=[-v_j,v_j]+Y_j. Hence Z_infinity has two
opposite boundary faces of length at least2 in each of the m distinct ray
directions. Its perimeter is at least4m, while a convex body in radiusR has
perimeter at most2piR (Cauchy's perimeter formula). Therefore R>=2m/pi.
This obstruction does not depend on the coefficient width q. Arbitrarily many
distinct rational unit rays exist. Compactness also shows that for any smaller
uniform radius some finite prefix must lack such a monotone certificate.
This is a representation-specific theorem, NOT exact-threshold hardness or a
general complexity lower bound. It explains why simply adding affine box
transport cannot make the restricted product construction general.

Substantive alternative tried: allow sign-dependent transport. If it still
satisfies the GLOBAL lifting identity B_(t+1)T_t^sign=B_t and box contraction,
it still implies the same physical inclusion and the obstruction survives.
To escape, transport must be valid only on the actual branch's coupled domain,
rather than on the whole coefficient box. For the simple coordinate sign rule,
the union of the two post-action active-coordinate intervals is again the full
box, so its physical image still cannot shrink. A successful representation
therefore needs coupled branch-specific constraints (constrained zonotopes,
piecewise convex tubes or another selector-closed body), and an efficient
universal construction/query proof for them is missing. None is assumed here.
