Setting: functions supported in Omega=(-R,R), extended by zero. Define
 Lambda_e f(x)=(1/pi) int_{|r|>e}(f(x)-f(x+r))/r^2 dr.
This is a bounded symmetric jump operator for each e>0. Its generator -Lambda_e preserves positivity. In a nonlinear equation q_t=-(q/c)(kappa Lambda_e q-beta kappa q+c lambda+B y), positivity is invariant for positive initial q and bounded coefficients on a finite interval.

1. Uniform torsion barrier bound

Let phi(x)=sqrt((R^2-x^2)_+). The exact full operator satisfies Lambda phi=1 inside Omega. For x>=0 put
 D_x(r)=2phi(x)-phi(x+r)-phi(x-r),
 I_e(x)=(1/pi)int_0^e D_x(r)/r^2 dr,
 so Lambda_e phi=1-I_e.

If x<=sqrt(3)R/2, D_x(r)>=0 for every r: inside both edges this is concavity; when only one shifted point remains in Omega its value is <=R<=2phi(x); outside both it is immediate.

If x>sqrt(3)R/2, the only interval where D_x can be negative is (r1,r2), where
 r1=x-sqrt(4x^2-3R^2), r2=x+sqrt(4x^2-3R^2).
It is nonnegative before r1 and after r2. Thus a negative minimum of I_e, if any, could occur only at r2. But nonnegativity of phi gives
 Lambda_{r2}phi(x)<=2phi(x)/(pi r2)<=2/(pi sqrt(3))<1,
 since phi(x)<=R/2 and r2>=sqrt(3)R/2.
Therefore I_{r2}>0, and I_e>=0 for every e. Reflection gives the other half interval. In particular
 Lambda_e phi<=1 uniformly in e and x in Omega.

This supplies the same positive subsolution barrier a(t)phi for the bounded jump approximations if q initially dominates it and the scalar/reaction coefficients have uniform finite-time bounds. At a first contact q=a phi, positivity of the jump kernel gives Lambda_e q<=a Lambda_e phi<=a, with the inequality direction needed for a lower barrier.

2. Uniform mass-tangent coercivity

The Fourier multiplier is
 m_e(xi)=(2/pi)|xi| int_{e|xi|}^infinity (1-cos z)/z^2 dz.
It converges locally uniformly to |xi| and satisfies
 m_e(xi)>=c min(|xi|,1/e)
for an absolute c>0. For e|xi|<=1 this follows by integrating over a fixed positive interval. For large lower limits, partition a comparable-length interval into whole cosine periods to bound the averaged 1-cos from below; the remaining compact range follows by continuity and strict positivity.

Suppose the full restricted form h[v]=kappa int |xi| |vhat|^2-beta kappa ||v||_2^2 is >=eta||v||_2^2 on int cv=0, for functions supported in Omega. Then h_e has the same constraint lower bound eta/2 for all sufficiently small e.

Proof by contradiction: otherwise take e_n->0, ||v_n||_2=1, int cv_n=0 and h_{e_n}[v_n]<eta/2. Their positive jump energies are bounded. For e_n<1/L the multiplier lower bound yields Fourier tail int_{|xi|>L}|vhat_n|^2<=C/L. Their fixed physical support and this tail bound imply L2 precompactness. A subsequence converges strongly to v, with ||v||_2=1 and int cv=0. Restricting Fourier integration to a fixed compact interval and then enlarging it gives liminf jump energy >= full |D| energy. Hence h[v]<=eta/2, contradicting the stipulated full constrained coercivity.
