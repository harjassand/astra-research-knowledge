# Retained local witnesses and the weighted alpha bound

Integration correction and reproducibility check, 14 October 2026.

The kinetic worker's frozen `analysis/proof_alpha_and_countermodel.md` calls the
raw target-block matrix K symmetric. That wording is inaccurate for the actual
producer `Compiler.refraw`: it is an ordinary reversible killed generator and
is generally nonsymmetric in Euclidean coordinates. The producer separately
constructs `refsym=diag(sqrt(p)) K diag(1/sqrt(p))`. The numerical alpha bound
remains valid in the correct geometry; no source model or endpoint is changed.
The worker's separate correction and the original frozen proof are both retained.

Let positive weights p satisfy `p_i K_ij=p_j K_ji`. Suppose K has nonpositive
off-diagonal entries, nonnegative row sums with positive killing somewhere,
and a connected transition graph. Then K is an irreducible nonsingular M-matrix,
its inverse is entrywise positive, and it is self-adjoint and positive definite
in L2(p). Positivity follows directly from the weighted edge Dirichlet form
plus killing; its symmetrized similar matrix S is Euclidean SPD.

For an exact retained vector u>0 and `delta=||Ku−1||_infinity<1`,

    Ku>=(1−delta)1,
    K^-1 1<=u/(1−delta),
    rho(K^-1)<=||K^-1||_infinity
             =max_i(K^-1 1)_i<=max(u)/(1−delta).

Similarity preserves eigenvalues, so

    lambda_min(S)>=alpha(u)=(1−delta)/max(u).

No equality of K and K^T is required. This is a standard positive-inverse and
matrix-norm argument, not a new coercivity principle. The global product
reference has nonnegative generators in its other blocks, so the target-block
alpha also lower-bounds its killed reference on the product weighted space.
The source/form comparison and full-source residual must still hold there.
