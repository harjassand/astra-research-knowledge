2. THE ONLY POSSIBLE VANISHING-GAP SCALE

for k=1,...,n-1. The full substitution from DLMF34.5.8 and34.5.11-14 is in
RACAH_MIDDLE_COMPACTNESS.txt Section1. It was independently audited.

We recall the short fixed-ratio argument so the logical closure is

|u_l|=(2l+1)|eta|/d->2t>0. Sign the columns so u_l is positive. Shift at

A constant-coefficient bilateral second-order recurrence has no nonzero

It must also have z->infinity. A bounded-z subsequence in z<=sqrt3 is

The equivalence of z->infinity and l^2/n->infinity under these conditions

4. FROZEN SYMPLECTIC ENERGY AND UNIFORM TRANSPORT

For k>=1 put h_k=p_(k-1)(v_k-v_(k-1)). Equation(11) gives EXACTLY

  h_(k+1)=h_k-q_k v_k,

Direct substitution shows it is invariant under h'=h-qf, f'=f+h'/p:

  H(p,q;f',h')=H(p,q;f,h).                               (18)

The negative cross sign is essential. With omega=sqrt(q/p), x=sqrt(q)f
and y=h/sqrt(p), H=x^2+y^2-omega xy. If omega<=1,

  (1/2)(qf^2+h^2/p)<=H<=(3/2)(qf^2+h^2/p).              (19)

At fixed nonzero(f,h), log-parameter differentiation gives

  partial_(log q)H=qf^2-(q/p)fh,

Each absolute derivative is <=(3/2)(qf^2+h^2/p)<=3H. Therefore along a
rectangle of positive p,q with q/p<=1,

  |log H(p',q';f,h)-log H(p,q;f,h)|
