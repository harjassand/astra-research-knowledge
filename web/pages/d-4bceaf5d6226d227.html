REVISION 02 — EXACT MULTIPARAMETER FISHER-LOSS INTERPRETATION
UTC: 2026-10-07T13:35:21Z
Worker: c04_l05

CLAIM

The restricted symmetric-separable Choi objective q_p has an equivalent operational interpretation as the minimum weighted SLD quantum-Fisher-information loss of one common POVM, evaluated at the maximally mixed point of a local multiparameter state model. This interpretation is exact and independently derived; it identifies what the convex-roof dual is measuring, but it supplies no new factor-two proof.

DERIVATION

Let H_x=H_x* and consider the local state family

  rho_theta = (I + sum_x theta_x H_x)/d

for theta in a sufficiently small neighborhood of zero. Since I/d is full rank, this is a valid state family locally. At theta=0, rho_0=I/d and ∂_x rho=H_x/d. The SLD equation ∂_x rho=(L_x rho+rho L_x)/2 gives L_x=H_x, so the diagonal SLD-QFI entry is

  J_xx = Tr(rho_0 L_x^2)=tau(H_x^2).

For a rank-one POVM M_y=d w_y P_y with sum_y w_y P_y=I/d, its outcome probability and derivative at zero are

  p_y(0)=w_y,       ∂_x p_y(0)=w_y Tr(P_y H_x).

Consequently its classical Fisher information in coordinate x is

  I_xx(M)=sum_y (∂_x p_y)^2/p_y
          =sum_y w_y (Tr(P_y H_x))^2.

Thus for each prior p, the q_p of INITIAL.txt is exactly

  q_p = inf_M sum_x p_x [J_xx-I_xx(M)],

where the infimum may be restricted to rank-one POVMs. General POVMs can be refined to rank one, which can only increase classical Fisher information by convexity of the squared score; thus allowing them cannot lower this infimum. The same POVM must serve every coordinate x. q_p is therefore a common-measurement incompatibility cost, not a sum of separately optimized single-parameter losses.

The frame ensemble form is also exactly a convex roof at rho=I/d:

  q_p = co[f_p](I/d),      f_p(P)=sum_x p_x Var_P(H_x),

where co denotes the lower convex envelope over density matrices and the primal decompositions are pure-state ensembles of I/d. The dual Y inequality from INITIAL is the standard affine-minorant representation of this convex roof.

A nearby established notion is “broadcasting quantum Fisher information” for a parameterized state family. Lu et al., arXiv:1206.2821, note that at a fixed parameter point the QFI of a single scalar parameter can be broadcast to any number of parties by measure-and-prepare using an optimal measurement; they prove infinite broadcasting over a whole family iff its QFI is uniform, while finite broadcasting need not require uniformity (Theorem 2 and surrounding discussion). This is a useful boundary, not the target theorem: here the issue is one common measurement for a finite, weighted set of tangent directions at a fixed maximally mixed point, and the hypothesis is a given 2-output-compatible channel. Per-direction optimal measurements do not furnish the common measurement in q_p.

PRIOR-ART BOUNDARY

Yu, “Quantum Fisher Information as the Convex Roof of Variance,” arXiv:1302.5311, proves for a *single* Hermitian generator H and unitary encoding that min over pure-state decompositions of rho of average Var(H) equals one quarter of that unitary-orbit QFI (arXiv source lines 3–4, 17–18). This theorem explains the single-observable roof but does not solve the present common-ensemble optimization: for rho=I/d, the unitary-orbit QFI and its variance roof are zero for each H separately (choose an eigenbasis), while a single ensemble need not minimize all directions simultaneously. The tangent-state SLD-QFI above is the local estimation metric for a different, nonstationary parameterization rho_theta=(I+sum theta_x H_x)/d. These two uses of QFI must not be conflated.

EXACT TWO-DIRECTION WITNESS

For one qubit with H_X=X,H_Z=Z and p_X=p_Z=1/2, the common-measurement loss is q_p=1/2. At any pure state with Bloch coordinates x,z, x^2+z^2<=1, so its weighted loss is 1-(x^2+z^2)/2>=1/2; the four Pauli eigenstates with equal weights form a tight frame attaining equality. Hence no common POVM can attain both diagonal SLD-QFI entries simultaneously. This is the n=1 instance of REVISION_01 and gives a direct metrological reading of its primal/dual certificate.

STRUCTURAL CONSEQUENCE

The theorem sought could be recast as follows: for every self-compatible tracial channel Phi and every finite local tangent model {H_x,p_x}, the minimum common-measurement weighted SLD-QFI loss at I/d is at most 2 sum_x p_x tau(H_x(I-Phi)H_x). This is a precise channel-compatibility versus multiparameter-estimation tradeoff. Standard data processing only gives coordinatewise QFI monotonicity and does not give a single POVM nearly attaining all coordinates. The dual coherent-state constraint remains the unresolved step; the metrology wording is a reparameterization, not a derivation of that simultaneous measurement.
