# General mass determinant: one useful identity and one exact extension obstruction

7 October 2026, cycle14. Our deduction; root independent gate requested for the exact counterexample. This concerns a proof mechanism, not universal detector failure.

For every symmetric Q and positive masses m, set Y=diag(sqrt(m)). The Hessian of f(y)=(y^2)^TQ(y^2) satisfies

    B_Q(m)=Y^-1 H(y)Y=4diag(Qm)+8Qdiag(m),
    h(m)=det B_Q(m).                           (1)

Although B_Q is generally nonsymmetric, it is a LINEAR mass pencil. Its eigenvalues at positive m equal those of the symmetric H. Hence, on H>0,

    partial_i log h=tr(B^-1 B_i),
    partial_ij log h=-tr(B^-1 B_i B^-1 B_j),
    Hess(h^(1/n))=(h^(1/n)/n)
                    [Hess log h+grad log h grad log h^T/n].          (2)

The nonsymmetry matters: standard concavity of logdet on a symmetric SPD linear pencil cannot simply be applied to (1). A similarity symmetrizer depending on m is not one common symmetrizer for all pencil coefficients.

## Exact dense QPD/HSPD counterexample to general root-concavity

Let

    A=[[-5,-1,0,4],[4,-2,3,-5],[5,5,-3,4],[-5,-5,4,5]]/3,
    Q=A^T A+diag(1/10,9/10,7/10,3/5),
    m=(71/100,63/50,23/25,5/4),
    delta m=(-65,64,15,-38).

This Q is dense, rational and positive definite. Its leading principal minors are

    919/90, 119663/2700, 45450767/729000,
    11498091029/32805000.

The rational congruence G=Y^-1 H Y^-1=4diag(Qm/m)+8Q is also SPD. Its leading minors are

    355114/3195,
    67790856452/9057825,
    43278093183088/9374848875,
    57754907239281536/1171856109375.

Direct linear-pencil differentiation in (2) gives

    (delta m)^T[Hess log h+grad log h grad log h^T/4](delta m)
      =199685871453119792090948797064584925
        /3257450498259780052978474092304 >0.    (3)

Thus (det H)^(1/4) is locally nonconcave at an H-positive point, despite QPD. There is no universal QPD upgrade of the negative rank-one mass-concavity proof on this basis.

To keep the obstruction narrow, the same point has nonzero L_V and tau=1 matrix-pencil Krylov determinants. The exact rational diagonal-conjugate replay reconstructs V from (2), L_V from quartic polarization and Jac(V) from mass differentiation. It verifies L_V x=V, Jac(V)x=-3V, and both nonzero determinants. Therefore (3) is not an E_F/C/L_V/N_tau counterexample, and no coefficient-only acquisition failure is claimed.

## Negative-off-diagonal frontier and exploratory boundary

If QPD has every off-diagonal entry nonpositive, H>0 forces every coordinate to be nonzero: at y_i=0, H_ii=4 sum_(j!=i) q_ij m_j<=0. Consequently any isolated H-positive projective maximum has full support. A general mass root-concavity theorem on a convex H-positive cone would close the same attractor route for this broader family. Formula (1) makes B a nonsingular M-matrix on the H-positive patch, but this does not by itself prove root-concavity or convexity of the whole mass domain.

A bounded floating discriminator searched for a positive curvature of (2) on this exact prospective family: 300 random n4 strictly diagonally dominant symmetric Q with nonpositive off-diagonals, 20 positive mass trials each, seed140071; 5996 trials had a numerically H-positive Hessian and none produced the threshold violation. This null is not a proof, guarantee, exact certificate or successful acquisition experiment. A separate bounded generic-QPD discriminator (500 matrices,10 masses,seed140072, stopping at first violation) found a violation after323 H-positive trials. The frozen rationalization above was then checked independently by exact arithmetic; no floating evidence is needed for (3).

These experiments had one falsifiable purpose: discriminate extension of root-concavity beyond the rank-one formula. They neither test universal flow/matrix nonvanishing nor estimate acquisition performance. The exact next question is a general nonpositive-Q concavity proof or exact counterexample, preserving both the failed general-QPD extension and the successful rank-one theorem.

Established ingredients are determinant differentiation, diagonal similarity and SPD leading-minor certificates. The extension obstruction and detector scope are our deductions.
