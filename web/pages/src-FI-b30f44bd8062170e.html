# Recoverable local-compliance gating — conditional research calculation

Date: 14 October 2026.

## Status

This package establishes elementary identities in a specified scalar Landau model,
checks two switching paths numerically, and provides an illustrative BaTiO3 parameter
screen. It does not establish historical novelty, a new law of physics, validity
of the scalar approximation for an actual sample, device feasibility, measured
energy recovery, a refrigerator coefficient of performance, or industrial advantage.
No physical experiment or atomistic simulation was performed.

## Proposed operation

Temporarily add a local positive quartic free-energy term by engaging an initially
unloaded elastic shunt coupled to electrostrictive strain. Apply a conjugate field
to traverse the now-convex free energy. Remove the shunt with compensating bias,
return the field to zero, and recover the shunt work. Original endpoint material
free energies are restored rather than permanently modified.

## Exact scalar construction

Let f0(q)=a q^2/2-b q^4/4+c q^6/6, b,c>0,
a=3b^2/(16c), qB^2=3b/(4c). At coexistence,
f0(q)=(c/6)q^2(q^2-qB^2)^2 >= 0.

Control f(q;u,h)=f0(q)+u q^4/4-hq. A simple protocol uses U=b:

1. q=0, h=0; raise u from 0 to U.
2. u=U; increase q from 0 to qB and apply h=a q+c q^5.
3. q=qB; lower u from U to 0 with h=u qB^3.

Stage 2 has f''=a+5c q^4 >= a. During stage 3,

f(q;u,u qB^3)-f(qB;u,u qB^3)
= f0(q)+u (q-qB)^2(q^2+2qB q+3qB^2)/4 >= 0.

Thus the selected state is a global minimum throughout, unique except at the
ungated coexistence endpoints; its local curvature never vanishes.

For 0<U<=b, the middle-stage global curvature bound is
mu=a-9(b-U)^2/(20c). The same construction works whenever mu>0,
using h=a q+(U-b)q^3+c q^5 in stage 2. U=0.55b gives mu=0.514a.

The more general elementary principle is tangent compensation: if F0 has equal
global minima A and B, G is convex and minimized at A, and F0+UG is strongly
convex, the auxiliary potential UG enables a unique-equilibrium traversal. During
removal at B use bias h=u grad G(B); the remaining energy difference contains
u times the nonnegative Bregman divergence of G. Implementing all components of
h and G is a separate physical requirement. This is not a historical novelty claim.

## Mechanical realization and work

Use local strain e and positive effective moduli C and K:

f(q,e)=f0(q)-hq+C(e-alpha q^2)^2/2+K e^2/2.

Eliminating e gives e*=C alpha q^2/(C+K),
u(K)=2 alpha^2 C K/(C+K), and
partial_K f_eff=e*^2/2=(q^4/4) du/dK.

The gate saturates at u_max=2 C alpha^2. A requested U<u_max needs
K/C=U/(u_max-U). A finite preload changes the quadratic coefficient and must
be included; pure quartic control is not automatic.

At fixed temperature the bare material-plus-shunt reversible work is
h dq+(q^4/4)du. Stage 2 supplies U qB^4/4; stage 3 returns the same amount.
Net work is zero only for the stipulated equal-free-energy, quasistatic endpoints
with ideal recovery. One phase-conversion stroke is not a complete thermodynamic
cycle. At coexistence latent heat is balanced by internal-energy change. Finite
thermal fluctuations and kinetic/control losses are not included in this formula.

## Spatial obstruction

A mean-only elastic gate F_gate=U/(4N)(sum_i q_i^2)^2 has the same homogeneous
energy as a local gate. But around a homogeneous q, a zero-mean perturbation has
curvature a-(3b-U)q^2+5c q^4, rather than the local-gate value
a-3(b-U)q^2+5c q^4. At U=b the minimum nonuniform curvature is -a/15.
Thus homogeneous stabilization alone is insufficient. Positive gradient energy
can stabilize small samples but not arbitrarily long-wavelength modes at scale.

More generally, positivity of elastic energy, or a quartic energy lower bound,
does not by itself prove convexity of a nonlinear order-parameter functional.
A full spatial Hessian, additional order parameters, defects, domain motion,
interface slip and fatigue must be assessed in a physical device.

## Numerical checks and restricted benchmark

The script checks exact symbolic identities with SymPy and all real stationary
roots at 1,203 sampled control points for each of two normalized paths. It also
checks the mean-clamp instability with a direct four-cell Hessian eigenvalue.
Floating-point checks are not interval certificates or independent proof review.

It compares against a *constant quadratic gate* k q^2/2 during traversal, matched
to the same global curvature margin. The required k=mu-a+9b^2/(20c). At U=0.55b
the quartic gate circulates approximately 57.47% of the quadratic gate's ideal
work, but requires a larger peak bias. This is not an optimization over arbitrary
charge control, feedback, evolving constraints or multicaloric protocols.

## Illustrative material screen

The BaTiO3 calculation takes the frozen polynomial coefficients B=-1.69e8 and
D=2.37e9 from Table 1 of Bar et al., arXiv:2604.26089v1, with
f0=A P^2+B P^4+D P^6. It sets a=2A by coexistence; it does not claim a
self-consistent temperature-dependent fit. It uses Q12=-0.045, Q11=0.11,
C11=178 GPa and C12=96.4 GPa listed in the same manuscript.

For symmetric in-plane strains exx=eyy=e and zero out-of-plane stress,
C_effective=2(C11+C12-2 C12^2/C11). The local effective shunt is assumed to
apply this equibiaxial loading. A real patterned frame has not been designed or
measured to meet it. The manuscript's end-note temperature formula for the
sixth-order coefficient is not used; the positive Table 1 value is used explicitly.

At U=0.55b the model requires approximately 2.50 MV/m peak field, 0.370 ratio of
reference to active effective biaxial stiffness, 74 MPa in-plane active stress,
and 0.118 MJ/m^3 circulated work. 500 nm thickness is an illustrative voltage
conversion only. These are qualification targets, not achieved performance.

## Prior work / novelty boundaries

- Jun, Gavrilov and Bechhoefer (2014), High-precision test of Landauer's principle
  in a feedback trap. PRL 113, 190601; arXiv:1408.5089. Barrier/tilt restoration
  protocols are established prior art.
- Akcay et al. (2008), Influence of mechanical boundary conditions on the
  electrocaloric properties of ferroelectric thin films. JAP 103, 024104;
  DOI: 10.1063/1.2831222. Static mechanical constraints affecting transition
  order and electrocaloric response are established prior art.
- Gottschall et al. (2018), A multicaloric cooling cycle that exploits thermal
  hysteresis. Nature Materials 17, 929-934; DOI: 10.1038/s41563-018-0166-6.
  Multiple physical stimuli in caloric cycles are established prior art.
- Kvasov and Tagantsev (2013), Role of high-order electromechanical coupling
  terms in thermodynamics of ferroelectric thin films. PRB 87, 184101;
  DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.87.184101. Higher-order coupling limits simple models.
- Luk'yanchuk et al. (2019), Harnessing ferroelectric domains for negative
  capacitance. Communications Physics; DOI: 10.1038/s42005-019-0121-0.
  Charge-controlled/negative-capacitance approaches are necessary comparators.
- Bar et al. (2026), Domain-induced control of latent heat in freestanding
  BaTiO3 membranes. arXiv:2604.26089v1, a preprint dated 28 April 2026.
  Relevant evidence and parameters, not validation of the proposed gate.

An exact earlier implementation of this combined local-compliance and work-recovery
protocol was not established in the targeted search; absence from that search is
not evidence of patentability or scientific priority.
