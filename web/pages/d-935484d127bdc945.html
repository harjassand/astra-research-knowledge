## Actual gradient estimator and its exact law

Use the contract of whole_sampler_transfer.md. For a requested residual RGO R^U_{a,v}, compute a finite center c and cache g_c=grad U(c). Put

    e=g_c+(c-v)/a, zeta=sqrt(a)|e|<=1,
    mu=c-ae=v-a g_c.

The proposal is X=mu+sqrt(a)G, an explicitly known Gaussian. For independent G' standard and T uniform[0,1], let theta=pi T/2 and

    Gamma=mu+sqrt(a)(sin(theta)G+cos(theta)G'),
    Vdot=(pi/2)sqrt(a)(cos(theta)G-sin(theta)G'),
    W=Vdot.[g_c-grad U(Gamma)].                     (R1)

Only one fresh gradient is queried per W; g_c is cached and every rejected W is charged. The endpoints of the path are mu+sqrt(a)G' and X. The fundamental theorem of calculus gives

    E[W|G]=-U(X)+g_c.X + E[U(mu+sqrt(a)G')-g_c.(mu+sqrt(a)G')].

Completing the Gaussian proposal square proves exp(E[W|G])Q is exactly R^U_{a,v} after normalization. No function value is evaluated. Unconditional EW=0 by identical endpoint distributions, so its normalizer Z=E_Q exp(E[W|G])>=1. Finite c creates no omitted approximation error: its known residual was integrated into the proposal mean mu.

## Dimension-free exponential and moment bounds under the proposal

For each fixed T, the orthogonal Gaussian rotation makes Gamma=mu+sqrt(a)Z and Vdot=(pi/2)sqrt(a)H, with independent standard Z,H. Therefore W conditional on Z is centered Gaussian with standard deviation (pi/2)R(Z), where

    R(Z)=sqrt(a)|grad U(mu+sqrt(a)Z)-g_c|.

Co-coercivity/Stein, followed by |mu-c|=sqrt(a)zeta, gives

    E R²<=2(ah)²(tau+zeta²)<=2(ah)²(tau+1).

R is ah-Lipschitz. The norm-square exponential inequality proved in nonodd_trace_lemma.md yields

    E exp(lambda W)<=exp[C lambda²(ah)²(tau+1)],
                                    |lambda|ah<=c, (R2)
    ||W||_p<=C ah[sqrt(tau p)+p], p>=2.             (R3)

For clarity, E exp(lambda W)=E exp[(pi²lambda²/8)R²]; substitute this coefficient in the norm-square inequality. Its logarithmic prefactor is absorbed because tau>=1. Both signs of lambda are covered, and the law conditional on T is the same for every T. Unlike the independent-time straight-line estimator, this estimator has no leading tau term. No realized Hessian-vector map or second-derivative modulus is involved.

## Complete clipping comparison from proposal information alone

This elementary lemma avoids assuming a target-moment bound or applying a density-ratio bound exp(ah tau/2), which would lose the improvement. Let w=E[W|X], wbar=E[clip_B(W)|X], D=|W|1{|W|>B}, and B<=1. Suppose EW=0, E exp(2W)<=C0, and ||W||_p<=B/4 for p>=2. Conditional Jensen gives

    Z=E exp(w)>=1, E exp(2w)<=E exp(2W)<=C0,
    ||w-wbar||_2<=||D||_2,
    exp(wbar)<=exp(B).

For normalized densities the exact elementary comparison is

    TV(Q_exp(w),Q_exp(wbar))<=E|exp(w)-exp(wbar)|/Z.

The inequality |exp(u)-exp(v)|<=|u-v|(exp(u)+exp(v)), followed by Cauchy-Schwarz, gives

    TV<=||D||_2[sqrt(C0)+exp(B)].

Finally E D²<=B^(-(p-2)) E|W|^p<=B²4^-p. Hence

    TV<=C B 2^-p.                                  (R4)

Source Algorithm3.2/FORS produces the clipped exponential tilt exactly. Equations (R2)-(R4) prove local TV delta using p=C log(2/delta) and

    ah[sqrt(tau p)+p]<=cB.                          (R5)

The selected condition also ensures E exp(2W)<=C, since (ah)²tau is small. Neither the true-target moments nor its unknown normalizer need acquisition. This is an independently reconstructed proof, not merely an invocation of the predecessor's theorem.

Expected attempts<=exp(2B) and estimator queries<=2B exp(2B); each estimator uses ONE fresh residual-gradient query. Every failed proposal/estimator is included. Finite center descent only needs zeta<=1, and contracts the computable residual by ah/(1+ah). Its cost under the existing gradient cap is O(L).

## Complete chain and cost substitution

Retain the acquired reference, translated residual, Gaussian initialization, self-contained OU/Langevin W2 bound A², ideal-chain gradient cap M² and first-mismatch coupling from whole_sampler_transfer.md unchanged. Let r=h/q,kappa=1+r, L=log(e+(tau+1)kappa/epsilon), p=C L. Substitute

    eta=min(q^-1,c/[h(sqrt(tau p)+p)]),
    N=ceil(C[1+r(sqrt(tau L)+L)]L),
    delta=epsilon/(10N), a=eta/(1+qeta).

Here log(N/epsilon)<=C'L, so p is enough for local (R4). Exact-chain TV mixing still follows from A(1+qeta)^(-(N-1))/sqrt(2pi eta); its logarithm is O(L). The ideal cap at |grad U(v)|²<=C N M²/epsilon retains the same Markov union bound. The fixed descent count is O(L) because aM²<=M²/q is polynomial only in r,tau and logN is O(L). Every local RGO at valid history has TV<=delta. Stop at first cap or replacement mismatch, so Ndelta and the IDEAL cap probability bound the entire implemented path error without transporting moments through approximate chains.
