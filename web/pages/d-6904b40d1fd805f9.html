## The September24 zero-fibre companion

This third manuscript was fully read to settle the mapping boundary. It is scientifically different from the October5 construction. In C[h,u,v,w], let

  x=u³+hv, y=−u²+hw,
  S=2u³v+3u⁴w+h(v²−3u²w²)+h²w³,
  P(x,y,S)=−2S²x+3Sy²−3S³y,
  F=h−P−1.

The elementary identity x²+y³=hS is exact. Put X=x+S³,Y=y−S². Then

  X²+Y³=S(1+F).

At (h,u,v,w)=(2,0,−1/2,1/2), one gets S=1,X=Y=0,F=−1. Differentiating the universal identity gives dF=0 at that point. Consequently F cannot be a coordinate after adjoining ANY number of variables: all old derivatives vanish there, and its added-variable derivatives remain zero. A polynomial automorphism with F as a component would have singular Jacobian, contradicting the chain rule for its inverse. Thus this companion cannot be the October5 one-stable example.

Its zero fibre is nevertheless A^3. The relevant reusable source tool is a polynomial lifting lemma over any commutative base B with x²+y³=hS and (h,y)=B. If αh+βy=1, the cusp presentation with variables U,V,W can be rewritten using G=V+UW:

  hG−yU=x,
  hW=y+U²=A, y²W=S−hG²+2UyG=B1.

Compatibility is hB1−y²A=hS−y³−(hG−yU)²=0. Since

  [α(1+βy)]h+β²y²=1,

the unique solution is W=α(1+βy)A+β²B1, with V=G−UW. The remaining relation has polynomial coordinates

  U=hT−βx, G=yT+αx, T=αU+βG.

No division or domain hypothesis is used in this lifting lemma.

For the actual zero fibre, take

  B=C[h,x,y,S]/(h−1−P,x²+y³−Sh).

The translation above identifies B with C[X,Y], since X²+Y³=S. The explicit certificate is

  α=1+2S²x+4S^5,
  β=(3S³−3Sy)(1+2S²x)−4S⁴y²,
  αh+βy−1=(1+2S²x)(h−1−P)−4S⁴(x²+y³−Sh).

In particular the 4S^5 term in α is required; omitting it breaks the free-polynomial certificate. The lifting lemma gives C[h,u,v,w]/(F)=B[T]=C[X,Y,T]. This reconstruction is literal algebra; it does not independently verify the Lean companion LND implementation.
