1. SPECTRAL PROFILE AND INVERSE COMPARISON

With c_l=l(l+2), the derived Berezin eigenvalue is

  b_l=prod_(a=0)^(l-1) (m-a)/(m+3+a),   a_l=1-b_l.

The logarithmic product estimate gives
  b_l<=exp[-c_l/(m+l+2)].
For 1<=l<=m, m+l+2<=4m. Using
  1-exp(-x)>=(1-exp(-1))min{x,1}
then gives
  a_l>=(c_* /4) min{c_l/m,1}, c_*=1-exp(-1).
The other direction follows from 1-prod(1-x_a)<=sum x_a:
  a_l<=sum_(a<l)(3+2a)/(m+3+a)<=c_l/m.
Thus
  1/a_l<=(4/c_*)(1+m/c_l),
where the additive 1 is necessary because the spectrum saturates at large
l. The minimum gap is a_1=3/(m+3). All inequalities hold at l=m and m=1;
no continuum approximation is involved.

2. ARBITRARY RIGHT-KERNEL GREEN LOWER

Let A be any complex matrix with ||A||_HS=1 and rank(A)<=d-h. Choose any
rank-h projection P in its right kernel, so AP=0. Write
  alpha=Tr(A)/d, X=A-alpha I, P0=P-(h/d)I.
Then X is traceless and, by cyclicity, Tr(PX)=Tr(PA)-alpha h=-alpha h.
For G_B(P)=sum_(l>=1)||P_l||^2/a_l, weighted Cauchy--Schwarz gives
  h^2|alpha|^2<=G_B(P) E_B(A),
while the first nonzero spectral gap gives
  ||X||^2<=E_B(A)/a_1.
Since 1=d|alpha|^2+||X||^2,
  E_B(A)>=1/[dG_B(P)/h^2+1/a_1].
This argument does not require A normal, positive, Hermitian, or a coherent
filter. The only kernel identity used is AP=0; the trace identity is valid
even when alpha is complex.

For completeness, set u_l=||P_l||^2. On vectorized matrix space, the
projector Pi_l onto H_(l,l) has partial trace
  Tr_2 Pi_l=(l+1)^3 I/d.
This follows because the partial trace commutes with the irreducible SU(3)
action and its trace is dim(H_(l,l))=(l+1)^3. Since vec(P) has squared
norm h and first support P,
  u_0=h^2/d, sum_(l>=1)u_l=h-h^2/d<=h,
  u_l<=h^2(l+1)^3/d.
The last inequality is the rank-one operator bound
|vec(P)><vec(P)|<=h(P tensor I).

Let R=(d/h)^(1/4), L=min(m,floor R). Splitting the Green sum at L,
  G_C=sum u_l/c_l
      <=(h^2/d)sum_(l<=L)(l+1)^3/[l(l+2)] + h/[(L+1)(L+3)],
with the tail omitted if L=m. For l>=1,
  (l+1)^3/[l(l+2)]<=(8/3)l,
so the low sum is <=(8/3)L^2. If a tail remains L+1>R. Hence
  G_C<=(11/3) h^(3/2)/sqrt(d).
The inverse-spectrum inequality now yields
  G_B<=(4/c_*)[h+mG_C]
      <=G0 h^(3/2),
  G0=(4/c_*)[1+11sqrt(2)/3]<52,
using m/sqrt(d)<=sqrt(2). When 1<=h<d/2,
  1/a_1=(m+3)/3<=m+1<=sqrt(2d)<d/sqrt(h).
Substitution gives
  E_B(A)>sqrt(h)/[(G0+1)d]>sqrt(h)/(53d).
Every cutoff endpoint and the omitted-tail case are consistent. This proves
the lower for every complex matrix of the required rank, not just a proposed
optimizer family.

3. EXPLICIT RADIAL CONDUCTANCE AND RANK CHECK

Index the occupation shell by ell=m-n3; it has ell+1 states. For diagonal
F=f(ell), summing the two external-root edges gives exactly
  ||F||_HS^2=sum_(ell=0)^m (ell+1) f_ell^2,
  E_C(F)=sum_(ell=0)^(m-1) C_ell(f_(ell+1)-f_ell)^2,
  C_ell=(m-ell)(ell+1)(ell+2).
For one undirected edge the two normalized Hermitian generators contribute
one factor |matrix element|^2=(n_i+1)n_3; summing the two choices i=1,2
and the ell+1 states gives C_ell. There is no extra factor two.

For the cap f_ell=(K+1-ell)/(K+1) through K and zero after K, summing the
shell norm and conductances gives
  ||F||^2=(K+2)^2(K+3)/[12(K+1)],
  E_C(F)/||F||^2=(4m-3K)/(K+2).
Together with E_B<=E_C/m, this gives the claimed <=4/(K+2) upper. The
boundary edge K to K+1 is included in the energy; omitting it would change
the formula.

For the near-full resistance filter, assume m>=8 and 1<=h<=m^2/128. Let
r=ceil(sqrt(2h)), B=2r. Then r<=m/4 and B<=m/2. Setting f=0 below r,
using increments proportional to 1/C_j on j=r-1,...,B-1, and setting f=1
from B onward gives zero-shell rank at least r(r+1)/2>=h. The resistance
R=sum_(j=r-1)^(2r-1)1/C_j has r+1 terms. On this interval
C_j<=m(2r)(2r+1)<=6mr^2, hence R>=1/(6mr), E_C=1/R<=6mr. The unit tail
contains d-B(B+1)/2 states and
  B(B+1)/[(m+1)(m+2)]<=m/[4(m+1)]<1/4,
so ||F||^2>3d/4. Thus
  E_B(F)/||F||^2<=8r/d<8(sqrt(2)+1)sqrt(h)/d<20sqrt(h)/d.
The overshoot h beyond the actual zero rank is permitted because the allowed
rank is an upper bound d-h. The remaining near-full regime is correctly
covered by the cap filter; for m<8 the stated finite bound is enough. The
regime split covers all integer Q, and the conversion to
f_d(Q)=sqrt(1/Q-1/d) uses the correct factor sqrt(2) on Q>d/2.

4. PURE ORBIT OPERATIONAL EQUALITY

For B in End(H_m), direct expansion of the rank-one commutator gives
  int ||[P_v,B]||_HS^2 dv
    =(2/d)sum_l(1-b_l)||B_l||_HS^2.
If a channel has Kraus A_i, put B_i=A_i/sqrt(d); TP implies
sum_i||B_i||_HS^2=1. Its Haar-average pure infidelity is exactly
  int [1-Tr(P_v Phi(P_v))]dv
    =sum_i sum_l(1-b_l)|| (B_i)_l||_HS^2.
Each B_i has rank at most the maximum hybrid branch dimension Q, even when
classical labels are unrestricted or the decoder has many Kraus operators.
By homogeneity of Lambda_m(Q), the average infidelity is at least Lambda.
Half-trace distance dominates pure-target infidelity, so this lower applies
to worst-case full-state error.

The positivity replacement in COLD_CARRIER is also valid. For Hermitian T
and an SVD A=U diag(sigma) V^dag,
  ||[T,A]||^2
   - (||[T,|A|]||^2+||[T,|A^dag|]||^2)/2
    =sum_(i,j)sigma_i sigma_j
       |<u_i|T|u_j>-<v_i|T|v_j>|^2>=0.
Integrating T=P_v shows at least one modulus has no larger energy, while
preserving rank and Hilbert--Schmidt norm. Compactness then supplies a
positive minimizing filter F. The covariant channel
  Phi_F(X)=d int F_U X F_U dU
is TP by Schur averaging. For a coherent input P_v, its output is invariant
under the stabilizer U(2)xU(1). The target line is the unique one-dimensional
trivial stabilizer component in Sym^m(C^3); equivalently, it is the unique
N=0 state and the U(1) charge separates it from N>0. Thus the output has no
coherence between P_v and its orthogonal complement. For a density matrix
with this block structure, half trace distance to P_v is exactly
1-Tr(P_v Phi_F(P_v)). Covariance makes this error constant in v, so the
variational minimizer attains the operational worst-case error Lambda.
This verifies the load-bearing whole-trace step; a dipole or fidelity claim
alone would not suffice without the stabilizer block argument.

5. THERMAL AND IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE CHECK

The vacuum eigenvalue is w0=1/Z and every nonvacuum eigenvalue is at most
qbar*w0. Hence entrywise in the occupation basis,
  ||[rho_U,B]||^2>=w0^2(1-qbar)^2||[P_v,B]||^2.
The self-score witness rho_U/w0 is an effect, yielding
  e_thermal>=w0(1-qbar)^2 Lambda >=(1-qbar)^4 Lambda,
since Z<=[(1-q1)(1-q2)]^-1. This uses a pointwise eigenvalue gap, not an
invalid lower transfer from a positive coherent mixture. For the upper,
pi(A)=d int pi(A^(1/2))P_v pi(A^(1/2))dv is a positive coherent mixture;
trace-norm convexity correctly transfers the pure upper in the upper-bound
direction only.

The radial finite cubature has degree at most 2m in vv^dag for the
completeness and composed channel. Phase grids of 2m+1 nodes annihilate all
nonzero Fourier modes; after phase averaging the stick variables have
polynomial degrees at most 2m+1 in t1 (including Haar density 2(1-t1)) and
2m in t2. The stated m+1-node positive Gauss rules therefore integrate
exactly. The label count [(2m+1)(m+1)]^2 is consistent. This checks a finite
CPTP realization, not its efficient gate synthesis, numerical conditioning,
or bit complexity.
