Problem: infer unknown nonlinear drift b(x), constant positive-definite D, and
nonreversible circulation from ONLY stationary snapshots under known additive drift
controls u_i; no trajectories. Full state observation and common b,D mandatory.

Candidate mathematical invariant: for p_i,p_j stationary, r=p_i/p_j,
w=grad(log r), delta_u=u_i-u_j, every admissible scalar weight q(r) obeys
  D : E_{p_i}[q(r) w w^T] = E_{p_i}[q(r) delta_u dot w].
Proof: J_i = r J_j + p_i(delta_u-Dw), div J_i=div J_j=0; multiply
by f(r), f'(r)=q(r)/r, integrate by parts. All probability currents cancel.
If weighted Fisher matrix tensors span Sym(d), constant D is unique.

Drift reconstruction: with d+1 regimes, W=[grad log(p_i/p0)]_{i=1}^d,
h_i=D:(Hess p_i/p_i-Hess p0/p0)-div(u_i)-u_i dot grad log p_i
    +div(u0)+u0 dot grad log p0;
where W invertible b=W^{-T}h, giving conditional full generator uniqueness.

Efficient restricted special case: if log(p_i/p0)=a_i^T x-c_i, A=[a_i] invertible,
U=[u_i], then B=U A^{-1}, D=sym(B), K=skew(B), b=B grad log p0.
Nonlinear p0 permitted. Logistic labelled snapshot classification learns a_i without
learning density scores; to get b or entropy production, score acquisition needed.

Counterexamples: 2 regimes fail to identify full 2D drift even with known D;
