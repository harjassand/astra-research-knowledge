# A constructive repair of the shared-RM carrier obstruction

Status: conditional derivation, being independently audited. Imports the released all-K3 exact KS / exterior-return / mixed ordinary-HC claims. Does not prove unrestricted mixed GHC or certify the imported release.

## The precise subclass

Let E be a real quadratic field with active embedding σ0 and inactive embedding σ1. Let S_1,...,S_r be projective complex K3 surfaces whose normalized transcendental modules V_i=T(S_i)(1) have E-dimension three, End_Hdg(T(S_i))=E, and joint Hodge group the full product ∏_i Res_(E/Q) SO(V_i,q_i). Assume the even Clifford quaternion algebras of all the three-dimensional E-quadratic forms have the same E-Brauer class D. Equivalently their adjoint spin groups can be identified with SL1(D). D is split at σ0 and definite quaternionic at σ1.

Conditional on the new family032 premises, the proposed conclusion is GHC for every mixed product of powers of these S_i, including the shared-field cross constituents that defeat the naive individual-carrier argument.

## Auxiliary K3 surfaces for each unordered pair

For i≠j take U_ij=D as a four-dimensional E-vector space, with invariant quadratic form −Nrd_D and the two spin groups acting by left and right multiplication. Choose the identifications with SL1(D) so that their active circle orientations are compatible. Conjugation by an E-rational quaternion of negative reduced norm at the split real place can reverse an orientation; weak approximation supplies the required signs. Alternatively scale the form by an E-element with independently prescribed real signs. Over a splitting field its standard representation is spin(V_i)⊗spin(V_j). Give it the Hodge circle induced by the two original circles.

At σ0, both spin factors have eigenweights ±1/2 under the weight-zero cocharacter, so U has eigenweights 1,0,0,−1. At σ1 both original circles are trivial. Equivalently the actual Hodge circle has eigenweights 2,0,0,−2 at σ0. After a Tate untwist, U is a weight-two Hodge structure with h^(2,0)=1.

The real quadratic signatures of −Nrd are (2,2) at the split real place and (0,4) at the quaternionic place. The trace form therefore has rational signature (2,6). Its polarization is compatible with the tensor Hodge structure. For independent periods the Hodge group is the image of Res SL1(D)_i×Res SL1(D)_j, namely full Res SO(U_ij).

This eight-dimensional rational quadratic Hodge structure can be realized as the transcendental cohomology of an auxiliary projective K3 surface S_ij. One needed lattice step is explicit: embed its rational form into the K3 rational lattice of signature (3,19) by selecting a fourteen-dimensional complement of signature (1,13), prescribed determinant, and local Hasse invariants. In dimension fourteen the local forms with those invariants exist; Hilbert reciprocity matches their global product. Hasse–Minkowski gives the rational complement. Intersect the image with the integral K3 lattice to obtain a primitive integral transcendental candidate. The period theorem realizes its period, and a positive class in the complement makes the surface projective. Full irreducibility ensures that no extra rational (1,1) part appears in U.

For a fully formal theorem, this lattice realization lemma should be written with the chosen K3 lattice determinant/Hasse invariants and the polarization normalization. No computational check replaces it.

## One exterior return can carry a cross token

At an embedding σ,

    wedge²_E U_ij,σ ≅ V_i,σ ⊕ V_j,σ.

This is the usual identity wedge²(A⊗B)=(Sym² A⊗wedge² B)⊕(wedge² A⊗Sym² B) for two-dimensional spin modules. Thus the direct-sum decomposition of wedge⁴_Q U_ij contains

    wedge² U_ij,σ0 ⊗ wedge² U_ij,σ1,

and hence contains V_i,σ0⊗V_j,σ1. The carrier is wedge FOUR, not wedge two. Its degree four still spends only one exterior return, because every exterior power of one K3 transcendental module has normalized level at most two and the exact-KS source constructs an H² return for all exterior degrees.

For a same-label token pair, wedge²_Q V_i contains V_i,σ0⊗V_i,σ1. For a token only at one embedding, V_i itself is the carrier.

## Exact token packing proves the carrier bound

Choose one complex irreducible constituent of a rationally simple H(d) in the tensor category generated by the V_i. For each i and σ, its SO3 highest weight is a nonnegative integer j_(i,σ), measured in units of the standard three-dimensional highest weight. Put

    Lσ = Σ_i j_(i,σ),    c = max(Lσ0,Lσ1).

The active cocharacter has maximum Lσ0 on that constituent. Its Galois conjugate occurs in the rational H and has maximum Lσ1. Product-group Weyl symmetries attain the corresponding negatives. Thus H has Hodge level exactly 2c. If its Hodge coniveau in H^(2d)(X) is at least p, then c≤d−p.

Form two token lists: at σ0 list i repeated j_(i,σ0) times, and at σ1 list i repeated j_(i,σ1) times. Pad the shorter list with blanks and match positions arbitrarily. For each position use:

- distinct nonblank i,j: wedge⁴ U_ij;
- equal nonblank i=i: wedge² V_i;
- one nonblank label i: V_i.

Every position is nonempty on at least one side. The tensor product of the c rational full exterior carriers contains the desired constituent by multiplying the corresponding highest-weight vectors and taking the Cartan component. The auxiliary circles were constructed from the original ones, so this is a Hodge constituent, including the finite spin-cover actions. The desired constituent has even spin parity on each involved original factor; therefore the finite lift kernels act trivially there.

Rationality then gives a nonzero rational Hodge map from this carrier product onto H(d), surjective by rational simplicity. As in MIXED_GHC_CARRIER_AUDIT.md, ordinary HC on products of original and auxiliary K3 surfaces algebraizes this quotient map. The tensor of c exterior returns and composition has target support codimension d−c≥p. Hence it realizes the required geometric coniveau.

This bypasses the unproved spin shuffle for this subclass by introducing actual K3 surfaces, rather than arbitrary new abelian spin objects. It uses only HC on mixed products of K3 surfaces for the transport, precisely the interface supplied by the new release.

## Boundaries

- Rank-three E-modules and common quaternion class are essential to this explicit construction. If D_i and D_j have distinct Brauer classes, a four-dimensional E-quadratic form with trivial discriminant cannot have the two inequivalent quaternion algebras as its two Clifford components. A larger auxiliary K3 representation or a genuine spin-shuffle algebraicity theorem is needed.
- An arbitrary product of general K3 transcendental modules cannot be placed in the tensor category of one auxiliary K3: the signature, h^(2,0)=1, and rational dimension ≤21 constraints are real.
- CM central weights, larger real endomorphism fields, and shared SO4 chains need their own carrier accounting. The construction above is not asserted to settle them.
- Prior-art novelty remains unverified. This is a direct consequence mechanism under extraordinarily strong new manuscript premises, not an independent resolution of those premises.

## Adversarial character fixture and nearest prior art

`EXTERIOR_CHARACTER_FIXTURE.json` enumerates the exact split-group weights of U at its two embeddings. The required highest weight (2,0,0,2) has multiplicity zero in wedge² U (dimension 28) and multiplicity one in wedge⁴ U (dimension 70). The fixture catches the exterior-degree error but certifies no geometry or algebraicity.

Nearest primary work that must be checked before a novelty claim:

- Salman Abdulali, *Hodge structures on abelian varieties of type III*, Annals of Mathematics 155 (2002), 915–928, https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2002/155-3/p07 and https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410024. The paper proves usual-HC-to-GHC implications and all-powers GHC for certain four-dimensional quaternionic abelian varieties. Its product/dominance hypotheses may cover parts of the present conditional subclass.
- Yuwei Zhu, *Hodge structure of K3 type with real multiplication and Simple Abelian Fourfolds with Definite Quaternionic Multiplication*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10157. Its arithmetic construction identifies all simple definite-quaternion abelian fourfolds as Kuga–Satake realizations of rank-six RM K3 structures, making the Abdulali comparison directly relevant.
- Ulrich Schlickewei, https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.2503, proves ordinary HC for self-products of specific rank-six RM K3 surfaces; this is an existing conditional/family-specific baseline.

No absence-of-prior-art inference is drawn from the searches.

## A sharper Brauer obstruction to uniform pairing

The tensor of the two complex spin standard modules has Tits algebra D_i⊗_E D_j. When their Brauer classes agree this is split and the tensor descends to a four-dimensional E-representation. When the classes differ, the product class is nonzero of order two. Over the number field E its index is two. Thus any E-rational realization of this complex irreducible tensor has multiplicity at least two and E-dimension at least eight. Its active (2,0) line is also doubled. It cannot be the transcendental Hodge structure of a K3 surface, which has h^(2,0)=1. The obstruction is not remedied by just increasing the rational quadratic dimension while retaining this irreducible spin-tensor character.

This identifies the smallest outside-subclass test for the unrestricted target: two independent rank-three RM K3 surfaces over the same real quadratic field with different finite-place quaternion ramification. Their level-two cross constituent still demands divisor support; the common-D auxiliary pairing no longer supplies it.
