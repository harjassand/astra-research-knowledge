# Cycle 9: canonical rational kinetics without denominator expansion

## Main result

I proved and implemented an exact polynomial-input-time certificate for a
useful reversible enzyme subclass that is strictly broader than a single
shared denominator. Each module may have its own nonsingular positive
binding-polynomial denominator and a common positive numerator regulator,
provided its forward and reverse mass-action/Hill numerator monomials are the
two endpoints of one reversible reaction and both directions share those
module factors. Clearing the denominators conceptually makes every term tuple a reversible edge pair. The
whole lifted graph is therefore weakly reversible, so every active edge at
an essential maximum points inward, including under exact source ties. The
implementation never builds the potentially exponential product support.

This yields an exact `CERTIFIED` ordinary-endotactic result in time polynomial
in the sparse factorized input bit length. It is an actual algorithm for the
named reversible syntax, not the broad C8 QF-LRA wrapper. The upper bound
remains a restricted structural theorem: general arbitrary positive rational
channels are coNP-complete by the separate C8 internal candidate.
The mathematical implication after expansion is standard: weakly reversible
finite networks are endotactic. This is an expansion-free compiler for a
specific factor-list encoding, not a new geometric principle or a priority
claim.

The executable also offers a **sufficient** standard-strong gate. The schema
requires a nonempty module list and rejects self-loops, so rank zero is not
accepted and every valid input has rank at least one. At rank exactly one it
returns strong `CERTIFIED`; otherwise it returns strong `UNKNOWN`. The strong
maximum is global over all sources, with neutral channels retained. A
nonsingular two-module fixture
demonstrates why this restraint matters: it is weakly endotactic but has an
exact direction where only neutral sources attain the global maximum and no
inward reaction is there.

## Mechanism and biological boundary

The positive control uses the canonical common-modular enzyme example
`A+B <-> 2C`. Liebermeister, Uhlendorf, and Klipp describe its denominator
as `1+a'+b'+a'b'+2c'+c'^2`, with denominator terms representing binding
states and the forward/reverse numerator states `AB00` and `00CC`.
The fixture receives weak and strong certificates. Its unit rational
constants are compiler placeholders, not experimental measurements. For
this one-module network, the strong-endotactic permanence theorem transfers
to the rational ODE by a positive time change. This is prior theorem use,
not a new permanence theorem or validation of an empirical model fit.

The sharp negative boundary is Schnell–Mendoza's multiple-alternative-
substrate QSSA law. Its shared occupancy denominator is positive and
nonsingular, but all substrate channels are irreversible. The compiler
returns an exact counterdirection in linear time in the module count; the
generic shared-factor LP checker independently finds a refutation, and its
zero-query budget correctly returns `UNKNOWN`. For positive pair totals
`T_i=S_i+P_i`, the reduced law also gives

\[
 S_i(t)\le S_i(0)\exp\!\left[-
 \frac{\kappa_i}{1+\sum_j T_j/K_j}\,t\right],
 \qquad \kappa_i=k_{cat,i}E_0/K_i.
\]

Thus this irreversible competing-substrate model is not permanent: all
substrates tend to zero. This is exact for the stated reduced QSSA ODE. Its
biological interpretation is subject to the paper's QSSA condition; no
approximation-error bound for the unreduced enzyme-complex mechanism is
claimed. For the synthetic fixture, the initial-state Eq. 38 scale ratios
are \(1/3,1/6,2/9\), with maximum \(1/3\); this does not demonstrate the
required much-less-than-one QSSA regime.

## Permanence statuses

For the general paired-reversible module class, the lift is endotactic. The
provided exact positive coefficient interval allows the internally
reconstructed N33 result to be used **conditionally**; this package does not
reprove N33. When the strong gate passes, published strong-endotactic
permanence applies to the finite lifted power-law system. If `F=G/D`, the
positive clock change `t(s)=integral_0^s D(y(u))du` transfers its permanent
orbit because `D` has positive finite bounds on the eventual compact
positive set.

No strong conclusion is inferred from weak reversibility alone. If modules
have different stoichiometric spans, the compiler returns `UNKNOWN` for
standard strong endotacticity unless the one-line gate applies. Ordinary
endotacticity remains certified. General rank-greater-than-one permanence
therefore remains conditional on N33 under this route.

## Costs and comparator

The input charges every endpoint coordinate, numerator/denominator factor
support, rational coefficient, and exponent bit. Rational fields are JSON
integers or exact integer, ratio, or finite-decimal strings without scientific
exponents; floats are rejected. Positivity and each denominator constant term
are checked syntactically. The certificate checks reversible endpoint pairing by
construction and uses exact rational arithmetic for a collinearity gate and
rate bounds. Its runtime and memory are polynomial in the input length.
For the simple directed graph used by the permanence box, coincident
same-direction tuple edges are summed and \(K=\sum_r M_r\) safely covers
collisions across modules. Two identical unit \(0\leftrightarrow X\) modules
therefore report merged-edge \(K=2\). The standalone exact CLI disables
Python's default decimal conversion cap, so large rational inputs and lift
count/bound outputs remain exact strings without floating-point conversion
or truncation; their digits are charged in the input/output length.

For `R` modules with `U_r` shared-numerator and `T_r` denominator factor-term
tuples, explicit denominator clearing may require up to
`2 sum_r U_r product_{s != r} T_s` lifted edge labels. With empty numerator
factors and `R` two-term denominators this is `2 R 2^(R-1)`. The tool reports
this integer, as well as an exact enclosing positive coefficient box, without
generating those labels. Existing MILP recognition for explicit finite
reaction diagrams is the comparator once that lift is materialized. No
experimental parameter acquisition is performed; structural results hold
for arbitrary positive coefficients within the declared syntax.

As a finite scaling check, the replay certified a 24-module factorized input
whose lift upper bound is 402,653,184 labeled edges without generating the
lift. This is implementation evidence for the no-expansion path, not an
asymptotic benchmark or a formal runtime proof.

## Evidence and remaining boundary

`replay_canonical.py` passed exact rational checks for the primary modular
reversible example, the weak-but-not-strong witness, and the Schnell–Mendoza
decay certificate. It also passes the cross-module merged-coefficient
counterexample, rejects the self-loop/rank-zero schema input, round-trips a
5,001-digit positive rational, and reports exact \(2^{15000}\) tuple counts
for a generated 15,000-factor input through the CLI without expanding it. The
QSSA diagnostic records the synthetic fixture's maximum Eq. 38 ratio \(1/3\),
so the fixture is not evidence that the QSSA smallness condition holds. The
C10 hostile audit found the former merged-edge-bound and decimal-conversion
defects; the exact repairs and regression results are in the repair record.
`child_complexity/replay.py` passed the inherited
positive shared-denominator tie/neutral/boundary regressions. The generic
common-factor backend returns `UNKNOWN` at `--max-lps 0` and `REFUTED` on the
same alternative-substrate fixture with a rational witness.

The results are internally proved and finite-tested; they have not received
independent external proof review. The primary papers support the model
forms and prior permanence/recognition results, but no novelty or priority
claim is made. The C8 broad coNP-hardness theorem is not transferred to this
reversible syntax. Conversely, the positive result does not cover
reaction-specific denominators when forward and reverse channels do not
share their module denominator, unpaired irreversible modules, signed
denominator circuits, or arbitrary rate laws outside the exact interface.
