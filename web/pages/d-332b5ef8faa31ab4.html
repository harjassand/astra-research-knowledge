WEIGHTED REVERSIBLE CENTER / QUANTUM FIBER LIFT
Worker c05_l05, resumed 2026-10-07. Internal derivation; external correctness and priority are unverified.

STATUS

An exact state-preserving extension of the center/fiber example works for every finite reversible Markov kernel, including nonuniform stationary weights and arbitrarily slow classical modes. For this entire explicitly represented subclass, one EB channel preserves the full center evolution and approximates every power of the corrected broadcaster marginal in normalized L-infinity-to-L-one norm with error at most 4^(-m), uniformly in the number of sectors, fiber dimension, stationary weights, and spectral gap. The marginal can remain non-EB at the first step. This settles the lift only for the named representation class; it does not extend to arbitrary positive selfcompatible channels.

1. TRACIAL ALGEBRA AND REVERSIBLE INSTRUMENT

Let r,q be positive integers. Let P=(P_wz) be a row-stochastic r-by-r matrix with a faithful stationary probability vector pi and detailed balance

  sum_z P_wz=1,       sum_w pi_w P_wz=pi_z,
  pi_w P_wz=pi_z P_zw.

On the finite C*-algebra A=direct_sum_(z=1)^r M_q, use the faithful normalized trace

  tau(A)=sum_z pi_z tr_q(A_z),       tr_q=Tr/q.

Define the UCP map L and the center conditional expectation E by

  L(A)_w=sum_z P_wz A_z,
  E(A)_z=tr_q(A_z) I_q.

L is unital by row stochasticity and tau-preserving by stationarity. Detailed balance gives tau-selfadjointness:

  <A,L(B)>_tau
    =sum_wz pi_w P_wz tr_q(A_w^* B_z)
    =sum_wz pi_z P_zw tr_q(A_w^* B_z)
    =<L(A),B>_tau.

E is the tau-orthogonal projection onto the center Z(A), so E is UCP, tau-preserving, selfadjoint, and entanglement breaking.

There is an explicit instrument decomposition of L. For each ordered pair (w,z), define the CP map L_wz(A) to have only block w nonzero, with

  (L_wz(A))_w=P_wz A_z.

Then L=sum_(w,z) L_wz. Its tau-adjoint effect is

  M_wz=P_zw P_z,

where P_z is the unit in block z: indeed tau(L_wz(A))=pi_w P_wz tr_q(A_z)=tau(A M_wz) by detailed balance. The effects sum to I, and tau(M_wz)=pi_z P_zw. The normalized prepared state is

  omega_wz=M_wz/tau(M_wz)=P_z/pi_z,

which has tau(omega_wz)=1. Hence the instrument-append map

  B(A)=sum_(w,z) L_wz(A) tensor omega_wz

is CP and tau-to-(tau tensor tau) preserving. Its first marginal is L. Its second marginal is

  sum_(w,z) tau(L_wz(A)) omega_wz=E(A).

Symmetrize B over the two outputs. The resulting broadcaster has common marginal C=(L+E)/2. Postprocess both outputs by C to obtain a genuine broadcaster with common marginal

  Phi=C^* C=C^2,

because C is tau-selfadjoint. This proves actual selfcompatibility by construction, not just Choi compatibility. The finite exact matrix-unit check for one nonuniform instance is `weighted_instrument_channel_check.py` and its JSON output.

This construction can also be seen in a physical block representation H=direct_sum_z C^q with central reference density sigma_z=pi_z I_q/q. The original instrument Kraus operators K_wz=sqrt(P_zw)|w><z| tensor I_q are CPTP and fix sigma. Their likelihood similarity is exactly the reversible UCP map L above. In the direct-sum algebra the weighted trace tau is tracial; do not interpret tau as a trace on the whole ambient M_(rq), where sigma is not central.

2. EXACT SUM OF SQUARES

Since C=(L+E)/2 and Phi=C^*C, expansion gives

  2(I-Phi)-(I-E)
    =(I-L^*L)+(E-E^2)+(L-E)^*(L-E)/2 >=0.

Each term is positive: L is an L2(tau) contraction by UCP plus tau preservation; E is an orthogonal projection; and the last term is a square. Thus a single EB map obeys

  I-E <= 2(I-Phi).

The coefficient 2 is the exact instrument identity used in the supplied cycle-4 attack. This weighted realization preserves the slow center instead of substituting a fixed-point projection.

3. FULL POWER LIFT WITH SLOW MODES RETAINED

As a Hilbert space,

  L2(A,tau)=L2(pi) tensor M_q
          = center scalars direct_sum blockwise-traceless fibers.

L acts as P on each matrix coordinate, while E is identity on center scalars and zero on each fiber-traceless component. If lambda is any eigenvalue of the reversible Markov operator P, then

  Phi|center(lambda)=((1+lambda)/2)^2,
  Phi|fiber(lambda)=lambda^2/4.

All eigenvalues of reversible stochastic P lie in [-1,1]. Consequently the corrected channel retains every center eigenmode with its exact eigenvalue ((1+lambda)/2)^2, however close lambda is to 1, while its fiber-traceless spectrum lies in [0,1/4]. E commutes with Phi. Define the EB channel

  Psi_m=E Phi^m.

It is EB as the composition of the EB conditional expectation E with a channel, and it agrees exactly with Phi^m on the whole center. On the complementary fiber subspace,

  ||Phi^m-Psi_m||_(2->2,tau) <= 4^(-m).

For normalized tau, ||A||_(2,tau)<=||A||_infinity and ||X||_(1,tau)<=||X||_(2,tau). Therefore, for every m>=1,

  ||Phi^m-Psi_m||_(infinity->1,tau) <= 4^(-m).

This is an induced norm statement without an ancilla; it is not a diamond-norm estimate. The bound has no dependence on r, q, pi_min, or the spectral gap. The comparator is allowed to depend on the supplied representation (P, sectors, pi).

For a concrete nonuniform two-sector chain, choose pi=(2/3,1/3) and gap g=1/256:

  P=[[767/768,1/768],[1/384,383/384]].

It has eigenvalues 1 and 255/256. The corrected slow center eigenvalue is 261121/262144 and its gap is 1023/262144. A fixed-point projection removes the nonconstant eigenmode, whereas E Phi^m preserves its entire center evolution.

4. NON-EB FIRST MARGINAL WITH THE SAME POWER LIFT

For any q, encode a q-dimensional physical state rho into the direct-sum algebra by relative density A_z=q rho for every z; decode by averaging the blocks with weights pi and dividing by q. Since L fixes this uniform-fiber encoding and E resets each fiber to I_q, the decoded action of Phi=C^2 is

  rho -> rho/4 + 3 I_q/(4q).

At q=4 the normalized Choi partial transpose has antisymmetric eigenvalue

  (1-1/4)/4^2 - (1/4)/4 = -1/64.

Thus the decoded channel, and hence Phi itself, is not EB. Nevertheless Psi_m above is EB and approximates Phi^m with error at most 4^(-m). This is a nonuniform, reversible, finite-algebra example where a non-EB quantum fiber coexists with arbitrarily slow classical relaxation and a dimension-free channel-level power lift.

5. EXECUTABLE EVIDENCE, COSTS, AND LIMITS

`weighted_instrument_channel_check.py` uses exact rational arithmetic with q=2, pi=(2/3,1/3), and gap 1/16. Across every matrix unit in the direct-sum algebra it checks stationarity/detailed balance, the physical-to-likelihood similarity, weighted selfadjointness, both append-instrument marginals, and the symmetrized broadcaster marginals. It reports corrected slow eigenvalue 961/1024 and fiber L2 bound 1/4. `deeper_center_fiber_checks.py` checks exact scalar SOS values and the q=4 Choi partial-transpose arithmetic. These are finite formula-transcription checks, not proofs of the uniform theorem; the derivations above supply those proofs.

The construction needs P, pi, sector labels, and the common fiber dimension q as supplied inputs. It has r^2 instrument outcomes and a finite r-sector representation. Acquisition, encoding/decoding cost, compilation, numerical conditioning, runtime, and energy are not bounded. The matrices are explicit but no efficiency claim is made. Detailed balance and equal fiber sizes are substantive hypotheses. No universal representation of an arbitrary compatible channel by this class is established.

The original representation counterexample in INITIAL.txt remains separate: it rules out demanding a CPTP square-root factorization for every selfcompatible map. Cross-exposed Sol result c05_s02 gives a stronger obstruction to promoting the canonical corrected-instrument representation itself to all admissible maps, but neither obstruction refutes the universal EB-lifting gate. The best next target is a broader representation or conditional expectation construction that handles interactions between slow noncommutative modes and quantum fibers while retaining a dimension-free map norm.
