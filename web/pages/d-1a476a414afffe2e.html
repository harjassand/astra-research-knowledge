Let sigma=diag(4/5,1/5), v_+=(2,1)/sqrt(5), v_-=(2,-1)/sqrt(5),
pi_+=|v_+><v_+|, pi_-=|v_-><v_-|, and p_+=p_-=1/2.
Then (pi_++pi_-)/2=sigma and
 M_+ = p_+ sigma^(-1/2) pi_+ sigma^(-1/2) = |+><+|,

Thus Psi(X)=Tr(M_+X)pi_++Tr(M_-X)pi_- is full-domain CPTP,
sigma-preserving, and sigma-Petz CANONICAL EB with a pure two-atom ensemble.

Its Heisenberg channel H_Psi=Psi* is KMS selfadjoint: with
Gamma_sigma(A)=sigma^(1/2) A sigma^(1/2),

The right side is a selfadjoint positive Gram operator for the ordinary
Hilbert-Schmidt inner product. This proves full KMS reversibility and
positive KMS spectrum. The measure-and-prepare channel also has an exact
broadcaster X -> sum_i Tr(M_iX) pi_i tensor pi_i.
