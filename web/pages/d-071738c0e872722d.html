# An actual local measurement anchor, and the cost of resolving the blind witness

Independent worker check_vector_measurements, 2026-10-06. This is a fixed finite-block acquisition theorem conditional on the separately proved witness constructions. It is not a general Hamiltonian learner or publication-priority claim. The old Cycle2 X/Z witness and the new Cycle3 full-X/Y/Z witness have distinct continuation equations and constants; the executable handles them separately.

## Exact question and admitted access

Does equality of the entire prescribed local SLD score operator force all physically available local measurements to agree? No: both constructed witness pairs are distinguishable by one already available single-site X measurement. Adding a measured moment is a different statistical constraint from adding another score derivation. In particular, these real density matrices have zero expectation for every Pauli string with an odd number of Y factors. A direct single-site Y readout has zero mean in both, even when adding a Y SCORE distinguishes the old X/Z witness. The new full-X/Y/Z witness is also blind to the Y scores themselves.

Admitted benchmark: the known rational six-qubit base and the chosen explicit rational continuation parameter s_star are supplied by the witness description. The target is promised to be one of its two states σ or ρ; one obtains independent target copies and may measure a local Pauli observable. The target's eigenbasis, score matrices and full density matrix are NOT supplied as experimental data, and no tomography of target copies is performed. The test below selects its observable and threshold from the rational base and certified continuation radius, without computing the unknown continuation root.

The offline witness construction, the promised two-state model, independent copy source, readout calibration, and any physical cost to prepare a target copy are distinct supplied/acquired resources. This narrow promise is essential. Applying the anchor to an unrestricted unknown Gibbs model would require additional acquisition/selection theorems.

## Exact rational observable selection

Set d=64. The common rational Bell base is U0=(V tensor W)Bell, with the two known 8-by-8 rational Cayley rotations. For the new witness the Bell columns are Gray ordered; in both constructions the first two columns still correspond to vec(I8) and vec(I4 tensor X), divided by sqrt8.

Let T0=U0(E01+E10)U0^T. Pauli orthogonality gives

Σ_{P≠I}(Tr(P T0))²=d||T0||F²=128.

Only even-Y strings contribute because T0 is real symmetric. Thus some ≤6-body Pauli has coefficient magnitude≥sqrt(128/4095). This is a deterministic selection criterion using the known base, not the target state. Direct exact rational enumeration of all2079 nonidentity even-Y strings finds the maximizer

P=IIXIII=X on physical qubit3,
c=Tr(P T0)=14461231298984/8533679146205≈1.694606869>0.

The same c applies to both witness bases because their first two columns agree. The executable independently reconstructs both Cayley rotations and these first two columns using rational Gauss–Jordan arithmetic; the sum-of-squares identity is exactly128. The selection therefore needs no target spectral gap, target eigenbasis or target tomography.

## Full gap bound without finding the continuation root

For either construction, p_i=s^{2i}/Z, Z=Σ_{i=0}^{63}s^{2i}, and U=U0 C(x(s)). The certified coordinate cube gives ||x||∞≤r, with m coordinates, so

η=||U−U0||op≤mr.

This follows from ||C(x)−I||op≤||Ω(x)||op≤Σ|x_j| for the real Cayley chart; the exponential chart has the same bound by integrating an orthogonal exponential.

In the σ eigenbasis the state difference is

B(s)=diag(s^i/sqrtZ)(P_ones−I/d)diag(s^i/sqrtZ),
B_ii=0, B_ij=s^{i+j}/(dZ) for i≠j,
ρ−σ=U B(s)U^T.

For0<s≤1/2, summing matrix-entry trace-norm bounds gives

||B(s)−(s/d)(E01+E10)||1≤10s²/d.

Indeed the two leading entries incur at most2s³/[d(1−s²)]. The remaining entries are bounded by twice the sum over i<j excluding(0,1), whose infinite geometric upper sum is
s²(1+s−s²)/[(1−s)(1−s²)].
This yields a total≤(28/3)s²/d<10s²/d. Also the exact finite geometric identity gives

||B(s)||1≤Σ_{i≠j}|B_ij|
 =2(s−s^d)/[d(1−s)(1+s^d)]≤4s/d.

This proves an actual measurable local gap. It does not identify every state in the full faithful state space. Within the explicit one-dimensional blind family ρ_t=σ+t(ρ−σ), this one moment is affine with nonzero slope and therefore eliminates that entire one-dimensional ambiguity.

## A threshold from the base alone

Observable P=X3 is one of the row-constrained X generators. Write B_P(s)=U^T P U and K=C_s Hadamard B_P. The construction imposes constant row sums λ. Since Tr B_P=0,

λ=(1/d)Σ_{i≠j}(C_s)_ij(B_P)_ij,
(B_P)_00=λ−Σ_{j≥1}(C_s)_0j(B_P)_0j.

Let B0=U0^TPU0 and S0=Σ_{i=0}^{62}(B0)_{i,i+1}. Use |(B_P−B0)_ij|≤2η, |(B_P)_ij|≤1, C_1=2s/(1+s²), and C_l≤2s^l. Expanding only the nearest band gives

|λ−(4s/d)S0|≤10s²+8ηs,
|Σ_{j≥1}C_j(B_P)_0j−2s(B0)_01|≤5s²+4ηs.

SinceΣ_{i≥1}p_i≤s²/(1−s²), replacing the target mean mσ=Σ_i p_i(B_P)_ii by(B_P)_00 adds at most(8/3)s². Thus

|mσ−s(4S0/d−c)|≤18s²+12ηs,

where2(B0)_01=c. Together with the gap bound, the actual midpoint(mσ+mρ)/2 differs from the known threshold

T=s(4S0/d−c+c/(2d))

by at most(19s+13η)s. Both certified parameter choices satisfy this≤g/8, verified with exact rational arithmetic. No continuation-root solve is hidden in selecting or calibrating the mathematical threshold.

## Finite-copy implementation and equally informed baseline

Measure X3 on N independent six-qubit target copies, producing outcomes±1. This uses one Hadamard rotation and one computational-basis readout per copy; it needs no entangling measurement circuit or target eigenbasis gate. Decideρ if the empirical mean exceedsT andσ otherwise. Because the threshold error is≤g/8, an empirical deviation≤g/4 cannot cause a wrong decision. Hoeffding gives error probability at mostδ for

N≥ceil[(32/g²)log(2/δ)].

This is an ideal readout bound. If an independently certified systematic mean bias is≤g/8, use empirical toleranceg/8 instead and N≥ceil[(128/g²)log(2/δ)]. Without such calibration, an arbitrarily small unknown detector bias can overwhelm this extraordinarily tiny signal. Calibration copies/operations are additional costs.

The certified values are impractical. Atδ=.05, the ideal bound has log2N≈296620.09 for the old witness and452059.02 for the new witness. These numbers are upper budgets from the deliberately conservative s_star; they are not claimed necessary optima at those constants. Exact rational thresholds have roughly148301/226020-bit denominator scales before other fixed rational factors. Representing and comparing the threshold does not require solving x(s), but it still requires that precision. The executor's arithmetic certificate took less than a second on this workspace; that observation says nothing about producing the target copies or conducting this enormous experiment.

Let C_prep be the actual cost of obtaining one independent target copy. The total experiment cost includes N C_prep, N local rotations/readouts, the observed dataset or sufficient statistic, and readout calibration. The source paper's existing-target-copy interface does not provide a low-temperature preparation algorithm for these witness states. We do not assert that C_prep is small. No extra preparation of a target eigenstate is used by this test.

An equally informed direct-moment baseline chooses this same X3 test and threshold, and has identical copy/gate guarantees. There is no special score-engine advantage. The useful repair is to retain a previously discarded physical moment alongside the score objective. For these specific witnesses the ordinary local frame's measurements already contain distinguishing information even though its SLD population loss has a blind fiber.

## A lower bound for every collective-copy protocol

Bothσ andρ have diagonal overlap p0 with the same pure vectorφ=Ue0. For N copies each has overlap p0^N withφ^tensorN. The standard pure-state trace-distance bound and triangle inequality give

TV(σ^tensorN,ρ^tensorN)≤2sqrt(1−p0^N)
 ≤2sqrt[N(1−p0)]≤2s sqrt[N/(1−s²)].

Any equal-prior discrimination procedure, including arbitrary collective or adaptive quantum measurements, with error≤δ<1/2 requiresTV≥1−2δ. Therefore necessarily

N≥(1−2δ)²(1−s²)/(4s²).

For s≤1/2 this is at least3(1−2δ)²/(16s²). Atδ=.05 the conservative lower log2 values are296598.01(old) and452036.94(new). Thus the direct local test matches the s^-2 scaling of the fundamental copy obstruction. This lower bound is independent of supplied eigenbasis knowledge or measurement computational power. Tensoring many blocks can trade number of global copies for number of physical blocks, but it still charges the total number of prepared/measured blocks; it does not remove this resource scale.

## Heat-bath comparison: decisive kernel obstruction, not a new sampler

The full-X/Y/Z witness, if adopted from its separately audited theorem, has a nonscalar Hermitian dressed commutant element centered underσ and zero harmonic SLD energy. A primitive Gibbs heat-bath Dirichlet form has only scalar kernel, hence assigns positive energy to that same element. No everywhere-positive lower comparison E_SLD≥a E_heatbath witha>0 can hold on that target. This tests kernels before metric constants and requires no Jensen or norm-equivalence argument.

The block target has a finite Hamiltonian expansion on six contiguous sites. Repeating independent blocks is a bounded finite-range1D model at one fixed finiteβ, although the admittedβ and interaction constants are enormous. Capel–Pérez-García–Scandi2610.03683v1, Theorem4.1 and Lemma4.6, state positive conditional/global heat-bath gap bounds at fixedβ for bounded finite-range1D interactions. Those forms use the correct GNS/KMS generators and kernels; they are not the SLD-filtered commutator form. We verified their primary statements, not the entire MLSI proof. The new full-frame collision therefore blocks a universal SLD-transfer theorem even in that broad1D finite-range class; a valid repair must change statistics/generators or strengthen the admitted model restrictions.

The constructive all-temperature KMS validation route identified by b_prior remains an alternative with its own existing-literature model-dynamics/test-net/copy costs. It is not established here as a cheaper substitute. Direct one-body Y expectation is not that mechanism, and a positive heat-bath gap alone does not implement a score estimator.
