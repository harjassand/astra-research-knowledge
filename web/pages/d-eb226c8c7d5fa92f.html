ACQUISITION_LEARNING — PROGRAMME RESULT
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: sharply scoped mathematical frontier; no field-breaking or novelty claim established.

The programme did not find an unconditional teacher-free method for recovering arbitrary hidden structure from passive predictive data. It did sharpen the acquisition boundary into independent obligations: the legal query family must distinguish task-relevant objects; the response must have enough noise/precision margin; finite observations must cover a complete predictive witness horizon; and the sampling process must expose the temporal events needed by the target. Representation identity also needs a declared gauge: data can identify an observable predictive law or subspace while leaving a designer's latent basis wholly unidentifiable. Several positive results hold once the matching promises or interface are made explicit.

Reusable acquisition principle: exact identifiability is a quotient property of the legal oracle, while noisy cost is governed by task-separating transcript KL per unit cost. Neither algebraic rank nor a well-conditioned finite observable block controls how much temporal information the sampling interface reveals about a long-range prediction. The rare-switch pair below makes that separation quantitative; the basis and dictionary examples show why the acquired object should be an observable predictive class, not an arbitrary latent basis.

LEAD RESULT: ROBUST OBSERVABILITY IS A NECESSARY ACQUISITION GATE

For two task-distinct candidates with legal scalar responses Y=f_theta(x)+N(0,sigma^2), let Delta=sup_x |f_0(x)-f_1(x)|. Every adaptive procedure with both error probabilities at most δ must use expected calls under theta_0 at least

  (2 sigma^2 / Delta^2) kl(1−δ || δ).

The proof is the stopped-transcript KL chain rule followed by binary data processing; choosing queries adaptively does not evade a uniform per-query response-gap bound. A cost-weighted form replaces Delta^2/(2sigma^2) by the best legal per-cost KL. The pair 0 and 2^-B x on [0,1] has degree one and O(B)-bit rational description but forces Ω(sigma^2 4^B) calls at fixed confidence and noise. Exact nonzero hitting or clipping/rounding therefore does not by itself imply robust noisy acquisition. Exact proof and finite arithmetic replay: `work/programmes/acquisition_learning/robust_observability.md`, `robust_observability_check.py`, and `robust_observability_replay.json`. Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE using standard testing inequalities; no novelty claim.

A stronger finite-class scalar frontier was obtained by a leaf. For the nested family F_(D,H)={0}∪{c x^k : 1≤a,b≤H, gcd(a,b)=1, c=a/b, 0≤k≤D}, with legal rational x∈[0,1], fresh Gaussian scalar noise, and supplied D,H,sigma,δ, but hidden target coefficient/degree, exact predictive-class acquisition has minimax adaptive query count

  Theta(1 + sigma^2 max{H^4,H^2 D^2} log(1/δ))

at an explicit sufficient answer precision O(log H+log D). Two nonadaptive probes, x=1 and x=D/(D+1), attain the upper bound; a near-constant coefficient pair and adjacent-degree monomials give matching lower bounds against every adaptive legal query. The overall grammar and global caps are supplied, not acquired; the theorem is not for arbitrary rational functions. See `leaves/unknown_class_scalar_frontier.txt`; no novelty/priority claim.

QUERY ALGEBRA AND TASK-EQUIVALENCE

For a query map Q:R→S and legal answer functionals χ_u, complete exact query profiles coincide iff Q(r)-Q(r') lies in the intersection of their kernels. If a requested task label differs inside such a fiber, no adaptive or randomized learner can recover it, regardless of query count. For free noncommutative polynomials, scalar evaluations over an infinite field identify the abelianization, not the free lift: 0 and xy−yx are indistinguishable by all scalar queries. This does not make commutative PIT impossible because its zero predicate is quotient-invariant. Over a finite field with only base-field scalar queries, formal zero versus x^q−x is also aliased. A tailored 2×2 query at (E12,E21) separates c[x,y] with unit margin for c∈{0,1}; dimension one cannot. This is scoped to the commutator family and is not universal PIT. Full proof and N51/N68 comparison: `leaves/oracle_quotient_acquisition.txt`; primary source scope for sparse NC matrix hitting is [ECCC TR26-120](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/120/), and the rational-formula source remains separately scoped in [arXiv:2309.15647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15647).

For exact rational formula acquisition, a separate audit reconstructs a conditional descriptor learner. Assuming the public-Q N51 feasible-affine-pencil hitting list, a regular centre and finite residual feature vectors are acquired from exact full-matrix responses; an injective residual detector plus breadth-first closure outputs a valid affine matrix-inverse descriptor for the original formula's semantic equivalence class while preserving its original inverse domain. The query bound is L0+(1+kR)L1+R; dimensions, all matrix response entries, exact rational bit growth, and computation are charged. The learned residual realization is minimal for the perturbed generic-entry series only; it need not be minimal for the original formula (for example, Phi(x)=x^-1). The all-field integer-list extension remains conditional on a local unreviewed source-derived premise; neither syntax recovery nor a practical generator run is claimed. Full proof and limitations: `leaves/rational_descriptor_audit.txt`.

PREDICTIVE BASIS ACQUISITION VERSUS BASIS IDENTIFIABILITY

A restricted predictive-automaton family gives a sharp oracle-adaptivity frontier. The supplied template is a path DFA for an unknown binary word w of length at most H; the hidden target is the prefix language of w#. Its minimal DFA size and real Hankel rank are |w|+3 and |w|+2, both unknown. Exact fixed nonadaptive membership queries need exactly M−1=2^(H+1)−2 calls for M=2^(H+1)−1 candidates, while an adaptive prefix walk uses at most 2H and information requires at least H+1. Exact passive IID membership samples have minimax order Θ(M log(1/δ)) with no extra log M; under BSC noise, the lower is Ω(M·kl(1−δ||δ)/d_ε) and the elementary upper leaves a log H gap. A stronger equivalence oracle can return w# in one query only by placing target search and a Θ(H)-symbol counterexample in the teacher; it is not comparable to scalar membership calls at unit cost. The exact finite verifier passes through H=3, with fixed-query family enumeration through H=2. This is a genuine adaptive/nonadaptive separation for a known one-path template, not general unknown-basis acquisition; exact proof, cost ledger, verifier and source context are in `leaves/predictive_automaton_query_frontier.txt` and `work/programmes/acquisition_learning/leaves/predictive_automaton_query_frontier/`. No novelty claim.

A finite predictive process admits a useful conditional positive result. Under a finite witness-horizon promise C_L, a supplied useful upper rank cap R, exact rational prefix-word queries, and one-sided column-space incoherence μ0, adaptive random-row column completion discovers the actual finite Hankel rank and a word basis without receiving the exact rank or basis. An independent residual-validation panel checks for a missed span direction; a failed check falls back to the full block. The query panel is sub-full when R^(3/2) μ0 log(R/δ)≪N. It still does not give a rank-free guarantee: with R=N the panel saturates the matrix, and C_L is not certified from a finite prefix transcript. The matrix core follows Krishnamurthy–Singh 2013; no novelty claim. Costs and proof are in `leaves/adaptive_basis_frontier.txt`.

The object recovered should be the predictive function/subspace, not an unidentifiable designer's latent basis. Two distinct rational orthonormal dictionaries can have zero coherence, unit condition number, bounded atoms and fixed two-sparse coefficient margins while inducing exactly the same full source trajectory law. Hence every source-only adaptive learner has zero mutual information about which dictionary was intended; when a later task asks for atom 1, the source-only average MSE floor is 1/65. This exact no-go is in `leaves/unknown_dictionary_acquisition.txt`. It does not prohibit choosing a canonical data-derived basis or learning the invariant source predictor.

FINITE HORIZON, SPECTRAL MARGIN, AND MIXING ARE DISTINCT

Unknown rank can be thresholded from a complete finite Hankel block under an explicit singular gap and sampling/rounding error bound. Without a gap, a two-component Bernoulli mixture of arbitrarily small mass has rank two but is statistically indistinguishable from a rank-one iid process at any fixed sample budget. Without a witness horizon or rank ceiling, delayed finite-state clocks match every finite transcript and differ later. The adaptive prefix-Hankel construction and counterexamples are in `leaves/unknown_basis_discovery.txt` and `leaves/spectral_unknown_rank.txt`; their finite-block algebra is standard realization theory, not a general teacher-free stopping algorithm.

A stricter sample-based adaptive CUR variant is now drafted for an entry-only word-event oracle: it uses a data-dependent rank cutoff, doubling row/column skeleton, fresh uniform residual validation, certified gap/rounding intervals, and returns UNKNOWN or falls back to the full block when its observable stop conditions fail. Its theorem needs left/right leverage bounds, a nonvanishing sampled-core gap, residual-leverage for finding an underfit cell, and enough precision. It is not a sample-count improvement over the matched raw-reset-path baseline, since one raw trajectory already supplies all its prefix indicators. See `leaves/adaptive_hankel_acquisition.txt`; this is a conditional matrix-adaptation route, not a process-wide guarantee.

For the two-state stationary rare-switch pair r0=1/(8L), r1=1/(2L), emission accuracy 3/4, the length-three reset law is only O(1/L)-separated while the L-step endpoint forecast after a three-symbol context differs by a constant. The passive minimax theorem proves Ω(L²) independent three-symbol reset episodes for constant expected-TV accuracy; adjacent-pair estimation supplies the matching order. Rank and observable pair-Hankel margins stay bounded away from zero, while minimum transition mass is Θ(1/L). This demonstrates that rank/conditioning alone does not control temporal acquisition. Exact statement and proof: `leaves/passive_hmm_minimax.txt`.

An independent internal audit now proves a matching Θ(L) one-stream rate for the same endpoint-prediction problem. A lag-s≈0.85L product of noisy endpoint observations has a constant mean gap; 5,179 independent nonoverlapping block products suffice for the stated fixed risk, with one stream start and O(1) memory. For a rounded finite-bit forecast at the same risk, 5,879 blocks split the error budget between model selection and output rounding; the output is O(1) bits and controlled powering uses O(log L)-bit guard precision. Conversely, a length-T stream has KL at most (T−1)/(2L), and the forecast-kernel separation gives an Ω(L) prediction lower bound. Against length-three resets, this is a factor-Θ(L) reduction in symbols: each such reset has KL O(L^-2), and Θ(L²) symbols are necessary and sufficient. The comparison is interface-specific: independent reset episodes of length Θ(L) match the stream in symbol order, and can lose only in charged reset starts. Both HMMs have constant rank and pair-moment margins, but min transition mass is Θ(1/L). Proof, exact constants, computation/output costs, and the reset comparison are in `leaves/passive_hmm_one_stream.txt`; scratch audit in `work/programmes/acquisition_learning/leaves/passive_hmm_one_stream/proof.md`. This is an internally checked two-model result, not an arbitrary-HMM guarantee or novelty claim. Its prior context is Hsu–Kakade–Zhang: [arXiv:0811.4413](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.4413).

ADAPTIVITY, INTERVENTIONS, AND TRANSFER CONTROLS

The persistent-readout family supports an adaptive exact predictive-model learner and local margin certificate, but majority/GF(2) decoding uses the same information and is an equally capable same-data baseline. The one-hot extension recovers an unknown atom using q+1 noisy subset calls; a nonadaptive q-bit group-test bank matches it. No algorithmic advantage over the best same-interface policy is established. See `leaves/observable_transfer_frontier.txt` and the earlier parity construction. The controlled-HMM pulse result gives a strict cost gain over idle-only passive sampling after action/reset costs, while proving that adaptive feedback offers no gain against a precommitted schedule with the same action allocation in the one-sided-erasure class (`leaves/active_passive_hmm_controlled.txt`). A separate theorem says active and randomized assignment have identical transcript risks only when the full observable-history-conditioned policy, schedule, stopping rule and logged actions match; equal action counts alone do not suffice (`leaves/causal_controlled_hmm_frontier.txt`).

An equal-information baseline that can run and retain the same derived structure can simulate its learner. Therefore any claimed transfer advantage must name the narrower baseline/resource constraint and charge acquisition, failed searches, validation, code, storage and later use. A separate total-variation counterexample shows exact predictive-state count is discontinuous under rare support additions: source and target laws can be ε-close, while literal source-machine reuse has conditional error 1 on ε-mass target histories (`leaves/representation_transfer_stability.txt`). This is an exact-structure and worst-case conditional-risk boundary, not a large average-risk or amortized-win claim.

Other scoped results banked in leaves: passive access to a rare known history of mass q^T costs Ω(q^-T delta^-2) informative samples, while a free conditional-sample oracle removes the history-generation cost and therefore supplies a different teacher interface (`conditional_history_access_cost.txt`); an unknown one-hot weighted automaton has a tight adaptive point-query frontier, while signed dense/group queries reduce call count only by transmitting a longer/denser query (`adaptive_hidden_word_frontier.txt`); and an aperiodic full-support stationary process with 2^T context states can exactly alias all length-≤T laws while its next conditional differs at every T-context (`stationary_predictive_alias.txt`). These are exact interface-controlled examples, with state-size/mixing, query-description, or sample costs retained in their reports.
