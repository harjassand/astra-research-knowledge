# Exact conversion of a bounded controlled target program

The executable preserves the complete action/observation/controller-coin record of a genuinely adaptive 1024-step target program, including its Boolean terminal report. Its expected paid source count is at most **115722553/122880 = 941.752547201** in every state. A separately certified fixed IID design converse requires at least **1042.513511872** expected source observations in some state when the comparator must preserve that complete record. The converse does not apply to the Boolean report alone. Models are supplied, and mathematical exactness assumes independent ideal random bits and fresh conditional source observations.

## Explicit program and interface

There are three states. Each known source action a returns a Bernoulli observation with one-probability 1/10 when a is the state and 9/10 otherwise, at unit cost. Target action 0 has rows G0=(.8,.1,.1), G1=(.2,.7,.1), G2=(.1,.2,.7). Target action 1 cyclically permutes these state rows: its row i is G[(i+1) mod 3]. All channels have full support.

At step t=0,...,n-1, the target controller draws an independent private fair coin. If its previous observation was 2 it chooses action 1; otherwise odd steps choose the coin value and even steps choose action 0. It then obtains a target observation. Initial previous observation is 0. Its terminal Boolean is the XOR of (a) parity of observations equal to 2 with coin XOR action equal to 1, (b) whether the observation sum modulo 3 equals 1, and (c) whether the first and last observation agree.

`execute_target(observation_oracle, private_coin, n)` and `report_from_record(record)` receive no candidate-state argument. Candidate k is supplied only to the proposed target observation oracle. The retained record consists of original controller coin outcomes, chosen actions, and observations. Internal channel-sampler integers and PRNG seeds are excluded: including them would make joint support state dependent and invalidate the likelihood-ratio argument. The program stores O(n) record symbols and six action/outcome counts, with no output-probability table. The horizon-three report-law enumeration is an audit only; runtime does not extract Boolean-report probabilities.

## Full-record likelihood and repair

Let H_i(z) be the probability of a valid full execution record. Controller choices are functions of the retained history, and original private coin probabilities are identical across states. Therefore

    H_i(z)/H_k(z) = product_t G_i^(a_t)(y_t)/G_k^(a_t)(y_t).

Every coordinate ratio lies in [1/8,8], so record ratios are at most 8^n. Six action/outcome counts evaluate the product exactly. Proposal generation runs the original target controller with candidate-state channel observations; it never conditions controller code on the candidate.

Use eta=1/(30n), the initial balanced classifier A0 with off-diagonal b0 and error at most eta, and the candidate-specific scalar source verifier described in ASYMMETRIC.md. Its success barrier is the smallest integer B_k(z) satisfying

    9^B >= max(1,max_j H_k(z)/H_j(z))/eta.

Thin passes with (1-eta)/(1+2eta). The accepted submass Q and residual T=H-Q satisfy pointwise eta H_i <= T_i <= 6eta H_i. This proof depends only on likelihood ratios, scalar pass probabilities, and classifier error; it does not require target observations to be IID.

For the repair classifier A_R=lambda I+bR J choose bR<=1/(36*8^n). Its inverse gives W=(T-bR JT)/lambda, with 0<=W_k<=7eta H_k. Every repair attempt takes a FRESH paid classifier, proposes a complete target execution record under its candidate k, and accepts with exact probability W_k(z)/(7eta H_k(z)). Under truth i, the accepted submass in one attempt is T_i(z)/(7eta). Repeated independent attempts thus return T_i/delta_i without computing delta_i=sum_z T_i. Combining the initial accepted and repaired branches gives Q_i+T_i=H_i exactly. The report is a deterministic projection, so its law is exact as well. Unequal delta_i cause no difficulty because every retry reclassifies.

The unconditional source repair cost is delta_i times (7eta/delta_i) times 3rR, namely 21eta rR. Mean auxiliary target proposals conditional on repair are at most 7; unconditional repair proposals are exactly 7eta. Thus expected target-program execution overhead is 1+7eta invocations. This is an expectation, not a deterministic execution-time limit: source walks, rejection loops, and rational randomizers have unbounded possible durations.

## Finite expected source budget

Every ordered channel-pair KL is at most .7 log 8, certified by the exact rational inequality product_y (G_i(y)/G_j(y))^(10G_i(y)) <= 8^7. Each one-step log ratio has absolute value at most log 8. For an adaptively chosen action, write the pairwise cumulative log ratio as the sum of its conditional KL means plus martingale differences. Its mean part is at most .7n log 8; orthogonal martingale differences have total variance at most n(log 8)^2. For the two alternatives, Jensen and the Euclidean norm give

    E max_j [log(H_i/H_j)]_+ <= log 8 * (.7n + sqrt(2n)).

No independence of the alternatives or target actions is required. Integer inequality 8^20<9^19 bounds log 8/log 9 by 19/20. Correct and incorrect verifier costs are at most 5B/4 and 5L/4 respectively. Every state consequently satisfies

    E_i N <= 3r0 + (5/4)[(19/20)(.7n+ceil sqrt(2n))+L+1]
                  + (5/4)eta L + 21eta rR.

At n=1024 the compiler chooses r0=9, L=5, rR=2088, yielding the stated rational upper bound. It is below the target horizon 1024. This is a known-channel construction and does not establish that arbitrary acquired scientific models admit these bounds.

## Competent fixed IID comparator

The parent certificate in work/team_d_experiments/cycle3/program_fixed_design_certificate.py computes exact action occupancies by a three-previous-symbol Markov recurrence, then encloses all logarithms using 48 rational atanh-series terms with a geometric remainder bound. Our verifier independently checks the recurrence against all horizon-three records and reconstructs the n=1024 certificate read-only.

For fixed source action frequencies q, arbitrary stopping/retention/reporting cannot increase pairwise KL above D(q_i+q_j)E_i N, D=.8 log 9. The full target record has pairwise KL sum_a E_i N_a KL(G_i^a||G_j^a). At n=1024, the strongest directions for the three unordered pairs are 1>0, 0>2, 2>1. Summing those three necessary inequalities gives worst-state source cost at least n times the sum of their per-step target KL values divided by 2D. Exact rational log enclosures certify the coefficient greater than 1.0180796014379. The comparator knows every law, retains its observations, and has arbitrary stopping/reporting; only source action frequencies are frozen IID. Predetermined nonstationary designs remain outside this converse. A simulator required to produce only the terminal Boolean may discard substantial information, so this lower bound would not apply.

## Evidence, complete costs, and preserved failures

Run `python3 work/d_example/cycle3/program/converter.py`, then `python3 work/d_example/cycle3/program/verify.py`. The independent small audit reconstructs all 216 valid horizon-three records and checks 648 exact state-record equalities. Boolean probabilities are exactly (13/25,553/1000,199/400). Internal channel-sampler randomness is never part of the augmented record.

The final seeded diagnostic execution contains 36 ordinary outputs, paying 26660 source observations, and six forced repair outputs, paying 81432 observations. Ordinary statewise means are 786.8333,678.5833,756.2500. No ordinary repair flags occurred, so these small runs do not measure the rare-tail mean or validate exact law empirically. Forced repairs took 4,7,2 paid classification attempts across two outputs per state. Auxiliary generated bits and simulated-environment bits are recorded separately in both branches; exact acceptance denominators reached 25468 bits. Compilation took 0.1960 wall seconds and 0.1904 CPU seconds, with cumulative process peak RSS 24.9 MB including the small audit. Runtime took 0.1691 wall seconds and 0.1543 CPU seconds, cumulative RSS 25.4 MB. These are observations on this Mac, not universal runtime estimates.

The initial controller algebraically used history but its coin canceled all conditional action dependence; that version and all its trials are preserved in initial_coin_coupled_controller. The replacement truly branches on history. A checker then found that threshold search ignored eta's bit size: h=(1,1,1), eta=9^-30 returned 16 instead of 30. This bug did not affect previous small or n=1024 fixtures. The fixed search includes eta sizes and asserts a valid bracket and minimal final threshold; regressions at eta=9^-30 and 9^-1000 pass. Pre-fix code and ledgers are preserved. The replay CSV is byte-identical, and all three executions are charged: **323441** source observations total. A verification-only tuple-versus-JSON-list equality assertion was corrected by JSON normalization; it changed no converter or samples.

The full cycle-three ledger includes symmetric 2999490, asymmetric 1019020, and program 323441 paid observations, totaling **4341951**, including discarded runs and forced branches. `check_selection` independently reconstructed all 216 records and 648 equalities, checked 12 large extreme-record cases and the eta regression, and separately rebuilt the action recurrence and fixed-design log bound with a different range-reduced logarithm scheme. These audits are results/check_selection/cycle3/program_converter_audit.json and program_fixed_design_audit.json. Own and independent internal checks support the arithmetic and scoped proofs; they are not independent external validation or evidence of historical novelty.

## Scientific scope and next falsifiable step

This demonstrates that a bounded adaptive target program can be converted through its full likelihood-bearing record without enumerating its exponentially many reports, using a supplied rational channel catalogue. Existing controlled-sensing source models/rates are classical prior art (Nitinawarat–Atia–Veeravalli 2013; source snapshots in sources/d_example). Novelty of the implicit fresh-classification repair remains unestablished. The construction does not acquire channel models or action meanings, and its general efficient-program consequences require explicitly bounded target execution and likelihood arithmetic. A concrete next check is a bounded target stopping/controller program with nonuniform original private randomness, independently audited for full-record likelihood cancellation, source budget, and genuinely equal-information comparators.
