CYCLE 6 DEEPER COUNTEREXAMPLE — GLOBAL COMPLEMENT DOES NOT CURE LOCAL PARITY MODES
Worker: c01_l04. This revision preserves the immutable INITIAL snapshot.

RESULT
The single 6-cycle bottleneck can be bypassed by adding a full-complement proposal, so I tested that repair rather than extrapolating the first obstruction. Two independent 6-cycles show that the global complement involution still leaves a parity cut. For an explicit rational 12x12 block-diagonal F, a connected Metropolis chain with both single-coordinate exchanges and a full global complement move has conductance Theta(epsilon^2); its paired two-replica kernel also violates any polynomial-in-bit-length additive-energy bound. This is a stronger scoped counterexample to that exact kernel design, not to cycle-local or mismatch-register worms.

INSTANCE AND MICROSTATE LAW
Let F6(epsilon) be the 6x6 matrix with cyclic entries F[i,i+1]=1, F[i,i-1]=epsilon, and all other entries zero, indices modulo 6. Set
  F12(epsilon)=diag(F6(epsilon),F6(epsilon)),  epsilon=2^-b, b>=1.
For fixed k=6, a microscopic paired basis is a 6-subset I of the 12 sites, with weight w(I)=|det F12[I,I^c]|^2. Block-diagonality forces exactly three selected row-lines in each 6-site block: if the first block has r selected rows, its complementary columns number 6-r, and the block determinant can be nonzero only when r=6-r. Therefore positive states factor as (I1,I2), where each Ij is a positive 3-subset for F6. There are 14^2=196 positive states, and
  Z12=Z6^2,
  Z6=2a+6epsilon^2+6epsilon^4,
  a=(1+epsilon^3)^2.
The six-site exact determinant table is in the previous revision and is also recomputed by the script below.

HEAVY MODES AND CUT
For each block define O={1,3,5}, E={0,2,4}. Its two heavy orientation states have weight a. Thus the four heavy 12-site states are OO, OE, EO, EE, each of weight a^2. Let S={OO,EE}; then
  s=pi(S)=2a^2/Z6^2 < 1/2,
  s -> 1/2 as epsilon -> 0.
The full complement I -> I^c flips both block modes, so it maps OO<->EE inside S and OE<->EO outside S. For every other positive state the complement remains outside S. Hence the global complement proposal preserves the cut exactly.

PRECISE KERNEL
On a one-replica state I, M chooses i in I and j outside I uniformly from 36 choices, proposes I'=(I\\{i}) union {j}, and applies Metropolis acceptance for w. Cross-block exchanges have zero determinant because the block row/column dimensions mismatch; they reject. Let R be the full global complement proposal I -> I^c, accepted by Metropolis using the exact determinant weights. It is a symmetric involution. Set Q=(M+R)/2. On two replicas define
  P=(Swap + Q_A + Q_B)/3,
where Swap exchanges A and B, Q_A updates only A by Q, and Q_B updates only B. All transitions are computable from rational determinants and P is reversible for pi tensor pi. The one-coordinate M moves connect the positive support, so the chain is irreducible.

CONDUCTANCE
At any state in S, the R move stays in S, and one exchange cannot reach a different heavy mode: changing O to E in one block needs three exchanges. Within-block neighbors of a heavy mode have amplitudes 0, epsilon, or epsilon^2; cross-block exchanges have amplitude zero. From each high state, summing the 36 exact Metropolis proposals gives
  M(I,S^c)=(epsilon^2+epsilon^4)/(6a).
Thus the one-replica Q conductance of S is exactly half this value. For the pair kernel, the cut SxS has mass s^2<1/4; Swap and R_A/R_B preserve it, while the two local components each contribute one sixth of the one-replica exit. Its conductance is exactly M(I,S^c)/3. Both conductances are Theta(epsilon^2) although the one-replica S mass tends to 1/2. With epsilon=2^-b and fixed dimension 12, the bottleneck is exponential in the entry bit length.

PAIR ADDITIVE-ENERGY WITNESS
Take f(I)=1[I in S], which is invariant under R, and G(A,B)=f(A)+f(B). Then
  Var_pi f=s(1-s),
  E_P(G)=s M(I,S^c)/3,
  L_required=3(1-s)/M(I,S^c)=(9+o(1))epsilon^-2.
The energy identity follows by linearity of Dirichlet forms: Swap and both R components contribute zero. Each Q_A/Q_B has mixture coefficient 1/3 and includes M with internal probability 1/2, so M_A and M_B have combined coefficient 1/3. Their Dirichlet contributions sum to D_M(f)/3=s M(I,S^c)/3.
For b=4,8,16 the exact Fraction script reports L=2349.2180..., 589869.0667..., and 38654705709.0003..., respectively; asymptotically these are 9*4^b. The exact conductance and energy expressions, not finite extrapolation, establish the lower bound.

WHY THIS REPAIR FAILS / WHAT WOULD FIX THIS EXAMPLE
A global complement flips both independent cycle modes, preserving their parity. The set {OO,EE} is one complement orbit pair; {OE,EO} is the other. Local exchanges must enter low-weight defects before changing just one block mode. A blockwise complement proposal would connect OO to EO or OE at unit determinant weight and constant probability, and would remove this particular cut. This finite example therefore rules out relying only on one global particle-hole/complement involution plus single-site Metropolis exchanges. It does not rule out detecting and flipping individual alternating cycles, bounded mismatch registers, auxiliary coordinate lines, or a more general Hilbert-space flow.

EXECUTION AND COST
Run: `python3 work/cycle6/c01_l04/revisions/global_complement_counter.py`.
The script uses exact Fraction arithmetic and 3x3 determinant expansions, verifies the complete six-site amplitude groups, forms the 196-state product support, checks that global complement preserves S, and computes exact Z, conductance, variance, and pair Dirichlet ratio for b=4,8,16. Results: `global_complement_counter_results.json`. No randomized or floating calculation is used in the proof. Input length is O(b); each acceptance ratio is rational with polynomial bit length. Proposal generation costs O(1) site choices and each determinant evaluation is polynomial (here dimension is fixed).

PRIOR-ART AND CLAIM LIMIT
This is a second exact boundary for a specified local Metropolis kernel. The baseline already notes hard directed-cycle disconnection for bounded fixed-sector moves; this construction adds a positive-epsilon connected-support conductance result and then defeats the obvious full-complement repair by taking two cycles. It makes no literature-priority claim. Weighted matroid parity optimization and counting minimum-weight Pfaffian-parity solutions are adjacent but distinct from this determinant-squared partition sampler; see SOURCE_METADATA.json and revisions/literature_boundary.txt. No conclusion is drawn against arbitrary two-replica energy compilers.
