HJW / CONDITIONAL-VARIANCE INTERFACE AND THE REMAINING GENERAL GATE
2026-10-09. No general finite-C proof or legal counterexample obtained.

For a traceless Hermitian frame {A_i} on C^d, write
tau=Tr/d, V=sum_i tau(A_i^2),
k=max_{sum_alpha p_alpha pi_alpha=I/d} sum_alpha p_alpha sum_i <A_i>_pi^2.
Then

 V-k=min_{sum_alpha p_alpha pi_alpha=I/d}
       sum_alpha p_alpha sum_i Var_pi_alpha(A_i).                  (H1)

The second-moment contribution is V for every admissible ensemble.
The minimization in (H1) uses a common pure ensemble for all observables.
Each single-observable variance roof can be zero at I/d while (H1)>0.

1. EXACT PURIFICATION / POVM EQUIVALENCE

Let |Psi>=(I tensor U)|Omega_d>, where U:C^d -> H_M is an isometry
into the full purifying memory, and |Omega_d>=sum_a |a,a>/sqrt(d).
A memory POVM {N_alpha} pulls back to M_alpha=U^dagger N_alpha U.
Its outcome probability and conditioned reference state are

 p_alpha=tau(M_alpha),
 rho_A|alpha=M_alpha^T/Tr(M_alpha).                               (H2)

Conversely, every input POVM {M_alpha} is realized on this full memory
by N_alpha=U M_alpha U^dagger, plus the outcome I-U U^dagger of probability
zero. A rank-one refinement yields pure conditioned reference states.
Refinement increases the total squared conditional means.

Consequently k is exactly the maximum squared conditional-mean score
of one common memory POVM; V-k is its minimum residual conditional
variance. The transpose in (H2) is essential: the pullback score uses
A_i^T. Transposing the full ensemble identifies k(A^T)=k(A), so this
convention does not alter the optimization.

This establishes the interface. It does not select a single measurement
from the two broadcast outputs with the needed score.

2. PRECISE UNPROVED TWO-OUTPUT LEMMA

Let R_ABC>=0 have trace one and arise as a normalized Choi state of a
two-output channel. Require R_A=R_B=R_C=I/d, R_AB=R_AC, and the
HS-self-adjoint Choi condition J=F J^T F for the common pair J.
Output swap may be imposed by averaging. Put

 W=sum_i(A_i^T tensor A_i tensor I+A_i^T tensor I tensor A_i).

The C=2 endpoint is precisely

 Tr(R W)<=V+k.                                                    (H3)

An equivalent rounding lemma would provide a common memory POVM whose
conditional-mean score is at least Tr(R W)-V. HJW alone does not provide
it. Per-observable measurements, separately optimal quantum predictors,
or the sum of single-observable QFIs cannot replace that common POVM.

No proof of (H3) for arbitrary d and arbitrary frame, and no fully legal
counterexample to it, was obtained by this worker.

3. FAILED STEP: QUANTUM PREDICTORS ARE NOT A FREE JOINT MEASUREMENT

On the full purification, the operator
X_i=U A_i^T U^dagger satisfies (A_i tensor I)|Psi>=(I tensor X_i)|Psi>.
Thus all separate operator-valued quadratic prediction errors are zero.
The X_i generally do not commute. Replacing them by one classical
conditional-mean vector would falsely conclude V-k=0.

For an exact counterexample to that replacement, take d=2 and frame
{sigma_x,sigma_z}. Here V=2 and k=1: every pure Bloch vector has
x^2+z^2<=1, and the equal ensemble of +/-x attains 1 with barycenter I/2.
Therefore V-k=1, although the full-purification quantum prediction
errors above are zero for both observables.

4. FAILED STEP: EQUAL OUTPUTS WITHOUT TRACIAL OUTPUTS

TRIPARTITE_SCOPE_OBSTRUCTION.txt gives an explicit isometry with
reference I/3 and equal outputs for the frame {J1,J2}, for which
Tr(R W)=4/sqrt(3)>20/9=V+k. Its outputs are
diag(2/9,2/9,5/9), so it is not unital and not HS-self-adjoint.
It is a boundary witness only. It also disproves the reference-only
operator lift W<=(sum_i A_i^2+G)_A^T using the optimal ensemble dual G.

5. CORRECTED BAYESIAN CONDITIONS

BAYESIAN_BRIDGE.txt proves two conditional rounding theorems with explicit
second-moment budgets: C=2 from first moments A_i and cost <=V+2delta;
C=4 from first moments Phi(A_i) and cost <=V. An explicit legal
four-dimensional cloning channel disproves the tempting assertion that
self-compatibility supplies either of these budgets automatically.
