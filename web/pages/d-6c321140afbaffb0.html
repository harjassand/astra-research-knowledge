\begin{abstract}
This run used the October mathematics release and Astra's accumulated research to identify changed technical premises, derive consequences, and attack their weakest steps. The strongest outputs are a conditional hyperbolic group-ring construction, effective acquisition of a unitary-synthesis oracle in polynomial space, an intrinsic criterion for approximate classical reconstruction of quantum experiments, a universal integer hitting list for noncommutative rational formulas, a weighted reconstruction criterion on arbitrary trees, and finite global certificates for rational reaction networks. These are research candidates with explicit proofs and internal adversarial audits. No externally validated historical breakthrough or comprehensive priority clearance is claimed. Several apparently promising transfers failed, and those failures are preserved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Highest-ceiling surviving targets}
\subsection{Hyperbolic groups with non-directly-finite characteristic-two rings}
\textbf{Candidate, conditional on release197.} The new ingredient is a disk extension of the released bounded-pattern exclusion. It applies to an arbitrary cyclically reduced nullword in the rose, rather than requiring the exterior word to lift to the defining graph. Minimal inner cone surgeries preserve that exterior word. Removing exterior comparisons loses at most its length. The same fixed extraction constants give
\[
 H < \frac{64}{\varepsilon}|w|,
\]
where $H$ is total inner cone-boundary length. A maximal-tree basis of graph cycles converts each boundary of length $h$ into at most $h$ ordinary relator cells. Consequently the finite presentation has a linear Dehn function.

Composed with the released torsion and parity assertions, this would produce a torsion-free word-hyperbolic group $G$ and elements of $R=\mathbb F_2[G]$ satisfying
\[
 ab=1,\qquad ac=0,\qquad c\ne0.
\]
In particular $ba\ne1$. The exact algebraic consequences include a nonzero projective $P=(1-ba)R$ with $P\oplus R\cong R$, hence $[P]=0$ in $K_0(R)$, and a faithful copy of the Jacobson algebra $\mathbb F_2\langle x,y\mid xy=1\rangle$.

The bounded exclusion is decidable by a finite search. Enumerating matching systems and testing it yields a terminating presentation-and-witness acquisition procedure if the released probability theorem holds. Its constants are astronomical; no concrete presentation was materialized.

\textbf{Important unresolved literature issue.} Some introductory statements assert a positive-characteristic idempotent consequence that would conflict with this construction. The audited proof chain supplied only trace restrictions in positive characteristic; the cancellation argument used characteristic-zero positivity or an additional stable-finiteness/soficity assumption. We therefore withdrew the purported established contradiction. This does not disprove those literature assertions or validate release197. Exact primary-source reconciliation and external review remain necessary.

\subsection{Acquiring the Boolean oracle for unitary synthesis}
\textbf{Constructive candidate, conditional on release283's mechanism.} For an exact unitary family whose entries can be approximated to $2^{-p}$ in space polynomial in $n+p$, the derived construction acquires a single total Boolean language in $\PSPACE$. Polynomial-size quantum circuits using that language approximate the unitary with clean workspace and phase-sensitive error $2^{-b}$, with resources polynomial in $n+b$.

The missing acquisition steps are explicit: short-seed sign searches preserve the required trace moments; rounded feedback matrices are evaluated by exact rational path sums; recursive precision grows additively; structural Fourier support avoids testing arbitrary real coefficients for zero; and rational prefix masses permit controlled state preparation. Exponential running time is charged. The oracle is not a polynomial-time classical evaluator.

Under the cited clean, phase-sensitive class definitions this gives
\[
 \mathrm{unitaryPSPACE}=\mathrm{unitaryP}^{\PSPACE}.
\]
Together with the EFI/commitment reduction in current primary literature, it gives the conditional consequence
\[
 \text{quantum commitments exist}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\BQP\ne\PSPACE.
\]
No unconditional complexity separation or existence of commitments follows. A separate audit reduces the cryptographic implication to constant-error effective synthesis, avoiding the stronger arbitrary-precision compilation dependency.

\subsection{An intrinsic test for approximate classicality}
\textbf{Direct theorem candidate; priority unresolved.} Put
\[
 \Delta(\rho,\sigma)=\|\sqrt\rho\sqrt\sigma\|_1-
             \Tr(\sqrt\rho\sqrt\sigma),\qquad
 e(\mathcal E)=\inf_{\Lambda\ \mathrm{measure\!\!\!-and\!\!\!-prepare}}
       \sup_{\rho\in\mathcal E}\tfrac12\|\Lambda(\rho)-\rho\|_1.
\]
For every experiment, without dimensional or domination assumptions,
\[
 e(\mathcal E)\ge\tfrac18\bigl(\sup_{\rho,\sigma\in\mathcal E}\Delta(\rho,\sigma)\bigr)^2.
\]
The final candidate converse assumes only uniform total boundedness in trace distance: every fixed positive accuracy admits a net of cardinality bounded independently of $n$. No reference, likelihood domination, score representation, or bound on Hilbert-space dimension is required. Under that hypothesis,
\[
e(\mathcal E_n)\longrightarrow0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \sup_{\rho,\sigma\in\mathcal E_n}\Delta(\rho,\sigma)\longrightarrow0.
\]
These are also equivalent to vanishing worst marginal error of one common channel broadcasting the unknown state to two receivers. A classical reconstruction record then serves every prescribed number $K_n$ of receivers, with the same marginal error bound independent of $K_n$. This conclusion permits correlations between outputs; it does not give independent copies or joint approximation to $\rho^{\otimes K_n}$.
The channel has finite classical alphabet at each fixed tolerance, independent of Hilbert-space dimension. No efficient matrix decomposition, net acquisition, measurement or state preparation is asserted.

The load-bearing step uses the canonical positive likelihood
\[
 X=\omega^{-1/2}(\sqrt\omega\rho\sqrt\omega)^{1/2}\omega^{-1/2},
 \quad X\omega X=\rho,\quad 0\le X\le\sqrt C I,
\]
and the polar bound
\[
 \|X\sqrt\omega-\sqrt\rho\|_2^2\le2\sqrt C\,\Delta(\omega,\rho).
\]
For a dominated finite family, random logarithmic spectral bins centralize these likelihoods; tracial almost-commuting approximation then constructs one common measure-and-prepare channel. The final escalation removes domination. A compatible noncommutative $L^p$ ultraproduct proves that fixed finite mixtures preserve asymptotic gap zero. The mean of a finite net then supplies its own dominating reference. The mixture modulus is qualitative, not an explicit computational bound. This avoids the false necessity of raw supplied-score commutators; a faithful qubit example already refutes that raw criterion.

The entropy hypothesis cannot simply be discarded: the union of $d+1$ mutually unbiased bases has pair gaps tending to zero but worst-case reconstruction error at least $1-2/(d+1)$. The final proof uses established multiplication-compatible ultraproduct results of Raynaud (2002). Independent internal checks found no unresolved step in that finite-mixture bridge; external review and prior-art clearance remain open. The gap and its exact zero-if-and-only-if-commuting property are prior art, explicitly treated by \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.14203}{Khouzani--Manjegani, Theorem 6.1}. The candidate contribution concerns dimension-uniform asymptotic reconstruction, not the introduction of this statistic.

The two-receiver converse now has two independently checked routes: a normal-channel ultraproduct and a finite-dimensional information argument. The latter is quantitative. If one broadcaster has marginal error at most $\varepsilon$ on two states, then for every integer $L\ge0$ a measure-and-prepare channel has error at most
\[
 L\varepsilon+\sqrt{\frac{\log 2}{2\,2^L}}
\]
on both states. Consequently their gap is $O(\sqrt{\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)})$. A Helstrom qubit example requires at least square-root order; removal of the logarithmic loss remains open in this run. The construction iterates the broadcaster to $2^L$ leaves and uses a leaf with little remaining information about the binary label. Measuring the preceding leaves and preparing the posterior average gives one common classical channel. Acquisition of its conditional states and measurements is charged and may be expensive.

The finite-label information extraction is established prior art: focused checking found direct antecedents in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.6829}{Li--Smith} and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.01507}{Qi--Ranard}, extending the mechanism of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.8640}{Brand\~ao--Piani--Horodecki}. The independently written proof is retained for its exact interface; it is not claimed as a newly invented extraction method. The finite-mixture argument supplying gap-to-reconstruction remains qualitative.

A finite-family refinement weakens the uniform entropy assumption for the broadcasting implication. For $m\ge2$ supplied states with two-broadcast error $b$, adaptive label tests and minimax give
\[
 e\le\inf_{L\ge0}\left\{Lb+\sqrt{\frac{m\log m}{2\,2^L}}\right\}.
\]
Consequently a family with trace-distance covering number $N_n(a)$ satisfies
\[
 e_n\le2a+\inf_{L\ge0}\left\{Lb_n+
            \sqrt{\frac{N_n(a)\log N_n(a)}{2\,2^L}}\right\}.
\]
In particular, $b_n\to0$ and $b_n\log N_n(a_n)\to0$ for some $a_n\to0$ suffice for classical reconstruction, even when covering entropy grows. This is a quantitative operational extension. It does not supply an effective intrinsic-gap-to-mixture bound. The minimax step gives existence of a common decoder; acquiring its measurements and prepared states remains a separate cost.

\textbf{A direct obstruction to removing complexity control.} The final escalation constructs finite faithful experiments for which both the pair gap and two-broadcast error vanish, while EB reconstruction error tends to one. Start with the dyadic family
\[
 \rho_\psi=\frac1L\bigoplus_{j=0}^{L-1}
       (|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)^{\otimes 2^j},
 \qquad
 \mathcal H=\bigoplus_{j=0}^{L-1}\operatorname{Sym}^{2^j}(\mathbb C^d).
\]
Reading the copy-count flag and splitting each nonbottom sector in half shifts a uniform ladder by one step. A fixed replacement handles its bottom sector. Both marginals have trace-distance error exactly $1/L$ for this channel. With $m=2^{L-1}$, the standard Haar state-estimation bound gives
\[
 e_{\rm EB}\ge1-\frac1L\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}\frac{2^j+1}{2^j+d}
              \ge1-\frac{m+1}{m+d}.
\]
For overlap $t=|\langle\psi,\phi\rangle|$, the intrinsic gap telescopes to $(t-t^{2^L})/L\le1/L$. Choose $d=Lm$; then the broadcasting error vanishes and the EB error tends to one. A tiny identity mixture makes every state faithful. Finite nets, or a finite exact moment design, retain the conclusion. The same family broadcasts to $K_L$ receivers whenever $\log K_L=o(L)$, with vanishing marginal error. It still cannot be reconstructed uniformly through a classical record. The construction and lower bound passed an independent audit; no historical-priority claim is made. Evidence: \texttt{broadcast\_entropy\_counterexample/PROOF.md} and its independent audit.

\subsection{One integer hitting list for every coefficient field}
\textbf{Constructive candidate with complete internal interface audits.} Given only variable count $n$ and size bound $s$, one deterministic machine prints a polynomial-size list of tuples of integer matrices, with polynomial dimension, entry length and total bit cost. The same list, reduced through the prime-subfield map, supplies a defined, invertible evaluation of every nonzero admissible noncommutative rational formula of size at most $s$ over every field. Arbitrary coefficient extensions are allowed; every inverse occurrence in the original expression retains its domain requirement.

The changed premise is a folded Newton reversal construction over $F(q)$ that works in every characteristic. Its lossless expansion replaces the ordinary-Wronskian restriction in the source's strong-pencil proof. A symbol-division lift and simultaneous semisimple descent give prime-specific lists. Clearing denominators and replacing generic inverses with
\[
 \lambda\,\operatorname{adj}(U_0)U_i
\]
gives an integer grid that works in characteristic zero and every sufficiently large characteristic. Appending the finitely many small-prime lists removes the characteristic input entirely. Independent audits covered arbitrary coefficient fields, all primitive-root branches, determinant degrees, complete integer-bit generation, and original rational-expression domains.

The construction also computes exact noncommutative rank given a service returning exact matrix evaluations or ranks. The matrices and their coefficients remain a charged input interface. This is no result about scalar commutative PIT or general arithmetic circuits, and it is not the first white-box noncommutative-rank algorithm.

The polynomial has severe constants and exponents: conservative parameters already give more than $4\cdot10^{62}$ tuples when $n=B=1$ in the underlying pencil construction. The full generator was not executed. Exact component diagnostics support only their specified identities. Source correctness and priority remain externally unverified.

\section{Other substantial mechanism advances}
\subsection{Four-state reconstruction with arbitrary edge noise}
\textbf{Source-dependent theorem candidate with exact source-certificate replay.} For a known locally finite tree and four-state Potts edge parameters $0<\lambda_e\le1$, set
\[
 R_e=\prod_{f\le e}\lambda_f^2.
\]
Reconstruction is equivalent to the existence of a positive-mass conserved flow $\theta$ with
\[
 \sup_{\xi\text{ ray}}\sum_{e\in\xi}(1-\lambda_e^4)
                  \left(\frac{\theta_e}{R_e}\right)^2<\infty.
\]
This removes bounded degree and uniform contraction from the inherited criterion. Deterministic edges contribute no artificial cost; near-deterministic subdivisions preserve the weighted energy. The key new occupation potential is quadratic, and it controls posterior-shape errors that a scalar Taylor expansion misses.

Independent checks covered all locally finite boundary cases and replayed the source's scalar and symmetry reductions, 4,479 row(a) cells and 1,935 row(b) cells. This remains dependent on the source's probabilistic posterior reduction. No labeled-SBM or HMM compiler theorem is claimed. Two four-state channels can have the same leading eigenvalue and critical tree yet opposite reconstruction outcomes, so the result does not extend by spectral analogy.

Evidence: \texttt{statistics\_learning/weighted\_capacity\_proof.md}, \texttt{weighted\_capacity\_audit/AUDIT.md}, \texttt{potts\_certificate\_audit/}.

\subsection{Global certificates for unbounded reaction classes}
\textbf{Candidate completeness theorem; direct certificate soundness.} Rational affine-family reconstruction extends fixed-scale rationalization to a family valid at every sufficiently small scale. With $h=z^q$, rational-power offsets become Laurent polynomials. Four finite real-closed-field obligations certify central-box coverage, global compactness of the plateau, inclusion of the class representative, and strict drift at every active nonneutral facet. Exact quantifier elimination decides each candidate. Enumeration therefore terminates on promised rational endotactic inputs if the reconstructed affine lemma holds.

This closes the previous global-recognition interface at the level of computability. No polynomial cost bound or general implemented CAD engine is supplied. A specialized exact search did acquire a genuinely unbounded example, rather than merely verify supplied coefficients. For
\[
 A\rightleftarrows2B,\qquad B\rightleftarrows2A,
 \qquad k_e(t)\in[1,4],
\]
the search examined 4,850 candidates and obtained
\[
 x,y\ge1/32,\qquad7/64\le x+y\le64/3
\]
as a common invariant absorber. The continuous-scale proof is exact; its execution validates this fixture and checker, not general termination.

The convex plateau mechanism also yields finite-time permanence for reaction--diffusion systems with one common positive scalar diffusion coefficient. Conserved projections homogenize by the heat equation; neutral-facet slack and a weak maximum principle give finite entry into the full concentration plateau. Exact finite-time entry into the mean stoichiometric class is false. Generic unequal-diffusion permanence remains unresolved.

\subsection{Quantum physical and statistical extensions}
The evidence includes a full-state radial-limit theorem for invariant spin backgrounds, a quantitative bounded-Gibbs perturbation modulus in reference-weighted $L^2$, and approximate-conservation classicalization. For bounded perturbations $A,B$ and arbitrary faithful $\omega$, the derived general continuity rate is logarithmic in $\epsilon=\Tr\omega(A-B)^2$, with a matching two-dimensional obstruction to any dimension-uniform H\"older bound. If the comparison perturbation commutes with $\omega$, a stronger square-root bound follows from Golden--Thompson.

For local Hamiltonians close in coupling strength to an invariant model, approximate conservation can suffice even when the total perturbation norm diverges with system size. Informative $N^{-1/2}$ field scaling requires additional uniform concentration and commutator assumptions, explicitly retained in the proof. A supplied one-dimensional Gibbs preparation algorithm gives a polynomial-time quantum encoder and decoder retaining only a fixed finite classical alphabet at each accuracy. Fresh reference preparations supply the conditional multiplicity states; capped rejection and a hybrid error bound account for rare cells. This is a quantum-circuit capability, not a classical laptop simulation. The newly claimed preparation theorem is imported and its full proof was not audited here.

The exact Gaussian boundary is stronger than a counterexample. For arbitrary known thermal means $n_i$ and unrestricted displacement parameters, the internally audited candidate minimax error over all entanglement-breaking channels is
\[
 e=\TV\!\left(\bigotimes_i\operatorname{Geom}(n_i),
                   \bigotimes_i\operatorname{Geom}(n_i+1)\right).
\]
With $s=\sum_i(n_i+1)^{-2}$, it tends to zero exactly when $s\to0$, and to one exactly when $s\to\infty$. Correlated non-Gaussian seeds do not improve the bound: a coordinatewise decreasing likelihood-ratio photon test and amplifier stochastic domination prove the collective lower bound. Pairwise defects instead depend on the largest individual reciprocal-temperature square. Thus many individually nearly classical modes can retain substantial collective quantum information. The proof does not assume tensorization of one-mode minimax errors.

A sharp obstruction prevents dimension-blind transfer of earlier fixed-dimensional results: there are faithful spectra with $d_n\to\infty$ and
\[
 \|d_n\operatorname{diag}(p_n)-I\|_{\rm op}\to0
\]
whose unknown-basis iid orbit has worst-case entanglement-breaking reconstruction error tending to one. The proof uses an exact collective Gaussian benchmark and diagonal fixed-dimensional LAN; it does not assert a uniform finite-$n$ LAN rate.
