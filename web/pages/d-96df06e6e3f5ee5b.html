## Exact interface and independently derived bound

Let H be a finite Hermitian Hamiltonian, beta>0, rho its full-rank Gibbs state.
Acquire an exact full-Hilbert-space logical qubit Pauli algebra O1,O2,O3, so
there is a factorization C^2 tensor H_A. A Pauli algebra only on the ground
band is insufficient. Take independent Hermitian couplings A_alpha,
nonnegative KMS rates gamma_alpha(omega)<=Gamma_alpha, and their spectral
components for this SAME full H. Let

    G=-i[K,.]+D_H,  [K,H]=0,
    c_j^H=(Tr rho [H,O_j]*[H,O_j])^(1/2),
    c_j^K=(Tr rho [K,O_j]*[K,O_j])^(1/2),
    k_alpha,j=sum_omega gamma_alpha(omega)
                        Tr rho C_omega* C_omega,
    C_omega=[O_j,A_alpha,omega],
    Lambda=2||K||+2sum_alpha Gamma_alpha||A_alpha||^2.

The generator is a completely positive trace-preserving semigroup and
D_H rho=0. If a Lamb shift is included, K=H+K_LS and its weighted
commutators must be charged. Scalar shifts of K can reduce Lambda.

There is an explicit CPTP encoder Enc(tau)=tau tensor rho_A and decoder
Dec=Tr_A, with Dec Enc=id, such that

    ||exp(tG)Enc(tau)-Enc(tau)||_1 <=t E,
    E=sum_j [c_j^K+sum_alpha sqrt(Gamma_alpha)||A_alpha||sqrt(k_alpha,j)]
       +(beta Lambda/2)sum_j c_j^H.                    (1)

The decoded error is at most the same bound. This is a finite-temperature
criterion; uniform beta->infinity and preparation of rho_A remain separate
obligations. No efficient acquisition or thermal initialization is inferred.

## Gibbs interpolation and honest encoding

For Hermitian O, C=[H,O] is anti-Hermitian. Duhamel gives, up to sign,

    [rho,O]=beta integral_0^1 rho^s C rho^(1-s) ds.

At Re(s)=0 and1, imaginary powers are unitaries and the trace norms reduce
to those of C rho and rho C. Hilbert-Schmidt factorization bounds both by
c=(Tr rho C* C)^(1/2); anti-Hermiticity gives CC*=C*C. Schatten three-lines
then yields ||[rho,O]||_1<=beta c, with no smallest-eigenvalue constant.

The exact logical Pauli twirl is

    bar_rho=(rho+sum_j O_j rho O_j)/4=I_L/2 tensor rho_A,
    delta=||bar_rho-rho||_1<=(beta/4)sum_j c_j^H.

For a Bloch vector b let F_b=I+sum b_j O_j. Its norm is at most2, and
Enc(tau)=bar_rho F_b. Hence the difference from rho F_b has trace norm at
most2delta. The intermediate rho F_b need not be Hermitian, so the
generator estimate used here must hold on arbitrary complex inputs.
The dual completely bounded pinching argument supplies precisely that
bound: ||G||_(1->1)<=Lambda.

## KMS product identity, with one weighted commutator orientation

Fix one Hermitian coupling S and put C_omega=[O,S_omega]. The convention
is positive omega for energy released to the bath. Then

    S_-omega=S_omega*,  C_-omega=-C_omega*,
    rho S_omega*=exp(-beta omega)S_omega* rho,
    gamma(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma(omega).

Expand the dissipator on rho O and subtract (D rho)O=0. Before reindexing
the exact expression is

    -sum gamma S_omega rho C_omega*
    +rho sum gamma C_omega* S_omega/2
    -rho sum gamma S_omega* C_omega/2.

KMS reindexing gives

    rho sum gamma S_omega* C_omega
                 =-sum gamma S_omega rho C_omega*,
    D(rho O)=[rho sum gamma C_omega* S_omega
                    -sum gamma S_omega rho C_omega*]/2.    (2)

This does NOT assume [O,H]=0. Put k=sum gamma Tr rho C_omega*C_omega.
Hilbert-Schmidt row Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the second term by
sqrt(Gamma)||S||sqrt(k). For the first use rectangular row
R=[sqrt(gamma) sqrt(rho) C_omega*]_omega and column
T=[sqrt(gamma) S_omega]_omega. Then ||R||_2^2=k,
||T||^2=||sum gamma S_omega*S_omega||<=Gamma||S||^2, and
the term is sqrt(rho)RT. Schatten Holder gives the same bound.
The two halves add to sqrt(Gamma)||S||sqrt(k), with no frequency count
and no additional opposite-orientation thermal quantity.

Since [K,rho]=0, the coherent term on rho O_j is at most c_j^K.
Stationarity, ||F_b||<=2 and the full complex trace-norm generator bound
give (1). The final integration uses trace contraction only on the
Hermitian derivative G Enc(tau), so it does not invoke an invalid
Jensen bound for a coherent generator.

## Optional Lamb shift for the protected exact centralizer route

Suppose H_d is a syndrome function, rho its Gibbs state, P_good commutes
with rho and the logical algebra, and each encoded state is O_tau rho,
||O_tau||<=2. Let K_LS be Hermitian with [K_LS,H_d]=0 and
[K_LS,O_tau]P_good=0. Then

    [K_LS,Enc(tau)]=[K_LS,O_tau](I-P_good)rho,
    ||[K_LS,Enc(tau)]||_1<=4||K_LS||p_bad.             (3)

Global commutation of K_LS with the logical algebra is unnecessary.
The full Gibbs state remains stationary. Approximate stationarity and
semigroup contraction add (3) to the Chesi drift bound.

For independent bath channels with real signed coefficients
|ell_alpha(omega)|<=L_alpha,

    K_LS=sum_alpha,omega ell_alpha(omega)A_alpha,omega*A_alpha,omega,
    ||K_LS||<=sum_alpha L_alpha||A_alpha||^2.          (4)

Indeed each channel lies in operator order between plus and minus
L_alpha times its unweighted pinching. Summing operator norms gives (4).
At fixed H and signed rates, rectangular columns with a diagonal sign
contraction similarly give

    ||K_T-K_S||<=L_alpha(||T||+||S||)||T-S||,
    ||[-i(K_T-K_S),.]||_diamond<=2||K_T-K_S||.         (5)

For syndrome H_d, each Fourier component of a q-qubit operator is a sum
of raw support-q Paulis times syndrome-diagonal projectors. In a product
A_omega*A_omega the raw Pauli products have support at most2q; diagonal
factors commute the logical algebra and P_good. Protection of ALL such
raw Paulis on an eroded stable projector therefore proves the premise of
(3), without a coefficient-l1 cost. Single-Pauli protection alone does
not automatically protect the intermediate syndrome of a product.
