N58 THREE-GAIN KINETIC REGULARIZATION: RECONSTRUCTION AND BRIDGE AUDIT
Date: 8 October 2026
Status: local rank proof reconstructed; exact rational fixture check replayed;
downstream nonconcentration is conditional on the pinned source's unchanged
kernel/tail/axis inputs; Landau and hydrodynamic transfers remain UNKNOWN.

DECISION

The seven-coordinate rank lemma has a precise qualitative use: with the
source's hard-sphere gain kernels, bounded parameter densities, compact
truncation, and the source's C1 convergence, three successive gains cannot
keep charging a bounded input that vanishes in measure on compact sets away
from the velocity axis. The finite-temperature three-gain replacement also
preserves the source cap contradiction, conditional on the other OA363
lemmas. This is a local compactness/nonconcentration statement.

A quantitative local version follows if one adds a uniform lower bound on a
seven-dimensional Jacobian and a rate for the input's L1 convergence. Those
constants and that rate are not supplied by N58. Almost-everywhere rank alone
has no convergence modulus.

No Landau coercivity estimate follows from this rank map, and it implies no hydrodynamic limit. The witness
uses a middle increment k2=e2 of fixed size. On bounded velocity sets, a
grazing cutoff of aperture delta permits only increments O(delta), so this
witness leaves the active collision support as delta tends to zero. The
normalized grazing kernel also loses the source's uniformly bounded
parameter-mass hypothesis. More decisively, the source's positive hot
generator has mass production of order delta^(-2); the standard signed
Boltzmann/Landau linearization restores conservation by the cold-parent loss
that removes the source branching mechanism.

1. EXACT INTERFACE AND SOURCE BOUNDARY

The pinned physical source is OA363, Nonuniqueness with local conservation
for the hard-sphere Boltzmann equation, at OpenAI/math commit
adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a, file
preprints/Nonuniqueness-with-local-conservation-for-the-hard-sphere-Boltzmann-equation-October-5-2026/build/paper/10-nonconcentration.tex.
The original source proves the five-gain version (labels
nc:plane-parameters, nc:uniform-pullback, lem:five-gain-rank and
nc:five-nonaxial-gains). The immutable R-package source supplies the
three-gain replacement proof dated 7 October 2026 and its downstream audit.

For fixed t0>0, x0 in R^3, and nonaxial v0 (its component perpendicular to
e3 is nonzero), choose three positive scales and signs. Let p_i(t,x)=
a_i(t,x_3)e3 be the source's real-analytic axial cold profiles. For times
t_i>0 and L_i in R^3, recursively define
  x_i = x_(i-1) - (t_(i-1)-t_i) v_(i-1),
  n_i = v_(i-1) - p_i(t_i,x_i),
  k_i = n_i cross L_i,
  v_i = v_(i-1) + k_i,                    i=1,2,3.
The output map is Phi_3(t1,t2,t3,L1,L2,L3)=(t3,x3,v3), from an open
12-dimensional parameter domain to the seven coordinates (time, position,
velocity). Its domain is (0,infinity)^3 x R^9; the physical history region
is 0<t3<t2<t1<t0. The lemma is for each fixed nonaxial endpoint, scales and
sign choices; it does not assert uniformity over those inputs.

The gain integration uses collision-plane increments k_i in n_i-perp with
area measure dA(k_i)/|n_i|. The plane-parameter identity
  integral_(n-perp) F(k) dA(k)/|n|
    = integral_R3 F(n cross L) |n| zeta(L dot n_hat) dL
holds for a bounded normalized one-dimensional density zeta. On compact
truncations with |n| bounded above and below, this gives bounded densities
in the redundant L coordinates. The finite-temperature maps add three
spectator vectors w_i, so the parameter domain used by the pullback argument
has 12+9=21 coordinates. For the source kernels, the gain operator
  (V_n F)(t,x,v)
    = integral_a^t integral F(s,x-(t-s)v,xi)
      K_n(s,x-(t-s)v,v; dp dxi) ds
is positive, with kernel mass at most C/t and the source uniform tail and
large-output-velocity bounds. In particular, for fixed a>0,
  ||V_n^j F(t)||_infinity
    <= ||F||_infinity [C log(t/a)]^j/j!,   a<=t<=1.
These constants, masks, tails, and axial estimates are inherited source
inputs, not consequences of the rank lemma.

2. FULL THREE-GAIN RANK PROOF

Claim. For every fixed axial profile triple (the witness needs only the
profile values and C1 regularity), every nonaxial v0, every t0>0, and every
positive scale/sign choice, rank D Phi_3=7 almost everywhere on
(0,infinity)^3 x R^9, hence almost everywhere in the strictly ordered
history region.

Proof of a full-rank witness. Rotate around e3 so v0=(R,0,z), R>0. Choose
0<h<tau<t0 and put
  t1=t2=tau,  t3=tau-h,  k1=0,  k2=e2=(0,1,0),  k3=0.
The k2 increment is admissible in its collision plane: n2 is orthogonal to
e2, and L2=-(n2 cross e2)/|n2|^2 gives n2 cross L2=e2. Take L1=L3=0.
The first two collision points agree. Since each p_i is axial, write
  n1=(R,0,b1), n2=(R,0,b2), v2=(R,1,z), n3=(R,1,b3),
where b1,b2,b3 are the actual profile-dependent axial components.
Every n_i is nonzero. Crucially n3 dot k2=1, independent of the profiles.

First, the angular variables L1,L2 span every final-velocity variation at
fixed collision times. At t1=t2, changing L1,L2 leaves x1,x2 fixed and
therefore produces no profile-position derivative in p2. Using [q]_x r =
q cross r,
  delta v2 = (I-[L2]_x)[n1]_x delta L1 + [n2]_x delta L2.
A vector q annihilating the second term must be parallel to n2. If also it
annihilated the first term, then (I-[L2]_x)^T n2 would be parallel to n1.
But
  (I-[L2]_x)^T n2 = n2 + L2 cross n2 = n2-k2 = (R,-1,b2),
which is not parallel to n1=(R,0,b1). Thus the combined angular derivative
is onto R^3.

Set L3=0. A first-two-gain variation producing delta v2=d then gives
(delta x3,delta v3)=(-h d,d). The derivative of p3 is killed by L3=0.
For any d in n3-perp, additionally vary L3 by
  delta L3=(n3 cross d)/|n3|^2.
This adds -d to delta v3 and zero to delta x3, giving the position-only
column (-h d,0). A basis of n3-perp therefore gives two independent
position directions.

The t2 column, at this witness with t3 fixed, is
  (partial_t2 x3, partial_t2 v3)
    = (-k2-h partial_t2 v2, partial_t2 v2).
Subtract the first-two angular variation with d=partial_t2 v2. This leaves
(-k2,0). Since n3 dot k2=1, k2 is outside n3-perp, so the two plane
directions and k2 span all three position directions. The first-two angular
variables already span all three final-velocity directions; subtracting
position-only columns gives three independent velocity-only directions.
Thus the (x3,v3) derivative has rank six while t3 is fixed. The output-time
coordinate t3 has derivative one in its own parameter column and zero in
these six selected directions, proving rank seven.

Analytic continuation. The recursion and profiles make Phi_3 real analytic
on the connected open domain (0,infinity)^3 x R^9. The witness makes some
7x7 minor a nonzero analytic function. The zero set of a nonzero real
analytic function on a connected open set has Lebesgue measure zero.
Rank failure is contained in that zero set. This proves the almost-everywhere
claim on the full domain and on its ordered subregion. The witness is on the
equal-time slice t1=t2, but the analytic argument is on the full connected
domain; continuity also gives nearby strictly ordered witnesses.

Minimality. With two gains there are two time variables and six L
coordinates, but each independent gauge direction delta L_i parallel to
n_i leaves n_i cross L_i and every later state unchanged. When n_i=0, its
angular block is zero and rank cannot increase. Hence the two-gain map has
rank at most 8-2=6 (or less), so cannot submerse onto seven coordinates.
Three gains are minimal for this particular seven-coordinate pullback.

This proof is independent of profile values/derivatives at the witness, and
of spatial masks. It is not uniform as R tends to zero, over scale/sign
families, over growing parameter compacts, or as the collision law changes.

3. WHAT THE R PACKAGE EXTENDS TO: QUALITATIVE NONCONCENTRATION

The source's abstract pullback lemma has the following interface. If
Phi_n -> Phi in C1_loc, rank D Phi=7 almost everywhere, parameter densities
a_n are supported in a common compact K and bounded by A, and F_n are
supported in a compact B, uniformly bounded by M and tend to zero in
Lebesgue measure on B, then
  integral |F_n(Phi_n(q)) a_n(q)| dq -> 0.
Proof: boundedness and convergence in measure on finite-volume B give
||F_n||_1 ->0. Remove an open neighborhood E of the critical set in K
with |E intersect K|<epsilon. Cover K\E by finitely many neighborhoods on
which some seven-coordinate minor is nonzero. If pi projects to the other
parameter coordinates, G=(Phi,pi) is a local diffeomorphism there; C1
convergence makes G_n=(Phi_n,pi) diffeomorphisms with uniformly bounded
inverse Jacobians on smaller neighborhoods. Change variables to
(y,z)=(Phi_n(q),pi(q)); each regular-chart integral is bounded by a fixed
constant times ||F_n||_1. The critical-neighborhood contribution is at most
A M epsilon. First n tends to infinity, then epsilon to zero. Arbitrary
measurable masks bounded by one are absorbed into a_n.

The R package's THREE_GAIN_DOWNSTREAM_CHECK applies this to the source
three-gain map. Here is the complete conditional proof structure, with the
unchanged source inputs kept visible. Fix a>0, take n to infinity first, and
assume convergent endpoints with a nonaxial limiting velocity, 0<=f_n<=2,
and f_n ->0 in measure on compact subsets away from the velocity axis.

First remove endpoint/path tails. At any one of the three gains, the source
uniform tail estimate makes the removed normalized mass arbitrarily small;
the other two gains and the input are bounded by the iterated estimate
||V_n^j F||_infinity <= ||F||_infinity [C log(1/a)]^j/j!. Sum over the three
possible deleted locations. The first-kernel large-output bound rules out
unbounded endpoint velocities. After bounded velocities and increments are
retained, the first-core spatial escape bound rules out endpoints escaping
all dilated periodic origins. Thus any subsequence with positive integral
has, after periodic translation and subselection, convergent endpoints, and
all retained paths lie in fixed compact sets.

On such a path, successively delete gains whose input transverse velocity
satisfies |v-bar_i|<b_i. At each stage the source off-axis small-input
estimate makes this cost as small as prescribed; choose b_i after earlier
thresholds, while the finitely many later gains are bounded by the same
iteration estimate. Since cold transverse widths vanish uniformly on
retained compact paths, the remaining collision normals satisfy
|n_i|>=b/2 for one b>0. Next delete collision points |y_i|<rho. Conditional
on earlier parameters, y_i is affine in t_i with derivative v-bar_(i-1);
the deleted time interval has length at most 2rho/b. The current kernel
mass is at most C/a and later gains are bounded, so all three deletions cost
at most C_a rho/b. Send rho small only after the b_i choices.

The remaining annular collision scales lie in one compact interval
[rho_-,rho_+] contained in (0,infinity). Scale-mesh convergence therefore
leaves finitely many scale/sign triples, all with positive limiting scales.
When U=1, remove t3>1-delta. Ordering forces all three collision times into
the top delta interval, so this costs at most
2(C/a)^3 delta^3/3!.
Remove also t3-a<kappa and each of the three gaps
t_(i-1)-t_i<kappa. These are four ordered-time boundary strips; each has
three-dimensional time volume O(kappa), with kernel factors bounded by C/a,
so their total cost is C_a kappa. After these deletions, the times lie in a
fixed compact subset of the strict ordering region.

For one retained scale/sign triple, write each collision-plane integral in
redundant L_i coordinates by the exact plane-parameter identity in section
1. The normals obey b/2<=|n_i|<=R on the compact path, and the kernel
amplitudes are bounded by C/a. The remaining H-ratios and masks are bounded
on the same compact set; all measurable restrictions are absorbed into the
parameter density. The finite-temperature map has 3 times, 9 L coordinates
and 9 thermal spectators, hence 21 input coordinates, and converges in C1
on this compact set to Phi_3. The output (t3,x3,v3) lies in a compact set
away from the velocity axis. Apply the uniform pullback proof above to F_n
on that compact: the integral for this fixed triple tends to zero. Sum over
the finitely many triples, then send all path, small-input, rho, delta and
kappa error tolerances to zero in their stated order. This proves
V_n^3 f_n(t_n,x_n,v_n)->0 in the nonaxial case.

For cap completion, the axial case is handled by the separate unchanged
two-axial-gain estimate: positivity and the source iteration bound give
(V_n^3 f_n)(t_n,x_n,v_n)
 <= 2 C log(1/a) (V_n^2 1)(t_n,x_n,v_n) ->0
at bounded axial endpoints. The endpoint/path truncation handles escape.
Thus both axial and nonaxial limiting endpoints have vanishing three-gain
residual, conditional on the imported source estimates.

With the source positive mild inequality
  f_n <= d_n + V_n f_n + e_n,
||d_n||_infinity<=a+ell_n, 0<=e_n<=epsilon_n,
three iterations give
  f_n <= (a+ell_n+epsilon_n)
       [1+C log(1/a)+(C log(1/a))^2/2] + V_n^3 f_n.
For cap endpoints with f_n>=1/2, bounded-path compactness and the
separate two-axial-gain lemma handle axial limits; the three-gain pullback
handles nonaxial limits. If the source's other hypotheses hold, the residual
tends to zero and the cap inequality becomes
  1/2 <= a[1+C log(1/a)+(C log(1/a))^2/2],
contradicting a downarrow 0. This is a valid conditional shortening of the
source's five-gain nonconcentration argument. It does not validate the
original manuscript's unrelated lemmas or global theorem.

4. QUANTITATIVE LOCAL RESULT THAT DOES FOLLOW WITH EXTRA INPUT

A local chart version is quantitative. Let U subset R^7 and Z subset R^q
be bounded, and suppose for each n and almost every z in Z the map
u -> Phi_n(u,z) is injective on U and
  |det D_u Phi_n(u,z)| >= sigma > 0.
Suppose |a_n(u,z)|<=A. The area formula and Fubini give, for any measurable
F in L1(R^7),
  integral_(U x Z) |F(Phi_n(u,z)) a_n(u,z)| du dz
      <= A |Z| sigma^(-1) ||F||_1.
If each map has multiplicity at most N rather than being injective, multiply
the right side by N. In particular, if |F_n|<=M, supp F_n subset B with B
finite measure, and
  |{y in B: |F_n(y)|>eta}| <= omega_n(eta),
then
  integral |F_n(Phi_n) a_n|
      <= A N |Z| sigma^(-1)
         [eta |B| + M omega_n(eta)].
This is a full proof by change of variables, and is an explicit quantitative
nonconcentration modulus once sigma, A, |Z|, N and omega_n are specified.

At any fixed full-rank witness of Phi_3, the inverse function theorem
provides a sufficiently small parameter chart with such a positive sigma
after freezing the complementary coordinates; C1-close finite-temperature
maps retain a smaller sigma on that chart. The source's rank proof therefore
does give a quantitative estimate on that chosen chart, with constants
depending on its profile values, endpoint, scales, chart, and density bound.
This only controls the chart contribution. To control the whole collision
integral quantitatively, one must also quantify the mass near the critical
set and give uniform bounds for all retained charts and the input's L1
decay. N58 only proves the critical set is null and assumes convergence in
measure. Its proof chooses a critical neighborhood of arbitrary small mass
before taking n to infinity; it gives no rate linking that mass to the
Jacobian threshold. This is why the global source conclusion is qualitative.

There is no universal rate even if rank is everywhere full and the density
is one. Let Phi(u,z)=u on (0,1)^7 x (0,1)^q. For any prescribed sequence
r_n downarrow 0, as slow as desired, put
  E_n=(0,r_n) x (0,1)^6,  F_n=1_(E_n).
Then F_n ->0 in measure, ||F_n||_infinity=1, rank D Phi=7 everywhere, but
  integral F_n(Phi(u,z)) du dz = r_n |(0,1)^q|.
Thus rank and convergence in measure cannot force any uniform decay rate.
This example has the exact same seven-coordinate interface; it falsifies a
quantitative-rate claim based only on the N58 assumptions.

5. LANDAU BRIDGE: EXPLICIT GEOMETRIC AND SIGNED-OPERATOR OBSTRUCTIONS

Geometric degeneration. In the hard-sphere witness k2=e2 has size one.
For a grazing collision of aperture delta, the elastic collision rule gives
|k_i|<=C |v_(i-1)-p_i| delta. If all retained velocities and cold profiles
are bounded by a fixed M, then |k_i|<=C_M delta, and three gains change
velocity by at most 3 C_M delta. The witness k2=e2 is outside the support
when delta<1/C_M. In the limiting zero-increment map,
  v3=v0,  x3=x0-(t0-t3)v0,
so the output (t3,x3,v3) has rank at most two, not seven. Landau diffusion is
built from the accumulated second moments of many small deflections; a
three-collision hard-sphere submersion does not supply that limit.
The grazing normalization m2,delta=int sin^2(theta) b_delta(dsigma) comparable
to one also forces total angular mass beta_delta=int b_delta(dsigma) at
least c delta^(-2). Thus the source's bounded-kernel-mass and bounded-density
hypotheses are not uniform under this scaling. This matches the actual
published bridge: Alexandre-Villani (2004) obtain the inhomogeneous grazing
approximation from finite mass, energy, entropy and entropy-production
estimates on finite time intervals; Carrillo-Delgadino-Wu (2021/2022) frame
the limit through lower-semicontinuous convergence of entropy dissipation.
Those estimates use the signed collision/entropy structure absent from
finite-dimensional rank.

There is an exact obstruction to transferring the source's positive hot
generator. Let B,h>=0 have finite nonzero masses and compact velocity
supports separated by positive distance. Let the kinetic factor be positive
and continuous on their support product. Let b_delta>=0 be supported on
deflection angles theta<=delta and satisfy
  c <= m2,delta=int sin^2(theta)b_delta(dsigma) <= C.
Writing beta_delta=int b_delta, sin^2(theta)<=delta^2 gives
  beta_delta >= c delta^(-2).
For the symmetric gain P_delta and loss frequency
  nu_delta,B(v)=beta_delta integral |v-w|^gamma B(w) dw,
elastic gain symmetry gives integral P_delta(B,h) dv
 = integral nu_delta,B(v) h(v) dv. Consequently for the source's positive
hot generator A_delta=2P_delta(B,h)-nu_delta,B h,
  integral A_delta(v) dv
    = beta_delta integral integral |v-w|^gamma B(w)h(v) dw dv
    >= c(B,h) delta^(-2).
The lower bound is positive because the compact support product is separated
and the kinetic factor has a positive minimum there. Elasticity keeps all
outputs in one fixed compact ball, so a fixed smooth compactly supported
test equal to one on that ball witnesses the same distributional divergence.
This is not merely mass escaping to infinity.

The full derivative of the ordinary Boltzmann collision operator at B is
  L_delta,B h
    = 2P_delta(B,h)-nu_delta,B h-nu_delta,h B
    = A_delta - nu_delta,h B.
The omitted cold-parent loss is precisely part of the order-delta^(-2)
cancellation. The standard grazing weak form uses the whole two-particle
increment phi(v')+phi(w')-phi(v)-phi(w), whose first-order angular term
cancels and whose second moment produces the Landau operator. Keeping the
full signed linearization preserves mass; keeping the positive hot
branching factor does not. Therefore this argument rejects a direct
positive-branching transfer to Landau. It proves neither Landau uniqueness
nor a universal obstruction to a different signed construction.

No new Landau coercivity estimate follows from this rank statement. The
map Phi_3 is independent of the density gradients and of a lower bound for
the Landau diffusion quadratic form. The missing estimate for a nonuniqueness
transfer is a uniform lower bound for the complete signed Landau
linearization on a specified off-axis observable, while the total density
stays nonnegative and has controlled entropy production. For ordinary
coercivity, one needs an explicit function class, mass/energy/entropy bounds,
and a quantitative lower bound on the Landau entropy-dissipation form.
Such estimates do exist independently: Desvillettes (2015) bounds Coulomb
Landau entropy dissipation below by a weighted H^1 norm of sqrt(f), with
homogeneous-equation consequences. Those estimates use the Landau quadratic
form and its own hypotheses; they are not consequences of Phi_3. As a
logical check, multiplying a gain kernel by lambda>0 leaves its collision
map and rank unchanged while multiplying any quadratic dissipation by
lambda; geometry cannot set the coercivity constant. The physical Landau
normalization is fixed, so this scaling observation proves insufficiency of
the geometric inference, not failure of known Landau coercivity results.

6. HYDRODYNAMIC-LIMIT BRIDGE

A rank/submersion statement about one finite hard-sphere gain chain contains
no Knudsen or Boltzmann-Grad scaling, no sequence of solutions, no collision
invariants or local conservation laws, no entropy/energy compactness, and no
identification of limiting moments or constitutive coefficients. It cannot
by itself imply a kinetic-to-fluid or particle-to-fluid limit.

Primary literature illustrates the missing interface. Bardos-Golse-Levermore
(1993) use relative entropy about a Maxwellian for properly scaled
DiPerna-Lions solutions and derive the required infinitesimal-Maxwellian,
incompressibility/Boussinesq, and conservation information. Golse-Saint-Raymond
(2004) prove a Navier-Stokes limit for appropriately scaled renormalized
solutions with bounded collision kernels. Bodineau-Gallagher-Saint-Raymond-
Simonella (2022) derive equilibrium fluctuating hydrodynamics from a
deterministic dilute-gas limit using covariance asymptotics and a Wick rule.
Those are equation/scaling/compactness or particle-correlation arguments;
none is furnished by finite-dimensional gain rank.

The pinned R report separately records a nonequilibrium fluctuation target:
fixed-lifespan finite-dimensional distributions do not give a path-level
limit, and the required horizon/data/graph-size-uniform recollision and
centering moduli are missing. The reported sharp-summability gate has base
C T/sqrt(L), requiring L>(C T)^2, while the occupancy sum costs
(1-r)^(-L); a diagonal schedule after fixed-parameter convergence supplies
no prescribed rate. The three-gain lemma does not repair any of those
bounds.

7. STATUS, COMPARATOR, FALSIFIER, AND RESTART

Comparator. The immutable OA363 proof uses five gains and a seven-coordinate
rank assertion. N58 replaces it with three; two cannot work for this
parameterization. The source's downstream audit checks the cap proof
truncations after the replacement. No claim is made about improving the
full theorem, changing its data class, or clearing priority.

Falsifiers and tests that change the decision.
* Rank claim: a real-analytic axial profile and a nonaxial endpoint for which
  every relevant 7x7 minor vanishes on positive measure. The explicit
  profile-independent witness rules this out under the stated interface.
* Quantitative global claim from rank alone: the slab example in section 4
  falsifies it.
* Uniform Landau transfer: a proposed proof must retain a full-rank patch
  with controlled Jacobian and uniformly bounded parameter mass after
  restricting every collision to theta<=delta. The O(delta) increments,
  rank-two limit and beta_delta>=c delta^(-2) currently fail these gates.
* Positive Landau amplification: prove a lower bound for the full signed
  operator, with the cold-parent loss included, and preserve B+g>=0 plus
  entropy bounds. The order-delta^(-2) mass calculation falsifies the
  existing positive-generator route.
* Hydrodynamic conclusion: specify the scaling and target equations, then
  prove uniform entropy/moment compactness, local conservation and
  identification (or, for particle fluctuations, uniform collision-tree
  recollision and centering control). Three-gain rank has none of these.

Next decisive experiment: formulate one signed Landau observable inequality
for a fixed smooth B,h with separated supports, under the standard grazing
normalization, and compute whether the full linearized form has an
off-axis lower bound uniform in delta. Keep the loss term -nu_delta,h B.
A failure on this fixed pair is a fast falsifier; success still requires
nonnegative total densities, entropy production, spatial transport and
uniform time control. For quantitative N58 use, independently obtain a
critical-set sublevel modulus for the least singular value of D Phi_3 and
an explicit L1 rate for f_n. Do not substitute numerical fixtures for
either estimate.
