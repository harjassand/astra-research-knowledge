REVISION 3 — CONDITION ON ONE ORIENTATION, THEN METROPOLIS-REFRESH ITS FIBER
Worker c01_l05 | 7 October 2026

RESULT

There is a stronger exact transition than proposing an entire (I,J) uniformly.
Choose the retained orientation I uniformly, sample J exactly from its
conditional determinant-squared fiber by a rational projection-DPP sampler,
and Metropolis-correct using only the fiber normalizer
  Z_I = det(F[I,I^c] F[I,I^c]^*).
The value Z_I is one polynomial-bit determinant, not the target c_k. This
gives a reversible microstate chain with exact pairwise-flow minorization
  pi(x) K(x,y) >= pi(x)pi(y) / binom(n,k).
For two independently updated replicas, this proves the additive energy
inequality with coefficient binom(n,k), while preserving the mismatch-register
purification from INITIAL. Every transition has polynomial bit cost and needs
no partition-function or hole-coefficient oracle.

The coefficient is still exponential when k is central. On the directed
2q-cycle, the chain has exactly two positive states and spectral gap
  2 / binom(2q,q).
Thus the stronger fiber sampler improves the proposal interface and removes
the extra binom(n-k,k) combinatorial proposal factor, but it does not close
the uniform polynomial-energy gate. The cycle calculation is an obstruction
to this kernel, not to every possible kernel.

MICROSTATE LAW AND EXACT CONDITIONAL

Let F be in Q(i)^(n x n), k<=n/2, I,J subsets [n], |I|=|J|=k, I intersect
J=empty. Set
  w(I,J)=|det F[I,J]|^2,
  Z_I=sum_{J subset I^c, |J|=k} w(I,J),
  c=sum_{|I|=k} Z_I,
  pi(I,J)=w(I,J)/c.
Put A_I=F[I,I^c], a k by (n-k) matrix. Cauchy–Binet gives
  Z_I=det(A_I A_I^*).
If Z_I>0, A_I has full row rank and
  P_I=A_I^*(A_I A_I^*)^{-1}A_I
is a rational-complex Hermitian orthogonal projection of rank k on the I^c
coordinates. For every k-subset J of I^c,
  det(P_I[J,J]) = |det A_I[:,J]|^2 / det(A_I A_I^*)
                 = w(I,J)/Z_I.
So the conditional law J|I under pi is exactly the rank-k projection DPP
with kernel P_I. If Z_I=0, that fiber has no positive microstate and a
proposal to it is rejected.

RATIONAL SEQUENTIAL SAMPLER

The projection-DPP sample can be generated without an eigendecomposition.
For a current rank-r projection P on m remaining coordinates, choose j with
probability P[j,j]/r. If P[j,j]=0, its probability is zero. On the other
coordinates set
  P' = P[-j,-j] - P[-j,j] P[j,-j] / P[j,j].
This is a rank-(r-1) orthogonal projection. Recurse; at rank zero stop.
The first chosen coordinate j has the indicated rational probability because
trace(P)=r. For a fixed size-r set S containing j, the Schur-complement
identity gives det(P'[S\{j},S\{j}])=det(P[S,S])/P[j,j]. Therefore each
ordered first choice contributes det(P[S,S])/r, and summing over the r
possible first elements gives exactly det(P[S,S]). This proves the sampler's
law by induction, including zero minors and rank-deficient conditional
branches. The initial conditional projection P_I is full rank k whenever
Z_I>0.

Every probability used is an exact rational number: the Hermitian diagonal
entries are nonnegative rationals and sum to the integer current rank. A
categorical draw is implemented from a common integer denominator plus
uniform-rank sampling. For Gaussian-rational input, determinants and inverses
have polynomial bit length by fraction-free elimination and determinant
minor bounds; the recursion performs at most k rational Schur updates, so
the exact random choices and arithmetic have polynomial bit cost. This is an
expected-polynomial-time exact conditional sampler (random bits included).
The finite checker uses real integer fixtures; the argument covers Q(i).

METROPOLIS KERNEL AND PROOF OF THE ENERGY COEFFICIENT

Let B=binom(n,k). From current positive state x=(I,J):
  1. draw I' uniformly from all k-subsets of [n];
  2. if Z_I'=0, stay at x; otherwise draw J' from the exact conditional
     projection-DPP law w(I',.)/Z_I';
  3. accept y=(I',J') with alpha=min(1,Z_I'/Z_I), else stay at x.
The probability of a non-null proposal y is
  q(y)=w(y)/(B Z_{I_y});
its total can be below one because unsupported I' values contribute rejection.
For positive x,y the Metropolis ratio is
  pi(y)q(x)/(pi(x)q(y)) = Z_{I_y}/Z_{I_x},
so the stated acceptance rule is exact. In particular, c_k is not used.

For x != y, the stationary directed flow is exactly
  pi(x)K(x,y)
   = w(x)w(y) / [c B max(Z_{I_x},Z_{I_y})].                 (1)
Since each fiber total Z_I is at most c, (1) implies
  pi(x)K(x,y) >= pi(x)pi(y)/B.                              (2)
The same off-diagonal formula gives detailed balance. Taking the Dirichlet
form and summing (2) yields for every real g
  E_K(g,g) >= Var_pi(g)/B.                                  (3)
No lower bound on a positive Z_I or on individual minors is assumed.

Now independently update both replicas with K, giving K tensor K on
pi tensor pi. For any f(I,J)=g(I,J first replica)+h(I,J second replica),
independence and stationarity make the cross term between the two increments
zero, hence
  E_(K tensor K)(g(x)+h(y)) = E_K(g)+E_K(h).
The product variance is Var_pi(g)+Var_pi(h). Applying (3) to both terms
proves
  Var_(pi tensor pi)(g(x)+h(y))
      <= B E_(K tensor K)(g(x)+h(y)).                       (4)
If the common-core mismatch register Delta_C(x,y) from INITIAL is attached,
the lifted state is the graph {(x,y,Delta_C(x,y))}; this is an injective
relabeling. Recompute Delta_C after every accepted pair update. The measure,
variance and Dirichlet identities (1)–(4) are unchanged, so the register
retains all independent product mass and the old equality-cut annihilation
does not return.

If the requested gate is the full Poincare inequality for arbitrary joint
functions H(x,y), replace K by its lazy version Kbar=(Id+K)/2. Then
E_Kbar>=Var/(2B), all eigenvalues of Kbar are nonnegative, and tensorization
gives
  Var_(pi tensor pi)(H) <= 2B E_(Kbar tensor Kbar)(H).
Thus the construction supplies either the sharper B coefficient on the
additive two-replica class in (4), or the full-joint-class coefficient 2B
after laziness. Both remain exponential for central k.

DIRECTED-CYCLE LIMIT FOR THIS KERNEL

Take F[i,i+1 mod 2q]=1, n=2q, k=q. Exactly two I's have Z_I>0: the two
alternating sets, and each has exactly one positive conditional J with weight
one. From either state, a uniform I proposal chooses the other supported I
with probability 1/B, and the ratio Z_I'/Z_I is one. All other I proposals
are rejected. Thus K is the two-state chain with switch probability 1/B and
gap 2/B. This is exp(-Theta(q)). For an indicator g of the first replica's
alternating orientation, Var(g)=1/4 and E_K(g)=1/(2B), so any constant in
(4) is at least B/2 on this instance. The claimed B bound is therefore tight
up to a factor two for this kernel family. This matches the obstruction in
INITIAL while improving its transition's fiber handling.

The sequential projection-DPP/Gram–Schmidt sampler is standard; Hough,

Surveys 3 (2006), Algorithm 18,
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503110). DPPy gives a finite-kernel derivation of
the exact projection-DPP chain rule and conditional Gram–Schmidt weights
(Gautier, Polito, Bardenet, Valko, JMLR 20 (2019),
https://jmlr.org/papers/volume20/19-179/19-179.pdf, Sec. 3, Eq. (4)). The
conditional identity, rational-complex bit-cost specialization, independence
Metropolis correction, flow minorization, additive two-replica inequality,
and cycle obstruction above are derived here. The source sampler references
are presentation background; our exact projection formula is Hermitian over
Q(i).

BOUNDARY / NEXT GATE

This revision adds a polynomial-time exact conditional sampler and an
all-pairs energy bound at scale B=binom(n,k), without computing the target
normalizer or enumerating J. For fixed k, B is polynomial in n; that sector
is already directly enumerable in n^(2k) and is not a general FPRAS result.
For central k, B is exponential and the directed-cycle family attains an
exponential gap. A successful general gate still needs an outer-I proposal
whose density tracks Z_I, or a lifted flow that crosses the two cycle
orientations without probability 1/B while paying only polynomial cost.
The present theorem supplies neither. The mismatch-purified representation,
orientation marginals, transition bit cost, supplied support/witness entry
point, and all known limits must remain separate claims.
