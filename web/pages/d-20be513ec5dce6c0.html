PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISONS AND NOVELTY GATES -- 2026-10-08

1. New KLS mechanism
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05474v1 (Bizeul-Klartag-Lehec, 4 Oct 2026)
Full TeX Sections 3-7 read and reconstructed. All-order cumulant and tilt
operator bounds are their source claims, not new claims of this task.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01447v2 (Song-Zhang, 4 Oct 2026 revision)
Coefficient/curvature theorem read from primary TeX. The finite-degree
curvature interpolation is already explicit there. Full proof unaudited.

2. Positive Gaussianization consequence depends only on a July premise
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24164 (Letwin, 27 Jul 2026)
Exact all-symmetric-M Q8 statement checked against the primary page, and
its use checked in BKL Lemma 4.2. Complete original proof not audited.
T1-T3 in proof.txt are consequences of Q8; calling them a consequence unique
to the new KLS resolution would overstate the causal dependency.

3. Gaussian moment projection and Fisher/SPD geometry already have prior art
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.11092 (Vishwakarma-Moosath, 13 Jul 2026)
Inspected primary abstract, Sections 1-2, the moment-projection construction,
and Fisher/SPD pullback discussion. It studies moment matching into finite
exponential families and geometric distances from SPD embeddings. It does
not state a log-concave dimension-uniform Q8 stability bound in the inspected
sections; exact phrase checks for log-concave and Lipschitz were negative.
That absence is not a priority proof. Older references include Calvo-Oller
(1990), Brigo-Pistone (2016), and Cheng-Tong (2025); these were identified
but not fully audited. The T1-T3 novelty gate remains UNKNOWN.

4. Fisher geometry applications to Gaussian approximations are established
https://stuart.caltech.edu/publications/pdf/stuart197.pdf
Primary search-result excerpt inspected: nonlinear Bayesian observation
updates form joint Gaussian approximations by moment matching and perform
Gaussian conditioning. This motivates a named moment-surrogate continuation
task; our theorem does NOT certify those algorithms' original posterior
approximation error. No claim of an implemented algorithm is made.

5. Entropic barriers/strong self-concordance are an earlier neighboring route
https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.1587 (Bubeck-Eldan, 4 Dec 2014)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10947 (Chewi, 21 Dec 2021)
https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.05656 (Laddha-Lee-Vempala, 13 Nov 2019)
Primary abstracts inspected. These are prior art for log-partition geometry,
Fisher Hessians, and covariance derivative control. The present arbitrary-
score, one-step chi-square bound is not being advertised as a new barrier or
a new sampler. Their full proof/cost comparisons remain outside this task.

6. Robust statistics/SoS is already a changed frontier, not an open corollary
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30105 (Storozhenko, 24 Sep 2026)
Primary abstract reports degree-m SoS moments for every isotropic log-concave
distribution, and dimension-free robust-estimation consequences, using
Letwin Q8 and covariance-adapted localization. This predates both October
KLS papers. Only the exact primary abstract was inspected; theorem validity
remains SOURCE-REPORTED. It blocks a novelty claim based merely on invoking
new KLS to remove the Kothari-Steinhardt Poincare dependency.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.07465 (Kothari-Steinhardt, 20 Nov 2017)
Primary abstract checked for its Poincare-dependent moment and clustering
baseline. No SoS implementation is supplied here.

7. Negative transfer results
The Gaussian-mixture Taylor/gap separation and tracial quantum third-cumulant
formula are fully derived in proof.txt T5-T6 without source theorem imports.
The phenomena of multimodal bottlenecks, rare-state cumulants, and Jordan
multiplication are classical. An exact prior-art search establishing priority
for these particular combined formulations was NOT completed. Their scoped
refutation does not rely on novelty, and does not refute classical KLS.
