## Question and legal interface

Does the actual recursive 3D memory retain a stretched-exponential thermal storage guarantee when the excitation penalties vary and weak finite-range interactions couple the check eigenvalues, including the two CSS sectors? Yes, under a uniform **legal witness-erasure energy margin**, Gibbs-stationary dynamics, and an explicit bound on bath strength. The result concerns thermal encoding, not an arbitrary ground-state initialization.

Let the original commuting Pauli checks be C_a, with D_a=(I-C_a)/2. At level2k there is one logical qubit. The realizable joint syndrome space is

    S=S_X × S_Z,   S_X=B^2(C_(2k)),   S_Z=the dual boundary image.

Every realizable joint sector has dimension2. In the decoder-defined tensor factorization a logical-independent Hamiltonian is

    H=I_log ⊗ E,   E=diag(E(σ_X,σ_Z)).                 (1)

Do not assume that its joint Gibbs distribution factors between CSS sectors. Mixing those sectors in E is allowed.

Use the actual BDL coarse-graining decoder above level2, with deterministic zero-respecting choices of the local corrections. At level2 use a deterministic global minimum-weight correction f2(s), including f2(0)=0. The separate base-decoder note repairs the source's literal syndrome-radius wording. Its conclusion is the usual implication: if the resulting coarse syndrome is zero at level2, every raw single-qubit Pauli preserves both dressed logical observables at the initial syndrome. Let P1 project onto the intersection of the two sector-wise good sets. This projector and the dressed Pauli pair commute with (1).

The following exact sufficient condition is the base energy interface used in this proof. For each fixed legal top-level witness τ in either CSS sector, and every realizable joint σ containing that witness, require

    E(σ)-E(σ\τ) >= κ |τ|,     κ>0.                  (2)

Here σ\τ erases only the excited check bits in τ and leaves every other check, including the opposite sector, fixed. Only witness erasers need satisfy (2); a positive marginal for every check is a convenient stronger assumption. No claim is made that (2) is necessary for memory or that it is the mathematically largest possible class of energies.

The separately proved FINITE_DEFECT_BUDGET_AND_FAILURE.md relaxes (2) to κ|τ|-C_k, paying exp(βC_k) once in the final probability and error bounds. It admits finitely many adverse check penalties and a sub-Q_k total erasure loss budget; the formulas in this main note use C_k=0.

## The actual eraser and coupled Gibbs measure

BDL's witness-boundary lemma proves τ is a legal coboundary. Hence σ\τ=σ+τ remains in S, since τ⊂σ and the opposite-sector syndrome is unchanged. For fixed τ, the map σ↦σ+τ is injective. It does not introduce excited checks, even though the coarse-graining corrections used to identify the witness may move syndromes.

Let p_β(σ)=exp[-βE(σ)]/Z. Directly from (2),

    Σ_(σ containing τ) exp[-βE(σ)]
       <=exp[-βκ|τ|] Σ_(σ containing τ) exp[-βE(σ+τ)]
       <=exp[-βκ|τ|] Z.                              (3)

Thus Pr_β[τ⊂σ]≤exp[-βκ|τ|]. This proof uses the full joint partition function. It neither factorizes the CSS sectors nor compares the two whole Gibbs measures by exp[O(N||V||)]. It also avoids paths through unrealizable one-bit intermediate syndromes.

The same argument holds after conditioning on any fixed opposite-sector syndrome. Consequently cross-sector finite-range interactions cause no new obstacle once (2) holds uniformly.

## Entropy, with the exceptional levels charged

The BDL witness facts used here are: a bad sector has a witness graph K with m≥Q_k=2^(k-1) nodes; each top witness is legal; the sum of its syndrome weights over all levels is at most4|τ|; the number of witness graphs with m nodes is at most ν(Δ+1)^(2m), where ν=|V(G2)| and Δ is the full decoding-graph degree. M bounds the number of syndrome slots in a local region, and Δ_l bounds the same-level graph degree. These are named model-specific prior inputs, with the corrected bookkeeping below. The new energetic step is (3).

The modified level2 lookup is not locally computable. Moreover, σ0=σ1=0, while nodes at those levels can be marked. Thus one must not apply the source's local syndrome-domination argument to G0,G1,G2. Set

    C_low=|V(G0)|+|V(G1)|+|V(G2)|,
    A=2(1+Δ_l²),
    Q_k>=2C_low.                                    (4)

At i≥3, every marked node is within graph distance2, in its connected component and the same level, of a syndrome-bearing node. Indeed, a correction node has an input syndrome neighbor by zero-respecting local computability; a node marked by the coboundary of that correction is at most one additional hop away. The connecting maps have only the X→Q→Z directions. A degree-Δ_l ball of radius2 has at most1+Δ_l² vertices. Hence the number of syndrome-bearing regions in K is at least

    (m-C_low)/(1+Δ_l²) >= m/A.                      (5)

Each region's nonempty syndrome sum at effective inverse temperature b=βκ is at most

    W(b)=(1+exp[-b/4])^M-1.                         (6)

Different local cochain blocks have disjoint syndrome coordinates. Summing over all syndrome-bearing-region sets costs at most2^m. Dropping compatibility constraints only enlarges the sum. Combining the history-weight inequality, (3), (5), and the graph count gives, for each CSS bad event,

    Pr[bad sector] <=ν Σ_(m>=Q_k) ρ(b)^m,
    ρ(b)=2(Δ+1)² W(b)^(1/A).                        (7)

For ρ<1, the joint projector bound is therefore

    p1:=Tr[(I-P1)ρ_β] <=2ν ρ^Q_k/(1-ρ).             (8)

The prefactor ν, missing from the displayed final sum in the source, is retained. Nothing assumes X/Z Gibbs independence.

A sufficient explicit threshold for ρ≤1/2 is

    βκ >= b0=4 log(2M)+4A log[4(Δ+1)²].              (9)

Proof: let r=[4(Δ+1)²]^(-A). Then u=exp[-b/4]≤r/(2M), so Mu≤1/2; (1+u)^M-1≤exp(Mu)-1≤2Mu≤r. Substitution into (7) gives ρ≤1/2. The proof uses log(1+u)≤u. The printed source inequality with an extra factor log2 has the wrong direction and is not used.

## Nonuniform penalties and explicit diagonal interactions

Suppose

    H=Σ_a J_a D_a + Σ_T V_T,
    J_a>=J_min>0,
    V_T belongs to the algebra generated by {C_a:a∈T}.

Each V_T is scalar on a realizable syndrome sector, with absolute value≤||V_T||. If τ∩T is empty it does not change under erasure. Otherwise its energy change is bounded below by-2||V_T||. Thus (2) holds with

    g_check=sup_a Σ_(T containing a) ||V_T||,
    κ=J_min-2g_check >0.                            (11)

This does not require an upper bound on J_a for the one-site bath theorem below. Such a bound is useful for finite-support bath erosion and a physical spectral-rate envelope. Finite range is not needed for (11) itself; it supplies a fixed g_check and actual physical locality.

Example: arbitrary check penalties in[J_min,J_max], and arbitrary-sign pair interactions K_ab C_a C_b on a finite-range check graph of degree z. If |K_ab|≤ε, then κ≥J_min-2zε. Taking ε≤J_min/(4z) gives κ≥J_min/2, so β≥2b0/J_min suffices. Edges may couple an X check to a Z check. The coefficients can vary with location and depth subject to the uniform bounds.

The factor2 is sufficient rather than sharp. Explicit scalar functions permit a one-sided erasure influence: if their coordinate-wise increase in removing one defect is at mostω_(T,a), evaluated on their declared Boolean domain, then κ≥inf_a[J_a-Σ_T ω_(T,a)]. Monotone nondecreasing functions of defect bits haveω=0 and need not be weak. For -εD_aD_b, ω_(T,a)≤ε, so the stronger κ≥J_min-zε holds. This optional test needs an actual declared scalar function; an operator norm on realizable sectors is not automatically a bound on arbitrary unrealizable bit assignments.

More generally one can admit nonuniform penalties with soft checks whenever every **actual legal witness** has total erased penalty at leastκ0|τ| and the adverse interaction loss is smaller than that margin. Uniform J_min is a transparent sufficient control, not a necessary per-check hypothesis of the erasure interface.

## Arbitrary bounded-range terms commuting with every original check

This stronger application uses the cycle13 local-generation result. Let V=Σ_X V_X be Hermitian, with Hermitian local terms V_X, each nonempty support X contained in a physical ball centered at a chosen q_X∈X of radius r_v, each |X|<d_(2k), and

    [V,C_a]=0 for EVERY original check,
    J=sup_q Σ_(X containing q) ||V_X||.              (12)

It is enough that the total V has this commutation property. Individual input terms need not. Stabilizer-twirl each term:

    E_S(V_X)=|S|^(-1) Σ_(g∈S) g V_X g†.

This map preserves physical support, contracts operator norm, and Σ_X E_S(V_X)=V. Every Pauli in E_S(V_X)'s expansion centralizes S and has weight<d, hence is a stabilizer or scalar. Cycle13 filling places every such stabilizer in the algebra of original checks whose **whole supports** lie inside B(q_X,R), R=C′max(r_v,1). All these Paulis belong to one common local check algebra, so E_S(V_X) is scalar on each syndrome sector and changes by at most2||V_X|| if an eraser affects that algebra. No Pauli coefficient-l1 factor is used.

For each nonidentity check a, fix a reference qubit q_a in its support. If a is in this algebra, q_X is within R of q_a, because the whole check support is inside B(q_X,R). Assigning each term to q_X and using (12) yields congestion≤B_R J, where a safe bound on physical qubits in a ball of radius R is

    B_R=4t(2ceil(R)+3)^3.                           (13)

Every vertex belongs to a fine face, each face has four vertices, and every unit cube is inside a unit ball; a cube cover gives (13), counting coincident qubits by their abstract multiplicity.

Consequently (11) holds with g_check=B_RJ and

    κ=J_min-2B_RJ.                                 (14)

For J≤J_min/(4B_R), κ≥J_min/2. This is an explicit nonzero intensive commuting-perturbation interval independent of depth. The constants are extremely small: C′≈2.078e37 and the density are paid in B_R. No experimentally relevant tolerance is inferred.

Commutation merely with H0=ΣD_a is insufficient. It allows degenerate syndrome hopping. The separate actual-bath note gives a local interior-plaquette example. Global check commutation is load-bearing in the twirl/local-algebra argument. Also |X|<d excludes logical terms; the condition is eventually automatic for fixed physical range under the admitted density and growing distance.

## Bath strength, resolved jumps, and the actual memory bound

Let F_α be Hermitian raw system-bath couplings, ||F_α||≤1, initially single-qubit operators (arbitrary single-qubit operators are in the linear span of the protected Pauli errors). Write

    F_(α,ω)=Σ_(ε-ε′=ω) Π_(ε′) F_α Π_ε,
    L(ρ)=Σ_(α,ω) h_α(ω)[F_(α,ω)ρF_(α,ω)†
                       -{F_(α,ω)†F_(α,ω),ρ}/2].

Require h_α(ω)≥0, h_α(ω)≤γ_max, and L(ρ_β)=0. Independent-bath KMS rates h_α(-ω)=exp[-βω]h_α(ω) are one way to ensure stationarity. The default Schrödinger Hamiltonian is (1), with no additional Lamb shift. A coherent addition K_LS may be included if it commutes with H, the dressed pair and the protected projector: then every encoded state still commutes with H+K_LS, and the same Duhamel proof applies with the old Gibbs state of H. Pauli-separated Davies channels have syndrome-diagonal Lamb shifts meeting these conditions. An arbitrary omitted Lamb shift is not silently covered.

Since Π_ε, H, P1 and the dressed pair commute, protection from raw F_α implies protection from every resolved F_(α,ω). This uses the exact actual Hamiltonian, including its changed energies. It does not require that the perturbed Davies generator be close to the old one.

Chesi Proposition1 already proves the following dimension-free rate bound:

    Σ_ω F_(α,ω)†F_(α,ω)=pinch_H(F_α†F_α)≤I,
    ||L||_(1,Hermitian)≤2K γ_max,                  (15)

where K is the number of raw coupling channels. Positivity of the weighted jump map bounds its trace norm; the anticommutator is bounded by the same positive sum. Counting frequency-resolved operator norms separately is unnecessary and can be grossly wrong.

Let U_dec define the syndrome-correction tensor factorization. The maps are

    Enc_H(τ)=U_dec[τ⊗γ_syn,H]U_dec†,
    Dec_H(ρ)=Tr_syn[U_dec†ρU_dec],
    γ_syn,H=exp[-βE]/Tr(exp[-βE]).                  (16)

They are CPTP and Dec_H Enc_H=id. The thermal ancillary state is the **new joint Gibbs state**. Its efficient preparation is not supplied by this proof.

Chesi Theorem1, now legally applicable to (1), (8), (15), gives for every logical density matrix τ and t≥0

    ||Φ_t Enc_H(τ)-Enc_H(τ)||_1
      <=32Kγ_max ν t ρ^Q_k/(1-ρ).                  (17)

The same bound holds after Dec_H by trace-norm contraction. For ρ≤1/2,

    error≤64Kγ_max ν t 2^(-Q_k),
    t_ε >= ε 2^Q_k/(64Kγ_max ν).                  (18)

For three independent Pauli channels per qubit, K=3N and the denominator is192γ_max Nν. Equations (10),(18) are an actual stretched-exponential thermal storage guarantee with a defined bath time scale, rather than an assertion about a ground band.

This class includes a nontrivial fully noisy thermal generator: take all3N raw Pauli couplings and h(ω)=Γ/[1+exp(-βω)] in the energy-loss convention above. These rates are strictly positive, obey KMS, and are bounded byΓ. Thus the example is a Gibbs-stationary Davies-form Markov bath, not a zero-noise memory. Constructing a physical bath with this spectrum is not an acquisition claim.

## Fixed finite-support raw bath couplings

If Ja≤J_max and g_check is finite, every one-qubit Pauli changes at most7 check bits, so

    |E(σ+syndrome(F))-E(σ)|≤g_flip:=7(J_max+2g_check).

For a weight-w Pauli use its actual Pauli prefixes, all of which give realizable syndromes. Thus the bound is w g_flip. This route does not evaluate energies on invalid Boolean intermediates.

Let F range over all Pauli prefixes of weight≤q_b-1, and define

    P_q=intersection_(F) F†P1F,
    M_q=Σ_(w=0)^(q_b-1) 3^w binomial(N,w).

All these projectors commute because they are syndrome-diagonal. A weight≤q_b Pauli applied from P_q factors into protected one-qubit steps, so it preserves both dressed logical observables. By linearity every raw operator supported on at mostq_b qubits is protected. Gibbs translation gives

    Tr[(I-P_q)ρ_β]≤M_q exp[βg_flip(q_b-1)]p1.       (19)

Replacing P1 by P_q in (17) multiplies its right-hand side by the explicit factor in (19). For fixed q_b and K=O(N), this is a polynomial-in-N prefactor and does not change the stretched-exponential conclusion. Fixed physical-range bath couplings have fixed q_b under admitted density. A supplied smaller prefix family for the actual bath supports can reduce the prefactor, but must be specified and counted.

There is also an explicit growing-support range. Since M_q≤(1+3N)^(q_b-1), if

    (q_b-1)[log(1+3N)+βg_flip] ≤(log2)Q_k/2,

then (18)'s dynamical error is at most64Kγ_maxνt2^(-Q_k/2). This admits q_b of orderQ_k/[log N+βg_flip] while retaining a stretched-exponential time for polynomialK. It is a support-cardinality theorem with supplied raw coupling operators, not an efficient acquisition of their dense matrices. Fixed finite-range baths are its immediate physical special case.

This finite-support extension was independently reconstructed by pde_gate10 and pde_spectrum9 at the named actual decoder/witness premises. It does not follow merely from code distance or from saying resolved jumps are local.

## Prior, failure cases, costs, and next falsifier

BDL provides the 3D model, decoder, syndrome reduction, and unweighted witness method. Chesi already admits arbitrary Hamiltonians commuting with the protected logical pair, uses the thermal factor encoding, and proves (15). Alicki et al0811.0033 is the earlier4D dressed-observable/Peierls precedent. Chesi–Röthlisberger–Loss0908.4264 studies interacting stabilizer/anyon energies in a different2D long-range setting. Accordingly the eraser comparison is an application of an established method to this new model, not a new general thermodynamic criterion. Priority of the precise commuting-neighborhood application is UNKNOWN.

The mathematical costs are explicit but not practical: the level2 minimum-weight lookup is finite only because ell is fixed; an exhaustive table can be exponential in N2=O(ell^4). Stabilizer twirling need not enumerate the whole group for a supplied q-body term—one can expand at most4^q Paulis and test commutation—but acquiring the filling representation may still require local algebra on an astronomically large fixed ball. Thermal state preparation and the finite-bit geometric embedding are not acquired. The scalar ledger does not simulate the recursive code or thermal dynamics.

If κ vanishes, the proof fails. At H=0 on the same code, a local Gibbs-stationary depolarizing bath mixes every encoded state toward the maximally mixed state in O(log N) time, demonstrating why arbitrary commuting energies cannot be admitted. If γ_max or K grows exponentially with Q_k, the time conclusion also fails; bath rates are part of the interface. Noncommuting perturbations remove the exact syndrome law and remain outside this theorem. Ground-band stability does not repair that loss.

The decisive next falsifier is either an actual legal witness eraser violating (2) in an admitted proposed Hamiltonian, or an actual coarse-difference/cluster-locality failure in the repaired source decoder. Explicit diagonal interaction lists make the former locally testable; the minimum-weight base decoder and the exceptional-level charge isolate the latter rather than concealing it in a spectral argument.
