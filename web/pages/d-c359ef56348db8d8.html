The strongest new scoped result in this programme is a counting obstruction for low-entropy libraries of precompiled graph-multiplication kernels: it extends the existing coordinate-alias obstruction to repeated kernels and q-sparse row/column mixing, while granting arbitrary output decoding for free. MAIN RESULT: A SPARSE-ALIAS LIBRARY CANNOT HANDLE ALL MASKS
==========================================================
Fix 0<η<1/8 and 0<δ<η/2. Let N grow through fourth powers, D=N^(1/4), and m=floor(N^(15/8)). The target inputs are independent variables X∈F^(N×D), Y∈F^(D×N), and a supplied size-m mask W⊂[N]×[N]. For each (i,j)∈W, the required exact polynomial is

    p_ij(X,Y)=sum_(k=1)^D X_ik Y_kj.

Consider the following explicit strategy model over any field F.

- Before W is known, a library has K_N≤N^a fixed division-free graph-multiplication kernels, with a fixed a. Kernel ℓ computes every output on a fixed bipartite graph H_ℓ⊂[U_ℓ]×[V_ℓ], with h_ℓ edges and no isolated vertices.
- After W is supplied, the strategy may evaluate an ordered sequence of library kernels. Kernel ℓ's inputs may be specialized by

      A_uk=sum_(i∈S_u) a_ui X_ik,       |S_u|≤q_N,
      B_kv=sum_(j∈T_v) b_vj Y_kj,       |T_v|≤q_N,

  where each support and coefficient vector is shared across all k. These may depend on W. The kernel's circuit is not rewritten after substitution.
- Every chosen kernel is evaluated in full and is charged at least

      c0 max{h_ℓ, U_ℓD, V_ℓD}

  arithmetic operations, for a fixed c0>0. The output decoder may take arbitrary linear combinations of every chosen kernel output and is granted free.

**Theorem.** If q_N≤N^(η/2−δ) and the total source-kernel arithmetic cost is at most B_N=C N^(2−η), then for all sufficiently large N at least one legal size-m mask is not computable in this model. The result holds for every polynomial-size library. It is not a lower bound against general arithmetic circuits.

**Proof.** Write each source output after specialization:

    sum_k A_uk B_kv
      = sum_(i∈S_u, j∈T_v) a_ui b_vj p_ij.

Thus all source outputs lie in the linear span of p_ij with (i,j) in

    E = union over invoked edges (u,v) of S_u×T_v.

Distinct p_ij are linearly independent because monomial X_i1Y_1j occurs in p_ij and no other p_i'j'. Therefore a successful decoder requires W⊆E, and |E|≤q_N² sum_ℓ h_ℓ≤q_N² B_N/c0.

The number of kernel invocations is at most B_N/(c0D); the total number of source vertices U_ℓ+V_ℓ is at most 2B_N/(c0D). For q≤N/2, the number of supports of size at most q is A_q(N)=sum_(r=0)^q binom(N,r), with log A_q(N)=O(q log N). The number of ordered kernel sequences and support assignments is at most

    (L+1) K_N^L A_q(N)^(2B_N/(c0D)),   L=floor(B_N/(c0D)).

Its logarithm is O(N^(7/4−η) q_N log N)=O(N^(7/4−η/2−δ)log N)=o(m log N). For each fixed configuration, at most binom(q_N²B_N/c0,m) masks are contained in its envelope. Since q_N²B_N/c0=O(N^(2−2δ)),

    log [ binom(N²,m) / binom(q_N²B_N/c0,m) ]
       ≥ m log( N² c0/(q_N²B_N) )
       ≥ m(2δ log N−O(1)).

This mask-count gap dominates the configuration count. So the library configurations cover fewer than the binom(N²,m) legal masks. ∎

The argument permits arbitrary coefficients, repeated use of kernels, shared arithmetic inside each source circuit, and arbitrary linear output decoding. It even grants mask-dependent support selection and does not charge map construction. For an implementation, add the cost to store/build the maps and decode outputs. The condition that each alias's support and coefficients are shared over the middle coordinate k is load-bearing; k-dependent supports and dense row/column mixing are outside this theorem. A mask-dependent circuit rewrite is also outside it, and its preprocessing/acquisition must be charged.

A fully charged exact bit algorithm also needs an entry-height promise. If inputs have b-bit integers, an explicit size-m edge list costs Θ(m log N) bits, and there are inputs for which each output has Θ(2b+log D) bits. Reading dense X,Y costs Ω(NDb) bits, and writing outputs costs Ω(m(2b+log D)). For b=O(log N), these representation costs fit below N² for every fixed η<1/8; they do not provide the missing arithmetic circuit.

WHAT THE OTHER ROUTES ESTABLISHED
=================================
* **Selective outputs.** L04's Ω(N²) theorem covers independently charged complete-block covers only. N12's 1445/744 exponent is asymptotic rank for each fixed finite (N,W), with tensor-power length tending to infinity. It does not produce a one-shot, uniformly acquired circuit for a changing arbitrary mask. A supplied shared-leg/9/4 exponent is arithmetic information, not a finite decomposition, bit algorithm, or selected-output method. The ten-term complete-array certificate fails its target parameter range, but this is not a general impossibility. Full source audit and exact scope are in [leaf15/RESULT.txt](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf15/RESULT.txt).
* **Conditional useful cases.** Dyadic row/column classes give an exact algorithmic accounting formula that charges shared input forms, pruned products, selected-coordinate decoding, and circuit acquisition. Direct dot products cost O(mD), and therefore are subquadratic for m=O(N^(7/4−η)); this does not reach m=N^(15/8). For a structured complete rectangle, the exact 9/4 square multiplier yields N^(31/16+o(1)) operations. Both claims retain acquisition and coefficient-height gates. See [leaf15](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf15/RESULT.txt) and [leaf09/PROOF.txt](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf09/PROOF.txt).
* **Rank versus border rank and bits.** The 2×2×2 W tensor has exact rank 3 but border rank 2. Its degeneration becomes an exact bounded-integer bit method if enough precision is used before rounding; the growing operands remove any bit advantage in this toy instance. For a *supplied* Laurent degeneration with r rank-one terms, exponent support in [−d,d], exact limit G(t)=T+O(t), and fixed coefficient extension degree k, leaf03 proves rank_F(T^⊗s)≤k³ r^s(2sd+1)² by coefficient extraction and scalar descent. This is tensor-power exactification, not an acquired rank-r circuit for T or a selective-product algorithm. Status and costs are in [leaf03/REPORT.md](leaf03/REPORT.md).
* **All fields.** The 9/4 arithmetic matrix-multiplication exponent's transfer to every fixed field is a conditional manual corollary of the primary complex-field theorem plus the source's finite-field separator/interpolation and characteristic-2 repairs. It does not transfer a characteristic-zero decomposition by reducing coefficients modulo p. Separately, Schönhage's theorem gives ω(F)=ω(F0) for F's prime field F0, so the exponent is constant on each characteristic; it does not identify exponents across different characteristics. A fixed multiplication tensor may have different ranks after field extension (F_(p²) over F_p has rank 3, versus 2 after scalar extension). See [leaf04](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf04/all_field_nine_fourths_audit.md) and [leaf11/RESULT.txt](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf11/RESULT.txt).
* **Noncommutative PIT.** The same-path ABP lift to a commutative polynomial fails on xy−yx: abelianization vanishes while the free polynomial is nonzero. A determinant gadget that detects commutative rank can still have a full 2×2 blow-up witness for every p, so that witness does not distinguish zero from nonzero p. A positive legal route remains for fixed-order, one-variable-per-layer ROABPs via the Raz–Shpilka NC-ABP PIT algorithm. Current primary manuscripts separately state polynomial-bit one-query hitting for size-bounded division-free formulas over characteristic zero and supplied positive characteristic; they do not cover shared circuits or inverse gates. The N68 one-query acquisition theorem and N51 rational-formula list also have separate interfaces. See [leaf05/RESULT.txt](leaf05/RESULT.txt), [leaf12/REPORT.md](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf12/REPORT.md), [leaf17/RESULT.txt](leaf17/RESULT.txt), and [leaf18/REPORT.md](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf18/REPORT.md).
* **Integral hitting explicitness.** Reconstructing the archived N51/R06 construction gives a conservative polynomial upper bound of O(M^503 log^3 M) bit time, O(M^45 log M) workspace, and O(M^492 log M) dense output bits, M=n+B+1. At n=B=1 its large branch emits about 4.91×10^62 tuples. This is impractical but not an asymptotic non-polynomial barrier; the full generator was not executed and coefficient-height details remain gated. Exact eigenvalue adversaries also give list-cardinality lower bounds, already present in an archived audit. See [leaf07](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf07/N51_TOTAL_DIMENSION_OBSTRUCTION.md) and [leaf17/RESULT.txt](leaf17/RESULT.txt).
* **Determinants.** The smooth Fermat form Σ_i x_i^r has an explicit affine determinant of order d(r−1)+3. Thus degree, number of variables, smoothness, singular-locus data, and Hessian rank alone cannot push a universal bound past O(rd). Since affine restrictions of Perm_m have degree ≤m and at most m² essential variables, that method reaches at most cubic scale; it does not refute a cubic lower bound. The posted Family108 Lean theorem files in the pinned snapshot end in `sorry`, so they do not provide kernel certification there. See [leaf13/RESULT.txt](leaf13/RESULT.txt).
* **Profile barrier.** The five reduced profile axioms for the 9/4 argument admit a pointwise-minimal explicit profile with maximal feasible t=3/4. Thus those numerical constraints alone cannot yield a strict improvement; no full tensor character or ω lower bound is built. See [leaf16/RESULT.txt](../../work/programmes/algebra_complexity/leaf16/RESULT.txt).
* **Changed frontier.** The candidate N129 claims a torsion-free hyperbolic G with a nontrivial idempotent in F2[G]. Its identities ab=1, ac=0, c≠0 imply e=1−ba is a nonzero idempotent. Bartels–Lück–Reich’s cited Formanek criterion applies to torsion-free word-hyperbolic groups and forbids nontrivial idempotents over any field. Therefore N129's proposed existence conclusion is REFUTED; at least one graph, probability, disk-extraction, or algebra premise fails, but the exact failure has not yet been isolated.
