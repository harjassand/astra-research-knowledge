# Direct DAG disjunction extraction without tree normalization

Status: universal construction and cost derivation independently reconstructed and accepted; five bounded whole proofs independently data-only replayed. The executable prototype supports FL_e/ILL primitives plus explicitly declared W/C, not arbitrary structural-schema matching. This is a repair and quantitative sharpening of an existing result, not a new disjunction-property priority claim. See logic_gate14/cycle18/DAG_FDP_REPAIR_AUDIT.md for the separate mathematical and executable gates.

## Question and interface

Fix a finite structural calculus G in the precise sense of Tabatabai, arXiv:2609.00422v1, Definition 2/4 (pp.8–10), with theorem logic contained in classical LK_!. Given a **closed DAG G-proof** pi of `=> A or B`, measured by L total formula occurrences across its sequents, can a source proof of one disjunct be constructed with polynomially many formula occurrences **without first replacing pi by a tree proof**?

Input is an actual finite encoded proof with explicit premise slots and formulas, not an oracle for provability. Rule soundness in LK_! is an assumption about the fixed calculus. The generated proof is also a DAG. Reusing an already proved closed sequent as the premise of different inferences is permitted in this interface and is charged once when acquired. No general source contraction rule is added.

## Construction

Give every needed formula F in the subformula closure of pi a shared fresh name `<F>`. Names are injective identifiers outside the entire original atom set. Their number is charged by the input formula representation, not bounded by L alone. Define t exactly as the source's Definition 9: atoms become their names, every constant c becomes `c & <c>`, and every connective result gains an additive conjunction with its own name. Standard substitution s replaces `<F>` by F.

Process **distinct source rows once** in their given topological order. Add the following local sequents to a global set S:

| Row rule | Added local assumptions |
|---|---|
| `=>1` | `=> <1>` |
| right additive top | `=> <top>` |
| R* introducing F*H | `<F>,<H> => <F*H>` |
| R& introducing F&H | `<F> & <H> => <F&H>` |
| R-or introducing F or H | the selected child name `=>` the result name |
| R-imp, Gamma => F->H | the multiset of names of Gamma `=> <F->H>` |
| promotion, !Gamma => !F | names of !Gamma `=> <!F>`, and `< !gamma> => !< !gamma>` for each antecedent |
| other base, cut, bang-left/weak/contraction, structural rows | none |

For every source row, the source's local translation argument yields its t-image from S in LK_!. This is an induction on a DAG topological order: previous translated rows may be reused as DAG rows. The structural cases work because t is a uniform substitution into the atomic structural schema and every such fixed rule is LK_!-derivable (Corollary 7(ii)). Cut uses exactly the same translated cut formula on both premise slots. No resource assertion about a source tree is required.

Let T be just the Horn subset of S. In Boolean semantics interpret bang as identity. The extra modal assumptions are then identities. Consequently every Boolean valuation satisfying T validates `<A> or <B>`. This follows from the root's translated sequent and does not require generating a large explicit classical proof.

Now acquire a closed source proof of `I(h^s)` for every h in T, where `I(Gamma=>C) = product(Gamma)->C`, with empty product 1:

* Unit and top cases use fixed closed proofs of `1->1` and `1->top`.
* R* and R& cases use identity for the exact packed body/result.
* R-or uses its fixed introduction proof, then implication introduction.
* For R-imp and promotion, start from the **actual original source conclusion row**. Fold its antecedent multiset by L* and use R-imp to close it. With empty antecedent, first use L1 to introduce 1, then R-imp. Append each closure to the original DAG once; do not copy its derivation tree.

The source's full Theorem 13 certificate can also include the modal extras, each using the fixed closed proof `!F -> !!F`, and combine all closed implications by R&. For disjunction extraction those extra certificates and the conjunction are unnecessary.

Run ordinary positive Horn least-model forward chaining on T. A clause fires when every atom in its antecedent conjunctions has been derived; occurrences of 1 require no atom. Choose each head's first provider and derive each head once. The least model must include `<A>` or `<B>`, by the Boolean entailment above.

Build the corresponding **closed source proof** while chaining. Earlier head atoms already have closed proofs after s. Construct each antecedent additive conjunction by closed R&, then fuse the separate antecedent formulas by closed R*. Empty antecedent uses a cached `=>1`. Given closed `=>P` and cached closed `=> P->C`, append:

1. identity `C=>C`;
2. L-imp from `=>P` and `C=>C`, obtaining `P->C => C`;
3. cut with `=>P->C`, obtaining `=>C`.

These three rows charge exactly 5 formula occurrences. They use no antecedent contraction: all reused source objects are **closed DAG proofs**. When the head `<A>` or `<B>` is reached, its source proof proves exactly A or B.

## Cost derivation

For a valid closed proof of a calculus contained in LK_!, every row has at least one formula (the empty sequent is classically invalid), so its number N of rows is at most L.

There is at most one Horn addition per source row. The combined antecedent atomic incidences and conjunction nodes are O(L): variable-width clauses occur only in R-imp or promotion rows, whose context widths are already charged in L. Modal extras have O(L) total incidences as well. This union bound is over distinct DAG rows, not paths in an unfolded tree.

Closing a row with k>0 antecedent formulas adds exactly k(k+1)/2 formula occurrences: L* gives widths k,k-1,...,2 and the final R-imp row has one formula. For k=0, L1 plus R-imp adds 3. The sum of k squared over the selected distinct original rows is at most L squared. Fixed non-context Horn proofs cost at most 5 formula occurrences each. Closed forward-chaining bodies add at most their total incidence and conjunction count; head elimination costs 5 per fired clause.

A safe explicit bound, including the original proof and an extra unit row, is

`l(tau) <= L^2 + 20 L + 1`.

This constant is for the displayed base logical templates. It is independent of the widths and symbol sizes of source formulas; added structural rules are inherited in the original proof rather than reacquired at each Horn firing.

Let S_enc be the total encoded input length, including formula trees, atom names and premise pointers. Formula interning and fresh identifiers are polynomial-time. Closing contexts repeats each original formula only O(L) times, and conjunction/forward-chaining outputs have total symbol size polynomial in S_enc. A straightforward implementation has polynomial time and output length (a coarse bound O(S_enc^3) allows explicit tree printing and pointer encoding). No assertion of polynomial runtime in L alone is possible when formulas themselves have arbitrarily many symbols.

## Consequences and limits

This supplies the same source disjunction conclusion as the recent Theorem 15 through a direct DAG construction, avoiding the contested tree line-equivalence step. Fixed Frege presentations can inherit the result only with actual fixed rule simulations to/from the DAG sequent calculus; theorem-set equality or merely admissible rules is insufficient. This note does not silently include arbitrary admissible rule additions, raw EF aliases, tree proofs, proof search without a supplied proof, or a new hard-family lower bound.

The Horn mechanism is old, and the recent paper explicitly asserts the disjunction theorem. Buss–Mints (1999), author PDF printed 5 February 1999, §2 pp.3–6, already closes a supplied intuitionistic proof under cuts against closed conclusions, then obtains a disjunct through Horn-style immediate derivability (Lemma 1, Theorem 2, Corollary 3). Thus **polynomial-time extraction itself is established prior**, rather than an innovation of this note. The present calculus has resource-sensitive multisets, fusion and exponentials, and the direct DAG construction keeps those interfaces explicit. Novelty is **UNKNOWN / a technical repair and exact quantitative construction**, not a new structural separation. The scientific value is isolating an unnecessary resource assumption and an exact algorithm that can be checked without exponential tree expansion.

Jeřábek's author PDF *Proof complexity of the cut-free calculus of structures*, printed 30 April 2008, Definition 2.5 / Remark 2.6 (p.6), explicitly distinguishes ordinary proof trees from DAG representations that replace repeated subproof occurrences by references, and warns that the classical tree/DAG equivalence need not hold for subsystems. This is strong prior for the convention boundary, not an exact prior unit-family lower-bound theorem.

Four producer attacks and four independent attacks reject altered premises, free inputs, undeclared contraction and illegal promotion.
