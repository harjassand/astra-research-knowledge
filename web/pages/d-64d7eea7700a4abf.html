1. INTERFACE AND ENDPOINTS
Let B_k(p)=C(N,k)p^k(1-p)^(N-k). Phase averaging
(sqrt(1-p)|0>+e^(i theta)sqrt(p)|1>)^tensor N has populations B_k(p).
Thus integral_0^1 B_k(p) P(p) dp=w_k with P>=0 is sufficient. Its total mass
is sum_k w_k since sum_k B_k=1. This is the UNNORMALIZED interface.
Necessity for symmetric-support states follows because each positive product
summand must lie in that support, and a product vector fixed by all swaps has
identical one-site rays. The relevant homogeneous coefficients are
q_k=w_k/C(N,k), never ordinary p-moments q_k.

For comparison, the generalized Stieltjes matrices are
H0=(q_i+j)_{0<=i,j<=floor(N/2)},
H1=(q_i+j+1)_{0<=i,j<=floor((N-1)/2)}.
Their PSD criterion is a CLOSED moment cone with endpoint p=1 allowed to
contribute only to q_N after logit weighting. Equivalently nonnegative
polynomials on [0,infinity) are sums of squares plus x times sums of squares
with the corresponding degree caps, and separation from the compact Bernstein
curve gives a measure on [0,1]. PSD must concern all quadratic forms, not just
leading principal minors at a singular endpoint. This density construction
bypasses that extension issue: P extends continuously to both endpoints.
The same finite moment criterion appears in Rutkowski et al., Theorems 5 and 8:
https://arxiv.org/html/1810.05522 . Its loose use of "positive definite" in some
PPT prose is not relied upon for the singular PSD statement.
