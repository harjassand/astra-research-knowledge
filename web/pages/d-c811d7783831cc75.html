1. EXACT CONDITIONAL CLAIM AND ENCODING

Keep precisely the multispecies theorem audited in 06_multispecies_exact_audit:
fixed K,d_p,alpha_p and public bounds, N_p/N>=lambda0>0, and the fixed
radius |delta|<=delta_star of its M13. Inputs are supplied known rational
coefficients/matrices, or fixed algebraic local matrices with charged
polynomial-time precision acquisition. No unknown parameter is learned.
General arbitrary exact-real inputs instead require their own precision
oracle; their running time is not silently included in a finite-bit bound.

Let Dphys=product_p d_p^N_p, q_p=d_p^2, and
 A= tensor_p End((C^d_p)^tensor N_p)^(S_(N_p)),
 Dalg=product_p binom(N_p+q_p-1,q_p-1).
Here Dalg is polynomial in N for fixed K,d_p, while Dphys is exponential.
For fixed rational 0<zeta_p<1/d_p, put
 C_zeta=conv{tensor_p sigma_p^tensor N_p:
                    Tr sigma_p=1, sigma_p>=zeta_p I}.

Conditional on the generic proof lemmas, one obtains for rational
Hermitian trace-one R in A with ||R||<=2 a rational product-species mixture
S, using at most Dalg nodes and dyadic/rational nonnegative weights, with
 T(R,S)<=distance_T(R,C_zeta)+u,
in time polynomial in N, input/table/cutoff bits L, and log(1/u), for
fixed K,d_p. This is the product-species iid cone, not the full separable
cone. The subsequent correction produces a sampler for general sitewise
product states; it is not an exact species-iid compiler.

For the admitted Gibbs family the reductions below then produce, for any
rational 0<epsilon<1, a bounded finite-bit classical sampler whose output
is a list of N rational one-site densities and whose expected product
state is within half trace distance epsilon of the WHOLE Gibbs density.
The conditional time/output bound is poly_(K,d)(N,L,log(1/epsilon)).
Public tiny cutoffs, certificate-radius bits, fixed-dimension exponents,
N emissions and local preparation errors are charged. This is an abstract
Turing-algorithm claim, not practical runtime or hardware evidence.

2. PRODUCT COMMUTANT: ACQUIRED TABLES, PSD, AND PHYSICAL TRACE

For each species use matrix-unit orbit sums B_(p,m_p) over N_p sites.
The basis B_m=tensor_p B_(p,m_p) has Dalg elements. Multiplication constants
are products of the local three-index-table constants; adjoints transpose
each species' pair table; Gram diagonals are
g_m=product_p [N_p!/product_ab m_(p,ab)!]>=1.
The trace of B_m is the product of local orbit traces, zero unless every
pair table is diagonal. Identity and collective local matrix-unit actions
are likewise explicit tensors of the local formulas.

The entire table can be built from product species tables. The local
three-index-table counts are binom(N_p+d_p^3-1,d_p^3-1); their product
and even Dalg^3 dense storage are polynomial for fixed K,d_p. Integer
structure coefficients have O(N log dmax) bits and factorial intermediates
O(N log N) bits. No ordered physical words, Clebsch-Gordan coefficients,
Schur unitary or species multiplicity oracle is required.

For X Hermitian in A, its Dalg-dimensional left multiplication L_X and
Gram matrix G obey y*G L_X y=Tr_phys(Y*XY). Thus
 X>=0 physically iff G L_X>=0.
The forward direction is direct. In the reverse direction a negative
spectral projector is a polynomial in X and belongs to A, furnishing a
negative test vector. Moreover X>=cI implies G L_X>=cG>=cI.
Realification gives rational PSD LMIs of size 2Dalg. These exact formulas
extend to the tensor algebra without a new operator-positivity oracle.
The trace objective is always Tr_phys from the orbit functional, NEVER
Tr L_X, which would use the wrong representation multiplicities.

3. FIXED STATE VARIABLES AND THE GENERIC ORACLE ADAPTATION

Parametrize every tau_p by d_p^2-1 real trace-one Hermitian coordinates
and set sigma_p=zeta_p I+(1-d_p zeta_p)tau_p. There are
P=sum_p(d_p^2-1) variables, FIXED in the admitted interface. Every tau_p
is constrained by its 2^d_p-1 principal minors and coordinate bounds.
The total number of constraints is fixed, and these positivity degrees
are fixed. No tensor-product density positivity is an additional
quantified constraint; each local tau_p is PSD.

For a supplied rational W in A, the objective
 F_W(tau_1,...,tau_K)=Tr_phys[W tensor_p sigma_p^tensor N_p]
is a rational real polynomial of total degree <=N in these P variables.
Its expansion has at most binom(N+P,P) monomials, polynomial for fixed P.
Coefficient bits remain polynomial in N and the supplied rational data.
Thus the generic fixed-variable existential decision import applies to
the PRODUCT of local PSD domains, with no growing variable block.

Its rational witness acquisition also adapts explicitly. Bisect one
joint feasible box at a near-optimal level; obtain tau_p* simultaneously
in the final box. Choose widths t/(80 dmax^2 N), delta=t/(20 dmax N),
form local rational midpoint matrices Q_p and
tau_hat_p=(Q_p+2delta I)/(1+2d_p delta).
The row-sum estimate gives ||Q_p-tau_p*||<=delta, and these tau_hat_p are
exactly normalized PSD. Set sigma_hat_p=zeta_p I+(1-d_p zeta_p)tau_hat_p.
Every floor is preserved exactly. Tensor telescoping gives
 ||tensor_p sigma_hat_p^N_p-tensor_p sigma_p*^N_p||_1
 <=sum_p N_p ||sigma_hat_p-sigma_p*||_1<=5dmax N delta.
For ||W||<=1/2 the objective loss is <=t/8, as in the generic proof.
The number of oracle box calls and all witness bits are logarithmic in
N/t, with P fixed, not an inverse-t net enumeration.

An emitted rational product node gives the exact valid atom cut
Tr_phys(W' tensor_p sigma_hat_p^N_p)-a'<=0. Common op-norm LMIs and scalar
boxes are unchanged. In a rational orthogonal Hermitian product basis,
the basis op norms are at most 2^K dmax^(2N); coordinate radii and
objective bounds therefore have O_(K,d)(N+log Dalg) logarithms.
The center (0,3/4), inner-radius proof, objective threshold mixing and
failed-volume proof in the generic algorithm use no special property
of a single local species. They survive every finite retained product
atom set. THIS is the point where the generic dual-recovery condition
is inherited; it is not replaced by knowing a near-optimal dual point.

The finite physical trace-norm SDP uses the same PSD tests and physical
trace. A uniform atom average and ||R||<=2 imply ||Delta0||<=3, so
Q0=4I-Delta0/2, P0=4I+Delta0/2 are >=2I. The simplex/box inner-radius
construction remains rational and explicit. Conditional finite-primal
recovery yields positive rational weights. Trace-one affine support
elimination leaves at most Dalg product nodes. It never rounds local
matrices in a way that could lose their zeta_p floors.

4. RATIONAL WHOLE-GIBBS TARGET AT EXPONENTIALLY SMALL ERROR

H_N belongs to A because the actual interface is homogeneous within
every fixed species pair. Collective one-site actions, their squares,
and cross products are assembled from the acquired tables. The complete
isotropic self-Casimir is basis independent and has the rational formula
Q_p=N_p(d_p-N_p/d_p)I+2 sum_(i<j in p)Swap_ij.
Equivalently, with U_ab=sum_(i in p)E_ab^(i),
Q_p=sum_ab U_ab U_ba-(N_p^2/d_p)I; these U_ab actions are already in
the acquired orbit tables. This constructs the swap SUM without treating
individual noninvariant swap matrices as additional acquired input.
Thus normalized SU(d) generator entries need not be an irrational hidden
input for this portion. Rational cross matrices/fields give rational
operator coefficients; fixed algebraic entries can instead be rounded
with O(log N+log(1/a)) precision bits and a separately charged acquisition
cost. Gibbs half-trace perturbation is <=||H-H'|| by normalized Duhamel.

For exact rational H, ||H||<=h=Hbar N and Tr exp(H)>=Dphys by the theorem's
trace mean. An elementary direct Taylor alternative is enough: choose b
with 2^-b<=a/4 and degree k satisfying k+1>=6h+2b. The operator error
of P_k=sum_(r=0)^k H^r/r! is at most
e^h(eh/(k+1))^(k+1)<=e^h 2^-(k+1)<=a/4.
Hence Tr P_k>0 and R=P_k/Tr P_k is rational Hermitian trace one with
||R-rho_Gibbs||_1<=a, for a<=1/64. Its op norm is <=1+a<=2.
The powers and normalization use the physical trace and acquired algebra,
so the degree and intermediate rational bits are polynomial in
N, input bits and log(1/a); no Dphys-by-Dphys matrix is built.

For algebraically approximated H use the earlier generic shifted-Taylor
argument, which does not assume its trace mean is still nonnegative,
and allocate half of a to coefficient perturbation. This retains the
same polynomial bit dependence when the precision interface is charged.

5. HETEROGENEOUS RATIONAL ORBITS AND EXACT WHITEBALL CORRECTION

At each species use the fixed rational orthogonal Hermitian basis
T_p0=I, X_ab, Y_ab and H_l=(sum_(a<=l)E_aa-l E_(l+1,l+1))/l.
Every nonidentity element is traceless with op norm <=1, squared HS norm
h_pa in [1,2], and h_p0=d_p. Its rational change of basis is acquired
by a degree-N_p symmetric-power polynomial expansion in each species,
then a tensor product. Fixed q_p gives polynomially many monomials and
polynomial rational bit complexity. This does not enumerate physical words.

For species count vectors m_p, define
 S_m=tensor_p S_(p,m_p),
 s_m=product_p N_p!/product_a m_(p,a)!,
 ||S_m||_HS^2=s_m product_(p,a)h_pa^m_pa.
There are Dalg pairwise orthogonal rational S_m. For trace-zero
E=R-S, its all-identity coefficient is exactly zero. Write
E=sum_(m!=all_identity)c_m S_m,
A_m=Dphys s_m |c_m|, A=sum_m A_m.
All these numbers and signs are acquired exactly rationally in polynomial
time/bits from R,S and the change-of-basis tables.

For each nonidentity ordered word v, (I+sign(c_m)P_v)/Dphys is fully
separable by a GLOBAL sign-parity ensemble on all active physical sites.
Its orbit average is
Omega_m=I/Dphys+sign(c_m)S_m/(s_m Dphys).
The species orbits are independently uniformly permuted; their product
is the correct heterogeneous orbit. A single parity constraint across
species is needed. Imposing one parity independently in each species
would in general retain unwanted proper-support cross terms.

Cauchy-Schwarz over ordered words gives
 sum_v |c_v|<=||E||_HS product_p(sum_a h_pa^-1)^(N_p/2)
                 <=Dphys ||E||_1,
and so A<=Dphys^2||E||_1. With w=product_p(d_p zeta_p)^N_p, every node
contains the same white component wI/Dphys. If A<=w the exact identity
 R=[S-wI/Dphys]+(w-A)I/Dphys+sum_m A_m Omega_m
is positive with total trace one and only fully separable pieces.
The coefficient-sum test A<=w is an explicit rational certificate;
||R-S||_1<=b_N=w/Dphys^2=product_p(zeta_p/d_p)^N_p is a sufficient bound.

7. SAMPLING THE CORRECTED LAW WITH BOUNDED FAIR-BIT COST

Top-level weights are 1-w, w-A and the A_m. In the first branch choose
a mixture node j by its positive weights. For site i of species p,
set t_i=d_p zeta_p and tau_jp=(sigma_jp-zeta_p I)/(1-t_i). Its factors
are I/d_p or tau_jp, with white probability t_i, CONDITIONED not all white.
Because w is identical at every node, conditioning does not bias j.

Let Q_i=product_(remaining after i)t_l. If no nonwhite factor has yet
occurred, the conditional white probability at i is
 t_i(1-Q_i)/(1-t_i Q_i),
and the nonwhite probability is (1-t_i)/(1-t_i Q_i). Once a nonwhite
factor occurs, all later choices use the original t_i. This formula
works for different species probabilities and is zero for a final white
choice if all previous choices were white. Products/powers have
O(N*cutoff_bits) rational bit length. Uniform per-species orbit sampling
uses remaining-label counts; choose fair signs at every active site but
the last, whose sign enforces the ONE global parity.

The rational-law sampler is exact abstractly, but a bounded fair-bit
implementation rounds its top-level, node and O(N) sequential conditional
probabilities to dyadics with total output-law TV budget epsilon/2.
There are polynomially many categories and O(N) adaptive transitions.
Using O(log(Dalg N/epsilon)) bits per rounded decision and ordinary
finite-category rounding suffices; conditional coupling sums the error
over transitions. All emitted one-site states remain rational PSD even
after probability rounding. The expected product density changes by at
most epsilon/2 in half trace distance.

Together with ||R-rho_Gibbs||_1<=a<=epsilon/4, the final whole-state
half-trace error is <epsilon. Its output is a compact program plus N
local states on each run, NOT an exponentially long list of every parity
product. Physical preparation still needs N local preparations and a
separately budgeted total preparation error.
