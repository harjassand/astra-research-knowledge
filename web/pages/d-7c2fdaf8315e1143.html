EXACT ACQUIRED ANGULAR LEMMA
Let B_l=(l+1)^2. Choose disjoint bands I_n=(B_{M_{n-1}},B_{M_n}] of complete
round-degree blocks, with M_{n-1}/M_n -> 0. In a sufficiently small fixed angular
neighborhood U, use the source's adjacent-double loops to implement one cyclic
permutation C_n of I_n, fixing every other ordered position. For each m form the
finite periodic program P_m consisting of the cycles of I_1,...,I_m. At a fixed
noncrossing phase s of P_m, the rank of label i in I_n after q repetitions is
sigma_s C_n^q(i). Consequently, over Q_m=lcm(|I_1|,...,|I_m|) repetitions, each
label visits each rank of I_n exactly Q_m/|I_n| times, regardless of the lengths
of all the other loops. Therefore

 average_i d(a^{-2}lambda_i) = (1/(|I_n| S_m)) integral_0^{S_m}
                                  sum_{j in I_n} d(a^{-2}lambda_j(H(s))) ds
 <= beta_n := a^{-1}sqrt(C_*)
       sum_{l=M_{n-1}+1}^{M_n}(2l+1)(l+1) / ((M_n+1)^2-(M_{n-1}+1)^2).

This identity is exact. beta_n/M_n -> 2sqrt(C_*)/(3a). It is independent of m.
Choose a=sqrt(v)>2/3 and q in (2sqrt(C_*)/(3a),1), and k_n=ceil(q M_n).
If transfer supplies one function for each band label with exponent <=beta_n,
the cumulative count is B_{M_n}-B_{M_0}, giving limsup >=1/q^2>1. Shrinking U
would allow 1/q^2 arbitrarily close to 9v/4, with no endpoint assertion.
