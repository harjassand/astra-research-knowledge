The finite transfer supplies an arbitrary Hermitian matrix H on a fixed n-dimensional orthonormal basis and a real diagonal A=diag(a_z). Set

\[
 W(z)=\sum_{z'\ne z}|H_{zz'}|[\cosh(a_z-a_{z'})-1],
 \qquad K=H-2A/\delta-W
\]

for δ>0. W is a nonnegative diagonal matrix. The claimed conclusion is

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(e^A e^{-\delta H}e^A)^q
 \le \operatorname{Tr}e^{-q\delta K}
 \quad\text{for every real }q>0.
\]

No stoquasticity, tracial state promise beyond the ordinary matrix trace, or commutation of A and H is required.

## Independent proof and adversarial checks

For |u|≤1 the Hermitian part of e^{uA}He^{-uA} has off-diagonal entries H_{zz'}cosh[u(a_z-a_{z'})]. Subtract H. The absolute value of its quadratic form is bounded by

\[
 \sum_{z,z'}|H_{zz'}|[\cosh(a_z-a_{z'})-1]|f_z||f_{z'}|
 \le \sum_z W(z)|f_z|^2.
\]

The last inequality counts both orientations of every edge and uses 2|f_zf_{z'}|≤|f_z|²+|f_{z'}|². Consequently there is no missing factor two: Sym(e^{uA}He^{-uA})≥H−W.

Define U(s)=e^{(-1+2s/δ)A}e^{-sH}e^A for 0≤s≤δ. It starts at I, ends at the positive definite X=e^Ae^{-δH}e^A, and satisfies U'=L(s)U with

\[
 L(s)=2A/\delta-e^{(-1+2s/\delta)A}He^{-(-1+2s/\delta)A},
 \qquad \operatorname{Sym}L(s)\le-K.
\]

All differentiation is ordinary finite matrix differentiation. On k tensor copies the generator is the sum of the same L on each factor. It preserves the alternating subspace, and its Hermitian part is at most minus the copy sum of K. If E_1≤…≤E_n are the eigenvalues of K, that sum restricted to the alternating subspace has minimum E_1+…+E_k. Gronwall therefore gives

\[
 \|\wedge^k X\|\le e^{-\delta(E_1+\cdots+E_k)}.
\]

No assumption that L(s) commutes at different times has entered the proof. Since X is positive definite, its decreasing eigenvalues x_i have prefix bounds

\[
 \sum_{i\le k}\log x_i\le-\delta\sum_{i\le k}E_i
 \quad (1\le k\le n).
\]

For decreasing sequences, the sum of positive parts above a threshold h is the maximum of their shifted prefix sums, including k=0. Thus

\[
 \sum_i(\log x_i-h)_+\le\sum_i(-\delta E_i-h)_+.
\]

Integrate this inequality against q²e^{qh}dh. The exact scalar integral over h≤y is e^{qy}. All integrands are nonnegative and the finite sums pose no interchange issue. This proves the conclusion for every q>0, including 0<q<1. A bound on only \|X\| or only Tr X would not have sufficed; the complete exterior-power family is load-bearing. The argument never uses exponential operator monotonicity, which would be invalid.

If W≤wI, min–max gives E_i(H−2A/δ−W)≥E_i(H−2A/δ)−w and hence

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}X^q
 \le e^{q\delta w}\operatorname{Tr}e^{-q\delta(H-2A/\delta)}.
\]

This last scalar comparison is also justified without an exponential operator-order assertion.

## CSS locality and retained controls

For a commuting CSS check Hamiltonian, choose A=λN_T, where N_T counts violated Z checks in a specified set T. Original X-check transitions preserve every Z-check sign, so their contribution to W vanishes exactly. Diagonal terms contribute zero as well. For Pauli perturbations of support at most w_0, check incidence at most z_c, and per-qubit coefficient intensity J, each contributing transition changes N_T by at most z_c w_0. Only terms meeting Q_T, the union of the supports of checks in T, can contribute. The triangle bound for entries with several Pauli contributions gives

\[
 W\le |Q_T|J[\cosh(z_cw_0|\lambda|)-1]I
 \le z_c|T|J[\cosh(z_cw_0|\lambda|)-1]I.
\]

This passes as an algebraic consequence of the supplied support/incidence bounds. I have not reacquired the actual recursive-code geometry proving z_c=7 in this cycle. Y terms are allowed by this finite trace proof; positivity-dependent path methods do not transfer to them automatically. Unbounded oscillator couplings are outside the finite-intensity interface.

The competent elementary control is important: if A≤0 then e^A is a contraction and the eigenvalues of e^Ae^{-δH}e^A are each at most the corresponding eigenvalues of e^{-δH}. To see this, compare the equal eigenvalues of e^Ae^{-δH}e^A and e^{-δH/2}e^{2A}e^{-δH/2}≤e^{-δH}. This gives Tr X^q≤Tr e^{-qδH} for all q>0. Replacing the retained local-field partition function by a scalar norm bound can therefore make the new estimate worse than this elementary control.
