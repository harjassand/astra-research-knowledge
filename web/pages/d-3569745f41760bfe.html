# Locally tracial interacting Gibbs experiments retain a nonclassical parameter

Status: exact elementary construction + transfer from established strong qubit LAN. It is a consequence/counterexample, not a new no-broadcasting or quantum-LAN theorem. The connected cluster-chain version strengthens the initial disjoint-dimer construction. No novelty claim.

## A connected finite-range example

Let N>=4 be even, arrange N physical qubits on a ring, and let U_N be the product of CZ gates on its edges (depth two). With K_i=Z_(i-1)X_i Z_(i+1), L_i=Z_(i-1)Y_i Z_(i+1), define

H_N(theta)=-sum_i [cos(theta) K_i+sin(theta) L_i],
Gamma_N(theta)=exp[-a H_N(theta)]/Tr exp[-a H_N(theta)], a>0 fixed.

The indices are periodic. Each term has support three consecutive sites and norm one. The interaction graph is connected. At each theta the N local terms commute because they are the fixed CZ conjugates of independent single-site cos(theta)X_i+sin(theta)Y_i. Across different theta the state family is noncommutative.

Put r=tanh(a) in (0,1) and tau(theta)=[I+r(cos(theta)X+sin(theta)Y)]/2. Exactly,

Gamma_N(theta)=U_N tau(theta)^(tensor N) U_N^*.

Thus applying the known circuit U_N^* is a zero-error bidirectional equivalence with N iid nontracial logical qubits.

Every physical one-site marginal is exactly I/2 for every theta, a and N. To see it, CZ conjugation fixes Z_i and maps X_i,Y_i to K_i,L_i. In the logical product state E Z_j=0, and therefore the expectation of every physical one-site Pauli is zero:

E Z_i=0; E X_i=E X_i^logical E Z_(i-1)^logical E Z_(i+1)^logical=0; similarly for Y_i.

Consequently neither infinitely accurate knowledge of all one-site densities nor the statement that all of them are maximally mixed can determine whether the whole local experiment is classical.

## Strong local obstruction

For fixed R>0 restrict theta=u/sqrt(N), |u|<=R. Under a fixed one-qubit change of basis, this is a one-parameter transverse unitary orbit about a faithful nontracial qubit reference. Guţă--Kahn, quant-ph/0512075, Theorem 1.1, gives parameter-independent channels in both directions to the real-displacement thermal oscillator family

T_u=D(c_r u) tau_thermal D(c_r u)^*,

tau_thermal=(1-q)sum_(m>=0)q^m |m><m|,
q=(1-r)/(1+r), c_r nonzero.

The precise c_r depends only on the unitary convention (their U(v)=exp(i v.sigma), whereas the angle theta here corresponds to exp(-i theta Z/2)); it does not affect the obstruction.

The family contains two noncommuting faithful states (u=0 and one nonzero u). Its infimum uniform EB reconstruction error is a strictly positive constant c(a,R). A compactness proof is summarized next. Composing any proposed classical archive on the cluster chain with the exact Clifford equivalence and the two strong LAN channels preserves the EB property. Hence

liminf_(N->infty) inf_(Phi EB) sup_(|u|<=R)
 T(Phi(Gamma_N(u/sqrt(N))),Gamma_N(u/sqrt(N))) >=c(a,R)>0.

All physical one-site marginals throughout this family are exactly I/2. The tangent parameter space is one-dimensional, so its restricted antisymmetric symplectic form is identically zero. Both premises can coexist with a positive whole-experiment reconstruction floor.

## Why the oscillator infimum is positive

If a sequence of EB channels asymptotically fixed the faithful thermal tau_0 and one displaced tau_u, their outputs on tau_0 would be uniformly tight in number-state projections. For every finite spectral projection P_m of tau_0, P_m<=C_m tau_0. Positivity then makes outputs of all positive inputs supported within P_m uniformly tight. A diagonal subsequence on finite matrix units yields a CPTP strong limit; trace-norm contractivity extends convergence to all trace-class inputs. EB channels are closed under this strong convergence: output states for every fixed ancilla/input remain in the closed separable cone. The limit would exactly fix the two states and would broadcast them by conditionally making two copies of its classical prepared output. Exact no-broadcasting (Barnum--Caves--Fuchs--Jozsa--Schumacher) then forces them to commute.

They do not commute: faithful thermal tau_0 has modular rotations generated by number N. A displaced thermal state with a nonzero mean rotates under that group, whereas every density commuting with tau_0 must be invariant. Contradiction.

This yields existence of c(a,R)>0; it is not an explicit sharp floor. General minimum sufficient algebra results already explain the obstruction: Guţă--Jenčová, quant-ph/0606213, Lemma 5.5, includes the modular orbit H-sigma_t(H), which generates the conjugate missing from a one-dimensional tangent.

## Simpler two-body dimer variant

On disjoint neighboring pairs, take h_j(theta)=-[cos(theta) Z_(2j-1)Z_(2j)+sin(theta)Y_(2j-1)Z_(2j)]. This is equivalent, by a CNOT with second qubit control and first target, to one logical nontracial qubit tensored with a known maximally mixed spectator. Again every physical one-site marginal is I/2. The connected cluster model above avoids relying on disconnected interaction components.

## Inference implication and limits

A scalar parameter can have an attainable single-parameter Fisher-information bound while its complete experiment still contains information for incompatible later decision problems. Replacing the entire state by an optimal estimate or a one-site marginal statistic cannot promise preservation for every later bounded test. This distinction is stated explicitly in Guţă--Kahn section 7.2 and in Guţă--Jenčová's modular sufficient-algebra analysis; the cluster construction applies it to local interacting Gibbs systems.

The construction does not show failure in generic connected noncommuting Hamiltonians: each fixed-theta cluster Hamiltonian is commuting and related to iid states by a supplied circuit. That is enough to disprove any unrestricted claim based only on local marginal traciality or a tangent symplectic form. It does not settle high-temperature classicalization for general nonconserved local perturbations.
