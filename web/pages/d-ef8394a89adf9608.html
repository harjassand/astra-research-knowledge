# Spectral barycenters in entangled threshold repetition

The independent gate is notes/frontier_check_repetition/cycle19/FINITE_REPETITION_GATE.md. Both checks are internal model work, not external validation. ## Question and exact interface

For a finite two-player one-round Boolean game G with question law mu, answer product d=|A||B|, and finite-dimensional tensor-product entangled value v<1, bound the probability that an arbitrary joint finite-dimensional strategy wins at least ceil((v+delta)k) of k independent questions. The state dimension is unrestricted but finite, the supremum need not be attained, and 0<delta<1-v. All logarithms are natural.

## Definition of the question chain

Let H={(x,y):mu(x,y)>0}. Its components H_j join pairs sharing either x or y. Put lambda_j=mu(H_j), mu_j=mu(.|H_j). Each local question identifies its component.

On L^2(mu_j), define orthogonal conditional-expectation projections

  (P_X f)(x,y)=sum_{y'} mu_j(y'|x) f(x,y'),
  (P_Y f)(x,y)=sum_{x'} mu_j(x'|y) f(x',y),
  P_j=(P_X+P_Y)/2.

The positive self-adjoint contraction P_j is the reversible chain that keeps a uniformly selected one of the questions and redraws the other. Its fixed space consists exactly of functions constant on H_j: equality in the projection norm requires membership in both local-function spaces, and connectedness then forces constancy.

For non-singleton H_j, let sigma_j be the smallest eigenvalue of I-P_j on the orthogonal complement of constants. It satisfies 0<sigma_j<=1. Let sigma=min_j sigma_j over non-singleton components; if all components are singletons set sigma=1. A singleton has zero variance, so this convention is harmless.

This is precisely the two-player conditional-resampling connection graph of Dinur, Harsha, Venkat and Yuen (2017), Definition 2, up to the choice of Laplacian normalization. The graph and Poincare inequality are prior art; the asserted contribution here is their use with the release's common Hilbert-space conditional vectors and their barycenters. The source's displayed variational formula has a possible factor-two convention; our definition by I-P fixes all constants without relying on that convention.

## Our quantitative result

For every proper tested set C with p=Pr(E_C)>0 and m=k-|C|, put

  tau=(log(1/p)+|C| log d)/m,
  sbar=m^{-1} sum_{i notin C} Pr(I_i=1|E_C).

Then

  sbar <= v + sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m)) + 2 sqrt(tau/sigma).            (1)

Consequently, every finite-dimensional joint strategy satisfies

  Pr(W_k >= ceil((v+delta)k))
     <= exp(-sigma delta^3 k / [2048(1+log d)]).                 (2)

Both statements are uniform in finite strategy dimension. They pass to the supremum. Where the released universal delta^5 theorem has been adopted with its external hypotheses, the larger of its exponent and (2) is valid.

In particular, for any product question law with at least two nontrivial local alphabets, sigma=1/2 regardless of the least positive atom. Thus this cubic threshold bound has no dependence on question alphabet sizes or least question mass. Specialized free-game repetition results predate this work and can be stronger; this example is a discriminator for the new proof mechanism, not a novelty claim for free games.

## Complete proof

### 1. Reconstructed source input

The common resolvent purification is F_T h(s)=T(T+sI)^{-1}h for effects T. Its Gram is T by the scalar integral int_0^infinity t^2/(t+s)^2 ds=t. For B=sum q_l A_l, the difference Gram is bounded by sum q_l f(A_l)-f(B), f(t)=t log t. Indeed, the inverse second-difference expansion yields the latter's integral

  int_0^infinity s sum_l q_l (B+sI)^{-1}(A_l-B)
          (A_l+sI)^{-1}(A_l-B)(B+sI)^{-1} ds,

whereas the difference Gram has the same integrand with the middle s(A_l+sI)^{-1} replaced by s^2(A_l+sI)^{-2}. The scalar spectral inequality s(A_l+sI)^{-2}<=(A_l+sI)^{-1} proves domination. Common kernels are first removed; the integrals converge on supp B. A common finite span of all images preserves all pairwise products, so the needed spaces are finite.

For any finer effects G_a summing to T, polar factor V_T=F_T T^{-1/2} gives transported positive measurements

  Gtilde_a=V_T T^{-1/2}G_a T^{-1/2}V_T^*
           +1_{a=a_*}(I-V_T V_T^*).

They sum to I and F_T^* Gtilde_a F_T=G_a. This preserves the exact answer law.

Source Section 4, Lemma 4.1 (lem:exposure), then exposes Alice's questions before a fixed live coordinate and Bob's after it. Unconditioned remaining question strings become independent after this mixed flag is fixed. The effects are averaged under the original question law, not under the postselected law. Two t log t entropy telescopes, each with range [-p(log(1/p)+|C|log d),0], imply the following finite input. This argument and the exact coarse/fine answer identities were reconstructed in full; no classical replacement of quantum states is assumed.

There are finite spaces, indices i,r, weights w_ir>=0 independent of the fresh pair z=(x,y), vectors f_ir(z), local anchors a_ir(x),b_ir(y), and effects 0<=W_ir(z)<=I formed from local POVMs, such that

  sum_ir,z w_ir mu(z)||f_ir(z)||^2=p,
  sum_ir,z w_ir mu(z)[||f_ir(z)-a_ir(x)||^2
                            +||f_ir(z)-b_ir(y)||^2] <= 2p tau,
  sum_ir,z w_ir mu(z)<f_ir(z),W_ir(z)f_ir(z)> = p sbar.           (3)

The weights need not sum to one. Finiteness, fresh-question independence of w_ir, a common codomain, and locality of the transported measurements are load-bearing.

### 2. Hilbert-valued Poincare inequality

For any vector-valued f on H_j, define its mean bar f=sum_z mu_j(z)f(z) and variance Var_j(f)=sum_z mu_j(z)||f(z)-bar f||^2. Decompose its coordinates into a finite orthonormal Hilbert basis. The scalar spectral theorem applies coordinatewise and yields

  sigma_j Var_j(f) <= <f,(I-P_j)f>
   = (1/2)[sum_z mu_j(z)||f(z)-P_X f(z)||^2
               +sum_z mu_j(z)||f(z)-P_Y f(z)||^2].             (4)

An orthogonal conditional mean minimizes squared distance to local functions. Thus P_X f can be replaced on the right by any a(x), and P_Y f by any b(y). Summing (4) with the weights from (3) gives, with

  E_j=sum_ir w_ir Var_j(f_ir),   E=sum_j lambda_j E_j,

the bound E<=p tau/sigma. Singletons contribute zero. No path diameter, least atom, root choice, or bounded Hilbert dimension enters this step.

### 3. The barycenters are legal shared states

Define

  bar f_ir,j=sum_z mu_j(z) f_ir(z),
  P_j^mass=sum_ir,z w_ir mu_j(z)||f_ir(z)||^2,
  Phat_j=sum_ir w_ir||bar f_ir,j||^2,
  T_j=sum_ir,z w_ir mu_j(z)<f_ir(z),W_ir(z)f_ir(z)>,
  That_j=sum_ir,z w_ir mu_j(z)<bar f_ir,j,W_ir(z)bar f_ir,j>.

Use P_j^mass to distinguish this scalar from the question chain P_j. Orthogonal mean decomposition gives the exact identity

  Phat_j=P_j^mass-E_j<=P_j^mass.                               (5)

If Phat_j>0, the normalized vector

  Phat_j^{-1/2} sum_ir sqrt(w_ir)|i,r>_A |i,r>_B bar f_ir,j

is a finite shared pure state independent of the new questions. Controlled transported POVMs depend on the shared flag and the player's own question. Its success in the component game G_j is That_j/Phat_j, so That_j<=v_j Phat_j, where v_j=omega^*(G_j). If Phat_j=0 the accepting mass is also zero.

The barycenter need not be physically acquired or locally computable from the players' original strategy. This is allowed for a value upper bound because the supremum ranges over all finite shared states. It would be an uncharged assumption in an algorithmic preparation claim; we make none.

For any effect W, |<a,Wa>-<b,Wb>|<=(||a||+||b||)||a-b||. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz and (5) give

  |T_j-That_j| <= (sqrt(P_j^mass)+sqrt(Phat_j))sqrt(E_j)
                  <= 2 sqrt(P_j^mass E_j).

Therefore

  T_j <= v_j P_j^mass + 2 sqrt(P_j^mass E_j).

After weighting by lambda_j and using sum lambda_j P_j^mass=p,

  sbar <= sum_j lambdatilde_j v_j + 2 sqrt(E/p),
  lambdatilde_j=lambda_j P_j^mass/p.                           (6)

This uses one mass comparison, rather than separately paying to move both the winning mass and the norm with the source's root-vector triangle inequality.

### 4. Drift of component probabilities

The exact question/answer identities identify lambdatilde with the average live-coordinate component distribution conditioned on E_C. Its full m-coordinate distribution has density <=1/p relative to lambda^{tensor m}, since conditioning any event of probability p has that likelihood-ratio bound. Hence its relative entropy is <=log(1/p). Product relative entropy dominates the sum of marginal relative entropies. Pinsker plus Cauchy-Schwarz gives

  TV(lambdatilde,lambda)<=sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m)).

Each v_j lies in [0,1]. Furthermore sum_j lambda_j v_j<=v because the players locally identify the component and can tensor together finitely many component strategies approaching their suprema. Combining this with (6) and E<=p tau/sigma proves (1).

For a connected support the component drift is exactly zero, yielding the sharper sbar<=v+2sqrt(tau/sigma). We retain the general bound for disconnected support and growing alphabets.

### 5. Threshold transfer and constants

The release's Section 2 Proposition 2.3 (prop:conditioning-to-tail) is a purely probabilistic transfer, reconstructed from its proof: if tau<=T<=delta/4 forces sbar<=v+delta/4 uniformly over all lengths and finite strategies, then the threshold rate is delta T/[16(1+log d)]. Its proof samples tested coordinates with replacement from the upper tail; the large-length proof is extended to every k by independent tensor copies. No commutativity of the repeated POVMs is used.

Take T=sigma delta^2/128. Since sigma<=1 and delta<1, T<=delta/4. At this budget the bound (1), using log(1/p)/m<=tau, gives

  excess <= sqrt(T/2)+2sqrt(T/sigma)
          <= delta/16+delta/sqrt(32) < delta/4.

The transfer gives (2). For connected support, T=sigma delta^2/64 gives excess<=delta/4 and improves the constant 2048 to 1024.

## Downstream use and boundaries

For efficiently specified game families whose conditional-resampling chain has sigma>=1/poly(input length), inverse-polynomial completeness/soundness gaps now have a direct cubic two-sided amplification schedule from this independently reconstructed mechanism. Taking threshold halfway between c and s with Delta=c-s gives soundness <=exp[-sigma(Delta/2)^3 k/(2048(1+log d))]; completeness retains the release's Hoeffding bound exp[-Delta^2 k/8]. This can be stronger than the released universal fifth-power schedule when sigma is large relative to Delta^2.

The theorem proved in this note covers joint finite-dimensional entangled strategies. A separately reconstructed extension to commuting-operator strategies, requiring an arbitrary-Hilbert-space transport proof rather than identifying the two strategy models, is in notes/frontier_check_repetition/cycle19/COMMUTING_THRESHOLD_EXTENSION.md; our independent operator gate is COMMUTING_RESOLVENT_INDEPENDENT_GATE.md. Neither interface supplies unrestricted representations of nonlocal verification tests as sparse Hamiltonians. They introduce no positive sparse common isometry for the BMVZ five-qubit amplified Hamiltonians, no preparation of the proof's barycenter, and no efficient evaluation of entangled value.

The graph gap is a genuine hypothesis. For mu(0,0)=mu(1,1)=(1-epsilon)/2 and mu(0,1)=mu(1,0)=epsilon/2, one obtains sigma=epsilon when 0<epsilon<=1/2. The square-root loss (4) is attained by an eigenvector of the conditional chain with its exact local conditional means, so deleting sigma is impossible in this generic barycenter step. This does not prove that a universal cubic entangled threshold theorem is impossible by another method.
