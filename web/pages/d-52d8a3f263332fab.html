## Scoped primary-literature check

- Mohit Bansil and Ilya Kachkovskiy, “On almost commuting matrices with respect to the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm,” https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31000, v1 submitted 31 August 2026. Its abstract gives commuting self-adjoint approximants for a fixed pair of self-adjoint contractions with normalized-HS distance at most five times the commutator norm to the one-third power; it states that this exponent is sharp within those constraints. This is a pair result. It does not produce a single canonical POVM for a channel-energy-weighted frame of rank growing with d, and it does not supply the operator-norm/normalization conversion required here.
- Lev Glebsky, “Almost commuting matrices with respect to normalized Hilbert-Schmidt norm,” https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.3082. This is prior for fixed-finite-tuple normalized-HS approximation. No quantitative growing-frame canonical-measurement conclusion is taken from it.
- “Quantum marginal problem and incompatibility,” https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.02941, is retained in the C5 source ledger for the standard channel-compatibility/quantum-marginal Choi representation. This branch uses the supplied broadcaster directly and needs no external compatibility theorem; no author attribution is needed for this note.

The proof in PROOF_AND_GATE.md is an internal finite-dimensional Stinespring derivation and depends on no external theorem beyond the supplied channel assumptions.
