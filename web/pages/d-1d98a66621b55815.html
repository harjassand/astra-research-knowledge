## Strongest claim

For a finite weakly reversible stochastic mass-action network whose complexes
have molecularity at most two, every infinite closed communicating class with
one-dimensional stoichiometric subspace has a genuine classwise Foster
function given by the N76 falling-factorial potential

  F(x) = min_{y in C, y <= x} sum_i log((x_i-y_i)!).

On the unbounded tail of the class, its generator satisfies

  L F(x(t)) = -c t^p log(t) + O(t^p),   c > 0,   1 <= p <= 2.

Thus LF is eventually at most -1, F is proper, and V=1+F also satisfies a
non-explosion Lyapunov bound LV <= C V. This gives non-explosion and positive
recurrence on that class. The claim is only a rank-one transfer. It does not
solve the multidimensional weakly reversible/bimolecular positive-recurrence
problem. The 1D recurrence conclusion is already covered by Wiuf-Xu's
Theorem 4.11 and Corollary 4.12, so this is a potential reconstruction and
certificate route, not a priority claim.

## Derivation

Let S=span(v) with primitive integer v, oriented toward an infinite class so
v_i >= 0 and v != 0. Write states on its tail as x(t)=b+t v, t integer; put
I={i:v_i>0} and G=sum_{i in I} v_i > 0. Every reaction vector is alpha_r v
for an integer alpha_r. Coordinates outside I are fixed on this class.
Discard linkage components whose fixed-coordinate requirements cannot be met
on the class. In every remaining linkage component, all complexes are
eventually available as t grows, since the I coordinates grow and every
complex in a component has the same fixed-coordinate values.

For a complex y define its tail degree d(y)=sum_{i in I} y_i. Its rate is

  lambda_r(t) = k_r product_i (b_i+t v_i)_{underline y_ri}
              = a_r t^{d(y_r)} + O(t^{d(y_r)-1}),   a_r > 0,

after absorbing the fixed-coordinate falling factorial into a_r. Let p be the
largest d(y) among active complexes with a nonzero reaction. Bimolecularity
gives p<=2. Also p>=1 on a nontrivial infinite class. Within a linkage
component, distinct complexes differ by alpha v, so their d-values differ by
alpha G. A source with d(y)=p is maximal in its component. Weak reversibility
then forces every non-self transition from that source to a lower-d target:
there is no higher-d target in the component, and distinct complexes cannot
have equal d. Therefore alpha_r<0 for every reaction with d(y_r)=p, and at
least one such reaction exists. Consequently

  sum_{r:d(y_r)=p} a_r alpha_r = -c_0 < 0.

Now put M(x)=max_{y<=x} product_i (x_i)_{underline y_i}. The potential has the
exact identity

  exp(F(x)) = product_i x_i! / M(x).

On the tail, M(x(t))=m t^p+O(t^{p-1}) for some m>0, while Stirling's formula
gives sum_i log((b_i+t v_i)!) = G t log(t)+O(t). Hence

  F(x(t)) = G t log(t)+O(t),
  F(x(t)+alpha v)-F(x(t)) = alpha G log(t)+O(1)

for each of the finitely many reaction increments alpha. Combining these with
the rate expansion,

  L F(x(t)) = sum_r lambda_r(t)[F(x(t)+alpha_r v)-F(x(t))]
            = -c_0 G t^p log(t) + O(t^p) + O(t^(p-1)log(t)).

The negative leading term dominates. F is nonnegative and proper on the
class, since F=G t log(t)+O(t). Thus LF<=-1 outside a finite subset. On the
remaining finite set, LF is bounded, so for V=1+F there is a finite C with
LV<=C V. The standard countable-state Foster/Khasminskii criteria give
non-explosion and positive recurrence for the irreducible closed class.

There is also an exact effective drift certificate using W(x(t))=t. The
generator is the rational polynomial D(t)=sum_r alpha_r lambda_r(t), with
negative leading coefficient -a<0 and degree p. If
 D(t)=-a t^p+sum_{j<p} b_j t^j and B=sum_{j<p}|b_j|, then for
 t>=max(1,ceil(2B/a)), D(t)<=-(a/2)t^p. The coefficients are exactly
computable from rational rates and the supplied class offset. This gives an
explicit finite Foster threshold for this rank-one subclass. F itself gives
the stronger logarithmic drift asymptotic above; the W certificate makes
threshold synthesis elementary.

## Event-clock boundary and diagnostics

The N76 inequality is one-sided. If Y_j is the reaction-event chain and
E[exp(F(Y_{j+1})-F(Y_j)) | Y_j] <= C, then
Z_j=exp(F(Y_j)-j log C) is a nonnegative supermartingale. In physical time,
the corresponding correction is exp(F(X_t)-N_t log C), where N_t is the
jump count. This correction depends on an external clock; it is not a
state-only Foster function. For V=exp(F), the direct generator bound is

  LV/V = sum_r lambda_r(x)(exp(delta_r F)-1)
        <= (C-1) Lambda(x),

which is not negative. So N76's event-count bound alone does not imply
recurrence or physical-time return. In rank one, the missing negative sign
comes from the highest-degree source reactions being strictly inward; weak
reversibility and the unique line order make that argument possible.

The bounded exact script `factorial_transfer.py` checks two diagnostics. For
A <-> 2A with unit rates, F(n)=log((n-2)!) for n>=2,

  LF(n)=n log(n-1)-n(n-1)log(n-2),

which is positive at n=3 and negative with leading term -n^2 log(n) at
infinity, while E[exp(delta F)] is 4/3 at n=3 and approaches 1 from above.
For A <-> B, at every diagonal state (n,n) the two legal exchange moves have
exactly exp(delta F)=1. This second network has finite conserved-mass classes;
it is a flat-drift diagnostic, not a counterexample to classwise recurrence.
The executed integer/Fraction checks are recorded in
`factorial_transfer_check.txt`; they are finite diagnostics only.

## Boundaries, costs, and status

- Inputs are a finite rational positive-rate mass-action network, weak
  reversibility, complex molecularity <=2, and a supplied infinite closed
  class with rank-one stoichiometric direction. Rate constants are fixed,
  not arbitrary time-dependent controls. No rate acquisition or class
  discovery algorithm is claimed.
- The rank-one proof uses a scalar tail order. In rank >=2, maximal reactant
  tiers can contain tangent transitions; the N76 moment bound controls only
  upward event-clock growth and supplies no negative drift after averaging
  those tangent cycles. A multidimensional boundary-face/recovery lemma is
  still missing.
- Rational exact threshold for W uses polynomial coefficient arithmetic in
  the supplied class parameterization; no full parser or all-class
  certificate compiler was implemented. The log-F drift is an asymptotic
  proof, not a supplied finite-bit threshold output.
- N76 itself is a supplied summary only; its linked note, code, and logs were
  absent. The general potential inequality and its 8034-state/25456-transition
  check are source-reported and were not replayed here. Our 1D derivation is
  independent of those finite checks.
- Primary prior art: Wiuf-Xu, arXiv:2012.07954v2, Theorem 4.11 and Corollary
  4.12 already establish positive recurrence on 1D weakly reversible mass-
  action classes (and more). Xu, arXiv:2409.05340v2, gives non-explosivity
  for second-order endotactic systems. This work does not claim novelty or
  external validation.
