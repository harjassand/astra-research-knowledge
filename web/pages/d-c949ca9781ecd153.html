Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: follow-up bounded primary-source audit for the general finite-dimensional nonlinear inequality J >= pi E under KMS symmetry, including the exact weak/strong p=1 L_p-regularity interfaces and prior status of pi sharpness. Exact endpoint translation
Use the Heisenberg positive-generator convention L, P_t=e^{-tL}; its trace-dual Schrödinger generator is L_*. Let Gamma_sigma(X)=sigma^{1/2}Xsigma^{1/2}, f=Gamma_sigma^{-1}(rho), and define the KMS-root generator H=Gamma_sigma^{1/2} L Gamma_sigma^{-1/2} on Hilbert--Schmidt space. Then the Kastoryano--Temme p=1 forms obey

  E_1(f)=J(rho)/2,
  E_2(I_{2,1}(f))=<sqrt(rho), H(sqrt(rho))>_HS=E(rho).

Consequently weak p=1 regularity gives J>=2E, while strong p=1 regularity gives J>=4E. These are base-algebra consequences: KT's definitions quantify over f in M_d and do not include an untouched reference. Their Conjecture 19 proposes weak regularity for every primitive Liouvillian and strong regularity for primitive reversible ones; the 2013 paper itself labels this unresolved. Thus it supplies a clear historical conjecture, not an established 2 or 4 theorem for arbitrary KMS dynamics.

The root-supplied candidate J>=pi E is strictly stronger than the weak endpoint and weaker than the strong endpoint. Its proposed coefficient does not follow by interpolating those two conjectural endpoints. Gao--Wang v2 proves a primitive KMS MLSI bound without assuming L_p regularity, but this is a different route to a lower bound by relative entropy; it neither proves weak p=1 regularity nor supplies an E comparison. Liu--Wan--Wu's primitive KMS qubit with CMLSI constant zero rules out using a uniform complete MLSI as the bridge, but does not contradict J>=pi E.

What is known about the appearance of pi
Franz--Hiai Theorem 2.2 gives a unique integral representation of nonnegative operator-convex functions on (0,infinity). Specializing to f_kappa(t)=(t-1)log(t)-kappa(sqrt(t)-1)^2 gives the exact static operator-convex threshold kappa<=pi, and at kappa=pi the resolvent density is (sqrt(lambda)-1)^2. Petz functional calculus yields the static Jeffreys-versus-square-root-Hellinger inequality for faithful state pairs. This is a genuine, sharp prior mechanism for a pi coefficient in a static operator-convex comparison.

It does not give the dynamical inequality J>=pi E: differentiating that static functional along a QMS introduces terms involving Dlog_rho[L_*(rho)] and Dsqrt_rho[L_*(rho)], not the KMS-root Dirichlet form. The tangent insertion of the generator is therefore not controlled by operator convexity alone. The exact missing conversion is recorded in ../cycle08_petz_pi_prior/REPORT.txt.
