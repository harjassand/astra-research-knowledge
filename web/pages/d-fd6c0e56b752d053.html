EXACT GENERAL GATE AND PRESERVED FAILED EXTENSIONS
2026-10-09. Universal finite C and sharp C=2 remain UNKNOWN.

ALL-FRAME FORM OF THE EXACT TARGET
Let tau=Tr/d and H_0 be the real traceless Hermitian HS space. For any
positive frame operator Q=sum_i |A_i><A_i| with A_i in H_0, put
  V(Q)=sum_i tau(A_i^2),
  s_Phi(Q)=sum_i tau[A_i Phi(A_i)],
  k(Q)=max_{sum_y p_y pi_y=I/d} sum_y p_y sum_i [Tr(pi_y A_i)]^2,
where pi_y are pure density matrices. Finite ensembles suffice by first-
and second-moment Caratheodory. k(Q) is the exact canonical support; it
does not mean a finite chosen seed hull or a PPT relaxation.

Canonical maps form a compact convex set, and all maps fix I. Cone
separation shows that a ONE-channel full-domain factor C comparator
exists for each Phi exactly when for EVERY positive Q
  V(Q)-k(Q)<=C[V(Q)-s_Phi(Q)].                       (G1)
The optimizing ensemble may depend on Q before separation, but the final
channel cannot depend on the requested observable. CANONICAL_REDUCTION.txt
shows that arbitrary TP EB comparators have the same support for this
full-domain problem. Thus canonical versus arbitrary EB is not the
remaining obstruction.

For the conjecturally sharp C=2, the missing inequality is
  2s_Phi(Q)<=V(Q)+k(Q)                              (G2)
for arbitrary Q and legal Phi. Let R be the normalized Choi state of the
supplied broadcaster, symmetrized on B,C. Then R>=0, Tr R=1, all three
single-register marginals are I/d, and its AB marginal is the Choi state
of an HS-self-adjoint channel. Writing
  W(Q)=sum_i (A_i)^T tensor (A_i^B+A_i^C),
we have Tr[R W(Q)]=2s_Phi(Q). The exact general star obligation is
  max_{R with those legal constraints} Tr[R W(Q)]<=V(Q)+k(Q).       (G3)
This is not an unrestricted operator-norm bound. The fixed tracial
marginals and Choi self-adjointness constraints are part of the target.

WHAT THE CLIFFORD MECHANISM CHANGES
For a Clifford span, all attainable expectation vectors lie in a unit
Euclidean ball; every direction has a reference-preserving spectral
ensemble. Therefore k(Q)=lambda_max(Q), V(Q)=Tr Q. Orthogonal changes of
frame preserve the Clifford relations. The weighted star lemma proves
||W(Q)||<=Tr Q+lambda_max(Q), stronger than (G3), and yields an explicit
single covariance S=(2T-I)_+ with a feasible canonical ensemble. For
arbitrary observable spaces these three facts fail: the expectation body
is not a round ball, its tracial ensemble covariances have extra
constraints, and rotating a frame need not retain a simple multiplication
table. The next obligation is (G3), or an actual reference-preserving
ensemble with covariance dominating 2T-I. It is not another pairwise
commutator estimate or a separate measurement for each eigenvector.

JOINT-VARIANCE DUAL: A PRECISE POSSIBLE PROOF INTERFACE
Let L=sum_i A_i^2. The convex-roof loss v(Q)=V(Q)-k(Q) has the dual
  v(Q)=sup_Y tau(Y)
  subject to L-Y-2sum_i x_i A_i+||x||_2^2 I>=0 for every real x.
The equivalence follows by maximizing 2x dot <A>-||x||^2 over scalar x
for every pure state. An admissible Y is a common variance lower bound.
For the legal R above define
  H(Q)=L_A^T+(L_B+L_C)/2-W(Q).
Tr[R H(Q)]=2[V(Q)-s_Phi(Q)]. Thus (G2) is equivalent to
  tau(Y)<=Tr[R H(Q)]
for EVERY such admissible Y and legal R. No noncommutative substitution
theorem proving this statement has been supplied here.

The naive substitution of noncommuting observables for scalar x is
already false. For q>=2 Clifford generators, L=qI and Y=(q-1)I, so the
scalar pencil I-2sum_i x_i Gamma_i+||x||^2 I is positive (its minimum
eigenvalue is (||x||-1)^2). Replacing x_i with Gamma_i on a second register
gives (q+1)I-2sum_i Gamma_i^T tensor Gamma_i, whose expectation on the
maximally entangled vector is 1-q<0. The supplied two-output constraint is
essential; this one-partner failure cannot be repaired by scalar Jensen.

EXACT LEGAL OBSTRUCTION TO UNIVERSAL SPECTRAL TRUNCATION
Let S be the symmetric nearest-neighbor walk on the seven-cycle, indexed
by Z/7Z: S_ij=1/2 if i-j=+1 or -1 modulo 7, otherwise 0. Let P=(I+S)/2.
Define
  Phi(X)=sum_{i,j} P_ij X_jj |i><i|,
  B(X)=sum_{i,j} P_ij X_jj |ii><ii|.
Both are CPTP, B has two marginals Phi, and Phi is unital, HS-self-adjoint
and EB. It is also HS-positive: its diagonal eigenvalues are
(1+cos(2pi k/7))/2>=0, and its off-diagonal HS eigenvalues are zero.

The tempting generalization Psi=(2Phi-I)_+ preserves I and satisfies the
abstract form inequality Psi>=2Phi-I, but is not a positive map. On
diagonal matrices it is S_+. The only positive Fourier eigenvalues of S
are 1,cos(2pi/7),cos(2pi/7), giving
  (S_+)_(0,3)=[1+2cos(2pi/7)cos(6pi/7)]/7
             =[1-cos(pi/7)-cos(3pi/7)]/7<0.
Indeed cos(pi/7)>sqrt(3)/2>6/7 and cos(3pi/7)=sin(pi/14)>1/7.
Thus Psi(|3><3|) has a negative diagonal entry at 0. This is an exact
finite channel and broadcaster, with no numerical solver premise.

It is NOT a counterexample to (G1): Phi itself is EB and supplies C=1.
It falsifies only the most immediate spectral-functional-calculus lift
of the Clifford construction. A general proof must build another feasible
ensemble covariance above 2Phi-I rather than taking its positive part.

POSITIVE-SPECTRUM REDUCTION
N93's corrected tracial interface assumes Phi>=0 as an HS operator;
N143/LA001 do not. For any legal Phi, Phi^2 is HS-positive and legal, with
broadcaster (Phi tensor Phi) composed with B. Its eigenvalues obey
  1-lambda^2<=2(1-lambda), lambda in [-1,1].
Hence a universal constant C on the positive class gives a constant 2C on
the full self-adjoint class. Conversely the full class includes the
positive class. Existence of some finite dimension-free constant is
therefore equivalent across the two premises; this reduction does not
preserve a conjecturally sharp factor two.

WHY OTHER TESTED ANALOGIES DO NOT CLOSE THE GATE
1. Independent spectral PVMs cannot be merged for free. The archived
   broadcaster tree solves one entire frame but pays its logarithmic rank.
2. Fixed-point projection deletes arbitrarily slow classical modes. A
   new spectral gap assumption changes the target.
3. Standard multiplicative Grothendieck score rounding gives a score
   alpha*s, whose deficit V-alpha*s=(1-alpha)V+alpha(V-s) has an additive
   floor. It does not itself prove the deficit comparison (G1), nor fix
   the reference barycenter or output an EB channel. A suitably centered
   new rounding theorem remains possible; it has not been established.
4. Pairwise/finite-wise POVM compatibility does not give all-family
   compatibility or the EB Dirichlet bound. Channel compatibility here
   comes with one supplied joint CP map; changing that premise is invalid.
5. Cloning-versus-estimation statements about arbitrary nearly identical
   pure ensembles omit the full tracial reference and often the unital
   HS-self-adjoint channel. No such ensemble was converted into a legal
   counterexample. Mitra-Mandayam's optimization (Eq. 16) is explicitly
   over a p,q cloning ansatz and a basis, not all legal broadcasters, and
   their fidelity objective does not automatically become (G1).
6. PPT is only a necessary separability relaxation. Neither numerical
   feasibility nor failure against a finite canonical seed hull proves
   EB membership or defeats unrestricted canonical support.

DECISIVE FALSIFICATION STANDARD
A sharp-C2 falsifier must exhibit exact matrices A_i and a legal Phi,B
with V-k>2(V-s_Phi), with a rigorous upper bound on unrestricted k. To
falsify all universal finite constants needs a legal sequence with
(V-k)/(V-s_Phi) diverging. A certified PPT relaxation with a ratio >2 could
refute C2, but not all finite C; finite floating-point SDP values cannot.
The cycle-seven construction supplies a failed-proof witness only.
