# r19_manybody: sharp conserved-charge locality and reusable detector records

Status (2026-10-06): completed scoped constructive theorem and explicit cost benchmark; independent r11 audit passes. Novelty unverified; retain as benchmark, not a breakthrough. Distinct from quantum score-learning.

Question/interface: one uniform n-spin fixed-charge shell, unknown density q=k/n, one binary occupation detector, a k-independent recovery supported on m sites. Recovery error sums trace-norm defects of retained detector branches. The exact optimum is

    εopt = 2 E|S/m−q|,  S~Hypergeom(n,k,m).

Every quantum CPTP competitor obeys the exterior-marginal lower bound, and a uniform permutation of the block attains it. Repeated outcomes are exactly iid conditional on the block's conserved charge S. Their empirical mean has MSE v+[q(1−q)−v]/r with v=q(1−q)(n−m)/[m(n−1)]. Full-block support makes the specimen density indefinitely readable; a finite subblock has an explicit sampling floor.

Physical refinement: known nearest-neighbor random-SWAP dynamics has exact branch density and detector covariance controlled by the one-particle heat kernel. The finite-rate detector mean has Var≤q(1−q)/r+2χ(L_G^+)aa/(rΔ). For a cycle the continuum coefficient is q(1−q)(n+1)/(6DT), while a fixed-dimensional torus has Green scaling L, log L, constant in dimensions 1,2,≥3. Swap attempts, finite detector calls, state preparation, and geometric implementation are charged separately. With arbitrary controlled swaps, the same one-site detector can route/read the whole block in m calls, acquire its sufficient charge, then simulate the record classically. This is a matched comparator; the twirl protocol has no established total-cost advantage. Autonomous evolution without routing control is a distinct interface.

Unknown ensemble p is a different target. One diagonal product n-spin state has a Bin(n,p) sufficient charge K. All p-independent adaptive quantum records are garblings of K. Exact classical minimax squared-error risk is 1/[4(√n+1)^2], including biased estimators and unlimited recovery time. Recovery with a p-dependent bath would alter the interface by supplying extra information.
