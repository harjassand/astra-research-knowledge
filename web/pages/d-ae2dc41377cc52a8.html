C03_L05 FINAL — LOW-RANK UPDATE OVER BOUNDED-TREEWIDTH SUPPORT
2026-10-07

Result. Given rational-complex F,S,U,V with F=S+UV^T and a supplied
width-t tree-decomposition of the undirected off-diagonal support graph of
S, all coefficients

  c_k(F) = sum_{|I|=|J|=k, I intersect J=empty} |det F[I,J]|^2

are computable exactly in 2^{O(t+r)} poly(n,r,L,L_T) bit operations, with
polynomial-bit rational intermediates. This is an internal theorem candidate;
the general DP has not been independently proof-audited or benchmarked.

Proof route. Schur-augment to M=[[S,U],[-V^T,I_r]], whose minors containing
all auxiliary indices equal the desired minors of F. The chiral determinant
det[[X,M],[-M*,Y]] expands into squared absolute minors of M. At physical
sites apply T(1)=0,T(x)=1,T(y)=z,T(xy)=1; at auxiliary sites apply
T(1)=1,T(x)=T(y)=T(xy)=0. The product yields exactly sum_k c_k(F)z^k.
Its Grassmann representation has four generators per site. A tree-decomposition
of G_S augmented by the r auxiliary vertices has width at most t+r. Standard
bag elimination stores 2^{O(t+r)} exterior states and polynomially many
z-coefficients. The decomposition/order certificate, support graph, and
F=S+UV^T are exactly verifiable. No discovery of S,U,V from F alone is
claimed.

For planar G_S and fixed r, the same route gives 2^{O(sqrt(n))} poly(n,L)
time via a planar separator decomposition. The direct four-port site-local
Pfaffian gadget is impossible: its signature has g0000=g1100=g0011=1 and
violates the four-port matchgate identity. This does not rule out a global
Pfaffian transform or every other planar algorithm.

Finite evidence: check_augmented_lowrank_identity.py passed 99 exact Schur
minor identities and 5,346 paired-permutation terms. augmented_grassmann_dp.py
passed six direct-minor comparisons (n=2..5,r=0..2) for two contractions.
general_support_graph_check.py passed twelve more comparisons at n=6 on star,
cycle, 2x3-grid, and K2,4 sparse supports, with r=0,1,2. Both contractions
matched direct original-minor enumeration, and augmented measured widths were
at most base width+r. These checks are finite bookkeeping validation only.

Attribution and boundary: the chiral contraction is independently compatible
with c03_s01's full-support-width contraction; it differs by exploiting sparse
base width plus rank-r augmentation. c03_s03's cut-rank route may provide
n^{O(t)}2^{O(r)} under a suitable order. c03_l08 covers low-rank F, whereas
this route permits rank(F)=Theta(n) when S itself has full rank. Peer claims
remain attributed; no peer code was copied or executed. Unrestricted parity
counting/sampling, an FPRAS, novelty, and external validation remain open.
