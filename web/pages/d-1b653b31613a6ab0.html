REVISION R1 — EXISTENTIAL BALANCE VERSUS SOFT-EVENT ACQUISITION
7 October 2026. Continues INITIAL; no peer exposure.

Question attacked: does the exact row-balance existence lemma close the balance/initialization gate, or can the natural soft-DPP acquisition interface still fail at a balanced point?

A. EXACT SCALING NORMAL FORM

Write a_ij=g({i,j})>0 and c_ij=log a_ij. Maximize sum_i y_i subject to y_i+y_j<=-c_ij. This is feasible. For n>=3, adding constraints on ij,ik,jk bounds y_i above; hence the finite LP optimum is attained. If every constraint incident to i were slack at an optimum, increasing y_i would improve the objective, so every i has a tight edge. Thus theta_i=exp(y_i) yields theta_i theta_j a_ij<=1 with equality for at least one j at each i: max_j theta_i theta_j a_ij=1.

For F'=D F D, D_ii=theta_i^(-1/2), every complementary minor satisfies
  det F'[I,R\I] = (product_{i in I}D_ii)(product_{j in R\I}D_jj) det F[I,R\I]
                 = (product_{r in R}D_rr) det F[I,R\I].
Therefore Z'_R=(product_{r in R}D_rr^2)Z_R, f'(U)/f'(empty)=theta(U)g(U), and the normalized microscopic full-basis law at U=empty is unchanged. This certifies exact balance existence if all a_ij are available. A basis optimum of the LP has half-integral coefficients in the log inputs on each nonbipartite active component, so the scale has a finite algebraic representation once the rational a_ij are given. This says nothing about discovering those a_ij: each is itself a normalized sum of squared minors.

B. A DISTINCT SOFT-OVERLAP FAILURE SURVIVES EXACT MARGINAL BALANCE

Let P be the 3-cycle permutation, u=2^-L, c=(1-u^2)/(1+u^2), s=2u/(1+u^2), and
  F = [[cP,-sP],[sP,cP]].
This is rational orthogonal, condition number one, has zero diagonal, and every nonzero entry is active in a nonzero hard minor. Under the ordinary size-6 DPP on columns [I,F^T], weight each selection by epsilon^(number of split original pairs), i.e. local filter (1,epsilon,1). The soft partition and hard-pair mass are
  a=c^6+3c^2s^4, b=3c^4s^2+s^6, a+b=1,
  Z_soft=a(6epsilon+2epsilon^3)^2+b[(1+epsilon)^6+(epsilon-1)^6],
  Z_hard=2b.
At uniform fields every column marginal is exactly 1/2 by complement symmetry of the orthogonal-frame DPP and of the split penalty. Nevertheless, for s<<epsilon and epsilon fixed,
  P_soft(hard)=2b/Z_soft ~ s^2/(6epsilon^2),
so with u=2^-L and epsilon=1/64 this probability is Theta(2^-2L), while the input bit length is O(L). The hard-event indicator estimator from IID soft samples has relative variance (1-p)/(mp), requiring m=Omega(1/p) samples for constant relative accuracy. This rules out a polynomial sample guarantee for that particular soft-event estimator even at exact uniform column marginals; it does not rule out importance sampling, a different proposal, or the existence scaling in A, whose ratio target is a different statistic.

C. EXACT FINITE CHECK

I independently enumerated all 924 size-six column sets for L=12 (u=1/4096), epsilon=1/64, using integer-scaled rational F and exact integer determinants. Results in soft_overlap_check.json:
- the exact partition equals the displayed closed form;
- all 12 column marginals equal 1/2 exactly;
- hard-event probability is exactly the listed rational and numerically 0.0001627073733;
- the asymptotic leading estimate s^2/(6 epsilon^2) is 0.0001627603973.
The check supports only this finite identity; the asymptotic is proved by expanding b and Z as s/epsilon tends to zero.

D. INTERFACE CONSEQUENCE

There are now two separate balance statements:
(1) hole ratios r_i=max_j f(ij)/f(empty), which can be normalized to exactly one by DFD if their exact values are known; and
(2) ordinary DPP column marginals, whose uniform value does not control the hard paired event probability.
Neither implies a polynomial-cost acquisition algorithm for the other. In particular, “stationary calibration” is insufficient as a sample-complexity proof unless it includes a lower bound on the actual event/proposal overlap or a variance-controlled estimator. The next decisive gate is to acquire hole ratios (or an alternative balanced proposal) without an oracle for f(ij), then prove the two-replica energy comparison on the same finite-bit interface.
