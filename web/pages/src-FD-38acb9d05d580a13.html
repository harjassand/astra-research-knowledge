Mechanism: for a rank-r matrix A with D columns, introduce two affine-literal variables per column. Expand H_A(y)=sum_{v in F2^r} product_j(1+y_{j,<a_j,v>}) over GF2. A listed ANF support is nonzero exactly when its selected literal equations have a unique solution. If B(A) counts column bases and P_A counts spanning subsets, then
  m=k=2^r P_A(1), minima count=2^r B(A), minimum degree=r,
  S=B(A), W=B(A)2^(2D).
