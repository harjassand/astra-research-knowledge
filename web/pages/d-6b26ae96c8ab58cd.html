# Robust numerical acquisition: cycle reconstruction, Hodge conditioning, and measurement interfaces

**Scout verdict, 7 October 2026.** There is a useful, tractable theorem package here, but no defensible breakthrough claim. Exact Gaussian cycle reconstruction should be completed with (i) an integer-lattice identifiability test, (ii) a Hodge condition number, and (iii) the variance and compilation cost of the actual measurements. I derived a sharp grid inverse bound and an exact four-site measurement distribution. These correct two tempting inferences: a covariance spectral margin does not give a uniform inverse, and a fourth-order signal does not by itself require eighth-order shot cost. The best next allocation is a charged implementation of the normalized charge measurement under a specified access model, compared with direct covariance tomography.

## 1. Internally proved inverse theorem: exact lattice versus robust gap

Let a connected graph have oriented incidence matrix D, E edges, and positive known covariance magnitudes r_e. Write C_ij=r_e u_e, u_e in U(1), with known diagonals. Gauge acts by vertex phases. Let B contain oriented integer cycle rows corresponding to measured holonomies h_f=product_e u_e^(B_fe).

**Exact statement.** Let Z=ker(D) intersect Z^E be the integer cycle lattice and L_B its sublattice generated by B's rows. The measurement ambiguity modulo vertex gauge is

    Hom(Z/L_B, U(1)).

Hence the holonomies identify every connection iff L_B=Z. Full real rank is insufficient: a finite index q leaves q discrete ambiguities. Proof: gauge orbits are precisely characters of Z; measurement restricts such a character to L_B. Smith normal form supplies a finite, charged check. This is standard character/lattice reasoning, not a claimed novel theorem.

**Local quantitative statement.** Equal magnitudes r_e=t simplify the metric. If B spans the real cycle space, its nonzero minimum singular value sigma controls the local covariance Frobenius inverse:

    kappa_h = sqrt(2) t / sigma_min(B).

This constant is sharp to first order. Choose the edge-phase perturbation orthogonal to vertex gradients. Holonomy differentiation is B delta_theta, while covariance variation has Frobenius norm sqrt(2)t ||delta_theta||_2. Singular vectors prove upper and lower bounds. For unequal magnitudes use B diag(r)^(-1) restricted to the weighted complement of the gauge directions; the same singular-value argument applies.

Raw cycle products W_f=p_f h_f, p_f=product_e r_e^(|B_fe|), instead have the weighted Jacobian diag(p) B. Thus query *representation* matters: a compact polynomial invariant can encode the same orbit much less robustly than a normalized holonomy. This is a condition number statement, not yet a sample lower bound for every possible measurement.

**Sharp open square grid.** For an L-by-L array of cells, oriented face rows form an integer cycle basis and

    B B^T = 4 I - A_dual,
    sigma_min(B) = 2 sqrt(2) sin(pi/[2(L+1)]).

Therefore kappa_h=t/[2 sin(pi/[2(L+1)])], asymptotic to tL/pi. For raw four-cycle products it is t^(-3)/[2 sin(pi/[2(L+1)])]. These are exact tangent constants in the stated l2 metrics.

There is also a global bound, with no phase branch promise. Given the componentwise shortest angle differences e between two face holonomy lists, solve beta=B^T(BB^T)^(-1)e. Multiplying the first connection's edges by exp(i beta_e) produces the second holonomies, hence the second connection up to gauge. Since |exp(ix)-1|<=|x|,

    inf_g ||C' - g C g*||_F <= sqrt(2)t ||e||_2/sigma_min(B).

In the maximum-edge metric a simpler gauge puts vertical error at column x equal to a sum of at most L face errors and horizontal error zero. Thus d_infinity<=t L ||e||_infinity. The source's uniform-face-flux boundary argument supplies a matching Omega(tL) lower bound. A fixed covariance margin persists for all phases when t<1/8, since the degree is at most four. This fully resolves the source's missing **geometry-dependent inverse** on this restricted known-magnitude grid; it does not supply noisy observable acquisition or interaction robustness.

## 2. Original cost calculation: four-site charge shots are fourth order

Take the half-filled induced cycle 1–2–3–4–1, all edge magnitudes t<1/8. Gauge the path 1–2–3–4 positive and put flux phi on 4–1. Set

    a = -2 atanh(2t)/t,
    O = i (N-2)[K_23,K_34],
    b = a^2 t^2,
    Y = O/b.

The source's expectation formula gives E[O]=2a^2 t^4 sin(phi). I additionally derive the **entire projective measurement distribution**:

    q = 1/4 - 8t^4(1-cos(phi)),
    Pr(Y=+1) = q/2 + t^2 sin(phi),
    Pr(Y=-1) = q/2 - t^2 sin(phi),
    Pr(Y=0) = 1-q.

Proof: the one-particle commutator has only its 2–4 hopping pair. On that hopping sector n_2+n_4=1, so (N-2)^2=(n_1+n_3-1)^2. Therefore

    Y^2=(1-n_1-n_3+2n_1n_3)(n_2+n_4-2n_2n_4).

Wick occupation moments are principal minors. Expanding gives E[Y^2]=1/2-4t^2-4 det(C); the induced-cycle determinant is 1/16-t^2+2t^4(1-cos(phi)), proving q. The expectation and the spectrum {-1,0,1} determine the probabilities.

Consequences under **supplied ideal projective access to O**:

* Var(O)=a^4 t^4 q-4a^4 t^8 sin^2(phi)=Theta(t^4), while its mean is Theta(t^4).
* Distinguishing phi=+pi/2 from -pi/2 takes Theta(t^(-4)) samples for fixed success probability. Upper: estimate the bounded Y mean, whose separation is 4t^2. Lower: the two outcome laws swap probabilities q/2 +/- t^2, and their KL is 2t^2 log[(q/2+t^2)/(q/2-t^2)]=Theta(t^4).
* This is **not** a universal copy lower bound: a supplied basis reference and direct closing-edge hopping measurement distinguish the same states in Theta(t^(-2)) copies. The ideal modular observable loses to that equally informed comparator unless its access is substantially different.

Thus the fourth-order signal cannot be used to assert t^(-8) shots by silently assigning unit variance. Nor does this calculation make the operator available from spectra or copies: physical measurement construction remains the load-bearing missing operation.

The longer even-cycle formula sharpens the warning. Its nested commutator is just A t^(ell-2) times a phased hopping operator between 2 and ell, where A is the known product of pair coefficients. After dividing by that known factor, the centered-charge observable has mean 2t^2 sin(phi), independent of ell. Its norm is O(ell), so even the crude ideal-projective shot bound is polynomial in ell and t^(-1). A fixed absolute-error expectation oracle for the unnormalized nested commutator has a very different precision cost.

## 3. Original conditional construction: coherent links remove raw moment attenuation

An even stronger supplied interface offers evolution by each pair modular quadratic Hamiltonian on arbitrary probe states. On a half-filled pair,

    k_e = a_e r_e (exp(i theta_e)|i><j| + exp(-i theta_e)|j><i|).

Evolve for tau_e=pi/(2|a_e|r_e). This performs a perfect phase-carrying swap. Prepare one particle coherently in the starting mode and an untouched reference mode. Applying these swaps successively around an ell-cycle returns the starting component to itself with phase i^ell exp(-i sum theta_e); the reference remains unchanged. Two reference interference settings estimate its real and imaginary parts.

With known magnitudes and ideal coherent pair evolution, a holonomy estimate to angular error epsilon and failure probability delta uses O(epsilon^(-2) log(1/delta)) probe preparations, ell pair calls each, and total evolution time O(epsilon^(-2) log(1/delta) sum_e r_e^(-1)) on a fixed covariance margin. Calibration and per-call implementation error must sum to O(epsilon), for example O(epsilon/ell) per edge. This is a polynomial **conditional construction**, with no controlled-unknown-unitary assumption: the extra single-particle reference mode is enough, and scalar modular offsets affect both branches equally.

Fatal interface test: the original source supplies expectations/spectra, not this coherent modular service. Gaussian state preparation does not grant modular evolution. If the service must be learned from copies, charge that learning and compare against direct sparse covariance tomography. Ordinary physical Hamiltonian evolution is also not automatically modular evolution. Wilson-loop interferometry is established prior art, so the construction's value is to prevent an invalid exponential lower-bound transfer between access models.

## Ranked targets and stopping criteria

1. **Complete the grid acquisition theorem, best expected value.** Combine the exact Hodge constant above with the true outcome variance and a concrete implementation of the charge probe; derive total copies, circuit time, and calibration precision for covariance error epsilon. Supplied: labelled sparse support, half filling, magnitude floor/spectral margin, and whatever coherent/measurement service is explicitly admitted. Acquired: magnitudes, complex face holonomies, gauge class. Fatal test: the direct two-point tomography comparator already receives enough control to acquire all edges cheaply. This would be a useful scoped theorem/benchmark, probably incremental rather than a major frontier result.
2. **Optimize robust invariant selection beyond the grid.** Require integral-lattice completeness and maximize the relevant weighted Hodge gap under full measurement costs, not merely minimize invariant degree or count. Cactus graphs with edge-disjoint bounded cycles have a constant gap; grids do not. Coherent long loops can repair conditioning under stronger access, whereas raw products can be exponentially attenuated. Main open technical question: efficient cost-aware integral cycle selection with guaranteed stable inverse. The 2026 cycle-basis work cautions against assuming this optimization is solved.
3. **Robustify L01's exact matrix-oracle learner, lower priority here.** A hidden dimension bound alone cannot support finite-precision exact minimality: an arbitrarily small independent component changes rank while staying below every fixed oracle tolerance. A usable statement needs a task loss and a margin/height/analytic-radius promise. Spectral automata and Hankel conditioning already address much of this; no new robust theorem emerged from this scout. Do not spend a wave merely restating exact detectability with tiny algebraic witnesses.

**Decisive failed route preserved.** My initial idea that the well-conditioned ell-cycle's t^ell invariant forces exponential acquisition was too broad. It is justified for noisy raw moments/principal-minor sampling (and already appears in DPP literature), but a homogeneous full-region eigenvalue oracle sees O(t/ell) flux-dependent shifts, and normalized/coherent measurement access can avoid product attenuation. Proper-subset spectra are exactly blind, but a claim about the whole spectrum must count that stronger information.
