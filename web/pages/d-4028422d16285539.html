## Interface and result

For every integer m≥1 there is an explicitly specified rational-coefficient harmonic polynomial P_m:R³→R of degree at most256m with at least8m³ real nondegenerate critical points. All these retained critical points have Morse index2. Its representation below uses24 complex linear forms; expansion yields real rational coefficients.

The construction is supplied, not learned from an unknown electrostatic field. Critical centers, coefficients, and truncation order are supplied by formulas. The positive-charge compiler is an independent deduction owned by r01, not a lemma of the July Maxwell paper. If that compiler is adopted, these points yield an Ω(n^{3/2}) lower bound; Kintali's prior explicitly states that exponent, so no novelty claim follows.

## 1. Entire harmonic packet

Put k=4m and

    U_k(x,y,z)=cos(kx)cos(ky)cosh(√2 kz)
              +cos(ky)cos(kz)cosh(√2 kx)
              +cos(kz)cos(kx)cosh(√2 ky).

Each summand is harmonic: its second derivatives sum to (−k²−k²+2k²) times the summand. Its exponential decomposition has24 terms of coefficient1/8. Each term exp(w·X) has two coordinate entries ±ik and the remaining entry ±√2 k, so w·w=0 (complex bilinear dot product). Consequently every homogeneous term (w·X)^j/j! is harmonic, including j=0,1 by direct differentiation.

Select the2m cosine zeros

    t_j=(j+1/2)π/k,  j=−m,...,m−1.

They all lie in (−π/4,π/4). At each of the8m³ points p=(t_i,t_j,t_l), all three cosines vanish, so ∇U_k(p)=0. Its Hessian has diagonal zero and offdiagonal entries

    H_xy=k² sin(kx)sin(ky)cosh(√2 kz),
    H_yz=k² sin(ky)sin(kz)cosh(√2 kx),
    H_zx=k² sin(kz)sin(kx)cosh(√2 ky).

If these entries are a,b,c, det H=2abc=2k⁶∏cosh(√2 k p_i)>0. Since trace H=0, it has one positive and two negative eigenvalues: every grid critical point is Morse of index2.

At the selected centers |p_i|<1. Every offdiagonal magnitude is between k² and k²e^{√2 k}; hence ||H||op≤2k²e^{√2 k}. The product of the three singular values is |det H|≥2k⁶, giving

    σ_min(H)≥(k²/2)e^{−2√2 k}≥(k²/2)e^{−3k},
    ||H^{-1}||op≤2k^{−2}e^{3k}.                 (1)

This conditioning bound is deliberately conservative and exponentially small. It is charged in the truncation and perturbation radius below.

## 2. An explicit harmonic polynomial and uniform Taylor bound

Let D=64k=256m and truncate the total-degree Taylor series of U_k at zero through degree D:

    P_m(X)=(1/8)Σ_{24 w} Σ_{j=0}^D (w·X)^j/j!.

This is harmonic by the null-vector calculation. Equivalently truncate each product's cos–cos–cosh power series by total degree. Only even powers of each variable occur; the √2 entries therefore appear with even powers. Since k is integer, this alternate expression proves all coefficients are rational. Subtracting its constant3 has no effect on its critical points and is convenient for shell compilation.

For X∈[−2,2]³,

    |w·X|≤2(2+√2)k<7k.

For any derivative of coordinate order at most2, its coefficient magnitude is at most2k² (k≥1). Differentiation of the exponential Taylor tail shifts its first retained degree down by at most2, so every corresponding entry of the C² tail is at most

    6k² Σ_{j≥D−1}(7k)^j/j!.

Write N=D−1≥63k. Since the ratio of successive terms is at most7k/(N+1)<1/2, and N!≥(N/e)^N,

    Σ_{j≥N}(7k)^j/j!≤2(e·7k/N)^N<2(1/3)^N≤2e^{−63k},

where e<3 and log3>1 suffice. In particular the convenient entrywise bound is

    max_{|α|≤2} sup_[−2,2]³ |∂^α(P_m−U_k)|≤12k²e^{−60k}.     (2)

On the same cube any third coordinate derivative of U_k is bounded by

    3(√2 k)³e^{2√2 k}<9k³e^{3k}.

Converting coordinate entries to the induced trilinear norm gives the safe bound

    ||D³U_k||op≤300k³e^{3k}.                              (3)

## 3. Quantitative persistence in every grid ball

For each center p choose

    r_k=e^{−7k}/(10⁴ k).

These closed Euclidean balls are disjoint because distinct grid points are separated by at leastπ/k. They lie inside[−2,2]³. Let H=D²U_k(p), and consider the frozen-H Newton map

    T_p(X)=X−H^{-1}∇P_m(X).

For X in the ball, equations(1)–(3) give

    ||DT_p(X)||≤||H^{-1}||·(||D²U_k(X)−H||+||D²(P_m−U_k)(X)||)
              ≤0.06e^{−k}+72e^{−57k}<1/4.

The factor3 in the second term converts a3×3 entrywise bound to operator norm. Since ∇U_k(p)=0, the displacement of the center is at most

    ||T_p(p)−p||≤42e^{−57k}≤r_k/2,

using the factor√3 for a vector bound and the elementary inequality840000k e^{−50k}<1 for k≥1. Consequently T_p maps the closed ball into itself and is a contraction there. Banach's theorem gives a unique fixed point, which is a critical point of P_m. The same bound ||I−H^{-1}D²P_m||<1 implies its Hessian is invertible by the Neumann-series criterion.

Along the segment between H and this Hessian, the same perturbation estimate guarantees invertibility. Symmetry then preserves its inertia: each retained critical point has index2. The argument supplies at least8m³ distinct critical points; it does not classify or count all other critical points of P_m.

Thus R(256m)≥8m³, where R(d) denotes the maximum number of real Morse critical points in degree≤d harmonic polynomials. The numerical constant is weak, but the cubic growth and all precision losses are explicit.

## 4. Charge realization interface and costs

r01 independently derives a positive product cubature compiler for every supplied harmonic polynomial P=Σ_{l=1}^dP_l. It uses(d+1)(2d+1) distinct unit-sphere sites and positive strengths, matches its jet through degree d after a small scaleρ, and bounds the rescaled C² tail. This is stronger than a bare existence appeal to Carathéodory: the sites are explicit Gauss–Legendre latitudes and equally spaced azimuths. Its density offset, positivity marginδ and scaleρ must be charged. Root also proposed a direct shell compiler for the entire packet; that is a separate route.

With d=256m, the supplied compiler implies a sequence with n_m=(256m+1)(512m+1)=O(m²) charges and at least8m³ critical points, hence Ω(n_m^{3/2}). Small generic site perturbations can make the entire positive Coulomb potential Morse while preserving these points. The global degree identity m₂−m₁=n−1 then gives total critical count≥16m³−n_m+1 from the retained index2 points; this is an additional consistency consequence, not necessary for the exponent.

The construction is not a practical high-count instance as stated. Exponentially small Morse-ball radii and the compiler's smallρ require high precision. Polynomial coefficients have rational numerator/denominator bit lengths O(d log d), but the complete charge-location/strength precision budget is owned by the compiler and must not be inferred merely from the source count. A finite-precision rational charge instance would require approximating sites and weights with margins determined by the retained Hessians. All such operations are mathematical construction, not structure acquisition.
