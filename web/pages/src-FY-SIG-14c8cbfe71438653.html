# State-dependent exchange with explicit reset

Status: restricted mathematical constructions and proofs, internally checked.
No acquired Li/Na transport kernel, physical antiporter, practical advantage, or
historical novelty is established. Every numerical transition probability below
is synthetic. These arguments extend the incoming static time-sharing question;
they do not validate its additive approximation.

## 1. A reset certificate that includes transient storage

Consider any finite directed multigraph of internal states. Distinguish different
reservoir channels even when they connect the same pair of states. Let D have a
-1 at an edge's tail and +1 at its head. Let z_e be the expected number of events
along directed edge e during an operation. This may arise from a Markov process,
feedback, or a deterministic recorded trajectory; the following accounting only
requires well-defined labels and no omitted transitions.

Then D z = p_end - p_start. A genuinely reset internal distribution gives D z=0.
Expected event flows are nonnegative and a circulation decomposes into directed
cycles: repeatedly follow a positive-flow edge until a vertex repeats, subtract
the smallest flow on that cycle, and continue. A finite graph terminates after
at most the number of initially positive edges, since each subtraction removes
at least one positive edge. Consequently output comes from complete cycles, not
from an unclosed loading excursion.

Let S_e be chloride output into the right reservoir, X_e half the Li-minus-Na
output there, and Q_e the net right-reservoir charge output in elementary-charge
equivalents. Include any additional resource-restoration rows in a matrix A:

    A = [ D ; S^T ; Q^T ; other required restoration rows ].

If X = A^T y for a known finite vector y, then X^T z=0 whenever A z=0. This is
an exact no-exchange certificate for that fully specified transition envelope.
Conversely, X vanishes on the entire signed kernel of A iff X lies in its row
space, by (ker A)^perp = row(A). Failure of the row-space certificate supplies a
signed algebraic direction, not necessarily a nonnegative circulation, a legal
causal schedule, or thermodynamically accessible pump. Those remain separate.

A particularly useful certificate is

    X_e = k S_e + h Q_e + phi(head(e)) - phi(tail(e)).

For a cycle with salt and charge closure, exchange is zero. Without exact reset,

    X_total - k S_total - h Q_total = phi^T(p_end-p_start).

Because both distributions have mass one,

    |phi^T(p_end-p_start)| <= osc(phi) TV(p_end,p_start),

where osc(phi)=max(phi)-min(phi) and TV=one-half the l1 distance. This extends the
incoming fixed-rate collinearity rejection to transient state changes: apparent
exchange may be a state-potential difference that disappears on reset. Multiply
by carrier-site moles when converting probability labels into moles.

Scope: the graph must include membrane loading, electrode chemistry, double-layer
charge, reference leakage, and any other claimed restored auxiliary. Reset of
one measured mean occupation need not imply reset of the full distribution. An
unobserved state can invalidate a proposed certificate or a claimed closure.

## 2. Exact phase composition instead of convex flux averaging

For a specified fixed-reservoir qualification condition, acquire the row-stochastic
kernel P_j of phase j, and its expected output vector r_j conditional on each
initial internal state. A phase includes its actual switching transient and any
reset action assigned to it. Then

    P = P_1 ... P_m,
    r = r_1 + P_1 r_2 + ... + P_1 ... P_(m-1) r_m.

For a periodic starting distribution pi satisfying pi P=pi, output per complete
cycle is pi r. This follows from conditional expectation at each phase boundary.
The same formula applies separately to species, work, elapsed time, degradation
and consumables when those rewards are defined and finite. There is no stationary
flux-additivity assumption.

If a controller uses state-dependent actions, an occupation-measure relaxation
can be written with mu(x,u)>=0:

    sum_u mu(y,u) = sum_(x,u) mu(x,u) P_u(x,y),  sum_(x,u) mu(x,u)=1.

Species and cost expectations are linear in mu. In a fully observed finite model,
mu induces a stationary randomized policy on occupied states. Physical partial
observability, unmeasured history and illegal gate combinations can make this
relaxation unattainable. It must not create an oracle for hidden membrane state.

Finite reservoirs change composition. A kernel qualified at one composition is
not valid indefinitely. Either directly measure complete cycles over the claimed
activity range, or acquire a composition-dependent kernel and include sensing,
retuning, and the finite-volume approximation error. No fixed-reservoir theorem
by itself supplies finite-gradient operation.

## 3. An exact cyclic antiporter outside the frozen-flux convex hull

States are empty (0), Li-bound (1), and Na-bound (2). A controlled phase opens
only one reversible species/reservoir binding edge. Its conditional bound-state
equilibrium fraction is h and its relaxation fraction is k=1/2:

    P(0 -> i) = k h,
    P(i -> 0) = k(1-h),

with complementary stay probabilities and the other species held fixed. These
are finite-time continuous-time two-state kernels: the total on-edge rate times
dwell is ln(2), and the forward/reverse rate ratio is h/(1-h). Each held phase
has zero stationary transport, since it communicates with only one reservoir.
Reducibility of a held phase is allowed; the complete period is irreducible and
aperiodic.

Use four phases:

| Phase | Active binding edge | h |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | empty <-> Li-bound, left reservoir | 3/4 |
| B | empty <-> Li-bound, right reservoir | 1/4 |
| C | empty <-> Na-bound, right reservoir | 3/4 |
| D | empty <-> Na-bound, left reservoir | 1/4 |

At periodic start pi=(7,5,5)/17, the exact sequence is

    (7,5,5)/17 -> (5,7,5)/17 -> (7,5,5)/17
                 -> (5,5,7)/17 -> (7,5,5)/17.

Thus each cycle acquires 2/17 of a carrier-site mole of Li from the left, releases
it to the right, acquires the same Na amount from the right, and releases Na to
the left. The right-reservoir output is (2/17,-2/17,0). Every convex average of
the frozen steady fluxes is zero, but the state-dependent cycle is nonzero.
This is a constructive counterexample to extending a static response-cone
failure into a universal nonlinear no-go.

The complete row-vector period matrix is

    [121/256  5/16  55/256]
    [ 77/256  9/16  35/256]
    [  7/16     0    9/16].

Exact rational calculation in code/check_cycle.py verifies pi P=pi and all
species outputs. It also gives a useful caution: reversing phase chronology
D,C,B,A pumps in the SAME direction, with q=2/19 in its own periodic state.
Swapping reservoir assignments gives q=-2/17. Reversal of chronology is not a
universal mechanism test; opposite useful operation must be measured from
matched starting external states under a genuinely opposite controller.

Physical premises are substantial: exclusive species gates, independently
selectable reservoir access, switchable binding affinity, sufficiently suppressed
leakage, and compatible charge handling during each partial cycle. These have
not been acquired in the gold/alumina ratchet. This construction is an ideal
alternating-access antiporter, a familiar mechanism class, not evidence of a new
physical law or a new operation-level invention.

## 4. Reset preparation and acquired-model error are chargeable

The period matrix above has Dobrushin coefficient kappa=9/16, computed as the
largest total-variation distance between two rows. Therefore

    TV(p P^N, pi) <= (9/16)^N TV(p,pi) <= (9/16)^N.

Preparing the periodic state by cycling consumes N complete cycles and their
work, transfer, losses and lifetime. It is not a free initial condition.

Suppose the true period kernel P_tilde has sup-row TV error at most epsilon_P,
and its corresponding bounded reward differs from r by at most epsilon_r in
sup norm. If pi_tilde is stationary for P_tilde, contraction gives

    TV(pi_tilde,pi) <= epsilon_P/(1-kappa).

Proof: replace pi_tilde P_tilde by pi_tilde P plus its perturbation, apply the
triangle inequality and contraction, and rearrange. Hence

    |pi_tilde r_tilde - pi r|
      <= epsilon_r + osc(r) epsilon_P/(1-kappa).

For Li output r=(1/16,5/16,0), so the bound is epsilon_r+(5/7)epsilon_P. This
requires acquired UNIFORM errors for a physically sufficient state model.
Marginal electrical traces do not provide those errors or certify Markov memory.
Chloride leakage, auxiliary drift and work require their own reward bounds.

## 5. Why changing a voltage is not yet the required gate

In a single-site ideal equilibrium model, let both monovalent bound states have
energies E_i^0+F psi. Then

    p_Li/p_Na = (a_Li/a_Na) exp[-(E_Li^0-E_Na^0)/(RT)].

The common electrical term cancels. Varying psi alone can change total occupancy
without switching equilibrium Li/Na selectivity. Species-dependent kinetic
barriers, hydration, local geometry, or different state coupling could escape
this model, but must be measured. This does not rule out diffusion-selective
flashing ratchets or impose a universal minimum of two external controls.

For a separate finite-state Arrhenius model with rates

    k_(i->j)(t) = g_ij exp[beta E_i(t)], g_ij=g_ji fixed >0,

there is a simple exact no-pumping proof. Define A_i=integral exp(beta E_i)p_i dt.
Integrated edge current is Phi_ij=g_ij(A_i-A_j). Periodic reset implies zero
divergence. Multiplying that relation by A_i and summing gives
sum_(i<j) g_ij(A_i-A_j)^2=0, hence every integrated current is zero. Fixed
energies with variable symmetric conductances also leave the common equilibrium
distribution stationary and give no periodic steady pumping under ergodicity.
These are restricted no-pumping cases from established stochastic-pump theory;
they require neither species indistinguishability nor zero external work.
See Rahav, Horowitz and Jarzynski, PRL 101, 140602 (2008),
https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.0015, and Mandal and Jarzynski (2011),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.2871. Physical voltage control need not keep absolute
barriers fixed, so this proof is not a universal no-go for the source membrane.

## 6. Work at a developed gradient

For symmetric chemical activity ratios rho=a_Li,R/a_Li,L=a_Na,L/a_Na,R>1,
the output chemical work per exchanged mole is 2RT ln(rho). Electrical-potential
terms cancel between equal-valence opposite transfers; their individual
transient charging costs do not disappear.

To maintain h_load=3/4 and h_release=1/4 in the ideal binding model, the required
binding-energy excursion is DeltaE=RT ln(rho)+2RT ln(3). Raise the occupied
species well after loading, lower it after release, and restore all well settings.
The modeled net well-control work is

    W_well = 2 q DeltaE,
    DeltaG_reservoirs = 2 q RT ln(rho),
    W_well - DeltaG_reservoirs = 4 q RT ln(3) > 0.

At rho=11/9, T=298.15 K and q=2/17, this is 1398.65 J per mole of carrier sites
per cycle, of which 117.05 J is chemical output. These are synthetic design
arithmetic, not measured performance. Gate actuation, parasitic charging,
Faradaic side reactions, hydraulics, product isolation, sensing, acquisition,
preparation and replacement are not supplied by this ideal well-work ledger.
In a real operation their costs must be added or measured jointly, with actual
energy recovery and the restored final auxiliary state. The three-state fixture
restores only its modeled binding distribution; it supplies no physical
electrode/regeneration closure.
