1. RECONSTRUCTION OF THE EXACT GENERATOR

Write k_N(m)=((I+m.sigma)/2)^tensor N. For a real unit n, u=n.m,
v=n-u m, r=n cross m, B_n=N u+v.grad, R_n=r.grad.
Differentiating e^(t n.sigma/2) tau e^(t n.sigma/2), including its trace,
gives {n.J,k}=B_n k. Pauli multiplication gives [n.J,k]=i R_n k.
Both differential operators commute with fixed matrix left/right action.
Thus B_n^2 k={F,{F,k}} and -R_n^2 k=[F,[F,k]]. The sum is
2(F^2 k+kF^2), proving
 {F^2,k}/2=(B_n^2-R_n^2)k/4.
The angular MINUS sign is correct and cannot be dropped.

The full coefficient expansion also checks. B_n^2 has zeroth-order term
N^2u^2+N(1-u^2), first-order 2Nu v+(v.grad)v and second-order vv^T.
R_n^2 has first-order (r.grad)r and second-order rr^T.
For C=A+(M+1)I with I<=C<=Lc I, Lc=2M+1,
V_N=[N^2 m^TCm+N(Tr C-m^TCm)]/(4N^(3/2))+N b.m/(2N^(3/4))
is exactly Tr[H k_N(m)], with H=Q_C/N^(3/2)+b.J/N^(3/4).

An additional exact simplification is
 (v.grad)v= -u n+(2u^2-1)m,
 (r.grad)r= u n-m,
so their difference is -2u v. Therefore the drift is exactly
 a_N(m)=(N-1)/(2N^(3/2))(I-mm^T)C m
              +(I-mm^T)b/(2N^(3/4)).                  (A1)
No eigenaxes or eigenvalue gaps are needed in this coefficient interface.
This agrees with the author's unsimplified formula and strengthens its bound.

The diffusion numerator is exactly
 D_C=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T.
For any z, the first term is >=(1-|m|^2)^2|z|^2 and the second <=Lc|m|^2|z|^2.
At r0^2=1/[16(Lc+1)]=1/[32(M+1)], the lower bound is >3/4.
The upper bound can be sharpened to D_C<=Lc I, because I-mm^T is a contraction
on the Bloch ball and the subtracted term is PSD. Its eigenvalues are thus
in [3/4,Lc] on the entire stopped ball. The square root is smooth with an
explicit positive margin; it is not a hidden PSD/gap oracle.

2. THE STOPPED PROCESS AND EXACT QUANTUM DUHAMEL

The SDE dm=a_N dt+sqrt(D_C/(2N^(3/2)))dW has Ito Hessian coefficient
D_C/(4N^(3/2)), precisely the generator above. Extend smooth coefficients
off a neighborhood of the closed ball by a cutoff to apply ordinary global
Lipschitz Picard existence, then stop at its first exit; the stopped law
does not depend on that extension. Freezing both m and its Feynman--Kac
weight at exit preserves positivity and identical-product form trajectorywise.

All kernel derivatives are finite for each N; weights are bounded by e^hN.
The stopped Ito integral is an actual martingale, and its expectation is zero.
Therefore, as an integral identity (derivatives are needed only almost everywhere),
W_t'=E[1_(t<tau) w_t M_H k(m_t)] with M_H X={H,X}/2.
Linearity gives
W_t'-M_H W_t=-E[1_(tau<=t)w_tau M_H k(m_tau)].
There is no discarded boundary distribution: stopped branches remain in W_t
and constitute exactly this residual.

Since e^(uM_H)X=e^(uH/2)Xe^(uH/2), its Hermitian induced trace-norm bound is
e^(u||H||). Also ||M_H k||_1<=||H||, because ||k||_1=1.
For hN=Lc sqrtN/4+Lc/(2sqrtN)+(B/2)N^(1/4), one has ||H||<=hN.
Finite-dimensional Duhamel consequently proves
 ||e^(M_H)rho0'-W_1||_1<=hN e^(2hN)P(tau<=1).
Initial seed replacement costs <=2e^hN pbad. These are the author's exact
raw bounds; my reconstruction found no positivity-of-global-G error.

The target e^(M_H)rho0 is e^K/Zbase since the seed Casimir is central.
Its trace is at least one: in each spin sector scalar Jensen gives
Tr e^(H_j)>=d_j exp(Tr H_j/d_j)>=d_j, because C>=I and linear spins are traceless.
For positive G,W, T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG. The normalization therefore
has no omitted rare-success or partition-function penalty.

3. TAILS AND UNIFORMITY CHECKED WITH EXPLICIT CONSTANTS

4. NEW UNIFORM TRACE-WEIGHT MOMENTS

This lemma concerns the actual stopped SDE law, not the unexecuted compiler.
Let p>=1 be fixed, Tc=Tr C<=3Lc, and N large enough that
L_N=Lc/(2sqrt N)<=1/4. Let Q=sup_(t<=1)|martingale_t| and F=B/(2N^(3/4)).
Gronwall on the stopped path gives
 sup |m_t| <= e^L_N(|m0|+F+Q),
so sup |m_t|^2 <=6(|m0|^2+F^2+Q^2), using e^(2L_N)<=2.
Completing the field square gives
 V_N(m)<= (Lc/4+1)sqrt N |m|^2+Tc/(4sqrt N)+B^2/16.
Define A_p=p(Lc/4+1), beta_p=6A_p and
C_p0=exp[p(Tc/4+B^2/16)+(3/2)A_p B^2].
Then
 w_1^p <= C_p0 exp[beta_p sqrt N(|m0|^2+Q^2)].

Each martingale coordinate has qv<=Lc/(2N^(3/2)); hence
P(Q>u)<=6exp[-u^2 N^(3/2)/(3Lc)]. Integrating that bound gives
 E exp(beta_p sqrt N Q^2)
 <=1+18Lc beta_p/[N-3Lc beta_p]<=7
when N>=6Lc beta_p. Consequently
 E[w_1^p|m0]<=7C_p0 exp(beta_p sqrt N |m0|^2).          (A3)

For the retained/replaced heat seed, m0=tanh(r/2)u or zero, so
sqrt N |m0|^2<=R^2/4 with R=N^(1/4)r. Let K=beta_p/4.
The radial seed is bounded, before its >=Lbase normalization, by
R^2exp[-R^4/768+R^2/24] in R<=2N^(1/4).
Outside, it is bounded by R^2exp[-delta sqrt N R^2/4+R/2].
For sqrt N>=8(K+1)/delta, the latter after tilt e^(KR^2) is at most
e^(1/16)R^2exp(-R^2). Therefore
 E_seed exp(KR^2)
 <=Lbase^-1 [integral R^2exp(-R^4/768+(K+1/24)R^2)dR
                     +e^(1/16)sqrt(pi)/4].
The quartic integral is at most
e^[384(K+1/24)^2]1536^(3/4)/2, using Gamma(3/4)<2.
Combining with (A3) proves E w_1^p=O_(M,B,p)(1), with explicit, deliberately
loose constants and thresholds. This holds for p=2 and higher fixed moments.

The mean weight is >=1-o(1) by the independently reconstructed raw error
and target trace >=1, so it is >=1/2 for sufficiently large N. Self-normalized
importance resampling with R proposals thus has O_(M,B)(R^-1/2) TV error.
There is no necessary exp(sqrt N) proposal count from the trace weights.
All constants remain very large and numeric-bound-dependent.

5. COMPILER BRIDGE AND ITS STILL-OPEN IMPLEMENTATION

Use a C^2 radial cutoff chi=1 at |m|<=3r0/4, chi=0 at |m|>=r0, multiplying
drift, noise, and V. On the annulus one may use the quintic polynomial
1-10t^3+15t^4-6t^5 in the affine coordinate t of |m|^2. D_C is uniformly
positive throughout the support; its square root and derivatives are bounded
by fixed M,B constants and the explicit 3/4 gap. Extend coefficients by zero.
The process stays in the ball (zero boundary coefficients and pathwise uniqueness).
It matches the original stopped law until reaching 3r0/4.
From |m0|<=r0/2, reaching 3r0/4 requires a martingale excursion at least r0/8
once drift <=r0/8. The same maximal tail is exp(-cN^(3/2)). Cauchy-Schwarz
with (A3) bounds the WEIGHTED law difference by exp(-c'N^(3/2)). This removes
the need to resolve an exact numerical hitting time.

Projected Euler with global Lipschitz coefficients is a concrete potential
compiler route. Its path strong L2 error can conservatively be bounded by
C_(M,B)sqrt(dt); potential gradient is O_(M,B)(sqrt N), whereas product-kernel
trace Lipschitz costs at most N|Delta m|/2. Weight differences satisfy
|e^u-e^v|<=(e^u+e^v)|u-v|, and uniform moments transfer the path error into
an O_(M,B)(N sqrt(dt)) averaged state bound. A complete projected-Euler
moment/discretization proof and interval implementation are NOT supplied here.
The plausible conservative budget dt=O(epsilon^2/N^2), R=O(epsilon^-2)
therefore remains a separately stated compiler gate, not a completed runtime theorem.
Finite normals, dyadic selection, square-root margin, elementary evaluations,

6. NEW GROWING-PARAMETER SEPARABILITY COROLLARY

This corollary is mathematical cone proximity, not uniform compiler efficiency.
Allow known bounds M_N,B_N to vary with N. Put Lambda=M_N+1 and Lc=2M_N+1.
Assume
 Lambda^3/sqrt N ->0,  B_N Lambda^2/N^(3/4) ->0.           (A4)
The same stopped construction then obeys, eventually and uniformly over
||A||<=M_N, |b|<=B_N,
 T(rho_N,sigma_N)<=exp[-c N/Lambda^2]                    (A5)
for an absolute positive c.

To verify it rather than silently extending fixed-bound constants:
r0^2=1/(32Lambda), a0>=r0, so
kappa0>=c0/Lambda^2, c0=min(1/786432,delta/4)>0;
kappa1>=1/(16384Lambda^2).
The explicit seed prefactor (A2) is (60/7)e^(Lambda/2+1/12) N^3/4.
The global hN satisfies
hN Lambda^2/N=O(Lambda^3/sqrt N+B_N Lambda^2/N^(3/4))->0.
Also (Lambda+log N)Lambda^2/N->0 under (A4).
The seed drift/temperature thresholds hold because Lambda/sqrt N->0 and
B_N sqrt Lambda/N^(3/4)->0, both implied by (A4).
Thus the initial term is eventually <=exp[-c0 N/(2Lambda^2)]. The stopped
term is eventually <=exp[-N^(3/2)/(32768Lambda^2)], since 2hN and log(6hN)
are negligible there. Absorb their sum into (A5), e.g. take c=c0/4.

In particular fixed anisotropy permits b_N=o(N^(3/4)): every shrinking
per-qubit field is covered by this cone-distance consequence. Anisotropy
may grow as M_N=o(N^(1/6)) subject to the joint field condition. This is a
broader interface than fixed bounded critical parameters, earned from the
explicit certificate. The limiting quartic experiment and finite-bit costs
are NOT asserted uniformly on this growing region.
