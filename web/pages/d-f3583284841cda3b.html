R1 — STRONGLY LOG-CONCAVE SAME-SPECTRUM PAIR WITH THICK PRODUCT SCORES
Worker c09_l04; 2026-10-07. This revision does not modify INITIAL.txt.

PURPOSE

I refined the frozen construction after the authorized cross-phase read. The
frozen tanh-Gaussian example is valid, but its product score marginal does not
have the bounded log-gradient property used by c09_l03. The replacement below
uses Gaussian quantiles to make the product score vector exactly uniform on a
cube. It is still full-support and uniformly strongly log-concave, and now
meets c09_l03's stated joint-posterior thickness hypothesis on the product
side. The shared-scalar side remains singular and fails that hypothesis.

R1 is a derivation and comparison record, not a new immutable checkpoint.
INITIAL.txt and its SHA-256 remain unchanged.

REFINED EXPERIMENTS

Let phi and Phi be the standard normal density and CDF. Put

    g(x)=2 Phi(x)-1,     a=10^-3,     s_i=a exp(-i),
    psi_i(x)=sqrt(2) cos(2 pi i Phi(x)),     i>=1.

Since Phi(Z) is exactly uniform when Z~N(0,1),

    g(Z) ~ Uniform[-1,1],
    E[g(Z)]=0,  E[g(Z)^2]=1/3,
    E[psi_i(Z)]=0,  E[psi_i(Z)psi_j(Z)]=1_{i=j}.

For finite d and ||v||_2<=1 define

    P_v(dx)=product_{i<=d} phi(x_i)[1+s_i v_i g(x_i)] dx,

    r_i=s_i/sqrt(3),
    C_v(dx)=phi(x)[1+sum_{i<=d} r_i v_i psi_i(x)] dx.

Both are normalized analytic densities on all of R^d and R, respectively.
The Fisher Gram matrices at v=0 are exactly the same:

    I_P(0)=I_C(0)=diag(s_i^2/3).

Both families' Fisher metrics stay uniformly comparable to this matrix over
the entire unit parameter ball; details are below.

STRONG LOG-CONCAVITY

For P, g'=2phi and g''=-2x phi, so
||g''||_infinity=2/sqrt(2 pi e)<1. In coordinate i,

    (log p_{v,i})''=-1 + s_i v_i g''/(1+s_i v_i g)
                         -(s_i v_i g'/(1+s_i v_i g))^2
                    <= -1 + a||g''||_infinity/(1-a)
                    < -0.9995.

This is uniform in dimension and v. Each factor is strictly positive since
|s_i v_i g|<=a.

For C write h_v=sum r_i v_i psi_i. By Cauchy-Schwarz,

    ||h_v||_infinity <= sqrt(2)||r||_2
       <= sqrt(2) a/sqrt(3) * sqrt(sum_i exp(-2i))
       < a/3,

using exp(2)>7, hence sum exp(-2i)<1/6. Also direct differentiation gives

    psi_i''=sqrt(2)[-(2 pi i)^2 cos(2 pi i Phi) phi^2
                         +(2 pi i)x sin(2 pi i Phi)phi],
    ||psi_i''||_infinity <=9sqrt(2)i^2.

The power-series identity

    sum_{i>=1} i^4 q^i=q(1+11q+11q^2+q^3)/(1-q)^5

and q=exp(-2)<1/7 give sum i^4 exp(-2i)<70/81<1. Therefore

    ||h_v''||_infinity
       <= [sum_i r_i^2 ||psi_i''||_infinity^2]^(1/2)
       < 3sqrt(6)a < 0.007349.

Since
    (log C_v)''=-1+h_v''/(1+h_v)-(h_v'/(1+h_v))^2,
we have (log C_v)'' < -0.9926 uniformly in v and d. In particular both
experiments have a dimension-independent strong log-concavity constant.

GLOBAL FISHER COMPARABILITY

For P the Fisher matrix is diagonal. Coordinate scores have zero mean under
their own factors, so cross terms vanish; its i-th entry is

    s_i^2 integral phi(x) g(x)^2/[1+s_i v_i g(x)] dx.

Thus it lies between (s_i^2/3)/(1+a) and (s_i^2/3)/(1-a).
For C, the Fisher quadratic form is

    I_C(v)[w,w]=integral phi(x)[sum_i r_i w_i psi_i(x)]^2/[1+h_v(x)] dx.

It is between (1+a/3)^-1 sum_i s_i^2w_i^2/3 and
(1-a/3)^-1 times that same sum. These are global dimension-uniform
comparisons to the exactly matching tangent covariance.

PRODUCT MEMORY LOWER

On v=+e_i and v=-e_i, the P densities relative to the common reference
mu=N(0,1)^d are 1+/-s_i g(x_i). Their equal mixture is mu exactly. For any
blind epsilon-TV reconstruction encoder/decoder, the bounded proper-score
argument used in INITIAL gives

    E_mu[(g(X_i)-g(Y_i))^2] <= 4 epsilon/s_i.

Here U_i=g(X_i) is exactly Uniform[-1,1], so h(U_i)=ln2; the U_i are
independent under mu. Conditional maximum entropy then gives

    I(U_i;g(Y_i),S) >= (1/2) ln[s_i/(2 pi e epsilon)]

whenever the right side is positive. Data processing, the cq chain rule,
independence of the U_i, strong subadditivity, and the dimension-D Holevo
bound sum these inequalities across coordinates even when each coordinate
uses a different decoder POVM. Hence

    log_2 D >= (1/2) sum_{i:s_i>2 pi e epsilon}
                          log_2[s_i/(2 pi e epsilon)].

For d at least ceil(ln(a/(2 pi e epsilon))), this is

    log_2 D >= (ln(1/epsilon))^2/(4 ln 2)-O(ln(1/epsilon)).

This lower applies to arbitrary unassisted quantum/hybrid encoders; all
input-dependent registers count and a shared seed is independent of U.

SHARED-SCALAR POSITIVE CODEC

For C, T=Phi(X) has density

    f_v(t)=1+sqrt(2) sum_i r_i v_i cos(2 pi i t),  t in R/Z,

and
    ||f_v''||_infinity <= M2,
    M2=sqrt(2)(2 pi)^2 (sum_i r_i^2 i^4)^(1/2)
       <= sqrt(2)(2 pi)^2 a/sqrt(3).

For J>=5 let eta_j be the periodic linear hat at j/J. Encode j with
probability eta_j(T), then decode T' from density J eta_j(t') and output
Phi^-1(T'). The joint kernel is K(t,u)=J sum_j eta_j(t)eta_j(u), so it is
positive, Markov, symmetric, and preserves the uniform law. Given output u,
the input t is a mixture of neighboring triangular hats. Their centers
average to u (with the circle lifted locally), and |t-u|<=2/J. The first
displacement moment is zero. Taylor's theorem gives

    ||K f_v-f_v||_infinity <=2M2/J^2,
    TV(K f_v,f_v)<=M2/J^2.

Thus J=max(5,ceil sqrt(M2/epsilon)) labels suffice, so

    B_cl^C(epsilon)<=0.5 log_2(1/epsilon)+O(1),

uniformly in d. This codec is classical, positive, unseeded, reference
preserving, and does not require the encoder to know v.

JOINT-POSTERIOR THICKNESS BOUNDARY

The normalized product scores are Z_i=g(X_i), which are iid uniform on
[-1,1]. Every finite score marginal has density 2^-k and zero coordinate
log-gradient. It satisfies c09_l03's stated joint-posterior thickness
condition with L=0. The C scores are Z_i=psi_i(X); their joint law is
supported on the one-dimensional image

    t -> (sqrt(2)cos(2 pi t),...,sqrt(2)cos(2 pi d t)),

so for d>=2 it has no d-dimensional density and fails that condition.
Therefore strong log-concavity of observation densities alone does not
replace a joint-posterior thickness assumption. This pair is consistent with
c09_l03's conditional theorem and identifies the precise role of that
hypothesis.
