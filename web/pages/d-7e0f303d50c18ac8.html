1. Exact theorem and architecture

Let P_phi and P_psi be the five-qubit rank-one BJSW seed projectors on
fresh wires, with

 phi=|->|00>|10>,
 psi=(|000>-|011>-|101>-|110>)/2 tensor |10>,
 c=<phi,psi>=1/(2sqrt(2)).

For T>=1 define the repeated seed OR projector

 Q_(v,T)=I-(I-P_v)^(tensor T).

After grouping all original data registers before all fresh seed registers,
the complete augmented BMVZ output has the form

 H=(1/2) H_D tensor I
       +(1/4) I tensor Q_(phi,T)+(1/4) I tensor Q_(psi,T),       (1)

where H_D is the COMPLETE amplified original-data Hamiltonian, including
every original color/path clause. Assume H_D>=0 and ker H_D is nonzero.
Let R be its kernel projector. No Pauli, Clifford, tensor-product or
computability property of R is assumed.

THEOREM. There are no a>=0, Clifford unitary U on the logical input plus
a ancillary qubits, diagonal physical Hermitian operator B, and numbers
0<alpha<=beta<infinity such that the isometry

 V|xi>=U(|xi> tensor |0^a>)

has A=V*BV satisfying alpha H<=A<=beta H. This is an instancewise
impossibility: alpha and beta may even depend arbitrarily on the instance.
The conclusion holds with arbitrary diagonal entries, ancilla count,
row degree, coefficient precision and computational cost. In particular,
no constant-gap enforcer C can rescue this architecture, even when a
polynomial-row constant-gap C for ran V is available as in RESULT D.

The same theorem covers fixed pure stabilizer ancillas, since their
preparation can be absorbed into the Clifford U. It covers Clifford mixing
of data with all ancillary registers. It does not cover non-Clifford V,
nondiagonal B, additive-error comparisons, or an encoding allowed to alter
the logical input Hamiltonian by an arbitrary non-Clifford change of basis.

2. Kernel of the complete amplified list

Set W=span{phi,psi}, let J be its orthogonal projector, and set S=I-J.
The seeds are linearly independent, so S has rank 30.

For each j in [T], Q_(v,T)>=P_v^(j), where the latter acts on copy j
and as identity on all other copies. To verify this, diagonalize the
commuting projections P_v^(1),...,P_v^(T): Q is their Boolean OR, which
dominates each input bit. Consequently a vector in the kernel of both
seed OR projections must be annihilated by both seed projectors in EVERY
copy. Conversely, every vector in ran(S^(tensor T)) is killed by both
OR projections. Thus, exactly,

 ker(Q_(phi,T)+Q_(psi,T))=ran(S^(tensor T)).                     (2)

This avoids an unsupported principal-angle assertion about intersections
of large tensor spaces. It remains valid for entangled vectors across
copies, since the single-copy annihilation conditions are operator
conditions and their commuting copywise kernel projectors multiply.

For positive operators, the kernel of a sum is the intersection of their
kernels. In (1) the data and seed summands act on different registers.
Their kernel projections commute. It follows that the kernel projector
of the ENTIRE Hamiltonian, including every hard-data term, is

 P_ker(H)=R tensor S^(tensor T).                                (3)

No hard-data term can cancel this conclusion; positivity forbids
cancellation, and the fresh seed registers carry identity in those terms.

3. An exact anticommuting pair in the full kernel projector

Write X=X_1 tensor I on the five seed wires and
Z=Z_1 Z_2 Z_3 tensor I on the same wires. They anticommute.
The rank-two span projector is obtained by inverting the seed Gram matrix:

 J=[P_phi+P_psi-c|phi><psi|-c|psi><phi|]/(1-c^2).                (4)

The required expectations are

 <phi,X phi>=-1,  <psi,X psi>=0,
 <psi,X phi>=-c,
 <phi,Z phi>=0,   <psi,Z psi>=1,
 <psi,Z phi>=c.

Hence, since c^2=1/8,

 Tr(J X)=(-1+2c^2)/(1-c^2)=-6/7,
 Tr(J Z)=(1-2c^2)/(1-c^2)=6/7,
 Tr(S X)=6/7,                 Tr(S Z)=-6/7.                   (5)

Let the hard-data Hilbert space have dimension d_D. In the unique Pauli
expansion of (3), the coefficients of

 L_X=I_D tensor X^(1) tensor I on the other seed copies,
 L_Z=I_D tensor Z^(1) tensor I on the other seed copies

are, respectively,

 (Tr R/d_D)*(3/112)*(15/16)^(T-1),
 -(Tr R/d_D)*(3/112)*(15/16)^(T-1).                             (6)

Both are nonzero because R is a nonzero projector. The factor 15/16 is
Tr S/32=30/32. L_X and L_Z anticommute. This is a full-list kernel witness,
not the seed Hamiltonian's Pauli-coefficient witness from RESULT E and not
a pair-only substitute for the target instance.

The coefficients become small as T grows. Their exact nonzero values are
sufficient for the exact multiplicative comparison gate; this note makes
no numerical robustness or additive-error impossibility claim.

4. Clifford compression of a diagonal operator

Expand B in physical Z strings. Clifford conjugation sends each string to
a signed tensor-product Pauli on the logical and ancillary registers.
Compression against |0^a> kills a term whose ancillary Pauli contains X or
Y. Every surviving term has only I/Z on its ancillary wires and therefore
produces a signed logical Pauli.

Any two surviving logical Paulis commute. Their physical Z strings commute,
Clifford conjugation preserves commutation, and the surviving ancillary
I/Z strings commute, so the logical commutation sign must be positive.
Thus A=V*BV belongs to a single abelian algebra generated by commuting
logical Paulis. This remains true for arbitrarily many surviving terms
and for arbitrary real diagonal B entries.

Every spectral projection of A belongs to this algebra. In finite
dimension this follows either by simultaneous diagonalization or by a
polynomial interpolating the indicator of a chosen subset of A's finite
spectrum. In particular its kernel projector belongs to the same algebra.
The Pauli support of every element in this algebra is pairwise commuting:
products of its commuting Pauli generators form an orthogonal Pauli basis
for the algebra. Uniqueness of the ambient Pauli expansion forbids extra
anticommuting Pauli coefficients.

Finally, alpha H<=A<=beta H implies A>=0 and ker A=ker H. If H kills a
vector, the upper bound and positivity imply A kills it. If A kills a
vector, the strictly positive lower factor implies H kills it. The kernel
projector of A must therefore equal (3). Its two nonzero anticommuting
Pauli coefficients (6) contradict membership in the abelian algebra.
This proves the theorem.

5. Explicit legitimate WHOLE generated YES instance

A concrete allowed input makes the nonzero-data-kernel premise exact;
perfect completeness of arbitrary QMA YES instances is NOT assumed.
Take a separate five-qubit data register and one base-color clause

 P_0=|00000><00000|,   H_base=P_0,

with that color of weight one. This is a legal rank-one 5-local Clifford
projection. Append the established two fresh seed singleton colors and
use total weights 1/2,1/4,1/4. All three colors are singleton commuting
layers; the data qubits have incidence one and the seed qubits incidence
two. Optional unused data qubits permit any larger prescribed input size.
The layer count is three and the minimum weight is 1/4.

The BMVZ small-layer rule repeats a singleton's own clause. After any
number of amplification iterations, with total repetition T, the exact
COMPLETE list has the three clauses Q_(0,T), Q_(phi,T), Q_(psi,T), with
their weights 1/2,1/4,1/4. No expander self-loop, missing path or unmodified
BJSW propagation-table assertion is needed. Iteration is exact because

 I-[I-Q_(v,r)]^(tensor s)=I-(I-P_v)^(tensor rs)=Q_(v,rs).

Here the data kernel projector is R=(I-P_0)^(tensor T), of rank 31^T.
For example, |10000> in each data copy and |00000> in each seed copy is a
zero-energy product state: the data vector is orthogonal to |00000>, and
the seed vector has clock suffix |00>, orthogonal to both seeds' |10>.
The instance is exactly frustration-free and lies on the YES side of any
nonnegative completeness threshold. Its full kernel has rank 31^T*30^T
and coefficients (6) with Tr R/d_D=(31/32)^T. Both remain nonzero for
every T>=1, including the actual iterated values T=(2t)^ell.

This is an admissible output of the SAME weighted-layer generator applied
to all legal inputs; it is a whole three-color instance, not merely its
two-seed suboperator. A compiler quantified over all those generated
instances is therefore refuted within the Clifford/diagonal architecture.

Membership distinction. A particular QMA-hard reduction image might be
defined to exclude this easy input. The cited sources do not identify that
image with all admissible layered inputs, and we do not claim otherwise.
There are two precise ways to state the family consequence:

 (i) For the legal weighted-layered projector promise class and its BMVZ
 outputs, the explicit instance above suffices. The class is QMA-hard via
 its established hard subclass; individual easy inputs need not be hard.

 (ii) Starting from any selected hard family, include the above easy
 sequence as a tagged, polynomially recognizable additional YES branch
 before applying the SAME seed augmentation and amplification. Hardness
 is preserved by routing every original hard instance through the old
 branch. Locality, degree, weights, input/output size and algebraic-bit
 promises are preserved. This is an explicit enlarged augmented hard
 family, not a claim about membership in an unnamed canonical reduction
 image. The obstruction proves that no Clifford/diagonal compiler works
 for that ENTIRE enlarged hard family.

A family restricted to an unverified reduction image with no known
nonzero-kernel member retains an exact membership gate. Likewise, for an
augmented instance whose data Hamiltonian is strictly positive, (3) is
zero and this kernel proof yields no contradiction. No all-YES statement
and no resolution of QMA versus QMA_1 is inferred.

6. Cost, prior-source and validation ledger

The explicit input uses constant-height rational projectors and constant
weights. The seed span projector S is rational (its entries have fixed
denominator dividing 7 after simplification). Tensor-expression evaluation
uses O(T) local factors and O(T) coefficient bits. No exponentially dense
matrix, approximate zero test or exponentially precise coefficient is
supplied to the compiler for free. The obstruction itself is algebraic and
holds even if such resources were permitted.
