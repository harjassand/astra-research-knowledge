Abstract.

An elementary proof is provided of sharp bounds for the varentropy of
random vectors with log-concave densities, as well as for deviations of the
information content from its mean. These bounds significantly improve on the bounds
obtained by Bobkov and Madiman (Ann. Probab., 39(4):1528–1543, 2011).

Key words and phrases: concentration, information, log-concave, varentropy

[24, 6]. These and other applications of the concentration of information
phenomenon are discussed in [26].
