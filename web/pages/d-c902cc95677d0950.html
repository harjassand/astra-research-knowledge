Strongest independent claim
---------------------------
For every finite chronological list of rational gates F_i=I+s_i A_i in the
following exact class, the rational trace of their product is an all-ones
coefficient of two explicit homogeneous log-concave measures with complete
positive support seeds and an exact coefficient-range bound:

  pair gate: A_e=3 alpha I+alpha(XX+YY)+gamma ZZ,
             alpha>=|gamma|, alpha,gamma in Q;
  field gate: A_v=(b+|c|)I+bX+cZ,
              b>=0, b,c in Q;
  step: s>=0 rational.

The spatial interactions can be any finite loopless multigraph, including a
triangle, with repeated and inhomogeneous gates. The claim is a construction
for rational gate traces. The approximate exp(beta H) algorithm remains
conditional on the imported Chen–Liu counting theorem and on the separate
symmetric-Euler reduction.

Derivation
----------
For a pair gate, with a=1+s(3alpha+gamma), beta=1+s(3alpha-gamma),
c'=2s alpha, retain row variables and complement the column bits. Its
homogeneous quadratic is

  q2=a(x1 x2+x3 x4)+beta(x1 x4+x2 x3)+c'(x1 x3+x2 x4).

Its Hessian eigenvalues are a+beta+c', a-beta-c', -a+beta-c',
-a-beta+c'. The last three equal 2s(gamma-alpha), -2s(gamma+alpha),
-2-4s alpha and are nonpositive. Since q2 is a nonnegative homogeneous
quadratic with at most one positive Hessian eigenvalue, it is log-concave.
The coefficient a of x1 x2 is a positive support seed.

For a field gate set r=b+|c|, u=1+s(r+c), v=1+s(r-c), d=sb. After the same
row/column convention its nonhomogeneous polynomial is q1=d+u x+v y+dxy.
The exact margin is

  uv-d^2=1+2sr+2s^2 b|c|>0.

Introduce two fresh variables z0,z1 and use

  q1_tilde=d z0z1+(u/2)x(z0+z1)+(v/2)y(z0+z1)+dxy.

It is rational, multiaffine, homogeneous of degree two and specializes to q1
at z0=z1=1. Positive rescaling of x,y by sqrt(v/u),sqrt(u/v) gives the
complementary pair weights d,sqrt(uv)/2,sqrt(uv)/2. Their Hessian eigenvalues
are d+sqrt(uv), d-sqrt(uv), -d, -d, so d^2<=uv proves log-concavity. The
square roots are proof-only; all stored coefficients are rational. For d=0,
the lift factors directly as (u x+v y)(z0+z1)/2. A positive seed is
(u/2)xz0.

Let e,f count pair and field gates. Every gate has two physical half-edges per
qubit occurrence, and each physical row output is wired to the next
occurrence's complemented input column. There are W=2e+f wires and 2f dummy
variables, hence N=2W+2f=4(e+f). Let P be the product of local q2 and
q1_tilde factors. Let

  Q=product_{wire {h,h'}}(x_h+x_h') * e_f(z_1,...,z_{2f}).

P and Q each have degree 2(e+f)=N/2. P is a product of homogeneous
log-concave local factors on disjoint variable sets. Q is a product of
partition-matroid and uniform-matroid basis polynomials. In the full
coefficient [x^U](P Q), each wire selects exactly one P endpoint, enforcing
the original row/column equality after column complementation. P then selects
W physical variables and exactly f dummies; summing complementary choices in
e_f substitutes all dummy variables by one and recovers every field gate.
Thus [x^U](P Q)=Tr(product_i F_i), times 2 for each untouched physical qubit.

The mu oracle multiplies exact local coefficient lookups. Its support seed
chooses a positive diagonal monomial at every pair gate and (u/2)xz0 at every
field gate. Its exact range is the product, over local factors, of largest
positive coefficient divided by smallest positive coefficient. The nu oracle
checks one endpoint per wire and exactly f dummies; every positive coefficient
is one. Its seed chooses one fixed endpoint per wire and any fixed f dummies.
Both seeds have size N/2. The empty circuit is evaluated directly because
the imported theorem is stated for nonempty ground sets.
