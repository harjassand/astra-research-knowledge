Primary integrability sources opened directly on 2026-10-08
----------------------------------------------------------
0. N. Shiraishi, “Complete classification of integrability and non-integrability of S=1/2 spin chains with symmetric next-nearest-neighbor interaction,” arXiv:2501.15506v3 (2026-04-10), J. Stat. Phys. 192, 170 (published 2025-11-25). Primary HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15506 . Opened lines 81-83 state the classification; lines 165-170 specify the periodic shift/inversion-symmetric model and require both coupling matrices nonzero; lines 185-197 give Theorem 2.1 and its only global-rotation exceptions. The present periodic bulk has J2_ZZ=V/4 and J1=diag(-J,-J,0), outside the classical ZZ-only and Bethe cross-coupling exceptions for J,V>0. The theorem is about periodic chains and the paper's local-charge notion, not a direct classification of the open finite chain.
1. M. Rigol and L. F. Santos, “Quantum chaos and thermalization in gapped systems,” Phys. Rev. A 82, 011604(R), published 2010-07-09. Official APS source: https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.82.011604 . Opened source lines 77-95 identify finite one-dimensional hard-core bosons, state that NNN repulsive interactions break integrability, and say the evidence uses full exact diagonalization. This is direct finite-size hard-core-boson evidence complementary to the periodic exact classification above.
2. L. F. Santos and M. Rigol, “Localization and the effects of symmetries in the thermalization properties of one-dimensional quantum systems,” arXiv:1006.0729 (submitted 2010-06-03, v3 2013-02-05), journal Phys. Rev. E 82, 031130. Primary e-print: https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.0729 . Opened HTML lines 36-49 specify half-filled hard-core bosons with nearest-neighbor hopping/interaction and NNN interaction, say the latter breaks integrability, and identify full exact diagonalization of symmetry sectors; lines 245-246 reiterate NN integrability and NNN breaking.
3. L. F. Santos and M. Rigol, “Onset of quantum chaos in one-dimensional bosonic and fermionic systems and its relation to thermalization,” Phys. Rev. E 81, 036206, published 2010-03-05. Official APS source: https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.81.036206 . Opened source lines 71-95 say nearest-neighbor-only models are integrable and adding next-nearest-neighbor hopping/interactions may cause onset of chaos, based on full exact diagonalization.

Interpretation restriction: Shiraishi provides an exact periodic-bulk classification in its local-conserved-charge sense, and the Rigol-Santos papers provide finite-size numerical evidence for hard-core bosons. Shiraishi does not directly classify the open finite chain or all possible nonlocal conservation laws. Our archive and free-approximation proofs do not rely on nonintegrability.
