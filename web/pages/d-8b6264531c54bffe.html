1. SCOPE AND STATIONARY OPERATOR
For a permutation-invariant N-qubit initial state, Schur-Weyl decomposition
has the form direct sum_j A_j tensor I_(d_j)/d_j, with spin-sector traces w_j.
In particular every whole-space finite axial Gibbs state has this form, with
magnetic-diagonal A_j. Collective J_+/- acts only on the spin irrep, so the
multiplicity identity and sector weights are preserved.

For nu>0 the stationary spin-j state is
 sigma_j(q)=sum_(r0..K) q^r |j,r-j><j,r-j|/Z_K,
 q=nu/(nu+1), K=2j, Z_K=sum q^r.
For nu0 it is |j,-j><j,-j|. Thus the stationary whole state is
 direct sum_j w_j sigma_j(q) tensor I_(d_j)/d_j.
K0 is a scalar spin irrep and is handled by the same singlet construction.
Arbitrary non-scalar initial multiplicity matrices are NOT covered by this
representation; they are conserved quantum data and cannot be twirled away
without changing the requested state.

Spin coherences do not obstruct this PI stationary formula. For nu>0, the
geometric spin state pi is faithful. If an adjoint spin eigenoperator X has
an imaginary-axis eigenvalue i omega, trace against pi the exact identity
 L^*(X^dagger X)-L^*(X^dagger)X-X^dagger L^*(X)
 =sum_a rate_a [L_a,X]^dagger[L_a,X].
The left trace is zero by stationarity and cancellation of the two eigenvalue
terms. Faithfulness and positive rates imply [J_-,X]=[J_+,X]=0. Irreducibility
of the spin irrep makes X scalar, hence omega0. The fixed adjoint space is
one-dimensional; duality gives the unique spin stationary state pi. Bounded
unital CP evolution excludes a Jordan block at zero, and all other spectral
real parts are negative. Thus the finite-dimensional spin evolution converges
to pi even from coherent A_j. At nu0, excited populations vanish by note19;
positivity |rho_mn|^2<=rho_mm*rho_nn removes every other spin coherence,
giving the unique lowest-weight pure state. No inter-sector coherence remains
to consider for a PI initial state, since distinct multiplicity irreps are
inequivalent. This does not assert a full quantum mixing GAP equal to the
population gap in note20.

2. CANONICAL SINGLET EMBEDDING AND EXACT PERMUTATION TWIRL
Let b=N/2-j, so N=2b+K. Take a fixed canonical pairing of2b sites and form
b pure spin singlets, tensor a symmetric K-qubit spin-j state sigma_sym,K(q).
Every singlet is an SU(2) scalar. The remaining symmetric K sites carry a
single spin-j irrep. Hence this entire canonical operator is supported ONLY
in total spin j. Its embedding into V_j tensor M_j is
 sigma_j(q) tensor |v_j><v_j|
for one fixed unit vector v_j in the multiplicity space. This follows from
the SU(2)-intertwining property: the canonical symmetric irrep is equivalent
to V_j, with a fixed multiplicity vector independent of the magnetic label.

Permutation operators commute with global SU(2). In the j block they act as
I_(V_j) tensor U_(M_j)(pi), and this S_N multiplicity representation is
irreducible. Averaging over all N! permutations gives exactly
 sigma_j(q) tensor I_(d_j)/d_j
by Schur's lemma and preservation of trace. There is no averaging of the spin
state, no leakage into another j, and no omitted multiplicity normalization.
Convexly mixing these twirled constructions with the actual w_j gives the
requested whole stationary state exactly.

3. THE GEOMETRIC SYMMETRIC REST IS FULLY SEPARABLE
The remaining K-qubit state is
 sigma_sym,K(q)=sum_(r0..K)q^r |D_(K,r)><D_(K,r)|/Z_K.
At q0 it is the all-ground product. For0<q<1 it is also exactly full SEP:
the symmetric projector resolution is the uniform coherent product mixture,
and a common local z filter of strength log q produces these geometric
populations. This is the delta0 field case, requiring no nonzero-anisotropy
moment gate. K0 is an empty product.

For a self-contained constructive version, draw x in[0,1] with density
 w_K(x)=[(K+1)/Z_K]*[1-(1-q)x]^K.
Set a=1-(1-q)x and p=q*x/a. Draw a uniform phase phi, and use on every
remaining site the pure qubit
 sqrt(1-p)|down>+exp(i phi)sqrt(p)|up>.
Phase averaging removes all Dicke off-diagonal entries. Its r population is
binomial(K,r)p^r(1-p)^(K-r)=q^r*B_(K,r)(x)/a^K.
Multiplying by w_K(x) and integrating uses the exact beta identity
integral_0^1 B_(K,r)(x)dx=1/(K+1), giving q^r/Z_K exactly.
The density normalizes because integral a^K dx=Z_K/(K+1).

The closed CDF and inverse are
 F_K(x)=[1-(1-(1-q)x)^(K+1)]/[1-q^(K+1)],
 x(U)=[1-(1-U(1-q^(K+1)))^(1/(K+1))]/(1-q).
These are exact-real representation formulas. At q0 simply prepare the
all-ground rest, and at the formal q1 limit use uniform x with p=x. No
finite-bit sampler implementation, conditioned numerical stability or exact
real-oracle access is being presented as acquired here.
