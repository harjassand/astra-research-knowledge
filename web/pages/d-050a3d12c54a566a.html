Main result
-----------
The strongest new result is a compact-rate-box strengthening of the sparse
paired-edge recurrence candidate from Cycle 3. Fix a finite bipartite support
E, fixed finite autocatalytic orders k_ji>=1, and a rectangular rate box with
all lower endpoints positive and all upper endpoints finite. For each
communicating class obtained by fixing isolated-substrate counts, the sparse
network admits one support-corrected exponential V and common C,c>0 with

    L_theta V <= C-cV

for every state and every rate vector in the box. The proof pairs death of a
last local catalyst with the same edge's q-removal term at large substrate
counts. High inactive substrates instead receive negative immigration
switch drift. Catalysts without a high adjacent substrate are controlled by
the strict near-one linear margin when adjacent counts are zero, or by a
reverse falling-factorial tail when at least one bounded neighbor is
positive. This repairs the exact support-switch obstruction without
transferring deterministic ODE permanence to jump chains.

The geometric drift gives physical-time nonexplosion, positive recurrence,
uniform V-moments and active-substrate exponential moments on the rate box.
Combining the common drift with a finite-path, common fixed-step
minorization gives V-geometric convergence with common constants over the
rate box, on the active-coordinate quotient. For classes with isolated
substrates, these claims are classwise and uniform over labels after
projecting out the frozen coordinates. The full-state family of class laws
is not uniformly tight when those labels range without bound.

An explicit rational certificate constructor is also available for this
paired sparse family on supplied positive rational boxes. It constructs a
rational potential and rational thresholds directly from the graph, orders,
and box endpoints; it does not enumerate states. Its claimed polynomial
binary-input bit cost is stated in the leaf report. It is a sufficient
certificate procedure, not a complete recurrence recognizer, and external
proof review remains open. The compiler closes the inherited Cycle 3
rate-box synthesis gap for this exact grammar; a separate in-programme
decidability audit found no fatal flaw in the construction, but this is not
external validation. Detailed proof and review are in
`leaves/sparse_geometric_drift/ROOT_AUDIT.txt`,
`leaves/sparse_switch_adversarial/AUDIT.txt`,
`leaves/sparse_certificate_effectivity/REPORT.txt`, and
`leaves/sparse_certificate_effectivity/DECIDABILITY_AUDIT.txt`.

Evidence and what it establishes
-------------------------------
The exact sparse generator identity was replayed on a second three-catalyst
path graph over 4,096 states. Seven mixed escape regimes were checked at
several scales; at n=16 and n=32 all seven had negative drift. These are
finite formula/diagnostic checks only. The explicit rational certificate
fixture checked 625 states and threshold arithmetic, yielding c=1/352 and
C=92275693/440. A separate compact-box physical-time check evaluated the
drift on 10,404 exact-Fraction states and derived exponential return exponent
23/200 for its chosen fixture. The common-minorization arithmetic also
passed on an eight-state finite set. These checks do not replace the
all-state proofs or the skeleton drift/minorization theorem.

The complete-pair uniform-mixing fixture was independently corrected for
path length and exit-rate bounds and replayed exactly. For the [1,2]^6
one-catalyst fixture it has L_D=643, Lambda_D=25,921,474 and
eta=T^643/(3*643!)>0. The resulting V-geometric rate is valid but extremely
conservative, not a useful numerical mixing-time estimate.

Other Cycle 4 extensions and negative results
---------------------------------------------
Separate fixed-rate all-rate Foster candidates enlarge the complete-pair
family in three directions: arbitrary reversible first-order catalyst
conversions; arbitrary reversible substrate transfers (neutral for an
exponential total-substrate potential); and arbitrary catalytic cross-feed
pairs (controlled by substrate-wise exponentials). The transfer and
cross-feed proofs are separate branches; their arbitrary union is not
proved. A three-species reversible catalyst-mediated substrate isomerization
also has a physical-time recurrence proof, a uniform mean bound
E[A+B+C]<=49, and positive uniform stationary occupation floors on its
stated rate box. All remain internally proved candidates, with no novelty
claim.

No qualifying higher-dimensional weakly reversible transient, null
recurrent, or explosive counterexample was obtained. A one-linkage,
three-species, order-three sparse example is positively recurrent on each
of its classes by an exact finite-resource gate argument. The previously
discovered N77 construction supplies the opposite quantifier warning:
infinitely many recurrent stochastic classes can escape in one deterministic
stoichiometric slice. That claim is source-reported in the prior record;
it is cross-class, not a within-class failure.

An exact four-species weakly reversible extension does refute unrestricted
reuse of the particular support-switch potential above. Add to the complete
paired family (all rates one, k=1) the reversible conversion
`A1+B1 <-> A2+B2` and the directed cycle
`B1+B2 -> A1+B1+B2 -> A1+A2+B1+B2 -> B1+B2`.
The network is complex-balanced at c*=(1,1,1,1), has one irreducible class,
and has stationary law `exp(-4)/(a1! a2! b1! b2!)`; complex-balanced
nonexplosion also holds. For the instantiated candidate

    V=a1+a2 +(6/5)^b1(1+h)+(6/5)^b2(1+h)+2*1_{a1+a2=0},
    h=1_{a1+a2=0},

the exact generator value at x_n=(1,1,n,n) is

    LV(x_n)=(6/5)^n(2n^2-3n/5+4/5)+2n^2+4n > 0,

and tends to positive infinity. The offending jump is a catalyst-deactivation
cycle edge of rate n^2 that turns on both substrate support corrections at
once; its reverse route uses two distinct jumps, so there is no compensating
opposite jump at x_n. This refutes only the unchanged potential for the
broader grammar, not recurrence, nonexplosion, or every possible Lyapunov
function. Full derivation and exact replay are in
`leaves/sparse_geometric_drift/REPORT.txt` and
`leaves/sparse_geometric_drift/validate_cycle4.py`.

Primary-literature boundary
--------------------------
The closest published coverage is Anderson–Cappelletti–Fan–Kim (2025),
Corollary 3.5: its layered catalyzed-birth/death conditions cover the
complex-balanced subfamily, including sparse support and arbitrary finite
k_ji. The condition imposes exact rate equalities and is not all-rate
coverage. The audited general weakly reversible recurrence criteria do not
cover the non-complex-balanced higher-order family: the binary/pure-complex
assumptions fail, and the tier inclusion fails on a reachable
catalyst-free high-substrate ray. Failure of sufficient hypotheses does not
imply nonrecurrence. Meyn–Tweedie Theorem 6.1 is a fixed-process
V-ergodicity theorem; the common parameter bounds here come from the
additional common drift and path minorization. Exact source scope is in
`leaves/sparse_prior_art/REPORT.txt`,
`leaves/sparse_theorem_coverage/REPORT.txt`, and
`leaves/geometric_ergodicity_prior/REPORT.txt`.
