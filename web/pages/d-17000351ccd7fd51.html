HARD-SUPPORT ENERGY DETECTION: EXACT CHOLESKY EXPONENT
2026-10-07. Internally derived and independently audited candidate proof; external correctness and priority unverified.

CONTRACT
Known finite-dimensional H>=0 with unique ground |0>, strictly positive H+ on its orthogonal complement. Known effect 0<E<=I, E|0>=|0>. For r parallel channel inputs, legal states have SUPPORT in P_{r,Q}=1_{sum_i H_i<=Q}; miss is Tr rho E^{tensor r}. This is the pure-null, zero-false-alarm model of N18/N32. Ground-pattern blocks are invariant for E and H; with arbitrary r only at most floor(Q/h_min) excited sites matter. No ancilla-assisted, adaptive, nonzero-false-alarm, unknown-E, preparation-work, or drive/reference-cost claim.

MAIN THEOREM
Choose any H eigenbasis ordered by nondecreasing energy and let E=T^*T be its upper-triangular Cholesky factorization. Set B=T^{-1}, A=B^*B=(TT^*)^{-1}. On the excited block define
  s_H = inf{s>=0: A+ <= exp(s H+)}.
Then, for p_*(Q)=inf over arbitrary r and legal rho of Tr rho E^{tensor r},
  p_*(Q) >= exp(-s_H Q) for every Q,
  lim_{Q->infinity} -log p_*(Q)/Q = s_H.
If E=I, s_H=0. Otherwise s_H is the unique positive solution of
  ||exp(-s H+/2) A+ exp(-s H+/2)|| = 1.
The scalar threshold is continuous and strictly decreasing in s; from H+>=h_min I, its norm falls by at least exp(-h_min delta_s) under an increment delta_s.

FINITE EXACT IDENTITY
T and B only map an energy eigenvector to eigenvectors of no greater energy. Hence T^{tensor r} and B^{tensor r} preserve each P_{r,Q} subspace. For the restrictions T_P,B_P, T_P^{-1}=B_P, and
  (P E^{tensor r} P)|ran(P) = T_P^* T_P,
  lambda_min((P E^{tensor r} P)|ran(P)) = ||B^{tensor r}P||^{-2}.
The corresponding maximizing-norm state can be mapped through B into a legal minimizing-miss state. This is why converting a minimum-effect problem to a maximum-norm problem avoids the exponential leakage obstructing a direct H-typical projection of the N18 expected-cost construction.

CONVERSE PROOF
A<=exp(s_H H) implies A^{tensor r}<=exp(s_H sum H_i) by tensor order. Restricted to P_{r,Q}, its maximum eigenvalue is <=exp(s_H Q). Apply the finite exact identity. Ground A=1 does not change the inequality.

ACHIEVABILITY PROOF (CONSTRUCTIVE)
For E!=I choose a unit vector w in the excited sector satisfying Cw=w, where C=exp(-s_H H/2) A exp(-s_H H/2)<=I. Write h=<w,Hw>>0 and delta_n=n^{-1/4}. Let Pi_n project onto the Hsum window [n(h-delta_n),n(h+delta_n)], and let psi_n=Pi_n w^{tensor n}/||Pi_n w^{tensor n}||. Scalar iid concentration in the H eigenbasis shows psi_n approaches w^{tensor n} in norm. Since ||C^{tensor n}||<=1 and w^{tensor n} is an eigenvector with eigenvalue1,
  d_n=<psi_n,C^{tensor n}psi_n> -> 1.
Put D=exp(-s_H H/2), M=B D, and
  chi_n=M^{tensor n}psi_n/sqrt(d_n).
M^*M=C<=I, so M is a legitimate contraction/filter. As B lowers energy and D preserves it, chi_n has support in Hsum<=n(h+delta_n). Moreover T^{tensor n} chi_n=D^{tensor n}psi_n/sqrt(d_n), so
  <chi_n,E^{tensor n}chi_n>
    = <psi_n,exp(-s_H Hsum)psi_n>/d_n
    <= exp[-s_H n(h-delta_n)]/d_n.
Thus rate divided by hard ceiling tends s_H. For all Q, choose n with n(h+delta_n)<=Q and n h/Q->1, or repeat an arbitrarily near-optimal finite block; the every-Q limit follows. The H-window preparation succeeds with probability1-o(1), and the subsequent local product filter M^{tensor n} succeeds with probability d_n=1-o(1). These are state-preparation operations, not free thermodynamic work.

DEGENERACY / BASIS INVARIANCE
Use energy blocks rather than a particular ordering within a degenerate eigenspace. Any two energy-block-upper factors T1,T2 satisfying E=Tj^*Tj obey T2=U T1 for a block-upper unitary U; a block-upper unitary is block diagonal and commutes with H. Thus B2=B1 U^*, A2=U A1 U^*, and the threshold inequality is unchanged. Changing the H eigenbasis within equal-energy blocks is handled by the same argument. The formula is physically basis-invariant despite its Cholesky presentation.

EXACT-TYPE ACHIEVABILITY AND QUANTITATIVE POLYNOMIAL PREFATOR
The variational auditor supplied a stronger alternative avoiding typical windows. Write w in the H eigenbasis, p_j=|w_j|^2, restricting to occupied indices with p_j>0, and let j0 have the lowest occupied energy. Take k_j=floor(n p_j) for j!=j0 and k_j0=n-sum_{j!=j0}k_j. The exact type energy q_n=sum_j k_j h_j satisfies
  nh-K<=q_n<=nh, K=sum_{j!=j0}(h_j-h_j0).
Let eta_n=Pi_type(k)w^{tensor n}/sqrt(p_type), an exact-energy Dicke vector. Its type probability is p_type=(n!/(prod k_j!))prod p_j^{k_j}; Stirling or the elementary method-of-types bound gives p_type>=c(n+1)^(-m), where m is the number of occupied indices and c>0 is fixed. Because C>=|w><w|,
  <eta_n,C^{tensor n}eta_n> >= p_type.
Since eta_n has exact energy q_n,
  ||B^{tensor n}eta_n||^2=exp(s_H q_n)<eta_n,C^{tensor n}eta_n>
    >= exp(s_H q_n)p_type.
Then chi_n=B^{tensor n}eta_n/||B^{tensor n}eta_n|| is legal at q_n and has miss<=exp(-s_H q_n)/p_type. Taking n=floor(Q/h) yields q_n<=Q and Q-q_n<=h+K, so for fixed E,H
  exp(-s_H Q)<=p_*(Q)<=C_0(Q+1)^m exp(-s_H Q).
In particular, 0<=s_H Q-[-log p_*(Q)]<=m log(Q+1)+O(1). The least required hard budget for miss<=epsilon is
  Q_epsilon=(log(1/epsilon))/s_H+O(loglog(1/epsilon)),
when E!=I. Exact-type projection can have polynomially small success; the window-filter construction above instead has success1-o(1). Input-only energy is the constrained resource in both constructions.

EXACT SEPARABLE COMPARATOR
For every positive distinct eigenvalue h of H, let E_h=(P_{H<=h} E P_{H<=h})|ran(P_{H<=h}), and define
  eta_H=max_{h>0} [-log lambda_min(E_h)]/h.
This is the exact arbitrary-use hard-support exponent for ALL separable inputs, including unequal products and correlated mixtures. A pure product has max support energy equal to the sum of the max energies h_i in each local factor; legal support means sum h_i<=Q. Each local miss>=lambda_min(E_{h_i})>=exp(-eta_H h_i), hence product miss>=exp(-eta_H Q). In a separable mixture with positive coefficients, every product component is legal: zero weight outside a subspace in a positive operator sum forces zero outside for each summand. Ground-only factors cost0 and miss1. Repeating a maximizing local state attains eta_H asymptotically.
Consequently, hard entanglement advantage occurs IFF A+ is NOT <=exp(eta_H H+). This gives a single-matrix, finite-dimensional exact criterion; commuting E,H yield no advantage.

SPECIAL CASE: GROUND PLUS TWO DISTINCT EXCITED ENERGIES
If H+=diag(h1,h2),0<h1<h2, and E+=[[a,c],[c*,b]], hard entanglement advantage occurs iff c!=0. Proof: if eta_H comes from the lower cutoff, exp(eta_H h1)=1/a=A11, while A12!=0, making exp(eta_H H)-A non-PSD. If eta_H comes from full E+, exp(eta_H h2)=1/lambda_min(E)=lambda_max(A). A top eigenvector has nonzero low-energy component when c!=0, so its quadratic form in exp(eta_H H)-A is negative. The diagonal case commutes and attains the separable rate. Noncommutation can still fail to give advantage in higher excited dimension when a stronger commuting sector determines the overall exponent.

SHARP DIMENSION BOUND FOR HARD ENTANGLEMENT GAIN
Let m=dim(H+), eta=eta_H>0. Then
  1<=s_H/eta_H<=min{m,kappa(H+)}.
The dimension upper bound is new in this derivation and differs structurally from expected-cost gain at fixed qutrit dimension. Proof: E<=I gives T^*T<=I, hence TT^*<=I and A>=I. In an ordered excited basis, the leading i-by-i block of A is B_i^*B_i, where B_i is the inverse Cholesky factor of the leading compression E_i. Therefore
  A_ii <=||B_i||^2=1/lambda_min(E_i)
    <=1/lambda_min(E_{h_i})<=exp(eta h_i).
Set K=A-I>=0 and D=exp(m eta H+)-I>0. A positive semidefinite matrix has norm bounded by trace, so
  ||D^{-1/2}K D^{-1/2}||
    <=sum_i K_ii/D_ii
    <=sum_i [exp(eta h_i)-1]/[exp(m eta h_i)-1]
    <=sum_i(1/m)=1.
Thus A<=exp(m eta H+), proving s_H<=m eta. The kappa bound follows from s_H<=alpha<=||-logE+||/h_min and eta_H>=||-logE+||/h_max (the full excited cutoff is an allowed separable state).

STRONGER: THE NUMBER OF DISTINCT POSITIVE ENERGY LEVELS
Let k be the number of DISTINCT positive eigenvalues of H. Then
  1<=s_H/eta_H<=min{k,kappa(H+)}.
This includes arbitrary energy-level degeneracies. Indeed, with energy spectral blocks indexed by j, the leading-prefix identity implies
  K_jj=(A-I)_jj<=[exp(eta h_j)-1]I_j.
For every positive block matrix K, the k-block pinching inequality is K<=k blockdiag(K_jj): it follows directly by applying Cauchy-Schwarz to cross block quadratic forms, then (sum_j sqrt(a_j))^2<=k sum_j a_j. Therefore
  K<=k[exp(eta H+)-I]<=exp(k eta H+)-I,
where the last scalar inequality is k(t-1)<=t^k-1 for t>=1. Hence A<=exp(k eta H+), proving the sharper bound. When k>1 and E!=I, the final scalar inequality is strict on every positive energy block, so s_H<k eta_H for each fixed model; k is a supremum. When k=1, E and H commute and s_H=eta_H exactly. The hierarchy family below proves that the supremum at k positive energy levels is exactly k. Degenerate extra excited dimensions can be added with E=I without changing the family rates.

SHARPNESS OF THE DIMENSION BOUND
For any m>=2, R>1, take h_j=R^{j-1}, h_0=0, h_m=R^{m-1}, v_j=sqrt((h_j-h_{j-1})/h_m), and
  E+=exp(-tau |v><v|)=I-(1-exp(-tau))|v><v|, tau>0.
Each cutoff at h_j has minimum effect eigenvalue1-(1-exp(-tau))h_j/h_m. Since x->[-log(1-c x)]/x is increasing for c in(0,1), eta_H=tau/h_m exactly, attained at the full excited cutoff.
As tau->0, energy-ordered Cholesky gives A(tau)=I+tau |v><v|+O(tau^2). Hence
  s_H(tau)/tau ->lambda_max(H+^{-1/2}|v><v|H+^{-1/2})
    =sum_j |v_j|^2/h_j
    =[m-(m-1)/R]/h_m.
For justification, for any t larger (smaller) than this generalized eigenvalue, exp(t tau H+)-A(tau) is strictly positive (has a negative direction) for all sufficiently small tau. Therefore
  lim_{tau->0}s_H/eta_H=m-(m-1)/R,
which tends m as R->infinity. The supremum of hard entanglement gain at fixed input dimension d=m+1 is exactly d-1. Near-maximal gain requires hierarchical positive energies in this construction; it does not violate fixed-kappa constraints. The effects approach I, so unbounded effect dynamic range is not needed for the sharp dimension bound.

  s_H=log((1+sqrt17)/2)=0.94061364210720876236...,

CALIBRATION ROBUSTNESS WITH PURE NULL PRESERVED
If (1-delta)E+<=E'+<=(1+delta)E+, 0<=delta<1, and E'|0>=|0>, then n active sites change log miss by at most n[-log(1-delta)]. Since n<=Q/h_min,
  |s_H(E')-s_H(E)| <=[-log(1-delta)]/h_min,
  |eta_H(E')-eta_H(E)| <=[-log(1-delta)]/h_min.
If ||E'-E||<=epsilon<lambda_min(E+), take delta=epsilon/lambda_min(E+). For N32 a sufficient asymptotic advantage-preserving norm margin is
  epsilon < (1/4)[1-exp(-(s_H-log2)/2)]≈0.02909611728,
provided E' remains an effect, full rank, and preserves the pure-null ground. This does not address nonzero false alarms or perturbations breaking pure-null structure.
