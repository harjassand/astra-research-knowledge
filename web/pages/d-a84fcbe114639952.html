Zero-temperature Dicke SEP results: close method, different generator
---------------------------------------------------------------------
N. S. Bassler, “Absence of Entanglement Growth in Dicke Superradiance,”
arXiv:2504.13646v3 (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13646, proves all-N
separability preservation for initially separable, symmetric, Dicke-diagonal
states under the one-way collective spontaneous-emission generator
Gamma D[J_-]. The state remains in the maximal-spin symmetric sector, and
separability is equivalent to a truncated Hausdorff moment representation
of the Dicke populations as mixtures of phase-averaged identical spin-coherent
products. The proof advances the moment Hankel conditions under small time
steps and composes those steps. The theorem is not the thermal two-way
generator: the raising channel nu D[J_+] is absent, and the population
generator is a one-way triangular cascade. The source contains no thermal,
temperature, Fokker–Planck, or diffusion treatment (full-text checks for
those terms found no occurrences).

Bassler's cited companion, P. Rosario et al., “Unraveling Dicke Superradiant
Decay with Separable Coherent Spin States,” PRL 135, 133602 (2025),
arXiv:2504.13418, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13418, likewise gives a positive
coherent-spin-state mixture for ideal decay from a fully inverted state. Its
stated scope is the zero-temperature Dicke decay; it does not provide the
all-N thermal two-jump/positive-diffusion antecedent sought here.

Closest inspected thermal all-N results: state law, not SEP-flow theorem
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Latune, Sinayskiy, and Petruccione, New J. Phys. 22, 083049 (2020),
arXiv:2004.00032, https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.00032, explicitly treats
collective thermal J_-/J_+ jumps for arbitrary ensemble size. Its sector
formula gives the exact thermal stationary truncated-geometric distribution
within each fixed-J multiplicity sector, with conserved weights inherited
from the initial state. The maximally mixed input has no cross-multiplicity
coherences, so it falls within that formula. This is a direct prior for the
thermal generator and its stationary sector law, not for all-time separability:
the inspected paper gives no Fokker–Planck equation, positive coherent-state
path representation, or SEP-preservation claim. In particular, its
stationary-state formula does not supply the missing two-jump infinitesimal
invariance argument.

Wood, Borneman, and Cory, PRL 112, 050501 (2014), arXiv:1305.1029,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.1029, independently give exact per-J thermal
Dicke stationary populations for a cavity-mediated collective jump model.
Their treatment is an all-J birth–death population law and cooling analysis;
the inspected result contains no separability or positive-Fokker–Planck
theorem.

Correction: thermal symmetric-Dicke SEP preservation follows by composition
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the same maximal-spin symmetric Dicke sector and the same diagonal-state
domain as Bassler's theorem, all-time SEP preservation for the warm generator
is already logically implied by that zero-temperature result. Let
U=X^{\otimes N}. Since U is a product of local unitaries, it preserves SEP;
it maps the symmetric Dicke basis vector with k excitations to the one with
N-k excitations and satisfies U J_- U^\dagger=J_+. Therefore

  D[J_+](rho) = U D[J_-](U^\dagger rho U) U^\dagger.

Conjugating Bassler's SEP-preserving zero-temperature semigroup by U proves
that exp(t Gamma nu D[J_+]) preserves SEP on that domain for every nu>=0:
spin flip maps a separable symmetric-Dicke-diagonal input to another such
input, and maps the resulting separable output back. Bassler's result also
directly gives preservation for exp(t Gamma (nu+1)D[J_-]), since this is
only a nonnegative rescaling of time.

In finite dimension, the Lie–Trotter product formula gives

  exp(t Gamma[(nu+1)D[J_-]+nu D[J_+]])
   = lim_{r->infinity}
       (exp(t Gamma (nu+1)D[J_-]/r)
        exp(t Gamma nu D[J_+]/r))^r.

Every finite-r composition maps the specified SEP state domain into itself.
The normalized finite-dimensional SEP set is closed, so its limit is also
separable. Thus the all-N warm symmetric-maximal-spin Dicke trajectory
preservation theorem is not new relative to Bassler, even though Bassler's
paper and its Rosario companion state and prove only the one-way zero-T
case. What remains distinct in c07_s02 is any explicit positive
Fokker–Planck/coherent-measure evolution, a quantitative path construction,
or a result outside this implication's hypotheses. This argument does not
extend by itself to the whole Hilbert space with lower-J sectors or to
initial states outside the SEP symmetric-Dicke diagonal domain. The previous
paragraph's “no exact combined statement” should be read only as “no explicit
two-jump Fokker–Planck source was found,” not as absence of the warm
separability-preservation theorem.
