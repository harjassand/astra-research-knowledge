Independent derivation before any Cycle08 proof exposure.
Notation, legal completion and positive generator convention are as in
BLIND_BASELINE_FROZEN_v1.txt. This is an exact finite characterization
for the stated fixed-first-noise class, not a proof that its gate always
succeeds and not a global nonlinear regularity theorem.

1. EXPLICIT ENTROPY COEFFICIENTS

Fix faithful s,q, D=s-q, Delta=s^2-q^2, ell=log(s^2)-log(q^2), and a
Hermitian original weighted noise B. Let d=q^(1/2),
K_1=s^(1/2)B s^(-1/2), C_1=s^(1/2)V_s(B)s^(-1/2), and Herm(A)=(A+A^*)/2.
Define the state-dependent Hermitian matrices
 R_E=Lyap_q^(-1)(D^2),
 R_J=Lyap_q^(-1)[Herm(d ell Delta d^(-1))],
 N_E=DBD,
 N_J=Herm(d ell K_1 Delta d^(-1)),
 A_E=Tr[D^2V_s(B)]/2,
 A_J=Re Tr[C_1 Delta ell]/2.
Then for every Hermitian second noise C the EXACT physical forms are
 E(C)=A_E+Tr[R_E C q C]-Tr[N_E C],
 J(C)=A_J+Tr[R_J C q C]-Tr[N_J C].

Proof for J. Its second drift term is
 Re Tr[Delta d^(-1)V_q(C)d ell]/2
 =Tr[V_q(C) Herm(d ell Delta d^(-1))]/2
 =Tr[R_J C q C],
using self-adjointness of Lyap_q^(-1) and V_q(C)=2Lyap_q^(-1)(CqC).
Its cross noise term is
 Re Tr[K_1 Delta d^(-1)C d ell]
 =Tr[C Herm(d ell K_1 Delta d^(-1))].
The first drift gives A_J. The energy calculation is the companion
baseline's direct cross action. No commutativity is assumed.

2. EXACT POSITIVITY AND SCHUR COMPLEMENTS FROM LOCAL PI

Apply the supplied universal local pi theorem to the legal completion
with ZERO first noise and arbitrary Hermitian second C. Its forms have
no constants or linear terms. Thus
Tr[(R_J-pi R_E)CqC]>=0 for every Hermitian C.
Choosing C=vv^* gives (v^*qv)(v^*(R_J-pi R_E)v)>=0, so
 R_J>=pi R_E>=0.
This matrix inequality is an exact consequence of the supplied theorem,
not an unproved positivity assumption on the raw noncommutative product.
In fact it holds for arbitrary positive definite s,q, without individual
trace normalization: use Omega=diag(s^2,q^2)/Z and offdiagonal
Y=D/sqrt(Z), Z=Tr(s^2)+Tr(q^2). Both Hessians acquire the common factor
2/Z, which cancels in the rank-one argument.

On the real HS space of Hermitian C set
 M_E(C)=q C R_E+R_E C q,
 M_J(C)=q C R_J+R_J C q.
Both are self-adjoint PSD, with M_J>=pi M_E. For kappa<=pi, write
 M_kappa=M_J-kappa M_E,
 N_kappa=N_J-kappa N_E,
 A_kappa=A_J-kappa A_E.
The same local theorem yields the full quadratic positivity
 A_kappa+(1/2)<C,M_kappa C>-<N_kappa,C>>=0
for every C (for kappa<0 this also follows by adding nonnegative energy).
Therefore N_kappa belongs to range(M_kappa), and the exact Schur bound is
 A_kappa-(1/2)<N_kappa,M_kappa^+N_kappa>>=0.
The minimum is achieved at C=M_kappa^+N_kappa modulo ker(M_kappa).
This gives exact optimized cross-deficit comparisons for every kappa<=pi.

Likewise J_min=A_J-(1/2)<N_J,M_J^+N_J>>=0 and
E_min=A_E-(1/2)<N_E,M_E^+N_E>>=0. These separate minima alone have no
positive original-energy guarantee, as the baseline collapse family proves.

3. JOINT COMPARISON: EXACT ELLIPSOID MAXIMUM

Assume R_E>0, as holds when D is invertible. Then M_E>0 and M_J>0.
All inverses below are ordinary finite-dimensional positive inverses.
For a fixed budget J_budget (in the desired transfer, J_budget=J_s(rho)),
put
 C_J=M_J^(-1)N_J,
 J_min=A_J-(1/2)<N_J,M_J^(-1)N_J>,
 E_J=A_E+(1/2)<C_J,M_E C_J>-<N_E,C_J>,
 T=M_J^(-1/2)M_E M_J^(-1/2),
 g=M_J^(-1/2)(M_E C_J-N_E).
Here 0<T<=(1/pi)I, and all vectors/operators act on the REAL HS space.
The exact coordinate Z=M_J^(1/2)(C-C_J) gives
 J(C)=J_min+||Z||^2/2,
 E(C)=E_J+<g,Z>+<Z,TZ>/2.

If J_budget<J_min there is no entropy-budget-feasible completion in
this class. Otherwise let R=sqrt(2(J_budget-J_min)). The maximum energy
of ALL such second-noise completions is exactly
 E_max=E_J+max_(||Z||<=R)[<g,Z>+<Z,TZ>/2].
The ball is compact, so this is an attained finite exact maximum.
For R=0 it is E_J. For R>0 it can be evaluated by the following spectral
secular formula, including its hard case; no optimizer is supplied as
an unverified mathematical premise.

Let tau be the largest eigenvalue of T. Usually choose the unique mu>tau
such that ||(mu I-T)^(-1)g||=R and set Z_*=(mu I-T)^(-1)g.
This case applies if g has a nonzero tau-eigenspace component, or if
g is orthogonal to that eigenspace but
||(tau I-T)^+g||>R. Monotone decrease of the displayed norm from its
boundary limit to0 proves existence/uniqueness.
In the remaining hard case, g is orthogonal to the top eigenspace and
||(tau I-T)^+g||<=R. Set mu=tau and
Z_*=(tau I-T)^+g+Z_top,
where Z_top belongs to that eigenspace and has squared norm
R^2-||(tau I-T)^+g||^2. Thus ||Z_*||=R and (mu I-T)Z_*=g.
This includes g=0, where a top eigenvector of norm R suffices.

To verify global maximality, for any ||Z||<=R a direct completion gives
 q(Z_*)-q(Z)
 =(1/2)<Z_*-Z,(mu I-T)(Z_*-Z)>
    +(mu/2)(R^2-||Z||^2)>=0,
q(Z)=<g,Z>+<Z,TZ>/2. Here mu>=tau>0. Hence
E_max=E_J+q(Z_*) is the exact optimum. The desired second noise is
C=C_J+M_J^(-1/2)Z_*, still Hermitian and still a legal stationary
completion by construction.

4. EXACT SCOPED TRANSFER CRITERION; MISSING UNIVERSAL LEMMA

For c>0 and positive original E_s(rho), the following are NECESSARY AND
SUFFICIENT for a completion in this fixed-first-noise class to satisfy
both sufficient comparison inequalities
 J(C)<=J_s(rho), E(C)>=(c/pi)E_s(rho):
 (i) J_s(rho)>=J_min,
 (ii) E_max>=(c/pi)E_s(rho), with budget J_s(rho).
Necessity follows from the exact constrained maximum; sufficiency follows
from its attaining second noise. Combined with the supplied local pi
theorem they imply the original nonlinear J_s(rho)>=cE_s(rho).
For c=2 the threshold is2/pi; for c=pi it is1.

Conversely, failure of either explicit condition is a precise obstruction
to THIS comparison-based completion class for that input. It does not
refute the original nonlinear inequality, nor an encoding that changes
the first noise list, uses identity-gauge freedom, transfers flags or
changes the stationary tangent. With multiple fixed first noises, use
their direct-sum real HS space and sum the constants; the same finite
quadratic/ellipsoid argument applies independently of individual labels.

No universal proof of conditions(i)-(ii) is established here. Claiming
their success merely because the original nonlinear inequality is
expected would be circular. The exact criterion identifies the missing
lemma and charges all state-dependent inverses/eigenvectors/noise data.
No efficiency, robustness or historical priority is asserted.

5. SINGULAR SECANTS: EXACT SUPPORT REDUCTION

The R_E>0 assumption in Section3 can be removed. If D=0 the target and
completion forms are all0 and there is no nontrivial transfer. Otherwise
M_E is nonzero, since M_E(I)=qR_E+R_Eq=D^2. The bounds and quadratic
positivity above give
 ker M_J subset ker M_E,
 N_J in range M_J, N_E in range M_E subset range M_J.
Both J and E are therefore independent of any component of C in ker M_J.
Restrict the real HS space to range M_J, replace M_J^(-1) by M_J^+
and M_J^(-1/2) by M_J^(+1/2), and keep all other formulas. On this
space M_J is positive definite, and 0<=T<=(1/pi)I is nonzero, so its
largest eigenvalue tau is still positive. The exact ball maximum and
secular/hard-case proof apply unchanged. This establishes the joint
criterion for every nonzero secant, including singular D. Extra null
second-noise components are irrelevant to both forms and can be set0.
