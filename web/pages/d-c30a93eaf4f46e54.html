Targeted primary-source check: stationary Hessian comparison — 2026-10-09

Question and scope
------------------
Check whether primary literature already states the universal stationary
quadratic inequality J_2 >= pi E_2 for finite faithful KMS-symmetric
generators, or supplies a decisive counterexample in the exact target form.
The target coordinates are supplied by the parent: for a physical Hermitian
tangent Z, write Z=sY+Ys, s=sqrt(sigma), so in a sigma eigenbasis

    Y_ij = Z_ij / (sqrt(lambda_i) + sqrt(lambda_j)).

The candidate forms are J_2 = Tr[L_*^+(Z) Dlog_sigma(Z)] and
E_2 = <Y,H Y>_HS, where L_*^+ is the positive Schrödinger generator and H
its Hilbert-Schmidt implementation. This is a bounded literature audit, not
a proof or a priority claim. The exact target definitions are recorded as
parent-supplied interfaces.

Closest checked primary result: Gao–Guo
---------------------------------------
Li Gao and Jingyu Guo, “Relative Entropy Decay via BKM coercivity for
Quantum Markov Semigroups,” arXiv:2609.15902v1 (submitted 14 Sep 2026),
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15902v1 . The checked version is v1; local PDF
SHA256 is recorded in SOURCES.txt.

Their Definition 2.10 and Eqs. (2.25)–(2.32) use the BKM map

    Gamma_BKM_sigma(X) = integral_0^1 sigma^t X sigma^(1-t) dt,
    H_sigma(A) = Tr[A Gamma_BKM_sigma^(-1)(A)],
    Q_sigma(A) = -Tr[L_*(A) Gamma_BKM_sigma^(-1)(A)].

Theorem 1.1 compares the infimum of Q_sigma/H_sigma with MLSI, under the
finite-dimensional QMS and faithful asymptotic conditional-expectation
hypotheses stated there. It does not identify H_sigma with the target E_2.
Although the numerator Q_sigma(A) has the same second-variation expression
as J_2 (after matching the positive-generator sign convention), its
denominator is the BKM metric on the physical state tangent. The target
denominator is an HS Dirichlet form on the square-root tangent Y.

Example 6.4 is a near miss that must not be cited as a counterexample to the
target. It gives a finite-dimensional, nonprimitive KMS-symmetric M_3
generator with faithful invariant state

    sigma_eps = diag(eps/5, 4 eps/5, 1-eps),
    W = [[0,1/sqrt(2),0], [sqrt(2),0,0], [0,0,1]],
    L(X) = W* X W - {W*W,X}/2.

The KMS gap is (9-sqrt(73))/8 > 0, yet the source's BKM coercivity is zero.
Its witness uses u=|1>/sqrt(2)+|2>,
X=|u><3|+|3><u|, and A_eps=Gamma_BKM_sigma_eps(X); E_* A_eps=0.
Equations (6.51)–(6.54) give the BKM quotient

    Q_sigma_eps(A_eps)/H_sigma_eps(A_eps)
      = log(2)/(3 log((1-eps)/eps)) + O(log(1/eps)^(-2)) -> 0.

That is a genuine BKM-coercivity/MLSI obstruction, but the quadratic
denominator is not the square-root Dirichlet energy in the candidate
inequality. In the source eigenbasis, Gamma_BKM_sigma(E_ij) is
m(lambda_i,lambda_j) E_ij, where m(a,b)=(a-b)/(log(a)-log(b)). Thus for
the source tangent A_eps=Gamma_BKM_sigma(X), the target conversion is

    (Y_eps)_ij = m(lambda_i,lambda_j) X_ij /
                  (sqrt(lambda_i)+sqrt(lambda_j)),

followed by E_2=<Y_eps,H Y_eps>_HS. This is not the source's BKM Hessian
H_sigma(A_eps)=Tr[A_eps X]. It is also distinct from the parent's
sandwich map Z=sXs; the notation Gamma_BKM above is deliberately named to
avoid conflating those maps.

As a finite numerical cross-check only, direct matrix evaluation of the
source example after this root-tangent conversion gave J_2/E_2 approximately
3.7595, 3.3695, 3.2998, 3.5467, 3.8667 at eps=0.5, 0.1, 0.01, 1e-4,
1e-8, respectively. These floating samples are neither a proof for all eps
nor an asymptotic result. They do show that the published BKM quotient
sequence cannot simply be relabelled as a counterexample to J_2 >= pi E_2.

Other checked mechanism and exact missing conversion

of arXiv:2610.09678v1) gives an exact KMS Dirichlet form, but no comparison

does not transfer to arbitrary KMS symmetry.

The checked primary sources therefore yield neither a theorem of the form
J_2 >= pi E_2 for the supplied all-finite-dimensional KMS target nor a
counterexample after the required tangent conversion. The literature status
and priority status remain UNKNOWN; absence in this bounded check is not a
novelty claim. The decisive open interface is the exact comparison between

(L_s+R_s)^(-1)Z, with all Schrödinger/Heisenberg signs and normalizations
