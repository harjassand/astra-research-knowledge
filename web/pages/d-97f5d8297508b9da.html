# Quantum information foundations: source and problem scout

Date: 2026-10-09. Scope: PPT-square/cube, tensor-stable positivity, bound entanglement, broadcasting/classicality. This is a candidate-selection report, not an independent proof audit.

## Decision

The highest-upside exact target is to independently audit the pinned OpenAI-math candidate that claims an explicit trace-preserving PPT channel \(\Theta:M_{21}\to M_{21}\) with \(\Theta^2\) not entanglement breaking. If its formulas and proof survive independent replay, it directly refutes PPT-squared and the general two-PPT-composition conjecture. The reported formal Lean proof is promising source evidence, not external validation; I did not rerun the build or audit the full dependency closure. This candidate should be selected ahead of another asymptotic DSP-rank theorem.

Second, ASTRA N219 is a high-value validation target: for every finite reference size \(r\), it claims an indecomposable positive map that is \(r\)-entanglement breaking, answering DMS Open Problem 1 negatively. Its exact interface does not imply one fixed map positive under all tensor powers and does not yield a physical PPT-channel counterexample.

## Opportunity 1 — certify the PPT-squared counterexample candidate

**Exact claim and scope.** The local preprint claims both (i) explicit PPT maps \(\Phi_1,\Phi_2:M_{10}\to M_{10}\) for which \(\Phi_2\circ\Phi_1\) is not EB, and (ii) an explicitly specified trace-preserving PPT channel \(\Theta:M_{21}\to M_{21}\) with \(\Theta\circ\Theta\) not EB. The second is a same-channel, channel-normalized counterexample. The claim does not establish a minimal dimension or anything about \(\Theta^3\).

**Why it matters.** Christandl–Müller-Hermes–Wolf formulate PPT-squared as the question whether compositions of arbitrary compatible PPT maps are EB and prove the qutrit case; the general case was explicitly open in their paper. Park’s 2026 v2 paper likewise states the general square question remains open (as of that version) while proving finite EB index. The candidate preprint dated 2026-09-27 would therefore remove a major composition obstruction if validated. Sources: [CMHW, *When Do Composed Maps Become Entanglement Breaking?*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01266); [Park, *Every PPT Channel Has Finite Entanglement Breaking Index*, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13551).

**Mechanism.** The M10 pair is built from a PPT map on M4 with twenty rank-deficient projective outputs. Passing to the symmetric square records quadratic monomials in dimension 10; an exterior-square map and complementary-pair dual create a nonzero positive Choi matrix \(Z=J(\Phi_2\circ\Phi_1)\) whose kernel contains twenty Veronese product vectors. Any nonzero product vector in \(\operatorname{ran}Z\) would force two nonzero quadratics to cover all twenty directions, but each vanishes on at most nine, so no such vector exists. A nonzero positive separable matrix must have a product vector in its range, hence \(Z\) is nonseparable. The separate channel-completion construction alternates the two PPT maps across two blocks and adds a flag/defect term to make the 21-dimensional map trace preserving; a Choi corner of \(\Theta^2\) is a positive multiple of \(Z\), so separability of the full Choi matrix would imply the impossible separability of \(Z\).

**Load-bearing checks and falsifiers.** Independently replay the complex (not real-only) product-free-range argument; verify the twenty directions’ quadratic general-position certificate and the explicit PPT properties of both maps; then verify the flag-completion’s trace-preserving identity and the exact Choi-corner identity. A single nonzero product vector in \(\operatorname{ran}Z\), a nonseparable-status failure of the range criterion’s hypotheses, a nonpositive/incorrectly normalized corner, or a failure of PPT/TP for \(\Theta\) would falsify this candidate. A direct separable decomposition of \(Z\) is an immediate contradiction to the central claim. The source Lean files contain no literal `sorry`, `admit`, or `axiom` hits in the directly inspected modules, but that is not a dependency audit or an independent kernel/build replay.

## Opportunity 2 — independently certify the finite-reference 2-EB counterexample

**Exact claim and scope.** ASTRA N219 claims: for every integer \(r\ge1\) and every \(d\ge 2^{23}r^3\), there exists a real-coefficient, complex-linear, unital, trace-preserving positive map \(P:M_d\to M_d\) such that \(P\circ T=P=T\circ P\), both \(P\) and its Hilbert–Schmidt adjoint are \(r\)-EB, and \(P\) is indecomposable with \(\inf_{D\text{ decomposable}}\|P-D\|_\diamond\ge6/55\). Taking \(r=2\) gives a claimed negative answer to DMS Open Problem 1 at sufficient dimension \(d=2^{26}=67{,}108{,}864\). This is a positive non-CP map, not a physical channel. It is an existence result with no admitted matrix materialized.

**Prior work and significance.** DMS Definition 3.1 defines k-EB by k-positivity plus separability of every k-reference output; its Open Problem 1 asks whether every 2-EB map is decomposable. Huber–Lami–Lancien–Müller-Hermes already use identity-plus-Gaussian PPT states and restricted Schmidt witnesses to get linear Schmidt number, so the Gaussian ingredient is not by itself new. N219’s additional mechanism is a single witness controlling both low-Schmidt cones, combined with separability of every small-reference Choi compression. Sources: [DMS, *Mapping Cone of k-Entanglement Breaking Maps*, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14991); [HLLMH, *High-Dimensional Entanglement in States with Positive Partial Transposition*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.04975).

**Mechanism and missing independent check.** The source draws a centered Gaussian \(G\) in the real symmetric, zero-marginal, partial-transpose-invariant subspace and claims a simultaneous event: bounded global operator norm, large Hilbert–Schmidt mass, and small bilinear norm over *all complex* Schmidt-rank-\(r\) unit vectors. With \(W=I-32G\) and \(\rho=(I+G/6)/d^2\), the two-sided block-positivity of \(W\) and the PPT property of \(\rho\) yield a fixed cone-separation gap and indecomposability. To get r-EB, every r-dimensional first-factor compression of \(W\) is placed inside an asymmetric separable ball: operator norm of \(E\) at most \(1/(2r-1)\) implies \(I+E\) is separable. Local filtering then covers every complex input and singular Schmidt basis. Independently verify the complex-rank net entropy/tail (especially the no-log-dimension extension from rank k to rank 2k), the separable-ball decomposition for noncommuting blocks, and the Choi compression/local-filter normalization. The precise falsifier is failure of any universal inequality or a counterexample to the stated separable-ball lemma; finite vector sampling cannot verify the universal premise.

## Quantifier, composition, and transfer barriers

- The exact hierarchy gate `G-PPT-SMALL-DIMENSION-DSP` remains open: a d=4 PPT state outside DSP2 needs a global complex Schmidt-rank-two witness; sampling, real-only tests, and the failed Breuer–Hall ansatz do not pass. The Breuer–Hall candidate has exact value \(1-k=-1\) at d=6,k=2 on the partial-transpose side.
- N213 gives a source-claimed linear lower bound \(K(d)\ge\lfloor d/2^{24}\rfloor+1\); N220 improves the sufficient constant to \(2^{23}\) and supplies a partial-transpose-invariant ensemble. Neither settles exact \(K(d)\) or the first useful low-dimensional case d=4,k=2. N216 excludes only a fixed, predetermined isometry family under its stated size contract; it is not a universal representation impossibility.

N215 and N225 give source-claimed physical PPT channels with EB squares (the N225 index is exactly 2); high DSP rank alone cannot obstruct the square or cube. The OpenAI candidate above is the only direct composition-sensitive lead located in this scout.
- N218 and N226 explicitly preserve source/read scope and failed transfers; neither promotes a mathematical claim to externally validated status. In particular, the Gaussian proof’s internal fixtures are not a theorem-sized global rank certificate or independent replay.
- N219’s \(\forall r\,\exists(d,P_r)\) changes both dimension and map with r; it does not imply \(\exists P\,\forall n, P^{\otimes n}\) positive. G-FIXED-MAP-ALL-TENSOR-POWERS remains open. The 2-producible cross-copy Bell alternative in N224 blocks the naive multiplicative overlap transfer; N223’s engineered family is one-copy distillable across a 2-versus-rest grouped cut and is not an NPT bound-entanglement example on that cut.

Its gate is `G-COMMON-EB-ARBITRARY-RANK` and remains open.
