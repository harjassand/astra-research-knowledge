generator, Bloch identities, inner-ball positivity estimate, heat-seed tail,
and stopped Ito/Feynman--Kac comparison are c07_s02's Revision 2 candidate in

identified in outputs/round6/frozen_initials/c07_s02/INITIAL.txt. The proof
candidate remains unaudited externally; this report is a constructive
continuation, conditional on those exact identities and bounds.

The added steps here are: (i) replace the stopped SDE by an explicit globally
Lipschitz cutoff SDE that agrees on a high-probability interior event; (ii)
Euler--Maruyama discretize its positively weighted endpoint law with an
explicit trace-norm error; and (iii) use ordinary accept--reject sampling of
the Feynman--Kac weight, so one accepted path directly prepares the averaged
product state. No finite pool/resampling estimate or unknown normalization
oracle is needed. Ito/Feynman--Kac, accept--reject, and Euler--Maruyama are
standard tools; the latter's global-Lipschitz strong-convergence setting is
documented, for example, by Higham, Mao & Stuart (2002), SIAM J. Numer. Anal.
40(3), https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S0036142901389530. The application
constants below are derived for this specific critical-spin construction.

INTERFACE

Fix finite public rational M,B and rational known data A,b with ||A||<=M,
|b|<=B. Put Lambda=M+1, Lc=2M+1, Tc=Tr(A+Lambda I)<=3Lc,
s=N^(3/4), t0=2/N-Lambda/s^2, C=A+Lambda I, and

  H = Q_C/s^2 + b.J/s,      K = t0 J^2 + H.

The target is rho=exp(K)/Tr exp(K). The output is an averaged state on N
qubits, with no external-reference guarantee. N is restricted to the explicit
large-N regime where t0>0, the seed/tail bounds of c07_s02 apply, and the
displayed residual below is at most epsilon/4. No statement is uniform in
growing M or B.

1. INTERIOR POSITIVE DIFFUSION AND AN EXPLICIT CUTOFF

For the ordinary Bloch vector m, let k_N(m)=((I+m.sigma)/2)^tensor N.
Use the exact c07_s02 identities, for unit n,

  {n.J,k_N}=B_n k_N,  [n.J,k_N]=i R_n k_N,
  {(n.J)^2,k_N}/2 = (B_n^2-R_n^2)k_N/4.

Diagonalizing C only to display its terms gives the sandwich generator
M_H(X)={H,X}/2 and its action

  M_H k_N = V_N k_N + a_N.grad k_N + (D_C:Hess k_N)/(4s^2),
  D_C=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T.

These equations are taken from c07_s02's exact derivation, not inferred from
finite spin tests. On |m|<=r0, where r0^2=1/[16(Lc+1)], the quoted elementary
bound gives D_C >=(3/4)I and D_C<=Tc I. The coefficient functions a_N,V_N
are exactly the polynomials in Sections 1--2 of the cited revision. They obey

  |a_N| <= A_N := Tc*r0*(2N+3)/(4s^2)+B*(1+r0^2)/(2s),
  |V_N| <= H_N := Lc*N*(N/2+1)/(2s^2)+B*N/(2s)
       = Lc*sqrt(N)/4 + Lc/(2sqrt(N)) + B*N^(1/4)/2.

The first bound is a deliberately loose direct bound from the displayed
generator expansion; the sharper c07_s02 beta_N may be substituted. For
N sufficiently large, A_N<=r0/8. Each Brownian coordinate in the SDE below
then has quadratic variation at most Tc/(2s^2).

Set q=r0^2. Define a C^2 piecewise-polynomial radial cutoff chi (its
coefficients are rational when M is rational):
chi(m)=1 when |m|^2<=q/4, chi(m)=0 when |m|^2>=9q/16, and in between

  u=(|m|^2-q/4)/(5q/16),
  chi(m)=1-10u^3+15u^4-6u^5.

It has 0<=chi<=1, vanishing first and second derivatives at the two joins,
and |grad chi|<=9/r0. On its support D_C is uniformly positive, so its
principal root is well-defined. Extend the following coefficients by zero
outside |m|<3r0/4:

  abar=chi*a_N,  sigmabar=chi*sqrt(D_C/(2s^2)),  Vbar=chi*V_N.

All three coefficients are globally Lipschitz. At |m|=3r0/4 both drift and
diffusion vanish. That sphere consists of equilibria; pathwise uniqueness
implies a solution started inside cannot cross it. Thus the SDE

  dX_t=abar(X_t)dt+sigmabar(X_t)dW_t

stays in the physical Bloch ball. Start from the heat seed law of
e^(t0 J^2), but replace every seed with |m|>r0/4 by m=0. Write p_seed for
the replaced mass. The heat-tail proof in c07_s02 Section 3, with r0/2 there
replaced by r0/4, gives

  p_seed <= C_(Lambda,delta) N^(3/4) exp(-kappa0' N),
  a0'=2 atanh(r0/4),
  kappa0'=min((a0')^4/768, delta/4),
  delta=1/2-logcosh(1)>0.

The replaced seed differs in trace norm from the exact heat seed by at most
2p_seed. The existing Maxwell rejection heat-seed sampler applies with
M replaced by Lambda; its fixed positive acceptance lower bound is denoted
p_seed_sampler below. The replacement requires no extra rejection: retain a
good sampled Bloch vector and map a bad one to 0.

2. CUTOFF ERROR IS EXPLICITLY SMALL

Couple the cutoff SDE to c07_s02's original SDE stopped at radius r0, from
the same replaced seed and Brownian motion. Before reaching r0/2, both laws
and their Feynman--Kac weights agree. From |X_0|<=r0/4, reaching r0/2 by time
one requires martingale displacement at least r0/8 after the drift budget.
The coordinate exponential-martingale maximal inequality and a union bound
give the conservative estimate

  p_mid := P(hit r0/2 by time 1)
       <= 6 exp[-kappa_mid N^(3/2)],
  kappa_mid := r0^2/(384 Tc).

The same inequality, or the weaker c07_s02 S9 bound from its larger start
ball, gives for hitting r0

  p_stop <= 6 exp[-kappa1 N^(3/2)],
  kappa1 := r0^2/(256 Lc).

Let W_cut=E[exp(int_0^1 Vbar(X_t)dt) k_N(X_1)] be the unnormalized positive
product mixture, and G=exp(M_H)rho_seed_exact. On the cutoff/stop mismatch
event both trace weights are at most exp(H_N), hence
||W_cut-W_stop||_1<=2 exp(H_N) p_mid. The exact stopped Duhamel bound from
c07_s02 is ||G_replaced-W_stop||_1<=H_N exp(2H_N) p_stop. The heat-seed
replacement costs at most 2 exp(H_N) p_seed under exp(M_H). Consequently

  ||G-W_cut||_1 <= Delta0_N,
  Delta0_N := 2 exp(H_N) p_seed
               + H_N exp(2H_N) p_stop
               + 2 exp(H_N) p_mid.                    (C1)

Every term tends to zero exponentially in N, with all weights charged.
c07_s02's sector Jensen argument gives Tr G>=1. For positive G,W, the
normalization inequality T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG therefore shows
T(rho,W_cut/Tr W_cut)<=Delta0_N. In the rest assume Delta0_N<=epsilon/4.
This implication is conditional on the parent stopped-Duhamel proof; (C1)
does not repair an error in that proof if one is later found.

3. EULER--MARUYAMA WITH WEIGHT AND PRODUCT-STATE ERROR

Let A0=sup|abar|, S0=sup||sigmabar||_F, La a global Lipschitz constant of
abar, Lsig a global Frobenius-Lipschitz constant of sigmabar, and Lv a
Lipschitz constant of Vbar. The following are explicit bounds in M,B,N;
they avoid any unknown eigenvalue-gap or hidden derivative oracle:

  A0 <= A_N,
  S0 <= sqrt(3Tc/2)/s,
  La <= (9/r0)A_N
        + Tc*[2N(1+2r_c^2)+3+4r_c^2]/(4s^2) + B*r_c/s,
  Lsig <= [(9/r0)*sqrt(3Tc) + 6Lc*r_c]/(sqrt(2)*s),
  Lv <= (9/r0)H_N + (N^2+N)Lc*r_c/(2s^2) + NB/(2s),
  r_c=3r0/4.

For La, differentiate the explicit vector fields v=n-(n.m)m and r=n x m;
on |m|<=r_c, ||Dv||<=2r_c, ||D^2v||<=2, ||Dr||<=1. For Lsig,
differentiate D_C; the displayed D_C formula gives
||dD_C[z]||_F<=6sqrt(3)Lc*r_c |z|. The Sylvester identity
S dS+dS S=dD_C, with S>=sqrt(3/4)I, gives
||d sqrt(D_C)[z]||_F<=6Lc*r_c |z|. Multiplication by chi and
|grad chi|<=9/r0 yields the stated Lsig. For Lv, use
||grad V_N||<=((N^2+N)Lc*r_c)/(2s^2)+NB/(2s).
For fixed M,B, these bounds scale as A0=O(N^-1/2), S0=O(N^-3/4),
La=O(N^-1/2), Lsig=O(N^-3/4), Lv=O(sqrt(N)).

Take T equal Euler steps h=1/T. Given Y_0 from the replaced seed and
independent standard three-dimensional normals Z_k, define

  Y_(k+1)=Pi_(|m|<=r0)[Y_k+h*abar(Y_k)+sqrt(h)*sigmabar(Y_k)Z_k],
  I_T=h*sum_(k=0)^(T-1) Vbar(Y_k),  w_T=exp(I_T).

Projection is nonexpansive and keeps every endpoint a valid Bloch vector;
the exact cutoff diffusion is inside r_c<r0. Let
Lambda0=La^2+Lsig^2 and

  Cx=2 sqrt(Lambda0) exp((1+2Lambda0)/2) sqrt(A0^2+S0^2).

A direct Ito/Grönwall estimate on the continuous Euler interpolation, with
projection at grid points, gives E|X_1-Y_T|^2<=Cx^2/T for T>=1. One
derivation is: within a step the interpolation increment has second moment
at most 2A0^2/T^2+2S0^2/T; Ito's formula for the coupled error yields
e'(t)<=(1+2Lambda0)e(t)+4Lambda0(A0^2/T^2+S0^2/T); projection can only
decrease the grid error. Grönwall over unit time yields the stated Cx.
This is the standard globally-Lipschitz Euler--Maruyama strong-error
argument with constants exposed.

For Bloch vectors |m|,|n|<=r0, the squared one-qubit fidelity satisfies
1-F(m,n)<=|m-n|^2/[4(1-r0^2)]. Tensor-product fidelity and
Fuchs--van de Graaf then give

  ||k_N(m)-k_N(n)||_1 <= sqrt(N)|m-n|/sqrt(1-r0^2).   (C2)

Since |int Vbar|<=H_N, |exp(x)-exp(y)|<=exp(H_N)|x-y| in the relevant
range. Coupling X and Y by the same Brownian increments, the left-Riemann
potential error obeys
E|int_0^1 Vbar(X_t)dt-I_T|
   <= Lv*(Cx+A0+S0)/sqrt(T).
Thus for the cutoff continuous and Euler unnormalized mixtures,

  ||W_cut-W_T||_1 <= Delta_EM(T),
  Delta_EM(T) := exp(H_N)*C_T/sqrt(T),
  C_T := Lv*(Cx+A0+S0) + sqrt(N)*Cx/sqrt(1-r0^2).     (C3)

W_T=E[w_T k_N(Y_T)] is a positive identical-product mixture, not a
potential-signed approximation. Choose the explicit integer

  T=ceil((8 exp(H_N) C_T/epsilon)^2).                 (C4)

Then Delta_EM<=epsilon/8. By C1, Tr W_cut>=1-Delta0_N>=3/4; hence
Tr W_T>=3/4-epsilon/8>=1/2 for epsilon<=1/4. Normalizing (C3) therefore
costs at most 2 Delta_EM<=epsilon/4 in trace distance.

For fixed M,B, C_T=O_(M,B)(1) under the displayed bounds, so
T=O_(M,B)(exp(2H_N) epsilon^-2), with H_N=O_(M,B)(sqrt(N)). This is an
explicit discretization cost. No diffusion simulation is left as a free
oracle.

4. DIRECT WEIGHTED-PATH SAMPLING, NO RESAMPLING POOL

For a proposal path drawn from the ordinary Euler law, accept it with
probability

  alpha(path)=w_T/exp(H_N)=exp(I_T-H_N) in [exp(-2H_N),1].

This is ordinary rejection sampling with a known envelope. Its acceptance
probability is Tr(W_T)/exp(H_N)>=exp(-H_N)/2. Conditional on acceptance,
the entire path law is exactly tilted by w_T/Tr(W_T); therefore one accepted
endpoint, followed by N preparations of tau_(Y_T)=(I+Y_T.sigma)/2, has
averaged density exactly W_T/Tr(W_T). It does not need to estimate Tr(W_T),
retain R candidates, or apply importance resampling. This is an exact
real-arithmetic representation of the discretized mixture.

The uncapped rejection sampler uses at most 2 exp(H_N) proposals in
expectation. To make a bounded algorithm, make

  Kcap=ceil(2 exp(H_N) log(4/delta_cap))

independent proposals and emit tau_0^tensor N if none is accepted. Since
p_accept>=exp(-H_N)/2, the fallback event has probability at most
delta_cap/4 (and hence at most delta_cap). Conditional on success the output
law remains exactly W_T/Tr(W_T); the capped averaged state differs from it
by at most the fallback probability in trace distance.

The resulting exact-real-arithmetic cost per output is at most Kcap paths,
each with T three-dimensional Gaussian steps, one heat-seed draw, O(1)
constant-size coefficient/root operations per step, and N one-qubit
emissions. Expected path-step cost is

  O(exp(H_N)*T) = O_(M,B)(exp(3H_N)*epsilon^-2),

and a delta_cap-bounded runtime has
  O_(M,B)(exp(3H_N)*epsilon^-2*log(1/delta_cap))
path steps, plus the heat-seed rejection and N emissions. Since H_N is
explicit above, this is exp(O_(M,B)(sqrt(N))) epsilon^-2, not polynomial in
N. That is the material runtime limitation of this constructive route.

5. FINITE-BIT COMPLETION AND ITS COST BOUNDARY

The real-arithmetic sampler can be turned into a finite-bit sampler for
rational A,b,M,B, but no such implementation was written or run here. A
fully charged route is as follows.

In a finite-bit implementation replace H_N by any certified rational upper
bound Hbar_N>=H_N in the acceptance denominator and in T,Kcap. This only
multiplies the weight by a path-independent constant, so the accepted law is
unchanged. The lower acceptance estimate becomes
Tr(W_T)/exp(Hbar_N)>=exp(-Hbar_N)/2. In the finite-bit cost formulas below,
Hbar_N replaces H_N in Kcap and T; the exact-real formulas above use H_N.

First, reuse the audited cycle-1 Maxwell heat-seed rejection construction
with M replaced by Lambda. It supplies a public rational lower bound p0>0
for seed acceptance and inverse-CDF Gaussian generation; the seed outside
|m|<=r0/4 is mapped to zero. Put

  Kseed=ceil(p0^-1 log(8Kcap/delta_seed)).

For each of at most Kcap outer proposals, cap seed attempts at Kseed;
union-bound seed exhaustion by delta_seed/8. Couple the finite-bit seed
output to the ideal seed within the stated dseed tolerance except on an
additional event of probability at most delta_seed/8. All seed normal
values are included in the normal-tail count. To account for the discontinuous
map that replaces |m|>r0/4 by 0, also choose dseed so the explicit heat-seed
mass of the dseed-shell around |m|=r0/4 is at most delta_seed/(8Kcap); this
shell mass is bounded by integrating the displayed radial heat density.

Second, set D=3*T*Kcap+3*Kcap*Kseed for all diffusion and seed normal
coordinates and L=sqrt(2 log(16D/delta_z)). The total probability of an
ideal normal coordinate outside [-L,L] is at most delta_z/8 by the Gaussian
tail bound.
Generate the truncated normals by bisection on dyadic uniforms and a
certified interval for the normal CDF; on the retained event, use a uniform
absolute normal-coupling error delta_G. The required dyadic precision can
be chosen explicitly from L, delta_G, and the number D (inverse-CDF
conditioning costs O(L^2+log(1/delta_G)+log D) bits, up to fixed constants).

Third, for the deterministic path update, put

  Pnum=exp(La + sqrt(3)*Lsig*L*sqrt(T)),
  eta_state=min{epsilon*sqrt(1-r0^2)/(16sqrt(N)),
                delta_num/[16*Kcap*(Lv+1)]}.

If the initial-seed coordinate error is <=eta_state/(2Pnum), and each
coefficient/root evaluation, normal coordinate, and arithmetic update is
guarded so that

  da <= eta_state/(8Pnum),
  dsigma <= eta_state/(8Pnum*sqrt(3T)*L),
  dGaussian <= eta_state/(8Pnum*sqrt(T)*S0),
  dupdate <= eta_state/(8Pnum*T),

then the discrete perturbation recurrence

  e_T <= Pnum*(dseed+da+sqrt(3T)*L*dsigma
                         +sqrt(T)*S0*dGaussian+T*dupdate)

gives e_T<=eta_state. The root is computable by a 3-by-3 Newton
square-root iteration with O(log(1/tolerance)) steps: on the support,
eigenvalues of D_C lie in [3/4,Tc], so its conditioning is bounded
explicitly. Evaluate the acceptance probability exp(I_T-Hbar_N) and its uniform
coin with interval
precision so each acceptance-decision coupling error is at most
delta_num/(8Kcap); compute I_T with per-step potential error at most
delta_num/[16Kcap*T]. Since
eta_state<=delta_num/[16Kcap*(Lv+1)], the state-induced log-weight error
and direct potential-evaluation error together make the acceptance
probability mismatch at most delta_num/(4Kcap). The dyadic acceptance coin
adds at most delta_num/(8Kcap). Finally compute each emitted qubit to trace
distance epsilon/(16N), adding at most epsilon/16 by tensor telescoping.
Choose delta_seed=delta_z=delta_num=epsilon/32; the listed seed, normal,
state-rounding, and acceptance couplings then sum to less than epsilon/8.

All ceilings can be taken after certified rational upper bounds are formed;
no exact comparison with exp(Hbar_N), a Gaussian tail, or a transcendental
integer boundary is required. Precision bits can be taken as the ceiling of
the logarithms of the displayed reciprocal tolerances, with enough guard
bits for Pnum and interval arithmetic. For fixed M,B, log(Pnum) is explicit
and at most O_(M,B)(N^-3/4*exp(Hbar_N)*L/epsilon + log N + log(1/epsilon));
thus this conservative finite-bit completion has at most
exp(O_(M,B)(sqrt(N))) times a polynomial in 1/epsilon and log(1/delta_cap)
precision bits, and total bit operations remain
exp(O_(M,B)(sqrt(N))) times a polynomial in input bit length, 1/epsilon,
and log(1/delta_cap). This is an existence/cost derivation, not an executed
compiler. The interface must charge rational input lengths and native
one-qubit preparation precision/cost; arbitrary noncomputable A,b and
unbounded-cost coefficient or hardware oracles are outside the claim.

6. TOTAL ERROR, COST, AND OPEN GATES

With Delta0_N<=epsilon/4, T from (C4), delta_cap<=epsilon/8, and
delta_seed=delta_z=delta_num=epsilon/32, the averaged capped finite-bit
output is within

  Delta0_N + 2 Delta_EM(T) + delta_cap
    + delta_seed/4 + delta_z/8 + delta_num + 2*epsilon/16
  <= epsilon

of rho, using the unnormalized-to-normalized inequality above and the local
emission budget. This is a constructive weighted-diffusion representation
with a finite-step, positive product-mixture output and an explicit
worst-case finite cap. Its classical path-step cost is
exp(O_(M,B)(sqrt(N))) epsilon^-2 log(1/epsilon), plus finite-bit arithmetic
and N local state emissions. It is not polynomial-time in N, and does not
establish quantum circuit depth, gate-set availability, hardware success,
source acquisition, or energy advantage.

The decisive remaining mathematical gate is independent verification of
c07_s02's exact Bloch-generator identity, positivity lower bound, stopped
Duhamel residual, and seed tail. This report does not establish the
parameter-independent decoder's rate or optimality, and the cutoff/SDE
construction depends on known A,b. External priority remains unknown. The
standard numerical-method references above establish only generic
Euler--Maruyama methodology, not the critical-spin theorem.
