THEOREM
Let Phi and Psi be fixed completely positive, trace-preserving, unital maps
on M_d, with Phi Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint. Suppose C>0 and, for every
Hermitian X,

 Tr[X(I-Psi)(X)] <= C Tr[X(I-Phi)(X)].                   (H)

Psi need not be HS-self-adjoint. Let sigma be ANY density matrix satisfying
Phi(sigma)=sigma. For every density rho with supp(rho) contained in
supp(sigma), set
 D_sigma=Tr[rho(log rho-log sigma)],
 b=||Phi(rho)-rho||_1/2, e=||Psi(rho)-rho||_1/2.
Logs and relative entropy are taken on supp(sigma), and all logs are natural.
Then the SAME Psi obeys

 e^3 <= (27C/8) D_sigma b.                              (R)

No spectral gap or minimum eigenvalue promise is assumed. Sigma may be
rank deficient; finiteness of relative entropy is precisely the stated
support condition. For C=4, e^2<=3(D_sigma+1)b^(2/3), by the already frozen
scalar conversion. References may vary among states without changing Psi.

1. FIXED STATES FORCE KRAUS COMMUTATION AND COMMON FIXATION
For any bistochastic CP map L with Kraus operators K_j, and every Hermitian
u, direct expansion using both sum_j K_j* K_j=I and sum_j K_j K_j*=I gives

 sum_j ||K_j u-u K_j||_2^2 =2 Tr[u(I-L)(u)].            (K0)

Indeed, the two square terms each have trace Tr(u^2), while each cross term
sums to Tr[u L(u)] (equivalently Tr[u L*(u)]). Hermitian trace pairings are
real. This identity proves nonnegativity of every quadratic form in (H)
and works for every Kraus representation, not only self-adjoint ones.

If Phi(sigma)=sigma, (K0) with u=sigma says that every Kraus operator of
Phi commutes with sigma. By (H),
 0<=Tr[sigma(I-Psi)(sigma)]<=C Tr[sigma(I-Phi)(sigma)]=0;
thus every Kraus operator of Psi also commutes with sigma. In particular
Psi(sigma)=sigma. Both sets commute with every spectral function of sigma,
including its support projection P. On P, they commute with log sigma,
and the two channels AND their HS adjoints fix log sigma.

Because all Kraus operators commute with P, both maps restrict to P M_d P.
Their restricted Kraus sums are P, so the restrictions remain unital and
TP on this matrix algebra. Phi's restriction remains HS-self-adjoint, and
(H) restricts with the same C. Normalizing trace by rank(P), instead of d,
rescales both sides by the same positive number. Hence one may work from
now on in M_n, n=rank(P), where sigma is faithful. No channel is changed
on the original full space; this is only analysis on an invariant support.

2. ENTROPY DISSIPATION WITH A STATIONARY NONTRACIAL REFERENCE
First let rho also be faithful on P and define P_t=exp[t(Phi-I)],
rho_t=P_t(rho). This is the same CP Poisson semigroup as in the first blind
proof. Since P_t(sigma)=sigma and positivity preserves rho>=c sigma for
some c>0, all rho_t are faithful on P. Trace differentiation gives

 -d/dt D(rho_t||sigma)
   =Tr[(I-Phi)(rho_t)(log rho_t-log sigma)]
   =Tr[(I-Phi)(rho_t) log rho_t].                       (R1)

The last equality follows from Phi*(log sigma)=log sigma; it does not
require rho_t to commute with sigma. Trace preservation removes the
additional derivative-of-x-log-x identity term. This calculation is valid
for each finite time without a quantitative lower-eigenvalue premise.

Put h_t=n rho_t and tau_n=Tr/n. Adding the scalar log n in (R1) contributes
zero by trace preservation. In an eigenbasis of h_t, with positive a_i,
k_ij=Tr[P_i Phi(P_j)] is nonnegative, doubly stochastic and symmetric,
using CP, bistochasticity and Phi's HS-self-adjointness respectively. Thus

 -D_sigma'(t)
   =(1/(2n)) sum_ij k_ij(a_i-a_j)(log a_i-log a_j)
   >=(2/n) sum_ij k_ij(sqrt(a_i)-sqrt(a_j))^2
   =4 tau_n[sqrt(h_t)(I-Phi)(sqrt(h_t))].              (R2)

The scalar inequality used here is
 (a-b)(log a-log b)>=4(sqrt(a)-sqrt(b))^2, a,b>0.
It was proved in the first freeze and is unchanged. Neither sigma nor its
logarithm appears in the final production because it is exactly conserved.
Nonnegativity of terminal quantum relative entropy implies

 integral_0^T E_Phi(sqrt(h_t))dt <=D_sigma/4.            (R3)

For completeness, finite-dimensional D(rho||sigma)>=0 follows without a
commutativity assumption: diagonalize rho with eigenvalues lambda_i and
sigma with eigenvalues nu_j>0. W_ij=|<i|j>|^2 is doubly stochastic. The
probability distributions p_ij=lambda_i W_ij and q_ij=nu_j W_ij obey
D(rho||sigma)=sum p_ij log(p_ij/q_ij)>=0 by the classical log inequality.
Zero p terms use the continuous convention. The support restriction above
ensures all relevant nu_j are positive.

3. THE SAME KRAUS ESTIMATE AND ORBIT AVERAGING
The dimension-free estimate proved in the first freeze holds for every
bistochastic CP L and density omega on P:

 (||L(omega)-omega||_1/2)^2
 <=2 E_L(sqrt(n omega)).                               (R4)

Explicitly use the rectangular block rows [K_j sqrt(omega)]_j and
[sqrt(omega)K_j]_j: their Gram matrices are L(omega), omega; both have
Hilbert-Schmidt norm one, and the squared difference is 2 E_L(...).
Schatten Holder supplies (R4), without L self-adjointness. Applying (H)
to sqrt(h_t) and then (R3) gives

 integral_0^T e_Psi(rho_t)^2dt <=C D_sigma/2.            (R5)

Trace contraction of P_t yields ||rho_t-rho||_1<=2bt. With
bar_rho_T=T^(-1) integral_0^T rho_t dt, linearity of the fixed Psi, convexity
and Cauchy-Schwarz give
 e_Psi(bar_rho_T)<=sqrt(C D_sigma/(2T)),
 ||rho-bar_rho_T||_1<=bT.
CPTP contraction of Psi and the two half-distance triangle terms imply

 e<=bT+sqrt(C D_sigma/(2T)) for every T>0.               (R6)

For positive b,D_sigma, take T=(C D_sigma/(8b^2))^(1/3), yielding
 e<=(3/2)(C D_sigma b)^(1/3), which is (R).
If b=0, let T tend to infinity in (R6). If D_sigma=0, let T tend to zero.
The case n=1 is immediate. No optimizing division by zero is used.

4. ALL RANK-DEFICIENT ENDPOINTS
For rho possibly rank deficient on P, use
 rho_eta=(1-eta)rho+eta sigma, 0<eta<1.
This is faithful on P, and both channels fix sigma by Section 1. Therefore
 b_eta=(1-eta)b, e_eta=(1-eta)e.
Apply the faithful theorem and let eta tend to zero. The term
Tr(rho_eta log rho_eta) converges by continuity of x log x, and
Tr(rho_eta log sigma) converges linearly on the faithful support P.
Thus D(rho_eta||sigma) tends to D(rho||sigma), proving (R) exactly.
No lower eigenvalue enters its final constant; an arbitrarily small
positive reference eigenvalue is permitted. Infinite relative entropy
outside the support condition yields no asserted finite bound.

5. UNTOUCHED REFERENCE SYSTEM AND FORM AMPLIFICATION
Let Phi_B and Psi_B satisfy the original hypotheses on M_dB. For ANY
finite-dimensional A, put Phi_AB=id_A tensor Phi_B and
Psi_AB=id_A tensor Psi_B. These are fixed bistochastic CP maps; Phi_AB is
HS-self-adjoint. Full real-Hermitian order (H) amplifies with the same C:
choose Hermitian F_a orthonormal for tau_A and expand any Hermitian X_AB
as sum_a F_a tensor X_a, where
 X_a=(tau_A tensor id_B)[(F_a tensor I_B)X_AB]
is Hermitian by cyclicity of the partial trace on A. Orthogonality yields

 E_(id_A tensor L)(X_AB)=sum_a E_L(X_a),                (Amp)

so summing the local inequalities proves the global one. This is why the
supplied FULL Hermitian order, rather than order only on a selected frame
or on density square roots, suffices at every ancillary dimension. Psi_B
need not be HS-self-adjoint, and no channel covariance is assumed.

For ANY rho_AB, the density sigma=rho_A tensor I_B/dB is fixed by both
channels. Its support contains that of rho_AB: for Q=I_A-supp(rho_A),
Tr[(Q tensor I_B)rho_AB]=0 and positivity imply (Q tensor I_B)rho_AB=0.
On this support,

 D(rho_AB||rho_A tensor I_B/dB)
   =I(A:B)_rho+D(rho_B||I_B/dB).                        (ID)

If rho_B=I_B/dB, the right side is exactly mutual information. Consequently,
with b_AB=||Phi_AB(rho_AB)-rho_AB||_1/2 and similarly e_AB,

 e_AB^3 <=(27C/8) I(A:B)_rho b_AB                       (MI)

for every rho_AB with tracial B marginal. For C=4,
 e_AB<=3*2^(-1/3)(I(A:B)b_AB)^(1/3),
 e_AB^2<=3(I(A:B)+1)b_AB^(2/3).
The SAME fixed Psi_B works for all such states and all ancillary dimensions
A. Rho_A changes the analytic reference/budget, never the comparator. The
regularization by rho_A tensor tau_B keeps both marginals unchanged.

If Psi_B is entanglement breaking, id_A tensor Psi_B leaves A untouched,
measures/prepares B, and gives an A:B separable output for every rho_AB.
It is generally NOT an entanglement-breaking map on the entire AB system,
because an arbitrary quantum reference entangled with A can survive. The
local EB property and global quadratic order must not be conflated.

Optional exact hypothesis sharpening: if instead a GLOBAL bistochastic CP
Psi_AB is supplied satisfying (H) against Phi_AB=id_A tensor Phi_B, it
necessarily factors as id_A tensor Psi_B. Indeed E_Phi_AB(H_A tensor I)=0
for every Hermitian H_A. Order and (K0) force each Psi_AB Kraus operator
to commute with the full M_dA tensor I_B algebra. Its commutant is
I_A tensor M_dB, so every Kraus operator is I_A tensor L_j. Their TP/unital
sums supply those properties for Psi_B, and testing X=I_A tensor X_B
recovers the local order. Thus no hidden reference disturbance is allowed
by the exact full form premise itself.
