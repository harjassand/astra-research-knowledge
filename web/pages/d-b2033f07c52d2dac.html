# Implicit rational-rate endotacticity: exact decision and hardness

## 1. Input and property

There are \(m\) labeled channels in dimension \(d\). Channel \(e\) has a rational
jump vector \(\nu_e\in\mathbb Q^d\) and a positive rational generalized
polynomial rate

\[
\rho_e(x)=\frac{P_e(x)}{Q_e(x)},\qquad
P_e=\sum_{a\in A_e}p_{ea}x^a,\quad
Q_e=\sum_{b\in B_e}q_{eb}x^b,
\]

where \(A_e,B_e\subset\mathbb Q^d\) are finite and nonempty and all displayed
coefficients are strictly positive. Rational exponents include the ordinary
Hill/Michaelis–Menten integer-exponent cases. Denominators are strictly
positive on the positive orthant. Zero terms are removed and duplicate
exponents are aggregated before the input is formed.

For a direction \(w\in\mathbb R^d\), define

\[
h_{A}(w)=\max_{a\in A}a\cdot w,\qquad
\tau_e(w)=h_{A_e}(w)-h_{B_e}(w),\qquad
E(w)=\{e:w\cdot\nu_e\ne0\}.
\]

The tested property is the P/N33 essential-source rule: if \(E(w)\ne\emptyset\),
every channel in \(E(w)\) attaining \(\max_{e\in E(w)}\tau_e(w)\) must satisfy
\(w\cdot\nu_e<0\). Neutral channels are removed *before* the maximum. Every
exactly tied active maximum is checked. This report does not silently switch
to the distinct two-dimensional “tropically endotactic” differential-inclusion
definition.

The executable also supports standard strong endotacticity of the lifted
diagram. This means endotacticity plus: for every direction with at least one
non-neutral channel, at least one inward channel occurs at the *global*
maximum source score \(\max_e\tau_e(w)\), with neutral channels retained in
that source maximum. A neutral source can therefore distinguish ordinary
endotacticity from strong endotacticity.

## 2. Exact no-expansion representation

Clear all denominators with the strictly positive common factor

\[
D(x)=\prod_{f=1}^m Q_f(x),\qquad
D(x)\sum_e\kappa_e(t)\rho_e(x)\nu_e
=\sum_e\kappa_e(t)P_e(x)\prod_{f\ne e}Q_f(x)\nu_e.
\]

The lifted support of channel \(e\) is the Minkowski sum
\(\widetilde A_e=A_e+\sum_{f\ne e}B_f\). For every direction, including support
ties,

\[
h_{\widetilde A_e}(w)
=h_{A_e}(w)+\sum_{f\ne e}h_{B_f}(w)
=h_D(w)+\tau_e(w).
\]

The additive height \(h_D(w)\) is common to all channels, so it changes neither
their ordering nor any cross-channel tie. If several product terms collide at
one exponent, their coefficients add positively; no tied source disappears.
The same argument follows directly from the log-ray asymptotic
\(\rho_e(e^{tw})=e^{t\tau_e(w)}(c_e(w)+o(1))\), where \(c_e(w)>0\) is the ratio
of the sums of *all* tied leading coefficients. The exact structural decision
therefore needs only the sparse supports. It does not need to construct
\(\widetilde A_e\), whose explicit tuple count can be
\(\sum_e |A_e|\prod_{f\ne e}|B_f|\).

This support argument requires positive coefficients. With signed terms,
leading terms can cancel on a tie face, and the support-only rule is false
without additional algebraic work. Arbitrary positive coefficient magnitudes
do not change the structural answer, but their positivity is part of the
input promise.

## 3. Refutation witnesses and the coNP upper bound

Failure is exactly the existential statement

\[
\exists w\in\mathbb R^d,\exists e:\quad
w\cdot\nu_e>0\quad\text{and}\quad
\bigl(w\cdot\nu_f\ne0\Longrightarrow\tau_e(w)\ge\tau_f(w)\bigr)
\ \forall f.
\tag{1}
\]

The weak \(\ge\) is essential: equality preserves an exact top tie and a single
outward member of that tie refutes the property. The implication excludes
neutral \(f\) before order comparisons.

Suppose (1) has a real witness. Select one maximizing exponent from each
numerator and denominator support at that \(w\), and select the sign/neutral
status of every \(w\cdot\nu_f\). Once these finite choices are fixed, all
support-maximizer constraints, score comparisons, and neutral constraints are
rational linear equalities or weak inequalities. The nonzero reaction
projections are strict inequalities. Normalize one coordinate of \(w\) to
\(+1\) or \(-1\) while bounding all coordinates in \([-1,1]\); introduce a
margin \(\delta\) and require each selected nonzero signed projection to be at
least \(\delta\), with \(0\le\delta\le1\). A real witness gives a feasible
point with \(\delta>0\). The bounded rational LP has an optimal rational basic
solution with positive rational \(\delta\); standard determinant bounds give
polynomial bit length in the encoded rational coefficients. Thus every
non-endotactic input has a polynomial-bit rational direction witness.

The witness itself is directly checkable in polynomial time: compute all
\(w\cdot\nu_e\), remove the zero projections, scan each explicit support to
compute its exact maximum, and verify that an outward channel ties the maximum
active score. Therefore `NON-ENDOTACTIC` is in NP and `ENDOTACTIC` is in coNP.

## 4. coNP-hardness with constant-size positive supports

Reduce a 3CNF formula with three distinct variables per clause. This restricted
3SAT remains NP-complete; clauses of fewer than three literals can be replaced
by fresh-variable 3-clauses, e.g. \((a\lor b)\) by
\((a\lor b\lor t)\land(a\lor b\lor\neg t)\), and
\((a)\) by the four clauses \((a\lor \pm s\lor \pm t)\). Delete tautological
clauses first. Let the direction coordinates be \((u_1,\ldots,u_n,z)\), one per
Boolean variable plus one guard coordinate. A literal on variable (i) has
sign \(\sigma\in\{+1,-1\}\). Make the following channels, with every
coefficient equal to one:

| Channel | Rate \(P/Q\) | Jump \(\nu\) |
|---|---|---|
| target (T) | \(1\) | \(+e_z\) |
| variable (V_i) | \(X_i Z/(X_i^2+1)\) | \(-2e_z\) |
| clause (C=(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)) | \(Z\prod_{i\in C}X_i/Q_C\) | \(\sum_{\ell=(i,\sigma)\in C}\sigma e_i-3e_z\) |
| guard (G_i^+\) | \(X_i/Z^2\) | \(-e_i\) |
| guard (G_i^-\) | \(1/(X_i Z^2)\) | \(+e_i\) |

For \(C\), set

\[
Q_C=
\sum_{(i,+)\in C}X_i^2\prod_{j\in C\setminus\{i\}}X_j
+\sum_{(i,-)\in C}\prod_{j\in C\setminus\{i\}}X_j.
\]

Every numerator and denominator has at most three positive-coefficient
monomials, with nonnegative integer exponents; every denominator is positive
for positive concentrations. The channel count is \(3n+m+1\), dimension is
\(n+1\), and all exponents/jumps have constant bit height. In a sparse term
encoding this is a polynomial-size construction.

The direction scores and projections are

\[
\tau_T=0,\quad \tau_{V_i}=z-|u_i|,\quad
\tau_C=z-\max_{(i,\sigma)\in C}\sigma u_i,\quad
\tau_{G_i^+}=u_i-2z,\quad \tau_{G_i^-}=-u_i-2z,
\]

\[
w\cdot\nu_T=z,\quad w\cdot\nu_{V_i}=-2z,\quad
w\cdot\nu_C=\sum_{(i,\sigma)\in C}\sigma u_i-3z,\quad
w\cdot\nu_{G_i^+}=-u_i,\quad w\cdot\nu_{G_i^-}=u_i.
\]

The clause order follows because the term associated with literal
\((i,\sigma)\) in (Q_C) has directional height
\(\sum_{j\in C}u_j+\sigma u_i\), while the numerator height is
\(z+\sum_{j\in C}u_j\).

### Satisfiable formula implies failure

For a satisfying assignment choose \(z=1\), and choose \(u_i=1\) if variable
\(i\) is true and \(u_i=-1\) otherwise. The target is active, outward, and has
score zero. Every variable channel has score zero and projection \(-2\). Every
satisfied clause has score zero; if it has \(r\ge1\) true literals, its
projection is \(2r-6\le0\), so it is inward or neutral. For each variable,
its two guards have scores \(-1\) and \(-3\), in an order determined by the
truth assignment. Thus the target is an outward member of an exact top tie. This proves
the reduction does not rely on perturbing away ties.

### Unsatisfiable formula implies no failure, for every direction

For a clause write \(S_C=\sum_C(\sigma u_i-z)\); its reaction projection is
exactly \(S_C\).

* If \(z>0\), target \(T\) is active at score zero. Variable channels are
  inward. An outward clause has (S_C>0), so some \(\sigma u_i>z\), giving
  \(\tau_C<0\); it cannot be top. Each outward guard also has score below
  zero: (G_i^+\) is outward only for (u_i<0), and (G_i^-\) only for
  (u_i>0), and in either case its score is negative. If (T) is top, then
  all variable scores are at most zero, hence \(|u_i|\ge z\). Every active
  clause score is at most zero, so its largest signed literal is at least
  \(z\). A neutral clause has \(S_C=0\), which gives
  \(\frac{1}{3}\sum_C\sigma u_i=z\); its largest signed literal is therefore
  at least \(z\) as well. Thus every clause has a literal with
  \(\sigma u_i\ge z\). The signs
  of the \(u_i\) then give a satisfying
  assignment, contrary to the hypothesis. If (T) is not top, the active
  maximum is strictly above zero, whereas every outward channel is below zero;
  the maximum is inward.
* If \(z<0\), target \(T\) is active, inward, and at score zero; every
  variable score is strictly below zero. An outward clause again has
  \(\tau_C<0\). Any outward guard is strictly below its inward twin: for
  (u_i<0), \(G_i^+\) is outward and
  \(\tau_{G_i^-}-\tau_{G_i^+}=-2u_i>0\); the (u_i>0) case is symmetric.
  So no outward channel is an active top maximizer.
* If \(z=0\), target and variable channels are neutral. Put
  \(H=\max_i|u_i|\). If (H=0), all channels are neutral and the test is
  vacuous. If (H>0), a guard at a coordinate with magnitude (H) has score
  (H) and points inward. Every guard has score at most (H); every clause
  has score \(-\max_C\sigma u_i\le H\). An outward clause has
  \(\sum_C\sigma u_i>0\), so \(\max_C\sigma u_i>0\) and its score is below
  zero. An outward guard has score (-|u_i|\le0<H\). Thus every active top
  channel at (z=0) is inward. The paired guards close the hyperplane that a
  Boolean-direction-only argument would miss.

It follows that the constructed system is endotactic iff the formula is
unsatisfiable. Hence `ENDOTACTIC` is coNP-hard and, with Section 3, coNP-complete
for this sparse positive-polynomial encoding. In particular this hardness
persists with at most three support terms per numerator/denominator and exact
support evaluation linear in the number of listed terms. Unless \(P=NP\), no
general polynomial-time exact recognizer exists in the dimension-varying
model. This result does not rule out an FPT algorithm in dimension.

## 5. Executable decision and exact statuses

`recognize_sparse_rational.py` encodes (1) directly as one existential
quantifier-free linear-real-arithmetic formula per candidate outward channel.
Each maximum is an exact rational `If`; the edge-activity test is an exact
nonzero implication. Therefore it avoids a prior explicit enumeration of
Minkowski-expanded sources and does not construct one LP for every fan cell.

The output contract is:

* `REFUTED`: Z3 found a rational direction and the separate `Fraction`
  implementation recomputed supports, neutral labels, all relevant maxima,
  and exact ties. For `--property endotactic`, it confirms an outward active
  top-tied channel. For `--property strong`, it either confirms that weak
  failure or confirms that a non-orthogonal direction has no inward channel
  at the global all-source maximum.
* `CERTIFIED`: for endotacticity, every candidate outward-channel formula is
  exactly `unsat`. For strong endotacticity, those formulas are unsat and a
  further formula for a direction with no inward global-maximum channel is
  unsat. The result trusts the installed Z3 exact arithmetic solver; the
  program records its version and does not emit a separately proof-checked
  UNSAT proof object.
* `UNKNOWN`: a solver timeout/resource interruption, explicit check-budget
  exhaustion, unavailable Z3 package, non-rational/unverifiable SAT model, or
  any solver `unknown` response. No such state is promoted to either answer.

The strong-refutation formula is the exact QF-LRA sentence

\[
\bigvee_e(w\cdot\nu_e\ne0)
\quad\land\quad
\bigwedge_f(\tau_f=\max_j\tau_j\Longrightarrow w\cdot\nu_f\ge0).
\]

It includes neutral channels at the lifted diagram's all-source maximum.
Its negation is the standard strong endotactic condition after requiring
endotacticity. The strong property is also in coNP: a counterdirection is
either a weak failure witness from Section 3, or one with an active channel
but no inward channel at the global maximum. Fixing support maximizers, one
global top channel, and the strict/equality/weak ordering of every score
against it reduces a real witness to a rational polyhedral branch. The same
bounded-margin LP argument gives a polynomial-bit rational witness. No
coNP-hardness lower bound for this stronger property is claimed here.

### Fixed-dimension arrangement bound

There is also a deterministic XP algorithm parameterized by direction/species
dimension \(d\). Form a central arrangement from channel hyperplanes
\(\nu_e\cdot w=0\) and within-support comparisons
\((a-a')\cdot w=0\) for pairs in every \(A_e\) and \(B_e\). Its number of
input hyperplanes is at most

\[
H=m+\sum_e\left[{|A_e|\choose2}+{|B_e|\choose2}\right].
\]

Every relatively open face fixes channel neutrality/sign and a maximizing
support term in each numerator and denominator. On that face each \(\tau_e\)
is a fixed linear form (any tied maximizer gives the same value throughout the
face). For each face and each outward active candidate \(e\), solve an exact
rational LP imposing the face equalities, signed nonzero arrangement forms at
least one, and \(\tau_e\ge\tau_f\) for every active \(f\). The unit margin is
exact: all constraints are homogeneous, and any point in the open face can be
positively scaled until every nonzero arrangement form has magnitude at least
one. Feasibility gives a counterdirection; exhausting the faces proves the
property.

A central arrangement with \(H\) hyperplanes in dimension \(d\) has
\(H^{O(d)}\) faces. Thus this method is polynomial in the explicit sparse
input for each fixed \(d\), and XP—not FPT—in \(d\). This is a theoretical
alternative, not the delivered implementation: the executable submits a
compact exact QF-LRA formula and does not explicitly enumerate arrangement
faces. Neither method removes the coNP-hard, potentially exponential cost when
dimension varies.

The top-level implication for `CERTIFIED` is exactly the selected finite
structural property above. It does not certify a global absorber, N33's proof,
a biological model fit, or stochastic permanence. coNP-hardness rules out a
polynomial-time general recognizer unless \(P=NP\); it does not establish a
particular exponential lower bound. The current implementation has one solver
call per channel, but Z3's internal search has no polynomial-time guarantee.
Input acquisition and validation costs include \(d\), \(m\), all
numerator/denominator support terms, rational exponent/jump bit lengths, and
positive coefficient validation. Exact model rechecking takes
\(O(d\sum_e(|A_e|+|B_e|))\) rational dot products; coefficient magnitudes do
not enter \(\tau\) after their positive signs are validated.

## 6. Conditional dynamical consequence and its gate

Suppose a rational-rate system has

\[
\dot x=\sum_e\kappa_e(t)\rho_e(x)\nu_e,
\qquad 0<\underline\kappa\le\kappa_e(t)\le\overline\kappa<\infty,
\]

with measurable rates and the finite positive-support input above. Clearing
denominators gives a finite generalized-power-law diagram with sources
\(A_e+\sum_{f\ne e}B_f\) and the same arrows ν_e. Every lifted coefficient
is a positive product of input coefficients times \(\kappa_e(t)\); because there are
finitely many terms, these coefficients lie in a common compact interval
bounded away from zero. A positive clock \(ds/dt=1/D(x(t))\), equivalently
\(dt/ds=D(x(s))\), reparameterizes the vector field to that diagram and
composes the measurable schedules with the clock. The tropical identity proves
that its essential-source property is exactly the rational-rate property.

For the strong branch, the standard Gopalkrishnan--Miller--Shiu theorem already
proves permanence for strongly endotactic confined reaction systems. Their
network definition permits arbitrary real complexes, and their tempering
permits each reaction multiplier to range in a fixed compact positive
interval. The finite lifted terms here have such intervals; their coefficient
schedules are linked through the original \(\kappa_e(t)\), which is a subset
of the independently varying schedules covered by that result. Thus the
strong branch is a computable structural transfer to established prior art,
not a new permanence theorem.

For the general endotactic branch, if the conditional N33 all-endotactic
permanence candidate is valid, this recognition result supplies its structural
premise to the lifted system.
Once the lifted trajectory is in N33's compact positive class absorber,
continuous positive (D) has a positive lower bound there, so the inverse
clock continues for unbounded original time. This is a conditional transfer,
not an independently established general permanence theorem. It inherits
N33's external-proof/priority gate and the positive lower/upper rate bounds;
it makes no stochastic, noisy-fit, or weak-reversibility claim. Existing
two-species power-law permanence results cover that narrower slice. Current
report coverage says the all-dimensional general endotactic permanence
conjecture remains source-candidate-level, not externally cleared.

## 7. Falsifiers and next gate

The support identity fails if a signed coefficient cancellation removes a
maximizing monomial, if an input denominator vanishes on the positive
orthant, or if the implementation counts a neutral channel or drops one
member of an exact top tie. A concrete implementation falsifier is any
`REFUTED` output whose exact Fraction replay does not satisfy (1), or any
`CERTIFIED` output for a known SAT-reduction witness. The current smoke checks
exercise an exact SAT tie, a reversible one-species endotactic/strong positive
control, a neutral high-source channel that separates weak from strong, and
the budget-to-`UNKNOWN` transition.

The next mathematically useful gate is independent verification of the
coNP-hardness reduction and of the exact rational witness-size argument, then
an external review of the N33 affine/weighted-drift proof before using this
compiler to claim permanence. An application gate would require a complete
measured gross-flux inventory with fixed positive supports and a validated
coefficient/rate box; none was acquired in this task.
