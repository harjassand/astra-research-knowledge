# Independent stochastic consequence of an inward affine certificate

7 October 2026. This is a proved conditional-interface theorem: the jump-process
bound follows from the supplied strict inward-facet certificate by an elementary
stopped-generator argument. It does not depend on an unproved large-deviation
principle, attracting equilibrium, a stochastic simulation, or an autonomous
rate schedule. Acquiring the certificate for every endotactic network remains
conditional on the source149 affine mechanism and this campaign's separate
all-endotactic transfer. Historical priority is not cleared.

## Exact interface

Let nu_r be finitely many fixed integer reaction increments. The concentration
process has jumps nu_r/V and predictable stochastic intensities

    V a_r^V(t),  a_r^V(t) >= 0.

One channel fires at each jump. A simultaneous batched change would require
its combined increment and intensity to be included as another channel; bounds
on individual-channel intensities alone would not control such a batch.

The intensities may depend on the past and the current pre-jump state. They must
be predictable/nonanticipating, not merely adapted labels that could encode
future information. No Markov or deterministic-ODE feedback uniqueness is
assumed. Work up to the first exit from a compact polytope in the process's
fixed affine stoichiometric class,

    Q = {x in c+S: g_i(x)=n_i dot x+b_i >= 0, i=1,...,m}.

Assume Q is contained in the strictly positive orthant and x_0 is a supplied
lattice-compatible point of its relative interior. Retain only proper relative
facets; constraints constant on c+S cannot be crossed and supply no inward
noise problem. Set

    s_ir = n_i dot nu_r,
    J_i = max_r |s_ir| > 0,
    a_r^V <= abar_r on Q,  Lambda=sum_r abar_r,
    Q_i = sum_r abar_r s_ir^2 > 0.

For each facet there are supplied collar width eta_i>0 and inward margin delta_i>0
such that, for every pre-exit state in Q with 0<=g_i<=eta_i and every allowed
predictable control,

    sum_r a_r^V(t) s_ir >= delta_i.

This is an actual finite-V drift condition. Replacing it by its ODE limit without
charging combinatorial corrections would be incorrect. Choose theta_i>0 with

    theta_i J_i <= 1,
    theta_i exp(theta_i J_i) Q_i / 2 <= delta_i.

One explicit choice is min{1/J_i, 2 delta_i/(e Q_i)}. Any smaller choice is valid.
No norm normalization of facet normals is required: their scaling is reflected
in eta, delta, J, Q and theta, and the dimensionless products theta*g are the
quantities that matter.

Let tau=inf{t: X_t not in Q}, the first actual jump outside the closed polytope.
Let alpha_i=theta_i g_i(x_0), rho_i=theta_i eta_i and
B_i=Lambda[exp(theta_i J_i)-1]. Then for every finite T,

    P(tau <= T) <= sum_i exp(-V alpha_i)
                  + V T sum_i B_i exp(-V rho_i).                 (1)

In particular, if all initial gaps are positive and 0<c<rho_*:=min_i rho_i,

    P(tau <= exp(cV)) <= sum_i exp(-V alpha_i)
                       + V sum_i B_i exp[-V(rho_i-c)] -> 0.       (2)

Thus one fixed concentration polytope protects all species for exponentially
long physical time, uniformly over the stated bounded predictable controls.
The small error, its prefactor, V threshold, and c are explicit certificate data.
The statement does not promise survival forever, mixing, equilibrium,
irreducibility, or deterministic/stochastic path closeness over that horizon.

## Complete proof of (1)

Put phi_i(x)=exp[-theta_i V g_i(x)] and Phi=sum_i phi_i. The predictable
generator of phi_i at a pre-exit state x is

    L_t phi_i(x)
      = V phi_i(x) sum_r a_r^V(t)[exp(-theta_i s_ir)-1].

For |s|<=J, Taylor's integral remainder gives

    exp(-theta s)-1 <= -theta s+(theta^2 s^2/2)exp(theta J).

Inside the facet collar, the displayed drift/noise conditions imply

    L_t phi_i <= V phi_i[-theta_i delta_i
                        +(theta_i^2/2)exp(theta_i J_i)Q_i] <= 0.

Outside its collar but still in Q, phi_i<=exp(-V rho_i), and every summand's
positive part is at most a_r^V[exp(theta_i J_i)-1]. Therefore

    L_t phi_i <= V B_i exp(-V rho_i) on all of Q,
    L_t Phi <= V sum_i B_i exp(-V rho_i).                         (3)

The pre-exit jump intensity is bounded by V Lambda. Thus there is no explosion
before tau. A single exit jump changes g_i by s_ir/V, so every post-exit margin
is at least -J_i/V. Consequently

    0 <= Phi(X_{t wedge tau}) <= sum_i exp(theta_i J_i),

uniformly in t and V. With finite expected stopped jump count V Lambda T,
the compensated-counting-process identity for this bounded stopped function is
a genuine martingale identity, not merely an uncontrolled local martingale.
Applying it at T wedge tau and using (3) yields

    E Phi(X_{T wedge tau}) <= Phi(x_0)+V T sum_i B_i exp(-V rho_i).

On {tau<=T}, some facet has g_i(X_tau)<0, hence phi_i(X_tau)>1 and Phi>=1.
On its complement Phi>=0. Markov's inequality proves (1). There is no reflection,
no clipping of the physical jump and no assumption that an exit lands on a
facet. Multiple facets, corners and overlapping possible excursions are covered
by the sum. This is the same conclusion independently identified by worker
statistical_mechanics, with the generator and stopping scope independently
audited by reaction_control_audit.

If safety is defined as hitting a facet rather than leaving the closed Q, the
same proof with tau=inf{g_i<=0 for some i} uses Phi>=1 at hitting and remains
valid. The strictly positive initial-buffer requirement is unchanged.

## Exact finite-volume mass action

For integer source complex y_r, m_r=sum_j y_rj, use the convention

    a_r^V(x,kappa)=kappa_r V^{-m_r} product_j (Vx_j)_{y_rj},
    lambda_r^V=V a_r^V,

where (n)_q=n(n-1)...(n-q+1), with (n)_0=1 and a zero factor when n<q.
Any reaction-factorial denominators are absorbed consistently into kappa_r.
For 0<=x_j<=R with R>=1, put

    C_r=sum_j y_rj(y_rj-1)/2,
    E_r=kappa_r,max C_r R^{m_r-1}     (E_r=0 if m_r<=1).

For every lattice state, including states with insufficient reactant counts,

    0 <= kappa_r x^{y_r}-a_r^V(x,kappa) <= E_r/V.                 (4)

To prove (4) when every count is sufficient, telescope the product of factors
x_j-l/V and bound the other m_r-1 factors by R. The sum of the shifts is C_r/V.
If some count n_j<y_rj, the stochastic factor is zero. For a univariate factor
q>=2, n<q implies n<=q-1<=q(q-1)/2, and
x^q<= [q(q-1)/(2V)]R^{q-1}. Telescoping across species gives the same bound.
This proof does not require a new positive-volume threshold just to establish
the error estimate.

It follows that abar_r=kappa_r,max R^{m_r} is valid. If the supplied polynomial
certificate has deterministic inward margin gamma_i in the collar uniformly
over kappa in its allowed box, define

    D_i=sum_r |s_ir| E_r.

Then the true finite-V inward drift is at least gamma_i-D_i/V. For

    V >= max_i 2D_i/gamma_i,

one may take delta_i=gamma_i/2 and
theta_i=min{1/J_i, gamma_i/(e Q_i)}. This explicitly pays the factorial
correction. A direct finite-V generator inequality can yield substantially
better constants and avoid that conservative threshold, as in the example below.

## How source149 provides the needed certificate

The exact October 5 source's `trapping.tex` defines a finite affine minimum F_h
whose compact plateau K_h is the intersection of affine superlevel sets. At a
proper relative facet, its nonconstant affine label is active, lies outside
S-perp, and has strictly positive projected drift. With rates in a compact
positive box, the campaign's independently audited flux estimate supplies a
uniform positive margin for those facets. Continuity on the compact facet and
the rate box then supplies a positive collar. A known rational polytope,
certified collar width and strict drift inequalities are enough for theorem (1).

The original source states weakly reversible fixed-rate permanence. The broad
all-endotactic, bounded-rate consequence is this campaign's reconstruction and
is not silently upgraded to external validation. The universal transfer also
needs integer source complexes to be a literal chemical jump model; the
deterministic real-exponent extension does not itself define falling-factorial
reaction counts.

Acquisition is not free. Given rational input facets, integer complexes and
rates, the collar inequality is polynomial and affine in each rate. All rate
corners, and the relative facet/collar geometry, can be certified by exact
semialgebraic methods or convergent rational subdivision once strict positivity
has been established. This is a terminating existence of a certificate search,
not a polynomial-time claim. The release gives no useful uniform bound on the
number of affine pieces, coefficient bit lengths, or required scale. If those
are not supplied, they must be acquired and charged.

The starting lattice point must actually lie in the fixed affine class and have
the stated buffer. Rounding a real starting point can change conservation laws;
that must be separately accounted for. Starting on a facet can give an O(1)
probability of immediate exit and cannot have the asserted exponential safety.

## Explicit robust chemical example and finite certificate

Take A <-> B with N_A+N_B=V, and x=N_A/V. Both rate coefficients can be arbitrary
predictable functions of the past, in [9/10,11/10]. The exact scaled intensities
are

    birth of A: kappa_B(1-x),  death of A: kappa_A x.

Choose Q=[1/10,9/10], g_-=x-1/10, g_+=9/10-x, collar widths eta=1/10, and
theta=log 3. This direct choice does not use Taylor bounds. In the left collar
x in [1/10,1/5],

    (L phi_-)/(V phi_-)
      = -(2/3)kappa_B(1-x)+2kappa_A x
      <= -(2/3)(9/10)(4/5)+2(11/10)(1/5) = -1/25.

In the right collar x in [4/5,9/10],

    (L phi_+)/(V phi_+)
      = 2kappa_B(1-x)-(2/3)kappa_A x <= -1/25.

Each expression is affine in x and in each rate separately. Its 16 endpoint/rate
corner checks therefore certify the whole intervals. The total scaled rate is
at most Lambda=11/10. Starting at x_0=1/2 for even V, theorem (1) gives

    P(exit Q before T) <= 2*3^{-2V/5}+(22/5) V T 3^{-V/10}.       (5)

Set T=3^{V/20}. For V=1000, T=3^50 approx 7.17898*10^23 reaction-time units, and

    P(exit before T) <= 2*3^-400+4400*3^-50 < 7*10^-21.

The reaction-count envelope over this horizon is (11/10)VT approx 7.90*10^26.
The certificate evaluates a finite expression; it does not execute or simulate
that many events. This is a reliable answer to the safety question under the
stated ideal kinetic model, not a claim that a physical vessel runs for that
duration without any additional processes or changes in the supplied rates.

`chemical_jump_checks.py` performs the exact rational corner checks and the
last exact rational probability comparison. It uses no rare-event sampling.
The global probability conclusion rests on the proof above, not on those
finite algebra checks.

## Decisive falsifications and limits

1. Infinite stochastic permanence is false even here. At constant rates one,
   N_A is an irreducible finite birth-death chain on {0,...,V}; it almost surely
   visits both zero-species endpoints, and does so repeatedly. The deterministic
   ODE converges to x=1/2. Hence a permanent ODE cannot imply never hitting the
   stochastic boundary. Finite exponential safety is the precise surviving claim.
2. Strict inward margin is necessary at this interface. An unbiased random walk
   with jumps 1/V and total intensity O(V) exits a fixed interval on a time scale
   O(V), rather than exp(cV). A nonnegative but zero drift condition does not
   prove (2).
3. An initial zero buffer makes the first term in (1) at least one. Positive
   concentrations alone do not imply a useful bound for a chosen polytope.
4. Unbounded state jumps or controls outside the declared rate box invalidate
   the generator inequalities. Neither is covered by deterministic permanence.
5. The theorem does not claim quasi-potential sharpness. Its exponent may be
   small and conservative for a given network. It offers a computable lower
   reliability exponent after certificate acquisition, not an optimal rare-event
   asymptotic or full trajectory simulator.
6. Even global stochastic nonexplosion cannot be inferred solely from
   endotactic deterministic permanence. Anderson, Cappelletti, Kim and Nguyen's
   Example 3.2 gives the strongly endotactic network
   0 -> 2A -> 4A+B -> 6A+4B -> 3A, whose stochastic model is explosive for every
   positive choice of fixed rate constants. The inspected proof exhibits an
   infinite reaction sequence of positive probability with finite expected
   elapsed time. See the exact primary example at
   https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05328v2#S3 . The present compact stopped bound
   neither requires nor supplies global nonexplosion after exit.

## Prior art and novelty boundary

Exponential exit-time behavior and exponential Lyapunov estimates for stochastic
chemical networks have established theory. Agazzi, Dembo and Eckmann's primary
paper proves large deviations and Wentzell--Freidlin exit asymptotics; its Lemma
2.1 and the immediately following proof already use a stopped exponential
Lyapunov expectation to obtain safety over exp(beta V) horizons. Exact pages
7-9 and Theorem 1.15 were inspected: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.02126 and the
author's PDF https://fiteoweb.unige.ch/~eckmannj/ps_files/andreaamir.pdf .

Its Lemma 2.1 controls escape to large concentration norm; its exit-time
asymptotics additionally impose domain and attractor/quasi-potential hypotheses.
That inspected lemma does not itself supply this report's lower-coordinate
facet certificate or the uniform predictable-control formulation. This is a
scoped comparison, not a claim that no older work proves such formulations.

The mathematical delta here is the explicit finite-facet certificate
(1), robust to predictable history-dependent rates, directly composable with
the recent affine plateau mechanism, with exact factorial errors and no
attractor/quasi-potential acquisition. This composition may provide a usable
broader certificate class if the all-endotactic affine transfer survives
external review. I do not claim invention of the exponential stopping method,
priority for the robust-control formulation, or a new first example of chemical
metastability.

## Retained resource ledger

Supplied or acquired inputs: exact reaction list and source matching, volume V,
integer counts, conservation class, finite facet description, collar widths,
rate bounds, drift-margin proof, projected jump and intensity bounds. Generic
certificate assembly requires O(m|R|) projected quantities after the polynomial
and geometry certificates have been acquired; the latter may be very expensive.
Evaluating the final bound needs the precision to represent its logarithm and
finite sums. Full stochastic simulation retains particle counts and executes
O(VT) expected events within the rate envelope, whereas this deliverable answers
only the exit-probability safety question from a finite proof object.

Independent mathematical audits: statistical_mechanics proposed the summed
facet Lyapunov route independently; reaction_control_audit checked its generator,
predictability, exact factorial correction and stopped-Dynkin justification.
These are internal checks, not external correctness or novelty certification.

## Finite graph transport follow-through

`chemical_graph_certificate.md` proves the finite graph extension for a full
dimensional Q, equal local volumes and bounded symmetric predictable edge
coefficients common across species. The exit bound explicitly multiplies by
the number of sites and charges molecular hopping turnover. Its exact
two-species example also demonstrates failure of automatic product-polytope
invariance when the species hop at different rates. The 200 common-coefficient
corner checks and buffered unequal-coefficient counterexample are recorded in
`chemical_graph_checks.json`; no stochastic trajectories are simulated.
