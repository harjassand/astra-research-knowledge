## Exact cross-force factor reconstructed

Maxwell wave potentials are linear in charge/current. Replacing a single source f by species alpha consequently multiplies its complete retarded field kernel, including the transport term and its initial boundary term, by e_alpha. The receiver beta feels lambda_beta(E+a cross B). Hence the force contribution is lambda_beta e_alpha times the charge-free kernel and the unsigned label g_alpha,0. The source acceleration inside that kernel is u'=(lambda_alpha/q)(I-u tensor u)(E+u cross B). It must NOT be multiplied by a second lambda_alpha outside the complete kernel.

The source/receiver times obey t'=d/D and r'=d/D-1 for every timelike characteristic, regardless charge signs. With H=I+n tensor a/D, g=(u-n)/d and k0=u-ne/D, the algebraic identity Hg=k0/d holds. The product rule yields

  -H/r partial_s^(u)g
  =-(Hg/r)' +(H/r)'g +(H/r) partial_s^(n)g.

For an a-variation A, partial_a k0[A]=n(A.k0)/D; together with a'=a_t d/D this gives n(a_t.k0)/(r D^2). Put N0=r n'. The remaining numerator after multiplying by r^2 d^2 is

  r d (k0)'_n + k0(N0.u-d r'-q^-2).

Using |u|^2=1-q^-2 gives N0.u-d r'-q^-2=-ed/D. Direct differentiation gives r d(k0)'_n=-(ed/D)[N0+n(N0.a)/D], and |a|^2=1-q_X^-2 gives

  N0+n(N0.a)/D+k0=(d/D)[a-n/(q_X^2 D)].

Thus the full kernel is exactly

  -(k0/(r d))'
  +e/(r^2 D^2)[n/(q_X^2 D)-a]
  +n(a_t.k0)/(r D^2).

Multiplication of BOTH force terms by their common signed constant lambda_beta e_alpha preserves this cancellation. Absolute values may then be taken with |lambda_beta e_alpha|g_alpha,0. Source acceleration disappears from the two residual terms without an appeal to positivity. Finally a_t=(I-a tensor a)K_beta/q_X because K_beta already includes lambda_beta; another receiver-charge factor would be an error.
