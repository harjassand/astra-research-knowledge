STATEMENT

Fix q>=2, beta>0 and B>=0, and use the complete local SU(q) generators
Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2, J_a=sum_i T_a^(i), C_N=sum_a J_a^2.
Let cF=(q^2-1)/(2q), D=q^2-1 and

 R=8beta+4B+4,   a=exp(-2R)/q,
 Hstar=beta+B/sqrt(2),
 Kstar=q beta(1+cF)+qB/sqrt(2),
 Lstar=2q sqrt(beta(Hstar+1)),
 eta=exp[q log(a)-Kstar-Lstar],
 Mstar=beta eta^2/80 >0.

For ANY fixed real symmetric A with ||A||<=Mstar and real |b|<=B,
and any field amplitude 0<=u<=1 (allowed to depend on N), the WHOLE Gibbs
density

 rho_N proportional to exp[((beta I+A) quadratic in J)/N +u J_b]

has an explicitly specified known-parameter positive identical-q-product
mixture sigma_N with

 T(rho_N,sigma_N)<=(2Cseed+Hstar N)exp(-2N),             (F1)
 Cseed=exp(1/beta)(8beta)^(D/2)Gamma(D/2+1),

for every integer N>=max{q, q(q-1)/(2sqrt(2)),1}. The bound can be capped
at one. The perturbation radius Mstar is extremely small and depends on q,
beta and B. This is a uniform neighborhood theorem, not a result for arbitrary
fixed anisotropy. No efficient preparation or finite-bit diffusion compiler
is supplied.

The scientific extension to a fixed small A/N rather than A/N^kappa with
kappa>1 was proposed by root and communicated by c04_s01/c04_s02. This
corollary independently closes the stopping/seed algebra using the explicit
positive heat law from revisions/03_su_q_casimir_stopped_filter.txt. It is an
alternate verification route, not an independent-origin priority claim.

FULL DERIVATION

1. Acquire a seed and separate the exact target.
The positive Weyl/Gaussian identity in Section1 of revision03 gives the
exact normalized heat seed rho0=exp[beta C_N/(2N)]/Zbase as the mixture
r_h^tensor N, r_h=exp h/Tr exp h, density proportional to

 exp[-N Tr(h^2)/beta] product_(i<j)sinch((h_i-h_j)/2)
                                             [Tr exp h]^N.

The Section2 elementary tail derivation, with t0=beta/(2N), gives
 P(||h||_F>R)<=Cseed exp(-c0N), c0=R^2/(8beta).
Replace every exterior branch by I/q, retaining its probability. The
resulting seed rho0' differs in trace norm by at most twice that tail and
all its local branches obey r>=a I.

Set alpha=1/N, C=(beta/2)I+A and H=Q_C/N+uJ_b. The Casimir commutes with
all collective J_a, so exp(M_H)rho0, M_H X={H,X}/2, is exactly the target
unnormalized Gibbs operator divided by Zbase. No Trotter error or changed
Hamiltonian is introduced.

2. Certify the local diffusion.
For k_N(r)=r^tensor N, the exact Lie/filter operators
 B_T=2N Tr(T r)+v_T.derivative, v_T={T,r}-2Tr(T r)r,
 R_T=z_T.derivative, z_T=i[T,r],
give M_H k_N=[(1/(4N))sum_j lambda_j(B_j^2-R_j^2)+uB_b/2]k_N,
where C=sum_j lambda_j n_j n_j^T. The potential is V=Tr(H k_N).
This fixes the polynomial drift and signed covariance

 D_C=sum_j lambda_j(v_j tensor v_j-z_j tensor z_j)

exactly; the SDE generator is G-V with covariance D_C/(2N).
For every traceless Frobenius tangent Y,

 <Y,D_IY>=2Tr[r(Y-Tr(rY)I)r(Y-Tr(rY)I)],
 D_I>=2lambda_min(r)^2 I,       |D_A|<=10||A|| I.

Thus, even on r>=eta I/2,
 D_C>=(beta eta^2/4-10Mstar)I=beta eta^2 I/8>0.
Also C>=0, ||C||<=beta/2+Mstar<=beta, and D_C<=10beta I.
Every margin is public. The positive square root gives a smooth diffusion
on a neighborhood of r>=eta I; its small margin is not treated as free.

Run the declared diffusion from each retained seed until tau, the first
time lambda_min(r_t)=eta, then freeze r and w_t=exp[integral V dt]. Its
weighted product expectation W_t is positive and an identical-product cone
element at all times.

3. Bound the exit using exact logdet coefficients.
Write mu_a=Tr(T_a r), Z_C=sum_j lambda_j T_(n_j)^2 and ell=logdet r.
Direct filtering/rotation differentiation gives exactly

 L ell=-(q/N)[(2N-1)mu^TCmu+Tr(rZ_C)]-u q mu_b,
 d<ell_martingale>/dt=(2q^2/N)mu^TCmu.

The identities |mu|^2=(Tr r^2-1/q)/2<=1/2,
Z_C<=beta cF I, and |mu_b|<=B/sqrt(2) imply

 L ell>=-Kstar,     <ell_martingale>_1<=q^2 beta/N.

Initially ell>=q log(a), while the stopping boundary satisfies
ell<=log eta=q log(a)-Kstar-Lstar (because det r<=eta). If tau<=1, the
martingale must drop by at least Lstar. The exponential martingale maximal
inequality therefore gives

 P(tau<=1)<=exp[-Lstar^2 N/(2q^2 beta)]
           =exp[-2(Hstar+1)N].                          (F2)

No factor involving 1/lambda_min(r) enters these exact logdet drift or
quadratic-variation formulas. It enters the PSD region and numerical
square-root/acquisition cost through eta.

4. Compare whole densities and normalize.
Completeness gives the exact Casimir maximum
||C_N||=N(q-1)(N+q)/(2q). For N>=q,

 ||H||<=beta ||C_N||/N+u BN/sqrt(2)<=Hstar N.

Hence weights and sandwich-semigroup trace norms are bounded by exp(Hstar N).
The exact stopped Ito residual and quantum Duhamel comparison (same as
revision03 Section6) give

 ||exp(M_H)rho0-W_1||_1
 <=2 Cseed exp[(Hstar-c0)N]
       +Hstar N exp[2Hstar N] P(tau<=1).

Since R=8beta+4B+4,
 c0=8beta+8(B+1)+2(B+1)^2/beta >=Hstar+2.
Use (F2) to bound this by (2Cseed+Hstar N)exp(-2N).
The unnormalized target trace is >=1: rho0 is SU(q)-invariant, so its mean
J_b is zero, Q_C is positive, and scalar Jensen gives
Tr(rho0 exp H)>=exp[Tr(rho0 H)]>=1.
For positive target G and W,
T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG. Normalize W_1, proving (F1).

THE SUPPLIED/ACQUIRED BOUNDARY AND COST

The only scientific inputs are known A,b,q,N,beta and the public norm bounds.
The heat mixing density is an explicit acquired mathematical law. Its seed
support is certified by the elementary tail, rather than silently supplied.
The SDE and stopping/weight law are explicit and have a known PSD margin.
No exact-real seed/Brownian draws, output-state oracle, or original Gibbs
state is supplied as an uncharged input. A certified stochastic finite-bit
compiler is NOT executed or established efficient. Rejection envelopes can
be exponential in N; the weights here are exp(O(N)), and achieving the
whole-density exponential error requires correspondingly strong precision.
The brute-force full q^N matrix/rational-product-cone acquisition fallback
in revision03 Section8 applies, with huge memory and combinatorial search.

Mstar has eta^2=a^(2q) exp[-2Kstar-2Lstar], including q^(-2q) and exp[-4qR]
factors. Thus neither the allowed anisotropy radius nor the apparatus is
dimension-free. No result is asserted for antiferromagnetic beta<=0, arbitrary
fixed A, higher-body interactions, heterogeneous sites, unknown parameters,
or a universal parameter-independent decoder. A scalar A has exact positive
heat/filter representation, so no positive family-wide entanglement lower
rate can be inferred.
