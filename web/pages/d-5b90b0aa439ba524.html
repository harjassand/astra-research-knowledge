# Exact conditional pin reduction, and its acquisition boundary

Pinned source: OpenAI, *Spontaneous magnetization in the quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet*, manuscript date 24 September 2026, released at commit `adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a` on 6 October. All eight main-body section files have been read. The finite lemma below is independently reconstructed; the physical main theorem is a separate, more extensive audit. ## 1. The exact interface

Let Π be a random column-forward permutation on a finite set V=J⊔Q, J nonempty. Put K=EΠ. Assume, for **every** k=0,…,|V|,

    E[∧^k Π]=∧^k K.                                           (1)

This is equality of all signed minor matrices, not merely their traces. Let G be the event that every cycle meets J, and on G let Σ be the permutation of next positive visits to J. Then

    Pr(G)=det(I−K_QQ),                                        (2)
    E[Σ|G]=K_JJ+K_JQ(I−K_QQ)^−1 K_QJ,                        (3)

provided Pr(G)>0. More strongly, for every complex J×J matrix W,

    E[det(I−WΣ)|G]=det(I−WH),                                (4)

with H the right side of (3). H is the first positive J-hitting kernel of a Markov chain that uses **fresh** K transitions at every turn.

## 2. Complete finite proof

For any deterministic D, the determinant/exterior expansion and (1) give E det(I−DΠ)=det(I−DK). Choose D=diag(W,I_Q). If a Π cycle misses J, its indicator is a fixed vector of DΠ, so the determinant vanishes. On G, Π_QQ is nilpotent, det(I−Π_QQ)=1, and its finite resolvent traces exactly the paths between consecutive J visits. Block elimination gives det(I−DΠ)=det(I−WΣ).

Consequently E[1_G det(I−WΣ)] equals the determinant of the block matrix with rows `(I−WK_JJ,−WK_JQ)` and `(−K_QJ,I−K_QQ)`. W=0 proves (2), including singular cases. A positive value allows inversion and a second Schur complement proves (4). The coefficient of t in (4) with W replaced by tW yields Tr(W EΣ)=Tr(WH) for every matrix unit W, proving **every entry** of (3). A diagonal W or trace identity alone would not suffice for returns from one member of J to a different member.

K is nonnegative and doubly stochastic. Its Q restriction is substochastic. If a Q state could not reach J, its reachable set would be a closed nonempty class, giving eigenvalue 1 in K_QQ and contradicting its invertibility. Finiteness therefore supplies a uniform positive chance of exiting Q in finitely many steps; its resolvent is the convergent nonnegative sum Σ_{n≥0}K_QQ^n. This proves the hitting interpretation.

The premise (1) is itself constructive for independent Bernoulli transpositions and deterministic permutations. A transposition T has T−I rank one. Every minor of I+u(T−I) is affine in u, hence E∧^k[(1−B)I+BT]=∧^k[(1−u)I+uT]. Independent factors compose because exterior powers are representations and expectations of independent matrix products multiply. Fixed permutations compose as well. Finite-dimensional limits preserve the equality. Uniform site-permutation completions can be obtained as limits of a lazy random-transposition walk. These are the source's unconditioned hole-picture factors.

## 3. What the actual exposure must retain

In the released argument the exposed objects are whole loops avoiding P, including their complete histories and endpoint mappings. At a fixed exposure a, the *unconditioned* hole law Q_a is composed of independent allowed exchanges, forced bijections and uniform completions. The actual conditional law is Q_a conditioned on every residual loop hitting P. Its outer exposure density includes the factor g_P(a)=Q_a(all residual loops hit P). This factor must not be discarded. The exterior identity applies before this conditioning; equations (2)–(4) perform the conditioning exactly.

Dense boundary pins are a fixed finite-volume limit. The relevant all-hit events increase; next-pin return events decrease, and positive residence times detect visits. This does not justify an infinite-volume normalizer limit or a uniform rejection cost. The resulting fresh-turn Markov process is a representation of a conditional first moment; it is not the repeated motion of the sampled loop configuration.

## 4. A useful acquired consequence, with its costs

For a **supplied rational K satisfying the interface**, the exact conditional mean can be computed by one determinant and one linear solve, without sampling a potentially rare all-hit event. There are O(n³) rational arithmetic operations with standard elimination. If each K entry has numerator and denominator bit length at most B, a common denominator has at most n²B bits; determinants, cofactors and the resulting H have polynomial bit length. For L supplied rational Bernoulli transpositions, K is constructed by L rank-one row updates, O(Ln²) arithmetic operations. This is an exact first-moment acquisition result, not an acquired sampler for the physical conditional Gibbs law. Near-recurrence still matters for floating-point accuracy, so the exact-rational interface must not be relabelled as a precision-independent numerical algorithm.

The older single-pin time-homogeneous all-cycle formula is explicitly credited by the source to Alon–Kozma, Theorem 1. The broader conditioning/first-moment identity is a released author claim checked here; priority outside the inspected sources remains unestablished.

## 5. Exact obstruction: all exterior moments do not determine the law

On S4 compare the uniform law with

    Pr_a(π)=[1+χ_(2,2)(π)/2]/24.                             (5)

The character takes values 2,0,2,−1,0 on cycle types 1⁴,2·1²,2²,3·1,4. Thus (5) is strictly positive and normalized. Both laws have K=11ᵀ/4 and satisfy (1), yet their identity probabilities are 1/24 and 1/12. One proof uses the decomposition of the permutation representation into trivial plus the (3,1) standard representation: its exterior powers contain only the trivial, (3,1), (2,1,1) and sign irreducibles. The omitted (2,2) character is orthogonal to all their matrix coefficients. A completely independent rational enumeration also checks all 70 minor identities for each law.

Thus even full exterior information cannot acquire the entire permutation distribution, much less an arbitrary conditional Gibbs sampler. This is a finite information obstruction within the stated interface, not a counterexample to (2)–(4).

## 6. Exact obstruction to the literal old-code spin-flip transfer

One physical X flip on two computational spins permutes the four configuration labels by T=(12)(34). A shared Bernoulli flip with probability u=1/3 has E det Π=1, whereas det(EΠ)=(1−2u)²=1/9. It violates (1) at k=4. For N spins, one common physical flip contains 2^(N−1) transpositions; its difference from I is not rank one. Replacing it by independent transposition clocks on exponentially many configuration edges changes the joint law and incurs the exponential configuration space.

This rules out the **literal** proposed transfer of the source's exterior premise to the current CSS field/bath permutation on computational configurations. It does not rule out another representation or a weaker local identity. In particular it is not a no-go theorem for passive product-bath memory.
