# Source audit for the sample confidence interface

7 October 2026. These are author/source claims and interface comparisons, not an assertion that no closer work exists. Read depths and recoverable versions are recorded in `sources/metric_use10/cycle13/METADATA.json`.

## Recent starting window, 6 April to 6 October 2026

**Martinez-Taboada, Gonzalez and Ramdas**, [Vector-valued self-normalized concentration inequalities beyond sub-Gaussianity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03606v2). Primary arXiv history verifies v1 on 5 November 2025 and v2 on **14 May 2026**. The retained v2 PDF's header says **18 May 2026**; this is separately recorded, not substituted for the submission date. Selected reading: introduction; §2.3 Fact2, PDF pp3–4; §3 Assumptions1–3, p4; §4.2 Theorem1, pp6–7; §4.4 Theorems2–3, p9. Their process is Mt=sum Xi epsilon_i, with predictable Hilbert directions Xi, conditionally centered scalar noise, and regularized empirical design self-normalization. Theorem1 derives a cosh supermartingale; later results give Bernstein/Bennett and empirical variants. This is close concentration prior, with established Pinelis machinery, not a theorem about an unknown fourth-cumulant frame and covariance alignment. The displayed Fact2 does not state an explicit mean-zero assumption on its increment; Theorem1 uses the conditional centering in Assumption1. Our proof states centering explicitly and does not adopt an unrestricted Fact2 inequality. No full-paper reading or experiment replication is claimed.

**Jiang**, [Finite-Sample Analysis of Nonlinear Independent Component Analysis: Sample Complexity and Identifiability Bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08850v1), **10 April 2026**, verified by primary arXiv history. Abstract only: author claims finite-sample identification/sample bounds for nonlinear ICA with neural encoders and an optimization analysis. This interface differs from raw bounded samples, rational linear calibration and a quartic posterior. No theorem is adopted; no full reading is claimed. Retained abstract HTML only.

**Mesters, Ribot, Seigal and Zwiernik**, [Causal discovery under mean independence and linearity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04381v1), **6 May 2026**, and **Ouyang, Wang and Seigal**, [Tensor-based second-order causal discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18074v2), **22 June 2026** (v1:16 June), are substantive recent frameworks already preserved/read in cycle12. Their causal assumptions do not validate our actual-source positive-pair-gap promise. The published LiMIAM Example2.2 is precisely why candidate positivity must remain distinct from physical-source positivity. These versions are reused without replacing cycle12 snapshots.

## Older load-bearing prerequisites

**Pinelis**, [Optimum bounds for the distributions of martingales in Banach spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.2200v2). The author posting is an original draft of the **1994** Annals of Probability22(4):1679–1706 paper, with an explicitly listed **1999 correction**. Primary arXiv history: posted10 August2012, revised **9 December2012**; the posting comments say v2 replaces a draft containing typos. Read §3 Theorems3.1–3.2 and the cosh derivative/Taylor/supermartingale argument, PDF pp8–10. The martingale centering premise is explicit through f in M(X). We independently reconstruct the finite-dimensional Hilbert specialization and conservative Bernstein radius; no optimal constant from the corrected Theorem3.3 is imported. Complete v2 draft retained; whole-paper reading is not claimed.

**Al-Ghattas, Chen and Sanz-Alonso**, [Sharp Concentration of Simple Random Tensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16916v2). Primary history verifies v1 on24 February2025 and v2 on **26 September2025**. Read introduction, §2.1 Eq(2.1), Theorem2.1, Eq(2.2) and Remark2.1, PDF pp1–4. The stated norm is the supremum over unit vector slots (equivalently the diagonal supremum for symmetric tensors), not the Hilbert/Frobenius norm used by the exact posterior. Theorem2.1 assumes iid centered sub-Gaussian, pre-Gaussian Hilbert vectors and gives effective-rank bounds with tensor-order constants; Eq(2.2) charges the directional sub-Gaussian constant. Neither the norm conversion nor that tail information can be silently supplied. Complete v2 retained; later proof and experiments not fully read.

**Mesters and Zwiernik**, [Non-Independent Components Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13668v4), **19 March2024** arXiv v4 (journal bibliographic date December2024 verified in cycle11). §2.1 Eq(2), PDF pp3–4, supplies the common-variance class epsilon=tau eta, with tau positive and independent of standardized eta. The paper's observation convention is A Y=epsilon; our calibration matrix A is its inverse after translating the observations. Our finite support distribution, unknown mean, Householder calibration and numerical counts are **our instantiation**, not published numerical data. Physical covariance alignment, fourth-cumulant Q and confidence composition are our deductions. The source already provides identification and estimation uses; no novelty for common-variance ICA is claimed.

**Cardoso**, [High-Order Contrasts for Independent Component Analysis](https://www2.iap.fr/users/cardoso/papers/neuralcomp_2ppf.pdf), **1999**, author-hosted reprint; cycle12 reading §§4.1–4.1.1 and AppendixA.1 establishes MaxKurt Jacobi plane updates. **Cardoso and Souloumiac**, *Blind beamforming for non-Gaussian signals*, **December1993**, author reprint posted January1997: cycle11 preserves the author-uploaded transcription §§3.4–4.1 and Propositions3–4 describing eigenmatrices/top-n selection. This excerpt is not a retained original PDF; the original journal metadata and access failures remain recorded. Their original source theorem assumes independent components. The top-subspace/probe construction on our dependent positive-common-variance subclass is a deduction using an established method, not an extension attributed to that paper. Both controls receive exactly the same observed data and confidence set.

## Novelty and scientific boundary

The concentration primitives, common-variance scientific model, whitening, eigenmatrices, MaxKurt and SOS contrast identity have substantial prior. The scoped internal result is an explicit unknown-mean, raw-sample confidence composition with the previously gated global posterior, valid simultaneously for adaptive proposed frames. Its potential use is checking whether an acquired frame is certified or ambiguous when a bounded observation model and true-source promise are scientifically justified. Current required sample counts obstruct affordable use. No historical novelty, practical recovery advantage, extension to all causal models, or general noisy coefficient-only metric acquisition is established.
