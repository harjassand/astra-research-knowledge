# Centered quantum theorem: bounded primary-source comparison

This comparison concerns the exact target interface, rather than the general idea of hidden global entanglement. Primary sources were checked on 8 October 2026. Acquisition hashes are in sources/phase2_acquisition.json; exact read spans and this artifact's creation checkpoint are in SOURCE_MANIFEST_PHASE2.json and SESSION_PHASE2.json. These are source extractions and comparisons, not independent validations of the cited papers.

## The candidate requiring comparison

For permutation-invariant N-qubit states with a uniform subGaussian M/sqrt(N) moment-generating bound, arbitrary internal spin-sector states have D(rho_r || rho_1 tensor r) and Petz chi-square O((r/N)^2), for every subextensive r. One-body cancellation is exact and higher supports are uniformly controlled. With a strict collective spin-variance violation, a bounded common-axis local test matches the ALLSEP preparation lower order N^2/r^2. Exact white Schur weights give an explicit static polarized example. Historical novelty of this whole interface is UNKNOWN.

## Closest checked primary comparisons

**Moharramipour et al., PRX Quantum 5, 040336 (2024), arXiv:2406.08542v2.** Theorem III.2 gives O(k/N) local trace error for maximally mixed invariant states under semisimple Lie groups, and exponential error for finite groups. Appendix A explicitly begins with constant k=O(1). Global entanglement with local invisibility is established prior art. The checked statement does not supply arbitrary spin-sector states with a subGaussian law, own-marginal quantum relative entropy, uniform subextensive k, or the ALLSEP copy tradeoff. The concluding reference to sampling complexity concerns quantum advantage in state sampling, not an explicitly posed entanglement-certification copy problem. Read depth: Theorem III.2, Appendix A premise and relevant discussion; not full proof validation. Primary links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08542 and https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/pdf/10.1103/PRXQuantum.5.040336.

**Johnson, Gavalakis and Kontoyiannis, Relative entropy bounds for sampling with and without replacement, arXiv:2404.06632 / JAP 62 (2025).** The paper records Stam's 1978 classical bound D <= (c-1)k(k-1)/[2(N-1)(N-k+1)] for hypergeometric versus multinomial sampling. Corollary 1.4 extends it to a classical iid mixture. A quadratic small-subsystem relative entropy exponent is therefore old in classical fixed-type sampling. This comparator does not establish noncommuting quantum sectors or arbitrary within-sector states. Read depth: introduction equations (4)-(6), Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.4. Primary link: https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06632.

**Singh and Cerf, Symmetry-driven thermalization via finite de Finetti, arXiv:2604.09410.** Lemma 3, equation (63), gives the same quadratic entropy bound to a mixture of iid energy-diagonal states for a maximally mixed energy shell. The assumption is invariance under all energy-preserving unitaries of a noninteracting sum Hamiltonian. A qubit shell can have one fixed type and therefore its own-marginal product as comparator. General shells use a mixture. The arbitrary quantum spin-sector target law is a different interface. Their Theorem 2 instead has a linear k/N term to a thermal mixture with a stated small-k regime. Read depth: Hamiltonian/invariance definitions, Appendix D Lemma 3 and Theorem 2, relevant type-shell premises; not full paper validation. Primary link: https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09410.

**Amini and Chalal, Quantitative propagation of chaos for Lindblad dynamics, arXiv:2605.06973.** Theorem 1 bounds normalized global entropy, not unnormalized entropy. Product initial data give normalized O(1/N) on a fixed interval, hence global O(1); block entropy subadditivity gives local O(k/N), not the candidate quadratic order. The finite-dimensional model uses mean-field Hamiltonian interactions and local Lindblad jumps, with a faithful limiting state and fixed time interval. Read depth: model, entropy normalization, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1. Primary link: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06973.

**Guo, Liang and Wang, Quantum Relative Entropy and the Mean-Field Limit, arXiv:2605.08652.** Theorem 3 assumes a normal one-site Lindblad operator and a faithful limiting state. It gives global D <= exp(Ct)(D_initial+log 2), so product initial data yield global O(1), not O(1/N). The subadditivity argument yields local O(k/N). Its centered fluctuation cancellation is relevant methodological prior art, but the result is neither a static arbitrary-spin-sector theorem nor an all-time collective-jump statement. Read depth: lattice model (2.7)-(2.8), Theorem 3 and block-subadditivity span. Primary link: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08652.

**Li, Pollmann, Read and Sala, PRX 15, 011068 (2025), arXiv:2406.08567.** The opened abstract explicitly concerns stationary states restricted to one symmetric subspace, primarily the trivial subspace, and exact entanglement measures under strong symmetries. It reinforces that global entanglement from strong non-Abelian symmetry is established. Abstract-only read depth cannot exclude related local-information results in its full text; this item is not used to claim an exhaustive novelty clearance. Primary links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08567 and https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.15.011068.

**Ye, Bergh and Datta, arXiv:2609.40343v1.** The recent operator-domination result gives a one-sided order approximation of a general permutation-invariant marginal by an iid mixture, with linear d^2 k/n error. This extraction was supplied by the resource auditor and thermal lead, who opened Theorem 1 and remarks; I have not independently read its primary PDF here. It is a relevant stronger de Finetti comparator, but domination by some mixture does not identify the product of the input's own one-body marginal. It is not used as proof or priority clearance for the candidate. Primary link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40343.

## What changed in the novelty assessment

Do not describe local hiding, strong-symmetry entanglement, centering, or a quadratic classical sampling exponent as discoveries of this campaign. The potentially distinct contribution is the quantitative noncommuting own-marginal bound for an entire subGaussian collective-spin class, uniform over internal sector states and subextensive access sizes, with a matching sound ALLSEP preparation protocol and explicit resource interface. Exact-wording searches and these source spans did not identify a theorem with that whole contract; that is bounded negative evidence, not proof of historical novelty.

The rare macroscopic-sector counterexample in mean_casimir_centered_counterexample_v1.md proves that the quantitative sector-tail assumption is genuinely load bearing. The same mean Casimir alone permits centered entropy of order r/N for log N << r=o(N).
