Checkpoint: 2026-10-06. Status: **closest adaptive prior established; exact historical priority UNKNOWN**. No claim that unsuccessful searches prove novelty. Mathematical adoption is separate: root's `notes/root_cycle5/SENSOR_RESOLUTION_THEOREM.md` and r08's independently reconstructed gate are the current theorem, rather than this literature audit.

## Exact question and supplied interface

Supplied: a finite irreducible row generator Q0, all baseline rates, the graph, calibrated physical time, full state labels, and the single absent edge u→v. Unknown: its added rate b>0 in Qb=Q0+b e_u(e_v−e_u)^T. Baseline shortest directed path u→v has L≥2. Each next lag may depend on retained endpoint observations and parameter-independent private randomness; there is no information from inside an unobserved interval. Initial state does not encode b. The target is constant-error discrimination of b versus b/2, or comparable relative-rate resolution, as b↓0.

The candidate read cost is Θ(b^{-L/(L−1)}), with physical elapsed time Θ(1/b). Constants depend on the supplied finite baseline. The constructive upper bound uses a supplied local rate bracket [β,2β], not an already acquired global estimate. This is a measurement-channel theorem under a known model; it does not acquire Q0, its graph, Markov validity, calibration, or an initial bracket.

## Our deduction: classical design already predicts the exponent

Write c_L=(Q0^L)_{uv}/L!>0. Since shorter baseline paths are absent, the matrix-exponential series gives

P_b(t)_{uv}=b t+c_L t^L+O(b t²+t^{L+1})

and ∂_bP_b(t)_{uv}=t+O(t²), uniformly for small b,t. Contributions with more than one added-edge factor are covered by O(b t²). Reducing a row-u observation to the indicator of endpoint v gives Bernoulli information

I_bin(b,t)=(∂_bP_{uv})²/[P_{uv}(1−P_{uv})] ~ t/(b+c_L t^{L−1})

on the balanced small-time scale. This is a deduction here, using the standard endpoint Fisher interface; it is not attributed as a theorem to one of the above papers. For L>2, maximizing its leading term gives

t_*=[b/(c_L(L−2))]^{1/(L−1)},

I_bin(b,t_*) ~ b^{−(L−2)/(L−1)}.

The local variance proxy for relative precision is N~1/(b²I), hence N~b^{-L/(L−1)}. Multiplying by t gives time~1/b for source-u trials. Acquiring departures from u and proving a finite error guarantee still require the actual theorem's occupancy/mixing and concentration argument. Cramér–Rao/pointwise normality alone do not prove its adaptive minimax lower bound or honest triangular-array acquisition.

**L=2 nuance.** Leading information t/(b+c_2t) increases to a constant for b≪t≪1; t∝b achieves the same exponent but need not maximize the exact Fisher constant. The unit directed three-cycle with one weak reverse edge numerically optimizes at t~sqrt(b), information→2, read proxy~b^-2. Do not advertise t∝b as the uniquely optimal interval for L=2.

It is not a novel adaptive Bayesian learner, a new generator-likelihood formula, or automatic discovery of unknown thermodynamic structure.
