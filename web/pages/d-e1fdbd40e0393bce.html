## E1. Actual equilibrium interface and the acquired premise

Use the admitted one-logical-qubit BDL family, its repaired deterministic
decoder, and its full-space decoder Pauli frame O_X,O_Y,O_Z. These are
Hermitian unitaries, anticommute pairwise, and commute with every original
check. Let P1 be the old single-Pauli stable syndrome projector. The retained
decoder protection gives [A,O_j]P1=0 for every single-site Pauli A.

For positive one-site fields take

    H = H0 - sum_i epsilon_i X_i,  epsilon_i>=0,
    rho = exp(-beta H)/Z_H,  v=sum_i epsilon_i<=N J.

The independently checked cycle15 quantum witness, under its explicit
temperature/activity/size assumptions, gives the NEW joint Born bound

    p=Tr rho(I-P1)<=6 nu 2^(-Q).                         (E1)

No old classical Gibbs law is used. Since [H0,O_j]=0, linearity of
protection gives [H,O_j]P1=0. Its anti-Hermitian adjoint also annihilates P1
from the left; hence C_j=[H,O_j]=(I-P1)C_j(I-P1). Positivity implies

    c_j=(Tr rho C_j* C_j)^(1/2)<=2v sqrt(p).             (E2)

This step does not require [P1,H]=0. Likewise an original single-site
Hermitian bath A satisfies ||[O_j,A]||_(rho,2)<=2||A||sqrt(p).
That last bare estimate does not control its actual frequency components.

Let D be a full-space unitary with D O_j D*=sigma_j tensor I_A. The prior
finite-temperature Gibbs interpolation and Pauli twirl give

    bar_rho=(rho+sum_j O_j rho O_j)/4,
    D bar_rho D*=I_L/2 tensor rho_A,
    rho_A=Tr_L(D rho D*),
    delta=||rho-bar_rho||_1 <=(beta/4)sum_j c_j
          <=(3 beta v/2)sqrt(6nu) 2^(-Q/2).             (E3)

Thus the quantitative approximate-factorization premise is acquired from
the proved NEW tail. The square-root exponent and beta v are paid. At
finite N with all epsilon_i>0, Perron--Frobenius gives a unique ground
state, so exact full-H Pauli conservation/factorization is impossible.
E3 is consistent with that obstruction and is not a ground-state result.

## E2. A deterministic encoder from one supplied NEW Gibbs sample

In this SAME acquired decoder frame define

    Enc_rho(tau)=D* (tau tensor rho_A) D,
    Dec_D(sigma)=Tr_A(D sigma D*).                       (E4)

These maps are CPTP and Dec_D Enc_rho=id, including a reference system.
The auxiliary marginal need not be a Gibbs state of a separately supplied
auxiliary Hamiltonian; it is the actual NEW marginal in E3. No exact
identity H=I_L tensor H_A is asserted.

E4 has an explicit one-copy implementation. Supply a fresh sample rho on
the N physical qubits and an unknown input qubit Q. Apply D, swap Q with
its logical output L, discard the outgoing Q, and apply D*. The resulting
state is exactly Enc_rho(tau). If tau is entangled with a reference R,
the output is exactly D* (tau_LR tensor rho_A) D. Old L--A correlations
are transferred to the outgoing Q and disappear when it is discarded;
the incoming unknown state is never measured or tomographed. No
postselection, repeated sample, or auxiliary reset is required here.

Equivalently the full-space embedded SWAP is

    S_QL=(I+sum_j sigma_j^Q tensor O_j)/2.               (E5)

The exact Pauli algebra proves S_QL*=S_QL and S_QL^2=I. In the D frame it
is ordinary SWAP tensor I_A. Apply E5 to tau_Q tensor rho and trace Q.
Recovery applies E5 to a fresh external qubit and the stored system, then
traces the physical system. It realizes Dec_D, without measuring three
incompatible logical observables. This is a standard subsystem SWAP,
not a new Gibbs sampler. Its nonlocal implementation cost is charged.

Supplying an independent sample rho_tilde with
||rho_tilde-rho||_1<=epsilon_G changes this encoder by at most epsilon_G
in diamond norm: partial trace contracts the marginal error, and tensoring
any unknown/reference input multiplies its trace norm by one. If each
use of D or D* is synthesized to operator error epsilon_D, the two-use
encoder error is at most epsilon_G+4epsilon_D; recovery contributes at
most 2epsilon_D. Storage under the EXACT unchanged physical semigroup is
contractive, so these are static input/recovery errors, not t-amplified
Hamiltonian approximation errors. For total static error 2^(-B), choose,
for example, epsilon_G<=2^(-B-1), epsilon_D<=2^(-B-4).
No rounding of the actual H or its secular frequencies occurs.
These bounds describe ideal gates plus synthesis error. Any certified
additional physical control error eta_ctrl for preparation and recovery
adds eta_ctrl by the same channel hybrid bound; it must fit the final
budget. Giving a gate list is not a proof that it can be executed with
that fidelity while the physical bath acts. Storage time begins after
the charged preparation service has succeeded. No exponentially long
initialization or unprotected circuit is silently counted as free.

## E2a. A paid conditional-good initialization for restricted arguments

For any old syndrome projector P commuting the full O_j algebra, in the
D frame P=I_L tensor P_A. Its bad mass under bar_rho is exactly its bad
mass p_P under rho: the Pauli twirl preserves that syndrome observable.
For every unknown input, including a reference,

    Tr[(I-P) Enc_rho(tau)]=p_P.                        (EG1)

This follows directly from the product encoder and P=I_L tensor P_A;
the physical marginal of a reference-entangled input has the same identity.
The auxiliary projector also permits a normalized conditional encoder

    rho_A,P=P_A rho_A P_A/(1-p_P),
    Enc_P(tau)=D*(tau tensor rho_A,P)D,  Dec_D Enc_P=id.

Project the supplied NEW Gibbs sample onto P before the embedded SWAP,
or project only the auxiliary factor afterward. Its success probability
is 1-p_P, independent of the unknown logical/reference input. The ideal
successful output has P-bad mass zero. The normalized gentle-measurement
bound on rho_A, followed by tensoring and unitary conjugation, gives

    ||Enc_P-Enc_rho||_diamond<=2sqrt(p_P).              (EG2)

This uses a TWO-OUTCOME projection, not measurement of every syndrome;
intra-P auxiliary coherence remains. Preparing the supplied Gibbs sample
and implementing the coherent predicate P are paid. Before the unknown
input is inserted, a failed sample can simply be discarded; expected
sample count is 1/(1-p_P). No efficiency follows from p_P alone. For an
eroded syndrome projector the separately gated shifted-witness theorem
can bound p_P, but the predicate/inventory still must be acquired.

EG1--EG2 supply an initialization interface for a restricted proof, not
invariance of P under the NEW H. In particular [H,O]P=0 does not imply
[H,P]=0, and zero initial bad mass does not imply zero later leakage.
A spectral energy cutoff generally does not commute the old Pauli frame,
so its postselection cannot be substituted here while asserting the same
input-independent success and exact recovery. A nonreducing good set must
still have its escape/dynamics bound proved at the chosen physical bath.

There is an exact energy-cutoff obstruction for that shortcut. In the
already retained logical/gauge seed H=delta Z_L X_G, delta>0, the NEW
Gibbs marginal is rho_A=I_G/2 and the old-frame encoder is tau tensor I/2.
The negative-energy projector Q_E=(I-Z_L X_G)/2 succeeds with probability
1/2 for EVERY tau, but

    Q_E (X_L tensor I) Q_E=0,
    Q_E (Y_L tensor I) Q_E=0.                          (EG3)

Consequently the two phase-opposite inputs |+><+| and |-><-| give the
SAME normalized postselected output. No final CPTP recovery can restore
both. This remains true for arbitrarily small delta, even though the
unconditioned Gibbs state approaches an exact logical product. It rejects
this OLD-frame spectral-postselection procedure, not all energy-restricted
encodings or the existence of the alternate protected frame in root's
original seed. Projector compatibility is a real additional premise.

## E3. Reference-safe form of the already-known weighted criterion

Keep the cycle14 stationary independent-channel Davies interface with
K commuting H, actual full-H jumps, KMS rates, and

    d_j=c_j^K+sum_alpha sqrt(Gamma_alpha)||A_alpha||sqrt(k_alpha,j),
    k_alpha,j=sum_omega gamma_alpha(omega)
                Tr rho[O_j,A_alpha,omega]*[O_j,A_alpha,omega],
    Lambda=2||K||+2sum_alpha Gamma_alpha||A_alpha||^2.

Its already-reconstructed product estimate is ||G(rho O_j)||_1<=d_j,
G rho=0, and ||G||_(1->1)<=Lambda on complex inputs. Thus

    ||G bar_rho||_1<=Lambda delta,
    ||G(bar_rho O_j)||_1<=d_j+Lambda delta.             (E6)

For an arbitrary input-reference density matrix write
tau_LR=(1/2)sum_(mu=0)^3 sigma_mu tensor R_mu. Here R_0=tau_R and
R_j=Tr_L[(sigma_j tensor I)tau_LR] are Hermitian with ||R_mu||_1<=1.
The latter follows by trace-norm duality against any Hermitian contraction
B_R and ||sigma_j tensor B_R||<=1. Consequently

    ||(G Enc_rho tensor id_R)(tau_LR)||_1
       <=sum_j d_j+4 Lambda delta=:E_ref.              (E7)

The derivative is Hermitian; complete trace-norm contraction of the CPTP
semigroup and integration yield

    ||(Dec_D exp(tG) Enc_rho tensor id_R)(tau_LR)-tau_LR||_1
       <=t E_ref.                                     (E8)

This supplies a reference-system guarantee explicitly; it does not assume
that the old unreferenced state bound automatically has the same constant
in diamond norm. Combining E2--E3 with no Lamb shift gives

    E_ref <= 6v sqrt(p)+6 beta Lambda v sqrt(p)
             +sum_(alpha,j) sqrt(Gamma_alpha)||A_alpha||sqrt(k_alpha,j).

All full-frequency k remain an unacquired premise. Root's cycle15
whole-logical secular fixture refutes replacing them by E2 and the bare

## E4. What acquisition of D costs

Given independent stabilizer rows and bare logical Pauli representatives,
symplectic Gaussian elimination constructs a Clifford D0 sending the
checks to N-1 syndrome Zs and the bare pair to one logical X,Z. This is
finite exact GF(2) work; O(N^3) bit operations and O(N^2) unrestricted
Clifford gates suffice conservatively. In that frame the old decoder has

    O_X=chi_X(s) X_L, O_Z=chi_Z(s) Z_L, chi_j(s) in {+1,-1}.

A syndrome-controlled logical Pauli
R(s)=Z_L^(b_X(s)) X_L^(b_Z(s)), with the explicitly crossed exponents
b_X=(1-chi_X)/2 and b_Z=(1-chi_Z)/2, fixes both signs: b_X names the
flip-X bit and b_Z the flip-Z bit. Its conjugation defines D=R D0.
Evaluating these bits coherently, applying the controlled
Pauli, and uncomputing the work avoids dephasing the syndrome register.
Any supplied deterministic classical sign routine with circuit cost C_chi
has a reversible implementation with paid work/ancillas. Neither D nor
the sign-controlled portion is assumed to be Clifford or constant depth.
Physical routing on the supplied graph, gate error and initialization
while the bath acts are separate costs; the mathematical maps alone
do not supply a fault-tolerant preparation schedule.

A completely explicit fallback is to tabulate all 2^(N-1) independent
syndromes, compute the repaired decoder's signs, and use minterm
multi-controlled gates. This costs O(N 2^N) elementary reversible gates
in addition to the tabulation work. The fixed bottom minimum-weight
lookup is acquired by enumerating the finite level-2 code with fixed
ties; its enormous family constant is retained. This fallback proves
finite computability, not practical or polynomial preparation. A claim
of an efficient coherent RG decoder must separately give and audit its
classical algorithm, workspace and routing bound; E1 does not imply it.

Most importantly, the supplied Gibbs sample costs a preparation service
C_G(N,beta,J,B), time t_G and fresh physical degrees of freedom. E5 uses
one such sample per unknown input. BDL v1 explicitly leaves efficient
passive initialization open (Outlook, paper.tex484--485); its proposed
rapid-initialization comparator requires an LDPC metacheck premise that
the actual code does not satisfy. E1--E3 do not acquire C_G.

## E5. A decisive rejected shortcut: old-syndrome measurement

Logical twirl closeness controls logical correlations, not auxiliary
coherences. In the simple exact Pauli factor L tensor A take

    H=I_L tensor sum_(i=1)^n [(I-Z_i)/2-X_i/2],
    beta=sqrt(2) arctanh(sqrt(2)/3),
    rho=I_L/2 tensor omega^tensor n,
    omega=I/2+(X+Z)/6.                                 (E9)

Here all logical Hamiltonian commutators vanish, logical twirling changes
nothing, and one may set P=I, so its bad mass is zero. Nevertheless
measuring the old auxiliary Z syndromes replaces omega by I/2+Z/6.
Already for one auxiliary qubit the trace distance is 1/3. For n qubits,
measure all auxiliary Xs. Their mean is 1/3 in omega and zero in its
Z-dephasing. Hoeffding at threshold 1/6 gives

    ||omega^tensor n-(dephase_Z omega)^tensor n||_1
       >=2[1-2exp(-n/72)] ->2.                        (E10)

This is an exact finite-family inference obstruction, not an actual BDL
memory failure or a lower bound on its initialization complexity. It
shows why a tail-controlled logical factor does not license measuring
and classically resampling the NEW auxiliary state. The same issue can
occur for arbitrarily small fixed positive X field: its one-site X mean
is nonzero, and the required number of spectator sites grows as its
inverse square. E4's coherent implementation or an actual NEW marginal
preparation theorem is required.
