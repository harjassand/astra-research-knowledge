Open high-ceiling target: attractive resonance

The genuinely nontrivial two-body scale is a_L=-4J+g/L. For g in a compact [g_-,g_+] with g_->0 and |lambda|<=B, the two-body phase has Robin limit u'(0)=g u(0)/(4J), equivalent after fermion-boson duality to repulsive Lieb-Liniger with operator -2J sum partial_i^2 +2g sum delta(x_i-x_j). The m-sector critical operator is exactly a killed SSEP configuration-graph Laplacian plus (g/L)W; if R is the number of occupied runs, W=m-R and W>=(2m-L)_+. Thus z_{L,m}<=binom(L-1,m)exp[-tau g_-L(2m-L)_+] implies total weight above m>=L/2+k0 is at most L exp[-(2 tau g_-k0-tau B-log(2))L] for any fixed integer k0 with 2 tau g_-k0>tau B+log(2). This still leaves m<=L/2 and a fixed-width band uncontrolled. A sector ground-energy floor alone cannot close the gap because binomial spectral degeneracy can overwhelm it for m of order log L. The unresolved target is a summable all-m heat-trace bound, e.g. z_{L,m}<=A^m exp(-c m^2) uniformly in L,m,g, or an equivalent grand-partition estimate on a slightly enlarged lambda interval. Then a separate uniform high-mode tail is still needed for full-Fock trace convergence/archive. Fixed-m Lieb-Liniger convergence is not enough. If the compact lambda window is dropped, lambda_L=aL² with a>4J+g_+ yields an all-down-state tail counterexample; this does not apply to |lambda|<=B.

Known obstruction: if g<0 in a_L=-4J+g/L, a coherent bulk droplet creates a particle sector m>=L/12 with weight exp(tau |g| L^2/48); low-mass sectors have exponentially smaller total weight.
