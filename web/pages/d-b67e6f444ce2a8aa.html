1. Strongest claim and exact counterexample

The factor-4 work theorem is tied to the specific invariant probability measure μ=dVol_g/Vol_g. Replacing h_μ(φ_1) by h_top(φ_1) in its conclusion is false for the theorem as stated, which does not require M to be connected.

Let S be the Sol mapping torus and X_S=∂_t from the supplied exact sharp example. Write V_S=Vol(S), a=log((3+√5)/2)>0. The baseline proof gives h_{μS}(φ_1)=a, Def X_S=diag(a,−a,0), and sustaining power W_S=4νV_Sa². Take a second component T=T³ with flat metric g_L=L²g_0, volume V_T=L³V_0, and a nonzero parallel field X_T=L^−1∂_z. It preserves volume, has Def X_T=0, zero metric entropy, and zero sustaining power. On the closed 3-manifold M=S⊔T with X=X_S⊔X_T, both component volumes are invariant and

  p=μ(S)=V_S/(V_S+L³V_0),
  h_μ(φ_1)=p a,
  W=4νV_Sa².

The entropy identity follows by adjoining the invariant component partition {S,T}: for every finite observation partition P, H_μ(P^n∨{S,T})=H_2(p)+pH_{μS}(P_S^n)+(1−p)H_{μT}(P_T^n); division by n and the entropy supremum give the weighted component formula. Topological entropy on a finite disjoint union is the maximum of the component topological entropies, so h_top(φ_1)≥h_{μS}(φ_1)=a. Therefore the proposed topological substitution would require

  W ≥ 4ν(V_S+L³V_0)h_top(φ_1)² ≥ 4ν(V_S+L³V_0)a² > 4νV_Sa²=W,

which is impossible for every L>0. Meanwhile the actual volume-entropy theorem is satisfied sharply on S and strictly on M: its RHS is 4νV_S²a²/(V_S+L³V_0)≤W. As L→∞, h_top remains at least a while normalized-volume entropy and power per total volume both tend to zero. The topological complexity sits on a component whose normalized Riemannian-volume weight tends to zero.

This counterexample is within the written closed-3-manifold hypotheses if, as usual in some conventions, a manifold may have finitely many components. If the intended convention requires connectedness, it does not settle that narrower topological-replacement question; no connected 3D substitute is claimed here. A higher-dimensional primary example confirms that the distinction is substantive even for connected smooth conservative flows: Dolgopyat–Fayad–Kanigowski construct a smooth compact volume-preserving flow with zero metric entropy and positive topological entropy (their Theorem A/B; the construction has dimension above three, so it is not used as a 3D witness).

The calculation is algebraically checked by `measure_weight_counterexample.py`; it checks only the volume fractions and work inequalities. Its dynamical inputs are the supplied exact Sol example plus the elementary zero-entropy parallel translation, not outputs of the script.

2. A precise sufficient interface for positive-volume computational cost

A topological code or a finite robust simulation is insufficient to enter the theorem. One sufficient entropy-producing contract is: fix T=φ_1, a finite measurable observation partition P, and an invariant measurable code set C with μ(C)=β>0; certify that the conditional invariant system (C,μ_C,T) has h_{μ_C}(T)≥κ nats per stated time-one unit. Entropy affinity for the invariant decomposition gives h_μ(T)=βh_{μ_C}(T)+(1−β)h_{μ_{C^c}}(T)≥βκ. The transport theorem would then imply

  W ≥ 4νV(βκ)².

If κ is stated in bits per time unit, replace it by κ ln 2. This is a valid conditional bridge, not a derived property of a universal-computation label. The contract must include an invariant positive-volume set (or a direct uniform lower bound on H_μ(P^n)/n), a fixed finite readout, a certified lower bound on its volume fraction and asymptotic conditional entropy rate, and an explicit physical clock. A code that only runs for finite horizons, whose admitted volume shrinks with horizon, or whose trajectories live on a null set need not produce h_μ>0. This identifies the missing load-bearing lemma for any robust-computation consequence.

Acquisition debt is substantial: the static theorem takes g, X, ν and exact h_μ as mathematical inputs; it gives no algorithm for estimating/certifying h_μ, β or κ from finite observations, no precision/tolerance budget, no finite-time entropy certificate, and no actuator/controller or readout cost. A solver run or finite partition diagnostic cannot certify the asymptotic limit. The Riemannian metric/volume form, force realization, and time unit must all be part of any physical interface.

3. Time, metric, regularity and force audit

- Entropy is Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy h_{dVol_g/V}(φ_1), in nats, of the particle time-one map. It is neither h_top, thermodynamic entropy, nor entropy of a PDE/state-space semigroup. Ergodicity is not needed. Ruelle’s imported theorem bounds h_μ for each specified invariant μ by μ-integrated positive Lyapunov exponents; the normal-cocycle/strain estimate and Cauchy–Schwarz are also integrated against this same μ. Taking a supremum over all invariant measures cannot replace μ by the volume measure in the L² step.
- The baseline theorem assumes smooth closed Riemannian dimension three, smooth divergence-free X, nowhere zero, ν>0, autonomous particle flow, natural logs, and a fixed physical time unit. Compactness supplies bounded derivative and inverse derivative for the Oseledets/Ruelle integrability hypotheses. It does not assume ergodicity. The later cycle-3 audit extends the bound to smooth zeros with α=μ(X≠0): W≥4νVH²/α when α>0, by recurrence on the invariant moving set and weighted Cauchy; α=0 gives X=H=W=0. That repair still uses volume entropy and does not establish computational robustness.
- The metric is part of the input. H depends on the chosen measure dVol_g/V, and X must remain divergence-free for that metric. Uniformly replacing g by c²g on a connected component leaves normalized volume and H unchanged, while V and W both scale by c³; the inequality is dimensionally consistent. On disconnected components, independently scaling g changes their normalized weights without changing component dynamics or topological entropy, which is the exact failure above. For a nonconstant conformal change dVol_{g'}=e^{3ψ}dVol_g, the same X remains divergence-free only if Xψ=0; one cannot vary the measure freely while keeping the hypotheses.
- The audited constitutive convention is F=∇_X X−2ν div_g(Def_g X)+grad p, with F acceleration/force per unit mass and ν kinematic viscosity. On a closed manifold, div X=0 makes advection and pressure pairings vanish, while ∫⟨div S,X⟩=−∫|S|², so W=∫⟨F,X⟩dVol_g=2ν∫|S|²dVol_g. If a constant mass density ρ is restored, physical total power is ρW; if dynamic viscosity is used, its coefficient is ρν. Pressure changes add only a zero-integral gradient pairing. F need not be divergence-free: the residual defines a smooth sustaining force, so actuator feasibility/acquisition is outside the theorem. Boundary work is absent only because M is closed. Hodge viscosity differs by curvature/Ricci terms; it cannot be silently substituted for strain viscosity.
- Under X↦cX, c>0, φ^c_t=φ_{ct}, H↦cH, Def X↦cDef X, and W↦c²W. Thus the theorem yields no speed-independent power floor or fixed energy per simulated instruction. At rate H>0, energy over duration T is T W and asymptotic energy per unit trajectory entropy is W/H≥4νVH; after slowdown c this lower bound scales by c. Any computational cost claim must specify throughput and clock.
