MATCHING-FLAG CLASSICALITY AUDIT — 7 OCTOBER 2026
Independent internal reconstruction; external correctness and historical priority unverified.

STRONGEST RESULT / DECISION
The proposed finite faithful matching family is correct, including optimization over
channels that change flags or introduce flag coherences:

  C(E)=c(a),   delta=g(a)/(n-1),   e_EB=a/4,
  b_2=a(1-1/sqrt(2))/2.

Here c(a)=[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2 and
g(a)=sqrt(1-a^2/2)-(1+sqrt(1-a^2))/2. These are exact, not orders.
It disproves bounded-Holevo-capacity + vanishing pair gap => simultaneous EB
classicality. It does NOT disprove bounded-capacity + vanishing b_2 => EB
classicality, since b_2 is a fixed positive constant for fixed a>0.

A useful extension, derived jointly with quantum_classicality and independently
checked here, uses affine-plane Clifford blocks. For prime powers r, n=r^2 labels,
t=r+1 flags and local r-generator Clifford systems:

  C(E)=c(a), delta=g(a)/(r+1), e_EB=a(1-1/r)/2,
  I_acc(uniform signed prior)=c(a)/r,
  b_2 >= (a/2)[1-1/sqrt(2)].

Thus delta and accessible information vanish, but EB error tends to the
discard-and-prepare-center error a/2. Broadcasting still stays bounded away from
zero. This is a sharper boundary, not resolution of the global broadcasting gate.

1. EXACT INTERFACE AND FAMILY

n>=2 is even, 0<a<1. Take a 1-factorization M_1,...,M_(n-1) of K_n. Its explicit
round-robin construction labels vertices by infinity and Z_(n-1): M_k contains
{infinity,k} and {k+j,k-j}, j=1,...,n/2-1. Every pair occurs once.

Write q=n/2, d=2^q, H=direct_sum_(k=1)^(n-1)(C^2)^tensor q, D=(n-1)d.
Each edge of M_k receives one qubit. For its endpoints choose any ordering and
assign X to the first, Z to the second, identity on other edge qubits. Denote these
operators A_i^k and put A_i=direct_sum_k A_i^k. Then A_i=A_i*, A_i^2=I,
Tr A_i=0 and tau(A_i A_j)=delta_ij, tau=Tr/D.

E={rho_(i,s)=(I+s a A_i)/D : 1<=i<=n, s=+/-1}. This is one unknown density matrix;
the label i,s is not supplied to the channel. Every state is strictly positive.
All optimization channels act on the full B(H) input algebra. Outputs may change
flags, have flag coherences, use arbitrary ancillas, and correlate receivers.

T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2.
Delta(rho,sigma)=||sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)||_1-Tr sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma).
The first term is ROOT fidelity, not squared fidelity. delta=max_pairs Delta.
e_EB=inf_EB Lambda max_E T(Lambda rho,rho).
b_2=inf_CPTP C:H->H tensor H max_(rho in E, receiver b=1,2) T(C_b rho,rho).
The capacities use natural logarithms. C(E)=sup_prior chi(prior,E).

Construction/storage: the explicit matching table has O(n^2) entries; an input
state has log_2(n-1)+n/2 physical qubits worth of Hilbert-space capacity and the
family has 2n labels. Bounded Holevo capacity is not bounded Hilbert dimension,
number of labels, trace covering, preparation cost or retained quantum registers.
No efficient acquisition of an arbitrary experiment or new computational speedup
is asserted. The channels are explicit once the matching description is supplied.

2. CAPACITY AND FIDELITY NORMALIZATION

Every rho has eigenvalues (1+a)/D and (1-a)/D with multiplicity D/2. Consequently
S(rho)=ln D-c(a). For every prior, chi=S(average rho)-ln D+c(a)<=c(a).
The uniform signed prior averages to I/D and attains equality.

For i!=j, exactly one flag makes their Pauli scores anticommute; all other flags
put them on different tensor factors. Let h=(1+sqrt(1-a^2))/2. In EVERY flag,
Tr sqrt(rho_i)sqrt(rho_j)=h, independent of signs. On commuting flags the root
fidelity is h. On the anticommuting flag it is sqrt(1-a^2/2), from the ordinary
qubit fidelity identity. Since all flags have the SAME weight 1/(n-1), both
affinity and root fidelity add with that weight. Thus Delta=g(a)/(n-1).
Same-index opposite-sign states commute and have gap zero.

For i!=j, all signs give T(rho_(i,s),rho_(j,u))=
(a/2)[1+(sqrt(2)-1)/(n-1)]. Same-index opposite signs have T=a. Hence a fixed-radius
trace cover at radius epsilon<a/4 needs at least n centers. Uniform trace-total-
boundedness fails. At n=2 this is exactly a faithful noisy BB84 four-state family,
and delta=g(a); there is no small-gap limit until n grows.

3. EB LOWER BOUND — ARBITRARY FLAG CHANGES

For any state eta on H set v(eta)_i=Tr eta A_i. Pinching flags changes none of these
expectations. Within each flag, the two expectations on each edge qubit satisfy
x^2+z^2<=1, even if eta is entangled with other qubits. Therefore the flagwise
vector has squared norm <=n/2. Convexity of squared Euclidean norm across flag
weights yields ||v(eta)||_2^2<=n/2 for ALL states eta, including coherent ones.

Take an arbitrary EB representation Lambda(R)=sum_x Tr(M_x R)sigma_x, with a POVM
M_x and arbitrary prepared states sigma_x. Put t_x=Tr M_x, eta_x=M_x/t_x and
lambda_i=tau(A_i Lambda(A_i)). Zero effects are omitted. Then

  sum_i lambda_i
   =sum_x (t_x/D) v(eta_x).v(sigma_x)
   <=sum_x(t_x/D)(n/2)=n/2.

No output flag preservation was used. Integrals replace sums for a continuous
representation without changing the argument; a finite representation exists in
finite dimension anyway.

Use the signed observable s A_i, whose operator norm is one, as the trace-norm
test. Averaging over signs cancels any Lambda(I)-I term, including all nonunital
behavior. Averaging over labels gives

  max_(i,s) T(Lambda rho_(i,s),rho_(i,s))
  >= (a/2)[1-(1/n)sum_i lambda_i] >=a/4.

This is a direct average-to-worst-case lower bound, not an unjustified minimax
interchange. There is no assumption of an optimal covariant or flag-preserving
channel in the lower bound.

For the upper bound, measure the flag, then with probability 1/2 measure all its
edge qubits in X, or measure them all in Z. Prepare the resulting product
eigenstate in the same flag. This is EB on the full domain after flag pinching,
fixes I/D, and sends every A_i to A_i/2. Its error on every signed input is a/4.
Thus the lower bound is attained exactly.

4. TWO RECEIVERS — ARBITRARY OUTPUT FLAG PAIRS

On H tensor H define B_i=(A_i tensor I+I tensor A_i)/2. The crucial simultaneous
measurement fact is valid globally. Condition only the output measurement on its
flag pair (k,l). The multigraph M_k union M_l consists of even alternating cycles;
when k=l, its edges are doubled and can still be 2-colored. Choose a bipartition.
For one coloring, measure A_i^k on receiver 1 for color-0 vertices and A_i^l on
receiver 2 for color-1 vertices. Each matching edge contributes at most one
measured endpoint per receiver, so all these measurements commute. Do this or
the complementary assignment with probabilities 1/2. Output one sign x_i per
vertex. This joint POVM {F_x} has first moments sum_x x_i F_x=B_i.

For any broadcaster C, pull this POVM back to the input: N_x=C*(F_x). For every
sign vector x, each flag satisfies

  ||sum_i x_i A_i^k||_op
   =sum_(edges {i,j} in M_k)||x_i X+x_j Z||_op=n/sqrt(2).

The factors commute, and the top eigenvalue is attained as a product of local top
eigenvectors, so equality is justified. Thus the same norm holds for direct sums.
With lambda_i^(b)=tau(A_i C_b(A_i)),

  (1/2)sum_(i,b)lambda_i^(b)
   =sum_x tau[N_x sum_i x_i A_i] <=n/sqrt(2).

Average the signed trace tests over inputs and both receivers. Every C has
b>= (a/2)(1-1/sqrt(2)). Output flag changes and coherences were covered by the
block-defined global POVM and by pulling it back through an unrestricted C.

Attainment requires UNITAL clone marginals. An economical equatorial cloner alone
can translate the normal Bloch component and corrupt maximally mixed spectator
factors; that shortcut would be invalid. Use
V|0>=|00>, V|1>=(|01>+|10>)/sqrt(2), Vminus=(X tensor X)V X, and
C_local(R)=[V R V*+Vminus R Vminus*]/2. Both marginals are unital and shrink X,Y by
1/sqrt(2), Z by1/2. Rotate the XY plane to XZ. Apply this channel independently to
every edge qubit, copy the known classical flag to both outputs, and pinch input
flags to define a full-domain CPTP channel. Spectators remain maximally mixed and
every A_i coefficient becomes 1/sqrt(2). This attains the lower bound.

5. EXTENSION: CLIFFORD BLOCKS ON AN AFFINE PLANE

For r>=2 choose r traceless Hermitian anticommuting involutions Gamma_j on local
C^ell, ell=2^floor(r/2). Partition n labels into n/r groups per flag, assign the r
Gamma generators within each group, and tensor different groups. All expectation
vectors now obey sum_i <A_i>^2<=n/r. The EB proof above gives
e_EB>=a(1-1/r)/2. Measure the flag, choose one local generator uniformly, measure
that generator on every factor, and prepare normalized eigenspace projectors.
This fixes the maximally mixed state and shrinks every A_i by1/r, attaining equality.

Take labels to be F_r^2 for r a prime power. A flag is a direction, and its groups
are the r parallel affine lines of that direction. There are r+1 directions;
each pair of distinct points shares one line in one direction. Thus n=r^2,
D=(r+1)ell^r, delta=g(a)/(r+1), and C(E)=c(a) exactly. General F_r^d gives
t=(n-1)/(r-1) flags and delta=g(a)/t. The construction is explicit finite-field
geometry; primal examples suffice for an infinite sequence without prime-power
implementation assumptions.

Accessible information is EXACT for the uniform signed-label prior. For any POVM
outcome y with p_y=Tr M_y/D and eta_y=M_y/Tr M_y, the conditional likelihood is
1+s a v_i(eta_y). Therefore
I(label;y)=(1/n)sum_y p_y sum_i c(a v_i). The power series
c(z)=sum_(k>=1) z^(2k)/[2k(2k-1)] implies c(a v)<=v^2 c(a), |v|<=1.
The squared-expectation bound gives I<=c(a)/r. Measuring the flag and one fixed
Gamma axis per local factor gives n/r coordinates with v_i=+/-1 and all others
zero at every outcome, attaining c(a)/r. In the original matching family this
quantity is c(a)/2 and does NOT vanish with n.

An independent useful operator consequence is sharp:
 inf_(commuting selfadjoint C_1,...,C_n) max_i ||A_i-C_i||_(2,tau)=sqrt(1-1/r).
Indeed, dephase in the common eigenbasis of the C_i. The average squared diagonal
score is <=1/r by the expectation-vector bound; conditional expectation is the
best HS approximant to its diagonal algebra, proving the lower bound. For the
upper bound, within each local group set B=(sum_j Gamma_j)/sqrt(r) and C_i=B/sqrt(r).
These approximants commute and each squared residual is1-1/r. Nevertheless every
pair satisfies ||[A_i,A_j]||_(2,tau)=2/sqrt(t). This is a growing-number normalized-
HS obstruction; commutator operator norms stay2, so it says nothing against Lin's
operator-norm pair theorem.

6. GENERAL CLIFFORD BROADCASTING LOWER BOUND AUDIT

The stronger constant bound supplied by the sibling's large-clique worker is
valid for ANY r-clique partitions, without the affine intersection assumption.
Let J_C be an unrestricted broadcaster Choi matrix;
J_C/D is positive of trace one. The score sum is Tr[(J_C/D)K], where
K=sum_i A_i^T tensor (A_i^B+A_i^C). K is block diagonal in input direction P and
the two output directions Q,R, even when J_C changes these flags coherently.

For two input anticommuting involutions Gamma_1,Gamma_2, let F=i Gamma_1 Gamma_2,
D_i=B_i+C_i and E_i=B_i-C_i. B_i and C_j commute because they belong to different
receivers; within-receiver output relations are arbitrary. Since B_i^2=C_i^2=I,
D_i^2+E_i^2=4I and [D_1,D_2]=[E_1,E_2]. The exact sum-of-squares identity is

  8I-[Gamma_1 D_1+Gamma_2 D_2]^2
   =[E_1+i F E_2]*[E_1+i F E_2]>=0.

Consequently every pair score has norm <=2sqrt(2). Each term of an r-clique appears
in r-1 pair scores. Triangle inequality over pairs gives clique norm <=r sqrt(2).
Summing over n/r input cliques gives ||K||<=n sqrt(2), in every triple of flags.
The averaged signed trace tests therefore yield b_2>=a(1-1/sqrt(2))/2. This
supersedes the valid weaker affine-specific bound sqrt((r+1)/(2r)) derived first.
The constant is not claimed optimal for r>2. It rules out using the affine
extension as a small-broadcasting-error counterexample. No output flag restriction,
Choi separability assumption, or finite-dimensional receiver compatibility was
smuggled into the SOS calculation.
