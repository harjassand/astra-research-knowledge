# Exact local SLD harmonic gap for diagonal states

Worker b_prior, 2026-10-06. Independent deduction; b_math checked the weighted-sector argument. This is an exact theorem, with numerical diagnostics separated below.

## Question and interface

For the fixed n-qubit frame A={X_i,Z_i}, can the SLD tangent map have a useful inverse bound without the global smallest eigenvalue of the target? We answer exactly when sigma=diag(p_x)>0 in the computational basis. The theorem allows arbitrary Hermitian perturbations, including coherences, and uses the physical SLD metric. It does not settle a general noncommuting sigma or provide a protocol for acquiring operator-valued scores.

Let T_sigma(A)_xy=C_xy A_xy, C_xy=2sqrt(p_x p_y)/(p_x+p_y), and K_a=T_sigma(A_a). Define the harmonic superoperator H_sigma=2L_sigma R_sigma/(L_sigma+R_sigma), acting entrywise by h_xy=2p_xp_y/(p_x+p_y). Its norm is ||M||_H^2=sum_xy h_xy|M_xy|^2. The scalar-orthogonality condition is Tr(sigma M)=0 since H_sigma(I)=sigma.

Define the continuous-time single-site heat-bath chain L_HB on functions of x, with each site updated at rate one:

(L_HB f)(x)=sum_i [p_{x^i}/(p_x+p_{x^i})][f(x^i)-f(x)].

Define its slowed version L_s by multiplying the individual flip rate by C_{x,x^i}^2. Both are reversible under p. Let lambda_HB and lambda_s denote their Poincare gaps in L2(p), with rates as above. Since p is positive, lambda_s>0 by connectedness of the hypercube.

## Exact theorem

For this diagonal target and fixed frame,

inf_{M !=0, Tr(sigma M)=0} sum_a ||[K_a,M]||_H^2 / ||M||_H^2 = 4 lambda_s.  (H)

The same infimum in the GNS norm ||M||_sigma^2=Tr(sigma M^dagger M) equals 4 lambda_HB. Complex matrices and Hermitian matrices yield the same gap. In particular the transformed local frame is irreducible at every finite temperature. These two metrics must not be silently interchanged: in the physical harmonic metric the chain has a real additional slowdown.

**Proof.** Split M into its matrix-support sectors M^(a), with entries only at pairs x,y satisfying x XOR y=a. All sectors are orthogonal in either norm, because both norms are diagonal in matrix units. K_Zi=Zi. K_Xi has entries C_{x,x^i} on precisely the i-flip edges. The commutator [M^(a),K_Xi] is supported in sector a XOR e_i; for each fixed i distinct input sectors therefore give orthogonal outputs. The Zi commutators preserve sectors. Thus the total quadratic energy is an orthogonal direct sum of sector energies. The zero sector is exactly the diagonal part f.

For an unordered edge {x,y=x^i}, the diagonal-sector harmonic commutator contribution is

2 h_xy C_xy^2 |f_x-f_y|^2 = 4 C_xy^2 [p_x p_y/(p_x+p_y)] |f_x-f_y|^2.

Summing over edges gives exactly 4 times the Dirichlet form of L_s. The diagonal norm is sum_x p_x|f_x|^2, and its zero-mean condition is the standard p mean. Thus the diagonal-sector gap is exactly 4lambda_s. In the GNS norm the edge contribution is (p_x+p_y) C_xy^2 |f_x-f_y|^2=4[p_xp_y/(p_x+p_y)]|f_x-f_y|^2, giving 4lambda_HB.

For a nonzero sector a, sum_i ||[Zi,M^(a)]||_H^2=4|a| ||M^(a)||_H^2, so every offdiagonal-sector gap is at least 4; the same equality holds in GNS norm. Finally lambda_s<=lambda_HB<=1: use any nonconstant single-spin function, centered under p. All updates but that spin contribute zero, while conditional variance is at most total variance and slowing decreases Dirichlet energy. Hence the smallest gap is the diagonal one, proving both exact identities. Real diagonal functions already attain the diagonal infimum, so the Hermitian restriction does not change it. □

## Local-cost corollary

For p_x proportional to exp(-beta E_x), suppose every single-site flip has |E_x-E_{x^i}|<=L. Then C_{x,x^i}>=sech(beta L/2), and classical Dirichlet-form comparison yields

lambda_s >= sech^2(beta L/2) lambda_HB,

gamma_H >= 4 sech^2(beta L/2) lambda_HB.  (LC)

The constant uses local energy changes and a classical mixing gap, with no global min_x p_x. It can be exponentially small in beta or degrade with system size through genuine metastability. For a diagonal bounded finite-range 1D Gibbs system at fixed beta, any validated size-independent heat-bath gap theorem therefore gives a size-independent harmonic SLD gap. This is a classical subclass and should be compared with classical ratio matching/pseudolikelihood, not described as quantum learning advantage.

## Physical tangent consequence

Set D=sqrt(sigma), delta rho=D M D with M=M^dagger and Tr(sigma M)=0. Let delta S_a be the first-order SLD change under this state perturbation. Differentiate I_rho(S_a)=partial_a rho, where I_sigma(Y)=(sigma Y+Y sigma)/2. The resulting residual is D(-i[K_a,M])D exactly, so

sum_a ||delta S_a||_sigma^2 = sum_a ||[K_a,M]||_H^2,

||delta rho||_{I_sigma^-1}^2=||M||_H^2.

Moreover ||delta rho||_1^2<=||delta rho||_{I_sigma^-1}^2: put U=sign(delta rho), use the I_sigma weighted Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and Tr[U I_sigma(U)]=Tr(sigma U^2)<=1. Combining with (H) gives

||delta rho||_1^2 <= (4lambda_s)^-1 sum_a ||delta S_a||_sigma^2.  (T)

This is a local differential stability theorem, valid for arbitrary coherence perturbations around a diagonal target. It is not an integrated finite-distance score theorem or sample complexity theorem. Integrating along a path requires a bound at every intervening state; an arbitrary path between states can leave this subclass.

A usable noncommuting extension would need a local-frame comparison (possibly with bounded blocks) to the validated heat-bath conditional Dirichlet form. That is the next missing theorem; the present exact diagonal identity is a stringent benchmark it must recover.

## Novelty and diagnostics

The classical heat-bath Poincare framework and modular detailed-balance techniques are prior art. The exact equality (H) at the physical SLD metric is our deduction, with no comprehensive priority claim. b_math already derived all-temperature computational-diagonal irreducibility and an unweighted local-flip bound in cycle 1; this theorem sharpens the metric/interface and identifies the exact conditioning bottleneck.

`work/b_prior/cycle2/harmonic_gap.py` diagonalizes the full commutator superoperator for n=1,2,3,4 random positive distributions, compares it with the slowed chain, and checks the tangent-score identity and trace-norm inequality on arbitrary Hermitian directions. All gaps matched 4lambda_s within numerical precision, and residuals were at most 2.3e-16. Results are saved separately in `results/b_prior/cycle2/harmonic_gap.json`. These finite checks are diagnostics; the sector proof establishes (H) for every n and every faithful diagonal state.

Next falsifiable step: construct a noncommuting bounded 1D example where the proposed local-frame Dirichlet comparison fails, or prove it with constants depending on beta, range and bounded interaction strength but not n. Full tomography or a global spectrum factor would not answer that interface.
