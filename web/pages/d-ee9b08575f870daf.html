LEAD RESULT — a weighted cooperative barrier for degenerate reaction-diffusion
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE. The proof is a direct comparison argument. External correctness review and novelty/priority are unverified.

1. Theorem and exact interface

Let Ω be a bounded domain with homogeneous Neumann boundary condition. Let m≥2 species have any fixed directed strongly connected transfer graph E. For every vertex i include autocatalytic reactions Xi ⇌ 2Xi, with forward rate a_i(t) and reverse rate b_i(t); for every directed edge (i,j)∈E include Xi→Xj with rate c_ij(t). Rates are positive, measurable in time, and uniformly bounded. Let D_i≥0 be arbitrary, with no lower bound required. The corresponding PDE is

  ∂_t u_i = D_i Δu_i + a_i(t)u_i - b_i(t)u_i²
                 + Σ_{j:(j,i)∈E} c_ji(t)u_j - Σ_{j:(i,j)∈E} c_ij(t)u_i.       (1)

Write A(t) for the Metzler linear reaction matrix:

  (A(t)z)_i = a_i(t)z_i + Σ_{j:(j,i)∈E}c_ji(t)z_j - Σ_{j:(i,j)∈E}c_ij(t)z_i.

Assume there is a supplied fixed vector w∈(0,∞)^m and constants α,Γ,b_-,b_+ such that almost everywhere in t,

  0<α ≤ (A(t)w)_i/w_i ≤ Γ <∞,       0<b_- ≤ b_i(t) ≤ b_+ <∞   for each i.       (2)

Let w_min=min_i w_i and w_max=max_i w_i. For any nonnegative initial datum u_0∈C(Ω̄)^m, the mild solution is global. If

  q_0=min_{i,x} u_i(0,x)/w_i >0,
  Q_0=max_{i,x} u_i(0,x)/w_i <∞,

then define

  B_- = b_+ w_max,     B_+ = b_- w_min,
  K_- = α/B_-,          K_+ = Γ/B_+,
  q_-(t)=K_-/[1+(K_-/q_0-1)e^{-αt}],
  q_+(t)=K_+/[1+(K_+/Q_0-1)e^{-Γt}].

The solution satisfies

  q_-(t) w_i ≤ u_i(t,x) ≤ q_+(t) w_i for every i,x,t.                            (3)

In particular, for every

  t ≥ T_* := max{ α^{-1} max(0, log(K_-/q_0-1)), Γ^{-1}log 2 },

with the logarithm in the first term used only when K_-/q_0−1>0, the common box

  w_min α/(2b_+w_max) ≤ u_i(t,x) ≤ 2w_max Γ/(b_-w_min)                       (4)

holds for all i,x. The box and entry time are independent of the diffusivities, spatial dimension, and domain/mesh geometry. The lower entry time depends on the supplied positive weighted minimum q_0; the upper entry time is independent of Q_0.

The same proof applies when D_iΔ is replaced, species by species, by any conservative Markov diffusion generator L_i with the maximum principle and L_i1=0. Thus (4) is exactly mesh-uniform for finite-volume or graph approximations with those properties. This theorem proves permanence for a structured weakly reversible family: the Xi form a strongly connected transfer graph and each 2Xi is joined bidirectionally to Xi, so the full reaction complex graph is strongly connected. It does not prove arbitrary weakly reversible or all-endotactic unequal-diffusion permanence, convergence to a single equilibrium, or stochastic recurrence.

The cyclic network Xi⇌2Xi, Xi→X(i+1) is the special case w=1, with (2) reducing to α≤a_i+c_(i−1)−c_i≤Γ. Leaf05's two-species network A⇌2A, B⇌2B, A⇌B with equal rates is another special case, giving [1/2,2]^2.

2. Proof

The reaction field is F_i(t,u)=(A(t)u)_i-b_i(t)u_i². It is cooperative because A(t) is Metzler: all off-diagonal entries are nonnegative. It is quasi-positive because at u_i=0, F_i(t,u)=Σ_{j≠i}A_ij(t)u_j≥0. The positive Neumann heat semigroups (identity when D_i=0) and locally Lipschitz reaction field give a local mild solution; quasi-positivity preserves nonnegativity.

For a scalar q(t)>0, the spatially constant vector q(t)w has zero diffusion and

  F_i(t,qw) = q(A(t)w)_i - b_i(t)q²w_i².

Using (2),

  F_i(t,qw) ≥ w_i[αq - b_+w_max q²],
  F_i(t,qw) ≤ w_i[Γq - b_-w_min q²].

Therefore q_-(t)w is a subsolution when q_-'=αq_-−B_-q_-², and q_+(t)w is a supersolution when q_+'=Γq_+−B_+q_+². The initial ordering follows from q_0,Q_0. The cooperative comparison principle proves (3). For graph/finite-volume diffusion, the same calculation works because each generator annihilates constants and has the maximum principle.

For y'=Ay−By² and y(0)=y_0>0, the solution is

  y(t)=(A/B)/[1+(A/(By_0)-1)e^{-At}].

For the lower barrier, if q_0≥K_-/2 then q_-(t)≥K_-/2 for every t; otherwise it reaches K_-/2 by time α^{-1}log(K_-/q_0−1). For the upper barrier, q_+(t)≤K_+/(1−e^{-Γt}) for all finite Q_0, so q_+(t)≤2K_+ for t≥Γ^{-1}log2. Multiplication by w_i and use of w_min,w_max proves (4).

The upper logistic barrier is bounded for all t≥0 by max(Q_0,K_+), so the C(Ω̄)^m norm cannot blow up in finite time. Standard continuation of the semilinear mild solution gives global existence. Smooth data give classical solutions under the usual parabolic regularity assumptions; species with D_i=0 retain their ODE component but the comparison still applies.

3. Scope, costs, matched baselines, and falsifiers

Supplied inputs: the transfer graph, rate paths, a common positive vector w, and certified constants α,Γ,b_-,b_+. The theorem does not acquire these from black-box rates. Checking essential inequalities for arbitrary measurable rate paths remains an input/certificate cost. Once they are supplied, the proof uses O(m+|E|) rate inequalities and no numerical PDE solve. The waiting-time formula is explicit.

The margins are load-bearing for a uniform box. On the diagonal network with all a_i=ε, equal c_i, b_i=1, and w=1, a spatially constant solution satisfies q'=εq−q² and tends to ε; hence no positive lower bound uniform as α↓0. With a_i=1, equal c_i, b_i=ε, the diagonal equilibrium is 1/ε, so no upper bound uniform as b_-↓0. No lower bound on any D_i is needed because the reaction barriers themselves are spatial constants.

Strongest matched baseline: N54's all-endotactic PDE theorem covers arbitrary diagrams under common scalar diffusion conditional on N33; its unequal-diffusion routes need extra relative-production or compatible-normal structure. The finite-graph update covers species-dependent amount-conservative transport on each fixed graph, but its source explicitly gives no mesh-uniform result. The present theorem gives an explicit mesh-uniform result for a structured cooperative weakly reversible family, using the common positive-vector barrier instead of N33 or a graph absorber. It is narrower in reaction structure and does not imply general unequal-diffusion permanence. Primary degenerate-diffusion entropy results establish stronger convergence for selected reversible systems under additional hypotheses; see the read ledger.

Falsifier within the stated interface: a global mild solution of (1) with (2) leaving either bound in (3). A rate path without a common w or with α≤0 is outside the theorem; that failure alone is not a counterexample to permanence. The exact family above shows the box constants necessarily deteriorate when the supplied positive margins vanish.

4. Prior art and status

Primary sources establish indirect diffusion and quantitative equilibration for selected degenerate reaction-diffusion systems, including the reversible four-species reaction U1+U2 ⇌ U3+U4 with one nondiffusing species. A related 2017 source treats first-order linear reaction networks with at least one moving species in a kinetic-transport model. These are substantial prior art for reaction-mediated spatial regularization; they block any broad novelty claim. The weighted common-barrier criterion for this time-switched mass-action family is proved internally here, but its historical novelty and significance remain UNKNOWN until exact comparison with cooperative-system/indirect-diffusion literature and external review.

No claim about unforced Navier–Stokes, arbitrary Landau solutions, general hydrodynamic limits, or stochastic permanence follows from this result.
