ALGORITHMIC ACQUISITION BARRIER
Given any AUDITED finitely presented nonsofic G, soficity is a Markov
property: the trivial group is a positive witness, and G cannot embed into
a sofic group because subgroups of sofic groups are sofic. Adian--Rabin
then makes soficity undecidable on arbitrary finite presentations.
In its standard construction a fixed undecidable-word-problem group U
gives presentations P_w with P_w trivial if w=1, and containing G if w!=1.
Thus P_w is nonsofic iff w!=1. Since the complement of U's word problem is
not recursively enumerable, nonsoficity is not recursively enumerable
either. A sound complete finite-certificate verifier for ALL nonsofic
presentations would make that set recursively enumerable, so cannot exist.
This is conditional only on an audited finitely presented negative
witness; it is not a new undecidability method and says nothing about
decidability within the promised hyperbolic subclass.
Primary Adian proof translation:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2208.08560v3
The theorem was live checked; the strengthened enumeration conclusion
uses the displayed reduction, not ambiguous older wording 'recognizable'.
