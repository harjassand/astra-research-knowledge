# Binary-additive sunflower gate: constant-factor theorem and exact FPT coupling

10 October 2026. Restricted to explicitly given binary linear block maps. This is not a general sunflower theorem, a polynomial-time sampler in all parameters, or a verified novelty claim.

## Established result

Let V=F₂^m and let L_i:V→V_i, i=1,…,w, be binary linear maps with joint kernel zero. Call a binary two-plane U good when every restriction L_i|U has rank 0 or 2, never 1.

**Theorem.** For w≥1, if there is no good two-plane, then m≤2w−1. Consequently m≥2w guarantees three distinct codewords forming a partite three-sunflower, regardless of output ranks or alphabet sizes. The corresponding sunflower-free additive code has at most 2^(2w−1) rows.

The proposed sharper statement m>w is neither proved nor disproved here. Its m=5,w≤4 case is now exhaustively verified, extending the prior m≤4 tests.

**Counting and coupling.** For arbitrary maps, even without joint injectivity, let

C = {(u,v)∈V²: rank(L_i on span{u,v}) is 0 or 2 for all i}.

Then |C|≥4^(m−2w). Sampling (u,v) uniformly from C and a independently uniformly from V gives the triple (a,a+u,a+v), with exact uniform marginals, valid blockwise equality patterns, and relative entropy at most 2w log 4 against three independent uniform rows.

**Native sampler.** There is an exact uniform sampler taking 5^w times a polynomial in the explicit binary input size. It uses signed Gaussian-system counting and uniform unranking. A simpler rejection sampler takes at most 16^w proposals in expectation. Both costs are exponential in w and are fully charged. Neither requires enumerating the entire alphabet or 4^m candidate pairs.

## Verified external ingredient and withdrawn-source warning

The proof uses A. E. Brouwer, “An inequality in binary vector spaces,” Discrete Mathematics 59 (1986), 315–317, specifically the theorem on page 315:
https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/2507/2507D.pdf

Its relevant consequence is: if k binary linear subspaces irredundantly cover V, their common intersection has codimension at most k−1. The paper also treats affine cosets. The original PDF, rather than a corrupted publisher abstract inequality, was checked.

Do not use Nagy–Pach–Tomon, arXiv:2205.03389, as an exponential general-abelian coset-cover theorem. It was withdrawn in October 2022; its arXiv page identifies a mistake in Claim 4.5 invalidating most of the paper. Search snippets still reproduce the unsupported theorem. That tempting route was explicitly rejected before establishing the result above. No step below relies on it.

## 1. Complete cover/restriction proof

Write K_i=ker L_i. A plane is good precisely when its intersection with every K_i has dimension 0 or 2. Assume no plane is good. We will find a nonempty set I of k blocks such that

H=⋂_{i∈I}K_i, with codim_V H≤2k−1.

There are two cases.

1. If ⋃K_i=V, choose an irredundant subcover indexed by I. Brouwer gives the stronger bound codim_V H≤k−1.

2. Otherwise choose x outside every K_i and set J_i=K_i+⟨x⟩. These J_i cover V. Indeed 0 and x belong to all J_i. For any y outside {0,x}, the plane ⟨x,y⟩ is not good, so some K_i contains exactly one of its three nonzero points. That point cannot be x; it is y or x+y. Hence y∈J_i.

   Take an irredundant subcover {J_i:i∈I}, and let D=⋂_{i∈I}J_i. Brouwer gives codim_V D≤k−1. Since x∉K_i, each K_i is a hyperplane in J_i. Therefore each D∩K_i has codimension at most one in D. Intersecting these k conditions costs at most k more dimensions, so codim_V H≤(k−1)+k=2k−1.

Restrict all remaining maps to H and delete the selected blocks. Their joint kernel is zero because it is the intersection of all original kernels. Any good plane for the remaining maps in H would also be good for deleted maps, which vanish on H. Thus no-good-plane status is preserved.

For a dimensional upper envelope F(w), this proves the exact recurrence

F(0)=0; F(w)≤max_{1≤k≤w} [2k−1+F(w−k)].

The base case follows because an empty family has joint kernel equal to its whole ambient space. Induction yields F(w)≤2w−1 for w≥1: if k=w, the bound is 2w−1; if k<w, it is at most (2k−1)+(2(w−k)−1)=2w−2. This proves the theorem.

This proof handles zero-dimensional spaces, zero maps, repeated maps, full-space kernels, and a one-element irredundant cover. No assumption that the cover members are proper is needed; a member equal to V is itself the one-element irredundant cover.

## 2. Counting all good difference pairs

First assume joint kernel zero and w≥1. A dependent pair is one of (0,x), (x,0), (x,x). If x≠0, joint injectivity gives a block with L_i x≠0, so that block has rank 1 on the pair. Thus the only admissible dependent pair is (0,0). Every good two-plane has exactly six ordered bases. If G denotes the number of good planes, then

|C|=1+6G.

Suppose m≥d=2w. Restricting the original maps to any d-dimensional subspace preserves joint injectivity. The theorem says each such subspace contains a good plane. Double-count pairs (U,W) where U is a good two-plane and W is a d-subspace containing it:

G · [m−2 choose d−2]_2 ≥ [m choose d]_2.

Here the brackets are Gaussian binomial coefficients, counting binary subspaces. Their ratio simplifies to

G ≥ ((2^m−1)(2^m−2))/((2^d−1)(2^d−2)).

Each factor (2^m−c)/(2^d−c), c=1,2, is at least 2^(m−d). Therefore

|C|≥1+6·4^(m−2w)≥4^(m−2w).

When m<2w, (0,0)∈C already gives the desired bound. When w=0, C=V² directly, so no plane-incidence formula with d=0 is used.

For arbitrary joint kernel H of dimension h, pass to V/H. Admissibility depends only on the quotient pair, and each quotient pair has exactly 4^h lifts. Applying the jointly injective result to the quotient yields |C|≥4^(m−2w) again. This includes the all-zero-map and m=0 cases.

## 3. Exact coupling and entropy

Take independent uniform a∈V and (u,v)∈C. For each fixed pair, a, a+u, and a+v are individually uniform. Therefore the three marginals are uniform.

In block i the values are L_i a, L_i a+L_i u, L_i a+L_i v. Rank zero makes them all equal; rank two makes them pairwise distinct. The block equality pattern is therefore valid.

The map (a,u,v)↦(a,a+u,a+v) is bijective onto its support, so the latent triple is uniform on 2^m|C| points. Against the independent uniform product law,

D_KL = log(4^m/|C|) ≤ 2w log 4.

Applying the original block maps cannot increase relative entropy. Joint injectivity preserves the displayed value exactly; otherwise it is an upper bound on observed relative entropy. No nonuniform original-row distribution is covered by this uniform-linear-source theorem.

For joint-injective maps with m≥2w, the same construction conditioned on (u,v)≠(0,0) yields three distinct rows. The preceding incidence estimate bounds this nondegenerate support as well. For general m, the unconditioned coupling may put mass on the diagonal; that is intentional and must not be confused with an unconditional distinct-sunflower assertion.

## 4. Exact 5^w counting and sampling algorithm

Represent each map by a list of binary row masks. For a block define δ_i(x)=1 if L_i x=0 and 0 otherwise. Its valid-pair indicator is exactly

f_i(u,v)=1−δ_i(u)−δ_i(v)−δ_i(u+v)+3δ_i(u)δ_i(v).

The three possible image ranks give values 1,0,1 respectively. Multiplying the w factors expands into at most 5^w terms. Each term is a signed integer coefficient times the indicator of a homogeneous binary linear system in 2m variables:

- no block constraint, coefficient 1
- L_i u=0, coefficient −1
- L_i v=0, coefficient −1
- L_i(u+v)=0, coefficient −1
- L_i u=L_i v=0, coefficient 3

Gaussian elimination counts each system exactly. With a fixed bit prefix, append the corresponding affine equations; an inconsistent system contributes zero, and a consistent rank-r system contributes 2^(2m−r). Summing gives the exact nonnegative number N(prefix) of valid completions. Signed intermediate sums are bounded in absolute value by 7^w·4^m, so O(m+w)-bit integer accumulators suffice, apart from input indexing.

Compute N(empty)=|C| and choose one uniform integer R in {0,…,|C|−1}. Traverse the 2m bits in fixed order. At a prefix let N0 be the number of valid completions with next bit 0. If R<N0, append 0; otherwise append 1 and replace R by R−N0. This is a bijective unranking of C. A uniform R therefore gives an exact uniform pair, not merely an approximately stationary sampler. An independent m-bit a completes the coupling.

Precomputing constraint bases and optionally merging identical systems takes 5^w poly(input) work and memory. Each of O(m) prefix counts uses at most 5^w Gaussian counts. A streaming variant trades recomputation for polynomial auxiliary memory. The algorithm is fixed-parameter tractable in w, not polynomial in w. Uniform-integer rejection uses finite expected random bits; the sampler has expected O(m) random-bit cost in addition to deterministic counting work.

The simpler baseline draws (u,v) uniformly from V² until valid. The count bound guarantees acceptance probability at least 16^(−w), hence expected at most 16^w proposals, each validated in polynomial explicit-input time. It does not provide a polynomial bound in w either.

None of these algorithms finds or learns an additive representation for an arbitrary set family. The maps and their binary coordinates are input, and reading them is included in the polynomial factor.

## 5. Exact verification and exploratory searches

Run with Python's standard library:

- `python verify_gate.py`: 101 jointly injective seeded cases; 32 constructive witnesses; 7 actual cover-restriction steps; 211,944 affine coupling triples checked exactly for validity and uniform marginals.
- `python fpt_sampler.py`: 80 small cases; 3,320 exhaustive prefix-count checks; exhaustive unranking-image checks in every small case; 805 sampled pairs validated. For m=30,w=3 with three disjoint rank-10 blocks, the 125-term count equals (4^10−3·2^10+3)^3 = 1,142,827,901,003,938,843 without enumerating the code.
- `python exact_m5.py`: replays all 47,905 distinct three-kernel families in dimension 4, then rigorously reduces dimension-5 families with a hyperplane to that result. Without hyperplanes, fix one maximum-dimensional kernel using GL(5,2) transitivity and exhaust all other choices, including zero kernels and families of size at most four. Across maximal dimensions 0–3 it checks 7,686,547 representative families, with no jointly injective counterexample. Some orbits are represented repeatedly, which is harmless. This is exhaustive, not a random scan.

The separate SciPy/HiGHS MILP files are exploratory diagnostics only. Dimension 5 reports floating-point optimum 5 within a restricted kernel pool, but the exact result above is the certificate being relied on. Dimension 6 hits its 120-second limit with upper bound 7 and lower bound 2; it proves no sharp-conjecture statement. Neither floating-point result is used in the theorem or coupling proof.
