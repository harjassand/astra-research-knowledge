STATUS AND BOUNDARY

This note does not edit REPORT.txt or opportunities.json. I use the supplied
spin-one support formula as an internal premise; I have not independently
verified its proof or the proposed anisotropic operator certificate. The
question here is whether primary literature already supplies the anisotropic
tracial roof or the tripartite bound needed by that certificate.

The supplied support formula says, for w_i >= 0, h_i=sqrt(w_i), and one
common pure-state ensemble with barycenter tau=I_3/3,

  k(w) = (1/3) min_{z_i >= 0, z_j+z_k >= 2 h_i} sum_i z_i^2,
  I(tau; sqrt(w_i)J_i) = V(w)-k(w),
  V(w) = (2/3) sum_i w_i.

The requested tripartite gate is Tr(R W(w)) <= V(w)+k(w), for R with each
one-body marginal tau. The proposed stronger certificate is D_corr >= 0
with G=diag(z_i^2), z optimal. These are distinct optimizations: the first
roof fixes one shared ensemble's barycenter; a one-state uncertainty support
minimizes over a single unrestricted state; QFI minimizes one observable at
a time; and a shareability theorem constrains extensions only in its stated
state family.

1. DAMMEIER–SCHWONNEK–WERNER: EXACT SINGLE-STATE SPIN-1 VARIANCE REGION

Lars Dammeier, René Schwonnek, Reinhard F. Werner, “Uncertainty Relations
for Angular Momentum,” arXiv:1505.00049v2 (02 Oct 2015),
https://arxiv.org/html/1505.00049v2.

In the spin-1 vector representation, after a phase and rotation every pure
state has the representative psi=(cos t, i sin t, 0). The paper gives its
mean vector and covariance matrix in Eq. (19), then describes the full pure
state variance-triple body by the rotation/permutation orbit (HTML lines
166–177). For a general Hermitian tuple it defines the lower convex hull of
the one-state variance region by the supports

  m(w) = inf_rho sum_i w_i Var_rho(L_i),

and notes the infimum may be restricted to pure states (Eqs. (20)–(22),
HTML lines 184–194). This is the closest exact anisotropic one-particle
result found, but it has no common-ensemble barycenter constraint.

There is a strict numerical separation for w=(1,1/4,1/4). The supplied T1
formula is Case A with z=(0,1,1), hence k=2/3, V=1, and the fixed-tau joint
variance roof is V-k=1/3. A spin-1 coherent state polarized along axis 1
has variances (0,1/2,1/2), so the paper's unrestricted single-state support
satisfies m(w) <= 1/4 < 1/3. The pure-state variance region therefore cannot
be substituted for the tracial common-ensemble roof.

The same paper's Proposition 5 (HTML lines 383–393) says one-particle spin
means and second moments have a classical sphere model, while explicitly
restricting that discussion to one particle and noting the two-particle
version fails even for spin-1/2 by Bell's inequality. It supplies no
classical replacement for the tripartite correlations in W(w).

2. TÓTH–FRÖWIS: THE GENERIC JOINT ROOF AND ITS ISOTROPIC SPIN-1 VALUE

Géza Tóth and Florian Fröwis, “Uncertainty relations with the variance and
the quantum Fisher information based on convex decompositions of density
matrices,” arXiv:2109.06893v5 (22 Jan 2024),
https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06893v5.

Observation 8 defines the exact common-decomposition quantity

  I({A_n},rho) = min_{rho=sum_k p_k |psi_k><psi_k|}
                    sum_k p_k sum_n Var_{psi_k}(A_n)

(Eq. (114), HTML lines 581–600). For one observable it is F_Q/4; for a
family it is at least sum_n F_Q[rho,A_n]/4 (Eqs. (116)–(117), lines
602–608). Thus this source matches the *definition* of the candidate roof
when A_i=sqrt(w_i)J_i, but does not calculate the anisotropic spin-1
tracial value. At tau every single-observable QFI is zero, so the QFI-sum
lower bound gives no positive anisotropic support.

The same paper gives the exact equal-weight special case: for spin j,
sum_i Var(J_i) >= j (Eq. (78), lines 426–435), with equality for spin
coherent states (lines 448–450). For j=1, averaging the coherent orbit gives
I_3/3, so the common-ensemble roof at tau equals 1. This matches the
supplied formula's w=(1,1,1) value, V=2 and k=1. It does not extend to
unequal weights or to correlations with two output systems.

3. JOHNSON–VIOLA: EXACT ISOTROPIC TWO-SHAREABILITY, NOT ANISOTROPIC MOMENT
   MONOGAMY

Peter D. Johnson and Lorenza Viola, “Compatible quantum correlations: on extension
problems for Werner and isotropic states,” arXiv:1305.1342v3 (11 Sep 2013),
https://arxiv.org/html/1305.1342v3.

Theorem III.6, Eq. (25), characterizes joint sharability of n isotropic
marginals in arbitrary local dimension d by

  sum_j Phi^+_{0,j} <= (d-1)
      + (sum_j sqrt(Phi^+_{0,j}))^2/(n+d-1).

For n equal parameters this is Phi^+ <= 1+(d-1)/n (Theorem III.8,
Eq. (27)). At d=3 and n=2 the exact threshold is Phi^+ <= 2, so the
two-shareable isotropic family includes entangled states with 1<Phi^+<=2.
Under normalized Choi correspondence these give relevant tracial,
self-adjoint depolarizing examples. The theorem is exact on this isotropic
scalar family; it does not bound Tr(R W(w)) for arbitrary anisotropic w.
It also shows why separable fixed-marginal support identities cannot be
applied directly to every legal two-output Choi state.

DIRECT EQUAL-WEIGHT OPERATOR CHECK (NOT A LITERATURE IMPORT)

For w=(1,1,1), the candidate has z=(1,1,1), G=I, and
L=sum_i J_i^2=2I. Since J_i^T=-J_i,

  W = - J_A . (J_B+J_C),
  D_corr = 3I-W.

Couple j_BC=0,1,2 with j_A=1. From
J_A . J_BC = [J_tot^2-J_A^2-J_BC^2]/2, the eigenvalues of W are
0; {2,1,-1}; and {3,1,-2}, respectively. Thus W<=3I and D_corr>=0
in the isotropic spin-1 case, even without imposing the marginal or channel
constraints. This verifies only the equal-weight point (and its common
positive rescalings); it does not establish the anisotropic certificate.

DECISIVE MISSING LEMMA AND SEARCH LIMIT

The closest sufficient lemma for the proposed route is the operator
inequality, for every w>=0 and every optimizer z in T1,

  (3/5)(L+G)_A^T + (1/5)[(L+G)_B+(L+G)_C]
       - sum_i w_i J_i^T(J_i^B+J_i^C) >= 0,

on (C^3)^{tensor 3}, with L=sum_i w_i J_i^2 and G=diag(z_i^2).
Tracing against R with all three marginals I_3/3 would give exactly
Tr(RW)<=V+k. A direct weaker bound restricted to legal symmetric Choi
states could also close the gate, but no imported theorem in this pass
does so.

isotropic value, and isotropic shareability above; it did not surface an
