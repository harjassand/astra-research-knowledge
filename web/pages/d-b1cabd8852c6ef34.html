QUANTUM CAPABILITY — CYCLE 3 PRODUCT-VECTOR ATTACK
2026-10-08

Result

I derived an exact rank-one/product-vector formulation of the global one-copy purity gate for equal-weight unitary branch channels. I then proved a scoped structural no-go: for every Pauli branch set that is Sidon in the elementary abelian phase-space group, the exact Bell spectrum combined with the standard maximum-Fourier-coefficient purity bound cannot certify any positive von Neumann MOE additivity gap. A tiny Gold-graph fixture in GF(8) attains the Fourier/additive-energy bound exactly and exhibits the failed certificate numerically.

This is a useful negative result about one explicit code architecture, not a new nonadditivity theorem and not a global obstruction to Pauli channels. The decisive positive product-vector/SOS certificate remains open. In particular, the exact Gram-radius identity for this product-vector gate already appears as Lemma 4.1 in arXiv:2609.18747v1; I re-derived its branch-channel form here and make no novelty claim for that identity.

The global identity is

  Tr Phi^c(|psi><psi|)^2
     = K^(-2)|| (sum_s W_s tensor conjugate(W_s))
                    (psi tensor conjugate(psi)) ||_2^2.

It shows exactly where a strong certificate is needed: only on the conjugate-product variety. The 2026 all-order source already converts this into a quadratic Gram radius and proves a free regular-representation bound uniformly over its coefficient matrices; its remaining finite realization uses high-probability Haar strong convergence or a non-random existence sequence. In the free direct-product example with K=256 and two 128-regular tree factors, the full doubled-space norm is 4 sqrt(127)>45, while the target scale from the source's purity inequality is sqrt(307.5)<17.54. Thus ordinary full-space 2-to-2 control is overstrong and cannot replace the source's product-vector/Gram-radius mechanism.

For a Pauli/Sidon set of size K>=3 in G=F2^(2q), N=|G|=n^2, the Bell entropy is exactly

  H_B = 2 ln K - ln 2 - ln(K/2)/K.

Fourier moments and Sidon additive energy imply beta^2>=R_K(N)/K^2, with R_K(N0)=K-2, R'_K(N)>=0, N0=1+binom(K,2), and limit R_K(infinity)=3K-2. Writing f_K(N)=N^(-1/2)+(1-N^(-1/2))R_K(N)/K^2, direct differentiation shows f_K has no interior minimum. Both endpoint values strictly exceed exp(-H_B/2). Therefore the max-Fourier purity certificate gives a nonpositive lower bound on the Bell gap for every such Pauli/Sidon set and every allowed power-of-two input dimension. K=2 is also negative; K=1 is trivial. Full derivation and exact constants are in DERIVATION.txt.

The exact GF(8) fixture has n=8, K=8, maximum nontrivial Walsh magnitude 4, Bell probabilities 1/8 once and 1/32 twenty-eight times, and purity bound 11/32. Its entropy lower bound is 1.0678406300 nats; Bell entropy is 3.2924491077 nats; the resulting gap lower bound is -1.1567678477 nats. This checks a failure of the certificate, not the actual channel's minimum-output entropy.
