RESULT

The deficit in the sharp entropy-power inequality does not imply uniform
pointwise or geometric control across the full smooth closed 3-manifold
class. Two scoped constructions survive same-cluster cross-exposure:

1. On one fixed connected Sol mapping torus, an intermittent smooth metric
   perturbation has fixed volume and entropy H=a, normalized gap
     Delta/H^2 = c^2(epsilon B2-epsilon^2 B1^2)/a^2 -> 0,
   but ||s-H||_infinity -> c>0. Sectional curvature diverges as
   epsilon^-1, while the exact sustaining force remains uniformly bounded.
   Thus an L2 deficit cannot yield a uniform strain modulus.

2. A sequence of varying-monodromy Sol suspensions has V=1, H=1 and
   Delta=0 exactly, but injectivity radius tends to zero and curvature
   tends to infinity. The monodromy is
     A_n=[[n,1],[n^2-1,n]],
   with eigenvalue lambda_n=n+sqrt(n^2-1); the covolume-one invariant
   eigen-coordinate lattice contains a noncontractible loop of squared
   length 1/sqrt(n^2-1). Covers of one fixed Sol model also have Delta=0,
   fixed H and diameter at least m/2.

PROOF INTERFACE

For a hyperbolic torus map B with eigenvalues e^(+-a), use its mapping torus
and metric
  g_u=dt^2+e^(2u(t))dx^2+e^(-2u(t))dy^2,
  u(t+1)=u(t)+a.
The volume density is independent of u, X=partial_t is unit and
divergence-free, the return map is B, and H=a. Its strain is
diag(u',-u',0), so the existing stationary work identity gives
  Delta = W/(4 nu V)-H^2 = integral_0^1 (u'-a)^2 dt.
The intermittent family has u'=a+c(b_epsilon-epsilon B1), where b_epsilon
is a smooth bump of width epsilon, max one, and B_j=int b^j. This yields
the gap and L-infinity lower bound above. Direct curvature calculation gives
K_xt=-(u''+u'^2), K_yt=u''-u'^2; the exact force is
F=4 nu (u')^2 X, so it stays bounded independently of u''.

The collapse calculation uses
P_n=(1/n)[[1,1],[d_n,-d_n]], d_n=sqrt(n^2-1),
|det P_n|=2d_n/n^2 and
Lambda_n=sqrt(|det P_n|)P_n^(-1)Z^2.
This lattice is preserved by diag(lambda_n,lambda_n^(-1)), has covolume
one, and the vector from (0,1) has squared length 1/d_n. On the Sol metric
u=a_n t, rescale X by 1/a_n. Abramov's flow entropy scaling gives H=1;
strain eigenvalues are (+1,-1,0), V=1, and W=4 nu. The proof does not
depend on finite numerical entropy estimation.

These examples do not refute the L2 defect, transported-line stability,
nonhyperbolic-volume bound, exact local equality classification, or
stability under extra geometry and derivative bounds. A useful modulus must
name its target distance and geometry class. The constructions also do not
provide a finite-bit metric/force acquisition interface or a robust positive-
volume computation. The strain-viscosity force is the formal residual
sustaining force; no actuator is built or costed.
