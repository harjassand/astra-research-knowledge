1. EXACT PROPOSED THEOREM AND VERDICT
For a nonempty family E of states on any finite-dimensional H, define
  T(rho,nu)=||rho-nu||_1/2,
  b2(E)=inf_CPTP B:H->H tensor H sup_(rho in E,j=1,2) T(B_j rho,rho),
  e_EB(E)=inf_EB Lambda:H->H sup_(rho in E) T(Lambda rho,rho).
The same full-domain broadcaster and the same full-domain EB channel serve
all labels; no label is supplied at runtime and outputs may be correlated.

In NATS, use
  D_obs(rho||sigma)=sup_(0<=F<=I) p ln(p/q),
  p=Tr(F rho), q=Tr(F sigma),
  0 ln(0/q)=0, and p ln(p/0)=+infinity when p>0.
In particular the p=q=0 term is zero. I is an admissible effect and gives
zero, so D_obs>=0. This is the one-event observational divergence, not
the supremum full binary relative entropy or measured relative entropy.

Let K=closed conv(E) and
  r=R_obs(E)=inf_(sigma in K) sup_(rho in E) D_obs(rho||sigma)<infinity.
Then, using the allowed frozen checkpoint03 theorem dependency,
  e_EB(E)<=min{1,400 sqrt[(r+1)/ln(1/b2(E))]}, 0<b2(E)<1.    (T)
At b2=0 there is ONE exact full-domain EB reconstruction; at b2=1 use
e_EB<=1. VERDICT: (T) survives this independent derivation. Every constant
below is explicit; the radius infimum, center error, support, and common
channel are handled without an unproved attainment or label-count premise.

2. THE OBSERVATIONAL CENTER IS ATTAINED
K is compact in finite dimension. For fixed rho and fixed effect F, p is
fixed. If p=0, p ln(p/q) is identically zero. If p>0, it is continuous for
q>0, lower semicontinuous at q=0 with value +infinity, and convex in sigma.
Thus
  J(sigma)=sup_(rho in E,F) p ln(p/Tr(F sigma))
is lower semicontinuous and convex on K. Since r is finite, a minimizing
sequence has a convergent subsequence sigma_n->sigma in K, and
J(sigma)<=liminf J(sigma_n)=r. Hence one common minimizing sigma exists
and satisfies D_obs(rho||sigma)<=r for EVERY promised rho.
This proof does not require E to be finite or closed.

If sigma has support P and a promised rho has positive mass on ker sigma,
the effect I-P gives q=0 and p>0, contradicting finiteness. Equivalently,
even in Jain--Nayak's convention excluding q=0, perturb that effect by
delta I and let delta down to zero. Therefore every rho is supported on P.
Since sigma is in closed conv(E), P is exactly the joint support of E.
After compression to P, sigma is faithful. No minimum eigenvalue enters
the error bounds. The smallest eigenvalue matters to implementation only.

3. THE CENTER HAS THE SAME BROADCAST ERROR, AND SUPPORT IS PHYSICAL
Let B be ONE actual broadcaster with uniform half marginal error <=b on E.
For any finite convex mixture sum_a t_a rho_a in conv(E), linearity and
trace-norm convexity give, for each j,
  T(B_j sum_a t_a rho_a, sum_a t_a rho_a)<=sum_a t_a b=b.
Continuity extends this to closed conv(E), in particular to sigma. Thus
sigma can be added to the promised family without increasing the same B's
error. D_obs(sigma||sigma)=0; adding it leaves the radius r unchanged.

Use the actual CPTP support compression, with codomain B(PH),
  R_P(Z)=P Z P+Tr[(I-P)Z]sigma.
It fixes all promised rho and sigma. Replace B by (R_P tensor R_P)B with
input restricted to B(PH). Its two half marginal errors cannot increase.
Any EB channel on B(PH) extends to the original full input algebra by
composition with R_P and output inclusion into H. It remains CPTP and EB,
and introduces no error on promised inputs. Neither a label nor an inverse
compression is supplied to a channel.

4. PRIMARY SUBSTATE INPUT, WITH CORRECT LOGARITHM AND FIDELITY

for normalized rho,sigma with supp rho<=supp sigma and any eta in (0,1),
there is normalized tau0 such that
  f(rho,tau0)^2>=1-eta,

There is no +ln2 conversion loss. In checkpoint03 that loss came solely
from bounding observational divergence by relative entropy in bits plus1.
Here the primary theorem takes the assumed divergence directly.

5. ONE COMPATIBLE SMOOTHER AND EXACT INCLUDED REFERENCE
Fix epsilon in (0,1), eta=epsilon^2/8, M as in (4.1), lambda=eta/M.
On the common feasible set 0<=tau<=M sigma, Tr tau<=1, take the UNIQUE
minimizer G(rho,sigma) of
  -f(rho,tau)+lambda Q_sigma(tau),
  Q_sigma(tau)=||sigma^(-1/4) tau sigma^(-1/4)||_2^2.
The frozen checkpoint03 stage proof Section 4 gives its compatibility
estimate for every CPTP marginal map; it depends only on M,lambda and the
input/reference residuals, not on relative entropy or capacity.

For completeness the accuracy substitution is exact: the JN witness is
feasible and Q_sigma(tau0)<=M, so minimality, f(rho,tau0)>=1-eta, and
lambda M=eta imply f(rho,G)>=1-2eta. Consequently v=Tr G>=1-4eta>1/2.
The normalized rhohat=G/v satisfies
  rhohat<=2M sigma, T(rho,rhohat)<=epsilon,
and its full residual is exactly ||B_j G-G||_1/v.

The reference is preserved exactly by the mathematical smoother:
  G(sigma,sigma)=sigma.
Indeed f(sigma,tau)<=sqrt(Tr tau), Q_sigma(tau)>=(Tr tau)^2, and
-sqrt(v)+lambda v^2 is strictly decreasing on [0,1] for lambda<1/8.
Sigma is feasible and attains the scalar minimum at v=1; uniqueness
identifies it. Thus the normalized smoothed experiment Ehat contains the
original sigma exactly. This is not an approximate or state-specific center.

Since the SAME B approximates rho and sigma with half error <=b, the frozen
compatible-smoothing estimate yields half residual <=beta(b) on Ehat,
  Xi(b)=(1+sqrt(M)+2lambda M^2)sqrt(2b)+M b,

For EVERY ONE EB channel L, trace contraction gives
  error_E(L)<=2epsilon+error_Ehat(L).                  (5.1)
The nonlinear smoother is used only for the existence comparison; it is
not a channel applied to an unknown labeled input.

9. GENUINE COMPLEXITY IMPROVEMENT, AND A NECESSARY NUANCE
Bounded max-prior Holevo capacity implies bounded R_obs, because the
checkpoint03 relative-entropy center belongs to closed conv(E) and JN
gives D_obs_nats<=D_rel_nats+ln2. But the converse fails: the explicit
family in RADIUS_CAPACITY_SEPARATION.txt has R_obs<=1 while C_chi->infinity.
Thus a uniform observational-radius bound is a strictly broader sequence
hypothesis. Likewise r_n+1=o(ln(1/b2(E_n))) yields e_EB(E_n)->0.

This does NOT mean r<=C_chi for every family, or that (T)'s displayed
coefficient always improves the old one. The swapped classical binary
family diag(5/8,3/8), diag(3/8,5/8) has optimal observational center I/2
by symmetry/convexity, and
  r>= (5/8)ln(5/4)
    > C_chi=(5/8)ln(5/4)+(3/8)ln(3/4).
One may use whichever of the two valid bounds is smaller. The improvement
is in the uniform complexity class, not a universal pointwise domination.
