# Complete slower trace sampler; failed fast-recursion transfer

2026-10-06. Team C cycle3. Exact-real gradient-oracle theorem below, independently reconstructed with r10; c_gaussian independently passed the complete mixing/cap/cost gate. Finite float replay is not a TV certificate. This theorem deliberately changes the failed fast correction to a direct source-style proximal/FORS baseline. ## Contract and theorem

Supply known q>0,h>=0,tau>=1, exact gradient access to V=q|x|²/2+U, and the uniform structural promise U convex C², 0<=H_U<=hI, tr H_U<=tau h. Supply Gaussian/uniform/Poisson generation and exact arithmetic, as in the source's oracle model. No residual subspace, value, Hessian, prox or hidden score is supplied. The target pi proportional to exp(-V) is normalizable by q-strong convexity. tau is supplied/certified structure, not acquired from sampled Hessians.

Let r=h/q, kappa=1+r, epsilon in (0,1/2), and

    L=log(e+(tau+1)kappa/epsilon).

For h>0, the construction below returns a law within epsilon TV of pi with expected gradient count

    C[1+r(tau+L)] L² + C_ref,                         (1)

where C_ref<=C kappa[1+log_+(|grad V(x0)|/sqrt(q))] acquires a reference from an arbitrary explicit x0. Alternatively, supply a reference with |grad V(xref)|<=sqrt(q) and omit C_ref. No factor d enters this query bound. At h=0, one gradient reveals the affine residual and the exact target Gaussian is sampled directly.

Each gradient/proposal operates on d coordinates. Arithmetic is O(d) per such operation, reference/vector Gaussian precision and input-oracle evaluation cost must be added. An arbitrary mathematical gradient oracle does not imply a finite-bit implementation. We prove the source-style exact-oracle claim and state the extra precision promise separately below.

## Reference acquisition and coordinate change

Gradient descent on V with step 1/(q+h) contracts the gradient residual by h/(q+h), because the integrated Hessian on an update lies in [qI,(q+h)I]. Its fixed iteration count gives the stated C_ref. Test the final gradient rather than assuming an exact mode.

Translate xref to zero. In these coordinates the residual is

    Ubar(x)=U(x+xref)+q xref.x+constant,

so grad Ubar(x)=grad V(x+xref)-qx, the same Hessian promises hold, and |grad Ubar(0)|<=sqrt(q). In the remainder call this residual U. This translation is essential: a large affine residual in the original coordinates is not silently treated as a small oracle offset.

Initialize X0~G0=N(0,q^-1 I). One useful exact entropy calculation is

    KL(G0||pi)<=|grad V(0)|²/(2q)+tau h/(2q)
              <=(1+tau r)/2.                         (2)

Indeed q-strong convexity bounds Z_pi by exp(-V(mode))(2pi/q)^(d/2), Gaussian entropy cancels the known quadratic d/2, Gaussian heat integration bounds E[U(G0)-U(0)]<=tau h/(2q), and V(0)-V(mode)<=|grad V(0)|²/(2q). q-strong convexity plus the standard T2 inequality would sharpen the W2 bound using (2). We do not need that theorem for the construction.

Here is a self-contained weaker coupling bound. Couple an OU process Z_t stationary at G0 with target Langevin X_t, driven by the same Brownian motion, initially X0=Z0. Let D_t=X_t-Z_t. Monotonicity of grad U gives

    (d/dt)|D_t|<=-q|D_t|+|grad U(Z_t)|.

The co-coercivity/Stein proof in nonodd_trace_lemma.md gives

    E|grad U(Z_t)|²<=2q+2tau h²/q.

Minkowski applied to the integrated differential inequality yields

    W2(G0,pi)² <= A² := 2[1+tau r²]/q.               (3)

Target Langevin converges by q-strong convexity; its stationary limit and the stationary OU marginal provide the coupling. This argument handles arbitrary rotating residual curvature and uses no d-coordinate bound.

## Exact proximal chain, mixing and dimension-free caps

The exact transition is Y=X+sqrt(eta)G, followed by X'~R^V_{eta,Y}. The joint density pi(x)N(y;x,eta I) proves stationarity. For two explicit centers y,y', synchronous posterior Langevin and q-strong convexity give

    W2(R^V_{eta,y},R^V_{eta,y'})<=|y-y'|/(1+q eta).

Thus the proximal kernel contracts W2 by alpha=(1+q eta)^-1. Its last step satisfies

    TV(mu K,nu K)<=W2(mu,nu)/sqrt(2pi eta),           (4)

by translating the Gaussian Y proposals, the exact equal-covariance Gaussian TV formula, coupling and data processing through the common posterior kernel. Consequently

    TV(Law(X_N),pi)<=A alpha^(N-1)/sqrt(2pi eta).     (5)

Choose universal sufficiently small c and large C,

    eta=min(q^-1,c/[h(tau+L)]),
    N=ceil(C[1+r(tau+L)]L).

Then (5)<=epsilon/10. The logarithm of A/sqrt(eta) is O(L), including r very small or large, which proves this choice without an ambient-dimension mixing log.

For every ideal-chain n, W2(Law(X_n),pi)<=A and triangle inequality gives W2(Law(X_n),G0)<=2A. The completed-square residual RGO center is

    z_n=k(X_n+sqrt(eta)G), k=(1+q eta)^-1.

Couple X_n to G0 and use the same extra Gaussian. The comparison center is Gaussian with covariance k²(q^-1+eta)I=[q(1+q eta)]^-1 I<=q^-1 I, and the coupling displacement has L2 norm<=2kA. Co-coercivity/Stein at this comparison Gaussian, followed by the h-Lipschitz gradient bound on the displacement, yields

    E|grad U(z_n)|²<=M²,
    M²=4q+4tau h²/q+8h² A².                         (6)

Set R²=C N M²/epsilon. By Markov and union bound, the ideal chain ever satisfying |grad U(z_n)|>R has probability<=epsilon/10 for large C. The implemented sampler checks this COMPUTABLE gradient before every local center solve and returns a fixed fallback on violation. No Euclidean position cap proportional to sqrt(d) is necessary.

## Local RGO implementation and charged work

Completion gives gamma=eta/(1+q eta), z=kY and R^V_{eta,Y}=R^U_{gamma,z}. At every non-capped z use the independently reconstructed centered direct FORS primitive in c_gaussian/cycle3/direct_rgo_and_hidden_audit.md; the complete moment proof is also in nonodd_trace_lemma.md.

Take delta=epsilon/(10N), p=C log(2/delta)<=C'L, a universal B in (0,1], and finite gradient descent on U+|x-z|²/(2gamma) until the mode residual e has sqrt(gamma)|e|<=cB/sqrt(p). Its starting gradient is bounded by R. With gamma h<=c/(tau+L), residual contraction gamma h/(1+gamma h) gives at most C L center-gradient calls. Include the residual exactly in the likelihood estimator; it is not an omitted approximate-mode bias.

For proposal X=c+sqrt(gamma)G and fresh independent G',S,T, the estimator

    W=sqrt(gamma)G'.[grad U(c+S sqrt(gamma)G')-grad U(c)]
       -sqrt(gamma)G.[grad U(c+T sqrt(gamma)G)-grad U(c)]
       +sqrt(gamma)e.(G'-G)

has conditional mean EH-H(G), where H is the exact target-vs-proposal log discrepancy. Its moments under BOTH proposal and true target are

    ||W||_p <= C{gamma h[tau+sqrt(tau p)+p]
                           +sqrt(gamma)|e|sqrt(p)}.

The chosen eta makes this <=cB. Clipping to [-B,B], followed by source Algorithm3.2 FORS, has local TV<=delta. Expected attempts<=exp(2B) and expected fresh gradients<=4B exp(2B), counting EVERY rejected proposal/estimator. Cached grad U(c) is included in the center ledger. No calls to another RGO, exact proximal map, Hessian or unknown-center oracle remain.

At each valid history the local error bound is uniform. Sequential maximal coupling with the ideal chain, stopping at the first error or cap, bounds their path/output discrepancy by Ndelta plus the ideal cap probability. This is the reason a cap justified under the IDEAL chain suffices; no unproved moment claim for the approximate chain is made. Optional geometric-attempt/Poisson caps at O(log(N/epsilon)) add at most epsilon/10 and give almost-sure finite work; leaving them uncapped retains almost-sure termination and the same expected ledger.

Combining (5), local errors and caps gives total TV<=epsilon after universal constants are chosen. Expected work is N times C L center gradients plus constant additional FORS work, which is (1). There is no recursive-cost obligation left in THIS substituted architecture. The original Picard/cloud forest is not transferred by this statement.

## Precision, comparators and status

Exact-oracle access and real-valued Gaussian randomness are explicit assumptions. With certified uniform Euclidean gradient error rho on queried locations, the likelihood perturbation is at most C sqrt(gamma)rho(|G|+|G'|); its mean under proposal and target is O(sqrt(gamma)rho sqrt(d)). Taking rho<=c delta/[sqrt(gamma)sqrt(d)] suffices for an additional O(delta) local estimator-bias allocation. Center stopping must use half the exact residual tolerance and require sqrt(gamma)rho<=tolerance/8. Inexact gradient descent obeys |e_(j+1)|<=alpha_mode |e_j|+rho, so its residual floor is rho/(1-alpha_mode); reserve this in the stopping margin. An observed gradient cap R corresponds to true ideal threshold R-rho; choose R>=2rho and enlarge the cap constant accordingly. These are conditional robustness statements under a supplied certified Euclidean gradient-error bound.

A purely discrete finite-bit output has TV one against a continuous density. A floating implementation must specify continuous dequantization or comparison with a discretized target, then separately certify its Gaussian/proposal-law and arithmetic errors. Our float64 replay does not provide these certificates. Acquiring them depends on the supplied representation and cannot be inferred from a mathematical gradient promise. No finite-bit TV theorem is claimed.

The equally informed comparator is the source's direct FORS RGO/proximal sampler (AppendixA.1 and Section6.3), with the known quadratic already available (A.2). In ambient dimension the source direct primitive permits eta of order 1/sqrt(d log(1/delta)), stronger than our trace-leading variance when tau is large. The source whole fast sampler has tilde-O(kappa d^(1/5)) queries; (1) can improve the dimensional factor when tau and accuracy logs are small relative to d^(1/5), but loses for tau near d. Fixed known residual subspaces should instead be reduced before sampling; this theorem does not claim an advantage over that stronger baseline.

The own finite replay uses full-span logsumexp residuals at d=2,8,32,64 and validates a known Gaussian projection plus all rejected query counts. These fixtures have additional bounded-gradient structure and are not evidence of worst-case advantage. Mathematical and algorithmic ingredients (Gaussian completion, co-coercivity/Stein, synchronous Langevin, proximal chain, FORS) are older prior art. The complete supplied-trace extension and first failed identities are useful scoped research deductions; publication novelty has not been established. The fast hidden residual-RGO likelihood and mean forest remain open, and the mean-shell obstruction says simply inserting tau into the unchanged high-moment proof is false.
