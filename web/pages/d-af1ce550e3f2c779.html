## G4. Unpaired atomic frequencies give weighted leakage directly

off-diagonal unitary between eta blocks, choose coordinates

    O=[0 T*; T 0],  T*T=TT*=I.

If the actual A_omega acts on precisely one block, say
A_omega=diag(B_omega,0), direct multiplication gives

    [O,A_omega]*[O,A_omega]
      =diag(B_omega*B_omega, T B_omega*B_omega T*).      (G5)

Gibbs block, and the second contributes nonnegatively. No modular norm
or comparison of rho_+ with T*rho_-T enters. The opposite unpaired block
has the same argument. O preserves s, so summing distinct sectors does
not introduce cross-sector interference. Hence

    k_O=sum_omega gamma(omega)Tr rho[O,A_omega]*[O,A_omega]
       >=gamma_min(I) mu_unpaired(I).                 (G6)

Combined with G4 this yields the NECESSARY condition

    mu_paired(I)>=m_*-k_O/gamma_min(I).                (G7)

This conclusion concerns any full-space involution with the stated
