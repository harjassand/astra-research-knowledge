## 1. Exact idempotent splitting and normalized branch rings

The proved degree10 identity gives z_i=U_iW_i∈C, where U_i=1−2m_is_i and W_i=1−4m_is_i. In the quotient define μ_i=−W_i and ρ_i=2U_i. Exactly μ_i+ρ_i=1, μ_iρ_i=−2z_i and μ_i²−μ_i=ρ_i²−ρ_i=2z_i. Thus they are orthogonal idempotents. Their eight products split C into a direct product of branch rings, without a radical operation.

In a mass branch μ_i=1, hence U_i=0 and m_is_i=1/2. In a root branch ρ_i=1, hence W_i=0 and m_is_i=1/4. Since U_i=1/2 in the latter branch, A_i=0 also gives V_i=0, that is s_i²=q_i. This root statement uses the full idempotent branch; V_i=0 by itself leaves m_i free.

Every s_i is a unit. Put u_i=1/s_i. On a mass branch u_i=2m_i; on a root branch u_i=s_i/q_i and m_i=u_i/4. The resulting branch ring has the presentation

    root i: q_i u_i²=1,
    mass i: q_i u_i²+q_ij u_i u_j=2, for every j≠i.

All u_i are units in this presentation: the root relation gives inverse q_i u_i; any mass relation gives inverse (q_i u_i+q_ij u_j)/2. Therefore no uncharged localization is required. Conversely recover s_i=q_i u_i for a root index, and s_i=(q_i u_i+q_ij u_j)/2 for a mass index using any j≠i; recover m_i=u_i/4 or u_i/2 respectively. These formulas show a ring isomorphism, not only a correspondence between points. They also show all auxiliary variables are linear expressions in u on each branch.

## 3. B is a unit and the generic locus is smaller

At these real points put d_i=q_i/s_i²>0 and r_ij=q_ij/(s_i s_j). Strict PD gives |r_ij|<sqrt(d_id_j). Root d_i=1. An edge incident to a mass index has r_ij=κ_i=2−d_i. Multiple mass indices have a common κ.

If a mass-root edge exists, κ²<2−κ implies −2<κ<1. In the all-mass case κ²<(2−κ)² implies κ<1. A root-root edge has |r_ij|<1. Thus every r_ij<1 and

    B/(s_1s_2s_3)=3−r_12−r_13−r_23>0.

B consequently vanishes at no point of this reduced algebra. Its multiplication matrix is an invertible rational matrix of size≤64. This reconstructs the unit ideal without an unspecified Nullstellensatz existence step. It does not bound the degree of lifting that inverse back to the original A,H generators.

If α_ij=q_ij²/(q_iq_j) are pairwise distinct, every non-root branch is empty: one-mass nonempty branches require equality of its two incident α; two-mass branches require equal α to the root; all-mass branches require all three α equal.
