# What secular projection preserves, and where continuity fails

Independent rounding_prior derivation, 7 October2026. The fixed-H operator bounds below PASS by exact algebra. They are standard consequences of the prior Davies/Chesi time-average mechanism, not a claimed new thermal-stability theorem. The discontinuity and Gibbs-tail examples are finite obstructions to stronger proposed transfer rules, not counterexamples to an actual self-correcting-memory theorem.

## Exact interface

Let H=sum_E E P_E be any finite-dimensional Hermitian Hamiltonian, grouping each degenerate energy into one projector. For an operator S define

    S_omega=sum_(E'-E=omega) P_E S P_E'.

For one independent bath take rates0<=gamma(omega)<=Gamma and the Schrödinger dissipator

    D_S(rho)=sum_omega gamma(omega)
           [S_omega rho S_omega* - {S_omega* S_omega,rho}/2].

The norm bounds do not need detailed balance. The Gibbs/Dirichlet statements additionally assume Hermitian S and KMS rates gamma(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma(omega). They omit the Lamb shift; a Lamb-shift commutator must be charged separately if present. All compared couplings in a fixed-H statement use the SAME Hamiltonian and bath rates.

## Operator pinching, with no frequency-count factor

Spectral-projector orthogonality gives the cross identity

    sum_omega A_omega* B_omega
       =sum_E P_E A* B P_E.                              (1)

Indeed the middle projectors force the output energies equal; equal frequencies then force the input energies equal. Consequently

    sum_omega S_omega* S_omega=pinch_H(S* S)<=||S||^2 I.   (2)

For positive rates bounded by Gamma the weighted sum is at most Gamma||S||^2 I. This is an operator inequality. The stronger-looking statement sum_omega||S_omega||^2<=||S||^2 is false: swap m disjoint pairs of levels with distinct pair energy gaps. Then ||S||=1 but2m individual frequency components have norm1. A concrete choice is energies E_j=j^2, pairs(2j,2j+1).

Define the Heisenberg CP map Phi_S(X)=sum gamma S_omega* X S_omega. Its completely bounded norm is ||Phi_S(I)||<=Gamma||S||^2. Adding its anticommutator gives

    ||D_S||_diamond<=2Gamma||S||^2.                       (3)

This dual cb/diamond bound is stronger than the Hermitian induced-trace bound used in Chesi0907.2807v2 Proposition1, which already proves2K h_max via time averaging. There is no exponential number-of-frequencies loss.

For T=S+E, put all weighted components into rectangular columns. The cross CP map obeys

    ||sum gamma A_omega* (·) B_omega||_cb
                 <=Gamma||A||||B||.

Use Phi_T-Phi_S=Phi_(T,E)+Phi_(E,S). Its value at I obeys the same norm bound, so

    ||D_T-D_S||_diamond
       <=2Gamma (||T||+||S||)||T-S||.                    (4)

Sum(3–4) over independent bath channels, paying each Gamma. Duhamel then gives semigroup difference at most t times(4). This is useful for fixed-spectrum/unitary pullback or controlled bath truncation. It is not a continuity bound under a changed Hamiltonian or secular frequency partition.

For an exactly isospectral H'=UHU*, covariance gives

    U* D_(H',S)(U rho U*) U =D_(H,U* S U)(rho).

The physical bath remains S on the original system; after pullback its coupling is U*SU. Replacing it silently by S would change the problem.

## Logical commutation is a load-bearing premise

If [O,H]=0, then

    [S_omega,O]=([S,O])_omega.

For any rho commuting with H, (1) gives

    sum_omega Tr rho [S_omega,O]*[S_omega,O]
                  =Tr rho [S,O]*[S,O].                  (5)

Under Gibbs stationarity, the dissipative Dirichlet form is one half of the rate-weighted commutator-square sum. Thus it is at most Gamma/2 times the right-hand side of(5), with the present dissipator normalization. Alicki0811.0033v1 SectionIII.C uses this same orthogonality mechanism with a looser constant.

Without full [O,H]=0 the inference fails: H=Z/2 and S=O=X have [S,O]=0, but the two components of S are raising/lowering operators and their commutators with X are nonzero diagonal matrices. At beta0, the dissipative Dirichlet form is1 while the proposed bare bound is0.

The autocorrelation Jensen argument also needs the dissipative, self-adjoint generator on the zero-frequency logical sector, or an explicit co-rotating observable. With D=0,H=Z,O=X, the full generator has -<O,L O>=0 but autocorrelation cos(2t). It cannot be lower bounded by1. This does not affect the older paper's actual commuting dressed-logical application; it rules out an unqualified extension to approximately commuting full-H logical observables.

The Chesi Theorem1 similarly requires the logical Pauli pair and protected projector P to commute with the FULL H. Under those commutations, raw coupling protection [O,S]P=0 transfers to every S_omega by time averaging. Ground-band logical dressing alone supplies none of these excited-state commutations.

## Arbitrarily small commuting Hamiltonian changes can cause O(1) Davies changes

Two qubits suffice. In order00,01,10,11, take

    H0=diag(0,1,1,2), H_epsilon=H0+epsilon |11><11|,
    S=X1, epsilon>0.

The bath coupling S is fixed and single-qubit. The perturbation is two-qubit, check diagonal, has norm epsilon, and leaves the ground vector and gap1 unchanged. The down-frequency jump at epsilon0 is

    J=|00><10|+|01><11|.

For every epsilon>0 its two terms belong to frequencies1 and1+epsilon and become two separate jumps J0,J1. Choose their downward rates1 and KMS upward rates exp(-beta omega); they can come from one fixed continuous bounded-on-the-relevant-window rate function.

For rho=|1+><1+|, all upward jump and upward anticommutator contributions vanish. The downward anticommutator is the same before and after splitting. Hence

    (D_epsilon-D0)(rho)
        =-(|00><01|+|01><00|)/2,
    ||(D_epsilon-D0)(rho)||_1=1.                         (6)

This holds at every fixed beta, independently of epsilon. The additional coherent commutator from the perturbation cannot cancel(6), because it is in the orthogonal first-qubit1 block. At beta0 all rates are rational1, permitting a fully exact finite replay. H_epsilon approaches H0 and its Gibbs state approaches the old Gibbs state, while the fully secular Davies dissipator is discontinuous.

This diagnoses the order of limits, not a failure of physical weak coupling theory. The Davies limit fixes epsilon before sending system-bath coupling lambda to0. When epsilon scales with lambda^2, Trushechkin2103.12042v3 SectionsII–III and AppendixA rigorously retain the nearly degenerate frequency cluster instead. That clustered-generator interface is a different approximation; it cannot silently replace the stipulated fully secular bath model.

## Gibbs continuity does not control an exponentially rare old sector

For two finite Gibbs states rho0,rho1 of H,H+V, symmetric relative entropy equals beta Tr V(rho0-rho1). Pinsker on both directions and Hölder therefore give

    ||rho1-rho0||_1<=min(2,beta||V||).                    (7)

For local perturbations sum V_X the global norm may be O(NJ), so(7) supplies no uniform thermal-memory tail estimate.

Nor may one infer a multiplicative projector-probability bound by treating the matrix exponential as operator monotone. For H0=diag(0,E), V=epsilon X, the old excited projector has

    p0=1/(1+exp(beta E)),
    p1=[1-(E/sqrt(E^2+4epsilon^2))
                         tanh(beta sqrt(E^2+4epsilon^2)/2)]/2.

At fixed epsilon>0,beta>0 and increasing E, p1 has an epsilon^2/E^2 contribution while p0 is exponentially small. Their ratio is unbounded despite ||V||=epsilon. This finite example does not satisfy a normalized LDPC-memory family interface; it rejects the unqualified Gibbs-order/tail comparison lemma only.

## Consequence and strongest prior comparator

The useful positive transfer is fixed-H bath pullback plus(2–4), or exact full-H logical/protection commutation plus(5) and the Chesi encoder. These are existing-prior mechanisms made explicit. A generic ground-band stability theorem cannot replace them.

The actionable alternatives are: establish a new Gibbs bad-sector witness bound and weighted commutators for a full logical algebra; work in the exact centralizer class where the Hamiltonian remains scalar on syndrome sectors; or explicitly change to an admitted clustered/finite-resolution bath model. The actual May2026 memory's thermal robustness under arbitrary small noncommuting perturbations remains unproved by this note.
