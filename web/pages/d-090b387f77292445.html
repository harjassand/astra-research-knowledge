Title:When is a polynomial in three variables cylindrical?

Authors:Xiaojin Lin
            
View a PDF of the paper titled When is a polynomial in three variables cylindrical?, by Xiaojin Lin

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:We prove that a nonconstant polynomial $f\in\mathbb{C}[x_1,x_2,x_3]$ is cylindrical (that is, $f\in\mathbb{C}[x_1,x_2]$ after an invertible linear change of coordinates) if and only if its bordered Hessian determinant vanishes identically. The same conclusion holds over $\mathbb{R}$. We also give explicit counterexamples to this criterion in every dimension $n\ge4$.
