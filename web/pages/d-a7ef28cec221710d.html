# Integrated proper loss: unique full-state critical point, but failed Hamiltonian alignment

Worker r09_algebra, 2026-10-06. This sharpens the optimization boundary: nonconvex Hamiltonian coordinates do not imply spurious critical points on the full density domain. A separate exact example does refute Hamiltonian radial monotonicity, even in a restricted physical Pauli span.

## Exact Hessian and nullspace

Let the known Hermitian derivation frame have scalar ordinary commutant. Define ∂_aq=−i[A_a,q], I_{q,λ}(X)=(1−λ)qX+λXq, S_{λ,a}(q)=I_{q,λ}^−1(∂_aq), and the homogeneous convex potential

Ḡ(q)=½Σ_a∫_0^1 ⟨∂_aq,I_{q,λ}^−1∂_aq⟩_F dλ.

For a Hermitian variation δ, differentiate the score equation:

I_{q,λ}(DS_{λ,a}(q)[δ])=∂_aδ−I_{δ,λ}(S_{λ,a}(q)).

The quadratic variational envelope for G_λ is

sup_{T_a}Σ_a[Re⟨T_a,∂_aq⟩_F−½⟨T_a,I_{q,λ}(T_a)⟩_F],

with unique optimizer T_a=S_{λ,a}(q). Differentiating its first derivative and using the displayed score derivative gives the exact Hessian quadratic form

D²Ḡ(q)[δ,δ]=Σ_a∫_0^1 ⟨DS_{λ,a}(q)[δ],I_{q,λ}(DS_{λ,a}(q)[δ])⟩_F dλ.

No derivative of the optimizing T was dropped: its contribution combines precisely with ∂δ−I_δS=I_q DS. Each integrand is nonnegative and the metric is positive definite for faithful q.

If this Hessian vanishes, every derivative score vanishes for almost every λ and then every λ by continuity (rational analyticity also suffices). Differentiating the entrywise resolvent identity ∫_0^1S_{λ,a}(q)dλ=∂_alog q gives

[A_a,Dlog_q(δ)]=0 for every a.

Ordinary irreducibility forces Dlog_q(δ)=cI. The inverse derivative is the logarithmic-mean map Ω_q(X)=∫_0^1q^tXq^(1−t)dt, and Ω_q(I)=q. Hence δ=cq. Conversely homogeneity makes every radial δ=cq a Hessian null direction. Therefore

ker D²Ḡ(q)=span_R{q}.

On normalized tangent space Tr δ=0 the Hessian is strictly positive definite. This is pointwise finite-dimensional positivity, not a dimension- or temperature-uniform conditioning bound.

## Unique critical point in the complete faithful state domain

The integrated population regret is the reverse Bregman divergence

R(q)=D_{Ḡ}(σ,q)=Ḡ(σ)−Ḡ(q)−⟨∇Ḡ(q),σ−q⟩.

Its candidate gradient is D²Ḡ(q)(q−σ). If q is stationary on Tr q=1, test its derivative against the valid tangent δ=q−σ. This yields

DR(q)[q−σ]=D²Ḡ(q)[q−σ,q−σ].

Strict positive definiteness on that tangent implies q=σ. Thus σ is the **unique interior critical point**, as well as the unique global minimum, on the full faithful normalized state domain. There are no other full-state stationary saddles or minima.

For fixed β≠0, unrestricted traceless Hamiltonians map diffeomorphically to faithful states by q_H=e^−βH/Tr(e^−βH). Therefore the unrestricted H objective also has exactly one critical point, H_σ=−β^−1logσ modulo its scalar gauge. The earlier exact negative-curvature example does not contradict this theorem and must not be described as showing spurious minima.

For a restricted Hamiltonian span V, only the projection of the pullback gradient onto V must vanish. The above test direction q−σ need not be tangent to that nonlinear Gibbs submanifold. Thus the full-state proof does not rule out restricted-model spurious critical points. No such spurious minimum is claimed here.

## Formal preconditioning and its exponential full-state cost

In an orthonormal traceless Hermitian basis, let H_q be the restricted Hessian matrix. It is positive definite. Exact preconditioning by H_q^−1 transforms density-gradient flow into

q̇=−(q−σ),

whose solution q(t)=σ+e^−t(q(0)−σ) stays faithful and converges globally. In unrestricted Hamiltonian coordinates, the corresponding pullback metric is J_H*H_qJ_H, with J_H the Gibbs Jacobian; it induces the same density flow.

This is a mathematical full-domain optimization certificate, not an efficient learner. The tangent dimension is p=d²−1=4^n−1 on n qubits. Explicit dense Hessian storage needs Θ(d^4) entries and a generic dense inverse/factorization costs Θ(d^6) arithmetic. Computing its ingredients also needs candidate matrix/eigensystem/score operations. The full gradient comprises p candidate-dependent target expectations; copy access to σ does not supply those coordinates or the target matrix for free. A single scalar loss evaluation is not an exact full gradient or a free Hessian preconditioner. No sparse or local inverse-preconditioner representation has been proved here.

## Exact failure of radial alignment in a restricted local Pauli model

Use one qubit with complete physical XYZ derivation frame, β=1, but admit Hamiltonians only in V=span{X,Z}. Set the true Hamiltonian

H*=8X+6Z,

and faithful target σ=e^−H*/Tr(e^−H*). Its Bloch vector is −t(4,0,3)/5 with t=tanh10. Test the candidate H=3Z, which belongs to the same admitted span.

From the independently derived integrated observable

Ō(H)=4sinh²r I+4(r+sinh r cosh r)(h/r)·Pauli,

the objective J(H)=Tr(σ Ō(H)) has, at H=3Z, writing S=sinh6 and C=cosh6,

∂_xJ=−(4t/5)(4+2S/3),

∂_zJ=4S−(3t/5)(4+4C).

The Euclidean Hamiltonian error direction is H−H*=−8X−3Z. Its gradient alignment is

∇J(H)·(H−H*)=[t(492+64S+108C)−180S]/15.

All coefficients multiplying t are positive and t<1. Hence its numerator is strictly less than

492−116sinh6+108cosh6=492−4e^6+112e^−6<0.

The final sign is exact: e^6>Σ_{j=0}^5 6^j/j!=899/5 and e^−6<1 give an upper bound 492−4·899/5+112=−576/5<0. Therefore

∇J(H)·(H−H*)<0.

Ordinary Euclidean H-gradient descent initially **increases** squared Hamiltonian parameter error at this point. The counterexample is fully within a two-parameter local Pauli span, has finite temperature and a faithful target. Its target norm10 corresponds to poor but finite spectral conditioning, which must be charged.

This disproves radial monotonicity in H and any argument that assumes positive alignment of the product of the potential Hessian and the Gibbs/logarithmic-mean Jacobian. It does not by itself refute a PL inequality: radial alignment and PL are different properties. In this particular qubit span the full gradient stays in the X/Z plane, so restricted stationarity implies full stationarity; there is still only one critical point. No restricted-model spurious minimum has been exhibited.

## Status and next falsifiable step

The Hessian envelope and nullspace were reconstructed independently from the exact score interface; they need no unknown target eigensystem. The qubit alignment failure has a rational/exponential sign certificate, rather than only a numerical diagnostic. No novelty claim is made. These results distinguish uniqueness, convexity, radial monotonicity, conditioning, gradient acquisition and computational implementation.
