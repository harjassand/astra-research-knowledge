## Primary-source theorem map

The governing convention in Xu (2026) is standard stochastic mass action: for an integer state `x`, a fixed positive rate constant `kappa`, and reaction `y -> y'`, the intensity is `kappa x_under_y`; the deterministic ODE instead uses monomials. Xu Definition 3.1 uses “regular” for stochastic non-explosivity together with global existence of the deterministic ODE. Recurrence and positive recurrence are defined separately by communicating class. This terminology is the principal audit guardrail.

Xu, *On the Regularity of Reaction Systems*, arXiv:2409.05340v2, Theorem 4.6, proves regularity of every second-order `1`-endotactic mass-action system and lists ordinary endotactic systems as a subclass. Here second-order bounds reactant molecularity by two; `1`-endotactic is a one-direction condition, and ordinary endotactic is the all-directions condition. The proof's stochastic mechanism is total-count drift: no order-two reaction can increase total count, so positive count drift comes only from order-zero/one propensities and is at most affine. That rules out explosion; it is not a negative drift/recovery estimate. The paper's Theorem 4.12 gives at most one stationary probability per closed communicating class, not existence. Theorem 4.13 says a bimolecular weakly reversible system is positive recurrent iff every communicating class has a stationary probability; its proof uses that weakly reversible classes are closed. Corollary 4.17 states the stationary-probability equivalence for a closed class of a second-order `1`-endotactic system. These are the exact bridges; none asserts universal classwise stationary-mass existence for all bimolecular endotactic networks.

Primary link: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2

Wiuf–Xu, *Classification and threshold dynamics of stochastic reaction networks*, arXiv:2012.07954v2, Theorem 4.11 has narrower one-dimensional hypotheses: H2, `dim S=1` with the first species not a catalyst; H3, nonempty positive and negative subnetworks; and H4, `omega*` is nonnegative, excluding conservative networks when targeting infinite classes. H1 (propensity is positive exactly when its reactant is available) is automatic for standard mass action. Under H2–H4 plus endotacticity, the process is non-explosive on `L_c`; positive irreducible components `P_c`, when present, are exponentially ergodic. The theorem separately gives uniform exponential quasi-ergodicity on the open quasi-irreducible components `Q_c` when its additional `R>1` condition holds. Corollary 4.12 gives positive recurrence on every PIC for weakly reversible systems with H2 and H4. The component/PIC/QIC language cannot be collapsed into a statement that an entire stoichiometric class is irreducible or recurrent.

Primary link: https://arxiv.org/html/2012.07954v2

Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz, *Product-form stationary distributions for deficiency zero chemical reaction networks*, arXiv:0803.3042, Theorem 4.1 gives a stationary product-Poisson solution when deterministic mass action is complex balanced. If the lattice is reducible, the normalized restriction to each closed irreducible component is stationary. Theorem 4.2 uses the deficiency-zero theorem: a weakly reversible, deficiency-zero network is complex balanced for every positive fixed rate vector. This by itself supplies an invariant probability/formal stationary solution, not a non-explosion proof. Anderson–Cappelletti–Koyama–Kurtz, arXiv:1708.09356, Theorem 3 separately establishes non-explosion of complex-balanced stochastic mass-action systems. On each closed irreducible component, invariant probability plus regularity therefore yields positive recurrence. This scope is a structural subclass, not arbitrary endotactic recurrence.

Primary links: https://arxiv.org/html/0803.3042 and https://arxiv.org/html/1708.09356

The counterexample boundary is also explicit in primary sources. Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen, *Tier structure of strongly endotactic reaction networks*, arXiv:1808.05328v2, Example 3.2 proves stochastic explosion for the strongly endotactic network `0 -> 2A -> 4A+B -> 6A+4B -> 3A`; the maximum reactant molecularity is ten. Xu Remark 4.8 reports that the minimum order for explosion among endotactic systems remains open. This is a deterministic-to-stochastic counterexample outside the bimolecular regime, not a bimolecular counterexample.

Primary link: https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05328v2

## Cross-exposure notes on Luna checkpoints

The Luna claims are attributed to their frozen `work/cycle6/c10_lXX/INITIAL.txt` files; this list records only the audit-relevant scope.

- `c10_l02`: a rank-one weakly reversible/bimolecular Foster reconstruction. Its positive-recurrence conclusion falls within Wiuf–Xu's one-dimensional result under the applicable infinite-class hypotheses; it is not a new general recurrence theorem. One written step has a genuine but repairable gap: `F(t)=G t log t+O(t)` does not alone imply a finite-difference formula `F(t+alpha)-F(t)=alpha G log t+O(1)`. The gap repairs as follows. For each eventually available complex `y`, `P_y(t)=x(t)_under_y` is a polynomial with positive leading coefficient; among a finite family of such polynomials, an eventual maximizer is a fixed polynomial (up to identical ties). Thus `M(t)=max_y P_y(t)=m t^p+O(t^{p-1})`, and `log M(t+alpha)-log M(t)=O(1/t)`. Separately, the exact factorial ratio satisfies `sum_i log((b_i+(t+alpha)v_i)!/(b_i+t v_i)!)=alpha G log t+O(1)` by expanding the finite product of `|alpha|v_i` consecutive factors (with the sign reversed for alpha<0). This supplies the required increment; the coarse asymptotic should not be cited for it.
- `c10_l03`: the source-swap endpoint lemma and thick-face one-jump recovery are graph/path statements. They give a component-preserving endpoint with non-increasing residual-factorial potential, but no expected negative drift; path probability may be polynomially small. The frozen report correctly leaves recurrence unresolved.
- `c10_l04`: stationary laws escape uniformly across infinitely many stochastic components in one deterministic stoichiometric class. Its displayed network has a source `3A+2B` of molecularity five, so it is not a bimolecular recurrence counterexample. Each component is positively recurrent; its product-Poisson restrictions are consistent with deficiency-zero prior art.
- `c10_l05`: an exact rational Foster certificate for a three-reaction family with an autonomous immigration/death cofactor. This is a valid scoped example; the general support-face synthesis gate remains.
- `c10_l06`: the linear total-count proof extends non-explosion to bounded predictable controls under a second-order endotactic interface. The exact count-one/count-two examples refute unconditional deterministic-face-to-lattice recovery, but the trapped communicating classes are finite; they do not refute classwise recurrence. The `1,1`-endotactic transient example in Xu is not ordinary all-directions endotactic, a distinction the report correctly calls out.
- `c10_l07`: exact rational LP synthesis and recovery is for a finite supplied two-species fixed-total class, with explicit scope/row caps. Its parity obstruction is an integer-lattice target-reachability counterexample, not a recurrence counterexample.
- `c10_l08`: scalar return bounds under predictable controls for a finite fixed-total conversion chain, with explicit positive drift margin. The finite-state property guarantees non-explosion; no multidimensional theorem follows.
- `c10_l09`: the reconstructed residual-factorial bound is indexed by reaction number under source-completeness and uniformly positive rates. It controls event-clock growth only; its own report correctly says it supplies no holding-time, non-explosion, negative drift, or recurrence result.

## Strongest unresolved interface and status

For fixed positive constant rates, the sharp primary-source reduction for general bimolecular weakly reversible networks is: non-explosion is already known, so positive recurrence reduces class by class to proving existence of a stationary probability on every actual communicating component (Xu Theorem 4.13). Complex balance solves this for the deficiency-zero subclass. The reviewed Sol certificates solve selected subclasses and c10_s02 rules out selected naive potential classes; neither side supplies the missing all-network stationary-mass or boundary-recovery theorem. Deterministic permanence/global attraction, non-explosion, finite-horizon safety, event-clock envelopes, stochastic cosets, and a finite exact compiler are not substitutes for that missing existence proof.

For predictable rate controls, no stationary-distribution conclusion is available because the controlled process need not be time homogeneous. The c10_l06 and c10_s03 non-explosion/recovery estimates have separate, explicit control quantifiers; the fixed-rate ACK, Wiuf–Xu, and product-Poisson results do not transfer to them without further argument.

Status: primary statements verified against the linked arXiv HTML versions; finite replay checks passed; no formal proof checker, external referee, exhaustive literature-priority search, or field-breaking claim. The rank-one finite-difference gap is repaired above, but full multidimensional stochastic recurrence/boundary recovery remains open in this worker's audit.
