## Strongest exact claim

The proposed Dirichlet constant 2 is sharp already for an explicit qutrit channel, and the obstruction is finite and exact. A 1-to-2 universal qutrit cloner has a positive, selfadjoint, selfcompatible likelihood map Phi with eigenvalue 5/8 on all traceless observables. An explicit 12-outcome qutrit mutually-unbiased-basis measurement gives the canonical EB map Psi with eigenvalue 1/4 on that subspace. Thus

    2 (I - Phi) = I - Psi

as an exact superoperator identity. No constant c<2 can work for every positive selfadjoint selfcompatible channel: an eight-element Gell-Mann frame and the collective-variance witness give variance gap 3 theta^2/8 against mean Dirichlet energy 3 theta^2/16. This is a sharpness result, not a counterexample to c=2.

The qutrit family does not refute a dimension-free EB reconstruction modulus. For fixed theta<=1/2 its residual remains order theta (for the selected frame, r=theta/4); there is no sequence with r->0 and EB error bounded below. A finite failure of a proposed Dirichlet constant, even if one were found, would only refute that quantitative route. Refuting the qualitative dimension-free implication requires a growing sequence with residual tending to zero while the collective variance q(E), equivalently EB error, stays bounded away from zero.

## Exact construction and derivation

Let d=3, F be the swap on C^3 tensor C^3, and P_+=(I+F)/2. The channel

    B(X) = (1/2) P_+ (X tensor I_3) P_+

is completely positive by its displayed sandwich and has symmetric outputs. Since

    Tr[P_+(X tensor I)P_+] = Tr[(X tensor I)P_+]
                          = 2 Tr X,

it is trace preserving. Expanding P_+ and taking the second partial trace gives

    Phi(X) = Tr_2 B(X) = (5 X + Tr(X) I_3)/8.

The first marginal is selfcompatible because B has both marginals Phi. Its Heisenberg/likelihood action is the same formula. It fixes scalars and acts by 5/8 on the eight-dimensional traceless space. It is UCP, trace-preserving for tau=Tr/3, positive and selfadjoint as an L2(tau) operator.

Take the four qutrit MUBs: the computational basis and the three bases with vectors

    |psi_(a,b)> = 3^(-1/2) sum_(j=0)^2 omega^(a j^2 + b j) |j>,
    a,b in {0,1,2},  omega=exp(2 pi i/3).

For their twelve rank-one projectors P_y, the exact identities are sum_y P_y=4 I and sum_y P_y tensor P_y=I+F. The effects M_y=P_y/4 form a POVM, tau(M_y)=1/12, and its canonical measurement/repreparation channel is

    Psi(X) = (1/4) sum_y Tr(P_y X) P_y
           = (X + Tr(X) I_3)/4.

This is EB, UCP, tau-preserving, positive and selfadjoint. It fixes scalars and acts by 1/4 on traceless observables. Therefore I-Psi is 3/4 times identity on the traceless space, while I-Phi is 3/8 times identity there; both vanish on scalars. This proves the exact equality 2(I-Phi)=I-Psi and realizes c=2 with a finite measurement.

For a lower-bound witness against c<2, choose a Hermitian traceless Gell-Mann basis G_1,...,G_8 normalized by Tr(G_a G_b)=3 delta_ab. Then tau(G_a^2)=1, max_a ||G_a||_infinity=sqrt(2), and the completeness relation is

    sum_a G_a tensor G_a = 3 F - I.

For every unit vector psi this implies sum_a <psi|G_a|psi>^2=2. Use the finite likelihood frame H_a=theta G_a/sqrt(2), with 0<theta<=1/2, and the uniform prior p_a=1/8. Its variance term is V(p)=theta^2/2. For every pure-state reference ensemble, the captured term is C(p)=theta^2/8, since the sum of squared expectations is 2 for each pure state. Thus V(p)-C(p)=3 theta^2/8. The mean channel Dirichlet energy is

    sum_a p_a <H_a,(I-Phi)H_a>_tau = (3/8)(theta^2/2)=3 theta^2/16.

The finite-frame minimax inequality required by a c-Dirichlet comparator would force 3 theta^2/8 <= c(3 theta^2/16), hence c>=2. This uses the same collective (single common measurement) variance as the target; separate measurements per direction would not be a valid comparator.

## Boundary and status

The universal cloner is prior art, not a new cloning result. Werner's primary paper gives the symmetric N-to-M cloner; specializing its formula to N=1,M=2 yields B above. The new work in this checkpoint is the exact translation into the Dirichlet and finite-frame variance boundary, with an explicit finite qutrit POVM attaining equality. The derivation does not assume Werner's optimality theorem; the explicit CPTP map suffices.

This does not establish the unrestricted c=2 theorem, nor disprove it. It supplies no growing near-fixed counterexample and no new EB modulus. It also does not certify novelty or priority. The existence of a finite exact qutrit witness is not asymptotic evidence.
