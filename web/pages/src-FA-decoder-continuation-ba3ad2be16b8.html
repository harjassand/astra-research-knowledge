Result
======

For the inherited G106/300 no-echo candidate, I reconstructed the source's
finite 3.0 us read plus 0.8 us active-reset schedule and added a passive
ringdown interval L=0, 0.8, or 1.6 us. In the restricted q-preserving linear
pole model, the best thresholded homodyne receiver is already weak for late
f->e changes: at a whole-window false-flag probability alpha=2%, its miss
probability is at least 95% for change times tau in [3.46310,3.8] us with no
tail, [3.55669,4.6] us with a 0.8 us tail, and [3.57146,5.4] us with a 1.6 us
tail. These are numerical threshold intervals for the specified schedule.

For a stronger carrier-only comparison, I also evaluated the exact binary
POVM tradeoff for the two pure coherent output states at each known tau. At
the 3.8 us decision endpoint, source-rate weighting plus survival in e gives
terminal-e mass 0.14043173 per initial f. Even the arbitrary coherent-carrier
POVM then misses terminal e with mass at least 0.05123364 per initial f, or
0.02561682 for a maximally mixed g/f code input. This leaves room relative to
the separate approximately 3.2% ancilla comparator; it does not show that a
physical receiver attains the carrier-only POVM optimum. If the decision is
delayed to 4.6 or 5.4 us with passive tails and transitions continue, the
corresponding mixed-code carrier-only POVM terminal-e miss floors rise to
0.03387559 and 0.04518117. These schedule points have different latencies and
are not interchangeable with the 3.8 us comparator.

The result is a restricted, internally reconstructed model gate. It excludes
all f->e jump photons, sidebands, other ports, nonlinear jump-conditioned
cavity states, and acquisition uncertainty. It is neither a universal quantum
bound nor a device result. Any receiver that collects extra jump outputs can
do better than the carrier-only result.

Model and bound
===============

The source schedule uses kappa=2*pi*0.21/us, eta=0.5, read drive
1.47180145439/us, and two reset drives (-1.86944015972+0.88711731645i)/us and
(-1.80486139164-0.68219895671i)/us. The q-specific poles are
lambda_e=0.65663973735/us and
lambda_f=0.65322493266+6.25615339732i/us, with carrier factors
v_e=0.99761491951 and v_f=0.99455789690. Let alpha_q be the source pole-state
coordinate and beta_q=sqrt(kappa)*v_q*alpha_q the output carrier. Between
source schedule boundaries,

  d alpha_q/dt = -lambda_q alpha_q + v_q u(t).

At a modeled f->e change point, alpha_e(tau)=alpha_f(tau) is imposed, so the
output carrier can step by the small v_e/v_f factor. Afterward the two branches
follow their e and f poles under the same schedule. This is an explicit
change-point convention in the linear model; it does not insert a jump photon
or derive the cavity state after a physical Floquet bath jump.

For W=3.8+L us, define the raw carrier displacement

  J(tau,L) = integral[tau,W] |beta_e(t;tau)-beta_f(t)|^2 dt.

The collected coherent-state overlap is c^2=exp(-eta*J). For phase-matched
homodyne, the whole-record matched statistic has distributions

  H0 (no jump): Z ~ N(0,1),
  H1 (known f->e time tau): Z ~ N(2*sqrt(eta*J),1).

Setting P0(flag)=alpha is the explicit false rejection of a no-jump code
record (equivalently, false acceptance of an erasure flag). Threshold
z_(1-alpha) gives the exact known-arrival homodyne miss

  beta_hom(tau,L) = Phi(Phi^-1(1-alpha)-2*sqrt(eta*J(tau,L))).

The KL divergence in the measured matched statistic is 2*eta*J. An unknown-
arrival homodyne detector with the same whole-window false-flag budget cannot
have lower miss at any fixed tau than this timing-aware Neyman-Pearson test.

For arbitrary binary POVMs on only these two pure coherent carrier states,
the exact minimum miss at the same false-flag size is

  beta_POVM = [sqrt((1-alpha)c^2)-sqrt(alpha*(1-c^2))]^2, if alpha<c^2;
              0, otherwise.

This is the optimal asymmetric binary discrimination result for the stipulated
pure states; it does not include any non-carrier output modes. The terminal-e
event density per initial f is

  p_term(tau;W) = r_fe*exp(-Lambda_f*tau)*exp(-Lambda_e*(W-tau)),
                  0 <= tau <= W,

using the source scenario r_fe=0.04131524752645586/us,
Lambda_f=0.04133833561408882/us, and Lambda_e=0.01753125447611327/us. The
extra survival factor is essential: returned e->g paths are not counted as
terminal e. Integrating p_term*beta gives the missed terminal-e mass. The
maximally mixed code value is half the per-initial-f value. Since an unflagged
terminal |e> is orthogonal to the code, that mixed-code missed mass is an
unnormalized accepted error contribution; after normalization by accepted
probability (at most one), it remains a lower bound within this carrier-only
instrument model.

For the passive tail, an analytic uniform distance bound is available. With
alpha_f(T) the source pole coordinate at T=3.8 us, delta_v=|v_e-v_f|, and
delta_lambda=|lambda_e-lambda_f|, for every tau in [T,T+L],

  J(tau,L) <= kappa*|alpha_f(T)|^2 * [delta_v^2*L
      + delta_v*v_f*delta_lambda*L^2
      + v_f^2*delta_lambda^2*L^3/3].

This follows from Re(lambda_e,f)>0, alpha_f(tau) decay, and
|v_e*exp(-lambda_e*s)-v_f*exp(-lambda_f*s)|
<= delta_v+v_f*delta_lambda*s. The exact schedule leaves
|alpha_f(T)|^2=8.54198883e-7 and |beta_f(T)|^2=8.44926905e-7. The bound gives
J<=7.46074e-6 for L=0.8 and J<=5.96310e-5 for L=1.6; consequently the
known-arrival homodyne misses are at least 0.979812 and 0.979465 for a terminal
e event occurring in those tails. The coherent-carrier POVM miss lower bounds
are 0.979456 and 0.978443. Events continue during every charged tail.

Numerical results
=================

At alpha=0.02 and eta=0.5, homodyne miss is at least 95% whenever
J<=0.08359767651. A dense schedule scan with bracketed root refinement finds
the late intervals listed above. For the 3.8 us endpoint, terminal-e mass is
0.1404317288 per initial f; its missed mass is at least 0.0747707193 for
homodyne and 0.0512336430 for the carrier-only arbitrary POVM. After the
maximally mixed code factor 1/2, those become 0.0373853597 and 0.0256168215.
At W=4.6 us the mixed-code POVM floor is 0.0338755884, and at W=5.4 us it is
0.0451811702. Tail-only mixed-code POVM missed terminal-e masses are 0.0135178793
and 0.0264073772, respectively.

The separate counterfactual fixed-e-hold integral weights every first
f->e arrival as though e remained occupied through the decision; it is kept in
NUMERICAL_RESULTS.json only as a diagnostic and is not used for terminal-e or
cascade claims. Returned cascades need a second change point and are not
modeled here.

Checks: analytic piecewise exponential integrals agree with independent direct
quadrature to maximum absolute error 5.56e-16. The homodyne closed form and
direct Gaussian integration agree at miss 0.7224963704 and FPR 0.0200000000
for a representative change point. Sixteen-, 32-, and 64-point event-time
quadrature agree for terminal-e homodyne/POVM missed mass to at most 4.45e-14 /
4.34e-11, respectively. The code and full receipts are
terminal_change_point_bound.py and NUMERICAL_RESULTS.json.
