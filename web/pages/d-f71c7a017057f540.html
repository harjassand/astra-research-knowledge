# The exact metric required for score-to-state calibration

2026-10-06, cycle2. This is our independent derivation, not a claimed source theorem. It identifies a reusable load-bearing lemma for learning beyond the high-temperature regime.

Let sigma>0 be a trace-one target, rho>0 a candidate, A_a Hermitian, S_a(q) its unitary-orbit SLD, and

I_sigma=(L_sigma+R_sigma)/2,
H_sigma=2L_sigma R_sigma/(L_sigma+R_sigma),
K_a=T_sigma(A_a)=H_sigma(sigma^-1/2 A_a sigma^-1/2).

In sigma's eigenbasis, H_sigma has weights h_ij=2p_i p_j/(p_i+p_j); K_a,ij=2sqrt(p_i p_j)/(p_i+p_j) A_a,ij. Inner products are <X,Y>_H=Tr X* H_sigma(Y). In particular H_sigma(I)=sigma and ||I||_H=1.

Define the harmonic dressed-frame gap

g_H(sigma,A)=inf_{X=X*,Tr(sigma X)=0,X!=0} [sum_a ||[K_a,X]||_H^2]/||X||_H^2.

Finite dimensionality and faithfulness make g_H>0 equivalent to scalar dressed commutant. This is a target-dependent mathematical constant, not an observable acquisition certificate.

## Exact differential score geometry

For a normalized Hermitian variation delta rho=sqrt(sigma)Xsqrt(sigma), Tr(sigma X)=0, differentiating the SLD equation gives

I_sigma(delta S_a)=-i[A_a,delta rho]-{delta rho,S_a(sigma)}/2
                   =sqrt(sigma)(-i[K_a,X])sqrt(sigma).

Therefore

sum_a Tr(sigma (delta S_a)^2)=sum_a ||[K_a,X]||_H^2,

Tr(delta rho I_sigma^-1(delta rho))=||X||_H^2.

Proof of both identities: conjugation C_sigma(X)=sqrt(sigma)Xsqrt(sigma) obeys C_sigma I_sigma^-1 C_sigma=H_sigma, since L_sigma and R_sigma commute. The first equality also uses ||I_sigma^-1 L||_I^2=<L,I_sigma^-1 L>. The score derivative is Hermitian although its commutator representative is anti-Hermitian; multiplying by -i preserves the H norm.

Thus the score Hessian is precisely a commutator Dirichlet energy in the harmonic metric, not in the unweighted Frobenius or ordinary sigma-weighted metric. A dimension-free unweighted gap alone need not control the physical SLD metric without spectral costs.

Trace-norm duality gives

||delta rho||_1^2 <= Tr(delta rho I_sigma^-1(delta rho))=||X||_H^2,

because for every Hermitian ||O||_infinity<=1, <O,I_sigma O>=Tr(sigma O^2)<=1. Hence a positive harmonic gap controls score-to-state error differentially with no global lambda_min(sigma).

## A finite, exact calibration bound under a relative-density cap

Let J_sigma(rho)=1/2 sum_a Tr[sigma(S_a(rho)-S_a(sigma))^2]. Suppose rho<=c sigma for some c>=1. Then

||rho-sigma||_1^2 <= [2c^2/g_H(sigma,A)] J_sigma(rho).

Proof: set X=sigma^-1/2(rho-sigma)sigma^-1/2, centered in H. For D_a=S_a(rho)-S_a(sigma), the residual is

R_a=-i[A_a,rho]-{rho,S_a(sigma)}/2=I_rho(D_a)
   =sqrt(sigma)(-i[K_a,X])sqrt(sigma).

Thus E_H(X)=sum_a <I_rho D_a,I_sigma^-1 I_rho D_a>. Positive superoperator domination rho<=c sigma implies I_rho<=c I_sigma. Therefore B=I_sigma^-1/2 I_rho I_sigma^-1/2 is positive with ||B||<=c, and

<I_rho D,I_sigma^-1 I_rho D>=||B I_sigma^1/2D||_F^2
 <=c^2 <D,I_sigma D>=c^2 Tr(sigma D^2).

Apply g_H and the trace-norm inequality to obtain the stated result. No commutation of rho with sigma or I_rho with I_sigma was assumed.

**Scope/cost.** The cap c is an operator likelihood-ratio bound, not a supplied measurement statistic. It is finite for every faithful pair, but may be exponential in system size. A local Hamiltonian coefficient box does not automatically imply a useful n-independent c. In a small admitted perturbation family the cap can be useful; a general Gibbs learner still needs a locally computable g_H bound and cost-accounted model/copy interface. This theorem does not establish optimization convergence or remove low-temperature lower bounds.

## Nonperturbative diagonal mechanism

Worker b_prior reconstructed a comparison to a classical slow heat-bath chain for diagonal sigma; details live in its cycle2 notes. The diagonal sector has H norm E_sigma|f|^2, and each flip-edge energy is 4 times the heat-bath Dirichlet edge multiplied by w_xy^2, where w_xy=2sqrt(p_xp_y)/(p_x+p_y). Different XOR sectors remain orthogonal after each dressed X_i commutator, so diagonal-sector energy cannot cancel against coherences. Z_i commutators control all offdiagonal sectors by at least4 times their H norm. Consequently

g_H >=4 min{lambda_slow_heatbath,1}
    >=4 min{sech^2(beta L/2)lambda_heatbath,1}

when each spin-flip energy difference is bounded by L. This avoids global lambda_min(sigma). A mixing/Poincare theorem, rather than merely absence of a kernel, becomes the exact remaining statistical-geometric input. The resulting diagonal estimator itself is established classical ratio matching.

## What would be consequential

For a genuinely noncommuting local Gibbs family, prove a local comparison of the dressed SLD harmonic Dirichlet form to an efficiently implementable Gibbs heat-bath/quantum Markov form with a known spectral or entropy bound. Alternatively construct a faithful fixed-local-Pauli collision showing that no such universally positive comparison exists. Detailed balance and modular eigenoperator hypotheses must be checked before borrowing any quantum-semigroup theorem. The general reflection obstruction prohibits a generic argument using CP positivity or involution spectra alone.

Current unresolved interface: computing K_sigma or its harmonic gap from an unknown target by tomography would defeat the intended learner. A useful sufficient bound must follow from admitted locality/temperature/model information, not access to sigma's eigenbasis.
