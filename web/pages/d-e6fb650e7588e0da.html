# Independent all-order logarithmic-frequency audit

7 October 2026. Proposal audited: `notes/r15_geometric_analysis/cycle6/LOG_FREQUENCY_REGULARITY.md`, including its explicit single-K clarification. **PASS.** One fixed real smooth pair u≠0,V satisfies every fixed-order derivative estimate; there is no derivative-order-dependent change of the construction. This is separate from the power-frequency theorem. No novelty claim or external validation follows from this internal proof audit.

## Statement, assumptions, and prerequisites

For each n≥3, the construction yields real smooth u,V with −Δu+Vu=0 and, for every Cartesian multiindex ν,

    |D^ν V(x)|≤C_ν (1+|x|)^−1/2
                      [log(2+|x|)]^(|ν|+3/2),          (1)
    |u(x)|≤C exp[−c|x|log(2+|x|)].                    (2)

Constants are finite and may depend on the one fixed construction and ν. No uniform control as |ν|→∞, analytic/Gevrey growth, prescribed norms, sign condition, relative symbol estimates, or acquisition of a supplied potential is assumed or proved. Each fixed derivative tends to zero at infinity and is globally bounded.

The prerequisite is the independently proved sparse-domain inverse in `WEIGHTED_LANDIS_AUDIT.md`: if U occupies at most a sufficiently small fraction of every ball B(x,ρ/a(x)), the radius has a bounded-overlap comparable cover, and a,h,E are uniformly locally comparable, then −Δz=hE, z∈H¹_0(U), obeys 0≤z≤Cρ²ha^−2E. Its proof uses the zero-extension subsolution, ball Green estimate, contraction of the weighted supremum on bounded smooth domains, and coercive weak exhaustion. It assumes n≥3, a_min>0, smooth positive weights and hE∈L². The tangent-cover and compact cosine-family sparsity argument is also a prerequisite, and is reconstructed for these scales below.

Frank–Ivanisvili 2608.00802v1 supplies the real harmonic-repair mechanism, its zeroth-order sparse inversion, and C∞ variable radial smoothing. Their Theorem 1 and Appendix A do not assert (1)–(2); all-order quantitative bounds below are deductions. Source versions/read locations/snapshots are already preserved in `sources/r07_analysis/cycle6/metadata.json`.

## 1. One fixed K and all elementary jets

Let K>e⁴ and set

    R=(K²+|x|²)^1/2, a=log R, ℓ=(R/a)^1/2,
    κ=a^(3/2)R^−1/2, η=κ/a²=(Ra)^−1/2,
    Φ=RlogR−R−(KlogK−K), E=e^−Φ.

The identities a/ℓ=a²ℓ/R=κ and ℓ/R=1/(aℓ)=η are exact. Also ℓ^−1≤a, a/R≤κ, and R^−2≤κ. The function η decreases on R≥K, so its supremum is η(K).

For every j≥1, derivatives of R obey |D^jR|≤C_jR^(1−j): differentiate the homogeneous regularized norm and use |x|/R≤1 and K/R≤1. Chain/product differentiation then gives

    |D^ja|≤C_jR^−j≤C_jaR^−j,
    |D^jℓ|≤C_jℓR^−j, |D^jκ|≤C_jκR^−j,
    |D^jΦ|≤C_jaR^(1−j).

All denominators logR are bounded below by 4. Derivatives of E are sums of products of derivatives of Φ. In a term of total order j containing r factors, its coefficient is at most C_j a^r R^(r−j)≤C_j a^j. Thus

    |D^jE|≤C_j a^j E, j≥0.                          (3)

These are individual fixed-order bounds for every K>e⁴. They do not impose a new smallness condition on K for larger j.

Local comparison on balls of radius ρ/a, ρ≤1, follows from |∇Φ|≤a and |∇R|≤1, exactly as in the power audit. The two repair weights h₁=κ and h₂=a²/R² have bounded relative logarithmic derivatives O(1/R), uniformly K>e⁴. Hence their micro comparability constants, and the sparse inverse constants, do not depend on a later derivative order.

The actual K selection uses only: slow variation/bounded overlap of ℓ and ρ/a; uniform pairwise frequency separation; C⁰/C¹ partition freezing; and zeroth-order repair smallness. Choose the fixed C∞ bump before constructing the cover. On that one cover, normalized partition functions obey |D^jχ_i|≤C_jℓ_i^−j for every j: the positive normalizing sum has a fixed positive lower bound and bounded overlap, so its reciprocal's derivatives follow inductively. Higher C_j are not required to satisfy C_jη(K)≪1. Thus the cover, directions, q, Ω, w and K are fixed once. A single fixed σ is chosen for smoothing after the zeroth-order parameters. Every later derivative estimate merely has a different finite constant.

## 2. Arbitrary derivatives of the cancelled residual

On each macro patch, a_i=a(x_i) is constant, t_i·x_i=0, and a_i is uniformly comparable to a(x). The same cap-selection argument on S^(n−2) gives separated frequency pairs on neighboring patches. Let

    c_i(x)=cos[a_i t_i·(x−x_i)], q=Σ_iχ_i c_i,
    f=Eq.

At each point only a bounded number of patches or their derivative supports occur. Differentiating c_i costs at most C a per derivative; differentiating χ_i costs at most ℓ^−1≤a. Consequently |D^νq|≤C_νa^|ν|, and (3) gives |D^νf|≤C_νa^|ν|E.

It is essential to differentiate a cancelled expression, not to estimate D^(m+2)f crudely. Write

    S=Δq+a²q
     =Σ_i[(Δχ_i)c_i+2∇χ_i·∇c_i+(a²−a_i²)χ_i c_i].

The first two families have base sizes ℓ^−2 and a/ℓ, at most Cκ; every extra derivative costs at most a. For the mismatch, |a²−a_i²|≤Ca²ℓ/R=Cκ on the patch. For k≥1,

    |D^k(a²−a_i²)|≤C_k a²R^−k,
    (a²R^−k)/(κa^k)=(aR)^(1/2−k)≤1.              (4)

Leibniz therefore gives |D^νS|≤C_νκa^|ν|.

For the cross term ∇Φ·∇q, its cutoff contribution contains ∇Φ and ∇χ_i, so its base size is a/ℓ=κ. Derivatives of ∇Φ have size at most C_j aR^−j, and the other derivatives cost at most a; the same bound follows. For a plane-wave contribution, use the tangency coefficient

    h_i=a_i(a/R)(x·t_i).

Its zeroth-order size is at most Ca²ℓ/R=Cκ because x_i·t_i=0. For k≥1, the product rule uses the linearity of x·t_i and yields

    |D^kh_i|≤C_k a²[ℓR^(−k−1)+R^−k].

The first term is κR^−k and the second fits κa^k by (4). Hence all derivatives of ∇Φ·∇q are bounded by C_νκa^|ν|.

The exact remaining envelope coefficient is

    B=|∇Φ|²−a²−ΔΦ=−K²a²/R²−ΔΦ.

Its derivatives satisfy |D^kΔΦ|≤C_k aR^(−k−1)≤C_kκa^k. The origin term satisfies

    |D^k(K²a²/R²)|≤C_k K²a²R^(−k−2),
    (K²a²/R²)/κ=K²√a R^−3/2≤√K√logK.             (5)

The last inequality uses monotonicity of √logR/R^(3/2) for R≥K>e⁴. Derivatives add a factor R^−k≤a^k. Thus |D^kB|≤C_(k,K)κa^k, allowing the necessary finite K-dependent origin constant.

Finally,

    Δf=E[S−2∇Φ·∇q+Bq].

Apply Leibniz with (3) and all the preceding bounds to obtain, for every multiindex ν on the same fixed construction,

    |D^νΔf|≤C_(ν,K)κa^|ν|E.                        (6)

This proof covers arbitrarily high fixed orders; no cancellation is inferred just from smoothness.

## 3. Sparsity and harmonic repair require only order zero

The macro radius is slowly varying; packing gives a bounded-degree overlapping cover. The tangent spheres have positive dimension for n≥3, so greedy forbidden-cap avoidance is possible. In micro coordinates, finitely many frequencies a_i t_i/a(x) have nonzero bounded modulus and separated ± pairs. Frozen nonnegative partition coefficients sum to one. Their cosine family is compact and never identically zero, by exponential independence on a generic line. Its analytic zero sets have measure zero, yielding a uniform small-sublevel measure. The freezing error is O(ρ/(aℓ))≤Cρ η(K); it involves only first partition derivatives. This supplies Ω={|q|<τ} with the required micro sparsity for one sufficiently large K.

On Ω, the zeroth residual satisfies |Δf|≤CE[κ+τK²a²/R²]. Apply the sparse inverse with h₁,h₂ and comparison of signed forcing:

    |w|≤Cρ²E[η+τK²/R²], −Δw=Δf,
    w∈H¹_0(Ω).                                     (7)

Select θ for the two inverse weights, then ρ so Cρ² is small, then τ from compact-family sparsity, and finally K so Cρ²η(K)≤τ/40 and the cover/freezing requirements hold. Since K²/R²≤1, this yields |w|≤τE/20. Also, for the same fixed K,

    K²/R²≤(√K√logK)η,
    |W|≤C_KηE=C_Kκa^−2E,                            (8)

where W is the zero extension. No derivative estimate for w or W is needed.

## 4. Composition lemma and arbitrary kernel jets

Choose one nonnegative radial normalized ψ∈C∞_c(B(0,1)) and one sufficiently small σ>0 based on τ and the q-gradient bound. Set ε=σ/a, Y(x,z)=x−ε(x)z and F=G+H, with

    G=∫ψ(z)f(Y)dz, H=∫K_x(y)W(y)dy,
    K_x(y)=t(x)^nψ[t(x)(x−y)], t=a/σ.

For every j≥1, |D^jε|≤C_j εR^−j. Thus DY is bounded and |D^jY|≤C_j a^(j−1) for j≥2. A term of order m in the multivariate composition rule has r derivatives of its outer function and inner factors whose orders sum to m and number is r. Their product costs at most a^(m−r). Therefore:

- if outer jets are bounded by a^rE, composition jets cost at most a^mE;
- if outer jets are bounded by κa^rE, composition jets cost at most κa^mE;
- if the outer function is D^pf, composition jets cost at most a^(p+m)E.

All weights at Y are comparable to those at x since |Y−x|≤σ/a(x). These bounds are an explicit order count, not a suppressed smallness condition on K.

For kernel jets, put ζ=t(x)(x−y). On the support, |x−y|≤1/t. Since |D^jt|≤C_jtR^−j,

    |D^jζ|≤C_j[R^−j+tR^(1−j)]≤C_(j,σ)a^j,
    |D^jt^n|≤C_jt^nR^−j.

Apply product/composition rules to get

    |D_x^jK_x(y)|≤C_(j,σ)a^(n+j),
    supp_y D_x^jK_x⊂B(x,ε(x)).                      (9)

Differentiation under the integral is valid on compact x sets because W∈L¹_loc and the kernel supports/derivatives there are uniformly bounded. Using (8), (9), micro comparability and volume O(a^−n),

    |D^jH|≤C_(j,K,σ)κa^(j−2)E.                     (10)

In particular |D^mΔH|≤C_(m,K,σ)κa^mE and |D^mH|≤C_(m,K,σ)a^mE since η≤1. This step never differentiates W across ∂Ω.

## 5. Differentiated smoothing commutators retain κ

For G the exact identity, obtained by the chain rule before estimating, is

    ΔG=∫ψ(z)[Δf(Y)−2Σ_ij z_i ε_j f_ij(Y)
                    +|∇ε|²Σ_ij z_i z_j f_ij(Y)
                    −(Δε)z·∇f(Y)]dz.               (11)

The first term's arbitrary m-th derivatives fit C_(m,K)κa^mE by (6) and the composition lemma. For a term in which k of the m derivatives fall on a commutator coefficient, use the following exact size ledger:

| coefficient | its k-th jets | remaining composed f jets | resulting bound |
|---|---|---|---|
| ε_j | C_k a^−1 R^(−k−1) | C a^(m−k+2)E | C(a/R)a^m(aR)^−kE |
| \(|∇ε|²\) | C_k a^−2 R^(−k−2) | C a^(m−k+2)E | CR^−2a^m(aR)^−kE |
| Δε | C_k a^−1 R^(−k−2) | C a^(m−k+1)E | CR^−2a^m(aR)^−kE |

Every final entry is ≤Cκa^mE because a/R≤κ, R^−2≤κ, and (aR)^−k≤1. Summing the finitely many multiindex allocations proves

    |D^mΔG|≤C_(m,K,σ)κa^mE.

Separately, the composition lemma applied directly to f gives |D^mG|≤C_ma^mE. Combine with (10):

    |D^mF|≤C_(m,K,σ)a^mE,
    |D^mΔF|≤C_(m,K,σ)κa^mE.                        (12)

A crude |D^(m+2)G| estimate would only give a^(m+2)E and would lose κ. Equation (11) is the load-bearing correction, and it supplies the required small factor for every m.

## 6. Smooth gluing and quotient induction

The fixed σ guarantees |q(y)−q(x)|≤τ/8 on its averaging ball. On O={|q|<τ/2} the entire ball lies in Ω, where f+W is harmonic. Radial normalized averaging gives F=f+W throughout O and hence ΔF=0 there. Outside O the repaired function has fixed sign and magnitude at least (3/8−1/20)τE on the ball, so |F|≥cτE.

Define V=0 on O and V=ΔF/F on {F≠0}. These open sets cover space; the formulas coincide on their overlap and glue smoothly. For |ν|=m, differentiating VF=ΔF gives

    F D^νV = D^νΔF
       −Σ_(λ≤ν,λ≠ν) binom(ν,λ)(D^λV)(D^(ν−λ)F).

Induction and (12) bound every right-hand term by C_(ν,K,σ)κa^mE. Divide by |F|≥cτE outside O. Inside O all derivatives vanish. At its boundary the quotient is smooth because F≠0 locally; this same bound applies there. Hence |D^νV|≤C_(ν,K,σ)κa^m globally. Since the already fixed K,σ are finite, their dependencies can be absorbed in C_ν, yielding (1) after replacing R,logR by comparable 1+|x|,log(2+|x|) with K-dependent constants.

No new K is chosen at this induction stage. The assertion is **there exist one u,V,K,σ such that for every ν there exists C_ν**, not a different potential for each derivative order.

## 7. Exact decay distinction, including lower witnesses

The envelope satisfies Φ≥c rlog(2+r)−C_K for large r, giving (2). Thus for every fixed A>0, e^(Ar)|u(x)|→0 as |x|→∞.

There is also a lower-witness consequence of the construction. For each x, sparse Ω leaves a point y∈B(x,ρ/a(x)) with |q(y)|≥τ. The safe lower bound gives

    |u(y)|≥cτE(y)≥c'τE(x).                         (13)

Take x=re₁→∞. Then |y|=r+O(1/logr) and E(x)≥exp(−C_K rlogr) for r sufficiently large. Therefore (13) contradicts any global bound |u(y)|≤C_εexp(−c_ε|y|^(1+ε)) with fixed ε,c_ε>0. **This same solution actually does not admit any fixed superlinear-power decay bound**, a stronger distinction than merely observing that its upper estimate does not imply one.

Nontriviality follows immediately from (13). The conclusion discriminates exponential unique continuation under absolute bounded/all-derivative-vanishing regularity assumptions. It does not produce a counterexample to every fixed-power weak formulation, and it does not show symbol bounds with an inverse-radius gain per derivative, analyticity, a sign condition, or endpoint |V|=O(r^−1/2) without logarithmic loss.

## Verdict and limits

The all-order logarithmic extension passes independently, including the single-object quantifier, residual cancellation, arbitrary kernel jets, differentiated commutators and quotient boundary. It uses only the already audited sparse-domain mechanism and the n≥3 tangent geometry. Absolute bounded derivatives, even vanishing derivatives at every fixed order, do not alone restore the exponential conclusion within this construction class. Oscillation frequency a=logR still grows; this is not relative spatial slow variation.

The proof is universal in derivative order through the multivariate product/composition structure; no finite sample can verify that quantifier, and none was used to establish the verdict. Internal agreement with r15/r12 is not external validation. Prior art/novelty and potential analytic or relative-symbol alternatives remain unassessed here.
