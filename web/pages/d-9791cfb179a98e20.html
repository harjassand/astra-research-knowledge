GENTLE EXACT MARGINAL REPAIR — ROOT DERIVATION
Internal candidate; no external/formal certification or priority claim.
This derivation was sent to foundational_transfer_sol after that worker's
independent commuting/tracial filter argument; the noncommuting formula
below was not imported from its proof. It works also for singular states.

For ANY two density matrices nu,sigma on the same finite-dimensional space,
put delta=||nu-sigma||_1/2. There is ONE full-domain CPTP R, depending only
on these marginals, such that R(nu)=sigma and for EVERY reference A and
EVERY extension omega_AB with omega_B=nu,
 T((id_A tensor R)omega,omega)<=sqrt(2delta-delta^2)<=sqrt(2delta).
This is a weighted input-state conclusion, not uniform diamond proximity.

Let
 M=nu+(sigma-nu)_+=sigma+(nu-sigma)_+,
 K=sqrt(sigma) M^(-1/2),
where inverses are on support M. We have M>=nu,sigma, TrM=1+delta,
and K* K=M^(-1/2)sigma M^(-1/2)<=P_M<=I. Thus K is a contraction.
Also K M K*=sigma, since supp sigma is contained in supp M. It follows
that sigma-KnuK*=K(M-nu)K*>=0. Write
 q=Tr(sigma-KnuK*)=1-Tr KnuK*<=Tr(M-nu)=delta.
When q>0 let eta=(sigma-KnuK*)/q; when q=0 choose ANY density eta.
Set R(Y)=K Y K*+Tr[(I-K*K)Y]eta. This is CP and TP on all matrices,
and R(nu)=sigma in both q branches. No action on A occurs.

The squared disturbance of the success Kraus operator on a purification is
 epsilon=||(I-K)sqrt(nu)||_2^2
 =||(sqrt(M)-sqrt(sigma))M^(-1/2)sqrt(nu)||_2^2
 <=||sqrt(M)-sqrt(sigma)||_2^2
 <=Tr(M-sigma)=delta.
The first equality uses supp nu subset supp M. The first inequality uses
M^(-1/2)nu M^(-1/2)<=I. For the second, operator monotonicity gives
sqrt(M)>=sqrt(sigma), hence Tr sqrt(M)sqrt(sigma)>=Tr sigma.
Expanding epsilon therefore yields
 2 Re Tr(nu K)=1+Tr KnuK* - epsilon=2-q-epsilon>=2-2delta.
For a purification |v> of nu, the output's squared fidelity with |v> is
the sum over channel Kraus operators of their squared overlaps. Retaining
K alone gives at least |Tr nu K|^2>=(1-delta)^2. Half trace distance is
at most sqrt(1-(1-delta)^2). Any extension of nu is obtained by tracing
an additional purifying reference, so trace contraction proves the SAME
bound for every extension. The same R is used for all of them.

APPLICATION INTERFACE
If a local channel T nearly fixes sigma and a target rho_AB has B marginal
sigma, then nu=T sigma and R above give U=R T with U sigma=sigma.
If b=T((id tensor T)rho,rho), delta=T(nu,sigma)<=b, then
 T((id tensor U)rho,rho)<=b+sqrt(2delta-delta^2).
If T is self-compatible, applying R to both outputs of its supplied joint
channel makes U self-compatible. If sigma is tracial, U is bistochastic.
For nontracial sigma, stationarity does NOT imply bistochasticity or the
tracial entropy/Dirichlet identities. This lemma alone closes no such gap.
