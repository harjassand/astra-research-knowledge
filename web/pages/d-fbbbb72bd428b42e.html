## 1. Positive continuous-time Gibbs loops, with all factorials present

In the computational Z basis write H=A-sum_l c_l T_l, where

    A = sum_(Z checks) h_a D_a + (1/2)sum_(X checks) h_a I,
    (c_l,T_l)=(h_a/2,C_a) for X checks, and (eps_q,X_q) for fields.

A is diagonal; every T_l is a nonnegative permutation matrix. The exact
Duhamel expansion of the trace is a positive sum over closed paths:

    Z_H=sum_(n>=0) sum_(l_1,...,l_n)
        integral_(0<t_1<...<t_n<beta)
        (product_j c_(l_j))
        sum_(z_0:z_n=z_0) exp[-sum_(j=0)^n (t_(j+1)-t_j) A(z_j)] dt,
    z_j=T_(l_j)z_(j-1), t_0=0,t_(n+1)=beta.                    (2)

The time-simplex volume is beta^n/n!, so no missing factorial or free Poisson
assumption is hidden in (2). Finite-dimensional bounded matrices give absolute
convergence at every finite beta,N. Initial-state restriction by a diagonal
Z-syndrome event inserts its indicator into the same positive measure.
Equivalently one can expand ONLY field insertions against exp(-sH0): H0 is
not diagonal, but its kernels are entrywise nonnegative and preserve every
Z-check sign. The uncounted X-check transitions inside them therefore cause
no touched-check charge. Equation(2) expands these kernels further and counts
only the tagged fields. Checks and fields remain separately TAGGED even if their permutations happen
to agree; tilting counts only field tags, not all off-diagonal jumps.

X-check jumps commute with every Z check by CSS orthogonality. Therefore only
field tags can change a Z-check syndrome along a path.

## 2. Exact translation by a legal Z-check eraser

Fix a legal Z-check witness tau of t=|tau|>0 checks. Legality means there exists
a pure-X Pauli R_tau whose Z-check syndrome is EXACTLY tau. Since the syndrome
map is linear, choose this eraser ONCE for the fixed tau, independently of
the ambient starting syndrome sigma. Its qubit support
may be much larger than t. This causes NO support charge in the path map:
R_tau is itself a permutation and commutes with every T_l in (2), including
all the fields. Translate every z_j by R_tau, keeping ALL tags and times.
It is a bijection on closed paths, preserving every jump amplitude, every
time-simplex factor, and the number of field events. It flips only the Z
checks in tau and preserves every other diagonal check energy.

Let U_tau be the union of the supports of the tau checks; |U_tau|<=q_c t.
Let r(path) be the number of DISTINCT tau checks touched by at least one
field event; touching a check means its qubit is in that check support.
Let N_U count field events with site in U_tau. Each event touches at most z_c
checks, so r<=z_c N_U. Count actual check incidence, not the long R_tau support.

Suppose the path starts with every tau check excited. An untouched check stays
excited at every time, because all X-check jumps and all other field events
preserve its sign. Its erasure lowers A by h_a. An affected check can cost at
most h_a after translation. At every point of the path,

    A(z)-A(R_tau z) >= h_min(t-r)-h_max r.

Define

    a=h_min/[2(h_min+h_max)],  gamma=a/z_c.                     (3)

If r<=a t, this energy gain is at least h_min t/2, uniformly for the WHOLE
imaginary-time interval. Hence the initial-tau-excited, few-touched path mass,
by the positive injective translation and division by the EXACT new Z_H, is
at most exp(-beta h_min t/2). The image need not be a good decoder syndrome;
its total weight is bounded by the full partition function. No pointwise
Gibbs comparison with H0, and no factor exp(beta N eps), is made.

If r>a t then N_U>gamma t. This complementary part is bounded in the
UNCONDITIONED new Gibbs loop measure in the next section. Thus far,

    Pr_H[tau excited] <= exp(-beta h_min t/2)

## 4. Opposite X-check witnesses: unconditional positivity lemma

All X checks commute with the FULL H in (1). For ANY fixed subset tau of t
X checks, put H_tau=sum_(a in tau)h_a D_a and H_rest=H-H_tau. Then
[H_tau,H_rest]=0, and H_rest is stoquastic in the Z basis, so
T=exp(-beta H_rest) has entrywise nonnegative real matrix entries.
Let P_+=product_(a in tau)(I+C_a)/2 and P_-=product_(a in tau)(I-C_a)/2.
Check dependencies are allowed; an inconsistent all-minus event has P_-=0.
Every product C_R of these unsigned X checks is a NONNEGATIVE permutation.
Thus

    P_+-P_-=2^(1-t)sum_(R subset tau, |R| odd) C_R,
    Tr(P_+ T)>=Tr(P_- T).                                    (10)

This compares UNCONDITIONED marginal traces while integrating every other
syndrome. It does NOT assert false monotonicity for a pointwise partial
erasure sigma -> sigma\tau or after arbitrary other-sector conditioning.
On P_- the removed energy is sum_tau h_a, and on P_+ it is0. Positivity of
all remaining trace blocks gives

    Pr_H[all tau X checks excited]
      =exp(-beta sum_tau h_a) Tr(P_- T)/Z_H
      <=exp(-beta sum_tau h_a)
      <=exp(-beta h_min t).                                  (11)

Indeed Z_H>=Tr(P_+ T)>=Tr(P_- T). Legality of tau is unnecessary for (11).
Unsigned X-check signs and nonnegative field coefficients are essential;
positivity of a Gibbs operator as an operator is not entrywise positivity.

## 7. Why this is not yet a memory theorem, and exact failure control

For nonzero fields P1 and the old dressed logical pair generally DO NOT
commute with H. Therefore Chesi's full-H logical-factor criterion cannot be
applied to(13). Small Born bad mass and bare local logical protection alone
do not control the actual full-frequency Davies components. The bath is not
replaced by a convenient local sampler, and rates/dynamics are not supplied
by an equilibrium path representation. A compatible dressed projector/logical
algebra or a quantitatively controlled Gibbs-weighted dynamics remains the
load-bearing missing step. No exponential-time microscopic approximation is
inferred. Preparation of rho_H or a logical quantum thermal factor is also
unexecuted and not free.

A beta-only extension of classical Peierls is false at fixed field strength.
For the actual interior RX edge shared by two Z plaquettes, transverse X_e
creates a two-check virtual pair. The two-state Hamiltonian in the even block

This block witnesses failure of the naive Boltzmann replacement, not of a
full memory theorem or a large decoder witness. Its existence/only-two-Z-
plaquette incidence was separately verified from the literal template in
cycle14. More globally, with every eps_q>0, the computational-Z Hamiltonian
is irreducibly stoquastic; its unique finite-N ground vector has strictly
positive amplitudes. Every nonempty diagonal Z-syndrome event has positive

vanishes solely with beta at fixed eps. Conditions(14)-(16) correctly make
the admitted field shrink as beta increases.

A sharper exact-factor obstruction, first communicated by pde_gate10 and
independently checked here, follows from that SAME positivity. When every
eps_q>0, the one-site flip graph connects all computational basis states, so
the finite-N ground space is one-dimensional by Perron--Frobenius. If two

their restrictions would give a two-dimensional Pauli representation in
EVERY eigenspace, which is impossible in this unique ground space. Thus an
exact full-H conserved logical pair and exact thermal factor of the sparse
