# Failures, corrections, and restart gates

## Corrections made during this pass

1. **Sand equation transcription corrected.** An earlier internal note incorrectly placed the diffusivity in the numerator. The primary Ji SI, Eq. (1), rendered from PDF page 3, says \(\tau^{-1/2}=2i/[\sqrt{\pi D_{eff}}c_0F]\). Combining with \(Q_{max}=i\tau\) yields \(VSC=i^2\tau=\pi D_{eff}c_0^2F^2/4\). This is internally consistent; the mistake was ours, not the paper.
2. **Do not overstate Sand fit agreement.** Ji's published Fig. 2f workbook labels unconstrained affine fits. For LiMgLa the reconstructed eight-point affine fit is \(0.00845084j+0.00117725\) (\(R^2=0.9763\)); the strict Sand model has zero intercept. Constraining through the origin gives a different slope (0.00927137) and inverse-square VSC (1.163×10^6 SI), compared with the table's 1.46×10^6. The free-slope inverse square (1.400×10^6) is closer but is not an exact Sand-law identity when the intercept is nonzero. Keep the fit choice/provenance ambiguity open; no source-equation defect is established.
3. **Corrected Fig. 4f model values.** For the imposed 1 mA cm−2 LiMgLa phase-field run, the publisher data give effective current 1.3347 mA cm−2 at 1.10 mAh cm−2, 2.401 at 1.50, 3.6698 at 1.9389, and 28.135 at 2.10. Earlier hand-read capacities were offset. These are model outputs only.
4. **70% scope.** The abstract does explicitly report the >70%-capacity prediction, but says it is shown by phase-field modelling; do not convert it into a universal measured threshold. Supplementary Note 8 changes the cycling VSC under rough contact and fatigue/SEI assumptions.

## Routes rejected or preempted

- **Generic current constriction as a new discovery:** refuted as a novelty route. Void-edge/current-hotspot models go back at least to Narayan–Anand 2022; later geometry/roughness studies and Rasool–Shin 2026 treat amplification directly. The conditional \(\kappa^2\bar jQ\) inequality is a useful design screen in a restricted model, not a priority claim.
- **Treating VSC as an independent measurement:** rejected. Under Sand's law it is an algebraic reparameterization of \(D_{eff}c_0^2\) or of the reciprocal-square fitted slope, not a second independent observable. VSC can still organize a protocol, but must be validated against local current, contact, and mechanical state.
- **Reading the Fig. 4f curve as measured current:** rejected. Eq. (15) constructs effective local current by renormalizing the applied current with modeled contact area. No experimental current map is in the source data. The normalization becomes singular as active area vanishes.
- **Claiming a universal 70% threshold failure:** rejected. The source claim is model-scoped and source-specific. No contradictory primary measurement has been established here.
- **Transferring Vema's oxide-particle mechanism directly to LiMgLa:** rejected. The source material/electrolyte, particle chemistry, pressure protocols and cycling histories differ. Ji's EDX maps identify Mg/La, not conductivity or particle transport. This remains the central experiment, not a conclusion.
- **Treating contact area alone as sufficient state:** rejected. Ji's source model, Vema's patchy deposits, Zhao's operando results, and Rasool–Shin 2026 all implicate geometry/history/pressure/kinetics. A usable \(\kappa\) must bound local flux over the protocol; impedance alone does not supply it.

## Restart gates

Resume this lead only if at least one gate is met:

1. **Source parameter gate:** obtain author/source clarification or independently reconstruct the exact effective-diffusivity estimator, including affine-intercept handling, for Fig. 2f and Table 3. Run alternative regressions with uncertainty from the raw data and report whether the difference is material at operating points.
2. **Inclusion observability gate:** acquire quantitative particle-size, volume-fraction, connectivity/coverage, and conductivity data for Mg/La-rich phases before/after partial and full stripping in LiMgLa|LLZTO. Element maps alone do not identify an insulating layer.
3. **Matched physical discrimination gate:** same LLZTO, temperature, stack pressure, geometry, and matched initial interface impedance; compare pure Li, LiMg, and LiMgLa with local-current/contact maps, three-electrode voltage/EIS during strip-rest-restart, and blinded postmortem cryo-PFIB/SEM/EDS. Preregister failure criterion and replicate plan.
4. **Flux acquisition gate:** produce a calibrated spatial upper bound \(\kappa(t)=\sup_x j(x,t)/\bar j\) in the actual test cell. If no measurement or validated inference can bound peak local flux, the theorem cannot supply an engineering controller.
5. **Model-transfer gate:** independently acquire \(D,c_0\), pressure-sensitive creep parameters, particle parameters, and initial interface resistance on the same material/protocol. A model fit to pure Li/LLZO is not permission to reuse its parameters for LiMgLa/LLZTO.

If gates 2–5 fail, retain the result as a conditional design envelope and stop describing it as a materials advance. No physical cells, specialist review, independently run COMSOL model, or source-author correspondence were available in this pass.
