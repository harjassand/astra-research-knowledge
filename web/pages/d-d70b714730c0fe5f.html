## Selected-beta moments on every path

Put a=delta_acq/8. At every k>=kstar, the acquisition's calibration failure costs at most delta_k and its conditional good-calibration continuation costs at most delta_k^(2b/beta_k)<=delta_k. This is uniform conditional on arbitrary preceding history. Consequently, by conditional iteration,

    Pr(reach kstar+j)
       <=product_(ell=0)^(j-1) a/(kstar+ell+1)^2
       <=a^j/(j!)^2, j>=1.                    (TAIL)

Stage independence is not assumed. Every incorrect late calibration/threshold outcome is represented in this unconditional bound. Almost sure termination follows. Since K>=kstar+j implies reaching that stage, the same tail bounds K. The additional old final point block occurs after K and does not change the selected-stage tail.

By definition of kstar, b/4<beta_star=beta_kstar<=b/2, including kstar=0 when b=1. For every r>0,

    E beta_K^-r
      <= beta_star^-r [1+sum_(j>=1) 2^(rj) Pr(K=kstar+j)]
      <= (4/b)^r sum_(j>=0) (a 2^r)^j/(j!)^2
      <= (4/b)^r exp(a 2^r).                 (MOMENT)

The first term bounds all K<=kstar paths, not just correctly selected brackets. The final inequality uses1/(j!)^2<=1/j!. Thus rare bad selections with arbitrarily small beta have finite, uniformly controlled p-th and first inverse moments. Conditioning this calculation on A would omit precisely the paths relevant to expected cost.
