Manuscript evidence (line numbers refer to ../.. / OA113.txt):

1. Lemma 5.1's potentially delicate inference in its exterior-not-good case is justified by the definition of S_v. S_v consists of the labels of the TWO ORIGINAL CYCLE EDGES incident to v (807-810), rather than the whole bag-membership set. If the exterior edge at v_0 cannot carry P but P belongs to S_v0, the first arc edge must carry P (905-908). This distinction removes an apparent but invalid objection.

2. The case split at 890-925 preserves odd lengths, strict split indices, admissible endpoints, and the required good opposite pair. The recursion uses original-cycle complementary arcs throughout (926-933). It therefore does not silently replace the relevant label sets or through patterns with diagonals created earlier.

3. The signed cancellation is deliberately NOT cancellation to zero: complementary arcs of a shared diagonal have the same converted matching, so their signed gains sum to D_C (1132-1138). Number of cells minus number of interior diagonals is one; equation (6.10) has the resulting sign consistent with (6.9).

Lemma 6.4's inverse recovers the original multiset union using bounded added/dropped lists, recovers the active cycle from a tagged corner, and recovers its layers with one orientation bit; outside this cycle layers were unchanged (1192-1197). Given the stated deterministic quadrangulation, the four corners identify the cell. I found no missing unbounded context information in that inverse. The tag count at 1198-1202 is polynomial.

5. The rare-guide issue is addressed, rather than estimated away: (6.12) retains p_X; (6.13)-(6.14) use conditional Jensen with 1/p_X; multiplication cancels it exactly (1244-1295). A lower bound on the probability of containing C_X is not needed. The coefficient 4 comes from two contexts, each with the factor 2 from the squared-difference bound.

6. In Lemma 6.5, T_X and C_X each match precisely the same vertices, so their union is a whole discrepancy component, with no external matching edges attached (1297-1302). For a fixed component, closing-chord choice and path direction determine X, giving the stated <=2N multiplicity (1303-1315).
