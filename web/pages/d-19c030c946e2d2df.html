1. WEIGHTED KADISON BLOCK PROOF

The orientations of the two Hilbert-Schmidt weight operators are correct:
  <A,R A>_HS=Tr(sigma A* A),
  <A,L A>_HS=Tr(sigma A A*).
Applying unital CP Schwarz to A* A and A A*, respectively, then using
stationarity, gives precisely T^dagger R T<=R and T^dagger L T<=L.
The commuting positive L,R make [[L,G],[G,R]] PSD for G=sqrt(LR).
Congruence by diag(T,T) yields off-diagonal M=T^dagger G T, which is
positive as a Hilbert-space operator, even when T is singular. Increasing
the two diagonal blocks to L,R is a legitimate PSD-order operation.

The Schur complement gives M L^(-1) M<=R. Congruence by L^(-1/2) gives
X^2<=D with X>=0 and D>0 exactly as stated. Positive square-root
monotonicity implies X=sqrt(X^2)<=sqrt(D), without assuming X,D commute.
The final equality L^(1/2)sqrt(D)L^(1/2)=G uses only the verified
commutation of L,R. The integral proof of square-root monotonicity has
the correct scalar normalization and inverse-order direction. Thus this
is a valid independent proof of full complex KMS contractivity.

It is distinct from the origin proof's CP-unital KMS-adjoint argument:
U^sharp U is a positive KMS self-adjoint UCP map, whose eigenvalues are
bounded by its ordinary operator-norm contraction. Both proofs give the
same norm and need no minimum-eigenvalue comparison coefficient.

2. CORRELATION MAP, CHOI ORDER AND TRANSPOSE

The expression Tr_2[S(I tensor C)] is Hermitian for Hermitian C because
partial trace is cyclic in the traced register. Sandwiching by the
inverse square root of sigma gives Gamma with the correct KMS pairing
  <A,Gamma C>_sigma=Tr S(A tensor C).
Gamma(I)=I follows from the first marginal, and its sigma-invariant
trace functional follows from the second marginal.

For the chosen sigma eigenbasis, the Choi blocks of
C -> Tr_2[S(I tensor C^t)] are exactly the B-index blocks of S, hence
its Choi matrix is the register swap of S. It is CP. The subsequent
sigma^(-1/2) output sandwich is CP; transpose fixes sigma and preserves
its trace functional in this basis. Thus Gamma composed with transpose
is a genuine sigma-stationary UCP map. Transpose is a KMS isometry.
This proves Gamma contracts the KMS norm, and swap symmetry makes it
KMS self-adjoint, so -I<=Gamma<=I is valid. CP of Gamma itself is never
assumed. No Gamma/H commutation is used.

The origin proof instead normalizes S on the first Choi register to
obtain an auxiliary stationary Schrodinger channel. The two approaches
give the same covariance bound with the transpose on the corresponding
dual register. Neither replaces the KMS metric by the Jordan metric.
