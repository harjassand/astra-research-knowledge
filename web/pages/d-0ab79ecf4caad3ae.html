1. Exact legal star/anchor dynamics and faithful arrow states

Use a center c, an isolated anchor a, and m leaves i. Choose r_i>0 and
 Z=2+sum_i r_i^2,
 s_raw=diag(1,1,r_i), s=s_raw/sqrt(Z), sigma=s^2,
 B=sum_i sqrt(r_i)(E_(c,i)+E_(i,c)).
The unnormalized Lyapunov convention is Lyap_s(X)=sX+Xs. Its legal
weighted generator is H_B(X)={V_B,X}/2-BXB, with
 (V_B)_(c,c)=sum_i r_i^2,
 (V_B)_(i,j)=2sqrt(r_i r_j)/(r_i+r_j),
 all anchor entries and center-leaf entries zero.
H_B is HS self-adjoint, fixes s, and transports its CP semigroup to
sigma-preserving KMS dynamics. Overall root normalization cancels in V_B.

The actual positive Schrodinger generator is
 L_*(rho)=(C rho+rho C^T)/2-K rho K^T,
 K_(c,i)=1,K_(i,c)=r_i,
 C_(c,c)=sum_i r_i^2,C_(i,j)=2r_i/(r_i+r_j),
 with all anchor and remaining entries zero. These entries are rational
whenever r_i are rational. The nonsymmetric drift C is retained:
 (C+C^T)/2=K^T K; its skew part gives the physical Hamiltonian correction.
Replacing C by the symmetric weighted drift V_B is not allowed.

Choose any real u_i with sum_i u_i^2/r_i^2<1. Define the raw arrow matrix
 R=1 on the center, and on anchor/leaves
 R_A=[[1,u^T],[u,diag(r_i^2)]],
and rho=R/Z. This is a faithful trace-one state by a scalar Schur
complement. The entire path rho(alpha) with u replaced by alpha u is
faithful for alpha^2 sum u_i^2/r_i^2<1. Its diagonal agrees exactly
with sigma. These are actual nonlinear states, not Hessian surrogates.

2. Exact genuine nonlinear J and E by arrow resolvents

Set D=diag(r_i^2), b_i=sqrt(r_i), and for t>=0 define
 a_t=1+t-sum_i u_i^2/(r_i^2+t)>0,
 F_t=sum_i sqrt(r_i)u_i/(r_i^2+t),
 G_t=sum_i r_i u_i/(r_i^2+t),
 H_t=sum_i u_i/(r_i^2+t).
The Schur inverse of R_A+tI has cross block
 [(R_A+tI)^(-1)]_(leaf,anchor)=-(D+tI)^(-1)u/a_t,
and its leaf compression differs from (D+tI)^(-1) by
 (D+tI)^(-1)u u^T(D+tI)^(-1)/a_t.
The usual log and square-root resolvent identities consequently give
 (log R_A)_(i,a)=u_i integral_0^infinity dt/[(r_i^2+t)a_t],
 b^T(sqrt(D)-(sqrt(R_A))_(leaf,leaf))b
   =(1/pi)integral_0^infinity sqrt(t)F_t^2/a_t dt.

The actual L_*(rho) has ONLY anchor-leaf cross blocks: its leaf-anchor
column is Cu/(2Z), with C here restricted to leaves. All diagonal,
center and leaf-leaf contributions cancel against the stationary sigma.
Thus the genuine entropy production J=Tr[L_*(rho)(logrho-logsigma)] and
the original root energy E=Tr[sqrt(rho)H_B(sqrt(rho))] are EXACTLY
 J=(1/Z)integral_0^infinity (G_t^2+t H_t^2)/a_t dt,
 E=(2/(pi Z))integral_0^infinity sqrt(t)F_t^2/a_t dt.
The first identity uses the algebraic cancellation
 [r_i/(r_i^2+t)+r_j/(r_j^2+t)]/(r_i+r_j)
    =(r_i r_j+t)/[(r_i^2+t)(r_j^2+t)].
For E, the original trace form is
 E=(2/Z)[sum_i r_i^2-b^T(sqrt(R_A))_(leaf,leaf)b],
because the center root equals1/sqrt(Z) and B has zero anchor row/column.
All factors refer to physical rho and sigma, and the Hamiltonian part
has not been dropped. The integrals converge for every faithful arrow
state by their finite-dimensional resolvent representations.

This reduction does not give a pointwise comparison: at fixed t and
three or more distinct r_i, a real u can annihilate G_t and H_t while
leaving F_t nonzero. The denominator a_t also depends quadratically on
u. Stationary weight a_t=1+t therefore cannot simply be reused away
from stationarity. A uniform weighted inequality remains the live gate.

3. Controlled finite-difference family at finite relative coherence

For an integer n>=1 and 0<h<1/n, set m=n+1,
 t_j=jh, r_j=(1+t_j)/(1-t_j), j=0,...,n,
 L_n=binom(2n,n),
 v_j=(-1)^(n-j)binom(n,j)/sqrt(L_n),
 u_j=(alpha/2)(r_j+1)v_j=alpha v_j/(1-t_j), 0<|alpha|<1.
The vector v has Euclidean norm1. Since r_j>=1,
 sum_j u_j^2/r_j^2
  =(alpha^2/4)sum_j [(r_j+1)/r_j]^2 v_j^2<=alpha^2<1.
Hence this complete nonlinear path is faithful for every |alpha|<1,
without an h-dependent positivity interval. For t_j<=1/2, sigma's
condition number is at most9. The earlier huge forward-difference
coefficients have been normalized: physical v is bounded, while the
energy can be extremely small, of order h^(2n).

At fixed n,alpha as h tends to0, put
 k_common=alpha n! h^n/sqrt(L_n).
Nth finite differences give the leading functions from the coefficients
of z^n in
 g(z;t)=(1+z)/[(1+z)^2+t(1-z)^2],
 hfun(z;t)=(1-z)/[(1+z)^2+t(1-z)^2],
 f(z;t)=sqrt(1-z^2)/[(1+z)^2+t(1-z)^2].
The leading denominator is
 a_t -> [(1+t)^2-alpha^2]/(1+t).
Let theta in(0,pi), t=cot^2(theta/2), and b_k be the coefficients of
b(z)=sqrt(1-z^2). Define
 S_n(theta)=sum_(k=0)^n b_k sin((n-k+1)theta).
The Chebyshev generating formula yields the coefficient identity
 g_n(t)^2+t h_n(t)^2=1/(1+t).
Consequently the controlled leading ratio is
 lim_(h->0) J/E
  = [atanh(alpha)/alpha]
      /[(1/(2pi))integral_0^pi
          S_n(theta)^2/[1-alpha^2 sin^4(theta/2)] dtheta].
The common factor k_common^2/Z cancels. At alpha tending to0 this
reduces to4/(sum_(k<=n)|b_k|^2), the stationary sharpness ratios.
This limit is derived from the exact nonlinear arrow integrals; it is
not a nonlinear inference from the stationary Hessian theorem.

For fixed |alpha|<1, as n tends to infinity the truncated b series
converges in H^2, and the bounded angular weight permits L^2 passage.
The oscillating boundary square has weak limit sin(theta), since
|b(e^(-i theta))|^2=2sin(theta) on(0,pi). Therefore the denominator tends
to atanh(alpha)/(pi alpha), and the two-stage limiting ratio is pi.
This is an obstruction to obtaining a ratio below pi merely by this
large-n/coalescing-node limit at fixed relative coherence. It is NOT a
uniform finite-h or finite-n lower bound, nor does it exclude another
nonlinear star path. No finite negative computation has been performed.
