## Exact question and regime

Acquire distinct rational unit vectors on S² whose equal-weight nonconstant harmonic mean through degree T has norm at most beta=1/[2048(T+1)^5], with empirical normalized harmonic Gram matrix greater than I/2. Input and output are entirely charged, including certified initialization, corrections, reset precision and final verification.

Frozen cases: (T,q,N)=(2,3,18),(3,4,32), with the same equal-area ring/wedge centroid initialization as the universal proof. N=2q². Both are outside q=600(T+1), which would require respectively 6,480,000 and 11,520,000 nodes. The stopping rule was set before execution: at least three corrections, at most five, stop after the minimum when exact mean<=beta/8 and exact Gram>I/2. Each case has a 180-second limit; each numerical LP proposal has a 15-second limit and at most two exact active reconstructions plus one feasible repair. No node-count or initialization optimization was performed after observing outcomes.

## LP proposals, exact reconstruction and acceptance

The rational harmonic basis has M=T(T+2) functions and exact diagonal squared norms nu. The original action equation is Jw=b, where J contains the unnormalized sum derivatives and b is minus the current sum of harmonic values; dividing both sides by N gives the average formulation. For each node, exact tangent projection preserves Jw, followed by the rational Cayley retraction and distinct reset.

SciPy/HiGHS proposes the coordinate-box action minimizing d subject to Jw=b, |w_a|<=d, 0<=d<=1. Its equations are whitened and the residual is scaled numerically only to keep small late residuals away from solver tolerance. Every raw proposal is retained as exact float.hex data and its binary-rational original equality/bound residuals are exported. A numerical success string is never acceptance.

For guessed active signs s_a, an exact square system solves the free coordinates and d. The original rational equations and every box bound are then verified. Exact optimality is separately certified by lambda and mu_a>=0 satisfying J^t lambda+s mu=0, sum mu=1 and d=-lambda dot b. Weak duality supplies the lower bound, so a feasible primal with the same value is a true box optimum. A dual-feasible but primal-infeasible candidate is rejected.

If neither bounded active guess gives a feasible optimal vertex, a rounded proposal is repaired on a numerically selected column block whose invertibility is checked by an exact solve. It cancels the full original equality residual exactly; d is recomputed as the maximum absolute coordinate and every bound is verified. This is explicitly an executed feasible-correction variant, not an optimum claim or a substitute for the theorem's polynomial-time exact LP primitive.

T=2 step0 retained a singular active-system attempt and a dual-feasible/primal-infeasible candidate, then accepted the exact feasible repair.
