## Interface and derivation

Let P(Y,zeta) be a real smooth family on one fixed fast torus T^d with (Delta_zeta+1)P=0. The slow coordinate is Y=(S,Y_t), d<=n-1. Each cell collar {|P|<tau} has the fixed gap >1 from `MULTIWAVE_RESPONSE_IDENTITY.md`. Write

    f_0=exp(-A s)b_0P(Y,zeta),
    eta=1/(A ell), kappa=A^2eta.

The exact conjugated Cartesian operator of the actual core calculation gives the principal source

    Q_Y=2beta S P-2partial_S P
          -2 sum_i Y_i partial_(zeta_i)P
          +2 sum_i partial_(Y_i)partial_(zeta_i)P.      (1)

The unsmoothed repaired principal potential is -A_(Q_Y)/P, with every zero-extension defect included. Define

    rho_tau(p)=p/tau^2 for |p|<tau, 1/p otherwise,
    m(Y)=mean[min(P^2/tau^2,1)],
    J_tau(Y)=mean[j_tau(P)], j_tau'=rho_tau.

The exact Green identity gives mean[-A_Q/P]=mean[rho_tau(P)Q]. The direct translation term in (1) has zero mean, since mean[rho_tau(P)partial_zeta P]=mean[partial_zeta j_tau(P)]=0. Integrating the last term by parts on the torus yields

    bar V_1^(0)(Y)
      =2beta S m(Y)-2partial_S J_tau(Y)
           -2 sum_i mean[rho_tau'(P)
                             partial_(Y_i)P partial_(zeta_i)P]. (2)

Here rho_tau' is 1/tau^2 inside and -1/P^2 outside. It is the bounded weak derivative of the continuous Lipschitz function rho_tau; there is no hidden delta at |P|=tau. Equation (2) retains a common-frequency term, a radial shape derivative, and a tangential shape/phase term. The last two have no fixed sign. Different component frequencies and nonradial partition derivatives cannot simply be assigned to the common-frequency channel.

For a packet depending only on S, (2) reduces to

    bar V_1^(0)=2beta S m-2partial_S J_tau.              (3)

A collective fast translation P(S,zeta)=P_0(zeta+phi(S)) keeps J_tau constant, and hence does not compensate the common-frequency term in this unsmoothed mean. Independent phase changes of a packet with more frequencies than its fast dimension can alter its shape; (3) does not identify them with collective translation.

## A limited closed-loop obstruction

Suppose S_1<S_2<0, beta>0 is the frozen local radial parameter, and P(S_2,zeta) is a fast translate of P(S_1,zeta). If the sparse fraction is at most theta<1, then m>=1-theta and J_tau(S_2)=J_tau(S_1). Integrating (3) gives

    integral_(S_1)^(S_2) bar V_1^(0) dS
        =2beta integral_(S_1)^(S_2) S m(S)dS
        <=beta(1-theta)(S_2^2-S_1^2)<0.                (4)

Thus a complete radial mixture loop inside an inward macro interval cannot have nonnegative unsmoothed principal mean everywhere. This is a local averaged obstruction, not a universal index theorem.
