## Exact interface and scientific use

Inputs are an explicit Boolean formula in polynomial equalities and strict inequalities, with integer polynomials represented by acyclic arithmetic circuits allowing sharing, signed binary constants and +,−,× gates. There are no division or exponentiation gates. Explicit variable lists define the two blocks ∃x∈R^r ∀y∈R^s. The output is the exact truth value.

The authors claim membership in one absolute fixed counting-hierarchy level, independent of dimensions, circuit degrees and coefficient magnitudes; the existential fragment is placed in C26P. This is a discrete uniform bit/oracle complexity statement, not a polynomial-time optimizer, a numerical sampling theorem or a rational-witness output bound.

A legitimate downstream use is exact robust polynomial specification: ∃design ∀uncertainty. The universal block is acquired through a decision reduction without compactness of the original uncertainty domain. A short oracle-level root label does not make a feasible design affordable or rational.

## Reconstructed mechanism and its costs

Introduce gate and auxiliary variables to represent failure of the formula as a zero of a nonnegative quartic F(x,z). For fixed x, write

    m_x(u)=min_z [Σ_i z_i^6+6uF(x,z)], u≥0.

Coercivity gives attained minima. If F(x,z0)=0, m_x(u)≤Σz0_i^6. Conversely a uniformly bounded sequence at u→∞ gives bounded minimizers; a convergent subsequence has F=0. Thus m_x diverges exactly when the fiber has no real zero (“good x”).

The critical equations are f_i=z_i^5+u ∂F/∂z_i, with remaining terms of degree<5 in z. Over any coefficient ring the quotient is free on z^α,0≤α_i<5, dimension D=5^n. This remains true after parameter specialization and for nonreduced algebras. Multiplication by h=Σz_i^6+6uF has characteristic polynomial Q(x,u,T), obtained implicitly.

The source's residue/trace identity computes tr(M_v) from a finite coefficient sum involving the Jacobian and powers of the lower-degree remainders. Newton identities modulo suitable primes turn these traces into characteristic coefficients. The dimension and degree can be exponential while the requested evaluations/signs live at fixed counting levels. No exponentially large multiplication matrix is materialized by the theoretical oracle procedure; a polynomial ordinary-time algorithm is not implied.

Set E(x,v)=Q(x,v^{D+1},v). Its coefficient E_D is1. Growth estimates distinguish bounded critical minima from diverging m_x. On a fixed actual-degree piece, the leading coefficient is locally bounded away from0; root bounds then prove that good and bad sets are both relatively closed. This is a clopen statement, not an assertion that goodness is constant on the entire degree piece.

For degree d, the polynomial

    W_d(x,t)=(E_d(x)t−1)^2+Σ_{j>d}E_j(x)^2

has a real zero set homeomorphic to that degree piece. The coercive penalized minimum on each closed lifted part produces parameter coordinates among roots of selected top-parameter coefficients of a characteristic polynomial (Proposition6.3). Short derivative/sign-fingerprint labels distinguish prescribed real roots without constructing all coefficient lists. Their verification still invokes the fixed-level counting machinery; the polynomial-length label is not a free proposer.

Two existential tests decide whether a selected finite description S(x) is nonempty and whether it contains a counterexample F(x,z)=0. Completeness uses a suitable nonzero coefficient and isolating labels; soundness checks every guessed label rather than trusting a geometric branch. Thus the candidate branch is acquired and checked at the decision-oracle level.

## Original-definition executable checks

work/metric_use10/cycle19/check_real_fiber_mechanism.py directly builds the degree5 quotient and multiplication matrix for n1, verifies its characteristic polynomial equals the exact resultant, and reconstructs trace values for powers1–5 from the finite residue formula. Both symbolic parameter families pass, including nonreduced specializations.

* F=(xz−1)^2: E has degree37 in v at nonzero x, degree30 at x0. At x0, Q=(T−6u)^5, showing that no radical quotient is assumed. Analytically the only good parameter is x0.
* F=(z²−x)^2: x−1 and x1 both have E degree44, but only the negative parameter is good. At x0, Q=T³(T−32u³)^2, also nonreduced. The degree piece therefore cannot be treated as a single goodness label; its disconnected components matter.
