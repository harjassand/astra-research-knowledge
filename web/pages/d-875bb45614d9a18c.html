## 2. Exact discontinuity, with smooth bounded KMS rates

Take the physical coupling Q and fixed rate

    gamma(omega)=e^(-omega²)/(1+e^(-beta omega)).

This is smooth, positive, bounded by1, and obeys KMS. It is
Schwartz on the real line, so its Fourier autocorrelation is
integrable. The usual nonnegative bosonic spectral density on
omega>0 may be chosen as gamma(omega)(1−e^(-beta omega)).
No jump in the bath rate is responsible for the phenomenon.

For H_delta=H0+delta S1, the block Hamiltonian is E_B I+delta Z.
At delta=0 its Q spectral component is X at zero frequency.
At each delta!=0 the components are the distinct raising and
lowering matrices at frequencies ±2delta. For F=X/2 embedded
as zero off B, ||F||_1=1 and direct multiplication gives

    D[X](F)=0,  D[sigma_+](F)=D[sigma_-](F)=−F/2.

The block is reducing for the global spectral projectors and
physical Q. Degeneracies elsewhere cannot restore a missing
cross term on this block. Since
gamma(2delta)+gamma(−2delta)=e^(-4delta²),

    D_H0(F)=0, D_Hdelta(F)=−e^(-4delta²)F/2,
    ||D_Hdelta−D_H0||_(1->1)>=e^(-4delta²)/2→1/2.    (1)

Now U=e^(i epsilon Q), delta=epsilon and
H_epsilon=U(H0+epsilon S1)U*. Only S1,S2 rotate, so
||H_epsilon−H0||<=5epsilon. Their qubit support union lies
within physical distance2 of the edge qubit, using actual unit
Tanner edges. It has at most seven qubits in this interior
plaquette patch, diameter<=4, and one-term intensive strength
<=5epsilon. The perturbation is genuinely noncommuting:

    [H_epsilon,H0]
      =2i(2−epsilon)sin(2epsilon) Q(I+S1S2) !=0.      (2)

This operator is nonzero on, for example, the legal equal-sign
S1=S2 sector reached from the ground state. It vanishes on B
because H0 is scalar there, which does not affect (1).

The physical bath Q commutes with U and F does too. Exact
Davies covariance therefore preserves (1). This is a decisive
counterexample to norm continuity of the exact secular Davies
generator under small local perturbations, even with an exact
one-site full-system dressing and an unchanged ground code.
It is not a thermal-memory failure.

## 3. The order of limits is paid explicitly

For a finite symmetric time window, use the candidate's valid
weighted jump

    L(t)=sqrt(gamma(2delta))e^(-2i delta t)sigma_-
         +sqrt(gamma(-2delta))e^(2i delta t)sigma_+.

In D[L(t)](F), the anticommutator contributes
−e^(-4delta²)F/2. The cross gain term, after window averaging,
is sqrt(gamma(2delta)gamma(-2delta))sinc(4delta T)F.
The square root equals e^(-4delta²)/(2cosh(beta delta)).
Hence

    D_delta,T(F)=e^(-4delta²)/2
        [sinc(4delta T)/cosh(beta delta)−1]F.         (3)

A diagonal block Gibbs state has zero cross gain and the usual
KMS population balance, so it remains stationary. Formula(3)
goes to zero as delta→0 at fixed T and to the secular value
at fixed nonzero delta as T→infinity. The iterated limits
therefore disagree. This finite-window model is an exact
pressure test, not a universal microscopic bath derivation.
Uniform full secularization must pay the splitting-resolution
scale; a ground gap does not do so.

## 5. The dissipative product identity needs only kplus

Let C_omega=[O,S_omega], S_omega*=S_-omega. KMS and
rho S_omega*=e^(-beta omega)S_omega* rho imply

    sum gamma_omega S_omega rho O S_omega*
      =rho sum gamma_omega S_omega* O S_omega.

    D_alpha(rho O)=1/2[
       rho sum gamma_omega C_omega* S_omega
       −sum gamma_omega S_omega rho C_omega*].       (5)

    sqrt(sum gamma Tr rho S* S) sqrt(k_alpha,j)
