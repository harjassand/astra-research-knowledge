Exact new mechanisms.
1. Corrected-body matching cut validation spans every epsilon<1/16 for
   quadratics. For m sampled POSITIONS per candidate half in one fresh true

     true mean<=2epsilon,
     false quadratic mean>=1/4-2epsilon-c_m,
     c_m=binom(2m,m)/(2*4^m).
   Choose Delta=1/4-4epsilon-c_m>0; threshold1/8-c_m/2 and
   T=ceil(2 ln(1/delta)/Delta^2) fresh PREFIX batches bound both errors by
   delta. Repeated raw sampled items and arbitrary fixed B are allowed.
   Greedy maximal matching uses <=m^2 comparisons and O(m^2) graph work.

2. Robust suffix completion needs only UNIQUE RM correction. Inside any
   true even fiber containing b bad items, observed predecessor-count label
   thresholded at half the fiber has <=2b wrong labels. Clean zero mistakes
   are bounded by b1, clean one mistakes by b0, and bad anchors by b0+b1.
   Global error is <=2epsilon, independent of fiber size. After acquiring
   n-r true toggles, enumerate their 2^r fiber seeds and filter prior suffix
   bits to label/correct the remaining coordinates. No list decoding is
   needed. Every bad-history fiber-size check occurs before source calls.

3. Direct median labels avoid random direction search. With odd s iid
   within-fiber comparisons and a fair internal coin for equal-input ties,
   global expected next-bit label error is
     rho_med<=2epsilon+eta_s,
     eta_s=binom(s,(s-1)/2)/2^(s+1).
   Shifted clean-rank grids charge b near-cut and b bad errors; convex
   midpoint quadrature gives eta_s uniformly even for two-item fibers.
   Fresh label seeds preserve the pinned-cube RM pairwise error calculation.

4. Entire-body NIS decoding removes the per-anchor confidence logarithm.
   Dvir-Shpilka gives L_n<=C_d*n^d positions and fixed decoder radius theta_d.

5. Degree-d stabilizers/toggles are polynomial to compute from coefficients.

gamma_med=gamma_direct-eta_s. For each margin choose power-of-two local cube

  Rcap=ceil(2 ln(2n/beta)), delta=beta/(2n Rcap^2),
  Tmed=ceil(ln(1/delta)/(2 gamma_med^2)),
  Tdirect=ceil(ln(1/delta)/(2 gamma_direct^2)),
  S_r=2^(r+1)-r-2,
  B_d=h*(L_n*Kmed*R0*s+s*Tmed)
       +(L_n*Kdirect*R0+Tdirect)*S_r.
Then Qmax<=Rcap*B_d and UNCONDITIONAL E Q<=(2+beta/2)*B_d. Correct-entry
progress probability>=1/2; all stage caps contribute<=beta/2; first wrong

Sharpness and falsifiers.
Root's adjacent-block partial-prefix construction was independently replayed
at d=2 and extended here to d>=1, r>=d. Let
  qb=x0+b*prod ui,
  Qb=(z,qb,u1+qb,...,ud+qb,w).
The q0 lower-half block u=1^d and q0 upper-half block u=1^d are adjacent.
Q1 swaps them; observed half-interleaving is explained by two bad sets,
each density2^(-d-2), while the last flagged prefix differs. Thus the
uniform noise threshold is sharp for that family. Quadratic r<2 and
other restricted prefixes are not covered by this sharpness assertion.
