THEOREM WITH ACQUIRED INTERFACE
Input: explicit rational-complex F in C^(n by n), a site ordering, positive
rational row activities lambda_i, and integer k with0<=2k<=n. Reorder F by
the same site permutation on rows and columns. For cut m put
 w_m=rank F[[0,m),[m,n)]+rank F[[m,n),[0,m)], w=max_m w_m.
Compute all these ranks and their exact rank factorizations by rational
Gaussian elimination. The ordering is supplied, or chosen explicitly; the
algorithm does not find an optimal ordering for free. There is no promise
about numerical singular values and no approximation of F.

Let c_k=sum_(I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k)lambda_I |det F[I,J]|^2.
An exact rational construction acquires the unnormalized Gaussian state's
MPS with bond D_m=2^w_m, then all c_k, occupation-prefix counts and conditional
samples. Time and bit complexity are polynomial in n, input bit length and
D=2^w. A conservative norm contraction cost is O(n^2 D^4) ring operations;
MPS acquisition uses polynomial(n,w) D^3 plus Gaussian/Pfaffian calculations.
Thus w<=C log n, with fixed public C, admits polynomial-bit exact relative
counting (indeed exact counting), expected-time exact draws and fixed-time
approximate draws. No inverse projection probability, hole-partition oracle,
positive overlay representation, balancing data or imported FPRAS is used.

The fixed-k dummy full-basis lift described in INITIAL.txt gives the actual
line-configuration sampler. Independently redrawing the first replica is a
computable reversible kernel with E_P(f1(A)+f2(B))=Var_pi f1 for every real
f1,f2; L=1. If both replicas are refreshed equiprobably, L=2. This exact
kernel is acquired only because the bounded-width law is acquired. It is
not an unrestricted microscopic energy compiler.

1. GAUSSIAN WAVEFUNCTION AND ITS EXACT CUT RANK
Use fermion modes a_i,b_i, ordered by physical sites (a_i,b_i). Set
 |G_F>=exp(sum_ij F_ij a_i^dagger b_j^dagger)|0>.
For equal-size occupied sets I,J, its coefficient equals det F[I,J] times
an explicit fermionic ordering sign. Its squared modulus is |det F[I,J]|^2.
The state always has equal a and b particle numbers. Removing the local11
states and selecting N_a=k gives precisely c_k, with lambda_i attached to
an occupied a_i. Local diagonal activities may be applied at norm contraction,
so no irrational square roots are required to count.

At cut L=[0,m),R=[m,n), factor F_LR=U1 V1^T and F_RL=U2 V2^T with full
column-rank factors of sizes r1 and r2. Define left creation forms
 alpha_l=sum_(i in L)U1_il a_i^dagger, l<=r1,
 beta_l=sum_(i in L)V2_il b_i^dagger, l<=r2,
and their paired right forms from V1 on b_R and U2 on a_R. All left forms
are linearly independent: the a forms and b forms lie in disjoint mode
spaces, and each factor has full column rank. The right forms are also
independent. There are w_m=r1+r2 such forms on each side.

All pair-creation terms commute (they are even products of creators), and
each rank-one cross term squares to zero. Factoring the exponential into
internal-left, internal-right and cross terms gives
 |G_F>=sum_(S subset[w_m])epsilon_S
           L_S |G_(F_LL)> tensor R_S |G_(F_RR)>,
where L_S and R_S are ordered products of the paired forms and epsilon_S is
a nonzero sign fixed by the fermionic ordering. There is one selected subset
per term; there is no sum over unknown amplitudes or mismatch projector.

The vectors L_S|G_(F_LL)> are independent. Indeed exp(internal pair creators)
is invertible on the finite Fock space (a finite unipotent exponential), it
commutes with every creation word, and the products of independent linear
forms acting on vacuum are independent wedge basis vectors. The right
vectors have the same property. Therefore the algebraic Schmidt rank is
EXACTLY2^w_m. These nonorthogonal left vectors span the actual Schmidt support.
This exact finite-rank claim is not a statement about approximate entanglement
or a small covariance norm.

2. RATIONAL NONORTHOGONAL SCHMIDT TENSORS
At every m choose the explicit left basis v_(m,S)=L_S|G_(F_LL)> above.
At m=0 the basis is vacuum; at m=n it is the single full vector |G_F>.
Every physical-site slice of v_(m,S) lies in the preceding left support.
To see this without a rank guess, apply a linear functional selecting its
independent right partner in the global state's cut-m decomposition. Taking
one more physical slice commutes with this right functional. The global
cut-(m-1) decomposition then places the resulting vector in the preceding
left span. Hence there are exact coefficients
 v_(m,S)=sum_(T,delta in{00,10,01,11})
                 Gamma_m[T,delta,S] v_(m-1,T) tensor |delta>.

Let G_(m-1)[T,U]=<v_T,v_U>. This Gram matrix is positive definite by the
independence just proved. Define O_delta[T,S]=<v_T tensor delta,v_(m,S)>.
Then Gamma_delta=G_(m-1)^(-1) O_delta. No eigenvalue gap, SVD, square root,
choice of approximately orthogonal eigenvectors or numerical truncation is
needed. Exact inversion automatically handles ill-conditioned bases in bit
arithmetic; its bit cost is charged below.

3. ALL OVERLAPS ARE ACQUIRED GAUSSIAN/WICK VALUES
For paired Gaussian states |G_A>,|G_B> on m sites, Z_AB=<G_A|G_B>
=det(I+A^dagger B). In the Gram calls A=B. In the transition calls A is the
previous principal block of B, padded by a zero row and column. Thus
I+A^dagger B is block triangular with diagonal blocks I+A0^dagger A0 and1;
Z_AB is nonzero, and all required inverses below exist without a promise.

Write Q=(I+B A^dagger)^(-1), R=(I+B^T conjugate(A))^(-1), and define
<.>_AB=<G_A| . |G_B>/Z_AB. Its nonzero contractions are
 <a_i a_j^dagger>_AB=Q_ij,
 <b_i b_j^dagger>_AB=R_ij,
 <a_i b_j>_AB=-(Q B)_ij,
 <a_i^dagger b_j^dagger>_AB=(conjugate(A) R)_ij.
Reverse orders follow from the CAR. Cross-species normal contractions and
same-species anomalous contractions vanish. These equations follow directly
from (a-Bb^dagger)|G_B>=0, (b+B^T a^dagger)|G_B>=0 and the corresponding bra
identities, together with the CAR. For example Q=I-QBA^dagger gives its
stated inverse; the anomalous aa/bb blocks vanish by particle-number difference.

For an ordered even list d1,...,d_(2ell) of linear creators/annihilators,
 <G_A|d1...d_(2ell)|G_B>=Z_AB Pf(K),
 K_ij=<d_i d_j>_AB for i<j, K_ji=-K_ij. Odd lists have overlap zero.
This is the finite fermionic Gaussian Wick identity, with its exact
hypotheses and all contractions given here. It can be proved by expanding
the finite Grassmann Gaussian generating function: the normalized generating
function is exp(sum_(i<j)eta_i K_ij eta_j), and its full Grassmann coefficient
is the defining Pfaffian expansion. Equivalently, successive contraction of
the first operator gives the Pfaffian's signed recursion. The required
Gaussian generating function follows by completing the finite Gaussian
quadratic form with I+A^dagger B invertible. This is standard Gaussian
algebra, not a new counting theorem or an unchecked frontier dependency.

Our overlap lists contain at most w_(m-1)+w_m+2 creators/annihilators. The
bra list is the reverse adjoint of the preceding basis creator word followed
by the chosen site's creator word, and the ket is the current creator word.
No tensor-sector probability is approximated. Pfaffians are evaluated by
exact skew elimination in O(ell^3) operations: move a nonzero first-row pivot
K_01 to position1 (record its sign), multiply it, and replace the remaining
block by
 K'_ij=K_ij-(K_0i K_1j-K_0j K_1i)/K_01.
An all-zero first row gives zero, not an inverse oracle.

5. FINITE BIT COMPLEXITY AND SAMPLING
Exact rank factors are obtained by Gaussian elimination with polynomial
entry heights (minors give standard rational height bounds). Gaussian
covariances, determinant norms and Pfaffians use matrices of dimensions
polynomial in n,w, with polynomial-height rational entries. Each Gram matrix
has dimension D_m=2^w_m; its determinant and inverse have bit heights bounded
by polynomial(n,input bits,D_m), by denominator clearing and Hadamard. Local
tensors and n successive norm contractions retain polynomial bit length in
n,input bits,D. Small numerical singular values can increase rational bit
length within these charged bounds; no numerical reciprocal is free.

There are O(n D^2) squared-transfer states including particle count, and a
conservative site update scans O(D^4) virtual pairs, giving O(n^2D^4) ring
operations. Gram inversion is O(D^3); there are O(D^2) Pfaffian overlaps per
site and physical label, with polynomial(n,w) work each. Sampling all n
sites by three exact completed-prefix norms costs at most n times the norm
contraction bound after the MPS has been acquired once.

A branch is drawn in proportion to its three exact nonnegative rational
weights. Sequential conditionals give the exact law without rejection on
the projected event. Integer rejection sampling is expected polynomial;
fixed-time dyadic categories with b>=ceil(log2(3n/epsilon))+1 fair bits per
site have epsilon-TV cumulative error. Exact zero sectors are reported
before attempting any sample. Neither runtime contains1/c_k or1/P(project).
For a purely rational-complex input, a nonzero determinant-squared summand
also gives a direct polynomial bound on -log c_k after denominator clearing,
but sampling does not rely on such a lower probability estimate.
