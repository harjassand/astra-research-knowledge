Exact retained-candidate counterexample, internally derived 2026-10-12

This is a rejected technology route, not a novelty or experimental claim.
The route was an autonomous molecular evidence counter. The proposed benefit
was exponentially accurate assembly in linear verification time. That headline
already occurs in Murugan, Huse and Leibler, PNAS 109 (2012), 12034-12039,
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1119911109, and biochemical likelihood accumulation
is explicit in Siggia and Vergassola, PNAS 110 (2013), E3704-E3712,
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314081110.

MODEL AND EXACT SOLUTION

For a fixed captured substrate S, an evidence counter occupies i=0,...,N-1.
Transitions i -> i+1 occur at rate u_S and i -> i-1 at rate d_S.
State -1 rejects; state N accepts. The *same* substrate independently escapes
at rate mu>0, causing rejection. Neither substrate identity nor infinite
retention is supplied. Let h_S(i) be acceptance probability before rejection.

  u h(i+1)+d h(i-1)-(u+d+mu)h(i)=0,
  h(-1)=0, h(N)=1.

Define
  r_+/- = [(u+d+mu) +/- sqrt((u+d+mu)^2-4ud)]/(2u).
Then, by direct substitution and uniqueness of the finite Dirichlet problem,

  h(i)=[r_+^(i+1)-r_-^(i+1)]/[r_+^(N+1)-r_-^(N+1)].        (1)

For mu>0, 0<r_-<1<r_+, so h(0)=Theta(exp[-N log r_+]).
The expected verification time is at most 1/mu because escape terminates it.
With finite capture capacity lambda_cap per site and candidate right fraction
q_R, correct-output rate is at most lambda_cap q_R h_R(0), even if reset is
free. Parallel sites multiply footprint and input flux by the same factor.

An especially clean trap is u_R=2,d_R=1,u_W=1,d_W=2 in units of one attempt
rate. Equation (1) gives exactly

  h_W(0)/h_R(0)=2^(-N)                                   (2)

for every mu>=0, not only asymptotically. Equal candidate fractions therefore
give product error e_N=1/(1+2^N), independent of escape. Yet for every fixed
positive mu the accepted right flux falls exponentially with N. The error
metric alone completely conceals the lost throughput.

For a more general discrimination pair with log r_+,W > log r_+,R > 0,

  e_N = Theta(exp[-N(log r_+,W-log r_+,R)]),
  right yield = Theta(e_N^[log r_+,R/(log r_+,W-log r_+,R)]).

This is a counterexample to inferring useful manufacturing throughput from
exponential discrimination plus linear conditional verification time.

RETENTION IS A CHARGED RESOURCE, NOT A UNIVERSAL IMPOSSIBILITY

If u_R>d_R and mu_N=C/N, expand the characteristic root around mu=0:
  log r_+,R=mu/(u_R-d_R)+O(mu^2).
Equation (1) gives the finite, positive limiting right yield

  lim h_R(0)=(1-d_R/u_R) exp[-C/(u_R-d_R)].                (3)

Thus a preconfigured sufficiently stable nonselective latch restores finite
yield. With a specified Arrhenius escape law mu=nu exp[-B/(k_B T)], it needs
B=k_B T log(nu N/C), only logarithmic in N. A confidence-dependent clamp
whose barrier instead rises linearly with evidence has no demonstrated
asymptotic resource advantage over this simple fixed-latch baseline.
Fabricating, capturing, releasing/resetting and reusing that latch, and the
fuel and implementation of the species-dependent counter rates, remain
physical engineering obligations. Equation (3) does not supply them.

STOP DECISION

No new operation beyond established nonequilibrium proofreading/sequential
recognition was identified. This route was abandoned after this exact
retention-loss calculation; no catalyst, assembly device or throughput claim
was constructed.
