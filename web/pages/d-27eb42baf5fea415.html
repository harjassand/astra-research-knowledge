CYCLE 3 LEAF — RECURRENCE OUTSIDE THE SMALL-GAIN LP REGION
Date: 2026-10-08 (Australia/Brisbane)
Status: exact counterexample to necessity of the LP criterion; no transient/null-recurrent/explosive example found in this leaf.

RESULT

The proposed small-gain LP is sufficient but is not necessary for stochastic recurrence in the stated multi-catalyst family. For r=s=2, there are exact strictly positive rates for which the LP is infeasible while the entire CTMC is complex balanced, nonexplosive in physical time, and positive recurrent on every closed irreducible communicating class. This rules out interpreting the LP feasibility boundary as a stochastic phase boundary. It does not show that every point outside the LP region is recurrent, and it does not rule out transient/null-recurrent/explosive parameter choices elsewhere outside it.

NETWORK AND EXACT RATE CHOICE

Take two A species A_1,A_2 and two B species B_1,B_2. For each j=1,2 include 0<->A_j with (alpha_j,delta_j)=(1,1). For every (j,i) in {1,2}^2 include

  A_j <-> A_j+B_i       with (p_ji,q_ji)=(1,1),
  A_j+B_i <-> 2A_j+B_i  with (u_ji,v_ji)=(2,2),

so every k_ji=1. All stochastic propensities use standard falling-factorial mass action, e.g. the forward channel of the last pair has intensity 2 A_j B_i and its reverse has intensity 2 (A_j)_2 B_i. Every listed directed channel has a positive rate constant. The finite network has four species and twenty directed reaction channels.

EXACT LP INFEASIBILITY

The candidate LP asks for ell_j,w_i>0 satisfying

  delta_j ell_j > sum_i p_ji w_i,
  q_ji w_i > k_ji u_ji ell_j   for every i,j.

For our constants, fix any j. The first inequality is ell_j>w_1+w_2. The second inequalities give w_1>2ell_j and w_2>2ell_j. Hence

  ell_j > w_1+w_2 > 4ell_j,

contradicting ell_j>0. Thus there is no feasible positive LP point. This is strict algebraic infeasibility, not a numerical solver result.

More generally, every feasible LP point must satisfy, for each i,j,

  k_ji p_ji u_ji < delta_j q_ji.

Indeed, the first inequality implies p_ji w_i<delta_j ell_j, while the second implies p_ji w_i> (k_ji p_ji u_ji/q_ji)ell_j. The chosen point violates this necessary condition by the exact factor 2 for each pair.

COMPLEX BALANCE AND PHYSICAL-TIME RECURRENCE

At c=(1,1,1,1), each reversible pair is individually balanced in deterministic flux:

  0 <-> A_j:                 1*c^0 = 1*c^{A_j}=1,
  A_j <-> A_j+B_i:           1*c^{A_j} = 1*c^{A_j+B_i}=1,
  A_j+B_i <-> 2A_j+B_i:      2*c^{A_j+B_i} = 2*c^{2A_j+B_i}=2.

Therefore c is a positive complex-balanced equilibrium. By the Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz product-form theorem, the full CTMC has invariant probability

  pi(n)= exp(-4)/(n_{A_1}! n_{A_2}! n_{B_1}! n_{B_2}!).

This alternative stationary-law proof applies to the actual fixed rate vector; no stochastic approximation or simulation is involved.

The mean total jump rate under pi is finite and can be evaluated exactly using Poisson factorial moments. Summing kappa_r c^{y_r} over directed channels gives 4 from the two basal reversible pairs, 8 from the four A_j<->A_j+B_i pairs, and 16 from the four A_j+B_i<->2A_j+B_i pairs, for

  E_pi[q(X)] = sum_r kappa_r c^{y_r} = 28 < infinity.

The finite-stationary-flux nonexplosion theorem for CTMCs therefore gives nonexplosion from every state in supp(pi), which is all of N_0^4. Also, every state-graph transition has its reverse enabled at the destination: if n>=y then n-y+y'>=y', so the reverse reaction y'->y can fire. Thus every communicating class is closed. Restricting pi to any nonempty class and normalizing gives an invariant probability on that irreducible class; together with nonexplosion, this implies positive recurrence on every class.

Consequently this exact outside-LP network is recurrent in physical time, including the holding-time clock. The argument does not infer CTMC recurrence from an embedded jump chain. The product-Poisson law controls physical time through the finite stationary mean jump flux 28.

ROBUSTNESS WITHIN THE DETAILED-BALANCE SUBFAMILY

This is not an isolated equality accident. Keep k_ji=1 and choose any positive rates with alpha_j=delta_j, p_ji=q_ji, and u_ji=v_ji for all pairs. Then c=1 still balances each reversible pair. If for at least one fixed j every i has u_ji>delta_j (as in the displayed example), the necessary LP inequality k_ji p_ji u_ji<delta_j q_ji fails. More generally it is enough that one pair has k_ji u_ji>=delta_j under p_ji=q_ji. Such rates remain complex balanced and classwise positive recurrent. The outside-LP recurrent set therefore contains a continuum of exact rate choices (within this detailed-balanced parameter subfamily).

This establishes non-necessity, not a complete phase diagram. It is possible that other outside-LP points are nonrecurrent, or that a broader nonlinear/corrector Lyapunov covers a larger region. This leaf does not classify them.

INTERPRETATION / SCOPE

The LP is a certificate for a particular weighted-mass-plus-boundary-indicator Foster function. Infeasibility means that those simultaneous linear drift inequalities cannot hold; it does not imply outward stochastic drift, transience, null recurrence, or explosion. Here the stronger rate-specific balance structure supplies an explicit invariant law and physical-time clock control even though the proposed linear inequalities are inconsistent.

No finite simulation was used. All LP and balance checks are exact. The proof relies on established product-form and finite-flux CTMC theorems; it is not a new general recurrence theorem for weakly reversible networks. It answers the current leaf question by exhibiting an exact point outside the LP region with proven recurrence, and leaves the global outside-region classification UNKNOWN.
