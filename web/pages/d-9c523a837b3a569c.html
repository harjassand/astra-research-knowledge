PRECISE CLASS AND INPUT ACQUISITION
Input is a finite list of rational positive boxes for only these reactions:
  0 -> X_i, X_i ->0 (at least one of each for every i);
  X_j -> X_i+X_j with rate beta_ij x_j, including i=j if present;
  X_i+X_j -> X_j with rate gamma_ij x_i x_j for i!=j;
  2X_i -> X_i with rate gamma_ii x_i(x_i-1), if present;
  optional X_i -> X_j conversions (i!=j).
Parallel channels are aggregated by summing their lower and upper bounds, a
sound rate envelope for every predictable choice of their original rates.
The coefficient beta_ij means species i is created with catalyst j. In contrast,
gamma_ji removes catalyst j in the presence of the created species i. For EVERY
present beta_ij, require that the RECIPROCAL removal gamma_ji has positive lower
bound. This index reversal is load-bearing; the literal reverse gamma_ij alone
need not suffice for this construction. The complex graph must be weakly
reversible (the implementation checks it), placing this class inside endotactic
networks. All actual source and product complexes have molecularity<=2.
All species immigrate and degrade with positive lower bounds. No certificate,
path, invariant law, numerical equilibrium or rate-point estimate is supplied.
All actual propensities are falling factorials, zero when reactants are absent.
Rate controls may depend predictably on the entire past and retain their boxes.

COMPACT CERTIFICATE AND COMPLETE GLOBAL PROOF
Acquire integer thresholds
 h_i=1+max({ceil(beta_ij^upper/gamma_ji^lower): beta_ij present} union {0}).
Define z_i=(x_i-h_i)_+, Z=sum_i z_i, and V=1+Z.
Let I=sum_i immigration_i^upper, let delta=min_i degradation_i^lower>0,
and let Kconv be the conversion constant sum_{i->j} rate_upper h_i. Set
             C=I+sum_{i,j} beta_ij^upper h_j + Kconv,
             B=C+delta.
Then at EVERY count state and for EVERY allowed control,
                    L_t V(x) <= C-delta Z = B-delta V.        (1)
All certificate data are rational, except h_i which are exact integers.

Here is the pointwise cancellation, with the enabled/disabled boundaries kept.
A birth of i increases z_i iff x_i>=h_i; it then increases V by1. A death of i
reduces z_i iff x_i>h_i; it then reduces V by1. Put A={j:x_j>h_j} and
E={i:x_i>=h_i}. Off-diagonal catalytic births contribute
                 sum_{i in E,j} beta_ij x_j.
For a large catalyst j in A, use its reciprocal quadratic death in the presence
of every recipient i in E. Its negative contribution is
                 -gamma_ji x_j x_i.
Since x_i>=h_i and gamma_ji^lower h_i>=beta_ij^upper, this cancels the corresponding
positive birth term pointwise. For i=j in A, the negative falling-factorial
term is -gamma_jj x_j(x_j-1). The integer inequality x_j>h_j implies x_j-1>=h_j,
so it likewise cancels beta_jj x_j. No x_j^2 approximation is used.

If the birth's catalyst j is outside A, its count is <=h_j. The remaining
positive term is therefore bounded by beta_ij^upper h_j, regardless of which
recipient is active. Degradation of each large species contributes at most
-delta_j^lower x_j, hence at most -delta z_j. Immigration is at most I.
A unary conversion X_i->X_j changes V by at most0 when x_i>h_i: its source hinge
falls by1 and its target hinge rises by at most1. If x_i<=h_i, its positive
contribution is bounded by rate_upper h_i. Summing these exhaustive cases proves
(1). Multiple reactions with coincident source complexes cause no difficulty;
cancellation uses actual lower/upper bounds and does not require rate coupling.

V is proper because N=sum_i x_i <= Z+sum_i h_i. Equation(1) gives nonexplosion
by the stopped proper-Lyapunov argument in INITIAL, without any deterministic
permanence transfer. With Rv=(B+1)/delta, the finite core K={V<=Rv} has count cap
Rn=ceil(Rv+sum_i h_i). Outside K, L V<=-1. Therefore
                          E_x tau_K<=V(x).                   (2)
The proof applies conditionally after every history. Also E V(X_t)<=V(x)+Bt.

EXPLICIT BOUNDARY RECOVERY AND ITS EXPANDED COST
For a chosen q>=1, use direct unary degradations to empty any x in K, then
q direct immigrations per species. This is an acquired path with at most
H=Rn+dq firings. It stays at count N<=D=max(Rn,dq) and ends exactly at (q,...,q).
Let U be the sum of upper bounds of all original reaction channels,
Q=U(D+1)^2, lmin the minimum of their positive lower bounds, alpha=lmin/(2Q),
p=alpha^H and t0=H/Q. The next required enabled channel fires before1/Q with
probability at least alpha: lower selected hazard lmin, total upper hazard Q,
and lmin integral_0^(1/Q)e^(-Qs)ds>=lmin/(2Q). This is uniform under every
predictable policy. Shorter paths only increase the resulting lower bound p.

Using attempts of duration t0, with return to K after a failed attempt, gives
        E_x tau_(q,...,q) <= V(x)+[Rv+(B+1)t0]/p.             (3)
It also bounds the first time all species counts are>=q. The retry expectation
is derived exactly as in INITIAL: conditional success>=p; unconditional return
cost on a failed attempt <=Rv+B t0; sum the geometric survival probabilities.
The small denominator p is not hidden or advertised as a fast physical recovery.
For q=2 the target enables every molecular reaction. Continued containment of
that target region is not asserted: it is not an invariant region.

Since direct birth/death edges connect every count state to zero and back,
fixed time-homogeneous choices are irreducible on all N_0^d. The finite expected
return to0 obtained from the same bounded-path argument proves positive
recurrence. History-dependent controls are given recovery and tail inequalities,
not a stationary law.

RATIONAL EXPONENTIAL FOSTER CERTIFICATE AND CAPACITY SAFETY
The hinge function's jump is always in{-1,0,1} on the admitted pattern. Let
P(x) and D(x) denote its total positive- and negative-jump intensities. The
above proof gives P-D<=C-delta Z. Moreover,
 P(x)<=C+A Z, where A=max_j sum_i beta_ij^upper.
The same C covers immigration and any positive conversion: a positive conversion
has a source count<=h_i, and each catalyst satisfies x_j<=h_j+z_j.
Acquire the exact rational data
 eta=delta/[2(A+delta)], a=1+eta>1, c=eta/a,
 r=ceil((3C+2)/delta), b=c(1+3C/2) a^r.
For Wexp(x)=a^Z, direct integer-jump arithmetic gives
 L Wexp/Wexp=(a-1)P-(1-1/a)D
            =c[(P-D)+eta P]
            <=c[(1+eta)C-(delta-eta A)Z]
            <=c[(3/2)C-(delta/2)Z].
Thus, outside Z<=r it is at most -c, and inside this finite set its upward
contribution plus cWexp is at most b. The exact rational global certificate is
               L_t Wexp <=-c Wexp+b 1_{Z<=r}.               (4)
This requires no real exponentiation or logarithm in certificate verification:
ratios across jumps are the rationals a,a^-1,1.

Stopped time-dependent Dynkin with e^(ct)Wexp and Fatou gives, for every policy,
 E Wexp(X_t)<=e^(-ct) a^Z0+(b/c)(1-e^(-ct)).
Hence the transient tail probability obeys
 P(Z_t>=m)<= [e^(-ct)a^Z0+(b/c)(1-e^(-ct))] a^-m.
Dropping the negative drift and stopping at first Z>=m also gives a rational
finite-horizon capacity bound for rational T:
 P(sup_{0<=t<=T} Z_t>=m) <= min(1,[a^Z0+bT] a^-m).           (5)
N<=Z+sum_i h_i converts it to total-count capacity. Fixed homogeneous invariant
laws have E_pi a^Z<=b/c, by time-averaged drift and Fatou, so
 P_pi(Z>=m)<=(b/c)a^-m. These are population upper-tail bounds; they do not claim
small low-species/boundary mass, energy-free operation or all-time containment.

ACQUISITION AND TOTAL BIT RUNTIME
Let L be the dense binary network/endpoint/q input length, b bound the rate
endpoint numerator/denominator heights, and R be the number of channels.
Pattern recognition, positive-flow checks, rate aggregation, graph reachability,
threshold ceiling divisions and the compact constants above are polynomial
ordinary bit operations in L. There is no LP/CAD search or unknown radius.
Each h_i is <=2+R 2^(2b) after aggregating parallel channels, and compact
rational constants use O(poly(L)) bits.
The compact certificate includes rational-power pairs (alpha,H) and (a,r).
Use compile_hinges(...,expand_probability=False) for the polynomial acquisition
path; the default True deliberately emits expanded powers and retry constants
and is charged at the exponential output cost.

as 2^O(L); their rational outputs therefore have up to 2^O(L) bits and can be
computed in 2^O(L) ordinary bit operations with a suitable larger absolute
constant in the exponent. This deliberately includes binary q, extremely small
rate lower bounds and the very large resulting physical time bound. There is
no polynomial expanded-output or physical-firing-cost claim.
