1. Root Section2: Kato average -- exact termwise identity

For a single legal Hermitian noise B and Q=U A, A=|Q|, choose a unitary
extension of the finite-dimensional polar isometry. Define

  A0=A^(1/2)U*BU A^(1/2), C=A^(1/2)B A^(1/2).

Both are Hermitian. Trace cyclicity gives EXACTLY

  Tr(Q*BQB)=Tr(A0 C),
  Tr(|Q*|B|Q*|B)=Tr(A0^2),
  Tr(|Q|B|Q|B)=Tr(C^2).

The first trace is real; root's real part is harmless. The drift equals
Tr[V(QQ*+Q*Q)]/2, precisely the average of the two absolute-value drifts.
Thus the difference is not merely bounded but has the exact expression

  E_(H_B)(Q)-[E_(H_B)(|Q|)+E_(H_B)(|Q*|)]/2
      =||A0-C||_HS^2/2>=0.                            (K)

The unitary extension outside the polar support has no effect on these
expressions. Summation proves root's Kato inequality for every finite
generator in the audited full representation. Neither V commuting with
Q nor modular covariance is needed.

Ordinary positive LSI2 is legitimately applied to the TWO positive
observables Gamma^(-1/2)|Q| and Gamma^(-1/2)|Q*| in the original M_d. If
the assumption was initially only for strictly positive inputs, continuity
