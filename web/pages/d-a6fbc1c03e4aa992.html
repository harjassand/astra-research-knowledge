# Exact Gaussian benchmark for observable reliability and mass repair

Worker c_gaussian, 2026-10-06. Status: complete analytic benchmark, finite numerical reconstructions, and two adversarial obstructions. No novelty claim for Gaussian diagonalization, entropy identities, transport, or LSI. Non-Gaussian inverse-RG reliability remains unresolved.

## Question and legal interface

On the periodic d-dimensional n-site-per-side lattice, N=n^d, let L be the positive nearest-neighbor graph Laplacian, with eigenvalues

λ(k)=4 sum_i sin²(π k_i/n), 0≤λ≤4d.

The known target is P_a=N(0,K_a^-1), K_a=aI+L, a=m²>0. The approximate generator is assumed, for the mass-only conclusions, to have exactly the law Q=P_b, b=a+δ>0. This is an assumption on the whole joint law, not a consequence of matching a finite diagnostic list. We observe independent generated fields, know L and a, and can read all N coordinates. A sample costs at least N writes. No claim is made that this interface describes learned φ⁴ generators.

The desired outputs are (i) error bounds for selected local or spectral observables; (ii) a certificate for the relevant mass direction; (iii) a correction with honest costs and comparison to a direct sampler.

## Exact divergence and global distinguishability

Write κ_k=a+λ_k, t_k=δ/κ_k. Every real eigenmode has target variance 1/κ_k and proposal variance 1/[κ_k(1+t_k)]. Direct integration of the Gaussian log density gives

D(P_a||P_b)=½ sum_k [t_k−log(1+t_k)],

D(P_b||P_a)=½ sum_k [(1+t_k)^-1−1+log(1+t_k)].

For |δ|/a small, both are δ²/4 sum_k κ_k^-2+O(sum_k |δ/κ_k|³). At fixed a>0 and fixed nonzero δ>-a, N^-1D converges to a strictly positive Brillouin-zone integral. More directly every mode contributes a positive amount bounded away from zero, because κ∈[a,a+4d]. Thus KL is Θ(N).

Large KL alone does not prove TV collapse. Integrate sqrt(pq) in each independent real mode instead. The affinity is

A(P_a,P_b)=product_k sqrt[2 sqrt(1+t_k)/(2+t_k)].

Each factor is <1 for nonzero δ; the factor is uniformly bounded below 1 on the compact κ interval. Consequently A≤exp(−c(a,δ,d)N), and TV≥1−A→1. This proves global distinguishability even when per-coordinate errors remain small. For small t, log affinity per mode=−t²/16+O(t³).

## Local and scale observables

Let Y=A x be any rank-r linear projection; A may select r sites, finite filtered coefficients, or r global linear observables. Write C=A K_a^-1 Aᵀ and D=A K_b^-1 Aᵀ. For ρ=|δ|/a<1,

(1+ρ)^-1 C ≤ D ≤ (1−ρ)^-1 C.

This follows by diagonalizing K and then applying congruence by A. No commutation of the projected covariance matrices is required. The eigenvalues s_i of C^-1/2 D C^-1/2 have this same range. Put u_i=1/s_i−1, so |u_i|≤ρ. The projected divergence is

D(P_Y||Q_Y)=½ sum_i [u_i−log(1+u_i)] ≤ rρ²/[4(1−ρ)].

Indeed u−log(1+u)=integral_0^u t/(1+t) dt≤u²/[2(1−ρ)]. Pinsker therefore gives TV(P_Y,Q_Y)≤ρ sqrt[r/(8(1−ρ))], independent of N. For δ≥0, the stronger bound D(P_Y||Q_Y)≤r(δ/a)²/4 follows from u_i∈[0,δ/a]. A bounded observable of Y has expectation error at most its range times this TV bound. Growing r is charged explicitly; simultaneous accuracy of all N sites does not follow from fixed-r accuracy.

For a positive quadratic observable F=xᵀH x with H≥0 supported in a spectral subspace λ≥ℓ, and δ≥0,

0≤(E_P F−E_Q F)/E_P F≤δ/(a+δ+ℓ).

Proof: on that subspace, [1−δ/(a+δ+ℓ)]K_a^-1≤K_b^-1≤K_a^-1; multiply by H and take traces. For a dyadic spectral shell ℓ=α/s², ultraviolet error is O(δ s²), saturating at O(δ/a) in the infrared. Exact normalized shell-power bias is

1−[sum_{k in shell}(a+δ+λ_k)^-1]/[sum_{k in shell}(a+λ_k)^-1].

Spectral shells are used here because their support is exact. A compact spatial wavelet has frequency leakage: a high-frequency lower bound cannot be silently assigned to such a wavelet. For an actual filter with Fourier weights w_k≥0, replace the shell sums by weighted sums, which yields an exact filter-specific certificate. Signed correlations may cancel, so a relative bound for positive powers does not give a relative bound for arbitrary covariance entries.

The normalized constant mode z=N^-1/2 sum_x x_x has variance 1/a under P and 1/b under Q. Susceptibility N E(mean x)²=1/a has relative proposal bias δ/(a+δ). This observable immediately exposes the mass direction. At critical scaling a=c/n², a fixed mass error destroys infrared accuracy; even δ=εa leaves constant susceptibility bias ε/(1+ε). In d=2 and δ=ε/n², the point variance difference remains O(1), while the target point variance grows Θ(log n), so relative point-variance error tends to zero. Thus excellent relative local power agreement need not force excellent infrared agreement. The finite calculation illustrates the slow approach and is not an asymptotic proof by itself.

## Exact dynamics and correction

Target Ornstein-Uhlenbeck evolution dx=−K_a x dt+sqrt(2)dB has covariance at time t

C_t=K_a^-1+e^-K_a t(K_b^-1−K_a^-1)e^-K_a t.

In eigenmode κ, the relative variance deficit is [δ/(κ+δ)]e^-2κt. Repair of modes κ≥ℓ+a takes t=O((a+ℓ)^-1 log(1/ε)). Repair of the relevant mode takes t=O(a^-1 log(1/ε)); this is the exact free-field analogue of a long thermal transient. It is specific to this OU update, not a lower bound on all algorithms.

If b is known, the deterministic transport

T=(K_b K_a^-1)^1/2, equivalently Fourier multiplier sqrt[(b+λ)/(a+λ)],

maps P_b exactly to P_a. Dense covariance multiplication reconstructs this identity to numerical precision in the experiment. It costs O(N log N) arithmetic and O(N) memory by FFT, with no importance weights or volume acceptance penalty.

The equally informed direct baseline x=K_a^-1/2 ξ, ξ standard real Gaussian, costs the same O(N log N), O(N) memory. Therefore mass transport is a repair mechanism, not evidence of a Gaussian sampling advantage or a need for learned multiscale sampling. FFT diagonalization uses known translation-invariant K; neither cost nor formula extends automatically to unknown or interacting targets.

## Mass certificate: coordinate choice changes sample cost

Zero-mode-only certificate: from M independent draws, v=M^-1 sum_j z_j², the pivot M b v is exactly χ²_M. Quantiles give an exact confidence interval for b and hence for δ=b−a. Relative b precision costs M=O(ε^-2 log(1/η)), independent of volume. Its utility assumes P_b membership. To make a global mass residual O(N^-1/2) at fixed a using this one mode alone costs M=O(N); this is not the best full-field estimator.

Full-field certificate, independently audited with the parent: the sufficient statistic is S=sum_j ||x_j||². The unconstrained MLE hat b solves sum_k(hat b+λ_k)^-1=S/M; it can be clipped to a bracket containing true b. The function is strictly decreasing. A scalar bisection costs O(N) per evaluation with stored eigenvalues and O(N log precision) arithmetic, in addition to reading M fields.

For any s>b, the event hat b≥s is S/M≤sum_k(s+λ_k)^-1. Exponential tilting by exp[−(s−b)S/2] gives the exact Chernoff rate D(P_s||P_b). The other tail is analogous. Since divergence is monotone away from b on either side,

Pr[D(P_hat_b||P_b)>u]≤2 exp(−M u).

This is a finite-volume bound, not an asymptotic Fisher argument. Clipping toward an interval containing b cannot increase this divergence. If b,hat b∈[a/R,Ra], then r_k=(hat b+λ_k)/(b+λ_k)∈[R^-2,R²]. For f(r)=r−1−log r, f(r)≤R² f(1/r) on this range. For r≥1, use f(r)=integral_1^r (v−1)/v dv, f(1/r)=integral_1^r (v−1)/v² dv, giving ratio≤r. For r≤1, reverse the ratio and use f(r)≤f(1/r). The constant R² is necessary for this simple bracket argument; a bracket [a/R,Ra] does not constrain pairs to a ratio R.

Applying the estimated transport sqrt[(hat b+λ)/(a+λ)] to a fresh independent P_b field produces R_hat with

D(R_hat||P_a)=D(P_b||P_hat_b)≤R² D(P_hat_b||P_b).

Hence TV(R_hat,P_a)≤ε with confidence ≥1−η whenever M≥R² log(2/η)/(2ε²). Here M is independent of N because each complete field carries O(N) information at fixed mass. Pilot acquisition costs MN, eigenvalue evaluation and solve are charged, the output uses a fresh independent field, and membership plus a true enclosing bracket are supplied assumptions. A high-confidence output-law certificate is conditional on the fitted pilot; the unconditional mixture has TV≤ε+η by averaging good and bad pilot events.

## Weighted score certificate, including noncommuting weights

For any smooth q with appropriate finite entropy and Fisher information, define Δs=∇log q−∇log p_a and I_W=E_q ΔsᵀWΔs, W positive definite. Put B=K_a^1/2 W K_a^1/2. Whitening gives the standard Gaussian LSI

D(q||P_a)≤½E_q ΔsᵀK_a^-1Δs≤I_W/[2 λ_min(B)].

Independent proof of the LSI: with h=q/γ in whitened coordinates and standard OU semigroup P_t, entropy differentiation gives d Ent_γ(P_t h)/dt=−I_γ(P_t h). The gradient identity ∇P_t h=e^-t P_t∇h plus weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives I(P_t h)≤e^-2t I(h). Integrate t from 0 to infinity, where entropy tends to zero, obtaining Ent≤I/2. Smooth approximation handles finite-information densities.

This matrix criterion is sharp for arbitrary q: for q=N(μ,K_a^-1), D=½||K_a^1/2 μ||² and I_W=(K_a^1/2 μ)ᵀB(K_a^1/2 μ). A minimum-eigenvector mean shift attains the coefficient 1/[2 λ_min(B)]. If only centered q are allowed, set C_q=K_a^-1/2[I+(r−1)vvᵀ]K_a^-1/2 for a minimum-eigenvector v. Then D=½(r−1−log r) but I_W=λ_min(B)(r−1)²/r. Thus λ_min(B)→0 hides a fixed divergence even without a mean shift. No commutation of W and K is assumed.

The score of the true generator q and the expectation under q must be available or certified to exploit this result. Plugging in an approximate learned score does not certify the actual q without an additional score-error bound. For W=I, the constant is 1/(2a), exposing critical sensitivity; W=K_a^-1 removes it but requires an inverse-precision action.

## Decisive adversarial obstruction

Let g~P_a and W independent with equal probabilities on 1−γ,1+γ, 0<γ<1; set x=sqrt(W)g. This smooth Gaussian mixture is centered, translation invariant, and has exactly covariance K_a^-1. Every expected quadratic observable, every scale power, every structure factor, and the susceptibility match the target perfectly. Fourth moments differ: E x_i^4=3(1+γ²)C_ii².

Nevertheless the normalized target energy E=xᵀK_a x/N concentrates at W under this mixture and at 1 under P_a. The event |E−1|<γ/2 therefore has target probability→1 and mixture probability→0, so global TV→1. A covariance certificate cannot establish distributional fidelity outside the Gaussian family. This adversary directly identifies the missing membership assumption behind mass-only calibration.
