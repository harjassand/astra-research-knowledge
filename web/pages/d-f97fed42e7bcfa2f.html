## Exact question and primary answer

Can a size-independent multiplicative filling collar, rather than a fixed additive collar, supply a published stability theorem? **Yes, conditionally through check soundness.** Chao Yin and Andrew Lucas, *Low-density parity-check codes as stable phases of quantum matter*, arXiv2411.01002v2 (20 August 2025), PRX Quantum6,030329(2025), formal Theorem2 in Appendix C, PDFp20(C1)–(C8), accepts a check-count function satisfying shell summability (B25). ## Q1. Assumptions on the actual Hamiltonian

Let H0=sum_a lambda_a(I−C_a)/2, with mutually commuting nontrivial Pauli checks, a nonempty code space of dimension2^K, 1<=lambda_a<=a0, code distance d, check weight<=q_c and each qubit in<=nu checks. The **actual qubit graph** joins two distinct qubits exactly when an actual check acts nontrivially on both, as in Yin–Lucas Definition10, PDF(A10). Assume it is connected. Root reports this independently established for the original memory; this note has not rederived that model-specific fact. Its degree is bounded by Delta; enlarge Delta to max(1,Delta,q_c) if needed in estimates.

Assume an embedding in R^p for a fixed positive integer p, actual check diameter<=R with R>=1, and at most rho qubits in any unit cube. Thus the number of actual checks wholly inside an ambient ball of radius t is at most nu*rho*(2t+3)^p: anchor each nonempty check at one of its qubits and use bounded check incidence. This estimate is deliberately coarse.

Assume a size-independent C>=1 and a filling scale r_fill such that every global stabilizer whose whole support lies in an ambient ball B(x,r), 1<=r<=r_fill, is a product of actual checks whose whole supports lie in B(x,C(r+1)). An all-radius statement is allowed. This is the load-bearing local-generation premise; homology/distance alone do not supply it.

Define

    L=C(R+1),   K0=max(1,nu*rho*(2L+3)^p),
    d_c=min(d, 1+floor(r_fill/R))

for finite r_fill (take d_c=d for all-radius filling). If r_fill is represented with other radius floors, substitute the corresponding conservative cutoff; the exact theorem explicitly uses min(d,d_c). Assume

    d_s=min(d,d_c) >= c_d log N

with fixed c_d>0. All constants above must be uniform in recursion/system size.

## Q2. Disconnected stabilizer supports give polynomial check soundness

Let g be any global stabilizer of weight M<d_c. Decompose its support into connected components S_i **of the induced actual qubit graph**, with sizes M_i. No actual check intersects two such components: otherwise its co-occurring support qubits would create an edge between them. Since g commutes with every check, each restricted Pauli factor g_i separately commutes with every check. M_i<=M<d excludes a logical factor. Choose each factor's ground-space scalar sign, so it is a member of the stabilizer group; their product is exactly g, since g itself has ground-space eigenvalue+1. Pauli phases do not change support/counts.

A spanning tree of S_i has at most M_i−1 edges, each of ambient length<=R. Hence S_i lies inside a ball centered at one of its qubits of radius at most R(M_i−1); enlarge to r_i=max(1,R(M_i−1))<=R M_i. Our cutoff ensures r_i<=r_fill. Filling then gives a generating set wholly inside radius C(r_i+1)<=L M_i. Repeated generators cancel modulo2, so use no more than the total number of checks in that ball. Therefore

    number of checks for g_i <=K0 M_i^p,
    number of checks for g <=K0 sum_i M_i^p <=K0 M^p.       (Q2)

This proves Definition1 check soundness for f(M)=K0 M^p on the legal integer weights. It proves a count bound, not a fixed/additive intrinsic collar. It closely follows the connected-component argument of Yin–Lucas Proposition17, but inserts the supplied ambient multiplicative filling premise directly; we do not infer their stronger fixed-collar LTO definition from it.

## Q3. Graph growth and an independently reconstructed summability bound

An actual graph ball of radius r is contained in an ambient ball of radius Rr, so

    Gamma_i(r)<=rho(2R+3)^p r^p=:c_g r^p,  r>=1.

The graph's shell cardinality gamma_i(r) is at most Gamma_i(r), not necessarily O(r^(p−1)). Definition7 demands bounds on both volume and shell. Its formal dimension can safely be chosen **p+1**: Gamma<=c_g r^(p+1) and gamma<=c_g r^p. This avoids silently equating ambient dimension with intrinsic shell growth. Root reports formal dimension4 for its original family.

For completeness, the formal theorem needs (B25), and we can verify it without its sharper ansatz estimate(B27)–(B28). Use f(x)=K0 x^p continuously, K0>=1, and the exact recurrence(B22):

    gtilde(1)=1,
    gtilde(r+1)=gtilde(r)+min(d_c,f^-1(gtilde(r)))/Delta,
    ftilde(r)=f^-1(gtilde(r)).

Since d_c>=1 and gtilde>=1, each increment is at least K0^(-1/p)/Delta. Consequently

    ftilde(r)>=a r^(1/p),
    a=K0^(-(p+1)/p^2) Delta^(-1/p)>0.                  (Q3)

For u=a delta_kappa>0, include r=0 separately and bound

    sum_r:ftilde(r)<d_c gamma(r) exp(−delta_kappa ftilde(r))
      <=1+c_g sum_r>=1 r^p exp(−u r^(1/p))
      <=1+c_g integral_0^infty (x+1)^p exp(−u x^(1/p)) dx
      <=1+c_g 2^(p−1) p [Gamma(p(p+1)) u^(−p(p+1))
                             +Gamma(p) u^(−p)].       (Q4)

The integral comparison holds pointwise on [r−1,r]: x+1>=r and exp(−u x^(1/p))>=exp(−u r^(1/p)). Substitute x=y^p to evaluate the integrals. All constants are independent of N and d_c. If k=p(p+1), this is bounded by C0(1+delta_kappa^(-k)). For delta_kappa<=1 use x^k<=max(1,(k/e)^k) exp(x), x=delta_kappa^-1; for delta_kappa>=1 enlarge the constant. Thus (B25) holds with some fixed c_f'', c_f'=1 and **alpha=1**. This deliberately weaker geometric estimate is enough for the source's formal theorem. It removes dependence on any sharp discrete ansatz comparison in Lemma24.

## Q4. Intensive smallness for arbitrary bounded-body perturbations

The exact primary norm is Definition13, PDF(A15):

    ||V||_kappa =max_i sum_S containing i sum_syndrome ||V_S,syndrome|| exp(kappa |S|).

Each term must be strongly supported as in Definition12(A13): it commutes with every check not wholly contained in S. Connectivity of S is not required.

Take an arbitrary bounded-body perturbation V=sum_X V_X, |X|<=q_v, and

    J=max_i sum_X containing i ||V_X||.

The X may be spatially nonlocal. Expand V_X into Pauli strings; normalized Hilbert–Schmidt Cauchy–Schwarz gives sum_P |coeff_P|<=2^(q_v)||V_X||. Each Pauli has a definite syndrome. Enlarge its actual support A to its one-neighborhood S=N(A) in the actual code graph; every check touching A lies wholly in S. Then |S|<=(Delta+1)q_v and strong support holds. Use the larger N(X) for charging: if i belongs to N(X), some j in N(i) belongs to X. Hence

    epsilon=||V||_kappa
       <=(Delta+1)2^(q_v) exp[kappa(Delta+1)q_v] J.      (Q5)

Grouping equal support/syndrome terms only lowers the upper bound by triangle inequality. Empty-support scalar terms are an overall energy shift. This direct route needs no CZ/grid ancillas once connectedness is proved for the original actual graph. It accommodates the source's spatially nonlocal bounded-body perturbations; finite physical interaction range is a special case. A finite-range perturbation with unbounded number of qubits per term would need an additional support-volume or weighted-norm bound.

## Q5. Consequence from the published formal theorem

Choose fixed kappa1>2/c_d and make J small enough that the right side of(Q5) is at most min(epsilon0,1/(3c1)), where the source constants depend on Delta,kappa1 and the fixed summability constants in(Q4). For N>=c3, Theorem2 gives exactly2^K eigenstates in a low-energy interval of half-width

    epsilon_star=c2 N epsilon exp(−kappa1 d_s/2),        (Q6)

up to an overall energy shift, separated from all other eigenvalues by at least1/2. The bandwidth is at most2 epsilon_star. It tends to zero because kappa1 c_d/2>1. For larger d_s it retains the exponential in d_s. This is finite-system spectral stability; it supplies no thermal lifetime, Davies bound, decoder performance, or independently quantified practical threshold. The constants may be extremely small, but are size-independent under the exact uniform premises.

If connectedness were missing, root's actual CZ/grid bridge is a possible fallback, with its separately charged qubit count, distance, filling and perturbation overhead. It is not needed for the reported connected original memory.

## Q6. The exact bookkeeping comparison

The source's Appendix C1 is different from the disputed De Roeck Eq4.8 step. For each **individual strongly-supported** V_S,s, define P_S, Q_S=I−P_S, H_S and

    A_S,s=P_S V_S,s Q_S H_S^-1−H_S^-1 Q_S V_S,s P_S.

H_S^-1 is zero on ker H_S. Outside checks commute with V_S,s by strong support and with P_S,H_S^-1 because all checks commute. Therefore they commute with A_S,s. H_S P_S=0 and H_S H_S^-1 Q_S=Q_S give, separately for each term,

    [H0,A_S,s]=−Q_S V_S,s P_S−P_S V_S,s Q_S.          (Q7)

For Hermitian V this A is anti-Hermitian. Thus the cancellation of the first-order off-diagonal term in(C14)/(C25) is a **term-level identity**, not a consequence only of a global ground block. The two local projections are explicitly support-dependent maps of the given decomposition, as the primary text acknowledges. ## Q8. A limited, independently checked repair of the later vanishing step

Further inspection found that the generic inference stated in PDF(C83)—commutation with an enlarged projector product plus outside checks implies commutation with the smaller projector—is not justified by those two facts alone. This does **not** disprove Lemma27: its extra definite-syndrome and code-distance hypotheses give the needed zero pattern directly. Root and metric_acquisition independently reconstructed this repair after receiving it.

Write D=D_S',s', A=A_S,s and U=S union S'. Under(C79), namely |S'|<ftilde(dist(S',C)) for every flipped check C in s, the following split establishes the exact required Pperp_U[D,A]=0 without enlarging/relabeling its support or increasing its norm:

* If s'=empty, every Pauli term in D centralizes all checks and has weight<d. Hence it is a scalar-phase global stabilizer. Its minimum-check expansion cannot contain a generator that touches any check C in s, by the minimum-expansion exclusion underlying(B23). Every generator therefore commutes with A, giving [D,A]=0 as an operator.
* If s' is nonempty, strong support puts at least one check flipped by D wholly inside S'. Thus P_S'DP_S'=0. Local block diagonality [D,P_S']=0 implies D P_S'=P_S'D=0. Condition(C79) also ensures A flips no check wholly contained in S', so [A,P_S']=0. Consequently [D,A] annihilates P_S' on both sides, and therefore annihilates P_U<=P_S' on both sides. Its local off-diagonal projection vanishes.

Strict endpoint check: if a minimum expansion has any check touching a qubit i with r=dist(i,S')>=1, the proof of(B23) gives weight(g)>=ftilde(r). Therefore strict |S'|<ftilde(dist(S',C)) rules out **every** generator qubit i in C, including the radius-equality boundary, since dist(i,S')>=dist(C,S') and ftilde is increasing. Use this pointwise exclusion; merely putting generators inside the closed radius-r neighborhood would not prove that it avoids C on the boundary. Distance0 is excluded by the condition for a nonempty S'; a scalar term commutes directly. If A has empty syndrome it is zero by(C15), so the conclusion is immediate.

This repairs the collection-level vanishing(C78) with its original support label and the original(C79) zero pattern, so the subsequent shell count(C85) needs no new multiplicity or decay penalty from this repair.
