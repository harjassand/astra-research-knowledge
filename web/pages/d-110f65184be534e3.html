# The rational hitting list is polynomial but its literal program is not a local experiment

Status: exact parameter deduction from the pinned family116 rational manuscript, not a timing measurement. This does not contradict polynomial asymptotics or exclude a substantially better generator.

The input is unary n,s >=1. In `Polynomial Hitting Lists for Noncommutative Rational Formulas`, Section2/Equation1 and Section5/Equation11 (source labels `eq:parameters`, `eq:generator-parameters`), the specified generator chooses

    w=n+1, B=2s+1, Delta=2w³B,
    M=smallest power of two strictly above B*Delta,
    N=M-Delta, D=M²/2, H=1+3(s+2)BM³.

It visits EVERY triple in {1,...,H}³, computes D-square matrices Z_i, and emits the normalized tuple whenever Z_0 is nonsingular. These visits are part of the stated program, not a supplied witness or an implicit oracle. Section5.3 bounds total encoding by O((n+s+1)^87 log(n+s+1)).

Already at n=s=1, its parameters are M256,N208,D32768,H452984833. Hence its outer loop has exactly92,950,340,097,909,002,166,337,537 iterations, before charging matrix construction/rank. A single dense D-square matrix has1,073,741,824 rational records. A simple formula of that tiny input size has much cheaper specialized tests; this is a cost observation about this universal literal construction, not a lower bound for the task.

There is also a large emitted-output lower bound IF the source's generic-invertibility lemma is accepted. The entries of Z_0 are polynomials in t,p,r of total degree <3M; the regular representation of the root-of-unity coefficient algebra changes no parameter degree. Thus deg(det Z_0) <=3MD=3M³/2. Proposition3.2 applied to the constant pencil1 proves det Z_0 is a nonzero rational polynomial: each primitive-root block is generically invertible, and their regular-representation determinant is their product. A nonzero total-degree delta polynomial has at most delta*H² zero grid points (induct on variables using the univariate root count). Therefore at least

    H³-(3MD)H²

triples are retained. For n=s=1, this is at least87,786,432,326,091,571,986,432,001 tuples, or94,259,963,968,270,127,295,738,800,005,709,824 dense rational records for the one-variable output. The program explicitly permits repetitions and prints them; deduplication is not part of this bound. The unconditional loop-count observation needs no nonzero-determinant premise; this output lower bound does.

The parameter-only script `work/metric_prior/cycle19/cost_envelopes.py` and JSON `results/metric_prior/cycle19/hitting_cost_envelopes.json` instantiate four cases without allocating ANY source matrix. Its entire computation took0.0000153s and ~16.7MB RSS. Those figures concern integer parameter arithmetic only, not hitting-list generation/evaluation.

The proper-recognizable length-marker route has a different interface and a single polynomial-dimensional jointly nilpotent tuple. It avoids arbitrary original-expression inverse-domain requirements and yields a plausible bounded exact experiment at three states. It does not replace the rational list's stronger defined-INVERTIBLE-value claim for arbitrary admissible rational formula trees. The next useful allocation is reducing the domain-protecting rational-list construction or extending the smaller proper realization interface; attempting its literal dense program on the local machine is not scientifically informative.
