## Target interface and exact open lemma

The research question is: for a sequence of finite-dimensional state families with uniformly bounded Holevo information, does vanishing error of a common two-output broadcaster force one common EB reconstruction channel with uniform trace error tending to zero, without an additional dimension/net/covering assumption?

The pinned evidence source is Astra `8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83`, especially W maps/cards and N46/N61/N67/N69/N92/N93. The relevant W package records that compatible smoothing reduces the broad question to faithful sandwiches σ/2 ≤ ρ ≤ 3σ/2 with σ included. Its missing quantitative step can be stated for a positive, tracial, self-adjoint, self-compatible likelihood map Φ:

> Find one EB map Ψ, independent of the test family, such that `I - Ψ ≼ c (I - Φ)` for a dimension-independent finite `c`, or prove a weaker common-channel directional/squared-displacement estimate that still implies the uniform EB lift.

The all-frame dual is important: in fixed dimension, a single such Ψ is equivalent by positive-cone separation to the variance-gap inequality for **every** finite Hermitian frame and prior. Proving a different good measurement for each frame does not establish the target. N93's centralization, smoothing, and transport bounds are useful reductions; they do not complete this common-measurement step. Its proposed ultraproduct argument also leaves a finite common EB reconstruction and a dimension-free normalized infinity-to-one bound outstanding.

I reconstructed the exact scope boundary in the strongest existing factor-two results. For any supplied POVM, the nonselective Lüders channel `L(A)=Σ_y sqrt(M_y) A sqrt(M_y)` satisfies `0 ≼ Ψ_M ≼ L ≼ I` for its canonical EB measurement channel, and an explicit instrument-append construction yields a two-output broadcaster with common marginal `(L+Ψ_M)/2`; hence `I-Ψ_M ≼ 2(I-S)`. For an arbitrary supplied bistochastic Kraus instrument, the cycle-four construction instead forms `C=(L+Ψ_M)/2` and the Petz-corrected marginal `Φ=C* C`; the SOS identity proves `I-Ψ_M ≼ 2(I-Φ)`. This is not a proof for an arbitrary initially supplied self-compatible Φ: it constructs a different marginal `C* C`. The exact record is `sources/d-48ed31af9b1d38fd.html`, sections 3 and 3A, with the broader missing gate in `sources/N93-bounded-holevo-gates.txt`.

For qubits, the exact comparator follows by diagonalizing the real symmetric Bloch matrix of a unital Hilbert–Schmidt-self-adjoint channel with one unitary conjugation. Write the diagonal entries as `λ_i` and set `ν_i=max(2λ_i-1,0)`. Each Bell overlap `p_k` of the Choi state is at most `3/4`: the symmetric extension has equal marginals and `||P_1+P_2||=3/2` for the two maximally entangled projectors sharing a qubit. The Bell probabilities are nonnegative. If one, two, or three `λ_i` exceed `1/2`, respectively, `Σν_i≤1` follows from `λ_i≤1`, `λ_i+λ_j=2(p_0-p_k)≤3/2`, or `Σ_iλ_i=4p_0-1≤2`. Thus the Pauli channel with eigenvalues `ν_i` is EB; on each Pauli coordinate, `1-ν_i≤2(1-λ_i)`. This is an exact qubit operator inequality and therefore a uniform finite-family comparator in dimension two. It does not answer the higher-dimensional question.

The universal `1→2` qudit cloner is a stress test, not a counterexample. Its marginal has depolarizing shrinkage `λ=(d+2)/(2(d+1))`. Under the stronger operator comparator, every bistochastic EB map has Hilbert–Schmidt superoperator trace at most `d`; comparison therefore requires `c≥2`. Haar measure-and-prepare gives equality. This establishes the sharp lower endpoint for any universal `c`, while leaving the existence of finite `c≥2` for arbitrary dimension open. Parallel root workers are independently handling the cloner and other simple lift attacks, so it is not promoted as a new result.

The qubit comparator extends to a higher-dimensional exact subclass. Let `d` be prime and let a Weyl-covariant channel have one real eigenvalue `λ_L` on each nonidentity Weyl mode in each maximal-isotropic line `L`, where the `d+1` lines partition nonzero phase space. Put `S=Σ_L λ_L`. Fourier inversion gives Bell probabilities `p_0=[1+(d-1)S]/d²` and `p_g=[1+dλ_L-S]/d²` for nonidentity Bell labels `g` on line `L`. Complete positivity gives `p_g≥0`; a symmetric two-output Choi extension gives each overlap `p_g≤(d+1)/(2d)`, since the two maximally entangled projectors have overlap norm `1/d` and sum norm `1+1/d`.

Set `ν_L=(2λ_L-1)_+`. If no line is active, `Σ_Lν_L=0`. If the active set `{L: λ_L>1/2}` has size `1≤k<d+1`, let `m` be the least inactive eigenvalue. Then `m≤1/2`, CP on its Bell line gives `S≤1+dm`, and the inactive sum is at least `(d+1-k)m`. Hence `S_active≤1+(k-1)m≤(k+1)/2`, so `Σ_Lν_L≤1`. If every line is active, the overlap bound on `p_0` gives `S≤(d+2)/2`, again implying `Σ_Lν_L≤1`. For the MUB dephasing channel `Δ_L` on line `L` and complete depolarization `D`,

`Ψ=(1-Σ_Lν_L)D+Σ_Lν_L Δ_L`

is EB, has Weyl eigenvalue `ν_L` on line `L`, and satisfies `I-Ψ≤2(I-Φ)` mode by mode. The proof allows signed `λ_L`, requires real eigenvalues, and does not cover arbitrary Weyl-diagonal channels. The universal cloner attains the sharp constant 2.

The factor-two property is closed under convex mixtures: mix the compatible extensions and EB comparators with the same weights. It also closes under direct sums on the declared tracial block algebra using blockwise comparators; a full-matrix embedding needs separate control of inter-block coherences. For finite tensor products or commuting compositions whose factors have nonnegative simultaneous spectra and clipping comparators `μ=(2λ-1)_+`, the product/composition of EB comparators remains EB and the factor-two inequality survives. For a joint mode, if one factor has `λ≤1/2`, the clipped product is zero and the channel-product eigenvalue is at most `1/2`; otherwise set `x_i=2λ_i-1∈[0,1]`. Induction gives `∏_i(1+x_i)≤2^(n-1)(1+∏_i x_i)`, so `1-∏_i μ_i≤2(1-∏_i λ_i)`. This establishes closure for positive qubit factors and for the positive-spectrum line-constant Weyl subclass. We do not extend this argument to signed spectra or arbitrary noncommuting compositions.


The d=5 higher-dimensional gate now has an exact non-line-constant test. In phase space `Z_5^2`, an explicit real vector `v` with entries in `Q(√5)` is fixed by the normalized symplectic Fourier transform. Set `q_m=v_m^2/||v||^2`; the rank-one kernel `X=vv^T/||v||^2` meets the finite Weyl compatibility criterion, so the associated unital CPTP Weyl channel is exactly self-compatible and Hilbert–Schmidt self-adjoint. Its unweighted projective-line peak budget is exactly `(6,294,997 + 953,616√5)/6,303,442 = 1.336943720657926…`, refuting the tempting arbitrary-spectrum extension of the line-cover bound. However, this is still not a factor-two counterexample: a single EB map built as a rational-weighted mixture of six Weyl twirls of product Choi states satisfies `I−Ψ≼2(I−Φ)` on all 24 nonidentity Weyl modes. The fixture decimals are interpreted as exact rationals, each ket and mixture are normalized exactly, and `scripts/d5_rational_interval_comparator.py` proves all direct gaps exceed `59/2500` and all clipped-threshold gaps exceed `139/10000` by exact rational interval arithmetic. It encloses fifth-root sine/cosine radicals using rational endpoints verified by integer squaring; no floating-point step enters the inequality checks. The exact specification, derivation, and checkers are in [`broadcast_lift/D5_AUDIT.md`](broadcast_lift/D5_AUDIT.md), [`D5_AUDIT_CLAIMS.json`](broadcast_lift/D5_AUDIT_CLAIMS.json), [`scripts/d5_exact_repair_audit.py`](broadcast_lift/scripts/d5_exact_repair_audit.py), and [`scripts/d5_rational_interval_comparator.py`](broadcast_lift/scripts/d5_rational_interval_comparator.py); the compatibility-kernel criterion is Haapasalo, [Corollary 2, Eq. (22)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.06113). This closes the restricted-construction test for one explicit dimension-five map only.

A related but different construction, clipping the *composite* spectrum as `μ_g=(2λ_g-1)_+`, is not tensor-stable. Here is an exact qutrit-pair witness. Use the four MUB lines in order slope `0,1,2,∞`, and write `Δ_L` for their dephasing channels and `D_{5/8}` for the depolarizing marginal of the qutrit universal `1→2` cloner. Set

`Φ₁=(1/4)D_{5/8}+(3/4)(.03Δ₀+.92Δ₁+.04Δ₂+.01Δ∞)`,

`Φ₂=(39/50)D_{5/8}+(11/50)(.33Δ₀+.26Δ₁+.36Δ₂+.05Δ∞)`.

Each dephaser has an extension that copies its classical basis label, the cloner marginal has its standard symmetric extension, and mixing those extensions shows that both `Φ_i` are self-compatible. Their product is self-compatible as well. The exact factor spectra in Weyl-label order `(a,b)=(0,0),(0,1),…,(2,2)` are `(1,143/800,143/800,131/800,677/800,149/800,131/800,149/800,677/800)` and `(1,5601/10000,5601/10000,997/2000,5447/10000,5667/10000,997/2000,5667/10000,5447/10000)`. For the product spectrum, the candidate composite clipping has inverse-Weyl probability `−71/162000` at label `g=((2,1),(1,0))`, so it is not CP and cannot be EB. The exact rational Fourier check is in `proofs/weyl_clipping_product_counterexample.py`. This falsifies that specific one-step clipping formula, not the factor-two comparator: the tensor product of the two factor EB comparators already gives a different EB comparator satisfying the factor-two inequality by the preceding closure argument. The remaining broader target is beyond line-constant factors and beyond this comparator construction.

## Costs and supplied/acquired boundary

The shared theme is that the channel theorem starts with a supplied mathematical representation. The lift assumes the broadcaster/channel and a state-family promise; it does not price learning the channel or constructing the common measurement. The bosonic results assume a bit-level Gaussian description and charge herald size, energy, accuracy, and filter probability separately. The fermionic sharpening assumes the canonical two-form is explicitly acquired; canonical decomposition precision and downstream sampling remain charged. Those are model contracts, not measured implementations.

## Research conclusion and next gate

No branch here clears external correctness or priority review. For the central lift, the next decisive output must be one of two things: (a) a proof of the all-frame/common-Ψ operator inequality for arbitrary self-compatible Φ, or (b) a bounded-Holevo family with vanishing two-output marginal error but EB error bounded away from zero. A useful proof must explicitly bridge from an arbitrary symmetric extension to a **single** finite EB channel and preserve the slow classical directions. The next higher-dimensional attack must test unrestricted comparators: find a compatible channel for which every EB map violates the factor-two order, or prove a common EB comparator exists in the full class. The exact d=5 example refutes the line-peak bound but itself has a six-product-state EB comparator. The bounded-Holevo family implication still additionally needs a dimension-free bridge from approximate broadcasting to one common finite EB channel. Repeating the cloner lower bound, fixed-capacity Clifford examples with nonvanishing broadcast error, or unbounded-Holevo ladders does not cross that gate.

The physics branches' immediate gates are independent: specialist review of the N100 refinement and HNKS rank-change argument; a bit-complexity audit of the N42/N81 reductions; and explicit dissipative/symmetry-breaking controls or device-calibrated retention/reset costs before making implementation claims.
