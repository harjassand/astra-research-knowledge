2. VACUUM FIBERS AND THEIR EXACT CROSS-PLANE CONTRACTION

The tensor factors use their invariant symmetric-tensor Hilbert norms.
For a unit normal v let E_v=v^perp, and let W_v be the isometric inclusion

  W_v: Sym^k(E_v) -> V_(k,m), x -> x tensor bar(v)^tensor m.

Every such tensor is harmonic because x is supported on v^perp. At v=e3

identifies every other v. Thus P_v=W_v W_v^dag is the actual rank-r
projector from MIXED_FIBER_SCORE.txt. It is not a rank-one coherent projector.

As maps between the two varying fiber spaces,

  W_v^dag W_w = <bar(v)^m,bar(w)^m>
                       Sym^k(P_(E_v)|_(E_w)),
  ||W_v^dag W_w||_op <= |<v,w>|^m.                       (2.1)

Indeed symmetric power of a contraction is a contraction. Phases of v,w
affect the displayed maps but not the projectors or norm. This formula
retains the entire hot fiber and its nontrivial transport. No independent
oscillator, constant internal vector, or tensor-product channel is assumed.

For a chain of t>=2 planes and vectors x,y in this carrier, insert W_i^dag
W_(i+1) between the endpoint restrictions. Equation (2.1) gives

 |<x,P_(v1)...P_(vt)y>|

The distinction a_(k,m)/d_m=1+k/(m+2) is essential below. Replacing it

                    int P_(lambda,v) B P_(mu,v) dv.       (3.2)

All rectangles remain in the proof. No sectorwise minimum or diagonal
restriction will be made. Set E_S(B)=<B,(I-S)B>, and Lambda_S(Q) to its
minimum over all complex B on H of rank<=Q and ||B||_HS=1.

Write the t-fold map with Kraus products F_(v1)...F_(vt). For unit vectors

This also covers superpositions over sectors; there is no factor |J|,

4. A SCALAR CP2 WALK USED ONLY AFTER THE OPERATOR DOMINATION

Let h_m(v,w)=|<v,w>|^(2m), and K_m=d_m h_m. Its integral in either variable
is one (the overlap squared has density 2(1-x), 0<=x<=1). Let K_m^s denote
the kernel of s convolutions. The scalar CP2 harmonic decomposition and
elementary coherent monomial calculation give

  beta_l=m!(m+2)!/[(m-l)!(m+l+2)!], l=0,...,m,
  dim H_(l,l)=(l+1)^3,
  ||K_m^s||_infinity=sum_(l=0..m)(l+1)^3 beta_l^s.       (4.1)

Here these are eigenvalues of the SCALAR convolution K_m, not of S. To
reconstruct (4.1), use the scalar coherent symbol map T:X-><v^m|X|v^m>.
The coherent Berezin map d_m T^dag T has the beta_l eigenvalues: for the
highest operator X_l=(a1^dag)^l a3^l its symbol is
[m!/(m-l)!](conj(v1)v3)^l; Dirichlet monomial integration and
||X_l||^2=(l!)^2 binom(m+l+2,2l+2) give exactly beta_l.
The dimensions sum to d_m^2, so these are all its nonzero harmonics.
The convolution d_m T T^dag has the same nonzero spectrum. Its zonal
projection kernels have diagonal (l+1)^3 and modulus no larger than that
diagonal by Cauchy-Schwarz. Since beta_l>=0, the diagonal attains the sup
in (4.1). This reproduces the standard scalar Berezin identity without
importing a matrix-valued spectrum or a large-m limit.

The product formula gives beta_l<=exp[-l(l+2)/(m+l+2)]. For 1<=s<=m+1,
put u=s/[2(m+1)]<=1/2. Since m+l+2<=2(m+1), with j=l+1,

 sum_l(l+1)^3 beta_l^s
 <=exp(u) sum_(j>=1) j^3 exp(-u j^2)
 <=exp(1) int_0^infinity (x+1)^3 exp(-u x^2) dx
 <=16/u^2 <=64(m+1)^2/s^2.

For the middle inequality integrate on [j-1,j]: (x+1)^3>=j^3 and
exp(-u x^2)>=exp(-u j^2). The four Gaussian integrals give
1/(2u^2)+3sqrt(pi)/(4u^(3/2))+3/(2u)+sqrt(pi)/(2sqrt u), bounded by
[2+5sqrt(pi)/4]/u^2<=17/(4u^2) for u<=1. Since exp(1)<3,
their product with exp(1) is less than 16/u^2. Thus 128 below is safe.
Thus the uniform bound used here is

  ||K_m^s||_infinity/d_m <=128/s^2,
                          1<=s<=m+1.                    (4.2)
