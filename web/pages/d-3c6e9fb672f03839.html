# Weighted cubature from acquired cells: no separation hypothesis

7 October 2026. Independent mathematical reconstruction by pde_gate10, verified Sol/max. **PASS for the stated analytic interface.** Acquisition of the cells and the correction trajectory is a separate producer theorem and must be gated separately. This note supplies its Sobolev consequence and the block invariant needed for retaining old nodes.

Let sigma be probability area on S2 and let w be measurable with integral w d sigma=1 and 1/2<=w<=3/2. Put t=T+1, q=10000t, m=2q^2 and h=50/q=1/(200t). Suppose R_1,...,R_m partition the sphere, each has w-mass 1/m, each is contained in B_h(c_i), and its final node x_i lies in B_h(c_i). These are exactly geometric hypotheses; no derivative bound for w, mesh ratio or node separation is assumed. Suppose also the normalized nonconstant harmonic moment error through T is at most epsilon in Euclidean norm:

    sum_(l=1)^T sum_k |Q_X(e_lk)-integral e_lk w d sigma|^2 <= epsilon^2.

Here the e_lk are L2(sigma)-orthonormal and Q_X is the equal-weight rule. The target norm in this hypothesis is not the gradient-L1 dual norm used to prove the correction trajectory.

## Direct occupancy

If x_i is in a cap B(y,r), the entire associated cell lies in B(y,r+2h). Summing cell masses, using w<=3/2 and sigma(B(y,u))<=(u^2)/4 (with the cap understood as the whole sphere for u>=pi), gives

    Q_X(B(y,r)) <= (3/8)(r+2h)^2
                 <= (3/4)r^2+3h^2.                         (W1)

Consequently

    # {x_i in B(y,r)} <= (3/4)m r^2+15000
                      <=15000(1+m r^2).                    (W2)

This counts multiplicity and therefore does not assume that an algorithm successfully kept the nodes apart. Distinctness remains a separate required output property.

## Elementary upper band sampling

We use the previously independently gated rotational L1 Bernstein identity: for every spherical polynomial Q of degree d, every ordered k-fold word of coordinate rotation generators has L1(sigma) norm at most d^k ||Q||_1. Variable-axis geodesic Taylor expansion then gives the local-sup estimate

    (1/n_a) sum_a sup_(R_a) |Q| <= exp(3D d)||Q||_1         (W3)

for an equal-area auxiliary partition of diameter at most D. A direct proof is to majorize its kth Taylor term by D^k/k! times the sum of absolute values of the 3^k rotation words, integrate over each cell and sum. The fixed global rotation words allow the geodesic axis to vary with the cell and the integration point. This is an analytic estimate, not continuum Jacobian replacement.

For any harmonic polynomial F of degree L>=T choose the original equal-area polar partition q_a=600(L+1), n_a=2q_a^2 and D=12/q_a. Apply (W3) to Q=|F|^2, whose degree is at most 2L. Since 6DL<=3/25,

    (1/n_a) sum_a sup_(R_a) |F|^2
        <= exp(3/25)||F||_2^2 < (25/22)||F||_2^2.          (W4)

Each auxiliary cell lies in a D-cap. Formula (W1) bounds its Q_X mass, even when x_i lies on a chosen half-open cell boundary. Multiplying by n_a yields

    n_a Q_X(R_a) <=216+54[(L+1)/(T+1)]^2.

The elementary inequality (L+1)/(T+1)<=L/T, for L>=T>=1, now gives

    Q_X(|F|^2) <= (25/22)[216+54((L+1)/(T+1))^2]||F||_2^2
               < 18^2(L/T)^2||F||_2^2.                   (W5)

Cauchy–Schwarz for Q_X and ||w||_2<=3/2 give

    |Q_X(F)-integral F w d sigma| <20(L/T)||F||_2.         (W6)

## Arbitrary real Sobolev order

Use the precise sphere norm

    ||f||_(H^r)^2=sum_(l>=0,k) (1+l(l+1))^r |f_lk|^2.

Let F_low be the sharp projection through degree T, and F_j the disjoint sharp bands 2^j T<l<=2^(j+1)T, j>=0. The low-band error is at most epsilon ||f||_(H^r). Applying (W6) at L_j=2^(j+1)T and then Cauchy–Schwarz gives, for every real r>1,

    sum_j |Q_X(F_j)-integral F_j w d sigma|
      <=40 sum_j 2^j ||F_j||_2
      <= [40/sqrt(1-2^(-2(r-1)))] T^(-r) ||f||_(H^r).

The sharp bands converge uniformly and absolutely for r>1: the addition theorem bounds ||F_j||_infinity by (L_j+1)||F_j||_2, and the same geometric Cauchy–Schwarz argument converges. Thus no exchange with an unproved pointwise series is used. We obtain

    sup_(||f||_(H^r)<=1) |Q_X(f)-integral f w d sigma|
      <= epsilon+C_r T^(-r),
    C_r=40/sqrt(1-2^(-2(r-1))).                            (W7)

Constants are uniform on every compact r-interval [r0,S] in (1,infinity). This proves the weighted high-band bridge directly from acquired cells. It does not invoke the published positive-cubature theorem for sigma as though it already stated a theorem for w sigma.

## Optional exact positive repair, with the correct target

For completeness, let psi=(1,e) and let S_X=Q_X(psi psi^t)>=aI, a>0. Write r_vec=(0,Q_X(e)-integral e w d sigma). Then

    a_i=(1/m)[1-psi(x_i)^t S_X^(-1) r_vec]                (W8)

has exact total mass one and exact w-moments through T. Addition gives |psi(x_i)|=T+1. Hence if epsilon<=a/[2(T+1)], all a_i>=1/(2m). The repair is rational whenever w and the nodes are given as rational ambient polynomials/rational coordinates: replacing the normalized harmonics by any rational harmonic basis leaves (W8) invariant under congruence, and spherical polynomial integrals are rational. No square-root normalization is required for executing the repair.

The squared coefficient change is

    m sum_i (a_i-1/m)^2=r_vec^t S_X^(-1)r_vec<=epsilon^2/a.

Thus |Q_a(f)-Q_X(f)|<=epsilon P_r/sqrt(a)||f||_(H^r), where

    P_r^2=sum_(l>=0) (2l+1)/(1+l(l+1))^r
         <=4+3/[2(r-1)].                                 (W9)

Exact positive repair is an auxiliary cubature rule; the retained-node acquisition and final QMC rule remain equal-weight.

It independently proves cap mass regularity without assuming cell geometry. For n=floor(T/2), K_n(y,x)=sum_(l=0)^n (2l+1)P_l(y dot x). At distance <=1/[4(n+1)], the derivative bound |P_l'|<=l(l+1)/2 gives K_n>=3(n+1)^2/4. Exactness of K_n^2 and w<=3/2 then imply

    Q_a(B(y,1/[4(n+1)])) <=8/[3(n+1)^2].                 (W10)

Here integral K_n^2 d sigma=(n+1)^2 and its degree is <=T. If a_i>=1/(2m) and m/T^2 is bounded by an absolute constant, a spherical-cap covering argument gives the same occupancy order as (W2). The direct cell argument has simpler constants and proves (W7) without this optional repair.

## Consequence and boundary

For an inherited decomposition X_N as K blocks, replace source-level separatedness by the invariant

    # (A_nu intersect B(x,r)) <= C(1+N r^2),              (W11)

with a fixed C for every old block, x and r. A newly acquired weighted block satisfies it by (W2), with m in place of N. Upon inserting that block the new total is N'=N+m; replacing N or m by N' preserves every inequality. Thus this invariant is preserved without moving any old node. It supplies the annular row estimates used in the filtered discrepancy argument when the filter scale L^2>=N.
