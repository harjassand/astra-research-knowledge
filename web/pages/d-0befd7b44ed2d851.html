## Strongest consequence: a finite-relational-signature influence theorem

[Complete proof and boundaries](../../../notes/frontier_combin3/cycle19/RELATIONAL_INFLUENCE_AND_NOISE.md).

The family186 method extends to a fixed finite signature of q positive-arity relations, maximum arity r>=2, under independent common Bernoulli(p) coordinates and full vertex relabeling. Ordered tuples, repeats/loops and unary labels are allowed. Boolean invariant properties satisfy

`Var_p(f)<=C_{r,q} I_p(f)/(log n)^(r/(r-1))`,

with `C_{r,q}=O_r(q^(1/(r-1)))` apart from a finite small-n term. Increasing properties inherit the corresponding quantile-width bound. This is our internally reconstructed deduction from the source's restriction mechanism, not a theorem explicitly read in the release.

The new step is exact: a Fourier support of s relation coordinates uses v vertices with `s<=q v^r` and at most rs incidences, hence has a positive-degree vertex of degree at most `r q^(1/r)s^((r-1)/r)`. Conditioning outside a random vertex block preserves large coordinate orbits; capturing that one vertex transfers restricted logarithmic Fourier control to original degree `(sigma log n)^(r/(r-1))`. The common-bias cancellation is preserved.

A quantitative resampling consequence is

`Cov(f(X),f(Y))<=2r m^(-1/4)I_p(f)+exp(-tL)Var_p(f)`,

where `L=(sigma log m/(64 r q^(1/r)))^(r/(r-1))`, the block capture condition is required, and Y resamples each coordinate with probability t. In particular, if `I/Var=O(n^beta)` for `beta<a/4`, `m=floor(n^a)`, and `t(sigma log n)^(r/(r-1))->infinity`, normalized covariance vanishes.

Adversarial gates: a nullary bit disproves the extended statement if nullary symbols are admitted; a distinguished coordinate disproves it without full vertex invariance; linear real-valued coordinate sums disprove a squared-gradient spectral-gap interpretation. One-hot colors and arbitrary unequal biases require additional work. Sparse constraint thresholds with p about `n^(-(r-1))` can fall outside the useful noise regime and make the absolute width bound weak. No influence-estimation or threshold-finding algorithm has been acquired.

Exact finite identities were independently checked by direct configuration-pair enumeration and Fourier evaluation, including 512 directed-loop and64 mixed unary/binary configurations. [Evidence](influence_identity_evidence.json), [code](../../../work/frontier_combin3/cycle19/influence_identity_checks.py). These checks validate conventions and identities, not an asymptotic universal theorem.

## Executable primitive: bounded clique-free interaction decorrelation

[Complete construction, costs and obstruction](../../../notes/frontier_combin3/cycle19/EXECUTABLE_CLIQUE_FREE_DECORRELATION.md).

Family184 Lemma2.2 has been independently reconstructed and turned into an exact rational compiler. Given a promised K_r-free finite graph, positive rational weights w and rational epsilon in(0,1/2], it acquires a law with at most n+1 atoms such that

`E m_v=1`, `m_v<=2^(r(r-2))epsilon^(-(r-2))`,

`E sum_{uv in E}w_u w_v m_u m_v<=epsilon(sum_v w_v)^2`.

Its atoms select disjoint terminal blocks and use a common reciprocal-probability multiplier within each block. This yields a compact partition/probability representation and a polynomial rational-operation/bit-cost deduction for the promised-input construction. Clique recognition is separately charged O(n^r) for fixed r. The source already states the multiplier theorem; our finite-law extraction and acquisition accounting are consequences of its proof.

The exact law for K_{30,30} plus ten disjoint edges reduces expected interaction mass910→20, uses multipliers at most2, and retains a nonzero sparse remainder. An equally informed independently sampled mean-one control leaves expected mass910. All14,214 declared small-graph arithmetic cases pass, plus explicit partite examples. [Evidence](sparse_partition_evidence.json), [code](../../../work/frontier_combin3/cycle19/sparse_partition_law.py). The universal statement rests on the written induction, not those finite cases.

The global optimizer remains a real obstacle to a useful independent-set pipeline. The source uses a global maximizer of `F(w)=sum w(1-logw)-M(w)`. On K_{D,D}, the uniform weights `w=W(D)/D` satisfy every stationary equation but violate the required cross-mass bound when D is large. The exact side certificate at D=2^4096 gives `t/logt>350>256=C_4`; the point is a saddle and its potential is far below a feasible asymmetric value. [Certificate](stationary_obstruction_certificate.json), [code](../../../work/frontier_combin3/cycle19/stationary_obstruction.py). No exponentially large graph was expanded. Thus stationarity/fixed-point output cannot supply the global comparison service. The source itself makes an existence claim rather than an efficient algorithm claim.
