## Tree interface and exact coefficient

Let S be a nontrivial pure-X logical CIRCUIT support of sized>=2 (a minimum support is one admissible example). Suppose every original Z check has intersection with S of size0 or2. Group checks with the same two endpoints into an edge e; assume the distinct underlying edges form a TREE G on S. Define

    g_e(s)=sum_(checks a restricting to e)(-1)^s_a h_a.

The restrictions have rankd-1 and only the full-support binary dependence. For a proper subset A of S the energy difference relative to base s is

    E_(s+syndrome(A))-E_s=sum_(e crossing A)g_e(s).

Let t_s be the raw sum over permutations/proper-prefix denominators in the root minimal-circuit lemma. Then the exact rational identity is

    t_s=(-2)^(d-1)/product_(edges e)g_e(s).              (T1)

For positive ground weights prove this by representing each inverse positive cut energy as a Laplace integral. The signs of d-1 resolvents give(-1)^(d-1). Each permutation orders the d site-flip times; consecutive gaps have nonnegative lengths and exponent sum_(proper prefixes A_j)cutenergy(A_j)·gap_j. Fix one root site's time0. The transformation between gap coordinates and the remaining site times has unit Jacobian. Summing all order regions covers R^(d-1), except null coincidence boundaries. Thus

    (-1)^(d-1)t_0
       =integral_R^(d-1) exp[-sum_e g_e|tau_u-tau_v|]d tau.

On a tree, orient edges away from the fixed root. Their time increments u_e=tau_child-tau_parent form a unit-Jacobian invertible triangular change of variables, and the exponent separates. Every integral is2/g_e. This proves(T1) for positive g_e; equality of rational functions extends it to every generic signed base through covariance. No divergent signed integral is being used as a physical measure.

Flipping site q changes g_e to-g_e precisely on its incident edges. Consequently

    t_(s+v_q)=(-1)^deg_G(q)t_s.                         (T2)

Even-degree sites are exactly invisible at this squarefree flux order; odd-degree sites are visible for every base syndrome. A path has exactly TWO odd-degree endpoints, so the root at-least-two lemma is sharp. In particular the claim that every physical bath site has a nonzero leading counterpart-gap difference is false for this circuit interface. A vanishing leading difference does not imply exact all-order gap pairing.

The identity is cheap once the conditional graph is supplied. A fundamental logical circuit can be selected polynomially from explicit matrices, but it need not have the conditional pair-check/tree geometry; no such actual recursive geometry is assumed. ## A sufficient nonarticulation residue criterion

For a connected pair-restriction graph G with cycles, let q have degree>=2 and suppose G-q remains connected. Then q is visible as a rational-function statement, even if its degree is even. This is a sufficient criterion, not needed for the general root trace argument.

Use the independent edge-weight variables as an algebraic device. Choose nonzero signed incident weights with delta=sum_(e incident q)g_e=0, and choose all nonincident weights sufficiently large and positive. Every cut except{q} and its complement then has strictly positive energy: a nontrivial cut of the remaining connected graph crosses a large edge, which dominates the finite incident terms. Along delta=0 the base0 and q-flipped state tie, while every other state stays higher. This witness can use signed weights; its role is to prove nonidentity of a rational function, not to give an admitted physical Gibbs model.

In t_0 only permutations with q FIRST or LAST acquire a pole; a prefix can reach the q syndrome only for{q} or S\{q}, by the circuit property. The pole is simple. Every other denominator at this witness is strictly negative, so its residue terms share one sign and cannot cancel. In t_(v_q), the pole denominator reverses sign while the other intermediate energies coincide at delta=0. Pair each q-first ordering in one function with the same remaining ordering followed by q-last in the other, and vice versa. The residues are opposite. Therefore t_0-t_(v_q) has twice the nonzero residue and is not identically zero.

This proof applies to independently weighted original checks realizing the pair graph, using grouped edge sums when necessary. Covariance extends nonidentity to any base syndrome, and a nonzero rational numerator cannot vanish on every positive open weight box. It does not prove the condition G-q holds for a particular actual minimum logical support, and it does not establish other-pair spectral noncollision, fixed-unit-weight behavior or a uniform amplitude. Those remain separate questions.
