STRONGEST CLAIM / STATUS

Self-contained derived construction, pending independent audit: an arbitrary
finite-dimensional bistochastic CP map T whose range unit ball admits a
bounded-coefficient k-generator description has an actual finite EB lift,
with an explicit normalized infinity-to-one modulus independent of Hilbert
dimension and trace-block weights. An acquired finite algorithm is given;
its worst-case enumeration cost is exponential, not claimed efficient.

The generator/cover complexity dependence is essential to this proof. This
does NOT prove a dimension-free modulus for arbitrary growing CP ranges,
the unrestricted beta_m->0 theorem, or bounded-Holevo broadcasting=>EB.
The exponent 3^{-(k-1)} is already present in current almost-commuting
literature. Novelty of the lift/cost formulation is unverified.

1. EXACT INTERFACE AND MODULUS

Let A be any finite-dimensional C*-algebra with faithful normalized TRACE
tau. Let T:A->A be UCP and tau-preserving; T need not be selfadjoint,
positive as a Hilbert-space map, or compatible. All norms below are
normalized tracial norms; the input norm is ordinary operator norm.

Supply Hermitian contractions B_1,...,B_k and a verified approximation
description: for each Hermitian contraction A, there are real c_i and a
scalar a such that

  ||T(A)-a I-sum_i c_i B_i||_2 <= epsilon,
  sum_i |c_i| <= K.                                             (1)

Supply/check ||[B_i,B_j]||_2<=delta for every i,j. The c_i may depend on
A; a common CP extension of these coefficients is NOT assumed. Instead,
the original map T is preserved and only its OUTPUT is pinched.

Define F_1(delta)=0, F_k(0)=0, and for k>=2, delta>0,

  t=((k-1)delta)^(1/3),
  F_k(delta)=min{1, sqrt[t^2+F_(k-1)(min{2,delta+4t})^2]}.          (2)

There is a maximal abelian subalgebra D within A, with trace-preserving
conditional expectation P_D, such that

  max_i ||B_i-P_D(B_i)||_2 <= F_k(delta).                         (3)

Consequently E=P_D T is UCP, tau-preserving, and EB, with

  sup_(A=A*, ||A||<=1) ||E(A)-T(A)||_1
        <= epsilon+K F_k(delta).                               (4)

The epsilon coefficient is ONE: I-P_D is an L2 orthogonal projection,
so ||(I-P_D)R||_2<=||R||_2 for the approximation remainder in (1).
For fixed k, F_k(delta)->0, independent of matrix dimension, block weights,
number of inputs, input labels, spectral gaps, or smallest eigenvalue.

There are explicit constants C_k with F_k(delta)<=C_k delta^alpha_k,
alpha_k=3^{-(k-1)}. One permissible recurrence is C_1=0 and

 C_k^2=(k-1)^(2/3)
       + C_(k-1)^2 [1+4(k-1)^(1/3)]^(2/3^(k-2)).                (5)

For delta<=1, use delta+4t <= [1+4(k-1)^(1/3)]delta^(1/3).
For delta>=1, F_k<=1<=C_k delta^alpha_k (k>=2). Formula (5) and induction
prove the claim. The recurrence, rather than an optimized constant, is
the reproducible modulus.

2. ONE-STEP PINCHING: FULL DERIVATION

Work first in a full matrix block, or in a direct sum of already selected
blocks. Diagonalize a Hermitian contraction X within those blocks, with
eigenvalues x_a. Choose width h>0 and a shift u uniform in [0,h). Partition
the spectrum into half-open intervals [u+zh,u+(z+1)h), keeping the existing
block labels. Let P_u be the associated block pinching.

Two eigenvalues a,b land in different intervals with probability

  Pr_u(separated)=min{1,|x_a-x_b|/h} <= |x_a-x_b|/h.             (6)

For any Hermitian contraction Y, writing its entries in this eigenbasis
and including the central trace weights w_a constant in each block,

 E_u ||Y-P_u(Y)||_2^2
  = sum_(a,b) w_a |Y_ab|^2 Pr_u(separated)
  <= h^(-1) sum_(a,b) w_a |x_a-x_b| |Y_ab|^2
  <= h^(-1) ||[X,Y]||_2 ||Y||_2
  <= delta/h.                                                 (7)

The second inequality is scalar Cauchy-Schwarz with factors
sqrt(w_a)|x_a-x_b||Y_ab| and sqrt(w_a)|Y_ab|. This derivation uses
normalization tau(I)=1 and contraction Y, not the number of entries.

For Y_1,...,Y_(k-1), sum (7). Some single shift u has

  sum_j ||Y_j-P_u(Y_j)||_2^2 <= (k-1)delta/h.                   (8)

Replace X on each new block by that interval's midpoint, clipped to
[-1,1], obtaining X_0. It is central in the new block algebra, a Hermitian
contraction, commutes with X, and ||X-X_0||_infty<=h/2. Let
Y'_j=P_u(Y_j). Every Y'_j remains a Hermitian contraction because pinching
is UCP. Choose h=((k-1)delta)^(1/3)=t. Then each ||Y_j-Y'_j||_2<=t.

For any j,l,

  ||[Y'_j,Y'_l]||_2
    <= ||[Y_j,Y_l]||_2
       +2||Y'_j-Y_j||_2+2||Y'_l-Y_l||_2
    <= delta+4t.                                              (9)

The universal contraction bound also gives <=2. All statements hold with
arbitrary faithful central block weights. A SINGLE global shift can be
chosen by minimizing the total weighted loss; no division by a small
block weight is needed.

3. RECURSION AND EB LIFT: FULL DERIVATION

Induct inside the new finite block algebra on the k-1 compressed Y'_j,
using its restricted faithful trace. This yields a MASA and expectation
P' there, with maximum displacement <=F_(k-1)(min{2,delta+4t}). Extend its
diagonal expectation to all of A. The final P_D refines P_u.

Because X_0 is block-central, P_D(X_0)=X_0 and

  ||X-P_D(X)||_2<=||X-X_0||_2<=h/2.                            (10)

For every Y_j, the two terms in

  Y_j-P_D(Y_j)=(Y_j-P_u(Y_j))+(Y'_j-P'(Y'_j))

are orthogonal in L2(tau): the first is off the new blocks, the second
lies inside them. Thus

  ||Y_j-P_D(Y_j)||_2^2
     <=t^2+F_(k-1)(min{2,delta+4t})^2.                         (11)

Every conditional expectation is an L2 orthogonal projection. Therefore
||B_i-P_D(B_i)||_2<=||B_i||_2<=1, giving the minimum in (2). For k=1,
diagonalize B_1 exactly. For delta=0, simultaneously diagonalize the
commuting family exactly. This completes the induction for (3).

Let Q_z be the minimal projections of D and p_z=tau(Q_z)>0. Then

  P_D(X)=sum_z tau(Q_z X) Q_z/p_z,
  E(A)=sum_z tau(M_z A) f_z,
  M_z=T_dagger(Q_z)>=0, sum_z M_z=I, f_z=Q_z/p_z>=0,
  tau(f_z)=1.                                                 (12)

Here dagger is the tracial adjoint. CP of T implies positivity of its
weighted adjoint; tau-preservation makes T_dagger unital. Formula (12)
is an explicit finite measurement/prepare channel, not a merely positive
approximation to an operator system. There are at most sum_b d_b outcomes
for A=directsum_b M_(d_b). The actual physical form for a central faithful
reference sigma is rho ->sum_z Tr(M_z rho) sigma f_z. Input pinching into
A extends this to an ordinary full-domain EB channel on ambient matrices.

P_D fixes scalars. Apply I-P_D to (1), use (3), triangle inequality,
orthogonal-projection contraction for the remainder, and ||.||_1<=||.||_2.
This proves (4). No trace-norm/measurement maximization is moved through
an average; ONE final P_D works on every bounded input.

4. TWO USEFUL COMPLEXITY COROLLARIES

(a) If the real Hermitian range of T has rank r, compactness gives a
maximum-volume basis B_1,...,B_r selected from T's image unit ball.
Relative to ANY coordinate projection that is injective on that range,
replace one basis column by X=T(A). Its determinant is c_i times the
maximal determinant, so |c_i|<=1. Therefore (1) holds exactly with K=r,
epsilon=0. If the ENTIRE range's commutator supremum is <=delta, (4) gives

      inf_EB ||E-T||_(infty->1, Hermitian) <= r F_r(delta).      (13)

This is uniform for bounded r; r may not be silently bounded as dimension
grows. An acquired finite rational-net version below has K<=2r.

(b) Supply an L2 epsilon-net {B_i}_{i=1}^N of T's entire Hermitian unit
ball image, with every B_i itself such an image. Use K=1 in (1), with one
nonzero coefficient. Then

      inf_EB ||E-T||_(infty->1) <= epsilon+F_N(delta).           (14)

Thus a supplied/acquired covering profile N(epsilon) yields an explicit
modulus, even when literal range rank grows. An empirical net without a
uniform covering certificate does not meet this premise.

Apply to T=Phi^m in the campaign's positive selfadjoint selfcompatible
class. The baseline proves delta_m<=2/sqrt(m+1)+4/(m+1). If range rank
r is bounded, (13) proves beta_m->0 for that subclass. If r or N grows,
one needs r F_r(delta_m)->0 or epsilon_m+F_(N_m)(delta_m)->0 respectively.
The exponent makes the resulting allowed growth very restrictive. At
delta=10^-12, the direct recurrence already gives F_5=1. This proof does
not establish the arbitrary growing-range theorem.

5. ACQUISITION AND COSTS — NO FREE FRAME/NET/EIGENBASIS

Finite rational implementation interface: supply or acquire the full
rational superoperator of T:M_a->M_b with input/output bit precision L;
check its Choi PSD, unital and trace-preserving equations. An EXACT full
matrix-output oracle needs a^2 public Hermitian basis queries to acquire
it. Finite-precision noisy queries do not give the exact interface without
an additional verified uncertainty envelope.

Select r independent coordinate rows and columns by rational Gaussian
elimination. Public Hermitian basis inputs e_ii, e_ij+e_ji,
i(e_ji-e_ij) are contractions. Let mu>0 be the absolute determinant of
their selected r-coordinate output matrix. This is a computed positive
rational lower bound on the optimum basis determinant.

Choose rational zeta<=min{1,mu/[4 r^(1+r/2)]}; replacing the radical by
a larger integer in this denominator is harmless. Enumerate Hermitian
input matrices G with each real coordinate on a mesh xi=zeta/(4a) in
[-1,1]. Retain those with -(1+zeta/2)I<=G<=(1+zeta/2)I, checked exactly
by rational PSD calculations, and use G/(1+zeta/2). This is an operator-
norm zeta-net of the input unit ball: entry rounding costs <=zeta/2 in
operator norm, and scaling costs <=zeta/2. It has at most

      M <= (1+8a/zeta)^(a^2)                                 (15)

points. UCP contraction makes its output a coordinatewise zeta-net.
Enumerate every r-tuple and choose maximum determinant D. Multilinearity
and Hadamard give total determinant approximation error <=
r^(1+r/2)zeta<=mu/4, so D>=3mu/4. For ANY output X and any basis-column
replacement, its nearest net replacement has determinant <=D, and the
one-column perturbation costs <=r^(r/2)zeta<=mu/(4r). Thus

      |c_i|<=1+1/(3r), sum_i |c_i|<=r+1/3<=2r.                (16)

This acquires a frame with K<=2r. Naive cost: M image evaluations and
M^r exact r-by-r determinants; every matrix entry, denominator and
determinant is charged. Reading a full explicit T costs O(a^2 b^2)
coefficients. log(1/zeta) is controlled by the computed mu and polynomial
determinant bit height, but M is exponential in a^2 and can be enormous.
No polynomial-time frame search is claimed or executed.

For a SUPPLIED frame, the spectral construction uses <=k-1 pinching
stages. At one stage its shifted partition changes only at eigenvalue
residues modulo h, at most sum_b d_b breakpoints. Evaluate the loss on
each interval and choose a minimizing shift. Dense real-arithmetic cost
is bounded by O(k^2 D^3), D=sum_b d_b; storage O(k D^2), plus the map.
This real-arithmetic statement does not assert polynomial bit complexity
of iterative exact algebraic diagonalization.

A gap-independent finite-bit EXISTENCE/search fallback avoids silently
charging inverse eigenvalue gaps: enumerate complex entry-grid matrices
Q approximating unitaries, discard ill-conditioned Q, and use the exact
polar unitary V=Q(Q*Q)^(-1/2). If ||Q-U||<=eta/4 then ||V-U||<=eta/2.
The corresponding diagonal expectations satisfy

      ||P_V(X)-P_U(X)||_2<=4||V-U|| ||X||_2.                   (17)

This follows by telescoping the two unitary conjugations around ordinary
diagonal pinching. A mesh eta/(8D) gives a search space at most
(1+16D/eta)^(2D^2). Exact algebraic calculations/interval certification
select a V passing frame-error threshold F_k(delta)+8eta. Formula (12)
then supplies actual positive effects and preparations. Its additional
uniform error is <=8K eta. This costs finite algebraic root isolation
and exponential search, not inverse unspecified spectral gaps. No full
certified algebraic engine or hardware compiler was implemented here.

The EB descriptor has <=D outcomes, D preparations, and D effect matrices;
in full M_d this is O(d^3) stored coefficients if effects are expanded,
and Kraus rank <=d^2. Keeping T and the diagonalizing unitary as a factored
descriptor reduces storage, but operational POVM/preparation/precision
costs remain charged. No dimension-independent apparatus size or energy
bound is claimed. Backend energy, token usage and hardware runtime are
UNKNOWN. Local diagnostics were bounded CPU work on the shared M4.
