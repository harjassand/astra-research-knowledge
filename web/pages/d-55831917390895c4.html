Disposition: no algebraic or quantifier defect found, conditional on the
named fixed-g Lieb-Liniger partition convergence, Tonks limit as g->infinity,
and free XX partition convergence.

The strict decrease d_j=f_infinity(g_j)-f_infinity(g_j+1)>0 is supported
by positive contact in the two-particle ground state. If that ground state

eigen-equation; its positive overlap with the free ground state forces it
to be the free ground state, which has nonzero contact. Hence the q=2
ground energy strictly increases with g, while all sector energies are
nondecreasing, so the positive grand partition strictly decreases.

For each j, fixed-g convergence at both g_j and g_j+1 gives a single
threshold valid for every later L such that
f_L(g_j)-f_L(g_j+1)>=d_j/2. Convexity yields

on finite blocks while still ensuring g_L->infinity and g_L/L->0.

The construction is existential, not an effective numerical schedule;
the source convergence results provide no explicit threshold. It refutes
uniform relative-entropy convergence across all g_L->infinity sequences,
not trace-norm convergence. Do not state rho_{L,0} as the g=0 resonant
state: it is the physical V=0 reference, corresponding to g=4JL.
