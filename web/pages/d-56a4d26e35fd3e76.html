Claim: Let U_s in U(n), supplied skew-adjoint corrections b_s satisfy max_s||b_s||_{2,n}<=C epsilon, and w be a relator word of length L. Define F_b(t)=w((e^{t b_s}U_s)_s), D=F_b(0)-I, and L_U(b)=F_b\prime(0). Assume E=D+L_U(b) satisfies ||E||_{2,n}<=eta. For tau,sigma>0 the report claims an exact decomposition F_b(1)-I=R_diffuse+R_debt with ||R_diffuse||_{2,n}<=eta+(L^2/2)tau C epsilon, rank(R_debt)/n<=2L|S|C^2 epsilon^2/tau^2+eta^2/sigma^2, and ||R_debt||_op<=2+sigma+(L^2/2)tau^2. Each corrected generator remains unitary and moves by at most C epsilon in normalized Hilbert-Schmidt norm.
Interface: Corrections b_s are supplied, skew-adjoint, and small in normalized Hilbert-Schmidt norm; the linearized residual E is also supplied through the assumption ||E||<=eta. The word length L, number of generators |S|, and cutoffs tau,sigma are explicit. This is not an algorithm for finding b_s.
Proof spine: The source splits b_s=l_s+h_s by spectral cutoff tau, bounds rank(h_s)/n by C^2 epsilon^2/tau^2, Taylor-expands the low-frequency path, telescopes high-frequency and inverse-letter changes into a rank-bounded term, and splits E by singular-value threshold sigma. The diffuse and debt terms are defined by that construction.
Costs: No end-to-end runtime or method for acquiring b_s is provided. For tau=epsilon^alpha the diffuse nonlinear term is O(epsilon^(1+alpha)) and rank debt O(epsilon^(2-2alpha))n, but the bounded debt may have Hilbert-Schmidt size O(epsilon^(1-alpha)), larger than epsilon.
Failure: Existence of suitable corrections, admissibility of the linear residual, and a compatible o(n)-enlargement that removes the debt are unproved. The source explicitly says this is not a superlinear total-error bound and supplies no flexible-stability conclusion.
Closest comparator: This is a conditional matrix update, not a proof of the flexible group-stability target. N607 separately records why generic low-rank debt cannot be completed for all approximants.
Open verification: Independently reconstruct the spectral truncation and Taylor estimates, especially inverse letters, the 2L|S| rank factor, operator-norm debt, and the singular-value split. Then identify a justified source of corrections and a compatible completion theorem before using it for group stability.
