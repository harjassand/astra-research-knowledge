TARGET (NOT A PREMISE)

Let sigma be any faithful finite stationary density of a CPTP QMS, with
positive Heisenberg generator L, exp(-tL) unital CP. KMS symmetry is NOT
assumed. Write s=sqrt(sigma), d=s^(1/2), G(X)=dXd, H=G L G^(-1).
For every faithful rho and every finite untouched reference, the question is

 J_L(rho)=Tr L_*(rho)(log(rho)-log(sigma))
                  >=(pi/2) E_sym(rho),
 E_sym(rho)=Re Tr sqrt(rho)H(sqrt(rho)).

Reference weights are faithful products tau_R tensor sigma, and the full rho
may be entangled. Stationarity and unitality give H(s)=H^*(s)=0, but H is
generally not self-adjoint. The actual KT E_1 for L uses the KMS-adjoint
physical entropy production, not J_L. No KMS endpoint is silently transferred.

1. EXACT PHYSICAL WEIGHTED FORM AND STATIONARITY

Start from the actual finite Lindblad form
 L_*(X)=(CX+XC^*)/2-sum_j K_j X K_j^*,
 (C+C^*)/2=sum_j K_j^*K_j.
Thus the physical Hamiltonian is retained in Im(C). Set
 B_j=d^(-1)K_j d, A=d^(-1) C d.
The weighted Heisenberg generator is

 H(X)=(A^*X+XA)/2-sum_j B_j^* X B_j.

Define P=sum B_j^*sB_j and Q=sum B_j s B_j^*. Then

 sA+A^*s=2P,              As+sA^*=2Q.                  (1)

In the s eigenbasis, for s_i!=s_k,
 A_ik=2(s_i P_ik-s_k Q_ik)/(s_i^2-s_k^2),
 V=(A+A^*)/2=Lyap_s^(-1)(P+Q),
 (A-A^*)_ik/2=(P_ik-Q_ik)/(s_i-s_k).
On equal-s blocks, stationarity requires P=Q; the commuting skew part of A
is an arbitrary Hamiltonian commuting with sigma. It contributes zero to
both J_L and E_sym, so it may be omitted from these forms. This is an actual
stationarity constraint, not an arbitrary noise/Hamiltonian choice.

For one noise B, let D=B^*. The finite frequency decompositions are
 [log(s),B_alpha]=alpha B_alpha,
 D_alpha=B_{-alpha}^*, [log(s),D_alpha]=alpha D_alpha.
No relation B_alpha^*=B_{-alpha} is assumed. For m=(alpha+gamma)/2 and
h=(alpha-gamma)/2, the symmetric weighted potential has coefficient

 V=sum_{j,alpha,gamma} [exp(m)/(2cosh(h))]
                     (B_{j,alpha}^*B_{j,gamma}
                            +D_{j,alpha}^*D_{j,gamma}). (2)

This agrees with the legal KMS symmetrization, but forward entropy retains
the entire skew stationary potential; (2) alone is insufficient for J_L.

2. EXACT FORWARD RELATIVE-ENTROPY POTENTIAL

Physical entropy production splits as

 J_L=Tr rho[ (sum K_j^*K_j) log(rho)
                         -sum K_j^* log(rho)K_j ]
          +Tr rho S_sigma,
 S_sigma=sum 2 K_j^* log(s)K_j-(C^*log(s)+log(s)C).      (3)

The physical Hamiltonian disappears from the log(rho) trace by cyclicity;
its log(s) contribution is STILL in S_sigma. It cannot be discarded before
the exact stationarity substitution. Using (1) for nonzero frequencies gives
the FULL coefficient identity

 S_sigma=sum_{j,alpha,gamma} 2exp(m)
      [(m-h coth(2h)) B_{j,alpha}^*B_{j,gamma}
             +h csch(2h) D_{j,alpha}^*D_{j,gamma}].     (4)

The coefficients are analytic at h=0: h coth(2h)->1/2 and
h csch(2h)->1/2. For a zero-frequency product, the exact stationarity
condition is sum_{j,alpha} exp(alpha)
(B_{j,alpha}^*B_{j,alpha}-D_{j,alpha}^*D_{j,alpha})=0.
It cancels the extra analytic-limit terms in (4), yielding the actual
zero-frequency term 2 sum alpha exp(alpha)B_alpha^*B_alpha from (3).
Equal-s blocks obey the same projected identity. Thus (4) covers diagonal
and degenerate stationary blocks, not just distinct frequencies.

Explicit coefficient check away from h=0: writing X=B_alpha^*B_gamma,
the physical potential C has X coefficient -exp(m-2h)/sinh(2h);
the D_alpha^*D_gamma coefficient is exp(m)/sinh(2h). Therefore the B
part of C^*log(s)+log(s)C is
2exp(m)log(s)X+2h exp(m+2h)X/sinh(2h), while
2K^*log(s)K has coefficient 2exp(m)(log(s)+alpha)X.
Their difference is 2exp(m)(m-h coth(2h))X.
The D contribution is 2h exp(m)D_alpha^*D_gamma/sinh(2h).
This separately verifies the sign and both physical-potential terms in (4).

3. FULL-STATE ORDERED-PAIR KERNEL, WITH COMPLEX NON-HERMITIAN NOISE

Diagonalize the FULL rho, rho=sum_i lambda_i |i><i|, lambda_i>0.
Let beta_ik=(log(lambda_i)-log(lambda_k))/2 and
c_alpha(i,k)=<i|B_alpha|k>. The log(rho) term of (3) has coefficient
-2lambda_k beta_ik exp(m) conjugate(c_alpha)c_gamma.
The B part of (4) combines with it to give
2lambda_k exp(m)(m-beta_ik-h coth(2h)).
The D part is reflected under
(i,k,alpha,gamma)->(k,i,-gamma,-alpha).
Because D_alpha=B_{-alpha}^*, the reflection converts its matrix-element
product exactly to the B product, while lambda_k becomes lambda_i and
exp(m) becomes exp(-m). Its coefficient becomes
2lambda_i exp(-m)h csch(2h).

Put x=beta_ik-alpha, y=beta_ik-gamma, z=(x+y)/2, w=(x-y)/2.
The resulting exact FORWARD kernel candidate is

 K_fwd(x,y)=2[ exp(-z)(-z-w coth(2w))
                           +exp(z)w csch(2w) ],         (5)
 K_fwd(x,x)=exp(x)-(1+2x)exp(-x).

The entire entropy production, with factor ONE, is

 J_L(rho)=sum_{j,i,k} sqrt(lambda_i lambda_k)
   sum_{alpha,gamma} conjugate(c_{j,alpha}(i,k))
       K_fwd(beta_ik-alpha,beta_ik-gamma)c_{j,gamma}(i,k). (6)

The same reflection in (2), and the direct jump term, give EXACTLY

 E_sym(rho)=sum_{j,i,k} sqrt(lambda_i lambda_k)
   sum_{alpha,gamma} conjugate(c_{j,alpha}(i,k))
       L_0(beta_ik-alpha,beta_ik-gamma)c_{j,gamma}(i,k),
 L_0(x,y)=cosh((x+y)/2)/cosh((x-y)/2)-1.                (7)

All full-state pairs are ordered, including i=k. Coefficients are arbitrary
complex matrix elements of the ACTUAL non-Hermitian physical weighted noise.
Reference factors commute with local B and A; the same derivation uses the
full joint rho spectrum and exact product stationary weight. The local
generator conserves every reference observable, so the log(tau_R) entropy
term is exactly zero. No fixed reference marginal is assumed.
