# Arbitrary-length normalized shear words and a second coefficient filter

## Exact class and principal theorem

Let A=C[x,y]. On the residual plane R=(rho,eta), allow any finite word in the two families

    U_f:(rho,eta)->(rho+f(b,eta),eta),
    V_f:(rho,eta)->(rho,eta+f(b,rho)),
    f in A[z],  f(b,0)=f_z(b,0)=0.                    (W1)

There is no limit on word length, fibre degrees or polynomial base coefficients. The coefficient identities hold at every base, not only at the known witnesses. Zero factors are deleted; consecutive factors of the same family combine by addition and may cancel. Hence every nonempty reduced word alternates families and has nonzero shear polynomials of fibre degree at least two. This reduction is elementary and uses no plane-automorphism classification.

Let T=(X,Y) be such a word, acting in the displayed functional order, and put

    psi=h(b)+G1(b)X+G2(b)Y.                            (W2)

For a reduced word of length at least two, a constant Hessian determinant forces the coefficient G_i of its last updated output to be a polynomial in one affine-linear base form. This holds for arbitrary h and nonzero G1,G2. Thus if both coefficients are noncylindrical, every reduced word of length at least two fails. The one-letter and zero-letter cases are separately checked below for the actual D2.

**Principal theorem.** With the acquired D2 and G=(2y,4x)D2 of cycle14, every word in (W1), including the empty word, gives a nonconstant reduced four-dimensional Hessian determinant. This extends the two-shear obstruction to arbitrary word length and arbitrary base-dependent tails, while preserving the actual source collision.

This is a theorem about the explicitly generated normalized coordinate-axis shear subgroup. It is not about all polynomial fibre automorphisms, all tame words with arbitrary intervening linear maps, a changed first jet, base/pivot-moving charts or arbitrary direct target repairs. In particular, tameness over the fraction field C(x,y) cannot turn a general source chart into this polynomial, zero-jet word class.

## Global legality and actual collision

Each generator in (W1) has polynomial inverse obtained by subtracting f. Reverse the word and use these inverses to obtain T^-1. It fixes the residual origin and has residual derivative I at that origin at every base; its Jacobian determinant is one. For the actual source set

    Theta_b=D2(x)T,
    f0=t^2+a(b)t+(2y,4x)Theta_b(R),
    beta=y^2(6x^2y^2+15xy+4),  a=13+2beta,
    sigma=-19/2,
    t*=(sigma-a)/2=-45/4-beta,
    h0=-(beta+45/4)^2.                                 (W3)

The pinned U,C normalizers of the primary Meng–Yang potential complete a global polynomial full source map. Here the OLD base and pivot are fixed; the map is t-independent. Its inverse is explicit from D2^-1, the reversed shear word and the inherited normalizers. The actual critical section is (W3), and the reduced potential is (W2) with h=h0.

The matrix and coefficient row are

    D2=[-x^2/2+x+25/2   x^2-17;
         37/4-x^2/4    x^2/2+x-25/2],
    G1=-x^3-x^2y+2xy+37x+25y,
    G2= 2x^3+2x^2y+4x^2-50x-34y.                     (W4)

At b+=(1,-3/2), b-=(-1,3/2), R=0, t*=0 and both reduced gradients are (0,0,-3,4); the common full transformed gradient is (0,0,-19/2,-3,4). All words retain this exact equality because their global first fibre jets are I. Every word also satisfies (psi_rho,psi_eta)=(x,y), since its residual Jacobian has polynomial inverse. This ideal assertion is specific to the old-base/old-pivot-fixed interface; it is not an assertion about arbitrary legal source automorphisms.

Nonlinear source changes do not preserve a constant full Hessian determinant. We require and test the actual four-dimensional determinant rather than transferring the primary five-dimensional determinant by coordinate invariance.

## Leading degree and derivative-gap induction

Assume first that the word starts with U_F. Undo this first shear by

    Phi(b,s,eta)=(b,s-F(b,eta),eta).                   (W5)

After the first generator the pair is (s,eta). The next generator is V_(f2), with d2=deg_z f2>=2. Its updated coordinate is eta+f2(b,s), whose s degree is d2, whose eta derivative has s degree zero, and whose second eta derivative is zero. The other coordinate s has both eta derivatives zero.

For the k-th generator, k>=2, let n_k be the s degree of the coordinate just updated. If a_k(b) is the leading coefficient of its polynomial f_k, then

    n_k=product_(j=2..k) d_j,
    c_2=a_2,
    c_k=a_k c_(k-1)^d_k,                              (W6)

where c_k is the nonzero leading s coefficient. The other coordinate has degree n_(k-1), using n_1=1. The added old coordinate has smaller degree than d_k n_(k-1), so it cannot cancel the new leading term. All coefficients in (W6) are polynomials in b, independent of eta.

For either coordinate after the k-th generator, if its s degree is n, the derivative bounds are

    deg_s partial_eta <= n-d2,
    deg_s partial_eta^2 <= n-2d2.                     (W7)

The degree of the zero polynomial is -infinity. The bounds are initialized just after the second generator; the unchanged s coordinate has zero eta derivatives. To pass from a dominant coordinate Z of degree n to f_k(Z), the chain rule gives

    partial_eta f_k(Z)=f'_k(Z) Z_eta,
    partial_eta^2 f_k(Z)
           =f''_k(Z) Z_eta^2+f'_k(Z) Z_etaeta.

Their degree bounds are respectively d_k n-d2 and d_k n-2d2. The old coordinate added to this expression already has smaller s degree and satisfies the same weaker new bounds. This proves the induction for arbitrary finite length; no sampled word lengths substitute for it.

Let D=n_m for the last generator, and let i be its updated output. For

    V(b,s,eta)=psi(Phi(b,s,eta))

one therefore has

    deg_s V=D>=2,
    V=E(b)s^D+O(s^(D-1)),   E=G_i c_m nonzero,
    deg_s V_eta <=D-d2 <=D-2,
    deg_s V_etaeta <=D-2d2 <=D-4.                     (W8)

The coefficient of s^(D-1) is consequently independent of eta. If the word starts with V_F, exchange residual names and G1,G2; the same argument applies. Only a constant permutation is used, not a rational source transformation.

## Corrected Hessian and arbitrary-length filter

The exact nonlinear chain rule is

    JPhi^T (Hess_old psi) JPhi=Hess_new V+V_s Hess F.  (W9)

Its determinants agree because det JPhi=1. In the order (x,y,s,eta), write the corrected matrix

    [ B    A    C ]
    [ A^T  U    Y ]
    [ C^T  Y    Z ],

    B=Hess_b V+V_s Hess_b F,
    A=grad_b V_s,
    C=grad_b V_eta+V_s grad_b F_eta,
    U=V_ss,  Y=V_seta,  Z=V_etaeta+V_s F_etaeta.      (W10)

Using (W8), its leading entries and bounds are

    B=(Hess E)s^D+O(s^(D-1)),
    A=D grad E s^(D-1)+O(s^(D-2)),
    C=D E grad F_eta s^(D-1)+O(s^(D-2)),
    U=D(D-1)E s^(D-2)+O(s^(D-3)),
    Y=O(s^(D-3)),
    Z=D E F_etaeta s^(D-1)+O(s^(D-2)).                (W11)

For a 2x2 B the complete determinant identity is

    det=(UZ-Y^2)det B-Z A^T adj(B)A-U C^T adj(B)C
                 +2Y A^T adj(B)C+(det[A,C])^2.       (W12)

The possible degree 4D-3 is supplied only by UZ det B and -Z A^T adj(B)A. The other four terms have degrees at most 4D-6, 4D-4, 4D-5 and 4D-4 respectively. Thus the exact highest possible coefficient is

    [s^(4D-3)]det Hess psi(Phi)
       =D^2 E F_etaeta M_D(E),
    M_D(E)=(D-1)E det Hess_b E
                  -D grad E^T adj(Hess_b E)grad E.   (W13)

The independently gated cylinder lemma says M_D(E)=0 precisely when E is a polynomial in one affine-linear form. Every divisor of such a polynomial is a product of parallel affine lines. Since G_i divides E, constant determinant therefore requires the final coefficient G_i itself to be cylindrical. This is the arbitrary-length filter. It also forces all a_2,...,a_m in (W6) into that same one-variable ring when the nonconstant G_i fixes its direction; no such conclusion about the FIRST shear's leading coefficient follows from (W13) alone.

For D2, the gradient coefficient vectors of G1 at x^2 and xy are (-3,-1) and (-2,0), with minor -2. For G2 they are (6,2) and (4,0), with minor -8. Both are noncylindrical. Since F_etaeta is nonzero by the zero first jet and nonzero shear, (W13) is a nonzero polynomial for every reduced word of length at least two.

For one generator, the potential is affine in the updated residual. The coefficient of that residual in its full determinant is -G_i f'' K_(G_i), where K_P=grad P^T adj(Hess P)grad P. For D2, K_(G1)(0)=-3700 and K_(G2)(0)=9248, so both one-letter cases fail. For the empty word the determinant is (Jac G)^2, with

    Jac G=-4[x^3+x^2y+10x^2+8xy+25x+17y+2],

which is nonconstant. This proves the principal theorem for every finite word, including cancellations. It does not use a statement that every polynomial fibre automorphism belongs to this subgroup.

## A second coefficient when the cylindrical filter passes

Suppose the final coefficient is nonconstant cylindrical. By a constant linear base frame, take E=E(x); no rational coordinate change is used. Define

    J_D(E)=D(E')^2-(D-1)E E'',
    Q(b)=[s^(D-1)]V.                                 (W14)

The degree gap makes Q independent of eta. If E has positive degree e in x, the leading coefficient of J_D(E) is proportional to e(e+D-1), so it is nonzero. Expanding (W12) one degree lower gives

    [s^(4D-4)]det
      =D^2 E J_D(E)
          [D E(F_yeta)^2-F_etaeta(Q_yy+D E F_yy)]
      =-D^2 E J_D(E)
          [D E det Hess_(y,eta) F+F_etaeta Q_yy].      (W15)

Here only the next yy entry of B and the leading x entries of B,A plus the leading y entry of C matter. They are respectively Q_yy+D E F_yy, E'', D E', and D E F_yeta. Contributions involving Y or a subleading xy/A_y entry are of degree at most 4D-5. Equation (W15) is independent of arbitrary h.

Constancy therefore forces

    det Hess_(y,eta) F+F_etaeta Q_yy/(D E)=0           (W16)

over C(x)[y,eta]. This is a proof field, not an acquired rational source chart. Since F and F_eta vanish identically at eta=0, let r>=2 be its least eta exponent. The determinant on the left of (W16) has eta order at least 2r-2, while the second summand has order r-2 unless Q_yy=0. Thus Q_yy=0 and det Hess_(y,eta)F=0.

This last equation forces F independent of y by an elementary coefficient argument specialized to its global zero jets. If m=deg_y F>=1, write its leading y coefficient as A(eta). It has eta order at least2 and positive degree n. The coefficient of y^(2m-2) in det Hess F is

    m[(m-1)A A''-m(A')^2].                           (W17)

Its leading eta coefficient is a nonzero scalar times -m n(n+m-1). It cannot vanish. Hence m=0.

**Additional necessary conditions.** Every constant-determinant word passing the nonconstant cylindrical coefficient filter must have its first normalized shear independent of the transverse base coordinate y, and Q must be affine in y. This strengthens the earlier leading filter without assuming a binary Hesse theorem. Further coefficients may still impose constraints; no sufficiency or all changed-jet classification is asserted.
