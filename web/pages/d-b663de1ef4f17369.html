# First derivative extension: separate proposed deduction

7 October2026. This is a further deduction from the audited weighted construction, not FI's stated theorem and not yet independently checked. It must be gated separately from WEIGHTED_LANDIS_HOSTILE_AUDIT.md.

Claim: the same potential can satisfy

    |grad V(x)|<=C_K kappa(x)*a(x)
               =C_K R(x)^((1-5beta)/3).                (1)

For beta>=1/5 this gives a globally bounded first derivative. For beta>1/5, both V and grad V tend to zero, while u retains superexponential radial decay. No statement about bounded derivatives of every order is made.

## Reconstruction

All notation and fixed parameters are as in the main audit. The partition has derivative estimates of every order, by its smooth fixed-bump construction. Since a*ell>=1, differentiating the patched oscillation three times gives |D^3 q|<=C a^3. Direct differentiation of the regularized radial phase gives |D^j Phi|<=C a/R^(j-1) for j=1,2,3. Thus |D^3 f|<=C a^3 E, with f=E q.

The sharper differentiated residual is

    |grad Delta f|<=C_K kappa*a*E.                     (2)

To prove this, use the fully expanded canceled residual, rather than differentiating the crude bound |D^2 f|<=a^2 E. Its four constituents are:

1. Delta q+a^2q, expanded into the frequency mismatch, partition-gradient and partition-Laplacian terms. The mismatch coefficient has size O(kappa) and derivative O(a^2/R)<=O(kappa*a). Its differentiated cutoff and cosine factors cost at most ell^(-1)<=a and a. For the gradient-cutoff terms, one derivative costs a^2/ell=kappa*a or a/ell^2<=kappa*a. For the Laplacian-cutoff terms it costs ell^(-3)+a/ell^2<=C kappa*a.

2. grad Phi dot grad q. Its partition term has coefficient grad Phi dot grad chi_j=O(a/ell)=O(kappa); differentiating the coefficient costs a/(R ell)+a/ell^2<=C kappa*a. For its oscillatory term, the coefficient is grad Phi dot a_j*t_j. Tangency gives size O(a^2 ell/R)=O(kappa). Its derivative is a_j D^2 Phi dot t_j=O(a^2/R)<=O(kappa*a), and differentiating the sine costs a.

3. (Delta Phi)q. The coefficient has size O(a/R)<=O(kappa), and derivative O(a/R^2)<=O(kappa*a); differentiating q costs a.

4. The origin coefficient m=K^2 R^(2alpha-2), multiplying q. For fixed K, m<=C_K kappa. Its derivative is O(m/R)<=C_K kappa*a. The derivatives of q and E cost a.

The explicit Delta f identity is E times the sum of these canceled constituents. Differentiating E costs at most aE, proving(2). Constants may depend on the already fixed K, as in the main theorem.

Write F=G+H as before. The exact Laplacian chain-rule identity in the main audit gives Delta G as the average of Delta f(Y), with Y=x-epsilon z, and three correction integrals. One derivative of the leading integral is bounded using(2) and |D_xY|<=C. For the correction integrals use

    |D^j epsilon|<=C/(a R^j), j=1,2,3,
    |D^j f|<=C a^j E, j=1,2,3.

Differentiating the second integral yields costs aE/R^2 and a^2E/R. Differentiating the third yields E/R^3 and aE/R^2. Differentiating the fourth yields E/R^3 and aE/R^2. Each is at most C kappa*a*E: a/R<=kappa and R>=1,a>=1 suffice. Microscopic comparability holds throughout the averaging ball. Therefore

    |grad Delta G|<=C_K kappa*a*E.                     (3)

For H, still do not differentiate W. With t=a/sigma and K_x(y)=t^n psi(t(x-y)), derivatives through order3 have support in the same ball and

    |D_x^j K_x(y)|<=C a^(n+j), j<=3.

To check the new order, |D^j t|<=C t/R^j. On |x-y|<=1/t, the derivatives of zeta=t(x-y) have sizes at most C a, C a/R and C a/R^2 for orders1,2,3. Leibniz and the third-order composition rule then bound each term by C a^(n+3). The support volume is O(a^(-n)). Applying the already audited |W|<=C_K kappa*a^(-2)E gives

    |grad Delta H|<=C a^(n+3)*a^(-n)
                         *C_K kappa*a^(-2)E
                     <=C_K kappa*a*E.                 (4)

Similarly |grad G|<=C aE and |grad H|<=C_K kappa*a^(-1)E<=C_K aE. Thus |grad F|<=C_K aE.

On the nonharmonic region, |F|>=c tau E. Differentiating the quotient yields

    grad V=(grad Delta F)/F-(Delta F)*grad F/F^2.

Equations(3)-(4), the audited |Delta F|<=C_K kappa E, and |grad F|<=C_K aE prove(1) there. On the open harmonic region V=0, hence grad V=0. The already audited smooth gluing guarantees the same statement across the cover.

## Scope and next falsifiable step

This is a potential regularity improvement, not a generic consequence of V being smooth. It uses one additional differentiated cancellation in Delta f and one additional kernel derivative. The claim must be independently checked, especially(2), before adoption. It does not supply |grad V|=O(r^(-beta-1)) or a monotonicity/sign condition. The derivative can oscillate on the shrinking wavelength a^(-1), so slow pointwise amplitude decay does not imply slow relative variation.
