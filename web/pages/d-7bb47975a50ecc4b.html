THE KMS LOG-LIKELIHOOD BILINEAR FORM IS NOT AN ENTROPY PRODUCTION
Cycle05 route A. Separate strengthened obstruction; prior freezes unchanged.

EXACT CLAIM FALSIFIED

Even for a faithful reference, a compatible sigma-canonical EB channel,
and bounded likelihood L, the quantity

  <L,(I-Psi)ln L>_sigma

can be strictly NEGATIVE while T(rho,Psi*(rho)) is strictly positive.
It therefore cannot be used directly to bound the actual trace error, and
is not a nontracial replacement for the tracial scalar entropy production.
This is stronger than the root-energy obstruction, but does NOT falsify
the genuine relative-entropy DPI deficit or the main power-law target.

LEGAL SINGLE RATIONAL INSTANCE

Set t=99/100 and

  H=[[1,t,0],[t,1,0],[0,0,1+t]], N=3+2t+3t^2,
  sigma=H^2/N, S=H/sqrt N,
  L0=diag(1/5,9/5,9/10),
  m=(1+t^2)/N, k=(1+t)^2/N, 2m+k=1,
  mu=Tr(sigma L0)=2m+(9/10)k=752429/792030,
  L=L0/mu, rho=H L H/N.

Both states are rational, faithful and normalized. The bounds
1/5 I<L<2I follow from 9/10<mu<1. Use the sigma replacer

  Psi*(X)=Tr(X)sigma, Psi(A)=Tr(sigma A)I,
  B(X)=Tr(X)sigma tensor sigma.

This is a full-domain compatible CPTP channel, KMS positive/self-adjoint,
and sigma stationary. It is sigma-canonical from the one-effect POVM {I},
with posterior sigma. Rank-one refinement of that POVM is unnecessary
for the claimed obstruction; the map itself is EB. The canonical
structure here is coarse and must not be called a pure canonical channel.

EXACT NEGATIVE SIGN CERTIFICATE

For a diagonal observable A, the first part of the KMS form uses weights
W_ij=|H_ij|^2/N and its mean uses diag(sigma)=(m,m,k).
Let c12=t^2/N-m^2. Substitution of the three entries of L0 gives

  <L0,(I-Psi)ln L0>_sigma = a ln9+c ln2,
  a=-(8/5)c12+(7/10)mk=-131609645/12546230418<0,
  c=(1/5)mk=784139401/31365576045>0.

Normalization subtracts only a constant from the log and scales L, hence

  <L,(I-Psi)ln L>_sigma=(a ln9+c ln2)/mu.               (E1)

Use ln9>2 and ln2<7/10. These elementary strict bounds follow from e<3
and from the first five positive terms in the exponential series at 7/10
already exceeding 2. As a<0<c,

  a ln9+c ln2 <2a+(7/10)c
    =-363835481/104551920150<0.                         (E2)

Equations (E1),(E2) are a finite exact certificate of negative sign. No
floating-point logarithm, solver tolerance or singular limiting reference
is involved.

ACTUAL TRACE ERROR IS NONZERO

The projection onto the third coordinate is an allowed effect. Since
Psi*(rho)=sigma, its probability difference gives

  T(rho,Psi*(rho)) >=k(1-(9/10)/mu)=(mk)/(5mu)>0.      (E3)

Thus any inequality T(rho,Psi*(rho))^2<=C times (E1) with C>=0 is false.
All matrices, the supplied broadcaster and the effect witnessing (E3)
are explicit. There is no omitted average-prior conversion.

SCOPE OF THE FAILURE

The two states are not claimed to violate a broadcasting/reconstruction
power law; the supplied broadcaster already has a positive marginal error.
Nor is (E1) the relative-entropy loss

  D(rho||sigma)-D(Psi*(rho)||sigma)=D(rho||sigma),

which is positive and controls (E3) by Pinsker. The result only rules out
calling the KMS log-LIKELIHOOD bilinear form an entropy production, or using
ordinary spectral functions of L to import the tracial log-gradient proof.
A route using a true relative-entropy DPI deficit, universal recovery or
a natural-cone argument remains a distinct open possibility.
