1. NONSEPARABILITY IS NECESSARY FOR THE ARBITRARY-GAUGE LEMMA

Theorem. Let a<b and 0<eta<(b-a)/2. Define on [a,b]^2
  E(s,t)=eta if s=t, and E(s,t)=|s-t|/100 otherwise.
There do not exist finite nonempty slope sets A,B with s_alpha,t_beta in
[a,b], and arbitrary real offset functions u_alpha(h),v_beta(h), such that
  F_h(x,y)=min_alpha(s_alpha*x+u_alpha(h))
           +min_beta(t_beta*y+v_beta(h))
satisfies the exact sequential active-label conclusion of the affine lemma
for ALL product labels (alpha,beta). No offset decay hypothesis is needed
for this impossibility result. Duplicate labels with the same slope do not
affect the conclusion. Offsets may be discontinuous or oscillate in h.

Proof. Suppose it does. The finite-label compactness argument proves a
uniform bound: for all sufficiently small h, every product label active
at (h^p,h^q), (p,q) in [a,b]^2, has distance to (p,q) less than its E value.
Otherwise pass to one fixed violating label and a convergent exponent
subsequence; the lemma's strict conclusion contradicts the limiting
non-strict violation.

At every diagonal exponent (q,q), the distance to an off-diagonal slope
(s,t) is at least |s-t|/2, which is greater than E(s,t). Hence, for every
sufficiently small FIXED h and every q in [a,b], EVERY active product label
at z=h^q, x=y=z, has s=t. A product label is active exactly when each of
its component labels is active in its one-dimensional minimum.

Fix such an h. At each z in the connected interval [h^b,h^a], choose an
active beta. If the first minimum had two distinct active slopes s_1,s_2
at z, both active products would have to satisfy s_1=t_beta=s_2, impossible.
Thus all first-minimum labels active at each z have ONE common slope s(z).
This finite-valued function is locally constant: the inactive labels with
different slopes have a strictly positive value gap at z, which persists
in a relative neighborhood by continuity of finitely many affine
functions. Labels with the same slope can tie but cannot change s(z).
Connectedness implies s(z) is constant on the whole closed interval.
The same argument holds for the second minimum and their common slopes
are equal. Thus one common slope value s_h serves EVERY diagonal exponent.

In particular the uniform activity condition at (a,a) and (b,b) gives
  |a-s_h|<eta and |b-s_h|<eta.
Their sum would give b-a<2*eta, contradicting the choice of eta. QED.

What is new in this worker's reasoning: the frozen baseline only ruled out
Cartesian interval-partition compilers. This proof rules out ALL finite
separable sums, even without offset asymptotics or static limiting cells.
It was derived after source-proof exposure and is not a pristine blind
baseline. It does not refute the unrestricted affine lemma. The source's
coordinate-set induction is nonseparable and escapes the obstruction.
No claim that this obstruction is historically new has been established.

2. THE MONOMIAL SPECIALIZATION IS EXACTLY ORDINARY ENDOTACTICITY

Take an actual exponent-arrow diagram with source y_e, vector nu_e, and
orders tau_e(r)=y_e dot r. The Gopalkrishnan-Miller-Shiu convention uses a
direction w, essential edges w dot nu_e!=0, and source complexes maximal
in w-projection among ESSENTIAL edges. Endotacticity requires all such
maximal essential edges to have w dot nu_e<0. Set r=-w. The essential sets
are identical, maximal w-source order becomes minimal r-source order,
and negative w-projection becomes positive r-projection. Scaling r into
[-1,1]^d preserves each of these signs and orderings. Thus (TE) on that
cube is equivalent to endotacticity on every real direction.

The frozen kinetic theorem therefore gives arbitrary-dimensional classwise
permanence for every endotactic actual exponent-arrow diagram, including
real/negative source exponents on the strictly positive orthant, whenever
rates have the displayed monomial orders and the uniform subpower envelope.
This monomial boundary overlaps existing ASTRA N33; it is not this worker's
new discovery. The UV transfer handles more general continuous rate orders
and unbounded state multipliers, beyond N33's globally bounded coefficients.

An elementary strict-scope witness in three species is the union
  X <-> 2X,
  3X+Y <-> 3X+2Y,
  0 -> Z, 2Z -> Z.
Each component is endotactic: in its nonneutral directions its extremal
essential source points inward. Translating all sources of one component
adds a common projection without changing this fact. A finite union of
endotactic diagrams is endotactic, because a union-minimal relevant source
is minimal in its own component. The stoichiometric span is R^3, its class
is the unbounded full positive orthant, and it is not weakly reversible
because of the terminal Z complex. It is also not strongly endotactic:
in direction w=(1,0,0), the global maximal sources are 3X+Y and 3X+2Y,
whose reaction projections are zero; the X reactions at lower source
projection are essential but do not satisfy the global-support requirement.
With independent subpower modifiers depending on all concentrations, the
rate family can couple all three coordinates. This witness distinguishes
the theorem's graph condition from the strongly-endotactic prior scope;
its simple unmodified dynamics are not themselves a new open-problem result.

3. COMPATIBILITY OF LOGARITHMIC PROFILES

UV automatically implies tau_e(0)=0 and positive homogeneity wherever
p and sp are in the exponent cube and s>0:
  tau_e(sp)=s*tau_e(p).
Proof: fix ANY one admissible rate path and one finite time t. At p=0 the
state is 1 independent of h, so log w_e(t,1)/log h ->0. For s>0, write
X_h(sp)=X_{h^s}(p) and log(h^s)=s*log h, then apply UV as h and h^s ->0.
Thus continuous profiles need not be linear or rational, but arbitrary
nonhomogeneous supplied profiles cannot actually satisfy UV. This is a
derived compatibility condition, not an added proof premise.

4. A SHARP FALSIFIER TO REMOVING UNIFORMITY IN TIME

The one-species weakly reversible graph 0 <-> X with rates
  w_+(t,x)=exp(-t), w_-(t,x)=x
has pointwise-in-time logarithmic profiles tau_+(r)=0,tau_-(r)=r and
satisfies the exact all-direction inward condition. For each FIXED finite
t, the valuation error is t/|log h| ->0, uniformly over r. However, the
supremum over all t is infinite, so UV fails. Its exact positive solution
is x(t)=(x0+t)exp(-t), which tends to 0. Therefore replacing all-time UV
by separate limits at each fixed time makes the permanence theorem false,
even for one species, one reversible pair and smooth rates. The all-time
uniform bound is a load-bearing hypothesis, not a cosmetic convenience.

5. OPTIONAL CLOCK OBSERVATION, NOT USED IN THE FROZEN MAIN PROOF

For rates w_e(t,x)=A(t)v_e(t,x), where the v family satisfies UV+TE and
A>=0 is locally integrable, the same geometric absorbers are invariant.
Drift becomes F_h'>=A(t)delta_h outside the plateau. If integral_0^infinity
A(t)dt=infinity, every trajectory enters the common absorber after a
finite amount of activity-clock mass, hence at a finite physical time.
Uniform entry across a family of clocks needs uniform divergence of their
cumulative integrals. This is a routine time-change consequence, not the
main foundational advance or a historical novelty claim.

Finite total activity cannot be dropped: for xdot=exp(-t)(1-x),
x(t)=1+(x0-1)exp[-(1-exp(-t))] tends to 1+(x0-1)exp(-1). As x0 ranges over
the positive class this limit is unbounded, so no common compact absorber
exists. The undeformed birth/death rates 1 and x satisfy UV+TE exactly.
