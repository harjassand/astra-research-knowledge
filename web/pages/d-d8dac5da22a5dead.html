THERMODYNAMIC INFERENCE: AN EXACT HIDDEN-METASTABILITY OBSTRUCTION AND A SCOPED REPAIR
2026-10-08. Status: internally reconstructed proofs; finite diagnostics completed;
external proof review and novelty/priority are unresolved. No major advance claimed.

Main result
-----------
If hidden topology and hidden-state count are unrestricted and no quantitative
mixing information is supplied, a uniformly valid stationary entropy-production
(EP) lower confidence bound based on one trajectory cannot be pointwise consistent,
even at a fixed, simple driven CTMC. An explicit one-extra-state construction gives
the power ceiling

    P_A{L_T >= c sigma_A} <= min(1, alpha/c),          0<c<1.       (N1)

This holds for every finite observation duration T. If the lower bounds are valid
jointly at all observation times, the same ceiling applies to the probability of
EVER certifying c sigma_A. With n independent stationary starts, the corresponding
necessary bound is alpha/c^n. This is the most promising research statement in the
bounded pass. It is not an assertion that every positive certificate is impossible:
the theorem limits high-power certification of a substantial fraction of the rate.

A usable repair is obtained by charging either genuinely independent stationary
replicates, or a supplied valid conditional mixing envelope. Bounded, predictable
reversal critics feed a standard betting supermartingale and yield a lower confidence
sequence without a stationary path-probability oracle or a hidden-state count.
The variational score and betting mechanism are prior-art ingredients. The candidate
contribution is the explicit EP-specific obstruction and precise acquisition boundary,
not a new KL estimator. An initially attractive symmetric Fenchel reparameterization
was reconstructed and found to be exactly the existing NWJ score; that failed novelty
route is preserved below.

1. Exact process, observation, reversal, and target contract
----------------------------------------------------------
The microscopic process X_t is a time-homogeneous irreducible CTMC on an unknown
finite set S, generator Q=(q_ij), q_ij>0 iff q_ji>0 for i!=j. It starts in its unique
stationary distribution pi. No rates, pi values, topology, or |S| are supplied.
All state variables are even under physical time reversal; systems with odd
variables or time-dependent driving need a different explicitly supplied reversal.
The dimensionless stationary EP rate is

    sigma(Q) = sum_{i!=j} pi_i q_ij log(pi_i q_ij / (pi_j q_ji)).  (C1)

Observation is a fixed deterministic map h:S->A into a fixed finite alphabet A,
possibly many-to-one. We allow time-grid sampling, jump records, or measurable
further coarsenings of a fixed-duration observed path, provided the observation
commutes with reversal. For a record Z of duration T, the observer knows a measurable
involution R with Z(X reversed)=R Z(X). Records must retain physical duration;
sample count or jump count is not silently substituted for elapsed time.
Memoryless stochastic sensing is also allowed if its conditional channel commutes
with reversal and introduces no extra autonomous dissipative dynamics. The proof
below only needs deterministic masking.

For stationary full microscopic paths, the forward/reversed path likelihood ratio is

    log(pi_{X_0}/pi_{X_T}) + sum_jumps log(q_forward/q_backward).

The holding-time factors cancel. Taking its expectation gives exactly T sigma(Q):
the stationary endpoint term has expectation zero, and expected edge-jump counts are
T pi_i q_ij. The pi terms in (C1) likewise sum to zero by stationarity. Hence data
processing gives the finite-duration identity/inequality

    D(P_Z || R P_Z) <= T sigma(Q).                             (C2)

This CTMC derivation is supplied here, rather than treating Ehrich's discrete-time
long-path rate formula as an unexplained finite-duration CTMC theorem. R P means
the pushforward of the same stationary forward-record law by the known reversal.

2. The one-extra-state obstruction, with complete proof
------------------------------------------------------
Model class M is closed under adding one microscopic state whose observation label
duplicates an existing label. This is true for unrestricted finite hidden topology
and state count. Take any base model A=(Q,pi,h) in M with sigma_A>0, any state a in S,
and any w in (0,1), epsilon>0. Append state b, leave all old off-diagonal rates as
before, and set

    q'_ab=epsilon,
    q'_ba=w pi_a epsilon/(1-w),
    q'_ib=q'_bi=0 for i!=a.

Adjust the two diagonals to make rows sum to zero, and assign h'(b)=h(a). The new
chain is finite, irreducible, and has reciprocal support. Its stationary distribution is

    pi'_i=w pi_i for i in S;       pi'_b=1-w.

Indeed, old internal stationarity is scaled by w, and the new bridge has equal
stationary flows pi'_a q'_ab=pi'_b q'_ba=w pi_a epsilon. The bridge contributes
zero EP, and every old edge contributes w times its former EP. Therefore

    sigma(Q')=w sigma_A.                                      (N2)

Couple a path of the base chain started at pi with a path of Q' started inside S.
Until the first bridge jump, the old jump rates agree. Conditional on a base path,
the probability that the independent bridge clock never fires is

    exp[-epsilon integral_0^T 1{X_t=a} dt] >= exp(-epsilon T).

The stationary probability of starting inside S is w. Thus for every measurable
event E in the observed-record space, including independent algorithm randomization,

    P_Q'(E) >= w exp(-epsilon T) P_A(E).                       (N3)

Suppose the same procedure L_T satisfies P_Q{L_T<=sigma(Q)}>=1-alpha for every
Q in M. Let E={L_T>=c sigma_A}, choose w<c, and use (N2). Then E is a coverage
failure for Q', giving

    w exp(-epsilon T) P_A(E) <= alpha.

Let epsilon decrease to zero, then w increase to c. This proves (N1).
The limiting model itself need not be irreducible: every positive epsilon used in
the argument is admissible, so the proof uses no inadmissible model as a premise.

Fixed-time consequence: an honest sequence L_T over M cannot converge in probability
to sigma_A at any driven base A. For c>alpha, convergence would give P_A(E)->1,
contradicting the ceiling alpha/c<1 at every T. This is stronger than saying that
the convergence rate is unknown. The entire pointwise consistency of uniformly
honest bounds fails over this extension-closed class.

Anytime consequence: let T_k increase to infinity and assume
P_Q{forall k, L_{T_k}<=sigma(Q)}>=1-alpha for every Q. Apply (N3) on the longest
finite prefix to E_N={there exists k<=N: L_{T_k}>=c sigma_A}. The same limiting
argument bounds P_A(E_N) by alpha/c; continuity from below gives the bound for
the event with any finite k. No deterministic duration can evade this result.

n-replicate consequence: for n independent stationary trajectories of finite durations
T_1,...,T_n, the no-bridge lower domination is
w^n exp[-epsilon sum_i T_i] P_A^{replicates}(E). Consequently

    P_A^{replicates}{L>=c sigma_A} <= min(1, alpha/c^n).        (N4)

To attain power p>alpha, it is necessary that
n >= ceil(log(p/alpha)/(-log c)). For alpha=.05,p=.95,c=.9 this is 28 independent
stationary starts, regardless of each finite trajectory's duration. Splitting one
trajectory into 28 segments does not supply 28 stationary independent starts.
No attainability theorem for this replica floor is claimed.

Scope: known fixed hidden-state count excluding this extension, a certified mixing
bound, interventions, nonstationary active-state initialization, or a known microscopic
generator are outside (N1)'s contract. Those may permit useful inference. The added
state is hidden even when the base record distinguishes every base state: its label
duplicates a, so the extension does not enlarge the observation alphabet.

Stronger dormant-copy variant: an independent blind reconstruction supplied a
refinement, checked here after that reconstruction was frozen. For each old state i
append a dormant copy b_i with h(b_i)=h(i), stationary weights w pi_i and (1-w) pi_i,
bridge rates q_{i,b_i}=epsilon(1-w)/w and q_{b_i,i}=epsilon, and no internal dormant
edges. The full chain remains irreducible; all bridges are detailed-balanced; EP is
exactly w sigma_A. The visible one-time stationary distribution is EXACTLY the same
as the base model's, and the active no-bridge submeasure is exactly
w exp[-epsilon(1-w)T/w] P_A. Thus (N1)-(N4) also hold for an extension-closed class
in which the exact visible stationary marginal is supplied to the estimator. The
one-extra-state construction is sufficient without that extra restriction; the
dormant-copy version charges |S| added hidden states to preserve the marginal.

3. Repaired adaptive confidence sequence
---------------------------------------
At stage i choose, using earlier records only, a physical duration T_i>0, a
reversal-compatible coarsening/observable, an antisymmetric critic f_i(Rz)=-f_i(z),
and a deterministic or predictable positive clip B_i with |f_i|<=B_i. Choices may
include training a neural critic, changing bins, and selecting features. The next
record must satisfy one of the following charged sampling contracts:

(I) A fresh independent trajectory of the same CTMC, initialized from its actual
stationary ensemble. The stationary probabilities are not numerically supplied.
Independent steady-state experimental replicas are a physical premise, not an oracle
that the procedure learns from one trajectory.

(M) One trajectory with a gap g_i before its next block, and a supplied valid envelope
beta(g) >= sup_x || law(X_g | X_0=x)-pi ||_TV, using TV=sup_event |P-Q|.
The gap and duration are selected before the new block. A burn-in gap is charged for
the first block if its initial law is arbitrary. Hidden-state filtering, observed
autocorrelation, or apparent renewal is not a substitute for a valid beta envelope.

The existing NWJ variational KL inequality and antisymmetry imply that the score

    G_i(z)=f_i(z)+1-exp[-f_i(z)]

obeys E_stationary[G_i] <= D(P_i||R P_i) <= T_i sigma. Its known lower and upper
limits and its range length are

    ell_i=-B_i+1-exp(B_i)<0,
    u_i=B_i+1-exp(-B_i),
    r_i=u_i-ell_i=2(B_i+sinh B_i).

Under (I) set b_i=0. Under (M) set b_i=r_i beta(g_i). The Markov property, the
uniform start-state TV premise, and TV contraction under the observation kernel give

    E[G_i | past] <= T_i sigma+b_i.                           (R1)

This explicitly handles adaptively trained critics. It does not assume that masked
states or consecutive observed transition pairs are renewal events.

For each candidate rate s>=0 and any predictable eta_i in [0,1], independent of s,
define

    E_n(s)=product_{i=1}^n [1-eta_i
             + eta_i (G_i-ell_i)/(T_i s+b_i-ell_i)].           (R2)

For every s>=sigma, each factor is nonnegative and has conditional expectation at
most one by (R1). Thus E_n(s) is a nonnegative supermartingale starting at one.
It is continuous and nonincreasing in s. Ville's inequality yields

    P{there exists n: E_n(sigma)>=1/alpha} <= alpha.

Let L_n be the supremum of rejected nonnegative rates
{s:E_n(s)>=1/alpha}, using L_n=0 when that set is empty. Then

    P{forall n, L_n<=sigma} >= 1-alpha.                       (R3)

One can retain the running maximum of L_n. A fixed convex mixture of several
predictable betting strategies is also a nonnegative supermartingale, with the same
monotonicity and coverage. A finite rate grid returns a conservative lower certificate
without a multiple-grid testing penalty: any rejected grid point above sigma would
also imply E_n(sigma)>=1/alpha. A root solver using exact/rigorously bounded numerical
score evaluations gives the continuous version.

Each critic may be bad; coverage still holds. There is no guarantee that it captures
all microscopic EP or even all observed path irreversibility. Improving a critic is
a power question. Memoryless/finite-resolution observations must commute with
reversal; an arbitrary sensing channel does not qualify.

4. Preserved failed novelty route and elementary fallback
--------------------------------------------------------
I initially reconstructed an apparently preferable symmetric Fenchel dual. Let
S=(P+RP)/2, x=(dP-dRP)/(dP+dRP), and F(x)=2x artanh x. Then
D(P||RP)=E_S F(x). F is even and F''=4/(1-x^2)^2. An odd critic h gives
D>=E_P[h-F*(h)] and |h|<=H gives a score range of exactly 2H.
However, write h=F'(t), |t|<1. Then F*(h)=2t^2/(1-t^2), and

    h-F*(h)=log((1+t)/(1-t))+2t/(1+t)
             = f+1-exp(-f),   f=2 artanh t.

Also h=f+sinh f. Thus this is exactly the known NWJ/Kim-Otsubo score with a changed
clipping parameter. It is NOT a new way around exponential score-range costs.
The finite diagnostics use this equivalent parameterization; H=B+sinh B.

With fixed H and equal-duration independent blocks, a simpler uniform union-bound
certificate is

    [sum_{i<=n} G_i-H sqrt(2n log(n(n+1)/alpha))]_+/(nT).

Conditional Hoeffding and alpha_n=alpha/[n(n+1)] prove its coverage. This fallback
was numerically very conservative in the chosen example (zero at n=10,000 with H=4),
which motivated the standard betting repair rather than a claimed new concentration
inequality.

5. Runnable evidence and resource/accounting limitations
-------------------------------------------------------
scripts/certificate.py is a standard-library prototype: reverse-involution callback,
finite rate grid, past-count-trained clipped log-odds critic, fixed mixture of four
bets, true physical block durations, optional supplied mixing-TV correction. It
retains the largest valid grid lower bound. It does not validate stationarity,
independence, the observation reversal, or beta. Floating-point scores/capital are
not a formally verified numerical certificate. A production rigorous implementation
should round G downward and denominators upward, and certify wealth lower bounds.

Cost: one new independent stationary start per block in (I); sum_i(T_i+g_i) physical
time in (M), including unrecorded gap time; one forward record and its computable
reversal per score; critic training is explicitly additional cost. The supplied
beta envelope is a premise that must itself be acquired or justified. Grid capital
storage/updates cost O(K J) for K candidate rates and J betting experts per block;
the dictionary critic costs O(number of distinct observed words) memory. No finite
memory bound is inferred merely from unknown finite microscopic state count.

scripts/checks.py computes exact finite-grid word probabilities numerically for a
four-state ring CTMC, with states 2 and 3 sharing one observed label. Each independent
word observes times 0,.25,.5. Generator matrix exponentials are computed by numerical
diagonalization; data are sampled directly from this calculated word law. This is a
diagnostic shortcut whose population law is available to the simulator, not to the
inference code. The base driven model has sigma=2.1972245773, while observed word
KL/T=.3443113487. The bridge check with w=.4 gives sigma'=.8788898309 exactly to
display precision and stationarity residual 7.8e-17. 1,000 random finite involution
laws satisfy the Fenchel identity to max error 4.7e-13; none violated the sampled
Fenchel inequalities.

1,000 simulated runs of 10,000 blocks each: the H=4 Hoeffding fallback never
exceeded observed word KL, but never became positive. The smoothed plug-in exceeded
observed word KL at at least one of three checkpoints in 88.3% of driven runs;
it exceeded true EP in every equilibrium run at those checkpoints.

200 runs of the betting version with H=1, alpha=.05 and a .05-spaced rate grid:
all driven runs had a positive lower certificate by n=10,000, with mean grid lower
bound .18675. No driven run exceeded observed word KL at any checked step. In the
equilibrium trials, 1/200 had a temporary erroneous positive certificate (0.5%),
below the nominal 5% budget. These finite frequencies are diagnostics, not evidence
of universal coverage; (R1)-(R3) are the proof. They do not establish efficiency
against matched optimal baselines or a thermodynamic experimental capability.
