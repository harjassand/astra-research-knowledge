INDEPENDENT AUDIT: GENERAL ROOT-SYSTEM REGULAR-CONE UPPER PROFILE
Date: 2026-10-08
Audited file: upper_profile_working.txt
Status: no substantive counterexample or missing hypothesis found for fixed
irreducible Phi and fixed regular-cone ratio K. The proof is valid after the
two endpoint/index clarifications below. This is an audit of an internal
derivation, not an external review or novelty assessment.

1. SCOPE AND CONCLUSION

Fix a reduced irreducible root system Phi, with p=|Phi+|, and the long-root-
length-2 metric. Let lambda have Dynkin labels n<=a_i<=K n, with K fixed.
On End(V_lambda), use the half-Casimir eigenvalue

  c2(nu)=(nu,nu+2rho)/2.

The stable principal-height cap, all-root recurrence, low-rank ramp, harmonic
near-full cap for p>=2, and Weyl-symmetric Cartan patch in
upper_profile_working.txt are mutually compatible, including for nonsimply-
laced types. Together with the general lower-profile note, they give the
matching fixed-Phi, fixed-K upper branches

  Lambda_lambda(Q) <= C_(Phi,K) (d/Q)^(1/p),       1<=Q<=d/2,

and, for p>=2,

  Lambda_lambda(d-k) <= C_(Phi,K) (k/d)^((p-1)/p), 1<=k<=d/2.

For A1 (p=1), the exact one-dimensional resistance instead gives
Lambda_lambda(d-k)<=C/log(e d/k). Constants can depend on Phi,K; no claim is
uniform in rank. No operational transfer follows from these profile bounds.

2. NORMALIZATION CHECK FOR SHORT ROOTS

Choose Chevalley root vectors [E_alpha,E_-alpha]=H_alpha, with H_alpha
acting by <.,alpha^vee>, E_-alpha=E_alpha^* in the unitary realization.
For the invariant form inducing the stated weight metric,

  (E_alpha,E_-alpha)=2/(alpha,alpha).

Thus the inverse-metric coefficient in the quadratic Casimir is
eta_alpha=(alpha,alpha)/2. For T_x=(E+E_-)/2,
T_y=(E-E_-)/(2i), and Hermitian radial F,

  ||[T_x,F]||_2^2+||[T_y,F]||_2^2=||[E_alpha,F]||_2^2,

so the energy contribution is eta_alpha times the E_alpha commutator norm.
This gives coefficient 1 for long roots and the correct squared-length
ratio for short roots (1/2 in B,C,F4 and 1/3 in G2). The coefficient is not
to be omitted when forming c_1 or comparing a long-root edge to simple-root
conductance. With eta_alpha included, the trace recurrence

  c_alpha(q+h_alpha)-c_alpha(q)=Tr_(W_q) H_alpha

has the stated sign and source-shell index: E_alpha sends depth q to
q-h_alpha, and c_alpha(q)=Tr_(W_q)(E_-alpha E_alpha).

3. LOAD-BEARING STEPS CHECKED

* Principal depth is q=<lambda-mu,rho^vee>; each positive root has depth
  ht(alpha), including in nonsimply-laced types. In Kostant's formula every
  nonidentity shift delta_w is a sum over inversions with coefficients
  <lambda+rho,alpha^vee> >= n+1, so ht(delta_w)>=n+1. Therefore the carrier
  shells through q<=n equal the PBW partition shells from
  product_(alpha>0)(1-z^ht(alpha))^(-1).
* For q<=epsilon n, the coroot trace is between c_Phi n g_q and
  C_(Phi,K)n g_q: the lambda pairing is at least n, while the weight
  displacement beta obeys |<beta,alpha^vee>|<=B_Phi q. Summing the exact
  recurrence gives c_alpha(q)<=C n V_(q-h_alpha). The simple-root sum is
  comparable to n V_(j-1). All constants are finite positive because Phi
  is fixed and all eta_alpha>0.
* A ramp of radius R has rank V_(R-1)~R^p, norm squared >=c R^p, and energy
  O(n R^(p-1)); it covers Q up to the stable cap by integer rounding and
  the cap's fixed ramp covers larger Q<=d/2.
* The harmonic taper has nullity V_R>=k, norm squared >=d/2, and exact
  simple-root energy 1/rho, where rho=sum_(j=B)^M c_1(j)^(-1). Since
  c_1(j)<=C n j^p, rho>=c/(n B^(p-1)) for p>=2 and B<=M/2. Long-root
  differences are bounded by telescoping the simple-shell increments;
  each increment participates in at most h_alpha source shells, yielding
  the required fixed-Phi energy comparison.
* The Cartan patch is valid: w_0 rho^vee=-rho^vee makes the spectrum of
  T_(rho^vee) symmetric; trace isotropy gives
  Tr(T_H^2)=d c2(lambda)||H||^2/dim(g) in this normalization. Since
  lambda(rho^vee)~n and c2(lambda)~n^2, the positive-part filter has norm
  squared bounded below by c_(Phi,K)d. Its Lipschitz root increments and
  the positive Casimir trace bound give energy O_(Phi,K)(d).

4. MINOR PRECISIONS FOR THE FINAL NOTE

The proof has no substantive gap, but make the following statements
explicit in the synthesized version:

(a) In the low ramp, for a root of height h, the only source depths with a
nonzero difference are q=h,...,R+h-1, exactly R shells. On these shells
q-h<=R-1, so all traces used are in the stable cap.

(b) If k=1, the least R with V_R>=k is R=0 and B=1; do not invoke V_(R-1).
For R>=1, strict increase of V gives k>V_(R-1)>=((R-1)/(p h))^p,
which yields B<=C_Phi k^(1/p). This is only an endpoint convention, not a
change to the estimate.

(c) In the short/long comparison, one can directly use monotonicity:
q-h_alpha<=j-1 whenever q-h_alpha+1<=j<=q, so
V_(q-h_alpha)<=V_(j-1). The bounded ratio V_q/V_(j-1) stated in the working
file is also true for fixed h, but is stronger than needed.

For finitely many small n below the stable-cap threshold, fixed Phi,K leave
only finitely many carriers. The asserted upper inequalities extend by
taking a finite maximum over admissible integer ranks; this is enough for
the theorem's all-n formulation. The proof otherwise states its explicit
construction for n large enough that M+h<=n and V_(M-1)<=d/2.
