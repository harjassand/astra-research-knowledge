C05_L04 CONTINUATION — RANDOM-MATRIX TIGHT-FRAME BROADCASTING OBSTRUCTION
Date: 2026-10-07. Origin: c05_l04, independent derivation after the cross-phase release.
The INITIAL snapshot is preserved; this is a deeper consequence, not a revision of its claims.

STATUS

The random-matrix route also fails as a vanishing-broadcast-error counterexample when its matrices form a bounded, approximately tight frame of traceless Hermitian directions. I give a dimension-uniform Choi witness for any such frame and an explicit Haar-conjugation ensemble for which matrix Chernoff proves that the frame condition holds with high probability using O(d^2 log d) directions. The resulting states have fixed sandwich, exact constant Holevo capacity, accessible information O(1/d), and broadcasting error bounded below by a positive constant. This extends the exact Pauli witness from a complete orthogonal basis to redundant, randomly oriented directions. It is an obstruction to a shortcut, not a counterexample to b2 -> 0 => eEB -> 0, because b2 stays bounded away from zero.

1. TIGHT-FRAME BROADCASTING LEMMA

Let d>=2, n=d^2-1, and let H_1,...,H_N be traceless Hermitian matrices in M_d such that

  tau(H_j^2)=1,  ||H_j||_infinity<=M,  tau=Tr/d.

Regard the real traceless Hermitian space as a real Hilbert space with inner product tau(AB). Its frame operator is

  S(X)=sum_j tau(H_j X) H_j.

Assume the tight-frame bounds

  (1-delta) kappa I <= S <= (1+delta) kappa I,
  kappa=N/n,  0<=delta<1/2.                              (1)

For 0<a<1/M define the signed experiment

  rho_(j,s)=(I+s a H_j)/d,  j=1,...,N, s in {+1,-1},
  sigma=I/d.

Every member is a state and obeys the fixed sandwich

  (1-aM) sigma <= rho_(j,s) <= (1+aM) sigma.

Claim: every actual two-output CPTP broadcaster B whose two marginal errors on this family are at most epsilon in half trace distance satisfies

  epsilon >= a [d^2(1-2 delta)-d]/[4 M (d^2-1)].             (2)

Proof. For each label j, receiver R, and sign, the error condition gives
||B_R(rho_(j,s))-rho_(j,s)||_1<=2 epsilon. Since ||H_j||<=M, the contrast of the two signed inputs obeys

  tau(H_j B_R(H_j)) >= 1-2M epsilon/a.                    (3)

Indeed the true signed contrast is 2a tau(H_j^2)=2a, and the two output errors change it by at most 4M epsilon. Sum (3) over j and both receivers.

For the Choi bound define

  F=sum_j H_j^T tensor H_j,
  W=F_RB tensor I_C + F_RC tensor I_B,

with the tensor factors permuted canonically in the second term. If J_B is the unnormalized Choi matrix (Tr J_B=d), then J_B/d is a state and

  sum_(j,R) tau(H_j B_R(H_j)) = Tr[(J_B/d) W] <= lambda_max(W).       (4)

Let D(X)=tau(X)I. For the ideal frame S_0=kappa(Id-D), the normalized-basis Choi identity gives

  F_0=kappa [d^2 |Omega><Omega| - I].

Thus

  W_0=kappa[d^2(Q_RB+Q_RC)-2I],

where Q_RB and Q_RC are rank-d maximally entangled projections. Their range overlap has norm 1/d, so lambda_max(W_0)=kappa(d^2+d-2).

To control the frame perturbation, set T=S-S_0. By (1), T vanishes on scalars and has Hilbert-space operator norm at most delta kappa on the n-dimensional traceless subspace. Its Frobenius norm is at most sqrt(n) delta kappa. With the normalized-trace convention, the Choi matrix satisfies ||J_T||_2=||T||_F and F-F_0=d J_T. Therefore

  ||F-F_0||_infinity <= d sqrt(n) delta kappa <= d^2 delta kappa,
  ||W-W_0||_infinity <= 2 d^2 delta kappa.

Combining (3)-(4) gives

  2N(1-2M epsilon/a) <= kappa(d^2+d-2+2 delta d^2).

Since N=kappa(d^2-1), rearrangement yields (2). This proof places no covariance, output-symmetry, output-flag, or Choi-extension restriction on B.

For fixed delta=1/8 and d>=2, (2) is at least a/(12M); as d grows it tends to 3a/(16M). Any EB channel is broadcast by preparing the same conditional state at both outputs, so eEB>=b2 and the same lower bound applies to EB reconstruction. The replacer channel gives eEB<=aM/2. Thus the family remains a constant distance from EB, but it is not a counterexample because its actual b2 is constant. When delta=0 and M=1, (2) reduces to the earlier complete-Pauli lower bound ad/[4(d+1)].

2. RANDOM MATRIX FAMILY WITH FIXED SANDWICH AND LOW ACCESSIBLE INFORMATION

Take even d, a fixed traceless Hermitian involution P with P^2=I and tau(P^2)=1, and independent Haar unitaries U_j. Set H_j=U_j P U_j^*. Each H_j is a traceless Hermitian involution, so M=1 and all rho_(j,s) have the same spectrum. Their Holevo capacity is exactly

  C(E)=c(a)=[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2,

independent of d and N: the uniform prior on the two signs of any one direction attains c(a), while every member has entropy ln d-c(a), so no prior can exceed it.

The Haar adjoint orbit is isotropic on the real traceless Hermitian space: irreducibility of conjugation on that space and trace S=1 give E |H_j><H_j|=I/n. Each X_j=|H_j><H_j| is a rank-one positive operator with X_j<=I. Tropp's matrix Chernoff theorem then implies

  Pr[ S not in [(1-delta)N/n I,(1+delta)N/n I] ]
    <= n exp(-delta^2 N/(2n)) + n exp(-delta^2 N/(3n))

for 0<delta<1 (using the standard scalar simplifications of its two tails). Thus N=ceil(6 n delta^(-2) ln(4n)) makes the failure probability less than 1/2; a successful family exists. For delta=1/8 this is N=O(d^2 log d). The state family uses 2N labels and dense dimension-d input matrices.

For any POVM {E_y}, put p_y=tau(E_y), eta_y=E_y/Tr(E_y), and x_(j,y)=Tr(eta_y H_j). Under the uniform signed prior, the outcome marginal is p_y and

  I(J,S;Y)=sum_y p_y/N sum_j c(a x_(j,y))
           <= a^2/[2(1-a^2)N] sum_y p_y sum_j x_(j,y)^2.

Write v_y=eta_y-I/d. The frame upper bound and ||v_y||_(2,tau)^2<= (d-1)/d^2 imply

  sum_j x_(j,y)^2 = d^2 <v_y,Sv_y>_tau <= (1+delta) (N/n)(d-1).

Consequently

  I_acc(E) <= a^2(1+delta)/[2(1-a^2)(d+1)] -> 0.

This is a global-POVM bound, not an LOCC-only data-hiding estimate. Together, the random family has fixed sandwich, bounded exact Holevo capacity, and vanishing accessible information, while the Choi frame witness forces b2 and eEB to remain constant. The decisive failed hypothesis for a counterexample is b2->0.

3. FINITE DIAGNOSTIC AND COST BOUNDARY

`random_frame_check.py` uses NumPy QR-generated approximate Haar unitaries and Pauli coordinates for d=2,4,8. With sample counts 40n, 24n, and 16n, respectively, it observed frame eigenvalue ratios [0.846,1.200], [0.663,1.369], [0.578,1.500] and tracial HS norm errors below 1.5e-15. These intentionally modest sample sizes do not meet the delta=1/8 theorem's O(n log n/delta^2) sufficient count. The diagnostic verifies coordinate normalization only; it is not evidence for the asymptotic concentration claim, which follows from the imported matrix Chernoff theorem.

The mathematical construction uses N=O(d^2 log d) directions and O(d^3 log d) scalar state entries if each dense H_j is materialized. Exact Haar draws use continuous randomness; an explicit finite-bit generation/compiler is not supplied. The frame property has a perturbation margin: if each unitary is changed by operator norm at most eta, then each H_j changes by at most 2eta and the total frame changes by at most 4N eta, so eta<=delta/(8n) preserves a delta/2 spare margin. This only quantifies tolerance; it is not an implemented rational-unitary sampler. There is no efficient implementation, device, energy, or external acquisition claim.

4. ATTRIBUTION, CONNECTION TO PEER EXPOSURE, AND LIMITS

Independent origin: the Choi witness in INITIAL already handled the exact complete Pauli frame and proved its matching universal-cloner value. The new derivation replaces exact orthogonality by a frame-operator tolerance and applies it to randomly conjugated involutions. No peer claim is treated as proof. - c05_l05 gives a slow-center actual-instrument example with an EB comparator that preserves its center modes, and a qubit selfcompatible positive channel with no CPTP C*C factorization. It rules out treating a missing factorization as an EB-lifting counterexample.
- c05_s01 and c05_s03 provide restricted range-lifting results under supplied anchor, basis-leakage, generator, or finite-cover data. None supplies those data for the full random-matrix family or for the universal growing CP range.
- c05_l01, l02, l03, l06, l08, l09, and l10 leave the whole-range EB lift open or conditional; c05_l09's locking example blocks one local log-cardinality information shortcut. The exact route here uses a collective Choi witness and makes no such shortcut.
- c05_l07 confirms a sharp c=2 Dirichlet comparison for the universal cloner, but a Choi-channel distance is not a fixed-family reconstruction error; equation (2) explicitly checks the experiment-level quantity.

No peer artifact is overwritten. The universal bounded-Holevo broadcasting implication remains unresolved. A genuine counterexample still needs a sequence with actual b2->0; a tight family of bounded observables instead triggers collective output monogamy. The open map-lifting issue is unchanged: the approximate frame lemma does not construct a universal EB lift for every positive selfcompatible CP range.
