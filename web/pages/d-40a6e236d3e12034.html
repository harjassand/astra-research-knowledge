RESULT
An exact six-site determinant-weighted ambient-hole instance refutes coefficientwise-power stability closure. The same instance has a connected bipartite support graph, det(F)=-1, 11 entries equal to one, and no zero rows or columns. Its baseline signed ambient-hole polynomial is real stable by the supplied chiral-determinant proof, while the coefficientwise square has Rayleigh difference -12 at an exact real specialization and an exact zero with all six variables strictly in the upper half-plane.

For every real coefficientwise power p>=2, the fixed Rayleigh certificate remains negative. With a=2^p and b=3^p, it is
  Delta(p)=14+18a+4a^2-6b-3ab.
Since a>=4 and b>=9a/4, Delta(p)<=14+(9/2)a-(11/4)a^2<=-12.
Thus this is a scoped all-powers obstruction, not just a p=2 fixture.

A permitted auxiliary extension does not remove the issue. Adding coordinate pair (e_0,e_1) with activity w preserves signed-hole stability under the already proved auxiliary rule. At the same test point, the coefficientwise-square Rayleigh difference is
  Delta_aux(w)=9w^2-12w-12,
which is negative for every 0<=w<2. The exact activity-one instance has Delta=-15 and its own direct rational upper-half-plane zero. The activity-range result is saved in `revisions/03_auxiliary_activity_range.txt`.

PRIOR ART AND LIMITS
The generic fact that multivariate Hadamard products can fail to preserve stability predates this run; no novelty claim is made for that phenomenon. The relevant added evidence is its explicit realization in this determinant-hole family, including a connected unimodular instance and persistence after a legal coordinate-pair auxiliary. The 2004 publisher record confirms the generic paper's title and claim, but its full text/domain-specific example was unavailable in this pass; priority for the restricted instance remains UNKNOWN. Borcea–Brändén's disk-stability Hadamard theorem and their right-half-plane symmetric-difference convolution theorem concern different domains/operations; neither gives the coefficientwise-square closure used here.

This does not refute the exact fixed-k dummy-coordinate homogenization identity, the all-size square-root exchange theorem, or every polarized/auxiliary representation. It only blocks stability-based shortcuts that coefficientwise-square these hole coefficients and then assume stability survives. It leaves the microscopic two-replica sampling/energy comparison unresolved.

The next gate is still to construct a computable microscopic two-replica kernel and prove polynomial integrated Dirichlet-energy comparison along the calibrated annealing path without an unknown hole-partition oracle. A new impossibility claim would need to name its kernel or representation precisely.
