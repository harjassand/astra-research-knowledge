THE CONCRETE RESULT
The 10-state rational model in work/sol/hidden_realization/exact_certificate_v1.json admits a SINGLE bounded statistic of its observed six-letter process, using seven fixed times, whose expectation is nonnegative for every stationary all-even equilibrium hidden CTMC, with no hidden-dimension or rate bound, but strictly negative for this model. Every reflected cylinder Gram kernel of this model is nevertheless completely positive, by the lead's exact observable-subspace proof.

All coefficients below are rational: use polynomial matrix exponentials, not exact matrix exponentials, when defining the statistic. The statistic can be evaluated from observed letters alone. A deliberately coarse certified normalization gives a positive entropy-production floor at least 2*10^(-89) nats per model time unit for every compatible stationary finite all-even CTMC. The numerical floor is extremely small. Independent-window Hoeffding acquisition at 95% confidence costs about 1.1*10^93 windows, and the smallest lag is 10^(-12). This is a constructive mathematical separation certificate, not a practical experiment or optimized inference scheme.

2. A LINEAR CIRCLE-DEFECT BOUND
For every y in P,
  dist(y,{v_i}) <=8(1-|y|^2).                                  (A)
Proof: choose a convex representation y=sum lambda_i v_i and k with lambda_k>=1/5. Since all vertices have norm one,
  1-|y|^2=sum_(i<j)lambda_i lambda_j |v_i-v_j|^2

The nearest vertex is at least as close. This finite-polytope bound avoids a square-root loss in the first geometric forcing step.

3. RATIONAL FUTURE FUNCTIONS AND FINITE GRID

Let

Write I0=1_{Y0=0}, psi_k=1_{Y0=0,Yh=k+1}. The two-by-two matrix B_h with rows b_4^T,b_0^T is invertible, with ||B_h^(-1)||_infinity<12/h. Define two rational bounded functions by

Then define the eight-component future function

Put G=U_t F-KF, where U_t shifts both arguments of F by t. Then ||G||_2<=61/h pointwise.

For a future function A, write A^+ for its value on the positive half of the observed window, and A^- for the same function with time labels reversed. Thus F^- uses Y0,Y_-h and (U_tF)^- uses Y_-t,Y_(-t-h). Every statistic below depends only on the seven observed letters at

5. UNIVERSAL EQUILIBRIUM INEQUALITY
Under any stationary reversible hidden Markov realization with deterministic state emissions, let xi(X0)=E[F^+|X0]. Conditional past and future independence and reversibility make each reflected statistic an ordinary conditional-feature inner product. In particular, on output 0 write xi=(1,x,y,0^5); on marker output k it is exactly xi_(5+k). Then
  p0=Pr(output0), s2=E[I0 |x|^2],
  R_h=E sum_k r_k^2, r_k=E[D_k|X0],
  R_t=E|r_t|^2, r_t=E[xi(Xt)|X0]-K xi(X0).
No invariance of the competitors' feature spaces, differentiability, rate bound or hidden cardinality enters this representation.

For internal x, actual marker future probabilities psi_cond are nonnegative. Therefore the facet-negative vector obeys
  ||l(x)_-||_2 <=50h sqrt(1+|x|^2)+(1/(eta h))||(r_k)_k||_2.
Let y=proj_P x, d=|x-y|, and D2=E[I0 d^2]. The Hoffman bound implies
  D2<=9[5000h^2(p0+s2)+200 R_h/h^2].                  (B)
Round y to its nearest vertex v_i and use field Z_i; use Z=0 for markers. If d_v=|x-v_i|, (A) gives d_v<=d+8(1-|y|^2).

The hidden two-time feature law is a symmetric coupling. Thus
  E Z_round(X0) dot [xi(X0)-xi(Xt)]
    =(1/2)E[(Z_round(X0)-Z_round(Xt)) dot (xi(X0)-xi(Xt))]
    >=-6E[I0 d_v].
This uses pairwise monotonicity at the exact ten frame points and ||Z_i-Z_j||<=6. Also ||I-K||_2<=3t/(1-3t)<1/3, so changing x to its rounded vertex changes Z_i dot (I-K)xi(x) by at most d_v. Cauchy-Schwarz for the regression residual gives cost at most 3 sqrt(p0 R_t).
Combining these statements with the Lipschitz bound for q yields
  S_q >=-8E[I0 d]-E[I0 |x|d]-72E[I0(1-|y|^2)]
            -3 sqrt(p0 R_t).
Since 1-|y|^2<=1-|x|^2+2|x|d,

    >=-150 sqrt((p0+s2)D2)-3 sqrt(p0 R_t).             (C)
Here sqrt(8^2+145^2)<150 gives the stated numerical constant.

Using (B), subadditivity of the square root, and sqrt(2)<=2 gives
