4. QUANTITATIVE CANONICAL KINETIC AND MULTIPLE-TIE EXCESS

archive.txt proves 1<=q_e<=m!, including ghost faces. For the recovery
(3.1), canonical minus labeled energy on an edge is proportional to
(m!-q_e)|F_d(z')-F_d(z)|^2. It vanishes when both endpoint tuples are
distinct. Otherwise at least one coordinate pair coincides at the source
or the destination of that edge. Since m!-q_e<=(m!-1)q_e, it suffices
to bound the labeled energy on those bad edges.

For a labeled edge in direction a and any implicated pair i<j, select
one of its coordinates DIFFERENT FROM a. The edge difference is still
low-sine in that unmoved coordinate. Conditional on every other coordinate,
the source or destination coincidence fixes just one value of it, possibly
shifted by one relative to the moved coordinate. Evaluation has norm
squared <=2Mh. Summing source/destination tests over p pairs therefore
bounds the bad labeled edge energy by 4pMh times its full energy.
Ghost-normal edges use the same argument; other ghost coordinates are
zero. No ordered-to-labeled orbit count is substituted for this evaluation.

   <=(Lh)^2[1+4(m!-1)pMh]<F,TF>.                    (4.1)

gL(prod r_v! W-2P)|F_d(z)|^2, nonnegative and <=gL m!m|F_d(z)|^2.
It vanishes at a tuple with only one pair tie. Every other collision
pattern obeys TWO DISTINCT pair equality constraints. Such two edges
form a forest; evaluate its two leaves conditional on the remaining roots.
Two-variable low-sine evaluation bounds that probability by (2Mh)^2.
A union bound over at most p^2 choices gives

   <=(Lh)q_g,contact(F)+4g_+L m!m p^2 M^2h^2||F||^2. (4.2)

This includes triple ties and two independent pairs; it never assumes

9. THE QUANTUM/CLASSICAL JOINT n,L CONSEQUENCE AND COSTS

thermal_eb.txt gives a continuum EB floor e0=5*10^-21 for EVERY n for
coupling pair g=.1 and .1+.1/sqrt(n), J1,tau.02,lambda0. Its selected
interval [.1,.2] fits this quantitative theorem. Transporting an arbitrary
finite-chain EB archive through the public I_L/TP channels loses at most
2n Delta_L, hence at most 2n min(2,C_L). For every explicitly checked
pair (n,L) with 2n C_L<=e0/2, its maximum full-state reconstruction error
is at least e0/2. In particular n(L)=floor(L^(1/1536)) eventually meets
this condition, by the power bound with alpha=1/768.
Any fixed copy exponent 0<beta<1/384 also works, with a public threshold
from (8.4) and an alpha between beta and 1/384. No optimal exponent is claimed.

The public quantum product archive has all-L error epsilon and persistent
q=O(n(1+log(n/epsilon))^(3/2)) qubits. Reconstructing n chains costs n
single-chain circuit applications and Nn output spins. Given the physical
copies, the trace-only K^* version from archive.txt has conservative gates
O(n r N^2 poly(log N)) plus polynomial multiset arithmetic, where
r=O(sqrt(1+log(n/epsilon))); working labeled registers are O(r log N) per
sequential application. Total channel synthesis error epsilon uses angle
precision O(log(G_total/epsilon)). Thus for n=floor L^beta and fixed total
epsilon, the stored quantum register is o(Nn), with polynomial supplied-input
gate cost. No input Gibbs generation is provided. If bounded physical
decoded energy is additionally required, use energy_decoder.txt and charge
its reusable Gram setup, factorial amplification and additional energy
precision separately; that stronger decoder has no polynomial-time claim.
The raw equal-information storage baseline is Nn qubits.
Taking the fixed total trace target epsilon=e0/4 therefore gives an
explicit finite task: every measure-and-prepare reconstruction has error
>=e0/2 beyond the public threshold, while the public quantum archive
achieves <=e0/4. This concerns reconstruction of the whole n-chain state,
not parameter estimation or prediction of a restricted observable bank.
Fixed PUBLIC g with variable lambda remains commuting and total number
is sufficient; its conditional interacting preparation remains a separate
cost. No practical-sized margin or optimal/sublinear-in-n quantum memory
is claimed. The polynomial n(L) exponent is deliberately small but explicit.

The exact continuum cell channels are mathematical Hilbert-space maps,
not a supplied infinite-dimensional hardware/simulation oracle. Operational
finite memory is provided by the already banked archive, which does not
need to calculate continuum eigenstates or implement the spectral projection
used in Section 7. Evaluating C_L requires scalar arithmetic on r factorials
and binomials, with bit lengths O(r log(r+Q)), and controlled public sum
precision; it requires no many-body matrix. These costs are distinct from
the physical input/output circuit costs.
