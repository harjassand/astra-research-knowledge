The same paper labels `Kvac` “not well known” and says it was chosen to limit FBP to a few hundred mM. Its CellML comment says reducing initial Pi (e.g. to 7 mM) gives imbalanced dynamics. Table 1 gives default initial `(F,A,P)=(2,1,10.4)`. The earlier van Heerden et al. Science 2014 core-model Figure 4 reports that its viable and imbalanced simulations differ only in initial Pi, 10.4 versus 9.4 mM. Combining that with the later paper’s unchanged FBP/ATP starting values yields the actual cited failure start `(2,1,9.4)` used in this audit. It lies inside the proposed `S`; this is why the conditional certificate directly covers the named failure start.

The base `kp` is 0.3, below the sufficient 0.304 threshold from the `A+P` face; the requested 0.4 change is +33.3% relative to reference. Source treats `kp` as a genotype parameter, not as a validated single-gene intervention. Under the source’s fourth-power enzyme-expression cost formula, changing only `kp` from 0.3 to 0.4 changes the modeled expression ATP flux from 5 to approximately 5.00000292 mM/min; the source model therefore assigns nearly zero cost to this small transport rate next to the 10 mM/min pathway capacities. That is a model cost, not a measured transporter-expression or resource cost.
