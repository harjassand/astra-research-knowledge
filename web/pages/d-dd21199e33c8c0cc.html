# Degree-one controlled retract for the actual replacements

## Actual induced complexes and incoming-boundary selectors

Use the paper's CSS cochain convention C0→C1→C2. Write A for the old
complex, F for the fine replacement, and E for the induced complex of
local block cohomologies. Paper.tex lines1674–1713 and1994–2034 give:

    R_X: E0=A0, E1=A1,
         E2=(A2⊕A2)⊕M2,       dE0=dA0,
         dE1=(dA1,dA1,0).

    R_Z: E0=(A0⊕A0)⊕M0, E1=A1, E2=A2,
         dE0(a,b,m)=dA0(a+b),  dE1=dA1.

The metacheck spaces M have zero maps to/from E1. Extra endpoint
cohomology therefore prevents a full old/fine equivalence in all degrees,
but does not obstruct the degree-one filling calculation.

Define chain maps J:A→E and Q:E→A as follows. In R_X, J0=J1=Q0=Q1=id,
J2(c)=(c,c,0), Q2(c,d,m)=c. In R_Z, J0(a)=(a,0,0),
Q0(a,b,m)=a+b, and J1=J2=Q1=Q2=id. Direct substitution verifies
commutation with each differential, QJ=id and J1Q1=id. These selectors
operate within a parent check tag and carry no long-distance solve.

## Explicit finite local contractions and perturbed maps

On each local block, choose exact binary decompositions

    C^j = B^j ⊕ H^j ⊕ L^j,
    Z^j = B^j ⊕ H^j,

where d:L^j→B^(j+1) is an isomorphism. Set i to inclusion of H, p to
its projection, and h to the inverse of d on B, zero on H⊕L. This gives
pi=id, ip=id+dh+hd and hi=ph=h²=0 over F2. Ordinary Gaussian
elimination constructs the bases; all matrices remain inside their local
blocks. It costs the patch-matrix size and arithmetic and is not a free
homology oracle. The patch dimensions are O(ℓ²) after fixing the bounded
parent degrees, so a naive contraction costs O(ℓ^6) binary operations per
local shape. The source's fixed ℓ is enormous, making this a mathematical
construction rather than a practical computation performed here.

The gluing map g follows parent tags X→Q→Z. The block-diagonal h does
not change tags. Hence (gh)^3=(hg)^3=0. The paper's Appendix A formulas
then become finite matrices

    i' = i+hgi+hghgi,
    p' = p+pgh+pghgh,
    h' = h+hgh+hghgh,

with i'p'=id+D h'+h'D for the fine differential D. The transferred
differential is initially pgi+pghgi; nilpotence alone does not remove
the second term. In R_X its only possible two-hop degree-zero source is
H0(C_X), but h_Q would land in C_Q0=0. In R_Z a two-hop degree-zero
source H0(C_X) ends in C_Z1, whose projection p_Z1 is zero because
H1(C_Z)=0. The remaining degree/tag possibilities vanish as well.
Thus pghgi=0 for these actual replacements, and the transferred
complex E is the induced complex displayed above. This check is needed
beyond naming its first-page structural description. Each
matrix term crosses at most two gluing edges. No unbounded geometric
series or iteration-dependent propagation occurs in one replacement.

For X boundaries take

    P1=Q1 p'^1, P0=Q0 p'^0,
    I1=i'^1 J1, I0=i'^0 J0, K=h'^1.

For a fine closed e, D1e=0 and J1Q1=id give exactly

    e + I1P1e = D0 K e.

Also P1D0=dA0P0 and D0I0=I1dA0, as required by the transfer lemma.
Thus every global fine X boundary can be generated by K e plus an
injected coarse X filling. This statement uses the original generators,
not merely an abstract isomorphic global code.

For Z boundaries use the dual complex with incoming map (D1)^T. The
dual HPL retraction exchanges injection/projection and transposes them.
The induced-to-old dual selectors exchange J/Q and transpose them:

    P1_Z=J1^T(i'^1)^T,
    P2_Z=J2^T(i'^2)^T,
    I1_Z=(p'^1)^T Q1^T,
    I2_Z=(p'^2)^T Q2^T,
    K_Z=(h'^2)^T.

The same degree-one identity holds for closed dual chains because
Q1^T J1^T=id. Their incoming-boundary and injection relations follow by
transposing the chain-map identities. This covers both CSS sectors; a
cochain proof alone would miss the second stability premise.

## Conservative physical support constants for one level

The random-embedding construction first sends each parent position z to
λz+c(z), with ||c(z)||≤αλ. Perturbed parent Tanner edges have length
at most ℓ, and the old Tanner edges have length at most1. The paper takes
ℓ to be the least odd integer greater than (2α+1)λ.

Each basis vector of a local block tagged by parent v lies in a replaced
parent face/edge/star. The piecewise-linear flattening puts it in the
convex hulls of parent simplices incident to v, at distance at most2ℓ
from the perturbed parent position. This follows from a parent square's
two-edge path to its opposite corner; its convex hull cannot exceed that
bound. A whole fine check adds at most one fine Tanner-edge length1.
The duplicated and flattened cells remain in the same hull.

P/P_Z may cross at most two parent adjacency edges. Thus a fine radius-r
qubit vector projects into a coarse ball of radius

    r/λ+a,       a=α+2ℓ/λ+2.

K/K_Z connect an input and output through at most two perturbed parent
edges. The input/output offsets are at most2ℓ each, the intermediate
tag displacement at most2ℓ and whole-check completion at most1. Hence
their whole generator support lies within radius

    r+b,         b=6ℓ+2.

For injection, two parent hops have coarse length at most2. If the
coarse radius bounds a check's whole qubit support, its center can be
one coarse Tanner edge farther away, adding one λ. Dilation,
perturbation and local offsets give the safe bound
(α+3)λ+2ℓ+1≤b. A coarse filling radius s therefore injects into fine
whole-check radius λs+b. Transposes reverse the tag paths without changing
these distance estimates. The conditional recurrence (R) consequently
has explicit depth-independent constants for the random construction.

These large coarse bounds do not assert that a local homotopy already
has the smaller unit collar. They use complete finite local blocks.

## Actual unresolved anchor

The recurrence's projection floor is aλ/(λ-1), approximately5α+4 for
the source's large λ. In contrast, every perturbed parent edge has length
at least(α2-1)λ and each parent simplex has width at leastγλ. Therefore
the anchor radius is much smaller than a complete parent edge or face.
This is a possible route to a relative local-chart proof: an interaction
component of a small physical ball cannot traverse a full parent edge;
unrelated cells that merely meet in the embedding are not glued.

To adopt that route we still need an exact classification of small-ball
interaction components and a relative filling proof at parent vertices,
edges and folded/degenerate seams, in both CSS sectors. Connected parent
X/Z links help, but merely naming them does not prove relative exactness.
Bounded density permits many unrelated nearby components, and accidental
geometric overlap must not be mistaken for Tanner adjacency. No uniform
anchor or constant-collar theorem is claimed here.

The paper's source values α=1000, λ≈5.4×10^24 and ℓ>(2α+1)λ make the
scale separation rigorous once their displayed chosen-parameter
inequalities are used, but also expose astronomical resource constants.
This derivation concerns the random construction with its proved
thermal-encoding lifetime; the explicit embedding's deferred analysis
is a separate model and is not silently substituted.
