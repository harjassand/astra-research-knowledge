FAILED / UNRESOLVED BLIND ROUTES, FROZEN WITH THE SCALAR PROOF

1. Operator Jensen is unavailable in general KMS geometry. The previously
frozen exact sigma=diag(4/5,1/5), pi_+/-=(2,+/-1)(2,+/-1)^*/5 example
has Psi(rho)-(T sqrt(rho))^2 nonpositive for rho=|+><+|. That failure does
not refute the scalar theorem; the new proof avoids it entirely.

2. A tempting sufficient fidelity lemma remains NOT PROVED here:
  sum_i [(B^(1/2)AB^(1/2))_ii]^2/(B^2)_ii
   <= ||AB diag(sqrt((A^2)_ii/(B^2)_ii))||_1,
for positive A,B with Tr A^2=Tr B^2=1 and shared support. This would
give e^2<=2E by fidelity, but it is not needed by the column proof.
Its status is UNKNOWN, not established or refuted by the new theorem.

3. The STRONGER inequality S<=Fidelity(A^2,Psi(A^2))^2 is exactly FALSE.
Take B0=[[1,3],[3,10]], B=B0/sqrt(119), u=(3,-1)/sqrt(10), A=uu^*.
Then sigma=B^2>0, and the two-atom canonical channel in the frame
representation has p=(10,109)/119, posterior vectors B0 e_i/sqrt(p_i*119).
Since det B0=1 and Tr B0=11,
  B0^(1/2)=(B0+I)/sqrt(13)=[[2,3],[3,11]]/sqrt(13).
Thus
  B0 u=(0,-1)/sqrt(10),
  B0^(1/2)u=(3,-2)/sqrt(130),
  S=81/(16900*10)+16/(16900*109)=8989/18421000,
  Fidelity(A^2,Psi(A^2))^2=1/10900.
Their difference is positive. Scaling B0 does not change either quantity.
This example does NOT refute S<=Fidelity or e^2<=2E.
NOTE: an initial handwritten sqrt denominator 12 was wrong: it is 13.
The frozen exact values above include that independent arithmetic repair.

4. Singularity / rare-posterior mechanism was explored analytically only.
For B with eigenvalues 1 and epsilon and a measurement-basis mixing
angle delta, a rare posterior can change by O(1) only when delta is at
least comparable to epsilon. In that same regime the corresponding
KMS Gram direction loses O(1) norm, so this simple fixed-d mechanism
does not yield E->0 with nonzero trace disturbance. No general claim
was inferred from this failed mechanism.

5. A star-positive-matrix family also did not obstruct the inequality.
For G=[[a,b 1^T],[b 1,c I_N]], Nb^2=ac, B=G^2, and pure target e_1,
put x=c/a. Then the pure output overlap is 1/(1+x), while
  S=(1+x)^(-3)+x/[(1+x)^3+x^3(N-1)].
Small x gives E~2x and e^2~x; this family does not create an unbounded
ratio. This calculation was only a failed route, not a theorem premise.
