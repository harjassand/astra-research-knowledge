## Full filtration, masks, and no-reset independence

Let F_i contain every observed endpoint, all clocks/actions and seed history. Before the next endpoint is seen, lag t_i, source state X_i, normalized predictor q_i and retain mask h_i are F_i-measurable. True conditional endpoint law is p_i(y)=exp(t_i Q)[X_i,y]. Baseline irreducibility and t_i>0 imply full support, including b=0. For Z_0=1,

Z_(i+1)=Z_i [q_i(X_(i+1))/p_i(X_(i+1))]^(h_i)

has conditional expectation Z_i: a retained factor integrates to sum q_i=1; an omitted factor equals1. Thus it is a nonnegative mean-one martingale. Ville gives P_Q(exists i:Z_i>1/alpha)<=alpha for each fixed true Q. No union over a continuous parameter set is needed. The resulting C_i={Q:L_i(Q)>=alpha M_i} has simultaneous pointwise coverage; it is not an optimizer and does not assert all candidates are true.

For row acquisition, complete return cycles of the fixed-lag sampled chain. At a known designated state x, mark the next endpoint as retained before observing it; then keep reading until x is reached again. Strong Markov makes successive first endpoints iid with row law P_x. Conditional on preceding row-block history, the next entire row block has the same fixed count law; all pilot blocks are finished before freezing qhat. Fresh confirmation blocks therefore have their fixed product-row law conditional on the full pilot history. No independent initialization is supplied. Omitted hitting/return observations influence the full filtration and are charged, while their martingale factor is1.

The confidence ratio itself has mask0 throughout the entire pilot: stored first pilot endpoints train qhat but add no likelihood-ratio factors. After freezing, only the first departures of fresh confirmation cycles have mask1. This is why D contains confirmation counts alone; an earlier pilot numerator penalty cannot be silently discarded from a process that had actually included it.

One must not choose h_i after observing the endpoint or refit qhat using confirmation outcomes. An exact counterexample in the independent checker has p_rare=1/10, q_rare=1/2, and retains only a realized rare outcome: expected wealth is7/5, not1. Predictability is a load-bearing assumption.

## Exact expected split penalty, including arbitrarily rare outcomes

For an n-category row p, pilot C~Multinomial(mp,p), let q_j=(C_j+1)/(mp+n). For p_j>0,

E[1/(C_j+1)]=[1-(1-p_j)^(mp+1)]/[(mp+1)p_j].

This follows directly from binom(mp,c)/(c+1)=binom(mp+1,c+1)/(mp+1). Consequently

E chi²(p,q)=(mp+n)/(mp+1) sum_j p_j[1-(1-p_j)^(mp+1)]-1 <=(n-1)/(mp+1).

At p_j=0 its contribution p_j² E[1/(C_j+1)] is zero; the same formula follows by continuity. Since log x<=x-1, KL(p||q)<=chi²(p,q), in exactly this direction.

Let r be a fresh confirmation empirical row from mc endpoints. Exact multinomial second moments give E chi²(r,p)=(support_size(p)-1)/mc <=(n-1)/mc, so E KL(r||p)<=that quantity. Conditional on the frozen pilot,

mc E KL(r||q)=mc E KL(r||p)+mc KL(p||q),

because E r=p and q is fixed. Summing n rows yields

D=log Lsat_confirm-log M_confirm=mc sum_x KL(r_x||q_x)>=0,
E D<=n(n-1)[1+mc/(mp+1)].

For mp=mc=m this is at most2n(n-1), uniformly in t,b,m and numerical ordinary rates. Thus D<=6n(n-1)/delta except with probability delta/3. This is a separate width event; even a poor pilot leaves coverage valid. The result removes a growing log m penalty in this split width analysis at fixed confidence. It is not a lower bound on all other predictors, and variable-confidence constants are not claimed logarithmic in 1/delta.

## Deterministic KL transport and nonlinear certificate

For probability laws r,s, k=KL(r||s), and a centered under s with |a|<=M and variance v, bounded-variable Bernstein expansion gives

log E_s exp(theta a)<=v theta²/[2(1-M|theta|/3)].

Gibbs' variational inequality yields theta E_r a<=k+log E_s exp(theta a). Choose theta=sqrt(2k/v)/(1+(M/3)sqrt(2k/v)) in either sign. Substitution gives

|E_r a-E_s a|<=sqrt(2vk)+(M/3)k.

The v=0 or k=0 cases follow by limits/support constancy. Applying row-wise and Cauchy–Schwarz proves the linear log influence bound sqrt(2V0 t^(L-2)k)+(M0/(3t))k, where k=sum_x KL(Phat_x||P_x). No empirical independence is needed for this deterministic inequality.

For an off-diagonal indicator, v<=lambda t and centered bound<=1, so each entry error is at most sqrt(2lambda t k)+k/3. Zero row sums give ||E||<=A0[sqrt(2lambda t k)+k/3], A0=2(n-1). If k<=t then ||E||²/t<=G0 k with the safe G0=A0²(4lambda+8/9). Precheck ||Phat-I||<=1/2 exactly. Since every candidate has ||P-I||<=1/4, its straight segment to Phat stays inside the norm-half log domain. The second derivative log-series bound is4||E||²; its integral Taylor remainder is at most2||E||²/t. Also log(exp(tQ))=tQ exactly in this near-identity domain.

Therefore every candidate with sum_x KL(Phat_x||P_x)<=k lies in

b in [braw-r(k),braw+r(k)],
braw=[log Phat]_uv/t,
r(k)=sqrt(2V0 t^(L-2)k)+[M0/(3t)+2G0]k.

At balanced confirmation completion, accepted candidates satisfy k<=B/m with B=D+log(3/delta). Thus this is an outer projection of the entire continuous confidence set; its endpoints need not be realizable generators. In particular it does not trust a nuisance optimizer. The ordinary log entries can also be clipped to their supplied rate interval; the same norm event gives ordinary-rate error O(sqrt(k/t)+k), hence O(sqrt(beta)) under the schedule. This is not an EP export or an arbitrary prescribed ordinary-rate precision theorem.

## Complete no-reset resource accounting and qualifiers

A supplied uniform time-one floor eta gives pi_x>=eta. For k_hit=ceil(1/t), P_Q(k_hit t)[x,y]>=eta; chunked hitting therefore has expected cost<=k_hit/eta reads from any starting state. Each completed return cycle has mean1/pi_x<=1/eta by Kac. There are2n row-block starts and2nm cycles. With the initial read,

H=1+[2n k_hit+2nm]/eta,
E reads<=H, E elapsed observation time<=tH, E jumps<=lambda tH.

Skipped reaching/return reads and both sample blocks appear here. An integer read cap above3H/delta has failure probability<=delta/3 by Markov on the uncapped reference experiment. This coupling proves confidence/success bounds without incorrectly conditioning sample laws on not hitting the cap. The capped protocol is finite on every path. Effective k_hit and cap values may be any integer above certified rational upper enclosures; an exact ceiling of a general computable real is unnecessary.

At fixed graph/envelopes, epsilon and delta, m and H have orders t^(L-2)/beta²+1/(beta t). Multiplying by t gives time t^(L-1)/beta²+1/beta. Substituting t=max(tau,kappa beta^(1/(L-1))) yields

E reads=O[beta^(-L/(L-1))+beta^-2 tau^(L-2)],

This does not acquire beta or show an unknown-scale global expected stopping law.
