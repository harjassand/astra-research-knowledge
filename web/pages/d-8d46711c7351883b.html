  arXiv:1810.12197: Theorem 2.3 is measured de Finetti with an explicit
  informationally complete trace distortion; Theorem 4.4 and Corollary 4.7
  use a distinct multi-input/no-signalling channel interface and dimension
  factors. The bounded-Holevo substitution in the measured chain-rule step
  leaves a dimension-sensitive trace lift.
- Horodecki, Horodecki, Horodecki, “Separability of mixed states: necessary
  and sufficient conditions,” arXiv:quant-ph/9605038 (1996): PPT is exact in
  2x2, the cone fact used to interpret the qubit EB SDP; solver values remain
  separate from the analytic witness.
- Werner, “Optimal cloning of pure states,” arXiv:quant-ph/9804001 (1998):
  universal-cloner context for the MUB controls. The exact constants are
  proved from Choi/design bounds in the cycle-4 note.
- Glebsky, “Almost commuting matrices with respect to normalized
  Hilbert-Schmidt norm,” arXiv:1002.3082 (2010), Theorem 4: commuting
  approximation for fixed k observables with a k-dependent modulus.
- Bansil and Kachkovskiy, “On almost commuting matrices with respect to the
  normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm,” arXiv:2608.31000 (2026): a two-observable
  estimate; it does not provide a growing-family common POVM.
- Kretschmann, Schlingemann, Werner, “A Continuity Theorem for Stinespring's
  Dilation,” arXiv:0710.2495 (2007): dilation continuity starts from
  completely bounded norm control, absent from family-relative broadcasting.
- Fawzi and Renner, “Quantum conditional mutual information and approximate
  Markov chains,” arXiv:1410.0664 (2015): recovery for a specified tripartite
  state with a small CMI; does not itself yield one full-domain EB decoder.
- Slansky, “Group theory for unified model building,” Physics Reports 79

  the channel cone and contractions are derived internally.
