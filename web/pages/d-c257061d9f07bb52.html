## Exact question and interface

For a Boolean polytope P, let k(P) be the maximum support of an **actual edge**. Given rational y∈P and rational A with every column's Euclidean norm at most one, can exact strong optimization over P produce, deterministically in polynomial rational bit complexity, an explicit law supported on at most dim F(y)+1 vertices, with mean y and discrepancy `||A(b−y)||∞=O(k(P))` on **every** supported vertex? For one matroid base polytope, k(P)=2; an independence oracle must suffice. This is the full target, as opposed to an unconstrained signing, a Gaussian tail, one good outcome, approximate marginals, or a stronger supplied exchange oracle.

D's reported constants are a general upper bound 1161k and a matroid bound 2209. Those are the handoff's claims; this prior-art audit does not establish the end-to-end D theorem. The implications below are independent deductions under explicitly stated source theorems or exact oracle interfaces.

## Equally informed comparators

The complete independent proofs are in `notes/rounding_prior/BASELINE_DERIVATIONS.md`. They use the same original A,y and strong optimization access, adding supplied exchange decompositions only where named.

1. **General P:** deterministic exact-LP favorable response with hard radius `kT`, where T=max_j Σ_i|a_ij|, then exact-mean extraction with at most n+1 atoms. With Δ nonzeros per unit-Euclidean column, T≤√Δ. For one arbitrary matroid the comparator is `2T≤2√Δ`; for dense m-row input, `kT≤k√m`. This is a weighted absolute-mass form of the standard iterative-relaxation proof, not a new research claim. A plain exact decomposition also has the computable radius `max_i Σ_j |a_ij|max(y_j,1−y_j)≤n`; choose the better guarantee.
2. **Cube:** Li plus hereditary dyadic coarsening gives an exact-mean law with hard radius `<99`, deterministic polynomial bits and at most n+1 atoms. Initial rounding toward favorable cost uses q≥256n and adds at most 1/256 feature error; Li's explicit strict constant leaves enough slack. The exact law is a deduction from the source theorem, not something attributed to Li's paper.
3. **Simultaneous exchange classes:** Li on all pair-exchange columns, a polynomial-sized q-point dyadic list, and global sign reversal gives favorable hard radius `<198` for strongly base-orderable bases **with computable/provided all-subset exchange maps**, and `<99ell` for an explicit Boolean TU equality system whose conformal circuits have support at most ell. Exact-mean extraction then preserves those radii. Partition matroids have the maps directly. Independence access alone does not certify a polynomial all-subset exchange finder for every strongly base-orderable matroid.
4. **Existential subclass benchmark:** Ercan's claimed `<6.84` signing constant gives strongly base-orderable hard radius `<13.68` with exact means, but its joint sampling theorem has a different algorithmic availability guarantee. Do not compare that theorem to a finite-bit deterministic implementation without its stated caveats.

The scientific claim left after these comparators is an improvement from kT to an absolute multiple of k for arbitrary Boolean P, with arbitrary matroids outside simultaneous-exchange classes as a concrete testbed. Cost preservation and extraction do not account for that improvement.

## Consequential obstructions and independent gate

* K4 spanning-tree bases B={12,23,34}, D={13,14,24} have no complementary all-subset exchange bijection: both 12 and 34 have only partner 14, violating Hall's condition. Yet their polytope edges have support two. This exactly blocks the black-box Ercan-5.1 transfer to all matroids.
* Independent fair choices in r disjoint rank-one pairs have exact marginals and supported errors ±r for the row (1,−1,...). Gaussian convex order can coexist with those outcomes. A correlated law can do much better; this is a rejection of an implication about that prior guarantee, not a nonexistence theorem for other laws.
* Perfect matchings of a 2r-cycle form a segment with edge support 2r. The two underlying partition matroids individually have k=2, but their joint feasible polytope does not. Exact marginals force equal weights on the alternating matchings, and an alternating feature row gives errors ±r. No automatic constant theorem for matroid intersection or coupled bases follows.

The new independent extractor note `notes/rounding_prior/EXACT_MARGINAL_GATE.md` checks the root-requested gate. For y=a/Q, every feasible finite separator subsystem has `||c||∞≤(n+1)!Q^(n+1)` by a min-infinity-norm LP and Cramer's rule. Doubling gives a uniform positive-volume ball in box4R0. Retrospective collected-system containment under explicitly outward rational central cuts forces its infeasibility after O(n³(log Q+log n)) calls. Exact LP and affine reduction give the exact law, with final common denominator at most `Q(n+1)^((n+1)/2)`. A conservative update and its bit/containment/volume proofs are written explicitly, including the n=1 special case. No mathematical extractor implementation lemma remains missing; its full software execution remains unperformed. ## Priority, practical limit, and next falsifiable step

If root's complete spectral/reset/progress favorable routine survives independent proof review, D has a credible route to a major structural theorem: uniform exact-mean hard discrepancy is controlled, up to an absolute factor, by actual edge support. The longest-edge midpoint lower bound is elementary; the general finite-bit upper bound would be the load-bearing advance. The inspected sources do not establish that upper bound. Prior absence has not been proved exhaustively.

The reported constants are not a practical advantage on ordinary sparse instances. The matroid constant 2209 beats the conservative `2√Δ` comparator only in a worst-case sparsity regime Δ≥1,219,921; the exact T comparison is better. It cannot improve the trivial n bound when n≤2209, and supplied exchange classes already have the smaller `<198` comparator. The general 1161k guarantee improves the kT comparator only when T>1161; T≤√Δ means such a regime requires Δ>1,347,921. These are comparisons of guaranteed bounds, not claims about measured runtime or realized error. Smaller exact k-dependent constants may change those thresholds.

The next discriminating step is a full rational favorable trajectory on a graphic matroid that fails simultaneous exchange, using fixed A,y,c, exact new-face recovery, normalized cost-slice resets, actual facet endpoints and the global tracking budget. Compare it with `2T` on the same instance; then run the separately checked extractor. Local spectral fixtures, exact arithmetic checks or model agreement cannot substitute for that composition. Publishability/priority and external correctness remain unvalidated.
