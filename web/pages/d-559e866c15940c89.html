P18 — Correlated Gaussian displacement at a rare herald

**Result.** For classical Gaussian displacement noise followed by local loss and a herald measurement only on H, the herald probability depends on the H-marginal displacement covariance C_HH, not on cross-covariance C_HS. Holding C_HH fixed therefore fixes the herald mass exactly. Cross-covariance can still change the conditional signal law. Correlations within H can change the herald mass even when every mode has the same variance and the total displacement energy is unchanged.

For any input state, partial trace gives

    Tr_S A_C(rho_HS) = A_{C_HH}(Tr_S rho_HS),

because the signal displacement is removed by the partial trace and the remaining Gaussian average uses only the herald marginal. This applies when subsequent loss/processing is local and the herald POVM acts only on H. Later H-S interactions are outside the claim. For an initially entangled source, zero added-noise cross-covariance does not imply full output independence.

On a vacuum reference with proper complex Gaussian amplitude covariance S after loss,

    E exp(-sum_j t_j |beta_j|^2) = det(I + S diag(t))^(-1).

Differentiating once in each herald coordinate at t=1 gives the exact-one PNR event; setting an unobserved signal coordinate to t=0 traces it out, while t=1 imposes zero signal counts.

An exact full-rank low-R fixture uses R=2, one signal mode, nu=eta=1/2. The independent baseline has pre-loss covariance nu I_3, herald mass 16/625 and P(N_S=0|H=11)=4/5. With the same per-mode variance and trace, take `Sigma=nu[(1/2)I_3+(1/2)J_3]`; its eigenvalues are nu/2, nu/2, 2nu. Then

    p_H = 27200/970299,       P(N_S=0|H=11) = 77198/103275.

If only cross-block correlations are added, with normalized covariance `[[1,0,1/2],[0,1,1/2],[1/2,1/2,1]]`, the matrix is positive definite and the H block remains independent. The herald mass stays exactly 16/625, but `P(N_S=0|H=11)=88520/117649`. Thus fixed herald mass is not conditional signal stability. A literal additive covariance `nu I_3+nu J_3` also changes the reference: it has trace twice the baseline and gives `p_H=2192/42875`, `P(N_S=0|H=11)=343/548`.

At general R, use one shared latent displacement `alpha_i=Z`, `Z~CN(0,nu)`. Its covariance is `nu J_R`, with the same diagonal and trace as `nu I_R`; include the signal in the same common mode if desired. For eta=1/R,

    p_common = R!(eta nu)^R/(1+R eta nu)^(R+1),
    p_ind    = [(eta nu)/(1+eta nu)^2]^R.

At `nu=R^(-a)`, fixed `a>0`, `log(p_common/p_ind)=R log R-R+o(R)`. Thus equal energy and equal per-mode variance allow an `exp(Theta(R log R))` change in the all-one vacuum-reference mass. With a shared signal, its conditional mean is `eta nu (R+1)/(1+R eta nu)`, versus `eta nu` for independent noise.

The low-R fractions are reproduced exactly by [check_p18.py](/Users/harjas/Documents/Codex/2026-10-08/use-the-astra-research-knowledge-repository-3/work/programmes/combinatorics_optimization/cycle3/rare_herald_noise/p18_correlated_noise/check_p18.py); the derivation and scope are in [RESULT.txt](/Users/harjas/Documents/Codex/2026-10-08/use-the-astra-research-knowledge-repository-3/work/programmes/combinatorics_optimization/cycle3/rare_herald_noise/p18_correlated_noise/RESULT.txt). No oracle is used. A dense rational covariance table costs Theta(R^2 log R) bits for fixed a; a latent shared-mode description is more structured and should be charged accordingly. The exact reference calculation transfers to a rare-herald source only with a source/reference error controlled relative to the new herald mass. This result makes no hardness or sampler lower-bound claim.
