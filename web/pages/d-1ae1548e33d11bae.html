1. Interface and preserved scope

Let sigma=s^2>0 and rho=q^2>0 be density matrices on a finite-dimensional
Hilbert space. Let L be the positive Heisenberg generator: exp(-tL) is unital
CP, and L^*(sigma)=0. The state curve is rho_t=exp(-tL^*)rho. Write
delta=L^*(rho), so rho'_0=-delta and Tr(delta)=0. Genuine entropy production is
J=Tr[delta(log rho-log sigma)]. KMS reversibility additionally means that
H=Gamma_s L Gamma_s^{-1} is HS selfadjoint, where
Gamma_s(X)=s^(1/2) X s^(1/2). Then H(s)=0 and E=Tr[q H(q)].

The endpoint asks J>=2E, with no dimension, condition-number, modular
covariance, or reference restriction. All matrices in this baseline are
faithful where logarithms/resolvents are differentiated. The scalar and
complete trace-disturbance theorems from cycle06_scalar_gate are immutable
and do not supply this entropy-to-energy comparison.

For the equivalent Hermitian-noise representation H(X)={V,X}/2-BXB,
V=2 Lyap_s^{-1}(BsB), the physical jump is K=s^(1/2)Bs^(-1/2). General finite
KMS generators are sums of these terms, as independently proved in the
Cycle06 blind baseline. The HS entropy operator M and exact R_K decomposition
are retained from the immutable Cycle06 packet. The three failed shortcuts

2. Exact trace observation and its quasi-entropy meaning

Diagonalize s with eigenvalues s_i>0 and u_i=log s_i, and write
q=sum_a q_a P_a. The audited potential is
R_K=sum_a q_a [ K(log q_a-u_i,log q_a-u_j) o P_a ],
where the scalar kernel is positive, and K(x,x)=k(x),
k(x)=x sinh x-cosh x+1. Thus R_K>=0.

For rho_a=q_a^2 and sigma_i=s_i^2 the diagonal identity is
s_i q_a k(log(q_a/s_i))
 = (rho_a-sigma_i)log(rho_a/sigma_i)/4
   -(rho_a+sigma_i)/2+s_i q_a.
Sum with (P_a)_ii=|<a|i>|^2. Normalization Tr rho=Tr sigma=1 gives exactly

Tr(s R_K)=[D(rho||sigma)+D(sigma||rho)]/4-1+Tr(sq).

Equivalently, with f(t)=(t-1)log t-2(sqrt t-1)^2,
S_f(rho||sigma)=4Tr(s R_K)
 =D(rho||sigma)+D(sigma||rho)-2||q-s||_2^2.
This is precisely the Petz quasi-entropy
<s,f(Delta)s>_HS, Delta=L_rho R_sigma^{-1}, since the left and right
multiplication operators commute. This argument uses spectral sums only for
an exact identity, not as a finite-grid substitute for positivity.

The integral representation, already independently established in Cycle06,
is
f(t)=(t-1)^2 int_0^infinity
 [(1+lambda)-(4/pi)sqrt lambda]/[(t+lambda)(1+lambda)^2] d lambda.
Its numerator is nonnegative: 1+lambda>=2sqrt lambda and 4/pi<2.
This proves operator convexity by the positive resolvent perspective.

3. A self-contained resolvent perspective and monotonicity proof

Put D0=rho-sigma and A_lambda=L_rho+lambda R_sigma for lambda>0.
A_lambda is a strictly positive HS operator, and define the generally
NON-HERMITIAN matrix
X_lambda=A_lambda^{-1}(D0),
so rho X_lambda+lambda X_lambda sigma=rho-sigma.
Let m_lambda=<D0,A_lambda^{-1}D0>_HS. It has the variational formula

m_lambda=sup_X { 2 Re Tr(D0 X)
                 -Tr(rho XX^*)-lambda Tr(sigma X^*X) }.

For a CPTP map Phi, U=Phi^* is unital CP. Schwarz gives
U(YY^*)>=U(Y)U(Y)^* and U(Y^*Y)>=U(Y)^*U(Y).
Substitution of U(Y) in the displayed supremum proves
m_lambda(Phi rho,Phi sigma)<=m_lambda(rho,sigma).
This proves the needed perspective data processing directly, with no
unproved primary-source premise.

For normalized states the exact scalar integral for
g(t)=t log t-t+1 is
g(t)=(t-1)^2 int_0^infinity
 lambda/[(t+lambda)(1+lambda)^2] d lambda.
Therefore
D(rho||sigma)=int_0^infinity w_D(lambda)m_lambda d lambda,
w_D(lambda)=lambda/(1+lambda)^2.
Similarly S_f=int w_f m_lambda, with
w_f(lambda)=[1+lambda-(4/pi)sqrt lambda]/(1+lambda)^2.
The expressions follow by spectral calculus for Delta, not by replacing
noncommuting matrix products by scalar quotients.

4. Exact positive gradient formula for genuine physical J

Differentiate the variational optimum at the faithful state rho along the
physical curve, with sigma fixed. The exact derivative is

I_lambda=-d/dt m_lambda(rho_t,sigma)|_0
 =2 Re Tr(delta X_lambda)-Tr(delta X_lambda X_lambda^*).

The order X_lambda X_lambda^* is load-bearing. Define the positive-generator
carre du champ
Gamma_L(A,B)=L(A^*)B+A^*L(B)-L(A^*B).
The two adjoint Sylvester equations are
rho X_lambda+lambda X_lambda sigma=D0,
X_lambda^* rho+lambda sigma X_lambda^*=D0.
They and stationarity Tr(sigma L(Y))=0 give

I_lambda=Tr[rho Gamma_L(X_lambda^*,X_lambda^*)]
          +lambda Tr[sigma Gamma_L(X_lambda,X_lambda)].

For transparency, the first two terms of Tr(rho Gamma_L(X^*,X^*)) become
Tr((X^*rho)L(X))+Tr((rho X)L(X^*)). Replace X^*rho and rho X by their
Sylvester values. The D0 terms give 2Re Tr(delta X), the last term gives
-Tr(delta XX^*), and the two remaining terms are minus lambda times
Tr(sigma Gamma_L(X,X)). This verifies all orientations and factors.

For physical GKSL jumps K_j the Hamiltonian part cancels and
Gamma_L(X,X)=sum_j [K_j,X]^*[K_j,X]. Thus

I_lambda=sum_j ||[K_j,X_lambda^*]q||_2^2
            +lambda sum_j ||[K_j,X_lambda]s||_2^2 >=0.

Differentiating the exact entropy integral yields

J=int_0^infinity lambda/(1+lambda)^2
 [sum_j ||[K_j,X_lambda^*]q||_2^2
   +lambda sum_j ||[K_j,X_lambda]s||_2^2] d lambda.

This holds for EVERY sigma-preserving finite-dimensional QMS, without KMS
symmetry. Faithfulness makes differentiation and endpoint domination valid:
X_lambda=O(1) near zero and O(lambda^{-1}) near infinity, uniformly on a
small faithful neighborhood of rho. Constants in this analytic justification
are not claimed as stability constants; no dimension-free conclusion uses
the minimum eigenvalues. The integral applies as well to a finite joint
system/reference. It is a factorization of J, not yet a comparison with E.

5. Exact square-root resolvent reconstruction and remaining comparison

Scalar divided differences give
sqrt t-1=(t-1)/pi int_0^infinity
 sqrt lambda/[(t+lambda)(1+lambda)] d lambda.
On HS space (Delta-I)s=D0 s^{-1}, and
(Delta+lambda)^{-1}(Delta-I)s=X_lambda s. Consequently the correctly ordered
matrix identity is

q-s=(1/pi)int_0^infinity [sqrt lambda/(1+lambda)] X_lambda s d lambda.

Individual integrands need not be Hermitian. The integral is q-s and is
convergent at both endpoints. Because H(s)=0,
E=||H^(1/2)(q-s)||_2^2.

The remaining exact, equivalent sufficient/necessary comparison is

||H^(1/2) (1/pi)int [sqrt lambda/(1+lambda)]X_lambda s d lambda||_2^2
 <= (1/2)int [lambda/(1+lambda)^2]
 { sum_j||[K_j,X_lambda^*]q||_2^2
    +lambda sum_j||[K_j,X_lambda]s||_2^2 }d lambda.

No proof of this inequality is claimed. It couples the KMS Dirichlet form
to physical GKSL commutators. A pointwise Schwarz argument must charge its
weight integral and keep left/right orientations; it cannot simply identify
the two gradients. The operator-convex potential R_K does not itself repair
that missing identification.

6. Exact failure: differentiating S_f is not J-2E

Let R_log=Tr(sigma Dlog_rho[delta]) and
A_sqrt=Tr(s Dsqrt_rho[delta]). Along the physical curve,
I_f=-d/dt S_f(rho_t||sigma)|_0=J-R_log+4A_sqrt.
The reverse relative entropy contributes -R_log to its dissipation, while
the affinity contributes +4A_sqrt. Data processing gives I_f>=0, but does
not identify I_f with J-2E.

Exact tracial control: sigma=I_2/2, rho=diag(9/10,1/10), B=sigma_x.
Then V=I, H(X)=X-BXB, and delta=diag(4/5,-4/5). Direct calculation gives
E=2/5, J=(4/5)log9, R_log=-32/9, A_sqrt=-(4/15)sqrt5.
Hence
I_f=(4/5)log9+32/9-(16/15)sqrt5,
J-2E=(4/5)log9-4/5.
Their difference is 32/9+4/5-(16/15)sqrt5>88/45>0, using sqrt5<9/4.
In particular the prospective correction R_log-4A_sqrt-2E>=0 is false.
This refutes only this shortcut. The target J>=2E holds in this control.
