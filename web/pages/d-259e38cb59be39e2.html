# Gaussian mass/scale reliability: solved benchmark; interacting gap open

Exact question: for P=N(0,(aI+L)^-1), Q=N(0,((a+δ)I+L)^-1), identify observable fidelity, global divergence, mass repair, and certificate costs.

Result: at fixed a>0, δ≠0, global TV→1 exponentially in volume, while fixed-r projected KL is bounded by r(|δ|/a)²/[4(1−|δ|/a)]. A positive quadratic observable restricted to Laplacian λ≥ℓ has relative bias ≤δ/(a+δ+ℓ), δ≥0. Infrared susceptibility bias remains δ/(a+δ); critical uniformity requires δ/a control. Compact-wavelet leakage must be calculated rather than replaced by exact spectral support.

Construction: exact Fourier transport costs O(N log N), matching the direct Gaussian sampler. A full-field mass MLE plus estimated transport gives confidence 1−η of global TV≤ε after M≥R² log(2/η)/(2ε²) independent pilot fields, provided the actual generator is exactly a centered Gaussian mass family and true/pilot mass lie in [a/R,Ra]. Full acquisition costs MN and is charged. This theorem was independently audited with the parent; details and direction constants are in the note.

Alternative certificate: weighted true-score Fisher residual controls KL exactly when λ_min(K^1/2 W K^1/2) is uniformly bounded below. Mean shifts make the LSI constant sharp; centered rank-one covariance perturbations give the same necessary sensitivity without commutation.

Decisive obstruction: a radial two-Gaussian mixture has exactly the target covariance and every quadratic scale expectation, but its normalized energy concentrates at two distinct values and global TV→1. Gaussian family membership or a certified true-score residual is load bearing. No interacting reliability theorem is claimed.
