1. CONVEX-WIDTH MINIMAX AND COVARIANCE DUAL
-------------------------------------------
Let X be trace-norm compact and P_x rank-one projectors. In B(H), equipped with sigma(B(H),S_1), the set
  F_Q={T: 0<=T<=I, Tr T<=Q}
is convex and weak-* compact. The operator unit ball is weak-* compact; positivity and I-T>=0 are weak-* closed by testing against positive rank-one trace-class functionals. For positive T, Tr T<=Q is equivalent to Tr(PT)<=Q for every finite-rank orthogonal projection P; each such condition is weak-* closed. Their intersection consists exactly of positive trace-class operators of trace at most Q. This works in separable infinite dimension as well as finite dimension.

Let Pi(X) be probability measures on compact X with the weak topology. Pi(X) is compact convex. For f(T,pi)=1-Tr(T sigma_pi), sigma_pi=int P_x dpi, f is affine and separately continuous: in T by the trace-class pairing; in pi because x->Tr(TP_x) is continuous. Thus the compact-convex bilinear minimax theorem applies (e.g. Sion), and
  inf_(T in F_Q) sup_x [1-Tr(TP_x)]
    = inf_T sup_(pi in Pi(X)) f(T,pi)
    = sup_pi inf_T f(T,pi).
The first equality holds because Dirac measures are included and no average exceeds sup_x.

For positive trace-class sigma, diagonalize sigma with eigenvalues lambda_1>=lambda_2>=...>=0. For any T in F_Q, only its diagonal entries in this eigenbasis affect Tr(T sigma), and 0<=T_ii<=1 with sum_i T_ii=Tr T<=Q. Filling the largest Q entries maximizes the trace, attained by the top-Q spectral projection (or the rank-min(Q,dim H) identity/top eigenspace when Q>=dim H). Hence
  inf_(T in F_Q) [1-Tr(T sigma)] = 1-sum_(i=1)^Q lambda_i(sigma),
with zero padding if the dimension or rank is below Q. This proves the dual formula.

The max over pi is attained: pi->sigma_pi is trace-norm continuous. One justification is to approximate the compact trace-class-valued map x->P_x uniformly by finite-valued continuous partitions of unity; weak convergence then gives norm convergence of integrals. The Ky Fan sum K_Q(sigma)=sum_(i<=Q)lambda_i(sigma) is trace-norm Lipschitz because K_Q(sigma)=sup_(rank P<=Q)Tr(P sigma), so |K_Q(sigma)-K_Q(sigma')|<=||sigma-sigma'||_1. Thus the dual objective is continuous on compact Pi(X). Its maximizer need not be efficiently found; using a selected prior is a converse witness, not a free acquisition procedure.

2. OVERLAP CHAIN AND kappa COST
-------------------------------
For two-mode normalized coherent states, the convention in the source gives
  |<alpha|beta>|=exp(-||alpha-beta||^2/2).
For ||alpha||=s<=R, take n=ceil(s) equal radial steps from vacuum to alpha. If s>0, each displacement has length s/n<=1, all intermediate points lie in the closed radius-R ball, and each adjacent overlap is at least exp(-1/2). Thus the weighted path length is at most n exp(1/2)<= (s+1)exp(1/2). At s=0 use the zero-edge path. Including kappa=max{1,sup_x inf_path ell(path)} gives
  kappa<=exp(1/2)(R+1),
including R=0 for this path bound. The use of overlap magnitude makes coherent-state phase irrelevant.

Substitution in the pure-family width lower e_Q>=omega_Q/(148 kappa^2) yields
  e_Q>=omega_Q/[148 exp(1)(R+1)^2].
There is no missing square/root: the theorem's path penalty is kappa^2. Since e^(1/2)(R+1)>=1, the explicit path estimate also bounds the max-with-one definition.

3. PHASE-SPACE PRIOR NORMALIZATION
----------------------------------
Take X={P_alpha: alpha in C^2, ||alpha||<=R}, R>0, in the two-mode bosonic Fock space, and use the uniform probability measure on the real four-ball. Its Lebesgue volume is Vol(B_R)=pi^2 R^4/2. The normalized coherent states resolve identity:
  integral_(C^2) P_alpha d^4 alpha/pi^2 = I.
This follows modewise from |alpha>=exp(-||alpha||^2/2) sum_(n1,n2) alpha_1^n1 alpha_2^n2/sqrt(n1!n2!) |n1,n2> and
  integral_C exp(-|z|^2) z^j conjugate(z)^k d^2z/pi = j! delta_(jk).
The uniform-ball mean state is
  sigma_R=(1/Vol(B_R)) integral_(B_R) P_alpha d^4alpha
         =(pi^2/Vol(B_R)) integral_(B_R) P_alpha d^4alpha/pi^2.
Positivity and the full-space resolution imply integral_(B_R)P_alpha d^4alpha/pi^2<=I; therefore sigma_R<=2/R^4 I. It remains positive trace class of trace one, despite the scalar upper bound being an infinite-rank operator bound.

By the exact dual, each of its top Q eigenvalues is at most 2/R^4, so
  omega_Q(X)>=max{0,1-2Q/R^4}.
Combining with the path estimate gives exactly
  e_Q(X) >= [1-2Q/R^4]_+ / [148 exp(1)(R+1)^2].
The positivity truncation is necessary when 2Q/R^4>=1.

4. THRESHOLD, RADIUS SCALING, AND ENDPOINTS
-------------------------------------------
Assume R>=1. If Q<R^4/4, then 1-2Q/R^4>1/2, so the bound is strict:
  e_Q(X)>1/[296 exp(1)(R+1)^2]
         >=1/[1184 exp(1)R^2].
Thus epsilon R^2<=1/[1184 exp(1)] is sufficient to rule out Q<R^4/4 whenever e_Q(X)<=epsilon. Equivalently Q>=R^4/4; since Q is integer, the operational bound is Q>=ceil(R^4/4).

For any fixed 0<c<=1/sqrt(1184 exp(1)) and 0<epsilon<=c^2, choose R=c epsilon^(-1/2). Then R>=1 and epsilon R^2=c^2 satisfies the threshold, giving
  Q>=c^4/(4 epsilon^2).
This is an Omega(epsilon^(-2)) lower with an explicit constant, conditional on the supplied experiment actually containing the radius-R coherent ball. If the family contains a larger domain, its worst-case risk is at least the risk on this subball. If its radius stays bounded as epsilon->0, this growing-radius argument does not apply. At R=0 the normalized uniform-ball prior is undefined, but the path bound is valid and the single vacuum state has width zero for Q>=1; the phase-space formula is only for R>0. At Q>=dim H in finite dimension, omega_Q=0; for the two-mode Fock space H is infinite and every finite Q is covered by the top-Q sum.

5. COST / SCOPE / FALSIFIERS
-----------------------------
The dual quantifies an exact converse but its worst-case prior/maximizer is not free to acquire. The coherent example supplies a concrete prior and needs no optimizer oracle. The family states are pure projectors and the decoder reproduces that same full state. The claimed Omega(epsilon^-2) is for an experiment whose domain grows at least as c epsilon^-1/2 in coherent amplitude; no tensor-product mixed-state, purification/reference, or iid transfer is justified. The source itself records the orthogonal-state counterexample to dropping finite overlap-path cost.
