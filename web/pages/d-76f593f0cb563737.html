# A whole logical algebra exposes the missing Davies hypothesis

Root deduction, independently reconstructed by pde_spectrum9, cycle15. This sharpens the one-observable secular-discontinuity example. It is a proof-interface obstruction, NOT a counterexample to the actual recursive memory or to the existence of another protected encoding.

Take two qubits, a logical L and a gauge G. Put B=Z_L tensor X_G, H_delta=delta B, A=I_L tensor Z_G. The same bare bath operator A commutes with every member of the logical Pauli algebra for every delta. Set P=I. Let rho_delta be the actual Gibbs state at a fixed beta. Every logical Hamiltonian commutator has rho-weighted2-norm at most2|delta|. Thus the bad-projector mass is0, bare bath protection is exact, and coherent logical drift tends to0.

For delta!=0 the actual spectral jumps are J_s=P_s A=(A+sBA)/2, with P_s=(I+sB)/2. They have the two opposite Bohr frequencies; assigning s to the signed frequency does not affect the sum below. Choose the smooth bounded KMS function gamma(omega)=2/(1+exp(-beta omega)), with convention [H,A_omega]=-omega A_omega. Its two rates sum to2 exactly and each is bounded by2.

X_L anticommutes with B and commutes with A. Therefore

    J_s^dagger X_L J_s=0,
    J_s^dagger J_s=P_-s,
    {P_-s,X_L}=X_L,
    D[J_s]^dagger(X_L)=-X_L/2.

The full Davies dissipator consequently gives D_H^dagger(X_L)=-X_L for every nonzero delta. The same holds for Y_L; Z_L is fixed. Also [J_s,X_L]=s BA X_L is unitary, giving the exact leakage

    sum_omega gamma(omega) Tr rho_delta [A_omega,X_L]^dagger[A_omega,X_L]=2.

At delta=0 the single spectral jump is A_0=A, which commutes with the entire original logical algebra. Its dissipator has zero action there, and the displayed leakage is0. Smooth rates and an unchanged bounded local bath do not remove the discontinuity: frequency projection is the issue. Thus a bound tending to0 with bad mass and approximate Hamiltonian commutation is false without additional spectral structure.

This does not prove memory failure. Indeed X'_L=X_L tensor Z_G together with Z'_L=Z_L tensor I_G is another exact logical algebra commuting with both H_delta and A. A changed encoding protects it. The example says that a low-Gibbs-bad-mass argument and small commutators for a chosen original decoder algebra do not automatically acquire the full-frequency commutators needed for that decoder's Davies theorem.

Concrete downstream use: the cycle15 positive-density separated-field theorem acquired an EXACT local diagonalizer and exact protected algebra, so it avoids this obstruction. The new full-density pure-X Gibbs witness argument alone does not. Next work must construct an appropriate full-spectrum dressed algebra, prove model-specific weighted-frequency bounds, or change to a justified finite-resolution/microscopic dynamics with its time/error budget. Merely inserting an exponentially small bare commutator in the existing Davies bound is not valid.
