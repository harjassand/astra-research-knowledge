1. Exact interface
------------------
For L>=2 put S=L-1, with physical sites 1,...,S and BOTH endpoint fields,
also when S=1:

  Hcrit = J sum_{i=1}^{S-1}(I-X_i X_{i+1}-Y_i Y_{i+1}-Z_i Z_{i+1})
          + J(I-Z_1)+J(I-Z_S),
  n_i=(I-Z_i)/2,  N=sum_i n_i,  W=sum_{i=1}^{S-1}n_i n_{i+1},
  B_L(kappa,lambda)=L^2 Hcrit-kappa W-lambda N,
  lambda_c=2 J pi^2,  kappa_t=lambda_c/3,  beta=tau L^2.

The Gibbs partition function considered here is Xi_L=Tr exp(-tau B_L),
with fixed J,tau>0 and lambda=lambda_c. This is the all-spin trace, not a
single-sector ground-state weight. The attractive contact parameter is
restricted to 0<=kappa<=kappa_t.

2. The tricritical coefficient after shape relaxation
-----------------------------------------------------
For theta in H^1_0(0,1), set rho=sin^2(theta/2) and

  E(theta)=(J/2) integral |theta'|^2
           -kappa_t integral rho^2-lambda_c integral rho,
  F(p)=inf{E(theta): integral rho=p}.

The all-shape constrained minimization gives

  F(p)= (4 lambda_c/9) p^3 + O_J(p^4),    p down to 0.

This is not just a fixed sine trial. The Taylor expansion is

  E(theta)=Q(theta)+(lambda_c/360) integral theta^6
             +O_J(integral |theta|^8),
  Q(theta)=(J/2) integral |theta'|^2-(lambda_c/4) integral theta^2.

Q vanishes on the first Dirichlet sine mode and has a positive spectral gap
on its orthogonal complement. At fixed small mass p, minimizers satisfy
||theta||_{H^1}=O(sqrt(p)); writing theta=t sqrt(2)sin(pi x)+eta, one obtains
t^2=4p+O(p^2), Q(eta)>=0 and integral theta^6=160p^3+O(p^4). The sine trial
matches the lower coefficient. Comparing lower and upper bounds further
gives ||eta||_{H^1}=O(p^2), so non-sine relaxation cannot change the p^3
coefficient. The complete derivation is in the independent leaf packet
`leaf_continuum_sixth/RESULT.txt`.

Normalization matters: for theta=a sin(pi x), the fixed-amplitude energy is
(lambda_c/1152)a^6+O(a^8), whereas p=a^2/8+O(a^4). The mass coefficient is
therefore 512 times the fixed-amplitude coefficient, namely 4lambda_c/9.
For kappa<kappa_t, the sine-profile coherent value at mass p is
((lambda_c-3kappa)/2)p^2+O_J(p^3). This is a trial upper bound on the
all-shape constrained infimum; this package does not use a matching
all-shape lower coefficient below kappa_t. These continuum statements are
variational facts and do not imply either canonical number law.

3. A finite-chain full partition lower bound
--------------------------------------------
The coherent product trial and number-window argument prove the following
partial theorem. For each fixed J>0, tau>0:

  if 0<=kappa<kappa_t, then
    log Xi_L >= (1/2) log L-(1/2) log log L-O_{J,kappa,tau}(1);
  if kappa=kappa_t, then
    log Xi_L >= (2/3) log L-(1/2) log log L-O_{J,tau}(1).

Both inequalities hold for all sufficiently large L. They are lower bounds
only and do not say where the Gibbs number distribution is concentrated.

Proof. For a real spin-coherent product state with angles
theta_i=a sin(pi i/L), theta_0=theta_L=0, its independent occupation
probabilities are p_i=sin^2(theta_i/2). Factorization gives

  <Hcrit>=J sum_{i=0}^{L-1}(1-cos(theta_{i+1}-theta_i)),
  <N>=sum_i p_i,   <W>=sum_i p_i p_{i+1}.

Using the exact discrete sine sums and Taylor remainders uniform as a->0,

  <B_L>=((lambda_c-3kappa)/128)L a^4
          +((15kappa-lambda_c)/4608)L a^6
          +O_{J,kappa}(L a^8+a^4+a^2/L).

In particular, if u=<N> and u/L->0, then

  <B_L>=((lambda_c-3kappa)/2)u^2/L+o(1)
      for u=Theta(sqrt(L)), kappa<kappa_t;
  <B_L>=(4lambda_c/9)u^3/L^2+o(1)
      for u=Theta(L^(2/3)), kappa=kappa_t.

Choose M_L=sqrt(L) in the first regime and M_L=L^(2/3) in the second.
For u in [M_L/4,M_L/2], each coherent product state then has
<B_L><=C uniformly in L. Dephase the state with respect to N; this leaves
<B_L> unchanged because [B_L,N]=0.

Take Delta_L=A sqrt(M_L log L), where A is a sufficiently large fixed
constant. Select K_L>=c M_L/Delta_L means u_j in [M_L/4,M_L/2] with gaps at
least 3 Delta_L. Then K_L>=c sqrt(M_L/log L). For each mean choose a so
that sum_i p_i=u_j, and let I_j=[u_j-Delta_L,u_j+Delta_L] intersect Z.
Bernstein's inequality for the independent Bernoulli occupations gives
Pr_j{N notin I_j}<=2 L^-12 after increasing A. The intervals are pairwise
disjoint.

The variance V_j=sum_i p_i(1-p_i) is at least c M_L, since max_i p_i=o(1)
and u_j>=M_L/4. Fourier inversion gives the explicit anti-concentration
bound

  max_m Pr_j{N=m}
    <= (1/(2pi)) integral_{-pi}^{pi} exp(-2 V_j sin^2(t/2)) dt
    <= sqrt(pi)/(2 sqrt(2 V_j)) <= C M_L^-1/2.

Condition the dephased state on I_j and normalize, obtaining sigma_j. Its
number-sector atom probabilities are at most 2C M_L^-1/2. Conditioning has
vanishing energy cost: ||B_L||<=C_J L^3 and the discarded probability is
O(L^-12), while [B_L,1_{I_j}(N)]=0. Thus Tr sigma_j B_L<=C+o(1).

Finally, sigma=K_L^-1 sum_j sigma_j has orthogonal number-window supports.
Its von Neumann entropy is at least the Shannon entropy of its N measurement,
which is at least log(K_L sqrt(M_L)/(2C)). The Gibbs variational principle
log Xi_L>=S(sigma)-tau Tr(sigma B_L) gives

  log Xi_L >= log M_L-(1/2)log log L-O(1),

which is the claimed pair of bounds. This trial-state construction has no
state-preparation runtime claim; it is a variational lower bound on a trace.

4. Exact finite-size normal-order correction and fixed-m caveat
---------------------------------------------------------------
The one-magnon block is the Dirichlet path matrix with eigenvalues

  epsilon_{k,L}=4J(1-cos(pi k/L)),   1<=k<=L-1.

Thus its exact lowest shifted energy is

  E_{L,1}=4J L^2(1-cos(pi/L))-2J pi^2
         =-J pi^4/(6L^2)+J pi^6/(180L^4)+O(L^-6)<0.

The strict inequality follows from 1-cos x<x^2/2. Therefore a proposed
positive lower curvature E_{L,m}>=c m^2/L that includes every m>=1 is
false. The correction is negligible at the candidate scales: m/L^2 is
O(L^-3/2) at m~sqrt(L), and O(L^-4/3) at m~L^(2/3).

Imported internal sublemma (leaf_exact_xxz/RESULT.txt, §3): for fixed m,
projection of the product sine mode yields a sector Rayleigh upper bound

  E_{L,m} <= binom(m,2)(lambda_c-3kappa)/L+O_{J,kappa,m}(L^-2).

This imported estimate is not a matching lower bound, and fixed-m estimates
cannot be extrapolated to m growing with L. The exact one-magnon formula refutes only
the all-m claim; it does not refute a mesoscopic sector law.

5. Why the Gibbs number law remains UNKNOWN
-------------------------------------------
The variational bound gives a lower bound on Xi_L, not an upper bound and
not an asymptotic for any canonical sector trace. The exact decomposition is

  Z_{L,m}=exp(-tau E_{L,m}) R_{L,m},
  R_{L,m}=Tr_{N=m} exp[-tau(B_L-E_{L,m})],
  1<=R_{L,m}<=binom(L-1,m).

An expansion of E_{L,m} alone leaves R_{L,m} uncontrolled. Existing
fixed-m heat-trace limits, fixed-cutoff escape, log Xi_L=o(L), and
N/L->0 in probability are all compatible with many sublinear number scales.
The prediction sqrt(L) below kappa_t and L^(2/3) at kappa_t is a Landau
balance, not a theorem about the Gibbs state.

A decisive next gate is a uniform mesoscopic sector-trace theorem. For
r_L=sqrt(L), p=2 below threshold, or r_L=L^(2/3), p=3 at threshold, one
sufficient target is, uniformly for x in compact subsets of (0,infinity),

  Z_{L,floor(x r_L)} -> C_tau exp(-tau c x^p),

with a matching small-x estimate and the tail condition

  lim_{R->infinity} limsup_{L->infinity} r_L^-1
       sum_{m>R r_L} Z_{L,m}=0.

The fixed-m free Dirichlet-boson trace, ground-energy asymptotics alone, and a
coherent Rayleigh upper bound do not supply this gate. One must control both
the near-ground factor R_{L,m} and the all-sector tail. A possible route is
to combine a mesoscopic open-chain Bethe/sector lower bound with a uniform
excited-sector heat-trace estimate; neither has been completed here.

6. Primary-source boundary and provenance correction
----------------------------------------------------
Primary-source comparison is limited. Napiórkowski and Seiringer,
arXiv:1912.01967v2 (2019/2021), prove low-temperature free-energy asymptotics
for the one-dimensional quantum Heisenberg chain against the ideal Bose gas
of magnons. That is relevant low-temperature spin-wave context, but the
source scope does not give this open-chain critical-field mesoscopic number
law or its tricritical interaction crossover. Sklyanin, J. Phys. A 21
(1988), DOI 10.1088/0305-4470/21/10/015, develops the reflection-algebra
framework for integrable open-chain boundary conditions; it supplies a
methodological route, not the scaling limit proved here.

An earlier leaf note named Kim and Kiryu, arXiv:1706.02989, as an open-XXX
two-magnon source. The live arXiv metadata identifies that ID as “Structure
constants of operators on the Wilson loop from integrability” (hep-th), so
it is an ID/title mismatch and is not used as evidence for the spin-chain
claim. Do not cite it for the XXZ boundary equations.

No novelty or priority claim is made. The theorem and partition lower bound
