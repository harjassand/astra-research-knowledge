PRIMARY-SOURCE EQUIVALENCE AUDIT: MARGINAL OPEN FERROMAGNET
Date checked: 2026-10-08
Scope: primary-source equivalence only; no audit of the candidate proof algebra and no novelty/priority claim.

Verdict
-------
Status: UNKNOWN whether the proposed theorem is new or follows from a combination
of prior results. In the close primary sources checked, no theorem directly
implies or refutes the claimed open-chain ground-energy limit or the full-Fock
speed-L trace limit under the exact interface below. Several ingredients have
strong and precise precedents: the exclusion-process Fisher/DV cost, a periodic
fixed-density DV Gamma-limit, open-boundary Dirichlet hydrodynamic LDPs, and the
weak-anisotropy Landau-Lifshitz scaling. The remaining transfer is not licensed
by those sources' stated hypotheses.

Target fixed for this audit
---------------------------
Use S=L-1 occupation sites, n_i in {0,1}, N=sum_i n_i,
W=sum_{i=1}^{S-1}n_i n_{i+1}, and

 H_L = Hcrit - (kappa/L^2) W - (lambda/L^2) N,
 beta_L=tau L^2,  kappa>=0, tau>0,

with Hcrit as in outputs/research/sol_ground_xxz/RESULT.txt. In the occupation
basis, -Hcrit is the sub-Markov exclusion operator with bulk nearest-neighbor
swaps at rate 2J and diagonal killing 2J(n_1+n_S). It has no transition to
sector N-1. The candidate limit is

 lim L E_min(H_L) = inf_{theta in H^1_0(0,1), 0<=theta<=pi}
   { (J/2) int theta'^2 - kappa int rho^2 - lambda int rho },
 rho=sin^2(theta/2),

along with a separate claim for L^{-1} log Tr exp(-tau L^2 H_L).

The target Fisher term is exactly the SSEP density expression in the interior:
chi(rho)=rho(1-rho)=sin^2(theta)/4 and
rho'=(sin(theta)/2)theta', hence rho'^2/chi(rho)=theta'^2.
For a rate-2J bulk generator, the usual DV coefficient (1/4 times the
mobility-weighted gradient square) becomes J/2. The source normalization and
macroscopic interval can change the displayed numerical coefficient; the
structural Fisher/angle identity is exact.

Finite-L boundary bridge (proved algebraically here)
----------------------------------------------------
A genuine zero-reservoir SSEP has endpoint removal jumps, unlike the target's
diagonal killing. Ordered by particle number, its generator plus any diagonal
potential kappa W+lambda N is block triangular. Its N-diagonal block is exactly
the target killed operator -Hcrit|_N plus that potential. Therefore the full
zero-reservoir tilted matrix has the union of the target block spectra, and
Tr exp of that matrix is exactly the sum of target sector traces. This is an
exact finite-L bridge, not an asymptotic theorem. It would permit transfer of
a theorem proved at zero reservoir density with the needed initial/tilt/limit
hypotheses. The checked open-boundary sources below either exclude zero
reservoirs or establish only fixed-time path LDPs, so they do not supply that
transfer as written.

Primary-source ledger
---------------------

1. Bodineau--Dagallier, “Large deviations for out of equilibrium correlations
   in the symmetric simple exclusion process,” arXiv:2212.11561v2 (2 May 2024);
   Electron. J. Probab. 29 (2024), paper 63, DOI 10.1214/24-EJP1121.
   Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11561v2
   Exact close result: §2.2.1, equations (2.13)--(2.22). For the reversible
   open SSEP with equal reservoir density r in (0,1), it evaluates the finite-N
   DV functional on the specific product law with smooth profile rhohat which
   equals r in boundary neighborhoods. The resulting leading cost is
   proportional to int chi(rhohat)|partial_x logit(rhohat)|^2 dx
   = int rhohat'^2/[rhohat(1-rhohat)] dx, the same Fisher density integrand.
   This is a product-profile asymptotic, not an arbitrary-law Gamma-liminf.
   The reservoirs are positive-density, and the profile is pinned to that
   positive density near the ends. The paper's main Theorem 2.3 is about
   time-averaged two-point correlations, not the target tilted ground state.
   It does not cover zero-density killing, the kappa int rho^2 tilt, variable
   particle number, or the full-Fock trace. Strongest role: direct prior for
   the Fisher integrand and DV mechanism; no direct theorem transfer.

2. Bertini--Gabrielli--Landim, “Concurrent Donsker--Varadhan and hydrodynamical
   large deviations,” arXiv:2111.05892v1 (10 Nov 2021); Ann. Probab. 51 (2023),
   1298--1341, DOI 10.1214/22-AOP1619.
   Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05892 ;
   https://arxiv.org/html/2111.05892v1
   Exact results: Theorem 4.1 proves equi-coercivity and Gamma-convergence of
   the scaled finite-system DV functional; Theorem 2.1 gives the joint
   system-size/time LDP. The model is weakly asymmetric exclusion on a
   d-dimensional torus. The microscopic process is in fixed-K sectors with
   K/N^d -> m in (0,1); the limiting DV/hydrodynamic functional has mobility
   chi(rho)=rho(1-rho), and its static zero-current SSEP cost is the Fisher
   term. This is the strongest rigorous Gamma-convergence analogue checked.
   It is periodic and mass-conserving at a fixed density. It does not provide
   the open interval's zero trace, endpoint killing, optimization across N
   sectors, or the local rho^2 plus rho potential. The theorem cannot be
   transferred solely by identifying its bulk integrand.

3. Franco--Goncalves--Neumann, “Large Deviations for the SSEP with slow
   boundary: the non-critical case,” arXiv:2107.06998v1 (14 Jul 2021);
   ALEA 20 (2023), 359--394, DOI 10.30757/ALEA.v20-13.
   Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06998 ;
   https://arxiv.org/html/2107.06998v1
   Exact result: Theorem 2.3 is a speed-n path LDP for empirical density over
   a fixed macroscopic time horizon T, for n^2-accelerated SSEP with boundary
   creation/removal reservoirs. The Dirichlet regime is theta in (0,1), with
   reachable profiles having traces rho(t,0)=alpha, rho(t,1)=beta. The stated
   model assumes 0<alpha,beta<1; the initial deterministic profile is
   continuous and bounded away from 0 and 1. The paper notes that its rate
   function matches the theta=0 rate from earlier work, but its theorem is
   proved for theta in (0,1), not zero reservoir density. The target boundary
   rate is order one (theta=0) and is removal-only (alpha=beta=0); its killed
   sector operators are diagonal blocks of that death process. Thus this is
   a close path-LDP/boundary analogue, but not a theorem for the target. Also,
   fixed-T path LDP is not by itself the long-time tilted principal-eigenvalue
   limit or the trace at fixed scaled time.

4. Kipnis--Olla--Varadhan, “Hydrodynamics and large deviation for simple
   exclusion processes,” Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 42(2) (1989), 115--137,
   first published March 1989, DOI 10.1002/cpa.3160420202.
   Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.3160420202
   The primary abstract states a hydrodynamic limit for weakly asymmetric
   SSEP, a large-deviation property for the symmetric case, and treatment of
   a slow reaction (creation/annihilation). This is a dynamic hydrodynamic-LDP
   precedent, including reaction mechanisms, but the source result checked
   does not state the target's zero-reservoir endpoint-killed static tilt,
   open-boundary profile Gamma-limit, or full-Fock trace asymptotic. No direct
   implication established in this audit.

5. Donsker--Varadhan, “On a Variational Formula for the Principal Eigenvalue
   for Operators with Maximum Principle,” Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 72(3)
   (1975), 780--783, DOI 10.1073/pnas.72.3.780.
   Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.72.3.780
   This supplies the generic finite-state principal-eigenvalue / Feynman--Kac
   variational lineage. At each fixed L the target is a finite symmetric
   matrix problem, and the sector ground energy is its Rayleigh minimum.
   This finite-L principle does not yield the L->infinity Fisher Gamma-limit,
   handle zero-trace compactness, or control the exponential trace. The
   zero-reservoir block-triangular identity above is an exact route between
   finite-L eigenvalue/trace objects; it does not cure missing asymptotic
   hypotheses in the cited LDP results.

6. Miao--Ilievski--Gamayun, “Semi-classical quantisation of magnetic solitons
   in the anisotropic Heisenberg quantum chain,” arXiv:2010.07232v6 (22 Apr
   2021); SciPost Phys. 10 (2021), 086, DOI 10.21468/SciPostPhys.10.4.086.
   Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07232 ;
   https://arxiv.org/html/2010.07232v6
   Sections 3 and 3.1 consider spin-1/2 ferromagnetic XXZ with periodic
   boundary condition and weak-anisotropy scaling Delta-1=O(L^-2), deriving
   the classical anisotropic Landau--Lifshitz Hamiltonian. Their low-energy
   Bethe scaling concerns macroscopic magnon number with long wavelengths;
   it is semiclassical spectral analysis, not a proved open-chain
   Dirichlet Gamma-limit. It has no target endpoint killing, local rho^2
   potential/chemical tilt, arbitrary-state lower bound, or all-sector trace
   theorem. This is a close continuum-spin mechanism and scale, not an
   implication of the target theorem.

7. Ruijsenaars, “The continuum limit of the infinite isotropic Heisenberg
   chain in its ground state representation,” J. Funct. Anal. 39(1) (1980),
   75--84, DOI 10.1016/0022-1236(80)90021-X.
   Primary source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/002212368090021X
   The proved result is strong-resolvent convergence of an infinite isotropic
   chain in its ground-state representation to free nonrelativistic bosons;
   projections onto bound-magnon sectors vanish strongly although their
   energies converge. This is a fixed low-excitation/infinite-volume
   continuum result, not a finite open interval, weakly attractive
   macroscopic-density limit, or thermal trace theorem. It is a warning that
   convergence of selected energies alone does not control a Fock trace.

Ground energy versus thermal trace
---------------------------------
The principal eigenvalue / ground-energy limit and the speed-L free-energy
limit are distinct claims. Here dim(Hilbert)=2^(L-1), so the crude entropy
allowance log dim=(L-1)log 2 is already order L, the same as the proposed
normalization of log Z. A ground-state limit only gives
log Z >= -tau L^2 E_min; the reverse bound needs a low-energy density-of-
states/heat-trace estimate. Likewise, a fixed-L Feynman--Kac eigenvalue
formula or a fixed-T path LDP does not alone control the full trace.

Source boundary / restart gate
------------------------------
The precise unfilled source gate is a theorem (or a new proof) for the
zero-reservoir, order-one, death-only boundary chain, with the local tilted
potential kappa W+lambda N, and estimates strong enough to pass either to
its principal eigenvalue at speed L or, separately, to the fixed-scaled-time
trace at speed L. For a trace result one also needs entropy/density-of-states
control; a ground-energy Gamma-limit is insufficient. The finite-L block
triangular bridge makes a zero-reservoir process formulation exact, but
existing positive-reservoir, fixed-mass periodic, or finite-time dynamic
results do not establish this gate. Equivalence, external validation, and
novelty/priority therefore remain UNKNOWN.
