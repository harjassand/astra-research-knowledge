T02 — Clone obstruction and trace-quotiented mixed curvature
Version: v2 (append-only mathematical record, 2026-10-09)
Status: exact raw-potential counterexample internally derived and integer-certified; trace-curvature inequality is a conjecture supported by finite tests only; Frankl target unresolved. No novelty or priority claim.

Target and prior record
----------------------
The target remains Frankl's union-closed sets conjecture: if F is a finite union-closed family of distinct subsets and contains a nonempty member, then some coordinate belongs to at least half the members. Version v1 established a valid multi-copy inverse-multiplicity projection theorem, but its scalar residual has no sharp frequency lower bound. This record freezes a failure of a naive coordinate-summed statistic and a clone-invariant replacement conjecture. It does not prove the target.

Notation and the failed raw statistic
-------------------------------------
Let N=|F|, let A,B be independent uniform members of F, let U=A union B, and let r(S)=#{(A,B) in F^2:A union B=S}. For an element i write X_i=1_{i in A}, Y_i=1_{i in B}, p_i=P(i in A), and

  M_i = E[(X_i-p_i)(Y_i-p_i) log r(U)],
  K_raw = -sum_i M_i.

For every nonconstant coordinate trace, conditioning on the two i-bits gives the exact identity

  M_i = p_i^2(1-p_i)^2 Delta_i,
  Delta_i = c_11-c_10-c_01+c_00,
  c_xy = E[log r(U) | X_i=x,Y_i=y].

This follows by expanding the centered product over the four independent Bernoulli(p_i) cells. Constants have M_i=0 and do not need conditional means.

Exact cloning counterexample to K_raw >= 0
------------------------------------------
Fix m>=2. On the ground set [m] plus M new elements C={c_1,...,c_M}, define

  F_{m,M} = 2^[m] union {T},   T=[m] union C.

This family has N=s+1 members, where s=2^m, and is union closed: the cube is union closed, while unioning T with any member gives T. The M new coordinate traces are all the same singleton trace {T}; this family is unchanged as a semilattice when M changes, but K_raw counts that trace M times.

The pair-union multiplicities are exact:

  r(S)=3^|S| for every S subset [m],
  r(T)=N^2-s^2=2s+1.

For a new coordinate i in C, p_i=1/N and q_i=1-p_i=s/N. Conditional on X_i=Y_i=0, both inputs are independent uniform cube members, so

  c_00 = E[|A union B|] log 3 = (3m/4) log 3.

If either input contains i, that input is T, hence U=T and c_10=c_01=c_11=log(2s+1). Therefore

  Delta_clone=(3m/4)log 3-log(2s+1),
  M_clone=(s^2/N^4) Delta_clone,
  K_clone=-M_clone.

In particular Delta_clone>0 when m=6, since (9/2)log 3>log 129, so each cloned coordinate contributes negatively to K_raw.

For completeness, the original cube-coordinate contribution can also be evaluated in closed form. Fix i in [m]. Its frequency is

  p=(s/2+1)/N.

For proper output S subset [m], r(S)=3^|S|. Summing (X_i-p)(Y_i-p) over all ordered pairs with union S gives

  3^(|S|-1) (1-p)(1-3p), if i in S,
  p^2 3^|S|,                         if i not in S.

Summing these terms against log r(S)=|S|log 3 uses

  sum_{R subset [m]\{i}} 3^|R|(1+|R|)=4^(m-2)(3m+1),
  sum_{R subset [m]\{i}} 3^|R||R|=3(m-1)4^(m-2).

For union T, the unnormalized sum of (X_i-p)(Y_i-p) over its pairs is

  (1-p)^2 + 2(1-p)s(1/2-p).

After division by N^2 and simplification this gives

  M_old = s^2/[16N^4] * [(-s^2-4s+3m-3)log 3 - 4log(2s+1)].

Thus the contribution of the m original coordinates is the M-independent quantity

  K_base = -m M_old
         = m s^2/[16N^4] * [(s^2+4s-3m+3)log 3 + 4log(2s+1)].

At m=6, s=64, N=65, this is 0.411661186384...; each cloned coordinate contributes -0.0000192615158553... to K_raw. The threshold for a negative raw sum is M>21372.21127744..., so M=22000 suffices. Direct enumeration of all 65^2 ordered pairs agrees with both closed forms to below 3e-12.

There is also an exact sign certificate, not just a floating-point comparison. At m=6 and M=22000,

  K_raw=(q^2 p^2/16)*[-1,557,978 log 3 + 352,024 log 129],
  p=1/65, q=64/65.

The integer inequalities

  129^40 < 3^177,
  177*352,024 < 40*1,557,978

imply log(129)/log(3)<177/40<1,557,978/352,024, so the bracket and K_raw are strictly negative. The runnable code checks both integer inequalities using arbitrary-precision integers. The family still has max frequency 33/65>1/2, attained by each original cube coordinate, so this is not a counterexample to Frankl. It refutes the unweighted raw-coordinate sign claim: at fixed N=65 the negative sum tends to minus infinity as M tends to infinity.

Sign check on the Boolean cube
------------------------------
For F=2^[1], N=2, p=1/2, and the four values of 1_{X union Y=1} are 0,1,1,1. Direct averaging gives

  E[(X-1/2)(Y-1/2) 1_{X union Y=1}] = -1/16,
  M_i=-log(3)/16,
  -M_i=+log(3)/16.

Thus the Boolean cube is consistent with positive K_raw. The clone obstruction, not the cube, is the sign failure. Constant traces are omitted in normalized curvature calculations because their conditional quadrants have zero probability; they do not separate any two members.

Clone-invariant trace curvature: exact definition
-------------------------------------------------
Let h:F->{0,1} be any nonconstant join homomorphism, meaning

  h(A union B)=h(A) OR h(B).

An ordinary element coordinate is such a homomorphism; its one-set is a prime filter of the finite join-semilattice F. For p_h=P(h(A)=1), define c_xy(h)=E[log r(U)|h(A)=x,h(B)=y] under independent uniform A,B and

  Delta(h)=c_11(h)-c_10(h)-c_01(h)+c_00(h).

If I is a finite family of nonconstant join homomorphisms separating F (the map A -> (h(A))_{h in I} is injective), define

  C_I(F) = -sum_{h in I} Delta(h).

The proposed semilattice inequality is

  C_I(F) >= log r(T),       T=union(F).                    (C)

It is invariant to exact coordinate cloning when I contains one representative of each distinct trace. Since the pairs with at least one operand T all have union T, r(T)>=2N-1; (C) would therefore imply C_I>=log(2N-1), a size-free logarithmic curvature bound. The m=6 cloned example has C_I=6.5057551020... for the m old traces and one clone trace, while log r(T)=log 129=4.8598124044.... Thus quotienting exact duplicate traces removes the amplification counterexample.

Finite evidence for (C), all explicitly scoped
-----------------------------------------------
Runnable implementation: clone_curvature.py. It uses Python's standard library and finite log evaluations in double precision; family membership, unions, pair multiplicities, homomorphism identities, and separation checks are exact.

1. Exhaustive original-coordinate scan: all 4,943 non-singleton union-closed families on [4] were enumerated. For each family, the code checked every separating subset of its distinct nonconstant coordinate traces: 5,233 separating subsets total. The minimum observed C_I-log r(T) was zero to floating precision (the full Boolean cube); none was negative. Among 3,484 coordinate cases with frequency 2/N through strictly below 1/2, the minimum observed -M_i was 0.01406223729..., with no negative case. These are finite diagnostics, not a proof for larger families.
2. Abstract semilattice-trace scan: the code enumerated every nonconstant join homomorphism F->{0,1} for each union-closed family on ground sets of size 1 through 4 with 2<=N<=8, including prime filters not present as original element coordinates. It checked every separating subfamily of those homomorphisms. This covered 3,482 families, 17,577 homomorphisms, and 13,800 separating subfamilies; no negative C_I-log r(T) occurred, and the minimum was zero for the two-member family with its unique nonconstant homomorphism. Constant homomorphisms were excluded because they cannot separate and have undefined four-cell conditional means.
3. Full-cube abstract trace scan: for F=2^[4], every nonconstant join homomorphism is OR over a nonempty subset of the four atoms. The code checks all 2,048 separating subfamilies of these 15 prime filters; the minimum C_I-log r(T) is zero, with C_I=log r(T)=log 81 at a minimum family.
4. Random generated coordinate-trace scan: with seed 939697, 500 random generator closures were attempted for each ground-set size 5,6,7,8. After retaining 1,866 families of size at most 40, every separating subset of distinct nonconstant original traces was checked. The minimum gap was zero, attained at an N=2 family. This is a seeded sample, not exhaustive, and it does not enumerate all abstract homomorphisms on these larger semilattices.
5. Exact clone check: for m=6 and M=22,000, pair enumeration gives K_raw=-0.012092162433...; the integer-power certificate above proves the sign exactly. The trace-quotiented raw K is +0.411641924868..., while C_I=6.50575510201... exceeds log r(T).

The key poset reformulation and the missing proof
-------------------------------------------------
The set P_h={A:h(A)=1} is a prime filter: it is upward closed and A union B lies in P_h exactly when A or B lies in P_h. Its complement is a union-closed lower ideal. A separating family of such filters embeds the semilattice into a Boolean lattice by A -> {h:h(A)=1}; joins become coordinatewise OR. Thus (C) is a law about nonredundant prime-filter embeddings, not about a chosen ground-set labeling.

There is a useful exact expansion. Put F_0^h={A:h(A)=0}, F_1^h={A:h(A)=1}, with sizes n_0,n_1, and let r^h_xy(S) count pairs with union S and h(A)=x,h(B)=y. Then

  -Delta(h) = sum_{S in F} log r(S) *
    [-r^h_00(S)/n_0^2 + r^h_10(S)/(n_0 n_1)
      +r^h_01(S)/(n_0 n_1) - r^h_11(S)/n_1^2].

For h(S)=0, only the 00 term exists and the bracket is -r(S)/n_0^2. For h(S)=1, r^h_00(S)=0 and r^h_10+r^h_01+r^h_11=r(S), so the bracket simplifies to

  [n_1 r(S)-N r^h_11(S)]/(n_0 n_1^2).

Equivalently, if a,b are independent uniform members, U=a union b, and

  t_h(S)=P(h(a)=h(b)=1 | U=S), p_h=n_1/N,

then the coefficient multiplying log r(S) in -Delta(h), after averaging over U with law P(U=S)=r(S)/N^2, is

  gamma_h(S) = -1/(1-p_h)^2,                                  if h(S)=0,
  gamma_h(S) = (p_h-t_h(S))/(p_h^2(1-p_h)),                  if h(S)=1.

It has E[gamma_h(U)]=0, and C_I=E[log r(U) sum_h gamma_h(U)]. This is an exact reduction, not a proof of (C). The unresolved step is to show that the prime-filter separation and join law force this covariance to be at least log r(T), despite the fact that individual terms can be negative (the clone trace has Delta>0). The finite evidence includes arbitrary nonconstant join homomorphisms on the small scanned semilattices, but no general charging argument has been found.

One natural shortcut for a private-top induction would be to upper-bound E log r(U) by log r(T). That statement is false. For the union-closed family with masks

  (2,3,5,7,20,21,22,23,33,34,35,37,39,49,51,53,54,55,63),

N=19, T=63, r(T)=37, and E log r(U)=3.6834694341... > log 37=3.6109179126.... The strict inequality has the exact integer certificate

  product_{S in F} r(S)^{r(S)} > 37^(19^2).

The runnable code verifies union closure and this arbitrary-precision integer comparison. Thus the private-coordinate term c_00 cannot generally be bounded by log r(T); any induction on prime-filter cuts needs a more refined charge.

Relation to the sharp frequency target
--------------------------------------
(C), even if proved, is not yet Frankl's conjecture. It gives a normalized curvature sum. In a feasible perturbation of the independent pair law, the corresponding unnormalized interaction is p_h^2(1-p_h)^2 Delta(h); rare prime filters can make these terms tiny. A further nonlinear or multi-copy coupling is needed to turn (C) into a frequency-sensitive entropy gain without a deterioration depending on the smallest filter. No such conversion is derived here. The target remains unresolved.

Prior-art boundary
------------------
The pair inverse-multiplicity law, KL projection, and double-centering approach in v1 are already present in Yunjiang Jiang, arXiv:2609.08291v1, §10 (especially §§10.1–10.7, accessed 2026-10-09); v1 records the exact theorem and bound. Costa–Sadhu, arXiv:2610.02295v1, Theorem 1.2 and §7, reports the 0.3828852549667978 bound and the pointwise product-relaxation ceiling near 0.3828852599667. These are the relevant scalar-entropy boundaries. The trace-quotiented curvature inequality (C) is an unproved candidate developed here; no novelty, priority, or external-validation claim is made. The original objective remains open in this record.
