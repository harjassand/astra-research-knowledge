8. EXACT MAX-PRIOR CAPACITY AND CONVEX OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS

Every state has the same flag weights, vacuum weight1-w and active
weights w/K. Its entropy is the corresponding classical flag entropy.
Any mean has those weights and entropy at most that flag entropy plus
(w/K)sum_j ln(N_j+1). The design mean reaches every block maximum
simultaneously. Thus MAX-PRIOR capacity is EXACTLY

    =(K-1)ln2/2+(1/K)sum_j ln(1+2^(-j)).             (14)

For the normalized active family let sigma_*=(1/K)sum_j I_j/(N_j+1).
For every promised state and effect only its block diagonal matters.
If f_j=<psi^N_j|F_j|psi^N_j>, then p=(1/K)sum_j f_j and
q>= (1/K)sum_j f_j/(N_j+1). Equality is attained by F_j=f_j P_(psi,N_j).
The function p ln(p/q) is jointly convex, being the perspective of
x ln x. A maximum over0<=f_j<=1 therefore occurs at a vertex. For a
given number of selected flags, selecting the largest j minimizes q.
Consequently at this common center the divergence is exactly

 R_K=max_(0<=r<K) [(K-r)/K]
            ln[(K-r)/(sum_(j=r)^(K-1) 1/(2^j+1))].   (15)

It is also the MINIMAX convex-center radius. For ANY sigma in the
active convex hull, each flag trace is1/K. For the tail effect
F_(psi,r)=sum_(j>=r)P_(psi,N_j), p=(K-r)/K, and the SAME design prior
has mean q=(1/K)sum_(j>=r)1/(N_j+1). Jensen for -ln q shows that some
promised state has divergence at least the r expression in (15).
This holds for every r and every convex center; sigma_* attains the upper
bound already derived. Thus no max-prior/radius cost is hidden in a
chosen center or a selected prior.

Orthogonal vacuum dilution has the EXACT scaling

 R(E)=w R_K.                                        (16)

For any active rho,sigma and total rho'=(1-w)vac+w rho,
sigma'=(1-w)vac+w sigma, an effect gives
p=(1-w)f_0+w p_a,q=(1-w)f_0+w q_a. Joint convexity gives
p ln(p/q)<=w p_a ln(p_a/q_a)<=w D_obs(rho||sigma).
Conversely set the vacuum effect to zero and choose any active effect,
which attains the scaled divergence. Convex hulls correspond under the
same affine embedding, so infima and worst-state suprema give (16).

To quantify (15), with n=K-r,
