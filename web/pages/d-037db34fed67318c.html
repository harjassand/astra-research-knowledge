2. Francesco Amoroso and Carlo Viola, "Approximation measures for logarithms of algebraic numbers," Ann. Scuola Norm. Sup. Pisa (4) 30 (2001), 225–249. 3. Raffaele Marcovecchio and Carlo Viola, "Irrationality and nonquadraticity measures for logarithms of algebraic numbers," J. Aust. Math. Soc. 92 (2012), 237–267, https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788712000274 . Full primary PDF downloaded and introductory pages 237–241 inspected, including Definitions 2.1 and Lemma 2.2. The paper provides Q(alpha)-irrationality and nonquadraticity measures from double integrals and all conjugate asymptotics, for selected algebraic arguments. Its explicit exponents depend on integral growth, degree and the leading coefficient. The displayed 2delta is a Dirichlet LOWER bound for approximation over K, not a universal UPPER bound for rational approximation to log alpha. Mistaking these would create a false novelty/no-novelty judgment.

4. Noriko Hirata-Kohno, Ryuji Muroi and Yusuke Washio, "Approximation measures for shifted logarithms of algebraic numbers via effective Poincare-Perron," arXiv:2406.10820v2, revised 10 December 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10820v2 ; complete HTML downloaded. Sections 1–3 and Theorem 3.4 were inspected. The theorem requires spectral inequalities at the embeddings and lambda=1/delta-log Q/(d log E)>0. Q involves denominator and conjugate spectral products. It establishes an effective K-approximation measure lambda^(-1), for a shifted logarithmic function. Its literal theorem is not the degree-only 2d statement above. Full implications over every permitted transformation have not been exhaustively ruled out.

5. Raffaele Marcovecchio, "The Rhin–Viola method for log 2," Acta Arith. 139 (2009), 147–184; author's institutional record https://ricerca.unich.it/handle/11564/648605?mode=complete checked.
