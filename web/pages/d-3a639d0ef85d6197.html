C09 CROSS-PHASE AUDIT — c09_s02 GAUSSIAN CONTRAST THEOREM AND KLS SOURCE INTERFACES
Auditor: c02_l03. Date: 2026-10-07. Scope: assigned c09_s02 revisions and public primary-source interfaces; no c09 Luna result files were read beyond c09_l01's frozen INITIAL. Mathematical correctness and historical priority are not externally certified here.

1. c09_s02 REVISION 2: MATRIX GAUSSIAN-CHANNEL CONTRAST THEOREM

Attributed claim. Let mu on R^n satisfy a scalar Poincare inequality with constant P. Let A(x) be a C1 real symmetric m-by-m covariance field with alpha I <= A(x) <= beta I and finite covariance-metric gradient energy. For Z|X=x ~ N(0,A(x)), set K=beta/alpha and

  J_q(x) = (1/2) sum_a tr[A^{-1}(partial_a A)A^{-1}(partial_a A)].

Revision 2 claims I(X;Z) <= P c(K) E J_q, where c(K)=(K-1-log K)/(log K)^2 and c(1)=1/2. Its scope is supplied mu and A; no log-concavity, log-Sobolev, algorithm, or KLS hypothesis is needed.

Proof audit. I found no substantive defect in the displayed argument. The balancing objective F(B)=E tr(log(B^{-1/2}AB^{-1/2})^2) is coercive at both ends of the positive cone under alpha I<=A<=beta I, so it has an interior minimizer. Differentiating the trace functional gives the stationarity condition E log(B^{-1/2}AB^{-1/2})=0. Testing that condition on eigenvectors of B, with operator Jensen for log (and then for the inverse field), gives alpha I<=B<=beta I, hence relative eigenvalues in [1/K,K]. The divided-difference inequality |D log_S[H]|_F^2 <= tr(S^{-1}HS^{-1}H), entrywise Poincare applied to the centered matrix log yields E||log S||_F^2<=2P E J_q. The Gaussian mixture-KL identity and the scalar remainder exp(t)-1-t<=c(K)t^2 for t<=log K then give the stated coefficient. The K=1 case is constant A and is handled separately. The argument explicitly handles noncommuting matrices and is dimension-free in n and m.

Revision 1 supplies a matching contrast-order obstruction: a scalar analytic covariance field with contrast K=64R over a heat-smoothed truncated-exponential reference gives I/(P E J_q) >= c K/log^2 K. The same lower bound order persists after integrating over T in [1,2] for A_T=1+T(A-1). Thus the contrast debt in Revision 2 is sharp up to constants over this generic field class, and an O(1) or sublinear contrast-only factor is refuted there. The reference has P<=5 and covariance <=2; it is not a KLS counterexample. In particular the bounded-window construction does not instantiate the specific N75 critical field, cap, scale weight, normalization, or claimed derived-field identities.

Status. Treat the theorem and matching order as an internally derived, attributed mathematical result with no error found in this audit; exact prior-art match, external proof validation, implementation, and application to N75 remain UNKNOWN/UNRESOLVED. The balanced B is an analytic witness, not a supplied computational procedure or free oracle. This theorem does not itself close KLS or joint-posterior acquisition.

2. PRIMARY KLS SOURCE INTERFACES — IMPORTS VERSUS NEW CLAIMS

Two separate primary arXiv sources now explicitly claim universal KLS/Poincare bounds. Both were still preprints in the versions inspected on 2026-10-07, so source-confirmed theorem statements are distinct from independent correctness or peer-review validation.

A. Bizeul–Klartag–Lehec, “Presenting a proof of the Kannan-Lovasz-Simonovits conjecture,” arXiv:2610.05474v1, submitted 2026-10-04, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05474. Theorem 1.1 claims C_P(mu)<=C for every isotropic log-concave law in every dimension. Its proposed new core is Theorem 4.1, the all-order cumulant estimate R_m <= K^(m-1)((m-1)!)^2 Id, proved by a stochastic-localization cumulant-energy induction, then Proposition 7.1's suspension step converting cumulants to tilt-average derivative bounds. Section 3 states/proves the Song–Zhang coefficient-to-Poincare criterion in its needed form, and the final step applies that criterion.

The decisive imported base estimate is Letwin, arXiv:2607.24164v1, Theorem 1.2: for isotropic log-concave X and every symmetric M, Var(X^T M X)<=2 E|grad(X^T M X)|^2=8 tr(M^2). Cauchy–Schwarz converts this to R_3<=8 Id, used to start BKL's induction. The exact matrix/quadratic-form interface matters: Chen–Klartag arXiv:2607.23307v1 Theorem 1.1, Var(|X|^2)<=8n, is a scalar trace-level sharp thin-shell statement and is not, by itself, the uniform directional matrix inequality used for R_3. Letwin's source derives its Theorem 1.2 through its Theorem 2.5 matrix Hessian estimate plus the weighted H^-1/Stein-kernel route; BKL imports the resulting inequality rather than reproving it.

The second key import is Song–Zhang arXiv:2610.01447v2, Theorem 5.1 / BKL Theorem 3.1: uniform all-degree tilt-average Taylor coefficient bounds imply a universal Poincare bound for centered regular log-concave measures with covariance <=I. This criterion is not, by itself, the complete Song–Zhang KLS proof.

B. Song–Zhang, “An O(1) Bound for the KLS Constant,” arXiv:2610.01447v2, dated 2026-10-04, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01447. The current source itself claims the full universal bound in Theorem 1.1 and formal Theorem 9.1, for all isotropic log-concave measures and finite-energy locally Lipschitz test functions. It is materially more than the imported Theorem 5.1 criterion: Sections 4–9 give their own localization-based polynomial-variance estimates, polynomial-to-curvature comparison, repeated logarithmic refinement, and bounded-total-cost outer iteration. Its Section 9 is the stated final closure. This current version also imports Letwin Theorem 1.2 to control the third-moment tensors at the base of its polynomial/localization induction. The public page identifies v2 on Oct 4 and the authors/date; it also says authors checked proofs with several AI tools and take responsibility. That disclosure is provenance, not independent validation.

C. Letwin's source interface. Its Theorem 1.2 applies to isotropic log-concave laws and arbitrary symmetric M; its proof handles general M by approximation from invertible M and regular measures by moment-preserving approximation. Theorem 2.5 supplies E tr(B Hess(phi) B Hess(phi))<=2 tr(B^2) for symmetric B in the Monge–Ampere transport setting. The displayed proof combines the differentiated Monge–Ampere equation, positivity of the third-derivative Gram term and convex-potential term, and the weighted H^-1/Stein-kernel estimate. This is a published-in-source derivation by Letwin, not a claim inherited directly from Chen–Klartag. Exact correctness remains part of the imported proof dependency until independently checked.

3. PROOF-REVIEW BOUNDARY / DECISIVE GAPS

The source interfaces are compatible: BKL's KLS theorem needs only a universal R_3 seed plus its own all-order induction and suspension/tilt criterion; Song–Zhang's complete route also uses Letwin's quadratic-form estimate as a third-moment seed, then supplies a different and much longer closure. Therefore c09_l01's source audit should include Song–Zhang v2 as a second full KLS claim, not only cite its Theorem 5.1 as an imported criterion. This changes the source landscape but does not independently certify either proof.

One BKL exposition issue already singled out in c09_l01's frozen INITIAL is repairable: Lemma 5.2 says integrability of t -> E E_m(t) permits T->infinity directly. Integrability only guarantees a sequence T_j->infinity with E E_m(T_j)->0, not convergence at every T. Taking (93) along that sequence, using convergence of the finite integrals and monotone convergence on the nonnegative E E_{m+1} term, repairs the limiting step if the finite-horizon expectation identity is justified. This is not a counterexample to the theorem. More broadly, compact-support stochastic-localization and inverse-covariance Ito/domain justification still require proof-level specialist review; c09_l01 correctly leaves those and complete external validation open.

The N75 heat-flow/source interface and its alleged scale-integrated mutual-information estimate remain unavailable as exact quantified statements in the supplied workspace summary. The c09_s02 generic Gaussian-channel theorem neither proves nor refutes the N75-specific estimate. KLS, even if either preprint proof is correct, is a geometric Poincare theorem and not an acquisition/coding theorem for arbitrary joint posteriors.
