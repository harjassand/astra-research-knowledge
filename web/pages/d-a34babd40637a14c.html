## G4. Weaker access cannot supply the mean oracle uniformly

The mass-value lower bound correctly uses point masses δ_v, whose generating monomials are real stable. Before a first oracle hit, answers are all zero; for each fixed private seed, q queries plus a last guess can identify at most q+1 secrets. Uniform sampling TV≤ε therefore requires q≥(1−ε)2^n−1. The input does not include a symbolic monomial, a feasible point, or a base sample, so these richer interfaces are not subject to this lower bound.

For base samples, δ_0 and Bernoulli(p), p=(1+e^B)^(-1), become δ_0 and Bernoulli(1/2) after the known field B. Until a success, sample streams and private randomness couple exactly. Under δ_0 the stopping count N_0 depends only on private randomness; the chance of separation before its stopping time is ≤pE_0N_0. Accurate outputs under both hypotheses differ by at least 1/2−2ε. The resulting expected-sample lower bound (1/2−2ε)(1+e^B) is therefore correct for ε<1/4. It does not establish hardness of averaged drift error along typical self-generated observations, or of sampling a law when fresh samples are supplied.
