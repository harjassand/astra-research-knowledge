INITIAL CHECKPOINT — c09_l07
Statistical memory: exact two-mode joint-posterior diagnostic and periodic codec

STATUS AND STRONGEST CLAIM

For the one-observation family on the unit circle

  f_v(t) = 1 + (1/4)[v_1 cos(2 pi t) + v_2 cos(4 pi t)],
  t ~ dt,  ||v||_2 <= 1,

the two centered score features U=cos(2 pi t) and V=cos(4 pi t) have an exactly
diagonal, full-rank Fisher Gram matrix (1/32) I_2, but their joint law is
singular and dependent: V=2 U^2-1 and Corr(U^2,V)=1. An exact rational
Laurent-polynomial check verifies these identities. Despite two orthogonal
score modes, the observation is one scalar and admits a positive reference-
preserving codec with J periodic labels and uniform TV error at most
3025/(588 J^2). At epsilon=1/1000, J=72 gives the exact certificate
3025/3048192 <= 1/1000. Thus the full finite two-mode family's classical
archive cost is O(log(1/epsilon)); the standard bounded-proper-score,
entropy-distortion argument on v=+/-e_1 gives the matching quantum/hybrid
lower order (1/2) log_2(1/epsilon)-O(1).

This is a scoped construction and counterexample check, not a new
dimension-uniform law. The broad same-Fisher/log-vs-log-squared comparison is
already in work/cycle1/statistical_memory.txt. This worker contributes a
smaller exact dependence witness and an independently derived, rationally
certified periodic codec instance. No novelty or priority claim is made.

INTERFACE AND BOUNDARIES

The encoder gets one t drawn from f_v and does not get v. It retains one label
j in {0,...,J-1}; all input-dependent memory is counted by D=J. The decoder
outputs a fresh continuous Y. The guarantee is sup_{||v||<=1}
TV(Law_v(Y), f_v dt) <= epsilon. Encoder/decoder randomness is independent
of v; no input-dependent side record is hidden. The construction uses the
known family and a continuous ideal probability kernel. Finite-bit random
sampling, finite precision, calibration/acquisition of the model, and the
cost of generating a continuous output are outside this certificate. An
atomic floating-point output is not TV-close to a continuous target.

EXACT JOINT-POSTERIOR CHECK

Write z=exp(2 pi i t). Represent cos(2 pi k t) by the Laurent polynomial
(z^k+z^{-k})/2; integration against dt is exactly extraction of the constant
coefficient. The script checks, using Fraction arithmetic,

  E U = E V = 0,
  E U^2 = E V^2 = 1/2,  E UV = 0,
  V - (2U^2-1) = 0 identically,
  E U^2 V = 1/4,  Var(U^2)=1/8, Var(V)=1/2.

Consequently Cov(U^2,V)=1/4 and Corr(U^2,V)=1 exactly. With rho=1/4 the
likelihood is normalized and strictly positive: by Cauchy-Schwarz,
|v_1 U+v_2 V| <= sqrt(2), hence f_v > 1-sqrt(2)/4 > 5/8. The score vector
is rho(U,V), so its exact Gram/Fisher matrix is

  rho^2 [[E U^2, E UV], [E UV, E V^2]] = (1/32) I_2.

Its rank is two; the joint feature distribution nevertheless lies on the
parabola V=2U^2-1, a one-dimensional subset of R^2. This verifies directly
why orthogonal tangent directions do not certify independent posterior
coordinates. It does not by itself prove an archive lower bound.

PERIODIC POSITIVE CODEC DERIVATION

For J>=3 equally spaced nodes t_j=j/J on the circle, let phi_j be the
periodic triangular hat centered at t_j, supported within circular distance
1/J, with height one. The hats are nonnegative and sum to one, and
integral phi_j=1/J. The blind encoder samples j with probability phi_j(t).
Given j, the decoder outputs Y with density J phi_j(y). The resulting
reference-preserving positive kernel is

  K f(y) = J sum_j phi_j(y) integral phi_j(t) f(t) dt.

In particular K1=1 exactly. The conditional density J phi_j around its node
is symmetric triangular, with mean t_j and variance 1/(6 J^2). If f is
periodic C^2 with ||f''||_infinity <= M, Taylor's theorem bounds the error
between the hat average and f(t_j) by M/(12 J^2). Linear interpolation of
the node values has error at most M/(8 J^2). Therefore

  ||Kf-f||_infinity <= 5M/(24 J^2),
  TV(Kf dt, f dt) <= 5M/(48 J^2).

For the stated family,

  f_v''(t) = -4 pi^2 (1/4)[v_1 U(t)+4v_2 V(t)],
  ||f_v''||_infinity <= pi^2 sqrt(17) < 5 pi^2 < 5(22/7)^2 = 2420/49.

Thus the exact rational TV certificate is TV <= (3025/588)/J^2. The script
chooses the least integer J>=3 meeting the rational bound for a requested
rational epsilon. For epsilon=1/1000 it returns J=72 and
TV<=3025/3048192<=1/1000. The alphabet has 72 labels (log_2 72 ideal bits;
7 fixed-length binary label bits). As epsilon tends to zero, J=O(epsilon^-1/2)
and log_2 J=(1/2)log_2(1/epsilon)+O(1).

LOWER-ORDER MATCHING BOUND

Restrict to the supplied pair v=+/-e_1. Their equal mixture is dt and the
posterior of the sign given t is p(t)=(1+rho U(t))/2. A bounded proper binary
score whose Bayes-risk curvature on this posterior interval is 2/rho gives
the standard posterior-regret transfer: any blind TV-epsilon reconstruction
under both alternatives must satisfy

  E_dt[(U(X)-U(Y))^2] <= 4 epsilon/rho.

Under dt, U=cos(2 pi t) has the arcsine density on [-1,1] and differential
entropy h(U)=ln(pi/2). The Gaussian entropy-distortion inequality and quantum
data processing/Holevo bound then give, for any total hybrid-memory dimension
D and any independent shared seed,

  ln D >= h(U) - (1/2) ln(2 pi e * 4 epsilon/rho)
        = (1/2) ln(1/epsilon) + O(1).

This lower uses only the two parameter points and is not a multi-query claim.
Together with the classical codec, it gives matching logarithmic order for
this finite two-mode family. The proper-score posterior-regret and
entropy-distortion steps are elementary; the quantum dimension bound is
standard Holevo/data processing. Exact priority for this particular family
and codec was not searched exhaustively.
