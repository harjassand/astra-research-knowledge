# BMVZ/ABN structure and the resolvent channel `K_a (I-H)^(-1)`

**Result.** BMVZ's path and layer structure gives a polynomial-size term generator and polynomial-time *specified-entry* access to each product clause. It does not give a polynomial row-list for the exact incidence block `K_a (I-H)^(-1)`. In fact, a simple hardness-preserving singleton-layer augmentation of the ABN-derived BMVZ start has a valid exact PREPARE completion for which one such channel has at least `2^R-1` nonzeros in one row, where `R` is BMVZ's polynomial repetition count. This is an output-size lower bound for that legal family and completion, separate from any lower bound for `C_0=I-VV*`.

The row lower bound does **not** prove that the same channel is dense for every fixed, unmodified ABN reduction output, every PREPARE completion, or every common isometry. It does prove that the BMVZ path generator, constant-color decomposition, and ABN tensor-network representation cannot by themselves certify a uniform polynomial row-list for this incidence construction.

## Exact target and encoding

Use the complement-LCU interface from the cycle3 construction. The logical Hamiltonian is a normalized positive projection sum

\[
H=\sum_{j=1}^{M}\pi_j h_j,\qquad \sum_j\pi_j=1,
\]

and the SELECT list also contains an identity reflection `R_0=I`. Put `R_j=I-2h_j`, `chi_0=1/sqrt(2)`, and `chi_j=sqrt(pi_j/2)`. For a PREPARE unitary `A` with first column `chi`, the data blocks of the isometry are

\[
K_a=\sum_{j=0}^{M}\overline{A_{ja}}\chi_jR_j,\qquad K_0=I-H.
\]

The exact incidence enforcer in the current interface has cross-blocks `K_aY^(-1)`, with `Y=I-H`. Thus row-listing this matrix is the gate under examination; this note makes no claim about the separate canonical projector `I-VV*`.

There is a particularly useful legal completion. Take the second PREPARE column to be

\[
A_{0a}=1/\sqrt2,\qquad A_{ja}=-\sqrt{\pi_j/2}\quad(j\ge1).
\]

It has unit norm and is orthogonal to `chi`. It can be completed to a unitary by exact algebraic Givens rotations. Then

\[
K_a=\tfrac12 I-\tfrac12\sum_j\pi_j(I-2h_j)=H.
\]

Consequently the target channel for this valid completion is

\[
K_a(I-H)^{-1}=H(I-H)^{-1}=(I-H)^{-1}-I. \tag{1}
\]

The last identity is exact and uses only that `H` commutes with its resolvent.
For rational BMVZ weights, these column entries are square roots of polynomial-bit rationals. If the label register is padded to a power of two, split the silent zero-projector weight among positive-weight zero terms; this changes neither `H` nor the two displayed columns. The support result is for the exact algebraic circuit, not an approximate finite-gate synthesis.

## What BMVZ and ABN actually provide

BMVZ Definition 1.6 starts from `H=sum_chi w_chi H_chi`, where `H_chi` is an expectation of commuting projections. For each color it builds an expander on the **clause labels**. A length-`t` path `f` produces the projection

\[
h_{\chi,f}=I-\bigotimes_{s=1}^{t}(I-\Pi^{\chi}_{f(s)}).
\]

The small-layer rule repeats clauses, so a singleton layer has an exact all-equal product clause; this does not rely on a graph loop. The clause generator has `d^t m_chi` outputs in one round. After `ell` rounds of the `2t` iteration,

\[
R=(2t)^\ell,\quad \ell=\left\lceil\frac{6\log p(n)}{\log t}\right\rceil,
\quad N_{out}=N_0R=\operatorname{poly}(n),\quad
M_{out}\le M_0d^{2t\ell}=\operatorname{poly}(n),
\]

for fixed `t` and inverse-gap polynomial `p`. The output clauses are exact `kR`-local tensor-product complements. A requested entry of one clause is a product of `R` constant-size entries and is exactly evaluable in time polynomial in `R` and the coefficient bit lengths. A row can nevertheless contain exponentially many columns. BMVZ explicitly distinguishes easy term measurement from sparse-matrix access and leaves exploiting this tensor-product structure for sparse qPCP open (Section 1.2, “Sparse quantum PCP”).

The BMVZ hard start is supplied by Claim 8.1 and Corollary 8.4, attributed to ABN23a: a QMA verifier with polynomially many gates is mapped to a polynomial-size 5-local projection Hamiltonian with negligible-versus-inverse-polynomial promise thresholds, at most seven incident terms per qubit, and an equitable coloring into a constant number of disjoint (hence commuting) layers. The conflict graph has maximum degree at most 35, so 36 colors suffice. This gives a bounded-degree, constant-layer term list; it does not imply bounded treewidth or a common diagonalizer for different colors.

ABN's primary construction uses an injective PEPS built from Choi states of circuit gates and local invertible perturbations. Its parent Hamiltonian is a sum of local positive terms, and its ground state encodes a noisy computation; the tensor network is a description of the ground vector and local parent terms. It is not a tensor-network formula for the inverse of the full parent Hamiltonian. ABN also establishes hardness of tensor-network contraction/observable tasks, so “has a PEPS description” is not an exact classical contraction algorithm. BMVZ imports a 5-local, bounded-incidence projection interface from this QMA-hard starting point; this note does not identify the individual BMVZ starting clauses with the unmodified 8-qubit parent terms in ABN's Section 2.

For the exact BMVZ layered decomposition, each color's amplified terms commute because they are functions of commuting projectors. Across colors the source only promises disjointness *within* a color; terms from different colors can overlap and fail to commute. Thus the resolvent is not a product of per-layer resolvents. A path expander acts on clause indices, not on the computational-basis row graph of `H` or on a history space.

## Exact row obstruction for a legal ABN-derived family

This construction keeps any ABN-derived QMA-hard base (and may absorb earlier finite local augmentations into that base before BMVZ) and adds one fixed five-qubit seed register disjoint from it. Let

\[
|v\rangle=(|00000\rangle-|10000\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\qquad \Pi_v=|v\rangle\langle v|.
\]

Append `Pi_v` as its own singleton commuting color and append a zero-projector singleton color. Give the base colors total weight `alpha=1/2`, the seed color weight `beta=1/4`, and the zero color weight `1/4`. All color weights remain bounded below by a constant. The base and seed act on disjoint registers. Completeness is preserved by choosing the seed register in `ker(Pi_v)`; soundness is at least half the base soundness by positivity. This is therefore still a QMA-hard, BMVZ-admissible family, with only a constant number of added qubits and colors at the start.

Under BMVZ's singleton rule, after `ell` rounds the seed-color term is exactly

\[
h_{v,R}=I-Q,\qquad Q=(I-\Pi_v)^{\otimes R},\qquad R=(2t)^\ell.
\]

Write `H_B` for the normalized amplified base Hamiltonian, so `0<=H_B<=I`. On the amplified base and seed registers the full weighted Hamiltonian is

\[
H=\alpha H_B\otimes I+\beta I\otimes(I-Q),
\qquad \alpha=1/2,\quad \beta=1/4.
\]

The zero layer has no operator contribution. In particular, `0<=H<=3I/4`, so `Y=I-H` is strictly positive and its inverse is exact.

Set `D=(1-beta)I-alpha H_B`. Then `D>=I/4` and `D+beta I=I-alpha H_B>=I/2`. Since `Q` is a projection and acts on the seed register,

\[
(I-H)^{-1}=D^{-1}\otimes(I-Q)+(D+\beta I)^{-1}\otimes Q. \tag{2}
\]

For any base computational-basis string `z` and distinct seed strings `x,y`, (2) gives

\[
\langle z,x|(I-H)^{-1}|z,y\rangle
=Q_{x,y}\,\Delta_z,
\quad
\Delta_z=\langle z|[(D+\beta I)^{-1}-D^{-1}]|z\rangle
=-\beta\langle z|D^{-1}(D+\beta I)^{-1}|z\rangle<0. \tag{3}
\]

The final strict inequality holds because `D` and `D+beta I` are commuting positive-definite functions of `H_B`; their inverse product is positive definite. It is independent of the ABN base's eigenvectors or gate set.

On the two basis strings `s_0=00000`, `s_1=10000`, the local complement has

\[
(I-\Pi_v)|_{\{s_0,s_1\}}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For `x` in `S^R`, where `S={s_0,s_1}`, the row `Q_{x,y}` is exactly `2^{-R}` for every `y in S^R` and zero for other seed strings. Therefore, for every `z`, the row of `(I-H)^(-1)` at `(z,x)` has at least `2^R-1` nonzero entries at `(z,y)`, `y in S^R\{x}`. By (1), the same `2^R-1` off-diagonal entries survive in `K_a(I-H)^(-1)` for the exact PREPARE completion with `K_a=H`.

This is an exact algebraic row-support statement: no approximation, numerical threshold, or cancellation heuristic is used. Since `R=p(n)^{Omega(1)}` for fixed BMVZ parameter `t`, while the full output has polynomially many qubits and terms, listing this row takes `Omega(2^R)` output operations and is superpolynomial in the reduction input and output description sizes. The individual seed factor `Q_{x,y}` is itself exactly one of `0` or `2^{-R}` with `O(R)` bits; the scalar `Delta_z` is a diagonal entry of a resolvent on the ABN base. The row lower does not require an algorithm for that scalar, and even a hypothetical polynomial-time exact entry oracle cannot list the row in polynomial time.

## Consequences and boundaries

1. **No path-DP interface from BMVZ.** Enumerating a length-`t` expander path and evaluating a specified product entry costs polynomial time; it does not enumerate the support of a product clause's row. Iterating the construction makes `R` polynomial, not constant. Equation (3) exhibits the resulting row support in an actual incidence channel.
2. **No commuting-layer inverse factorization.** The seed calculation uses a commuting projection on its own subsystem and still yields an exponential row. For the full ABN-derived base, different colors need not commute, so a layerwise inverse identity is not available. Commuting within each color is insufficient.
3. **No PEPS inverse theorem.** An ABN tensor-network ground state and local parent terms do not imply an exact tensor-network of polynomial bond dimension for `(I-H)^(-1)` or for its rows. The resolvent sums over the entire spectrum, not only the encoded ground vector.
4. **Scoped result.** The exact lower bound is for the displayed legal singleton augmentation and the displayed exact PREPARE completion. It does not settle the channel for the *unmodified* canonical ABN-to-BMVZ output, for the specific two-seed completion used in another artifact, or for every PREPARE completion. For any one fixed `H`, a different column completion can change the `K_a`; and the base-only channel's support remains unclassified here. This is a counterexample to a uniform row-list claim based solely on the BMVZ/ABN input structure, not a universal no-go for N11.
