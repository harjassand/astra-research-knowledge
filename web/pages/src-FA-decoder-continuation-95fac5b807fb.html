One valid restricted observation, reconstructed by the coordinator:
If T has a unique inclusion-minimal support A, every monomial contains A.
Thus F=x_A G, where G has m distinct nonzero coefficients on the other
variables and a nonzero constant. The already-known Karpinski-Luby union
lemma applied to G gives Pr(G!=0)>=1/m because its term union is the whole
outside cube. Therefore Pr(F!=0)>=Pr(x_A=1)/m. Drawing the product law
conditioned on A being true and rejecting F=0 gives exactly the original
conditional law, with <=m expected proposals, no mass estimate.

This is ordinary common-factor reduction plus the inherited known-method
lemma. It does not settle multiple minimal supports, establish novelty, or
beat the strongest symbolic factoring control. It recovers the easy family
on which the inherited generic sampler already lost. Do not count it as
a new general sampler or a consequential capability.
