THE POSITIVE RESULT

This concerns reconstruction of DECLARED covariance observations. It does
not reconstruct the original X law and is not a general archive theorem for
unknown posterior families. Within that interface it supplies an explicit
parameter-blind decoder, a uniform guarantee, support for multiple requested
observations and a finite-table scalar codebook compiler.

Let mu be a probability law, A(x) a measurable positive semidefinite real
m-by-m field with finite E_mu tr A and finite matrix entropy. Let a known
experiment family {P_v} obey P_v<<mu with dP_v/dmu<=C, for a supplied finite
C>=1. The encoder sees one X~P_v and is blind to v. Choose once a deterministic
partition J(x) with at most D nonempty cells. Write
 p_j=mu(J=j), M_j=E_mu[A|J=j],
 H_J=E_mu tr(A log A)-sum_j p_j tr(M_j log M_j), 0 log0=0.
It retains ONLY the label j. The partition and codebook are global acquired
model information, not input-dependent free records; their acquisition and
storage outside the archive must be charged separately.

For L>0, f_L(S)=S(I+S/L)^-1. The target experiment draws T with a supplied
law w(T)dT, then Z|X,T~N(0,I+f_L(T A(X))). Assume w is a probability density
and w(T)<=c_w/T^2 for all T>0, with known c_w<infinity. The decoder draws the
same T independently and emits
 Ztilde|j,T ~ N(0,I+f_L(T M_j)).

For k independent copies of this scale/observation channel conditional on
the ONE original X, the D-label classical memory has the guarantee
 sup_v TV(Law_v[(T_i,Z_i)_(i<=k)],
          Law_v[(T_i,Ztilde_i)_(i<=k)])
       <=sqrt(k C c_w H_J/4).                             (1)
The stronger same-X coupling joint-law guarantee also holds, replacing the
two laws by their joint laws with X. No parameter, latent X, covariance
matrix, posterior or sign is supplied to the decoder per observation.
Its classical label can be read repeatedly. The k outputs are counted in
the error and computational costs; no copying of a quantum state is claimed.

For the source weight restricted to [T0,T1], 0<T0<T1<infinity, put
 W=1/T0-1/T1, w(T)=1/(W T^2) on that interval. Then c_w=1/W, so a certificate
 H_J <=4 W epsilon^2/(k C)                               (2)
is sufficient for the declared uniform epsilon-TV simulation.

No positive-definiteness or common-nullspace premise is necessary: A may
have changing kernels. The independent log-field dT/T theorem of c09_s03
does need common support, while the entropy upper and source dT/T^2 weight
used here permit zeros. This is an exact weight/interface distinction.

PROOF OF THE CONDITIONAL INFORMATION / KL BOUND

For a single cell, condition mu and write M=E A. Let
 C_A(T)=I+f_L(T A), C_M(T)=I+f_L(T M).
The inverse-map variational identity implies operator Jensen
 E(L I+T A)^-1 >=(L I+T M)^-1.
Since f_L(T A)=L I-L^2(L I+T A)^-1, it follows that E C_A<=C_M.
The Gaussian divergence formula therefore gives
 E KL(N(0,C_A)||N(0,C_M))
 =.5[tr(C_M^-1 E C_A)-m+tr log C_M-E tr log C_A]
 <=.5[tr log C_M-E tr log C_A].                          (3)
The inequality uses positivity under the trace; simultaneous diagonalization
of A values or M is unnecessary. All observation covariance matrices lie
between I and (1+L)I, so every fixed-scale divergence is finite.

Set a=1/L and b=1+1/L. Spectral functional calculus gives
 tr log C_A=tr log(I+b T A)-tr log(I+a T A).
For any c>0 the matrix Jensen gap
 F_c(T)=tr log(I+c T M)-E tr log(I+c T A)
satisfies
 int_0^infinity F_c(T) dT/T^2
   =c [E tr(A log A)-tr(M log M)].                       (4)
This is proved in the earlier independent matrix_entropy_information_upper
revision: integration by parts, the resolvent representation of x log x,
and cancellation of the linear trace term. It remains valid for PSD fields,
including zero eigenvalues; M's nullspace is killed by A almost surely.
Finite entropy permits subtracting the two finite nonnegative integrals.
As b-a=1, integrating (3) yields
 int E KL(N(0,C_A)||N(0,C_M)) dT/T^2 <=H_cell/2.          (5)
Average the cells to obtain H_J/2. H_J is nonnegative by trace convexity.

For any P_v, the integrand KL is a nonnegative function of X, so domination
by C transfers (5). Multiplying by the bounded scale density gives joint
KL <=C c_w H_J/2. Conditional independence makes k-output joint KL the sum
of the k conditional divergences, hence <=k C c_w H_J/2. Pinsker and marginal
data processing prove (1). The reference-bin mean is chosen from mu once;
it never uses v. There is no unjustified inference from small MI alone to
a universal original-observation codec: an explicit reference decoder is
constructed, and only this declared downstream channel is approximated.

SCALAR FINITE-TABLE ACQUISITION AND MINIMUM-LABEL COMPILER

Implemented input is a complete rational table of positive probabilities
p_1,...,p_K (summing to 1), scalar covariances a_i>=0, cap L, domination
certificate C, k, scale endpoints and desired epsilon. The scalar A values
are sorted; equal values can be combined. This reads and charges every table
entry. It does not infer hidden covariances from a lone archival observation.
For a cell of consecutive entries, its unnormalized entropy cost is
 g(l,r)=sum_(i=l)^r p_i a_i log a_i
           -s(l,r) m(l,r) log m(l,r),
 s=sum p_i, m=sum p_i a_i/s.                              (6)
Means and cell weights are exact rationals. Zero terms are defined exactly.

It is sufficient to optimize over consecutive cells. Indeed, with phi(a)=
a log a and any assigned centroid m>0, the Bregman distortion is
 d_phi(a,m)=a log(a/m)-a+m.
For two ordered centroids, the difference of their distortions is affine
in a, with slope log(m2/m1). Reassigning each a to its best fixed centroid
therefore yields ordered interval cells. Updating a centroid to the weighted
arithmetic mean weakly decreases distortion, since its derivative is
1-Ea/m. Starting with an optimum over the finite set of label assignments
gives a consecutive optimum with no greater number of nonempty labels.
Zero centroids only represent all-zero cells; their infinite divergence for
positive a makes the same ordering boundary explicit. This is the standard
one-dimensional Bregman quantization mechanism; exact historical priority
of this observation-channel formulation remains UNKNOWN.

Dynamic programming computes minimum sums of (6) for every D<=K in O(K^3)
rational endpoint comparisons (or O(D K^2) for a chosen budget). The transparent
implementation directly sums each of its O(K^2) cells, using O(K^3) arithmetic
for precomputation too; prefix sums could reduce that stage to O(K^2), but
that optimization was not implemented. Logarithms use the rational atanh series:
normalize x=2^b z with 1<=z<2 and use t=(z-1)/(z+1), |t|<=1/3;
 log z=2 sum_(r=0)^(N-1) t^(2r+1)/(2r+1)+tail,
 0<=tail<=2 t^(2N+1)/[(2N+1)(1-t^2)].
The same series at t=1/3 encloses log2. Sign-aware exponent multiplication
and interval arithmetic enclose each entropy cost. Identically constant cells
are assigned exact zero. Convexity permits clipping a tiny negative lower
endpoint to zero. The compiler minimizes upper endpoints to select a safe
partition, and independently minimizes lower endpoints to bound the true
optimum for each label count.

It accepts the first D whose certified upper is <=(2). If every smaller D
has a lower endpoint >threshold, it certifies the minimum deterministic label
count FOR THIS SUFFICIENT ENTROPY CRITERION. Otherwise the minimality status
is UNKNOWN, although the selected code remains valid. This is not a claim of
optimal memory for the original experiment or even among all randomized
codes. D=K always supplies exact zero distortion, so validity does not depend
on resolving nearly equal logarithms. Precision is charged and explicit.
The supplied table's global optimality is finite mathematical optimization,
not a theorem that arbitrary unknown covariance fields are cheaply acquired.

The executable returns the encoder's sorted interval map, rational codebook
means, retained dimension D, fixed binary packing cost ceil(log2 D), rigorous
entropy intervals and a rational bound on TV squared. It provides an exact
function for the decoder covariance 1+f_L(T m_j). Ideal Gaussian sampling is
part of the mathematical observation interface; a finite-bit/lazy-real output
implementation has NOT been supplied. Atomic floating output has TV1 to a
continuous Gaussian target. No quantum implementation or hardware run is
claimed. Repeated outputs cost k Gaussian samples and scale draws plus the
required precision, separately from the retained label.

The fixture's D=4 counts its per-observation label only. Its four rational
reference means require another 48 bits in an Elias-gamma encoding of
numerators-plus-one and denominators. A fixed seven-state row-index encoder
map uses 14 payload bits (two bits per input state) in addition to the
2-bit label, codebook size/header and source-field/model metadata. Thus a
total budget counting the compiled model includes at least those 64 payload
bits; it is not a claim of a 2-bit complete calibrated system. A scalar
threshold representation is an alternative with its own stated encoding.
The raw finite table, optimizer workspace, log intervals and certificates
are charged separately if retained. No data-dependent codebook is silently
excluded when the chosen application counts total learned state.

RELATION TO THE AUDITED CONTRAST THEOREM AND KLS

The independent c09_s02 theorem gives I<=P c(K)EJ for bounded covariance

valid analytic proof device but its acquisition algorithm was absent. Here
the bin decoder uses an ordinary reference conditional mean and an explicit

variability. These are complementary admitted regimes, not an implication
