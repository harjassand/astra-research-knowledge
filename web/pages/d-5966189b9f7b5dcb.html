# Independent audit of the conserved-shell reusable detector

2026-10-06. Worker r11_logic. Audited artifact: `results/r19_manybody/RESULT.md`, retained byte snapshot in `sources/r11_logic/cycle5/r19_RESULT_snapshot.md`. Independent checker: `work/r11_logic/cycle5/independent_exclusion_audit.py`; results: `results/r11_logic/cycle5/independent_exact_replay.json`.

**Verdict:** the sharp selected-instrument locality theorem and stationary exclusion covariance/variance identities pass independent reconstruction. They are a useful exact calibration benchmark. Neither a novel quantum acquisition advantage nor an improvement over equally informed classical sufficient-statistic acquisition is established. The strongest new-to-this-audit addition is a fully biased-estimator-safe, exact minimax preparation-parameter boundary, obtained from standard binomial decision theory. Its historical novelty is zero. The finite-rate detector extension was independently derived by r19 and this auditor and is standard spectral covariance summation.

## 1. Interface and quantifiers

Initial specimen is the diagonal density matrix σ(n,k)=uniform mixture of all n-bit strings with exactly k ones. The selected instrument reads occupation at one designated site. A channel acts on an m-site block containing the detector, and acts as identity on its exterior. The recovery channel and control seeds are independent of unknown k. k may be fixed but unknown; if supplied, the target mean q=k/n is already known. This distinction does not invalidate the channel theorem, but matters to acquisition claims.

The defect is the sum, over the two subnormalized instrument branches, of unhalved trace norms against outcome probability times σ. This is the measurement interface in Chen's Definition I.5/Eq. (10), **specialized to one instrument**. It does not establish strong recovery for every local instrument or arbitrary local Kraus operator. In particular, the fixed shell is rank deficient, and a local X flip creates other charge sectors; charge-preserving SWAP evolution cannot restore that noise. The separate sparse-charge decoder/reprepare construction pays for global detection and preparation and restores only its known discrete shell codebook. It preserves no unknown coherent quantum logical state.

One supplied initial shell, fresh control randomness, local projective detection, and a recovery implementation are the resources. Preparation is not acquired by the recovery proof. The exact uniform permutation channel is stronger control access than an autonomous nearest-neighbor stochastic evolution. Neither access model supplies an unknown-parameter bath.

## 2. Sharp optimum against every local quantum CPTP competitor

Let C be an exterior configuration. Its block charge is S=k−|C|. Conditional on C, the input block is uniform on its S-shell, hence the detector success probability is S/m. A trace-preserving channel on the block leaves the subnormalized exterior marginal unchanged, including after either selected measurement branch. Therefore the outcome-1 exterior defect is

    Σ_c Pr(C=c)|S(c)/m−q| = E|S/m−q|.

The outcome-0 defect is the same. Partial trace contracts Hermitian trace norm, giving ε(R)≥2E|S/m−q| for **every** block-supported quantum CPTP map. Knowing k, nonclassical block ancillas, or choosing a different outcome-dependent recovery cannot evade this exterior bound.

The block permutation twirl attains it: averaging the marked detector site uniformly among m block sites turns branch 1 into σ S_B/m, and branch 0 into σ(1−S_B/m). These diagonal operators have exactly the two exterior lower-bound norms. The same twirl works for every k. Consequently

    ε_opt(n,k,m)=2 Σ_s [C(m,s)C(n−m,k−s)/C(n,k)] |s/m−k/n|.

The argument is universal and elementary. The checker independently enumerates all configurations, builds exterior fibers, and evaluates their channel outputs with Fraction arithmetic for 238 triples n=2,…,8, k=0,…,n, m=1,…,n. Both full output norms and untouched exterior norms equal the formula exactly. This is finite implementation validation in addition to the proof, not a replacement for the proof.

The optimum can remain bounded away from zero for fixed m and interior density as n increases, although every pairwise occupation covariance tends to zero. This is consistent with Chen's use of clustering between the detector and the **entire exterior**, rather than pairwise small-small correlations. The complete collective exterior can retain the block charge information.

## 3. Exact record law, and the matched comparator

Conditional on S=s, each twirled branch returns the conditional shell, with success probability s/m. Thus r detector bits are iid conditional on S, while their unconditional law is a hypergeometric mixture of iid Bernoulli laws. With v=q(1−q)(n−m)/[m(n−1)], total variance gives

    E(qhat−q)^2 = v+[q(1−q)−v]/r.

The floor concerns this record and empirical estimator. It does not constrain experiments that touch the exterior. Full block m=n gives S=k deterministic, exact branch recovery, and true iid Ber(k/n) outcomes for each supplied shell. Unknown shells are mutually orthogonal, so acquiring their conserved label is fully compatible with the standard no-imprinting boundary.

Marginal preservation holds even for m<n because the two branches sum to σ. Retaining outcomes changes the posterior on S and therefore the conditional full state. Independent reuse cannot be inferred from marginal preservation. Retaining control seeds can change the information interface; the minimal-record theorem discards these seeds as specified.

The sufficient-statistic comparator is not merely a stronger global detector under the arbitrary-SWAP control interface. Using that same designated occupation detector, swap each original block bit to its site and read it once. m calls and m−1 all-to-all controlled SWAPs recover S exactly. Thereafter classical simulation of Ber(S/m) generates exactly the same distribution over detector records as the repeated-twirl experiment. An optional final twirl returns the same conditional shell posterior. This dominates repeated twirling for acquiring the scalar block statistic. At m=n it recovers unknown k exactly with n detector calls. Local nearest-neighbor routing can instead cost O(m²) swaps on a path. Under strictly autonomous exclusion dynamics no routing controller is admitted, so this comparator must not be smuggled into that different interface.

## 4. Unknown preparation parameter: exact minimax boundary

For ρ_p=diag(1−p,p)^⊗n with unknown p∈(0,1), a single initial classical bitstring η has K=|η|~Bin(n,p). Every p-independent adaptive quantum protocol with p-independent ancillas defines an output kernel W(output|η). Conditional on K, η is uniform over its weight-K strings independently of p. Hence the full quantum record is a p-independent stochastic garbling of K. Conversely K is physically measurable from the initial register. K is therefore the strongest statistical experiment allowed by this interface, regardless of recovery, coherent processing, number of records, or adaptive choice of measurements.

The Fisher inequality follows from score conditioning: score_record=E[(K−np)/(p(1−p))|record], giving I_record≤n/[p(1−p)]. Full twirl yields empirical-mean MSE p(1−p)[1/n+(1−1/n)/r]. Neither fact alone is a universal pointwise MSE bound for biased estimators.

A complete worst-case bound is available. Set a=√n/2 and use δ(K)=(K+a)/(n+2a). Its squared-error risk is

    R_p(δ)=[np(1−p)+a²(1−2p)²]/(n+2a)²
          =1/[4(√n+1)²],

constant in p. δ is the posterior mean for the proper Beta(a,a) prior, so it minimizes average risk under that prior. Every estimator has worst-case risk at least its average risk, which is at least this Bayes risk. The constant-risk estimator attains the lower bound. Thus the exact minimax risk when observing K is 1/[4(√n+1)²]. All p-independent quantum record protocols have minimax risk at least this value by garbling; direct charge acquisition attains it. This includes biased estimators and arbitrary quantum processing. A parameter-dependent bath or control family would add another information source and invalidates this premise.

The elementary Uniform[0,1] Bayes lower bound 1/[6(n+2)] is also correct but weaker. Its posterior is Beta(K+1,n−K+1), and K is marginally uniform. The checker verifies its exact arithmetic for n=1,…,20 and verifies the minimax polynomial identity symbolically with n=z², a=z/2. Twelve exact record/Fisher fixtures independently verify the empirical law. This is established binomial decision theory: a 2015 Stanford primary teaching note, Example 4, already gives this minimax rule. No claim of new metrology or no-cloning result is warranted.

## 5. Exclusion recovery and finite-rate trajectory estimation

Let L be the positive one-particle Laplacian with edge rates D_e, and Q the positive exclusion Laplacian on the weight-k configuration space. If C is the matrix with rows η, direct inspection of a swap gives Q C=C L. Therefore e^(−tQ) C=C e^(−tL) for every t≥0, by uniqueness of the finite matrix ODE. Uniform shell reversibility transfers this observable identity to the evolved branch density:

    M_t(P1σP1)=σ Σ_x p_t(a,x)η_x,  p_t=e^(−tL).

Cov_shell(η_x,η_y)=χ(δ_xy−1/n), χ=q(1−q)n/(n−1). Therefore

    ε_t=2 E_shell|Σ_x p_t(a,x)η_x−q|
       ≤2 sqrt{χ[p_(2t)(a,a)−1/n]},
    Cov(η_a(t),η_a(0))=χ[p_t(a,a)−1/n].

These require a stationary shell initially; arbitrary deterministic configurations need preparation/transient analysis. They do not acquire the stationary initial law. The quantum random-SWAP embedding remains completely classical on this diagonal family. No measurement back-action conclusion for a noncommuting initial state follows.

For continuous occupation average and nonzero eigenpairs λ_j,u_j,

    Var(qhat_T)=(2χ/T²)Σ_j |u_j(a)|²[T/λ_j−(1−e^(−λ_jT))/λ_j²]
              ≤2χG_aa/T,  G=L^+.

A finite detector schedule removes the need for ideal continuous readout. Read at r equally spaced times with interval Δ, using nondemolition occupation projection on the diagonal trajectory. Then

    Var(qhat_r)=χ/r² Σ_j |u_j(a)|²
       [r+2Σ_l=1^(r−1)(r−l)e^(−λ_jlΔ)]
       ≤q(1−q)/r+2χG_aa/(rΔ).

Indeed, extend the nonnegative covariance sum to infinite l, use 1+2Σ_l≥1 e^(−λlΔ)=coth(λΔ/2)≤1+2/(λΔ), and Σ_j |u_j(a)|²=1−1/n. Defining charged duration T=rΔ, the extra term is q(1−q)Δ/T. The first-to-last read spans (r−1)Δ; rΔ is a convenient larger budget convention, not an exact timestamp claim. r actual detector calls are charged. Both workers derived this schedule bound independently. Twenty-seven floating schedule fixtures validate its implementation only; the displayed derivation proves it.

For an n-cycle, G_aa=(n²−1)/(12Dn), so the bound is q(1−q)(n+1)/(6DT). It is a valid all-T upper bound, but loose in the pre-mixing regime; it should not be described as an exact n/T rate at every T. Nine independently rebuilt rational graph fixtures verify Q C=C L, the shell covariance matrix, and G=(L+11ᵀ/n)^−1−11ᵀ/n; eight cycle fixtures have the stated exact diagonal, including rational D=3/2.

For an L-side d-torus, choose centered integer representatives |z_i|≤L/2. sin(π|z_i|/L)≥2|z_i|/L, so

    G_aa≤L^(2−d)/(16D) Σ_z≠0 1/|z|².

The number in max-norm shell r is at most 2d(2r+1)^(d−1)≤2d3^(d−1)r^(d−1). Summing to floor(L/2) gives, for fixed d≥3,

    G_aa≤d3^(d−1)/(2^(d+1)D),

a deliberately loose but explicit volume-uniform bound. For d=2 the same sum is harmonic and gives O(log L/D); d=1 the cycle formula gives O(L/D). The volume-uniform variance statement is thus rigorous for fixed d≥3. It concerns local estimation from stationary trajectories, not full mixing or global quantum recovery. These are standard exclusion/Green-function/additive-functional mechanisms; Sethuraman–Xu (1996), introduction pp1843–1845, already relates the asymptotic variance to twice the integrated covariance/inverse generator.

## 6. Cost and adoption boundary

Chebyshev gives failure δ at precision τ with T≥2χG_aa/(δτ²) in the continuous formula; no logarithmic δ guarantee follows from this variance alone. The finite schedule adds q(1−q)Δ. Digital all-edge random SWAP simulation attempts |E|DT gates on average, including scheduled null swaps. For a cycle this is nDT, so inserting the conservative n/(Dτ²δ) duration incurs O(n²/(τ²δ)) gate actions. For d≥3, constant-in-volume walltime still incurs O(n/(τ²δ)) extensive swap actions at fixed dimension. Analog parallel dynamics can pay these actions simultaneously, but engineering/energy and initial preparation remain resources. Detector calls are r, plus hardware-specific implementation error.

The recent Chen–Gilyén v2 protocol must retain its actual interface: known candidate Hamiltonian, controlled Hamiltonian simulation and observable block encodings, several independent initial Gibbs copies, correlated sequential measurements within each copy, and nonadaptive sequencing. Its Lemma 2 and Theorem 1 proof explicitly use marginal state preservation, independent copies across specimens, and a union bound across observables. It does **not** need branchwise iid restoration. Independent-copy concentration is not transferable to a single correlated exclusion specimen without the explicit covariance argument above. Conversely, this commuting scalar protocol does not compete with that general noncommuting many-observable theorem.

Adopt r19 as a **proved scoped calibration benchmark**, with historical novelty unverified for the exact selected-instrument optimum, and established-prior status for covariance, binomial minimax, and no-information boundaries. It can invalidate bad resource accounting and identify when a Laplacian pseudoinverse beats a crude global-gap estimate. It does not yet yield a consequential new research capability.

Next discriminating step: select a genuinely noncommuting local Lindbladian and an unknown-state/unknown-observable target that cannot already be reduced to a cheaply acquired classical sufficient statistic. Prove controlled Heisenberg closure **and** the instrument back-action law under the actual detector, then compare walltime, preparation, detector calls and extensive actions with a competent same-access prior method. Failure of closure or required branch-control access is the decisive obstruction to extending this example. The recent abstract Markov theorem, pairwise clustering, or finite simulator agreement does not supply that missing lemma.
