## Original fixed-step lower bound: independently reconstructed

with coefficients (+1,-1) on every pair. Every feature column has norm
one and exact optimization is one cheaper-item choice per pair. For

`q=n>R^2=121`, `p_j=1<=q/K`.

This row is retained and large, not a removed or artificial row. Until
the first proper original-face hit, all coordinates stay active and
cleanup does nothing. Large-row constraint A.h=0 and the diagonal

`M=I+eta11^T`, `L=129/128<8`, `Phi=-eta||x||^2`.

The true source gradient contract implies

`|x.h|<=eps_g/(2eta)=tau/(256n)`.

For either sign and any legal source motion s<=tau with norm h<=1,

`||x+sh||^2-||x||^2<=tau^2[1+1/(128n)]`.

Every allowed reset stays in the cube and has displacement below delta_o,
so its additional squared-norm increase is below `2n delta_o`. The first
term defining delta_o gives `delta_o<=tau^2/(2^18n^2)`. Per iteration,
regardless of the allowed direction and reset choices, norm growth is
therefore at most

`C_n tau^2`, `C_n=1+1/(128n)+1/(2^17n)`.

Initially norm is zero. A proper face of this pair product fixes an entire
pair to (+1,-1) or its reverse, giving squared norm at least two. A
same-original-face reset cannot itself create an earlier proper face.
Hence the first face hit requires at least

`ceil(2/[C_n tau^2])` source iterations.

The active cutoff cannot terminate before it, since n>16. This is an
actual lower bound, rather than inferring slowness from a huge upper bound.
For n=130 it exceeds `3.28*10^51` iterations. For n=4422 the theorem
radius 2209 is nonvacuous because an all-first-items base has error 2211;

was parameter arithmetic only; no such trajectory was run.

This obstruction does not refute polynomial-time formal correctness:
tau^-2 is a polynomial in the encoded dimension/parameters with very
large constants and exponents. It refutes any practical claim for this
unchanged schedule even on these simple, legal inputs.
