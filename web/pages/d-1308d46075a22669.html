# Cycle 2 verdict

**Verdict: the September 2026 preprint's update is a useful correction to its own earlier pseudoreplication, but it does not establish that the age-associated state split is biologically irreversible or that DamAge measures the same latent process as mortality.** The current claim is narrower and more defensible: in two small human-fibroblast reprogramming datasets, Horvath scores decrease on average, while donor-level DamAge changes are not detected during the sampled identity-retaining comparisons; near the iPSC endpoint, both clocks decrease. The DamAge intervals and donor counts preclude an equivalence/resistance conclusion, and the preprint itself now says so.

**The mortality paper's 89–93% “latent” share is conditionally estimated, not a direct latent-state observation.** Its primary fit is the shared-slope cross-sectional biomarker/Gompertz survival model; the full person-level phase-type likelihood was not tractable. The finite-grid simulation recovery and Lean invariant proofs answer narrower questions: estimator behavior under the assumed model, and algebraic properties of the ratio. Neither identifies the latent's biological substance. A direct falsifier/gate is longitudinal repeated biomarker sampling sufficient to test cross-sectional `lambda_j` against within-person drift while modelling assay error, selection, cohort and frailty.

**Independent mathematical result:** in a two-mode washout model the scalar-observation observability determinant is `a*b*(mu-lambda)`. The two modes can be reconstructed only with nonzero, sufficiently distinct readout loadings/rates (or a full-rank calibrated two-readout matrix). Even with perfect model fit, exact persistence `mu=1` cannot be distinguished at finite horizon from sufficiently slow recovery `mu=1-delta` when the induced signal is below measurement resolution. This supplies a precise experimental gate: calibrate mode loadings and a minimum recovery rate of interest, use washout time series plus independent functional/lesion readouts, and report finite-horizon persistence rather than irreversibility.

For the parent research program, the defensible use of the preprint is as an informed prior-art and assay-design constraint. It is not a confirmed two-state biological theorem. The strongest next action is a donor-blocked, lineage-aware pulse/washout experiment with clock modality, direct lesion, identity and function readouts; analyze a calibrated state-space model only if its whitened observability matrix is well-conditioned and it predicts held-out donors better than a one-mode alternative.
