Title:Ample vector bundles without Griffiths-semipositive metrics

Authors:Yun-Heng Du, Song-Yan Xie
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Ample vector bundles without Griffiths-semipositive metrics, by Yun-Heng Du and Song-Yan Xie

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:We construct a rank-two ample holomorphic vector bundle on an abelian surface that admits no smooth Griffiths-semipositive Hermitian metric, thereby giving a counterexample to the Griffiths conjecture. The proof combines a curvature obstruction that persists under deformations with the openness of ampleness in an irreducible moduli space of stable sheaves.
