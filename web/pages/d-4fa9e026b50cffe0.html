## Occurrence trees and the exact potential

For the **unbounded bang-free base FL_e**, set weights `w(1)=1`, `w(0)=w(atom)=0`, tensor to sum, implication to `w(B)-w(A)`, additive conjunction to max, and additive disjunction to min. For a single-conclusion sequent, `d(Gamma=>Delta)=w(Delta)-sum w(Gamma)`, with empty-succedent weight 0.

Every identity/zero leaf has deficit zero; a primitive `=>1` leaf has deficit 1. L1 decreases deficit by 1. R0 and L* preserve it. R-imp preserves it. L-imp, R* and cut add the two premise deficits. L& and R-or do not increase the selected premise deficit. R& and L-or take the maximum of their two premise deficits. Therefore in a genuine premise-occurrence tree, deficit is at most the total number of primitive unit leaves, even with arbitrary cut formulas.

Let U0=1 and U(n+1)=Un*Un. Then deficit of `=>Un` is `2^n`. The displayed DAG has n+1 singleton-sequent rows, reusing each preceding row twice as the two R* premise slots. An ordinary tree proof requires at least `2^n` unit leaves. In the binary base, Boolean soundness excludes formula-free `=>empty` rows, so its exact minimum total formula occurrences is `2^(n+1)-1`, attained by the pure unit tree.

This does **not** refute polynomial symbol-size equivalence: the ordinary tree formula Un already has `2^(n+1)-1` constructors. It does refute a literal polynomial occurrence-tree line bound depending only on the given DAG's n+1 formula occurrences. If “premise of at most one rule” allows one row to fill two slots of that rule, the short DAG is already “tree-like” under that nonstandard convention. The recent theorem thus needs an explicit convention, rather than an inferred unqualified error label.

The weight is a **proof-cost potential**, not a residuated algebra truth model: its multiplicative unit has weight 1, while a monoidal truth interpretation would give it the additive identity. The primitive unit rule supplies exactly the charged resource.

## Proper controlled contraction collapses this family

Declare the uniform structural rule knot^2_3:

`Gamma,A,A,A => D / Gamma,A,A => D`.

For arbitrary source formula A, four **distinct** identity leaves A=>A and three R* rows give `A,A,A,A => (A*A)*(A*A)`. Use knot first with Gamma=A, then with Gamma empty, obtaining successively three and two A occurrences. L* gives

`A*A => (A*A)*(A*A)`.

This uses exactly 10 rows / 28 formula occurrences, zero primitive unit leaves, and no ordinary contraction. Every premise slot is a separate occurrence and every row is an ancestor of its endpoint. There is no associativity conversion: the parenthesized output is exactly the square of the parenthesized input.
