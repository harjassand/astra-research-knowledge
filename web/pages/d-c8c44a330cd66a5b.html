# Finite-extension descent for trilinear degenerations

7 October 2026. Root derivation. Status: elementary transfer lemma independently reconstructed. The application now passes the scoped manual gate below; it remains dependent on the released proof and has no external or compiled formal validation.

Let F⊆E be a degree-k field extension, and let A be a trilinear tensor over F. Suppose a restriction or Laurent-polynomial degeneration of L copies of A over E has leading tensor B defined over F. Choose an F-linear functional ell:E→F with ell(1)=1. Expand each of the three leg maps in a fixed F-basis e_1,...,e_k of E. Applying ell to all scalar coefficients of the resulting trilinear form gives a sum of at most Lk³ restrictions of A over F: the coefficient of the (a,b,c) term is ell(e_a e_b e_c), which is absorbed into one leg map.

Coefficient projection preserves every vanishing lower Laurent coefficient and fixes B. Consequently

    A^(direct sum Lk³) degenerates over F to B.

This argument applies coefficient by coefficient and does not require ell to be multiplicative. Treating ell as multiplicative would be an error; the k³ expansion is exactly the cost of avoiding it. All coefficients and exponents of the original finite maps remain represented explicitly if they were explicitly supplied. This is an algebraic statement, not an assertion that an existential rank decomposition can be cheaply acquired.

For a Fourier separator with M labels over F_q, choose k so that L=q^k−1>3(M−1). Then k=O_q(log M), L=O_q(M), and E=F_(q^k) contains a primitive Lth root; L is invertible in E. Character orthogonality works exactly in E. Integer exponents of a formal degeneration parameter are integer valuations, not elements reduced modulo the characteristic. If the separator uses only these facts, descent costs O_q(M log³ M) source copies. For M exponential in tensor power, the polynomial-in-power factor has zero asymptotic entropy cost.
