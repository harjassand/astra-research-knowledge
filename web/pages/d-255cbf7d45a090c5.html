1. Claus Scheiderer, *Spectrahedral shadows*, arXiv1612.07048v3, revised4December2017 (first21December2016; source journal citation SIAGA2018). Read the primary abstract/history, statements and proofs of Theorems3.3–3.4, Remark3.7, and the statement of Corollary3.8; read Section5.2–5.4 and the relevant introduction, not the entire paper. Theorem3.4 characterizes the closed convex hull of a semialgebraic set by one finite-dimensional SOS pullback space on an auxiliary variety. Remark3.7 explains why uniform finite-dimensionality is needed for transfer to real closed extensions. Section5.4 asks whether the framework can settle the hyperbolicity-shadow question. The new release's finite positive-functional test is closely related to this prior, so it is not claimed as a new general principle. The original2017 statement of what was open is historical, not a current-status assertion.

2. Manuel Bodirsky, Mario Kummer, Andreas Thom, *Spectrahedral Shadows and Completely Positive Maps on Real Closed Fields*, arXiv2206.06312v2, revised3October2022 (first13June2022). Read primary abstract/history, introductory mechanism, Definition2.1, Lemma2.3 and its proof, Theorem2.13 and its proof; other sections/appendices retrieved but not fully read. Lemma2.3 identifies preservation of affine LMI formulas by unital real-linear positive maps; existential witnesses transfer by applying the map, so shadows inherit this preservation. Theorem2.13 gives the positive-functional/Hahn-series construction. This is a strong nearest comparator for moment functionals as a universal lift obstruction. It supplies a preservation framework rather than a supplied affordable finite-degree classifier for arbitrary hyperbolicity cones. No theorem from unread parts is imported into our proof.
