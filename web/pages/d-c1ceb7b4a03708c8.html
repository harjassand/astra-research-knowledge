The source apartment oracle is ordinary linear algebra. Column elimination of V^(-1)S starts at the bottom row. Each selected column has lowest nonzero index j and zeros below it. Replacing e_j by that column yields an upper triangular nonsingular T; U=VT preserves EVERY old prefix and represents S by its selected U columns. The producer's extra gauge subtracts only preceding columns and scales by a nonzero coefficient, so it also preserves every prefix. No submodular optimizer, LP, complete-subspace enumeration, or hidden extension-field search occurs in this kernel.

I reconstructed the same update through sums and intersections, without importing either producer or hessian_check12 code. In a source-aligned apartment, an active S-step adds alpha=t*u to selected weights, clips at C and sorts. For every0<h<=C this gives exactly

    F_new(h)=F(h)+(S intersect F(h-alpha)).

The negative source step gives F_new(h)=F(h+t). These equalities follow by separating selected/unselected apartment coordinates; they are independent of apartment choice and tied-weight completions. All candidate positive levels are old levels and their alpha shifts, plus alpha, clipped at C. Direct row Gaussian elimination, nullspaces and the identity (A intersect B)^perp=A^perp+B^perp implement these equations over Q or the supplied prime field.

The objective is a valid dual upper bound: lambda_i=w_i-w_(i+1)>=0 and y_k=max(0,c_k-a_(S_k)) satisfy each packing dual constraint. ## 3. Actual error and exact rational weights

At sweep i=0,...,N-1 the exact source step is

    t_i=C/[M ceil(sqrt(i+1))].

Its interval is D/[2LM sqrt(i+1)]<=t_i<=D/[LM sqrt(i+1)], with D=C sqrt(r), Lipschitz bound L=sqrt(r). Goodwin's projected-ray inequality yields a full-sweep recurrence

    d(x^(i+1),x*)^2 <= d(x^i,x*)^2
          -2t_i(f(x^i)-tau)+M^2 L^2 t_i^2.

To reconstruct the coefficient, sum the individual projected-step inequalities, use each constituent's L-Lipschitz bound, and bound distance from the sweep start after k microsteps by kLt_i. The accumulated extra term is 2L^2t_i^2 sum_(k=0)^(M-1)k+ML^2t_i^2=M^2L^2t_i^2. Telescoping over a late half of the sweeps bounds the minimum observed objective. Hirai's displayed bound is

    min_(i=1)^N f(x^i)-tau <=4(1+ln3) M C r/sqrt(N+2).

A return of the best completed-sweep value, also allowing the feasible initial value if smaller, satisfies this bound. An arbitrary final iterate is not the guaranteed output.
