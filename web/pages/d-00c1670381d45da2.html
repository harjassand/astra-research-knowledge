# Equal positive charges: primary prior-art audit

7 October 2026. Worker `equal_charge_prior`. **Historical novelty remains UNKNOWN.** This audit narrows the possible contribution to charge-count-controlled realization of harmonic jets and its superlinear equilibrium consequence. Qualitative approximation by equal positive charges is old.

## Exact question and interface

Root's `notes/root_cycle7/EQUAL_CHARGE_JET_COMPILER.md` takes a real harmonic polynomial of degree D and a supplied exact spherical 2D-design, moves the sites radially, and retains strengths exactly +1. It realizes the nonconstant jet, then uses spatial rescaling and C² persistence to transfer finitely many nondegenerate critical points. Its consequential count is N=O(D²). The complete compiler and harmonic critical-point construction have separate independent mathematical gates; this document is a prior audit, not another proof validation.

Charges here are fixed sources in three-dimensional Euclidean space. We count zeros of the field experienced by a test charge. This is not the equilibrium configuration of the source particles, the Thomson problem, central configurations of the n-body problem, or Newtonian dynamics with a centrifugal term. Searches involving those problems do not supply comparable results.

## Qualitative approximation is already enough to transfer any fixed finite pattern

This is our deduction from the older approximation theorem, not a new theorem claimed for the programme. Take a harmonic polynomial P and disjoint closed balls around its finitely many nondegenerate critical points, all strictly inside a larger ball Omega. Piele's theorem applies to a smooth spherical boundary. Let U_j+c_j converge to P uniformly on a slightly larger compact set. The error is harmonic there. Interior harmonic derivative estimates upgrade uniform convergence to C² convergence on the critical-point balls. Nondegenerate gradient zeros then persist. Additive constants never affect the critical points.

Thus the mere existence of some finite number of unit charges realizing a prescribed finite critical pattern is already a consequence of the 1974 statement. Repeated source sites, if allowed in the theorem, can be split into sufficiently small distinct displacements while preserving C² accuracy on the interior compact set. Generic position perturbations at fixed strengths make the full potential Morse and finite; that genericity step also occurs explicitly in Kintali's Proposition 1.3 and is attributed to earlier critical-point theory in Arathoon et al.

For Shklyaev's convention F(x)=−1/(4pi|x|) in dimension three, apply the density theorem to −P/(4pi), then multiply the entire approximation by −4pi. The result has positive unit Coulomb coefficients. The minus sign in the fundamental-solution convention is not a mixed-charge obstruction.

Neither qualitative density statement, on its own, controls the count of approximating sources as P or the requested accuracy varies. Therefore neither statement by itself yields an Omega(n^(3/2)) equilibrium bound. **Whether a quantitative refinement buried in the unaccessed proofs or other literature already gives the required count remains an explicit historical gap.**

## Candidate quantitative increment and its scope

The root compiler's potential increment over the accessed statements is exact control of the entire nonconstant degree-D harmonic jet using O(D²) unit sources, for every sufficiently large N relative to D². Rescaling then gives C² accuracy with a source count independent of the final small spatial scale. This is stronger source-count information than qualitative density, but it permits extremely small scales and does not bound experimentally useful signal size or polynomial placement precision.

A simple dimension check clarifies this interface. The nonconstant harmonic-jet space has M=D(D+2) real dimensions. For N unit charges with freely moving sites away from the expansion point, the moment map is analytic on an open subset of R^(3N). If 3N<M, its derivative has rank below M everywhere; Sard's theorem implies its image has measure zero and cannot contain a neighborhood in jet space. Hence any universal exact local jet compiler requires N≥M/3. Under fixed ray directions and radial displacements only, it requires N≥M. This establishes the **order-optimality of the O(D²) universal exact-jet parameter count**, conditional on that exact universal interface. It is not an upper bound on the number of Coulomb equilibria. A special family of polynomials might be realized with fewer sources, so it does not obstruct a future quadratic or larger equal-charge construction.
