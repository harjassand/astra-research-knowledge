1. Precise interface
--------------------
Let s=sqrt(sigma)>0 and q=sqrt(rho)>0 in an arbitrary finite dimension, with
Tr(s^2)=Tr(q^2)=1. Let B=B^* and define

  ell_s(X)=(sX+Xs)/2,
  V_B=ell_s^(-1)(BsB),
  H_B(X)=(V_B X+X V_B)/2-BXB,
  r=s^(-1/2)q^2s^(-1/2),
  z=s^(1/2)(log(q^2)-2log(s))s^(1/2),
  C=(rz+zr)/2-2q^2,
  W=ell_s^(-1)(C).

Here ell_s equals half of the full Sylvester operator, so W=2Lyap_s^(-1)(C)
in the parent's full-Sylvester convention. Every allowed finite KMS generator
is a sum of these legal noises. The exact entropy-energy gap for one noise is

  F_B=J_B-2E_B
     =Tr[BsBW]-Tr[BrBz]+2Tr[BqBq].                    (1)

Equivalently the Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint, adjoint-preserving superoperator

  M=(L_s R_W+L_W R_s)/2-(L_r R_z+L_z R_r)/2+2L_q R_q

must be positive semidefinite. Positivity on all Hermitian B is equivalent to
full complex Hilbert-Schmidt positivity because M preserves adjoints; no
complete-positivity claim about -M is made.

In an eigenbasis s_i of s, put u_i=log(s_i), and write q=sum_a q_a P_a. The
supplied audited potential identity is

  R_K=sum_a q_a [K(log(q_a)-u_i,log(q_a)-u_j) Schur P_a],
  K(x,y)=[x sinh(y)+y sinh(x)-4sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)]
                                             /[2cosh((x-y)/2)],
  R_K>=0,
  W=z+2s-4q+4R_K.                                   (2)

The PSD assertion uses the full many-point scalar kernel, not just a two-point
inequality. The identity requires summing all P_a; an individual spectral
summand can have a residual independent of q_a. This worker did not reproduce
the sibling's potential proof, and keeps (2) explicitly supplied and attributed.

2. Exact commutator defect: independent derivation in this attack
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use complex Hilbert-Schmidt inner product <X,Y>=Tr(X^*Y), and let D_B(X)=[B,X].
For Hermitian B, D_B is selfadjoint on that complex Hilbert space; its value at
a Hermitian matrix is skew-Hermitian, so the adjoint in the inner product must
be retained. From the stationary Lyapunov equation,

  V_B=B^2-(1/2)ell_s^(-1)(D_B^2(s)).                  (3)

Indeed ell_s(B^2)-BsB=(B^2s+sB^2-2BsB)/2=D_B^2(s)/2.
Thus H_B=D_B^2/2+{V_B-B^2,.}/2. Insert this into J_B=Tr[H_B(r)z] and
E_B=Tr[qH_B(q)], and use selfadjointness of D_B and ell_s^(-1). This gives

  F_B=(1/2)<D_B r,D_B z>-||D_B q||_HS^2
                              -(1/2)<D_B s,D_B W>.   (4)

Every term is real. Substituting the supplied (2) into (4) and completing the
commutator square yields the exact cancellation formula

  F_B=(1/2)<D_B(r-s),D_B z>-||D_B(q-s)||_HS^2
                                      -2<D_B s,D_B R_K>.  (5)

This makes the residual stationary commutator visible. In particular, R_K>=0
does not control the sign of <D_B s,D_B R_K>.

Exact legal sign controls. Set sigma=diag(1/2,3/10,1/5), and B=E_12+E_21.
For rho_+=diag(3/5,3/10,1/10), q_2=s_2, R_(K,22)=0, while
R_(K,11)=q_1 k(log(q_1/s_1))>0, where k(x)=x sinh(x)-cosh(x)+1.
Since s_1>s_2,

  <D_B s,D_B R_K>=2(s_1-s_2)R_(K,11)>0.

For rho_-=diag(1/2,2/5,1/10), q_1=s_1, R_(K,11)=0 and R_(K,22)>0, so

  <D_B s,D_B R_K>=-2(s_1-s_2)R_(K,22)<0.

All states are faithful and both noises are legal KMS generators by (3). These
examples refute a sign shortcut, not the endpoint: rho_+ and rho_- commute with
sigma and therefore satisfy the stronger constant 4 by the earlier exact pair
control. Their purpose is to expose the correction's genuinely indefinite sign.

3. Complete strong control when the noise commutes with s
--------------------------------------------------------
If [B,s]=0, then V_B=B^2, H_B=D_B^2/2 and the last term of (4) vanishes.
The sandwiches in r,z cancel inside the commutator pairing:

  <D_B r,D_B z>=<D_B(q^2),D_B(log(q^2))>.

Diagonalize q, with eigenvalues q_a>0. The elementary scalar inequality

  (x-y)(log x-log y)>=4(sqrt(x)-sqrt(y))^2

gives <D_B(q^2),D_B(log(q^2))> >=4||D_B q||_HS^2. Hence

  J_B>=4E_B,   F_B>=||D_B q||_HS^2>=0.               (6)

This holds for every rho in every finite dimension, and for an arbitrary
untouched reference when B=B_A tensor I and [B_A,s_A]=0. It is a direct control,
not a full KMS theorem or novelty claim. An arbitrary nontracial KMS generator
need not admit a decomposition solely into noises commuting with s.

4. Relative-Cayley Hardy Gram: independent derivation in this attack
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Let L_q(X)=qX and R_s(X)=Xs. These commuting positive selfadjoint operators on
complex Hilbert-Schmidt space define the selfadjoint contraction

  T=(L_q-R_s)(L_q+R_s)^(-1),  ||T||<1,
  D=q-s,
  Psi_n=T^n(D)s^(-1/2)/sqrt(2),  n>=0.               (7)

In mixed q-eigen/s-eigen coordinates, T has eigenvalue
t_ai=(q_a-s_i)/(q_a+s_i)=tanh((log(q_a)-log(s_i))/2). The Hardy proof in the
independent baseline gives a bounded PSD operator A_H on scalar H^2 whose
coefficient matrix C_nm in the monomial basis has generating kernel

  C(t,u)=[a(t)+a(u)-b(t)b(u)]/(1-tu),
  a(t)=(1-t^2)atanh(t)/t, a(0)=1,
  b(t)=sqrt(1-t^2),
  A_H=M_a+M_a^*-M_bM_b^*
     =(pi/2)M_b^*M_b-M_bM_b^*>=0.

The scalar feature relation is K(x,y)=2sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)C(tanh(x/2),tanh(y/2)).
Since

  sqrt(2q_a)sinh((log(q_a)-log(s_i))/2)
                                  =(q_a-s_i)/sqrt(2s_i),

the mixed-coordinate entries of Psi_n satisfy

  (P_a Psi_n)_(ki)=t_ai^n(q_a-s_i)(P_a)_(ki)/sqrt(2s_i).

Multiplication, summation over orthogonal P_a, and the generating kernel give
the exact operator identity

  R_K=sum_(n,m>=0) C_nm Psi_n^* Psi_m.                (8)

Convergence is in operator norm: ||Psi_n||_HS<=||T||^n||Ds^(-1/2)||_HS/sqrt(2),
and the coefficients C_nm are bounded by ||A_H||, so the double norm sum is
geometrically summable. Formula (8) is an explicitly positive Gram expression;
every finite principal C block is PSD, and the limit remains positive. It
does not require forward application of a Sylvester map.

For X_n=Psi_n B s^(1/2), the positive part of the supplied full gap becomes

  Tr[BsB R_K]=sum_(n,m) C_nm <X_n,X_m>
            =||A_H^(1/2)(X_0,X_1,...)||^2.           (9)

Thus another exact remaining formulation is

  F_B=4||A_H^(1/2)(X_0,X_1,...)||^2
                     +Tr[B(s-r)Bz]+2Tr[BDBD].        (10)

The sequence norm is on H^2 tensor the finite matrix Hilbert-Schmidt space.
Equations (8)-(10) supply a concrete relative-modular positive part. They do
not compare it with the signed bilinear terms, so they are not a proof of F>=0.
This bounded attack performed no new numerical search or catalogue.

operation cannot be used as a positive-order step. Those are supplied exact
