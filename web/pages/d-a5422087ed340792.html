THEOREM AND LEGAL INTERFACE

Let A=direct_sum_(k=1)^r M_(d_k), and let
  tau(X)=sum_k alpha_k Tr(X_k)/d_k,
where alpha_k>0 and sum alpha_k=1. Thus tau is a faithful tracial state.
Let Phi:A->A be unital CP, tau-preserving, and self-adjoint for the real
Hermitian inner product <X,Y>_tau=tau(XY). Assume one ACTUAL compatible
joint Heisenberg channel Lambda:A tensor A->A, unital CP, with
  Lambda(X tensor I)=Lambda(I tensor X)=Phi(X).
No center preservation, block invariance or blockwise application of Phi
is assumed; Phi can map central elements to noncentral elements.

Then there exists ONE finite pure canonical channel relative to tau,
  Psi(X)=sum_j p_j rho_j tau(rho_j X),
  rho_j=pi_j/tau(pi_j),
where pi_j is a minimal rank-one projection in one block,
  p_j>=0, sum p_j=1, sum_j p_j rho_j=I,
such that on the ENTIRE Hermitian HS_tau space
  Psi >= (2Phi^2-I)_+,
and consequently I-Psi<=4(I-Phi).
Psi is an ordinary blockwise unital TP EB channel and fixes the entire
center of A, even when Phi does not preserve that center.

If compatibility is supplied in the relative Schrodinger picture, take
B=Lambda^dagger_(tau,tau tensor tau):A->A tensor A. It is CP, preserves
the specified reference trace in the sense
(tau tensor tau)B(X)=tau(X), and has both partial-tau marginals Phi.
Conversely these properties of a relative B give the stated UCP Lambda.
For finite direct sums the weighted-trace adjoint of a CP map is CP:
each ordinary trace-adjoint block component is CP and is multiplied by
a strictly positive ratio of central trace weights. Unitality of Lambda
is equivalent to relative trace preservation of B; self-adjoint Phi
identifies the relative marginal and Heisenberg Phi. This is NOT an
assertion that B is ordinarily unital, or that Phi is ordinarily TP in
an arbitrary global matrix embedding with unequal block weights.

1. THE TREE AND COVARIANCE ARGUMENT ARE ALGEBRAIC

The unital CP Schwarz argument in the matrix baseline applies in A;
trace preservation gives HS_tau contraction and a real tau-orthonormal
Hermitian eigenbasis with lambda in [-1,1]. Identity is a fixed coordinate,
and its orthogonal complement H0 has dimension D=sum_k d_k^2-1.

Symmetrize Lambda under its two arguments. The state
sigma_1=tau composed with Lambda on A tensor A has both marginals tau.
For Hermitian X, positivity of the square
(X tensor I +/- I tensor X)^2 yields a symmetric sibling covariance
Gamma with -I<=Gamma<=I. This uses only tracial one-register marginals,
not a product state or a scalar-normalized global matrix trace.

Define the Heisenberg tree
  U_0=id, U_(n+1)=Lambda composed with(U_n tensor U_n).
Its leaf marginal is Phi^n and its output reference state is tau U_n.
Every leaf has marginal tau because tau Phi=tau. Choose an orthonormal
fast eigenbasis E_1,...,E_m with |lambda|>1/sqrt2 and F=diag(lambda).
At each leaf use the randomized spectral POVM of these E_a with retained
uniform label. Spectral projections are legitimate elements of A and
sum to its identity, including all blocks. The local initial score
covariance is mI. Average leaves and rescale by F^-n as in the matrix
baseline. Input first moments are exactly E_a. The classical covariance
obeys, for the same tracial sibling matrix C,
  N_n=(1/2)F^-1(N_(n-1)+C)F^-1,
  N_0=mI,
  C<=I,
so
  N_n<=R_n=m(2F^2)^-n+sum_(s=1)^n(2F^2)^-s
        ->(2F^2-I)^-1.
Every formula holds on the global eigenbasis of Phi on A. There is no
restriction of Phi to an individual summand or to the center.

2. THE POSTERIOR NORMALIZATION IS RELATIVE TO TAU

Let M_y=U_n(output_effect_y), so M_y>=0 and sum M_y=I. Set
  p_y=tau(M_y), rho_y=M_y/p_y.
Then tau(rho_y)=1, and the posterior state functional is
  omega_y(X)=tau(rho_y X).
The exact barycenter is sum p_y rho_y=I, equivalently sum p_y omega_y=tau.
Using a FULL tau-orthonormal traceless basis, define x_alpha(y)=omega_y(E_alpha).
The unbiased first moment gives E_p[x Z^T]=J, the fast coordinate inclusion.
The full classical Gram block therefore yields
  C_n=E_p[xx^T]>=J N_n^-1J^T>=J R_n^-1J^T.
This is global HS_tau positive order, including all center/noncentral
and inter-summand coordinate cross terms.

Refine M_y=summation_l c_(yl)pi_(yl) into minimal rank-one block projections.
Put t_(yl)=tau(pi_(yl))=alpha_k/d_k for its block, and
  p_(yl)=c_(yl)t_(yl), rho_(yl)=pi_(yl)/t_(yl).
Then sum p_(yl)=1 and sum p_(yl)rho_(yl)=sum c_(yl)pi_(yl)=I.
The parent posterior rho_y is their convex mixture with weights
p_(yl)/p_y. Jensen therefore increases the full covariance after this
PURE refinement. Using Tr(M_y) or a global uniform 1/d here would be
incorrect when block weights or dimensions differ.

3. COMPACTNESS AND ORDINARY BLOCKWISE EB FORM

The pure state space of A is the finite union of the block projective
pure-state spaces. The densities pi/tau(pi) form a compact set because
tau is faithful and has finitely many strictly positive block weights.
Their first/covariance moment image is compact. The matrix baseline's
finite-dimensional Caratheodory/closed-slice proof therefore gives compact
pure canonical channels with barycenter tau and at most
  1+D(D+3)/2
atoms, where D=sum d_k^2-1. Taking the convergent tree subsequence proves
Psi|H0>=(2Phi^2-I)_+|H0, and identity adds its fixed eigenvalue one.
If the fast space is empty, any blockwise rank-one dephasing comparator
suffices. The factor4 residual is again exactly2(I-Phi)^2.

For a minimal projector pi in block k let t=alpha_k/d_k. Traciality gives
  tau(pi X)=t Tr_k(pi X_k),
so a pure canonical atom is
  p rho tau(rho X)=(p/t)pi X pi.
Thus Psi has block-rank-one Kraus operators sqrt(p_j/t_j)pi_j, with
sum_j(p_j/t_j)pi_j=I. It is ordinarily unital and TP on each matrix block,
and EB by measure/prepare into rank-one states. In each block the total
atom probability is alpha_k, as follows by taking the block trace of the
barycenter. With q_j=p_j/alpha_k it is exactly
  Psi_k(X_k)=d_k sum_(j in k)q_j Tr_k(pi_j X_k)pi_j,
  sum_(j in k)q_j pi_j=I_(d_k)/d_k.
Hence Psi fixes each central block projection. This center preservation
is a consequence for the comparator, not a premise for Phi.

4. FRAMES, COSTS, AND THE WEIGHTED INTERFACE BOUNDARY

For a positive frame define V_tau=sum tau(A_i^2), s_Phi=sum tau[A_i Phi(A_i)],
and k_tau=max sum_j p_j sum_i omega_j(A_i)^2 over ALL pure state ensembles
with functional barycenter tau. The same canonical comparator gives
  V_tau-k_tau<=4(V_tau-s_Phi).
This is the faithful-tracial generalization of the original M_d interface.
The reference must not be silently replaced by uniform global I/N.
