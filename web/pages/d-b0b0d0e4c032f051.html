INDEPENDENT AUDIT: CONDITIONAL SMOOTH-PI SAMPLER
7 October 2026. Worker poincare_sampler_audit. No source modification or publication.

VERDICT

The exact-real, supplied-warm-start transfer in cycle1 is consistent with the
source gates I inspected. I found no decisive mathematical counterexample to
that CONDITIONAL statement. This does not certify OA139, its entire manuscript,
or an implemented sampler. The primitive remains the substantive imported
result. Treat this branch as a standard proximal consequence of that primitive,
not an independently established field-breaking algorithm.

An independently reconstructed strengthening improves the transfer's moment
budget: polynomial chi-squared warmness costs only O(log d) in the gradient
second-moment estimate, rather than d^(a/2). The total outer approximation
error becomes polylog(d) d^[1/2-e(K-1)], removing a from the choice of K.
The elementary score estimate is not claimed novel.

The decisive LIMITATION is computational scope. A uniform bit-runtime theorem
does not follow from this interface. The continuous-TV version is impossible
for finitely encoded outputs; even after quantizing the target, arbitrary
translations and arbitrarily large oracle answer encodings prevent a runtime
bound depending on d alone. Cycle1 already lists the relevant missing gates.
Its conditional exact-query theorem survives these limitations because it
expressly uses exact real oracle answers, continuous randomness, and separately
charges initial-sampler acquisition and drawing.

1. EXACT INTERFACE THAT SURVIVES THIS AUDIT

Fix a>=0, beta>0, C>0 and theta>0. For all sufficiently large d, uniformly over
V in C^2(R^d) with ||Hess V||op<=beta, finite Z=integral exp(-V), finite target
position second moment, and PI(pi)<=C, SUPPLY both:

  (i) exact gradient evaluation at arbitrary points of R^d;
 (ii) a draw from rho_0 with chi^2(rho_0||pi)<=d^a.

Conditional on OA139 thm:primitive, there is a finite randomized procedure
returning a law within TV 1/10 of pi, using <=C_theta,a,beta,C d^theta gradient
queries on EVERY execution. One warm draw is an additional supplied operation;
its acquisition, gradient calls, encoding, and draw cost are not proved cheap.
The PI constant and warmness certificate are hypotheses, not acquired data.
No target normalizer, potential value, original minimizer, mean oracle, or RGO
sample is used by the large-d conditional procedure.

2. FOURTH MOMENT: VALID, AND STRONGER ESTIMATES ARE AVAILABLE

Write g=grad V and s=||g||^2. Since g has linear growth and the assumed target
position second moment is finite, g is initially in L^2(pi). Compact-cutoff
integration by parts gives

  E_pi s = E_pi Delta V <= beta d.

For k>=2, let f_L(s)=[L(1-exp(-s/L))]^(k-1). It is bounded and nonnegative,
f_L<=s^(k-1), and 0<=f_L'<=(k-1)s^(k-2). For fixed L, radial cutoffs justify

  E_pi[f_L(s)s]
   = E_pi[2 f_L'(s) g^T(Hess V)g + f_L(s) Delta V]
   <= beta(d+2k-2) E_pi s^(k-1).

The boundary term vanishes using the boundedness of f_L and E_pi||g||<infinity.
Take the cutoff radius to infinity first, then use Fatou as L increases.
Induction yields, for every integer k>=1,

  E_pi ||g||^(2k) <= beta^k product_{j=0}^{k-1}(d+2j).       (1)

For k=2 this is exactly beta^2 d(d+2), as claimed in cycle1. All constants are
sharp for V(x)=beta||x||^2/2. Convexity is unused.

There is a shorter direct proof of the needed exponential estimate. The upper
Hessian bound gives, for every u,x,

  V(x-u) <= V(x)-u.g(x)+(beta/2)||u||^2.

Rearrange, multiply by exp(-V(x)), and translate the integral. This proves

  E_pi exp(u.g(X)) <= exp(beta||u||^2/2)                  (2)

for every u. This proof needs only the upper Hessian bound and finite Z; it
does not assume convexity or target position moments. Integrating (2) with
u=sqrt(2t)G for a standard Gaussian G proves, by Tonelli,

  E_pi exp(t||g(X)||^2) <= (1-2 beta t)^(-d/2),
  0<=t<1/(2 beta).                                        (3)

Equation (1) has its own IBP proof; coefficientwise moment domination is not
being inferred merely from an MGF inequality.

Let Y=X+sqrt(h)G with X~pi, independent G, and beta h<=1. Lipschitzness gives
||g(Y)||<=||g(X)||+beta sqrt(h)||G||. Thus

  E_tildepi exp(||g(Y)||^2/(8 beta))
   <= E_pi exp(||g(X)||^2/(4 beta))
      E exp(beta h||G||^2/4)
   <= 2^d,    tildepi=pi*N(0,hI).                         (4)

For any mu with KL(mu||tildepi)<=L, the entropy variational inequality,
applied first to a bounded truncation of ||g||^2/(8 beta), gives

  E_mu||g(Y)||^2 <= 8 beta[d log 2+L].                     (5)

In particular chi^2(mu||tildepi)<=W implies KL<=log(1+W), so

  E_mu||g(Y)||^2 <= 8 beta[d log 2+log(1+W)].               (6)

This is substantially stronger than cycle1's fourth-moment/Cauchy-Schwarz
bound O(beta d sqrt(1+W)). It supplies the same intended ideal-history
control without any assumption about the actual approximate trajectory.
The score bound does NOT turn a KL-warm initialization into a chi-squared-warm
one; the mixing proof still needs the stated chi-squared hypothesis.

3. CHI-SQUARED WARMNESS AND THE REVERSE CHANNEL

The convolved target has PI constant <=C+h: conditional variance gives the
Gaussian contribution h, and applying PI to E_G f(x+sqrt(h)G) gives C by
Jensen. The simultaneous forward heat identity, with covariance-time s,
is d/ds chi^2(mu_s||pi_s)=-E_pi_s||grad(dmu_s/dpi_s)||^2. Hence the forward
channel contracts by C/(C+h).

The reverse channel is NOT ordinary backward evolution of mu's own heat flow.
It is the fixed reference-target channel with drift grad log(pi_{h-s}), whose
endpoint is kappa_y(dx) proportional to exp[-V(x)-||x-y||^2/(2h)]dx.
I read Chen-Chewi-Salim-Wibisono Lemma 15's proof, not only Theorem 4: the two
Fokker-Planck equations give the same chi-squared dissipation sign. Integrating
1/(C+h-s) gives C/(C+h) for this reverse channel starting from ANY input law
of finite relative chi-squared divergence. This justifies the Y-order chain

  P_h(y,dy')=kappa_y*N(0,hI),
  chi^2(mu P_h^T||tildepi)<=[C/(C+h)]^(2T) chi^2(mu||tildepi).

Adding Gaussian noise to the supplied initial draw obeys data processing:
chi^2(rho_0*N(0,hI)||tildepi)<=chi^2(rho_0||pi). Ideal-prefix warmness therefore
never exceeds W. The fourth-moment and logarithmic-warmness calculations both
use the correct convolved reference law.

4. LOCAL PRIMITIVE, TELESCOPING, AND NOISE REMOVAL

Set delta=d^-e and h=delta/beta. Then F_y(z)=V(y+sqrt(h)z) has gradient
Lipschitz constant <=delta, including for nonconvex V. Each evaluation of
grad F_y is exactly one evaluation of the original gradient, scaled by
sqrt(h). OA139 thm:primitive explicitly permits arbitrary F with this
Lipschitz bound and arbitrary centers. Its output targets nu*N(0,I), so after
the physical affine map it targets precisely kappa_y*N(0,hI), not kappa_y.
Its TV error is

  B_d delta^K [sqrt(d)+sqrt(h)||g(y)||],    B_d=polylog(d).  (7)

For W=d^a, (6) shows the ideal-prefix average bracket is O(sqrt d), since a is
fixed. Choose e<min(theta/2,1/4), an integer K with e(K-1)>1/2, and then
0<t<1/[160(K+2)]. T=O((1+beta C)d^e log d) ideal proximal steps give constant
mixing accuracy. Replace kernels in the valid order

  rho P_h^j (P_hat_h-P_h) P_hat_h^(T-j-1).

Each local error expectation is under an IDEAL prefix, so (6) and (7) give

  outer implementation TV error <= polylog(d)d^[1/2-e(K-1)]=o(1).  (8)

The actual prefixes need not remain chi-squared warm. The reverse replacement
order would have needed an unavailable moment bound; cycle1 uses the safe
order. Its original K>1+(a+2)/(4e) is sufficient, simply unnecessarily large.

For the final restricted conditional, the variance-halving identity
Z=Y/2+Z_new/sqrt 2 given Y=Z+N is exact. With r_j=2^-j/2 and ideal prefixes,
the initial gradient bound is now

  E||grad F_y(0)||^2=h E_tildepi||g(Y)||^2<=8 delta d log 2.

The source recurrence b_{j+1}<=(1+C delta r_j^2)b_j+C delta r_j sqrt d
uses only the Lipschitz constant, and both sums over r_j,r_j^2 are finite.
Thus b_j=O(sqrt d) uniformly. Ideal-history telescoping costs
O(J B_d delta^K sqrt d) for J=O(log d). The mode-centered Gaussian lemma
uses strong convexity of the LOCAL Gaussian-regularized potential, supplied
by ||Hess F||<=delta; it does not require convexity of original V.
Its TV error is O(delta 2^-J sqrt d), and fixed-point mode iteration has
contraction delta 2^-J. Fixed O(log d) iteration counts suffice in expectation
under the ideal history. Replacing the incoming law by stationary tildepi
uses TV data processing, so no absolute position/minimizer bound is needed.

Consequently Q<=(T+J)B_d delta^-t+O(log d)=d^[e(1+t)+o(1)]<d^theta for large d.
All outer, descent, and base iteration counts are prescribed before oracle
answers. The supplied warm operation's cost remains additional.
Declared beta C=d^o(1) can be included with a fixed positive error margin;
the same argument permits log(1+W)=d^o(1) when that growth bound is declared.
It does not cover ordinary exponentially cold chi-squared starts W=exp(Theta(d)).

The audit went beyond thm:primitive's statement:

* jets.tex, lem:appell-cumulants and prop:gaussian-jets: repeated ambient
  Poincare bounds apply to Appell polynomials of S=(Z,f(Z)/L), whose law may
  be singular. Pullback needs only Df; no high derivative of original V is
  charged as an oracle operation. The two-group log generating function
  preserves every matrix split, rather than just rank-one test directions.

* divergence.tex, thm:tensor-divergence: adjoints are removed by IBP in a
  2s-th trace moment. Each primary tensor retains its original trace-chain
  bundles; Hessian vertices can be placed between their earliest and latest
  primary neighbors and have no mutual edges. Cutting one original bundle
  gives a composition of nontrivial matrix unfoldings with one final trace,
  costing d^b with b<=j+C_V. Taking s>=(j+C_V)log(d+1) absorbs that cost.
  The simultaneous j,p bounds and degree count control the growing moment
  order. The final vector divergence pays sqrt d once. I identified no
  missing d^K factor in these inspected steps.

* centering.tex and numerics.tex: the skew stationary flow's product density
  is preserved by a complete flow, and the momentum displacement gives a
  noised mean. The numerical proof uses fixed-time RMS derivatives, not an
  unjustified random-path supremum. Global Picard depth N=ceil(10(J+2)) is
  chosen before mesh exponent w and interpolation order m. Increasing m
  changes constants and fan-out, not the dependency exponent's depth.
  Singular 1/r factors cancel in the retained forward transport correction;
  the zero-endpoint Hermite condition removes the 1/rho singularity.

* implementation.tex, lem:compiler-absorption/count and prop:compiler-accuracy:
  noisy center translation is absorbed by reducing the independent seed
  covariance, then restoring it with center noise. An inaccurate center is
  first handled by fixed-seed Lipschitz stability, and standalone accuracy
  is invoked only after absorption at an ideal noised center. Uncached term
  occurrences have disjoint seed blocks, so conditional product comparisons
  are legitimate. The labels bound routine height before choosing w,m;
  terminal gradient calls and slot count are capped for all seeds and answers.

This is substantive analytic inspection, not formal/external verification.
The decisive imported gates remain the all-split divergence theorem and the
full finite numerical/compiler construction. A counterexample to either
invalidates the consequence. No full OA139 sampler was executed here.

High-order cost warning: K,m and all polylogarithmic exponents are fixed for a
given desired theta, but they may be enormous. As theta decreases, K grows,
t must shrink, and w is chosen only after the large dependency and recursion
depths. Then m must satisfy w(m-3)>J+10. There is no certified useful dimension
threshold or practical constant. Treating those constants as small would be
an unsupported computational claim, but their size alone is not an error in
the asymptotic exact-query theorem.

6. ARITHMETIC, BIT PRECISION, DENSITIES, AND EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLES

The source computes no high-order tensor at runtime. They analyze vector
formulas whose leaves query the original gradient. The slot-space covariance
square root is rank one:

  P=I-vv^T/[4D_s^2(1+sqrt(1-||v||^2/(4D_s^2)))], ||v||<=D_s.

Applying it to m d-dimensional seed slots costs O(md), with a denominator
bounded away from zero. This supports cycle1's near-linear VECTOR-arithmetic
observation given effective scalar tables; it does not supply a uniform
ordinary-bit implementation. Exact sine/exponential/logarithmic mesh and
quadrature constants are allowable real constants in the source model.
Counting their generation, approximation, and certification is additional
work, not an oracle query.

The following gates are substantive, not cosmetic:

* Finite outputs versus continuous TV. The set of finitely encoded outputs is
  countable and has pi-measure zero. Taking that set as the TV test gives
  TV=1. A bit theorem must target a specified quantization Q_b pi (with a
  target-dependent encoding specification) or retain continuous outputs.

* No uniform magnitude/encoding cost. Let pi_m=N(m e_1,I_d), beta=C=1, and
  supply rho_0=pi_m, so chi^2=0 and all analytic hypotheses hold. For any
  proposed runtime p(d), choose m among more than 2^{p(d)+1} integer centers
  separated by ten. Their probability windows of radius three are disjoint
  in coordinate one. There are fewer than 2^{p(d)+1} output strings of length
  <=p(d), so they cannot put mass in a window for every center. This counting
  obstruction applies even to compressed numeric encodings. With standard
  binary rational encoding, a center of bit length L also directly forces
  Omega(L) input/output work. The stated interface bounds neither m nor L.

* A smooth exact real oracle need not provide certified p-bit answers at
  rational query points in polynomial time. The release's exact answers and
  random real inputs cannot silently be replaced by floating-point vectors.
  A bit model must specify coordinate accuracy, input encoding, query-radius
  bounds, answer magnitude, and gradient computation cost.

* Final Gaussian noise can absorb small local perturbations: a final reserve
  sigma G gives TV <=E||Delta||/(2 sigma). The primitive has sigma=1/2 in
  normalized coordinates, hence sqrt(h)/2 physically. This helps a compiler
  once local error and magnitude bounds are effective. It does not certify
  whole-run precision by multiplying pathwise Lipschitz constants over T
  steps; that product can grow superpolynomially. Kernel telescoping is the
  correct route, but the required digital oracle/table/Gaussian routines have
  not been furnished or run.

No potential density/normalizer oracle is needed by the conditional real
algorithm. The relevant density precision problem concerns interpreting its
exact random oracle queries and its finite output on a digital machine.

Warm acquisition is independently nontrivial. With pi=N(0,(2/3)I_d) and a
standard Gaussian warm candidate, 1+chi^2=(2/sqrt 3)^d despite beta C=1.
The score improvement cannot cure the resulting Theta(d/h) ideal mixing
time. Counting only the conditional algorithm's queries cannot erase warm
sampler acquisition or its cost to draw.
