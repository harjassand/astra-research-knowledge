ONE FACTOR-TWO COMPARATOR ON EVERY CLIFFORD OBSERVABLE SUBSPACE
Independent mathematical derivation, 2026-10-09.
Status: proof supplied; external correctness and historical priority UNKNOWN.
This is a restricted theorem. It does NOT prove the arbitrary-frame target.

THEOREM
Let Gamma_1,...,Gamma_q in M_d, q>=2, be Hermitian involutions satisfying
Gamma_i Gamma_j=-Gamma_j Gamma_i for i!=j. Let Phi:M_d->M_d be CPTP,
unital, HS-self-adjoint and self-compatible, supplied with a CPTP joint
channel B whose two marginals are Phi. No positive-HS-spectrum premise is
needed. There exists one full-domain canonical EB channel Psi such that

  <A,(I-Psi)A>_tau <= 2 <A,(I-Phi)A>_tau

for every A in span_R{I,Gamma_1,...,Gamma_q}. The same Psi works for every
direction in this subspace. There is an explicit matrix-level construction
with at most 2q+1 POVM effects. It is not a hardware compiler.

The decisive lemma is the following genuinely simultaneous star bound.
It also allows the three registers to carry different Clifford
representations, and does not require their dimensions to agree.

STAR LEMMA
Let G_i,B_i,C_i be q anticommuting Hermitian involutions on three separate
registers. Operators on different registers commute. Put

  W_q=sum_i G_i(B_i+C_i).

Then
  ||W_q|| <= sqrt(q(q+2))  for q even,
  ||W_q|| <= q+1          for q odd.

For matched irreducible Gamma representations, with G_i=Gamma_i^T,
the bounds are equalities. Only the upper bounds are needed below.

PROOF OF STAR LEMMA
Define commuting Hermitian involutions
  s_i=G_i B_i,  z_i=B_i C_i.
Within each family, s_i s_j=s_j s_i and z_i z_j=z_j z_i. Between families,
  s_i z_j=(-1)^(1-delta_ij) z_j s_i.
Moreover W_q=sum_i s_i(1+z_i).

Even q. Define f_i=product_(j!=i) s_j. The relations give
f_i z_i=-z_i f_i and f_i z_j=z_j f_i for j!=i. The f_i commute mutually.
These are q independent Pauli pairs. Every finite-dimensional
representation of these relations is a direct sum of the usual q-qubit
Pauli representation: simultaneous z-eigenspace projections and the f_i
flips explicitly give matrix units, and the 4^q ordered monomials span
the universal algebra. Thus write f_i=X_i,z_i=Z_i. Also
s_i=product_(j!=i) f_j=P f_i, where P=product_j f_j. Put
  a_i=f_i(1+z_i)/2,  L=sum_i a_i.
Each a_i is the ordinary |1><0| lowering operator on one Pauli factor.
Since P L P=L*, W_q=2P L is Hermitian and W_q^2=4L*L.
Writing the q-qubit space as total-spin sectors, L=J_-, and
  L*L |j,m>=(j+m)(j-m+1)|j,m>, j<=q/2.
For even q, m is an integer; the maximum is q(q+2)/4 at j=q/2,m=0 or 1.
This proves the even bound.

Odd q. The two central involutions product_i s_i and product_i z_i
commute with all generators. Restrict to a joint central block with their
values eta,delta in {+1,-1}. On the q-qubit parity subspace
H_delta={product_i Z_i=delta}, set
  z_i=Z_i, s_i=epsilon_i P X_i,
where epsilon_i in {+1,-1}, product_i epsilon_i=eta.
These operators have exactly the required relations and central values.
The simultaneous z-eigenspaces are one dimensional, and the s_i flips
change every sign except sign i. Those flips act transitively on the fixed
parity sign vectors. The generated algebra therefore contains all matrix
units of End(H_delta). The ordered monomials modulo the two central
relations span at most 4^(q-1) elements, exactly dim End(H_delta).
Consequently this model realizes the full central block of the universal
algebra, and every finite representation is a direct sum of this model.

In the model W_q=2P sum_i epsilon_i a_i. Conjugating by products of Z_i
removes the signs epsilon_i from L up to an overall sign on P, without
changing its norm. The full q-qubit lowering norm bounds the parity-block
norm. For odd q, m is half integral and
max_(j,m) (j+m)(j-m+1)=(q+1)^2/4 at j=q/2,m=1/2.
Hence ||W_q||<=q+1.

For the matched irreducible odd representation, product_i s_i=I and
product_i z_i=(-1)^(q(q-1)/2)I. Thus eta=1 and the parity of the Dicke
state |j=q/2,m=1/2> is exactly delta. W_q acts on that state as q+1.
For even q the Pauli algebra is simple and its full spin sector occurs in
each nonzero representation, yielding equality as well.

WEIGHTED STAR BOUND
For any w_i>=0, sort weights in nonincreasing order, set w_(q+1)=0, and
write W(w)=sum_k (w_k-w_(k+1)) W_{first k}. Every k-subset is a Clifford
family. Its norm is at most k+1 (for k=1 this follows directly from
||G_1(B_1+C_1)||<=2). Triangle inequality gives

  ||W(w)|| <= sum_k (w_k-w_(k+1))(k+1)
           = sum_i w_i + max_i w_i.                         (1)

This layer-cake argument retains a universal constant and is independent
of q and d. It differs from the archived pairwise averaging bound
q sqrt(2), which loses the sharp large-q behavior.

FROM BROADCASTER TO ONE CHANNEL
The Gamma_i are traceless and tau-orthonormal. Tracelessness follows by
conjugating Gamma_i by a different Gamma_j. Orthogonality follows by cyclic
trace and anticommutation. Define the real symmetric compression matrix
  T_ij=<Gamma_i,Phi(Gamma_j)>_tau.
Self-adjointness makes T symmetric. Let O diagonalize T, with eigenvalues
lambda_i and rotated generators Gamma'_i=sum_j O_ji Gamma_j. The rotated
generators again satisfy the Clifford relations.

Let R=J(B)/d be the normalized Choi state of the SUPPLIED broadcaster.
For weights w_i>=0 its two equal marginals give
  2 sum_i w_i <Gamma'_i,Phi(Gamma'_i)>_tau
      =Tr[R sum_i w_i (Gamma'_i)^T (Gamma'_i^B+Gamma'_i^C)]
      <=sum_i w_i+max_i w_i                              (2)
by (1). In particular choose w_i=1 on the eigenvalues lambda_i>1/2, zero
otherwise. Equation (2) implies
  sum_i (2lambda_i-1)_+ <=1.                              (3)
If no eigenvalue is >1/2, the sum is zero.

Set t_i=(2lambda_i-1)_+, s=sum_i t_i<=1. For each Gamma'_i use the
two-effect POVM P_(i,+/-)=(I+/-Gamma'_i)/2. Its canonical EB channel is
  Psi_i(X)=tau(X)I+tau(Gamma'_i X)Gamma'_i.
The output states are (I+/-Gamma'_i)/d. Let Psi_0(X)=tau(X)I, whose POVM
is the single effect I. Define ONE channel
  Psi=sum_i t_i Psi_i+(1-s)Psi_0.
Retaining the mixing coin in the outcome makes it a canonical POVM channel
with effects t_i P_(i,+/-) and (1-s)I; zero effects may be omitted.
Its compressed score matrix is S=O diag(t_i) O^T=(2T-I)_+.
Since S>=2T-I, I-S<=2(I-T). Both maps fix I and have no identity cross
terms. This proves the stated inequality simultaneously on the whole
subspace, without assigning a separate measurement to each query.

CLASSICAL SUPPORT AND SHARPNESS ON A UNIFORM FRAME
For any state rho, x_i=Tr(rho Gamma_i) obey sum_i x_i^2<=1 because
(sum_i n_i Gamma_i)^2=||n||^2 I. Therefore a uniformly weighted q-generator
frame has classical canonical support exactly 1 and joint-variance loss
q-1. The upper support is attained by the eigenbasis ensemble of Gamma_1,
whose barycenter is I/d. The star lemma gives broadcast loss at least
q-||W_q||/2. For odd q this is exactly (q-1)/2; for even q it is slightly
larger. Thus these frames obey the C=2 all-frame comparison. They cannot
produce a diverging-constant counterexample.

CHARGED INTERFACE AND SCOPE
The proof assumes exact finite matrices for Phi and the supplied Clifford
generators, and uses self-compatibility as a premise with a supplied B.
At the mathematical level it computes q^2 HS correlations, diagonalizes a
q-by-q real symmetric matrix, and writes at most 2q+1 dense POVM effects.
No acquisition, physical gate count, spectral measurement implementation,
or finite precision robustness is free. Dense input/output matrix costs
still scale with d. No output side register has been supplied or acquired.
The construction need not control observables outside the displayed
Clifford span. It also does not prove a uniform state-family EB theorem
unless that family's likelihoods lie in this span.

PRIMARY-LITERATURE BOUNDARY
The two-setting Clifford/CHSH monogamy mechanism is established prior art:
Toner and Verstraete, Monogamy of Bell correlations and Tsirelson's bound,
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0611001.
The spin representation of Pauli lowering operators is standard algebra.
The exact above central-Pauli reduction was independently derived here;
no claim of historical novelty follows. The archived N93 large-clique
proof supplies a pair-SOS obstruction with q sqrt(2), but this theorem
assumes matched Clifford families on all three registers to obtain the
stronger star spectrum. It does not supersede N93's more general
unrestricted-output-observable/flag-reorganization theorem.
