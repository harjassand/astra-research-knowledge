# Resumable Hessian-algebra identities; larger-block cyclicity remains open

7 October2026, cycle12. Algebraic derivations recorded for the next investigation. These identities are not a universal dense-QPD acquisition theorem, and no additional fixture sweep or solver was launched. The ternary-block theorem and its implemented pencil use L_V, not the alternate Gram matrix introduced here.

On h!=0 set V=H^-1 grad log h, M=H^-1(T:H^-1), and L_u=12H^-1 T(x,u,.,.). The product has unit x and each L_u is H-selfadjoint. Define the bilinear trace form and endomorphism

    S(u,w)=tr(L_u L_w),   R=H^-1 S.

At H>0, S is positive definite: H-selfadjoint L_u has real eigenvalues, tr(L_u^2) is their squared sum, and L_u x=u prevents L_u=0 for nonzero u. This only proves positivity on an H-positive patch; S/H are not claimed to be aligned hidden metrics or supplied to the learner. Existence of that patch is already proved in the gated ternary-block note. R is GL-similarity-covariant, homogeneous of degree-4 and block diagonal on direct sums.

Because L_x=I and grad log h(u)=2tr(L_u),

    R x=V/2.                                      (1)

For coordinate directions e_i, H_i=24T(x,e_i,.,.) and H_ij=24T(e_i,e_j,.,.). The logdet differentiation formula gives

    Hess log h=24(T:H^-1)-4S.

Cubic symmetry gives(dH[e_i])V=(dH[V])e_i, and dH[V]=2H L_V. Therefore

    Jac(V)=24M-4R-2L_V.                           (2)

In particular H Jac(V) is symmetric, even away from radial critical points. The existing covariant family contains an H-positive endomorphism through(2). This is a useful structural fact, but an invertible or positive operator can still have a one-dimensional initial-vector orbit (the identity is the simplest example). Neither(1) nor(2) closes the needed cyclicity condition for one arbitrary dense block of dimension>=4.
