EXACT INTERFACE

Let the ambient dimension be even r. A line l has two explicit rational
vectors v_l,w_l in Q(i)^r and positive rational activity a_l. A microscopic
full-basis state A is a set of r/2 lines. Set

  W(A)=(product_{l in A} a_l) |det V_A|^2,

where V_A contains both columns of every selected line in a fixed order.
The law pi(A)=W(A)/Z is restricted to states of positive weight. Point
weights, determinant ratios and zero tests are polynomial-bit computable;
the sum Z is not supplied. For the canonical hard-BCS class v_i=e_i and
w_i=f_i, the transpose of row i of F, one has

  |det V_A|^2=|det F[A,A^c]|^2

when r=n and |A|=n/2. Coordinate lines (e_i,e_j) may also be added.
For a reversible kernel P on pi tensor pi, write

  E_P(H)=1/2 sum_{x,y} (pi tensor pi)(x) P(x,y)(H(x)-H(y))^2.

The intended comparison quantifies over ALL real f1,f2:

  Var_pi f1 <= C E_P((A,B) -> f1(A)+f2(B)).                 (E)

The equal-functions direction f1=f2 is included. Controlling only
f2=0, or only antisymmetric functions, is insufficient.

1. A BALANCED POSITIVE WELL-CONDITIONED REASSIGNMENT COUNTEREXAMPLE

For any integer L>=2 put d=2^-L and use one-based labels with

             [0  1+d  1  1]
  F_d =      [1+d  0  1  2].
             [1   1   0  1]
             [1   2   1  0]

Every off-diagonal entry is in [1,2], every diagonal is zero, and F is real
symmetric. All line activities can be one. Take the full-basis line sets

  A={1,2}, B={3,4}, A'={1,3}, B'={2,4}.

Each pair tuple uses every original line exactly once, so the multiset
union is preserved. The four complementary minors are exactly

  det F[A,A^c]=det F[B,B^c]=1,
  det F[A',A'^c]=det F[B',B'^c]=d.

Consequently

                 W(A')W(B')/[W(A)W(B)]=d^4=2^-4L.          (1)

This is a four-line reassignment with perfectly comparable activities.
It exhibits exponential Metropolis rejection in the binary input length,
although no matrix entry is small and no complex phase is present.
Cancellation of two positive products in a selected minor is sufficient.

Full-matrix conditioning is uniformly bounded. With F_0 obtained by d=0,

 F_0^-1=[-1  1/2   0  1/2;
          1/2 -1/2 1/2 0;
          0  1/2  -1  1/2;
          1/2 0   1/2 -1/2].

Its maximum absolute row sum is 2. It is real symmetric, hence its
spectral norm is at most 2. The perturbation F_d-F_0 has spectral norm d.
The Neumann inverse bound therefore gives

  ||F_d^-1||_2 <= 2/(1-2d) <=4,
  ||F_d||_2 <= ||F_d||_infinity <=4+d <=17/4,
  kappa_2(F_d)<=17.                                       (2)

No asserted lower bound on every selected minor follows from (2).

The ambient-hole law is already within the exact baseline balance window.
For even holes U write f(U)=Z_{[4]\U} and g(U)=f(U)/f(empty).
The all-occupied partition is the sum over the six two/two cuts:

                 f(empty)=4(1-d+d^2).                     (3)

For two holes {i,j}, let {a,b} be their complement. Since F is symmetric,
f({i,j})=2 F_ab^2. For 0<d<=1/4 this gives

  13/4<=f(empty)<=4,
  1/2<=g({i,j})<=32/13<4.

Thus EVERY two-hole coefficient is bounded between two positive constants,
and each row maximum r_i=max_{j!=i} g({i,j}) satisfies the required
1/8<=r_i<=4 without any balancing oracle. Aggregate stability and exchange
are fully compatible with the exponentially bad microscopic ratio (1).

The ratio cannot be repaired by gauge choice. More generally, let
G in GL_r(Q(i)) be a common ambient preconditioner and let each line's
two columns be multiplied by U_l in GL_2(Q(i)). Let its activity change
arbitrarily to a'_l>0. Then

 W'(A)=|det G|^2 product_{l in A}[a'_l |det U_l|^2] |det V_A|^2.

For any A,B,A',B' having the same multiset union, each line has the same
total multiplicity in numerator and denominator. All G, U_l and activity
factors cancel. The reassignment product ratio is EXACTLY unchanged.
Adding coordinate auxiliary states changes Z but cannot change this ratio
between the four pre-existing full-basis states. Normalization cancels too.

Therefore an analogue of the graph proof's acceptance-one union swap or
bounded comparable-activity weight comparison cannot hold for every such
determinant reassignment, even after balancing or general linear
preconditioning. A legal energy proof must sum over better moves, retain
extra information, or use an integrated bound that tolerates individual
exponentially small capacities. This statement does not prove that any
particular complete proposal has small energy.

2. AN ADDITIVE ESCAPE-CAPACITY OBSTRUCTION

Lemma. Let nu be a stationary law for a finite Markov kernel P. Let H be
a subset with nu(H^c)=eta. Suppose a function T takes values in [0,2] and
is constant on every legal transition with both endpoints in H. Then

                        E_P(T)<=4 eta.                    (4)

Reversibility is not needed for this bound; stationarity suffices.
Indeed only directed edges incident to H^c can contribute. Their total
stationary flow is at most

  sum_{x in H^c,y}nu(x)P(x,y)+sum_{x in H,y in H^c}nu(x)P(x,y)
  <=eta+eta=2eta,

where stationarity bounds the second term by all inflow to H^c, namely eta.
The squared difference is at most 4; multiplication by 1/2 proves (4).

For a product law nu=pi tensor pi, let an ordinary subset O have pi(O)=1-b.
Take H=O x O. Then eta=2b-b^2. If a one-layer h in [0,1] has
h(A)+h(B) invariant on every H-to-H transition, comparison (E) with
f1=f2=h requires

               C >= Var_pi h/[4(2b-b^2)].                 (5)

Arbitrary rapid motion inside H, including global layer swaps, does not
remove this additive variance direction. A stationary refresh proposal
whose useful target entrances are rare is subject to the same bound.

3. EXACT COORDINATE-AUGMENTED CYCLE APPLICATION

Take n=2q>=4, original canonical lines (e_i,e_{i+1}) on the cyclic
coordinates i modulo n, with unit activity. F is the cyclic permutation
matrix; its condition number is one. Add one coordinate line (e_i,e_j)
of activity epsilon for EVERY unordered pair i<j. The original lines and
coordinate lines retain distinct colors when their endpoints agree.
Every nonzero full determinant has absolute value one. Thus microscopic
states are colored perfect matchings of the complete coordinate graph.

Exactly two states contain no coordinate lines: the two alternating
matchings A_O and A_E on the cycle, each of weight one. Let O={A_O,A_E}
and let b be the target mass outside O. With M=n+binom(n,2) total line
labels and 0<epsilon<=1, at most 2^M line subsets exist. Each rare state
uses a coordinate line and has weight at most epsilon. Therefore

                  b<=(epsilon 2^M)/2.                    (6)

Consider ANY stationary two-replica kernel whose permitted transitions
are each of either type:

 (i) replace at most b0<q original line labels IN EACH layer; coordinate
     line edits and proposal probabilities may otherwise be arbitrary;
 (ii) preserve the multiset union of all line labels across both layers,
      allowing unbounded global interchanges and arbitrary decomposition.

No oracle restrictions are needed for this lower bound. It holds even
for a proposal designed with perfect knowledge of the target law.

For (i), an H-to-H transition cannot change either layer, because A_O and
A_E are disjoint q-line sets and switching them replaces q original
labels. For (ii), the number of ordinary layers equal to A_O is determined
by original-line multiplicities in the union, hence cannot change.
Set h(A)=1_{A=A_O}. Then h(A)+h(B) is invariant on H-to-H transitions.
Since pi(A_O)=(1-b)/2,

                 Var_pi h=(1-b^2)/4.

Combining this with (5) proves the exact necessary bound

             C >= (1-b^2)/[16(2b-b^2)].                    (7)

Choose epsilon=2^-(M+L) with L>=1. Then b<=2^-(L+1), so (7) implies,
for example, C>=3*2^L/64. The bit length of epsilon is M+L+1. For fixed
q>b0 this is an exponential lower bound in a line-activity encoding
length, despite perfectly conditioned F, computable point weights,
strictly positive coordinate auxiliaries, and unlimited union swaps.
An explicit coordinate-dominated initial law does not imply a polynomial
energy bound at this endpoint. At epsilon=0 the energy is exactly zero
for h+h, so no finite additive comparison exists in this move class.

This example is a graph-matching subclass, already easy to count and
sample by other algorithms. It is NOT evidence of computational hardness.
It does NOT contradict OA113: its endpoint lacks the source's calibrated
reference network guaranteeing adequate auxiliary capacities. With
inverse-polynomial defect activities the estimate above is only a
polynomial necessary loss and may be harmless. The useful implication
is that a proposed compiler must supply quantitative target escape
capacity, not only positive auxiliaries, a base refresh and pair swaps.
