ACQUIRED POLYNOMIAL-SIZE FLAT-ENSEMBLE POVM
c04_s01 independent construction. Complements HYPEROCTAHEDRAL.txt.

1. FINITE-FIELD CHARACTER FRAME
Let q=2^t be the least power of two >=d+1. Thus q<2(d+1). Construct the
field F_q using a monic irreducible binary polynomial of degree t and
choose d distinct nonzero labels x_i. For a,b uniform in F_q define
 s_i(a,b)=(-1)^Tr_Fq/F2(a x_i+b x_i^3).
This supplies q^2 sign vectors, using 2t seed bits, and they are exactly
four-wise independent. To see this, a product of k<=4 distinct signs is
unbiased whenever (sum x_i,sum x_i^3)!=(0,0). For k1/2 this is immediate;
for k3 with zero first sum, the cubic sum is xyz!=0. For k4 with zero
first sum, it is (x1+x2)(x1+x3)(x2+x3)!=0. The characteristic-two identity
is verified by expanding. Therefore all required sign products match
independent Rademachers exactly.

Set psi_s=d^(-1/2)sum_i s_i|i>, P_s=|psi_s><psi_s|,
 M_s=(d/q^2)P_s. Pairwise character orthogonality gives sum_s M_s=I.
The canonical EB map sum_s Tr(M_s A)P_s satisfies
 Psi_Rf(A)=[A+A^T-2Diag(A)]/d+tau(A)I,
so its D,S,A eigenvalues are 0,2/d,0 as required. Every entry of M_s and
P_s is rational (sign/q^2 and sign/d). No Haar integral, unknown design,
Monte Carlo estimate or coefficient oracle is required.

2. EXACT WALSH MEASUREMENT
Use the trace-dual binary coordinates of (x_i,x_i^3) to make distinct
2t-bit strings L_i satisfying s_i(a,b)=(-1)^dot((a,b),L_i).
Embed |i> into |L_i>, apply 2t Hadamards, and measure in the computational
basis. Its analysis matrix has entries s_i/q and realizes exactly M_s.
The label embedding is an isometry because L_i are distinct. It can be
compiled reversibly from finite-field arithmetic (the trace pairing is
nondegenerate and its first t coordinates encode x_i invertibly).
After measuring s, prepare psi_s. Uniform-amplitude preparation on d
known basis states and its sign phase are explicit known operations;
no input-dependent repeat-until-success measurement is used.

sign_povm.py implements irreducible-polynomial acquisition, finite-field
arithmetic, trace-dual labels and exact checks of all distinct subsets
of at most four labels for d=2,3,4,5,8,12,16. sign_povm.json saves the
acquired labels and integer certificates. General exactness is proved
by the cubic identity, not extrapolated from those checks.

3. NULL-FLAT ENDPOINT BY A COSTED CLASSICAL MIXTURE
Let z_j=exp(pi i j/d), so sum_j z_j^2=0 and |z_j|=1. Choose a uniform
permutation pi of the d phases and conjugate the real-flat POVM/preparations
by the known diagonal unitary diag(z_pi(j)). The permutation is a retained
classical coin, independent of the input. Four-wise signs supply the exact
full-sign fourth moments, and averaging the permutation supplies precisely
the signed-permutation orbit second moment of the null-flat seed. Hence
this map has D,S,A eigenvalues 0,(d-2)/[d(d-1)],1/(d-1).
The conceptual outcome space has d!q^2 elements; it is implemented by a
classical mixture, not by writing an exponential matrix. Store pi with
O(d log d) classical bits and the actual measurement seed with 2t bits.
All retained registers are charged. This is not an optimal archive-size
claim, nor a dimension-free apparatus-size claim.

4. OTHER TWO ENDPOINTS AND COST BOUND
The diagonal endpoint is basis dephasing. The two-coordinate null endpoint
has the explicit d(d-1) effects
 M_(ij,+/-)=P_((e_i +/- i e_j)/sqrt(2))/(d-1), i<j,
and the corresponding rank-one preparations. Summing the two signs removes
coherence and each coordinate occurs in d-1 pairs, proving exact POVM
normalization. It has the N2 eigenvalues in the companion proof.
An explicitly given O(d^2)-by-d Naimark isometry allows ordinary two-level
rotation synthesis in O(d^3) rotations (conservative), with the logarithmic
index-register overhead. Its entries are known algebraic constants; this
is an ordinary constructive matrix algorithm, not a supplied POVM oracle.

At worst, irreducible-polynomial enumeration uses O(d poly(log d)) binary
operations; labels use O(d poly(log d)) operations and O(d log d) bits.
The implemented diagnostic does extra O(d^4) subset verification, which is
not needed in the general generation algorithm. Uniform permutation
sampling has O(d log d) expected random-bit/index work by rejection for
finite integer draws. Coefficients and phase/rotation approximations at
P=O(log d+log(1/delta)) bits support additive channel error delta after
polynomially many known gates; a conservative full synthesis budget is
poly(d, input-coefficient bits, log(1/delta)), dominated by the explicit
Naimark matrix routine. Exact lambda parameters produce exact rational
four-endpoint weights by the displayed formulas, including the zero-
denominator d2 boundary case. Approximate unknown lambda acquisition is
not supplied by this construction.

Only the finite-field frame generator and exact certificates ran. A full
Naimark/gate compiler and hardware realization did not run, so their
algorithmic cost bounds are constructions rather than measured runtimes.
Gate/drive energy is UNKNOWN. Input/output channel arithmetic, physical
preparation, outcome record size and finite precision remain distinct.

PRIORITY QUALIFICATION
Small k-wise independent sign spaces and their coding-theoretic constructions
are established methods, not claimed new here. Alon--Babai--Itai (1986),
https://www.cs.bu.edu/faculty/gacs/courses/cs535/papers/Alon_Babai_Itai.pdf,
and Alon--Goldreich--Hastad--Peralta,
https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~oded/PSX/aghp.pdf, describe this family
of derandomization ideas and O(n^(k/2)) sample-size constructions. The
particular degree-three character frame was reconstructed and implemented
here; the candidate increment is its exact canonical-POVM/Walsh realization
within the new rounding construction. Exact priority for that application
remains unverified.
