Derived proposition:
Let W be a nonzero smooth mean-zero divergence-free field on T3. Let a be
nonnegative bounded C1 with a(0)=0 and integral_0^infty a=infinity, and set
U=aW, F=a'W+a^2(W dot grad)W-nu a Delta W, nu>0. Then F is not L1 in time
with values in L2_x (nor in spatial supremum norm).

Proof: incompressibility and integration by parts give
  <(W dot grad)W,W>=0,
  <-Delta W,W>=||grad W||_2^2>0.
Hence
  integral_0^T <F(t),W>dt
    =a(T)||W||_2^2 + nu||grad W||_2^2 integral_0^T a(t)dt
    ->infinity.
If integral_0^infty ||F(t)||_2dt were finite, Cauchy-Schwarz would bound
the left side by ||W||_2 integral||F||_2, a contradiction.
The source slow clock a=(1+t)^(-1) fits this barrier. Its body force and
velocity can have L2 time norms and finite total forcing work while still
requiring infinite signed impulse along W. Force norm and work are distinct.

The barrier is deliberately limited to this scalar-clock construction.
The companion rapid-forcing source replaces scalar clocks with shrinking
spatial records and explicitly avoids it:
https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Computation-under-Rapidly-Vanishing-Navier-Stokes-Forcing-September-27-2026/build/main.tex
Each machine step lasts one physical time unit, displacements shrink,
and all fixed mixed derivatives decay faster than every inverse power.
Thus integrable-force computation is already claimed by a distinct source
construction; the scalar barrier is not a general no-go theorem.
