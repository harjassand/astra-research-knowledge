TARGET
For a nonempty family E of states in fixed finite dimension d, with
T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2, b(E) is the infimum over one full-domain
CPTP map B:A->A_1 A_2 of sup_{rho in E} max_j T(B_j(rho),rho). The
reconstruction error e(E) is the infimum over one full-domain EB channel
Lambda of sup_{rho in E} T(Lambda(rho),rho). The radius is
R(E)=inf_{sigma in trace-closed conv(E)} sup_{rho in E} D_obs(rho||sigma),
where D_obs is sup over effects F of p ln(p/q), p=Tr(F rho), q=Tr(F sigma).
The requested uniform statement is e(E)^2 <= K(R(E)+1)b(E)^alpha with
universal finite K and alpha>0. The supplied current estimate is
e(E)<=400 sqrt((R(E)+1)/ln(1/b(E))).

BEST FINITE-OUTPUT ROUNDING MECHANISM
Qi and Ranard, Theorem 1 (2021, Quantum 5, 555; arXiv:2001.01507v3), is the
closest reviewed source theorem to a finite-output common-measurement
rounding. For any CPTP channel Lambda:A->B_1...B_n, any tolerance eps>0,
and any output subset size t, it supplies one POVM {M_alpha} on A and an
excluded set Q of size at most
  2 d_A^6 ln(d_A) t / eps^2
such that every output subset R of size t disjoint from Q has a
measure-and-prepare approximation
  E_R(X)=sum_alpha Tr(M_alpha X) sigma_R^alpha
with ||Lambda_R-E_R||_diamond <= eps. The POVM is independent of R;
prepared states may depend on R. Their diamond norm is the ordinary channel
diamond norm, with an arbitrary input reference and unhalved output trace
norm. Thus this is a strong full-domain, full-reference estimate, but its
dimension cost is d_A^6 ln d_A and it requires a single n-output channel.

It gives an explicit consequence for the present family problem. Fix any
two-output broadcaster B with per-marginal error b' on E. Recursively apply
B to every current leaf for m rounds, obtaining Lambda_m:A->A^{tensor n}
with n=2^m leaves. Every leaf marginal is a composition B_{j_m}...B_{j_1}.
Trace-distance contraction proves by induction that its error on E is at
most m b':
  T(B_j C(rho),rho) <= T(C(rho),rho)+T(B_j(rho),rho).
Take t=1 in Qi-Ranard and choose n>2 d^6 ln(d)/eps^2. At least one leaf j
lies outside Q. The single EB channel E_j then obeys
  e(E) <= m b' + eps/2,
  m=ceil(log_2 n).
Taking eps=b' and then b' down to b(E) gives the finite, dimension-dependent
bound
  e(E) <= b(E) [ceil(log_2(2 d^6 ln(d)/b(E)^2 + 1)) + 1/2]
up to an arbitrarily small infimum slack. In particular, at fixed d this is
O_d(b log(d/b)); after absorbing the logarithm it implies a power bound
with any exponent strictly below 2 and a d-dependent constant. This is a
real finite-cost mechanism: the tree uses n-1 calls to the supplied B, but
has n=O(d^6 ln(d)/b^2) leaves. The paper describes a constructive
optimization procedure for finding the blanket and measurement, but the
theorem statement does not give a charged bit-complexity or acquisition-cost
bound for extracting the POVM.

This does not prove the requested inequality. The constants still depend on
d, and neither d nor ln(d) is controlled by the supplied R(E) in general.
The center constraint is also not removable by simply applying the theorem
to a Choi state: its Choi reference is maximally mixed and has entropy ln d.
An input family with a singleton E={sigma} has R(E)=0 even when
S(sigma)=ln d is arbitrarily large, although that singleton is trivially
EB-reconstructible. This falsifies only the naive substitution S(sigma)<=R;
it is not a counterexample to the target.

QI-RANARD'S ENTROPY BUDGET AND THE MISSING FAMILY CONVERSION
Theorem 2 of the same paper gives a state-level version. For
rho_AB1...Bn, it finds a common ensemble {p_alpha,rho_A^alpha} and, outside
an excluded region Q of size at most 2 ln(2) S(A) t/eps^2, approximates
rho_AR by sum_alpha p_alpha rho_A^alpha tensor sigma_R^alpha in the
one-way LOCC_leftarrow norm, for every size-t region R disjoint from Q.
The direction is from R to A: the theorem maximizes measurements on R and
then communicates their result to A. It is not trace norm. Its own
conversion to trace norm has factor
Omega_{d_A,d_R}=min{d_A^2,4d_A^(3/2),4d_R^(3/2),sqrt(153 d_A d_R),2d_R-1}.
The channel proof further uses ||N_1-N_2||_diamond <= d_A ||rho^N1-rho^N2||_1,
which yields the d_A^6 term above. Their alternative diamond-LOCC bound is
not a full diamond or full trace bound.

There is a direct proof-level refinement, not stated as their theorem:
their chain-rule proof bounds the sum of positive conditional mutual
information increments by S(A) after measuring the selected output blocks.
Data processing also bounds that same sum by the unmeasured I(A:B_1...B_n).
So in Proposition 1's entropy budget one can replace S(A) by
min{S(A), I(A:B_1...B_n)}. Re-running their Proposition 1 then gives the
same residual conditional-information bound with this smaller budget J;
their state conversion gives one-way-LOCC error at most
sqrt(2 ln(2) J t/q) for excluded-set budget q and target output-block size
t. This may help a prior-dependent route, but it does not itself produce
an EB approximation uniformly over E. For a fixed
prior p on E, the natural classical label X has I(X:A)=chi(p,E); data
processing budgets I(X:outputs) by chi(p,E), hence at most the max-prior
Holevo capacity C(E). The resulting state theorem remains one-way LOCC and
prior-averaged, while the target is worst-state output trace error for one
channel. The supplied interface gives R(E)<=C(E)+ln(2), not a usable
conversion C(E)<=R(E)+O(1); do not substitute the Holevo budget for R.

The exact missing conversion is therefore twofold: (i) a family-restricted
common-measurement theorem whose output count / entropy budget is controlled
by the observational radius R(E), rather than by d or S(A), and (ii) an
upgrade from any prior-average or one-way-LOCC statement to sup_{rho in E}
output trace distance for the same full-domain EB map. The first is a
center/radius-to-quantum-information budget lemma; the second must preserve
the POVM while moving the testing quantifier from one fixed prior and one
output measurement to worst input state and its own Helstrom test.

WHAT THE ENTROPIC NO-BROADCASTING RESULTS DO NOT SUPPLY
Lemm and Wilde, Theorems 4 and 5 (Phys. Rev. A 96, 012304 (2017),
arXiv:1608.07569v2), give a genuine quantitative entropy-loss / recovery
mechanism, but for a different task. A single n-output channel must
approximately broadcast one input state sigma_1 (identical output
marginals) and approximately clone a second state sigma_2 into a product
of output states. Under finite D(sigma_1||sigma_2) support assumptions,
they prove
  D(sigma_1||sigma_2)-D(tilde_sigma_1||tilde_sigma_2)
    >= (n-1)D(tilde_sigma_1||tilde_sigma_2)
    >= (n-1)||tilde_sigma_1-tilde_sigma_2||_1^2/2.
Their stronger Theorem 5 constructs a recovery channel exactly recovering
sigma_2 from its product clone and approximately recovering sigma_1 when
the relative-entropy loss is small. This is pairwise and reference-state
dependent; the broadcaster is not assumed to product-clone a center in
the present problem, and the conclusion is recovery, not one common EB
channel for every state of E. It cannot be applied by setting sigma_2 to
the radius center.

Janzing and Steudel's “broadcasting Holevo information” is explicitly an
average-information optimization for a fixed ensemble. Their 2006 paper
states that they do not solve the general problem and only derive bounds
for pure time-orbit ensembles. It does not control trace distance to input
states or a worst-state EB reconstruction. Likewise, results that identify
discord with asymptotic average mutual-information loss do not by
themselves give the target's finite worst-state trace bound.

BOTTOM LINE FOR THIS ROUTE
The strongest prior mechanism does remove the exponential truncation for
fixed d: recursively amplify a supplied two-output broadcaster and apply
Qi-Ranard's finite common-POVM channel theorem. It yields a concrete
dimension-dependent power law and a common full-domain EB map. To reach
K(R+1)b^alpha, a new family-restricted entropy/radius theorem must remove
the d-dependence without losing the common POVM or the worst-state trace
norm. No reviewed primary theorem supplies that conversion or a matching
obstruction. This is a concrete gate, not a novelty conclusion.
