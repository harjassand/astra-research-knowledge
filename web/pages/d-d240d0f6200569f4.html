1. AN EXACT DIFFERENTIAL IDENTITY FOR THE GIBBS SANDWICH GENERATOR

Work in ordinary Bloch coordinates m, |m|<1, not the scaled variable of INITIAL.
Set k_N(m)=((I+m.sigma)/2)^tensor N. For a unit vector n define
 u_n=n.m, v_n=n-u_n m,
 B_n=N u_n+v_n.grad_m, R_n=(n cross m).grad_m.
Direct normalized local filtering and local rotation give EXACT identities
 {n.J,k_N}=B_n k_N, [n.J,k_N]=i R_n k_N.
The differential operators commute with constant matrix left/right action.
Thus
 { (n.J)^2,k_N}/2=(B_n^2-R_n^2)k_N/4.                   (S2)
There is no asymptotic or commuting-anisotropy assumption.
The minus sign reflects backward angular diffusion and is essential.

Choose Lambda=M+1 and C=A+Lambda I. Then
 I<=C<=(2M+1)I. Let Lc=2M+1 and Tc=Tr C<=3Lc.
Set s=N^3/4, t0=2/N-Lambda/s^2>0, and
 H=Q_C/s^2+b.J/s.
Then K=t0J^2+H. Diagonalize C=sum lambda_i n_i n_i^T.
The quantum sandwich generator M_H(X)={H,X}/2, on product kernels, equals
 G k_N, where
 G=sum_i lambda_i/(4s^2)(B_ni^2-R_ni^2)+B_b/(2s).        (S3)
Here B_b=N(b.m)+(b-(b.m)m).grad, by linearity.

Expanding (S3) writes it as
 Gf=V_N(m)f+a_N(m).grad f
                    +D_C(m):Hess f/(4s^2),             (S4)
where
 D_C(m)=sum_i lambda_i[v_i v_i^T-r_i r_i^T],
 r_i=n_i cross m,
 a_N=sum_i lambda_i/(4s^2)
       [2N u_i v_i+(v_i.grad)v_i-(r_i.grad)r_i]
       +(b-(b.m)m)/(2s),
 V_N= [N^2 m^TCm+N(Tc-m^TCm)]/(4s^2)
                      +N b.m/(2s).
V_N=Tr H k_N exactly. All coefficients are explicit polynomials except the
positive square root used for diffusion. The resulting representation is
basis independent; eigenaxes merely display the derivation.

2. POSITIVE DIFFUSION ON A FIXED INNER BLOCH BALL

With cross-product matrix [m]_cross,
 D_C=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T.
The sign of [m]_cross is irrelevant to its outer product.
For every real vector z,
 z^TD_C z >=[(1-|m|^2)^2-Lc|m|^2]|z|^2.                (S5)
Indeed C>=I bounds the first term below and C<=Lc I bounds the second above.

Choose r0^2=1/[16(Lc+1)], so r0<=1/sqrt32<1.
For |m|<=r0, (S5) is >3|z|^2/4, uniformly in the unknown admitted A.
Also D_C<=sum lambda_i v_i v_i^T<=Tc I because
 |v_i|^2=1-(2-|m|^2)u_i^2<=1.
Thus D_C is uniformly positive and smoothly square-rootable on this ball.

Let m_t solve until its first hit tau of |m|=r0:
 dm_t=a_N(m_t)dt+sqrt(D_C(m_t)/(2s^2)) dW_t,
with ordinary three-dimensional Brownian motion.
All coefficients are smooth and Lipschitz on a neighborhood of the closed
ball; standard finite-dimensional SDE existence/uniqueness until tau applies.
No PSD test with an unknown spectral gap is used: (S5) is an explicit margin.
After tau freeze both m and its positive Feynman--Kac weight
 w_t=exp[int_0^(t wedge tau) V_N(m_u)du].
Every path output w_t k_N(m_(t wedge tau)) is a positive identical-product
operator. Its expectation W_t is therefore in that cone.

Global operator bound on H:
 hN= Lc (N/2)(N/2+1)/s^2+B N/(2s)

Hence |V_N|<=hN, w_t<=e^hN and

These costs are kept explicit rather than treated as free normalization.

4. STOPPING PROBABILITY, INCLUDING DRIFT AND WEIGHTS

6. WHY A NAIVE GLOBAL CLAIM WOULD FAIL

D_C need not be positive on the entire Bloch ball. At |m|=1 its transverse
restriction is C_transverse minus its 90-degree-rotated version. For a diagonal
C with two different transverse eigenvalues it has both signs. Thus (S3) cannot
be declared a global positive diffusion for arbitrary anisotropy. Stopping in
the explicitly certified inner ball, and bounding the exact stopped residual
(S10), are load-bearing. Dropping the angular term changes the target and gives
only the baseline CP local-filter approximation.

Nothing here proves exact separability of every admitted finite-N target,
or a sharp exponential entanglement tail. The scalar A=aI exact case in INITIAL
shows that any positive family-wide matching lower rate is false.
The stopped construction depends on A,b and cannot improve the unchanged
universal reverse decoder's sharp N^-1/2 rate by substitution.
