rational for rational t. Define a SINGLE time-independent channel for each t,
 Phi_t=Theta Ad_U(t).
It is exactly TP, CP, and coCP for every t. These obligations follow by
composition with a unitary CP channel and do not depend on a small-parameter

apply. It changes the alignment of each output with the next PPT noise step.

Phi_t^3. Thus the exact target is

The same U(t) is used at both intermediate steps. Success would therefore
produce a genuine fixed-channel cube counterexample, not an adaptive-filter
