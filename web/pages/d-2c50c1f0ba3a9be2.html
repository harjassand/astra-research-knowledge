Title:Simplicial Volume and Scalar Curvature on Closed Kähler Surfaces

Authors:Jie Min, Fangyang Zheng, Bo Zhu
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Simplicial Volume and Scalar Curvature on Closed K\"ahler Surfaces, by Jie Min and 2 other authors

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:Let $M$ be a closed Kähler surface. We prove that every Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$ with $\operatorname{Sc}_g\geq-\lambda^2$, where $\lambda\geq 0$, satisfies $$

\lVert M\rVert\leq \frac{27}{2}\,\lambda^4\operatorname{vol}_g(M). $$ This proves Gromov's quantitative scalar-curvature--simplicial-volume conjecture for closed Kähler surfaces. We also construct infinitely many non-Kähler symplectic 4-manifolds of general type with positive simplicial volume for which the same estimate holds.
