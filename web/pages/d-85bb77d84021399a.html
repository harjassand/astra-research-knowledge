## Chi-Fang Chen, arXiv:2605.02877v1

Primary full HTML/text and PDF preserved. Full technical reading: Definitions I.1–I.5, Lemmas I.1–I.5, Lindbladian preliminaries II, the III.1 proof's dependence on the preceding metastability paper, III.2 converse, and all IV repeated-measurement and geometry arguments.

Authors' claim relevant here: a measurement instrument that recovers each subnormalized branch approximately permits near-iid repeated outcomes, with an error accumulating linearly in the number of rounds. The same recovery must be shared when comparing states. The operator and measurement versions have different quantifiers; conversion from per-Kraus to summed instrument error can cost the Kraus count. A local recovery cannot alter exterior conditional correlations, which explains the strong-Markov-to-clustering implication.

Independently reconstructed: repeated-record telescoping via a classical-quantum CPTP instrument; branch-mixture covariance identity; exterior-marginal obstruction. We do not use the paper's Gibbs local-recovery theorem as a premise for our exclusion process. Its approximate detailed-balance assumption involves all single Pauli jumps over AB and is stronger than stationarity under charge-conserving swaps. The mathematical link to a prior theorem was identified but that prior 2510.08538 proof was not fully acquired or audited in this worker run.

Minor algebraic checks: printed Eq. (32) needs a factor 1/2 on its commutator sum, because [V,O]V†+V[O,V†]=2(VOV†−O). This changes an absorbable constant in the stated coarse bounds; it is not evidence that the theorem is false. The middle inequality in Eq. (66) writes ε/(4τ²) after obtaining ε/(2τ²); the final stated bound follows directly from the latter without that middle expression. No correction is being sent to the author.

## Chen–Gilyén, arXiv:2603.16845v2

Verified 17 March v1 and 29 September v2 dates and title change on primary abs page. Both HTML versions preserved; substantive reading is of v2 excerpts: introduction, exact access model, Theorem 1, detailed-balanced weak measurement Definition 1, Methods 3.2, Discussion, and Appendix A concentration proof. Other methods/readings were skimmed; no full audit of all block-encoding implementation or black-box lower-bound machinery is claimed.

Nearest comparator: O(log(M/δ)/ε²) independent Gibbs samples, observable block encodings, controlled H evolutions, and total simulated H time roughly βM/ε² up to logarithms. Outcomes can be strongly correlated within a sample. Averaged preservation suffices because the concentration uses independent initial copies and a uniform rare-event bound for repeated weak measurements; union bounds do not require different observables' estimates to be independent. This is a stronger general constructive result than a demand for full branchwise resetting. It already anticipates the distinction between autocorrelation and global mixing times. Our model should be retained as a solvable benchmark rather than marketed as a new general tomography route.
