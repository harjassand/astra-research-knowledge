# Physical analytic measurements: prior-art audit (2026-10-06)

Exact question: for jointly analytic m:M×Z→R (dim M=d, Z connected finite-dimensional analytic, law absolutely continuous), does full-data pair and tangent separation imply that 2d+1 independent physical samples give an injective immersion, hence ambient-norm bi-Lipschitz recovery on compact K⊂M?

## Independent derivation: immersion add-on
Let tangent separation mean for every a and nonzero v∈T_aM, some z has d_a m(a,z)[v]≠0. In each analytic parameter chart use projectivized nonzero tangent directions; local affine projective charts form a countable analytic cover of dimension2d−1. The scalar h(a,[v],z)=d_a m(a,z)[v] is analytic and not identically zero in z. Its zero fiber has dimension at most dim Z−1. For N independent inputs, tangent-bad incidence dimension is at most(2d−1)+N(dim Z−1). Projection to Z^N is null for N>2d−1, i.e.N≥2d. Pair badness requires N>2d. Thus2d+1 handles both. No claim that Sontag printed this tangent variant; it is an immediate repeat of the established incidence proof.

For compact K in embedded C1 M, injective immersion G implies positive lower secant ratio. If otherwise a_n≠b_n in K have ||G(a_n)−G(b_n)||/||a_n−b_n||→0, take convergent subsequences. Distinct limiting points contradict injectivity. Equal limit a use an embedded chart φ(t), finite-dimensional chart coordinates and normalized parameter secants w_n→w, giving numerator/coordinate-distance→||DG(a)Dφ(t)w||>0; denominator/coordinate-distance→||Dφ(t)w||>0. Contradiction. Upper secant bound follows local C1 control and compact separated pairs. Hence ambient-norm bi-Lipschitz on K. No convexity or smooth boundary of K is needed.

## Assumption obstruction and quantitative gap
Pair separation alone does not imply tangent separation: m(a,z)=a^3 z on M=R,Z=R is analytic and full-data injective but sampled map has zero derivative at0; inverse is only1/3-Hölder. This rules out unconditional Lipschitz upgrade of the current uniqueness theorem.

Even with full injectivity and immersion, no dimension-only uniform stability: m_δ(a,z)=δ a z for δ>0,K=[−1,1] gives stability constant[δ(Σ z_i²)^1/2]^{-1}. Fix any sampling law; constants become arbitrarily bad as δ↓0. Uniform quantitative conclusions must charge forward differential scale, covariance/input energy, model geometry and normalized secant complexity. Gaussian covariance positivity alone does not provide a fixed lower scale in all physical directions.

Concrete downstream use: audit noise-amplification in low-dimensional PDE priors and design certified conditioning thresholds rather than market sample count as stable recovery.

A mere quantile-existence argument follows from positivity almost surely and is not new quantitative theory.
