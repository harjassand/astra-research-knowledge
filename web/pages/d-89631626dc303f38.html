STRONGEST CLAIM AND STATUS
A complete candidate derivation and dependency-free exact implementation give
an acquired rational Foster/boundary-recovery certificate for the precisely
admitted open bimolecular class below. It has a finite total bit/runtime bound,
not an unbounded enumeration promise. The certificate is valid at EVERY count
state, including disabled-reactant boundary states, uniformly over predictable
history-dependent controls in a supplied positive rational rate box. It proves
nonexplosion and a computable finite expected hitting time to counts at least q
of every species. Fixed time-homogeneous rates have one positive recurrent
closed communicating class containing zero. These are internal derivations,
not independently reviewed or formally machine-proved results.

This does not recognize all bimolecular endotactic networks. A detailed-balanced
bimolecular counterexample below is positively recurrent yet is rejected by the
compiler. LP/Foster tests and support-exhaustion constructions have substantial
published prior art. The present concrete contribution is their exact rational,
fully costed, robust boundary-path implementation, including a hybrid diagonal
quadratic admission test. Novelty of this packaging/bound is UNKNOWN. A general
endotactic stochastic recovery theorem and field-breaking advance remain OPEN.

INPUT AND ADMITTED CLASS
Input consists of d>=1 species, R>=1 integer reactions y_r -> y'_r with
|y_r|_1,|y'_r|_1 <=2, supplied rational bounds 0<l_r<=u_r, and integer q>=1.
The propensity is exactly k_r(t,past) (x)_under_y_r, with
(x)_under_y=product_i x_i!/(x_i-y_i)! if x>=y, otherwise zero.
Coefficients k_r may be any predictable processes taking values in their boxes.
There is no point-estimate, real-number oracle, stationary-distribution oracle,
positive-orthant surrogate, fitted volume limit, or supplied Lyapunov weight.
Volume factors, if used, must first be included in the rational rate bounds.

The compiler checks three structural conditions and one Foster condition:
(A) The complex reaction graph is weakly reversible, checked by reachability.
This places the class inside endotactic networks. Weak reversibility is not
used by the stochastic proof once the following acquired certificates exist.
(B) In the graph of unary reactions e_i -> e_j or e_i ->0, every species has a
directed path to0. Acquire a simple path of length <=d for each species.
(C) Starting with no produced species, repeatedly add a species occurring in
the product of a reaction all of whose reactant species were already produced.
The resulting support closure must contain every species. Acquire one such
reaction per species, ordered acyclically by its production dependencies.
(D) For some partition S,T of species and w_i>=1, write
 c_y(w,k)=sum_{r:y_r=y} k_r w.(y'_r-y_r).
For all box rates, require:
  c_e_i <=-1 for i in T;
  c_2e_i <=-1 for i in S, and c_2e_i <=0 for i in T;
  c_(e_i+e_j) <=0 for i!=j.
No restriction is imposed on first-order source coefficients for i in S.
If a required source is absent, its coefficient is0; a required <=-1 test then
fails. This is an explicitly defined sufficient class, not a claim that all
endotactic systems satisfy (B)-(D). Strict negative margins of an unnormalized
positive weight can be scaled to these normalized inequalities.

EXACT SYNTHESIS, SOUNDNESS, AND TERMINATION IN THIS CLASS
For each of the 2^d partitions S,T, each source-wise rate-box vertex gives a
rational linear inequality in w. Affinity in the independent rates makes these
vertex inequalities equivalent to the full-box conditions. Add w_i>=1. The
resulting rational polyhedron lies in the positive orthant and has no line.
If it is nonempty it has a vertex: minimize sum_i w_i below any feasible sum;
the resulting nonempty minimizing face is compact, and a vertex of this face
is a vertex of the original polyhedron. At a vertex d active constraints have
independent normals. Enumerate every d-row basis, solve it exactly, and check
all inequalities. Thus synthesis terminates and succeeds exactly when (D)
holds. A failed search rejects this certificate class only.

The implementation uses fractions.Fraction and exact Gaussian elimination.
It first checks the all-one weight as a verified candidate, then exhausts bases
if needed. It neither invokes a floating LP solver nor assumes efficient CAD.
The acyclic support grammar and unary drain paths are acquired from the input.

GLOBAL FOSTER INEQUALITY — EXACT AT THE BOUNDARY
Set W(x)=w.x, N(x)=sum_i x_i and wmax=max_i w_i. For any acquired w,S,T, compute
 b0=sum_{r:y_r=0} u_r w.y'_r >=0,
 c_i=max_box c_e_i(w,k) for i in S,
 g_i=max(0,c_i+2),
 B=b0+(1/4)sum_{i in S}g_i^2.
All these quantities are exact rationals. The generator satisfies
                L_t W(x) <= B-N(x) <= B-W(x)/wmax.             (F)
Indeed, source e_i contributes at most -x_i for i in T. Sources 2e_i for i in T
and every mixed quadratic source contribute nonpositively because their exact
falling factorials are nonnegative. For i in S, the first/diagonal pair is at
most c_i x_i-x_i(x_i-1) =-x_i^2+(c_i+1)x_i. For every x_i>=0,
 -x_i^2+(c_i+1)x_i <= -x_i + g_i^2/4,
by completing the square in -x_i^2+(c_i+2)x_i; if c_i+2<0 its maximum on the
nonnegative half-line is0. Summing proves (F), including x_i=0 or1. No molecular
Taylor error or deterministic concentration assumption enters this identity.

Nonexplosion follows directly rather than from deterministic permanence. Stop
at first W>=m. Since L W<=B and W>=0, the stopped Dynkin bound gives
 P(reach W>=m before t) <=(W(x)+Bt)/m.
A bounded W region contains finitely many count states, each with a uniformly
bounded total intensity, so infinitely many jumps there in finite time are
impossible. Letting m increase establishes nonexplosion for all admitted
predictable controls. The same argument gives E W(X_t)<=W(x)+Bt.

Define Rw=2 wmax(B+1), K={W<=Rw}, and Rn=ceil(Rw). Outside K, (F) is at most -1.
Stopping Dynkin at K, large W and time t gives E(tau_K stopped)<=W(x), since
its terminal W is nonnegative. Nonexplosion and Fatou yield
                         E_x tau_K <= W(x).                  (R)
The argument is conditional on every current history, hence may be restarted
under non-Markov controls.

BOUNDED GLOBAL BOUNDARY RECOVERY — NO SUPPLIED PATH OR BASIN
Every molecule in a state x in K can be sent along its acquired unary drain
path. Suppress competing firings for this witness path. The total count never
increases, and at most d N(x)<=d Rn reactions suffice to reach zero. Molecules
need not be physically labeled: a desired unary reaction is enabled whenever
at least one molecule of its source remains.

For a newly acquired species i, define Produce(i) recursively: manufacture one
extra molecule for every occurrence in the selected reaction's reactant, using
recipes of earlier species, then fire that reaction. Inductively, this word is
enabled from EVERY initial x and ends at a state >=x+e_i. The extra reactants
cancel the consumed counts and the selected product contains the new species.
Source size<=2 gives L_i<=1+2 max(previous L), hence L_i<=2^i-1 in acquisition
order. Repeating Produce(i) q times for every species gives an acquired word
of length A<=q(2^(d+1)-d-2), ending at a state >=q in every coordinate.
The script uses the actual word length A when it is smaller.

Let G_q={x_i>=q for all i}. For q=2 every admitted complex is enabled in G_q.
Combine the drain and production witnesses, and define exact rational data
 H=d Rn+A, D=max(Rn,2A), U=sum_r u_r, Q=U(D+1)^2,
 lmin=min_r l_r, alpha=lmin/(2Q), p=alpha^H, t0=H/Q.
Every state along the witness has N<=D. Each enabled desired firing has rate
at least lmin and total competing rate at most Q, because 0th/1st/2nd-order
factorials are <=(D+1)^2. The probability that the next event is the desired
reaction and occurs within1/Q is at least
 lmin integral_0^(1/Q) exp(-Qs) ds >= lmin/(2Q)=alpha.
This bound is valid after conditioning on every past history; upper and lower
hazard bounds persist under arbitrary admissible predictable controls.
The witness has <=H steps, so from every state in K,
                         P(tau_G_q<=t0)>=p.                  (P)
This is a bound on an actual molecular path, not just graph connectivity.

Attempt recovery for t0 time units from K. On a failed attempt, wait to return
to K and retry. Its unconditional expected return cost is at most the expected
W at the end of the attempt, at most Rw+B t0 by the stopped drift bound. Thus
every attempted cycle has expected cost at most Rw+(B+1)t0. Conditional failure
probability is <=1-p, so summing the geometric tail and using (R) proves
       E_x tau_G_q <= W(x) + [Rw+(B+1)t0]/p.                  (HIT)
Every number in the displayed bound is acquired and rational. The potentially
very small p is retained explicitly; this is not a polynomial physical-time
claim or an uncharged rare-event shortcut.

For fixed time-homogeneous rates, the same attempt argument using only the
drain word proves a finite expected hitting time to0 from every count state.
A return to0 after departure has finite expected duration: its initial holding
time is finite since support production requires a positive zero-source rate,
and any first jump has W<=2wmax. All states reachable from0 communicate back
to0 by the drain word and form a closed class Gamma. No different closed class
can exist because every count state has a path to0. Finite mean return to0
therefore gives positive recurrence of Gamma and a unique stationary law there.
This conclusion does not assign invariant distributions to history-dependent
controllers.

TOTAL BIT/RUNTIME BOUND AND CHARGED COSTS
Let b bound numerator/denominator bit lengths of every input rate endpoint,
and let L be the ordinary dense binary input length including q. Integer
complex entries lie in {0,1,2}. After clearing source-wise denominators, every
inequality has integer coefficient/RHS height at most 2^h, where a sufficient
 h=R b+ceil(log2(2R+1))+1.
There are at most C=3d+R 2^R inequalities per partition. Hadamard/Cramer's rule
bounds every numerator/denominator of a feasible vertex weight by 2^t for
 t=d h+ceil((d/2)log2(max(d,1)))+2.
Exact basis enumeration costs at most 2^d binom(C,d) times a polynomial in
C,d,h,t in ordinary bit operations. This is a finite worst-case algorithm,
not the unimplemented general polynomial LP claim from a numerical solver.

The compact weight, B, Rw,Rn,H,D,Q,alpha, grammar and drain graph have polynomial
binary description size in L. Actual expanded production words have length
at most q 2^(d+1), so expansion is already potentially exponential in binary q.
The numeric core radius Rn is at most 2^poly(L), and H is too. Expanding the
rational p=alpha^H and the recovery constant requires at most 2^poly(L) output
bits; exponentiation and exact arithmetic can be performed within that same
singly exponential type of bound. A conservative combined bound for all
synthesis, word expansion, rational output and checking is 2^O(L^3) bit
operations and bits. No general claim of polynomial total runtime is made.
A compact certificate may retain (alpha,H) as a rational-power expression;
its exact expanded value is computable within the stated bound.

Physical use has a different cost: the finite expected recovery bound can be
astronomically large. The algorithm does not execute that recovery, simulate
all firings, establish small stationary boundary mass, or price fuel/control
energy. Evaluation of actual propensities, volume factors, count encoding and
reaction firings are charged separately. Tokens, backend compute, energy and
end-to-end research CPU are UNKNOWN; no paid job or download was used.

PRECISE REJECTION / FRONTIER OBSTRUCTION
All rates1 in 0<->A,0<->B,A<->A+B,B<->A+B give a strongly connected bimolecular
weakly reversible graph. Every reversible edge is detailed-balanced by means
(1,1): for pi(a,b)=e^-2/(a!b!), direct neighboring-state balance holds for
immigration/death and for the birth-B rate a versus reverse rate a(b+1), and
symmetrically for A. The normalized product-Poisson law and the published
nonexplosion theorem imply positive recurrence. Equivalently its elementary
reversible stationary construction already shows the ordinary stable case.
Yet no compiler certificate exists: no diagonal 2A or2B source permits S to
be nonempty, and for S empty the first-source inequalities require
 -w_A+w_B<=-1 and w_A-w_B<=-1, a contradiction. Its total-count drift is
2-2ab and remains positive along the zero-species axes. This is the missing
multistep boundary mechanism. Failure of our certificate is not recurrence
failure, and the compiler does not report it as such.
