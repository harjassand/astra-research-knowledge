1. EXACT STATEMENT

Work in arbitrary finite dimension. Let sigma>0, Tr sigma=1. Let Phi and
Psi be sigma-preserving CPTP channels, KMS reversible, and commuting with
every U_t(X)=sigma^(it)Xsigma^(-it). Suppose, on the FULL KMS Hilbert space,
 I-Psi* <= C(I-Phi*), C>=0,
where <A,B>_sigma=Tr sigma^(1/2) A* sigma^(1/2) B.
Then, for every density rho and this SAME Psi,
 T(Psi(rho),rho)^3 <= (27/4) C D(rho||sigma) T(Phi(rho),rho).
T is HALF trace norm and D uses natural logarithms. The constant is
independent of dimension, sigma_min, spectrum, and ensemble size. EB is
not needed for the inequality, but is preserved if supplied. No primitivity
or fixed-point-algebra assumption is needed.

KMS reversibility PLUS modular covariance is the usual GNS detailed-balance
class. KMS reversibility alone is not the stronger hypothesis used here.

2. MODULAR-FREQUENCY KRAUS PAIRS

For a modular-covariant CP channel Lambda, the Choi operator splits into
modular-frequency blocks. Diagonalizing those positive blocks supplies
Kraus operators K_j with sigma K_j = mu_j K_j sigma, mu_j>0.
For KMS-reversible Lambda its Petz reverse equals itself. The reverse
Kraus of K_j is K_j#=sigma^(1/2)K_j* sigma^(-1/2)=mu_j^(-1/2)K_j*.
Replace an arbitrary frequency Kraus family by the union of its family
and its reverse, each scaled by 1/sqrt(2). The map remains Lambda, and
this new family is closed under #, with the partner frequency mu_j^(-1).
Always sum over this entire paired family. Since Lambda is TP,
 sum_j K_j* K_j = I.

Its Hilbert-Schmidt KMS implementation is
 T_Lambda(X)=sigma^(-1/4)Lambda(sigma^(1/4)Xsigma^(1/4))sigma^(-1/4)
            =sum_j mu_j^(-1/2) K_j X K_j*
            =sum_j mu_j^(-1/2) K_j* X K_j.
The last equality follows by replacing every j by its # partner. It is
selfadjoint, CP, fixes sigma^(1/2), and is a Hilbert-space contraction.

3. ENTROPY PRODUCTION CONTROLS THE CORRECT ROOT ENERGY

For a faithful rho diagonalized as sum_i r_i|i><i|, define
 I_Phi(rho)=Tr[(rho-Phi(rho))(log rho-log sigma)].
The modular relation gives
 K_j* (log sigma) K_j=K_j* K_j log sigma+(ln mu_j)K_j* K_j.
Consequently
 I_Phi(rho)=sum_(j,i,k) r_i |(K_j)_(k i)|^2 ln[r_i/(mu_j^(-1)r_k)]
 =(1/2)sum_(j,i,k)|(K_j)_(k i)|^2
       (r_i-mu_j^(-1)r_k) ln[r_i/(mu_j^(-1)r_k)].
The second line uses paired Kraus indices, i/k reversal, and mu reversal.
The scalar inequality (a-b)ln(a/b)>=4(sqrt(a)-sqrt(b))^2 yields
 I_Phi(rho)>=2sum_(j,i,k)|(K_j)_(k i)|^2
                  (sqrt(r_i)-mu_j^(-1/2)sqrt(r_k))^2.

Let q=sqrt(rho), Q_rho=sigma^(-1/4)q sigma^(-1/4). With both paired sums
of r_i and mu_j^(-1)r_k equal to Tr rho=1, the same expansion gives
 E_Phi(Q_rho)=<Q_rho,(I-Phi*)Q_rho>_sigma
 =Tr q[q-T_Phi(q)]
 =(1/2)sum_(j,i,k)|(K_j)_(k i)|^2
                   (sqrt(r_i)-mu_j^(-1/2)sqrt(r_k))^2.
Thus I_Phi(rho)>=4 E_Phi(Q_rho), proved here with modular Kraus algebra.
This is not asserted for general non-modular KMS channels.

4. MODULAR COMPARATOR ENERGY CONTROLS ITS PHYSICAL TRACE DISTURBANCE

For the Psi paired Kraus family, put A_j=K_j and
B_j=mu_j^(-1/2)q K_j. Operator Cauchy-Schwarz and TP give
 (sum_j A_j*B_j)*(sum_j A_j*B_j)
 <= sum_j B_j*B_j
 =sum_j mu_j^(-1)K_j* rho K_j = Psi(rho),
again using the # pairing in the last equality. Since sum A_j*B_j=T_Psi(q)
is positive, this proves T_Psi(q)^2<=Psi(rho). Square-root monotonicity
then gives sqrt(Psi(rho))>=T_Psi(q).

Write S=Tr[q T_Psi(q)] in [0,1]. Root fidelity obeys
 F(rho,Psi(rho))=||q sqrt(Psi(rho))||_1 >= Tr[q sqrt(Psi(rho))]>=S.
The standard fidelity/half-trace inequality T^2<=1-F^2 therefore gives
 T(Psi(rho),rho)^2<=1-S^2<=2(1-S)=2 E_Psi(Q_rho).
The comparator order now gives this <=2C E_Phi(Q_rho).
The modular hypothesis of Psi was used in the paired scalar-frequency
Kraus Cauchy-Schwarz step; the independent canonical qubit witness shows
why its operator Jensen conclusion cannot be assumed for general Psi.

5. DISSIPATIVE POISSON ORBIT AND THE CONSTANT

Let P_s=exp[s(Phi-I)] be the Schrödinger Poisson semigroup and rho_s=P_s rho.
It is CPTP, sigma-preserving, KMS reversible and modular covariant. Its
derivative satisfies
 -d/ds D(rho_s||sigma)=I_Phi(rho_s)>=4 E_Phi(Q_(rho_s)).
Thus, for every t>0, some s in [0,t] has
 E_Phi(Q_(rho_s))<=D(rho||sigma)/(4t).
Since ||P_u(Phi(rho)-rho)||_1<=||Phi(rho)-rho||_1,
 T(rho_s,rho)<=s epsilon<=t epsilon, epsilon=T(Phi(rho),rho).
The SAME Psi remains contractive in trace norm, so with e=T(Psi(rho),rho),
 e<=T(Psi(rho_s),rho_s)+2T(rho_s,rho)
  <=sqrt[C D(rho||sigma)/(2t)]+2t epsilon.
For positive CD and epsilon, minimize a t^(-1/2)+b t with
a=sqrt(CD/2), b=2epsilon. Its minimum cubed is 27a^2 b/4=(27/4)CD epsilon.
If epsilon=0 let t increase to infinity; if D=0, rho=sigma. The zero-C
case follows directly from the comparator order. No logarithmic floor
or sigma_min estimate is used.

All displayed entropy identities can first be applied to
rho_eta=(1-eta)rho+eta sigma, which remains faithful throughout its orbit.
Then let eta decrease to zero. Finite-dimensional entropy continuity,
T-residual continuity, and sigma>0 give the same statement for every rho.

6. DISTINCT EXISTENCE COROLLARY: ONLY PHI NEEDS TO BE MODULAR

Suppose Phi is modular covariant and a supplied sigma-Petz canonical EB
Psi has the full KMS order above, but Psi need not be modular. Let G be the
compact closure in the unitary group of {sigma^(it):t real}. Every g in G
commutes with sigma and Phi commutes with Ad_g. Conjugation is unitary for
the KMS inner product. Therefore
 Psi_g=Ad_g^(-1) Psi Ad_g
is sigma-Petz canonical EB, KMS reversible, sigma-preserving, and obeys
 I-Psi_g*<=C(I-Phi*).
Its Haar average Psi_bar has all these properties and commutes with G.
Section 1 consequently applies to THIS ONE selected averaged comparator:
 T(Psi_bar(rho),rho)^3 <= (27/4) C D(rho||sigma) T(Phi(rho),rho).
This is an existence corollary. It does not prove the same bound for the
original unaveraged Psi, and it does not remove modular covariance of Phi.

To justify exact finite canonical implementation after a continuous
group average, choose a real orthonormal Hilbert-Schmidt basis of the
Hermitian matrices, of dimension n=d^2. For pure pi let
 G_pi=|pi><pi| be its real symmetric rank-one Gram operator. Its ordinary
superoperator trace is Tr(pi^2)=1. The compact pure-state set generates a
compact convex body of such Gram operators, of affine dimension at most
n(n+1)/2-1. The canonical map is determined by
 Gamma_sigma Psi* = sum_i p_i G_(pi_i),
and applying this Gram operator to I gives sum_i p_i pi_i=sigma.
Conjugates and their Haar average stay in this compact convex body.
Caratheodory therefore represents the averaged Gram map by at most
n(n+1)/2=d^2(d^2+1)/2 pure atoms, with exactly the same barycenter sigma.
Those atoms implement Psi_bar exactly. This does not assert a finite
exact quadrature for Haar measure or the group orbit itself.
