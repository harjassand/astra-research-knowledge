## Work completed

- Reconstructed the homogeneous A-homeostatic scalar reduction and the classical KPP tail-selection branch. For the source notebook seed, `q0=.005333 μm⁻¹`, `q*=.061637 μm⁻¹`, and `q0/q*=.0865`. The scalar prediction is a comparator only; no novelty is claimed for Fisher–KPP theory.
- Implemented the notebook’s centered-space/forward-Euler solver and tridiagonal viscous-force solve in `replay_coupled_fd.py`. At `Nx=200,Nt=100`, it reproduces the published `φ`, `ρ`, and `V` arrays to roundoff (RMSE `1.81e−15`, `1.27e−17`, `4.81e−14`). Base-profile OLS front speed is stable through refinement to `Nx=3200,Nt=25600` (`21.73529` to `21.73416 μm/h`); the secant stabilizes at about `21.775 μm/h`.
- Replayed the source notebook, Rmd, and interpolated-level front definitions. On the public finite output they yield `18.75`, `20.588`, `21.735` μm/h OLS and `21.774` μm/h secant. The notebook residual is a signed-mass cancellation metric, not L1 shape error.
- Recomputed the source scalar comparison with matched secant and time-integrated local-flow estimands. For the public seed, KPP shallow-tail plus sampled Eulerian drift predicts `21.685 μm/h`, only `0.090 μm/h` below the `21.775` secant. Matched steepness sweeps show close conditional agreement through `q0≈q*`, with larger discrepancy at very steep profiles.
- Constructed a non-identifiability pair in the **full nonlinear simulator**: `(aρ=aφ=4, α=3.5e−6)` and `(aρ=aφ=8, α=1.934e−6)` yield secants `21.77502` and `21.77423 μm/h` at `Nx=3200,Nt=25600`; `α` differs about 45%, but sampled local velocity differs `10.923` versus `17.998 μm/h`. This blocks speed-only inversion across the family, while showing which additional observables can distinguish the pair. It does not refute the paper’s joint fit under its fixed initial profile.
- Examined extreme matched profile steepness. The 0–8 h mean front rate relative to local flow changes sign between `aφ=115` and `116`; the 6–8 h sign changes earlier. This is not a robust time-independent threshold and its 6.5 μm length is below density-derived cell spacing and below the published mesh. It is recorded as a stop rule for continuum-profile control claims.

## Corrections retained

- An earlier comparison subtracted unweighted mean `V` from an OLS front slope and therefore suggested negative material-frame motion too soon. Steep trajectories accelerate; that mixed-estimand conclusion was withdrawn. All reported material-frame rates now use a front secant minus the time-integral of local velocity on the same interval.
- An initial sweep changed only `aφ` while holding density steepness `aρ=4`. It changed the model’s stiffness/density alignment and was not used as the main intervention analogue. Final sweeps keep `aρ=aφ`, as in the source initialization.
- After correcting estimands, the scalar KPP comparator is close to the exact full model over moderate tested steepness. The report no longer claims it fails by several μm/h in those cases.
