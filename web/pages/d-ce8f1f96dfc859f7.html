1. SAME-MAP, FAITHFUL-ORBIT AND ENTROPY INTEGRATION AUDIT (SOURCE LINES 4-32)

The hypotheses are finite-dimensional CPTP Phi with Phi(sigma)=sigma for
a faithful density sigma, ONE fixed CPTP Psi, and a finite A>=0 with

  e(x)^2<=A J(x) for EVERY faithful density x,
  e(x)=||x-Psi(x)||_1/2,
  J(x)=Tr[(x-Phi(x))(log x-log sigma)].

The global state hypothesis is essential: an inequality only at the
original rho, or maps Psi_x chosen independently for different states,
does not support the time integral. The same actual Psi acts throughout.

The Poisson expansion P_t=e^(-t)sum_n t^n Phi^n/n! is CPTP and fixes sigma.
For faithful rho and finite t, P_t(rho)>=e^(-t)rho>0, so every finite orbit
state is legal for the hypothesis. The orbit is differentiable with
rho_t'=Phi(rho_t)-rho_t. The finite faithful trace-entropy derivative and
Tr rho_t'=0 give

  dD(rho_t||sigma)/dt=-J(rho_t).

Integrating only to a finite T first yields

  integral_0^T e(rho_t)^2 dt
      <=A[D(rho||sigma)-D(rho_T||sigma)]<=A D(rho||sigma).

Nonnegativity of genuine relative entropy is the only endpoint fact used.
For completeness, in a rho eigenbasis, scalar concavity gives
<i|log sigma|i><=log<i|sigma|i>, reducing D>=0 to classical Gibbs inequality.
No ergodicity, unique fixed point, positive entropy gap, convergence of
rho_t, or interchange of an unbounded derivative/integral is assumed.
Take increasing T to infinity only for the nonnegative integral e^2.
The case A=0 is harmless: the hypothesis itself gives e=0 for every faithful
state. The hypothesis at sigma forces Psi(sigma)=sigma for all A>=0.

2. TRACE-LIPSCHITZ AND THE ENTIRE LOWER CONE

Phi-I commutes with P_s, since P_s is its exponential. The exact identity

  P_t(rho)-rho=integral_0^t P_s[Phi(rho)-rho] ds

and trace contraction on Hermitian differences imply

  T(P_t(rho),rho)<=t b, b=||rho-Phi(rho)||_1/2.

No per-orbit residual is substituted for the original b. CPTP Psi gives

  |e(x)-e(y)|
      <=(1/2)||(I-Psi)(x-y)||_1<=||x-y||_1=2T(x,y).

Thus e(rho_t)>=(e(rho)-2tb)_+. For b>0, changing variable u=e-2bt gives

  integral_0^infinity (e-2bt)_+^2 dt=e^3/(6b).

Combining this lower integral with the entropy upper integral proves
e^3<=6A D b. The coefficient 6 is the exact cone-area coefficient under
these two Lipschitz bounds. This is not a universal optimality claim.
Using a single time would not recover this full-cone improvement.

5. INDEPENDENT RECONSTRUCTION OF THE TRACIAL GATES (SOURCE LINES 46-61)

  E_Phi=Tr[sqrt(rho)(I-Phi)sqrt(rho)],
  E_Psi=Tr[sqrt(rho)(I-Psi)sqrt(rho)], sigma=I/d.

  Tr(A B)>=Tr[A Psi(A)]=1-E_Psi.

  e^2<=1-|Tr(A B)|^2<=2E_Psi-E_Psi^2<=2E_Psi.

For the entropy gate, diagonalize a faithful rho as diag r_i and put
P_ij=<i|Phi(|j><j|)|i>. CP, unitality and TP make P nonnegative doubly
stochastic; HS self-adjointness gives P_ij=P_ji. Direct expansion gives

  E_Phi=(1/2)sum_ij P_ij(sqrt r_i-sqrt r_j)^2.

(x-1)^2/[x(x+1)^2]>=0; exchange a,b for the other case. Therefore J>=4E_Phi.
