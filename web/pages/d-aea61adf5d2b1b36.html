EXACT THEOREM

Let A=direct_sum_j M_(d_j) be a finite-dimensional C*-algebra with faithful
normalized trace

 tau(A)=sum_j w_j Tr(A_j)/d_j,       w_j>0, sum_j w_j=1.

Let Phi:A->A be UCP, tau preserving and selfcompatible, supplied with an
actual two-output broadcaster for the constructive/existence argument.
There is NO assumption that Phi preserves individual blocks or its centre,
nor that it is positive/selfadjoint as a Hilbert-space operator.
For every m>=1 there is one UCP tau-preserving EB map E_m:A->A with

 ||Phi^m-E_m||_(infinity -> 1, Hermitian)
    <= epsilon_m
    :=sum_j w_j min{2, d_j^2 sqrt(2 ln(d_j)/2^m)}.           [C]

The d_j=1 term is defined as zero. The norm and EB status refer to the
likelihood representation. In physical coordinates the map is one genuine
full-domain measure-and-prepare channel after pinching the input into A.
It fixes the central reference sigma EXACTLY.

In particular if all irreducible input matrix blocks have d_j<=q, with q
fixed, then epsilon_m<=q^2 sqrt(2 ln(q)/2^m), uniformly over the number and
weights of blocks, the physical dimension and arbitrary block-changing
channel dynamics. This includes growing classical sector count. A sharper
weighted tail condition epsilon_m->0 also permits unbounded rare blocks.

The hypotheses concern the ACTUAL input algebra. One cannot arbitrarily
split a full M_d block into small fibres, erase its coherences, and apply
[C] as though that restriction were free. Supplied centralization/pinching
must carry its own approximation error. Nothing here acquires a bounded
block decomposition for an arbitrary full-matrix power range.

1. A FINITE-DIMENSION TRACE/MEASUREMENT LEMMA

For a Hermitian L on C^q tensor H_B define

 nu(L)=sup_(finite POVMs M on B) ||(id_R tensor M)(L)||_1.

Then, independent of dim(B),

 ||L||_1 <= q^2 nu(L).                                     [4]

Here ordinary, UNNORMALIZED Schatten trace norms are used. A short complete
proof avoids introducing a dimension-dependent IC POVM on B. Expand
L=sum_(a,b) |a><b| tensor L_ab, with L_ba=L_ab^*.
Triangle inequality gives ||L||_1<=sum_ab ||L_ab||_1.
For diagonal blocks, measuring B in an eigenbasis of L_aa and testing R
with |a><a| gives nu(L)>=||L_aa||_1.
For a!=b, the Hermitian contractions

 H_ab=|a><b|+|b><a|,
 K_ab=i(|a><b|-|b><a|)

on R access respectively L_ab+L_ba and i(L_ab-L_ba). For either Hermitian
block combination, a spectral measurement on B and its sign as a classical
output test realizes its trace norm. These product tests are among the
allowed tests after measuring B, so

 nu(L)>=max{||L_ab+L_ba||_1, ||L_ab-L_ba||_1}.

But

 ||L_ab||_1<= [||L_ab+L_ba||_1+||L_ab-L_ba||_1]/2<=nu(L).

Summing q^2 blocks proves [4]. This is the mechanism in the cited Lemma 5;
no stronger dimension-free conversion to full trace norm is assumed.

2. SYMMETRIC EXTENSION WITH SMALL QUANTUM INPUT

Consider a CPTP channel T:M_q->B(H_B) with an actual N-output extension
whose N marginals are ALL T. Symmetrize it over output permutations.
Its normalized Choi state is Omega_RB1...BN, with Omega_R=I/q and symmetry
under permutations of the Bs. I claim there is an EB channel F, preserving
T(I/q) EXACTLY, whose normalized Choi state J(F) obeys

 ||J(T)-J(F)||_1 <= q^2 sqrt(2 ln(q)/N).                    [5]

For q=1 T is itself EB. For q>1 all logs below are natural.

Build a sequence of measurements on distinct B receivers. Let Z_t be the
classical history after t measurements. On an unused receiver define

 G_t=sup_M I(R:X_M | Z_t),

where the choice of finite POVM M may depend on the classical history.
Remaining receivers are conditionally symmetric, so G_t does not depend
on which unused receiver is chosen. Choose at step t a measurement within
zeta>0 of the supremum. The classical chain rule gives

 I(R:Z_(t+1))-I(R:Z_t) >= G_t-zeta.

The history is classical; its Holevo information satisfies
I(R:Z_N)<=S(Omega_R)=ln q. Therefore

 sum_(t=0)^(N-1) G_t <= ln q + N zeta.

At least one t<=N-1 has G_t<=ln q/N+zeta. Fix that history channel and choose
one still unmeasured receiver B. For outcome z write its conditional state
Omega^z_RB and conditional marginals Omega^z_R, Omega^z_B. Define

 S_RB=sum_z p_z Omega^z_R tensor Omega^z_B.

This is separable. Nonselective measurements on OTHER receivers do not
change either R or B, hence its marginals are exactly

 S_R=I/q,        S_B=T(I/q).                               [6]

Thus it is the Choi state of a genuine EB CPTP channel, explicitly

 F(X)=q sum_z p_z Tr[(Omega^z_R)^T X] Omega^z_B.

The effects q p_z (Omega^z_R)^T sum to I, and every preparation has trace 1.
Equation [6] proves the exact restricted-reference preservation, not just
an error bound. This fact is useful in the weighted assembly below.

Let L=J(T)-S. For every fixed finite POVM M on the unused B, conditional
quantum Pinsker and the triangle/Cauchy--Schwarz inequalities give

 ||(id_R tensor M)(L)||_1
 <=sum_z p_z ||(id_R tensor M)
              (Omega^z_RB-Omega^z_R tensor Omega^z_B)||_1
 <=sqrt(2 I(R:X_M|Z_t))
 <=sqrt(2 G_t).

Quantum Pinsker uses D(rho||sigma)>=||rho-sigma||_1^2/2 in natural logs.
The fixed POVM is permitted in the history-adaptive supremum; no measurement
maximization has been moved inside an average to claim a trace norm.
We first take the supremum over M in this measured norm, THEN apply the
explicit q^2 conversion [4]. This yields

 ||L||_1<=q^2 sqrt(2ln q/N+2zeta).

Let zeta down to zero. Finite-dimensional separable Choi states with the
fixed R and B marginals form a compact set, so a convergent subsequence
retains EB, trace preservation and [6]. This proves [5]. Finite-history
POVMs suffice at each approximating stage; the compactness step establishes
existence, not an efficient measurement-search algorithm.

This is a standard measured-information mechanism specialized to identical
receiver marginals. Compared with the arbitrary-recipient result, no bad
fraction of already measured recipients need be paid: one unused recipient
with the common original marginal is enough. All q dependence is visible.
An ordinary diamond conclusion would cost another q via Choi-to-diamond;
[C] needs only product tests and does NOT pay that extra factor.

3. ASSEMBLY OF INPUT BLOCKS AND POWERS

A selfcompatibilizing binary broadcaster can be applied independently at
every leaf of a depth-m binary tree. It is CPTP and has N=2^m output
marginals, each exactly Phi_phys^m. Average output permutations for symmetry.
This is an actual legal joint extension, not N independent copies. Its
input/output reference marginals are the supplied sigma.

Let i_j:M_(d_j)->B(H) inject input states into central block j and set

 T_j=Phi_phys^m composed with i_j.

Restricting the binary-tree extension to this input block gives N identical
marginals T_j. Apply Section 2 with q=d_j. If preparations are not already
in A, pinch each output into A; this preserves the actual T_j outputs,
never increases trace error and keeps EB. Obtain F_j with

 ||J(T_j)-J(F_j)||_1<=d_j^2 sqrt(2ln d_j/N),
 F_j(I/d_j)=T_j(I/d_j).

For terms where that bound exceeds 2, use instead the constant EB channel
X ->Tr(X) T_j(I/d_j). Its normalized Choi trace distance is at most 2 and
it has the SAME restricted-reference preservation. For d_j=1 use T_j
exactly. These choices explain every term of epsilon_m.

Define one global physical EB map by measuring the central input block and
using its F_j:

 E_phys(rho)=sum_j F_j(P_j rho P_j).

All dependence on the input, including the block label, is counted in the
classical measurement record. This is a full-domain EB CPTP map. Moreover

 E_phys(sigma)=sum_j w_j F_j(I/d_j)
              =sum_j w_j T_j(I/d_j)
              =Phi_phys^m(sigma)=sigma.                   [7]

Consequently the likelihood map E_m is both UCP and tau preserving.

For a Hermitian A in A with ||A||<=1 and an arbitrary Hermitian output
contraction B in A, the normalized block-Choi identity gives

 Tr[B (T_j-F_j)(A_j/d_j)]
       =Tr[(J(T_j)-J(F_j))(A_j^T tensor B)].

The product test has operator norm <=1. The physical reference-weighted
input is sigma A=sum_j w_j i_j(A_j/d_j), so by triangle inequality,

 |tau[B (Phi^m-E_m)(A)]| <=sum_j w_j min{2,d_j^2 sqrt(2ln d_j/N)}.

Centrality identifies physical trace duality with normalized L1(tau).
Take the supremum over B and A to obtain [C]. No max over blocks, no number
of input labels, and no total Hilbert dimension appears in the bound.
This completes the restricted lifting derivation.

For likelihoods I+H with ||H||<=theta<1, tau(H)=0, and common marginal

 ||Phi^m(H)-H||_1<=m r.

Because E_m fixes sigma exactly, [C] applies just to H, giving

 sup_H T(E_phys(rho_H),rho_H)
       <=(theta epsilon_m + m r)/2.                       [8]

For fixed maximum block size q, choose m tending to infinity with m r->0;

6. AUXILIARY EXPLICIT EB REFERENCE BALANCING LEMMA

This independently derived tool is NOT needed to pay a loss in [C], since
[7] already fixes the reference. It is useful when another EB approximant
F is merely tau preserving. Let S=F(I)>=0, tau(S)=1, and

 delta=||S-I||_1,       R=min(I,S^(-1/2)),
 Delta=I-R S R=(I-S)_+, t=tau(Delta)=delta/2,
 D=I-F^*(R^2)>=0.

Define on the kernel of S the harmless R=I. Because F is trace preserving,
F^*(I)=I, so D>=0; also tau(D)=t. If t>0 put

 F_bal(A)=R F(A) R + tau(D A) Delta/t.                     [9]

This is CP, EB, tau preserving and unital, exactly. CP/EB follows by
filtering each old preparation and adding the positive effect D and
preparation Delta/t. Trace preservation follows from the defining D.
Unitality is R S R+tau(D)Delta/t=I. For t=0 F already is unital.

For a Hermitian contraction A, positivity gives -S<=F(A)<=S, hence
F(A)=S^(1/2) X S^(1/2) for a Hermitian contraction X on supp(S).
Expanding the difference into its two filter terms and using tracial
Holder gives

 ||F(A)-R F(A)R||_1
    <=2 sqrt(tau[S(I-R)^2]) <=2sqrt(t).

The added rank-one map has norm <=tau(D)=t. Therefore

 ||F_bal-F||_(infinity->1)<=2sqrt(delta/2)+delta/2.          [10]

It preserves the actual central weighted reference physically. Finite-
precision spectral thresholding and preparation acquisition remain extra
costs. Ten small random EB fixtures in d=2..6, forty contraction probes each,
passed the implementation transcription checks. Maximum unital error
6.76e-16; maximum trace-preservation error 4.47e-16. No global search or
independent theorem verification was inferred from these fixtures.
