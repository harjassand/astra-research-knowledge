A PUBLIC DECODER WITH BOUNDED CRITICAL OUTPUT ENERGY
2026-10-08. INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; external validity/priority UNKNOWN.
Separate refinement of archive.txt, whose simpler K^* decoder stays banked.
Scope here adds a supplied finite upper coupling g<=g_+.

1. EXACT CLAIM AND ITS LIMIT

For public J,tau,B,0<g_-<=g_+<infinity and 0<epsilon<=1, there is a
parameter-blind archive for every L, unknown |lambda|<=B and
g in [g_-,g_+], with trace error <=epsilon and

  q(epsilon)=O((1+log(1/epsilon))^(3/2))

stored qubits. Its decoded state has uniformly bounded positive critical
energy tau L^2(Hcrit+(g/L)W), at fixed epsilon, uniformly in L and all
allowed unknown parameters. This removes the contact-jump UV divergence
of the K^* decoder.

It does NOT prove that decoded critical energy expectation differs from
the input by o(1) as epsilon decreases. Energy expectation accuracy and
optimal setup/gate cost remain separate gates. No LL continuum theorem
is assumed. The public upper g_+ is new and necessary for the energy
bound furnished by this construction; the original trace-only theorem
continues to allow all g>=g_-.

2. A LOW-MODE INVERSE AND ITS GRAM MATRIX

Fix m<=r and n=L-m. Let J_{M,m,n}, denoted Jm below, be the isometric
embedding of the symmetric m-particle low-sine-mode archive into the full
symmetric cube, with labels <=min(M,n). K_m from archive.txt is invertible
onto that full symmetric space. Define

  A_m=K_m^{-1}Jm,
  G_m=A_m^*A_m,
  V_m=A_m G_m^{-1/2}.                                  (2.1)

V_m is an exact isometry to the original spin m-sector. Both its amplitude
and Gram matrix are public, depending only on L,m,M, not lambda,g.
In ordered coordinates A_m samples the symmetric low-mode vector F as

  (A_m F)(x)=sqrt(m!)F(y_1,...,y_m), y_i=x_i-i+1,        (2.2)

including at contacts. Thus the contact amplitude jump of K_m^* is absent.

If r_v are multiplicities in a labeled cube tuple z, then

  <F,(G_m-I)F>
    =sum_z (prod_v r_v! -1)|F(z)|^2 >=0.                (2.3)

For each coordinate pair i<j restricted to one-particle labels <=M,

  1_{z_i=z_j} <= [2M/(n+1)] I                           (2.4)

as a form on that low-mode space. To see this, fix all coordinates other
than z_i, including z_j=v. Evaluation of a low-mode one-variable function
at v has squared norm <=sum_{k<=M}|phi_k(v)|^2 times its squared norm;
|phi_k(v)|<=sqrt(2/(n+1)). Sum over the remaining coordinates. If M>n,
replace M by n; the stated upper bound remains valid.

The coefficient in (2.3) vanishes unless some coordinates coincide and
is at most m!-1. A collision indicator is at most the sum of all pair
collision indicators. Consequently

  0<=G_m-I<=eta_m I,
  eta_m=(m!-1)m(m-1)M/(n+1).                            (2.5)

For m=0 or 1 the Gram matrix is exactly I. At L>=2r, uniformly for m<=r,

  eta_m<=eta:=2(r!-1)r(r-1)M/L.                         (2.6)

The matrix inverse square root is therefore well-conditioned: its spectrum
lies in [(1+eta)^(-1/2),1]. Its acquisition cost is still charged below.

3. TRACE ERROR WHEN THE DECODER IS REPLACED

Put B_m=K_m^*Jm, the successful old decoder. The identities
A_m^*B_m=I, B_m^*B_m=Jm^*K_m K_m^*Jm<=I imply

  ||A_m-B_m||^2<=||G_m-I||<=eta,
  ||V_m-A_m||<=sqrt(1+eta)-1<=eta/2.

Thus ||V_m-B_m||<=sqrt(eta)+eta/2. Moreover

  0<=I-B_m^*B_m<=G_m-I<=eta I.                         (3.1)

Indeed for the positive contraction S=K_m K_m^*,
S^{-1}-I>=I-S, and compression by Jm is (3.1).

On an arbitrary positive archive signal of trace <=1, replacing the
old completed B_m decoder by the isometric V_m decoder changes the output
in trace norm by at most

  2||V_m-B_m||+||I-B_m^*B_m|| <=2sqrt(eta)+2eta.          (3.2)

The first term bounds the difference of successful states by factoring
V sigma V^*-B sigma B^*. The second bounds the old missing-trace vacuum
completion. The same estimate holds sectorwise/direct-sum, and for an
additional reference system. The encoder remains the public Jm^*K_m
contraction plus overflow. The overflow is decoded to vacuum.

Use archive.txt with analytic tolerance epsilon/2, so its r,M,L0 are
chosen using epsilon/2. Enlarge the large-L threshold to

  L1>=max{L0,2r,128(r!-1)r(r-1)M/epsilon^2}.             (3.3)

Then eta<=epsilon^2/64 and (3.2)<=epsilon/4+epsilon^2/32.
Together with the old epsilon/2 error this is <=25epsilon/32, leaving
implementation margin. For L<L1 use the same number-only storage branch.
Its error remains <=2t_r. No unknown parameter is estimated.

The new small-L storage dimension is bounded by
1+sum_{m<=min(r,L1-2)}binom(L1-2,m). Since

  log L1=O(log(1/epsilon)+r log r),
  r=O(sqrt(log(1/epsilon))), M=O(epsilon^-1),

we still obtain q=O((1+log(1/epsilon))^(3/2)) at fixed public constants.
The factorial threshold is disclosed; it is subdominant in log L1 but
need not be a benign practical constant.

4. UNIFORM CRITICAL OUTPUT ENERGY

The edge-count comparison in archive.txt also gives the reverse estimate
for the inverse sampling A_m, at the cost of m!:

  A_m^*Hcrit,m A_m <=m! Jm^*H^free_{n,m}Jm.             (4.1)

Each chamber edge/endpoint has at least one labeled preimage. In A_m
the chamber coefficient is m! times the labeled function's squared
difference; multiplying the full labeled-cube form by m! covers it.
The largest low one-particle eigenvalue is bounded by
2Jpi^2 M^2/(n+1)^2. For L>=2r,

  L^2 A_m^*Hcrit,m A_m <=8Jpi^2 r! r M^2 I.             (4.2)

The contact form obeys

  <A_m F,W A_m F>
    <=m! sum_{i<j}<F,1_{z_i=z_j}F>
    <=m! m(m-1)M/(n+1)||F||^2.                         (4.3)

Here original W counts equal consecutive ordered y coordinates, which is
at most the count of all equal labeled coordinate pairs; the chamber sum
is at most the full labeled sum after the same factor m!. Hence

  L^2 A_m^*(Hcrit+(g/L)W)A_m
    <=[8Jpi^2 r!rM^2+2g_+ r!r(r-1)M] I =:Edec I.       (4.4)

Because ||G_m^{-1/2}||<=1, V_m inherits (4.4). Vacuum overflow has zero
positive energy, so the actual large-L decoded state has

  Tr[tau L^2 Hpos rho_dec]<=tau Edec.                  (4.5)

The small-L number-only branch projects commuting number sectors and
replaces overflow by vacuum; it can only decrease the expected positive
Hpos energy of a number-conserving input. Its bound is E_* from archive
Section 1. Thus max{E_*,tau Edec} is uniform in L at fixed epsilon.
The chemical contribution is separately bounded by tau B r on decoded
states, since their particle number is <=r. This is a positive-energy
bound, not a proof of expectation accuracy or energy-optimal recovery.

5. PUBLIC GRAM ACQUISITION AND NUMERICAL CONDITIONING

The decoder is parameter-blind but is not granted a free Gram matrix.
Let d_m=binom(M+m-1,m), D=sum_{m<=r}d_m (padding unused labels if needed).
A brute-force finite sum over original configurations already computes
G_m, at cost polynomial in N with exponent m, plus the low-mode dimension.
A more explicit linear-N arithmetic alternative uses the stabilizer identity

  prod_v r_v! =sum_{sigma in S_m} prod_i 1_{z_i=z_{sigma(i)}}. (5.1)

Insert normalized symmetrized sine products for each archive basis state.
There are at most (m!)^2 labeled-mode permutations in a matrix element.
For each sigma in (5.1), the constrained z sum factors over permutation
cycles; a cycle contributes one known n-term sum of products of at most
2m sine amplitudes. Cycles may take the same value: no distinctness
condition is needed, and the stabilizer counts the required multiplicity.
This gives the conservative arithmetic bound

  O((r!)^3 r N D^2)

for all Gram entries. Dense inverse-square-root acquisition costs O(D^3)
arithmetic and O(D^2) classical storage. These epsilon-dependent costs can
be large and are part of decoder setup, not erased from the resource ledger.
Only public L,M,r appear. The spectral enclosure [1,1+eta] controls numerical
conditioning, while factorial sum growth requires guard bits of order
r log r+log N+log D+log(1/desired error). Outward error bounds on Gram
entries and its functional calculus are needed for an implementation.

6. A FINITE DETERMINISTIC DECODER CIRCUIT; NO POSTSELECTION

The mathematical isometry V_m can always be extended and synthesized as
a finite unitary on occupation-ranked registers. Generic dense synthesis
would be expensive in the original sector dimension. A construction using
the existing public maps has the following explicit alternative.

First build a unitary whose successful block is

  B=V_m/sqrt(m!)=K_m^{-1}Jm G_m^{-1/2}/sqrt(m!).         (6.1)

G_m^{-1/2} is a known logical contraction, with a standard two-block
unitary dilation using sqrt(I-G_m^{-1}). Embed the logical low modes via
the known symmetric-mode map and inverse sine transforms. Then apply the
inverse gap-compression filter with amplitude
sqrt(prod_v r_v!)/sqrt(m!)<=1 and undo the normalized multiset isometry.
All computed arithmetic is erased on success. Each step is a contraction
with a unitary flag completion, and their composed successful block is
exactly (6.1). Since V_m^*V_m=I, success probability is the same 1/m! for
EVERY logical vector, including coherent superpositions/reference systems.

For completeness a deterministic amplification follows from a two-plane
calculation. Put theta=arcsin(1/sqrt(m!)) and

  q_m=ceil[pi/(4theta)-1/2], theta'=pi/(4q_m+2)<=theta.

Add a public success-flag attenuation sin(theta')/sin(theta). The new
unitary U has success amplitude sin(theta')V_m and an orthogonal failure
isometry of amplitude cos(theta'). Let P be its valid logical input with
zero work ancillas, and Q the success flag subspace. On each logical
vector's good/bad plane, U(2P-I)U^*(I-2Q) is the rotation taking
(sin theta',cos theta') to (sin 3theta',cos 3theta'). All planes share
the same angle. After q_m iterations the angle is
(2q_m+1)theta'=pi/2: success is deterministic and the output is V_m.
The m=0,1 cases require no amplification. This is standard amplitude
amplification specialized and explicitly counted, not a novel algorithm.

There are O(sqrt(r!)) repetitions of (6.1), its inverse and reflections.
Dense logical Gram dilation synthesis adds O(D^2 poly(log D)) gates per
repetition to the earlier O(r N^2 poly(log N)) sine/coordinate work.
Thus a conservative decoder gate bound is

  O(sqrt(r!)[r N^2 poly(log N)+D^2 poly(log D)]),

plus reversible arithmetic and the public Gram setup above. The stored
quantum register remains log2(1+D); O(r log N) labeled registers, flags,
arithmetic work, the Gram table and N output spins are extra resources.
Finite exact rotations are ideal mathematical operations; approximation
and acquisition precision are charged next.

7. TRACE AND ENERGY PRECISION ARE DIFFERENT REQUIREMENTS

Trace-error synthesis alone would allow total circuit error independent
of L. To keep the critical output energy bounded, that is insufficient:
an arbitrary small implementation error could put probability in UV states.
The supplied upper coupling gives the worst-case operator norm

  ||tau L^2 Hpos||<=tau(4J L^3+g_+ L^2).                (7.1)

Choose total implemented-channel trace error delta_L at most both the
remaining trace margin and 1/[tau(4J L^3+g_+L^2)]. Then (4.5) changes
by at most one. G-gate synthesis needs angle errors O(delta_L/G) and
O(log(G/delta_L)) bits, including an extra O(log L) energy-precision cost.
The public Gram computation and inverse square root require corresponding
certified precision. Persistent quantum memory is unaffected, but setup
bits/work and gate synthesis are L-dependent and explicitly charged.

Approximate physical Hamiltonian/input preparation has its own energy
requirement; a small trace error alone is not a free input-energy guarantee.
No thermal preparation, optimal public recovery, fast Gram acquisition or
energy-expectation-accurate continuum decoder is claimed.

8. STATUS AND DECISIVE GATES

The mathematical refinement is an internal candidate proof. Finite Gram,
isometry, energy and amplification checks are recorded separately. Root
agreement and finite spectra are not external validation. Standard
amplitude amplification reference: Brassard, Hoyer, Mosca and Tapp,
arXiv:quant-ph/0005055 (2000-05-15), AMS Contemporary Mathematics 305
(2002), DOI 10.1090/conm/305/05215. Primary abstract/metadata checked;
the needed equal-angle calculation is shown above rather than importing
an unread generalized circuit theorem.

Falsifiers: failure of low-mode contact operator (2.4), Gram normalization,
inverse sampling energy (4.1)-(4.4), constant-success block (6.1), or
reflected-plane angle. Remaining gates: external validity/priority,
critical-energy expectation accuracy as epsilon decreases, better constants
and setup, and the still independent LL whole-trace experiment.
