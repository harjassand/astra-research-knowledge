FIXED-SOURCE COUNTEREXAMPLE TO SQUARED-MAP TOTAL-VARIATION LIPSCHITZ STABILITY
Independent chain attack, 7 October 2026

Claim refuted in dimension 2:
  integral |T_mu - T_nu|^2 d rho <= C TV(mu,nu)
for one fixed compactly supported bounded-density absolutely continuous source,
uniformly over targets in one fixed compact container. The counterexample works
even if C may depend on the entire fixed source, not merely its density bound.
TV means sup_A |mu(A)-nu(A)| = half the total variation norm of mu-nu.

1. FIXED SOURCE

For n >= 1 define
  k_n = n+4, s_n = 2^(-n), H_n = 2^(-3n-3),
  w_n = H_n/(100 k_n^2),
  R_n = [-w_n,w_n] x [s_n-H_n,s_n+H_n].
The rectangles are disjoint. Their closure together with the origin is compact
in [-1,1] x [0,1]. Set V = sum_n 4 w_n H_n, which is finite and positive, and
rho = V^(-1) 1_(union R_n) dx dy. Thus rho is one fixed probability measure
with bounded density V^(-1). No source parameter depends on the targets below.

2. TARGETS AND CONVEX POTENTIALS

Fix n and suppress its subscript. Put k=k_n, H=H_n, w=w_n, s=s_n, and
  z_i = -1 + 2i/k, sigma_i = (-1)^i, p_i = (sigma_i,z_i), 0 <= i <= k.
Add the two opposite-rail endpoint sites (-sigma_0,-1) and (-sigma_k,+1).
Equivalently, there are sites on both rails at both endpoint heights, and one
alternating-rail site at every internal height. All sites lie in Y=[-1,1]^2.

For each site p=(sigma,z) use the affine score
  F_p(x,y) = sigma x + z(y-s) - H z^2/2.
Define phi=max_p F_p and q=H/(10k), psi=max_p (F_p-qz).
Then psi(x,y)=phi(x,y-q). Both potentials are convex. Let
  mu=(grad phi)_#rho, nu=(grad psi)_#rho.
Their gradients are the quadratic-cost Brenier maps from rho to mu and nu,
because a convex gradient is optimal for its pushforward, and rho is AC.
The nondifferentiable interfaces have rho-measure zero.

3. OUTSIDE THE n-TH RECTANGLE, THE MAPS ARE IDENTICAL

Write t=y-s. For t>=H, the endpoint (sigma,+1) dominates every interior site
on its own rail, since its score difference is
  (1-z)[t-H(1+z)/2] >= 0.
For t<=-H, the endpoint (sigma,-1) dominates every interior same-rail site,
since its score difference is
  (1+z)[-t-H(1-z)/2] >= 0.
The winning extreme site is therefore (sign x,+1) above and (sign x,-1)
below, independent of t. Translation by q preserves these assignments whenever
both t and t-q are outside [-H,H] on the same side.

Every R_m, m != n, has this property. For example H_m<=s_m/32. If m<n,
  y-s_n >= (31/32)s_m-s_n >= (15/32)s_m >= (15/16)s_n.
If m>n,
  y-s_n <= (33/32)s_m-s_n <= -(31/64)s_n.
These distances are much greater than H_n+q_n, since H_n<=s_n/32 and
q_n<=H_n/50. Hence grad phi=grad psi on every other source rectangle.

4. ALL INTERNAL LAYERS ON R_n ARE ACTIVE AND TRANSLATE EXACTLY

For x != 0, choose the winning bottom and top endpoint rail sign=sign x.
At fixed x, the active sites then have increasing heights -1,z_1,...,z_(k-1),1.
The interface thresholds t=tau_i(x), i=0,...,k-1, are
  tau_0 = -H+H/k + (k/2)(|x|-sigma_1 x),
  tau_i = -H+(2i+1)H/k + sigma_i kx, 1 <= i <= k-2,
  tau_(k-1) = H-H/k + (k/2)(sigma_(k-1)x-|x|).
Every consecutive spacing is at least
  2H/k-2kw = 1.98H/k > q.
The thresholds are inside [-H+H/k,H-H/k]. Thus all k interfaces and their
translated counterparts tau_i+q remain inside R_n; their swept strips are
disjoint. Increasing crossings certify the indicated ordered upper envelope.

Each swept strip has rho-mass
  delta = (2wq)/V.
Every internal site's vertical cell length is unchanged for each x, so its
target mass is unchanged. Each bottom endpoint gains a=delta/2=wq/V, and
each top endpoint loses a. Consequently
  nu-mu = a(delta_(+1,-1)+delta_(-1,-1)
             -delta_(+1,+1)-delta_(-1,+1)),
  TV(mu,nu)=delta.

5. MAP DISCREPANCY

The k-2 central interfaces join opposite rails and adjacent heights, with
squared site distance 4+4/k^2. Each endpoint interface joins the same rail on
half the x interval and opposite rails on the other half; its averaged squared
site distance is 2+4/k^2. Therefore the exact squared map discrepancy is
  E_n = integral |grad phi-grad psi|^2 d rho
      = delta[(k-2)(4+4/k^2)+2(2+4/k^2)]
      = delta(4k-4+4/k).
In particular E_n/TV(mu,nu)=4k_n-4+4/k_n tends to infinity.
This refutes the proposed bound for one fixed rho and one fixed compact Y.

6. W-INFINITY DOES NOT CONTRADICT THE SEPARATE SHARP-BRIDGE CANDIDATE

There is an explicit coupling of mu and nu with maximum displacement 4/k.
On each rail, list its supported heights in increasing order. Move mass a from
each node to its predecessor, and leave the remaining mass at that node.
At the bottom this gives incoming mass a; at internal nodes incoming/outgoing
mass cancel; at the top this removes a. This is feasible: each interior site
has old mass at least (2w/V)(2H/k-2kw)>a, and each top endpoint has old mass
at least wH/(kV)>a. Successive heights on a rail differ by at most 4/k.
Hence W_infinity(mu,nu)<=4/k.

It is also >=4/k. The bottom site (+1,-1) gains positive mass a. Its nearest
distinct supported target site is (+1,-1+4/k), at distance 4/k; opposite-rail
sites have distance >=2. Any coupling must import some mass to (+1,-1) from
a distinct site. Since k>=5, the nearest distance is exactly 4/k. Thus
  W_infinity(mu,nu)=4/k.
Since delta=H^2/(500Vk^3),
  E_n/W_infinity(mu,nu)=(k^2-k+1)delta = O(H_n^2/(Vk_n)) -> 0.
This counterexample refutes TV control, but supplies no counterexample to a
squared-map estimate linear in W_infinity.

7. FAILURE LEDGER AND SCOPE

The initial uncapped alternating-rail construction did not isolate the n-th
source component: endpoint-vs-penultimate slope gaps shrink like 1/k, so fixed
earlier source widths eventually cause exterior cells to move. Adding both
rails at both extreme heights fixes this exactly; the same-rail endpoint
dominance inequalities above hold for every x.

The source is disconnected/nonconvex. This does not decide the same TV bound
for a source with convex support and density bounded above and away from zero.
In dimension 1, the bound does hold: quantile optimality gives E<=D W_1 and
W_1<=D TV for target diameter D, hence E<=D^2 TV.
For every d>2, take the product of rho with uniform measure on a fixed cube
in the remaining coordinates, and append zero target coordinates. The same
proof and discrepancy hold.

All assertions above are direct calculations, not numerical evidence or
literature priority claims. Strongest next attack: determine whether the
W_infinity linear estimate survives arbitrary compact AC bounded-density
sources, or identify a geometric/analytic obstruction beyond TV rerouting.
