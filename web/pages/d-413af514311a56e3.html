C05_L04 FINAL — BROADCASTING BOUNDARY AND RANDOM-MATRIX ATTACK
2026-10-07. Requested model/effort gpt-6-luna/max; observed backend UNKNOWN.

STATUS

No qualifying counterexample to bounded-Holevo broadcasting-to-EB is found.
The original independent INITIAL remains unchanged and hash-verified. The
deeper post-release result is a robust collective Choi witness for approximate
traceless-Hermitian tight frames. It rules out a natural random-matrix route:
randomly conjugated involutions can have fixed sandwich, exact bounded
Holevo capacity and accessible information tending to zero, while their
actual two-output broadcasting error remains bounded below by a constant.
The crucial missing counterexample condition is b2->0.

INITIAL CLAIMS, PRESERVED

1. For the full traceless Hermitian Pauli frame, states
   rho_(i,s)=(I+s aP_i)/d have capacity c(a), accessible information at most
   a^2/[2(1-a^2)(d+1)], and exact optimal two-output error
   b2=ad/[4(d+1)]. The lower bound is a full Choi witness; the upper bound
   is the universal qudit 1-to-2 cloner.
2. Every supplied random-unitary bistochastic instrument yields an actual
   selfcompatible corrected channel Phi with
   ||Phi^m-D||_(infinity-to-one,tracial)<=4^-m. This closes the expander
   route only for that instrument class.
3. Global POVMs exactly reconstruct orthogonal symmetric/antisymmetric
   hiding states; restricted-LOCC hiding does not imply an EB obstruction.
   The symmetric-copy ladder either fails the fixed sandwich/bounded capacity
   gate, or its fixed-sandwich noise repair collapses its replacer error.

NEW POST-EXPOSURE RESULT

Let H_1,...,H_N be traceless Hermitian, tau(H_j^2)=1, ||H_j||<=M, with frame
operator S(X)=sum_j tau(H_jX)H_j obeying
(1-delta)(N/(d^2-1))I <= S <= (1+delta)(N/(d^2-1))I. For states
rho_(j,s)=(I+s aH_j)/d, any actual broadcaster with maximum marginal
half-trace error epsilon satisfies

  epsilon >= a[d^2(1-2delta)-d]/[4M(d^2-1)].

The proof compares each signed-state contrast to a Choi expectation. The
ideal frame Choi operator is kappa[d^2|Omega><Omega|-I], and the two
receiver projections have maximum overlap 1/d. The frame tolerance changes
the witness by at most 2d^2 delta kappa in operator norm. No broadcaster
covariance, symmetry, flag, or particular implementation is assumed.

For independent Haar conjugates H_j=U_j P U_j* of a traceless involution P,
the expected frame operator is I/(d^2-1). Matrix Chernoff gives a successful
delta-tight sample for N=O(d^2 log d) with positive probability. At delta=1/8
and M=1, the family has sandwich (1-a)sigma <= rho <=(1+a)sigma, exact
Holevo capacity c(a), and accessible information at most
a^2(1+delta)/[2(1-a^2)(d+1)]. Yet b2 is at least a/12 for all d>=2 and tends
to 3a/16. It is not a counterexample because b2 does not vanish. Any EB map
is itself broadcastable, so its uniform reconstruction error is at least
this b2 lower bound.
