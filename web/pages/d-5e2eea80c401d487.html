Let sigma>0 be stationary for the finite-dimensional UCP semigroup
exp(-tL), let L be KMS-self-adjoint, set Gamma(A)=sqrt(sigma)A sqrt(sigma),
s=sqrt(sigma), and H=Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2). Then H is ordinary
HS-self-adjoint and H(s)=0. The semigroup exp(-tH) is CP because it is
the composition of exp(-tL) with two positive congruences. Thus G=-H
generates a CP semigroup, though its HS representation need not be unital.

Use the Choi convention |A>>=sum_i |i> tensor A|i>. Write e=|I>>/sqrt(d)
and P=I-|e><e|. Since
 J_(exp(tG))=|I>><<I|+t J_G+O(t^2)>=0,
projection onto P and division by t show C=P J_G P>=0. Every relevant
map is Hermiticity preserving, hence its Choi matrix is Hermitian.
The antiunitary R|A>>=|A*>> fixes e and commutes with P. HS-self-adjointness
of G gives R J_G R=J_G, so C also commutes with R. Diagonalize this
positive finite matrix in an R-real eigenbasis on P. Absorbing the square
roots of its eigenvalues gives finitely many traceless Hermitian N_j with
 C=sum_j |N_j>><<N_j|.
At most d^2-1 nonzero noises are needed.

Let C0(A)=sum_j N_j A N_j and let J_res=J_(G-C0). By construction
P J_res P=0. Its Hermitian decomposition is
 J_res=|e><w|+|w><e|+gamma |e><e|,
w perpendicular to e, gamma real. Antiunitary invariance gives Rw=w.
Define the Hermitian W by
 |W>>=w/sqrt(d)+gamma |I>>/(2d).
Then J_res=|W>><<I|+|I>><<W|, which corresponds exactly to the map
A->WA+AW. Therefore
 G(A)=sum_j N_j A N_j+WA+AW,
 H(A)={V,A}/2-sum_j N_j A N_j, V=-2W Hermitian.

The condition H(s)=0 fixes V uniquely:
 (Vs+sV)/2=sum_j N_j s N_j.
The Lyapunov operator ell_s(V)=(Vs+sV)/2 is invertible because s>0.
Its inverse has the positive integral form
 ell_s^(-1)(Y)=2 integral_0^infinity exp(-ts)Y exp(-ts) dt.
Thus V=sum_j V_j, V_j=ell_s^(-1)(N_j s N_j), and
 H=sum_j H_(N_j), H_N(A)={V_N,A}/2-NAN.

Each summand is itself legal at the SAME sigma. It is HS-self-adjoint and
fixes s; its negative generates a CP semigroup by Trotter product of
exp(t Ad_N), a positive sum of CP powers, and the anticommutator congruence
semigroup. Similarity by Gamma^(-1/2) makes it unital, since s maps to I.
HS symmetry makes it KMS self-adjoint, and the KMS adjoint fixes sigma.
This proves legal common-reference decomposition, not just positive
operators lacking a Markov interface. Each V_N is positive as well, though
its positivity was not needed to make the congruence CP.

Finally N->N+cI changes V_N by 2cN+c^2 I and leaves H_N identically
unchanged. Hence traceless noises suffice, and on M2 all noises are real
linear combinations of X,Y,Z. Both J and E are linear in H/L, so the
per-noise lower bound sums without choosing a new generator or reference.
