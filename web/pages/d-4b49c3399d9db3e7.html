CYCLE07 GLOBAL QUBIT CONSTANT: INDEPENDENT GENERALIZED ANGULAR PROOF

Conclusion and scope
--------------------
THEOREM. For every faithful pair sigma,rho on C^2 and every KMS-symmetric
finite quantum Markov semigroup with positive Heisenberg generator L,

  J(rho)=Tr[L_*(rho)(log rho-log sigma)]
     >=pi Tr[sqrt(rho) H(sqrt(rho))]=pi E(rho),
  H=Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2), Gamma(X)=sqrt(sigma)Xsqrt(sigma).

No primitivity or modular covariance is assumed. A classical untouched
reference also inherits the nonlinear pi bound by summing its blocks. A
nonlinear arbitrary quantum-reference pi or C2 bound is NOT claimed.

This is a model-assisted complete internal analytic proof. External/formal
validation is absent; novelty and priority are UNKNOWN. The derivation in
this new phase is a generalization of this worker's own standalone-qubit
angular proof, using the already audited Hardy coefficient extension. The
root-origin stationary pi extension from the preceding phase is an available
premise, but does not itself imply this global theorem. No new sibling proof
was read in this phase. All prior completed files and indexes remain untouched.

1. Available representation and exact scalar formulas
-----------------------------------------------------
The immutable earlier independent baseline is
../cycle06_weak_regular/INDEPENDENT_BASELINE.txt,
SHA256 25af2026955373c23399683fbacdc2ccd94d145671e42c3d707eeb7ba2e2a690.
It proves every finite KMS generator is a sum of legal elementary noises

  s=sqrt(sigma), ell_s(V)=(sV+Vs)/2,
  V_B=ell_s^(-1)(BsB), H_B(X)={V_B,X}/2-BXB,

with B Hermitian, and that identity shifts of B cancel. This all-generator
representation is used here, not a covariance-restricted subclass. It also
provides the exact J_B,E_B Bloch coefficients used below.

Diagonalize sigma and rotate rho's scalar off-diagonal entry to be real:

  sigma=(I+tanh(alpha)Z)/2,
  rho=(I+r[sqrt(1-c^2)X+cZ])/2,
  alpha>=0, beta>=0, r=tanh(beta), -1<=c<=1.

Write

  C_alpha=cosh(alpha), S_alpha=sinh(alpha), A=cosh(alpha/2),
  k_alpha=tanh(alpha/2), h=1-sech(beta), G=beta tanh(beta).

For B=X+aZ, the exact coefficients are

  E_0=C_alpha-1+h c^2-S_alpha r c,
  E_1=2sqrt(1-c^2)(k_alpha r-hc),
  E_2=h(1-c^2),
  J_0=G[(C_alpha-1)+(C_alpha+1)c^2]
       -2c[alpha r C_alpha+beta S_alpha]+2alpha S_alpha,
  J_1=2sqrt(1-c^2)[(beta S_alpha+alpha r)/A-2A Gc],
  J_2=2G(1-c^2),
  E_B=E_0+aE_1+a^2E_2, J_B=J_0+aJ_1+a^2J_2.         (1)

2. Angular reduction with a general constant
--------------------------------------------
Let 0<kappa<4, lambda=kappa/2, and define

  k_lambda(x)=x sinh(x)-lambda(cosh(x)-1),
  K_lambda=G-lambda h=k_lambda(beta)/cosh(beta),
  U_lambda=(beta S_alpha+alpha r)/A-2lambda k_alpha r,
  W_lambda=2(lambda h-A G).

For beta>0, K_lambda>0: x coth(x/2)>=2, so
k_lambda(x)=(cosh(x)-1)[x coth(x/2)-lambda]>0 for x!=0 and lambda<2.
The limiting x=0 value is zero. Inserting (1) gives

  F_kappa(a,c)=J_B-kappa E_B
    =F_0(c)+2a sqrt(1-c^2)(U_lambda+W_lambda c)
                               +2a^2K_lambda(1-c^2),
  F_0=J_0-kappa E_0.

For |c|<1, completing the square yields

  F_kappa(a,c)>=Q_kappa(c)
       =F_0(c)-(U_lambda+W_lambda c)^2/(2K_lambda).    (2)

The quadratic coefficient of Q_kappa is exactly

  (C_alpha+1)G-2lambda h-W_lambda^2/(2K_lambda)
       =-2lambda G h(A-1)^2/K_lambda<=0.              (3)

Hence Q_kappa is concave on [-1,1] and is bounded below by its two endpoint
values. The optimized endpoint limits are retained, even though the minimizing
a can diverge as c approaches an endpoint.

3. Generalized exact endpoint identity
--------------------------------------
For eta=+-1 put b=eta beta and delta=b-alpha. Define

  T_lambda(x,y)=x sinh(y)+y sinh(x)
                                   -4lambda sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2).

At c=eta the diagonal-state identities give

  E_0=(cosh(delta)-1)/cosh(beta),
  J_0=2delta sinh(delta)/cosh(beta),
  F_0=2k_lambda(delta)/cosh(beta).

The generalized cross coefficient is exactly

  eta A cosh(b)(U_lambda+eta W_lambda)=-T_lambda(b,delta).  (4)

Indeed its left side expands as

  b[S_alpha cosh(b)-(C_alpha+1)sinh(b)]
      +alpha sinh(b)-2lambda sinh(alpha/2)sinh(b)
      +2lambda A(cosh(b)-1)
   =-b sinh(delta)-delta sinh(b)
      +2lambda[cosh(b-alpha/2)-cosh(alpha/2)]
   =-T_lambda(b,delta).

Thus

  Q_kappa(eta)
   =[4A^2 k_lambda(b)k_lambda(delta)-T_lambda(b,delta)^2]
                      /[2A^2 cosh(beta)k_lambda(beta)].  (5)

4. Hardy kernel at lambda=pi/2
------------------------------
For real x,y define

  K_lambda(x,y)=T_lambda(x,y)/[2cosh((x-y)/2)].

Its diagonal is k_lambda(x). Set t=tanh(x/2),u=tanh(y/2) and use the analytic
disk functions a(t)=(1-t^2)atanh(t)/t, a(0)=1, b(t)=sqrt(1-t^2), b(0)=1.
The same algebra as in the original Hardy proof gives

  K_lambda(x,y)=2sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2) C_lambda(t,u),
  C_lambda(t,u)=[a(t)+a(u)-lambda b(t)b(u)]/(1-tu).     (6)

The original boundary identity 2Re a=(pi/2)|b|^2 shows that the Hardy operator
representing C_lambda on the full disk is

  D_lambda=(pi/2)M_b^*M_b-lambda M_bM_b^*.

For 0<=lambda<=pi/2,

  D_lambda=(pi/2-lambda)M_b^*M_b
            +lambda(M_b^*M_b-M_bM_b^*)>=0.            (7)

Analytic-multiplier hyponormality follows from
||M_b^*f||=||P_+(conj(b)f)||<=||conj(b)f||=||M_bf||.
At lambda=pi/2 only the positive hyponormal commutator remains. Therefore
C_(pi/2) is PSD on every finite point set, and so is K_(pi/2) by the signed
diagonal congruence in (6). Its two-by-two principal minors give

  T_(pi/2)(x,y)^2
    <=4cosh((x-y)/2)^2 k_(pi/2)(x)k_(pi/2)(y).        (8)

Zero arguments are obtained directly or by continuity; no sign condition on
x,y is needed. Apply (8) to x=b,y=delta, noting x-y=alpha. The numerator of
(5) is nonnegative, so both endpoints of (2) and all the real-plane noises
satisfy J_B>=pi E_B. The beta=0 and |c|=1 cases follow by continuity for fixed
finite noise; pure Z follows from its complete strong constant 4.

5. Perpendicular sector and all generators
-------------------------------------------
In the chosen scalar-qubit basis sigma and rho are real, so conjugation
separates the imaginary Y-Pauli sector from the real X,Z noise plane. The
earlier exact identities give

  E_Y-E_X=h(1-c^2), J_Y-J_X=2G(1-c^2),
  (J_Y-kappa E_Y)-(J_X-kappa E_X)
                          =2K_lambda(1-c^2)>=0.       (9)

At kappa=pi this proves the perpendicular sector as well. Scaling and identity
noise cancellation cover every Hermitian qubit noise. Summing the elementary
inequalities via the full KMS representation proves the stated global theorem.
For a classical reference, J and E are weighted sums of the corresponding
qubit quantities, proving the nonlinear pi extension for that reference class.

6. Exact characterization of the infimal global qubit constant
---------------------------------------------------------------
For ANY 0<kappa<4, the preceding algebra implies the following equivalence:

  Universal global qubit J>=kappa E
  IFF every real pair x,y has
     4cosh((x-y)/2)^2 k_(kappa/2)(x)k_(kappa/2)(y)
                                  -T_(kappa/2)(x,y)^2>=0.  (10)

The forward implication is not inferred from full-kernel necessity. If a real
two-point determinant is negative, reorder the pair to x>y, set alpha=x-y>0,
beta=|x|>0, eta=sign(x). Zero nodes or equal nodes cannot give a negative
determinant. Equation (5) gives Q_kappa(eta)<0. Continuity gives an interior
c near eta with Q_kappa(c)<0, and the finite legal noise

  a=-(U_lambda+W_lambda c)/(2K_lambda sqrt(1-c^2))

has F_kappa=Q_kappa(c)<0. All states are faithful. Conversely, nonnegative
two-point determinants close (5), (2), and (9), proving the bound for all
states/noises/generators. This yields an exact scalar extremal problem for
the best global constant.

The full Hardy PSD argument proves that this best constant is at least pi.
It does not show equality: the global scalar-qubit proof needs only real
two-point positivity, whereas full disk/many-point positivity is stronger.
No inference from failure of the full disk kernel above pi to a physical
qubit counterexample is made. Any sharper global upper certificate or exact
optimization is preserved separately in a post-freeze addendum.
