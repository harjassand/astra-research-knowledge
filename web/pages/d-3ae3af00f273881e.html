A. EXACT CLASSICAL SAMPLING OF A FINITE QUANTUM TREE RECORD
Suppose B:M_d->M_d tensor M_d has exact rational-complex unnormalized Choi
matrix J>=0, Tr_output J=I. Rational pivoted LDL* gives
 J=sum_j q_j v_j v_j*, q_j>0 rational, v_j rational-complex,
with at most d^3 summands. Zero PSD pivots have zero rows/columns and may
be discarded. Standard rational elimination/determinant bit bounds are
polynomial in d and the dense rational input bitlength; no eigenvectors or
high-degree algebraic numbers are needed. Reshape v_j into L_j:d->d².
Then K_j=sqrt(q_j)L_j are Kraus operators and sum_j q_j L_j*L_j=I.
The instrument map for outcome j is X->q_j L_j X L_j*, entirely rational.
It is the instrument of a supplied description, not access to a hidden
black-box environment granted for free.

Use B at every vertex of a finite binary tree, depth m, K=2^m leaves,
K-1 internal nodes. A leaf POVM has polynomially many rational rank-one
effects. The fixed informationally complete leaf construction uses basis
vectors and +/- real/imaginary pair superpositions with uniform weights,
so each effect is c_z a_z* a_z with c_z rational and a_z rational-complex.
The full record comprises one Kraus index per internal node and one leaf
outcome per leaf. There are exponentially many possible full records but
only O(K) recorded random variables, each with polynomial(d) domain.

For ANY partial record, define each unrecorded leaf message as I, and each
recorded leaf message as its effect. At an internal node let S_v be all
Kraus indices when unrecorded, or its singleton fixed index when recorded.
Propagate the d-by-d positive rational message
 E_v=sum_(j in S_v) q_j L_j*(E_left tensor E_right)L_j.
At the root, p=Tr(E_root)/d is EXACTLY the probability of the partial event
under reference I/d. This follows by the adjoint channel composition rule.
It does not assume sibling independence and does not simulate/store the
full d^K-dimensional output state.

To sample the next local variable, evaluate p_a for each candidate value,
leaving every other unrecorded variable summed out. Linearity gives
sum_a p_a=p. A sampled prefix always has p>0. Draw value a with the exact
rational probabilities p_a/p. Continue for all 2K-1 variables. The resulting
record law is exactly the quantum instrument law, by the elementary chain
rule of conditional probabilities. A direct implementation uses O(K²)
tree-node contractions times the polynomial local-domain and d^6 arithmetic
costs; caching can improve this but is not a premise.

Bit costs are polynomial too. Clear a common denominator of the rational
local instrument/leaf data. A complete tree contraction is a sum of products
of O(K) local entries; multiplication grows denominator bitlength linearly
in K and summation adds O(K log d) bits. Partial-record sums obey the same
bound. Conditional ratios consequently have polynomial bitlength even for
exponentially unlikely prefixes. Obtain a common denominator across the
polynomially many candidates, sample a uniform integer below it using
binary rejection, and invert cumulative counts. Rejection succeeds with
probability at least1/2, so expected random-bit cost is polynomial in the
rational description length. There is no rare-event postselection.

For a FULL record, each leaf message is rank1 and each internal map is a
single Kraus map. Induction gives E_root=c a* a with c>0 rational and a a
rational-complex row vector; zero-probability records never arise. Its
posterior pi=E_root/Tr E_root=a*a/||a||² is a rational pure-state projector.
One may compute it using row messages
 a_v=(a_left tensor a_right)L_j,
with positive scalar factors tracked separately. This proves that exact
sampling and exact posterior extraction are classical expected-polynomial
procedures for a finite tree and explicit rational local channel.

Scope: this is a tree tensor-network contraction. It does NOT simulate an
arbitrary quantum circuit, remove the polynomial dependence on d (which is
exponential in input qubit number), or acquire a black-box Choi matrix.
It may enable classical sampling in the separately proposed polynomial
comparator compiler; that compiler's covariance concentration, residual
completion, verifier and termination proof remain separate obligations.

B. CANONICAL POVM CHANNEL IS STABLE WITHOUT MINIMUM OUTCOME PROBABILITY
Let M=(M_z) and N=(N_z) be finite POVMs on the same d-dimensional input,
indexed by the same outcome set. Define
 Psi_M(X)=sum_z Tr(M_z X) M_z/Tr M_z,
with zero effects omitted; similarly for N. Each map is canonical CPTP,
unital, HS-selfadjoint, and EB, but not necessarily pure canonical.
Put eta=(1/d)sum_z ||M_z-N_z||_1.
Then the normalized Choi states satisfy
 ||J_norm(Psi_M)-J_norm(Psi_N)||_1<=3 eta,
and consequently ||Psi_M-Psi_N||_diamond<=3d eta.
No lower bound on Tr M_z or Tr N_z appears.

Proof: write A_z=M_z/d=p_z rho_z and B_z=N_z/d=q_z sigma_z.
Their Choi contributions are p_z rho_z^T tensor rho_z and
q_z sigma_z^T tensor sigma_z. For p_z,q_z>0, the triangle inequality gives
norm difference <=||A_z-B_z||_1+q_z||rho_z-sigma_z||_1.
Since q_z||rho_z-sigma_z||_1
 <=||A_z-B_z||_1+|p_z-q_z|<=2||A_z-B_z||_1,
the result follows by summing. Zero masses satisfy the same bound directly.
The usual Choi-to-diamond estimate supplies the factor d.

If the underlying tree channels/instruments differ in diamond norm by
at most epsilon per local call, the hybrid bound gives global difference
at most (K-1)epsilon. Applying both to half of a maximally entangled input
and the same final classical measurement gives eta<=(K-1)epsilon.
Thus the coarse canonical comparator has a polynomial dimension/call
stability bound even when individual records have tiny probabilities.
This may support approximate Choi acquisition, but a complete tomography,
CPTP rational fitting, implementation and HS-order-error theorem is NOT
proved by this lemma alone.
