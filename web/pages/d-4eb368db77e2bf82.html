Title:Sum of squares decomposition of positive polynomials with rational coefficients

Authors:Santiago Laplagne
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Sum of squares decomposition of positive polynomials with rational coefficients, by Santiago Laplagne

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:We present an example of a strictly positive polynomial with rational coefficients that can be decomposed as a sum of squares of polynomials over $\R$ but not over $\Q$. This answers an open question by C. Scheiderer posed as the second question in Section 5.1 of the paper Sums of squares of polynomials with rational coefficients (2012). We verify that the example we construct defines a nonsingular projective hypersurface, giving also a positive answer to the third question posed in that section.
