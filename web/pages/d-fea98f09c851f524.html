# Compatibility and disturbance audit for complement-LCU failure flag

Date: 2026-10-08  
Status: **INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE**. No-cloning creates no obstruction to the stated aggregate compression. An exact disturbance identity explains what the failure measurement does impose; it is not a restriction in the current encoding interface.

## Interface being tested

Let \(h_1,\ldots,h_M\) be projections, \(w_j>0\), \(\sum_jw_j=1\),
and \(H=\sum_jw_jh_j\), so \(0\le H\le I\). The complement-LCU target
uses one isometry \(V:\mathcal H\to\mathcal K\), one diagonal failure
projection \(B\), and an image enforcer \(C\), with
\[
\alpha H\le F:=V^*BV\le\beta H,\qquad
\ker C=\operatorname{ran}V,\qquad
C\ge\gamma(I-VV^*).
\]
The inequalities are full-space operator inequalities on the logical space
and the enforcer gap is on the entire physical orthogonal complement. The
interface does not require B to report each \(h_j\) separately, does not
require the measurement to preserve \(\operatorname{ran}V\), and does not
require B and C to commute.

## The LCU failure measurement is a compatible outcome POVM

Pad the normalized term list if needed with zero projections so its label
register has dimension \(L+1\). Use an identity label with probability
\(1/2\) and term label j with probability \(w_j/2\). Put
\[
R_{\rm id}=I,\qquad R_j=I-2h_j,
\]
and choose a label preparation A with
\(A|0\rangle=|\chi\rangle\), where
\(\chi_{\rm id}=1/\sqrt2\) and \(\chi_j=\sqrt{w_j/2}\). With
\(\mathrm{SELECT}=|{\rm id}\rangle\langle{\rm id}|\otimes I+
\sum_j|j\rangle\langle j|\otimes R_j\), let
\[
U=(A^*\otimes I)\,\mathrm{SELECT}\,(A\otimes I),\qquad
Vx=U(|0\rangle\otimes x).
\]
The \((0,0)\) block of U is
\[
K_0=\langle0|U|0\rangle
=\tfrac12 I+\tfrac12\sum_jw_j(I-2h_j)=I-H.
\]
Write \(Vx=\sum_{a=0}^L|a\rangle K_ax\). Then U unitary gives
\(\sum_aK_a^*K_a=I\). The output-label projectors
\(D_a=|a\rangle\langle a|\otimes I\) are one common diagonal PVM, and
\[
F_a:=V^*D_aV=K_a^*K_a\ge0,\qquad \sum_aF_a=I.
\]
Thus the output label outcomes are a simultaneous POVM on the logical input.
They are not, in general, measurements of the separate clauses \(h_j\):
after A-uncomputation, each \(K_a\) is a coherent linear combination of
the reflections \(R_j\).

For the failure projection \(B=\sum_{a\ne0}D_a\), the failure effects
are \(F_a\) for \(a\ne0\), and their total is exactly
\[
F=\sum_{a\ne0}F_a=I-K_0^*K_0
=I-(I-H)^2=2H-H^2.
\]
Consequently, on every logical vector x,
\[
\langle x,Hx\rangle\le\langle x,Fx\rangle
\le2\langle x,Hx\rangle,
\]
because \(F-H=H(I-H)\ge0\) and \(2H-F=H^2\ge0\). This verifies
\(\alpha=1,\beta=2\) for the full noncommuting list. Since
\(f(\lambda)=2\lambda-\lambda^2\) vanishes on \([0,1]\) only at zero,
\[
\ker F=\ker H=\bigcap_{j:w_j>0}\ker h_j.
\]
For \(x\in\ker H\), the stronger outcome-level statement follows from
\(0=\langle x,Fx\rangle=\sum_{a\ne0}\|K_ax\|^2\): every failure block
annihilates x. Conversely, if every failure block annihilates x, then
\(Fx=0\), hence \(Hx=0\). The full-list measurement therefore detects
exactly whether the state is outside the common satisfying subspace, in
aggregate. It does not identify which clause is violated.

## Exact image kernel and information disturbance

Let \(P=VV^*\), and take the canonical image enforcer \(C=I-P\). It is a
positive projection, \(\ker C=\operatorname{ran}V\), and has gap exactly
one. This construction is available for every isometry, independent of H,
B, or term commutation. It can be dense; the sparse-row cost is a separate
open constraint. In particular, the existence of an exact image kernel
introduces no new compatibility condition between the logical effects
\(F_a\).

There is a precise disturbance identity. Since B is a projection, set
\(X=(I-P)BV\), the component that leaves the encoded image after applying
the failure test. Then
\[
X^*X=V^*B(I-P)BV=F-F^2.
\]
For complement-LCU,
\[
F-F^2=(2H-H^2)(I-H)^2,
\qquad
\|Xx\|^2=\langle x,(2H-H^2)(I-H)^2x\rangle.
\]
On an H-eigenvector of eigenvalue \(\lambda\in[0,1]\), the squared
disturbance is \(\lambda(2-\lambda)(1-\lambda)^2\), at most \(1/4\).
It vanishes on \(\ker H\) and at eigenvalue one, and is positive at every
intermediate eigenvalue. For example, in the two-seed Hamiltonian
\((P_\phi+P_\psi)/2\), the nonzero eigenvalues
\((1\pm|\langle\phi,\psi\rangle|)/2\) are intermediate, so the failure
test disturbs those encoded vectors.

This is an information-disturbance tradeoff, not an obstruction: the target
does not require B's measurement to leave the image invariant or to permit
recovery of the input after the outcome. If one added that stronger
nondisturbance condition \((I-P)BV=0\), the identity would force
\(F^2=F\). For the complement-LCU polynomial this means
\(\operatorname{spec}(H)\subseteq\{0,1\}\), which fails for any instance
with an intermediate eigenvalue. Nothing in the stated kernel/gap interface
imposes this extra condition. Likewise, B and C need not commute; if they
did commute for \(C=I-P\), the compression F would be a projection, again
contradicting intermediate spectrum. The LCU flag is an ordinary disturbing
measurement, as allowed.

## What the order and kernel conditions do, and do not, force

For any positive \(H,F\), if \(0<\alpha\) and
\(\alpha H\le F\le\beta H\), then \(\ker F=\ker H\): positivity and the
upper inequality give \(\ker H\subseteq\ker F\); the lower inequality
gives the reverse inclusion. For a projection-valued positive sum
\(H=\sum_jw_jh_j\), this is the intersection of the individual term
kernels. This is the only support-level information in the aggregate order
bound. It gives no term-by-term order \(F_a\succeq c_jh_j\), and the
physical condition \(\ker C=\operatorname{ran}V\) is a condition on a
different space.

More generally, every finite POVM \(\{E_a\}\) is a compression of a
diagonal PVM: the isometry \(W x=\sum_a|a\rangle\sqrt{E_a}x\) obeys
\(W^*W=I\), and \(W^*(|a\rangle\langle a|\otimes I)W=E_a\). It has the
dense exact enforcer \(I-WW^*\) with gap one. Thus POVM compatibility is
not in conflict with an exact image kernel unless additional physical
access requirements are imposed on that enforcer or the isometry.

There is also a full-list randomized-test POVM independent of the LCU
implementation:
\[
E_j=w_jh_j,\qquad E_0=I-H.
\]
These effects sum to I, so their Naimark dilation is a single common
measurement with \(V^*D_jV=w_jh_j\) and
\(V^*(\sum_{j\ge1}D_j)V=H\), exactly. This returns one sampled violated
term, not a joint sharp assignment to all noncommuting \(h_j\). The seed
pair's rank-one incompatibility bound is respected: a term receives its
small mixture weight, not unit-strength \(P_\phi\) and \(P_\psi\) effects.
For the augmented BJSW pair, weights 1/4 are below the exact two-seed
threshold \(1/(1+1/(2\sqrt2))\). The algebraic dilation is not itself an
efficient circuit guarantee because \(\sqrt{I-H}\) may be global. It
nevertheless refutes a no-cloning impossibility based only on noncommutation
and aggregate order.

## Conclusion and unresolved boundary

No new no-cloning or information-disturbance obstruction follows from
combining (i) the full-list order bound with (ii) an exact image kernel. In
the complement-LCU construction, the single failure event is a compatible
measurement of \(2H-H^2\), its failure outcomes annihilate exactly the common
kernel, and the enforcer can be the exact gap-one projector \(I-VV^*\).
The flag does disturb intermediate-energy encoded states, by the exact
formula above; this is permitted by the interface. What remains unresolved
is whether the canonical LCU enforcer or another enforcer for this specific
image can meet polynomial row support/efficient row enumeration while
retaining the exact kernel and constant gap. The present compatibility
argument neither solves nor rules out that costed problem. A stronger theorem
would need a new hypothesis, such as constant-strength individual term
effects, nondisturbance/recoverability, or sparse physical C; it cannot be
deduced from the aggregate compression equations alone.
