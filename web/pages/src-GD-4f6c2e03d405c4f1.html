# Asynchronous parity schedules: a history lift and a causal obstruction

Author: gd_async_genesis. Date: 2026-10-16.

**Status.** The full asynchronous product-Q theorem is neither proved nor
refuted here. There is no legal parity-process or private-sign/Walsh
counterexample in this report. The two analytic results below are (i) an
explicit absorbing-history lift within the broad parity uncertainty class,
and (ii) a small exact obstruction to obtaining the scheduled bound from
entire type, real-axis isometry, reflection parity and deletion zeros alone.
The latter deliberately lacks own-gap chronology. Neither result is a
hardware qualification or a historical-originality assertion.

## 1. The target and its two information contracts

Let Z_i be commuting Hermitian involutions on a finite Hilbert space K,
with a specified initial joint sector s. An arbitrary reference may be
tensored throughout. During a prescribed finite deterministic schedule,

    H(t) = H_0(t) + sum_i V_i(t),
    [H_0,Z_i] = 0,
    {V_i,Z_i} = 0,
    [V_i,Z_j] = 0  (i != j),
    ||V_i(t)|| <= j_i(t).

The j_i are nonnegative integrable known envelopes. Propagators exist;
the sector-preserving baseline has no norm/action cap in this mathematical
contract. Each coordinate has its own prescribed complete-pinch nodes.
The node schedule and coefficients do not read private signs or latent
record outcomes. Pinch records, when retained, are isolated mathematical
outputs and are unavailable as feedback to the original dynamics.

Write a_ik for the envelope action on the kth own gap, and

    s(a) = sin^2(min(a,pi/2)),
    Q_i = 1 - product_k (1-s(a_ik)).

The open endpoint claim is the operator inequality

    P_s Phi^*(all selected terminal parities wrong) P_s
        <= (product_i Q_i) P_s.                         (T)

The stronger recorded event B_i is that coordinate i changes its label
on at least one own gap. Since its initial label is specified, B_i is
equivalently that at least one of its recorded node labels, including
the terminal label, differs from its initial label. Terminal wrong
implies B_i. The proposed stronger claim replaces the terminal event
in (T) by intersection_i B_i.

The literal private-sign receiving contract is narrower. It requires an
additive common dilation

    H_(f,b)(t) = H_base(t)
                + sum_i (-1)^f_i xi_i,b(t) A_i(t),

whose baseline and coefficients are independent of every selected seed
and selected refresh-bit path. There are no products of selected signs.
The virtual terms have the factorized form X_seed,i tensor A_i. A theorem
for the broad parity class transfers to that subclass. A counterexample
or a construction in the broad class need not retain this factorization.

## 2. Absorbing-history lift

### Theorem

For any finite prescribed broad-parity process as above, there is a
finite enlarged broad-parity process with the following properties:

1. Its terminal all-wrong effect, compressed to the initialized added
   register, is exactly the original all-ever-mismatched effect.
2. Its bounded odd interactions have norms no larger than the original
   ones and the same own-gap action budgets, after adding finitely many
   zero-interaction control slots if finite-duration controls are wanted.
3. Its selected parities are fixed commuting involutions, and all odd,
   cross-even and baseline-even identities hold on the **whole** enlarged
   Hilbert space, not merely on reachable states.
4. Its controls are predetermined coherent unitaries. No latent outcome
   is measured and fed back to the Hamiltonian.

Consequently, over the universal broad finite-bath class closed under
such zero-action padding, the endpoint product-Q claim and the stronger
all-ever product-Q claim are logically equivalent. This is a reduction;
it establishes neither claim.

### Registers and fixed new parities

For every internal own node (i,k), append a fresh flag bit r_ik initialized
to 0. There are exactly sum_i N_i such bits. Put

    C_i = (I + s_i Z_i)/2,       W_i = I-C_i,
    Pi_i = product_k |0><0|_(r_ik),
    G_i = C_i tensor Pi_i,
    Z_i' = 2 G_i - I.                                    (1)

All omitted tensor factors are identities. Thus Z_i' is correct (+1)
exactly when the original physical parity is correct and every own flag
is zero. The G_i commute, so the Z_i' are fixed commuting involutions.
Their initial joint + sector is precisely the old initial joint sector
tensored with all flags zero. Unequal dimensions of the new parity
sectors are allowed by the broad model.

### Hamiltonian decomposition and all-input identities

Define

    V_i' = Pi_i V_i,
    H_0' = H_0 + sum_i (I-Pi_i) V_i.                     (2)

Flags and physical operators commute. On Pi_i=1, Z_i' is s_i Z_i;
on Pi_i=0, Z_i' is the scalar -I and V_i' is zero. Hence

    {V_i',Z_i'}=0,       [V_i',Z_j']=0  (i!=j),
    ||V_i'|| <= ||V_i||.                                 (3)

Each summand (I-Pi_i)V_i commutes with Z_i', because G_i vanishes on
its flag subspace, and commutes with Z_j' for j!=i by the original
cross-parity identity. Therefore [H_0',Z_j']=0 for every j.
Moreover,

    H_0' + sum_i V_i' = H tensor I_flags.                (4)

The actual total physical evolution between nodes is the original one,
independent of the flags. The flag dependence in (2) is a decomposition
of that same total Hamiltonian. It is permissible because the broad
contract bounds only its specified odd terms and places no action cap
on the sector-preserving baseline.

### Node operation

Immediately before the own pinch at (i,k), apply the fixed controlled
copy unitary

    U_ik = C_i tensor I_(r_ik) + W_i tensor X_(r_ik).    (5)

It copies whether the original physical parity is wrong into its fresh
flag. The bit is still zero on entry because it has never been used.
The gate commutes with every Z_j' on the entire enlarged Hilbert space.
For j!=i this is the original commutation of physical parities and
disjoint flags. For j=i, the gate acts nontrivially only on W_i, where
G_i is zero regardless of any flag values. Thus it also preserves Z_i'.
After this copy apply the prescribed complete pinch D_(Z_i').

The copy is a legal baseline unitary, rather than outcome-dependent
classical feedback. For example it is generated by

    L_ik = (pi/2)(I-U_ik),

since U_ik is a Hermitian involution and exp(-i L_ik)=U_ik. This generator
commutes with all Z_j', and its norm is at most pi. A control window of
duration delta realizes it with baseline norm at most pi/delta and
integrated baseline action at most pi. All odd interactions can be set
to zero in these inserted windows. Finitely stretching the clock and
placing zero envelope there preserves every a_ik and all strict node
orders. This is a statement about the universal mathematical class;
it does not claim an unchanged implementation for a separately fixed
hardware envelope that forbids such windows.

### Equality of recorded probabilities

Let Delta_R dephase all flag bits in the computational basis. The flags
are never subsequently changed, except that each fresh bit is written
once. Equation (4) implies that future physical unitaries preserve every
flag label. Thus off-diagonal flag blocks never contribute to a final
observable diagonal in flags.

On a fresh initialized flag, copying (5) and then applying Delta_R is
exactly the retained projective instrument of the **original** Z_i pinch:

    rho -> C_i rho C_i tensor |0><0|
           + W_i rho W_i tensor |1><1|.                 (6)

On each such diagonal record block, the original physical Z_i label is
fixed. The new Z_i' label is consequently fixed as well: it is + only
if that physical label is correct and all earlier own flags are zero;
otherwise it is -. The additional pinch D_(Z_i') acts as the identity
on each diagonal record block. It therefore changes neither (6) nor
any later diagonal-record probability. Off-diagonal flag blocks remain
irrelevant because all later total Hamiltonians are (4) and all further
copies leave existing flag labels untouched.

Induction over the fixed nodes proves equality of the full original
diagonal record distribution and the lifted flag/physical distribution.
At the final time,

    Z_i' wrong  <=>  [some own internal label was wrong]
                       OR [the original terminal label is wrong]
                 <=> B_i.                               (7)

The equivalence is simultaneously valid for all selected i. For the
initialization isometry J psi = psi tensor |0...0>_R, the effect identity is

    J^* Phi_lift^*(product_i (I-G_i)) J
       = E_original(intersection_i B_i),                 (8)

compressed to the old specified initial joint sector. It holds for every
initial bath/reference state and hence as an operator identity there.

### Consequence and boundary

If (T) were universal for the broad class, applying it to the lift and
using (3), unchanged a_ik and (8) proves the all-ever product bound for
the original process. The reverse implication follows from event
inclusion without a lift. A legal all-ever violation therefore would
refute broad endpoint universality too. An abstract noncausal function
violation does not meet the antecedent.

The lift does **not** prove the same equivalence within the literal
private-sign subclass. The new odd term Pi_i V_i and baseline summand
(I-Pi_i)V_i need not be X_seed,i tensor A_i with one selected-path-
independent baseline. In particular moving an original selected-sign
term into a putative physical baseline can violate that contract.
The new OR parity instrument is also an additional exact ideal primitive,
not a claim that an original physical single-qubit controller supplies it.

If a broad application requires literal balanced Pauli representatives
rather than arbitrary commuting involutions, a finite dark extension
removes the sector-rank issue. Let d_chi be the joint sector dimensions of
the new Z_i', and D=max_chi d_chi. Add D-d_chi dark dimensions to every
joint sector, extend odd/baseline generators by zero and copy gates by the
identity there. All joint sector dimensions are then D. A fixed unitary
identifies the extended space with n parity qubits tensor C^D, with Z_i'
equal to the usual Pauli Z_i tensor I_D. The initialized old sector and
its effects are unchanged. This padding does not force the transformed
odd terms to be X_i tensor a seed-independent A_i, so the narrower Walsh
qualification remains open.

Paid resources of the mathematical reduction: sum_i N_i fresh coherent
flag bits, that many predetermined parity-controlled copy gates, their
finite control windows and baseline action (at most pi per gate in the
explicit construction), initialization/reset, and the stipulated exact
new parity pinches. No public flag readout is used. The broad theorem
has no cap on these baseline resources; a device comparison must pay them.

## 3. A small exact causal obstruction for function-only proofs

The following construction was invented independently in this worker.
It concerns two proposed **own-gap** variables of one coordinate.
Define the entire real vector

    F_0(x,y)  = 1-(1+cos x) cos^2(y/2),
    F_10(x,y) = sin x cos(y/2),
    F_01(x,y) = (1+cos x) sin y / 2,
    F_11(x,y) = 0.                                      (9)

All components have exponential type at most one in each variable.
F_10 is odd in x and even in y; F_01 is even in x and odd in y; F_0
is even in both. The mismatch component for a variable vanishes when
that variable is deleted. Put u=cos x and r=cos^2(y/2). On R^2,

    |F_10|^2+|F_01|^2
       = (1-u^2)r + (1+u)^2 r(1-r)
       = 1-[1-(1+u)r]^2.

Consequently the full vector has norm exactly one on the whole real
plane. It is an isometric scalar-to-vector function with independent
reflection grading, not merely a bounded scalar example.

Choose t in (0,pi/2) with sin t=3/5 and cos t=4/5. Then

    |F_10(t,t)|^2 = 81/250,
    |F_01(t,t)|^2 = 729/2500,
    |F_10|^2+|F_01|^2 = 1539/2500,
    1-cos^4 t = 1476/2500.                              (10)

The putative two-own-gap Q bound fails by 63/2500. There is no double-
mismatch component, so the same example defeats an abstract terminal-
odd bound as well as an abstract OR bound. Half frequencies in y are
permitted by the exponential-type hypothesis.

An explicit real-unitary origin makes the missing condition clear.
Let R_ab(theta) rotate the plane spanned by basis vectors e_a,e_b.
Up to a fixed sign on its zeroth output, the vector (9) is the first
column of

    R_02(y/2) R_01(x) R_02(y/2).                       (11)

Thus the y-dependent interaction occurs both before and after the
x-dependent interaction. Distinct own gaps of one coordinate are
disjoint chronologically ordered intervals, so (11) is **not** a legal
two-own-gap implementation. Interpreting x,y as two different selected
coordinates instead changes the target event: the joint all-flipped
component is F_11=0. That interpretation does not supply a counterexample
either.

The lesson is exact and limited: entire type, isometry, reflection parity
and deletion-null identities cannot by themselves recover scheduled Q.
Any successful proof must use additional causal/completion structure of
the genuine parity process. Equation (9) does not refute (T), the source
singleton theorem, or the private-sign receiving theorem.

## 4. What remains open after this investigation

The source Bernstein argument establishes one-gap products, synchronized
schedules and one-unpinched-coordinate cases. I checked its scalar
ingredient against Redheffer, Theorem 11, publisher PDF p. 832. That
classical real-entire derivative inequality supports the endpoint-phase
operator reduction. It does not supply a multigap causal tensorization.

For two genuinely asynchronous multiple-gap schedules, the missing
operation remains a causal operator argument retaining both own-gap
survival discounts, or an actual admissible finite process that violates
their product. The present searches tested only explicitly described
finite control families. Failure to find a violation is not a universal
proof or a quantitative confidence claim. The independent sink equality
is a lower witness only. The broad history lift identifies a useful
equivalence of targets over the universal broad class, while preserving
the narrower Walsh obligation as a separate question.
