## Result and interface

**Mathematical deduction: PASS for the precisely defined proof profiles below.**
The classical affine Frege system in Tables6–8 of Tabatabai–Jalali
arXiv:2601.22393v2 can be strengthened by arbitrarily many ordinary contraction
instances

    c(A,B) = (A -> (A -> B)) -> (A -> B)

provided each instance has either a syntactically certified negative-null
codomain, `S_B => bottom`, or a syntactically certified closed positive domain,
`=> T_A`. The conclusion is the same actual exponential lower bound for ordinary
Frege proof lines of their conservative hard family `Theta*_n`. The certificate
classes and all charged work are specified below. There is no bound on the
number, depth or reuse of admitted contractions. The source proof may be a DAG.

This is a restricted proof profile, not an ordinary substitution-closed new
propositional logic. Arbitrary uniform-substitution inference is not part of the
interface. Ordinary source affine schemata still have arbitrary substitutions;
the added contraction instance must pass its stated guard after instantiation.
Fresh acyclic definitional EF is covered only when the guard is tested **after
global definition unfolding**. A separate operational sequent consequence is
proved in Section6, with ordinary right contraction excluded.

The target is the source paper's `LJ_!`, with ordinary intuitionistic weakening
and contraction. Corollary10 already lower-bounds that target. Using its ordinary
contraction is therefore legitimate. The positive-domain branch is stronger:
its local simulation uses an affine intuitionistic target, with no contraction.
Our four-state certificates likewise require no ordinary contraction.

The unrestricted translation remains false. An exact Boolean model refutes
raw `t(c(p,linear0))`, even if the target is strengthened to classical logic.
An additional exact Heyting model refutes raw translation of the standard weak
contraction/divisibility axiom CWC. These failures constrain the next research
direction rather than merely documenting an unsuccessful finite search.

## 2. The local contraction lemma, with actual proof cost

Write `a=T_A,u=S_A,b=T_B,v=S_B`, and define

    d = (a->b) & (v->u)
    e = (a->d) & ((a*v)->u).

Then, by literal expansion of Definition11,

    t(c(A,B)) = (e->d) & ((a*v)->(a*(a*v))).

### Codomain branch

Suppose a previously proved exact endpoint is `v=>bottom`. Select `a->d` from
e and `a->b` from d. Two left-implication steps yield `e,a,a=>b`. One ordinary
target contraction gives `e,a=>b`, hence `e=>a->b`. Independently, cut the cached
`v=>bottom` with bottom elimination to get `v=>u`; weaken by e and discharge v.
Right additive conjunction gives `e=>d`, then implication gives `=>e->d`.

For the second conjunct, weakening and left fusion turn the same cached
endpoint into `a*v=>bottom`; bottom elimination yields `a*v=>a*(a*v)`. Discharge
the assumption and combine the two closed theorems by right conjunction.

This is a uniform proof for arbitrary formula slots a,u,b,v, from the **sole**
input `v=>bottom`; no Pair obligation is needed. The exact serialized certificate
has22rows/50formula occurrences including its one input row. Installing the
actual cached endpoint avoids charging a new input. Exactly one ordinary target
contraction occurs.

### Positive-domain branch

Suppose a previously proved exact endpoint is `=>a`. Select `a->d` from e and
apply that closed theorem to obtain `e=>d`; discharge e. Let `k=a*v`. Right
fusion of `=>a` with identity `k=>k` yields `k=>a*k`; discharge k and combine.
The certificate has9rows/14formula occurrences including its sole input row,
with no weakening, contraction, Pair or bang rule. The smaller construction
was suggested by `logic_gate14` and independently reconstructed here after a
less efficient27row derivation.

All certificate premises are earlier exact sequents, with multisets checked.
Formal formula slots can be substituted by arbitrary compound formulas. This
is schematic proof evidence, not evidence extrapolated from ground valuations.

## 3. A fixed syntactic certificate calculus

Four Boolean bits at every source syntax node represent explicit cached proofs:

| Bit | Exact endpoint |
|---|---|
| `t+` | `=>T_A` |
| `t-` | `T_A=>bottom` |
| `s+` | `=>S_A` |
| `s-` | `S_A=>bottom` |

The following deterministic rules define the guard. A bit is present only when
this table constructs its proof; the absence of a bit asserts no semantic
nonprovability. In particular this is not complete theorem recognition.
Use `a_t+` etc for child flags and `&`,`or` on flags for Boolean operations.

| Source node | `t+` | `t-` | `s+` | `s-` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| atom p |0|0|0|1|
| 1 or top |1|0|0|1|
| linear0 or bottom |0|1|1|0|
| A&B |`a_t+ & b_t+`|`a_t- or b_t-`|`a_s+ or b_s+`|`a_s- & b_s-`|
| A or B |`a_t+ or b_t+`|`a_t- & b_t-`|`a_s+ & b_s+`|`a_s- or b_s-`|
| A*B |`a_t+ & b_t+`|`a_t- or b_t-`|`(a_t- or b_s+) & (b_t- or a_s+)`|`(a_t+ & b_s-) or (b_t+ & a_s-)`|
| A->B |`(a_t- or b_t+) & (b_s- or a_s+)`|`(a_t+ & b_t-) or (b_s+ & a_s-)`|`a_t+ & b_s+`|`a_t- or b_s-`|
| !A |`a_t+`|`a_t-`|`a_t-`|`a_t+`|

Proof construction is elementary and exact. For example, `=>x` plus
`y=>bottom` gives `(x->y)=>bottom` by left implication; `x=>bottom` gives
`=>x->y` by bottom elimination and right implication. Falsehood of one product
factor uses weakening and left fusion. Bang theoremhood promotes a closed
theorem, so its promotion side condition holds; bang refutability uses
dereliction. Additive clauses are their exact left/right rules.

Topologically process each source DAG node once, caching each of at most four
endpoints. All105 local schematic cases (four binary source constructors with
five child states each, plus bang's five cases) have serialized checked proofs.
Each constructor adds at most9rows/16formula occurrences. Constants add at most
that bound; one reusable bottom identity adds2occurrences. Therefore all cached
states cost at most `16V+2` occurrences for V input DAG nodes. No named input
remains in the actual inductive cache.

The possible computed bit sets are exactly among

    {}, {s-}, {t-}, {t+,s-}, {t-,s+}.

The initial states have this property and direct substitution of these five
sets into each displayed rule preserves it. This is a finite inductive proof
over the fixed signature, independently inspectable in the table. In
particular `t+` implies `s-`, `s+` implies `t-`, and `t-` and `s-` never coexist
in this calculus. The tempting converse `t-=>s+` is false: `p->linear0` has
only `t-`. It was rejected during this audit.

The simpler negative-null grammar

    R ::= p | 1 | top | R&R | R or F | F or R | F->R
    F ::= any source formula

lies inside `s-` and strictly contains the authors' fully conservative grammar:
the antecedent F is unrestricted and one null disjunct suffices. The full table
also admits `!1`, `1*p`, and `linear0->linear0`. It rejects `linear0`, `p->linear0`
and `p*q` as null codomains. Its `t+` branch admits linear par `p+q`, written
`(p->linear0)->q`, and more generally `(F->linear0)->G` when F and G have `s-`.
This matches the sibling's independent proper-domain example.

## 4. Typed skeleton compression and the actual hard-family transfer

**Lemma.** An L-line proof under the mixed guard `s-(B) or t+(A)` has an
equivalent same-target L-line proof with common syntax DAG `O(L+|H|)` that still
passes those guards. H is its actual final formula.

Freeze the same rule skeleton as in cycle14: line variables, fixed copies of
each used schema, premise/conclusion equations, and the final rigid term H.
Ordinary constructor unification has `N=O(L+|H|)` nodes. The original proof is
a finite ground solution, so the quotient has no mismatching constructors or
constructor cycle. For every guarded contraction choose the originally
satisfied branch and demand that bit at its A or B class.

At a quotient class with a forced constructor, use its original ground term's
local proof of every demanded bit to choose the finite-table witness and add
its required child demands. There are at most four bits per class; repeated
demands are cached. Traversal therefore adds only `O(N)` state annotations and
no new source constructors. The original ground solution certifies joint
compatibility of all demands even when several schema variables unify.

For a constructor-free class, one atom is not always sufficient. Complete it
as follows: use source1 if `t+` is demanded; use source linear0 if `t-` or `s+`
is demanded; otherwise use a fresh source atom. The five-state invariant makes
these choices jointly satisfy every demanded bit. Required `s-` together with
`t+` is satisfied by1; `s-` cannot be jointly required with `t-` or `s+` because
the original ground term would then have an impossible classifier state.
Final H stays rigid. Inductively all chosen local witnesses remain valid.
The completion adds only a fixed number of constant/atom nodes.

This is an existence/compression proof; it does not claim an algorithm can
read tree-sized enormous source formulas in time polynomial in L. With the
skeleton itself supplied, its unification and state graph are compact. There
is no object-level substitution inference or new extension atom.

Now let H be `Theta*_n`, whose ordinary tree size is `n^{O(1)}`. The frozen
cycle14 compiler contributes `C0(V+L)` target occurrences. Cached states add
at most `16V+2`; each added contraction adds at most50occurrences. The positive
domain branch is cheaper, so this bound covers either choice. Append the
primary conservative-target repair, safely charged `O(|H|^2)`, and one cut.
After typed compression,

    l_LJ_!(proof of Theta*_n) <= C1 L + C2 |Theta*_n|^2.

Corollary10 supplies the actual lower bound `2^{n^{Omega(1)}}`, so the mixed
guarded source proof has exponentially many ordinary Frege lines. There is no
unfolded translated-tree-size term on the upper-bound side. This result is
stronger than a bound obtained by charging every contraction through a
proof-tree unfolding: all guarded contractions are free of a contraction
budget. It is still a proper restricted class of classical source proofs.

The source strengthening is genuine. Already `c(p,q)` is admitted by the
codomain branch but not an affine theorem: in the Łukasiewicz affine algebra,
`p=1/2,q=0` gives its source value1/2. Par `p+q` is also not uniformly source
contractible: `p=q=1/4` gives par1/2 and `par->par*par` value1/2. Avron's uniform
admissibility classification agrees with this distinction. Target theoremhood
of T_A does not imply source theoremhood of arbitrary A; final repair is used
only on the specified conservative hard formula.

## 5. Definitional EF: successful interface and decisive failed alternative

With fresh acyclic definitions `e<->F`, e absent earlier/from F/from the final
formula, first globally unfold all definitions. Require every added source
contraction's guard on its expanded operands. The expanded proof has a syntax
DAG of size `O(S)` when S is input EF symbol size. Expanded definitions are
reflexive equivalences, each replaceable by a fixed number of ordinary affine
Frege lines. The number of expanded Frege lines is `O(E)`, with E input EF
lines. Its added contractions still pass the guard by definition of the input
interface. The preceding compiler gives the EF symbol bound; typed Frege
compression of the expanded proof gives the EF line bound. Both are exponential
for `Theta*_n` in this **guarded-after-unfolding** EF profile.

The raw atomic-alias alternative fails completely. Define fresh e to name any
F. Its unexpanded source-atom state has `s-`, so a raw checker admits `c(A,e)`.
The definition theorem and fixed implication congruence replace e by F in the
fixed contraction context, deriving arbitrary `c(A,F)` at constant line cost.
Thus raw alias admission recovers unrestricted contraction. The independent
`rounding_prior` worker has an exact18row alias macro; this note does not claim
to have replayed that macro. Our expanded-guard proof never uses raw Chu
translation of definitions, whose failure was established in cycle14.

No ordinary substitution-closed logic or ordinary unrestricted EF lower bound
is claimed by this guarded extension. No F-versus-EF separation follows from

## 6. A precise sequent contraction profile

The cycle14 sequent skeleton/context argument extends to the source affine

    Gamma,A,A => Delta
    ------------------ C_L
     Gamma,A => Delta

only when one specified member of Delta has state `s-`. Ordinary right
contraction remains excluded; native exponential rules stay available.
Empty Delta consequently fails this added guard.
The same argument also allows the alternative guard `t+(A)`, using the
positive-domain branch; for that explicitly stated variant Delta may be empty.

Pack the nonprincipal antecedents into `G=*Gamma` and the succedent into
`D=+Delta`. Define par by `X+Y=(X->linear0)->Y`, or use its primary equivalent
definition `neg(negX*negY)`. For the first choice the table gives

    t-(X->linear0) = s-(X),
    s-(X+Y) = s-(X) or s-(Y).

The first equality uses `t+=>s-` and the known states of linear0. Hence the
specified succedent member proves `s-(D)` for any fixed ordering/grouping, and
`s-(G->D)` holds regardless of G. A finite affine currying/permutation macro
turns the already proved interpretation `(G*A*A)->D` into
`A->(A->(G->D))`. Use the legal guarded instance `c(A,G->D)` and MP; uncurry
to `(G*A)->D`. These are fixed constant-size schemata in slots G,A,D. They
introduce no dependence on the actual unfolded context length.

Freeze the original sequent rules, occurrence wiring and the chosen guarded
succedent occurrence. With ell the **total written formula occurrences**,

has at most twice as many premise slots as conclusion slots. The cycle14

The resulting target occurrence cost is `O(ell^4+|H|^2)`, so `Theta*_n` has

## 7. Standard weak contraction: an actual obstruction

Arthan–Oliva's primary CWC schema is

    A*(A->B) -> B*(B->A).

Let A=`p->linear0`, B=q, and use a three-element Heyting algebra `0<1<2` as
a sound target model. Erase bang by interpreting it as identity, fusion as
meet, both truth constants as2, both false constants as0, and
`x->y=2` if `x<=y`, otherwise y. Put p=q=1, D=N=0. Direct Chu evaluation gives

| Formula | T | S |
|---|---:|---:|
| A |0|1|
| B |1|0|
| A->B |2|0|
| B->A |0|1|
| A*(A->B) |0|1|
| B*(B->A) |0|2|
| CWC |1|0|

The translated theorem has value1 rather than2. This algebra validates all
ordinary intuitionistic structural rules and also CWC itself, since
`x & (x->y)=x & y` in every Heyting algebra. Thus adding full target contraction
or target CWC cannot repair the unchanged Chu template.

The more elementary unrestricted instance `c(p,linear0)` translates to a
formula whose T is p in the two-element Boolean algebra. At p=0 it is false,
so even full classical target reasoning cannot repair that raw translation.
These exact sound-model counterexamples prove nonderivability, as opposed to
successful finite valuations, which would not prove universal derivability.

Known negative translations are a different interface. Their success does not
establish an economical proof of `Theta*_n` itself. Indeed polynomial classical
proofs become polynomial intuitionistic proofs of the usual negative
translation; a polynomial intuitionistic repair from that translation back to
the present hard formula would contradict the established lower bound.
Any changed-translation route must identify a different target hard family or
an explicit new lower-bound invariant, rather than assume final repair.
