THEOREM

For each integer K>=4 there is a finite algebraic state family E_K on
H_K, dim H_K=2^K+K, with one explicitly specified weighted finite prior,
such that, using half trace distance and nats,

 1/(6K^2) <= b2(E_K) <= 1/K^2,
 floor(log_2 K)/(10K^2) <= e(E_K)
                         <= [ceil(log_2 K)+1]/K^2,
 ln 2/12 <= R(E_K) <= C(E_K) <= 3 ln 2/4.

Here b2 is the infimum worst marginal error of one full-domain CPTP
H_K -> H_K tensor H_K; e is the infimum worst error of one full-domain
EB H_K -> H_K; and R is the infimum over closed-convex-hull centers of
the worst observational divergence. No label is supplied to a channel.
The true MAX-PRIOR Holevo capacity is exactly

 C(E_K)=K^(-2) sum_(i=0)^(K-1) ln(2^i+1) ->(ln 2)/2.

Consequently b2=Theta(K^-2), e=Theta(log K/K^2), and R is uniformly
bounded above and away from zero. For every fixed alpha>=2 and every
finite constant A, the bound e^2<=A(R+1)b2^alpha fails on this sequence.
The boundary alpha=2 is included. This construction does not decide any
exponent alpha in (0,2). It is a mathematical existence sequence, with
exploding spaces and exact-data costs, not an efficient acquisition claim.
The states below are not faithful; faithfulness was not required in the
supplied statement and is not silently assumed in this theorem.

1. EXACT FINITE ALGEBRAIC FAMILY AND SUFFICIENT MOMENTS

Let w=1/K, a=w/K=1/K^2, N_i=2^i for i=0,...,K-1, m=2^(K-1). Include
a vacuum sector indexed -1, with N_(-1)=0 and D_0=1. Set

 H_K=C|vac> directsum_(i=0)^(K-1) Sym^(N_i)(C^2),
 rho_psi=(1-w)|vac><vac|+a directsum_i psi^(tensor N_i),

where psi in a density expression abbreviates |psi><psi|. Thus the fixed
flag probabilities are p_(-1)=1-w and p_i=a for active i>=0.
The flag law is independent of the unknown direction. Each active
conditional state is a pure copy tensor. The dimension is
1+sum_i(2^i+1)=2^K+K.

Use an exact weighted qubit projective design of order T=2m=2^K, not
merely m+1. This covers EVERY input/output pair moment N_i+N_j used
against arbitrary EB flag changes. Here is an exact algebraic construction.
Let q=m+1 and r_phase=2m+1. Take q-node Gaussian quadrature for the
uniform weight on [0,1], with algebraic nodes y_u in (0,1) and positive
algebraic normalized weights v_u. Let

 psi_(u,k)=(sqrt(y_u) exp(2pi i k/r_phase), sqrt(1-y_u)),
 lambda_(u,k)=v_u/r_phase, k=0,...,r_phase-1.

There are (m+1)(2m+1) weighted labels. The degree-q monic polynomial L_q
orthogonal to all lower-degree polynomials on [0,1] has rational
coefficients: its coefficients solve the positive-definite rational
moment Gram system. If it had fewer than q distinct interior
sign-changing roots, multiplying it by the product of the corresponding
linear factors would give a polynomial of degree less than q whose
product with L_q has one sign and nonzero integral, contradicting
orthogonality. Hence all q roots are simple and interior. Let l_u be
their degree-(q-1) cardinal Lagrange polynomials and set
v_u=integral_0^1 l_u(y)dy. For every polynomial of degree <=2q-1,
division by L_q leaves a remainder of degree <q and a quotient of degree
<q. Orthogonality removes the quotient term, and cardinal interpolation
of the remainder proves the quadrature formula. Applying it to l_u^2
gives v_u=integral l_u^2>0; applying it to one gives sum_u v_u=1.
All nodes and weights are algebraic. Thus this quadrature is exact
through degree 2q-1=2m+1; no design-existence oracle is assumed.

For tensor order T, phase averaging kills all different occupation
counts because r_phase>T. For matching count k, the normalized occupation
diagonal coefficient is

 binom(T,k) integral_0^1 y^k(1-y)^(T-k)dy=1/(T+1).

The quadrature integrates this exactly. Therefore

 sum_x lambda_x psi_x^(tensor T)=P_sym^T/(T+1).

Taking partial traces supplies every lower moment through T. Equivalently
the weighted family matches Haar projective integration for all polynomial
expressions of bidegree at most (T,T) arising below. Define E_K to consist
of rho_psi for these finitely many directions. Every state has algebraic
entries in the normalized occupation basis. Repeated directions, if any,
may be combined with their positive weights; this does not change moments.

2. FULL-DOMAIN BROADCASTER AND EXACT DISPLAYED ERROR

Let P_v,P_i be the orthogonal input sector projectors. For i>=1, grouping
the N_i tensor coordinates into equal halves gives an isometry

 V_i:Sym^(N_i)(C^2)->Sym^(N_(i-1))(C^2) tensor Sym^(N_(i-1))(C^2)

with V_i psi^tensor N_i=psi^tensor N_(i-1) tensor psi^tensor N_(i-1).
It is an isometry on the whole sector, not just the promised vectors.
In occupation basis its entries are square roots of the multinomial
Vandermonde ratios, so they are algebraic. Embed the outputs in H_K.

For ALL X in B(H_K) define

 B(X)=Tr[(P_v+P_0)X]|vac,vac><vac,vac|
      +sum_(i=1)^(K-1) V_i P_i X P_i V_i^*.

Each summand is CP. The replacement term has trace Tr[(P_v+P_0)X],
the isometric terms have traces Tr(P_i X), and the sum is Tr X. Thus
B is a full-domain CPTP channel, with a finite algebraic Kraus description.
Input flag coherences are pinched, and no unknown label is supplied.

On rho_psi, both marginals have vacuum mass 1-w+a and the correct pure
state of weight a in each flag 0,...,K-2, with no top flag. Their difference
from the input is

 a|vac><vac|-a psi^tensor m in the top flag.

The supports are orthogonal, so the half trace error is EXACTLY a=1/K^2.
The outputs are correlated; a joint product-state approximation is not
required or claimed. This proves b2<=1/K^2.

3. LOWER BOUND ON OPTIMAL b2, WITH THE VACUUM ACCOUNTED FOR

Define a full-domain CPTP decoder D:H_K->C^2 by pinching the flag,
tracing all but one coordinate in each active sector, and replacing a
vacuum input by I/2. Then

 D(rho_psi)=(1-w)I/2+w psi,
 Tr[psi D(rho_psi)]=(1-w)/2+w.

Let an arbitrary broadcaster have worst half marginal error b0. Decode
both outputs with D. Its bottom active-input restriction is an arbitrary
qubit CPTP Q:C^2->C^2 tensor C^2. Its unnormalized Choi J has Tr J=2.
Write P_AB,P_AC for normalized two-dimensional EPR projectors tensored
with the unused output identity. The design's two-copy moment gives
average two-marginal pure score Tr(J Omega), where

 Omega=[I+P_AB+P_AC]/6.

The EPR projection ranges have overlap matrix I/2, so the norm of their
sum is 3/2 and ||Omega||=5/12. Consequently the bottom sector's average
two-marginal decoded score is <=(5/12)Tr J=5/6.

For a vacuum input the decoded outputs are independent of psi, so the
one-copy design moment gives score average EXACTLY 1/2. Every other active
input sector has score at most one. Averaging the full promised mixture
therefore gives an actual score at most

 (1-w)/2+a(5/6)+(K-1)a
 =(1-w)/2+w-a/6.

Trace contraction and testing against psi say each decoded marginal score
is at least its target value (1-w)/2+w minus b0. Hence b0>=a/6. This
applies to every competing full-domain channel, with arbitrary outputs and
flag changes, and proves b2>=1/(6K^2). Displayed error was not mistaken
for optimality.

4. ARBITRARY EB CHANNELS INDUCE A LEGAL FLAG-FLOW COUPLING

Fix ANY full-domain EB Lambda with worst error epsilon on E_K. A finite
measure-and-prepare representation is

 Lambda(X)=sum_y Tr(M_y X)sigma_y,

where M_y>=0, sum_y M_y=I and sigma_y are arbitrary normalized densities
on H_K. They may be mixed and may have coherences between output flags.
This representation follows from a separable finite Choi decomposition;
finite dimensionality permits a finite decomposition. No purity or
flag-preserving restriction on Lambda is imposed.

Compress M_y to input sector i, obtaining M_(y,i), and compress sigma_y
to output sector j, obtaining the unnormalized positive sigma_(y,j).
Let D_N=N+1 for qubit symmetric sectors, including N=0 for the vacuum.
Define nonnegative flows

 f_ij = [p_i/D_(N_i)] sum_y Tr(M_(y,i))Tr(sigma_(y,j)).

Each row sums to p_i. Its column sum q_j=sum_i f_ij is the average output
flag probability over the SAME finite design. This follows from the
N_i moment. Thus q is an actual probability law, not an invented coupling.
The target flag law is p. Flag measurement is contractive in half trace
distance, so TV(q,p)<=epsilon, even if the output flag law depends on psi
before averaging.

For active output flag j, the average score against psi^tensor N_j coming
from input sector i equals

 [p_i/D_(N_i+N_j)] sum_y
   Tr[(M_(y,i) tensor sigma_(y,j))P_sym^(N_i+N_j)]
 <= [D_(N_i)/D_(N_i+N_j)] f_ij.

The design degree 2m covers every required moment. Positivity and
0<=P_sym<=I prove the inequality for arbitrary MIXED prepared blocks.
For a vacuum output j=-1 the score equals its flag mass. Define

 ell_ij=N_j/(N_i+N_j+1) for j>=0, and ell_i,-1=0.

The effect F_psi=P_v+sum_(j>=0)psi^tensor N_j, block by block, is a
projector satisfying Tr(F_psi rho_psi)=1. Therefore each state's trace
error is at least 1-Tr(F_psi Lambda(rho_psi)). Averaging and using the
score bounds gives

 epsilon >= L_flow :=sum_ij f_ij ell_ij.                 (A)

Together, (A), the row sums, and TV(q,p)<=epsilon cover mixed outputs,
cross-sector effects, output flag changes and possible vacuum assistance.

5. A POINTWISE DUAL POTENTIAL FOR THE LOGARITHMIC LOWER BOUND

Let h=floor(log_2 K), gamma=1/(8K), beta=h/(8K). The flag indices run
from -1 to K-1. I claim for EVERY input/output pair

 ell_ij >= beta*1_(j>=0)+gamma(j-i).                    (B)

For a vacuum output j=-1, the right side is gamma(-1-i)<=0. For a
vacuum input and active j, ell_-1,j=2^j/(2^j+1)>=1/2, while the right
side is <=gamma(h+K)<=3/16 because h<=K/2.

For active i,j write s=i-j. Then

 ell_ij=1/[2^s+1+2^(-j)] >=1/(2^s+2).

If s>=h, (B)'s right side is nonpositive. If s<0, the displayed lower
bound is >=1/3 while gamma(h-s)<=gamma(h+K)<=3/16. If 0<=s<h, put
u=h-s, an integer in {1,...,h}. Since 2^h<=K,

 1/(2^s+2)>=1/(K2^(-u)+2)>=u/(8K).

The last inequality is equivalent to u2^(-u)+2u/K<=8; in fact the
left side is <=2 because u<=h<=K/2 and u<=2^u. This proves (B) in all
cases. The bound h<=K/2 follows from h<=2^(h-1)<=K/2 for h>=1.

Sum (B) against the legal flows. The row sums give

 L_flow>=beta q_active+gamma[sum_j j q_j-sum_i i p_i].

The target flag indices are the same at input and output. Their range
has length K, so TV(q,p)<=epsilon gives

 q_active>=w-epsilon,
 sum_j j(q_j-p_j)>=-K epsilon.

Hence

 epsilon>=L_flow>=beta(w-epsilon)-gamma K epsilon,
 epsilon>=beta w/(1+beta+gamma K).

Since beta<=1/16 and gamma K=1/8, the denominator is <=5/4. Therefore

 epsilon >=h/(10K^2).

This holds for EVERY EB channel and proves the claimed optimum lower
bound. The logarithm arises from a global flag-transport constraint;
one-copy decoding alone would not establish it.

6. MATCHING FULL-DOMAIN EB UPPER BOUND

Let r_shift=ceil(log_2 K), which is <=K-1 for K>=2. Map the vacuum and
the r_shift smallest active input flags to the vacuum. On input flag
i>=r_shift, perform the finite covariant pure-state estimation POVM

 M_(x,i)=(N_i+1)lambda_x psi_x^tensor N_i

and prepare psi_x^tensor M_i in output flag i-r_shift, where
M_i=2^(i-r_shift). The design moment N_i makes these POVM effects sum
to that input identity. Combined with the low-flag replacement this is
one finite, algebraic, full-domain EB channel. Its output label is a
measurement outcome, not the unknown input label.

For arbitrary psi the output density on that low flag is exactly the
Haar-covariant density because the finite design matches moment
N_i+M_i<=2m. Its score on psi^tensor M_i is

 F_i=(N_i+1)/(N_i+M_i+1).

That coherent vector is an eigenvector of the output density. To see
this directly, rotate psi to |0>. Haar phase averaging diagonalizes the
prepared state in the occupation basis, with coherent eigenvalue F_i;
the finite design channel is the identical operator and rotates back.
Consequently its HALF trace error to the pure target is exactly

 1-F_i=M_i/(N_i+M_i+1)<=2^(-r_shift),

rather than only a square-root fidelity estimate.

The output vacuum has excess mass r_shift*a, the top r_shift target
flags are absent, and the retained low flags have the correct weight a.
Their orthogonal direct-sum differences give total half trace error

 r_shift*a+a sum_(i>=r_shift)(1-F_i)
 <=[r_shift+(K-r_shift)2^(-r_shift)]/K^2
 <=[r_shift+1]/K^2.

This proves the matching e upper order with one legal channel on all
inputs. Together with the transport lower bound, e=Theta(log K/K^2).

7. TRUE MAX-PRIOR CAPACITY AND CONVEX-CENTER OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS

Every promised state has entropy H(p), the entropy of its fixed flag law.
For any prior the mean has the SAME flag law; its conditional active
blocks have entropy <=ln(N_i+1). Thus Holevo information is at most
a sum_i ln(N_i+1). The design prior attains all conditional block means
I_(N_i)/(N_i+1) simultaneously. Therefore MAX-PRIOR capacity is exactly

 C_K=a sum_i ln(2^i+1).

It tends to (ln 2)/2: sum_i i=K(K-1)/2 and sum_i ln(1+2^(-i)) is bounded.
The elementary inequality 2^i+1<=2^(i+1) gives
C_K<=ln 2*(K+1)/(2K)<=3 ln 2/4 for K>=2.

Choose the actual convex-hull center

 sigma_*=(1-w)P_v+a directsum_i I_(N_i)/(N_i+1).

For any effect F, put alpha_v=<vac|F|vac> and
alpha_i=<psi^tensor N_i|F_i|psi^tensor N_i>, all in [0,1]. Then

 p_F=(1-w)alpha_v+a sum_i alpha_i,
 q_F>= (1-w)alpha_v+a sum_i alpha_i/(N_i+1).

The log-sum inequality gives
p_F ln(p_F/q_F)<=a sum_i alpha_i ln(N_i+1)<=C_K.
The vacuum contribution is zero because its sector dimension is one.
This proves R_K<=C_K with a single valid center. It is a max-prior/
convex-center argument, not a uniform-prior accessible-information bound.

For the nonzero lower bound let sigma be ANY center in closed conv(E_K).
Every active sector still has trace a. Set j0=ceil(K/2), ell=floor(K/2)
and let J={j0,...,K-1}. Use the effect

 F_psi,J=directsum_(j in J) psi^tensor N_j,

with zero on the other flags. Its target probability is p_J=ell*a.
The common finite design gives average center probability

 sum_x lambda_x Tr(F_(psi_x),J sigma)
 =a sum_(j in J)1/(2^j+1)<=a 2^(1-j0).

Jensen for -ln q, with q=0 interpreted as infinity, yields

 sup_(rho in E_K)D_obs(rho||sigma)
 >=(ell/K^2)[(j0-1)ln 2+ln ell].

For K>=4, ell>=K/3 and j0-1>=K/4, so this is >=ln 2/12. The
argument holds for EVERY convex-hull center, including singular centers.
It proves the required nonzero lower radius. A one-top-flag test would
give only order 1/K and would not justify this assertion.

8. POWER-LAW REFUTATION AND SCOPE

For alpha>=2 and any fixed finite A, combine the e lower bound, b2 upper
bound and R upper bound:

 e_K^2/[(R_K+1)b2_K^alpha]
 >= [h^2/(100(1+3 ln 2/4))] K^(2alpha-4) ->infinity.

At alpha=2 the diverging factor is h^2; for alpha>2 the power of K
also diverges. This proves the exact proposed obstruction, with uniformly
bounded nonzero radius and even bounded true max-prior Holevo capacity.
No conclusion for alpha in (0,2) follows: the displayed lower ratio then
decays rather than diverges. No C4 or general upper theorem is needed.

FAILURE LEDGER

- A one-copy decoded EB test gives only a sum_i 1/(N_i+2)=Theta(1)
  times a, hence Theta(K^-2), and misses the required logarithm.
- Coherent loss alone is insufficient because sending every input to the
  vacuum has zero active coherent loss. Flag TV must be combined with it.
- A displayed broadcasting error alone says nothing about the optimum;
  the decoded bottom-sector Choi bound supplies the matching lower order.
- A top-flag radius test gives a ln(m+1)=Theta(1/K), which vanishes.
  Testing a whole upper band of flags is essential for a nonzero radius.
- A chosen prior's small information is not the capacity. The entropy
  upper bound and simultaneous finite moment means prove the max-prior
  value and the valid convex-hull center explicitly.
- A degree-m+1 design suffices for one-copy estimation but not all
  arbitrary input/output sector pairs. Degree 2m is used throughout.
- The theorem does not claim faithful states or an efficient design/channel
  compiler. All spaces, exact algebraic nodes, bases, flags and matrices
  are charged input data; none of the asymptotic spaces is materialized.
