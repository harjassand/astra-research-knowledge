Claim: Fix D>=2, n=D^2-1, r_0=sqrt((D-1)/D), and H_0(D), the real Hilbert space of traceless Hermitian D by D matrices with Hilbert-Schmidt inner product. Let K_D={A_rho=(rho-I/D)/r_0: rho>=0, Tr(rho)=1}. For supplied unitaries U_1,...,U_t with V_t=U_t...U_1 and any B in the Hilbert-Schmidt unit ball, the target bit law is p_*(+|A,U_1,...,U_t,B)=1/2(1+delta Tr(B V_t A V_t^*)). For fixed D, 0<delta<=1/n and 0<epsilon<delta/(2n), the minimum number of classical Markov states that uniformly approximates all preparations, terminal queries, and words of length at most T is Theta_(D,delta,epsilon)(T^(D-1)). The static positive factorization size is exactly D^2; the quantum realization uses Hilbert-space dimension D; the state-count law corresponds to (D-1)log_2 T+O_(D,delta,epsilon)(1) classical bits.
Interface: The preparation rho=I/D+r_0 A ranges over all density matrices; B ranges over the Hilbert-Schmidt unit ball; controls are arbitrary supplied unitary words; the binary effect is E_B=1/2(I+delta B/r_0). The classical simulator must meet uniform additive error epsilon for all inputs and all word lengths <=T, with arbitrary measurable time/current-unitary stochastic transitions. Only the finite internal state stores history; no persistent seed, correlated external noise or stored continuous state is free.
