THEOREMS AND INPUT MODEL

Let d>=2, Phi:M_d->M_d be CPTP, unital, and Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint.
Supply one CPTP B:M_d->M_d tensor M_d with both marginals Phi. Hermitian
quadratic-form order uses ordinary HS or tau=Tr/d; rescaling is immaterial.
For every delta>0 there is an explicitly finite pure canonical EB Psi_delta
such that

  I-Psi_delta <=4(I-Phi)+delta I                           (T1)

on the FULL real Hermitian space. No spectral-gap premise is needed.

For a Turing-effective statement, B's unnormalized Choi matrix is supplied
as exact rational-complex entries, each numerator/denominator of at most L
bits, with its structural promises as above. Supply rational 0<delta<=1.
Then an exact CLASSICAL Las Vegas compiler returns a rational finite pure
canonical channel satisfying (T1), with at most

  T+d outcomes, T=ceil(64d^2/delta^2).                     (T2)

Every returned channel is certified by exact rational PSD checks. The
algorithm terminates almost surely, with at most two batches in expectation.
Its expected bit runtime, random-bit count, and output bit length are
polynomial in d, 1/delta, and the matrix/accuracy input bit lengths. The
admittedly large explicit bounds and
representation/gate distinctions are in COSTS_AND_STABILITY.txt. There are
no physical quantum sampling calls or hidden full-record enumeration.

For arbitrary exactly supplied real B, the finite matrix construction is
valid algebraically, but Turing computability requires a representation or
oracle for its entries. An abstract exact-real channel is not a bit input.
The d=1 case is the identity channel; delta>=1 is covered by any canonical
EB because 0<=Psi<=I and I-Phi>=0 in the self-adjoint setting.

1. A FIXED RATIONAL LEAF POVM: NO PHI EIGENVECTOR ACQUISITION

Put r=2d-1. Take the d standard basis pure projectors and, for every j<k,
the four projectors onto (e_j +/- e_k)/sqrt(2) and
(e_j +/- i e_k)/sqrt(2). Call the full list pi_z, z=1..R, R=dr.
They have rational-complex entries and sum to r I. The leaf POVM is

  F_z=pi_z/r.

Its canonical channel S_0(A)=sum_z pi_z Tr(pi_z A)/r fixes I. Direct
matrix-unit computation gives its eigenvalues on the traceless space:

  1/r on off-diagonal Hermitian modes,
  (d-1)/r on traceless diagonal modes.                    (1)

Thus S_0>=I/r on that space. If {E_b} is any tau-orthonormal traceless
basis, let a_z,b=Tr(pi_z E_b) and take x_0=S_0^(-1)a_z in these coordinates.
At reference I/d the outcome weight is 1/(dr), and

  E[a_z x_0^T]=I, E[x_0]=0,
  sum_z (x_0)_b F_z=E_b,
  N_0=E[x_0x_0^T]=S_0^(-1)<=r I.                         (2)

These estimators are used ONLY in the proof. The physical/compiler leaf
POVM is fixed in advance and needs no diagonalization or inversion of Phi.

2. THE FINITE C4 BOUND AVOIDS THE CRITICAL SPECTRUM

Symmetrize supplied B by averaging with its output swap. This preserves
Phi and rationality. Let T_m be the full depth-m binary tree of B, K=2^m.
The coarse leaf-record POVM pulls back through T_m to the input.

For analysis only choose a tau-orthonormal eigenbasis of Phi, and select
those traceless modes with lambda_a>3/4. On them Lambda is positive,
2lambda_a^2>9/8, and the selected leaf estimator covariance obeys N_0<=rI.
Set x_m=Lambda^(-m)2^(-m)sum_leaves x_0. Its exact operator pullback is E_a.

For rho_BC=B(I/d), define C_ab=Tr[rho_BC(E_a tensor E_b)]. Square positivity
of A tensor I-I tensor A gives C<=I. Product-subtree first moments, WITHOUT
independence of their states or outcomes, give the sibling moment C and

  N_m=(1/2)Lambda^(-1)(N_(m-1)+C)Lambda^(-1).

The same order-preserving congruence recursion, starting from (2), yields

  N_m<=D_m, (D_m)_aa=r t_a+(1-t_a)/a_a,
  a_a=2lambda_a^2-1, t_a=(2lambda_a^2)^(-m).              (3)

For any refinement of the coarse input POVM, retaining its estimator, the
FULL posterior covariance S_m has crossmoment J with x_m, where J embeds
the selected coordinates in all d^2-1 traceless coordinates. Therefore

  [[S_m,J],[J^T,N_m]]>=0,
  S_m>=J D_m^(-1)J^T.                                   (4)

N_m is invertible because J is injective. On each selected mode,

  1/(D_m)_aa=a_a/[1+t_a(r a_a-1)]
             >=a_a-r t_a>=a_a-r(8/9)^m.                 (5)

If r a_a-1>=0, the loss is
a_a t_a(r a_a-1)/[1+t_a(r a_a-1)]<=r a_a^2 t_a<=r t_a;
if it is negative, the loss is nonpositive. Also

  a_a-(4lambda_a-3)=2(1-lambda_a)^2>=0.

On all omitted traceless modes lambda<=3/4, the desired 4lambda-3 is
nonpositive, while any canonical channel is HS-positive. Equation (4)
is a full block order, not just separate diagonal bounds, so altogether

  Psi_m >=4Phi-3I-r(8/9)^m I.                            (6)

Identity is fixed exactly and has zero cross terms. Negative eigenvalues,
lambda=-1, lambda=+1, and modes arbitrarily near +/-1/sqrt(2) cause no
division or convergence problem: negative/critical modes are omitted,
and +1 has 2^-m decay. Select an integer m with

  r(8/9)^m<=delta/8.                                    (7)

For example m=ceil[ln(8r/delta)/ln(9/8)]. Then
K<=2(8r/delta)^alpha, alpha=ln2/ln(9/8)<5.885. Equation (6) already proves
the requested finite additive comparator after any pure refinement.

3. A PURE RATIONAL REFINEMENT WITH A TREE FACTORIZATION

Use the convention

  J(B)=sum_(i,j) |i><j| tensor B(|i><j|),
  J(B)_((i,alpha),(j,beta))=B(|i><j|)_(alpha,beta).

It is PSD on dimension d^3, and Tr_output J(B)=I_d. Exact pivoted rational
LDL* decomposition gives

  J(B)=sum_(j=1)^s q_j l_j l_j*, q_j>0 rational, s<=d^3,
  (W_j)_(alpha,i)=(l_j)_((i,alpha)).

Then K_j=sqrt(q_j)W_j is a Kraus family with sum_j K_j*K_j=I. The CP
instrument branch I_j(X)=q_j W_j X W_j* has rational-complex coefficients;
its sum is B. Each branch has one Kraus operator. Zero LDL pivots are
discarded; PSD ensures a zero diagonal has a zero row and column in the
corresponding Schur complement. No eigenvector, square-root matrix, or
spectral-gap acquisition is required.

Refine the tree record by retaining EVERY internal Kraus label j and the
rank-one leaf label z. The leaf effects have the form f_z u_z u_z*, with
u_z a rational-complex vector (e_j or e_j +/- e_k, e_j +/- i e_k), and
f_z=1/r or 1/(2r), respectively. Define rational row messages

  v_z=u_z* at a leaf,
  v_w=(v_left tensor v_right)W_j at an internal node.

For every complete fine record w its input effect is

  M_w=t_w v_w* v_w,
  t_w=product_leaves f_z product_internal_nodes q_j>0.     (8)

All square-root Kraus scalars factor into t_w and cancel in the posterior.
Thus every nonzero effect is rank one, with rational pure posterior

  pi_w=M_w/Tr(M_w)=v_w*v_w/||v_w||^2,
  p_w=Tr(M_w)/d.

By complete local instruments, sum_w M_w=I and sum_w p_w pi_w=I/d. The
channel Psi_m(X)=sum_w M_w Tr(pi_w X) is a genuine pure canonical CPTP,
unital, HS-positive/self-adjoint channel. The coarse estimator is retained
on all refined records, so (2)-(6) apply unchanged. This proves finite
purity directly, without a compact limit. The raw alphabet is at most
s^(K-1)R^K; it need NOT be listed by the compiler.

4. EXACT CLASSICAL SAMPLING OF FINE RECORDS

For a partial assignment h of internal and leaf variables, compute effect
messages upwards. At an assigned leaf use F_z, at an unassigned leaf I.
At an assigned internal label use I_j^*(A tensor C); at an unassigned
label use B^*(A tensor C)=sum_j I_j^*(A tensor C). The root message M_h
satisfies

  P(h)=Tr(M_h)/d.                                       (9)

Proof: at a leaf this is the effect for its allowed outcomes. Induction
uses the Heisenberg CP branch pullback and sums over precisely the free
Kraus labels. It never factors a quantum joint state or assumes conditional
independence. It is valid for arbitrary interleavings of assigned variables.

For the next variable, fix each candidate value and compute P(h,value).
They are nonnegative and sum to P(h), so exact rational conditional sampling
is legal whenever P(h)>0. With cached subtree messages each candidate
changes only its vertex and at most m ancestors. There are 2K-1 variables,
each with at most max{s,R}<=max{d^3,2d^2} values. After a complete sample,
its nonzero rank-one root effect supplies pi_w by normalization.

All relevant probabilities are rational with a common denominator of bit
length polynomial in K,d,L; COSTS_AND_STABILITY.txt supplies the explicit
denominator. An integer rejection sampler uses expected polynomial random
bits; zero-probability events are never selected. No quantum sampling,
tomography, exponentially large joint density matrix, or postselection is
used. The interaction graph is exactly this tree; no claim of efficient
simulation of arbitrary quantum circuits follows.

5. CERTIFIED POLYNOMIAL-SUPPORT COMPRESSION

Independently sample T pure posteriors pi_1,...,pi_T with probabilities p_w.
Define on the ordinary-HS real Hermitian space

  S=(d/T)sum_i pi_i,
  G=(d/T)sum_i |pi_i><pi_i|.

Here |pi><pi| denotes A->pi Tr(pi A), so E G=Psi_m and E S=I.
Because each pi is pure, its HS norm is 1, and independence gives

  E||S-I||_F^2<=d^2/T,
  E||G-Psi_m||_F^2<=d^2/T.                              (10)

Take epsilon=delta/4 and T=ceil(4d^2/epsilon^2). Markov plus
||.||_operator<=||.||_F show that with probability at least 1/2,

  ||S-I||_operator<=epsilon,
  ||G-Psi_m||_operator<=epsilon.                         (11)

FIRST check exactly S<=(1+epsilon)I. If it fails, restart the batch. Set

  F_i=d pi_i/[T(1+epsilon)],
  A=I-sum_i F_i>=0.

Exactly rational-LDL-refine A into at most d rank-one effects A_j. Together
F_i,A_j form a rational rank-one POVM, hence one pure canonical channel

  Psi_cand=G/(1+epsilon)+Psi_A.

Here Psi_A is PSD as an HS operator. For every Hermitian X, scalar Jensen
gives <X,GX><=Tr(S X^2)<=(1+epsilon)Tr(X^2). Thus

  Psi_cand>=G/(1+epsilon)>=G-epsilon I.                  (12)

Under (11), (6)-(7) and (12) imply

  Psi_cand>=4Phi-3I-(delta/8+2epsilon)I
           =4Phi-3I-(5delta/8)I.                        (13)

SECOND check exactly the full Hermitian quadratic-form PSD condition

  (3+delta)I-4Phi+Psi_cand>=0.                           (14)

Use any fixed rational-complex Hermitian basis and its quadratic-form
matrix; no orthonormalization or eigenvalue computation is necessary.
All entries are real rational. Exact pivoted rational LDL decides PSD,
including zero pivots. If (14) fails, restart; otherwise return the channel.

Every returned channel satisfies (T1) by its explicit certificate, even
outside event (11). Event (11) guarantees both checks succeed with a margin
3delta/8, so a batch succeeds with probability >=1/2. Fresh batches are
independent; termination is almost sure and expected batches <=2. Support
is <=T+d, bit costs are polynomial, and the states/effects are explicitly
acquired. This is computable extraction, not merely abstract Caratheodory.

6. CONSEQUENTIAL SCOPED COROLLARIES

For arbitrary bistochastic selfcompatible Phi (not necessarily HS
self-adjoint), Theta=Phi*Phi is positive-HS self-adjoint and selfcompatible
via (Phi* tensor Phi*) composed with its supplied B. Apply the compiler
to Theta; exact rational descriptions remain rational. Since

  I-Phi*Phi<=2(I-Sym Phi),

one obtains I-Psi_delta<=8(I-Sym Phi)+delta I. This is the finite C8 version.

For a tracial family rho_H=(I+H)/d with H=H*, tau(H)=0, ||H||<=theta,
and T(Phi rho_H,rho_H)<=b uniformly, one common finite Psi_delta above gives

  T(Psi_delta rho_H,rho_H)
     <=sqrt(2theta b+delta theta^2/4).                    (15)

Indeed tau[H(I-Phi)H]<=2theta b. Canonical 0<=Psi<=I and (T1) imply
||H-Psi H||_(2,tau)^2<=8theta b+delta tau(H^2)
<=8theta b+delta theta^2; trace Cauchy-Schwarz and factor1/2 yield (15).
For C8 replace 2theta b by 4theta b, using Sym Phi in the same quadratic
form. No theorem about acquiring B, bounded-capacity reductions, infinite
dimensions, dimensions hidden from cost, or practical runtime is implied.
