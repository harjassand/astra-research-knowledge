THE PROVED LOW-BAND THEOREM

Let n=2j>=1, d=n+1. Let Phi:M_d -> M_d be UCP, tau-preserving and
SU(2)-covariant, with an actual UCP joint J having BOTH marginals Phi.
Covariance gives real sector eigenvalues lambda_l in [-1,1], l=0,...,n.
Let Psi_coh be the canonical spin-coherent measure/prepare channel

  Psi_coh(X)=d int_SU(2) Tr(P_g X) P_g dg,
  P_g=U_j(g)|j,j><j,j|U_j(g)*.

It is EB, bistochastic, positive selfadjoint. Its sector eigenvalues are

  mu_l = n!(n+1)!/[(n-l)!(n+l+1)!]
       = product_(r=0..l-1) (n-r)/(n+r+2).                (1)

On the growing set of multipoles

  [l(l+1)]^2 <= 3n(n+2),                                 (2)

the SAME channel Psi_coh satisfies the squared error comparison

  (I-Psi_coh)^2 <= 9(I-Phi).                             (3)

Consequently, for ANY experiment of bounded Hermitian likelihoods H with
tau(H)=0, ||H||<=theta<1, rho_H=(I+H)/d, whose multipole support lies in
(2), and r=sup_H||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau),

  sup_H T[Psi_coh(rho_H),rho_H] <= sqrt(9 theta r)/2.       (4)

The number of admitted nontrivial multipoles grows asymptotically like
sqrt(n); their combined operator-space dimension grows like n. This is
an arbitrary-spin dimension-free error constant on a growing spectral
band, not a fixed-j extrapolation and not the full all-mode theorem.
Signed lambda_l are allowed in (3),(4). No Holevo bound is needed under
this exact supplied structural promise.

The comparison (3) is deliberately weaker than I-Psi<=c(I-Phi). A
squared comparison already controls physical reconstruction in trace
norm, so a sharp all-frame convex-roof theorem is not required on this
route. The coherent comparator itself fails c=2 even at spin2; no c2
claim for it is made.

1. EXACT COMPATIBLE CP DEFECT AGGREGATION

Fix one multipole l with N_l=2l+1. Use a Hermitian tau-orthonormal basis
B_a of its sector. SU(2) covariance and irreducibility imply

  sum_a B_a^2=N_l I.                                     (5)

Choose a Stinespring representation J(Z)=V* pi(Z)V with V an isometry,
P=VV*, and let A_a=pi(B_a tensor I), C_b=pi(I tensor B_b). They commute
across outputs. Define rectangular operators from the input space to the
Stinespring space G,

  X_a=(I-P)A_a V,   Y_b=(I-P)C_b V.

The common-marginal identity gives V*A_aV=lambda B_a and V*C_bV=lambda B_b.
Since J is unital, (5) implies EXACTLY

  sum_a X_a*X_a=sum_b Y_b*Y_b=N_l(1-lambda^2)I,
  sum_b Y_bY_b*<=(I-P)sum_b C_b^2(I-P)=N_l(I-P).          (6)

The second inequality uses P<=I; its coefficient is N_l, not the smaller
N_l(1-lambda^2). Confusing these is an invalid stronger noise claim.
Using ordinary trace on G and normalized trace on the input, (6) gives

  sum_ab ||X_a*Y_b||_(2,tau)^2
    =(1/d)Tr_G[(sum_a X_aX_a*)(sum_b Y_bY_b*)]
    <=N_l sum_a tau(X_a*X_a)=N_l^2(1-lambda^2).           (7)

Commutativity across the supplied outputs yields

  [lambda B_a,lambda B_b]=-X_a*Y_b+Y_b*X_a.

Hilbert-Schmidt triangle/Cauchy gives ||Z-Z*||_2^2<=4||Z||_2^2.
Therefore

  lambda^4 sum_ab ||[B_a,B_b]||_(2,tau)^2
      <=4 N_l^2(1-lambda^2).                             (8)

This proof does not twirl the actual joint channel, assume independent
outputs or replace compatibility by positivity of a marginal. It uses
the actual Stinespring dilation of the legal two-output map.

2. THE EXACT SCALAR RACAH QUANTITY

The normalized Kraus map

  D_l(X)=sum_a B_a X B_a/N_l

is UCP, tau-preserving and SU(2)-covariant. Its eigenvalue on multipole k
is eta_l(k)=(-1)^l beta_l(k)/N_l, with beta in GENERAL_SPIN_GATE.txt.
Insertion of the normalized spherical tensors into the standard CG
recoupling identity gives

  eta_l(l)=(-1)^n(n+1){j j l; j j l}
          = _4F_3(-l,l+1,-l,l+1;1,-n,n+2;1).             (9)

One may alternatively take the terminating rational series below as the
explicit coefficient formula, with no asymptotic approximation.

Because sum_a B_a^2=N_l I,

  sum_ab ||[B_a,B_b]||_(2,tau)^2
    =2N_l^2-2 sum_ab tau(B_a B_b B_a B_b)
    =2N_l^2[1-eta_l(l)].                                 (10)

Equations (8),(10) prove the ALL-SPIN, ALL-MODE compatibility constraint

  lambda_l^4[1-eta_l(l)] <=2(1-lambda_l^2).                (11)

The coherent eigenvalue (1) follows by evaluating the sole nonzero
coherent multipole expectation T_l0(j,j), using the elementary highest-
weight Clebsch-Gordan coefficient. All representation coefficients are
exactly specified; the frame and representation are supplied inputs.

3. UNIFORM ANALYTIC RACAH GAP IN THE GROWING BAND

Put L=l(l+1), N=n(n+2), zeta=L/sqrt(N). The terminating series in (9) is

  eta=1-t_1+t_2-...+(-1)^l t_l,
  t_0=1,
  t_r=(n+1)(n-r)!/(n+r+1)!
             *[(l+r)!/((l-r)!r!)]^2,
  t_(r+1)/t_r=(L-r(r+1))^2
                   /[(r+1)^2(N-r(r+2))].                (12)

For EVERY admitted n>=l>=1 and r=0,...,l-1,

  (L-r(r+1))^2/(N-r(r+2)) <= L^2/N.

For r>0, after expansion this is equivalent to

  N(r+1)[2L-r(r+1)] >= L^2(r+2).

Since N=n(n+2)>=L=l(l+1), it suffices for r>0 to check

  (r+1)[2L-r(r+1)]-L(r+2)=r[L-(r+1)^2]>=0,

which follows from r+1<=l. For r=0 the ratio comparison is equality.
Thus ALWAYS t_(r+1)/t_r<=zeta^2/(r+1)^2. Now put x=zeta^2 and assume
x<=3, exactly (2). The terms beginning with t_1 decrease, because their
next-term ratios are <=x/(r+1)^2<=3/4 for r>=1. The alternating remainder
in 1-eta=t_1-t_2+t_3-... is therefore nonnegative after the first two.
We obtain

  1-eta>=t_1-t_2>=x-x^2/4>=(3/4)min(x,1).                (13)

For l=1 take t_2=0; the same displayed lower bound holds. Also (1) and
the union bound for a product of numbers in [0,1] give

  1-mu_l<=min[1,sum_(r=0..l-1) 2(r+1)/(n+r+2)]
          <=min[1,L/(n+2)]<=min(1,zeta).                  (14)

Combining (13),(14) proves the analytic, uniform lower bound

  1-eta_l(l)>=(3/4)(1-mu_l)^2                            (15)

for all spins and every admitted mode (2). For x<=1, the last step in
(13) uses x-x^2/4>=3x/4; for 1<=x<=3 it uses x-x^2/4>=3/4. In particular,
the transition region 1<=x<=3 has the stronger statement eta<=1/4.
No finite diagnostic is used for (12)-(15). For modes outside (2), these
two-term alternating bounds do not provide a uniform positive scalar
gap; this proof cannot be extrapolated to unbounded x.
