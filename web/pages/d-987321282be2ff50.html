# All-temperature stabilizer-diagonal SLD identification and coercivity

Cycle2, r03_quantum. Independent construction followed by adversarial algebra check from b_math. Universal proof below; novelty not established. This does not cover arbitrary noncommuting Gibbs Hamiltonians.

## Exact interface

Let sigma=U diag(p_x) U^dagger be a faithful state on n qubits, x in F2^n, with every p_x>0, and U Clifford. Supply a fixed Hermitian Pauli frame {A_a} whose symplectic labels span F2^(2n); the specific local frame Xi,Zi is included. Define the SLD-transformed frame

K_a=T_sigma(A_a)=sech((1/2)log Delta_sigma)(A_a).

Claim: its joint commutant is scalar at every positive finite spectrum. More strongly, if gamma_A is the undressed Hilbert–Schmidt commutator gap on traceless matrices, then

sum_a ||[M,K_a]||_F² >= w_min² sum_a ||[M,A_a]||_F²
                        >= w_min² gamma_A² ||M||_F²,

for every traceless complex M, where w_min is the smallest weight on any nonzero Pauli transition in the sigma eigenbasis. For the fixed local Xi,Zi frame gamma_A²=4, independent of n. Thus gamma_K>=2 w_min>0.

## Reduction to diagonal and exact transition representation

Conjugation by U^dagger preserves Frobenius norms and commutants. It maps each supplied Pauli into a Pauli P_a with label (t_a,z_a). In the diagonal eigenbasis write

(P_a)_(x,x+t_a)=eta_a (-1)^(z_a dot x),

where eta_a is a unit complex phase, independent of x; Hermitianity fixes its value but it will cancel from every norm. Define

w_a(x)=2 sqrt(p_x p_(x+t_a))/(p_x+p_(x+t_a))>0.

Then K_a has exactly the same transition support and phase, multiplied by w_a(x), and w_a(x+t_a)=w_a(x). A diagonal Pauli has t_a=0 and w_a=1.

## Exact XOR-sector coercivity identity

Decompose any M into XOR sectors: (M_r)_(x,x+r)=m_r(x), all other entries zero. The sectors are orthogonal, and commutation by one monomial Pauli sends sector r into sector r+t_a, so residuals from different r remain orthogonal for that a.

For a fixed generator and sector, evaluating the commutator at (x,x+r+t_a) and removing its common unit phase gives

e_a,r(x)=u_x m_r(x)−chi v_x m_r(x+t_a),

u_x=w_a(x+r), v_x=w_a(x), chi=(-1)^(z_a dot r).

Both u and v are invariant under x→x+t_a. Therefore, by expanding the squares and shifting the summation index,

sum_x |e_a,r(x)|²
 =sum_x u_x v_x |m_r(x)−chi m_r(x+t_a)|²
  +sum_x (u_x−v_x)² |m_r(x)|².

Check of coefficients: the left squared diagonal coefficient is u_x²+v_x² after the shift. The first term on the right has diagonal coefficient 2u_xv_x and the second adds (u_x−v_x)²; cross coefficients are identical. This identity is valid for complex m_r.

The first right term is at least w_min² times the undressed generator's commutator energy in the same sector, and the second is nonnegative. Summing sectors and generators proves the first inequality stated above. No eigenvalue floor or path-length estimate is used beyond the strict positivity of the relevant transition weights.

## Why the undressed gap is positive

In the n-qubit Pauli basis, sum_a ||[M,A_a]||_F² is diagonal. A Pauli string B with symplectic label b has eigenvalue 4 times the number of supplied labels a that anticommute with b. Full symplectic span guarantees at least one such label for every nonidentity B, so gamma_A²>=4. For the local Xi,Zi frame there is a weight-one X or Z string anticommuting with exactly one generator, so gamma_A²=4 exactly. Consequently the dressed commutant is scalar.

Alternative identification-only proof, independently checked by b_math: if M commutes with the filtered frame, a sector r obeys

m_r(x+t_a)=(-1)^(z_a dot r) [w_a(x+r)/w_a(x)]m_r(x).

For r!=0, full Pauli-label span gives a generator word with total label (0,z) and z dot r=1. Its translation closes while its sign is negative and its accumulated weight ratio is positive, forcing m_r(x)=−R(x)m_r(x), R>0, hence zero. For r=0, all ratios are 1 and the translations span F2^n, forcing a constant diagonal. The quantitative square identity is stronger and simpler than a loop-based bound.

## Locally bounded commuting stabilizer Gibbs models

Suppose sigma is the Gibbs state of commuting stabilizer terms H=sum_j theta_j S_j at known beta, with |theta_j|<=1. Its joint stabilizer eigenbasis is a Clifford basis after selecting independent generators and resolving degeneracies with commuting Paulis. Conjugating a frame generator flips every stabilizer term it anticommutes with. If each supplied local Xi or Zi anticommutes with at most r Hamiltonian terms, each transition changes energy by at most 2r. Thus

w_min>=sech(beta r), gamma_K>=2 sech(beta r).

This is valid at every finite beta and is independent of system size when r is bounded. More generally use a known maximum transition energy L for gamma_K>=2 sech(beta L/2). Sparse bounded-range stabilizer Hamiltonians satisfy the required incidence bound; a Clifford representation alone does not imply it, so specify r rather than assuming a shallow or local encoding.

Examples: commuting graph-state stabilizer Hamiltonians on bounded-degree graphs; Bell-diagonal two-qubit states; arbitrary stabilizer-diagonal populations (positive gap but potentially tiny w_min). Although such Hamiltonians contain non-diagonal physical Paulis, their terms commute. This is not an all-temperature theorem for noncommuting Xi+Zi interactions.

## Scope, novelty, use, next step

The score-fiber theorem from cycle1 converts scalar dressed commutant into exact identification among all faithful competing states. This proof does not establish sample acquisition, a global optimizer, or parameter-loss calibration; endpoint and low-temperature statistical obstructions remain. The gap is exponentially small in beta r in the worst case, consistent with thermally hidden directions.

Nearest verified source: Dong–Leng arXiv2609.28391v1 Theorem9 supplies general local-Hamiltonian identification at high temperature; Lemma21 supplies the undressed local Pauli gap. b_math's cycle1 computational-diagonal result uses unchanged Z directions. The present transition-pairing proof works for any monomial Pauli frame after Clifford conjugation and requires no surviving diagonal generators. Stabilizer and signed-Cayley-graph literature novelty has not been searched thoroughly. Treat as a proved subclass construction/conditioning baseline, not a claimed breakthrough.

Concrete downstream use: exclude entire stabilizer-diagonal families from finite-temperature fixed-Pauli collision searches; give a locally specified coherence certificate for commuting stabilizer inverse problems; compare candidate noncommuting extensions against a nonperturbative baseline.

Load-bearing missing lemma for the unrestricted question: an arbitrary sigma eigenbasis destroys the monomial single-transition structure and the exact reverse-edge square identity. Ordinary Pauli tensor relations alone have not yet supplied a replacement. A universal arbitrary-state claim requires a new inequality or an exact fixed-local-Pauli counterexample.
