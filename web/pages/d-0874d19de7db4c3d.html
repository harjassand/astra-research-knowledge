## What closes the operational gap

White-state discrimination costs Theta(N/r) at the polarized thermal time, but is not a complete entanglement test. The centered null tau^tensor N is globally fully separable and has identical one-body data. Removing all degree-one information makes its local relative entropy quadratic and forces the larger cost.

The honest qubit upper uses the probability exp[-(J_n/sqrt(r)-h_N sqrt(r)(n dot b)/2)^2]. Finite white binomial moments fix h_N. Its target-minus-ALLSEP gap is

    kappa_r r(N|b|^2-1)/[6(N-1)] + o(r/N).

The proof bounds all Pauli supports and separately excludes large collective means of heterogeneous product arrays. Convexity covers every fully separable null. A bounded-mean calculation alone would not have sufficed. The readouts are local and the same-copy outcomes are explicitly pooled and charged as r-site access.

Known b may be supplied by a verified target model. Otherwise independent pooled three-axis calibration costs O(N/r log(1/eta)) preparations at fixed strict margin. Freeze and clip its estimate v; soundness holds for every frozen v, while the target gap loses exactly N||v-b||^2. See calibration_and_static_example.md for confidence allocation. Finite axis randomness, coin rounding, threshold accuracy and arithmetic are charged in finite_randomness_and_costs.md.

The upper is sound for arbitrary identical fully separable inputs on independent fresh copies. Conditional/adversarial soundness additionally covers a global null separable across physical laboratories with arbitrary within-laboratory correlations, or conditional full separability of each round. Unconditional separability of each copy marginal alone is not the correlated-null model. Target preparations remain independent for power.

At the saturated scale, charge m global preparations, mN=Theta(N^3/r^2) prepared qubit instances and mr=Theta(N^2/r) local readouts. With deterministic variable budgets r_i, the lower proves sum r_i^2=Omega(N^2), hence total actual exposures Omega(N^2/r_max). A common cap by itself is not an actual readout lower bound for a protocol that does not use that cap. Unbounded stopping/expected-budget versions remain unproved.

Trusted null Casimir changes the problem: a separable state with <J^2>=3N/4 has |b|<=1/sqrt(N), whereas the polarized target exceeds it. Promise-aware one-body inference can cost O(N) preparations. The centered product null violates that trusted constraint, so the quadratic black-box theorem must not be applied there. Acquiring global constraints experimentally is charged separately.

## Primary comparators and priority limits

[Moharramipour et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.08542), Theorem III.2 p8, already gives local trace hiding for MMIS, including O(k/N) for semisimple Lie groups. Its introductory result and appendix frame fixed k; its conclusion asks for sampling-complexity analysis. Our class permits arbitrary spin-sector states and uniform tails, compares to the fixed matched product, and gives an ALLSEP fresh-copy lower with a sound qubit upper. Hiding itself is not a novelty claim.

[Johnson, Gavalakis and Kontoyiannis](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06632v1), Eq(6) and Corollary1.4, records the Stam classical quadratic KL bound for sampling without replacement and finite de Finetti. The exponent is therefore established classically. [Singh and Cerf](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09410v1), Lemma3 Eq(54), transports that bound for diagonal energy-shell mixtures under energy-preserving-unitary invariance. Neither displayed premise directly covers arbitrary coherent U(d) sector states with the present K-tail interface.

[Ye, Bergh and Datta](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.40343), 30Sep2026, Theorem1 pp3-4, gives general PI-state operator domination by an iid mixture with epsilon<=d^2 k/n. It yields O(k/n) max-relative entropy to a mixture. It does not directly imply the quadratic Petz/KL bound to rho_1^tensor r, but is an essential recent comparator; only its theorem and remarks were read here, not its full proof.

[Toth et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0702219), Eq(2b), gives the established collective spin-variance inequality used by the two-site tangent witness and credits Toth PRA69,052327(2004). The spin criterion is not newly claimed. The possible source-scope advance is the uniform growing-access entropy/certification interface and its tail-controlled coherent-sector extension. Historical novelty needs a dedicated broader primary-source review and remains UNKNOWN.
