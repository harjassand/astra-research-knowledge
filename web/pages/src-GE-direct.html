# Direct pair certificate for the exact averaged endpoint

Root derivation, 16 October 2026. Base Astra revision `adb0a306e9bfdc33b9fbd105efb58baed0cc021e`. This is internal mathematical work, not external validation, historical originality, an acquired physical model or a practical superiority claim.

The supplied two-sided Duhamel theorem is the receiving premise. The new construction takes its fast-rate limit **inside the certificate**, retaining the boundary escape term. It removes the need to choose an artificial finite boost and separately certify boosted-component mixing. Ordinary finite-dimensional stochastic averaging and block resolvent algebra are established machinery.

## 1. Exact input and target

Let r be fixed nonnegative symmetric interchange rates on a finite vertex set. Choose additional nonnegative symmetric fast rates a. The positive-rate components of a are the declared blocks C. A block may be a singleton; every nonsingleton block is connected in the supplied fast graph. Let B contain the unchanged rates on edges outside the support of a. On a fast edge replace r_e by W a_e, retaining all other original rates. Thus the boosted generator is W A+B, with W>0. The original rates inside a block but outside the support of a remain in B.

Let Pi average uniformly over within-block permutations. For every T>0 the boosted interchange endpoint converges to

```
P_average(T) = Pi exp[-T Pi B Pi] Pi.
```

This is the standard conditional-uniform boundary-event sampler on the **actual fast connected components**, not on an arbitrary index suffix. The claim is uniform full endpoint total variation over deterministic initial permutations, and therefore over their mixtures and all color projections. It is not a trajectory, current or relative rare-event certificate. Supplied rates and all sampling/output costs remain required.

## 2. Pair objects available without a permutation state space

For any symmetric graph rate v, H_v is the killed unordered-pair backward generator: each endpoint walks independently until meeting, which kills at twice the joining-edge rate. Put

```
S_r,e(t) = [exp(-t H_r) 1]_e.
```

For a fast block C, let H_A,C be this killed generator restricted to pairs entirely in C, with the fast rates a and no external edges. It is positive definite: two independent walkers on a finite connected graph meet almost surely. Its inverse is nonnegative. Define

```
h_C = H_A,C^{-1} 1.
```

For a pair z={x,y} in C and another block D, define the nonnegative exit matrix

```
E_C[z,{C,D}] = sum_(v in D) (B_xv+B_yv).
G_C = H_A,C^{-1} E_C.
```

All unlisted columns are zero. Every starting fast edge e lies inside one block C, so h_e and row G_e are well-defined. These are exact rational quantities for rational inputs, obtainable by positive M-matrix solves. Their conditioning and bit costs are charged; an unknown graph is not supplied by this theorem.

Define block-walker rates

```
beta_CD = (sum_(x in C,y in D) B_xy)/|C|.
```

On unordered distinct block pairs, K is the backward generator of two independent block walkers killed on entering the same block. It is generally nonsymmetric, reversible with weight |C||D|. The meeting killing rate at {C,D} is beta_CD+beta_DC. Put

```
barS(t) = exp(-t K) 1,
g_e(t) = G_e barS(t) >= 0.
```

This coarse process has binomial(number_of_blocks,2) states. Its rates, denominators and geometry are included in the input/cost contract. Internal finite B edges do not contribute to E_C or K, since they vanish after block averaging.

## 3. The measure limit and its proof

On the full unordered-pair space, write H_W=W H_A+H_B. Let P be the orthogonal projection onto ker H_A and R=H_A^+. P vanishes on same-block pair rows and averages over cross-block positions. For any fast edge e and every z>0, block inversion on ran P plus its orthogonal complement gives

```
W e_e^T (zI+W H_A+H_B)^{-1} 1
 -> e_e^T R 1
    -e_e^T R H_B P (zP+P H_B P)^{-1} P1.
```

Here the inverse on ran P exists since z>0. On the complement, W times the inverse converges to R; the Schur correction is O(1/W) in finite dimension. No inverse of a singular full H_A is taken. The row e_e^T R is supported in the same-block killed domain and equals the corresponding row of H_A,C^{-1}. Acting on block-constant cross-pair functions, -H_B P is exactly E_C, and P H_B P acts as K in the block-value coordinates. Therefore the limit is

```
h_e + G_e (zI+K)^{-1} 1.
```

It is the Laplace transform of the nonnegative locally finite measure

```
mu_e(dt) = h_e delta_0(dt) + g_e(t) dt.
```

The prelimit measure is `W S_W,e(t) dt`, also nonnegative. For completeness, the transform convergence identifies locally weak convergence: at any fixed z>0, the transform at z/2 uniformly bounds the e^(-zt)-weighted tail past M by a constant times e^(-zM/2), while the transform at z bounds mass on each finite interval. Subsequential local weak limits therefore exist and have the displayed transforms. Uniqueness follows by weighting with e^(-zt), substituting x=e^(-t), and polynomial density on [0,1]. Thus every subsequence has the same limit. There is no limiting atom at any T>0 because g_e is continuous there. A continuous bounded function times the indicator of [0,T] may consequently be integrated through the limit, retaining the atom at zero.

This argument does not assume that the pair semigroup has a graph-uniform positive spectral gap, or that a floating eigendecomposition is accurate. It uses only finite dimension, positivity and the exact block resolvent limit. A disconnected retained block graph is allowed.

## 4. Direct endpoint bound

Apply the supplied finite-boost theorem at horizon T:

```
TV(P_r(T),P_W(T))
 <= sum_(fast edges e) |W a_e-r_e|
      integral_0^T S_r,e(T-t) S_W,e(t) dt.
```

Since |W a_e-r_e|/W tends to a_e, and the original survival is continuous, the measure limit and the finite-dimensional averaged endpoint limit yield

```
TV(P_r(T),P_average(T)) <= C(T),
C(T) = sum_e a_e [ h_e S_r,e(T)
                   + integral_0^T S_r,e(T-t) g_e(t) dt ].       (D)
```

The convention is ordinary TV, one half of the row l1 distance. Cap the bound at one if desired. The boundary term is nonnegative, not a signed cancellation that may be dropped. For no retained block boundaries it is zero. The atom is the instantaneous initial block equilibration transported through the original endpoint dynamics.

When a consists of one whole connected graph, symmetry gives the useful normalization

```
sum_e a_e h_e = |C|(|C|-1)/4.
```

Indeed H_A,C 1 has value 2a_e at joining-edge pair states and zero otherwise; multiply H_A,C h=1 by 1^T. This is a check on the factors of two, not a new general mixing theorem. Different choices of connected fast graph a can give different valid certificates for the same exact averaged target; optimization over them is a separate problem.

## 5. Boundary escape cannot be omitted

Take three vertices, fast edge 0–1 of rate a=1, retained edge 1–2 of rate b>0, and original fast rate zero. The within-block pair matrix is [2], so h=1/2. The coarse pair {{0,1},{2}} has K=[3b/2], and G=[b/2]. Hence

```
g(t)=(b/2)exp(-3bt/2),
C(T)=1/2 + (1-exp(-3bT/2))/3.
```

The original chain has only two reachable permutations. The exact averaged endpoint gives their combined mass `1/3+exp(-3bT/2)/6`, so

```
actual TV = 2/3-exp(-3bT/2)/6 > 1/2.
```

An atom-only bound would assert TV<=1/2 and is false for every T>0. This is a decisive legal interchange counterexample, unlike an unconstrained abstract analytic foil. The full direct bound survives. T=0 is deliberately excluded: the fast-rate limit has an instantaneous initial projection there.

## 6. Positive Erlang receiving interface

The independent sparse worker has developed a positive Erlang resolvent certifier. It can be composed with (D) without reintroducing finite W. Let `P_r=(I+H_r/s)^{-1}`, `P_K=(I+K/s)^{-1}`, and Gammak,s have integer shape k>=2 and rate s>0. Put F_e,D=a_e G_e,D for fast-edge rows, zero elsewhere. Monotonicity of survival gives C(T)/T nonincreasing: each atom contribution S_r,e(T)/T decreases, and after t=T v each integrand S_r,e(T(1-v))g_e(Tv) decreases.

Exact integration against the Gamma density gives

```
E[C(Gamma)/Gamma]
 = s/(k-1) sum_e a_e h_e (P_r^(k-1)1)_e
   + 1/(k-1) sum_(j=1..k-1)
       1^T P_r^j F P_K^(k-j)1.                         (E)
```

The first identity changes the Gamma shape by one. For the second, write the convolution variables u,v>=0 and expand (u+v)^(k-2); the factorials and powers of s cancel, giving precisely 1/(k-1). Thus

```
C(T) <= T E[C(Gamma)/Gamma]/Pr(Gamma<=T).              (F)
```

The denominator must be positively enclosed, not replaced by an uncharged approximate CDF. All inverse actions are positive substochastic resolvents. Exact rational or fixed-point-plus-exact-residual enclosures can therefore supply a finite-bit certificate on arbitrary graphs. Formula (E) is a root composition after the worker's independent Erlang theorem, not a claim that Erlangization is new. ## 7. Capability and originality boundaries

This is a direct exact-averaging certificate, not a new averaging sampler. It avoids artificial boost precision, the separate Aldous-gap/full-permutation mixing tail and a source wrapper's path-index partition assumptions. It still requires original pair survival or resolvent actions, exact fast-block Green solves, actual block structure and full-output costs. No physical exchange rates or scientific workload are acquired by the algebra. One-point outputs generally already have a cheaper one-walker solver. Full correlated endpoint or channel tasks must justify bearing this cost.

Historical priority for this precise certificate has not been established. Standard singular perturbation, Green functions and Erlangization are strong predecessors. All new claims remain externally unreviewed. A legal counterexample to the finite-boost premise, the measure limit, or the retained boundary term would invalidate the corresponding receiving statement.
