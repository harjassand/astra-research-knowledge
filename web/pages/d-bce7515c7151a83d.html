## Decision

The strongest lead is the exact positive-characteristic gap between a published hyperbolic idempotent assertion and the proof scopes of its cited sources. Bartels–Lück–Reich (BLR) explicitly claim that every idempotent in `F[G]` is trivial when `char(F)=p`, `G` has no p-torsion, and no nonidentity element is conjugate to a p-power; they then state this applies to torsion-free word-hyperbolic groups. The cited Formanek theorem is characteristic zero, while Burger–Valette’s characteristic-p lemma proves only that the identity-coefficient trace is 0 or 1. The citation/proof mismatch is real; it does not show BLR's conclusion false. A verified ASTRA witness would directly refute that conclusion.

There are three distinct source interfaces that must not be collapsed. OpenAI `lean/docs/197.md` has a misleading “torsion-free” title: its detailed characteristic-two scope gives a finitely presented group with an odd-prime-order element, and its separate odd-characteristic statement also uses torsion. It cannot supply ASTRA's torsion-free premise. A separate OpenAI manuscript dated October 4 states a finitely presented torsion-free `F_2` witness with a finite two-dimensional classifying complex, but does not state hyperbolicity and has no materialized presentation in the pinned source tree. ASTRA N47/N129 add a conditional hyperbolic extension; those cards say no concrete presentation or acquired `a,b,c` is available. All downstream claims therefore remain source-conditioned.

A second, lower-confidence opportunity is the exact measurable-cohomology bridge for the algebraic defect shift. Existing source work already narrows it to a precise twisted `H^1` equation. The useful new direction is to attack that equation as a class-equality problem, using the exact finite-power product split to nominate the base map, instead of trying another approximate finite-tail insertion. This is not a proof that the equation has a solution.

## Lead 1 — the positive-characteristic hyperbolic idempotent gap

**Exact target.** For every prime `p`, every field `F` of characteristic `p`, every torsion-free word-hyperbolic group `G`, and every `e in F[G]` with `e^2=e`, prove `e=0` or `e=1`; or produce one such `(F,G,e)` with `e` nontrivial. This is the precise idempotent assertion BLR print. The N47/N129 candidate is stronger: its `ab=1`, `ac=0`, `c != 0` witness would give the particular nontrivial idempotent `e=1-ba` and also violate direct finiteness. Merely having a nontrivial idempotent is not equivalent to failure of direct finiteness in a general ring.

**Why it matters.** A valid torsion-free hyperbolic counterexample would give a nonsofic hyperbolic group: Elek–Szabó prove direct finiteness for sofic-group algebras over arbitrary fields. It would refute the possibility that every word-hyperbolic group is sofic. The October 4 OpenAI manuscript, if correct, already makes the torsion-free finite-presentation claim over `F_2`; the ASTRA extension is what adds hyperbolicity. These are candidate manuscript conclusions, not independently verified theorems.

**Primary-source audit.** BLR, *On the Farrell–Jones Conjecture and its applications*, Journal of Topology 1 (2008), p. 63 (preprint p. 9), explicitly state the positive-characteristic trivial-idempotent claim and the hyperbolic corollary. The same article's Theorem 1.12 lists stable finiteness, characteristic zero, or soficity as the separate conditions used to deduce direct finiteness from its K-theory input. Formanek, *Idempotents in Noetherian Group Rings*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 25 (1973), Theorem 9, assumes characteristic zero. Burger–Valette, *Idempotents in complex group rings: theorems of Zalesskii and Bass revisited* (1998), Lemma 4.1, assumes characteristic `p` and proves `tau(e) in {0,1}`; Proposition 4.2 proves the complex-group-ring conclusion using infinitely many primes and reduction modulo prime ideals. Those statements do not supply the full positive-characteristic conclusion as BLR state it. The positive-characteristic step in their cited trace argument is insufficient on its own: it kills nonidentity conjugacy-class traces and leaves the standard trace in `{0,1}`.

For a proposed witness `ab=1`, `ac=0`, `c != 0`, let `e=1-ba`. Then `e^2=e`; `e != 0,1` follows from `ba != 1` and `ba != 0`; and cyclicity of the coefficient trace plus augmentation give `tau(e)=0` and `aug(e)=0`. Thus the stated trace conclusion allows exactly the candidate defect. In the ASTRA source audit, the corresponding nonzero projective right ideal has zero `K_0` class, so rational `K_0` is not a positivity argument either. No inference from trace or `K_0` alone resolves this.

**New route / decisive missing lemma.** Make the disputed proof transition the primary target, independently of the random-graph construction: either give a characteristic-`p` rigidity argument that upgrades the class-trace identities to `e=0,1` for torsion-free hyperbolic groups, or show that no such upgrade follows and verify an explicit counterexample. A plausible mathematical entry point is a support-sensitive refinement using hyperbolic conjugacy/centralizer geometry together with a faithful dimension or module invariant for the projective ideal `eF[G]`; the invariant must distinguish a nonzero projective with zero `K_0` class. This is a research direction, not an existing lemma. The trace-only route is exhausted: adding more class-constant linear traces cannot distinguish coefficients inside one conjugacy class.

**Early falsifier and compatibility.** If an actual finite presentation and support words are ever acquired, verify torsion-freeness and hyperbolicity independently, then check `e^2=e`, `e != 0,1` in the presented group ring. That one witness would falsify BLR's printed characteristic-`p` assertion; no new probability or disk proof is needed to settle the literature clash. If the exact BLR proof instead supplies an extra hypothesis or valid argument beyond the cited trace step, the candidate must fail at witness preservation or another construction interface. For any hyperbolic realization, also test Delzant's large-displacement criterion: if the group has an action on a hyperbolic space with infimum displacement `>9 delta`, its group ring has no zero divisors, contradicting `ac=0`, `a,c !=0`. N47/N129 do not supply such an action or establish that threshold; graph girth alone is not that test.

**Status.** Source-conditioned candidate; `G-GROUP-RING-PREMISE-FAILURE` stays open. N47 is conditional on an earlier source family and has no emitted presentation. N129 is a separate provisional, LLM-assisted seven-page manuscript, with imported random-matching estimates and no explicit graph, presentation, or `a,b,c`. The citation mismatch is a proved source-scope observation; neither BLR falsehood nor the candidate counterexample is proved here.

## Lead 2 — exact cocycle class for Bernoulli absorption

**Exact target.** Conditional on an acquired countably infinite torsion-free `G` and `a,b,c in F_2[G]` with `ab=1`, `ac=0`, `c != 0`, let `X=F_2^G` with Haar measure and the shift action, `A=T_a`, and `K=ker A=im(T_{1-ba})`. Decide whether some finite power of `K` is Bernoulli, or whether there are `epsilon>0` and an exact pmp `G`-conjugacy `X ~= X x B_epsilon`, where `B_epsilon` is a Bernoulli shift with base Shannon entropy `epsilon`. For `epsilon=ln 2`, this would give `2^G ~= 4^G`.

**Why it matters and the prior boundary.** Seward's 2022 theorem classifies Bernoulli shifts with equal base Shannon entropy for all countably infinite groups; it does not settle unequal-base isomorphism. Under ASTRA's conditional strict upper `s_G=h_sup^Rok(G)<ln 2`, the listed subgroup Rokhlin, ordinary Koopman, and factor-monotone invariants do not separate `2^G` and `4^G`; that is not a conjugacy result. The original family-197 sign `s_G=0` versus `s_G>0` remains open. The `lean/docs/197.md` group has odd torsion in its detailed scope, so it cannot serve as the torsion-free premise for the later KST construction. The Haar splitting itself is conditional on an actual witness and does not imply `K` is Bernoulli.

**New route / missing lemma.** Use the exact finite-power extraction `K^m ~= X x R` (reported in N252, conditional and not independently reconstructed) to nominate a pmp base isomorphism `Q=(j,z):Y ~=_G Y x B_epsilon`. For a Borel section `s:Y -> X` of the relevant compact-group extension with kernel `D`, define `alpha(g,y)=g s(y)-s(gy)`. The exact bridge is the twisted measurable-cohomology identity

`alpha(g,jy) - sigma(alpha(g,y)) = t(gy) - g t(y)`

for every `g in G` almost everywhere, for some `sigma in Aut_G(D)` and measurable `t:Y -> D`. If it holds, the explicit fiber formula gives `X ~= X x B_epsilon`. The new angle is to compare the two cocycle classes in `H^1(G action Y,D)` directly, using the exact finite-power product split to build `Q`, and to use the algebraic module/extension data to calculate or obstruct that class equality. Boundary amenability for hyperbolic groups is at most a possible source of averaging kernels; it does not itself imply exact measurable `H^1` vanishing. Approximate tail maps and cocycles converging in probability do not satisfy this lemma.

**Early falsifier.** After fixing the candidate `Q` and `sigma`, restrict the cocycle class to an explicit infinite cyclic subgroup `<g>` and test whether the two restrictions are cohomologous in measurable `H^1(<g> action Y,D)`. A nonzero restriction obstruction rules out that particular exact bridge. Conversely, a successful bridge must exhibit measurable `t` and verify the equation on generators with a proof that the relation extends to all of `G`; finite checks alone do not pass the gate. This tests the exact cohomology route, not Bernoullicity of `K` in general.

**Status.** Conditional opportunity only. N166 supplies an exact compact Haar splitting and a strict entropy upper bound but no sign. N252 reports a Borel-coloring finite-power product, with no independent proof intake and no finite/computable coloring. N255 gives the cocycle identity but no `Q,sigma,t`. N257 rules out several easy invariants, not measurable conjugacy. `G-ORIGINAL-FAMILY197-ENTROPY` and `G-DEFECT-BERNOULLICITY-OR-COCYCLE` remain open.
