# Spectral filter for the proposed constructions

7 October2026. **Our standard-functional-analysis deduction**, conditional only on the already constructed u,V and their superexponential decay. This is a comparator/hypothesis check, not a novel theorem.

Both the power and logarithmic constructions give bounded real V→0, nonzero u∈L² with Δu=Vu∈L². Thus u∈H² is a zero-energy eigenfunction of the self-adjoint H=−Δ+V on H²(R^n). Since multiplication by bounded V→0 is relatively compact with respect to −Δ (local Rellich compactness, followed by arbitrarily small exterior sup norm), σ_ess(H)=[0,∞). The zero eigenfunction lies at the essential-spectrum threshold. It is not a negative-energy ground-state construction.

In fact **H has negative spectrum**. Suppose its quadratic form q(v)=∫|∇v|²+∫V|v|² were nonnegative. Since q(u)=0 and the Sobolev absolute-value identity gives q(|u|)=q(u)=0, positivity of the form implies q(|u|,v)=0 for every v∈H¹: apply the quadratic discriminant inequality, or the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for a nonnegative form. Therefore |u| solves H|u|=0 weakly. Elliptic regularity gives a smooth representative. Harnack implies it is strictly positive everywhere, since it is nonnegative and nonzero.

The Harnack constant on unit balls is uniform in their centers because V is globally bounded. Chaining overlapping unit balls from any point where |u|>0 to x gives a lower bound |u(x)|≥c exp(−C|x|), with constants depending on V and that point. This contradicts either exp(−c r^p), p>1, or exp(−c r log r) upper decay. Hence the form cannot be nonnegative. A compactly supported smooth test function can approximate a negative-form vector in H¹, so inf σ(H)<0. Because σ_ess(H) starts at0, there is at least one negative discrete eigenvalue.

Thus any improved exponential unique-continuation theorem whose interface assumes H≥0 or an equivalent nonnegative generalized principal eigenvalue is compatible with our construction. Reality, V→0, and even all fixed-order absolute derivatives→0 do not supply that positivity. The state at0 is an excited threshold state above negative spectrum. This removes an unhelpful comparison with nonnegative-potential or positive-principal-eigenvalue results.
