## Outcome and adoption status

The strongest immediate research interface is the explicit graph bundle in family050. Its algebraic symmetric-power filtration and analytic invariant-measure obstruction can be reused to derive two stronger statements:

1. **Uniform twist ratio:** there is ε₀>0 such that f_m*G⊗O(r,r) admits no smooth Griffiths-semipositive Hermitian metric whenever m≥1,r≥0 and r/m<ε₀. Every r≥1 member is nevertheless ample; every even n with nr−1≥3m has a very ample n-th tautological power. The opposite bound is constructive: E_{m,3m} is globally generated and supports a smooth semipositive metric by dual evaluation, and E_{m,3m+1} supports a strict metric by tensoring P. Hence both minimal metric twist budgets grow **linearly in m**, while fixedr1 suffices algebraically. The evaluation space has dimension81m²+54m+2; its basis/metric matrix was not explicitly acquired and no acquisition-runtime bound is claimed.
2. **Explicit nef/metric separation:** G is algebraically nef and non-ample, but for each fixed positive Kähler form ω there is ε*>0 below which no smooth Hermitian metric has Griffiths curvature lower bound−ε*ωId.

These are complete author derivations in notes/hessian_bridge12/cycle19/UNIFORM_HERMITIAN_POSITIVITY_GAP.md, submitted to root for independent reconstruction. **At this report version they remain pending that independent gate**, rather than being silently adopted.
