1. LEGALITY OF ONE NOISE
The inverse Lyapunov map is exactly
  Lyap_s^(-1)(X)=integral_0^infinity exp(-ts)Xexp(-ts)dt.
It is positive and Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint. Hence V>=0 and
  (Vs+sV)/2=BsB, H(s)=0.
H is Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint. The semigroup exp(-tH) is CP by a
Dyson expansion of the CP drift exp(-tV/2)Xexp(-tV/2) and CP jump BXB.
Conjugate it back to physical trace-class coordinates with
  Gamma_s(X)=s^(1/2)Xs^(1/2).
The physical semigroup Phi_t=Gamma_s exp(-tH) Gamma_s^(-1) is CP.
For all X, trace preservation is
  Tr Phi_t(X)=<s,exp(-tH)Gamma_s^(-1)X>
             =<s,Gamma_s^(-1)X>=Tr X,
using selfadjointness and exp(-tH)s=s. It fixes sigma because Gamma_s(s)=s^2.
The KMS implementation is exp(-tH), so KMS symmetry is exact. No GNS or
sigma-modular covariance is claimed. The spectrum of H is nonnegative:
its real eigenvalues have exp(-t lambda)<=1 by similarity with a physical
CPTP channel. Thus E>=0.

The quantity J is genuine physical relative-entropy production:
  delta=Gamma_s H(r),
  J=Tr delta(logrho-logsigma), Tr delta=0.
It is not the erroneous ordinary-likelihood bilinear E(L,logL).

2. FULL FINITE-DIMENSIONAL GENERATOR SCOPE

Write L=-H, e=|I>>, n=||e||^2=d, Q=ee^*/d, P=I-Q. Use the Choi convention
  J_Phi=sum_ij |i><j| tensor Phi(|i><j|),

  C=P J_L P>=0.

J_Phi-adjoint=R J_Phi R. Hilbert-Schmidt symmetry implies RJ_LR=J_L,

roots of eigenvalues to write C=sum_j |B_j>><<B_j| with B_j=B_j^*.

4. TWO STRONG SUBCLASSES
(a) If rho and sigma commute, choose s,q diagonal. For each ordered pair,
let a= rho_i s_j/s_i and b=rho_j s_i/s_j. Then
  E=(1/2)sum_ij |B_ij|^2(sqrt(a)-sqrt(b))^2,
  J=(1/2)sum_ij |B_ij|^2(a-b)log(a/b).
The scalar inequality (a-b)log(a/b)>=4(sqrt(a)-sqrt(b))^2 proves J>=4E.
This uses arbitrary Hermitian B, not [B,s]=0.
(b) If [B,s]=0, then V=B^2 and H(X)=[B,[B,X]]/2. The log sigma term
vanishes, and in the rho eigenbasis the same scalar inequality gives
J>=4E for arbitrary rho. Both results are exact, all-dimensional, and
do not settle the case with all three noncommuting objects.

5. EXACT FAILURE OF A TOO-STRONG CP-GENERATOR LIFT
One might hope that M(I)=0 and M^*=M can be strengthened to: -M is a CP
Markov generator. That would imply M>=0, but the extra assertion is false
even in commuting dimension 3, where section 4 already proves J>=4E.

Take t>1, raw q=(1,t,t^2), raw s=(1,t^2,t^4), normalize q by
sqrt(N), N=1+t^2+t^4, and normalize s by sqrt(1+t^4+t^8).
In the common diagonal basis, M is the Schur multiplier M(E_ij)=m_ij E_ij,
with zero diagonal and
  g(t)=(t^2-1)logt-(t-1)^2,
  m_12=g(t)/N, m_23=t^2g(t)/N, m_13=g(t^2)/N.
Choose c=(t,-1-t,1), so sum_i c_i=0. Direct expansion gives
  sum_ij c_i c_j m_ij
   =(2t/N)[g(t^2)-(1+t)^2g(t)]
   =(2t/N)(t^2-1)(t-1)^2logt>0.
The conditional Choi matrix of -M, tested on sum_i c_i|ii>> perpendicular
to |I>>, therefore has NEGATIVE quadratic form. Thus -M is not a CP
generator. At t=2 the exact negative form is -4log(2)/7.
This is a refuted sufficient route, NOT a counterexample to M>=0 or
J>=2E. It shows that the desired operator lift cannot merely assert that
the entropy quadratic operator is a Lindblad generator.
