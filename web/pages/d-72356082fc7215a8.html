1. NEW EXACT CONSEQUENCE: THE NORM-TRANSFER GAP SURVIVES SELFCOMPATIBILITY

The INITIAL map was not claimed selfcompatible. In fact, the same map is an
explicit entanglement-breaking channel with an explicit two-output broadcaster.
This strengthens the topology obstruction while still not giving a target
counterexample.

Let d be even, U,V traceless Hermitian unitaries, tau=Tr/d, and 0<=t<=1. Set
P_+=(I+U)/2, P_-=(I-U)/2 and
  sigma_+=(I+tV)/d, sigma_-=(I-tV)/d.
The P_s are a POVM. Each sigma_s is a density matrix because V has eigenvalues
+/-1, t<=1, and Tr(V)=0. For every density input rho,
  sum_{s=+,-} Tr(P_s rho) sigma_s
    = [I+t Tr(U rho)V]/d
    = Phi_t(rho).
By linearity this is the exact channel identity
  Phi_t(X)=tau(X)I+t tau(UX)V.
Thus Phi_t is EB. Its full-domain symmetric broadcaster is
  B_t(X)=sum_{s=+,-} Tr(P_s X) sigma_s tensor sigma_s.
It is CPTP (a POVM followed by product-state preparation), invariant under
output swap, and both partial-trace marginals are exactly Phi_t.

The normalized Choi and full-Hermitian-ball norm formulas from INITIAL still
hold:
  Jhat(Phi_t)-Jhat(D)=t(U^T tensor V)/d^2,
  ||Jhat(Phi_t)-Jhat(D)||_2=t/d,
  ||Phi_t-D||_(infinity-to-one,tau)=t,
where D(X)=tau(X)I and equality in the last line is witnessed by X=U.
The upper bound is |tau(UX)| tau(|V|)<=1 for every Hermitian contraction X.
The ratio is exactly d even though Phi_t is EB and selfcompatible. This shows
that no dimension-free conversion based only on normalized-Choi Hilbert-Schmidt
distance controls the entire-ball normalized infinity-to-one map norm, even on
the selfcompatible subclass. It does NOT obstruct EB rounding: Phi_t itself
is an exact EB comparator, and the example has no small-residual /
large-EB-error behavior.

Executable check:

The d=2,4 diagnostics passed for normalized Choi positivity, a positive joint
Choi matrix, trace preservation, both marginals, and the t/d versus t norm
values. Positivity of the broadcaster Choi matrix in every even d follows
directly from
  Jhat(B_t)=d^(-1) sum_s P_s^T tensor sigma_s tensor sigma_s >=0;
the partial-trace identities follow by summing the POVM and tracing each
prepared density. The script is a bounded floating transcription check, not
the all-d proof.

One useful cross-cluster connection is c05_s01's conditional broadcaster
projection construction. It proves a dimension-free induced

is small, with error <=2 sqrt(eta)+eta. This uses reference-Choi TRACE norm;

is finding such a basis/sign system from a general low-energy family (or
proving another EB lift). The pairwise commutator estimate in c04_l02 does
not itself provide that system or a conditioning bound.

3. SOURCE-INTERFACE AUDIT, UPDATED

The peer cross-exposure does not supply a literature theorem that closes the
source-to-target transfer. The exact scope distinctions recorded in INITIAL
remain:
- exact no-broadcasting is a commutativity characterization, not an
  approximate dimension-free modulus for growing bounded families;
- KSW's dimension-free information-disturbance statements use cb/diamond
  control on the appropriate full-channel/decoder interface;
- joint POVM compatibility does not by itself provide a same-system
  nondisturbing instrument;
- Brandao-Harrow's local measurement theorem preserves its stated
  max/expectation order and does not license exchanging it;
- Bose-supported HS de Finetti and CP-order channel de Finetti retain their
  support, local-dimension, and multi-input hypotheses.
My explicit EB channel shows the Choi-HS to full-ball norm conversion fails
even with selfcompatibility. In contrast, reference-Choi trace distance does
control the induced norm by trace duality with A^T tensor B, but obtaining
that trace-distance approximation remains a separate gate.
