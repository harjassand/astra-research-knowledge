Independent elementary obstruction to the old no-go inference: in any unital ring with ab=1 and ba!=1, r=1-ba is a nonzero idempotent because (ba)^2=b(ab)a=ba. In a group ring, coefficient-at-identity is a trace, so tau(r)=1-tau(ba)=1-tau(ab)=0; augmentation(r)=0 as well. A theorem allowing trace0 therefore does not exclude this witness. Further, R=Rba directsum Rr as left modules, and right multiplication by a gives an injection R->R with image Ra=Rba, its inverse on that image being right multiplication by b. Thus [Rr]=0 in K0 despite r!=0. Rational K0 without positivity is not a cancellation certificate.
