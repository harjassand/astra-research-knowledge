ALGEBRA / COMPLEXITY — CYCLE 3 SYNTHESIS
Date: 8 October 2026
Scope: changed-frontier mechanisms and precise barriers. Cycle 2 is preserved. No commits, external messages, or publication.

Decision
--------
The strongest actual algebraic construction is now a finite, rational, positive-support representation of the N125 binomial kernel at scale a=1/N: for inverse-polynomial accuracy it uses Theta(N) atoms and has polynomial bit cost, including root isolation and normalization. The result closes a finite-support/precision bottleneck, but it does not establish an operator-level separability algorithm. On the selective determinant side, a new exponential lower bound rules out every fixed universal positive shortlist of Pfaffian-sign evaluations, even with input-dependent nonnegative coefficients and relative error below one. This remains a representation-class barrier, not hardness for randomized per-input methods or general Z_k algorithms.

The main near-term research gate is therefore not “find a universal short sign list” and not “weighted perfect matching still has no FPRAS.” OA113 source-reports the latter algorithm for simple unweighted graphs, and a separate internally proved binary gadget plus denominator clearing conditionally extends it to nonnegative rational weights. The load-bearing gap is the reverse transfer: handling signed determinant interference in a general rational Z_k input with a positive, input-dependent proposal/representation whose bit height, normalization and conditioning stay polynomial.

Five mechanisms
----------------
1. N125 finite Newton truncation and rational finite support. For every integer N>=1, k=0,...,N, x=(k-N/2)^2 and interpolation nodes A_i=(N/2+i)^2, the degree-(2N-1) Newton interpolant P to exp(-x/N) has positive Newton terms and

       0 <= 1 - P(x)/exp(-x/N) <= 2 exp(-N/40).

For 0<epsilon<=1/2 and N>=40 ln(16/epsilon), the rational-support leaf constructs q=ceil((5N-1)/2)=Theta(N) points p_i in (0,1) and positive rational weights mu_i with |sum_i mu_i Binom(N,k)p_i^k(1-p_i)^(N-k)-exp(-x/N)| <= (epsilon/4) exp(-x/N), simultaneously for all k. The proof uses a finite hypoexponential survival identity, a centered Chernoff bound, positive beta-kernel components and exact Gauss–Legendre integration followed by rational Sturm root isolation/rounding. Polynomial bit/time bounds include exp(8N) Lagrange cancellation (O(N) guard bits), O(N^2) coefficient denominators, O(N^2+log(1/epsilon)) root precision, rational output and stable normalization. Tests: lead checker N=1..400 passes; rational quadrature diagnostic N=1,2,3,4,6,8,10 passes. These are finite diagnostics, not validation of all source claims. Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE. The main falsifier would be a failure of the finite interpolation identity, stochastic domination, or the displayed all-k Chernoff/tail bound. Still absent: a downstream map that accepts this mixture with controlled conditioning, exact representation, or any all-a / broader-scale theorem. Details: `lead_newton_bound/RESULT.txt`, `newton_correction/RESULT.txt`.

2. Sparse noncommutative query and domain barrier. For rational division-free NC formula trees with a supplied unary support bound t<=poly(description size), the N68 affine encoding in dimension 2t gives a one-query zero/nonzero PIT: distinct words receive distinct base-(n+1) integer codes, and 2t matrix moments include a nonsingular Vandermonde system. Dense query size is O(n t^3 log n) bits; full response, coefficient heights and evaluation are charged. A size-(4k-1) product formula with 2^k words refutes replacing t by formula size. Exact examples also show the query is not N51's stronger “defined and invertible original rational tree” interface: x-1 has a nonzero singular response and (x-2)^(-1) is undefined at the matrix query despite being nonzero at a legal scalar point. Replay: 378 sparse rational-polynomial cases pass; product-support and domain witnesses pass. Status: internally proved scoped theorem plus exact obstruction; no novelty claim, no general formula PIT, and no automatic rational-expression transfer. Details: `nc_query_efficiency/RESULT.txt`.

3. Permanent Hessian invariant ceiling. Reconstructing the Mignon–Ressayre zero-Hessian method gives rank Hess(det(M(x)))<=2r at every zero for every affine r-by-r determinant, while an explicit rational zero of perm_m has full Hessian rank m^2. Hence every affine determinant representation has size r>=ceil(m^2/2). Because the invariant saturates the permanent's ambient m^2 Hessian dimension, this method cannot yield a superquadratic determinant-size bound (in particular it cannot beat the cubic target by itself). The unrestricted Hessian variant is false at det_r(I), where rank is r^2. Exact Q checker: orders 2–8 pass. This is a proved ceiling on one invariant, not a general impossibility result and not validation of the missing N79 package. Details: `permanent_invariant/RESULT.txt`.

4. Fixed positive Pfaffian-sign support barrier for the legal full-sector target. On n=2k and rational block-pair matrices with pairs pi and positive rational entries a_i,b_i, the target is product_i(a_i^2+b_i^2), while its normalized Pfaffian-square integrand is product_i(1-gamma_i sigma_{u_i}sigma_{v_i}). Fourier uniqueness across all pairings forces every nontrivial even Walsh coefficient to vanish. Therefore an input-independent exact sign quadrature needs all 2^(n-1) classes modulo global sign, and this is tight. More strongly, for a fixed atom support that must relatively approximate every all-unit pairing instance with error epsilon<1, nonnegative coefficients may depend on the input but the support must hit every pairing event. Each balanced sign splits exactly k! of (2k-1)!! pairings, so support size is at least ceil(binomial(2k,k)/2^k)>=ceil(2^k/(2k+1))=2^Omega(n). Inputs are explicit nonnegative rational matrices; no arbitrary CNF encoding is used. Exact n=6 checker passes all 15 pairings, 31 nontrivial even characters, quotient Walsh rank 32 and the support lower bound 3. Status: internally proved for this fixed-support quadrature class. It does not rule out input-dependent random sampling, signed/implicit aggregation, or a general Z_k FPRAS. Details: `fermionic_reverse/RESULT.md`.

5. Adjacent N129 local budget proof (cross-frontier control, not an algebraic-complexity theorem). Conditional on the provisional PDF's stated guard, interval-count and separator inputs, the page-4 component-selection arithmetic leaves at least 55H/64 mass and produces a component satisfying its closed bounded-system exclusion. The exact proof is in `hyperbolic_local_audit/RESULT.txt`; 1,548 Fraction cases pass. This validates one local extraction step only. The source is a seven-page provisional PDF with no stable public URL/editable supplement, and this does not establish the upstream disk estimates, hyperbolicity or direct-finiteness conclusion. It is retained because the changed frontier intersects characteristic-p/direct-finiteness claims; it is not counted as algebraic-complexity progress.

OA113 correction to the matching baseline
-----------------------------------------
The pinned 23 September 2026 primary manuscript (commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a) states Theorem 1 for finite simple unweighted undirected graphs: rational epsilon in (0,1), delta in (0,1/2), relative 1+-epsilon estimate with probability 1-delta, certain zero output when no perfect matching, and worst-case bit time polynomial in graph input length, epsilon^-1 and log(delta^-1). The theorem does not state weighted input. The theorem and proof spans, source links, exact weight adapter, and status are pinned in `oa113_weighted_transfer/RESULT.txt`.

Separate PC-05 proof: clear rational denominators by D=product q_e and replace each positive integer W_e=Dw_e with a simple-graph port gadget of O(log W_e) vertices and O(log^2 W_e) edges whose perfect-matching boundary signature is (1,W_e,0,0). An even-vertex host graph with r=|V|/2 then satisfies #PM(expanded)=D^r PM_w(G). This gives a polynomial-bit transfer to nonnegative rational weighted PM, conditional on OA113 correctness. The gadget's signatures were exhaustively checked for W=1,...,20; its universal signature follows from the DAG/path proof, not from enumeration. OA113's long proof is supplied by the manuscript but has not been independently audited or formally replayed. Thus say “SOURCE-REPORTED unweighted theorem; conditional rational-weight extension,” not “independently established weighted algorithm.”

The existing reduction `weighted PM -> Z_{N/2}` therefore does not yield a new unresolved-PM approximation consequence. It also does not run backwards. Generic Z_k remains UNKNOWN. The material reverse-route barrier is signed determinant interference; the fixed-support result above rules out a universal positive shortlist but says nothing about proposals chosen from A.

Scope and costs
---------------
No arithmetic-to-bit transfer is implicit. Each claim records its supplied bounds, rational input and coefficient sizes, query/output size, conditioning, normalization, exact-zero branch, and random/runtime costs. For the rational-weight PM adapter, the expanded graph is O(eL) vertices and O(eL^2) edges for binary input length L and e positive edges; D^r has O(rL) bits. For the Newton mixture, numeric N and log(1/epsilon) are charged; for PIT, t is supplied and unary/polynomially bounded; for Pfaffian quadrature, the barrier is only on number of evaluations/support points. Finite checks are scoped evidence and do not replace the proofs or source validation.

Strongest baselines and restart
-------------------------------
Use OA113+PC-05 as the conditional weighted-PM baseline. Use standard sparse NC PIT/N68 as the baseline for the one-query lemma; the result here is an interface reconstruction and barrier, not priority. Use the classical Mignon–Ressayre Hessian theorem as baseline for the invariant ceiling. For the Pfaffian class, compare with the exact full-sector sign identity and direct algorithms on block-pair instances (which themselves are easy); the lower bound is only on a universal fixed evaluation list. For N125, compare to the infinite positive expansion and its strict defect: finite truncation improves finite-scale approximation without removing that defect. Continue by attacking one of two gates: (a) an operator-level downstream consumer with polynomial conditioning for the rational N125 mixture, or (b) a positive input-dependent Z_k estimator/representation with polynomial normalization and exact-zero control that avoids signed interference.
