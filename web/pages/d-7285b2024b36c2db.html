# Independent hostile audit of the corneal cue-pair interaction

The strongest defensible result is a **conditional supra-Bliss excess on a chosen linear K15-GFP MFI-gap scale**, not a model-free or cell-state interaction. Directly reading the official source workbook confirms that the half-dose IGF1+CCL2 group mean is 5.155M MFI, the vehicle-control mean is 5.078M, and the full-dose monotherapy means imply a linear-gap Bliss upper bound of 4.886M. The pair therefore exceeds that bound by 269,189 MFI, or 0.09483 of the control-minus-clodronate gap. If response is capped at the vehicle control, the surplus is 6.77 percentage points.

The algebra is correct under its stated assumptions: both single-factor dose responses are nondecreasing through the half-to-full dose interval, normalized MFI-gap recovery is a valid bounded effect, and independent action means multiplying the remaining gap. The source does not validate those assumptions. MFI is an unbounded continuous signal, not a measured probability of stem-cell conversion. The combination mean is already 1.5% above the control mean, and capping at control is an unmeasured biological ceiling.

This audit supplies two concrete falsifiers of a model-free claim. First, a bounded increasing response map (h(y)=1-e^{-(y-L)/(U-L)}), whose asymptote is 1 rather than the observed control, makes the pair 0.129 below its full-dose Bliss ceiling. Its asymptote is not empirically justified, but it proves the sign can depend on what response maximum is chosen. With the observed control held fixed as the maximum, any monotone transform preserves a positive sign because the pair sample mean exceeds control; only the magnitude changes (6.77 pp linear, 3.67 pp log, 5.06 pp square-root). Second, a monotone additive-MFI model fits the pair mean exactly using unobserved half-dose increments of 1.000M and 1.916M, both below the observed full-dose increments. Therefore, the same data do not identify an interaction independent of the response null.

The group sizes are four. A covariance-aware delta-method contrast that treats the five arm means as independent, approximately normal biological-replicate means gives an excess of 269,189 MFI, SE 60,797, Satterthwaite df 5.09, and approximate 95% t interval [113,755, 424,623] MFI. The shared control and clodronate baselines are included in its gradient. The empirical bootstrap also stays positive, but (n=4) makes its tails weak evidence. The workbook contains no animal, eye, or batch IDs, so replicate uniqueness and within-mouse clustering cannot be checked. The source p-values are pooled unpaired tests of each treatment against clodronate; they do not test the interaction. Reproducing G62 against clodronate gives the stored (p=1.9235\times10^{-7}).

An independent R-base check reproduced the delta-method t intervals and all seven treatment-versus-clodronate workbook p-values to maximum absolute difference 8.7e-19. The pair-specificity contrast has approximate 95% t interval [-0.0136, 0.2355] normalized units, crossing zero (p=0.0753).

The comparison with CXCL16+CXCL10 is descriptive. Their point excess is (-0.01610) versus (+0.09483) for IGF1+CCL2, but a covariance-aware comparison gives approximate (p=0.075). The all-four group shares the pair's half-doses of IGF1 and CCL2 but is 132,806 MFI below it, with SE 68,208; this does not establish pair specificity.

The precise remaining prediction under the declared linear-gap Bliss model is that a true population half-dose pair mean cannot exceed 4,885,560 MFI if the monotherapies are monotone and independent on that scale. Same-dose single-agent means would replace the conservative full-dose ceiling with the exact Bliss prediction. Even if reproduced, the result would concern this marker endpoint only. The paper separately shows lineage tracing and long-term function in natural regeneration; it does not show those functions for the cytokine rescue arm. The article itself notes that CCL2 may recruit additional monocytic cells, so the current assay cannot distinguish a direct epithelial effect from a niche-mediated path.

No novelty or nature-level capability claim is cleared. Bliss and Loewe are established null models, and the paper already reports the same cue combinations as a rescue screen. This audit is a conditional reanalysis of one source endpoint.
