## 1. Exact question and statement

Let f(x)=((Lx)^2)^TQ((Lx)^2), unknown rational invertible L and unknown rational symmetric Q>0. Suppose an unknown permutation makes Q block diagonal with block sizes at most3. Blocks may have arbitrary signs, zero entries, repeated copies and all PD degeneracies. The block partition, Q, L, axes, aligned metric, condition number and hidden coefficient heights are not supplied.

There is a coefficient-only intrinsic polynomial matrix P_tau, of degree6n-4, whose Krylov determinant has degree

    d_new=n+(6n-4)n(n-1)/2.

For all but at most2n rational scalar parameters tau the determinant is nonzero. Every hidden coordinate hyperplane divides it. Existing multiplicity-aware rational-line normal extraction, verified reflections, rational invariant-metric acquisition and the terminal even-QPD/positive weighted-square checker therefore give expected polynomial-bit acquisition on this entire promised family, without first acquiring its blocks. This is a complete arbitrary-dimensional extension of cycle11 binary-block coverage, using a different matrix.

## 2. A normalized rational field and a tensor multiplication

Use the symmetric polarization f(x)=T[x,x,x,x]. Put

    H=Hess f=12T(x,x,.,.),  h=det H,
    F=adj(H) grad h,  V=F/h^2=H^-1 grad log h,
    L_V=12 H^-1 T(x,V,.,.),
    N_tau=Jac(V)+tau L_V.                       (1)

The rational objects are used on h!=0. All polynomial identities below extend across h=0 after clearing denominators. Both Jac(V) and L_V transform by similarity under any linear change x=A z, with no relative character. They are homogeneous of degree-4. The commutative product u star v=12H^-1 T(x,u,v,.) has unit x, so

    L_V x=V,   Jac(V)x=-3V.                    (2)

The second identity is Euler homogeneity of V. Thus the first Krylov vector of N_tau is merely(tau-3)V; the detector does not introduce a second independent vector field at that first step. Higher matrix powers are different from successive flow derivatives. In particular tau=3 is bad whenever n>1.

Clear the common denominator explicitly:

    P_tau=h^3 N_tau
         =h Jac(F)-2F(grad h)^T
                 +12 tau adj(H) T(x,F,.,.).    (3)

Every entry is a polynomial of degree6n-4. For g=f composed with A,

    P_(g,tau)(z)=det(A)^6 A^-1 P_(f,tau)(Az) A,
    K_(g,tau)(z)=det(A)^[3n(n-1)-1] K_(f,tau)(Az),

where K_tau=det[x,P_tau x,...,P_tau^(n-1)x]. Therefore nonvanishing and hidden hyperplane divisibility transfer to the observed rational coordinates. In hidden coordinates sign equivariance makes P_tau preserve each coordinate face, so all its Krylov columns have zero i-th coordinate on y_i=0. Hence y_i divides K_tau, including repeated or extra factors.

## 3. Universal invertibility primitive, requiring no isolated or full-support maximum

For every positive homogeneous real quartic, L_V is generically invertible. This lemma is broader than the QPD class.

First H has an SPD point. Minimize f on the Euclidean unit sphere. At a minimizer u, f(u)>0, grad f(u)=4f(u)u and the constrained second variation gives H(u)>=4f(u)I on the tangent space. Euler H(u)u=3grad f(u)=12f(u)u kills radial/tangent cross terms, so H(u)>0. This minimizer is a proof device; no optimization oracle or supplied metric is used by the acquiring algorithm.

The level f=1 is compact by positive quartic coercivity. Choose any nonempty component of its H>0 subset. Its closure is compact; h>0 inside and h=0 on its boundary, since an SPD-boundary Hessian is singular. Thus h attains a positive maximum at an interior point p of that component. Lagrange multipliers and Euler identities yield

    grad h(p)=(n h(p)/2) grad f(p),
    V(p)=(n/6)p,
    L_V(p)=(n/6)I.                             (4)

Consequently det L_V is a nonzero rational function. The argument does not claim that p is isolated, has full support, attracts an open basin, or can be efficiently computed. Those missing properties blocked earlier flow-attractor arguments but are unnecessary here.

## 4. Cyclicity in dimensions at most three

Let a block have dimension b and denote its normalized field by V_b. Its flow extactic is E_V=det[x,D_Vx,...,D_V^(b-1)x]. Multiplication of a derivation by h_b^-2 changes its rows by a triangular transformation, so E_V=h_b^[-b(b-1)]E_F.

Write A_b=Jac(V_b). By(2),

    det[x,A_b x]=-3E_V                         (b=2),
    det[x,A_b x,A_b^2 x]=9E_V                 (b=3),

because A_b^2x=-3A_bV_b=-3D_VV_b. The all-QPD binary and ternary flow nonvanishing theorems are already independently gated. Therefore A_b has a generically cyclic initial vector for every permitted b<=3. For b=1 the initial coordinate alone is cyclic.

For one block, D_b(tau,x)=det(A_b+tau L_(V_b)) has tau degree at most b and leading coefficient det L_(V_b)!=0. Its Krylov determinant C_b(tau,x) has tau degree at most b(b-1)/2 and is nonzero at tau=0 by the preceding identities. A rational witness x_0 where both det L_(V_b) and C_b(0,x_0) are nonzero exists: the rational functions have a nonempty open intersection. Evaluating at x_0 shows that at most b values of tau can make D_b(tau,.) identically zero, and at most b(b-1)/2 values can make C_b(tau,.) identically zero. This witness is not found or supplied by the algorithm.

Summing over all hidden blocks gives at most

    sum_b b(b+1)/2 <=2n                       (5)

bad scalar values. This count includes zero eigenvalue obstructions, repeated blocks and tau=3; no generic-spectrum input hypothesis is imposed.

## 5. Compose unknown blocks by proof-only scaling

For a direct sum f=sum_b f_b, H is block diagonal, h=product_b h_b and log h=sum_b log h_b. Consequently V, Jac(V) and L_V split into their block versions exactly. Thus N_tau is block diagonal. Using Jac(F) directly instead would introduce derivatives of the other blocks' h factors and destroy this separation; normalization by h^2 is essential.

Fix a good tau from Section4. Each block has a rational point where h_b, its N_tau determinant and its cyclicity determinant are all nonzero. Choose such points only for the existence proof. Under block input scaling x_b->s_b x_b, N_tau scales by s_b^-4. Each block's spectrum consists of finitely many nonzero eigenvalues. Independent positive rational scales can avoid every cross-block eigenvalue collision. Repeated eigenvalues within a block and non-diagonalizable blocks are allowed.

Every block's minimal polynomial has its full block degree by cyclicity; the scales make these polynomials pairwise coprime. The Chinese remainder theorem then makes the direct-sum initial vector cyclic for the full normalized N_tau. Its first n Krylov columns have nonzero determinant. Multiplying column k by the common global h^(3k) recovers K_tau of(3), proving K_tau is not the zero polynomial. No block partition, witness heights, eigenvalues or separating scales are part of the algorithm's input or computation.
