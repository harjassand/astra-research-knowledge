# Acquiring a hidden SOS face through a repeated tensor eigenspace

2026-10-06. Strongest technical result of this lane: a complete exact rational acquisition/synthesis construction for hidden odd-cycle quartics. It handles a regime in which every deterministic second-order covariant is scalar and the relevant fourth-order eigenspace is repeated. The coordinate transform, factor subspace, polynomial zeros, and SOS are acquired, not supplied. This is a restricted theorem with an executable replay, not a general SOS discovery result. Historical novelty and advantage over a full competent polynomial-equivalence solver remain unverified. Independent worker audit is requested; not yet passed.

## 1. Question, interface and source motivation

Given only the expanded rational coefficient list of p in n variables, synthesize an exact nonnegativity proof without receiving a Gram face or enumerating an exponential zero set. Consider the promise, for odd n>=5,

  p(x)=q(Lx)^T Q q(Lx), q_i(y)=yi²−1,
  Q=I+epsilon A(C_n), 0<epsilon<1/4, epsilon rational,
  L in GL_n(Q) unknown.

Neither L nor epsilon is input. The algorithm can scan candidate eigenspaces and verify its final certificate. It returns a verified certificate or UNKNOWN for out-of-class/non-completed acquisition; failure says nothing about nonnegativity. The current code is a class replay and uses assertions for malformed/out-of-class inputs; the theorem's soundness comes from an exact final expansion and positive rational LDL certificate. Arbitrary precision approximation inputs and numerical stability are outside the interface.

Recent starting sources, actual text read: May2026 NSPI (2605.15445v1), August2026 algebraic graph-construction discovery (2608.08118v1), September2026 LieDiscover (2609.33663v1). The latter two emphasize acquiring a symbolic construction/library rather than merely verifying supplied objects. This led to the hidden-coordinate regime here. ## 2. Acquired metric and quartic operator

Q is PD since ||A(C_n)||<=2 and epsilon<1/4. Let b=1+2epsilon>0. Since Q1=b1, the degree-two part of p is

  p2(x)=−2b x^T L^T Lx.

Thus the coefficient list supplies a rational positive-definite metric

  M=−(1/4) Hess(p2)=b L^T L.

Let T be the symmetric rational fourth-order tensor defined by p4(x)=T(x,x,x,x). It is directly acquired from coefficients: divide the coefficient of a monomial by its multinomial multiplicity. Define on symmetric n×n matrices

  C_M(S)_ab=sum_cd T_abcd (M^-1 S M^-1)_cd.

This is a rational N×N matrix for N=n(n+1)/2, computed without L. If S=L^T S' L then C_M(S)=L^T C'(S')L. In y=Lx coordinates, M'=bI, and Tiiii=1, Tiijj=epsilon/3 for cycle edges, Tiijj=0 for nonedges. Therefore C' decomposes into:

* Diagonal matrices: the n×n block b^-2[I+(epsilon/3)A(C_n)].
* Cycle-edge symmetric matrices Eij=ei ej^T+ej ei^T: eigenvalue lambda_e=2epsilon/(3b²), multiplicity n.
* Nonedge symmetric matrices: eigenvalue zero, multiplicity n(n−3)/2.

The diagonal block has eigenvalues at least (1−2epsilon/3)/b², strictly larger than lambda_e when epsilon<1/4. Hence lambda_e is an isolated rational eigenvalue with exactly n-dimensional eigenspace

  U=L^T span{E_i,i+1 : i mod n} L.

Characteristic-polynomial factorization over Q and rational nullspace computations acquire U. The theorem can choose the positive eigenvalue of multiplicity n; the code scans rational multiplicity-n eigenvalues and exact-checks reconstruction. At n=5, the zero-eigenvalue complement also forms a C5 and may be selected first; this does not supply the axes and yields an equally valid recovered face. At n>=7 the nonedge dimension differs from n.

## 3. Every deterministic quadratic covariant is blind

The canonical polynomial is invariant under independent yi sign flips and cyclic coordinate permutations. Any deterministic equivariant symmetric-matrix function F of the polynomial/metric satisfies F(p)=D F(p)D for every sign diagonal D, so F(p) is diagonal. Cyclic invariance then forces all diagonal entries equal. Thus F(p)=cI in canonical coordinates; metric endomorphism M^-1F(p) is scalar in arbitrary coordinates. This holds for every such covariant, of any polynomial degree in T, not only the first contraction or a finite list of them.

This is a restriction on deterministic equivariant SINGLE quadratic summaries. It does not obstruct returning an unordered coordinate-frame set, using randomized symmetry breaking, higher-order tensors, or equivariant subspaces. Orthotropic/cubic symmetry obstructions and higher-covariant reconstruction are established prior mechanisms (Olive et al.2017 Sections4–5). We make no general impossibility claim for competent tensor algorithms.

## 4. Determinant of the repeated eigenspace identifies its hidden edges

Choose any rational basis B1,...,Bn of U and form S(z)=sum_k zk Bk. There are n independent rational linear forms ell_i(z), the hidden edge coordinates, satisfying

  S(z)=L^T [sum_i ell_i(z) E_i,i+1] L.

For an odd cycle,

  det S(z)=2 det(L)² product_i ell_i(z).

Proof by determinant permutations: a nonzero permutation term uses only cycle edges, has no fixed points, and decomposes into oriented cycles. Every proper cycle of the graph is an edge traversed as a 2-cycle. Since n is odd, 2-cycles cannot cover all vertices. The only remaining terms are the two orientations of the full n-cycle, each sign (+1) for odd n. They each use every edge once. Thus the displayed determinant identity is exact for every odd n. Basis independence of the ell_i follows from the fact that B is a basis of the edge space.

The determinant has an exponentially large expanded expression for a generic basis. We do NOT expand or factor it as a dense multivariate polynomial. Its matrix-pencil representation permits the following acquired factorization through rational inverses and traces.

## 5. Recovering the linear factors without a determinant expansion

At z0 with all ell_i(z0)!=0 let r_i=grad ell_i / ell_i(z0), regarded as column vectors. Choose rational direction h. Then

  H2=−Hess_z log|det S|(z0)=sum_i r_i r_i^T,
  H3=(1/2) D_h Hess_z log|det S|(z0)
      =sum_i alpha_i r_i r_i^T,
  alpha_i=ell_i(h)/ell_i(z0).

The r_i are independent, so H2 is PD. Writing R=[r1 ... rn],

  H2=RR^T, H3=R diag(alpha)R^T.

When the alpha_i are distinct, the generalized eigenvectors u_i solving H3 u_i=alpha_i H2 u_i are proportional to R^-T ei. Hence

  ell_j(u_i)=0 for j!=i,

and S(u_i) is a nonzero multiple of the hidden SINGLE edge matrix L^T E_i,i+1 L. The desired edge matrices have therefore been acquired from the pencil, even though its ordinary tensor eigenvalue is repeated.

All ingredients are computed from rational matrices. For A_i=S(z0)^-1 B_i and A_h=sum h_i A_i,

  (H2)_ij=tr(A_i A_j),
  (H3)_ij=(1/2)tr(A_h A_i A_j+A_i A_h A_j).

These identities follow by differentiating logdet: d logdet(S)[B]=tr(S^-1B), dS^-1[B]=−S^-1 B S^-1. Sign/constant in the split determinant disappears under differentiation. Rational factoring of det(tH2−H3), whose roots alpha_i are rational and simple, and rational nullspaces recover u_i exactly. This is the established simultaneous-moment diagonalization mechanism applied to a newly acquired determinant pencil; we do not claim that simultaneous diagonalization itself is novel.

Choose z0,h uniformly from a rational integer grid of size K>=4n² per coordinate. At most n independent linear equations cause ell_i(z0)=0, so the base failure probability is <=n/K. Conditional on a valid z0, each ratio collision alpha_i=alpha_j is a nonzero linear equation in h, so collision probability is <=n(n−1)/(2K). Total probability is <1/4 for this choice. Repeat until exact checks pass: expected constant attempts; a fixed T budget has an explicit failure probability at most4^-T under the promised class. The replay uses a 4n²+1 sized integer grid and a30-trial cap. Exact checks, rather than probabilistic evidence, establish soundness of returned certificates. Intermediate coefficients have polynomial bit size under standard fraction-free matrix/factoring algorithms.

## 6. Edges recover coordinate axes and the SOS face

For an acquired edge matrix S_i proportional to L^T E_j,j+1 L, put A_i=M^-1 S_i. Since M=bL^TL,

  A_i is similar to a scalar multiple of E_j,j+1,
  kappa_i=(1/2)tr(A_i²)>0,
  P_i=A_i²/kappa_i=L^-1(E_jj+E_j+1,j+1)L.

Thus the P_i are commuting rational rank-two idempotents. They can be checked exactly. Their joint eigenspaces are the n coordinate axes: vertex j has the unique two-edge incidence signature {j−1,j}. Acquire the joint spaces by repeatedly splitting each current invariant subspace W into column(P_iW) and column((I−P_i)W). Commutativity guarantees invariance at each split. There are at most n final pieces, all one dimensional. Rational basis vectors assemble an invertible V with LV a permutation times diagonal scaling.

Substitute x=Vy into the original polynomial. It becomes q'(y)^T Q' q'(y) with q'_i=yi²−a'_i, rational ai'>0 and Q' PD. The coefficient-only algorithm in SOS_FACE_DISCOVERY.md recovers Q',a' and exact rational LDL weighted squares. Transforming those factors back yields the input-coordinate certificate. The unique degree-at-most-two Gram face has dimension n and ambient rank n despite the 2^n isolated real zeros. No zero was enumerated and no generator coordinate was delivered to the learner.

## 8. Exact obstruction and next falsifiable step

Even cycles admit two perfect matchings. For C6,

  det S=−(ell0 ell2 ell4+ell1 ell3 ell5)².

The determinant no longer splits into n independent linear factors; the derivative diagonalization proof does not apply. More general graphs introduce many cycle-cover terms. This is the exact failed implication: repeated eigenspace -> independent linear determinant factors. We have not proved impossibility of even-cycle face acquisition.

Next mathematical target: acquire individual hidden edge directions from an even-cycle or a bounded-degree vertex-transitive graph eigenspace using polynomial-cost matrix-pencil invariants, without assuming simple tensor eigenvalues or an axis-separating quadratic covariant. A minor, rank-locus, or exterior-power pencil may replace the determinant, but its factor geometry and bit cost must be proved. Do not supply the hidden graph coordinates as an oracle. A clean first falsifiable fixture is hidden C6 with the same positive metric and exact rational input; require a recovered n-dimensional SOS face and compare total work against exact Gram/facial-reduction and polynomial-equivalence methods.

Concrete downstream use now: a rational acquisition module for symmetry-degenerate proof synthesis, plus a rigorous adversarial family for systems claiming numerical precision or covariance summaries suffice to recover SOS faces. The even-cycle/general-graph regime is the load-bearing missing extension. No claims of universal polynomial SOS recognition, new general tensor decomposition, or AI-specific learning advantage are made.
