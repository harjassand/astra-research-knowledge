# Cycle 3 core lemma: a two-pulse observable despite exact CLE matching

## Interface

Let (X\in\mathbb N^4), (H\in\mathbb Z_3), (H\to H+1\) at unknown rate \(\kappa\), initially uniform, and (X_0=0). Action (a\in\{A,B\}) causes Poisson burst events at rate \(\lambda=2r\); each event adds fixed stoichiometry (v\in\{0,1\}^4). The mark law is uniform iid on 16 masks, (K_{iid}), or uniform on 8 even masks, (K_{even}).

(M_+) uses (K_{iid}) for both actions at (H=0); (M_-) uses it for (A) at (H=0) and (B) at (H=2). Elsewhere both use (K_{even}). (H) is hidden; observe only (P=\sum_iX_i\bmod2) at the end of each pulse through independent error \(\epsilon\).

For every action and (h), both mask laws have identical inclusion moments for any subset of at most three coordinates. Hence (b_i=\lambda/2), (A_{ii}=\lambda/2), (A_{ij}=\lambda/4), and all third jump moments match at every (x,h) under every action mixture. The four-distinct-coordinate jump moment differs (1/16 versus 1/8). Thus full-state CLE drift/covariance equality is exact; only the jump kernel beyond that order and hidden action/phase coupling differ.

## Formula and minimum word

The exact observable reduction has six states ((h,p)). Under a pulse (a), the column generator (Q_a) has (h\to h+1) at rate \(\kappa\) and (p\to1-p) at rate (r\) only in that action's iid phase. Let (mu(h,0)=1/3), (D_p) project onto parity (p), and (E_{p_1p_2}) be the endpoint-parity table after (AB):
\[
E_{p_1p_2}=\mathbf1^\top D_{p_2}e^{\tau Q_B}D_{p_1}e^{\tau Q_A}\mu.
\]
The observed event (Z=1\{Y_1=1,Y_2=0\}) is both noisy increment estimates equal to one because the initial parity is known zero.

Passive and one-letter observed laws agree; same-action (AA) and (BB) laws agree by hidden-phase rotation. Thus a mixed two-letter word is minimum. At \(\kappa=0\), (q=(1-e^{-2r\tau})/2) and (P_+(U=V=1)-P_-(U=V=1)=q^2/3). With known initial parity and endpoint bit error \(\epsilon\), the (Z)-event gap is \((1-2\epsilon)q^2/3\).

## Continuous bounded-rate certificate

For \(\tau=0.38,r=1,\epsilon=0.08\) and unknown \(\kappa\in[0,3]\), a 30,001-point grid (spacing (10^{-4})) plus the bound
\[
|\partial_\kappa P_m(Z=1)|\le4\tau=1.52
\]
certifies
\[
\inf_{[0,3]}P_+(Z=1)\ge0.0772213718,\qquad
\sup_{[0,3]}P_-(Z=1)\le0.0722226256.
\]
The nominal gap is at least 0.0049987462; classify at threshold 0.0747219986. For independent wells, Hoeffding gives error at most (e^{-N\Delta^2/2}); (N=239{,}779) suffices for error 0.05 if pulse rate, duration, and reporter error are exact. With supplied bounds \(|\tilde\tau_a-0.38|\le10^{-5}\), \(|r_a-1|\le10^{-4}\), and \(|\epsilon-0.08|\le10^{-4}\), a Duhamel/emission derivative debit of 0.000512008 per model leaves gap 0.0039747302 and raises the sufficient size to 379,244. This charges one independent AB well per sample plus culture, verified initialization, two snapshots, pulse exposure, and calibration.

The compact-rate condition is load-bearing. At high \(\kappa\), each action averages to \(\bar K=(K_{iid}+2K_{even})/3\) under both models. Exact noisy-endpoint TV falls from 0.01030 at \(\kappa=3\) to 1.12e-5 at \(\kappa=100\); no uniform finite-word test over unbounded \(\kappa\) is possible. Report `UNKNOWN` unless a calibrated upper bound or data-supported rate confidence set stays in the separated regime.
