1. FINITE RANDOM-BIT CLIPPED NORMAL
Code: certified_normal.py. Configured rational 1<=L<=8 and integer 8<=ell<=64.
Put h=2^-ell, q=2^(-ceil(L^2)-3), delta=h q/32, b=ell+ceil(L^2)+8.
All ceilings are of rational numbers. A b-bit uniform integer defines the
dyadic prefix Uhat=floor(2^b U)/2^b of an ideal continuous uniform U, with
0<=U-Uhat<delta. The normal generator computes only this finite prefix.

The standard normal density satisfies phi(z)>=q on [-L,L]. Indeed pi<4 gives
1/sqrt(2pi)>1/8, and log(2)>=1/2 follows by integrating 1/x over [1,2]. Thus
2^(-ceil(L^2)-3)<=exp(-L^2/2)/8<phi(L). No transcendental ceiling or density
oracle is requested. The clipped inverse z(U)=clip(Phi^-1(U),[-L,L]) is
1/q-Lipschitz, including the constant endpoint pieces.

CERTIFIED CDF. Use the identity pi=16 atan(1/5)-4 atan(1/239).
It follows directly from tan(2 atan(1/5))=5/12 and tan(4 atan(1/5))=120/119:
tan(4 atan(1/5)-atan(1/239))=(120*239-119)/(119*239+120)=1.
This angle is in (0,pi/2), so it is pi/4. The branch is fixed by the positive
elementary integral bounds for arctan; no numerical angle selection is used.
Alternating rational arctangent partial sums have error at most their next term.
Square-root/inversion intervals therefore enclose c=1/sqrt(2pi).

For z>=0, integrate the Taylor polynomial of exp(-t^2/2):
 I_n(z)=sum_(j=0)^n (-1)^j z^(2j+1)/(2^j j! (2j+1)).
The scalar Taylor remainder of exp(-x), x>=0, has magnitude at most
x^(n+1)/(n+1)! because its Lagrange derivative magnitude is <=1. Hence
 |integral_0^z exp(-t^2/2)dt-I_n(z)|
   <=z (z^2/2)^(n+1)/(n+1)!.
The implementation advances the exact rational recurrence until this is
<=delta/32, then encloses Phi(z)=1/2+c I(z). Phi(-z)=1-Phi(z).
Every returned CDF interval is checked to have width <=delta. At each point
the interval contains the exact CDF. Fixed guard failure raises rather than
emits a certificate. P=b+ceil(log2(L+1))+32 working bits; its log2 ceiling is
computed by powers-of-two comparisons of rationals.

BISECTION. Compare Uhat to the enclosures at -L,L, then to midpoint CDF intervals.
If a comparison is unresolved, return that midpoint/endpoint immediately.
Its CDF difference from U is <=2delta, so its distance to the clipped ideal
normal is <=2delta/q=h/16. If all comparisons resolve, a bracket width <=h/2
gives midpoint error <=h/4 relative to z(Uhat), plus delta/q=h/32 from coupling U.
Therefore the returned rational normal satisfies, for EVERY U under the prefix
coupling,
 |Zhat-clip(Phi^-1(U),[-L,L])|<=9h/32<h.
No infinite comparison refinement occurs, and endpoint equality is allowed.

The omitted unclipped tail has probability <=2 exp(-L^2/2), by a Gaussian
Chernoff bound. The code certifies a rational upper bound on this expression
using 2 divided by a positive Taylor LOWER sum for exp(L^2/2). A cap of D draws
would have union probability <=min(1,D times this bound). This is not by itself
a bound for an importance-weighted normalized Gibbs law; that application must
use its complete coupling/weight budget. A discrete normal always has TV=1
relative to a continuous normal, so no discrete-to-continuous TV claim is made.

2. GAP-FREE PSD ROOT AND RESIDUAL CERTIFICATE
Code: certified_psd_root.py. Input is a rational symmetric 3-by-3 C with acquired
PSD certificate and absolute row-sum norm <=2. q=2^-ell, configured ell in [2,32].
All seven principal minors are evaluated exactly. For a Hermitian 3-by-3 matrix,
their nonnegativity is equivalent to PSD: use a positive diagonal pivot and its
Schur complement, or, when a diagonal is zero, the 2-by-2 minors force its row
and column to vanish. This gives an elementary inductive proof.

Put alpha=q^2/64, D=C+alpha I, choose the smallest dyadic gamma>=1 with
gamma^2>=row_norm(C)+alpha, and w=q^2/[64(gamma+1)]. Choose k so gamma/2^k<=w.
The exact Newton path starts X0=gamma I and uses
 F(X)=X/2+(D X^-1+X^-1 D)/4.
Since exact iterates commute with D, each eigenvalue update is
x_next=(x+lambda/x)/2. When x>=sqrt(lambda),
x_next-sqrt(lambda)=(x-sqrt(lambda))^2/(2x)
                    <=(x-sqrt(lambda))/2.
Thus all exact iterates have least eigenvalue >=sqrt(alpha)=q/8 and final
operator error to sqrt(D) <=gamma/2^k<=w. No eigenvalue gap of C is needed.

In the q/32 neighborhood of this path, inverse norms are at most 16/q.
The inverse identity gives a conservative Lipschitz factor
 Lnewt=1+4(row_norm(C)+alpha)/alpha
for the symmetric map. Choose dyadic working precision P so the nearest-entry
rounding operator error 3/(2*2^P) is at most
 r=min(w,q/8)/(16 k Lnewt^k).
Then the perturbation recurrence bounds every rounded path error by
 k Lnewt^(k-1) r<=w/16, which closes the neighborhood induction.
The code also checks each rounded X>=q I/16 by exact principal minors.
This verifies the inverse floor in the executed run rather than hiding it.
P=O(ell^2+Lin) for fixed norm bound; exact 3-by-3 rational inverse intermediates
have polynomial heights in P+Lin, since every update is rounded back to dyadics.
There are O(ell) matrix steps. This is deliberately conservative guarded work,
not a claim of optimal precision or a new Newton method.

FINAL INDEPENDENT CERTIFICATE. The code computes the rational residual
 R=X^2-C and delta=max_i sum_j |R_ij|.
Since R is symmetric, ||R||op<=delta. The final exact checks X>=0 and delta<=q^2
prove ||X-sqrt(C)||op<=q without a lower eigenvalue of C.
Here is a complete proof of that familiar square-root continuity bound.
For PSD A,
 sqrt(A)=(2/pi) integral_0^infinity A(A+t^2 I)^-1 dt,
by diagonalizing A and the scalar arctangent integral. Positive-definite matrix
inversion reverses order. Thus A<=B implies
A(A+t^2I)^-1<=B(B+t^2I)^-1, proving square-root order preservation on integrating.
From C<=X^2+delta I, this gives sqrt(C)<=sqrt(X^2+delta I)<=X+sqrt(delta)I;
the last step is scalar spectral calculus of X. From X^2<=C+delta I obtain
X<=sqrt(C+delta I)<=sqrt(C)+sqrt(delta)I. Combining both inequalities gives
-sqrt(delta)I<=X-sqrt(C)<=sqrt(delta)I and the asserted norm bound.
This proof works without commutation of X and C. It is established matrix
analysis, not a claimed novel theorem; the useful increment is the executed
exact rational certificate/interface for spin-filter columns.

The three acquired columns of X approximate those of sqrt(C) within q, and
sum_j X_col_j X_col_j^T=X^2 approximates C within delta. Entrywise enclosures
X_ij+[-q,q] follow from the operator norm. No spectral axes or eigenvalue oracle
has entered the construction. Extremely small positive eigenvalues are allowed.
