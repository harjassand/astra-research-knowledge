1. METRIC, CARRIERS AND PRECISE SCOPE

Take the compact connected simply connected group with Lie algebra
so(2l+1), sp(l), or so(2l), respectively (B_l, C_l, D_l). The same
conclusion applies to a central quotient when the selected irrep
descends. B_l is simple for l>=1, C_l for l>=1, D_l for l>=3;
D_2 is a product and must be treated factorwise. Ranks and Lie type
remain FIXED before N and memory Q.

Realize the compact Lie algebra as a real vector space of Hermitian
defining matrices X. For orthogonal types X=iA with A real skew;
for C_l use Hermitian X satisfying X^T J+JX=0 with the usual real
symplectic J. Conjugation is the compact adjoint action. Give this space
the invariant real metric

                         <X,Y>=(1/2)Tr_fund(XY).             (1.1)

Choose a real orthonormal Hermitian basis t_A, A=1,...,q, so here
Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=2 delta_AB, rather than the SU(m) packet's delta/2.
Every carrier Hermitian generator T_A corresponds to this same basis.
Energy means E(F)=sum_A||[T_A,F]||HS^2 in THIS specified metric.
Changing metric by a fixed factor rescales constants, not the exponent.

The orthogonal Cartan matrix is block diagonal with blocks
 i[[0,z_i],[-z_i,0]], plus a zero entry for B_l. The symplectic Cartan
matrix is diag(z_1,...,z_l,-z_1,...,-z_l). In either case the Cartan
basis h_i is orthonormal for (1.1), so the Cartan point has coordinates
z_i and norm squared sum z_i^2.

Supply an irreducible carrier V_(N lambda), where N>=1 and the
normalized dominant integral weight lambda=highest-weight/N ranges in
a FIXED compact set K inside the OPEN dominant Weyl chamber. Equivalently

Allowed weights and the representation are supplied, not acquired from

The supplied orbit is

  tau_(h,g)=exp[(h/N)U_g T_H U_g^dagger]/Z_h,
  T_H=sum_A x_A T_A,            h in [h0,h1].                (1.2)

2. ODD MATRIX POWERS ARE EXACT EQUIVARIANT GRADIENTS

For every j>=1 define a real homogeneous invariant polynomial

              f_j(X)=Tr_fund(X^(2j))/(4j).                  (2.1)

The gradient in metric (1.1) is the EXACT Lie-algebra-valued polynomial

                          Z_j(X)=X^(2j-1).                   (2.2)

To prove this, cyclicity of scalar matrix trace gives
df_j(X)[Y]=(1/2)Tr(X^(2j-1)Y). The odd power lies in the Lie algebra:
for SO it is still Hermitian and transpose-skew; for Sp the identity
X^T J=-JX gives (X^(2j-1))^T J=-JX^(2j-1). Thus no projection error
or scalar identity component is present. Equivariance follows directly
from powers of conjugate matrices. All component coefficients are real
homogeneous polynomials in the real coordinates of X, because both
X^(2j-1) and t_A are Hermitian.

On the Cartan, the gradient's components are

             v_j(z)=(z_i^(2j-1))_(i=1,...,l).                 (2.3)

For B_l and C_l take j=1,...,l. The l-by-l value matrix M with columns
v_j has the EXACT determinant

        det M=(prod_i z_i)prod_(i<k)(z_k^2-z_i^2).            (2.4)

This follows by factoring z_i from row i and taking the Vandermonde in
u_i=z_i^2. The B_l roots are e_i, e_i±e_k and the C_l roots are 2e_i,
e_i±e_k. Therefore (2.4) is nonzero at every regular Cartan point and
vanishes precisely at their root hyperplanes. No Chevalley restriction
or polynomial-generation theorem is needed for this assertion.

3. THE EVEN-ORTHOGONAL PFAFFIAN REPAIR, INCLUDING z_l=0

For D_l, taking all l odd powers would wrongly require prod z_i!=0.
The D_l roots are e_i±e_k; a regular Cartan point MAY have one zero
coordinate. Fix instead j=1,...,l-1 and the extra invariant

                          f_pf(X)=Pf(-iX).                   (3.1)

Here -iX is real skew, and the usual polynomial Pfaffian is real on
this compact real space. Its invariance under SO(2l), hence Spin(2l),
is exact: Pf(R A R^T)=det(R)Pf(A)=Pf(A). A direct proof expands the
l-th exterior power of the associated two-form; changing basis multiplies
the top exterior form by det R. Invariance under the disconnected full
O(2l) is FALSE; a determinant-minus-one element changes its sign.

The gradient Z_pf in metric (1.1) is a real homogeneous equivariant
polynomial of degree l-1, obtained by differentiating the Pfaffian's
finite matching sum. Its defining trace-coordinate components are

            Z_(pf,A)(X)=df_pf(X)[t_A].                       (3.2)

At a Cartan point f_pf=prod_i z_i, so its Cartan components are

                  w_i(z)=prod_(k!=i) z_k.                   (3.3)

Every invariant gradient at a Cartan point lies in the Cartan: it is
fixed by its torus stabilizer, whose Lie-algebra fixed space is exactly
the Cartan. Hence the coordinate derivatives in (3.3) are the complete
gradient, not just a projection which could miss other components.

Let M_D have columns v_1,...,v_(l-1),w. Its determinant is

           det M_D=(-1)^(l-1)prod_(i<k)(z_k^2-z_i^2).         (3.4)

For all z_i!=0, factor z_i from each row. The last column becomes
(prod z_i)/z_i^2. Multiplying rows by u_i=z_i^2 and moving the last
column to the first reduces to the Vandermonde in u_i, with the sign
(-1)^(l-1); the factors cancel since (prod z_i)^2=prod u_i.
Both sides of (3.4) are polynomial, so equality extends to points with
zero coordinates without dividing by zero. Exactly the D_l regular
condition z_i^2 distinct remains. In particular z_l=0 is permitted.
Repeated degrees when l is even cause no degeneracy: (3.4) already
establishes independence of the two same-degree covariants if present.

4. UNIFORM CLASSICAL GRAM AND EXPLICIT CONDITIONING

Choose R>0 and epsilon>0 fixed from compact regular K such that

compactness, the root lists, and regularity. For D_l no bound below on

  B_l,C_l: det H >= epsilon^(2l^2),
            Tr H <= A_BC=l sum_(j=1)^l R^(4j-2);

            Tr H <= A_D=l[sum_(j=1)^(l-1)R^(4j-2)+R^(2l-2)].  (4.1)

With A the appropriate fixed trace bound and D its determinant bound,

                  lambda_min(H)>=kappa=D/A^(l-1)>0.         (4.2)

For rank one the exponent zero is understood literally. These are
explicit positive Gram bounds for actual polynomial gradients on the

invariant ring; it only needs l independent covariant vectors.

  C_N=N^2|lambda|^2+N beta(lambda), beta=2<lambda,rho>,

  E_(Y_s)(F)<=C_s E_T(F)/N^2,
