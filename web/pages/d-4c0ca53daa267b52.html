## Independent deductions that constrain further transfer

1. **Cyclic collision and repair.** The3-state series Σ_{k≥1}(x₁ᵏ−2x₂ᵏ) is nonzero but has identically zero unmarked scalar hyperlog image. This holds in every jet dimension. Its full matrix is not zero; the failure is specifically the first-column extension. Marking λ^|w| makes the image nonzero over F(λ); the same m-dimensional Moura bound controls the z jet, and λ degree≤z degree lets a nilpotent λ shift retain it. The36-dimensional m3,n2 example has marked first-column coefficient−1/4 at λ¹z².
2. **Commutative shuffle obstruction.** The complete shuffle lift of x₁⋯x_n contains a coefficient-flattening identity minor of order binom(n,k). Every weighted-state realization, even cyclic, must therefore have dimension≥binom(n,⌊n/2⌋). An O(n)-size commutative monomial can require exponentially large NC representation in this proposed repair. The handoff’s qz+[a,b] construction remains faithfully noncommutative regardless of the abelianization of q. This excludes a specific full-shuffle bridge, not general PIT.
3. **Exact samples are insufficient at polynomial cost.** Distinguishing Bernoulli1/2 from Bernoulli1/2+2^−B with success≥2/3 needs at least2^(2B)/18 samples. Thus an exact identity/matrix oracle is not uniformly acquired at polynomial sample cost from iid data. Approximate prediction may ignore below-tolerance differences; no universal approximate-learning impossibility follows.
4. **KL-close teachers can remain full rank.** For a horizon2L fair-bit target, K=2^L prefix/suffix probabilities form a rank1 matrix. Mix ε of the law that repeats its L-bit prefix. The teacher has matrix eigenvalues1/K and ε/K with multiplicityK−1, hence rankK, while KL(target||teacher)≤−log(1−ε), independent of K. Exact acquisition correctly keeps the teacher’s tiny directions. It does not produce the compact approximation guaranteed by the target’s rank.
