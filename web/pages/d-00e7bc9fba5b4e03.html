C04_L02 REVISION 02 — AUDIT OF THE LATE c04_l10 POWER ROUTE
7 October 2026. INITIAL hash remains bf3ae2a9fdbfff5aa6684ab35eae05bc5ab30f42cbe441c012c639b77dc5fd0e. REVISION-01's exact weighted block lemma and diagnostic are unchanged.

ATTRIBUTED CLAIM

After its INITIAL became available, I read c04_l10/INITIAL.txt, SOURCES.json, verification.txt and verify_symplectic_dirichlet.py. The worker claims: if a bistochastic UCP map Phi is selfadjoint on normalized Hilbert-Schmidt space and Phi^2 is EB, then Psi=Phi^2 is a selfadjoint EB comparison map with I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi). It also gives an imported symplectic-covariant PPT channel family whose square is EB, and imports an index-3 EB-power theorem for the source's class DSP_2. Origin is c04_l10, not c04_l02.

INDEPENDENT ALGEBRA CHECK

For any selfadjoint linear operator Phi on the finite-dimensional Hilbert space L2(tau),

  2(I-Phi)-(I-Phi^2)=(I-Phi)^2 >= 0.                       (R2)

If Phi is a channel and Phi^2 is EB, composition closure gives a UCP/TP EB map Psi=Phi^2. Selfadjointness is inherited by Phi^2, so Psi is a valid quadratic comparison object. This calculation does not require Phi>=0 on L2; indeed the factorization (R2) is exact. Thus the worker's conditional power-to-Dirichlet implication is internally correct.

The source claims were checked against primary arXiv HTML after the Luna initial snapshot appeared. Prudhoe v3 states PPT-squared for all Cartan-covariant channels and specifically derives that the square of the extreme non-EB symplectic PPT channel lies in its EB region (https://arxiv.org/html/2501.03959v3, abstract and symplectic-channel discussion around lines 37-39 and 230-242). Park v2 states finite EB index for every PPT map but explicitly says a channel-independent index bound is unknown, while Theorem 1.2 gives index at most 3 for PPT maps in DSP_2 (https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13551v2, lines 61-76 and 77-109). These support the imported scope as written. They do not establish that every compatible channel is PPT, belongs to DSP_2, or has a bounded EB index.

TARGET BOUNDARY

The power route is a valid additional subclass, but it does not attack the all-frame gate for arbitrary selfcompatible Phi unless one proves the missing implication “selfcompatible => an EB power with uniformly bounded exponent” or another uniform EB-power condition. No such implication is in c04_l10, the baseline, or the sources checked. Composition of a broadcaster with itself keeps powers compatible but does not make a finite power exactly EB by itself. In particular, a finite shareability observation is not separability.

There is a simple exact rejection of the stronger tempting shortcut “every selfcompatible channel has an EB square.” The 1-to-2 universal qubit cloner has the actual symmetric joint output, so its marginal is selfcompatible, and its Bloch shrink is lambda=2/3 (Werner, arXiv:quant-ph/9804001; also the c04_l03 calibrated derivation). Its square is the qubit depolarizing channel with shrink 4/9. The normalized Choi state is J_lambda=(1-lambda)I_4/4+lambda|Omega><Omega|, and J_lambda^Gamma=(1-lambda)I_4/4+(lambda/2)F, whose antisymmetric eigenvalue is (1-3lambda)/4. At lambda=4/9 this equals -1/12, so the square is NPT and therefore not EB. The cube has shrink 8/27<1/3 and is EB by the qubit depolarizing separability criterion, giving an EB power but not the c=2 power shortcut. This rejection leaves open whether some other EB map attains the sharp c=2 comparison; indeed c04_l04/l07's cloner analysis supports that such an EB comparison can exist even when Phi^2 is not EB.
