# Sources, priors, and scope

Date: 2026-10-08.

## Primary theorem sources checked

1. U. Haagerup, “The Grothendieck inequality for bilinear forms on C*-algebras,” *Advances in Mathematics* 56 (1985), 93–116. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(85)90026-X>. The theorem gives the four-state inequality for bounded bilinear forms with coefficient \(\|u\|\); the paper also records a Hilbert factorization for maps into a dual space with product bound \(2\|T\|\), and the little-GT state domination for Hilbert-valued maps.
2. U. Haagerup and M. Musat, “The Effros–Ruan conjecture for bilinear forms on C*-algebras,” arXiv:0711.1851. <https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1851>. Theorem 1.1 gives the jointly completely bounded version with constant one relative to the jointly completely bounded norm.
3. D. de Laat, “On the Grothendieck theorem for jointly completely bounded bilinear forms,” arXiv:1210.3256. <https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.3256>. Used as an alternate proof/interface reference, not as a channel-measurement result.

The constants above belong to their stated theorem norms. They are not asserted to be constants for the target order inequality. In particular, the jcb constant one is relative to \(\|u\|_{jcb}\); this audit did not bound the relevant jcb norm by a dimension-free quantity while also constructing a legal measurement.

## Mathematical priors and exact scope

- The supplied symmetric broadcaster \(\mathcal B\) is a unital CP map in the Schrödinger convention, with both marginals \(\Phi\), and \(\sigma_{AB}=\mathcal B(I/d)\) has tracial marginals.
- The desired witness is an actual finite positive \(AB\)-POVM whose pullback effects \(M_y=\mathcal B^*(E_y)\) sum to \(I\), with real estimators satisfying exact first moments and the quadratic penalty gate (G*).
- The ordinary noncommutative Grothendieck and little-GT theorem conclusions are state/functional or factorization bounds. Their hypotheses and norms were not silently replaced by a POVM-valued factorization.
- The \(A_d\) example is a comparison of an unconstrained trace-class 2-summing norm with the canonical support under barycenter \(I/d\). It is not a broadcaster, channel-comparator counterexample, or impossibility theorem for refinements of Grothendieck factorization.
- Finite canonical completion from arbitrary state-valued atoms enforces the input barycenter, but it does not by itself give an \(AB\)-effect preimage under \(\mathcal B^*\). Only the actual \(AB\) sub-POVM completion preserves the supplied physical interface automatically.
