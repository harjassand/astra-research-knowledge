The strongest result is the extracted Schatten-3 triangle certificate.
The entire dyadic triangular manuscript (October 5, 2026) was read and
its finite matrix, conditional-child, total-energy, telescoping and
density steps reconstructed. The same proof extends its constant 40
to every base q≥2, complex L³ inputs, and arbitrary real child-constant
mean-zero multipliers bounded by one. The q-independent step is a
two-way Jensen rounding of the q-way partition. The complex phase
mechanism also appears in the annular companion's matrix section,
which was subsequently read in full; that part is not a new priority
claim. The q-uniform extension is a deduction of the release mechanism,
with broader novelty unresolved.

Pairwise independence of child coordinates is essential. Uniform
marginals alone permit an exact 2×2 example with local trace −1/8 and
energy gap 3/4−√2<0. This records a concrete obstruction to transferring
the dyadic proof to arbitrary correlated or geometric refinements.

The second result is a generalized capacity decomposition. The entire
diagonal restriction manuscript (September 24, 2026) was read. Its
Lemma 3.2 extends to any positive-density exponent r>1: for n weighted
atoms and a legal geometric test capacity, there are at most
`min(n,2+floor((1+1/(r−1))log₂ n))` disjoint coordinate pieces with

```
Σ_j m(u^j)K(u^j)^{r−1}
 ≤ [4^{r−1}C_geo^{r−1}+1] ∫H^r.
```

The bound has no dependence on coefficient dynamic range. If weights
are squared complex coefficients and the oscillatory map is linear,
the output norm power is 2r and the resulting grouping loss is
J^{2r−1}; r=3/2 reproduces the source's log² loss. The capacity oracle's
acquisition cost remains separate from the logarithmic piece count.

The full sharp restriction claim remains conditional in this report on
the sphere packet propagation Theorem 6.4 and the 3D Kakeya maximal
Theorem 1.1. I read the exact packet interface and targeted portions of
