# Finite H completions do not enter the positive isotropic-orbit route

This strengthens the receiving applicability of the inherited orbit
audit. It is not a new QC proof or historical-priority assertion.

The earlier sharp-ray exclusion alone would leave a logical loophole:
J_r outside a positive source cone does not imply J_r+cH remains outside
that cone. The common shear contact closes this loophole.

Use the source class and compact acoustic base in the assigned W
`CLOSED_ORBIT_ADDENDUM.txt`: positive sums of left-SO(2) averages of
globally ROC two-sided-invariant quartics under invertible real left/right
changes, plus left-invariant convex quartics, then coefficient closure.
Every averaged orbit has

    E(A)=a q_T(A)^2+b q_T(A) j+c j^2,
    q_T(A)=tr(A^T A T), tr T=1, T>0,
    a>=0, |b|<=4a sqrt(det T)<=2a.

The same representation and bounds hold for coefficient limits after
normalization, allowing T>=0. Indeed a is bounded by its rank-one
coordinate evaluations, b by the displayed bound, and c by E(I)=a+b+c;
the trace-one PSD T have a compact base. No source QC assumption is
required for these necessary coefficient bounds beyond source ROC.

Suppose such an orbit limit has the shear contact D2E(I)[e12,e12]=0.
Write T=[[x,z],[z,y]], x+y=1. Exact differentiation gives

    0=a(8z^2+4y)+2by.

If a=0, b=0 and E=cj^2. If a>0,y>0, the equation forces

    b=-2a-4az^2/y <=-2a.

But |b|<=4a sqrt(det T)<=2a. Equality is therefore forced throughout:
z=0, det T=1/4, T=I/2, b=-2a. Hence

    E=(a/4)H+(c-a)j^2.

If y=0, PSD forces z=0, T=e1 tensor e1 and b=0, so

    E=a u^2+c j^2.

ROC forces each remaining determinant-square coefficient nonnegative
(the contact-face acoustic gamma test, or a direct rank-one Hessian
evaluation). All these orbit limits are globally PC. Convex source
limits are also globally PC.

The inherited acoustic functional Lambda is strictly positive on every
nonzero invariant ROC quartic and has compact normalized base. Every
element of the closed positive source cone is a finite positive
combination of points in the closure of its normalized source class.
If the resulting element has the shear contact, nonnegative acoustic
evaluations force every point used with positive weight to have that
same contact. The preceding classification makes every such summand PC.
Therefore

    closed positive isotropic-orbit-plus-convex cone
       intersect shear-contact face
