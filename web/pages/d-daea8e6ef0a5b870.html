Title:On a conjecture of Serre on abelian threefolds

Authors:Gilles Lachaud, Christophe Ritzenthaler
            
View a PDF of the paper titled On a conjecture of Serre on abelian threefolds, by Gilles Lachaud and 1 other authors

            Abstract:  In this article, we give a reformulation of a result from Howe, Leprevost and Poonen on a three dimensional family of abelian threefolds. We also link their result to a conjecture of Serre on a precise form of Torelli theorem for genus 3 curves.
