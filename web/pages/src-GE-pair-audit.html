    integral_0^T P(X>u)P(Y>T-u)du <= E[X]E[Y]/T.

    sum_e r_e E_e[tau] = binomial(n,2)/2

    TV(P_r(T),P_q(T))

    TV(original endpoint,capped endpoint)

version replaces the square by the sum of component squares. Taking
