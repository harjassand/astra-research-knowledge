# One-sided radial discriminator: exact established prior

7 October2026. This closes the requested quick prior check. The positive result is established prior, not a novelty lead; our β>0 convex-weight proof is a standard independent reconstruction of a weaker special case.

**Primary:** Escauriaza–Kenig–Ponce–Vega, *Unique Continuation for Schrödinger Evolutions, with applications to profiles of concentration and traveling waves*, arXiv:1010.1906v1, submitted10October2010 10:06:48UTC; journal CMP305(2),487–512(2011), DOI10.1007/s00220-011-1256-3. Theorem5, equations1.27–1.31, is explicitly stationary: Δw+Vtilde w=ζw, w∈H¹, Vtilde bounded, Vtilde=Vtilde1+Vtilde2, |Vtilde1|≤c1⟨x⟩^(−1/2), Vtilde2 real and supported outside the unit ball, −min(∂rVtilde2,0)≤c2/r. One sufficiently large fixed exponential L² weight implies w=0. Theorem6 is its linear-weight Carleman estimate. Section6 retains the symmetric operator's square as well as the commutator; an auxiliary weighted identity produces coercivity even though the radial Hessian of r vanishes. This is stronger than requiring every exponential weight and r^(−1−β) radial control. [Primary](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1010.1906v1).

## Exact application to our sign convention

Let physical V∈C¹(R^n;R)∩L∞ obey (∂rV)_+≤C/r eventually, and suppose (−Δ+V)u=0 with e^(Ar)u∈L² for all A>0. Choose a smooth radial cutoff χ=0 for r≤1 and χ=1 beyond a sufficiently large radius covering the hypothesis's compact exception. Put

    Vtilde=−V, Vtilde2=−χV, Vtilde1=−(1−χ)V, ζ=0.

Vtilde1 is compactly supported and bounded, so |Vtilde1|≤c1⟨x⟩^(−1/2) after adjusting c1. On the exterior, −min(∂rVtilde2,0)=(∂rV)_+≤C/r. In the finite transition annulus its radial derivative is bounded by C¹ regularity and bounded V, hence ≤c2/r with a larger c2. Inside the unit ball Vtilde2=0. This proves the source's coefficient hypotheses exactly. No tangential-derivative magnitude, lower bound on ∂rV, sign of V, or spectral nonnegativity is supplied or needed.

Bounded-potential Caccioppoli with exhausting cutoffs gives u∈H¹ from u∈L² (and local elliptic regularity). A stationary u satisfies the source eigenvalue equation. The source selects a finite λ0 depending on its coefficient bounds; our all-A premise includes that weight. Hence u=0.

In particular β=0 already belongs to established prior. There is no reason to retain the relative first-jet all-exponential question as unresolved under this exact one-sided C/r upper bound. A meaningful weaker condition would have to depart from it, for example a genuinely unbounded positive r∂rV, or an averaged condition not implying pointwise C/r control. No assertion about those weaker classes is made here.
