Q07 critical-qubit precision crossover
2026-10-08 | Status: MIXED / CONDITIONAL

Model: reconstruct rho_b^tensor n, rho_b=(I+b.sigma)/2, uniformly on the critical qubit ball ||b||<=R/sqrt(n), with all input-dependent memory counted. D=sum_x d_x is total hybrid algebra dimension; the bit/qubit-equivalent budget is log2 D.

Above the finite-n bridge, epsilon*sqrt(n)->infinity, the inherited N08 iid-to-Gaussian comparison has error O_R(n^-1/2). Combined with N07's three-dimensional Gaussian memory theorem, it gives the conditional sharp rate

    D_opt=Theta(epsilon^-3/2),   log2 D_opt=(3/2)log2(1/epsilon)+O(1).

The upper is classical and the lower permits quantum/hybrid memory. For epsilon=n^-alpha, alpha<1/2, this costs (3alpha/2)log2 n+O(1) bits. The transfer says nothing sharp when epsilon=O(n^-1/2).

An exact Schur-sector cutoff gives an all-epsilon quantum upper. Retain the spin-L block for L<=J and send the remaining Schur mass to a one-dimensional overflow branch. The exact qubit sector law and a binomial local-tail bound imply P(L>J)<=epsilon for J=O_R(sqrt(n[1+ln(1/epsilon)])). Since dim(V_L)=L+1, all retained branches together have

    D<=C_R n[1+ln(1/epsilon)],

and full Schur storage gives the sharper cap D=O(n^2) at extremely small epsilon. In qubits this is at most log2 n+log2(1+ln(1/epsilon))+O_R(1), capped by 2log2 n+O(1). This is an upper bound, not a matching quantum optimum.

For an equal-information classical control at the bridge scale, N01's universal qubit coherent archive has at most 2(n+2)^5 labels and error 2delta/(1+delta)=O_R(n^-1/2) throughout this ball: at most 5log2 n+O(1) bits. It meets sufficiently large constant multiples of n^-1/2, but not smaller targets.

If every classical/measure-and-prepare archive has the reassignment-supplied floor e_EB>=c/sqrt(n ln n) on this same family, then epsilon_n=1/(sqrt(n)ln n) separates the models: the Schur code has D=O_R(n ln n), or log2 n+log2 log n+O(1) qubits, while every classical archive fails by a factor c sqrt(ln n), regardless of its label count. This separator is conditional: I did not locate that EB theorem in the local cards or primary sources. N39's worker bound has different assumptions and does not establish this claim for bounded critical kappa.

Unresolved strip: (n ln n)^(-1/2)<<epsilon<=O(n^-1/2), when epsilon*sqrt(n) does not diverge. The Gaussian rate does not transfer there, and the stated EB floor does not rule out classical archives. The Schur code gives only an upper bound. The next decisive audit is the exact family and proof behind the logarithmic EB floor.

Primary comparator: Yang, Chiribella & Hayashi, arXiv:1606.02893v3, establishes Schur-based compression for fixed-spectrum qubit sources, but not this moving-critical all-epsilon rate. Guţă & Kahn, arXiv:quant-ph/0512075v3, gives local qubit LAN about a fixed polarized reference; it does not certify the inherited maximally-mixed bridge rate.
