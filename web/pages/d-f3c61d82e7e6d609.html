1. GENERATOR AND CENTRAL CHARACTER CONVENTIONS

Our Hermitian t_A satisfy Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2 and
[t_A,t_B]=i f_ABC t_C. On V_(a,b) tensor V_(b,a), identified with
HS(V_(a,b)), set X_A=T_A left, Y_A=-T_A right. These are two copies
of su3 commuting with one another, and K_A=X_A+Y_A=ad(T_A).
The source's traceless matrix
generators are represented by

 e_ij^(1)=2sum_A(t_A)_(ji)X_A,
 e_ij^(2)=2sum_A(t_A)_(ji)Y_A.                            (1.1)

Its highest-weight parameters are shifted: m1=a+1,m2=b+1,
m1'=b+1,m2'=a+1. For an output constituent with Dynkin(p,q),
m1''=p+1,m2''=q+1. These shifts cannot be omitted in the explicit
tridiagonal coefficients. Source quadratic/cubic Casimirs satisfy

 K_source=2C2, L_source=2C3,
 l1=2C3, l2=-2C3 on our tensor product.                  (1.2)

For example its eigenvalue formulas at the shifted parameters agree
exactly with these factors. On an output LT block of ordinary Casimir
c, source k3=2c.

2. POLARISED TRACE CONVERSION AND THE AFFINE MAP

Let A3=d_ABC Y_A X_B X_C, B3=d_ABC X_A Y_B Y_C, and Z=sum X_A Y_A.
The elementary defining trace is
Tr(t_A t_B t_C)=(d_ABC+i f_ABC)/4. Substitution into the source's
oriented polarised traces gives

 T_source^(1,1,2)=2A3-3Z,
 T_source^(1,2,2)=2B3-3Z.                               (2.1)

The degree-two correction follows from
sum_AB f_ABC X_A X_B=(3i/2)X_C and the identical Y relation.
It cancels in their difference. The source chooses

 X_source=(T^(1,1,2)-T^(1,2,2))/2+(l1-l2)/3,

so on our representation

                 X_source=A3-B3+4C3/3.                 (2.2)

Our EXACT invariant mixed commutator operator, previously derived, is

 M=sum_A ad(T_A)ad(S_A)
  =[2C3+A3-B3-alpha LT]/N.

Combining with(2.2), with no asymptotic approximation, yields

                 M=[X_source+2C3/3-alpha LT]/N.         (2.3)

Changing polarised-trace orientation or exchanging the two tensor
factors changes the sign of X_source; equation(2.3) uses precisely
the source equation(16) convention and X=left,T;Y=minus-right,T.
Do not substitute a half-normalized A3/B3 for the quantities above.

3. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SUPPLY TO THE WEIGHTED GATE

On a block LT=c>0, the already proved invariant row-operator bound is
LS>=M^2/c. Hence it gives the useful spectral lower

 L_w>=c/N^2+(X_source+2C3/3-alpha c)^2
                                    /(c N^3 sqrt(C_S)). (3.1)

In particular an eigenvalue below t can be counted using a source-X
window centered at alpha c-2C3/3 of halfwidth

                       sqrt(c N^3 sqrt(C_S)t).           (3.2)

The additional factor N comes from converting M into X_source and
must not be dropped. The equivalent window for M has halfwidth
sqrt(c N sqrt(C_S)t).

Our posterior measures full LS energy, not X_source alone. Positive
supplementary channels LS-M^2/c may be essential. Therefore source-X
anti-concentration is a sufficient route to a lower bound when strong
enough, not a necessary interface assumption or a proved sharp model
of LS. The source's exact tridiagonal action supplies a concrete
multiplicity calculation; it does not by itself prove the weighted
spectral-count hypothesis H or any wall capacity law.

4. EXACT QUARTIC MAP: THE FULL SECOND-TENSOR OPERATOR

The source's quartic centralizer also gives the SUPPLEMENTARY channels
exactly, rather than retaining only M^2/c. Define

 C_D=sum_A(D_X)_A(D_Y)_A, Z=sum_A X_A Y_A,
 Omega=C2(C2/3+1/4).

Use the fundamental operator matrices U=sum t_A X_A,V=sum t_A Y_A.
Although Y is minus right multiplication, its symmetric quadratic
D_Y is plus right multiplication by D; hence D_X-D_Y=ad(D).
The defining multiplication table and the su3 commutator give

 U^2=(C2_X/6)I+sum t_A[(D_X)_A/2-3X_A/4],

and the same identity for V. Reversing the two fundamental matrices
in this square changes the minus sign to plus. Their ordinary
fundamental traces therefore give

 T_source^(1,1,2,2)=(4/3)C2_X C2_Y+2C_D
                                      -3(A3+B3)+(9/2)Z,
 barT_source^(1,1,2,2)=(4/3)C2_X C2_Y+2C_D
                                      +3(A3+B3)+(9/2)Z.

These formulas respect the noncommuting order within each tensor copy;
the two copies commute with each other. Also T_source^(1,2)=2Z and
T_source^(i,i)=2C2_i. Substitution into source equation(17) yields

                Y_source=2C_D-C2_X C2_Y/3-Z^2/3+Z/2.    (4.1)

On our identical-dual tensor product, C2_X=C2_Y=C2 and
Z=(LT-2C2)/2. Since N ad(S)=D_X-D_Y-alpha K, expanding its square gives

 N^2 LS=2Omega-2C_D-2alpha N M-alpha^2 LT.

Using(4.1) and simplifying exact scalar Casimir terms gives

 N^2 LS=-Y_source-LT^2/12
                     +(C2/3+1/4-alpha^2)LT-2alpha N M.   (4.2)

Equivalently, using(2.3),

 N^2 LS=-Y_source-2alpha X_source-LT^2/12
       +(C2/3+1/4+alpha^2)LT-(4/3)alpha C3.              (4.3)

In particular on a Casimir-c multiplicity block the FULL weighted
operator is exactly

 L_w,c=cI/N^2+(1/(N^3 sqrt(C_S)))
   [-Y_source,c-2alpha X_source,c
    -c^2 I/12+(C2/3+1/4+alpha^2)cI-(4/3)alpha C3 I].     (4.4)

This is an exact full block, not the weaker cubic-square proxy. Given
the primary source's explicit common tridiagonal matrices for X,Y,
it supplies a concrete tridiagonal realization of L_w,c. Highest-weight
label shifts and the actual positive basis metric must be included
when using that realization. No eigenvalue count, ellipticity or
anti-concentration is asserted merely from its tridiagonal shape.
