1. CANONICAL MEASUREMENT CHANNELS AND THE JOINT-VARIANCE MINIMAX

For any finite POVM M_y in A, put p_y=tau(M_y)>0, ignoring zero effects,
and define

  Psi_M(A)=sum_y [tau(M_y A)/p_y] M_y.                       (1)

Its Schrodinger form is the full-domain EB map

  rho -> sum_y Tr(M_y rho) [sigma M_y/p_y].                 (2)

Centrality of sigma ensures sigma M_y is positive. Equation (2) preserves
sigma exactly. Psi_M is UCP, tau-preserving, selfadjoint and positive in
L2(tau), and Psi_M<=I follows from scalar/POVM Schwarz:

  <H,Psi_M H>_tau=sum_y tau(M_y H)^2/p_y <=tau(H^2).        (3)

For the measurement channel alone, sandwiched Renyi-2 data processing is
exactly the elementary loss identity

  Q_sigma(rho_H)-Q_p(M rho_H)
      =tau(H^2)-sum_y tau(M_y H)^2/p_y
      =<H,(I-Psi_M)H>_tau.                                (4)

Let E={H_x} and define

  q(E)=inf_M sup_x <H_x,(I-Psi_M)H_x>_tau.                 (5)

Rank-one-refined kernels suffice in (5) and form a compact convex set in
each finite dimension. Convexity follows by retaining the coin selecting
a POVM as part of its outcome. Refining each effect into rank-one effects
within individual matrix blocks can only INCREASE every score in (3), by
weighted scalar Cauchy--Schwarz. Rank-one kernels are finite convex
combinations of a compact set of pure-state frame operators, subject to
their reference-mean constraint; Caratheodory gives a finite outcome bound
depending on dimension only. This is an existence argument, not an outcome
or compilation efficiency claim.

For a full matrix algebra, rank-one POVMs correspond exactly to pure-state
ensembles sigma=sum_y w_y |psi_y><psi_y|, with M_y=d w_y|psi_y><psi_y|.
For a direct sum, use pure states supported in individual blocks and the
fixed total reference weight of each block. Define, for a prior p over x,

  V(p)=sum_x p_x tau(H_x^2),
  C(p)=max_(pure reference ensembles)
       sum_y w_y sum_x p_x <psi_y|H_x|psi_y>^2.             (6)

Finite-dimensional convex minimax gives the exact formula

  q(E)=sup_p [V(p)-C(p)]
      =sup_p min_(pure reference ensembles)
          sum_y w_y sum_x p_x Var_(psi_y)(H_x).            (7)

For finite E, this is ordinary bilinear compact-convex minimax. For a
possibly infinite E take its compact closure in the finite-dimensional
bounded likelihood space; finite priors approximate the displayed
supremum. There is no hidden fixed-label assumption. Equation (7) is a
COLLECTIVE joint-variance convex roof, not separate minimization for each
observable. Minimizing each variance independently would always give
zero for a single tracial observable and would lose the common measurement.

Since (I-Psi_M)^2<=I-Psi_M and weighted trace Holder gives ||Z||_1<=||Z||_2,
one actual canonical EB map satisfying (5) yields

  e_EB(E) <= (1/2)sqrt(q(E)).                              (8)

Conversely, let ANY EB map Lambda have half-trace error <=e on the physical
experiment, including sigma. Factor it as preparation after its POVM M.
For an ambient full-matrix map, first pinch its outputs into A and its POVM
effects into A. Output pinching cannot increase error on the promised
block-diagonal states; effect pinching preserves all their probabilities.
Thus the same error bound has an EB factorization with its POVM in A.
Renyi-2 DPI gives measurement loss <=Lambda's Q loss. The already audited
sandwich continuity gives, with d_H,d_sigma<=2e,

  q(E) <= 8 theta e+6 theta^2 sqrt(2e).                    (9)

No exact sigma-preservation by Lambda is assumed in (9). If Lambda fixes
sigma exactly the sharper q(E)<=4theta e holds. Taking infima preserves
these inequalities. Thus q(E)->0 is EXACTLY equivalent to uniform EB
error->0, with explicit dimension-independent conversions. This does not
prove that broadcasting makes q small; it isolates its joint witness.

3. AN ACTUAL ALL-DIMENSION POVM / LUDERS FACTOR-TWO THEOREM

Fix ANY POVM {M_y} in the tracial algebra, and define its nonselective
Luders channel

  L(A)=sum_y sqrt(M_y) A sqrt(M_y).                        (13)

Both L and Psi_M are UCP and tau-preserving. They are positive selfadjoint
L2(tau) contractions. Positivity of L as a Hilbert-space operator follows
term by term from
Tr[A* K A K]=||K^(1/2) A K^(1/2)||_2^2 for K=sqrt(M_y)>=0,
including arbitrary central block weights.

In fact

  0<=Psi_M<=L<=I.                                         (14)

To prove the nontrivial middle inequality, diagonalize one effect M within
each block. Its contribution to <H,LH> is a sum of NONNEGATIVE off-diagonal
terms and the diagonal terms sum_a w_a m_a |H_aa|^2. Its contribution to
<H,Psi_M H> is |sum_a w_a m_a H_aa|^2/(sum_a w_a m_a).
Weighted scalar Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the latter by the diagonal part of
the former. Sum over effects. This does not assume the different effects
commute or that L and Psi_M share eigenvectors.

There is an explicit full-domain CP instrument-append joint channel:

  B_0(rho)=sum_y sqrt(M_y) rho sqrt(M_y)
                    tensor [sigma M_y/p_y].                (15)

  2(I-S)-(I-Psi_M)=I-L>=0.                                (16)

Thus (11) holds with c=2 and an explicit reconstruction (2). Applying

3A. ARBITRARY BISTOCHASTIC INSTRUMENTS: EXACT PETZ-CORRECTION EXTENSION

Supply an arbitrary finite Kraus family K_y in a full matrix algebra with

  sum_y K_y* K_y=sum_y K_y K_y*=I.

Thus L(A)=sum_y K_y A K_y* is bistochastic; its trace adjoint is
L*(A)=sum_y K_y* A K_y. In the full matrix case sigma=I/d. More generally
the same construction works for K_y belonging to the tracial block algebra
A, so that they commute with its central sigma and both sums remain I.
No block-changing Kraus representation is silently assumed here. All
full-matrix bistochastic channels admit such a supplied Kraus interface.

Put M_y=K_y* K_y, p_y=tau(M_y), and Psi=Psi_M. Define

  C=(L+Psi)/2,  Phi=C* C.                                 (16a)

Psi is EB, UCP, tau-preserving and a positive selfadjoint L2 contraction.
L is an arbitrary bistochastic L2 contraction, with L*L<=I. It need NOT
be selfadjoint or positive. C is UCP and reference-preserving. Its actual
joint channel is the equal average of the instrument-append map

  B_K(rho)=sum_y K_y rho K_y* tensor [sigma M_y/p_y]

and its receiver-swapped version. Its common physical marginal is
C_phys(rho)=sigma C(sigma^(-1)rho).
Every summand is CP, B_K is TP by sum_y K_y*K_y=I, and the prepared
states have trace 1. The other normalization sum ensures that L and C
preserve tau and their physical maps fix sigma. The tracial Petz recovery
is the actual channel R_phys(rho)=sigma C*(sigma^(-1)rho). Applying
R_phys to BOTH outputs of this joint channel gives the physical marginal
sigma Phi(sigma^(-1)rho) with Phi=C*C, UCP, tau-preserving, positive
selfadjoint in L2(tau), and selfcompatible. This explicitly retains the
physical/likelihood-map conversion for arbitrary central block weights.

4. TENSOR AND MIXTURE CLOSURE WITHOUT LOCAL ORDERING OR COMMUTATION

Suppose 0<=Psi_j<=I and 0<=S_j<=I as Hilbert-space operators, Psi_j is
EB, and I-Psi_j<=c(I-S_j), with the SAME c>=1. No Psi_j<=S_j assumption
is needed. Put a=1-1/c and b=1/c; then S_j<=a I+b Psi_j. Tensor order
for positive operators and the exact two-factor identity give

  S_1 tensor S_2 <=(a I+b Psi_1) tensor(a I+b Psi_2),
  [a I+b(Psi_1 tensor Psi_2)]
      -[(a I+b Psi_1) tensor(a I+b Psi_2)]
    =ab (I-Psi_1) tensor(I-Psi_2) >=0.                     (17)

Thus S_1 tensor S_2<=a I+b(Psi_1 tensor Psi_2), equivalently
I-Psi_1 tensor Psi_2<=c(I-S_1 tensor S_2). Induction gives the SAME
constant for any number of factors. Neither local simultaneous
diagonalization nor Psi_j<=S_j was used. Positivity of both S_j and
Psi_j IS required for tensor order. Tensor products of canonical EB
maps are canonical EB maps, and products of actual broadcasters are
broadcasters. Therefore the corrected instrument class (16a)-(16b)
retains the factor two under arbitrary finite tensor products.

Convex mixtures of paired (S_j,Psi_j), with one common reference trace,
preserve every Loewner inequality; mixtures of EB maps are EB. Conjugation
by a trace-preserving unitary automorphism preserves them as well. Direct
sums with inherited faithful trace weights also preserve them.
Here a direct sum acts block by block; this closure does not assert
arbitrary additional cross-block mixing.

An earlier PSD telescope proof used the stronger condition Psi_j<=S_j.

is not an attempt to replace negative affine bounds by positive parts;

5. VALID INFORMATION-CHAIN INPUT, AND THE MISSING UPGRADE

There is a useful dimension-independent entropy continuity bound in the
exact-reference sandwich. If alpha sigma<=rho,nu<=beta sigma and both
have trace 1, log monotonicity gives

  log(alpha)I<=log(rho_t)-log(sigma)<=log(beta)I,
  rho_t=(1-t)rho+t nu.

Differentiate D(rho_t||sigma); trace-zero differentiation permits subtracting
the midpoint scalar. Therefore

  |D(rho||sigma)-D(nu||sigma)|
      <=(1/2)log(beta/alpha)||rho-nu||_1.                  (18)

For any ensemble prior, the exact identity
chi=sum_x p_x D(rho_x||sigma)-D(mean||sigma) gives

  |chi(E)-chi(Phi_*E)|<=log(beta/alpha) r.                 (19)

This proof has no log(dim), minimum eigenvalue or entropy-of-label factor.
Here alpha=1-theta, beta=1+theta. It validates the intended bounded-capacity
entropy control after correction; ordinary Fannes continuity is unnecessary.

A binary tree of m actual selfcompatible splitters has 2^m outputs with
common marginal Phi_*^m and full-trace residual <=m r, by contraction and
telescoping. This is sharper than counting every leaf as a separate error.
The complete output ensemble has Holevo information <=input capacity C
by data processing; each marginal retains its original chi up to the bound
in (19) with r replaced by m r. None of these facts forces a measurement
on other outputs to recover the remaining STATES in trace norm.
