# Cycle18: an acquired certificate for direct kernel dependence

Author: pde_gate10, 7 October 2026. Companion to `CAUSAL_QUERY_ORACLE_BOUNDARY.md`, C6–C7. This is an explicit finite experiment interface; routine identities and sensitivity estimation are prior mechanisms. It is not a new universal observational causal-discovery theorem.

## P1. Legal input and output

Input: a binary target T; candidate A; a full noncandidate pre-target assignment Z; an acquired probability law λ(Z)π(A|Z); a model probability q(A,Z) in[0,1]; and the ability to repeat an actual target experiment independently at the SAME(A,Z). Under a fully observed ordered SCM, positivity and a minimal target-kernel DAG, p(a,z)=P(T=1|a,z) is the mechanism kernel. A witnessing query law must assign positive mass to any proposed parent dependence. Whole-prefix experimental reset/replay, randomized treatment/context access and physical feasibility are supplied and charged. Merely querying a learned model does not supply these experiments.

Output: a simultaneous finite-confidence certificate of D(p)>0, where

\[
D(p)=\mathbb E_{Z,A,A'}[p(A,Z)-p(A',Z)]^2,
\quad A,A'\text{ independent with law }\pi(\cdot|Z).
\]

As in C6, a certificate concerns direct conditional-mechanism support in this minimal kernel DAG. It does not identify an individual's counterfactual actual cause or infer a hidden-variable DAG.

## P2. Observable model error without an entropy floor

Draw n iid(A,Z) pairs from the actual query law ρ. At each pair perform two conditionally independent Bernoulli target measurements Y_1,Y_2, and compute

\[
W=(q-Y_1)(q-Y_2).
\]

Conditional independence gives E[W|A,Z]=(q−p)^2. This equality is exact; no unknown H(P) or perfect benchmark model is involved. If both outcomes agree, W is q² or(1−q)² in[0,1]; if they disagree it is−q(1−q) in[−1/4,0]. Thus W lies in[−1/4,1], width5/4.

For J candidate tests, Hoeffding and a union bound give, with probability≥1−α/2, simultaneous squared prediction-error upper bounds

\[
e_j^2:=\mathbb E_\rho(q-p)^2\le U_j,
\qquad
U_j=\max\left\{0,\overline W_j+
\frac54\sqrt{\frac{\log(4J/\alpha)}{2n}}\right\}.
\tag{P2}
\]

The max does not invalidate coverage. Since paired errors share Z and have conditionally independent A draws,

\[
|\sqrt{D(p)}-\sqrt{D(q)}|
\le\sqrt{2}\,e_j\le\sqrt{2U_j}.
\tag{P3}
\]

This is a query-law validation certificate. It avoids observational overlap κ because the actual validation experiments are taken under the query law; the price is acquiring that experimental law. Validation samples must be independent of model selection, or a valid selection-adjusted bound is required.

## P3. Two model queries per paired score trial

Independently take N iid(Z,A,A') trials, evaluate q(A,Z),q(A',Z), and average their squared difference. A second Hoeffding/union event of probability≥1−α/2 gives

\[
D(q)\ge L_j:=\max\left\{0,\overline D_{q,j}−
\sqrt{\frac{\log(4J/\alpha)}{2N}}\right\}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\sqrt{D(p)}\ge\max\{0,\sqrt{L_j}-\sqrt{2U_j}\}.
\tag{P4}
\]

Accept when L_j>2U_j. Simultaneously for all J candidates, accepted tests cannot be nonparents on the event above. No statement of complete discovery without a witnessed effect margin and adequate model accuracy is made. For a root effect margin λ=sqrtD, plain Hoeffding requires n,N of order λ^(−4) log(J/α) to make all squared-score/error tolerances order λ². A model whose query-law error is already much smaller than λ is still a premise for power; the observed U certifies it when the sample budget is large enough. These are conservative rates, not optimality claims.

If model probabilities are rounded and clipped to absolute error u, the two-query root-score discrepancy is≤2u. In P4, subtract another2u when the error validation refers to an unrounded q but score uses a rounded q. More simply, use the SAME rounded/clipped q in W and D, so P2–P4 apply directly to that implemented predictor. For the compact bit ledger, every implemented q is rounded on ONE fixed B-bit dyadic grid, denominator2^B. Products then have denominator2^(2B), and the empirical W and D denominators have O(2B+log(n+N)) bits. This height assertion does not apply to arbitrary unrelated rationals of individual height B; their combined denominator can grow with the number of terms. Conservative rational upper/lower approximations to the displayed confidence radii can be used in the acceptance inequality. Model evaluation, context-law sampling and physical target-reset costs are additional.

## P4. Direct experiment comparator with four measurements per trial

A model is optional at this interface. At one shared Z, choose A,A' independently. Obtain independent replicas Y_(a,1),Y_(a,2),Y_(a',1),Y_(a',2), and set

\[
V=(Y_{a,1}-Y_{a',1})(Y_{a,2}-Y_{a',2}).
\]

Then E[V|Z,A,A']=(p(A,Z)−p(A',Z))² and V∈[−1,1]. With M iid trials per candidate, all direct estimates obey

\[
|\overline V_j-D(p)|\le
\sqrt{\frac{2\log(2J/\alpha)}{M}}
\]

with probability≥1−α. Positivity of the lower interval certifies D(p)>0 without a model-error oracle. For a known squared-effect margin d_0>0, take M≥8d_0^(−2)log(2J/α) to make the error at most d_0/2. The comparator pays4JM actual conditional target measurements. P2–P3 instead use2Jn validation measurements plus2JN model probability queries. Thus cheap prediction queries can reduce repeated physical measurements only when model accuracy is certified; no hidden zero-cost experiment is introduced.

## P5. Consequence and scientific limits

The ancestor chain in C2 gives D_A=0 and D_M=1/8 when the complete noncandidate context is retained, so the certificate targets the desired parent set. The XOR mechanism in C4 gives D_A=1/8 despite cancellation of its marginal intervention mean. The rare-context model in C5 has D(p)=1/8 and D(q)=0 under R=1, while its repeat-experiment error is e²=1/16; P3 pays exactly the missing query-law error rather than importing tiny observational loss.

Prior: the paired squared-difference statistic is the established Sobol total-effect identity; conditional independent-replicate debiasing, Hoeffding and change of measure are standard. Panin1912.11070v2 §3.1.1–3.1.2 already gives model-approximation/changed-sampling-law sensitivity bounds and positive-total-effect thresholding, with its displayed holdout RMSE example explicitly noiseless. Therefore the transfer/thresholding mechanism is established. Novelty of this particular noisy conditional-experiment causal-model audit packaging is UNKNOWN and no broad priority claim is made. The concrete use is to validate proposed direct mechanisms in an experimental simulator or resettable controlled system, then compare that acquired certificate with observational-prediction claims under the same contexts.
