1. TREE-BAG AXIOMS VERSUS PROOF-LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

I checked the source's quadrangulation section directly (OA113 build/main.tex, §§“Cells on a colored alternating cycle,” “A two-coordinate energy inequality,” lines 1181–1518 and proof lines 1557–1982 at pinned commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a).

At a cycle vertex v the source fixes S_v to the one or two labels of its incident cycle edges. Each S_v is either a singleton or an adjacent pair in the label tree, so the cyclic edge-label sequence is a walk on that tree. An odd arc is admissible exactly when its endpoint label sets intersect; the bag clique then supplies a chord in any common label. A long arc is good when its two leaves share a label, permitting the leaf-repair edge. The recursive splitting lemma cuts every simple even alternating cycle into (m−2)/2 quadrilateral cells whose sides are admissible odd arcs; every cell has a good opposite pair, and if both sides in one opposite pair are long, the other opposite pair is good. The split is constructive in O(m²) label comparisons. These precise conditions, not merely bounded treewidth, ensure every conversion used in the signed identity is a legal matching pattern.

For each cell the four side chords, arc-boundary edges and leaf repairs lie within tree distance 6. Activity bounds h_l/D≤W≤3h_l therefore give pairwise factor at most 3D·2^6=192D, enlarged to A0=1000D; common clamping is log-Lipschitz and preserves this ratio. This yields local safe-switch acceptance ≥A0^−2, color-change acceptance ≥A0^−1, and the aggregate switch/color/cycle energy bounds stated in INITIAL.

The exact abstract interface needed by this proof can be stated without the literal tree-bag syntax: (i) a polynomial-time certificate procedure that, for every discrepancy cycle, builds the stated admissible-arc quadrangulation and good-arc repairs; (ii) all edge weights compared in one cell are within a factor A0 polynomial in the numerical parameter D along each annealing law; (iii) two-matching encodings for switch and context demands have polynomial preimage load and polynomial weight distortion; (iv) an involution on demand pairs preserves product stationary mass and sends context errors to legal cycle-swap energy. The tree-bag axioms provide (i) and (ii), while the explicit two-matching encodings prove (iii)–(iv). This is the least restrictive interface I can justify from this proof. It is not a logical-minimality theorem for the inequality: another representation/kernel may satisfy the four obligations by different means.

An important boundary in the source is that the identity's long-arc conversions are not necessarily transitions of the chain. Their status is an exact signed algebraic identity with errors controlled by pair-cycle swaps. Describing the proof as ordinary canonical paths or assuming every long conversion has a legal bounded-cost path would misstate the argument.

2. DETERMINANT-VOLUME PRIOR ART: EXACT INTERFACE COMPARISON

The verified primary reference Anari–Oveis Gharan–Rezaei (PMLR 49, 2016), Theorem 2 and Corollary 3, proves rapid mixing for the lazy one-coordinate exchange Metropolis chain on k-homogeneous strongly-Rayleigh laws. Its Poincare constant is ≥C_mu≥1/(2kn); its state is one supported k-subset and its move replaces one selected element by one unselected element. k-DPPs are covered because DPPs are strongly Rayleigh and truncation preserves that property. Its support is matroid bases. The same paper identifies k-volume sampling weights det(X_S X_S*) as k-DPPs after factoring the ensemble matrix. This is an excellent comparator for ordinary volume sampling, but it assumes a different support and proves a different inequality.

The 2020 primary “Log-Concave Polynomials IV” result covers down-up walks on matroid bases and distributions of homogeneous log-concave polynomials, including determinantal distributions, with O(k log k) mixing. It likewise concerns a single state and a down-up exchange chain. Neither reference supplies a two-coordinate kernel with cycle swaps, arbitrary f1,f2, or Var_pi(f1)≤L E(f1(x)+f2(y)). I also searched targeted primary literature queries for a DPP two-replica/additive pair-energy theorem and found no matching result. This is a bounded search result, not an exhaustive novelty claim.

The target c_k(F) is a pair-indexed block restriction of a 2k-column volume law via P(I)={e_i,f_i:i∈I}. The directed 4-cycle from INITIAL proves this restricted pair-index support may fail matroid basis exchange, so neither the standard strongly-Rayleigh support theorem nor Anari's one-element exchange chain is directly available. A representation in the ambient 2n-column DPP does not solve the issue: the target only retains block-complete subsets P(I), a conditioning/restriction for which the needed stability and support properties have not been established.

3. NEW EXACT WEIGHT-DISTORTION DIAGNOSTIC

I added work/cycle6/c01_l01/det_reassignment_ratio.py and outputs/round6/c01_l01/det_reassignment_ratio.json. For

    F_delta = [[0,δ,1,0],[δ,0,0,1],[1,0,0,1],[0,1,1,0]],

take two complementary minor states initially (I,J)=({0,1},{2,3}) and its reverse ({2,3},{0,1}). Their determinant amplitudes are 1 and 1. Reassign the same four indices to ({0,2},{1,3}) and its reverse ({1,3},{0,2}); both switched amplitudes are δ. All four line activities can be 1 and the union of the selected labels is unchanged, yet the two-replica squared-determinant weight ratio is

    (δ²·δ²)/(1²·1²)=δ^4.

With δ=2^−b, the matrix has O(b)-bit rational entries while the reassigned-pair weight is 2^−4b. The executable checks this exactly for b=1,4,8,16; the symbolic minors establish the family formula. Thus a determinant counterpart cannot inherit OA113's safe-switch acceptance estimate from local activity comparability alone. This refutes that particular proof transfer, not every possible kernel: an alternative could use a different transition graph, preserve a richer determinant state, or route around the reassignment.

4. STATUS AND NEXT GATE

No direct determinant-volume additive pair-energy theorem was identified in primary work checked. The precise missing theorem remains a computable, reversible microscopic determinant kernel and a polynomial, annealing-uniform additive energy coefficient; it must not query aggregate hole partitions. The exact `δ^4` example makes local activity ratios insufficient, while the cycle support obstruction makes the standard strongly-Rayleigh exchange route insufficient. Neither is a general no-go theorem. Next useful attack: look for a richer state/transition representation whose forward/reverse capacity ratios are controlled by computable local Gram data and whose demand encodings retain independent replica orientations. Do not claim this construction exists until proved.
