## Geometry and source precision

The four exact source-face normals and their maximizing-arrow projections were reconstructed independently. The open vertex cones are positive combinations of adjacent normals, so the certificate covers every nonzero direction. The reaction vectors span R^2 and maximal reactant molecularity is five.

Use [Anderson-Cappelletti-Kim-Nguyen Definition 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.05328), which excludes directions orthogonal to the stoichiometric span. Here that means nonzero directions. [Xu v2 Definitions 4.2-4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2) print a strong condition for all u without excluding u=0; a literal strict inequality at zero is impossible for every network. This is a source wording caveat, not a mathematical failure of the intended geometry. The network is not weakly reversible, and the target theorem makes no such claim.

One metadata correction is advisable: `chemistry/session.json` lists a "Finite CTMC" assumption. The state space is countably infinite N_0^2; the reaction list is finite. A finite-state chain with fixed finite rates cannot have this explosion. The actual theorem statements use the correct state space.

[Xu Theorem 4.6 and Remark 4.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2) were opened at the primary current source: they state order-two regularity and the minimal-order question alongside an order-ten example. This audit does not certify novelty, absence of an equivalent known order-five example, or the order-three/four boundary. The cited order-two bound plus the internally reviewed order-five example supports an interval 3 through 5, conditional on the ordinary source theorem and with priority still UNKNOWN.

## Independent checks and false extensions

`independent_checks.py` was written without reading the target check scripts. Exact rational diagnostics passed 36,864 active state/parameter cases across 49 rate tuples, including the fixture, every adverse corner of the stated closed parameter box, and c0=1/1000, 1, or 10^6. `COUNTEREXAMPLES.md` gives proof-level counterexamples to several false extensions:

- Stopped D1 marks need not be independent of D: from (3,3), D=1 forces D1_count=1, with positive-probability witness 36/205.
- Requiring the fast-only minimal excursion forever has probability zero because the excluded slow U hazard is harmonic.
- An explicit irreducible CTMC has explosion probability between 1/3 and 2/3. Positive explosion probability and irreducibility do not supply the audited renewal theorem.
- With c0=0, (0,0) is absorbing; with c2=0 an axis start is a nonexplosive constant-rate input process. If the zero-rate c5 arrow is removed, the active graph loses endotacticity.
- The strongly endotactic order-five network 0<->5A is nonexplosive. Order and geometry alone do not force explosion.
- Ordinary-power rates without falling-factorial disabling can jump to negative populations. Linear escape with rate n has infinite physical time. Almost-sure explosion gives no deterministic sure deadline.
- The exact network's product 3A lies outside the source hull; strong endotacticity tests maximal sources, not containment of every product.
