Title:Thermodynamics of the Coarse-Graining Master Equation

Authors:Gernot Schaller, Julian Ablaßmayer
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Thermodynamics of the Coarse-Graining Master Equation, by Gernot Schaller and Julian Abla{\ss}mayer

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:We study the coarse-graining approach to derive a generator for the evolution of an open quantum system over a finite time interval. The approach does not require a secular approximation but nevertheless generally leads to a Lindblad-Gorini-Kossakowski-Sudarshan generator. By combining the formalism with Full Counting Statistics, we can demonstrate a consistent thermodynamic framework, once the switching work required for the coupling and decoupling with the reservoir is included. Particularly, we can write the second law in standard form, with the only difference that heat currents must be defined with respect to the reservoir. We exemplify our findings with simple but pedagogical examples.
