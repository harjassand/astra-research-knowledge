# Positive-density one-site fields: an acquired commuting normal form

7 October 2026. rounding_spectral, verified GPT-6.1 Sol/max; no nested workers.
A restricted class of genuinely noncommuting physical input perturbations,
not a generic perturbation theorem or a supplied unitary-conjugation promise.
Transport and spectrum independently reconstructed the central algebra.

## Question and exact input

Let H0=sum_a h_a(I-C_a)/2 be the actual BDL2605.10943v1 CSS Hamiltonian,
with 0<h_min<=h_a<=h_max, original check arity and per-qubit check count<=7.
The supplied input is the check incidence/Pauli list, its admitted geometric
realization, and arbitrary Hermitian single-site fields V_i with ||V_i||<=eps.
Choose a set A of sites such that no original check contains two members of A.
Assume every chosen site has both an X-check and a Z-check neighbor. Below we
prove this for ALL qubits of the actual recursive family, including boundary
qubits. Generic fields do not commute with H0; no dressing is supplied.

We construct from this input a unitary U, supported near these sites, for which

    U(H0+sum_(i in A) V_i)U^* = Hd = H0+sum_(i in A) D_i,           (1)
    D_i in Alg{C_a: i in supp C_a},  ||D_i||<=||V_i||.              (2)

Because the incident check lists are disjoint, the legal eraser loses at most
2eps per erased check: kappa=h_min-2eps. Thus eps<=h_min/4 gives kappa>=h_min/2.
This permits order-one relative field strengths on a positive fraction of
sites, in a structured spatial pattern. It does not admit fields on every
site or arbitrary overlapping many-body perturbations.

## Incident algebra is exactly maximal abelian

Fix i. Let S_i be the binary span of its incident stabilizer Paulis and r_i
its independent rank, 2<=r_i<=7. Let P_i be the two-dimensional binary space
spanned by X_i,Z_i. Both-color incidence makes the commutator map
P_i -> S_i^* have rank2: X_i anticommutes a Z check, Z_i anticommutes an X
check, and those two CSS signatures are independent. In particular no
nonidentity one-site Pauli centralizes all checks, and P_i intersect S_i=0.
Set J_i=S_i+P_i, dimension r_i+2.

For a word s+p with s in S_i,p in P_i, commuting with S_i forces p=0.
Consequently the centralizer of Alg(S_i) WITHIN Alg(J_i) is precisely
Alg(S_i). It is a maximal abelian algebra there. This uses both-color
incidence, not merely code distance: distance excludes local logicals but
permits one-site stabilizers/frozen ancillas.

The symplectic form on J_i has matrix [0 B; B^T J2], with rank(B)=2.
Its radical has dimension r_i-2: a radical word first has p=0 by commuting
with S_i, then lies in the kernel of its two site commutators. Hence the
commutator rank is4. Elementary finite Pauli algebra decomposition gives

    Alg(J_i) = direct sum_(at most 2^(r_i-2) center characters) M_4,
               with an unused tensor multiplicity.              (3)

There are at most32 sectors and every quantum matrix block is4 by4. In each
block Alg(S_i) is the ordinary diagonal MASA of dimension4. No dense2^43 by2^43
matrix is required to find the spectral normal form.

## Exact diagonalization and norm control

Let H_i0=sum_(a incident i) h_a D_a and H_i=H_i0+V_i. Both lie in Alg(J_i).
For each center character, order the four eigenvalues of H_i0 and H_i,
counting multiplicities. Match them in that order, selecting arbitrary
orthonormal bases within degeneracies. The minmax principle gives

    |lambda_l(H_i)-lambda_l(H_i0)|<=||V_i||   for every l.          (4)

Choose a block unitary U_i sending the H_i eigenbasis to the correspondingly
ordered H_i0 diagonal basis. The direct sum U_i lies in Alg(J_i). It yields

    U_i H_i U_i^* = H_i0+D_i,
    D_i in Alg(S_i), ||D_i||<=||V_i||.                            (5)

This is a full-spectrum comparison in each actual center sector. A ground
space perturbation estimate would not suffice. Eigenvalue matching does not
require U_i to be close to identity, even when V_i is arbitrarily small and
H_i0 has degeneracies.

Let R_i be the union of whole supports of its incident checks, including i.
Then |R_i|<=1+7(7-1)=43, and R_i is in the physical radius2 ball about i.
Every generator of J_i commutes with every check not incident to i. If i,j
are selected and distinct, no incident check of i meets j or vice versa.
Thus J_i and J_j commute elementwise: stabilizers commute, the two bare-site
algebras have distinct supports, and all mixed pairs commute. U_i,U_j commute
EVEN IF their physical supports R_i,R_j overlap at a third qubit. They also
commute with each other's H_j. Therefore U=product_i U_i gives (1) exactly;
untouched check terms do not change.

## Both-color incidence of the actual boundary templates

This is a direct induction on the primary's actual main construction, not on
an arbitrary Tanner graph or a different explicit-sheaf appendix construction.
The main seed, paper.tex2200–2220 and figs-4/Tanner-base.png, has five qubits in
order NW,NE,C,SW,SE and check matrices

    H_X=[11100;00111], H_Z=[10110;01101].                          (6)

Every column is nonzero in both matrices. The four X/Z overlaps are2; all
parent square corners are distinct.

In RX (paper.tex1435–1665), every new qubit is either a degree1 CY_x edge or
a degree1 CQ_q vertex. Y_x consists of two subdivided parent X-stars and the
cylinder over their opposite-edge boundary. Every abstract edge has distinct
endpoints and is in at least one2-cell: this is true of edges within each
subdivided parent square, boundary edges of either sheet, and the vertical
and horizontal cylinder edges. A cylinder over each nonempty old boundary
edge is a square, so boundary/corner belt edges are not dangling. Its two
endpoint cochain checks give X neighbors; an incident2-cell gives a Z
neighbor. Gluing does not identify the two endpoints of an edge; geometric
flattening identifies positions only, not abstract cells.

Y_q is the doubled subdivided old q-to-Z star, with endpoints joined by the
cylinder intervals. It has at least one old spoke since q has a Z neighbor,
and every vertex of this graph has an incident edge, including both copied
q apices and all joined endpoint vertices. CQ_q vertex qubits therefore have
an incident CQ_q edge Z check. Choose any old X neighbor x of q. The literal
map g_xq^0 is cochain restriction along Y_q into Y_x, so the same vertex has
an X neighbor. The inclusion exists for every old X-Q incidence: choosing a
Z neighbor of q and using even nonzero X/Z overlap supplies the other common
qubit of an actual parent face. The existing0/2 overlap invariant makes that
face regular and fixes its corner correspondence. The connecting maps are
additions to distinct direct-sum check blocks, so they do not cancel an
already nonzero incidence within one chosen block.

There are no other RX qubits. RZ is the color-swapped, degree-reversed
transpose of these actual blocks and restrictions; the two arguments swap
X and Z. Thus every actual qubit is eligible at every level, including the
collapsed purple belt and inherited corner types. Source geometry does not
create or remove abstract incidence when sheets coincide.

## Positive density and original-bath cone

On the original qubits form the conflict graph by joining distinct sites
sharing an original check. Its degree is at most7(7-1)=42. A greedy maximal
independent set A therefore has |A|>=N/43. It can be acquired from the sparse
check list in O(N) fixed-arity incidence work, with O(N log N) label bits.
Choosing A after viewing field coefficients is not needed for the theorem;
one may first acquire A, then supply arbitrary fields on it.

For any single-site raw physical bath operator A_b on q, conjugation by U
requires ONLY U_i whose ORIGINAL R_i contains q. Indeed all other U_i commute
with A_b initially, and all U_i commute with one another, so they cancel from
U A_b U^* before any expansion. There are at most43 such centers, and their
support union, together with an uncovered bare q, has at most43*43=1849 qubits, all within physical radius4 about q. If no factor meets q the support is just q.
For a raw support of size q_b, the resulting support is at most1849 q_b.
The raw bath's operator norm is unchanged. Its number of channels K is also
unchanged; decomposing a pulled-back coupling into Paulis is a proof of
protection, not a construction of4^1849 independent bath channels.

If a physical scheduling of U is desired, each qubit is in at most43 R_i,
so the overlap graph of these supports has degree<=43*42=1806. At most1807
layers of disjoint-support gates implement their exact product. This is a
loose constant-depth upper bound, not an executed hardware construction.
The sharper bath cone above uses commuting cancellation and is not1849^1807.

## Actual Gibbs memory with the original bath

Apply the accepted cycle14 legal-erasure theorem to Hd. An erased check can
change at most one D_i term, by at most2eps, so (2) gives kappa=h_min-2eps.
Cross-CSS dependence of D_i is allowed; no Gibbs factorization is assumed.
At beta kappa>=b0 and Q_k>=2C_low, its dressed-good tail is

    p1,d <=2nu rho^(Q_k)/(1-rho), rho<=1/2.                        (7)

The Pauli energy-shift envelope is

    g_d <=7(h_max+2eps).                                         (8)

For each pulled-back bath support B_b include all its Paulis and prefixes;
set M_B<=1+sum_b4^|B_b|. A syndrome-diagonal prefix intersection protects each
raw pulled-back operator, with Gibbs bad mass at most

    p_safe,d <=M_B exp(beta g_d q') p1,d, q'<=1849q_b.             (9)

It commutes with Hd, and the dressed pair commutes with Hd globally. Hence
all actual spectral components of the original bath, after conjugation,
are protected. For normalized raw couplings and gamma the ACTUAL supremum
of their spectral rates, pinching gives ||L||<=2K gamma. Chesi's full-H
thermal factor theorem, conjugated back by U, gives the original-bath
trace-distance bound

    error(t)<=64K gamma nu t M_B exp(beta g_d q') 2^(-Q_k).        (10)

Thus, if log(K gamma M_B)+beta g_d q'<=(log2)Q_k/2, the stored qubit survives
for time of order2^(Q_k/2), divided by the explicit64nu prefactor and desired
error tolerance. The NEW Gibbs auxiliary state of Hd is used for encoding,
and Enc_H=Ad_(U^*) Enc_d, Dec_H=Dec_d Ad_U. Original physical bath couplings
are retained. No old-state initialization or guessed jump rates are used.
Independent-channel Lamb shifts can be admitted with the extra same-channel
crossed-product protection proved by the dynamics workers; (10) itself uses
no optional Lamb-shift term. Arbitrary cross-channel coherent shifts need a
separate hypothesis and are not silently covered.

For h_a=1 and eps<=1/4, kappa>=1/2, g_d<=10.5, so beta>=2b0 suffices. The
admitted symbolic calibration gives b0<=126625 and k>=228 before the bath
prefix condition; q'=1849 adds another finite large-k threshold. Nothing
implies that this code size or temperature is practical. Q_k scales at least
(N/5)^(1/436)/2, so the asymptotic thermal exponent is retained. The distance
exponent does not replace this smaller decoder exponent.

## Acquisition, nearest controls and limits

An incident Pauli algebra has dimension2^(r+2)<=512. Binary symplectic
elimination on at most43 qubits reduces it to two quantum qubits and at most
five central bits. For rational h_a,V_i, all the finite local spectral data
come from at most32 Hermitian4x4 matrices; their eigenvalues are real
algebraic roots of quartics, and eigenvector/unitary coefficients may be
algebraic. Ordering is exact via root isolation; degeneracies may be kept
symbolically. Inverse center-Fourier and two-qubit Pauli transforms express
U_i,D_i using at most512 Pauli words on R_i. Exact algebraic acquisition is
finite and polynomial in the input bit length for fixed block size; this is
not a claim of rational eigenvectors or a tolerance-independent floating
implementation. The exact finite acquisition in work/rounding_spectral/cycle15/acquire_actual_seed_fields.py
uses the ACTUAL five-qubit seed, selected NW and SE, and two-component rational
X/Z fields at both. It acquires their four eigenvalues and rational unscaled
eigenvectors, records positive-square-root normalizations, and certifies the
actual Pauli embeddings and cross-algebra commutation despite a three-qubit
support overlap. All60 exact assertions pass. The fixed fixture uses no Y
coefficient, has one M4 block per field, and is not a thermal trajectory, a
generic-field execution, or universal evidence for the theorem.

The unitary normal form, full Gibbs encoding, enormous level2 decoder lookup,
bit-acquired global geometry, and a hardware realization of its43-body gates
are not supplied free. Exact thermal preparation remains unexecuted. An
approximate local unitary can leave an extensive noncommuting remainder;
no existing exact theorem here absorbs arbitrary such remainders for
exponential storage time. Preparing/simulating a dense N-site family is
not inferred from the small algebra dimension. Actual rate gamma must be
charged over the new Hamiltonian's frequencies, and can vary with input.

Earlier cycle14 results admitted a SUPPLIED finite-depth exact unitary orbit
of a check-diagonal Hamiltonian. The present restricted input class acquires
that orbit from arbitrary one-site field data, with an incident-check norm
bound, so it is a substantive larger physical interface. It is also a
standard finite-dimensional Pauli-algebra/Weyl mechanism; novelty UNKNOWN,
not a new general quantum stability theorem. Generic overlapping dense
perturbations remain open.

A control for the both-color hypothesis is S={Z1Z2,Z2}. Site1 has local
centralizer Z1 not in its incident-check span. A field vZ1 depends on the
additional center Z2. For H_i0=(I-Z1Z2)/2 and 0<v<1/2, its eigenvalue
pairs at Z2=+1 and -1 are respectively {v,1-v} and {-v,1+v}. A function
of Z1Z2 alone would give the same pair in both central sectors. Therefore
the incident-only normal form does not exist in this example. Global distance>1 does not remove
this issue if there are frozen ancillas. The next falsifier is an actual
boundary qubit lacking one check color, a field placement with a shared
original check, a claimed Ui outside its incident crossed algebra, or an
unpaid bath/preparation/precision cost. The broader correctable-island
construction of transport allows local centralizers with a separate filling
and congestion cost; it is not substituted silently for the singleton claim.
