MAIN CLAIM
Take even perfect-square N and positive rational kappa_N with
 kappa_N -> infinity, kappa_N=o(N).
Set b=0 and A_N=-(kappa_N+2)sqrt(N) I. Then
 rho_N = exp[-(kappa_N/N)J^2]/Tr exp[-(kappa_N/N)J^2]
and
 inf_(sigma fully separable) T(rho_N,sigma) -> 1.       (G1)
Thus near-maximal obstruction already occurs with anisotropy bound
M_N/sqrt(N) growing arbitrarily slowly, along this scalar negative direction.
For example kappa_N=ceil(log2 N) gives M_N=O(sqrt(N)log N), much smaller
than L04's original order N^(3/2) example. This does not contradict any
fixed-M or the audited sufficient M=o(N^(1/6)) exponential-cone theorem.
It does not locate an optimal full-family transition boundary.

1. ALL-SEPARABLE CENTRAL-BAND WITNESS

The full proof of S01's repair is reviews/L04_WITNESS_REPAIR.txt. It gives,
for each atom of the randomized-axis measurement of J_n, averaged over
n uniform on S^2, probability <=3/sqrt N for EVERY fully separable state.
By summing atoms, for L>=0 and N even,
 Pr_sigma(|J_n|<=L)<=min(1,3(2floor(L)+1)/sqrt N).        (G2)
This remains a single valid measurement event, with the random axis part
of the common measurement. Nonidentical product states and their mixtures
are included. Every spin-j sector with j<=L has event probability one.

2. UNIFORM LOW-SPIN CONCENTRATION UNDER THE ANTIFERROMAGNET

Write n=N/2 and multiplicity d_N(j). Relative to the j=0 sector,
 d_N(j)/d_N(0)=(2j+1) R_N(j),
 R_N(j)=product_(l=1)^j (n-l+1)/(n+l+1).
Consequently the relative sector weight is exactly
 W_j=(2j+1)^2 R_N(j) exp[-kappa j(j+1)/N],
where W_0=1 and 0<=R_N(j)<=1.

For j<=n/4 every x_l=2l/(n+l+1)<=1/2. The elementary inequality
log(1-x)>=-2x on [0,1/2] yields
 log R_N(j)>=-(4/n)sum_(l=1)^j l=-4j(j+1)/N.           (G3)
This is an exact multiplicity lower bound, not a Stirling asymptotic.

Assume kappa>=4 and N>=16(kappa+4), plus N sufficiently large that the
next J lies below n/4 (automatically true for this regime, N>=8). Set
 J=floor(sqrt[N/(4(kappa+4))]).
The unfloored value is >=2, so J>=sqrt[N/(kappa+4)]/4. Also
(kappa+4)J(J+1)/N<=1/2. From (G3),
 Zrel=sum W_j >=e^-1/2 sum_(j=0)^J (2j+1)^2
              >=c_Z [N/(kappa+4)]^(3/2),
 c_Z=e^-1/2/64.                                       (G4)

Let a=kappa/N<=1/16 and L>=2/sqrt a. Put u=sqrt a L>=2. For j>L,
(2j+1)^2<=9j^2 and j^2 exp(-a j^2) is decreasing there. Thus
 sum_(j>L) W_j <=9[L^2 exp(-a L^2)
                      +integral_L^infinity r^2 exp(-a r^2)dr]
 =9a^-3/2[sqrt a u^2 e^-u^2+integral_u^infinity z^2 e^-z^2 dz].
Integration by parts and the Gaussian tail bound give
 integral_u^infinity z^2e^-z^2 dz <=(u/2+1/(4u))e^-u^2.
Dividing by (G4), using (kappa+4)/kappa<=2 for kappa>=4, proves the
explicit uniform estimate

 Pr_rho(j>L)<=C0 (u^2+u+1)e^-u^2,                     (G5)
 C0=9*2^(3/2)/c_Z.

For L=floor[sqrt N kappa^-1/4] and kappa sufficiently large, u differs
from kappa^(1/4) by at most sqrt a<=1/4. In particular
 u>=kappa^(1/4)/2>=2 and u^2<=kappa^1/2.
Equation (G5) is therefore at most
 C1(1+sqrt kappa)exp(-sqrt kappa/4),
with a fixed explicit absolute C1, e.g. 3C0. This tends to zero for every
kappa_N->infinity with kappa_N=o(N), without requiring kappa_N>>log N.
The nontrivial partition-function lower bound (G4) is essential for that
arbitrarily-slow-growth conclusion; the crude denominator W_0=1 is inadequate.

3. TRACE-DISTANCE LOWER BOUND AND CONSEQUENCES

Since every j<=L sector has |J_n|<=L surely, (G5) and (G2) imply, uniformly
over ALL fully separable outputs,

 T(rho_N,sigma)
 >=1-C1(1+sqrt kappa)exp(-sqrt kappa/4)
       -6kappa^-1/4-3N^-1/2.                           (G6)

Taking the infimum and the stated sequence gives (G1). The measurement is
local once its common random axis is supplied. There is no claim of an
efficient entangled-state preparation obstruction or lower bound on unknown
parameter acquisition, energy, gate complexity, or runtime.

The same certificate also gives constant separation already at M_N=Theta(sqrt N)
for a sufficiently large FIXED kappa: (G6) has a constant positive right side
once kappa is chosen large enough. For instance kappa=10^8 has separable
central-band term .06, exponentially negligible tail, and N eventually
large enough for (G4), so the lower bound exceeds .9. This is a negative
scalar family outside the positive heat-seed domain, not growing traceless
anisotropy alone. It demonstrates that unrestricted M=Theta(sqrt N) is
already physically incompatible with a vanishing full-separable-cone bound.
