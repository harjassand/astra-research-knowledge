## 1. Recent sources and exact interfaces

**DreamProver**, Zhang et al., arXiv:2604.26311v1, submitted 2026-04-29. Read main text, Appendix A/B/C cost and length reports; sampled lemma appendix D. Interface: pretrained proprietary LLM + Lean/mathlib or supplied specialized domain axioms + up to 100 domain training tasks; solve fresh test tasks after five wake/sleep cycles. Wake discovers verified intermediate statements via recursive proof decomposition. Sleep clusters natural-language theorem descriptions, proposes abstract lemmas, prunes with first-order tree similarity and usage heuristics, then proves retained candidates. Learned library has fewer than 100 lemmas and is included in prompts.

Authors report improved held-out success, direct use of 58% of learned lemmas, coverage of 71% of successful test proofs, and inference token/length improvements. §4.3 Table 3 is unusually informative: removing clustering drops inequality successes to 53, below no-library 55; complete system 104. This is empirical evidence that indiscriminate retention can harm search, and that the particular pruning/abstraction pipeline matters. No universal acquisition, abstraction, or search-improvement theorem is proved. Appendix B Table 7 separately reports training output tokens; no statement in this audit treats acquisition as free. Cost units are ambiguous between table captions ('thousands') and figure descriptions ('millions'); raw source retained. The plane-geometry prose says library learning uses the IMO subset of LeanGeo-Bench while reporting Olympiad evaluation. The exact split identity and overlap need a dataset-level audit before adopting that result as clean held-out transfer. This audit did not resolve it.

Nearest comparator supplied by authors: no-library pipeline and single-pass evolved library plus retrieval ('LEGO-Prover'), plus Hilbert recursive prover. Remaining matched-control question: same training tasks, verified artifacts, total token/checker cost, and retention opportunity, with symbolic anti-unification/subsumption selection instead of semantic clustering. Merely beating fixed-library prompting does not isolate a special abstraction mechanism.

**Self-Supervised Theorem Discovery in a Formal Axiomatic System**, Ota/Osa/Harada, arXiv:2606.28747v1, submitted 2026-06-27. Full main text and appendices read. Interface: classical propositional Hilbert system with Ax1/Ax2/Ax3 and MP; schemas instantiated by fresh first-order unification; fixed seven-action rollout horizon. No pretrained model or human task corpus in discovery. Transformer learns goal-conditioned action policy from hindsight proof prefixes, then starts a new network after adding macro theorem actions. Generality filtering retains minimal substitution schemas; low posterior reprovability selects macros. Six generations add 20,10,5,2,1,0 macros, total 38. Each generation has 25*8192*7 = 1,433,600 simulator steps; six generations 8,601,600, plus training/filtering cost.

Authors show 30% coverage of 30 human propositional tasks and improvements to one-shot GPT proof generation when macros supplied. This is concrete external transfer in a narrow calculus. Soundness comes from recorded primitive derivations and schema instantiation; no acquisition completeness or universal useful-theorem guarantee follows. Grounded novelty is not discovery of new logical theorems: these are classical tautologies; the novelty is the learning/search construction and usefulness test. Generality order is technically a preorder before quotienting variable-renaming equivalence. More general schemas replace instances without increasing minimum macro-call proof length under explicit fresh instantiation; a learned action policy need not find the replacement efficiently.

**Discovering New Theorems via LLMs with In-Context Proof Learning in Lean**, Kasaura et al., arXiv:2509.14274v4. Primary history: first 2025-09-16; recent v2 2026-05-06, v3 2026-06-24, v4 2026-06-29. Main text read, appendix focused-proof excerpt read. Interface: initial definitions of semi-open/alpha-open/preopen sets, whole mathlib imported, o3 conjecturer/prover with formal feedback and retained verified proofs. SPL baseline also retains outputs; CPL separates conjecturing from up to 16 proof retries.

§4's theorem is a stationary iid sampling model, explicitly ignoring context. The conclusion is a retry-induced distribution shift, not a guarantee of learning new strategies. Authors compare 20 runs at 14 million tokens per run: alpha-open intersection rediscovered in 5/20 CPL versus 0/20 simultaneous loops; CPL generates fewer total theorems. Reproving the target with successful prior contexts yields 7/80 versus 0/80 without context. The authors correctly identify the target as an older Njastad theorem; no new mathematical discovery is established. `exact?` failure is called novelty but only establishes non-detection by that bounded tactic, not semantic independence from mathlib or mathematical novelty. Author inference from frequent model 'false' responses to absence from pretraining is not warranted; a finite response sample cannot establish training-corpus absence.

**Rzk: a Proof Assistant for Synthetic Infinity-Categories**, Kudasov/Sim/Ahrens, arXiv:2607.12207v1, submitted 2026-07-13. Deep excerpts §§2-5 and Appendix E; later algorithmic implementation scanned. Precise open question: full dRzk conservativity over Riehl-Shulman simplicial type theory (RSTT), Conjecture 5.9. Theorem 5.8 only covers derivations with ext-style hypotheses and tail concluded types (Definition 5.7). Ext-style restrictions occur directly as shape-Pi codomains with boundary contained in the shape; free-standing restrictions may be concluded but not bound as variables. Published erasure back-translation needs an equation x=a on boundary phi; an arbitrary variable x:A[phi↦a] has no predecessor derivation from which to reconstruct that equation. Abstract omits this restriction; the full text explicitly states it. See `leads/r11_logic.md` for the reconstructed local construction and its two unresolved interface gates.

## 2. Older prerequisites

**LLM Library Learning Fails: A LEGO-Prover Case Study**, Berlot-Attwell/Rudzicz/Si, arXiv:2504.03048v1, submitted 2025-04-03. Read substantive main-text §§1-7, methods excerpts. Authors find negligible direct/soft reuse and no gain when total cost is matched to simple prompting. Useful as an adversarial comparator requirement; its negative outcome is specific to tested LEGO-Prover/models, not a universal impossibility theorem for libraries. DreamProver explicitly targets this objection and has stronger direct-use evidence, so it should not be dismissed by inheriting the earlier negative result.

**A type theory for synthetic infinity-categories**, Riehl/Shulman, arXiv:1705.07442, latest archived version/date in metadata. Read pp7-11, §§2.1-2.2; visually checked Fig.4 p11. Fig.2 gives unit-cube uniqueness s≡star. Fig.4 gives extension beta, eta, boundary computation. These suffice for the local singleton proof when its contextual boundary is legal. Literal original shape definition (2.1) is a cube plus a tope in the corresponding singleton cube context. Original Fig.4 presents domain/boundary shapes globally, separate from ambient Xi. Therefore translating a singleton extension with boundary phi(t), where t is already ambient, needs a contextual-shape admissibility lemma or an explicit target-system generalization. Rzk accepting it does not prove this RSTT side condition.

## 3. Exact sampling reconstruction and cost obstruction

Let a conjecture T be drawn iid with mass s(T), and independent proof trials succeed with probability r(T). Cap retries at N. Set h_N(r)=sum_{i=0}^{N-1}(1-r)^i; q_N(r)=r*h_N(r). Expected cycle trials is H=E_s h_N(r). Renewal-reward theorem gives exact asymptotic target successes per proof trial lambda_CPL(T0)=s0*r0*h_N(r0)/H, versus lambda_SL=s0*r0. The target benefits iff h_N(r0)>H. This is an asymptotic rate, not an exact finite-budget first-hit probability. Total discovery rate weakly falls: E q_N / E h_N <= E r, because h_N is decreasing and covariance(r,h_N)<=0. If conjecture generation costs c and each proof costs p, replace denominators by c+pH and c+p; the benefit condition becomes (c+p)h_N(r0)>c+pH. Correlated feedback/context invalidate the iid reduction.

An exact counterexample to 'verified compression implies faster proving': judgments are words generated freely by append-a/append-b, with empty word axiom. Add the sound acquired macro b^2. Training target b^2 shortens from 2 actions to 1. Future target a^d has unchanged shortest length d and no proof containing the macro. Enumerate valid proof scripts by length with a last lexicographically. Scripts inspected through the target increase from sum_{i=0}^d2^i to sum_{i=0}^d3^i (at d8, 511 to 9841). This models naive script enumeration, not an optimal/deduplicating prover. A competent equally retaining comparator can store the macro and ignore it. Thus the implication from compression to acceleration is false without a search interface and task distribution; no architectural separation is established.

Two future distributions concentrated respectively on b^2 and a^d have the identical acquisition history yet opposite utility signs. No history-only utility certificate can certify a strict improvement for both. A fallback dovetail runs base and enriched search in alternating charged steps; with pausable unit-step search and negligible scheduling cost, its per-goal time is at most 2*min(C_base,C_enriched)+O(1). Acquisition K is still charged: total K+sum search costs. All these results are elementary reconstructions, not asserted novel.

## 4. Downstream decisions

Best foundational lead is the Rzk conservativity gap, with a checked local construction and named remaining conversion/shape-interface obligations. Lemma acquisition is practically credible but lacks a new theoretical separation: pursue only with fully charged acquisition, retained matched controls, causal removal of actually used lemmas, and clean held-out task splits. Do not treat proof shortness, kernel acceptance, or model agreement as independent validation of research value or semantic novelty.
