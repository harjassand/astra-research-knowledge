# Exact locality obstruction for the balanced transport projection

## Scope and conclusion

This is a targeted feasibility test of Sections 3–5 and the open theorem G1 in `research_note(20261012-124559).md`. It does not re-audit that source's infinite-volume transport proof.

**Result:** The exact balance projection is not exponentially local in physical support, even for the first effective-operator action on the uniform current in the nearest-neighbor dephased XXZ chain. The obstruction is an exact nonzero discrete harmonic moment on the three-particle exclusion gap graph. Positive dephasing does not remove it: the projection is independent of the dephasing strength.

This rules out importing an ordinary local-generator Lieb–Robinson bound through the projection without further work. It does **not** rule out economical nonlocal/multipole representations, certified adaptive Poisson solvers, or a different high-accuracy transport algorithm. No such end-to-end algorithm, accuracy rate, or computational improvement is established here. The source's coarse transport sandwich is unchanged.

## 1. Exact covariance-space graph

Use the source's conventions

- H_hop = −(t/2) Σ_i (X_i X_(i+1)+Y_i Y_(i+1));
- A = −i[H, ·];
- j_i = (t/2)(X_i Y_(i+1)−Y_i X_(i+1));
- P = projection onto diagonal operators;
- d = 4γ > 0.

For translation sums of finite Pauli words, use the per-volume product-trace inner product. The diagonal degree-three vectors

V_(a,b) = Σ_x Z_x Z_(x+a) Z_(x+a+b),   a,b ≥ 1,

are orthonormal in this inner product. The one-hop words Σ_i j_i Z_S, where S is a fixed two-site spectator pattern disjoint from the bond, are mutually orthogonal and have squared norm t²/2. Dividing them by |t|/√2 gives the unweighted edge metric.

Each allowed nearest-neighbor swap in a degree-three monomial is an edge of the graph with vertices (a,b) ∈ N² and allowed moves

(+1,0), (0,+1),
(−1,0), (−1,+1) when a > 1,
(0,−1), (+1,−1) when b > 1.

Let L be the positive unweighted graph Laplacian, L u(v)=Σ_(w~v)[u(v)−u(w)]. On this sector,

−P A² P = 2 t² L,

because the diagonal Hamiltonian commutes with every diagonal input. Consequently, the source's pressure operator is M=(2t²/d)L.

Write C=P A restricted to the one-hop odd space. Then C* = −A P and C C*=2t²L. The orthogonal balance projection is

Π_K = I − C* (C C*)† C,   K=ker C.

All one-hop strings have the same dephasing weight d. Other odd strings are already invisible to C. Thus both the ordinary and source-weighted balance projections have the same pressure correction; d cancels. Diagonal interactions also do not enter C.

## 2. A model-allowed local source

Take the range-four translation-invariant odd observable

F = Σ_i j_i Z_(i+2) Z_(i+3).

Direct Pauli algebra gives

P A F = t² [V_(2,1) − V_(1,1)].

Set b=δ_(2,1)−δ_(1,1), and let u solve L u=b in the finite-energy sense, modulo a constant. Then

Π_K F = F + (1/2) A u.

The gradient exists in the edge Hilbert space because b is the divergence of the single compact edge flux corresponding to F. This statement needs no inverse-gap assumption.

## 3. Exact harmonic-moment obstruction

Define

h(a,b)=(a−b)(2a+b−2)(a+2b−2).

It obeys L h=0 everywhere on the infinite graph, including the reflecting edges and corner. One direct verification is:

1. The sum of its six bulk finite differences is zero.
2. The missing-jump identity h(0,b)+h(0,b+1)−2h(1,b)=0 holds identically.
3. The analogous identity holds on b=1.
4. At (1,1), h(2,1)+h(1,2)−2h(1,1)=0.

However,

Σ_v b(v) h(v)=h(2,1)−h(1,1)=6.

Suppose the pressure gradient, or equivalently the exterior tail of Π_K F, decayed exponentially in the gap radius a+b in edge ℓ² norm. It would then decay exponentially pointwise. The exterior of a large gap ball is connected, with paths of length O(radius) connecting each bounded-size annulus. Summing gradient differences shows that u approaches a common constant at infinity; after subtracting it, u itself decays exponentially, with any slightly weakened exponential rate.

Since h has polynomial growth and L has bounded degree, all sums below would then be absolutely convergent. Discrete summation by parts would imply

6 = Σ_v h(v) L u(v) = Σ_v u(v) L h(v) = 0,

a contradiction.

Therefore **no exponential physical-support tail bound is possible for this projection**. The conversion between gap radius and the diameter of the corresponding Pauli word changes only a bounded offset. The statement also excludes an exponentially accurate compact-support approximation: outside that support every approximation has exactly the same uncancelled projected tail.

The argument does not assert a sharp algebraic exponent or a quantitative tail upper bound. Neither is needed for the closed obstruction.

## 4. The obstruction occurs in the physical current corrector

It is not necessary to choose an artificial trial flux. Let

H_diag = U Σ_i Z_i Z_(i+1),   U ≠ 0,

and split A=A_hop+A_diag. With J=Σ_i j_i and

F_mid = Σ_i Z_(i−1) j_i Z_(i+2),

exact identities are

A_hop J = 0,
A_diag² J = −8 U² (J − F_mid),
P A F_mid = t² [V_(1,2) − V_(2,1)].

For example, A_diag j_i = U t (Z_(i−1)−Z_(i+2))(X_iX_(i+1)+Y_iY_(i+1)), and the double commutator follows directly.

The cross term A_hop A_diag J has no nearest-neighbor one-hop component. The same-bond hopping commutator is zero; overlapping neighboring hopping bonds produce distance-two coherences; hopping beside a spectator produces four-flip coherences. Therefore

P A (A_hop A_diag J)=0.

The raw positive effective-operator action on J includes −A²J, with only dephasing-number weights on the other flip sectors. Its unbalanced nearest-neighbor part is consequently 8U²(J−F_mid). The pressure source is proportional to δ_(1,2)−δ_(2,1), whose harmonic moment is

h(1,2)−h(2,1)=−12.

Thus the first projected effective action on the actual uniform XXZ current already requires a non-exponentially-local pressure correction. The factors W_o^(−1/2) and W^+ from Section 5 leave its one-hop part unchanged and do not turn the other flip sectors into a pressure source.

This is an obstruction to exponential locality of the projected iteration. It is not a lower bound on the computational complexity of D, a proof that the resolvent corrector has the identical tail, or a lower bound on every possible transport certificate.

## 5. What remains constructively possible

The projection problem itself has a finite-local variational certificate. If D is a fixed orientation of the graph gradient and b=D*F, then for any compactly supported potential v and compact flux w satisfying D*w=b,

2⟨b,v⟩−||Dv||² ≤ ||D L†b||² ≤ ||w||².

Its computable gap is

||w||²−2⟨b,v⟩+||Dv||² = ||w−Dv||².

In particular F−w is exactly balanced and differs from Π_K F by at most the square root of that gap in edge norm. Every fixed pair is finitely checkable. This does not supply a quantitative convergence rate, a guarantee of economical gap closure, or a high-accuracy transport algorithm as the corrector's degree and representation grow. It is included to prevent the locality obstruction from being misread as an impossibility of certified finite computation.
