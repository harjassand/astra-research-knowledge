C03_L02 REVISION R02 — repair of source-subclass boundary and charge-projector prior art
7 October 2026 UTC. INITIAL and r01 remain unchanged.

CORRECTION / SCOPE REPAIR
The bipartite-easy calculation in INITIAL §4 and its restatement in r01 is valid only for the role-separated source route: up-idler support A and down-signal support B must remain disjoint in site labels, so that F has support only A×B. If the signal-only passive unitary can mix down modes into site labels occupied by up idlers, the final matrix can have I∩J overlap and the hard projector becomes active. In that case the coefficient is no longer e_k(MM*), and Knill's FLO simulator does not apply to the post-projection distribution. I do not claim that all pair-source-plus-passive-unitary routes are easy.

Algebraically, a diagonal independent-pair source Λ followed by separate orbital transformations U on the up modes and V on the down modes has pair matrix F=UΛV^T. A generic such F can have overlapping row/column site labels. Conversely the SVD expresses any complex F in this form; exact physical gate synthesis from a rational F still needs a bit-complexity argument, so this is an algebraic closure statement, not a finite-gate compiler. The formula C_k=e_k(MM*) remains exact when hard projection is redundant (the role-separated support condition), or for the unprojected source norm. It is not an obstruction once the hard projector is active.

This correction narrows the INITIAL claim: the explicit direct bipartite replacement is easy, but a more general source-mode mixer can leave that subclass. The literal fermionic compressor is still exactly zero for R>1, and no general static circuit map has been constructed. The broad no-PP-transfer conclusion should be read as “not established by the direct/bunching route,” not as a no-go for any F.

PROJECTOR IDENTIFICATION AND PRIOR ART
At a site, with commuting occupation projectors n_u,n_d,

  P_hard = I - n_u n_d = P_0 + P_1,

where P_q projects onto total charge q in the two modes. Thus the hard no-double filter is exactly the 0-or-1 charge outcome (“not doubly occupied”), not the even/odd two-mode parity projector P_0+P_2 versus P_1.

DiVincenzo–Terhal, “Fermionic Linear Optics Revisited,” arXiv:quant-ph/0403031, §2 and Corollary 1, explicitly discuss the grouped two-mode measurement P_{0,1}=P_0+P_1 versus P_2. They state that this grouped measurement can function in a nondeterministic implementation of quantum computation; their no-go theorem rules out an exact zero-failure implementation with these charge measurements and FLO. This makes the local hard projector a known non-Gaussian resource in an interleaved measurement model. It also prevents the opposite overclaim that FLO simulability rules out computation once P_hard is available. See https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0403031v1, especially lines 145–163 and 223–245 in the current HTML rendering.

Beenakker–DiVincenzo–Emary–Kindermann, “Charge detection enables free-electron quantum computation,” arXiv:quant-ph/0401066, §§1–3, gives a nondeterministic/near-deterministic CNOT construction from one-body beam splitters, spin rotations and charge detection, and a parity-meter variant. It says the charge-one projector is a sum of two occupation products and that summing over many such accepted patterns can make classical evaluation exponential. Its exact resources include repeated nondestructive measurements, classical outcome use and feedforward. That sequential interface is not the target's single global pair state plus one terminal product of P_hard projectors. See https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0401066v1, especially lines 27–48 and 66–81.

The exact four-mode witness in r01 is still decisive against the naive attempt to turn an interleaved sequence into a final layer by commuting P through G. It does not contradict the nondeterministic universality literature, which uses measurements interleaved with later gates. The genuinely relevant unresolved question is whether the entire interleaved charge-measurement computation can be recompiled into one explicit rational opposite-spin pair matrix and the final P_hard layer, with a known positive branch lower bound and a legal final readout. A space-time/teleportation encoding is a possible direction; no such construction is supplied here. The physical Pauli-zero R-buncher obstruction remains independent of this static-completeness gate.
