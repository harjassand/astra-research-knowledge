INDEPENDENT SCORE AUDIT — EVERY OUTPUT SECTOR
2026-10-08

Scope: quantum_tail_precision.txt §§3–5, checked against quantum_branch.txt
§§2–5. I rederive the pair score and near/far sector estimates, including
Kraus normalization and arbitrary sector transport. The Schur band-mass
proof and tail upper code are outside this audit.

Verdict

No gap found. Under the stated large-n critical regime, 1/r<=Q<=n/128,
and j in [16Q,32Q], every output sector k and every nonzero rank-at-most-Q
Kraus component have score deficit

  Delta_(jk) >= 1/(786432 r Q^3).

The pairwise estimate uses neither an output-sector marginal condition nor a
sector-preserving decoder. The conversion to the 1/384 coefficient in tail
(5.1) is exact, conditional on the band-mass lower bound (not checked here).

1. Pair deficit and signs

For a normalized vectorized Kraus operator A:V_j->V_k, its Schmidt rank is
rank(A)<=Q. Let H=-sum_i J_i^(j) tensor J_i^(k), m=min(j,k), M=max(j,k),
and let g=jk+m-<H>_A>=0. The Choi/time-reversal convention in
quantum_branch.txt (5.4) gives the Kraus score

  w [a_j a_k <H>_A/(jk) - a_k^2/2].

The target spin-j score is T_j=a_j^2/2+a_j^2/j. Subtracting and using
m/(jk)=1/M yields

  Delta_(jk) >= (a_j-a_k)^2/2
      + a_j^2/j - a_j a_k/M + a_j a_k g/(jk).             (A)

The middle term is a_j(a_j-a_k)/j>=0 for k<=j, because a is increasing;
it is a_j(a_j/j-a_k/k)>=0 for k>=j, because a(u)/u is decreasing.
For k=0 the output score is zero and Delta_(j0)=T_j>=a_j^2/2. For j=0,
the target score is zero and the actual output score is nonpositive. Thus
every pair has nonnegative deficit before selecting the rare input band.

2. Near output

Write m=min(j,k), M=max(j,k), and assume |j-k|<=M/(16Q). Since
j>=16Q, M>=16Q and the projector/energy lemma (quantum_branch.txt (4.6))
applies: g>=M/(32Q). Also m>=15M/16 and rj>=16, rk>=15, so the exact
thermal bounds imply a_j,a_k>=1/3. The last term in (A) gives

  Delta_(jk) >= (1/9) M/(32Qjk)
             = 1/(288Qm)
             >= 1/(288Qj)
             >= 1/(9216Q^2)
             >= 1/(9216rQ^3).

Here j<=32Q and rQ>=1. This is stronger than the common 1/(786432rQ^3)
constant. No assumption about k being an allowed input-sector index is
needed beyond it being a valid output spin.

3. Far lower output

Let s=1/(16Q), k<j(1-s), and k0=j(1-s). For k>0, k<k0; throughout
[k0,j], u>=k0>=15j/16>=15/r>=2/r. Therefore the exact derivative bound
a'(u)>=1/[4r(u+1)^2] applies. As (j+1)^2<=4j^2,

  a_j-a_k >= a(j)-a(k0)
      >= (j/(16Q))/(4r(j+1)^2)
      >= 1/(256rjQ).

Since a_j>=1/3, the middle term in (A) yields

  Delta_(jk) >= a_j(a_j-a_k)/j
             >= 1/(768rj^2Q)
             >= 1/(786432rQ^3),

using j<=32Q. If k=0, T_j>=1/18. Since r<=1/2 and Q>=1/r,
rQ^3>=1/r^2>=4, so the claimed common bound is at most
1/(4*786432)<1/18.

4. Far higher output

Let k>k0=j/(1-s). Since rj>=16, the exact bound
(a(u)/u)'<=-1/(2u^2) holds on [j,k0]. Monotonicity extends the comparison
to every k>k0, however large:

  a_j/j-a_k/k >= a(j)/j-a(k0)/k0
      >= integral_j^k0 du/(2u^2)
      = 1/(32jQ).

The middle term in (A) is at least

  a_j(a_j/j-a_k/k) >= 1/(96jQ)
      >= 1/(3072Q^2) >= 1/(3072rQ^3).

Again j<=32Q and rQ>=1. The near region includes equality at both
boundaries; the two far regions are exactly its complement for k<=j and
k>=j, respectively.

5. Kraus weights and sector-changing decoder

After encoding into a register of dimension at most Q and any
label-dependent decoding, each composite Kraus operator has rank at most Q.
Pinching in output spin sectors left-multiplies these operators by
projections and preserves rank. Restricting inputs to a spin-j block
right-multiplies by a projection and also preserves rank. Thus arbitrary
sector-changing decoders fit the maps A_(kjz):V_j->V_k.

Trace preservation on input block j gives

  sum_(k,z) A_(kjz)^* A_(kjz)=I_j,
  w_(jkz)=||A_(kjz)||_HS^2/(2j+1),
  sum_(k,z)w_(jkz)=1.

These are conditional prior-averaged transition weights because the
direction-averaged input in V_j is I_j/(2j+1). The deficit must be applied
to each normalized nonzero Kraus operator and then weighted by w; summing
unweighted component deficits would be invalid. Since every pairwise
deficit is nonnegative, outputs from other sectors cannot cancel the
selected band's contribution. The score is block diagonal, so discarded
cross-sector output coherences do not affect it.

The global score interval [-3/2,1/2] has width 2. Shifting and scaling it
to an effect gives integrated score gap <=2e for half trace error e. Thus,
conditional on the tail file's band mass P_band,

  2e >= P_band/(786432rQ^3).

Substitution of its displayed lower bound (4.3) gives

  e >= exp(-2c1^2/3)/(384 r n^(3/2))
       * exp(-(8192/3)Q^2/n),

and r sqrt(n)<=c1 gives the stated c_T=exp(-2c1^2/3)/(384c1). The
factor 1/384 is correct.
