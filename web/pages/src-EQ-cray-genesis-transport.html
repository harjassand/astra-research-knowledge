A constructive result: finite certificates for infinite-system transport
I developed a translation-orbit current compiler: a finite algebraic computation that produces exact, two-sided certificates for an infinite-lattice diffusion coefficient. I implemented it, generated rational witnesses, and checked them with a separate exact-arithmetic verifier that does not trust the numerical optimiser.
The investigation also produced a density-uniform analytical enclosure and a structural limitation: for an important cooperative model, increasing the size of this exact local-current search can never produce a positive lower bound. That failure identifies what a stronger computational operation must change.
The variational duality itself is not new. Shapira’s June 2026 paper already establishes a Thomson-type principle for diffusion and explicitly proposes rigorous numerical lower bounds. The contribution developed here is the constructive compiler and its consequences—not a relabelling of that principle. Its publication priority remains unverified. arXiv

1. Selecting the missing computation
I screened three representations before selecting the transport problem.
Direct recovery of scientific functionals from inverse data. The attractive possibility is to recover the desired quantity without reconstructing an entire unknown field. The unresolved issue in this screen was identifying a genuine information gain, rather than merely computing fewer coordinates of an already identifiable object.
Observable-only quantum dynamics. Avoiding the full wavefunction is also not, by itself, a new operation. Influence-matrix methods already pursue this, including constructions designed to avoid intermediate temporal-entanglement barriers. A consequential advance would need to control the complexity of the representation actually constructed; I did not establish a new mechanism doing that here. arXiv
Direct transport certification. Here the scientific output is a diffusion coefficient, rather than a trajectory or a many-body state. Kinetically constrained lattice gases are useful targets because their equilibrium measure can be simple while their dynamics remain nontrivial: certain particle exchanges are forbidden by the surrounding configuration. arXiv
The relevant specialised baseline is already strong. Arita, Krapivsky and Mallick developed finite-dimensional variational calculations giving rigorous upper bounds on diffusion coefficients. Consequently, “replace simulation by a local variational calculation” would not constitute a discovery. arXiv
The constructive question I pursued was narrower and more decisive:
Can a finite algorithm automatically construct a current that certifies a lower bound against every possible infinite-system correction—not merely against the finite collection used during optimisation?

That distinction is essential. A numerically well-optimised current is not a lower-bound certificate unless its conservation condition holds exactly.
2. The operation: compile conservation through translation classes
Mathematical setting
Consider configurations
\[
\eta\in\{0,1\}^{\mathbb Z^d}
\]
with known product Bernoulli equilibrium of particle density \(p\), and write
\[
\chi=p(1-p).
\]
Particles exchange across nearest-neighbour bonds. The rate \(c_i(\eta)\) for the bond \((0,e_i)\) is local, nonnegative and invariant under exchanging its endpoint occupations. Rates are allowed to vanish exactly.
For a direction \(\ell\), define
\[
a_i(\eta)=\ell_i(\eta_0-\eta_{e_i}),
\]
and, for a local function \(f\),
\[
G_i f
=
\sum_{x\in\mathbb Z^d}
\left[
(\tau_x f)(\eta^{0,e_i})-(\tau_x f)(\eta)
\right].
\]
Only finitely many terms contribute.
The starting object is the standard bulk variational coefficient
\[
D_\ell
=
\frac{1}{2\chi}
\inf_{f\ \mathrm{local}}
\mathbb E_p\sum_i c_i(a_i+G_i f)^2.
\tag{1}
\]
This is a formulation of collective diffusion, not tagged-particle self-diffusion. The normalisation gives \(D=1\) for one-dimensional, rate-one simple symmetric exclusion. The underlying variational approach is established; identifying it with a particular hydrodynamic limit remains a separate, model-dependent theorem. arXiv
What must be certified
A trial current \(j_i\) must be antisymmetric under its bond exchange and satisfy
\[
j_i=0\qquad\text{where }c_i=0.
\]
The difficult condition is
\[
\mathbb E_p\sum_i j_iG_i f=0
\qquad
\text{for every local }f.
\tag{2}
\]
Checking this against a finite training dictionary is insufficient: an omitted corrector could invalidate the proposed bound.
The compiler replaces that infinite family of conditions with a finite, exact algebraic test.
The translation-orbit map
Set
\[
h=\sum_i j_i.
\]
Every local binary function has a unique squarefree expansion
\[
h(\eta)=\sum_A h_A\eta_A,
\qquad
\eta_A=\prod_{x\in A}\eta_x.
\]
Two finite sets have the same translation class when one is a translate of the other. For each class \([A]\), define
\[
(\mathcal O h)_{[A]}
=
\sum_{B\sim A}h_B.
\tag{3}
\]
These sums are finite.
Local admissibility theorem. For local antisymmetric currents,
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_p\sum_i j_iG_i f=0
\text{ for every local }f
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal O h=0.
}
\tag{4}
\]
Thus an infinite-system conservation requirement becomes the nullspace of a finite integer matrix. The matrix is independent of density.
Proof
Introduce the spatial covariance pairing
\[
\langle\!\langle u,v\rangle\!\rangle
=
\sum_x\operatorname{Cov}_{\mu_p}(u,\tau_xv).
\]
Locality and product equilibrium make this a finite sum for local \(u,v\).
Exchange symmetry gives
\[
\mathbb E_p\sum_i j_iG_i f
=
-2\langle\!\langle h,f\rangle\!\rangle.
\tag{5}
\]
Now expand \(h\) in orthonormal centred products,
\[
\xi_A
=
\prod_{x\in A}\frac{\eta_x-p}{\sqrt{\chi}},
\qquad
h=\sum_{A\ne\varnothing}\widehat h_A\xi_A.
\]
The constant coefficient is zero because each current has zero equilibrium mean.
Independence implies
\[
\langle\!\langle h,h\rangle\!\rangle
=
\sum_{[A]}
\left(
\sum_{B\in[A]}\widehat h_B
\right)^2.
\tag{6}
\]
If the left side of (4) holds, choose \(f=h\). Every orbit sum in (6) must vanish. Within each orbit, the corresponding terms can therefore be written as a finite sum of translation differences. Expanding the centred monomials back into occupation monomials preserves those differences, giving \(\mathcal O h=0\).
Conversely, \(\mathcal O h=0\) expresses \(h\) as a finite sum of terms \(\tau_x k-k\). Their spatial covariance pairings telescope to zero. Equation (5) then proves (2).
This proves an exact characterisation, not merely a sufficient ansatz.
Why the representation is useful
In one dimension, every antisymmetric current has the form
\[
j=(\eta_0-\eta_1)g(\eta_{\mathbb Z\setminus\{0,1\}}).
\]
An environment monomial supported on \(A\) contributes \(+1\) to the class
\([A\cup\{0\}]\) and \(-1\) to \([A\cup\{1\}]\). Before imposing forbidden-transition conditions, the orbit map is therefore an incidence matrix on a graph of finite configuration shapes.
The compiler searches for circulations in that graph, subject to exact local legality constraints.
It does not assume an unexplained compact representation. Squarefree monomials span every function on the chosen binary patch. The cost of that complete representation is paid explicitly.
3. From a compiled current to a rigorous interval
Define the primal trial value
\[
U(f)
=
\frac{1}{2\chi}
\mathbb E_p\sum_i c_i(a_i+G_i f)^2,
\]
and the dual trial value
\[
L(j)
=
\frac{1}{2\chi}
\mathbb E_p\sum_i
\left(
2a_i j_i-\frac{j_i^2}{c_i}
\right).
\tag{7}
\]
On forbidden transitions, both the current and its quotient contribution are zero.
For a current passing the compiler’s exact test,
\[
\boxed{
U(f)-L(j)
=
\frac{1}{2\chi}
\mathbb E_p
\sum_{i:c_i>0}
\frac{\left[c_i(a_i+G_i f)-j_i\right]^2}{c_i}.
}
\tag{8}
\]
To prove this, expand the square. The only extra term is
\[
2\mathbb E_p\sum_i j_iG_i f,
\]
which vanishes by (4).
Since (8) holds for every local \(f\), taking the infimum in (1) yields
\[
\boxed{L(j)\le D_\ell\le U(f).}
\tag{9}
\]
The completion-of-squares argument is familiar duality. The constructive gain is obtaining admissible \(j\) automatically, while preserving exact admissibility through numerical search and coefficient rounding.
No convergence claim about the optimiser is needed for validity. No spectral gap, mixing-time bound, trajectory length or finite-volume correction appears. An imperfect optimiser merely produces a wider valid interval.

I implemented the construction for the one-dimensional Bertini–Toninelli model,
\[
c(x,x+1;\eta)=1-\eta_{x-1}\eta_{x+2}.
\tag{10}
\]
The physical rate is unchanged: the forbidden exchanges remain forbidden. This is the same model used for explicit flow constructions in the recent dual-principle paper. arXiv

What the program actually constructs
The lower-bound environment contains twelve binary sites around the central bond. Its complete dictionary produces a matrix with 3,072 columns and 5,460 orbit constraints, whose exact nullspace has dimension 204.
The program evaluates these current coordinates on all \(4,096\) environment assignments, of which \(3,072\) permit the exchange. Weighted least squares selects a trial current.
Crucially, rounding occurs only in the 204 free nullspace coordinates, to denominator \(2^{40}\). Multiplication by the exact integer nullspace basis preserves all conservation identities.
The upper-bound search uses a ten-site corrector dictionary with 511 translated-shape representatives.
A separate verifier then reconstructs the current from the exported coefficients, checks every orbit identity using unbounded integers, and evaluates both functionals with rational arithmetic.

5. A symbolic consequence beyond numerical precision
A smaller, five-dimensional dual kernel gives an explicit bound valid at every density \(0<p<1\):
\[
L_3(p)
=
\frac{(1-p)(-p^4+4p^3+8p+5)}
{5+3p+p^2-p^3}.
\tag{11}
\]
An explicit four-site corrector gives
\[
U_4(p)
=
\frac{(1-p)(p^3-p^2+5p+3)}
{(3-p)(p+1)}.
\tag{12}
\]
Therefore,
\[
\boxed{L_3(p)\le D(p)\le U_4(p).}
\tag{13}
\]
The upper witness is particularly short. With \(h_i=\eta_i-p\),
\[
f
=
\frac{h_0h_1h_3-h_0h_2h_3}
{(p-3)(p+1)}.
\tag{14}
\]
The lower witness has nineteen nonzero rational coefficient functions, supplied in the bundle. The symbolic script constructs that witness, checks all orbit cancellations, derives the relevant moments and verifies its value.
The exact gap is
\[
U_4(p)-L_3(p)
=
\frac{2p^2(1-p)^3(1+2p-p^2)}
{(3-p)(p+1)(5+3p+p^2-p^3)}.
\tag{15}
\]
Set \(q=1-p\), the vacancy density. Expanding the two rational bounds gives
\[
L_3(1-q)
=
2q-\frac32q^2+\frac78q^3+O(q^4),
\]
\[
U_4(1-q)
=
2q-\frac32q^2+q^3+O(q^4).
\]
The squeeze proves
\[
\boxed{
D(1-q)=2q-\frac32q^2+O(q^3).
}
\tag{16}
\]
No analyticity assumption about \(D\) is needed. The statement follows from two explicit inequalities.
This deduction was obtained here. I have not established independent publication priority for the coefficient \(-3/2\).
6. The consequential failure: exact local kernels miss cooperative transport
The next test was the harder cooperative setting.
Call vacancy dynamics box-closed when a configuration filled outside a finite rectangle cannot make a legal exchange that creates the first vacancy outside that rectangle.
For such dynamics, the compiler encounters a structural impossibility.
No-go theorem
Let \(j_i\) be finite-range, legal, antisymmetric currents satisfying the exact orbit condition. Then, for every coordinate \(k\),
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_p[(\eta_0-\eta_{e_k})j_k]=0.
}
\tag{17}
\]
Consequently,
\[
L(j)\le0.
\tag{18}
\]
No increase in patch size can make this exact local-current hierarchy certify positive diffusion in a box-closed model.
Proof
Take a configuration filled outside a finite box \(\Lambda\). Only finitely many current terms can be nonzero. Orbit cancellation gives the pointwise identity
\[
\sum_{x,i}j_i(\tau_x\eta)=0.
\tag{19}
\]
Legality and box closure eliminate all currents across the boundary. Thus (19) is a divergence-free signed flow on the finite configuration graph inside the box.
Multiply it by the particle first moment
\[
M_k(\eta)=\sum_{x\in\Lambda}x_k\eta_x
\]
and average with respect to the Bernoulli product measure inside \(\Lambda\), with the exterior fixed occupied.
For an interior bond, exchange symmetry gives
\[
\mathbb E[j_{x,i}M_k]
=
-\frac12\mathbb E[j_{x,i}\nabla_{x,x+e_i}M_k]
=
-\frac12\delta_{ik}
\mathbb E[j_{x,i}(\eta_x-\eta_{x+e_i})].
\tag{20}
\]
Hence the sum of displacement-current pairings over interior bonds in direction \(k\) is zero.
Away from the boundary, each expectation equals the infinite-product expectation in (17). The remaining terms occupy only a fixed-width boundary layer. Dividing by box volume and sending the side lengths to infinity proves (17). Substitution into the dual functional proves (18).
Why this matters for the intended hard problem
The square-lattice \(k=2\) Kob–Andersen rule requires an additional vacant neighbour besides the destination. With all vacancies inside a rectangle, an exterior particle cannot make the first boundary-crossing jump: its only possible vacant neighbour is the destination itself. Thus the box-closure hypothesis applies. assafshap.github.io
Yet this model has a positive variational diffusion coefficient. The relevant rigorous result is formulated through a zero-softening limit; that variational limit equals the hard-rate infimum. This does not assert a hard-constraint hydrodynamic theorem for arbitrary initial configurations. assafshap.github.io
The implication is stronger than “small patches are inaccurate”:
Taking exact local conservation kernels and taking the infinite-range limit do not commute.

The fully admissible infinite-system current cannot be approximated in energy norm by a sequence of exactly admissible finite-range currents of this kind.
Related obstructions were already present in the literature: cooperative models are non-gradient, and unbounded comparison paths were already identified as necessary. The result here is a direct exclusion of this entire local signed-current certificate hierarchy under an explicit geometric hypothesis—not the first recognition that cooperative transport needs nonlocal mechanisms. assafshap.github.io
7. Retaining the hard problem: a different interface is required
The no-go theorem rules out the obvious continuation: simply enlarge the local nullspace.
Softening forbidden transitions is not a replacement either. Increasing rates gives a monotone upper comparison; it does not automatically certify a lower bound for the original hard dynamics.
The viable different object is a local current with a controlled conservation defect and a certified nonlocal completion.
Concretely, construct a computable local current \(j_R\), without requiring exact orbit cancellation, and an admissible completion \(J\) satisfying
\[
\|J-j_R\|_{c^{-1}}\le\varepsilon_R,
\qquad
\|v\|_{c^{-1}}^2
=
\mathbb E_p\sum_{i:c_i>0}\frac{v_i^2}{c_i}.
\tag{21}
\]
Expanding the dual functional and applying Cauchy–Schwarz gives
\[
\boxed{
D_\ell
\ge
L(j_R)
-
\frac{
2\|ca-j_R\|_{c^{-1}}\varepsilon_R+\varepsilon_R^2
}{2\chi}.
}
\tag{22}
\]
All finite terms in this expression can be compiled.
The decisive missing quantity is \(\varepsilon_R\). It must not be assumed as an oracle. It must come from an explicit legal completion procedure with certified routing congestion and tail probabilities. An unknown spectral gap or an assertion that boundary errors are small does not supply it.
This changes the representation requirement: finite stages must retain the information needed to complete their conservation defects, rather than annihilating those defects prematurely and thereby eliminating the transport they are meant to represent.
I have not constructed an affordable completion of this kind for cooperative Kob–Andersen dynamics. Existing unbounded-path constructions are relevant prior art, not a completed answer to that computational problem.
Assessment
The preserved result is substantive and reproducible: an exact local admissibility characterisation, a working compiler, three rational enclosures for an infinite-system coefficient, a density-uniform asymptotic deduction, and a proof identifying where the construction necessarily fails.
What is not established is equally specific: independent novelty, polynomial accuracy complexity, an advantage over the strongest optimised implementation, or a practical solution of cooperative glassy transport.
The computational operation demonstrated here is finite compilation of globally valid transport witnesses. The more consequential operation still required is constructive nonlocal current completion with affordable, certified error control. The no-go theorem shows why that must be a genuinely different construction, rather than a larger execution of the successful local one.
