The primary Bizeul–Klartag–Lehec preprint claims a universal KLS theorem, but this audit does not certify its imported base theorem or external novelty/acceptance.

The primary source is Bizeul–Klartag–Lehec, arXiv:2610.05474v1, posted 4 October 2026: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05474. It claims universal KLS through all-order cumulant bounds, Eldan localization, suspension, and a tilt-average criterion. The tilt-average criterion is proved within Section 3 of that paper (with Song–Zhang credited for its origin), so it is too strong to call it an unchecked import. The difficult imported base is R3<=8I, obtained from Letwin's quadratic-form variance theorem, with anisotropic generalized thin-shell/moment-measure work behind it. Letwin source: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24164; Chen–Klartag source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23307. I checked the source statements and derivation path, not the complete proofs of those imported results.

I checked the cumulant-energy Itô algebra and factorial recurrence from the BKL primary text. Whitening by A_t^{-1/2} is a congruence in the energy functional, so the displayed drift estimate does not introduce a dimension or condition-number multiplier. The third-cumulant matrix bound supplies the universal Cm^2 error coefficient. For b_m=K^(m-1)((m-1)!)^2, the binomial/factorial cancellation and recurrence ratio in Section 6 check as printed; K>=max(5,9C,64) closes that algebra. This remains conditional on the source's SDE, imported R3 estimate, and expectation identities.

I derived a finite-horizon inverse-covariance estimate for compactly supported isotropic input under that same R3 premise. For every p>0 and finite T, it bounds E[(det A_t)^(-p)] by exp(5pnT+4p^2 n^2T), then bounds E[lambda_min(A_t)^(-p)] using the support radius. It rules out finite-time covariance collapse and supplies all negative moments needed to make fixed-order whitened cumulant and Itô coefficients integrable on finite horizons. The dimension-dependent constants enter only this regularity justification, not the universal K induction. Lemma and assumptions are detailed in revisions/02 and /03; this is a conditional repair, not an independent proof of R3 or the entire localization construction.

The source's Lemma 5.2 uses integrability to pass T to infinity. A sequence T_j with E E_m(T_j)->0 suffices; the finite-T inequality and monotone convergence then give the stated result. The revision spells out that sequence argument and true-martingale justification under the conditional moment bound.
