## Independent reconstruction of the small covariance-lift ingredient

For logconcave pi∝exp(-V), the exponential lift pi^Z(dx,dt)∝exp(-dt)1[V(x)≤dt] dxdt has conditional law T|X=(V(X)+E)/d, E~Exp(1) independent. Thus Var T=(Var V+1)/d². The sharp varentropy theorem, Fradelizi–Madiman–Wang [1508.04093v2, Theorem 2.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.04093v2), says Var V≤d. For ||u||²+s²=1,

    Var(u·X+sT)≤(sqrt(Λ)||u||+sqrt(Var T)|s|)²≤Λ+2/d.

This covariance proof is reconstructed; the varentropy theorem is a cited prerequisite, its full proof not independently re-proved. Ground-set absorption uses a density maximum bound from the contained unit ball, then Gaussian maximum entropy on the whitened distribution to bound determinant covariance. One must retain the ground-set scaling when claiming Λ alone.

## Why constant-time hidden-mean correction does not extend to arbitrary finite-variance samples

Here is an elementary reconstruction of the known bounded-variance obstruction, specialized to a hypothetical Gaussian-mean correction interface. An unknown iid source is either P₀=δ₀ or P₁=(1-t²)δ₀+t²δ_(1/t), with 0<t≤1/2. P₁ has mean t and variance 1-t²≤1; P₀ has mean zero. Suppose an adaptive sampler outputs a law within t/20 TV of N(E_P X,1) for both sources. The target Gaussians have TV=2Phi(t/2)-1≥c t, hence its two output laws must differ by ≥c' t. Coupling both runs until a nonzero source draw occurs bounds their output TV by t² E₀ N: along the all-zero transcript, the conditional chance of the first differing draw is t² at each requested oracle call. Therefore E₀ N≥c'/t.

This coupling handles random stopping because N is the number of calls on the P₀ transcript, and sum_{j≥1}Pr₀(N≥j)=E₀N. It is a marginal output-law lower bound; it does not require estimating the mean or transcript-conditional accuracy. If a sampler allows failure, include the failure symbol in the output law or charge its probability; otherwise conditioning on success could evade the task definition. This is a deduction illustrating the source's Proposition 4.1, not a new sampling lower bound.

## Unexplored and rejected inference routes

1. Rejected: generic unbiased estimator plus bounded variance suffices for polylog-accuracy correction. The preceding two-point family defeats it.
2. Rejected: small nonconvex FI certifies missing-mode recovery. Explicit smooth mixture in fisher_obstruction.md defeats it.
3. Rejected: rank-one pointwise derivatives imply rank-one global covariance. The sine-curve construction in technical_lead.md has full-rank covariance.
4. Unproved: differential effective dimension survives the cloud paper's nonlinear gradient secants. This is the leading discriminating lemma.
5. Unexplored: adaptive annealing based only on observed radial variance. Certifying the variance throughout unobserved parameter intervals is a separate problem; a finite grid does not establish a pathwise bound.

No nearest-prior-art exhaustion or independent external validation is claimed. The universal elementary obstruction and reconstructed exact-oracle corollary are separated from author claims and finite checks.
