4. SPARSE QUANTUM PCP: RETAINED POSITIVE CONSTRUCTION AND EXACT INTERFACE

Sparse quantum PCP seeks QMA hardness at constant normalized energy gap with polynomial-row, efficiently computable Hamiltonians; weaker than constant-locality quantum PCP. BMVZ tensor amplification [R1] (first Oct 2025, CCC 2026) supplies a constant-promise-gap hard family of polynomially many nonlocal structured tests h_j=I−⊗_i(I−Π_ji), with small seed projectors; its authors already proposed sparsification. Formal seeds are general projections, not automatically rank-one stabilizer terms.

Retained restricted class: H=Σ_j w_j(I−P_j), w_j≥0, Σw_j=1, P_j=∏_i[(I+R_ji)/2]; R_ji Hermitian Clifford involutions with polynomial descriptions, commuting within a clause; clauses may not commute. A derives normalized K on O(n²) qubits, polynomial row support/entry-oracle costs, with
  3(1−ε)λ_min(H)/19 ≤ λ_min(K) ≤ 3λ_min(H)/19,
fixed 0<ε<1. Exact zero completeness and protection against arbitrary physical/auxiliary states. Randomized high-probability expander construction, not deterministic/certified reduction. Required QMA hardness of this restricted class unestablished.

Mechanism: stabilizer-frame coherent isometry V|ψ>=D_n^(-1/2) Σ_L|L>C_L†|ψ>, D_n=∏_(i=1)^n(2^i+1), L maximal isotropic Pauli spaces; exponentially many frames with O(n²)-bit succinct labels, not explicitly stored. Clifford U lifts to monomial ρ(U), ρ(U)V=VU; products remain one-nonzero/row. Neighboring frames have two-nonzero/row basis changes; finite symplectic geometry plus sampled transitions yields positive sparse A with ker A=ran V, A≥γ_0(I−VV†), γ_0=3/16. Explicit finite-field small-bias sampler has unwanted biases [0,ε], enabling positive sparse B with (1−ε)H≤H_app:=V†BV≤H and BV=VH_app. Set K=(A+γ_0B)/(1+γ_0); λ_min(K)=3λ_min(H_app)/19. Monomial/stabilizer, geometry, concentration and small-bias ingredients have prior art; this particular derivation needs review.

Critical boundary: rank-one stabilizer Π=C|0^k><0^k|C† has reflection I−2Π Clifford only for k≤2, generally non-Clifford for k≥3 (3-qubit controlled-controlled phase). BJSW hard local-Clifford Hamiltonians [R2] use stabilizer projectors up to five qubits: “Clifford projector” ≠ “Clifford reflection.” No sparse-PCP proof follows. B's audit sketches bounded-degree Feynman–Kitaev [R3] + BJSW decomposition, SWAP core stabilizer eigenbasis, padding/splitting smaller terms into ≤8 rank-one five-qubit terms. This is a separate derived reduction sketch, not BMVZ's verbatim theorem; cone obstruction below does not need hardness.

General sufficient OPEN interface: for actual amplified hard H, construct one common efficiently described isometry V and sparse positive contractions C,B with P=VV†, ker C=ran V, C≥γ(I−P), αH≤V†BV≤βH; α,β,γ>0 independent of size/depth.
