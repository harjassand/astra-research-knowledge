## Access attempts and limits

- A 2024 Eindhoven University thesis titled “Heavy-atom kinetic isotope effects for investigating reaction mechanisms of the methanol-to-hydrocarbons reaction” was located as a possible (^{12})C/(^{13})C kinetic-isotope-effect source. The portal record was readable, but the linked 8.84 MB PDF returned HTTP 403 in the browser tool; command-line retrieval returned a 5.6 KB HTML error page rather than a PDF. The failed page was discarded. No numerical KIE claim is based on this source.
- The isotope article's main PDF was accessible through the TU Delft repository. A direct ACS web open failed, but the ACS Figshare API and download endpoint for the SI succeeded.
- The 4TU archive was indexed only to determine dataset scope. No 695 MB download was attempted after confirming it was the non-isotope FASPA dataset.
