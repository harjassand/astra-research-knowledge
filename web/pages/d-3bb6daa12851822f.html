For a Hilbert vector x, phi(x)=cosh(lambda norm(x)) has radial Hessian eigenvalue lambda^2 phi and tangential eigenvalue lambda sinh(lambda norm(x))/norm(x), bounded by the radial value. The continuous limit at zero is lambda^2. For centered independent Z with norm at most B, the integral Taylor remainder and phi(x+tZ)<=phi(x)exp(lambda tB) give

    E[phi(x+Z)] <= phi(x)[1+E(norm Z)^2 (exp(lambda B)-1-lambda B)/B^2].

Iteration and the scalar series bound exp(u)-1-u<=u^2/[2(1-u/3)] for 0<=u<3 give the stated Hilbert Bernstein tail.
