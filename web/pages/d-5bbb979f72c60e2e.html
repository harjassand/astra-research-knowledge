1. INPUT AND EXACT RECOGNIZED GRAMMAR

The raw input is a finite dense table of integer complexes y->y', source AND
product sizes <=2, rational boxes 0<l<=u, and integer q>=1. Propensities are
k_r(t,history) x_under_y, exactly zero when y is unavailable. All rate
endpoints, dimensions, nonnegativity and integer entries are checked.

Species may be peeled in some order into a nonempty core and attached leaves.
At the step adding Y, EVERY reaction involving Y has one of the forms
  X_i -> X_i+Y,       X_i+Y -> X_i,
with i in a nonempty set P of already acquired species. Parallel channels
are aggregated by summing lower/upper endpoints; every parent has both
directions with positive lower bounds. No other reaction involving Y is
silently retained. Different stages may use previously attached species as
catalysts. The following gate tests are made against the entire previous
subsystem, including its actual binary removals:
  (i) Some zero-source reaction produces a member of P.
  (ii) Whenever a previous reaction decreases S_P=sum_{i in P}x_i,
       its complete reactant support lies inside P.
Thus P is a syntactically drain-closed activation gate. P consisting of all
previous species automatically satisfies (ii); it satisfies (i) because the
base has immigration. A small parent subset need not pass.

The base is the EXACT recognized hinge class of HINGE_RECOVERY.txt: primitive
0<->X_i for every base species, catalytic single births X_j->X_j+X_i,
single-count binary removals, optional unary conversions, and a positive
reciprocal removal of catalyst j with recipient i for every birth of i from j.
The code also permits additional binary drains y->0 with |y|=2, provided
the full base remains weakly reversible. These extra drains decrease every
coordinate hinge and its exponential, hence only improve their generators.
The remaining hinge pattern is checked independently after removing these
extras. Every leaf pair is reversible. Consequently the whole recognized
network is weakly reversible, hence endotactic. This claim is not a generic
endotacticity recognizer and never equates a real coset with communication.

The code searches possible terminal leaves and memoizes the species subsets.
The unpeeled table for a subset is uniquely the original reactions supported
inside it. It either finds a decomposition satisfying every test or returns
REJECTED_CLASS. This is complete for the stated peeling grammar and admitted
base, not for all stable binary networks. No partition, stationary law,
activation oracle, real-field solver or supplied Lyapunov function is used.

2. FINITE-JUMP RECIPROCAL KILLING LEMMA

Consider ANY previous binary subsystem passing the gate tests, not necessarily
a collection of independent immigration/death roots. Acquire from its raw
table
  b=sum lower endpoints of zero-source edges increasing S_P >0,
  U1=sum upper endpoints of S_P-decreasing unary-source edges,
  U2=sum upper endpoints of S_P-decreasing binary-source edges,
  D=U1/2+2U2,
  beta=min parent catalytic-death lower coefficients for the new Y,
  alpha=max parent catalytic-birth upper coefficients for Y,
  c=min(1,beta/(2D)) if D>0, and c=1 if D=0,
  g(S)=1+c/(S+1), G=1+c, gamma=min(cb/2,beta/2), eta=gamma/G.
All coefficients are acquired positive rationals except D, which may be zero.

At S=0, a negative S jump is disabled: by (ii) its nonzero source is entirely
in P. Every positive S jump makes g smaller. A zero-source jump adding h>=1
members of P changes g by -ch/(h+1)<=-c/2. Therefore
  L_t g(0)<=-cb/2.
This remains true even with huge counts outside P and adaptive rates.

At S>=1, discard all positive S jumps because their change of g is negative.
A negative jump of size h is bounded by the source size, so h=1 or2. Its
exact change is
  c h / [(S+1)(S-h+1)].
Every unary-source propensity is <=S and has h=1, giving contribution
at most c U1/(S+1)<=c U1/2. For a binary source supported in P,
its falling factorial is <=S(S-1); in particular it vanishes at S=1.
When h=1 the contribution is <=c U2(S-1)/(S+1)<=c U2.
When h=2 (S>=2) it is <=2c U2 S/(S+1)<=2c U2.
Summing separate reactions with their upper endpoints gives
  L_t g(S)<=cD<=beta/2.
No continuum monomial replaces a disabled propensity in these inequalities.

The per-particle death intensity of Y is a_Y(t)=sum_i beta_i(t)x_i>=beta S.
Since g>=1, the exact coefficient of Y in the generator of g(S)Y is
  L_t g-a_Y(t)g <= -gamma                          (KILL)
at S=0 by the first case, and for S>=1 by beta/2-beta S<=-beta/2.
Also the birth intensity ell_Y<=alpha S and g<=G. This is the new algebraic
attachment gate; its proof charges actual unary AND binary parent losses.

3. COMPOSE THE ACQUIRED GLOBAL FOSTER FUNCTION

The existing base acquires h_i, Z=sum_i(x_i-h_i)_+, H0=sum_i h_i,
V_h=1+Z and exact L_t V_h<=B_h-delta_h V_h. Extra binary drains make
both V_h and its exponential no larger, so preserve these inequalities.
Set the normalization K0=H0+1 and V0=K0 V_h. Then
  N_core<=Z+H0<=K0(1+Z)=V0,
  V0>=1, L_t V0<=B0-delta0 V0,
  B0=K0 B_h, delta0=delta_h, V0<=K0(1+N_core).
These are acquired by the hinge formulas, not assumed on an unknown core.

At an attachment step suppose V_old>=max(1,N_old),
L_t V_old<=B_old-delta_old V_old. Acquire
  M=max(1,(G alpha+1)/delta_old),
  V_new=1+M V_old+g(S)Y,
  delta_new=min(1/M,gamma/G),
  B_new=M B_old+delta_new.
Core reaction vectors never change Y, and new leaf vectors never change core
counts. Rate choices MAY depend on Y and the whole history; every inequality
is pointwise in the rate box and therefore still applies. Exact generator
decomposition and (KILL) give
  L_t V_new
   <=M B_old-M delta_old V_old+G alpha S-gamma Y
   <=M B_old-V_old-gamma Y
   <=M B_old-delta_new(V_new-1)
    =B_new-delta_new V_new.                         (FOSTER)
Here S<=N_old<=V_old and M delta_old>=G alpha+1. This proves the global
inequality at EVERY count state, not merely at a boundary fixture.
V_new>=1+N_old+Y, and if V_old<=A_old(1+N_old), then
V_new<=[1+M A_old+G](1+N_new). Thus the acquired function is proper, has
rational bounded reciprocal coefficients and is uniformly comparable to
total count. Iterate the step for every acquired leaf. Write the final
constants as B,delta,V; always V>=max(1,N).

Why normalization matters for the cost claim: no shrinking lower-coercivity
constant is recursively divided by itself. M>=1 retains V>=N. After the
first attachment delta<=1; subsequent M=(G alpha+1)/delta_old. The bit height
of delta grows additively under division by these acquired rational factors
or resets to the gate constant gamma/G. This avoids an unsupported polynomial
bit claim based only on counting arithmetic operations.

Stopped compensators at finite count balls prove nonexplosion from LV<=B and
proper V: P(exit V-level m by time t)<=(V(x)+Bt)/m. Rates in each finite ball
are bounded, including under the controls, so infinite bounded-state firing
cannot cause explosion. Removing localization gives EV(X_t)<=V(x)+Bt and
the standard integrating-factor moment bound
  EV(X_t)<=e^(-delta t)V(x)+(B/delta)(1-e^(-delta t)).
These statements can be restarted after any observed history; Markov policies
and environmental independence are unnecessary.

4. ACQUIRED PATH TO A POSITIVE BUFFER AND ITS PHYSICAL DEADLINE

The base has a direct primitive immigration/death for each species. To
Produce(i) for a base species use its immigration. To Produce(Y), choose one
already acquired parent i, first Produce(i) and then fire X_i->X_i+Y.
The acquired word is enabled from EVERY initial state and ends at a state
>=x+e_Y. Each selected event adds exactly one total molecule. Its length
L_Y=L_i+1, with base lengths1. Hence every L_i<=d and A1=sum_i L_i<=d^2.
The stored recursive grammar is acyclic; no expanded word is required at
acquisition. Repeated q times, its word length is Aq=q A1 and ends at counts
at least q in every coordinate.

Set W=1+V and Re=2(B+delta+1)/delta. Then outside Ke={W<=Re},
  L_t W<=-(delta/2)W.
Thus E_x exp[(delta/2)tau_Ke]<=W(x) outside Ke by stopped integrating factors;
inside Ke the hitting time is zero. Also E_x tau_Ke<=V(x), because outside
Ke the original Foster drift is <=-1. Let Rn=ceil(Re); every core state has
N<=Rn since N<=V<W<=Re.

Starting inside Ke, produce one of EVERY species in the acquired order.
This costs A1 events and ends with every species positive and N<=Rn+A1.
Drain leaves in reverse attachment order. Their chosen catalysts are lower
in the order, remain positive and are never consumed by a leaf removal.
Drain base species by their direct deaths after all leaves vanish. Every
drain removes one molecule, so the number of drains is the molecule count
after activation, <=Rn+A1. The state is now zero. Produce the q-buffer word.
Therefore every Ke state has an acquired target word of length at most
  H=Rn+2A1+Aq,
all of whose states have total count <=D=max(Rn+A1,Aq).

Let U=sum all original upper rate endpoints, Q=U(D+1)^2, lmin=min original
lower endpoints, a=lmin/(2Q), p=a^H, t0=H/Q. Any desired enabled reaction has
intensity >=lmin and total competing intensity <=Q throughout its waiting
interval as long as no event occurs. Predictable hazard integration gives
  P(desired next event occurs within1/Q | current history)
   >=lmin integral_0^(1/Q)e^(-Qs)ds >=lmin/(2Q)=a.
After that desired event condition again on the complete current history.
The whole word occurs by t0 with conditional probability at least p, even
when it is shorter than H, because a<=1. This proves actual buffered target
Gq={all coordinates>=q} recovery, not merely complex-graph connectivity.

The mean retry argument gives the explicit rational bound
  E_x tau_Gq <= V(x)+[Re+(B+1)t0]/p.                  (MEAN)
Every failed attempt has unconditional expected return cost bounded by the
end-of-attempt V, whose expectation is <=Re+Bt0. Conditioning after each
history and summing failure probabilities <=(1-p)^n justifies the bound;
one never replaces a conditional failure law by an independent trial law.

5. A COSTED EXPONENTIAL CLOCK FOR THE RARE TARGET

This strengthens the mean retry bound and makes total firing costs possible.
Acquire eta0=min(delta/2,1/(2t0)) and M0=2(1+Re+Bt0). On a failed attempt,
let L be its t0 duration plus the subsequent return time to Ke. The return
exponential moment above, conditioned at the end of the attempt, implies
  E[e^(eta0 L)1{failure} | attempt history]
   <= e^(eta0 t0) E[W(X_t0)] <= M0,
since e^(eta0 t0)<=e^(1/2)<2. Also P(failure | history)<=1-p.
Put theta=p/[2(M0+p)] and zeta=theta eta0. Conditional Holder gives
  E[e^(zeta L)1{failure} | history]
   <=(1-p)^(1-theta) M0^theta.
Using log(1-p)<=-p and log M0<=M0-1, its log is at most
  -(1-theta)p+theta(M0-1)<=-p/2.
Consequently the failure-cycle transform is <=r=1/(1+p/2)<1, because
e^(-p/2)<=1/(1+p/2). A successful attempt reaches its target by t0 and
has transform <=2. Success after n failures therefore contributes <=2r^n,
by repeated conditional expectation, without requiring independent cycles.
Summing, and then charging the first return from x to Ke, gives
  E_x exp(zeta tau_Gq) <= W(x)(2+4/p).                (CLOCK)
This is finite in physical time for every input/policy/state admitted here.
p,theta,zeta and the right side are rational numbers; only proof inequalities
use exponential/logarithmic functions. The compact certificate records powers
and rational expressions; the enormous expanded rational cost is not hidden.

6. TAGGED SURVIVAL AND ACQUIRED FOURTH MOMENTS

The gate lemma also gives Lg-a_Y g<=-eta g because g<=G and eta=gamma/G.
Label leaf particles only for this proof; a mass-action death selects a
uniform present particle, so a live tag has per-particle death intensity a_Y.
Its death changes no argument of g. The killed compensator of
1{tag alive}g is exactly 1{alive}(Lg-a_Yg), including under rate feedback.
Stopped integrating factors and g>=1 prove, conditionally at any time sigma,
  P(tag survives sigma+u | F_sigma)<=G e^(-eta u).    (TAG)
No conditioning on the future environment or independence among tags is used.

For the base, HINGE_RECOVERY acquires a rational exponential base A=1+eps,
decay c0, exponent radius r0 and offset b0 such that
  L A^Z<=-c0 A^Z+b0 1{Z<=r0},
  sup_t E A^{Z(t)}<=A^{Z(0)}+b0/c0.
Extra two-count drains contribute nonpositively to this generator as well.
For integer z>=6, binom(z,4)>=(z/2)^4/24; for smaller z use z^4<=1296.
The binomial expansion therefore gives for every integer z>=0
  z^4<=1296+384 eps^(-4)A^z.
N_core<=Z+H0 and (u+v)^4<=8(u^4+v^4) give the acquired uniform base bound
  M4_core(x)=8[1296+384 eps^(-4)(A^{Z(x)}+b0/c0)+H0^4]
  >=sup_t E N_core(X_t)^4.                           (MBASE)

At an attachment, ell_Y<=alpha N_old, so Lambda4=alpha^4 M4_old bounds
the fourth moment of the birth intensity at every deterministic time.
For a birth count K_u on any interval of length <=1, the compensator,
(K+1)^4-K^4<=4(K+1)^3<=16(K^3+1), Young's inequality
K^3 ell<=(3/4)K^4+(1/4)ell^4 and ell<=1+ell^4 give
  d/du E K_u^4 <=12 E K_u^4+20 Lambda4+16.
Localization, Gronwall and e^12<=2^24 yield the rational bound
  E K_u^4<=B4=(2^24/12)(20 Lambda4+16).               (BIRTHS)

Partition births before a deterministic t into backward intervals of length
one. At an interval endpoint sigma, its birth count K is known. For its S
survivors at t, S^4<=K^3 S and (TAG) applied to every tag alive at sigma give
  E[S^4 | F_sigma]<=K^4 G e^(-eta(t-sigma)).
This remains true if some of the interval's particles already died by sigma.
Minkowski over the finitely many cohorts and the initial y particles gives
  ||Y(t)||_4<=G^(1/4)[y+ B4^(1/4)/(1-e^(-eta/4))].
Since e^(eta/4)>=1+eta/4,
  sup_t E Y(t)^4 <=8G[y^4+(1+4/eta)^4 B4].            (MLEAF)
This is the c10_s01 tagged/cohort mechanism applied to the new gate; it is
credited, not newly claimed as a probability principle. No Poisson-mixture
identity or independent environment is used.

N_new=N_old+Y and (u+v)^4<=8(u^4+v^4) then give a rational affine recurrence
in M4_old, y^4 and constants. The code stores one recurrence node per stage,
avoiding an exponentially duplicated expression tree. Iteration supplies a
compact rational-expression M4(x)>=sup_t E N(X_t)^4 for the full hierarchy.
Materializing A^{Z(x)} can be exponential in binary initial-count size and is
explicitly an evaluation cost, not cheap certificate acquisition.

7. EXPECTED REACTION FIRINGS TO THE BUFFER — NO FREE PHYSICAL CLOCK

For the complete network, actual total intensity lambda_tot(t)<=U(N(t)+1)^2.
By the fourth-moment bound,
  sup_t E lambda_tot(t)^2<=Mlambda=8U^2(M4(x)+1).
(CLOCK) gives P(tau_Gq>t)<=Dclock e^(-zeta t),
Dclock=W(x)(2+4/p). Cauchy-Schwarz gives
  E[lambda_tot(t)1{tau_Gq>t}]
   <=sqrt(Mlambda Dclock)e^(-zeta t/2).
Integrate and use localized counting compensators, then monotone convergence:
  E_x number of all reaction events up to tau_Gq
   <=(2/zeta)sqrt(Mlambda Dclock)
   <=(2/zeta)(1+Mlambda Dclock).                      (FIRINGS)
The last bound is rational. It counts desired AND competing physical firings,
not just the length of a prescribed word. State encoding, propensity arithmetic,
controller computation/random bits, energy/fuel and real experimental work are
additional costs. Arbitrary predictable policies need not be computable, so
this theorem is not a generic simulator for such a policy.

For fixed homogeneous coefficients, the base primitive paths and reverse
leaf-pair paths show the entire lattice communicates: activation, reverse-order
leaf drainage and base drainage give a path to zero; primitive core births and
leaf adjustments with catalysts retained give paths from zero. The proper
Foster estimate therefore gives positive recurrence and a unique invariant
law. Its low moments can be bounded by tight Cesaro laws/Fatou with the
uniform fourth moments above. This fixed-rate existence is substantial known
prior-art overlap, not a new all-network recurrence theorem. Stationary Markov
feedback in the box has the same count communication and Foster estimate.

8. ORDINARY BIT ACQUISITION, EXPANDED OUTPUT AND OPERATION COST

Let L be the total explicit binary input length, including q. Every gate uses
finite sums of positive rational endpoints, a constant number of products,
ratios, fourth powers and comparisons. Its coefficients have polynomial
ordinary bit height in L. Base hinge thresholds and compact exponential data
also have polynomial height; numerical thresholds may be exponential.
The normalization in Section3 ensures delta heights accumulate additively,
M heights are polynomial, and B/upper-factor heights are at most a further
polynomial sum over <=d steps. The rational Foster function coefficients,
core thresholds, grammar, total-rate cap and fourth-moment recurrence therefore
have polynomial binary output length. The subset search costs at most
2^d poly(L) ordinary bit operations. It does not rely on a floating solver.

A1<=d^2. Rn,H,r0 may have numerical magnitudes 2^poly(L); q is binary encoded.
The compact certificate stores p=a^H and the base exponential offset as base/
exponent pairs plus short rational formulas for theta,zeta and moment constants.
No powers are silently expanded by compile_network. V(x) and its exact generator
at a binary state use polynomial rational work in L and the state's bit length.
Ordinary expanded rational p/clock/firing outputs may require 2^poly(L+b_x)
bits, where b_x is initial-count encoding. A finite exponentiation/rational
algorithm computes them within 2^poly(L+b_x) ordinary bit operations; allowing
a conservative larger polynomial includes numerator/denominator multiplications
and expanded q-words. Hence this is a fully terminating costed algorithm, with
polynomial compact data and singly exponential worst-case explicit output.

Physical expected recovery and firing bounds can be vastly larger than the
encoded certificate and are retained exactly. No polynomial physical-time,
work, energy, general local-intelligence or asymptotic hardware-speed claim is
made. Backend/tokens/total research CPU and unmodeled energy remain UNKNOWN.
