## 1. A canonical, connected counterexample to the collection inference in Eq.(4.8)

Use two physical qubits q1,q2 and checks

    Ci=Z1 Z2, Cj=Z2,
    Gi=(I+Ci)/2, Gj=(I+Cj)/2, Ei=I-Gi, Ej=I-Gj,
    H0=Ei+Ej, P=Gi Gj.

The actual check graph has edge i--j, since both checks act on q2. The qubit graph also connects q1,q2. The stabilizer group fixes a unique state. Both input Paulis Z1 and X1 are supported only on q1, whose actual check incidence is {i}. Their minimum connected qubit cover is {q1}; hence their canonical Section3.3 extension is the singleton {i}.

For the actual Hermitian perturbation

    Z(0)=mu Z1 + eps X1

the canonical initial decomposition gives

    M_i=mu Gi Z1 Gi=mu Cj Gi,           pure g word {i},
    D_i=mu Ei Z1 Ei=-mu Cj Ei,          pure e word {i},
    V_+=eps R, R=Ei X1 Gi,             raising word {i},
    V_-=eps R*.

All word supports are connected and all underlying operators have actual support q1, satisfying primary Eq.(3.7). This establishes canonical initial reachability, rather than assuming an arbitrary allowed collection.

On the global ground space,

    [M_i,R] P = -mu R P.

Thus the ghost coefficient in primary Lemma5.1 is q=-mu and Delta=-mu. There is only one non-ghost D word. Its products satisfy

    D_i R=-mu Cj R.

All these products remain admissible singleton raising words. Summing the primary Eq.(5.14) geometric series, or its Eq.(5.16) expansion, gives

    A_+ = i eps R (I-mu I-mu Cj)^(-1).                    (1)

This series converges for sufficiently small mu, for example mu=1/8. Its denominator is 1-2mu in the Cj=+1 sector and1 in the Cj=-1 sector. It is a valid singleton raising-word operator: if phi_+=(1-2mu)^(-1), phi_-=1, a=(phi_++phi_-)/2 and b=(phi_+-phi_-)/2, then

    A_+= Ei [i eps(a X1 - b Z1 X1)] Gi.

The bracketed underlying operator is supported on q1. The hidden Cj factor in the outer projectors does not invalidate the class.

The exact commutator with the actual unperturbed plus block-diagonal operator is

    [H0+mu Z1,R]=(I-2mu Cj)R.

Therefore the actual linear residual is

    L_+ = i[H0+mu Z1,A_+] + eps R
        = -2mu eps R Ej.                                 (2)

It is not the zero operator, but (I-P)L_+P=0. Adding the adjoint generator and V_- gives the Hermitian residual

    L = -2mu eps X1 Ej.                                  (3)

Crucially, the prescribed commutator collection uses only the singleton {i}. Each product of an e or g singleton with an i+ singleton is another i+ singleton. The operator in (2) is itself a legal i+ class:

    L_+=Ei[-mu eps(X1+Z1 X1)]Gi.

Consequently the restriction to the pure+/g words has a nonzero **linear** residual. Global ground-block cancellation, primary Eqs.(4.4),(4.5), does not make that restriction vanish. Thus primary Eq.(4.8) does not follow as the claimed operator-collection statement under the explicitly specified rules, even at a canonical initial step. This is stronger than the first disconnected-paramagnet example, which was not a legal connected ghost fixture and is not used here.

For mu=1/8 and eps=1/1024 the nonzero residual entries have coefficient -1/4096. The exact symbolic replay verifies the identities above for indeterminates mu,eps and separately records the rational instance.

This does not exploit an excluded asymptotic code interface. If desired, tensor this fixed, unique-ground, two-qubit component with any code family satisfying the theorem's assumptions. The tensor preserves that family's distance and logical dimension, modifies fixed incidence constants only, and connected sets remain within a component. The same local collection issue persists. Nevertheless, the toy's actual spectrum is stable for small perturbations, and no counterexample to Theorem2.1 is asserted.

## 2. Exact missing step, and why graph extension remains unclosed

In this toy one can replace (2) by a D word on {i,j}, with i+ and j=e. This is a new decomposition, not the restriction of the collection defined by primary Eq.(3.17). The source does not state that re-decomposition or a bound on its norm before using Eq.(4.8) to prove the double-exponential flow.

For a general ghost, its scalar property uses a stabilizer representation in B_(ell|S|)(S), rather than necessarily in S. A repair that expands all projectors in that buffer can add a number of check labels exponential in |S| on the allowed growth graphs. Its intensive **word-cardinality** norm cost cannot be silently absorbed into a fixed decay loss. A controlled conversion, or a different normal form with proved generator and relative bounds, is the load-bearing missing lemma.

Thus the audits of connectivity, actual versus declared support, ghost buffer bounds and relative boundedness are useful local lemmas, but they do not yet prove a full declared-support stability theorem. The note `DECLARED_SUPPORT_STABILITY_EXTENSION.md` is now explicitly marked unclosed for this reason. The CZ-grid construction can be used as a conditional reduction to the **published** actual-graph theorem; it is not an independent verification of this unresolved KAM proof step. ## 3. Two smaller source errors and their scoped repairs

**Ghost support, Eq.(5.6).** Take checks Z0Z1 and Z1Z2 and the valid ghost G_first with underlying I and S={first}. Its Pauli component Z0Z1 has check incidence {first,second}, so the claimed containment supp_c(p) subset S is false. Its actual qubit support is contained in the union of actual supports of checks in S. That latter property suffices for TQO-I, TQO-II and the primary coefficient count. This is a repair of a support statement, not a theorem counterexample.

**Initial conversion, AppendixB.1.** Regrouping by r=|M(p)| makes the compensating exponential and radius count consistent. It proves the stated initial conversion with the same final constants. We do not need |ext(p)|=|M(p)|. The displayed grouping by |ext(p)| is not generally the indicated equality.

**Reverse conversion, AppendixB.2/Proposition3.2.** There is an actual counterexample to the printed estimate, not only a missing factor in a loose proof. Use one check C=Z^tensor8 and a connected singleton ghost word. Let A=(X+Y)/sqrt(2), and

    O_S=G_C A^tensor8=G_C A^tensor8 G_C.

A is a Hermitian unitary anticommuting with Z, so A^tensor8 commutes with C. The operator norm is1. Its Pauli expansion has128 nonzero full-support terms, each coefficient1/16. Thus the intensive Pauli norm at mu=0 is8. The claimed reverse word-norm bound gives exp(log(4wq))||O_S||=4 because wq=1 and |S|=1. The corrected safe bound is

    |||O|||_mu <= Wq ||O||_{Wc(mu+log4)}
               <= ||O||_{Wc(mu+log4)+log Wq}.

The ground-band theorem does not require this reverse conversion. A visual PDFpage48 check confirms the printed exponent; this finding is not an OCR inference.
