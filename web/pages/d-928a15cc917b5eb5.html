FINITE MODEL AND TARGET
The ambient graph is the nearest-neighbor square lattice Z^2. Let V be
ANY finite set of free vertices, with all exterior neighbors and any
additional vertices fixed. Edges involving a free vertex impose
|h(x)-h(y)|<=1. Each free component is connected to a fixed vertex. The
uniform Lebesgue law on the resulting compact polytope is considered only
when its volume on the free coordinates is positive. No regularity of V
or bound on its number of components is assumed. In the zero-data law
every fixed height is zero; extend the field by zero to the ambient lattice.
For bounded data, every prescribed/tested fixed height has magnitude <=B.
Edges entirely between fixed sites impose only deterministic feasibility
conditions and play no role in the uniform density.

Fix c0,C0>0 and 0<=B<infinity. For EVERY R>=1 and EVERY deterministic real
weight family q of finite support, suppose
  sum_x |q_x| <= C0,
  max_x |q_x| <= C0 R^(-2),
  supp(q) is in a coordinate box Q with diameter <= C0 R.
Diameter may be taken in the ambient l-infinity metric; changing to
Manhattan or Euclidean diameter only changes constants. Assume EITHER
  (Z) sum_x q_x = 0 EXACTLY,
OR
  (P) a nearest-neighbor connected set K of fixed vertices has diameter
      >=c0 R, and K union Q lies in a coordinate box of diameter <=C0 R.

Claim: There exist a>0 and M<infinity depending ONLY on c0,C0,B and the
fixed square lattice, such that
  E_b exp(a |sum_x q_x h(x)|) <= M.
This is uniform over V, component count/shape, R, q, and all positive-volume
bounded pin data. The exponent a can in fact be chosen independently of B;
if (a,M0) work at zero pins, M=exp(a B C0) M0 works at bounded pins.
Mixtures are allowed only if conditioning on the pin values gives exactly
the specified uniform law. Restricting weights is permitted only when
(Z) or (P) and the displayed weight bounds still hold. Renormalizing after
restriction has no automatic guarantee.

This is the square counterpart of the ACTUAL f:averages proposition,
foundations.tex lines 12-44 in the pinned triangular manuscript. That
manuscript supplies a transfer template, not an established whole-proof
premise or a square-field conclusion.

ADMITTED INTERNALLY ESTABLISHED LOCAL PREMISES
1. Square midpoint deficit. Fix even L, 0<delta<=delta0<=1/16,
k0>=4/delta0 and L>=8(k0+2)/delta0. For Q_z=Lz+{0,...,L}^2, F_z(s) is
the logarithm of the full (L-1)^2-dimensional filling volume for shell s.
If a feasible midpoint filling h has edge slack delta throughout Q_z and
|h(x)-h(y)|<=(1-delta0)d1(x,y) for d1(x,y)>=k0, and the endpoint fibers
K_z(s+chi),K_z(s-chi) have positive volume, then
  g_z(s,chi):=F_z(s)-[F_z(s+chi)+F_z(s-chi)]/2
       >= c_* |chi(v)-chi(w)|^2
for every pair of side midpoints v,w, with c_*>0 fixed by the parameters.
No triangular non-tie test is assumed. Origin: cycle10_random_fields_luna/
REPORT.txt; separate finite audit: cycle10_square_block_review/REPORT.txt.
These are internally reconstructed/audited, not externally certified.

arbitrarily small animal parameter by first reducing rho.
