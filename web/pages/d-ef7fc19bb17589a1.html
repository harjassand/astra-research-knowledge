Theorem 1 has a full reduction to quantum random access coding. Its source
laws are P_(i,sigma)(x)=2^(-d)(1+sigma*r*x_i). Relative to uniform cube U,
the likelihood is 1+sigma*r*x_i. For any query POVM output Z_i in {-1,+1},
TV error eps implies signed-mean error at most 2eps. Adding/subtracting the
sigma=+1 and sigma=-1 inequalities yields E_U,S[X_i Z_i]>=1-2eps/r.
Thus the same memory decodes each of d independent uniform bits with average
error at most eps/r. Holevo plus Fano/Nayak gives
log_2 D >= d(1-H_2(eps/r)). This treats a hybrid direct sum as one Hilbert
space of dimension D=sum_j d_j and allows each i its own, mutually
incompatible POVM. Storing the full x is a d-bit exact upper bound.

Theorem 2 uses an exact threshold oracle b_j. Under the null b=0 the
transcript is all zero. Under a single uniformly hidden active mode b=e_J,
the transcript is identical until J is queried. With q queries the hit
probability is at most q/m; equal-prior distinguishing success is at most
1/2+q/(2m), so error delta requires q>=m(1-2delta). The amplitude version
uses 0 versus 2eps and query precision eps/4.
