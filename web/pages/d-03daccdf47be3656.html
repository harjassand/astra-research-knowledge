BOUNDED PRIMARY-SOURCE AUDIT: CRITICAL IID QUTRIT COLLISION MEMORY
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: bounded source reconstruction; no novelty/priority claim and no
independent proof verification of the current internal sandwich/filter notes.

QUESTION AND CONTRACT

For fixed supplied 1/3<a<1/2 and c in a supplied compact positive interval,
let

  p_n=(a+c/(2 sqrt(n)), a-c/(2 sqrt(n)), 1-2a),
  rho_(U,n)=(U diag(p_n) U^dagger)^{tensor n}, U in U(3).

The encoder sees the density operator but not U, a plane, a frame, or a
purification. It must reconstruct the full n-qutrit density with worst-case
half trace error. No entanglement assistance, receiver side information, or
feedback is supplied. A legal archive may retain arbitrary dephased
classical labels y and a conditional quantum register of dimension q_y;
Q=max_y q_y is the branch-dimension objective. All labels and their
representation/precision costs are charged in total hybrid dimension
D=sum_y q_y. Results for D do not imply results for Q when label count is
unbounded. The spectrum, a,c and source law above are supplied; any estimate
of U, of a local reference, or of collision geometry must be acquired from
the input and stored through counted branches. Finite decoder precision,
frame labels, and output-generation costs are separate from ideal channel
theorems.

PRIMARY-SOURCE RECONSTRUCTION

1) Yang–Chiribella–Hayashi, “Optimal Compression for Identically Prepared
Qubit States,” arXiv:1606.02893, submitted 2016-06-09, v3 dated 2016-12-01,
Phys. Rev. Lett. 117, 090502 (2016):
https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.02893

The paper fixes a known qubit state rho=diag(p,1-p), p>=1/2, and lets its
basis vary over SU(2). It uses worst-case trace distance for reconstruction
of rho_g^{tensor n}. For fixed p>1/2, its known-spectrum encoder measures
Schur spin J, discards multiplicity, and stores a cloned/converted spin state
in dimension d_enc=2 J_0+1, J_0=(p-1/2)(n+1); hence d_enc=Theta(n) and
log d_enc=log n+O_p(1). Decoder samples K from the Schur distribution and
reconstructs the full tensor state. The conversion estimate is controlled
by delta=|J-K|/J, with trace-norm error bounded by delta^(1-s)+O(delta)
for fixed s>0. For fixed p, J_0=Theta(n) while typical |J-J_0|=O(sqrt(n)),
so delta->0. Its known-spectrum optimality is a memory-size/Holevo result
for this fixed-p ensemble; it is not a maximum-branch theorem with free
classical labels.

Critical suborbit calculation. On the first-two-eigenvalue U(2) suborbit of
the qutrit family, the exact experiment is variable length: a parameter-
independent flag measurement produces M~Bin(n,2a) qubits and, conditional
on M, the normalized qubit radius is

  r_n = c/(2a sqrt(n)),
  p_qubit = 1/2+r_n/2,
  J_0=(p_qubit-1/2)(M+1) ~= r_n M/2.

On the flagged typical length M=Theta(n), J_0=Theta(sqrt(n)), while spin
fluctuations remain Theta(sqrt(n)); thus |J-J_0|/J_0 is order one on the
typical scale. A conditional fixed-p code can be run for each M, but its
uniform vanishing-error step is missing. The fixed-p conversion estimate therefore does not prove
vanishing error in this triangular sequence. Substituting p_n in the formula
does yield a nominal register dimension Theta(sqrt(n)), but without a
vanishing-error guarantee. The fixed-p Holevo evaluation chi=log n+O_p(1)
is also nonuniform: at critical polarization r_n~n^-1/2 the local orbit
signal n r_n^2 is O(1), so this fixed-spectrum lower cannot be read as a
log n or epsilon-dependent branch lower. The theorem also studies a qubit
orbit; it does not combine the qutrit's simultaneous hot U(3)/U(2) plane
and cold near-degenerate U(2) directions.

2) Yang–Bai–Chiribella–Hayashi, “Compression for Quantum Population
Coding,” arXiv:1701.03372, submitted 2017-01-12, v8 dated 2019-01-26,
IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 64(7), 4766 (2018):
https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03372

Theorem 1 assumes a generic finite-dimensional family rho_theta^{tensor n}
with independent classical and quantum parameters, and uses worst-case
half-trace reconstruction error. Its finite-dimensional setup takes a fixed
full-rank reference spectrum mu_1>...>mu_d>0; its local unitary chart
contains factors 1/sqrt(mu_j-mu_k). For any fixed delta in (0,2/9), the
reported code uses [(1/2+delta) f_c + f_q/2] log n classical bits and
f_q delta log n qubits, with error O(n^{-kappa(delta)})+O(n^{-delta/2}).
Counting qubits as bits, this fixed-delta achievability cost is
[(f_c+f_q)/2+delta(f_c+f_q)] log n; the infimal leading coefficient
(f_c+f_q)/2 is approached as delta decreases, while the error exponent
also depends on delta. The theorem's lower says a memory below
[(f_c+f_q)/2-delta'] log n for fixed delta'>0 cannot be asymptotically
faithful. For a fixed known
generic qutrit spectrum, f_c=0,f_q=6: the stated construction has 3 log n
classical bits and 6 delta log n quantum bits. If interpreted as one
conditional quantum register, that is Q=n^{6 delta}; this is an achievability
allocation, not an optimal Q law.

Theorem 2 states that a family with at least one independent quantum
parameter cannot be asymptotically faithfully compressed using purely
classical memory, regardless of the amount of classical memory. It is a
fixed-family qualitative obstruction, not a quantitative lower for Q>1,
and not a uniform triangular-sequence error bound. The qutrit collision
sequence has a gap p_1-p_2=c/sqrt(n); the reference is not fixed across n,
and the chart factor for this pair diverges as n^(1/4). No theorem constant
or Q-LAN remainder uniform in this closing gap is stated. Even if one
applies the fixed-spectrum result separately at each n, it supplies no
uniform error-vs-Q relation. The classical-only theorem does not exclude a
fixed Q>1 with arbitrarily many labels.

3) Schmidt number and rank-Kraus memory boundary.

Terhal–Horodecki, “A Schmidt number for density matrices,” arXiv:
quant-ph/9911117, submitted 1999-11-29, Phys. Rev. A 61, 040301 (2000):
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9911117

Chruściński–Kossakowski, “On Partially Entanglement Breaking Channels,”
arXiv:quant-ph/0511244, submitted 2005-11-28, Open Syst. Inf. Dyn. 13,
17–26 (2006): https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0511244

For the induced channel Phi=decoder o encoder from the n-qutrit input to
the reconstructed output, a dephased hybrid archive with q_y<=Q has a
Kraus decomposition in which every composite Kraus operator has rank at
most Q: each composite map factors through the branch C^{q_y}. By
vectorization, its normalized Choi state has Schmidt number at most Q.
Conversely, in finite dimensions Schmidt number at most Q is equivalent
to the existence of a Kraus decomposition with all Kraus ranks at most Q;
factor each such Kraus operator through a Q-dimensional branch, store its
Kraus index as a charged classical label, and decode conditionally. Thus
the exact resource translation is

  max quantum branch dimension Q
       <=> induced channel has Choi Schmidt number <= Q
       <=> some Kraus representation has every Kraus rank <= Q.

The Chruściński–Kossakowski Theorem 1 states the channel Schmidt-number /
r-partially-entanglement-breaking equivalence and the Kraus-rank
characterization. The rectangular input/output version needed here follows
from the same Choi vectorization and rank factorization. Public random
mixtures preserve the convex Schmidt-number bound. This converts arbitrary
classical labels into an exact structural constraint but supplies no
distortion-vs-Q rate. A new witness must exploit the actual iid orbit and
trace-error requirement. Choi/Schmidt monotonicity alone cannot establish
Q=Theta(epsilon^-2).

WHAT THE NEARBY CONTROL LEDGER DOES AND DOES NOT SUPPLY

The following internal cards are source-derived/unreviewed records; they
are controls/blockers, not primary-literature priority clearance.

* N07 (Gaussian-memory): one observation X~N(theta,I), fixed bounded mean
  set with affine dimension s, blind encoder/decoder preserving the whole
  experiment in TV, all input-dependent labels charged in total D. It gives
  classical upper and arbitrary quantum/hybrid lower e_D=Theta(D^{-2/s}).
  It is not a Q/branch theorem and its constants are not uniform under
  collapsing mean geometry.
* N08 (block-critical Gaussian): conditional iid/Gaussian bridge error
  eta_n=C_rho0(delta+n^-1/2). In the stated fixed positive critical,
  known-block algebra window, N07 gives the same total-D rate; the qutrit
  affine-span count s=8 yields D=Theta(epsilon^-4) only when the comparison
  remainder is smaller than the target error. This is a total-memory,
  restricted block-family statement. The full unknown U(3) collision
  additionally moves the hot plane/flag and needs a compatible simultaneous
  ground/Fock factorization; the N08 card marks that unrestricted transfer
  open. Neither N07 nor N08 controls max branch Q.
* N21 (tapered support) is a one-real-observation moving-support experiment
  with TV rate D^{-min(gamma+1,2)}. It is a classical rate-distortion
  control, not a quantum state-family or branch-dimension theorem.
* N22 (pure branch memory) does give Q and D laws with unlimited dephased
  labels for a coherent pure local qudit chart. Its lower uses pure-state
  preservation and Schmidt/fidelity arguments; the present qutrit orbit is
  mixed full rank, so the interface does not transfer.
* N23 (rank-boundary Poisson) gives Q=Theta(1+min(n,h_epsilon)) for a
  qubit family including a pure endpoint, and a finite-n O(1/n) comparison
  to Poisson(lambda) x coherent(z). Its total-D lower is open below that
  bridge scale; it does not treat the fixed-temperature mixed qutrit
  collision. Do not transfer its pure/coherent Q law across this boundary.
* N30/N31/N36/N37/N66 are Gaussian, Gaussian-sequence, later-query, and
  regular-sensor information archives. They preserve observation laws or
  selected decisions and charge total D. Their proper-loss, posterior, and
  spectral-volume arguments are useful rate-distortion controls; they do
  not reconstruct rho_U^{tensor n}, do not isolate Q, and require their
  declared observation/sensor model to be supplied.
* N39 is the nearest direct collision control: its source record gives an
  entanglement-breaking (Q=1) reconstruction floor for a full unknown-basis
  moving-spectrum orbit, including arbitrary classical outcomes and
  sector-changing decoders. It is qualitative about the Q>1 gate: an EB
  floor does not imply that Q must grow at any specified rate. The critical
  qubit EB result is likewise Q=1 only.

Consequently, the classical controls answer two separate questions:
(i) on a valid Gaussian comparison, how much total D is needed to match
classical coding, and (ii) where Q=1 stops. Neither replaces the strongest
Q-aware adversary: arbitrary labels with a Q-dimensional quantum branch,
equivalently a Choi-Schmidt-number-Q channel, tested directly on the full
orbit. Charge frame/plane acquisition and label precision before comparing
quantum and classical rates; do not equate number of quantum bits with
maximum branch dimension or total D.

CURRENT INTERNAL CANDIDATE, KEPT SEPARATE FROM PRIOR ART

The supplied internal SANDWICH_WALK_CONVERSE.txt asserts for the full iid
positive-c qutrit orbit, with error epsilon<=1/8 and
log((n+1)/epsilon)<=c*n,

  Q >= c' min{epsilon^-2, n/log((n+1)/epsilon)}.

The separate JOINT_CAPTURE.txt asserts a finite hybrid upper with
Q=O_alpha(epsilon^-2) for epsilon=n^-alpha, 0<alpha<1/2, and labels counted
at O_alpha(n^7 log n); their conjunction gives an internal candidate
Q=Theta(epsilon^-2) in that polynomial window. This is not a conclusion of
the cited fixed-spectrum literature. The global upper below the collision
scale has the extra factor max{1,n^-1/2/epsilon}, while the sandwich lower
saturates at n/log((n+1)/epsilon); the cold flagged-suborbit lower is a
separate Q>=min{C r_n/epsilon,1/(32 r_n)} constraint. No matching joint law
across epsilon~n^-1/2 or below follows from this audit. The cited internal
proofs remain candidate/internal until independent analytic review.

FALSIFIERS / NEXT DECISIVE GATES

1. To upgrade the prior-art transfer claim, produce a cited theorem with
   constants uniform as p_1-p_2=c/sqrt(n) closes, for the full U(3) orbit,
   worst-case half-trace reconstruction, and maximum branch dimension Q
   under arbitrarily many charged classical labels. The inspected 2016 and
   2017 statements do not provide these hypotheses or conclusion.
2. To refute the current internal Q=Omega(epsilon^-2) claim in
   epsilon=n^-alpha, exhibit fixed Q_n=o(epsilon^-2) and an explicit
   encoder/decoder with worst-case error o(n^-alpha), charging labels and
   all acquired nuisance. The EB floor alone is not such a counterexample.
3. To establish a complete rate across the collision threshold, match the
   internal lower to a branch-Q upper below epsilon~n^-1/2 while allowing
   arbitrary sector changes and separate finite label costs.
