# Cycle 2: exact executable conversion of a branching rational target

Status: six exact finite converters, three stronger direct-repair comparators, actual runtime execution, and independent worker audits completed. No universal runtime bound, sharp controlled-target coefficient, or novel architecture benefit is claimed. Cycle 1 artifacts are untouched.

## Interface and complete target policy

Hidden state i in {0,1,2} is fixed. Source action a gives a fresh Bernoulli signal with P_i^a(Y=1)=1/10 if a=i and 9/10 otherwise. Every source observation costs one. The source laws, target laws and every policy branch are supplied. Randomization is state independent, and the runtime policy sees only source samples through an observation callback. Full support and rational probabilities are explicit.

The target starts with the ternary report G_i(x)=4/5 for x=i and 1/10 otherwise. After x, stop with probability s_x, where (s_0,s_1,s_2)=(1/4,1/3,1/2). Otherwise choose target binary action B^x, whose probability of 1 is 3/4 when i=x and 1/4 otherwise. If B^x=1, stop. If B^x=0, choose a DIFFERENT binary action C^(x+1 mod3), with probability of 1 equal to 7/10 when its action index is i, and 3/10 otherwise, then stop. Report the complete sequence of target action names and observed outcomes.

There are 12 positive terminal atoms and horizon at most 3. Every statewise terminal law H_i is enumerated by exact Fraction multiplication. Expected target samples are (469/240,181/96,839/480). The experiments for n=1,2,3 independent repetitions of this whole branching policy report all n terminal blocks, with 12^n atoms, expected cost n times these quantities, and horizon at most 3n. This is a genuine controlled target menu, not just a fixed target channel sampled a fixed number of times; at the block-report level it does define a fixed composite experiment H.

## Exact compiler

Use eta=1/(30n). The initial classifier reads every source action r0 times, classifies its smallest success count, and breaks ties uniformly. r0 is the first integer giving own-state classification error <=eta. Its exact confusion matrix A0 has diagonal

    sum_k P(Bin(r,.1)=k) * [P(Bin(r,.9)>k)^2
         + P(Bin(r,.9)>k)P(Bin(r,.9)=k)
         + P(Bin(r,.9)=k)^2/3].

Off-diagonal entries are half the remaining probability by symmetry. This is count enumeration, not Monte Carlo. Initial r0 is 3,3,4 for n=1,2,3, costing 9,9,12 source observations.

Given classifier output k, propose y from H_k. Pairwise source log likelihoods are integer multiples of log9. For each alternative j, the exact integer success threshold is the smallest b_kj with 9^b >= max(1,H_k(y)/H_j(y))/eta. The reverse threshold L is the smallest integer with 9^L>=1/eta. All threshold comparisons are rational; logarithms and floating-point rounding are absent.

**Candidate-specific verifier.** Always select source action k. Both candidate-versus-alternative scores are the same scalar walk. Let B=max_j b_kj. Stop at +B (accept) or -L (reject). Its upward probability is 9/10 under state k and 1/10 under every other state. This is a finite absorbing Markov chain with B+L-1 transient states. For p not equal to1/2, ratio r=(1-p)/p,

    h(s)=[1-r^(s+L)]/[1-r^(B+L)],
    t(s)=[(B+L)h(s)-(s+L)]/(2p-1).

Every boundary value and every exact harmonic/expected-cost recurrence is verified in Fraction arithmetic. The maximum transient-state count in the run is 9.

**Uniform verifier design.** Draw each source action independently uniformly during VERIFICATION ONLY. The candidate-versus-two-alternative walk is two dimensional. Action k changes both coordinates; an alternative action changes only its associated coordinate. Reject if either coordinate <=-L; accept if both reach their own success threshold. After M=48 observations, reject any still-live path as a timeout. Integer path weights at layer t share denominator30^t: each action/observation transition contributes numerator1 or9. The finite forward calculation records acceptance, rejection, timeout, and the expected verification cost as the sum of live mass before each step.

The uncapped simultaneous-pass lattice is not automatically finite: one coordinate can make arbitrarily high excursions while the other remains below its threshold. The timeout is an explicit implementation choice, not silently discarded probability. `fixed_uniform` in filenames names this VERIFIER design only. Both its initial classifier and its residual tail use deterministic balanced action batches. It is not an overall frozen-IID source converter, so Cycle 1's fixed-IID lower bounds do not apply to these finite costs. This uniform choice is not claimed optimized over all fixed verifier mixtures.

Thin every accepted proposal independently by a=(1-eta)/(1+2eta). Compute its accepted mass exactly:

    Q_i(y)=a sum_k A0_i(k) H_k(y) p_i(accept | k,y).

The thresholds and change-of-measure inequality imply Q_i(y)<=(1-eta)H_i(y); this is also checked entry by entry. Let delta_i=1-sum_yQ_i(y), R_i(y)=(H_i(y)-Q_i(y))/delta_i. Each R_i is a strictly positive rational probability vector. Every timeout contributes to delta_i. The truncated uniform verifier is NOT claimed to satisfy the untruncated lemma's own-state false-rejection or O(eta) missing-mass bound.

For exact repair, increase balanced classifier length r until K=A_r^-1 R is entrywise nonnegative. Every rejected shorter length and its negative minimum entry is retained. Row sums are one by A_r 1=1 and R1=1. On a rejection or thinning flag, use FRESH balanced samples, classify as k, and draw the output from K_k. A_rK=R and Q_i+delta_iR_i=H_i hold as exact rational equalities. All tested kernels are strictly positive. The chosen repair lengths are r=2,5,8 in both verifier modes.

The exact total expected source cost in state i is

    3r0 + sum_{k,y} A0_i(k)H_k(y) E_i verifier(k,y) + delta_i*3r.

Classifier, failed verifier, timeout and repair samples all count. No observation is free. Compile states, Fraction bit sizes, operation counts, CPU/wall time and process RSS are reported separately; they are not folded into a false universal sample-cost claim.

## Competent comparators and decisive finite result

A stronger direct baseline skips proposals/verifiers and solves K=A_r^-1 H directly. Search preserves the negative entries at every earlier balanced classifier length. The first feasible lengths are r=2,5,8, giving exact sample costs6,15,24 in every state. These are optimal lengths within this particular balanced-classifier inversion construction; not optimal over all exact simulators.

Candidate-specific verification beats the uniform-verifier construction in every state at every tested n, but LOSES to the stronger direct baseline at n=1 and2. It beats both at n=3. EVERY tested converter costs much more than directly taking target measurements. These small exact implementations therefore do not yet attain the theorem's eventual same-budget conclusion; they do not disprove that asymptotic statement either. The initial classification and residual machinery dominate at these horizons.

At n=3, candidate-route costs are 19.5104, 19.4349, 19.2976 versus target costs 5.8625, 5.65625, 5.24375. Uniform-verifier timeout probabilities are 3.07e-6, 2.76e-6, 2.25e-6 and are included exactly in the residual. Tiny timeouts do not account for the larger overall sample overhead.

## Computation, execution and independent checks

Reproduce:

    python3 work/d_example/cycle2/exact_branching_converter.py --max-blocks 3 --mixture-horizon 48
    python3 work/d_example/cycle2/verify.py
    python3 work/d_example/cycle2/runtime.py

Six full certificates are in results/d_example/cycle2. The own verifier independently reconstructs the target tree, verifies nonnegative repair kernels and final laws, checks every cost decomposition and the direct baselines, compares nine depth-4 exhaustive likelihood paths with the aggregated DP, and checks transition signs directly from source probability ratios: 11,352 checks passed.

The independent `check_selection` worker rebuilt 11,304 state/output entries and enumerated actual likelihood-ratio paths through depth 6 (28,531 nodes per truth), matching pass/reject/timeout/cost exactly. Its records are results/check_selection/cycle2/branching_audit.json. Independently, `r11_logic` recomputed the balanced classifier count distribution and exact inverse kernels, matching all six residual kernels at r=2,5,8, with separate count-distribution recurrence checks: results/r11_logic/cycle2/checks_summary.json. These are independent internal reconstructions, not external validation.

The runtime converter consumes only an observation callback, uses exact integer draws for every rational proposal/thinning/repair law, and executes 24,000 seeded trials (one and three blocks), charging 391,859 source observations. The output counts and every trial's source action counts are saved. Empirical mean costs agree with the exact expectations at this diagnostic scale. Empirical output TV for n=3 is around .25 because 1,000 draws cover a 1728-atom report; it is not claimed validation of exact equality. The algebra supplies equality. Randomizer denominator bit lengths reach 507 for candidate repair and 957 for uniform-verifier repair at n=3, so the free-randomization sample model is separated from actual integer computation.

Compiler maximum-live verifier states are 831 for uniform design, with 75 cached parameter profiles and 913,761 total layered states across profiles; largest integer path weight uses 214 bits. At n=3 the residual kernel numerator/denominator bit sizes are 498/506 for candidate routing versus 949/953 for uniform verification. Full H has 5,184 rational entries. Candidate n=3 compilation took about 3.1 seconds; uniform about 1.64 seconds with shared cached verifier profiles. These times are not a clean paired algorithm-speed comparison. Cumulative process peak RSS was about 46.6 MB. Each n=3 repair search performs 373,248 matrix Fraction multiplications in addition to classification/proposal/verifier calculations. All exact fractions, rejected repair lengths and these measurements are retained.

Current enumeration grows as 12^n; n=6 has 2,985,984 report atoms and n=10 has 61,917,364,224. This is a limitation of this explicit-report compiler, NOT a necessary lower bound: repeated blocks can be compressed to histogram counts and a uniform output ordering reconstructed afterward. That promising exact compression is not implemented here. For general rational source laws whose likelihood increments are not commensurate, the scalar finite-lattice shortcut also disappears; bounded-horizon verification plus exact residual inversion still gives a finite construction but may incur much greater time/bit cost.

One independent-test development failure is retained: the initial depth4 exhaustive checker used1-path_probability instead of1-outcome_probability on a zero observation, creating mass greater than one. Its assertion failed; the test complement was corrected, then all checks passed. The main integer-weight verifier was unchanged.

## Novelty, obstruction and next falsifiable step

The source family and adaptive testing policy are classical. The exact residual primitive follows the supplied checker and the independently reconstructed controlled-source finite-output construction; internal input snapshots and hashes are saved under sources/d_example/cycle2. This work fills part of the executable finite-rational implementation gap. It does not establish novelty of the broader theorem, uniform polynomial compilation, optimal controlled-target menu rates, or acquisition of unknown likelihoods.

The decisive practical obstruction in this fixture is that existence of a low first-order sample coefficient does not remove classifier/repair cost or explicit report compilation. The next falsifiable step is histogram compression of repeated block reports, followed by exact replication at larger n with all compiler bits and costs charged. Compare against the direct exact-repair baseline, optimized candidate-independent verifier mixtures, and target measurement cost. A successful larger-horizon sample saving is still a known-model statistical conversion result, not discovery of a scientific model or intervention.
