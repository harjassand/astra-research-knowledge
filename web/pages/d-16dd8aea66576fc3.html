STRONGEST NEW IMPLEMENTED INTERFACE

Input: explicit F in Q(i)^(n x n), binary encoding L, hard pair sector k,
arbitrary occupation-prefix forced bits. The algorithm scans exact support,
checks a supplied/input ordering's frontier, or uses explicit bounded
balanced-separator enumeration to acquire an ordering. It never calls a
minimum-width or hole-balancing oracle.

For ordering frontier v, norm/prefix counts use
 O(n^2(v+1)16^(v+1)) rational operations and
 O(n16^(v+1)) stored coefficients,
with O(n(L+log n)) coefficient bits. Count every canonical sector directly;
zero is exact. A density matrix on at most v+1 active sites propagates the
finite commuting pair-creation product; local hard/prefix projection and
charge-tagged partial trace preserve holes and interference exactly.

Fixed separator budget q has conservative acquisition O(n^(q+3)), and
produces v<=q*(floor(log_2 n)+1). The resulting count bound is the deliberately
loose O(n^(4q+2) q log n) rational operations plus polynomial bit arithmetic.
Parameter growth is charged; this does not claim a practical exponent.

Exact child norm ratios acquire supported prefixes. A total capped sampler
uses at most 4n+1 exact count queries, O(log(n/eps)) rational rejection attempts
per occupation bit, polynomial bits per rational threshold, and a supported
deterministic fallback on coin-cap failure. Under INDEPENDENT FAIR bits,
coupling proves Born-law TV error <=eps. Its every-execution rational-operation
cost is O(n^3(v+1)16^(v+1)) plus the charged random-bit arithmetic. Seeded PRNG
executions are finite diagnostics rather than certification of fair coins.

The exact counter also supports standard reversible conditional rotations
and exact determinant phase recovery with deterministic polynomial-bit
budgets and no inverse norm. That circuit existence consequence is derived
in the proof. Reversible arithmetic, phase/rotation gate synthesis and a
quantum circuit were NOT implemented or run here.
