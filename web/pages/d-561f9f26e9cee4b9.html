COVARIANT OBSERVABLE FRAMES: EXACT FAMILY ELIMINATIONS AND FIRST PROFILE GATE
2026-10-09. Independent reconstruction by covariant_obstruction_sol.
Universal finite C and general sharp C=2 remain UNKNOWN.

RESULTS FROZEN IN THIS REPORT
1. For every spin j>0, the maximal-output-spin broadcaster has a single
   coherent-orbit canonical EB comparator satisfying full-domain C=2.
   This is an exact analytic theorem for this channel family, and rank one
   saturates it. It does not bound arbitrary covariant channels componentwise.
2. Every legal SU(2)-covariant spin-3/2 channel has an explicit full-domain
   C=2 comparator from a mixture of coherent and extreme-cat orbits.
   This eliminates the entire covariant channel family in dimension four.
3. The same two-orbit comparator hull fails at spin 5/2 against an exact,
   HS-positive legal channel. The actual unrestricted rank-four support is
   exactly 2; adding an explicitly given third orbit repairs this witness.
   This is a failed-generalization certificate, not a counterexample to C=2.
4. For half-integer j, the highest multipole has exact support k=(2j+1)/2.
   Its exact parameterized star blocks are given below. A fixed deterministic
   diagnostic of those blocks did not find a sharp-two falsifier; no
   asymptotic/all-spin conclusion is inferred from those finite eigenvalues.

I. EXACT CONTRACT AND COVARIANT REDUCTION

Let H_j be the irreducible spin-j representation, d=2j+1, tau=Tr/d.
Use a tau-orthonormal Hermitian basis T_{l,a} of each irreducible tensor
operator subspace H_l, l=0,...,2j, with T_{0,0}=I. Thus
  tau(T_{l,a}T_{l',a'})=delta_{ll'}delta_{aa'}.
For a pure state pi=|psi><psi| define
  r_l(psi)=sum_a [Tr(pi T_{l,a})]^2.
Then r_0=1 and sum_{l>=1} r_l=d-1.

For an invariant frame Q=sum_{l>=1} w_l P_l, w_l>=0, exactly
  V(Q)=sum_l (2l+1)w_l,
  k(Q)=max_{||psi||=1} sum_l w_l r_l(psi).               (1)
The unrestricted support in (1) is exact: its upper bound holds for each
pure seed and hence every ensemble; the SU(2) orbit of an optimizing seed
has barycenter I/d by irreducibility and attains the same score. It is not
a finite chosen-seed or PPT relaxation. First/second moment Caratheodory
replaces every such orbit by a finite ensemble with the same canonical
channel, with at most 1+h+h(h+1)/2 points, h=d^2-1. This is existence,
not a uniform efficient finite-bit acquisition guarantee.

Any TP channel covariant from H_j to itself is unital. Since its operator
representation is multiplicity free and has Hermitian real tensor bases,
it acts by real scalars lambda_l on H_l, hence is HS-self-adjoint. If it
is self-compatible, average a supplied joint Choi state over SU(2) and
the output swap. The result still supplies the same marginal channel.

Let Y|j,m>=(-1)^{j-m}|j,-m>, Theta=Y conjugation, so
  Y U_j(g)^* Y^*=U_j(g),
  Y T_{l,a}^T Y^*=(-1)^l T_{l,a}.
Convert the Choi input to an ordinary spin-j register with Y. The star
operator for rank l becomes
  W_l=(-1)^l [K_l(A,B)+K_l(A,C)],
  K_l(A,B)=sum_a T_{l,a}^A T_{l,a}^B.                  (2)
On pair total spin L, K_l has exact eigenvalue
  kappa_l(L)=d(2l+1)(-1)^{2j+L}{j j l; j j L}.         (3)
Braces denote the ordinary real Wigner 6j symbol.

Couple BC first to L, then A with BC to total T. Allowed L are
  |T-j|<=L<=min(2j,T+j), L integer.
The recoupling matrix, up to an irrelevant common phase, is
  U_{LK}=sqrt((2L+1)(2K+1)){j j K; j T L}.
Put D_l=U diag[(-1)^l kappa_l(K)] U^t. Output-swap parity is
(-1)^{2j-L}. Therefore the exact star block on either parity is
  W_l^{T,parity}=2 (D_l restricted to that parity).    (4)
Equation (4) parameterizes all covariant broadcaster optimization; it
does not replace legal tracial constraints by an unjustified norm bound.
Here the norm bound is exact: every invariant state has three single-
register marginals I/d. A top eigenvector xi in a fixed T and parity block
gives the invariant density matrix
  tilde R=(1/(2T+1)) sum_{M=-T}^T |T,M;xi><T,M;xi|,
and R=(Y^* tensor I tensor I) tilde R (Y tensor I tensor I).
The broadcaster is exactly
  B_R(X)=d Tr_A[(X^T tensor I_BC)R].                  (5)
R>=0, Tr R=1, Tr_BC R=I/d imply CPTP; output symmetry gives equal
marginals; output single-register traces I/d give unitality. Its marginal
is covariant and hence HS-self-adjoint. In particular
  lambda_l=Tr(tilde R W_l)/(2(2l+1)).                 (6)

For a covariant Phi, V and s_Phi are unchanged on replacing any positive
frame Q by its SU(2) average bar Q. Since k is a convex invariant support
function, k(bar Q)<=k(Q). Proving G2 on invariant frames therefore proves
G2 on every frame for a covariant marginal Phi. The final single channel
comparator is explicit in the results below, not merely a directionwise
choice of measurement.

II. ALL-j MAXIMAL-OUTPUT-SPIN CLONER: EXACT FULL-DOMAIN C=2

Let P_{2j} be the maximal-total-spin projection in H_j tensor H_j.
Define the supplied broadcaster
  B_j(X)=d/(4j+1) P_{2j}(X tensor I)P_{2j}.           (7)
Compression and identity adjoining are CP. Irreducibility gives
Tr_C P_{2j}=(4j+1)I/d, proving TP. The maximal-spin subspace is symmetric
under output swap, so the two marginals coincide. Covariance gives the
unital and HS-self-adjoint conditions. This is the N=2j to M=4j qubit
Werner symmetric cloner viewed as one spin-j input and two spin-j outputs.

The coherent-orbit canonical EB map is
  Psi_j(X)=d int_{SU(2)} Tr(pi_g X) pi_g dg,
  pi_g=U_j(g)|j,j><j,j|U_j(g)^*.
Its rank-l eigenvalue is
  c_l(j)=(2j)!(2j+1)!/[(2j-l)!(2j+l+1)!],             (8)
with c_0=1. The exact marginal eigenvalues of (7) are
  lambda_l(Phi_j)=c_l(j)/c_l(2j).                    (9)

Independent derivation of (9): identify the maximal output subspace with
H_{2j}; let D:H_{2j} states -> H_j states be partial trace after embedding.
Then Phi_j=(d/(4j+1)) D D^*. In tau-normal tensor bases, D^*(T_l^j)=b_l
T_l^{2j}; D(T_l^{2j})=((4j+1)/d)b_l T_l^j, so lambda_l=b_l^2.
At the highest-weight vector, the embedding is |2j,2j> -> |j,j>|j,j>,
giving b_l=<j,j|T_l^j|j,j>/<2j,2j|T_l^{2j}|2j,2j>. The squared zonal
expectation is (2l+1)c_l, yielding (9). Formula (8) can also be derived
directly by expanding the coherent overlap ((1+x)/2)^{2j} in Legendre
polynomials and integrating by parts with Rodrigues' formula.

Proof of the componentwise C2 inequality. Set n=2j and
  f_l(n)=1/c_l(n/2)=prod_{r=0}^{l-1}(n+r+2)/(n-r)
        =prod_r(1+x_r), x_r=2(r+1)/(n-r)>0.
For l<=n, x_r at 2n is <=x_r/2. Expand the positive product by subsets:
  f_l(2n)-1<=prod_r(1+x_r/2)-1
           <=(prod_r(1+x_r)-1)/2.
The second inequality uses 2^{-|S|}<=1/2 for every nonempty subset S.
Consequently f_l(2n)<=(1+f_l(n))/2, or
  lambda_l=c_l(n/2)/c_l(n)<=(1+c_l(n/2))/2.           (10)
Thus Psi_j>=2Phi_j-I as an HS operator on the full Hermitian space, i.e.
  I-Psi_j<=2(I-Phi_j),                               (11)
for every observable simultaneously. Equality occurs at l=1; l>=2 is
strict. No Clifford algebra or numerical premise is used in this proof.

For A_i=J_i, i=x,y,z, exactly V=j(j+1), k=j^2 (maximum spin length j,
coherent orbit attains it). Equation (9) gives lambda_1=(2j+1)/(2j+2),
so V-s=j/2 and (V-k)/(V-s)=2 for every j>0. Alternatively the exact
star -J_A dot(J_B+J_C) has top eigenvalue j(2j+1), attained at BC spin
2j and total spin j. This supplies a legal normalized projector Choi
state via (5), and saturates V+k. It eliminates this whole linear-spin
attack, including the large-j slow-mode regime lambda_1 ->1.

Asymptotics for (7), not for arbitrary covariant channels. Write L=l(l+1).
At fixed l as n=2j ->infinity,
  c_l(j)=1-L/n+(L+L^2/2)/n^2+O(n^-3),
  lambda_l=1-L/(2n)+(3L/4+L^2/8)/n^2+O(n^-3),
  c_l(j)-(2lambda_l-1)=L(L-2)/(4n^2)+O(n^-3).
For l/sqrt(n)->alpha, c_l(j)->exp(-alpha^2) and
lambda_l->exp(-alpha^2/2); the coherent-support deficit ratio tends to
1+exp(-alpha^2/2)<=2, and unrestricted support can only reduce it.
At l=n, lambda_n is exponentially small, asymptotic to
(3sqrt(3pi n)/8)(27/64)^n. These formulas do not assert that coherent
states optimize the classical support for arbitrary higher-harmonic frames.

III. PURE-STATE TIME-REVERSAL SUPPORT IDENTITIES

Vectorize pi into |psi> tensor Theta|psi>. The rank-l multipole satisfies
  r_l(psi)=d ||P_l(|psi> tensor Theta|psi>)||^2.        (12)
For half-integer j, Theta^2=-I, hence <psi|Theta psi>=0. Its symmetric-
pair probability is 1/2. Pair-spin swap parity is (-1)^{2j-l}; hence
  sum_{l odd}r_l=d/2,
  sum_{l even,l>0}r_l=d/2-1.                         (13)
For the highest rank n=2j odd, (13) gives r_n<=d/2. The extreme cat
  |cat>=(|j,j>+|j,-j>)/sqrt(2)
has r_n=d/2: the two equal-extreme-spin components contribute 1/2, and
the opposite-extreme-spin part of |cat> tensor Theta|cat> is antisymmetric,
orthogonal to maximal total spin. Its orbit therefore proves exactly
  k(P_{2j})=d/2, V(P_{2j})=4j+1.                    (14)

More generally, the full odd and even traceless frames have support
d/2 and d/2-1, respectively. Their star bounds are exact in every even
dimension d, and are useful without re-testing Clifford variants.
Let F be register swap and P the antisymmetric maximally entangled
spin-singlet projector. Completeness gives
  K_all=dF, sum_l(-1)^l K_l=d^2 P.
Therefore
  W_odd=[d^2(P_AB+P_AC)-d(F_AB+F_AC)]/2,
  W_even0=[d^2(P_AB+P_AC)+d(F_AB+F_AC)]/2-2I.
Their top eigenvalues are respectively
  d(d+2)/2 = V_odd+k_odd,
  d^2/2-2 = V_even0+k_even0  (d>=4).                 (15)

Proof of (15): for each vector v use u=S_AB tensor v_C,
v'=S_AC tensor v_B, w=v_A tensor S_BC. Their overlaps are 1/d,-1/d,1/d.
On u+v', P_AB+P_AC has eigenvalue 1+1/d and F_AB+F_AC eigenvalue -1,
giving the values in (15). Put x=u-v'. On span{x,w}, W_odd has matrix
[[d^2/2,0],[d,0]], with eigenvalues d^2/2 and 0. W_even0 has matrix
[[d^2/2-d-2,-d],[-d,-2]]. Its top eigenvalue is <=d^2/2-2 because at
t=d^2/2-2 the characteristic product is
(t-(d^2/2-d-2))(t+2)-d^2=d^2(d/2-1)>=0, and t exceeds both diagonal
entries. On the orthogonal complement, P_AB+P_AC=0 and swap-sum norm<=2;
the remaining tops are <=d and <=d-2. These are bounded by (15). The
d=2 even traceless subspace is zero. All vector subspaces above and their
orthogonal complements are invariant under the two star operators.

Consequently every self-compatible channel covariant under the compact
symplectic group USp(d) has both nontrivial harmonic-sector eigenvalues
<= (d+2)/(2(d+1)); the uniform-pure-state canonical EB channel has
eigenvalue 1/(d+1) on both sectors and gives full-domain C=2. This is a
second multiplicity-free family elimination; it does not imply the
individual SU(2) harmonic bounds, since USp(d) merges many harmonics.

IV. ALL LEGAL SPIN-3/2 COVARIANT CHANNELS: EXPLICIT C2 COMPARATOR

For j=3/2, (13) gives r_2=1 and r_1+r_3=2. Since
  r_1=3|<J>|^2/[j(j+1)],
exactly 0<=r_1<=9/5. The coherent seed attains 9/5; the extreme cat
attains 0. Thus for all w_1,w_2,w_3>=0,
  k=w_2+max{(9/5)w_1+(1/5)w_3, 2w_3}.              (16)
These are unrestricted upper bounds and reference-preserving orbit
attainers, not assumptions about a sampled hull.

For every legal covariant marginal, exact star inequalities give
  lambda_1<=4/5                         (linear-spin star),
  lambda_2<=3/5                         (even-frame (15)),
  3lambda_1+7lambda_3<=6                (odd-frame (15)),
  lambda_3<9/14.                        (rank-three blocks below)
For the last inequality the exact global top is
  mu_3=(6+2sqrt(345))/5<9,
so lambda_3<=mu_3/14=(3+sqrt(345))/35<9/14.
Every block appears in highest_harmonic_exact_small.json. The only
block needing the top radical is total T=3/2, BC labels [1,3]:
  [[0,8sqrt(21)/5],[8sqrt(21)/5,12/5]].
Other block tops are -28/5,0,28/5,-12/5,
(-9+sqrt(305))/5,2/5,2,-2/5; all are smaller than mu_3.
The strict margin V+k-mu_3=(39-2sqrt(345))/5 is positive because
39^2>4*345. The optimizing legal Choi projector uses T=3/2 and a
multiplicity vector proportional to (8sqrt(21)/5,mu_3). Its full spectrum is
  lambda_1=7/15+1/sqrt(345),
  lambda_2=2/5+6sqrt(345)/575,
  lambda_3=(3+sqrt(345))/35,
all positive. This exact channel gives ratio 10/(14-mu_3)<2 for Q=P_3.

Set t=max{0,(5/3)(2lambda_1-1)}. The first bound gives 0<=t<=1. The
last two bounds give
  t<=5-(70/9)lambda_3.
Let Psi_t=t Psi_coh+(1-t)Psi_cat. Its eigenvalues are
  (3t/5, 1/5, 2/7-9t/35).
These dominate (2lambda_1-1,2lambda_2-1,2lambda_3-1) respectively.
Thus Psi_t>=2Phi-I on the entire Hermitian domain, establishing C=2
with one explicit legal canonical comparator for every such Phi.

V. EXACT SPIN-5/2 FAILURE OF THE TWO-ORBIT LIFT; ACTUAL SUPPORT REPAIR

This section falsifies only the proposal that the same coherent/cat
mixture works in all spin dimensions. It does not falsify G1/G2.

Take j=5/2, d=6, total T=5/2, and BC vector
  xi=(|L=3>+|L=5>)/sqrt(2).
Both BC labels have positive output-swap parity, so (5) constructs an
exact legal broadcaster. Its full marginal spectrum is
  lambda_1=3/5,
  lambda_2=31/100+5sqrt(77)/588,
  lambda_3=31/105+25sqrt(77)/882,
  lambda_4=1/7+13sqrt(77)/294,
  lambda_5=109/924+13sqrt(77)/396.
All are positive, so this witness also obeys the positive-HS subclass.
The rank-four star block on BC [3,5] is
  [[33/14,39sqrt(77)/49],[39sqrt(77)/49,39/14]].
Its expectation on xi is 18/7+39sqrt(77)/49.

Both coherent and extreme-cat seeds have the same even-rank profile:
  r_2=25/14, r_4=3/14,
hence every mixture has canonical lambda_4=1/42. C2 domination on rank
four would require star <=9+3/14=129/14. Instead the exact margin is
  18/7+39sqrt(77)/49-129/14
    =39sqrt(77)/49-93/14>0.                           (17)
For example sqrt(77)>17/2 proves (17) immediately. The corresponding
channel lambda_4 is approximately 0.530866, whereas the maximal-output
cloner has lambda_4=13/84; componentwise domination by that cloner is
therefore also exactly false. Even its lambda_1 is 3/5, while maximal-
output lambda_1=6/7, so the profile tradeoff matters.

Unrestricted support is larger. By (13), r_2+r_4=2 for every pure state,
so r_4<=2. The explicitly normalized seed
  |eta>=(2|5/2,5/2>+sqrt(5)|5/2,-1/2>)/3
has all quadrupole expectations zero: its populated magnetic labels
differ by 3, eliminating rank-two off-diagonal expectations, and
<J_z^2>= (4/9)(25/4)+(5/9)(1/4)=35/12=j(j+1)/3.
Thus r_2=0, r_4=2. Its orbit proves exactly
  k(P_4)=2, V(P_4)=9.                               (18)
The witness in (17) has ratio 14/[18-(18/7+39sqrt(77)/49)]<2. An explicit
full-domain repair is Psi=(Psi_coh+Psi_eta)/2. Eta's harmonic covariance
profile r_l is
  (r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4,r_5)=(5/21,0,40/27,2,242/189),
so its canonical eigenvalues are
  (5/63,0,40/189,2/9,22/189).
The half-mixture with coherent eigenvalues
  (5/7,5/14,5/42,1/42,1/462)
dominates 2lambda_l-1 in every sector (sqrt(77)<9 suffices to verify
all inequalities). Therefore this concrete witness has a single repaired
canonical C2 comparator on all observables, and is not a global failure.

VI. PRECISE REMAINING ARBITRARY-SU(2)-CHANNEL GATE

For a fixed j define the actual compact orbit-profile hull
  K_j=conv{(r_1(psi),...,r_{2j}(psi)):||psi||=1}.
All SU(2)-averaged canonical comparators have eigenvalues
  (r_1/3,r_2/5,...,r_{2j}/(4j+1)), r in K_j.
The exact full-domain C2 question for a legal covariant channel profile
lambda is whether there exists ONE r in K_j with
  r_l>=(2l+1)(2lambda_l-1) for every l.               (19)
This follows either directly by covariance domination or from support
separation. Covariant joint Choi profiles are exactly generated by PSD
multiplicity blocks in (4)-(6), with both output parities available.
The spin-3/2 proof completely characterizes K_j. The spin-5/2 witness
proves that replacing K_j by coherent/cat hull is invalid, while (18)
shows why rejecting a restricted hull cannot be promoted to a falsifier.

An additional exact algebraic profile reduction is available for every j.
Let q_L=||P_L(psi tensor psi)||^2. Only L with (-1)^{2j-L}=+1 occur, and
sum q_L=1. Equations (3) give
  r_l=d(2l+1) sum_{L=symmetric} {j j l;j j L} q_L.    (20)
Thus the orbit-profile hull is an affine image of the convex hull of
attainable symmetric-pair spin probabilities, not the whole probability
simplex. At spin 5/2, q has labels 1,3,5, so K_j lies in an affine plane.
The exact coefficient table is j_five_halves_fierz_table.json; negative
coefficients in that table rule out treating every probability vector as
a realizable pure seed. Characterizing the true plane region or proving
(19) by another construction is the first remaining substantive gate.
No all-j finite-C theorem or exact >2 witness has been established here.

VII. TARGETED DIAGNOSTICS AND FAILURES

targeted_spectrum.py forms all 6j coefficients and matrix entries exactly
in SymPy, then uses NumPy eigvalsh only for reported diagnostics. The
highest-harmonic n=2j sequence tested was 1,3,5,7,9,11,13,17,21,25.
The exact k is (n+1)/2 in every row. Numerical maximizing ratio falls
from 2 at n=1 to about 1.63023 at n=25. Numerical winners lie at total
spin T=j, odd BC labels. This does not prove the winner location, a limit,
the all-spin C2 inequality, or the absence of another invariant frame.
Only n=1,3 were symbolically certified here. No random SDP was run.

direct_choi_replay.py independently builds the two legal projector states
from Clebsch-Gordan vectors in the full tensor-product basis, without
using the Racah block formula. It checks the full Choi traces, output
equality, unitality, HS-self-adjointness and all harmonic eigenvalues.
Both cases pass with residuals below 6e-16; tiny negative eigenvalues of
order 1e-16 are floating-point roundoff, not legality certificates. This
replay checks conventions; algebraic legality follows from (5).

Additional n=4,5 rank-two blocks were computed to test the specific
fixed-even-profile obligation; their numerical tops 6.11511 and 6.37663
did not defeat the coherent/cat rank-two support. They are diagnostics,
not a broader theorem. The n=5 rank-four top diagnostic 10.57167 is
likewise not needed for (17), which uses an exact two-dimensional block
and a fixed exact vector. The exact upper support (18) does not rely on
any floating point optimization.

Failed lifts preserved: (a) maximal-output cloner componentwise dominates
all legal covariant channels; the rank-four channel above refutes this.
(b) coherent/cat mixtures suffice in all half-integer spins; (17) refutes
it. (c) every symmetric-pair probability vector is a legal pure seed;
the negative r_l coefficients in (20) refute it. These are failed proof
mechanisms, not failed C2 or finite-C targets.
