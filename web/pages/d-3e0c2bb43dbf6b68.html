# Deeper attack: total versus per-coordinate information

This revision is independent of all peer outputs. It sharpens the scope boundary in `INITIAL.txt`.

## Exact counterexample to an unnormalized operator-norm bound

Fix a noise variance \(s>0\). For every dimension \(d\), let

\[
X=(X_1,\dots,X_d),\qquad X_i\stackrel{ind}{\sim}\operatorname{Unif}[-1,1],
\]

and observe the Gaussian heat channel

\[
Y_i=X_i+\sqrt{s}\,G_i,\qquad G_i\stackrel{ind}{\sim}\mathcal N(0,1).
\]

The prior is centered, compactly supported, and log-concave, with \(\operatorname{Cov}(X)=I_d/3\), so \(\|\operatorname{Cov}(X)\|_{op}=1/3\) for every \(d\). Define the posterior covariance field

\[
A(Y)=\operatorname{Cov}(X\mid Y)=\operatorname{diag}(c_s(Y_1),\ldots,c_s(Y_d)),
\quad c_s(y)=\operatorname{Var}(X_1\mid X_1+\sqrt{s}G_1=y).
\]

Use \(f(u)=u/(1+u)\), so \(J=\int_0^\infty f(u)^2u^{-2}du=1\), and set

\[
Z_T\mid Y\sim\mathcal N(0,I_d+f(TA(Y))).
\]

For one coordinate, the posterior density is the exponential tilt

\[
p(x\mid y)\propto\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}(x)\exp\!\left(-\frac{x^2}{2s}+\frac{yx}{s}\right).
\]

Its variance \(c_s(y)\) is positive at finite (y), continuous, and tends to zero as \(|y|\to\infty\), since the tilt concentrates at an endpoint. Hence \(c_s(Y_1)\) is nonconstant. Since (f) is strictly increasing, the conditional laws \(Z_T\mid Y_1=y\) have different variances on sets of positive probability for every (T>0). Thus

\[
I(Y_1;Z_T)>0\qquad(T>0).
\]

The coordinates and their channel noises are independent, so mutual information tensorizes exactly:

\[
I(Y;Z_T)=d\,I(Y_1;Z_T).
\]

Consequently the integrated total information also equals (d) times a strictly positive one-coordinate integral. It is finite by the proved bound (1), which gives

\[
\int_0^\infty I(Y;Z_T)\frac{dT}{T^2}
\le\frac14\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}A(Y)
\le\frac d{12}.
\]

The left side therefore grows linearly in (d), while \(\|\operatorname{Cov}(X)\|_{op}=1/3\) stays fixed. No dimension-free bound on **total** scale-integrated information can follow from covariance operator norm alone, even for a compact log-concave prior and its Gaussian heat observation, under this channel construction.

## What survives

The normalized information does obey the dimension-free estimate

\[
\frac1d\int_0^\infty I(Y;Z_T)\frac{dT}{T^2}\le\frac1{12}.
\]

More generally, the correct unnormalized quantity is the posterior trace \(\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}\operatorname{Cov}(X\mid Y)\), not only its largest eigenvalue. A spectral sensing map (B) replaces this trace by \(\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}(B\operatorname{Cov}(X\mid Y)B^*)\), with the covariance bound charged as \(\|\operatorname{Cov}(X)\|_{op}\operatorname{Tr}(B^*B)\).

This example is a boundary for any proposed N75 transfer that asks for an unnormalized, dimension-free mutual-information bound using covariance operator norm alone. It does not refute an N75 estimate with per-coordinate normalization, a finite-trace spectral weight, or additional structure on the field (R(Y)). The missing U note is needed to determine which target is intended.

## Status and reproducibility

This is an analytic exact construction; no numerical run is needed. The positive-integral claim follows because (I(Y_1;Z_T)>0) for every (T>0), the channel depends continuously on (T), and the integrated upper bound is finite. The posterior channel is exactly Gaussian heat, and the prior meets the compact log-concave hypotheses. No external theorem beyond the Gaussian posterior formula and the entropy bound already proved is required.
