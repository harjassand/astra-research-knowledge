Q05 exact restricted-family EB search: solved exact rational LPs for n=1,2,3,4,5,6,8 on the critical grid 0.49≤r√n≤1, with physical r≤1/2 and target signals 0.5 or 1. Exact selected r fractions and full outputs are in the work packet. Every reported point has an exact primal/dual certificate; solver tolerance is zero. n=8,r√n≈1 was not completed and is omitted.

The channel measures total spin and spin weight along an input-independent shared Haar-random axis, then prepares arbitrary mixtures of all output spin sectors/weights. Thus sector changes are included. The exact optimum is only within this restricted measure/prepare family; general covariant POVM seeds and arbitrary coherent prepared states are not optimized. The result is an upper bound on unrestricted EB error, not its optimum.
