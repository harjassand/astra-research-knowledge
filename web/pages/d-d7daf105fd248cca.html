EXACT STRONGEST CLAIM
For every d>=2, let Phi:M_d(C)->M_d(C) be UCP, normalized-trace preserving,
positive on the Hilbert-Schmidt space, selfcompatible, and covariant under
conjugation by EVERY real orthogonal d-by-d matrix. Then there is an explicit
positive selfadjoint bistochastic canonical EB channel Psi with

                   I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi).

No supplied instrument-append representation is required. The dimension and
number of tested likelihood directions may grow. Products, mixtures,
unitary conjugates and block-preserving weighted direct sums of this paired
class inherit c=2 by the baseline affine tensor identity. This is a new
restricted extension relative to the supplied baseline, not a proof of
universal rounding or external/priority validation. O(d) covariance, including
its real structure, is an acquired/supplied promise; twirling an arbitrary
channel does not license transfer of the conclusion back to that channel.

1. TWO EIGENVALUES AND NOTATION
Use tau=Tr/d. Decompose any A as
 A_0=tau(A)I,
 A_a=(A-A^T)/2,
 A_s=(A+A^T)/2-tau(A)I.
Orthogonal covariance gives Phi(A)=A_0+lambda_a A_a+lambda_s A_s.
For d>=2 these are the scalar, antisymmetric and symmetric-traceless
O(d) representations. Positivity on HS and the UCP tracial contraction
property imply 0<=lambda_a,lambda_s<=1. These maps are HS selfadjoint.
Let r_a=d(d-1)/2 and r_s=(d-1)(d+2)/2 be their Hermitian real dimensions.
A trace-orthonormal Hermitian basis in each subspace has total normalized
variance V_a=r_a/d and V_s=r_s/d.

2. THREE NECESSARY COMPATIBILITY INEQUALITIES
Let B be any actual two-output compatible broadcaster with common marginal
Phi. Its normalized Choi state omega on R,B,C has input marginal I/d and
satisfies, for trace-orthonormal H in either eigen-subspace,
 Tr omega [H_R^T (H_B+H_C)]=2 lambda/d.
Let P_RB and P_RC be normalized maximally entangled projectors, and let S_RB,
S_RC be the respective swaps (identity on the unused register). Write
 P=P_RB+P_RC, S=S_RB+S_RC. Completeness of the selected Hermitian bases gives

 K_a=sum_(a) H_R^T(H_B+H_C)=(d P-S)/2,
 K_s=sum_(s) H_R^T(H_B+H_C)=(d P+S)/2-(2/d)I,
 K_all=K_a+K_s=d P-(2/d)I.

Their upper spectral bounds are
 K_a <= (d/2)I,
 K_s <= [d/2+3/2-2/d]I,
 K_all <= [d+1-2/d]I.                                      (1)
Consequently selfcompatibility implies

 lambda_a <= d/[2(d-1)],
 lambda_s <= (d+4)/[2(d+2)],
 r_a lambda_a+r_s lambda_s <= (d-1)(d+2)/2.                 (2)

The second inequality is deliberately a non-sharp but sufficient bound.
No numerical SDP or sufficiency claim for (2) enters this proof.

3. COMPLETE DERIVATION OF THE STAR BOUNDS
For v in C^d, use unnormalized contraction vectors
 x1(v)=sum_i |i,i>_RB tensor |v>_C,
 x2(v)=sum_i |i>_R tensor |v>_B tensor |i>_C,
 x3(v)=|v>_R tensor sum_i |i,i>_BC.
Their span W is a direct sum of d copies of a three-dimensional space whose
Gram matrix is G with diagonal d and off-diagonal 1. It is strictly positive
for d>=2. In the ordered x1,x2,x3 basis, the coefficient action matrices are

 dP = [[d,1,1],[1,d,1],[0,0,0]],
 S  = [[1,0,1],[0,1,1],[1,1,0]].                            (3)

These follow directly by contracting/switching indices. W is invariant
under P and S; W-perp is annihilated by P and invariant under S. On W-perp,
||S||<=2, so K_a<=I, K_s<=(1-2/d)I, K_all=-(2/d)I.

On W the K_a matrix is
 [[(d-1)/2,1/2,0],[1/2,(d-1)/2,0],[-1/2,-1/2,0]],
with eigenvalues d/2,(d-2)/2,0, hence its maximum is d/2. The nonorthogonal
basis causes no ambiguity: G>0 and the represented operator is Hermitian.

For K_s, x1-x2 has eigenvalue d/2-2/d. On span{x1+x2,x3} its coefficient
matrix is
 [[(d+2)/2-2/d,1],[1,-2/d]].
The upper eigenvalue is
 [(d+2)+sqrt((d+2)^2+16)]/4-2/d.
It is <= d/2+3/2-2/d because
 (d+4)^2-[(d+2)^2+16]=4(d-1)>=0.
This also exceeds every stated W-perp upper bound. Finally P on W has
eigenvalues 1+1/d,1-1/d,0, proving the K_all bound. Equations (1) and (2)
are therefore analytic all-d statements, not finite extrapolations.

4. TWO EXPLICIT CANONICAL EB ENDPOINTS
For a rank-one projector P_psi, an orbit distribution with E P_psi=I/d
induces the canonical measurement channel
 Psi(A)=d E[P_psi Tr(P_psi A)].
Its POVM effects are d P_psi times the orbit measure and preparations P_psi.
Thus it is EB, unital, trace-preserving, HS selfadjoint, and HS positive.
It is an exact Haar integral with a finite-outcome realization by moment
Caratheodory; physical finite-bit compilation is discussed below.

The real-vector orbit psi=O e1 gives
 Psi_R(A)=[A+A^T+Tr(A)I]/(d+2),
 with eigenvalues mu_a=0, mu_s=2/(d+2).
This follows directly from the real-sphere fourth moment
 E n_i n_j n_k n_l=(delta_ij delta_kl+delta_ik delta_jl+
                    delta_il delta_jk)/[d(d+2)].

The null-vector orbit psi=O(e1+i e2)/sqrt(2), with psi^T psi=0, gives
 Psi_N(A)=[d A-2 A^T+d Tr(A)I]/[(d-1)(d+2)],
 with mu_a=1/(d-1), mu_s=(d-2)/[(d-1)(d+2)].              (4)
A short derivation avoiding an unevaluated Stiefel integral is as follows.
For a pure P=|psi><psi| and t=|psi^T psi|^2,
 ||P_a||_HS^2=(1-t)/2,
 ||P_s||_HS^2=(1+t)/2-1/d.
By O(d) covariance the canonical orbit map is scalar on these irreps.
Its superoperator trace on an irrep is d E||P_irrep||_HS^2, so division
by r_a,r_s gives
 mu_a=(1-t)/(d-1),
 mu_s=[d(1+t)-2]/[(d-1)(d+2)].
Taking t=0 gives (4); t=1 reproduces Psi_R. This simultaneously proves
complete positivity/EB of (4) without an inference from generic PPT.

5. ROUNDING AND ITS FULL CASE CHECK
Set
 m=max(0,2 lambda_a-1), eta=(d-1)m in [0,1],
 Psi=(1-eta)Psi_R+eta Psi_N.
It has eigenvalues
 mu_a=m,
 mu_s=2/(d+2)-[d/(d+2)]m.                                 (5)
These are nonnegative and at most 1. Automatically
 1-mu_a<=2(1-lambda_a).
To prove the symmetric comparison:
 - If lambda_a<=1/2, m=0 and (2)'s separate lambda_s bound gives
   2 lambda_s-1<=2/(d+2)=mu_s.
 - If lambda_a>1/2 and lambda_s<=1/2, then 2 lambda_s-1<=0<=mu_s.
 - If both eigenvalues exceed 1/2, the third inequality in (2) gives
   r_a(2lambda_a-1)+r_s(2lambda_s-1)<=d-1.
   But r_a mu_a+r_s mu_s=d-1 identically in (5), so
   2lambda_s-1<=mu_s.
All cases establish I-Psi<=2(I-Phi) on the entire HS space. Constants act
identically on both maps. This includes arbitrary tested frames, not just
the covariance generators.
