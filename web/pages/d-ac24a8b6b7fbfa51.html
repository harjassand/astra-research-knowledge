# Cycle 3: source architecture and native-fast checkpoint

Worker r10_probability, 6 October 2026. Primary source arXiv:2609.38710v1, exact version and saved snapshot in sources/r10_probability. Read-depth: §§3.4,6.1–6.3 and AppendixA.1–A.3/B.7–B.8 excerpts; not an independent validation of the entire source theorem.

## Exact charged constraints

The source first uses convex normalized curvature kappa^{-1}I≤HessianV≤I; the cloud core Theorem6.4 fixes kappa≤kappa0. Its phase step h_step, smoothing eta, cloud theta and retained position variance v_pos are distinct parameters. The main choices are equations(6.11)–(6.12):

    h_step=c Lcal^{-3/2}(d+Lcal)^{-1/5},
    eta0=C_eta Lcal h_step², v_pos=c_pos h_step³,
    theta_n=C_cl Lcal^{9/2} eta_n h_step^4 sqrt(d+Lcal),
    J~Lcal, K~Lcal/log Lcal, N~Lcal/h_step.

Retained variance requires N v_pos/eta0≤1/4; eta_n stays within[eta0,5eta0/4]. Conditions include eta≤1/2, theta≤eta/8 and h_step Lcal^{3/2}≤c/kappa. Contraction is 1-c h_step/kappa, initial W2≤C sqrt(kappa d); polynomial numerical errors are made exp(-C Lcal) with quadrature nodes J and Picard depth K. Initial dimension remains inside accuracy logarithms even if corrections become trace-dependent.

Local source fluctuation scale is L_fluc²[√(Jd p)+p]+L_fluc^4 Jd, with L_fluc²≤C(eta+theta)h_step^4/theta. The interior source mean displacement is at most

    b_int≤C sqrt(J) h_step²/sqrt(theta)
                   (eta^{3/2}+theta/sqrt(eta)) sqrt(d+p).

Its clipping constraint is sqrt(p)b_int+b_int²≤c B_mean. Hidden RGO requires

    sqrt(theta p)(epsilon_anchor+theta score_bound)
       +sqrt(eta theta)(sqrt(dp)+p)≤c B_RGO.

Source anchor and score bounds are h_step² sqrt(kappa(d+Lcal)) and sqrt(kappa(d+Lcal)) from(6.15)–(6.16). These cannot be relabeled trace-dependent solely because a fluctuation Jacobian is low energy.

## Forest accounting is dimension-independent once primitives close

Equations(6.5)–(6.7) set

    calB_fluc=K B_fluc+B_fluc,end,
    calB_mean=K B_mean+B_mean,end,
    Qloc=m_child=m_RGO=C Lcal²,
    lambda_child=C(calB_fluc K_series+calB_mean J)/m_child,
    lambda_RGO=C J calB_mean/m_RGO.

The decisive subcriticality condition is

    Lcal²(B_fluc+B_mean)
       +Lcal(B_fluc,end+B_mean,end)≤c.

Then expected contracted vertices≤4N/3 and

    E queries≤(4N/3)[Qloc+Q_RGO C J calB_mean]
                +terminal RGO cost.

Lemma6.2 demands a **uniform conditional** Q_RGO at each adaptively requested explicit center. Multiplying an average RGO cost at a convenient stationary center would not justify this step. Global slots are capped by C(N+Lcal³)Lcal², with exponential tail. Adaptive TV replacement is charged by a deterministic slot cap or expected ideal kernel errors; rare local failures must be included.

These are reference-query counts. AppendixB.7 charges C Lcal gradients per local block or mean estimator, excluding its RGO requests; values evaluate J blocks, whereas importance-sampled covariance actions request selected blocks. Final gradient-cost accounting must retain this conversion.

## Improved RGO is an acyclic internal construction

AppendixA normalizes an explicit RGO at its mode and solves a known harmonic quadratic. Residual Hessian is O(eta), and its own phase parameters( A.13) are

    h_R=c Lcal^{-5/4}[eta²(d+Lcal)]^{-1/4},
    sigma0²=C_sigma Lcal² h_R²,
    theta_R=C_cl Lcal^{9/2} eta² sigma² h_R^4 sqrt(d+Lcal).

Its explicit sub-RGOs have variance O(eta sigma²). These are small enough for the direct baseline A.2 because eta sigma²[√(d Lcal)+Lcal]≤c. Thus AppendixA does not invoke Theorem6.4 or recursively call the oracle it is proving. In the initial direct regime eta√((d+Lcal)Lcal)≤c, no cloud core is needed.

TheoremA.1 counts gradient/proximal/full-Hessian reference calls:

    Q_RGO≤C[1+Lcal^{17/4}(eta²(d+Lcal))^{1/4}].

Gradient implementation B.8 instead gives

    C Lcal_y+C Lcal^{21/4}(eta²(d+Lcal))^{1/4},
    Lcal_y=log[d(1+eta||gradientV(y)||)/(eta epsilon)].

The powers improve to15/4 and19/4 in the large-d regime. Approximate initial modes and center-dependent gradient norms are charged in Lcal_y and B.13. Thus a trace-based full theorem needs a separately proved direct RGO primitive, not a circular invocation of the new full sampler. The direct primitive in full_trace_sampler.md closes this interface without a cloud recursion.

## Native-fast mean substitution reaches a concrete checkpoint

c_nonlinear reports a safe direct residual-mean bound proportional to h_U sqrt(eta)(sqrt(tau)+sqrt(p)), which lacks the source Stein estimator's eta^{3/2} smallness. Whitening its interior update gives, suppressing J and logarithms,

    b_direct~h_U h_step³ sqrt(tau)/sqrt(theta)

at eta~h_step². With the affine-baseline nuclear fluctuation scale, the minimal cloud variance is schematically theta~q h_U tau h_step^6. Substituting it gives b_direct~sqrt(h_U/q), independent of h_step. This cannot enforce B_mean~Lcal^{-2} merely by shrinking the step. It is the first failed implication in attempting to preserve the source's fast parameter schedule.

Larger theta could reduce that mean bound, but changes hidden-RGO likelihoods, covariance completion and the internal-RGO cost schedule. No complete native-forest theorem follows from this substitution alone. We therefore keep the complete proximal/direct-FORS theorem as the checkpoint (subsequently sharpened from tau-linear to sqrt(tau L)+L in rotation_trace_refinement.md) and do not claim a speculative improved tau exponent. The next load-bearing lemma for the fast architecture is a gradient-only residual mean estimator retaining additional powers of eta, or an independently justified enlarged-cloud schedule with all hidden-RGO, endpoint and forest constraints closed.
