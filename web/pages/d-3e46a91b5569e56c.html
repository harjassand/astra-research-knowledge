Candidate interface
-------------------
Fix a compact connected simple group G and an irreducible known carrier
V_Lambda, with highest-weight scale N=sum_i n_i. The unknown one-copy state
is rho_(Lambda,theta)=exp(T_theta/N)/Z, ||theta||<=h, and the input is n
identical copies. The candidate constructs one parameter-independent EB
channel on the n-copy space by Schur-decomposing, measuring the irrep label,
using the highest-weight coherent EB channel inside that irrep, resetting its
maximally mixed multiplicity factor, and inverting Schur. Its retained label
count is polynomial in nN. The asserted error is independent of n and the
total-irrep size: relative entropy <= B_G h/N and half trace distance
<=1-exp(-B_G h/N). All labels/cubatures, Schur acquisition and implementation
costs are existential or separately charged, not an efficient compiler.

1. Closest thermal-spin compression: Yang--Chiribella--Hayashi (2016)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Yang, Chiribella and Hayashi, “Optimal Compression for Identically Prepared
Qubit States,” PRL 117, 090502 (2016), arXiv:1606.02893:
https://arxiv.org/html/1606.02893v3

This is a close structural antecedent. For n identical qubits with known
spectrum and unknown basis, Schur--Weyl decomposition gives spin-J blocks
whose states are Gibbs states in the spin-J representation; their sector
weights and maximally mixed multiplicities are retained/reset. The protocol
uses universal cloning channels to move between nearby spin sectors and
faithfully compresses into a quantum memory of log n qubits. The paper
explicitly presents the spin-block Gibbs form, Schur sectors and multiplicity
reset in Eqs. (1)--(2), and the encoder/decoder in Eq. (8), lines 47--89 of
the arXiv HTML.

The interfaces differ in the decisive resource and limit. Yang et al. retain
a growing quantum register and make the error vanish as n grows for fixed
local qubits/spectrum. The current candidate is EB (classical retained label
only), but its error vanishes with local highest-weight scale N, not with n;
at fixed N its theorem gives only a fixed error floor for arbitrarily many
copies. It applies to arbitrary fixed simple G and highest-weight faces, and
uses a coherent measure-prepare decoder rather than inter-sector cloning.
Thus it complements the qubit thermal compression result and does not
contradict its fixed-d no-fully-classical-faithful-compression boundary.

The closest internal archive antecedent is N01, “Size-uniform qudit archive
via a Gibbs generator defect” (work/astra_prior/cards/N01-qudit-gibbs.txt).
That card reports a parameter-independent, fully classical coherent-EB
archive for n copies at fixed local dimension d, with alphabet
O_d(poly(n)) and error

  1-(lambda_min/lambda_max)^(d(d-1)/2),

uniform in n. Its stated proof primitive is the same operator Jensen plus
coherent-generator-defect argument, and it explicitly credits the
Leung--Li--Ma coadjoint-orbit identity. The candidate generalizes the claimed
dimension scale from fixed d to d=d_Lambda growing with N, with the exponent
replaced by a root-system constant divided by N, and uses all total-irrep
blocks without a bound on their effective temperature. However N01 is marked
source-derived/unreviewed and its linked proof pack is absent here; it is a
strong internal overlap pointer, not independent source verification or
priority clearance.

The internal prior N02, “Correlated weak-field experiment and spherical
operational limit” (work/astra_prior/cards/N02-correlated-sphere.txt), is
another adjacent thermal-spin construction: a known correlated rotationally
invariant reference with total spin concentrated around j/N -> m and field
b/N is encoded into one tilted-sphere observation, then decoded to the whole
density state. It assumes a specific spin concentration law and a single
collective correlated source. Its linked source/payload is absent from this
checkout, so treat this only as an internal source-derived overlap lead, not
independently verified primary literature or a priority clearance. The
candidate instead handles iid tensor powers of one irreducible local carrier,
arbitrary highest-weight face, all n, and does not assume a concentration law
for its Schur labels.

2. Berezin/coherent-channel overlap: Aschieri--Ruba--Solovej (2024)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Aschieri, Ruba and Solovej, “SU(2)-equivariant quantum channels: semiclassical
analysis,” CMP 405 (2024), article 298, arXiv:2404.04133:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04133

The paper defines the spin coherent symbol map H_J and coherent-state
quantization Op_J; Op_J H_J is exactly the coherent measure-prepare channel
used in the SU(2) candidate. Theorem 1, Eq. (1.22), proves for all Schatten
p norms

  ||(I-Op_J H_J) A||_p <= C/(2J+1) ||(1+4 Q) A||_p,

where Q A=sum_{a=x,y,z}[J_a,[J_a,A]] is the adjoint Casimir. This is a direct
Berezin smoothing estimate, with a dimension penalty canceled when the input
has controlled Casimir content. The same paper's coherent multiplier equals
the candidate's SU(2) Berezin eigenvalue. See the full-text Theorem 1 and
Eq. (3.18): https://d-nb.info/1357312571/34

There is a direct qualitative SU(2) thermal consequence. For
omega_(J,beta)=exp(beta J_z)/Z, Q annihilates I and the elementary bound
||omega-I/(2J+1)||_1 <= 2 beta J combines with the exact geometric-diagonal
estimate ||Q omega||_1 <= beta(4J+1) to give

  (1/2)||omega-Op_J H_J(omega)||_1 <= C' beta

uniformly in J. (The second estimate is the exact calculation in the
candidate's §6; it is not asserted as a separate theorem by Aschieri et al.)
After the usual Schur decomposition, each spin block of n identical qubit
Gibbs states has the same beta, and the EB channel preserves sector weights.
This yields an all-n O(beta) whole-state error; a finite coherent cubature
gives a polynomial outcome alphabet. Therefore the candidate's qualitative
SU(2) all-n thermal-spin conclusion is already strongly suggested by a
published Berezin estimate plus the exact thermal Casimir calculation.
The candidate's exact coefficient 1-exp(-beta) is sharper and comes from a
separate exact CP-product argument.

The candidate's actual extension beyond this source is not a generic claim
that Berezin quantization approximates all states. It uses a root-system
constant generator defect for every G-irrep, then exact tensor-Schur weights
and covariance. Aschieri et al. treat SU(2) and derive regularity-dependent
approximations for coherent channels; they do not state this arbitrary-G,
all-highest-weight/all-n EB archive theorem or its exact trace coefficient.
The route through their Theorem 1 is a likely SU(2) proof shortcut and must
be disclosed when describing the contribution. Historical priority of the
all-G bridge remains unestablished.

3. Quantum local asymptotic normality and quantum population coding
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Kahn and Guţă, “Local asymptotic normality for finite dimensional quantum
systems,” CMP 289 (2009), 597--652, arXiv:0804.3876:
https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.3876

Their theorem fixes the local system dimension d and lets n -> infinity;
the local statistical model is represented by classical Gaussian and
quantum displaced-thermal oscillator modes. It is a comparison of
experiments, not an EB reconstruction theorem. It does not state estimates
uniform as d grows with N, and its noncommuting modes remain quantum.

Yang, Bai, Chiribella and Hayashi, “Compression for quantum population
coding,” TIT 64 (2018), 4766, arXiv:1701.03372:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03372

This fixed-finite-dimensional parametric-source result gives asymptotically
faithful hybrid memory of (f/2)log n bits, including quantum memory; it
states that fully classical memory cannot be faithful for a noncommuting
family, even with arbitrarily many classical bits. This does not contradict
the candidate: at fixed N the candidate only gives an error upper bound of
order 1/N, independent of n, so it does not claim fixed-d faithful
classicalization as n -> infinity. Its small-error limit is the different
joint regime N -> infinity, where the one-copy carrier dimension also
grows. Moreover, its parameter family is a compact Lie Gibbs orbit with
known irrep structure, not an arbitrary fixed-d smooth family.

Conclusion / source boundary
----------------------------
The closest confirmed external overlap is the Schur-block Gibbs/coherent-spin
structure of Yang--Chiribella--Hayashi and the exact SU(2) Berezin channel
estimate of Aschieri--Ruba--Solovej. In SU(2), the latter plus the candidate's
thermal Casimir bound appears to recover the qualitative all-n O(beta) EB
claim, though not the exact 1-exp(-beta) coefficient. The all-G theorem
extends that mechanism to every fixed compact simple group and arbitrary
highest-weight faces with a uniform-in-total-block bound. Quantum LAN and
population-coding compression address fixed-d, n-asymptotics and generally
retain quantum memory; they neither imply nor contradict this growing-local-
carrier EB statement.

The closest internal conceptual match is N01's fixed-d all-n coherent Gibbs
archive; proving that its constants and construction lift uniformly when d
grows along irreducible G-carriers is exactly the scale distinction at issue.
