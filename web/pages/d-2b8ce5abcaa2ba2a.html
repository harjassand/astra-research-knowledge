EXACT THEOREM

For every positive integer L, set T=2^L and take the canonical four-site
matrix (one-based indexing)

       [0 T T 1]
 F_T = [0 0 T 1].
       [0 0 0 1]
       [0 0 0 0]

Its original line i consists of (e_i,transpose(row_i F_T)), with activity
one. Add all six coordinate lines c_ij=(e_i,e_j), each with activity
sigma, where 0<sigma<=1 is fixed. Original labels o_i and coordinate
labels c_ij remain distinct, including the parallel lines o_3 and c_34.

Let pi be the exact full-basis law on two-line states, proportional to
activity product times squared determinant. Let P be ANY stationary
two-replica kernel with law pi tensor pi. Every transition is required
to satisfy at least one of:

 (a) at most one selected line is replaced in EACH layer;
 (b) the multiset union of selected line labels across both layers is
     unchanged. This permits arbitrarily large, coordinated layer swaps.

Then a uniform additive energy inequality for all f1,f2 must have

     C >= T^2/240.                                         (N)

This holds even when proposal probabilities are designed with knowledge
of every partition. It therefore includes every oracle-free single-pair
Gibbs/Metropolis/repair kernel augmented by arbitrary union swaps. It
does not include arbitrary two-pair updates; an explicit cheap two-pair
crossing is shown below.
It also does not refute an algorithm that chooses a different balanced
frame and THEN adds a different auxiliary law. A scope guard and an
explicit acquired rescue for this family are given at the end.

The original instance ALREADY satisfies the source's stronger balance
window 1/4<=r_i<=1, with every row maximum r_i exactly one. We may set
sigma=1/[10^8(4+1)^4], precisely the standard uniform polynomial floor
probe scale. No exponentially weak probe activity is used in (N). The
exponential factor T^2=4^L comes from an input vector coordinate having
L+1 bits. Thus the single-pair compiler fails the polynomial-bit gate
on this admitted balanced canonical family. This result is stronger
than the weak-probe cycle obstruction in INITIAL.txt, while its move
scope is narrower (one line per layer).

PROOF: ORIGINAL COEFFICIENTS AND CALIBRATION

Among all six two/two complementary cuts on
four sites, the only nonzero original full-basis line set is {o_1,o_3}.
Its determinant has absolute value T. The other possible set {o_1,o_2}
has complementary minor det[[T,1],[T,1]]=0. Every other cut includes
row4 or column1 in a zero position. Hence

                   f(empty)=Z_original=T^2.

For two retained coordinates {i,j}, their squared complementary minor
sum is |F_ij|^2+|F_ji|^2. Thus, listing hole pairs rather than retained
pairs,

  f(12)=f(13)=f(23)=1,
  f(14)=f(24)=f(34)=T^2.

Dividing by f(empty), every row of g(ij) has a maximum exactly one.
These are aggregate coefficients obtained by the explicit four-site
formula, not assumed general coefficient-oracle access. The pre-existing
coordinate line o_3 has activity one, so its coordinate bottleneck plus
the source floor is B_ij=1 for every pair. Adding sigma=B_ij/D0 with
D0=10^8*5^4 is therefore the ordinary calibration floor in this example.

For arbitrary sigma, the augmented partition computed below satisfies

  Z_aug/Z_original=1+3sigma+3sigma(1+sigma)/T^2.

At the stated source scale this is below two and retains the aggregate
capacity/balance regime. There is no conflict with the baseline aggregate
inflation bound. The obstruction is to a specific microscopic kernel.
In fact the post-probe maxima are all (T^2+sigma)/Z, which are in [1/8,4]
at the chosen scale, so even the augmented instance remains balanced.

PROOF: EXHAUSTIVE SYMBOLIC STATE TABLE

Any full basis has two selected line labels. A nonzero state of weight
proportional to T^2 is exactly one of

  {o_1,o_3}: T^2;
  {o_1,c_24}: sigma T^2;
  {o_1,c_34}: sigma T^2;
  {o_2,c_14}: sigma T^2.                                   (H)

All remaining positive states are

  {o_1,c_23}: sigma;
  {o_2,c_13}: sigma;
  {o_3,c_12}: sigma;
  {c_12,c_34}, {c_13,c_24}, {c_14,c_23}: sigma^2 each.       (R)

Every other two-line set is zero. Derivation: two original lines use
the complementary-minor formula above. For one original plus one
coordinate line, duplicates of e_i give zero when the coordinate
line meets i; otherwise its complementary coordinate to e_i must be
supplied by f_i. Reading that single entry gives exactly the six
displayed one-original states. Two coordinate lines are nonzero
precisely when they form a perfect matching, with determinant +/-1.
No interpolation assumption or approximate numerical zero is used.

Therefore

  Z=T^2(1+3sigma)+3sigma(1+sigma),
  beta=pi(R)=3sigma(1+sigma)/Z.                             (1)

Call H the four-state set in (H), and define h(A)=1_{o_1 in A}, a
linear line-occupancy statistic. Its exact probability of value one is

  p=[T^2(1+2sigma)+sigma]/Z,
  1-p=[sigma T^2+2sigma+3sigma^2]/Z,
  Var_pi h=p(1-p).                                         (2)

A transition replacing at most one line in a layer can move between
two two-line states only if they share a selected line label. The
only H state with h=0 is {o_2,c_14}; it shares NO label with any of
the three H states with h=1. Thus every type-(a) transition having
both pair endpoints in H x H preserves h in each layer individually.
Every type-(b) transition preserves h(A)+h(B) on the ENTIRE state
space, since the statistic counts total multiplicity of o_1. It
follows that h+h is constant on every H x H to H x H transition.

By the initial stationary escape lemma,

 E_P(h+h)<=4 eta, eta=(pi tensor pi)((H x H)^c)=2beta-beta^2.

The comparison with f1=f2=h therefore requires the exact bound

  C>=p(1-p)/[4(2beta-beta^2)].                              (3)

For T>=1 and 0<sigma<=1,

 p(1-p)>=T^4 sigma(1+2sigma)/Z^2,
 4(2beta-beta^2)<=8beta=24sigma(1+sigma)/Z.

Hence

 C>=T^4(1+2sigma)/[24(1+sigma) Z].                         (4)

Also

 Z<=T^2[1+3sigma+3sigma(1+sigma)]<=10T^2,
 (1+2sigma)/(1+sigma)>=1.

Substituting in (4) gives (N). At a fixed small probe scale, the sharp
asymptotic expression in (3) is

 C >= T^2(1+2sigma)/[24(1+sigma)(1+3sigma)] (1+o(1)),

so the elementary T^2/240 lower bound has spare constants. No mixing
experiment is needed. The stationary flow calculation proves the
obstruction for every kernel in the defined class.

EXACT BOUNDARY: TWO-LINE MOVES EVADE THIS EXAMPLE

The high states {o_1,o_3} and {o_2,c_14} differ in two selected lines.
Their weight ratio is sigma, independent of T. A symmetric proposal
making this two-line replacement has Metropolis acceptance sigma.
It changes h and crosses the obstructed cut through dominant mass.
With sigma inverse polynomial in dimension, that capacity is admissible.
Thus this report does NOT refute the actual OA113 two-edge switch
architecture or a general two-pair determinant compiler. Replacing two
lines, while carefully choosing the determinant completion, is a
necessary extra capability relative to the naive single-pair route.

The example does not imply approximate-counting hardness. It has ten
positive states and its partition, sampler and all coefficients can be
acquired directly. It is a rigorous counterexample to a universal
local-energy theorem, not a counterexample to every algorithm.

RELATED POSITIVE LOCAL REPAIR IDENTITY AND ITS LIMIT

Fix an arbitrary canonical full basis A containing original line i, and
keep its other lines as the ordered column list R. Let

  D=det(R,e_i,f_i), D_j=det(R,e_i,e_j).

Multilinearity gives the exact, acquired identity

                   D=sum_{j!=i} F_ij D_j.                 (5)

If coordinate activity lambda_ij is positive wherever F_ij is nonzero,
Cauchy-Schwarz yields

 |D|^2 <= [sum_{j!=i}|F_ij|^2/lambda_ij]
              [sum_{j!=i}lambda_ij |D_j|^2].              (6)

After including the original activity a_i and unchanged other activities,
the total weight of single-pair coordinate replacements obeys

 sum_j W(A-o_i+c_ij) >= W(A)/S_i,
 S_i=a_i sum_{j!=i}|F_ij|^2/lambda_ij.                     (7)

The finite sum on the left is computable with O(n) determinant evaluations
without any ambient-hole partition oracle; all zero candidates can be
recognized exactly. A maximizing or weighted choice is acquired locally.
This is a useful precise repair identity, not a global energy theorem.
Large S_i may be exponential in binary vector coordinates.

The new example shows that aggregate row balance does NOT bound S_i and
does NOT even bound the actual local escape ratio from each original
pair. At A={o_1,o_3}, removing o_1 leaves R=(e_3,e_4). The sole nonzero
coordinate completion retaining the canonical anchor e_1 is c_12. Its
weight is sigma, whereas W(A)=T^2. Even permitting ALL coordinate
completions of R leaves only c_12. The complete one-pair fiber consists
of A and {o_3,c_12}, with exact heat-bath escape probability

                     sigma/(T^2+sigma).                   (8)

This is exponentially small despite r_i=1 and calibrated sigma. The
energy obstruction above explains why other single-pair moves and
replica union swaps cannot collectively repair this particular cut.
A two-line repair can, as explicitly demonstrated.

ADVERSARIAL SCOPE GUARD: A DIFFERENT AUXILIARY LAW RESCUES THIS FAMILY

For square T=2^(2m), choose rational ambient diagonal scales

  D=diag(T^-1/2,T^-1/2,T^-1/2,T^1/2).

After applying D to all ambient vectors and normalizing each canonical
anchor e_i within its pair by an internal determinant-one transform,
the original canonical matrix becomes F'=D F_T D=F_1. Its hard full-basis
law is unchanged, because every full-basis weight is multiplied by the
same |det D|^2=T^-2. Both F_T and F_1 satisfy original r_i=1.

If the existing augmented law is preserved too, a coordinate line
c_ij=(e_i,e_j) must receive its transformed activity

                   sigma'_ij=sigma(d_i d_j)^2.

Thus the three core coordinate activities become sigma/T^2 while the
three core-to-fourth-coordinate activities stay sigma. Every augmented
state weight scales by T^-2, so the local-energy obstruction is exactly
unchanged. Representational preconditioning of a fixed target is not
a rescue.

However, adding FRESH UNIFORM sigma probes AFTER this rescaling gives
the entirely T-independent F_1 augmented system. Its locally computable
one-pair heat-bath energies are independent of L. This changes the
enlarged auxiliary target law, while preserving the original hard law.
The required rational scale is acquired directly from the explicit T
and uses O(L) bits in this example; no unknown partition oracle is needed.
