1. EXACT WEIGHTED OPERATOR AND NORMALIZATION

Let a>=b>=1,N=a+b,B=b+1,d=(a+1)B(N+2)/2. Define on HS(V)

 L_T=sum ad(T_A)^2, L_S=sum ad(S_A)^2,
 L_w=L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)),
 D_w(t)=Tr_HS 1_[0,t](L_w).                               (1.1)

The energy is E_wall(A)=<A,L_w A>. Its nullspace is exactly CI,
because its L_T term already has this nullspace on an irreducible
carrier. It is SU3-conjugation invariant and self-adjoint positive;
its nonzero gap is at least3/N^2. This gap follows because the center
acts trivially on End(V), so its nontrivial SU3 constituents lie in
the root lattice, whose least quadratic Casimir is3 (the adjoint).

We have elementary dimension comparisons

  BN^2/4<=d<=2BN^2.                                      (1.2)

The original range for the separate explicit wall comparators was
b>=256 and a>=8B. These finite cutoffs ensure that their admitted
low-rank/hole ranges overlap. They are not assumptions on the
converse or its posterior theorem, which already holds more broadly.
The single principal family now removes those cutoff assumptions from
the actual all-Q upper, so H and the current profile hold for ALL a>=b>=1.

3. ALL-MATRIX LOW-RANK CONSEQUENCE OF(H)

For any conjugation-invariant spectral projector P_t on HS(V), Schur
gives Tr_2 P_t=(D_w(t)/d)I in vectorization. A normalized rank-Q matrix
has Schmidt rank<=Q, so

  <A,P_t A><=Q D_w(t)/d.                                 (3.1)

This requires no positivity or kernel orientation. Thus
E_wall(A)>=t[1-Q D_w(t)/d]. For1<=Q<=d/2 set

  f_Q=(1/N)min{Q^(-1/3),sqrt(B/Q)},
  epsilon=min{c0,1,(8C)^(-1/2),(8C)^(-1/3)},
  t=epsilon f_Q.                                         (3.2)

It lies in(H)'s interval. Each nontrivial term in QD_w(t)/d is at most
Cepsilon^2 or Cepsilon^3 respectively, while Q/d<=1/2. Consequently
QD_w(t)/d<=3/4, and

  Lambda_wall(Q)>=epsilon f_Q/4, 1<=Q<=d/2.              (3.3)

This supplies an arbitrary-Kraus lower through the already proved
weighted posterior converse, at one fixed admitted small h and regular
H. A presumed tensor-product allocation or coherent optimizer is not
used in (3.1)--(3.3).

4. ALL-MATRIX CODIMENSION CONSEQUENCE OF(H)

Let A have rank<=d-k, choose a rank-k projection P onto a subspace of
its RIGHT kernel, and write A=zI+X with TrX=0. The relation AP=0 gives
Tr(PX)=-kz, also for nonnormal A and complex z. Put
Y=P-kI/d and G_P=<Y,L_w^+Y>. Spectral Cauchy--Schwarz and the gap give

  k^2|z|^2<=G_P E_wall(A),
  ||X||^2<=N^2 E_wall(A)/3,
  E_wall(A)/||A||^2 >=[dG_P/k^2+N^2/3]^(-1).              (4.1)

The vectorized rank-k P has norm squared k. Projector partial trace
and Schmidt rank give, with the trivial component removed,

 M_P(t)=||1_(0,t](L_w)Y||^2
       <=k^2[D_w(t)-1]/d,
 M_P(t)<=||Y||^2<=k.                                     (4.2)

Choose eta=min(c0,1) and

  K=(eta/N)min{k^(-1/3),sqrt(B/k)}<=c0/N.

The exact spectral layer cake, including the gap, is
G_P=int_0^infty M_P(t)t^(-2)dt. Apply(H) on[0,K] and only the total
norm bound for t>K. This yields

 G_P<=Ck^2[(N^2/B)K+(N^3/2)K^2]+k/K
     <=A_eta N max{k^(4/3),k^(3/2)/sqrt(B)},
 A_eta=eta^(-1)+Ceta+Ceta^2/2.                            (4.3)

For k<=B^3 the first expression in the max is larger; for k>=B^3
the second is larger. Let

  g_k=(1/(Nd))min{k^(2/3),sqrt(Bk)}.

For1<=k<=d/2, (1.2) implies N^2 g_k<=sqrt2. Equations(4.1)--(4.3)
therefore prove the uniform arbitrary-matrix lower

  Lambda_wall(d-k)>=g_k/(A_eta+sqrt2/3).                  (4.4)

No scalar-Casimir positivity replacement or coherent-kernel theorem
is needed for this lower. Using D_w rather than D_w-1 in (4.2) would
create a false divergent integral at zero; the subtraction matters.

6. AN EXACT MULTIPLICITY-SAFE OPERATOR LOWER AID

Define M=sum_A ad(T_A)ad(S_A). Each same-index pair commutes by
adjoint covariance, so M is self-adjoint. It is invariant under SU3
conjugation and therefore preserves every L_T isotypic block, including
all its multiplicity coordinates. On a block of Casimir c>0,

                L_S >= M^2/c.                           (6.1)

Proof: for a vector psi in that block, Mpsi lies in it. Project each
ad(S_A)psi back to the block before applying ad(T_A); the latter commute
with its projector. The row operator {ad(T_A)} has row norm sqrt(c)
because sum ad(T_A)^2=cI there. Cauchy gives
||Mpsi||^2<=c sum||P_c ad(S_A)psi||^2<=c<psi,L_S psi>.
This argument does not assume L_S preserves each individual copy, or
that M and L_S commute within a multiplicity block.

The already proved exact adjoint block, in the normalized basis
T/sqrt(C2),S/sqrt(C_S), is

  M=[[0,3sqrt(C_S/C2)],
     [3sqrt(C_S/C2),-6alpha/N]],
  eigenvalues=(3/N)[-alpha+/-sqrt(C2/3+1/4)].               (6.2)

The wall slow/fast splitting in this ONE block is exact. It is not
an all-spectrum count. On V tensor V* let X_A=T_A left, Y_A=-T_A right,
K=X+Y=adT, A3=d_ABC Y_A X_B X_C, B3=d_ABC X_A Y_B Y_C. Direct
expansion of K dot(D_X-D_Y) gives

  M=[2C3+A3-B3-alpha L_T]/N.                              (6.3)

Thus a cubic missing-label operator supplies a scalar lower aid, but
its spectrum across the growing multiplicity spaces is not inferred
from the ordinary Casimir decomposition. Equation(6.1) may be weaker
than the required vertical elliptic comparison. No unproved linear
comparison between L_S and |M| is used.
