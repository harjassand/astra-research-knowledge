# Finite topology data cannot decide preparable-state factorization: a sharp 2D TQFT fixture

Date: 2026-10-07. Status: universal elementary construction proved below; finite exact replay PASS for N=2,...,9. Novelty unestablished and no general quantum-gravity conclusion. This is our deduction, not a claim made by the recent papers.

## Exact interface and result

Input class: finite dimensional unitary oriented two-dimensional TQFTs, with N circle states, no charged operator/source insertions, and exact partition-function access on closed connected oriented surfaces of genus 0 through h. Disjoint unions supply only products of these values. Cost is maximum available genus, plus exact-real data; it is not laboratory acquisition or a finite-precision algorithm. Factorization below refers to the spaces spanned by states prepared from the empty manifold using the specified source-free bordisms. The complete local TQFT Hilbert spaces already obey the ordinary tensor-product axiom.

For each N>=2 there exist two such theories with identical closed partition functions for every genus 0,...,N-1. In the first theory the maximal unitary source-neutral symmetry is S2 and the two-circle preparable space strictly contains the tensor product of one-circle preparable spaces. In the second the symmetry is trivial and the preparable spaces factorize. The first different closed partition function is at genus N. Conversely, in this supplied N-state class genus 0,...,N determines the whole theory and its symmetry. Thus the worst-case maximum-genus threshold N is sharp. Without a supplied bound on N there is no universal finite-genus threshold.

This does not contest McNamara–Wang's reconstruction from ALL closed manifolds satisfying the full axioms. It shows that replacing their interface by a bounded genus menu loses even a qualitative conclusion about preparable-state factorization.

## Explicit reflection-positive theories

For positive theta_i, take A=C^N with idempotents e_i e_j=delta_ij e_i, unit sum_i e_i, counit epsilon(e_i)=theta_i, inner product <e_i,e_j>=delta_ij theta_i, involution complex conjugation, and coproduct Delta(e_i)=theta_i^(-1)e_i tensor e_i. Multiplication and coproduct are adjoints, as are unit and counit. The algebra is associative, commutative and unital; the coproduct is coassociative and satisfies the Frobenius identity componentwise. These provide the usual oriented 2D TQFT sewing relations. Reflection of a preparation produces its Hilbert adjoint, so every reflected Gram matrix is positive semidefinite. No negative or complex weights are admitted.

Put x_i=1/theta_i>0. A disk with g handles prepares sum_i x_i^g e_i; closing it gives

Z_g = sum_i theta_i^(1-g) = sum_i x_i^(g-1).

In particular Z_0=sum_i 1/x_i and Z_1=N. Any algebra automorphism permutes primitive idempotents, and preservation of counit permits exactly permutations of equal theta_i. Hence the maximal unitary Frobenius symmetry is the product of symmetric groups on repeated weights.

## All-N pair and certified positive roots

Define, with the empty product interpreted as 1,

P_N(x)=(x-1)^2 product_(j=2)^(N-1)(x-j),
c0=P_N(0)=(-1)^N (N-1)!,
c1=P_N'(0)=(-1)^(N-1)(N-1)! (1+H_(N-1)),
Q_(N,eta)(x)=P_N(x)+eta(c0+c1 x).

Here H_m=sum_(j=1)^m 1/j. The base roots are {1,1,2,...,N-1}. Let

eta_N=(3/4)^(N-2) / [32 (N-1)! {1+(1+H_(N-1))(N-3/4)}].

For any 0<eta<=eta_N, Q has N distinct positive real roots. Proof: use intervals [3/4,1], [1,5/4], and [j-1/4,j+1/4] for j=2,...,N-1. At the two outer endpoints adjacent to 1, P has sign (-1)^N and magnitude at least (1/16)(3/4)^(N-2). At endpoints of an interval about a simple integer root, P changes sign and its magnitude is at least (1/4)(3/4)^(N-1), which is at least the preceding bound. All endpoints are at most N-3/4, and

|c0+c1 x| <= (N-1)! {1+(1+H_(N-1))(N-3/4)}.

Our eta bounds the perturbation by half the smallest endpoint magnitude, so these signs persist. At x=1, Q=eta(c0+c1)=-eta c0 H_(N-1), with the opposite sign to the two adjacent outer endpoints. Thus intermediate value gives one root in each of the N disjoint positive interval interiors. Degree N precludes further roots or multiple roots. This proves the universal assertion; Sturm computations merely replay finite examples.

Both root multisets define the positive-weight TQFT above, with theta_i=1/x_i. Algebraic real weights are allowed; rational roots are not required.

## Exact genus agreement and first distinction

Write a monic degree-N polynomial as x^N-e1 x^(N-1)+...+(-1)^N eN. Q leaves all coefficients of x^k for k>=2 unchanged, so e1,...,e_(N-2) agree. Newton identities imply equal power sums p_k=sum_i x_i^k for 1<=k<=N-2. Also its constant and linear coefficients both scale by 1+eta; the reciprocal sum is -c1/c0 and therefore agrees exactly. The degree gives equal torus Z1=N. These facts prove Z_g equality for 0<=g<=N-1, including N=2 where the positive-power agreement is empty.

Newton at the next degree gives

p_(N-1)(Q)-p_(N-1)(P)=(-1)^N (N-1) eta e_(N-1)(P),

which is nonzero because all roots of P are positive. Consequently genus N distinguishes them. Equality extends to arbitrary disjoint unions whose connected components all lie within the admitted genus cutoff, by the TQFT multiplicativity axiom.

## Factorization actually differs, despite these identical data

For P, only the first two weights coincide, so the symmetry is S2. The disk-with-handles vectors span the invariant one-circle subspace: there are N-1 distinct x values, and the corresponding Vandermonde matrix has full rank. Its dimension is N-1. Let w=(e1-e2)/sqrt(2); theta1=theta2=1, so w is normalized and odd under the swap. The annulus preparation is the coevaluation state sum_i v_i tensor v_i, where v_i=e_i/sqrt(theta_i) is orthonormal. Its component along w tensor w is 1. It is invariant but cannot belong to H_prep(S1) tensor H_prep(S1), since w is orthogonal to each one-circle preparable state. Thus the annulus supplies a genuinely additional preparable two-circle direction. At least this direction, not a dimension heuristic alone, proves strict failure of preparable-space factorization.

For Q all x_i are distinct, the maximal symmetry is trivial, and disk-with-handles vectors for g=0,...,N-1 span all A by Vandermonde. Disjoint preparations consequently span A^(tensor k) for every k. Thus every multi-circle preparable space equals the full tensor product and factorizes. Both underlying local theories remain unitary and tensorial throughout.

## Competent reconstruction comparator and charged costs

For supplied N, query Z0 and p_k=Z_(k+1), k=1,...,N-1. Newton identities

k e_k=sum_(j=1)^k (-1)^(j-1) e_(k-j) p_j, e0=1,

reconstruct e1,...,e_(N-1). Since Z0=e_(N-1)/eN>0, set eN=e_(N-1)/Z0. The resulting monic polynomial reconstructs all weights up to permutation, including their multiplicities, hence the TQFT up to unitary isomorphism. Z1 independently reports N if it was not supplied. This comparator has the same exact closed-surface interface; it does not receive charged preparations unavailable to the lower-bound procedure. The threshold concerns maximum genus, not optimal arbitrary-query cardinality or bit complexity. Exact root/multiplicity determination is not silently priced as finite precision.

There is also an explicit precision charge at the sharp topology threshold. For eta=eta_N, let D_N=|Z_N(Q)-Z_N(P)|. Substitution gives

D_N=(3/4)^(N-2)/32 * (N-1)(1+H_(N-1)) / [1+(1+H_(N-1))(N-3/4)].

Since H_(N-1)>=1, the last ratio lies between 4/7 and 1. Thus (3/4)^(N-2)/56 <= D_N < (3/4)^(N-2)/32. For N>=3, the base value satisfies (N-1)^(N-1) <= Z_N(P) <= N (N-1)^(N-1). Distinguishing this pair using only the genus<=N menu therefore requires absolute resolution smaller than D_N/2 on its only unequal entry. Under a worst-case relative-error oracle, a sufficient condition is error below D_N/[2 max(Z_N(P),Z_N(Q))], while overlapping error intervals obstruct a guarantee at errors of order D_N/Z_N(P). Its relative precision bits grow as (N-1)log2(N-1)+(N-2)log2(4/3)+O(log N)=Theta(N log N). This is a precision lower bound for this fixed topology menu and fixture, not a prohibition on more expensive higher-genus queries or charged sources. Exact-real reconstruction has therefore not been mistaken for a polynomial-bit acquisition procedure.

A related robust obstruction follows directly from continuity: for fixed N and any finite list of genera, Q_(N,eta)'s positive roots approach P_N's roots as eta decreases to zero, and every listed Z_g converges to its P_N value. Yet its symmetry remains trivial for every eta>0 whereas the limiting theory has S2 and the extra annulus direction. Therefore no uniformly reliable finite-error test of this exact categorical property is possible over this unrestricted class. A separation/discriminant promise or a different tolerance-defined target is necessary. No quantitative noisy reconstruction guarantee is claimed.
