RESULT: MONOTONE SITE RESPONSE TO THE TOTAL HALF-HOLE COUNT

Let
  h(x)=sum_{U even} f(U) x^U
be a nonzero multiaffine polynomial with real nonnegative coefficients that is
zero-free when all coordinates have positive real part. Fix disjoint sets S,T
of forced and forbidden holes. On the remaining coordinates C, let
  g(x_C)=(partial_S h)|_{x_j=0, j in T}
be the localized fiber. Assume it is not zero. Give each free coordinate j an
activity w_j>=0, and choose one free coordinate i with activity s>=0. For a
global fugacity tau>0, define the finite probability law

  P_tau(W) proportional to f(S union W) (product_{j in W} w_j)
                         tau^K,
  K=(|S|+|W|)/2,

on W subset C with f(S union W)>0 and |S union W| even. Empty weighted
fibers are excluded. Write I_i=1_{i in W}. Then, for every nonempty weighted
fiber and every tau>0,

  0 <= Cov_tau(I_i,K) <= Var_tau(I_i)
                       = P_tau(I_i=1) P_tau(I_i=0).

In particular, the inclusion probability of any one free hole is a
nondecreasing function of the global half-hole fugacity, even after arbitrary
forced/forbidden coordinate localization. This survives zero coefficients
and boundary activities. The assertion is about covariance with the total
count, not pairwise positive association, configuration-wise monotonicity, or
a sampler.

The upper endpoint is attained already by h(x,y)=1+xy at unit activities:
I_x=K on its two supported configurations, so Cov(I_x,K)=Var(I_x). The lower
endpoint is attained whenever localization fixes i's state.

FULL DERIVATION FOR STRICTLY POSITIVE ACTIVITIES

The standard derivative/specialization limits for half-plane stable
polynomials show g is stable or zero. It is not zero by assumption. Let
epsilon=|S| mod 2. Every monomial of g has degree congruent to epsilon mod 2.
Fix i in C and strictly positive w_j on C\{i}. Decompose g=g_0+x_i g_1
and evaluate the other variables at t w. Their parity gives real polynomials
P,Q such that

  g_0(tw)=t^epsilon P(t^2),
  g_1(tw)=t^(1-epsilon) Q(t^2).

If both pieces are nonzero, each is a stable specialization of g (g_1 is
obtained by a derivative and g_0 by x_i=0), so neither radial polynomial
vanishes for Re(t)>0. The bivariate specialization

  G(u,t)=g(x_i=u, x_{C\{i}}=t w)=g_0(tw)+u g_1(tw)

is zero-free for Re(u),Re(t)>0. Its unique root in u is -Phi(t), where

  Phi(t)=g_0(tw)/g_1(tw)
        = P(t^2)/(t Q(t^2))       if epsilon=0,
        = t P(t^2)/Q(t^2)         if epsilon=1.

Since that root cannot have positive real part, Re(Phi(t))>=0 on Re(t)>0.
Phi is an odd real-rational function. It has no poles in the right half-plane
because g_1(tw) is stable. Oddness excludes left-half-plane poles as well:
any such pole would have a reflected pole in the right half-plane.

Here is the needed positive-real decomposition, including its justification.
After cancelling common factors, every finite pole of Phi is on the imaginary
axis. A pole of order at least two is impossible: its leading Laurent term
would have real part of both signs as the argument approaches the pole from
the right half-plane. At a simple pole, positivity of Re(Phi) on every
right-side approach forces the residue to be real and nonnegative. The
polynomial part at infinity has degree at most one, since a leading term of
degree at least two also has real part of both signs on large right-half-plane
semicircles. Oddness removes a constant term. Pairing the equal residues at
opposite imaginary poles therefore gives

  Phi(t)=a t + b/t + sum_j c_j t/(t^2+lambda_j),
  a,b,c_j >= 0, lambda_j > 0.

(The factor 2 from paired residues is absorbed into c_j.) Consequently, with
z=t^2,

  L(z)=Phi(sqrt(z))/sqrt(z)
      = a + b/z + sum_j c_j/(z+lambda_j),

where the identity is first read for z>0 and then as a rational identity. For
epsilon=0 this is P(z)/(z Q(z)); for epsilon=1 it is P(z)/Q(z). Thus L(z)>0
and

  0 <= -z L'(z) <= L(z),    z>0,

term by term: the constant contributes zero, b/z contributes b/z to both
sides, and z c/(z+lambda)^2 <= c/(z+lambda).

For epsilon=0, the terms excluding i have radial sector polynomial P(z),
while those including i have s z Q(z); hence
  p_i(z)=P_tau(I_i=1)=s z Q(z)/(P(z)+s z Q(z))=s/(s+L(z)).
For epsilon=1 the two terms are z^(r+1)P(z) and s z^(r+1)Q(z), with
r=(|S|-1)/2, giving the same formula p_i(z)=s/(s+L(z)). Therefore

  d p_i / d log(z)
    = s(-zL'(z))/(s+L(z))^2
    = p_i(1-p_i) (-zL'(z)/L(z))
    is in [0,p_i(1-p_i)].

For an exponential-family weight tau^K, differentiation of a marginal gives
  d P_tau(I_i=1)/d log(tau)=Cov_tau(I_i,K).
This proves the displayed covariance bounds. If g_0=0 or g_1=0, every
supported configuration respectively contains i or omits i, so p_i is 1 or
0 and both covariance bounds hold with equality at zero.

ZERO COEFFICIENTS AND BOUNDARY ACTIVITIES

The stability operations above are Hurwitz limits, so a localization can be
identically zero; then there is no probability law and it is explicitly
excluded. No step divides by a coefficient. For a nonempty fiber with some
other w_j=0, replace each such activity by a positive delta and let delta
decrease to zero. The distribution is a ratio of finite polynomials in the
activities; since its limiting partition mass is positive, probabilities and
covariances converge. If s=0, I_i is identically zero. This covers boundary
activities, exact zero sectors, and fibers with only one supported site state.

This is consistent with the INITIAL result: every nonempty localized total
count polynomial is a shifted real-rooted polynomial and has a Poisson-binomial
count marginal. The new statement resolves a different question: it certifies
the sign and a sharp Bernoulli-variance upper bound for the response of each
individual hole indicator to the global count fugacity. It does not provide
coordinate independence or conditional sampling.

ADVERSARIAL LIMIT: NOT PAIRWISE POSITIVE ASSOCIATION

For n=4, the exact ambient-hole coefficient vector
  [5,0,0,0,0,1,0,0,0,0,5,0,8,0,0,1]
comes from the Gaussian-integer matrix recorded in
weighted_hole_total_covariance_checks.py. At unit activities, exact rational
arithmetic gives Cov(I_0,I_3)=-1/50. Thus the nonnegative Cov(I_i,K) result
does not imply pairwise positive association. In fact, the ambient-hole
distribution retains negative pair covariance. The n=2 polynomial 1+xy and
its negative log-Hessian determinant for xy<1, already recorded in INITIAL,
also continues to block a transfer to full multivariate log-concavity.

EXECUTABLE DIAGNOSTICS AND COST

Run:
  python3 work/cycle6/c02_l02/weighted_hole_total_covariance_checks.py

The script uses exact Fraction arithmetic on all forced/forbidden/free status
assignments in four dimension-6 determinant fixtures, with fugacities 1/3, 1,
and 3; it also checks two coordinate-pair auxiliary fixtures and zero-valued
activities. It checked 27,279 coordinate/fugacity marginals; all satisfied
0<=Cov(I_i,K)<=Var(I_i). Empty coefficient fibers and weighted fibers
annihilated by boundary activities are counted separately. The dimension-4
negative-pair-covariance control was exactly -1/50. The results are in
weighted_hole_total_covariance_checks.json.

These computations are finite diagnostics, not proof; the derivation above is
the universal argument. Their CPU and storage cost is negligible at n=6, but
the underlying exact determinant fixtures use bounded enumeration over at
most six coordinates. No root-finding, partition evaluation algorithm, or
sampling algorithm is claimed. Computing L or the marginal itself may require
the same unevaluated sector partition sums; no evaluation oracle is supplied,
so no FPRAS, annealing runtime, or microscopic hole kernel follows.
