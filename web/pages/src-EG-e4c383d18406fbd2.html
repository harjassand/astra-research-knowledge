Strongest retained root result
-------------------------------
Root independently derived a sharp constrained thermal-state preparation bound.
For k equal-frequency bosonic modes initially thermal with occupation n, require
every final local occupation to remain n while a balanced rank-r collective
subspace has occupation at most nu<n. The exact minimum free-energy increase is

  W_bal = k_B*T*[k*g(n)-r*g(nu)-(k-r)*g(b)],
  b=(k*n-r*nu)/(k-r), g(x)=(x+1)ln(x+1)-x ln(x).

The entropy proof covers arbitrary non-Gaussian competing states. An explicit
Gaussian state attains the entropy bound. W_bal is a work lower bound and an
ideal reversible-control infimum, not a finite-time engineering equality.
It also obeys

  W_bal-W_cold=(k-r)*k_B*T*D(tau_b || tau_n)>=0,

where W_cold is the ideal work to cool r modes directly from n to nu. The latter
comparison has a different final local-port contract and does not compare complete
converters. Warm delivery loss eta adds selected occupation (1-eta)*n; calibration
misalignment also couples in the hot complementary modes. Example: n=100,nu=0.1,
k=2,r=1 gives W_bal/(k_B*T)=4.58489 versus direct cooling 4.28102; 1% independent
warm loss raises selected occupation to 1.099.

This identifies the low-noise resource behind correlated thermal inputs. It does
not show that the minimum work is prohibitively large, invalidate conditional
scattering calculations, or forbid feedback/common-bath dark modes. Entropy and
free-energy principles are established; historical novelty of this exact
constrained formula is UNKNOWN. It is not advertised as a new scientific power.
Proof: root/THERMAL_RESOURCE_THEOREM.txt. The physical Sol worker independently
checked the proof and the direct-cooling subtraction.

Root's other constructive attempts and decisive tests
----------------------------------------------------
1. Rare-transition certification. Root proposed a potential/unit-flow enclosure
   and then a normalized cross-flux sampler q_e=j_e*Delta u_e. On an acyclic
   monotone flow, sample a flow path and intersect it at a uniform potential level.
   This constructs q without an equilibrium normalization constant. With certified
   full energy support, weights a=c*Delta u/j and b=j/(c*Delta u) give
   [1/E(b),E(a)] as a capacity enclosure. A two-channel example proves that a
   normalized feasible flow can entirely miss an arbitrarily large conducting
   channel, invalidating the sampled upper bound. Berman-Konsowa already supplies
   the flow-induced path law and a lower bound at least as strong as the Thomson
   endpoint. No advantage over that matched baseline survives.
   Derivation: root/CROSS_FLUX_SAMPLER.txt; worker review: rare_event_opening/.

2. Acquisition lower bound. Root proved a precise named-channel oracle bound:
   on M parallel two-edge channels, an algorithm giving a relative-width
   1+epsilon capacity interval with success >=1-alpha on every instance must make
   at least M*(1-2*alpha) expected queries on the null instance. A hidden enhanced
   channel changes capacity beyond the interval width while all other responses
   agree. The proof is an elementary coupling/testing argument. It does not apply
   to an oracle supplying aggregate exit rates, weighted neighbors or a full
   generator; those are stronger acquired interfaces.
   Proof: root/HIDDEN_CHANNEL_BOUND.txt.

3. Internally prestressed separator. Root constructed a compressed conducting
   disk surrounded by a tensile shrink-fit ring and derived its exact ideal
   Lamé stress, interference and fill-fraction constraints. A traction-free body
   has integral sigma=0. Thus ceramic fraction f compressed by P needs
   f*P<=(1-f)*S in a tensile counterpart of allowable stress S. The circular ring
   imposes the sharper f<=(S-P)/(S+P). This accounts for the balancing force and
   exposes transport, yield, insulation, buckling and relaxation obligations.
   The targeted follow-up then found that the motivating Cui experiment already
   uses a retained shape-memory-alloy ring. Thermal-mismatch LLZO laminates and
   stress-balanced layers also have explicit earlier disclosures. The proposed
   architecture is rejected as a new mechanism.
   Derivation: root/PRESTRESSED_SEPARATOR.txt; corrections: scientific_obstruction/
   PRESTRESS_TRANSFER.md and computation_opening/PRESTRESS_PRIOR_ART.md.
