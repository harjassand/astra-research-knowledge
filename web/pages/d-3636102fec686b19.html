# Metabolic flux thermodynamics: result and status

## Result

I did not find a defensible new general theorem about metabolic flux thermodynamics. The starting proposal—bounding steady-state flux by a Gibbs-affinity margin and a mass-balance residual—is the direct identity `vᵀSᵀμ = μᵀSv`; classic cycle-based thermodynamic FBA and later integrated stoichiometric/thermodynamic/kinetic formulations already cover its substance.

The stronger candidate is a narrow counterexample to a tempting deterministic-to-stochastic transfer. An explicit reversible, detailed-balanced mass-action network has one globally attracting deterministic equilibrium in the positive stoichiometric class `B+C=2`, but infinitely many closed, irreducible, positive-recurrent count classes in that same real class. Their exact stationary distributions escape every common compact set. This is a reversible completion of Astra U/N77’s source-reported one-way example. It is a model-level candidate, not an externally novel result; a targeted prior-art search did not certify priority.

The load-bearing point is that deterministic stoichiometric classes and stochastic communicating classes are different objects. Local detailed balance removes net cyclic flux but does not force different stochastic components inside one deterministic class to have nearby stationary laws.

## Candidate statement

See [CANDIDATE_PROOF.md](CANDIDATE_PROOF.md) for the full network, assumptions, exact stationary law, and proof. In brief, the four reversible pairs are

`2C ⇄ A+B+C`, `A+B+C ⇄ 3A+2B`, `3A+2B ⇄ 2C`, and `2C ⇄ 2C+D`, with all dimensionless forward/reverse constants equal to one. The class `B+C=2` has detailed-balanced equilibrium `(1,1,1,1)` and the complex-balanced global-attractor theorem applies. For each integer `n≥0`, the CTMC has the closed class

`Γ_n = {(n,0,2,d), (n+1,1,1,d), (n+3,2,0,d) : d≥0}`.

The invariant `A−B(B+1)/2=n` is not a deterministic invariant. The stationary law is the unit product-Poisson law restricted to `Γ_n`; in particular `A≥n` almost surely and the family is not tight.

To give the formal model an elemental balance, the proof note includes a chemostatted balancing species `Q` at unit activity. It leaves the internal dynamics unchanged. The example uses high-molecularity reactions and is not claimed to describe a natural metabolic pathway.

## Thermodynamic and kinetic reconciliation

The internal reaction vectors satisfy `ν₁+ν₂+ν₃=0`. Any exact internal steady flux has `v₁=v₂=v₃` and `v₄=0`. Since reaction free energies are potential differences, `ΔG₁+ΔG₂+ΔG₃=0`. If each active net flux is strictly downhill, summing `vᵣΔGᵣ≤0` forces zero net cycle flux; under the chosen reversible mass-action law zero affinity also means zero net flux. The CTMC nonetheless has positive forward and reverse *gross event rates* in stationarity, equal for every pair. This separates loopless net flux from stochastic reaction turnover without claiming a thermodynamic violation.

## Falsifier and costs

At class index `n`, the predicted phase probabilities are proportional to

`(1/2, 1/(n+1), 1/[2(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)])`;

`D` is an independent `Poisson(1)`, and every oriented channel has stationary event rate `1/Z_n`, where `Z_n` is the sum of those three weights. Any observed reaction transition that changes `B+C` or `A−B(B+1)/2` falsifies the exact network/propensity model. After burn-in, phase counts and the `D` histogram can be compared with the stated probabilities. The rare top phase has probability asymptotic to `n⁻³`; obtaining one independent observation there with probability at least `0.95` takes about `3n³` stationary samples. The fastest top-phase propensities are also `Θ(n³)`, so time resolution and event logging must be charged. The full protocol and limitations are in [EXPERIMENT_AND_COSTS.md](EXPERIMENT_AND_COSTS.md).

## Prior-art verdict

The broad flux theorem route is rejected as duplicative. Beard–Liang–Qian’s Theorem 6.1 characterizes thermodynamic steady fluxes through cycle constraints; Fleming et al. already combine mass balance, energy conservation, second-law inequalities, reversible kinetics, and bounded parameters in one formulation. Loopless FVA/TFA/PTA also treat thermodynamically infeasible cycles and uncertainty.

The counterexample uses established results: complex-balanced mass-action systems globally attract their positive stoichiometric-class equilibrium, and the Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz theorem gives a product-Poisson stationary law on every closed irreducible count class. Those results do not themselves give the displayed escaping family. Astra U/N77 already reports the same escape phenomenon for a one-way complex-balanced cycle; the reversible completion here makes detailed balance explicit but may be an immediate, previously known construction. No novelty or priority claim is made.
