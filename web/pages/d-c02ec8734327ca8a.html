EXACT PRIMARY-SOURCE THEOREM

I retrieved the pinned primary PDF for OpenAI math family 093,
“A dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for subgaussian log-concave
measures,” at commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a, from:
https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-dimension-free-logarithmic-Sobolev-inequality-for-subgaussian-log-concave-measures-September-23-2026/paper.pdf
The paper's Section 7, Proposition 7.2, states the exact Gaussian-observation
information upper used in the critical contradiction. Its input is the
specific counterexample sequence, not an arbitrary bounded-covariance law:

- Y_t has stationary law eta, with eta satisfying LSI at parameter lambda and
  lambda -> 1 along the sequence.
- R(y) is a PSD matrix field constructed from the preceding tensor hierarchy;
  E tr R^2 -> 1, E|DR|^2 -> 1.
- Its derivatives have Sylvester form D_iR=(Hhat_i R+R Hhat_i)/2, and
  sum_i E||[Hhat_i,R]||_HS^2=o(1).
- For fixed L>=1, before the sequence limit, C_T(y)=I+f_L(T R(y)^2),
  f_L(q)=q/(1+q/L), and Z_T|Y_t is centered Gaussian with covariance C_T.

Then the source proves
  limsup_sequence integral_0^infinity I(Y_t;Z_T) dT/T^2 <= 9/4
for each fixed t>0 and fixed L>=1. The limit order is essential: first fix L,
then send the sequence index to infinity. The statement is not uniform for
L=L_n growing with the sequence from the displayed estimates alone.

Proof skeleton independently rechecked from the source equations:
1. LSI applied to the square root of the observation posterior gives
   I(Y_t;Z_T) <= (1/(4 lambda)) E sum_i tr(C_T^-1 D_iC_T C_T^-1 D_iC_T).
   The Gaussian score calculation uses E(g*Bg-tr B)^2=2 tr(B^2).
2. In an eigenbasis of R with eigenvalues v,w>=0,
   c_T(v)=1+f_L(Tv^2)=(1+bTv^2)/(1+aTv^2), a=1/L, b=1+1/L,
   and the divided difference is
   (c_T(v)-c_T(w))/(v-w)=T(v+w)/[(1+aTv^2)(1+aTw^2)].
3. Integration in T with weight dT/T^2 gives the multiplier M_L(v,w)
   printed in Lemma 7.1: M_L<=9 when 1/2<=v/w<=2, and M_L<=4log(1+L)
   for all pairs. Comparable pairs therefore cost at most 9 E|DR|^2.
   Outside that spectral band, the Sylvester equation gives
   |(D_iR)_vw|^2 <= (9/4)|[Hhat_i,R]_{vw}|^2; after integration their
   contribution is at most 4log(1+L)o(1). Taking the sequence limit at fixed
   L removes this term. Finally lambda->1 and E|DR|^2->1 give 9/4.
4. The source explicitly treats Sobolev R by the chain rule because C_T has
   spectrum in [1,1+L]; no additional numerical constant is introduced by
   the finite-dimensional form-domain approximation.

This validates the primary source's *stated mechanism and interface*. It is
not my independent proof of the earlier tensor hierarchy, the critical law,
or the LSI premise. Nor is the source's use of LSI a consequence of a
Poincare bound: it comes from the preceding subgaussian-log-concave LSI
assumption/contradiction setup.

WHAT THE THREE C09 SOL INITIALS DO AND DO NOT ESTABLISH

c09_s01 and c09_s02 supply analytically argued negative examples against a
generic replacement of observation-likelihood LSI by a covariance/Poincare or
Fisher-energy bound. Their interfaces are broader than Fisher spectra, and
are relevant counterexamples to the shortcut “bounded covariance + bounded
Poincare implies integrated information control.” They do not, on their
stated boundaries, refute family093 Proposition 7.2: they do not satisfy its
near-LSI premise, and c09_s01's scalar logistic field does not have the
source's sharp E|DR|^2 -> 1 saturation. They also do not construct the exact
matrix field from the critical tensor hierarchy or its full source-side
constraints. Thus they refute a generic covariance-only theorem, not the
specific source proposition or KLS.

c09_s03 proposes a different positive theorem for A_T=I+f_L(TB):
  integral_0^infinity I(Y;Z_T)dT/T
       <= L^2/24 E||log B-E log B||_HS^2,
for positive definite B and finite log-field second moment, with a Poincare
corollary when log B has the stated Sobolev regularity. The proof route is
plausible on direct inspection: maximum Gaussian entropy, a Hessian bound for
-logdet on matrices >=I, scalar integration of the rational saturation, and
the spectral-overlap identity for noncommuting B,B'. Its scale measure is
dT/T, whereas the load-bearing primary Proposition 7.2 uses dT/T^2 and a
specific R^2 field. It therefore does not fill that exact gap unless a new
change-of-measure/field argument proves the missing conversion and verifies
finite log-field variance uniformly. No such conversion appears in the
INITIAL. Its rational script checks finite identities; it cannot certify the
general theorem. (A verifier run during this audit rewrote that peer-owned
script's JSON output; I notified root immediately. No peer file will be
changed again.)

EXACT REMAINING THEOREMS, WITH SEPARATE SCOPE

A. For the older N75 route, the specific missing result is a replacement for
the LSI step in primary Proposition 7.2 for the critical field R generated by
the tensor hierarchy: prove the same fixed-cap, dT/T^2 scale-integrated bound
from the admissible covariance/Poincare/first-gap/small-curvature structure,
or give a counterexample satisfying all those source-specific hypotheses.
Generic Poincare-only control is false by c09_s01/s02. The full N75 technical
note was absent, so I cannot state its exact competing premises or claim a

new BKL KLS preprint is correct.

B. For “statistical memory beyond Fisher spectra,” a separate missing theorem
is uniform acquisition/reconstruction from the full joint posterior under an
explicit observation and resource interface. Same Fisher eigenvalues alone
are demonstrably inadequate, but neither a Poincare bound nor the family093
mutual-information inequality is itself an archive codec or a general
joint-posterior theorem. Need specify supplied versus learned channel,
posterior access, classical/quantum memory, error criterion, and charged
sample/bit/precision costs before stating a precise universal target.

C. Separately, Bizeul–Klartag–Lehec arXiv:2610.05474v1 (4 Oct 2026) claims

counterexample, but the proof is not externally validated here; in particular
stopped Ito/expectation removal with inverse-covariance coefficients and
imported Letwin / Song–Zhang proofs require full audit. Thus KLS is now a
strong primary-source claim, not an established field fact in this report.

A useful exact mismatch check: under B -> cB for c>0, the dT/T integral is
unchanged by T' = cT, while the dT/T^2 integral is multiplied by c. Thus the
S03 theorem cannot be converted to the primary weighted integral by a harmless
scale renaming. In addition, S03 assumes B>0 a.s. (or a removable common
nullspace with finite log moments); the primary R^2 may be singular, and the
source exploits that its kernel-kernel derivative is zero. These are genuine
interface differences, not just notation.
