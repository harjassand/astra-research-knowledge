GENERIC GRAM LEMMA
Suppose positive Bernstein measure P(x)dx has P>=w>0 on I=[1/4,3/4].
For g(x)=sum_(i=0)^d v_i x^i(1-x)^(d-i),
 integral_I g(x)^2 dx >= ||v||_2^2/[2(d+1)^2 64^d].         (1)

To prove(1), put u=2x-1/2 and expand f(u)=g(1/4+u/2) in shifted Legendre
polynomials R_l(u),0<=l<=d, with coefficients a_l. Orthogonality gives
 integral_I g^2=(1/2)sum_l a_l^2/(2l+1).
The degree-l homogeneous Bernstein expansion of R_l has coefficients
(-1)^(l-j)binom(l,j)^2. Substitute u=(3/2)x-(1/2)y,
1-u=-(1/2)x+(3/2)y. The coefficient l1 norm of each substituted monomial
is at most2^l. Summing binom(l,j)^2=binom(2l,l)<=4^l gives8^l. Elevating
to homogeneous degree d by(x+y)^(d-l) gives coefficient l1 norm
<=8^l 2^(d-l)<=8^d. Thus ||v||_2<=8^d sum_l |a_l|. Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 sum_l|a_l|<=(d+1)sqrt[sum_l a_l^2/(2l+1)].
Combining these two estimates proves(1), with all normalizations explicit.

APPLICATION TO THE TWO HANKEL FORMS

For N=2n+1, the forms are integral P(1-x)g_n^2 and integral P x g_n^2.
