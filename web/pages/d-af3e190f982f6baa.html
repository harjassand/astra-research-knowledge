N09 GLASS MARGINALITY — APPEND-ONLY PRECISION SUPPLEMENT v2
Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: make the measure and regularity assumptions in frozen v1 explicit.
The v1 record and its companion diagnostic.py are unchanged.

1. Exact hypotheses behind the exponent bookkeeping
----------------------------------------------------

The formula in v1 is best read as a local asymptotic statement on a compact
positive chi interval. Let the post-chart, pre-preparation density satisfy

    p0(kappa,tau,chi) = kappa^a h(kappa,tau,chi),   kappa>0,

with h tending to a finite nonzero limit on the region being integrated. Let
the protocol weight be W_p=kappa^q w(tau,chi)(1+o(1)), with q>-a and all
unrestricted tau/chi integrals finite and nonzero. Then the unrestricted
curvature density scales as kappa^(a+q), giving

    D(omega) ~ omega^(2(a+q)+1).

For a gate |tau|<=sqrt(3 kappa chi), an additional near-zero condition is
needed: uniformly over the compact chi interval,

    w(tau,chi) ~ c(chi)|tau|^b,   b>-1,

and c(chi) must have a finite positive integral against the chi measure. Then

    integral_{|tau|<=sqrt(3 kappa chi)} |tau|^b d tau
       = 2 (3 kappa chi)^((b+1)/2)/(b+1),

so p(kappa)~kappa^(a+q+(b+1)/2) and

    D(omega)~omega^(2a+2q+b+2).

For the smooth quartic-jet chart a=1. If h is singular in tau, chi reaches
zero with its own singularity, the tau support itself depends on kappa, or the
protocol weight has correlations outside this factorization, the displayed
power need not hold. These conditions are exactly why fitting q or b is not a
preparation mechanism.

2. Exact hypotheses behind the barrier CDF
-------------------------------------------

For B=2 kappa^3/(3 tau^2)(1+o(1)), a clean sufficient condition is that,
conditional on kappa, tau has a limiting density h(tau) supported in
tau_*<=|tau|<=tau^* for constants 0<tau_*<tau^*<infinity, with convergence
uniform enough to exchange the small-kappa limit and the tau integral. If
p(kappa)~C kappa, then

    Pr(B<b) ~ (C/2)(3/2)^(2/3) b^(2/3)
                integral |tau|^(4/3) h(tau) d tau.

The support-away-from-zero condition can be relaxed if the conditional laws
are uniformly integrable in the |tau|^(4/3) moment and the tau->0 sector gives
an asymptotically smaller contribution. Without one of these controls, a
singular concentration at tau=0 can change the barrier exponent. Likewise,
if tau scales with kappa, use the exact stationary roots and do not use
B~kappa^3/tau^2 uniformly. The analytic barrier expansion itself only needs
chi bounded, tau nonzero for the sampled sequence, and bounded higher
derivatives near y=0.

3. Basin measure domain
----------------------

For the deterministic basin integral in v1, take a smooth finite-dimensional
potential modulo translational zero modes, nondegenerate critical points, an
initial measure absolutely continuous with respect to configuration-space
volume, and gradient trajectories converging to minima almost surely. Then
stable manifolds of saddles have zero initial measure and the minima basins
partition the initial ensemble up to a null set. Inertial dynamics uses phase
space and its actual dissipative flow; a finite-temperature schedule uses its
transition kernel rather than the static gradient basin. These definitions
preserve protocol dependence; they do not assume basin probabilities are
uniform.

4. Elastic and probe regularity conditions
------------------------------------------

The dipole bath calculation presumes an elastic continuum down to q=0, finite
nonzero dipole tensor, finite sound speeds, and a finite renormalized Z. For
d>2, the q-integrals giving the static self-energy and the coefficient of
omega^2 are infrared convergent. With the normalized x-coordinate spectral
measure, a resonance kernel of unit mass and relative width Gamma/omega->0
preserves any locally smooth oscillator density D0(omega)~A omega^beta under
convolution. The local projected force measure further presumes that the
probe-overlap variance with a QLM core approaches a finite positive constant;
otherwise it contributes its own power or logarithm. A compact force with
zero monopole and dipole moments has a phonon overlap proportional to q^2,
and hence S_f^ph~omega^(d+3); this does not mean every laboratory actuator has
that moment structure. The proposed omega^6 phonon background is specifically
the d=3 quadrupole-probe prediction.

These controls establish only conditional robustness against a weak single-
defect elastic bath. They do not prove that a real glass's defect-defect
interactions, rare collective events, non-harmonic aging, or preparation map
obey the independent-resonance hypotheses.
