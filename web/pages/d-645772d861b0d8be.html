MAIN FINDING

The covariance-only heat plateau can be proved directly for compact, centered, full-dimensional log-concave laws, without importing the source paper's log-Sobolev theorem or assuming KLS. If X~mu in R^n, Sigma=Cov(X), sigma^2=||Sigma||op, mu_s=mu*N(0,s I), and P_s is the optimal Poincare constant in Var(f)<=P_s E|grad f|^2, then

  0 <= P_s' <= sigma^2 P_s/(P_s-s)^2   for a.e. s>0,
  P_0 <= P_s <= P_0 + sigma^2 s/(P_0-s)   for 0<s<P_0.

The proof is recorded in full in work/programmes/spectral_geometry/leaves/L01_spectral_derivative.txt. Its load-bearing identities are the posterior covariance bounds 0<=Db_s<=I and 0<=K_s<=I/s; the gradient-eigenfunction estimate E<grad phi,K_s grad phi><=lambda_s |E grad phi|^2; the exact heat-contact identity (1-lambda_s s)E[phi Y]=E[phi b_s(Y)]; and the transported-Rayleigh-quotient derivative P_s'=P_s^2 E<grad phi,K_s grad phi>. The covariance contraction E[b_s b_s^T]<=Sigma then yields the derivative bound. Domain, eigenfunction, and initial-time arguments are included. This establishes the heat plateau as an internally proved candidate under the stated hypotheses; it is not itself a KLS counterexample or a KLS proof.

A second concrete result isolates a non-LSI route for the observation problem. For any random Y and centered conditional Gaussian Z|Y~N(0,C(Y)), with C(Y) positive definite and finite log determinants,

  I(Y;Z) + D(Law(Z)||N(0,E C(Y)))
    = (1/2)[log det E C(Y) - E log det C(Y)].

Thus the scale-integrated mutual information is at most one half of an integrated matrix log-det Jensen gap. For the capped N75 profile C_T=I+f_L(TA), A=R^2, f_L(u)=Lu/(L+u), a uniform bound on

  J_L(A)=int_0^infty [log det(I+E f_L(TA))-E log det(I+f_L(TA))] dT/T^2

would suffice. This route is independent of KLS and gradient inequalities, but the plateau does not currently control J_L, and its uniform-in-cap estimate is not proved. See L19.

MODELS AND FALSIFIERS

No explicit compact log-concave family tested reaches sigma^2/P->0 or falsifies the heat plateau. Cube products have P/sigma^2=12/pi^2; the isotropic regular simplex and cross-polytope have dimension-free P by class-specific prior results, so their ratios stay bounded away from zero. Radial log-concave laws also have P comparable to their scalar covariance by an established radial spectral-gap bound. The tests in L15-L16 are not source-field tests. For the declared normalized diagonal field R=diag(|X_i|)/sqrt(d), L10 proves the exact integrated Fisher identity K_F(L)d^-1 sum_i E[X_i^2/(X_i^2+epsilon^2)]<=K_F(L)<=2 and the cap-uniform MI bound d^-1 sum_i(E X_i^4/4+log 2) at epsilon=0. This uses conditional-coordinate subadditivity and the scale change s=T/d; without the 1/sqrt(d) normalization the energy grows with d. L15 separately proves a cap-independent radial toy-channel bound (d/2)Cov(Q,log Q). The exact results and limits are in L10, L15, and L16.
