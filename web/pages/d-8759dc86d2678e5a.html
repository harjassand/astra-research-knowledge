## First load-bearing lemma: independent differences preserve the operator promise

Put Y=(X_1−X_2)/√2 for independent observations. Its mean is exactly zero and Cov(Y)=Σ=AAᵀ. If c=Eτ⁴ and β_i=cκ4(η_i)>0, the original source fourth moment is3c||u||⁴+Σβ_i u_i⁴. For the latent difference Z_d=(Z_1−Z_2)/√2, independence and symmetry give, for every u,

    E(uᵀZ_d)⁴=(1/4)[2E(uᵀZ)⁴+6(E(uᵀZ)²)²]
               =3c_d||u||⁴+Σβ_di u_i⁴,
    c_d=(c+1)/2, β_di=β_i/2>0.                     (1)

Thus the covariance is unchanged and the positive diagonal excess gap halves. The difference law need not itself be representable as one common scale times independent variables. Only its exactly proved second/fourth moments are used subsequently. Its deterministic envelope is ||Y||≤D/√2. Mean estimation and its fourth-order error expansion are removed entirely, at the charged cost of disjoint pairs and half the excess gap. Pair symmetrization is established prior methodology.

## Preserved concentration theorem and exact confidence function

Wang–Ramdas, *Sharp Matrix Empirical Bernstein Inequalities*, arXiv2411.09516v5, Theorem3.1, Eqs(15)–(17), applies to even S independent symmetric matrices T_i with spectra in[0,1], common mean/variance. Define paired variance

    V*=S⁻¹ Σ_(j=1)^(S/2) (T_(2j−1)−T_(2j))².

Let v be a certified upper bound for ||V*||op and

    L=log(Sd/((S−1)α)), J=log(2Sd/α),
    EB(S,d,v,α)=L/(3S)+√(2vL/S)+(√(5/3)+1)√(LJ)/S. (2)

The theorem gives an upper eigenvalue tail of size α. Applying it also to I−T_i, whose paired variance is exactly the same, gives

    ||mean(T)−ET||op≤EB(S,d,v,α/2)

with failure at most α. The genuine independent pairing order is retained; sorting multinomial counts into artificial consecutive pairs does not supply this statistic. Fixed sample counts are used; optional stopping on these fixed-time radii is not licensed.

## Independent fourth-moment sample and simultaneous coverage

Use the remaining2N observations for fresh differences, independent of the pilot. Conditional on the entire pilot, W is fixed. Form z_i=WᵀY_i and traceless quadratic features

    φ_i=P0(z_iz_iᵀ), P0(M)=M−tr(M)I/n,
    H0=Sym_n∩I⊥, d0=n(n+1)/2−1.

Their squared Frobenius norms equal (1−1/n)||z_i||⁴ and are at most

    H=(1−1/n)U² K_w⁴.

Hence T4_i=|φ_i><φ_i|/H are PSD operators on H0 with spectra in[0,1]. Their conditional mean is the population trace-free fourth-moment operator K_cand/H. Compute Khat=N⁻¹Σ|φ_i><φ_i| and its genuine paired normalized variance upper bound v4. Then, conditional on every pilot outcome,

    ||Khat−K_cand||op≤ε4,
    ε4=H EB(N,d0,v4,α4/2)                         (5)

has failure at most α4. Integrating this conditional guarantee and unioning (3) gives a single event of probability at least1−α2−α4. ## Costs, controls and unresolved affordability

The actual observation count is exactly2(M+N). There is no count-compressed sampling substitution. Pilot accumulation costs O(Mn²), dense fourth-operator/paired-variance accumulation O(Nd0²)=O(Nn⁴), storage O(d0²)=O(n⁴), and dense spectral/certificate linear algebra up to O(d0³)=O(n⁶), before precision/refinement and candidate joint diagonalization. A rank-two formula makes each paired variance update O(d0²), avoiding an unnecessary d0³ square per pair. All operator norm and eigenvalue scalars in the output need outward bounds; mere floating eigenvalues are diagnostics, not certificates.

Ordinary covariance/eigenmatrix joint diagonalization and MaxKurt get the same raw samples, pairing, pilot metric, physical model/support promise and posterior(3)–(7). The certificate is shared. An SOS proposal has the same data/confidence but its own full optimization cost; no SOS solve was performed. Repeated β_i do not invalidate the full true diagonal subspace, but they remove a simple single-eigenvector axis acquisition shortcut. Proposal success or practical runtime has not been proved here.

The known support term in(2) is proportional to H log(Nd0/α4)/N. For isotropic bounded coordinates with D² proportional to n, H is proportional to n². Therefore even a tiny observed paired variance does not avoid an n²/N range charge. The actual gap halves under differences, and covariance/transport confidence can dominate. The new posterior may be substantially more informative than the earlier full-tensor Frobenius certificate, but that requires an actual same-data execution or a quantitative theorem, not cosmetically smaller constants.
