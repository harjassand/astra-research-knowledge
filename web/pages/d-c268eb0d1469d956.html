INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE ACKN EXAMPLE 3.1 LOCAL INWARD CERTIFICATE

The following calculations are exact. A collar convention caveat appears
in section 4; it matters if the certificate is meant to hold outside Q.

1. Drift and Jacobian

For empty -> 2A+B -> 4A+4B -> A with deterministic constants (1,5,4),
the reaction vectors are (2,1),(2,3),(-3,-4), and therefore

    f_a=2+10a^2 b-12a^4 b^4,
    f_b=1+15a^2 b-16a^4 b^4.

At c=(1,1), f(c)=0 and

    J=[[-28,-38],[-34,-49]].

Its trace is -77 and determinant 80, so the equilibrium is asymptotically
stable by the linearization theorem.

2. Hessian and Taylor remainder

The Hessian entries are

    f_a,aa=20b-144a^2b^4,
    f_a,ab=20a-192a^3b^3,
    f_a,bb=-144a^4b^2,
    f_b,aa=30b-192a^2b^4,
    f_b,ab=30a-256a^3b^3,
    f_b,bb=-192a^4b^2.

On [.99,1.01]^2 the largest of the elementary absolute-value triangle
bounds is

    30(1.01)+256(1.01)^6=302.049158553856<310.

Thus |H_ij|<=310 for both components throughout this box. Put

    epsilon=1/10000,
    Q={|a-1|<=7 epsilon/5, |b-1|<=epsilon}.

For h=x-c in Q, Taylor's theorem gives, componentwise,

    |R_i| <= (310/2)(|h_a|+|h_b|)^2
            <=155(12epsilon/5)^2
            =(4464/5)epsilon^2
            =0.08928 epsilon.

3. Facet slabs inside Q

For ell=epsilon/100, consider the upper-a slab inside Q, namely

    7epsilon/5-ell <= h_a <= 7epsilon/5,
    |h_b|<=epsilon.

Then

    f_a <= [-28(7/5)+38+28/100+0.08928]epsilon
         =-0.83072 epsilon.

For the upper-b slab inside Q, namely epsilon-ell<=h_b<=epsilon,
|h_a|<=7epsilon/5,

    f_b <= [34(7/5)-49+49/100+0.08928]epsilon
         =-0.82072 epsilon.

The lower-a and lower-b slabs have the reversed bounds with exactly
the same positive margins. Consequently gamma=(4/5)epsilon works on
all four facet slabs INSIDE Q. The normals are coordinate unit normals.

4. If an ambient collar extends tangential coordinates too

If the intended collar means an ambient ell-neighborhood of the facets,
then |h_a| can be as large as (7/5+1/100)epsilon and |h_b| as large as
(1+1/100)epsilon. In that convention the preceding constants cannot be
used unchanged. A valid uniform Taylor bound is

    |R_i| <=155(2.42epsilon)^2=0.0907742epsilon.

The normal coordinate may move inward by ell and the tangential
coordinate may move outward by ell. Hence guaranteed margins are

    upper/lower a: [1.2-(28+38)/100-0.0907742]epsilon
                  =0.4492258epsilon;
    upper/lower b: [1.4-(34+49)/100-0.0907742]epsilon
                  =0.4792258epsilon.

Thus gamma=0.8epsilon is not certified for that larger ambient collar.
Either use gamma=0.4epsilon with ell=epsilon/100, or use
ell=epsilon/1000. For the latter the guaranteed margins become
1.044571138epsilon and 1.227571138epsilon, and gamma=0.8epsilon works.
This caveat does not affect strict inward drift on Q itself.

5. Fixed-volume transience inference

ACKN's stochastic mass-action convention is falling factorial without
a reactant-factorial divisor. The source molecularities are 0,3,8.
Classical volume scaling therefore gives effective count-level constants

    kappa_0=V, kappa_1=5/V^2, kappa_2=4/V^7.

All are strictly positive for every fixed finite V>0. Example 3.1's
transience statement applies to each such parameter choice. The
scaled count process has the displayed deterministic drift in the
large-volume limit; falling-factorial rates mean it is asymptotic,
rather than exact polynomial density dependence at finite V.
