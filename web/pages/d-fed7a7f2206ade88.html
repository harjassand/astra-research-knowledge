FINAL — c05_l01 — PRIMARY STABILITY/LIFTING AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-07. Requested model gpt-6-luna/max; observed model UNKNOWN.

STATUS

The audit found no primary theorem that gives a dimension-independent EB
approximation of the entire growing CP image of the operator-norm unit ball
in normalized infinity-to-one trace norm. This is a scoped source finding,
not proof that no such theorem exists and not a counterexample to the
broadcasting conjecture.

The strongest new consequence is a fixed-real-range-rank channel theorem:
Hadwin–Li's fixed finite-tuple tracial stability, combined with a
maximum-volume basis and pinching of the original UCP map onto the commuting
approximants' abelian algebra, gives one trace-preserving EB map with error
<2 eta whenever the full-image commutator defect is below a threshold
depending on range rank r and eta. Bansil–Kachkovskiy yields an explicit
fixed-rank alternative for M_d, with a rapidly deteriorating rank-dependent
exponent. Neither result controls r growing with d.

The abelian lifting theorem in Hadwin–Li is a different interface: it lifts
trace-preserving *-homomorphisms from countably generated abelian von
Neumann algebras into ultraproducts of diffuse coordinate algebras. It does
not lift an approximately commuting UCP channel image to an EB channel.
Raynaud's result identifies L_p ultrapowers; it is not a channel-lifting
theorem. The frozen INITIAL bibliographically misattributed “A Note on
Approximate Liftings” to Hadwin–Shulman; this FINAL corrects its authors to
Hadwin–Li, preserving the immutable INITIAL and recording the repair in the
revision.
