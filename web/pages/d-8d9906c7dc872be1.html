Me, My Math Proofs and AI – Part II

August 7, 2026

Uncategorized

I have three new results on Maxwell Bounds. I used AI tools to write code to verify several identities in these papers. AI tools also helped me verify several of my formulae and derivations.

1. A Superlinear Lower Bound in Maxwell’s Equilibrium Problem: The best known lower bound on M(n) is 10n − 26, for n ≥ 3 odd, and 10n − 36, n ≥ 4 even. I improved this lower bound to n^{3/2}. This is the first superlinear lower bound.

Paper link: https://shivakintali.github.io/papers/Maxwell-Lower-Bound.pdf

2. A Sharp Maxwell Bound for Three Point Charges: Gabrielov, Novikov, Novikov, Shapiro improved M(3) <= 12 to M(3) <=6. In my paper, I proved that M(3) = 4. This proves that Maxwell’s conjecture is true for n=3.

Paper link: https://shivakintali.github.io/papers/Maxwell3.pdf

3. An Eight-Charge Counterexample to the Maxwell Bound: Recently Arathoon, Ball and Kvalheim gave the first counterexample to Maxwell Conjecture. Their counterexample works for n=5 and n=7. In my paper I extended their construction to give a counterexample for n=8.

Paper link: https://shivakintali.github.io/papers/Maxwell8.pdf

Stay tuned for more updates. Feel free to follow me on Twitter and LinkedIn. List of my paper with pdf links are on my homepage: https://shivakintali.github.io
