C05_L09 FINAL — N46 finite-cover bridge and stronger-estimate audit
Completed 2026-10-07 UTC. INITIAL.txt remains unchanged.

RESULT

I independently reconstructed the finite-ensemble bridge from a two-output
broadcaster. For m promised states with marginal error at most b, tree depth
L yields one EB channel with worst-case error

  L b + sqrt(m ln(m)/(2*2^L)).

The derivation uses 2^L−1 broadcaster calls, adaptive Helstrom measurements,
the information chain rule, and minimax over the compact convex set of EB
channels. Extending from an a-net gives

  e_EB <= 2a + min_L [L b + sqrt(N(a) ln N(a)/(2*2^L))].

The continuous optimizer is at

  L*=(2/ln 2) ln((ln 2)*sqrt(m ln(m)/2)/(2b)),

when positive; rounding to an adjacent integer costs O(b). Thus this route
has scale b[ln m+ln(1/b)+ln ln m+O(1)]. It proves that a->0 and
b ln N(a)->0 suffice, but it proves neither necessity nor a dimension-free
bound when covering entropy is uncontrolled.

DEEPER ATTACK

I built a finite restriction of the N61 copy-ladder experiment by adjoining
an exact symmetric-t moment design to a large projective packing. For
ell levels, m=2^(ell−1), d=ell*m, and t=m+1, the full-space level-shift
broadcaster has error at most 1/ell while every EB map has error at least
1−(m+1)/(m+d), which tends to one. The packing has at least
(4/3)^(d−1) members separated by at least sqrt(3)/2 in state trace
distance. The exact moment design preserves the EB lower bound and has at
most binom(d+t−1,t)^2 members. Hence for fixed a<sqrt(3)/4 the finite
experiment has ln N(a)=Theta(d), so

  b*sqrt(ln ln N(a)) -> 0

while its EB error tends to one. This falsifies that stronger sufficiency
criterion for unrestricted experiments. A common full-rank perturbation of
size ell^(-3) preserves the conclusion. This ladder does not satisfy the
target's fixed sandwich and its Holevo capacity diverges, so it is a
boundary result rather than a counterexample to the bounded-Holevo target.

The locking ensemble in INITIAL falsifies the tempting one-leaf upgrade
from an m factor to ln(m): accessible information can be exponentially
small despite constant average trace distance. Its constant pairwise
affinity gap also means the N46 two-state condition prevents its marginal
broadcast error from tending to zero. It blocks that local proof route but
is not itself a growing-dimension broadcast counterexample.
