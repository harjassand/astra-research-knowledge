Cycle 6 / c07_l03 — collective dark-sector entanglement priority audit
Date: 2026-10-08

Question
--------
Identify primary prior art for the whole-Hilbert-space phenomenon that a
collective lowering Lindbladian can entangle an initially fully separable,
full-rank state, and for the separable-state witness

    <J_+ J_-> >= <E>^2/N,       E = J_z + N/2 = sum_i sigma_i^+ sigma_i^-.

Keep this separate from Bassler's maximal-spin Dicke-sector result. INITIAL
is unchanged (SHA-256 remains
55c7861b1440c2c1f76699c8e61f94cc50ced043f6fc0306d5ae1252481b45ca).

Direct prior: two qubits, maximally mixed input, collective decay
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Shang-Bin Li and Jing-Bo Xu, “Stationary entanglement and nonlocality of two
qubits or qutrits collectively interacting with the thermal environment: The
role of Bell singlet state,” arXiv:quant-ph/0505216 (2005; v2, 2008).
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505216

This is an exact match to the N=2 pure collective-decay instance. Their
two-qubit Markov equation (Eq. (1)) is the common-reservoir thermal generator
with collective J_±. At bath occupation nbar=0 it reduces, up to rate
normalization, to D[J_-]. Their initial Werner family is

    rho_W(r) = r |singlet><singlet| + (1-r) I_4/4.

At r=0 the input is I_4/4: full rank, fully separable, and maximally mixed.
Equations (5)–(6) give its zero-temperature stationary state as

    rho_inf = (3/4)|00><00| + (1/4)|singlet><singlet|.

The paper explicitly states that this r=0 input becomes stationary-entangled
(its concurrence formula is Eq. (10)); therefore this already preempts the
phenomenological claim that collective spontaneous decay can create
entanglement from a fully separable full-rank state. Since the limiting
state is strictly NPT, continuity also implies entanglement at some finite
time, although the paper emphasizes the stationary endpoint. Direct partial
transpose calculation gives the least eigenvalue (3-sqrt(10))/8.

Their Eq. (10) also covers a nonzero-temperature common bath. At r=0 the
concurrence is

    max(0, [1 - 6 nbar(nbar+1)]/[4(3 nbar(nbar+1)+1)]),

so the stationary state remains entangled for
nbar < (sqrt(5/3)-1)/2 ~= 0.1455. This is a bath-temperature bound, not an
all-temperature result. The same paper proves that two spin-1 qutrits,
initially I_9/9, become stationary-entangled under collective zero-temperature
spin-one lowering; that is a useful higher-local-dimension analogue, not an
N-qubit theorem.

Adjacent reservoir-induced-entanglement precedent
-------------------------------------------------
Daniel Braun, “Creation of Entanglement by Interaction with a Common Heat
Bath,” Phys. Rev. Lett. 89, 277901 (2002), arXiv:quant-ph/0205019:
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0205019

Braun derives entanglement between initially pure product qubits coupled to a
shared thermal oscillator bath. The model is a common-bath dephasing/phase
mechanism with a bath-induced phase, not the one-jump collective amplitude
damping semigroup D[J_-], and the initial state is not full rank. It is
important historical mechanism-level context, but not the precise theorem
above.

Boundary with Bassler's Dicke-sector theorem
-------------------------------------------
N. S. Bassler, “Absence of Entanglement Growth in Dicke Superradiance,” Phys.
Rev. A 112, 053713 (2025), arXiv:2504.13646v3:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13646

The primary text specifies the initial state and all subsequent dynamics in
the symmetric subspace of maximal total spin J=N/2, with density matrix
diagonal in the Dicke basis (arXiv HTML §§II–IV, especially the master
equation and scope at lines 79–104 and conclusion at 159–165). Its theorem
that separability is preserved therefore does not apply to I/2^N or a
general full-space thermal state: those populate lower-J irreducible sectors
and multiplicity spaces, which D[J_-] preserves. The N=2 Li–Xu example is
already a direct counterexample to extending the maximal-spin result to the
whole Hilbert space. The two claims are compatible.

Prior art nearest to the proposed witness
-----------------------------------------
The inequality itself has a short direct proof. For a product factor let
p_i=<sigma_i^+ sigma_i^-> and c_i=<sigma_i^->. Positivity of the local
qubit state gives |c_i|^2 <= p_i(1-p_i), hence
p_i-|c_i|^2 >= p_i^2. Therefore

    <J_+J_-> = |sum_i c_i|^2 + sum_i (p_i-|c_i|^2)
             >= sum_i p_i^2 >= (sum_i p_i)^2/N.

For a convex mixture of product factors, apply this to each factor and then
Jensen's inequality. Thus a measured <J_+J_-> below <E>^2/N certifies
entanglement. In a dark state with <J_+J_->=0 and <E> > 0 the violation is
immediate.

I did not locate, in this bounded primary-source pass, this exact nonlinear
bound in the same notation. Closest verified primary precedents are:

* Géza Tóth, “Detection of multipartite entanglement in the vicinity of
  symmetric Dicke states,” J. Opt. Soc. Am. B 24, 275 (2007),
  https://www.gtoth.eu/Publications/JOSAB07.pdf. It develops collective
  spin-moment entanglement criteria whose observables are directly related
  to emitted radiation intensity, but it focuses on witnesses around
  symmetric Dicke states and uses different bounds. Do not cite it as the
  exact inequality without a separate derivation/reduction.
* Frederik Lohof et al., “Signatures of superradiance as a witness to
  multipartite entanglement,” Phys. Rev. Lett. 131, 063601 (2023),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12515. Its structure-factor witness uses
  pairwise spin correlations and is related to the correlated-emission
  term; it explicitly finds N^2 emission enhancement necessary but not
  sufficient for multipartite entanglement. It studies cavity/emitter
  dynamics with additional local processes and is not the dark-sector
  witness above.
* Pérola Milman, “Witnessing entanglement from N-qubit radiation emission,”
  Phys. Rev. A 74, 042317 (2006),
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.74.042317. The accessible primary
  abstract says emitted radiation can detect a wide class of entangled
  N-qubit states. The full text was blocked in this audit, so I cannot
  verify whether it contains the exact <J_+J_-> >= <E>^2/N inequality.
* Korbicz, Cirac, and Lewenstein's spin-squeezing inequalities (Phys. Rev.
  Lett. 95, 120502 (2005), arXiv:quant-ph/0504005) are a general collective
  second-moment witness framework; no exact equivalence to the stated
  excitation-conditioned intensity lower bound was established here.

Priority assessment / safe claim boundary
-----------------------------------------
The finite-dimensional phenomenon is known: Li–Xu is a direct exact N=2
prior, and Braun is an earlier broader common-reservoir precedent. Bassler
does not preempt a whole-space statement because it is restricted to
J=N/2. This audit did not find an all-N theorem for arbitrary full-space
full-rank initially-separable thermal states under pure D[J_-], nor did it
verify the exact nonlinear witness in the cited witness literature. Any
novelty statement must be limited to the additional all-N/full-space
quantifiers, the stated thermal family, and a proved finite-time/quantitative
bound, after a wider primary-source search. No novelty claim is made here.

Adjacent 2026 source: thermal Gibbs inputs plus collective decay
----------------------------------------------------------------
Riccardo Grazi, Donato Farina, Niccolò Traverso Ziani, and Dario Ferraro,
“Charging Quantum Batteries via Dissipative Quenches,” arXiv:2604.08151v1
(9 April 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08151. This is a recent nearby
primary source: it explicitly starts from Gibbs states of two- and
four-qubit XX chains (Sec. II, Eq. (4)), then studies a collective
amplitude-damping channel with jump L=sqrt(gamma) sum_i sigma_i^- (Sec. II,
Eq. (12)). Its collective-bath analyses report temperature-dependent
steady ergotropy for N=2 and N=4 (Sec. III.1–III.2), and identify overlap
with the nontrivial dark subspace as the mechanism for surviving population
and coherences (Sec. III.2, Eqs. (21)–(27)).

Scope boundary: the paper studies ergotropy, not separability, PPT, or an
entanglement witness. It does not certify its Gibbs inputs as fully
separable, and its post-quench generator retains the XX Hamiltonian
commutator in addition to collective damping. It therefore supplies
adjacent precedent for thermal Gibbs preparations, collective lowering, and
dark-subspace retention, but does not establish the present pure-D[J_-]
full-space entanglement claim, the all-N finite-time conclusion, or the
bound <J_+J_-> >= <E>^2/N. It does not displace Li–Xu as the directly
verified maximally-mixed-input entanglement precedent or alter the
maximal-spin/full-space distinction with Bassler.
