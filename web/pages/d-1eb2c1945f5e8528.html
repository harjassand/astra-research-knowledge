1. GENERAL PROFILE INFORMATION THEOREM

The initial soft-saturation result extends beyond its particular profile.
Let g:[0,infinity)->[0,L] satisfy h(t)=g(exp t) locally absolutely continuous
and E_g=integral_R |h'(t)|^2 dt<infinity. For any measurable positive definite
real symmetric m-by-m field B(Y) with finite Var_F(log B), put
 Z_T|Y~N(0,I+g(TB(Y))). Then

 integral_0^infinity I(Y;Z_T) dT/T
 <= E_g/4 Var_F(log B).                              (G)

Proof: the initial fixed-scale bound is I<=1/8 E||g(TB)-g(TB')||_F^2.
For two positive scalars b,c and t=log(b/c),
 integral [g(Tb)-g(Tc)]^2 dT/T
 =integral_R [h(s+t)-h(s)]^2 ds
 <=t^2 integral_R |h'(s)|^2 ds.
The last bound follows from Cauchy-Schwarz on
 h(s+t)-h(s)=integral_0^t h'(s+r)dr, followed by Tonelli; a negative t is
handled by swapping b,c. Expand the matrix difference in two eigenbases
as in INITIAL: its weights are |<u_i,v_j>|^2. Summing converts the scalar
log-distance squares into ||log B-log B'||_F^2, even when matrices do not
commute. Average independent copies: the latter expectation is twice the
log-field variance. This proves (G).

For g(x)=Lx/(L+x), E_g=L^2/6 and (G) recovers L^2/24.
For g(x)=min{x,L}, E_g=L^2/2 and the bound is L^2/8 Var_F(log B).
The exact scalar hard-cutoff integral, for b>=c>0, is
 L^2 [log(b/c)-1+c/b], obtained by dividing the scale integral at L/b,L/c.
Thus applicability to an actual supplied profile can be checked by its ONE
scalar log-scale derivative-energy integral; the exact soft formula is no
longer required. The remaining N75 gate is control of log R^2, including
kernel projections, not the choice between these two saturation profiles.
Neither (G) nor the initial bound establishes that control.

The scalar Y uniform[-1,1], B=Y^2 example has B>0 ALMOST SURELY, so it already
lies in the original theorem without a truncation argument. Its log-field
variance is exactly4: E log|Y|=-1 and E log^2|Y|=2. Consequently the soft
profile information integral is <=L^2/6, although its integrated absolute
field-gradient energy is infinite. This corrects only the initial wording
'extends by truncation', not the initial claim or constant.

If g has unequal limits at zero/infinity and the support projections of B(Y)
vary with positive probability, the covariance observations have a nonconstant
large-T limit. Gaussian likelihoods with distinct bounded covariances have
strictly positive mixture information. Dominated convergence applies because
all covariance eigenvalues lie in[1,1+L], yielding a positive limit of I(Y;Z_T)
and hence an infinite logarithmic-scale integral. A finite integral therefore
requires a common kernel, or another mechanism changing the observation
profile at infinity. A common nullspace can be removed exactly for a supplied
finite rational table. This is an admitted profile boundary, not a verdict
on the absent source field.

2. COMPLETE FINITE-DIGITAL GAUSSIAN CODEC

Known R>=0, L=1+R; unknown |theta|<=R; ONE X~N(theta,1). Define the public
Cauchy compander
 G_L(x)=1/2+atan(x/L)/pi.
The acquired observation is the p-bit bin k=floor(2^p G_L(X)). This is the
finite experiment being archived. Its acquisition costs p bits and the
specified calibrated compander. An exact continuous sample or an unknown
calibration is NOT a free input. The decoder outputs another p-bit bin.

For any 0<epsilon<1, choose an integer J>=3 satisfying
 J^2>=846 L^2/epsilon,
and p satisfying 2^p>=J/epsilon. There is an implemented blind classical
stochastic codec with exactly J labels and
 sup_|theta|<=R TV(Law(decoded bin),Law(input bin))<=epsilon.             (C)

Input dependence retained: ONE label j, requiring ceil log2 J bits.
Independent random bits: p+1 encoder bits and 2p+1 decoder bits.
No input-dependent seed, continuous statistic, old sample or auxiliary record
is retained. No exponentially large decoder table is constructed.

For R>=1, J=O(R/sqrt(epsilon)), recovering the strong-range scalar upper
order from N30 with a complete independent proof and finite-digital compiler.
For fixed R>0 the precision exponent is1/2. This construction does NOT
obtain the better sqrt(R/epsilon) count uniformly as R->0, is not exactly
Gaussian-null-preserving, and cannot replace N31's weak-signal tensor upper.
Cauchy reference is exactly preserved in its ideal coordinate-space kernel.

2.1 Positive stencil and its second-order L1 estimate.
Treat u in[0,1) as a circle. Let h=1/J, t_j=j/J, and a_j be periodic linear
hats of height1 supported within circular distance h of t_j. They partition
unity and have integral h. The ideal encoder picks j with probability a_j(u).
The ideal decoder density in u is J a_j(u). Therefore the resulting density
operator is
 Kq=sum_j a_j [h^-1 integral a_j q].
It is a positive Markov reconstruction, fixes the uniform circle law,
is selfadjoint and PSD. PSD is not needed for the fixed-scalar proof below.

For every periodic C^2 density q,
 ||Kq-q||_1<=7/(24J^2) ||q''||_1.                     (S)
For the nodal linear interpolant Lq, the positive interval Green kernel
has integrated mass (s-a)(b-s)/2<=h^2/8. Thus
 ||Lq-q||_1<=h^2/8 ||q''||_1.
The node averaging error is, by the symmetric Taylor formula,
 qbar_j-q(t_j)=integral_(-h)^h
       [(h-|s|)^3/(6h^2)] q''(t_j+s) ds.
Since ||a_j||_1=h, summing the absolute errors gives
 ||Kq-Lq||_1<=h sum_j |qbar_j-q(t_j)|
 <=h^2/6 ||q''||_1.
At every s there are two adjacent terms, whose cubed distances sum to at
most h^3. Add the two estimates to obtain(S). TV is one half L1.

2.2 Uniform Gaussian compactification derivative budget.
Write phi_theta(x)=phi(x-theta), Z=x-theta, and
 A(x)=dx/du=pi(L^2+x^2)/L.
The u-density is q_theta=A phi_theta; it and all derivatives are flat at the
circle seam because the Gaussian dominates every rational endpoint factor.
Differentiating twice, and using du=dx/A,
 ||q_theta''||_1
 <=integral |(A'^2+AA'')phi_theta+3AA'phi_theta'+A^2phi_theta''| dx
 <=pi^2 E[2+6X^2/L^2
           +6|X|(1+X^2/L^2)|Z|
           +(L^2+2X^2+X^4/L^2)|Z^2-1|].
Here X=theta+Z, |theta|<=R<=L and L>=1. The three expected brackets are at
most8, 96L^2, and186L^2, respectively:
 E X^2<=L^2;
 |X|<=L+|Z|, X^2<=2L^2+2Z^2, X^4<=8L^4+8Z^4;
 E|Z|<=1, E|Z|^3=2E|Z|, EZ^2=1,EZ^4=3,EZ^6=15;
 and |Z^2-1|<=Z^2+1.
Thus ||q_theta''||_1<=290 pi^2 L^2. With pi^2<10, (S) yields
 TV(Kq_theta,q_theta)<=423 L^2/J^2<=epsilon/2.        (I)
These constants are conservative and were derived, not fitted to fixtures.

2.3 Digital acquisition and exact compiler.
Given only the input bin k, encode its midpoint u_k=(2k+1)/2^(p+1).
Write q,rem=divmod(J(2k+1),2^(p+1)). Uniformly draw p+1 random bits r;
return(q+1) mod J if r<rem, otherwise q mod J. This is exactly the ideal
hat encoding at u_k. Its label-law TV from encoding the unobserved U directly
is <=J/2^(p+1), because the hat probability vector has l1 Lipschitz constant
2J and |U-u_k|<=2^(-p-1). The decoder is a Markov kernel, so it cannot increase
this error. Quantizing the ideal decoder output also cannot increase (I).
The two errors sum to(C).

For label j, let F_j be the CDF of density J a_j on[0,1]. At any dyadic
endpoint k/2^p, 2^(2p+1) F_j(k/2^p) is an INTEGER. Explicit formulas are
implemented. Away from the wrap label j=0, put x=Jt:
 F_j(t)=0 for x<=j-1;
 F_j(t)=(x-j+1)^2/2 for j-1<x<=j;
 F_j(t)=1-(j+1-x)^2/2 for j<x<=j+1;
 F_j(t)=1 otherwise.
For j=0 the first and last half-triangles are separated by a constant1/2
CDF plateau; the script gives its polynomial formula.

Draw exactly2p+1 independent random bits r. Binary search for the first bin
whose upper-edge CDF integer exceeds r, and output that bin. All comparisons
are integer/rational exact; it takes at most p search steps. Encoder work is
O(M(p+log J)); decoder work is O(p M(p+log J)) under a standard multiplication
cost M, with O(p+log J) working bits. If the transient random registers or
working state are physically archived they must be counted; they are discarded
here before the storage period. Output generation uses p bits per coordinate.

The public J,p selection uses rational inequalities, not a trusted floating
square root. Public R/L encoding, compander calibration, and source precision
are charged inputs. The theorem concerns the declared quantized experiment.
An atomic numerical x output still has TV1 from a continuous Gaussian; no
claim of continuous-output finite-bit TV is made.

Fixed dimension s, independent Gaussian box ranges R_i, has a direct product
codec using scalar epsilon/s and mixed-radix labels. Its dimension is
 O_s(product_i (1+R_i) epsilon^(-s/2)), all input/output bits and random bits
sum over coordinates, and decoder cost sums. This is a charged fixed-s upper;
there is an explicit s error-allocation cost, no dimension-uniform ellipsoid
claim. Known whitening/SVD, inverse-model acquisition and quantum bridges
remain separate algorithmic costs.

3. COSTS, ENERGY AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES

The initial rational table certificate reads the entire supplied reference law
and covariance diagonal; it therefore does not conceal acquisition in a model
oracle. For the optional sampling version, the2n draws and certified log ranges
are separate from the one observation being archived. Unknown reference tails
cannot be learned with the distribution-free guarantee asserted for a supplied
table. General matrix-log acquisition is not implemented.

The covariance-observation theorem computes a mathematical scale integral;
it does not physically sample infinitely many scales. At one scale the normalized
quadratic output energy E||Z_T||^2 lies between m and m(1+L). Sampling q scales
requires q*m Gaussian outputs and at least q*m of this normalized energy.
Integrating the unavoidable noise energy over all logarithmic scales diverges.
The finite-table certificate avoids that experiment by analytic calculation.
A physical Hamiltonian, thermal implementation and joule cost are not supplied,
so physical energy costs are UNKNOWN. CPU power/energy and backend token/GPU
costs likewise remain UNKNOWN; no energy or compute advantage is claimed.

The compactification construction was already described, without a full proof
packet, in N30's supplied summary. This contribution is independent complete
reconstruction and an exact digital implementation, not a new asymptotic
Gaussian compression order. Positive Durrmeyer stencils, Gaussian maximum
entropy, approximate sufficient statistics, and stochastic quantization are
established antecedents. Exact priority of the general profile/log-field
information inequality remains unverified. No external validation or
