# Cycle 2: attack on the Poincaré-only information lemma

**Result.** The literal replacement of Family 093 Proposition 7.2 under only its displayed field/moment/Sylvester/commutator hypotheses is false. The exact rate-2 Laplace law gives the shortest counterexample; a smooth Gaussian-convolution and exact-rescaling variant also has best Poincare constant exactly 1, exact scalar Sylvester identity, zero commutator defect, and the two required limiting energies, while its scale-integrated mutual information grows at least logarithmically with the fixed cap `L`. The tail-escape profile cannot have the full Family 093 critical-law provenance because the source near-unit LSI forces bounded entropy; this report also identifies a separate one-dimensional obstruction in Proposition 5.1 conditions (5.2)-(5.3). The actual N75 covariance-only estimate under the full high-dimensional hierarchy remains open.

## Exact target and quantifiers

Family 093, Proposition 7.2, §7.1, Eqs. (7.1)–(7.8), p. 52–53 of the pinned PDF, starts with (j\to\infty), stationary laws (Y_{j,t}\sim\eta_j), best LSI constants (\mathcal R(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1}\) with (\lambda_j\to1\), PSD fields (R_j), and original symmetric matrices (\widehat H_{j,i}) satisfying
\[
\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}R_j^2\to1,\qquad \mathbb E|DR_j|^2\to1,
\]
\[
D_iR_j=\tfrac12(\widehat H_{j,i}R_j+R_j\widehat H_{j,i}),\qquad
\sum_i\mathbb E\|[\widehat H_{j,i},R_j]\|_{HS}^2=o(1).
\]
For **each fixed** (t>0), (L\ge1), and all (T>0), independently take (G\sim N(0,I)) and set
\[
f_L(q)=q/(1+q/L),\quad C_{j,T}(y)=I+f_L(TR_j(y)^2),\quad Z_{j,T}=C_{j,T}(Y_{j,t})^{1/2}G.
\]
The LSI proposition gives
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\int_0^\infty I(Y_{j,t};Z_{j,T})\,\frac{dT}{T^2}\le 9/4,
\]
with coefficient independent of the fixed (t,L); the (j\)-limit is taken at each fixed (L).

The attempted covariance replacement changes only the LSI premise to (P(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1}\), (\lambda_j\to1\), and seeks the same type of finite absolute upper bound independent of (t,L). The counterexample below shows that no such bound follows from the displayed field/moment/Sylvester/commutator hypotheses alone.

## Counterexample

Let (\eta(dy)=e^{-2|y|}dy\), the centered two-sided exponential (rate 2) probability law. It is log-concave. Its sharp Poincaré constant is (P(\eta)=1): conjugating the reversible generator (-\partial_y^2+2\operatorname{sgn}(y)\partial_y) by (e^{-|y|}) gives the Schrödinger operator (-\partial_y^2+1-2\delta_0), whose ground state has eigenvalue 0 and whose remaining spectrum begins at 1. Let (Y_{j,t}) be any stationary diffusion with this invariant law, for example the reversible diffusion with generator (\partial_y^2-2\operatorname{sgn}(y)\partial_y\). It is stationary for every fixed (t>0\).

Choose one fixed smooth nondecreasing cutoff (\chi:\mathbb R\to[0,1]\) with (\chi=0\) on (( -\infty,0]\) and (\chi=1\) on ([2,\infty)\). Put (\psi(y)=\int_{-\infty}^y\chi(s)ds\), so (\psi=0\) for (y\le0\) and (\psi(y)=y-c_\chi\) for (y\ge2\), where (c_\chi\) is fixed. For (j\ge2\), set (\epsilon_j=1/j\), (a_j=1-\epsilon_j\),
\[
c_j=\bigl(\mathbb E_\eta e^{2a_j\psi(Y)}\bigr)^{-1/2},\qquad
r_j(y)=c_j e^{a_j\psi(y)},\qquad R_j(y)=[r_j(y)],\qquad \widehat H_j(y)=[a_j\chi(y)].
\]
Here brackets denote (1\times1) matrices. Then (R_j>0), (c_j^2\sim2\epsilon_j e^{2c_\chi}\to0), and
\[
\mathbb E_\eta\operatorname{tr}R_j^2=1,\qquad
\mathbb E_\eta|DR_j|^2
=a_j^2 c_j^2\mathbb E_\eta[\chi(Y)^2e^{2a_j\psi(Y)}]\longrightarrow1.
\]
The Sylvester equation is exact since (r'_j=(a_j\chi)r_j\): (D R_j=(\widehat H_jR_j+R_j\widehat H_j)/2\). All scalar commutators vanish, so (\mathbb E|[\widehat H_j,R_j]|_{HS}^2=0\). The Poincaré premise holds with (\lambda_j=1). Thus every displayed hypothesis of the proposed replacement is met (with a fixed one-dimensional, centered, log-concave invariant law).

Write (X_j=R_j(Y)^2\). On (y\ge2\), (X_j=c_j^2e^{2a_j(y-c_\chi)}). Consequently, above the vanishing cutoff (x_{0,j}=c_j^2e^{2a_j(2-c_\chi)}\),
\[
\mathbb P(X_j>x)=A_jx^{-\beta_j},\quad
\beta_j=1/a_j\downarrow1,\quad
A_j=\tfrac12e^{-2c_\chi}c_j^{2\beta_j}\le1
\]
for all sufficiently large (j). Also, for each fixed (K>0\),
\[
\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge K}]
=\frac{c_j^2e^{-2a_jc_\chi}}{2\epsilon_j}e^{-2\epsilon_j y_{K,j}}
\longrightarrow1,
\quad y_{K,j}=c_\chi+\frac{\log(K/c_j^2)}{2a_j}.
\]
This is the key size-biased tail: almost all of the first moment of (X_j\) escapes past every fixed threshold, even though (\mathbb E X_j=1\).

For a fixed (L\), the scalar channel is
\[
Z_{j,T}=\sqrt{1+f_L(TX_j)}\,G.
\]
Let (p_0=\mathbb P(|G|\ge\sqrt2)>0\), choose an absolute (h_0\) large enough that
\[
p_0(h/4-\log3)-\log2\ge (p_0/8)h\quad(h\ge h_0),
\]
and take any fixed (H\) with (h_0<H\le L\). If the realized latent value is (X_j=x\ge K_H:=He^{H/4}\), choose scales (T=h/x\), (h_0\le h\le H\), and the output event (A_h=\{|Z|\ge\sqrt h\}\). Since (h\le L\),
\[
\operatorname{Var}(Z_{j,T}\mid X_j=x)=1+f_L(h)\ge1+h/2,
\]
so (\mathbb P(A_h\mid X_j=x)\ge p_0\). For an independent (X'_j\), on (TX'_j\le1\) the output variance is at most 2; therefore the marginal event probability (q_h\) obeys
\[
q_h\le2e^{-h/4}+\mathbb P(X'_j>x/h)
\le3e^{-h/4}.
\]
The last step uses (x/h\ge e^{H/4}\), the Pareto tail formula, (A_j\le1), and (\beta_j\ge1\). Binary data processing and the binary-entropy bound give
\[
D(\mathcal L(Z_{j,T}\mid Y=y)\|\mathcal L(Z_{j,T}))
\ge p_0(h/4-\log3)-\log2\ge(p_0/8)h.
\]
The threshold event may depend on the fixed pair ((y,T)); data processing is applied separately to each conditional KL before averaging in (y\).

Now integrate the conditional KL over scales and retain only (X_j\ge K_H\), (T\in[h_0/X_j,H/X_j]\). With (h=TX_j\), (dT/T^2=X_j,dh/h^2\), so
\[
\int_0^\infty I(Y_{j,t};Z_{j,T})\frac{dT}{T^2}
\ge \frac{p_0}{8}\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge K_H}]\log(H/h_0).
\]
For each fixed (H\), the expectation tends to 1 as (j\to\infty\). Hence
\[
\liminf_{j\to\infty}\int_0^\infty I(Y_{j,t};Z_{j,T})\frac{dT}{T^2}
\ge\frac{p_0}{8}\log(H/h_0).
\]
Taking (H=L/2\) for any fixed (L>2h_0\) makes this lower bound grow without limit as the fixed cap (L\) is increased. Therefore an absolute (C_{\rm P}\) independent of (L\) cannot follow from Poincaré plus the displayed hypotheses.

## Smooth exact-Poincaré version

The preceding Laplace construction is already a literal counterexample to the displayed moment/Sylvester interface; its density is not smooth at zero. The same obstruction can be made smooth while retaining the exact normalization (P(\eta_j)=1).

Let `V` have density `exp(-2|x|)`, let `G ~ N(0,1)` be independent, and put
\[
U_\delta=V+\sqrt\delta G,\qquad p_\delta=P(\mathcal L(U_\delta)),\qquad b_\delta=\sqrt{p_\delta},\qquad Y_\delta=U_\delta/b_\delta.
\]
The density of `U_delta` is smooth, positive, and log-concave. Tensorization gives `p_delta <= 1 + delta`. For every fixed compactly supported smooth test `f`, its Poincare quotient under `Law(U_delta)` converges to the quotient under the rate-2 Laplace law as `delta -> 0`. Taking the supremum over these tests gives `liminf p_delta >= P(Laplace) = 1`, because compact smooth tests determine the sharp Poincare constant. Therefore `p_delta -> 1`. By scaling, `P(Law(Y_delta)) = 1` exactly. The rate-2 Laplace variance is `1/2`; the lower-limit argument uses its sharp tail spectral gap, not the linear test.

For `u >= 2`, direct convolution gives
\[
p_{U_\delta}(u)=e^{2\delta}\left[e^{-2u}\Phi\!\left(\frac{u-2\delta}{\sqrt\delta}\right)+e^{2u}\Phi\!\left(\frac{-u-2\delta}{\sqrt\delta}\right)\right],
\]
so for all sufficiently small `delta`,
\[
\frac12e^{2\delta}e^{-2u}\le p_{U_\delta}(u)\le e^{2\delta}e^{-2u}.
\]
Write `b_j = b_{delta_j}`, choose `delta_j = j^(-4)`, `epsilon_j = j^(-1)`, and `a_j = b_j - epsilon_j`. Define `eta_j = Law(Y_{delta_j})`, and use the same fixed smooth cutoff `chi` and function `psi` as above. Set
\[
Z_j=\mathbb E_{\eta_j}e^{2a_j\psi(Y)},\quad q_j=Z_j^{-1/2},\quad R_j(y)=[q_je^{a_j\psi(y)}],\quad \widehat H_j(y)=[a_j\chi(y)].
\]
Here `b_j -> 1`. For all sufficiently large `j`, the rescaled density satisfies the following bounds uniformly for `y >= 4` (so `b_j y >= 2`):
\[
\tfrac12b_je^{2\delta_j}e^{-2b_jy}\le p_{\eta_j}(y)\le b_je^{2\delta_j}e^{-2b_jy},
\]
with ratio to the upper expression tending uniformly to one. Since `2a_j = 2b_j - 2epsilon_j`, the bounds imply `Z_j` is comparable to `epsilon_j^(-1)`, `q_j^2` is comparable to `epsilon_j`, and the probability measure proportional to `exp(2a_j psi) d eta_j` puts mass `1-o(1)` on `y >= 4`; the bounded interval below 4 contributes only `O(1)`. Consequently
\[
\mathbb E_{\eta_j}R_j^2=1,\qquad \mathbb E_{\eta_j}|DR_j|^2=a_j^2(1-o(1))\to1.
\]

The scalar Sylvester relation is exact: `D R_j = Hhat_j R_j`, so `D R_j = (Hhat_j R_j + R_j Hhat_j)/2` and all commutators vanish. Also `E R_j -> 0`; under the `R_j^2`-size-biased law, `Hhat_j^2 -> 1`, hence `E[R_j^2(Hhat_j^2-1)^2] -> 0`. These match several scalar consequences used later in §6, but do not supply the tensor construction.
For each fixed `K > 0`, the threshold `y_K,j = c_chi + log(K Z_j)/(2a_j)` is `O(log j)`, hence `epsilon_j y_K,j -> 0`; eventually it exceeds 4. The same tail-density asymptotic gives
\[
\mathbb E_{\eta_j}[R_j^2\mathbf1_{\{R_j^2\ge K\}}]\to1.
\]
Finally, for `u >= u_0,j = q_j^2 exp(2a_j(4-c_chi)) -> 0`, the tail upper bound yields
\[
\mathbb P(R_j^2>u)\le A_ju^{-\beta_j},\qquad \beta_j=b_j/a_j>1,\quad A_j=\tfrac12e^{2\delta_j-2b_jc_\chi}q_j^{2\beta_j}\to0.
\]
Thus, eventually, `P(R_j^2 > u) <= u^(-1)` for every `u >= 1`. The same binary-event calculation below applies. This smooth version has a smooth positive log-concave invariant density, exact Poincare constant 1, exact scalar Sylvester identity, zero commutator, and the same logarithmic-in-`L` integrated-MI lower bound.

## Binary-KL scale lemma without Pareto asymptotics

The channel lower bound only uses two properties of `X_j = R_j^2`, not an exact Pareto formula. Since the channel depends on `Y` only through `X`, `I(Y; Z_T) = I(X; Z_T)`. Suppose `E X_j = 1`, for every fixed `K > 0`, `E[X_j 1_{X_j >= K}] -> 1`, and eventually in `j`, `P(X_j > u) <= 1/u` for every `u >= 1`. Fix a cap `L`, take any `h_0 < H <= L`, and for each realized `x >= H exp(H/4)` and `h in [h_0,H]`, set `T=h/x` and `A_h={|Z|>=sqrt(h)}`. Since `f_L(h)>=h/2`, the conditional variance is at least `1+h/2`, so `P(A_h|X_j=x)>=p_0=P(|G|>=sqrt(2))`. The marginal event probability is at most `2 exp(-h/4)+P(X_j' > x/h)<=3 exp(-h/4)`, because `x/h>=exp(H/4)>=exp(h/4)>=1`.
\[
D(\mathcal L(Z_{j,h/x}\mid X_j=x)\|\mathcal L(Z_{j,h/x}))\ge p_0(h/4-\log3)-\log2\ge (p_0/8)h
\]
for `h >= h_0`, with `h_0` an absolute sufficiently large constant. Integrating on `T in [h_0/X_j, H/X_j]`, and using `dT/T^2 = X_j dh/h^2`, gives
\[
\int_0^\infty I(X_j;Z_{j,T})\frac{dT}{T^2}\ge \frac{p_0}{8}\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge He^{H/4}}]\log(H/h_0).
\]
Thus, for every fixed `L >= H > h_0`, taking `j -> infinity` first gives `liminf J_j(L) >= (p_0/8) log(H/h_0)`. Choosing `H = L/2` for fixed `L > 2h_0` makes this lower bound unbounded as `L` ranges over fixed caps. No Pareto asymptotics are used in this lemma; the smooth construction verifies its two conditions through the explicit convolution tail bounds.

## Why the scalar falsifier cannot have the full source provenance

The display-only replacement is refuted, but this does not realize a field from Family 093's critical sequence. The decisive additional hypothesis already appears before the tensor construction: Proposition 3.9, equations (3.28)–(3.30), requires the invariant law to satisfy
\[
\mathcal R(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1},\qquad \lambda_j\to1,
\]
where `Ent_eta(f^2) <= 2 R(eta) integral |Df|^2 d eta`. The same condition is an explicit premise of Proposition 7.2. It is not merely a smoothness assumption.

For any scalar nonnegative `R_j` with `E R_j^2 = 1`, define `X_j = R_j^2`. If `E[X_j 1_{X_j >= K}] -> 1` for every fixed `K > 1`, then
\[
\operatorname{Ent}_{\eta_j}(R_j^2)=\mathbb E[X_j\log X_j]\ge (\log K)\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge K}]-1/e.
\]
Since `K` is arbitrary, the entropy diverges to infinity. But the source's near-unit LSI applied to `R_j` gives
\[
\operatorname{Ent}_{\eta_j}(R_j^2)\le2\mathcal R(\eta_j)\mathbb E|DR_j|^2\le2\lambda_j^{-1}\mathbb E|DR_j|^2\to2,
\]
a contradiction. For a matrix field `R_j`, put `m_j = E tr(R_j^2) -> 1` and `X_j = tr(R_j^2)/m_j`. Apply LSI to `sqrt(X_j) = ||R_j||_HS/sqrt(m_j)`; its squared-gradient energy is at most `E|D R_j|^2/m_j`. Escape of the normalized trace mass implies the same tail escape for `X_j`, so the same entropy contradiction applies. Thus **no** tail-escape field of this type can meet the full Family 093 near-unit-LSI provenance, independent of whether its Sylvester matrices can be generated from a tensor hierarchy.

The smooth construction satisfies the smooth-positive-density regularity stated in Proposition 5.1, log-concavity, exact `P(eta_j)=1`, stationarity for the reversible diffusion, the two energy limits, and the displayed scalar Sylvester/commutator conditions. It even has an approximate-eigenfunction candidate: with `A_eta=-d^2/dy^2-(d log p_eta/dy)d/dy`, `w_j=R_j=phi_j'` for `phi_j(y)=integral_0^y R_j`, the tail drift tends uniformly to `-2b_j`, and on the `R_j^2`-size-biased tail `(A_eta-1)w_j/w_j = 2a_j b_j-a_j^2-1+o(1) -> 0`; the bounded core has vanishing `R_j^2`-mass. The mean of `w_j` tends to zero and its derivative energy tends to its squared norm.

The prediction-curve input (4.20)/(5.1)(i) is also consistent with this scalar tail mode and follows by completing the Gaussian square. At fixed `r>0`, in the tail region carrying the `w_j^2` mass, `E[w_j(Y)|Y+sqrt(r)G=u]` has leading factor `q_j exp(a_j(u-c_chi)) exp(-2a_j b_j r + a_j^2 r/2)`, while the observation density has leading factor `C_j exp(-2b_j u+2b_j^2 r)`. Dividing the resulting squared-prediction integral by `E w_j^2` gives
`m_j(r) = exp((a_j^2-4a_j b_j+2b_j^2)r)+o(1) -> exp(-r)`.
This uses the explicit uniform tail approximation; it does not verify the other hypotheses of Proposition 5.1.

There is a concrete obstruction to completing the one-dimensional hierarchy input. Proposition 5.1 condition (5.2) says every deterministic projection of rank at most `c I_j` captures `o(I_j)` of `w_j` for each fixed `c`, where `I_j=E w_j^2`. For a one-dimensional field `w_j` proportional to `R_j`, (5.2) forces `I_j -> 0`: otherwise a fixed `c` eventually permits the identity projection. But then condition (5.3)'s rank bound `C_epsilon I_j` is eventually less than one, so its projection must be zero and it requires `E b_j^2 <= epsilon` (the `omega` term is nonnegative). The fixed-`s=1/8` decomposition in (5.1) gives `b_j(y)=y+s D log eta_j(y)-s omega_j(y)`. For `omega=0` or `omega=w_j` with `w_j` scaled so `I_j->0`, on `1<=y<=2` one has `D log eta_j(y)->-2` and `omega_j(y)->0`, hence `b_j(y)->y-1/4`; since `eta_j([1,2])` tends to a positive Laplace mass, `E b_j^2` stays bounded below. Thus this natural one-dimensional `w_j` cannot meet (5.2)-(5.3) under any amplitude rescaling. The source's drift-curvature bounds and strict-branch condition (5.4) were not separately verified.

The smooth reversible law does provide a stationary `Y_t` for every fixed `t`. Its far-tail drift gives the scalar lognormal multiplier expected from the square root of (6.24), but we did not establish Proposition 6.8's required mean-square frozen-matrix approximation or construct the totally symmetric tensors `T_l`, band reduction `R=(Psi Psi*)^(1/2)`, and `H_i` from Lemma 6.3. The actual source sequence also has an additional near-unit LSI condition from Proposition 3.9, which gives a dimension-independent obstruction to this tail-escape profile.

Independently of the one-dimensional projection obstruction, Proposition 3.9 requires the near-unit LSI stated above. Exponential tails already rule out finite LSI by sub-Gaussian concentration, and the entropy calculation excludes any tail-escape field under that premise. The test law is also not shown to be the fixed-`s=1/8` posterior/tilted critical law. Smoothness and stationarity are not the obstruction. These exclusions apply to this falsifier; they do **not** decide whether another covariance-only argument exploiting the actual high-dimensional hierarchy could work.

## What this does and does not establish

- **Established for the displayed interface:** the covariance-only replacement of Proposition 7.2 is false if its hypotheses are precisely Poincaré near-extremality plus (7.1)–(7.2). This falsifier is stronger than the generic observation that Poincaré controls Hellinger but not KL; it computes a divergent scale-integrated MI lower bound in the required covariance Gaussian channel.
- **Not established:** this example is not shown to come from Family 093's earlier near-extremal eigenfunction, symmetric tensor hierarchy, and frozen matrix construction. A replacement lemma restricted to those additional provenance/structure constraints could still be true. The N75 application therefore remains open until one proves that the actual fields have additional structure excluding this example, or proves a new estimate using that structure.
- **Proof route status:** the maximum-entropy/log-determinant and Poincaré-on-square-root routes do not resolve the target. The falsifier shows why no proof can use only the listed assumptions; any successful proof must charge and exploit a strictly stronger source-specific hypothesis.
- **Cost/provenance:** no numerical experiment or external validation was performed. The derivation uses a one-dimensional exact channel calculation and the primary source's displayed interface. The only non-elementary input is the standard sharp spectral gap of the rate-2 Laplace law; it is also directly visible from the conjugated one-dimensional Schrödinger operator above.
