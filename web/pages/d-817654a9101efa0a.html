Current result
--------------

An exact mesh-uniform pointwise absorber is proved for the coupled detailed-balanced network

    A ⇌ 2A,  B ⇌ 2B,  A ⇌ B

with all six rates equal to λ>0 and unequal positive species diffusivities. The reaction vector is λ(B−A²,A−B²). Cooperative comparison with the spatially constant diagonal logistic solution gives

    Qλ(m₀,t) ≤ A(x,t),B(x,t) ≤ Qλ(M₀,t),

and the invariant absorber [1/2,2]² enters by the exact time formula in RESULT.txt. The same proof works on every finite mesh whose species diffusion matrices are conservative Markov generators, even when the two generators differ. The constants do not depend on mesh size or diffusion coefficients. The exact relative entropy dissipation is also recorded.

What this does and does not settle
----------------------------------

This is an unconditional theorem for one genuinely coupled, full-span, two-species complex-balanced and endotactic network. It does not settle arbitrary N54 unequal-diffusion permanence. The proof applies to other systems only if their cooperative order structure and a suitable invariant comparison curve are verified. No fixed-mesh absorber is being passed through a limit. Stochastic recurrence, nonexplosion, stationary laws, and all-time survival remain outside this deterministic result.
