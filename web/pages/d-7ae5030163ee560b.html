# Local logical-sector indistinguishability at finite bath resolution

independently reconstructed in written gates recorded by the cycle16 handoff.
It concerns the

## 1. A small local insertion cannot supply a large logical word

Use the CSS Hamiltonian, logical L, graph G_Z and pure-X distance d=d_X of
LOGICAL_OPEN_LOOP_AND_BATH_RESPONSE.md. Let P be any phased Pauli supported
on at most q<d sites. In the computational basis, P is a unit-modulus diagonal
phase times the permutation X_v, with |v|<=q. Absolute values of the positive
tagged expansion therefore dominate |Tr[L P exp(-beta H)]| by the positive
open-path expansion with permutation endpoint X_(l+v).

If f is the parity sum of its field tags and s is the parity sum of its
original X-check tags, then f+s=l+v. Consequently f+v=l+s is a closed
nontrivial pure-X logical word. As before, it has a G_Z-connected nontrivial
component of size at least d. A connected d-site subset S of that component
contains at most q sites of v, so at least d-q of its sites were actually
touched by field tags. For w-body tags this forces N_S>=(d-q)/w.

Tilt tags meeting S. The inserted permutation need not commute with H:
nonnegative entries give positivity, and |Tr(X_(l+v) T)|<=Tr T for positive
T gives the same partition upper bound. With intensity J and |S|=d,

    mgf_open /Z <= exp[beta J d (e^theta-1)].

If beta*w*J*d < d-q, choose e^theta=(d-q)/(beta*w*J*d). The positive mass
for this S is at most

    [e beta w J d/(d-q)]^((d-q)/w).

Counting connected sets therefore gives, for EVERY such Pauli,

    |Tr(rho_H L P)| <= delta_q,
    delta_q=min{1, N(D+1)^(2d)
                     [e beta w J d/(d-q)]^((d-q)/w)}.      (L1)

The phase of a Y or Z insertion was removed only by an upper bound. No
stoquasticity of the inserted observable or conditional-sector erasure was
assumed. For q=0, this agrees with the earlier open-loop bound.

For any operator O on these q sites, Pauli orthogonality gives

    O=sum_P c_P P,
    sum_P |c_P| <= 2^q ||O||,

because sum|c_P|^2=2^-q Tr(O^*O)<=||O||^2 and there are4^q Paulis. Thus

    |Tr(rho_H L O)| <=2^q ||O|| delta_q.                  (L2)

This coefficient toll is explicit; operator norm alone cannot be inserted
without proving a better local operator estimate.

## 2. Reduced states in the conserved bare-L sectors

Let rho_+/-=P_+/-rho_H/Tr(P_+/-rho_H), with P_+/-=(I+/-L)/2. Put
r=Tr(rho_H L), |r|<=delta_0. For any norm-one local O, write
a=Tr(rho_H O) and b=Tr(rho_H L O). Even if O changes parity, its diagonal
sector expectation is

    Tr(rho_+/- O)=(a+/-b)/(1+/-r).

Consequently

    ||Tr_out(rho_+-rho_-)||_1
       <=2(2^q delta_q+delta_0)/(1-delta_0^2).             (L3)

Here the partial trace is onto ANY supplied set of q sites; no spatial
connectedness of that support was imposed. Formula(L3) is a current-Gibbs
local-indistinguishability statement, not a ground-band approximation.

For one-site fields J=epsilon, D<=42, suppose

    beta epsilon <=1/[8e(D+1)^4], q<=d/2.                 (L4)

Then 2^q delta_q<=N2^(-d/2) and delta_0<=N2^(-d/2).
Whenever delta_0<=1/2, the deliberately loose bound is

    ||Tr_out(rho_+-rho_-)||_1 <=8N2^(-d/2).              (L5)

The accepted cycle15 unit-penalty field schedule beta epsilon<=

## 3. An explicit locality estimate for the unchanged bath

For this section restrict to one-site fields. Let G be the actual qubit
graph joining sites in ANY original check. It has degree<=42, and a graph
edge has physical length<=2 because its two qubits share a check vertex
with unit Tanner edges. Remove the scalar check contributions and write

    H=sum_Z Phi_Z, Phi_check=-(h_a/2)C_a,
    Phi_site=-epsilon_q X_q.

Define the nonnegative matrix

    K_ij=sum_(Z:i,j in Z)||Phi_Z||.

For mu>0,

    kappa_mu=max_i sum_j e^(mu dist_G(i,j)) K_ij
       <=(49/2)e^mu h_max+epsilon.                       (L6)

There are at most7 checks per qubit, each with at most7 sites. Their
supports are graph cliques; fields are one-site. No hidden Hamiltonian
norm proportional to N appears in this velocity.

For completeness, the usual commutator iteration gives, for A supported
on S and norm-one B_j on site j,

    ||[A(t),B_j]|| <=2||A|| sum_(i in S)[exp(2|t|K)]_ij.  (L7)

One direct derivation is the interaction-picture commutator integral
C_B(X,t)<=C_B(X,0)+2 sum_(Z:Z intersects X)||Phi_Z||
integral_0^|t| C_B(Z,s)ds. Overcount each overlap by its incident i in X,
and iterate. The initial term is bounded by2||B|| sum_(i in X)1_(i=j).
The resulting nonnegative path series is exactly the displayed matrix
exponential majorant.

To make the internal-interaction step explicit, for a FIXED initial A in
the algebra on X put f(t)=[tau_t(A),B] and
D_X(t)=sum_(Z intersects X)tau_t(Phi_Z). It is Hermitian, and Jacobi gives

    f'=i[D_X(t),f]
       -i sum_(Z intersects X)[tau_t(A),[tau_t(Phi_Z),B]].

The homogeneous term is time-dependent unitary conjugation and preserves
operator norm. Variation of constants bounds the forcing by
2||A|| sum||Phi_Z|| C_B(Z,t), proving the stated integral. This includes
terms wholly inside X; it does not assume X remains invariant under full H.
The graph-weighted matrix iteration then gives

    sum_(j:dist(j,S)>R)[exp(2|t|K)]_ij
       <=exp[2kappa_mu |t|-mu R]   (i in S).              (L8)

Let A_R(t) be the normalized Pauli twirl of A(t) over every site outside
the graph R-neighborhood of S. It is supported in that neighborhood and
has norm at most||A||. Telescoping the one-site twirls and applying(L7)
to their three Pauli generators gives the safe bound

    ||A(t)-A_R(t)||
       <=4 |S| ||A|| exp[2kappa_mu |t|-mu R].             (L9)

This is a standard Lieb--Robinson/quasilocality mechanism, reconstructed
here with a finite matrix majorant. Its derivation assumes actual finite-range
check supports, not an enlarged graph whose extra edges have no interactions.
Our graph contains only original check incidences, so folded close sheets
do not create couplings. The older locality prior is Nachtergaele--Ogata--Sims,
math-ph/0603064v1; its source/read scope is recorded separately. That prior
is not claimed newly re-proved or read in full here.

For a one-site A of norm a, this also gives the literal PHYSICAL-radius
corollary needed for a stationary quasilocal-generator composition. Choose
the graph radius floor(R/2) and twirl outside it. Its support is within the
physical R ball because every graph hop has physical length at most2.
Since kappa_1<=74h_max+epsilon and 4e<=12,

    ||A(t)-A_R(t)||
       <=a min{2,12 exp[-R/2+(148h_max+2epsilon)|t|]},
    q_R<=2t_geom(2R+3)^3<=54t_geom(1+R)^3.                (L9p)

The support count uses the literal square-density predicate: every actual
qubit belongs to a Tanner square, each square has two qubits, and any unit

## 4. Finite-resolution original-bath correlations

define the real-time sector autocorrelations

    F_+/- (t)=Tr[rho_+/- A(t) A], A(t)=e^(itH) A e^(-itH).

The operator A_R(t)A is supported on at most q_R sites and has norm<=1.
It may be complex and need not commute with L; (L2)--(L3) remain valid.

    |F_+(t)-F_-(t)|
       <=2(2^q_R delta_(q_R)+delta_0)/(1-delta_0^2)
           +8s exp[2kappa_mu |t|-mu R].                  (L10)

    R=2Q, T=Q/(2kappa_hat), Q=2^(k-1).                   (L11)

## 5. Why this is still not the missing memory theorem

Near-identical local Gibbs states and finite-time autocorrelations permit
arbitrarily close but DISJOINT exact transition atoms. Exact Davies
secularization takes an unbounded time average and removes cross-frequency
interference. A tiny nonzero logical holonomy can therefore be invisible on
the window(L11) yet remain exactly resolved by that idealization.

In particular, (L13) says neither that an old dressed logical has small
full-frequency jump commutators nor that a finite-window generator fixes
rho_H. Original couplings are retained; replacing the generator requires
a separately justified bath-resolution, stationarity, Lamb-shift and
microscopic approximation budget over the proposed memory time. Those
debts are being audited by other workers, not silently supplied here.

No new Gibbs state, bath trajectory, huge geometry, or matrix fixture was
simulated. Directly constructing A_R(t) can require exponentially large
local matrices in q_R; the proof is not an efficient simulator. The primitive
graph/check/logical representation, canonical input precision, Gibbs
preparation and full physical bath costs remain paid. The source of a useful
time window here is d growing far faster than Q^3, not an assumed polynomial
algorithm or weak-coupling accuracy for exponential times.
