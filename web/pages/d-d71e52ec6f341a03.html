## Exact source interface and what fails

At d4,n2, the PMI and reflectional zero patterns both forbid the x³y and xy³ coefficients.

Assumption6.5, **PDFp17**, says the input rows are iid copies of a population-whitened vector: mean0 and population covarianceI. It does not require empirical covarianceI. Proposition6.7, **PDFp18**, allows any empirical minimizer, assumes a positive Riemannian Hessian at every point of a population minimizing signed-permutation orbit, and states inequality(12) with a constant depending only on tensor order:

    distance_to_true_orbit
       <= (Cd/kappa)(d||mu_d||F+d||mu_hat_d-mu_d||F)||mu_hat_d-mu_d||F.

No nearby-branch or quantitative global-separation premise is stated. AppendixA.3, **PDFpp28–30**, uses a local geodesic/Taylor comparison, absorbs remainders for sufficiently small error/nearby minimizers, and finally calls inequality(12) deterministic given the tensor realization. Local curvature alone cannot justify that literal uniform deterministic statement.

Our family proves this last assertion. It does **not**, by itself, refute the separate high-probability claim: the adverse sample event can be rare. ## Exact bounded-support family

Set epsilon=1/k, integer k>=1, a=3, b=3+epsilon. Put probability1/(2a) at each of +/-sqrt(a)e1, probability1/(2b) at each of +/-sqrt(b)e2, and the remaining probability at0. The distribution is centered, has population covarianceI, and is bounded in norm by2 uniformly in k. Its coordinates are pairwise mean independent by sign symmetry and disjoint support. Its fourth-moment polynomial is

    mu(x,y)=a x^4+b y^4.

For a rotation with c=cos(theta), s=sin(theta), the odd polynomial coefficients are, up to signs,

    4cs(-a c²+b s²), 4cs(-a s²+b c²).

The squared tensor-Frobenius/Bombieri residual is their squared sum divided by4. Since a!=b, a zero residual requires cs=0: if cs!=0, both zero equations force a=b and c²=s². Hence the population orbit is uniquely identified modulo signed permutations. This is verified uniqueness, **not Zariski-genericity in the entire PMI tensor space**.

Near every signed permutation the objective is

    F(theta)=4(a²+b²)theta²+O(theta⁴).

The tangent rotation has squared Frobenius norm2, so the Riemannian Hessian lower eigenvalue is kappa=4(a²+b²)>=72. Thus the local condition remains uniformly strong as epsilon tends to0.

Let N=6(3k+1)² and take the positive-probability iid count event:

    each x-axis atom: (3k+1)² observations;
    each y-axis atom: 9k² observations;
    origin: 18k²+24k+4 observations.

All counts are positive integers and sum toN. Total empirical axis frequencies are1/3 and3/b². Therefore

    mu_hat(x,y)=3(x⁴+y⁴), ||mu_hat-mu||F=epsilon.

The45degree rotation is an empirical global PMI minimizer with zero residual. Its distance to the closest signed permutation is sqrt(4-2sqrt(2)), independent of k. Meanwhile ||mu||F<=5, epsilon<=1 and kappa>=72 make the literal inequality's right side at most C4*epsilon/3. No finite tensor-order-only C4 works for the whole family. The all-k count/moment formulas and Hessian are reconstructed symbolically, rather than inferred from the three numerical k cases.

In the45degree representation the empirical even coupling matrix is

    Qalt=[[3/2,9/2],[9/2,3/2]], half_pair_gap=-3.

The global positive-gap certificate rejects this alternative. At the coordinate candidate Qc=3I, qc=3, rho=0; it gives sqrt(d)<=sqrt(2)epsilon/3 for every compatible true positive-gap frame. Thus it supplies precisely the missing global assumption in this structured subclass.

## Empirical whitening is a separate interface

Section6.1/Algorithm1, **PDFp16**, empirically whitens its sample matrix. For the same population family, take equal total frequencies1/3 on each axis and1/3 at0, with N divisible by6. Sample means are0. The raw covariance and fourth moments are

    Cov_hat=diag(1,b/3), raw_fourth=3x⁴+(b²/3)y⁴.

Whitening by diag(1,sqrt(3/b)) makes both the empirical covarianceI and the fourth moment exactly3(x⁴+y⁴). The same45degree global PMI solution is available. Transforming the mixing estimate back gives

    Ahat=diag(1,sqrt(b/3)) R45,
    ||Ahat-R45||F=sqrt(b/3)-1 -> 0.

The wrong branch therefore stays far from the actual identity mixing orbit as epsilon->0. This is a pipeline ambiguity example. Rows transformed by an estimated sample whitening matrix are no longer iid; they are **not** silently substituted into Assumption6.5. The literal deterministic counterexample uses the separate population-whitened input above. Both events and distinctions are retained in the exact producer/checker.

## Recent LiMIAM framework and an externally published gate

Mesters, Ribot, Seigal and Zwiernik, [arXiv2605.04381v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04381v1), submitted **6 May 2026**, supplies a substantive recent application framework: causal discovery in a recursive linear model with dependent disturbances. We read §§2–4, the simulation design and selected application discussion. The full PDF/version is retained.

Assumption3.2, **PDFp7**, takes X=A epsilon, A=(I-B)^-1 for an unknown DAG, diagonal nonzero disturbance covariance, finite order-d moments, and one-sided zeros E[epsilon_i^(d-1)epsilon_j]=0 when j is not an ancestor of i. It does not require a reflectional tensor or positive pair gaps. Theorem3.6, **PDFp9**, gives generic identification from the pair (mu2,mu_d), constructively:

    S_ij^(d)=E[Xi Xj^(d-1)]E[Xj²]-E[Xi Xj]E[Xj^d].

Sources have every score0; generically each non-source has a nonzero score. Remove a source by regression, recurse, then recoverB by LDL/regression in the resulting order. This is a **competent, more broadly applicable equally informed moment/covariance control**. Full reflection-axis acquisition is unnecessary for its generic source-node task. Its distribution-level algorithm uses a stronger explicit exogenous-variable detectability condition, Assumption3.12/PDFp11, for conditional-mean residual tests. These assumptions must be charged.

Published **Example2.2, PDFpp5–6**, gives an exact numerical model:

    X1=epsilon1, X2=epsilon1+epsilon2;
    epsilon_i=sigma Zi;
    Zi iid uniform[-sqrt(3),sqrt(3)];
    sigma² in {0.1,1.9}, equal probabilities.

Population covariance of epsilon isI; fourth cumulants are k1=k2=129/500 and c=81/100. Its homogeneous cumulant is

    h(u,v)=k(u⁴+v⁴)+6c u²v².

The actual disturbance Q has diagonal k, off-diagonal3c and half-pair-gap k-3c=-543/250. It is outside our positive-gap/QPD true-source promise. At45degrees the same polynomial becomes a QPD quartic with diagonal168/125, off-diagonal-207/250 and half-gap543/250. Both exact transformations, its observed polynomial h(x+y,y), and covariance[[1,1],[1,2]] are independently checked.

This is a scientific semantic obstruction: **existence of some positive-gap/QPD representation does not certify that the actual physical or causal disturbances have that representation**. Fitted candidate positivity cannot validate the generative promise. A noise certificate under a false physical promise can identify the alternate frame perfectly while conveying the wrong causal interpretation.

The fourth-moment DirectLiMIAM scores for this equal-kurtosis unit-edge example also vanish for both variables: S_21=0 and S_12=k1-k2=0. It is exceptional for the paper's generic theorem. Exchangeability gives E[epsilon1|epsilon1+epsilon2]=(epsilon1+epsilon2)/2, so the reversed linear residual also satisfies mean independence; Assumption3.12 detectability fails here. This is **not a contradiction to the generic theorem**, and the source uses the example to exhibit ICA failure, not to claim universal direct recovery at this parameter.

Our executed noisy positive common-variance fixtures are our own model instantiations, not this published numerical data. No oil-market, biological or general dependent-noise success is claimed.

## Older competent controls and novelty boundary

Its §4.1.1 stability criterion is algorithmic stability under independent components, not a verified global coefficient-noise theorem for our dependent pair-gap promise.

For observed binary coefficients [a,b,c,d,e], the identical classical contrast is

    C(theta)=(3(a+e)+c)/4 + ((a+e-c)/4)cos4theta
                                   + ((b-d)/4)sin4theta.

Its maximum is constant+sqrt(A²+B²). Our control uses this old closed form, rationally rounds its proposed frame and applies the same exact posterior certificate. An explicit global SOS upper certificate is

    2 amp(amp-A X-B Y)=(amp X-A)²+(amp Y-B)²

on X²+Y²=1 and amp²=A²+B², amp>0; zero amplitude is constant contrast. It is checked symbolically, without supplying a free optimization oracle or running a commercial SDP. The degree-four orthogonal contrast deficit is a separate explicit SOS. Equally informed tensor/eigenmatrix proposals are also postchecked.
