Abstract
We construct a new class of multipartite states possessing
orthogonal symmetry. This new class defines a convex hull of
multipartite states which are invariant under the action of local
unitary operations introduced in our previous paper On
multipartite invariant states I. Unitary symmetry. We study
basic properties of multipartite symmetric states: separability
criteria and multi-PPT conditions.
pacs03.65.Ud, 03.67.-a
I Introduction
In a recent paper [1] we analyzed multipartite states
invariant under local unitary operations. For bipartite systems
one has two classes of unitary invariant states: Werner states
[2] invariant under
ρ⟶U⊗U​ρ​(U⊗U)†,\rho\ \longrightarrow\ U{\,\otimes\,}U\,\rho\,(U{\,\otimes\,}U)^{\dagger}\ ,
(1)
for any U∈U⁡(d)U\in U(d), where U⁡(d)U(d) denotes the group of unitary d×dd\times d matrices, and isotropic states [3] which
are invariant under
ρ⟶U⊗U¯​ρ​(U⊗U¯)†,\rho\ \longrightarrow\ U{\,\otimes\,}\overline{U}\,\rho\,(U{\,\otimes\,}\overline{U})^{\dagger}\ ,
(2)
where U¯\overline{U} is the complex conjugate of UU in some
basis. In [1] we proposed a natural generalization of
bipartite symmetric states to multipartite systems consisting of
an arbitrary even number of dd-dimensional subsystems (qudits).
In the present paper we introduce a new class of states which
combines above symmetries (1) and (2), i.e. it
contains states which are both U⊗UU{\,\otimes\,}U and U⊗U¯U{\,\otimes\,}\overline{U}–invariant,
that is, states invariant under all unitary operations UU such that U=U¯U=\overline{U}:
ρ⟶O⊗O​ρ​(O⊗O)T,\rho\ \longrightarrow\ O{\,\otimes\,}O\,\rho\,(O{\,\otimes\,}O)^{T}\ ,
(3)
with O∈O⁡(d)⊂U⁡(d)O\in O(d)\subset U(d), where O⁡(d)O(d) denotes the set of d×dd\times d orthogonal matrices. Such states were first considered
in [4] (see also [5]). In a slightly
different context symmetric states were studied also in
[6]. Recently [7] unitary invariant 3-partite
states were used to test multipartite separability criteria.
Here we present a general construction of
O⊗OO{\,\otimes\,}O–invariant states for multipartite systems consisting of
an arbitrary even number of dd-dimensional subsystems. It turns
out that orthogonally invariant states of 2​K2K–partite system
(with KK being a positive integer) define (3K−1)(3^{K}-1)–invariant
simplex. We analyze (multi)separability criteria and the hierarchy
of multi-PPT conditions [1, 8, 9]. It is hoped that these
new family would serve as a useful laboratory to study
multipartite entanglement
[10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15].

IV 2​K2K–partite states
IV.1 General contruction
Generalization to 2​K2K–partite system is straightforward.
Following [1] we introduce 2​K2K qudits with the total space
ℋ=ℋ1⊗…⊗ℋ2​K=(ℂd)⊗ 2​K\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{H}_{1}{\,\otimes\,}\ldots{\,\otimes\,}\mathcal{H}_{2K}=(\mathbb{C}^{d})^{{\,\otimes\,}2K}. We may still interpret the total system
as a bipartite one with ℋA=ℋ1⊗…⊗ℋK\mathcal{H}_{A}=\mathcal{H}_{1}{\,\otimes\,}\ldots{\,\otimes\,}\mathcal{H}_{K} and ℋB=ℋK+1⊗…⊗ℋ2​K\mathcal{H}_{B}=\mathcal{H}_{K+1}{\,\otimes\,}\ldots{\,\otimes\,}\mathcal{H}_{2K}. Equivalently, we may introduce KK
Alices and KK Bobs with ℋAi=ℋi\mathcal{H}_{A_{i}}=\mathcal{H}_{i} and
ℋBi=ℋK+i\mathcal{H}_{B_{i}}=\mathcal{H}_{K+i}, respectively. Then
ℋA\mathcal{H}_{A} and ℋB\mathcal{H}_{B} stand for the composite KK
Alices’ and Bobs’ spaces.
Now, let 𝜶\alpha be a trinary KK-dimensional
vector, i.e. 𝝈=(α1,…,αK)\mbox{\boldmath$\sigma$}=(\alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{K}) with αj∈{0,1,2}\alpha_{j}\in\{0,1,2\}. In
analogy to (34) let us define a family of 2​K2K–partite

(60)
with 𝐎=(O1,…,OK)\mathbf{O}=(O_{1},\ldots,O_{K}), and

IV.3 Reductions
It is evident that reducing the 2​K2K partite state ρ∈ΣK\rho\in\Sigma_{K} with respect to Ai⊗BiA_{i}{\,\otimes\,}B_{i} pair one obtains
2​(K−1)2(K-1)–partite state ρ′∈ΣK−1\rho^{\prime}\in\Sigma_{K-1} living in
ℋ1⊗…​ℋˇi⊗…⊗ℋˇi+K⊗…⊗ℋ2​K,\mathcal{H}_{1}{\,\otimes\,}\ldots\check{\mathcal{H}}_{i}{\,\otimes\,}\ldots{\,\otimes\,}\check{\mathcal{H}}_{i+K}{\,\otimes\,}\ldots{\,\otimes\,}\mathcal{H}_{2K}\ ,
(73)
where ℋˇi\check{\mathcal{H}}_{i} denotes the omitting of
ℋi{\mathcal{H}}_{i}. The corresponding fidelities are given by
π(α1​…​αK−1)′=∑βπ(α1​…​αi−1​β​αi​…​αK−1).\pi^{\prime}_{(\alpha_{1}\ldots\alpha_{K-1})}\ =\ \sum_{\beta}\,\pi_{(\alpha_{1}\ldots\alpha_{i-1}\beta\alpha_{i}\ldots\alpha_{K-1})}\ .
(74)
Note, that reduction with respect to a ‘mixed’ pair, say Ai⊗BjA_{i}{\,\otimes\,}B_{j} with i≠ji\neq j, is equivalent to two ‘natural’ reductions
with respect to Ai⊗BiA_{i}{\,\otimes\,}B_{i} and Aj⊗BjA_{j}{\,\otimes\,}B_{j} and hence it gives
rise to 2​(K−2)2(K-2)–partite invariant state. This procedure
establishes a natural hierarchy of multipartite 𝐎⊗𝐎\mathbf{O}{\,\otimes\,}\mathbf{O}–invariant states.
