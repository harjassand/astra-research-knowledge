## Claim and independent reconstruction

The baseline claims an exact rational, one-step Foster and recovery compiler
for the admitted open weakly reversible bimolecular class (unary drain paths,
support-production closure, and a partitioned linear/diagonal-quadratic drift
certificate). It does not claim recognition of all endotactic systems. I
reconstructed the load-bearing drift step: for (W=w\cdot x), a quadratic
source contributes its exact falling factorial (x_i(x_i-1)), not (x_i^2).
For (i\in S), combining (c_i x_i-x_i(x_i-1)) gives
\(-x_i^2+(c_i+1)x_i\le -x_i+\max(0,c_i+2)^2/4\); for (i\in T) the
linear and diagonal terms are at most (-x_i). Zero-source terms are bounded
by (b_0), while mixed terms are nonpositive. This yields
\(LW\le B-N\le B-W/w_{\max}\), including the zero/one-count boundary.
The stopped proper-Lyapunov argument then gives nonexplosion and finite mean
return to the finite core. The drain/production words are enabled molecular
paths; the lower hazard bound \(\alpha=\ell_{\min}/(2Q)\) and geometric
retries support the stated explicit, possibly enormous, hitting-time bound.
I found no algebraic counterexample to these baseline derivations.

The exact basis search is complete for the declared certificate class: the
constraints are rational linear inequalities in (w\), (w_i\ge1) makes the
feasible polyhedron pointed, and a nonempty pointed polyhedron has a vertex.
Enumerating all (d\)-row bases and checking them exactly finds a feasible
vertex if one exists. This certifies only condition (D), not general
recurrence. The support-production word is an explicit implementation of a
known support-exhaustion/accessibility mechanism.

## Counterexample and runnable evidence

The source's all-rate-one network
\(0\leftrightarrow A, 0\leftrightarrow B,
A\leftrightarrow A+B, B\leftrightarrow A+B\)
is a valid exact obstruction to completeness of that one-step certificate.
For (\pi(a,b)\propto1/(a!b!)\), every listed forward/reverse edge satisfies
detailed balance. Immigration and death make the chain irreducible. Its total
count drift is exactly \(L(A+B)=2-2ab\le2\); stopping at count (M) bounds the
probability of reaching (M) by time (t) by \((N_0+2t)/M\), which proves
nonexplosion because rates are bounded on each finite count set. The
normalized product-Poisson stationary law is therefore a positive recurrent
counterexample to completeness, not to recurrence.

No diagonal (2A) or (2B) source exists, so either species in (S) makes
the required source inequality (0\le-1) impossible. With (S=\varnothing),
the first-source coefficients are \(-w_A+w_B\le-1\) and
\(w_A-w_B\le-1\), whose sum is the contradiction (0\le-2). This
independently verifies the rejection reason. The fresh exact checker
`c10_s03_baseline_audit.py` performs 450 local detailed-balance checks and 625
exact count-drift checks; all pass. The identities above, rather than the
finite grid, prove the all-state statements.

## Cost audit, prior art, and remaining gaps

For dense binary input length (L), (d,R\le L), endpoint bit height
(b\le L). Clearing at most (R) rational denominators gives constraint
height (h=O(Rb+\log R)). Hadamard's bound gives vertex numerator and
denominator bit length (O(dh+d\log d)=O(L^3)). There are at most
\(2^d\binom{3d+R2^R}{d}=2^{O(L^2)}\) candidate partition/basis pairs; exact
Gaussian elimination and feasibility tests are polynomial in those heights.
This is a finite singly exponential bit algorithm, not a polynomial-time
solver. The recovery word may have length \(q2^{O(d)}\); the attempt exponent
\(H\) and expanded rational \(\alpha^H\) can therefore be exponential in
binary input length. The claimed conservative overall \(2^{O(L^3)}\) bound is
consistent with the described height and search bounds under the stated
dense encoding, but the output itself may be that large. Physical hitting
time, actual firings, and the cost of operating a controlled process are not
made small by computability of the certificate. I found no smaller uniform
bound in the baseline.

Published prior art includes Foster-Lyapunov criteria and LP/SDP sufficient
tests for important unimolecular/bimolecular subclasses (Gupta, Briat &
Khammash, 2014, PLoS Comput Biol 10:e1003669,
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003669), and support exhaustion as an
accessibility construction (Gupta & Khammash, arXiv:1312.2879, Lemma 3.1,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.2879). Thus the Lyapunov and support principles are
not new. The exact rational vertex enumerator, boundary word plus hazard/retry
compiler, and cost packaging may be useful, but novelty is UNKNOWN; no priority
search or external proof review establishes a new theorem.

The baseline's own unresolved gates are real: finite diagnostics do not prove
the symbolic theorem; full external proof/implementation review is absent; the
certificate is deliberately incomplete and rejects a recurrent detailed-
balanced example; no general costed synthesis for arbitrary bimolecular
endotactic systems follows. The later c10_s03 hinge report is an author-reported
separate continuation; this audit does not count its claims as independently
verified.
