5. All twelve scalar channels and constant SPD metric averaging

Every real bilinear form in A and the six compatible jets that is invariant
under simultaneous left outputSO(2) is a linear combination of the twelve
dot/cross channels(zplus,zminus). Their independence and completeness
follow also from the24-feature decomposition in Section6: these are the
entire weight-zero sector, of dimension12.

A priori a scalar-only certificate can have an arbitrary real12 PSD Gram,
including zplus/zminus cross terms, and an arbitrary closed omega. To
reduce it to common6, use the actual symmetries of H_K:

  input quarter-turn:
  (x,y,X,Y,Z)->(y,-x,Z,-Y,X),
  (zplus,zminus)->(-zminus,zplus);

  paired output/input reflection:
  (x,y,X,Y,Z)->(T x,-T y,T X,-T Y,T Z), T=diag(1,-1),
  (zplus,zminus)->(zplus,-zminus).

These actions and invariance of H_K are checked exactly. Averaging a PSD
Gram over the reflection kills the cross blocks. Averaging over C4 then
equates the two diagonal blocks. PSD is preserved by each congruence and
by averaging, so any scalar12 certificate yields a common6 PSD certificate.
The preceding impossibility therefore excludes all scalar12 certificates.

For completeness, include a constant SPD diffusion metric M. Let the
matrix-space transformation be Lambda(A)=T A R, where R is the orthogonal
domain transformation above. The compatible derivatives transform as

  B'_i=T(sum_p R_{pi}B_p)R.

The weighted Hessian term becomes E_{R M R^T}. The two-form term becomes
det(R)(Lambda^*omega)(B1,B2). The determinant sign is essential for the
domain reflection. Pullback commutes with exterior differentiation, and
the sign is constant, so closedness is preserved. The transformed Gram
is again PSD and uses the same scalar channel space.

Averaging over these finite symmetries sends M to(trace M/2)I. For the
usual normalization trace M=2 it gives I; otherwise divide the identity,
correction and Gram by the positive scalar trace M/2. It then produces
the common6 certificate already ruled out. Thus changing the constant
SPD metric does not repair the scalar-bilinear-SOS route at this boundary.

This does NOT exclude arbitrary non-SOS nonnegative corrected densities,
gradient-dependent/nonconstant metrics, nonlinear or nonlocal flows, or
unrestricted bilinear-SOS certificates. The latter succeed here by spin2.
