# A length marker extends the release's hitting construction to cyclic recognizable series

Status: internal deduction, conditional on the family116 characteristic-zero/positive-characteristic hitting primitives reconstructed below. `/root/frontier_check_hitting` independently reconstructed the characteristic-zero extension before this note was saved. This is neither external validation nor a historical-priority claim. The original manuscripts do not state this cyclic-series construction.

## Exact question and legal oracle

Let F be a field, n,m >=1, and suppose an unknown proper recognizable noncommutative series has SOME representation

    R(x)=u(I_m - sum_i B_i x_i)^(-1)v
        =sum_w (u B_w v) x_w,

where u is 1 by m, v is m by1, and the B_i are m by m over F. States and coefficient heights are not supplied. Only n and an upper bound m are supplied. A smaller representation is padded with zero states. Proper here means the displayed pencil has constant term I; it does NOT mean an arbitrary expression for the same rational function is defined at zero.

The matrix-evaluation oracle returns the exact D by D matrix

    (u tensor I_D)(I_(mD)-sum_i B_i tensor X_i)^(-1)(v tensor I_D)

when defined. Every jointly nilpotent input tuple is in this realization's domain. The algorithm must generate its matrices independently of all unknown coefficients. Word-coefficient queries, samples from a stochastic process, and evaluations of an expression with canceled but illegal inverses are different interfaces.

Objective: one universal tuple W on which every nonzero R in this state-bounded class is nonzero. Nonzero means some coefficient uB_wv is nonzero. No invertible-value conclusion is requested.

## Why removing acyclicity directly fails

Use n2,m3,

    B1 = [[0,0,0],[1,1,0],[1,1,0]],
    B2 = [[0,0,0],[0,0,0],[-2,-1,0]], u=e3^T, v=e1.

The proper rational series is

    R=(1-x2)(1-x1)^(-1)x1-2x2.

Its degree-one coefficients are 1,-2, so it is nonzero. Let J_i(a)=integral_0^z a(t)/(t+i) dt, the release's characteristic-zero word operators. The ODE Y'=sum_i B_i/(z+i) Y, Y(0)=v has exact solution Y=(1,z,0)^T: its second derivative component is (1+z)/(z+1)=1 and its third is (1+z)/(z+1)-(2+z)/(z+2)=0. Thus R(J)1=0 identically. This is not a counterexample to the paper: its faithfulness theorem is for FINITE word sums, and its formula/ABP representation is acyclic. An infinite recognizable sum can cancel the encoded series completely.

The counterexample disproves applying the paper's first-column faithfulness to arbitrary cyclic m-state systems without another argument. It does not show that every other column of R(T) vanishes, and does not rule out another hitting tuple.

## Characteristic-zero construction and proof

Family116, 'One Rational Matrix Hitting Point for Noncommutative Formulas', 24September2026, pinned adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a:

* Lemma2.1, PDFpp5-6: finite word series h_w are linearly independent over every characteristic-zero field. Its proof uses triangular monodromy spanning and transfers a nonzero rational minor.
* Theorem4.2, PDFpp9-10: for G'=P/q G, G invertible in F[[z]], q(0) nonzero, degq=n, degP<=n-1, a nonzero constant scalar component has order <=(n-1)binom(m,2)+m-1. The Wronskian/minor proof is the older Moura multiplicity method, reproduced in the release.
* Equation13, PDFp10: in degree0,...,d-1 coefficient coordinates,

      (T_i)_(r,q)=(-1)^(r-q-1)/(r i^(r-q)), 0<=q<r<d,

  and zero otherwise. These matrices implement J_i modulo z^d.

Set d=(n-1)binom(m,2)+m. Introduce a coefficient indeterminate lambda, and define

    G_lambda(z)=sum_w lambda^|w| B_w h_w(z).

Every z^r coefficient receives only words of length <=r, because ord h_w>=|w|. Consequently G_lambda belongs to Mat_m(F[lambda][[z]]) and also to Mat_m(F(lambda)[[z]]). It is invertible at zero and satisfies

    G_lambda'=lambda (sum_i B_i/(z+i)) G_lambda,
    G_lambda(0)=I.

The coefficient of lambda^ell in uG_lambda v is the finite word encoding of R's homogeneous degree-ell slice. If R is nonzero, some such slice is nonzero, and finite word independence makes that coefficient a nonzero z series. Hence uG_lambda v is nonzero over F(lambda)[[z]]. The same m-dimensional multiplicity theorem, applied with coefficient field F(lambda), implies that a nonzero coefficient occurs below z^d.

Since T is jointly nilpotent of index d,

    R(lambda T)=sum_(|w|<d) lambda^|w| (uB_wv) T_w

is a polynomial matrix in lambda of degree <d. Its first column is precisely the retained coefficients of uG_lambda v. It is nonzero. Replace lambda by the regular multiplication matrix S_d on F[lambda]/(lambda^d), with S_d e_j=e_(j+1) for j<d-1 and S_d e_(d-1)=0. Define

    W_i=S_d tensor T_i, dimension D=d^2.

Reduction modulo lambda^d preserves every polynomial entry just shown to be nonzero. The regular representation of this coefficient algebra is injective (apply multiplication to1). Therefore R(W) is nonzero. In fact its column at e_lambda0 tensor e_z0 is nonzero. Word products W_w vanish for |w|>=d, so its proper domain is automatic.

This works simultaneously over EVERY characteristic-zero F with the same rational W, and uses no bound on inverse nesting of an arbitrary expression because no such expression is part of this oracle. For m1,d1 the tuple is zero; a nonzero one-state proper series necessarily has nonzero uv, so this edge case is valid.

## Every positive characteristic

Use 'Uniform Matrix Hitting Points in Every Positive Characteristic', 4October2026, same pinned commit. Proposition2.2 finite-word independence uses multiplicative-shift poles; Theorem4.1, PDFpp8-10 bounds a nonzero m-state shift component by n binom(m,2), including p2. Equations22,23,26 and Lemma5.1, PDFpp10-12 give

    N=1+n binom(m,2),
    U_i[r,q]=(1+t)^(i(r-q-1))/(t^r-1), q<r<N,
    E=1+binom(N,2)+n(N-1),
    D_t(t)=product_(r=1)^(N-1)(t^r-1).

Replace B_i by lambda B_i in the same formal system. Finite word independence degree by degree makes uH_lambda v nonzero over F(t,lambda)[[z]], and the shift-order theorem detects it below z^N. The matrix R(lambda U) is polynomial in lambda of degree <N. The source's triangular-chain argument clears every word entry by D_t, independently of all coefficients, with t-degree <E. D_t(0) is a unit, including p2.

Thus the nonzero polynomial entries of D_t R(lambda U) survive in

    F[t,lambda]/(t^E,lambda^N).

Represent both coefficient variables by regular multiplication. If T_i is the source prime-field matrix of size NE, the new tuple is

    W_i=S_N tensor T_i, D=N^2 E=O(n^4 m^8).

The proper inverse is a finite geometric sum on W, so descent preserves the actual evaluation; no arbitrary inverse-domain assertion is needed. The construction is deterministic polynomial bit time in n,m,log p, with primality PROMISED, and works over every extension field of F_p. No shift automorphism on a truncated ring is asserted.

## Charged cost, useful consequence, and boundaries

In characteristic zero D=O(n^2 m^4). Dense generation/output uses O(n D^2) rational records, each of bit length O(log d+d log(n+1)). Sparse Kronecker storage needs only O(n d^2) nonzero entries plus the known shift, although an oracle may require full dense matrices; charge that conversion. The original triangular tuple can be integerized by one polynomial-height diagonal similarity (frontier_experiments' independently derived corollary), but that is not a numerical-conditioning theorem.

In positive characteristic D=N^2E. Dense output has nD^2 residues of log p bits. All loops are polynomial. The state coefficients and oracle response bit lengths can be arbitrarily large: generator cost is not total evaluation cost. Exact black-box tests can be exponentially attenuated in the original Taylor basis. No noisy acquisition theorem follows.

The immediate consequence is exact equality testing of two proper recognizable series: put their difference in a direct-sum realization of at most m1+m2 states and use the tuple for that bound. It may also supply an injective residual feature map for output-only matrix-oracle automaton reconstruction. The peer worker develops that acquisition proof; this note does not import unverified query/dimension bounds from it.

The strongest same-release competitor already includes rational-coefficient affine pencils: 'Polynomial Hitting Lists for Noncommutative Rational Formulas', Corollary5.2, PDFp18, universally hits a feasible pencil of bounded order, and Corollary6.1, pp21-23 obtains noncommutative rank from nonadaptive ordinary-rank responses. Proper recognizable rational functions have a bounded affine realization and root augmentation, so the rational list supplies another route after its nonzero-to-invertible witness argument. It uses a three-parameter grid, a cyclotomic representation and polynomial bound O((n+s+1)^87 log(n+s+1)) on encoded output. Our length marker is a simpler nonzero-only, nilpotent, all-coefficient-field construction; no practical superiority has been measured.

Older primary comparators: Raz–Shpilka2005 already give polynomial WHITE-BOX formula/ABP PIT; Forbes–Shpilka arXiv1209.2408v2 (22September2013), Corollary4.7 gives quasipolynomial black-box NC-ABP hitting; Arvind–Chatterjee–Mukhopadhyay2309.15647v4 (11July2025), Theorem2/Corollary3 gives quasipolynomial rational-formula black-box hitting. The sparse integer singleton of Lakhani–Mukhopadhyay, ECCC TR26-120 revision2 (20July2026), Theorem2.1, is parameterized by SPARSITY, not state or formula size; a small ABP can have exponentially many words. Exact Hankel automaton learning itself is classical and often uses coefficient-membership AND equivalence queries; an output-only matrix oracle must not be conflated with these oracles.
