# Independent gate: local PMI curvature does not select a global branch

7 October 2026, root, cycle12. **PASS for the counterexample to the literal deterministic inequality (12) of Ribot–Seigal–Zwiernik arXiv2510.07525v1, Proposition6.7.** This is a version-specific internal mathematical audit, not external validation or a challenge to fixed-source asymptotic consistency. It does not by itself refute the separate high-probability inequality: the adverse empirical event can be rare. Historical priority of this observation is unknown.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07525v1, PDF pp17–18 and AppendixA.3 pp28–30. Recoverable PDF/text are in `sources/metric_use10/cycle12/`. Section6.3 Assumption6.5 explicitly assumes independent copies of a population-whitened random vector: mean zero and population covariance identity. It does not require empirical covariance identity. ## Exact population and empirical event

Let epsilon=1/k, integer k>=1, a=3 and b=3+epsilon. Give each point +/-sqrt(a)e1 probability 1/(2a), each point +/-sqrt(b)e2 probability 1/(2b), and the origin the remaining probability. These probabilities are positive, the mean is zero, and covariance is I. The raw fourth tensor has homogeneous form

    mu(x,y)=a x^4+b y^4.

The vector is bounded in norm by2, uniformly in k, hence has a uniform sub-Gaussian bound. The symmetry and disjoint support also give pairwise mean independence.

For a planar rotation with c=cos(theta), s=sin(theta), the two distinct odd tensor entries, up to harmless signs, are

    c s (-a c^2+b s^2),  c s (-a s^2+b c^2).

The PMI squared tensor-Frobenius residual is four times the sum of their squares. Since a!=b, its zero set is precisely signed coordinate permutations. Thus this example has unique population identification modulo the stated orbit. No Zariski-genericity hypothesis appears in Proposition6.7; this example is not asserted to be Zariski generic in the entire PMI tensor space. Near every signed permutation,

    F(theta)=4(a^2+b^2)theta^2+O(theta^4).

The tangent rotation has squared Frobenius norm2, so the stated population curvature condition holds with kappa=4(a^2+b^2)>=72.

Take N=6(3k+1)^2 independent draws and the positive-probability count event

    each x-axis atom: (3k+1)^2 observations,
    each y-axis atom: 9k^2 observations,
    origin: 4(3k+1)^2-18k^2 observations.

The counts sum to N and are positive. Their empirical raw fourth tensor is exactly

    mu_hat(x,y)=3(x^4+y^4),  ||mu_hat-mu||_F=epsilon.

Its PMI objective vanishes at the45degree rotation. That rotation is therefore an empirical global minimizer, while its distance to the closest signed permutation is

    d0=sqrt(4-2sqrt(2))>0.

Since ||mu||_F<=5 and epsilon<=1, the right-hand side of the paper's deterministic inequality (12) is at most

    (C4/72)*4*(5+1)*epsilon=C4*epsilon/3.

No finite C4 depending only on order can bound d0 for all k. Root independently reconstructed the moment, curvature, signed-permutation distance and integer counts; the argument does not rely on a numerical optimizer or a local Hessian approximation away from the true orbit.

## Interfaces and consequences

The count event above need not have empirical covariance I; that is legal under the explicit iid population-whitened finite-sample assumption. The separate algorithm that estimates whitening has a related, different example: equal total empirical frequencies1/3 on each axis, with1/3 at zero, give raw sample covariance diag(1,b/3) and raw fourth coefficients(3,b^2/3). Estimated whitening then gives exactly(3,3) and admits the same45degree PMI solution. Whitened rows in that pipeline are not iid, so the second observation is not silently substituted into Assumption6.5.

The example does not deny that a fixed, identified source has a positive separation outside every fixed neighborhood of its orbit. It shows that the local curvature and tensor norm alone do not control that separation uniformly over this family, and the separation is absent from the stated deterministic bound.

The independently gated global contrast certificate in `GLOBAL_AXIS_CERTIFICATE_GATE.md` uses a different hypothesis. For a candidate positive pair-gap quartic it compares both contrast deficits, bounding every compatible positive pair-gap hidden frame. The45degree even fit to3(x^4+y^4) has negative pair gap; the certificate correctly rejects that branch. This is a concrete downstream use of the audit: certify a global identifying contrast or acquire a global separation margin, rather than infer a global error guarantee from curvature at the desired solution.
