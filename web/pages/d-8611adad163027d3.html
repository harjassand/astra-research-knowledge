# An exact field barrier to a generic pair-replication shortcut

Question: do identical neighboring laws plus a product-preserving simultaneous two-replica move supply the polynomial additive constant required by the replication lemma? No. This is a complete elementary obstruction to that inference, not a counterexample to the matching source's separate energy comparison.

Let Ω={0,1}, μ(1)=p, μ(0)=1-p, with 0<p<=1/2. Propose to flip both coordinates (x,y)->(1-x,1-y), and accept by Metropolis for μ⊗μ. The kernel is stationary and reversible. It preserves x xor y and therefore has full pair gap zero. On the states00 and11 the edge conductance is p²; on01 and10 it is p(1-p). For any additive function a(x)+b(y), constants can be discarded, giving f=A x+B y and

    E_K(f)=p²(A+B)²+p(1-p)(A-B)²,
    Var_mu(a)=p(1-p) A².

The optimal constant for **all** additive functions is exactly

    C_min=1/(4p).

Indeed, completing the square gives the identity

    [1/(4p)] E_K(f)-Var_mu(a)
       = [B+(2p-1)A]²/4 >=0.

Taking B=(1-2p)A and A!=0 attains equality, so a smaller C fails. The usual bound from the choice A=B alone is weaker and does not establish the optimal constant.

Set p=2^-b, an explicitly known rational with O(b) bits. Neighboring tiers may all have the identical law μ, so every density ratio is exactly one. Nevertheless C_min=2^(b-2), and the replication theorem's q>=16C requires at least2^(b+2) slots per tier. The required memory is already exponential in the law's encoding length. This is not remedied by accurately implementing the inexpensive Metropolis arithmetic.

An equally informed control samples the target law exactly: draw b independent fair bits, regard them as a uniform integer J in{0,...,2^b-1}, and output1 precisely when J=0. It costs O(b) bits/work and fixed time, without a counting oracle. Replication is an expensive valid construction for this input, not a necessary sampler or an advantage. The example shows why a polynomial bound on C, not merely stationary pair invariance or closeness of adjacent laws, is a load-bearing input to an efficient new application.

This construction is the biased independent-spin analogue of the uniform simultaneous-flip control in `ADDITIVE_PAIR_REPLICATION_LEMMA.md`. For p=1/2 the exact constant is1/2, as in that control. It makes no claim about zero-field spin-glass cluster dynamics or the OpenAI matching comparison. Its algebra is our reconstruction of a classical two-state Metropolis primitive; historical novelty is unknown and no priority claim is made.
