## Log-factorial enclosure

For integer n≥1 define

    S2(n)=(n+1/2)log n−n+(1/2)log(2π)+1/(12n)−1/(360n³).

The bound S2(n)≤log(n!)≤S2(n)+1/(1260n⁵) is the classical positive-real Stirling remainder bound. Its load-bearing sign can also be reconstructed from Binet's formula, NIST DLMF5.9.10:

    log Γ(n)=(n−1/2)log n−n+(1/2)log(2π)
             +2∫₀∞ arctan(s/n)/(e^(2πs)−1) ds.

Since the identity

    arctan x=x−x³/3+∫₀ˣ u⁴/(1+u²) du

holds for every x≥0, its remainder lies between0 andx⁵/5, without a small-x restriction. Integrate these bounds against the positive Binet kernel. The elementary moment formula, obtained by expanding1/(e^(2πs)−1) into positive exponentials and integrating each,

    ∫₀∞ s^r/(e^(2πs)−1) ds = r! ζ(r+1)/(2π)^(r+1),

gives the first correction1/(12n), second−1/(360n³), and upper remainder1/(1260n⁵). Finally log(n!)=log Γ(n)+log n. This proves the displayed enclosure, rather than treating an asymptotic series as exact. [DLMF5.9](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.9), [DLMF5.11(ii)](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11).

The code uses this only for n≥32; smaller factorials are exact integers. π is enclosed by Machin's identityπ=16atan(1/5)−4atan(1/239), with40 alternating terms and the signed next-term bound for each arctangent. All logarithms and positive coefficients are propagated outward. Thus a cell's log predictive probability

    log 2! +Σ_j log C_j!−log(N+2)!

is enclosed by subtracting the upper factorial bound for its lower endpoint and the lower bound for its upper endpoint. Counts and arithmetic are rational/integer throughout. The 60-bit final outward rounding is included. A finite comparison to the exact integer-denominator reference checks four synthetic counts up to3M per row; it is a replay check, not the proof of the enclosure.

## Fixed-point logarithm enclosure

Write x=2^k y with1≤y<2. Round y outward at96 fractional bits; the rounded endpoints stay in[1,2]. Set z=(y−1)/(y+1), so0≤z≤1/3. With48 terms,

    log y=2Σ_(j=0)^47 z^(2j+1)/(2j+1)+R,
    0≤R≤2z^97/[97(1−z²)].

This follows by bounding every remaining denominator below97 and summing the geometric series. At scale S=2^100, enclose z by integers zl/S≤z≤zh/S and z² by z2l/S≤z²≤z2h/S. At every multiplication use floor for the lower recurrence and ceiling for the upper recurrence. Consequently the lower/upper powers pl/S,ph/S enclose z^(2j+1) inductively, including the unprinted power z^97 after the last iteration. Sum lower floor(2pl/(2j+1)) and upper ceil(2ph/(2j+1)). The tail in integer units is at most

    ceil(2ph S/[97(S−z2h)]).

Its denominator is strictly positive because z2h/S encloses at most1/9 plus rounding. This proves the returned interval contains log y. The separately enclosed log2 term is multiplied by k, reversing endpoints when k<0, then rounded outward at60 fractional bits. Finite105-input checks return identical dyadic intervals to the older growing-rational reference; the recurrence and analytic tail establish the universal arithmetic guarantee.
