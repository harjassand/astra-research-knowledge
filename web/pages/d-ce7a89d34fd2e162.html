# Robust sampled-code power bound: T-invariance, zeros, and clock scale

This is a post-freeze consequence of the independently derived `invariant_code_power_lemma.md` (whose timestamp precedes `CROSS_PHASE.json`). Cross-phase attribution is explicit: c08_l02 independently froze a Fano/readout-error interface for flow-invariant regions; c08_l10 independently froze an iid decoded-stream bridge and a zero-field global bound. The result below combines those information-theoretic interfaces with the stronger map-invariant localized cocycle estimate already derived here. It does not claim novelty or external review.

## Theorem

Let `(M^3,g)` be closed, let `X` be a smooth divergence-free field (zeros are allowed), let `U={X!=0}`, `mu=dVol_g/V`, `S=Def_g X`, and `nu>0`. Assume the strain-viscosity stationary force law

```text
∇_X X - 2 nu div_g S + grad_g p = F,
W = ∫_M <F,X> dVol_g = 2 nu ∫_M |S|² dVol_g.
```

Choose a physical sampling interval `tau>0` and put `T=phi_tau`. Let `C` be measurable and T-invariant modulo `mu`, with `alpha=mu(C)>0` and `mu(C\U)=0`; write `eta=mu|C/alpha`. Let `(Ω,P,Theta)` be a stationary extension of `(C,eta,T)`: `P` is Theta-invariant and a factor map `pi:Ω→C` satisfies `pi o Theta=T o pi` and `pi_*P=eta`. Let `D:Ω→{1,...,K}` be an ideal source symbol and `chat:C→{1,...,K}` a measurable physical decoder. If

```text
P{D != chat o pi} <= delta,
R_src <= h(D o Theta^j)   (nats per sample),
F_K(delta) = h_2(d) + d log(K-1),  d=min(delta,1-1/K),
```

then

```text
h_eta(T) >= [R_src-F_K(delta)]_+,
W >= 4 nu V alpha [R_src-F_K(delta)]_+² / tau².
```

The joining is essential if `D` is an external/ideal source rather than a measurable function of the physical state; a source law with no stationary coupling to the decoded trajectories implies nothing about the flow. If a lower bound `R_dec<=h(chat(T^j x))` on the physical decoded process itself is directly certified, the stronger conclusion is `W>=4 nu V alpha R_dec²/tau²`; no mismatch premise or Fano loss is needed. Likewise, if `D` is itself a deterministic factor of `(C,eta,T)`, its rate directly lower-bounds `h_eta(T)`. If a rate is specified in nats per physical time unit rather than per sample, omit `1/tau²`. For a binary/q-ary iid source, set `R_src=log K`; a rate in bits per sample is multiplied by `log 2` before applying the formula.

## Derivation

On `U`, set `e=X/|X|`, `P=I-e⊗e`, `q=<Se,e>`, and let `s>=0` be the positive eigenvalue of the trace-free part of `PSP|_{e-perp}`. The three-dimensional orthogonal block identity gives `|S|²=2s²+(3/2)q²+2|PSe|²>=2s²`.

For every T-invariant probability `eta` supported on `U`, the moving-normal quotient cocycle satisfies

```text
log ||Q_(n tau)(x)|| <= Σ_(j=0)^(n-1) ∫_0^tau s(phi_t(T^j x)) dt
                          - (1/2) log(|X(T^n x)|/|X(x)|).
```

Zeros away from `eta`'s support do not invalidate this estimate. The projected cocycle and its inverse are integrable because they are bounded by the full derivative and inverse derivative on compact `M`. Poincare recurrence for the invertible probability map `T` gives a subsequence `T^{n_k}x→x` for `eta`-a.e. recurrent `x∈U`; thus the line exponent of `X(x)` is zero, even though `|X|` need not be bounded below on all of `M`. Volume preservation gives normal determinant `|det Q_t|=|X(x)|/|X(phi_t x)|`; the full spectrum is therefore `chi,0,-chi`, `chi>=0`. Dividing the displayed estimate by `n`, using recurrence to remove the endpoint and Birkhoff for `s_tau(x)=∫_0^tau s(phi_t x)dt`, gives `∫chi d eta<=∫s_tau d eta`. Ruelle's inequality for `T` under this invariant probability yields

```text
h_eta(T) <= ∫ s_tau d eta.
```

Because `C` is T-invariant, `eta` is T-invariant. It need not be invariant under the entire continuous flow. For every `t`, `phi_t` preserves `mu` and `mu(phi_t C)=alpha`. Therefore

```text
tau ∫_M s² dmu
  >= ∫_0^tau ∫_(phi_t C) s² dmu dt
  = alpha ∫_0^tau ∫_C s²(phi_t x) deta(x) dt
  >= (alpha/tau) (∫_C s_tau deta)²
  >= (alpha/tau) h_eta(T)².
```

The middle inequality is Jensen on `eta × (dt/tau)`. Combining this with `|S|²>=2s²` and the integrated force identity proves `W>=4 nu V alpha h_eta(T)²/tau²`.

For the readout step, stationarity of the joining makes the one-coordinate mismatch bound hold at every sample. Fano and the block chain rule give

```text
H(D_0,...,D_(n-1) | chat_0,...,chat_(n-1)) <= n F_K(delta),
chat_j = chat(T^j(pi(omega))).
```

Thus `H(chat_0,...,chat_(n-1)) >= H(D_0,...,D_(n-1))-nF_K(delta)` and, after division by `n` and passage to entropy rates, `h(chat)>=h(D)-F_K(delta)`. Since `chat` is a finite partition of the physical state, `h(chat)<=h_eta(T)`, proving the entropy-rate lower bound. A direct bound on `h(chat)` also implies `h_eta(T)>=h(chat)` without Fano.

## Scope and attribution

The added interface relative to c08_l02's frozen claim is that `C` needs to be invariant only under the sampled map `phi_tau`, not every `phi_t`, and the ambient field may have zeros outside `C`. Relative to c08_l10's frozen statement, direct localization gives `alpha` rather than the `alpha²/beta` factor whenever the certified code region `C` is supported on `U`; when `C=U` this agrees with its sharper active-support special case. The proof charges power only for the stated continuum strain-viscosity force law. It still assumes a supplied/acquired invariant sampled code set, its volume fraction, clock, rate certificate, and decoder-error bound. It does not cover finite-horizon labels, nonstationary worst-case trajectories, a null-volume attractor, or an unverified empirical rate. Geometry, regularity, sensors, forcing hardware, coefficient precision, and the acquisition cost of `alpha`, `R`, and `delta` remain outside the theorem.
