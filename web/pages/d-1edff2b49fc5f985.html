The main construction replaces Euler local gates by rational quadratic gates
P2(tA)=I+tA+(tA)^2/2. On the admitted easy-plane XXZ cone, the edge weights
obey the six-vertex triangle inequalities and the field lift is homogeneous
log-concave. A palindromic layer has generator error O(tau^3/m^2), giving
m=O(tau+tau^(3/2)/sqrt(epsilon)) for a relative-epsilon partition trace.
The full derivation and explicit constants are in
revisions/SECOND_ORDER_AUDIT_AND_HIGH_SPIN.txt. The same gate was independently
frozen in c06_l08 before cross-exposure; this is a duplicate construction,
and my audit found its sharper unprojected constant valid.

The stronger cross-phase consequence composes the absolute one-step P2 norm
bound with c06_s01's all-q symmetric-spin projectors. Compression at each
layer preserves the absolute error, and the resolvent formula for log gives
the same second-order trace schedule for higher-spin XXZ. For c_v=0, the P2
signatures, projectors, and wire/quota partner are complement-invariant, so
c06_s03's no-field-balancing symmetry lemma also applies. A projector-aware
O(log N) transition is specified by grouping reinsertion candidates by row
versus column and sampling within groups with order-statistic trees; that
sampler and full wrapper were not implemented. These are attributed
compositions: c06_s01 supplies the spin projector, c06_s03 the no-balancing
argument, and c06_l03 the P2 gate/remainder/compression.
