Under the stated hypothesis that every isotropic log-concave law in every
dimension obeys the directional cumulant bound b, covariance-whitening the
entire suspension is valid. The transformed X-coordinate vectors form an
orthonormal frame, the first transformed vector has norm one, and it need not
be orthogonal to the frame. The exact same-replica tensor blocks imply

  sup_{regular isotropic mu, Var(f)=1} ||T_d^mu f||_HS
    = (1/d!) sup_{isotropic log-concave nu, |u|=1}
              ||kappa_{d+1}^nu(u,.,...,.)||_HS,

using regular approximation for the reverse direction. Thus Proposition 7.1's
factor 2 can be removed. In the source's K^d(d!)^2 cumulant bound this improves
the sufficient Poincare constant from 2 C K to C K. This does not change the
dimension-free nature or close a new KLS gate, and it does not validate the
source's separate localization/cumulant induction.

I derived a finite counterexample compiler from any acquired tilt-response
witness. If alpha <= ||T_d f|| is certified, q=d!alpha/sqrt(b)>1, M bounds
||Hess f||, and Hess V>=aI, take beta^2=4/(q^2-1) and
N=max(1,ceil(4M^2/(a^2(q^2-1)))). The resulting isotropic log-concave
suspension has a directional cumulant norm squared >b in dimension nN+1.
The near-threshold costs scale as N=O(M^2/(a^2(q-1))); whitening has
condition number O(1/(q-1)) in the worst case. A Gaussian example with
f=(x^2-1)/sqrt(2) has ||T_2 f||^2=1/2, and at beta^2=12,N=24 creates a
third-cumulant selected-block lower bound 12/7>3/2. Exact rational checks
passed in revisions/suspension_exact_sanity.py.

The only direct archive consequence I found is a narrow local D=1 codec for

the L2 likelihood-ratio expansion gives TV(mu_z,mu)<=Rr/[2(1-Rr)] when

claimed novel and does not handle the full c09 statistical-memory family.
The full suspension witness costs a, M, alpha, N base samples/evaluations,

no uniform polynomial acquisition or archive construction is obtained.
