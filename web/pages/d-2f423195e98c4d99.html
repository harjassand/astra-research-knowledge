1. EXACT STATEMENT AND RADIUS CONVENTION

Let E be any nonempty family of density matrices on a finite-dimensional H.
All logarithms below are natural. Define
 T(rho,nu)=||rho-nu||_1/2,
 b2(E)=inf_B sup_(rho in E,j=1,2) T(B_j(rho),rho),
 e(E)=inf_Lambda sup_(rho in E) T(Lambda(rho),rho),
where B is ONE full-domain CPTP map H -> H tensor H and Lambda is ONE
full-domain entanglement-breaking (EB) channel H -> H. Output correlations
are arbitrary; the unknown label is never provided to either channel.

For an effect 0<=F<=I put p=Tr(F rho), q=Tr(F sigma), and define
 D_obs(rho||sigma)=sup_F p ln(p/q).                        (1)
The p=0 term is zero. A term with p>0,q=0 is infinity. The radius used HERE is
 R(E)=inf_(sigma in closed conv(E)) sup_(rho in E)
                                      D_obs(rho||sigma). (2)
The convex-hull restriction is load-bearing: it makes the supplied
broadcaster approximately fix the SAME reference sigma. An arbitrary
external reference with small divergence but uncontrolled broadcast error
is not covered by the statement below.

Theorem candidate. Under precisely the complete internally reconstructed
Cycle03 weighted-tracial C4 / finite pair / centralization dependencies,
for every such E and 0<b2(E)<1,

 e(E) <= min{1,400 sqrt[(R(E)+1)/ln(1/b2(E))]}.           (3)

At b2=0, e=0; at b2=1 use e<=1. More concretely, for every actual B with
half marginal residual at most b, there exists ONE EB Lambda on the full
original domain satisfying (3) with that b. Dimension, family cardinality,
reference eigenvalues and POVM cardinality do not occur in the constant.
This is an existence result; no efficient acquisition or implementation is
provided. The radius can be computed by different binary tests for different
rho, so it is not the information obtained from one common measurement.

2. REFERENCE EXISTENCE, SUPPORT AND COMPATIBILITY

Closing E changes neither the channel errors nor its finite-prior Holevo
capacity C(E), by continuity in fixed finite dimension. It also changes
neither R(E): the function p ln(p/q), with the extended conventions above,
is jointly lower semicontinuous, hence its supremum over effects is lower
semicontinuous. A bound on D_obs along E therefore holds on its closure.
At p=q=0, the lower semicontinuity follows also from p ln(p/q)>=-q/e.

For fixed rho,F with p>0, -p ln(Tr F sigma) is convex and lower
semicontinuous in sigma. Supremizing over rho,F retains both properties.
The set closed conv(E) is compact. A finite radius exists: the Cycle03
Holevo-center proof supplies sigma_C in this set with D(rho||sigma_C)<=C
for every rho; Jain--Nayak gives D_obs<=D+ln 2 in natural units. Thus

 R(E) <= C(E)+ln 2 < infinity.                          (4)

The lower semicontinuous objective therefore attains its infimum at some
sigma. Finite R forces supp rho <= supp sigma for all rho: the effect
projecting onto ker sigma would otherwise give an infinite term in (1).

Every marginal T=B_j of the SAME supplied B obeys T(T sigma,sigma)<=b,
because sigma is a limit of convex combinations of promised states, trace
norm is convex, and T is continuous. Adding sigma to the experiment does
not increase this error or the reference bound: D_obs(sigma||sigma)=0.
No sample or hidden-label information is acquired by this mathematical step.

Let P=supp sigma. The actual CPTP replacement
 R_P(X)=PXP+Tr[(I-P)X]sigma
fixes all promised states and sigma. Replacing B by (R_P tensor R_P)B
on the input algebra B(PH) cannot increase the error. Sigma is faithful
there. Any EB channel on PH extends to the full original H by composition
with R_P. This supplies trace-preserving support compression, rather than
a nonphysical deletion of the orthogonal output block.

4. A STRICT SEPARATION FROM MAX-PRIOR HOLEVO CAPACITY

This elementary commuting example is an acquired exact construction here;
the general divergence/Holevo separation phenomenon is prior art. For N>=1,
let H_N have one vacuum vector and, for n=1,...,N, a classical orthogonal
flag sector of dimension 2^n. Put
 p_0=1/(N+1), p_n=1/[n(n+1)].
The weights sum to one. For every label tuple
 x=(x_1,...,x_N), x_n in {1,...,2^n}, define
 rho_x=p_0 |vac><vac| + directsum_(n=1)^N p_n|x_n><x_n|.
There are 2^[N(N+1)/2] labels and dim H_N=2^(N+1)-1.
All coordinates and probabilities are exact rational data.

Every state has entropy H(p_0,...,p_N). For every prior the mean has the
same flag weights; maximizing entropy within each flag gives
 C(E_N)<=sum_n p_n ln(2^n).
The uniform product prior attains every within-flag uniform mean
simultaneously. Hence the MAX-PRIOR capacity is EXACTLY

 C(E_N)=ln 2 sum_(n=1)^N 1/(n+1)
       =ln 2 [H_(N+1)-1] -> infinity.                  (10)

The uniform-prior center is
 sigma=p_0|vac><vac| + directsum_n p_n I_(2^n)/2^n.
It is faithful and is a convex combination of the promised states. We now
bound ALL effects, not only a chosen projective test. Since states are
diagonal, only the diagonal of an effect affects p,q. Let f_0 be its vacuum
entry and f_n its entry at the promised selected coordinate x_n. Then
 p=p_0 f_0+sum_n p_n f_n,
 q>=p_0 f_0+sum_n 2^(-n)p_n f_n.
If p>0, let k=ceil(2/p). The mass in flags n>k is at most
 sum_(n=k+1)^infinity 1/[n(n+1)]=1/(k+1)<=p/2.
At least p/2 of the selected mass is therefore in vacuum or flags n<=k.
On those flags the sigma probability is at least 2^(-k) times the selected
rho probability. Thus
 q>=p 2^(-k-1),
 p ln(p/q)<=p(k+1)ln 2<=(2+2p)ln 2<=4ln 2.
The p=0 case is zero, so

 R(E_N)<=4ln 2 for every N.                            (11)

This proves that a uniformly bounded convex observational radius does NOT
imply uniformly bounded max-prior Holevo capacity. It is a strict extension
of the sufficient complexity class in (3). It is not a sharp broadcasting
obstruction: these states commute, so both b2 and e are exactly zero.
Measuring the classical basis attains their Holevo information, so their
MAX-PRIOR accessible information also diverges. Calling (11) a bounded
accessible-information-capacity example would be incorrect.
