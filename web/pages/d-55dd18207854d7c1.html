STATEMENT AND LEGAL INPUT

Let n>=1 and rho=sum_(k=0)^n pi_k |D_nk><D_nk|, with pi simplex. Promise:
rho is FULLY separable across all n qubits. Populations are either explicit
rationals, or come with a charged absolute-approximation procedure taking
poly(n,L,P) bit work to acquire error2^-P. Finite exponentials of known
rational exponents have such a procedure by max-shift and clipping below.
Unencoded arbitrary real populations or a free state-acquisition oracle are
not included. L includes the actual input and tolerance encodings.

For any rational0<eps<1, a classical algorithm returnsO(n^2) positive pure
iid product labels with expected-state trace distance<eps and total bit
cost polynomial in n,L,log1/eps. Exact algebraic pure descriptors are direct;
exact rational pure2x2 density matrices and a bounded-fair-bit sampler follow
with the charged rounding in Sections7--8. No model-specific density/eigenvalue
floor, prefix conditioning table or strict N2 moment margin is needed.
The exact FULL-separability promise is indispensable. This theorem is not
an exact separability proof for additional Gibbs parameters or arbitrary
positive Dicke-diagonal states. Physical preparation is a separate cost.

1. FROM THE FULL-SEP PROMISE TO A POSITIVE BERNSTEIN MEASURE

rho is supported on the symmetric subspace. Refine a finite full-separable
decomposition into positive pure product projectors (existence only). Since
Tr(P_sym_perp rho)=0, every positive-weight product vector lies in Sym^n.
For n>=2, invariance of a simple tensor under each swap forces its local
vectors to be proportional. Hence every vector is phi^tensor n. n1 is
trivial. Thus the symmetric full-separable state is an iid PURE mixture.

Average this mixture under common axial phase rotations. rho itself is
Dicke diagonal, so this does not change it. A local pure state with up
probability p has phase-averaged populations
B_nk(p)=binom(n,k)p^k(1-p)^(n-k). Therefore a positive probability measure
mu on[0,1] exists with pi_k=integral B_nk(p)dmu. This witness is not
acquired; the following moment transform eliminates that requirement.

2. UNIVERSAL REGULARIZATION AND TRUE MOMENTS

Set eta=eps/8 and tau_sym=P_sym/(n+1). Its Dicke populations are1/(n+1),
and integral_0^1 B_nk(p)dp=1/(n+1). Hence it is the coherent Haar average
corresponding to the uniform p-measure dp. Define
 rho_reg=(1-eta)rho+eta tau_sym,
 mu_reg=(1-eta)mu+eta dp.
mu_reg is a probability measure and mu_reg>=eta dp, even if mu is atomic.
Also T(rho_reg,rho)=eta T(tau_sym,rho)<=eta. There is no dimension factor.

The ordinary moments are acquired from populations by
 u_l=integral p^l dmu=sum_(k>=l) [binom(k,l)/binom(n,l)] pi_k.
Indeed binom(n,k)binom(k,l)=binom(n,l)binom(n-l,k-l), and the remaining
binomial sum is one. All transform coefficients lie in[0,1]. Thus
 u_l^reg=(1-eta)sum_(k>=l)[binom(k,l)/binom(n,l)]pi_k+eta/(l+1),
 u_0^reg=1 EXACTLY.
These are ORDINARY moments, not the homogeneous q_k=pi_k/binom(n,k).

3. ACQUISITION AGAINST THE TRUE REGULARIZED MEASURE

The acquired pi_hat need NOT remain iid-separable. It may lie outside the
Bernstein moment body while still being a positive normalized probability
vector. No existence argument is applied to that rounded vector. Instead,
all perturbation bounds compare its acquired u_hat to the TRUE u_reg above,
whose measure has the explicit eta component.

If each population is acquired to error<=zeta/[4(n+1)], the positive
binomial transform has error<=zeta/4. Round each resulting moment dyadically
to another error<=zeta/4, and keep uhat0=1 exactly. Then moment errors are

input bits and log1/zeta. Rational exact populations can instead be transformed
exactly and only the moments rounded.

For pi_k=exp(e_k)/sum exp(e_j) with rational known exponents, acquire
Emax=max e_j by exact rational comparisons. exp(e_k-Emax) is in[0,1] and
at least one raw term equals one exactly. Clip losses x>=b+1 to zero and
acquire the rest by the proven polynomial-b-bit exp-negative kernel. Both
normalizers are>=1. Raw absolute error tau per term gives normalized L1
error<=2(n+1)tau, so choosing tau<=zeta/[8(n+1)^2] suffices for the preceding
population error requirement. The needed b hasO(logn+log1/zeta) bits of
precision. Huge binary exponent losses cost rational comparisons, not a
numerical-magnitude-dependent series. There is no huge-field cutoff or
relative accuracy on rare populations.

5. CONSTRUCTIVE GAUSSIAN/RADAU RULE AND FINITE ACQUISITION

For odd n=2m+1 let H=(u_i+j), K=(u_i+j+1), i,j0..m. Positive Cholesky
H=LL^T gives J=L^-1 K L^-T, a positive contraction. u0=1 fixes L^T e0=e0.
For j<m, K e_j=H e_(j+1), hence J^j e0=L^T e_j. Splitting power indices
proves e0^T J^l e0=u_l through2m; K supplies2m+1. Cyclicity proves distinct
interior eigenvalues and positive spectral weights at e0. This is the
positive Gaussian rule, derived without a root/witness oracle.

For even n=2m, the top-left A and last-column v of K are known. Fill its
one unknown last entry with v^T A^-1 v. The completed K is EXACT PSD rankm.
The EXISTING true next moment would only increase that bottom entry, so
H-K>=H-K_true>=alpha I. The same Krylov identity gives exactness through2m,
one node0, and positive interior nodes/weights. An unknown next moment is
not acquired. These arguments still work when the ORIGINAL measure was a
single atom, because they are applied to the regularized measure.

Allocate qerr=eps/8. Choose the precision parameter t>0 with

7. EXACT RATIONAL PURE LOCAL DENSITIES WITHOUT POSITIVITY REPAIR

For the SAME chart sign, exact algebra gives

8. POSITIVE DYADIC WEIGHTS AND BOUNDED FAIR-BIT SAMPLING

The rational rule/phase weights need not be dyadic, so exact bounded-fair-bit

There is no unbounded rejection or exact non-power-of-two uniform oracle.
The output label specifies n identical exact rational pure local matrices;
writing/preparing n actual systems is an additional output/hardware interface.
