## P2. The within-sector initializer has concrete extra inputs

Bergamaschi, Gheissari and Liu, [Rapid mixing for Gibbs states within a logical sector: a dynamical view of self-correcting quantum memories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10976v1), v1, 15 July 2025, is the recoverable version behind the source's SODA2026 citation. Its exact framework requires bounded-degree original checks AND metacheck syndrome network, erasable small syndrome components, local correction of them, and amenability. These appear in the connectedness definitions at source1263–1390 and the formal conditional-block mixing theorem at1850–1874.

The block chain pins boundary-touching violated components and their adjacent zero syndromes, then samples the remaining conditional VALID syndrome distribution. The physical update applies the old and new local correction operators. This is an active acquisition procedure with conditional distribution and correction inputs; it is not automatically the original physical Davies bath.

The formal bound is m exp(-t/m)+max(m,t) exp[-Omega((beta-beta0)L^(1/c))] for the syndrome chain starting at zero, subject to its stated geometry and box restrictions. The CSS reduction and maximally mixed-code corollary give the corresponding within-sector/global preparation statements under those assumptions. Existence of polylogarithmic block locality does not imply polynomial two-qubit implementation: the authors explicitly retain a general quasipolynomial cost (intro598–610 and section2190–2202). Their stronger toric implementation uses an even-subgraph worm sampler, with polynomial block-size gates (source2202–2217); validity of an arbitrary syndrome linear subspace is not the even-subgraph hypothesis.

Consequently neither the source's recursive family nor its noncommuting positive-X perturbation inherits this initializer merely from a Peierls witness tail. An actual alternate sparse metacheck/local conditional sampler remains a possible route. I have NOT proved that no such route exists. I also have not acquired a disjoint positive-density metacheck inventory to declare naive independent-syndrome rejection exponentially slow; that proposed stronger obstruction remains UNKNOWN.

## P3. The recent near-commuting cone theorem is not its sampler theorem

Putterman, Zlokapa and Cotler, [When quantum thermal states look classical](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28536v2), v2, 5 October 2026 (v1, 30 July 2026), provide a particularly relevant recent comparator. Their near-commuting activity is

    Delta(beta)=max_mu[a_mu+sum_(nu compatible with mu) a_nu],
    a_mu=|v_mu| eta_beta(kappa_mu),
    eta_beta(kappa)=exp(beta kappa)[exp(beta kappa)-1]/kappa,

with the continuous value beta at kappa=0. Thus Delta is already temperature dependent; it is not an unweighted perturbation norm. The pinning lemma at source1654–1669 proves stabilizer-cone membership when Delta(beta)<=1/72. Its randomized operator invariant has E sigma_chi=exp(-beta H), with each terminal sigma_chi positive in the stabilizer cone. The exact normalized law is proportional to Pr(chi) Tr sigma_chi. Cone membership and an unnormalized expectation do not remove this trace weight.

Their separate classical-preparation theorem, source517–541, is a NORMALIZED sampler for beta<=1/(4096 e s k). It outputs a random pure PRODUCT stabilizer state whose average is close to the Gibbs state, in the explicitly displayed polynomial runtime. The proof's global pinned partition functions and odds appear at source2569–2620; the partition-ratio lemma at2910–2938 is also explicitly restricted to that high-temperature bound and gives ratio<=5. The proof biases each proposed step by its trace ratio. I read these exact premises and rejection-normalization steps; I did not independently re-prove the entire graph-sampling/cluster-expansion analysis.

For the admitted recursive memory, tiny near-commuting activity can coexist with very large beta s k. Hence the cone theorem may apply while those normalized high-temperature ratio premises are absent. It would be incorrect either to claim an efficient low-temperature sampler from the invariant alone or to claim the published cone proof is nonconstructive/false. The missing acquired interface is an efficiently sampled trace-weighted terminal law with an efficient component preparation at the actual temperature.

## P4. An exact local normalization control

For commuting positive original checks, da=exp(-beta ha) and Pa=(I+Ca)/2 give

    exp(-beta H0)=E_ind P_F,
    Pr_Gibbs(F)=Pr_ind(F) 2^(N-rank F)/Z0.

Drawing the independent proposal and normalizing every P_F first changes the mixture. The heat-bath inclusion probabilities are 1-da for a dependent added check, and (1-da)/(1+da) for an independent one. These follow from the exact rank/trace ratio.
