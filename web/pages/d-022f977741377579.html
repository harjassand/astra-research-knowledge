All entropies and information quantities use natural logarithms unless an
explicit division by ln 2 or the notation h_2 is present.

1. EXACT CLASSICAL-MEMORY INTERFACE

A classical generator has a finite random memory variable C_t at the cut after
output X_t and before output X_{t+1}. Conditional on C_t, future draws use only
C_t and fresh independent randomness. Consequently

  P(X_t=x,X_{t+1}=y)=sum_c w_c a_c(x)b_c(y).

The output symbols must use the stated alphabet. Any persistent external random
seed, stored output history, or environment that remains accessible must be
included in the charged memory. Free access to the whole past is a different
interface. Both standard state-emitting and edge-emitting HMMs satisfy this
factorization at a suitable state cut. Hidden stationarity is unnecessary for
the lower bound; the target output process is stationary, and the same bound
applies at every cut of any exact generator.

Memory entropy means the Shannon entropy H(C_t) of this random internal state,
not I(C_t;past), the number of predictive coordinates, or the logarithm of an
uncharged data structure. K distinct states give H(C_t)<=ln K; large rank alone
does not force large entropy, so separate entropy and state-count proofs follow.

2. FINITE STATIONARY OUTPUT PROCESS

Let m>=3, V=F_2^m\{0}, N=2^m-1, and d=2^{m-1}-1. For x,y in V put
  A_xy=1 iff x dot y=0 over F_2.
The symmetric N by N matrix A has exactly d ones in every row and column.
Let X_t be the stationary Markov chain with uniform marginal 1/N and
  T_xy=A_xy/d.
Thus its adjacent-output law is
  P_xy=A_xy/(Nd),
uniform on its Nd permitted pairs. Self-loops are allowed. The alphabet has
N symbols, exponential in m, and this cost is part of the construction.

3. COMMUTING QUANTUM HIDDEN MARKOV REALIZATION

Use the N-dimensional Hilbert space with basis {|y>:y in V}. Set
  pi=I_N/N,
  q_x=(1/d) sum_{y in V:x dot y=0} |y><y|.
The output instrument has outcomes y in V and completely positive maps
  E_y(rho)=<y|rho|y> q_y.
A Kraus representation is K_{y,z}=d^{-1/2}|z><y| for nonzero z orthogonal to y.
The sum of E_y is trace preserving. Start from pi. After the observed output y,
the normalized state is q_y; the next output law is q_y's diagonal. Since A
is regular, sum_y E_y(pi)=pi. This gives precisely the stationary Markov process
above. The reachable family conditioned on observed histories is {pi} union
{q_x:x in V}, and forgotten histories give convex mixtures of this family.
The Kraus preparation index z is unobserved fresh noise, not a separately
available output. Fine-graining the instrument to reveal it would change the
reachable family and is not the interface used here.

All these states commute. The one common entanglement-breaking channel
  Gamma(rho)=sum_y <y|rho|y>|y><y|
fixes every reachable state exactly. The channel
  Delta(rho)=sum_y <y|rho|y>|y,y><y,y|
broadcasts the entire diagonal family exactly: both output marginals equal rho
for every diagonal rho. These copies are correlated; this is broadcasting,
not production of independently sampled copies rho tensor rho.

4. MAX-PRIOR HOLEVO CAPACITY IS CONSTANT

Every q_x obeys q_x<=(N/d)pi as diagonal matrices, and
  D(q_x||pi)=ln(N/d).
The same relative-entropy upper bound holds for their convex mixtures and for
pi. For any prior p over a finite selection of reachable states rho_j, with
average bar_rho, the relative-entropy decomposition gives
  sum_j p_j D(rho_j||pi)=chi(p)+D(bar_rho||pi).
Hence chi(p)<=ln(N/d). Uniform prior over the q_x has average pi, so equality
holds. The exact max-prior Holevo capacity is therefore
  C_H=ln(N/d)<=ln(7/3)<ln4,
and it tends to ln2 as m grows. Because the states commute, this is also the
ordinary channel capacity for x mapped to a uniformly chosen orthogonal y.
The stationary adjacent-output mutual information is
  I_P(X;Y)=ln(N/d),
and is constant as well. For this first-order stationary process, the mutual
information across the infinite past/future cut is the same quantity.

This small capacity is NOT a bound on the quantum memory's entropy or size:
the stationary density pi has von Neumann entropy ln N, q_x has entropy ln d,
and the Hilbert dimension is N. The construction demonstrates a metric/interface
gap; it establishes no constant-dimensional quantum realization or quantum
memory advantage.

5. EXACT CLASSICAL ENTROPY LOWER BOUND

Take any conditional-product decomposition P=sum_c w_c a_c tensor b_c, with
w_c>0. Since P has zero mass on nonorthogonal pairs, each product component has
support S_c times R_c consisting entirely of orthogonal pairs. Let
U_c=span(S_c) and W_c=span(R_c). Bilinearity gives U_c perpendicular W_c, so
  dim U_c+dim W_c<=m.
Both supports contain only nonzero vectors. Therefore
  |S_c||R_c|<=(2^r-1)(2^{m-r}-1)<=B_m,
where
  B_m=(2^{floor(m/2)}-1)(2^{ceil(m/2)}-1)<N+1.
This maximum is attained by complementary orthogonal subspaces of balanced
dimensions. Independent variables with those supports have conditional entropy
at most ln B_m. Thus
  I_P((X,Y);C)>=ln(Nd)-ln B_m,
  H(C)>=ln(Nd/B_m)>=ln(Nd/(N+1))=Omega(ln N).
This applies to any exact classical HMM memory at the specified cut, including
nonunifilar models. A visible Markov realization has N states and H(C)=ln N,
so the entropy lower bound is within a constant of that simple upper bound.

6. WYNER COMMON INFORMATION CAN BE COMPUTED EXACTLY HERE

Define C_W(P)=inf I((X,Y);C) over all C for which X and Y are conditionally
independent. This is a common-information quantity, not the same definition as
the minimum H(C), and not by itself a full-process HMM construction.

The support proof gives C_W(P)>=ln(Nd/B_m). For equality, let r=floor(m/2),
choose a uniformly random r-dimensional subspace U of F_2^m, and conditionally
draw X uniformly from U\{0} and Y uniformly from U-perp\{0}, independently.
Every conditional rectangle has cardinality B_m. Each fixed nonzero orthogonal
pair (x,y) belongs to the same number of these rectangles: such U are the
r-dimensional subspaces containing span(x) and contained in y-perp, equivalently
(r-1)-dimensional subspaces of the (m-2)-dimensional quotient y-perp/span(x).
Nonorthogonal pairs belong to none. Counting all incidences shows that the
resulting pair law is exactly P. It follows that
  C_W(P)=ln(Nd/B_m)=ln N-ln2+o(1).
The subspace latent variable generally has many more than N values and much
larger H(U). This factorization witnesses the Wyner upper bound; it does not
claim an HMM with that entropy or that state set generates all higher-order
output laws. For exact classical HMM memory, the proven bounds remain
  ln(Nd/B_m)<=inf H(C_t)<=ln N.

7. EXACT NUMBER OF CLASSICAL STATES IS N

For distinct nonzero x,z, their two linear constraints are independent, so they
have c=2^{m-2}-1 common nonzero orthogonal vectors. For x=z the count is d.
Consequently
  A^2=(d-c)I+cJ=2^{m-2}I+(2^{m-2}-1)J.
This matrix is positive definite over the reals, so rank A=N. Every mixture of
K product components has ordinary matrix rank at most K. The adjacent-output
law therefore requires K>=N. The visible Markov chain uses N states, proving
that the minimum exact classical HMM state count is exactly N.

The same rank observation shows why an exact common EB channel cannot be
assumed to have a constant alphabet: the q_x span the full N-dimensional
diagonal vector space. Any finite measure-and-prepare channel fixing them has
range dimension at least N, and therefore needs at least N prepared outcomes.
Our explicit common EB channel has exactly N outcomes.

8. FIXED POSITIVE TWO-STEP TV ERROR STILL FORCES LARGE ENTROPY

This is an independent robust lemma, without a rank-stability assumption.
Let Q be any law on V times V with TV(Q,P)<=delta<=1/100. Suppose
  Q=sum_c w_c a_c tensor b_c.
Let h_2(p) be binary Shannon entropy in bits and fix eta=1/20. For a single
component draw X~a, Y~b independently, and set
  e=Pr(X dot Y=1),
  G={y:Pr(X dot y=1)<=eta}, tau=Pr(Y not in G).
Markov's inequality gives tau<=e/eta. Let r=dim span(G) and pick a basis of G.
Each of these r independent parity coordinates of X has one-probability <=eta.
Complete them to an invertible linear coordinate map on F_2^m. Subadditivity
then gives, in bits,
  H(X)<=m-r+r h_2(eta).
Conditioning Y on membership in G gives
  H(Y)<=h_2(tau)+(1-tau)r+tau m<=r+tau m+h_2(tau).
Combining these inequalities, and H(Y)<=m, yields
  H(X)+H(Y)
  <=[1+h_2(eta)+(1-h_2(eta))tau]m+(1-h_2(eta))h_2(tau).
This also covers G empty or tau=0/1 by the usual zero-mass conventions.

Since P assigns zero mass to nonorthogonal pairs, Q's total such mass is <=delta.
Thus the weighted component error has average <=delta, and average tau<=delta/eta.
Jensen's inequality and monotonicity of h_2 on [0,1/2] imply
  H_Q(X,Y|C)/ln2
  <=[1+h_2(eta)+(1-h_2(eta))delta/eta]m
     +(1-h_2(eta))h_2(delta/eta).
Entropy continuity on the full N^2-symbol pair alphabet gives
  H_Q(X,Y)/ln2>=log_2(Nd)-2delta m-h_2(delta).
For m>=3, log_2(Nd)>=2m-2. Therefore
  H(C)/ln2>=I_Q((X,Y);C)/ln2
  >=alpha_delta m-2-h_2(delta)-(1-h_2(eta))h_2(delta/eta),
where
  alpha_delta=1-2delta-h_2(eta)-(1-h_2(eta))delta/eta.
At delta=1/100, alpha_delta is approximately 0.55088243 and the subtracted
constant approximately 2.596. In particular the clean, rigorous weaker bound is
  H(C)>=(m/2-4)ln2,
  K>=2^{m/2-4}=sqrt(N+1)/16.
For this simplified bound one only needs h_2(1/20)<0.3, h_2(1/100)<0.1, and
h_2(p)<=1: then alpha_delta>0.54 and the subtracted constant is <4. The first
two entropy inequalities follow from h_2(p)<=p(ln(1/p)+1)/ln2, ln2>2/3,
ln20<3 and ln100<5. These elementary logarithm bounds follow from the positive
Taylor series for exp and the atanh series for ln2.

No linear Omega(N) state lower bound is asserted for fixed TV error. What is
proved is Omega(ln N) entropy and at least a fixed positive power of N states.
The bound is necessary for a full-process approximate HMM whenever its specified
adjacent-output law is within this TV error; no hidden stationarity is required.

9. COMPLETE COST AND SCOPE ACCOUNTING

Parameter m is not the output-alphabet size. N=2^m-1 is exponential in m.
Each output symbol requires m bits to write. Quantum memory has Hilbert dimension
N (m qubits suffice to embed it), stationary entropy ln N, and average reachable
entropy ln d. The broadcasting output space has dimension N^2; the explicit EB
measurement has N outcomes. A fully enumerated transition table or instrument
has Theta(N^2) permitted Kraus transitions/table entries, each with O(m)-bit
rational probabilities 1/d or algebraic amplitudes sqrt(1/d). The dot-product
rule is succinct and can be computed from m-bit vectors; a uniform orthogonal
nonzero vector can be sampled using fresh randomness and linear algebra with
cost polynomial in m (zero exclusion can use rejection with bounded expected
overhead). This does not make its N-state memory alphabet or m-bit output free.
The subspace witness for C_W has its own large latent alphabet and is used only
for the common-information calculation, never advertised as a small HMM.

There is no quantum-vs-classical computational or memory advantage claim. The
proved obstruction is precisely that all-prior Holevo capacity plus exact
broadcastability, or even one exact common EB channel on the reachable family,
does not supply an alphabet-independent bound on exact classical HMM state
count or Shannon entropy. Mutual information can remain constant while the
common information needed to screen two observations grows.
