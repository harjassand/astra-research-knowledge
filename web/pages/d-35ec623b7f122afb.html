2. PHYSICAL CONVENTIONS AND EXACT THEOREM

Let rho and sigma be faithful density matrices on any finite-dimensional full
matrix algebra. Put s=sqrt(sigma), d=s^(1/2), q=sqrt(rho). Let L be a positive
Heisenberg QMS generator: exp(-t L) is unital completely positive, preserves
sigma in the Schrödinger picture, and is selfadjoint for the sigma KMS inner
product. Write L_* for its trace adjoint. The weighted selfadjoint generator is
H(X)=d^-1 L_*(d X d) d^-1. The physical quantities are

  J = Tr[L_*(rho)(log rho - log sigma)],
  E = Tr[q H(q)].

The supplied ALL-KMS representation is a finite sum of Hermitian-noise terms

  H_B(X) = (V_B X + X V_B)/2 - B X B,   B=B*,
  V_B = 2 Lyap_s^-1(B s B),
  Lyap_s(X) = s X + X s.                     [unnormalized convention]

Each term is a legal sigma-stationary KMS QMS generator, and all finite KMS QMS
generators in this setting admit a sum of these terms. The physical positive
Schrödinger generator of one term is

  L_{B,*}(X) = (C X + X C*)/2 - K X K*,
  C=d V_B d^-1,  K=d B d^-1,  C+C*=2 K* K.

The skew part of C, and hence the physical Hamiltonian, is retained throughout.
Using a drift V_B instead of C would change J and is not authorized here.

THEOREM. For every such L, rho, sigma and every 0 <= kappa <= pi,

  J - kappa E >= 0.

The same theorem holds for id_R tensor L on any finite untouched reference R,
with any faithful product reference tau_R tensor sigma and every faithful
joint state rho_RQ. No commutation or prescribed reference marginal is needed.

3. THE SUPPLIED ALL-REAL HARDY KERNEL

For all real x,y define

  K_kappa(x,y) =
    [x sinh y + y sinh x - 2 kappa sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)]
      / [2 cosh((x-y)/2)].

The kernel matrices [K_kappa(x_i,x_j)] are PSD for every finite real node list
when 0 <= kappa <= pi. There is no sign or nonzero-node assumption.
For clarity, the previously sealed Hardy realization is

  t=tanh(x/2), u=tanh(y/2),
  a(z)=(1-z^2)atanh(z)/z, a(0)=1,
  b(z)=sqrt(1-z^2),
  N_kappa(t,u)=[a(t)+a(u)-(kappa/2)b(t)b(u)]/(1-tu),
  K_kappa(x,y)=2 sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2) N_kappa(t,u).

The analytic branches have a(0)=b(0)=1 on the unit disk. With the Hardy
reproducing vector k_t(z)=1/(1-tz),

  N_kappa(t,u)=<k_t,D_kappa k_u>,
  D_kappa = (pi-kappa)/2 M_b* M_b
            + (kappa/2) H_bar(b)* H_bar(b) >= 0.

Thus h_kappa(x)=sqrt(2)sinh(x/2) D_kappa^(1/2) k_t satisfies
<h_kappa(x),h_kappa(y)>=K_kappa(x,y). The realization is real on real nodes.
In particular h_kappa(0)=0; opposite signs of nodes merely change the feature
sign, and no division by a node occurs in the theorem proof.

4. MODULAR COMPONENTS AND THE PHYSICAL DRIFT

Fix one Hermitian noise B. Let the spectral projections of log s be Q_u. Define

  B_w = sum_{u-v=w} Q_u B Q_v.

There are finitely many real w, B=sum_w B_w, [log s,B_w]=w B_w, and
B_w*=B_{-w}. Equal eigenvalues and coincident frequencies cause no difficulty.
Write w,v for two such frequencies, and set

  m0=(w+v)/2,  Delta=(v-w)/2,  c=cosh Delta.

Since B_w* B_v has frequency v-w, the Lyapunov equation gives exactly

  V_B = sum_{w,v} [exp(m0)/c] B_w* B_v,
  C   = sum_{w,v} [exp(v)/c] B_w* B_v,
  C*  = sum_{w,v} [exp(w)/c] B_w* B_v,
  K   = sum_v exp(v/2) B_v,
  K* K= sum_{w,v} exp(m0) B_w* B_v.

For example B_w* s B_v = exp(v) B_w* B_v s, whereas
Lyap_s(B_w* B_v)=(1+exp(v-w)) B_w* B_v s. This proves the V coefficient.
Conjugation by d supplies the physical C coefficient. The identity C+C*=2K*K
then follows from (exp(v)+exp(w))/cosh Delta=2exp(m0).

The Heisenberg adjoint of this physical term is

  L_B(X)=(X C + C* X)/2 - K* X K.

Using log s B_v = B_v log s + v B_v gives

  L_B(log s) = sum_{w,v} exp(m0)
      [-m0 + Delta tanh Delta] B_w* B_v.          (4.1)

The coefficient is computed as

  (v-w)exp(v)/(2c) - v exp(m0)
    = exp(m0)[Delta(1+tanh Delta)-v]
    = exp(m0)[-m0+Delta tanh Delta].

Equation (4.1) includes the contribution of the physical Hamiltonian. It is not
the result of discarding that term from L_B. The Hamiltonian drops out only
from the log-rho part of entropy by cyclicity and [rho,log rho]=0.

5. FULL-STATE SPECTRAL PAIRS

Diagonalize q, with no relation assumed to the Q_u above:

  q = sum_a q_a P_a,   q_a>0.

The P_a may have arbitrary rank. For every ordered spectral pair (a,b), define

  U_ab,w = P_b B_w P_a,
  g_ab(w,v) = Tr[U_ab,w* U_ab,v],
  l_ab = log(q_a/q_b),
  x_ab,w = l_ab+w.

For fixed a,b, g_ab is the HS Gram matrix of the complex matrices U_ab,w.
No positivity or phase restriction on individual entries is needed.

The log-rho part of physical entropy satisfies

  Tr[L_{B,*}(rho) log rho]
   = Tr[rho log rho K*K] - Tr[rho K* log rho K]
   = sum_{a,b,w,v} 2 q_a^2 exp(m0) l_ab g_ab(w,v).    (5.1)

To obtain the first equality, cyclicity replaces the physical drift by
(C+C*)/2=K*K inside Tr[rho log rho (...)]. It does not replace the full physical
generator by a symmetric drift. The second equality uses the spectral
resolutions of rho=q^2 and log rho=2 log q on the two sides of B_v.

Since log sigma=2 log s, (4.1) gives

  -Tr[L_{B,*}(rho) log sigma]
   = sum_{a,b,w,v} 2 q_a^2 exp(m0)
       [m0-Delta tanh Delta] g_ab(w,v).            (5.2)

The energy, without any entropy expansion, is

  E_B = Tr[rho V_B] - Tr[q B q B]
      = sum_{a,b,w,v}
          [q_a^2 exp(m0)/c - q_a q_b] g_ab(w,v).   (5.3)

These formulas are exact at the given faithful rho, rather than stationary
Hessians, derivatives, or secant approximations.

6. HERMITIAN REVERSE PAIRING AND THE EXACT UNIVERSAL GRAM

Because B_w*=B_{-w}, the summation involution

  (a,b,w,v) -> (b,a,-v,-w)

preserves the complex Gram entry itself:

  g_ba(-v,-w)
   = Tr[P_b B_v P_a B_w* P_b]
   = Tr[P_a B_w* P_b B_v P_a]
   = g_ab(w,v).

It is important that the transformed frequencies are -v,-w, in that order.
The involution sends l_ab -> -l_ab and m0 -> -m0, and leaves Delta unchanged.
Set m=l_ab+m0, so that x_ab,w=m-Delta and x_ab,v=m+Delta. Then

  q_a^2 exp(m0) = q_a q_b exp(m),
  q_b^2 exp(-m0)= q_a q_b exp(-m).

Averaging each summand with its involuted partner converts (5.1)-(5.3) into

  J_B = sum_{a,b,w,v} 2 q_a q_b
          [m sinh m - Delta tanh Delta cosh m] g_ab(w,v),

  E_B = sum_{a,b,w,v} q_a q_b
          [cosh m / cosh Delta - 1] g_ab(w,v).    (6.1)

The two elementary hyperbolic identities are

  (m-Delta)sinh(m+Delta)+(m+Delta)sinh(m-Delta)
     =2[m sinh m cosh Delta - Delta cosh m sinh Delta],

  cosh m - cosh Delta
     =2 sinh((m-Delta)/2)sinh((m+Delta)/2).

Consequently the full PHYSICAL gap is exactly

  J_B - kappa E_B
   = 2 sum_{a,b} q_a q_b sum_{w,v}
        K_kappa(l_ab+w,l_ab+v) Tr[U_ab,w* U_ab,v].   (6.2)

Every outer weight is positive, the kernel is PSD on each finite node list,
and g_ab is a Gram matrix. Equivalently,

  J_B - kappa E_B
   = 2 sum_{a,b} q_a q_b
       || sum_w h_kappa(l_ab+w) tensor U_ab,w ||^2 >= 0.  (6.3)

Here the tensor norm is Hardy-space tensor matrix-HS norm. Complex matrix
phases are allowed. Real symmetry of the kernel (or a real Cholesky factor)
ensures precisely the entry order in (6.2). Summing over the Hermitian noises
of the supplied representation proves the theorem for ALL finite KMS QMS.
No commutant/coherence-block estimate, GNS symmetry, modular covariance of the
generator, or rho/sigma commutation was imposed.

7. COMPLETE QUANTUM-REFERENCE SCOPE

Let a finite reference have any faithful state tau_R and put t=sqrt(tau_R).
The joint stationary square root is t tensor s. For a local noise use
B_total=I_R tensor B. Its Lyapunov drift is I_R tensor V_B since

  Lyap_{t tensor s}(I tensor V_B)=2 t tensor B s B.

Its physical drift and jump are I tensor C and I tensor K. It is exactly the
untouched-reference generator id_R tensor L_*, and its modular frequencies are
the same w because log(t tensor s)=log t tensor I+I tensor log s.
Apply the full-state spectral-pair proof to q=sqrt(rho_RQ), which may have
entangled spectral projectors. This proves the complete inequality.

The value of J is independent of which faithful tau_R was chosen: the term
with log tau_R vanishes since the reference marginal is conserved. The weighted
generator id_R tensor H and hence E are also independent of that choice.
This cancellation does not require rho_R=tau_R. It is consistent with the
earlier complete-C4 classification, rather than an extra restriction here.

9. FAILED SHORTCUTS AND THE ACQUIRED REPAIR

The stationary pi theorem does not itself integrate to this theorem: away
from stationarity the physical generator cannot simply be copied onto a new
stationary reference. The earlier secant obstruction and its explicit control
remain valid. No such copying is used in (6.2).

The earlier general positive potential R_kappa does not settle the physical
gap by dropping its remainder: that remainder can be strictly negative even
when J=E=0. No termwise positivity of that remainder is asserted here.

The own matching-leaf star/anchor spectral mixture changes only a dynamically
dark reference block. A general state's full spectral projections need not
have that property. The new repair is to keep the ORIGINAL physical generator,
decompose its noise by stationary modular frequencies, and pair the forward
and reverse full-state spectral entries. The pairing generates exactly the
shifted Hardy kernel, with no generator transport.

Ignoring the physical Hamiltonian in the log-sigma term would remove the
Delta tanh Delta correction in (4.1) and invalidate the Gram. It is retained
and then combines correctly with log-rho entropy.
