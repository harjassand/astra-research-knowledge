UNRESTRICTED COLLECTIVE VARIANCE ATTACK: PRECISE REMAINING GATE
c04_s01; independent; no peer result used.

The baseline's collective roof is the relevant quantity. For an arbitrary
Hermitian weighted frame H_j on a tracial algebra, set
 V=sum_j tau(H_j^2),
 C=max_(pure reference ensembles sum wP=I/d) sum_(y,j)w_y<psi_y|H_j|psi_y>^2.
The canonical EB Dirichlet target c=2 is equivalent by separation to
       V-C <=2 sum_j tau[H_j(I-Phi)H_j].
For a selfcompatible Phi, a normalized joint Choi omega has
 F=sum_j tau[H_j Phi(H_j)] = (1/2)Tr omega K_star,
 K_star=sum_j H_j^T tensor(H_j tensor I+I tensor H_j).
Thus the exact scalar dual gate is
                   Tr omega K_star <= V+C.               (U1)
Both channel's prescribed reference marginals and its positive HS class
must be retained. General constant c or a weaker q->0 modulus remains
unproved. Failure of c2 alone would not refute those weaker targets.

A TEMPTING INPUT-ONLY CERTIFICATE IS EXACTLY FALSE
The ensemble roof has a dual upper certificate X such that
 <psi|X|psi> >=sum_j <psi|H_j|psi>^2 for all pure psi.
It is tempting to claim
 sum_j (H_j^2)^T_R+X_R^T -K_star >=0.
This fails even for ONE COMMUTING observable and a perfectly valid dual X.
Let d3, H=diag(-1/2,1/6,1/3), X=H^2. Jensen/variance makes X a valid
pure-state dual certificate and, at the tracial reference, tau(X)=C=V
since the spectral ensemble attains it. On the product basis vector
|R=1/6,B=1/3,C=1/3>, the proposed operator has eigenvalue
 2(1/6)^2-2(1/6)(1/3)=-1/18.
Hence no PSD proof may silently put all reference-dual budget on the input.
The target itself is trivial for this one-observable family (basis
dephasing is an exact EB reconstruction); only the specified shortcut is
refuted. Its defect is the missing output marginal constraints.

A stronger output-aware candidate is
 sum_j(H_j^2)^T_R+(X_B+X_C)/2-K_star >=0.                  (U2)
For one observable, X=H^2 makes (U2) the sum of two squared differences.
Taking expectation in omega with all reference marginals I/d would prove
(U1) for any dual optimum X if (U2) held generally. No general proof or
counterexample to (U2) is claimed. It is a stronger sufficient certificate
than required channel-constrained duality; not an earned universal lemma.

BOUNDED COUNTEREXAMPLE SEARCH / NULL RESULTS
pauli_cycle_probe.py/.json builds eight explicit five-cycle anticommutation
frames among the fifteen two-qubit Paulis. The compatible Choi fixture is
the maximally mixed state on the largest eigenspace of K_star; Pauli
covariance gives tracial marginals and a diagonal HS channel. All its
HS eigenvalues in these fixtures are positive (minimum approx0.468328).
The star maximum is numerically 2+2sqrt(5)=6.472135955, while the tested
c2 thresholds 7 (if the sharper C=2 held) and 5+sqrt(5) remain larger.
No counterexample appeared. The graph-theoretic C estimates were search
heuristics, not imported proofs, and no exact C=2 or theta-bound theorem
is claimed from these tests. This finite floating search neither proves
nor refutes universal rounding.

The exact O(d), signed-permutation and compact-symplectic derivations show
how covariance collapses (U1) to explicit finite witness systems and EB
moment polytopes. They do not supply a decomposition/twirl retaining every
slow direction of a general channel. That representation/separation gap
is the unrestricted remaining obstruction. An exact noncovariant positive
selfcompatible channel with a certified roof gap would change the answer;
so would an all-frame proof of (U1) or a dimension-free vanishing modulus.
