## Independently reconstructed transport estimate

Let C be the acquired-coordinate population metric with spectrum inside `[1-z,1+z]`, and let `S_C(M)=C^(1/2) M C^(1/2)` on all symmetric matrices. Its eigenvalues are `sqrt(c_i c_j)`, so `||S_C||<=1+z` and `||S_C-I||<=z`. Write the non-rank-one full ideal operator as `H=lambda I+sum beta_i |E_i><E_i|`. Its norm is `lambda+beta_max`. Expanding `S_C H S_C-H` into `(S_C-I)H S_C+H(S_C-I)` gives norm at most `(2z+z^2)(lambda+beta_max)`.

The remaining full isotropic rank-one part has coefficient `c_d=lambda/2`. After trace projection its vector is `P0 C`, and hence its norm is at most `(lambda/2)||P0 C||_F^2 <= (n/2)lambda z^2`. The ideal rank-one identity vector vanishes under P0. Therefore, independently reconstructing pde_gate9 T1,

    rho <= a(lambda+beta_max)+(n/2)lambda z^2,
    a=2z+z^2.

This is a triangle bound, not a claim that every inequality is simultaneously tight.

## Shared budget and the floor subtraction

Since B is PSD and lambda positive, `M0=||K0||=lambda+||B||`. Put `p_i=P0 E_i/sqrt(1-1/n)`. It has Frobenius norm one. Its own term contributes exactly `beta_i(1-1/n)` to `<p_i,Bp_i>`; the remaining terms are nonnegative. Thus

    beta_max <= n||B||/(n-1)=n(M0-lambda)/(n-1).

This is a norm of B bound, which is stronger than replacing B by the full K0 norm. Substitution gives

    rho <= k0 M0+d lambda,
    k0=n a/(n-1), d=(n/2)z^2-a/(n-1).

For `d<=0`, the supplied universal floor `lambda>=2` yields `rho<=k0 M0-t`, where `t=-2d>=0`. This uses Jensen and standardized second moments, rather than the fixture's disclosed numerical source moments. For n=3, `d=z^2-z`, `k0=(3/2)(2z+z^2)` and `t=2z(1-z)`. Hence `d<=0` throughout the admissible `0<=z<1` range. More generally the sign condition is `z<=4/[n(n-1)-2]` for n>=3; for n=2 it holds at every nonnegative z.

If `k0<1`, triangle inequality gives `M0<=Mhat+epsilon4+rho`, hence

    M0 <= (Mhat+epsilon4-t)/(1-k0),
    e_refined = (epsilon4+k0 Mhat-t)/(1-k0).

On the valid model/confidence event these right sides obey `M0>=2` and `e_refined>=0`. Negative values or invalid denominators must not be clipped into an accepting zero-error output. Our replay explicitly checks nonnegative e and an upper norm at least 2. This refinement is unavailable for general tensors without the model floor.

The alternative universally sufficient coefficient `k_star=n a/(n-1)+(n/2)z^2` follows by dropping the negative subtraction and bounding remaining lambda by M0. It improves the original coefficient by exactly a. The n=3 floor-subtracted bound is strictly stronger in the present run. The Rayleigh coefficient `n/(n-1)` cannot be reduced uniformly using only positive beta: one dominant beta with all remaining positive betas tending to zero approaches equality.
