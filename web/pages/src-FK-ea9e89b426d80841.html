- Li et al., “Harnessing dislocation motion using an electric field,” *Nature Materials* 22, 958–963 (2023), [publisher article / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41563-023-01572-7); author preprint [arXiv:2211.14933](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14933).
- Access: full 42-page arXiv author manuscript, including main text, Extended Data, and figure captions; official Nature article page inspected; separate author-provided, unedited supplementary PDF inspected. No line-by-line comparison of the accepted manuscript to the final copyedited publisher text was performed.
- Read/inspected: main Figs. 1–2; Extended Data Figs. 2 and 7–8; supplementary Figs. 1–6 and 9–13; supplementary Videos 1–3 captions/text. Main/SI operating values, defect labels, field estimates, voltage/current, beam-off interval, reversibility and retention statements were checked against captions and text. Temperature is not stated in the inspected materials.
- Local inputs: `2023_nature_materials_arxiv2211.14933.pdf` (4,683,629 bytes; SHA-256 `a29c8fec023553751cd2bf76297c845d19a3400b33cca03081f6735140a7ce49`) and `2023_nature_materials_supplementary.pdf` (2,216,879 bytes; SHA-256 `f1c1e901b483e2a1e97942b39028dfb9f9118eb58117de1df20cac8f2494c8ef`).
- Limits: the field study reports displacement/glide and class-dependent mobility, not a reaction. The experimental field estimates differ between the main/table report and Supplementary Fig. 3 caption; the report preserves the discrepancy and uses an order-of-magnitude description. The atomistic barrier estimate uses a much larger modeled field.

### Hughes et al. (2019), carrier-assisted filtering in AlGaAs/GaAs/Ge/Si

- Hughes, Shah, and Mukherjee, “Glide of threading dislocations in (In)AlGaAs on Si induced by carrier recombination: Characteristics, mitigation, and filtering,” *Journal of Applied Physics* 125, 165702 (2019), [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5088844); primary author manuscript at [UC eScholarship](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1d28b1jf).
- Access: the complete 27-page primary-source PDF was read through eScholarship’s web PDF text extraction, including main-text sections, Methods, and captions/text for Figs. 1–5. Figure captions and associated paragraphs were text-inspected; the figure image pixels were not visually rendered. A direct local PDF fetch returned HTTP 403, so no PDF bytes were saved. The four supplementary video files mentioned in the article were not downloaded or inspected.
- Read/inspected: Methods; Table 1; Figs. 1–5; Secs. III.A–C and conclusions. The filtering sample is the indium-free $x=0$ double heterostructure on the article’s pre-threaded Si stack, with measured residual tensile strain 0.17% at room temperature. The stated CL settings include 5 kV, 1 frame/s, estimated carrier concentration $10^{17}$ e–h pairs/cm³, and about 3 nA absorbed sample current for Figs. 2 and 5. Fig. 5 counts threads in 1000 µm²: starting density about $10^7$ cm⁻², an initial ≈20% count rise from de-clustering, followed by ≈30% reduction from the peak after 40 min; a fast first stage lasts 300 frames at roughly 100 nm/frame, the count is not at steady state, and slower threads remain. The authors attribute less than 5% of density change to source/reaction events and say the threads mostly glide out of the imaged region. The Fig. 5 scan temperature is not stated separately.
- Limits: this is real carrier-assisted area-local filtering, but the mapped event is thread exit from the imaged area, not selected pair annihilation. The article does not provide Burgers-vector-resolved outcomes or a separate post-beam retention measurement. Its broader TD mobility groups are not assigned a unique core type from glide data alone. This source materially narrows novelty claims about carrier-assisted dislocation reduction.
