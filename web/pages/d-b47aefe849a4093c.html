P19 — Exact finite evidence and exponent phase diagram for rare-herald noise
Date: 2026-10-08. Status: internally derived candidate; scalar finite checks exact; independent proof review and novelty/priority audit not done.

For R=4^k, lambda=2^(-R^2), eta=1/R, nu=R^(-a), a fixed positive integer, compare an ideal two-mode squeezed-vacuum source with the same source after independent phase-insensitive additive Gaussian noise of mean nu on every signal/idler mode before uniform loss. Split the idler uniformly into R modes and herald exactly one photon in each; retain the signal. This is a single noisy-TMSV versus noisy-vacuum-reference comparison, not the matched-energy/matched-herald TMSV-versus-thermal-source family.

Exact formulas: q_R=R!/R^(2R); t=eta*nu=R^(-(a+1)); p0=[t/(1+t)^2]^R; rho=lambda/p0. For x=lambda^2(1-eta), the ideal posterior K=N-R is negative binomial with P(K=j|H)=binom(R+j,R)(1-x)^(R+1)x^j. The ideal retained mean is 1+((R+1)/R)x/(1-x), and its TV distance from Bin(R,1/R) is at most ((R+1)/R)x/(1-x). The noisy conditional law is at TV distance at most rho/(1-rho)+t/(1+t) from vacuum when rho<1.

Asymptotically, log2 rho=-R^2+(a+1)Rlog2R+o(1), so the noisy conditional law tends to vacuum; the ideal law tends to Poisson(1). Their TV gap tends to 1-e^(-1). The b-threshold for lambda=2^(-R^b) and polynomial noise eta=R^(-c), nu=R^(-a) is b>1 for this generic postselection-continuity certificate; b<=1 makes its ratio diverge. This is a proof-method threshold, not an actual robustness threshold. A sharper posterior bound shows the ideal retained law remains close to Bin(R,1/R) for c=1 whenever lambda->0. Exponentially small noise nu=2^(-R^d) gives certificate threshold b>d+1 (or coefficient C>1 for lambda=2^(-C R^(d+1))).
