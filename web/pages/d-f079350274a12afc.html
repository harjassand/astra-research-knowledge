## Source-reported model and experimental interfaces

- Supplementary Eqs. 18–20 give the density, osteoblast-fraction, and force-balance PDEs. The fraction equation contains relative diffusive flux, density-gradient transport, and phenotype-dependent proliferation/differentiation. Stiffness is `E=E_A+(E_B-E_A)φ`, and the proposed stiffness-sensitive differentiation rate is `k_D=α(E-E_A)`.
- Supplementary pp. 12–13 give a reduced scalar Fisher–KPP discussion and the fitted-parameter description. Supplementary Table S1 reports `D=15 μm²/h`, `E_A=30 Pa`, `E_B=1000 Pa`, `τ=10 h`, and fitted `α=3.5×10^-6` (the notebook’s working-unit conversion is used in the replay; no extra physical units for α are inferred here).
- The pinned Mathematica notebook converts pressure with `PaToNewUnits=12.96`, uses `eO=12960`, `eM=388.8`, `alpha=3.5e-6`, homeostatic densities `.0067` and `.0077`, `D=15`, `L=1500 μm`, `aPhi=4`, `Nx=200`, `T=100`, and `tmax=8 h`. It states that the coupled PDE is explicitly finite-differenced because built-in `NDSolve` was unstable; its forward Euler scheme is preferred.
- The companion Rmd maps output rows to `x=-L+2L(X-1)/Nx`; output arrays are 201×101. Its strict `φ<0.5` plotting pipeline and origin-constrained regression differ from the notebook’s two-window profile-shift estimator, so both are reported separately.
- The ex-vivo interface file contains four data-set time series. The replay independently fits each over 0–6 h. `Velocities_means.csv` reports three locations’ mean tracked-cell speeds and between-track standard deviations. Those are comparators, not independent validation of the coupled PDE or evidence for Fisher selection in living tissue.

## Scope, costs, and unacquired observations

The numerical work reads selected pinned CSVs and executes a standalone Python/NumPy transcription in [replay_coupled_fd.py](replay_coupled_fd.py). It matches the source arrays to floating-point roundoff at `Nx=200,Nt=100`; the base seed is refined to `Nx=3200,Nt=25600`. This is not Mathematica execution, an external code review, or biological validation. No additional data collection or wet-lab work was performed.

**Parameter discrepancy:** Supplement Table S1, visually checked on page 14 of `source_refs/skullwave_supplement.pdf`, prints `η=10⁴ Pa·s`; the pinned notebook parameter cell sets `η=10³*PaToNewUnits*SecToHr`. Both sources use `γ=0.8×10³` in corresponding converted units. The released data exactly match the notebook value. Replacing it with the table value at the original grid gives φ RMSE `2.37×10⁻⁶`, density RMSE `1.28×10⁻⁷`, and velocity RMSE `6.36×10⁻⁴` relative to the released arrays, with less than `0.003 μm/h` front-secant change for the source baseline. The notebook and table remain inconsistent; no intent is inferred.

The reviewed package does not supply an experimentally measured initial exponential tail for the in-vivo front, a matched BAPN front-speed series, an independent front-speed holdout for fitting α, or direct evidence that biological propagation is pulled rather than pushed. The article also notes that active migration and bulk tissue displacement are difficult to disentangle without membrane-dynamics observations. Its 15 μm spatial mesh cannot resolve the scalar pulled-threshold length (about 16.2 μm) or the extreme-profile stall diagnostic near 6.5 μm. No cell-scale, held-out biological control law is supported by these model outputs.
