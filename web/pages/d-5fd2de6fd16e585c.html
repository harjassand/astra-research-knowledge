# Research notes: causal transport of cell perturbations

## Scope and decision

Question: when does a randomized perturbation response measured in a source cell model identify or predict the causal response in a target cell model, culture condition, tissue, or species? The lane was asked to reconstruct formal transportability/invariant-mechanism boundaries, find a precise positive theorem or no-go, and propose a discriminating experiment with matched controls.

Decision: no universal “cell-model response transfers” theorem is defensible without cross-domain restrictions. The strongest exact result is a small non-identifiability theorem showing source RCT plus an observationally matched target does not identify target ATE under unrestricted mechanism/confounding shift. The positive route is the standard conditional transport formula, where target differences are allowed in pretreatment-state distribution but not in the conditional causal response mechanism, plus source positivity. A direct target randomized panel discriminates the no-go worlds and estimates the treatment-by-domain contrast. The error decomposition makes the unmeasured mechanism-shift term explicit; the bound itself is elementary and not presented as novel.

## Task and information contract

- Source inputs: source randomized perturbation outcomes, measured pretreatment Z, treatment assignment/guide/drug version, source domain metadata.
- Target inputs available for prospective prediction: target baseline Z distribution and declared treatment metadata; without target perturbation outcomes, the target response mechanism remains unobserved.
- Target evaluation data: randomized target perturbation and matched-control outcomes, held out from model fitting. These identify the target intention-to-treat effect under consistency/randomization, not any unmeasured molecular exposure effect.
- Units/cost: donor/sample blocks and independent wells are experimental units. Cell measurements are nested; reagent/dose calibration, target-engagement assays, sequencing/proteomics, orthology maps, and batch controls are separate costs.
- Requested scientific output: target average perturbation effect, and possibly state-specific effects. Transcriptome prediction is not itself the causal effect unless the endpoint/estimand says so.

## Candidate transfer model

Domain D may shift the distribution of pretreatment cell state/donor context Z, while leaving each intervention-specific response mean invariant conditional on Z. This is the exact condition needed for the displayed standardization formula in PROOF_AND_TRANSPORT.md. Mechanism-invariance is about `E[Y^a|Z,D]`, not just matching untreated gene-expression profiles. Similar baseline embeddings do not logically force equal causal response mechanisms.

Intervention versions must be declared. A guide assignment can have species-dependent efficiency; the direct randomized estimand is the guide-assignment effect. A shared target knockout is a different cross-domain estimand that requires measured/standardized knockdown. The same nominal drug dose may correspond to different intracellular exposure. “Same perturbation label” is not enough.

## Counterexample attack

The two target SCMs in PROOF_AND_TRANSPORT.md agree on the complete target observational distribution and the source observational and randomized distributions. They yield target ATE 1 and 0, respectively. Under consistency, the target diagonal observational law allows every ATE in [0,1], so the source effect 1 cannot sharpen the target identified set without cross-domain assumptions. An arbitrary transfer learner receives the same data in both worlds and cannot certify a point estimate. Exact error radius lower bound for any point estimate is at least 1/2.

This establishes a model-class result, not a claim that a particular cell perturbation is confounded or that no biological regularity exists. Under actual target randomization, target assignment effect is measured; the remaining question is whether dose/target engagement/endpoint correspond to a common intervention.

## Prior art attack

- Formal transportability already states that transport needs assumed graph/mechanism similarities, with do-calculus providing the identification query. The no-go is therefore foundational rather than novel.
- ICP uses invariance across observed experimental regimes to infer causal predictors under structural assumptions; held-out gene interventions are useful validation. This does not assert a new future tissue or species follows the same invariant equation.
- Prediction papers (scGen, CellOT) report cross-cell/context/species out-of-sample performance. These are real predictive benchmark findings, but they are not nonparametric identification theorems and have bounded tested context.
- Recent broad benchmarks differ in metrics/conclusions. 2026 Sci Adv reports no one model consistently generalizes across cell types and highlights effect-size/metric dependence. 2025 Nature Methods reports no consistent advantage over basic baselines on its unseen-perturbation tasks. 2026 Nature Biotechnology reports metric calibration can reverse apparent model-vs-baseline comparisons. Therefore no blanket model ranking is used; exact same-information baselines and a locked multi-metric score are required.

## Empirical control precedent and limits

Hagai et al. use matched treatment/control conditions across species, with common culture protocols and species-aware IFN reagents. Their result is biologically heterogeneous: some immune response output genes diverge, while regulator groups show more conservation; cell-to-cell response variability also matters. This is consistent with testing invariance by endpoint/mechanism rather than assuming it globally. Since these are in-vitro stimuli in fibroblasts/phagocytes, this supports no direct inference about intact tissue or organism.

## Proposed next gate

Pick a mechanistically credible single perturbation and endpoint; include source and one recipient cell domain; randomize the perturbation and matched negative control within donor/sample blocks; measure baseline Z from a pretreatment aliquot and target engagement independently; lock source-only predictions before exposing held-out recipient perturbation outcomes. Estimate recipient-minus-source treatment effect after standardizing over shared pre-treatment Z. Use `h` as a predeclared practical tolerance and biological blocks—not cells—for the uncertainty calculation. A failed target-engagement control is intervention mismatch, not evidence that the response pathway changed. A successful finite equivalence panel remains support scoped to assayed contexts.

## Unknowns

- No specific cell target, phenotype, or host context was supplied; the protocol remains generic and does not name a clinical hypothesis.
- A finite number of target validation domains cannot prove mechanism invariance in arbitrary new tissues/species. A useful nontrivial upper bound on mechanism shift ε needs either direct recipient perturbation measurements, independently justified mechanistic structure, or a validated smoothness/graph constraint.
- The selected Astra static routes had no cell-perturbation causal theorem. N28's compressed card mentions candidate environment identification but requires an un-opened proof source; no inference relies on it.
