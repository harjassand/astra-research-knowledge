STATUS
A precise conductance and additive-energy obstruction is proved for the one-coordinate orientation-exchange pair kernel defined below. Its irreducible positive support has singleton-mode conductance Theta(epsilon^2), even though the mode has stationary mass tending to 1/2. The pair-energy comparison constant for this kernel is therefore Omega(epsilon^-2), exponential in the O(log(1/epsilon))-bit input length. This softens the already-known directed-cycle disconnection into a connected, finite-bit bottleneck. It does not refute richer worm kernels, the OA113 matching kernel, an arbitrary microscopic energy inequality, or determinant-parity counting.

EXACT INTERFACE
Take n=6, k=3, cyclic indices 0,...,5, and rational epsilon=2^-b for integer b>=1. Define F_epsilon by
  F[i,i+1 mod 6]=1,
  F[i,i-1 mod 6]=epsilon,
  all other entries 0.
A microstate I in Omega is a 3-subset of [6]: select the F-row line for each i in I and the coordinate line for each j outside I. Its determinant amplitude and weight are
  a(I)=det F_epsilon[I,I^c],   w(I)=|a(I)|^2,
with pi(I)=w(I)/Z and Z=sum_I w(I). Zero-weight states are omitted from Omega_+; a proposal to one is rejected.

Define the one-replica kernel M as follows: choose i in I and j outside I uniformly from the 3x3 choices, propose I'=(I\{i}) union {j}, and accept with Metropolis probability min(1,w(I')/w(I)). This is a symmetric-proposal reversible chain on Omega_+. Define the two-replica kernel
  P = (1/2) Swap + (1/4)(M tensor Id) + (1/4)(Id tensor M)
on Omega_+^2. Swap sends (A,B) to (B,A) and is accepted with probability one. Thus P is reversible for pi tensor pi. This is a deliberately simple local paired-basis worm candidate: a single F-line/coordinate-line exchange is its only within-replica move. It is not asserted to represent every natural worm construction.

EXACT CONDUCTANCE DERIVATION
Let O={1,3,5} and E={0,2,4}. Direct 3x3 determinant expansion gives
  a(O)=a(E)=1+epsilon^3,
  Z=2(1+3 epsilon^2+2 epsilon^3+3 epsilon^4+epsilon^6).
The nine one-exchange neighbors of O have amplitudes: three equal to epsilon, three equal to epsilon^2, and three zero. Hence their total weights are 3 epsilon^2+3 epsilon^4. Since w(O)=(1+epsilon^3)^2 and each proposal has probability 1/9, for epsilon<=1/2 every positive neighbor is downhill and
  Phi_M({O})=M(O,Omega_+\\{O})
            =(epsilon^2+epsilon^4)/(3(1+epsilon^3)^2).
Also Z-2w(O)=6epsilon^2+6epsilon^4>0, so pi(O)<1/2; this is the smaller side of the cut. Therefore its conductance is exactly Phi_M({O})=Theta(epsilon^2), while pi(O) tends to 1/2 as epsilon tends to zero.
For every epsilon>0 the positive support has 14 states and the one-exchange graph on those states is connected. The small conductance is not caused by a disconnected positive support or a zero-mass mode.

For the pair-energy interface, set f(I)=1[I=O] and G(A,B)=f(A)+f(B). Then
  Var_pi(f)=p(1-p), p=pi(O),
  E_P(G)=(1/2)p Phi_M({O}).
The second identity follows because replica swap leaves G unchanged; the two equally likely one-replica updates contribute half the one-chain Dirichlet energy p Phi. Consequently every constant in Var_pi(f)<=L E_P(G) must satisfy
  L >= 2(1-p)/Phi_M({O}) = (3+o(1)) epsilon^-2.
For 0<epsilon<=1/2, Phi<=5 epsilon^2/12 and p<1/2, so L>12/(5 epsilon^2). With epsilon=2^-b this is greater than (12/5) 4^b, whereas the 6x6 rational input has O(b) bits. The local kernel cannot have a polynomial-in-input-bit-length energy constant along this family.

BOUNDARIES AND COSTS
The construction uses a constant 6x6 matrix with one entry epsilon=2^-b; its rational encoding length is O(b). All determinants, weights, proposal probabilities, conductance, and energy are exact rationals of polynomial bit length in b. The bottleneck is thus intrinsic to this specified proposal kernel, not numerical precision or an uncharged computation. The test concerns the fixed k=3 paired-basis sector and single-coordinate orientation changes, plus whole-replica swapping. It does not test arbitrary-size defect moves, an independently retained mismatch register, auxiliary-coordinate enlargements, nonlocal determinant updates, or alternate annealing schedules. Those may cross the bottleneck.
