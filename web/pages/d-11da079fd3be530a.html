UNIVERSAL DIMENSION-FREE BROADCASTING COMPARISON
Cycle03 proof packet. 2026-10-09.

Theorem: every finite-dimensional bistochastic, Hilbert-Schmidt-self-adjoint self-compatible quantum channel Phi has ONE canonical pure-state entanglement-breaking channel Psi with
  Psi >= (2Phi²-I)+,
  I-Psi <=2(I-Phi²)<=4(I-Phi),
in full Hermitian Hilbert-Schmidt quadratic-form order.
Arbitrary bistochastic self-compatible channels admit the analogous factor8 bound against I-symPhi. No dimension, spectral gap or per-query measurement is required.
