C08_L10 INITIAL — POSITIVE-VOLUME INFORMATION RATE AND STEADY STRAIN POWER
7 October 2026. Independent outward-search checkpoint. Origin: c08_l10, requested gpt-6-luna/max; observed model UNKNOWN. No peer-result exposure.

STATUS
A conditional, exact bridge is derived from the existing entropy-power estimate: an invariant positive-volume subsystem that robustly emits a stationary random symbol stream at a certified rate forces a quadratic lower bound on sustaining power for the stated strain-viscosity model. This is not a lower bound for Turing completeness, reachability hardness, or ordinary finite computations. A source-specific exact conversion also turns the triple-exponential H^1 lower bound in a published robust tape-bounded Beltrami simulator into a triple-exponential lower bound on the external strain-viscous power needed to hold that same field stationary. That source explicitly says its H^1 bound is construction-specific. No universal computational-cost theorem, novelty clearance, or field-breaking result is claimed.

1. EXACT POSITIVE-VOLUME THROUGHPUT THEOREM
Let (M,g) be a smooth closed Riemannian 3-manifold of volume V. Let X be a smooth divergence-free field, phi_t its flow, and mu=dVol/V. Put U={X!=0}, beta=mu(U)>0, S=Def X, and define H=h_mu(phi_1) using natural logarithms. Suppose a smooth force F and pressure p sustain X under the physical strain-viscosity equation
  nabla_X X - 2 nu div(S) + grad p = F,  nu>0,
and set W=integral_M <F,X> dVol.

Let E be a measurable set invariant under every phi_t, with alpha=mu(E)>0 and E subset U modulo null sets. Let mu_E=mu|E/alpha. For some sampling interval tau>0 and finite alphabet Sigma of size q>=2, suppose there is an ideal measurable symbol process D:E -> Sigma^Z such that
  D(phi_tau x)=shift(D(x)) a.e.,
  D_*mu_E is the iid uniform q-symbol shift.
Let Dhat:E -> Sigma^Z be a measurable, shift-equivariant decoded readout. Assume for every coordinate k the stationary symbol error probability satisfies
  mu_E{ x : Dhat(x)_k != D(x)_k } <= epsilon,
where 0<=epsilon<=1-1/q. Define
  r_epsilon = max(0, log q - h_2(epsilon) - epsilon log(q-1)),
with h_2(e)=-e log e-(1-e)log(1-e).
Then
  H >= alpha r_epsilon/tau,
  W >= 4 nu V alpha^2 r_epsilon^2/(beta tau^2).
If U=E modulo mu, this sharpens to the active-volume density statement
  W >= 4 nu V alpha (r_epsilon/tau)^2,
  W/(V alpha) >= 4 nu (r_epsilon/tau)^2.
For perfect q-ary symbols, r_0=log q. If q=2 and tau is a fixed physical sampling interval, the active-volume floor is 4 nu alpha (log 2/tau)^2 per unit total volume, or 4 nu (log 2/tau)^2 per unit active volume. This is a quadratic power-versus-information-throughput law; it is not a Landauer heat-per-erasure bound.

DERIVATION
The independent cycle-3 audit proves the zero-field extension of the entropy-power estimate. Writing beta=mu(U), the normal cocycle and three-dimensional strain decomposition give
  H <= integral_U s dmu,
  |S|^2=2s^2+(3/2)q_0^2+2|b|^2,
where q_0=<S e,e>, e=X/|X|, b=(I-e tensor e)Se and s is the magnitude of the trace-free part of S on e-perp. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz therefore gives
  H^2 <= beta integral_U s^2 dmu <= (beta/2) integral_M |S|^2 dmu.
The stationary power identity is W=2 nu V integral |S|^2 dmu, so W>=4 nu V H^2/beta.

The invariant-set entropy decomposition is
  H=alpha h_{mu_E}(phi_1)+(1-alpha)h_{mu_{E^c}}(phi_1) >= alpha h_{mu_E}(phi_1).
Factor entropy cannot increase under a measurable factor, so shift-equivariance of Dhat gives h_{mu_E}(phi_tau)>=h(Dhat_*mu_E). For the ideal iid process Z=D(x) and decoded process Zhat=Dhat(x), q-ary Fano plus the per-coordinate error bound gives, for every n,
  H(Zhat_0,...,Zhat_{n-1}) >= n[log q-h_2(epsilon)-epsilon log(q-1)].
Thus h(Dhat_*mu_E)>=r_epsilon. Entropy of a constant-speed flow scales as h(phi_tau)=tau h(phi_1); hence h_{mu_E}(phi_1)>=r_epsilon/tau and H>=alpha r_epsilon/tau. Substitution into the power estimate proves the displayed bound.

This theorem charges only the sustaining mechanical power W for the specified continuum equation. It assumes the input/readout law already exists as a stationary invariant factor. It does not charge the construction or acquisition of that law, sensor energy, external controls or resets, force synthesis, metric fabrication, clock costs, or encoding/decoding computation. Worst-case perturbation robustness implies the displayed average-error condition only after an invariant input distribution and an equivariant readout have been supplied. A point-to-set halting simulator or a finite list of robust trajectories does not meet this interface.

2. SOURCE-SPECIFIC ROBUST BELTRAMI COMPUTATION COST
Cardona, Miranda and Peralta-Salas, arXiv:2111.03559v3, Theorem 2 and Section 6, construct on the standard flat T^3 a Beltrami field v with curl(v)=lambda v that robustly simulates a tape-bounded Turing machine of tape size s_b. The source states
  ||v||_{H^1(T^3)} >= A exp(exp(exp(B s_b)))
for positive constants A,B, and explicitly cautions in Remark 3 that this lower bound comes from its encoding/localization construction and is not asserted for all implementations. The source defines robustness under uniform C^0 perturbations of the field and initial conditions, but its object is finite tape-bounded reachability, not a positive-volume stationary symbol stream.

For this same field on the flat torus, div(v)=0 and Delta v=-lambda^2 v. The identity (v dot nabla)v=nabla(|v|^2/2) lets pressure cancel the advective term. Under the strain-viscosity model, choose p=-|v|^2/2 and F=nu lambda^2 v. Then
  W=integral <F,v> = nu lambda^2 ||v||_2^2.
On a flat periodic domain, integration by parts gives ||Def v||_2^2=(1/2)||nabla v||_2^2=(lambda^2/2)||v||_2^2, agreeing with W=2nu||Def v||_2^2. With the inhomogeneous Sobolev norm ||v||_{H^1}^2=(1+lambda^2)||v||_2^2,
  W=nu lambda^2/(1+lambda^2) ||v||_{H^1}^2.
The construction uses positive odd integer lambda, so lambda>=1, and therefore
  W >= (nu/2)||v||_{H^1}^2
    >= (nu A^2/2) exp(2 exp(exp(B s_b))).
This is a derived, source-specific forced-power consequence. It is not a lower bound on the work of every fluid implementation of a tape-bounded machine, and it gives no rate per machine step. The field is an Euler steady state; the nonzero F above is the force required to hold it steady under strain viscosity. Controller and forcing hardware costs remain uncharged.

3. OUTWARD SEARCH: WHAT THE COMPARATORS DO AND DO NOT GIVE
Reliable computation and geometric complexity. The Beltrami paper's Theorem 2 is an especially close comparator: robustness and a quantitative tape-space cost are already present, but the simulation is finite-state/tape-bounded reachability from specified points, not positive-volume streaming. Its Theorem 6 also gives Turing-complete smooth flows and diffeomorphisms on S^2 with zero topological entropy; this is a topological/computability separation, not a volume-preserving 3D counterexample. Bruera et al., arXiv:2404.07288v3 (9 Apr 2026), prove positive topological entropy for branching Turing machines and transfer it to the usual continuous-encoding area-preserving disk/Euler-flow constructions. Their coded invariant set is the square Cantor set, which is area-null. The same paper constructs a universal (indeed reversible, in the stated theorem) Turing machine of zero topological entropy and zero speed. These results block a general computation-complexity -> entropy implication. Positive topological entropy on an area-null code set also does not supply positive normalized-volume metric entropy. A full ambient volume-entropy calculation for the cited disk/flow extensions was not found and is not claimed here.

A newer exact Navier-Stokes comparator is Dyhr et al., arXiv:2507.07696 / PNAS Nexus (2026), Theorem A: after a generally not small metric deformation, a Hodge-admissible closed 3-manifold admits a stationary Turing-complete Navier-Stokes field for the Hodge Laplacian, with any viscosity. Their harmonic 1-form is annihilated by Hodge viscosity. On curved manifolds this does not mean the strain-viscosity force vanishes: the Hodge and strain operators differ by a Ricci term. For the same harmonic field, the cycle-3 audit derives a strain-viscous sustaining work W=2nu integral |Def X|^2 dVol (equivalently the Ricci work after pressure projection). The paper does not charge the metric deformation or establish a positive-volume output rate. This is a constitutive-model and geometry-cost boundary, not a contradiction.

Mixing. Lin, Thiffeault and Doering, arXiv:1009.0834, study time-dependent incompressible stirring on a periodic box using H^-1 as a mix norm and instantaneous RMS speed or strain/power constraints. Their equations (12)-(13), (17)-(27) give finite-time limits for H^-1 decay under those budgets. That is a different interface from autonomous steady-flow sustaining work versus Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy. The supplied Sol equality example has positive volume entropy but its time-one map is (cat map) x (identity roof coordinate), so a nonconstant roof-coordinate observable is invariant and the map is not mixing. Thus the sharp entropy-power estimate alone gives no decay rate for passive scalars, no mixing-time bound, and no implication of rapid mixing.

4. CONSEQUENCES, BOUNDARIES, AND NEXT GATE
The useful consequential target is a passive incompressible device that must continuously emit a stationary random symbol stream on an invariant set of known positive volume, with a certified average readout error, at a fixed physical sampling clock. For that admitted interface the sustaining-power law above is exact and scales quadratically in rate. The T^3 source gives a separate, concrete lower bound for one robust finite-machine construction after converting its H^1 cost to actual strain-viscous work.

The decisive missing gate for broad robust-computation claims is not the entropy-to-power inequality; it is an acquired positive-volume, rate-bearing symbolic factor in the exact steady strain-viscous model, with fixed metric and explicit perturbation/error tolerances. Existing halting/reachability and Cantor-set coding results do not provide it. A sharp counterexample route is to realize a robust computation with all coded trajectories confined to a null-volume invariant set while the volume entropy remains zero; this audit found the null code set but did not verify zero volume entropy of the entire 3D extension. A stronger positive result would build the positive-volume factor and charge geometry, force generation, source acquisition and clock costs. No executable evidence was needed for the theorem composition; all deductions are analytic. Finite checks from prior work were not rerun or promoted.
