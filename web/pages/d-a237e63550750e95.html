Then ||K||<=HN, the exact positive stopped path has rho>=eta I and

function even at coincident eigenvalues. Its first derivatives vanish at the
support boundary; a C2 spectral formulation is available but not needed for
the Euler strong-rate proof.

gap lower bounds when implementing it.

5. EXPLICIT SEPARABLE CORRECTION AND ITS OUTPUT SIZE

Set a=d theta, rho_j'=(rho_j-theta I)/(1-a). These are exact densities.
Expand each rho_j^tensorN in local factors I/d or rho_j'. The all-white weight
is always w=a^N. Retain all nonwhite products with weights

For each c_v!=0 let P_v have Ta on its r nonidentity sites and I elsewhere.
The remainder has exact form
 w I/D+E=(w-S)I/D+sum |c_v|[I+sign(c_v)P_v]/D.
Each bracket is the uniform parity-sign mixture of 2^(r-1) local products
(I+s_i Ta)/d with product signs=sign(c_v). At r=1 it has one term.
Since S<=w/2, all weights are nonnegative with residual white mass>=w/2.
The COMPLETE density is EXACTLY rho_tilde; its trace-one property makes all
weights sum to one. This is generally a nonidentical-product decomposition.

There are at most R(2^N-1) nonwhite terms plus
 sum_(r=1)^N binom(N,r)(d^2-1)^r 2^(r-1)

There are d^(2N)-1 coefficients, not a double-exponential number. Computing
dense tensor matrices and all coefficient contractions naively costs at most
R poly(N)d^(O(N)) bit-arithmetic calls. The representation length is also
exp(O(N)) with fixed d. No compact/poly(N) exact decomposition is claimed.

COMPUTABLE SIGNS AND ZERO COEFFICIENTS. Choose the correction basis as standard

6. FINITE-BIT PATH ACQUISITION: EXPONENTIAL BITS, NOT DOUBLE EXPONENTIAL WORK

but its required BIT COUNT is

Arithmetic/evaluation is polynomial in P, so remains exp(O(N)); it is not
polynomial in xi^-1. Enumerating a grid at that precision would break the
