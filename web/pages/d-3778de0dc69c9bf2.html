# Primary sources and evidence map

1. **Allman, Matias & Rhodes (2009), “Identifiability of Parameters in Latent Structure Models with Many Observed Variables.”** [Annals of Statistics paper](https://doi.org/10.1214/09-AOS689), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.5032). Corollary 3 gives generic three-view latent-class identifiability under Kruskal-rank conditions. The theorem here uses a same-state pre-read block and constructs a particular whitening/certificate route; the source does not cover the physical slow-read model or adversarial endpoint blocks.
2. **Anandkumar, Ge, Hsu, Kakade & Telgarsky (2014), “Tensor Decompositions for Learning Latent Variable Models.”** [JMLR article and PDF](https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/anandkumar14b.html). Theorem 5.1 gives the robust orthogonal tensor-power perturbation result used by the separate paired-tensor estimator. This block-mean route avoids that inverse-emission tensor exponent by requiring repeated same-state reads and a disturbance envelope.
3. **Weissman, Ordentlich, Seroussi, Verdú & Weinberger (2003), “Inequalities for the \(L_1\) Deviation of the Empirical Distribution.”** [HP Labs report PDF](https://shiftleft.com/mirrors/www.hpl.hp.com/techreports/2003/HPL-2003-97R1.pdf). Finite-alphabet concentration prior art; the repeated-block Euclidean bound here follows from a one-hot variance bound and bounded differences.
4. **McDonald & Yuksel (2020), “Exponential Filter Stability via Dobrushin's Coefficient.”** [Primary arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08463). Theorem 12 addresses controlled filter stability and Dobrushin contraction, prior art for the uniform history-dependent control setting in the no-freeze companion result.
5. **Huber (1964), “Robust Estimation of a Location Parameter.”** [Annals of Mathematical Statistics DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177703732). Classical contamination model context. Exact overlap formulas for replacement-total-variation balls and Huber-mixture neighborhoods are derived in this report and are kept as distinct models.

## Physical protocols (not theorem validation)

- **NV-14N:** Zhang et al. (2025), [publisher article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64772-6), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64772-6). The 80,000-RIM/1.695-s point and source-data T1 scan are audited in ../robust_nv/REPORT.md and ../robust_nv/SOURCES.md. The report does not certify a uniform block-ramp envelope or shared classical emission matrix.
- **Force-smFRET:** Hohng et al. (2007), [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3558530/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1146113). Their reversible force protocol identifies force-dependent Holliday-junction kinetics, not a statewise scalar slowdown with a uniform per-block jump bound. Details: ../application_smfret/REPORT.md and ../application_smfret/SOURCES.md.
- **Cryo-arrest:** Masip et al. (2016), [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5038880/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.3921). Repeated imaging of selected cooled samples does not show \(Q\mapsto\rho Q\) for arbitrary fate states or validate the needed disturbance margin. Protocol details and limitations: ../slow_readout/SOURCES.md.
