3. THE PRECISE DENSITY FACTOR AND HOW OUR INDUCTION PAYS IT

Report lines 82-103 correctly identify the factor
  gamma(B_s(y))/s^k
that appears after Tonelli. Disjoint tiny balls alone do not control it.
Our proof does not insert a desired global upper density at this point.
For fixed equal atomic radii a and fixed 10a-separated F, it starts at
scales [a,2a], where every such ball contains at most one atom. At upward
scale t_i it assumes only the bound already proved up to t_i/2.
Euclidean covering extends THIS known bound to the weaker A_w=33^n A
bound through 4t_i. The relevant Tonelli factors have s<=t<=2t_i and
2t<=4t_i, so both are paid by that weaker, already justified bound.
The beta integral below a is zero; no atomic density below a is used.

The SAME universal beta inequality applies to the inner-ball restriction
gamma'. The cumulative telescope pays all radii in its Carleson integral:
  integral_0^t W(y,s) ds/s
    = integral_{5t}^{6t}[P(y,u)-P_0(y)] du/u
    <= log(6/5) eta_*.
Hence the local Carleson constant is B A_w^2 2^k log(6/5) eta_*.
Choose eta_* once to make that smaller than the NV threshold. NV gives
the STRONG density at t_i; interpolation completes the next interval.
This is an upward induction using weaker old-scale information. It does
not presume the strong new-scale estimate it obtains from NV.

All weights in this proof are exactly omega_k a^k. Restrictions discard
no target inner-ball atom, and the published conclusion, after scaling,
counts their full sum. Arbitrary variable-radius stopping packings are
NOT covered by this specific bootstrap. They are not needed to construct
finite-H^k small-tail pieces and obtain sigma-finite rectifiability.
The report's suggested Carleson bound for EVERY variable-radius packing
would be a stronger useful route to a whole-set theorem; we have not
claimed that bound merely from the equal-radius induction.

Report line 103 is a separate and valid gate: finite mass alone is not
rectifiability. Our proof takes gamma_j=H^k restricted to the newly finite

  integral gamma_j(dx) integral_0^{R_j} beta_gamma_j(x,r)^2 dr/r
    <= B A_* log(6/5) eta_* gamma_j(R^n)<infinity.
Larger radii have finite square integral by finite total mass and their
positive lower cutoff. AT Corollary 1.3 then gives countable rectifiability.
No Hausdorff-null exception is claimed to be pointwise geometric regularity.

Our theorem preserves that quantifier, so none falsifies its scope.
Our n=1 tuple conclusion is stronger than the report's generic k=0
