2. PROOF OF THE NEW SQUARE-ROOT FIELD CERTIFICATE

Write D(A||B)=tr[A(log A-log B)-A+B] for PSD A and positive B, with the
continuous convention at zero. For M=EA,

  E D(A||B)=H(A)+D(M||B)>=H(A).                      (V)

its Hessian in direction H is tr[H Dlog(X)[H]], which in an eigenbasis is

1/x_i. Integrating the Hessian along a segment yields the supporting-tangent
inequality defining D. PSD endpoints follow by continuity. Formula (V) is
then exact by expanding and using EA=M. This is established matrix entropy
machinery (Chen-Tropp Lemma4.1); it is not claimed new.

Choose B=S^2, S=ER. In eigenbases of R and S, with eigenvalues u_i>=0,
v_j>=s, let w_ij be squared overlaps. Their row/column sums are one and

  D(R^2||S^2)=sum_ij w_ij
     [2u_i^2 log(u_i/v_j)-u_i^2+v_j^2],
  ||R-S||_F^2=sum_ij w_ij (u_i-v_j)^2.               (F)

For t>=0 define b(t)=(2t^2 log t-t^2+1)/(t-1)^2, b(1)=2, b(0)=1.

Q and (t-1)^3 have the same sign. Continuity supplies t=0,1.
Every ratio u_i/v_j<=r/s. Consequently (F) implies

  D(R^2||S^2)<=b(r/s)||R-S||_F^2.

For stable certified arithmetic near v=1, also use b(v)<=2+2log v for v>=1.
