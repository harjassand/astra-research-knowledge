because the potential is scaled by s⁻ʲ to preserve total current. The fixed finite-dimensional current-to-voltage map is invertible on the zero-sum plane, so the quadratic energy is strictly positive on every nonzero source vector.

\[
\boxed{\mathcal E[Z_j]=4e_b(2s)^{-j},\qquad

\tag{3}

This is an interval of equality, rather than merely one pair. The source's gauge proof handles every smooth boundary trace and then all H¹/² traces by continuity. Crucially its annihilation is weighted by u−u*, where u* is common to that one nested cell.
