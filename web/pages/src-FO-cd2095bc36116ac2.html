## 2. Reconstruction of the supplied mathematical capital

### Reflection-invariant classification

For m≥2 and left-O(m)-invariant homogeneous quartic F on R^(m×2), write A=(x|y), u=|x|², v=|y|², w=x·y. The six invariants u²,v²,uv,w²,uw,vw span the class. To see sufficiency of the two-dimensional invariant calculation for arbitrary m, rotate span{x,y} into a fixed plane; the same Gram variables determine its orbit.

Restrict F to e(s,t) for fixed unit e. Rank-one convexity makes the binary quartic P(s,t) convex and nonnegative. If P is positive off zero, factor it into two positive-definite real quadratic forms. Whiten one and diagonalize the other, then rescale both coordinates. This yields P=s⁴+2rs²t²+t⁴ with r≥1. Its Hessian at (1,1) in direction (1,-1) gives r≤3.

Two invariant quartics with the same rank-one restriction differ by a multiple of Δ=uv-w². Thus, in these coordinates,

    F=u²+v²+2rw²+λΔ.

At background e2(1,-1) and direction e1(1,1), the rank-one Hessian is 8(1+λ-r), so λ≥r-1. The exact decomposition is

    F=((3-r)/2)H1+((r-1)/2)H3+(λ-r+1)Δ,
    H1=u²+v²+2w²=tr((AᵀA)²),
    H3=(u+v)²+4w²=(|x+y|⁴+|x-y|⁴)/2.

Both H1 and H3 are convex. For H1, D²H1(A)[B,B]=2|AᵀB+BᵀA|²+4tr((AᵀA)(BᵀB))≥0. A nonnegative convex quartic's fourth root is the gauge of its balanced convex unit ball. If that gauge has a nonzero kernel, it is translation invariant along the kernel. Hence a degenerate binary restriction becomes as⁴, and the full integrand becomes au²+λΔ with a,λ≥0. Undoing the right-coordinate change preserves convexity and Δ's nonnegative coefficient.

Since Δ is the sum of squares of the 2-by-2 minors, this is a convex function of A and its minors. It proves polyconvexity and quasiconvexity; the reverse quasiconvex-to-rank-one implication completes the supplied classification. This reconstruction found no missing m≥2 case. It is a symmetry-restricted result, not a reduction of arbitrary quartics.

### Orientation-sensitive class and acoustic interface

Left-SO(2) invariance permits exactly the nine basis elements above; a reflection reverses the last three. After positive rank-one normalization,

    F=u²+v²+2rw²+λq²+q(ell u+mu v+nu w),
    1≤r≤3, epsilon=3-r≥0, D=1+λ-r≥0.

At rank-one input directions (1,1) and (1,-1), the even acoustic blocks have eigenvalues (12(1+r),4epsilon) and (4(1+r),4D). The cross couplings are ell+mu±nu, 2(mu-ell), and -3(ell+mu±nu), with the corresponding signs after exchanging the small eigendirections. The 2-by-2 minors and the two three-dimensional Schur complements give the source's necessary two-slack and joint bounds. They are not sufficient conditions. A zero diagonal in a positive semidefinite matrix forces its row to vanish, so either epsilon=0 or D=0 kills all three odd coefficients. For a degenerate restriction as⁴, the b=e2 acoustic zero rows first force mu=nu=0, then b=e1 forces ell=0; λ≥0 remains.

For every nine-coefficient quartic, output rotations reduce rank-one positivity to a biquadratic in x=(a,b,c,d) and the binary input direction (s,t). Its acoustic Gram has the form

### Analytic implication, including the boundary and infinity steps

Put G=F*−γq². Because ω is quadratic and closed, Cartan's formula with the Euler field gives the global polynomial primitive α=(1/4)i_Euler ω. For v(x)=A0x+ψ(x), ψ Schwartz, (Dv)*ω=d((Dv)*α). Every coefficient of the pulled-back one-form contains a derivative of Dv and is rapidly decreasing. Therefore its exterior derivative has zero integral over R². Integrating (1) gives

    ∫ Σ_i D²G(Dv)[∂i Dv,∂i Dv] ≥ 0.              (2)

For φ smooth and compactly supported, let φ_t=e^(tΔ)φ and A_t=A0+Dφ_t. The relative energy

    E(t)=∫[G(A_t)−G(A0)−DG(A0):Dφ_t]

is finite. Integration by parts gives E'(t)=−∫Σ_iD²G(A_t)[∂i A_t,∂i A_t]≤0. Quartic growth bounds its integrand by a constant times |A0|²|Dφ_t|²+|A0||Dφ_t|³+|Dφ_t|⁴. In dimension two the heat estimates ||Dφ_t||_p≤C_p t^(−1+1/p)||Dφ||_1 for p=2,3,4 make E(t)→0 as t→∞. Strong heat continuity in these spaces and Hölder's inequality give E(t)→E(0) at zero. Thus E(0)≥0. Since ∫Dφ=0, this is quasiconvexity for G.

Approximation in W^(1,4), together with the quartic difference bound C(|A|+|B|)³|A−B|, passes the result to every bounded Lipschitz domain and every W^(1,∞)_0 perturbation. Finally q is a null Lagrangian: the mean determinant of A0+Dφ is q(A0). Jensen therefore proves quasiconvexity of γq² for γ≥0. Consequently F* is quasiconvex.

This reconstructs the heat/exact-form mechanism already present in [Bruno–Pasqualotto, sections 2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1) and N740. ## 5. A failed positive-generator transfer, proved exactly

Consider the closed conic hull of all right-coordinate images of homogeneous left-and-right-SO(2)-invariant rank-one-convex quartics. The known isotropic cone and its quasiconvexity are given in [Bruno–Pasqualotto, Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1#A1). F* is not in this generated cone.

Define the linear functional on the nine coefficients by

    ell(p)=(10/7)[4(p1+p2)−(p3+p4)]+p7+p8.

For an isotropic quartic a|A|⁴+bq|A|²+cq², rank-one tests give a≥0 and |b|≤4a. If a=0, then b=0 and c≥0. After A→AS with S invertible, factor a|det S|²>0 and normalize T=SSᵀ/|det S| so det T=1. Write

    T=[[h+delta,k],[k,h−delta]], h>0,
    h²−delta²−k²=1, beta=(b/a)sign(det S), |beta|≤4.

The normalized transformed polynomial is σ²+beta qσ+chi q², where σ=(h+delta)u+2kw+(h−delta)v. Direct coefficient evaluation yields

    ell=(10/7)(2h²+14delta²+4)+2beta h
         ≥(20/7)(h−7/5)²+20delta²+4/35 > 0.

The cq² ray has ell=0. Positivity survives sums, positive scaling, limits and singular right-coordinate limits. But ell(p*)=−3/35. This exactly refutes the named positive-generator transfer for F*. It does not cover arbitrary separately added convex quartics or arbitrary quasiconvex constructions. The heat certificate succeeds by a broader compatible-jet operation.

The higher-leverage next target is a cone inclusion. Let C_R={p:∃K skew, Q8(p,K)≥0}. Let C_H be the cone of coefficients admitting γ≥0 and a globally closed homogeneous-quadratic ω such that E_(F_p−γq²)+ω≥0 on every compatible jet. An inclusion C_R⊆C_H would settle the whole nine-dimensional class by the analytic proof above. Pure compatible jets show the reverse inclusion C_H⊆C_R. This is a sufficient-certificate classification target; quasiconvexity might hold by other methods even if this particular inclusion fails.

The computable inner cone C_H,SOS further requires a positive semidefinite bilinear Gram on the 24 monomials. F* and the explicit cube lie in this inner cone. A proof C_R⊆C_H,SOS would suffice. An exact separating rank-one-convex input would refute only this identity-diffusion SOS certificate class; it would not refute quasiconvexity, the unrestricted pointwise positivity cone C_H, or N740's broader constant-anisotropic-diffusion gate. A failed numerical SOS search establishes none of these separations.
