2. POSITIVE RANK MINIMIZER: EXACT SVD ARGUMENT

S is CP, unital and TP since int F_v^2=I. It is self-adjoint and positive
as an operator on Hilbert-Schmidt space, because
Tr A^dag F_v A F_v=||F_v^(1/2) A F_v^(1/2)||_HS^2>=0.
A unital TP CP map contracts HS norm: Kadison-Schwarz followed by trace
preservation gives ||S A||_HS<=||A||_HS. Thus spec(S) is contained in [0,1].

For any complex A, write A=sum_i s_i |u_i><v_i| in an SVD. For one Hermitian
F_v, taking real parts and 2 Re(z conjugate(w))<=|z|^2+|w|^2 gives

  Tr A^dag F_v A F_v
  <=(1/2){Tr |A^dag| F_v |A^dag| F_v
                  +Tr |A| F_v |A| F_v}.                (2.1)

Indeed the left side is sum_(i,j) s_i s_j
<u_i,F_v u_j><v_j,F_v v_i>; the two square sums are the two right terms.
Here |A|=(A^dag A)^(1/2) and |A^dag|=(A A^dag)^(1/2). Both positive
matrices have exactly the rank and HS norm of A. Integrating (2.1),

  E_S(A) >= [E_S(|A|)+E_S(|A^dag|)]/2.                 (2.2)

The rank-constrained unit HS sphere is compact in finite dimension. An
energy minimizer therefore exists, and at least one of the two moduli in
(2.2) is a POSITIVE minimizer of the same rank bound. This reduction is on
the whole reducible H, including rectangular sector blocks. It does not
assert that individual Kraus maps of an arbitrary archive can be positive.
