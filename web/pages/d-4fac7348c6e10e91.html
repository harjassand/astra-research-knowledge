# Bounded Gaussian experiment memory: prior-art and scope audit

7 October 2026. Source workspaces were read only. Target: Section 3A of `work/quantum_collision.md`, equations (3A.1)--(3A.19).

**Finding.** No primary source located in this bounded search states the exact theorem below. This is not novelty clearance. The posterior-quantization viewpoint is established statistical decision theory, and the lower-bound exponent can be derived from established Shannon/Holevo inequalities. The defensible candidate contribution is the precise fixed-experiment TV statement, its matching positive reconstruction without a tail logarithm, and the resulting critical-orbit precision law. Calling either posterior quantization or the dimension-versus-squared-distortion exponent new would overstate the evidence.

## 1. Exact claim being compared

Let `Theta` be a fixed bounded subset of an affine Gaussian mean space of dimension `s>=1`. After discarding parameter-independent orthogonal Gaussian coordinates and whitening its fixed positive covariance, the experiment is `P_theta=N(theta,I_s)`. An encoder sees one draw `X`; it does not receive `theta`. All input-dependent retained memory is a quantum/classical direct sum of total dimension `D=sum_j d_j`. The decoder outputs a classical observation `Y`. Arbitrary input-independent shared **classical** randomness is free; no input-dependent side register or shared entanglement is included. Define

\[
 e_D(\Theta)=\inf_{\mathcal E,\mathcal D}
    \sup_{\theta\in\Theta}
    \operatorname{TV}\bigl(\mathcal D\mathcal E(P_\theta),P_\theta\bigr).
\]

The claimed law is

\[
 c_\Theta D^{-2/s}\le e_D(\Theta)\le C_\Theta D^{-2/s},
 \qquad 0<c_\Theta\le C_\Theta<\infty.
\]

The lower permits arbitrary quantum memory; the upper is wholly classical. A finite set of `s+1` affinely independent means suffices. This compares **output distributions for every parameter**, rather than squared error of `X`, estimating `theta`, or simulating a known source law. The exponent is the dimension of the continuously varying posterior statistic. It need not equal the dimension of the parameter manifold.

## 3. The quantum lower exponent is already a corollary of standard inequalities

This derivation is an analysis of the proposed theorem, not a claim that any of the cited papers contains its entire statement. It identifies how much novelty can reasonably attach to the converse.

Choose `s+1` affinely independent means `theta_0,...,theta_s`, with positive prior weights `pi_i`. Let `X` follow their Gaussian mixture, and put `T=m(X)=E(theta|X)`. Set

\[
 V=[\theta_1-\theta_0,\ldots,\theta_s-\theta_0],\qquad
 w_i(x)=\frac{\pi_i\exp(\theta_i\cdot x-\|\theta_i\|^2/2)}
 {\sum_j\pi_j\exp(\theta_j\cdot x-\|\theta_j\|^2/2)}.
\]

Then

\[
 m(x)=\theta_0+V(w_1(x),\ldots,w_s(x))^\mathsf T,
 \qquad
 \det Dm(x)=(\det V)^2\prod_{i=0}^s w_i(x)>0.
 \tag{P.1}
\]

The determinant follows from `Dm=V(diag(w_1,...,w_s)-ww^T)V^T` and the matrix determinant lemma. Strict monotonicity makes `m` injective. Each `log w_i(x)` is bounded in absolute value by `C(1+||x||)`, while the mixture has finite first moment and finite differential entropy. Change of variables therefore gives

\[
 h(T)=h(X)+2\log|\det V|+\sum_i E\log w_i(X)\in\mathbb R.
 \tag{P.2}
\]

This also explains why a finite parameter set still presents a continuous `s`-dimensional compression problem.

Fix any admissible code of error `e`. Let `R` be any input-independent shared seed and let `Y` be the decoded observation. Conditional Holevo/data processing gives

\[
 I(T;Y,R)=I(T;Y\mid R)\le\log D.
 \tag{P.3}
\]

Classical side branches fit in the direct sum. The seed may have any alphabet; conditioning bounds each branch by the same memory dimension. The standard continuous squared-error Shannon lower bound, applied to `T` and the reconstruction `m(Y)`, gives, with `L=E||T-m(Y)||^2`,

\[
 I(T;Y,R)\ge h(T)-\frac{s}{2}\log\frac{2\pi\mathrm e L}{s},
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 L\ge\frac{s}{2\pi\mathrm e}\exp\!\left(\frac{2h(T)}s\right)D^{-2/s}.
 \tag{P.4}
\]

The source and estimate need not be iid repetitions for this information inequality; iid block coding is needed for general achievability statements, not for the inequality itself. Equivalently, (P.4) follows by comparing the joint law to a centered Gaussian conditional reference density of variance `L/s`.

With `B=max_i||theta_i||>0`, the bounded loss `||theta-m(y)||^2/(4B^2)` and the original parameterwise TV bound imply

\[
 L=E\|\theta-m(Y)\|^2-E\|\theta-m(X)\|^2\le4B^2 e.
 \tag{P.5}
\]

The equality is the posterior orthogonality identity for the Markov chain `theta -> X -> memory -> Y`. Combining (P.4)--(P.5), and then taking the infimum over codes, yields

\[
 e_D\ge\frac{s}{8\pi\mathrm e B^2}
       \exp\!\left(\frac{2h(T)}s\right)D^{-2/s}.
 \tag{P.6}
\]

Thus the exponent against arbitrary quantum memory follows from standard information theory once the posterior geometry and bounded-loss conversion are supplied. Section 3A's direct central small-ball/trace proof remains useful: it is elementary, avoids entropy calculations, and gives a geometric constant. It should be described as a direct proof of this scoped converse, not as the discovery of a new quantum rate-distortion dimension principle.

## 4. What remains specific, and what may still be old

For a fixed encoder, a classical memory experiment is a garbling of the original experiment. Optimizing its decoder is exactly reverse deficiency. Without the free seed, the classical problem is therefore a finite-experiment approximation problem in the existing Le Cam/Strasser framework. With a seed, it is the same constrained bottleneck conditional on that external randomization. The new theorem's subject is a rate inside that framework, not a new notion of approximate sufficiency.

The concrete upper is stronger than simply quoting ordinary quantization: the fixed broad reference `g=N(0,4)` sends each bounded-mean target to a periodic `W^{2,1}` density under `u=Phi(x/2)`. Its positive encode/decode hat kernel is a valid Markov kernel and cancels the first moment. `J^s` labels produce uniform TV `O(J^-2)` without cutting off a Gaussian tail. Probability transforms, companding, and positive linear approximation are established ingredients. I did not locate a primary theorem already combining them into the exact present bottleneck statement. This absence supports continued technical use, not a historical priority assertion.

The finite-hypothesis and orbit consequences are useful ways to expose the scope. In particular, a critical known-spectrum unitary orbit can have affine span `d^2-1` despite lower manifold dimension. The posterior observation varies in the full affine space. That consequence is not supplied by a growing-signal parameter-count memory formula alone. Its iid transfer nevertheless remains conditional on the separately inherited Gaussian bridge.

The information-theoretic conclusion is equality of **exponents and dimension order**, not equality of classical and quantum optimal errors or constants. The proofs permit a quantum constant-factor advantage. They also do not give a sharp asymptotic constant, or solve a parameter set whose radius or spanning geometry varies with `D`.

## 5. Scope and cost statements to retain in a release

1. Fix `s`, covariance, `Theta`, and its affine geometry before taking `D -> infinity`. Nearly colliding means can make the lower constant arbitrarily small. A mean set with affine dimension zero has a one-label exact simulator.
2. Specify memory dimension rather than the number of real parameters used to describe a density matrix. All input-dependent classical registers contribute to `sum_j d_j`. The decoder does not revisit `X`.
3. State free independent classical randomness explicitly. Preserved input-dependent side information and entanglement assistance are different resource models.
4. State that reconstruction is the marginal law of a fresh decoded observation. It does not preserve the original observation jointly with an external record, or arbitrary quantum purification.
5. Treat the hat encoder and inverse-normal decoder as measurable stochastic channels. The law alone is not a finite-bit running-time theorem. A deterministic finite-bit output alphabet is atomic and has TV one against the continuous Gaussian; a digital implementation requires a declared output discretization or continuous-output sampling convention.
6. For the iid corollary, retain the bridge remainder. With critical `delta_n=Theta(n^-1/2)`, the claimed matching precision law is justified only in the stated regime `epsilon_n sqrt(n) -> infinity`; the theorem does not settle precision below the comparison error.

Suggested positioning: **“We give matching finite-memory bounds for bounded Gaussian location experiments under uniform total variation reconstruction, including finite affine-spanning hypothesis sets and unrestricted quantum memories with independent shared randomness. The proof combines classical experiment quantization and a posterior-distortion converse with an explicit positive reconstruction kernel.”** Add “new” or “first” only after a deeper search of quantitative deficiency/quantization results, including the inaccessible portions of Torgersen and older high-resolution decision-quantization papers.
