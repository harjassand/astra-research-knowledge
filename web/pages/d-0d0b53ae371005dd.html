QUANTUM CLASSICALITY: NEW DEDUCTIONS AND SHARP BOUNDARIES
2026-10-07. Internal proof status; external correctness and priority unresolved.

Strongest actual deduction: a sharp receiver-growth phase diagram for the N61
copy-ladder obstruction. Let L,d>=2, m=2^(L-1),
H=direct_sum_{j=0}^{L-1} Sym^(2^j)(C^d), and
rho_psi=L^-1 direct_sum_j psi^(tensor 2^j). b_K is the best worst-state,
worst-receiver half-trace-distance error of one CPTP H->H^(tensor K), with
arbitrary correlated/entangled outputs and full input-algebra domain.

For every integer K>=1,

 b_K >= L^-1 sum_{2^j<K} [(d-1)/(d+2^j)](1-2^j/K),

 |b_K-min(log_2 K/L,1)| <= 1/L+(m+1)/(d+m).

Thus if d/m->infinity, uniformly in ANY receiver-count sequence K_L,

 b_(K_L)=min(log_2 K_L/L,1)+o(1),
 b_(K_L)->0 iff log K_L=o(L).

This is a necessary-and-sufficient growth threshold, beyond the supplied
N61 upper-only statement. The same finite exact (m+1)-design experiment
works for every K; a common faithful perturbation eta_L->0 loses at most
2eta_L in the lower bound and eta_L in the upper bound. e_EB->1 still holds.
There is no independent-clone or purification-preservation assertion.

Proof: the exact CPTP one-copy decoder sends every ladder member to psi.
For any proposed K broadcaster, restrict decoder-composed channel to each
input copy sector N=2^j. Haar/receiver-average universal cloning fidelity is
at most N/K+[(K-N)/K](N+1)/(N+d) for N<K, otherwise <=1. Sum over the uniform
input flag; geometric summation gives the lower bound. Binary splitting
gives upper ceil(log_2 K)/L, and a direct envelope gives the uniform formula.

Primary import: Keyl-Werner, Optimal Cloning of Pure States, Judging Single
Clones, https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9807010 , Theorem 1 PDF p3, Section II
unrestricted-output convention p2, Section III.A symmetrization pp3-4.
The import permits arbitrary full tensor-product outputs; this is essential.
Full proof: ladder_many_receiver/PROOF.md.
Independent audit: ladder_many_receiver/cloning_simple/FULL_PROOF_AUDIT.md.

BOUNDED-HOLEVO QUESTION: GLOBAL IMPLICATION REMAINS UNRESOLVED.

Target: can C(E)=sup_priors chi(pi,E) replace trace covering so that bounded C
and b_2->0 imply e_EB->0, dimension independently? No proof or counterexample
with small b_2 was obtained. Do not upgrade any scoped conclusion below.

Actual positive theorem on the entire PURE-ladder mechanism: for ANY set of
unknown directions S_L in arbitrary ambient dimension, the uniform ladder
restricted to S_L with C(E_L)<=C satisfies e_EB=O(((C+1)/L)^(1/4))->0.
Bounded pure-family capacity gives an entropy-maximizing center whose spectral
subspace of rank<=exp(C_j/a) captures at least1-a of every promised vector.
Shifting down r levels supplies 2^r actual copies of each output block; an
ordinary covariant pure-state estimator reconstructs it. Preserve all output
flags exactly. Drop the top eta fraction; choose eta and accuracy epsilon of
the same order and r=O(C/(eta epsilon^2)+log1/epsilon). Condition r<eta L
gives the fourth-root rate. No extra copies, dimension bound or covering
assumption; centers/POVMs must be acquired, no efficient algorithm asserted.
Full proof: information_radius/counterexample/ladder_capacity/
LADDER_CAPACITY_OBSTRUCTIONS.md section4. Weighted restricted pure ladders
with small flag-shift variation have a corresponding vanishing-error bound.
Simple arbitrary fixed-state dilution or full-Haar sector dilution cannot
keep capacity bounded while retaining the ladder obstruction either.

Exact finite faithful Pauli benchmark blocks the naive proof route:
rho_(P,s)=(I+s aP)/d for every nonidentity Hermitian q-qubit Pauli, s=+/-1,
d=2^q, 0<a<1. Its Holevo capacity equals
c(a)=[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2 <=ln2;
e_EB=a d/[2(d+1)]->a/2;
uniform-prior accessible information is <=a^2/(d+1)->0.
These identities follow by Pauli Parseval, D<=classical chi-square, and the
measure/prepare trace contraction sum. Anticommuting Pauli pairs have a fixed
positive fidelity-affinity gap, so b_2 stays bounded away from zero. THIS IS
NOT A COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE GLOBAL TARGET. It proves that bounded capacity
cannot by itself convert measured posterior residual into trace residual.
Existing finite-label extraction improves only to
e<=inf_ell[ell b_2+sqrt(m C(E)/(2*2^ell))]; the m factor remains.

SCOPED ROBUST BOUNDARY FOR N53 THERMAL EXPERIMENTS.

For an arbitrary number m of known product thermal modes with occupations
N_i>0 and known center Theta, let rho_alpha=D(alpha)Theta D(alpha)^*.
Heterodyne each mode and prepare coherent amplitude lambda_i y_i,
lambda_i=sqrt(N_i/(N_i+1)). The output is EXACT rho_(lambda alpha), with the
same thermal covariance as input and Theta fixed exactly. Hence if
sup_alpha D(rho_alpha||Theta)=sup sum_i|alpha_i|^2 ln(1+1/N_i)<=C,

 e_EB<=sqrt(C/2) max_i[1-sqrt(N_i/(N_i+1))].

For bounded C and min_i N_i->infinity this tends to zero with no mode-count
penalty, even when sum_i(N_i+1)^(-2)->infinity and the unrestricted N53 orbit
has error->1. Example equal N, m=N^3: constrained radius error O(sqrtC/N),
unrestricted benchmark error->1. This is a direct standard Gaussian-channel
consequence; no iid/LAN growing-dimension transfer or new optimality asserted.
The radius about a supplied center is stronger than bounded Holevo capacity.
Continuous classical outcomes must be quantized separately to count bits.
Proof and primary sources: THERMAL_RADIUS_PROOF.md; Holevo Gaussian EB
criterion https://arxiv.org/pdf/0802.0235 Theorem2/Section6; thermal heterodyne
law https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.1843 SectionIV. Novelty not claimed.
