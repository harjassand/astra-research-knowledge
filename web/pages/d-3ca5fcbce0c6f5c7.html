# c01_l10 final research note

## Outcome

The outward stochastic-localization route is mathematically relevant but does not transfer directly to parity volumes. Two exact barriers were derived: on the cyclic-shift two-mode family, the transport-stability and semi-log-concavity constants required by the published localization sampler grow with n; and a natural constant-term randomized-determinant estimator for the pair-volume Laplace transform has exponential relative variance on a direct sum of 2-by-2 swap blocks.

These are precise failures of named transfers, not lower bounds against all algorithms. A pair-aware localization theorem, an efficient Laplace oracle, importance sampling, or a different estimator remain open. No polynomial sampler or field-breaking theorem is claimed.

## Exact randomized-determinant attack

For a frame V in C^(n x 2n), put L=V*V and pair columns (e_i,f_i). The pair-volume partition function with activities y_i>0 is

  Z_k(y) = sum_{|S|=k} det L[P(S)] product_{i in S} y_i.

Define D(theta,y) with diagonal entries sqrt(y_i) exp(+i theta_i), sqrt(y_i) exp(-i theta_i) on the paired columns, and

  X(theta,y) = [z^(2k)] det(I_n + z V D(theta,y) V*).

Expanding by Cauchy-Binet and integrating independent uniform theta_i annihilates every column set with unequal counts in a site's two positions. Since a pair has only two columns, the surviving terms select both or neither, so E X=Z_k(y). Thus randomized determinant evaluation gives an unbiased constant-term estimator of the needed partition function and tilted Laplace numerator.

For n=2m, take F=direct_sum_m [[0,1],[1,0]], V=[I,F^T], and k=m. A positive-volume support chooses exactly one site per 2-site block; every such set has weight one, so Z_m(1)=2^m and the target law is a product of m fair bits. For one block the top determinant coefficient is Y=2+u+u^(-1), with u uniform on the unit circle. Hence E Y=2 and E|Y|^2=6. Independence across blocks gives E X=2^m, E|X|^2=6^m, and relative variance (3/2)^m-1. The relative mean-square error after N phase samples is exactly ((3/2)^m-1)/N. Balanced local activities maximize the one-block relative second moment, so the same exponential factor survives at the balanced tilt.

This is an idealized sample-count result: determinant evaluations and continuous phases are granted at no precision or random-bit cost. It rejects this direct phase-averaging estimator; it does not rule out other randomized determinant methods. Exact diagnostic: [script](revisions/stochastic_localization_random_phase_attack.py) and [output](revisions/stochastic_localization_random_phase_attack.output.txt).

## Exact failure of generic stochastic-localization hypotheses

On the cyclic-shift family from INITIAL, the target law is uniform on two complementary alternating site sets, represented by x and -x in {+-1}^n. Their Hamming distance is n. Tilting by t e_i moves total mass (1/2)tanh(t) between these two points, so

  W1(mu,tau_(t e_i)mu) / ||t e_i||_1 = (n/2) tanh(t)/t -> n/2.

Thus the O(1) W1-ell1 transport-stability requirement of Theorem 1 of Anari et al. fails. For the semi-log-concavity condition, tilt by s x/n: the tilted mean is tanh(s)x, giving

  ||mean(tau_(s x/n)mu)-mean(mu)||_2 / ||s x/n||_2 = n tanh(s)/s -> n.

So the O(1) semi-log-concavity requirement of their Theorem 3 fails as well. The theorem's generic O(n) stability option gives quasi-polynomial processor count only with an exact Laplace-transform oracle. For the general parity-volume target that oracle is evaluation of the weighted pair-principal-minor polynomial, the computation this route was meant to avoid. The cyclic witness has a trivial two-term oracle, but does not solve the arbitrary-frame case.

The same two-point geometry forbids an irreducible support-preserving Markov chain with jumps changing fewer than n coordinates: stationarity bars transitions to zero-mass states and the two positive-mass states are distance n apart. This excludes the constant-site local-chain consequence on this family.

## Exact stability-polynomial refinement

For p_q(z)=product_even z_i + product_odd z_i, with q sites in each parity class, the exact fractional-log-concavity threshold is alpha=1/q. For A=product_even z_i^alpha, B=product_odd z_i^alpha, and r=A/(A+B),

  Hess log(A+B) = -D + alpha^2 r(1-r) v v^T,

where D_ii=alpha r/z_i^2 on even sites, D_ii=alpha(1-r)/z_i^2 on odd sites, and v_i=+1/z_i or -1/z_i by parity. Then v^T D^(-1)v=q/(alpha r(1-r)), so the rank-one PSD criterion gives negative semidefiniteness exactly when alpha q<=1. This strengthens the INITIAL necessary Hessian bound to an exact global threshold; it still decays with q and does not meet constant-parameter transfer hypotheses.

## Literature boundary and unresolved gate

Anari, Huang, Liu, Vuong, Xu, and Yu, “Parallel Discrete Sampling via Continuous Walks,” STOC 2023, DOI 10.1145/3564246.3585207, Theorem 1 assumes O(1) W1-ell1 transport stability plus an approximate Laplace-transform oracle; Theorem 3 assumes O(1) semi-log-concavity plus an exact Laplace-transform oracle. Their Corollary 2 covers DPPs and partition-constrained DPPs with O(1) parts. Here the event is one local 0-or-2 occupancy constraint per site, not an exact count in a constant number of partition classes. Treating n site constraints as n parts is outside that corollary. Source metadata and theorem locations: [SOURCES.json](revisions/SOURCES.json).

The next decisive gate is either (i) a relative-accurate, polynomial-cost oracle for Z_k(y) on arbitrary rational F, or (ii) a pair-aware stability argument that bypasses the cyclic two-mode sensitivity witness. Neither is established. Existing reports from earlier cycles document exponential-variance behavior of principal-deletion and random-sign estimators; this work's new phase calculation is a separate exact estimator analysis, not a general variance theorem.
