# Primary prior boundary for family116 and output-only reconstruction

Read/retrieved 7October2026. This note distinguishes exact oracle interfaces. Historical priority of our length-marker composition remains UNKNOWN; the reconstruction principle itself is established.

## Closest matched reconstruction controls

**Beimel–Bergadano–Bshouty–Kushilevitz–Varricchio, Learning functions represented as multiplicity automata.** Author-hosted manuscript `sources/metric_prior/cycle19/prior/BBBKv2000.pdf`; author publication index verifies JACM47(3):506–530,2000, with FOCS1996 predecessor. The snapshot is a31-page manuscript, rather than the25-page journal pagination. The text layer is unusable font encoding; rendered PDFpp6–9 and12 were read, with OCR only for navigation. Section2.1/Theorem2.4 on PDFp7 identifies the minimum nonzero weighted-automaton state count with Hankel rank. Section2.2/PDFp9 supplies word-value membership AND equivalence queries returning a counterexample word. Theorem3.3/PDFp12 gives r equivalence queries, O((|Sigma|+log ell)r²) membership queries, and field-operation time O(|Sigma|rM(r)+ell r³), where ell is the longest counterexample and M(r) is matrix-multiplication cost. This is already a cyclic-series learner. It does not compile observations into an output-only matrix-evaluation oracle.

**Klivans–Shpilka, Learning Restricted Models of Arithmetic Circuits.** Primary journal PDF `prior/KS2006.pdf`, publication date28September2006 printed PDFp1, DOI10.4086/toc.2006.v002a010. Section2.1/PDFp5 defines value-membership and counterexample-equivalence observations. Theorem2.4/PDFp6 imports multiplicity learning, and Theorems5.6/6.4 give the restricted branching-program/formula consequences. It supplies the established algebraic learning reduction used in the next comparator.

**Forbes–Shpilka, Quasipolynomial-time Identity Testing of Non-Commutative and Read-Once Oblivious Algebraic Branching Programs.** Pinned `prior/FS2013.pdf`, arXiv1209.2408v2. The verified arXiv version timestamp/footer is22September2013; the downloaded first-page title date says22June2021. Preserve both; do not silently treat its title date as the arXiv submission date. Theorem1.5/PDFp6 explicitly includes NC-ABP same-class reconstruction from membership/EQ, then implements these from output black-box access in randomized polynomial or deterministic quasipolynomial time. Corollary4.7/PDFp26 gives a hitting set of size at most (2(D+1)^4 n²r³)^(ceil(log2D)+1) on (D+1)-square matrices for width r, depth D, provided |F| >= (2(D+1)^5nr^4)^2. Thus output-only ABP reconstruction is prior. Our potentially useful difference is polynomial-time universal residual features, not output-only capability by itself.

**Arvind–Mukhopadhyay–Srinivasan, New results on Noncommutative and Commutative Polynomial Identity Testing.** `prior/AMS2008.pdf`, arXiv0801.0514v1,3January2008. Section3/Theorem3.5/PDFp12 reconstructs a homogeneous layered ABP in deterministic poly(d,s,n) time, but explicitly supplies matrix evaluations at ANY GATE, indexed by its layer/gate number. Corollary3.2/Theorem3.3/PDFp10 give word-coefficient extraction/sparse interpolation from matrix outputs. This is a useful control with a stronger hidden-structure observation interface, not the closest output-only control once Forbes–Shpilka is included.

## Closest identity-testing controls and recent window

**Arvind–Chatterjee–Mukhopadhyay, Black-Box Identity Testing of Noncommutative Rational Formulas in Deterministic Quasipolynomial Time.** `prior/ACM2025.pdf`, arXiv2309.15647v4,11July2025. Theorem2/Corollary3/PDFp5 give rational-formula hitting in (ns)^(O(theta^5 log s)) time/list size, dimension s^(O(theta)); the final balanced bound after Remark52/PDFpp36–37 is quasipolynomial. The primary SIAM DOI page independently verifies online publication1June2026, submitted4September2024, accepted13November2025. That journal date is inside the requested recent window, while the theorem predates it in preprint form. Formula size/inverse-domain semantics must be charged; this is not a polynomial bound for arbitrary shared circuits.

**Lakhani–Mukhopadhyay, Hitting point for sparse noncommutative polynomials.** `prior/LM2026.pdf`, ECCC TR26-120 revision2 timestamp20July2026 verified from ECCC. Theorem2.1/PDFp2 and Section2/PDFpp2–4 construct ONE integer s-square tuple, bit height O(s log(n+1)), for sparsity at most s and unbounded degree. The affine base-(n+1) word encoding and Vandermonde proof are direct. A state-small ABP can have exponentially many words, so sparsity is a different parameter. Its matrices are not jointly nilpotent and do not promise a proper-realization inverse is always defined. This is a substantive same-window singleton control, not a direct bounded-state cyclic learner.

**Arvind–Joglekar, Rational Identity Testing for Noncommutative Circuits is in Polynomial Space.** `prior/AJ2026.pdf`, arXiv2609.37319v2: first submitted29September2026, revised2October2026; manuscript title date27September2026. Theorem1.1/Theorem6.2, PDFpp3/13–14, concern WHITE-BOX correctness of all inverse gates plus zero identity for unrestricted rational circuits. Theorem2.5/PDFpp4–6 evaluates uniform bounded-fanin circuits on succinct bit-oracle inputs in space O(depth+log size+index width+uniformity space)+oracle space, with exponential-depth time. Lemma4.9 supplies a succinct exponentially sized pencil; the final composition uses a separately assumed recent uniformNC noncommutative-rank algorithm. This does not make exponential formula unrolling polynomial time or provide hidden-model reconstruction. The theorem's main rank dependency has not been independently audited here.

## Old cross-field mechanism already load-bearing in family116

Claire Moura's `prior/Moura2003.pdf`, arXivmath/0312323v1,17December2003, Section2/Theorem1/PDFpp3–4 recalls a general meromorphic-linear-system multiplicity bound and gives the Krylov-minor/Wronskian proof. With n finite simple poles plus the simple pole at infinity, the bound becomes (n-1)binom(m,2)+m-1 for state dimension m. The family116 characteristic-zero manuscript reproduces this mechanism with field transfer. Our length marker uses that supplied order bound with coefficient field F(lambda); it does not claim a new multiplicity estimate.
