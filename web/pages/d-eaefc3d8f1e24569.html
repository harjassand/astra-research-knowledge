Exact interface retained
The input is one unknown-direction state tau_(h,v) on the supplied
irreducible carrier H_N=Sym^N(C^m), not N iid copies of a fundamental
state. The risk is worst-case whole-density half-trace distance. A legal
branch has quantum dimension at most Q; unlimited classical labels are
allowed in the converse, and explicit finite label counts are separately
charged in the upper. No direction is supplied to the code. The proof does
not cover a purification/reference, a reducible multi-carrier input, or an
iid-sector transport theorem.

1. Score normalization and the exact converse
Write d=dim H_N, s=m−1, r=r_N(h), and use the stated normalizations
Tr_fund(t_a t_b)=delta_ab/2, B_v=I/m+(2/N)sum_a z_a(v)T_a. The two
projective moments are

  integral z_a z_b dv = delta_ab/[2m(m+1)],
  integral z_a tau_(h,v) dv = r T_a/[d N(N+m)].

The second follows by adjoint multiplicity one and pairing with T_b;
the trace index in (2.2) cancels the factor 2m(m+1) in the first moment.
The ideal score is

  integral Tr(B_v tau_(h,v))dv = mu_N/N
       = 1/m + s r/(mN).

For any CPTP channel with Kraus A, the output score is

  integral Tr(B_v Phi(tau_(h,v)))dv
    = 1/m + 2r/[d N^2(N+m)]
                  sum_(A,a) Tr(A^dagger T_a A T_a).

The score expression has the opposite-looking A/A-dagger order from
(2.4), but they are equal termwise for Hermitian T_a. Diagonalizing T_a
with eigenvalues lambda_i gives both traces as
sum_(i,j) lambda_i lambda_j |A_ij|^2 (swap i,j for the second form).
Thus no normality or positivity of A is needed. Combining the scalar
Casimir identity and sum_A ||A||_HS^2=d yields exactly

  ideal score − channel score
    = r/[N^2(N+m)] sum_A w_A Ebar(A),
  w_A=||A||_HS^2/d,   sum_A w_A=1.

The coefficient has no missing d or factor 2. Since B_v is an effect,
its score difference at each v is bounded in absolute value by the
half-trace distance. The averaged deficit above is nonnegative, so it is
a valid lower bound on the worst-case risk. Each composite Kraus operator
of an encoder/decoder branch has rank at most q_y<=Q. This argument is
unchanged for continuous/infinite classical labels: either integrate the
nonnegative weighted identity directly or use finite-dimensional
Caratheodory on the rank-Q Choi cone. The converse does not charge or
assume a finite alphabet.

The finite-N polarization estimate is also consistent. The h=0 uniform
composition law has Var_0(n_1/N)=s(N+m)/[m^2(m+1)N]. On [0,1], the tilted
law satisfies p_h>=e^(−h)p_0, hence its two-draw variance is at least
e^(−2h) times the untilted variance. Integrating d E_h x/dh=Var_h x
gives r_N(h)/N >= h0 e^(−2h1)/[m(m+1)] for h in [h0,h1]. Multiplication
by 1/(N+m) in the exact score lower then gives the stated factor
eta/(m+1) times Lambda/N^2. The h0>0 condition is essential; at h=0
all inputs are I/d and Q=1 recovers exactly.

2. Positivity replacement for the rank-energy optimizer
For A=U Sigma V^dagger, let L=|A^dagger|=U Sigma U^dagger and
R=|A|=V Sigma V^dagger. Their rank and HS norm equal those of A. For
U^a_ij=<u_i,T_a u_j> and V^a_ij=<v_i,T_a v_j>, the scalar Casimir
identity gives the exact difference

  E(A) − [E(L)+E(R)]/2
    = sum_(a,i,j) sigma_i sigma_j |U^a_ij−V^a_ij|^2 >=0.

The cross term is the real part of U_ij conjugate(V_ij); Hermiticity of
T_a supplies the conjugate matrix entries. Thus at least one of L,R is a
positive semidefinite rank-at-most-Q matrix with energy no larger than
E(A). Since positive matrices are a subset of all matrices, this proves
both directions of the equality in (5.2), and a positive minimizer exists
by compactness. I found no unsupported claim that each modulus separately
decreases energy.

3. Noncommuting whole-state trace upper
Let D_F(X)=integral [F_g,[F_g,X]]dg. Haar covariance and the single
adjoint copy in End(H_N) imply

  D_F(B_v)=E(F)/[d C_N] (B_v−I/m),
  integral ||[F_g,B_v]||_HS^2 dg
       =Tr(B_v D_F(B_v))=2E(F)/[m(m+1)N^2].

These coefficients follow by Hilbert–Schmidt pairing with the generators;
the sum of generator norms is d C_N. With delta_g=[F_g,·], the exact
ordered-product expansion of delta_g^2(B^k) has k same-factor terms and
k(k−1) ordered cross terms. After Haar averaging, the same-factor terms
combine because D_F(B) is proportional to B−I/m and hence commutes with B;
their trace norm is at most k E(F)/C_N. Each cross term has the form
B^a C_g B^b C_g B^c with C_g=[F_g,B]. No commutation of C_g with B is
assumed: Schatten Holder bounds its trace norm by ||C_g||_2^2 because
each B power is a contraction. Averaging and using the displayed
commutator variance gives exactly (7.1), including the factor 2 in
2k(k−1)/[m(m+1)N^2].

The double-commutator identity has sign
Phi_F(X)−X=−d D_F(X)/(2 S_F). Since B>=0 and h>=0,
Z_N(h)=Tr exp(hB)>=d. Termwise trace-norm summation of the Gibbs series,
followed by the half-trace factor 1/2, gives

  e(Phi_F;h) <= Ebar(F)e^h/4
                    [h/C_N+2h^2/(m(m+1)N^2)].

Using C_N=N s(N+m)/(2m) yields K_(m,h1) Ebar(F)/N^2 with the K in
(7.3). This is a whole-state trace-norm argument; it is not an inference
from the first moment or a commuting-multipole assumption. I found no
noncommutative product-rule gap in this step.

4. Exact finite projective cubature and charged registers
For the explicit filters, F_v=f(n_v) with interpolation polynomial
degree at most N on the eigenvalues 0,...,N. Since n_v has entries
bilinear in v and conjugate(v), entries of F_v are projective polynomials
of bidegree at most (N,N); entries of X->F_v X F_v have bidegree at most
(2N,2N). Homogenization by powers of ||v||^2 makes lower-degree terms
exactly homogeneous on projective space.

In the stated stick coordinates, a monomial v^alpha conjugate(v)^beta
has each relative-phase frequency in [−2N,2N]. The 2N+1-point phase
grid therefore kills every nonzero frequency. Surviving terms have
alpha=beta and reduce to p^alpha with |alpha|=2N. In stick variable u_j,
the degree is sum_(i>=j) alpha_i<=2N, including the residual p_m term.
The N+1-node Gauss–Jacobi rule is exact through degree 2N+1 for each
weight (m−j)(1−u_j)^(m−j−1). Its nodes are interior and weights positive.
The product rule consequently integrates every matrix entry of the
superoperator exactly, so it reproduces the Haar channel and the
trace-preserving identity exactly; this is not approximate Haar sampling.

The node count is [(N+1)(2N+1)]^s. A branch encoder and decoder factor
the weighted Kraus sqrt(d p_y/S_F) F_y through supp(F), so branch
dimension is rank(F)<=Q; labels require at most ceil(log2 C_exp) bits
and total direct-sum dimension is at most Q C_exp. The separate global
Caratheodory upper gives at most d^4+1 labels for an arbitrary minimizer,
but no efficient node-selection/compiler claim. Algebraic descriptions,
gate counts, and precision-certified preparation costs are not proved.

5. Audit boundary and finding
Within the requested score normalization, positivity replacement,
noncommuting trace upper, and finite quadrature, I found no algebraic gap.
The passage from the displayed rank-energy bounds (9.3), (9.5), (9.6) to
the fixed-fraction order (9.7)–(9.8) is algebraically correct; the sharp
rank-energy profile itself is a separate spectral-geometry dependency and
is not re-proved in this audit. Nor does this audit establish novelty,
external proof review, efficient acquisition of a Lambda minimizer, an
optimized total hybrid dimension, or gate/bit-precision cost.

The candidate theorem is strictly for a supplied single Sym^N(C^m)
carrier and worst-case whole-density reconstruction with v blind. The
unrestricted classical-label converse is valid; explicit upper label
counts are charged. Claims about iid input models, multiple irreducible
carriers, growing h, h0->0, reference preservation, or other coadjoint
orbits require new arguments. The finite numerical checks in the source
are not used here as proof.
