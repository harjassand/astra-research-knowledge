## Recent starting point and actual obligations

quantum phases and self-correcting quantum memory*, PRX Quantum,

APS HTML. It is in the starting window. The recoverable substantive text
read here is arXiv2509.15204v2, dated24 December2025. The journal page is

The relevant prior is Theorem2, SectionIV, source main.tex584–667, with
the cleaning/local-reversibility proof in AppendicesH–I1066–1155 and the
approximate-stationarity argument in AppendixJ1156–1187. Theorem3, source
668–685, combines it with Gibbs circuit connectivity under stable clustering.
The code criterion is diameter error correction, not just weight distance.
Its generator is a sum of individually bounded quasilocal terms that EACH
fix the Gibbs state. This frustration-free condition is stronger than
global stationarity. A fully secular Davies generator for a generic
noncommuting Hamiltonian is not automatically in this physical quasilocal
interface. The source itself uses Gibbs-sampler constructions to give
examples, not an automatic identification with every physical bath.

An independently explicit version of the memory-step argument is useful.
Assume two encoded states C(omega),C(Pi) are delta_LI locally
indistinguishable outside any diameter-ell/2 region, C(Pi) is within
epsilon_C of rho, and term L_X has norm at most J_X, fixes rho, and
has a same-region approximation L_X^ell/2 with norm error a_X exp(-alpha ell/2).
Choose an exterior channel D_X with
||D_X C(Pi)-C(omega)||_1<=delta_LI. Commutation of D_X with the local
approximation and exact L_X rho=0 give

    ||L_X C(omega)||_1
      <=J_X(delta_LI+epsilon_C)+2a_X exp(-alpha ell/2).

For a CPTP inverse circuit with error epsilon_inv, the decoded error is
at most epsilon_inv plus t times the sum of these bounds. Thus the paid
intensity is sum_X J_X and sum_X a_X. Polynomially many unit-strength
terms give the paper's polynomial prefactor; arbitrary duplicate bath
channels cannot be hidden in an unqualified poly(L). This derivation does
not adopt the whole Gibbs circuit acquisition theorem or stable clustering
for our actual recursive family.

The paper's discussion and AppendixG main.tex781,974–1062 explicitly leave
a rigorous stable-clustering proof for the low-temperature4D toric code
desirable. Consequently its theorem is a stronger conditional comparator,
not a closed robustness theorem for the May2026 3D construction.

There is a repairable exponent sign error in the pinned arXiv version's
Theorem3 proof main.tex680–682, rendered as Eq33: a stretched exponential
times a polynomial is said to be bounded by exp(-L^(1/c2+epsilon)).
That inequality is false. The correct conclusion is exp(-L^a) for any
fixed0<a<1/c2 and sufficiently large L. This retains a positive
stretched-exponential exponent and does not invalidate the underlying
theorem. The journal version has not been checked for this typo.

## Chesi supplies the strongest matched thermal encoding criterion

Chesi, Loss, Bravyi and Terhal, arXiv0907.2807v2,30 September2009,
Theorem1/SectionVI source arXiv.02.tex775–848, its Davies specialization
898–1045, and its COMPLETE two-part proof1049–1177 were substantively read.
No fatal flaw was found for this exact interface. Its theorem permits an
arbitrary stationary Lindblad dissipator and a full-Hilbert-space logical
Pauli pair protected from the jumps on P, provided the pair AND P commute
with the full H. It gives

    error<=8t||L||_1 Tr rho_beta(I-P),
    Enc(tau)=tau tensor eta_aux,  Dec=Tr_aux.

This is thermal encoding. A ground-state input or efficient preparation
of eta_aux is not supplied. The abstract theorem needs stationarity,
not detailed balance. A Hermitian independent-bath Davies construction
with KMS rates supplies stationarity. Full-H logical/P commutation then
transfers raw protection to all Fourier components.

Proposition1 source988–1031 already bounds ||L||_1<=2K h_max by
time averaging, without counting frequencies. Its premise is the norm
of the K raw couplings, not the exponentially many resolved jumps.
The exact cross-pinching and cb/diamond formulation in our
SECULAR_PINCHING_AND_CONTINUITY.md is a reconstruction/extension of this
prior mechanism, not a new discovery. The same-H coupling-difference
bound and signed Lamb-shift extension retain this comparator.

## Earlier dressed-observable and secular-limit prerequisites

Alicki, M. Horodecki, P. Horodecki and R. Horodecki, arXiv0811.0033v1,
2 November2008, SectionsII andII.A–B/source top-magn6.tex292–511 were read
in substance. They define a full-space dressed Pauli frame, use a Gibbs
product ancillary state, and exploit spectral orthogonality to bound
Dirichlet decay from bare coupling commutators on commuting observables.
The detailed4D loop-filling/Peierls proof has not been reconstructed here.
Their actual dressed-observable application lies in the zero-frequency
sector. An unqualified statement that the FULL coherent-plus-dissipative
generator is positive, or a Jensen estimate for an observable not commuting
H, is false; H=Z,O=X with no dissipation is an exact obstruction.
This rejects that stronger transfer, not their4D memory application.

Trushechkin arXiv2103.12042v3,25 July2021, SectionsII–III/source
UniGKLS.tex95–224 and COMPLETE AppendixA441–688 were read. The rigorous
theorem separates H^(lambda)=H^(0)+lambda^2 deltaH with commuting bounded
deltaH and nearly degenerate Bohr clusters. Its CAR bath, bounded coupling,
integrable correlation and bounded van Hove time assumptions are explicit.
It produces a clustered rather than fully secular generator, with uniform
weak-coupling convergence on0<=lambda^2 t<=tau_1. This is the strongest
matched prior explanation of our fixed-epsilon Davies discontinuity.
It neither proves a thermal memory lifetime nor justifies uniform
microscopic accuracy for exponential storage times.
