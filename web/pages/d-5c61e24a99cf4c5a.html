RESULT
For the mass-action CTMC on N_0^2 with reactions
  0 -> B              rate lambda,
  B -> A              rate lambda*b,
  A+2B -> 0           rate kappa*a*b*(b-1),
where lambda,kappa>0 are fixed, the entire state space is irreducible and the process is nonexplosive. It is transient if kappa<2lambda and null recurrent if kappa>=2lambda. There is no invariant probability distribution for any kappa.
The rate convention is the falling factorial b(b-1), without a 1/2. With binomial propensity kappa*a*binom(b,2), the threshold is kappa=4lambda.
This closes the explicitly unclassified mu=lambda line of MR1 in state/2026-10-08-stochastic-universality/RESULT_AND_RESTART.md. It is a concrete phase diagram for a single irreducible component, not a claim about uniform tightness over distinct components. The network is not weakly reversible, so it does not resolve or refute the weakly reversible recurrence conjecture.

The deterministic system a'=lambda*b-kappa*a*b^2, b'=lambda-lambda*b-2kappa*a*b^2 has the unique globally attracting positive equilibrium (3lambda/kappa,1/3). Its strong endotacticity and permanence are already covered by established results. The new mathematical deduction is the critical stochastic classification, especially the exact second threshold inside the zero-speed line. Novelty remains unverified.

1. IRREDUCIBILITY AND NONEXPLOSION
Every (a,b) reaches (a+b,0) by conversions. From (a,0) with a>=1, two immigrations followed by annihilation reach (a-1,0). Hence every state reaches (0,0). From (0,0), repeated immigration-conversion pairs create any desired a; further immigrations create any b. Every finite listed path has positive probability, proving irreducibility.
Let N_0,N_1,N_2 count immigration, conversion and annihilation. Then
  B_t=b_0+N_0(t)-N_1(t)-2N_2(t)>=0,
so N_1+2N_2<=b_0+N_0. Since N_0 is a constant-rate Poisson process, infinitely many reaction events cannot occur in finite time. This is physical-time nonexplosion, not merely an embedded-chain result.

2. A UNIFORM EXCURSION BOUND
Observe the reaction-event chain during an excursion with B>0 and kill it when B=0. Let J be the number of further events until killing. At (a,b), total rate is
  D(a,b)=lambda(1+b)+kappa*a*b*(b-1).
Write r=sqrt(2), q=2sqrt(2)/3<1 and V(b)=r^b.
At b=1, conversion kills; the remaining birth contributes killed ratio r/2<=q.
At b>=2, birth probability p<=1/(b+1)<=1/3. Every other event decreases B by at least one, or kills it. Consequently
  E[V(B_next)1_{B_next>0}|a,b]/V(b)
  <= p*r+(1-p)/r <= r/3+2/(3r)=q.
Iteration gives
  P_{a,b}(J>j)<=r^(b-1)q^j,   j>=0.
This is uniform in a and kappa. In particular, from b=1 (and also any fixed b<=4) J has uniformly bounded exponential moments. During the excursion |Delta A|<=J. All displacement moments therefore exist uniformly in the initial A.
The excursion's physical duration also has a uniform exponential moment: every event rate is at least lambda, and conditional on the event skeleton each hold has exponential moment at most lambda/(lambda-theta). Choose theta>0 small enough that this factor is less than 1/q, and sum over J. A cycle also includes its initial B=0 hold Exp(lambda).

3. THE RETURN CHAIN AND ITS EXACT FIRST TWO ASYMPTOTIC MOMENTS
Let Z_j be A at successive returns to B=0 after leaving it. All cycles finish almost surely by Section 2. The strong Markov property makes Z an irreducible Markov chain on N_0. Its one-step displacement Delta=Z_{j+1}-Z_j has uniform exponential moments.
From (n,0), immigration takes the process to (n,1). The next event is conversion or immigration with probability 1/2 each. Conversion finishes the cycle with Delta=+1. Immigration reaches (n,2), where
  D_n=2kappa*n+3lambda.
Call an event at b>=2 exceptional if it is not annihilation. At (n,2), annihilation, conversion and immigration have probabilities 2kappa*n/D_n, 2lambda/D_n and lambda/D_n.

If all subsequent high-B events annihilate, the first conversion exception gives (n+1,1), whose two equally likely continuations finish at n+2 or n. The first immigration exception gives (n,3), then annihilation gives (n-1,1), whose two equally likely continuations finish at n or n-2.
The reference one-exception distribution is therefore
  Delta +1: 1/2,
  Delta -1: kappa*n/D_n,
  Delta +2: lambda/(2D_n),
  Delta  0: 3lambda/(4D_n),
  Delta -2: lambda/(4D_n).
The probabilities sum exactly to one. Its first moment is 2lambda/D_n and second moment is 1+3lambda/(2D_n).

UNIFORM REMAINDER JUSTIFICATION (load-bearing step): Before the second exceptional event, the path has at most five events from (n,1), A=n+O(1), and B<=3. Immediately after the second exception B<=4 and |A-n|<=3. For n>=6, every high-B event on such a prefix has exceptional probability at most C/n, where C depends only on lambda/kappa. There are at most finitely many eligible prefixes. Thus probability of at least two exceptional events is O(n^-2).
Condition on the state immediately after the second exception. The killed-kernel bound in Section 2 supplies uniformly bounded moments of the remaining event count, since B<=4. Therefore E[|Delta|^m; at least two exceptions]=O(n^-2) for every fixed m, not merely o(1).
For paths with exactly one exception, the reference distribution replaces finitely many subsequent annihilation probabilities by 1. Each replacement differs by O(n^-1), multiplied by the O(n^-1) probability of the first exception. The displacement is bounded on these paths, so the first- and second-moment replacement errors are O(n^-2). Zero-exception paths are exact.
It follows that
  m_1(n)=E[Delta|Z_j=n]=2lambda/D_n+O(n^-2)
        =lambda/(kappa*n)+O(n^-2),
  m_2(n)=E[Delta^2|Z_j=n]=1+3lambda/(2D_n)+O(n^-2)
        =1+O(n^-1).
The constants are uniform in n for the fixed positive rate pair. No large-volume or diffusion approximation enters these statements.

4. LAMPERTI CLASSIFICATION, INCLUDING EQUALITY
Apply Lo and Wade, arXiv:1512.04242v2, Theorem 2.2, with its auxiliary line set a singleton. Assumption (A) holds by irreducibility/local finiteness/unboundedness. Every (B_p) holds by Section 2. The line transition matrix is exactly (1), so both Q-infinity conditions are automatic. We have D_L with c=lambda/kappa and s^2=1. Moreover D_L^+ holds for, for example, delta_1=1/2 because the errors are O(n^-2) in m_1 and O(n^-1) in m_2.
The theorem gives transient Z when 2lambda/kappa>1 and null recurrent Z when 0<2lambda/kappa<=1. The equality case uses the strengthened polynomial errors; an informal leading-order comparison alone would not justify it.

5. PHYSICAL-TIME RECURRENCE AND NULLITY
Visits of the original CTMC to (0,0) occur exactly at the return-chain visits Z_j=0, after the initial departure. Recurrence/transience of this state transfers, and irreducibility makes it the class property of the whole CTMC.
In the recurrent regime, let M be the number of return cycles before the next Z=0. Null recurrence of Z gives E[M]=infinity. Each cycle incurs a B=0 hold H_j of mean1/lambda; H_j is independent of the past and its subsequent event skeleton because the sole reaction at B=0 is constant-rate immigration. The event {M>j} is measurable before that hold. Therefore, by Tonelli,
  E[physical return time]>=E[sum_{j=0}^{M-1}H_j]
    =lambda^-1 sum_{j>=0}P(M>j)=infinity.
Hence recurrence is null recurrence in physical time. No catalytic clock has been silently applied. Independent Exp(lambda) holds also prevent accumulation of return cycles in finite time.

6. INDEPENDENT EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO ANY STATIONARY PROBABILITY
Set P=b mod2 and W(a,b)=a-floor(b/2). Annihilation preserves W exactly. Direct calculation gives
  G W=lambda(b-P)[3/2+(-1)^P/2]>=0,
strictly positive whenever b>=2.
Suppose an invariant probability pi existed. Couple B with an immigration-death process D having immigration lambda and per-particle death lambda, starting at D_0=B_0. Extra annihilation only removes B, so B_t<=D_t. With B_0 distributed according to pi's B-marginal, D_t converges in law to Poisson(1), because B_0 is finite almost surely. Stationarity therefore implies pi_B is stochastically dominated by Poisson(1); in particular E_pi B<infinity.
Let W_R=max(-R,min(W,R)). It is bounded and1-Lipschitz. Annihilation is invisible to W_R, and |G W_R|<=lambda+2lambda*b. Stationarity and integrability give E_pi G W_R=0. Dominated convergence R->infinity yields E_pi G W=0. Irreducibility gives pi(b>=2)>0, contradicting the strict positivity of G W there.
This argument does not assume a finite A moment and does not infer stationarity from formal Q-balance alone.

7. THE DETERMINISTIC CONNECTION AND THE RELEASE149 BOUNDARY
Source slopes (0,0),(0,1),(1,2) have inward cones: at 0, maximality forces v<=0; at B, maximality forces v>=0,u+v<=0 and (1,-1) projection u-v<=0; at A+2B, maximality forces u+v>=0,u+2v>=0 and annihilation projection -u-2v<=0. On each tied boundary ray one maximal source has a strict inward reaction. Thus the network is strongly endotactic with rank2.
Established strong-endotactic permanence (Gopalkrishnan--Miller--Shiu, restated in Anderson et al. 1808.05328v2 Theorem5.5) supplies a compact interior absorber. The ODE has unique positive equilibrium (3lambda/kappa,1/3); its divergence is -kappa*b^2-lambda-4kappa*a*b<0. The equilibrium has negative trace and positive determinant. Planar permanence and Bendixson exclusion give global convergence.
