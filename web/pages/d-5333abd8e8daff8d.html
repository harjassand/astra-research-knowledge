# Cycle 3 source reading ledger

Retrieval date: 2026-10-06. Ten selected recent primary sources, with older prerequisites. Read-depth is **excerpt**: the specified technical sections and decisive assumptions were read closely, but neither every proof nor every experiment was audited. Exact arXiv submission histories are in `sources/r04_statphys/cycle3/metadata.json`; recoverable HTML versions are saved. This ledger separates source claims from our derivation in RESULT.md. Superseded versions remain preserved, not silently replaced.

1. **Salehkaleybar, One Intervention per Component is Enough**, [2609.19955v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19955v1), submitted 17 September 2026. Read problem setup, Theorems 3.1/3.3/3.6, Remark 3.2, Appendices A.1/A.2/C.2 and the cyclic example. Stationary OU means/covariances, unknown diagonal diffusion, and ideal row knockouts are supplied. The generic conclusion depends on explicit rank assumptions; Remark 3.2 does not prove their genericity. The symmetric-star hidden rotation in RESULT is exceptional and allows additional edges, so it does not refute the author's generic statement or a supplied complete support. It exposes a uniform intervention and dissipation-acquisition obstruction.

2. **Boussard, Debouchage, Saulus, Identifiability Guarantees for Drivers and Dynamics of Delayed Physical Systems**, [2609.37944v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37944v3), first 29 September, selected version 1 October 2026. Read Assumptions 3, Theorems 1/2/4 and architecture/tail conditions; v1 also preserved. Known delays, candidate support containing the truth, and population-risk comparison are substantial inputs. The finite support result retains those structural conditions. Its graph/risk identifiability should not be read as acquiring an unrestricted physical model or all delay variables from finite snapshots. No counterexample to the stated theorem is claimed.

3. **Gu, Zhang, Miles, Identifiability Limits of Physics-Informed Inference for Spatial Stochastic Dynamics from Static Snapshots**, [2607.01749v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01749v1), 2 July 2026. Read Section 2 observation/PDE interface, Theorem 3.1, Theorem 4.5 and Proposition 6.4. Static profiles can absorb changes in diffusion into source terms; a point-source regularity kink and suitably independent profiles can restore identification under specified conventions. Dimensionality, source regularity, boundary conditions and stochastic convention are load bearing. This motivated changing the physical experiment rather than hoping an objective resolves an exact observational equivalence.

4. **Ceccarelli, Browning, Baker, Structural identifiability of partially-observed stochastic processes**, [2605.13504v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13504v1), 13 May 2026. Read trajectory versus density interfaces in Sections 3/4 and parameter recovery arguments based on elimination/Taylor coefficients. Individual paths and aggregate densities supply different information. Structural results use ideal observations and do not themselves give finite precision acquisition costs. A revised v2 dated 16 July 2026 exists and was not read; conclusions here are restricted to the preserved v1, rather than asserted current across versions.

5. **Simpson, Plank, When do trajectories matter?**, [2604.15598v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15598v1), 17 April 2026. Read Sections 2.3/3, tagged trajectories, bulk mean-field PDE, endpoint likelihood and the unknown population-size count term. The density approximation and tagged observations have distinct interfaces. This is a useful retaining baseline, not an arbitrary stochastic-process identifiability theorem. v2 dated 14 May exists but was not read; only the preserved v1 supports this note.

6. **Zweig, Lin, Azizi, Knowles, Towards Identifiability of Interventional Stochastic Differential Equations**, [2505.15987v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15987v5), first 21 May 2025, selected revision 10 July 2026. Read Section 2, Section 4.1, Proposition 4.1/Theorem 4.3 and their proof structure. Shift interventions identify structured low-rank drift under supplied decay/activation assumptions. The known diagonal decay is not known diffusion. The adversarial intervention-span construction gives a genuine information obstruction. Older v3, dated 29 September 2025, was initially read and is preserved; its theorem numbering/stronger old statements are not used as latest claims.

7. **Aguilar, Muñoz, Azaele, The Limits of Inference in Complex Systems**, [2509.24977v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24977v3), first 29 September 2025, revision 24 July 2026; PRX publication 23 July 2026, DOI 10.1103/tmkr-9kl2. Read Sections III–VI and Appendix J. Path likelihood, bridge inference and sampling frequency affect distinguishability. The data-collapse discussion is not a universal identifiability phase transition. Our rotation cost calculation is a separate Gaussian calculation with calibrated control and independent stationary ensembles.

8. **Faul, Hoessly, Xia, Identifiability of SDEs for reaction networks**, [published paper](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-journal-of-applied-mathematics/article/identifiability-of-sdes-for-reaction-networks/7D2B84958974B0400D0DAC737FF823F2), online 23 April 2026, DOI 10.1017/S0956792526100382. Read Example 1.1, Theorem 3.3 and proof Section 7.1. Reaction coefficients enter drift/covariance through vectors (u,uuᵀ); linear dependence can make distinct microscopic reaction rates induce the same diffusion law. Acquiring an OU generator therefore does not acquire microscopic reactions. Publisher HTML was saved; arXiv 2505.07638v2 HTML failed through web retrieval and was not used for full reading.

9. **Das, Manikandan, Localising entropy production along non-equilibrium trajectories**, [published paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42005-026-02667-8), 8 May 2026, DOI 10.1038/s42005-026-02667-8. Read Methods Equations 1–8 and trajectory-data construction. The diffusion convention is D=BBᵀ/2, hence our noise power D0=2D. Irreversible current and the quadratic entropy-production functional supply the physical target. Their acquisition uses trajectories; this does not make entropy production observable from a stationary density alone. The reported long synthetic trajectory is not a universal sample guarantee.

10. **Wang, Wang, Li, Gong, Zhang, Disentangling Continuous-Time Latent Dynamics**, [2606.28228v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28228v2), first 26 June, selected revision 1 October 2026. Read Sections 2/3, Theorems 3.1/3.2 and proof sketches, Sections 4.1/4.2. Two diagonal diffusion regimes with distinct variance ratios, common drift and diffeomorphic observation mixing break coordinate symmetries. The input is equality of **full observed path laws**; quadratic variation supplies diffusion geometry. Implementation uses consecutive pairs and a transition approximation. This is strong prior art for diffusion shifts and simultaneous whitening, but a different information interface from stationary covariance increments with unknown baseline power. Full appendix proofs were not audited.

## Older prerequisites and unresolved priority

- **Anderson, The inverse problem of stationary covariance generation**, Journal of Statistical Physics 1(1), 133–147, March 1969, DOI 10.1007/BF01007246. Read only [author-institution abstract/metadata](https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/publications/the-inverse-problem-of-stationary-covariance-generation/), not full paper. The stated object is stationary output covariance or spectral matrix of a linear system, with minimal realization characterized by a quadratic matrix inequality. Ordinary HTTP snapshot access returned403 although the web tool retrieved the record. The abstract does not justify treating this as an instantaneous covariance-intervention theorem; nor does it exclude priority elsewhere in the paper.
- **Hayden, Yuan, Goncalves, Network Reconstruction from Intrinsic Noise**, [1310.0375v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.0375v2), first 1 October 2013, revision 19 December 2014. Abstract/metadata only, saved. The output spectral density and spectral factorization are explicit inputs; minimum-phase structure or an algebraic Riccati equation governs ambiguity. This is a stronger time-resolved baseline than single stationary snapshots.
- Independent auditor `notes/check_wcrg/cycle3/ou_identification_audit.md` inspected **Dettling et al., Identifiability in Continuous Lyapunov Models** and **Dettling's 2024 thesis §4.4**. The latter already augments drift unknowns with diagonal volatility unknowns and regularization. We use the auditor's scoped prior-art report, not a claim that r04 personally read the full thesis. Appending diffusion unknowns and using a Lyapunov equation are not new ideas.

Priority remains unresolved. The exact for-each-fixed-system, one-full-SPD-increment stationary-snapshot theorem with unknown diagonal baseline is a candidate contribution, not established historical novelty. Physical implementability, bounded preparation costs and restricted actuators remain unresolved. The decisive next research question is calibrated diagonal/rank-one actuation with acquired conditioning, against an equally informed timed-observation baseline.
