## Exact interface and accepted theorem

A known list of distinct canonical nonidentity Hermitian Pauli strings P_j has support size≤k and at most r strings touching each site. The target is σ=e^(−βH*)/Z*, H*=Σθ*jP_j, |θ*j|≤J, β>0 known. The candidate is ρ=e^(−βH)/Z using that same list and β; its finite coefficients need no bound for this population result. Identity coefficients are excluded. Let s=min(n,1+r(k−1)), b=βJrs and

α=e^(−2b)/(1+b·2^(s/2)e^b).

The exact unitary-orbit weighted scores solve

−i[A,τ]=((1−λ)L_τ+λR_τ) S_A^(τ,λ).

Define the summed full uniform population regret

Jbar=(1/2)Σ_A∫_0^1 Tr[(ΔS_A,λ)† I_σ,λ(ΔS_A,λ)]dλ.

For local physical X/Z,

Jbar≥2β²α Σ_j |supp P_j|(θ_j−θ*j)² ≥2β²α||θ−θ*||₂².

For X/Y/Z the factor is4. At fixed β,J,k,r, α is independent of n. An objective averaged over sites must divide this bound by n. β=0 and identity gauges do not identify Hamiltonian coefficients and are explicitly outside this assertion.

## Independently checked load-bearing steps

**Uniform λ and the correct metric.** In a σ eigenbasis the multiplier of ∫_0^1 I_σ,λ⁻¹dλ is1/L(p_i,p_j), with L the logarithmic mean. Also ∫S_A^(τ,λ)dλ=−i[A,logτ]. Completing the square with Y=Ω_σ(∫ΔS) yields

∫||ΔS||²_σ,λdλ≥||∫ΔS||²_Ωσ,

where Ω_σ(X)=∫_0^1σ^tXσ^(1−t)dt. Thus Jbar≥(β²/2)Σ_A||−i[A,H−H*]||²_Ωσ. The additive log-partition constants disappear under commutators. This is the **global** BKM metric of the supported observable; replacing it by the reduced-state BKM metric without proof would be a defect. The draft does not make that replacement.

For every finite-dimensional faithful target and candidate, the score matrices and all inverse Sylvester operators have finite continuous limits at λ=0,1. Therefore the endpoint integrations and square completion are legal. Their bounds need not be uniform in n or in unbounded candidates for acquisition purposes. Uniform on the entire open interval has the same integral as the closed interval; a clipped law is a different objective and does not inherit the displayed argument.

**Reduced marginal without false exponential monotonicity.** Split H*=I_S⊗H_rest+V_S and h_S=||V_S||op. Scalar Jensen in the spectral measure of H*, applied to ψ⊗e_a with e_a an eigenvector of H_rest, bounds the partial-trace numerator below by e^(−βh_S)Z_rest. Weyl eigenvalue perturbation bounds the total partition function above by d_S e^(βh_S)Z_rest. Therefore

σ_S≥e^(−2βh_S)I_S/d_S.

This is a quadratic-form bound proved from scalar convexity and trace estimates. It never asserts that the matrix exponential is operator monotone. For supported Hermitian Q, G=TrσQ²≥e^(−2βh_S)τ_S(Q²), and G≥e^(−2βh_S)||Q||op²/d_S. At most r|S| terms intersect S, so h_S≤Jr|S|.

**Global BKM transfer by the second modular moment.** In the σ eigenbasis set a_ij=(p_i+p_j)/2, u_ij=(logp_i−logp_j)/2. Hermiticity makes G=Σa_ij|Q_ij|². The scalar inequality tanh(u)/u≥1/(1+|u|) follows from e^(2u)≥1+2u for u≥0 and evenness. Jensen for1/(1+t), then Cauchy, gives

||Q||²_Ωσ≥G/[1+(1/2)√(E₂/G)],

E₂=Trσ[βH*,Q]†[βH*,Q]. The moment equality is exact: because commutator entry magnitudes are symmetric, its arithmetic weights equal the σ-weighted quadratic norm. Only V_S contributes to this commutator, giving E₂≤4β²h_S²||Q||op². Combining with the marginal bound yields

||Q||²_Ωσ≥α_Sτ_S(Q²),
α_S=e^(−2βh_S)/(1+βh_S√d_S e^(βh_S)).

The Q=0 case is immediate and avoids division by G. This proves the full-system BKM bound using the reduced marginal only as one ingredient.

**Star supports and factors2/4.** Each C_Ai=−i[A_i,H−H*] is Hermitian and supported within the union of the at most r model terms touching i. Its support has at most1+r(k−1) sites. Hence the common α above applies. Normalized Hilbert–Schmidt norms on that star and the full system agree. Multiplication by a fixed Pauli preserves orthogonality of distinct strings, so

τ(C_Ai²)=4Σ_{j:{A_i,P_j}=0}δθ_j².

Each nonidentity single-site component anticommutes with at least one of X,Z, and exactly two of X,Y,Z. Therefore the summed commutator norm is≥4Σ_jwt(P_j)δθ_j² for X/Z and exactly8Σ_jwt(P_j)δθ_j² for XYZ. The original1/2 regret factor then gives2/4. No hidden factor of Hilbert dimension or site count is omitted.

## Integrated potential and candidate observable

I also checked draft section5. Uniformly integrated potential for each involution A is

Gbar_A(τ)=Trτ(logτ−AlogτA)=D(τ||AτA).

The general Hermitian-direction formula(1/2)Trτ[A,[A,logτ]] follows by eigenbasis expansion. For the involution case, selfadjointness of the Fréchet logarithm derivative gives

∇Gbar_A(τ)=logτ+I−AlogτA−Dlog_τ(AτA).

Positive homogeneity implies Trτ∇Gbar=Gbar. The score regret is exactly the reverse Bregman expression Gbar(σ)−Gbar(ρ)−Tr∇Gbar(ρ)(σ−ρ). One can verify this from the variational representation of each weighted quadratic potential: the envelope derivative evaluated on σ equals Re⟨S_ρ,∂σ⟩−(1/2)⟨S_ρ,I_σS_ρ⟩. Expanding the squared-score loss gives the same quantity.

For K=βH, candidate observable Obar_A=−∇Gbar_A becomes

Obar_A(K)=K−AKA−I+Dlog_{e^(−K)}(Ae^(−K)A).

The partition function cancels by inverse scaling of Dlog in its base. The signs,1/2 potential factor, involution assumption and unnormalized-versus-normalized scaling are correct. Convexity of Gbar in density matrices does not imply convexity or gradient dominance of this reverse regret in Gibbs parameters. The draft appropriately makes no such inference.
