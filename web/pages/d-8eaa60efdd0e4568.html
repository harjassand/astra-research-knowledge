## 2. Independent capacity optimization and the literal tree

For a spherical tree with N_n edges at level n, conserved flow mass M has
average edge flow M/N_n under a uniform random ray. Jensen applied to every
finite partial path gives average potential at least
M^(p-1)*sum_n (R_n/N_n)^(p-1). Thus a pointwise potential cap1 enforces this
bound. Uniform flow attains it, including the zero-capacity divergent case.
Consequently Cap_p=[sum_n(R_n/N_n)^(p-1)]^(-1/(p-1)).

Define j_n=floor(3*log2(n+1)/4), k_n=n+j_n and N_n=2^k_n, with k_0=0.
The increase of 3*log2(n+1)/4 over one step is <1, so j_n-j_(n-1) is0or1
and every vertex has 2 or 4 children. Integer comparisons 2^(4*j)<=(n+1)^3
acquire j_n without floating rounding. With lambda=1/sqrt2 and R_n=2^n,
R_n/N_n=2^(-j_n) is between (n+1)^(-3/4) and twice that value. Its sum
diverges, while its squared sum is <=4*sum_{n>=1}(n+1)^(-3/2)<=8 by the
integral test. The uniform rational flow mass1/3 has squared potential<=8/9.
Hence Cap2=0 and Cap3>=1/3. General exponent alpha changes the summability
thresholds at1 and1/2 respectively. This is a universal calculation; finite
prefixes would not establish either convergence or divergence.

## 3. Independent polynomial expansion for the local constants

Let x,y be independent symmetric Hadamard posterior coordinates, with
a=E|x|^2, b=E|y|^2 and signed third moments tau_x,tau_y. Symmetry gives
zero coordinate means, second moments (a/3)*identity and (b/3)*identity,
and no third moments except all three distinct coordinates. Put
s=x dot y and N_i=x_i+y_i+x_j*y_k+x_k*y_j. Direct expansion gives

    E sum_i N_i^2 = a+b+2*a*b/3,
    E[s*sum_i N_i^2] = 2*a*b/3 + 4*tau_x*b + 4*a*tau_y

quadratic information is E[sum_i N_i^2/(1+s)]. A channel-scaled second
