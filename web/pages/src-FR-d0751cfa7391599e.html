# Exact retained-workload correlations under reference-tracked exchange

Date: 15 October 2026 (Australia/Brisbane). Internally derived by the root
researcher; historical originality and external review are UNKNOWN.

## Contract

Fix an observed condition c and let N independent, identically prepared modules
have joint state rho_XE, where X=RS includes the donor's tracked reference R.
The physical exchange permutes S; logical reference reassignment follows its
donor, so the retained logical X includes R from the same original module.
Environment registers remain in place. The joint physical implementation must
be the intended exchange on the relevant joint states, within any separately
justified error. Reduced gate characterization does not establish this premise.

Let pi(i) be the donor delivered to recipient i. For a retained set T of k sites,
put D=pi(T) and m=|T intersect D|. No independence assumption between X and E
inside an original module is made. Finite-dimensional registers suffice below;
the entropy conclusions extend when all displayed entropies are finite.

## Theorem 1: exact state and information

Up to relabeling tensor factors, the retained state is exactly

    rho_T = (tensor over j in T intersect D of rho_(X_j E_j))
            tensor (tensor over j in D minus T of rho_(X_j))
            tensor (tensor over j in T minus D of rho_(E_j)).

The X_j factor is placed in the logical output whose donor is j. Consequently,

    I(X_D : E_T)_(rho_T) = m I(X:E)_rho.

In particular, all retained data jointly decorrelate from all retained
environments exactly when m=0, unless rho was already a product. For a
derangement pi, every individual retained site has state rho_X tensor rho_E,
yet the total correlation among the k output *sites* is

    sum_(i in T) S(X_pi(i) E_i) - S(rho_T) = m I(X:E)_rho.

For T equal to all sites this is N I(X:E), and the global data/environment
mutual information is unchanged. Exchange relocates these correlations.

Proof. Each original module contributes either both its factors, only X, only
E, or neither to the partial trace. Independence of original modules gives the
displayed tensor factorization. Additivity of entropy gives

    S(rho_T)=m S(XE)+(k-m)[S(X)+S(E)],
    S(X_D)=k S(X),    S(E_T)=k S(E).

Subtracting yields the mutual information identity. For a derangement each
one-site marginal is a product, which proves the total-correlation identity.
The tensor factorization, rather than a finite numerical check, is the proof.

If fixed points are allowed, the cross-partition mutual information is still
m I(X:E), but total correlation among sites is (m-f) I(X:E), where f is the
number of fixed points in T. A fixed point keeps correlation inside its site.

## Theorem 2: sharp routing reservoir overhead

For fixed registers with dim X>=2 and dim E>=2, routing to produce k jointly
independent product-marginal outputs for every unknown rho_XE requires
N >= 2k, and that bound is sufficient. This statement also holds for randomized
routing selected independently of all input modules, even when the route
record is forgotten. If either register is one-dimensional, every input is
already product and the necessity statement does not apply.

Sufficiency: use k recipient modules and k disjoint donor modules, exchange
each pair, and retain one endpoint of each pair. Reference tracking preserves
each donor RS marginal, including its internal entanglement. The retained
outputs are rho_X tensor rho_E independently. The other endpoints carry the
complementary correlations and are discarded from the workload.

Necessity: choose the classical state X_j=E_j=Z_j with independent fair bits
Z_j. Define A=sum_(i in T) X_pi(i), B=sum_(i in T) E_i. Then exactly

    Cov(A,B)=|T intersect pi(T)|/4.

Averaging over independently randomized routing gives E[m]/4 because both
conditional means equal k/2. The ideal product target has covariance zero, so
m must vanish almost surely. Disjoint k-element T and pi(T) require N>=2k.
For fixed retained sets the pigeonhole lower bound m>=max(0,2k-N) also gives

    Cov(A,B) >= max(0,2k-N)/4.

This proof is a routing-class impossibility. A separate worker supplies a
stronger polynomial-degree bound for arbitrary fixed quantum instruments.
Independent same-team review identified the explicit nontrivial-dimension and
input-independent-routing qualifications above. The initial authored draft is
retained as ROUTING_CORRELATION_THEOREM_v0001.txt; the current statement includes
these qualifications. Neither draft is externally certified.

## Workload interpretation and conditional matching

One X or E can contain an entire internally correlated workload. Swapping
between two independent condition-matched workload modules preserves the
donor's full data/reference state and replaces its associated environment.
The theorem does not require independent qubits or a microscopic bath model
inside a workload. It does require independent whole workload preparations,
matched continuation, and a bound on collateral environmental action.

Conditional matching applies separately for each c. Retaining floor(N_c/2)
outputs per condition can change the condition distribution. Thus neither this
construction nor postselection silently preserves the population p_c. Use
predeclared strata with correct target weights, or report the actual routed
intervention and its weights. Acquisition of rare conditions, buffers,
reference storage, reset and all discarded modules must be charged.

This result clarifies the distinction between a one-site causal assay and a
joint workload. It does not establish a new laboratory operation or advantage
over direct randomized controller testing. Local continuation/readout permits
less conservative estimation than a general joint workload; that is a distinct
dependency-graph question, investigated separately.

## Prior art and verification boundary

Two-copy subsystem recombination is established prior art, including the
experimental cavity-QED study by Coeuret Cauquil et al. (2026):
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07011v2 . Its existence precludes a claim to have
invented product-marginal recombination. The result here is an explicit
retained-subset accounting and operational resource boundary for the supplied
reference-tracked construction. Historical priority for these formulations has
not been established.

The checker verify_routing_correlations.py exhaustively enumerates small
classical routing cases and tests quantum entropy identities for selected mixed
states. Those checks corroborate formulas; they do not establish physical
obtainability, independent correctness or novelty.
