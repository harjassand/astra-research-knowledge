Relative equilibrium capacity certificates from hidden residuals
Root proof reconstruction, 12 October 2026. No novelty or external validation claim.

Let Q be a finite irreducible REVERSIBLE CTMC with stationary pi. H=-Q is
self-adjoint nonnegative in L2(pi), E labels, P conditional expectation on labels.
For a coarse vector x write f=Ex. Define
 A=-E^T diag(pi)QE, B=(I-P)HE, Gamma=B^T diag(pi)B.
A is the k-by-k conductance Laplacian, using unnormalized indicator coordinates.
Assume the reflected within-label generators have certified gaps >=gamma>0.
Then every h with Ph=0 satisfies <h,Hh> >=gamma ||h||²: restrict the full Dirichlet
form to within-label edges and apply each conditional Poincare inequality.

For ALL coarse x and hidden h,
 <Ex+h,H(Ex+h)> =x^T A x+2<h,Bx>+<h,Hh>
 >=x^T A x-||Bx||²/gamma
 =x^T(A-Gamma/gamma)x,
by completing the square. If Gamma <=eta gamma A in Loewner order, eta<1,
then this is >=(1-eta)x^TAx, simultaneously for every coarse direction.

For disjoint unions of full labels Aterm,Bterm, capacity is the minimum Dirichlet
energy subject to f=1 on Aterm and f=0 on Bterm. For every admissible microscopic
f, x=P f has the same boundary label values and h=f-Pf has Ph=0. Therefore
 (1-eta) cap_coarse(Aterm,Bterm) <=cap_fine(Aterm,Bterm)<=cap_coarse(Aterm,Bterm).
The upper bound restricts the minimization to label-constant potentials. The lower
bound relaxes hidden boundary values, which can only decrease the infimum.
The hidden residual must include boundary-adjacent forcing; computing B only
from the interior low/hidden operator and dropping fixed-terminal forcing is wrong.

In orthonormal coarse coordinates, write H=[Ahat Bhat*;Bhat D]. Exact elimination
over all hidden fluctuations gives the PSD energy form S=Ahat-Bhat*D^-1 Bhat.
This is a constrained-means Schur complement. It need NOT be a Markov generator
on labels and need NOT equal every hard-boundary trace generator. The previous
capacity enclosure remains valid without either claim.

For s>0, R_s=P(s+H)^-1P on the coarse space is
 (s+Ahat-K_s)^-1, K_s=Bhat*(s+D)^-1 Bhat.
Define K_0=Bhat*D^-1Bhat. Since D>=gamma,
 K_s<=K_0<=(1+s/gamma)K_s.
Thus resolvent observations plus Ahat and gamma give another certified enclosure
 Ahat-(1+s/gamma)K_s <= S <= Ahat-K_s.
Stationary visible snapshots at an independent Exp(s) clock acquire
J_s=s E^T diag(pi)(s+H)^-1 E. With M=diag(pi E),
R_s=M^-1/2 J_s M^-1/2/s. Inversion, mean masses, endpoint preparation, rate Ahat,
precision and sampling cost are all acquired interfaces, not free consequences.
