## Competent CAN comparator with the same dataset: PASS

The primary CAN theorem and its source-verified uniform Q support, operator norm and deterministic expectation-error interface are supplied premises, not a theorem independently reproved here. Under them, the product-Pauli conversion is mathematically valid.

Every needed Q fits in a predetermined maximal interval of s=Oβ(log1/ε) qubits and has norm ≤Mβ uniformly over queried candidates. On a simultaneous marginal event `||ρhat_L−ρ_L||1≤τ`, trace duality gives `|Tr((ρhat_L−ρ_L)Q)|≤τMβ` for **every** such Q, including data-adaptive choices. Local reconstructions may be Hermitian trace one without positivity; `||ρhat||1≤1+τ` suffices. Certified operator error ζ adds at most `(1+τ)ζ`. Thus the original deterministic local tests can use these approximate expectations without any extra adaptive union bound.

If each local Pauli coefficient estimate has error ≤ν, normalized Pauli expansion gives Frobenius error²≤2^sν² and trace error≤2^sν. Setting ν=τ/2^s gives copy cost `O(36^sτ⁻² log(n4^s/δ))`. Overlapping windows may share the same copies. Neither a target matrix nor candidate Gibbs preparation is needed. A straightforward all-moment implementation charges `O(nN4^s s)` classical work and the raw local-measurement data.

At fixed β, the finite local correction-net size is independent of ε,n by the cited source conditions. The number of iterations is logarithmic. Since `2^s≤constantβ·(1/ε)^{Oβ(1)}` and τ is proportional to ε with β-dependent constants, acquisition and offline dense local Q compilation have polynomial inverse-accuracy cost and near-linear n dependence. This is stronger asymptotically than the proposed DP and has the stronger maximum-coordinate error guarantee. Its beta-dependent constants can be huge; neither this audit nor the original source asserts that it uniformly dominates the DP practically or at changing β.

One safeguard was sent to the comparator owner: the local-strength/real-time compiler bounds must cover every queried background/correction Hamiltonian, possibly with a larger fixed-β coefficient bound if original iterates leave [-1,1]. Bounded correction sizes and geometrically decreasing iteration radii supply a fixed-β bound; enlarging the constants preserves polynomial accuracy. Uniform norm and support estimates, not an unverified assumption that iterates remain in exactly the initial cube, are the needed premises.

## Decision boundary

Both compositions pass within their stated source/oracle interfaces. Neither has been implemented here. The proper-mixture DP is a useful mathematical construction and control, but its global fitting cost is weaker in accuracy than a competent established learner after the same reusable-data conversion. A broad first all-temperature, near-linear-n, non-SOS or reusable-Pauli learner claim is unsupported. Checkpointing is justified unless a new sharply costed feature changes this comparison.
