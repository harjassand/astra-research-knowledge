What is—and is not—established
The conditional entropy theorem, five-coordinate obstruction, same-coset strengthening and infinite amplification have explicit proofs in the note. Their historical novelty remains unverified, and they have not received independent review.

The construction also does not yet constitute an efficient algorithm. Acquiring legal directions and solving all compatibility problems can require exponential work. A theorem conditional on those data must not be presented as an efficient compiler.

The remaining obstacle is now exact: local freedom to move coordinates does not imply globally compatible marginal dynamics. The full irredundant-cover structure must either force that compatibility or support a genuinely different operation. Until that step is established, this is a conditional bridge with rigorous failure boundaries—not the foundational mathematical power requested.
