# Late novelty addendum: algebraic precedent

Date: 8 October 2026 (UTC). This supplements, without replacing, FAMILY197_RESEARCH.md.

The orthogonal-idempotent tower used in equation (2.6) has classical one-sided-inverse precedent. Jacobson, “Some Remarks on One-Sided Inverses” (1950), DOI https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1950-0036223-1, is the historical reference identified in the literature audit. The coordinator's attempt to open that DOI failed; the original article is not represented as fully read.

An accessible primary source is Iovanov–Sistko, “On the Toeplitz-Jacobson algebra and direct finiteness,” https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.00109, Theorem 1, p. 2. It explicitly displays the analogous idempotents
$$
f_n=y^{n-1}x^{n-1}-y^nx^n
$$
and their countable direct-sum ideal decomposition in the universal algebra with $xy=1$. Substituting the group-ring witness $x=a,y=b$ produces the idempotents $p_{n-1}$ in the report. No novelty is claimed for this tower or for constructing such matrix units from a one-sided inverse.

The group-specific quotient $R/U$ and its compact Haar dynamics are proved directly in the report; structural classification results about the universal Toeplitz-Jacobson algebra are not imported to the whole group ring. The exact maximizing-tail and relative-entropy deductions remain internally audited applications of established general theorems, with no exact source match located and no historical-priority certification.

The final literature audit also distinguishes “not of finite type” from “not linear-sofic.” Only the former is established for this tail by the cited 2021 linear-CA results. No claim that the tail is not linear-sofic is made.

The entropy sign and Bowen's measurable Bernoulli-isomorphism question remain unresolved.
