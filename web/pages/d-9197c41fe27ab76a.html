1. EXACT OUTPUT INTERFACE

Fix local dimension d>=2 and fixed public rational 0<zeta<1/d. Put
 n=d^N, q=d^2, D=binom(N+q-1,q-1), p=d zeta,
 w=p^N, r=(zeta/d)^N=w d^(-2N).                              (W1)

First, a correction-only statement. Supply an exactly rational, Hermitian,
permutation-invariant, trace-one R in the acquired matrix-unit orbit algebra,
and a rational mixture
 S=sum_j lambda_j sigma_j^{tensor N},
 lambda_j>=0, sum_j lambda_j=1, sigma_j>=zeta I, Tr sigma_j=1. (W2)
Assume polynomially many nodes and polynomial input bit length. The algorithm
computes an exact rational certificate A defined below. Whenever A<=w it
outputs a polynomial-length program/data description of a FULLY SEPARABLE
law whose mean is exactly R, and for any rational 0<eps<1 a sampler using
a bounded number of fair bits whose mean is within trace distance eps/4
of R. Each individual emission is an N-tuple of exactly physical rational
one-qudit states. All bit costs are polynomial in N, input length and
log(1/eps) for fixed d. A flat list of every product term is not the output.
Local factors in a sampled tuple may differ.

Second, the all-accuracy Gibbs consequence. Supply a fixed-parameter
collective-quadratic Hamiltonian covered by the all-N c04_s01 theorem,
with an acquired rational/efficiently computable local encoding of length L
and a known numerical norm bound ||K_N||<=h=poly(N). The stopped source law
has all local factors >=eta I and UNHALVED trace error
 ||rho_K-S_*||_1 <= (eta/d)^N exp(-2N).                     (W3)
Choose a fixed certified rational zeta in [eta/2,eta]. Then the preceding
compiler and correction produce a finite-bit product-state sampler with
mean rho_out satisfying T(rho_K,rho_out)<=eps in
 poly_d(N,L,h,log(1/eps)) time and output bits.              (W4)
For fixed bounded local parameters h=O(N), this is
 poly_d(N,L,log(1/eps)).

The admitted family has Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab and
 K_N=Q_(alpha I+A)/N+F_b, alpha>0 fixed, ||b||<=B fixed,
 ||A||<=delta_*=(alpha/40)eta^2,
with eta and delta_* explicitly defined in the source's E4. This radius
is extremely small. There is no arbitrary-anisotropy or variable-d claim.
An efficiently computable constant is not a free arbitrary-real oracle.
The precision and numerical bound h are charged: an arbitrarily large
coefficient with only its binary encoding is not silently treated as O(1).

2. A RATIONAL ORTHOGONAL LOCAL BASIS

Use T_0=I. For a<b use
 X_ab=E_ab+E_ba, Y_ab=i(E_ab-E_ba),
and for 1<=l<d use
 H_l=(1/l)sum_(a=1)^l E_aa-E_(l+1,l+1).
These q matrices are a basis of M_d over C, with entries in Q(i).
They are Hermitian, T_a is traceless for a>0, and
 Tr(T_a T_b)=0 for a!=b;
 h_0=d, h_X=h_Y=2, h_Hl=1+1/l.                             (W5)
For l<k the diagonal inner product is l/(lk)-1/k=0. X and Y have
eigenvalues +/-1 on their two-dimensional support. H_l has l eigenvalues
1/l, one -1 and other zeros. Thus ||T_a||_op<=1 for a>0 and
 (I+s T_a)/d is a density for s=+/-1.                       (W6)
No Hilbert-Schmidt normalization or algebraic-number sign oracle is used.

For a count vector m=(m_0,...,m_(q-1)), sum m_a=N, let F_m be the SUM of
the distinct ordered tensor words P_v=tensor_i T_(v_i) having these counts.
There are
 s_m=N!/prod_a m_a! <= q^N
words. Different ordered words are HS-orthogonal, so F_m form a rational
Hermitian basis of the permutation commutant and
 ||F_m||_2^2=s_m h_m, h_m=prod_a h_a^m_a.                  (W7)
Their number is D. All except m=(N,0,...,0) have trace zero; that orbit
is I with trace n. Therefore E=R-S has the exact expansion
 E=sum_(m nonidentity) c_m F_m                              (W8)
with real rational c_m and exactly zero identity coefficient.

3. ACQUIRE THE CHANGE OF BASIS WITHOUT ORDERED-WORD ENUMERATION

The preceding compiler stores R=sum_beta b_beta B_beta, where B_beta sums
matrix-unit tensor words with counts beta=(beta_ab), and has orbit size
s_beta=N!/prod beta_ab!. The FIXED local inverse coefficients are
 e_(ab,l)=Tr(T_l E_ab)/h_l=(T_l)_(ba)/h_l,
 ell_ab(z)=sum_l e_(ab,l) z_l.
Define the fixed-q-variable degree-N polynomial
 P_R(z)=sum_beta b_beta s_beta prod_(ab) ell_ab(z)^{beta_ab}. (W9)
Then its coefficient at z^m is s_m times the coefficient of F_m in R.
Indeed each of the s_beta matrix-unit words expands into local T words;
commuting the variables collects all words in a final count orbit, which
have a common coefficient by permutation invariance. Thus
 c_m^R=[z^m]P_R/s_m.                                      (W10)

Repeated polynomial multiplication uses only
binom(N+q-1,q-1)=D possible degree-N monomials and the lower-degree
analogues. For fixed q this is polynomial time/storage in N. Each
coefficient is a sum of exponentially many elementary terms but their
number contributes O_d(N) bits, not exponential enumeration. Denominators
are powers of fixed local rational denominators times supplied denominators;
the factorial factors have O(N log N) bits. Exact rational arithmetic has
polynomial bit cost. Equivalently acquire and solve the D-dimensional
symmetric-power change-of-basis matrix by fraction-free elimination.

For the mixture, compute directly
 v_ja=Tr(sigma_j T_a)/h_a,
 c_m^S=sum_j lambda_j prod_a v_ja^{m_a}.                   (W11)
There is NO orbit multiplicity s_m in (W11): F_m already sums its words.
Compute c_m=c_m^R-c_m^S. Hermiticity implies exact real coefficients;
an imaginary residual or nonzero identity coefficient is a failed
implementation check. Signs/zeros are integer numerator tests in Q.

4. AN EXACT POSITIVE CORRECTION AND ITS CHECKABLE MASS

Set A_m=n s_m |c_m| and A=sum_(nonidentity m) A_m. A has polynomial
encoding and can be computed/tested exactly; zero orbits are discarded.
For a word v in orbit m with t=N-m_0>=1 active sites, draw t signs
uniformly conditioned on their product being sign(c_m). Emit (I+s_i
T_(v_i))/d at active sites and I/d elsewhere. Averaging sign characters
kills all proper nonempty characters and gives
 (I+sign(c_m) P_v)/n.
This also holds at t=1, when its one sign is forced. Uniform averaging
over the orbit yields the fully separable state
 Omega_m=I/n+sign(c_m)F_m/(n s_m).                         (W12)
Consequently A_m Omega_m=(A_m/n)I+c_m F_m, and
 R=[S-w I/n]+(w-A)I/n+sum_m A_m Omega_m.                  (W13)

The first term is positive and fully separable: writing
 sigma_j=p I/d+(1-p)tau_j,
 tau_j=(sigma_j-zeta I)/(1-p),                            (W14)
its all-white product component has the COMMON weight p^N=w. The other
components are positive products. That term has trace 1-w; the remaining
weights sum to w. Thus A<=w makes (W13) an exact convex decomposition,
and certifies positivity of R as well. It does not require R's positivity
to have been assumed before optimization.

For a sufficient analytic certificate, tensor orthogonality and
Cauchy-Schwarz give
 sum_m s_m|c_m|
 <=||E||_2 [sum_m s_m/h_m]^(1/2)
 <=||E||_1 (sum_a h_a^-1)^(N/2)
 <=d^N ||E||_1.
Thus A<=d^(2N)||E||_1, and ||E||_1<=r suffices.             (W15)
The exact A test can be much stronger than this loose ball bound.

5. SAMPLE EVERY BRANCH IN POLYNOMIAL TIME

Top-level probabilities are 1-w, w-A, and the nonzero A_m; they sum to
one and there are at most D+1 categories. No division by A is needed.
The white branch emits N copies of I/d.

For the nonwhite branch choose j with probabilities lambda_j. Conditioning
does not change these probabilities, because the all-white probability
w is the same for each node. Generate independent white/nonwhite choices
with white probability p, conditioned not all white. While an all-white
prefix persists and t sites remain, the next-site probabilities are
 p(1-p^(t-1))/(1-p^t) for white,
 (1-p)/(1-p^t) for nonwhite.                              (W16)
At t=1 nonwhite is forced. After the first nonwhite, the subsequent choices
are independent with white probability p. Emit I/d on white sites and
tau_j on nonwhite sites. Its exact mean is (S-wI/n)/(1-w).
Powers and probabilities have O(N bits(p)) encoding; denominators 1-p^t
are positive and >=1-p. This uses O(N) transitions, not rare-event rejection.

For an orbit branch m choose each next label proportional to its remaining
count, remove it, and repeat. Every distinct ordered word has probability
prod m_a!/N!=1/s_m. Then use t-1 fair signs and fix the last active sign
by the required parity. This takes O(N) transitions and emits N matrices;
the s_m 2^(t-1) possible products are never listed by the compiler.

An alternative bounded sampler draws b bits U, sets rank=floor(U s_m/2^b),
and un-ranks the multiset lexicographically. At a prefix with t remaining
labels, the subtree count for label a is s_current m_a/t, an exact integer.
Each rank has either floor(2^b/s_m) or ceil(2^b/s_m) preimages, so its
TV error from uniform is <=s_m/2^b. Taking b=O(N+log(1/eps)) makes the
error <=eps/32. This alternative is used in the small exact fixture.

6. BOUNDED FAIR BITS AND ALL ERRORS

Exact rational categorical sampling could require unbounded random bits.
For a deterministic bound, round all but one probabilities DOWN to a
common b-bit dyadic grid and give the residual to a VALID positive-support
category. This has TV error <=(k-1)2^-b for k categories. Skip zero weights
and zero remaining counts. Top-level residual may go to nonwhite since
1-w>0; at the last white transition the forced choice remains exact.
All outcomes remain positive product states even after rounding.

With K<=D mixture nodes, the sum of category counts along an execution is
at most D+K+(q+2)N+2. Choose
 b=ceil log2[32(D+K+(q+2)N+2)/eps].                       (W17)
Successive conditional coupling, including adaptive histories, bounds
total output-law TV by the sum of the conditional TV errors, <=eps/32.
Mixture mean trace distance is at most law TV. The rank alternative adds
another <=eps/32. Either implementation has ample room within eps/4.
Random bits, deterministic sampling time and emitted state descriptions
are polynomial in N, supplied bit length and log(1/eps).

8. OPTIONAL LOCAL PREPARATION INTERFACE, WITHOUT A SPECTRAL GAP ORACLE

The theorem's native output is rational local density descriptions. For
an abstract accurate-local-rotation backend, one can additionally prepare
them with total trace error <=eps/4. Exact rational PSD elimination gives
rho=L diag(d_j)L^*, with rational L,d_j>=0 and zero pivots recognized
exactly. A zero PSD diagonal has a zero row/column; positive pivots recurse.
At fixed d all arithmetic and output bits are polynomial in input length.
Let l_j be a nonzero column; z_j=d_j||l_j||^2 are nonnegative rational
weights summing to one and the normalized pure rays l_j/||l_j|| give rho.
There are at most d rays. Acquire their probabilities to dyadic error
eps/(16N), and their pure state/rotation instructions to eps/(16N).
Square roots of positive rationals and the fixed-d rotations are computable
to this precision with polynomial bit cost; tiny nonzero values cost their
input bits, not an assumed uniform eigenvalue gap. Tensor telescoping sums
the N local errors. Only a fixed number of two-level local rotations is
needed per pure ray in the continuous-rotation instruction model.

A fixed finite native gate-set extension requires a separate bit-certified
gate compiler interface. Dawson–Nielsen's Solovay–Kitaev exposition gives
polylog accuracy arithmetic/gate counts for a fixed inverse-closed dense
instruction set, but explicitly says on p7 that finite arithmetic precision
is not analyzed. That caveat is retained. This file does not infer a full
Turing gate compiler solely from those arithmetic counts. Native hardware,
energy costs, quantum speed advantage and reference-preserving maps are
outside the executed/classical claim. Sampling/preprocessing are classical.
