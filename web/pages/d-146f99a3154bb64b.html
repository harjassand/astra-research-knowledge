# Cycle14: shared Chu proofs retain the affine lower bound

The internally gated result is a legal shared Chu compiler for the recent
Tabatabai–Jalali affine-linear Frege system. It uses at most `13m+93L`
intuitionistic formula-occurrence lines from m common syntax nodes and L
source steps. Combined with independently reconstructed finite-skeleton
normalization and the primary prior lower bound, the authors' conservative
hard family requires `2^(n^Omega(1))` affine Frege **lines**, and exponential
total size even with standard fresh acyclic definitional extensions.

The complete theorem and its assumptions are in
`notes/handoff_sol_max_check/cycle14/SHARED_CHU_THEOREM_AND_USE.md`.
The detailed derivation is in `SHARED_CHU_PROOF_LEDGER.md`. This is an internally
checked mathematical deduction, not external validation. Historical novelty
remains UNKNOWN. The result concerns affine linear logic; it is not a solution
to the general classical Frege/extended-Frege lower-bound problem.

## Source claim, new deduction, and strongest control

The primary is *Proof Complexity of Linear Logics*, Amirhossein Akbar Tabatabai
and Raheleh Jalali, [arXiv2601.22393v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22393v2).
Its initial submission was29 January2026; the qualifying recent revision
was9 July2026. Original PDF, HTML and TeX are preserved. The source proves
affine proof-symbol-size lower bounds and identifies proof-line/extension
robustness as future work. Exact v2 locations: proof conventions in Section2.1;
Frege Tables6–8, identified by TeX labels `Fig: FL_e`, `Fig: linear`,
`Fig: contraction-free logics`; Definition11 and Lemma13 for Chu; Theorem14
for the compiler; Definition16/Lemma19/Theorem20 for conservativity;
Corollary10 and Theorem22 for the hard family; Section7 for the full compiler
proof. Full decisive sections were read; unrelated deferred proofs are not
claimed as fully read.

Our deduction replaces the dependence on expanded formula-tree symbols by
common syntax nodes, through ordinary legal target proof DAGs. A fixed finite
proof skeleton then provides O(L+|A|) common syntax, even when an original proof
had enormous intermediate trees. This is the step that transfers the known
intuitionistic line lower bound to affine Frege lines.

The strongest older control is Jeřábek's *Substitution Frege and extended Frege
proof systems in non-classical logics*, author version24 November2008,
published2009: Section2.2 and Propositions3.2–3.3 already supply generic
Frege/circuit/EF compression relationships. Theorem6.37 supplies the relevant
intuitionistic EF lower-bound reduction. Jalali's arXiv2006.09705v2,
20 August2020 (published2021), Definition3.9 supplies the standard fresh
definitional EF convention. Those older methods are credited, not rediscovered
as novelty. Their intuitionistic hypotheses do not already establish the
present classical affine-with-cut line transfer. Their decisive pages and
version conventions were checked against preserved primary sources.

## Exact interface and decisive obstruction

Input: a closed proof in the specified finite LLW-Frege system, with exact
formula constructors, ordinary rule references, and multiplicity-preserving
contexts. Target: ordinary intuitionistic affine linear logic ILLW with cut.
Proof-DAG reuse is allowed by the source's definitions. Sharing formulas does
not permit contracting an unmodalized antecedent. For example, two uses of
`p=>p` in right fusion yield `p,p=>p*p`, never `p=>p*p`.

Each source constructor receives true Chu components and one cached proof
`T_A,S_A=>bottom`; all axiom/rule instances receive bounded fixed proof
templates. Named Pair inputs are already proved endpoints, not assumed
equations between arbitrary positive/negative atoms. Exact template
substitution preserves every resource and exponential side condition.

Raw extension translation fails: in the retained exact Łukasiewicz model,
p=3/4 and e=1/2 satisfy e equivalent to p*p, yet `t(e->p*p)=3/4`.
The valid extension route globally unfolds fresh acyclic definitions first,
redirecting syntax roots, and then applies Chu. No object-level uniform
substitution inference or semantic circuit-equivalence oracle is introduced.

Two source display slips are explicitly repaired: adj_u uses closed `=>T_A`
and `=>1` followed by right AND; necessitation begins from `=>T_A`.
The direct certified double-negation template uses only Pair_A; literal
source-style negation-duality wrappers also remain O(L) if cached.

## Consequence, remaining scope, and next falsifier

The concrete use is a more robust starting separation for research on
controlled contraction: syntax naming and the existing linear-exponential
rules do not evade these affine Frege lower bounds. A theorem-prover/compiler
can use the exact template interface to distinguish legal proof reuse from
an illegal copied assumption. This is a proof-theoretic result and reusable
method, not measured theorem-prover improvement.

The standard fresh acyclic EF assumptions are theorem hypotheses; the local
redirection script is not a complete arbitrary EF freshness/trace checker.
The assembled whole mixed-proof installer has not yet been executed. An
independent end-to-end replay on a mixed proof with repeated large operands
is the next useful implementation discriminator. A discovered template
requiring unbounded uncached operands, illegal context duplication or expanded
tree-size work would refute the claimed compiler interface.

The arbitrary affine sequent occurrence-line extension is kept separate in
prior14's `SEQUENT_LINE_EXTENSION.md`. This worker has not gated its context
packing/promotion proof and does not use it for the above consequence. The
general classical Frege/EF and unrestricted substitution-Frege lower-bound
