STATUS AND CLAIM

The unrestricted broadcasting-to-entanglement-breaking statement remains open. I have not proved the CP-range lifting theorem. Conditional on a precisely stated, dimension-free lifting modulus for the entire range of a UCP map from normalized L2-commutator control to normalized infinity-to-one trace norm, the existing power argument and flagged centralization give an explicit global modulus. The centralization threshold can be optimized exactly, and the power depth can be optimized against the corrected residual. This is a costed conditional reduction, not closure of the scientific gate.

The central claim is Theorem C below. It preserves one reconstruction channel for the whole state family, allows dimensions/reference spectra/family sizes to grow, and exposes the missing theorem and all error allocations. It relies on the supplied N46 pair-affinity bridge and on the internally derived centralization and Petz-correction arguments in the baseline. Those are not re-proved or externally validated here.

THEOREM C: CONDITIONAL GLOBAL MODULUS

Fix the smoothed interface from the baseline: a finite-dimensional experiment contains its faithful reference sigma and states rho satisfying sigma/2 <= rho <= 3 sigma/2; its two-output broadcaster has worst marginal half-trace error b; the uniform Holevo bound is fixed. The N46 pair bridge supplies a dimension-free v(b) tending to zero with

  Delta_pair := sup_rho ( ||sqrt(sigma)sqrt(rho)||_1
                           - Tr(sqrt(sigma)sqrt(rho)) ) <= v(b),
  v(b) = O(sqrt(b log(1/b))) as b down to zero.

Set C0=3/2 and M=sqrt(C0). The canonical-likelihood centralization estimate in the baseline gives

  Xi <= x_b := 2 sqrt(2 M v(b)),
  eps_b(t) := 4 sqrt(3 M x_b/t) + 8 t,       0<t<=1.

Let eps_b^* = inf_{0<t<=1} eps_b(t). The flagged finite encoder/decoder transfers the experiment to a finite tracial algebra and its broadcaster error is at most b_c := b+eps_b^*/2.

The Petz correction estimate at likelihood radius theta=1/2 then gives a positive, trace-selfadjoint, selfcompatible UCP marginal Phi with full trace-norm residual

  r_b := sqrt(4 b_c + (3/2) sqrt(2 b_c)).

For m>=1 define

  d_m := m^m/(m+1)^(m+1),
  delta_m := 2 sqrt(d_m) + 4 d_m.

Assume the following still-open lift, with a known or abstract modulus omega:
For every finite tracial C*-algebra (A,tau) and every UCP, tau-preserving, positive tau-selfadjoint selfcompatible Omega, if

  sup_{A,C=A*, ||A||,||C||<=1} ||[Omega(A),Omega(C)]||_(2,tau) <= delta,

then there exists one tau-preserving EB map E (a full-domain measure-and-prepare channel in physical coordinates) such that

  sup_{A=A*, ||A||<=1} ||E(A)-Omega(A)||_(1,tau) <= omega(delta),
  omega(delta) -> 0 uniformly over dimensions and faithful traces.

The conditional error bound is

  e(original experiment) <= F(b) := eps_b^*/2
       + (1/2) inf_{m in N, m>=1}
             [ (1+theta) omega(delta_m) + m r_b ],       theta=1/2.       (C)

This is a single-channel conclusion, not a separate decoder for each likelihood. If omega is explicit, (C) is an explicit optimizable modulus. If omega is only known to tend to zero, (C) still proves F(b)->0: when r_b>0 take m_b=ceil(r_b^(-1/2)); then m_b r_b<=sqrt(r_b)+r_b->0 and delta_(m_b)->0, so omega(delta_(m_b))->0. If r_b=0, let m->infinity directly. This diagonal choice is qualitative unless an effective omega is supplied.

DERIVATION AND OPTIMIZATIONS

1. The power-range estimate is uniform over the whole Hermitian operator-norm unit ball. For A=A* with ||A||<=1 put A_m=Phi^m(A). Positive selfadjoint L2 contraction spectral calculus gives

  ||Phi(A_m)-A_m||_2 <= max_(0<=lambda<=1) (1-lambda)lambda^m = d_m,
  tau(Phi(A_m^2)-Phi(A_m)^2)
     <= max_(0<=lambda<=1) (1-lambda^2)lambda^(2m) = d_m.

The first maximum occurs at lambda=m/(m+1), the second at lambda^2=m/(m+1). Let J be a joint UCP extension for Phi. Its two commuting input-register embeddings and the Stinespring Schwarz-defect estimate give

  ||[Phi(A_m),Phi(C_m)]||_2 <= 2 sqrt(d_m).

Replacing Phi(A_m),Phi(C_m) by A_m,C_m costs at most 4d_m, since multiplication by contractions is contractive in tracial L2. Therefore

  sup_(A,C contractions) ||[Phi^m(A),Phi^m(C)]||_2 <= delta_m.

Also ||Phi^m(H)-H||_1 <= m r_b by telescoping and trace-norm contraction. The result is dimension-free and has no selected-label dependence. This exact d_m is the scalar maximum, not the looser bound 1/(m+1). As m grows,

  d_m = (e^(-1)+O(1/m))/m,
  delta_m = (2/sqrt(e)+o(1))m^(-1/2).

2. The logarithmic-bin threshold can be optimized rather than set to Xi^(1/3). For a fixed upper bound x>0 on Xi, differentiate

  eps(t,x)=4 sqrt(3 M x) t^(-1/2) + 8t.

The unique unconstrained minimum is

  t_*(x)=(3 M x/16)^(1/3),
  eps_*(x)=24(3 M x/16)^(1/3),

provided t_*<=1; otherwise the constrained minimum is at t=1. The centralization error is increasing in Xi, so substituting x=x_b is valid. A rational threshold within a factor two of t_* changes eps by at most a factor 1.14; exact implementation/precision is separately charged below. This optimization changes constants, not the b^(1/12) order implied by v(b)=O(sqrt(b log(1/b))).

3. After the encoder/decoder, each broadcaster marginal has half-trace error at most b_c=b+eps*/2. At theta=1/2 the baseline Petz estimate with full trace errors d_r,d_s<=2b_c is

  ||S rho-rho||_1 <= sqrt(2 theta(d_r+d_s)+6 theta^2 sqrt(d_s))
                   <= sqrt(4b_c+(3/2)sqrt(2b_c)) = r_b.

This is the full residual used in the power step. It is not a half-trace error; retaining this factor of two is necessary in (C).

4. Apply the assumed lift to Omega=Phi^m, using its commutator bound delta_m. For rho=sigma(I+H), ||H||<=theta, and the EB map E supplied by the lift, channel contraction gives

  T(E(rho),rho) <= (1/2)[(1+theta)omega(delta_m)+m r_b].

The (1+theta) term pays for the input operator I+H in the infinity-to-one norm; the m r_b term pays for returning the original likelihood after m powers. Compose with the finite centralization decoder. This adds eps*/2 and yields (C). No factor 2^m is needed for this pair-output lift: a pair broadcaster for Phi can have Phi^(m-1) applied to each output. The theorem's main requirement is the single EB lift, not a many-clone experiment.

5. Power-depth optimization when omega(delta)<=K delta^alpha for some K,alpha>0. Let p=alpha/2 and A=(1+theta)K(2/sqrt(e))^alpha. Then, for small r, the m-dependent term in (C) has asymptotic envelope

  (1/2) inf_m [ A m^(-p) + m r ]
     ~ (1/2)(1+p) A^(1/(p+1)) (r/p)^(p/(p+1)),
  m_opt ~ (p A/r)^(1/(p+1)) = (alpha A/(2r))^(2/(alpha+2)).

Thus the conditional lifting/power contribution is O(r^(alpha/(alpha+2))). For alpha=1, m scales as r^(-2/3) and the contribution as r^(1/3). This is the optimized balance for the available delta_m~m^(-1/2) bound. It is not a rate for the unresolved lift itself.

The outer centralization threshold should be minimized first: both the direct eps*/2 and the downstream R(b+eps/2) are monotone in eps, so a larger centralization error never buys a smaller later residual. Given a certified upper bound on omega, the remaining integer m can be chosen by minimizing (C) directly; no separate arbitrary split of the error budget is needed.

The pair-affinity quantity Delta need not be measured; x_b can be computed from the imported upper bound v(b). Without a certified error/capacity bound, the thresholds are not acquired from data.
- For physical dimension d, the exact flagged centralization has at most d+1 nonzero shift intervals, hence at most d+1 flags and total Hilbert-space dimension at most d(d+1). It uses sigma's eigenbasis and log-eigenvalue breakpoints. The canonical X_rho is only a proof witness; the encoder itself depends on sigma,t, not the state label. This is finite-dimensional existence, not a dimension-independent implementation cost.
- Exact interval weights involve logarithms of eigenvalues. A rational grid of N shifts gives an explicit finite approximation: with at most d breakpoints per period, at most d grid cells meet a breakpoint, so a bounded trace-distance loss in [0,2] changes by at most 2d/N. Choose N>=2d/eta to keep this discretization contribution <=eta. Approximate each log-eigenvalue residue to absolute error xi; the union of bad shifts has probability at most 2d xi/t, giving at most 4d xi/t additional loss. Taking xi<=eta*t/(4d) keeps this contribution <=eta. Random shift sampling then costs ceil(log2 N) random bits per flag draw; an explicit mixture stores N weights. Eigenbasis, eigenvalue, filter, state-normalization and channel-description precision are additional costs. Rational CPTP approximation can preserve positivity/trace by retaining the replacement-state form, but a complete bit-complexity compiler and its error bound are not supplied here.
- The power part uses m sequential applications of the corrected channel; for a supplied Kraus description naive composition can grow Kraus count exponentially in m unless compressed. Petz recovery requires the reference and output marginal/support data. The lift's measurement outcomes, preparation states, runtime, query cost, and precision are entirely absent from the hypothesis unless the lift supplies them.
- No source family, reference, channel, broadcaster, finite experiment, or algorithm is acquired by the abstract theorem. No number of output labels, Choi data acquisition, classical sample count, runtime, or device cost follows. All those costs are UNKNOWN. A dimension-free trace-norm conclusion is not an efficient reconstruction protocol.
