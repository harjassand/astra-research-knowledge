  eps log(e/eps)<=c/B, with 0log(e/0):=0,
  m>=C B^2 W.                                             (2.1)
The projected refinement additionally needs eps<=c_mean and m>=C_mean D_p; (SC),(IG),(RG) can always be checked directly to avoid reliance on an unstated numerical constant. Thus a direct common-sample sufficient choice is
  m>=max(C B^2 W, C_mean D_p),
under the two stated contamination conditions. This replaces the redundant implicit log(1/eps_alg) bound for sample selection; every source-PCA runtime still uses eps_alg, including eps=0.

Proof of absorption, including the floor. For m0=C B^2 W>=1,
  log m0=log C+2log B+log W<=log C+3W,
as B>=1 and W>=1. The function (1+log m)/m is nonincreasing for m>=1. Consequently, for every m>=m0,
  (1+log m)/m<=(log C+4)/(C B^2).                          (2.2)
Since eps_alg=max(eps,1/m) and tlog(e/t) is increasing on (0,1),
  eps_alg log(e/eps_alg)
  <=max(eps log(e/eps),(1+log m)/m).
Equation (2.2) makes the floor term arbitrarily small compared with 1/B by selecting the fixed C large enough; C/log C tends to infinity. This also forces 20eps_alg<gamma, beta<1/2, and the scalar contamination/sample conditions after c is chosen sufficiently small.
