Main result
-----------
For a nontrivial family of higher-molecularity weakly reversible reaction
networks, a physical-time Foster function proves recurrence for every
strictly positive finite rate vector, even when the prior strict linear
small-gain LP is infeasible. A multiplicative catalyst-free boundary
correction strengthens the pointwise result to a common geometric drift
over any compact box of strictly positive rates. This yields uniform
stationary tightness and uniform exponential moments of substrate counts
over that box.

This is a family-specific internal proof candidate. It is not a solution to
the general higher-dimensional weakly reversible recurrence problem, and
no novelty or priority claim is made.

The family has finite r,s>=1, integer k_ji>=1, and reactions

  0 <-> A_j,
  A_j <-> A_j+B_i,
  A_j+B_i <-> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i,

with strictly positive finite rate constants and standard stochastic
mass-action propensities. Its reaction graph is one linkage class and is
weakly reversible. Its stoichiometric rank is r+s; the full nonnegative
integer lattice is one irreducible closed class. The reverse autocatalytic
source has molecularity k_ji+2>=3.

For each fixed rate vector, Section 2 of
leaves/exponential_foster_outside_gain/RESULT.txt uses

  V_add(a,b)=sum_j lambda_j a_j + sum_i R_i^(b_i) + K 1_{A=0}

to show that {LV_add>-1} is finite. The exponential substrate weight makes
the q_ji a_j b_i removal dominate at large B; the k_ji+1 degree reverse
factorial reaction controls large A whenever a substrate is present; and
strict catalyst death controls A when B=0. Localized stopped Dynkin in
physical time proves nonexplosion before the continuous-time Foster
criterion is applied. The proof is pointwise in rates; it alone gives no
exponential stationary moment.

The stronger compact-rate-box extension is in
leaves/geometric_rate_box.txt. It uses

  V_geo(a,b)=A+(1+1_{A=0}) sum_i R^(b_i).

For a compact box with all rates bounded above and away from zero, choose R
so delta_min^- - s p_max^+(R-1)>0. The q-term eventually dominates every
positive exponential and linear contribution uniformly. Splitting
substrate states into a finite box and a large-coordinate region gives one
geometric drift LV_geo<=C-cV_geo with constants independent of the rate
vector. This yields a common invariant V moment bound, hence uniform
stationary tightness and a uniform R-exponential moment for each substrate
coordinate. Catalyst counts have a uniform first moment. No uniform
mixing-time bound is asserted.

Exact non-complex-balanced witness outside the LP
--------------------------------------------------
For r=1,s=2,k_1i=1, choose

  alpha=delta=1, p_i=1, q_i=2, u_i=1, v_i=2.

The linear certificate requires lambda>w_1+w_2 and 2w_i>lambda for both
i, which is impossible. The rates are not complex balanced: 0<->A forces
a=1, while 2A+B_i<->A+B_i forces a=1/2. The exponential Foster proof
therefore gives a full-rank, three-species, weakly reversible,
non-complex-balanced, LP-infeasible positive-recurrence fixture. Its
stationary law is unique but not computed in closed form. This refutes only
necessity of that linear certificate within the specified family; it is
not a transient, null-recurrent, or explosive example.
