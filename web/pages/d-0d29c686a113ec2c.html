# Independent audit: faithful six-qubit collisions for the fixed local X/Z SLD frame

Status: **proved existence**, using an independently reconstructed exact rational nonzero-minor certificate and an analytic contraction argument. This is an original team deduction, not a claim of Dong–Leng, arXiv:2609.28391v1. It does not contradict their high-temperature sufficient theorem. It disproves the proposed unrestricted extension to every faithful target for the same fixed local X/Z frame.

## Question and interface

For six qubits, supply all twelve physical Hermitian observables \(A_a\in\{X_i,Z_i:1\le i\le6\}\). Their ordinary commutant is scalar. Given a faithful target \(\sigma\), define its SLD score by
\[
 -i[A_a,\sigma]=\tfrac12\{\sigma,S_a^\sigma\}.
\]
Does this entire score tuple identify every faithful \(\sigma\)? **No.** There exist distinct faithful \(\rho,\sigma\) with all twelve scores equal. The proof below supplies a precise family and a positive, though extraordinarily conservative, existence interval.

For \(\sigma=U\operatorname{diag}(p_i)U^T\), use eigenbasis coordinates and define
\[
 K_{a,ij}=\frac{2\sqrt{p_i p_j}}{p_i+p_j}(U^TA_aU)_{ij}.
\]
The independently derived fiber criterion is
\[
 S_a^\rho=S_a^\sigma\ \forall a
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \rho=\sqrt\sigma Y\sqrt\sigma,\quad Y>0,\quad
 \operatorname{Tr}(\sigma Y)=1,\quad [Y,UK_aU^T]=0\ \forall a.
\]
Indeed, the score equation is \((A-iS/2)\rho=\rho(A+iS/2)\); for the target these two operators equal \(\sqrt\sigma K\sigma^{-1/2}\) and \(\sigma^{-1/2}K\sqrt\sigma\). Thus equality is exactly the commutation condition. The source locations are the SLD definition and Appendix A.1–A.2 of the saved 2609.28391v1 snapshot; the unrestricted fiber equivalence is our deduction.

## Exact base point and verified Jacobian

Let \(d=64\), \(m=12(d-1)=756\), with indices \(0,\ldots,63\). Split the qubits into two triples. Let \(P_i\) run in lexicographic order over \(\{I,X,Z,J\}^{\otimes3}\), where \(J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\). The vectors \(\operatorname{vec}(P_i)/\sqrt8\) form a real Bell orthonormal basis. Every vector is maximally entangled across the split.

Take the two explicit integer skew matrices in the certificate
`results/b_compute/cycle2/bell_jacobian_seed261006006_p65521_cols2016.json` and set
\[
 V=(I-S_V)(I+S_V)^{-1},\qquad W=(I-S_W)(I+S_W)^{-1},
 \qquad U_0=(V\otimes W)\operatorname{Bell}.
\]
These are rational orthogonal half rotations. Hence
\(B_a=U_0^TA_aU_0\) has exactly zero diagonal: the reduced state of every rotated Bell column is \(I/8\), and each local Pauli is traceless.

Let \(E^{ij}=e_ie_j^T-e_je_i^T\), \(i<j\). Retain the 756 distinct coordinate pairs selected by `pivot_columns_full`, where the full ordering is lexicographic over the 2016 pairs. Define
\[
 \Omega(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{756}x_k E^{i_kj_k},\qquad
 U(x)=U_0e^{\Omega(x)}.
\]
Introduce an analytic scalar filter around \(s=0\),
\[
 C_{ii}(s)=1,\qquad
 C_{ij}(s)=\frac{2s^{|i-j|}}{1+s^{2|i-j|}}\quad(i\ne j),
 \qquad K_a(x,s)=C(s)\circ U(x)^TA_aU(x).
\]
Define 756 real equations, for \(a=1,\ldots,12\) and \(i=0,\ldots,62\),
\[
 F_{a,i}(x,s)=\sum_j K_{a,ij}(x,s)
 -\frac1{64}\sum_{k,j}K_{a,kj}(x,s).
\]
They say exactly that all row sums of every \(K_a\) coincide. The omitted row follows because all 64 centered row sums add to zero. Since \(C(0)=I\), \(F(0,0)=0\). The derivative of \(U(x)^TA_aU(x)\) at zero is \([B_a,E^{ij}]\), whose diagonal equals \(-2B_{a,ij}\) at vertex \(i\) and \(+2B_{a,ij}\) at vertex \(j\). Its diagonal mean is zero. Thus the square Jacobian \(\mathcal J=D_xF(0,0)\) is precisely the selected incidence-weighted minor.

## Quantified analytic existence, beyond numerical conditioning

The usual real analytic implicit-function theorem already gives \(x(s)\) solving all equations for sufficiently small \(|s|\). The following bounds provide an explicit positive interval without numerically inverting a badly conditioned state.

Each rational Jacobian entry has absolute value at most 2 and denominator dividing \(D\). Since its determinant is nonzero, \(|\det\mathcal J|\ge D^{-m}\). Expansion of a cofactor gives
\[
 \|\mathcal J^{-1}\|_\infty\le L,
 \qquad L=m!\,2^{m-1}D^m.
\]
This integer bound is extremely loose, but exact and finite.

For real skew \(\Omega\), its exponential is orthogonal. The integral derivative formula for the exponential shows that each coordinate derivative of \(U(x)\) has operator norm at most 1, and each mixed second derivative at most 1. Therefore entries of the first and second derivatives of \(U(x)^TA_aU(x)\) are bounded by 2 and 4, respectively. For \(|s|\le1/2\), \(|C_{ij}(s)|\le1\), \(|C'_{ij}(s)|\le2\), and \(|C_{ij}(s)-C_{ij}(0)|\le2|s|\). Accounting for the centered row sum and the induced infinity matrix norm gives
\[
 \|F_s(x,s)\|_\infty\le4d,
 \qquad
 \|D_xF(x,s)-\mathcal J\|_\infty
 \le8dm\bigl(m\|x\|_\infty+|s|\bigr).
\]
Set
\[
 r=\frac1{32dLm^2},\qquad
 \epsilon=\frac1{256d^2L^2m^2}.
\]
For \(|s|\le\epsilon\), the map
\(T_s(x)=x-\mathcal J^{-1}F(x,s)\)
is a contraction of factor at most \(1/2\) on the closed cube \(\|x\|_\infty\le r\): the Jacobian error is at most \(1/(2L)\), and
\(\|T_s(0)\|_\infty\le4dL\epsilon=r/2\).
It maps the cube into itself. Iteration from zero converges to a unique solution there. These bounds justify every \(0<s<\epsilon\), rather than only an unquantified numerical suggestion. Analyticity of the solution also follows from the implicit-function theorem.

## Faithful physical states and exact score collision

For any such positive \(s\), let
\[
 p_i=\frac{s^{2i}}{\sum_{j=0}^{63}s^{2j}},\qquad
 \sigma_s=U(x(s))\operatorname{diag}(p_i)U(x(s))^T.
\]
All \(p_i\) are strictly positive. Their genuine SLD filter equals \(C_{ij}(s)\) exactly, since
\(2\sqrt{p_ip_j}/(p_i+p_j)=2s^{|i-j|}/(1+s^{2|i-j|})\).
The equation \(F=0\) ensures that \(\mathbf1\) is a common eigenvector of all real symmetric \(K_a\). Therefore
\[
 P_s=U(x(s))\frac{\mathbf1\mathbf1^T}{64}U(x(s))^T
\]
commutes with all physical filtered generators. In the target eigenbasis its diagonal is constantly \(1/64\), so \(\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_s P_s)=1/64\).

For any \(-64/63<t<64\), set
\[
 Y_t=I+t(P_s-I/64),\qquad
 \rho_{s,t}=\sqrt{\sigma_s}Y_t\sqrt{\sigma_s}.
\]
The eigenvalues of \(Y_t\) are \(1+63t/64\) on \(P_s\) and \(1-t/64\) on its complement. Hence \(\rho_{s,t}\) is faithful and normalized. For \(t\ne0\), it differs from \(\sigma_s\); the fiber criterion proves all twelve scores coincide. One may fix \(t=1\). All constraints are real because the supplied physical observables and the chosen eigenbasis are real; there are no omitted imaginary row constraints.

## Scope, nearest prior result and next discriminating step

The closest paper mechanism is Dong–Leng's state-dependent filtered-commutant sufficient criterion, not a universal assertion that ordinary irreducibility is enough. Earlier team reflection examples proved that ordinary irreducibility can fail for freely chosen involution frames; they did not retain Pauli tensor relations. The present certificate retains the exact fixed physical six-qubit X/Z frame and rules out the all-faithful extension. It complements our positive theorem for Clifford-diagonal/stabilizer targets. No external novelty search has yet certified priority for this counterexample method.

This result concerns exact identifiability with the full target score tuple already supplied. It gives no measurement protocol or sample-complexity advantage. The target can be written as a finite-temperature Gibbs state of a generally dense Hamiltonian; the construction does not preserve bounded-degree locality of the Hamiltonian, uniformly bounded local terms or the source's high-temperature regime. The faithful spectrum is extremely spread, and the explicit interval is not a practical temperature estimate. The complete local X/Y/Z frame remains unresolved by this real construction because local Y adds imaginary constraints absent here.

Concrete downstream use: any theorem or learning objective that assumes local X/Z score identification for arbitrary faithful noncommuting targets must add a condition on the filtered commutant or restrict the model class. The next falsifiable step is an interval-certified continuation at a moderate positive \(s\), or an exact lower-dimensional construction, to determine how far this collision can move from the singular limiting spectrum. A separate full-X/Y/Z Jacobian or obstruction is required for that stronger measurement interface.
