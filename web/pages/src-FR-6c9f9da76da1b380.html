## 1. The incoming trace obstruction applies explicitly to the heat-certified Fstar

Use precisely the FE definition, with A's columns x,y:

    u=|x|², v=|y|², w=x·y, q=det A,
    Fstar(A)=u²+v²+4w²+(7/2)q²−(29/10)q(u+v).

Thus Fstar(I)=−3/10. The main FO correction to this value is correct. An early message from this audit mistakenly gave −13/10; that arithmetic error was corrected before any saved theorem or verification. The final constants below are exact.

Identify R² with C. On an open strip of width T=1+log 2 define

    φ(x,y)=r(x)e^{iy},
    r(x)=x/2                      for 0≤x≤1,
    r(x)=(1/2)e^{x−1}             for 1≤x≤1+log 2.

This is an actual compatible potential, from zero to U+(y)=e^{iy}. It is piecewise smooth and in W^{1,4}. Interior corners may be smoothed while retaining the endpoints and taking the action limit.

On the first segment, left SO(2) invariance gives Fstar(Dφ)=2^{-4}Fstar(diag(1,x)). Direct integration yields

    ∫₀¹ Fstar(diag(1,x)) dx
      = ∫₀¹ [1+x⁴+(7/2)x²−(29/10)x(1+x²)] dx
      =23/120.

On the exponential segment, Dφ=r(x)R_y I, so its action is

    Fstar(I)·(1−2^{-4})/4.

Consequently the whole entry action and fourth-power gradient energy are

    W_entry = −3/40 + (4/15)·2^{-4} = −7/120,
    E_entry = 2^{-4}·(28/15)+(1−2^{-4}) =253/240.

The return potential ψ(x,y)=φ(T−x,−y) goes from U−(y)=e^{-iy} to zero. Its gradient is −Dφ(T−x,−y); even quartic homogeneity therefore gives the same action and norm energy. These are two different oriented endpoints, not a negative closed cycle.

For every positive integer N rescale

    φ_N(x,y)=N^{-3/4}φ(Nx,Ny),   0≤x≤T/N.

The gradients scale by N^{1/4}, their fourth powers by N, and strip width by 1/N. The action is still −7/120. Thus every dominating storage P on complete profiles must satisfy

    P(N^{-3/4}e^{iNy})−P(0) ≤−7/120,
    P(N^{-3/4}e^{-iNy})−P(0) ≥+7/120.

These profile sequences tend strongly to zero in subcritical W^{s,4}, s<3/4, and weakly to zero in critical W^{3/4,4}. Hence no such P can be subcritical continuous, or weakly lower or upper semicontinuous at zero in the critical topology. The same incoming theorem also excludes smooth first-derivative local densities at B=0: Fstar(tI)=−3t⁴/10 cannot be bounded below by κ det(tI)=κt² for all t. This does not exclude nonlocal, orientation-sensitive, strongly critical storage.

    ∫Fstar(Dφ) ≥(1/36000)∫|Dφ|⁴.

## 2. A general bath-spoof theorem for the incoming exchange assay

Let A denote the followed data-reference register RS and E the resident environment. Fold a fixed continuation and its bounded score into an effect 0≤K≤I on A⊗E. For a prepared A state σ define the conditional bath effect

    K_σ=Tr_A[(σ⊗I_E)K],
    β(K)=sup_σ [λ_max(K_σ)−λ_min(K_σ)].

K_σ is a Hermitian effect. For the ideal decorrelated product σ⊗τ, an otherwise perfect exchange followed by an unobserved bath unitary V changes the score by Tr[K_σ(VτV†−τ)]. The sharp worst-case absolute change, over τ and bath unitaries V, is the spectral diameter of K_σ. It is attained with τ an extremal eigenstate and V an involution swapping the maximum and minimum eigenvectors. Thus the universal worst-case score spoof is exactly β(K), with 0≤β(K)≤1.

Every implemented two-copy gate

    W=SWAP_{S1,S2}⊗V_{E1}⊗V_{E2},   V²=I,

has exactly the ideal reduced action on all accessible R/S registers, even for arbitrary joint inputs. It also satisfies W²=I. Reduced-register process tomography and a perfect double-swap echo therefore cannot distinguish all gates achieving that sharp spoof. The example can begin with independent, completely uncorrelated A/E replicas; no true conditional correlation is needed for the false signal.

Moreover,

    β(K)=0  iff  K=K_A⊗I_E.

If β=0, every K_σ is scalar. Pairing with every traceless Hermitian E operator, and using the linear span of density matrices on A, implies that K has only the identity bath component. The converse is immediate. Such an effect has identical expectation on any ρ_AE and ρ_A⊗ρ_E, so it cannot measure the correlation-removal effect. Therefore a nontrivial correlation-sensitive continuation cannot also be universally insensitive to every hidden bath operation invisible to a reduced gate audit. This is a general identifiability obstruction, extending the incoming single Bell-state counterexample. It is not an assertion of historical novelty.

For the incoming Bell/CZ witness, K_Φ+=diag(1,0), β(K)=1, and V=X_E changes the score from 1 to 0 while preserving the full RS marginal. `verify_transfers.py` reconstructs this exactly with rational matrices.

## 3. Explicit source current for the open PR85 remote-response family

PR85's one-excitation factor is

    J=Σ_{i=1}^{m−1} c_i q_i* q_i+e e_m e_m*,
    q_i=e_i*−[i/(i+1)]e_{i+1}*,   c_i=(i+1)/i,
    b=e_1.

The weighted Thomson current can be written without an inverse:

    f_i=1/i  (1≤i<m),   f_terminal=1/m.

It satisfies Σ q_i* f_i+e_m f_terminal=e_1 exactly: the internal coefficients telescope. Its dual energy is

    Σ_{i=1}^{m−1}|f_i|²/c_i+|f_terminal|²/e
      =Σ1/[i(i+1)]+1/(m²e)
      =(m−1)/m+1/(m²e).

This equals the source response, so it is the exact minimum-energy completion. In N816's Euclidean physical-row notation the same witness has w_i=1/sqrt(i(i+1)) and w_terminal=1/(m sqrt(e)). It supplies a concrete source current beyond the late FO adjudication's abstract J=LH^+b recipe. Its O(m) arithmetic cost matches the tridiagonal incumbent, so no algorithmic advantage follows.

With e known only in [e_minus,e_plus]⊂(0,∞), the sharp response interval is

    [(m−1)/m+1/(m²e_plus), (m−1)/m+1/(m²e_minus)].

Feasibility of the weighted current is independent of e, but its energy requires a positive acquired lower endpoint. If e_minus=0, no finite uniform response upper endpoint exists. Exact gap-free completion therefore moves the unresolved physical requirement to a source-specific terminal coefficient; it does not erase acquisition precision. PR85's evolution-only Ω(1/e) interrogation lower bound remains compatible with this exact symbolic completion.
