# Revision 01 — finite-dimensional duality and conversion audit

UTC: 2026-10-07. Independent follow-up to immutable INITIAL.

## 1. Measurement kernels as a compact convex set

Fix one finite-dimensional direct sum \(\mathcal A=\bigoplus_bM_{d_b}\) and a faithful trace \(\tau(A)=\sum_b w_b\operatorname{Tr}(A_b)/d_b\), with all \(w_b>0\). A rank-one POVM can be written as a finite positive measure \(\mu\) on the disjoint union of the projective spaces of the blocks, with moment constraint

\[
\int P\,d\mu(P)=I_{\mathcal A}.
\]

The measure's total mass is \(\sum_b d_b\), since the ordinary matrix trace of the moment identity has that value. The disjoint union of projective spaces is compact. Thus the set of such measures with this fixed moment is weak-* compact and convex. For fixed \(\tau\), the function \(P\mapsto\tau(P)\) has positive minimum \(\min_b w_b/d_b\), so the canonical score-map integrand \(P\mapsto\tau(PH)P/\tau(P)\) is continuous. Its image, the set of rank-one canonical measurement maps, is compact and convex. This is a per-dimension compactness statement; its modulus and outcome representation can deteriorate when dimensions grow or \(\min_b w_b/d_b\to0\).

A coin-labelled disjoint union of POVMs realizes convex combinations of their maps. Merging outcomes without retaining the coin does not in general realize that convex combination. This retained classical outcome is part of the protocol and is the precise reason affine minimax applies.

## 2. Separation proof of the Dirichlet all-frame dual

Work on the real Hilbert space of Hermitian elements modulo the identity direction, with inner product \(\langle H,K\rangle_\tau=\tau(HK)\). Identity has zero energy for both \(I-\Psi_M\) and \(I-\Phi\), so it can be removed. Set \(D_M=I-\Psi_M\), \(G=I-\Phi\), and let \(\mathcal D=\{D_M\}\), compact convex by the preceding frame-measure argument. The requested comparator is \(D_M\preceq cG\) for one \(M\).

If \(\mathcal D\) is disjoint from \(cG-\mathcal K\), where \(\mathcal K\) is the cone of positive self-adjoint operators, strong separation gives a self-adjoint functional \(Y\) with

\[
\inf_{D\in\mathcal D}\operatorname{Tr}(YD)>c\operatorname{Tr}(YG).
\]

The supremum of the functional over \(cG-\mathcal K\) is finite only if \(Y\succeq0\). Conversely, if the displayed strict inequality holds for a PSD \(Y\), no \(D\preceq cG\) exists. Decompose \(Y=\sum_j\lambda_j|H_j\rangle\langle H_j|\), with \(\lambda_j\ge0\) and Hermitian \(H_j\). Then

\[
\inf_M\sum_j\lambda_j\langle H_j,D_MH_j\rangle
=\sum_j\lambda_j\tau(H_j^2)-\sup_M\sum_y\frac{\sum_j\lambda_j\tau(M_yH_j)^2}{\tau(M_y)}.
\]

Rank-one refinement identifies the supremum with the best reference-ensemble capture. Hence universal validity of the all-frame variance inequality is equivalent to existence of one \(M\) satisfying the operator comparison. A frame's individual optimizer is not the separator's common optimizer. For the probability-prior form, scale the \(H_j\)'s and normalize the nonnegative weights; arbitrary Hermitian test vectors are covered.

## 3. Rechecked bridge from arbitrary EB reconstruction to \(q(E)\)

This checks a potentially hidden assumption that the EB map fix \(\sigma\). Let \(\Lambda=R\circ M\) be any EB channel with half-trace error at most \(e\) on \(\{\sigma\}\cup\{\rho_H:H\in E\}\). Pinch the effects into \(\mathcal A\); this preserves probabilities on promised block-diagonal inputs. Pinch outputs into the same block algebra; this fixes targets and cannot increase trace error. Write \(d_H=\|\Lambda(\rho_H)-\rho_H\|_1\) and \(d_\sigma=\|\Lambda(\sigma)-\sigma\|_1\), both at most \(2e\).

Sandwiched-2 data processing for the measurement followed by preparation gives

\[
Q_{M\sigma}(M\rho_H)\ge Q_{\Lambda\sigma}(\Lambda\rho_H),
\]

while \(Q_\sigma(\rho_H)-Q_{M\sigma}(M\rho_H)=\ell_M(H)\). The supplied sandwich continuity estimate applies to the *channel pair* \((\Lambda\rho_H,\Lambda\sigma)\); CP preserves \((1\pm\theta)\)-domination of that pair. It yields

\[
\ell_M(H)\le 2\theta(d_H+d_\sigma)+6\theta^2\sqrt{d_\sigma}
\le 8\theta e+6\theta^2\sqrt{2e}.
\]

Thus the same POVM from one EB factorization gives the uniform bound over all labels, even when \(\Lambda\sigma\ne\sigma\). The reference input is essential to this stated bound. No \(\lambda_{\min}(\sigma)^{-1}\) appears. Conversely, a canonical map with \(q(E)\) has \(\|(I-\Psi_M)H\|_{1,\tau}\le\sqrt{\langle H,(I-\Psi_M)H\rangle_\tau}\), since \(0\preceq I-\Psi_M\preceq I\) and \(\|Z\|_{1,\tau}\le\|Z\|_{2,\tau}\). Therefore its single EB map has half-trace error at most \(\sqrt{q(E)}/2\) on the whole experiment.

## 4. Label-size quantifiers

For each finite \(n\), the primal minimax is exactly over every label in \(E_n\), regardless of \(|E_n|\). A finite-dimensional dual witness exists with at most \(D_n\) vectors where \(D_n\le\sum_b d_{n,b}^2-1\) is the real dimension of the traceless Hermitian test space (rank decomposition suffices). This support bound grows with the algebra dimension. Thus a proof establishing bounds only for each fixed support size \(m\) does not imply a bound for \(q(E_n)\) when the family and algebra grow. A uniform constant Dirichlet comparison is genuinely all-frame. A weaker target must supply a modulus uniform over the actual growing family, or assume and quantify a covering/entropy condition.

This distinction matches the supplied N46/R01 theorem: the exact family-level broadcasting-to-EB implication is stated under uniform trace-distance total boundedness, while its explicit growing-entropy route requires \(b_n\log N_n(a_n)\to0\) for some \(a_n\to0\). The N61/R16 ladder only demonstrates the importance of complexity control for its own unrestricted experiment; it lies outside the fixed-sandwich hypotheses and cannot be used as a counterexample here.

## 5. Exact benchmark and source boundary

For \(E=\{X,Y,Z\}\), prior \(p_i=1/3\), every pure qubit state has \(\sum_i\langle\sigma_i\rangle^2=1\), so its total average variance is \(2/3\). The six-effect Pauli POVM \(M_{\pm i}=(I\pm\sigma_i)/6\) has \(\Psi_M\) equal to the depolarizing map of Bloch shrink \(1/3\), and reaches that value. The primary-source universal 1-to-2 qubit cloner has marginal fidelity \(5/6\), hence isotropic Bloch shrink \(2F-1=2/3\). For this selfcompatible \(\Phi\), its averaged Dirichlet energy on the frame is \(1/3\), so the ratio is exactly 2. This is a sharp lower bound on any universal constant, not a counterexample to \(c=2\).

Primary references read: Bužek–Hillery, arXiv:quant-ph/9607018 (universal qubit cloner); Gao–Li–Marvian–Rouzé, arXiv:2302.02341, Theorem 1.5 (Petz recovery from symmetric-inverse \(\chi^2\) loss); Beigi, arXiv:1306.5920 (sandwiched Rényi DPI for \(\alpha>1\)). The finite-dimensional minimax and Pauli calculations above are direct derivations. No general Dirichlet theorem, asymptotic rounding result, or novelty claim has been established.
