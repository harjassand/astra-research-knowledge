## Evidence boundaries

1. The SI algebra errors are directly visible in the primary supplement and follow by ordinary inversion. They do not by themselves show the released forward solver or its fixed-alpha trajectories are wrong.
2. The alpha physical-unit issue is conditional because Table S1 omits alpha units while the executable code uses a converted internal stress. Keep it `UNRESOLVED` unless author metadata or a corrected source establishes the intended units.
3. The scalar exponential-tail dispersion prediction uses the scalar KPP reduction and its front-like initial-tail assumptions. The full numerical solver is bounded, coupled, and nonuniform; the exact run is a finite-time diagnostic only.
4. Article text says one parameter was fitted to expansion velocities and compares spatial profiles; SI Table S1 says alpha was fitted to front velocity using Eq. 32. No trial/condition split or independent held-out front-speed evaluation is provided in the source package. Do not call BAPN a held-out *speed* validation.
5. BAPN is an in-vivo collagen-crosslinking intervention with measured local stiffness, collagen fiber area, reporter gradient, and endpoint bone area. The article does not report a matched BAPN front-speed trajectory or a quantitative BAPN-parameterized PDE prediction; the area result is directionally consistent but not a test of Eq. 32, alpha, or KPP speed selection.
