1. FIXED-POINT ALGEBRA IS ABELIAN AND ITS LIMIT MAP IS EB

Assume the INITIAL interface: finite-dimensional (A,tau), faithful normalized
trace, UCP tau-preserving positive L2-selfadjoint Phi, and a compatible UCP
map J:A tensor A -> A. Let F=ker(I-Phi), and let P=1_{ {1} }(Phi) be the L2
spectral projection at eigenvalue one.

First F is a C*-subalgebra. If x is fixed, Kadison-Schwarz gives
Phi(x*x)-Phi(x)*Phi(x)>=0, while its tau-trace is
tau(x*x)-tau(Phi(x)*Phi(x))=0. Faithfulness makes the defect zero; applying
the same argument to xx* puts x in the multiplicative domain of Phi. Thus
products and adjoints of fixed elements are fixed.

To prove commutativity, dilate J(z)=V* pi(z)V and set
pi_1(a)=pi(a tensor I), pi_2(b)=pi(I tensor b). For fixed a,b, equality in
Schwarz for the two marginals gives pi_1(a)V=Va and pi_2(b)V=Vb. The two
represented tensor factors commute, so

  ab=V* pi_1(a)pi_2(b)V=V* pi_2(b)pi_1(a)V=ba.

The middle expression equals ab by first using pi_2(b)V=Vb; the last equals
ba by first using pi_1(a)V=Va. Hence F is abelian.

Since Phi is selfadjoint with spectrum in [0,1], Phi^n converges in finite
dimension to P. Therefore P is UCP, tau-preserving and idempotent, with range
F. It is the trace-preserving conditional expectation onto the abelian
subalgebra F. If q_1,...,q_s are the minimal projections of F, its exact
formula is

  P(x)=sum_i tau(q_i x) q_i/tau(q_i).

In physical coordinates rho=sigma x for central faithful reference sigma,
the corresponding channel is

  P_phys(rho)=sum_i Tr(q_i rho) [sigma q_i/tau(q_i)],

whose effects q_i sum to I and whose preparations are states. Thus the
fixed-point limit is an actual EB channel, not merely a commuting range
map. Exact fixed spectral mass is removed from every Q_k in INITIAL.

2. A GAP-DEPENDENT QUANTITATIVE LIFT

For Hermitian A, spectral calculus gives the exact input profile

  ||Phi^m(A)-P(A)||_(2,tau)^2
       = integral_[0,1) lambda^(2m) d mu_A(lambda).

Consequently

  ||Phi^m-P||_(infinity -> 1,tau)
       <= sqrt(kappa_m),
  kappa_m := sup_(A=A*, ||A||<=1)
       integral_[0,1) lambda^(2m) d mu_A(lambda).

This is a dimension-free sufficient criterion if a sequence has a certified
uniform profile kappa_m -> 0. In particular, if all nonfixed spectrum lies
in [0,1-gamma] for one gamma>0, then

  ||Phi^m-P||_(infinity -> 1,tau) <= (1-gamma)^m.

Because P is EB, this proves beta_m -> 0 on uniformly gapped subclasses.
This is conditional, not a resolution without a gap. The c05_l05 two-sector
instrument example has a nonconstant central eigenvalue (1-delta)^2 with
delta arbitrarily small. Its EB sector-measurement map preserves that slow
classical mode, while P erases it. Choosing delta much smaller than 1/m
shows that this particular P-based approximation is not uniform. It does
not show that Phi^m is far from every EB map; c05_l05 explicitly supplies an
EB comparator retaining the mode.

3. SLOW-SPECTRUM COMMUTATION

The exact input-sensitive theorem yields a local statement about the whole
near-fixed Hilbert spectral sector. Let eta in [0,1), and take Hermitian
contractions A,C with spectral measures supported on [1-eta,1]. Then

  Q_0(A)=tau(A*A)-tau(Phi(A)*Phi(A))
         = integral (1-lambda^2)d mu_A(lambda)
         <= (2 eta-eta^2)||A||_2^2 <= 2 eta,

and similarly for C. Applying the m=1 case of INITIAL gives

  ||[Phi(A),Phi(C)]||_2 <= 2 sqrt(2 eta).

Moreover ||Phi(A)-A||_2<=eta and ||Phi(C)-C||_2<=eta. Since all four
operators are contractions, tracial L2 multiplication yields

  ||[A,C]||_2 <= 2 sqrt(2 eta)+4 eta.                 (5)

Thus the near-fixed spectral sector is pairwise almost commuting as eta->0,
with an explicit dimension-free bound and without a spectral gap. The
spectral projection defining this sector is only an L2 projection; it is
not asserted to be CP, and (5) does not give a single EB map for a growing
sector. The unresolved step is still simultaneous CP-compatible rounding
of arbitrarily many directions, not pairwise commutation.

5. OPEN GATE AFTER THE SECOND ATTACK

The fixed spectral fibre has a canonical EB map; uniformly gapped fibres
have an explicit exponential lift; and any near-fixed spectral subspace is
dimension-free pairwise almost commuting. The missing theorem is a single
trace-preserving EB channel approximating Phi^m on the entire Hermitian
operator-norm unit ball when the number of slow directions and their trace
covering complexity grow without bound and no gap or anchor frame is given.
Hilbert spectral projections are not CP filters, so separately pinching
spectral bands does not yet compose into a legal channel. No counterexample
to this exact channel-level statement was obtained.
