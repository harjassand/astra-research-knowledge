SUMMARY

The unphased random-sign estimator has exponential relative variance even on
a dense, full-rank rational family arbitrarily close to a perfect matching.
For n=2k and F_delta=M+delta J (M is a direct sum of k swaps, J is all
ones, any rational delta>0), its exact relative variance is 2^k-1. This
extends the sparse swap-block example already reported by c03_s02 to dense
matrices, and shows that the low-k estimator's exponential-in-k cost is not
an artifact of zero entries.

The c03_s01 alternating real/imaginary phase-matched estimator behaves very
differently on this same family: its sample is exactly constant for every
sign word, for every rational delta>0. I also independently derived the
general certificate's uniform bounded-sample consequence using Weyl's
singular-value inequality. Finally, the certificate is sufficient but not
necessary: delta=1/(16n) makes F_delta dense/full-rank and relatively close
to M, yet fails the current deterministic residual certificate for n>=6;
the phased estimator remains exactly constant. The code's resulting
NOT_CERTIFIED is a conservative false negative, not an incorrect answer.

1. EXACT DENSE COUNTEREXAMPLE FOR THE UNPHASED ESTIMATOR

Index sites 0,...,2k-1 and let M_{2j,2j+1}=M_{2j+1,2j}=1, all other
entries zero. Let J=11^T and F_delta=M+delta J, delta>0 rational. Every
matrix entry is positive, and

  det(F_delta)=det(M)(1+delta 1^T M^{-1}1)=(-1)^k(1+2k delta),

so F_delta is dense and full-rank. Its relative Frobenius perturbation from
M is

  ||F_delta-M||_F^2 / ||M||_F^2 = delta^2 (2k).

Thus, for example delta=1/(64 n^2), n=2k, approaches M in relative
Frobenius norm at rate 1/(64 n^(3/2)).

At full sector k=n/2, every legal pair (I,J) has J=I^c, so

  c_k(F_delta)=sum_{|I|=k}|det F_delta[I,I^c]|^2.

If I contains both vertices of any matching pair, it also omits both
vertices of some other pair (because |I|=k). The two rows in the
complementary minor from the full pair have no M entries in I^c, and both
are the same all-delta row. Its determinant is zero. Otherwise I selects
one vertex from every matching pair. After permuting rows/columns,
F_delta[I,I^c]=I_k+delta J_k, whose determinant is 1+k delta. There are
exactly 2^k such I, hence

  c_k(F_delta)=2^k(1+k delta)^2.                         (1)

For the original unphased estimator, A_s=D_sF_delta-F_delta^T D_s.
Since F_delta is symmetric, A_s=D_sF_delta-F_delta D_s. Its entries within
the plus-sign and minus-sign classes vanish. If the plus class P is not of
size k, rank(A_s)<=2 min(|P|,2k-|P|)<2k and its full Pfaffian is zero. If
|P|=k, the matrix is off-diagonal in P,P^c blocks and

  |Pf(A_s)|^2 = 4^k |det F_delta[P,P^c]|^2.

The minor calculation above says this is nonzero for exactly 2^k sign words,
with the same value 4^k(1+k delta)^2. Out of 2^(2k) equally likely words,

  X_k(s) = 4^k(1+k delta)^2 with probability 2^(-k),
           0 otherwise.

Its mean is (1), and its relative variance is exactly

  E[X_k^2]/c_k^2 - 1 = 2^k - 1.                         (2)

For M independent samples, the empirical mean is zero with probability
(1-2^(-k))^M. Since a zero estimate has relative error one on this positive
instance, constant-confidence relative estimation by this raw sample mean
requires M=Omega(2^k). This lower bound is only for the unphased IID sample
estimator; F_delta has the explicit closed form (1), so this is not a
hardness result for counting.

2. EXACT PHASE-MATCHED VARIANCE ON THE SAME DENSE FAMILY

Use c03_s01's alternating phases

  phi_{2j}=s_{2j},  phi_{2j+1}=i s_{2j+1},
  A_phi=Phi F_delta-F_delta^T Phi.

Write A_phi=A_0+delta(phi 1^T-1 phi^T), where A_0=Phi M-M Phi is a direct
sum of 2x2 skew blocks. In block j its upper-right entry is
c_j=phi_{2j}-phi_{2j+1}; hence |c_j|^2=2 for every sign word and
|Pf(A_0)|^2=2^k.

For invertible skew A, the rank-two Pfaffian update is

  Pf(A+u v^T-v u^T)=Pf(A)(1+v^T A^(-1)u).

Take A=A_0, u=delta phi, v=1. On each pair block,
1^T A_0^(-1)phi contributes
  (phi_{2j}-phi_{2j+1})/c_j=1,
so 1^T A_0^(-1)phi=k. Therefore every phased sample is exactly

  X_k^phase(s)=|Pf(A_phi)|^2=2^k(1+k delta)^2=c_k(F_delta).

Its relative variance is zero for every delta>0, including the dense
near-matching sequence in (2). This exact identity independently checks
that the phase change removes this rare-event failure mode.

3. INDEPENDENT ROBUSTNESS DERIVATION FOR THE ACCEPTED CERTIFICATE

For the real-matching code path, the phase-matching source chooses a fixed
perfect matching G, retaining diagonal entries and real parts a_j,b_j of
each pair of matched entries; it sets beta_j=a_j^2+b_j^2 and
beta_min=min_j beta_j. Let E=F-G and r=||E||_F. For its fixed alternating
real/imaginary phases multiplied by independent site signs,

  A=Phi F-F^T Phi,  A_0=Phi G-G^T Phi,
  ||A-A_0||_2 <= ||A-A_0||_F <= 2r.

The diagonal of G cancels. A_0 is block diagonal with block singular values
sqrt(beta_j), each repeated twice. The exact certificate is

  beta_min>0,   r^2 <= beta_min/(256 k^2).

It implies ||A-A_0||_2<=sqrt(beta_min)/(8k). By Weyl's inequality, after
sorting the k paired singular values, each sigma_j(A) satisfies

  (1-1/(8k))sqrt(beta_j) <= sigma_j(A)
                           <= (1+1/(8k))sqrt(beta_j).

For skew A, X_k(A)=[t^k]sqrt(det(I+t A* A)) is
e_k(sigma_1(A)^2,...,sigma_{n/2}(A)^2). Let
B_k=e_k(beta_1,...,beta_{n/2}), the acquired matching baseline norm.
Termwise comparison in this nonnegative elementary-symmetric sum yields

  (1-1/(8k))^(2k) B_k <= X_k(A) <= (1+1/(8k))^(2k) B_k,
  (3/4)B_k <= X_k(A) <= (4/3)B_k.                     (3)

The lower bound is Bernoulli's inequality. The upper follows from
(1+1/(8k))^(2k)<=exp(1/4)<4/3. Thus every accepted input has a
constant-bounded, positive sample, uniformly in n and k. Using the exact
unbiased identity c=E X, set a=3/4,b=4/3 and mu=c/B_k. The inequality
(X-aB_k)(bB_k-X)>=0 implies

  Var(X)/mu^2 <= (b-mu/B_k)(mu/B_k-a)/(mu/B_k)^2
                <= (b-a)^2/(4ab)=49/576.

Equivalently E X^2/c^2 <=625/576. This is a stronger relative-variance
bound than the simple range ratio (16/9)^2. It gives an O(epsilon^-2)
sample mean bound with constants independent of k, conditional on the
certificate. This derivation is a mathematical audit of the certificate's
stated threshold, not an external peer review.

4. CERTIFICATE BOUNDARY: A SAFE FALSE NEGATIVE

For the same family F_delta, the matching acquired by c03_s01 keeps each
diagonal delta and each matched entry 1+delta. Therefore exactly n(n-2)
off-matching off-diagonal entries remain in E and

  r^2=n(n-2)delta^2,  beta_min=2(1+delta)^2,
  threshold=beta_min/(256k^2)=(1+delta)^2/(32n^2).

With delta=1/(64n^2),
  r^2/threshold=(n-2)/(128n(1+delta)^2)<1/128,
so the certificate accepts with margin.

With delta=1/(16n),
  r^2/threshold=n(n-2)/(8(1+delta)^2)>1  for n>=6.

But this second sequence still has relative Frobenius perturbation squared
delta^2 n=1/(256n)->0. The exact phase identity in Section 2 still gives
zero variance. Thus the current certificate excludes some asymptotically
near-matching dense matrices on which this estimator is perfect. The actual
code path returns NOT_CERTIFIED for these inputs; it makes no wrong estimate.
This is a conservatism boundary, not a certificate soundness failure.

6. BOUNDARIES

The raw estimator is useful in its declared low-k regime: its general
relative second moment is at most C_k=binom(2k,k), which is n^{O(1)} for
k=O(log n). Equation (2) only demonstrates exponential-in-k raw variance
when k grows linearly with n. It does not imply AP-hardness, nor does the
near-matching false-negative family negate the phase-matched FPRAS on its
certified domain.

The phase-matching argument assumes an acquired fixed real matching G with
positive beta_min and the exact residual threshold. The derivation verifies
the sample bound under those hypotheses; acquiring such a matching, proving
that a general F has a certificate, or extending the class to low-rank or
structured residuals are separate questions. The source code's exact
certificate is sufficient, not necessary, as the rank-two dense residual
shows. Full target-complexity hardness, general dense-sector approximation,
and external novelty/priority remain unresolved.
