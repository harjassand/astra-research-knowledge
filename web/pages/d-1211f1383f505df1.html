2. EXACT BRANCHING ISOMETRY, WITHOUT BOSONIZING THE CRITICAL ROOT

Let lambda=(lambda1,lambda2,lambda3) be any partition of n, k=lambda1-lambda2,
m=lambda2-lambda3. V_lambda is the irreducible U(3) carrier. Under U(2) on
the first two coordinates and U(1) on the third, its restriction is

  direct_sum_(s=0..k,t=0..m)
       V^(2)_(lambda1-s,lambda2-t) tensor [weight lambda3+s+t].       (2.1)

This is the multiplicity-one interlacing rule. A short character proof is
available directly from semistandard tableaux: remove entries equal to 3.
Their cells form a horizontal strip, so the residual shape mu satisfies
lambda1>=mu1>=lambda2>=mu2>=lambda3. Summing tableaux gives
s_lambda(x1,x2,x3)=sum_mu s_mu(x1,x2)x3^(|lambda|-|mu|).
Distinct U(2) characters imply (2.1); invariant inner products make the
character intertwiner unitary.

Set H_k=V^(2)_(lambda1,lambda2), dimension k+1, and let F be the bosonic Fock
space over the dual fundamental (C^2)^*. The number-N sector of H_k tensor F
has the multiplicity-one decomposition

  H_k tensor Sym^N((C^2)^*)
    = direct_sum_(s=0..min(k,N)) V^(2)_(lambda1-s,lambda2-N+s).        (2.2)

Indeed V_(lambda1,lambda2)=det^lambda2 Sym^k(C^2),
Sym^N((C^2)^*)=det^(-N) Sym^N(C^2), and
Sym^k tensor Sym^N=direct_sum_s det^s Sym^(k+N-2s). The last identity follows
by multiplying two-variable complete homogeneous characters and telescoping.

Define P_m, on each N sector in (2.2), to retain s>=max(0,N-m). Thus the
retained component has t=N-s<=m; no component remains for N>k+m. There is
a U(2) x U(1) equivariant isometry

  W_lambda: V_lambda -> P_m(H_k tensor F),                         (2.3)

where the third charge on F is lambda3+N. P_m is the identity on the ENTIRE
product number sector whenever N<=m. A dimension check, also exact, is

  dim V_lambda=sum_(s=0..k,t=0..m)(k-s+t+1)
              =(k+1)(m+1)(k+m+2)/2.

The product basis is not the uncoupled basis of the full U(3) action.
Choosing its Clebsch-Gordan/branching phases is needed before deriving the
transverse generators; it does not affect the identity below.

3. EXACT THERMAL IDENTITY AND EXPONENTIALLY SMALL EXTERNAL SUPPORT DEFECT

For any p1>=p2>p3>0, let R_lambda(p)=pi_lambda(diag p)/Tr pi_lambda(diag p).
Let tau_k(p1,p2) be the normalized H_k representation of diag(p1,p2);
up to its determinant scalar this is the spin-k/2 Gibbs density with
geometric ratio p2/p1. Write Theta(q)=(1-q)sum_(u>=0)q^u |u><u|,
q_i=p3/p_i, and

  Omega=tau_k(p1,p2) tensor Theta(q1) tensor Theta(q2).

Then (2.3) yields the exact normalized identity

  W_lambda R_lambda(p) W_lambda^dag
       =P_m Omega P_m / Tr(P_m Omega).                             (3.1)

Proof: on the product representation, the positive diagonal action is
pi_(lambda1,lambda2)(diag(p1,p2)) tensor Gamma(diag(q1,q2)), times the scalar
p3^lambda3. A dual boson removes one first-plane weight and adds one third
weight. Equation (2.2) and the U(1) charge identify these weights with (2.1).
P_m commutes with this action: it is a sum of U(2) irreducible summands, hence
also commutes with the complexified positive diagonal action. Normalizing
removes all scalar factors and proves (3.1).

Since P_m commutes with Omega, the embedded half-trace comparison error is
EXACTLY 1-Tr(P_m Omega). It is bounded by the product thermal mass at N>m.
With q*=max(q1,q2), stochastic domination by two independent geometric(q*)
variables gives

  T(W R_lambda W^dag,Omega)
     <=q*^(m+1)[1+(m+1)(1-q*)] <=(m+2)q*^(m+1).                  (3.2)

The first tail formula follows by summing
(N+1)(1-q*)^2 q*^N from N=m+1 upward. For the supplied p_n, q* is bounded
strictly below 1 for sufficiently large n. Thus m>=m0 n makes (3.2)
exponentially small in n, uniformly over the supplied spectrum interval.

First, the transverse temperatures are anisotropic:

  q2-q1=b c/[sqrt(n)(a^2-c^2/(4n))].                              (4.1)

Their joint internal rotation matters at the proposed precision. For any
isotropic product Theta(q) tensor Theta(q), the bounded observable

  T(Omega, sigma_spin tensor Theta(q) tensor Theta(q))
    >=(1/2)(1-q1)(1-q2)|q2-q1|=Theta(n^(-1/2))                    (4.2)

Second, transverse root generators retain an internal-spin commutator. The
exact U(3) Lie relations give

  [pi(E13)/sqrt(k+m), pi(E32)/sqrt(m)]
           =pi(E12)/sqrt(m(k+m)).                                (4.3)
