SU(3) WALL WEIGHTED COUNT: A NEAR-WALL JACOBI LOWER BOUND
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally reconstructed proof of the small-window count H, with
independent internal audits of the Jacobi estimate and count conversion.
It is conditional on the exact CPV source block formulas and quartic map in
sol_semiclassical_memory/wall_su3/MISSING_LABEL_MAPPING.txt. The compact
potential identity is checked symbolically in
verify_narrow_wall_weighted_count.py. This is not an external validation.

1. RESULT

Let a>=b>=1, N=a+b, B=b+1, and on End(V_(a,b)) put

  L_w=L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)),
  D_w(t)=Tr 1_[0,t](L_w).

There are absolute constants C and c0=1/100 such that

  D_w(t)-1 <= C d[(N^2/B)t^2+N^3t^3],
  d=dim V_(a,b), 0<t<=c0/N.                         (1)

The proof splits at b/N=1/100. If b/N>=1/100, positivity gives a direct
comparison with the ordinary SU(3) Casimir count. If b/N<=1/100, an exact
birth-death decomposition of each low-output CPV Jacobi block proves the
sorted fiber estimate

  kappa_l >= (1/5)[j_l+eta j_l^2],
  eta=(a-b)/N, j_l=delta+l-1, j_l>=1,                 (2)

throughout the entire spectral window c2(p,q)<=N/100. The j=0 slot is
unconstrained and is the only slot discarded in the count. Applying the
already proved implication (2)->(1) in
INTERPOLATING_FIBER_SPECTRAL_GATE.txt proves (1) in the near-wall case.

2. EXACT CPV BLOCK AND BIRTH-DEATH GAUGE

For p<=q with p congruent q modulo 3, write

  delta=(q-p)/3, r=p+delta=(2p+q)/3, Q=r+delta,
  chi=c2(p,q)=r^2+r delta+delta^2+2r+delta,
  j=delta,delta+1,...,Jmax=min(r,b).

The CPV quartic map gives an irreducible real tridiagonal matrix K=L_S on
this multiplicity space. Its two directed entries on edge j<->j+1 are

  A_j=K_(j+1,j)
      =(a-Q+j+1)(j+1)(j+1-delta)
        [(a+2b)/3+2alpha+Q+1-j]/N^2,
  C_j=K_(j,j+1)
      =(Q-j)(r-j)(b-j)
        [(2a+b)/3-2alpha+j+2]/N^2,

where alpha=C3(a,b)/C2(a,b), with

  C2(a,b)=(a^2+ab+b^2+3N)/3,
  C3(a,b)=(a-b)(2a+b+3)(a+2b+3)/18.

For p>=q use the other case in CPV (Eq. (18)): with the same symmetric
definitions delta=|p-q|/3, r=(2 min(p,q)+max(p,q))/3 and Q=r+delta, the
top xi triple is again (N+1-Q,b+1,b+1-delta), while the lower pair
(b-Q,b-r) is swapped to (b-r,b-Q). The diagonal formulas use symmetric
power sums of all six xi_i; the offdiagonal formulas use products over
the lower triple; Lambda and lambda_+/- depend only on their sums. Thus
the same directed rates and potential hold after p and q are exchanged.
This is a source-matrix symmetry, not an identification of GT vectors.
There is also a direct operator reason the p<=q calculation covers the
other half of End(V): the antiunitary J(X)=X^dagger maps the (p,q)
isotypic space to (q,p). Since every S_A is Hermitian,
J ad(S_A) J=-ad(S_A), hence J L_S J=L_S. The two blocks therefore have
identical spectra; counting both only changes an absolute factor.

Both rates are nonnegative, and are positive on every interior edge. After
diagonal symmetrization followed by alternating signs, K is isospectral to
the symmetric Jacobi matrix

  K_sym = G+diag(P_j),
  G=sum_(j=delta)^(Jmax-1)
       |sqrt(A_j)e_(j+1)-sqrt(C_j)e_j|
       |sqrt(A_j)e_(j+1)-sqrt(C_j)e_j|^*,
  P_j=K_(j,j)-A_(j-1)-C_j,

with absent endpoint rates set to zero. In particular G is positive
semidefinite. The exact potential is the following compact polynomial.
Define

  nu=(2a+b+3)b(b+2)/(3C2(a,b))
     =(2a+b+3)/3-2alpha,
  t0=(nu-b)/2,  w=a+1-nu.

Then for every allowed j,

  N^2 P_j=(delta+1)(delta+2)j^2
   +(delta+1)[(N+2)w-delta(delta+2)-r(2t0+delta+1)]j+P0,

  P0=r^2 t0(t0+delta)
     +r delta[(t0+b)^2+nu]
     +r delta^2 t0
     +r[2t0^2+b(nu+1)]
     +delta(delta+1)(t0+b)(t0+b+1).                 (3)

Identity (3) follows by substituting the CPV diagonal polynomials X_j,Y_j
and the quartic map K=(-Y-2alpha X+sigma I)/N^2, then subtracting A_(j-1)
and C_j. The exact symbolic verifier confirms the identity after substituting
alpha=C3/C2; it does not numerically sample the parameters.

3. UNIFORM LOWER BOUNDS IN THE NARROW WALL WINDOW

Assume

  b/N<=1/100, chi<=N/100.                              (4)

Since b>=1, N>=100 and a>=99b. The carrier-only residual obeys

  0<=nu<=9b^2/N<=0.0009N.                              (5)

Indeed, 3C2=a^2+ab+b^2+3N>=a^2, b+2<=3b, and
(2a+b+3)/a<=2+4/99, while N/a<=100/99. Thus
nu<=((2+4/99)3(100/99))b^2/N<9b^2/N. Consequently

  -b/2<=t0<=-0.455b,
  w=a+1-nu>=0.9891N>=0.98N.                            (6)

From chi in (4), r^2<=N/100, delta<=r, Q=r+delta<=2r, and therefore

  r/N<=0.01, Q/N<=0.02, delta<=r, j<=r, j<=b.           (7)

For the linear coefficient in (3), use nu<=b and

  delta(delta+2)+r(2t0+delta+1)
    <=delta(delta+2)+r(delta+1)
    <=chi.

The last inequality follows by subtracting the middle expression from
chi; the difference is r^2+r-delta>=0. Hence by (4),(6), for N>=100,

  (N+2)w-delta(delta+2)-r(2t0+delta+1)>=0.75N^2.       (8)

The constant term in (3) satisfies

  P0>=r^2 t0^2-b delta r^2
     >=b^2r^2/5-b delta r^2.                            (9)

To see this, split r^2t0(t0+delta)=r^2t0^2+r^2t0delta. The only other
possibly negative term is r delta^2 t0. Since |t0|<=b/2 and delta<=r,
the two negative pieces together are at least -b delta r^2; all remaining
terms of P0 are nonnegative. Also t0^2>=b^2/5 by (6). Dividing (9) by
N^2 and using br^2/N^2<10^-4 gives

  P_j >=0.7(delta+1)j+0.2 z^2,  z=br/N, j>=1.          (10)

For completeness, the linear term contributes at least
0.75(delta+1)j by (8); the possible negative contribution from (9) is at
most 10^-4 delta, which is absorbed by the stated slack in (10). The
positive j^2 term in (3) is discarded.

The directed rates obey

  A_(j-1)>=(0.97)(0.98)j(j-delta)>=0.9j(j-delta),
  C_j<=0.18z^2+0.0004j,                                  (11)

for j>=1. For the first estimate, a-Q>=0.97N by (7), and
(a+2b)/3+2alpha+Q+2-j=N+3-nu+Q-j>=0.98N by (5),(7). For the second,
the last factor in C_j is nu+j+1. Thus

  C_j<=br(r+delta)(nu+j+1)/N^2.

For the nu term use (5), r+delta<=2r, and epsilon=b/N<=0.01 to obtain
18 epsilon z^2<=0.18z^2. For the j+1 term use
r(r+delta)<=chi<=N/100 to get
0.01 epsilon(j+1)<=0.0002j<=0.0004j.

4. FROM RATES TO SORTED EIGENVALUES

For any vector x in the symmetric, alternating-sign gauge, the exact
quadratic form is

  <x,K_sym x>=sum_j |sqrt(A_j)x_(j+1)-sqrt(C_j)x_j|^2
                +sum_j P_j |x_j|^2.

The elementary inequality |u-v|^2>=|u|^2/2-|v|^2 therefore gives, on
each coordinate j>=1,

  P_j+A_(j-1)/2-C_j
   >=0.45j(j-delta)+0.7(delta+1)j-0.18z^2-0.0004j+0.2z^2
   >=0.4j(j+1).                                         (12)

When delta>=1, (12) yields K_sym>=diag(0.4j(j+1)) on the whole block.
The diagonal entries increase with j, so the min-max principle gives

  kappa_l>=0.4 j_l(j_l+1).

When delta=0, restrict to the codimension-one subspace x_0=0. The same
estimate applies to the principal tail j>=1; the edge 0<->1 contributes
A_0|x_1|^2 there, which is at least the half-edge term used in (12).
Cauchy interlacing gives kappa_l>=0.4(l-1)l for l>=2, and kappa_1>=0
because L_S is positive. Thus in both cases, for every nonzero spin index
j_l>=1,

  kappa_l>=0.4j_l(j_l+1)>=0.2[j_l+eta j_l^2],
  eta=1-2b/N>=0.98.                                    (13)

This is the sorted eigenvalue estimate needed by the conditional counting
lemma; no identification of CPV row labels with eigenvectors is made.

5. CLOSE THE COUNT IN BOTH CARRIER REGIMES

Set t=T/N^2, 0<T<=N/100. A counted block has chi<=T, so the near-wall
case b/N<=1/100 satisfies (4), and (13) gives (2). Moreover, when
N>=100, every counted output satisfies

  p+q<=2 sqrt(T)<=0.2 sqrt(N)<=a,

so the exact low-output branching and CPV block formulas apply. The proof in
INTERPOLATING_FIBER_SPECTRAL_GATE.txt §3 then yields

  D_w(T/N^2)-1<=C[T^2+(B/N)T^3].                       (14)

In that count, the one unconstrained j=0 slot per p=q block is handled
separately: there are O(sqrt(T)) such labels and each output has dimension
O(T^(3/2)), so their total is O(T^2). Only positive j use (13).

If b/N>=1/100, use L_w>=L_T/N^2. Therefore

  D_w(T/N^2)-1<=D_T(T)-1,

where D_T is the ordinary End(V_(a,b)) Casimir count. For N>=100, every
counted output again has p+q<=2 sqrt(T)<=a, so the low-output branching
formula gives multiplicity at most min(p,q)+1. For c2(p,q)<=T, the SU(3)
Weyl dimension is O(T^(3/2)), the number of output labels is O(T), and
the multiplicity is O(sqrt T).
Hence D_T(T)-1<=C T^3 for T>=3. Since B/N>=b/N>=1/100,

  T^3<=100[T^2+(B/N)T^3].

For T<3 there is no nontrivial root-lattice output (the first nonzero
Casimir is 3), so D_w(T/N^2)-1=0. This proves (14) in the interior and
completes (1).

Finally, d=(a+1)(b+1)(N+2)/2 satisfies
N^2B/4<=d<=2N^2B for a>=b>=1. With t=T/N^2,

  d[(N^2/B)t^2+N^3t^3] is comparable to
  T^2+(B/N)T^3,

so (14) is exactly the stated form (1), up to absolute constants.

The small-N endpoint causes no exception: if N<100, then T<=N/100<1<3,
so the same zero-count observation applies.

6. STATUS AND CONSEQUENCES

The proof establishes H in the exact normalization of
ANISOTROPIC_SPECTRAL_GATE.txt, subject to the exact source block formulas
audited in MISSING_LABEL_MAPPING.txt and the algebraic identity (3). It
does not claim external review or priority. Once H is accepted, the
all-matrix low-rank and codimension profile consequences (including
arbitrary complex Kraus matrices) follow from §§3-4 of the conditional
gate note, and the supplied-carrier operational equivalence follows from
the already banked operational proof. The proof does not replace the
weighted operator by the ordinary Casimir near the wall: the birth-death
potential and both rates use the full quartic CPV block.
