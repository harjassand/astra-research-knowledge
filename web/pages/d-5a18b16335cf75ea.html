N01 — Cosmic inference, version 3
2026-10-09

Status: internally derived necessary-and-sufficient linear-stress realization theorem for one declared leading-derivative isotropic three-scalar solid, on an interval with positive enthalpy; no data fit, named-MG exclusion, UV completion, novelty claim, or external validation. Versions 1 and 2 remain unchanged.

Target from Phase II
====================
Version 2 derived the initial-offset-free response identity

  Pi_N = a(N) Pi + K(N) U,    K=2mu,    a=K_N/K,

and the phonon speed bounds for one isotropic solid. This identity and the background alone do not establish that a reconstructed effective stress tensor is a solid: they do not constrain its density and isotropic-pressure perturbations. Here I determine the missing constitutive conditions and give an explicit local F(X,Y,Z) that realizes every compatible target stress at linear order. Along the one-dimensional FRW background trajectory, there is no further mixed-derivative integrability obstruction.

1. Fixed class and reconstructed stress
======================================
Fix a constant M_Pl, standard known matter minimally coupled to the metric, a spatially flat FRW background, and a single dark component represented in Einstein form by

  T^E_{mu nu} = M_Pl^2 G_{mu nu} - T^known_{mu nu}.

Assume T^known is separately conserved and includes all ordinary components needed at the accuracy of the calculation. Then Bianchi plus known-matter conservation makes T^E separately conserved. This equality does not itself constitute a dark-energy action.

The proposed realizing class is exactly one isotropic medium with three material coordinates and action

  S = integral d^4x sqrt(-g) [M_Pl^2 R/2 + F(X,Y,Z)],
  B^{IJ}=g^{mu nu} partial_mu phi^I partial_nu phi^J,
  X=[B], Y=[B^2]/[B]^2, Z=[B^3]/[B]^3,
  background phi^I=x^I.

Use the first-derivative theory only, no viscosity or higher-derivative operators, linear perturbations, and k != 0. Fix Poisson gauge

  ds^2=a^2[-(1+2Psi)d tau^2+(1-2Phi)delta_ij dx^i dx^j].

For every Fourier transfer solution use the conventions from v2:

  Q^i_j = k_hat^i k_hat_j - delta^i_j/3,
  xi^i=i k^i xi,
  (delta T_E^i_j)_TF = Pi_E Q^i_j,
  theta_E=i k_i v_E^i,
  U_E=-theta_E/Hcal,
  S_E=k^2 xi,
  N=ln a, Hcal=aH.

Here delta rho_E=-delta T_E^0{}_0 in Poisson gauge and delta p_E=(1/3)delta T_E^i{}_i. The Poisson-gauge velocity is the gauge-fixed/Bardeen velocity. The material displacement alone is gauge-dependent; in another gauge the spatial metric shear must be retained in the material strain. The equations below are stated in Poisson gauge throughout.

2. Exact constitutive equations in Poisson gauge
================================================
Battye & Moss (2007), arXiv:astro-ph/0703744v1, eqs. (34a)-(34c), give the stress perturbations of an isotropic elastic medium in terms of displacement xi^i, spatial metric perturbation h^i_j, enthalpy h_E=rho_E+p_E, bulk modulus beta and shear modulus mu. Their formula has

  delta T_E^0{}_0 = h_E D,
  delta T_E^i{}_0 = -h_E dot(xi^i),
  delta T_E^i{}_j = -delta^i_j (beta-2mu/3)D
                       -mu(2 partial_(j xi^{i)}+h^i_j),
  D = partial_i xi^i + h/2.

In Poisson gauge h^i_j=-2Phi delta^i_j, h=-6Phi, and h^i_j has no traceless scalar part. With xi^i=i k^i xi, the trace, momentum, and traceless parts therefore imply

  delta rho_E = h_E(S_E+3Phi),                         (1)
  delta p_E   = beta(S_E+3Phi),                         (2)
  Pi_E        = 2mu S_E,                                (3)
  U_E         = (S_E)_N.                               (4)

For (4), theta_E=-k^2 dot(xi), hence (S_E)_N=k^2 dot(xi)/Hcal=-theta_E/Hcal. These relations are linear and exact for the declared constitutive class; no QSA or subhorizon growth approximation is used.

A first-order perturbation of B^{IJ} around its isotropic value has one trace and a traceless part. Since Y and Z are invariant under an overall rescaling of B, their first variations vanish on the isotropic trajectory: delta Y=delta Z=0. Thus the isotropic pressure perturbation is purely adiabatic, and for F(X,Y,Z)

  beta/h_E = c_a^2 := p_E,N/rho_E,N,
  delta p_E = c_a^2 delta rho_E,                        (5)

where the denominator is nonzero. Equations (1)-(5) identify the density-fixed material strain

  S_rho(k,N) := delta rho_E(k,N)/h_E(N) - 3 Phi(k,N).  (6)

For a solid, S_rho=S_E. In particular the unknown additive initial displacement C_k in S_E=C_k+integral U_E dN is fixed by the reconstructed density and potential, rather than left as a free nuisance.

3. Linear realization theorem
=============================
Let a reconstructed effective stress T^E have a smooth, separately conserved background and linear transfer components in Poisson gauge. Suppose on an interval I in N that

  (i)  h_E=rho_E+p_E>0;
  (ii) rho_E,N=-3h_E and rho_E,p_E are smooth;
  (iii) for every k and every independent initial-mode transfer solution,
       S_rho,N = U_E;
  (iv) delta p_E=c_a^2 delta rho_E;
  (v)  there exists one smooth function mu(N), the same for all k and transfer modes,
       such that Pi_E=2mu S_rho;
  (vi) 0 <= mu/h_E <= 1 and
       0 <= c_a^2 + 4mu/(3h_E) <= 1.

Then there is a smooth first-derivative local F(X,Y,Z) which reproduces the background stress and the full linear stress T^E on I. One explicit choice is

  X=3a^-2, N(X)=-1/2 ln(X/3),
  rho_0(X)=rho_E(N(X)),
  h_0(X)=h_E(N(X)), mu_0(X)=mu(N(X)),
  G(X)=(9/4)[h_0(X)-mu_0(X)],
  F(X,Y,Z) = -rho_0(X) + G(X)(Y-1/3).                 (7)

The additive convention in N(X) assumes the normalization X=3 at a=1 and can be shifted with the comoving-coordinate normalization.

Proof, background: Endlich, Nicolis & Wang (2012), arXiv:1210.0569v1, eqs. (3.3)-(3.4), give rho=-F and p=F-(2/a^2)F_X=F-(2/3)XF_X on this trajectory. Since X_N=-2X, separate conservation gives

  rho_E,N=-3h_E=-2X rho_0,X,
  rho_0,X=3h_0/(2X)>0.

On Y=1/3 the G term in (7) vanishes and its X derivative also vanishes. Thus F=-rho_0 and F_X=-rho_0,X, so

  p_F=-rho_0+(2/3)X rho_0,X=-rho_E+h_E=p_E.

There is no independent background equation to solve: the stipulated conservation law is exactly the condition that the reconstructed background be the restriction of a function of X.

Proof, kinetic and propagation: The quadratic phonon action in Endlich et al. eqs. (2.13)-(2.17) has time-kinetic coefficient -F_X X/3. Here

  -F_X X/3 = rho_0,X X/3 = h_E/2 > 0.              (8)

On the isotropic path F_Y=G and F_Z=0. Their sound-speed expressions (2.16) give

  c_T^2 = 1 + (2/3)G/(F_X X)
         = 1 - (4/9)G/h_E = mu/h_E.                 (9)

Also (c_a^2=-1/3+(2/3)X rho_{0,XX}/rho_{0,X}), obtained by differentiating p_F and rho_0. Substitution into Endlich et al.'s longitudinal expression yields

  c_L^2 = c_a^2 + (4/3)c_T^2
         = c_a^2 + 4mu/(3h_E).                       (10)

Thus (vi) gives positive/nonnegative gradients and subluminal characteristics for both polarizations, with strict inequalities if nonzero speeds bounded away from marginal zero. Equation (8) excludes a ghost on I.

Proof, stress and field equations: Since delta Y=delta Z=0 at first order, F_0=-rho_0 supplies exactly the background-determined density and isotropic pressure response (1)-(2),(5). The coefficient G fixes the shear response through (9), so (3) is exactly the desired Pi_E. The displacement is defined by S_E=S_rho and its time derivative is U_E by (iii), so the momentum response also equals the target. This matches all linear components, not just slip or anisotropic stress. The stress from the constructed action is conserved on the target metric because it equals T^E. For a scalar action, its divergence is proportional to the scalar Euler-Lagrange expressions times partial^nu phi^I. The background matrix partial_i phi^I=delta_i^I is invertible, so the spatial components of stress conservation imply the three material-field equations (also at linear order). There is therefore no extra linear field-equation integrability condition beyond the full stress closure above.

Necessity follows by reversing the same constitutive equations (1)-(5) and the sound speeds. Hence, within this fixed class and for h_E>0, conditions (i)-(vi) are necessary and sufficient for linear stress realization, assuming smooth transfer functions. The result is an exact action-level statement at background plus quadratic-action/linear-stress order. It is not a nonlinear equivalence theorem.

4. What the v2 rank test misses
===============================
Equation v2 (3), Pi_N=a Pi+K U, with K=2mu and a=K_N/K, is obtained by differentiating only the shear relation and displacement momentum. It forgets the integration constant in displacement. Conditions K_N=aK, the sound bounds, and background compatibility let one write an action (7) with that background and modulus, but they do not imply that an arbitrary reconstructed T^E is the stress of that action. The density, pressure, and momentum must also obey (1),(2),(4),(5). For a target stress the direct full test is

  S_rho = delta rho_E/h_E - 3Phi,
  S_rho,N = U_E,
  delta p_E = c_a^2 delta rho_E,
  Pi_E = 2mu(N) S_rho,
  0 <= mu/h_E <= 1,
  0 <= c_a^2+4mu/(3h_E) <= 1.                         (11)

If S_rho=0 at an isolated mode/time, relation (11) requires Pi_E=0 there; do not divide by S_rho. The v2 differential equation is a consequence of (11) wherever mu>0, and its determinant rank condition is a useful necessary diagnostic but not the complete theorem. If K is inferred from v2, the inferred smooth K must also reconstruct the density-fixed S_rho, not merely an arbitrary S_E with a chosen initial constant.

Anisotropic stress and growth/lensing alone may not reconstruct all the entries in (11). The action-level question needs the 00 and trace of ij Einstein equations as well as 0i and traceless ij, with ordinary matter subtracted. This is why the original slip estimator, though amplitude/bias/Ω_m0 free, cannot by itself establish action realizability.

5. Singular and boundary cases
==============================
- mu=0 on an interval: the log derivative a=K_N/K is undefined, so use the product law Pi_E=0 and the full stress equations (11). In (7), G=9h_E/4. The transverse characteristic speed is zero; this is a marginal fluid-like limit, not a strictly propagating transverse solid. It can have a healthy longitudinal mode only if c_a^2 is nonnegative (and at most one). A barotropic constant-w accelerating fluid with w=c_a^2<0 fails this gradient condition unless shear modulus is positive. At an isolated zero of mu, use Pi_N=2mu_N S+2mu U rather than dividing by mu.
- h_E=0: conservation implies rho_E,N=0, so the material background has F_X=0 and the quadratic phonon kinetic term (8) vanishes. A nonzero mu would give a finite spatial-gradient coefficient with no kinetic term and hence is not a healthy propagating realization in this truncated action; causal bounds as h approaches zero force mu to vanish at least proportionally to h. The exact constant-F cosmological constant is allowed, but the three material scalars are nondynamical at quadratic order. It is not an h=0 continuation of the solid response test, and c_a^2, U_E, K/(2h_E) are undefined there. Additional fields or higher-derivative kinetic operators change the class.
- h_E<0: equation (8) has a negative kinetic coefficient, so this F(X,Y,Z) class has a ghost. A crossing from positive to negative h passes through the degenerate h=0 case and cannot remain a healthy propagating solid across the crossing. This is a background-only exclusion for this class, not a statement about all dark-energy theories.
- c_T^2=0 or c_L^2=0: the nonnegative inequalities allow marginal characteristics. For strictly positive sound speeds replace the lower bounds by strict inequalities. Zero-speed limits may be strongly coupled or require symmetry protection; the two-derivative quadratic check does not settle that question.
- S_rho=0: no ratio Pi/(2S_rho) is defined; the product condition requires Pi=0 for finite mu. The linear response may still be consistent and other modes can determine mu.
- rho_E,N=0: for separately conserved T^E this is h_E=0, already treated; do not use c_a^2=p_N/rho_N by division.

6. Unknown matter density, bias, and initial amplitudes
======================================================
The action theorem is conditional on a specified T^known. For example, in flat FRW with dust and radiation,

  rho_E = 3M_Pl^2 H^2 - rho_m - rho_r,
  p_E   = -M_Pl^2(2 dot(H)+3H^2) - p_r,
  h_E   = -2M_Pl^2 dot(H) - rho_m - (4/3)rho_r.

Thus Omega_m0 enters h_E, c_a^2, delta rho_E, and the effective momentum U_E. The anisotropic-stress residual is less sensitive to dust because pressureless dust has no traceless stress, but standard neutrino/photon shear must be included. The action test is not Omega_m0-free. With uncertain matter content the correct claim is existential/profiled: test whether there is any externally allowed matter density and ordinary-species transfer consistent with all conditions (11). A pass at one nuisance value is conditional, not independent of Omega_m0.

For one adiabatic primordial mode, every linear transfer component is proportional to the same random seed R_*(k). Conditions (1)-(5),(11) are homogeneous in this common amplitude, so A_s and the transfer normalization cancel. In ideal deterministic linear data, a transfer ratio can be estimated from cross-spectra, e.g. P_XY/P_YY=T_X/T_Y, only when both fields share that single seed and noise/selection terms are controlled. Unknown galaxy bias can be avoided for the v1 metric-slip reconstruction by using velocities and lensing, but the full stress test also needs ordinary matter density and momentum to subtract from Einstein's equations. Exact density continuity is delta_m,N=U_m+3Phi_N in this convention; replacing it by delta_m,N=U_m is a subhorizon/slow-potential approximation and is not used in theorem (11).

With multiple independent primordial modes alpha,

  X(k,N)=sum_alpha T_X^alpha(k,N) R_alpha(k),
  P_XY=sum_{alpha,beta} T_X^alpha P_{alpha beta} T_Y^{beta*}.

If the primordial covariance P_{alpha beta} and transfer columns are unknown, the observed auto/cross spectra do not in general identify the separate transfer solutions needed for (11). Every independent solution must satisfy the same mu(N), but a rank test on an unresolved covariance mixture is not equivalent. Isocurvature freedom therefore remains an actual data-identifiability condition, even though the arbitrary displacement integration constant is removed by the reconstructed density.

7. Scope and remaining discriminator
====================================
The explicit action (7) shows there is no integrability obstruction for a prescribed smooth background with h>0 and a prescribed smooth shear modulus obeying the speed interval: F0(X) fits rho,p and G(X) independently fixes mu along the single background trajectory. Mixed derivatives such as F_XY are fixed to G_X by the explicit smooth extension and do not impose an extra restriction on linear response because delta Y starts at second order. This is a realization result for background plus linear stress, not proof that a reconstructed dark stress satisfies its hypotheses.

It gives a precise route to physical distinction within a model class: infer all components of T^E from H, the two metric potentials, their time derivatives, and independently calibrated ordinary matter; test (11) over k, redshift, and independent transfer modes. Failure, after nuisance profiling and scale/systematics control, decisively rejects one stable subluminal leading-derivative isotropic solid. Passing constructs the explicit linear F above and shows that this response can be called GR plus local DE in that class. It still does not distinguish this GR+DE realization from every modified-gravity description that produces the same metric.

The test is not universal. A sum of two independent local solids has Pi=sum 2mu_A S_A and total momentum sum h_A U_A; relative-displacement modes generally violate the single-fluid closure while remaining local and healthy. Higher derivatives, extra fields, entropy variables, anisotropy, dissipation, or non-Einstein tensor dynamics also enlarge the response class. A failed single-solid test is therefore not proof of modified gravity. Conversely, the action construction does not supply a UV completion, nonlinear stability, or a quantified strong-coupling scale. The v1 solid's quadratic positivity and subluminal speeds are low-energy evidence only. Standard siren tensor-speed data constrain c_GW in certain propagation laws; they do not constrain the solid phonon c_T or prove the broad ontology claim.

No observed data or named MG action has yet been put through (11). The quantitative discriminator remains conditional on reconstructing transfer functions, knowing/profiling Omega_m0 and ordinary-species stress, and resolving primordial modes. Lensing projection, velocity reconstruction, derivatives, relativistic number counts, nonlinear corrections, and massive-neutrino anisotropic stress must be included in a real inference. This is the exact unresolved empirical step, not a claimed detection or exclusion.
