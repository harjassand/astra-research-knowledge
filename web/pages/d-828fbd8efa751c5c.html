## Actual patch and a legal invariant sector

The original RX template has a doubled square-grid CYx complex.
Choose layer0 and an interior CYx1 edge e shared by two distinct
CYx2 plaquettes, away from the belt and the embedded Yq radial sides.
For example use grid edge (1,1)–(2,1) with N=(ell-1)/2>=3.
The two plaquette generators S1,S2 are Z checks; Q=X_e flips exactly
these two checks. Other Z checks do not meet e, and all X checks
commute with Q. The X→Q restriction vanishes on that interior edge;
there is no direct CYx1→CYz2 gluing. This is source paper.tex1559–1645,
not an assumption inferred from global homology or Tanner density.
Rounding_spectral independently verified these source-map facts.

Choose another interior edge that borders S1 and a third plaquette
without bordering S2. Apply its X Pauli to a ground state. The result
|psi> has s1=-1,s2=+1 and a compensating third flipped check. All
metacheck relations are automatically legal because this is an actual
Pauli error state. The state Q|psi> has s1=+1,s2=-1 with every other
check eigenvalue unchanged. The two-dimensional span B of these
states is invariant under H0,Q,S1. Since H0=-sum_a S_a,

    H0|_B = E_B I,  Q|_B = X,  S1|_B = Z               (1)

after choosing the ordering and phases. The final even RZ template
has the transposed construction: an interior Z_e flips exactly two
X plaquette checks. Hence this mechanism exists on actual final
members of the main alternating family too.

## Fixed physical bath and bounded KMS rates

Keep a single Hermitian physical coupling A=Q, norm1 and support1.
Fix beta>0 and the smooth bounded rate

    gamma(omega)=exp(-omega^2)/(1+exp(-beta omega)).        (2)

It is positive, gamma<=1, gamma(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma(omega),
and gamma(0)=1/2. It has rapidly decreasing high-frequency tails;
the associated autocorrelation is integrable. On positive frequencies
it can be obtained from a nonnegative bosonic spectral density
J(omega)=gamma(omega)(1-exp(-beta omega)), with the chosen fixed
temperature. No discontinuous rate function creates the effect.

The Gibbs state is stationary. The bath need not be primitive; neither
the abstract Chesi theorem nor a universal generator-closeness claim
requires primitivity. This single legal bath channel suffices to test
that latter claim. No fast loss of the encoded logical qubit is claimed.

## Small local Hamiltonian change, order-one generator change

First let H_delta=H0+delta S1, 0<delta<1/4. Its ground stabilizer
space is unchanged and its check coefficient remains positive. The
active block is E_B I+delta Z. At delta=0 the Q jump restricted to B
has only frequency0 and is X. At every delta!=0 its restrictions
are the two separate raising/lowering operators X_+,X_- at opposite
frequencies of absolute value2delta. The fully secular sum discards
their cross terms however small delta is.

Let F=X/2 on B and0 on its orthogonal complement; ||F||_1=1.
The block is reducing for the full physical coupling and Hamiltonian,
so restricting the exact global Davies generator to it is legitimate.
With D[L](F)=LFL^dagger-{L^dagger L,F}/2,

    D[X](F)=0,
    D[X_+](F)=D[X_-](F)=-F/2.                         (3)

Therefore

    D_H0(F)=0,
    D_Hdelta(F)=-exp(-4delta^2) F/2,
    ||D_Hdelta-D_H0||_(1->1)
      >= exp(-4delta^2)/2 -> 1/2.                    (4)

Spectral coincidences in other spectator blocks do not restore the
missing cross term on B. Q preserves that block and the two distinct
frequencies inside it remain distinct.

Now make the perturbation genuinely noncommuting relative to H0:

    U_epsilon=exp(i epsilon Q),
    H_epsilon=U_epsilon(H0+epsilon S1)U_epsilon^dagger,
    V_epsilon=H_epsilon-H0.                           (5)

Only the two plaquette checks change. The union of their supports
has bounded size and lies in a fixed physical ball around e; the
actual unit Tanner range was independently proved in cycle13.
Using ||U_epsilon-I||<=epsilon and ||-S1-S2||=2 gives

    ||V_epsilon|| <= 5 epsilon.                       (6)

For 0<epsilon<1/4,

    [H_epsilon,H0]
      =2i(2-epsilon)sin(2epsilon) Q(I+S1S2) != 0.      (7)

The exact local unitary U_epsilon dresses the unchanged ground code.
All rotated weighted checks still commute with one another: (5) is a
particularly controlled admissible subclass, not a claim of arbitrary
interacting noncommuting terms.

The physical bath Q commutes with U_epsilon. Exact Davies covariance
therefore gives D_Hepsilon=Ad_Uepsilon D_Hdelta Ad_Uepsilon^dagger,
with delta=epsilon and the same rates. F also commutes with U_epsilon,
so the order-one bound (4) holds unchanged. Thus even an EXACT local
full-system dressing, a stable ground code, and continuous bounded KMS
rates cannot imply a perturbation-uniform generator-norm estimate
||D_Hepsilon-D_H0||<=f(||V_epsilon||) with f(v)->0.

The coherent generator adds a commutator orthogonal to F in the active
Bloch plane and cannot cancel its dissipative X component. An included
Davies Lamb shift is diagonal in the split active block and has the
same property. The dissipative statement (4), sufficient for the
rejected bridge, does not depend on a Lamb-shift convention.

## Exact order-of-limits ledger

A finite symmetric averaging window preserves cross-frequency terms.
On B define the explicitly weighted time-dependent jump

    L(t)=sqrt(gamma(2delta)) exp(-2i delta t) X_-
          +sqrt(gamma(-2delta)) exp(2i delta t) X_+,
    D_(delta,T)=average_[-T,T] D[L(t)].                  (8)

This is a valid GKSL generator. In this reducing block its diagonal
Gibbs state is stationary even before taking T to infinity: cross
products vanish on a diagonal state. Direct evaluation gives

    D_(delta,T)(F)
      = exp(-4delta^2)/2
        [sinc(4delta T)/cosh(beta delta)-1] F.           (9)

Here sinc(0)=1. At fixed finite T, (9) tends to0 as delta->0;
at fixed delta!=0, T->infinity gives (4). Consequently

    lim_delta->0 lim_T->infinity D_(delta,T)(F)=-F/2,
    lim_T->infinity lim_delta->0 D_(delta,T)(F)=0.        (10)

The discarded term is small only after a resolving time of order
1/delta. In the weak-coupling derivation the relevant oscillatory
phases involve frequency differences divided by g^2 on rescaled time.
Uniform use of full secularization as both many-body splittings and
g change therefore requires a resolution condition; an isolated
ground gap does not furnish it.

Equation (8) is an exact finite-window pressure test, not a claim that
this chosen averaging procedure is the universal microscopic bath
model. It makes the noncommuting limits explicit without changing
the underlying actual replacement block.

## Consequence, prior boundary and next falsifier

The actual thermal bridge must bypass generator-norm perturbation
continuity, or impose and pay a physical frequency-resolution scale.
Direct Chesi protection at a weighted commuting Hamiltonian may still
give a long lifetime despite (4); an exact unitary-orbit argument also
remains available. Ground-only spectral flow, full Gibbs transfer and
the bath's frequency-resolved logical action remain distinct questions.

Secular discontinuity is an older open-system mechanism, not a claimed
new universal discovery. The directly read prior comparator is
Trushechkin2103.12042v3, SecsII–III and AppendixA: splitting of order
g^2 remains inside a retained frequency cluster in its rigorous limit.
That generator is Gibbs-stationary for its reference H^(0), rather
than automatically for the full shifted H; its hypotheses and
stationary-state interface must be retained if used instead of the
stipulated fully secular Davies model. The model-specific source-map realization and
the explicit small noncommuting local perturbation are our deductions.
No external priority or full thermal-failure theorem is asserted.
