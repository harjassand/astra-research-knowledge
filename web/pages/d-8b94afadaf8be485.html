# Encounter costs instead of route-length costs

Pinned source: OpenAI, *Bose–Einstein condensation at positive temperature in the dilute hard-sphere gas*, manuscript date 5 October 2026, release commit `adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a`. ## 1. Precise question

Can a change involving arbitrarily many path coordinates be compared to a conditioned product law without paying its total length? The answer is yes under uniform insertion compatibility and exact preservation of the outside marginal. The second moment of an **averaged** change pays only encounters between two changes. This is the concrete mechanism that can avoid an extensive norm/relative-entropy estimate; it is not a blanket comparison for all Gibbs measures.

## 2. Exact abstract interface

Let λ be a finite product of probability references P_i, conditioned on finitely many one- or two-coordinate indicator checks. A change S preserves its entire outside marginal. For a fixed outside sample it replaces S by independent factors Π_{S,i}, then conditions them on the required checks. Each factor carries a primary coordinate and optional auxiliaries, with primary marginal g_{S,i}P_i, 0≤g_{S,i}≤G and ∫g_{S,i}dP_i=1. Every original reference check touching a changed primary is included in the proposal. Proposal factors and allowed sets are fixed **before** the outside sample is drawn.

Assume that inserting an original coordinate or a whole proposal factor passes all its incident checks with probability at least a:=1−χ>0. This bound is uniform over **every** individually permitted choice of the other coordinates, including choices violating other pair checks, and remains valid when coordinates/checks are deleted. This uniform quantifier is essential.

For two changes S,T partition S=C_S⊔F_S and T=C_T⊔F_T. Far sets are disjoint from the opposing list. No coordinate carried by a far proposal factor interacts with a coordinate carried by the opposing proposal or its original reference coordinates, and original far sets have no mutual checks. When a factor is deleted, delete **all** its carried path instances and incident checks. Do not replace an auxiliary by an original coordinate merely because their physical labels agree.

Then, for their primary likelihoods L_S,L_T,

    ∫ L_S L_T dλ ≤ G^{|S∩T|} a^{−2(|C_S|+|C_T|)}.         (C1)

## 3. Independent reconstruction of the proof

Fix outside S∪T. Denote conditional reference normalizers by Z_U,Z_S,Z_T, proposal normalizers by Y_S,Y_T, and unnormalized primary proposal densities by H_S,H_T. Disintegrating the auxiliaries gives exactly L_S=(Z_S/Y_S)H_S on reference-admissible configurations. The primary-reference checks ensure zero likelihood on forbidden configurations.

Delete all near factors, retaining the far factors and the fixed outside of U. Their reference/proposal normalizers are Z_FS,Y_FS and Z_FT,Y_FT. Their densities H_FS,H_FT depend only on their own primaries and that fixed outside. The hypotheses give

    Z_U≥a^c Z_FS Z_FT,
    Z_S≤Z_FS,   Y_S≥a^{|C_S|}Y_FS,
    Z_T≤Z_FT,   Y_T≥a^{|C_T|}Y_FT,
    c=|C_S|+|C_T|.                                          (C2)

For Z_U insert at most c remaining distinct reference coordinates; far reference integrals factor. For Y_S insert the near factors one at a time against arbitrary permitted opponents. The estimates are pointwise in opposing near coordinates, not a claim that the original normalizers are constants. Positivity of all divisors follows from the same insertion bounds.

Deleting near proposal checks gives H_S≤H_FS∏_{i∈C_S}g_{S,i}, and likewise for T. Drop the full reference-admissibility indicator for an upper bound. Far integrations produce Y_FS Y_FT. A primary appearing once integrates to one; one appearing twice costs at most G since ∫g_Sg_TdP≤G∫g_SdP=G. Thus

    ∫ H_S H_T 1_admissible dP_U
       ≤Y_FS Y_FT G^{|S∩T|}.                               (C3)

Combining (C2),(C3) with the two likelihood ratios and division by Z_U cancels **all four far normalizers**. The surviving insertion factors are a^{−2c}, proving (C1). Integrating the fixed outside law completes the proof. No independence of the full conditional coordinates is assumed.

## 4. Why route averaging helps

In the source each vertex factor has a primary path and an auxiliary path in **different copies**, sharing a midpoint; their mutual collision is not tested. The deterministic one-opponent collision bound is averaged over that midpoint. It is false pointwise at a prescribed midpoint equal to the opponent's midpoint. Each factor meets at most CM opponents, giving χ_D=CM e_D→0 at fixed M, with e_D=O_t(D^{−1/4}). Density G is fixed after the short bridge time t, before M and D.

Two independent route choices share one exposed environment. Declare factors near at distance≤30 from the other route. If J is the number of route-vertex encounter pairs, c≤2J, so (C1) costs G^{overlap}(1−χ_D)^{−4J}. Independent choices from at least M/2 accessible labels at each common departure cell give

    E_labels G^{overlap}≤G exp[2(G−1)J/M].                    (C4)

Choose M after G to make 2(G−1)/M≤κ/2; then increase D to make −4log(1−χ_D)≤κ/2. The averaged likelihood has squared integral bounded by

    C_2=G E[1_E e^{κJ}]≤G C_path,                           (C5)

using the source's **annealed** route encounter moment. E remains an event with probability at least 1/2; it is not silently normalized away. This argument avoids the exponential-in-route-length bound that results from estimating one route likelihood first and only then averaging.

## 5. From common measure to occupation, with the bath held fixed

The Gibbs purification kernel Φ=Z^{−1/2}e^{−βH/2} is nonnegative. The midpoint sample retains W', the purification configuration, throughout. Two midpoint assignments along one route remove different tags and leave the same ordered bath X' and W'. The common observed measure ν has mass≥1/2. Forgetting latent paths/tag identities cannot increase its two squared likelihood integrals.

For any finite P,Q and common ν of mass m, Hölder with exponents4,4,2 gives Aff(P,Q)≥m²/√(C_PC_Q). Thus the source obtains affinity≥1/(4C_2) per retained group. There are G_D≥ℓD/(8M) disjoint groups, and tag normalization supplies 1/D. At least υV² cell pairs are admissible, υ=1/2000. Consequently

    B_occ≥c_*:=υℓ/(32M G C_path)>0.                         (C6)

These constants are independent of scaled volume K, density D above threshold, and 0<T≤1/D after the ordered choices of α₀,ℓ,θ,b,H₁,t,M,D_*. The method proves an equilibrium off-diagonal lower bound, not a sampling/mixing/runtime theorem.

## 6. Actual physical temperature and its limitation

The source dilation yields

    T_phys=ρ^{3/2} a^{5/2}/(4α₀^{5/2}),
    0<ρ<α₀³/(4D_*²a³).                                    (C7)

It is a positive fixed temperature for each fixed a,ρ and is independent of volume. It is much lower in dilute scaling than the ideal-gas ρ^{2/3} temperature scale. No sharp transition temperature, near-one condensate fraction, hard-sphere experiment, or algorithmic preparation cost follows from (C6).

## 7. Transfer boundary and next falsifier

The reusable object is (C1), not the physical BEC theorem detached from its path-law premises. For the older quantum programme a positive Gibbs kernel alone is insufficient: one must acquire the exact conditional factorization, unchanged outside marginal, uniformly bounded primary likelihoods and uniform insertion success. Real-time evolution does not preserve positivity of such a kernel.

Even a single nonzero Gaussian displacement has an unbounded likelihood ratio on its full support. Averaging its two opposite displacements gives a cosh likelihood that is still unbounded. Thus the source's finite G cannot simply be imported for displaced oscillator preparations. Truncating to obtain G requires an explicitly charged tail and parameter choice. Full-density changes can also make two random supports overlap extensively, eliminating the encounter advantage. These are limitations of the proposed transfer, not counterexamples to (C1) or the source BEC argument.

Nearest inspected mechanisms: strong-Rayleigh/exclusion preservation, determinant/exterior identities, unpredictable paths with exponential intersection tails, and average-before-likelihood-second-moment methods for perturbed point processes. The source explicitly credits earlier release manuscripts for its conditional normalization construction. No independent priority claim is made here.

Next falsifier: construct a concrete local resource transformation in the actual model meeting (C1)'s pointwise compatibility and outside-marginal conditions, and exhibit a route law with subextensive encounter cost. Without that interface the old physical initialization/dynamics bottleneck remains open.
