Temporal controller-state clarification, 2026-10-09
Append-only supplement to coordinator audits v1 and v2.

T22 identified and Sol6 confirmed the repeated-hardware boundary: every
fixed-q decoder invocation is stateless except for the supplied syndrome
and data. Controller scratch is fresh or reset at the invocation/tile
entrance, and all reset locations and reset faults are charged within
that tile. Any copying of the prescribed code schedule is part of the
fixed circuit description, not a persistent noisy global runtime bit.

This clarification is needed when the ideal static circuit is compiled
as a noisy component of the repeated memory/native-gate theorem. Without
resetting runtime scratch, a faulty persistent controller bit could have
unbounded cross-round influence, which would not follow merely from the
within-invocation bounded-fanout causal-cone estimate. The circuit model
with counted resets avoids that inference. This changes no algebraic
decoder claim and does not validate the separate coherent-noise bound.
