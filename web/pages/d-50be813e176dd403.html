TRACIAL ENTROPY CONSEQUENCE OF ONE COMMON CANONICAL EB COMPARATOR
2026-10-09. Independent algebraic audit; no descendants; sources unchanged.

STATUS AND SUPPLIED/PROVED BOUNDARY
The implication below is proved completely in this file. It assumes a
single canonical pure-state entanglement-breaking comparator satisfying
the stated quadratic-form bound. The existence of this comparator with
C=4 for self-adjoint bistochastic self-compatible channels, or C=8 for
arbitrary bistochastic self-compatible channels, was supplied by the
coordinator as an independently reconstructed candidate theorem. That
existence theorem is not re-audited here. No nontracial extension, novelty,
historical priority, or external/formal validation is claimed.

The original proposed 4q and (D+1/e)/log(t) estimates are valid but can be
sharpened to 2q and D/log(t). The proposed non-self-adjoint cross-term
equality is false. A positive-map clipping argument proves the required
estimate without that equality, so both supplied C=4 and C=8 cases apply.

1. EXACT HYPOTHESES AND CLAIM
Let d be any positive integer, tau=Tr/d, and Phi:M_d -> M_d a completely
positive trace-preserving unital map. Its Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint is Phi*,
and symPhi=(Phi+Phi*)/2. Let C>=0 and suppose ONE map Psi is fixed, with
  Psi(A)=d sum_a p_a pi_a Tr(pi_a A),
  pi_a pure density matrices, p_a>=0, sum_a p_a=1,
  sum_a p_a pi_a=I/d,
and, for EVERY Hermitian A,
  E_Psi(A):=tau[A(A-Psi(A))]
       <= C tau[A(A-symPhi(A))] = C E_symPhi(A).           (1)
If (1) is initially stated only for traceless Hermitian A, it implies the
displayed version by subtracting tau(A)I: both maps are bistochastic and
fix I. Psi is independent of the input state, t, and any state ensemble.

For every density matrix rho, put
  epsilon=||rho-Phi(rho)||_1,
  D=D(rho||I/d)=Tr rho log(d rho),
with natural logarithms and 0 log 0=0. Then for EVERY t>1,
  ||rho-Psi(rho)||_1
       <= 2 q_t + sqrt(C t epsilon/2)
       <= 2 D/log(t) + sqrt(C t epsilon/2),              (2)
where
  X=d rho, f=min(X,tI), g=(X-tI)_+=X-f, q_t=tau(g).
The trace norm here is the full Schatten 1 norm, not half the trace norm.
One can cap the right-hand side at 2 and take its infimum over t>1.

Under the supplied common-comparator existence theorem this gives C=4
for self-adjoint bistochastic self-compatible Phi and C=8 for arbitrary
bistochastic self-compatible Phi. Self-compatibility is used only in
obtaining (1); the consequence from (1) needs no broadcaster.

2. CANONICAL COMPARATOR SPECTRAL FACTS
Psi is CP because each summand is measure and prepare with positive
rank-one effect d p_a pi_a. The barycenter condition makes it unital and
TP. Its Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint is itself. For every Hermitian A,
  tau[A Psi(A)]=sum_a p_a [Tr(pi_a A)]^2 >= 0,
and variance positivity in each pure state gives
  sum_a p_a [Tr(pi_a A)]^2
       <= sum_a p_a Tr(pi_a A^2)=tau(A^2).
Thus, as a self-adjoint operator on the real Hermitian HS space,
  0 <= Psi <= I.
Its eigenvalues lie in [0,1], hence (I-Psi)^2 <= I-Psi and
  tau[(A-Psi(A))^2] <= E_Psi(A).                         (3)
In particular, the Schatten inequality ||Z||_1<=sqrt(d Tr Z^2) yields
  ||(A-Psi(A))/d||_1 <= sqrt(E_Psi(A))                  (4)
for Hermitian A. Canonicality is used here to ensure HS positivity;
self-adjointness and trace preservation alone would not justify (3).

3. CLIP ENERGY: VALID EVEN WHEN Phi IS NOT SELF-ADJOINT
The real quadratic form of Phi and symPhi agrees:
  E_Phi(f)=tau[f(f-Phi(f))]=E_symPhi(f).                 (5)
Indeed tau[f Phi*(f)]=tau[Phi(f) f]=tau[f Phi(f)]. This
is a quadratic identity in the SAME f on both sides; it is not a cross
identity with X in one slot.

Spectral calculus gives fg=tg, g>=0 and 0<=f<=tI. Positivity and TP give
  tau[f(I-Phi)g]
    =t tau(g)-tau[f Phi(g)]
    >= t tau(g)-t tau[Phi(g)]=0.                       (6)
The middle inequality follows because Phi(g)>=0 and tI-f>=0:
Tr[(tI-f)Phi(g)]>=0. No commutation between f and Phi(g) is assumed.
It follows from X=f+g that
  E_symPhi(f) <= tau[f(I-Phi)X].                        (7)
Since X=d rho and Phi is TP,
  tau[f(I-Phi)X]
    =Tr[f(rho-Phi(rho))]
    =Tr[(f-tI/2)(rho-Phi(rho))]
    <= ||f-tI/2||_infinity ||rho-Phi(rho)||_1
    <= (t/2) epsilon.                                 (8)
Together, (7)-(8) prove
  E_symPhi(f) <= t epsilon/2.                          (9)
Only positivity and TP are used in (6)-(8). Unitality is part of the
overall comparator setting. Nothing here assumes every bistochastic
quantum channel is a mixture of unitary channels.

4. DIRECT TRACE-NORM TAIL COMPARISON: 2q, NOT 4q
Because X=f+g,
  rho-Psi(rho)=(f-Psi(f))/d+(g-Psi(g))/d.
For the first term, (4), (1), and (9) give
  ||(f-Psi(f))/d||_1 <= sqrt(C t epsilon/2).
For the second, positivity and TP of Psi give
  ||(g-Psi(g))/d||_1
       <= [Tr g+Tr Psi(g)]/d = 2q_t.                   (10)
This proves the first inequality of (2). The estimate compares a positive
subnormalized tail directly; no extra truncation comparison is needed.

The coordinator's normalized truncation construction is also correct:
  rho_t=(f+q_t I)/d.
It is positive and has trace 1 because tau(f)=1-q_t. Unitality of Psi
gives rho_t-Psi(rho_t)=(f-Psi(f))/d. Also
  ||rho-rho_t||_1=||g-q_t I||_1/d<=2q_t.
Applying the triangle inequality and TP trace contraction on both sides
of rho_t yields the proposed weaker 4q_t+sqrt(Ct epsilon/2) bound.

5. ENTROPY TAIL: NO ADDITIVE 1/e LOSS IS REQUIRED
Define h(x)=x log x-x+1 for x>=0, with h(0)=1. Then h(x)>=0.
For x>t>1, h'(x)=log x>=log t, so
  h(x)>=h(t)+log(t)(x-t)>=log(t)(x-t).
For 0<=x<=t the right-hand side log(t)(x-t)_+ is zero. Consequently
  h(X) >= log(t) (X-tI)_+
in the functional-calculus order. Taking the normalized trace gives
  log(t) q_t <= tau[h(X)]
      =tau[X log X]-tau(X)+tau(I)=D.                  (11)
This proves the second inequality of (2). The proposed estimate
q_t<=(D+1/e)/log(t), using x log x>=-1/e, is a valid weaker estimate.
Subtracting x-1 uses the exact normalization tau(X)=1 and removes that
additive loss.

6. DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT MODULUS AND ZERO-ERROR ENDPOINT
For 0<epsilon<1 choose t=epsilon^(-1/2). Equation (2) becomes
  ||rho-Psi(rho)||_1
       <= 4D/log(1/epsilon) + sqrt(C/2) epsilon^(1/4).  (12)
For the supplied constants this last coefficient is sqrt(2) at C=4
and 2 at C=8. If D<=H uniformly over dimensions, the right-hand side
goes to zero with epsilon, uniformly in d. t depends only on epsilon,
not on d or the spectrum of rho. No nontracial reference is involved.

At epsilon=0, choose any t>max{1,||d rho||_infinity}; then g=0 and (9)
gives E_symPhi(X)=0. Equations (1),(3) give rho=Psi(rho) exactly.
The d=1 case is included: rho=I, D=epsilon=0.

7. ENSEMBLE CONSEQUENCE AND THE CHI CONDITION
For any finite or integrable ensemble (p_a,rho_a), the SAME Psi works.
Let barD=sum_a p_a D(rho_a||I/d) and
barepsilon=sum_a p_a ||rho_a-Phi(rho_a)||_1. For every t>1,
  sum_a p_a ||rho_a-Psi(rho_a)||_1
       <= 2 barD/log(t)+sqrt(Ct barepsilon/2).          (13)
The square-root step is Jensen/Cauchy-Schwarz. Neither the truncation
level nor the comparator is chosen separately to satisfy each state.
Individual f_a and q_a do of course depend on rho_a.

If sum_a p_a rho_a=I/d, then barD equals the Holevo quantity
  chi=log d-sum_a p_a S(rho_a).
Thus (13) holds with barD=chi, and (12) holds with D=chi and
epsilon=barepsilon for 0<barepsilon<1. For a nontracial barycenter,
  barD=chi+D(bar rho||I/d),
so replacing barD by chi alone is invalid. This is an identity explaining
the scope, not a claimed nontracial-reference theorem.

8. EXACT LEGAL FAILURE OF THE PROPOSED CROSS EQUALITY
Take d=3, indices modulo 3, and sigma(j)=j+1. Set
  Phi(Z)=sum_j Z_jj |sigma(j)><sigma(j)|,
  B(Z)=sum_j Z_jj |sigma(j),sigma(j)><sigma(j),sigma(j)|.
Phi and B are CPTP, both output marginals of B are Phi, and Phi is
unital. Phi is EB and not HS-self-adjoint: Phi* reverses the 3-cycle.
This is a fully legal arbitrary bistochastic self-compatible channel.

Let X=diag(0,1,2), rho=X/3, t=3/2, f=diag(0,1,3/2),
g=diag(0,0,1/2). Then, exactly,
  tau[f(I-Phi)X]       =5/6,
  tau[f(I-Phi*)X]      =2/3,
  tau[f(I-symPhi)X]    =3/4,
  E_symPhi(f)=E_Phi(f) =7/12,
  tau[f(I-Phi)g]       =1/4,
  q_t=1/6, epsilon=4/3, t epsilon/2=1.
Thus the proposed cross equality fails, whereas (7)-(9) hold. This is
not a counterexample to the repaired entropy result. The basis-dephasing
canonical comparator Psi(Z)=sum_j Z_jj |j><j| even satisfies (1) with
C=1 for this example: on the diagonal HS subspace,
Psi-symPhi=I-(P+P^T)/2=(3/2)(I-11^T/3)>=0; on off-diagonal entries it
vanishes. The example's rho itself is fixed by this Psi.
