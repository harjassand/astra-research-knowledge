1. THE EXACT VERSION DIFFERENCE

The locally pinned arXiv:1504.02043v5 statement of Theorem 3.4, PDF p.24,
allows a collection of disjoint balls lying in B2 and estimates the radii
whose centers lie in B1. The published Annals 185 (2017) statement,
printed p.157 / PDF p.27, also explicitly requires its centers in B1.
The old component names the published theorem but restricts gamma to
B_{3t_i/2}(q'). That restriction satisfies the arXiv statement; it need
not satisfy the additional published inner-center condition after scaling
B_{2t_i}(q') to B2. Thus a literal published-source pass requires repair.

2. REQUIRED REPLACEMENT

At every upward induction step, replace ONLY
  gamma' = gamma restricted to B_{3t_i/2}(q')
by
  gamma' = gamma restricted to the OPEN ball B_{t_i}(q').       (A.1)

Every rescaled center is now in B1, as required by the published theorem.
Every a-ball still lies in B_{2t_i}(q'), since a<=t_i/2 and
|y-q'|<t_i. The original 10a separation makes these balls disjoint.
The scaling gamma_ti=t_i^{-k}(T_ti)#gamma' gives exactly the published
packing measure with weights omega_k(a/t_i)^k. Furthermore,
  gamma'(B_{t_i}(q'))=gamma(B_{t_i}(q')).                      (A.2)
Thus the published conclusion estimates the ENTIRE target inner-ball
mass, not a partial subset of that mass.

All previous weak density bounds for gamma remain valid for gamma'.
The beta inequality is legal for this new gamma' because the SAME P and
the SAME beta constant hold for EVERY finite center measure carried by Z.
Neither a separate tuple nor a new frequency is selected at this step.

For ANY B_t(v) contained in B_{2t_i}(q'), if t<a the beta integrand at
each atomic integration center vanishes on all scales s<t. If t>=a,
Tonelli is applied first over a<=s<=t, on which the already acquired
weak bound gamma'(B_s(y))<=A_w s^k is available. It gives
  I <= B A_w integral_{B_{2t}(v)} integral_a^t W(y,s) ds/s dgamma'(y)
    <= B A_w integral_{B_{2t}(v)} integral_0^t W(y,s) ds/s dgamma'(y)
    <= B A_w^2 2^k log(6/5) eta_* t^k.                       (A.3)
There is no assertion of atomic density below a. The exact telescope
uses the CUMULATIVE tail P(y,64R)-P_0(y), not just a small per-band
increment. With t_i<=2R, t<=2t_i gives 6t<=24R<64R. Thus (A.3)
uses only the original tail premise. The original choice of eta_* makes
(A.3) strictly below delta_DR^2 t^k. The published source's thresholded
displacement is no larger than full beta; Definition 3.1 and Remark 3.2
allow exactly this stronger hypothesis.

3. VOLUME NORMALIZATION AND BOUNDARIES

The source conclusion bounds sum (a/t_i)^k, whereas our target is mass
omega_k sum a^k. Define D_DR>=omega_k D_published in the DR interface.
This is the D_DR already intended in the mass-form paraphrase. It depends
only on n because k=n-1 is fixed. Do not equate the two constants without
their omega_k factor. A>=max(omega_k,2^k D_DR,1) is unchanged.

Balls in (A.1) and the source application may be taken open. The source
domain has a strict boundary margin: a-balls lie inside B_{3t_i/2}, hence
inside B_{2t_i}. No a-ball approaches that outer boundary. With open
target balls and F subset the open B_R, the proof already closes.

The following alternative handles CLOSED target balls and every possible
boundary atom directly. It was suggested in the independent primary audit
by information_lower and checked here before this addendum's freeze.
Replace (A.1) by gamma'=gamma restricted to CLOSED B_{t_i}(q'), and put
  T=(1+xi)t_i, 0<xi<1.
Apply the source on B_{2T}(q'). Its inner centers lie STRICTLY in B_T;
the a-balls lie strictly inside the outer domain. The same A_w=33^n A
bound can be extended to a<=s<=8t_i, using ONLY the old density up to
t_i/2: a maximal (t_i/2)-separated covering of B_s has at most
(1+4s/t_i)^n<=33^n balls of radius t_i/2 when s<=8t_i. For s>=t_i/2,
each such ball has mass <=A(t_i/2)^k<=A s^k; smaller scales use the
induction hypothesis. Thus no new density is presumed.

Every test ball B_t(v) inside B_{2T} has t<=2T<4t_i. Its beta radii are
<4t_i<=8R<1/64, its second density factor has radius 2t<8t_i, and its
telescope ends at 6t<24t_i<=48R<64R. Consequently the SAME calculation
(A.3), SAME A_w, and SAME eta_* work uniformly for every xi in (0,1).
The source estimates the entire mass of gamma', since all its centers
lie inside the inner B_T:
  gamma(CLOSED B_{t_i}(q')) <=D_DR T^k.
Let xi decrease to zero; the left side is fixed. This yields the desired
closed-ball bound D_DR t_i^k with no extra constant. It also handles the
final count when F meets |y-q|=R and the first dyadic t_i equals R.
Every weight is counted. No continuity assertion at a boundary atom is
needed, and the induction need not be extended past its previous endpoint.
