## Actual lower-order attempts and failure proofs

1. **A legal strongly endotactic order-four near-miss.** The diagram
   `0->2A; 2A->3A+B; 3A+B->2A+2B; 2A+2B->4A; 2A+2B->2A` passes every
   direction. It is nevertheless nonexplosive from every finite state for
   every fixed positive rate tuple. The proper function
   `V=A+B+K/(B+1)`, K>4, has a globally bounded generator. The all-state
   proof is `ORDER4_NONEXPLOSIVE_NEARMISS.md`. This rules out the actual
   candidate rather than diagnosing it by an averaged fast limit.

2. **Coordinate compression to order three.** The invertible map
   `(A,B)->(A-B,B)` maps every source and product of the successful diagram
   to nonnegative integer complexes. It preserves strong endotacticity and
   reduces its source order to three. However its true mass-action rates
   change. The compressed diagram
   `0->2A;2A->2A+B;2A+B->A+2B;A+2B->3A;A+2B->2A` is nonexplosive for every
   fixed positive rate tuple. The proper function
   `V=A+B+K A/(B+1)`, any K>0, has globally bounded generator. The proof is
   `ORDER3_SHEAR_NONEXPLOSION.md`. Carrying over the old propensities would
   change the kinetic class and would not settle Xu's mass-action question.

3. **A dimension-independent restricted obstruction.** In any endotactic
   diagram of order at most four with maximum A-reactant coefficient three
   and no pure `3A` source, every maximum-A arrow has reactant `3A+Z_j`.
   It makes `A-(total auxiliary count)` nonincreasing. An A-neutral such
   arrow only redistributes its single auxiliary molecule. Every pure `2A`
   arrow makes that same quantity nonincreasing. Thus every axis-to-axis
   reaction word consisting only of those reactants has nonpositive A gain,
   even with arbitrarily many accepted extra quadratic events. Exact
   directions and quantifiers are in `LOW_ORDER_BOUNDARY_OBSTRUCTION.md`.
   This excludes our simplest multi-species repairs, not every order-four
   process.

4. **Three-species bounded-token resets fail a precise face.** For
   `0->2A+C; 2A+C->3A+B; 3A+B->3A+C; 3A+B->2A+C`, use direction
   `(A,B,C)=(1,0,1)`. Sources `2A+C` and `3A+B` both score three. The
   growth and A-loss arrows are orthogonal, while the A-neutral B-to-C reset
   has outward projection one at an essential maximal source. Adding a
   two-C inactive guard at source `2A+2C` does not repair every direction:
   `(1,0,2/5)` gives scores 2.4,3,2.8 for the growth, reset, and added guard
   sources; the reset is still outward at the sole maximal source.

5. **A lower-A auxiliary return also fails.** In
   `0->2A;2A->3A+B;3A+B->2A+C;2A+C->3A`, the direction `(1,-1,0)` makes
   the growth and B-to-C arrows orthogonal at score two and the final
   C-clearing A growth outward at score two. Its sole essential maximal
   outward arrow violates endotacticity. Adding a protective `3A+C` source
   activates another cubic single-token mechanism rather than a disabled
   pair gate; no finite-rate escape mechanism was found.

6. **Catalyst substitution did not yield a legal construction.** Replacing
   `3A` by `A+C` only saves molecular order if C grows faster than A. Pure
   cubic feedforward C creation needs a higher geometric protective source;
   fixed mass-action clearing then prevents the assumed scaling. Conserving
   A/C exchange can make C of order A squared, but then quadratic A births
   grow total population only linearly in that larger population. No
   explosion claim rests on quasi-equilibrium, a formal fast limit, or an
   unconstructed catalyst relation.
