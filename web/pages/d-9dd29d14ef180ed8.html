REVISION — SHARP UNIFORM CP-RANGE LIFT FOR THE CLONER FAMILY
Worker c05_l07 | 7 October 2026
INITIAL is immutable. Peer attribution is explicit.

Result

Let Phi_d(X)=p_d X+(1-p_d)Tr(X)I/d be the universal 1-to-2 qudit cloner
marginal, where p_d=(d+2)/(2(d+1)). For any integer m>=1, its m-th power
has the exact distance

  inf_{E EB, TP} ||Phi_d^m-E||_(infinity->1,tau)
    = kappa_d (p_d^m-1/(d+1))_+,
  kappa_d = 4 floor(d^2/4)/d^2,
  tau=Tr/d.                                                   (R1)

Consequently the exact dimension-uniform error for this family is

  sup_{d>=2} inf_{E EB,TP} ||Phi_d^m-E||_(infinity->1,tau) = 2^(-m). (R2)

The supremum in (R2) is approached along even d tending to infinity. At
every fixed finite m there are arbitrarily large d for which Phi_d^m is not
EB, so (R2) is approximation of the whole channel range, not eventual exact
EB membership. It supplies a fully explicit dimension-free exponential
lifting modulus for this special CP-power family. It does not resolve the
universal beta_m gate.

Proof of the finite-d formula

Write Delta_t(X)=tX+(1-t)Tr(X)I/d. Then Phi_d^m=Delta_{p_d^m}.
The norm is

  ||Delta_s-Delta_t||_(infinity->1,tau)
    = |s-t| ||Id-Delta_0||_(infinity->1,tau)
    = kappa_d |s-t|.

Indeed for Hermitian ||H||<=1, the scalar part cancels and the remaining
quantity is |s-t| tau|H-tau(H)I|. This is convex in the eigenvalues of H,
so a maximum over [-1,1]^d occurs at a sign vector. If k entries are +1,
the value is 4k(d-k)/d^2; maximizing gives kappa_d. This computation also
shows the normalized map norm and its parity dependence exactly.

Let E be any EB channel. Twirl it by unitary conjugation,

  Ebar(X)=integral U* E(UXU*) U dU.

The twirl remains EB and TP. Convexity and unitary invariance of the
infinity-to-normalized-trace norm show that its distance from the covariant
Delta_{p_d^m} cannot exceed that of E. Every U(d)-covariant TP map is
Delta_q for a real q. Its normalized Choi state is

  J_q=q |Omega><Omega|+(1-q)I/d^2.

For a product vector, squared overlap with |Omega> is at most 1/d, by
Cauchy-Schwarz on its Schmidt coefficients; convex combinations preserve
that bound. Thus separability of J_q implies q<=1/(d+1). The endpoint is
EB by the Haar measure-and-prepare decomposition

  Delta_{1/(d+1)}(X)
     = d integral <psi|X|psi> |psi><psi| dpsi.

When p_d^m exceeds the EB endpoint, every EB covariant comparator is at
distance at least kappa_d(p_d^m-1/(d+1)); the endpoint attains it. When
p_d^m is below the endpoint, Delta_{p_d^m} itself is EB (mix the endpoint
with the completely depolarizing channel), so the distance is zero. This
proves (R1).

Proof of the sharp uniform supremum

Put t=1/(d+1), so p_d=(1+t)/2. Since 0<t<1 and t^j<=t for j>=1,

  (1+t)^m
    =1+sum_{j=1}^m binom(m,j)t^j
    <=1+(2^m-1)t.

Therefore

  (p_d^m-t)_+
    <= 2^(-m)[1+(2^m-1)t]-t
     = 2^(-m)(1-t)
    < 2^(-m).

As kappa_d<=1, this proves the upper bound in (R2). For even d, kappa_d=1.
Taking even d to infinity sends t to zero and p_d to 1/2, so the left side
of (R1) tends to 2^(-m). This proves equality of the supremum.

For every fixed m, choose any d with d+1>2^m. Then
  p_d^m > 2^(-m) > 1/(d+1),
so Phi_d^m is not EB. Taking, for example, even d_m=2^(m+1), gives a
concrete growing-dimension sequence of non-EB maps whose exact EB distance
is nevertheless at most 2^(-m), tending to zero.

Meaning and limit

For this example, EB approximation of the entire CP image improves
exponentially with composition depth even though exact EB membership can
fail at every finite depth in a dimension chosen to grow with m. Thus
“not EB” is too weak to obstruct the channel-norm lift. The result gives
neither a uniform bound over all positive selfcompatible channels nor a
counterexample to it. The cloner’s one-step reconstruction residual on the
full likelihood ball remains bounded away from zero, so this construction
does not meet the fixed-sandwich broadcasting counterexample gate.
