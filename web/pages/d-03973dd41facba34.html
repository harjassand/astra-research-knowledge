## Exact question and legal interface

Given a coarse-to-fine generator, which errors can be certified and corrected without an accuracy constant deteriorating with physical volume? Distinguish (i) a fixed observable/marginal, (ii) the full configuration distribution, (iii) sampling an explicitly learned synthetic law, and (iv) sampling the physical target. Fast mixing of (iii) does not establish (iv).

Targets below are explicitly known. Calibration access is independent complete generator draws, NOT free exact target samples. Score certificates additionally require a normalized, smooth synthetic density and its actual score, plus integration/tail conditions. One cannot insert scores of a stationary synthetic density while using samples from a nonstationary approximate kernel without a separate sampling-error bound.

Recent motivation, verified metadata and selected full-text excerpts: Hasenfratz–Neil–Parato–Schwartz, arXiv:2608.28580v1 (28 Aug 2026), report fast short-distance rethermalization but slow relaxation of deliberate thermal mistuning. Bandini–Biroli–Charbonneau–Gambassi, arXiv:2608.31114v1 (31 Aug 2026), measure scale-conditional decorrelation and accuracy of learned distributions. Their claims are empirical; no universal accuracy theorem is attributed to either source. ## A. Global accuracy can collapse while reported observables pass

Let K_a=L+aI, a>0, on an N-site periodic lattice; Q_t=N(0,K_t^-1), t=a+delta>0. Let lambda_k be the Laplacian eigenvalues, and r_k=(lambda_k+a)/(lambda_k+t). Exact Gaussian formulas give

    D(Q_t || Q_a) = 1/2 sum_k [r_k-1-log r_k],
    Aff(Q_t,Q_a) = product_k sqrt(2 sqrt(r_k)/(1+r_k)),
    TV(Q_t,Q_a) >= 1-Aff(Q_t,Q_a).

For fixed a,t with delta!=0, a positive fraction of eigenvalues lie in a bounded interval, each affinity factor is uniformly <1, hence Aff <= exp(-cN), and TV tends to 1. A fixed-rank spectral marginal instead has a KL bound depending on its rank and relative covariance mismatch, not N. On a band lambda>=Lambda, the relative variance error for delta>=0 is at most delta/(a+delta+Lambda); zero-mode susceptibility relative bias is delta/(a+delta). Thus UV tests can pass while the physically relevant thermal error stays large as a tends to zero.

This is an exact obstruction, not a criticism that a finite empirical paper claimed universal TV accuracy.

## B. Necessary and sufficient soft-mode coverage for score certificates

For P=N(0,K^-1), K positive definite, normalized smooth q, define score residual d(x)=grad log q(x)-grad log p(x), and

    I_W(q||p)=E_q[d(x)^T W d(x)],
    B=K^(1/2) W K^(1/2), c=lambda_min(B)>0.

Then

    D(q||p) <= I_W(q||p)/(2c),
    TV(q,p) <= 1/2 sqrt(I_W/c).

Proof: whiten z=K^(1/2)x, so P becomes standard Gaussian and residual score becomes K^-1/2 d. Its Fisher information is I_K^-1. Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev gives D<=I_K^-1/2; W>=c K^-1 gives I_K^-1<=I_W/c.

For completeness the Gaussian inequality follows directly from the OU semigroup: for density ratio h_t relative to standard Gaussian, H'(t)=-I(t), gradient commutation grad P_t h=e^-t P_t grad h and Cauchy–Schwarz give I(t)<=e^-2t I(0); integration to the invariant density yields H(0)<=I(0)/2. Smooth integrable approximations extend the statement when the quantities are finite.

Sharpness and necessity, including noncommuting matrices: let v be a unit eigenvector of B with eigenvalue c, and q=N(mu,K^-1), where K^1/2 mu=s v. Then D=s²/2 and I_W=c s². Any universal D<=C I_W therefore needs C>=1/(2c). In a sequence of systems, a volume-uniform inequality of this form exists iff inf_N lambda_min(K_N^1/2 W_N K_N^1/2)>0.

Centered adversary: covariance K^-1/2[I+(r-1)vv^T]K^-1/2, r>0, gives

    D=1/2(r-1-log r), I_W=c(r-1)²/r.

So an unweighted loss W=I can vanish on a soft eigenvalue kappa=L^-2 with fixed nonzero marginal KL. Weights W=K^-1 fix this failure exactly. A noncommuting 7-dimensional matrix reconstruction computes KL and Fisher directly from dense covariances and agrees within 1e-10 with these rank-one formulas. The theorem is a precise diagnostic reconstructed from classical LSI, not a new LSI theorem.

If orthogonal spectral bands P_j sum to I, take W=K^-1 and set d_j=E||P_j K^-1/2 d||². Then I=sum_j d_j. A global TV tolerance epsilon requires a TOTAL budget sum_j d_j<=4epsilon². Averaging this budget per site destroys the certificate; a dimension-independent constant does not make extensive errors volume independent.

## C. Nontrivial interacting sufficient case

Let p(x) proportional to exp[-x^T K x/2 - g sum_i x_i^4/4], g>=0, K>0. Under z=K^1/2 x, Hessian V_z>=I. Bakry–Emery therefore gives the SAME D(q||p)<=I_K^-1/2 for arbitrary admissible q. The entropy-dissipation proof uses H'=-I and I'=-2 E[||Hess log h||_F²+grad log h^T Hess V grad log h]<=-2I. Integration gives the result. A primary research proof of the invoked curvature criterion appears in Cordero-Erausquin–McCann–Schmuckenschläger (2006), Theorem 4.1, journal p.626; snapshot saved.

No Hessian upper bound is needed for this entropy certificate. Raw quartic potentials have unbounded upper curvature, so this argument does NOT supply a finite-condition-number MALA cost.

Two-site test, genuinely g>0: q=N(0,C), C=K^-1. Residual is g x^3. Wick's formula gives exactly

    I_K^-1 = g² sum_ij C_ij [9 C_ii C_jj C_ij + 6 C_ij³].

The KL is E_q U + log E_q exp(-U), U=g sum x_i^4/4, independently checked by Gauss–Hermite integration. See `work/team_c_multiscale/weighted_fisher.py` and `results/team_c_multiscale/weighted_fisher.json`. For a=1,g=.1, K=[[a+1,-1],[-1,a+1]], quadrature KL=.010263818 and analytic bound=.034814815. For a=.1,g=1 the bound is very loose and quadrature convergence is less precise; retained rather than hidden.

## D. Finite-sample global correction under an exact one-parameter promise

Assume generator draws EXACT Q_t=N(0,(L+tI)^-1), with unknown t in [b_minus,b_plus], b_minus>0. Let B=b_plus/b_minus; target is Q_a, a>0. Given M iid full fields X_m, S=M^-1 sum ||X_m||², choose clipped MLE hat t by

    tr[(L+hat t I)^-1]=S

when the solution lies in the bracket, else choose the appropriate endpoint. Calibration fields are held separate from a fresh draw to correct.

For every u>0,

    Pr[D(Q_hat_t || Q_t)>u] <= 2 exp(-M u).

Full proof: Q_t is a one-parameter exponential family with natural statistic T=||x||²/2 and log-partition psi(t)=-logdet(L+tI)/2 plus a constant. E_t||X||²=tr K_t^-1 is strictly decreasing. For s>t the event hat t>=s requires sample T<=E_s T. Chernoff tilting from t to s gives exponent D(Q_s||Q_t). For s<t the opposite tail gives the same oriented KL. D(Q_s||Q_t) grows monotonically away from t on each side (differentiate in s); choosing thresholds where it equals u and taking the union proves the claim. Clipping handles endpoint thresholds by shrinking tail events.

Transport a fresh X~Q_t by

    Y_k = sqrt[(lambda_k+hat t)/(lambda_k+a)] X_k.

Its relative covariance against Q_a is r_k=(lambda_k+hat t)/(lambda_k+t), so D(Law(Y)||Q_a)=D(Q_t||Q_hat_t). For r in [1/B,B], f(r)=r-1-log r obeys f(r)<=B f(1/r). For r>=1, the stronger f(r)<=r f(1/r) follows from log r>=2(r-1)/(r+1); for r<=1, f(r)<=f(1/r). Summing gives

    Pr[TV(Law(Y|hat t),Q_a) > sqrt(B log(2/alpha)/(2M))] <= alpha.

Sample FIELD count is independent of volume; calibration data/work is MN; a bisection root solve with stored eigenvalues costs O(N log[(bracket width)/parameter tolerance]), and exact spectral application costs O(N log N) on a torus. The mathematical theorem uses the exact clipped root; the floating-point script is an implementation check, with no interval-arithmetic certification of numerical error. This is conditional certification of the random fitted output law. Do not use calibration fields again as if they were independent fresh corrected draws.

Finite audit (300 repetitions each, M=8,32; N=64..4096; fixed positive mass and a=4/L²) agrees with the Chernoff tail bound. At fixed a=.2,t=.24,M=32, corrected KL mean stays .0144-.0169 as N grows while uncorrected KL grows .0125 to .621. On N=64 calibration slightly WORSENS KL. Data in `results/team_c_multiscale/mass_calibration.json`; runnable `work/team_c_multiscale/mass_calibration.py`. Finite checks are not the proof.

## E. Family-misspecification and acquisition obstruction

The mass calibration theorem assumes the very property the general scientific learner must establish. Construct diagonal q covariance c_k(1+eta h_k), c_k=(lambda_k+t)^-1, h zero on low modes, positive/negative on remaining modes chosen so sum c_k h_k=0. Then every preserved low marginal AND E||X||² match Q_t exactly. The population mass MLE returns t. Yet D(q||Q_t)=1/2 sum[eta h-log(1+eta h)]=Theta(N eta²), and affinity tends to zero. Script adversary eta=.2: N=4096 KL=34.19 and TV lower bound=.99983; N=16384 KL=136.44. It is invisible to the population calibration statistic. This is not a claim its finite-sample statistic distribution is identical: repeated norms can expose a variance discrepancy, and mode-resolved diagnostics can expose h directly.

Stronger all-quadratic adversary (independently derived c_gaussian): take X=sqrt(W)G, G~Q_a, and W independent with probabilities 1/2 on 1-gamma and 1+gamma. Since EW=1, EVERY expected quadratic observable, entire covariance, every scale power, structure factor, and susceptibility match Q_a exactly. But X^T K_a X/N tends to W, while under Q_a it tends to 1; the event |energy/N-1|<gamma/2 separates the laws, proving TV tends to 1. Fourth moments differ. This adversary requires no hidden high-frequency covariance perturbation. See `notes/c_gaussian/derivation.md` and `results/c_gaussian/benchmark.json`.

Likewise a Monte Carlo average of I_W is not automatically an upper confidence certificate. Finite M and arbitrary q allow rare components with arbitrarily large score residual. For example q=(1-rho)p+rho p_shift has KL>=rho||shift||²/2-h_binary(rho); choosing a large shift can keep this large while most finite samples miss the component. This concerns certifying KL/Fisher expectation, not a constant-TV lower bound (TV<=rho). A verified bound on residual range, exponential moments, or an exactly solvable parametric family is needed for rigorous numerical upper bounds.

## F. Strong comparators and downstream use

Gaussian target known in Fourier form can be sampled EXACTLY by FFT in O(N log N), without calibration. Therefore D proves a sufficient repair mechanism but NO computational advantage. Weighted score evaluates an existing generated law without target samples, provided actual q-score and tail control are acquired; costs include score computation and K^-1 application. For general graphs even the inverse metric requires a solver and precision accounting.

An exact two-spin near-phase obstruction from c_priorart makes the necessary stability factor explicit. P assigns aligned states each (1-epsilon)/2 and antialigned states each epsilon/2; Q changes aligned masses to .1(1-epsilon) and .9(1-epsilon), retaining antialigned masses. Then target influence C_12=1-2epsilon, averaged conditional defect b=.8epsilon(1-epsilon) tends to zero, but the local plus-spin probability error is .4(1-epsilon), tending to .4. The Dobrushin resolvent factor 1/(2epsilon) exactly recovers this bias. Even tiny local conditional residual is insufficient without response control. See `notes/c_priorart/DERIVATIONS.md`.

Interacting high-temperature local observables can be repaired on finite patches using Dobrushin contraction: c_nonlinear proved/tested a volume-independent patch bound. A direct patch sampler with an arbitrary boundary is an equally informed strong baseline, and exact 1D transfer matrices dominate the demonstration. The observed warm-start gain is small. This is useful certification logic, not a new multiscale advantage.

Concrete use: diagnose soft-mode blindness before interpreting learned sampler output as scientific evidence; allocate an unnormalized per-scale error budget; distinguish a mass-only correction promise from a general approximation; require normalizability and sampling stationarity before using scores. The obstruction explains why UV agreement, small raw score loss, and rapid conditional decorrelation can coexist with wrong susceptibilities.
