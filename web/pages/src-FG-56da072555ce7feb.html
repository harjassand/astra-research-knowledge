SOURCE-AWARE COMPOSITION OF COMPACT HIDDEN-REGION CERTIFICATES
14 October 2026. Internal derivation; external correctness and novelty UNKNOWN.

1. Exact contract

Let a finite grounded reversible network have positive definite physical
conductance matrix A and a fixed source/mass vector m. Partition its represented
coordinates O and hidden coordinates H. All statements also hold for a positive
self-adjoint form with a finite, fully covered boundary-load space, provided
the displayed inverses and pairings exist. The finite proof below is complete.

Write D=A_OO, H=A_HH, J=A_HO, a=m_O, and h=m_H. The hidden response required
by the exterior variational problem is the joint Gram matrix

  B=[J,h],  G=B^T H^(-1)B.

Its last diagonal entry is the internal residence response gamma. Its mixed
entries encode the source load. They cannot be inferred from a homogeneous
boundary response alone. The source mass M=sum_i m_i is a separate datum:
the mean lifetime is F/M, where F=m^T A^(-1)m. A source Gram matrix does not
encode M automatically. If only certified M_low<=M<=M_high are available,
divide a nonnegative F lower bound by M_high and an upper bound by M_low.

Suppose a source-response certificate supplies symmetric matrices G0,Gamma
such that

  0 <= G-G0 <= Gamma,  Gamma>=0.                         (S1)

This can be obtained from the packet's residual identity with a supplied or
acquired coercive minorant C<=H. It is conditional on that certificate's
actual source/load columns, form, domain, precision and coverage. If h or J
is uncertain, S1 must hold uniformly for the actual joint columns; certifying
one fitted source does not establish uniform coverage. The present theorem
does not acquire C, omitted states, masses, coupling geometry or global ranges.

2. Source-aware Schur interval

For z on the represented coordinates, eliminate the hidden trial field in
the quadratic variational principle. This gives

  F=sup_z Psi_G(z),
  Psi_G(z)=2*a^T z-z^T D z+v(z)^T G v(z),
  v(z)=[-z;1].                                           (S2)

Indeed the maximizing hidden field is H^(-1)(h-Jz), so the final term is
(h-Jz)^T H^(-1)(h-Jz). This reconstructs the full K,b,gamma identity while
preserving their correlations.

Partition G0 and Gamma into exterior and last-source blocks. Define

  K0=D-(G0)_OO, b0=a-(G0)_Os, gamma0=(G0)_ss,
  K+=D-(G0+Gamma)_OO,
  b+=a-(G0+Gamma)_Os, gamma+=(G0+Gamma)_ss.

K0 is positive definite because K0>=D-G_OO, the actual positive Schur
complement. Consequently

  F0=gamma0+b0^T K0^(-1)b0 <= F.                         (S3)

If K+ is positive definite, then

  F <= F+=gamma+ + b+^T (K+)^(-1)b+.                    (S4)

Proof. S1 gives Psi_G0(z)<=Psi_G(z)<=Psi_G0+Gamma(z) for every z.
Taking suprema proves S3-S4; completing the square gives their formulas.
G0 need not itself be positive semidefinite. If F0 is negative, use zero
as the lifetime lower bound. If K+ lacks positive definiteness, S4 does not
supply a finite upper bound. Do not report an inverse of an indefinite K+
as a lifetime certificate. These bounds are generally conservative and
are not asserted sharp over physically realizable completions.

3. A compact output-specific stability bound

Let z0=K0^(-1)b0 and suppose a verified relative boundary-error estimate

  Gamma_OO <= eta*K0,  0<=eta<1                         (S5)

is available. This finite inequality can itself be checked by exact PSD
arithmetic. It is not a free hidden-bath spectral-gap or condition-number
assumption. Set

  d0=v(z0)^T Gamma v(z0)>=0.

Then

  F0 <= F <= F0 + d0/(1-eta).                           (S6)

Thus the relevant numerator is the residual of the combined source and
boundary load at the represented response, rather than separate worst-case
errors for gamma, b and K. Correlated source/escape errors can cancel for
this objective; componentwise absolute error budgets can discard that fact.

Proof. Write c=Gamma_OO*z0-Gamma_Os. Completing the square around z0 gives

  F+ - F0 = d0+c^T (K0-Gamma_OO)^(-1)c.                 (S7)

The block PSD Cauchy inequality gives

  c^T K0^(-1)c <= eta*d0.

One way to see this is to factor Gamma=T^T T. The exterior block satisfies
||T_O K0^(-1/2)||^2<=eta; c=-T_O^T T v(z0). Applying that operator norm
bound gives the inequality. From S5,
(K0-Gamma_OO)^(-1)<=(1-eta)^(-1)K0^(-1). Insert both inequalities into S7
to obtain S6. All costs of obtaining G0,Gamma and checking S5 remain charged.

4. Exact objective preservation without boundary stability

If d0=0, then F=F0 even when no eta<1 bound holds, provided the actual
matrix A remains positive definite. This conclusion follows from S1 alone.

Proof. E=G-G0 satisfies 0<=E<=Gamma. Since v0^T Gamma v0=0, E*v0=0.
Therefore E_Os=E_OO*z0 and v0^T E v0=0. The actual reduced source and
matrix obey

  b=b0-E_Os=(K0-E_OO)*z0=K*z0.

The actual positive K makes z0 its unique maximizing response, and both
variational values equal F0. No unproved limit through an indefinite K+
is used. This is objective-specific exactness, not a certificate of the
entire homogeneous boundary map or of other preparations.

An exact physical two-state example shows this distinction. Take

  A=[[2,-1],[-1,1]], m=(1,1), O={0}, H={1}.

Then H=1, B=[-1,1], true exterior response z=2, and F=5. Choose approximate
hidden responses V=[1,5] and minorant C=1/10. The packet residual construction
gives

  G0=[[-3,-9],[-9,-15]],
  Gamma=[[40,80],[80,160]],
  K0=5, b0=10, gamma0=-15, z0=2, F0=5.

Although Gamma_OO/K0=8 and K+=-35, the combined error d0 is exactly zero.
The source-aware certificate proves F=5 for every completion satisfying S1
and actual K>0. A certificate insisting on a uniformly accurate boundary
map would reject this example unnecessarily. Positive physical masses are
retained; the cancellation is in response residuals, not negative mass.

5. Patch composition and its exact boundary

If the hidden interior splits into disjoint patches H_j with no interactions
between them after the represented interface O is fixed, then A_HH is block
diagonal. Let B_j=[A_Hj,O,m_Hj]. Their joint Gram responses add:

  G=sum_j B_j^T H_j^(-1)B_j.

Therefore certified G0_j,Gamma_j compose by
G0=sum_j G0_j and Gamma=sum_j Gamma_j. Equations S3-S6 then give a retained
source-aware certificate without constructing the interiors jointly. Total
hidden mass, or a valid mass interval, still has to be supplied or acquired.

Coupled patches cannot silently use this additive rule. Their cross-interface
couplings must be retained in O or handled by a separately proved form bound.
Likewise, a finite boundary source basis supports a full exterior upper bound
only when it spans every load A_HO*z that the exterior problem can generate,
or when the omitted loads have a valid error enclosure. An incomplete basis
may supply a variational lower bound but does not automatically cover the
full Schur response. Hierarchical composition must preserve those conditions
and source normalization at each level.

6. What changed and what did not

This synthesis makes the packet's source-matrix residual bound composable
with the N793/N795 Schur interface and identifies a useful combined-load
error quantity d0. It states an exact stability condition and an objective
cancellation case, rather than promising that every small tensor response
captures all hidden residence. The mathematics uses established Schur,
variational, PSD and residual identities; historical novelty is UNKNOWN.

Independent finite rational checks are in source_schur_checks.py. They test
