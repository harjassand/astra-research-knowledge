1. A LOWER WEIGHT BOUND THAT DOES NOT NEED THE QUANTUM COMPARISON

For N>=2 write y=N^(1/4)|m|. Since C>=I and the Tr C term is positive,
 V_N(m)>=(1-1/N)y²/4-B y/2>=y²/8-B y/2>=-B²/2.
S01's U=chi V has the same bound on the support, and is0 outside. Thus
every continuous or Euler weight is >=exp(-B²/2), and its mean has that
uniform lower bound. This replaces the old '>=1/4 for sufficiently fine mesh'
normalization gate in the conservative bridge. Uniform upper moments still
use the acquired large-N heat-seed thresholds and cannot be made free.

2. ROOT-ORIGIN SCALED EULER IMPROVEMENT, RECONSTRUCTED HERE

The acquired bounded smooth cutoff has, at fixed M,B,
 ||A_N||_infty,Lip(A_N)<=K N^-1/2,
 ||Sigma_N||_infty,F,Lip(Sigma_N)<=K N^-3/4,
 Lip(U_N)<=K sqrt N.                                  (R1)
Each follows directly from the explicit a,D,chi formulas; the B N^-3/4
term is <=B N^-1/2. Cutoff and Cholesky derivative constants depend only
on fixed M,B and the known3/4 gap. The parameters are supplied rationals.

Let X be the true cutoff path and Y the continuous unprojected Euler
interpolation, barY its left node. Exact conditional Gaussian increments give
 I_lag=integral_0^1 E|Y_t-barY_t|²dt
 <=A_infty² h²/3+Sigma_infty,F² h/2
 <=K(N^-1 h²+N^-3/2 h).                               (R2)

Subtract the integral equations, use Cauchy-Schwarz on the drift and Doob-L2
on the stochastic integral, then split X-barY=(X-Y)+(Y-barY). This yields
 D(t)=E sup_(u<=t)|X_u-Y_u|²
 <=Q integral_0^t [D(u)+E|Y_u-barY_u|²]du,
 Q=4Lip(A_N)²+16Lip(Sigma_N)²<=K N^-1.
Gronwall with (R2) proves
 D(1)<=Q exp(Q)I_lag<=K'(N^-2 h²+N^-5/2 h).            (R3)
This is an integral-equation derivation, not an inference from Euler paths.

The logweight difference obeys
 E|log w-log w_E|²<=Lip(U_N)² integral E|X_t-barY_t|²dt
 <=K[N D(1)+N I_lag]<=K'(h²+N^-1/2 h).                (R4)
The lag term must be retained: evaluating V only at the Euler nodes causes
the leading time-quadrature error even when endpoint strong error is smaller.
Uniform second moments plus |e^x-e^y|<=(e^x+e^y)|x-y| therefore imply
 E|w-w_E|<=K(h+N^-1/4 sqrt h).                        (R5)

3. S03 FULL-RANK OUTPUT AND PRODUCT DISTANCE

Project only the final Euler endpoint onto radius r0, rather than radius1.
The true X remains in r0, so this projection is1-Lipschitz and does not
increase the endpoint error. Every local qubit now has least eigenvalue
lambda_*=(1-r0)/2>0. For tau_m=(I+m.sigma)/2,
 ||sqrt(tau_m)-sqrt(tau_n)||F<=|m-n|/[2sqrt(2lambda_*)].
This follows from the Sylvester equation for the difference and the positive
spectral floor, with ||tau_m-tau_n||F=|m-n|/sqrt2.
Vectorized square roots give normalized purifications with real overlap a.
Since 1-a²<=||purification_m-purification_n||² and
1-a^(2N)<=N(1-a²), tensoring and tracing the purifiers gives
 T(k_N(m),k_N(n))<=sqrtN |m-n|/[2sqrt(2lambda_*)].      (R6)
Using (R3), the weighted branch error is at most
K sqrtN sqrt(D(1))=K(N^-1/2h+N^-3/4sqrt h), which is smaller than (R5).
Normalization uses the lower mean exp(-B²/2). Thus the averaged Euler
mixture error is at most K(h+N^-1/4sqrt h).

Choose m=O_(M,B)(eta^-1+N^-1/2 eta^-2), R=O_(M,B)(eta^-2).
The real scalar/matrix update count improves from the conservative N² eta^-4
to O_(M,B)(eta^-3+N^-1/2 eta^-4), plus seed sampling and N emissions.
All constants, thresholds and finite random/interval bits remain charged.
The conservative bit guard in S01's bridge remains valid with the new m;
it can also substitute the actual scaled Lipschitz constants in its product.
Classical TOTAL bit work stays polynomial in N,eta^-1,Lin at fixed M,B,
not automatically linear in that real-update count. Exponential eta still
costs exponential work. No integrated stopped-SDE interval run is supplied.

4. S03 FIXED IDEAL CLIFFORD OUTPUT OBSERVATION

The inner ball has r0<=1/sqrt32, hence l1(m)<=sqrt3 r0<1. After sufficiently
fine inward dyadic approximation, all output Bloch vectors retain l1<1.
For exact dyadic m, decompose
 tau_m=sum_i |m_i| |sgn(m_i),i><sgn(m_i),i|
               +(1-sum_i|m_i|) I/2.
The weights are nonnegative common-denominator dyadics. An exact finite-bit
selector samples the axis/sign term; the I/2 branch uses one fresh fair Z
coin. Each selected pure state is prepared from |0> with at most3 H,S,X
Clifford gates. Selectors and mixed coins MUST be independent for each
qubit conditional on m; sharing the selector would create a correlated
mixture rather than tau_m^tensor N.
This replaces generic arbitrary-angle native preparation in an ideal exact
Clifford/reset/fair-bit mathematical model, costing N dyadic selectors,
N resets and at most3N gates. Gate noise and physical runtime/energy remain
separate; this note does not claim executed hardware or finished S03 output
implementation. The endpoint approximation error is separately charged by
(R6) or conservative tensor telescoping. It changes neither the mixture
accuracy floor nor the known-parameter nature of the compiler.
