1. Exact target and quantifier order

Let m>=1 be finite, a<b real, Q=[a,b]^m, and E:Q->(0,infinity) arbitrary.
Write X_h(p)=(h^{p_1},...,h^{p_m}) for 0<h<1. A label consists of a fixed
slope r in Q and an offset b_r(h). Repeated slopes may have distinct offsets.
For a finite nonempty label set Lambda define

  F_h(x)=min_{lambda in Lambda} (r_lambda.x+b_lambda(h)).

Every label attaining the minimum is active, including every tie. The claim is

  for every (m,a,b,E), there exists finite nonempty Lambda,
  with fixed slopes and b_lambda(h)=o(h^a), such that
  for every h_n->0+, every p^n in R^m with p^n->p in Q,
  and every fixed lambda active at X_{h_n}(p^n) for all n,
       ||p-r_lambda||_infinity < E(r_lambda).

Approximating vectors may lie outside Q. No lower bound on inf_Q E, no
continuity, and no measurability of E are assumed. The strengthening proved
by the same induction is that every offset can be a finite negative sum

  b_lambda(h)=-sum_l h^{gamma_{lambda,l}}, gamma_{lambda,l}>a,

including the empty sum. Thus the constructed offsets can be smooth on
0<h<1. Offset continuity is not required by the compactness argument.

The uniform conclusion is separately quantified:

  after fixing that Lambda there exists h_act>0 such that
  for every 0<h<h_act, p in Q, and every active lambda,
       ||p-r_lambda||_infinity < E(r_lambda).

There is no quantifier over varying E after Lambda is fixed. There is no
asserted computable or numerical bound on h_act in the general statement.

2. Complete proof of the affine lemma

Exponent fact. If p^n->p, h_n->0, and p_i>q, choose eta>0 with
p_i^n>=q+eta eventually. Then h_n^{p_i^n}/h_n^q<=h_n^eta->0.
If p_i<q the same argument with the reverse gap gives divergence to infinity.
No claim of this kind is used when the limiting exponents are equal.

Induct on m. Write mu(p)=min_i p_i. The full dimension-one case is obtained
by the gluing argument below from singleton local families. For fixed
t in [a,b], first build a local family Lambda_t that works for limits with
mu(p)=t. Include the baseline label (t*1,0). All slopes in this family will
have minimum coordinate t. When m=1 or t=b this singleton already works on
that slice, because the limiting p is exactly t*1.

Suppose m>1 and t<b. A type-k slope has exactly k coordinates strictly
larger than t and all others equal to t. Starting with the type-zero
baseline, construct types k=1,...,m-1 in that order. After type k-1 is
complete, choose beta_k with

  t<beta_k<b;
  beta_k<beta_{k-1} for k>1;
  beta_k-t<E(r) for every already constructed type-(k-1) slope r.

Only finitely many positive E(r)'s are involved in this choice. For every
k-coordinate set U, apply the FULL lower-dimensional induction statement on
[beta_k,b]^U to the tolerance s->E(r(s)), where r(s) is s on U and t off U.
For each resulting label (s,d(h)), append to Lambda_t the lifted label

  r=r(s), c(h)=-h^{beta_k}+d(h), d(h)=o(h^{beta_k}).

The lower-dimensional family is finite and nonempty. The lifted slope really
has type k because its raised coordinates are >=beta_k>t. All local offsets
are o(h^t), and all lifted pure-power offset exponents exceed t.

Fix a type-k local label that is active along h_n->0 and p^n->p in Q with
mu(p)=t. Let U be its raised coordinates. When k>0, comparison with the
baseline gives

  sum_{i in U}(r_i-t)h_n^{p_i^n}
       <=h_n^{beta_k}-d(h_n)=(1+o(1))h_n^{beta_k}.

Every coefficient on the left is at least beta_k-t>0. A limiting coordinate
p_i<beta_k would make its term, divided by h_n^{beta_k}, diverge. Therefore
p_i>=beta_k for i in U. Within the family lifted on THIS U, the baseline
terms off U and the new -h^{beta_k} cancel. The corresponding lower-
dimensional label is active there. Its limit lies in [beta_k,b]^U, so
induction proves |p_i-r_i|<E(r) for every i in U. This step needs only the
limit in the smaller cube; p_U^n may approach its faces from outside.

For k<m-1, suppose an unraised coordinate j had p_j>beta_{k+1}. Choose
any type-(k+1) competitor lifted on U union {j}. All limiting coordinates
in that set exceed beta_{k+1}: raised old coordinates are >=beta_k>
beta_{k+1}, and the new one was assumed strictly greater. Its value minus
the type-zero baseline is

  -h_n^{beta_{k+1}}+o(h_n^{beta_{k+1}}).

The putative active type-k value minus the baseline is zero when k=0.
For k>0 it is at least -h_n^{beta_k}+o(h_n^{beta_k}), since the raised
coordinate contributions are nonnegative. Because beta_k>beta_{k+1},
the competitor is strictly smaller eventually. This contradicts activity.
It follows that all unraised p_i<=beta_{k+1}, and therefore

  |p_i-r_i|=p_i-t<=beta_{k+1}-t<E(r).

For k=m-1, the single unraised limiting coordinate is t: all raised limits
exceed t and the overall minimum equals t. Its error is zero. This proves
the entire strict local estimate. Equalities p_i=beta_k cause no unsupported
asymptotic comparison, and equality at beta_{k+1} is harmless because the
last displayed tolerance inequality is strict.

Extend the local guarantee to nearby minima. For each fixed local label
lambda let A_lambda be all p in Q realizable as limiting exponents along
actual activity sequences in Lambda_t. These sequences may approach from
outside Q. The set A_lambda is closed. To prove this, if z_j in A_lambda
converges to z, select from a realizing sequence for z_j an actually active
point u_j with 0<h_j<1/j and ||u_j-z_j||_infinity<1/j. Then u_j->z and
this diagonal sequence realizes z in A_lambda. No offset continuity is used.

Consequently

  B_t = union_{lambda=(r,c) in Lambda_t}
          {p in A_lambda: ||p-r||_infinity>=E(r)}

is compact. Each E(r) here is one FIXED number, and the union is finite.
The local proof shows that B_t misses the whole closed slice mu(p)=t.
If B_t is nonempty, compactness and continuity of mu give

  min_{p in B_t}|mu(p)-t|>0.

Choose rho_t>0 smaller than this distance; if B_t is empty any initial
positive radius works. Shrink rho_t further so t+rho_t<min_k beta_k
for a nonsingleton family. Singleton offsets are zero. Every local offset
then satisfies c(h)=o(h^{t+rho_t}), and the sequential local guarantee
holds whenever mu(p) belongs to I_t=(t-rho_t,t+rho_t).

Glue finitely many local families. The open intervals I_t cover [a,b].
Choose a finite subcover and remove every contained interval, keeping one
if intervals coincide. Order the survivors by centers t_1<...<t_N and write
I_{t_j}=(L_j,R_j). Both endpoint lists are strictly increasing. For if
two intervals have the opposite endpoint order, one contains the other;
and endpoint equality also gives containment. Their centers, being the
midpoints of endpoints, have the same order. Pruning preserves the cover.

The first survivor covers a and the last covers b. Consecutive survivors
overlap: otherwise R_j<=L_{j+1}, and a point in [R_j,L_{j+1}] lies between
their centers inside [a,b], while no surviving interval covers it. Choose
for each j<N a cut

  max(t_j,L_{j+1}) < q_j < min(t_{j+1},R_j).

This is possible because consecutive intervals overlap and each contains
its own center. Put q_0=a, q_N=b. Then t_j<q_j<t_{j+1} and every CLOSED
range [q_{j-1},q_j] lies inside I_{t_j}. This also treats cut equality.
For N=1 the one interval covers all of [a,b].

Combine the selected local families, replacing each local offset c(h) in
family j by

  c_tilde(h)=c(h)-sum_{ell<j} h^{q_ell}.

The slopes do not change. All these offsets are o(h^a): local exponents
decay beyond t_j+rho_t>a and each cut q_ell exceeds t_ell>=a. Common shifts
preserve activity inside each local family. The pure-power strengthening
is preserved because the lift and gluing operations only append negative
pure powers, each with exponent strictly greater than the current lower
cube endpoint.

Suppose a fixed label from family j is globally active along h_n->0 and
p^n->p in Q. If j<N and mu(p)>q_j, compare it with the shifted baseline
of family j+1. The active value minus that competitor equals

  (r-t_{j+1}*1).X_{h_n}(p^n)+c(h_n)+h_n^{q_j}
       =h_n^{q_j}+o(h_n^{q_j})>0.

All limiting coordinate exponents exceed q_j, and c(h)=o(h^{q_j})
because q_j<R_j=t_j+rho_{t_j}. This contradicts activity.

If j>1 and mu(p)<q_{j-1}, compare with the shifted baseline of family j-1.
The difference is

  (r-t_{j-1}*1).X_{h_n}(p^n)+c(h_n)-h_n^{q_{j-1}}.

Every coefficient in the scalar product is >=t_j-t_{j-1}>0. At least one
coordinate has a limiting exponent below q_{j-1}, so the scalar product
divided by h_n^{q_{j-1}} diverges to positive infinity. Also
c(h)=o(h^{q_{j-1}}), since q_{j-1}<t_j<t_j+rho_{t_j}. Again activity
is contradicted. At the two outer endpoints the missing bound is provided
by p in Q. Thus q_{j-1}<=mu(p)<=q_j, which is inside I_{t_j}.
Global activity implies activity within its local family. The local
guarantee now supplies ||p-r||_infinity<E(r). This completes the induction.

Uniform cutoff. If no uniform h_act existed, there would be h_n->0,
p^n in Q, and active labels violating the strict tolerance bound.
Finiteness yields a subsequence with ONE fixed label. Compactness of Q
yields a further subsequence converging to p in Q. The violation
||p^n-r||>=E(r) passes to the limit since r and E(r) are fixed. The
sequential lemma gives the strict opposite inequality. Contradiction.
No continuity of E or numerical estimate of inf E has been substituted.

3. Independent consequences needed at the N33 interface

Supply a finite D of nonzero comparison vectors that includes all coordinate
vectors. On [-1,1]^d use

  E_D(r)=min({1/2} union {|r.v|/(2||v||_1): v in D, r.v!=0}).

Fix the above family first, THEN put

  H=min({1} union {|r_lambda.v|/2: lambda in Lambda, v in D,
                                             r_lambda.v!=0})>0.

For an active slope r at x=X_h(p) in B_h=[h,h^{-1}]^d,
|(p-r).v|<|r.v|/2 whenever r.v!=0. Therefore p.v has the same sign
as r.v and |p.v|>=H. In particular, if r.u>r.w for source complexes,
then x^u/x^w=h^{p.(u-w)}<=h^H. No positive lower bound on E_D over
the whole cube has been used. Its finite set of values at the output
slopes is sufficient.

A useful exact consequence is the converse zero constraint

  p.v=0 and r active  ==>  r.v=0.

If r.v were nonzero, the preceding strict inequality would instead give
|r.v|=|(p-r).v|<|r.v|/2. Thus comparison hyperplanes force exactly
orthogonal active slopes; a numerical approximation to zero is not enough.

Choose a positive representative c and h sufficiently small that
||log(c)/log(h)||_infinity<H. Every label active at c has zero slope:
a nonzero coordinate r_i would give |log(c_i)/log(h)|>=H. Hence some
constant label has value M_h=F_h(c), which is a GLOBAL upper bound on F_h.
Its value is the least of all constant labels. The level set

  K_h={x:F_h(x)=M_h}
      =intersection_lambda {x:r_lambda.x+b_lambda(h)>=M_h}

is closed, convex, and contains c. A least constant label is active at
every point of K_h. On K_h intersect B_h its activity estimate implies
||p||_infinity<E_D(0)=1/2, so it lies in the interior of
B_sqrt(h). If K_h had a point outside B_h, its segment from the interior
point c would meet the boundary of B_h while still in K_h, contradicting
that strict containment. Therefore the entire GLOBAL level set is compact
and positive; the proof is not only about its intersection with a box.

  r.f >= x^{y_*}(2 kappa H-K L h^H)

This calculation retains projected arrow weights. It does not import the

4. Concrete failures of overinterpreting the interface

4a. Finite covering is a necessary condition.
Any valid finite activity family must satisfy

  Q subset union_{lambda in Lambda} {p:||p-r_lambda||_infinity<E(r_lambda)}.

For fixed p choose h_n->0 and one active label at each scale. A finite
pigeonhole subsequence freezes the label, and the sequential lemma applies
to the constant exponent sequence p^n=p. This argument is independent of
the offset representation.

4b. A minimal rational-slope counterexample.
Take m=1, Q=[0,1], t=sqrt(2)/2, E(t)=1, and
E(r)=|r-t|/2 for r!=t. This E is positive everywhere. At p=t no slope
r!=t has |p-r|<E(r). Hence every valid finite family MUST contain the
irrational slope t. Rational-only output is impossible. This does not
contradict the stated lemma, which permits real slopes.

Even the network-shaped tolerance can force irrational directions when
the supplied diagram is real. Let alpha=sqrt(2)/2, v=(1,-alpha), and
D={e_1,e_2,v,-v}; this is obtained from the valid actual reversible
two-vertex diagram 0 <-> v (negative real coordinates are allowed by N33).
At p=(alpha,1) in [-1,1]^2, p.v=0, so an active slope must satisfy r.v=0.
The only rational r with r_1-alpha r_2=0 is r=0. But
||p||_infinity=1>=E_D(0)=1/2. Thus no rational-only family has the exact
network activity property for this real diagram. Reversible diagrams are
endotactic, so the obstruction is not a non-endotactic input. Rational
diagrams have rational comparison hyperplanes and are a different interface.

4c. No total point-evaluation-only compiler for arbitrary E.
The computational model is a deterministic adaptive procedure on m=1,
Q=[0,1]. Its only access to arbitrary E is point queries E(r), with exact
positive answers, and it must terminate after finitely many queries and
output a finite list of fixed real slopes and any offsets claimed to satisfy
the sequential lemma. It may use arbitrary exact real arithmetic between
queries; this lower bound concerns missing information, not numerical error.

Maintain a nondegenerate compact interval J inside (0,1). At a first query r,
choose a nondegenerate compact subinterval J' of J that does not contain r,
and answer a positive rational epsilon_r<dist(r,J')/2. Store the answer,
replace J by J', and answer repeated queries consistently. At every finite
stage all balls (r-E(r),r+E(r)) at queried slopes miss current J. If the
procedure terminates, choose t in the current interval different from all
finitely many unqueried output slopes. Assign each such slope s a positive
E(s)<|s-t|/2, preserving all queried answers, and define positive values
at every remaining point, including t. Every output ball misses t. The
necessary finite covering property refutes its returned family.

If the procedure never terminates, the nested compact intervals have a
nonempty intersection. Any t there stays a positive distance from each
individual queried slope, because each earlier interval was separated from
that query. Define E at queried slopes by the recorded positive answers and
define it positively elsewhere. This is a legitimate total tolerance for
which the procedure did not terminate. Therefore no such procedure can be
correct and terminating on every arbitrary positive point-oracle tolerance.

No fixed query count, running time, precision bound, or even eventual
termination follows from the general arbitrary-E interface. The result
does not obstruct a compiler given an explicit finite semialgebraic network
tolerance, a finite-cover oracle, or other stronger information. In
particular it does not refute N50's separate conditional rational/QE route.

N33 supplies a finite actual diagram, the assumption/certificate of

affine existence proof acquires none of these from trajectories or samples.
It produces existence of a fixed finite family by local lower-dimensional

An exact finite all-scale verifier has a concrete interface when ALL these
numbers have rational/algebraic representations. For rational D, rational
slopes, and rational cut/power exponents, E_D(r) is rational. Choose a common
denominator q and set h=z^q. Clear positive Laurent denominators. The
uniform activity property for a candidate can be written as the finite
real-closed-field sentence

  for every 0<z<z0 and every x in [z^q,z^{-q}]^d,
  if label lambda is active, then for every coordinate i,
    h^{r_{lambda,i}+E_D(r_lambda)} < x_i
             < h^{r_{lambda,i}-E_D(r_lambda)}.

After enlarging q to cover the displayed rational exponents this sentence
is polynomial. The direction of the inequalities uses 0<h<1. Its finitely
many non-strict activity comparisons include all ties. Similar finite
polynomial sentences express plateau containment, class constraints, and
strict projected drift for every rate-box point. Rational source powers can
be represented with positive auxiliary roots. Exact quantifier elimination
therefore decides THESE SUPPLIED finite obligations. This is a verifier
soundness interface, not a proof that enumeration acquires such data for
every input. N50 claims the latter conditionally on an additional joint-
parameter rational affine existence argument. I did not audit that additional
argument here, and its general CAD engine remains unimplemented per its card.

N34's existing checker accepts finite supplied rational affine candidates,
boxes, and witnesses. Its local safety and finite compact-initial-set chain
semantics cannot replace an all-scale obligation on an unbounded class.
Finite grid checks of h or source coordinates cannot supply the missing
universal quantifier. The new scalar fixture gives an explicitly acquired
small all-scale object only; it does not close N50 general implementation.

Costs that remain charged:

  * N=|Lambda|, dimension d, total pure-power offset term count B, and exact
    coefficient/argument representations. Fixed-scale affine evaluation is
    O(N*d) arithmetic AFTER the B power terms/offsets have been obtained.
  * Exact hyperplane membership r.v=0. Arbitrarily small nonzero products
    cannot be replaced by numerical zero; this can require large bit heights
    or algebraic-number isolation. Comparisons at ties need exact arithmetic
    or interval certificates that preserve every possible active label.
  * Computing h_act, H, and h_P. Even after H is supplied, the rate-dominance
    cutoff requires roughly |log h| >= log(K L/(kappa H))/H when K L>0.
    A very small H can make the scale impractical. Class/initial-point log

  * Exact fixed-scale offsets and the size/conditioning of the plateau;
    quantifier elimination can depend doubly exponentially on variables and
    has no useful bound established by this proof. No complexity improvement

  * Waiting-time bounds involve h^{-M}, affine extrema, and a finite initial-
