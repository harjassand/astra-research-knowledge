1. GENERATOR AND CONSERVED DATA
Use D[L]rho=LrhoL^dagger-{L^dagger L,rho}/2, J_-=sum sigma_-, J_+=J_-^dagger.
The generator is Gamma[(nu+1)D[J_-]+nu D[J_+]], Gamma>0, nu>=0.
Scaled time is s=Gamma*t. Nu0 is already proved in frozen note19; the
reversible estimates below require nu>0 and are not applied to a singular pi.
Let w_b be the conserved spin-sector weights, j=N/2-b, K=2j. Define
 beta=sum_b b*w_b=<B>, jbar=sum_b j*w_b=N/2-beta,
 B=(N+1)I/2-sqrt(J^2+I/4).
For full rank and N>=2, beta>0. E=J_z+N/2>=B implies <E(s)>=beta at all times.
Fields and initial coherences do not change these operator inequalities or
conserved weights. Axial Gibbs states additionally have scalar multiplicity
blocks and diagonal magnetic populations. Only diagonal populations enter
the witness estimates; a full quantum trace-distance claim is not needed.

2. EXACT GEOMETRIC STATIONARY POPULATIONS AND WITNESS MARGIN
In a spin-j sector, r=j+m is in0..K. Birth and death rates are
 b_r=nu(K-r)(r+1), r0..K-1,
 d_r=(nu+1)r(K-r+1), r1..K.
Detailed balance gives b_r/d_(r+1)=q=nu/(nu+1), hence
 pi_r=q^r/Z_K, Z_K=sum_(r0..K)q^r.
Each K>=1 chain is irreducible; K0 is already constant. Thus the stationary
population mixture has the SAME weights w_b and the above conditional pi.

From the adjoint generator, using J_-J_+=J_+J_--2J_z,
 d<E>/ds=-(nu+1)R+nu(R+N-2<E>)=-R+nu(N-2<E>).
At stationarity this gives exactly R_infinity=nu(N-2E_infinity).
The geometric pi is decreasing; pairing r with K-r proves its mean<=K/2,
consistent with E_infinity<=N/2. Also E_infinity>=beta, so
 R_infinity<=2nu*jbar.

Consequently the STRICT margin
 Delta=beta^2/N-2nu*jbar>0
proves the stationary output entangled. The fixed linear full-SEP witness
 W_beta=J_+J_--(2beta/N)E+(beta^2/N)I
has expectation at most -Delta there. Equality Delta0 does not close a
witness argument; no entanglement conclusion is imported on that boundary.
For every fixed finite full-rank Gibbs state, sufficiently small positive nu
satisfies this condition, but no positive universal nu works for all Gibbs
parameters: beta may approach zero near exclusive maximum-spin support.

3. TWO CORRECT MAXIMALLY-MIXED SUFFICIENT WINDOWS
For rho0=I/2^N, <J^2>=3N/4. Cauchy-Schwarz and j^2<=j(j+1) give
 jbar<=sqrt(3N)/2, beta=N/2-jbar>=(N-sqrt(3N))/2.
For N>3 this last lower bound is positive. Substitution in Delta yields
 nu<(sqrt(N)-sqrt(3))^2/[4sqrt(3N)]
as a sufficient thermal-bath window. The root's factors and N powers are
correct: (N-sqrt(3N))^2/(4N sqrt(3N)) equals the displayed expression.

The separate sharper-small-size bound beta>=(N-1)/4, proved in note19, gives
for EVERY N>=2 the other sufficient window
 nu<(N-1)^2/[16sqrt(3)*N^(3/2)].
Both windows are sufficient; neither is asserted optimal or universally
stronger than the other. For each fixed finite nu the first is eventually
positive and satisfied. Equivalently, writing x=sqrt(N/3)>1, it requires
 nu<(x-1)^2/(4x), or x>(sqrt(nu+1)+sqrt(nu))^2.
Thus N>3(sqrt(nu+1)+sqrt(nu))^4 suffices. An elementary conservative
admission is N>3(4nu+2)^2, because sqrt(nu(nu+1))<=nu+1/2.
These are analytic conditions, not acquired large-N simulations.

4. DIRICHLET COMPARISON AND GAP WITHOUT A NUMERICAL SPECTRUM
For K>=1, both positive rate factors obey
 (K-r)(r+1)>=K and r(K-r+1)>=K.
Compare with the reflecting path of constant birth a=Knu and death c=K(nu+1).
It has the SAME normalized geometric pi. The exact reversible Dirichlet forms
are sums over edges pi_r*b_r*(f_(r+1)-f_r)^2. Edgewise rate domination implies
the actual chain's gap is at least the comparison gap.

After the pi square-root similarity, the comparison negative generator is
B_edge^T B_edge, where row r has sqrt(a) in column r and -sqrt(c) in column r+1.
The K-by-K matrix B_edge B_edge^T has diagonal a+c and neighboring entries
-sqrt(ac). For any real vector z its quadratic form is >=
 (a+c-2sqrt(ac))*sum z_r^2,
by 2sum z_r z_(r+1)<=2sum z_r^2. The nonzero eigenvalues of B_edge^T B_edge
are those of B_edge B_edge^T; its single zero vector is sqrt(pi). Thus
 gap_j>=K(sqrt(nu+1)-sqrt(nu))^2>=K/(4nu+2).
The last denominator bound follows from
(sqrt(nu+1)+sqrt(nu))^2=2nu+1+2sqrt(nu(nu+1))<=4nu+2.
This reconstructs the root gap bound by exact algebra and variational
comparison. No floating eigenvalue computation or imported gap formula is used.

5. EXPLICIT FINITE-TIME ONSET AND CHARGED PARAMETER DEPENDENCE
For an arbitrary initial conditional population p, reversible L2 contraction
gives ||p_s/pi-1||_(L2(pi))<=exp(-gap_j*s)||p_0/pi-1||.
The initial norm is <=pi_min^(-1/2), since sum p_r^2/pi_r<=1/pi_min.
Cauchy-Schwarz therefore gives TV(p_s,pi)<=pi_min^(-1/2)exp(-gap_j*s)/2.
Here pi_min=q^K/Z_K>=q^N/(N+1). Average over the conserved w_b; K0 contributes
zero. Uniformly over all initial magnetic populations,
 TV_pop(s)<=(1/2)sqrt(N+1)q^(-N/2)exp[-s/(4nu+2)].
This is a POPULATION total variation bound. With coherences it is not being
presented as a full-state trace-distance bound. For diagonal axial Gibbs inputs
the same population description suffices exactly for the state and witnesses.

Let M_N=floor((N+1)^2/4)=||J_+J_-||. Because its population values lie in
[0,M_N], the expectation error is bounded by M_N*TV_pop, not an unnecessary
factor2. No E mixing estimate is needed: E(s)>=beta throughout.
For the strict Delta>0 above, the fully explicit sufficient scaled time is

 s>(4nu+2){ log[M_N sqrt(N+1)/Delta]+(N/2)log(1+1/nu) }.

It makes TV_pop<Delta/(2M_N), hence R(s)<beta^2/N-Delta/2 and
 <W_beta>_s<-Delta/2<0.
All later times satisfy the same bound. Divide by Gamma for physical time.
The expression charges numerical nu and1/Gamma, and logarithms of the inverse
margin and small stationary probabilities. It is not a uniform polynomial
bound in the bit length of arbitrarily large nu or arbitrarily small Gamma.
Computing a certified positive Delta from Gibbs parameters is a separate
numerical acquisition; no tiny positive population or precision is free.
The nu->0 singular L2 prefactor does not contradict the finite zero-T bound;
use the separate frozen pure-death proof when nu0.
