Quantitative uniqueness estimates for the
general second order elliptic equations
Ching-Lung Lin ∗ Jenn-Nan Wang†
Abstract
In this paper we study quantitative uniqueness estimates of so-
lutions to general second order elliptic equations with mag netic and
electric potentials. We derive lower bounds of decay rate at inﬁnity for
any nontrivial solution under some general assumptions. Th e lower
bounds depend on asymptotic behaviors of magnetic and elect ric po-
tentials. The proof is carried out by the Carleman method and the
bootstrapping arguments.
