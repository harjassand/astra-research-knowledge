R4 — Independent audit of c09_l02's covariance-channel MI lemma

Audit verdict
-------------
The Gaussian entropy/Jensen calculation and the positive-eigenvalue scale integral in c09_l02 are correct as written. The standalone lemma needs one additional pointwise hypothesis, f(0)=0, because the profile is only assumed measurable and its Lebesgue integral J_L does not see its value at zero. Without this condition the claimed bound (1) is false. With f(0)=0, the proof is valid under the stated finite-trace and measurability assumptions. The N75 transfer remains conditional on the exact identification of A(Y), f_L, and their acquisition/trace cost.

Corrected statement
-------------------
Let A(Y) be a measurable PSD d-by-d matrix, E Tr A<infinity. Let f:[0,infinity)->[0,infinity) be measurable with f(0)=0 and

  J = integral_0^infinity f(u)^2 du/u^2 < infinity.

For Z_T|Y ~ N(0,I+f(T A(Y))) using spectral functional calculus, the c09_l02 bound follows:

  integral_0^infinity I(Y;Z_T) dT/T^2 <= (J/4) E Tr A(Y).

For almost every T, F_T=f(TA(Y)) is PSD with E Tr(F_T^2)<infinity: Tonelli gives integral E Tr(F_T^2)dT/T^2=J E Tr A<infinity. Thus E Tr F_T<infinity as well, and the covariance of the unconditional zero-mean Z_T is I+E F_T. Maximum Gaussian entropy gives, for almost every T,

  I(Y;Z_T) <= (1/2)[log det(I+E F_T)-E log det(I+F_T)].

For g(M)=log det(I+M),

  D^2 g(M)[H,H]=-|| (I+M)^(-1/2) H (I+M)^(-1/2)||_F^2 >= -||H||_F^2.

Taylor expansion at EF_T with H=F_T-EF_T and E H=0 therefore gives

  I(Y;Z_T) <= (1/4) E||F_T-EF_T||_F^2 <= (1/4)E Tr(F_T^2).

Diagonalizing each A(Y), for each positive eigenvalue a,

  integral_0^infinity f(Ta)^2 dT/T^2 = a J.

For zero eigenvalues the contribution is zero precisely because f(0)=0. Tonelli then gives the result. This is a full matrix argument and does not need eigenmode independence. The exceptional T set is null, which suffices for the Lebesgue scale integral.

Exact counterexample to the uncorrected statement
--------------------------------------------------
Take d=2, Y uniform on {+,-}, A(+)=diag(1,0), A(-)=diag(0,1), and f(0)=1, f(u)=0 for every u>0. Then f is measurable/nonnegative and J=0, while E Tr A=1. For every T>0,

  Z_T|Y=+ ~ N(0,diag(1,2)),   Z_T|Y=- ~ N(0,diag(2,1)).

These laws differ, so I(Y;Z_T)>0 for every T. Thus the scale integral is infinite, contradicting the zero right side. This failure is due to the varying kernel orientation of A(Y), which the uncharged value f(0) exposes at every scale. It disappears under f(0)=0. The counterexample is analytic only in the sense of the discrete latent/covariance family; no claim about the specific N75 profile is made.

Transfer and provenance boundary
-------------------------------
This cross-audit uses the frozen c09_l02 INITIAL as the target and reconstructs its derivation independently. The R2/R3 codec results do not prove this information bound and do not resolve the N75 continuous-latent memory interface. If the exact N75 profile is continuous with f_L(0)=0 and has finite J_L, and if its A(Y) is a posterior covariance (or another field whose expected trace is bounded by the allowed global covariance cost), the lemma is a promising conditional scale-information estimate. The supplied N75 card alone does not certify those identifications. If the profile is continuous and f_L(0)=0, the specific missing point-value hypothesis is automatic; the exact profile integral and trace normalization still need verification.
