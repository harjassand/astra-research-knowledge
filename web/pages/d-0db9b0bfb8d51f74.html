# Exact six-qubit local-Pauli Jacobian certificate

2026-10-06. Fixed physical frame: `{X_i,Z_i}_{i=1}^6`. This is an exact finite algebraic certificate supporting the analytic/contraction construction developed by team B. It is not an extrapolation from a numerical search.

## Exact question and outcome

Can a faithful state have a nontrivial joint commutant of the SLD-filtered **fixed local** Xi,Zi frame? The selected Jacobian minor below is exactly nonzero over the rationals. Combined with the analytic construction, this yields faithful six-qubit states with a common eigenvector of all twelve transformed generators, hence a continuum of exact SLD-score collisions. Earlier n2/n3 searches remained negative; the new result uses n6 and does not claim a minimal qubit count.

The source paper arXiv:2609.28391v1 already makes identifiability conditional on the transformed commutant and proves a high-temperature result. This construction does not contradict those statements. It obstructs extending local-frame global identifiability to every full-rank target without a temperature or other structural condition. Prior-art novelty is not certified here.

## Rational base point preserving the physical tensor relations

Let d=64 and k=8. Let `P_j`, j=0,...,63, be the lexicographic real orthogonal matrix basis `{I,X,Z,J}^{tensor3}`, where `J=[[0,1],[-1,0]]`. They satisfy `Tr(P_i^T P_j)=8 delta_ij`.

Two explicit integer skew-symmetric8x8 matrices S_V,S_W are saved in `results/b_compute/cycle2/bell_jacobian_seed261006006_p65521_cols2016.json`. Define the **rational** orthogonal Cayley matrices

    V=(I-S_V)(I+S_V)^(-1), W=(I-S_W)(I+S_W)^(-1).

The real orthogonal64x64 Bellbasis U0 has columns `vec_row(V P_j W^T)/sqrt8`. Every column is maximally entangled across the3|3 partition. Every physical local Xi or Zi therefore has exactly zero diagonal in this basis.

Keep the twelve physical local Pauli matrices A_a fixed. In the Bellbasis, their entries are rational and computed by

    B_left,ij=(1/8) Tr(P_i^T V^T A_left V P_j),
    B_right,ij=(1/8) Tr(P_i^T P_j W^T A_right W).

No freely chosen reflection generators replace these matrices. Their squares, balance, cross-site commutation, and same-site anticommutation are inherited from the fixed physical frame. The implementation also checks all these relations modulo the certificate prime.

## Analytic faithful family and constraints

For real s near0 define an analytic Schur multiplier

    C_s,ii=1,
    C_s,ij=2 s^|i-j|/(1+s^(2|i-j|)) for i!=j.

For every s>0 this is precisely the SLD generator filter for populations `p_i=s^(2i)/sum_j s^(2j)`. Every population is strictly positive; the limiting s=0 point is used only to solve an analytic equation, not as a faithful target.

Choose756 skew rotation coordinates y corresponding to the pivot column pairs in the certificate. Put

    Omega(y)=sum_(i,j in selectedpairs) y_ij(E_ij-E_ji),
    C(y)=(I+Omega(y)/2)(I-Omega(y)/2)^(-1),
    U(y)=U0 C(y), B_a(y)=U(y)^T A_a U(y).

This local Cayley chart is real orthogonal and rational in y; its derivative at0 is exactly deltaOmega. Thus it has the same certified Jacobian as the exponential chart used to motivate the construction. For fixed rational s, all entries of F are rational functions of y. The unique nonsingular contraction root is therefore algebraic after clearing nonzero denominators. This refinement supplies an exact implicit algebraic faithful state, without changing the modular certificate.

The Cayley-chart first/second derivative estimates, denominator-clearing rank argument, and algebraicity conclusion were separately audited by b_math after this refinement. No new generic-rank assumption or numerical root was substituted for the certified minor.

For each generator let r_a(y,s) be the vector of row sums of `C_s Hadamard B_a(y)`. Define F_a as r_a minus its mean, and retain its first63 coordinates. The total constraint map has756 coordinates. `F(y,s)=0` is equivalent to all twelve filtered generators having `w=(1,...,1)^T` as a common eigenvector.

At (y,s)=(0,0), F=0 because all B_a have zero diagonal. The derivative of the mean vanishes since `Tr([B_a,Omega])=0`. The Jacobian in the canonical i<j pair coordinate has entries

    J_(a,i),(i,j)=-2 B_a,ij,
    J_(a,j),(i,j)=+2 B_a,ij,

with the row at vertex63 omitted. All other entries in that column are zero. This formula is independently reconstructed from the physical tensor matrices by another worker; it does not rely on storing a floating-point Jacobian.

## From nonzero Jacobian to exact score collisions

Real analytic implicit-function theory supplies y(s) with F(y(s),s)=0 for all sufficiently small finite positive s. Equivalently one may use the explicit contraction radius below. Then

    sigma(s)=U(y(s)) diag(p_i(s)) U(y(s))^T

is faithful and all its filtered **fixed local** generators preserve `v=U(y(s))w/sqrt64`. Let `P_v=vv^T`. For every t in `(-64/63,64)` define

    rho_t=sigma^(1/2)[I+t(P_v-I/64)]sigma^(1/2).

Tr(sigma P_v)=1/64 because v has equal squared coordinates in sigma's eigenbasis. Hence rho_t is normalized and faithful on the stated interval, differs from sigma for every t!=0, and has exactly the same SLD scores. This last implication is the independently derived fibre identity in `notes/b_compute/SCORE_FIBRE_AND_REPAIRS.md`.

The original local frame remains ordinarily irreducible because it generates the full tensor matrix algebra. The state-dependent transformed frame is reducible. Tensoring sigma and rho_t with maximally mixed ancillas extends the collision to every n>=6. Both states are Gibbs states of finite6-qubit Hamiltonians. They can be embedded as isolated6-site blocks with bounded locality and finite interaction degree; a common sufficiently large inverse temperature rescales their Pauli coefficients into a bounded coefficient box. No high-temperature, efficient-measurement, or robust-separation guarantee follows.

## Explicit finite parameter via conservative contraction bounds

Let m=756, q=12, d=64 and use the common denominator Q above. Entries of J have absolute value<=2. A nonzero integer determinant of QJ, together with the cofactor bound, gives

    ||J^(-1)||_2 <= K=m(2m)^(m-1) Q^m.

The following conservative analytic derivative estimates hold on `||y||<=1`, `|s|<=1/2`,

    ||F(0,s)||_2 <= A|s|, A=4dq=3072,
    ||D_yF(y,s)-J||_2 <= L(||y||_2+|s|), L=16dq=12288.

For a direct Cayley-chart verification, put R=(I-Omega/2)^(-1). Since Omega is real skew-symmetric, ||R||_op<=1. The exact derivatives are `DC=R deltaOmega R` and `D²C=(R deltaOmega1 R deltaOmega2 R+reverse)/2`. Selected skew basis matrices have Frobenius norm sqrt2 and are mutually orthogonal. Consequently `||DC||_HS<=sqrt2||delta y||` and `||D²C||_HS<=2||delta y1||||delta y2||`. With ||B_a||_op=1 and C orthogonal, `||D² B_a||_HS<=8||delta y1||||delta y2||`. Row summation has operator norm at most sqrt(d), and the mean-removal projection has norm1, giving `||D²_yF||<=8sqrt(dq)`. Also max_ij |C_s,ij-C_0,ij|<=2|s| gives `||D_yF(0,s)-J||<=4sqrt(2dq)|s|`. Each base row has off-diagonal absolute sum at most `4|s|/(1-|s|)<=8|s|`, hence `||F(0,s)||<=8sqrt(dq)|s|`. The displayed A and L are larger than all these bounds.

Set `r=1/(4KL)` and the explicit positive rational

    s_star=1/(16 K^2 L A).

The frozen-Jacobian Newton map `y -> y-J^(-1)F(y,s_star)` is a contraction of the closed radius-r ball into itself: its derivative norm is below1/2 and its value at0 has norm at most r/4. Its iterates from0 therefore define an exact root y_star. Its Jacobian is nonsingular by the same bound. At rational s_star, clearing the rational Cayley and Schur denominators gives756 polynomial equations over the rationals with an isolated nonsingular root, so its coordinates are algebraic. This makes the faithful target an exact constructive implicit algebraic object at a specified finite parameter rather than an unspecified singular limit. These bounds are extremely conservative; s_star and the smallest population are extraordinarily small. The certificate establishes exact nonidentifiability, not a practical experimental preparation or a useful sample complexity.

Because the Cayley chart, F, J^(-1), and s_star are rational at rational coordinates, every frozen-Newton iterate starting from0 is rational. The contraction estimate bounds the error after N steps by a constant at most r times2^(-N). This specifies an exact computable approximation algorithm for the algebraic root. The enormous rational bit sizes and construction cost are not charged away; only the small modular Jacobian certificate was actually executed here, not the full exact target preparation.
