## Equality is not characterized by the KL coefficient alone

Cycle2 establishes strict gamma_i<1 as an eventual same-budget sufficient condition, with gamma_i<=1 necessary. Cycle3 supplies explicit counterexamples at equality.

Binary identical-directed-KL construction: source Bern(.1)/Bern(.9), whose stopped likelihood ratios lie in9^Z; target includes an observed state-independent mixture of two symmetric binary branches, with ratios4^Z and16^Z, chosen to match both directed KL values. ## Exact conversion versus paid model acquisition

An unknown target Bernoulli parameter q independent of the known source gives a sharp acquisition obstruction: any method producing an exact n-product target law needs E_q M>=n paid target/calibration observations. Input stopped KL is E_q M*kl(q||r), while output KL is n*kl(q||r). This is a sampler lower bound, not merely an estimator bound. Retaining n labelled target draws plus a source state-orientation test is a competent unconditional one-report baseline.

A different contract conditions on the retained calibration dataset and permits future reports selected after seeing it. Confidence-valid fresh n-product generators require Omega(n log(1/beta)/epsilon^2) target calibration. Target-only calibration plus a coarse source orientation promise achieves that rate; source classification costs O(log(1/epsilon)), independently of n. Approximate additive-TV conversion can therefore replace exact KL-rate conversion by classification, while moving the main burden to acquiring the target law. Cached exposed target banks do not meet the conditional fresh-law contract; private reserves remain valid finite alternatives. Neither lower bound may be misstated as a universal epsilon^-2 bound for unconditional approximate samplers.

Target-specific information can be much cheaper. For a majority report of >=1024 iid bits, a coarse target promise q>=.6 can be accepted from231 labelled samples with99% confidence; a source gap promise p>=.75 permits31 orientation calls. The accepted majority-quotient report has TV<=.02075434 and costs262 total observations for one report. It may return UNKNOWN; it preserves this report menu, not full trajectories. ## Prior art and decisive next step

Classical controlled sensing already supplies the targeted source family and adaptive statewise KL designs. Devroye1986 chapterII section4.6, printedp74, gives signed-mixture rejection and explicitly warns that acceptance rate omits evaluation work, citing Bignami–deMatteis1971. Li–Liu already supplies exact classifier-inverse repair. Consequently no new signed-mixture primitive, normalizer-free rejection principle or adaptive-classification invention is claimed. The combination of a sharp physical-observation rate with an efficient full-record evaluator and finite same-budget proof is the candidate constructive contribution. Older sequential-comparison originals remain inaccessible and historical novelty remains unestablished.

The consequential remaining question is whether scientifically supplied structure can itself be acquired, rejected or refined through calibrated interventions at a lower total cost than direct retaining controls. For the compiler, the immediate falsifier is an exact full-record audit on a controlled target program whose terminal-law extraction is hard, with no extraction hidden in preprocessing, and a complete execution/calibration ledger. For the invariant route, it is an intervention that tests the hidden reversibility/preparation-duality premise while preserving an honest forecast-cost comparison. Neither is resolved by the finite supplied-model savings alone.
