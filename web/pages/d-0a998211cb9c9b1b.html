EXACT LEGAL SHARPNESS OF THE UNIVERSAL STATIONARY CONSTANT PI

Theorem
-------
For every real kappa>pi there exist a finite-dimensional faithful density
matrix sigma, a legal sigma-stationary KMS Markov generator L, and a
self-adjoint Y with Tr(sqrt(sigma)Y)=0 such that E2>0 and J2<kappa E2.
The generator can also be made primitive while preserving the strict
inequality. Combined with the exposed pi theorem, this proves that pi
is the optimal dimension-independent universal STATIONARY constant.

This is not a proof of a nonlinear global inequality at pi or at2. The
negative stationary Hessian does imply that a proposed universal global
constant kappa>pi cannot hold: the corresponding faithful linear physical
perturbations violate it for sufficiently small nonzero perturbations.
There is no EB/self-compatibility assertion for the star semigroup here;
the legal class is the all-finite-KMS generator class in the assigned gate.

1. Exact legal rank-one-G realization of ANY finite real kernel matrix

Take distinct real nodes t_i in (-1,1), i=0,...,m-1, and set
 r_i=(1+t_i)/(1-t_i)>0, x_i=(log r_i)/2=atanh(t_i).
Use an orthonormal basis with a center c, an isolated anchor a, and m
leaves i. Define the unnormalized positive stationary root
 s_raw=diag(1 on c,1 on a,r_i on leaf i),
 Z=Tr(s_raw^2)=2+sum_i r_i^2,
 s=s_raw/sqrt(Z), sigma=s^2.
Define the Hermitian star noise
 B=sum_i sqrt(r_i)(E_(c,i)+E_(i,c)).
The anchor row and column of B are zero. Use the UNNORMALIZED convention
 Lyap_s(X)=sX+Xs,
 V_B=2Lyap_s^(-1)(BsB), H_B(X)={V_B,X}/2-BXB.
Uniform scaling of s cancels in V_B. Its exact entries are
 (V_B)_(i,j)=2sqrt(r_i r_j)/(r_i+r_j) for leaf indices,
 (V_B)_(c,c)=sum_i r_i^2,
 with anchor row/column and center-leaf entries zero.

Legality is complete: H_B is HS self-adjoint, H_B(s)=0, and its drift/noise
Dyson expansion gives a CP semigroup exp(-tH_B). Sandwich transport by
d=s^(1/2) gives a unital CP Heisenberg semigroup preserving sigma and
KMS symmetry. These are exactly the legal Lyapunov-noise hypotheses of
the frozen baseline; no arbitrary positive Markov operator is substituted.

Let U be the complex HS subspace spanned by E_(i,a). Both BXB=0 on U
and the anchor part of the anticommutator vanishes. U is invariant, with
 H_U(i,j)=sqrt(r_i r_j)/(r_i+r_j).
The modular coordinate on E_(i,a) is delta_i=log r_i, hence x_i=delta_i/2.
The transformed physical GNS form is therefore EXACTLY
 G_U(i,j)=H_U(i,j)cosh(x_i-x_j)=1/2.
Thus G_U is the rank-one all-ones matrix divided by2, and
 (Q_kappa)_U=N_kappa(t_i,t_j)/2.

The conjugate HS subspace spanned by E_(a,i) is invariant and has the
same real H matrix. Its frequencies are -x_i; a_disk and b are even,
so N_kappa(-t_i,-t_j)=N_kappa(t_i,t_j). There is no coupling between the
two subspaces. For any real coefficients gamma_i put
 Y=sum_i gamma_i(E_(i,a)+E_(a,i)).
This is Hermitian and Tr(sY)=0 automatically. The exact physical forms are
 <Y,Q_kappa Y>=gamma^T N_kappa gamma,
 E2=<Y,H_B Y>=2gamma^T H_U gamma,
 J2-kappa E2=2gamma^T N_kappa gamma.

For distinct nodes H_U is strictly PD: it is the Cauchy Gram
 H_U(i,j)=integral_0^infinity sqrt(r_i)e^(-r_i v)
                                  sqrt(r_j)e^(-r_j v) dv,
and finitely many exponentials with distinct positive rates are linearly
independent. Thus E2>0 for every nonzero gamma. ANY finite real-node
negative N_kappa direction is now an actual legal stationary violation.

2. An explicit negative Hardy direction for every kappa>pi

Use H^2 with its ordinary normalized-circle norm. The bounded analytic
 b(z)=sqrt(1-z^2)=sum_j b_j z^j
has coefficients
 b_0=1, b_(2m)=-c_m, b_(2m+1)=0,
 c_m=binom(2m,m)/(4^m(2m-1)), m>=1.
Every c_m is a positive rational number. Boundary integration gives
 ||b||_(H^2)^2=integral |1-e^(2i theta)| dtheta/(2pi)=4/pi.
Let
 S_n=sum_(j=0)^n |b_j|^2.
For the unit vector z^n, multiplication leaves its norm equal to ||b||,
whereas the adjoint gives
 ||M_b^* z^n||^2=S_n.
Consequently
 <z^n,D_kappa z^n>=2-(kappa/2)S_n.
Since S_n increases to4/pi, this is negative for all sufficiently large n.

Here is a finite parameter rule with an explicit margin. Put eta=kappa-pi>0,
choose any integer
 M>kappa*pi/eta,
and put n=2M. Since c_m<=1/(2m-1)<=1/m,
 4/pi-S_n=sum_(m>M)c_m^2<=1/M.
Define the EXACT positive margin
 mu=(kappa/2)S_n-2.
The displayed M bound implies
 mu>=2eta/pi-kappa/(2M)>3eta/(2pi)>0.
No numerical discovery of a negative eigenvalue is needed. The finite
sum S_n is rational and the proof's target kappa can be arbitrary real.

3. Finite distinct REAL nodes and exact rational coefficients

Choose a positive dyadic h=2^(-ell) satisfying
 h<=1/[2n(n+1)] and
 h<=mu/[12(pi+kappa)n(n+1)].
Such a finite integer ell exists; the least eligible ell is one explicit
parameter choice. Take the n+1 distinct real nodes

and the EXACT rational coefficients

They are the nth forward difference of the real Szego kernel
 k_t(z)=1/(1-tz), divided by n! h^n. Their combination is exactly
 f_h(z)=sum_j gamma_j k_(jh)(z)
       =z^n/product_(j=1)^n(1-jhz).
The rational-function identity follows either from partial fractions or
from the Stirling-number identity for nth forward differences.

Let r_err=||f_h-z^n||_(H^2). Because nh<=1/2,
 product_(j=1)^n(1-jh)^(-1)<=exp(hn(n+1)).
The first h bound makes hn(n+1)<=1/2, and exp(v)-1<=2v on that interval.
Expanding the product in its nonnegative coefficients, or bounding on

The exact finite-node certificate is consequently

This uses ONLY finite real nodes in [0,1/2] and rational gamma. The same

5. Primitive regularization preserves exact sharpness

If primitivity is desired, add to the Heisenberg positive generator L
the sigma replacement generator with a positive coefficient xi:
 L_0(X)=X-Tr(sigma X)I, L_xi=L+xi L_0.
L_0 is GNS/KMS symmetric and sigma-preserving; the total generator is
KMS symmetric but is generally NOT GNS symmetric. Its weighted form is
 H_0=I-|s><s| because Tr(s^2)=1.
For any sigma-preserving unital L, L_0 commutes with L, and
 exp(-tL_xi)=exp(-xi t)exp(-tL)
                  +(1-exp(-xi t))[X->Tr(sigma X)I].
The Schrödinger adjoint has a positive faithful sigma replacement
component for every t>0, so it is positivity improving and primitive.

For the fixed constructed tangent put
 F=J2-kappa E2<0,
 F_0=4< CY,AY >-kappa||Y||_2^2,
since Y,AY,CY are orthogonal to s. Choose, for example,
 0<xi<(-F)/[2(|F_0|+1)].
Then F+xi F_0<F/2<0. This gives a primitive, full-domain, faithful KMS
generator with the same strict stationary ratio below kappa. It does
not assert that the total generator becomes GNS symmetric.

6. Size, conditioning and precise optimality scope

This exact family has m=n+1 leaves and total Hilbert-space dimension
 n+3=2M+3, where M>kappa*pi/(kappa-pi). No fixed-dimensional optimality
claim is made. The dyadic h is bounded explicitly in Section3. For bounded
kappa approaching pi, dimension is O((kappa-pi)^(-1)); the coefficients
may be extremely large and the actual perturbation interval correspondingly
small. Finite exact formulas, not computation efficiency, establish the
universal sharpness claim.

All t_j lie in [0,1/2], so 1<=r_j<=3 and the condition number of sigma is
at most9. Taking a still smaller dyadic h preserves the certificate and
makes the stationary eigenvalue ratios arbitrarily close to1. Exact
tracial sigma itself has the stronger stationary constant4; no uniform
continuity conclusion in growing dimension/generator conditioning is
implied. A generator spectral-gap constraint is not supplied and not
assumed. Overall positive rate rescaling changes neither stationary ratio.

The conclusion is sharpness of the universal all-finite-dimensional
