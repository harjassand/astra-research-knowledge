A. LYAPUNOV-RATE STABILITY WITH THE SAME DEFICIT

Use INITIAL's smooth nowhere-zero autonomous divergence-free3D interface:
H>0, J=W/(4nuV), delta=J-H^2. Let chi(x)>=0 be the positive normal
Lyapunov exponent. Then

 integral_M (chi-H)^2 dmu<=delta,                               (A1)
 mu({chi=0})<=delta/(H^2+delta).                                (A2)

This does not say the whole flow is Anosov, that its unstable line is
smooth, or that entropy/exponents are acquired computational inputs.

Proof. The normal estimate gives chi(x)<=bar_s(x), the invariant time
average of the nonnegative s (normal trace-free eigenvalues +/-s). Put
s_1(x)=integral_0^1 s(phi_t x)dt. Discrete Birkhoff gives
bar_s=E(s_1|I_phi1). Jensen and volume invariance yield
 integral chi^2 <=integral bar_s^2 <=integral s_1^2
                                  <=integral s^2 <=J.
Ruelle gives lambda=integral chi>=H. Expanding the square proves A1:
 integral (chi-H)^2 <=J-2Hlambda+H^2 <=J-H^2.
For beta=mu({chi=0}), support-weighted Cauchy gives
 H^2<=lambda^2 <=(1-beta)integral chi^2 <=(1-beta)J,
which is A2. Also
mu({|chi-H|>=epsilon H})<=delta/(epsilon^2 H^2).
No pointwise derivative bound or certified numerical entropy estimate
is obtained.

B. QUALITATIVE RIGIDITY UNDER SUPPLIED COMPACTNESS

Precise sequential theorem. On a fixed closed smooth3-manifold let
(g_j,X_j) be smooth metrics and smooth divergence-free fields converging
in C^2 to a SMOOTH pair (g,X). Assume X nowhere zero. For normalized
g_j-volume entropy H_j, assume H_j->h>0 and
 (1/2)integral |Def_(g_j) X_j|^2 dmu_j-H_j^2 ->0.
Then the limiting pair has the exact locally homogeneous Sol/sl2 frame
on a finite orientation cover. Entropy continuity is NOT assumed or used.
This is a compactness theorem, not an explicit stability modulus.

Proof. Eventually X_j is uniformly nowhere zero. Identify its normal
bundles with N=X^perp using near-identity transverse projections; their
projectivizations become one fixed compact space. The normal projective
flows converge uniformly for each bounded time interval, since their
linear ODE coefficients and base fields converge in C^1. Let eta_j be
the invariant maximizing lifts constructed in INITIAL. Pass to a weak
convergent subsequence eta. Its marginal is mu because volume measures
converge. Its invariance follows from the uniform convergence of the
projective flows. INITIAL's identity and lambda_j>=H_j give
 integral |S_j-H_j(2Pi_l-P_j)|^2 d eta_j <=2delta_j ->0.
The integrands converge uniformly. Hence
 integral |S-h(2Pi_l-P)|^2 d eta=0.
Full support and continuity force q=b=0 and normal strain eigenvalues
+/-h everywhere. The only zero-integrand line is the +h eigenline E_+.
Thus eta is its graph measure. Projective invariance and full support
make E_+ invariant everywhere. SX=0 gives normal invariance; the pairing
ODE makes the orthogonal E_- invariant. Growth is exactly e^(+/-ht).

Every smooth first integral f has df=0: use negative time on E_+,
positive time on E_-, and invariance on X. In particular |X| is constant.
Orient E_+,E_- on a finite cover; smooth unit sections satisfy
 [X,U_+]=-hU_+, [X,U_-]=hU_-.
Write [U_+,U_-]=cX+dU_++eU_-. Jacobi gives Xc=0, Xd=hd, Xe=-he.
Compactness forces d=e=0, and the first-integral conclusion makes c
constant. Structure constants and frame metric coefficients are constant.

Uniform qualitative corollary: in ANY externally supplied C^2-compact
set K of SMOOTH pairs with smooth limits, uniformly nowhere-zero fields
and H>=h_0>0, distance of near minimizers to the local equality class
tends to zero with delta. Otherwise take a countersequence at distance
bounded below; compactness and bounded strain energy give a pair limit
and a convergent H-subsequence, and the theorem contradicts that distance.
This does not compute a modulus. Bounded power, volume, force or speed
does not supply such a K. INITIAL's family has C^1 convergence but
unbounded C^2 norms, consistently with this result.

C. EXPLICIT GEODESIC CURVATURE-VARIANCE PENALTY

Admitted subclass: Sigma is a closed oriented smooth surface with strictly
negative Gaussian curvature K. M=T^1 Sigma, X its unit-speed geodesic field,
mu normalized Liouville measure, H=h_mu(phi_1). The canonical frame has
 [X,H_0]=K V_0, [X,V_0]=-H_0, [H_0,V_0]=X.
For a supplied CONSTANT b>0 give these orthogonal fields squared lengths
1,b^2,b^-2. This metric g_b is Sasaki when b=1. Its volume is exactly
Liouville volume for all b, and X remains divergence-free and geodesic.
Retain the strain-viscosity sustaining force law.

Put m=-E_mu K>0, k2=E_mu K^2, v=Var_mu K=k2-m^2. Then
 J_b=W_b/(4nuVol(g_b))=(1/4)E_mu(b^2-K/b^2)^2,
 delta_b=J_b-H^2 >=(1/4)E_mu(b^2+K/b^2)^2.                     (C1)
Thus the natural Sasaki metric obeys
 delta_1 >=(1/4)E_mu(K+1)^2.                                   (C2)
Optimizing the RHS over the ONE constant b gives b^4=sqrt(k2), and for
every b,
 delta_b >=[sqrt(m^2+v)-m]/2
          =v/[2(sqrt(m^2+v)+m)].                               (C3)

These are explicit curvature-variance costs in this admitted geodesic
subclass. They supply no C^2 closeness of the base metric and do not cover
arbitrary flows or point-dependent transverse rescalings. Changing b
changes apparatus geometry and is not a free physical operation.

Complete derivation of the needed entropy comparison. Compactness and
K<0 give constants 0<kappa_-<=kappa_+ with
-kappa_+^2<=K<=-kappa_-^2. Along each geodesic solve j''+Kj=0 with a
zero at time -T and positive derivative there, then normalize j(0)=1.
Negativity keeps j,j' positive after -T. Its logarithmic derivative
satisfies u_T'+u_T^2+K=0. Riccati comparison with constant curvatures gives
 kappa_- coth(kappa_- T)<=u_T(0)<=kappa_+ coth(kappa_+ T).
The past-zero comparison makes u_T(0) decreasing in T. Indeed for S>T
the solution with past zero -S is positive at -T; the difference of
logarithmic derivatives satisfies
 (u_T-u_S)'=-(u_T+u_S)(u_T-u_S)
and retains the positive sign of the near-(-T) difference. Consequently
u(z)=lim_T u_T(0) exists and kappa_-<=u<=kappa_+.
Shifting the geodesic and taking the limit gives the invariant unstable
Jacobi line and Xu+u^2+K=0 along the flow. The measurable u and Xu are
bounded, so invariance and dominated difference quotients give
E Xu=0 and E u^2=-E K=m. The unstable Jacobi norm is
|j|sqrt(1+u^2) in the Sasaki metric. The square-root factor is bounded
above and below. Its exponent is the time average of u and is positive;
the opposite exponent is negative, the central one zero. Hence Ruelle,
Birkhoff and Cauchy give
                    H<=E u<=sqrt(E u^2)=sqrt(m).
No Pesin equality or Freire-Mane theorem is needed for this upper bound.

For the power calculation use the Lie derivative of g_b. In the
orthonormal normal frame H_0/b,bV_0, both offdiagonal strain entries are
(b^2-K/b^2)/2; all diagonal and mixed-X entries vanish. This gives J_b.
Since H^2<=m=-E K,
 delta_b>=J_b+E K=(1/4)E(b^2+K/b^2)^2.
For tau=b^4 the last expression equals
 (tau-2m+k2/tau)/4
 =(sqrt(k2)-m)/2+(tau-sqrt(k2))^2/(4tau).
This proves the optimizer; rationalizing sqrt(m^2+v)-m proves C3.

D. ACQUIRED FINITE PERMUTATIONS WITH ZERO SUSTAINING POWER

Input: an explicitly supplied finite permutation p on {0,...,N-1}.
Its full transition table is supplied capital. Acquire its cycles
C_0,...,C_(q-1), lengths k_i, and L=lcm(k_i). On the fixed flat
unit-volume3-torus with coordinates (theta,y,z), let
X=(1/L)partial_theta. It is Killing, divergence-free and geodesic.
F=0,p=0 sustain it; W=0 and its time-one translation has H=0.
Decode by i=floor(q y), j=floor(L theta) mod k_i, output C_i[j].
Time-one translation increases j by one; circle wrap works since k_i
divides L. Thus the decoder executes p except on cell boundaries.
Each state in cycle i has positive cell volume 1/(q k_i). Open interiors
are robust to sufficiently small perturbations for a specified finite
horizon and guards.

This is a uniform actual compiler from an acquired transition table,
not an unbounded-computation theorem. Store cycles and L rather than
materializing L angular intervals. The table takes O(N log N) bits;
log L<=sum_i log k_i<=O(N), so lcm acquisition is polynomial in the bit
length. A canonical encoding of C_i[j] uses
theta=(j+1/2)/L, y=(i+1/2)/q, with O(log L+log q) position bits.
For M steps with angular disturbance at most eta per step, the initial
half-cell guard is retained if M eta<1/(2L); timing/position precision
therefore costs O(log L+log M) bits. Decoding, initialization, clock
production and thermal noise are outside the sustaining-power law and
cannot be called free because its W vanishes.

check_second_attack.py constructs one10-state permutation, L=12, checks
144 exact rational interior fixtures and cell normalization. GENERAL
correctness is the modular proof above, not those fixtures. All seven
second-attack checks pass in0.23sec. This is a classical reversible/
integrable mechanism, not novel reversible computing, a violation of
thermodynamic laws, or an evasion of INITIAL's infinite distinct-state
volume bound. It shows why a per-step floor needs a more restricted
physical reliability/encoding interface.
