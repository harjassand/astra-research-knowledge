The highest-ceiling result is a complete, proper three-dimensional metric for which the coefficient-4 scalar-curvature form is uniformly positive, but every subcritical coefficient has form infimum minus infinity. This blocks every finite global diagonal torus stabilization of that one metric. The same example appears to contradict the finite-rank compact-exhaustion identity printed in Gromov, SIGMA 20 (2024), 038, §1.B1. Treat this as an internally proved counterexample candidate and a serious source-interface mismatch; external mathematical review and convention reconciliation remain open.

A second strong direction reconstructs field pairs from pure-symbol vanishing. For arbitrary characteristic-p height-one field pairs K^p⊆H and L^p⊆C with relative p-rank at least two, an already supplied global F_p-linear bijection preserving the pure-symbol-zero predicate in both directions determines a unique field-pair isomorphism, up to exactly one scalar in F_p*. The handwritten proof extends to infinite relative p-rank and imperfect bases. Rank one fails for every p. The theorem is a structural information result: it does not supply finite acquisition. For a computably presented H=K^p subclass, individual pure-symbol queries are decidable while isomorphism of the complete invariant is Π^0_3-complete.

The geometry counterexample

For a smooth complete base (M,g), define
  Q_a(φ)=∫_M (a|dφ|²+R_g φ²)dV_g,
  λ_a=inf_{φ∈C_c∞, φ≠0} Q_a(φ)/∫φ²dV_g.
The explicit warped cylinder M=R×S², g=dx²+f(x)²g_{S²}, f=sin q, in leaf10 has disjoint locally finite rounded dips in q, with dip depth δ_j=2^{-(j+5)} and rounding scale ε_j=δ_j³. Its metric is proper and complete. For w=2 log f, direct differentiation gives
  D=R_g+2Δw−|dw|²=2+2 cot²(q)(1−(q')²)≥2.
Taking v=e^{-w/2}=1/f, the ground-state identity is
  Q_4(φ)=4∫v²|d(φ/v)|²dV_g+∫Dφ²dV_g,
so λ_4≥2 for every compactly supported test.

The leaf10 cutoff tests φ_j=ζ(x−3j)/f have one fixed positive L² norm, a uniform upper bound Q_4(φ_j)≤C, and gradient energy at least 1/(72δ_j). Hence, for every fixed real a<4,
  Q_a(φ_j)=Q_4(φ_j)−(4−a)∫|dφ_j|²,
and λ_a=−∞. The proof uses one metric and individual compactly supported tests; it does not change the metric with a.

For any finite k≥1 global warp factors p_i, put P=Σ_i p_i. The scalar formula and Cauchy–Schwarz give
  R_{g×}≤R_g−2ΔP−(k+1)|dP|²/k
       =R_g−a_k ΔU/U,
  U=exp((k+1)P/(2k)),  a_k=4k/(k+1)<4.
If R_{g×} had any finite global lower bound σ, then (-a_kΔ+R_g)U≥σU. The ground-state transform would imply Q_{a_k}(φ)≥σ∫φ² for every compact test, contradicting λ_{a_k}=−∞. Thus every finite global rank has inf_M R_{g×}=−∞.

On each compact piece K, finite rank can depend on K: equal warps p_i=−w/k give R_{g×}=D−|dw|²/k there. The bounded-geometry leaf12 theorem gives a useful repair: if |Sec_g|≤K_0 and λ_4≥2 in dimension n, then R_g≥−n(n−1)K_0 and λ_{4k/(k+1)}≥(2k−n(n−1)K_0)/(k+1); every integer k>n(n−1)K_0/2 admits a positive global supersolution and a positive scalar lower bound. The counterexample escapes this hypothesis through unbounded curvature at shrinking necks.

Primary-source check: the publisher PDF defines Gromov’s invariant using one finite N and global warps (pp.1–2), then states Sc⋊(X)=lim_i Sc⋊(X_i) for compact equidimensional exhaustions in §1.B1 (p.4). Each compact piece of the constructed metric has Dirichlet λ_4≥2; hence the printed identity would give a finite-global value at least 2, whereas the fixed-metric theorem gives no finite global rank with any scalar lower bound. The gap is the exchange of a rank supremum with an exhaustion limit: rank may depend on i on each compact piece, while the whole-space definition fixes one finite N. The source’s exact intended convention and an external correction are unknown. The 2026 local-finite/countable warp interface is broader and does not erase this finite-global distinction.

Field reconstruction and acquisition

The pair invariant in leaf05 is V(K,H)=K*/H* as an F_p-vector space and
  N(x,y) iff x⊗y vanishes in
  W=(V⊗V)/span{[f]⊗[1−f]: f∈K\{0,1}}.
For K^p⊆H, L^p⊆C and relative p-ranks ≥2, the proof constructs relative p-basis Euler derivations. For p-independent s,t, the alternating logarithmic derivative pairing
  β([f],[g])=(D_s f/f)(D_t g/g)−(D_t f/f)(D_s g/g)
kills every Steinberg generator and satisfies β([s],[t])=1. This yields the local implication N([s],[u])⇒u∈H(s). A two-variable intersection computation, then a projective-line reconstruction, gives Θ=r α_* with α(H)=C; r∈F_p* is unique, and α is unique. Finite-support p-basis arguments make the derivation apply at infinite relative p-rank. This is internally reconstructed; the source release’s Theorem 1.1 states a narrower finite-type absolute case, so the relative/infinite extension still needs independent formal and expert review.

The rank-one failure is explicit: (F_p(t),F_p(t)^p) and (F_{p²}(u),F_{p²}(u)^p) have nonisomorphic fields but isomorphic countably infinite V spaces and all-zero degree-two symbol predicates, because Ω² of a one-variable function field is zero and Bloch–Gabber–Kato identifies the symbol quotient with logarithmic differential forms. The relative p-rank threshold is therefore sharp.

Acquisition has a separate barrier. Under an explicit opaque-handle finite-query interface, any finite adaptive pure-symbol transcript can be preserved while adjoining fresh p-basis variables; no such protocol can uniformly determine relative p-rank or isomorphism type. This does not apply to interfaces that separately provide a p-basis, rank, or complete effective enumeration. In the special computable family K=k(X,Y), H=K^p with k computable algebraically closed of fixed characteristic, N is decided by a Jacobian determinant. Nevertheless, isomorphism of the whole (V,N) structure is Π^0_3-complete by reduction from computable algebraically closed-field isomorphism using the 2026 reconstruction theorem. Thus pointwise query decidability does not make global invariant acquisition decidable.
