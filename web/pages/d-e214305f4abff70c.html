1. Exact ring-algebra consequences

In a nonzero unital ring R, if ab=1, ac=0, and c!=0, then ba!=1: otherwise c=bac=0. Hence e=1-ba is a nonzero idempotent. It is proper because ba!=0 (if ba=0, then abab=ab=1 but also a(ba)b=0). Conversely, a proper idempotent alone does not imply failure of direct finiteness. The associated right ideal P=eR is a nonzero finitely generated projective, and the one-sided inverse decomposition gives P⊕R≅R; thus [P]=0 in K_0(R). This explains why K_0(RG)≅Z alone does not exclude the proposed witness: there is no cancellation or positivity conclusion from that equality alone.

The independent Sol audit gives a stronger control example: for the bicyclic algebra T=k⟨a,b | ab=1⟩, its finite-matrix ideal and Laurent-polynomial quotient yield K_0(T)≅Z with augmentation inverse, while e=1-ba remains proper, nonzero, and has class zero. T is not a group algebra, so this is not a counterexample to BLR; it proves that K_0 plus augmentation, without an added cancellation/detection premise, cannot supply the missing step.

Release197 claims that its constructed torsion-free finitely presented G has a,b,c∈F_2[G] with ab=1, ac=0, c!=0. If its full construction is correct, the displayed algebra therefore yields a proper nontrivial idempotent and failure of direct finiteness. The graph, presentation, and witnesses have not been materialized in this workspace; the existence statement is SOURCE-REPORTED and its truth remains UNKNOWN here.

2. Published hyperbolic idempotent conflict and its exact scope

BLR, “The K-theoretic Farrell–Jones conjecture for hyperbolic groups,” *Inventiones Mathematicae* 172 (2008), arXiv:math/0701434v2, prints in its applications list that if R is a field of prime characteristic p and every finite subgroup of G is a p-group, then RG has only idempotents 0 and 1. The field branch has no soficity assumption. Thus, if the N129 output were a torsion-free word-hyperbolic group with the release197 witness, the two statements would be incompatible at p=2.

The companion BLR article, “On the Farrell–Jones Conjecture and its applications,” *Journal of Topology* 1 (2008), arXiv:math/0703548, separately states a generic K_0 route. Its Theorem 0.12 requires stable finiteness, characteristic zero, or (for skew-fields) soficity. Those conditions do not automatically apply to the hypothetical nonsofic F_2[G]. The companion’s §1.6 nevertheless attributes a positive-characteristic idempotent criterion to Formanek and Burger–Valette and says torsion-free word-hyperbolic groups satisfy it.

The inspected primary proofs do not support that attribution as written. Formanek (1973), §2, Lemmas 5–7, cancels each nonidentity conjugacy-class trace in characteristic p. Burger–Valette (1998), p.226, Lemma 4.1, concludes only that the canonical trace τ(e) is 0 or 1. Their Proposition 4.2 obtains trivial idempotents in CΓ by invoking Kaplansky’s characteristic-zero faithful-trace theorem; Remark 4.3 applies this to hyperbolic groups over C. The missing step is a characteristic-p detector taking τ(e)∈{0,1} to e∈{0,1}. The char-p trace statement alone does not provide it.

This is a proof-support mismatch, not a counterexample to the printed BLR theorem. A separate BLR/FJ argument, a later correction, or a gap in the N129 construction could resolve the apparent collision. The exact state is: BLR’s statement is printed and published; the N129 conclusion is conditional; their conjunction would be contradictory; mathematical resolution UNKNOWN.

4. Independent approximate-permutation route

N06/N27/N65 target nonsoficity by a finite rank obstruction. Under the explicit triangle/rectangle interface, for every D-point permutation assignment, triangle defects δ_t and rectangle fixed fractions λ_i obey

  Δ ≤ Σ_t δ_t + (θ r²/4) Σ_i λ_i,
  Δ = θNr/2 − 2m − r|E|.

The proof passes from permutation trace moments to real rank, controls loss modulo q with bounded integer minors, and uses rank-one polynomial interpolation on the D(5,q) incidence graph. It never divides by D in F_q, so q|D is harmless. At q>r the safe real-to-finite-field rank factor is 1/2; if q>r³, the stronger factor 5/6 is available. Exact checks of the supplied parameter budgets show the stated tolerances contradict the inequality.
