# Adversarial re-audit of the modified ternary product-R theorem

7 October2026 programme continuation, cycle11. Re-audit of `notes/metric_acquisition/cycle10/MODIFIED_TERNARY_FACE_NONVANISHING.md`; complete proof candidate still requires an independent root/peer adoption gate. No new fixture claims are used as proof.

The statement survives this re-audit: Q is real3-by-3 positive definite; R is a nonzero product of real diagonal quadratics; Gamma=R adj(Hess f) grad[(det Hess f)R] has nonzero three-coordinate extactic. The crucial assumptions are real diagonal factors and QPD principal binary restrictions. Arbitrary complex factors or arbitrary non-diagonal factors are not covered.

## 1. Re-derived normal cross term

Fix a binary pair z=(u,v), put t=y_k, p=f(z,0), h_2=det Hess p, q=4(Q_ki u^2+Q_kj v^2), w=(Q_ki u,Q_kj v). Write R=c t^(2m)R_0+O(t^(2m+2)) with c!=0, and q!=0 first. Direct block inversion at generic q h_2!=0 gives

    (adj H)_II=q adj H_2+O(t^2),
    (adj H)_Ik=-8t adj H_2 w+O(t^3).

The normal derivative of hR is 2m c t^(2m-1) q h_2 R_0+O(t^(2m+1)). Thus its off-diagonal-adjugate contribution is -16m c t^(2m) q h_2 R_0 adj H_2 w. Multiplying by the outer R changes the derivative of q from coefficient1 to coefficient1-2m, not1-m. The complete coefficient is

    [t^(4m)]Gamma_I
      =c^2 q^(2m+1)R_0 adj H_2 grad(h_2 R_0 q^(1-2m)).

It is a polynomial after canceling powers; the rational expression is only a convenient generic identity. For m=0 the normal cross term starts two orders later and the same formula specializes correctly. For q=0 the whole quartic splits and the Hessian is block diagonal for all t, making the leading coefficient (12Q_kk)^2c^2 t^(4m+4)R_0 adj H_2 grad(h_2R_0). No division by q is used in that branch.

## 2. Audit of all degree and factor exceptions

The binary p has four simple noncoordinate complex roots, with nonreal squared-coordinate ratios. Every nonzero real diagonal quadratic and h_2 are invertible at these roots. The effective rational potential g has degree e=6+2r_0-4m, possibly positive, zero or negative. For e!=0, its simple-root Jacobian evaluation eP'G is nonzero regardless of the sign of e; poles of g occur only away from the roots used in the argument.

When e=0, the implication Jac(p,g)=0 forces g constant on P^1. Here m>=2 and r_0=2m-3, so q^(2m-1) is a polynomial positive power. The resulting identity h_2R_0=constant*q^(2m-1) cannot be repaired by factors in R_0: h_2 divides its left side and hence would divide the single-q power on its right. For b=Q_ij!=0, h_2 as a quadratic in u^2,v^2 has discriminant (ac-b^2)(9ac-b^2)>0 and two distinct nonzero squared-variable factors. A single real diagonal quadratic q is only one linear factor in those squared variables. For b=0, h_2 is proportional to u^2v^2; q cannot contain both coordinate factors unless it were proportional to uv, excluded by diagonality. Thus h_2 never divides a q power. This addresses both axis factors and all repeated factors of R.

The projective-pencil lemma applies to Gamma because homogeneity and full sign equivariance are preserved by the outer R and the even hR. It does not require R positive, Gamma nonsingular on coordinate faces, or a nonzero Jacobian at every point. The pair calculation proves Gamma is not projectively radial and rules out each possible coordinate pencil center, which is exactly what that lemma needs.

## 3. Why this is not yet a general induction

For an active set I and omitted set J, when no omitted row form is identically zero on I, the full canonical field restricts to

    Gamma_I=R_I adj(H_I) grad(h_I R_I),
    R_I=product_(j in J)4 sum_(i in I)Q_ji y_i^2.

This gives a hereditary family. A zero full extactic, however, need not give a zero extactic of every coordinate restriction: rank deficiency in n variables can still allow full rank in a smaller number of active variables, and rational relation coefficients may have poles there. Polynomial matrix rank itself cannot increase under a defined restriction. One must use polynomial rank minors or a proved coordinate-span inclusion, not restrict those coefficients blindly.

When some omitted rows vanish identically, epsilon normalization is subtler for derivative minors than for the pair identity. Let Z be those rows. On y_Z=epsilon z and all other omitted coordinates zero, F_I has leading epsilon^(4|Z|)h_Z^2 Gamma_I. But F_Z is order epsilon^(4|Z|-3); derivatives of the epsilon corrections in F_I along F_Z can appear before the nominal tangential D_I iterates. Thus the clean lowest coefficient proof of no pair ratio does not automatically identify a higher-order coordinate extactic with E_Gamma. This is the precise unresolved step in a zero-row induction.

Even with all row forms nonzero, the ternary pencil mechanism is specific to P^2. A higher-dimensional trajectory lying in a hyperplane need not make that entire hyperplane invariant. The already retained face-curvature example also precludes replacing this missing step by an assumed full-support attractor. A valid global invariant must address these two obstructions explicitly.
