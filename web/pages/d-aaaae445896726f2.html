## Proposition 1: each single normalized inverse-support generates a counterexample

**Statement.** Let k be any field and let a,b in k[G] satisfy ab=1 but ba != 1. For any t in supp(b), put b'=b t^{-1} and a'=t a; let H_b be the subgroup generated by supp(b'). Then k[H_b] itself contains a one-sided inverse pair c,b' with c b'=1 but b' c !=1. Dually, for any t in supp(a), set a'=t^{-1}a, b'=b t, H_a=<supp(a')>; there exists d in k[H_a] with a'd=1 but d a' !=1. Consequently, each of H_a and H_b is nonsofic and nonamenable (using sofic direct finiteness and amenable => sofic), irrespective of what the rest of G does.

**Proof for b.** Both a'b'=1 and b'a'=ba !=1. Decompose k[G] as the direct sum of the left-coset subspaces u k[H_b]. Right multiplication by b' preserves each summand, and 1 lies in the identity summand k[H_b]. Let c be the k[H_b]-component of a'. Projecting a'b'=1 gives c b'=1. If b' c=1, then a'=a'(b'c)=(a'b')c=c, yielding b'a'=1, contradiction. Hence b'c !=1.

**Proof for a.** Here a'b'=1 and b'a'=ba !=1. Decompose k[G] as direct sum of right-coset subspaces k[H_a]u. Left multiplication by a' preserves them. Let d be the k[H_a]-component of b'. Then a'd=1. If d a'=1, then b'=(d a')b'=d(a'b')=d, hence b'a'=1, contradiction. QED.

This is a **support-localization**, stronger than merely generating a witness subgroup from supp(a) union supp(b). It is an elementary coset-projection argument and may be implicit in published cellular-automaton subgroup-restriction results. It is **not** certified as new to the wider literature. For family-197 it applies to the *original* group and its particular a,b, without changing the group.

**Corollary 1 (uniform single-support expansion).** For k=F_2, fix t in S_b=supp(b) and set K=S_b t^{-1}, so 1 in K and H_b=<K> nonamenable. There is an epsilon_b>0 depending only on K such that every nonempty finite T subset G obeys

    |T S_b| = |T K| >= (1+epsilon_b)|T|.

To prove existence, if there were finite nonempty F_n subset H_b with |F_n K|/|F_n| -> 1, then because 1 in K, for each k in K we have |F_n triangle F_n k| <= 2(|F_n K|-|F_n|)=o(|F_n|). The same asymptotic holds for k^-1 and products, producing a right Folner sequence for the finitely generated group H_b, contradicting nonamenability. For arbitrary T subset G, partition T into left H_b-cosets and apply the H_b inequality in each coset; summing yields the claim. The identical statement holds for S_a with the appropriate left-multiplication convention (or after inverting elements). In particular, the normalized support must contain >=3 elements: one- or two-element supports generate an amenable subgroup.

## Proposition 2: rare-source entropy expands rather than contracts in every fixed window

**Statement.** Let G be countable, k=F_2, b=sum_{s in S} b_s s nonzero, X=(X_g) iid Bernoulli(p) with 0<p<1, and Y=T_b X, (T_b X)(g)=sum_s b_s X(gs) modulo 2. For any nonempty finite T subset G, set U=T S. Then, as p decreases to 0 with T fixed,

    H(Y restricted to T)= |U| p log(1/p) + O_T(p).

Moreover, under the one-sided inverse hypothesis ab=1 != ba,

    lim_{p->0} H(Y_T) / (|T| H_2(p)) = |T S|/|T| >= 1+epsilon_b,

where H_2(p)=-p log p -(1-p) log(1-p).

**Proof.** Each u in U yields the nonzero single-activation signature v_u=(b_{t^{-1}u})_{t in T} in F_2^T. It is nonzero because u belongs to t S for at least one t. Let n_v be the number of u in U having signature v !=0; sum_{v !=0}n_v=|U|, even when signatures collide. Since there are |U| independent input bits influencing Y_T, as p->0

    P(Y_T=v)=n_v p+O_T(p^2) for v !=0,
    P(Y_T=0)=1-|U|p+O_T(p^2).

Consequently
    H(Y_T) = |U|p log(1/p) + p(|U|-sum_{v !=0} n_v log n_v)
             + O_T(p^2 log(1/p)).
As H_2(p)=p log(1/p)+p+O(p^2), the ratio follows. In particular, confusing the number of **distinct signatures** with the number of activating **input coordinates** gives a false, potentially spectacular zero-entropy argument: collisions do not change the leading p log(1/p) coefficient.

**Interpretation / scope.** B is injective and X(p) is Bernoulli, so the image pmp action is measure conjugate to Bernoulli(p). For every *fixed* observation window T, for sufficiently small p the naive upper bound H(Y_T)/|T| is actually strictly *larger* than H_2(p), and thus cannot contradict Seward's formula h_Rok(Y)=min(H_2(p),h_sup). This directly blocks the simple nonlinear strategy of switching from uniform Haar to very sparse iid inputs and extracting a fixed-window entropy deficit from pattern collisions. **Crucial quantifier:** the error and the threshold p_T depend on T; this does NOT prove a lower bound uniform in T, or imply positive Rokhlin entropy, or preclude a genuinely nonlocal nonlinear generator whose window changes with p.

## Distinct approaches audited and unresolved mathematics

**Zero direction.** The existing Haar splitting and strict upper bound do not iterate automatically to zero. Sparse biased input coding was examined separately; Proposition 2 identifies a countervailing *support expansion* at first order. A strategy based on counting only distinct single-defect signatures is mathematically invalid, since multiplicities contribute to first-order Shannon entropy. Nonlocal measurable generators with p-dependent observation scales remain entirely open. Infinite products of algebraic kernels are not equivalent to infinite-base Bernoulli shifts without an independent classification/entropy theorem.

**Positive direction.** Proposition 1 finds finitely generated *nonsofic* support subgroups, and Corollary 1 gives an isoperimetric inequality. Neither a Cheeger constant nor nonamenability alone gives a **lower bound** on Rokhlin entropy of a free ergodic action. Importing a sofic entropy lower bound would be circular, because G and the support subgroup are nonsofic. Positive l2-Betti number, cost and mixing were considered as candidate interfaces, but none was established for the original G in a form yielding a positive Rokhlin entropy bound. No positive action was constructed.

**Bernoulli conjugacy.** No claim of measured isomorphism of different Shannon bases. Seward Thm 1.10 says h_sup>0 separates uncountably many finite-base Bernoulli shifts. Seward Corollary 7.7 establishes h_sup=0 if and only if **no free ergodic pmp action has positive Rokhlin entropy**, finite or infinite. Thus Theorems 1.10 and 1.12 give zero Rokhlin entropy for **all** nontrivial Bernoulli shifts, including those with infinite-Shannon bases, when h_sup=0. Equal Rokhlin entropy does not yield measured isomorphism.

**Exact missing bridge.** To establish zero, one needs a generating partition for Bernoulli(p)^G of arbitrarily low Shannon entropy, e.g. a nonlinear G-equivariant lossless code beyond all fixed-window rare-event bounds, with a rigorous count of mass of coding states. To establish positive, one needs an entropy lower-bound invariant or a free ergodic action with proven positive Rokhlin entropy that survives *nonsoficity*. The support-localization and Cheeger estimates alone supply neither.


## Correction of predecessor's mistaken infinite-entropy caveat

The predecessor file \`state/2026-10-08-bowen-bernoulli-direct-finiteness/RESEARCH.md\`, Theorem C(2)--(4), and its accompanying audits, suggest that h_sup^Rok(G)=0 could coexist with an infinite-positive-Rokhlin-entropy free ergodic action. **This assertion is false**: Seward, *Krieger's finite generator theorem for actions of countable groups II*, **Corollary 7.7** proves that h_sup^Rok(G)=0 iff every free ergodic pmp G-action has Rokhlin entropy zero (including the infinite-entropy possibility). It combines his finite generator theorem with the infinity case; it must be included in the dependency chain. Accordingly the "POS versus h_sup=0" extra obstacle is spurious. The actual unsolved "POS implies INF?" question in Seward is whether any **positive** Rokhlin entropy action forces unbounded finite Rokhlin entropies, i.e. h_sup=+infinity, not whether positive actions can exist with h_sup=0. Since this family has a finite strict upper bound, such a general theorem would **imply h_sup=0** for the family, but the converse is not known from the displayed assumptions and the conjecture is open.

The original Bowen full measurable-isomorphism question remains separate: h_sup=0 and equality of Rokhlin entropies of all Bernoulli bases are necessary but not sufficient results for isomorphism. No isomorphism construction or obstruction beyond entropy was found.
