Verdict: the exact literature supports non-explosion for fixed-rate bimolecular ordinary endotactic stochastic mass action, not recurrence in every communicating class. The decisive unresolved bridge is stationary probability per closed class (or a separate classwise Foster/recovery proof). No broad recurrence breakthrough or external priority clearance is established.

Xu (arXiv:2409.05340v2), Definitions 3.1 and 4.1–4.4 and Theorem 4.6: “regular” means stochastic non-explosion plus deterministic global existence; all second-order 1-endotactic systems are regular, so ordinary endotactic systems are included. The proof controls total-count growth and is not an inward-drift theorem. Theorem 4.12 gives uniqueness only if a stationary probability already exists in a closed class. Theorem 4.13 reduces positive recurrence of a bimolecular weakly reversible system to existence of stationary probability in every communicating class, which is closed for weakly reversible networks. Corollary 4.17 gives the same existence/recurrence equivalence on a closed class for second-order 1-endotactic systems.

Wiuf–Xu (arXiv:2012.07954v2), Theorem 4.11 / Corollary 4.12: the stronger recurrence statement is one-dimensional and class-structural. It assumes H2 (rank-one stoichiometry and a noncatalyst), H3 (both directional subnetworks nonempty), H4 (nonnegative gcd direction), plus standard fixed-rate mass action and endotacticity. It distinguishes closed positive irreducible components from open quasi-irreducible components. Weakly reversible one-dimensional infinite classes fall under its positive-recurrence result; c10_l02 is therefore a reconstruction, not new recurrence prior art.

Weakly reversible deficiency-zero networks are a separate solved subclass. ACK (arXiv:0803.3042), Theorems 4.1–4.2, gives product-Poisson stationary laws and normalized restrictions on closed irreducible components. ACKCK (arXiv:1708.09356), Theorem 3, supplies non-explosion for complex-balanced stochastic mass action. Together these give componentwise positive recurrence, but do not cover arbitrary endotactic systems.
