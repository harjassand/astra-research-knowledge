SU(3) WALL WEIGHTED OPERATOR: EXACT ADJOINT BLOCK AND A FAILED
ORDINARY-CASIMIR COUNT SHORTCUT
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: exact block calculation and analytic counterexample. This does not
determine the full weighted rank profile or its weighted spectral count.

1. SETUP AND THE EXACT ISOTYPIC FORM

Let a,b>=1, N=a+b, lambda=(a,b), and use the supplied normalization

  Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2,
  C2=(a^2+ab+b^2+3a+3b)/3,
  C3=(a-b)(2a+b+3)(a+2b+3)/18,
  alpha=C3/C2,
  Delta=C2(C2/3+1/4)-C3^2/C2,
  C_S=Delta/N^2,
  T_A=d pi(t_A),  S_A=(D_A-alpha T_A)/N.

For a matrix X on V_(a,b), set

  L_T X=sum_A[T_A,[T_A,X]],
  L_S X=sum_A[S_A,[S_A,X]],
  E_wall(X)=<X,(L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)))X>.

The interior adjoint frame identities are

  Tr(T_A T_B)=d C2 delta_AB/8,
  Tr(S_A S_B)=d C_S delta_AB/8,
  Tr(T_A S_B)=0,
  [T_A,S_B]=i f_ABC S_C,
  sum_A T_A^2=C2 I,  sum_A S_A^2=C_S I.

The conjugation action decomposes as

  End(V_lambda)=direct_sum_nu V_nu tensor M_nu.

Covariance of S and invariance of the summed index imply [L_S,L_T]=0 and
L_S|_(V_nu tensor M_nu)=I_(V_nu) tensor K_nu for a positive matrix K_nu.
Thus the exact blockwise operator is

  (E_wall)_nu = I_(V_nu) tensor
       [ c2(nu)/N^2 I_(M_nu) + K_nu/(N sqrt(C_S)) ].       (1)

In particular E_wall>=L_T/N^2 on every block. Formula (1) isolates the
only unresolved operator: the multiplicity-space matrix K_nu.

2. EXACT TWO-BY-TWO MATRIX ON THE ADJOINT ISOTYPIC BLOCK

For every interior SU(3) carrier the adjoint multiplicity is exactly two;
the two copies are spanned by {T_A} and {S_A}. For fixed A, put

  G_T=||T_A||_HS^2=d C2/8,
  G_S=||S_A||_HS^2=d C_S/8,
  e_T=T_A/sqrt(G_T),  e_S=S_A/sqrt(G_S).

The adjoint-block matrix of L_S in this orthonormal multiplicity basis is

  K_adj = [ 3 C_S/C2,  -6(alpha/N) sqrt(C_S/C2) ;
             -6(alpha/N) sqrt(C_S/C2),  kappa ],        (2)

where kappa=E_S(S_A)/||S_A||_HS^2 is real and satisfies

  12 alpha^2/N^2 <= kappa <= 3 C_E,
  C_E=115/6.                                               (3)

Proof of the first column: covariance gives [T_B,S_A]=i f_BAC S_C.
The SU(3) adjoint contraction sum_(B,C) f_ABC^2=3 yields

  <T_A,L_S T_A>=E_S(T_A)=3G_S.

Also [S_B,T_A]=[T_B,S_A], so

  <S_A,L_S T_A>
    =sum_B <[S_B,S_A],[S_B,T_A]>
    =E_TS(S_A).

The exact adjoint double-commutator identities in
wall_su3/GENERIC_FILTER_OBSTRUCTION.txt give

  E_T(S_A)=3G_S,
  E_TS(S_A)=-(2alpha/N)E_T(S_A)=-(6alpha/N)G_S.

Since L_S is covariant and the adjoint multiplicity is two, L_S(T_A) lies
in span{T_A,S_A}; these two pairings prove

  L_S(T_A)=(3C_S/C2)T_A-(6alpha/N)S_A,

which is the first column of (2). Self-adjointness gives the other
off-diagonal entry. Positivity of K_adj gives its determinant inequality,
which is the lower bound on kappa in (3).

For the upper bound, the exact arbitrary-matrix estimate from the SU(3)
commutator calculation is

  E_S(X) <= [40 C2/(3N^2)+2alpha^2/N^2] E_T(X).

Here C2<=4N^2/3, alpha^2<=C2/3+1/4, and N>=2, so the bracket is at most
115/6. Since E_T(S_A)=3||S_A||^2, kappa<=3C_E. This also bounds the
largest eigenvalue of K_adj by 3C_E.

Consequently the two eigenvalues (each repeated eight times in the full
adjoint isotypic component) of E_wall there satisfy

  3/N^2 <= lambda_- <= 3/N^2 + 3 sqrt(C_S)/(N C2),       (4)

  3/N^2 + 12 alpha^2/(N^3 sqrt(C_S))
       <= lambda_+ <= 3/N^2 + 3 C_E/(N sqrt(C_S)).       (5)

The lower bound in (5) is the Rayleigh quotient at e_S; the upper bound
uses K_adj<=3C_E I. The upper bound in (4) uses the Rayleigh quotient at
e_T, while positivity of L_S gives its lower bound.

3. EXACT NEAR-WALL TWO-MODE CROSSOVER IN THIS BLOCK

Consider any sequence with N=a+b->infinity, a>=b>=1 and b/N->0. Then

  C2/N^2 -> 1/3,
  alpha/N -> 1/3,
  C_S/[b(b+2)] -> 1/4.                                  (6)

The last limit follows directly from

  C_S/[b(b+2)] = a(a+2)(N+1)(N+3)/(12 C2 N^2).

It follows from (4) that

  lambda_-=(3+o(1))/N^2.                                 (7)

Indeed, the correction in (4) divided by 3/N^2 is
N sqrt(C_S)/C2=(3/2+o(1))sqrt(b(b+2))/N=o(1).

Since alpha/N->1/3 and sqrt(C_S)=o(N), (5) gives

  lambda_+ = Theta(1/(N sqrt(C_S)))
            = Theta(1/(N sqrt(b(b+2)))).                 (8)

Thus when b is fixed the two adjoint-copy costs are respectively Theta(N^-2)
and Theta(N^-1); for b->infinity with b=o(N), the second is Theta(1/(Nb)).
In a regular interior sequence b/N->theta>0, C_S=Theta(N^2); (1) and
K_adj<=3C_E I instead give both adjoint costs Theta(N^-2). This is a
rigorous two-copy crossover: the T-aligned mode remains on the Casimir
scale, while the other adjoint mode has the fiber penalty scale near the
wall. It is a diagnostic for the proposed fiber picture, not a theorem
about all multiplicity blocks.

In particular, no carrier-uniform secondary ellipticity estimate

  K_nu >= c c2(nu) I, c>0,                               (9)

can hold for all interior carriers: on the adjoint block, the T-aligned
Rayleigh quotient of K_adj divided by c2(1,1)=3 is C_S/C2, which tends to
zero like b^2/N^2. The full weighted operator still has the first term
c2(nu)/N^2 in (1); (9)'s failure is not a failure of E_wall>=L_T/N^2.

4. THE PROPOSED ORDINARY-CASIMIR COUNT ROUTE IS FALSE

The proposed shortcut was: if, uniformly for a>=b>=1 and
0<T<=cN,

  D_T(T):=sum_(nu:c2(nu)<=T) mult(V_nu,End(V_(a,b))) dim(V_nu)
        <= C [T^2+(b/N)T^3],                              (10)

then E_wall>=L_T/N^2 would imply the desired weighted spectral count.
The estimate (10) is false. In fact, an exact stable tensor range gives a
lower bound of order T^3 for T up to order b^2.

First, the Weyl character identity gives, for any dominant nu,

  mult(V_nu,End(V_lambda))
   = mult(V_lambda in V_lambda tensor V_nu)
   = sum_(w in W) (-1)^ell(w)
         mult_(V_nu)(lambda+rho-w(lambda+rho)).           (11)

For lambda=(a,b), a>=b, every nonidentity shift
delta_w=lambda+rho-w(lambda+rho) is in Q_+ and has simple-root height
at least b+1. Indeed, in the inversion sum for delta_w every coefficient
<lambda+rho,alpha^vee> is at least b+1 and every positive root has height
at least one. Every weight gamma of V_(p,q) satisfies

  <gamma,rho^vee> <= <(p,q),rho^vee> = p+q,

because rho^vee is dominant and the weight polytope is conv(W(p,q)).
Thus, if p+q<=b, no nonidentity delta_w is a weight of V_(p,q), and (11)
reduces exactly to

  mult(V_(p,q),End(V_(a,b)))=dim V_(p,q)[0].             (12)

For SU(3), this zero-weight multiplicity is

  dim V_(p,q)[0] = min(p,q)+1 if p congruent q (mod 3),
                    0 otherwise.                         (13)

For completeness, (13) follows from a Gelfand-Tsetlin pattern for the
GL(3) highest weight (p+q,q,0). Zero SU(3) weight requires all bottom
weights equal s=(p+2q)/3, so it is possible exactly when p congruent q
mod 3. The middle row (m1,m2) then obeys m1+m2=2s and

  q<=m1<=p+q,  0<=m2<=q,  m1>=m2.

The admissible integer interval has min(p,q)+1 points.

Now take r=floor(b/2)>=12 and
I={ceil(r/2),...,r}. For every p,q in I with p congruent q mod 3,
p+q<=2r<=b, so (12)-(13) apply. By Cauchy-Schwarz across the three
residue classes, the number of these pairs is at least |I|^2/3>=r^2/12.
Each has multiplicity at least r/2 and

  dim V_(p,q)=(p+1)(q+1)(p+q+2)/2 >= r^3/8.

Also c2(p,q)=(p^2+pq+q^2+3p+3q)/3 <= r^2+2r <=(r+1)^2.
Therefore the exact cumulative dimension obeys

  D_T((r+1)^2) >= r^6/192.                               (14)

Choose, for example, b=floor(N^(1/4)), a=N-b. Then r->infinity,
b/N->0, and T_N=(r+1)^2<=cN for every fixed c>0 once N is large.
From (14),

  [T_N^2+(b/N)T_N^3]/D_T(T_N)
       <= C0 [r^-2+b/N] -> 0,

for a fixed numerical C0. Hence no carrier-independent C can make (10)
hold in the stated range. This counterexample uses an exact Weyl-character
calculation; it is not a finite numerical extrapolation.

Because the cumulative eigenvalue count of E_wall is bounded above by the
count of L_T/N^2 only when (10) holds, this route cannot prove the weighted
count. The failure does not show the weighted count itself fails: L_S may
lift many of the low ordinary-Casimir components. The adjoint calculation
above confirms such a lift for the secondary adjoint copy while leaving a
T-aligned low copy.

5. WHAT THIS AUDIT DOES NOT RESOLVE

Equation (1) is an exact all-isotypic reduction, but K_nu remains unknown
for general nu and growing multiplicity. The adjoint block establishes
the near-wall scale of the first fiber-spin mode only. The exact ordinary
count counterexample rules out dropping L_S from a proposed weighted
projector bound. To prove a matching wall profile one still needs either
an operator comparison for K_nu in terms of a justified U(2)-branching or
fiber-spin operator, or a counterexample in a growing block showing that
such a comparison fails. No Sol upper filters, operational transfer, or
optimal all-Q law is claimed here.
