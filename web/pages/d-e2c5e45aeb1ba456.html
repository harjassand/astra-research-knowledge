Conventions and exact legal example
------------------------------------
This packet uses positive Heisenberg L, so the paper's dissipative generator
is mathcal L=-L. For p>1 and positive f,

 E_p(f)=p/[2(p-1)] <I_(p/(p-1)),p(f), L(f)>_sigma.

Take sigma=I/2 and the primitive tracial qubit generator

 L(f)=f-[Tr(f)/2]I,
 exp(-tL)=e^(-t)Id+(1-e^(-t))[Tr(.)/2]I.

This is unital CP, sigma stationary, tracial/KMS selfadjoint and primitive.
The positive observable f=diag(1,2) is strictly positive. The source weighted
power maps reduce exactly to ordinary positive powers for tracial sigma, and
<a,b>_sigma=Tr(ab)/2. At p=3,

 I_(3/2),3(f)=f^2, I_2,3(f)=f^(3/2)=diag(1,2sqrt2),
 E_3(f)=(3/4)[Tr(f^2 Lf)/2]=9/16,
 E_2(I_2,3(f))=(2sqrt2-1)^2/4=(9-4sqrt2)/4.

Therefore the literal printed weak condition has gap

 E_3(f)-2E_2(I_2,3(f))=2sqrt2-63/16<0.

The inequality is exact, for example sqrt2<3/2 gives a negative upper bound.
No state search or floating-point approximation is used. The failure occurs
already for a positive observable, so it is separate from any signed-power
scope issue.

For any p>2, a positive classical perturbation f=I+epsilon g of a constant
in this same tracial model has
E_p(f)/E_2(I_2,p(f)) ->2/p as epsilon->0. Since p-1>2/p for every p>2,
the printed p-1 lower bound fails sufficiently near the positive constant.
The exact p3 example alone already refutes the universal literal statement.

Independent source sign-scope inconsistency
--------------------------------------------
The displayed absolute-value definition of I_p,q on printed p6 produces
I_p,p(f)=Gamma^(-1/p)(|Gamma^(1/p)f|), which is not f for negative Hermitian
f. The same page nevertheless states I_p,p(f)=f for all Hermitian f.
For tracial sigma and f=-diag(1,2), the displayed absolute power gives
I_2,2(f)=|f|, and the literal Def7 E2 becomes
<|f|,Lf>_sigma=-E2(|f|)<0, contrary to Prop8's quadratic E2 formula.
Positive inputs avoid this problem. A signed-polar power convention would
be an amended definition and must be labeled explicitly rather than silently
substituted for the printed absolute-value map.
