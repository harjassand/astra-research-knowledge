Title:Noncommuting mixed states cannot be broadcast
Authors:H. Barnum, C. M. Caves, C. A. Fuchs, R. Jozsa, B. Schumacher
View a PDF of the paper titled Noncommuting mixed states cannot be broadcast, by H. Barnum and 4 other authors
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:  We show that, given a general mixed state for a quantum system, there are no physical means for {\it broadcasting\/} that state onto two separate quantum systems, even when the state need only be reproduced marginally on the separate systems. This result generalizes and extends the standard no-cloning theorem for pure states.
