The positive theorem, with exact interface
------------------------------------------
Base p: full-dimensional log-concave Lebesgue density, known mean m and
covariance Sigma. Target q=r p: arbitrary density, possibly non-log-concave,
with supplied/acquired chi-square cost delta=E_p(r-1)^2. Assuming
Var_p(Y^T M Y)<=8||M||_HS^2 for whitened Y, the exact moment Gaussians obey

  ||Sigma^(-1/2)(m_q-m)||^2 <= delta,
  ||Sigma^(-1/2)Sigma_q Sigma^(-1/2)-I||_HS <= sqrt(8delta)+delta.

If eta=sqrt(8delta)+delta<1,

  KL(G_q||G_p) <= delta/2+eta^2/[4(1-eta)].

For an unnormalized likelihood with known log-range B, the sharp bound is
delta<=sinh^2(B/2), independent of its unknown normalizing constant. In
particular B<=epsilon<=1/4 certifies TV(G_q,G_p)<=epsilon. One may reuse
the old Gaussian without acquiring the target covariance. The statement
controls the entire HS covariance perturbation, not merely its largest
eigenvalue. Its normalization removes n and Sigma's condition number from
the mathematical guarantee; it does not remove numerical acquisition costs.

Named consequence: Gaussian moment-surrogate continuation for a sequential
posterior or likelihood-tempering task. When refresh-point posteriors are
log-concave, a total supplied log-likelihood contrast L allows at most
ceil(L/epsilon)+1 moment refreshes. Loewner-only covariance certificates
need contrast steps of order epsilon/sqrt(n). The stronger Q8 premise removes
that size penalty from the refresh CERTIFICATE. Full moment-oracle cost,
O(n^2) covariance storage, O(n^3) ordinary factorization and conditioning
remain charged. The strongest equally informed Q8 baseline reproduces this
duality argument, so no computational separation or new estimator is claimed.

Gaussian likelihoods on an unbounded state space usually have infinite
global log-range; they do not automatically meet this certificate. A finite
chi-square/score-information budget must be supplied or acquired. Finite
samples do not establish a global contrast bound. Approximation of the
original posterior, actual MCMC warm starts, and acceptance probabilities
are outside the result.

General path consequence and its boundary
-----------------------------------------
For any differentiable log-concave density path with L2 score s_t, its
moment Gaussian has Fisher speed squared <=5 E s_t^2. This yields
Hellinger and TV bounds <=sqrt(5J(p,q))/2 for two log-concave endpoints with
finite Jeffreys divergence. It covers changes from nonlinear scores, beyond
natural linear tilts. Exp(1) versus Exp(epsilon) gives Gaussian KL of order
epsilon^(-2) versus original Jeffreys divergence of order epsilon^(-1), so
global linear KL domination is false. The Fisher constant is at least

Exact transfer refutations
--------------------------
1. Smooth one-dimensional Gaussian mixture
  mu_sigma=[N(-a,sigma^2)+N(a,sigma^2)]/2, a^2+sigma^2=1.
It is isotropic and uniformly subgaussian and satisfies EVERY tilt Taylor
operator bound |T_d f|<=4^d||f||_2, uniformly in sigma. Nevertheless its
Poincare constant is at least c sigma^3/a exp(a^2/(2sigma^2)). Thus the
Song-Zhang high-order condition does not force a gap without convexity.
The failure is at the Bochner step. This does not refute stated classical KLS.

2. Quantum Gibbs at the tracial state, complete Pauli bank.
For d-level Hilbert dimension, orthonormal traceless Hermitian scores and
every normalized score B, the third-cumulant contraction HS squared is
d^2/2-2. For d=2^k each individual Pauli score is an operator contraction;
state conditioning and Fisher covariance are perfect. A diagonal-only bank
already gives d-2. Thus no dimension-uniform extension of the BKL cumulant
premise holds for arbitrary quantum Gibbs models.

The obstruction directly affects Gaussian moment surrogates: for
rho_t=(I+tB)/d, quantum chi-square=t^2 and trace distance=|t|/2, but the
Gaussian formed from Pauli means and SYMMETRIZED covariances has
KL(G_t||G_0)=-(d^2/8)log(1-t^2). Its infinitesimal Fisher amplification is
d^2/4. This is a precisely named noncommuting moment-proxy interface, not
a claim that all notions of quantum log-concavity or all quantum covariance
definitions have been refuted.
