INDEPENDENT RECHECK OF THE MAPPING-TORUS INTERFACE

Let A in SL(2,Z) have eigenvalues lambda>1 and lambda^-1 and set
H=log(lambda). In the mapping torus
  M_A=(T^2 x R)/((x,t)~(Ax,t-1)),
take a smooth G:R->P with P={G=G^T>0,det G=1} and
  G_(t+1)=A^T G_t A.
The metric g=dt^2+dx^T G_t dx descends. Its volume density is the product
Haar density dx_1 dx_2 dt, X=partial_t is unit and divergence-free, and
the time-one map is measurably A x id_[0,1). Therefore normalized-volume
entropy is h_Haar(A)=log(lambda). This is the toral automorphism entropy,
not topological entropy of an arbitrary suspension.

Because L_X g=dot G_t on the fiber and vanishes on the X and mixed blocks,
S|_fiber=(1/2)G_t^-1 dot G_t has eigenvalues +/-s(t), while q=b=0.
Thus |S|^2=2s^2 and
  Delta=W/(4 nu V)-H^2=integral_0^1 s(t)^2 dt-H^2.

Define on P the invariant metric
  ||dot G||_G^2=(1/2)tr[(G^-1 dot G)^2].
For z=x+iy in the upper half-plane, the explicit coordinates
  G(z)=y^-1 [[1,x],[x,x^2+y^2]]
turn this into (dx^2+dy^2)/y^2, so P is the CAT(0) hyperbolic plane.
The path speed is 2s. Congruence T_A(G)=A^T G A is an isometry. If
sigma_G(A) is the operator norm of A in the G inner product, then the
relative SPD matrix has eigenvalues sigma_G(A)^2 and sigma_G(A)^-2.
Consequently
  d_P(G,T_A G)=2 log sigma_G(A)>=2 log rho(A)=2H.
The translation length is ell=2H and its axis consists exactly of the
fiber metrics making the two real eigenlines of A orthogonal.

Let L=2 integral s=ell+epsilon be the curve length and let
d0=d_P(G0,G1)=ell+delta0, 0<=delta0<=epsilon. For any t, the two
subpath lengths through G_t sum to L. If a,b are the distances from G_t
to G0,G1, their triangle excess over base d0 is at most epsilon-delta0.
The Euclidean comparison triangle height h satisfies
  h^2 <= d0(epsilon-delta0)/2+(epsilon-delta0)^2/4
       = ell epsilon/2+epsilon^2/4-ell delta0/2-delta0^2/4
       <= H epsilon+epsilon^2/4.
CAT(0) comparison puts G_t within h of [G0,G1]. Jensen gives
  Delta >= (L/2)^2-H^2=H epsilon+epsilon^2/4,
and hence every G_t lies within sqrt(Delta) of that segment.

For a general starting metric, the hyperbolic translation formula in Fermi
coordinates gives
  sinh(d0/2)=cosh(r0)sinh(H),
  r0=dist_P(G0,Axis(T_A))
     <= arcosh(sinh(H+epsilon/2)/sinh(H)).
The segment [G0,G1] stays within r0 of the axis, since distance to a
convex geodesic is convex and both endpoints have distance r0. Therefore
  sup_t dist_P(G_t,Axis(T_A)) <= sqrt(Delta)+r0.

There is a stronger same-gauge version when G0 lies on the axis. Then
d0=ell and the geodesic segment is an axis segment gamma:[0,ell]->P.
Write L1(t)=2 integral_0^t s and let gamma(u_t) be the CAT(0) projection
of G_t onto the segment. The CAT(0) tube gives d(G_t,gamma(u_t))<=sqrt(Delta).
The subpath slack obeys 0<=L1(t)-d(G0,G_t)<=epsilon, while
  integral_0^1(s-H)^2 dt=Delta-H epsilon<=Delta.
Thus |u_t-2Ht|<=3sqrt(Delta)+epsilon and
  sup_t d_P(G_t,gamma(2Ht)) <=4sqrt(Delta)+epsilon,
  epsilon<=2(sqrt(H^2+Delta)-H)<=Delta/H.
This compares to one fixed Sol path in the supplied clock. It is stronger
than mere closeness to the axis as a set, but remains confined to this
mapping-torus metric ansatz and the supplied axis initial condition.

If d_P(G,G')<=rho, the eigenvalues of G^-1/2 G' G^-1/2 lie in
[exp(-rho),exp(rho)], so this is a uniform multiplicative comparison of
transverse lengths. It supplies no control on derivatives of G_t.

EXACT PATH IDENTITY AND DIAGNOSTIC

For A diagonal in its orthonormal eigenbasis, set
  D_t=diag(exp(Ht),exp(-Ht)),
  G_t=D_t Gtilde_t D_t,
  Gtilde_t=[[exp(u),v],[v,(1+v^2)exp(-u)]], det Gtilde=1.
The upper-half-plane coordinate is
  z_t=(v+i)exp(-2Ht-u).
Its hyperbolic speed satisfies the exact identity
  4s(t)^2=(2H+u')^2+((2H+u')v-v')^2.
The distance to the Sol axis is exactly asinh(|v(t)|). For periodic
u=a sin(2pi t), v=b sin(2pi t), with real amplitudes a,b, exact integration
gives
  Delta=pi^2 a^2/2 + H^2 b^2/2 + pi^2 b^2/2 + pi^2 a^2 b^2/8.
In particular Delta>=pi^2 b^2/2 and
  max_t dist_P(G_t,Axis)=asinh(|b|)<=|b|<=sqrt(Delta).
This checks the general tube estimate on an exact nonconstant periodic path.

diagnostics/exact_path_identity.py uses SymPy exact simplification to check
det G=1, endpoint gluing, the speed identity and the integrated formula;
all four checks pass. The separate diagnostics/mapping_torus_path.py uses
floating Simpson quadrature and sampled distance for three parameter sets;
its outputs are illustrative only. Neither script verifies the general
CAT(0) theorem or computes a certified interval for an arbitrary supplied
path. Both use one bounded process and no solver or bulk download.

RELATION TO OBSERVED PEER COUNTEREXAMPLES / LIMITS

The c08_s01 diagonal fast-oscillation metrics remain on the Sol axis: their
metric path can have very small time displacement while second derivatives
and curvature grow. The c08_s01 shear metric has, in these coordinates,
G=[[exp(2at),h(t)],[h(t),exp(-2at)(1+h(t)^2)]], whose distance to the Sol
axis is asinh(|h(t)|). Its supplied fast-shear sequence has h amplitude
of order k^(-3/2), even though its strain-line infinitesimal rotation and
curvature grow. Thus the path theorem is consistent with, and cannot
upgrade past, those counterexamples: it controls the transverse metric
position, not C1/C2 frame derivatives or curvature.

The exact equality path G_t=A^{2t} in an orthonormal eigenbasis for
A=[[2,1],[1,1]] is the Sol mapping torus, with H=log((3+sqrt(5))/2)
and Delta=0. In this class the sharp entropy-to-power inequality is the
translation-length inequality in P followed by Jensen. This is a clean
verification representation, not a new general entropy theorem. To make
it an effective numerical certificate for arbitrary input paths, one still
needs an admitted exact path encoding, proof of positive definiteness,
interval bounds for H and path energy, and a certified quadrature or exact
integral. No bit-complexity bound for those tasks is established. The metric,
matrix A, entropy source, force law, and clock are supplied; force hardware,
metric acquisition and any computation device remain uncharged.
