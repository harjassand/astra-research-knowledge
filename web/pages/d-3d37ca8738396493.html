Exact strongest theorem
-----------------------
Let F be an explicitly encoded n by n matrix over Q(i), with total binary
encoding length L. Fix 0<=k<=floor(n/2). Let

 c = sum_{|I|=|J|=k, I intersect J=empty} |det F[I,J]|^2,
 C_k = binom(2k,k).

(A) There is a capped randomized relative approximation to c in
 C_k poly(n,L,eta^-1,log(1/delta)) bit operations. It returns exact zero on
 every zero instance. On a positive instance it returns a relative-eta
 estimate with probability >=1-delta; an output zero is not a deterministic
 certificate of zero. More explicitly the sample count is
 O(C_k eta^-2 log(1/delta)).

(B) The same asymptotic bound applies to EVERY physical occupation prefix:
 prescribed disjoint sets U,D,H forced up, down, and empty, respectively.
 Prefix masses are the original c sum restricted by U subset I, D subset J,
 and (I union J) intersect H=empty. No prefix probability lower bound is
 needed. There is an explicit polynomial-bit program, not an opaque oracle.

(C) Under the promise c>0 there is a capped classical TV-epsilon Born sampler
 for (I,J) in C_k poly(n,L,epsilon^-1) bit work, including logarithmic
 confidence amplification. Its output distribution is within epsilon of
 |det F[I,J]|^2/c. A low-probability estimator-failure fallback may have zero
 target weight; every execution still outputs a legal hard k-pair pattern.

(D) A randomized classical compiler can construct a uniform quantum circuit
 of C_k poly(n,L,epsilon^-1,log(1/delta)) size that, with probability >=1-delta
 over its classical compiler seed, prepares the normalized hard k-pair
 determinant-amplitude state to trace distance <=epsilon. This is a
 mathematical compiler consequence with standard finite-bit reversible
 arithmetic/rotation synthesis, not implemented quantum gate synthesis.

In all statements, the inverse sector norm, projection probability,
determinant magnitude, and smallest conditional probability do not enter the
running time. For a fixed public constant A, k<=A log_2(n+1) makes the bounds
polynomial. The algorithms are exponential in k in general.

1. Reconstruction of the random-sign identity
---------------------------------------------
Write u_i=e_i and v_i=row_i(F)^T, and put V=(u_1,v_1,...,u_n,v_n).
For signs s_i in {+1,-1}, set
 A_s=sum_i s_i(u_i v_i^T-v_i u_i^T)=diag(s)F-F^Tdiag(s).
The transpose here is not the adjoint: A_s is complex skew-symmetric.

Let omega_s=sum_{a<b}(A_s)_{ab} e_a wedge e_b. Then
 omega_s=sum_i s_i u_i wedge v_i.
Even degree-two forms commute, and (u_i wedge v_i)^2=0. Therefore

 omega_s^k/k! = sum_{|S|=k} (product_{i in S}s_i)
                        wedge_{i in S}(u_i wedge v_i).

Taking the coordinate of e_R for a 2k-row set R gives
 p_R(s)=Pf(A_s[R])=sum_{|S|=k} d_{R,S} chi_S(s),
 d_{R,S}=det V[R,P(S)]. The ordered interleaved columns P(S) fix its sign.
The identity columns force d_{R,S}=0 unless S subset R. For S subset R,
expansion along those columns gives
 |d_{R,S}|^2=|det F[S,R minus S]|^2.
Thus orthogonality of Boolean characters gives
 E|p_R|^2=sum_S|d_{R,S}|^2, and summing over R yields exactly c.
There is one-to-one correspondence (R,S)=(I union J,I). No sum over hidden
matchings, support oracle, or supplied norm is being substituted here.

2. Computing one sample without enumerating minors
--------------------------------------------------
Define X_k(A)=sum_{|R|=2k}|Pf(A[R])|^2. The coordinate vector of
omega^k/k! transforms under a unitary exterior power when A undergoes
unitary congruence, so X_k is invariant. For a complex skew matrix, the
unitary congruence normal form is a sum of blocks
 [[0,sigma_j],[-sigma_j,0]], sigma_j>=0, and zeros. In that form,

 sum_k X_k(A)t^k=product_j(1+t sigma_j^2),
 det(I+t A* A)=product_j(1+t sigma_j^2)^2.

Hence X_k is the coefficient q_k of the unique formal square root with
q_0=1. This covers complex matrices, odd dimension, repeated singular values,
rank deficiency, and zero. We use no numerical singular-value decomposition.
The normal form is the standard imported Youla theorem, specifically
Corollary 2 in the primary 1961 paper inspected during this audit:
https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1961-059-8 .

For B=A* A, compute T_j=Tr(B^j), j=1,...,k. Newton identities give
 d_0=1,
 m d_m=sum_{j=1}^m (-1)^(j-1) d_{m-j} T_j,
 q_0=1,
 2q_m=d_m-sum_{j=1}^{m-1}q_j q_{m-j}.
These need O(k n^3+k^2) exact arithmetic operations using naive matrix
multiplication. Return q_k. If 2k exceeds the matrix dimension, return zero.
For Gaussian-integer A the output and the displayed exact divisions are
integers. Production coefficient_by_traces() implements precisely this path;
principal Pfaffian/minor enumeration appears only in diagnostic comparators.
The Luna check script, by contrast, enumerates principal minors to obtain
its determinant coefficients on tiny fixtures. That is a valid diagnostic,
but its runtime is not the claimed per-sample polynomial algorithm.

3. Sharper pointwise and second-moment bound
-------------------------------------------
For each R there are at most C_k=binom(2k,k) nonzero Fourier terms, since
S subset R and |S|=k. For EVERY sign word, complex Cauchy--Schwarz gives
 |p_R(s)|^2 <= C_k sum_S |d_{R,S}|^2.
Consequently

 0<=X_k(A_s)<=C_k c,       E X_k=c,       E X_k^2<=C_k c^2.       (3)

This elementary bound is stronger than 9^k and eliminates the
hypercontractivity dependency for the special BCS identity-column interface.
It does not claim that the C_k constant is optimal for Pfaffian polynomials.

The original Luna complex-hypercontractivity argument is valid, with a
conservative factor four. Indeed Minkowski gives
 || |p|^2 ||_2 <= ||(Re p)^2||_2+||(Im p)^2||_2
 <=3^k(E(Re p)^2+E(Im p)^2)=3^k E|p|^2,
so even that route gives E X^2<=9^k c^2. The needed real degree-d (2,4)
inequality is present in the primary author lecture, Corollary 1.3:
https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/boolean-analysis/lecture16.pdf .
No complex-valued hypercontractivity theorem was silently assumed.

For eta in (0,1/2], average M=ceil(4C_k/eta^2) independent samples.
Chebyshev bounds relative failure by 1/4. Use an odd number of independent
batches r>=8 ln(1/delta); Hoeffding bounds a majority of failed batches by
exp(-r/8). The median has relative error <=eta with probability >=1-delta.
The code uses the larger exact integer choice r=16 ceil(log2(1/delta))+1.
If c=0, (3) forces every sample to be zero. If c>0, individual samples and
even the median can be zero on the confidence-failure event. This distinction
is material to support/zero interfaces.

4. Exact nonnegative physical-prefix sample
------------------------------------------
Let a=|U|, b=|D|, and N=[n] minus (U union D union H). Infeasible cardinal
conditions a>k, b>k, or n-|H|<2k give exact zero. Otherwise for free signs
s_i, i in N, define real scalar activities
 tau_i(z)=z for i in U; s_i for i in N; 0 for i in D union H.
Let A(z)=diag(tau(z))F-F^Tdiag(tau(z)).

For R subset [n] minus H of size 2k, p_R(z)=Pf(A(z)[R]) has degree at most
a in z. The wedge expansion already proved that each chosen pair appears
at most once. Its coefficient of z^a is

 h_R(s)=sum_{S subset R, |S|=k, U subset S,
             S intersect(D union H)=empty} d_{R,S} chi_{S minus U}(s).

Taking the highest coefficient in |p_R(z)|^2 for REAL z selects exactly
|h_R(s)|^2. This is why it is z^(2a), not an arbitrary coefficient where
cross terms could have signs. Define

 Y(s)=sum_{R subset [n] minus H, |R|=2k, D subset R} |h_R(s)|^2.  (4)

It is nonnegative for every sign word. Fourier orthogonality gives E Y equal
to the true prefix mass c_{U,D,H}. Here S is the up set and R minus S is the
down set, so (4) enforces both role restrictions in the original physical
law, not just a parity-line marginal.

Acquire (4) with only polynomially computable X_k calls. For each T subset D,
retain coordinates W_T=[n] minus(H union T), and compute

 Q_T(z)=X_k(A(z)[W_T]),
 top_T=[z^(2a)]Q_T(z)
      =1/(2a)! sum_{j=0}^{2a}(-1)^(2a-j)binom(2a,j)Q_T(j).

The forward-difference formula is exact because degree Q_T<=2a. Then
 Y(s)=sum_{T subset D}(-1)^|T| top_T.
Indeed a row set R appears with multiplicity
sum_{T subset D minus R}(-1)^|T|, which is one exactly when D subset R and
zero otherwise. Intermediate operations have signs, but the final value is
the explicit nonnegative norm (4). No relative-precision subtraction is used.
Each sample costs 2^b(2a+1) calls to the polynomial coefficient program.

For a fixed eligible R, free up choices number at most
 C_ab=binom(2k-a-b,k-a)=binom(2k-a-b,k-b).
The same pointwise argument proves
 0<=Y<=C_ab c_{U,D,H},  E Y=c_{U,D,H},
 E Y^2<=C_ab c_{U,D,H}^2.
Thus M=ceil(4C_ab/eta^2) samples per batch suffice.

There is no additional exponential factor from forcing down sites:
 C_ab<=binom(2k-b,k), and
 f_b=2^b binom(2k-b,k) satisfies
 f_(b+1)/f_b=2(k-b)/(2k-b)<=1, 0<=b<k.
Hence 2^b C_ab<=f_0=C_k. Including the ceiling in M changes only a constant,
as eta<=1/2 and C_ab>=1. The 2a+1 factor is polynomial. EVERY physical
prefix count therefore has C_k poly(n,L,eta^-1,log(1/delta)) bit work.
When C_ab=1, Y equals its mean on every sign word: a nonnegative variable
bounded by its mean cannot be smaller on any point of the finite uniform
sign space. One exact evaluation then gives the exact prefix mass. The
implementation exploits this shortcut.

5. Uniform bit bounds and boundaries
------------------------------------
Clear all real and imaginary denominators by one positive integer D with
log2 D<=O(L), and write G=DF. Its Gaussian-integer entries have O(L) bits.
All samples for a k-sector have common denominator D^(2k). Work on G and
divide only at the final output. Thus summing M samples adds log M bits,
instead of building unrelated denominators. All prefix programs have the
same scaling c_prefix(F)=D^(-2k)c_prefix(G).

For evaluated z<=2a<=2k, skew entries have O(L+log(k+1)) bits. Entries of
B^j and its traces have O(j(L+log n+log(k+1))) bits. Newton/square-root
recurrences therefore have polynomial bit height through j<=k<=n/2.
Alternatively Hadamard bounds each coefficient by a polynomial-bit integer.
Forward differences multiply by binomial coefficients and add 2a+1 values;
factorials cost O(a log(a+1)) bits. Inclusion-exclusion sums 2^b terms and
adds at most b bits. These are polynomial heights even when the final
positive prefix is exponentially smaller than its intermediate terms.
Every exact arithmetic operation has polynomial bit cost. No approximate
eigenvalues, numerical conditioning, inverse norm, or finite-field false
zero allocation is present. This applies to explicit exact rational data;
it says nothing about approximating an unknown F from rounded measurements.

At k=0, c=1 and the unique occupation is vacuum. If forced U or D is
nonempty, the k=0 prefix is zero. Illegal k or overlapping role constraints
are rejected by the implementation. Odd n, nonzero diagonal entries,
singular F, complex phases, exact cancellations, and c=0 are admitted.
Diagonal entries cancel in A_s and cannot appear in disjoint physical minors.

6. Capped classical Born sampling
---------------------------------
The conservative linear-TV budget below is a complete earlier proof. After
an authorised c03_s03 message, HELLINGER_SAMPLER_ADDENDUM.txt reconstructs its
stronger classical affinity argument and the implementation uses that later
eta=epsilon/(16ceil(sqrt(n))) budget. This saves one factor n in count work;
it does not change the headline central-binomial theorem or quantum hybrid.

Assume c>0. Scan sites in order; at each current physical prefix compute the
three child masses by independent capped relative-prefix calls. Use
 eta=epsilon/(8n), delta_call=epsilon/(12n).
If all three estimates are zero, return a fixed legal hard k-pair occupation.
That event cannot occur at a positive true prefix when all estimates satisfy
their relative bounds. It is included in the estimator-failure probability;
the fallback need not be a positive-amplitude witness.

On a good call at a positive prefix, let exact children have weights w_j,
sum W, and estimates hat_w_j within (1+-eta)w_j. True zero children are
exact zero on every execution. The normalized law obeys
 |hat_p_j/p_j-1|<=2eta/(1-eta), so
 TV(hat_p,p)<=eta/(1-eta)<=2eta.
Choose the branch using one uniform bit word with denominator 2^s>=8n/epsilon
and floor the two cumulative thresholds. Its TV rounding error is at most
2/2^s<=epsilon/(4n). This uses exactly s bits and no rejection loop.

There are at most 3n adaptive estimator calls. Conditional on each past
history, its fresh randomness satisfies the same delta_call bound. A union
bound and sequential coupling therefore give total TV error at most
 2n eta+3n delta_call+n epsilon/(4n) <=3epsilon/4 < epsilon.
If one estimate is positive, its true prefix is positive (a true zero
produces only zero samples), so unsupported branches occur only through the
charged fallback. The maximum runtime is n times three capped prefix counts,
regardless of c or prefix probabilities. Invalid dimensional prefixes are
exact zero shortcuts. At c=0 the normalized law is undefined: this sampler
has the positive-norm promise, unlike the relative counter which admits zero.

7. Coherent preparation consequence: complete weighted hybrid
-------------------------------------------------------------
Let Omega_k be legal hard patterns, a(x)=det F[I(x),J(x)] with the desired
computable fermionic ordering sign, and psi=c^(-1/2)sum_x a(x)|x>.
First prepare nonnegative amplitudes sqrt(p(x)). This section independently
states the general N63 shared-depth method; it does not claim that method
originated with this audit or rely on the unverified Chen--Liu FPRAS.

For every depth d allocate one padded random tape T_d, reused among all
prefixes at that depth. Give each of the three child counters disjoint tape
segments and individual failure at most beta/3. A fixed positive prefix then
has bad-count probability <=beta. Dependence among DIFFERENT prefixes on the
same tape is allowed; there is no exponential union bound. Pad each program
to the common C_k poly bound, including all arithmetic and signs, using the
prefix-dependent sample count and inclusion-exclusion cost already bounded
above. Tape length and padded work are both C_k poly. Reversible computation
of three rational weights and their ratios is uniform and finite; histories
can be uncomputed after the conditional next-site rotation because the
earlier prefix remains unchanged.

On an all-zero estimated vector, choose a child with a feasible remaining
CARDINALITY, and set its conditional probability to one. Feasibility is
purely a,b<=k and enough remaining sites; it can be checked exactly in
polynomial time. This makes a normalized three-entry vector and keeps every
prepared basis string in Omega_k. It need not have positive determinant.
At target-zero prefixes its behavior is irrelevant to the ideal weights.

At a positive prefix where all child counts are good, the estimated ratios
hat_p_j/p_j lie between (1-eta)/(1+eta) and its reciprocal. For eta<=1/2,
 |sqrt(hat_p_j/p_j)-1|<=4eta.
Thus the local prepared vector is within 4eta in Euclidean norm of the
ideal one. On a bad prefix its norm difference is at most two.

Let B_d(T_d)=sum_{prefix x at depth d}p(x)1_(x is bad). Then E B_d<=beta.
For the hybrid that changes one conditional unitary at a time, its input
before that gate is the IDEAL prefix superposition, giving gate-error norm
at most 4eta+2sqrt(B_d). The subsequent approximate unitaries preserve norm.
Triangle inequality and Cauchy--Schwarz give

 ||phi_T-sum_x sqrt(p(x))|x>||_2
 <=4n eta+2sum_d sqrt(B_d)
 <=4n eta+2sqrt(n sum_d B_d).

Since E sum_d B_d<=n beta, Markov gives sum_d B_d<=n beta/delta with
probability >=1-delta. For such compiler seeds the norm error is at most
 4n eta+2n sqrt(beta/delta).
Set eta=epsilon/(16n), beta=delta epsilon^2/(64n^2): this is <=epsilon/2.
The chosen compiler seed need not have an efficiently recognized success
flag; the guarantee is over its random generation.

Allow another epsilon/2 norm error for finite-bit rotations and the final
phase. Conditional ternary rotations use exact rational probabilities in
[0,1], with a defined zero-denominator case, and are synthesized to absolute
error O(epsilon/n) per depth. Determinants and fermionic signs are computed
exactly from the occupied sets in polynomial bit time; on zero or invalid
strings use phase one. To compute the nonzero determinant phase, scale its
real and imaginary parts by their maximum absolute value before normalizing:
one component has magnitude one and the norm is between one and sqrt(2).
This avoids an inverse-small-determinant analytic cost. Finite precision
phase/rotation computation is treated explicitly in the later
QUANTUM_FINITE_BIT_APPENDIX.txt. It gives a fixed finite gate set and
elementary synthesis with polynomial epsilon^-1 cost, and unrolls each
prefix-cardinality class to preserve the C_k factor. This avoids using a
real-arithmetic Solovay--Kitaev statement as a finite-bit theorem. These
are mathematical constructions, not implemented gate synthesis.

The diagonal phase operation gives a state within epsilon Euclidean norm of
psi on the good seed event. Trace distance for pure states is at most that
norm. The resulting circuit description and its evaluation use
C_k poly(n,L,epsilon^-1,log(1/delta)) work and size. No exponentially sized
prefix table is compiled. All random signs become fixed classical bits in
the circuit description. Quantum gate synthesis, physical energy, and
hardware performance have not been executed or measured.

8. Necessary exponential boundary of THIS estimator
---------------------------------------------------
Take n=2k and F a direct sum of k two-site swap blocks [[0,1],[1,0]].
Every legal nonzero full-sector configuration orients each block, so c=2^k.
The skew matrix A_s has block entry s_(2j)-s_(2j+1). Its only full-size
principal Pfaffian yields
 X=product_j(s_(2j)-s_(2j+1))^2,
which is 4^k with probability 2^-k and zero otherwise. Therefore
 E X^2/c^2=2^k.
With M independent raw samples, all are zero with probability
(1-2^-k)^M. To see even one positive sample with fixed high probability
requires Omega(2^k) samples. A median/mean based solely on these raw sign
samples cannot make this a general polynomial-time method at k=Theta(n).
The block norm itself is exactly easy; this is variance, not hardness.
