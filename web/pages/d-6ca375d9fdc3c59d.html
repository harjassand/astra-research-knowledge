FIXED-SANDWICH BROADCASTING: BOUNDED STEERING DOES NOT RESCUE TWO STANDARD
HIGHLY EXTENDIBLE / DATA-HIDING EXTENSION LADDERS
7 October 2026. Worker /root/sandwich_broadcast_counter.

STATUS / STRONGEST RESULT

The unrestricted fixed-sandwich implication remains OPEN here. No experiment
with sigma/2 <= rho <= 3sigma/2, b_2 -> 0 and uniform EB error bounded away
from zero was obtained. In particular, no bottom-sector, entangled-Choi or
pair-gap lower bound is presented as a global EB reconstruction lower bound.

Two internally derived scoped obstructions are proved below. Both cover
ARBITRARY bounded steering contractions, including effects on the entire
large reference system and arbitrary finite restrictions of those effects.
Both have actual full-domain two-output CPTP splitters with marginal error
exactly 1/L. Both also have concrete replacement EB channels with uniform
error O(1/L). Thus arbitrary global measurements of high input sectors
cannot rescue the required EB lower bound for these constructions.

1. Random flagged disjoint-EPR extension, with arbitrary product reference:

   T(rho_(A,r),sigma_r) <= (a/2) sqrt(r/K),
   e_EB(dyadic ladder) <= a(2+sqrt(2))/(2L).

2. Normalized antisymmetric extension, d >= K+1:

   T(rho_(A,r),omega_r)
      <= (a/2) sqrt[r/((d-1)(d-r))],
   e_EB(dyadic ladder)
      <= (a/(2L))[1+(sqrt(2)+1)/sqrt(K)].

Here K=2^(L-1), r ranges over 1,2,...,K dyadically, and 0<a<=1/2.
The result is an obstruction to a plausible negative route, not a solution
of the broad conjecture or a priority-cleared new theorem.

INTERFACE

T(X,Y)=||X-Y||_1/2 for density matrices. b_2 is the infimum over one CPTP
B:B(H)->B(H tensor H) of the uniform worst receiver marginal error. e_EB
is the analogous infimum over one full-domain EB endomorphism. Correlated
outputs, global POVMs, changed output flags and arbitrary known ancillas
are allowed. Hilbert dimension, number of labels and apparatus costs may
grow without bound. All reference supports are compressed into their exact
finite-dimensional sector Hilbert spaces, so the stated references are
faithful. One may choose ANY finite family of the contractions used below,
including A=0 to include sigma; every displayed uniform bound survives.

The supplied compatible-smoothing reduction in

audited in this report. The present scoped conclusions are direct and do not
depend on that reduction. The bosonic mixed-orbit obstruction and the flagged

For both families, the sandwich directly gives C(E)<=ln(1+a)<=ln(3/2):

PART I. FLAGGED DISJOINT EPR / PRODUCT-PURIFICATION EXTENSION

1. Exact steered states

Take K reference registers R_1,...,R_K, each of dimension q, with arbitrary
faithful product reference tau=tau_1 tensor ... tensor tau_K. Choose a fixed
basis and the product canonical purification |sqrt(tau)> in R tensor Q.
For each r, let

   H_r = direct_sum_(S subset [K], |S|=r) tensor_(i in S) Q_i,
   m_r = binomial(K,r),
   sigma_r = (1/m_r) direct_sum_S tau_S^T.

This is the r-output marginal of the permutation-symmetrized product of
K separately flagged purifications. The unordered subset flags used here
are a sufficient quotient of the usual ordered distinct partner flags.

Let A=A* on all R, ||A||_op<=1, Tr(tau A)=0. Define the conditional
expectation E_S(A)=Tr_(S^c)[(I_S tensor tau_(S^c)) A], and put

   rho_(A,r) = (1/m_r) direct_sum_S
       [ sqrt(tau_S)(I+a E_S(A))sqrt(tau_S) ]^T.          (I.1)

These are normalized states: every subset block has trace 1/m_r, since
Tr(tau_S E_S(A))=Tr(tau A)=0. The successful steering effect is
F_A=(I+a A)/2, with probability EXACTLY 1/2, independent of A, K and r.
E_S is unital CP, so -I<=E_S(A)<=I. Hence

   (1-a)sigma_r <= rho_(A,r) <= (1+a)sigma_r.            (I.2)

This genuinely permits a noncommuting full-reference A. It is not a
restriction to an effect on one known EPR partner or to a classical effect.

2. Dimension-free bounded-effect signal bound

Use the weighted Hilbert norm ||X||_(2,tau)^2=Tr(tau X*X). For each site i,
let Q_i replace its operator by its tau_i expectation times I_i. The Q_i
commute and are orthogonal projections in this product GNS Hilbert space.
Define the orthogonal tensor ANOVA components

   A_T = [product_(i in T)(I-Q_i)]
         [product_(i notin T)Q_i] A.

The empty component is zero, and

   sum_(T nonempty) ||A_T||_(2,tau)^2
      = ||A||_(2,tau)^2 <= 1.

Embedding E_S(A) back into the full register gives sum_(T subset S) A_T.
For S a uniform r-subset,

   E_S ||E_S(A)||_(2,tau_S)^2
      = sum_(T nonempty) Pr(T subset S) ||A_T||_(2,tau)^2
      <= (r/K) ||A||_(2,tau)^2 <= r/K.                 (I.3)

The only combinatorial inequality is
Pr(T subset S)=(r)_(|T|)/(K)_(|T|)<=r/K for every nonempty T.
Weighted Schatten Holder gives

   ||sqrt(tau_S) E_S(A) sqrt(tau_S)||_1
      <= sqrt[Tr(tau_S E_S(A)^2)].

Average this over S, then apply scalar Cauchy-Schwarz and (I.3). The exact
block trace norm in (I.1) proves

   T(rho_(A,r),sigma_r) <= (a/2) sqrt(r/K).              (I.4)

This estimate holds simultaneously for ALL bounded centered A, with no
net size, label count, spectral condition number or qudit dimension factor.
It is the missing operational distinction from entangled-state separation.

3. A full-domain broadcaster and a full-domain EB reconstruction

Set r_j=2^j, j=0,...,L-1, K=r_(L-1), H=direct_sum_j H_(r_j), and

   rho_A=(1/L) direct_sum_j rho_(A,r_j),
   sigma=(1/L) direct_sum_j sigma_(r_j).

To broadcast, dephase the level and subset flag on ALL inputs. On a sector
with r=2s, choose a uniform s-subset T of its flag S, set U=S\T, and reorder
the physical tensor factors into Q_T tensor Q_U. Embed the two factors into
their corresponding H_s sectors. Each reordering V_(S,T) is an isometry;
mixing their channels with weight 1/binomial(r,s) is trace preserving.
On the bottom input sector, discard it and prepare sigma_1 tensor sigma_1.

Explicitly, with P_(j,S) the input block projections,

   B(X)=Tr(P_bottom X) sigma_1 tensor sigma_1
       + sum_(j>=1,S, |T|=r_j/2, T subset S)
           V_(S,T) P_(j,S) X P_(j,S) V_(S,T)*
           /binomial(r_j,r_j/2).                       (I.5)

This is CP, and its Kraus/isometry trace identities sum to I_H. It therefore
acts on the complete input matrix algebra, including arbitrary coherences
and inputs outside the promised experiment.

Conditional expectations are consistent under partial trace. A uniform
r-subset followed by a uniform s-subset gives a uniform s-subset, by
binomial(K,r)binomial(r,s)=binomial(K,s)binomial(K-s,r-s).
Consequently both marginals in every nonbottom sector are exactly the
previous level's rho_(A,s). On the whole ladder, for either receiver,

   B_i(rho_A)-rho_A = (1/L) sigma_1_bottom
                     -(1/L) rho_(A,K)_top,
   T(B_i(rho_A),rho_A)=1/L.                            (I.6)

The displayed channel's error is exact; optimal b_2 is not asserted to equal it.

Now use the EB replacement Lambda(X)=Tr(X)sigma. By (I.4),

   sup_A T(Lambda rho_A,rho_A)
      <= (a/(2L)) sum_j sqrt(2^j/K)
      <= a/[2L(1-1/sqrt(2))]
       = a(2+sqrt(2))/(2L).                            (I.7)

Thus these ladders pass the fixed sandwich and broadcasting tests, but
fail the required EB-error lower test. A concrete EB map already achieves
vanishing UNIFORM error. Allowing a global high-sector POVM only enlarges
the EB optimization and cannot invalidate this upper bound.

4. Why the known one-way LOCC lower example does not contradict (I.4)

Brandao-Christandl-Yard's example has a single B with a classical flag for
which reference qubit it shares an EPR pair with. Bob reads that flag, then
Alice keeps the selected qubit. This is the reverse one-way communication
direction in their Eq.(21), and demonstrates constant entangled-state
separation. A bounded steering effect chosen before seeing Bob's random
flag cannot choose that partner. Equation (I.3) quantifies the aggregate
penalty even for an arbitrary effect acting jointly on all reference qubits.
The original example remains valid in its own norm/interface.

PART II. ANTISYMMETRIC / WERNER EXTENSION

5. States and exact variance

Let d>=K+1, H_r=wedge^r(C^d), D_r=binomial(d,r), omega_r=I/D_r. Let
Omega_(r+1) be the normalized projector onto wedge^(r+1)(C^d), embedded
in R tensor H_r. It is the (r+1)-body marginal of Omega_(K+1).
For a traceless Hermitian contraction A on R=C^d, write

   J_A^(r)=sum_(t=1)^r A^(t), restricted to H_r.

Steering with (I+a A)/2 has probability 1/2, and gives exactly

   rho_(A,r) = [I-a J_A^(r)/(d-r)]/D_r.                 (II.1)

Derivation: diagonalize A. In the determinant basis indexed by an r-subset
S of its eigenvectors, the antisymmetric conditional reference particle is
uniform on S^c. Since sum_i a_i=0, its A expectation is
-sum_(i in S)a_i/(d-r), giving (II.1). Unitary covariance supplies arbitrary
noncommuting A. Since the complement also has d-r entries of magnitude <=1,
||J_A^(r)/(d-r)||_op<=1. Therefore the same fixed sandwich (I.2) holds.

Uniform r-subset sampling without replacement has the exact variance

   (1/D_r)Tr[(J_A^(r)/(d-r))^2]
      = [r/((d-1)(d-r))] Tr(A^2)/d
      <= r/((d-1)(d-r)).                               (II.2)

Indeed the diagonal sum has variance r(d-r)/(d-1) times the population
second moment; division by (d-r)^2 gives the formula. Cauchy-Schwarz implies

   T(rho_(A,r),omega_r)
      <= (a/2) sqrt[r/((d-1)(d-r))].                    (II.3)

6. Broadcaster and replacement EB channel

The canonical grouping embeds wedge^(2s)(C^d) isometrically in
wedge^s(C^d) tensor wedge^s(C^d): a totally antisymmetric tensor is separately
antisymmetric on each half. Splitting either marginal of rho_(A,2s) gives
rho_(A,s), by consistency of the underlying antisymmetric extensions.
Dephase ladder flags, apply these isometries above the bottom, and replace
the bottom by omega_1 tensor omega_1. Exactly the same full-domain proof and
boundary identity as (I.5)-(I.6) gives marginal error 1/L.

Replace all input by sigma=(1/L) direct_sum_j omega_(2^j) instead. The top
term of the square-root bound (II.3) is <=1 because d>=K+1. For r<=K/2,

   sqrt[r/((d-1)(d-r))] <= sqrt(2r)/K.

Summing the geometric sequence for j<=L-2 gives

   sum_j sqrt[r_j/((d-1)(d-r_j))]
       <= 1+(sqrt(2)+1)/sqrt(K).

Hence the claimed uniform EB upper bound follows. This covers the full
traceless-contraction steering family, rather than only rank-one effects or
the one-particle Werner-Holevo channel. Even the maximally entangled hole
description at r=d-1 supplies only a top-level contribution of weight 1/L.
