# Initial independent checkpoint — c10_l03

## Strongest claim

I prove a source-swap boundary lemma for finite weakly reversible stochastic mass-action networks, and combine it with the exact falling-factorial potential recorded in N76. It gives a component-preserving, bounded-length route that can change the active source complex without increasing that potential. Separately, a direct face criterion gives a one-jump stochastic recovery whenever a deterministic coordinate face is non-invariant and the currently present species exceed the network's source thresholds. The lemma is an exact bridge from source-complex geometry to paths in the stochastic communicating class. It does not prove a global Foster drift, existence of a finite stationary distribution, or positive recurrence.

## Definitions and full derivation

Let a finite reaction network have complex set \(C\subset\mathbb N_0^d\), directed reaction edges \(y\to y'\), and assume the complex graph is weakly reversible (each linkage class is strongly connected). Stochastic propensities are \(\lambda_{y\to y'}(x)=\kappa_{y\to y'}x_{\underline y}\), with positive rates. For \(x\in\mathbb N_0^d\), write \(y\le x\) when the reaction source is enabled and

\[
M(x)=\max_{y\in C,\ y\le x}x_{\underline y},\qquad
F(x)=\min_{y\in C,\ y\le x}\log((x-y)!).
\]

Here factorials and falling factorials are coordinatewise products. Since \(x_{\underline y}(x-y)!=x!\), \(F(x)=\log(x!)-\log M(x)\). Every complex is a source in a weakly reversible graph. Choose an available maximizer \(y\) of \(x_{\underline y}\), and any \(z\) in its linkage class. Strong connectivity supplies a directed complex path \(y=c_0\to c_1\to\cdots\to c_\ell=z\), with \(\ell\le |C|-1\) after taking a shortest path. Starting from \(x\), the corresponding stochastic states are exactly

\[
x_k=x-y+c_k\quad (0\le k\le\ell).
\]

Indeed, \(x-y\ge0\); at step \(k\), the source is \(c_k\) and \(x_k-c_k=x-y\ge0\), so it is enabled, and the reaction update gives \(x_{k+1}=x-y+c_{k+1}\). The reverse complex path proves the endpoint communicates back with \(x\). At the endpoint, \(z\) is an available source and \((x-y+z)-z=x-y\). Therefore

\[
F(x-y+z)\le \log((x-y)!)=F(x).
\]

This is an endpoint statement; intermediate path states need not have nonincreasing \(F\). It is a discrete source-geometry move with a fixed spectator vector, and it remains valid at zero-count boundaries because enablement is checked exactly rather than replaced by a monomial approximation.

There is also a direct face version. For \(I\subseteq[d]\), let \(F_I=\{u\in\mathbb R_+^d:\operatorname{supp}(u)\subseteq I\}\). This face is forward invariant for positive-rate deterministic mass-action ODEs iff every reaction with \(\operatorname{supp}(y)\subseteq I\) has \(\operatorname{supp}(y')\subseteq I\). If such a reaction creates a missing coordinate \(j\), its source monomial is positive at every relative-interior point of \(F_I\); all reactions that could consume \(j\) have a source containing \(j\) and vanish there. Hence \(\dot u_j>0\). Conversely, with no such edge, every missing-coordinate derivative is zero on the face.

Let \(m_i=\max_{y\in C} y_i\). If the face is non-invariant and an integer state \(x\) has support \(I\) with \(x_i\ge m_i+1\) for every \(i\in I\), choose a face-crossing reaction \(y\to y'\). It is enabled because \(y\le x\), and after it fires, each old coordinate remains positive because \(x_i-y_i\ge1\); each newly produced coordinate is positive. Thus the next state has strictly larger support. Weak reversibility puts that jump inside the same communicating class by the source-swap return path. This is a one-step claim only: after a jump, the new species may have count one and later source thresholds may still block further recovery.

For a prescribed weakly reversible source path of length \(\ell\), all complexes of a bimolecular weakly reversible network have molecularity at most two. If \(N=\|x\|_1\), \(M_* = \max_{c\in C}\|c\|_1\le2\), and \(0<\kappa_{\min}\le\kappa_e\le\kappa_{\max}\), every path-step rate is at least \(\kappa_{\min}\) and total rate at its state is at most \(|R|\kappa_{\max}(1+N+M_*)^2\). Thus the exact prescribed next-jump word has probability at least

\[
\left[\frac{\kappa_{\min}}{|R|\kappa_{\max}(1+N+M_*)^2}\right]^\ell,
\]

and its conditional mean elapsed holding time is at most \(\ell/\kappa_{\min}\). This cost bound is deliberately crude, but explicit and polynomial in population for fixed network and path length. Rational rates make the displayed bound exactly computable from their binary encoding.

## Exact counterexample to unconditional boundary recovery

For the weakly reversible bimolecular network \(A+B\rightleftharpoons2A\), the deterministic face \(B=0\) is non-invariant: the reaction \(2A\to A+B\) creates \(B\) at every positive point of that face. Yet the stochastic state \((A,B)=(1,0)\) enables neither edge and is an absorbing singleton class. At \((2,0)\), the same crossing reaction is enabled and reaches \((1,1)\) in one jump, with a return path through the reverse edge. This shows exactly why a face-geometric criterion needs a count threshold and why no universal recovery claim applies to every component/state. The companion network \(A\rightleftharpoons2A\) also has the absorbing state 0 beside a positive-state class.

## Executable evidence and scope

`boundary_recovery.py` implements the fixed-spectator path update, face-edge criterion, rational path-probability lower bound, and small exact checks. It ran successfully under Python 3 on two networks. The A+B/2A fixture checked the absorbing count-one state and the count-two recovery path; exhaustive states with coordinates 0 through 7 produced five thick-face checks. The A/2A fixture checked the separate zero class. These finite checks confirm the implementation on the fixtures; the proof above is the general argument. The script is not a recurrence solver, a general reachability algorithm, or a Foster-certificate synthesizer.

## Primary sources and prior-art boundary

- Chuang Xu, *On the Regulary [sic] of Reaction Systems*, arXiv:2409.05340v2 (August 2026), https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2. Theorem 4.6 proves non-explosivity for every second-order endotactic mass-action system. Corollary 4.17 states that a second-order 1-endotactic class is positive recurrent iff it has a stationary probability distribution; Theorem 4.13 gives the same reduction for bimolecular weakly reversible systems. Thus the live recurrence gate is finite stationary-mass existence per class, not non-explosion. This paper does not prove the boundary lemma above.
- Chuang Xu, *Classification and threshold dynamics of stochastic reaction networks*, arXiv:2012.07954v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2012.07954v2. It identifies communicating-class/essentiality issues and proves one-dimensional recurrence results under its stated hypotheses; it is not a multidimensional boundary-recovery theorem.
- Anderson, Cappelletti, Kim, and Nguyen, *Tier structure of strongly endotactic reaction networks*, arXiv:1808.05328v2, https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05328v2. This is relevant to the deterministic/stochastic source-tier distinction, but the source-swap derivation above is independent.
- Astra N76 (`cards/N76-factorial-reaction-boundary.txt` at pinned revision `37a01798e92a0763e64a4649ae00024079d2a266`) records the same falling-factorial potential and an event-chain upward bound, but only a supplied summary is available; its linked proof/code packet is absent. The algebraic source-swap endpoint inequality above is reproduced in this checkpoint rather than accepted on the summary's authority.
- Astra N77 (`cards/N77-stationary-tightness-counterexample.txt`, same pinned revision) reports a counterexample to common classwise stationary tightness, also summary-only. It cautions against turning deterministic common attractors into uniform stationary bounds. I did not use its unprovided details as a premise.

## Status, costs, and unresolved gates

Status: `VERIFIED_INTERNAL` for the source-swap and thick-face one-step lemmas; `UNRESOLVED` for the assignment's full global recovery/Foster objective. The source-swap path costs at most \(|C|-1\) jumps; a chosen endpoint's probability can be as small as an explicit inverse polynomial of degree at most \(2(|C|-1)\) in \(N\), with rate-bit dependence through \(\kappa_{\min}/\kappa_{\max}\). A deterministic face-crossing edge is found by scanning the finite reaction list. No cost for a general path-tree search or semialgebraic synthesis is claimed.

The decisive missing step is repeated recovery from *thin* newly-created species counts and the resulting negative drift in physical time or finite stationary mass on each actual closed communicating class. The source-swap inequality gives a nonincreasing potential only at selected endpoints, not negative expected drift; the path probability bound can be polynomially small. Neither deterministic permanence (N33), non-explosivity, a closed communicating class, nor existence of a possibly infinite stationary measure closes this gap.
