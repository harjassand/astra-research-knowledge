1. In the separation paragraph, name the cone as the PSD cone on the **traceless transfer space**. Unitality and trace preservation make both $\Psi$ and $2\Phi-I$ equal to the identity on the scalar block and block diagonal, so full-map comparison is equivalent to positivity on the traceless block. The separating dual is then exactly a traceless-space $Q\succeq0$, covered by (9). If retaining the full PSD cone, state that the common scalar contribution cancels and compress the separating $Q$ to the traceless block.
2. Keep “canonical EB” explicit wherever $S(Q)$ is used. The support calculation optimizes over maps $3\int\operatorname{Tr}(P X)P\,d\mu$; it does not compute support over arbitrary entanglement-breaking channels with unrelated measurement and preparation states. The claimed canonical comparator is sufficient.
3. The refinement sentence is valid when each effect is prepared in its normalized effect state: splitting $E=\sum_j e_jP_j$ replaces $|E\rangle\langle E|/\operatorname{Tr}E$ by $\sum_j e_j|P_j\rangle\langle P_j|$, a positive weighted-variance increment. State this convention or omit the sentence and define the rank-one canonical set directly.

These are scope and notation clarifications, not failed bounds. The star certificate does not establish the unrestricted bounded-Holevo or arbitrary-channel statement.
