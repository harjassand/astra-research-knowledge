All layer edges of Y_x are contained in a subdivided parent square,

The graph Y_q doubles the nonempty Q–Z star and joins its endpoint

## Consequences and retained bounds

Every actual qubit is eligible: it has both an X- and a Z-check
neighbor. No nonidentity one-site Pauli centralizes all checks;
there is neither a one-site stabilizer nor a one-site logical.
For its incident stabilizer span, conjugation by X_q and Z_q gives
two independent incidence characters, even after dependent check
rows are removed. Their characters are supported on the separate
Z- and X-check rows and are both nonzero.

The primary degree induction includes bulk crossings, boundary
subcomplexes with up to4 adjacent sectors, and inherited corners.
It gives Tanner degree<=7 for qubit and check vertices. Thus the
qubit interaction graph has degree<=7(7−1)=42. Greedy selection
of nonadjacent centers yields at least N/43 eligible sites with no
shared touched check. The safer radius-two interaction packing
gives N/1765 disjoint complete check neighborhoods.
