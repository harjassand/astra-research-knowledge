STATUS AND INTERFACE

Conditional on two named, internally audited mathematical imports below,
this is a complete classical Turing reduction with polynomial bit complexity.
It imports constructive classical LP/SDP/real-algebraic algorithms; the bounded
fixture does not implement the complete generic fixed-variable iid compiler.
No physical apparatus, quantum preparation, unrestricted parameter acquisition,
external specialist validation or novelty/priority claim is made.

Input: integer N>=1 (a numerical size), rationals alpha>=0, 0<=delta<=1,
h in Q, and rational 0<eps<1, with total parameter bit length L. Define

 J_a=(1/2) sum_{i=1}^N sigma_a^(i),
 rho=exp[alpha J^2/N-delta J_z^2/N+h J_z]/Tr exp[...].              (1)

The computational basis convention is |0>=spin-down, |1>=spin-up, so
J_z|D_kl>=(l-k/2)|D_kl>. Equivalently choose the simultaneously rotated Pauli
triple with sigma_z=diag(-1,+1). This fixes the field sign in all sampled
complementary strings; the rotationally invariant J^2/Pauli-moment formulas
are unchanged by that basis choice.

Output: a polynomial-size hierarchical classical distribution. Every output
label specifies N exactly rational 2-by-2 PSD trace-one local density matrices.
The sampled tensor product is a separable state. Its expectation rho_hat obeys

                   (1/2)||rho_hat-rho||_1 < eps.                  (2)

Both compilation and one subsequent label draw use time polynomial in
N,L,log(1/eps); each draw has a finite predetermined upper bound on fair-bit
use. It can be padded to a fixed total draw count if desired. This is
an ensemble sampler, not a claim of classical TV between nonunique quantum
decompositions. Any supplied common measurement has output TV at most (2).
The output may use mixed local states. Optional pure-state/hardware refinements
are separate interfaces and are not needed for (2).

The accompanying REUSABLE_ZERO_FIELD_COMPILER.txt improves the number of
generic iid compiler calls from O(N^2) to N: compile one zero-field state for
each subset size, then acquire and exactly apply rational common local tilts
to its atoms. Its own amplification/finite-bit ledger fits inside the block
allowance below. That refinement works for mixed as well as pure local atoms.

Unbounded binary magnitudes of alpha and h ARE admitted here. Sections 4 and 5
explicitly avoid dependence polynomial in their numerical magnitudes. Without
those steps, the raw Taylor/norm/filter route would only be polynomial in
N,L,alpha N,|h|N,log(1/eps), which is a weaker statement.

1. ACQUIRE ALL K_k EXACTLY; NO RADIAL ACQUISITION

Define

 K_k=binom(N,k)^-1 sum_{|S|=k}
        [P_sym,S/(k+1)] tensor [I/2]^tensor(N-k),  k=0,...,N.       (3)

Every column is a trace-one positive SU(2)- and permutation-invariant state.
The symmetry of the normalized pure-coherent Haar integral gives
P_sym,S/(k+1)=integral_{sphere} |n><n|^tensor k dOmega.

For a Pauli word with r nonidentity factors and counts (a,b,c), its coefficient
in K_k is exactly

 2^-N binom(N-r,k-r)/binom(N,k) * mu(a,b,c),                      (4)
 mu=0 if any count is odd, otherwise
 mu=[(a-1)!!(b-1)!!(c-1)!!]/(r+1)!!,  (-1)!!=1.

The coefficient is zero if r>k. To prove (4), choose S uniformly: the probability
that it contains the word's support is binom(N-r,k-r)/binom(N,k). The remaining
coefficient is the spherical moment of n_x^a n_y^b n_z^c. Reflection kills
odd counts. For even counts, integrate the corresponding moments of an
isotropic standard Gaussian, separating its radius and direction; the three
one-coordinate Gaussian double-factorial moments divided by the radius
moment give the displayed ratio. This also includes mu(0,0,0)=1.

There are D_N=binom(N+3,3) Pauli-orbit types. Thus all N+1 columns are acquired
with O(N D_N) rational coefficients of O(poly(N)) bits. Formula (4) uses the
SUM of distinct words of each type, not their average, so no extra multinomial
belongs in the orbit coefficient. It can be converted to matrix-unit orbits
by expanding each of the four Pauli letters in the four E_ab letters and
convolving their fixed-size coefficient tables. Polynomially many type tables
and O(poly(N)) coefficient bits suffice; no physical 2^N matrix is formed.

The positive heat integral from I1 writes the normalized isotropic state
G=exp(alpha J^2/N)/Tr exp(alpha J^2/N) as a positive radial mixture of
integral tau_(r n)^tensor N dOmega, 0<=r<1. Expand
tau_(r n)=r|n><n|+(1-r)I/2. The radial integrand is

             sum_{k=0}^N binom(N,k) r^k(1-r)^(N-k) K_k.           (5)

The Bernstein coefficients are nonnegative and sum to one. Integration proves
G in conv{K_0,...,K_N}. We use this only to establish feasible good weights.
We do not sample the heat radius, numerically integrate an infinite tail, or
assume its normalization/acquisition cost away. Alpha=0 gives K_0 exactly.

2. SMALLER EXACT SECTOR COLUMNS AND PHYSICAL TRACE-DISTANCE LP

Let b=0,...,floor(N/2), j_b=N/2-b, and

 d_b=binom(N,b)-binom(N,b-1),
 D_b=(N-2b+1)d_b.                                                 (6)

The spin-j_b sector has physical dimension D_b. Standard SU(2)/permutation
duality makes an operator invariant under both actions scalar on each such
sector. For completeness, the multiplicity formula also follows without
Clebsch-Gordan acquisition: a spin-j irrep has one vector of each magnetization
m=-j,...,j; the N-qubit magnetization-t subspace has dimension binom(N,t).
Consecutive positive-magnetization dimensions therefore give d_b. The spin
sectors may equivalently be specified by the explicit polynomials
P_b=prod_{c!=b}(J^2-j_c(j_c+1)I)/(j_b(j_b+1)-j_c(j_c+1)); all coefficients are
rational with polynomial bits. No Schur transform is an input oracle.

The exact sector probability of K_k is

 A_{b,k}=Tr(P_b K_k)
  =(N-2b+1)[binom(N-k,b)-binom(N-k,b-k-1)]/[(k+1)2^(N-k)].         (7)

Out-of-range binomials are zero. Proof: in the whole magnetization-t subspace,
(3) has probability

 M_t=[(k+1)2^(N-k)]^-1 sum_{l=0}^k binom(N-k,t-l).

SU(2) invariance implies its spin-j_b sector probability is
(2j_b+1)(M_{N-b}-M_{N-b+1})=(2j_b+1)(M_b-M_{b-1}). The last difference
telescopes to the two binomials in (7). In particular all columns are
nonnegative and sum_b A_{b,k}=1. The formula acquires O(N^2) rational data,
even when the Pauli-orbit columns are retained independently as certificates.

The target probabilities are

 q_b=D_b exp[alpha j_b(j_b+1)/N] / sum_c D_c exp[alpha j_c(j_c+1)/N]. (8)

For any simplex weights w, the EXACT PHYSICAL trace distance is

         T(G,sum_k w_k K_k)=(1/2)sum_b |q_b-(Aw)_b|.              (9)

Proof: both operators are scalar with eigenvalue q_b/D_b or (Aw)_b/D_b
repeated D_b times. Those physical multiplicities are already included in
(9); a trace in a compressed regular representation must not replace them.

3. NORMALIZED FILTER AND ITS COMPLETE BRANCH DISINTEGRATION

Put F=exp[-delta J_z^2/(2N)+h J_z/2]. All K_k and G commute with J_z;
rho=F G F/Tr(F^2 G). Define C=delta N/4+N|h|. Since |J_z|<=N/2,

        kappa(F)^2 <= exp(C).                                    (10)

For positive trace-one X,Y, write Phi_F(X)=FXF/Tr(FXF). The trace ideal bound
||F(X-Y)F||_1<=||F||op^2||X-Y||_1, normalization, and
Tr(FXF)>=lambda_min(F)^2 give

                  T(Phi_F(X),Phi_F(Y))<=2 exp(C) T(X,Y).         (11)

This estimate applies to the positive simplex mixture acquired below. It is
not postselection/rejection work; the normalizer is computed classically.

For a fixed k, write n=N-k and condition the n complementary sites on a
computational string with u ones. Its magnetization is m=u-n/2. The filtered
k-site symmetric block is diagonal in Dicke states, with exponents

 e_{k,u,l}=-delta(l-k/2+m)^2/N+h(l-k/2+m),
 Z_{k,u}=sum_{l=0}^k exp(e_{k,u,l}),
 C_{k,u}=Z_{k,u}^-1 sum_l exp(e_{k,u,l}) |D_kl><D_kl|.             (12)

Thus the unnormalized TYPE branch mass for X=sum_k w_k K_k is

 B_{k,u}=w_k binom(n,u) Z_{k,u}/[(k+1)2^n],
 beta=sum_{k,u} B_{k,u}=Tr(F^2 X).                               (13)

After sampling a type (k,u) with probability B/beta, choose S uniformly among
k-subsets and a u-one complementary string uniformly. On S draw a label from
an iid compiler for C_{k,u}; place the specified pure computational states
on its complement. There are (N+1)(N+2)/2 type branches. In fact
l-k/2+m=l+u-N/2, so only N+1 distinct scalar exponentials are needed. A prefix
sum of these values gives every interval Z_{k,u}=sum_{t=u}^{u+k}exp(e_t) in
one subtraction; there are O(N^2) such interval sums. No exponential family
of complementary strings is acquired.

The iid-cone admission of every C_{k,u} follows explicitly from I1. Set
delta'=delta k/N<=1 and h'=h-2delta m/N. Up to a positive scalar,

 C_{k,u} proportional to
 exp(h' J_z,S/2) S_k(delta') exp(h' J_z,S/2).                     (14)

The filter in (14) is a tensor product of the SAME one-qubit positive operator
on every site. It takes each iid pure product to another iid pure product,
with a positive scalar reweighting. Therefore C_{k,u} belongs exactly to the
theta=0 iid cone. k=0 is a scalar/empty product; k=1 is trivial. No false
inference from permutation symmetry alone is used.

4. REMOVE UNBOUNDED |h| BEFORE PRECISION ACCOUNTING

Let p=ceil(log_2(1/eps)) and
 q=ceil(log_2(128(N+1)/eps)), acquired by integer comparisons with powers of
two; no transcendental logarithm oracle is needed. If |h|>=delta+q, return
the all-up product for h>0, all-down product for h<0.

This shortcut has trace error at most eps/64, uniformly in alpha>=0. In the
orientation of h, let r=N/2-sign(h)m be the number of flipped spins. Relative
to the unique extremal j=N/2,m=sign(h)N/2 eigenstate, every (j,m) energy has
nonpositive alpha contribution, and

 delta[(N/2)^2-m^2]/N=delta(r-r^2/N)<=delta r.

So the total relative contribution at r is at most
binom(N,r) exp[-(|h|-delta)r]. Let t=exp[-(|h|-delta)]<=2^-q. The whole other
mass is <=(1+t)^N-1<=exp(Nt)-1<=2Nt<=eps/64. The density commutes with the
extremal projector, so its trace distance to that pure product is its other
normalized mass, no square-root loss.

Otherwise |h|<delta+q. Hence C=O(N log(N/eps)). Put

 s=ceil(2C), gamma=eps*2^(-s-8), and A=N+s+p+10.                  (15)

Since exp(C)<=2^s, only O(N log(N/eps)+log1/eps) accuracy bits are needed in
the remaining filter stage. Binary comparison handles an enormous supplied h
in poly(L) time without creating a representation of exp(h).

5. REMOVE UNBOUNDED ALPHA; ACQUIRE A POSITIVE ISOTROPIC APPROXIMATION

If alpha>=A, choose w_N=1 and every other w=0. The top-spin sector is exactly
K_N=P_sym,N/(N+1), with energy difference alpha to the next sector. Thus

                  T(G,K_N)<=2^N exp(-alpha)<=gamma/4.             (16)

The remaining filter error is bounded by (11), with s already polynomial.
If alpha<A, the numerical generator norm alpha(N+2)/4 is at most
O(N[N+s+p]), polynomial in N and log1/eps. This explicitly charges the
alpha N norm used by the matrix-exponential backend if one chooses that
backend. The simpler scalar acquisition below avoids forming that matrix.

For every b compute the nonnegative rational argument
x_b=alpha b(N+1-b)/N. Equation (8) becomes q_b=D_b exp(-x_b)/Z with x_0=0,
Z>=D_0>=1. Acquire dyadic 0<=vhat_b<=1 with

 |vhat_b-exp(-x_b)|<=tau_G,  tau_G=gamma*2^(-N-4), vhat_0=1.

Then qhat_b=D_b vhat_b/sum_c D_c vhat_c is an exactly positive trace-one
rational probability vector, and

                 TV(q,qhat)<=2^N tau_G<=gamma/16.                (17)

The bound follows by sum D_b=2^N, positivity of both normalizers and the
standard normalization l1 inequality. Denominators are common dyadics before
normalization, so no exponential LCM occurs.

Solve this explicit finite rational LP with feasible nonnegative weights:

 minimize (1/2)sum_b t_b,
 sum_k w_k=1, w_k>=0,
 t_b>=(Aw)_b-qhat_b, t_b>=qhat_b-(Aw)_b, 0<=t_b<=4.                (18)

The true q belongs to the K_k hull by (5), so opt<=gamma/16. Request additive
objective error gamma/16 from a standard Turing LP algorithm. The returned
rational weights are exactly simplex-feasible. This is not rounding a
possibly negative numerical solution into positivity without an error bound.

Explicit conditioning data: eliminate w_N, take w_k=1/(N+1), t_b=2. All
residual slacks are >=1 and all simplex coordinates are 1/(N+1). A reduced-
coordinate Euclidean ball of radius r_LP=1/[8(N+1)^2] lies in the feasible
polytope; one may take R_LP=5(N+1) around this center. These follow by bounding
the last simplex change by N r_LP and each residual change by (N+1)r_LP.
For an explicit diagonal SDP conversion, use the N+1 weight slacks, the two
residual slacks per b, and the t_b,4-t_b box slacks. There are N+1+4M_s slacks,
M_s=floor(N/2)+1. The slack derivative has norm at most 4(N+1): each row has
norm <=sqrt(N+1), and there are at most 3N+5 rows. Its inverse has norm <=1,
since it includes each reduced w coordinate and each t coordinate as its own
diagonal entry. Thus r_SDP=r_LP and R_SDP=20(N+1)^2 are valid relative Frobenius
radii about the explicit diagonal center. Set all off-diagonal entries to
zero, acquire the affine relations by rational Gaussian elimination, and read
the objective from the t slacks. This supplies the center/radii interface of
the cited rational SDP theorem, if that backend is preferred. Data sizes,
bit lengths and log(R/r) are polynomial. Boundary optimal weights do not
require a well-conditioned optimum.

Round the first N weights down to a common denominator Q_w=2^b_w with
Q_w>=16(N+1)/gamma; put the residual mass in w_N. Weights remain nonnegative
and sum to one. The weight TV change is <=N/Q_w<=gamma/16. Equations (17),(18)
and this rounding imply

                   T(G,sum_k w_k K_k)<=gamma/4.                  (19)

Alpha=0 may directly choose w_0=1. N=1 may directly compute its one-qubit
diagonal state, though the general construction also covers it. Huge rational
alpha is compared to A before any numerical exponential is acquired.

6. SCALAR EXPONENTIALS AND ALL NORMALIZERS COST BITS

For rational x>=0 and integer precision b>=1, acquire e^-x with dyadic error
at most 2^-b as follows. If x>=b+1, output zero: e^-x<=2^(-b-1). Otherwise,
form the positive rational Taylor partial sum S_r=sum_{j=0}^r x^j/j! for
r=8(ceil(x)+b+4). The tail is at most twice its first term, since successive
ratios are <=1/2. The elementary factorial bound n!>=(n/e)^n and e<3 give
tail<=2(3x/(r+1))^(r+1)<=2^(1-r-1), smaller than 2^(-b-4).
Thus |1/S_r-e^-x|<=2^(-b-4). Round 1/S_r down to a denominator 2^(b+1);
the total error is <2^-b and the value remains in [0,1]. x=0 returns one.
Every denominator/numerator has polynomial bits in b and input bits; r=O(b)
on the nonzero branch. The supplied large x is handled by rational comparison.
No unevaluated real exponential or floating promise is an output.

For all branch terms use the common upper shift H=|h|N/2 and
v_{k,u,l}=v_{u+l}=exp(e_{k,u,l}-H). Acquire only the N+1 distinct v_t values
and their prefix sums. Every such value lies in [exp(-C),1], hence
is >=2^-s. Acquire nonnegative dyadic vhat with absolute error <=gamma.
All vhat are strictly positive, since gamma<2^-s/256. Set

 Zhat_{k,u}=sum_l vhat_{k,u,l},
 Bhat_{k,u}=w_k binom(N-k,u) Zhat_{k,u}/[(k+1)2^(N-k)].             (20)

The common shift cancels in all normalized probabilities. The scaled total
normalizer beta'=exp(-H)beta is >=exp(-C)>=2^-s. Moreover the sum of absolute
raw branch errors is <=gamma, because
sum_{k,u} w_k binom(N-k,u)(k+1)/[(k+1)2^(N-k)]=1. Therefore

               TV(B/beta,Bhat/sum Bhat)<=gamma 2^s=eps/256.       (21)

Round these normalized branch probabilities down, assigning the residual to
one final branch, with a dyadic denominator Q_B>=128 M/eps, where
M=(N+1)(N+2)/2. This adds at most eps/128 in label TV. Zero w_k branches can
be omitted; if retained, the final residual may add mass to one such branch,
which is harmless provided its conditional block is compiled too.

Normalize the SAME vhat terms into phat_l=vhat_l/Zhat for each block. Then
TV(phat,p)<=gamma 2^s=eps/256. Acquire its exact matrix-unit orbit coordinates:
for a k-site table m=(m_00,m_01,m_10,m_11), the coefficient is

 phat_l/binom(k,l) if m_01=m_10 and l=m_11+m_10,
 zero otherwise.                                                (22)

This follows directly from the equal entries of |D_kl><D_kl|. It is a rational
PSD trace-one operator Rhat_{k,u}, with op norm <=1 and
T(Rhat,C_{k,u})<=eps/256. Formula (22) acquires D_k polynomial-size columns;
no rational approximation to a Schur basis is needed.

Apply I2 with eta=eps/32. Because C_{k,u} is in its exact iid cone,
dist(Rhat,C_iid)<=eps/256 and the output satisfies

                      T(S_hat,C_{k,u})<=5eps/128.                (23)

There are at most M calls, each with k<=N and polynomial input bits. At most
sum_{k=0}^N(N-k+1)binom(k+3,3)=binom(N+5,5) local iid labels are stored,
including an empty-product label for k=0. The generic oracle's constants
and degrees can be large; polynomial bit complexity is not a practical speed
claim. The output's one-qubit atoms and inner weights are exactly feasible,
so it cannot generate a negative probability or non-PSD local state.

7. FINITE BITS FOR SUBSETS AND COMPLEMENTARY STRINGS

Sample each dyadic branch or iid-node weight by its exact common-denominator
integer cumulative intervals. That uses its fixed denominator bit count and
never rejects. The algorithm must not assume an exact bounded-bit uniform
integer over a non-power-of-two range.

For an intended uniform rank in {0,...,M0-1}, choose Q0=2^b>=128 M0/eps,
draw U uniformly from {0,...,Q0-1} with b bits, and set rank=floor(M0 U/Q0).
Every rank has either floor(Q0/M0) or ceil(Q0/M0) preimages, so its TV from
uniform is <=M0/Q0<=eps/128. M0=1 needs zero bits. Exact binomial unranking
maps this to the k-subset, and separately to the u-one complementary string.
The two approximations add <=eps/64. Rank precision b<=N+O(log1/eps), integer
bits are O(N+log1/eps), and unranking needs polynomially many binomial steps.
This charges finite-bit sampling without a random unbounded rejection loop.

8. ERROR AND COST LEDGER

In the non-extremal-field branch:
 isotropic acquisition, including the large-alpha branch: <=gamma/4;
 normalized filter amplification via (11): <=eps/512;
 scalar branch probabilities: <=eps/256;
 branch dyadic rounding: <=eps/128;
 iid blocks, already including target acquisition and inner dyadic weights:
     <=5eps/128 uniformly over every type;
 uniform subset and complementary string approximations: <=eps/64.

The total is at most 35eps/512<eps/8<eps. Convexity of trace norm charges
conditional errors by their branch probabilities; label-TV errors charge the
same trace distance because every conditional product average has norm one.
The large-field branch has error <=eps/64. All claimed constants are slack.

Cutoff q=O(log N+log1/eps), s=O(N[log N+log1/eps]), A=O(N[log N+log1/eps]).
Scalar precision is O(N+s+log1/eps) and exponent bit length is poly(N,L,s).
The bounded generator norm, if used, is O(N[N+s+log1/eps]); the algorithm does
not instantiate an exponentially large alpha N or |h|N parameter. Exact
combinatorial integers have O(N) bits, and rational LP/SDP/iid-oracle data have
polynomial sizes and bit lengths. The N+1 exponential evaluations, O(N^2)
interval sums, and O(N^2) conditional calls therefore cost poly(N,L,log1/eps).
Acquiring all conditional orbit coefficients is polynomial too. Output data are polynomial,
each label specifies N systems, and fair-bit draw/unranking work is charged.

The expectation guarantee is deterministic about the compiled distribution.
Only selecting one output label is randomized. It has no confidence-failure
event, no classifier/unknown-Hamiltonian oracle, and no uncharged postselection.
The physical cost of preparing the N local density matrices is not supplied.
If an independent one-qubit preparer is given, its precision, N calls, random
bits, circuit compilation and apparatus must be added separately.
