Status: internally audited; finite counterexample verified; external review not performed.

Verdict. The W-dependent half of the trace proof extends to every finite-
dimensional Hermitian W >= 0 with WP=0. Neither [W,M]=0 nor number-sector
preservation is used; W may mix sectors inside Q. The free estimates, projection
cost, and entropy cost are unchanged. Once P and the free reference are fixed,
this proof half needs no locality or density-polynomial form for W.
For example, at L=4 and R=1, W=|psi><psi| with
|psi>=(|110>+|111>)/sqrt(2) is positive and satisfies WP=0, while its cross
term changes M, so [W,M]!=0.

The exact extra term in the relative-entropy comparison is

  Gamma(W)=beta Tr(sigma W)+log[ZW/Z0],
  ZW=Tr exp[-beta(H0+W)], Z0=Tr exp[-beta H0].

The same comparison closes with D<=G_L when Gamma(W)<=0; a proven upper bound
Gamma(W)<=c gives D<=G_L+c. If Gamma(W)>0, slack elsewhere could still yield
D<=G_L, but this comparison alone no longer does so. Gamma(W)<=0 is the exact
scalar condition that removes the extra W term, so there is no unique weakest
operator-only hypothesis. A useful structural sufficient condition is W>=0
and WP=0. Hermiticity and positivity
give the min-max eigenvalue ordering and ZW<=Z0. Also, W sigma=0. No operator
ordering of heat matrices is asserted. For W>=0, PWP=0 is equivalent to WP=0,
since PWP=(W^(1/2)P)^*(W^(1/2)P). For indefinite W, PWP=0 makes Tr(sigma W)=0
but does not control ZW/Z0.

The diagnostic at
work/programmes/spin_statistical/cycle2/leaves/leaf02_noncommuting_scaling/evidence/nonpositive_PWP_counterexample.py
uses the exact source conventions at L=3 (two sites), J=tau=1, lambda=0, R=1,
beta=9. In basis |00>,|01>,|10>,|11>, P=diag(1,1,1,0), H0 has eigenvalues
0,2,6,8. The indefinite Hermitian operator
W=-100(|00><11|+|11><00|) satisfies PWP=0 but WP!=0. The spectrum of H0+W
is 4 +/- sqrt(10016), 2, 6. Exact block calculation gives log(ZW/Z0)=864.7197,
Tr(rhoW Q)=0.480016, and Tr(rho0 Q)=5.38e-32. Measurement Q certifies
||rhoW-rho0||_1>=0.480016. The proposed bound evaluates to 0.011453 with
a_L=3.291e-7 and G_L=5.310e-5. Thus mere PWP=0, without positivity or another
partition-function condition, is a finite counterexample to the claimed
comparison. Numeric details are saved beside the script.

Scope remains the open 1D free-fermion H0, beta=tau L^2, the fixed-range P, and
the explicit free estimates in the audited proof. This does not establish a
finite-range/local-lattice theorem for another geometry or free reference. For
tW, W>=0 and WP=0, the finite-L bound is uniform in t and passes to the
t->infinity Gibbs limit on ker(W), which contains ran(P); the limit lies only
on ran(P) if ker(W)=ran(P). No external priority or novelty claim is made.
