BOUNDED OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS, UNBOUNDED MAX-PRIOR HOLEVO CAPACITY
2026-10-09. Independently derived explicit family; no optimizer premise.

1. EXACT DENSITIES
For N>=1 set a=1/8 and
  d_N=8*2^N*(N+1)!,
  P_j=a/[j(j+1)], Q_j=P_j/2^j, j=1,...,N,
  n_j=d_N Q_j=2^(N-j)*(N+1)!/[j(j+1)].
Each n_j is a positive integer because j and j+1 are distinct factorial
factors. Put
  P_0=1-sum_(j=1)^N P_j=1-aN/(N+1)>7/8,
  Q_0=1-sum_(j=1)^N Q_j>15/16,
  n_0=d_N Q_0=d_N-sum_j n_j,
  lambda_j=2^j, j>=1; lambda_0=P_0/Q_0.
Since Q_j<P_j and Q_0<=1, 7/8<lambda_0<1.

Define a faithful diagonal density rho_N whose eigenvalue is lambda_j/d_N
on n_j coordinates for j=0,...,N. Its trace is
sum_j n_j lambda_j/d_N=sum_j P_j=1. Let sigma_N=I/d_N.
Either take the finite family E_N of all coordinate permutations of rho_N,
or take its whole unitary orbit. Both contain a finite prior with mean
sigma_N (uniform coordinate permutations suffice). Thus sigma_N is in
conv(E_N), exactly, with no closure or dimension approximation.

2. UNIFORM OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS <=1 NAT
For rho_N relative to sigma_N, let the random log-likelihood under rho_N
be L=ln lambda_j, with mass P_j. The bulk likelihood has L<0. For s>0,
put k=floor(s/ln2)+1. Telescoping gives
  Pr_(rho_N){L>s}
       =sum_(j=k)^N a/[j(j+1)]
       <=a/k<=a ln2/s,                                (2.1)
with zero sum when k>N. This is an exact analytic tail bound.

For ANY effect 0<=F<=I, in rho_N's eigenbasis its diagonal entries lie in
[0,1]; off-diagonal entries do not affect either outcome probability.
Let p=Tr(F rho_N), q=Tr(F sigma_N). If p=0 use the zero convention. If
q=0 then F=0 because sigma_N is faithful, so p=0 too. For p,q>0 let
ell=ln(p/q). If ell<=0 the objective is nonpositive. If 0<ell<1,
p ell<=ell<1 since p<=1. If ell>=1, divide coordinates into
L<=ell/2 and L>ell/2. The first part contributes at most
  p_low<=exp(ell/2)q=p exp(-ell/2),
while the second contributes at most Pr{L>ell/2}. Hence (2.1) gives
  p[1-exp(-ell/2)]<=2a ln2/ell,
  p ell<=2a ln2/[1-exp(-ell/2)]
       <=6a ln2<1.                                   (2.2)
For the penultimate inequality, ell>=1 and exp(1/2)>=1+1/2=3/2 imply
1-exp(-ell/2)>=1/3. The last inequality uses 6a=3/4 and ln2<1.
Thus every effect, including fractional effects, has p ln(p/q)<=1.
Every permutation/unitary conjugate has the same bound against sigma_N,
so R_obs(E_N)<=1 for all N. This is unrestricted D_obs, not a finite
chosen collection of test effects or a numerical optimization.

3. CAPACITY IS EXACT AND DIVERGES
Every state in E_N has entropy S(rho_N). Therefore every finite prior has
Holevo information <=ln d_N-S(rho_N). The uniform permutation prior has
mean sigma_N and attains this upper bound. Thus, EXACTLY,
  C_chi(E_N)=D(rho_N||sigma_N)
      =sum_(j=1)^N P_j ln(2^j)+P_0 ln lambda_0
      =(ln2/8)(H_(N+1)-1)+P_0 ln lambda_0.             (3.1)
Here H_m=sum_(j=1)^m 1/j. Since lambda_0>7/8 and P_0<=1,
  P_0 ln lambda_0>=ln(7/8).
Consequently C_chi(E_N)>=(ln2/8)(H_(N+1)-1)+ln(7/8)->infinity,
while R_obs(E_N)<=1. The divergence is logarithmic in N; d_N is very
large and no efficient representation or acquisition claim is intended.

The finite permutation family is commuting and exactly reconstructible
by one basis-dephasing EB channel. The full unitary orbit contains genuine
noncommuting pairs: mix a lambda_0 and lambda_1 eigenvector by the 2 by 2
Hadamard. The commutator with rho_N has a nonzero off-diagonal magnitude
(lambda_1-lambda_0)^2/(2d_N^2). The radius and capacity calculations remain
valid for that larger family. This separation is a theorem about complexity
quantifiers, not an asserted obstruction or lower bound for broadcasting.

4. THE CENTER IS OPTIMAL, IF THAT EXTRA FACT IS DESIRED
For the permutation family, J(sigma)=sup_(rho in E_N)D_obs(rho||sigma)
is convex in sigma and invariant under coordinate permutations. Every
sigma in conv(E_N) is diagonal; its permutation average is sigma_N.
Jensen gives J(sigma_N)<=J(sigma). For the full unitary orbit use any
finite unitary twirl with output I/d (or its Haar barycenter, allowed in
closed conv(E_N)). Thus sigma_N is an observational-radius minimizer in
both cases. The exact value J(sigma_N) is unnecessary; the upper bound1
and exact capacity (3.1) establish the strict separation.
