# Independent gate of the local-generation transfer

7 October 2026. `rounding_prior`, verified GPT-6.1 Sol/max; no nested
workers. Independently read `notes/rounding_spectral/cycle13/LOCAL_GENERATION_TRANSFER.md`
and the primary Bravyi–Hastings–Michalakis, arXiv1001.0344, §§2.2–2.4.
This is a gate of a conditional lemma, not of the new 3D memory.

The criterion passes. For a signed abelian stabilizer group S with -I
absent, partial trace of its code projector keeps exactly the Pauli
terms supported in A. The reduced state is a scalar multiple of a
projector onto that signed subgroup's code. For locally generated S_B,
the same argument keeps S_B(A). Equal kernels therefore give equal
support projectors and, by Pauli-basis coefficient uniqueness, equal
signed groups. Thus TQO-2 is exactly S(A)=S_B(A), equivalently every
global stabilizer supported in A is generated by whole checks inside B.
This is precisely the older primary's Lemma2.1. For CSS codes a mixed
Pauli's support is the union of the X/Z supports; Y never cancels to I
outside A. Two separate filling proofs are necessary.

The support recurrence also passes under its explicitly supplied maps.
For a global boundary e=delta_f u, P e is a coarse boundary because P
is a chain map. A coarse local filling v yields

    e = delta_f(K e + I v).

The unknown global filling u only proves membership and never determines
the radius. The stated whole-check support hypotheses imply

    f_(i+1)(r) <= max(r+b, lambda f_i(r/lambda+a)+b).

Let r*=a lambda/(lambda-1). The projected radii satisfy
r_j <= r/lambda^j+r*(1-lambda^(-j)). The positive floor proves that
recursing all the way to a seed does not by itself localize the filling.

With the separate level-uniform radius-(r*+1) anchor M, stop after
j=ceil(log_lambda r), or at the seed, for r>=1. The lifted anchor costs
at most lambda r(M+b/(lambda-1)). An intermediate homotopy costs

    lambda^s(r_s+b)+b(lambda^s-1)/(lambda-1)
    = r + lambda^s[r*+b+b/(lambda-1)]-r*-b/(lambda-1),

which is at most r+lambda r[r*+b+b/(lambda-1)]. In the seed-first case,
the bound f_0(r_j)<=r_j+M costs at most
r+lambda r[r*+M+b/(lambda-1)]. The peer's displayed C exceeds all
three coefficients. Hence the conclusion f_i(r)<=Cr is valid with
constants independent of recursion depth, provided both sectors have
the maps and anchor. It gives no anchor for free.

The collar distinction is load-bearing. BHM §2.2 compares A with its
one-neighbor enlargement, an additive collar. Cr-ball filling is only
a proportional collar. It therefore does not directly satisfy that
theorem's stated TQO-2 premise. It may support another localization
or stability theorem, but that requires a new proof or exact prior.

The nearest obstruction is already stronger than an informal warning:
BHM §2.4 replaces plaquette checks by adjacent plaquette products and
one pinned plaquette. The global ground space remains toric, but local
generation fails away from the pin and an O(1/number-of-plaquettes)
local perturbation closes the gap. Thus unchanged homology/code space
does not imply local completeness or perturbation stability.
