STRONGEST CLAIM AT THIS CHECKPOINT

The known-parameter critical-spin product-mixture preparation already has a
positive trace-preserving sampler interface with first-order Lie splitting cost
O_(M,B)(eta^-3)+N. Replacing each one-slice Lie product by the equal positive
mixture of its forward and reverse order keeps every branch a valid local-filter
product mixture, but cancels the first splitting defect in the averaged map.
For the exact same target and the same importance-resampling variance bound, the
finite semigroup trace error becomes G_N/m^2. The number of Trotter slices falls
from O_(M,B)(eta^-1) to O_(M,B)(eta^-1/2), so the full real-arithmetic cost falls
to O_(M,B)(eta^-5/2)+N. The state guarantee remains E_sep,N+2 eta in averaged
trace distance. This is a scoped algorithmic improvement, not a new general
randomized product-formula principle or a field-breaking theorem.

INTERFACE AND SUPPLIED VERSUS ACQUIRED DATA

There are N spin-half sites, J_i=(1/2) sum_a sigma_i^(a), public fixed finite
M,B, supplied known real symmetric A with ||A||<=M and supplied known b with
|b|<=B. The input is the whole density

  rho = exp(K)/Tr exp(K),
  K = 2 J^2/N + Q_A(J)/N^(3/2) + b.J/N^(3/4),
  Q_A(J)=sum_ik A_ik (J_i J_k+J_k J_i)/2.

The target is the averaged output density only. There is no claim of preserving
an external reference/purification, acquiring A,b, a local-lattice extension,
uniformity as M,B grow, a physical postselection success probability, or a
hardware gate-set implementation. Rational A,b and finite-bit input costs are
required for a polynomial bit-cost statement.

Let C=A+M I>=0, s=N^(3/4), t0=2/N-M/N^(3/2)>0, and take the three columns v_i
of sqrt(C). Define F_i=v_i.J and

  H = sum_i F_i^2/N^(3/2) + b.J/s,
  L_0(X) = {b.J/s,X}/2,
  L_i(X) = [F_i^2 X+2 F_i X F_i+X F_i^2]/(4 N^(3/2)),
  L = L_0+L_1+L_2+L_3.

This is the prior audited positive generator: L(X)={H,X}/2 minus the sum of
double commutators /4. Each exp(delta L_i), delta>=0, is exactly a Gaussian
average of congruences, and exp(delta L_0) is one deterministic congruence.
They preserve mixtures of identical one-qubit product states. The continuous
separable comparison state is sigma=exp(L)(exp(t0 J^2))/Tr exp(L)(exp(t0 J^2));
the existing finite-sector proof supplies T(rho,sigma)<=E_sep,N(M,B), with
E_sep,N=O_(M,B)(N^-3/2).

POSITIVE RANDOM-ORDER REPRESENTATION

For h=1/m, set

  P_+(h)=exp(hL_0) exp(hL_1) exp(hL_2) exp(hL_3),
  P_-(h)=exp(hL_3) exp(hL_2) exp(hL_1) exp(hL_0),
  Pbar(h)=(P_+(h)+P_-(h))/2,
  W_m=Pbar(1/m)^m(exp(t0 J^2)),  sigma_m=W_m/Tr W_m.

At every slice, toss one fair bit to select the forward or reverse ordering.
All four factors on either branch are completely positive local-filter maps.
Thus Pbar and W_m are positive; expanding Pbar^m gives an exact positive
mixture over the m orientation bits and the Gaussian filters. This costs one
extra unbiased bit per slice and does not double the number of filters.

For each one-slice product, the degree-0 and degree-1 Taylor coefficients match
exp(hL). The degree-2 coefficient of P_+ has each same-letter term L_i^2/2
and one ordering of every distinct pair; P_- has the opposite ordering. Their
average is exactly L^2/2. If q_j=sum_i ||L_i||_(1->1) on a spin-j block, the
degree-k absolute norm majorant for either product and exp(hL) is (h q_j)^k/k!.
Consequently

  ||Pbar(h)-exp(hL_j)||_(1->1)
       <= 2 sum_(k>=3) (h q_j)^k/k!
       <= (h q_j)^3 exp(h q_j)/3.

Also ||Pbar(h)|| and ||exp(hL_j)|| are at most exp(h q_j). Telescoping m
steps therefore gives

  ||Pbar(1/m)^m-exp(L_j)||_(1->1)
       <= exp(q_j) q_j^3/(3m^2).

The constants can be made explicit in the critical-spin sectors. Since
sum_i ||v_i||^2=Tr C<=6M and ||F_i||<=||v_i|| j,

  q_j <= 6M x_j+B ell_j =: Q_j,
  x_j=j(j+1)/N^(3/2),  ell_j=j/N^(3/4).

Let d_j=2j+1, m_N(j)=(2j+1)/(N+1) binom(N+1,N/2-j),
a_N(j)=d_j m_N(j) exp(2j(j+1)/N), and
Z_base=sum_j a_N(j) exp(-M x_j). The spin-block denominator has the lower
bound Tr exp(L_j)(I_j)>=d_j: L_j is Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint, so scalar
Jensen in I_j gives Tr exp(L_j)I_j/d_j >= exp(Tr H_j/d_j)>=1, since C>=0
and Tr(b.J)=0. Combining the block error with t0 and the above bound yields

  T(sigma_m,sigma) <= G_N/m^2,
  G_N = [sum_j a_N(j) exp(5M x_j+B ell_j) Q_j^3]/[3 Z_base].

The established quartic sector envelope a_N(j)/(2^N N^(3/4)) ->
c r^2 exp(-4r^4/3) with integrable fixed tilts exp(c_1 r^2+c_2 r) and
polynomial factors implies G_N=O_(M,B)(1). For M=B=0, G_N=0 and the split
is exact. Thus m=max(1,ceil(sqrt(G_N/eta))) makes this term at most eta.

IMPORTANCE SAMPLING AND TOTAL COST

Each sampled branch is a single-qubit matrix g and seed tau, giving the product
state (g tau g*/Tr(g tau g*))^(tensor N) and positive weight
w=[Tr(g tau g*)]^N. The seed rejection envelope, the determinant-one identity
Ew>=1, and the two-replica central supersolution bound Ew^2<=K2*(M,B) from the
audited Addendum D apply uniformly to every ordering: each generator is used
for total time one, and the same factors remain in SL(2,C). With R proposals,
weighted-reservoir resampling gives averaged-state trace error at most
sqrt(K2*/R). Set R=max(1,ceil(K2*/eta^2)). Then

  T(rho, output average) <= E_sep,N + G_N/m^2 + sqrt(K2*/R)
                         <= E_sep,N+2 eta.

Seed rejection has the previous explicit constant expected attempt bound
C_g/L_0, and the finite-bit construction caps attempts at
K=ceil(p_0^-1 log(16R/epsilon)) using the same public acceptance lower bound.
Each path still has 3m normal draws and 4m filter/matrix factors; the new
orientation choice costs m exact random bits. Sector sums cost O(N) scalar
terms. The resulting real-arithmetic work is O_(M,B)(R m + R K + N)=
O_(M,B)(eta^-5/2)+N, versus the audited O_(M,B)(eta^-3)+N bound.

The earlier finite-bit compiler's error proof is order-agnostic: path norms
depend on the sum of absolute factor exponents, multiplication roundoff on the
same 4m updates, and seed/weight/output guards on the same branch quantities.
It carries over with m replaced by ceil(sqrt(G_N/eta)); add m exact fair bits
per candidate. Normal draw count remains 3R(K+m), the retained-path U,Q and
precision h formulas remain unchanged at the new m, and weight sampling remains
dyadic/positive. This is a mathematical finite-bit existence transfer, not an
implemented interval compiler. It still has polynomial-in-N public sector-sum
preprocessing, bounded rejection with charged K, R=O(eta^-2) proposals, and
N local emissions. For fixed M,B, the total bit cost is polynomial in rational
input length, N, eta^-1, epsilon^-1; no optimal bit exponent is claimed.

https://arxiv.org/html/1805.08385v2 . It already identifies the second-order

separability-preserving spin semigroup, with a sector-uniform normalized trace
bound and the pre-existing trace-weighted local-product preparation sampler.
