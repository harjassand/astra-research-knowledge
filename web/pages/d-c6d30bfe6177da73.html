## Concrete established mechanism

[Chen–Anshu–Nguyen, arXiv2504.02706v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02706v1), submitted 3 April 2025 15:42:23 UTC. Read LemmaIII.1 and its double-Bohr proof, III.4 local faithfulness statement and selected proof, LemmasIV.1/IV.3/IV.4 and IV.5/AlgorithmIV.1, and main theorem statements. The exact identity turns a KMS defect integral into beta/2 times the KMS pairing of O with [A,H-H']. Taking O=[A,H-H'] yields a positive norm and ordinary local algebra generation then identifies the Hamiltonian. The Gaussian/Fourier-regularized observable Q localizes this information to bounded patches without requiring target eigenvectors, target thermal-state preparation or a partition function.

LemmaIII.6 quantitatively compares the maximally mixed norm of a supported operator B with its target KMS norm; the factor depends on beta, support size and interaction degree rather than the global smallest eigenvalue. LemmaIII.7 converts local full-Pauli commutator bounds into coefficient accuracy. These are useful supported-operator bounds, not a theorem for SLD-filtered commutators of arbitrary matrices.

The precise cost-bearing interface is important. LemmaIV.4 certifies a candidate H' when Q is small against every localized test Hamiltonian G on the specified coefficient net. Replacing this quantified test family by G=H' is not licensed by that lemma. LemmaIV.5 costs O(exp(beta Omega')/(beta eta^2) log(1/delta)) target copies per Q, plus local gates polynomial in beta, patch volume, logarithmic net precision and exp(beta Omega'/2). AlgorithmIV.1 multiplies the copy bound by the overlap coloring number chi. The bounded-degree exhaustive protocol uses radius O(beta^4+(beta+1)log(1/epsilon)); its coefficient-net search can be enormous. The lattice iterative protocol in TheoremI.2 achieves near-optimal n,epsilon scaling but requires its full update/initialization analysis.

Our usable deduction is a certification route: retain a proposed SLD learner, then require the finite Q test family and the stated truncation/net/statistical tolerances before certifying local coefficients. This can reject a blind SLD minimizer within the promised Hamiltonian model. It inherits the prior algorithm's overhead, not a new cheap loss or convergence theorem. A worthwhile improvement would reduce the quantified G family for an already-close candidate with an explicit proof and equally informed comparator.

## Why Gibbs-sampler gaps do not prove SLD identification

[Bergamaschi–Chen, arXiv2510.08533v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08533v2), v1 9 October 2025 17:51:03 UTC, v2 23 January 2026 00:02:59 UTC. Read main interface, TheoremsV.1/VI.1 statements, LemmaVI.1, and AppendixA LemmaA.2 proof. For bounded nearest-neighbor 1D Hamiltonians and fixed beta/local dimension, V.1 gives a size-independent gap for the UNFILTERED local-Pauli KMS commutator form. VI.1 compares a Gaussian-frequency Gibbs Lindbladian Dirichlet form with that form. LemmaVI.1 is an integral of squared norms of commutators with frequency/time-resolved operators. It does not square a single averaged dressed observable. Thus it cannot be substituted for the SLD form without an additional reverse comparison. Our exact collision demonstrates that such a universal comparison fails for unrestricted faithful six-site blocks.

LemmaA.2 bounds complex-time local evolution through a factorial annulus tail. This permits local model-side endpoint-score approximation at fixed beta in 1D, but its constants grow doubly exponentially in beta. It does not alone establish an endpoint learner's optimization rate, statistical conditioning or gradient cost.

## Remaining scientific question

All-temperature local KMS sufficient statistics and efficient lattice Hamiltonian learning already exist. A consequential new repair must show something additional: substantially cheaper validation of an SLD candidate, better beta dependence, a broader accessible model, or a score geometry whose actual data/measurement interface remains favorable. Adding more local SLD axes is now ruled out universally by the full XYZ obstruction.
