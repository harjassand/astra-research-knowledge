The useful change to the research programme is an explicit separation of three questions: acquiring an exact hidden quartic representation, recovering its axes under noisy coefficients, and bounding a downstream energy when axes are ambiguous. ## Adopted local theorem and universal obstruction

The legal exact model is

\[
 f(x)=h_Q(Lx),\qquad h_Q(z)=(z^2)^TQ(z^2),\quad Q\succ0.
\]

The local stage supplies an exact candidate \((L_0,Q_0)\), certified \(Q_0\succeq qI\), \(B\ge\|Q_0\|_F\), and a matched branch. After absorbing row scaling into Q, use

\[
 L=(I+A)L_0,\quad \operatorname{diag}A=0,\quad Q=Q_0+BE,
 \quad p^2=\|A\|_F^2+\|E\|_F^2.
\]

In the Bombieri coefficient norm, the frame derivative has gap \(2q\). Its odd monomials are orthogonal to every unknown-Q derivative, whose gap is at least \(B/\sqrt3\). For the rational lower bound \(\gamma=\min(2q,B/2)\), the four-slot tensor expansion gives

\[
 \|DF(A,E)-DF(0,0)\|\le46Bp\quad(p\le1).
\]

The constant follows from the squared Frobenius norm \(28^2+32^2+15^2=2033<46^2\); it uses the joint parameter norm. On the convex radius \(r=\gamma/(92B)\) ball,

\[
 \|F(\theta)-F(\theta')\|_B\ge\frac\gamma2\|\theta-\theta'\|.
\]

This is an inverse bound on the image of a specified branch. If a true promised quartic is in that branch, an observed coefficient error \(\delta\) and candidate residual \(\rho\) imply

\[
 p\le\frac{2\|L_0^{-1}\|_{op}^4(\delta+\rho)}\gamma.
\]

The proof also bounds the corresponding axes, coordinate reflections and chosen-gauge metric. The supplied branch, fitting cost, row normalization, discrete matching and coordinate condition factors are part of the interface. A small residual alone does not certify the branch, and a noisy polynomial need not belong to the model image. Full derivation: `notes/metric_use10/cycle11/NOISY_INTERFACE_AND_LOCAL_STABILITY.md`. Independent mathematical gate: `notes/root_cycle11/LOCAL_STABILITY_GATE.md` (**PASS**).

The need for a gap is universal, including arbitrary n. Let \(Q_\varepsilon=J+\varepsilon I\), and compare the quartic with a rational orthogonal rotation of its first two coordinates by the block \(\bigl[\begin{smallmatrix}3/5&-4/5\\4/5&3/5\end{smallmatrix}\bigr]\). Both frames have condition number 1 and both quartics dominate \(\|x\|^4\). Their unordered axis sets are distance \(3/5\) apart, while their coefficient distance is only \((24\sqrt2/25)\varepsilon\). The midpoint observation therefore forces axis error at least \(3/10\) for one model at noise \((12\sqrt2/25)\varepsilon\). Giving the identity metric to both estimators does not remove the ambiguity. The skew frame tangent has exactly gap \(2\varepsilon\), so the local \(1/q\) scaling has the necessary order on this family. Coercivity and frame conditioning cannot replace anisotropy or global separation.

## A downstream observable which can remain stable

For a supplied/acquired candidate \(h_Q(L_0x)\) and aligned quadratic \(a^T(L_0x)^2\), suppose the actual quartic and quadratic have certified pointwise errors \(\delta\|z\|^4\) and \(\eta\|z\|^2\) in candidate coordinates. Bombieri coefficient errors imply these after charging coordinate action and candidate residual. The target may depart from the hidden class.

Set \(J=11^T\), \(S_\pm=Q\pm\delta J\), \(b_-=a+\eta1\), \(b_+=a-\eta1\), and

\[
 \tau(Q)=\frac1{1^TQ^{-1}1}.
\]

When \(\delta<\tau(Q)\), the actual global energy minimum lies between the minima of \(m^TS_-m-b_-^Tm\) and \(m^TS_+m-b_+^Tm\) over \(m\ge0\). Both are strictly convex quadratic programs. Their rational KKT solutions give exact degree-four weighted SOS witnesses via

\[
 P(m)-P_*=(m-m_*)^TS(m-m_*)+\mu^Tm,
 \quad \mu=2Sm_*-b\ge0,\quad \mu_i m_{*,i}=0.
\]

The interval is sharp for the pointwise uncertainty class, not claimed sharp for a Bombieri coefficient ball. It bounds global energy values; it does not preserve the local-minimum landscape under off-class perturbations. For \(Q=uJ+vI\), \(\tau=u+v/n\) remains positive as anisotropy \(v\to0\), while the axis gap vanishes. This is a concrete reason to return a useful energy interval with ambiguous axes.

An equally informed ordinary order-two SOS relaxation has exactly the same endpoint optimum: the displayed witness gives its lower bound and the attained endpoint minimum gives its upper bound. Four witnesses are independently reconstructed in the original expanded coordinates. No numerical SDP or runtime advantage is claimed. ## Actual source class and the stronger competent control

[Mesters–Zwiernik arXiv2206.13668v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13668v4), revised 19 March 2024, §2.1 Eq(2), supplies the variable-dimensional dependent-components model \(AY=\tau\eta\). Our stated specialization takes independent symmetric standardized \(\eta_i\), positive excess kurtoses \(\kappa_i\), \(E\tau^2=1\), and \(E\tau^4=c>1\). Its fourth cumulant is exactly

\[
 3(c-1)\|t\|^4+c\sum_i\kappa_i t_i^4,
 \quad Q=3(c-1)J+c\operatorname{diag}\kappa.
\]

Observed coefficients have hidden frame \(L=A^{-T}\). Its inverse-frame axes are directions of the rows of the unmixing matrix A. A finite-support source realization gives the obstruction family above after a positive scalar rescaling, so the failure of gap-free recovery expresses weak identification in an actual published statistical class. Numeric distributions and rational calibrations are our instantiations, not a published experiment.

The covariance \(G=A^{-1}A^{-T}=L^TL\) is available from the same observations and is used by the published estimator. It supplies a metric to both comparators. Equal positive kurtoses fail one generic contraction-profile hypothesis of the source theorem; that difference does not imply an acquisition advantage. A covariance-informed classical eigenmatrix control succeeds on this case too:

* For the supplied positive radial-plus-odeco subclass, the covariance-adjusted middle tensor operator is conjugate to \(D\mapsto\alpha(2D+\operatorname{tr}(D)I)/3+\operatorname{diag}(\beta_iD_{ii})\).
* Its top n-dimensional eigenspace is the complete latent diagonal algebra, with separation at least \(\min_i\beta_i\). This remains true when all kurtoses coincide.
* The resulting commuting, G-self-adjoint matrices recover axes using a finite moment-curve search and ordinary diagonalization.

The exact callable receives only raw quartic coefficients, covariance and the same declared subclass. It withholds the latent Q and frame, reconstructs axes/reflections, and passes an independent derivative-tensor check on our n=4 rational fixture. Its 0.134 s run is a finite identity demonstration, not a practical/noisy comparison. The implementation checks a rational-middle-spectrum guard; the analytic subspace identity is more general. Acquisition of covariance, the full cumulant tensor, the \(n(n+1)/2\)-dimensional operator, joint diagonalization, sample errors and spectral gaps remain charged.

This control follows established eigenmatrix/orthogonal-tensor methods: Cardoso–Souloumiac's December 1993 paper (corrected author reprint January 1997) and [Boralevi–Draisma–Horobet–Robeva v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.08031v1), 25 December 2015, Proposition7/PDFp5 and Proposition14/PDFpp9–10. The dependent-common-variance top-subspace identity is our specialization, with historical priority unassessed; their independence/odeco theorems are not misattributed as directly stating the complete dependent model.
