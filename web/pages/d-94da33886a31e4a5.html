THEOREM

For each j choose one of these supplied local real test spaces:
  (i) V_j=span{I,J1,J2,J3} inside M3, with the vector spin-one generators;
  (ii) V_j=span{I,G1,...,Gk} inside M_d, with supplied traceless Hermitian

Let Phi_j be ANY unital TP self-compatible CPTP local channel, with a
supplied equal-marginal broadcaster. Phi_j need NOT be HS-self-adjoint,
normal, covariant, or preserve its chosen local test space.

For Phi=tensor_j Phi_j and V=tensor_j V_j, there is ONE canonical pure-
ensemble EB map Psi on M_D, D=product_j d_j, such that for EVERY Hermitian
A in V, including arbitrary entangled linear combinations,

  tau_D[A^2]-tau_D[A Psi(A)]
     <=2{tau_D[A^2]-tau_D[A Phi(A)]}.                          (N1)

The same map controls every A in V. The conclusion is full-domain only
when every local V_j is full-domain, e.g. all three Pauli directions for
each qubit. This proves a full growing-dimensional C2 family without
local HS-self-adjointness. Spin-one factors retain the five local
quadrupole directions outside their test span; those directions are
covered instead by the separate full-covariant-qutrit theorem when its
scalar/covariance hypotheses hold.

1. THE SUPPLIED SPIN CERTIFICATE ALSO BOUNDS MINUS THE STAR

For diagonal weights w_i>=0 and the exact optimizer G from the supplied

The supplied theorem proves D-W>=0. Its generic block structure proves

  D+W>=0.                                                     (N2)

This is not inferred by substituting noncommuting variables or discarding
the sign. Here is the complete sign argument, on the same D2/BC-swap
block decomposition as the supplied certificate.

In each BC-symmetric parity-i sector, sign-flip the source's two y leaves
and order the five vectors as (x,yj,yk,uj,uk). The diagonal of 5(D-W) is
  (5Ki,3Ki+2Kj,3Ki+2Kk,Ki+4Kj,Ki+4Kk),

  14=24=-5sqrt(2)wk; 15=35=-5sqrt(2)wj; 45=-5wi.

  v^dagger 5(D+W) v >= |v|^T 5(D-W) |v| >=0,                 (N3)
