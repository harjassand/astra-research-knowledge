## Interface mapping

The elaborated theorem has this public form (the audit output records `#check`):

```lean
∀ (m : Nat) (a b : Real) (E : (Fin m → Real) → Real),
  a ≤ b →
  (∀ r, InCube a b r → 0 < E r) →
  ∃ F : BoxFamily m a b,
    FamilyLittleOAt F a ∧ FullApproximation a b E F.family
```

`BoxFamily` packages a finite, nonempty indexed family and a coordinatewise
`[a,b]` bound on each slope. `value` is the finite dot-product sum
`Σ_i slope_i * Real.rpow h (p i)` plus the bump. On positive `h`, `Real.rpow`
is the ordinary real power used in the source's `X_h(p)`. `Active` is weak
comparison (`≤`) against every family label, so ties are retained.
`LittleO f α` is `Tendsto (fun h => f h / Real.rpow h α)` from the positive
neighborhood of zero to zero, exactly encoding `f(h)=o(h^α)` as `h→0+`.
`FullApproximation` only constrains the limiting `p` to the cube; `pSeq` is an
unrestricted function-valued sequence. Its conclusion is
`∀ i, |p i - slope i| < E slope`, the coordinatewise form equivalent to the
source's strict infinity-norm inequality on finite `Fin m`.

The theorem is slightly stronger than the source lemma at the interface level:
it also handles `m=0` and `a=b`, and requires only eventual weak activity rather
than activity at every sequence index. `E` is represented by a total real-valued
function, but positivity is assumed only at cube points. Every occurrence of
`E` in the conclusion is at a cube slope; the lower-dimensional restriction is
shown positive by lifting into the original cube. Thus no positivity outside
the cube is assumed. No continuity, measurability, or computability of `E` is
present.

## Composition traced against the source proof

The recursive `OracleHyp` at `GlobalAffineComposition.lean:30–40` is a local
function obtained from the strong induction hypothesis. The exported theorem
does not accept `SliceOracle`, `LocalWindowData`, a pre-proved local guarantee,
or any custom axiom as input.

The source's fixed-minimum stage and its raised-coordinate comparisons are
carried through `SliceFamily`, `StageLocalEstimate`, and `StageLocalCompose`.
`StageArchive`/`StageCertificate` retain each stage's labels in the common
terminal finite family. `BadNeighborhood` then turns the exact-slice local
bound into an open minimum-coordinate neighborhood by a closed active-limit
bad set inside the compact cube; its only use of `E` in the bad set is the fixed
scalar at the fixed label slope. This does not assume continuity of offsets or
of `E`.

The outer proof constructs the local open cover, prunes it by choosing a
minimum-cardinality finite subcover, and orders the retained centers
(`AffineInduction.lean:57–111`; `GlobalAffineComposition.lean:43–101`). The
`N33FiniteGlue.exists_cover_gluing_data` contract matches the source geometry:
the retained intervals are symmetric, centered in `[a,b]`, positive-radius,
cover `[a,b]`, and no retained open interval is contained in another. It gives
strictly ordered cuts in the proper adjacent intersections and strict outer
containment of `a,b`. The composition passes those exact hypotheses and uses
the resulting cuts for the tagged union.

`GluePlacement.shiftedUnion` uses the source's bump
`c_j(h) - Σ_{ℓ<j} h^(q_ℓ)`. `active_minimum_in_slab` compares a global weakly
active tag with the neighboring baseline labels; the strict power gaps put the
limiting minimum in that tag's closed cut slab. The finite-glue inheritance
lemma turns the closed slab into strict membership in the local open window
(both cut endpoints lie strictly inside the adjacent windows), and cancels the
common shift to recover local weak activity. Finally, each local bump is
`o(h^(center+radius))`, every internal cut exponent exceeds its center and thus
`a`, and finite sums/subtractions preserve the required global `o(h^a)` bound.
No approximant-range condition is introduced at any of these steps.

I found no mismatch in the requested finite-cover or active-label gluing
contract: the actual proof uses the noncontainment/center-order hypotheses to
make both endpoint sequences ordered, excludes gaps via the cover, chooses a
cut in each proper consecutive intersection, and routes the active label into
its local neighborhood. Weak all-ties comparisons are preserved throughout.
