FOUR-DIMENSIONAL QUARTIC-DISPERSION FILTER: INDEPENDENT DERIVATION
2026-10-08. Status: proof candidate; constants are uniform but not optimized.
This note is independent of the active leaf's promised result. It is not an
external validation or a priority claim.

1. MODEL AND CLAIM

Let Lambda_L={1,...,L-1}^4, S=(L-1)^4, and h_L be the Dirichlet nearest-
neighbor graph Laplacian with edge weight J>0. Its sine eigenvalues obey

  4J |k|^2/L^2 <= eps_k <= J pi^2 |k|^2/L^2,
  1<=k_j<=L-1.

Take K_L=dGamma(h_L^2/128), beta=tau L^4, and
H_0=K_L-lambda M/L^4, with lambda in [-B,0]. Let w be any nonnegative
closed form whose kernel contains the no-double-occupancy subspace, and
assume q_0+w is densely defined. The proposed uniform conclusion is

  D(rho_w || rho_0) <= C / sqrt(log L),
  ||rho_w-rho_0||_1 <= sqrt(2C) (log L)^(-1/4)

for all sufficiently large L, with C depending only on J,tau,B. The same
conclusion holds for hard compression onto any closed allowed subspace
containing the no-double-occupancy subspace, provided the restriction of
the free quadratic form to that subspace is densely defined. w need not be
bounded or number conserving.

2. FREE THERMAL BOUNDS

Write q_k=beta eps_k^2/128-tau lambda. Put a=tau J^2/8. Then
q_k>=a |k|^4. Choose a number tilt alpha=a/4. Since |k|^4>=1,
q_k-alpha >= (3a/4)|k|^4. The sums

  N_s=sum_{k in N^4}(exp((3a/4)|k|^4)-1)^(-1),
  E_s=sum_{k in N^4}|k|^r(exp((3a/4)|k|^4)-1)^(-1), r=1,2,4,

are finite public constants (enlarge them as needed below). In both rho_0
and rho_alpha proportional to exp(alpha M)rho_0, the mode occupations
and all fixed polynomial mode moments are bounded by these sums. Also

  C_alpha:=Tr(rho_0 exp(alpha M)) <= exp(N_s),
  M^p <= c_{p,alpha} exp(alpha M),

so any fixed particle-count polynomial multiplying a positive diagonal
observable is absorbed into a single tilted free state. No m! sector
factor is used.

The normalized four-dimensional sine modes satisfy
  |phi_k(x)|<=4/L^2,
  |phi_k(x+e)-phi_k(x)|<=4 pi |k_j|/L^3,
with zero sine values outside the box. Hence, uniformly in sites and in the
tilted/untilted free states,

  |gamma(x,y)|<=C L^-4,
  gamma_bb(x,x)<=C L^-6,
  Gamma_2(x,y):=<a_x^*a_y^*a_y a_x><=C L^-8,
  Gamma_3(x,y,z):=<a_x^*a_y^*a_z^*a_z a_y a_x><=C L^-12,
  Gamma_{2,grad}(x,y):=<b_x^*a_y^*a_y b_x><=C L^-10,

where b_x=a_{x+e}-a_x. The last three bounds follow from the exact
gauge-invariant boson Wick/permanent identities: Gamma_2 is a two-term
permanent, Gamma_3 a six-term permanent, and
Gamma_{2,grad}=gamma_bb gamma_yy+|gamma_by|^2. Cauchy-Schwarz for gamma
gives |gamma_by|^2<=gamma_bb gamma_yy. These identities include repeated
sites and use normal-ordered factorial occupations.

Finally, Tr rho_0 M<=C and beta Tr rho_0 K_L<=C, since each thermal mode
has energy O(|k|^4/L^4) and q_k>=a|k|^4.

3. SMOOTH LOG FILTER AND COLLISION LOSS

Set ell=floor(sqrt(L)), t=log ell. Let r=|z|_2 and let
chi(s)=10s^3-15s^4+6s^5 on [0,1]. Define f(z)=0 for r<=1,
f(z)=chi(log(r)/t) for 1<r<ell, and f(z)=1 for r>=ell. The first two
derivatives of chi vanish at both endpoints, so this radial filter is C^2
across the inner and outer cutoffs. Define the symmetric diagonal Fock
multiplier

  F_m(x_1,...,x_m)=product_{i<j} f(x_i-x_j),  F_0=F_1=1.

Then 0<=F<=I, [F,M]=0, ran F is contained in the no-double-occupancy
subspace, and F has finite rank: a configuration with F_m>0 has mutual
separation greater than one, so m is bounded on the finite lattice. Thus
w vanishes on ran F even for an arbitrary positive form as in Section 1.

Let delta=1-Tr rho_0 F^2 and q_M=Tr rho_0 M(I-F^2). Since
1-product u_i <= sum(1-u_i) for 0<=u_i<=1,

  delta <= (1/2) sum_{x,y} Gamma_2(x,y) 1_{|x-y|<ell}
         <= C ell^4/L^4,
  q_M <= C ell^4/L^4.

The q_M estimate uses M<=c exp(alpha M), tilts the free state once, and
then applies the same two-point Wick bound. For ell=sqrt(L), both losses are
O(L^-2).

The lattice filter satisfies the two discrete capacity estimates

  A_1:=sum_z sum_{e in +/- coordinate directions}|f(z+e)-f(z)|^2
       <= C ell^2/t^2,
  A_2:=sum_z |(Delta_disc f)(z)|^2 <= C/t.

Indeed, for 2<=|z|<=ell, |grad f(z)|<=C/(t|z|) and
|D^2 f(z)|<=C/(t|z|^2). The endpoint-flat cutoff prevents a first-derivative
jump at |z|=ell. In four dimensions the shell count is O(r^3), so the first
sum is bounded by C t^-2 sum_{r<=ell}r=O(ell^2/t^2), and the second by
C t^-2 sum_{r<=ell}r^-1=O(1/t). The O(1) innermost shells contribute
O(t^-2). This is the critical logarithmic capacity estimate; a piecewise
linear cutoff with a nonzero outer slope would not give the A_2 bound.

4. KINETIC FILTER COST WITHOUT STOQUASTICITY

On the labeled m-particle tensor space write h_i for h_L acting on
coordinate i, so K_m=128^-1 sum_i h_i^2. For any positive density rho
supported on the symmetric subspace and any 0<=F<=I,

  Tr rho F K F = 128^-1 sum_i ||h_i F rho^(1/2)||_2^2
  <= (1+eta) Tr rho K
     +(1+eta^-1)/(128) C_F,
  C_F:=sum_i Tr rho [h_i,F]^*[h_i,F].                 (4.1)

To prove (4.1), expand h_iF=Fh_i+[h_i,F], use
||u+v||^2<=(1+eta)||u||^2+(1+eta^-1)||v||^2, and use
h_i F^2 h_i<=h_i^2 as positive forms, since F^2<=I. Summing and tracing
does not require rho to commute with individual h_i. In particular this
avoids a false individual-coordinate commutation claim for the symmetrized
boson Gibbs block. No positivity of exp(-beta h_L^2/128) as a matrix is
used.

Here is the local commutator estimate needed in (4.1). Extend every
labeled wavefunction by zero outside the Dirichlet box and evaluate the
geometric F_m on ghost coordinates as well. For one coordinate and any
wavefunction psi, direct expansion of the graph Laplacian gives

  [h_i,F]psi = (h_i F)psi
       - J sum_e (D_e F)(D_e psi),                 (4.2)

where D_e is the forward difference in direction e and h_i F denotes the
ordinary discrete Laplacian of the multiplier. The identity includes ghost
neighbors: their psi value is zero, while the full-lattice product rule
keeps exactly the boundary terms.

The discrete product rule for F_m and Cauchy-Schwarz over at most m^2
terms imply pointwise

 |h_iF_m|^2 <= C J^2 m^2 [
   sum_{j!=i}|Delta_disc f(x_i-x_j)|^2
   +sum_{j<k, j,k!=i}sum_e
       |D_e f(x_i-x_j)|^2 |D_e f(x_i-x_k)|^2 ],

and |D_eF_m|^2 <= m sum_{j!=i}|D_ef(x_i-x_j)|^2. The second term in the
first bound is the exact cross term in the discrete Laplacian of a product.
The fixed exponential number tilt absorbs m^2 in the first estimate and m
in the second. The ordered-pair and ordered-triple sums are exactly the
normal-ordered Gamma_2 and Gamma_3 observables; the occupation-to-labeled
embedding has multiplicity m!/product_x n_x!, which cancels against the
weight of each labeled representative. Thus no factorial sector overhead
appears.

Using (4.2), |u+v|^2<=2|u|^2+2|v|^2, and Cauchy over the eight directions,
the three resulting free observables are bounded respectively by

  C J^2 L^-4 A_2,       (pair Laplacian term)
  C J^2 L^-8 A_1^2,     (three-particle cross-gradient term)
  C J^2 L^-6 A_1.       (derivative-pair term)

For the last term the exact observable is
sum_{x,y,e}|D_ef(x-y)|^2 b_{x,e}^*a_y^*a_y b_{x,e}; the derivative Wick
bound above applies. Consequently

  C_F <= C [L^-4/t + L^-8 ell^4/t^4 + L^-6 ell^2/t^2],
  beta C_F <= C/t,                                     (4.3)

for ell=floor(sqrt L). Equations (4.2)-(4.3) are the load-bearing
many-particle estimate; they should be checked against the exact chosen
Fock-to-labeled normalization before promotion.

5. RELATIVE ENTROPY AND POSITIVE PENALTY COMPARISON

Put p=Tr rho_0F^2=1-delta and sigma=F rho_0 F/p. The finite-rank state
sigma lies in the zero-form subspace of w. Its entropy loss obeys

  S(rho_0)-S(sigma) <= h_2(delta)+q_M log(S+1)+delta.    (5.1)

This is the two-outcome filter instrument bound: the success branch has
state sigma; the failure branch has mean particle number q_M/delta. The
number reference exp(-sM), s=log(S+1), has partition
(1-exp(-s))^-S with log at most one. The usual flagged entropy inequality
then gives (5.1), first with finite number cutoff and then by the free
exponential number tail.

From (4.1),

 beta[Tr sigma K-Tr rho_0 K]
   <= [beta(delta+eta)Tr rho_0 K
       + beta(1+eta^-1) C_F/128]/p.

Because lambda<=0 and F commutes with M,
 beta(-lambda/L^4)[Tr sigma M-Tr rho_0 M]
   <= tau B delta Tr(rho_0 M)/p.

The identity
D(sigma||rho_0)=S(rho_0)-S(sigma)+beta Tr(sigma-rho_0)H_0,
the bounds beta Tr rho_0 K<=C, Tr rho_0 M<=C, delta,q_M=O(L^-2),
and beta C_F=O(1/t) yield, with eta=t^-1/2,

  D(sigma||rho_0) <= C/sqrt(t) = C/sqrt(log L).          (5.2)

For the interacting state, Gibbs variational principle with trial sigma
gives log(Z_0/Z_w)<=D(sigma||rho_0), since sigma has zero w-energy.
Also
  D(rho_w||rho_0)=log(Z_0/Z_w)-beta Tr rho_w w
                 <=log(Z_0/Z_w).
The trace class and energy identities follow from H_0+w>=H_0>=0 and the
finite free half-temperature partition. Pinsker gives the stated full-Fock
trace-norm bound. The same trial-state comparison applies to any hard
compression whose restricted free form is densely defined and needs no
convergence of penalty Hamiltonians.

6. DEPENDENCIES / AUDIT GATES

This derivation supports the claimed d=4 critical logarithmic universality
if the stated discrete product-rule estimate, exact derivative-pair
normalization, and entropy cutoff passage are verified as written. The
mechanism is different from the quadratic d=2 filter only in its kinetic
commutator: h^2 is controlled through [h,F], not a positive quartic heat
kernel. The key rates are A_2=O(1/log ell), A_1=O(ell^2/log^2 ell),
collision loss O(ell^4/L^4), and ell=sqrt(L). The result is only an internal
candidate until those interfaces receive an adversarial proof audit.
