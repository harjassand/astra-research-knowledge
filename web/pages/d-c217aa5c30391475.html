## Three exact supporting lemmas

1. **Scalar descent, Lemma 2.1.** Let T be finite-dimensional over Q, let P be a complex-linear subspace of T_C, and take t in T tensor_Q Qbar. If every sigma in Gal(Qbar/Q) sends t into P, then t belongs to (T intersect P) tensor_Q Qbar. The same argument applies to a fixed Hodge-type subspace in a Hom space. The proof takes a finite Galois coefficient field K, forms Tr_(K/Q)(a t), and uses the trace-dual basis to recover each rational coefficient component inside T intersect P. Exact source: pinned TeX build/sections/02-tensors.tex, lines 34-61; PDF p. 5. Status: source proof read and extracted; no independent external validation.

2. **Transfer of an embedding line, Lemma 2.4.** Let H and H' be direct sums of rank-one weight-one Hodge modules over CM fields contained in a common Galois CM field E. For embedding lines L in H_Qbar and M in H'_Qbar with the same complete type function, extend any Qbar-linear map T:L -> M by zero on all other source embedding lines. Then T is in Hom_Hdg(H,H') tensor_Q Qbar. If H and H' are degree-one cohomologies of abelian varieties, this is an algebraic-coefficient linear combination of pullbacks of abelian morphisms, up to rational denominators. The proof checks each scalar conjugate and invokes Lemmas 2.1 and 2.2. Repeated rational summands are retained separately; stabilizer invariance is not required. Exact source: pinned TeX lines 259-297; PDF p. 8. Status: source proof read and extracted; no independent external validation.

3. **Effective realization of a simple CM Hodge structure, Lemma 8.1.** If W is a nonzero simple polarizable rational Hodge structure of CM type and weight k, put r0 = min{p : W^(p,k-p) is nonzero} and n = k - 2r0. Then n >= 0 and there is a CM abelian variety B, possibly a point, with a surjective rational Hodge map H^n(B,Q)(-r0) -> W. For n > 0, the source makes U = W(r0) a one-dimensional E-module for a CM field E, records its embedding types (a_sigma,n-a_sigma), chooses n CM types whose incidence counts are a_sigma, realizes each by a CM abelian variety, then uses multiplication E tensor_Q ... tensor_Q E -> E on the Kunneth summand. Exact source: pinned TeX build/sections/07-consequences.tex, lines 55-152; PDF pp. 47-48. The proof does not require E to be Galois. Status: source proof read and extracted; no independent external validation.
