EXACT DERIVATION

Let x_i=1[i in I]. Since F is the cyclic permutation matrix, the submatrix
F[I,I^c] has a nonzero determinant exactly when its nonzero columns are a
permutation of I^c. Its row i has its unique nonzero in column i+1, so this is
equivalent to I+1=I^c, or x_(i+1)=1-x_i for every i cyclically. For even n the
only solutions are I_even and I_odd. Each minor is a permutation matrix and
has determinant of absolute value one. Thus Omega={I_even,I_odd}, pi is uniform,
and the supports differ in all n coordinates, i.e. by n/2 pair exchanges.

Write s(I_even)=1 and s(I_odd)=-1. Then E_pi[s]=0 and Var_pi(s)=1. For a
radius-t proposal with t<n/2, neither supported state proposes the other.
Zero-weight proposals are rejected, so the one-replica kernel is identity on
Omega. On Omega^2, add the reversible layer swap (A,B)->(B,A). Every move
preserves s(A)+s(B), while the target product law is uniform on four states.
Consequently its exact Dirichlet energy is 0, although the first-coordinate
variance is 1.

For the global proposal, let M=binom(n,n/2) and a=1/M. A proposed state is
uniform over all M fixed-cardinality subsets; its acceptance probability is
min(1,w(J)/w(I)), so zero weights reject and the normalizer is not used. On the
two supported states the transition probability in either direction is a.
The one-replica eigenvalues are 1 and 1-2a, giving gap 2a.

For the pair kernel P=(Q tensor Id + Id tensor Q + Swap)/3 and any centered
additive observable F(A,B)=x s(A)+y s(B), direct summation over the four
product states gives

  E_P(F) = (2a/3)(x^2+y^2) + (1/3)(x-y)^2.

For fixed x, the unique minimizer is y=x/(1+2a), and the minimum energy is
4a(1+a)x^2/[3(1+2a)]. Since Var_pi(x s)=x^2, the exact optimal inequality
constant is

  L = 3(1+2a)/(4a(1+a)) = 3 M (M+2)/(4(M+1)).

For exhaustive heat-bath updates, choose a coordinate with probability 1/2
and replace it by an exact independent pi sample. Its Dirichlet energy on F is
(Var_pi f1+Var_pi f2)/2, proving L=2 and showing equality when f2 is constant.
This uses Z=sum_I w(I). Enumerating and storing all M integer weights computes
Z exactly; it is not an f(U) oracle hidden inside a transition.
