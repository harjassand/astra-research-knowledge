OUTCOME

The universal EB Dirichlet / arbitrary-frame variance theorem remains OPEN. I proved two exact pieces that narrow the gate:

1. For every positive selfadjoint tracial selfcompatible channel Phi with an actual common two-output UCP broadcaster, Hermitian contractions A,C satisfy

  ||[A,C]||_2 <= (2+sqrt(2))(sqrt(e_Phi(A))+sqrt(e_Phi(C))).

Thus an arbitrary weighted frame has pairwise squared commutator average at most 4(2+sqrt(2))^2 times its average Dirichlet energy. Full proof and initial independent provenance are in immutable INITIAL.txt.

2. A direct random-shift argument converts the average commutator energy of one pivot against an arbitrarily large weighted family into one common *block-algebra* approximation. For A and (Y_x,p_x), q_A=E_x||[A,Y_x]||_2^2, a single spectral interval partition exists with block-scalar A_0 and block pinching E such that

  [A_0,E(Y_x)]=0 for every x,
  ||A-A_0||_2^2 + E_x||Y_x-E(Y_x)||_2^2 <= delta^2+sqrt(q_A)/delta.

Choosing delta=q_A^(1/6) gives 2q_A^(1/3) for q_A<=1. Selecting a pivot H_z from the frame with q_(H_z)<=E_(x,z)||[H_x,H_z]||_2^2 and applying the first claim yields a block-algebra family loss at most

  2(4(2+sqrt(2))^2)^(1/3) theta^(4/3) ebar^(1/3),

when ||H_x||_op<=theta and alpha=4(2+sqrt(2))^2 ebar/theta^2<=1, with ebar=E_x e_Phi(H_x). There is no dimension or label-count factor. Full proof is in revisions/REVISION-01.txt.

The crucial gap is precise: the common block algebra can contain full matrix blocks. Its pinching is UCP and trace preserving but is not EB unless all blocks are one-dimensional. Turning this first-step block approximation into one MASA/one common EB measurement with error controlled independently of dimension and frame size remains unresolved. This is not an EB theorem, a bounded-Holevo theorem, or a field-breaking claim.

WHY THE BLOCK/EB GAP IS REAL

For even d, take every balanced diagonal sign observable H_h=diag(h), uniformly weighted. All commute, so pairwise commutator energy is exactly zero. Fix any balanced pivot z and use its two spectral blocks B_+,B_- of size d/2. The block pinching preserves every H_h. But the EB channel that measures only B_+/B_- and prepares the normalized identity in the observed block has exact mean variance loss 1-1/(d-1), tending to one. More generally, any fixed partition into m blocks followed by block-center preparation captures exactly (m-1)/(d-1) on this family, so a fixed K binary-pivot center recursion with m<=2^K still loses nearly all average variance as d grows. This is not a counterexample to common EB rounding: measurement in the computational basis fixes the entire frame. It shows that one pivot or block-center preparation cannot resolve the residual algebra. The exact rational enumerations for d=4,6,8,10 are in one_pivot_partition_check.json.

The next mathematical gate is a uniform refinement of residual matrix blocks to a MASA, or an independent actual-compatible-channel counterexample. A fixed-number center-only pivot recursion is insufficient on the balanced-sign example by the exact capture formula above. Existing almost-commuting tuple theorems do not remove this issue: Bansil–Kachkovskiy Theorem 1.3 has a sqrt(N) factor for a common pivot and their Corollary 1.4 allows constants depending on N. My one-pivot estimate is a self-contained weighted statement, not a replacement for their all-tuple theorem.
