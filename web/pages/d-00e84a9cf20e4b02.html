1. EXACT OA113 SOURCE THEOREM

Primary source: OpenAI, “A Fully Polynomial Randomized Approximation Scheme for Perfect Matchings in General Graphs,” dated 23 September 2026, pinned release commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a. The relevant source is §“A two-coordinate energy inequality,” Theorem “Pair energy inequality,” in the build/main.tex file:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Fully-Polynomial-Randomized-Approximation-Scheme-for-Perfect-Matchings-in-General-Graphs-September-23-2026/build/main.tex
Pinned HTML: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Fully-Polynomial-Randomized-Approximation-Scheme-for-Perfect-Matchings-in-General-Graphs-September-23-2026/build/main.tex

Representation and law. A tree-bag graph has a finite rooted tree of labels and an even finite vertex set. A label at level j has height 2^j. Each vertex belongs to one label or two adjacent labels. For every pair of distinct vertices and each label they share, there is exactly one edge of that label's color; there are no other edges. For integer D≥1, each reference edge activity W_xy,l is positive rational and satisfies h_l/D ≤ W_xy,l ≤ 3h_l. The theorem also holds after clamping all activities to any one common interval [a,b] contained in (0,∞). Require N≥2 vertices and at least one perfect matching. Let M be all colored perfect matchings, wt(M)=product of its edge activities, and π(M)=wt(M)/Z. No balance condition is assumed in this energy theorem.

For state (A,B)∈M², select one of three equally likely proposal classes: (i) choose two distinct edges of A and one alternative pairing of their four endpoints, assigning each proposed edge a color index among the at most two common labels; an absent index holds; (ii) select an edge of A and a color index, holding if absent; (iii) choose a discrepancy alternating cycle of A,B uniformly and exchange its two layers, holding if there is none. Metropolis acceptance uses π⊗π. The proposal is symmetric; a cycle swap preserves the product edge weight and is accepted with probability one. With E the stationary Dirichlet energy of this pair kernel and F(A,B)=f1(A)+f2(B), for every pair of real-valued functions f1,f2 on M,

    Var_π(f1) ≤ L E(F),       L=(10^4 N D)^100.

The same L works for every common-interval clamp. The theorem quantifies variance of the first coordinate function for arbitrary additive probes. It does not bound Var_{π⊗π}(g) by E(g) for arbitrary g on M².

2. WHAT THE PROOF ACTUALLY USES

Set A0=1000D and Tcal=(10N)^30. The proof builds cells in an alternating cycle by quadrangulating it in a way adapted to the label tree. The four side chords, cycle-boundary edges and leaf repairs in any one cell have labels within tree distance 6. Since adjacent-label heights differ by 2, their activities are within 3D·2^6=192D≤A0, and a common clamp preserves this ratio. Thus each cell supplies safe local switches, including leaf repairs.

Elementary stationary-energy comparisons bound the aggregate squared increments of safe two-edge switches by 60N²A0²E, color changes by 12NA0E, and discrepancy-cycle swaps by 6NE. For a long discrepancy cycle, the quadrangulation gives an exact signed identity: its whole-cycle f1 difference is the sum of local switch gains and context/error terms. The long-arc conversions appearing in that identity need not be legal single transitions. The switch and error demands are encoded by pairs of matchings; their preimage multiplicity is at most Tcal and their changed-edge weight distortion is charged by powers of A0. Context terms are controlled by actual cycle swaps. To sum demands, the proof toggles all earlier discrepancy cycles in both independent demand matchings; this is an involution preserving their product weight, union and selected cycle.

The resulting display is Var_π(f1) ≤ (12N²A0+324N⁴A0^6 Tcal)E ≤ 1000N⁴A0^6Tcal E = 10^51 N^34 D^6 E ≤ L E. These comparisons use only the cell-comparability and adjacent-label color-change bounds, so survive common clamping.

This is sufficient structure for the published proof, not a theorem that the literal tree-bag axioms are logically minimal among all possible representations. The load-bearing proof obligations are: (a) enough legal label-adapted chords/repairs to produce the signed quadrangulation identity, (b) polynomial activity distortion for every local repair and color change after clamping, (c) polynomially bounded multiplicity and weight distortion for demand encodings, and (d) a product-measure-preserving involution or equivalent charge that moves context errors to legal pair-kernel energy. A different representation could replace tree bags if it independently supplies these obligations. No such broader determinant representation is established here.

The manuscript separately lifts the additive inequality. Adjacent clamped tier laws have pointwise density ratio in [1/2,2]; the pair bound becomes 2L across adjacent tiers. It uses q=32L replicas per tier, T=O(N²(K+log D+1)) tiers, and M=Tq²+q reversible updates, including exact refreshes of q base-tier slots from the unit-activity tree DP. Ordered ANOVA components make each pair residual's last two coordinates unique, so their residual squared norms sum to at most total variance. Absorption yields product-chain gap at least 3/(8M), hence at least 1/(20LM). This second theorem—not the pair-energy theorem alone—supports sampling. The constants are intentionally very large: L has numerical-input degree 100 and q is proportional to L. The source reports polynomial bit arithmetic and charges finite-bit simulation error in its full algorithm; this snapshot has not independently validated that complete FPRAS or its external priority.

3. PRIMARY DETERMINANT/VOLUME COMPARISON AND EXACT OBSTRUCTION

The target recorded in NEW_FRONTIER is

    c_k(F)=Σ_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k} |det F[I,J]|²
          =Σ_{|I|=k} det((V*V)[P(I)]),

where V=[I,F^T] and P(I) chooses both columns (e_i,f_i) for each i∈I. Thus it is a principal-minor/volume weight on the special block-complete 2k-column sets P(I), not the unrestricted fixed-cardinality DPP sample space. A representation as a restricted DPP weight does not itself preserve strong Rayleigh properties under the pair-completeness restriction.

Primary prior art checked:
• Anari, Oveis Gharan and Rezaei (2016), “Monte Carlo Markov Chain Algorithms for Sampling Strongly Rayleigh Distributions and Determinantal Point Processes,” PMLR 49:103–115, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v49/anari16.pdf. For every k-homogeneous strongly-Rayleigh law, its lazy single-state exchange chain has Poincare constant at least C_μ≥1/(2kn); this includes k-DPP / k-volume sampling. The paper's chain exchanges one selected and one unselected element, and its support is a matroid's bases. It supplies a genuine determinant-volume single-replica analogue, not the pair-additive theorem above.
• Anari et al. (2020), “Log-Concave Polynomials IV: Approximate Exchange, Tight Mixing Times, and Near-Optimal Sampling of Forests,” https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07220. The abstract gives O(k log k) down-up mixing for bases of matroids and distributions associated with homogeneous log-concave polynomials, including determinantal distributions. Again this is a one-state exchange-chain result, not an additive two-replica comparison.

Exact small counterexample (script and JSON below). Let F be the directed-cycle permutation matrix on 4 indices, F[i,i+1 mod 4]=1. For a 2×2 minor to be nonzero, the column set must equal the successor image of its row set. Disjointness then permits exactly I={0,2} with J={1,3}, or I={1,3} with J={0,2}; both squared minors are 1. Hence the support of the pair-indexed c_2 law is exactly {{0,2},{1,3}}. These two 2-sets cannot be bases of a rank-2 matroid: for A={0,2}, B={1,3}, replacing either a∈A by either b∈B gives {0,3}, {0,1}, {2,3}, or {1,2}, all outside support, violating basis exchange. Therefore this target does not generally meet the matroid-support hypothesis needed for the strongly-Rayleigh single-exchange theorem. The exact check is executable:
