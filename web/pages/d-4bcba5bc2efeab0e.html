## c10_s01 — two-layer catalytic reciprocal Foster certificate

Frozen source: c10_s01, `d079448f084850567851bd59f0ff7a141822549f2edfd685d135aa5ff2674adf`.

The advertised interface is exactly the two-layer architecture: independent
root immigration/death, each child born/dead only with root parents, no child
as catalyst or extra reaction. I manually reconstructed the decisive boundary
identity. For a child with parent-count sum S and
`g(S)=1+c/(S+1)`, root immigration contributes
`-c B/((S+1)(S+2))`; root death contributes
`1_{S>=1} c sum(delta_i r_i)/(S(S+1))`. At S=0 there are no parent deaths,
and the remaining term is exactly `-cB/2`. For S>=1, bounding the positive
term by `cD/(S+1)` and using `cD<=beta` gives
`L_root g - a_child g <= beta/(S+1)-beta S <= -beta/2`.
The weight choices then cancel each root's child-birth coefficient and yield
the reported all-state inequality `LV<=C-lambda V`. The zero-parent face is
handled correctly; that is the load-bearing part of this construction.

Independent exact diagnostic: I reconstructed the report's non-complex-balanced
fixture `0<->A`, `0<->C`, `A<->A+B` (rates 1), `C<->B+C` (birth 2, death 1),
with `V=3A+5C+(1+1/(A+C+1))B`, `C=8`, `lambda=1/5`. My Fraction-only
script checked `LV<=8-V/5` on 729 states in `[0,8]^3` and the exact `LV=8-B`
formula on all 9 zero-parent states. Output:
`outputs/round6/c10_l02/peer_exact_checks.txt`.

Audit status: the algebraic drift, linear nonexplosion estimate and
irreducibility argument appear valid within that explicit syntax. The
recovery/hitting-time derivation is coherent but not machine-proved. I did not
independently reconstruct the total bit-height bound or reread the
environment-modulated monomolecular paper's exact theorem hypotheses. The
source itself flags its prior-art overlap with Cappelletti–Pal Majumder–Wiuf,
arXiv:1912.00401; this is a close environment-modulated prior-art route, not
proof that their theorem covers the exact candidate. No generic weakly
reversible certificate follows.

## c10_s02 — recurrent counterexample to direct N76/concave transfer

Frozen source: c10_s02, `14d1817522a7de31dba58a8a3ec318c1a6726260cdb0ab9295311876690f3989`.

The exact network is the unit-rate reversible deficiency-zero network
`A+C <-> A+B <-> B`, `C <-> 0`. I independently reconstructed
`exp(F)=a!b!c!/max(1,b,c,ab,ac)` from the five complexes. At `(a,0,1)`, the
three enabled moves have rates `a,1,1` and exact F ratios `1,a,1`; hence
`LF=log(a)>0`, `L exp(theta F)/exp(theta F)=a^theta-1>0`, and every strictly
increasing scalar `h(F)` has positive drift because only the C-death move
changes F upward. At `(0,n,0)`, the two moves preserve F. These identities
are not a recurrence counterexample.

The product-Poisson detailed-balance weights are proportional to
`1/(a!b!c!)`. The components are `Gamma_0={(0,0,c)}` and
`Gamma={(a,b,c):a+b>=1}`. The drift of total count is
`LN=b-ab+1-c<=N+1`, sufficient for nonexplosion; the source's explicit
communication paths make Gamma irreducible. Thus conditioning the product
Poisson law on `a+b>=1` gives a genuine stationary law on Gamma. I checked
480 exact reversible edge identities in a bounded box and component
predicate preservation on 729 states, in addition to the factorial-ratio
checks described above. The formulas and irreducibility proof, not those
finite fixtures, carry the infinite claims.

I also checked the concavity no-go: on the convex domain
`D={(a,b,c)>=0:a+b>=1}`, coercivity along the two coordinate rays out of
`(0,n,0)` forces every first unit increment in the A and C directions to be
strictly positive for a concave W. The only enabled jumps there are exactly
those directions, so `LW(0,n,0)>0`. This excludes strict or geometric Foster
drift for all globally coercive concave candidates, but leaves nonconcave or
path-corrected functions open. The c10_l02 two-jump ray calculation is in
`FACTORIAL_SKELETON.txt` and is an independent continuation, not a repair to
the frozen Sol counterexample.

Prior-art boundary: Anderson–Cappelletti–Fan–Kim, arXiv:2309.06970, Example
2.3 already studies a closely neighboring two-species catalyst chain and
proves exponential ergodicity by a path/spectral method when ordinary Foster
and canonical paths are inadequate. That example is not this three-species
network or its F identity. Xu, arXiv:2409.05340v2, Theorem 4.13 gives the
existence-of-a-stationary-distribution criterion for second-order weakly
reversible systems, while leaving that classwise existence question as the
gate. Exact novelty is UNKNOWN.

## c10_s03 — admitted rational affine/quadratic certificate and recovery

Frozen source: c10_s03, `30a72c0206ef4ef5162a39dfbaab61fcfef794733daadad9fa62b9f5b481e3f6`.

The claimed scope is a strict sub-class: weak reversibility, a unary drain
path for every species, support producibility from zero, and exact source-wise
rate-box Foster inequalities for an acquired `w>=1`. I independently
checked the displayed one-step inequality algebra. For S coordinates,
the linear and pure-diagonal quadratic terms are bounded by
`-x_i + g_i^2/4` by completing the square; T-coordinate linear sources are
at most `-x_i`; mixed quadratics and other T diagonals are nonpositive; zero
sources contribute at most `b0`. Hence `LW<=B-N<=B-W/wmax` does hold,
including zero/one counts. The unary-drain and support-production path
arguments are logically aligned with their stated hypotheses. The detailed-
balanced rejected fixture is correct: its two first-source inequalities
would require both `w_B-w_A<=-1` and `w_A-w_B<=-1`, although it has the
positive product-Poisson invariant law.

Status: promising candidate compiler and path recovery proof for the admitted
class, not a general theorem. I did not independently execute its compiler,
rederive the full `2^{O(L^3)}` total bit/runtime bound, or replay the 4,044
source-reported checks; these remain verification gaps. Established prior art
already includes linear Foster/LFP conditions, quadratic drift tests, support
exhaustion/reachability arguments, and robust-rate criteria (including Gupta,
Briat and Khammash's published work and Gupta–Khammash arXiv:1312.2879). The
bit-costed exact packaging may be incremental, but priority is UNKNOWN. Its
own detailed-balanced rejection isolates the missing multistep boundary
mechanism; rejection by this certificate is not instability.

## Combined consequence for the assignment

The frozen c10 Sol results span complementary mechanisms rather than converge
on a general transfer: s01 has an explicit nonconcave rational correction for
a two-layer architecture; s02 proves N76 F, every increasing scalar transform
of F, and globally coercive concave Foster functions fail on a recurrent
fixture; s03 gives a rational one-step affine/quadratic compiler for a strict
admitted class and rejects another recurrent complex-balanced fixture. The
common open gate is a computable, state-only or skeleton-level correction for
fast tangent cycles and boundary recovery under the full bimolecular
weakly-reversible interface. My two-step formulas close two explicit
offending rays in s02, but they do not close the other states or acquire a
generic compiler.
