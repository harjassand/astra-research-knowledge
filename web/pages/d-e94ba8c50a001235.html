## 1. Completion of squares preserves the hidden interface

Put a=1+q eta, b=eta/a, z=y/a, and v=theta/a^2. Completion gives exactly

R^V_{eta,y}=R^U_{b,z},  prox_{eta V}(c)=prox_{b U}(c/a).

Integration by parts under this RGO gives E X=z-b E grad U(X). Consequently

g^V_eta(y)=q y/a + g^U_b(z)/a,  where g^U_b(z)=E_{R^U_{b,z}} grad U(X).

The observable D=C/a has law N(z,v I), with v<=b. The lawful proposal estimator is

psi(C,G)=q C/a + grad U(P(D)+sqrt(b)G)/a,  P=prox_{b U}.

Its expectation has known affine part qy/a. The residual mean to correct is exactly

delta=(g^U_b(z)-E grad U(P(D)+sqrt(b)G))/a.                       (M1)

Neither z nor y is evaluated. The change from sqrt(eta) to sqrt(b) in the proposal is intentional and must propagate through the fluctuation and endpoint operators. The q C/a fluctuation remains present; removing the q mean burden does not remove the q derivative energy. For stacked nodes all clouds must remain independent conditional on the fixed hidden Picard centers. In particular D for the mean estimator and the cloud used to acquire X must be independent, and both independent of Gadget 3 proposal/reference draws.

## 2. Three trace moment lemmas, uniform in inaccessible z

We use the standard concentration consequence of a log-Sobolev constant s: an L-Lipschitz scalar function has centered L^p norm at most C L sqrt(s p). An RGO for convex U has s<=b by its Hessian lower bound 1/b; a Gaussian with variance v has s=v. This consequence follows from the entropy/Herbst argument. These uses are standard prerequisites, not a new theorem. For p>=2 a nonnegative L-Lipschitz norm F therefore satisfies ||F||_p<=sqrt(E F^2)+C L sqrt(s p).

Let p_z=P(z), R(x)=grad U(x)-grad U(p_z). Gradient co-coercivity gives R(x).(x-p_z)>=||R(x)||^2/h. The RGO score is (x-p_z)/b+R(x). Integration by parts, justified by linear growth and Gaussian tails, gives

E[R(X).((X-p_z)/b+R(X))]=E tr H_U(X)<=tau h.

Hence E||R(X)||^2<=b tau h^2/(1+bh). Norm R is h-Lipschitz, so

||grad U(X)-grad U(p_z)||_p <= C h sqrt(b)(sqrt(tau)+sqrt(p)).     (L1)

For f=grad U o P, Df=H_U(P)(I+bH_U(P))^-1 is symmetric positive semidefinite, with op norm <=h/(1+bh) and trace <=tau h. Co-coercivity of the gradient f, then Gaussian integration by parts, gives

E||f(D)-f(z)||^2 <= [h/(1+bh)] v E tr Df(D)
                  <=v tau h^2/(1+bh).

Gaussian concentration of its norm gives

||grad U(P(D))-grad U(p_z)||_p <= C h sqrt(v)(sqrt(tau)+sqrt(p)). (L2)

For each fixed p_0, co-coercivity and Gaussian integration by parts similarly give

E_G||grad U(p_0+sqrt(b)G)-grad U(p_0)||^2<=b tau h^2,

and the conditional p-th norm is <=C h sqrt(b)(sqrt(tau)+sqrt(p)), uniformly in p_0. Integrating over D yields the same bound.                       (L3)

If h=0, U is affine and all three differences vanish exactly. The formulas are understood by this limit. Large affine parts of U cause no dimension factor; analysis subtracts grad U(p_z) although the algorithm never evaluates it.

## 3. Actual replacement estimator and moments under both likelihood laws

Draw fresh independent D=C_mean/a, X~R^U_{b,z}, and G standard Gaussian. Set

Delta_hat=[grad U(X)-grad U(P(D)+sqrt(b)G)]/a.                   (M2)

It is unbiased for (M1). Adding and subtracting grad U(p_z), grad U(P(D)), applying (L1)-(L3), and using v<=b proves

||Delta_hat||_p <= b_p := C h sqrt(b)(sqrt(tau)+sqrt(p))/a.      (M3)

This is uniform in z,d,theta, and the affine part of U. It is NOT the source scale b^(3/2)+v/sqrt(b); it pays sqrt(b). Thus the source parameter/cost balance cannot be reused unchanged.

To correct a stacked mean, let Delta=sum_j A_j Delta_hat_j, with deterministic matrices A_j conditional on phase inputs. Minkowski gives b_p,total<=sum_j ||A_j||op b_p,j, without assuming unavailable cross-node independence. The likelihood construction uses independent Y,Y'~Q=N(mu,I) and W=<Delta,Y-Y'>. Under Q, the difference is N(0,2I). Under the true mean tilt Q_w=N(mu+d0,I), d0=E Delta, it is N(d0,2I). Delta remains independent under either law. Conditioning on Delta and using ||d0||<=||Delta||_p gives

||W||_{L^p(Q)}+||W||_{L^p(Q_w)} <= C[sqrt(p)b_p,total+b_p,total^2]. (M4)

Here w(Y)=<d0,Y-mu> and its normalizer exp(||d0||^2/2)>=1. Source clipping Lemma 4.1 gives TV <=C B[(C(sqrt(p)b_p,total+b_p,total^2))/B]^p for 0<B<=1. Choose p>=C log(1/delta), and the source smallness condition sqrt(p)b_p,total+b_p,total^2<=cB. This is an actual likelihood moment bound, not a static covariance substitute. For bounded bias epsilon in the mean vector, its likelihood bias under Q and Q_w is at most C epsilon(1+||d0||), using a one-dimensional Gaussian projection. This avoids a spurious sqrt(d) from bounding ||Y-mu||.

## 4. Local work and precision, including failed attempts

One exact (M2) replica costs one fresh mean cloud; one independent hidden RGO draw; one prox_{b U}; two residual-gradient values; one d-dimensional Gaussian; O(d) vector arithmetic. The cloud/RGO draws are not free. Source FORS with clipping B uses mean Poisson count 2B and success probability >=exp(-2B). Therefore all attempts combined cost <=exp(2B) proposals and <=2B exp(2B) replicas/reference-Q draws in expectation. Multiply the preceding local costs by the latter factor; charge hidden RGO invocation costs including its own rejections separately.

For a gradient implementation of P(D), minimize F_D(x)=||x-D||^2/2+bU(x) with step 1/(1+bh), starting x_0=D. The map x -> x-[x-D+b grad U(x)]/(1+bh) contracts by rho=bh/(1+bh). Since ||D-P(D)||<=b||grad U(D)||, a supplied/checkable bound Gamma on ||grad U(D)|| gives a FIXED count

M=max(0,ceil(log(b Gamma/epsilon_P)/log((1+bh)/(bh))))

when the numerator is positive. Each iteration is one grad V and O(d) work; h=0 is exact in one residual-gradient query. The implemented estimator's uniform mean and p-norm change is <=h epsilon_P/a. If both gradient values have deterministic uniform Euclidean error epsilon_g, add 2epsilon_g/a; errors used inside the proximal loop must be included in its epsilon_P guarantee (iteration error <=b epsilon_g/(1-rho), or use the exact oracle). For a desired vector bias epsilon set epsilon_P<=a epsilon/(2h) and epsilon_g<=a epsilon/4, with h=0 handled separately. On the source prescribed radius/gradient caps this is O(log(1/epsilon)+log(Gamma)+log(1+b)) gradients for bounded bh. No Hessian/secant/smoothing precision is needed.

Only a TV-accurate RGO is actually available. Since (M2) is unbounded, TV alone does NOT transfer its mean or moments. Use radial clipping of the estimator at computable radius R_Delta. If exact ||Delta_hat||_s<=b_s (s>1), clipping mean error <=b_s^s/R_Delta^(s-1). If RGO (or independent cloud) approximation has TV epsilon_R, the clipped vector changes in mean by <=2R_Delta epsilon_R and its coupled L^p difference by <=2R_Delta epsilon_R^(1/p). Thus choose these explicitly; p logarithmic in final accuracy may require log(1/epsilon_R)=O(p log(R_Delta/epsilon_moment)), possibly a squared logarithm. No uncharged moment-transport promise is used. An optional stop before queries outside prescribed input/Gaussian caps adds its probability and the exact tail mean to the budget; test caps BEFORE an out-of-region oracle query. Count the rejected/stopped work. The parent's actual hidden RGO and adaptive-tree error allocation must meet these costs.

## 5. Exact first failed trace substitution in unchanged source estimator

The residual application of Proposition 5.3 is lawful with (eta,theta,V,C) replaced by (b,v,U,D), then multiplied by 1/a. The first source term admits (L1)-(L2) and hence norm <=C h^2 b^(3/2)(sqrt(tau)+sqrt(p))/a. The divergence term is

(v/(a sqrt(b))) Z_time H_U(W_T) B_D G,
W_t=e^-t X+(1-e^-t)P(D)+sqrt(b(1-e^-2t))G,
B_D=(I+bH_U(P(D)))^-1,
T density k(t)/Z_time, k(t)=e^-t(1-e^-t)/sqrt(1-e^-2t),
Z_time=pi/2-1.

The tempting identity E||H_U(W_T)B_D G||^2=E tr[B_D H_U(W_T)^2 B_D] does NOT hold: G determines W_T, and the matrix depends on G. Even a possible L2 estimate would not give the high Lp bound required in (M4). The following admissible family disproves a uniform tau-only high-p bound for this unchanged random estimator.

Fix q=1/2, h=1/4, eta=1, theta=1/8, y=0, hence a=3/2,b=2/3,v=1/18. This also satisfies the source hidden-RGO condition v<=(b-2v)/4. Set t0=log2, v0=b+v(1-e^-t0)^2=49/72, R=sqrt(v0 d). Let psi(s)=0 for s<=0, psi(s)=3s^2-2s^3 for s in [0,1], psi(s)=1 for s>=1, and c=min(1/3,v0/2)=1/3. Define U_d radial by

u_d'(r)=(c h/R) psi(R(r-R)),  u_d(0)=0, U_d(x)=u_d(||x||).

This is C^2 and convex, zero inside radius R. Its Hessian eigenvalues are lambda_rad=c h psi'(R(r-R)) and lambda_tan=u_d'(r)/r. Since psi'<=3/2, lambda_rad<=h/2; for r>=R, (d-1)lambda_tan<=c h(d-1)/R^2<h/2. Thus op H<=h and tr H<=h (tau=1) everywhere. The full V is uniformly strongly convex with Hessian between q I and (q+h)I<=I.

The exact RGO X has law N(0,bI) tilted by exp(-U_d). Since U_d is zero on ||x||<=R and nonnegative, its TV distance from that Gaussian is <=P(||sqrt(b)G||>R), exponentially small in d. Likewise P(D)=D and B_D=I except on an exponentially small event. Compare to independent Gaussian X_0~N(0,bI), D~N(0,vI). Then W_t is centered Gaussian with variance v_t=b+v(1-e^-t)^2, and conditional on W_t=w,

G~N((sigma_t/v_t)w,(1-sigma_t^2/v_t)I), sigma_t=sqrt(b(1-e^-2t)).

Take |T-t0|<=c1/sqrt(d) and R+1/(4R)<=||W_T||<=R+3/(4R). The T event has probability Theta(d^-1/2). The chi radial density at R in this time window is bounded above/below by positive constants, so the annulus has conditional probability Theta(d^-1/2). Here lambda_rad>=c2 h. The radial projection G.W_T/||W_T|| has mean (sigma_T/v_T)||W_T||=Theta(sqrt(d)) and variance <=1. It is >=c3 sqrt(d) with probability tending to one. The exceptional X/D tails cost exp(-c4 d), negligible against Theta(d^-1). Therefore, for the actual residual source estimator,

||Z_time H_U(W_T)B_D G||_p >=c5 h d^(1/2-1/p),  every fixed p>2. (O1)

The weighted factor v/(a sqrt(b)) is a positive constant. The source first term is uniformly bounded in Lp by the bound above, so reverse triangle inequality gives the same divergence for their sum. Radial symmetry makes the true missing mean zero, so Q_w=Q: this failure occurs under the very likelihood law needed by Gadget 3. It is a failure of the proposed trace strengthening, NOT a contradiction of the author's valid sqrt(d) bound.

The gradient secant estimator in B.4 converges to this exact estimator for each fixed d as its gradient/prox smoothing and secant scales vanish and proximal tolerance tends to zero. For this C^2 family, bounded Hessians and Gaussian moments provide domination. Fatou therefore prohibits a uniformly dimension-free high-p bound for all sufficiently precise B.4 approximations. A redesigned averaging/coupling estimator could still work; (O1) does not rule out all mean algorithms. Direct (M2) is such a replacement, at weaker scale.
