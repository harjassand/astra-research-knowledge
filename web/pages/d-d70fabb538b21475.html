Title:Stable ample vector bundles without semipositive Kobayashi curvature

Authors:Kefeng Liu, Xueyuan Wan
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Stable ample vector bundles without semipositive Kobayashi curvature, by Kefeng Liu and 1 other authors

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:We construct slope-stable ample rank-two vector bundles on every Hirzebruch surface $\mathbb{F}_e$ that admit no smooth strongly pseudoconvex Finsler metric with semipositive Kobayashi curvature. These examples give a negative answer to Kobayashi's problem. The same bundles give counterexamples to the smooth strongly pseudoconvex Finsler formulation of Fulton's problem and to the Hartshorne--Schneider conjecture on the convexity of complements. For $e=1$, these counterexamples have a simply connected Fano base that is not a product.
