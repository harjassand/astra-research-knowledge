## A. Infinite-rank PDE bridge: audit passes under named source facts

Read primary arXiv1808.04737v3 (3January2019), Harrach, §§2.1–2.2 Definitions2.1–2.2 and Lemmas2.5–2.8; own metadata/snapshot recorded. Verified real normalized partial NtD operator derivative identity and monotonicity signs. Harrach–Ullrich2013 preprint uploaded as arXiv1812.05300v1 (13December2018) defines the outer support in Definition2.3(c,d) by relatively open boundary-connected regions where the characteristic function vanishes. §4.3 explicitly carries arguments to arbitrarily small open boundary parts. The rendered hull definition in Harrach2019 omits the avoidance clause; original2013 definition restores it. Do not interpret the broken rendered equation literally.

Detailed reconstruction: a nonzero piecewise analytic contrast κ has a boundary-accessible sign patch by Harrach Lemma2.8: κ≥0 on Ω\D₂ and κ≥c>0 on open D₁, with D₁\D₂ nonempty and accessible. Select ball B₀ whose closure lies in that accessible patch; N+1 disjoint closed balls inside it. In dimension n≥2, removing finitely many such interior balls preserves boundary access: choose pairwise disjoint annuli about them, contained in B₀, and detour any path's sections through obstacles along connected spherical annuli. Thus each target ball remains reachable avoiding D₂ and all other balls. Apply Lemma2.7 per ball and normalize its target gradient L² norm to1; its gradient norm in D₂ and other balls tends to0. Solve N current constraints using N+1 currents and coefficient vector of Euclidean norm1. The gradient blocks on the union of balls equal an isometry plus an operator of norm≤√(N+1)ε, while D₂ gradient norm≤√(N+1)ε. Therefore contrast energy≥c(1−√(N+1)ε)²−||κ||∞(N+1)ε²>0 for smallε. The negative patch gives the reversed strict sign. Currents can have arbitrarily large norms; none are bounded or used experimentally in the one-shot result.

The conclusion holds in every finite-codimension current subspace. Taking the kernel of a hypothetical finite-rank self-adjoint derivative contradicts its zero quadratic form there. For T=Λ(σ₁)−Λ(σ₂), κ=σ₂−σ₁, use lower bound integralκ|∇u_σ₂|² when κ has positive patch, and upper bound integralκ|∇u_σ₁|² when negative. Both source directions check. This gives infinite rank of all secants and nonzero directional derivatives.

Load-bearing scope: all σ and contrasts/directions belong to a fixed vector space consisting entirely of Harrach-piecewise-analytic functions; sum/difference closure is not automatic for arbitrary unrelated partitions (source explicitly notes this). Smooth bounded connected Ω,n≥2 and nonempty relatively open access Σ remain assumptions. Choosing potentials normalized to zero mean on Σ yields H=L²_zero-mean(Σ) self-adjoint operator; all energy identities ignore additive constants, so this consistent normalization avoids source notation ambiguities. Analyticity of Λ in operator norm follows coercive weak inverse Neumann series on the positive coefficient cone. No rank theorem for arbitrary oscillatory L∞ contrasts, anisotropic tensors, or moving interfaces is proved.

## B. Uniform Gaussian vector-input argument: audit passes

For C¹ finite-dimensional parameter family F:M→L(H,Y), uniformly rank≥r differences and k independent Gaussian inputs with trace-class injective covariance, kr>2d suffices for global injection. At each off-diagonal pair choose finite covariance-eigenvector head and r output functionals with a nonzero matrix minor. It remains nonzero in a pair neighbourhood. Condition on arbitrary independent tails. The finite head collision equations form a C¹ submersion in head coordinates; their zero manifold has dimension2d+kn−kr. Its sampling projection is null ifkr>2d. Countable pair cover and measurable/Suslin projections justify union/Fubini. Tangent bundle dimension2d−1 and C²F supplies the analogous immersion. Infinite ranks allow r arbitrarily large, hence k=1. Compact injective immersion yields qualitative inverse Lipschitz via normalized secants. This is a general-position proof; external novelty remains unresolved.

## C. Stronger quantitative theorem: all inverse moments for compact infinite-rank secants

This is an independently verified deduction, not an external source claim.

Let H,Y be real separable Hilbert spaces, f∼N(0,C) in H, with C positive trace-class injective and τ=trC>0. Let T⊂L(H,Y) be a compact operator-norm family such that every T∈T has infinite rank. Suppose actual uniform operator-norm covering numbers satisfy N(T,ε)<=A ε^(−D) for0<ε<=ε₀, with centers in T (A,D,ε₀ supplied constants). Defineκ(f)=min_{T∈T}||Tf||_Y. Then E[κ^(−p)]<∞ for every finite p>0. Thusκ>0 almost surely, and the lower-Lipschitz inverse condition number has every finite moment. This remains model/covariance dependent; no dimension-only numeric bound is established.

### C1. Uniform singular values

TC^(1/2) is compact (C^(1/2) is compact). It has infinite rank: if TC^(1/2) had finite-dimensional range E, density of ranC^(1/2) and continuity of T imply ranT⊂E, contradicting rank∞. Consequently its r-th singular value is positive for every integer r. The singular value map obeys |s_r(S)−s_r(S')|<=||S−S'||, and ||(T−T')C^(1/2)||<=||T−T'||·||C^(1/2)||. Compact T therefore gives σ_r=min_Ts_r(TC^(1/2))>0 for each fixed r. The constantsσ_r may decrease extraordinarily rapidly with r.

### C2. Uniform small-ball estimate

For fixed T, choose the r leading left singular directions of TC^(1/2). The projections of Tf on these directions are independent centered real Gaussians with variances s_j²>=σ_r². If ||Tf||<=u, their Euclidean norm<=u. Bounding their product density by(2πσ_r²)^(−r/2) and integrating the r-ball gives
 P(||Tf||<=u) <= b_r (u/σ_r)^r,
 b_r=1/[2^(r/2)Γ(r/2+1)].
The directions can depend on T; only pointwise probability is used at finitely many net centers, so no measurable choice of directions is required.

### C3. Net and explicit input-norm tail

For t,R>0 choose an operator t/R-net with at mostA(R/t)^D centers. Ifκ<=t and||f||<=R, compactness attains the minimizing T, and its center T_j obeys||T_jf||<=2t. Thus
 P(κ<=t) <= A R^D b_r(2/σ_r)^r t^(r−D) + P(||f||>R),
provided t/R<=ε₀.

In a covariance eigenbasis, for any partial Gaussian sum,
 Eexp(||f_n||²/(4τ))=Π_j<=n(1−λ_j/(2τ))^(−1/2)<=exp(1/2),
because0<=λ_j/(2τ)<=1/2 and−log(1−x)<=2x on that interval. Monotone convergence extends it to f. Markov gives
 P(||f||>R)<=e^(1/2)exp(−R²/(4τ)).
For q>0, chooseR(t)=sqrt(4τq log(1/t)) for0<t<e^(−1). Then
 P(κ<=t) <= A b_r(2/σ_r)^r(4τq log(1/t))^(D/2)t^(r−D)+e^(1/2)t^q.
Fix p>0, choose q>p and integer r>D+p. Both terms are integrable against p t^(−p−1)dt near0. On t away from0 inverse tail integration is harmless. Therefore every positive inverse moment is finite. This proof establishes more than existence of confidence quantiles, while still hiding enormousσ_r-dependent constants. Noncentered Gaussian needs a modified density bound/norm tail; the present statement is centered exactly as root's design.

### C4. Entropy contract for finite-dimensional conductivity models

Let F:U⊂R^d→L(H,Y) be C², where U is convex (true for affine conductivity positivity). For compact K⊂U, its convex hullL is compact and stays in U: compactness gives uniform positivity ofσ_a; convex combinations preserve it. Choose a compact convex neighbourhood of L still in U. Its sup derivative bounds B= sup||DF|| and H₂=sup||D²F|| are finite. SetD₀=diamK. Every normalized positive secant lies in the family
 S(a,v,t)=∫₀¹DF(a+stv)[v]ds,
 a∈K, |v|=1, 0<t<=D₀, a+tv∈K.
Its closure in (a,v,t) is compact, including t=0 derivative valuesDF(a)[v]. The segment remains inL. For two admissible triples,
 ||S(a,v,t)−S(a',v',t')|| <= H₂|a−a'| +(B+H₂D₀/2)|v−v'| +(H₂/2)|t−t'|.
This follows from segment point distance≤|a−a'|+sD₀|v−v'|+s|t−t'| and integration. It uses C², not C³. Grid K in a bounded d-box, sphereS^(d−1) by a standard sphere net, and t∈[0,D₀] gives exponent d+(d−1)+1=2d. Selecting an admissible representative per occupied cell doubles the cover radius; charge that factor in A. A crude ambient sphere-box grid gives2d+1 and also suffices for all moments. No semianalytic assumption on K is needed for this coarse entropy bound.

The image family of normalized secants is compact; each nonzero-t secant operator is infinite rank by PDE difference theorem, and t=0 members infinite rank by derivative theorem. Thus the theorem applies to one current and full partial-boundary continuum voltage for every compact K in the admitted finite-dimensional conductivity model. Equivalent parameter norm constants are charged if expressing inL∞ conductivity norm. Finite electrode/detector output has finite rank and cannot meet the every-rank condition; all-moments assertion must not be transferred to that interface.

## D. Useful downstream corollary, with exact costs

After one Gaussian input is good, the response family is a compact injective immersive finite-dimensional model. Independent generic scalar Gaussian output functionals can reduce its retained data to2d+1 scalar values with compact qualitative inverse Lipschitz (tangent incidence plus pair incidence). This needs an additional output measurement theorem and supplied detector distribution. It does not preserve the all-inverse-moments conclusion for finite detector data, and does not certify manageable current bandwidth, detector resolution, or noise tolerance. The continuum input and detector design complexities remain separate costs.

## Unresolved load-bearing quantitative step

The every-moment theorem is mathematically rigorous given compactness/entropy/infinite rank, but obtains σ_r by a compact minimum. It does not compute or boundσ_r using partition geometry, positivity, covariance spectrum, or d. For a concrete robust experimental advance, certifyσ_r and entropy constants for an actual model and compare total field bandwidth, voltage modes, signal amplitudes and noise against competent deterministic/multi-current controls.
