## 1. Check soundness from ambient filling, including disconnected support

Let a global stabilizer p have M<dc actual sites. Split its actual support into connected components S_i of the induced **actual qubit graph**, with M_i=|S_i|. A check cannot meet two components: its actual support is a clique, and any two touched sites would already join them. Since p commutes with every check, its restricted Pauli p_i consequently commutes with every check separately. M_i<=M<d, so p_i is a stabilizer up to an absorbed Pauli phase, by the actual distance. The component phases multiply to the phase of p; the resulting product representation is exact.

Choose x_i in S_i. A path in that component uses at most M_i-1 actual graph edges, so S_i lies in a physical ball of radius2(M_i-1). Set r_i=max(1,2(M_i-1))<=2M_i<r_fill. By the filling premise p_i is a product of original checks wholly supported in B(x_i,Cr_i), a subset of B(x_i,2CM_i).

That ball intersects at most (4CM_i+2)^3 unit cubes. The number of possible check indices wholly in it is at most w_q times its qubit count: assign each nonempty check to one of its sites and charge the bounded incidence. Thus p_i uses no more than

    w_q t(4CM_i+2)^3 <= K M_i^3

distinct checks. Repeated indices cancel. Summing components and using sum_i M_i^3 <= (sum_i M_i)^3 proves the exact (dc,f)-check-soundness of primary Definition1. No intrinsic distance of the selected generators is required by that definition. This is why the new route requires neither declared support nor grid ancillas.

This component argument is also the one used in prior Proposition17, but the input here is a proportional ambient buffer, rather than assuming a fixed-collar LTO statement. The new application is not a new soundness method.

## 3. Direct uniform proof of B25, bypassing the ansatz comparison

Assume dc>=1, as the logarithmic theorem regime eventually ensures. Since g(r)>=1 and K>=1, every increment is >=eta=1/(Delta K^(1/3)). Thus

    g(r)>=1+eta(r-1)>=a r,

    ftilde(r)=(g(r)/K)^(1/3)>=b r^(1/3), b=(a/K)^(1/3)>0.

The constants do not depend on dc or n. This lower bound remains true after the recurrence's dc cap is active; restricting to ftilde(r)<dc only reduces the sum.

For every delta>0,

    sum_{r:ftilde(r)<dc} gamma(r) exp(-delta ftilde(r))
       <= 1+216t sum_{r>=1} r^3 exp(-b delta r^(1/3)).

Group integer r into m^3<=r<(m+1)^3. The group has at most7m^2 indices, r^3<=2^9 m^9 and r^(1/3)>=m. Therefore the sum is <=a fixed multiple of sum_{m>=1} m^11 exp(-b delta m). By an elementary integral bound this is at most C_b(1+delta^(-12)). Finally

    C_b(1+delta^(-12)) <= C'_b exp(12/delta)

after enlarging the fixed prefactor, separately for delta<=1 and delta>=1. This proves primary Eq.(B25) with **alpha=1**, c'_f=12 and a finite c''_f charging t,K,Delta. No claim about an efficient numerical threshold is made. The stronger growth estimate from the source is unnecessary.

## 4. Perturbation norm, with no physical graph hull supplied

Let V=sum_X V_X be Hermitian, every X contain at most k0 qubits and

    J = sup_x sum_{X:x in X} ||V_X||.

The supports can be physically or graphically disconnected. For fixed body order k0 the sum of absolute Pauli coefficients of V_X is at most2^{k0}||V_X||, by normalized Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonality and Cauchy–Schwarz. Remove scalar terms.

For a Pauli p choose strong support S(p) to include its actual support and the whole supports of every check touching it. Put a=1+w_c w_q. Then |S(p)|<=a k0 and S(p) lies in the actual graph one-neighborhood of supp p. Every check not contained in S(p) commutes with p, so the primary strong-support condition(A13) holds, with its actual syndrome.

At a fixed site x, a transformed support S(p) can contain x only if the original X contains some site in its one-neighborhood. There are at most a such sites. Triangle inequality when grouping equal S,syndrome pairs gives the explicit sufficient bound

    ||V||_kappa1 <= a 2^{k0} exp(kappa1 a k0) J.             (4)

Thus a fixed-body perturbation with sufficiently small intensive J satisfies the prior hypothesis. The same formula covers physical finite-range terms: bounded density gives a fixed k0<=t(R+2)^3 for physical diameter R. Merely bounding each term separately without controlling the sum J at each site would be insufficient.

All one-neighborhood, Pauli expansion and decay costs are charged in(4). No minimum connected-cover oracle, supplied metric, geometric shortcut or graph padding is needed. ## 6. Repair of the far-syndrome argument C78 without projector faithfulness

The printed inference near Eq.(C83), from commutation with a larger product projector to commutation with P_(S union S'), is not a general operator implication. The needed claim nevertheless has a direct **definite-syndrome** proof.

Let D_{S',sigma} be small (|S'|<ds), strongly supported and locally block-diagonal: [D,P_S']=0. Let A_{S,s} have definite syndrome s. Suppose C79 holds: |S'|<ftilde(dist(S',C)) for every check C in s.

* If sigma is empty, every Pauli component of D commutes with all checks and has actual weight<d, hence is a global stabilizer. The pointwise B23 exclusion, not merely a closed-radius collar, gives a minimum representation that touches **no site** of any flipped check in s, including its boundary sites under the strict C79 inequality. Every generator therefore commutes with A. Thus [D,A]=0 exactly.
* If sigma is nonempty, strong support contains a flipped check wholly in S'. Its anticommutation yields P_S'DP_S'=0; local block diagonality then gives DP_S'=P_S'D=0. C79 also implies A commutes with every check wholly in S', so [A,P_S']=0. Therefore [D,A] annihilates P_S' on both sides and hence annihilates P_(S union S') on both sides. Its locally off-diagonal projection is zero.

This proves the required C78 used in Lemma27 while avoiding the unsupported standalone projector inference. It preserves the same support, syndrome and norm bounds; no re-decomposition adding a large buffer to an operator term is performed.

The small-generator suppression ||A_{S,s}||<=||Pperp V_{S,s}||/|s| follows because a state produced from P_S flips |s| contained checks, each costing at least1. For s=empty the off-diagonal part is zero. When the projected commutator can be nonzero, it is anchored to a flipped check of A; the B25 shell sum cancels the |s| denominator and gives C31.
