COLD AUDIT RECEIPT — CATALYST-WEIGHTED CYCLE 5 EXTENSION
========================================================
Audited: ../RESULT.txt and verify_catalyst_weighted_generator.py
Scope: exact inactive-face coefficient, finite theta_* cutoff, all-state
Foster split, and the stated non-complex-balanced example. The theorem source
was not edited.

Conclusion
----------
I found no theorem-level counterexample in the stated family/rate region.
The inactive-face formula and theta tail argument are correct; the three
Foster regions close under the stated assumptions and yield the claimed
linear bound. The explicit rate instance is weakly reversible and not
complex balanced. I did find one false/ambiguous proof sentence: for K=1,
the *full* C_k-immigration jump at a+S=0 can be positive. This is harmless
to the Foster bound because its positive part is uniformly bounded and its
rate is constant, but the sentence should be corrected and that constant
explicitly absorbed.

1. Inactive face and exact theta
--------------------------------
At a=0, write u(S)=1/(1+S), W=R^b. The correction is epsilon W u(S).
Its coefficient divided by epsilon W is exactly

  F(c) = -alpha u(S) - sum_k zeta_k c_k u(S)
         + sum_k gamma_k [u(S+1)-u(S)]
         + sum_{k:c_k>0} mu_k c_k [u(S-1)-u(S)].

For S>=1, the gamma increment is -Gamma/((S+1)(S+2)); the death
increment is sum_k mu_k c_k/[S(S+1)] = mu_bar(c)/(S+1). Hence

  F(c) = [-alpha - sum_k zeta_k c_k - Gamma/(S+2) + mu_bar(c)]/(S+1)
       = -theta(c).

For S=0, F=-alpha-Gamma/2, agreeing with theta(0)=alpha+Gamma/2.
The A immigration and reverse-toggle losses are respectively
-alpha/(1+S) and -sum zeta_k c_k/(1+S); they are included. No substrate
edge fires on a=0.

For all S>=1,

  theta(c) >= [alpha-mu_max+zeta_min S]/(S+1).

Let D=max(mu_max-alpha,0). For every integer

  S >= T := ceil(1+2D/zeta_min),

the lower bound is at least zeta_min/2. Thus theta_*>0 is exactly
checkable from finitely many count vectors with S<T plus this positive
tail bound; for rational rates all comparisons are exact rational
arithmetic. The simpler alpha>mu_max condition gives
(theta(c)>=min(alpha-mu_max,zeta_min)) for S>=1, and theta(0)>0, as stated.

2. All-state Foster split
-------------------------
The non-correction generator is consistent with direct reaction increments:
A+B birth/removal contributes a[p(R-1)W-q(1-R^-1)bW], autocatalysis
contributes uab-v(a)_2b, C immigration/death contributes
sum w_k(gamma_k-mu_k c_k), and the toggle pair contributes
sum c_k(zeta_k-eta_k a). On a=0 the correction contributes
-epsilon theta(c)W. On a>=1 it is zero except when a=1 and A death or a
forward toggle turns off the last A; the total positive part is at most

epsilon W [delta+sum eta_k c_k]/(1+S)
  <= epsilon(delta+eta_max)W.

The weighted catalyst drift is bounded by

  sum_k c_k(zeta_k-eta_k a-w_k mu_k) <= -kappa S,
  kappa=min_k(w_k mu_k-zeta_k)>0.

For b>=N with G(b)<=-(q(1-R^-1)/2)bR^b:
* If a=0, the inactive correction gives -epsilon theta_* W, while the
  weighted C drift is at most a constant minus kappa S.
* If a>=1, aG(b) gives a negative term of order a bW. Increasing finite N
  makes it dominate the correction gain O(W), and any chosen positive
  multiples of a and W; -v(a)_2 b is also nonpositive.

For b<N, W is bounded. At b=0 the A coefficient is exactly
-(delta-p(R-1))a=-chi a. For each of the finitely many 1<=b<N, the term
-v(a)_2 b is quadratic negative and dominates the remaining terms linear
in a. The correction gain is bounded on this region, the K-indicator
positive contribution is at most K max(delta,mu_max), and the weighted C
drift supplies -kappa S plus a constant. These cases give
LV<=C0-c_a a-c_S S-c_W W with positive constants. Since
V<=C1+a+w_max S+(1+epsilon)W+K, this implies LV<=C-cV. The reasoning is
in physical-time generator units; no embedded-chain clock is used.

3. Exact boundary-jump repair
-----------------------------
At a=0,S=0, a C_k immigration changes the potential by

  Delta_k V = w_k - K - epsilon W/2,

because S changes 0->1 while h(a)=1, and the K-indicator turns off. Thus
for K=1, epsilon=1, R=3/2, b=0, and the stated example's k=2 (w_2=3),
Delta_2 V=3-1-1/2=3/2>0, at rate gamma_2=1. Therefore the sentence
“the exact K=1 boundary at a+S=0 has no positive jump” is false if it
means the full V jump. Only the K-indicator component is nonpositive
(Delta K=-K). The positive part of the full C-immigration jump is bounded
by (w_k-K)^+ uniformly in b, so its generator contribution is bounded by
sum_k gamma_k (w_k-K)^+ and is absorbed into C. A immigration has
Delta V=1-K-epsilon W<=0 for K>=1. Recommend replacing the sentence with
this precise distinction; no change to the theorem assumptions is needed.

4. Non-complex-balanced instance and verification
--------------------------------------------------
For the displayed r=2 example, chi=1/2; w_k mu_k-zeta_k=1 for both k; and
alpha=2>mu_max=1, so theta_*>=(min(1,1))=1. The toggle-pair complex-balance
equations at A+C_1 and A+C_2 force a*=zeta_1/eta_1=1 and
 a*=zeta_2/eta_2=2, respectively, impossible for a positive activity
vector. Hence it is not complex balanced (and so not detailed balanced).
Each listed pair is reversible, so the network is weakly reversible.

I ran the exact Fraction checker: it passed direct-generator equality on
875 fixture states, theta>=1 on c_1,c_2=0,...,20, and the conflicting
toggle-ratio check. Those are finite diagnostics; the formulas and Foster
proof above supply the all-state argument. No external review or novelty
claim is made here.
