DIMENSION-FREE CLASSICAL RECONSTRUCTION FROM APPROXIMATE BROADCASTING
Cycle03 local candidate proof packet — full endpoint internal audit PASS.

For a quantum state family E, define C as its Holevo information maximized over ALL priors (in nats), b as the best worst-state two-output marginal broadcasting error, and e as the best worst-state error of ONE common measure-and-prepare (entanglement-breaking) channel. Both errors use half trace norm. Original broadcasters need not be unital, reversible, or independent across outputs.

The complete assembled candidate proves
  e <= min(1, 400 sqrt((C+1)/ln(1/b))) for 0<b<1,
  e=0 when b=0.
Dimension and number of states do not occur in the bound. In particular bounded capacity and vanishing broadcasting error force one common classical reconstruction channel; capacity may even grow as o(log(1/b)).
