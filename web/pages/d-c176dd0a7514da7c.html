4. EXACT TRANSVERSE COMMUTATOR COST OF SPIN FILTERING

sum_ij||[X_ji,R]||^2=2E_2(R), while
Tr_N a_l a_j^dag=delta_lj(N+1)(N+2)/2.

As a continuous function of component s, T'(s)=-(k+N+1-2s), and direct
differentiation of (3.1) yields

  d beta/dT=1/[2A B(A+B)^3],
  d alpha/dT=-[(A+B)-alpha]/[2A B(A+B)^2].                 (4.3)

Under k+N+1<=m/2, A>=sqrt(m/2), B>=sqrt m and A+B>=sqrt(2m).

  ||[h(T),R]||_HS<=Lip(h)||[T,R]||_HS

  [B_i^dag,R]=a_i^dag[alpha,R]+D_i[beta,R]+[D_i,R]beta.

The sums of squared norms of these three terms, divided by
(N+1)(N+2)E_2(R)/m, are at most 1/3, 1/6 and 1/2. The first uses
sum_i a_i a_i^dag=(N+2)I; the second uses the D_i operator bound and

This bound charges the exact noncommuting spin/external coupling. In
