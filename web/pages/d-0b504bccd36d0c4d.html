# Cycle4 independent restricted OU forcing audit

Date 2026-10-06. Worker check_wcrg. No children. Exact class and resource assumptions are inherited explicitly from cycle3: stable real A, constant drift input b, positive baseline diffusion POWER D0, all physical coordinates sensed, additive calibrated independent white forcing with unchanged A,b, and stationary Gaussian ensembles. This note independently derives results, then audits r04's cycle4 proof. No historical priority or actual laboratory acquisition is claimed.

**Decisive result:** diagonal actuation does suffice with two perturbed settings for every fixed stable drift and almost every second diagonal probe, even when baseline noise is unknown and correlated. A universal deterministic design is harder: ANY pair of fixed positive diagonal probes fails uniformly in dimension≥3. We give a full construction and exact rational replay. Thus fixed-truth genericity cannot be strengthened to two universally successful prescribed settings.

## Covariance response and the rank-one lemma

Let L_A(G)=AG+GAᵀ. The baseline covariance is Σ0=L_A⁻¹(D0), and the response to diagonal power Q is Γ(Q)=ΣQ−Σ0=L_A⁻¹(Q). The unknown baseline cancels. For Q≻0, Γ(Q)≻0. For the coordinate projector Ejj, Gj=L_A⁻¹(Ejj) is PSD and may be singular.

Fix any positive diagonal Q1, whiten Γ1=Γ(Q1) by W=Γ1^(-1/2), and put B=WAW⁻¹, Cj=WGjW, wj=Wej. Then

B+Bᵀ=WQ1W≻0, BCj+CjBᵀ=wjwjᵀ, and ∑j(Q1)jj Cj=I.

Every positive eigenvalue of Cj is simple. Indeed compression onto an r-dimensional eigenspace for eigenvalue λ>0 gives λP(B+Bᵀ)P=PwjwjᵀP. The left side is positive definite on that space and has rank r; the right has rank≤1. Thus r≤1.

For x∈ker Cj, taking its quadratic form in the Lyapunov equation gives wjᵀx=0. Applying the equation to x then gives CjBᵀx=0, so ker Cj is Bᵀ-invariant. Every intersection N of these kernels is invariant too. In range/kernel coordinates B has lower-left block0. Compressing the equation of another Ck onto N therefore gives B_N(Ck)_N+(Ck)_N B_Nᵀ=(wk)_N(wk)_Nᵀ; B_N+B_Nᵀ remains PD. Consequently the positive eigenvalues of each such compression are simple.

## Two positive diagonal settings: every-fixed-drift generic theorem

There exists a positive combination ∑qjCj with simple spectrum. Construct it incrementally. C1 has simple positive eigenvalues and exact kernel N1. Add εC2 with ε>0 sufficiently small. Old positive eigenvalues remain distinct and separated from zero. Eigenvalues emerging from zero equal ε times the positive eigenvalues of (C2)_N1 plus O(ε²), which are simple by the compressed lemma; the Schur complement of the old positive block gives this first-order formula. Remaining zero eigenvalues have exact kernel N1∩ker C2 since the sum is PSD. Repeat with successively small positive coefficients. The previous sum's kernel is always an invariant common kernel, so the induction applies. Finally the common kernel vanishes because ∑(Q1)jjCj=I. Every eigenvalue is then positive and simple.

For fixed A,Q1, C(Q2)=WΓ(Q2)W is linear in the n diagonal amplitudes of Q2. Its characteristic discriminant is a polynomial in those amplitudes; the construction gives a positive-cone point where it is nonzero. Thus its zero set has n-dimensional Lebesgue measure zero. Any absolutely continuous random positive diagonal Q2 succeeds almost surely for this fixed A,Q1. No sparsity, graph or controllability assumption is needed.

The first response fixes the symmetric part of the whitened A, and the second simple-spectrum response fixes its skew part by commutator inversion. Recover D0=AΣ0+Σ0Aᵀ and b=Aμ0. This works for arbitrary unknown full SPD D0. For n≥2 two increments are minimal in that full-baseline class: one Γ permits A+ηΩΓ⁻¹ with nonzero skew Ω, with baseline D0 and b adjusted; small η remains physical. For n1 one increment suffices.

The unknown-diagonal-baseline interface might admit one generic diagonal increment in every dimension, but this proof does not settle it. Simple response spectrum is proved; the separate F determinant from cycle3 still needs a nonzero witness on the diagonal cone. Selected bad probes do not disprove the every-fixed-truth generic claim.

## Exact n2 answer for an unknown diagonal baseline

Let A=[[a,b],[c,d]] be any real positive-stable 2×2 matrix. Stability implies a+d>0 and det A=ad−bc>0. For Γ(q)=L_A⁻¹(diag(q1,q2)), the 2×2 linear map M:q→(Γ11,Γ22) satisfies

det M=1/(4 det A)>0.

Independent symbolic elimination verifies this identity. For baseline Σ0=Γ(d1,d2) and positive diagonal q,

Γ11 Σ0,22−Γ22 Σ0,11=(q1d2−q2d1)/(4 det A).

Because Γ≻0, every homogeneous drift preserving Γ has H=ωJΓ⁻¹. Its baseline symmetric residual has offdiagonal entry proportional to the displayed expression. Unknown diagonal baseline changes can absorb its diagonal entries, so the joint operator is invertible exactly when q1d2−q2d1≠0. Hence:

- One random positive diagonal increment succeeds for every fixed 2D truth; its bad set is exactly the ray q∝d.
- No single prescribed positive diagonal increment works uniformly over unknown baselines, by choosing d=q.
- Two nonproportional prescribed positive diagonal increments identify every stable 2D truth with unknown diagonal baseline: both cannot lie on the same unknown bad ray.

These statements concern positive-definite diagonal increments. A single coordinate rank-one increment may have singular Γ and does not identify every system: diagonal A leaves the unprobed coordinate's scalar drift/noise scale unresolved. One coordinate rank-one probe identifies a fixed 2D truth if Γ is PD (the coordinate pair is controllable); do not extend that argument through a singular Γ.

## Any two prescribed positive diagonal probes fail uniformly for n≥3

Normalize Q1 to I by a diagonal physical-coordinate change; Q2 becomes diag(t1,...,tn), ti>0. Set the normalized baseline D0=I. If some two ratios coincide, choose A=rI and a nonzero skew rotation within that pair: it preserves the baseline covariance and both response covariances.

If all ratios are distinct and n≥3, select three with a<c<b. On those coordinates choose a positive diagonal R=W² with entries p,1,q, and put α=c−a>0, β=b−c>0. Choose p,q so αp≠βq. Let

u=(√[βq/(αp+βq)],0,√[αp/(αp+βq)]), v=(u3,0,−u1),

E=span(e_middle,u), and τ=(vᵀWQ2Wv)/(vᵀRv).

E is two-dimensional and its compression of W(Q2−cI)W is zero. Moreover τ−c=(βq−αp)/(vᵀRv)≠0; τ>0 is a positive weighted average of a,b. Let C act as cI on E and τ on span(v). Let B have symmetric part R/2. In E⊕span(v), set its skew cross-block to

K_Ev=[(WQ2W)_Ev−(c+τ)R_Ev/2]/(τ−c),

and its within-E skew block to zero. Then BC+CBᵀ=WQ2W: the E block follows from the isotropic compression, the v block from τ's definition, and the cross block from this formula. B+Bᵀ=R≻0 ensures stability. Set A=W⁻¹BW, Γ1=W⁻², Γ2=W⁻¹CW⁻¹. These satisfy the two noise-response equations, and Σ0=Γ1 since D0=Q1=I.

Choose any nonzero skew Ω supported within E. It commutes with C. H=W⁻¹ΩW obeys HΓj+ΓjHᵀ=0 for j1,2 and likewise for Σ0. Every A+ηH remains positive stable because its whitened symmetric part stays R/2, and keeps D0 fixed. Set b=0. Thus baseline and both complete Gaussian stationary laws are identical for a continuum of distinct stable drifts. Other coordinates can be independent damped modes. Undoing the diagonal normalization proves the result for ANY prescribed pair of positive diagonal powers, for all n≥3.

A rational example already covers even equally spaced distinct ratios:

Q1=I, Q2=diag(1,2,3), D0=I,

A=[[2,0,−5/6],[0,1/2,0],[10/3,0,1/2]], Σ0=Γ1=diag(1/4,1,1),

Γ2=[[11/34,0,3/17],[0,2,0],[3/17,0,31/17]],

H=[[0,−1/2,0],[2,0,2],[0,−2,0]].

W=diag(2,1,1) makes B's symmetric part diag(2,1/2,1/2), and WΓ2W has eigenvalues2,2,19/17. Exact symbolic multiplication verifies every covariance identity for all real η. The baseline diffusion is known and unchanged here; baseline uncertainty is not responsible for the obstruction. Distinct physical diffusion ratios do not imply distinct response-covariance ratios.

## A fixed finite uniform protocol and minimum counts within stated families

n coordinate probes Ejj identify A uniformly for every stable A, even with arbitrary unknown SPD baseline. A homogeneous ambiguity H preserves every Gj. Whiten G*=∑Gj≻0 so transformed H is skew K and commutes with every Cj. Each Cj has simple positive eigenvalues. Commutation forces K to annihilate every positive eigenvector of Cj; it can act only in ker Cj. The intersection of all kernels is zero since ∑Cj=I, hence K=0. This proves uniqueness without choosing a basis for a nonzero rotation-frequency space.

The same result holds with n positive diagonal probes spanning diagonal matrix space: their acquired responses synthesize all Gj by linearity. For example I and I+Ejj, j1,...,n−1, span that space. n coordinate rank-one probes are necessary within that coordinate-only family: a diagonal A leaves any unprobed coordinate's scalar rate/noise scale ambiguous. That restricted minimum does not prove n is necessary among mixed diagonal amplitude patterns.

For unknown positive diagonal baseline, the exact uniform minimum of **positive diagonal** perturbed settings is1 for n1,2 for n2,3 for n3. Sufficiency uses the spanning protocol; necessity uses the universal one/pair obstructions. For n≥4 this audit proves bounds3≤m_uniform≤n, not an exact minimum. For fixed-truth generic designs, two suffice in every dimension even with unknown full baseline, one suffices for unknown diagonal baseline in n1,n2, and the one-probe question for n≥3 remains unresolved. For unknown full baseline n≥2, two generic settings are exactly minimal.

## Acquired uncertainty, improved sample bound, and actual cost

For the two-response reconstruction, acquire three covariance estimates Σhat0,Σhat1,Σhat2. On a simultaneous confidence event with operator errors εj, responses Γhatj have error≤ε0+εj. For the stacked known-right-side drift operator P(A)=(AΓ1+Γ1Aᵀ,AΓ2+Γ2Aᵀ),

δP=2√[(ε0+ε1)²+(ε0+ε2)²].

Charge both calibration errors ζj in Frobenius norm, ζ=√(ζ1²+ζ2²). A conservative O(n³) empirical gap certificate uses Γhat1's conditioning and Chat=Γhat1^(-1/2)Γhat2Γhat1^(-1/2). With c=||Chat||, g=minpair|ci−cj|>0, β=√[(1/2+c/g)²+1/g²],

γhat≥λmin(Γhat1)/[√cond(Γhat1) β].

For any Ahat with empirical residual r and γhat>δP,

||Ahat−A||F≤[r+δP||Ahat||F+ζ]/(γhat−δP).

This follows by the same perturbation proof independently audited in cycle3. For n1 omit the skew terms. Baseline D0 and input b require propagation through Σhat0 and μhat0. Stability/positivity and unchanged mean may be checked against the confidence set; those checks do not establish the OU class from finite data.

A sharper elementary iid Gaussian sample radius replaces the earlier n²/δ Markov bound. For m=N−1 centered sample-covariance degrees of freedom, standardize by unknown Σ. For each unit vector, m times its empirical quadratic form is χ²_m. Its centered log-mgf is bounded above by mλ²/(1−2λ) for 0<λ<1/2, and by mλ² for negative λ. Chernoff optimization gives upper2√(mx)+2x and lower2√(mx), with total failure≤2e^(-x). A sphere1/4-net has≤9^n points, and a symmetric matrix's operator norm is≤2 times its maximum absolute quadratic form on that net. Consequently choose

x=n log9+log(2/δ), ρ=4√(x/m)+4x/m,

so ||Σhat−Σ||op≤ρ||Σ||op with probability≥1−δ. If ρ<1, the fully acquired radius is ε=ρ||Σhat||op/(1−ρ). This establishes O((n+log(1/δ))/ρ²) independent snapshots for small ρ, rather than the previous conservative n²/δ dependence. It does not provide a parameter sample bound without the measured inverse gap. Unknown mean is handled by the exact centered Wishart law m=N−1, not by pretending that the true mean is supplied.

Allocate a joint failure budget over settings and a summable budget over adaptive doubling batches. Continue until the parameter certificate meets its requested tolerance; otherwise retain an explicit unresolved gap. Finite-bit noise amplitudes need their own discrete success analysis or measured certificate: a.e. claims are not failure bounds for a finite grid.

Physical actuation requires n independent calibrated Gaussian force amplitudes per mixed diagonal setting, with no cross-coordinate noise mixing. Rank-one coordinate settings require one force channel per condition but n conditions for the uniform fallback. All covariances still need full-state sensing: estimating only marginal variances discards the load-bearing information. Two-response arithmetic costs O(n³)/O(n²) storage; an n-setting fallback stores O(n³) covariance entries and charges all n+1 ensembles. It can synthesize positive combinations of the responses and choose a simple covariance pencil after acquisition, propagating the linear-combination uncertainties; dense stacked least squares is not free.

Stationary ensembles are a substantive supplied resource. Preparation, independent resets or justified mixing, elapsed experimental time, bandwidth of the white-noise approximation, amplitude limits, calibration, sensor noise and drift changes remain unaccounted physical costs. Stability alone supplies no uniform mixing rate over the class. High rotation can shrink informative anisotropy while damping/mixing remains fixed, so long preparation and poor inverse conditioning are distinct costs. Full covariance confidence alone is not an end-to-end discovery certificate.
