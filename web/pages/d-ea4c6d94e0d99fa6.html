## Premises and exact projector

Let J be the exact rational kernel of current original-face, queried cost,
large-row, protected-barrier, and approximate-gradient equations. Let Z
be any full-column-rank rational basis. The orthogonal projector

`P=Z(Z^T Z)^(-1) Z^T`

is rational, symmetric, satisfies `P^2=P`, and has norm one. All rational
elimination/inverse encodings are polynomial in the equation bits by
determinant bounds. The algorithm does not pay inverse singular values as
an operation count or numerical precision condition; small singular
values are represented through the given rational input bits.

Suppose a symmetric true search form F has an r-dimensional subspace
E contained in J on which `F<=-2eta I`, and rational T approximates F
with operator error at most eta/4. Then `T<=-7eta I/4` on E.
For rational `M0>=||T||+eta`, form

`S=PTP+M0(I-P)`.

S commutes with P, acts as M0I on J-perp, and has at least r
eigenvalues at most -7eta/4 by min--max on E. These negative eigenvectors
are entirely in J, because the perpendicular block is positive.

## Filter and removal of the perpendicular block

Li's rational polynomial filter applied to S with threshold eta and
certified integer r produces symmetric Q with

`0<=Q<=I`,

`tr(Q^2)>=3r/4`,

`Q(S+eta I)Q <= eta r/(4096N) I`.

Here N is the active ambient dimension. Because Q is a polynomial in S,
it commutes exactly with P. Define `Q_J=PQ=QP=PQP`.
This rational symmetric matrix is zero on J-perp, is a contraction, and
maps every vector into J exactly.

Removing the perpendicular block preserves the trace bound for a concrete
reason: on each of the r strongly negative eigenvectors, Li's scalar
filter has eigenvalue greater than 7/8. All those eigenvectors lie in J,
so `tr(Q_J^2)>49r/64>3r/4`, independently of Q's other eigenvalues.
Compression of the leakage inequality gives

`Q_J(S+eta I)Q_J <= eta r/(4096N) P <= eta r/(4096N) I`.

Thus Q_J is itself a soft projector. No small constraint residual is
introduced. A mere approximate commutation or approximate projector would
not establish this exact interface.

## Conditional moments and physical normalization

Let M linear forms f_l have norm at most one, including the N coordinates
and all medium-barrier gradients divided by two. Choose integer p with
`NM 2^(-p)<=r/8`. For signs xi consider

`Zeta(xi)=||Q_J xi||^2 - N/(4p)^p sum_l(f_l^T Q_J xi)^(2p)`.

Uniform-sign moments satisfy
`E(a^T xi)^(2p)<=(2p-1)!! ||a||^(2p)<=(2p)^p` for norm a<=1,
by coefficient comparison of cosh and exp(z^2/2). Hence

`E Zeta>=tr(Q_J^2)-NM2^(-p)>=5r/8`.

Fix each sign using the larger exact rational conditional expectation.
The resulting vector h0=Q_J xi lies exactly in J and has
`||h0||^2>=5r/8`. If any form has squared value at least 4p, its penalty
is at least N; since `||h0||^2<=N`, Zeta would be nonpositive, a
contradiction. Divide h0 by the least dyadic power at least its norm,
found from exact squared comparisons. Then

`1/2<||h||<=1`, `max_l |f_l^T h|^2<8p/r`,

and leakage yields

`h^T T h<=-(1-1/2048)eta ||h||^2`.

The precise transferred bound for F is

`h^T F h<=-(3/4-1/2048)eta ||h||^2`.

This is strictly negative, as the candidate note claims. It is slightly
weaker than the old `-3eta/4` relative contract, so it should not be
silently identified with that exact constant. For this pure uncharged
premise, using T+eta I/16 inside the J block of S restores a bound
strictly below -3eta/4 while E remains strongly negative.

For a **charged** search form whose guaranteed subspace is only at
`-3eta/2`, use a different construction: approximate within eta/16 and

normalization and transfer give curvature at most
`-(7/8-1/2048)eta||h||^2<-3eta||h||^2/4`. The positive shift for the
pure premise cannot be reused for this charged premise. The shift choice
