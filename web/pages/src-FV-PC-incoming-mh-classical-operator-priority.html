The following independent algebra explains the tail mismatch. Write
eta=R*(mu-delta), alpha=||eta|| and choose beta with alpha<beta and
R*R>=beta R. Put S=|eta|/beta, q=S(R)=alpha/beta<1,

 nu=(1-q)(R*R)*sum_{k>=0} S^{*k}.

The identity (delta-S)*sum S^{*k}=delta and eta+beta S>=0 show
mu*nu>=0. If the input and R have all ordinary moments, S does too;
geometric weights control every fixed polynomial moment. Exponential
moments instead require m_S(g)<1. They can diverge once this threshold
is reached even when every fixed polynomial moment is finite. Therefore
this classical positive-smoothing mechanism does not prove the
all-exponential characterization. Its existence also means that broad
claims of unprecedented convolution positivity are untenable.
