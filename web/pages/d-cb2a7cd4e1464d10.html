1. Actual same-map definitions and the general transported representation

Let L be the POSITIVE Heisenberg generator, exp(-tL) unital CP, L_*(sigma)=0,
sigma>0, s=sqrt(sigma), and Gamma_s(X)=s^(1/2) X s^(1/2).
H=Gamma_s L Gamma_s^(-1) need not be HS selfadjoint. H(s)=H*(s)=0 holds.
The genuine physical quantities for the ORIGINAL generator are
  J_L(rho)=Tr[L_*(rho)(log rho-log sigma)],
  E_L(rho)=Re Tr[sqrt(rho) H(sqrt(rho))]
           =Tr[sqrt(rho) Hsym(sqrt(rho))].

A general useful orientation is
  H(X)=(D*X+XD)/2-sum_j B_j* X B_j,                         (1.1)
not the selfadjoint Hermitian-noise KMS representation. Its two stationarity
relations are
  D*s+sD=2Y, Y=sum B_j* s B_j,
  Ds+sD*=2Z, Z=sum B_j s B_j*.                              (1.2)
If D=V+iW, V=V*, W=W*, then
  {V,s}=Y+Z, i[s,W]=Y-Z.                                   (1.3)
Thus the anti-HS part can contain BOTH a commutator and an asymmetric CP-noise
difference. It need not be a Hamiltonian derivation. Hsym is nevertheless a
legal KMS generator, since the KMS-adjoint physical QMS is legal and stationary.
For a single B, in a nondegenerate s eigenbasis with entries s_i, equations
(1.2) give
  D_ij=2(s_i Y_ij-s_j Z_ij)/(s_i^2-s_j^2), i!=j,
  Re D_ii=Y_ii/s_i, with required Y_ii=Z_ii.                (1.4)
An imaginary diagonal term commuting with s is freely permitted.
This is an exact reusable representation, with its diagonal/degeneracy
compatibility condition explicit. It was derived independently here; it does
not claim that arbitrary prescribed B satisfy that compatibility.

Conversely, matrices B,D satisfying (1.2) yield a legal stationary QMS.
Set K=s^(1/2) B s^(-1/2) and C=s^(1/2)D s^(-1/2). Then
  L_*(X)=(CX+XC*)/2-KXK*, C+C*=2K*K.                       (1.5)
The latter is the positive-generator Lindblad trace-preservation condition.
Its Hermitian Hamiltonian is (C-C*)/(2i), with its full contribution retained.
Equation H*(s)=0 transports to L_*(sigma)=0. No KMS symmetry is assumed.

2. A phase-balanced rank-one block mechanism

Let A0=s_1+s_2+s_3, Y=B* S B=A0 |u><u|,
Z=B S B*=A0 |v><v|. Both have identical diagonals. Define D by (1.4) using

The scaling of S cancels from D. Every matrix is finite. This is a legal
stationary generator with H(S)=H*(S)=0, but D is not Hermitian and H is not

3. Exact physical stationary perturbation and Hessian

Use h=(1,2,1), Q=sum_i h_i sqrt(s_i)(E_0i+E_i0), and the physical state family
  rho_epsilon=(S+epsilon Q)^2/Tr[(S+epsilon Q)^2].           (3.1)
Q has only center-leaf coherences, Tr(SQ)=0. All sufficiently small nonzero
epsilon give faithful states. The entropy and root forms are analytic there.
With q_0=S/sqrt(Z0), delta_rho={S,Q}/Z0, the exact leading coefficients are
  J_L(rho_epsilon)=epsilon^2 J2+O(epsilon^3),
  E_L(rho_epsilon)=epsilon^2 E2+O(epsilon^3),
  J2=Tr[L_*(delta_rho) Dlog_sigma(delta_rho)],
  E2=Re Tr[Q H(Q)]/Z0.                                    (3.2)
No state-channel KMS relation is used in J2. Normalization in (3.1) has no
effect on these leading terms: L_*(sigma)=0, trace preservation removes the
scalar logarithm, and the root-energy kernel contains s.

Writing x_i=-log s_i, R_i=x_i/sinh x_i, C_i=1/(2cosh(x_i/2)), and
z_i=h_i(1+s_i), the physical (original-generator) coherence Hessian is
  J2=(1/Z0) z*[(R D+D* R)/2]z,
  E2=(1/Z0) z* C V C z.                                   (3.3)
For this ROW-phase noise, J2's leaf kernel equals
  A0(R_i+R_j)/(s_i+s_j) when v_i v_j=+1,
  A0(R_i-R_j)/(s_i-s_j) when v_i v_j=-1,
with diagonal A0 R_i/s_i. Physical log divided differences independently give
these expressions. The signs are opposite for the column-phase transposed B.

5. Primitivity with an explicit coefficient

Thus the strict negative Hessian margin survives this EXPLICIT positive weight.
