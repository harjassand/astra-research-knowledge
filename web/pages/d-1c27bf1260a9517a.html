A. Exact obstruction to the tempting CONTINUOUS Mellin interpolation
Let c=N/2+1, a=delta/N>0. The interpolated, central, normalized function is
F(z)=Gamma(c+z)Gamma(c-z)/Gamma(c)^2 * exp(-a z^2).
If a positive measure on real logit s had moment generating function F(z)
for every real z in ANY open neighborhood of 0, finite positive and negative
exponential moments would give an analytic transform on a vertical strip.
Analytic identity then forces its characteristic function to equal F(it)
for every real t. But this is impossible since |characteristic function|<=1.
For integer c=m+1,
Gamma(c+it)Gamma(c-it)=pi*t/sinh(pi*t) product_{j=1}^m(j^2+t^2).
For half-integer c=m+3/2,
Gamma(c+it)Gamma(c-it)=pi/cosh(pi*t) product_{j=0}^m((j+1/2)^2+t^2).
These follow directly from Gamma recurrence and reflection, and F(it) grows
as a positive polynomial times exp(a t^2-pi|t|), hence is unbounded.
Background identity source: https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5#E3 .
This DOES NOT obstruct the N+1 integer Bernstein/logit moments. An analytic
identity theorem cannot be invoked from finitely many samples. The successful
c07_s02 construction truncates in the pole-free strip and applies a finite
exact correction, so it respects this obstruction rather than contradicts it.

B. Conditional-PSD log-Hankel bound is an extra sufficient test
For f(k)=log Gamma(k+1)+log Gamma(N-k+1), its adjacent-difference kernel is
K_shift[i,j]=log[(i+j+shift+2)/(i+j+shift+1)
                    *(N-i-j-shift)/(N-i-j-shift-1)].
It is positive definite: each log ratio has a positive Frullani integral,
respectively the Gram features exp(-i t) and exp(+i t), t>0.
For the damped sequence the kernel subtracts (2delta/N)11^T. Therefore
log-Hankel conditional positivity suffices when
delta <= N/(2 * 1^T K_shift^-1 1) for both blocks.
Conditional positivity implies positivity of entrywise exp by centering at
one index, factoring diagonal exponentials, and the Schur-power series.
This is a sufficient criterion, not a necessary separability condition.
The OWN cpsd_bound.json gives high-precision diagnostic thresholds through
N128; at N32 it is about1.321 and N128 about1.113, already below2log2.
Failure of this sufficient test cannot be reported as an entanglement witness.
It does not establish a useful uniform positive range by itself.

C. Positive Beta expansion is not automatically exact
Write s_k=k(N-k), a=delta/N, and divide Gaussian populations by their k0 value,
so the target is exp(a s_k). For integer l>=0 define
b_l(s)=product_{j=1}^l(s+j(N+j))/product_{j=1}^l j(N+j).
At s=s_k these are the population ratios of a positive density proportional
to [p(1-p)]^l, calibrated so its k0 population equals1. As l->infinity,
b_l(s_k)->C(N,k); this is the delta mass at p1/2 with k0 population1.
The recurrence s*b_l=(l+1)(N+l+1)(b_l+1-b_l) suggests a positive pure-birth
mixture as a time-evolution proof. But its rates are quadratic; the sum of
mean waiting times is H_N/N<infinity, and explosion has positive probability
before every a>0. Once its endpoint is treated honestly as absorbing, its
mixture F_a satisfies F'_a=s(F_a-b_infinity P[explosion<=a]), not F'_a=sF_a.
Thus this natural minimal positive process is not the exact target exp(a s).
No positive representation is excluded; only this naive exactness argument
is rejected. There is no promoted theorem from this route.
