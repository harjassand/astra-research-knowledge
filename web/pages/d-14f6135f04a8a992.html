# A computable outer projection of the continuous predictive confidence set

7 October 2026. Companion to predictive_confidence_and_cost.md. This is a deterministic certificate conditional on observable norm/KL checks; it does not promise an informative interval on the frozen benchmark. No numerical optimizer or fitted nuisance value is credited. Independent gate pending.

## Inputs and confidence budget

Retain the original supplied continuous finite-generator class: known irreducible baseline support, ordinary compact positive interval, b in[0,1], parameter-independent starting state and calibrated passive clocks. Let lambda bound every escape rate, including b<=1. At a chosen lag t with lambda t<log2,

    rho0=2(1-exp(-lambda t))<1

bounds ||P_Q(t)-I||_infinity uniformly: P_xx>=exp(-lambda t) and stochasticity makes the row absolute sum2(1-P_xx). This general domain can be less restrictive than1/4. All candidate semigroup paths P_Q(st),0<=s<=1, stay in the same log domain. Differentiating the absolutely convergent log series along the commuting path gives derivative tQ, hence log P_Q(t)/t=Q exactly.

At the chosen level, let each row have at least m retained endpoints and empirical row matrix Phat. From an all-level predictable numerator M, or a restarted masked numerator, let

    B=log Lsat_all-log M+log(1/delta).

This budget must correspond to exactly the same numerator and retained/all-record likelihood factors. Lsat_all is the product of saturated likelihoods for those cells. On candidate acceptance L_Q>=delta M,

    sum_cells N_xj KL(Phat_xj||P_Q(t_j)_x)<=B.

Therefore at the chosen level sum_x KL(Phat_x||P_Q(t)_x)<=k=B/m. It is legitimate to extract one level because all omitted KL terms are nonnegative. If B<0, the confidence set is empty: a candidate likelihood cannot exceed Lsat_all. Do not replace a negative B by a fictitious positive-budget coverage argument. Exact rational/interval comparisons must handle uncertain signs conservatively. Rows with zero counts cannot support this chosen-level certificate.

## Binary KL enclosures without nuisance fitting

For every entry r=Phat_xy, solve the univariate inequality

    kl(r||p)=r log(r/p)+(1-r)log[(1-r)/(1-p)]<=k

for p in[0,1], obtaining an outward enclosure [l_xy,u_xy]. Data processing ensures every accepted candidate P_xy lies in that interval. For r=0, the upper endpoint is1-exp(-k); for r=1, the lower is exp(-k). General roots are monotone on each side of r and can be certified by bisection with log enclosures. Stochasticity can tighten these intervals but is not needed for validity.

Form the nonnegative envelope Zup with off-diagonal entries u_xy and diagonal entries1-l_xx. It dominates |P_Q-I| entrywise for every accepted candidate. If ||Zup||_infinity<1, compute

    Rup=(I-Zup)^-1=sum_(j>=0)Zup^j.

The inverse has nonnegative entries and dominates every candidate's absolute resolvent. Its interval inverse can be obtained by a rational Neumann sum plus norm tail; a floating-point inverse alone is not a certificate. If this check fails, return full supplied b-range/UNKNOWN. Failing this sufficient test does not show nonidentifiability or falsity of a candidate.

Define off-diagonal row-tangent influence envelopes

    A_xy=Rup_ux(Rup_yv+Rup_xv)/t, y!=x,
    A_xx=0,
    Vup=sum_(x,y!=x) u_xy A_xy^2,
    Mup=2 max_(x,y!=x) A_xy.

The exact log-series derivative implies |a_xy(P)|<=A_xy. Row variance is at most sum_y P_xy a_xy^2, hence total variance<=Vup. Centering a row coefficient changes its magnitude by at most twice its largest absolute value, so Mup bounds centered coefficients. The variational Bernstein transport proved in the companion note yields

    |[Dlog_P(Phat-P)]_uv/t|
         <=sqrt(2 Vup k)+(Mup/3)k.

No Hessian symmetry, reversibility, embeddability of Phat, or supplied ordinary numerical rate is assumed.

## Remainder and output

Let

    Emax=max_x sum_y max(|u_xy-Phat_xy|,|l_xy-Phat_xy|),
    rhoh=||Phat-I||_infinity,
    rho=max(rho0,rhoh).

If rhoh>=1, return full range/UNKNOWN. Otherwise every segment P+theta(Phat-P) has norm distance from I<=rho<1. The log-series second derivative is bounded by

    ||D^2log_(I+K)[E,E]||
      <=sum_(j>=2)(j-1)rho^(j-2)||E||^2
      =||E||^2/(1-rho)^2.

Taylor's integral remainder is at most Emax^2/[2t(1-rho)^2]. Therefore every accepted candidate's exact rare rate belongs to

    [braw-r,braw+r] intersect[0,1],
    braw=[log Phat]_uv/t,
    r=sqrt(2 Vup k)+(Mup/3)k
          +Emax^2/[2t(1-rho)^2].         (OBS)

The output includes outward log/lag/radius arithmetic enclosures. This proves an outer projection of the continuous confidence set, including an initially unknown b. It does not prove that every point in the interval is attainable. If the confidence set is empty, any claimed confidence-point output needs a failure convention; an empty set is permitted as a set-valued output on the coverage failure event. A certified narrow outer interval supports stopping; an optimizer's feasible witness supports only inclusion.

The additional direct rare-event inequality

    P_Q(t)_uv>=b t exp(-lambda t)

follows by taking exactly one rare jump and no other jump. It gives the legal upper envelope b<=exp(lambda t)u_uv/t for every accepted Q. Thus an informative data-dependent small-b restriction can be *proved* from the same accepted-class conditions; it need not be assumed from the controller's lag choice. Using that restriction in a support-weighted Cp/Cr calculation is another possible certificate. Its whole unknown-scale stopping/time theorem is not established by this observation alone.

## Computation and limits

For fixed n the sufficient certificate requires n^2 one-dimensional KL root enclosures, a nonnegative resolvent enclosure, one convergent matrix log and scalar arithmetic. At a strict finite margin all these terminate using rational interval arithmetic. Very small margins may require large precision; no bit-uniform margin lower bound is supplied. Binary intervals and the absolute-resolvent majorant can be extremely conservative, especially when the lag is large, rare samples are scarce, or all-level prediction regret is large. The certificate can be cheap to evaluate and still fail to narrow. This distinction is essential for the frozen benchmark.

This derivation is independent of the numerical diagnostics. It reuses the classical analytic matrix logarithm and standard entropy-variational bound; historical novelty is unverified. Next falsifiable step is a certified evaluation on fixed retained endpoint matrices and deliberately adversarial candidate generators. Do not retune lag design or predictor after examining frozen-data outcomes.
