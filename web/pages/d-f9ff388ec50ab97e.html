711-30-29. LAWRENCE G. LEWIS, University of Utah, Salt Lake City, Utah 84112. A conformally invariant 
boundary using extremal length. Preliminary report. 
Suppose n is any bounded plane domain with euclidean boundary on. Using the notion of a multiple 
transversal, a separating family is defined fo~ each sequence in n which clusters to on. Fundamental sequences are 
those whose separating family has extremal length zero. Equivalence classes of such sequences form a conformally 
invariant ideal boundary. This generalizes Schlesinger's result [Amer. J. Math. 80 (1958), 83-102] to regions of 
arbitrary connectivity. (Received October 31, 1973.)
