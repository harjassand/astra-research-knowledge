EXACT THEOREM AND DEPENDENCIES
For finitely many nonzero vectors nu_e in R^d, S=span nu, consider
xdot=sum_e nu_e w_e^f(t,x), with positive Caratheodory rates, standard local
well-posedness and compact continuation. There must be common continuous
tau_e on [-1,1]^d and epsilon(h)->0 satisfying
  sup_{f,e,t>=0,p in cube}|log(w_e^f(t,h^p))/log h-tau_e(p)|<=epsilon(h).
For EVERY r in the cube, EVERY order minimizer among the relevant edges
{e:r dot nu_e!=0} must have r dot nu_e>0. All faces and ties are included.
Then every positive class (c+S) cap orthant admits one common compact convex
invariant absorber K_P, independent of x0 and f; entry time can depend on
x0 but is uniformly bounded over f. The complete proof is frozen in

STRONG EXPLICIT RATE CLASS AND ITS REAL LIMIT
For ANY weakly reversible graph, or any endotactic actual exponent-arrow
diagram, let
  w_e(t,x)=kappa_e(t)x^{y_e}
             exp[c_e(t)(1+sum_i(log x_i)^2)^{alpha/2}],
where 0<alpha<1, positive kappa_e(t) lie in fixed compact intervals, and
c_e(t) lie in fixed bounded real intervals. Paths may be merely measurable.
The logarithmic distortion is bounded uniformly by
  O(1/L)+C_e(1+dL^2)^{alpha/2}/L ->0, L=-log h.
The factors may diverge to infinity or tend to zero at state extremes and
need not be uniformly comparable to the source monomial by constants.
One common absorber handles the whole rate-path family. alpha=1/2 was
already N173's example; arbitrary fixed alpha in (0,1) follows analytically.

At any FIXED positive state, UV still forces all-time upper and positive
lower rate bounds. It DOES NOT permit arbitrary unbounded temporal
amplitude there. Removing the all-time uniformity is false even for
0<->X: rates w+=exp(-t), w-=x have the right profiles at each fixed time
but x(t)=(x0+t)exp(-t) tends to zero. This is a sharp falsifier to an
attractive overstatement. The optional activity-clock observation is
kept separate and does not enter the main theorem.
