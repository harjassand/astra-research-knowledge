1. EXACT RATIONAL STATIONARY STATES AND A LEGAL KMS GENERATOR

Fix distinct rational t_j in[0,1), j=0,...,n, and set
r_j=(1-t_j)/(1+t_j)>0. Let H have the orthonormal basis
|r> (spectator), |h> (hub), and |a_j> (active levels). Its dimension
is n+3. Put Z=2+sum_j r_j^2 and

 sigma=diag(1,1,r_0^2,...,r_n^2)/Z,
 s=sqrt(sigma)=diag(1,1,r_0,...,r_n)/sqrt(Z).

This is an exact faithful rational stationary-state candidate. Introduce
the Hermitian weighted jump
V=sum_j sqrt(r_j)(|h><a_j|+|a_j><h|).
Define a Hermitian matrix K with zero spectator row/column, no hub-active
entries, and

 K_hh=sum_j r_j^2,
 K_(a_j,a_k)=2sqrt(r_j r_k)/(r_j+r_k).

These satisfy Ks+sK=2VsV by entrywise multiplication. Define the
Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint weighted generator

 -H(X)=VXV-(KX+XK)/2.

It fixes s. Its semigroup is CP on ALL input matrices: the VXV part has
a CP exponential (its power series is a nonnegative sum of compositions),
the negative anticommutator part exponentiates to the CP filter
X->exp(-tK/2)Xexp(-tK/2), and the finite-dimensional Trotter product is
a norm limit of CP maps. This is a legality argument on the full algebra.

Let Gamma(X)=sXs and take the physical Schrödinger generator
L*=Gamma^(1/2)(-H)Gamma^(-1/2), with its ordinary HS adjoint L.
The filtering similarity preserves CP of the entire semigroup. Since
Hs=0 and H=H*, L(I)=0, so the physical dual semigroup is TP.
Also L*(sigma)=0 and Gamma L=L* Gamma. Thus it is a faithful stationary
KMS QMS in the specified convention, not merely an abstract symmetric
operator satisfying necessary inequalities.

There is an even more explicit physical GKSL form. Set

 Q_(a_j,a_k)=(r_j-r_k)/[2i(r_j+r_k)],

with Q zero on all other entries. Q is Hermitian. Then exactly

 L*(rho)=L_jump rho L_jump* -{L_jump*L_jump,rho}/2-i[Q,rho].

To verify the correction, set B=s^(1/2)K s^(-1/2). Its active entries
are2r_j/(r_j+r_k), and (B+B*)/2=L_jump*L_jump, while
