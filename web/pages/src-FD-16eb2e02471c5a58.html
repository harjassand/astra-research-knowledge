DIRECT ROBUST MEDIAN CONTROL: A STRONGER ACQUISITION ROUTE
2026-10-13. Internal self-contained construction. No novelty/priority claim.

Conclusion. The matching gate is valid, but its per-direction body acquisition
is avoidable with a robust median/RM control. Under exactly the same quadratic
bijection, supplied partial-flag-existence and fixed bad-item promises, acquire
each next flagged bit directly from randomized comparison-majority labels.
Then compute its toggle by exact linear algebra. This removes the random
direction-exposure factor 2^r from the flagged-prefix comparison term. It
does not establish uniform practical or all-resource superiority: the noise
gap constants in median sampling and local correction can be substantial.

1. A global expected-error bound for randomized median labels.
Work at a TRUE prefix fiber F of even size L=2t. Let s be odd. To label x,
draw s iid uniform Y in F; for Y!=x use C(Y,x), and for Y=x use an independent
fair coin instead of the strict-order diagonal zero. Return the majority.
The equal-input tie coin is deliberate, internal and costs at most one random
bit per draw. It prevents a small-fiber diagonal bias; equality queries can
be skipped. No true rank or clean-item flag is used.

Let b0,b1 be bad counts in the true next-bit halves, b=b0+b1. For a clean
x with clean predecessor rank j, each randomized comparison has success
probability in
  [(j+1/2)/L, (j+b+1/2)/L].
All s draws/coins are independent conditional on x and the fixed source.
Let F_s(p)=Pr[Bin(s,p)>s/2]. It is increasing, satisfies
F_s(1-p)=1-F_s(p), and is convex on [0,1/2]. The latter follows directly
from F_s'(p)=s*binom(s-1,(s-1)/2)*p^((s-1)/2)*(1-p)^((s-1)/2).

Clean true-zero ranks are j=0,...,t-b0-1. Their errors are bounded by
F_s((j+b+1/2)/L). At most b1 of these shifted grid points are above 1/2;
charge each such error by one. The others are a subset of the ideal lower
half midpoint grid. Clean true-one ranks are j=t-b0,...,L-b-1. At most b0
of these grid points are below 1/2; charge each by one. The others form a
subset of the ideal upper grid. Hence clean expected mislabels are at most
  b + L * e_L(s),
where
  e_L(s)=(2/L) sum_(j=0)^(L/2-1) F_s((j+1/2)/L)
is the no-corruption midpoint-grid error. Convexity gives the midpoint bound
  e_L(s)<=2 integral_0^(1/2) F_s(p) dp
        = eta_s=binom(s,(s-1)/2)/2^(s+1).
For completeness, write s=2a+1 and C=s*binom(2a,a). Integration by parts
and the derivative of p^(a+1)(1-p)^(a+1) yield
2 integral_0^(1/2) F_s = C*4^(-a-1)/(a+1)
=binom(2a+1,a)/2^(2a+2), the displayed eta_s.
Charge at most b more wrong labels for bad x. Thus the mean label error
inside this fiber is at most 2b/L+eta_s. Averaging uniform raw inputs over
all equal-size true prefix fibers gives
  Pr_(uniform x, fresh internal randomness)[median_label(x)!=qnext(x)]
  <=2epsilon+eta_s.
No iid corruption or transitivity among faulty comparisons is assumed.

2. Randomized labels are admissible to the pinned-cube RM corrector.
Set gamma=1/8-2epsilon>0. Choose odd s with eta_s<gamma, for example the
least odd s with eta_s<=gamma/2. Put gamma_med=gamma-eta_s. In a local RM
cube, give every evaluation fresh independent median seeds/coins. For two
distinct nonzero t,u in F_2^l, the raw points x+A t and x+A u are independent
uniform, and their internal median seeds are independent. Thus their error
indicators are independent in the unconditional pairwise calculation, even
if the sampled linear map sometimes has repeated images. The pinned t=0
term is charged by 1/K. Exactly the expectation/variance calculation in
PROOF.txt section 2 applies with rho=2epsilon+eta_s. This is a statement
about the implemented randomized oracle, not an assumed fixed received word.
Choose Kmed=2^l>=4/gamma_med^2, l>=3, and majority repetitions as before.

3. Direct learner, with no random candidate search or matching requirement.
At stage k<h=n-r, use the acquired true-prefix sampler inside every raw
median evaluation. RM-correct at all M=1+n+binom(n,2) global quadratic
anchors and interpolate f. Find v by solving the exact linear equations
  v in S_k, B_f(v,.)=0, f(v)+f(0)=1.
If there is no solution, return UNKNOWN. On a correct interpolation f=qnext,
the original promised next partial-flag direction is a solution, regardless
of which earlier acquired true toggles were selected. Select any solution
and append f,v. The algebraic equations guarantee exact acquired-model
projection/fiber sampling even on bounded bad histories.

After h bits, use the deterministic full-fiber rank-threshold raw labels
of PROOF.txt section 4 for each of the r residual coordinates. Each such
raw label has global error<=2epsilon, needs no own-toggle, and costs L_j-1
comparisons after enumeration/filtering of an acquired 2^r base fiber.

Give every corrected anchor across all n coordinates failure budget
eta_label=beta/(nM). Let R be the least odd integer >=8 ln(nM/beta).
Before the first wrong interpolation all samplers/fibers are true, so each
label has its advertised conditional correction probability. A union bound
over the first wrong anchor gives wrong-or-UNKNOWN probability<=beta.
If correction is true, a promised toggle exists and filtered suffix sizes
are correct, so no other UNKNOWN event is possible. After a first wrong
anchor the algorithm remains capped; no semantic statement is used there.

The deterministic comparison cap is
  Qmedian <= h M Kmed R s
             + M Kdirect R (2^(r+1)-r-2),
where Kdirect>=4/gamma^2 is a power of two, at least eight. For fixed r,
epsilon below 1/16 by a fixed gap, and beta, this is
  O(n^3 log n + 2^r n^2 log n).
This is a proved upper bound, not an executed large-n result. Arithmetic,
random bits, source setup/rank work and conditional sampling remain charged.
The median raw evaluation uses s(n-k) fiber-seed bits plus up to s tie bits,
s projections and at most s comparisons. Local cubes add l*n random bits
per repetition. Stabilizer/toggle linear algebra and dense polynomial work
are polynomial. Residual enumeration and source-free inverse cost 2^r.

4. Compact execution and exact limits.
Use the same basis conversion and compact payload of PROOF.txt section 5:
n^2 basis bits plus Dprefix flagged coefficients and rM suffix coefficients.
This conversion uses no source queries. It does not reduce the nM anchors
already corrected during acquisition. Decode, invert and sample requested
prefixes with zero new source queries; all 2^r arithmetic/search work remains.

At epsilon=1/16, root's adjacent-block construction gives two valid quadratic
partial-flag ideals and the same observed TOTAL ORDER, each explained by a
bad set of density 1/16, with different requested flagged prefix (r>=2;
n>=4 for r=2, and n>=r+1 for r>=3). It is independently replayed in
robust_cut_checks.py. Thus the noise radius is sharp for this uniform family;
it is not a lower bound for every more restricted r/prefix configuration.
