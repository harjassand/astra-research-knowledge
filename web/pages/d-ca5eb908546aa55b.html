C04_S03 EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO UNIVERSAL INSTRUMENT-APPEND ACQUISITION
7 October 2026. INITIAL preserved, no peer exposure.

The strengthened EB-branch theorem does NOT become universal by asserting
that every supplied selfcompatible raw marginal can be represented as its
symmetrized instrument-append construction. This representation assertion
is FALSE even for a tracial qubit. The following proof is exact and uses the
same explicit universal broadcaster already present in the baseline.
It does not refute the universal EB Dirichlet comparison, nor show that the
Petz square lacks every possible alternative factorization.

For d>=2, let F swap the two output factors and P_sym=(I+F)/2. The map
 B(X)=2 P_sym(X tensor I)P_sym/(d+1)
is completely positive. Its trace is Tr X since
 Tr P_sym(X tensor I)=(d+1)Tr X/2. Both marginals are
 C(X)=[(d+2)X+(Tr X)I]/[2(d+1)]
     =lambda X+(1-lambda)(Tr X)I/d,
 lambda=(d+2)/(2(d+1)).
The marginal identity follows by expanding both symmetrizers: the four
partial traces are d X, X, X, (Tr X)I. Thus C is actually selfcompatible,
reference preserving, positive tracial-selfadjoint, with the supplied B.
This is the established universal-cloning formula, not a novelty claim about
cloning or its optimality; optimality is not needed in this obstruction.

Suppose C=(L+Q)/2 where L=sum_y I_y is a CPTP physical instrument total,
M_y=I_y^*(I), and Q(X)=sum_y Tr(M_y X)rho_y is an EB branch using its effects.
Neither central reference restrictions nor a particular preparation choice
is needed for the ensuing contradiction.
Let omega_N=(id tensor N)(|Omega><Omega|) be the normalized Choi state,
|Omega>=d^-1/2 sum_i |ii>, and f(N)=<Omega|omega_N|Omega>.
For every channel f(L)<=1. For every EB channel f(Q)<=1/d: each normalized
separable product state alpha tensor beta has overlap
 Tr(alpha^T beta)/d<=1/d, and mixtures preserve the bound.
For the above depolarizing C,
 f(C)=[1+(d^2-1)lambda]/d^2=(d+1)/(2d)=(1+1/d)/2.
Linearity f(C)=(f(L)+f(Q))/2 therefore forces BOTH bounds to saturate:
 f(L)=1 and f(Q)=1/d.

A trace-one positive Choi state with unit overlap with |Omega> is exactly
|Omega><Omega|. Hence L is the identity channel. The Choi matrices J_y of
its instrument branches are positive and sum to the rank-one Choi matrix
of the identity. Every J_y is therefore supported on that same one-dimensional
range: J_y=alpha_y J_id, alpha_y>=0, sum alpha_y=1.
So I_y=alpha_y id, M_y=alpha_y I, and
 Q(X)=(Tr X) sum_y alpha_y rho_y
is a replacement channel. Its singlet fraction is exactly 1/d^2, for ANY
prepared mixture, contradicting f(Q)=1/d because d>=2.

Consequently the supplied universal raw cloner is outside every such
symmetrized instrument-append representation, including the arbitrary-
preparation and arbitrary-reference generalization proved by this worker.
Merely making the representation search exact does not supply it universally.
Our finite general canonical-measurement search is a different route: it
searches the actual Dirichlet comparator and remains conditional on the
unproved universal comparison. The exact contradiction concerns raw C;
C^dagger C may admit another instrument factorization, which is not excluded
here. No larger CP-range rounding theorem is refuted.

Source attribution: the explicit cloning channel is standard; see Werner,
Optimal Cloning of Pure States, https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9804001 and the
baseline detailed_balance_sos_attack.txt section5. All identities and the
representation contradiction are derived here, without importing optimality.
