# Fresh secretary theorem: compression obstruction is prior-derived

7 October2026. rounding_prior. Outside-quantum portion of cycle18; close this lead without a new allocation. No whole-paper theorem adoption from partial reading.

The fresh primary2609.20797v1, Bérczi–Dughmi–Livanos–Soto–Verdugo, submitted17September2026, states a1/e ordinal secretary algorithm for finite-field linear matroids whose representation arrives online. Its exact invariant is

    E_W dim(W intersect L) <= (1-k/|X|) dim(L)

for EVERY ambient subspace L. Its finite LP construction stores counterfactual distributions mu_Y for every observed subset Y, not merely the actual prefix. Full uncrossing proof was read, along with the invariant/LP/counterfactual recursion and the introduction's computational qualification. The authors explicitly make no polynomial-time implementation claim. Linear representation acquisition from an independence oracle is a separate paid interface, not this online-vector model.

A tempting research-method question was whether it suffices to certify ground-element marginals or only spans of observed subsets. The exact finite control below rejects that simplification.

Over F2^3 let the six observed vectors be integers2,3,4,5,6,7 in binary. Put equal probability on the planes{0,1,2,3}, {0,1,4,5}, and{0,1,6,7}. Each plane is the span of an independent observed pair. The hidden line{0,1} belongs to every plane. At density2/3, all15 observed-generated subspaces pass the invariant, but that hidden line has expected intersection dimension1>2/3. Every observed vector has acceptance-span marginal1/3, yet an unseen vector1 is always blocked. At n=7,k=2, the next prescribed probability1/3 therefore cannot be obtained from this distribution. This is a legal compression-interface counterexample; it is not asserted reachable under the published full LP algorithm and is not a lower bound for all efficient secretary algorithms.

Our six-vector fixture has a different density and an explicit next-arrival control, but it does not add a new obstruction mechanism.

Consequential boundary: any efficient implementation claim must acquire control of hidden ambient subspaces and the counterfactual distribution recursion, rather than replacing the theorem's invariant with observed flats. This is useful for auditing a proposed compressed implementation; it is not a new breakthrough. Strongest control is the original full finite LP at the same revealed representation, with its table/subspace and exact-rational costs paid. Polynomial compression/separation or a sampler for all required counterfactual distributions remains an unexplored separate problem. Historical priority of our obstruction is resolved to prior-derived; this fixture does not justify a fresh team.
