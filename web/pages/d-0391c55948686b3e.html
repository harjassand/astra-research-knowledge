2026-10-09. Owner: quadratic_dilation_sol. No descendants or source edits.

STATUS
The universal finite-C arbitrary-frame gate remains UNKNOWN. This report
proves a counterexample to a precise two-output dilation lemma, not a
counterexample to that gate. The counterexample uses exact matrices and a
legal supplied broadcaster. A smaller unrestricted operator-positivity

1. EXACT INTERFACE AND THE DILATION LEMMA THAT WOULD SUFFICE

For traceless Hermitian A_1,...,A_r in M_d, put L=sum_i A_i^2 and
V=Tr(L)/d. A Hermitian Y is dual-admissible if
  L-Y-2 sum_i x_i A_i+||x||^2 I >= 0             (1)
for every real x. Its objective is tau(Y)=Tr(Y)/d.

For a normalized legal tripartite Choi state R with all single marginals
I/d, BC-swap symmetry and an HS-self-adjoint marginal channel, define
  M_i=(A_i^B+A_i^C)/2,
  H=L_A^T+(L_B+L_C)/2-2 sum_i A_i^T M_i.
The sharp desired gate is tau(Y)<=Tr(RH) for every admissible Y and legal R.

Here is a natural genuinely two-output replacement for scalar
substitution. Given A and legal R, seek ONE POVM {F_z} on BC and real
labels x_z satisfying
  sum_z F_z=I, F_z>=0,
  D_i:=sum_z x_{zi}F_z=M_i for every i,
  N:=sum_z ||x_z||^2 F_z,
  Tr(rho_BC N)<=V, where rho_BC=Tr_A R.        (MD)
One may allow general outcome spaces with finite second moments; all
arguments below use integrals in the same way. By the spectral theorem,
a commuting self-adjoint dilation N_i with an isometric compression
N_i -> M_i yields precisely such POVM first and second moments. The d=3
argument below also permits equality of the first-moment columns on the
reference support. Mere equality of doubly compressed operators is weaker
and is treated separately below. The d=4 refinement has full reference
support and therefore removes this distinction.

Why (MD) would prove the gate: integrate the positive matrix polynomial
(1), with the coefficient matrices on A transposed, against the positive
effects F_z. This gives the positive operator
  K=(L-Y)_A^T+N-2 sum_i A_i^T D_i >=0.
Hence, with the tracial A marginal and D_i=M_i,
  tau(Y)<=V+Tr(rho_BC N)-Tr(RW)<=2V-Tr(RW)=Tr(RH),
where W=2 sum_i A_i^T M_i. Thus (MD) is an actual sufficient lemma for
the intended sharp gate. It is not merely a scalar/Jensen analogy.

The next sections prove that (MD) is FALSE, including its trace-constrained
version for a legal R. It cannot be imported from standard normal-dilation
theorems.

2. EXPLICIT MATRICES AND LEGAL BROADCASTER

Let d=3, with active subspace span{|0>,|1>} and dark vector |2>. Write
  P=diag(1,1,0), D=|2><2|,
  A_1=sigma_x direct-sum 0,
  A_2=sigma_y direct-sum 0,
  A_3=sigma_z direct-sum 0,
where
  sigma_x=[[0,1],[1,0]],
  sigma_y=[[0,-i],[i,0]],
  sigma_z=[[1,0],[0,-1]].
Then L=3P and V=2.

Let |Omega>=(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2), supported on two active subspaces.
On A,B,C define the three normalized vectors
  u=|Omega>_AB |2>_C,
  v=|Omega>_AC |2>_B,
  w=|2>_A |Omega>_BC,
and set
  R=(|u><u|+|v><v|+|w><w|)/3.                (2)
R is positive and normalized. Every register is active in two of the
three terms, with conditional marginal P/2, and dark in one term. Its
single marginals are therefore (2/3)(P/2)+(1/3)D=I_3/3. R is invariant
under permutations of A,B,C, hence under BC swap.

The supplied broadcaster can equivalently be written explicitly as
  B(X)= (PXP)_B tensor D_C/2
        +D_B tensor (PXP)_C/2
        +X_22 |Omega><Omega|_BC.              (3)
Each term is CP and their summed trace is Tr(PX)+X_22=Tr(X); thus B is
CPTP. Its normalized Choi state is exactly (2). Both its marginals are
  Phi(X)=PXP/2 + Tr(PX)D/2 + X_22 P/2.        (4)
This is TP and unital. Its Hilbert-Schmidt bilinear form is
  Tr[X^* Phi(Z)]
   =Tr[(PXP)^* PZP]/2
    +conj(X_22)Tr(PZ)/2+conj(Tr(PX))Z_22/2,
which is Hermitian symmetric, proving HS self-adjointness. In particular,
  Phi(A_i)=A_i/2.
All legal premises of the supplied gate are satisfied, including use of
one joint CP map. No output record or efficient implementation is assumed.

3. THE UNRESTRICTED OPERATOR-POSITIVITY SHORTCUT FAILS

Take Y=2P. It is dual-admissible: the active block of (1) is
  (1+||x||^2)I_2-2 sum_i x_i sigma_i,
whose least eigenvalue is (||x||-1)^2. The dark block is ||x||^2.

On u, the A and B active reduced states are P/2, C is dark, and
  <u|sum_i A_i^T A_i^B|u>=3,
  <u|sum_i A_i^T A_i^C|u>=0.
Consequently <u|H|u>=3+3/2-3=3/2, whereas
<u|Y_A^T|u>=2. Thus
  <u|(H-Y_A^T)|u>=-1/2.                     (5)
BC symmetrizing |u><u| with |v><v| gives the same value. The legal R in
(2) contains both witness components. This establishes that no global
operator inequality H>=Y_A^T, or a supportwise version on this legal R,
can be inferred from scalar-pencil positivity and two output copies.

The trace inequality survives: Tr(RH)=2, tau(Y)=4/3, so
Tr[R(H-Y_A^T)]=2/3>0. The first obstruction uses a nonconstant L and a
nonscalar Y. It does not refute an operator bound for all constant-L,
scalar-Y tuples; that narrower universal question was not proved or
refuted in this worker.

4. EVEN THE LEGAL TRACE-BUDGET MOMENT DILATION FAILS

The BC marginal of (2) is the orthogonal-sector mixture
  rho_BC=|Omega><Omega|/3
         +(P tensor D)/6+(D tensor P)/6.     (6)
Its sector weights are 1/3 on active-active with pure state |Omega>,
1/3 on active-dark with tracial active qubit, and 1/3 on dark-active
with tracial active qubit. There is no dark-dark mass.

Suppose any POVM and labels satisfy the global equations D_i=M_i.
Compressing every F_z to each sector still gives a POVM on that sector.
The same labels continue to represent the corresponding compressed first
moments. Cross-sector entries of the effects do not evade this step, and
the expectation of N in (6) depends only on those compressions.

Active-dark sector. Here M_i=sigma_i/2. Write E_z for the compressed
qubit POVM, p_z=Tr(E_z)/2, and
  r_{zi}=Tr(E_z sigma_i)/Tr(E_z)
for nonzero effects. Positivity gives ||r_z||<=1 (the qubit Bloch ball),
and sum_z p_z=1. The exact first moment equations give
  sum_z p_z x_z dot r_z
    =sum_i Tr[sigma_i (sigma_i/2)]/2=3/2.
Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz therefore yields
  9/4 <= (sum_z p_z ||x_z||^2)(sum_z p_z ||r_z||^2)
       <= sum_z p_z ||x_z||^2.
Thus the conditional second-moment cost is at least 9/4. The dark-active
sector has the identical lower bound.

Active-active sector. For any POVM with D_i=sum_z x_{zi}F_z,
  sum_z (x_{zi}I-D_i) F_z (x_{zi}I-D_i)
    =sum_z x_{zi}^2 F_z-D_i^2 >=0.
Summing over i proves N>=sum_i D_i^2 (Kadison's inequality, here directly
proved). On this sector D_i=M_i and
  M_i=(sigma_i tensor I+I tensor sigma_i)/2,
  sum_i M_i^2=(3/2)I+(1/2)sum_i sigma_i tensor sigma_i.
The EPR expectations of the three tensor products are +1,-1,+1. Hence
  <Omega|sum_i M_i^2|Omega>=2,
and the conditional second-moment cost is at least 2.

Combining these three exact lower bounds with (6), every such POVM obeys
  Tr(rho_BC N)>= (9/4+9/4+2)/3=13/6 > 2=V.  (7)
This is a legal trace-constrained counterexample to (MD). Allowing a
commuting dilation of larger dimension, more outcomes, or continuous
outcomes cannot repair it. The same proof holds under the column
equations (D_i-M_i)Pi=0, with Pi the support projection of rho_BC: the
qubit sectors are still full, and D_i|Omega>=M_i|Omega> still makes the
Kadison bound equal 2. The gap is exact 1/6. We do not assert that 13/6
is the optimum; its strict lower bound is sufficient.

Scope correction that matters. This example does NOT falsify the weaker
doubly compressed equations Pi D_i Pi=Pi M_i Pi. On the active-active
part of Pi, the support is only span{|Omega>}, and the compressed M_i
are zero. Indeed a sector measurement can use qubit effects
  E_(+i)=(I+sigma_i)/6, E_(-i)=(I-sigma_i)/6
with labels +/- (3/2)e_i in the active-dark and dark-active sectors,
and label zero in the active-active sector. These compressed first
moments match, and their reference second cost is
(9/4+9/4)/3=3/2<=2. This does not give the global first moments in (MD).
It demonstrates why this lower bound must not be advertised as a
counterexample to every reference-support-only or correlation-only lift.

5. EXACT CANONICAL SUPPORT AND WHY THIS IS NOT A GATE FALSIFIER

For any pure state in the 3D space let p=<P>. Its active expectation
vector for the three A_i has squared norm p^2 (or at most p^2 for mixed
states). For any pure ensemble averaging to I_3/3,
  sum_z p_z sum_i [Tr(pi_z A_i)]^2
    <=sum_z p_z Tr(pi_z P)=2/3.
An ensemble of active pure states averaging to P/2, with total weight
2/3, plus the dark state of weight 1/3 achieves equality. Thus the exact
unrestricted canonical support is k=2/3, not a finite seed bound.

The exact quantities are
  V=2, k=2/3, v=V-k=4/3,
  s_Phi=1, V-s_Phi=1, Tr(RH)=2.
Hence v/(V-s_Phi)=4/3<2. The conjecturally sharp C=2 gate passes this
example, and the dual optimum Y=2P is already attained. The obstruction
is solely to a proof that requires unbiased first moments M_i and a total
second-moment budget V on the two quantum partners.

6. OPTIONAL REFINEMENT: CONSTANT L AND SCALAR Y DO NOT SAVE (MD)

The first operator witness uses a dark block. The stronger moment-budget
failure also occurs with constant L and scalar dual Y, so the dark block
is not essential to that failure.

Let d=4 and take five Clifford generators
  Gamma_1=sigma_x tensor I,
  Gamma_2=sigma_z tensor I,
  Gamma_3=sigma_y tensor sigma_x,
  Gamma_4=sigma_y tensor sigma_z,
  Gamma_5=sigma_y tensor sigma_y.
They are traceless Hermitian involutions and pairwise anticommute.
Set A_i=Gamma_i, L=5I and Y=4I; (1) is admissible by the same
(||x||-1)^2 calculation. Take the legal depolarizing broadcaster with
R=(I_4/4) tensor (I_4/4) tensor (I_4/4). Its BC marginal is tracial.

For M_i=(Gamma_i tensor I+I tensor Gamma_i)/2, every density matrix on
BC has an M-expectation vector in the unit ball: each local Clifford
expectation vector has norm at most one, and their mean does too.
For any POVM first moments D_i=M_i, put p_z=Tr(F_z)/16 and let r_z be
the conditional M-expectation vector. Then
  sum_z p_z x_z dot r_z
   =sum_i Tr(M_i D_i)/16=sum_i Tr(M_i^2)/16=5/2.
The same weighted Cauchy-Schwarz proof gives
  Tr(rho_BC N)>=25/4>5=V.
This refutes (MD) even under constant L and scalar Y. It does not refute
H>=Y_A^T in that subcase, which is true for this Clifford tuple by its
special star estimate. Neither example refutes any universal finite-C
expectation gate.

7. THE PRECISE CONSTRAINED REPLACEMENT STILL NEEDED

One sufficient and weaker state-dependent route would seek one BC POVM
with arbitrary first moments D_i and second moment N satisfying
  2 sum_i Tr[R A_i^T D_i]-Tr(rho_BC N)
    >=Tr(RW)-V.                              (CR)
The integrated scalar pencil then gives tau(Y)<=Tr(RH). Equivalently,
the conditional states on A produced by that single BC measurement must
have average joint-variance loss at most Tr(RH); refinement to pure
states preserves the tracial barycenter and decreases this loss.

(CR) permits controlled bias and only constrains the actual correlations
with A. Our counterexamples do not refute it. A proof or counterexample
to (CR), or another constraint-sensitive dual certificate, remains open.
No standard dilation theorem checked here supplies it. A completely
different reference-preserving ensemble need not arise from measuring
the given BC extension at all, so failure of (CR) itself would still
require care before calling it failure of the original gate.
