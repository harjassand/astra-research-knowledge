# Deterministic global finite-secant certificate at controlled contrast

Independent mathematical construction, 2026-10-06. This is a conditional perturbative theorem and an explicit nonradial resistor-network instance. It does not establish unrestricted conductivity recovery or novel priority.

## Variational interface

Let V be a real Hilbert energy space. In a PDE take V=H¹(Ω)/R with reference conductivity σ₀ bounded below by α₀>0, inner product (u,v)₀=∫Ωσ₀∇u·∇v. Currents are bounded functionals on V (boundary traces require the usual bounded trace operator on a Lipschitz domain). Let σ_θ=σ₀+∑_{j=1}^d θ_jκ_j for known bounded real κ_j, with θ in a convex compact box K. Define self-adjoint bounded B_j by
(B_j u,v)₀=∫Ωκ_j∇u·∇v,
B(θ)=∑θ_jB_j.
Require |∑θ_jκ_j|≤ρσ₀ almost everywhere, uniformly θ∈K, with ρ<1. Then ||B(θ)||≤ρ and σ_θ≥(1−ρ)σ₀≥(1−ρ)α₀. This is the explicit coercivity constant. Also a certified coefficient β with ||B(v)||≤β||v||₂ is supplied; for example β≤esssup_x sqrt(∑(κ_j(x)/σ₀(x))²).

Reference Riesz coordinates of a current f∈V* give h_f∈V with f(u)=(h_f,u)₀. The solution of the conductivity variational equation is u_θ=(I+B(θ))^−1h_f. Thus the measured current/voltage pairing g(u_θ)=(h_g,R(θ)h_f)₀, with R(θ)=(I+B(θ))^−1, is exactly NtD in energy coordinates. Only h_f reachable from admissible physical boundary currents may be selected: the construction never assumes arbitrary interior energy vectors can be acquired. Normalize ||h_f||₀=||h_g||₀=1. Any restricted boundary access must be reflected in the candidate list and sensitivity Gram matrix below.

For a resistor network V is mean-zero node voltages, reference energy u^TA₀v, B_j=A₀^−1/2L_jA₀^−1/2 on V, and R is the whitened inverse graph Laplacian. Boundary-only networks use harmonic elimination or retain only physically reachable currents; full node access must be explicitly stated.

## Complete uniform normalized-secant bound

For every θ,η∈K and θ≠η write v=(θ−η)/||θ−η||₂ and θ_t=η+t(θ−η). Convexity keeps θ_t in K. Resolvent differentiation gives
DR(θ_t)[v]=−R(θ_t)B(v)R(θ_t).
Since ||R||≤1/(1−ρ), ||R−I||≤ρ/(1−ρ),
||R B(v) R−B(v)||≤||R−I||·||B(v)||·||R||+||B(v)||·||R−I||
≤βρ(2−ρ)/(1−ρ)²=:ε.
The fundamental theorem of calculus in operator norm yields
(R(θ)−R(η))/||θ−η||₂=−B(v)+E_{θη},  ||E_{θη}||≤ε.
This controls every secant at once; it is stronger than knowing a pointwise lower Jacobian rank. No secant net or uncharged global pair minimization is hidden. If desired the explicit second derivative is
D²R(θ)[v,w]=R B(w)R B(v)R+R B(v)R B(w)R,
with ||D²R||≤2β²/(1−ρ)³. The sharper ε above uses the exact resolvent identity and is the bound used in the certificate.

## Finite acquisition design and certificate

Fix N accessible candidate pairs (h_l,k_l), each unit reference energy. Define c_l∈R^d by (c_l)_j=−(k_l,B_jh_l)₀. Choose weights w_l≥0, ∑w_l=1; acquire one scalar m_l(θ)=(k_l,R(θ)h_l)₀ for every positive weight, and retain the weighted data Y_w(θ)=(sqrt(w_l)m_l(θ))_l. Let W(w)=∑w_l c_lc_l^T and s(w)=sqrt(λ_min W(w)). The reference linear secant vector has norm at least s(w); the remainder vector has norm at most ε because each scalar error ≤ε and ∑w=1. Consequently
||Y_w(θ)−Y_w(η)||₂≥[s(w)−ε]||θ−η||₂.
The upper bound is β/(1−ρ)² times parameter distance by the exact derivative formula and the same weights. Therefore s(w)>ε supplies a deterministic global bi-Lipschitz certificate on K with lower constant s(w)−ε and upper β/(1−ρ)². It includes box faces and all crossing coordinate directions.

The finite design SDP is maximize t subject to W(w)≽tI, w≥0, ∑w=1. Certified feasible primal weights give a rigorous lower bound t; an independently verified dual gives an optimum bound. A numerical solver status alone is not a certificate. In any numerical application propagate operator/row errors: if each true c_l differs from its certified approximation by norm ≤δ_l, then the weighted reference map differs in operator norm by at most sqrt(∑w_lδ_l²), which must be subtracted from sqrt(t). Also add certified forward discretization and calibration errors to data uncertainty. At most d(d+1)/2+1 candidates are needed to reproduce any feasible W by convex-hull Carathéodory, but finding them and certifying their currents are charged tasks.

## Explicit nonradial three-edge network

Consider the triangle network on three accessible nodes with unknown positive conductances c_j=1+θ_j, θ∈[−0.1,0.1]^3. All nodes may inject current and measure voltage. Work on mean-zero V⊂R³, and let b₁=(1,−1,0), b₂=(0,1,−1), b₃=(−1,0,1). Reference graph Laplacian A₀=∑b_jb_j^T=3I_V. Hence B_j=b_jb_j^T/3 and ∑B_j=I_V. The parameters vary independently; this is not a common radial scaling family.

Since each B_j is positive semidefinite and |θ_j|≤0.1, −0.1I≼B(θ)≼0.1I, so ρ=0.1 is certified. The coefficient Gram G_ij=tr(B_iB_j) has diagonal 4/9 and off-diagonal 1/9, with largest eigenvalue 2/3. Thus ||B(v)||op≤||B(v)||F≤sqrt(2/3)||v||₂, giving β=sqrt(2/3).

For l=1,2,3 choose h_l=k_l=b_l/sqrt(2) in whitened coordinates. The physical current is f_l=A₀^(1/2)h_l=sqrt(3/2)b_l, and measurement m_l=f_l^TA(θ)^†f_l, where † is inverse on mean-zero voltages. Each current has reference energy f_l^TA₀^†f_l=1. These are three accessible edge-pair effective-resistance measurements with a fixed known scaling.

The row matrix C has diagonal −2/3 and off-diagonal −1/6. Its eigenvalues are −1,−1/2,−1/2. Uniform weights w_l=1/3 give W=C^TC/3 with eigenvalues 1/3,1/12,1/12. Thus s=1/(2sqrt3). The error ε=sqrt(2/3)·0.1·1.9/0.9². The exact positive margin is
c=1/(2sqrt3)−19sqrt(2/3)/81 >0
(the inequality follows by squaring positive quantities: 1/12>722/19683). Numerically c≈0.09715. The upper constant is sqrt(2/3)/0.81≈1.00802. These certify every pair in the full three-dimensional box from exactly three acquired scalars, without a finite-grid surrogate.

Uniform design is SDP-optimal for this candidate set. The symmetric group on three nodes acts by permuting edges, parameters and candidate rows. Averaging any feasible w over the group yields uniform weights; λ_min is concave and orthogonally invariant, so symmetrization cannot worsen the objective. The displayed λ_min=1/12 is therefore the exact optimum. In particular an equally informed competent retaining baseline choosing these same three measurements obtains the same guarantee. Optimization confers no special architectural advantage on this symmetric instance.

## Costs, limitations and discriminating next step

Offline acquisition includes the known reference operator A₀, parameter basis κ_j or L_j, admissible current/output list, certified contrast bound, β, all c_l, and the SDP solve/verification. PDE computations require certified reference solves and trace accessibility; none are free teacher data. Online cost is support(w) scalar measurements, input energy support(w) if each uses unit energy, outputs at specified detector precision, and weighted postprocessing. Weight normalization does not reduce physical acquisition count or input energy. If instead weights scale injected currents/output tests, the experiment and noise model must be restated; identical algebra is not identical acquisition.

This theorem provides no noise robustness at uncharged precision. With weighted data error ≤δ, any recovered parameter whose residual is ≤δ differs from truth by at most 2δ/c. Finding that parameter on a nonconvex forward model has computational cost and is not solved here. Repeated noisy measurements should be designed by a noise-weighted Fisher/Gram objective with their actual energy/count budget, not interpreted as free weights.

No generic advantage against random or deterministic measurement schemes follows. Candidate covariance improvement can only be asserted after fixing the same physical access, energy, noise, offline knowledge and online count. Poor covariance can destroy W's lower eigenvalue; a matched baseline allowed the same SDP removes that advantage. Controlled contrast is decisive: if ε≥s this certificate is inconclusive rather than a disproof of identifiability. Next falsifiable step is a non-symmetric, boundary-only network with accessible finite ports: certify W and β, compare uniform and optimized designs at fixed charged budgets, and check whether the optimized certificate is positive while the competent baseline's is not. Wider boxes require sharper global bounds or other methods; local Jacobian rank is insufficient.
