# Counterexamples, failures, and stopping gates

These cases bound the endpoint result. They are retained even though the affine/ramp identity passes the finite simulator.

## 1. Shared stationary law: no order signal

For a two-state generator with stationary occupancy π and relaxation λ, its duration-τ transition map is

\[
F(x)=e^{-\lambda\tau}x+(1-e^{-\lambda\tau})\pi.
\]

If A and B share π, both maps are scalar contractions toward the same fixed point, so (F_A\circ F_B=F_B\circ F_A) for every choice of λ_A,λ_B,s,t. AB/BA cannot recover their rates. This is precisely why the active mechanism leaves the common-stationary class in the c4 lower bound: the force inputs must move the equilibrium.

## 2. No state information in the common read channel

If (L_0=L_1), or if a chosen scalar statistic has equal conditional means under the two hidden states, then ζ=0 and every endpoint order contrast is zero no matter how large the hidden occupancy effect is. A no-label/no-photon/no-FRET read is not repaired by the pulse design.

## 3. History-dependent detector or dye channel creates a false positive

The proof needs one common (L_0,L_1) after both order arms. Let the hidden chain have identical A/B maps, so the true hidden order contrast is zero, but suppose the AB endpoint read law has (E[f(Y)]=1/2+\epsilon) because that schedule leaves more acceptor blinking/photobleaching, while the BA law has (E[f(Y)]=1/2). The measured contrast is ε even though the hidden dynamics commute. Source-off/no-tether controls may detect some background changes but cannot certify the absence of state-correlated dye-history effects by themselves.

## 4. Opposite-signed kinetic subpopulations break the rate bracket

For classes i, adjacent duration contrasts obey (d_{2,i}=r_i d_{1,i}), but population pooling gives (d_2/d_1=\sum_iw_i r_i d_{1,i}/\sum_iw_i d_{1,i}). If the d1_i signs differ, this is not a convex average. The finite script supplies a two-class example with rates 0.223 and 0.916 s^-1 and signed curvature coefficients +1 and −0.30; its aggregate exponential ratio is 0.742 while the component exp(-lambda*tau) values lie in [0.944,0.986]. A pooled inferred rate would be spurious. The current MAESTRO study reports multiple kinetic classes, so sign alignment requires evidence rather than assumption.

## 5. Hidden class can be exactly invisible

A subpopulation with (C_{1,i}=0) in equation (4) contributes no duration curvature, regardless of its abundance or λ_i. Thus the method cannot guarantee acquisition of every rare state; it detects only classes whose force-loop curvature and read contrast are nonzero. Without a lower response-mass bound, no finite sample gives a uniform lower bound on the invisible class fraction from this statistic.

## 6. Ramp response can mimic duration dependence

If the A-ramp duration/shape changes with the nominal plateau duration s, or if the A loop differs depending on whether it follows B, then the pre/post maps are not fixed and equation (4) can acquire non-exponential terms. A physical force error in pN cannot be converted into an occupancy-map error without a measured rate-versus-force modulus. Record force/position telemetry and estimate loop-specific ramp kernels; if the two order arms fail waveform equivalence, report `UNKNOWN`.

## 7. State-space order failure

The starting-law cancellation is specific to scalar affine occupancy, i.e. a two-state hidden chain. For three or more states, stochastic maps act on a simplex and AB−BA generally remains an initial-vector-dependent map. A common terminal FRET bin can hide such dependencies. Extending the protocol to an HJ open/intermediate-state model requires a new identifiability argument; the two-state formula is not an automatic solution.

## 8. Founder and endpoint budget can be prohibitive

With the illustrative force/FRET scales, a one-duration order-effect test needs around 2,276 molecules per arm under the corrected distribution-free 95% Hoeffding threshold. Estimating a relaxation rate from three duration contrasts with relative uncertainty around 12% costs about 59.8 million independent endpoint molecules per arm (359 million records across six treatment arms), before sign/range pilot, background blanks, force telemetry, and molecule-capture losses. These are conservative bounds, not minimax constants, but they reject a casual claim that the rate ratio is a practical low-sample method. A full photon trajectory/HMM is the mandatory comparator.

## 9. Model validation failure from current HJ evidence

Hohng et al. report force-dependent changes in both directional rates and stationary populations, not a single scalar slowdown. MAESTRO reports multi-state intermediates and multiple kinetic classes under some force geometries. A successful endpoint order effect therefore establishes an assay-level causal response; interpreting it as a two-state λ_A/λ_B estimate additionally requires held-out predictive checks against three-state/heterogeneous alternatives.
