Let G be a finite two-player one-round Boolean game; mu is its arbitrary
correlated question law, d=|A||B|, v=omega*(G)<1. The value is the supremum
over finite-dimensional tensor-product states and arbitrary local POVMs,
with no attainment assumption. For each non-singleton support component,
let P=(P_X+P_Y)/2 act on L2(mu_j), where P_X and P_Y are conditional
expectation projections. Let sigma be the least gap of I-P off constants,
using sigma=1 when all components are singletons. The audited bound is

    Pr[W_k >= ceil((v+delta)k)]
      <= exp[-sigma delta^3 k/(2048(1+log d))],

for k>=1, 0<delta<1-v, every joint finite-dimensional strategy, and its
supremum. For connected support the proof gives denominator 1024.

The root graph is on positive-mass pairs (x,y), adjacent when either local
question agrees. Zero-mass questions may have arbitrary complete POVMs.
No positive atom is discarded. Logarithms are natural.

## 1. Resolvent lemma reconstructed

For T>=0 define F_T h(s)=T(T+sI)^(-1)h, s>0. Spectral integration gives
F_T*F_T=T from int_0^infinity t^2/(t+s)^2 ds=t, including t=0.
For B=sum q_l A_l and D_l=A_l-B, the inverse identity is

    (A_l+s)^(-1)=(B+s)^(-1)-(B+s)^(-1)D_l(B+s)^(-1)
      +(B+s)^(-1)D_l(A_l+s)^(-1)D_l(B+s)^(-1).

Summing cancels its linear term. The integral for f(t)=t log t gives

    sum q_l f(A_l)-f(B)
     = int_0^infinity s sum q_l (B+s)^(-1)D_l
                      (A_l+s)^(-1)D_l(B+s)^(-1) ds.

The difference Gram of F_A_l-F_B has the corresponding integrand with
middle factor s^2(A_l+s)^(-2). Domination follows from
s(A_l+s)^(-2)<=(A_l+s)^(-1), by congruence and integration. If B has a
kernel, positivity and q_l>0 make it a common reducing kernel; both sides
vanish there. On its finite-dimensional support B is bounded below, so
the displayed operator integrals converge in norm.

All effects used at one finite repetition length form a finite collection.
Compressing the span of ALL F_T h into ONE space preserves all cross
Grams and distances; its dimension is at most the number of effects times
the original local dimension. Compressing each effect separately would
be invalid. This correct compression may grow greatly with k; the value
upper bound needs only finiteness, not efficient preparation or a uniform
dimension bound.

For finer effects G_a>=0 summing to T, let V=F_T T^(-1/2), using inverses
only on supp T. Then V*V=Pi_T and

    Q_a=V T^(-1/2)G_a T^(-1/2)V*
          +1_(a=a_*)(I-VV*)

is a complete POVM with F_T*Q_aF_T=G_a. The kernel and T=0 cases are
covered by the completion term. Applying the formula to both local tensor
factors preserves joint probabilities exactly. No quantum sampler is
needed for this fixed-law argument.

## 2. Exact exposure and entropy reconstruction

Fix proper tested C, p=Pr(E_C)>0, m=k-|C|, d_C=d^|C|, L=log(d_C/p).
Order live coordinates once and define Omega_j=(X_C,Y_C,X_<=j,Y_>j).
Under the ORIGINAL question law, a positive-mass flag fixes one question
at each live coordinate. The unfixed local strings therefore become
independent: conditional laws factor over coordinates. This uses product
sampling ACROSS coordinates, not product questions within a coordinate.

At mode i, r0=(X_C,Y_C,X_<i,Y_>i) is independent of the new pair (x,y).
For tested answer strings alpha,gamma define averaged coarse effects
F_x^alpha, H_y^gamma. Put F_y=sum_x mu(x|y)F_x and
H_x=sum_y mu(y|x)H_y. The testing mask theta depends only on
r0,alpha,gamma. With w_i(r)=nu_i(r0)theta(r),

    Psi_xy^r=(F_{F_x^alpha} tensor F_{H_y^gamma})psi,
    Psi_x^r=(F_{F_x^alpha} tensor F_{H_x^gamma})psi,
    Psi_y^r=(F_{F_y^alpha} tensor F_{H_y^gamma})psi.

Their norms satisfy
||Psi_xy||^2=chi(F_x,H_y),
||Psi_x||^2=E_(y|x)||Psi_xy||^2,
||Psi_y||^2=E_(x|y)||Psi_xy||^2.
The conditional transcript/question mass is exactly
w_i(r)mu(x,y)||Psi_xy||^2/p. Weights w_i need not sum to one.
The norm supplies the answer probability; normalizing w_i alone is wrong.

Finer effects G_x^(alpha,a) and K_y^(gamma,b) reproduce the joint answer
mass chi(G_x,K_y), because the remaining local questions factor after
r0,x,y are fixed. Transported POVMs depend only on (i,r,x) for Alice and
(i,r,y) for Bob. These are analysis variables until a question-independent
shared state is constructed below.

Define U_j=E_Omega_j sum theta chi(f(F_j),H_j) and
Z_j=E_Omega_j sum theta chi(F_j,f(H_j)). Both lie in [-pL,0]. For example,
the positive functional A->chi(A,H) has mass g<=1 and h=chi(F,H). Scalar
spectral Jensen gives -chi(f(F),H)<=h log(g/h)<=-h log h. Jensen over
the flag and the d_C answer pairs gives -U_j<=p log(d_C/p).
The zero-mass g=0 case contributes zero.

Changing one flag from Y_i to X_i gives

    U_i-U_(i-1)=E theta chi(sum_x mu(x|y)f(F_x)-f(F_y),H_y),
    Z_(i-1)-Z_i=E theta chi(F_x,sum_y mu(y|x)f(H_y)-f(H_x)).

The resolvent inequality bounds the corresponding squared distances from
Psi_xy to Psi_y and Psi_x. Both changes are nonnegative; each telescope
costs at most pL. Thus, averaging i and writing w_ir=w_i(r)/m,

    sum_ir,z w_ir mu(z)||Psi_ir(z)||^2=p,
    sum_ir,z w_ir mu(z)c_ir(z)<=2p tau,
    sum_ir,z w_ir mu(z)<Psi_ir(z),W_ir(z)Psi_ir(z)>=p sbar,

where tau=L/m, c=||Psi-a_x||^2+||Psi-b_y||^2 and W is the winning
effect constructed from the transported local POVMs. These identities
were independently reconstructed before reading the worker's spectral
note. In particular, effects are averaged before postselection; averaging
under E_C instead would invalidate this telescope.

## 3. Spectral replacement, normalization and legality

On each connected H_j the projections P_X,P_Y are orthogonal in L2(mu_j).
The fixed space of P is their intersection, hence consists exactly of
constants on H_j. The finite gap is positive. Coordinatewise application
of the scalar spectral theorem to a vector-valued f gives

    sigma_j Var_j(f)
      <= (||f-P_Xf||_mu_j^2+||f-P_Yf||_mu_j^2)/2
      <= E_mu_j(||f-a_x||^2+||f-b_y||^2)/2.

The last inequality is the least-squares property of conditional means.
This step needs a common Hilbert codomain but no bound on its dimension.
Summing exposure weights gives E=sum_j lambda_j E_j<=p tau/sigma,
where E_j=sum_ir w_ir Var_j(Psi_ir).

Set beta_ir,j=E_mu_j Psi_ir(z). Define component original norm P_j and
barycenter norm P_hat_j with the same weights. Orthogonal decomposition
gives the exact mass identity P_hat_j=P_j-E_j<=P_j. Thus a zero
barycenter norm has zero accepting mass, and no division by an almost-zero
norm is needed in the comparison.

For P_hat_j>0 use the shared vector

    P_hat_j^(-1/2) sum_ir sqrt(w_ir)|ir>_A|ir>_B beta_ir,j.

It is finite dimensional and independent of the new questions. Alice's
POVM is block diagonal on her own flag, with block Mtilde_(r,x); Bob's
uses his own flag and Ntilde_(r,y). The flag blocks kill all cross terms.
Its success in G_j is T_hat_j/P_hat_j, so T_hat_j<=v_j P_hat_j.
Both players may learn the component from their local input because all
positive pairs sharing a local question belong to that same component.
Tensoring finitely many component strategies proves sum_j lambda_j v_j<=v,
without attainment. The reverse inequality is also immediate, hence in
fact equality holds; only the displayed direction is needed.

For an effect W,
|<a,Wa>-<b,Wb>|<=(||a||+||b||)||a-b||. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives

    |T_j-T_hat_j|<=(sqrt P_j+sqrt P_hat_j)sqrt E_j<=2sqrt(P_j E_j),
    T_j<=v_j P_j+2sqrt(P_j E_j).

Hence sbar<=sum_j lambda_tilde_j v_j+2sqrt(E/p), with
lambda_tilde_j=lambda_j P_j/p. There is no extra linear E/p term and
no second normalization comparison. My first independent expansion used
a weaker linear-error bound; the worker's sharper norm comparison is
correct.

## 4. Disconnected-component drift and constants

Directly from the norm law,

    lambda_j P_j/p=(1/m)sum_i Pr[J_i=j|E_C].

Unconditional J-vector law is lambda^tensor m. Its conditioned density
is at most 1/p, so joint relative entropy<=log(1/p), which bounds the
sum of the marginal relative entropies. Pinsker and Cauchy-Schwarz give
TV(lambda_tilde,lambda)<=sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m)). This drift is necessary:
postselection can increase the frequency of easy components. Omitting it
would be a genuine gap. For connected support it is exactly zero.

The final conditioning error is

    sbar<=v+sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m))+2sqrt(tau/sigma).

With T=sigma delta^2/128 and tau<=T, it is at most
delta/16+delta/sqrt(32)<delta/4. Since T<=delta/4, the source transfer
gives rate delta T/(16(1+log d))=sigma delta^3/(2048(1+log d)).
For connected support T=sigma delta^2/64 gives denominator 1024.

The transfer samples tested indices WITH replacement, so a large tail
selects a fixed tested set with sufficiently high conditional remaining
success and budget below T. Its scalar rate arithmetic checks. At short
k, taking N independent tensor copies gives a finite strategy of length
Nk; N ceil(qk)>=ceil(qNk), and taking N-th roots removes the large-length
restriction. This is valid even if values are unattained or dimensions
grow with N. Thus the cubic power and all-k quantifier survive.

## Attacks, scientific status and next falsifiable step

* Tiny atoms: no minimum-atom term occurs in Poincare; product laws retain
  gap 1/2 even with arbitrarily small positive atoms. Tiny bridges may
  nevertheless make sigma small. No atom is deleted, no universal cubic
  constant without sigma is established, and floating eigenvalues are
  not certificates for a promised gap.
* Disconnected support: labels are locally identifiable; the conditional
  component-frequency drift is charged above. Singleton variance is zero,
  so sigma=1 for an all-singleton law is harmless.
* Dimension: common-span compression is exact for a finite collection.
  It may produce enormous finite dimensions and does not acquire or
  prepare a barycenter economically. Uniform rate is an existence/value
  statement only.
* Finite falsification: own code `check_exposure_independent.py` evaluates
  the analytic cross-Gram kernel ab log(a/b)/(a-b), tests 30 noncommuting
  singular/nonsingular ensembles, and enumerates a 3-coordinate strategy
  with heterogeneous correlated laws and fractional reward masks. Gram,
  fine-answer and mass residuals are at floating error; entropy margins
  are positive. Results are diagnostics, not proof.

Nearest prior art: the resampling connection graph and vector Poincare
step occur in DHVY2017 (arXiv1610.08349v2), Definitions 2/4 and Theorem 6.
That theorem is classical multiplayer all-wins repetition, with a
different rate; it is not this entangled threshold theorem. Specialized
