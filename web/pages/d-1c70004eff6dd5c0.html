For frozen orientations and independent exponential channel loss, the ensemble
survival is S(t)=average_u exp[-Gamma(u)t]. Its initial slope is -Gamma_avg,
while its mean lifetime is average_u[1/Gamma(u)]. Static detuning already has
mean lifetime /T0=1+x^2/3, with T0=1/Gamma0, despite its initial rate following
atan(x)/x. Its worst-orientation lifetime remains T0. Thus the proved vector
advantage is a possible UNIFORM-orientation guarantee, not a demonstrated
order-of-growth improvement in mean molecular lifetime. Other population-loss
channels further restrict any inference from the one-channel calculation.

NO CROSS-SPECIES PERFORMANCE PREDICTION

The measured numbers above belong to different systems. They must not be
combined into an inferred operating field, bandwidth, lifetime or chemical
yield for a candidate material. No same-species d0,kappa pair meeting the
one-channel resonant weak-transfer assumptions was acquired. This missing
calibration, alongside the known-method collision, stops physical promotion.

For a future SAME-SPECIES calibration, let kappa_tilde=kappa/(2pi c) in cm^-1
and s=d0/(2pi c) in cm^-1/(MV/cm). Then the dimensionless numerical table would
convert by E_rms[MV/cm]=x*kappa_tilde/s and
B/(2pi)[THz]=(B/kappa)*kappa_tilde*0.0299792458. These are unit identities,
not measured capabilities. A frequency-dependent Stark response is needed if
the specified bandwidth extends beyond the quasistatic calibration regime.

Vacuum electric energy density alone is at least eps0 E_rms^2/2. If supplied
as a propagating field, RMS irradiance is eps0 c E_rms^2. Stored-field energy
is not dissipated-energy consumption; irradiance is not a near-field power
bound. Field delivery, electrode/antenna loss and vector calibration remain
additional costs. Local field enhancement cannot be silently treated as free.

CONTINUOUS-CONTROL COLLISION

Spherical design curves already give the sphere-moment operation in mathematics.
Continuous unitary designs give analogous universally robust quantum control
with noncommuting full-axis control. Neither supplied a measured frozen
orientation-ensemble IVR sample with known differential Stark slope, adequate
bandwidth and achievable peak field. No broad novelty claim remains supportable;
no same-interface physical advantage was established. The scalar no-go remains
valid in its scalar setting, while the specialized vector model theorem is
positive. Retain both scoped statements rather than flattening them into a
universal no-go or an invention headline.
