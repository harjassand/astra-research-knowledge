Partial-flag extension for a dominant highest weight
Date: 2026-10-08

Scope/status: fixed compact connected G with simple Lie algebra. This note
checks selected fundamental projective lines for a dominant weight with
possibly proper active support I, gives the exact normal Hessian and a
computable positive global overlap constant, and proves injectivity of the
highest-weight coherent symbol on End(V_Lambda). No uniformity across root
systems or across Lambda is asserted.

1. Central quotients and the common stabilizer
----------------------------------------------

Work first on the simply connected compact cover G_tilde with maximal
torus T_tilde and simple roots alpha_1,...,alpha_r. Let

  Lambda=sum_i n_i omega_i,  n_i>=0,  I={i:n_i>0},  J={1,...,r}\I.

For each i in I, let V_i=V_(omega_i) and v_i its highest unit vector.
For a positive root alpha=sum_j c_j alpha_j, its coroot has simple-coroot
expansion

  alpha^vee=sum_j m_j(alpha) alpha_j^vee,
  m_j(alpha)=c_j |alpha_j|^2/|alpha|^2 >=0.

The compact line stabilizer of [v_i] is the connected subgroup generated
by T_tilde and the root subgroups for which m_i(alpha)=0. The Lie algebra
of the common stabilizer of the selected lines is the Levi algebra; the
finite-component argument below verifies that the full stabilizer is
exactly the corresponding connected Levi subgroup

  L_I = < T_tilde, root subgroups with m_i(alpha)=0 for every i in I >,

equivalently the Levi generated by T_tilde and the simple root subgroups
indexed by J. Its Lie algebra consists of t and exactly the root planes
whose coroot support avoids I.

For completeness, the finite-component issue can be resolved without
assuming intersection-of-connected-subgroups is connected. Let H be the
common line stabilizer. Its Lie algebra is l_I by the root-vector test,
and L_I is contained in H, so H^0=L_I. Any h in H normalizes H^0. Since
hTh^-1 is a maximal torus in L_I, multiply h on the left by an element
of L_I to arrange that h normalizes T. Its Weyl element fixes every
omega_i, i in I, because it preserves each selected highest line. The
pointwise stabilizer in the Weyl group of these weights is W_J, the
parabolic Weyl group generated by s_j, j in J: equivalently it is the
stabilizer of the face weight sum_(i in I) omega_i. Representatives of
W_J lie in L_I. Hence h is in L_I and H=L_I exactly; there is no extra
finite stabilizer.

For a quotient G=G_tilde/Z by any central subgroup, every central element
acts as a scalar in each V_i. Therefore the projective actions of the
fundamental representations descend to G even when the linear
representations do not. Since G/L_I is naturally
G_tilde/L_(I,tilde), the map

  G/L_I -> product_(i in I) P(V_i),
  gL_I |-> ([pi_i(g)v_i])_(i in I)                         (1)

is well-defined and has stabilizer exactly L_I. The same root-plane
calculation below shows its differential has kernel zero on the normal
space. Thus (1) is an injective immersion; compactness of G/L_I makes it a
smooth embedding. For I={1,...,r}, this recovers the full-flag embedding.

The line of v_Lambda in V_Lambda has the same stabilizer L_I: its
simple-root Dynkin labels are positive exactly on I. Thus the partial flag
in (1) is also the orbit of the Lambda coherent state.

2. Exact normal Hessian and a global fixed-(G,I) constant
--------------------------------------------------------

Normalize the basic invariant metric so long roots have squared length 2
(equivalently -Killing/(2 g^vee)). For i in I define

  a_i(g)=<v_i,pi_i(g)v_i>,
  F_I(g)=sum_(i in I)(1-|a_i(g)|^2).

Moduli are independent of the lift and of all projective phases. F_I is
left and right L_I-invariant, vanishes exactly on L_I, and descends to the
partial-flag distance d(g,L_I).

The normal tangent space at eL_I is the sum of real root planes for
positive roots alpha whose coroot support meets I. Let X_alpha,Y_alpha be
an orthonormal real basis for a root plane. For
X=sum_alpha(x_alpha X_alpha+y_alpha Y_alpha), direct sl_2 root-string
calculation gives

  F_I(exp(tX)) = c_I(X)t^2 + O(t^3),

  c_I(X)=sum_(alpha>0)
       [ |alpha|^2/4 * sum_(i in I) m_i(alpha) ]
       (x_alpha^2+y_alpha^2)
       = (1/4)sum_(alpha>0)
           [sum_(i in I) c_i(alpha)|alpha_i|^2]
           (x_alpha^2+y_alpha^2).                          (2)

The second equality uses the displayed coroot expansion. For a normal
root, at least one active coefficient c_i(alpha) is a positive integer.
The minimum in (2) is attained at an active simple root, so the exact
smallest local coefficient is

  c_(G,I)= (1/4) min_(i in I)|alpha_i|^2.                   (3)

In particular the normal Hessian is positive definite, proving immersion
and the local quadratic separation. With long-root-squared-2
normalization, (3) is 1/2 for simply-laced types, 1/4 for types with
lacing number 2, and 1/6 for G2, provided the active set contains a
short simple root in the nonsimply-laced cases; if it contains only long
simple roots, use the exact minimum in (3), which is 1/2.

Here is an effective global constant. Set

  B_(G,I)=8 sum_(i in I)||omega_i||^3,
  r_(G,I)=3 c_(G,I)/B_(G,I).

Along a unit-speed normal geodesic exp(tX)L_I, every weight of V_i has
norm at most ||omega_i||, so for A_i=d pi_i(X),
|a_i^(k)(t)|<=||omega_i||^k. Differentiating |a_i|^2 three times gives
|(1-|a_i|^2)'''|<=8||omega_i||^3. Taylor's theorem and (2) therefore yield

  F_I(g) >= (c_(G,I)/2) d(g,L_I)^2
       when d(g,L_I)<=r_(G,I).                             (4)

Let Dbar_G be any computable upper bound on diam(G), hence on
diam(G/L_I); for example, take the largest norm in a bounded fundamental
parallelepiped of the exponential kernel lattice in t. Define

  m_(G,I)=min { F_I(g): d(g,L_I)>=r_(G,I) },

with m_(G,I)=+infinity if the set is empty. Since the selected-line map
is an embedding, this compact far set has strictly positive minimum when
nonempty. Then

  gamma_(G,I)=min(c_(G,I)/2, m_(G,I)/Dbar_G^2)>0             (5)

satisfies, globally,

  sum_(i in I)(1-|a_i(g)|^2)
       >= gamma_(G,I) d(g,L_I)^2.                           (6)

For each fixed root system and I, (5) is computable: the fundamental
representation matrices and F_I are obtained from a Chevalley basis;
the cocharacter lattice gives Dbar_G; distances follow from the compact
group exponential/logarithm and a bounded torus fundamental domain. A
certified adaptive net on G/L_I computes a positive lower bound on m:
F_I has Lipschitz constant at most 2 sum_(i in I)||omega_i|| and distance
to L_I is 1-Lipschitz. Retain cells that can meet the far set, subtract
the Lipschitz mesh error from sampled F_I, and refine. Positivity of the
far minimum guarantees termination. This is a fixed-(G,I) algorithm;
its cost and gamma can deteriorate with G or I. No higher-rank numeric
far-set minimum is claimed here.

The local root constant (3) can be checked from positive-root coefficient
lists using

  c_alpha(I)=1/4 sum_(i in I)c_i(alpha)|alpha_i|^2,

and minimizing over roots with support intersecting I. This reduces to
(3), and is a small finite root-data calculation for every type.

3. Injectivity of the Lambda coherent symbol on all operators
------------------------------------------------------------

Let P_Lambda=|v_Lambda><v_Lambda| and define the covariant symbol

  sigma_Lambda(A)(gL_I)
       =<pi_Lambda(g)v_Lambda,
          A pi_Lambda(g)v_Lambda>,   A in End(V_Lambda).    (7)

It is well-defined on the partial flag since L_I fixes the highest line
projectively. The map A |-> sigma_Lambda(A) is injective for every
dominant Lambda, including singular weights.

Proof by holomorphic polarization. Let G_C be the complexification,
P_Lambda the parabolic stabilizing [v_Lambda] in P(V_Lambda), and
P_Lambda^- the opposite parabolic stabilizing the dual covector line
[v_Lambda^*] in P(V_Lambda^*). The complex orbit

  O=G_C . ([v_Lambda],[v_Lambda^*])
      subset P(V_Lambda) x P(V_Lambda^*)

is open dense: its stabilizer is
P_Lambda intersect P_Lambda^-=L_(I,C), so its dimension is the full
product dimension. The compact orbit G/L_I is a maximal totally real
submanifold of O. For A define the holomorphic section

  s_A([x],[ell])=ell(Ax)

of the natural bidegree-(1,1) line bundle on the product. On the compact
real orbit, s_A is exactly (7). If sigma_Lambda(A) vanishes everywhere,
the identity theorem on a maximal totally real submanifold makes s_A zero
on the open complex orbit, hence on the full product by density. Therefore
ell(Ax)=0 for every coherent vector x in the highest-line orbit and every
covector ell in the opposite dual orbit. Each of these two orbits spans
V_Lambda and V_Lambda^*, respectively, by irreducibility. Hence every
matrix coefficient of A is zero and A=0.

For Lambda=0, V_Lambda=C and the symbol is the constant A; it is injective
trivially. In that case I is empty and the metric bound has no nontrivial
content.

This proves informational completeness of the highest-weight coherent
orbit POVM on End(V_Lambda). It does not by itself give a quantitative
lower bound on the smallest singular value of the symbol map; that is a
separate conditioning problem.
