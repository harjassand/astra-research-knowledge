# Independent check of r12 first derivative extension

7Oct2026. Read `notes/r12_geometry/cycle6/FIRST_DERIVATIVE_EXTENSION.md` and reconstructed its differentiated residual and kernel argument. **PASS in its exact existential interface.** Internal proof audit, not external validation or priority.

The stated bound is |∇V|≤C_K κa=R^((1−5β)/3). Crucial checks:

* Frequency-mismatch derivative is O(a²/R). Compared with κa=a³ℓ/R its ratio is 1/(aℓ)≤1. Partition cutoff derivative costs at most a, since ℓ^(−1)≤a.
* Tangent cross coefficient is h_j=a_j R^(α−1)(x·t_j), with x_j·t_j=0. Its size is O(a²ℓ/R)=κ. Its derivative is O(a²/R)+O(a²ℓ/R²), bounded by κa using 1/(aℓ)≤1 and 1/(aR)≤1. No derivative of the selected direction t_j is taken.
* The origin coefficient K²R^(2α−2) is at most C_Kκ. Its derivative costs 1/R≤a, so it introduces no growing relative error after K is fixed.
* For ε=σ/a, D^jε=O_j(1/(aR^j)), j≤3. Differentiating the exact Laplacian smoothing identity produces its largest commutator at a²E/R, which is ≤κaE because a/R≤κ. The other terms gain further inverse powers of R.
* Third kernel derivatives are O(a^(n+3)) on volume O(a^(−n)); multiplying W=O(κa^(−2)E) gives κaE. The nonsmooth correction W is not differentiated.
* Quotient differentiation is only on the set with |F|≥cτE; on the harmonic neighborhood the potential and its derivative are identically zero. The open-cover gluing already audited in the main proof makes the interface legitimate.

Thus β=1/5 gives bounded gradient, and β>1/5 gives ∇V→0 while maintaining the fixed power p=(4−2β)/3>1. This is stronger decay of the solution than the separate logarithmic all-order construction, but controls only the explicitly proved derivative orders. It does not imply relative ∇V=O(κ/R).
