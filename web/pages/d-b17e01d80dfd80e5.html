# The Gram trace form does not supply a Jordan or invariant-trace structure

7 October 2026, cycle13. **Full independent reconstruction PASS:** `notes/metric_geometry10/cycle13/GRAM_STRUCTURE_INDEPENDENT_BOUNDARY.md`. This is an exact obstruction to importing extra algebraic hypotheses into the cycle12 Gram route. It is not a counterexample to any acquisition detector. Novelty UNKNOWN; the underlying algebra/geometry constructions are established prior.

For f homogeneous of degree4, on H=Hess f invertible, the product

    u star v=12H^-1T(x,u,v,.)

is precisely the positive Christoffel product of the Levi-Civita connection of H: Gamma_u v=(1/2)H^-1 f'''(u,v,.)=u star v. It is unital, with unit x, and H is an invariant form by cubic symmetry. Its Killing-type trace form is

    S(u,v)=tr(L_u L_v), R=H^-1 S.

The cycle12 identities R x=V/2 and Jac(V)=24M-4R-2L_V remain valid. On an H-positive patch S is SPD, but neither invariance of S under star nor the Jordan identity follows.

## Primary hypotheses and exact exclusion

Daniel J. F. Fox, *Commutative algebras with nondegenerate invariant trace form and trace-free multiplication endomorphisms*, arXiv:2004.12343v2 (14 May2020), Section1 equations(1.1),(1.3)-(1.4), distinguishes an invariant metric from an invariant Killing trace form. The latter is an extra condition; even for Jordan algebras the Killing-type form need not be invariant. Our unital product is also not trace-free: L_x=I has trace n. This source was read in the introduction/definitions excerpt, not all58 pages.

Roland Hildebrand, *Hessian potentials with parallel derivatives*, arXiv:1303.7366v1 (29 March2013), Section2 equation(1), Section3 Lemma3.3 and Theorem3.9, obtains metrised Jordan algebras from the difference tensor under a parallel-third-derivative hypothesis. That condition cannot hold for a nonzero homogeneous quartic on any H-invertible patch: put C=f''' and let X denote the radial position field. Homogeneity gives ordinary partial_X C=C, while Gamma_X=I. Hence

    LeviCivita_derivative_X C=C-3C=-2C.

If C were parallel it would vanish; its contraction with X is2H, contradicting invertibility. Hildebrand's difference tensor is -Gamma, which changes this sign convention but not the missing hypothesis. This source was read in the stated definitions/results excerpts, not as a full-paper review. The exclusion is our elementary radial calculation.

## An exact positive-definite fixture falsifies both stronger algebraic properties

Take Q=[[2,1,1],[1,3,1],[1,1,5]], f=(x^2)^TQ(x^2), and x=(1,2,3). Q>0 has leading principal minors2,5,22; H>0 there has leading principal minors76,13728,7443456. Build L from independent fourth differentiation T=f''''/24 and compute S by matrix traces. With e_1,e_2 the first two standard coordinate vectors,

    S(e_1 star e_1,e_2)-S(e_1,e_1 star e_2)
       =19796593217/5462500211328 !=0.

Thus S is not invariant. For u=e_1, the (1,1) entry of

    L_(u star u) L_u-L_u L_(u star u)
       =-2480919/227604175472 !=0

violates the Jordan identity. The check is not an inference from a sweep: one exact witness disproves both proposed universal structural assertions. The runnable discriminator saves the full rational matrices and checks every required input/metric condition, unit and H-invariance identities.

## Consequence and next step

General dense pencil cyclicity must be established without declaring this Hessian product a Jordan algebra or declaring S invariant. Positivity of R and H-selfadjointness of Jac(V), L_V remain useful but do not alone make x cyclic. A next structural theorem would need a property actually forced by the quartic and QPD promise, or a direct spectral argument. The separate dense distinct-residual rank-one witness does this with a diagonal-plus-rank-two multiplication matrix; it invokes neither false property.
