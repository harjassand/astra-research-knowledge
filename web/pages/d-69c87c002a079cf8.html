## Primary developmental mechanism

Stefanie Lehr, David B. Brückner, Thomas George Minchington, Martina Greunz-Schindler, Jack Merrin, Edouard Hannezo, and Anna Kicheva. “Self-organized pattern formation in the developing mouse neural tube by a temporal relay of BMP signaling.” *Developmental Cell* 60(4), 567–580 (2025). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2024.10.024 . Open article/PDF: https://cytoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Self-organized-pattern-formation-in-the-developing-mouse-neural-tube-by-a-temporal-relay-of-BMP-signaling.pdf . Open-access source repository cited by article: https://github.com/dbrueckner/NeuralTubeColonies ; Zenodo snapshot: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13335390 .

Read in the primary article: abstract and main-text summary (PDF pp. 1–2); perturbation windows and first/second pSMAD phases (pp. 3–5); LMX1A/WNT positive-feedback mechanism and measured degradation half-lives (pp. 5–8); model equations and boundaries (STAR Methods, pp. 22–27). The article reports (a) local BMP and inhibitor diffusion, (b) pSMAD activation through a Hill response to BMP/inhibitor, (c) a delayed LMX1A/WNT/endogenous-BMP positive relay, (d) pSMAD half-life `0.43 +/- 0.03 h`, and (e) LMX1A half-life `4.7 +/- 0.5 h`. It also reports that the model is radially symmetric and contains reaction-diffusion for molecular fields; its listed equations do not include stochastic cell motion.
