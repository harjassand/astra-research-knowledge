## Main mathematical investment: exact implicit Schur certification

The receiving result is the supplied sharp two-gap value

\[
 F=Q+\max_{\|z\|_1\le1}\{2h\operatorname{Re}(c^*z)-\alpha z^*Bz\},
 \quad \alpha=pq,\ h=\sqrt{pq(1-p)(1-q)},\ Q=p+q-pq.
\]

For the full independent binary-generator kernel, `C_ST=product_(j in S xor T) eta_j`, `c_S=C_S,empty`, and B excludes the good empty label. The input is m multipliers; the explicit optimization has `2^m-1` coordinates. The kernel and its full-joint physical interpretation are supplied assumptions.

The exact attained dual

\[
 \max f(z)=\min_v\{\alpha v^*Bv+2\|hc-\alpha Bv\|_\infty\}
\]

holds even for singular B and zero alpha. The [short proof](workers/gf_primary_prior/SCHUR_DUAL_AND_PRIOR.md) uses the concave first-order condition at an attained primal optimizer. It is established convex optimization, not a claimed invention. What was missing computationally was evaluating the **full omitted-coordinate residual** without constructing the full sector array.

### Root construction: exact singleton region

The [root proof](root/SINGLETON_SCHUR_CERTIFICATE.md) solves the binding, positive-multiplier singleton restriction by sorted diagonal-plus-rank-one KKT equations. Let its weights be `v_j=(tau eta_j-mu)/(1-eta_j^2)`, sum v=1, and `k=mu/tau`. Active multipliers satisfy `eta_j>=k`, inactive ones `eta_j<=k`. For any subset S the full residual is

\[
 r_S=\alpha\tau\prod_{j\in S}\eta_j
 \left[1-|S\cap A|+k\sum_{j\in S\cap A}\eta_j^{-1}\right].
\]

Coordinate monotonicity on the active region proves that its worst negative value at each cardinality occurs on the largest-multiplier prefix. If those prefix expressions are `G_l`, then

\[
 \|r\|_\infty=\alpha\tau\max\{k,\max_l(-G_l)\}.
\]

This is the exact maximum over every omitted real or complex-l1 direction. The singleton vector is globally optimal **iff every `G_l>=-k`**. Otherwise the same computation gives a valid lower/upper bracket and an improving direction; it does not claim to solve the full optimizer. Costs are O(m log m) sorting and O(m) arithmetic with charged exact encoding growth. No valid positive-multiplier prefix returns UNRESOLVED.

The executable [prototype and receipt](root/SINGLETON_SCHUR_RECEIPT.json) include exact bounded enumeration, the supplied homogeneous m=32 value `4305/8192`, and a new unequal m=128 family certified without enumerating its sectors. ### Complementary worker construction: screening and a general sparse oracle

For a candidate supported on a retained generator set A, tensor factorization proves the exact identity

\[
 \|g_{\rm full}\|_\infty
 =\max\{\|g_{A,\rm bad}\|_\infty,\ \mu_E|g_{A,0}|\},

\]

For any N-sparse candidate, group generators by their N support-bit patterns. There are at most `g=min(m,2^N)` nonempty classes. Holding the other group variables fixed, a residual is `A x+B/x`; its squared modulus is convex in log x. Maximizers occur at all/none group endpoints, with one anchored nonempty class also allowing its largest singleton. The anchor is essential to exclude the good empty label. At most

\[
 2^g-1+g2^{g-1}

actual sector candidates suffice for the **exact** full residual norm. Zero multipliers follow by a fixed-candidate continuity argument; the implementation evaluates original products without division. Complex independent multipliers admit a phase gauge; replacing a general complex kernel by its real part would be invalid.
