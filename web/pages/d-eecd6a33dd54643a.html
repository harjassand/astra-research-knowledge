# Weighted orthogonal-quartic pivot and an exact stationary-search interface

2026-10-07. Root derivation. This is an algebraic proof helper for the unresolved weighted landscape in `notes/r16_proof_methods/WEIGHTED_ORTHOGONAL_LANDSCAPE.md`, not a solution of that conjecture. Its exact implementation passes116 rational pivot cases including nonstationary residuals. Independent universal reconstruction remains separate. Historical novelty is unverified.

## Setting

Let W be a real n-by-n orthogonal matrix and G a real symmetric matrix. Positive definiteness is needed for the proposed landscape consequence, but not for the identities here. Let

    F_G(W)=sum_l m_l^T G m_l,
    M=W entrywise squared, m_l=M[:,l],
    H=G M, S=W^T(W entrywise multiplied by H).

First variation along W exp(tA), A skew, vanishes for all A exactly when S is symmetric. Define sym(X)=(X+X^T)/2. For a chosen entry(i,j), write

    q=W_ij, p=M_ij, e=e_i, f=e_j, x=W^T e,
    m=M[:,j], c=M M^T e,
    z_l=W[:,l] entrywise multiplied by W[:,j],
    A=x f^T-f x^T.

This is an unnormalized rank-at-most-two skew pivot. If p<1, its normalized version is A/sqrt(1-p). The unnormalized form avoids a denominator and is convenient for exact rational arithmetic.

Define the symmetric coefficient matrix

    K_ij = sym[c(m-e)^T]
             +2 sum_l M_il z_l z_l^T
             +2p e e^T +(1-4p)sym(e m^T)-m m^T.       (1)

Then the exact, unconditional identity is

    F_G''(W exp(tA))|0 /4
       = tr(G K_ij) + q f^T(S-S^T)x.                  (2)

In particular, at every stationary W the final term vanishes. The normalized-pivot second variation divided by4 is tr(G K_ij)/(1-p).

## Derivation with the stationarity use exposed

For V=WA and Z=WA^2, columnwise differentiation gives

    F''/4 = 2 sum_l (W[:,l] circ V[:,l])^T
                         G(W[:,l] circ V[:,l])
                 + sum_(k,l) H_kl V_kl^2
                 + tr(S A^2).

Orthogonality gives V=e f^T-W[:,j]x^T and

    A^T A=x x^T+f f^T-q(x f^T+f x^T).

The first term is

    2p G_ii-4p H_ij+2 sum_l M_il z_l^T G z_l.

The second is (1-2p)H_ij+c^T G m. For the last term,

    x^T S x=e^T G c,    f^T S f=m^T G m,
    x^T S f=q H_ij.

Without stationarity f^T S x need not equal qH_ij. Keeping that difference gives precisely q f^T(S-S^T)x. The remaining terms combine into(1). Omitting this residual would incorrectly export a stationary identity to generic W, precisely the kind of invalid curvature test this helper is meant to catch.

For G=I, both c^T G m and sum_l M_il z_l^T G z_l equal(M M^T M)_ij. Equation(1) therefore reduces to the numerator in Jin2607.12431v2 Eq.(8). Our weighted identity does not inherit that paper's positivity argument: mixed G terms need a new bound or a counterexample.

## The gauge disappears exactly

Because M is doubly stochastic,

    F_(G+1a^T+a1^T)(W)=F_G(W)+2 sum_i a_i.

Only the centered contrast metric PGP matters, P=I-11^T/n. Directly from(1),

    K_ij 1=0.                                        (3)

Indeed c and m have sum1; z_l^T1 is the orthogonality indicator l=j. The five contributions to K1 are(m-e)/2,2p m,2p e,(1-4p)(e+m)/2,and-m, which cancel. The stationarity residual also vanishes on the gauge. Thus the stationary curvature question genuinely lives on the(n-1)-dimensional contrast space. Any strict positive contrast metric can be extended to a positive-definite ambient G by an appropriate gauge choice; requiring positivity on the unidentifiable all-ones component is unnecessary for search.

A potential proof strategy is a dual certificate: at a stationary W, find nonnegative pivot weights and multipliers for its linear stationarity constraints so that their combined coefficient matrix is positive on the contrast space. Such a certificate would give an ascent direction for every positive contrast metric compatible with that W. It is a proposed proof method, not a completed universal certificate.

## Executed checks and scientific selection

`work/root_cycle6/weighted_pivot.py` separately differentiates F along W exp(tA) and compares it with(2) using exact rational arithmetic. It tests generic rational reflections with nonzero stationarity residuals, stationary regular-weight reflections, and a Hadamard point with arbitrary positive-definite G. All116 pivot identities and their unweighted specializations pass. `results/root_cycle6/weighted_pivot_checks.json` retains all fixtures and extrema; the checks do not prove the general no-trap conjecture.

r09 and r16 independently found two changes to the search: constant-entry Householder stationary families have a positive aggregate curvature, and generic fixed rational W admits no nonconstant positive stationary metric because the centered stationarity map is generically invertible. Read their full cycle6 proofs before adopting those general claims. Both observations make random fixed-W infeasibility a weak diagnostic, not evidence of a benign landscape. The remaining task is to analyze the actual algebraic stationary locus, derive a universal weighted pivot/dual inequality, or find an exact positive-metric local trap with the full Hessian checked.
