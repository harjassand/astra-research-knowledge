# c_priorart: local and scale certificates

Status: **prior-art baseline established; critical-regime extension unresolved**.

The exact target is a volume-independent computable bound for a specified local/scale observable under an approximate coarse-to-fine Gibbs sampler, including coarse relevant-mode mismatch and finite-step conditional errors. Classical Dobrushin comparison already provides such local bounds under a bounded influence resolvent; block extensions are explicit. Guth et al. ICML2023 already provide hierarchical conditional KL decomposition, conditional Fisher-to-KL, and learning/sampling guarantees under conditional strong log-concavity. Diffusion Stein theory already converts fast coupling into Poisson/Stein sensitivities.

Decisive gap: compute and certify the appropriate influence/resolvent or Poisson sensitivity for actual non-Gaussian coarse-conditioned laws in a critical/frustrated regime. Uniform wavelet covariance is insufficient. Tiny conditional defects can coexist with O(1) local phase bias; an explicit two-spin family demonstrates this and saturates the Dobrushin response factor.

Newest close architectural baseline: arXiv2604.10209v1 (verified submission Apr11 2026), conditional mixtures plus continuous flows plus exact model restriction and multilevel control variates. Its finite-n 'unbiased' language for self-normalized weights fails in a two-point one-sample counterexample. Recent Aug31 WCRG and Aug28 inverse-blocking papers provide empirical conditional decorrelation and thermal-mistuning diagnostics, respectively; neither supplies the targeted rigorous certificate.

Gaussian mass-only MLE plus diagonal spectral transport is a useful checked subclass but an elementary exp-family/calibration baseline. Direct target FFT sampling is the decisive comparator and prevents a computational-advantage claim without a different access interface.
