JOINT MATRIX-ENTROPY SUFFICIENT CONDITION FOR THE EXACT SCALE OBSERVATION
Independent continuation; no peer reads. Complete internal derivation.
Status: CONDITIONALLY USEFUL UPPER; exact priority UNKNOWN; matrix entropy and
Gaussian maximum entropy are established machinery. Not a covariance-only
KLS solution or an acquired statistical memory codec.

THEOREM

Let Y be arbitrary and A(Y)>=0 a measurable real symmetric d-by-d matrix with
M=E A and E tr A<infinity. Restrict to the support of M; A has no component
outside it almost surely. Define the UNNORMALIZED matrix entropy functional
  H(A)=E tr(A log A)-tr(M log M), 0log0=0.
Assume H(A)<infinity; all logarithms are natural. For every cap L>0 let
  f_L(q)=q/(1+q/L), C_T(Y)=I+f_L(T A(Y)),
  Z_T|Y ~N(0,C_T(Y)), with independent Gaussian noise.
Then for EVERY finite d, without commuting matrices, derivative hypotheses,
Poincare, covariance control or log-Sobolev,
  integral_0^infinity I(Y;Z_T)dT/T^2 <= H(A)/2.           (UP)
The numerical constant is independent of L,d and the distribution of Y.

COROLLARY / AUDITABLE JOINT ENVELOPE

If supplied or acquired field information proves
  A(y)<=kappa M almost surely, kappa>=1,
then
  H(A)<=tr(M) log kappa,
  integral I(Y;Z_T)dT/T^2 <=[tr M/2] log kappa.          (ENV)
This is a joint distribution/order condition; the spectrum of M alone does
not supply kappa. For the normalized source field A=R^2, tr M=1.

1. GAUSSIAN ENTROPY AND OPERATOR CONCAVITY

The covariance cap guarantees I<=C_T<=(1+L)I. The Gaussian maximum-entropy
inequality at a fixed covariance therefore gives the exact finite bound
  I(Y;Z_T)<=1/2 [tr log(E C_T)-E tr log C_T].             (G)
No Gaussian assumption is made on the marginal mixture Z_T. Its mean is
zero and its covariance is E C_T; the conditional entropy is exactly the
Gaussian entropy. Gaussian maximum entropy follows directly from
KL(P_Z||N(0,E C_T))>=0 and the matched quadratic expectation.

The cap is operator concave:
  f_L(TA)=L I-L^2(L I+T A)^-1.
For positive definite matrices, E B^-1 >=(E B)^-1. A direct proof is the
variational identity <v,B^-1v>=sup_w[2<v,w>-<w,Bw>], followed by
E sup>=sup E. Hence
  E f_L(T A)<=f_L(T M), E C_T<=I+f_L(T M).
Monotonicity of log determinant in positive matrix order gives from (G)
  I(Y;Z_T)<=G_L(T)/2,
  G_L(T)=tr log(I+f_L(T M))-E tr log(I+f_L(T A)).        (J)
The integrand is nonnegative: log is operator monotone/concave, and its
composition with the operator-concave cap is operator concave. Alternatively
combine Jensen log(E C_T)>=E log C_T with the preceding matrix order.

2. EXACT SCALE INTEGRAL IDENTITY

Put a=1/L,b=1+1/L. Functional calculus gives
  log(I+f_L(T A))=log(I+bT A)-log(I+aT A).
For c>0 define
  F_c(T)=tr log(I+cT M)-E tr log(I+cT A)>=0.
We now prove the exact identity
  integral_0^infinity F_c(T)dT/T^2 =c H(A).             (ID)

It suffices to take c=1 and rescale. The logarithm derivative gives
  F_1'(T)=tr[M(I+T M)^-1]-E tr[A(I+T A)^-1]>=0,
because X->X(I+T X)^-1 is operator concave by the inverse variational proof.
At zero, F_1(T)/T->tr M-E tr A=0 by dominated convergence, since
log(1+T x)/T<=x. At infinity, F_1(T)/T->0 because
0<=F_1(T)<=tr log(I+T M)=O(log T).
Integration by parts, allowing the integral to be infinite, yields
  integral F_1(T)dT/T^2=integral F_1'(T)dT/T.
Substitute s=1/T. The latter becomes
  integral_0^infinity [tr M(M+sI)^-1
                       -E tr A(A+sI)^-1] ds.           (RES)
Every bracket is nonnegative by operator concavity; Tonelli applies.

Finally the scalar functional-calculus representation
  x log x=integral_0^infinity [x/(1+s)-x/(x+s)]ds
holds for every x>=0, with 0log0=0. At finite integration cutoffs, expectation
and trace are legal because E tr A<infinity, and the linear terms cancel
between A and M. Removing cutoffs gives exactly H(A); the negative parts of
xlogx are bounded by 1/e per eigenvalue and the assumed positive expectation
is finite. Thus (RES)=H(A), proving (ID).

Since H(A) is finite, both integrals in (ID) for a and b are finite, and
  integral G_L(T)dT/T^2 = (b-a)H(A)=H(A).
Integrating (J) proves (UP). The cap cancels EXACTLY after integration.
For infinite H(A) the resulting upper is vacuous; one must not subtract two
infinite integrals to infer anything. No infinite-dimensional extension is
silently asserted; growing finite dimensions are uniform in the constant.

3. PROOF OF THE ENVELOPE COROLLARY

Restrict to the support of M. From A<=kappa M, operator monotonicity of log
gives log A<=log M+(log kappa)I whenever A is positive definite. For singular
A use A+epsilon M<= (kappa+epsilon)M, then remove epsilon by functional
calculus/monotone approximation of the entropy expression. Taking the trace
against A>=0 gives
  tr[A(log A-log M)]<=tr A log kappa.
Expectation uses E A=M and therefore
  E tr[A(log A-log M)]=H(A).
This proves (ENV). The restriction to support is sound: for v in ker M,
E<v,Av>=0 with nonnegative integrand, so Av=0 almost surely; a finite basis
removes the null set simultaneously.

4. INTERPRETATION AND RELATION TO THE OBSTRUCTION

There is an exact joint-ensemble decomposition. Let m=tr M>0,
a(y)=tr A(y), define the ENERGY-TILTED law nu(dy)=a(y)mu(dy)/m, and define
rho_y=A(y)/a(y) where a(y)>0. Its average state is rho_bar=M/m. Expanding
log A=log a+log rho_y gives
  H(A)=m[D(nu||mu)+chi(nu,{rho_y})],
  chi=S(rho_bar)-E_nu S(rho_y).
Thus (UP) is equivalently
  integral I(Y;Z_T)dT/T^2 <=m/2 [D(nu||mu)+chi].
This is a joint-field information condition: intensity tilting and the whole
matrix orientation ensemble both count. The matrix rho_y is an auxiliary
mathematical state from the field; it is not a free physical memory register.
For scalar R, chi is zero and the rare-support obstruction is entirely the
relative entropy of the energy-tilted observation law against the original.

The usual source energy already controls the RELATIVE FISHER information of
this tilt, but that is exactly the missing entropy-to-Fisher direction. For
a(y)=tr R(y)^2 and m=E a, Cauchy-Schwarz gives
  |D a|^2/a<=4|D R|_HS^2,
  I(nu||mu)=m^-1 E_mu[|D a|^2/a]
       <=4m^-1 E_mu|D R|_HS^2.
The scalar exact-gap examples therefore have bounded relative Fisher for nu
and unbounded relative entropy. One cannot silently turn this bounded Fisher
into D(nu||mu)<=C without an entropy premise restricted to these tilts.

H(A) uses the COMPLETE random matrix field, including the joint distribution
of eigenvalues/eigenvectors; it is not a function of the Fisher spectrum or
even the average field M. Independent or commuting blocks are not premises.
The rare-support examples can place nearly all tr A mass on rare events
without changing E tr A or the Dirichlet energy. Their entropy diverges.
The tensor lift has M=I_d/d but
  H(A)=E R_scalar^2 log R_scalar^2,
because the -log d terms cancel. A diffuse M therefore does not solve the
entropy acquisition problem. The matrix envelope would need kappa growing
at least like the inverse rare carrier mass in these constructions.

This supplies a concrete sufficient gate in place of the vague suggestion
to extend subgaussianity: acquire or prove a uniform H(R^2) bound, or a field
order envelope R^2<=kappa E R^2 with bounded log kappa. Its proof is independent
of the proposed family093 subgaussian theorem. It is stronger than an affinity
estimate because it controls the additive integrated information directly.
It is NOT known here whether N75's hypothetical small-covariance hierarchy
forces either condition. Covariance/Poincare and first-gap saturation alone
cannot force it, by heated_exact_gap_obstruction.txt.

5. ACQUISITION / PRECISION / MEMORY BOUNDARIES

M, H(A), the envelope kappa and values of A(Y) are not supplied by the Fisher
spectrum. Proving an envelope for a known symbolic R may be feasible in an
admitted compact domain. Statistical certification of a uniform almost-sure
envelope cannot follow from finite samples without a tail/domain certificate.
If 0<=A<=B I and M has a supplied positive minimum eigenvalue m0, evaluation
and sampling can estimate matrix entries and entropy with costs depending on
d, B, log(B/m0), inverse accuracy and source sampler costs. Those bounds are
substantive; no such oracle or implementation is claimed in the KLS setting.
The rare-tail examples explicitly make the field range exponential and
falsify any free entropy/envelope acquisition assumption.

A bounded observation-information upper is not itself an archive codec. The
blind single-draw experiment still needs a positive encoder/decoder, all
retained input-dependent registers count, and known-reference sampling and
finite-bit continuous output costs remain separate. No claim of an acquired
quantum/classical archive dimension follows from (UP) alone.
