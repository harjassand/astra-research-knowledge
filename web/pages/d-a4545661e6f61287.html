2. STRONGEST RESULT: EXPONENTIAL FULL-LAW SPECTRAL AMBIGUITY

Complete theorem and proof: SPECTRAL_PROOF.txt.

Observation contract: independent exact real draws from nu=mu free-convolved
SC_t, with unknown positive noise variance t and unknown signal mu having at
most k atoms in a known compact interval. This is not the joint eigenvalue
experiment of one finite random matrix.

For a,b>0, use the k-point semicircle quadrature
  x_j=2 sqrt(a) cos(j pi/(k+1)),
  p_j=2 sin^2(j pi/(k+1))/(k+1).
The models (delta_0,a+b) and (sum p_j delta_xj,b) have noise variances separated
by a, yet their entire output densities become exponentially close in k.
The explicit proof uses a rational Chebyshev resolvent identity, continuation
of the physical subordination solution, a two-root disk argument that remains
valid at spectral edges, and a uniform density bound. It imports standard
free-convolution subordination, not any unreviewed Astra theorem.

For a=b=1 and k=100, the analytical TV bound is approximately 8.121e-13.
A separate standard-library rational certificate verifies a slightly looser
bound and proves that even 850,000,000,000 independent exact-law draws cannot
estimate variance with error strictly below 1/2 at 75% success under both models.
The estimator may know the two possible models. Unlimited computation does
not change this information lower bound.

This strengthens the initial bounded-height noisy-transform result. Lowering
query height does not repair the positive-noise full-law ambiguity. At zero
lower noise, a discrete-versus-continuous support test already distinguishes
the known pair with one exact draw; that is a stronger baseline than our
initial proposed near-axis query repair. Two independently noisy matrices
sharing the same unknown signal also permit ordinary difference-based variance
calibration, but obtaining those repeated measurements is additional access.

Costs: the explicit k-atom family takes O(k) arithmetic/storage; theorem
constants can be checked without acquiring any samples. Scientific inference
still incurs the proven exponential independent-sample cost. Diagnostics
checked transform identities and subordination numerically; they are not a
numerical proof of the uniform theorem. The displayed850-billion statement
has an additional exact rational arithmetic certificate.

Limitations: the full-law theorem uses an atom-count upper bound, not an exact
count. It does not establish a lower bound for correlated eigenvalues, full
matrix entries, or an experimentally implemented instrument. Exact priority
is UNKNOWN. Prior free-deconvolution work already establishes severe
ill-posedness; no first discovery of that broad phenomenon is claimed.

3. RARE EVENTS: LOW-ORDER MODEL CHECKS DO NOT VALIDATE A RARE TAIL

Complete proof, executable construction and positive comparator:
RARE_EVENT_PROOF.txt.

For P0=Bernoulli(a)^d with 0<a<=1/2, let p=a^d be the probability of all ones.
Using the orthonormal biased product basis, project the point-evaluation
kernel onto degrees above k. Set
  S=sum_{j=0}^k binom(d,j)((1-a)/a)^j,
  K=a^(-d)-S.
When S<=K, a nonnegative alternative law P1 preserves every marginal involving
at most k coordinates but doubles the all-ones probability. Its chi-square
distance from P0 is exactly1/K<=2p. The perturbation minimizes that distance
among all perturbations with the same moment constraints and event change.
A scaled version supplies the stated epsilon-dependent relative-estimation
lower bound. Full-vector acquisition can therefore still cost
Omega(1/(epsilon^2 p)) despite all supplied low-order marginals being exact.

An adaptive assay that reveals at most k coordinates of each fresh specimen
has exactly identical transcript laws under the two models, even with
unlimited specimens. Reusing one specimen until more than k distinct
coordinates are observed changes that contract. A compact exact rejection
sampler for the constructed alternative has acceptance probability 1/2;
polynomial construction of the counterexample is not costly acquisition of
which real-world law holds.

Positive regime and fair comparison: if the full product structure is known
to hold and failure modes are explicitly supplied as a polynomial-size DNF,
empirical product measures plus weighted witness sampling give rarity-free
calibration/computation bounds with all data and arithmetic charged. The core
is established product-form Monte Carlo and Karp-Luby sampling, not a new
learning invention. Validating unrestricted hidden dependence is precisely
the missing information. Conditioning on the rare prefix in the counterexample
would help only if that conditional law could itself be acquired cheaply;
rejection sampling restores the rare-event cost.

Exact replay covers the marginal identities, kernel construction, event
bounds, ANOVA variance identity, and compact sampler. Synthetic calibrated
DNF examples do not establish a new general rare-event capability.

4. QUANTUM SIMULATION: LOW ENERGY DOES NOT REMOVE RARE CONDITIONING

Complete proof summary: GAUSSIAN_CONDITIONING_PROOF.txt; full independent
reduction audit and executable interfaces are in the evidence tree.

A particular family of rational, nonnegative pure Gaussian pair kernels with
operator norm<1/2, total mean photon number<5/12, and only two unheralded output
modes has the following property: efficient arbitrary-accuracy classical
conditional sampling is polynomially equivalent to a general-graph
perfect-matching FPRAS.

Attach two leaves to an edge uv of a graph. Herald one photon in every original
graph mode. The remaining output is 00 or 11, with
  q=R^2 p_uv^2/(1+R^2 p_uv^2),
where p_uv is the uniform perfect-matching edge marginal. Dyadic calibration
keeps the relevant output probability away from zero and one. Estimation and
self-reduction recover the count; approximate counting supplies the converse.
Both directions explicitly guard zero heralds and charge accuracy and bits.

The reduction uses O(n^3 epsilon^-2 log(n/delta)) conditional samples, with an
additional smaller coarse-search term. Its required conditional sampler is
unimplemented. The calibrated physical herald has probability at most
(128/63)4^-n, so literal postselection costs at least (63/128)4^n preparations
per accepted herald. An unconditional variable-subset Gaussian simulation
algorithm is an important existing comparator but does not provide this
conditional guarantee.

OpenAI Math's claimed matching FPRAS was inspected on selected load-bearing
steps, without finding a local flaw. That is not full verification. Its
prescribed replication already exceeds 10^1892 per tier on the smallest audited
profile, so it supplies no useful implementation here. Two independently
written exact finite checkers and a clearly labeled oracle-control test double
passed. No physical experiment or practical matching sampler was produced.

5. POSITIVE CONSTRUCTION: A FINITE EQUILIBRIUM WITNESS, STILL IMPRACTICAL

Complete theorem: EQUILIBRIUM_PROOF.txt.

From the pinned ten-state rational seed Q, construct the discrete-time chain
P=I+Q/2. This changes the target process; it is not sampling the original CTMC.
Its observed six-letter process is reversal invariant, passes every centered
reflection complete-positivity test, and has completely monotone scalar
correlations. A single rational five-letter statistic nevertheless has
nonnegative mean for every stationary reversible hidden Markov model under
the stated deterministic-emission interface, while its target mean is below
-0.00148638878931527.

The mechanism replaces infinitesimal marker reconstruction with exact
one-step features, uses all five markers through a rational least-squares
left inverse, removes an irrelevant constant field component, and sharpens
the geometric rounding bound. The proof retains arbitrary hidden cardinality.
It does not assume that the competitor shares the target's feature invariance.
82 rational seed/geometry checks and all 7,776 words were checked exactly.

A dyadic entrywise upper bound on transition probabilities gives an exact
second-moment enclosure. A distribution-free empirical-Bernstein test then
has type-I error<=2.5% and target power>=97.5% with
  2,057,310,156,600,085,612,368 independent stationary windows.
This requires more than 10^22 observed letters plus independent stationary
preparation. It is not practically adequate. No experiment of that size was
performed. A stronger baseline can also use more observation information;
no superiority to every contemporary thermodynamic estimator is claimed.

For the unchanged original CTMC, a separate variance-aware calculation improves
