1. LEADING INTERPRETATION-SPACE CANDIDATE

Consider a fixed parameter-free binary two-dimensional interpretation program P
with FO plus Härtig initialization/step/halt/output tests, least-equivalence-
closure quotienting, and existential-representative relation semantics. The
candidate supplies an isomorphism-invariant linear-consistency query decidable
in ordinary polynomial time and opposite-answer input pairs of size N. Every
fixed P correctly distinguishing those pairs would require maximum intermediate
universe size S >= exp(c_P N) on at least one input, for all sufficiently large
pairs. The input is included in S; finite runtime is otherwise unrestricted.

The route is coefficient-uniform vector cofilling -> support length linear in
log orbit size -> rank-independent HF transfer -> fixed-width interpretation
encoding. It is not a lower bound for arbitrary computation or for Gaussian
elimination. A growing-width/program family must retain its width dependence.
Qualitative polynomial equivalence to CPT/DeepWL does not, by itself, transfer
this arbitrary-resource quantitative bound.

Exact intermediate statement: for a prime r and uniform collective-cofilling
constant kappa, let H be the face-coordinate central extension and K its central
cycle subgroup. Every H-set point with orbit r^h has a coordinate K-support T
with |T| <= kappa*r/(r-1)*h. “Support” means central flows vanishing on T fix that
point; the atom gadget converts coordinates to ordinary atom supports.

For graph expansion alpha, maximum degree Delta, n base vertices, and fixed
translated program width m=m(P), set
  M=floor(alpha*n/(4*Delta)), C=(Delta+1)*(1+Delta/alpha),
  L_m=max(4,m+1,C*max(2,m)), t=floor((M-1)/L_m).
Every recursively occurring object in the actual state encoding has orbit at
most S^2. The claimed exact consequence, for t>=1, is
  S > r^((r-1)*t/(2*kappa*r)).
For r=3 this is S>3^(t/(3*kappa)); bounded local gadgets give N=Theta(n).

The support mechanism, abstract HF transfer, fixed-width encoding, and stronger
S^2 constituent-orbit estimate passed separate internal first-pass mathematical
checks. A safe explicit width is m(P)=max(7,v_P+1), where v_P bounds distinct
variable names in the fixed program formulas. KOW's formal theorem package
supplies coefficient-uniform vector cofilling for one fixed sufficiently large
construction prime p different from r. In particular r=3 is available.

The result still depends on imported KOW/upstream expansion theorems and the
stated expander-to-gadget base equivalence/homogeneity arguments. The vector
audit tracks the quantifier order and supplies a truncated-ring K_2/stability
check for a compressed source step; it does not re-prove every upstream input.
The HF theorem is explicitly conditional on those base/support hypotheses.
No complete source repository was formally verified here. No quantitative

2. CENTRAL-STATE ENTROPY THEOREM

Let N=2n>=2, all logarithms natural, m_(N,J) the spin-J multiplicity, and P_(N,J)
the full spin-J projector. For an arbitrary probability law p_(N,J), define
  rho_N=sum_(J=0)^n p_(N,J)*P_(N,J)/((2J+1)*m_(N,J)),
  M_N=sum_J p_(N,J)*log(J+1).
For an absolute C_* and every integer k>=1,
  k*[0.5*log(N+1)-C_*-4*M_N]
    <= inf_(sigma in FULLSEP_(N,k)) D(rho_N^tensor k || sigma)
    <= k*log(n+1).
FULLSEP_(N,k) is the convex hull of products across the N original laboratories;
each laboratory may entangle its own k registers. Thus bounded M_N gives a
uniform all-copy Theta(log N) bracket with coefficient between 1/2 and 1.
The report supplies explicit conservative constants and the analytic proof.

The upper bound holds for every central state. Centrality means scalar on each
full spin-J isotypic component, a stronger condition than merely commuting with
collective SU(2). This is a sufficient condition, not a characterization. An
exact leading coefficient and distillation by the N separate laboratories are
not established. The earlier factorial example is a special case.

The singlet logarithmic law, representation-space distillation mechanism,
ordinary bipartite LOCC interpretation, and resulting all-copy FULLSEP lower
bound have substantial prior art. Fixed nonzero (J,M) sectors are also already
treated. The candidate extension is the explicit quantitative low-global-spin
robustness estimate for unflagged, full-irrep-averaged central mixtures and its
original-lab comparator. The targeted literature comparison is not exhaustive
and does not certify novelty.

3. EXACT FINITE CONTACT OBSTRUCTION

For ell_i(z)=log(1+z/i), i=1,...,4, the primitive integer multiaffine polynomial
P4 in contact/extremal_multiaffine_4.json is, up to scale, the unique nonzero
polynomial with its first 15 Taylor coefficients zero. Its contact is exactly
15. It cannot equal det(A0+sum_(i=1)^4 x_i*A_i) over C when each rank(A_i)<=1,
at any matrix size. Consequently it has no read-once determinant expression;
a nonzero determinant in this restricted class has contact at most 14.

Independent exact arithmetic reproduced the fixture, jet ranks, the finite-free
quotient norm 17 mod 101, and the universal Nanson certificate 5 mod 101. The
quotient-unit argument justifies exclusion over C; this is not an inference
from lack of points over a single finite field. Finite fixtures n=2,3,4,5 have
contacts 3,7,15,31. They do not demonstrate a uniform small circuit family.
General determinants, general circuits, and the size-sensitive logarithmic
contact conjecture remain unresolved.

4. WHAT WAS RULED OUT OR CORRECTED

- Scalar cofilling alone yields a quadratic-in-log-orbit support estimate.
  The stronger linear estimate needs simultaneous vector/union-support control.
- Expansion only modulo cocycles cannot silently replace expansion modulo
  gradients. A nonzero H^1 class produces an index-prime small-orbit obstruction.
- In characteristic two, the extension may have exponent four. Preserving the
  alternating commutator alone does not guarantee a center-fixing automorphism.
  A tested shear is a counterexample. The triangular-cocycle query requires
  mu_(e,u,z)=-z+B_e(u,u); the uncorrected witness fails finite checks.
- Bounded Shannon entropy of total spin alone does not imply logarithmic
  entanglement: the normalized maximal-spin projector is fully separable.
  Arbitrary noncentral singlet states also invalidate a universal logarithmic
  FULLSEP upper bound; products of pair singlets provide the obstruction.
- The maximal-contact P4 fixture has no rank-one-variable/read-once determinant
  compression. This is a narrow finite obstruction, not a general circuit bound.
- Any two admitted Gaussian-ensemble channels satisfying the report's norm
  events have entanglement-breaking composition, even with correlated draws.
  A Frobenius separability certificate of 2/9 rules out this proposed escape.
  No universal PPT-composition conjecture is resolved.
