Let H=diag(0,h1,...,hd), hi>0. Let 0<E<=I, E|0>=|0>. Choose energy-ordered upper Cholesky E=T* T, B=T^-1, A=B*B. Then T,B preserve every cumulative lower-energy spectral subspace of H, and their tensor powers preserve total-energy cutoffs. Ground is a direct block1.
For P_n,c=1[sum H_i<=nc], min_{rho=P rho P} Tr rho E^tensor n = ||B^tensor n P_n,c||^-2. This is exact: compressed quadratic form is Gram of the invertible restriction T^tensor n|RanP, whose inverse is B^tensor n|RanP.

Define phi(s)=log ||e^(-sH/2) A e^(-sH/2)|| =2 log||B e^(-sH/2)||, s>=0.
Derived and subsequently audited: g(c)=lim_n n^-1 log||B^tensor n P_n,c||^2 = inf_{s>=0} {phi(s)+sc}, c>=0.

Upper: A<=exp(phi(s))exp(sH). Tensor order implies ||B^tensor n P||^2<=exp(n phi(s)+snc).
Convexity of phi: operator-valued analytic function B exp(-zH/2); unitary imaginary factors give the three-lines norm interpolation. Hence phi convex, nonincreasing, and phi(s)=0 for all s>=s_*=inf{s:A<=exp(sH)}. If E!=I then s_*>0 finite. At s0 top eigenspace W of C_s=e^-sH/2 A e^-sH/2 has eigenvalue lambda=e^phi(s). Restricted derivative equals -lambda P_W H P_W. Thus subgradient interval is [-hmax(W),-hmin(W)]. Any c in [hmin,hmax] is expectation of H in some unit w in W.

Lower at interior dual optimum s>0: choose unit top eigenvector w with <H>=c. Let psi=P_window w^tensor n, with window [n(c-delta),nc]. For positive variance of H under w, iid classical CLT gives q=||psi||^2->1/2; zero variance gives q=1. (Alternative avoid CLT by choose mean c'<c and use full typical window, then continuity in c.) Set Cnorm=C_s/lambda, 0<Cnorm<=I, Cnorm w=w. Since Cnorm^tensor n >= |w^tensor n><w^tensor n|, <psi,Cnorm^tensor n psi> >= q^2. Put y=e^-sHsum/2 psi, which is legal. Then
||B^tensor n y||^2 =lambda^n <psi,Cnorm^tensor n psi> >=lambda^n q^2,
||y||^2 <= exp[-sn(c-delta)]q.
Therefore ||B^tensor n P||^2 >=exp[n(phi(s)+s(c-delta))] q.
Take n->infty then delta->0. At boundary s=0, an active top eigenvector with H-mean<=c exists by dual optimality; if equality, same truncated window argument. If c above maximal level, P=I and g=log||A||. At c=0, ground block gives g=0, agreeing infphi=0. At c>0 dual minimizer exists in compact [0,s_*]. This yields actual n-limit, not limsup, for every c.
