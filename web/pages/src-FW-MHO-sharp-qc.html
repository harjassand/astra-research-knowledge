SHARP ACOUSTIC BOUNDARY: EXACT COMPATIBLE-HEAT CERTIFICATE
15 October 2026

Result
------
For every r in the closed interval I=[-21/8,-5/2], define

  u=a^2+c^2, v=b^2+d^2, j=ad-bc,
  H=(u+v-2j)^2,
  F_(alpha,r)=alpha H + r(r-1)u^2/6 + j^2 + r u j.

Then

  F_(alpha,r) is quasiconvex
    if and only if it is rank-one convex
    if and only if alpha >= alpha_*(r):=3r/[8(r+2)].

The new load-bearing result here is a rational compatible-heat certificate at
alpha=alpha_*(r) throughout I. The previous source certificate required
alpha=3 (or a subtraction improvement of 3/16000), so it did not settle this
boundary. Here alpha_*(r) ranges from 63/40 to 15/8. This is a theorem on the
saturated beta slice above, not the full nine-coefficient invariant cone or
the general planar Morrey problem.

Exact algebra has been checked by a portable SymPy verifier. External review,
formal proof verification, and historical priority remain unresolved. The
proof uses the established heat-flow/exact-two-form method of Bruno and
Pasqualotto; that method is not claimed as a new contribution.

1. Necessity has a direct exact witness
--------------------------------------
Let

  A0 = [[0,(2r+1)/3],[1,0]],   R=e1 tensor e1.

Direct differentiation gives

  D^2 F_(alpha,r)(A0)[R,R]
    = 16(r+2)^2/9 * (alpha-alpha_*(r)).

For r in I, the prefactor is strictly positive. Every alpha below the claimed
floor therefore fails the Legendre-Hadamard condition. This is a concrete
rank-one-convexity obstruction and is independent of certificate feasibility.
It also makes necessity of the quasiconvexity threshold immediate.

2. The two acoustic contacts force the correct Gram support
----------------------------------------------------------
At alpha=alpha_*, write the target-e1 rank-one acoustic form as

  D^2F(A)[e1 tensor (n,m),e1 tensor (n,m)] = vec(A)^T M_r(n,m) vec(A).

The exact determinant is

  det M_r(n,m) = 9 m^4 n^4 r^4.

The original contact is at A=I and (n,m)=(0,1). It has the exact flat curve

  A_old(t)=[[1,-(r+1)t],[(r+1)t,1]],  xi_old(t)=(t,1),

  D^2F(A_old(t))[e1 tensor xi_old(t)]^2
    = (2/3) r(r+1)^2(r+2)t^4.

The sharp floor adds a second contact, at A=A0 and xi=(1,0). An exact flat
curve through it is

  A_new(t)=[[-3(r+1)t/(2r+1),(2r+1)/3],[1,(r+1)t]],
  xi_new(t)=(1,t),

  D^2F(A_new(t))[e1 tensor xi_new(t)]^2
    = 6 r(r+1)^2(r+2)t^4/(2r+1)^2.

The notation [R]^2 in these displays means the repeated Hessian direction
[R,R]; it does not square the Hessian value.

For each curve, apply a target rotation R_theta simultaneously to A and e1,
and form the compatible rank-one second jet

  z=(R_theta e1) tensor xi tensor xi,
  Z=vec(R_theta A) tensor z.

On this jet B1 and B2 are proportional, so every alternating two-form
correction vanishes. For any constant positive input diffusion D, the heat
value is (xi^T D xi) times the corresponding acoustic value and is O(t^4).
If a positive-semidefinite Gram matrix Q represents the corrected heat
density, it follows that QZ(0)=0 and QZ'(0)=0. Target rotations supply three
independent vectors for each of these two orders. The original curve forces
six null vectors; the new curve forces six more. Their combined rank is 12
throughout I.

The certificate C_r is a 24 by 12 basis for the orthogonal complement of this
twelve-dimensional kernel. Its bottom twelve rows are the identity. The
portable verifier checks both acoustic identities, annihilation of all twelve
kernel vectors, and the full column rank of C_r. The earlier source support
was 18-dimensional and only incorporated the first contact.

Restricting to diagonal diffusion loses no possible SOS or pointwise-positive

4. Exact rational choice and continuum positivity
-------------------------------------------------
Set t=8(r+21/8), so t ranges over [0,1]. The rational choice in the reduced
face is

  y0 = -159/10 - 7t/5,
  y1 = 9/4 + 3t/5,
  y3 = 86/5 + 19t/5,
  d1 = 117/100 - 3t/100,
  d2 = 83/100 + 3t/100.

The y labels refer to the free coordinates of the regenerated linear face;
they are provenance only. A verifier need not reconstruct that face because
the resulting C, S, and form coefficients are exported explicitly.

Both d1 and d2 are positive on I and d1+d2=2. The common denominators are

  C_r = C_N(r)/[9(r+1)(2r+1)],
  S_r = S_N(r)/[-(r+2)],
  W_r = W_N(r)/[-(r+2)].

W is the 6 by 10 array of form coefficients, in the explicit pair and
monomial orders recorded in the JSON. The verifier checks the universal
rational identity

  Z^T C_r S_r C_r^T Z
    = d1 D^2F(A)[B1,B1] + d2 D^2F(A)[B2,B2]
      + omega_A(B1,B2),

  B1=(z0,z1,z3,z4), B2=(z1,z2,z4,z5),
  Z=(a,b,c,d) tensor (z0,z1,z2,z3,z4,z5),
  d omega = 0.

Every coefficient is matched symbolically in r, the four A entries, and the
six independent compatible Hessian entries. The verifier also checks the
Euler primitive eta=i_E omega/4, so omega=d eta globally.

The polynomial matrix S_N(r)=-(r+2)S_r has degree five. Under the t coordinate
it has the exact Bernstein representation

  S_N(r(t)) = sum_(i=0)^5 binom(5,i)t^i(1-t)^(5-i) B_i.

All six constant rational matrices B_i have exact LDL factorizations with
twelve strictly positive pivots each. Bernstein weights are nonnegative and
sum to one. Hence S_N and S_r are positive definite for every r in I,
including both endpoints. The denominator -(r+2) is positive there. This is
the continuum positivity proof, not a finite parameter grid.

5. Analytic implication: quasiconvexity at every affine background
----------------------------------------------------------------
Fix r, a constant B, and a compactly supported smooth perturbation phi. Evolve

  phi_tau = exp(tau(d1 partial_xx+d2 partial_yy)) phi,
  A_tau=B+D phi_tau.

Use the relative quartic energy

  E(tau)=integral [F(A_tau)-F(B)-DF(B):D phi_tau].

The closed homogeneous quadratic two-form has the global polynomial primitive
eta=i_E omega/4. Its pullback by A_tau is exact. Derivatives of the heat
perturbation are rapidly decreasing, and the primitive pullback has zero
boundary integral at infinity. Thus its integral vanishes. Integration by
parts and the exact certificate give

  E'(tau) = -integral Z_tau^T C_r S_r C_r^T Z_tau <= 0.

For p=2,3,4, the positive anisotropic Gaussian has the usual two-dimensional
estimate ||D phi_tau||_p <= C tau^(-(1-1/p)) ||D phi||_1. Quartic Taylor growth
therefore gives E(tau)->0 as tau->infinity. Strong L2, L3, and L4 continuity
at tau=0 gives E(0)>=0. The integrated linear term is zero. Strong W1,4
approximation proves the affine Jensen inequality on every bounded Lipschitz
domain for W0^(1,4) perturbations. The usual periodic heat/Stokes argument
also gives the periodic version.

For alpha>alpha_*, add (alpha-alpha_*)H, which is convex. This proves the
claimed exact quasiconvexity threshold. Quasiconvexity implies rank-one
convexity; the explicit negative acoustic witness proves failure below it.

6. Coefficient-sector consequence
---------------------------------
For gamma>0, r=delta/gamma in I, and

  alpha >= gamma*alpha_*(r),

the four-coefficient integrand alpha H+beta u^2+gamma j^2+delta u j is
rank-one convex if and only if it is quasiconvex if and only if

  beta >= gamma*r(r-1)/6
       = delta(delta-gamma)/(6gamma).

Sufficiency follows by positive addition of H and u^2 to gamma times the
sharp certificate. Necessity of this beta condition follows from the source
acoustic curve A_old(t), whose t^2 coefficient is
12 beta-2gamma r^2+2gamma r. This consequence assumes the displayed alpha
bound; it does not say that the bound remains necessary when beta is above
its saturated value. For gamma=0 and delta=0, alpha,beta>=0 are the immediate
convex limiting case.

7. Non-polyconvexity and limits of the result
--------------------------------------------
Every sharp base member remains non-polyconvex. Indeed,

  F(I)=(r+2)(r+3)/6 <= -5/128.

At J=diag(1,-1), H(J)=16 and alpha_*<=15/8. Bounding the remaining terms over
I gives F(J)<=4507/128. Give +/-40I probability 1/[2(1+40^2)] each and +/-J
probability 40^2/[2(1+40^2)] each. This law has mean matrix zero and mean
determinant zero, but its mean F value is strictly negative because

  1600 F(I)+F(J) <= (-8000+4507)/128 <0.

It therefore violates convex Jensen in (A,det A). The law is not asserted to
be a compatible gradient law and is not a quasiconvexity counterexample.

The certificate here proves quasiconvexity at the sharp acoustic floor on I.
It does not prove the same statement for every r<-2 or r>0, exclude every
transformed isotropic decomposition, establish an extreme-ray result, or
prove a uniform pure-fourth-order Jensen constant. Those are separate
obligations. The direct non-SOS contact constraints were also reconstructed
as exploratory work, but no non-SOS obstruction is claimed or needed because
the exact SOS certificate succeeds.

8. Prior art and provenance
----------------------------
The closest primary prior art inspected live is Giuseppe Bruno and Federico
Pasqualotto, Quasiconvexity for the Dacorogna-Marcellini Energy,
arXiv:2608.06367v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1 . Their Lemmas 3-4 supply
the heat-flow and exact-two-form criterion. Their Theorem 2 establishes
rank-one-convexity/quasiconvexity equivalence for quartics with both left and
right rotational invariance. The present family is left invariant and
anisotropic on the right, so direct applicability of that theorem has not
been established. Searches for anisotropic/left-invariant quartics did not
locate a direct primary theorem settling this family; this search outcome
does not prove priority.

The local package sources/astra_compatible_heat_result supplied the family
definition, the older alpha=3 moving-kernel certificate, and structural
context. This sharp certificate was regenerated from the integrand and all
free coefficients. It is not a frozen-source continuation. The unchanged
source package explicitly identified the optimal alpha threshold and the
full moving face as open next obligations.

An additional exact symmetry, potentially useful beyond I, is

  h=(2r+1)/3, K_r=[[0,h],[1,0]],
  F_sharp(A K_r)=h^2 F_sharp(A), K_r^2=h I.

It is an elliptic quarter-turn symmetry, not a proof of full right rotation
invariance. The natural trace-two diffusion suggested by averaging it is
d1=(2r+1)/(r-1), d2=-3/(r-1). The present frozen certificate uses the simpler
affine rational diffusion written above; a universal-r certificate is not
claimed in this report.
