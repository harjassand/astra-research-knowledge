1. Exact same-map interface.
Let sigma=s^2>0. A general positive physical Lindblad generator satisfies
L_*(X)=(CX+XC^dagger)/2-sum K_j X K_j^dagger,
(C+C^dagger)/2=sum K_j^dagger K_j and L_*(sigma)=0.
Set B=s^(-1/2)K s^(1/2), A=s^(-1/2)C s^(1/2). Then
H(X)=(A^dagger X+XA)/2-sum B^dagger X B,
sA+A^dagger s=2Y, As+sA^dagger=2Z,
Y=sum B^dagger sB, Z=sum BsB^dagger.
All antisymmetric physical drift is retained. No Hermitian-noise KMS
representation is used for the nonsymmetric H.

For r=1/p in(0,1), q=sqrt(rho), define t=1-2r and
g=s^(-t/2)q^(1+t)s^(-t/2), h=s^(t/2)q^(1-t)s^(t/2).
The exact requested T_r is Tr gH(h), a real number. Write G(X)=s^(1/2)Xs^(1/2).
For f=s^(-r)rho^r s^(-r), h=G(f), g=G(I_(q,p)(f)). Consequently
E_p(f)=T_r/[2(1-r)] in the stated positive-generator KT convention,
and G(I_(2,p)(f))=q. This is actual SAME-map T_r, not its KMS-adjoint
value. No equality T_r=T_(1-r) is assumed for a fixed generator.

2. Drift potential: derive before state-pair pairing.
Decompose each genuinely non-Hermitian B=sum B_omega under
[log s,B_omega]=omega B_omega. Put delta=nu-omega, m=(omega+nu)/2.
For delta!=0, the stationary equations determine the A coefficients on
B_omega^dagger B_nu and B_omega B_nu^dagger respectively as
 exp(nu)csch(delta), exp(-omega)csch(delta).
These follow either by solving both scalar s-eigenvalue equations or by
using B_omega^dagger sB_nu=exp(nu)B_omega^dagger B_nu s and
B_omega sB_nu^dagger=exp(-nu)B_omega B_nu^dagger s.
The coefficients of A^dagger are obtained by swapping omega,nu.

Since gh=s^(-t/2)rho s^(t/2), hg=s^(t/2)rho s^(-t/2),
the drift in T_r is Tr rho V_t, where
V_t=[s^(t/2)A s^(-t/2)+s^(-t/2)A^dagger s^(t/2)]/2.
Thus its two coefficients are
 exp(m) sinh((1+t)delta/2)/sinh(delta),
 exp(-m) sinh((1-t)delta/2)/sinh(delta).                 (D)
Their continuous values at delta=0 are exp(m)(1-r), exp(-m)r.
For equal s-eigenvalues, stationarity only fixes A+A^dagger, and summed
Y=Z on that entire degenerate block. The above continuous split differs
from the t=0 half/half split by a multiple of that zero Y-Z block; it
is therefore exact after noise summation. The unfixed anti-Hermitian
part commutes with s and contributes zero: Tr A_asym(gh-hg)=0 by cyclicity.
Individual jumps need not satisfy the degenerate-block balance.
