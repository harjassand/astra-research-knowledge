## 1. Exact interface and physical normalization

Fix N<infinity, masses m_alpha>0 and charges e_alpha in R. In physical momentum p let

\[
\epsilon_\alpha(p)=\sqrt{m_\alpha^2+|p|^2},\qquad
u_\alpha(p)=p/\epsilon_\alpha(p).
\]

The equations are

\[
\partial_t f_\alpha+u_\alpha\cdot\nabla_x f_\alpha
e_\alpha(E+u_\alpha\times B)\cdot\nabla_p f_\alpha=0,
\]
\[
\partial_tE-\nabla\times B=-\sum_\alpha e_\alpha\int u_\alpha f_\alpha\,dp,
\quad
\partial_tB+\nabla\times E=0,
\]
\[
\nabla\cdot E=\sum_\alpha e_\alpha\int f_\alpha\,dp,\qquad
\nabla\cdot B=0.
\]

Every f_alpha,0 is nonnegative, smooth and compactly supported in (x,p). E_0,B_0 are smooth, have every spatial derivative bounded, belong to L^2, and satisfy these signed constraints. There is no background charge or external force. Constants need not be uniform in N, masses or charges.

Set

\[
v=p/m_\alpha,\qquad
g_\alpha(t,x,v)=m_\alpha^3 f_\alpha(t,x,m_\alpha v),\qquad
\lambda_\alpha=e_\alpha/m_\alpha.
\]

Then q(v)=sqrt(1+|v|^2) and u(v)=v/q are common to all species,

\[
\partial_t g_\alpha+u\cdot\nabla_xg_\alpha+
\lambda_\alpha(E+u\times B)\cdot\nabla_vg_\alpha=0,
\qquad
(\rho,j)=\sum_\alpha e_\alpha\int (1,u)g_\alpha\,dv.
\tag{1}
\]

The physical kinetic energy is sum_alpha m_alpha int q g_alpha. Every geometric inverse-momentum deficit in the source paper is therefore in normalized v. Source and receiver masses do not insert extra factors into 1-|u|^2=q^{-2} or 1-|a|^2=q_X^{-2}.

The characteristic flow of each species preserves phase volume: multiplying its momentum vector field by the fixed lambda_alpha leaves its divergence zero. Each g_alpha is transported by its own flow and preserves its nonnegative L-infinity bound.

## 2. Positive energy survives signed currents before absolute values

Define

\[
\mathcal U=\frac{|E|^2+|B|^2}{2}
+\sum_\alpha m_\alpha\int qg_\alpha\,dv,\qquad
\mathcal S=E\times B+\sum_\alpha m_\alpha\int qug_\alpha\,dv.
\]

Multiply each normalized Vlasov equation by m_alpha q. Its work term is

\[
m_\alpha\lambda_\alpha\int u\cdot E\,g_\alpha\,dv
=e_\alpha\int u\cdot E\,g_\alpha\,dv.
\]

Summing gives E dot j with its actual signs; Maxwell work is -E dot j. Thus

\[
\partial_t\mathcal U+\nabla\cdot\mathcal S=0,\qquad
|\mathcal S|\le\mathcal U,\qquad
\int\mathcal U=\mathcal H_0.
\tag{2}
\]

This is an exact signed cancellation before any majorization. It requires positive masses and nonnegative distributions, and imposes no charge-sign restriction.

For a backward cone with y=x-rn, r=t-s, put d=1-n dot u and G=|E+n cross B|+|n dot B|. The field part of U-n dot S is

\[
\frac12\bigl(|E+n\times B|^2+|n\cdot B|^2\bigr).
\]

The particle part is sum_alpha m_alpha int qd g_alpha. Hence the same shrinking-ball argument as source Lemma 3.1 gives

\[
\int_0^t\int_{S^2}r^2
\left[G^2+\sum_\alpha m_\alpha\int qd\,g_\alpha\,dv\right]dn\,ds
\le4\mathcal H_0.
\tag{3}
\]

The exact particle and field cone identity is positive because q>0, d>0, m_alpha>0 and g_alpha>=0, not because rho>=0.

## 3. One common unsigned budget, with the full source cost

Let

\[
\Lambda=\max_\alpha |e_\alpha|/m_\alpha,\qquad
F=\sum_\alpha |e_\alpha|g_\alpha,\qquad
F_\infty^0=\sum_\alpha |e_\alpha|\|g_{\alpha,0}\|_\infty.
\]

The two pointwise weight inequalities are

\[
|e_\alpha|\le\Lambda m_\alpha,\qquad
|e_\alpha|\,|\lambda_\alpha|\le\Lambda |e_\alpha|.
\tag{4}
\]

Consequently F<=F_infinity^0,

\[
\sup_s\int qF(s,y,v)\,dy\,dv\le\Lambda\mathcal H_0,\qquad
\int_{\mathrm{cone}}qdF\,dy\,dv\le4\Lambda\mathcal H_0.
\tag{5}
\]

Source-acceleration terms use sum_alpha |e_alpha lambda_alpha|g_alpha<=Lambda F. Receiver force multiplication costs |lambda_beta|<=Lambda. Thus the transport density costs at most Lambda F and source-acceleration density at most Lambda^2 F. No factor has been omitted or declared free. If all charges vanish, the fields are vacuum and the particles ballistic; handle that case directly.

**F generally does not satisfy a one-species Vlasov equation.** It is a positive accounting density; applying the author's one-species theorem directly to F would be invalid. The proof-level transfer uses the disjoint label space

\[
\mathcal Z=\bigsqcup_{\alpha=1}^N\{\alpha\}\times\operatorname{supp}g_{\alpha,0},
\qquad
d\mu(\alpha,z)=|e_\alpha|g_{\alpha,0}(z)\,dz.
\tag{6}
\]

At fixed source time the pushforward of this measure has density F, by phase-volume preservation on each sheet. Retarded changes of variables hold sheetwise and may then be summed. There is no assumption of one characteristic passing through a phase point across all sheets.

For a fixed receiver beta, define its bin measure

\[
\mathsf M_F=\int_{\mathrm{bin}}dt\,ds\,dn\,r^2D
\int F(s,X_\beta(t)-rn,v)\,dv,\qquad D=1-n\cdot a(t).
\]

The same identity holds exactly:

\[
\mathsf M_F=\int_{\mathcal Z}d\mu(\alpha,z)
\int_{\mathrm{hits\ of}\ (\alpha,z)}ds.
\tag{7}
\]

The combined sheetwise Jacobian is d mu ds = D |e_alpha|g_alpha dt dy dv. A same-time collision is a null set on each sheet by the source's time-grid/volume argument, so the union is null under mu. Finite N is used here.

For the source dyads p, theta, phi, sigma, h, normalized u makes the same cap estimate valid:

\[
N_F\le C F_\infty^0\,p^3\nu^2,\qquad
\mathsf M_F\le C\Lambda\mathcal H_0\max(I,h)/p,\qquad
\mathsf M_F\le C\Lambda\mathcal H_0 I\phi^2/\theta^2.
\tag{8}
\]

Assume the signed increment bootstrap simultaneously for every supported characteristic of every species, with common M,A,P. On a stable cell, the stopping argument bounds normalized source and receiver momentum increments by epsilon m omega. Each hitting label therefore has q comparable to p at a common source time. Its total weighted mass is at most C Lambda H_0/p. Monotone crossing bounds each label's hit duration by C h k_1, without any source charge sign.

The common energy budget is particularly clean. Use exactly the source's H^(1)_p,phi and H^(2)_p,phi indicators for this fixed receiver, and set

\[
\mathcal E^F_{p\phi}=\frac1I\int_{J^*}\int qF
(H^{(1)}_{p\phi}+H^{(2)}_{p\phi})\,dy\,dv\,ds.
\tag{9}
\]

At each (s,y,v), the indicators have bounded overlap in p,phi independent of species. The retarded time in H^(2) depends on the receiver and (s,y), and is unique. Therefore

\[
\sum_{p,\phi}\mathcal E^F_{p\phi}\le C\Lambda\mathcal H_0.
\tag{10}

There is no separate free energy budget for each source species. The full common F budget pays their sum. Averaging the weighted mass of hitting labels over full stable cells, with at most two clipped cells treated by (8), gives the same refined estimate

## 4. Exact cross-force identity before absolute values

Fix receiver beta. Its normalized momentum derivative is

\[
K_\beta=\lambda_\beta(E+a\times B),\qquad
a_t=(I-a\otimes a)K_\beta/q_X.
\tag{12}
\]

The second identity has no additional receiver lambda. A neutral receiver has K_beta=0.

For source alpha, the retarded field is linear in e_alpha g_alpha. Let d=1-n dot u, D=1-n dot a, eta=1-a dot u, Q=DI+n tensor a and H=Q/D. Its acceleration is

\[
b_\alpha=u_\alpha'=\frac{\lambda_\alpha}{q}
(I-u\otimes u)(E+u\times B).
\]

The actual cross-force contribution in receiver time/initial labels is lambda_beta e_alpha g_alpha,0 Q mathscrK dt dz. After dt/ds=d/D it becomes

\[
\lambda_\beta e_\alpha g_{\alpha,0}(z)\mathcal K_\alpha\,ds\,dz,\qquad
\mathcal K_\alpha=-\frac Hr\partial_s^{(u)}\frac{u-n}{d}
-\frac{H(u-n)}{r^2q^2d^2}.
\tag{13}
\]

The source lambda_alpha is already in b_alpha in the first term. Adding it outside (13) would incorrectly multiply the transport field by a second source charge.

Write k_0=u-eta n/D. Pure timelike geometry gives

\[
t'=d/D,\quad r'=d/D-1,\quad rn'=(d/D)(a-n)+n-u.
\]

The exact identity is

\[
\mathcal K_\alpha=-\left(\frac{k_0}{rd}\right)'
+\frac{\eta}{r^2D^2}\left(\frac{n}{q_X^2D}-a\right)
+\frac{n(a_t\cdot k_0)}{rD^2}.
\tag{14}
\]

Independent reconstruction: differentiate k_0/(rd) through r,n,u,a using the displayed geometric rates, u'=b_alpha and a'=a_t d/D. Substitute q^-2=1-|u|^2 and q_X^-2=1-|a|^2. Rotational covariance permits n=(0,0,1). The three rational coordinate residuals are identically zero for arbitrary u,a,b_alpha,a_t,r. Likewise D_a k_0[A]=n(A dot k_0)/D has zero residual. These checks do not insert a force law or a charge sign into b_alpha.

Apply (14) labelwise with its signed constant lambda_beta e_alpha intact. It multiplies both original force terms, so it multiplies the whole identity and the transport/source-acceleration cancellation. Only after integration by parts and summation do norms use |lambda_beta e_alpha|. The negative source charge never reverses a required inequality.

Source cutoff rates acquire |lambda_alpha|; after summation their direct majorants are bounded by Lambda F. Receiver cutoff, receiver acceleration, spatial residual and time-weight terms use F with the outer |lambda_beta|. The selected-part cutoff cancellation is applied separately on each species sheet with its common physical multiplier factored out; differentiating the fixed charge constant gives zero.

At a fixed receiver time the coefficient of |K_beta| is at most

\[
C_{\mathrm{data,species}}\,U,\qquad
U=w^{-1}\min\{h^2(p\theta)^3,(p\theta h\phi^2)^{-1}\}.
\tag{15}
\]

For the near half, integrate F theta/(wr phi^2): the radial integral is O(h^2), momentum cap is O(F_infinity^0 p^3 theta^2), and receiver-angle cap is O(phi^2). For the far half, divide that kernel by qdF and use (5), obtaining C Lambda H_0/(wp theta h phi^2). The outer receiver multiplier is |lambda_beta|. Taking the maximum of these two fixed constants yields (15).

The representative selection uses the paper's uncharged pure occupation entries and its W=(P theta)^(-epsilon)phi^epsilon h^epsilon. Physical constants stay outside the pure inequalities. Conditional on its exponent lemma, U<=w^(-1/2)W, so

This pays the square-root-w loss in the absolute force estimate. Charges affect the fixed constant, not the dyadic exponent, selection, or differentiation. The common sum over p,phi in (10) also pays the spatial-transition calculation; no new logarithm follows merely from summing species.

## 7. Decisive obstruction, significance and next falsifier

Their signed rho and j initially vanish; choose a nonzero smooth compact divergence-free E_0 and B_0=0, compatible with rho=0. Then at t=0

\[
(\partial_t+u\cdot\nabla_x)(g_1+g_2)=0,
\]

whereas the positive one-species equation for F=2g would require adding 2E_0 dot grad_v g, which can be nonzero. Such E_0 exists as the curl of a compact smooth vector potential, and g can be chosen in a region where that field is nonzero. The accounting reduction has no autonomous one-species dynamics.
