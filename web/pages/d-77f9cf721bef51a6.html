TWO CONCRETE SCALABLE COUNTERMECHANISMS ATTACKED

1. Covariant large spins, different from the balanced-wedge construction.
A is a qubit, B,C spin j. Select BC maximal spin2j and global total
spin2j-1/2; normalize that projector by its rank4j. Both CMIs equal

and tend to zero as ln(4j)/(4j). Nevertheless the EXACT separable distance

projector witness gives the lower bound and an anticoherent product

Thus this mean-field/covariant limit and its tensor amplification do
not produce the counterexample. I(A:B) stays bounded by the qubit size.
