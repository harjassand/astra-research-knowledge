PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISON: APPROXIMATE BROADCASTING TO ONE COMMON EB DECODER
8 October 2026. Focused literature route; source interfaces and a conditional
synthesis, not a novelty or priority claim.

STATUS

The target remains UNKNOWN: for finite state experiments E_n on growing
finite-dimensional spaces, assume (i) Holevo capacity C(E_n)<=C uniformly,
and (ii) one CPTP map B_n:A_n->A_n tensor A_n has both marginals within
half-trace error b_n->0 of the identity on every rho in E_n. Does there
exist one full-domain EB CPTP decoder Lambda_n with
sup_{rho in E_n} (1/2)||Lambda_n(rho)-rho||_1->0, with a modulus independent
of dimension and family size? The sources below provide exact endpoints,
dimension-dependent finite-extension estimates, and a potentially useful
proof synthesis; none of them alone supplies this dimension-free conclusion.

1. Exact no-broadcasting endpoint: Barnum--Caves--Fuchs--Jozsa--Schumacher

Primary source: H. Barnum et al., “Noncommuting mixed states cannot be
broadcast,” arXiv:quant-ph/9511010 (1995/1996),
https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/9511010v1 . The paper's setup is a physical
channel producing a bipartite output whose two marginals reproduce the
unknown member of a pair {rho_0,rho_1}. It proves exact broadcastability iff
rho_0 and rho_1 commute (the proof reduces equality in fidelity monotonicity
to commutation; see its construction and converse in Sections II–III).
For a commuting family the common eigenbasis measurement and diagonal
preparation give a common EB reconstruction. This is the exact zero-error,
fixed-instance statement. There is no quantitative approximate modulus,
no growing-dimension control, and no Holevo-capacity hypothesis in the
theorem. Compactness at a fixed dimension does not yield a uniform modulus
when dimensions vary.

2. State-extension de Finetti: close to separable, with local-dimension cost

Primary source: M. Christandl, R. König, G. Mitchison, R. Renner,
“One-and-a-half quantum de Finetti theorems,” arXiv:quant-ph/0602130v4,
https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0602130v4 . The exact general statement is
Theorem II.7': if xi^{A B^n} is a state permutation-invariant relative to
the reference A and k<=n output systems are retained, then xi^{A B^k} is
within trace distance 2 d_B^2 k/n of a mixture
sum_i p_i xi_A^i tensor (omega_B^i)^{tensor k}. Their trace distance is
one half of the Schatten-1 norm. The proof purifies the relative-symmetric
state into a Bose-symmetric state after enlarging the purifying space, applies
the symmetric-state result, and traces the purifying system; see Lemma II.5',
Theorem II.7', and the proof at the end of Section II.4. Under the stronger
Bose-symmetric-relative-to-A assumption, Theorem II.8' improves d_B^2 to
d_B. The authors' Theorem II.10 and discussion in Section II.3 show the
k/n scaling is tight for fixed local dimension; the displayed general bound
does not become dimension-free as d_B grows.

This is genuine compatibility-to-separability machinery after a many-output
extension, but it is a state approximation, not by itself a TP EB channel
approximation: the separable approximant need not have reference marginal
I_A/d_A exactly. A direct Choi-to-diamond conversion also costs input
dimension. At n=2,k=1 the general numerical upper bound is vacuous for
growing d_B. A two-output broadcaster can be recursively iterated to obtain
many symmetric output marginals, but the per-leaf approximation error grows
with the tree depth. No bounded-Holevo hypothesis removes the d_B factor in
Theorem II.7'.

3. Measured de Finetti and channel de Finetti: closest quantitative bridge

Primary source: M. Berta, F. Borderi, O. Fawzi, V. B. Scholz,
“Semidefinite programming hierarchies for constrained bilinear optimization,”
arXiv:1810.12197, Math. Programming 194 (2022), 781–829,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12197 and
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10107-021-01650-1 .

Theorem 2.3 is their linear-constraint, measured de Finetti statement. If
rho^{A B_1...B_n} is permutation invariant relative to A and satisfies the
stated linear constraints Lambda_A(rho)=X tensor rho_{B^n} and
Gamma_{B_n}(rho_{B^n})=rho_{B^{n-1}} tensor Y, then its one-copy marginal
rho^{AB} is within raw trace norm

  min{ f(A,B), f(B|.) } sqrt( (2 ln 2) log_2(d_A) / n )

of a mixture sum_i p_i sigma_A^i tensor omega_B^i; each product factor
individually preserves the linear constraints. The proof is Section 2.5:
measure B_1...B_n, use the chain rule to find a conditioning prefix with
small average conditional mutual information, then use an informationally
complete measurement to lift measured closeness back to trace norm. Its
explicit universal distortions are f(A,B)<=18 sqrt(d_A d_B) and the
stronger f(B|.)<=sqrt(18 d_B^3) (Section 2.4, including the stated
operator-space improvement). Thus even its best displayed side-information
route has a local-system factor d_B^{3/2}.

Their Theorem 4.4 is a channel-Choi extension with extra constraints. It
approximates the reduced Choi state by a mixture of product Choi states of
CPTP channels, with raw trace error

  k f(B Bbar|.) sqrt( (2 ln 2)
       [log_2(d_A d_Abar)+(k-1)log_2(d_B d_Bbar)]/(n-k+1) ).

Corollary 4.7 converts this to channel diamond norm with the additional
factor d_A d_B^k. Its hypotheses (equations (16)–(18)) require a channel
acting on n local input sites, permutation covariance across sites, and
no-signalling/linear marginal constraints. That is not a single-input
broadcaster A->B^n: assigning the broadcaster's input to the local A side
violates the corollary's condition that the B-output sites not depend on A;
assigning it to one of the B input sites violates permutation covariance.
So Corollary 4.7 is not a direct channel theorem for the target direction.

4. A conditional synthesis from the Berta et al. proof

The measured-de-Finetti proof does suggest a state-family decoder bound; this
is a derivation from their proof, not a theorem stated in that paper. Let E
be a finite d-dimensional experiment, C_2 its maximum Holevo information in
bits over all priors, and B:A->A tensor A a two-output broadcaster whose
individual marginals have uniform half-trace error b on E. Iterate B in a
binary tree of depth s and symmetrize the 2^s leaves. For every rho in E,
each leaf marginal is within half-trace error s b of rho, by trace-norm
contractivity at each tree level.

Fix any prior p on E and let X be its classical label. The output state
omega_{X B_1...B_N}, N=2^s, is permutation invariant relative to X. Holevo
data processing gives

  I(X:B_1...B_N) <= chi_p(E) <= C_2.

In the proof of Berta et al. Theorem 2.3, the only use of log_2(d_A) in the
chain-rule step is the upper bound on I(A:Z_1...Z_N). With A=X classical,
that step can be replaced by I(X:Z_1...Z_N)<=C_2 for the measured output
registers Z, again by data processing. The rest of that proof gives an
expected conditional *trace-norm* decoupling bound at scale
f_d sqrt(2 ln 2 C_2/N), where the same informationally complete POVM on
one output system has distortion f_d<=sqrt(18 d^3). Measuring the selected
prefix outputs and preparing the conditional average state of one retained
output defines a legitimate prior-dependent EB CPTP map on the original
input: it is the composition of the full-domain tree broadcaster, a POVM,
and state preparation. The prefix outcome is a classical register, so the
decoder is EB on the full input algebra. Adding the retained-output
approximation error yields

  inf_{EB Lambda} sum_x p_x T(Lambda(rho_x),rho_x)
       <= s b + (f_d/2) sqrt(2 ln 2 C_2 / 2^s),

where T=||.||_1/2. The factor 1/2 converts the source's raw trace norm to
half trace distance.

For finite E, the EB CPTP maps form a compact convex set and
g(Lambda,p)=sum_x p_x T(Lambda(rho_x),rho_x) is convex in Lambda and linear
in p. Sion's minimax theorem therefore gives

  inf_{EB Lambda} max_x T(Lambda(rho_x),rho_x)
       = max_p inf_{EB Lambda} sum_x p_x T(Lambda(rho_x),rho_x).

If the displayed prior-specific bound is uniform over p, minimax yields one
common full-domain EB decoder, not a different decoder for each label or
prior. Taking 2^s >= 18 d^3 max{C_2,1}/b makes the second term O(sqrt b),
while s=O(log(d^3 max{C_2,1}/b)); thus a sufficient condition is
b log(d^3/b)->0 (for bounded C_2). This is a valid dimension-dependent
route if all details of the measured-conditioning implementation are kept;
it does not meet the target when d may grow so fast that b log d fails to
vanish. The load-bearing missing estimate is an experiment-sensitive or
capacity-controlled replacement for f_d that stays bounded (or grows slowly
enough) uniformly over all priors and all posterior conditional state
differences. The literature checked here does not provide it.

The minimax placement above is legitimate because the decoder set is convex
and the payoff is convex in the decoder and affine in the prior. It is not
the invalid interchange of a measurement infimum with an ensemble average
inside a convex-roof formula. The common decoder follows only after a
uniform-in-prior average-error theorem has been proved.

5. Why bounded Holevo does not supply a trace-covering bound by itself

For m>=2, use the diagonal states on basis x=(x_1,...,x_m) in {+1,-1}^m
with probabilities

  P_i(x)=2^{-m}(1+a x_i),  i=1,...,m,   0<a<1.

They have pairwise trace distance T(P_i,P_j)=a/2. Hence for any cover radius
r<a/4, the family needs at least m balls (no ball can contain two of these
states). Nevertheless its Holevo capacity is bounded by the constant

  C(E_m) = sup_p chi_p(E_m) <=
      (1/2)[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)],

independent of m. Indeed with u the uniform distribution,
chi_p(E_m)=sum_i p_i D(P_i||u)-D(sum_i p_i P_i||u), and each first term is
the displayed constant. These states commute and one dephasing channel
reconstructs them exactly, so this is not a counterexample to broadcasting
implies EB. It does rule out deriving a uniform trace-net cardinality from
the capacity bound alone, and explains why N46's finite-cover sufficient
condition cannot be invoked solely from C<=C_0.

6. Internal N46 interface, kept separate from external prior art

The current programme card work/astra_prior/cards/N46-compact-quantum-
classicality.txt states a stronger exact family theorem under a different
complexity control: for uniformly trace-total-bounded experiments with
trace-covering numbers N_n(a), a common EB decoder exists if
b_n log N_n(a_n)->0 for a_n->0. It also states a finite-family bound

  e <= inf_{L>=0} {L b + sqrt(m log(m)/(2*2^L))}.

This is directly aligned on channel direction, state-family worst-case
error, and one common full-domain EB decoder. However its provenance in
this checkout is an internal source-derived package (GENERAL_CRITERION.md
hash d-fc53e2ac7ba8887c), not a public primary-source theorem inspected in
this comparison. The diagonal example above shows why capacity alone does
not imply its net hypothesis. The Berta-proof synthesis may provide another
dimension-dependent sufficient condition, but does not bridge that gap.

COMPARISON RESULT

Barnum et al. give the exact commuting iff broadcastable endpoint.
Christandl et al. give full trace-norm state de Finetti with d_B^2 k/n
(d_B k/n under Bose symmetry) after an n-extension. Berta et al. give a
closer measured-information route and channel-level de Finetti under a
different multi-input/no-signalling channel interface; their global trace
lift costs at least the displayed d_B^{3/2} distortion, and their channel
corollary adds Choi/diamond dimension factors. Replacing their label entropy
by bounded Holevo information does work in the chain-rule portion for any
prior, and minimax can then produce one common decoder, but the trace
distortion remains dimension-sensitive. No source checked here establishes
dimension-free bounded-Holevo two-output broadcasting to one common EB
decoder. Priority and the general theorem itself remain UNKNOWN.
