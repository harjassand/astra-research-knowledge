Long arc conversions are terms in a signed identity, not asserted legal one-step transitions. Rare guide-event probabilities are carried explicitly and canceled via conditional Jensen, rather than being silently bounded below. The unit-activity bottom law is acquired by a tree dynamic program. Clamped weight tiers and finite-bit choices are charged before counting.
