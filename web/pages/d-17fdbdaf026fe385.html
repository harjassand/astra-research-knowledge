RESULT

The original conditional entropy-throughput theorem remains as stated in the
immutable INITIAL. It requires a stationary invariant positive-volume code
subsystem with a certified symbolic rate and decoder error; it does not apply
to point-to-set reachability alone.

The deeper source-specific result is an exact work-volume tradeoff for the
robust tape-bounded Beltrami simulator of Cardona, Miranda and Peralta-Salas
(arXiv:2111.03559v3). Let its positive eigenvalue be lambda, normalize
||v||_2=1 as in their Proposition 31, and let the controlled patch be
K=Psi^{-1}(B_N/lambda) in the standard flat torus
T^3=(R/2pi Z)^3. For N/lambda below the injectivity radius,

  mu(K)=N^3/(6 pi^2 lambda^3),
  W_strain(v)=nu lambda^2,
  W_strain(v) mu(K)^(2/3)=nu N^2/(6 pi^2)^(2/3).             (1)

Here W is sustaining work for the specified steady strain-viscosity model,
not unforced Euler work or total actuator cost. The source's robustness and
inverse-localization estimates imply, for tape size s_b and source constants,

  lambda >= c exp(exp(exp(c' s_b))),
  W_strain >= c^2 nu exp(2 exp(exp(c' s_b))),
  mu(K) <= C exp(C' s_b) exp(-3 exp(exp(c' s_b))) -> 0.          (2)

The constants may differ between inequalities. This law quantifies a specific
construction: as its robust simulation scope grows, the controlled patch
shrinks and the sustaining power rises. It is not a universal physical
complexity lower bound.

DERIVATION

The source gives curl v=lambda v and Delta v=-lambda^2 v on the flat torus.
Using div v=0, the vector identity nabla_v v=grad(|v|^2/2)-v x curl v,
and 2 div(Def v)=Delta v, choose pressure p=-|v|^2/2 and forcing
F=nu lambda^2 v. Then

  nabla_v v - 2nu div(Def v) + grad p = F,
  W=int F dot v = nu lambda^2 ||v||_2^2 = nu lambda^2.

The Euclidean ball B_(N/lambda) has volume 4pi(N/lambda)^3/3, and the torus
has volume 8pi^3; division gives the first formula of (1), then algebra gives
the third. This result is unchanged by no choices of units beyond the source's
fixed torus; rescaling the field by a multiplies W by a^2 while leaving K
unchanged.

For (2), Proposition 31 bounds the robust C0 tolerance by
  epsilon <= C0 exp(-exp(exp(c0 s_b))).
Its proof chooses localization error delta<epsilon/2 and uses the inverse
localization relation lambda>=c1/delta. Thus lambda>=c2
exp(exp(exp(c0 s_b))) for sufficiently large s_b. The same proof uses
N(s_b) comparable to exp(c3 s_b). Substituting in (1) yields (2). Their
reported H1 lower bound is consistent with the periodic identity
  ||v||_(H1)^2=(1+lambda^2)||v||_2^2,
which gives W=nu lambda^2/(1+lambda^2)||v||_(H1)^2 >=(nu/2)||v||_(H1)^2
when lambda>=1. The source's bound and this conversion are construction-
specific; external force preparation is not charged.

WHY THIS DOES NOT CLOSE THE POSITIVE-VOLUME CODE GATE

Theorem 26 controls the simulation only while trajectories remain in K; the
paper explicitly says the whole torus cannot replace K because outside
behavior and later returns are uncontrolled. K is not asserted invariant and
there is no stationary positive-volume symbolic factor or entropy-rate
certificate to insert into the conditional theorem in INITIAL. The shrinking
volume bound therefore measures the geometric footprint of this implementation,
not its volume entropy.

OUTWARD SEARCH AND BOUNDARIES

Cardona et al. robust simulation is a finite-tape, point-to-set reachability
construction, and its theorem explicitly limits the triple-exponential H1
cost to its own encoding. The 2026 Bruera et al. paper on topological entropy
of Turing-complete dynamics shows positive topological entropy for branching
machines and transfers it to area-preserving disk/Euler models on a square
ternary Cantor invariant set. Its planar area is zero since its dimension is
2 log(2)/log(3)<2; the suspended set still has zero ambient 3D volume. Their
existence of universal zero-topological-entropy machines also rules out a
general inference from universality to positive topological entropy.

The primary passive-scalar stirring literature (Lin, Thiffeault and Doering,
arXiv:1009.0834) instead bounds H^-1 mix-norm decay under time-dependent
incompressible flow with prescribed instantaneous velocity or strain/power
budgets. It does not identify that decay rate with particle-flow KS entropy.
The baseline Sol time-one map is cat dynamics on each fiber and identity in
the suspension coordinate, so it has positive volume entropy but is not
mixing. Thus neither mixing rate nor computation throughput follows from
entropy alone. Related turbulence papers using “topological entropy” for
material-line growth are distinct observables, as separately recorded in the
c08_l01 primary-source audit.
