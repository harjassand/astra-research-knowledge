Two-time transparency lemma
===========================
For one fixed real Fourier sine/cosine sector, a quadratic action of the displayed form has canonical momentum

  p=K qdot+A q+F^T h,
  qdot=K^{-1}(p-Aq-F^T h).

Because Y is the action-conjugate output, its instantaneous dependence on h is

  Y_contact=-(M+F K^{-1}F^T)h.

The retarded q,p evolution has no instantaneous feedthrough from a bounded h step: q and p stay continuous, while qdot jumps by -K^{-1}F^T Delta h. Thus zero susceptibility forces M+F K^{-1}F^T=0. With M>=0 and K>0, both summands are positive semidefinite, so M=0 and F=0 individually. The unimodular tensor parametrization and mixed-stress conjugacy clarification in v3 is needed for this sign statement; it correctly removes spurious volume/background-pressure terms from the matter contact.

After F=0, the retarded field Green matrix for the second-order system obeys

  G_R(t'+s,t')=s K^{-1}(t')+O(s^2),  s down to 0.

This follows from continuity of q and the unit jump in canonical velocity across an impulse. Consequently the first regular output kernel coefficient is E K^{-1} E^T. If the full response kernel vanishes, positive definiteness of K implies E=0. Time-dependent coefficients and gyroscopic terms do not affect the leading coefficient. The allowed qdot^T A(p,t)q term can include the first-derivative action's time-space principal mixing qdot D_i partial_i q at fixed p; this should be understood in the “most general” quadratic form. This is already consistent with v2's statement that A may depend on the fixed momentum.

The implication from matching the metric's complete sourced retarded Green function to zero matter susceptibility is valid when source normalization and initial conditions are fixed as stipulated. Equality only of a free-wave speed, one on-shell branch, or a static relaxed modulus would not imply it. The short-time coefficients are exact coefficients of the local stipulated EFT; they are not directly inferred from a finite-band noisy observation.

Rotational and scalar-stress audit
==================================
At an isotropic background, an internal spin j field without derivatives couples linearly to spin-2 stress only for j=2; a time derivative has the same selection rule. One spatial derivative carries spin 1, so a spin-2 output is allowed exactly for j=1,2,3. For a tensor wave along z, the derivative has m=0. The j=2 and j=3 tensor-helicity coefficients quoted in v2 are nonzero; therefore E(p)=0 for both tensor helicities and an open p interval kills their reduced spatial-derivative couplings. The no-derivative/time-derivative spin-2 couplings are killed by the same E=0 and F=0 conditions. The j=1 derivative channel is invisible to helicity +/-2, as stated.

That exceptional j=1 channel is precisely the material-triplet longitudinal scalar sector. Holding all other field directions fixed, the singlet/triplet Ward identity gives

  mu_T=C_z(b_x,b_x)>=0,
  Pi_triplet=2 i p C_z(b_x,w_x).

The full-space condition C_z>=0 is essential. If mu_T=0, b_x is a null vector of this positive semidefinite form, hence C_z(b_x,w_x)=0 for every triplet displacement, including zero-background-gradient triplets and arbitrary cross-couplings. This kills the remaining scalar shear. The PSD-null inference would fail for an indefinite spatial form; v2's explicit diagnostic records such a counterexample outside the hypotheses.

The list of channels is complete for a smooth first-derivative P(phi,B) action: the linear stress contains at most one perturbation field or its first derivative, and the tensor output transforms in spin 2. Higher internal j fields cannot couple to spin-2 stress at this derivative order. Once each allowed reduced tensor-tested vertex vanishes, rotational equivariance also removes its helicity-zero scalar counterpart. Independent homogeneous scalar initial modes therefore do not reopen the stress channel.
