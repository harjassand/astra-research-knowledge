  Location: paper's p. 165 (scan page 1), abstract and opening paragraph.
  The abstract gives the sharp constant A_p in the M. Riesz inequality for
  the conjugate function: tan(pi/(2p)) for 1<p<=2 and cot(pi/(2p)) for p>=2.
  The exposed candidate factor eta_p=sin(pi/p)/(1+|cos(pi/p)|) is exactly
  1/A_p. This source theorem is an Lp operator-norm statement for the scalar
  conjugate-function transform, not a Markov-generator or Dirichlet-form
  inequality.
