## Reduction and normalization of the first two directions

Delete zero tails. Adjacent parallel directions have proportional annihilating forms and can be combined into one polynomial tail after absorbing the constant scale. Their sum still has globally zero constant/first jets; it may vanish and trigger further reduction. Thus a nonempty reduced word has consecutive nonparallel directions, each with fibre degree at least two.

Suppose the reduced length is at least two. Choose a constant residual frame (r,e) with e=ell1 R and r=mR, where m v1=1. Since ell1 v1=0 and ell1 is nonzero, the frame is invertible and the first shear becomes r+=F(b,e), e unchanged.
