3. EXPOSED ELEMENTARY COMPLETION OF THE AFFINITY STEP

This argument was derived AFTER exposure of Q72. It is not retroactively
part of the blind baseline. Work first with bounded positive X,Y in M,
||X||_2=||Y||_2=1, and any contraction v in M. Tracial Cauchy--Schwarz
and contractivity of left/right multiplication give

 |tau(XYv)|^2
 =|tau[(X^(1/2)Y^(1/2))(Y^(1/2)vX^(1/2))]|^2
 <=tau(XY) tau(X v*Yv)
 <=tau(XY)||X||_2||v*Yv||_2
 <=tau(XY).

The L1 norm's contraction-duality formula yields
  ||XY||_1^2<=tau(XY).
If x=Xu_x,y=Yu_y have norm1 and u_x,u_y are their polar partial
isometries, cyclicity and the contraction u_yu_x* yield
  |<x,y>|=|tau(XY u_yu_x*)|<=||XY||_1.
Consequently
  ||X-Y||_2^2=2-2tau(XY)
    <=2(1-|<x,y>|^2)<=2||x-y||_2^2.
Thus the transport estimate used in Q follows by trace inequalities
alone. No Uhlmann theorem, matrix approximation, or finite-vN affinity
source extension is required. Bounded approximations suffice for the
actual finite protocol; the same inequality extends to L2 limits because
L2 multiplication is continuous into L1. The blind 2x2-dilation proof
remains the independently acquired route and is not edited.
