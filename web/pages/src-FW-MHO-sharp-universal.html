UNIVERSAL NEGATIVE BRANCH: QUASICONVEXITY AT THE SHARP ACOUSTIC FLOOR
15 October 2026

Theorem
-------
For every real r<-2, let

  A=[[a,b],[c,d]], u=a^2+c^2, v=b^2+d^2, j=ad-bc,
  H=(u+v-2j)^2=((a-d)^2+(b+c)^2)^2,
  F_(alpha,r)=alpha H+r(r-1)u^2/6+j^2+r u j,
  alpha_*(r)=3r/[8(r+2)].

Then F_(alpha,r) is quasiconvex if and only if it is rank-one convex if and
only if alpha>=alpha_*(r). Quasiconvexity holds at every constant affine
background, on every bounded Lipschitz planar domain, for W0^(1,4)
perturbations. The periodic analogue also holds.

This extends the separately frozen interval certificate to the entire
negative branch r<-2. It uses a different rational coefficient choice and
different diffusion from the interval artifact. The interval JSON, verifier,
and report have not been overwritten.

The certificate is exact rational algebra. Internal independent review is
being conducted separately. External review, formal proof verification, and
historical priority remain unresolved. The inherited analytic method is the
heat-flow/exact-two-form method of Bruno and Pasqualotto, not a new method
claimed here.

1. A direct witness proves necessity
------------------------------------
At A0=[[0,(2r+1)/3],[1,0]] and R=e1 tensor e1,

  D^2F_(alpha,r)(A0)[R,R]
    = 16(r+2)^2/9 * (alpha-alpha_*(r)).

The prefactor is positive for every r<-2. Hence every alpha<alpha_*(r)
fails rank-one convexity and therefore quasiconvexity. This necessity does
not depend on an optimization result or a failed certificate.

2. The correct twelve-dimensional moving support
------------------------------------------------
At the sharp floor, the target-e1 acoustic matrix satisfies

  det M_r(n,m)=9m^4n^4r^4.

There are two contacts, each with a fourth-order flat acoustic curve:

  A_old(t)=[[1,-(r+1)t],[(r+1)t,1]], xi_old(t)=(t,1),
  D^2F(A_old(t))[e1 tensor xi_old(t),e1 tensor xi_old(t)]
    = (2/3)r(r+1)^2(r+2)t^4;

  A_new(t)=[[-3(r+1)t/(2r+1),(2r+1)/3],[1,(r+1)t]],
  xi_new(t)=(1,t),
  D^2F(A_new(t))[e1 tensor xi_new(t),e1 tensor xi_new(t)]
    = 6r(r+1)^2(r+2)t^4/(2r+1)^2.

For any positive fixed input diffusion and target rotation, the rank-one
compatible second jet makes B1 and B2 proportional, so every alternating
two-form correction vanishes. Positivity of a Gram representation and the
O(t^4) value force its kernel to contain both Z(0) and Z'(0). Three target
rotation coefficients for each curve/order give twelve independent vectors
for r<-2. C_r is a 24 by 12 basis for their orthogonal complement; its bottom
twelve rows are the identity. Its explicit entries are in the portable JSON.

The construction admitted all 78 reduced Gram coefficients, all 60

exact matching system has rank 134 among 140 columns and no linear diffusion
obstruction. No old source coefficient was frozen.

3. Elliptic quarter-turn symmetry and the natural diffusion
----------------------------------------------------------
Put h=(2r+1)/3<0 and K_r=[[0,h],[1,0]]. Exact coefficient expansion gives

  F_sharp(A K_r)=h^2 F_sharp(A),   K_r^2=h I.

Thus M_r=K_r/sqrt(-h) is a determinant-one elliptic quarter-turn and
F_sharp(A M_r)=F_sharp(A). This is a discrete right symmetry; full right
SO(2) invariance is not asserted.

The positive trace-two diffusion invariant under this input symmetry is

  d1=(2r+1)/(r-1),   d2=-3/(r-1),   d1+d2=2.

Both coefficients are positive for every r<-2. Equivalently, for u=-r-2>0,
d1=(2u+3)/(u+3) and d2=3/(u+3). After target rotation/reflection and the
elliptic quarter-turn symmetries, the remaining reduced Gram face has just
two free parameters. With D=(r-1)(r+2), choose

  y1=9/(2D),   y3=-9(2r+1)/D,
  y0=((2r+1)^2/9)y1+r(2r+1)^2/D.

The y labels are construction coordinates, not standalone proof inputs;
the final rational matrices are exported explicitly.

  B1=(z0,z1,z3,z4), B2=(z1,z2,z4,z5),
  Z=(a,b,c,d) tensor (z0,z1,z2,z3,z4,z5).

Let omega be the two-form whose 6 by 10 homogeneous-quadratic coefficient

  Z^T C_r S_r C_r^T Z

All matrices have rational entries whose denominators are nonzero on the

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The explicit 12 by 12 matrix V_r in the JSON satisfies

  det V_r=-432(r+1)/(2r+1)^3,

so it is invertible for every r<-2. Exact congruence produces four diagonal
blocks of sizes 4,4,2,2. Each block has the exact LDL factors recorded in the
JSON. Their pivot functions are as follows.

First block:
  -3(r+1)^2(2r+1)/[(r-1)(r+2)],

Second block:

  -9(17r^2+23r+8)/[(r-1)(17r^2+22r+9)],

  3(2r+1)/(r+2),

Fourth block:
  -9/(r+2),
  27(r+1)^2(r+2)/[(r-1)^2(2r+1)].

Every pivot is strictly positive for r<-2. The two displayed quadratics
have positive leading coefficients and negative discriminants, -128 and

substitute r=-2-u, u>0, cancel each rational pivot, and simultaneously
normalize numerator/denominator signs. Every resulting numerator and
denominator has nonnegative rational coefficients with at least one
strictly positive coefficient. Such polynomials are positive for every

entry. Therefore S_r is positive definite for every r<-2.

This proves continuum positivity on the unbounded branch. No compact

6. Analytic implication at all affine backgrounds
-------------------------------------------------
Fix any r<-2. Its d1,d2 are fixed strictly positive constants. For a constant
matrix B and a compactly supported smooth phi, evolve

  phi_tau=exp(tau(d1 partial_xx+d2 partial_yy))phi,
  A_tau=B+Dphi_tau,
  E(tau)=integral [F(A_tau)-F(B)-DF(B):Dphi_tau].

The exact two-form has the global polynomial primitive i_E omega/4. Its
pullback by A_tau is exact; each coefficient of the primitive pullback
contains a derivative of A_tau, so it is rapidly decreasing. Stokes gives
zero integral for the form correction. Integration by parts yields

  E'(tau)=-integral Z_tau^T C_r S_r C_r^T Z_tau <=0.

The positive anisotropic heat kernel gives, for p=2,3,4,
||Dphi_tau||_p <= C_(p,r)tau^(-(1-1/p))||Dphi||_1. Quartic Taylor growth
therefore gives E(tau)->0 as tau->infinity. Strong L2,L3,L4 continuity at
tau=0 gives E(0)>=0, and the integrated affine linear term is zero. Strong
W1,4 approximation proves the all-background quasiconvexity inequality on
every bounded Lipschitz planar domain. The analogous periodic heat and
Stokes proof gives the periodic claim.

No constant is asserted to be uniform as r approaches -2 or negative
infinity. Each fixed r has positive finite diffusion, a global polynomial
primitive, and a positive reduced Gram matrix, which suffices for the
theorem. Adding (alpha-alpha_*)H, a convex function, proves sufficiency for
every alpha>=alpha_*. The direct acoustic witness proves necessity.

7. Negative-delta contact-face consequence
------------------------------------------
Let alpha,beta,gamma,delta be real and delta<0. Suppose

  P=[[6beta+delta,delta],[delta,gamma]] >=0,
  Q=[[8alpha,delta],[delta,2beta+delta]] >=0.

Then the four-coefficient integrand

  alpha H+beta u^2+gamma j^2+delta u j

is quasiconvex. To see this, set p=6beta+delta, rho=p/delta, and
lambda=delta^2/p. The two PSD assumptions and delta!=0 imply p>0,
2beta+delta>0, rho<-2, lambda>0. Direct coefficient matching gives

  F=lambda Fsharp_rho +(gamma-lambda)j^2
       +(alpha-delta^2/[8(2beta+delta)])H.

Both remainder coefficients are nonnegative by P,Q. Fsharp_rho is
quasiconvex by the universal certificate, j^2 is polyconvex, and H is convex.
This proves the consequence directly. Necessity of P,Q for rank-one
convexity is the independent contact-face classification, not rederived
from the heat certificate here. The formula does not treat delta>0 or the
general nine-dimensional invariant cone.
