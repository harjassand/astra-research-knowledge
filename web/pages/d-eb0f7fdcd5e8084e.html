# Focused bottleneck screen — 2026-10-12

Bounded pass over primary experimental literature and primary systems analysis. Candidate invention targets only; no claim of a new principle or novelty clearance. Each gate is deliberately a falsifiable threshold, not a proposed proven design.

## 1. Primary ironmaking: make low-grade/fine ore usable without the H₂/DRI pellet chain

**Demonstrated route and constraint.** Devlin et al., “Global green hydrogen-based steel opportunities surrounding high quality renewable energy and iron ore deposits,” *Nature Communications* 14, 2578 (published 2023-05-15), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-38123-2. This is a primary, facility-level techno-economic optimization informed by process simulation, not an industrial demonstration. It uses ≥67% Fe DR-grade ore, assumes ≥50 kg H₂/t DRI and 94% metallisation, and finds DR-grade ore is only 4% of current global seaborne trade. In Kazakhstan’s modeled 20% Fe resource region, DR-grade pellet premium rises from $40 to $122/t ore and ore cost contributes 45% of green steel cost. Shaft-furnace fines are constrained by the counterflow bed; ultrafines and sticking are named failure modes. Source details: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-38123-2 (especially lines 120–122, 222–228, 249–255).

**Opening for a non-incremental primitive.** A reduction/contact architecture that tolerates natural gangue and fines could bypass the quality bottleneck, but only if it removes rather than relocates beneficiation, pelletization, and slag losses. Candidate directions: selective oxygen transport/reduction front or a solids-contact geometry that makes particle size/gangue nonfatal while preserving high Fe yield and useful steel chemistry. This must be compared against fluidized-bed DRI and DRI-melter-EAF, the closest known accommodation routes.

**Quantitative gate.** On a representative sub-67% Fe, fine, real ore, demonstrate ≥94% metallisation and recover iron into a steelmaking product while (i) omitting agglomeration/beneficiation and (ii) accounting for all gangue, slag, reductant, heat, and electricity per tonne. If unavoidable gangue handling consumes the beneficiation/pelletization burden or the reactor cannot sustain stable continuous throughput with fines, stop. The 50 kg H₂/t DRI reference is the reduction floor for comparing against H₂-DRI, not an asserted thermodynamic minimum.

## 2. Cement: circular calcium recovery still has a reagent loop and clinker integration gap

**Demonstrated route and constraint.** Rumscheidt et al., “Low-emission cement clinker precursor production, enabled by electrolytic extraction of calcium from waste cement,” *Nature Communications* 16, 9302 (published 2025-10-21), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64339-5. Primary experimental work: a cement electrolyzer plus digestion and Ca(OH)₂ isolation vessels; reports >80% Ca(OH)₂ yields and up to 300 mA/cm², and 99.8% CO₂ reduction for fresh waste / 80% for aged waste under the study’s boundary. Crucial hidden constraint: its closed-loop electrolyte needed an initial 0.2 M CaCl₂; without this, yield fell to 3.6%. Decomposition required 44.4 mol acid/L aged cement and 45.4 mol/L fresh cement. The paper estimates its H₂/O₂ coproduct can fuel a clinker kiln, but the tested unit makes precursor; calcining/clinkering and demolition sorting remain system steps. Sources: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64339-5 (especially lines 144–150, 238–245, 249–265).

**Opening for a non-incremental primitive.** Seek a closed material cycle in which chemical potential and ion transport are regenerated internally, so calcium liberation and silica separation proceed without a consumable acid/base inventory or persistent membrane/electrode burden; simultaneously make the product directly clinkering-ready. This is a coupled reaction/transport/separation problem, not just a better kiln heater.

**Quantitative gate.** In a continuous mass-balanced run on real demolition feed, achieve high Ca recovery and clinker-grade phase composition while showing net acid/base make-up tending to zero per tonne and counting all electricity, membrane/electrode replacement, sorting, and kiln duty. Immediate no-go if the 44–45 mol acid/L-equivalent demand or externally supplied CaCl₂ is regenerated only by importing stoichiometric reagents/energy that erase the claimed carbon/energy advantage. The source’s CO₂ percentage is its stated boundary, not whole-sector validation.

## 3. Lithium from hard-rock spodumene: remove roast, but current density/lifetime/materials do not yet scale

**Demonstrated route and constraint.** Zhang et al., “Direct extraction of lithium from ores by electrochemical leaching,” *Nature Communications* 15, 5066 (published 2024-06-13), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48867-0. Primary experiment on α-spodumene at room temperature in 0.5 M H₂SO₄ with H₂O₂ promotion: 92.2% Li leaching after 12 h, at 0.95 V vs SCE, and reported 71.5% Faradaic efficiency. The 3-D collector/current test is described as high-throughput but reached only 18 mA on up to 5 g material; current retention was 65.26% after a 20 h hold. Architecture includes Au catalyst and in-situ H₂O₂. Conventional route converts α to β at 1100°C. Sources: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-48867-0 (especially lines 71–82, 117–119, 172–189, 202–218).

**Opening for a non-incremental primitive.** The core issue is advancing a selective reaction front through an electronically insulating mineral without relying on precious-metal mediator generation, while exporting Li⁺ and passivating/disposing of the depleted aluminosilicate. A potentially valuable primitive would make reaction-front progress self-propagating through mineral/electrolyte interfaces and continuously renew conducting access without grinding/roast or a fragile catalyst coating.

**Quantitative gate.** Preserve ≥90% Li extraction and ≥70% Faradaic efficiency on untreated α-spodumene while demonstrating ≥10× current per active area/volume over the reported 18 mA demonstrator, ≥90% current retention over 1,000 h, and no Au or unrecovered peroxide/acid inventory; include Li purification and aluminosilicate waste/product balance. Failure of durability, throughput, or full-process separation means this result remains a lab leaching trick rather than a production primitive. The 1,000 h / 10× values are proposed campaign gates, not literature benchmarks.

## Screening judgment

The strongest fresh invention opportunity is **iron from variable low-grade/fine ore**, because the dominant limitation is a structural feedstock eligibility constraint and a breakthrough would affect a huge material flow. Cement is the most compelling near-term electrochemical circularity opening, but its published reagent/calciner boundary must be closed first. Lithium leaching is a cleaner, highly specific mechanistic target with strong room-temperature proof but obvious longevity/throughput failure modes. These are three distinct problem families: particle reduction/bed transport, coupled acid-base recovery/clinkering, and mineral-interface reaction-front propagation.
