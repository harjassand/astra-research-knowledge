## A. Exact obstruction to unchanged-event substitution

Use a memory syndrome qubit with stabilizer Z and an untouched logical spectator. The weak footprint is the syndrome qubit, so its commuting Paulis are I and Z and the full-footprint/no-local-logical condition holds. The stipulated channel has its old canonical independent skip/complete-pinch refinement. On a retained middle pinch, the event of at least one block mismatch has probability 1-(3/4)^2=7/16. On a skipped middle pinch, the sole block mismatch probability is sin^2(pi/3)=3/4. Thus the OLD parent event has probability 19/32.

The new binary endpoint formula gives F_1(pi/6,pi/6,1/2)=9/16=18/32. Indeed the actual endpoint mismatch probability is (1/2)(2(1/4)(3/4))+(1/2)(3/4)=18/32. The omitted 1/32 consists of retained-pinch paths that leave and return to the entering syndrome. They are bad in the old event and good in the new endpoint event. Consequently F_1 cannot bound the old event even for a single parent and trivial bath.

This is not a controller, geometry, or logical-error counterexample. It isolates exactly the predicate mismatch.
