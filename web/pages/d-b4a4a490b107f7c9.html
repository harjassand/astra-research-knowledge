## Audit of the load-bearing Li–Liu mechanism

Primary source: Li–Liu, arXiv:2608.19897v2, Theorem 2 and Appendices B/C, saved in sources/check_selection/ranking_2608.19897v2.html. Authors/dates verified at the versioned abstract. The following facts are reconstructed rather than treated as an opaque theorem.

1. **Output-space independence.** A classifier confusion matrix A_n has size |Θ|×|Θ| regardless of the number of report symbols. For a common-support residual R, K=A_n^{-1}R satisfies K1=1. Its entrywise lower bound is R_i(y)[1-e^{Δ(R)}||A_n^{-1}-I||∞]. The threshold n=O(1+Δ(R)) depends on likelihood diameter and the fixed source, not the number of columns or minimum report probability separately. Arbitrarily rare output columns are covered by their relative likelihood ratios. Outputs of zero probability in every state are removed.

2. **Strict positivity of residual repair.** Thinning accepted mass by (1-η)/(1+(|Θ|-1)η) gives Q_i(y)≤(1-η)H_i(y), not merely Q_i(y)≤H_i(y). Hence ηH_i(y)≤H_i(y)-Q_i(y). Missing masses obey η≤δ_i≤2|Θ|η. Thus R_i=(H_i-Q_i)/δ_i retains common support and Δ(R)≤Δ(H)+log(2|Θ|/η). Without strict slack the inverse-kernel argument can fail through support mismatch. Every branch must use a fresh source tail; conditioning on rejection changes the past, but not the future statewise law.

3. **All randomized target kernels.** Conditional auxiliary randomization at a finite recorded target history is state independent: its compatible-seed factor depends only on that history and the policy; the state likelihood factor is the product of observation probabilities and cancels. This extends to controlled targets only if histories record selected actions as well as observations. With finite menus and alphabets every bounded stage has finitely many histories/reports, even if the original policy has continuous auxiliary randomness. A positive report probability in one state contains a positive finite-history term in every state because observation laws have full support.

4. **Unbounded stopping times.** Direct finite-H conversion has a diameter term ηΔ(H), which need not be bounded by E_iT for arbitrary integrable policies. A truncation proof with a policy-dependent diameter would not establish the uniform theorem. The valid construction exactly reproduces entire bounded continuation reports in blocks 2^k, preserving target histories at their checkpoints. Reach probability is P_i(T≥2^k), and block remainder C2^{k/2}. The pathwise geometric sum is ≤C√T, so Tonelli gives C E_i√T≤C√E_iT. No second moment is needed. At least one source observation per entered stage prevents infinitely many stages from accumulating at a finite source time. Immediate stopping is copied by free auxiliary randomness and incurs zero source cost.

5. **Exact arithmetic and effective implementation.** Mathematical probability kernels exist, but computing H_i, verifier success probabilities, Q_i and A_n^{-1}R exactly may require infinite sums or arbitrary real operations. Even for computable source laws, runtime, finite precision, comparisons at exact logarithmic thresholds and exact sampling are not supplied by the expected measurement bound. The cycle1 rational certificate validates one finite instance only. The strongest justified theorem is exact statistical attainability with a measurement-cost bound; it is not an efficient executable compiler for arbitrary target policies.

These points pass mathematical reconstruction under the stated finite known-law interface. The source theorem is strong in terminal-law/output-space uniformity, and weak with respect to computational implementation.

## Why output-dependent iid mixtures are insufficient as a proof shortcut

For source gauge φ_i(v)=min_{w≥0} c·w subject to D_iw≥v, choose the optimum separately for every output likelihood vector v=[λ_i(y)]_+. An optimal allocation may put no weight on actions distinguishing i from a direction with v_j=0. It can therefore fail candidate-versus-alternative identification altogether. Adding a vanishing exploration probability restores positive drifts but makes the fixed-mixture passage constant depend on y and Δ(H). There is no output-uniform lower bound on these drifts, so the cycle1 fixed-q passage lemma cannot simply be invoked. This is a real missing step, repaired by deterministic batches below.

## Finite-H gauge lemma with uniform remainder

Finite source menu: deterministic costs c_a>0; finite full-support laws P_i^a; collective identification. Write D_ij^a=KL(P_i^a||P_j^a). Fix an identifying baseline mixture q_* and its finite positive drift constants. For every i and v∈R_+^{|Θ|-1},

    φ_i(v)=min c·w,  w≥0,  Σ_a D_ij^a w_a≥v_j for all j≠i.

The gauge is finite, monotone, positively homogeneous, subadditive and convex. A fixed feasible baseline allocation gives φ_i(v)≤KΣ_j v_j. Positive c_min gives Σ_a w_a≤φ_i(v)/c_min for an optimum. All constants below depend only on fixed menus and costs.

**Claim.** Every finite common-support experiment H has one exact controlled-source reproduction with

    E_i cost ≤ B_i(H)+C[√(1+B_i(H))+log(1/η)+η(1+Δ(H))],
    B_i(H)=E_{Y~H_i}φ_i([λ_ij(Y)]_{j≠i,+}),

uniformly over H, its report alphabet and 0<η≤η_0. The positive part is coordinatewise.

### Construction and the critical cost audit

Classify candidate k using O(g) baseline observations, error ≤η, g=log(1/η). Propose y~H_k. Set v_j=[λ_kj(y)]_+ and b=v+g1. Choose a gauge-optimal w for b and deterministic counts n_a=ceil(w_a). Execute these action counts in a fixed known order. Monitor ANY reverse score S_jk≥g at each observation and reject immediately if it occurs. Do not declare success until the whole planned batch has finished. At its end, declare success if all S_kj≥b_j; otherwise continue under q_* until all these inequalities hold or any reverse rejection occurs. Rejection wins ties.

The planned number n obeys

    planned cost ≤φ_k(b)+Σ_a c_a
                 ≤φ_k(v)+gφ_k(1)+Σ_a c_a,
    n≤C[1+φ_k(v)+g]≤C[1+Δ(H)+g].

Under true k, the hypothetical full planned batch has E_kS_kj≥b_j, independently generated bounded increments, and Var_kS_kj≤L^2n. Hence

    E_kΣ_j[b_j-S_kj]_+≤Σ_j E_k|S_kj-E_kS_kj|≤C√n.

Even when the batch rejected early, define its unobserved remainder hypothetically; it has the intended independent laws. The actual baseline tail is paid only on batch survival. The conditional tail cost bound is nonnegative for every hypothetical final score, so

    E[1_{survival} tail_cost_bound(full score)]
       ≤E[tail_cost_bound(full score)].

This inequality is the necessary conditioning repair; bounding the *conditional* surviving-score distribution by its unconditional mean would be unjustified.

Given a surviving full score vector, let a_j=[b_j-S_kj]_+. Baseline simultaneous passage costs at most CΣ_j a_j+C√(1+Σ_j a_j)+C; negative deficits can be replaced by zero. Averaging and Jensen give O(√n+n^{1/4}+1). Planned cost plus this tail is therefore

    φ_k(v)+C[g+√(1+φ_k(v))].

No output-dependent drift appears: only q_* is used in this passage estimate. This is the important improvement over output-specific iid mixtures.

Under wrong true state i≠k, the main batch costs O(Δ(H)+g). If it survives, the baseline reverse i/k score starts no lower than -Ln. Its positive identifying drift gives expected direct-rejection duration O(g+n+1), uniformly in y. Consequently wrong-candidate expected cost is O(Δ(H)+g+1); preliminary classification assigns total probability ≤η to these branches. Their entire contribution is O(ηΔ(H)+ηg+η), fitting the permitted finite-H remainder. Wrong-candidate cost need not be independent of H in this version.

Under true k, reverse likelihood ratios remain nonnegative mean-one martingales throughout the deterministic batch and baseline tail, since every action kernel is common across states and predictable. Ville bounds false rejection by (|Θ|-1)η. Own-state successful passage is almost surely finite by positive baseline drifts, so own acceptance probability is ≥1-(|Θ|-1)η. Success always has S_ki≥[λ_ki]_++g≥λ_ki+g. Stopped change of measure gives p_i(k,y)≤ηH_i(y)/H_k(y)p_k(k,y), unchanged by output-dependent scheduling.

The cycle1 thinning and baseline inverse-confusion residual correction therefore reproduce H exactly with missing mass O(η) and residual diameter Δ(H)+O(g). Correct-candidate costs integrated under H_i give B_i+C[g+√(1+B_i)]. Wrong candidates and residual correction contribute O(ηΔ(H)+ηg+η). Baseline classification adds O(g). Absorb ηg and constants to obtain the claim. All branches are integrable under every state, validating stopped change of measure and pasting.

## Sharp controlled-source/controlled-target theorem

The target now has a finite known action menu b with finite full-support observation laws G_i^b, deterministic costs β_b>0 and arbitrary state-independent adaptive choice/stopping/report kernels. Target stopping time T is integrable in every state, terminal output Z is finite. Write g_i^b=(KL(G_i^b||G_j^b))_{j≠i}, and

    γ_i=max_b φ_i(g_i^b)/β_b.

There exists one exact controlled-source reproduction with, simultaneously for every i,

    E_i source_cost ≤γ_i E_i target_cost+C√(E_i target_cost),

where C depends only on the two fixed menus and their positive costs. It is uniform over target policies and finite terminal output spaces. The vector γ_i is optimal among universal first-order coefficients.

### Bounded target derivation

For T≤M, action/observation history likelihood factors have a state-independent control component, which cancels. Let A_ij(T)=Σ_{t≤T}g_ij^{B_t} and centered martingale M_ij(T)=S_ij(T)-A_ij(T). Observation log ratios are bounded uniformly over the finite target menu. Predictable stopped increments are orthogonal, giving E_iM_ij(T)^2≤K E_iT. Consequently E_iΣ_j|M_ij(T)|≤K√(E_iT).

The conditional terminal likelihood identity and Jensen imply λ_ij(Z)≤E_i[S_ij(T)|Z]. Coordinatewise monotonicity and convexity of x↦φ_i([x]_+) then give

    B_i(H)≤E_iφ_i([S_i(T)]_+)
          ≤E_iφ_i(A_i(T))+E_iφ_i([M_i(T)]_+)
          ≤E_iΣ_{t≤T}φ_i(g_i^{B_t})+K√(E_iT)
          ≤γ_i E_iΣ_{t≤T}β_{B_t}+K√(E_iT).

The middle inequality uses subadditivity and [A+M]_+≤A+[M]_+ with A≥0. Since β_min>0, √(E_iT)≤β_min^{-1/2}√(E_i target_cost). Every bounded target likelihood ratio is at most exp(M L_G), so Δ(H)≤ML_G. Applying the finite-H gauge lemma with η=η_0/M gives a bounded-stage cost γ_i E_i target_cost+C√M. This single constant covers every finite target continuation history.

### Integrable policies and sharpness

Apply the audited full-history dyadic recursion, now preserving target action labels. Costs of disjoint target blocks sum exactly to target_cost. Source remainder sums to C E_i√T≤Cβ_min^{-1/2}√(E_i target_cost). Time-zero stopping and positive-duration stages are treated exactly as in cycle1. This proves the universal upper bound.

For any target action b, repeat it m times and report the complete observation transcript. Exact source reproduction implies Σ_a E_iN_a D_ij^a≥m g_ij^b for every j, by stopped-record likelihood cancellation and data processing. Hence E_i source_cost≥mφ_i(g_i^b). Target cost is mβ_b. Taking the maximum over b proves that any universal state-i coefficient is at least γ_i. There is no claim that a single fixed target action is simultaneously hardest in every state; universal quantification permits a different witness action for each necessary coefficient.

## Adversarial conclusions and limitations

The sharper batch-plus-baseline construction appears to close both degenerating-drift and survivor-conditioning gaps. It achieves a true square-root remainder; the simpler fixed batch with extra √(M logM) margin is also sufficient for sharp first-order rates but is weaker quantitatively. I do not see a mathematical obstruction under finite known full-support menus and strictly positive deterministic costs.

Hard boundaries: zero-cost informative target actions break comparison by target cost; unknown laws remove exact H/Q/kernel access; missing action labels invalidate controlled likelihood cancellation; arbitrary continuous/infinite menus lose fixed bounded-increment and baseline-identification constants. A finite numerical certificate cannot establish universal exactness or computational efficiency. Candidate discovery of the theorem must be reported separately from unresolved novelty and external verification.

Closest prior art remains Li–Liu fixed-source exact conversion and classical controlled sensing max-min KL design. The controlled-target gauge theorem combines them with output-dependent batching and exact residual correction; no claim that the classical design LP itself is new. Next falsifiable steps: independently implement one genuinely branching controlled target with exact rational terminal laws; audit a full primitive-constant derivation; inspect sequential experiment-comparison prior art for equivalent controlled exact attainment.
