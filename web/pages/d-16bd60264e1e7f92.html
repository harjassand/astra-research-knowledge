## Independent fixed-ball probability

Let a,b,c be the perturbed triangle vertices. Let the actual side upper
bound be L0lambda and the common altitude lower bound gamma0lambda.
They are deterministic for every allowed cap draw. Assume
lambda>=6rho/gamma0. If the triangle meets B(z,rho), select a point y
in the intersection and a vertex a with barycentric coefficient
theta_a>=1/3. With b,c fixed, writing y=theta_a a+(1-theta_a)q for
q on bc gives

    dist(z,line(bc)) >= theta_a altitude_a-rho
                      >=gamma0lambda/6.             (2)

Let P be the plane containing the fixed line bc and z, and Q the
triangle plane. Writing d=dist(z,line(bc)), the distance of z to Q is
d times the sine of their dihedral angle. It is<=rho. Consequently

    dist(a,P) <= |a-line(bc)| rho/d
              <=6L0rho/gamma0.                      (3)

P is fixed after b,c are conditioned on; it is not a plane selected
from the subsequently varied vertex a. This resolves that quantifier.
The area of a sphere of radius alpha lambda inside a slab of half
thickness T is at most4pi alpha lambda T. Dividing by cap area
2pi alpha^2lambda^2(1-cos(alpha1/(2alpha))) and summing over the three
possible selected vertices proves

    Pr[triangle intersects B(z,rho)]
       <=Gamma0 rho/lambda,
    Gamma0=36L0/[alpha gamma0(1-cos(alpha1/(2alpha)))]. (4)

Set L0=2002,gamma0=1. The same rational lower Taylor bound on1-cos(.08)

## Exact Lovasz-local-lemma rule and admitted template inflation

Let F>=1 be a fixed upper bound for the number of fine2-cells per unit
ball divided by the perturbed-parent formal-triangle density. F is a
template/geometric constant, independent of recursion depth, lambda,
and input size, once the ratios in(1) and ell/lambda are fixed.
An independent positive-area/collapsed-strip derivation of F=2^26 is
saved by pde_spectrum9 in ACTUAL_TEMPLATE_DENSITY_INFLATION.md and
independently reconstructed in our DENSITY_INFLATION_PEER_GATE.md.
No literal source64pi constant is adopted. The following rule is exact
for any such proved F; use that admitted conservative value here.

Set k=300, t'=100k, t=Ft'. Triangles of one triangle color have disjoint
vertices and independent perturbations. For a k-set Delta of one color,
let E_Delta be the event that all its images meet some common radius1
ball. Choose a vertex v of its first triangle. If the event occurs,
the center is within(alpha+3)lambda of lambda v. Round that center to
the nearest Z^3 lattice point u, changing it by<1. Each triangle then
meets B(u,2); u is within(alpha+4)lambda of lambda v. There are at most

    C0lambda^3 such points, C0=(2alpha+11)^3.

Equation(4), independence and the union bound give

    Pr(E_Delta)<=C0(2Gamma0)^k lambda^(3-k).          (5)

If E_Delta and another event share a random vertex v, every triangle
of that other event intersects the old B(v,2alpha+4): perturbations
move points at most alpha lambda, and any first-triangle point is at
old distance<=2 from v. Cover that old ball by at most(4alpha+11)^3
unit balls. The old square density t implies formal-triangle density
<=2t, so at most

    M0=2t(4alpha+11)^3

triangles can occur. There are at most M0^k candidate k-sets through
one vertex, and an event uses3k vertices. Its dependency degree is
<=3kM0^k. The sufficient symmetric LLL condition, using e<4, is thus

    4(3k+1)C0(2Gamma0 M0)^k <=lambda^(k-3).          (6)

It is satisfied by a finite fixed integer lambda, e.g. the least power
of2 >=10^24 whose(k-3)rd power is at least the left side. This is an
exact integer test, with log2lambda=O(log F+log k) for fixed alpha.
No event occurs, so a unit ball meets at most(k-1) triangles per color,
hence fewer than t' triangles. Inflation by F closes the original
density t and permits iteration. The seed's finite density can be
absorbed by increasing F if necessary.

This argument uses the finite asymmetric geometry of the actual input:
candidate sets are local before perturbation. It does not count all
far-away triangles as dependent or turn physical crossings into graph
edges. Generalized Moser--Tardos is source prior; the present admission
does not prove a finite-bit implementation of its continuous geometric
event oracle. At best there is a mathematical existence/acquisition
interface until exact sampling, event testing and precision are charged.
