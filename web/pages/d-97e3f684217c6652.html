TARGET CLAIM / INTERFACE

The target is the normalized full N-qubit Gibbs operator
  rho_{N,alpha,delta,h} ∝ exp[ alpha J^2/N - delta J_z^2/N + h J_z ],
where J=(1/2)Σ_i sigma^(i), alpha≥0 is arbitrary and can grow without bound, 0≤delta≤delta_* is an N-independent bound, and h is any finite field. The desired conclusion is full SEP across the N physical qubits, retaining every total-spin sector and its multiplicity. The current candidate of interest is delta_*=1/4. This is stronger than symmetric-subspace SEP, PPT across selected cuts, pairwise concurrence, spin-squeezing/non-squeezing, or a thermodynamic-limit classical free-energy result.

MODEL MATCH FOUND, BUT NO MATCHING SEPARABILITY THEOREM

Mathé, Usui, Gühne, and Vitagliano, “Estimating the best separable approximation of non-pure spin-squeezed states,” Quantum 10, 2078 (2026), arXiv:2504.07814, studies the exact finite-N, fully connected spin-1/2 XXZ Gibbs family on the full tensor product. Their Hamiltonian is
  H = (g/N)(J_x^2+J_y^2) + (g_z/N)J_z^2 + h_p J_z,
with Gibbs exponent -H/T. The target is exactly embedded by
  g/T=-alpha,   g_z/T=delta-alpha,   h_p/T=-h.
Their displayed partition function sums all J=0,1,...,N/2 sectors (appropriate parity) with their multiplicities (Eq. 22), rather than imposing J=N/2. Thus this paper is an exact model/space match, not an exact SEP result. Its contributions are BSA lower bounds from spin-squeezing witnesses and upper bounds from a symmetry-adapted iterative separable ansatz. It reports numerical behavior and asymptotic witness bounds, not an analytic region theorem certifying SEP for all N, all alpha, or arbitrary h. It states the ansatz search is difficult and the bounds are generally not tight for N>3. This is the most relevant recent prior-art check; it does not preempt the uniform-δ claim.

The paper reports a re-entrant thermal-entanglement observation in an FM XX setting when a field makes the ground state product. That observation is not a counterexample to the present zero-field δ≤1/4 target: its plotted field/temperature parameters are not the target's h=0 uniform anisotropy slice, and no conversion placing it at δ≤1/4 was established. Do not cite it as a counterexample to the candidate theorem. The parameter map above is the necessary first step for any comparison.

EXACT CRITERION THAT REDUCES THE GATE, NOT AN ALL-N BOUND

Nengkun Yu, “Separability of Bosonic Systems,” Phys. Rev. A 94, 060101(R) (2016), arXiv:1501.02957, gives a necessary-and-sufficient finite criterion for a state supported entirely in the totally symmetric N-qubit subspace and diagonal in the Dicke basis. If p_k are normalized Dicke probabilities, define q_k=p_k/binom(N,k). Full SEP of that symmetric state is equivalent to PSD of the two truncated Hankel/localizing matrices built from q_0,...,q_N. Yu also proves PPT iff SEP within this GDS class. This is applicable to the alpha→∞ maximum-spin limit, not directly to the full finite-alpha target. In the axial Gaussian case p_k∝exp[-delta(k-N/2)^2/N], it makes the all-N question an exact truncated moment positivity problem. It does not prove PSD for any fixed positive delta uniformly in N. The candidate endpoint delta=2 ln 2 has only finite numerical evidence in c05_s03's audit; the δ≤1/4 all-N claim still requires an analytical gate or a certified counterexample.

OTHER CLOSE PRIMARY SOURCES AND THEIR EXACT LIMITS

• Sidles, “Positive P-Representations of the Thermal Operator from Quantum Control Theory,” arXiv:quant-ph/0401165: for each single irreducible spin-j space, the linear-axis thermal operator exp(-β t·s) has a positive diagonal spin-coherent P function, explicitly proportional to 1/Q_{j+1}(-x). This is a linear one-spin Hamiltonian. It contains no J_z^2 anisotropy and gives no physical-N-qubit full-separability statement across all Schur sectors. Theorem 1 is an exact construction but not the desired theorem.
• Barry and Drummond, “Qubit phase-space: SU(n) coherent-state P representations,” PRA 78, 052108 (2008), arXiv:0808.2226: their broadly applicable positive-P kernels are off-diagonal and live on a doubled phase space for stochastic simulation. Positivity of that representation does not imply a mixture of diagonal product projectors, hence does not imply full SEP.
• Lieb, “The classical limit of quantum spin systems,” CMP 31 (1973), 327–340, and later Berezin–Lieb bounds: coherent-state lower/upper symbols yield partition-function/free-energy bounds and asymptotic classical limits. They do not assert positivity of the exact contravariant P symbol or an exact finite-N separable decomposition for the target. The Biskup–Chayes–Starr positive-temperature extension (arXiv:math-ph/0509017) is likewise a matrix-element classical approximation under reflection-positivity/classical-limit hypotheses; genuine ferromagnetic/reflection positivity is a mismatch and the bounds are not exact SEP certificates.
• Alon and Kozma, “The mean-field quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet via representation theory,” arXiv:1811.10530, and Alon et al., “Quantum Spins and Random Loops on the Complete Graph,” CMP (2020): exact representation-theoretic formulas and thermodynamic-limit magnetization/partition-function asymptotics for isotropic or XXZ mean-field models. These observable/free-energy results are not finite-N full-separability decompositions and do not supply a uniform δ gate.
• Canosa–Matera–Rossignoli, PRA 76, 022310 (2007), arXiv:1012.4762, and Rossignoli et al., PRA 73, 022347 (2006), give exact or approximate concurrence/global bipartite entanglement borders in fully connected XXZ chains. Their quantities are pairwise or bipartite; a vanishing concurrence or negativity is not full N-party SEP.

SEARCH BOUNDARY / STATUS

Primary searches targeted (i) exact phrases for positive spin-coherent P of quadratic J_z^2 thermal operators, (ii) all-to-all XXZ full separability, and (iii) Gaussian-Dicke/binomial truncated moment positivity. The retrieved closest exact sources above are criteria or narrower constructions; none had the desired uniform finite-N quantifier set. This is a bounded literature audit, not a proof that no theorem exists anywhere or a novelty/priority certification.

The clean mathematical reduction already in the permitted post-freeze peer artifact work/cycle6/c05_s03/revisions/04_axial_all_alpha_reduction.txt says the finite-alpha, all-alpha full-space theorem is equivalent to the all-N symmetric Gaussian-Dicke moment gate at a fixed endpoint delta_*. Yu's result supplies the criterion; the missing imported result would have to establish PSD of its Hankel/localizing matrices for q_k∝exp[-delta(k-N/2)^2/N]/binom(N,k), uniformly over N at a fixed delta>0. I found no literature theorem supplying that missing claim. Therefore report status as “no directly matching prior theorem located; exact import reduces to the open truncated-moment positivity gate,” not as novelty proof.
