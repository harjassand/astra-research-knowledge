C02_S02 PHASE TWO — ACQUIRED MICROSCOPIC KERNEL AND ITS EXACT SCOPE
7 October 2026. INITIAL is preserved. Luna exposure authorized by the owned
phase-two packet. Internal mathematical/algorithmic result, not external review.

RESULT

There is an acquired reversible two-replica kernel, with additive Dirichlet
constant L=2, for an explicit class of arbitrary vector-pair systems: their
rectangular vector array is block-local plus a supplied fixed-rank update,
and each block contains only O(log N) ambient rows plus pair labels. This uses
c02_l05's audited signed tag transfer, an independently implemented exact
conditional sampler, and my independent two-private-row canonical embedding.
No aggregate partition oracle is supplied. The transfer computes its own
conditional masses with the true complex interference.

This is a restricted acquired kernel, using global exact refresh rather than
a bounded-pair local update. It does not solve the arbitrary dense-F gate.
In fact, the complete coordinate-probe enlargement required by the baseline
path forces rank to grow nearly linearly in the original ambient dimension
inside this representation class. A second result proves zero additive energy
for bounded single-replica pair exchanges supplemented by arbitrary-sized
union-preserving two-replica swaps on an actual canonical directed-cycle F.
This refutes that specific local-plus-swap repair, not an auxiliary sampler.

1. ACQUIRED SIGNED CONDITIONAL SAMPLER

Supply rational-complex F=G+XY^T, a coordinate partition into contiguous
blocks of size <=b, block-diagonal G, and N-by-r X,Y. A permutation making
blocks contiguous is charged and eventually inverted on output. Also supply
nonnegative rational row/pair activities lambda_i. For a fixed k, the actual
microscopic state space is disjoint I,J with |I|=|J|=k and point weight

  w(I,J)=lambda_I |det F[I,J]|^2.

At full occupancy N=2k, J=I^c and the state is exactly the canonical paired
full-basis label I. At lower k, J is an explicitly retained minor register;
its projection onto I has the paired Gram-volume law. No coefficient f(U)
of the ambient-hole polynomial is given to the sampler as an oracle.

The complete proof is c02_l05/revisions/fixed_rank_block_tensor.txt, read in
full. Here is the operational interface. Expand each determinant by tags
tau=(p,R,C,S), p<=r, with coefficients
  kappa_tau=det X[R,S] det Y[C,S].
The complementary G minor factors blockwise whenever each residual block
has equal row and column counts, and otherwise is zero. Squaring retains
ALL ordered tag pairs; dropping cross-tag terms is invalid (the audited
rank-two fixture has true c_2=0 but positive diagonal-tag mass 2).

For a tag pair, a block assignment (I_t,J_t) contributes its two residual
determinants d_t conjugate(d'_t), multiplied by lambda_(I_t), and by the
cofactor sign. Prefix row and column counts compute that sign: within-block
position sums plus the two prefix parities. A bivariate count DP up to k
therefore evaluates the exact cross term. Summing the signed tag-pair terms
gives an exact, real, nonnegative completion mass. Fixing any prefix simply
restricts the corresponding local tables; it does not add an oracle.

For the next block, evaluate every one of its <=3^b child completion masses.
They are nonnegative and sum exactly to the parent mass. Clear denominators
and draw from the resulting positive integer weights by power-of-two
rejection; the expected number of trials is <2. Repeating through the blocks
is an exact microscopic sampler. A zero root mass is an empty sector and
returns no distribution. An implementation must retain phases until the
final summed completion mass; cancellation is handled by exact arithmetic.

The owned implementation is fixed_rank_micro_sampler.py. It supports exact
Gaussian rationals, specified rational activities, O(d^3) determinant
elimination, completion masses, exact conditional draws, and the pair kernel
below. Exhaustive direct enumeration is used ONLY by its bounded diagnostics.
The sampler's operational mass routine uses the signed block DP.

There are at most
  T_r=sum_(p=0)^r binom(N,p)^2 binom(r,p)
tags. One completion mass costs T_r^2 poly(N,3^b,b,L_in). The entire draw
uses at most m*3^b such computations and is expected polynomial bit time for
fixed r and b=O(log N). Caches are optional; for a charged finite run of T
draws the stored prefix masses are O(T*m*3^b), or can be cleared between
draws. The local tag-entry cache itself is polynomial for fixed r and b.

Polynomial output-bit length is not inferred from the number of conceptual
terms. Let D clear the rational input denominators. Each amplitude term has
denominator dividing D^(k+p), each squared cross term divides
D^(2k+2r); a common activity denominator adds polynomially many bits. There
are <=3^N*T_r^2 conceptual cross terms, whose LOG number is O(N+log T_r).
Hadamard bounds their numerators, so the accumulated exact rational numerator
and denominator have polynomial bit length. This remains true despite the
exponentially many assignments summarized by the DP. Time, rather than bit
size, becomes superpolynomial when r or b grows.

ADMISSION REPAIR: X,Y,G must be supplied, or independently acquired with

for N>1. Factoring the natural residual only admits the STRICTER class whose

1A. RANK-ONE REFINEMENT: FOUR DEFECT STATES REPLACE ALL ORDERED BLOCK PAIRS

For r=1 use c02_l05's local moment polynomials A_t,B_t,C_t,U_t,V_t. On a
balanced local assignment, put D=det G[I_t,J_t] and
Delta=det(G[I_t,J_t]+u_(I_t)v_(J_t)^T)-D; this requires no inverse and
remains valid for singular minors. The A,B,C coefficients respectively sum
lambda_(I_t)|D|^2, lambda_(I_t)D conjugate(Delta), and
lambda_(I_t)|Delta|^2 at degree |I_t|. For row surplus |I_t|=|J_t|+1,
U sums lambda_(I_t)|det[u_(I_t),G[I_t,J_t]]|^2 at degree |J_t|; V is the
analogous extra top row v_(J_t)^T for column surplus. Signs of that row or
column placement disappear only after its actual determinant is squared.

Rank one repairs at most one unit of total block imbalance. Thus a nonzero
global determinant is either balanced in every block, or has exactly one
row-surplus block and one distinct column-surplus block, all other blocks
balanced. For the balanced sector, G0=product D_t and
G1=sum_t Delta_t product_(s!=t)D_s, and the amplitude is G0+G1. The moments
P=sum|G0|^2, Q=sum G0 conjugate(G1), R=sum|G1|^2 obey

 P'=P A,
 Q'=Q A+P B,
 R'=R A+P C+conjugate(Q) B+Q conjugate(B).

Its charge-k mass is [z^k](P+Q+conjugate(Q)+R). All phase is retained.

The original revision enumerated ordered surplus/deficit block pairs. This
is unnecessary. Maintain four nonnegative polynomials E00,E10,E01,E11,
recording whether a row surplus and a column surplus have appeared. Initially
E00=1 and all others zero. A block update is

 E00'=E00 A,
 E10'=E10 A+E00 U,
 E01'=E01 A+E00 V,
 E11'=E11 A+E10 V+E01 U.

Each transition adds at most one defect in the current block, so the two
defects are necessarily distinct. Conversely every ordered pair of distinct
defect blocks appears exactly once, in either chronological order. Hence the
defect mass is [z^(k-1)]E11. This proves that the entire coefficient needs
only three complex moment polynomials and four positive defect polynomials,
with O(m*k*b) exact arithmetic after local O(m*3^b*poly(b,L_in)) preprocessing.
The potentially quadratic enumeration of defect categories is eliminated.

The same recurrence evaluates any fixed-prefix completion mass by restricting
each fixed block's local table to its assigned (I_t,J_t). Consequently it can
replace the general tag mass routine in the actual sampler and pair kernel,
preserving exact positive child masses at its interface. A naive full draw
costs O(m^2*3^b*k*b) arithmetic after local-table preprocessing; no claimed
sublinear block update or hidden suffix oracle is needed. The owned
rank_one_four_state_refinement.py checks every one of273 prefix masses in
three weighted rank-one sectors against both the signed-tag DP and direct
enumeration, a Gaussian-rational case, and the canonical two-odd-block full
sector whose mass7 comes entirely from the defect state E11.

2. CANONICAL EMBEDDING PRESERVES THE FIXED-RANK BLOCK PRESENTATION

Supply n even, m arbitrary vector pairs (u_l,v_l), rational activities lambda_l,
and V=[u_1,v_1,...,u_m,v_m]. Partition ambient rows into R_t and pair labels
into L_t. Assume
  V=W+XY^T,
where W only couples ambient rows R_t to columns of pairs L_t, X is n-by-r,
Y is 2m-by-r, and r is fixed. Assume
  |R_t|+2|L_t| <=b=O(log N),  N=n+2m.
The partition and W,X,Y are supplied, or their acquisition is separately
charged; the previous admission warning applies.

Append private coordinates s_l,t_l and define canonical K by
  row_(s_l)(K)=u_l^T+e_(t_l)^T,
  row_(t_l)(K)=v_l^T,
with all original ambient rows zero. The canonical pair at coordinate i is
(e_i,row_i(K)^T). Give s_l activity one and t_l activity lambda_l.

Every nonzero canonical full basis must select every s_l: its private row
occurs only in the identity column e_(s_l). Original ambient-row pair labels
are never selected, since their second column is zero. If t_l is absent,
the private t_l row forces the e_(t_l) part of row s_l, removing the old
pair. If t_l is present, a column operation clears that part and the old
u_l,v_l survive. Expansion on the private rows gives a bijection

  old paired full bases A <-> canonical I'={all s_l} union {t_l:l in A},

with exactly the same squared determinant and activity product. This is the
two-row embedding in my independent revision, not a peer-derived identity.

For the local part W, K is block diagonal on R_t plus the private s_l,t_l
for l in L_t. Its update has rank <=r: extend X by zero on private rows,
and place the appropriate rows of Y on the private s/t rows (zero on the
old rows), giving
  K=K_local + Y_tilde X_tilde^T.
Thus the acquired signed sampler applies in dimension N with block size b,
at full sector k=N/2. Mapping its sample I' back by t_l membership samples
the actual arbitrary-vector-pair full-basis point law.

The original arbitrary-pair stability theorem makes these systems legal
members of the same signed-hole/Hurwitz class; it supplies no coefficient
oracle. Here the supplied block/rank presentation, not stability alone,
acquires the conditional masses. The exact owned fixture has n=2,m=2,
two canonical blocks of size3, rank1, old paired weights1 and6, and canonical
full partition7. It checks both the rank factorization and the point-law
bijection, and actually draws canonical samples before mapping them back.

3. AN ACTUAL TWO-REPLICA ENERGY KERNEL WITH L=2 IN THIS CLASS

Let pi be the nonempty microscopic paired full-basis law just compiled.
On (A,B), with probability1/2 refresh A by the acquired exact sampler and
keep B; otherwise refresh B and keep A. Thus

 P((A,B),(A',B'))=
   (1/2) pi(A') 1_(B'=B) + (1/2) pi(B') 1_(A'=A).

This is an actual algorithm: the library pair_step method calls the signed
conditional sampler. Its transition probabilities are rational and
computable in polynomial bit time from the same DP; no f(U) values are
provided externally. Reversibility follows immediately from the symmetric
product pi(A)pi(B)pi(A') for the first branch, and similarly for the second.

For every real f1,f2, put H(A,B)=f1(A)+f2(B), and use the convention
 E_P(H)=1/2 E_(pi tensor pi,P)[(H(X)-H(X'))^2].
An independent A refresh has expected squared change2 Var_pi f1, and the
B branch has2 Var_pi f2. Hence exactly
  E_P(H)=1/2 (Var_pi f1+Var_pi f2),
and
  Var_pi f1 <=2 E_P(H).
There are no aggregate capacities, guide-event probabilities, or rare-event
denominators in this proof. The bound is uniform over all rational activities
and all row gauges as long as the same fixed-rank/log-block presentation is
retained and the sector is nonempty. Its refresh is global, not a claim of
a bounded number of pair-label changes. Since exact counting is already
acquired in this class, the energy theorem does not imply a new unrestricted
FPRAS. It is an explicit scoped completion of the microscopic interface.

4. WHY THE BASELINE COMPLETE-PROBE PATH LEAVES THIS CLASS

The supplied compiler cannot silently be applied to the full coordinate
enlargement. The following bound holds even allowing arbitrary local values
W and a supplied rank-r residual, rather than only the natural residual.

Take n ambient coordinates and include one distinct line (e_i,e_j) for
EVERY i<j. Partition all ambient rows and lines into groups satisfying
|R_t|+2|L_t|<=b. There are at most n groups containing an ambient row.
The total number of coordinate lines assigned to such nonempty-row groups
is at most
  sum_(R_t nonempty) (b-|R_t|)/2 <= n(b-1)/2.

All remaining coordinate lines are in groups with no ambient rows. Their
two local W columns are zero. Both e_i and e_j must therefore lie in the
column space of the rank-r residual XY^T. A rank-r subspace contains at
most r distinct coordinate vectors, so at most binom(r,2) distinct complete
graph lines can be assigned to these empty-row groups. Therefore necessarily

  binom(n,2) <= n(b-1)/2 + binom(r,2),
  r(r-1) >= n(n-b).

For n>b this forces r>=n-b+1. In the canonical embedding of the baseline
F plus all coordinate probes, N=Theta(n^2), so b=O(log N)=O(log n) forces
r=n-O(log n). The tag transfer's fixed-rank polynomial guarantee is lost.
This is a representation-class rank lower bound, not a lower bound against
every sampler. Keeping a single large block instead makes 3^b exponential.

5. BOUNDED EXCHANGES PLUS ARBITRARY UNION SWAPS HAVE ZERO ENERGY

For q>2 let n=2q and F_(i,i+1 mod n)=1, with all other entries zero. Its
canonical pairs are (e_i,e_(i+1)), the edges of a 2q-cycle. There are exactly
two paired full bases: even edge labels A and odd labels B. Their determinants
have modulus one. At unit activities pi(A)=pi(B)=1/2.

Consider ANY reversible microscopic pair kernel whose moves are either
(a) a single-replica move replacing at most2 pair labels, or
(b) a two-replica move preserving the multiset union of the selected labels.
The size bound can be any fixed s<q in the same argument. A nontrivial
single-replica move would replace q pair labels and is impossible on support.
The only nontrivial union-preserving move swaps (A,B) and (B,A); the diagonal
states (A,A),(B,B) cannot leave their union fibers.

Let f1=f2 be the indicator of state B. The additive values on the four
pair states are0,1,1,2, and every allowed move preserves this value. Thus
  E_P(f1+f2)=0,  Var_pi f1=1/4.
No finite additive-energy constant L exists for this entire proposal class,
even though arbitrarily large cycle/layer swaps are allowed. This strengthens
the known bad bounded line-label walk by showing that union-preserving
replica swaps alone do not repair its required additive energy.

The statement survives arbitrary positive activities (variance remains
positive), invertible ambient row changes (every full determinant gains the
same factor), and the exact two-row canonical embedding. These law-preserving
operations cannot repair this fixed proposal class. c02_l08's precise gauge
identity supports that scope; its fixed-window balance counter also blocks
treating weak row calibration as accurate counting data.

Positive coordinate auxiliaries give extra microscopic states and invalidate
the two-state support premise. This counter is NOT an impossibility theorem
for the fully enlarged target, a defect register, non-union-preserving
two-replica moves, or a global acquired refresh. The unrestricted microscopic
energy compiler along the baseline calibrated path remains OPEN.
