2. Complement of the minimum-coupling SU(2) vertex

Let j,L be nonnegative half-integers with K=j+L. In the irreducible tensor
product H_K tensor H_L, the spin-j summand occurs with multiplicity one.
Let V:H_j -> H_K tensor H_L be its normalized intertwining isometry. The
two partial traces are covariant channels Phi and Phi-hat. Let

  |n;s> = U_s(g_n)|s,s>

denote the spin-s coherent vector pointing in direction n, and let dmu be
normalized area measure. The spin-j coherent POVM satisfies

  (2j+1) integral |n;j><n;j| dmu(n) = I_j.

So the proposed measure/prepare map

  Psi(rho)=(2j+1) integral <n;j|rho|n;j>
                                |-n;L><-n;L| dmu(n)

is CPTP and SU(2)-covariant.

For rho=|j,j><j,j|, put x=(1+cos(theta))/2. Under normalized sphere
measure, azimuth integration leaves dx. The POVM weight is
(2j+1)x^(2j) dx. In the state |-n;L>, the z-spin-m probability is

  binom(2L,L+m) x^(L-m)(1-x)^(L+m).

Consequently

  <L,m|Psi(rho)|L,m>
    = (2j+1) binom(2L,L+m)
       B(2j+L-m+1,L+m+1)
    = (2j+1)(2L)!(2j+L-m)!
       /((L-m)!(2j+2L+1)!).

The Clebsch–Gordan coefficient of V on the highest input vector is

  |<K,j-m;L,m | j,j>|^2
    = (2j+1)(2L)!(2j+L-m)!
      /((L-m)!(2j+2L+1)!),

the standard factorial form of the minimum-spin CG coefficient. Thus Phi-hat
and Psi have equal diagonal outputs on |j,j><j,j|; U(1) covariance ensures
there are no off-diagonal entries in that basis.

Covariance gives equality on every coherent projector U_j(g)|j,j><j,j|U_j(g)*.
These projectors span B(H_j): in stereographic coordinate z, their
expectations against an arbitrary matrix X, after multiplication by
(1+|z|^2)^(2j), form a polynomial whose coefficients are the entries X_ab
times nonzero binomial factors. If this polynomial vanishes for all z, every
entry of X vanishes. Hence the orbit is informationally complete, and by
linearity Phi-hat=Psi on all matrices. This proves Phi-hat is entanglement
breaking.

For any channel Phi with complement Phi-hat, a pure input and its Stinespring
output have equal nonzero output/environment spectra. The same is true for
tensor-product Stinespring isometries, including inputs entangled across
uses. Entropy is concave, so the minimum over all states is attained on a
pure state. Therefore

  S_min(Phi tensor Psi0)=S_min(Phi-hat tensor Psi0-hat),
  S_min(Phi)=S_min(Phi-hat),
  S_min(Psi0)=S_min(Psi0-hat).

Shor's theorem states that MOE is additive for an entanglement-breaking
channel tensor an arbitrary channel. Apply it to Phi-hat tensor Psi0-hat:

  S_min(Phi tensor Psi0)
    = S_min(Phi-hat)+S_min(Psi0-hat)
    = S_min(Phi)+S_min(Psi0).

This holds for every finite-dimensional Psi0 and iterates to all tensor
powers. The channel's global single-use MOE is not needed for this strong
additivity conclusion.

For j=1/2, any pure input is SU(2)-related to |up>, so every pure output has
the same spectrum. Specializing the coefficient above with L=K-1/2 gives

  p_m=(L-m+1)/((L+1)(2L+1)),

which is an exact global single-channel entropy certificate. Mixed inputs
cannot have smaller entropy by concavity.

Scope: the SU(2) result covers only the vertex whose input is the minimum-spin
irrep in output tensor environment. It does not cover other vertices or
interior convex combinations of the irreducibly covariant channel simplex.

4. Moore-bound obstruction for the exact permutation realization

This is an internal structural observation scoped to the permutation/high-
girth realization. Assume permutations P_1,...,P_64 on q points satisfy

  Tr(P_w)=0 for every nonidentity reduced word w of length at most 4m.

Join v to P_i v and P_i^-1 v for every i. A word of length one rules out
loops. A nontrivial word of length two rules out collisions among distinct
incident moves (the only excluded case is traversing an edge and immediately
reversing it). Thus the resulting simple graph is 128-regular. A simple
cycle of length at most 4m would read as a reduced nonidentity word fixing
its initial vertex, contradicting the trace hypothesis, since a permutation
trace counts fixed points. Hence the graph has girth greater than 4m.

Breadth-first expansion from any vertex to radius 2m has no collision: a
collision would create a cycle of length at most 4m. Therefore

  q >= 1 + 128 sum_(j=0)^(2m-1) 127^j.

In particular,

  log2(q^2) >= 2 log2(128) + 2(2m-1)log2(127),

whose leading coefficient is 4 log2(127) ~= 27.9547 per m. The source's
explicit q_m=2^(24m-7) gives log2(q_m^2)=48m-14. This quantifies a
construction-family gap only. It does not constrain arbitrary channels or
nonpermutation representations, and reaching the Moore scale would still
leave a very large input.
