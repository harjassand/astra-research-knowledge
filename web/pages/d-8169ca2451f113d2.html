1. CAPACITY CENTER: INDEPENDENTLY DERIVED
For a finite experiment, the capacity supremum is achieved by some
prior p*. Set sigma=sum_i p*_i rho_i and restrict to the common support
of all states. At a maximum this sigma has full support on that union:
otherwise adding an omitted-support state with weightt gives a positive
t log(1/t) entropy improvement, contradicting maximality. Differentiating
the Holevo objective toward a point mass at labeli gives
 D(rho_i||sigma)-C_chi(E)<=0.
Thus one center in conv(E) obeys D(rho_i||sigma)<=M for EVERY i, including
zero-weight labels. Alternatively the average relative-entropy identity
sum_i p_i D(rho_i||omega)=chi(p)+D(sum_i p_i rho_i||omega) proves the
information-radius lower bound; the directional derivative proves its
converse at the maximizing center. There is no dimension/labelcount cost
in this mathematical existence step. A finite experiment can have huge
dimension and many labels despite this radius bound.

2. UNIFORM SMOOTH DOMINATION: CHECKED PRIMARY IMPORT
Jain-Nayak, Short proofs of the Quantum Substate Theorem,
arXiv:1103.6067v2 (12 January2012), Theorem1 page3, was inspected at
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1103.6067 and the authors' copy
https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~anayak/papers/JainN12.pdf.
It gives a normalized rho'_i with squared fidelity>=1-eta and
rho'_i<=sigma 2^(D_obs(rho_i||sigma)/eta)/(1-eta).
D_obs<=D_bits+1. Therefore in natural-log units, for any epsilon in(0,1),
one may take eta=epsilon^2 and obtain
 T(rho'_i,rho_i)<=epsilon,
 rho'_i<=K sigma,
 K=exp[(M+log2)/epsilon^2]/(1-epsilon^2).
The primary result is imported, not independently reconstructed as a new
theorem. The theorem's fidelity convention is squared fidelity. This
normalization matters when converting its parameter to half trace norm.
Each smoothed state can be chosen separately; this is NOT a common
measurement or EB map, and by itself proves no endpoint.

3. FAITHFUL WEIGHTED TRACIAL FINITE ALGEBRA: VERIFIED EXTENSION

tau(X)=sum_r t_r Tr(X_r)/d_r, t_r>0,sumt=1. The reference density
S=direct_sum_r(t_r/d_r)I_(d_r) is CENTRAL. Every unital CP tau-preserving
map is a contraction on the real Hermitian tau-Hilbert space, even when
it mixes center blocks. Its tau-adjoint is CP, unital and tau-preserving:
tau-adjoint= S^-1 ordinary_trace_adjoint(S X), and multiplication by the
positive central S or its inverse is a CP congruence within A. The same
formula works between A and A tensor A, with output density S tensor S.
Consequently all operator steps of the independently audited universal
C4 proof carry over: one randomized spectral POVM; tau-stationary node
marginals; correlated sibling bound C<=I by square positivity; signed
congruence recurrence; full posterior Gram/Schur bound; pure spectral
refinement and compactness. No center invariance or fixed block weights
along individual transitions is required. The center is included among
the tau-orthogonal Hermitian coordinates rather than discarded.
Its arbitrary-map adjoint-composition corollary gives C8 as well.
For a physical channel preserving the central density S, the relative
channel Phi_rel(X)=S^-1 Phi_phys(SX) is unital CP tau-preserving. A
physical broadcaster similarly becomes a tau-preserving relative
broadcaster. The tau-adjoint is physical Heisenberg pullback; the relevant
postprocessing adjoint in density form is S Phi_phys^* S^-1, which is
CPTP exactly when Phi_phys(S)=S. Thus the legal symmetrization does not
assume ordinary unitality of Phi_phys.
The resulting physical EB channel measures effectsM_y and prepares

 ||rho-Psi(rho)||_1^2<=C theta ||rho-Phi_phys(rho)||_1.
Centrality makes Tr S|X| the physical trace norm of SX. All blocks may

4. GENERAL APPROXIMATE REFERENCE AND FAILED NAIVE STATIONARIZATION
After BC symmetrization the marginal Phi is CPTP self-compatible and
T(Phi rho_i,rho_i)<=b. Since sigma is a convex combination,
T(Phi sigma,sigma)<=b. This is only approximate stationarity, not
unitality or exact preservation. Also Phi^* need not be trace-preserving,
so the uniform-reference Phi^*Phi construction cannot simply be applied.
One possible exact stationary perturbation is
 Phi_delta=(1-delta)Phi+delta prep_sigma,
 sigma_delta=delta sum_(n>=0)(1-delta)^n Phi^n(sigma).
It is self-compatible using the same mixture with prep_(sigma tensor sigma),
preserves sigma_delta, and has T(sigma_delta,sigma)<=b/delta. Moreover
sigma_delta>=delta sigma. However the immediate relative domination is
rho'_i<=(K/delta)sigma_delta, while its preservation error includesdelta.
The naive state-C8 estimate then contains(K/delta)*delta=K and fails to
vanish. This is a preserved failed argument, not a counterexample to the
endpoint. A separate smoothing/repair theorem with controlled reference
domination could conceivably fix this, but was not proved here.

5. CENTRALIZATION/NONTRACIAL GATE: NOT ASSUMED
A faithful density sigma generally is not central in fullM_d. Pinching
into its exact eigenspaces makes it central but can erase coherences;
bounded relative entropy alone does not give a vanishing pinching loss
as b->0 without using the broadcasting information. Flattening coarse
spectral bands and invoking the tracial theorem requires explicit
control of state disturbance, reference error, and the legal broadcaster.
Those controls are the main missing bridge in this baseline.
A potential alternative nontracial KMS route emerged late but is UNVERIFIED
here. In KMS inner product Tr sigma^(1/2)A sigma^(1/2)B, stationarity and
Petz adjoints are CP. A single leaf's spectral variance is instead
Tr sigma A^2, not its KMS norm. For sigma=diag(s,1-s), A proportional to
PauliX, their ratio is1/[2sqrt(s(1-s))], unbounded as s->0. Thus the
tracial square argument only yields C<=the GNS variance matrix, notI.
It is possible that a separate coupling/KMS contraction argument restores
C<=I; that statement, and the required stationary-reference perturbation,
were not checked to theorem status. No nontracial C4/C8 theorem is claimed.

6. FALSIFICATION CONTROLS AND SCOPE
Capacity does not imply bounded ordinary likelihood rho/(I/d), bounded
dimension, or a small label count. For d orthogonal basis labels take
rho_i=(1-alpha)I/d+alpha|i><i|, alpha=M/logd when<1. A flagged preparation
argument gives C_chi<=alpha logd=M, but the maximal density ratio toI/d
is1-alpha+alpha d, unbounded. These states commute, so their e_EB is
exactly0; this is a counterexample only to the density-bound shortcut.
Experiments uniformly bounded aroundI/d are already controlled by the
audited C8 state theorem with a sqrt(b) modulus. Families with a common
central faithful tracial reference and bounded relative operator norm
are likewise controlled by Section3. Known copy-ladder failures without
capacity control do not refute the proposed endpoint. No valid fixed-M,
b->0,e_EB bounded-away-from-zero construction was obtained.
The elementary reverse b2<=e_EB is valid: a measure/prepare EB map has
a two-output broadcaster preparing the same output state on both legs
conditional on its one outcome. It does not prove the converse modulus.
