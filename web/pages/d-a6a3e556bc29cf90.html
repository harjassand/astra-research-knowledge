# Prior-art attack

## The initial residual-bound route is established algebra

For reaction matrix `S`, signed flux `v`, and chemical-potential vector `μ`, reaction free energies satisfy `g=Sᵀμ`. Therefore `vᵀg=μᵀSv`; at steady state the inner product vanishes, and near steady state it is controlled by the measured mass-balance residual. Adding a uniform affinity margin gives an immediate norm bound. This is a useful diagnostic identity, but it is not a new theorem.

Beard, Liang, and Qian (2004), Theorem 6.1, already characterize which steady-state flux vectors obey both mass balance and thermodynamic constraints by the internal stoichiometric cycle/oriented-matroid structure. Their introduction states that the stoichiometric matrix alone supplies constraints on thermodynamically feasible fluxes. Fleming et al. (2010), Sections 2–3 and 8, formulate mass conservation, energy conservation, second-law inequalities, reversible enzyme kinetics, and experimental/theoretical bounds together; they explicitly say kinetic rate laws dictate flux magnitude at a given concentration. Thermodynamic flux analysis and PTA subsequently account for uncertain standard Gibbs energies and concentrations. Loopless FVA directly removes thermodynamically infeasible internal cycles.

Sources:

- Beard, Liang & Qian (2004), *Thermodynamic constraints for biochemical networks*, Theorem 6.1: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2004.01.008
- Fleming et al. (2010), *Integrated stoichiometric, thermodynamic and kinetic modelling of steady state metabolism*, equations (1), (3), (27), Table 1 and Section 8: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2010.02.044
- Schellenberger et al. (2018), *Accelerating flux balance calculations in genome-scale metabolic models by localizing the application of loopless constraints*, TIC definition and loopless-FVA formulation: https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/34/24/4248/5026660
- Noor et al. (2021), *Probabilistic thermodynamic analysis of metabolic networks*, joint uncertainty in free energies/concentrations and flux-direction sampling: https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/37/18/2938/6182675
- Haraldsdóttir et al. (2021), *multiTFA*, multivariate thermodynamic uncertainty and component-contribution estimates: https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btab392

## Reversible stochastic escape candidate

The candidate does not improve the general deterministic global-attractor theorem or the stochastic product-form theorem. Craciun’s 2015 proof of the Global Attractor Conjecture establishes global attraction for complex-balanced mass-action systems in each positive stoichiometric compatibility class. Anderson, Craciun, and Kurtz (2010), Theorem 2.1/Section 4 in the arXiv version, prove product-Poisson stationary laws on each closed irreducible stochastic component when the deterministic system is complex balanced; the deficiency-zero theorem supplies the relevant equilibrium for weakly reversible deficiency-zero networks. The key mismatch is that stochastic components can be proper subsets of the integer states inside one real compatibility class, and product form is normalized separately on each component.

Astra U/N77 already reports a one-way, complex-balanced example with the same escape of stationary laws. The present candidate adds reverse channels so the exact witness is detailed-balanced. The explicit product-Poisson weights follow from established theory and a three-state class calculation. I found no exact match for this reversible completion in the bounded search below, but that is not a novelty result.

Sources:

- Craciun (2015), *Toric Differential Inclusions and a Proof of the Global Attractor Conjecture*: https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.02860
- Anderson, Craciun & Kurtz (2010), *Product-form stationary distributions for deficiency zero chemical reaction networks*, Theorem 2.1 and Section 4: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-010-9517-4 ; preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3042
- Cappelletti & Wiuf (2016), product-form distributions and complex balance on stochastic components: https://doi.org/10.1137/15M1029916

## Search boundary

The targeted search checked primary sources on thermodynamic flux-cycle criteria, loopless FVA/TFA, thermodynamic/kinetic integrated models, complex-balanced global attraction, and product-form stationary distributions. The established results subsume the initial identity and all proof ingredients for the candidate. They do not certify the candidate’s historical priority. No theorem about uniform tightness over all count components should be inferred from “there is a product-Poisson stationary law on each component.”
