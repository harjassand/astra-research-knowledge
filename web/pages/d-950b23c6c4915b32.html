# C9 failure ledger and restart gates

## Failed or demoted routes

1. **Promote `ell <= D/v` into a Fog-to-wave-speed law — rejected.** The result is exact only for a source-free scalar tail with constant material-frame `D`, nonnegative first-order clearance, and no local source/gain. The 2026 slow-Fog component is not chemically identified as active/receptor-bound, its FCS coefficient is not established as material-frame mobility, and no matched active-pool clearance, MyoII length, and cell-row speed is supplied. The 2019 paper additionally reports increased Fog/MyoII without increased front speed. Keep the C8 result as a narrow comparator.

2. **Infer a numeric threshold from MyoII exponential length, amplitude, or thresholded range — rejected.** The reported `>` ranges are censored; different readouts are not interchangeable; the 2026 profile data are not a matched speed sweep; and force/activity coefficients are unmeasured. Such an inversion is not identifiable from the supplied summaries.

3. **Treat the fast/slow FCS decomposition as direct receptor occupancy — rejected.** The slow population is interpreted as membrane/receptor associated, not chemically established; FCS is measured at distinct spots/conditions rather than by tracking the same active molecules along cell material trajectories.

4. **Treat monotone integrin amplification as the whole story — demoted.** The 2026 data place integrin downstream of or alongside clustering as a signaling cofactor, while 2024 gives integrin-mediated adhesion a load-bearing role. A model omitting either side cannot derive the conditional sign switch. The proposed saturation and force closures remain assumptions, not source-fitted laws.

5. **Treat a sharp local Hill fold as the tissue invasion threshold — rejected.** The model attack derives a fold for one assumed ODE, but its drive, feedback gain, mechanical coupling, and detachment law are not identified. Local bistability is not a traveling-wave criterion. Do not use a generic Turing condition or a simulation movie as a replacement.

6. **Use a front-frame spatial exponential alone to identify transport — rejected.** An exact contact-age waveform reproduces the same spatial profile. Source-reported cell movies add information but do not pair active-pool mobility/clearance with calibrated force/contact state in a way that identifies the proposed threshold. This is a bounded nonidentifiability result, not a claim that all source data are identical under both mechanisms.

7. **Claim new GPCR/endocytosis mechanism — rejected.** Jha et al. (2018) already establish Fog/Smog clustering and Gprk2/β-arrestin receptor-removal control. C9’s narrow candidate is the explicit dual integrin cofactor/load interface and its weakest-cell implication; external novelty remains unverified.

## Gates to resume the biological threshold claim

### Gate 1 — acquire the state variables in the same cells

Measure first-contact time, front speed relative to tracked material rows, `theta_i`, MyoII, a validated receptor activity/occupancy state, active integrin/cofactor state, adhesion state, and detachment timing/force from the same embryos. Pair mobility and pulse-chase clearance of the identified active pool in the same condition. Use embryo as the replicate; preserve matched diffusible versus tethered Fog and initial invagination controls.

**Pass:** state variables and uncertainty are separately calibrated, and force reserve `Delta_i` can be estimated per cell.  
**Fail/stop:** fluorescence proxies cannot be converted or independently validated; keep C9-M1/M2 as model-only and retain the transport-threshold no-go.

### Gate 2 — test the actual cellwise relay condition

Predeclare `Delta_i` and the detachment rule. Measure enough serial rows to distinguish strict serial recruitment from bypass or long-range mechanical transmission. Compare observed detachment/recruitment with `min_i Delta_i>0` on held-out cells/embryos; stratify on angle and contact geometry.

**Pass:** all held-out propagation/stall outcomes agree within calibrated uncertainty and no-bypass structure is observed.  
**Fail/demote:** propagation crosses reliably nonpositive cells, cells detach under nonpositive calibrated reserve, or geometry/mechanics require a different law; replace the threshold model and preserve that failure.

### Gate 3 — test the sign-switch, not an assumed monotone dose response

At fixed or stratified receptor state and angle, titrate integrin/cofactor/load; evaluate `dDelta/dI`. Fit the saturating cofactor and adhesive-load terms separately, then test the predicted switch on held-out `O, I, theta` conditions. Include a matched mechanical-relay baseline, receptor-only monotone model, and integrin-amplifier-only model.

**Pass:** the force-calibrated reserve changes sign with integrin in the predicted direction and predicts held-out detachment better than equal-information baselines.  
**Fail/demote:** sign remains single over a sufficiently broad and powered range, the inferred saturation fails, or a simpler relay predicts held-out outcomes equally well. Do not claim a tissue intervention principle.

### Gate 4 — distinguish transport, contact age and coupled relay

Compare on identical train/holdout splits: (A) passive transport with independently measured `D,k,v_rel`; (B) local contact-age response; (C) Fog-gated integrin/mechanics with measured contact, angle, and detachment. Hold out at least one Fog/Gprk2 level and one integrin/contact condition. Score per-embryo profile, timing, and speed prediction with uncertainty and parameter-count cost.

**Pass for transport:** measured inputs predict held-out active-pool and output profiles, the passive bound holds where assumptions apply, and the contact-age/mechanical models lose on equal-information prediction.  
**Pass for C9 relay:** calibrated cellwise reserve predicts held-out propagation better than the alternatives.  
**Fail/demote:** contact-age or mechanical relay predicts equally well/better, the passive bound fails without independently measured gain/source, or no model predicts a held-out dose.

## Cost and stop condition

The papers do not provide data to calculate power or money costs. A pilot must estimate embryo-level variance before sample sizing. Required costs include custom genotypes/reporters, staged embryos, time-resolved confocal tracking, FCS/pulse-chase, a force or detachment calibration method, blinded extraction, and independent embryo replication. No public-health, wound-healing, regeneration, or cross-species application follows from the Drosophila model.

Stop the numeric-threshold programme if the active pool cannot be identified, force reserve cannot be measured, or the model loses the held-out comparison. The next valid product in that case is the no-go and the strongest measured relay description, not a more elaborate uncalibrated PDE.
