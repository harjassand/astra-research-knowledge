# Applied foundational capability screen

Date: 2026-10-10.

The goal was a genuinely new scientific/computational capability, rather than
a faster familiar solver. This record preserves constructive work without
inflating it into a discovery.

## Substantially different interfaces considered

### 1. Discover hidden nonlinear networks from terminal responses

Nonlinear constitutive responses can carry information absent from a linear
transfer map. However, an architecture-level novelty claim collides with
existing nonlinear electrical inverse problems and higher-order response
tomography. Johnson's primary work explicitly extends circular-planar
recoverability to nonlinear and signed conductors:

https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.4045
https://sites.math.washington.edu/~reu/papers/2017/willjohnson/nonlinear.pdf

No new identifiable network class or native measurement mechanism was derived.
This was a screen, not a proved impossibility result.

### 2. Preserve a physical system's history for later destructive readout

This would matter wherever time-resolved observation destroys or perturbs
the specimen. But the broad capability is already demonstrated in molecular
recording. DNA Typewriter records ordered symbols; molecular time capsules
and transcriptome-storage devices preserve earlier cellular states.

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04922-8
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10614764/
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adz9353

No new physical recording primitive was supplied.

### 3. Compile specified computations into autonomous chemistry

A putative inverse compiler from desired input/output behavior to chemistry
would be a useful interface. However, rate-independent chemical computation,
composability, neural-network compilation, and equilibrium chemical
computation already have substantial literature. One direct primary example:

https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2111552119

No new computational class or physically robust compiler was established.

### 4. Recover population transition rules from deliberately coded marginals

This was selected for a complete positive derivation rather than stopped at
a superficial negative screen. The construction includes:

- Exact cancellation of diagonal growth/retention at the derivative level.
- A deterministic code and min decoder for sparse incoming transitions.
- An exact finite-interval occupation/endpoints identity.
- Generic sparse identifiability, a constructive support decoder, and
  explicit conditioning and measurement-error requirements.
- Rational tests of 16,256 individual rates and finite-interval recovery of
  every row of an eight-state system.

See `CODED_POPULATION_CONSTRUCTION.md`, `verify_coded_population.py`, and
`coded_population_exact_results.json`.

**Acquisition-advantage verdict: not established.** With joint single-entity
code readout, ordinary origin barcoding already supplies the information.
With only bulk marginals, the method is a combination of tracer tomography,
sparse identification and known coding. Arbitrary state-dependent writing
is an unsupplied native-access assumption. Spatial/spectral implementations
would need comparison against molecular-tagging velocimetry and exchange
spectroscopy, rather than assuming those fields lack the capability.

The construction is therefore downgraded to a conditional synthesis of known
tools, not presented as a civilization-scale invention. It does not prove
that a useful implementation or narrower original result is impossible.

### 5. Endpoint-only temporal-order spectroscopy

A parallel constructive branch derived a small noncommuting probe and an
unbounded hierarchy of forward-versus-reversed path-statistic witnesses
without time-resolved recording. Its full proof, tests and prior-art status
are preserved under `measurement_interface/`. Noncommutativity, Chen
iterated integrals, path signatures, commutator control, rotating-field
spectroscopy and quantum time-reversal probes are established. Any new
capability must survive those comparisons and supply the native interaction
that acquires an otherwise inaccessible observable.

**Acquisition-advantage verdict: not established.** The only demonstrated
separation is from autonomous commuting integrators. The signal-dependent
coherent coupling and reference readout are standard. The rotation example
is a spin-1 Rabi problem; the completeness ingredients substantially overlap
existing sparse path-characteristic-function theory. No concrete observable
inaccessible to existing native sensors was identified. This branch is also
downgraded to a narrower instrument-design lead.

Relevant primary boundaries:

https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.51.652
https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.6.020320
