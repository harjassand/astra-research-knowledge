STATUS AND EXACT CONSEQUENCE

Self-contained derived extension of the campaign's existing flagged matching
family; pending independent audit. The FAMILY and its constant EB loss are
baseline, not new. The stronger calculation here controls the ENTIRE inherited
operator-norm unit ball of its linear operator system, including every linear
combination, rather than only the displayed generators.

There exist finite-dimensional operator systems S_n in M_(D_n), n even,
whose entire Hermitian unit balls obey

  sup_(X,Y in S_n, Hermitian, ||X||,||Y||<=1) ||[X,Y]||_2
      = 2/sqrt(n-1) ->0,                                      (A)

but

  inf_(Lambda ambient EB, TP) sup_(X in S_n, Hermitian, ||X||<=1)
       ||Lambda(X)-X||_1 = 1/2.                               (B)

Normalized trace is tau=Tr/D_n. Also any simultaneous commuting approximation
to the n generators has maximum L2 error >=1/sqrt(2). Thus increasing a
finite-family proof to the entire LINEAR operator-system ball does not itself
remove growing-size dependence.

This is NOT a counterexample to the campaign's actual full-C*-domain UCP
power-range theorem. Indeed, any full-C*-domain UCP map T whose range is
contained in S_n and whose input unit ball approximates every generator has
a dimension-free error floor

  max_i inf_(X_i=X_i*, ||X_i||<=1) ||T(X_i)-A_i||_2
        >= (3-sqrt(5))/4 = 0.1909830056... .                    (C)

Formula (C) is a quantitative multiplicative-domain obstruction, proved
below. It makes the CP supplied/acquired boundary concrete: an abstract
almost-commuting operator system, even with a UCP identity map on itself,
cannot be silently upgraded to a full-algebra CP range with the same ball.

1. CONSTRUCTION — BASELINE MATCHING FAMILY

Use a 1-factorization F of the complete graph on even n vertices. There
are n-1 flags, each a partition into n/2 disjoint pairs, and each unordered
pair appears in exactly one flag. An explicit round-robin construction has
vertices {0,...,n-2,infinity}. Flag f pairs infinity with f and pairs
f+j with f-j modulo n-1 for j=1,...,n/2-1.

At flag f, put one qubit on each pair {i,j}; assign Pauli X to the smaller
endpoint and Pauli Z to the larger endpoint. Let A_i^(f) be that Pauli on
i's qubit and identity on other qubits. On

  H_n=directsum_(f in F) (C^2)^(tensor n/2),
  D_n=(n-1)2^(n/2),

define A_i=directsum_f A_i^(f) and S_n=span_C{I,A_1,...,A_n}.
Every A_i is a traceless Hermitian involution, and
tau(A_i A_j)=delta_ij. S_n is a finite-dimensional operator system, not a
C*-subalgebra. This same family is in work/cycle1/quantum_classicality.txt;
its broadcast obstruction was independently audited in cycle3. Nothing
here turns its positive broadcasting floor into a vanishing one.

2. EXACT NORM OF ALL LINEAR COMBINATIONS

For real c=(c_1,...,c_n), let A(c)=sum_i c_i A_i. Within one flag,
the term on pair {i,j} is c_i X+c_j Z, with eigenvalues
+/-sqrt(c_i^2+c_j^2). Different pairs act on independent tensor factors.
Their simultaneous extreme eigenvectors show

  ||A(c)||_infty=max_f sum_({i,j} in f) sqrt(c_i^2+c_j^2).       (1)

Each flag's spectrum is symmetric around zero. Consequently

  ||a I+A(c)||_infty=|a|+||A(c)||_infty,
  ||A(c)||_infty>=sqrt(sum_i c_i^2)=||c||_2.                   (2)

Therefore every Hermitian X=aI+A(c) in the inherited unit ball of S_n
has ||c||_2<=1. This is a bound for every coefficient vector in the full
unit ball, not a sign-vector or selected-generator restriction.

3. EXACT COMMUTATOR OF ALL LINEAR COMBINATIONS

On flag f, only the paired labels anticommute. Hence

  [A(c),A(d)]|_f
       = sum_({i,j} in f) (c_i d_j-c_j d_i)[A_i^(f),A_j^(f)].   (3)

Each commutator is +/-2iY on its pair qubit, with normalized squared
Hilbert-Schmidt norm4. The different pair-qubit Y operators are mutually
orthogonal under normalized trace. Averaging the n-1 flags and using
the one-occurrence property gives

  ||[A(c),A(d)]||_2^2
    = 4/(n-1) sum_(i<j) (c_i d_j-c_j d_i)^2
    = 4/(n-1) [||c||_2^2 ||d||_2^2-(c dot d)^2].              (4)

The last equality is Lagrange's identity, obtained by expanding the
squares. Scalar multiples of I drop out. Formula (2) now bounds (4) by
4/(n-1) throughout the whole Hermitian operator-system unit ball. Equality
is attained at c=e_i,d=e_j, so (A) is exact.

For arbitrary non-Hermitian unit contractions, split real and imaginary
parts, each still a Hermitian unit contraction in S_n. Four commutators
give a bound 8/sqrt(n-1), also tending to zero. Thus the phenomenon is not
an artifact of restricting to chosen real generators.

4. COLLECTIVE NUMERICAL RANGE AND EB LOSS — FULL RECONSTRUCTION

For every density matrix eta on H_n, set v_i=Tr(eta A_i). Pinching the
flag does not change these expectations. Within a flag and each qubit,
the two relevant expectations x,z satisfy x^2+z^2<=1: for u=(x,z), the
operator xX+zZ has norm sqrt(x^2+z^2), while its expectation is x^2+z^2.
Summing qubits gives sum_i v_i^2<=n/2 on a flag. Mixing flags preserves
the same bound by convexity of squared Euclidean norm. Therefore

       sum_i [Tr(eta A_i)]^2 <=n/2                          (5)

for EVERY state, including entangled qubit states and coherent flag states.

Let Lambda be any full-domain TP EB map. Write its actual finite factorization

  Lambda(Z)=sum_z Tr(M_z Z) rho_z,
  M_z>=0, sum_z M_z=I, rho_z>=0, Tr rho_z=1.

Put w_z=Tr M_z/D_n, m_(z,i)=Tr[(M_z/Tr M_z)A_i], and
v_(z,i)=Tr(rho_z A_i), ignoring zero effects. Both m_z,v_z obey (5), so

  sum_i tau[A_i Lambda(A_i)]
    =sum_z w_z (m_z dot v_z)
    <=sum_z w_z ||m_z||_2 ||v_z||_2
    <=n/2.                                                    (6)

If Lambda approximates every A_i in normalized L1 to error q, trace
duality and ||A_i||=1 imply tau[A_i Lambda(A_i)]>=1-q for each i.
Formula (6) then gives q>=1/2. This covers every TP EB map, without
unitality, output-flag preservation, POVM outcome bound, or separate
measurements for different i.

For attainment, measure the flag. On each of its n/2 qubits apply the
EB channel (D_X+D_Z)/2, where D_X and D_Z are complete Pauli eigenbasis
dephasings. Their tensor product is EB and maps both local X and Z to
half themselves. Keep the measured flag in the output. Thus the full EB
map Lambda_0 fixes I and sends every A_i to A_i/2. For aI+A(c) in the
unit ball, its error is

  ||Lambda_0(aI+A(c))-(aI+A(c))||_1
       = ||A(c)||_1/2 <= ||A(c)||_infty/2 <=1/2.

Together with (6), this proves (B) exactly.

For the actual state experiment rho_(i,+/-)=(I+/-a A_i)/D_n,
0<a<1, sign subtraction gives the baseline exact EB half-trace loss a/4.
Its Holevo capacity is the bounded c(a) recorded in the baseline; that
capacity fact is not needed in this new unit-ball calculation.

For any ambient MASA expectation P_D, apply (5) to its eigenbasis vectors.
Then sum_i ||P_D(A_i)||_2^2<=n/2, whereas sum_i ||A_i||_2^2=n.
Orthogonal projection gives sum_i ||A_i-P_D(A_i)||_2^2>=n/2, hence maximum
error >=1/sqrt(2). If arbitrary commuting approximants C_i are supplied,
their common eigenbasis P_D fixes all C_i; then
||(I-P_D)A_i||_2<=||A_i-C_i||_2. The same lower bound applies.

5. THE FULL-C*-DOMAIN CP OBSTRUCTION — FULL DERIVATION

First note a structural fact: for every i!=j, A_i A_j is orthogonal to
S_n in L2(tau) and has norm1 in L2. Indeed on any flag it is either
+/-iY on one qubit, or a two-qubit product of X/Z on distinct qubits.
Both have trace zero and are orthogonal to I and every single-qubit
X/Z generator. This holds flag by flag. Thus

       dist_2(A_i A_j,S_n)=1.                                 (7)

Let B be ANY unital C*-algebra and T:B->M_(D_n) any UCP map with
T(B) contained in S_n. Suppose Hermitian contractions X_i in B obey
||T(X_i)-A_i||_2<=eta for all i. Write U_i=T(X_i), also contractions.
For eta<=1, ||U_i||_2>=1-eta. Kadison positivity and X_i^2<=I imply

  0<=tau[T(X_i^2)-U_i^2] <=1-(1-eta)^2<=2eta.                 (8)

Take a Stinespring isometry V for T and projection Q=I-VV*.
For input contractions X,Y,

 T(XY)-T(X)T(Y)=V* pi(X) Q pi(Y) V,
 ||T(XY)-T(X)T(Y)||_2
       <=||X|| sqrt(tau[T(Y*Y)-T(Y)*T(Y)]).                    (9)

The inequality follows from ||V*pi(X)||<=||X|| and computing the
normalized Hilbert-Schmidt norm squared of Q pi(Y)V. It does not need
an invariant domain trace, trace preservation, selfadjointness of T, or
compatibility. Apply (8)-(9) to X_i,X_j:

  ||T(X_i X_j)-U_i U_j||_2 <=sqrt(2eta),
  ||U_i U_j-A_i A_j||_2
     <=||U_i-A_i||_2 ||U_j|| + ||A_i|| ||U_j-A_j||_2
     <=2eta.                                                  (10)

Since X_i X_j belongs to the FULL input algebra, T(X_i X_j) belongs
to S_n. Combining (7) and (10),

       1<=sqrt(2eta)+2eta.                                    (11)

Set z=sqrt(2eta). Then z+z^2>=1, giving
eta>=(3-sqrt(5))/4. This proves (C). At eta=0 this is the ordinary
multiplicative-domain argument: a UCP map carrying contractions to exact
unitaries must carry their products too. Its range cannot stop at S_n.

The robust version also handles approximate ranges: if all complex unit
inputs Z satisfy dist_2(T(Z),S_n)<=epsilon, (11) becomes
1<=epsilon+sqrt(2eta)+2eta. If only Hermitian input range proximity is
promised, decomposing X_iX_j into real/imaginary parts costs 2epsilon.

An identity map id:S_n->M_(D_n) is UCP as an OPERATOR-SYSTEM map and has
the uniformly commuting ball (A), but its ambient EB approximation still
has error1/2 by (B). A full CP extension can be the ambient identity;
its image contains all products and its full input-ball commutators are
not small. This is the exact place at which a naive Arveson-extension
or abelian-ultraproduct lifting route changes the interface.
