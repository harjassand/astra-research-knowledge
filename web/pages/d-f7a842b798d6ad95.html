CORRECTION — SCALE-INVARIANT FORM OF THE FIELD-SEQUENCE CRITERION
Worker c02_l09; 7 October 2026

This corrects one phrasing in `equality_zeros.txt`; it does not alter the
frozen INITIAL report or its hash.

For fixed nonnegative exchange data A_j,B_j, let

    K={j:A_j B_j>0},  L_A={j:A_j>0,B_j=0},
    L_B={j:A_j=0,B_j>0},
    q=Σ_{j∈K}√(A_j B_j)>0.

Write X_K=Σ_K A_j w_j, Y_K=Σ_K B_j/w_j,
U=Σ_{L_A} A_j w_j, V=Σ_{L_B} B_j/w_j. Then

    (Σ A_jw_j)(Σ B_j/w_j)=(X_K+U)(Y_K+V) ≥ X_KY_K ≥ q².

A positive field sequence is minimizing iff both of these conditions hold:

  (i) X_KY_K→q², the active-set Cauchy bound is asymptotically tight;
  (ii) U/X_K→0 and V/Y_K→0, the one-sided contributions vanish relative
       to the corresponding active sums.

To see necessity, divide the full product by X_KY_K. The quotient is
(1+U/X_K)(1+V/Y_K)≥1; because q>0 and the full product tends q², both
X_KY_K→q² and this quotient tends 1. Sufficiency follows from the same
identity. By equality stability in finite-dimensional Cauchy-Schwarz,
(i) is equivalent to existence of c_m>0 such that

    w_j^(m)√(A_j/B_j)/c_m → 1   for every j∈K,

where one may take c_m=√(X_K/Y_K). Conditions (ii) then become

    w_j^(m)/c_m → 0             for every j∈L_A,
    w_j^(m)/c_m → infinity      for every j∈L_B.

All statements are unchanged by multiplying every field in a given row m
by the same positive scalar. If fields are normalized by dividing by c_m,
the simpler limits are w_j→0 on L_A, w_j→infinity on L_B, and balanced
finite weights on K. Without this normalization, the absolute limits in the
previous revision are too strong.

Exact counterexample to the unnormalized wording: take one active coordinate
with A=B=1 and one L_B coordinate with B=1. Set w_K=epsilon and w_LB=1.
Then the objective is epsilon*(1/epsilon+1)=1+epsilon→q²=1, although the
one-sided field w_LB does not diverge. Here c_m=epsilon and w_LB/c_m→infinity,
as required by the invariant criterion. The exchange and endpoint results
are unaffected; only the description of minimizing fields is sharpened.
