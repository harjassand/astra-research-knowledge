PUBLIC ACQUISITION AND FINITE CIRCUIT COSTS FOR THE COLLECTIVE COMPRESSOR
2026-10-08. Internal candidate; no uncharged spectral/Galerkin/Gibbs oracle.
Companion to proof.txt. External validation and priority UNKNOWN.

1. SUPPLIED DATA, COMPUTABILITY, AND COST CONVENTION

Supplied: n copies of ONE unknown-parameter finite-chain Gibbs density;
the chain size L and public J,tau,B,g_-,g_+,n,epsilon; the standard local
occupation/spin basis and the ability to perform elementary quantum gates.
No estimates of unknown parameters, eigenstates, spectra, gaps, compressed
Hamiltonian, Gibbs-state preparation, or infinite-dimensional physical
operations are supplied. The mathematical continuum theorem asserts
channels on Fock space; its finite hardware realization uses a proxy below.

For the literal exact-arithmetic acquisition algorithm, take public
J,tau,B,g endpoints and requested error budgets rational. The channels
exist for arbitrary real public parameters. Computable real parameters
can be handled by certified interval arithmetic with a supplied AND
charged precision modulus; noncomputable real inputs are not a finite
algorithmic claim. Constants involving pi/Gamma/Gaussian sums are enclosed
by elementary integrals and the banked rational rate majorants.

Persistent state-dependent memory is the quantum/classical archive in
proof.txt. Public descriptions, regenerated basis tables, quantum input,
reconstructed output, classical/quantum work space, preprocessing, and
gate count are separate resources, explicitly charged below. They can be
enormous. No fit in a 16-GB local machine or practical runtime is asserted.

2. TWO-BODY HEAT TRACES WITHOUT DIAGONALIZATION

In each finite number sector, A_L(g) is an explicitly known rational real
symmetric matrix in the occupation basis, dimension a=binom(L-1,m).
Its entries are obtained from the exact configuration-graph form. The
simple known norm bound is

                  0<=A_L(g)<=Lambda I,
                  Lambda=4J L^3+g_+ L^2.               (2.1)

For the derivative-constant acquisition use coupling bound 2g_+ here.
The bound follows by summing positive exchange and endpoint operator
norms: ||Hcrit||<=4J(L-1); W<=L I. Sharper sector-specific bounds are
optional and not required for termination.

For a rational positive matrix A and rational tau, compute

                   T_k=Tr(I+(tau/k)A)^(-k)

by exact rational inversion and binary powering. The scalar Euler bound
in quantitative_rate.txt Section 6 gives |T_k-Tr exp(-tau A)|<=2a/k for
k>=max(1,tau). Take k>=4a/alpha to obtain a certified trace enclosure
of radius <=alpha/2. All arithmetic can be exact, without a spectrum.
This costs O(a^3 log k) rational arithmetic operations (matrix inversion
and powering). BIT complexity also charges the growing rational numerator
and denominator sizes: direct elimination/powering yields a finite
bound polynomial in a,k and the input coefficient bit lengths, so the
log k arithmetic count is not being called a log k bit-time algorithm.
One may reduce fractions; no favorable condition number is assumed.

This gives every finite two-body evaluation in proof.txt Section 4 and
every F_L bisection evaluation. For z1 use the known one-particle rational
matrix and the same algorithm, or certified sine-level sums. The acquired
offline inverse constant may force a huge terminating L_j. Record its
actual j,L_j,k,a and arithmetic precision if run; this programme has NOT
executed that enormous acquisition or certified a useful numerical value.

For a continuum trace accuracy alpha, choose public R_aux large enough
that the exponential term in H_2 is <=alpha/4, and public proxy L large
enough that the explicit decreasing-power terms are <=alpha/4. Then the
finite two-body Euler evaluation to alpha/2 gives the desired enclosure.
This is a fixed-sector FINITE algorithm with no limiting oracle.
At bisection accuracy alpha=O(n^-1/2), the explicit H_2 power bound permits
log L_proxy=O(log(n/eta)) at fixed public parameters. Matrix dimension
O(L_proxy^2) and exact rational bit costs are charged. No polynomial with
a small or empirically relevant exponent is claimed.

3. OUTER ENERGY THRESHOLDS WITH A PUBLIC GAP, NO INNER GAPS

Choose integer R satisfying proof.txt (7.1). Let Dbar be any integer
certified ABOVE D(R) from proof.txt (6.1), where D(R) bounds rank below 2R.
Obtain Dbar by evaluating that scalar formula to a rational upper bound
and rounding upward; an extra unit avoids deciding equality to an integer.
There are at most Dbar eigenvalues (counted with multiplicity) <=2R in
the ENTIRE Fock space. The same is true within every retained sector.

Set K=2Dbar+2, s=R/K, Delta=s/4, and consider K rational midpoints

                   E_j=R+(j-1/2)s, 1<=j<=K.

The intervals [E_j-Delta,E_j+Delta] are disjoint and lie strictly inside
(R,2R). Each eigenvalue can spoil at most one of these intervals. There
are more intervals than eigenvalues in (R,2R), so at least one interval
contains NO spectrum. For each retained sector compute exact rational
inertias of A-E_j-Delta and A-E_j+Delta. Reject a zero determinant at an
endpoint; accept equal negative-inertia counts. The accepted E has

                   dist(E,spec A)>=Delta,
                   R<E<2R.                            (3.1)

These tests are finite, exact, and terminate in <=K trials. Inertia can be
computed by rational symmetric elimination: use a nonzero diagonal as a
1x1 pivot; if every remaining diagonal is zero but an offdiagonal is
nonzero, use the corresponding invertible 2x2 indefinite pivot; a zero
remaining matrix records nullity. Congruence preserves inertia, and each
Schur complement is rational. Work <=O(K a^3) rational operations plus
the stated coefficient bit growth. Degeneracies INSIDE either cluster
are legal. No individual eigenvalue separation or eigenvector condition
number is being used. Different sectors can use different accepted E;
the total retained rank is still <=Dbar because all E<=2R.

4. AN EXPLICIT POLYNOMIAL FOR THE CLUSTER PROJECTOR

Fix one accepted sector of dimension a, and its exact rank d below E.
Ranks d0/a are handled as zero/identity, requiring no basis acquisition.
Otherwise choose a rational K0>=Lambda+2R+1, X=(A-E)/K0, and
delta_gap=Delta/K0. Then ||X||<=1, |spec X|>=delta_gap. With

 b_l=binom(2l,l)/4^l<=1,
 P_q=(I-X sum_(l=0)^q b_l(I-X^2)^l)/2,

the binomial inverse-square-root series gives

 ||P_q-1_(A<E)|| <= exp[-(q+1)delta_gap^2]/(2delta_gap^2). (4.1)

Indeed the missing inverse-square-root series tail is at most
(1-delta_gap^2)^(q+1)/delta_gap^2, and multiplication by |X|<=1 and 1/2
gives this bound. Choose

 q>=delta_gap^-2[log(1/zeta)+2log(1/delta_gap)+1].

All coefficients are rational; dense Horner evaluation costs O(q a^3)
rational operations, with all precision/coefficient bit sizes charged.
This deliberately crude degree may be very large: it grows quadratically
in K0/Delta. There is no assertion of efficient Hamiltonian simulation.

5. CERTIFIED RANGE BASIS WITHOUT AN EIGENVECTOR ORACLE

This section retains a valid conservative exhaustive fallback. The
preferred refinement in greedy_basis.txt instead uses rational greedy
selection and volume exchanges with polynomial dependence on the physical
matrix dimension and polynomial Gram conditioning. Its finite-bit and
TP perturbation budgets feed into Sections 6--8 unchanged. No generic
rank-revealing algorithm novelty or polynomial many-body runtime is claimed.

Let P=1_(A<E), 0<d<a, Bbin=binom(a,d), and target isometry error
0<nu<=1. Take the projector error in (4.1) to be

                zeta<=nu/[1024 a 2^d Bbin^2].           (5.1)

This exposes even a very crude condition penalty. For a set I of d
standard columns, let C=P[:,I], G=C^*C; and use tilde C=P_q[:,I],
tilde G=tilde C^*tilde C. If U is any orthonormal basis of ran P,
then G=U_I U_I^*. Cauchy--Binet gives

                  sum_(|I|=d)det G_I=1.

Thus some I has det G_I>=1/Bbin. Since ||G-tilde G||<=3zeta and both
Gram norms are <=2, the determinant multilinear/telescoping bound gives
|det G-det tilde G|<=3d 2^(d-1)zeta<1/(8Bbin). Search the finitely many
Bbin column sets and accept one with det tilde G>=3/(4Bbin), testing exact
rational determinants. At least one exists. The accepted true Gram has
det G>=5/(8Bbin). Its eigenvalues are <=1, so

                  lambda_min(G)>=5/(8Bbin),
                  lambda_min(tilde G)>=1/(2Bbin).

Define EXACT isometries from C^d

                  U0=C G^-1/2, W0=tilde C tilde G^-1/2.

U0 ranges in the desired spectral subspace; W0 is acquired from the
explicit rational matrix. The inverse square-root integral identity gives
||G^-1/2-tilde G^-1/2||<=||G-tilde G||/(2 l^(3/2)), l=1/(2Bbin).
It follows that

                 ||W0-U0||<=8 Bbin^(3/2)zeta<nu/64.    (5.2)

To calculate tilde G^-1/2, scale its spectrum into [1/(4Bbin),1]
(e.g. divide by 2) and use the same binomial series. A public degree
O(Bbin log(Bbin/nu)) ensures any prescribed operator error. Exact
rational series evaluation followed by a near-identity Gram polar repair
produces a computed isometry to the desired nu tolerance. Square roots
and rotations are certified to finite bit accuracy. Their eigenvalue
interval is supplied by the Gram bounds above, not an unknown singular
value. Additional orthogonal columns can be obtained by Householder/Givens
column elimination; no inner energy diagonalization is needed.

The conservative basis setup work includes at least
O(Bbin a^3 + q a^3) rational operations and O(Bbin) inverse-square-root
series terms, with the coefficient/input bit precision and all table
sizes charged. This can be exponential. It is a fully specified finite
acquisition, not an efficient-spectrum theorem or an oracle convention.

6. HOW THE APPROXIMATE BASIS ERROR ENTERS THE TP PROTOCOL

The ideal main proof can choose U0 as its basis in the estimated spectral
range. Replace it by W0 in both encoder and decoder. The pass encoding
map has isometry adjoint U0^* (resp. W0^*); the failure projector is
I-U0U0^* (resp. I-W0W0^*), followed by a fixed failure density. A coherent
instrument dilation has norm difference <=3||W0-U0||. Tracing its
environment gives a channel trace error <=6||W0-U0|| on every input.
The inverse success isometries have channel error <=2||W0-U0||. Therefore
both sides, including all count-controlled branches, cost <=8n nu in
trace by a telescoping argument. This avoids putting n nu under a square
root in the gentle success bound; the whole ideal protocol was already
proved before applying implementation perturbations.

Choose nu<=epsilon/(100n). Compile basis rotations to that accuracy,
Schur transforms to total trace error <=epsilon/4, and multiplicity/order
sampling to <=epsilon/4. The ideal epsilon/4 error plus these charges
and <=8n nu is below epsilon. Complete TP extensions on failed/arbitrary
inputs are included. An acquired basis close to the known-size spectral
cluster, not eigenvector-by-eigenvector fidelity, is all that is needed.

7. INPUT MEASUREMENT, ROTATIONS, SCHUR, AND DECODER PREPARATION COSTS

Measuring total particle number means a COHERENT reversible sum into one
ancilla, followed by measurement of that ancilla. Measuring each n_i
separately would destroy within-sector coherences and is NOT this encoder.
Number measurement costs O(n N polylog N) elementary arithmetic gates
and work registers. Sorting sector groups costs a classical order record
of O(n log(r+1)) transient bits and up to O(n N polylog n) SWAP gates.
It is discarded after sorting; only counts persist. Input/output storage
is nN qubits and is explicitly outside the persistent archive theorem.

Let a_max=max_(m<=r)binom(L-1,m), omitting absent sectors. A public basis
rotation on its sector-occupation register can be synthesized densely in
O(a_max^2 poly(log a_max,log(n a_max/epsilon))) gates per copy. Reversible
occupation-subset rank/unrank maps cost polynomial in N,r and their bit
precision. Total basis gates therefore have a conservative upper bound
O(n a_max^2 poly(N,r,log(n a_max/epsilon))) plus all classical setup in
Sections 2--5. This cost is NOT n^o; a_max can scale as L^r.

Bacon--Chuang--Harrow supply Schur circuits of size
n poly(Dbar,log n,log(1/xi)); with Dbar=n^o at fixed public parameters,
this Schur STAGE has n^(1+o(1)) size. Its source-reported construction is
not re-proved in this programme. Exact sufficiency is proved in proof.txt.
Worst error is allocated over all group transforms and inverse transforms.

Given a measured Young diagram, append the uniform multiplicity basis
label by sampling an integer uniformly from [1,dim V_alpha]. Hook-length
integers and branching tableaux counts let one rank/unrank the label in
polynomial time in n,Dbar and integer bit sizes. Entropy/work registers
up to O(n log Dbar) bits/qubits are generated transiently and charged.
A rejection sampler uses expected <2 trials, or truncate after
O(log(n/epsilon)) trials for a certified error budget. The decoder does
NOT prepare the unknown within-sector Gibbs density: it reconstructs it
from the retained quantum representation register. Its other preparations
are fixed multiplicity identities, failure vacuum, and a uniform output
permutation. Uniform permutation sampling costs O(n log n) random bits
and polynomial n transient work; the order randomness is not stored.

For a fixed PUBLIC g the exact classical control DOES require preparing
a public conditional n-copy density after recording total number. Such
preparation can be done by a finite dense matrix algorithm with its full
size/precision cost; no efficient sampler is implied. Both controls are
equally informed about public J,tau and have the same supplied Gibbs
input. No unknown-state preparation shortcut is given to either.

8. CONTINUUM ACQUISITION AND FINITE HARDWARE TRANSPORT

The ideal continuum encoder in proof.txt uses finite-dimensional spectral
clusters in Fock space, but measuring those clusters is not an available
finite hardware primitive. Two options have sharply different interfaces.

(a) Supplied finite-L inputs: use the direct finite-L algorithm above.
It already has the uniform trace guarantee, with every matrix/gate charged.
No continuum basis acquisition is necessary for this operational theorem.

(b) Mathematical continuum experiment: its TP channels exist on Fock
space. To physically realize an approximation, select a finite proxy Lp
using the audited PUBLIC C_L certificate so 2n C_Lp<=xi. Apply the exact
public TP reverse embedding on continuum inputs, run the finite proxy
compressor, and apply the exact embedding to decode. As mathematical
channels the resulting full trace error is <=epsilon+xi and memory has
the same n^o bound, uniform in proxy size. Implementing the input/output
Fock embeddings themselves requires a specified physical representation;
without one, there is no claim of finite hardware access to arbitrary
continuum inputs. A finite-chain realization avoids that representation
gate completely. Its basis/circuit/setup costs grow with the chosen proxy
and are those in Sections 2--7, not a free Galerkin conversion.

The scalar rate yields log Lp=O(log(n/xi)) at any fixed exponent
alpha<1/384, after the public threshold. Consequently with r=O(sqrt(log n))
one can bound log a_max=O((log n)^(3/2)) for a polynomial proxy size.
This still makes the conservative dense setup/rotation EXPENSIVE; no
poly(n) implementation of the full interacting compressor is asserted.

9. EXACT BOUNDARIES AND FAILURES

The theorem is blind compression of identical unknown-parameter mixed
states in full trace. It is not reference-preserving/entanglement-fidelity
compression; Schur multiplicity discarding would fail that stronger task.
It is not a protocol for independent unknown g_i on different copies;
counts do not then determine a common coupling or identical sector states.
It does not claim robustness to model drift or uncontrolled measurement
noise; if p_* or the inverse slope changes, the conditioning constants
and error accounting must be re-certified. Uniform positive g_- is used
in the all-m tail and leakage bound. At g_-=0 these particular constants
diverge, so the claimed uniform construction does not include that limit.
The archive metric is trace, with no decoded physical energy guarantee.
Actual practical preprocessing memory/time, optimal rate, generic
infinite-dimensional priority, and external validation remain UNKNOWN.
