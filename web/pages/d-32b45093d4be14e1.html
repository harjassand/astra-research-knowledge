1. EXACT FINITE-ALPHA N2 BOUND
For N2, the j1 triplet has J^2=2 and the singlet has J^2=0. Put
a=exp(alpha-delta/2),b=exp(alpha). Before normalization the computational-basis
state has diagonals(a,(b+1)/2,(b+1)/2,a), and off-diagonals between01 and10
equal(b-1)/2. Its partial transpose has the00/11 block
 [[a,(b-1)/2],[(b-1)/2,a]].
For alpha>=0, its lower eigenvalue is a-(b-1)/2. Thus the whole finite-alpha
state is NPT if and only if
 1-exp(-alpha)>2exp(-delta/2),
or equivalently
 delta>2log[2/(1-exp(-alpha))], alpha>0.
Consequently every delta>2log2 admits an explicit finite-alpha entanglement
counter by choosing alpha>-log[1-2exp(-delta/2)]. The universal all-alpha
floor can never exceed2log2. This is a finite-alpha calculation, not reliance
only on closedness at infinity. Separability in the complementary region also
has a direct construction: let c=(b-1)/2 and E be the phase-average of equatorial
identical pure products. E has every computational diagonal1/4 and01/10
off-diagonal1/4. The unnormalized target is exactly
 4c E+(a-c)(|00><00|+|11><11|)+|01><01|+|10><10|.
All coefficients are nonnegative if a>=c. Thus the boundary is exact without
importing a general PPT-sufficiency theorem. No historical novelty is claimed
for this elementary XXZ calculation.

2. ROOT-SUPPLIED CONDITIONAL EVENTUAL-SYMMETRIC CONSEQUENCE
Fix a target delta>0 and suppose there exists k0 such that every symmetric
Gaussian-Dicke S_k(delta) is fully separable for every k>=k0. This is an
UNPROVED HYPOTHESIS for target values beyond the existing floor. With the
proved uniform floor delta_bar=5/4, the whole Gibbs family at this delta is
fully separable for every finite alpha>=0 and axial field whenever
 N>=ceil[delta*(k0-1)/delta_bar].
For subsets k>=k0, delta*k/N<=delta and Gaussian local filtering transfers
the hypothesized endpoint S_k(delta) certificate downward. For k<k0, the
admission ensures delta*k/N<=delta_bar and the already proved uniform theorem
applies. The positive heat/subset decomposition then gives the whole state.

This consequence separates a possible eventual symmetric threshold from the
few-qubit obstruction. It does NOT establish such a k0, a threshold2, or a
whole-Gibbs result for delta>5/4. The supplied future hypothesis remains a lead.
