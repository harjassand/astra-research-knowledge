INTERFACE
Let (M_d, tau) have normalized trace. Let Phi be a unital, trace-preserving, positive selfadjoint contraction on L2(tau), with an actual two-output broadcaster whose Heisenberg joint map J:M_d tensor M_d -> M_d is UCP and has both marginals Phi. For Hermitian A with ||A||_op<=1 define its Dirichlet energy

  e_Phi(A) = tau(A(I-Phi)A) >= 0.

The frame target is: for every finite weighted frame (H_x,p_x), find one EB map/POVM, or prove the corresponding max-prior joint-variance convex roof is at most a universal factor times sum_x p_x e_Phi(H_x). The assignment asks for a transfer from monogamy/SOS or matrix convexity that is uniform in the frame size.

STRONGEST INDEPENDENT CLAIM: PAIRWISE COMMUTATOR CONTROL
For any Hermitian contractions A,C,

  ||[A,C]||_(2,tau) <= (2+sqrt(2)) (sqrt(e_Phi(A)) + sqrt(e_Phi(C))).       (1)

For ||H_x||<=theta, rescaling gives

  ||[H_x,H_y]||_(2,tau)
    <= theta(2+sqrt(2))(sqrt(e_x)+sqrt(e_y)).                              (2)

Consequently, for any finite prior p,

  sum_(x,y) p_x p_y ||[H_x,H_y]||_(2,tau)^2
    <= 4 theta^2(2+sqrt(2))^2 sum_x p_x e_x.                               (3)

This is independent of dimension, labels, and frame size.

FULL DERIVATION
Since Phi is positive selfadjoint and unital, it is a positive L2 contraction, so 0<=Phi<=I as a Hilbert-space operator. For A=A*, spectral calculus gives Phi^2<=Phi and

  ||A-Phi(A)||_2^2
    = tau(A^2)-2 tau(A Phi(A))+tau(Phi(A)^2)
    <= tau(A^2)-tau(A Phi(A)) = e_Phi(A).                                  (4)

Kadison-Schwarz and trace preservation give the Schwarz defect

  d_A := tau(Phi(A^2)-Phi(A)^2)
       = tau(A^2)-tau(Phi(A)^2)
       <= 2 tau(A^2-Phi(A)A)=2 e_Phi(A),                                  (5)

because 1-lambda^2<=2(1-lambda) for every eigenvalue lambda in [0,1].

Take a Stinespring representation J(Z)=V* pi(Z)V and set P=VV*. The two commuting output algebras yield X=pi(A tensor I), Y=pi(I tensor C), with [X,Y]=0, ||X||,||Y||<=1 and V*XV=Phi(A), V*YV=Phi(C). Expanding the compression products gives

  [Phi(A),Phi(C)] = -V*X(I-P)YV + V*Y(I-P)XV.

The normalized Hilbert-Schmidt norm of (I-P)YV is sqrt(d_C), and similarly for X,A. Hence

  ||[Phi(A),Phi(C)]||_2 <= sqrt(d_A)+sqrt(d_C)
                        <= sqrt(2)(sqrt(e_A)+sqrt(e_C)).                  (6)

Finally,

  [A,C]-[Phi(A),Phi(C)] = [A-Phi(A),C]+[Phi(A),C-Phi(C)].

Use ||[R,S]||_2<=2||R||_2||S||_op, (4), and ||Phi(A)||_op<=1 to bound this difference by 2(sqrt(e_A)+sqrt(e_C)). Combining with (6) proves (1). Scaling A=H_x/theta proves (2); squaring with (u+v)^2<=2(u^2+v^2) and averaging independent x,y proves (3).

The derivation uses the actual joint UCP map, not a finite-dimensional Choi/SOS diagnostic. It sharpens the prior report's residual-based approximate-commutator estimate by charging each observable's Dirichlet energy directly. Its mechanism is Stinespring multiplicative-defect/SOS transferred from broadcasting monogamy.

WHY THIS DOES NOT YET PROVE THE ALL-FRAME CLAIM
Small average pairwise commutators are not themselves a common EB map. A valid next lemma would have to turn (3), uniformly over arbitrary finite weighted frames, into one abelian measurement (or another canonical POVM) whose average L2 loss tends to zero. The recent Bansil–Kachkovskiy theorem gives dimension-independent near-commuting approximants for a fixed pair and for fixed N-tuples, but its constants depend on N; its Corollary 1.4 has a rapidly N-dependent exponent. It therefore does not supply the required uniform-in-frame-size lifting. A tracial-ultraproduct abelian limit also does not on its own construct finite-dimensional EB maps. No unrestricted factor-two or qualitative all-frame variance theorem follows from this checkpoint.
