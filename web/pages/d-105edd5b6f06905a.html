1. A COMPLETE SIGN FRAME AND ITS EXACT PINCHING IDENTITY

Let P_1,...,P_r be mutually orthogonal minimal projections inside each matrix
block, summing to I; D=span(P_i) is a MASA in A. Write p_i=tau(P_i)>0.
The trace-preserving conditional expectation is

 E_D(X)=sum_i tau(P_i X) P_i/p_i.

Choose k=ceil(log_2 r), assign distinct v_i in F_2^k, and for t in F_2^k set

 U_t=sum_i (-1)^(v_i dot t) P_i,       N=2^k<2r (r>1).

For r=1 use N=1. These are Hermitian unitaries and character orthogonality
makes

 (1/N) sum_t U_t X U_t = E_D(X).

For any X in A, traciality then gives the EXACT identity

 (1/N) sum_t ||[X,U_t]||_2^2 = 2||X-E_D(X)||_2^2.           [1]

It follows either by expanding the two commutator products and averaging,
or by matrix entries: the average of (s_i-s_j)^2 is 2 off the diagonal and
0 on the diagonal. Central block weights cancel in precisely the same way.
This uses r growing anchors but does not multiply the approximation error
by r. No random sampling is needed to implement the finite frame.

2. PROOF OF THE APPROXIMATE PREIMAGE LIFT [A]

Assume the following inputs are SUPPLIED, not acquired by commutator decay:
Hermitian B_t in A with ||B_t|| <= kappa, satisfying

 a^2 = (1/N) sum_t ||T(B_t)-U_t||_2^2.

Let delta bound ||[T(A),T(C)]||_2 for every pair of Hermitian contractions
A,C. For an arbitrary Hermitian contraction A, put X=T(A); ||X||<=1.
By homogeneity of the entire-range assumption,

 ||[X,U_t]||_2
   <= ||[T(A),T(B_t)]||_2 + ||[X,U_t-T(B_t)]||_2
   <= kappa delta + 2||U_t-T(B_t)||_2.

Minkowski in the finite t-average, followed by [1], yields

 ||T(A)-E_D T(A)||_2 <= (kappa delta+2a)/sqrt(2).

Normalized trace Holder gives the same upper bound in L1. This is uniform
in A. E_D T is UCP, tau preserving, unital and EB, with the explicit form

 E_D T(A)=sum_i tau(T^*(P_i) A) P_i/p_i.

The effects T^*(P_i) are positive and sum to I; the preparations P_i/p_i
have tau=1. Thus no approximate CP repair is hidden in [A]. The physical
map is ordinary measure-and-prepare after the central-reference conversion.

If T is tau-selfadjoint and positive as an L2 operator, put F=E_D T E_D.
It is UCP, tau preserving, selfadjoint, Hilbert-positive and EB. Write
T-F=(I-E_D)T+E_D T(I-E_D). Duality of the Hermitian infinity-to-one norm and
selfadjointness give

 ||E_D T(I-E_D)||_(infinity->1)
   =||(I-E_D)T E_D||_(infinity->1)
   <=||(I-E_D)T||_(infinity->1),

because E_D is an operator-norm contraction. Therefore its error is at most
twice [A]. No Dirichlet domination is claimed by this norm estimate.

for that sequence tends to zero. This conditional statement retains every

3. PROOF OF THE BROADCASTER PROJECTION-AND-DEFICIT CONSTRUCTION [B]

Use the physical representation of A with central sigma. Choose a basis
compatible with its blocks and D, so sigma=sum_i p_i P_i and p_i=tau(P_i).
Let B_phys be an actual CPTP map to two receiver systems with both marginals
Phi_phys and Phi_phys(sigma)=sigma. Use the normalized reference purification

 |Omega_sigma> = sum_i sqrt(p_i)|i>_R |i>_input,
 Omega_RBC=(id_R tensor B_phys)(|Omega_sigma><Omega_sigma|).

Both receiver marginals and the R marginal are sigma. Define the orthogonal
matching projection

 P = sum_i P_i^R tensor I_B tensor P_i^C.

Its success probability is

 Tr(P Omega)=sum_i p_i Tr[P_i Phi_phys(P_i)]
             =sum_i tau(P_i Phi(P_i))=1-eta.

For each i define a positive operator on B by a partial matrix element,

 u_i = <i_R,i_C|Omega|i_R,i_C>/p_i.

Its trace is at most 1; put a_i=1-Tr(u_i)>=0. Tracing receiver C in the
successful projected state eliminates every R off-diagonal block:

 Tr_C(P Omega P)=sum_i p_i P_i^R tensor u_i.                [2]

Let

 delta_B=sigma-sum_i p_i u_i.

This is positive. To see it without subtracting unrelated positive
operators, put Q=I-P. Partial trace over R,C kills P Omega Q and Q Omega P
because P is diagonal in the traced R,C indices. Hence

 delta_B=Tr_RC(Q Omega Q)>=0,       Tr delta_B=eta.

If eta>0 set R_i=u_i+a_i delta_B/eta. Then R_i>=0, Tr R_i=1, and

 sum_i p_i R_i=sum_i p_i u_i + delta_B=sigma.

Thus the full-domain channel

 E_phys(rho)=sum_i Tr(P_i rho) R_i

is EB, CPTP and fixes sigma EXACTLY. All R_i belong to A, hence commute with
central sigma; in likelihood coordinates it is an EB_tau UCP map with
preparations sigma^(-1)R_i. If eta=0 positivity forces Omega=P Omega P,
a_i=0 and R_i=u_i, so the same formula gives E=Phi exactly.

Its normalized reference-Choi state is

 Omega'_RB=sum_i p_i P_i^R tensor R_i
           =Tr_C(P Omega P)+(sum_i p_i a_i P_i^R) tensor delta_B/eta.

The final added positive term has trace eta. For completeness, the
projection gentle bound used here needs no imported theorem. A pure vector
with projected success 1-x has

 || |psi><psi|-P|psi><psi|P ||_1 = sqrt(x(4-3x)) <=2sqrt(x),

by diagonalizing its 2 by 2 success/failure block. Apply this to a pure-state
decomposition of Omega and use concavity of sqrt to obtain

 ||Omega-P Omega P||_1 <=2sqrt(eta).

Partial trace contracts trace norm. Therefore

 ||Omega_RB-Omega'_RB||_1 <=2sqrt(eta)+eta.                 [3]

For Hermitian contractions A,B in A, centrality makes the reference-Choi
identity

 tau(B (Phi-E)(A))=Tr[(Omega_RB-Omega'_RB)(A^T tensor B)]

literal: Phi_phys(sigma A)=sigma Phi(A). Since ||A^T tensor B||<=1, [3]
plus trace duality proves [B] uniformly over the entire input unit ball.
No observation/measurement is chosen per promised state.

This proves a dimension-free restricted lifting statement outside the
