1. OPERATOR-KERNEL THEOREM
Let a(z)=(1-z^2)atanh(z)/z, a(0)=1, b(z)=sqrt(1-z^2), b(0)=1.
Use the bounded real-Taylor analytic functions on the unit disk from the
supplied Hardy construction. On H^2 let
  D=M_a+M_a^*-M_bM_b^*
   =(pi/2-1)M_b^*M_b+H_bar_b^*H_bar_b >=0,
where H_bar_b=P_- M_bar_b restricted to H^2. Positivity of this D is the
named supplied/internal dependency. It is a full operator Gram statement.

For any finite collection of bounded selfadjoint X_i on a common Hilbert
space K, put
  T_i=tanh(X_i/2), S_i=sinh(X_i/2), C_i=cosh(X_i/2).
Then ||T_i||<1. Define
  N_ij=sum_(n>=0) T_i^n
        [a(T_i)+a(T_j)-b(T_i)b(T_j)] T_j^n,
  K_ij=2 S_i N_ij S_j.
The series converges in operator norm. The block kernels [N_ij] and [K_ij]
are positive semidefinite, although the distinct X_i need not commute.
In particular this is a genuine noncommuting operator lift, not an entrywise
claim about a simultaneously diagonalizable family.

Proof of positivity. Set Q_T:K->H^2 tensor K by
  Q_T v=sum_(n>=0) e_n tensor T^n v.
It is bounded, with norm at most (1-||T||^2)^(-1/2). Expanding the real
Taylor coefficients of M_a, M_a^*, and M_bM_b^* gives exactly
  N_ij=Q_(T_i)^*(D tensor I_K)Q_(T_j).
For example, the first term is sum_n T_i^n a(T_i) T_j^n; the second is
sum_n T_i^n a(T_j) T_j^n; the third is sum_n T_i^n b(T_i)b(T_j) T_j^n.
All orderings agree with the displayed formula, without commuting T_i and
T_j. Hence [N_ij]>=0. The real selfadjoint block diagonal congruence
diag(sqrt(2)S_i) gives [K_ij]>=0, including negative/zero spectra of X_i.

The explicit Gram refinement from D is also valid:
  G_T=sqrt(pi/2-1)(M_b tensor I)Q_T,
  H_T=(H_bar_b tensor I)Q_T,
  N_ij=G_(T_i)^*G_(T_j)+H_(T_i)^*H_(T_j).
Thus all constants and the auxiliary Hardy-space cost are explicit. The
auxiliary space is infinite dimensional, but this is an existence/proof
factorization, not an efficiency or finite-implementation assertion.

2. EXACT NONCOMMUTATIVE DENOMINATOR
The geometric series solves the Sylvester equation
  N_ij-T_i N_ij T_j=a(T_i)+a(T_j)-b(T_i)b(T_j).
The linear operator Z->Z-T_iZT_j is invertible in operator norm because
||T_i||||T_j||<1; its inverse is the displayed convergent series. Consequently
the lifted hyperbolic kernel has the EXACT defining identity
  2[C_i K_ij C_j-S_i K_ij S_j]
   =X_i sinh(X_j)+sinh(X_i)X_j-4S_iS_j.                (K)
Indeed C_i b(T_i)=I and 2S_i C_i a(T_i)=X_i, by functional calculus in
each individual operator. Multiplying the Sylvester equation on the left
by 4S_iC_i and right by S_jC_j proves (K) in the stated order.
The scalar formula is recovered by X_i=x_i I. In general the denominator
is the invertible SUPEROPERATOR
  Z->C_i Z C_j-S_i Z S_j
    =C_i[Z-T_i Z T_j]C_j,
not multiplication by a naive inverse of cosh((X_i-X_j)/2). The latter
replacement is unjustified when X_i and X_j do not commute.
Equivalently, on a Hilbert-Schmidt space this denominator is
cosh((L_(X_i)-R_(X_j))/2), since left/right multiplications commute.
Identity (K) and block positivity do not themselves identify the entropy
operator M from the blind baseline with a kernel congruence.
