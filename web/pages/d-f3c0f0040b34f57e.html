## P1. The strongest recent state-based comparator is conditional here

Ma--Khemani--Sang, *Circuit-based characterization of finite-temperature
quantum phases and self-correcting quantum memory*, arXiv2509.15204v2:
first18September2025, v2 24December2025. Publisher primary metadata verifies
PRXQuantum7,033052,10September2026, DOI10.1103/sh48-wmy4, within the recent
window. The arXivv2 setup/Definitions and complete Theorem2--3 proof were
read; publisher metadata/abstract was read, not the full version-of-record
proof. These versions are not silently identified.

Theorem2 needs an acquired locally reversible channel C from a diameter-ell
code to rho, small approximation/reversibility errors, and range(C)<=ell/4.
Dynamics must have quasi-local TERMS L_X, each annihilating rho separately.
Theorem3 obtains the state circuit from a stable-clustering path. The full
proof pays a polynomial factor times (t+1) multiplying preparation,
reversibility and quasi-locality errors. Generic fixed-bath noncommuting
Davies dynamics and a ground-band spectral flow do not supply these inputs.
The v2 final choice of an exponent with +epsilon has a sign error; replacing
it by a smaller positive power recovers the intended stretched bound. This
does not establish the missing premises for the actual recursive memory.

Our sparse-support saturation/patch uses a different exact route: a reducing
safe projector, a FULL globally conserved Pauli logical algebra, and actual
frequency protection. Our heralded congruence has a flag and a paid reset;
it is not the deterministic locally reversible circuit in Theorem2. Thus
neither prior is promoted to an unacquired thermal-phase certificate.

## P3. Cleaning and thermal congruence are established mechanisms

Bravyi--Terhal, arXiv0810.1983v2, *A no-go theorem for a two-dimensional
self-correcting quantum memory based on stabilizer codes*: first11October
2008, v2 4November2008, NJP11,043029(2009). SectionII Lemma1 and its complete
proof were read from the primary PDF. It gives the exact dichotomy between
a logical supported in a region and cleaning all logicals off that region,
using incident stabilizer generators. Our GF(2) symplectic proof is the same
standard mechanism; no cleaning novelty is claimed.

Hastings, *Quantum Belief Propagation*, arXiv0706.4094v3: first28June2007,
v3 7February2008, PRB76,201102(2007). All five PDF pages were read. Equations
7--11 give a thermal derivative and ordered operator congruence; the paper
uses locality to approximate that operator numerically. It explicitly
leaves algorithmic error quantification for further work. Our exact
finite-support filter avoids a truncation error only because H_out commutes
with H_touch+V. It is a special exact congruence with paid normalization,
not a new generic QBP mechanism or a generic preparation complexity theorem.
