CONVENTIONS
J(F)=sum_ij |i><j| tensor F(|i><j|), unnormalized. CP iff J>=0.
PPT means CP and T_out F CP. EB means J separable. TP means Tr_out J=I.
S(F) is the matrix of F on the Hilbert-Schmidt orthonormal matrix-unit basis.
R denotes realignment, so R(J(F)) is S(F) up to transpose/permutation.

1. EB CONVEX ADMIXTURE CANNOT AMPLIFY THE INDEX

Theorem. Let Psi be a CP map, E an EB map, and 0<=lambda<=1. Then
 n_EB(lambda Psi+(1-lambda)E) <= n_EB(Psi).
This includes quantum channels and does not require PPT.

Proof. Expand the n-fold composition into its 2^n ordered words. Every word
containing E is EB: EB is a two-sided ideal under composition with CP maps,
and a convex cone. The only other word is lambda^n Psi^n. If Psi^n is EB,
every word is EB, hence their sum is EB. This uses a noncommutative word
expansion, not an unjustified binomial formula. The statement is tautological
when n_EB(Psi)=infinity. End proof.

Consequence. A tiny admixture of a fixed nonEB PPT channel into complete
dephasing, any replacer, or any other EB channel cannot create an arbitrarily
long EB lifetime. A proposed family that varies the base Psi with lambda must
already establish the required large index of those base maps. This is an
exact obstruction, not a conclusion drawn from a spectral gap or finite scan.

2. EXACT RATIONAL TINY PPT ENTANGLEMENT NEAR DEPHASING, INDEX EXACTLY TWO

This construction is a failed long-lifetime route. It proves that entanglement
can be arbitrarily small at an EB boundary while disappearing at the square.

Use indices 0,1,2 in C^3, with standard vectors e_i. The real TILES product
vectors are the normalized versions of
 e0 tensor (e0-e1), (e0-e1) tensor e2,
 e2 tensor (e1-e2), (e1-e2) tensor e0,
 (e0+e1+e2) tensor (e0+e1+e2).
Let P be the sum of their five orthogonal rank-one projectors. All entries of
P are rational. J=I_9-P is a rank-four positive projector and J^Gamma=J.
Define F0:M3->M3 by J(F0)=J. Its effect matrix E=F0^*(I) is
 [ 7/6, 1/6, -1/3;
   1/6, 5/3, 1/6;
  -1/3, 1/6, 7/6 ].
It is positive with trace four. Therefore H=I_3-E/4 is positive.

On C^7 use mutually orthogonal blocks A=span(e0,e1,e2),
B=span(e3,e4,e5), and flag f=e6. Write C=B+span(f).
Define the TP PPT map F:M3->B(C) by
 F(X) = (1/4) embed_B F0(X) + Tr(H X)|f><f|.
Its Choi matrix is a direct orthogonal-output sum of J/4 and
H^T tensor |f><f|, so CP and coCP are exact. Trace preservation follows from
E/4+H=I. Neither matrix inverse nor postselection is used to implement F.

Let D_A and D_C be full diagonal dephasing on the respective blocks, killing
all other input matrix units. For 0<epsilon<=1 define
 Phi_epsilon(X)=(1-epsilon)D_A(X)+epsilon F(X_AA)+D_C(X).
The three Choi contributions are positive and PPT, and the map is TP.
It tends linearly to full seven-dimensional dephasing as epsilon tends to zero.

An explicit rational entanglement witness is
 W=P-mu I_9, mu=1/320000,
lifted by zero to the A_input tensor B_output corner of C^7 tensor C^7.
For any full product vector, that local compression is again a product vector,
so block positivity of W implies block positivity of the lifted witness.
Its exact expectation is
 Tr(W_lift J(Phi_epsilon)) = -epsilon/320000 <0.
Consequently Phi_epsilon is nonEB for every positive epsilon. For the normalized
Choi state divide this expectation by seven.

PROOF OF BLOCK POSITIVITY, WITHOUT SAMPLED PRODUCT VECTORS

For unit complex vectors a,b, the expectation f=<a,b|P|a,b> is
 (|a0|^2|b0-b1|^2 + |a0-a1|^2|b2|^2
  +|a2|^2|b1-b2|^2 + |a1-a2|^2|b0|^2)/2
  + |a0+a1+a2|^2 |b0+b1+b2|^2/9.
Assume f<mu and put s=1/20, so mu=s^4/2. For each of the first four products,
at least one of its two linear factors has modulus <s. Choose one such factor
for each tile. This gives sixteen choices of constraints on a and b.

The a linear forms are a0, a0-a1, a2, a1-a2. The b forms are
b0-b1, b2, b1-b2, b0. Any three forms in either list are independent, with an
inverse matrix having entries in {0,1,-1}. If three constraints fall on one
factor, all its coordinates have modulus <=3s, so its norm is <=3 sqrt(3)s<1,
a contradiction. The only possible choices therefore have two constraints on
each factor (six cases).

For every pair of forms, the positive 2x2 Gram matrix has det/trace>=1/4.
Its least singular value is therefore >=1/2. The distance r from a to the
one-dimensional common kernel is at most 2 sqrt(2)s<3s; the same holds for b.
In each of the six complementary pairs of kernels, the sum of coordinates of
a kernel vector has modulus at least its norm. For a, the possible kernel
directions are e2,e1,(0,1,1),(1,1,0),(1,1,1),e0; the complementary b kernels
have the same property. Thus
 |sum a_i| >= (1-3s)-sqrt(3)*3s >=1-9s=11/20,
and likewise for b. The stopper contribution is at least
(11/20)^4/9=14641/1440000>mu, a contradiction. Hence f>=mu for all complex
unit product vectors. Homogeneity handles nonunit vectors. This proves W is
block positive and therefore nonnegative on the entire separable cone.
The matrices and all sixteen rank cases are checked exactly in check_exact.py.

WHY THE SQUARE IS EB

There is no long-lived quantum component hidden in this channel. Direct
composition, for arbitrary X, gives
 Phi_epsilon^2(X) = (1-epsilon)^2 D_A(X)
  +epsilon(1-epsilon) F(D_A(X)_AA)
  +epsilon D_C(F(X_AA)) +D_C(X).
Every summand is EB. The first and fourth are dephasing. The second is F after
an EB map, and the third is an EB map after F. All coefficients are nonnegative
for 0<=epsilon<=1. Therefore n_EB(Phi_epsilon)=2 for epsilon>0.

This is an explicit positive separable decomposition, not merely a witness
failing to detect the square. For example, its Choi sum consists of
 (1-epsilon)^2 sum_iA |i><i| tensor |i><i|,
 epsilon(1-epsilon) sum_iA |i><i| tensor F(|i><i|),
 epsilon sum_kC F^*(|k><k|)^T tensor |k><k|,
 and sum_kC |k><k| tensor |k><k|.
Each tensor factor is positive. The third sum's effects are embedded on A.
The same lifted witness has expectation
 879991 epsilon(2-epsilon)/2880000 >=0 on the square.
Exact matrix-unit composition/TP checks are in exact_results.json.

3. UNFILTERED REALIGNMENT IS BLIND TO EVERY PPT CHANNEL POWER >=2

Theorem. For PPT TP maps Phi,Psi:M_d->M_d,
 ||R(J(Psi Phi)/d)||_1 <=1.
Consequently the usual CCNR/realignment criterion cannot witness any PPT
channel power Phi^n for n>=2.

Proof. The reduction map Red(X)=Tr(X)I-X is coCP: Red T has Choi I-F_swap>=0.
Since J(Phi) is PPT, applying id tensor Red gives
 I tensor I-J(Phi)>=0 (the input marginal is I by TP).
Thus 0<=J(Phi)<=I and Tr J(Phi)=d, so Tr J(Phi)^2<=d.
Reshuffling preserves Frobenius norm, hence ||S(Phi)||_2<=sqrt(d), and the
same holds for Psi. Schatten Holder gives
 ||S(Psi)S(Phi)||_1 <=||S(Psi)||_2||S(Phi)||_2<=d.
Divide by d and use the realignment/superoperator identity. For n>=2 the two
factors Phi^(n-1),Phi are PPT TP. End proof.

This rules out a tempting spectral witness search. It does NOT prove EB. A
primary literature search plus an unrelated web record surfaced prior versions
of this exclusion; no originality is claimed here.

4. EXACT AND APPROXIMATE FIXED-PROJECTION OBSTRUCTIONS

Theorem. Let Phi be unital CP and coCP. If P is a projection and Phi(P)=P,
then [P,Phi(X)]=0 for every X. In particular, if Phi fixes every rank-one
basis projection, its range is diagonal, and Phi is EB.

Quantitative version. If ||Phi(P)-P||<=delta<=1/2, then
 ||[P,Phi(X)]|| <=2(sqrt(delta)+delta)||X||.
Operator norms are used. Unitality is an actual assumption.

Proof. Put A=Phi(P). For a unital CP map, Stinespring/Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 ||Phi(PX)-A Phi(X)|| <=sqrt(||A-A^2||)||X||.
The same inequality for the unital CP map T Phi, followed by output transpose,
gives
 ||Phi(PX)-Phi(X) A|| <=sqrt(||A-A^2||)||X||.
Transpose reverses multiplication, which is exactly why the second expression
has the opposite order. Subtract to bound the commutator with A by twice the
right side. A is a positive contraction. Since its eigenvalues are within delta
of the eigenvalues 0 and 1 of P, ||A-A^2||<=delta. Replacing A by P adds at most
2delta||X||. At delta=0 the claim follows exactly. If every basis projection
is fixed, all offdiagonal output entries vanish; any CP map with diagonal range
is a positive-effect measure-and-prepare map, hence EB. End proof.

This makes an exactly protected classical sector unusable as a protected
quantum coherence sector. The approximate statement does not prove exact EB
after a uniform number of steps: small noncommutation is compatible with tiny
PPT entanglement, as the explicit construction above illustrates.

A related entrywise tangent bound requires only PPT TP. Set
a_i(alpha)=<alpha|Phi(|i><i|)|alpha>. Positivity of the 2x2 minors of J and
J^Gamma gives, for all i,j,alpha,beta,
 |<alpha|Phi(|i><j|)|beta>|^2
 <=min(a_i(alpha)a_j(beta),a_i(beta)a_j(alpha)).
If 1-a_i(i)<=epsilon for all i, then for i!=j and alpha!=beta the displayed
coherence-transfer entry has modulus <=epsilon. For alpha=beta the universal
bound is sqrt(epsilon), not epsilon. Discarding these diagonal measurement
terms would make a claimed tangent no-go false. A slow classical mode can
also regenerate small output coherences when blocks are coupled.

6. CURRENT DECISIVE GATE AND WHAT FAILED

Endpoint A: for every N there exist d and a PPT TP Phi:M_d->M_d with
Phi^N nonEB. Endpoint B: every PPT TP map has an EB cube. Neither is proved.

A real tangent opportunity must exhibit a non-convex PPT deformation Phi(t)
and a block-positive witness W(t) with a rigorously negative leading term of
Tr(W(t) J(Phi(t)^3)); all CP, coCP, and TP obligations must hold on an interval.
The central missing lemma is precisely this negative third-iterate witness,
or a positive decomposition proving it impossible. Ordinary mixing with an
EB channel, an approximately protected classical population, realignment
norm, large Kraus/Schmidt complexity, or a slowly closing spectral gap cannot
substitute for that lemma.

The exact TILES boundary construction failed at the square for a structural
reason, and it is retained. A spectral CCNR scan was rejected before running
because its criterion is provably blind. Diagonal-unitary-covariant channel
ansatzes were rejected because a published primary result already proves their
PPT squares EB. A fixed-identity perturbation was rejected because PPT channels
cannot approach the identity channel: the normalized Choi fidelity with the
maximally entangled state is <=1/d by J<=I. The trace distance from the identity
Choi state is therefore at least 2(1-1/d).
