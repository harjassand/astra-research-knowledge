QUANTUM CAPABILITY — EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO UNFILTERED MOMENT TRANSFER
2026-10-08

Question

Can the scalar-coefficient sphere penalty in the current finite MOE
construction be removed by transferring a free-group purity bound directly
to its finite high-girth permutation representation? Can a single common
operator-valued/SOS estimate replace that penalty?

Result: exact negative answer to the first route; the second remains open.
The counterexample is only to unfiltered, normalized-trace-only transfer. It
does not refute the source's filtered theorem, and it gives no lower bound
against every possible filter.

1. The exact fixed-vector obstruction

Use the p=1 branch family in Zhen–Chen–Wang, arXiv:2610.06533v1, Corollary
2.2 and Sections 4.1, 4.2, 5.3, and 6.6. There are D=256 real permutation
branches W_s on a finite vertex set of size n=q_m^2. Every permutation fixes
the normalized constant vector v=n^(-1/2)1. The source's finite-moment
condition can still hold exactly:

  tau_n(W_w)=1_{w=e}

for every nonidentity reduced word through its required length, because the
normalized trace counts fixed points and the high-girth action has no such
fixed points. This trace identity does not change W_w v=v.

With F=I, the source channel formula gives

  [Phi_I(|v><v|)]_{st}
      = D^(-1) <v,W_t^* W_s v> = 1/D.

Thus Phi_I(|v><v|)=|+_D><+_D| is pure and its purity is exactly 1. In
contrast, the free-model estimate in source Section 4.1 has J^2=50 for
gamma=2, k=64, D=256, and its corresponding purity scale is

  (D+J^2)/D^2 = 306/65536.

The finite quotient's exact short normalized traces therefore do not imply
the free-model global-purity or operator-norm estimate. This is not an
approximate-trace issue: the short traces match exactly while the exceptional
fixed line has normalized trace weight only 1/n.

There is an equivalent coefficient witness. Let d=D(D-1)=65,280 and let

  A^o=(11^T-I_D)/sqrt(D(D-1)).

Then A^o is real symmetric, has zero diagonal, and has Hilbert–Schmidt norm
one. For the source's T_A=sum_(s neq t) A_st W_s^*W_t,

  T_(A^o) v = sqrt(D(D-1)) v.

Hence ||T_(A^o)|| >= sqrt(65,280) ~= 255.499. The free Haagerup bound is
||T_A^f|| <= sqrt(50)||A||_HS ~= 7.071. The unfiltered finite operator is
larger by at least a factor sqrt(65,280/50) ~= 36.13. The source target
compression constant C=sqrt(103/2) ~= 7.176 is also missed by a factor
about 35.60.

2. What a source-style filter must do on this line

The p=1 spherical penalties in source Section 6.6 preserve v. Indeed
W_s^*W_t v=v and W_s v=v, so T_A v=(sum_(s neq t)A_st)v and
L_z^*L_z v=|sum_s z_s|^2 v; every polynomial and coefficient-spherical
average in R_2 and R_infinity leaves the line Cv invariant. Thus the source
filter F=(I+R)^(-1/2) has Fv=fv for some 0<f<=1.

For this input, its filtered channel output is exactly

  rho_v = [f^2 11^T + (1-f^2) I_D]/D,
  Tr(rho_v^2) = 1/D + (D-1)f^4/D.

The global-purity target Tr(rho^2)<=1/D+C^2/D^2 therefore requires

  f^4 <= C^2/[D(D-1)],
  f^2 <= C/sqrt(D(D-1)) ~= 0.0281,

equivalently the R-eigenvalue on v must be at least
sqrt(D(D-1))/C - 1 ~= 34.60. The same necessity follows from
||F T_(A^o) F||<=C.

This requirement is not itself an expensive obstruction: attenuating one
dimension loses at most 1/n normalized trace mass, and n=2^37,608,898 in
the displayed source example. It only shows that the filter cannot be
omitted or leave the common line untouched. A useful new filter must handle
all exceptional input directions while keeping its normalized trace cost
small.

3. A second failed shortcut: full doubled-space norm

For F=I, the purity expression is a product-vector expectation of

  G=sum_(s,t) (W_s^*W_t) tensor overline(W_s^*W_t).

The maximally entangled vector vec(I)/sqrt(n) is fixed by every summand, so
G has eigenvalue D^2=65,536. Consequently a full operator-norm estimate
G <= (D+C^2)I cannot hold. The desired purity claim only tests product
vectors psi tensor overline(psi), not arbitrary vectors in the doubled
space; a successful SOS must encode that restriction (or an equivalent
separability condition). A full-space spectral estimate is over-strong.

4. Precise status and hard gate

INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE: the all-ones input exactly falsifies unfiltered
global purity and any attempt to infer its operator-norm bound solely from
the finite normalized trace moments. The calculations are elementary and
the exact arithmetic replay is in evidence/structural_purity_obstruction.py.

NOT PROVED: no obstruction is shown against the actual Chebyshev filter,
against a different representation with no common invariant vector, or
against a product-vector/SOS certificate. The source's existing filter
passes the fixed-line test by suppressing it, and its proof remains
SOURCE-REPORTED pending full independent review.

The concrete next gate for a net-removal result is one finite certificate,
uniform for every ||A||_HS=1 and every input vector, that implies

  +/- T_A <= C(I+R),
  (D+1) tau_n(I-F^2) <= delta,
  F=(I+R)^(-1/2),

with a symbolic operator-valued/nonbacktracking construction whose degree
and dimension depend polynomially (or substantially less than 2^(Theta(D^2)))
on D and the target margin. It must explicitly attenuate the common
permutation-invariant line, and it cannot be derived from normalized short
traces alone. Numerical candidates and full doubled-space norm bounds do not
meet this gate.

Costs and baseline: this note adds a constant-size exact check and no matrix
construction. The witness dimension remains the source's n=q_m^2; no channel
or filter was materialized. The equally informed baseline is the source's
Chebyshev spherical filter, which already proves the stated theorem but pays
for the coefficient sphere of real dimension D(D-1). This note provides no
improved channel size or entropy gap.
