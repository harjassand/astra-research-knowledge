A. EXACT AFFINE LEMMA

For every integer m>=1, real a<b, and arbitrary E:[a,b]^m -> (0,infinity),
there exists a fixed finite nonempty family Lambda. Each label has a slope
r_lambda in [a,b]^m independent of h and an offset b_lambda(h), defined for
all sufficiently small h>0, satisfying b_lambda(h)=o(h^a). Put
  X_h(u)=(h^{u_1},...,h^{u_m}),
  F_h(x)=min_lambda(r_lambda dot x+b_lambda(h)).
EVERY minimizing label is active, including ties. If h_n -> 0+, p_n in R^m
converges to p in [a,b]^m, and a FIXED label lambda is active at X_{h_n}(p_n)
for every n, then
  ||p-r_lambda||_infinity < E(r_lambda).
The approximating p_n may approach any face from OUTSIDE the cube. There is
no continuity, measurability, or positive infimum assumption on E. The source
does not require monotone h_n; a monotone subsequence version also follows.

A1. COMPLETE PROOF BY DIMENSION INDUCTION

Let mu(p)=min_i p_i. A fixed strict limiting exponent gap is enough for every
power comparison used here: p_i>q gives h_n^{p_{n,i}}=o(h_n^q), whereas
p_i<q gives h_n^{p_{n,i}}/h_n^q -> infinity. We never infer either comparison
when p_i=q. This protects logarithmic-scale boundary sequences.

For each fixed t in [a,b] first construct a finite local family Lambda_t,
containing the label (t*1,0), all of whose slopes have minimum coordinate t.
The local goal is the lemma restricted to limits p with mu(p)=t.
If m=1 or t=b, the singleton (t*1,0) suffices for that local goal.

Otherwise m>1 and t<b. A type k label has k coordinates above t and all
others exactly t. Begin with the type 0 label. For k=1,...,m-1, after the
finitely many type k-1 labels have been chosen, choose beta_k so that
  t<beta_k<b;
  beta_k<beta_{k-1} if k>1;
  beta_k-t<E(r) for EVERY type k-1 slope r already selected.
This choice needs only finitely many positive tolerance values, so exists.
For each k-element coordinate set U, use the dimension-k induction hypothesis
on [beta_k,b]^U with gauge E(r(s)), where r(s)_U=s and r(s)_{U^c}=t.
For every resulting sublabel (s,d(h)) add the type k label
  r=r(s), c(h)=-h^{beta_k}+d(h), d(h)=o(h^{beta_k}).
All local offsets are o(h^t). The lower-dimensional cubes have genuine
positive width, and k<m, so this is a well-founded dimension induction.

Suppose a type k label remains active locally along p_n -> p in [a,b]^m,
mu(p)=t. Write U for its raised coordinates. For k>0 its comparison to the
all-t label gives
  sum_{i in U}(r_i-t)h^{p_{n,i}} <= (1+o(1))h^{beta_k}.
Every coefficient is >=beta_k-t>0. Hence p_i>=beta_k for i in U: a smaller
limiting exponent would give an unbounded positive left-hand ratio.
Within labels raised on the SAME U, the off-U coordinates and -h^{beta_k}
cancel. Thus the sublabel is active in its lower-dimensional family. Its
limiting vector lies in [beta_k,b]^U, so induction gives
  max_{i in U}|p_i-r_i|<E(r).
This use of induction is valid even if the p_{n,U} approach a face from
outside, including p_i=beta_k or p_i=b.

For k<m-1, every coordinate j not in U satisfies p_j<=beta_{k+1}. If instead
p_j>beta_{k+1}, compare to any type k+1 label raised on U union {j}. All
limiting exponents on that set exceed beta_{k+1}: this is automatic for j,
and for i in U it follows from p_i>=beta_k>beta_{k+1}. Relative to the all-t
label, this competitor has value
  -h^{beta_{k+1}}+o(h^{beta_{k+1}}).
The supposedly active label has value 0 if k=0, and value at least
  -h^{beta_k}+o(h^{beta_k})
otherwise, because its raised slope contributions are nonnegative. Since
h^{beta_k}=o(h^{beta_{k+1}}), the competitor is strictly smaller, a
contradiction. Therefore the off-U error is at most beta_{k+1}-t<E(r),
where the last inequality was imposed for all type k slopes. At k=m-1 the
sole off-U coordinate equals t, because mu(p)=t and all on-U coordinates
are >t; its error is zero. Thus the local goal is proved for every type.

Extend the local guarantee from mu(p)=t to nearby minima. For each selected
local label lambda define A_lambda as the set of p in [a,b]^m realizable as
limits of ACTUAL active sequences, permitting p_n in R^m. This set is closed:
if z_j in A_lambda tends to z, select from a sequence witnessing z_j an active
pair with h_j<1/j and ||p_j-z_j||<1/j. Then h_j ->0 and p_j ->z witnesses z.
One can require h_j<h_{j-1}/2 if monotonicity is wanted. This step uses no
continuity of offsets in h. Consequently
  B_t=union_{lambda in Lambda_t}
      [A_lambda intersection {p:||p-r_lambda||>=E(r_lambda)}]
is compact. The finite union and fixed numerical E(r_lambda) are crucial;
continuity of the full gauge E is irrelevant. The local proof makes B_t
disjoint from mu^{-1}(t). By compactness and continuity of mu, choose rho_t>0
such that B_t has no point with mu(p) in I_t=(t-rho_t,t+rho_t).
Shrink rho_t further so every local offset c(h)=o(h^{t+rho_t}); for a
nonsingleton family use t+rho_t<min_k beta_k, and for a singleton use c=0.

Choose finitely many of the open I_t covering [a,b]. Remove contained
intervals and duplicates. Sort their centers as t_1<...<t_N. The left and
right endpoints L_j,R_j are then both strictly increasing. Consecutive
intervals overlap: otherwise a point between their endpoints in
(t_j,t_{j+1}) would be uncovered. Select
  q_j in (max(t_j,L_{j+1}), min(t_{j+1},R_j)), j=1,...,N-1,
and q_0=a,q_N=b. This interval is nonempty: each of its two possible lower
endpoints is smaller than each possible upper endpoint. Thus
  t_j<q_j<t_{j+1}, and [q_{j-1},q_j] subset I_{t_j}.
Replace each local offset c from Lambda_{t_j} by
  c_tilde(h)=c(h)-sum_{ell<j}h^{q_ell}.
Each c_tilde=o(h^a), since t_j+rho_j>a and each q_ell>t_ell>=a.
Labels from a family receive a common shift, preserving within-family
activity. They need not form a Cartesian product or a grid.

Suppose a label from family j is globally active along a sequence as in the
lemma. If j<N and mu(p)>q_j, compare to the shifted all-t_{j+1} label. The
active label minus its competitor equals
  (r-t_{j+1}*1) dot X_h(p_n)+c(h)+h^{q_j}
  =h^{q_j}+o(h^{q_j})>0,
because all limiting exponents are >q_j and c=o(h^{t_j+rho_j})=o(h^{q_j}).
This contradicts activity. If j>1 and mu(p)<q_{j-1}, comparison to the
shifted all-t_{j-1} label gives
  (r-t_{j-1}*1) dot X_h(p_n)+c(h)-h^{q_{j-1}}.
All scalar-product coefficients are >=t_j-t_{j-1}>0, and at least one
limiting exponent is <q_{j-1}. Dividing by h^{q_{j-1}} tends to +infinity;
c=o(h^{q_{j-1}}). This again contradicts activity. Outer bounds follow from
mu(p) in [a,b]. Therefore mu(p) in [q_{j-1},q_j] subset I_{t_j}, including
both cut equalities. The label is active within its local family, whose
nearby-minimum guarantee yields the desired STRICT tolerance. Induction is
complete. All ties are included throughout.

B. COMPLETE KINETIC THEOREM

Let d>=1, let finitely many nonzero vectors nu_e in R^d be fixed, and let
S=span{nu_e}. Let F be ANY family of positive rates w_e^f(t,x) on
[0,infinity) times R^d_{>0}. Assume standard local Caratheodory well-posedness
and compact continuation: measurability in time, continuity in x for a.e.
time, and on each positive compact set and finite time interval an integrable
Lipschitz bound in x. Uniform local Lipschitz bounds across F are sufficient
but are not needed in the estimate below. Assume there exist continuous
tau_e:[-1,1]^d ->R and epsilon(h)->0 as h->0+ such that for all sufficiently
small h, all f in F, all e, all t>=0, and all p in [-1,1]^d,
  |log w_e^f(t,X_h(p))/log h - tau_e(p)|<=epsilon(h).              (UV)
An a.e.-time formulation gives the same trajectory result when the drift
estimate holds on a common full-measure set for each admissible path.

For EVERY r in [-1,1]^d put E_r={e:r dot nu_e !=0}. Whenever E_r is nonempty,
require that EVERY minimizer of tau_e(r) over E_r has r dot nu_e>0. (TE)
This includes all boundary directions, all reaction-normal faces, and ties.
No convexity, linearity, or rationality assumption on tau is imposed.

Conclusion: for every c>0 and P=(c+S) intersection R^d_{>0}, there is a
nonempty compact convex K_P subset P, depending on P and the common family
data but not on f or x0, such that ALL positive solutions in P are global,
K_P is forward invariant under EVERY admitted path, and for each x0 in P a
finite T(x0) can be selected UNIFORMLY over f with x(t) in K_P for all
t>=T(x0). If S={0}, take K_P={c}.

B1. THE EXACT SLOPE GAUGE
For each r not in S^perp choose e_+(r) minimizing tau over E_r. TE gives
a(r)=r dot nu_{e_+}>0. Every e with r dot nu_e<0 satisfies
tau_e(r)>tau_{e_+}(r). By continuity and finiteness choose a radius R(r)>0
and gap g(r)>0 such that
  tau_e(p)-tau_{e_+}(p)>g(r)
for all such negative edges and all p in the cube with ||p-r||<R(r).
If there are no negative edges the gap restriction is vacuous; choose any
positive g and R. For r in S^perp no flux-gap restriction is needed.
In every case choose a positive gauge E(r)<=1/2, and enforce
  E(r)<|r_i|/2 for every nonzero coordinate r_i,
  E(r)<R(r) when r not in S^perp.
The gauge can approach zero discontinuously near coordinate or reaction
hyperplanes. Part A applies without a positive lower bound for E.

Fix its finite affine family on [-1,1]^d once and for all. By A2, all active
labels on B_h=[h,h^{-1}]^d have ||p-r||<E(r) for sufficiently small h.
Among the finitely many nonneutral selected slopes take positive minima
  a_0=min r dot nu_{e_+(r)}, g_0=min g(r),
and let B=max_r sum_e |r dot nu_e|. If the set of nonneutral slopes were
empty, the drift claim outside the plateau on a class is vacuous; the
subsequent bounded plateau also implies S={0}, already separated above.

B2. UNIFORM DRIFT, INCLUDING MEASURABLE TIME PATHS
For an active nonneutral r at x=X_h(p), UV and the tolerance give, for a
negative edge e,
  w_e^f(t,x)/w_{e_+}^f(t,x)
    =h^{tau_e(p)-tau_{e_+}(p)+theta_e-theta_{e_+}}
    <=h^{g_0/2},
after choosing h so small that 2*epsilon(h)<g_0/2. This estimate is uniform
over all t, f, p, and selected slopes. Discarding other positive projections,
  r dot sum_e nu_e w_e >= (a_0-B h^{g_0/2}) w_{e_+}
                             >=(a_0/2)w_{e_+}.
Since tau is bounded on its compact cube, choose a finite M>=1 with
tau_e(p)+epsilon(h)<=M for all e,p at every sufficiently small h. Then
w_{e_+}>=h^M, because 0<h<1 reverses the order of exponents. Thus
  r dot xdot >=delta_h=(a_0/2)h^M>0 for each active r not in S^perp,
  r dot xdot =0                         for r in S^perp.          (D)
This uses uniform all-time rate orders, not continuity or differentiability
in time. Every stage below may use the SAME finite family of slopes.

UV also implies a useful regularity fact: on every positive compact D all
rates are bounded above and away from zero uniformly over time and F. Choose
one sufficiently small h with D subset B_h, and use bounded tau and epsilon
at that fixed scale. In particular the vector field is bounded on D. The
given local Caratheodory hypotheses then permit compact continuation.
Unbounded temporal amplitude at a FIXED positive state is NOT admitted by
UV; claiming it is would overstate the theorem.

B3. COMPACT GLOBAL PLATEAU, WITHOUT ASSUMING ITS EXISTENCE
Fix c in P. For sufficiently small h, p_c=log c/log h is in the cube and
tends to 0. The finitely many nonzero selected slopes have a positive
minimum of |r_i|/2 over their nonzero coordinates. If p_c is smaller than
that minimum in norm, each nonzero slope violates its coordinate tolerance
in some coordinate and hence cannot be active. Therefore there is an
active zero-slope label. Let
  M_h=F_h(c), K_h={x in R^d:F_h(x)=M_h}.
The active zero label makes F_h(x)<=M_h for EVERY x in R^d. More precisely,
M_h is the minimum offset among zero labels, which is independent of c.
Consequently
  K_h=intersection_lambda {x:r_lambda dot x+b_lambda(h)>=M_h}
is a nonempty closed convex polyhedron containing c.

Every point of K_h intersection B_h has the zero label active. By A2 its
exponent p satisfies ||p||<E(0)<=1/2. Thus it lies in the STRICT interior of
B_{sqrt(h)}. If K_h had any point outside B_h, its segment to c would meet
the boundary of B_h at a point of K_h, which has just been ruled out. Hence
  K_h subset int B_{sqrt(h)}.
This proves K_h is compact and positive, including the possibility of
nonpositive points in the ambient R^d before this argument. For x in P\K_h,
no selected slope in S^perp can be active: its affine value is constant on
c+S and is at least F_h(c)=M_h, whereas F_h(x)<M_h.
For S!=0 this compactness also forces the selected nonneutral slope set to
be nonempty. Otherwise F_h and K_h would be invariant under translations
by the nonzero space S, contradicting the nonempty compact K_h.

B4. TRAJECTORY CHAIN RULE AND INVARIANCE
For fixed h, F_h(x(t)) is absolutely continuous along any absolutely
continuous trajectory. At almost every t, its derivative is the minimum
of r_lambda dot xdot over all active labels. One way to justify ties is to
differentiate the finitely many affine functions and their minimum at a
common differentiability time: for an active label, its difference from the
minimum is nonnegative and zero, so its derivative is zero. Thus all active
derivatives actually agree at those a.e. times.
Inside B_h, (D) gives F_h'>=0. On the class outside K_h, B3 excludes neutral
active slopes, so F_h'>=delta_h. A solution starting in K_h cannot leave it:
until a hypothetical first exit from B_h, F_h is nondecreasing but at most
M_h, hence remains M_h and x remains in K_h. Since K_h is strictly interior
to B_h, it cannot exit B_h either. Compact continuation gives global
positivity and existence for such a starting point.

B5. EVERY INITIAL POINT, NONNESTED SCALES, AND UNIFORM ENTRY
For every x0>0, the same finite-slope coordinate argument shows a zero label
is active at x0 for all sufficiently small h. At such a scale it is also
active at c; BOTH values equal the same minimum zero offset. Thus x0 in K_h.
This is stronger than mere inclusion in B_h and avoids an unproved claim
that arbitrary trajectories cannot leave B_h before approaching a plateau.

Fix h_P small enough for all preceding bounds and the c plateau construction.
Given x0, choose 0<h_0<=h_P with x0 in K_{h_0}. This immediately gives global
existence and positivity. Set h_{j+1}=min(sqrt(h_j),h_P); after finitely many
steps J this reaches h_P, since -log h is halved until the fixed cap is met.
For each step,
  K_{h_j} subset B_{sqrt(h_j)} subset B_{h_{j+1}}.
After entry into K_{h_j}, invariance keeps the trajectory in that box for
all later times, even if the next plateau is not nested with the old one.
While it is outside K_{h_{j+1}}, the next affine minimum increases at least
delta_{h_{j+1}} a.e. Its available range on the compact box is finite:
  W_h=(M_h-min_{x in B_h}F_h(x))/delta_h.
Therefore it enters K_{h_{j+1}} within W_{h_{j+1}} time units. Otherwise
integration would force F_h>M_h. Inducting gives entry into K_{h_P} no later
than T(x0)=sum_{j=1}^J W_{h_j}, uniformly over all admissible f. No nesting
of plateaux was assumed. Finally
  K_P=K_{h_P} intersection (c+S)
is nonempty, compact, convex, positive, invariant and absorbing as claimed.

C. ALL WEAKLY REVERSIBLE NETWORKS: A SELF-CONTAINED COROLLARY

Let a finite directed reaction graph have complexes y in R^d (in classical
mass action, y in N^d), with every edge y->y' lying on a directed cycle.
Discard zero vectors y'-y. For rates with logarithmic orders
  tau_e(p)=y_e dot p,
TE holds for EVERY r, including all lower-dimensional faces and ties.
To prove it, minimize r dot y_e among the relevant edges and suppose a
minimizing edge has r dot (y'-y)<0. Its target has r dot y'<m. A directed
return path y' -> ... -> y exists. Along that path there must be a first
edge with nonzero r-projection, since the total projection is m-r dot y'>0.
Its source still has r dot source=r dot y'<m (all preceding projections
are zero). It is a relevant edge with smaller source order, contradicting
minimality. Hence every relevant minimizing edge has positive projection.
This proof does not require a single linkage class, strong endotacticity,
complex balance, deficiency zero, or fixed coefficients.

In particular, classical autonomous mass action w_e=k_e x^{y_e}, k_e>0,
satisfies UV with epsilon(h)=max_e|log k_e|/|log h|. The all-dimensional
weakly reversible global permanence statement is therefore a COROLLARY of
A+B+C, without invoking the original source's mass-action permanence proof.
It is an independently checked consequence of the reconstructed affine
mechanism, not an independent discovery of the source's stated theorem.

C1. EXPLICIT ALLOWED RATE FAMILY BEYOND BOUNDED STATE MODULATIONS
For ANY such weakly reversible graph, fix 0<alpha<1 and finite constants
0<kappa_-<=kappa_+, C_e>=0. Permit arbitrary measurable paths
  kappa_e(t) in [kappa_-,kappa_+], c_e(t) in [-C_e,C_e],
and define
  rho_alpha(x)=(1+sum_i(log x_i)^2)^{alpha/2},
  w_e(t,x)=kappa_e(t) x^{y_e} exp(c_e(t) rho_alpha(x)).
These rates are locally Caratheodory with uniform local state-Lipschitz
bounds. At X_h(p), with L=-log h,
  |log w_e/log h - y_e dot p|
    <=max(|log kappa_-|,|log kappa_+|)/L
      +C_e(1+dL^2)^{alpha/2}/L ->0
uniformly over all t, p, and admitted paths. Thus B applies to the whole
family with ONE class-dependent compact convex absorber and one entry
bound per initial state. alpha=1/2 is N173's displayed example; the proof
works for every fixed alpha in (0,1).

For constant c_e>0, exp(c_e rho_alpha(x)) is unbounded as any coordinate
approaches 0 or infinity; for c_e<0 it tends to zero. Hence neither the
rate multiplier nor its reciprocal has a global state-independent bound.
Along a nonzero log ray its logarithm is order L^alpha, not O(1), so it is
not uniformly comparable to the SAME source monomial by fixed constants.
Measurable c_e(t), such as C_e*sgn(sin(t^2)), allow arbitrarily rapid temporal
switching. Nevertheless at each FIXED positive state this whole temporal
family remains bounded above and below; arbitrary unbounded temporal
amplitude is excluded by UV. This exact boundary must accompany the claim.

D. FALSIFIERS AND LIMITATIONS

D1. The independent diagonal-gauge test refutes only a product compiler.
For 0<eta<(b-a)/2 take E(s,t)=eta on s=t and E(s,t)=|s-t|/100 otherwise.
No off-diagonal tag can be active at a diagonal limiting exponent, because
its distance to the diagonal is >=|s-t|/2>E(s,t). In a product subdivision,
the closed rectangular cells on either side of each coordinate cut meet
the diagonal and force both adjacent coordinate tags to equal the same
opposite-axis tag. Iterating forces all tags to coincide, preventing eta
approximation of the entire diagonal. The actual nonseparable A1 construction
escapes this obstruction and preserves all closed-cell ties.

D2. All reaction-normal faces are essential. The mass-action edges
  0->Y, 2Y->Y, X+Y->Y
with unit coefficients give xdot=-xy, ydot=1-y^2. For y(0)=1 the solution
x(t)=x0 exp(-t) goes extinct. It satisfies TE on every r with r_y!=0 but
fails at r_x>0,r_y=0, where only X+Y->Y is relevant and its projection is
negative. A generic-directions-only criterion is therefore false. This is
N173's existing exact counterexample, rechecked algebraically here.

D3. UV supplies an existential geometric theorem, not an effective affine
compiler. Arbitrary E is supplied as a full function; an algorithm cannot
silently acquire it through finitely many oracle queries with unknown
regularity. A1 chooses compactness radii and a finite cover without label
count, bit-complexity, or numerical concentration bounds. No simulation or
finite face sampling proves A or B in general.

D4. The result does not give equilibrium convergence, uniqueness of an
equilibrium, stochastic permanence, reaction-diffusion permanence, or
experimental validity. A compact absorber can contain oscillations or
multiple equilibria. No formal proof-assistant replay or independent
expert review has been performed by this worker.

D5. Historical comparison must include Craciun arXiv:1501.02860v3, revised

invariant regions for bounded positive solutions and complex-balanced
