THEOREM

Work in the real vector representation of spin one on C^3, with
J_i=-i epsilon_(i,j,k), and tau=Tr/3. For real a,b define

  Phi_(a,b)(X)=tau(X)I+a(X-X^T)/2
                     +b[(X+X^T)/2-tau(X)I].                   (Q1)

These are the SO(3)-covariant two-scalar qutrit maps: their multipliers are
1 on the identity, a on the three spin/vector directions, and b on the
five real symmetric traceless quadrupole directions. In this theorem each
Phi_(a,b) is SUPPLIED as CPTP with a legal equal-marginal broadcaster.
No characterization of the sufficient self-compatibility region is claimed.

For every such supplied channel there is an explicit canonical PURE-ensemble
EB channel Psi_mu with at most 21 atoms satisfying

  I-Psi_mu <= 2(I-|Phi_(a,b)|) <= 2(I-Phi_(a,b))               (Q2)

on the ENTIRE nine-dimensional Hermitian Hilbert-Schmidt space of M3.
The absolute value here is a positive HS operator, not a purported CP map.

Consequently, for every finite n and every supplied collection of these
channels, ONE canonical EB comparator on M_(3^n) satisfies

  I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi_1 tensor ... tensor Phi_n)                 (Q3)

on the FULL Hermitian matrix space, including arbitrary quadrupole products,
mixed multipole products, and entangled linear combinations. The constant
two is sharp. The same constant survives supplied bistochastic adjoint
sandwiches, global unitary encodings, and finite mixtures. Thus this is a
natural full growing-dimensional family, beyond a supplied spin-only span.

1. EXACT CP REGION AND NORMALIZATION

The map (Q1) is TP and unital. Written on all complex matrices,

  Phi_(a,b)(X)=(1-b)Tr(X)I/3+(a+b)X/2+(b-a)X^T/2.            (Q4)

Its NORMALIZED Choi state is

  J_(a,b)=(1-b)I_9/9+(a+b)P_Omega/2+(b-a)Swap/6,            (Q5)

where |Omega>=(|00>+|11>+|22>)/sqrt(3). The orthogonal eigenspaces are
the line Omega, the three-dimensional antisymmetric subspace, and the
five-dimensional symmetric subspace perpendicular to Omega. Their
respective eigenvalues are

  e0=(1+3a+5b)/9,
  e1=(2+3a-5b)/18,
  e2=(2-3a+b)/18.                                             (Q6)

Complete positivity is EXACTLY the three conditions e0,e1,e2>=0.
The multiplicity-weighted sum e0+3e1+5e2 is one. In particular,

  a>=-1/2,       b>=-1/2.                                    (Q7)

For a, add the numerators of e0 and e1; for b, add the numerators
of e0 and e2. These are CP consequences, not cloning assumptions.

3. AN EXPLICIT 21-ATOM PURE CANONICAL COMPARATOR

There are two fixed pure canonical EB maps on M3.

REAL MAP R. Use the seven real unit vectors:
  e1,e2,e3, each with probability 2/15;
  (1,1,1)/sqrt(3), (1,-1,-1)/sqrt(3),
  (-1,1,-1)/sqrt(3), (-1,-1,1)/sqrt(3), each with probability 3/20.
Their rank-one projectors pi_t=t t^T have barycenter I/3. Direct second-
and fourth-moment calculation gives

  R(X)=3 sum_t p_t pi_t Tr(pi_t X)
      =[Tr(X)I+X+X^T]/5.                                    (Q12)

Its multipliers are 1,0,2/5 on identity, vector, quadrupole respectively.

COHERENT MAP C. Use fourteen real directions n:
  +-e1,+-e2,+-e3, each with probability 1/15;
  all eight sign vectors (+-1,+-1,+-1)/sqrt(3),
  each with probability 3/40.
For each direction take the PURE spin-coherent projector

  pi_n=[I-n n^T+J(n)]/2,       J(n)=sum_i n_i J_i.             (Q13)

Since J(n)^2=I-n n^T and J(n)n=0, each pi_n is Hermitian, idempotent,
and has trace one. The direction weights have
E[n_i]=0, E[n_i n_j]=delta_ij/3, and

  E[n_i n_j n_k n_l]
    =(delta_ij delta_kl+delta_ik delta_jl+delta_il delta_jk)/15.

The odd moments through degree three vanish. Thus the projectors have
barycenter I/3 and their canonical channel is exactly

  C(X)=3 sum_n q_n pi_n Tr(pi_n X)
      =(3/10)Tr(X)I+(3/10)X-(1/5)X^T.                       (Q14)

Its multipliers are 1,1/2,1/10. These moment identities are finite exact
algebra, not a Haar sampling approximation or an acquired design oracle.

For any mu in [0,1/2], set

  Psi_mu=(2mu)C+(1-2mu)R.                                   (Q15)

The explicit ensemble uses probabilities 2mu q_n on the fourteen coherent
projectors and (1-2mu)p_t on the seven real projectors, omitting zero
weights. It is a single pure ensemble with barycenter I/3. Its three
multipliers are

  1,       mu,       beta_mu=(2-3mu)/5.                      (Q16)

All 21 atoms and all probabilities are specified. No mixed POVM effect

I-tensor Y_j<=C(I-tensor X_j). Its proof uses X_j<=(Y_j+(C-1)I)/C

  p(A tensor B)+(1-p)I
  -[pA+(1-p)I] tensor [pB+(1-p)I]
