## 1. Exact named finite presentation and constants

Write `neg A := A->0`, `A^2 := A*A`, and associate higher powers to the left.
The author's S' has S1–S13 for FL_ew, S14 double negation, S15 weak condensation,
and MP alone. For N3 add the literal Proposition 8 schema

    N(A,B) := ((A^2->B) and ((neg B)^2->neg A)) -> (A->B).

defines `1=neg0` and `A*B=neg(A->negB)`; the finite presentation takes them as
primitive. We retain primitive fusion to avoid charging an unexamined weak-

## 2. Two-copy stabilization: a uniform potency lemma

Let `a=T_A`, `u=S_A` be arbitrary target formulas. No Pair or state assumption
is needed. The literal Chu recursion for source powers is

    P_1=a, Q_1=u;
    P_(n+1)=P_n*a;
    Q_(n+1)=(P_n->u) and (a->Q_n).

In LJ_!, ordinary C/W gives `a <=> P_n` for every n>=1 and
`(a->u) <=> Q_n` for every n>=2. The second equivalence deliberately starts at
two, because `Q_1=u` need not be equivalent to `a->u`.

For every **fixed k>=2**, there is a zero-input ordinary LJ_! proof of

    t(A^k->A^(k+1)) = (P_k->P_(k+1)) and (Q_(k+1)->Q_k)

with 9k rows and 22k−6 formula occurrences, using k explicit ordinary C rules.
Its largest antecedent has two occurrences. A direct optimized k=2 construction
has 14 rows/34 occurrences and two C rules.

Here is the full recurrence behind the general bound. Let `h=a->u`.
`delta_1=epsilon_1=id(a)`. Construct

* `delta_n: a=>P_n` by `R*(delta_(n−1),id a)`, then C on its two a occurrences;
* `epsilon_n: P_n=>a` by weakening `epsilon_(n−1)` with a, then L*;
* `eta_(k+1): Q_(k+1)=>h` by applying the first conjunct `P_k->u` to delta_k,
  selecting it with L-and, then right-implication on a;
* `theta_2: h=>Q_2` by right-and on `id h` twice;
* for n>=3, the first conjunct of `theta_n: h=>Q_n` follows from epsilon_(n−1),
  `id u`, L-implication and R-implication; the second follows by weakening
  theta_(n−1) with a and R-implication. Join them with R-and.

Cut epsilon_k with delta_(k+1), right-implicate P_k. Cut eta_(k+1) with
theta_k, right-implicate Q_(k+1). Right-and finishes. Each operation is an
ordinary multiset rule. The recurrence yields the displayed exact counts for
all k, rather than inferring them from finitely many generated examples.

If k is supplied as an unbounded extra parameter, charge O(k), or the sum of
parameters over uses. The finite Frege line statement fixes k as part of the
system. Established k-potency notation varies: the Nelson source calls
`a^2<=a^3` **three-potency**, and its footnote notes that other authors call it
two-potency. The source axiom itself, rather than that terminology, is pinned.

## 3. The exact finite S15 schema

S15 is

    (A->(A->(A->B))) -> (A->(A->B)).

Put `a=T_A,u=S_A,b=T_B,v=S_B` and define

    d=(a->b) and (v->u), av=a*v;
    e=(a->d) and (av->u), aav=a*av;
    g=(a->e) and (aav->u), aaav=a*aav.

Its **unnormalized literal** translated endpoint is

    t(S15) = (g->e) and (aav->aaav).

For the first positive conjunct, g,a,a entails d by selecting the first
conjunct of g, applying once to a, then selecting/applying the first conjunct
of e. One explicit C on the two a occurrences gives g,a=>d. For the second,
g,a,a,v entails u by constructing aav and applying the second conjunct of g.
One C on a and packing a*v gives g,av=>u. Right implications and right-and
give g=>e, then g->e. To obtain the negative conjunct, a,a,av entails aaav;
one C reduces the two explicit a occurrences and L* packs aav, then R->.
Right-and concludes. The exact certificate uses **26 rows/72 occurrences,
three ordinary C, no Pair or state inputs**, uniformly in all four target slots.

S15 is source-equivalent, at fixed schematic cost, to `A^2->A^3`: currying
rewrites it as `(A^3->B)->(A^2->B)`. Potency implies this by composition;
setting B=A^3 and applying reflexivity proves potency from S15. In the
involutive source, its B=0 instance also expresses the negated-power form.
We install the literal published schema, so the theorem does not depend on
accepting an informal rewritten endpoint.

The meaningful new instance is **A=p, B=linear0**. It is the source contraction
instance `c(p,neg p)`, whose domain p has no old tt witness and whose codomain
`neg p` has no old sf witness. A=p,B=q is not a strict example: it is
`c(p,p->q)`, already admitted by sf(p->q). The concrete certificate checks the
actual Chu endpoint, not an ideal swap of its negative components.

## 4. The literal power Nelson axiom and its one charged obligation

Fix arbitrary target slots `a,u,b,v`, with solely the ordinary proved input

    Pair_B: b,v=>bottom.

The exact source negation wrappers are

    alpha=T_(neg A)=(a->bottom) and (1->u), S_(neg A)=a*1;
    beta=T_(neg B)=(b->bottom) and (1->v), S_(neg B)=b*1.

Define

    a2=a*a, beta2=beta*beta;
    s2a=(a->u) and (a->u);
    s2nb=(beta->(b*1)) and (beta->(b*1));
    e=(a2->b) and (v->s2a);
    g=(beta2->alpha) and ((a*1)->s2nb);
    h=e and g, d=(a->b) and (v->u), av=a*v;
    se=a2*v, sg=beta2*(a*1).

The literal primary tensor-power endpoint is exactly

    t(N(A,B)) = (h->d) and (av->(se or sg)).

For h=>a->b, h,a,a entails b by selecting a2->b and constructing a2; one C
on a gives h,a=>b, then R->. Pair_B gives `v=>b->bottom`; the unit rule gives
`v=>1->v`; R-and joins them into `v=>beta`. Projecting `1->u` from alpha and
applying the closed unit theorem gives `alpha=>u`, without any Pair_A input.
Selecting beta2->alpha in g, constructing beta2, and applying alpha=>u gives
`h,beta,beta=>u`; one C gives `h,beta=>u`. Cut with v=>beta and R-> yields
h=>v->u. R-and and R-> produce the first conjunct. For the second, a,a,v
entails se by R* twice; one C on a and L* give av=>se. Introduce the disjunction
se or sg and R->; R-and concludes.

There are **34 rows/86 formula occurrences including the one Pair_B input
row**, hence **33 new rows/83 new occurrences** once Pair_B is cached. Three
ordinary C rules are explicit. The curried source version, with real negation
wrappers, independently has 37 rows/95 occurrences including that input, but
we use the actual primary power version for the named theorem.

Pair_B is essential in this exact macro: arbitrary Boolean slots
`a=u=0,b=v=1` refute the literal translated axiom if the Pair premise is dropped.
Actual source pairs always satisfy it by the memoized Pair construction. Thus
there is no uncharged semantic assumption. Neg B's wrapper nodes add only
constantly many syntax nodes per axiom use; no new unbounded family of Pair
operands is generated. **Only B itself needs Pair here.**

## 5. Quantitative theorem and lower-bound transfer

Let H be the published finite S' presentation, or that presentation plus the
literal Nelson axiom for N3. Let pi be an ordinary H proof with L formula
lines, common formula/operand DAG with V nodes, and last formula A. Then

    LJ_! proves =>T_A with at most C_H (V+L) formula occurrences.

First replace every S1–S14 line with its fixed canonical ALL derivation from
Section 1. There are at most **51 canonical source rows per old named axiom**,
and at most 70 supplied constructor/atom nodes per such fixed template. This
adds O(L) rows and syntax constructors on the same operands.
Use the cycle14 memoized Chu compiler for the canonical affine lines; install
the literal S15 and N templates above for their lines. Compute Pair on each
input syntax node once, topologically. Its O(V) rows/occurrences remain valid
in the stronger LJ_! target. MP is its old bounded closed-theorem template.
Cached proved sequents are referenced, never supplied as new linear resources.
The new templates have constant antecedent width and cost at most 83 added
occurrences; the old ALL local bound was at most 93 added occurrences. The
overall named-basis constant C_H also accounts for the finite basis adapters;
we do not assert a whole named-basis numerical constant before replaying them.

The old cycle14 finite-skeleton lemma applies **unchanged**, since H has a
fixed finite set of ordinary schemata and no syntax side condition. An L-line
proof of A has a same-conclusion L-line representative with V=O(L+|A|): freeze
rule choices and earlier-line references; introduce term variables for line
and operand roots; equate schema premise/conclusion terms and the rigid last
tree A; quotient by constructor unification; ground unconstrained classes
with one atom. The original proof is a finite ground solution, so labels never
conflict and no constructor cycle is possible. Each branch strictly decreases
original formula height. The quotient has O(L+|A|) nodes. Arbitrary substituted
operands remain legal in **all** S15/N instances; there are no guard annotations
to preserve.

If A is conservative in primary Definition 16, Lemma 19 supplies an ordinary
target proof `T_A=>A` with O(|A|^2) formula occurrences. Append it and one cut:

    LJ_! proves =>A with at most C'_H (L+|A|^2) formula occurrences.

For A=Theta*_n, primary Corollary 10 states BFL provability, polynomial tree
size, and a **2^(n^Omega(1)) ordinary LJ_! formula-occurrence lower bound**.
The same target already has unrestricted ordinary C and W, and its lower-
bound proof erases bangs. S' contains FL_ew and N3 strengthens S', so these
same bang-free formulas are theorems of the named systems. Therefore

    L_H(Theta*_n) >= 2^(n^Omega(1)).

This bound is exponentially large in the paper's parameter n; since |Theta*_n|
is polynomial in n it is also stretched-exponential in target formula size.
It is not a size statement deduced merely from |pi|>=L. The smaller target
proof has been bounded directly in the exact metric used by Corollary 10.

For fresh acyclic **strong definitional EF**, a definition is
`(e->F) and (F->e)`, e fresh, absent from F/earlier formulas/final A, and F using
only earlier extension atoms. Globally expand extension atoms in chronological
order as a DAG. Every definition becomes `(G->G) and (G->G)`, with an **explicit
11-row published S' proof using only S2/S3/S8/S12 and MP**, uniformly in G.
For its identity part, S3 gives G->1->G; S2 and MP give 1->G->G; MP with S12

and equal codomainsG->G twice using the same proved line, then MP with S12.
This is reuse of closed theorems through MP, not structural contraction in a
source context. The eleven serialized rows were independently replayed here.
All other rule instances remain instances after substitution. Thus an L-line
EF proof gives an at most **11L-line** ordinary H proof with the same A,
though its explicit trees may be huge. Skeleton-compress that ordinary proof
and apply the theorem. Hence the same exponential bound holds for **EF lines
and EF symbol size**. Do not raw-translate extension definitions: the cycle14
e=p*p counterexample still refutes that shortcut. The new uniform schemata
survive expansion; no EF alias can circumvent a source grammar because no
grammar guard is imposed for these two axioms.

No conclusion is asserted for a uniform-substitution inference rule, arbitrary

## 6. Properness and previous barriers

These source axioms do not imply ordinary unrestricted strong contraction.
The exact three-element Lukasiewicz algebra `{0,1/2,1}`, with additive min/max,
fusion `max(0,x+y−1)`, residual `min(1,1−x+y)`, and 0/1 false/true, satisfies
square-to-cube, S15, **and the literal Nelson axiom**, but refutes both
`p->p*p` and `c(p,0)` at p=1/2, value 1/2. Bang can be interpreted as Boolean
floor; the independently checked old modal axioms remain valid. Thus even the
uniform potency-plus-Nelson modal extension is weaker than unrestricted strong
contraction. This is a complete finite sound-model obstruction: all carrier
assignments to each axiom schema were checked. The continuous Lukasiewicz
model refutes potency
at p=2/3, value 2/3, so the uniform potency branch is not already an affine
theorem. The full literal N3/potency logic is established prior; the finite
algebra check is a scoped exact control, not evidence for an arbitrary logic.

The cycle15 raw unrestricted-contraction Boolean obstruction and raw CWC
Heyting obstruction remain. Simply giving the target stronger rules does not
translate arbitrary source contraction/CWC. The new successful primitives
have a different balance: powers stabilize at two in both Chu coordinates;
Nelson's backwards doubled-negative premise, together with Pair_B, supplies
precisely the missing negative implication. Target C duplicates explicitly
listed ordinary formulas only. This is a direct derivation, not an appeal to
unrestricted preservation of a negative translation.
