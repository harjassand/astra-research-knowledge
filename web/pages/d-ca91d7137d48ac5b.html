## Nearest prior art and novelty status

Dollard reference dynamics, Coulomb-wavefunction perturbation, relative optical theorems, scalar/matrix contractivity, and soft-radiation decoherence are established prior art. We have not discovered those mechanisms. The quantitative moving-front lemma in technical_constructions.md was independently derived from elementary Bessel estimates during this run; a literature-wide novelty search for precisely this inequality has not been completed. The simple constant is loose and the leading kernel is restricted, so even if absent from the cited sources this alone is not a consequential foundational breakthrough.

The two independently developed 2026 approaches expose complementary interfaces: (i) finite Coulomb partial waves with an asymptotic reference choice, and (ii) analyticity/positivity for a detector scaling limit. They cannot be combined merely by multiplying out a phase: real radiation participates in crossing, and exact hard-only measurements need a multi-Kraus process whenever unresolved soft profiles differ.

## Three credible paths, ranked by missing physics

1. **Quantified finite-detector dispersion bounds.** Start with the uniform front lemma, prove an all-energy integrable remainder including recoil and radiation, then construct an explicit interval for an EFT coefficient. This has direct practical consequences. The high-energy incomplete-Mellin or physical Regge envelope is the next concrete analytical bottleneck.
2. **Amplitude-to-instrument dispersive framework.** Form a positive Gram object for unresolved soft channels, with an actual detector instrument, and reconstruct its crossing-compatible spectral constraints. Rank-one hard amplitudes cannot preserve the full interface, but positivity of the channel alone is insufficient; analytic completion is genuinely unresolved here.
3. **Crossing-based radiative completion from a solved Coulomb reference theory.** August Lippstreu suggests this possibility. A discriminating calculation is a dynamical-source one-loop scalar-QED amplitude, including the radiative term and recoil, with both crossed continuations compared. The static background does not furnish that answer.

No path is yet proved universally. The concrete counterexample and front theorem reject overly strong shortcuts while leaving these alternatives open.
