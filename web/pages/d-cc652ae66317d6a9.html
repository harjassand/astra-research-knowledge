FINAL — c01_l02: PAIRED DETERMINANT EXCHANGE WITH MISMATCH REGISTERS
UTC: 2026-10-07

Assignment: transfer determinantal basis-exchange / volume-sampling couplings
to paired configurations while retaining mismatch registers and charging all
costs.

Strongest proved result: for two nonsingular d-column bases A,B of one
represented linear matroid, fix e=A_i in A\B and exchange it with f=B_j in
B\A. If A^{ij},B^{ji} are the two slot-preserving symmetric exchanges, then

  det(A)det(B) = sum_{j:B_j in B\A} det(A^{ij})det(B^{ji}),
  |det(A)det(B)|^2 <= k sum_j |det(A^{ij})det(B^{ji})|^2,

where k=|B\A|. This follows exactly from C=A^{-1}B:
det(A^{ij})=det(A)C_ij, det(B^{ji})=det(B)(C^{-1})_ji, and
sum_j C_ij(C^{-1})_ji=1; common columns have C_ij=0. For paired block bases
A_S,A_T with block size two and i in S\T, k=2|T\S|<=d. Every nonzero output
has a two-site split-block mismatch at i and the site of f. The labeled
register (e,f) makes the exchange reversible. This improves the INITIAL
two-column expansion from binom(2|T\S|,2) candidates to 2|T\S| candidates.

For rational-complex inputs the exact proposal probability for f is
proportional to R_ij=|C_ij|^2 |(C^{-1})_ji|^2. The normalizer is positive by
the signed identity; the pointwise bound implies sum_j R_ij>=1/k. A reversible
kernel on ordered pairs of full bases is

  P(X,Y)=mu(Y)/(d^2(mu(X)+mu(Y)))

for each valid symmetric-exchange neighbor, with remaining mass assigned to a
self-loop and mu(A,B)=w(A)w(B), w(A)=activity(A)|det A|^2. It is exactly
reversible by detailed balance; its probabilities can be evaluated as
R_ij/(d^2(1+R_ij)), without the partition function. Its fixed-e outgoing
reversible conductance is at least mu(X)/(2d^3). Compute C and C^{-1} in
O(d^3) exact rational field operations, then all up to d^2 exchange weights;
Hadamard/fraction-free bounds give polynomial bit lengths in d and the input
bit size L. Exact rational sampling over O(d^2) outcomes costs polynomially
many random bits in expectation. These are per-step, not mixing/counting,
bounds.

An even directed-cycle family is an exact stress test. For n even and F the
cyclic shift, paired block determinants are nonzero only on the two alternating
size-n/2 block sets. Thus the paired one-block-exchange support is disconnected
and ordinary volume-sampling rejection accepts with probability 2^{1-n};
requiring both replicas paired has probability 2^{2-2n}. In the enlarged
fixed-union pair-basis component, however, the exchange graph is the n-cube.
The explicit Hamming extension of a function on the two paired antipodes has
target variance n^3 times its Dirichlet form under the kernel above. This is a
family-specific polynomial lift, not a general construction. Exact Bareiss
checks and exact rational enumeration for n=4,6,8 are in
cycle_family_lift_check.py/.json; the one-column exchange fixture is in
paired_single_exchange_check.py/.json. The earlier two-column check and
ordinary basis check remain in the directory.

The central gate remains open. The reversible pair-basis kernel preserves the
multiset union, and the local capacity inequality does not prove global
connectivity, a polynomial spectral gap, or an extension of every paired
observable. In particular, do not infer connectedness solely from matroid
symmetric exchange: Matt Larson's July 2026 arXiv preprint gives a disconnected
two-basis exchange graph for a binary rank-9 matroid. That binary example is
not automatically a Q(i)-representable counterexample, so it is a warning
about generic matroid arguments, not a disproof for the actual complex-linear
hard-BCS inputs. Regular matroids do have polynomial exchange paths by
Bérczi–Mátravölgyi–Schwarcz (2026), but the assignment has no regularity
assumption. Even a connectivity theorem for the relevant represented class
would still need weighted congestion/Poincare control uniform along annealing.

No microscopic two-replica energy theorem, general paired sampler, or FPRAS
is established. The most useful next attack is to find a general observable
extension/capacity argument for the mismatch kernel, or a counterexample
within the actual Q(i)-represented class, while preserving exact activities,
all weights, and the annealing cost.

Primary literature used for scope: Anari et al., “Log-Concave Polynomials IV,”
arXiv:2004.07220v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2004.07220v2; Larson,
“Counterexamples to two conjectures about matroids,” arXiv:2607.02208,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02208 (preprint self-discloses LLM-assisted search
and provides written proofs); Bérczi, Mátravölgyi, and Schwarcz,
“Reconfiguration of basis pairs in regular matroids,” arXiv:2311.07130,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07130, published JCTB 177 (2026). These delimit
comparators; novelty and external validation are not claimed.
