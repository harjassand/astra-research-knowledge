2026-10-09. Elementary proof; external validation UNKNOWN.

For every integer d>=2, let F swap the two d-dimensional tensor factors,
P=(I+F)/2, and define the supplied broadcasting channel
  B_d(X)=2 P(X tensor I)P/(d+1).
It is completely positive. Its trace is
  2 Tr[P(X tensor I)]/(d+1)=Tr X,
because Tr[P(X tensor I)]=(d+1)Tr X/2. It is invariant under swapping the
two outputs. Expanding P shows both marginals are
  Phi_d(X)=((d+2)X+(Tr X)I)/(2(d+1)).
Thus Phi_d is CPTP, unital, HS-self-adjoint, HS-positive, and self-compatible
with this explicit B_d. On traceless Hermitian matrices its eigenvalue is
  eta_d=(d+2)/(2(d+1)).

The pure Haar reference ensemble has mean I/d and second moment
  integral pi tensor pi dmu(pi)=(I+F)/(d(d+1)).
Its canonical EB channel is
  Psi_d(X)=(X+(Tr X)I)/(d+1),
with traceless eigenvalue 1/(d+1). It satisfies EXACTLY
  I-Psi_d=2(I-Phi_d).
The Haar moment can be represented by a finite convex combination by
Caratheodory (a safe bound is d^4+1 pure states), so a finite canonical POVM
exists. This is an existence statement, not an efficient design compiler.

To prove that arbitrary EB channels cannot improve the constant, take any

Any full-domain quadratic-form inequality I-Lambda<=C(I-Phi_d) therefore
forces C>=2 by taking its operator trace. No optimal-cloner theorem is
