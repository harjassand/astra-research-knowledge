# Arbitrarily slow loss at the critical Landis amplitude: prior supplement

7 October 2026. Prior-art audit, not a mathematical proof gate. Read r15's `ADMISSIBLE_SLOW_ENVELOPES.md` completely. Its claimed construction and the independently reconstructed gates must be assessed separately from this historical comparison. Historical priority **UNKNOWN**.

The exact target is: for each positive function L(r) tending to infinity, and each n≥3, construct real smooth nonzero u and real smooth V satisfying −Δu+Vu=0, |V(x)|≤C r^(−1/2)L(r) eventually, each fixed Cartesian derivative D^mV(x)→0, and e^(τr)u∈L² for every τ>0. The constants, staircase, starting radius, V and u may depend on L. This is not a simultaneous construction for all L, an endpoint O(r^(−1/2)) construction, or a fixed-power exp(−r^(1+ε)) statement.

The existing critical amplitude boundary is already established; the proposed advance would approach it from every divergent loss while retaining absolute C∞ decay. ## What the new quantifier would add

A family |V_ε|≤C_ε r^(−1/2+ε), ε>0, cannot simply be passed to ε=0. For example L(r)=loglog(r+e^e) tends to infinity more slowly than every r^ε. No fixed ε power estimate implies |V_ε|≤C r^(−1/2)L(r), and no compactness of this family was supplied. The proposed staircase supplies a new single construction depending on that L rather than an illicit interchange of quantifiers.

The staircase route has a clear balance: choose a→∞, a= r^o(1), a^(3/2)≤L eventually. Then κ≤r^(−1/2)L and κ a^m=r^(−1/2+o(1))→0 for each fixed m; Φ(r)/r→∞ follows from monotonicity and a→∞. These are conditional consequences of the construction's estimates, not historical novelty evidence. They identify precisely what a prior theorem would need to match.

Rossi's radial/spectral-positive theorems, Pan–Zhang's signed radial monotonicity, and Sirakov–Souplet2609.15821 Theorem2.10 remain meaningful restrictions. In particular, a successful example must have λ1(−Δ+V)<0 by the sphere-integral argument in the main audit. It does not contradict nonnegative-operator Landis results.
