## 1. Direct quartic Hessian on the symmetric leaf slice

Consider one real normalized star. Let its center mass be r>0 and every leaf mass be1, so y_0=sqrt(r), y_i=1. Set s=t r and a=3-q. Direct differentiation gives

    H_00=12r-4kt,
    H_ii=12-4tr=4(3-s),
    H_0i=H_i0=-8t sqrt(r),
    H_ij=0 for distinct leaves.

The determinant follows by the leaf Schur complement:

    h(r)=4^(k-1) * (48/t) * (3-s)^(k-1) p(s),
    p(s)=-s^2+(3-q)s-q,
    f(r)=r^2-2ktr+k=d(s)/t^2,
    d(s)=s^2-2qs+q.                                    (3)

For0<q<1, d is positive on the whole real line. The two roots of p are

    s_+/-=[(3-q)+/-sqrt((1-q)(9-q))]/2.

Both are positive and s_+<3. Thus H>0 precisely on the interval s_-<s<s_+. On this slice the projective logdet ratio is, up to a constant,

    G(s)=(k-1)log(3-s)+log p(s)-(k+1)/2 log d(s).        (4)

It tends to minus infinity at both interval endpoints. A critical point can be analyzed exactly without global concavity.

## 2. Every positive-dimensional critical equation lies in a smaller interval

Differentiate (4):

    G'(s)=p'(s)/p(s)-(k-1)/(3-s)-(k+1)(s-q)/d(s).

After a common positive denominator, the critical numerator is N0-k N1, where

    N0=q(1-s) P2(s),
    P2(s)=2s^2-(3+q)s+9-7q,
    N1=p(s)[(3-q)s-2q].                                (5)

The discriminant of P2 is(q-1)(q+63)<0, so P2>0 for every real s. Put s0=2q/(3-q). Then

    0<s0<1,
    p(s0)=q[1-4q/(3-q)^2]>0,
    p(1)=2(1-q)>0.

Both s0 and1 lie in the positive-Hessian interval. For s<s0 in that interval, N0>0 and N1<0, so N0-kN1>0. For s>1, N0<0 and N1>0, so N0-kN1<0. The endpoints s0,1 are not critical either. Thus every critical point for k>0 must lie in(s0,1) and obey

    k=k(s)= q(1-s)P2(s) / {p(s)[(3-q)s-2q]}.           (6)

On this smaller interval, k(s) tends to positive infinity at s0 and to0 at1.

## 3. A constant-degree certificate makes k(s) strictly decreasing

Direct rational differentiation of (6) yields

    k'(s)=3q(1-q) P4(s,q)
            / {[(3-q)s-2q]^2 p(s)^2},                 (7)

where

    P4=q^2 s^2-6q^2 s-11q^2
       +q s^4+8q s^3-16q s^2+12q s+27q
       -s^4-24s^3+63s^2-54s.

Here is a complete exact negativity certificate on the whole needed region. Set e=1-q in(0,1), u=1-s, and v=u(e+2)/(3e). The inequalities s0<s<1 are equivalent to0<v<1. Define

    R(e,v)=(e+2)^4 P4(1-3ev/(e+2),1-e).

The following identity holds over Q[e,v]:

    R(e,v)=sum_(j=0)^4 b_j(e) binom(4,j)v^j(1-v)^(4-j),

with

    b0=-16 e^2(e+2)^4,
    b1=-e^2(e+2)^3(13e+32),
    b2=-e^2(e+2)^2(e+8)(17e+16)/2,
    b3=-e^2(e+2)(e+8)^2(5e+4)/2,
    b4=e^2(e-1)(e+8)^2(5e+4).                         (8)

Every coefficient is strictly negative for0<e<1; all Bernstein weights are nonnegative and sum to1. Thus R<0, P4<0 and k'(s)<0. This is a fixed-degree polynomial identity and sign proof, not a floating optimization or a finite-dimensional sweep. The checker reconstructs (5)-(8) symbolically from (3)-(4).

Consequently, for every k>0 there is a unique solution s_* in(s0,1) of k(s_*)=k. Factor the derivative as

    G'(s)=[((3-q)s-2q)/((3-s)d(s))] [k(s)-k].

At s_* the first bracket is positive and k'(s_*)<0, so G''(s_*)<0. In the actual log-ratio coordinate z=log r (equivalently log s), the second derivative is s_*^2 G''(s_*)<0. The critical point is the unique maximum of the symmetric leaf slice, with strictly negative curvature in its one nonradial symmetric direction. No uniqueness of all full-space critical points is claimed.

## 4. Leaf contrasts supply the remaining negative Hessian directions

Return to all log masses z_i=log m_i. For every Stieltjes Q, including this strong star, the exact identity

    K(z)=Y^-1 H Y^-1
        =8Q+4diag(Qm/m),
    h=(product_i m_i) det K(z)

proves log h concave on K>0: K'' is diagonal nonpositive in every direction, and

    (logdet K)''=tr(K^-1 K'')-tr(K^-1 K'K^-1 K')<=0.

This global logh fact does not imply global concavity of G.

At the symmetric masses r=s_*/t,1,...,1, a log-mass leaf contrast is u_0=0 and sum_(i>=1)u_i=0. Set C=PQP and c=C1. Then

    u^T C u=sum_(i>=1)u_i^2,
    c_leaf=1-s_*, c^T u=0.

The exact logf Hessian formula therefore gives

    u^T Hess(log f)u=2(2-s_*)/f *sum_(i>=1)u_i^2>0.     (9)

Since logh has nonpositive Hessian, G=logh-(k+1)/2 logf has strictly negative Hessian on every nonzero leaf contrast.

The leaf permutation group makes the center/common-leaf direction and the leaf-sum-zero subspace have zero Hessian cross terms. More explicitly, any invariant bilinear cross functional on leaf contrasts equals its permutation average, which is zero. The radial constant-shift direction is a Hessian null direction by homogeneity. The one remaining symmetric projective direction is negative by Section3, and all k-1 leaf directions are negative by (9). Hence the full projective Hessian is negative definite. For k=1 the leaf-contrast space is empty and Section3 alone suffices.

This is a full critical point, not merely a critical point constrained to symmetry: the center component of grad G is sG'(s)=0; all leaf components are equal, and the sum of all components is0 by radial homogeneity. Thus every leaf component also vanishes. We have obtained a full-support nondegenerate local maximum in H>0.
