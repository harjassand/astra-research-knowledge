# Fresh foundational-theory screen, 2026-10-10

## Outcome

No historic new capability was obtained. The strongest retained result is an exact structural falsification of a concrete, efficiently computable synchronization potential.

For every m-state automaton over at least two letters, a transition-explicit extension to 2m-1 states preserves its entire nonempty reset language while forcing the minimum spanning tree of pair-merging distances to have its smallest possible weight, 2m-2. Applying this to the classical Černý family gives reset threshold (m-1)^2 with MST 2m-2. Hence no fixed multiple of pair-distance MST can bound reset length. The precise construction, proof, limits, and primary sources are in RESET_PROXY_THEOREM.md.

This is an independently derived mechanism-specific negative result. Priority is unestablished. It rules out the attempted amortization; it does not improve a reset-word upper bound or resolve any named open conjecture.

## Breadth screen and stopped routes

- Local-to-global consistency: naive pairwise marginal agreement does not imply a global joint object; established junction-tree and consistency methods already make tree-like cases exact. No new efficient general gluing mechanism was found, so no new primitive was claimed.
- Spectral graph identification: fixed-particle noninteracting quantum walks were screened out because a primary paper already proves they cannot distinguish all strongly regular graphs: Rudinger et al., Physical Review A 86, 022334 (2012), https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.86.022334 . No graph-isomorphism claim was made.
- Closure-native entropy/transport: an explicit seeded computation found the 17-set C5 upward-matching counterexample. A primary-source check then found the identical counterexample already given by Alec Edgington in 2016. It is preserved and attributed in UNION_TRANSPORT_CLOSED.md; the natural isotone-weight repair was also already known. This line was closed.
- Approximate algebra repair: the apparent general primitive overlaps an existing property-testing literature. Goldreich and Tauber, On Testing Group Properties (January 2, 2024), https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~oded/R1/group.pdf , already gives a near-linear-in-domain-size tester. No new correction theorem survived screening.
- Synchronization by compatible contractions: this was selected for concrete work because pair distances are acquired explicitly in polynomial time. The MST proposal, its small-instance correction, and finally every constant-factor rescaling were falsified. See the main theorem file.

## Indispensable remaining obstruction

The reset problem requires one word to act on all states. Pair-merging distances optimize a different word for each pair. A successful polynomial-size quadratic potential must charge this shared-control compatibility. Replacing the potential by the actual reset distance, by a maximization over exponentially many reachable subsets, or by a shortest-reset oracle does not acquire that missing capability. No such replacement was obtained in this screen.

The initial conjecture that pair-distance MST might always be quadratically bounded was not proved or disproved here. It is no longer sufficient for the proposed mechanism because its amortized payment obligation fails by an unbounded factor.
