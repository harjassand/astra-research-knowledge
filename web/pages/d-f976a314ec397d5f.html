Result
======

I found no valid consequence of the frozen independent-input amplifier EPnI
that by itself lowers the unassisted thermal-attenuator positivity threshold.
The direct proposed transfer—replace every S(X) in the amplifier theorem by
S(X|R), then apply g^{-1}—is false even for a finite-energy two-mode Gaussian
reference state with positive conditional entropies and exact conditional
independence. The candidate theorem itself remains consistent: its
unconditioned statement applies to the A marginal and vacuum port, and is
saturated in this example.

The closest valid reference-conditioned primitive is already known: the
quantum conditional entropy power inequality (CqEPI) of De Palma–Trevisan.
It handles negative conditional entropies by exponentiating S(X|R), but gives
a lower bound on S(C|R). For unassisted coherent information,

  I_c(rho,Phi) = S(B) - S(RB)
                = S(B) - S(B|R) - S(R),

where R purifies the channel input. A lower bound on S(B|R) therefore gives
an upper bound on I_c once S(B) is fixed. Correction note (2026-10-10): the
first displayed equality in the initial version incorrectly included a
`+ S(R)` term. The corrected identity above is used throughout; the subsequent
sign/orientation discussion and downstream claims remain unchanged. Positivity
requires a lower bound on I_c, equivalently an upper bound on the joint
complement entropy S(E'F) in a thermal-environment Stinespring dilation.
The amplifier EPnI lower-bounds the receiver entropy S(B); it supplies no
such upper bound on S(E'F). This is the missing inequality for the proposed
capacity transfer.

The candidate defines N(rho)=g^{-1}(S(rho)/n) and assumes the two amplifier

Gaussian falsification of the naive conditional EPnI
====================================================

Use one mode each, natural-log g(x)=(x+1)log(x+1)-x log x, vacuum
quadrature variance 1/2. Prepare R,A by a two-mode squeezer of parameter
s=1/4 on tau_{1/2} tensor tau_{1/2}; let B be vacuum and independent. Thus
rho_RAB=rho_RA tensor |0><0|_B and I(A:B|R)=0 exactly. The covariance of RA
has blocks a I, a I, c Z, where

  a=cosh(1/2),   c=sinh(1/2).

Apply the candidate amplifier of gain G=6/5 to A and B, retaining C. Then

  a_C=(6/5)a+1/10,   c_C=sqrt(6/5)c,
  Delta=a_C^2+a^2-2c_C^2,
  d=(a_C a-c_C^2)^2,
  nu_+-=sqrt((Delta +- sqrt(Delta^2-4d))/2).

The input RA symplectic eigenvalues are exactly (1,1), so
S(A|R)=2g(1/2)-g(a-1/2). The output is
S(C|R)=g(nu_+-1/2)+g(nu_--1/2)-g(a-1/2).
An outward interval calculation is reproduced in support/e6_conditional_amplifier_gaussian.py.
It encloses

  S(A|R) in [0.8243342843866402628683053408951648,
             0.8243342843866402628683053408951649],
  S(C|R) in [1.1161306649035093385331732789398432,
             1.1161306649035093385331732789398433].

Monotonicity of g and the same interval calculation give
  g^{-1}(S(A|R)) > 0.3895,
  g^{-1}(S(C|R)) < 0.6607.
So the proposed conditional amplifier EPnI would require

  g^{-1}(S(C|R)) >= (6/5)g^{-1}(S(A|R)) + 1/5 > 0.6674,

contradicting the certified upper bound 0.6607. Direct high-precision roots
give 0.3895082025004649 and 0.6606217149899746, a gap of -0.0067881280105833.
Both conditional entropies in this counterexample are positive, so the
failure is not just the inverse g domain issue. For the natural pure RA
purification used in coherent information, S(A|R)=-S(R)<0, and g^{-1} of
that conditional entropy is not defined at all.

As a scope check, the A marginal is thermal with mean a-1/2. Its amplifier
output is thermal with mean (6/5)(a-1/2)+1/5, exactly the unconditioned
candidate EPnI equality. Thus the counterexample falsifies only the
reference-conditioned strengthening, not the stated independent-input
theorem.

The established CqEPI is consistent with this example: its one-mode
amplifier form is exp(S(C|R)) >= G exp(S(A|R))+(G-1)exp(S(B|R)). Here the
intervals give left side about 3.05301817 and right side about 2.93643462.
This is the weaker exponential-von-Neumann-entropy bound, not the additive
photon-number bound above.

Capacity-transfer check
=======================

The primary 2026 thermal-attenuator paper writes
Phi_{eta,nu}=A_G o L_tau with G=1+(1-eta)nu and tau=eta/G. At the target
(eta,nu)=(0.8,1), this is exactly G=6/5 and tau=2/3. A valid EPNI application
can lower-bound the receiver output entropy through the loss and amplifier
stages. But the one-use coherent information is S(B)-S(E'F), where F
purifies the thermal bath; knowing a lower bound for S(B) does not upper-bound
the complementary joint entropy S(E'F). CqEPI conditioned on R instead
lower-bounds S(B|R), which is the wrong sign for proving I_c>0.

The current primary result leaves eta_c(1) between 0.75 (antidegradability)
and 0.7841 (a certified non-Gaussian positive-coherent-information input).
It proves a positive non-Gaussian witness already at eta=0.8, where every
single-mode Gaussian input has nonpositive coherent information. The witness
uses an explicit finite-rank input and a cutoff-erasure lower bound on
coherent information. This emphasizes that a scalar output-entropy lower
bound is not the missing ingredient: the transfer needs an upper bound on
the reference-sensitive complementary entropy, with enough non-Gaussian
resolution to certify S(B)>S(E'F).

Recommendation
==============

Close the direct conditional-EPnI transfer gate as false, with the Gaussian
counterexample above. Keep the candidate theorem as a possible receiver
entropy lemma only. Do not advertise it as a quantum-capacity threshold
result. A future capacity attack would need a distinct complementary-output
upper bound (or a direct coherent-information estimate); it should be tested
first against the explicit non-Gaussian witness and against the antidegradable
boundary. The known CqEPI is not that new primitive and has converse-side
orientation for this objective.
