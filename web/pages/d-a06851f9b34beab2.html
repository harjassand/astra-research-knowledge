# Independent audit of r12's scalarization obstructions

2026-10-07. Independently reconstructed the arguments in notes/r12_geometry/cycle7/CYLINDER_SCALAR_REDUCTION_OBSTRUCTIONS.md. All three main obstructions pass in their stated interfaces; none resolves the scalar Euclidean finite-index question.

For a coordinate diffeomorphism the principal contravariant tensor transforms as DF A DFᵀ. Its inverse transforms as a metric; nowhere-zero scalar conjugation does not change the principal tensor, and positive equation multiplication only changes its conformal class. Thus conformal flatness is a necessary condition for the proposed reduction to principal tensor I. For g=dθ²+dx²+f(x)²dφ², the surface curvature is K=−f''/f, Ricθθ=0 and scalar curvature2K. The three-dimensional Schouten component Pθθ=−K/2 has no theta connection corrections, giving Cθθx=−K'/2. Conformally Euclidean metrics have zero Cotton tensor. If this component vanished across the full transition, K would be constant and zero from the endpoint neighborhoods; f would be affine and, because constant on a left neighborhood, constant throughout. Endpoint values2 and1 contradict that. The claimed allowed transformations therefore cannot flatten the full supplied block.

The critical-zero calculation also reconstructs exactly. For u=e^{−kx}(cos kθ+cos2kφ) at θ=π/k,φ=0, both u and its gradient vanish while Δu=−3k²e^{−kx}. Multiplying by a smooth nowhere-zero gauge leaves the Laplacian equal to that nonzero residual times the gauge at this point. It cannot solve Δ(gu)=Vgu with bounded scalar V. This concerns gauge-only preservation of the specific state and should not be confused with the operator-coordinate obstruction.

For the exact real two-mode ansatz A(x)coskθ+B(x)cosmφ, when both amplitudes are nonzero, choose two cosine values with A coskθ+B cosmφ=0 and a small nonzero cosmφ. The zero-set compatibility condition gives (A''−k²A)/A=(B''−m²B)/B. It then forces V to be axial wherever the slice state is nonzero and, by angular density and continuity, over the whole slice. When B=0,A≠0, first use coskθ=0,cosmφ=1 to derive B''=0; only afterward conclude axial V. If both amplitudes vanish, independent cosine coefficients force both second derivatives zero. Torus averaging V gives a continuous global axial function v, and the two homogeneous ODEs A''=(k²+v)A, B''=(m²+v)B hold everywhere, including zero slices. Uniqueness excludes switching on an amplitude that vanishes on an incoming interval.

Real amplitudes and independent cosine coordinates are load-bearing. This does not exclude complex mode-engineering or richer real mode families, and it does not give a universal classification of nodal sets. The result is a useful obstruction to copying the source's exact two-real-mode switching blocks with scalar V.
