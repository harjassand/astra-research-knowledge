A. Fixed-G face-regular operator theorem
----------------------------------------
For a fixed compact connected simple root system, arbitrary nonzero dominant
Lambda (including walls) has carrier-specific full-isotypic comparison

  C_W = 72 p^2 C_Lambda r_W c_W /
        [g gamma_(G,I)^2 (n_*+1)^2].

On a supplied face-regular cone n_i>=eta N for active i, the same coherent EB
channel gives the dimension-free-in-carrier PBW-degree-k coefficient

  C_(G,eta)(k)=144 p^2 M_G hvee (1+a_G)^p k^(p+2)/
                 (g gamma_G^2 eta^2).

A factor sqrt(2) is now explicit between basic-metric unit tangents and the
physics-scaled Lie generators used in the half-Casimir/Stinespring identity.
The earlier Section 4 estimate was corrected by root review: convolution
loss (11)-(12) doubles and the face constants quadruple; the Stinespring

faces. Component audits are in partial_flag_geometry.txt,

B. Separate all-wall natural-polynomial Gibbs theorem
----------------------------------------------------
For every dominant carrier, including arbitrary walls, natural states
rho_x=exp(X_x)/Tr exp(X_x) with X_x a fixed-degree polynomial of uniformly
bounded coefficient l1 in Xi_a=T_a/N have one common coherent EB decoder
under an actual compatible covariant broadcaster. The operator norm is
implied by the supplied coefficient and degree bounds as h_pol=A R_G^D,
R_G=max(1,Omega_G/sqrt(2)). The
all-wall coherent first-bias and ordered-polynomial source estimate is
K_src/N; the target comparison costs Gamma_G N^2 (1+k)^sigma,
sigma=max(p-2,0). The result is

  e_EB=O_(G,D,A)(b^(1/4)
          [log(1/b)/loglog(1/b)]^(sigma/4)).

This is a structured bounded-Holevo result (chi<=2h_pol), not the global N93
theorem. The exact shifted moment identity is established in
Leung--Li--Ma, arXiv:2010.14339v1, Theorem 2.4/Eq. (2.5); its cycle6
face/sign/divergence reconstruction and source interpolation were read by
root and independently audited. The source metric is half the basic
root-length-2 metric (long roots squared length 1), matching the physical
generator basis. The all-wall interpolation remains an internal candidate,
not external validation or a priority claim.

Sol's separately audited operator-valued source estimate also gives
D(rho_x||Psi(rho_x))<=C/N^2. By additivity, the same product coherent EB
channel approximates m=o(N^2) independent copies uniformly in trace norm.
This is a source-only archive consequence; its continuous product POVM is
not an efficient learned decoder and it does not strengthen N93.
