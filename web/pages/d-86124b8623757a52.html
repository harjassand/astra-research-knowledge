1. AN EXPLICIT UNIFORM POWER MODULUS ON THE HALF SANDWICH

Then 0<w<=1, and u<=w.

 v(u)<=5sqrt(w).                                         (1.1)
This uses an actual error upper bound u; no optimal broadcaster is acquired.
