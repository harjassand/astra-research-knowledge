ATTACK 1 — CRITICAL POTTS INNOVATION INVERSION (HIGHEST UPSIDE)

Source family 236: The sharp factor-of-IID threshold for the free Ising
model on regular trees, September 26.
https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-sharp-factor-of-IID-threshold-for-the-free-Ising-model-on-regular-trees-September-26-2026/article.pdf
Local source folder has the same title. Read introduction, critical-information
estimates and selected spectral argument; read the final small-field tail,
log-loss and Osgood comparison in full. Main FIID assembly, compatibility,
weighted-path estimates and all formalization dependencies were not audited
in full. Treat the source's exact threshold theorem as an unvalidated import.

Mechanism: observations X_v(t)=t sigma_v+B_v(t) have independent innovations.
Brownian innovations alone do NOT imply X is determined by them. The source
takes two conditional copies sharing the innovations and proves their
discrepancy V=E(h_o-h'_o)^2 is zero. At the Kesten-Stigum point, shell
growth cancels squared edge decay. A t^-1 singular coefficient is repaired
using E|h|^p<=exp(Kp^2)t^(p/4), the resulting exp[-c log^2(1/t)] tail,
and vanishing derivative of log a(h) at zero. It obtains an integrable
Osgood coefficient 1+t^-3/4+1/[t log^2(1/t)].

Independent consequence (full proof in lemma file): if fields have moments
exp(Kp^2)t^(alpha p), the relevant response g satisfies |g'|<=C|h|^a,
and a discrepancy obeys V'<=C[(1+t^-gamma)V+E|g(Y)-g(Y')|^2/t], then
V=0 for gamma<1, alpha*a>0. For an acquired positive initial discrepancy
delta the same estimate gives V(t)<=(eK0)^(1-eta)delta^eta,
eta=exp(-integral k). This supplies a quantitative stability primitive;
it supplies no finite-ball cutoff rate, posterior oracle, or efficient sampler.

Exact Potts diagnosis: on T=1-perp set P=I-11^T/q and
psi(h)=P log[(theta I+(1-theta)11^T/q)exp(h)]. Then
Dpsi(th)=theta I+theta(1-theta)t P diag(h)P+O(t^2).
For q=3,h=(2,-1,-1), the tangent eigenvalues of P diag(h)P are +/-1.
Thus the scalar Ising flatness does not hold in raw Potts coordinates.
Yet F(h)=q[softmax(h)-1/q] satisfies F(psi(h))=theta F(h) exactly.
The pullback metric G=q^2(Dsoftmax)^T(Dsoftmax) gives
Dpsi^T G(psi)Dpsi=theta^2 G(h) exactly. After this repair, additive field
fusion has mixed Hessian P(u elementwise v); at q=3, u=h,
v=(0,1,-1) gives -v. No local coordinate choice can flatten both.

Concrete decisive gate: for symmetric 3- or 4-state Potts at b theta^2=1,
prove a conditional-copy path/noise estimate that cancels this unavoidable
linear fusion-response variation strongly enough to obtain
V'<=k(t)V[1+log(K/V)] with k integrable at zero. A precise sufficient
target is noise variance <=C V[t^-gamma+(1+log(K/V))/(t log^2(1/t))],
gamma<1, under the planted posterior law. Matrix products do not commute;
the cancellation must be valid with the same discrepancy weights, not just
unconditionally centered messages. This is a new missing proof task, not
something N49's reconstruction/capacity criterion settles.

Falsification: the raw scalar replacement fails by exact q=3 algebra;
posterior-coordinate flattening alone fails by the mixed fusion Hessian;
without flatness/cancellation the toy V=t^c disproves singular uniqueness.
Any claim of cheap generation must additionally acquire finite-volume,
finite-time, numerical and bit-cost bounds. The release expressly claims
no finitary coding radius or computational bound.

Prior art: Nam/Sly/Zhang already built the Brownian-innovation FIID method
in a restricted parameter range and posed the full-threshold conjecture.
https://arxiv.org/html/2012.09484v2 (Theorem 1 and Section 2)
The generic Osgood comparison and posterior softmax change are established
types of techniques; novelty of the abstract lemma/coordinate obstruction
is not established. The actual high-upside target is the missing Potts
conditional-copy cancellation, not relabeling stochastic localization.

ATTACK 2 — FULL MIXING FROM COMPLEMENT-CONDITIONED CIRCUIT MARGINALS

Source family 238 companion Random coordinate frames and partial permutation
laws, September 26, Section 5, prop:lift:orbit-sum (read complete proof).
https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Random-coordinate-frames-and-partial-permutation-laws-September-26-2026/build/sections/05-lift.tex

Independent generalization: let any finite G contain an internal product
H1 x ... x Hk. If every complementary coset law is pointwise bounded by
B times uniform, each D-dimensional irrep contracts by
sqrt[(B/D) sum_a sum_{dim pi<=D^(1/k)}(dim pi)^2]. A single vector's
subgroup orbit costs dim(pi)^2 regardless of multiplicity. Aggregate TV
coset error delta can be trimmed once and adds only 2delta to the
operator contraction bound. Plancherel then gives full mixing of convolution
powers once every low-dimensional exceptional representation is controlled.

Concrete attack: structured random linear/symplectic or permutation circuits
with disjoint block subgroups. First establish a complement-conditioned
coset cap with an error uniform over every feasible revealed environment;
then prove the group's truncated local degree sum is o(D) in the relevant
high-dimensional sectors. Treat determinant/sign and other small sectors
separately. This may turn a tractable marginal calculation into a full
random-circuit/design guarantee without inferring full independence.
Both gates are explicit; no such new circuit theorem was proved here.

Exact obstruction checked: uniform A_n has EXACTLY uniform ordered image
laws for every list omitting at least two cards, but stays TV=1/2 from
uniform S_n through every convolution power. Sign remains 1. Thus an
unconditional 'large marginals imply full mixing' pitch is false.
This matches, rather than refutes, the source's separate sign treatment.

Costs: coset laws can have factorial/exponential size; empirical caps,
conditioned environments and group representation data are acquired inputs.
Counting a shuffle/circuit layer does not count random bits, full permutation
materialization or all coefficient work. This source contains twelve
companions; the complete Thorp theorem and all proofs were not certified.
Prior partial-card conditional-energy methods: Hoang/Morris/Rogaway,
https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~rogaway/papers/shuffle.html; the release also
credits Alon/Lovett for group-action uniformity/Fourier repair. The arbitrary
group extension is close to the source proof; first-in-literature is unknown.

ATTACK 3 — ACQUIRED BOUNDARY FLAGS AS A SPECTRAL CERTIFICATE (LOWER PRIORITY)

Source 251, strong-ulam-stability.tex, prop:flags and lem:uniform-list:
https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Strong-Ulam-Stability-Characterizes-Amenability-October-5-2026/build/strong-ulam-stability.tex
Read both flag construction proofs. The full circuit theorem remains a
source claim, partly audited already by W-frontier-groups.

New deduction uses only F2/F3: if F flags are at most beta per departure
row, E flags at most beta per arrival column, then for every finite A,
average_(x,y)|A s(x,y) minus A| >=(1-2beta)|A|. Hence beta<1/2 certifies
nonamenability and a lazy symmetric-walk l2 gap >=(1-2beta)^2/4.
The proof is an exact E-edge count plus the elementary Cheeger inequality.
Stage compatibility F1 is not needed. This is a reusable acquisition target
for a fixed explicit group/boundary, with actual word lengths and list sizes
charged, not a presentation-to-witness algorithm.

Decisive gate: make the source's compact-boundary/Hahn-Banach lists effective
for a specific group of interest and prove their global flag inequalities.
An arbitrary finite group/permutation realization cannot do so: equal total
departure/arrival E counts require beta>=1/2; additive defects obey
delta_out+delta_in>=1-2beta. The action is DECORATED and lacks invariant
probability, so this is not a soficity obstruction. Nor does it produce
finite-dimensional unitary instability: checked prior work supplies nonamenable
higher-rank lattices with finite-dimensional Ulam stability.
https://arxiv.org/html/1010.0565v1
This is a sharpening/consequence of known capital; prioritize 1/2 over it.
