Abstract.

We study 
p

We show that if a set family is large under the product Bernoulli-
p

p

Consequently, we establish two comparison theorems between 
p

p

1. Introduction

Talagrand’s convexity problem asks whether one can create convexity by applying a bounded number of Minkowski sums to a large but otherwise arbitrary set in high-dimensional Gaussian space [10, 11, 13].
In [6], Hua, Song, and Tudose resolved this problem, building on Song’s formulation that the convexity problem is equivalent to a sum-of-Gaussian problem for sub-Gaussian vectors [9].

Talagrand also raised two discrete counterparts of the convexity problem, one integral and one fractional version, replacing Gaussian with Bernoulli random variables and Minkowski sums with unions [10, 11, 12, 13].
This paper settles the fractional version of the discrete convexity problem.
