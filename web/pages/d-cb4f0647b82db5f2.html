## Source and executable interface

Hirai arXiv2608.13972v1 was submitted 14 August 2026 and remains the sole
listed version in the primary history retrieved this cycle. Read all proofs
in Section2 (Lemmas2.1--2.7) and Sections3.1--3.2: the flag apartment oracle, Busemann formula,
projection, dual uncrossing and formulation, rational-step convergence bound
and exact incremental procedure. The source's Theorem2.4 imports the prior
Goodwin--Lewis--Lopez-Acedo--Nicolae Theorem6.4; its rational-step modification
is explicit in Hirai. The prerequisite arXiv2412.06730v4 (10 March 2026;
publisher 4 March 2026) is separately pinned. Read Section6 AssumptionA,
Lemma6.1, Algorithm2, Lemma6.3 and Theorem6.4 with their proofs. No full
49-page reading is claimed.

Input is an explicit basis matrix for each S_j over K, integer costs c_j>=0,
rational caps 0<=u_j<=1, and a chosen sweep horizon N. The stored point is a
complete flag given by a basis V and nonincreasing rational p in[0,cmax]^n.
Lemma2.3 is an ordinary Gaussian-elimination oracle, not a separation or
submodular minimization oracle: obtain reverse-row column echelon L of
V^-1 S_j, replace columns of identity by its normalized pivot columns and
multiply by V. The resulting U=VT has upper triangular T with nonzero
diagonal, so every flag prefix is unchanged. The selected U columns span S_j.

The published iteration is implemented literally. At sweep i starting at0,
use t_i=cmax/[(m+1)ceil(sqrt(i+1))]. Subtract t_i from every p coordinate and
clip below0. For each S_j in the frozen order, align, compute
c_j-sum_(selected) p, and, only if positive, add t_i*u_j to selected weights,
clip above cmax, then sort the weights and corresponding basis columns.
An additional canonical column gauge removes earlier pivot components and
normalizes each new pivot; it preserves every ordered prefix. This is a
legal choice of representation of the same source point, not a height theorem.

After each complete sweep evaluate the FULL objective

    f=sum p_i + sum_j u_j max(0,c_j-sum_(selected j) p_i).

Return its best full-sweep value and corresponding flag, rather than the
last iterate. All retained points give a feasible dual: lambda_i=p_i-p_(i+1)
with p_(n+1)=0, y_j=max(0,c_j-sum_(selected j)p_i). The routine does not
recover a primal packing vector. Known fixture vectors below are independent
correctness certificates and stopping-error controls, not outputs of the
source iteration or an LP substitute.

Hirai's explicit bound is

    min full-sweep f - tau <= 4(1+ln3)(m+1)cmax*n/sqrt(N+2).

The integer-safe constant9 gives a sufficient universal horizon
N>=ceil([9(m+1)cmax*n/epsilon]^2). We freeze a much smaller bounded execution
at512 sweeps and epsilon1/16. An attained fixture gap is certified exactly
against the independently proved optima below; a failure to meet epsilon
is retained as failure, not upgraded by the asymptotic big-O assertion.

## Explicit rational-coordinate denominator

For K0=ceil(sqrt(N)), D_u=lcm denominators of caps, and

    D=(m+1) D_u lcm(1,...,K0),

every source weight throughout the firstN sweeps is a multiple of1/D.
The initial weights are0, each t_i*u_j is such a multiple, and both clipping
endpoints are integers. The implementation stores integer ticks p_i*D;
hinge tests, clipping and sorting are therefore exact. This changes no
source update. A direct bit bound is

    log2 D <= log2(m+1)+sum_j log2 denominator(u_j)+K0 log2 K0.

Weights have numerator at most cmax*D. Full objective denominators divide
D*D_u, since multiplying by a cap can contribute its denominator again.
This sharpens the explicit scalar ledger; it does not control rational flag
matrices. The finite-field
matrix entries have at most log2 p bits on the true-prime event.

## Frozen meaningful family and strongest exact controls

No exhaustive subspace enumeration or LP optimization supplies an iterate.

* triangle_capped: n=3,m=3; consecutive pairs of columns
  (1,1,1),(1,-1,1),(1,2,4),(1,-2,4),(1,3,9),(1,-3,16).
  Costs are(2,2,2), caps(1/3,2/3,5/6), height boundH=5.
  Over Q the three independent plane normals imply every line belongs to
  at most two planes. Thus x=(1/3,1/2,1/2) is feasible: a line contributes
  at most1, an input plane at most2, another2-plane at most3/2, and the whole
  space at most8/3<3. Conversely, the line S2 intersect S3 imposes x2+x3<=1,
  so the capped objective is at most2(1+1/3)=8/3. The value is exactly8/3.
  A dual takes coefficient2 on that line and cap penalty y1=2. This argument
  applies over any prime field where the three normal vectors remain
  independent. F7 is the deliberate negative control: the planes have a
  common line, so sum x<=1, and x=(1/3,2/3,0) attains objective2. The same
  source algorithm is run over Q, the sampled large modulus, and F7.

* double_decomposition4: n=4,m=4; S1,S2 are the two consecutive standard
  coordinate2-planes. S3,S4 are consecutive column pairs of the Vandermonde
  matrix with columns(1,t,t^2,t^3), t=1,2,3,4. Costs are all2, caps are
  (1/3,1/2,2/3,3/4), and H=6. Each pair is a direct decomposition of the
  whole space; the second determinant is12. An integral packing choosing
  either decomposition is feasible for every subspace by directness.
  Their convex combination x=(1/3,1/3,2/3,2/3) is feasible under the caps,
  with objective4. The ambient-space inequality gives upper bound4;
  uniform dual p=(1,1,1,1) attains it. Run the SAME source algorithm over
  Q and the sampled modulus. More generally two decompositions intoq
  2-planes yield this certified arbitrary-n family with n=2q.

These exact primal/dual controls are stronger on this admitted family than
an iterative optimizer. They expose output errors and do not establish a
practical optimizer advantage. Operator-scaling weak/exact BL methods and
CLV/weighted fractional matching remain broader strong comparators; they
are not silently replaced by different timed implementations.

## Actual prime acquisition and probability charge

Use the cycle16 explicit bad-prime count B separately for the two DISTINCT
subspace inputs, then sum their bounds. Changing caps/costs or adding theF7
negative control does not require additional events. This single sample
protects both frozen rational polytopes on the simultaneous good-prime event.

With total delta=1/100, set q=ceil(3/delta),
L=4[bit_length(B)+bit_length(q)+20], T=2L bit_length(q), and choose the
smallest s with4^s>=Tq. Each candidate is a uniform oddL-bit integer.
Reject an explicitly found small divisor, or use s independent strong
Miller--Rabin bases uniformly in{2,...,n-2}, accepting the first survivor.
Return UNKNOWN if T candidates are exhausted; no silent resampling.

Couple this stream to exact first-prime sampling. True primes always pass.
The ideal stream times out with probability<=1/q. Its first prime is bad
with probability<=1/q. Any false composite acceptance has probability
<=T*4^-s<=1/q. On the complement the practical and ideal outputs coincide;
total failure is<=delta. The accepted probable-prime distribution is NOT
claimed exactly uniform on primes. No deterministic prime certificate is
acquired. A noninvertible matrix operation or input rank loss yields failure,
not a repaired modulus.

Exact base-range generation uses random-bit rejection, expected at most two
draws per base; total draws and bits are logged. The bit-time guarantee of
this implementation is EXPECTED polynomial. The outer candidate/MR work is
bounded, but an uncapped uniform base primitive is not worst-case bounded.
Random-bit independence/uniformity is the standard model for OS entropy;
the run does not certify a physical entropy source. The CRT quarter-liar
theorem is independently reconstructed in the audit, including the chosen
base range. Source access failures are retained rather than hidden.

## Execution and evidence contract

FROZEN_PROTOCOL.json and the code hashes are saved before sampling or an
iteration run. Each field run uses the same subspaces, costs, caps, sweep
horizon, source order, step rule, tie sort and canonical gauge. Field-dependent
nonzero pivots are interpreted in the appropriate exact field. Q uses Python
Fraction; Fp uses residue arithmetic with modular inverses. No floating
optimization or approximate Gaussian pivoting is used.

Save every before/after source microstep, exact rational weights, full-sweep
objectives, best flag, field-operation counts, maximum operand/result bit
lengths, common coordinate denominator, wall/CPU times and process peak RSS.
The sampler saves every rejected candidate, small-divisor or MR witness,
accepted bases and entropy counts. Code/protocol/trace hashes are retained.
The independent hessian_check12 worker audits steps using the basis-free
weighted partial-filtration formula, not the producer alignment code.
stat_exec15 independently audits source/output/cost and the prime coupling.
Finite runs remain finite evidence, and no historical novelty is asserted.

Whole-polytope equality does not imply equality of individual Q/Fp intermediate
flags, incidence profiles or trajectories. Matching means the same legal source
procedure and declared inputs; trace agreement, if observed, is checked separately.
