The construction handles zero coordinates in the index type by the same general theorem and does not discretize or truncate exponents.

`reactionEnvelope_derivative_nonneg_ae` composes the pointwise weighted-drift estimate with the c10 finite-minimum AC chain rule along the actual vector path and its a.e. ODE identity. It covers every weakly active branch, including ties. `reactionEnvelope_derivative_ge_margin_below_ae` strengthens this to the uniform positive margin below the representative level inside the fixed class, using the nonperpendicular-slope lemma.

`reactionEnvelope_enters_plateau_by_range` is the one-scale finite-entry theorem. Define

\[
\delta=\kappa\,\mathrm{slopeMargin}(D,F)\,h^{\mathrm{sourceBudget}(D)},\qquad
m_0=\min_j\bigl(b_j(h)-\|s_j\|_1h^{-1}\bigr),\qquad
T=a+\frac{\mathcal E_h(c)-m_0}{\delta}.
\]

The theorem proves `∃ t ∈ [a,T], ReactionPlateau F h c (x t)` for an already-given AC path `x` on `[a,T]`, provided the explicit small-scale inequality `K * reactionBudget * h^slopeMargin ≤ κ * slopeMargin` holds, the representative lies in the positive scale box, its envelope is a global ceiling, and the entire path is supplied to remain in that box and the same stoichiometric class on the interval. Rate bounds and the coordinatewise ODE identity are required almost everywhere. The floor lemma proves `m₀ ≤ envelope(x(t))` throughout that box; the range/time estimate then forces contact with the ceiling. Thus the entry time is path/initial-state dependent, not claimed uniform over the class.

5. Compose one-scale entry and invariance across the source's scale sequence (including its common classwise absorber). No common absorber or global-in-time trajectory theorem has been proved.
