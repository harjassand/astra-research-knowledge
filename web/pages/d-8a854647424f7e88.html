RESULT AND LEGAL INTERFACE

Input is an explicit n x n matrix F over Q(i), total bit length L, an integer
0 <= k <= n/2, and rational error/confidence parameters. Let

 W_k(A,B,E) = sum_{|I|=|J|=k, I intersect J=empty,
                    A subset I, B subset J, (I union J) intersect E=empty}
                    |det F[I,J]|^2,

where A,B,E are disjoint site sets. There is an acquired, nonnegative unbiased
per-sign estimator for every physical prefix mass W_k(A,B,E), not merely a
row marginal. With a=|A|, b=|B|, its pointwise and second-moment bounds are

  0 <= X <= C W,  E X = W,  E X^2 <= C W^2,
  C = binom(2k-a-b,k-a).

Impossible counts give exact zero. If C=1, every draw equals W exactly.
Each draw uses at most 2^b(2a+1) polynomial-bit matrix-coefficient operations.
Because 2^b C <= binom(2k,k), all physical prefixes admit relative randomized
approximation in binom(2k,k) poly(n,k,L,eta^-1,log(delta^-1)) bit time.

Sequential self-reduction therefore gives an acquired TV-epsilon sampler for
Pr[I,J]=|det F[I,J]|^2/c_k(F), under the promise c_k(F)>0, in
binom(2k,k) poly(n,k,L,epsilon^-1,log(n/epsilon)) bit time. The implementation
has deterministic sample/random-bit caps. It returns a supported pair (I,J),
or FAIL; FAIL has target mass zero and is charged within the TV guarantee.
Every successful output is supported even when an estimator confidence event
fails. No zero-support oracle, normalizer, supplied cut ordering, annealing
kernel or exact sampler is assumed. c_k=0 is exactly zero on every estimator
draw, but observing estimator output zero on a positive instance is possible
with probability at most delta, so it is not an exact-zero certificate.

For k=O(log n) both relative counting and approximate classical Born sampling
are polynomial. For k=Theta(n) the central-binomial factor remains exponential.
This does not close the unrestricted canonical-sector FPRAS or energy gate,
nor furnish coherent state preparation, approximation hardness or novelty.

1. PFAFFIAN FOURIER EXPANSION, INCLUDING EXACT SIGNS

Let u_i=e_i, v_i=(row_i F)^T, and order paired columns as
(u_1,v_1,u_2,v_2,...). For real activities x_i set

 K(x) = sum_i x_i(u_i v_i^T - v_i u_i^T)
      = diag(x) F - F^T diag(x).

Expanding the exterior power of sum_i x_i u_i wedge v_i gives, on an ordered
ambient subset R of size 2k,

 Pf K(x)[R] = sum_{I subset R, |I|=k} d_{R,I} product_{i in I} x_i,
 d_{R,I}=det V[R,P(I)].

Terms with I outside R vanish because an identity column is zero there.
Expanding the identity columns shows d_{R,I}=signed det F[I,R\I]. The signs
are irrelevant after Parseval, but are retained in the per-sign amplitude.
No positivity of F, determinants or Pfaffian coefficients is assumed.

To force up sites A, set their activities to a common real scalar z; force
down sites B to activity zero; keep independent Rademacher activities on free
sites; delete E from the ambient indices. The coefficient of z^a in each
Pfaffian consists exactly of row sets I containing A and avoiding B. All such
I have k-a free sites, so it is a homogeneous multilinear Fourier polynomial.
Further retain only ambient R containing B. Thus define

 X(s) = sum_{R subset [n]\E, |R|=2k, A union B subset R}
         |sum_{I subset R, |I|=k, A subset I, I intersect B=empty}
                  d_{R,I} product_{i in I\A} s_i|^2.

This manifestly nonnegative expression is an identity, not the exponential
production algorithm. Orthogonality of distinct sign characters yields E X=W.
For each R there are at most C=binom(2k-a-b,k-a) terms. Cauchy-Schwarz gives
the squared amplitude <= C sum_I |d_{R,I}|^2 for every sign word. Summing R
proves X<=CW; multiplying by X and averaging gives E X^2<=CW^2. If W=0,
all coefficients vanish, so all sign words give exactly zero. If C=1, X<=W
and E X=W on a finite full-support sign space imply X=W for every word.

2. POLYNOMIAL COEFFICIENT ENGINE, WITHOUT PRINCIPAL-MINOR ENUMERATION

For any complex skew matrix K, define
 H_k(K)=sum_{|R|=2k}|Pf K[R]|^2.
Then H_k is [t^k] sqrt(det(I+t K* K)), the square root with constant term one.
One standard proof uses unitary congruence into 2x2 skew blocks and invariance
of the exterior-power norm. The classical complex skew normal form is in
Youla (1961), introduction equations (2)-(3); its primary indexed PDF introduction was retrieved; direct PDF opening failed.
An alternative entirely algebraic proof is the block-Pfaffian identity

 (-1)^{n(n-1)/2} Pf [[tK,I],[-I,-conjugate K]]
     = sum_{R even} t^{|R|/2} Pf K[R] conjugate(Pf K[R]).

The off-diagonal identity block forces the same internally matched subset R
in the upper and lower blocks. For |R|=2k, move upper R, lower R, upper R^c,
lower R^c into that order. The upper/lower within-block permutation signs
occur twice, and moving 2k lower sites past n-2k upper sites is even. The
remaining identity-block Pfaffian has sign (-1)^((n-2k)(n-2k-1)/2); the lower
internal Pfaffian contributes (-1)^k. Their product equals the fixed sign
(-1)^(n(n-1)/2), since the exponent difference is 2k(n-k-1). Squaring this block Pfaffian and
swapping/eliminating the identity block gives det(I-t conjugate K K), which
is det(I+t K* K) because K*=-conjugate K. This proves the formal-square-root
identity without numerical singular values. The direct-Pfaffian comparator
checks the identity independently on the finite diagnostic fixtures.

Production uses B=K* K and traces p_j=tr(B^j), j=1,...,k. For q(t)=sqrt det(I+tB),
formal differentiation gives

  q'/q = (1/2) tr(B(I+tB)^-1),
  q_0=1,
  q_d = (1/(2d)) sum_{j=1}^d (-1)^{j-1} p_j q_{d-j}.

Hence O(k n^3+k^2) Gaussian-integer operations compute H_k after common-
denominator clearing. No determinant coefficient or square-root oracle is
used. Although the trace recursion has cancellation, all divisions are exact
for cleared input, and the resulting q_d are nonnegative integers.

3. PHYSICAL PREFIXES BY TOP COEFFICIENT AND AMBIENT INCLUSION-EXCLUSION

For T subset B, retain ambient [n]\(E union T), with the activities described
in section 1. Each Pfaffian has degree at most a in z, so its squared norm
polynomial H_k has degree at most 2a. Its top z^{2a} coefficient equals the
sum of the squared z^a Pfaffian coefficients. The exact finite-difference
formula for a degree-2a polynomial P is

 [z^{2a}]P(z) = (1/(2a)!) sum_{z=0}^{2a}
                  (-1)^{2a-z} binom(2a,z) P(z).

Inclusion-exclusion sum_{T subset B}(-1)^{|T|} retains precisely the ambient
sets R containing all forced down sites B. The resulting value is exactly the
manifestly nonnegative X(s) in section 1. Cancellation in this computational
formula cannot introduce relative precision error because arithmetic is exact.
There are at most 2^b(2a+1) coefficient calls per draw. Deleting many empty
sites only decreases matrix dimension; their number does not increase the
exponential parameter.

If a,b<=k, first remove the a forced-up requirements from the central binomial:

 binom(2k-a-b,k-a) / binom(2k-b,k)
      = product_{h=0}^{a-1} (k-h)/(2k-b-h) <= 1.

Then
 binom(2k-b,k) / binom(2k,k)
      = product_{h=0}^{b-1} (k-h)/(2k-h) <= 2^-b.

Consequently 2^b C <= binom(2k,k). This is the reason prefix constraints do
not multiply the final cost by an additional unconditional 2^k factor.

4. RELATIVE ESTIMATION WITH A DETERMINISTIC CAP

For W>0, normalize each draw Y=X/(CW). Then 0<=Y<=1 and E Y=1/C. Convexity
of exp(theta y) on [0,1] gives its Bernoulli-chord bound. Multiplying that
bound over independent draws and optimizing theta gives, for 0<eta<=1/2,

 Pr[|mean X-W|>eta W] <= 2 exp(-m eta^2/(3C)).

The familiar estimates (1+eta)log(1+eta)-eta>=eta^2/3 and
eta+(1-eta)log(1-eta)>=eta^2/2 establish the displayed two tails.
The implementation takes

 m=ceil(3 C eta^-2 ceil(log_2(2/delta))).

Since log_2 r >= log r for r>=1, this ensures failure probability <=delta.
An exact mean of m rational samples is the output. It is always nonnegative
and gives true zero exactly. There are no unbounded rejection loops.

If the complete free-sign space has size at most m, the optional enumeration
computes its exact mean instead, with no greater coefficient cost and no
confidence failure. A private cache only memoizes the deterministic draw
value for repeated sign words; all m independent words are still generated
and charged. The cache has at most m entries, so it does not violate the bound.
C=1 uses a single exact draw. Tests also disable enumeration to exercise the
complete capped randomized path. Supplying a seeded Python RNG is a diagnostic;
SystemRandom is the default randomness interface. The mathematical guarantee
is under independent fair-bit access, not a claim about empirical seed quality.

5. FINITE-BIT PHYSICAL SAMPLER AND TV PROOF

Scan all n sites. At a positive current prefix, estimate its three child masses
(up,down,empty) with eta=epsilon/(8n), delta=epsilon/(12n). Normalize their
nonnegative estimates. Given simultaneous relative success, each positive
child has estimate between (1-eta)W_j and (1+eta)W_j. If p,p' are exact and
estimated normalized child laws, then

 TV(p,p') <= eta/(1-eta).

Indeed, if e_j=estimate_j/W_j-1 and e=sum p_j e_j, then
p'_j-p_j=p_j(e_j-e)/(1+e), and sum_j p_j|e_j-e|<=2eta.
Zero children have estimate zero on every sign draw.

Let Q=2^ceil(log_2(8n/epsilon)). Draw a uniform integer in [0,Q), compare it
with the floors of Q times the two cumulative estimated probabilities. Each
cumulative error is <1/Q, so categorical TV error is <=2/Q. Equal endpoints
for a zero child remain equal, including trailing zero children; therefore
rounding never introduces a zero-estimated branch. Every selected branch has
strictly positive estimate, hence true positive mass. Induction ensures that
any completed word has positive determinant-squared weight. All-zero estimates
return FAIL, never an unsupported completed word.

For adaptive FPRAS calls, conditioning on the current prefix still leaves
fresh independent sign words, so each conditional failure probability is
<=delta. At most 3n calls imply total bad-estimation probability <=3n delta
=epsilon/4. Couple the ideal and implemented prefix choices at each step;
first divergence is bounded by the sum of conditional TV errors. On good
estimation events, a positive parent has at least one positive child estimate
and cannot return FAIL. Thus, on the output space enlarged by FAIL of target
mass zero,

 TV(output,target) <= 3n delta + n eta/(1-eta) + 2n/Q
                   <= epsilon/4 + epsilon/7 + epsilon/4
                    = 9 epsilon/14 < epsilon.

Here eta<=1/8 makes 1/(1-eta)<=8/7. This charges abort, branch approximation
and dyadic rounding separately. No unknown small prefix probability is divided
by or sampled using rejection. At zero total norm the normalized law is
undefined; the promise c_k>0 is explicit. The zero-instance diagnostic returns
FAIL but does not turn a positive-instance estimator zero into a certificate.

6. BIT COMPLEXITY AND CHARGED COSTS

Acquire a common denominator D of all real/imaginary input entries, bit length
at most O(L), and G=D F over Gaussian integers. Every coefficient call is on a
submatrix of diag(x)G-G^Tdiag(x), with |x_i|<=2k. Entry heights are O(L+log k).
Matrix powers through degree k and their traces have height
O(k(L+log n+log k)); their number of operations is polynomial. Pfaffian norms
have denominators D^{2k} before clearing, with integer numerator height
O(k(L+log n+log k)). Finite differences add O(k log k) bits; summing at most
2^b terms adds O(k) bits. Factorial divisions are exact. A mean of m samples
adds only log m bits. The compiler never forms an LCM of exponentially many
unrelated sample denominators: all share D^{2k}. Every cost remains polynomial
in L, k and logarithms of the requested accuracy/confidence.

An estimator call has at most O(C eta^-2 log(delta^-1)) draws, each costing
O(2^b k^2 n^3) Gaussian-integer operations by the conservative trace bound.
Thus it costs O(binom(2k,k) k^2 n^3 eta^-2 log(delta^-1)) operations on
polynomial-bit operands. The complete 3n-child sampler consequently costs

 O(binom(2k,k) k^2 n^6 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon))

Gaussian-integer operations, plus at most O(binom(2k,k) n^4 epsilon^-2
log(n/epsilon)) sign random bits and n ceil(log_2(8n/epsilon)) categorical bits.
Use the polynomial bit cost of those integer operations for total bit time.
The k=0 case returns the vacuum in constant scientific work after input
validation. The bounds are intentionally conservative, not a hardware claim.

7. EXECUTABLE EVIDENCE AND LIMITS

revisions/low_sector_sampler.py is standalone, standard-library code. Production
coefficient calculations never enumerate minors. revisions/check_low_sector.py
uses separate small Leibniz determinants and recursive principal Pfaffians as
comparators. Recorded run: 16,536 exact assertions, 156 exhaustive cases,
1,072 sign words, all passed. It checks all physical prefixes on a generic
four-site matrix, noncontiguous forced sets, rational-complex denominator 30,
exact tiny weights at L=8,64,256, independent swap blocks through k=4, and
combinatorial prefactor inequalities through k=30. Independent swap blocks
have exact relative second moment 2^k, preserving an exponential worst-case
variance witness at large k rather than pretending universal low variance.

It also executes 12 full-cap non-enumerating relative count calls, a zero-norm
call and zero-instance FAIL, and 52 complete non-enumerating generic Born
sampler runs (n,k)=(3,1),(4,2),(5,2). All returned supported configurations.
The three sampler blocks charged 11,457,888 sign draws and 34,504,728 sign
bits, with exact deterministic value caching. Runtime of the recorded finite
script was about 1.01 seconds; shared CPU contention/backend work/tokens/energy
are UNKNOWN, and that scoped time is not an asymptotic or independent hardware
benchmark. A comparator initially expected a cleared tiny-weight numerator
of one instead of D^2; that assertion was repaired, documented in
LOW_K_REPAIRS.json, and the production code was unchanged.

Finite tests corroborate algebra and implementation only. No formal proof
