Central completed theorem
-------------------------
If a group has an injective rank-metric ultraproduct model over finite fields
of one fixed characteristic p (degrees may vary), its group algebra is stably
finite over every field of characteristic p. Proof: extend each coordinate
field to contain an arbitrary finite F_{p^d}, restrict scalars while
preserving normalized rank, apply AP Theorem 7.4 and Proposition 2.8, then
specialize any hypothetical matrix one-sided inverse over an arbitrary field
to a finite extension using a polynomial system plus Zariski's lemma.

Central open gate
-----------------
Construct a rank model of fixed characteristic p from a complex linear-sofic
or hyperlinear input, or find another positive-characteristic detector. AP
Theorem 8.2 supplies finite fields with unprescribed characteristics; its
2012 Question 8.6 asks for a prescribed finite field. This package makes no
claim about current global status.

Adversarial evidence
--------------------
* Any fixed complex finite-stage rank-minor model is p-safe for all but
  finitely many p, but the bad finite set may vary with the stage.
* The exact rational model of Z with D_k=diag(P_k,...,P_k^k), P_k the product
  of the first k primes, inverts every fixed prime at all sufficiently late
  stages. This falsifies automatic reduction of a chosen model only; Z has
  other fixed-characteristic models.
* One local r=s=t=2 N65 wiring block has an exact GL_D(F_q) solution over odd
  q with W=-I_D for every rectangle. Thus local triangle wiring alone does
  not force rectangle rank loss. The global shared-X and interpolation
  constraints remain the relevant bottleneck.
