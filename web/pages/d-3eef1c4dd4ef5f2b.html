1. EXACT CLASS, RADIUS AND DISPLAYED-VERSUS-OPTIMAL ERROR

Let d>=2,J>=1 be integers, N_j=2^j (j=0,...,J), D_j=binom(N_j+d-1,N_j).
Let p_j>=0 sum to1 and 0<w<=1 be supplied rational probabilities. On
 H=C|vac> directsum directsum_j Sym^(N_j)(C^d)
use the family
 rho_psi=(1-w)vac+w directsum_j p_j P_(psi,N_j).
Use all Haar directions or a fixed exact weighted projective design of
degree2N_J; finite algebraic designs are specified in our frozen Cycle04
quadrature proof. The SAME design prior matches every pair moment, not
only the block means. For w=1 the vacuum block has zero promised weight
but remains part of the channel's full input domain.

Use T(rho,nu)=||rho-nu||_1/2. Let e(E) be the infimum over ONE common
full-domain EB channel of the worst T error on E, and b2(E) the analogous
infimum over full-domain CPTP two-output broadcasters of the worst error
over both marginals. Define R(E) by the infimum over sigma in closed
conv(E) of the supremum over rho in E and binary effects0<=F<=I of
p ln(p/q), p=Tr(F rho),q=Tr(F sigma), with the usual zero/infinite
conventions. Logs are natural; all channel input matrices are admitted.

Write p_*=max_j p_j and delta=w p_*.
Define a restricted class of full-domain CPTP broadcasters as follows:
vacuum is mapped to vacuum on both outputs; on input flag j, EACH output
is supported in vacuum or an active flag k<j. This support condition may
allow arbitrary correlations and states. It excludes same/higher-flag
cloning and does not describe every legal quantum broadcaster.
Let b_div be the infimum HALF worst marginal error within this class.

Theorem for this class:

 b_div=delta=w p_* EXACTLY.                          (1)
 e(E)<=min{1,3delta ln[12(R(E)+1)/delta]}.            (2)
For every fixed alpha in(0,2),
 e(E)^2 <=K_alpha(R(E)+1)b_div^alpha,
 K_alpha=36*12^(2-alpha)/(2-alpha)^2<infinity.        (3)

The exponent2 endpoint fails already for the UNPERTURBED finite
vacuum-qubit member w=1/K,p_j=1/K,d=2 in the separate frozen proof.
Its actual b2 is comparable to b_div. That proof ALSO makes a faithful
version by adding identity noise, but this restricted class calculation
is asserted for the unperturbed promises. On bounded-R division families,
(2)'s logarithmic order is attained up to constants by this same member.

For an arbitrary legal broadcaster only b2<=b_div is established. Replacing
b_div by b2 in (2)--(3) would assert a missing theorem. The distinction is
the precise surviving endpoint, not an overlooked quantifier.

2. AN EXACT OPTIMAL QUANTILE COUPLING FOR NONUNIFORM WEIGHTS

Let P_r=sum_(j>=r)p_j, with P_(J+1)=0. Define desired child tails
 Q_r=max{0,P_r-p_*}, r=0,...,J+1,
 q_j=Q_j-Q_(j+1), q_vac=p_*.
Then q_j>=0,q_j<=p_j, sum_j q_j=1-p_*, and
 Q_r<=P_r-p_r=P_(r+1).
Thus the child flag law is stochastically below the parent flag shifted
down one step. An explicit monotone quantile coupling gives child k<j:
partition [0,1] into parent intervals with lengths p_j and child intervals
with lengths q_vac,q_j in increasing flag order. The length of each
intersection gives its joint probability. The tail inequality implies
that a nonzero intersection has child k<=j-1. All endpoints and conditional
probabilities are rational supplied data (zero-weight parents are ignored).

For active parent j and child k>=0, group the input into two N_k-copy
blocks and a remaining N_j-2N_k block. Grouping Sym^(N_j) is an isometry
into the product of the corresponding symmetric spaces. Trace out the
remainder; the resulting map is CPTP on ALL input matrices. On promised
P_(psi,N_j) it returns P_(psi,N_k) tensor P_(psi,N_k) exactly. If child
is vacuum, replace by vac tensor vac. Pinch parent flags and mix these
maps with the rational conditional coupling probabilities; copy vacuum.
This constructs ONE full-domain CPTP B in the required class.

Its marginal law on promised states has active weights w q_j and vacuum
weight1-w+w p_*. Since q_j<=p_j, all active discrepancy blocks are negative
multiples of their promised ray, and the vacuum discrepancy is positive
of trace w p_*. Its HALF error is EXACTLY w p_*.

Conversely ANY broadcaster in this class, even with label-dependent
quantum transition probabilities and arbitrary output correlations,
has output probability in active tail r at most w P_(r+1). The target
tail probability is w P_r. The binary tail-flag measurement therefore
gives marginal HALF error at least w p_r for each r. Maximizing r proves
b_div>=w p_* and completes (1). The full-domain lower restriction and
the explicitly attained error are separate statements.

The naive adjacent splitter has error
 w[p_0+sum_(j=0)^J |p_(j+1)-p_j|]/2, p_(J+1)=0,
which can be much larger than delta for oscillating weights. Optimizing
that adjacent total variation alone would miss the quantile broadcaster.
We therefore stop the naive adjacent-scan branch and use exact optimum(1).

3. EXACT CONVEX OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS

For the normalized active family define
 sigma_* =directsum_j p_j I_j/D_j.
Its finite design mean is sigma_*, so it is an admissible convex center.
At this center arbitrary effects reduce exactly to numbers0<=f_j<=1:
 p_test=sum_j p_j f_j,
 q_test>=sum_j p_j f_j/D_j,
with equality attained by F_j=f_j P_(psi,N_j). Joint convexity of
g(p,q)=p ln(p/q) implies that a maximum over this cube occurs at a vertex.
A positive maximizing vertex cannot select a lower j while excluding a
higher k with positive weight: moving a small amount of selected rho mass
from j to k preserves p_test and strictly lowers q_test, increasing g.
Hence a maximizing selected set is a tail of the positive-weight flags.

For any r with P_r>0 put Q'_r=sum_(j>=r)p_j/D_j. This gives the exact
centered worst divergence
 max_r P_r ln(P_r/Q'_r).
It is also the convex minimax radius: ANY convex center has flag traces
p_j, and averaging the tail projector effect over the SAME design prior
gives q mean Q'_r, while p_test=P_r is constant. Jensen for -ln q makes
some promised-state divergence at least P_r ln(P_r/Q'_r), for every r.
Thus the center upper and arbitrary-center lower agree. Vacuum dilation
scales observational divergence exactly, as proved by joint convexity
and active-only effects in VACUUM_FLAG_POWER_OBSTRUCTION, Section8.
Consequently

 R(E)=w max_r P_r ln[P_r/(sum_(j>=r)p_j/D_j)].        (4)
 Cchi(E)=w sum_j p_j ln D_j EXACTLY.                 (5)

These are max-prior/convex-minimax quantities, not a selected-prior proxy.
Zero weights simply delete terms; singular alternative centers with a

5. EXPONENTIAL RADIUS OBSTRUCTION FOR A FIXED EB DEFECT

Assume p_*<=1/2. Set r=floor(1/(2p_*)). The first r flags have mass
at most r p_*<=1/2, so P_r>=1/2. Necessarily r<=J from normalization.
Since every j>=r has N_j>=2^r, (4) implies

 R(E)>= (w/2)ln binom(2^r+d-1,2^r)
      >= (w ln2/2) min{2^r,d-1}.                    (7)

The binomial bound follows by taking the smaller of2^r,d-1 as the
chosen index: the upper binomial index is at least twice that number,
and each product ratio is at least2.

If e(E)>0, choose h=floor(e/(2delta)). Since e<=w, h<=r.
In (6), h delta<=e/2, hence
 d-1>=e 2^h/(2w).

 R(E)>= (e ln2/4)2^h
      >= (e ln2/8)2^[e/(2delta)].                   (8)

The e=0 case is trivial. A constant EB defect and small delta therefore
force a radius exponential in1/delta. Nonuniform weights cannot remove

6. LOGARITHMIC RATE AND ALL EXPONENTS BELOW2 IN THIS CLASS

If p_*>1/2, e<=w<2delta, which already implies (2).
Otherwise if e<=delta, (2) is immediate. For e>delta, (8) implies

   <=3delta ln[12(R+1)/delta],
using ln2>=2/3 and positivity of the logarithm ensured by (8).
This proves (2) for all profiles, with the e<=1 bound also available.

For a=R+1>=1 and c=12/delta>=12>exp(2), the function
ln(ca)^2/a decreases for a>=1, since its derivative has sign
2ln(ca)-ln(ca)^2<0. Thus (2) implies

 e^2<=9(R+1)delta^2 ln(12/delta)^2.                 (9)

Let beta=2-alpha>0 and t=ln(12/delta). Then
 delta^beta ln(12/delta)^2
    =12^beta exp(-beta t)t^2
    <=4*12^beta/beta^2.

For fixed bounded R, (2) is an O[delta ln(1/delta)] modulus, and the
vacuum-qubit family w=1/K,p_j=1/K has exactly this order. Consequently
the logarithmic loss at exponent2 is necessary WITHIN this class.
