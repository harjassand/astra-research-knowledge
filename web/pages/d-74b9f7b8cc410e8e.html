# Uniformly smooth missed-mode obstruction to Fisher-information interpretation

Status: complete elementary derivation, with numerical formula checks. This is a sharp diagnostic construction, not a new resolution of nonconvex sampling complexity.

## Exact question and interface

Do the improved nonconvex Fisher-information guarantees in [Chewi–Wibisono, 2605.15859v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15859v1) and [Chen et al., 2608.05022v1, Section 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.05022v1#S6) imply globally accurate sampling, or accurate expectations, when only smoothness and bounded initial divergence are supplied?

The authors describe Fisher information as first-order stationarity and do not claim this implication. The construction below rejects a possible downstream overinterpretation. The source's reduction needs high-accuracy RGO subroutines and a proximal oracle; the August improvement gives d^(1/3) rather than d^(1/2) dependence, with proximal calls separately charged. Small FI is the target metric.

## Construction and complete calculation

For a≥1 let

    pi_a = ½ N(a,a)+½ N(-a,a),  mu_a=N(a,a),
    pi_a(x)=exp(-a/2)/sqrt(2 pi a) * exp(-x²/(2a)) cosh x.

Up to an additive normalizing constant, V_a(x)=x²/(2a)-log cosh x. Therefore

    V'_a(x)=x/a-tanh x,
    V''_a(x)=1/a-sech² x ∈[1/a-1,1/a].

Thus |V''_a|≤1 uniformly: this is not caused by worsening the supplied smoothness constant. Both densities are smooth, positive, and normalizable. Also mu_a/pi_a≤2, hence every forward Rényi divergence R_q(mu_a||pi_a)≤log 2 and KL≤log 2. Initialization is uniformly warm even in R_infty.

The relative score is

    (log mu_a-log pi_a)'=1-tanh x.

Set c_a=exp(-a/2)/sqrt(2 pi a). Its FI is

    FI(mu_a||pi_a)
      =c_a ∫ exp(-x²/(2a)) exp(x)(1-tanh x)² dx
      =c_a ∫ exp(-x²/(2a)) sech x dx
      ≤c_a pi=:B_a=sqrt(pi/(2a)) exp(-a/2).          (F1)

The second equality uses exp(x)(1-tanh x)²=exp(-x)sech² x and symmetrizes the integral: the odd sinh term integrates to zero. The integral ∫sech x dx=pi. Dominated convergence implies FI/B_a→1, so the bound gives the exact exponential and polynomial scale.

For TV, pi_a is an equal mixture, so

    TV(mu_a,pi_a)=½ TV(N(a,a),N(-a,a))
                 =Phi(sqrt(a))-½ →½.                (F2)

For the 1-Lipschitz observable f(x)=x,

    E_mu_a f-E_pi_a f=a →infinity.                 (F3)

Thus no modulus TV≤g(FI), g(t)→0, or expectation bias≤g(FI), can hold uniformly with only d=1, β=1, and forward initial Rényi/KL bounded by log 2.

## Isoperimetry identifies the omitted resource

The odd bounded test f(x)=tanh x satisfies

    Var_pi_a f=1-E_pi_a sech² x=1-FI(mu_a||pi_a),
    E_pi_a |f'|²=c_a∫exp(-x²/(2a)) sech³ x dx
                      ≤c_a pi/2=B_a/2.

The Poincaré Rayleigh quotient therefore gives the rigorous lower bound

    C_PI(pi_a) ≥2/B_a-2
      =2 sqrt(2a/pi) exp(a/2)-2.                   (F4)

The test is not compactly supported, but smooth cutoffs converge in the variance and Dirichlet terms because the measure has Gaussian tails. LSI constant is at least C_PI. Hence conversion through LSI (KL≤C_LSI FI/2) can cost exponentially in a. That missing mode cost explains the coexistence of extremely small FI and poor global accuracy; it does not contradict the sources' stationarity theorem.

Numerical check at a=64: FI≈1.94749e-15, TV≈0.5, mean bias=64, and the tanh Rayleigh quotient≈1.01172e15. These are checks of the explicit formulas, not evidence of a universal theorem. See `results/r10_probability/checks.json`.

## Prior art, novelty, use, falsifiable next step

Fisher information as a sampling stationarity criterion was developed in Balasubramanian et al., [arXiv:2202.05214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05214). The distinction from global sampling and mode-weight failure of score metrics is classical. The present choice of variance equal to mean separation gives a convenient fixed-β and uniformly warm explicit family with sharp FI and Poincaré exponents. Novelty of this exact parameterization has not been established; do not advertise the general distinction as a discovery.

Use this family to test any future claim converting local score stationarity into downstream global posterior expectations. A discriminating experiment must measure mode mass, the mean, FI, and the Rayleigh quotient separately. A claimed improvement in FI alone passes the stationarity task but fails the global task. Future positive results should supply an isoperimetric certificate, a mode-mass query interface, or assumptions that eliminate this family. Unexplored: a controlled reduction adding explicit mode-weight estimation to within-mode FI convergence, with all mode-discovery costs charged.
