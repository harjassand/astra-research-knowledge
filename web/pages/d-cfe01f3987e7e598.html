C01_S03 -- REUSE ONLY N ZERO-FIELD IID COMPILER CALLS
7 October2026 UTC. Refinement of AXIAL_FINITE_BIT_COMPILER.txt.
Root observed c03_s01's exact polynomial quadrature tilting and suggested
reusing one zero-field rule per k. The reduction below works for the already
audited generic rational iid compiler too, including MIXED one-qubit atoms.
It does not depend on a fresh quadrature-stability theorem.

Keep every input, cutoff, isotropic LP and outer branch weight of the main
proof, with the currently internally audited delta<=1 existence import.
The large-|h| shortcut is performed first. Thus |h|<delta+q and the admitted
delta is bounded by1. Put

 Hcap=|h|+delta, B=ceil(2 N Hcap), eta=eps 2^(-B-10).             (R1)

Every branch field h'=h-2delta m/N satisfies |h'|<=Hcap.
For each k=1,...,N acquire the zero-field symmetric target

 C_k^0= sum_l exp[-delta(l-k/2)^2/N]|D_kl><D_kl| / Z_k^0.       (R2)

It belongs to the iid cone by I1 at delta'=delta k/N<=1. Let
C0_k=delta k^2/(4N), s0_k=ceil(2C0_k). The raw exponential values lie between
2^-s0_k and1. Acquiring each with absolute dyadic error
tau0=eta 2^(-s0_k-4) and then normalizing gives a PSD rational orbit target
R_k with T(R_k,C_k^0)<=eta/16. Use the main proof's exact Dicke orbit formula.
Apply I2 at error eta/2. Its rational iid mixture
X_k=sum_j w_kj sigma_kj^tensor k then obeys

                        T(X_k,C_k^0)<eta.                      (R3)

These are N compiler calls, not one call for every (k,u). The zero-field
exponent values are also shareable: across all k their argument is delta z^2/N
with z on the half-integer grid in[-N/2,N/2]; only O(N) distinct values occur.

For a branch (k,u), take D=diag(exp[-max(h',0)/2],
                                    exp[min(h',0)/2]).
Its ratio of up/down entries is exp(h'/2), and multiplication by an irrelevant
positive scalar turns D^tensor k into exp(h' Jz,S/2). Consequently

 C_{k,u}=Phi_{D^tensor k}(C_k^0),
 kappa(D^tensor k)^2=exp(k|h'|)<=2^B.                         (R4)

By the normalized-filter inequality, the exactly tilted mixture X_k is
within 2^(B+1)eta=eps/512 of C_{k,u}. This explicitly charges the accuracy
amplification instead of assuming a zero-field approximation survives tilt.

Acquire nonnegative dyadic Dhat entries in[0,1] with each entry error at most

                    tauD=eta/(N+1).                           (R5)

The larger diagonal is kept exactly1. Since each smaller true entry is at
least exp[-Hcap/2] and tauD is much smaller, both acquired entries are strictly
positive. The main proof's scalar exp routine has polynomial bit cost at
these precisions, with no square-root or arbitrary real output.

For A=D^tensor k,Ahat=Dhat^tensor k, tensor telescoping gives
||A-Ahat||op<=k tauD. For a state X, the unnormalized channels differ by at
most2k tauD in trace norm. Their exact-channel normalizer is at least
exp[-k|h'|]>=2^-B. The standard normalization inequality therefore gives

 T(Phi_A(X),Phi_Ahat(X))<=2k tauD 2^B<=eps/512.                 (R6)

Now do the entire approximate tilt in EXACT rational arithmetic. Set

 a_j=Tr(Dhat sigma_kj Dhat)>0,
 sigma'_j=Dhat sigma_kj Dhat/a_j,
 w'_j=w_kj a_j^k / sum_i w_ki a_i^k.                           (R7)

All local states are exactly rational PSD trace-one, and all weights are
nonnegative rational with sum1. If a one-qubit matrix is encoded as
(d0,d1,re,im), its new entries are
(D0^2 d0/a,D1^2 d1/a,D0 D1 re/a,D0 D1 im/a),
a=D0^2d0+D1^2d1. Its determinant is the original nonnegative determinant
times D0^2D1^2/a^2. This proves positivity without a numerical repair or
unacquired algebraic square root. Product weights a_j^k cost polynomial bits;
normalizers are positive rational sums acquired exactly.

Round these inner weights dyadically with denominator >=128 D_k/eps, assigning
the residual to the final atom. This adds <=eps/128. The complete branch
error is thus <=eps/512+eps/512+eps/128=3eps/256, smaller than the main proof's
5eps/128 block allowance. Its final total error ledger is unchanged.

The pure-quadrature special case of(R7) is the familiar x transformation
x'=exp(h')x/[1-x+exp(h')x], w'=w[1-x+exp(h')x]^k after a common scalar is
canceled. Quadrature degree k makes the exact polynomial tilt reproduce the
target populations. The generic mixed-state variant above avoids requiring
an exact pure descriptor and supplies finite-bit/filter-error accounting.

There are at most D_k base atoms for each k. Only N zero-field iid compiler
calls are made. Branch-dependent rational atom/weight transforms and dyadic
sampling remain polynomial; they can be computed from the stored base atoms
on demand or precomputed for all O(N^2) branches. Hcap=O(log(N/eps)), so B,
log1/eta, log1/tauD and all data bits are polynomial in N,L,log1/eps. As in
the main proof, a polynomial-time theorem does not assert that its full
certified generic optimizer has been implemented or practically benchmarked.
