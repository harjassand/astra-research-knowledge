CYCLE 4 LEAF — WEAKLY REVERSIBLE STATE-DEPENDENT ESCAPE SEARCH
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact family and classwise
classification below. No weakly reversible transient/null/explosive example
was found. No general non-explosion or recurrence claim is made.

Question and result
-------------------
The search target is a weakly reversible (WR), fixed-rate, stochastic
mass-action CTMC in which a nonperiodic/state-dependent path, a separation
between event-chain and physical-time behavior, or a sparse boundary class
might yield transience or explosion. The exact test network below has three
species, stoichiometric rank two, maximum source molecularity three, one
linkage class, deficiency one, and a non-complex-balanced positive rational
rate vector. Its entire communicating-class decomposition is explicit, and
every class is positive recurrent and nonexplosive. The proof controls all
paths in these classes; it does not rely on a prescribed reaction word.

This is a scoped no-go example, not a counterexample to the general WR
recurrence problem. It shows that a natural sparse-gate/high-order-return
mechanism cannot give escape when the gate total is conserved and positive
quadratic catalyst-dependent loss is uniformly available on each active
class.

1. A sparse one-linkage WR network
----------------------------------
Species are A,B,C, with propensities in the standard falling-factorial
convention. The reversible reactions and rates are

  A+B  -> 2A+B       rate 2
  2A+B -> A+B        rate 1
  A+C  -> 2A+C       rate 1
  2A+C -> A+C        rate 1
  A+B  -> A+C        rate 1
  A+C  -> A+B        rate 1.

Write a,b,c for the three counts and M=b+c. The complex graph is the
bidirected path

  2A+B <-> A+B <-> A+C <-> 2A+C.

It is weakly reversible with one linkage class. The reaction vectors span
the A direction and the B-C direction, so the stoichiometric rank is two;
M is the only nonzero linear conservation law. There are four complexes, so
the deficiency is 4-1-2=1. The largest source is 2A+B or 2A+C, of order
three. All three species change on the active classes.

The rates are not complex balanced. Every edge of the complex graph is a
bridge, so complex balance would force equality of forward and reverse flux
on each edge. The A+B pair requires c_A=2/1=2, while the A+C pair requires
c_A=1/1=1. No positive complex-balanced equilibrium exists. Thus the
classification below does not reduce to the product-Poisson complex-balance
case.

2. Exact communicating classes
------------------------------
All reactions preserve M. Every complex contains at least one A, so when
a=0 every reaction is disabled. If M=0, all reactions are also disabled for
every a. Therefore:

  * for M=0, each state (a,0,0) is a singleton absorbing class;
  * for M>=1, each state (0,b,M-b) is a singleton absorbing class;
  * for M>=1, Gamma_M^+ = {(a,b,c): a>=1, b+c=M} is one closed
    irreducible class.

For the last claim, A+B <-> A+C moves the gate count b one step in either
direction whenever a>=1, and M>=1 guarantees at least one gate molecule.
The A birth pair increases a by one at any a>=1 in some enabled gate state;
the reverse pair decreases a by one whenever a>=2 in some enabled gate
state. These finite positive-rate paths connect any two states in Gamma_M^+.
No transition can leave it because the only A-decreasing reaction maps
a=2 to a=1, and M is conserved. This lists every class; it also exposes the
boundary degeneracy rather than treating an inactive face as an escape route.

3. Physical-time non-explosion and positive recurrence
------------------------------------------------------
On Gamma_M^+, M>=1, the total A-birth and A-death rates are exactly

  lambda(a,b,c) = a(2b+c) = a(M+b),
  mu(a,b,c)     = a(a-1)(b+c) = a(a-1)M.

The A-catalyzed B<->C exchange reactions have total rate aM and do not
change A. Hence for V(a,b,c)=a,

  L V = a[b+(2-a)M].                                             (1)

Since 0<=b<=M, this gives the exact statewise bounds

  a=1: LV=M+b<=2M;
  a=2: LV=2b<=2M;
  a=3: LV=3(b-M)=-3c<=0;
  a>=4: LV<=a(3-a)M<=-aM<=-4.

For each fixed M the gate coordinate ranges over a finite set. Thus V is
norm-like on Gamma_M^+, and F_M={(a,b,c) in Gamma_M^+:1<=a<=3} is finite.
The last line is a finite-set Foster drift, but the physical-time clock is
checked separately before invoking recurrence.

For non-explosion, (1) gives LV<=2M globally. Let tau_N be the first time
a>=N, for N>a_0. A-jumps have size one, and on {a<N} the state set is
finite with bounded total rate, so localized stopped Dynkin is valid:

  E_x[V(X_(t wedge tau_N))] <= a_0+2Mt,
  P_x(tau_N<=t) <= (a_0+2Mt)/N.                                 (2)

If explosion occurred while a remained bounded, it would remain in a finite
set (M is fixed) with bounded rates, which cannot support infinitely many
jumps in finite time. Therefore explosion by t requires hitting every large
N by t. Sending N to infinity in (2) proves physical-time non-explosion.
Now the standard finite-set continuous-time Foster criterion, applied to
the irreducible class Gamma_M^+, gives positive recurrence. The finite
absorbing classes listed above are trivially positive recurrent. Hence every
initial state of this exact network lies in a positive recurrent,
nonexplosive class.

The drift proof allows arbitrary state-dependent reaction choices and
arbitrary nonperiodic paths; it bounds the process rather than a single
word. In particular, no such path has positive probability of finite-time
explosion in this family.

4. Event chain versus physical-time clock
-----------------------------------------
At a state (a,b,M-b) in Gamma_M^+, the total exit rate is

  q(a,b)=lambda+mu+aM = a^2 M+a(M+b),

and the embedded reaction-event chain has the exact drift

  E[Delta A | (a,b)] = LV/q
     = a[b+(2-a)M]/[a^2M+a(M+b)].                               (3)

For a>=8, since b<=M,

  2 a[b+(2-a)M] + q(a,b)
       = 3ab+5aM-a^2M
       <= aM(8-a) <= 0,

so the embedded-chain drift is at most -1/2 outside the finite set
{a<=7}. Its increments in A are in {-1,0,1}; its gate component is finite.
The discrete-time Foster criterion therefore also gives positive recurrence
of the embedded chain on Gamma_M^+.

This calculation separates two facts that must not be conflated. The
embedded chain is pulled back from large A, while q(a,b) grows on the order
of M a^2 and holding means shrink on the order of (M a^2)^-1. A jump-chain
recurrence assertion alone would not prove that the physical clock is
nonexplosive. Here the independent stopped-Dynkin estimate (2) controls
physical time; (3) independently controls event count. In general CTMCs,
the event transition probabilities are lambda_r(x)/q(x), while holding
times are Exp(q(x)); an event-index estimate alone has no elapsed-time
content.

For example, the event that A strictly increases at every reaction event
has probability zero: for a>=4 the chance that the next jump is an A-birth
is at most lambda/mu <= 2/(a-1) <= 2/3, and the product of these bounds
over successive levels vanishes. That elementary word bound does not handle
paths that interleave gate switches or downward moves; the Foster arguments
above do.

5. Sparse-boundary and WR path-lifting audit
--------------------------------------------
The all-zero-A face is sparse and closed here, but its points are absorbing
singleton classes, not transient escape states. Each positive-M active class
retains both members of every reversible pair and is recurrent by (1).
More generally, a missing catalyst cannot disable the return route *after*
an enabled WR reaction fires. If x>=y enables y->y', and
y'=z_0->z_1->...->z_m=y is a directed complex path, then the state path

  x-y+z_0 -> x-y+z_1 -> ... -> x-y+z_m=x

is enabled at every step: the current state contains the source complex
z_i. Thus every actual one-step successor can return to its predecessor,
and every communicating class is closed. This graph-lifting fact proves
neither recurrence nor non-explosion; it only rules out a hidden irreversible
boundary edge. If a purported face class cannot execute the lifted return
path, then an intermediate reaction leaves that face and the class was not
contained in it.

The standard explosive strongly-endotactic word remains a useful negative
control, not a WR example. Anderson--Cappelletti--Kim--Nguyen's path
2A->4A+B->6A+4B->3A gains one A per word and has summable holding means.
Closing its complex graph by adding 3A->2A makes the first return source
3A available at (n,0). Its rate is k_4(n)_3, whereas the intended
2A->4A+B rate is k_1(n)_2. Thus

  P(intended next channel at (n,0))
    = k_1/[k_1+k_4(n-2)] = O(1/n),

and the probability of repeating that same word is zero for every fixed
positive k_1,k_4. The total holding means along the desired base states
could still be summable; the path-probability failure is a separate
obstruction. This calculation addresses that direct closure and word only.

No broader conclusion follows for nonperiodic or lengthening paths, nor for
an event defined only by state jumps when multiple reaction channels share
the same net increment. For such an event the channel rates must be
aggregated; a zero probability for each prescribed channel word is not by
itself a proof that the union of all adaptive paths has zero probability.

6. Transfer and unresolved boundary
-----------------------------------
The exact transfer is: let a closed irreducible class have a fixed finite
gate-state set, A>=1, and let gate reactions leave A unchanged. If, uniformly
over that gate set, A-birth rates satisfy lambda_A(a,g)<=C_M a and paired
A-death rates satisfy mu_A(a,g)>=c_M a(a-1), with C_M<infinity and
c_M>0, then V=a is proper on the class and

  LV <= C_M a-c_M a(a-1).

This is bounded above globally and is <=-1 outside a finite set. The same
localized stopped-Dynkin argument as in (2), followed by the CTMC Foster
criterion, proves non-explosion and positive recurrence. No upper bound on
gate-transition rates as a->infinity is needed for this physical-time
claim, provided each state has finite rates: bounded-A sublevel sets are
finite and hence have bounded rates. If additionally the total gate rate is
O(a) uniformly on the fixed gate set, then the embedded-chain drift is also
<=-1/2 for all sufficiently large a, since mu_A is quadratic while all
competing non-death rates are O(a). The displayed network is an exact
one-linkage, three-species realization at a non-complex-balanced rate
vector. These hypotheses are sufficient for this topology, not a general
WR tier criterion.

The proof stops when the conserved gate total is removed or the uniform
quadratic return coefficient is lost. It gives no bound uniform in M:
the Foster finite set and constants depend on M, while V=A does not control
an unbounded gate population. It does not cover general higher-order WR
networks in which several species grow on different scales, top-tier
channels change the gate state, or return propensities vanish on faces.
Those are precisely the open path mechanisms left after the fixed-word
audit. No exact transient, null-recurrent, or explosive WR class was found
in this search.

7. Status, costs, falsifiers, and sources
-----------------------------------------
Status: the class decomposition, generator identities, stopped-Dynkin
estimate, and both recurrence arguments are INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE.
The finite checker is only a transcription diagnostic. No novelty,
priority, or comprehensive-search claim is made.

Inputs: the six reversible reactions, fixed positive rational rates, and
standard unit-volume stochastic mass-action propensities. The proof is
exact and uses no simulation, acquired model parameters, time-varying
control, numerical approximation, or external compute. Constants in the
general finite-gate transfer depend on the fixed gate total M and rates;
there is no uniform-in-M, uniform-in-rate, mixing-time, or stationary-tail
bound. The finite checker covers only displayed small M,a values.

Strongest baseline: product-Poisson plus a finite-stationary-flux theorem
covers complex-balanced rates, but the selected rates here are not complex
balanced. The classwise theorem here is an exact network-specific Foster
proof, not an extension to arbitrary WR systems. The prior fixed periodic
channel-word obstruction is route-specific and does not subsume the
state-dependent argument used for this family.

Direct falsifiers: an enabled transition not among the six listed reactions;
a nonzero change of M; a discrepancy in lambda, mu, q, or LV; any state in
Gamma_M^+ not connected by the displayed positive-rate moves; or an
explosion path contradicting (2). The general transfer requires a uniform
positive lower bound c_M on the coefficient of a(a-1) and finite gate
state space. A sparse face that violates either condition is not covered.
