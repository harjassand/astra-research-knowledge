# Gaussian operator experiments: covariance equivalence, a commuting boundary, and a randomized-design obstruction

Status2026-10-06: complete finite Gaussian comparison theorems independently derived below; no PDE-specific advantage or field novelty claimed. This supplements the inverse team's qualitative one-current result while keeping old causal results untouched.

## Interface and charged budgets

Finite real input H=R^p, output R^q after one fixed common detector projection P (absorb P into F). For k currents with columns G=[g1,...,gk], observe

Y=F(θ)G+σZ,

where the entries of Z are independent N(0,1), σ>0 known, G chosen independently of θ, and any realized randomized G is revealed to the estimator. Hard total input energy is tr(GGᵀ)≤E. Uncompressed acquisition has qk noisy scalar channels. Retaining only sufficient statistics can reduce storage to qr, r=rank(G), but does not erase physical detector acquisition cost. Comparisons below explicitly state acquisition/retention counts. No input setup cost or temporal acquisition penalty is presumed.

C=GGᵀ alone suffices under this fixed-common-output/noise interface. If output detectors h_j change by channel, the sufficient design is instead the lifted linear map vec(F)↦(h_jᵀF g_j)_j; its precision is Σ_j(g_jg_jᵀ)⊗(h_jh_jᵀ)/σ_j² in consistent vectorization. Charging current energy per scalar channel would overcharge a shared excitation, so group such channels by actual distinct currents. Detector restrictions and signal bandwidth are additional physical constraints.

## Theorem1: designs with the same C are Blackwell equivalent

Let G=U diag(√λ1,...,√λr)Vᵀ be a thin SVD. Complete V to an orthogonal k×k basis. The right-rotated Y consists of

YV=F(θ)U diag(√λ)+σZr

and q(k−r) independent pure-noise entries. Dropping pure noise and applying/inverting known rotations are parameter-independent stochastic maps. Thus the canonical informative experiment depends only on C=U diag(λ)Uᵀ. A qr-dimensional linear sufficient statistic exists. Any two finite current systems with the same C, even different k, are Blackwell equivalent after deleting/adding ancillary noise. All risks for every prior/loss/decision problem agree. This is stronger than equal Fisher information or equal mean squared error.

Recoverable coordinates: YGᵀ has mean F(θ)C and independent row covariance σ²C; restricting each row to ran(C) gives qr sufficient scalars.

## Theorem2: Loewner dominance gives a concrete Gaussian garbling

Use a p-column canonical experiment X1=F(θ)C1^(1/2)+σZ; null directions are independent pure noise. If C1≽C0≽0, range(C0)⊂range(C1), and

K=C1^(†/2)C0^(1/2)

has KᵀK≼I and C1^(1/2)K=C0^(1/2). Generate

X0=X1K+σZ′(I−KᵀK)^(1/2),

with independent white Z′. Its mean is F(θ)C0^(1/2); its row noise covariance is σ²I. This is a parameter-independent garbling, proving experimentC1 Blackwell-dominates experimentC0. The contraction follows by whitening the inequality C0≼C1 on ran(C1).

Equal trace/E is a crucial boundary: C1≽C0 and trC1=trC0 forces equality. Therefore arbitrary optimized equal-energy PSD designs are usually incomparable by this sufficient order. An averaged random covariance need not Blackwell-dominate each realized covariance or the revealed-design mixture.

## Exact local linear information

For F(θ)=F0+Σ_a θ_aHa, a fixed G has information

J(C)_ab=σ^(−2) tr(Ha C Hbᵀ).

After subtracting F0G, vectorizing gives ordinary Gaussian linear observation M_Gθ+σz. Its statistical experiment depends on the Gram matrix M_GᵀM_G=σ²J(C), including rank-deficient cases, by SVD. This yields a sharper family-specific equivalence than C equality. For nonlinear F, the same expression is local Fisher information with Ha=∂aF, not a global equivalence claim.

For arbitrary θ,θ′, fixed G gives exact

KL(Pθ||Pθ′)=tr((Fθ−Fθ′)C(Fθ−Fθ′)ᵀ)/(2σ²).

Finite global secant objectives are PSD covariance design problems; a finite-net optimum is not a certified continuum minimum without a covering error bound.

## Theorem3: a common singular basis removes any multi-current information advantage

Suppose Fθ=U diag(f1(θ),...,fn(θ))Vᵀ, with known fixed orthogonal U,V, full common output access and white noise. This includes commuting self-adjoint families (simultaneously orthogonally diagonalizable), but the common-left/common-right-basis condition is the actual sufficient condition. Rotate into these bases.

For a multiple-current G, row i of Y has mean fi(θ) times row i of VᵀG. Matched filtering that row by its known current vector, divided by its norm, yields one scalar with mean √ci fi(θ), noise variance σ², where ci=(VᵀCV)ii. Orthogonal row complements are pure noise and different informative rows have independent noise. Rows with ci=0 contain no signal.

A single deterministic current g=V(√c1,...,√cn)ᵀ has energy Σci=trC and produces exactly those same n independent informative channels. Thus one current and arbitrary multicurrent C are globally Blackwell equivalent on this family, for nonlinear fi as well as linear parameters. Off-diagonal C is irrelevant. One full response acquires at most n channels; the full multiple-current experiment acquires nk but has at most n informative retained statistics. If hardware can measure only the informative matched-filter channels, count that as a different detector interface explicitly.

Hence noncommuting sensitivities can be load-bearing for a genuine current-rank design gap. Noncommutation is not asserted necessary or sufficient in every restricted family; the exact local criterion is whether rank-one current designs can reproduce a desired J(C).

## Theorem4: no universal randomized one-current advantage at equal finite budgets

Let n≥2, d=n(n+1)/2, and let A vary over the closed Frobenius ball {A∈Sym(n):||A||F≤R}. Set F_A=I+τA with τR<1, so every operator is SPD. Observe full finite output as above. One current may be deterministic or random, known to the estimator, independent of A, with hard energy||g||²≤E. Any additional noisy linear detectors of the same response F_Ag cannot be more informative than exact noiseless Ag.

The linear map L_g:Sym(n)→R^n, A↦Ag, has rankn when g≠0. Rotate g to a multiple of e1: its first output coordinate accesses A11 and remaining ones access Ai1; it has invisible dimensiond−n=(n−1)n/2. Rank0 when g=0 only worsens the bound.

Place the θ-independent prior uniform on the Frobenius sphere||A||F=R in the d-dimensional Euclidean space Sym(n). In noiseless observation, one obtains the orthogonal projection of A onto the n-dimensional row space of L_g. The conditional mean in its orthogonal complement is zero by reflection symmetry of the spherical prior. By isotropy, the expected squared unseen component is R²(d−n)/d. Therefore noiseless Bayes Frobenius risk is exactly this value for g≠0. Gaussian detector noise is a garbling of noiseless Ag, so cannot lower the Bayes risk.

Condition on a realized random g and then average. The same prior is independent of g and the bound does not depend on its direction or norm. Thus

inf_estimator E_prior,randomg,noise ||Â−A||F² ≥ R²(d−n)/d.

Consequently the unconditional frequentist minimax risk over the fixed ball is at least this Bayes bound, for every such randomized one-current design. This avoids an invalid minimax argument in which the adversarial θ is selected after seeing the random current. It also shows why expected Fisher information can conceal a per-experiment nullspace: E_gJ(g) may be positive definite even though every realized J(g) has rank≤n.

Now use n deterministic currents g_j=√(E/n)e_j. Total energyE; acquisitionn² scalars; retained at mostn² (and onlyd are informative after symmetrization). Write

Y=√(E/n)(I+τA)+σZ.

Estimator Â=(sym(Y)/√(E/n)−I)/τ has exact Frobenius risk

E||Â−A||F²=σ² n d/(τ²E).

Proof: each diagonal noise has varianceσ² and each symmetrized off-diagonal noise entry has varianceσ²/2, with each off-diagonal counted twice in Frobenius norm. Thus E||symZ||F²=n+n(n−1)/2=d. Scaling gives the expression. Projection onto the Frobenius ball cannot increase its squared error and preserves this upper bound.

Both arms obey the same maximum acquisition and retention budget B=n². A one-current arm ordinarily acquires onlyn channels; allowing it to use all remaining channels for arbitrary repeated/linear detector readings of that same response does not beat exact noiseless Ag and therefore does not weaken the lower bound. No excitation overhead is charged. As σ→0, multi-current risk→0 while every randomized one-current risk is bounded below. This is a complete finite SPD counterexample to any universal one-current statistical advantage. It is not a theorem about conductivity PDE realizability.

Concrete n2 fixture: R=1/2, τ=1, E=2, σ=1/100, B=4. Every one-current randomized strategy has minimax risk≥1/12≈0.083333, whereas two equal-energy currents attain risk≤3/10000=0.0003. Matrix H=[[1/2,−1/2],[−1/2,1/2]] has||H||F=1 and H(1,1)ᵀ=0; F_{±RH} are SPD and collide for that deterministic current. The fixed spherical-prior proof, rather than this g-dependent two-point example, supplies the randomized lower bound.

## Random current covariance is not a multi-current experiment

If g is sampled once and revealed, the experiment is the mixture labelled by g. Expected KL equals the Cbar=E[ggᵀ] covariance criterion, but minimizing a realized secant then averaging cannot be exchanged: E[min_T tr(TggᵀTᵀ)]≤min_T tr(TCbarTᵀ). Expected Fisher information also does not determine risk of the labelled mixture.

For a linear θ model with Gaussian prior covariance Γ≻0, conditional posterior covariance is (Γ^−1+J(g))^−1. Matrix inverse is convex: xᵀM^−1x=sup_u(2xᵀu−uᵀMu), hence averaging and supremum give

E[(Γ^−1+J(g))^−1]≽(Γ^−1+EJ(g))^−1.

The right side is the posterior covariance of a deterministic multi-current system realizing Cbar, if its required rank/current/detector budget is feasible. This proves a Bayes squared-error comparison for that linear/Gaussian-prior interface, not universal Blackwell domination of random mixtures. A Gaussian input N(0,Cbar) obeys the energy budget only in expectation; hard-energy normalization changes its covariance and must be rederived.

## Scientific consequence and next falsifiable step

A qualitative one-current injectivity theorem (especially with continuum output and infinite-rank secants) is compatible with arbitrarily poor finite-detector/noisy efficiency. At finite Gaussian resolution the proper comparator is a feasible PSD covariance/current-detector design with the same energy, scalar readings, bandwidth and setup costs. Our finite Sym(n) construction proves that rank-one exposure can be a strict information bottleneck; the commuting theorem gives a complete condition under which it is not.

The stronger inverse-team block lift F_A=⊕_l w_l(I+τA) with w_l↓0 retains infinite-rank differences but weights additional one-current directions weakly. See check_vector_measurements' independent exact construction. Its quantitative weights, detector budgets and physical realizability must be preserved before relating it to EIT. We have not shown this operator family is a conductivity NtD family.
