# Restart gates: Fog-front range–speed control candidate

Status at 2026-10-08: `CANDIDATE / UNKNOWN`; no wet-lab capability or causal transport mechanism is established. Resume only if a gate yields a new observation that can distinguish models or estimate a manipulable control parameter.

## Gate 0 — provenance and biological interface

- **Inputs:** the 2026 Mundhe et al. Fog/GPCR paper and supplements; 2019 Bailles et al. wave paper; 2024 Collinet et al. adhesion/de-adhesion paper; 2025 Hydra mechanical-organizer paper; classic morphogen-transport prior art in `sources/NOTES.md`.
- **Pass:** a specific same-system surface pool, cell-relative spatial coordinate, and intervention variable can be mapped to observable measurements.
- **Fail/stop:** pool identity or geometry cannot be distinguished from generic apical motion; report only the source-study mechanism and abandon the transport-specific claim.
- **Current outcome:** partial pass as a candidate interface only. Molecular pool identity, material-frame transport, and relevant parameter pairing remain absent.

## Gate 1 — matched-embryo parameter identifiability

- **Acquire per embryo/condition:** front speed relative to tracked cell rows `v_rel`; slow-pool lateral mobility `D` in the same material region; active-pool clearance by pulse-chase or equivalent; cell age since first contact; MyoII time series and spatial profile; contact duration/geometry and detachment time. Keep embryo, not FCS spot or cell, as the biological replication unit.
- **Required control conditions:** WT, a graded Fog source condition, a graded Gprk2/clearance perturbation, and integrin/contact perturbation; match initial primordium invagination and record source strength. Preserve source-restricted diffusible versus membrane-tethered Fog as an initiation/dispersion control.
- **Pass:** independent estimates of `D`, `k`, and `v_rel` exist for the same perturbation condition, with embryo-level uncertainty/covariance; fitted field and response coordinate are predeclared.
- **Fail/stop:** only cross-paper proxies are available, pool is not independently measurable, or parameter estimates are confounded. Keep all range numbers as dimensional screening and do not report a fitted biological `k`.

## Gate 2 — adversarial held-out model comparison

- **Models:** (A) source-free moving surface-pool transport with independently measured `D`, `k`, `v_rel`; (B) local contact-age waveform with no lateral ligand transport; (C) an equally informed contact/integrin mechanochemical relay with measured adhesion/de-adhesion variables. Permit model D only if a predeclared integrin/contact-gated source term is independently measured.
- **Fit/holdout:** fit a subset of embryos/doses; hold out at least one Fog or Gprk2 dose and one integrin/contact condition. Compare embryo-level held-out profile and speed prediction with uncertainty, penalizing free parameters. Do not let a fitted spatial exponential count as prediction of its own profile.
- **Pass:** the transport model predicts held-out surface-pool and downstream profiles/speeds better than equally informed B/C, and the measured passive range satisfies `v_rel ell <= D` when its assumptions apply.
- **Fail/demote:** B/C predicts equally well or better; `v_rel ell > D` without measured local gain/source; or the independently measured `k` does not covary with response range. In that case transport is a descriptive reparameterization, not the mechanism.

## Gate 3 — bounded intervention capability

- **Only after Gate 2 passes:** perturb the source/clearance/contact variables to hit a preregistered target activation footprint while preserving a specified minimum front speed, normal developmental completion, and embryo viability. The objective is the measured Pareto frontier of reach, speed, detachment, and viability, not maximum MyoII amplitude.
- **Pass:** independently replicated, held-out settings reach the target range-speed region with stated embryo-level uncertainty and without a viability/development penalty above a preregistered limit.
- **Fail/stop:** the intervention cannot separate range from adhesion-mediated slowing, causes developmental failure, or has no advantage over the mechanical relay alone. No therapeutic, wound-healing, or cross-species claim follows from a Drosophila result.

## Resource and provenance gate

Required work includes custom reporter/perturbation genotypes, staged embryo cohorts, confocal tracking/FCS or pulse-chase, embryo-level replication, blinded profile extraction, and preregistered held-out analysis.
