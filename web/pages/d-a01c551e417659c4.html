REAL SYMMETRIC ALL-ACCURACY FAMILY / c02_s03 / phase two

This is an extension of the explicit nonsymmetric family in the main proof.
It uses the SAME acquired residual norm guard and code, and makes the class
include real symmetric pair matrices (the opposite-spin spin-singlet case).
No additional oracle or algorithm is imported.

For even n=2q>=4 and any fixed rational radius alpha in (0,1], set

  G0=5I+S+S^T+S^2+(S^2)^T,
  D_ij=1/(i+j+1) for i!=j, D_ii=0,
  B0=n^2+4n,
  delta=alpha/[4 n q B0^(q-1) 2^n],
  F=G0+delta D,
  H=the bandwidth-two truncation of F.

Here S is the first upper shift matrix. All entries are explicit rationals
with polynomial total encoding in n,L_alpha. F is real symmetric and every
offdiagonal entry is positive, so its support is complete and every support
frontier ordering has width n-1. H has frontier two in the input ordering.

The submatrix of H with even rows and odd columns is lower bidiagonal. Its
diagonal entries are 1+delta D_(2a,2a+1)>=1, so its determinant is at least
one. Hence Z_H=c_q(H)>=1, from a displayed positive hard-sector witness.
Every D entry is <=1, eta2<=n^2 delta^2, and the acquired rational B<=B0.
The same calculation as the main proof gives

  d2 <= alpha^2 binomial(n,q)/(16*2^(2n))
     <= 2^(-n)/16 <= Z_H*2^(-n)/16.

Thus the F-only band2/rank0 UNIFORM admission passes for ALL n and alpha.
The matrix is admitted before any later requested epsilon is selected. The
implemented approximate/fine-exact scheme supplies a relative norm estimate
and TV sampler for every epsilon in (0,1], in the main proved cost bound.

These matrices have linearly large exact cut rank despite that admission.
Across the middle cut, D[R,L] is the q-by-q Cauchy matrix 1/(i+j+1), with
i=q,...,n-1 and j=0,...,q-1. It has rank q: if
sum_j a_j/(t+j+1) vanishes at all q distinct values t=q,...,n-1, multiplying
by product_j(t+j+1) gives a polynomial of degree at most q-1 with q roots.
It is zero identically, and its residues at t=-j-1 force every a_j=0.
G0[R,L] has rank at most two because only its first two rows can be nonzero.
The elementary inequality rank(A+B)>=rank(A)-rank(B) gives

  rank F[R,L]>=q-2.

By symmetry the reverse block has the same rank, so the directed cut-rank
sum is at least n-4=Theta(n). This is a lower bound in the charged input
ordering; optimal reordering and exact rank q for every parameter value
are not asserted. Exact diagnostics find rank q on the displayed finite
fixtures; the uniform theorem only needs the proved q-2 lower bound.

Finally ||G0-5I||op<=4 by the four shift terms, and ||delta D||op<=delta n
<=1/16. Hence sigma_min(F)>=15/16, sigma_max(F)<=145/16, and condition
number<=145/15<10. The explicit diagonal enables this conditioning bound
but affects no hard disjoint minor. A zero-diagonal conditioning theorem
is NOT claimed.

This is an easy exponentially small neighborhood of a bounded-width
model, not a general FPRAS for symmetric F, a claim of new MPS principles,
or a physical Hamiltonian/energy result. Full hard support of every
complementary minor is not claimed. The contribution is the complete
acquired all-accuracy approximation interface, with its honest exact-fallback
cost and sharp projected-norm dependence. Novelty and external validation
remain UNKNOWN.
