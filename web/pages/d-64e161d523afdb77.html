1. SETUP AND MEASUREMENT / PREPARATION CONTRACTIONS
Let A be a finite-dimensional C*-algebra with faithful normalized trace tau,
physically represented by a central faithful density sigma: tau(X)=Tr(sigma X).
All matrix-block dimensions and positive central weights may grow. Let M_y in A
be a finite POVM, sum M_y=I, p_y=tau(M_y)>0; zero effects can be removed by
faithfulness. Supply density states rho_y in A such that
  sum_y p_y rho_y=sigma.
Put A_y=sigma^(-1/2) rho_y sigma^(-1/2), so A_y>=0, tau(A_y)=1 and
sum p_y A_y=I. Centrality allows the equivalent sigma^(-1)rho_y expression.

Use the commutative algebra C^m with weighted trace tau_p(f)=sum p_y f_y.
Define unital CP trace-preserving maps
  T(X)_y=tau(M_y X)/p_y,
  P(f)=sum_y p_y f_y A_y.
T is positive because each coordinate is a state. P is CP because its domain
is commutative and the A_y are positive. Unitality and trace preservation are
exactly the displayed normalizations. Kadison Schwarz followed by the trace
shows ||TX||_2,p<=||X||_2,tau and ||Pf||_2,tau<=||f||_2,p.
The trace adjoint is
  T^dagger(f)=sum_y f_y M_y.
Thus the canonical map
  Psi=T^dagger T,
  Psi(X)=sum_y tau(M_y X) M_y/p_y
is UCP, tau-preserving, selfadjoint and positive as an L2 operator. It obeys
0<=Psi<=I. Its physical Schrödinger implementation is the EB channel
  rho -> sum_y Tr(M_y rho) sigma M_y/p_y.
Central sigma makes every preparation positive; all have trace one.

Let Q=P T. Physically it is the ordinary EB map
  Q_phys(rho)=sum_y Tr(M_y rho) rho_y.
It fixes sigma. The previous contraction proves the load-bearing inequality
  Q^dagger Q=T^dagger P^dagger P T<=T^dagger T=Psi.       (1)
Q need not be positive/selfadjoint as an L2 operator; its preparations may
be pure, rotate, change blocks, or differ from canonical preparations.

2. THE GENERAL FACTOR-TWO IDENTITY
Let L:A->A be ANY UCP tau-preserving channel, hence L^dagger L<=I. Set
  C=(L+Q)/2, Phi=C^dagger C.
Phi is UCP, tau-preserving and positive selfadjoint on L2. Exact expansion:
  2(I-Phi)-(I-Psi)
    =(I-L^dagger L)+(Psi-Q^dagger Q)
                           +(L-Q)^dagger(L-Q)/2 >=0.    (2)
The first term is PSD by Schwarz, the second by (1), the third is a square.
Consequently I-Psi<=2(I-Phi). No local commutation, Q selfadjointness,
Psi<=Phi ordering, spectral gap, minimum p_y or rank hypothesis is needed.
The quadratic comparison itself does not require compatibility of L and Q.

For Hermitian H with ||H||_infinity<=theta, 0<=Psi<=I gives
  ||(I-Psi)H||_1,tau^2
   <=||(I-Psi)H||_2,tau^2
   <=<H,(I-Psi)H>_tau
   <=2<H,(I-Phi)H>_tau
   <=2 theta ||(I-Phi)H||_1,tau.                        (3)
The first step is normalized Holder; the second is (I-Psi)^2<=I-Psi;
the last is trace duality. Physical likelihood states sigma(I+H), tau(H)=0,
||H||<=theta<1 therefore admit ONE EB reconstruction with half-trace error
  e_EB <= (1/2) sqrt(2 theta r),
  r=sup_H ||Phi(H)-H||_1,tau.
The same map handles arbitrarily many supplied or unspecified directions.

3. AN ACTUAL SELFCOMPATIBLE REALIZATION WITH BLOCK TRANSITIONS
Supply physical CP instrument maps I_y on A with effects M_y=I_y^*(I),
sum_y I_y=L_phys CPTP, and L_phys(sigma)=sigma. Effects and states are in A;
the maps may move matrix blocks. Define the likelihood coordinate channel
  L(X)=sigma^(-1/2) L_phys(sigma^(1/2)X sigma^(1/2)) sigma^(-1/2).
It is UCP because L_phys fixes sigma, and tau-preserving because L_phys is TP.
Unlike the baseline inner-Kraus formula, this argument allows block movement.

The full-domain CP instrument-append channel is
  B_0(rho)=sum_y I_y(rho) tensor rho_y.
It is TP, and its marginals are L_phys and Q_phys. Average B_0 with its output
swap; both marginals become C_phys=(L_phys+Q_phys)/2, which fixes sigma.
The actual Petz recovery of C_phys at sigma is
  R_phys(z)=sigma^(1/2) C_phys^*(sigma^(-1/2)z sigma^(-1/2)) sigma^(1/2).
This is CP and TP; trace preservation follows from C_phys(sigma)=sigma.
In central likelihood coordinates it is C^dagger. Applying R_phys to BOTH
outputs of the actual symmetrized joint channel gives common marginal
Phi_phys(rho)=sigma^(1/2) Phi(sigma^(-1/2)rho sigma^(-1/2)) sigma^(1/2).
Thus Phi really is selfcompatible, not merely an algebraic positive map.
For represented direct sums, first pinch input block coherences; all promised
states are fixed. Embed output blocks physically. This yields ordinary CPTP
channels and an ordinary EB reconstruction on the full ambient matrix algebra.
Rho_y need not share a block with M_y. Only the reference-mean condition is
required. Arbitrary instruments without L_phys(sigma)=sigma do not qualify.

5. RARE-OUTCOME-SAFE CANONICAL POVM CONTINUITY
For any positive effects a,b and Hermitian ||H||<=1 define
  F_H(a)=tau(a H) a/tau(a), F_H(0)=0.
Write p=tau(a), q=tau(b); assume p>=q, interchanging if necessary. Then
  F_H(a)-F_H(b)=[tau(aH)/p](a-b)
                    +b[tau(aH)/p-tau(bH)/q].
The first term has L1 norm <=||a-b||_1. The second norm is
  |(q/p)tau(aH)-tau(bH)|
   <=(q/p)|tau((a-b)H)|+((p-q)/p)|tau(bH)|
   <=||a-b||_1+|p-q| <=2||a-b||_1.
The q=0 case follows directly. Hence
  ||F_H(a)-F_H(b)||_1<=3||a-b||_1,
  ||Psi_M-Psi_N||_(infinity->1,tau)
      <=3 sum_y ||M_y-N_y||_1,tau.                     (4)
Matched zero effects are allowed. There is no division by p_min in the bound.
The maps each remain canonical EB and preserve the exact reference.

6. FINITE-BIT POVM COMPILER WITH EXACT POSITIVITY / NORMALIZATION
For a supplied algebraic POVM with m effects, choose rational e>0 and round
Hermitian entries to rational grid spacing q<=e/(2 m d), where d is the total
ambient matrix size. Enforce the same conjugate entries; preserve block zeros.
The operator error of each rounded matrix B_y is <=e/m=:u (row-sum bound).
Set N_y=B_y+u I. Then 0<=N_y-M_y<=2u I, and
  I<=S=sum N_y<=I+2e I.
Define exact rational effects
  M'_y=N_y/(1+2e), M'_0=I-S/(1+2e).
They are PSD and sum to I; a zero remainder can be dropped. Also
  sum_y ||M'_y-M_y||_1 + ||M'_0||_1 <=6e.
For original outcomes, triangle inequality gives <=4e in total; the remainder
has trace <=2e. Equation (4), with original zero remainder, proves
  ||Psi_M'-Psi_M||_(infinity->1,tau)<=18e.               (5)
The rounded map's full-trace experiment error is therefore at most
  sqrt(2 theta r)+18 theta e                            (6)
for the admitted class above, and its half-trace error is half this quantity.
This gives a concrete precision budget e=eta/(18 theta) for additive full
error eta. The coefficient 18 is conservative, not asserted sharp.
