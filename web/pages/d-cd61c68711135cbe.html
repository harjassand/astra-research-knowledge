# Exact affine obstruction and its nonlinear boundary

Worker: hessian_check12/cycle12. Date: 2026-10-07.

## Question and result status

Question: after the Meng--Yang descent from six variables to five, can a
constant linear direction, mixed linear-direction pivot, or a constant
Hessian block be recovered by affine source changes and quadratic repair?

**No for the displayed family.** We independently established an exact
stronger statement, with a proof below: every nonzero constant symmetric
contraction of the Hessian is nonconstant. Root proposed the universal
fiber-linear extension; this worker independently reconstructed it. Peer
hessian_bridge12 proposed a fifteen-row symbolic minor; this worker rebuilt
the polynomial derivatives and independently verified it without reading or
executing peer or author code.

This is an obstruction to that specific affine/constant-block route. It is
not a disproof of HC4 and not a proof that every nonlinear descent fails.
Indeed, the independently recovered prior nonlinear construction at the end makes A a coordinate,
but fails the needed Hessian-determinant and collision interfaces.

## Exact potential and source scope

Let `(x,y,p,q,r)=(x1,x2,y1,y2,y3)`, u=1+xy, and define

`g=(u³,3xu²,-x³)`, `A=p u³+3xq u²-x³r`.

The source B is

`B=p y²u(4+3xy)+q[y+3xy²(4+3xy)]+r[2x-3x²y]`.

Meng--Yang [arXiv:2607.22198v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22198v1),
Section 6, uses `Psi=A²+11A+2B`; v1 submitted 2026-07-24.
[v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22198v2), submitted 2026-07-27, changes the
chosen normalization to `A²+13A+2B`. The exact linear coefficient is version
dependent; the obstruction is unaffected. Preserve the version when quoting
a collision witness.

In v1, independently expanded Psi has degree 14 and 42 monomials. Fresh exact
arithmetic gives the nonzero constant determinant 128, both from a reconstructed
Schur/block certificate and a direct five-by-five polynomial determinant.
The source witnesses

`(±1,∓3/2,-26,∓3/4,±27/16)`

have common gradient `(0,0,-1/2,0,0)` and common A=1. The direct determinant
calculation took approximately 88 seconds under SymPy 1.14.0. The full source
formula and exact identities, rather than the author's scripts or generic
fiber-cardinality assertion, are sufficient for noninjectivity.

For the scaled parameter family `f=L A²+M A+N B`, the same block proof gives
`det Hess f=8 L N^4`. To see this directly, let
`M0(s)=Hess(B+sA)` and `a=grad A`. Its determinant is zero, and the bordered
doubling identity is `a^T adj(M0(s)) a=4`, independently of s. The Hessian
of f is

`N M0((2L A+M)/N)+2L aa^T`.

The rank-one determinant update therefore gives `2L N^4*4=8 L N^4` for
N!=0, and polynomial continuation covers N=0. If L N!=0, put
`s0=(13N-2M)/(4L)` and use points

`(±1,∓3/2,-26s0,∓3s0/4,±27s0/16)`.

Their gradients agree at `(0,0,-N/4,0,0)`. Here A=s0, so the effective
coefficient `(2L A+M)/N` is 13/2, and the equal-gradient calculation is the
linear scaling of the already verified doubled witnesses. A fresh symbolic
substitution with free L,M,N independently confirms it. This expresses the
source v1/v2 normalization and their parameter freedom in one formula;
it is not a new counterexample priority claim.

## Elementary constant-direction obstruction

Write v=(a,b,c,d,e). The degree 14 part of Psi is the single monomial
`x⁶y⁶p²`. The degree 12 component of D_v² Psi therefore has the six distinct
terms

| Monomial | Coefficient |
| --- | --- |
| x⁴y⁶p² | 30a² |
| x⁵y⁵p² | 72ab |
| x⁶y⁴p² | 30b² |
| x⁵y⁶p | 24ac |
| x⁶y⁵p | 24bc |
| x⁶y⁶ | 2c² |

If D_v² Psi is constant, a=b=c=0. The remainder is exactly

`D_v² Psi=2[3d x(1+xy)²-e x³]²`.

It vanishes on x=0, so a constant value must be zero. In a characteristic-zero
polynomial domain the square can vanish only if its base vanishes. Dividing
by x and setting x=0 yields d=0; then e=0. Thus no nonzero constant direction
has constant second derivative, including zero. This proof is universal,
not a finite search over directions.

## Stronger universal theorem

Let k be any field of characteristic zero. Let lambda in k be nonzero.
For arbitrary polynomials b1,b2,b3,C in k[x,y], set

`f=lambda A²+b1(x,y)p+b2(x,y)q+b3(x,y)r+C(x,y)`.

For every constant symmetric five-by-five matrix K over k,

`tr(K Hess f) is constant  =>  K=0`.

Consequently the positive-degree coefficient matrices of Hess f span the
entire fifteen-dimensional space of symmetric five-by-five matrices. This
statement does not require det Hess f to be constant or nonzero, and does
not say that every member of this family has a gradient collision.

### Proof by degree in the three fiber variables

Partition K into its two-by-two base block K0, two-by-three cross block E,
and symmetric three-by-three fiber block T. The convention is

`tr(K Hess f)=kxx fxx+2kxy fxy+kyy fyy`
`             +2 sum E_ij f_(base_i,fiber_j)+sum T_ij f_(fiber_i,fiber_j)`.

The proposed constant identity is a polynomial identity in p,q,r.

**1. The degree-two fiber component forces K0=0.** The coefficient of r²
comes only from lambda g3²=lambda x⁶. It is
`30 lambda x⁴ kxx`, hence kxx=0. The coefficient of p² comes from
lambda u⁶. At x=0, u=1, `(u⁶)xy=6`, `(u⁶)yy=0`. Since kxx=0, its value
is `12 lambda kxy`; hence kxy=0. The remaining p² coefficient is
`30 lambda x²u⁴ kyy`, hence kyy=0.

Arbitrary fiber-linear B and base C have no degree-two contribution, so
they cannot cancel this step.

**2. The degree-one fiber component forces E=0.** Let Ex,Ey be the two
constant rows of E, and put

`P=Ex.g`, `Q=Ey.g`, `D=P d/dx+Q d/dy`, `R=Px+Qy`.

The coefficient of each fiber variable in the cross contraction, divided
by4lambda, is

`D g_i+R g_i=0`, for i=1,2,3.

The fiber-linear remainder contributes degree zero to these cross
derivatives; its degree-one base-Hessian terms disappeared because K0=0.

Eliminate R between the first two equations. In the rational function field,
`D(g2/g1)=0`. But `g2/g1=3x/u`, and direct differentiation with u=1+xy gives

`D(3x/u)=3(P-x²Q)/u²`.

Thus P=x²Q. The substitution map k[x,u] -> k[x,y], u |-> 1+xy, is
injective: on x!=0 its rational inverse is y=(u-1)/x. In independent
variables x,u, P and Q are homogeneous cubics, while x²Q is homogeneous
of degree five. They cannot agree unless P=Q=0. The three components
u³,3xu²,-x³ are linearly independent over k, so Ex=Ey=0.

**3. The fiber block is zero.** Only the term `2lambda g^T T g` remains
from the Hessian contraction. In independent x,u it is homogeneous of
degree six, so it cannot equal a nonzero constant. Its six underlying
products are proportional to

`u⁶, xu⁵, x²u⁴, x³u³, x⁴u², x⁶`.

They are distinct monomials. Characteristic zero and lambda!=0 therefore
force all six entries of T to vanish. This proves the theorem.

The assumptions are load-bearing: lambda=0 gives potentials linear in the
fiber variables with all fiber--fiber Hessian entries zero; allowing a
fiber-quadratic remainder permits it to cancel lambda A² entirely.

## Affine changes, quadratic repairs, and exact exclusions

For an invertible affine source change w=S z+b and arbitrary quadratic R(z),

`Hess[f(Sz+b)+R(z)]=S^T Hess(f)(Sz+b) S+Hess(R)`.

If a constant symmetric C contracted with this matrix were constant, then
`S C S^T` would give a constant symmetric contraction of Hess f. It is
nonzero exactly when C is nonzero, and Hess R only adds a constant. Thus
the full-span property survives every such change and repair.

This excludes:

* any nonzero direction whose second derivative is constant or zero;
* any two nonzero directions with constant mixed derivative, since their
  symmetrized tensor defines a nonzero symmetric contraction;
* a nonempty constant Hessian block on a constant linear subspace;
* restoring the ordinary scalar constant-pivot Schur descent by affine
  coordinate changes and quadratic repair.

It does **not** exclude polynomial or rational nonlinear changes, a
nonconstant Hessian block with constant determinant, or a partial transform
whose rational expressions cancel to polynomials. It also does not show
that an arbitrary candidate family is a counterexample to any conjecture.

## Recovered prior nonlinear construction and its failure

The construction below was independently derived before reading the pinned repository note. It matches Sections 1--2 of [HC5_NONLINEAR_TORIC_DESCENT.md](https://github.com/royvanrijn/jacobian-research/blob/caf5685db1099cb90e608556d4b8d825734f29cb/research/archive/non-elliptic/HC5_NONLINEAR_TORIC_DESCENT.md), at a commit dated 2026-09-15. It is prior, not a new construction or novelty claim. Its scalar-pivot and interface failures are independently confirmed here.

The above exclusions are sharp about their coordinate interface. A can
nevertheless be made a polynomial coordinate while x,y stay fixed. Define

`alpha=u²-5xyu+10x²y²`,
`beta=10u²y³-5xuy⁴+x²y⁵`.

The identity `(u-xy)^5=1` gives `alpha u³-beta x³=1`. Hence

`(p,q,r) -> (a=A,q,z=-beta p+alpha r)`

has determinant1 and polynomial inverse

`p=alpha(a-3xu²q)+x³z`,
`r=beta(a-3xu²q)+u³z`.

For the v1 source potential, writing t=xy, the transformed polynomial is

`f'=a²+11a+2[Ka+Hq+Iz]`,
`K=y²(6t²+15t+4)`,
`H=-y(18t^5+81t^4+120t^3+60t²-1)`,
`I=x(t+1)(t+2)`.

It has the constant a-pivot 2. But a scalar Schur complement followed by
the exact doubled two-dimensional determinant identity gives

`det Hess f'=32 [Jac(H,I)]²`,

where

`Jac(H,I)=144t^7+945t^6+2394t^5+2910t^4+1680t^3+357t²-6t-2`.

This is nonconstant. Moreover the transformed source witnesses have
gradient images

`(1377/64,1209/32,-19/2,57/8,-1/2)` and
`(-1377/64,-1209/32,-19/2,-57/8,1/2)`,

which differ. A polynomial source automorphism of determinant1 does not
preserve either Hessian constancy or equality of gradients: the chain rule
includes derivatives of the source change. This explicit failed route is
recorded so it is not silently upgraded into a nonlinear obstruction or a
new HC4 counterexample.

## Prior art, novelty status and next falsifiable step

Meng--Yang v2 Remark 4.2 says the displayed Schur mechanism stops after one
step because its dual linearity is spent. Quadratic dependence alone is
not a general proof of that assertion: for an affine identity Keller map,
successive scalar descents of its doubling do work. For their displayed
example, the constant-direction and full-span proofs here supply an exact
certificate for all constant affine pivots. This is a strengthened and
parameter-robust obstruction, not a claim that we first noticed
self-termination. Historical novelty of the full-span theorem is pending
the root/peer prior-art checks.

The concrete downstream use is a reliable filter for affine constant-pivot
searches, and a warning to preserve the gradient/Hessian interface during
nonlinear changes. The next falsifiable step in a nonlinear route is to
find a completion of the unimodular row g for which the resulting planar
coefficient map has constant Jacobian **and** an independently verified
gradient collision survives. The explicit completion above fails both
checks. No claim is made that all completions fail; that alternative is
unexplored.
