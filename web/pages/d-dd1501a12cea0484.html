CYCLE05 WEIGHTED KMS / PETZ PRIMARY-LITERATURE TRIAGE
Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: bounded primary-source comparison. The proposed weighted theorem and its proof were not inspected. It remains a candidate under independent review; this report makes no priority or novelty claim.

THE CANDIDATE INTERFACE

For finite-dimensional B, a faithful state sigma, and a CPTP map Phi with
Phi(sigma)=sigma, assume Phi has one two-output CPTP extension whose two
marginals are both Phi. Let H=Phi* be the adjoint for
<X,Y>_sigma=Tr[sigma^(1/2) X* sigma^(1/2)Y], and assume H is self-adjoint
in this inner product. The candidate asserts that one sigma-preserving EB
channel Psi, chosen from (Phi,sigma) alone, has K=Psi* satisfying the full
KMS-form operator inequalities

  K >= (2 H^2 - I)_+,
  I-K <= 4(I-H).

The consequential downstream claim supplied with the task is: for a fixed
rho_AB with rho_B=sigma and an actual B-local two-output channel whose two
AB marginals each have half-trace error at most b from rho_AB, one EB map
Lambda_B, independent of A and of the correlations, reconstructs rho_AB
within 72 sqrt((I(A:B)_rho+1)/ln(1/b)) in joint half-trace distance. This
is recorded as a conditional assembled claim, not independently derived
here. The theorem's KMS form control is not a diamond-norm estimate, and
the displayed state bound is state-specific through I(A:B). The candidate
uses faithful sigma; extension of the assembled claim to rho_B with a kernel
is part of the supplied derivation and was not checked in this source audit.

STRONGEST DIRECT CHANNEL ANTECEDENT

Piani, “Hierarchy of efficiently computable and faithful lower bounds to
quantum discord,” arXiv:1501.06855v1 (2015), PRL 117, 080401 (2016),
Eq. (4), pp. 2–3, states that a k-Bose-symmetric-extendible channel
Lambda_B^Sym+(k) has an EB channel Lambda_B^EB such that

  sup_{rho_AB} 1/2 ||(id_A tensor Lambda_B^Sym+(k))(rho_AB)
                    -(id_A tensor Lambda_B^EB)(rho_AB)||_1 <= d_B/k.

The supremum over reference inputs is a diamond-distance guarantee. The
extension is required to output inside the fully symmetric subspace of
B_1...B_k; this is stronger than merely having a permutation-invariant
extension, and stronger than general channel self-compatibility. At k=2,
the bound has a d_B factor. It does not state the candidate's dimension-free
KMS Dirichlet majorization for a sigma-preserving KMS-self-adjoint,
two-self-compatible channel. The candidate's order inequality is a weighted
2-form comparison and supplies no trace or diamond-distance estimate by
itself; its extension hypothesis is broader, while it adds a reference
state and reversibility condition. These results do not imply one another
as stated.

Piani, “Local Broadcasting of Quantum Correlations,” arXiv:1608.02650v1
(2016), Theorem 5, p. 5, gives the exact one-sided boundary: a state is
perfectly broadcast by a channel acting on B iff it is quantum-classical
on B, rho_AB=sum_j p_j rho_A^j tensor |b_j><b_j| for an orthonormal
family. Theorem 7, p. 7, gives the corresponding mutual-information
equality characterization. The same paper's Eqs. (31)–(35) compare
state-specific broadcasting fidelity and EB fidelity to discord, but the
inequality is not a stability theorem taking near-perfect broadcasting to
small discord or trace distance to a classical-quantum state.

If the candidate's downstream bound holds, it gives a dimension-free
robust local-broadcasting-to-EB-reconstruction result under a bound on the
single state's total mutual information. Applying an EB channel on B makes
(id_A tensor Lambda)(rho_AB) separable, so the bound implies closeness to a
separable state. It does not by itself imply closeness to the quantum-
classical states in Piani's exact theorem: a general EB channel may prepare
nonorthogonal output states, and its output can be separable while retaining
discord. The exact classicality endpoint would require another argument.

KMS REVERSIBILITY, PETZ RECOVERY, AND SUFFICIENCY

Alhambra and Woods, “Dynamical maps, quantum detailed balance and Petz
recovery map,” arXiv:1609.07496v4 (2017), Eqs. (3)–(5), defines the same
KMS inner product and detailed-balance self-adjointness. Their Lemma 1 and
Theorem 2, Eqs. (8)–(11), relate relative-entropy production under Davies
maps to recovery by the Petz reverse; under detailed balance that reverse
is the forward semigroup. Their Theorem 4 extends a fidelity recovery
bound to quantum dynamical semigroups satisfying detailed balance and
time-translation symmetry. These are semigroup and state-recovery results;
they do not assume self-compatibility or construct an EB channel.

Petz's original “Sufficient Subalgebras and the Relative Entropy of States
of a von Neumann Algebra,” Commun. Math. Phys. 105, 123–131 (1986),
Theorem 4, p. 127, identifies weak sufficiency of a subalgebra for a pair
of states with equality of relative entropy and equivalent cocycle/Petz
conditions. Ahmad and Klinger, “Emergent Geometry from Quantum
Probability,” arXiv:2411.07288v1 (2024), published PRD 111, 105015 (2025),
Sec. 3.2–3.3 and Theorem 3.2, restates the corresponding channel
sufficiency criterion: equality in relative-entropy data processing for a
pair of states is equivalent to recovery by the Petz dual (under its
support hypotheses). Fawzi and
Renner, arXiv:1410.0664v3 (2015), Theorem 5.1, gives a CPTP recovery map
with fidelity controlled by conditional mutual information, including
separable Hilbert spaces. These results control recovery, not replacement
by an EB channel. Their information quantity is relative-entropy loss or
I(A:C|B), rather than the candidate's KMS quadratic form and I(A:B).

Thus Petz's name and KMS geometry are genuine structural antecedents, but
the queried bridge—self-compatible reversible dynamics to one EB map with
a universal KMS form majorant—does not follow from the cited recovery
theorems.

MEASUREMENT COMPATIBILITY AND THE NEED FOR ONE GLOBAL CLASSICAL MAP

Heinosaari, Kiukas, Reitzner, and Schultz, “Incompatibility breaking
quantum channels,” arXiv:1504.05768v2 (2015), Proposition 7 and Theorem 2,
Secs. 3.2 and 5, prove IBC=intersection over all n>=2 of n-IBC and that
EB channels form a proper subclass of incompatibility-breaking channels.
They exhibit white-noise channels that render every pair (and, in suitable
dimensions, n-tuples for finite n) of measurements compatible while the
channel is not EB. A self-compatible channel therefore gives pairwise
measurement compatibility in the dual picture, but pairwise compatibility
does not provide one common POVM for all observables or an EB comparator.
The literature sharply distinguishes those quantifiers.

Girard, Plávala, and Sikora, “Jordan products of quantum channels and
their compatibility,” arXiv:2009.03279v1 (2020), published Nature
Communications 12, 2129 (2021), Eqs. (14)–(15) and Result 2 (Sec. Results),
show that every EB channel is self-compatible, while there exist compatible
pairs of EB channels with no EB compatibilizing channel. The latter is not
the candidate problem: it is a warning that an existing extension cannot
be assumed EB. The candidate would instead construct a separate EB
comparison channel satisfying the KMS order bounds.

QUANTUM DARWINISM AND APPROXIMATE SUFFICIENCY

Brandao, Piani, and Horodecki, “Generic emergence of classical features in
quantum Darwinism,” arXiv:1310.8640v2 (2015), Theorem 1 and Eqs. (1)–(2),
shows that for a channel distributing an input to n fragments, at least
(1-delta)n effective fragment channels are close in diamond norm to
measure-and-prepare channels, with one common POVM and error
((27 ln 2) d_A^6 log(d_A)/(n delta^3))^(1/3). This has an explicit input
dimension cost and a many-fragment hypothesis. If the present candidate
survives, its local consequence would trade those features for two outputs,
a single fixed input state, and a bound on I(A:B); it would not prove
redundancy, provide one pointer POVM shared by many fragments, or establish
spectrum broadcasting. It is not a replacement for the Darwinism theorem.

For approximate sufficient statistics, exact Petz sufficiency says that a
channel loses no relative entropy for the relevant family and can then be
reversed on that family. Approximate recoverability supplies a recovery
channel when a relative-entropy or conditional-mutual-information loss is
small. The candidate has a different direction: it proposes an EB
same-system channel that preserves the low-dissipation content of a
self-compatible KMS-reversible channel. This could support a new notion of
classical approximate sufficiency for low-energy observables, but the
candidate as supplied does not itself define a statistical family, prove
relative-entropy sufficiency, or recover inputs. Those consequences need
separate statements and derivations.

CONSEQUENTIAL EXACT PROBLEM IF THE CANDIDATE SURVIVES

The historically significant exact endpoint is robust unilocal
broadcasting under bounded total correlation: for every finite dimension,
every bipartite rho_AB with fixed marginal sigma, and every actual channel
B->B_1B_2 whose two A:B_j marginals are each b-close to rho_AB in half
trace distance, produce one B-local EB channel Lambda (depending only on
the broadcaster and sigma, not on A or the correlations) such that

  1/2 ||(id_A tensor Lambda)(rho_AB)-rho_AB||_1
      <= 72 sqrt((I(A:B)_rho+1)/ln(1/b)).

The dimension-free rate and the channel dependence are the substantive
quantifiers. The bound is state-specific and uses von Neumann mutual
information in nats; it is not a diamond-norm statement and it does not
assert closeness to a classical-quantum state. Existing checked direct
results give an exact zero-error characterization, dimension-dependent
many-output diamond bounds, or average/fidelity formulations. They do not
state this two-output, fixed-state, bounded-I(A:B), local-EB implication.
The bounded source pass cannot establish that no equivalent theorem exists.

The decisive mathematical gate is the candidate's weighted theorem itself:
one EB, sigma-preserving map must satisfy both full KMS operator orders for
every finite d, faithful sigma, and legal self-compatible KMS-self-adjoint
Phi. A useful first falsification family is reversible thermal qubit
channels with non-maximally-mixed faithful sigma and an explicit symmetric
two-output extension; check the two orders against the unrestricted EB
Choi cone, rather than only against a finite measurement ansatz. The
downstream trace estimate is a separate gate. Priority remains UNKNOWN.
