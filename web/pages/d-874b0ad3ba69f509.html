## Established extension-field invariance

Alman–Vassilevska Williams, *A Refined Laser Method and Faster Matrix
Multiplication*, TheoretiCS 3 (2024), Article 21, printed publication date
4 September 2024, p9 §2.5 and footnote 1, explicitly states that the matrix
multiplication exponent depends only on characteristic. The footnote cites
Schönhage, *Partial and Total Matrix Multiplication*, SIAM J. Comput. 10(3)
(August 1981), pp434–455, DOI10.1137/0210032. I read the 2024 PDF's header
and complete p9, and visually checked p9. Its matrix-tensor recursion and
asymptotic-rank discussion also match the interface used here. Thus exponent
invariance under field extension is established prior, not our discovery.
[Primary PDF](https://theoretics.episciences.org/14213/pdf).

The 1981 publisher landing page verifies the title, author, issue, pages and
an arbitrary-field algorithm claim in the abstract. Its displayed July 2006
online date is not the date of the original result. Full-PDF access failed
with HTTP403; I do not claim direct reading of its purported Theorem2.8.
[Publisher](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0210032).

## Same-rank rationalization is a different interface

Moran–Schwartz–Yuan, *Complex to Rational Fast Matrix Multiplication*,
arXiv2602.13171v1, was submitted13 February2026. The primary abstract page
shows only that archived version. Its ISSAC2026 publication dates are
12 July online/13 July print, according to the conference DOI metadata.
I read introduction/contributions, §§2.1–2.3, §3.1 through Theorem2, and
selected §5 discussion. Its transformations preserve the rank of a given
scheme over characteristic-zero subfields. Specific complex schemes can
fail to have a rational/real equivalent of the same rank. This does not
rule out our descent, which spends a constant number of complete tensor
copies and preserves only the asymptotic exponent. It supplies no general
complex-to-positive-characteristic theorem.
[Primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13171v1).

Alman–Li–Pratt, *The edge of the asymptotic spectrum of tensors*,

## What the new deduction actually needs

Same-characteristic invariance does not make a complex exponent bound
automatically valid in characteristic2 or3. A fixed characteristic-zero
finite rank witness can usually be spread out away from finitely many
bad primes, but for a limiting exponent the exceptional set may depend
on the selected slack and finite witness. That observation alone does
not establish a uniform statement in every characteristic.

Our contribution is the explicit composition with the October2 separator:
primitive roots in an extension of degree O(log M), a linear projection
expanded into at most k³ whole tensor copies, and polynomial overhead for
degeneration interpolation. The separator costs O_q(M log³ M); its
overhead vanishes in the type-entropy limit. For fixed convolution dimensions
one fixed extension before tensor powers gives the exact character bound
lambda(C(a,b))≤a+b−1. The integral determinant/three-sector maps then carry
the source growth argument unchanged. These are deductions from a read
source, not source claims or new generic field-descent principles.
