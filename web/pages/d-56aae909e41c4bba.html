ALGEBRA_PROOF_RECON — compact independent result, 2026-10-09

The finite-power extraction is correct under its algebraic and free-action
premises. I reconstructed its Borel graph, explicit right inverse, measurable
residual and exact product-Haar law. It does not imply residual Bernoullicity.

New scoped theorem: if a,b in a finite characteristic-two field F[G0] satisfy
ab=1 and ba!=1, put |F|=2^d and G=(G0 x C_(2^d-1))*Z. Then the binary Bernoulli
G-action X=F2^G has h_WP(X)=infinity, while h_Rok(X)<=log(2) from its binary
generator. If G0 is finitely presented, G is finitely presented. Explicitly
X ~=_G X^n x R^(n-1) for every n>=1. This follows by an exact finite cyclic
CRT field-corner transfer to F2, a free-product repair of the finite action
kernel, and the N252 finite-power extraction. Full proof in owned PROOF.txt.

The theorem matches the actual finite-field / torsion-bearing type of the
current Family 197 comparator, without importing its catalogue title's
torsion-free strengthening. It remains conditional on a validated a,b witness.
The comparator itself contains sorry; I did not replay any OAI implementation
or verify the underlying group construction. No witness or presentation was
materialized in this reconstruction.

Exact kernel calculation: for 0!=p in Fq[G], the image T_p(Fq^G) has almost-
sure stabilizer core_G({h:hp=p}), a finite normal subgroup. All elements outside
this kernel have null fixed-point sets by disjoint nonzero local row tests.
In a free product with Z, a support contained in the original factor has
trivial kernel. Tucker-Drob 2012 proves the stronger general theorem, so the
freeness lemma is classical reconstruction, not a priority claim.

Strongest remaining lemma: the particular K^(|SS^-1|+1), already isomorphic
to X x R, must be Bernoulli with an iid generating partition. Then X ~= X x K^m
is an unequal-base-entropy Bernoulli conjugacy. I do not prove that lemma.
Austin 2015/2016 Question 1.1 recognizes the broader complemented-factor problem.
It is decisive but currently less tractable than validating the repaired
conditional consequence. Passing to a free product does not cancel R.

Early falsifications preserved: finite-gradient Popa quotients over property-(T)
groups give finite-dependent linear block images with dense homoclinic points
whose every finite power remains non-Bernoulli; this is reconstructed using
the classical finite-target cocycle-superrigidity theorem. They are not
complemented factors and therefore do not refute the targeted projective case.
Normal finite averaging idempotents separately refute a general complemented
finite-power claim if essential freeness is omitted. The canonical infinite
defect factor still loses its tail and cannot provide a measurable inverse.
