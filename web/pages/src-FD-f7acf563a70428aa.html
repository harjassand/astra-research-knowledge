## Exact contract and theorem

Let S and E be finite-dimensional, with dim(S)=d. E may have any finite
dimension. Let H and R=R_S tensor I_E be Hermitian. Suppose supplied, valid
coarse bounds satisfy

    ||R|| <= Rcap,
    ||[H,R]|| <= 2 J ||R||,
    Lambda = 2 J + Rcap > 0.

For CRAY, J is the sum of norms of terms of K0 touching S. Terms internal to
the outside commute with R and need no norm bound. No tensor-product
decomposition of the interactions touching S is required.

Define

    E(t) = exp(iHt) exp(-i(H+R)t),
    rho_tau = I_S/d tensor tau,
    p_tau(t) = Tr[rho_tau (E(t)-I)^dagger(E(t)-I)]/4,

where tau is any outside density operator. At every 0<t<2/Lambda,

    p_tau(t) >= c(t) ||R||^2,
    c(t) = t^2 (1-Lambda t/2)^2/(4d).             (1)

In particular, t=1/Lambda maximizes this sufficient coefficient and gives

    p_tau >= ||R||^2/(16 Lambda^2 d).            (2)

These assertions quantify over every outside state, including pure states and
states conditioned on an arbitrary earlier measurement history.

### Proof

Differentiation gives

    E'(s) = -i exp(iHs) R exp(-i(H+R)s),
    E''(s) = exp(iHs) ([H,R]-R^2) exp(-i(H+R)s).

The supplied bounds and unitary invariance of operator norm imply

    ||E''(s)|| <= (2J+Rcap)||R|| = Lambda ||R||.

Thus, for D(t)=E(t)-I+itR, Taylor's integral formula yields

    ||D(t)|| <= Lambda t^2 ||R||/2.              (3)

If R=0, (1) is immediate. Otherwise choose a unit vector v in S with
R_S v = r v and |r|=||R||. For every unit vector x in E, the reverse triangle
inequality and (3) give

    ||(E(t)-I)(v tensor x)||
      = ||-it r(v tensor x)+D(t)(v tensor x)||
      >= t||R||-||D(t)||
      >= t(1-Lambda t/2)||R||.

The last expression is positive in the claimed time range. Resolve any tau
as a convex combination of pure states. The preceding squared inequality is
valid for each member of the combination, so

    Tr[(|v><v| tensor tau)(E-I)^dagger(E-I)]
      >= t^2(1-Lambda t/2)^2 ||R||^2.

Both (I_S-|v><v|) tensor tau and (E-I)^dagger(E-I) are positive. Their trace
product is nonnegative. Consequently replacing |v><v| by I_S can only increase
the trace. Divide by 4d to obtain (1). The scalar function
t(1-Lambda t/2) is positive on (0,2/Lambda) and is maximized at t=1/Lambda,
which proves (2).

The identity relating p to Ramsey failure is exact: for a unitary E,
(E-I)^dagger(E-I)=2I-E-E^dagger. Therefore
p_tau=(1-Re Tr(rho_tau E))/2, as in the source physical sequence.

## Arbitrary retained memory and confidence

The physical reset must replace S by I/d without leaving its correlations with
the outside. Conditioned on any earlier classical record, ideal reset gives
rho_j=I/d tensor tau_j. The outside may evolve and remain unreset between
trials. It may depend on the entire past. A reference system entangled with it
can be included in E, with the same operator bounds.

Suppose calibrated apparatus errors change each conditional failure probability
by at most gamma, uniformly over all allowed outside states and histories.
With R and the chosen comparison/time fixed for the validation record,

    Pr(Y_j=1 | past) >= q_* = max(0,c(t)||R||^2-gamma).

For fixed predeclared n, a process with those conditional lower bounds dominates
Binomial(n,q_*) in its lower tail. To see this, recursively realize each
conditional failure law by a fresh independent U_j uniform in [0,1]:
Y_j=1{U_j<=p_j(past)}. Then Y_j>=1{U_j<=q_*} pointwise; the comparison bits
are iid Bernoulli(q_*). This is a mathematical coupling, not an assumption that
the experimental bits or outside states are independent.

Let q_up(s;n,alpha) be an upper confidence bound obtained by lower-binomial-tail
inversion for observed failure count s. With coverage at least 1-alpha,

    ||R|| <= min(Rcap, sqrt((q_up+gamma)/c(t))).   (4)

When q_*=0 the conclusion also follows, since then c(t)||R||^2<=gamma.
This argument does not allow unchecked optional stopping, comparison selection
using the validation record, or a mere average gate-infidelity bound in place
of the stated state-uniform gamma.

At t=1/Lambda, (4) is

    ||R|| <= 4 Lambda sqrt(d(q_up+gamma)).        (5)

Conditional on a zero-failure record, q_up=1-alpha^(1/n)<=log(1/alpha)/n.
Thus n >= 16 Lambda^2 d epsilon^(-2) log(1/alpha) is sufficient for the
zero-failure radius epsilon with gamma=0. This is a record-conditional
sufficient budget, not a promise of zero failures or successful sign detection.
The apparatus-only floor in (5) is 4 Lambda sqrt(d gamma); actual failures can
raise it.

## Why the dimension factor cannot simply disappear

Even without an environment, take H=0 and R=epsilon |0><0|. Then the identical
reset/readout experiment has

    p=(1-cos(epsilon t))/(2d).

Against R=0, the null experiment always returns pass. At fixed epsilon t,
the n-shot record distributions have total variation distance
1-(1-p)^n. Therefore order d trials are necessary for a nontrivial uniformly
valid discrimination under this interface. When epsilon t is small, p is
asymptotic to epsilon^2 t^2/(4d). Requiring the probability of the all-pass
record under this alternative to be at most alpha gives exactly
n >= log(alpha)/log(1-p), with leading order
4d epsilon^(-2)t^(-2)log(1/alpha).

This lower bound is scoped to the given maximally mixed reset and X-failure
interface at a fixed interrogation time. It says nothing against deliberately
targeted preparations, phase readout, longer coherent interrogation, or extra
structure. Accordingly the improved d dependence is sharp for this interface;
it is not a universal lower bound on all Hamiltonian-learning protocols.

## Optional spectral refinement

Repeating the proof on every local eigenvector gives the valid identity-free
lower bound

    p_tau >= (1/(4d)) sum_i
      max(t |r_i|-Lambda t^2||R||/2,0)^2.

This can exploit a supplied effective-rank promise. Without a justified
structural spectral promise, the operator-norm certificate uses only its
extremal term. No spectrum is acquired or assumed in (1)-(5).

## Review of the dominated module-norm draft

For finite E, N(O)^2=||Tr_S(O^dagger O)/d|| is a norm because it is the supremum
of weighted Hilbert-Schmidt norms over outside density operators, and a faithful
maximally mixed outside state detects every nonzero O. Left multiplication by a
unitary is isometric. Right multiplication by a product unitary is isometric.
For X=O^dagger O>=0, outside tau>=0,

    Tr[(I tensor tau)(A^dagger tensor B^dagger)X(A tensor B)]
      = Tr[X(AA^dagger tensor B tau B^dagger)]
      <= ||A||^2 Tr[X(I tensor B tau B^dagger)]
      <= ||A||^2 ||B||^2 d N(O)^2.

The first order inequality uses AA^dagger<=||A||^2 I and positivity of B tau
B^dagger. The last uses the defining supremum after normalizing B tau B^dagger;
the zero-trace case is trivial. This establishes the claimed multiplier bound.

For H+R=H_local+V, put U(t)=U_local(t) W(t) where
W'=-i V_I(t)W and V_I(t)=U_local(t)^dagger V U_local(t). The interaction-picture
operator is a sum of product terms with the same sum-of-product-norm budget
kappa. Its chronological Dyson products act on the right of O U_local(t);
the multiplier bound yields N(O U(t))<=exp(kappa t)N(O). No reversal of the
interaction-picture order is needed. Local Hilbert-Schmidt commutator bounds
give N([H,R]-R^2)<=(2J+2kappa+Rcap)sigma. The integrated draft remainder and
its pointwise outside-state inference follow. Its constants 36 and 6 are safe.

At the level of the operator-norm certificate, the new bound dominates: at the
same Lambda, f_kappa(t)>=t^2/2, so t-Lambda f_kappa(t) is no larger than
t(1-Lambda t/2). The draft's stronger bound involving the full sigma can remain
useful when extra spectral/Hilbert-Schmidt structure is supplied; this review
does not claim every consequence of that separate theorem is dominated.

One cannot replace kappa by ||V|| in that multiplier proof: for equal dimensions
take V=SWAP and O=|0><0| tensor I. Then ||V||=1 but
N(OV)/N(O)=sqrt(d). This refutes the unjustified multiplier substitution; it
does not refute the stronger null theorem above, which uses a different proof.

## Remaining boundaries

The argument assumes finite dimensions and a true collar/outside factorization;
unbounded hosts require separate domain and convergence work. It assumes the
comparison residual is collar-supported or that tails/model errors have been
charged. It inherits coherent branch control, calibrated reset/readout/timing,

Useful local witnesses are still not generic. It provides neither a physical
