| Heinosaari, Kiukas, Reitzner, Schultz, [“Incompatibility breaking quantum channels,” arXiv:1504.05768](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.05768), §§3–5, especially Definitions 2–4, Proposition 6, Proposition 7, Theorem 2 | Defines n-incompatibility breaking and all-incompatibility breaking. Shows EB channels are incompatibility breaking, but the converse fails; also constructs strict levels of the finite-n hierarchy. Proposition 6 identifies rank-one POVMs with at most d² outcomes as a *complete incompatibility set*, in the sense of a single joint measurement for that entire set. | A K-output broadcaster makes any chosen family of at most K output POVMs jointly measurable after the channel power. This does not make the power K-IBC for arbitrary K-independent families, and incompatibility breaking is weaker than EB. The paper does not state a common EB map or an operator-order/Dirichlet bound. The complete-set proposition cannot be invoked from only finite-wise compatibility. |
| Heinosaari, Miyadera, [“Incompatibility of quantum channels,” arXiv:1608.01794](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.01794), Proposition 4 and §4.3 | Channel compatibility is existence of a joint broadcasting channel. A channel is self-compatible iff it is below a conjugate channel in the concatenation preorder, equivalently antidegradable. Channels that are n-self-compatible for every n are exactly EB (see the n-self-compatibility discussion in §4.3). | Confirms the exact input interface and that 2-self-compatibility/antidegradability is weaker than EB. Iterating a supplied two-output broadcaster gives a 2^m-output extension of Φ^m, not an extension of Φ with 2^m outputs. This structural characterization does not give a quantitative EB comparator for Φ. |
| Xie, Fang, Wang, Duan, [“Approximate broadcasting of quantum correlations,” arXiv:1705.06071](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.06071), Lemma 3, Eq. (3) | For a CPTP map from A into n outputs and any 0<δ<1, one common input POVM and at least n(1−δ) output positions give EB channels E_j with diamond distance at most 3((ln(2)|A|^6 log₂|A|)/(nδ³))^(1/3) from each corresponding marginal. | This is the closest channel-level finite-copy result found. It is an absolute diamond-norm approximation for most output marginals, with explicit dimension/copy dependence. The tree candidate instead gives a relative quadratic-form domination of I−Φ by a single canonical EB comparator, with K≈d², and makes no diamond-norm claim. The Xie bound at K≈d² is not a useful small-error substitute. |
| Hastings, [“Random unitaries give quantum expanders,” Phys. Rev. A 76, 032315 (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.76.032315); Junge and Wang, [“Generalized Poincare inequality for quantum Markov semigroups,” arXiv:2601.06005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06005) | Quantum expanders use a nontrivial spectral gap to force rapid mixing. The 2026 Poincare paper proves noncommutative (p,p)-Poincare inequalities for trace-symmetric quantum Markov semigroups under a spectral-gap assumption. | These support a simple stronger special case: if the HS-selfadjoint channel has gap γ on traceless operators, the depolarizing EB channel Δ already satisfies I−Δ ≤ γ^−1(I−Φ). This is an elementary spectral consequence, not a new expander theorem. Poincare/Markov-type inequalities do not supply the general EB comparator when arbitrarily slow modes remain. |
| Hanson, Rouzé, Stilck França, [“Eventually Entanglement Breaking Markovian Dynamics,” arXiv:1902.08173](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.08173), discrete-time results; Park, [“Every PPT channel has finite entanglement breaking index,” arXiv:2608.13551v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13551), Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 | Hanson et al. give eventual-EB criteria and index bounds under faithfulness/mixing hypotheses. Park proves every PPT map has finite EB index and proves index at most 3 for the PPT cone class DSP₂. | If Φ^n is EB and Φ is HS-selfadjoint unital/TP, Ψ=Φ^n is a comparator with I−Ψ ≤ n(I−Φ), by scalar calculus on spec(Φ)⊂[−1,1]. Thus PPT DSP₂ channels have c≤3. This covers a structured subclass, not all self-compatible channels; no general log-d comparator is asserted by these papers. Park is an August 2026 arXiv preprint, not peer-reviewed status claimed here. |
| Barnum, Caves, Fuchs, Jozsa, Schumacher, [“Noncommuting mixed states cannot be broadcast,” arXiv:quant-ph/9511010](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9511010) | Exact broadcasting of a set of states is possible iff the states commute pairwise. | Qualitative exact no-broadcasting is not a quantitative approximate Dirichlet domination theorem. It does explain why commuting test families are exactly EB-measurable without using the tree. |

This is a negative search result, not proof of novelty.
