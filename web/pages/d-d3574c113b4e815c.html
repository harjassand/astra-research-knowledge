UNEQUAL-SPIN POSITIVE COHERENT SYMBOL AND TP MASS SYMMETRIZATION
2026-10-08 | /root/sol_collision_precision

STATUS

INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE two reusable finite lemmas. They extend the
single-carrier flag argument to positive full matrices with unequal-spin
rectangles, and preserve an archive's trace-preserving mass constraint
after positive modulus reduction. They are not an iid converse by themselves.
Independent/external correctness and priority UNKNOWN.

1. EXACT POSITIVE MATRIX SYMBOL

Let E=direct_sum_lambda Sym^(k_lambda)(C2) with integers k_lambda>=0,
r_lambda=k_lambda+1. At a unit spinor u choose coherent isometries
V_(lambda,u):C->Sym^(k_lambda)(C2), z->z |u^(k_lambda)>.
Let V_u=direct_sum V_(lambda,u):C^(labels)->E and
D_r=diag r_lambda^(1/4). Define the matrix symbol

  T(f)(u)=D_r V_u^dag f V_u D_r.                         (1.1)

For f>=0, T(f)(u)>=0. Its HS norm g(u)=||T(f)(u)||_HS is invariant under
the phase choice of u, because u->exp(i theta)u conjugates T by the
diagonal sector unitary diag exp(i k_lambda theta). Hence g is a scalar
function on CP1. Offdiagonal symbols are retained; they are not replaced
by a sum of diagonal symbols.

With uniform CP1 probability, the integrated adjoint square is

  T^dag T(f)=S_v(f):=int C_u f C_u du,
  C_u=direct_sum_lambda sqrt(r_lambda) P_(lambda,u),
  P_(lambda,u)=|u^(k_lambda)><u^(k_lambda)|.              (1.2)

Since int P_(lambda,u)=I/r_lambda, int C_u^2=I_E.
S_v is CP, unital, TP, self-adjoint and positive as an HS operator,
therefore an HS contraction. Thus T contracts the FULL matrix HS norm
after integration over u, including every unequal-spin rectangle.
It is not pointwise HS contracting at a fixed u.

2. EXACT RECTANGULAR MULTIPLIERS WITHOUT A CG TABLE

On a rectangle Sym^v(C2)->Sym^u(C2), assume u>=v by adjoint symmetry.
The commutator SU2 action is J^(u) B-B J^(v), decomposing with multiplicity
one into spins ell=delta+s, delta=(u-v)/2, s=0,...,v. A highest operator
for this component in two-mode oscillator notation is

  B_s=(a_1^dag)^(u-v+s) a_2^s:Sym^v->Sym^u.

It has weight 2ell and is annihilated by the raising commutator, since
a_1^dag and a_2 both commute with J_+=a_1^dag a_2. These highest vectors
give all v+1 components by the standard SU2 ladder, whose dimensions
sum to (u+1)(v+1).

The normalized coherent matrix coefficient is exactly

  <z^u,B_s z^v>
    =sqrt(u!v!)/(v-s)! (conjugate(z1))^(u-v+s) z2^s.

Uniform CP1 integration uses |z1|^2 uniform on [0,1], so its squared
integral is

  [u!v!/(v-s)!^2] (u-v+s)! s!/(u-v+2s+1)!.

On the number basis n1+n2=v,

  ||B_s||_HS^2
   =sum_(n1=0..v-s)
      [(v-n1)!/(v-n1-s)!] [(n1+u-v+s)!/n1!]
   =s!(u-v+s)! binom(u+s+1,u-v+2s+1).                  (2.1)

The final identity is ordinary Vandermonde: multiply the generating
functions (1-z)^(-(s+1)) and (1-z)^(-(u-v+s+1)) and take the coefficient
of degree v-s. Dividing the symbol integral, with its normalization
sqrt((u+1)(v+1)), by (2.1) gives the EXACT multiplier

  b_(delta+s)=sqrt((u+1)(v+1))u!v!/
                                      [(v-s)!(u+s+1)!]. (2.2)

In particular b_delta=sqrt((v+1)/(u+1)), and

  b_(delta+s)=b_delta prod_(j=0..s-1)(v-j)/(u+j+2).

All multipliers lie in (0,1]. The unequal-spin coherent symbol is not
an isometry even on its lowest delta component; that loss is explicit.

Since 1-(1+x)^(-1/2)<=x/2 for x>=0 and 1-prod q_j<=sum(1-q_j),

  1-b_delta<=delta/(v+1),
  1-b_(delta+s)
   <=delta/(v+1)+s(2delta+s+1)/(v+2)
   <=ell(ell+1)/(v+1).                                 (2.3)

The last step uses ell(ell+1)=delta(delta+1)+s(2delta+s+1).
No small delta or fixed-spin approximation is made.

3. FULL RECTANGULAR NORM LOSS, VERTICAL ENERGY AND TRACE WEIGHT

Let E_SU2(f)=sum_(i=1..3)||J_i f-f J_i||_HS^2 on E. Its rectangle
Casimir eigenvalue is ell(ell+1), and in particular a k_lambda,k_mu
rectangle has minimum delta(delta+1), delta=|k_lambda-k_mu|/2.
For r_min=min_lambda r_lambda, (2.3) gives

  ||T f||_L2HS^2>=||f||_HS^2-E_SU2(f)/r_min.            (3.1)

For f>=0, define g(u)=||T f(u)||_HS. Then

  int g<=int Tr T f
          =sum_lambda Tr f_(lambda,lambda)/sqrt(r_lambda). (3.2)

This trace inequality bounds all rectangular symbols because T f is
positive as a matrix on the label space. It does not discard sector phases.

To bound the scalar vertical energy, work first on SU2 rather than choose
a singular CP1 phase gauge. For h in SU2,
T_h(f)=D_r V_0^dag pi(h)^dag f pi(h) V_0 D_r. The HS norm is invariant
under the right U1 stabilizer and therefore descends to CP1. Differentiating
its norm and using |d||X||||<=||dX|| pointwise, then the integrated symbol
contraction from Section 1, gives

  E_(CP1,scalar)(g)<=E_SU2(f).                          (3.3)

The scalar generator normalization has eigenvalues ell(ell+1) for integer
CP1 harmonics. Right-U1 charges in individual matrix entries do not add
scalar heat dimensions, since their phase-conjugation drops from the HS norm.
At zeros of the norm, the inequality holds by the usual Lipschitz/weak
derivative bound or by replacing ||X|| by sqrt(||X||^2+eta^2) and taking
eta down to zero. The CP1 function is finite smooth-symbol data before
this norm, so no domain difficulty is hidden.

If these f are the cold sections f(v)=D_a W_v^dag F W_v D_a with F>=0,
then the full flag scalar g(v,u)=||T f(v)(u)|| has

  ||g||_1<=sum_lambda Tr F_(lambda,lambda)/
                                           sqrt(a_lambda r_lambda).

On a band with a_lambda>=a_min and r_lambda>=r_min this is at most
sqrt(Q/(a_min r_min)) ||F||_HS whenever rank F<=Q. Horizontal unitary
plane transport sends each coherent line to the corresponding line with
a sector phase; conjugation by that label-space phase is unitary. The
reverse triangle inequality followed by integrated T contraction therefore
transfers the true horizontal connection energy to g, as in JOINT_FLAG_NASH.

4. POSITIVE MODULUS PAIRS PRESERVE APPROXIMATE TP MASS

Let sigma>0 be a trace-one reference on a finite H. For a TP channel
with rank-Q composite Kraus A_i put B_i=A_i sqrt(sigma). Then

  sum_i B_i^dag B_i=sigma,
  sum_i B_i B_i^dag=Phi(sigma),
  sum_i||B_i||_HS^2=1.                                 (4.1)

Replace each B_i by the TWO positive matrices

  F_(i,1)=|B_i|/sqrt(2), F_(i,2)=|B_i^dag|/sqrt(2).

Every F has rank<=Q, sum||F||_HS^2=1, and exactly

  sum_(i,z) F_(i,z)^2=[sigma+Phi(sigma)]/2.              (4.2)

For every Hermitian field L_x the pointwise SVD commutator identity
proves

  sum_(i,z)int||[L_x,F_(i,z)]||^2
                       <=sum_i int||[L_x,B_i]||^2.     (4.3)

The same pair works simultaneously for several score fields. It is NOT
necessary, and generally invalid, to choose one modulus separately and
claim it preserves the reference. Equation (4.2) keeps both.

If the actual ensemble mean is sigma and its average half-trace code error
is e, then T(Phi(sigma),sigma)<=e by convexity. Thus every orthogonal
sector projector P_C obeys

  sum_(i,z)||F_(i,z)P_C||_HS^2
       =Tr P_C[sigma+Phi(sigma)]/2
       >=Tr P_C sigma-e/2.                             (4.4)

This is a legal mass constraint, not free conditioning on a constant-mass
input subset. It can prevent a rank variational converse from being killed
by rare small-spin sectors, but transport out of C must still be bounded
before a projection onto a broader band is used.

5. SCOPE AND COSTS

The lemmas involve finite representation/linear algebra only. They provide
no input-dependent frame to an archive, no code, no finite alphabet upper,
no gate/runtime theorem and no all-Q capacity by themselves. Whole matrix
rank and all complex rectangles are retained before positive symmetrization.
The normalized trace weights, unequal-spin loss and exact approximate mass
constraint are the imported interfaces for an actual iid proof.

SU2 ladder/coherent symbols and Nash reasoning are established prior. The
oscillator/Vandermonde calculation above proves every multiplier used here
without an unread CG coefficient or a numerical extrapolation. Dedicated
priority and external correctness remain UNKNOWN. No routine old finite
suite was rerun. Falsifiers are a wrong oscillator highest vector or
Vandermonde norm, wrong right-U1 invariance, loss of the stationary volume,
or using (4.4) while ignoring energetically unbounded output transport.
