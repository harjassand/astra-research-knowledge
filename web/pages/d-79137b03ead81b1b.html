THEOREM

Fix any finite-dimensional B and any density sigma_B tracial on its
support P, so sigma_B=P/r with r=rank P. Fix an ACTUAL full-domain CPTP
broadcaster C:B -> B_1 tensor B_2, with B_j isomorphic to B. There is
ONE full-domain EB CPTP Lambda_B:B -> B, depending only on C and sigma_B,
that preserves sigma_B and has the following simultaneous property.
For EVERY finite-dimensional quantum reference A and EVERY density rho_AB
with rho_B=sigma_B, define
 b=max_{j=1,2} (1/2)||(id_A tensor C_j)(rho_AB)-rho_AB||_1,
 I=I(A:B)_rho=D(rho_AB||rho_A tensor sigma_B) in NATS.
Then, for 0<b<1,
 T((id_A tensor Lambda_B)rho_AB,rho_AB)
 <=min{1,72 sqrt[(I+1)/ln(1/b)]}.                        (T)
At b=0 the SAME channel reconstructs rho_AB exactly. At b=1 use 1.
In particular sup I<infinity and b->0 imply vanishing LOCAL EB error
uniformly in BOTH dimensions. A is preserved as a quantum reference.

This is not an unconstrained diamond-norm bound. All tested joint states
have the specified B marginal, and their actual input-state broadcasting
errors and mutual information enter the estimate. The selected Lambda_B
receives no A system, state label, or conditional ensemble at runtime.
No independent copies, short classical alphabet, efficient acquisition,
or closeness to a particular classical-on-B basis state is asserted.

1. LOCAL SUPPORT REDUCTION AND ONE FIXED MARGINAL

Every rho_AB with rho_B=sigma_B is supported on A tensor PB; similarly
rho_A is faithful after restricting A to its support, purely as an
analytic domain restriction. Define the actual local CPTP support map
 R_P(X)=PXP+Tr[(I-P)X]sigma_B, with codomain M_r.
It fixes every such rho_AB when tensored with id_A. Replace C by
(R_P tensor R_P)C restricted to PB. Its joint marginal errors cannot
increase. We henceforth work on this B support, where sigma_B=I/r.
A channel on PB extends to the original B by composition with R_P,
preserving EB and all joint reconstruction conclusions.

If r=1, every target rho_AB is rho_A tensor sigma_B and the constant
preparation channel reconstructs it exactly. Hence assume r>=2.
Symmetrize the two compressed outputs by a random swap, obtaining one
common marginal T_B=(C_1+C_2)/2. Its joint residual is <=b. By partial
trace over A,
 T(T_B sigma_B,sigma_B)<=b.
The actual symmetric joint channel witnesses T_B's selfcompatibility.

2. GLOBAL RELATIVE ENTROPY AND COMPATIBLE SMOOTHING

For a given target rho_AB, put omega=rho_A tensor sigma_B. This is
faithful on the support just described and D(rho||omega)=I. Fix any
epsilon in (0,1), let D=I+1, and set
 eta=epsilon^2/8,
 M=(1-eta)^(-1) exp[(I+ln2)/eta], lambda=eta/M.
Minimize over 0<=tau<=M omega, Tr tau<=1 the strictly convex objective
 J(tau)=-f(rho,tau)+lambda Q_omega(tau).
Let G be its unique minimizer. By JN, a normalized feasible tau0 has
f(rho,tau0)>=1-eta and Q_omega(tau0)<=M. Thus minimality gives
f(rho,G)>=1-2eta, and t=TrG>=f(rho,G)^2>=1-4eta>1/2.
The normalized hatrho=G/t therefore satisfies
 hatrho<=2M omega, T(rho,hatrho)<=epsilon.
The smoother is an ANALYTIC state comparison, not a nonlinear physical
preprocessing map applied by the final device.

For any CPTP endomorphism F with d_r=||F rho-rho||_1,
d_s=||F omega-omega||_1, the exact compatible-smoothing estimate is
 ||F G-G||_1
 <=(1+sqrt2)sqrt[xi/lambda]+2M sqrt(d_s)+M d_s,
 xi=sqrt(d_r)+(sqrt M+2lambda M^2)sqrt(d_s)+(M/2)d_s.   (S)
Its proof uses strict quadratic convexity, fidelity monotonicity, B2,
and comparison of the unique minimizer under changing rho,omega. Reference
transport tau=sqrt(omega)H sqrt(omega) -> sqrt(nu)H sqrt(nu), with
0<=H<=MI and trace clipping, gives the exact remainder terms. Since
the estimate is for an arbitrary CPTP endomorphism on arbitrary finite
matrices, it applies directly to F=id_A tensor T_B on AB. It does NOT
require a broadcaster on AB or a duplicated A.

Here d_r,d_s<=2b, including d_s=||rho_A tensor(T_B sigma_B-sigma_B)||_1.
Normalize by t>1/2. The same local marginal therefore has half residual
on hatrho at most
 beta(b)=(1+sqrt2)sqrt[Xi(b)/lambda]+2M sqrt(2b)+2M b,
 Xi(b)=(1+sqrt M+2lambda M^2)sqrt(2b)+M b.
On the included omega the smoother equals omega exactly: f(omega,tau)
<=sqrt(Trtau), Q_omega(tau)>=(Trtau)^2, and -sqrt t+lambda t² decreases
on [0,1] because lambda<1/8. Thus the same estimate also bounds the
reference residual. For 0<=b<=1,
 Xi(b)<=6M sqrt b,
 beta(b)<=24M epsilon^(-1)b^(1/4).                     (B)
This follows from lambda=epsilon²/(8M), sqrt M<=M and b<=sqrt b;
the scalar prefactor is (1+sqrt2)sqrt48+2sqrt2+2<24.

4. LOCAL PETZ CORRECTION AND EXACT TENSOR FACTORIZATION

Let nu_B=T_B sigma_B, P_nu=supp nu_B, and define a full-domain local
CPTP transpose map
 R_B(Y)=sqrt(sigma_B) T_B*(nu_B^(-1/2)P_nu Y P_nu
                         nu_B^(-1/2))sqrt(sigma_B)
                +Tr[(I-P_nu)Y]sigma_B.
It is CP, TP, and R_B(nu_B)=sigma_B. Since sigma_B is faithful,
ALL T_B outputs have support P_nu: T_B*(I-P_nu)>=0 has zero expectation
against faithful sigma_B and hence is zero. Consequently the completion
term never occurs in R_B T_B.

On AB, the reference output is rho_A tensor nu_B. Its transpose map's
first term factors as id_A tensor the displayed first term of R_B;
the rho_A square roots and inverse square roots cancel. Choose the
LOCAL completion id_A tensor R_B, which preserves A. The usual global
completion resetting all of AB would differ off the output support,
but both compositions with id_A tensor T_B agree exactly. This avoids
silently replacing the quantum reference by rho_A after a support event.

Put S_B=R_B T_B. It fixes sigma_B, is CPTP, and is selfcompatible
via applying R_B to both outputs of the supplied local joint channel.
For G_sigma(X)=sigma^(1/4)X sigma^(1/4), B2 gives a contraction
V=G_nu^(-1)T_B G_sigma; direct adjoint calculation gives
 G_sigma^(-1)S_B G_sigma=V*V, 0<=V*V<=I.
Because sigma_B is tracial, S_B is therefore HS-self-adjoint and
HS-positive; stationarity makes it unital as well as TP. All U
hypotheses now hold on B alone.

The product-reference Petz recovery estimate on the global sandwich u
is applicable to id_A tensor S_B, precisely because this composition
is the product transpose correction just checked. Its FULL residual is
 ||(id_A tensor S_B)u-u||_1
 <=p_theta(z):=sqrt[8theta z+6theta² sqrt(2z)].          (P)
For completeness, the estimate follows from

5. AMPLIFIED C4 AND JOINT TRACE RECONSTRUCTION

Choose ONE canonical Psi_B from U for this fixed S_B. This selection
depends only on C and sigma_B, BEFORE rho_AB,A,epsilon are chosen.
It is EB, unital, HS-self-adjoint and 0<=Psi_B<=I. The real Hermitian
B quadratic-form comparison amplifies to the full Hermitian AB space:
expand in a Hermitian HS-orthonormal basis on A; each coefficient is a
Hermitian B operator. Hence
 I-id_A tensor Psi_B <=4(I-id_A tensor S_B).

Let G_omega(X)=omega^(1/4)X omega^(1/4). Since sigma_B is tracial,
both B maps commute with G_omega: its nontrivial congruence is only on
A, where both maps are identity. Put X=G_omega^(-1)u. For Hermitian
Y, ||Y||_1²<=Q_omega(Y), by Schatten Holder and Tromega=1. Then
 ||(id_A tensor Psi_B)u-u||_1²
 <= <X,(I-id_A tensor Psi_B)^2 X>_HS
 <=4<X,(I-id_A tensor S_B)X>_HS
 =4 Tr[(omega^(-1/2)u omega^(-1/2))
            (u-(id_A tensor S_B)u)]
 <=4theta ||u-(id_A tensor S_B)u||_1.
The last inequality subtracts the identity from the bounded likelihood,
uses its norm <=theta, and uses that the residual has trace zero.
Thus the ACTUAL JOINT half trace reconstruction error on u is
 <=sqrt[theta p_theta(z)].                             (C)
No LOCC norm or measurement on A replaced the joint trace norm.

Psi_B preserves sigma_B, so id_A tensor Psi_B fixes omega EXACTLY.
Affine inversion therefore makes its error on hatrho equal to the error
on u divided by a. Two applications of trace contraction transfer from
hatrho to rho at cost <=2epsilon. The same fixed Psi_B satisfies
 e_rho(Psi_B)<=2epsilon+a^(-1)sqrt[theta p_theta(z)]
 <=2epsilon+(24/5)M[24M epsilon^(-1)b^(1/4)]^(1/8).    (E)
Indeed for theta=1/2,z<=1, p_theta(z)<=(5/2)z^(1/4), so
sqrt[theta p_theta(z)]<=sqrt(5)/2 z^(1/8)<=(6/5)z^(1/8);
and a^-1<=4M. Clipping z means no small-beta condition was omitted.

6. EXPLICIT LOGARITHMIC OPTIMIZATION AND THE ZERO ENDPOINT

M<=2exp[8D/epsilon²] and 24^(1/8)<3/2, 2^(9/8)<3 give
 e_rho(Psi_B)<=2epsilon+
       24 epsilon^(-1/8) exp[9D/epsilon²] b^(1/32).     (E2)
For 0<b<1 let L=ln(1/b). If L<=576D then 72sqrt(D/L)>=3>1,
so the trivial trace bound proves (T). Otherwise choose epsilon²=576D/L,
which is admissible. The second term R in (E2) is
 R=24epsilon^(-1/8) exp(-L/64).
Its ratio to epsilon is 24[L/(576D)]^(9/16)exp(-L/64).
The logarithmic derivative is (9/16)/L-1/64<0 for L>=576D>=576.
At the left endpoint the ratio is <=24exp(-9)<1. Hence R<=epsilon,
and e_rho<=3epsilon=72sqrt(D/L). Combining with e_rho<=1 proves (T).

When b=0, (E) gives e_rho(Psi_B)<=2epsilon for EVERY epsilon in (0,1).
Psi_B was fixed before epsilon, so letting epsilon down to zero gives
e_rho=0 for that SAME map. There is no epsilon-dependent compact-selection
change of channels at this endpoint. Extend Psi_B by R_P to obtain the
single original-full-domain Lambda_B stated in the theorem.

7. WHAT REMAINS FOR THE FULL NONTRACIAL CONTRACT

With arbitrary faithful sigma_B, product-reference smoothing and local
Petz STILL work, without assuming any spectrum bound. However S_B is
positive self-adjoint only for the sigma_B-weighted KMS/Q norm. It need
not be HS-self-adjoint or unital. The amplified C4 comparison is ordinary
HS, so Step 5 is NOT justified: G_omega no longer commutes with S_B or
an arbitrary C4 comparator. A non-TP whitening congruence does not fix
this physical-channel mismatch. A broad weighted-KMS comparison or a
LOCAL centralization theorem in JOINT trace norm would close the gate;
neither is asserted here. The existing whole-state centralization proof
uses a full two-state broadcaster and cannot be inserted for AB when
only B can be broadcast. Conditional ensembles or k-extensions also do
not fulfill that missing local/reference-preserving premise.

The full candidate remains OPEN in this freeze. The tracial-B restriction
is a substantive reference-preserving consequence of U, different from
the already-known exact unilocal broadcasting/zero-discord theorem, but
no claim about historical priority or overall foundational importance
is made before primary comparison and independent hostile review.
