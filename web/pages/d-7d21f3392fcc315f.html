# Fixed observation scale and linear placement bits by cancelling extra moments

7 October 2026. Worker `charges_design9`. **Complete internal derivation; root independent mathematical gate PASS:** `notes/root_cycle9/FIXED_SCALE_CHARGE_GATE.md`. This is a scale and precision improvement of the adopted equal-charge compiler. The source count remains O(D²), the equilibrium count remains cN^(3/2), and finite direction acquisition remains exponential. Historical novelty is UNKNOWN.

## Exact question and interface

Must the physical observation scale shrink exponentially with the degree, and must rational source coordinates have O(D²) bits, in the audited equal-unit-charge harmonic-grid construction? No: those two costs came from cancelling moments only through the target degree. Cancelling a constant multiple of that degree removes both costs.

Input: m≥1, k=4m, D=64k=256m, and the specified rational harmonic polynomial P_m of degree at most D, with the quantitative critical-point witnesses in `notes/harmonic_grid_audit/audit.md`. Choose L=5D. Let C be the fixed dimension-2 design-existence constant in Bondarenko–Radchenko–Viazovska, *Well-separated spherical designs*, arXiv:1303.5991v2, Theorem 1. Give an integer N>C(2L)². One may choose N=O(D²).

Output: N distinct rational sites carrying strengths exactly +1, with at least 8m³ nondegenerate critical points. The retained critical points occur within the fixed physical ball B_(1/4), with grid spacing of order 1/D. Each coordinate has O(D+log N) bits, and the total output description has O(N(D+log N)) bits. In the optimal-count regime this is O(D³), improving the previous O(D⁴) output bound.

Directions are acquired by the existing finite rational-net search at degree L. The candidate count remains exp(O(N log(NL))), hence exp(O(D² log D)) for N=O(D²). No efficient design oracle is assumed or established. The post-acquisition radial arithmetic is polynomial in N,D and now uses only O(D+log D) placement/iteration precision.

We prove only the retained critical points nondegenerate. A globally Morse configuration is a separate generic real perturbation statement; this note does not claim a rational bit bound for that added requirement.

## 1. Acquired direction interface and radial conditioning

Use real harmonics through L, with M=L(L+2), evaluation matrix E, b=E1/N, S=EEᵀ/N, and Lambda_(lm)=l+1. Acquire N distinct rational unit directions satisfying

    S > (1/2) I,       ||b||₂ ≤ r_L/8,
    r_L = 1/[256(L+1)^5].                         (1)

The rational stereographic search and exact rational Gram/mean tests in `notes/charge_precision/POLYNOMIAL_BIT_COMPILER.md` apply with D replaced by L. An exact 2L-design guarantees a passing rational net set exists. Its mesh and rational node height are

    delta_L = 2^(-14)(L+1)^(-7),
    Q_net = 2^18 (N+2)(L+1)^7,
    coordinate height O(log(NL)).                 (2)

The same right inverse and contraction now act on the L-jet:

    B = -Eᵀ S^(-1) Lambda^(-1),
    ||B||_(2→infinity) ≤ L+1,
    Lip(T_z) ≤ 1/4 on ||h||₂≤r_L.               (3)

For ||z||₂≤r_L/4, the contraction maps that ball into the radius-r_L/2 ball. Therefore an exact solution exists, all radial movements satisfy

    |t_i| ≤ (L+1)r_L = 1/[256(L+1)^4] < 1/8,

and every exact source radius is greater than 7/8. This slightly stronger radius floor supplies the placement-distance margin needed at a larger fixed observation scale. It follows directly from the existing inverse bounds, rather than assuming a tighter design theorem.

## 2. Target degree D and cancelled degree L are different

Write P_m=P_0+sum_(l=1)^D sum_j p_lj |y|^lY_lj(y/|y|). Set

    rho = 1/8,
    epsilon = 2^(-(D+1)),
    eta = epsilon rho^D 2^(-(D+12))
        = 2^(-(5D+13)).                          (4)

Request moments

    z_lj = (2l+1)epsilon rho^(D-l)p_lj,   1≤l≤D,
    z_lj = 0,                           D<l≤L.   (5)

The high zero block is essential. Changing only epsilon or rho in the old degree-D inverse would not produce this conclusion. The normalizing amplitude is epsilon rho^D, even though the inverse cancels through L.

The grid coefficient bound already reconstructed from its 24 null waves is

    S_P = [sum_(l,j) (2l+1)²p_lj²]^(1/2)
        ≤3(2D+1)e^(2k).

Because rho≤1, ||z||₂≤epsilon S_P. Since e²<8 and D=64k,

    epsilon S_P < 3(2D+1)2^(-61k-1).

It suffices for the radial gate that

    1536(5D+1)^5(2D+1) ≤ 2^(61k).             (6)

At k=1 this is the explicit integer comparison

    1536·321^5·129 < 2^61.

When k increases by one, each of the six linear factors on the left grows by less than 2, so that side grows by less than 2^6, whereas the right grows by 2^61. Thus (6) holds for every integer k≥1, including the actual k=4m. This proves ||z||₂≤r_L/4 without floating arithmetic.

## 3. Entire Coulomb tail on a fixed ball

Let a_i be the exact inverse sources and V(x)=N^(-1)sum_i |x-a_i|^(-1). The matching identity is now

    [V(rho y)-V(0)]/(epsilon rho^D)
        = P_m(y)-P_0 + E(y),                   (7)

where E starts at multipole degree L+1. Every source radius exceeds 7/8, in particular 3/4. On B_3 in y-space the Legendre expansion has geometric ratio at most 4rho=1/2. Therefore its unscaled tail supremum is bounded by

    3(4rho)^(L+1).

Here 3 is larger than (4/3)/(1-1/2)=8/3. Dividing by the actual amplitude in (7) gives

    ||E||_(infinity,B3) ≤3·4^(L+1)rho^(L+1-D)/epsilon.

E is harmonic on a neighborhood of B_3. The normalized-sphere Poisson derivative kernels on unit balls centered in B_2 bound the gradient by 3 times this supremum and the Hessian operator norm by 10 times it. Consequently each derivative error is at most

    tau = 30·4^(L+1)rho^(L+1-D)/epsilon
        = 30·2^(-D),                          (8)

because L=5D and rho=2^(-3): (2L+2)-3(L+1-D)+(D+1)=-D.

This is the decisive cancellation: the unused block D+1,...,5D supplies four D degrees of additional tail decay while consuming only a fixed factor in source count. There is no assumption on unprescribed high moments beyond the uniform source-radius floor.

## 4. Rational placement and all rounding costs

Use the rational harmonic coordinates and rounded contraction from the precision compiler, again at degree L. Set gamma=eta/[100(L+1)], round each g-coordinate to a dyadic mesh of width at most gamma/[(L+1)(2L+1)], and take n iterations with 4^(-n)r_L≤gamma. Their fixed-point error satisfies

    ||g_n-g_*||_(nu^(-1)) ≤4^(-n)r_L+4gamma/3
                            ≤7gamma/3.

Applying B gives radial error ≤7eta/300<eta/4. Round each radius onto the dyadic eta/4 grid. Each final rational source lies within eta/2 of its exact source and remains on its acquired rational unit ray. Distinct positive rays give distinct sites. No minimum inter-ray separation is needed.

The final radial denominator has exponent 5D+15. Intermediate g mesh precision and the iteration count are O(D+log D); factorial-size harmonic norms and rational right-inverse entries contribute polynomial intermediate bits exactly as in the adopted compiler. The rational resets prevent denominator explosion.

For |y|≤2, |rho y|≤1/4. Every source-to-observation line segment has distance at least 7/8−1/4−eta>1/2. The kernel derivative estimates give unscaled gradient error at most 16eta and Hessian error at most 384eta after normalization by N. In y-coordinates normalized as in (7), these are respectively

    16rho eta/(epsilon rho^D),
    384rho² eta/(epsilon rho^D).

For rho=1/8 the larger coefficient is 384rho²=6. By (4), both errors are at most

    (3/2048)·2^(-D).                           (9)

Thus a common C² derivative error bound for both the entire tail and source rounding is

    Delta ≤(30+3/2048)2^(-D) <31·2^(-D).       (10)

Rational stereographic coordinates from (2) have denominators at most 9Q_net² and numerator magnitudes at most that scale. Multiplication by the rounded radius adds 5D+O(1) to their coordinate heights. Hence source coordinates have O(D+log(ND)) bits, as claimed. This is an output-height assertion; it does not erase the polynomial-size matrices or the exponential direction enumeration.

## 5. Every retained grid point survives

The audited grid uses witness radius s=e^(-7k)/(10000k) and frozen Hessian inverse norm H_inv≤2k^(-2)e^(3k). The needed perturbation threshold is

    s/(16H_inv) = k e^(-10k)/320000.

Since e<4, that threshold exceeds k2^(-20k)/320000. From D=64k, (10) is below it whenever

    9,920,000 < k2^(44k).

The inequality holds at k=1 because 9,920,000<2^44 and strengthens with k. Thus the new derivative error is below the same threshold used in the independent polynomial-bit compiler gate.

For completeness, the grid Newton map had derivative norm below 1/4 and center movement at most s/2. Adding (10) increases center movement by at most s/16 and derivative norm by at most s/16<1/16. The resulting map contracts by less than 5/16 and maps each witness ball into its concentric radius-7s/8 ball. Banach supplies one critical point in each of the 8m³ disjoint balls. The symmetric Hessian interpolation stays nonsingular by the Neumann criterion, so all retained points have the original index 2.

Their physical centers are rho·(t_i,t_j,t_l), t_j=(j+1/2)pi/k, hence lie within radius sqrt(3)pi/32<1/4. The physical center separation is pi/(8k), of order 1/D. Their certified physical witness radii are still rho s, exponentially small in k: this note improves the observation scale and required placement bits, but does not claim a fixed physical noise tolerance.

## 6. Source-count versus scale tradeoff and all-N padding

The identical argument gives three convenient choices, all with epsilon=2^(-(D+1)) and eta=epsilon rho^D2^(-(D+12)):

| Cancelled order L | Fixed rho | Tail coefficient multiplying 2^(-D) | eta denominator exponent | Count factor relative to degree-D inverse |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2D | 1/64 | 15/4 | 8D+13 | 4 |
| 3D | 1/16 | 15 | 6D+13 | 9 |
| 5D | 1/8 | 30 | 5D+13 | 25 |

The hardest amplitude inequality is the L=5D case proved above; the other two follow. Every tail coefficient is below 31, so the same uniform Morse comparison works. The count factors compare the same design-existence cardinality coefficient and do not assume a new explicit value of it.

For the established all-N theorem use BRV directly at degree 2L. Alternatively, after acquiring n_0 sources for a convenient admissible count, add N−n_0 unit sources at (R+j,0,0), 1≤j≤N−n_0, with the integer R=N/eta. On |y|≤2, |rho y|≤1/4 and added-source distance≥R/2. Normalizing by n_0, their rescaled gradient and Hessian errors are at most

    4N rho/[n_0 epsilon rho^D R²],
    16N rho²/[n_0 epsilon rho^D R³].

Using eta=(epsilon rho^D)2^(-(D+12)), epsilon rho^D≤1, and rho≤1/8, each is less than 2^(-D). The full bound becomes Delta<32·2^(-D), still below the grid threshold (10,240,000<2^44). Added coordinates have O(D+log N) bits. This legal distant padding leaves the common shell, as in the adopted padding lemma.

The September Xu–Xu Theorem 1.1 would conditionally supply n_0=216L²=5400D² for L=5D. Its proof has not been independently adopted here, so the main theorem uses established BRV and makes no new explicit-source-count assertion based on Xu–Xu.
