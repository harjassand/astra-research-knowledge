STRONGEST CLAIM

For a finite faithful tracial algebra A = direct-sum_j M_(d_j), with central
reference density sigma = direct-sum_j (w_j/d_j) I_(d_j), a finite likelihood
experiment E = {H_x : x in X}, |X|=m, has an optimal canonical rank-one
measurement for the joint-variance minimax q(E) with at most

    K <= N + m,       N = sum_j d_j^2,

outcomes. This is an explicit support bound, not an efficient search
algorithm. If the whole canonical EB superoperator must be preserved, rather
than only its quadratic scores on E, an optimal channel has a rank-one POVM
representation with at most

    K <= N + N(N+1)/2

outcomes. The output-state preparation of such a block-supported rank-one
POVM does not require learning the central weights w_j: they cancel exactly.
Nevertheless, the measurement optimizing q(E) generally depends on those
weights and on the acquired experiment. For known rational finite data, that
finite optimization is computable by real-algebraic quantifier elimination,
but no useful polynomial runtime or output-description bound follows.

The outcome bound is dimensionally necessary even inside the fixed-sandwich
selfcompatible interface. In the abelian d-point algebra with tau uniform,
sigma=I/d, theta=1/2, and

    H_i = theta (P_i - I/d),     rho_i = sigma (I + H_i),

include sigma and all rho_i. The dephasing/identity channel on the abelian
algebra is selfcompatible and has zero residual on this family. Exact EB
reconstruction requires at least d measurement outcomes; d suffice. More
generally, if every state is reconstructed with half-trace error epsilon,
then any K-outcome EB channel obeys

    K >= d [1 - (4d+1) epsilon].

Thus exact reconstruction has no dimension-independent POVM-size bound,
even when the reference and all likelihoods are known exactly and the
Dirichlet residual is zero. This does not contradict the target's existence
claim; it charges its apparatus size.

DERIVATION OF THE OUTCOME UPPER BOUNDS

Write tr_j = Tr/d_j, so tau(A)=sum_j w_j tr_j(A_j), and N is the real
dimension of A_sa. Any POVM effect can be pinched into the central blocks
without changing its action on promised block states. Within a block, write
an effect spectrally as M=sum_a alpha_a P_a, with rank-one projectors P_a.
Replacing it by its spectral effects cannot decrease any captured score:

  tau(MH)^2/tau(M)
      <= sum_a tau(alpha_a P_a H)^2/tau(alpha_a P_a),

by weighted Cauchy-Schwarz. Refinement therefore weakly lowers every
quadratic loss and permits restriction to rank-one effects M_y=alpha_y P_y.

For P_y in block j, p_y=tau(M_y)=alpha_y w_j/d_j. The canonical channel is

  Psi_M(H) = sum_y alpha_y Tr(P_y H_j) P_y,
  <H,Psi_M H>_tau
       = sum_y p_y [Tr(P_y H_j)]^2.

The ensemble weights p_y sum to one and satisfy sum_y p_y P_y=sigma. For a
finite E, attach to each atom P in block j the feature

  (P, ([Tr(P H_(x,j))^2]_(x in X))).

The first coordinate lies in the trace-one affine space of real dimension
N-1; the remaining coordinates have dimension m. Caratheodory compresses
any rank-one POVM ensemble to at most N+m atoms while preserving sigma and
every captured score. It therefore preserves every loss in the finite
experiment and q(E), including an optimizer.

For preservation on every H, encode the entire selfadjoint superoperator by
the second moment of v_P=sigma^(-1)P in L2(tau):

  Psi_M = sum_y p_y |v_(P_y)><v_(P_y)|.

This moment is in the real symmetric operator space of dimension
N(N+1)/2. Applying Caratheodory jointly to the barycenter sigma and this
moment gives K <= (N-1)+N(N+1)/2+1. The second bound is O(d^4) for one full
matrix algebra M_d. It preserves the channel exactly. The finite-family bound
is O(d^2+m) for M_d and O(d+m) for the d-dimensional abelian example.

FINITE-RATIONAL COMPUTABILITY, AND ITS LIMIT

For rational block weights w_j and rational finite H_x, use variables
alpha_y >= 0 and unit vectors v_y in the assigned blocks, with
M_y=alpha_y v_y v_y*. The POVM constraints are sum_y M_y=I. The finite
minimax objective is the least t satisfying, for each x,

  t >= tau(H_x^2)
       - sum_y alpha_y (w_(j(y))/d_(j(y)))
                    (v_y* H_(x,j(y)) v_y)^2.

These are polynomial equalities and inequalities in real and imaginary parts
of v_y, alpha_y, and t, with rational coefficients. The outcome bound above
reduces the search to finitely many variables. The feasible set is compact;
the optimum is a real algebraic number, and general quantifier elimination
over real closed fields can in principle return that number and an algebraic
witness. This is an algorithmic computability statement only. I have not
implemented quantifier elimination, and it supplies no polynomial-time,
practical, or short-certificate bound. If E is an implicit/infinite family,
this finite encoding does not apply.

MATCHING LOWER BOUND INSIDE THE FIXED SANDWICH

In A=C^d, let P_i=|i><i|, tau=Tr/d, sigma=I/d, and theta=1/2. Each H_i is
traceless and ||H_i||_infinity <= theta; rho_i=sigma(I+H_i) is a faithful
state and the reference is also in E. Let Phi be the dephasing channel on
M_d, or identity on the diagonal algebra. It is positive, tau-selfadjoint,
UCP, and selfcompatible: measuring the basis and preparing |ii><ii| on two
outputs gives common marginal Phi. On E it has residual r=0. The d-outcome
basis measurement reproduces all E exactly.

For any K-outcome EB channel Lambda(omega)=sum_y Tr(M_y omega) omega_y,
restrict input and output to their diagonals. Let
 A_(y i)=<i|M_y|i>, B_(i y)=<i|omega_y|i>, and Q=BA. Each column of Q is a
probability distribution, sum_y A_(y i)=1, 0<=A_(y i)<=1, and each column
of B is a probability distribution. Exact reconstruction of sigma and all
rho_i forces Q u=u and Q(e_i-u)=e_i-u, hence Q=I. But

  d = sum_i Q_(ii)
    = sum_y sum_i B_(i y) A_(y i)
    <= sum_y sum_i B_(i y) = K.

For approximate error epsilon, diagonal measurement contracts trace distance.
The diagonal law of rho_i is u + (theta/d)(e_i-u). Errors at most epsilon on
sigma and rho_i imply

  TV(Q e_i,e_i) <= epsilon + 2d epsilon/theta
                    = (4d+1)epsilon.

Thus Q_(ii)>=1-(4d+1)epsilon and the same trace argument gives the stated
lower bound. This is an exact dimension-growing apparatus obstruction, not a
counterexample to qualitative EB reconstruction or a lower bound on the
computation needed for approximate instances.

REFERENCE ACQUISITION: WHAT CANCELS AND WHAT DOES NOT

For a block-supported rank-one outcome M_y=alpha_y P_y,

  sigma M_y/tau(M_y) = P_y.

So once the block and effects are supplied, the physical measure-and-prepare
channel Lambda(rho)=sum_y Tr(M_y rho) P_y is implemented without estimating
the central block weights. This is an exact cancellation, not a general
reference-acquisition theorem. Selecting the optimal POVM still depends on
the weighted objective and the family H_x. If the algebra, states, or
likelihood coordinates are accessible only through an oracle or copies, the
reports do not supply a complete acquisition protocol.

Conditionally, suppose the block projectors are known and copies of sigma can
be prepared. Measuring those projectors gives iid block labels with
distribution w=(w_1,...,w_J). The empirical estimate satisfies, with
probability at least 1-delta,

  TV(w_hat,w) <= (1/2)sqrt(J/n) + sqrt(log(1/delta)/(2n)),

by the empirical-distribution expectation bound and McDiarmid. For fixed
likelihoods H_x with ||H_x||_infinity<=theta, every block's normalized
quadratic loss is in [0,theta^2]. Consequently, uniformly over measurements
and labels,

  |q_w(E)-q_(w_hat)(E)| <= theta^2 TV(w,w_hat).

Using a measurement optimal for w_hat costs at most an additional
2 theta^2 TV in q_w. If the separate Dirichlet comparison q_w(E)<=c theta r
is available, this yields the conditional reconstruction bound

  e <= (1/2)sqrt(c theta r + 2 theta^2 TV(w,w_hat)).

In the worst case q_w(E)=0, ensuring an added half-trace error at most eta
by this route requires TV <= 2 eta^2/theta^2, hence
n=O((J+log(1/delta)) theta^4/eta^4) reference copies. Estimating arbitrary
J-block weights to TV epsilon itself has the classical
Theta((J+log(1/delta))/epsilon^2) sample cost. These are conditional costs
for learning only the central weights with known block projectors and fixed
likelihoods. They do not acquire H_x from unknown physical states; inversion
of unknown weights in rho_x=sigma(I+H_x), family acquisition, and discovery
of the central algebra remain separate.

BIT PRECISION AND PHYSICAL REALIZATION

The exact measurement witness is not guaranteed to have a short rational
description. In a finite rational instance quantifier elimination gives an
algebraic witness in principle, but its degree and coefficient sizes have
not been bounded here. Once a real rank-one witness is available, approximate
each alpha_y and vector v_y by b-bit rationals with
  |alpha_y-alpha_tilde_y| <= zeta,
  ||v_y-v_tilde_y||_2 <= zeta.
Each effect changes in operator norm by at most zeta+2zeta. For K outcomes,
the rounded sum differs from I by at most 3K zeta. Scale all rounded effects
by 1/(1+s), where s is that sum-error bound, and add the positive slack
I-sum_y M'_y as a residual outcome (split by block if needed). This gives a
valid POVM. A direct measure-and-prepare trace-norm bound charges O(K^2 zeta)
for effect changes, scaling and slack, plus O(zeta) for prepared-vector
changes. Thus zeta=O(eta/K^2) suffices for channel error O(eta), using

  b = O(log(K^2 sqrt(d_max)/eta))

bits per vector coordinate and weight. A straightforward classical
description then costs O(K d_max log(K d_max/eta)) bits, plus block labels.
This is a post-witness approximation budget only: it does not include the
algebraic witness encoding, solver, source-state acquisition, calibration,
or physical gate synthesis. The precise reconstruction theorem has none of
those bit or apparatus bounds.

BOUNDARIES / NEXT DECISIVE GATE

This work does not prove or refute universal I-Psi <= c(I-Phi), does not prove
that broadcasting forces q(E)->0, and does not construct a polynomial-time
POVM synthesizer for arbitrary compatible channels. The Caratheodory and
real-algebraic claims address only finite-dimensional finite experiments;
the reference sampling bound assumes known center blocks and fixed supplied
likelihoods. No external specialist reviewed the derivations or priority.
The strongest next gate is to determine whether the full target admits a
costed finite input interface (acquired reference, family/net, and channel
description) on which a low-complexity POVM can be synthesized; without that,
the existing stability/existence statements must not be called an algorithm.
