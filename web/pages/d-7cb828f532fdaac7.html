# Cycle19 number-theory frontier report

The strongest output is a complete handwritten extension of family009's characteristic-p reconstruction mechanism to arbitrary relative exponent-one field pairs, including infinite p-rank, from a smaller invariant. It is a mathematical construction with a sharp obstruction and local exact computation; it is not a catalogue-only report.

## Main result candidate

For characteristic-p field pairs K^p⊆H⊆K and L^p⊆C⊆L with relative p-rank at least two, an Fp-linear isomorphism K*/H*→L*/C* preserving only pure-symbol vanishing in the relative degree-two Steinberg quotient is r·α_* for a unique field-pair isomorphism α and r∈Fp*. The complete derivation handles infinite p-bases, common-scalar propagation, projective lifting, and multiplicativity. Root independently identified the relative-base strengthening and is reviewing the derivation.

Technical note: [RELATIVE_RECONSTRUCTION.md](../../../notes/frontier_number1/cycle19/RELATIVE_RECONSTRUCTION.md).

The local lemma has an added iff classification and explicit witness: for p-independent X,Y and nonmonomial U∈C(X),V∈C(Y), the intersection X C(Y/X)∩U C(V/U) is nonzero iff a unique n gives U^n=a+bX^n,V^n=c+dY^n. The intersection is then exactly C·(cU^n−aV^n)^r, with nr≡1 modp. This also yields a finite coordinate certificate and an O(p³) C-arithmetic support-test algorithm, with the coordinate oracle charged as supplied.

Concrete consequence: recover arbitrary height-one purely inseparable field pairs, and recover function fields over finite or arbitrary perfect named bases when the base is the perfect core. This provides a structural interface for exact birational/inseparable reconstruction; an integral model or an efficient acquisition algorithm is not supplied.
