# C12 source notes

## Primary source used for quantitative analysis

Dimri-Wagh et al., “Aged differentiated cells reverse into native stemness-like state by niche cytokines to sustain lifelong homeostasis and tissue repair,” *Nature Communications* 17 (2026), 5743. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-026-72331-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72331-w), PMID 42034629, PMCID PMC13324013. Article XML: `PMC13324013_fullTextXML.xml` (downloaded from Europe PMC). Public scRNA-seq project: GSE303805 / BioProject PRJNA1297714. The official supplementary Source Data workbook is `41467_2026_72331_MOESM11_ESM.xlsx`, worksheet `Figure 5`, section `Fig. 5p`.

The workbook SHA-256 recorded during intake is `e74d84dea486e151c31c55ecdab34533d34e597d366289cace5e79c533601adc`. The source-data rows used here contain four biological replicates per rescue arm. The paper’s Fig. 5 legend says the groups were compared by two-tailed unpaired Student t-tests, but the workbook’s p-value columns do not identify comparison targets in their header. This report therefore does not attribute those p-values to a particular contrast and does not recalculate source inferential statistics.

For Fig. 5p, the source reports: K15-GFP MFI read on day 25; clodronate on days −2, 3, and 8; recombinant factors every other day from day −1 through day 10; individual doses of CCL2 100 ng, IGF1 5 ng, CXCL10 100 ng, CXCL16 100 ng in 30 μL/eye; factor doses halved in combinations. The article reports approximately 77% macrophage reduction by day 1 after clodronate. It separately uses anti-F4/80 depletion with approximately 73% reduction and a larger marker reduction, but that is not the rescue arm.

The same paper supplies evidence for a different, stronger endpoint in its untreated injury model: single-cell RNA-seq at D0/2/5/10/40; lineage tracing; central corneal epithelial cell transplantation into a denuded limbal niche; and long-term homeostasis and repeated injury repair by regenerated cells. Those outcome experiments do not use the cytokine rescue regimen. The paper also reports that conjunctival donor cells do not restore functional LSCs. In discussion it explicitly notes that CCL2 may recruit additional monocytic cells, and that human primary hLSCs were tested under high-calcium conditions for six days. Human mature central corneal-cell conversion was not tested.

GEO metadata resolves the scRNA time course to five samples, one sample titled `rep1` at each of D0, D2, D5, D10, and D40. The article describes four mice per timepoint, so these appear to be pooled timepoint samples rather than four independent single-cell libraries. Cell-level descriptive trajectories can be inspected, but the deposited time course does not provide animal-level replicate variance or an identifiable per-animal transition rate.

## Alternative 2026 primary source examined but not used for numerical reconstruction

Fang et al., “Conversion of Transplanted Mature Hepatocytes into Afp+ Reprogrammed Cells for Liver Regeneration After Injury,” *Advanced Science* 13(18) (2026), e17126. DOI: [10.1002/advs.202517126](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202517126), PMID 41609487, PMCID PMC13042875. Full text is locally saved as `PMC13042875_fullTextXML.xml`.

This paper reports donor hepatocyte reprogramming after transplantation into Fah−/− mice, 29,130 tdTomato+ hepatocytes across eight timepoint samples, an Afp-low proliferative branch and Afp-high stress-adapted/p21 branch, an AFP–PPARγ metabolic feedback axis, neutrophil TNF-α/TNFR1/AP-1 proliferation signaling, OSM identity support, and TGF-β-associated migration followed by zonation. In vivo AFP and AP-1 perturbations alter repopulation and host survival. It is a plausible state-specific, multi-input control architecture. Its Data Availability Statement says underlying supporting data are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request; the paper itself acknowledges no AFP-Cre lineage tracing to establish the Afp+ state as a formal precursor of repopulating cells. No author contact was made and no raw reconstruction is claimed.
