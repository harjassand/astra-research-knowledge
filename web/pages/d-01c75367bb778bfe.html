INDEPENDENT SUPPORT-BOUND RECONSTRUCTION

Let A=direct-sum_j M_(d_j), sigma_j=(w_j/d_j)I, tau(X)=Tr(sigma X),
N=sum d_j^2, and n=N-1. Refine a POVM spectrally inside its blocks. The
quadratic score cannot decrease, since for every H

  tau(MH)^2/tau(M) <= sum_a tau(M_a H)^2/tau(M_a)

for M=sum_a M_a, by weighted Cauchy--Schwarz. Thus a minimizer of the joint
worst-loss problem can be rank one without increasing its value.

For rank-one outcome M_y=alpha_y P_y in block j, put
  p_y=tau(M_y)=alpha_y w_j/d_j,
  v_y=sigma^(-1)P_y,   u_y=v_y-I.
Then tau(v_y)=1, u_y belongs to the n-dimensional real centered space, and
POVM completeness is equivalent to
  sum_y p_y v_y=I, or sum_y p_y u_y=0.
For centered H,
  Psi(H)=sum_y p_y tau(u_y H)u_y.
The identity direction is fixed. Therefore the full canonical channel is
determined by the pair
  (sum_y p_y u_y,  sum_y p_y |u_y><u_y|)
in R^n plus Sym^2(R^n). The first coordinate enforces completeness; the
second preserves the whole centered channel. Caratheodory gives at most

  n+n(n+1)/2+1 = N(N+1)/2

rank-one outcomes preserving the full canonical channel. For a finite family
of m likelihoods, replace the covariance coordinate by the m scores
tau(u_y H_x)^2. Preserving their vector and the zero mean gives at most
n+m+1=N+m outcomes, exactly the finite-family cap in INITIAL.txt. Hence for
finite m the sharper combined existence statement is

  K <= min{N+m, N(N+1)/2};

for arbitrary/infinite E, full-channel preservation gives K<=N(N+1)/2.

This independently verifies and sharpens the outcome-count calculation in
c04_s03/INITIAL.txt, section 7, whose stated full-channel cap is
d^2(d^2+1)/2 for M_d (the same N(N+1)/2 formula). It also corrects the
looser valid cap in my frozen report. The finite-family proof does not supply
an efficient search or a dimension-free outcome alphabet.

ATTRIBUTED PEER RESULT: BOUNDED-HOLEVO ALPHABET LOWER BOUND

c04_s03/revisions/EXPECTED_RECORD_LOWER.txt gives a stronger result than my
frozen d-state lower bound. I reconstructed its proof and found no gap.
For even d and fixed 0<theta<1, take sigma=I/d and the balanced-sign family

  rho_h=(I+theta diag(h))/d,
  h in {+/-1}^d,  sum_i h_i=0.

The common relative entropy is
  c(theta)=[(1+theta)ln(1+theta)+(1-theta)ln(1-theta)]/2,
so every ensemble has Holevo information at most c(theta), independent of d.
The diagonal dephasing map is an exact, selfcompatible EB channel for the
family (zero reconstruction error and zero residual).

For an m-outcome EB map Lambda, its restriction to the diagonal input space
has rank at most m. Its kernel K therefore has dimension k>=d-m. Every h in
K has sum_i h_i=0 by trace preservation. A vertex of K intersect [-1,1]^d
has at least k active coordinate constraints, so some h in the intersection
has sum_i |h_i|>=d-m. The trace-zero cube's vertices are exactly the balanced
sign vectors, so both rho_+=(I+theta diag(h))/d and rho_- with -h are convex
mixtures of family members. Uniform EB error epsilon extends to these mixtures.
Since Lambda(rho_+)=Lambda(rho_-),

  theta sum_i|h_i|/d = T(rho_+,rho_-) <= 2 epsilon,

and therefore m>=d(1-2epsilon/theta). For epsilon<=theta/4 this forces
m>=d/2. Thus the bounded-capacity target itself can require Omega(d)
measurement outcomes even at zero channel residual and with known reference.
The lower bound is on a finite EB outcome alphabet; it does not lower-bound
expected record length under a prior, physical energy, or circuit size.

Attribution: this family and kernel/polytope lower bound originated in
c04_s03/revisions/EXPECTED_RECORD_LOWER.txt. My own INITIAL lower bound uses
only sigma plus d individual likelihoods, so it is subsumed as a simpler
special case; I do not claim the stronger bounded-Holevo construction.

ATTRIBUTED PEER RESULT: RARE-OUTCOME-SAFE RATIONAL POVM COMPILER

I independently checked the proof in c04_s03/INITIAL.txt, section 6, and its
small implementation povm_compiler_check.py. For effects M_y in M_d, let
e>0, q<=e/(2md), u=e/m. Round every Hermitian entry to a q-grid, giving B_y
with ||B_y-M_y||_infinity<=dq<=u/2 by the Hermitian row-sum/Gershgorin bound.
Then N_y=B_y+uI satisfies

  0 <= N_y-M_y <= 2uI,
  I <= S=sum_y N_y <= I+2eI.

The scaled effects M'_y=N_y/(1+2e) and residual M'_0=I-S/(1+2e) are exactly
positive rational effects summing to I. In normalized trace,

  sum_y ||M'_y-M_y||_1 <= 4e,
  ||M'_0||_1 <= 2e.

For a Hermitian contraction H, set F_H(M)=tau(MH)M/tau(M), F_H(0)=0.
Using p=tau(M), q=tau(N), p,q<=1, and p>=q after swapping labels, split

  F_H(M)-F_H(N)
    = [tau(MH)/p](M-N)
      +N[tau(MH)/p-tau(NH)/q].

The first L1 term is at most ||M-N||_1. The second is bounded by
|tau((M-N)H)| plus |p-q|, hence by 2||M-N||_1. Zero effects follow by a
limit/direct bound. Summing outcomes gives

  ||Psi_(M')-Psi_M||_(infinity->1,tau) <= 18e.

This proves no inverse-minimum-outcome factor in the analytic error. The
small Python artifact checks a real symmetric d=2 instance including a
2^-200 rare effect, exact positivity and normalization; it is a transcription
fixture, not a proof of the general matrix inequality. The dimension-free
constant and general complex-matrix claim rest on the symbolic bounds above.

For direct-sum central references, the algebraic compiler remains valid in
the full block algebra. A block-supported rank-one outcome prepares P
independently of w_j; a general mixed outcome's canonical preparation
sigma M_y/tau(M_y) still needs the supplied reference. If using a finite
precision rational reference and effects, p_y and preparations have bit costs
from those input denominators. Error does not blow up as p_y tends to zero,
but exact input representation and physical measurement synthesis remain
charged.

S03's second-phase rational-grid search adds a finite exhaustive procedure
for a supplied rational superoperator and candidate c,delta. Its support cap

DECISION / NEXT GATE

The cost debt has three separate layers. (i) Outcome alphabet is at least
Omega(d) on an exact bounded-Holevo example, while a canonical full-channel
POVM can always be represented with at most N(N+1)/2 outcomes; this is a
polynomial-size existential representation. (ii) For a supplied POVM, exact
rational perturbation can achieve dimension-free infinity-to-1 error with
O(m d^2) entries and no minimum-probability penalty. (iii) Finding a suitable
POVM from a bare compatible channel is only a finite real-algebraic or
astronomical grid search; no polynomial-time acquisition, reference learning,
or physical circuit compiler is established. The unrestricted collective
variance / EB Dirichlet theorem remains the load-bearing mathematical gate.

After the earlier audit, work/cycle6/c04_l10/INITIAL.txt appeared and was read
under the CROSS_PHASE authorization. Its exact power lemma checks: for a
Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint channel Phi,

  2(I-Phi) - (I-Phi^2) = (I-Phi)^2 >= 0.

If Phi^2 is EB, the comparison map Psi=Phi^2 is itself EB; no self-
compatibility premise is needed for this restricted statement. The source-
reported symplectic PPT-square result is primary paper Prudhoe,
arXiv:2501.03959v3: its symplectic section derives the square parameters and
checks the EB region for all even n>=2; the nontrivial non-EB extreme exists
for the stated higher-dimensional cases. The public source establishes this
as a covariant subclass result, not as a universal channel theorem.

Park, arXiv:2608.13551v2, states that every PPT map has finite EB index and
that a class strictly containing SP_2 has index at most 3 uniformly in
dimension. Conditional on that source theorem and the worker's DSP_2
identification, Phi^3 EB; with P=Phi^2, P^2=Phi^4 EB and
I-P^2 <= 2(I-P). The factor 2 is relative to the two-step coarse-grained
map P. It does not state I-Phi^4 <= 2(I-Phi); composing the one-step
quadratic inequalities only gives the looser factor 4 relative to Phi.
Both source-derived routes have an implementation advantage in a physical
oracle model: composing the retained channel twice/four times needs no POVM
acquisition. If a standalone classical measure-prepare description is
required, the decomposition of the EB power is still a separate charged
