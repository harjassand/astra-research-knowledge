## A candidate exact legal flat-direction primitive

Here is our construction, using the source's proved filter and conditional-expectation lemma. It resolves the exact-linear-constraint part locally without depending on the smallest nonzero singular value of a face/cost matrix.

At a cleaned state let J be the exact kernel of the rational original-face, queried cost, large-row, protected-barrier, and approximate-gradient equations. Recover its rational orthogonal projector P_J by exact elimination and a Gram inverse. Its operator norm is one. The existing dimension argument supplies a subspace E contained in J on which the true physical Hessian is <=-2 eta I. The spectral restrictions used only in that existence proof need not be rationally imposed on the output direction.

Choose a rational symmetric Hessian approximation T with operator error <=eta/4, and a rational polynomial-size M0>=||T||_op+eta. Define

    S=P_J T P_J +M0(I-P_J).

S commutes with P_J, is positive on J-perp, and has at least r>=1 eigenvalues <=-7 eta/4 from E. For any rigorously certified integer r not exceeding dim E, apply Li's rational polynomial filter to S with threshold eta and r. Let Q be its soft projector. Define the exactly legal soft projector

    Q_J=P_J Q=Q P_J=P_J Q P_J.

All equalities are exact: Q is a polynomial in S. Thus Q_J is rational and symmetric, 0<=Q_J<=I. Removing J-perp loses no strongly negative eigenvalues. The proof of Li's filter therefore still gives

    trace(Q_J²)>=3r/4,
    Q_J(S+eta I)Q_J <=eta r/(4096s) I.

The second inequality follows by compressing the source's matrix inequality with P_J. The conditional-expectation proof applies directly to Q_J. For normalized forms f_l (including all coordinate forms and medium-barrier gradients divided by two), choose an integer p with s M_forms 2^(-p)<=r/8. It returns rational h in J with

    1/2<||h||<=1,
    h^T T h<=-(1-1/2048)eta ||h||²,
    max_l |f_l^T h|²<8p/r.

Since h is in J, h^T S h=h^T T h. Transferring to the true Hessian costs only eta/4, leaving strictly negative curvature. Every original face/cost/large/protected equation is exact, and no offset correction or projection with an uncharged constraint residual is used. This is a stronger direction interface than merely finding one short negative direction: it controls coordinate and barrier flatness.

### Bit and dimension accounting

P_J has polynomial bits from the exact rational state/equations and does not amplify operator error. M0 is bounded by the existing Hessian/Taylor estimates, a polynomial in n,K with explicit fixed constants; it does not depend on inverse face widths or singular values. Li's Newton–Schulz iteration count is O(log(sM0/(eta r)))=O(log(nK)). Clear a common denominator for each matrix: the next iterate's denominator divides a fixed-data denominator times the cube of the old one. The resulting degree 3^J and all rational encodings are polynomial in n,K and the state bits. Conditional moments have degree O(log(sM_forms/r)) and are also polynomial bits. The ordinary independence/strong-optimization access used to acquire the original-face equations is unchanged.

The admitted parameter family proves dim E greater than

    s/k-13s/R²-[4+768/(5 alpha)]s/K-2.

Any positive integer below that quantity is a safe choice of r; if the quantity lies below one, existence still certifies r=1. Large r gives a useful flatness gain, while r=1 gives little improvement. A numerical estimate of eigenspace dimension is unnecessary.

## Remaining consequential obstruction

The local primitive does **not** inherit Li's global update count. D currently uses (L-8)_+^3 and fixed eta. This scalar is C² but not C³ across eight, whereas Li's fourth-order centered-step argument uses (L-1)_+^4, a charged Hessian, and a weighted resolvent estimate. The missing lemma is a uniform constrained, charged, quartic step certificate whose negative eigenspace survives the original-face reserve and whose endpoint/reset/row ledger closes after noncoordinate face hits. The exact face-compatible reset must replace cube-grid compression, with its additional energy/charge losses charged to the new epoch budget. General strong-oracle face/endpoint/LP costs must also be counted; Li's unconstrained matrix-operation complexity is not the complete general-P runtime.

The peer safe-event certificate already permits long steps when a rational whole-segment bound proves L<=8 and all barriers<=0. It bypasses the tiny-step Taylor rule in that region. Neither it nor the local flat generator certifies high-L long steps or a complete fast general-P trajectory. That is the next load-bearing mathematical target. Retain source cold/warm screening and epoch ideas as alternatives, but do not advertise <99, O(n² log n) updates, or polybit general constrained rounding as an already transferred result.
