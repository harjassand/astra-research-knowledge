1. Exact BMVZ term generation and a hardness-preserving seed-pair witness

BMVZ first partitions the input terms into commuting colors. Within a color,
the output labels are length-t walks in its regular expander, one label per
walk, and the clause is

    h_(chi,f) = I - tensor_(a=1)^t (I - Pi_(f(a))^chi).

The term list is not the full Cartesian power of vertices; a constant tuple
would require an unasserted self-loop. Accordingly, the abstract source does
not establish that the two BJSW seeds occur together in the canonical,
unmodified term table. This remains UNKNOWN without a specific adjacency
list.

There is, however, an exact allowed augmented hard family. Start with a
constant-color, bounded-degree 5-local QMA-hard family
H_base=sum_chi v_chi H_chi. Append two BJSW five-qubit seed projectors on fresh
ancillas, each as a singleton color of weight 1/4, and scale the original
color weights by 1/2. Definition 1.6 permits positive layer weights, and this
weighted layered instance has

    H_aug = (1/2) H_base tensor I + (1/4) P_phi + (1/4) P_psi.

The common kernel of the two seed projectors has dimension at least 30.
Therefore
lambda_min(H_aug)=lambda_min(H_base)/2. The promise gap and QMA-hardness are
preserved up to this constant rescaling; the number of colors stays constant
and all color weights remain bounded below. Each new seed qubit has incidence
two. The BMVZ small-layer rule emits
the exact repeated seed clause in each new color, at every iteration, without
using an expander self-loop.

For T repeated blocks, each seed's amplified term is

    Q_(v,T) = I - (I-|v><v|)^{tensor T}.

For the BJSW vectors phi_5=|->|00>|10> and
psi_5=(|000>-|011>-|101>-|110>)/2 tensor |10>,
|<phi_5,psi_5>|=1/(2sqrt(2)). Their computational supports have sizes 2 and
4 and intersect in one basis vector. An explicit row of the full augmented
output Hamiltonian has at least 2^T+4^T-1 nonzeros. The pair-only repeated
term has exact Clifford relative-entry magnitude 1/(2sqrt(2)); the existing
standard-library exact checker verifies the seed preparations and the
R=1,2 row counts. These are computational-basis facts about this constructed
output, not lower bounds invariant under a change of basis.

2. Pair-only positive logical lift and reversible-connector tradeoff

Let U_0,U_1 be Clifford decoders taking |0^5> to the two seed vectors, and
write U_x=tensor_i U_(x_i) for x in {0,1}^T. The isometry

    V|psi> = 2^(-T/2) sum_x |x> U_x|psi>

has a polynomial-size controlled-Clifford circuit. Let

    D = I - tensor_i(I-|0^5><0^5|_i),   B=I_branch tensor D.

B is a positive contraction, diagonal and one-sparse per row. Its compression
is E=I-tensor_i(I-Q_i), where Q_i=(P_phi+P_psi)/2. Put

    q_-=(1-1/(2sqrt(2)))/2 > 0.32,
    H_pair=(Q_(phi,T)+Q_(psi,T))/2.

At one block Q_i has nonzero eigenvalues q_- and 1-q_-. The largest
nontrivial principal cosine between the two product-kernel spaces defining
H_pair is 1/(2sqrt(2)); tensor-product principal cosines multiply. Thus both
E and H_pair have the same kernel K and lie between q_- P_(K-perp) and
P_(K-perp). In particular

    q_- H_pair <= V*BV <= q_-^(-1) H_pair.

So the seed-only test has an explicit constant-factor, positive 1-row-sparse
lift.

For any reversible uniform-stationary Markov kernel K on the T-bit cube with
transitions changing at most q bits, its Poincare gap is at most 2q/T. To
prove this, use F_epsilon(x)=sum_i epsilon_i(-1)^(x_i), whose variance is T.
Average its Dirichlet form over independent signs epsilon_i; cross terms
cancel and a transition changing h bits contributes 2h, so the average is
at most 2q. The Rayleigh quotient gives the bound. The covariant positive
connector

    C_K=(1/2)G((I-K) tensor I)G*,   G=direct-sum_x U_x,

has the same kernel as V, norm at most one, and gap half that of K. A
transition changing h selector bits has relative Clifford block with exactly
8^h nonzeros per row. If K has degree s and jump radius q, C_K has at most
1+s*8^q row entries. Therefore polynomial row support forces q=O(log T) and
this connector's gap is O(log T/T). The bit-flip walk attains row support at
most 1+8T and gap 1/T for C_K. This is a no-go only for reversible
covariant connectors on this pair interpolation; arbitrary positive
enforcers and active data-workspace isometries are outside it.

3. Direct-frame row obstruction with pure workspaces

For a direct branch isometry V|psi>=sum_c sqrt(w_c)|c>U_c|psi>|a_c>, where
each a_c is a normalized pure workspace vector, collapse any output row by
its branch-specific workspace amplitudes. If the branch frames split into r
monomial-equivalence classes and the maximum cross-class matrix entry is
mu, rank counting forces some row of every exact enforcer
ker C=ran V to contain at least ceil(1+1/mu) nonzeros. This allows arbitrary
workspace dimension, branch amplitudes, and overlap between the a_c. For
the pair-only BJSW frames, mu=2^(-3T/2), giving
ceil(1+2^(3T/2)). The earlier, more general nonmonomial-Clifford estimate
under only mu<=2^(-T/2) gives ceil(1+2^(T/2)).

This is not a full-list no-go. Across all BMVZ paths, two branches can differ
at only one seed coordinate, so the maximum coherence over all branch pairs
need not decay in T. One branch per color also does not by itself represent
the within-color average over paths. A proof for a full-list direct-branch
architecture needs the explicit isometry and all-pairs coherence bound.
Data-dependent workspace states/isometries remain a still broader escape.

4. Full-list common history encoding and exact costs

For the complete augmented output H=sum_(j=1)^M w_j h_j, under the standard
explicit polynomial-bit exact algebraic matrix representation of its local
projectors, flatten each
iterated BMVZ clause to its R original fixed-k local projector factors. Use
an exact local algebraic diagonalizer U_i and diagonal projector D_i for
each base term P_i. Then h_j=U_j*D_jU_j, where U_j is the tensor product
of its R local decoders and D_j is the diagonal OR of the R local predicates.
This includes the Clifford seed pair. For adjacent clauses, U_(j+1)U_j*
factors into R unitaries, each on the union of two k-wire supports. Each
constant-size algebraic unitary has an exact decomposition into a constant
number of one- and two-qubit algebraic gates. Thus the total transition count
L is O(MR) for fixed k and polynomial for the BMVZ hard family.

Choose a polynomial integer Q clearing all rational weights q_j=Qw_j, with
Q>=L and Q>=1/min_j w_j. Make a clock with a q_j-position tail carrying
decoder U_j for every clause j. Insert ell_j-1 gate positions between
successive tails, then idle positions to reach the next power of two K.
The construction gives Q<=K<4Q. With the clock and data register define

    V|psi> = K^(-1/2) sum_(s=0)^(K-1) |s> U_s|psi>,
    e_s = (1/4)[ |s><s|+|s+1><s+1|
                -|s+1><s|G_s-|s><s+1|G_s* ],
    C=sum_s e_s,
    B=sum_(j,s in tail j)|s><s| tensor D_j,

where U_(s+1)=G_sU_s and idle steps use identity. Each e_s is positive.
Gauge-conjugating by the controlled U_s turns C into one quarter of the path
Laplacian, so

    ker C=ran V,
    C >= [(1-cos(pi/K))/2](I-VV*),
    ||C||=(1+cos(pi/K))/2<1.

The compression is the exact operator identity V*BV=(Q/K)H with Q/K>1/4.
Thus the full QMA-hard list has positive contractions C,B and constant
logical comparison, but the all-state image gap is only Theta(K^-2).

Resource ledger: K and the output qubit count n'+log_2 K are polynomial;
V has polynomial circuit size; every C row has at most 9 entries and a
polynomial-time row/entry oracle; B has one entry per row and a polynomial-time
diagonal-entry oracle. B entries are 0/1. C entries are rational multiples
of one-/two-qubit algebraic gate entries, with polynomial bit height and
constant algebraic degree for fixed k and the stated input encoding. Each C
edge acts on the full log_2 K clock plus at most two data qubits, so its qubit
locality is log_2 K+2, not constant. B's diagonal D_j can act on the entire
data register, so B is not established as constant-local. Row sparsity and
locality are separate costs. Rescaling C while preserving contraction cannot
remove the path gap.

4A. Arbitrary positive enforcers of this exact history image

The independent audit in sol_history_gap_independent_audit.txt strengthens
the gap limitation for the *fixed V of Section 4*. Let P=VV* and suppose a
row i and column set J have projection mass sum_(j in J)|P_ij|^2>=m, with
each entry square at most a. For any positive contraction C with exact kernel
projection P and maximum row support s, the Chebyshev filter

 p_l(x)=T_l((1+gamma-2x)/(1-gamma)) / T_l((1+gamma)/(1-gamma))

approximates P within 2 exp(-2l sqrt(gamma)) in operator norm, where gamma is
the first positive eigenvalue of C. Its row support is at most (s+1)^l.
The missing mass in a selected P row then yields, whenever
D=log(m/(2a))/log(s+1)>1,

 gamma <= [log(8/m)/(4(D-1))]^2.

For the augmented singleton seed tails, the history projector block is
K^-1 U_u U_v*. The seed decoder convention is U_j=c_j*, where
c_j|0>=|j>. Exact arithmetic gives 8 nonzeros per row in
U_phi U_psi*=c_phi*c_psi, each of square 1/8. After aggregating identical
singleton clauses the psi tail has q=Q/4 positions. A selected phi-tail row
therefore has m=q/K^2, a=1/(K^2 8^T), and
D=log(q*8^T/2)/log(s+1). Every positive contraction enforcing this exact
whole-list history image obeys

 gamma <= [log(8K^2/q) log(s+1) /
            (4[log(q*8^T/2)-log(s+1)])]^2.

For polynomial K,s the displayed bound is O((log K log(s+1)/T)^2) once
T dominates log K+log s; it tends to zero, for example, when
T>>log K log(s+1). In particular it vanishes for K,s<=n^b and T>=n^c
with fixed b,c>0. Thus the fixed image cannot have a constant all-state
gap under any polynomial-row positive enforcer in that regime. The proof does not assume
reversibility or covariance and allows arbitrary intermediate vertices.
It survives basis-injective monomial workspace recodings (classical
reversible computation and phases). It does not cover a coherent quantum
workspace map or a redesigned V, and it places no further restriction on B.
The separate Clifford stabilizer construction gives a constant-gap sparse
enforcer for Clifford images in general, so this obstruction is specific to
the fixed repeated-seed history image rather than Cliffordness alone.

The selected-row argument also extends beyond Clifford decoders. For ANY
factorwise seed diagonalizers U_phi,U_psi with rank-one diagonal tests
|r_phi><r_phi| and |r_psi><r_psi|, the row r_phi of U_phi U_psi* has a
distinguished entry at column r_psi of square |<phi,psi>|^2=1/8. Since the
row has norm one, every entry in that row has square at most 7/8. Tensoring
over T copies bounds the maximum squared entry of the selected product row by
(7/8)^T, while its total mass over q psi clock blocks remains q/K^2.
Consequently the same Chebyshev lemma gives the bound above with
log(q*8^T/2) replaced by log(q*(8/7)^T/2). It still tends to zero when
T>>log K log(s+1); in particular it vanishes for polynomial K,s and
T>=n^c with fixed positive exponents.

The same maximum-entry estimate holds if each decoder acts on an entire
base data copy and diagonalizes P_phi tensor I or P_psi tensor I. A row in
the phi diagonal support maps back into ran(P_phi tensor I); the identity
P_phi P_psi P_phi=(1/8)P_phi gives exactly 1/8 row mass in the psi diagonal
support and 7/8 in its complement, so no individual entry exceeds 7/8.
This generalization still requires factorization across the T copies and a
uniform history clock with these endpoint decoders. A global decoder for the
amplified OR projector, a coherent active-workspace isometry, or a redesigned
history architecture remains outside the theorem.

5. Independent L02 operator-domain audit

The fresh referee reconstruction finds no counterexample in L02's
zero-spectrum Jensen, mixed exposure, component barycenter, Pinsker drift,
or ceiling transfer. For bounded positive T, the map
F_T h(s)=T(T+sI)^(-1)h obeys F_T*F_T=T by vector spectral integration. The
Jensen Gram inequality follows first after epsilon-shifting all operators;
||F_(T+epsilon)-F_T||<=sqrt(epsilon) then gives operator-norm convergence
at zero, including continuous spectrum accumulating at zero. POVM transport
uses bounded regularizations converging strongly and von Neumann algebra
strong closure; no bounded inverse on supp(T) is assumed. The exposure law
includes the transported vector norm, giving total mass p; disconnected
component drift is TV<=sqrt(ln(1/p)/(2m)). The checked source constants stay
2048 for disconnected support and 1024 for connected support.

This is an internal proof audit, not formalization, external validation,
historical-priority evidence, or a Hamiltonian consequence. No named
constant-gap verifier/positive encoding is obtained from the repetition
theorem.

constant-gap gate, even with a more general positive C. A successful route
must exhibit one explicit V, positive contractions C and B,
ker C=ran V, a size-independent C gap against *every* orthogonal vector,
and alpha H<=V*BV<=beta H with alpha,beta constants. It must charge the
workspace and V circuit, coefficient encoding, locality, maximum row degree,
entry/row running time, and construction time. A successful active encoding
would be the first escape from the current scoped barriers; a no-go must
control data-dependent workspace row dependencies or a more general positive
enforcer, not only direct frames or a normalized local syndrome budget.
