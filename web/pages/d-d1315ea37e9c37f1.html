Abstract.

We study quasi-Galois points, which are a generalization of Galois points.
We characterize smooth quartic surfaces admitting a quasi-Galois point in terms of

Moreover, we also determine all quasi-Galois points of the Fermat quartic surface and show that it has exactly 28 quasi-Galois points.
