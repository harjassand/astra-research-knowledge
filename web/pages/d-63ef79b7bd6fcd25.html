1. Exact weighted definitions
------------------------------
Write s=sqrt(sigma), Gamma^r(X)=s^r Xs^r and
<X,Y>_sigma=Tr[X^*Gamma(Y)]. The source uses dissipative mathcal L=-L.
For positive f the original power maps are

 I_a,b(f)=Gamma^(-1/a)[(Gamma^(1/b)f)^(b/a)].

For general Hermitian f this baseline explicitly defines instead

 I_a,b^signed(f)
   =Gamma^(-1/a)[sgn(X)|X|^(b/a)], X=Gamma^(1/b)f.

This agrees with the source on positive inputs and gives I_p,p^signed(f)=f.
For p'=p/(p-1), define

 E_p^signed(f)=p/[2(p-1)] <I_p',p^signed(f),L(f)>_sigma,
 E_2(G)=<G,L(G)>_sigma.

All operators in the pairing are Hermitian and the value is real. At p2
the signed definition gives the usual quadratic E2. On positive inputs
E_p^signed is precisely the source E_p, so the main positive claim(P)
uses no amended input convention.

Set

 theta=1-2/p, eta=|theta|<1,
 X=Gamma^(1/p)f,
 Q=sgn(X)|X|^(p/2), G=I_2,p^signed(f)=Gamma^(-1/2)Q,
 H=Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2).

For nonsingular Hermitian inputs, Q has real nonzero eigenvalues. Singular
inputs are handled by continuity at the end. The exact HS transports are

 R_theta=Gamma^(1/2)f
   =s^(theta/2)sgn(Q)|Q|^(1-theta)s^(theta/2),
 T_theta=Gamma^(1/2)I_p',p^signed(f)
   =s^(-theta/2)sgn(Q)|Q|^(1+theta)s^(-theta/2).

Consequently

 A_theta=Tr[T_theta H(R_theta)]=(1+theta)E_p^signed(f),
 E_2(G)=Tr[QH(Q)].                                     (1)

These are the actual physical weighted interpolations, not powers of f or
rho with the stationary sandwich omitted. Q^2 need not have trace1; all
forms and frequency identities are homogeneous and no normalization is used.

2. Legal noises and frequency kernel
-------------------------------------
Use the inherited audited full KMS representation as a sum of
H_B(Y)={V,Y}/2-BYB, B=B^*, V=ell_s^(-1)(BsB).
For the intrinsic frequencies [log s,B_omega]=omega B_omega,
B_omega^*=B_-omega, the inherited independently derived coefficients are

 V=sum_{omega,nu} e^c/cosh(delta) B_omega^*B_nu,
 c=(omega+nu)/2, delta=(omega-nu)/2.

Diagonalize Q only: Q|a>=epsilon_a q_a|a>, epsilon_a=+-1,q_a>0.
Put t_ab=q_aq_b, beta_ab=ln(q_a/q_b),
b_omega^{ab}=<a|B_omega|b>, e_ab=epsilon_a epsilon_b.
All state pairs below are ordered, including a=b. Arbitrary complex matrix
elements and all coherent cross frequencies are retained.

The drift in A_theta is
Tr[Q^2(s^(theta/2)Vs^(-theta/2)+s^(-theta/2)Vs^(theta/2))]/2.
Its unsymmetrized frequency coefficient is
q_b^2 e^c cosh(theta delta)/cosh(delta).
The jump term is

 Tr[(s^(theta/2)Bs^(-theta/2))^*
      sgn(Q)|Q|^(1-theta)
      (s^(theta/2)Bs^(-theta/2))sgn(Q)|Q|^(1+theta)].

Its coefficient is e_ab t_ab exp[-theta(beta_ab-c)]. Averaging the total
sum under (a,b,omega,nu)->(b,a,-nu,-omega) gives exactly

 A_theta=sum_{a,b}t_ab sum_{omega,nu}
       T_(theta,e_ab)(beta_ab-omega,beta_ab-nu)
                             conj(b_omega^{ab})b_nu^{ab},
 T_(theta,e)(x,y)
       =cosh(m)cosh(theta d)/cosh(d)-e cosh(theta m),
 m=(x+y)/2, d=(x-y)/2.                                 (2)

The same expansion at theta0 gives

 E_2(G)=sum_{a,b}t_ab sum_{omega,nu}
       L_e(beta_ab-omega,beta_ab-nu)conj(b_omega^{ab})b_nu^{ab},
 L_e(x,y)=cosh(m)/cosh(d)-e.                            (3)

For e=+1, L_e=2sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)/cosh(d). For e=-1 it is
2cosh(x/2)cosh(y/2)/cosh(d). No physical covariance is assumed: the
frequency decomposition is just the finite spectral decomposition of log s.

3. Two exact scalar kernels
-----------------------------
Let a=(1+eta)/2, b=(1-eta)/2 and c_eta=cos(pi eta/2)>0.
Since (2) is even in theta, the same two kernels cover both signs of theta.
Hyperbolic addition identities give

 T_(theta,+)(x,y)-c_eta L_+(x,y)
   =[sinh(ax)sinh(by)+sinh(bx)sinh(ay)
                         -2c_eta sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)]/cosh(d),
 T_(theta,-)(x,y)-c_eta L_-(x,y)
   =[cosh(ax)cosh(by)+cosh(bx)cosh(ay)
                         -2c_eta cosh(x/2)cosh(y/2)]/cosh(d).  (4)

Both are PSD on arbitrary finite real node sets. The proof below is full
many-node positivity, rather than a two-point scalar inequality.

4. Hardy proof of both kernels
-------------------------------
For z in the open unit disk set w=atanh(z), with |Im w|<pi/4. Define

 r_s(z)=sinh((1-eta)w)/sinh((1+eta)w),
 h_s(z)=sinh(w)/sinh((1+eta)w),
 r_c(z)=cosh((1-eta)w)/cosh((1+eta)w),
 h_c(z)=cosh(w)/cosh((1+eta)w).

All are bounded analytic functions for eta<1. The sinh ratios have removable
zeros at w0. Their moduli are at most1 because
|sinh(u+iv)|^2=sinh^2(u)+sin^2(v), the arguments scale monotonically and
|(1+eta)v|<pi/2. For the cosh ratios use
|cosh(u+iv)|^2=sinh^2(u)+cos^2(v); their denominators are bounded away from
zero for bounded u since cos((1+eta)pi/4)>0, while larger |u| only increases
the denominator. In particular their moduli are bounded by
sec((1+eta)pi/4). There are no interior denominator zeros.

At a boundary point z on the unit circle except +-1, write
w=u+-i pi/4. Product-to-sum identities yield, for BOTH j=s,c,

             2Re r_j(z)=2c_eta |h_j(z)|^2.              (5)

For example the real part of
sinh((1-eta)w)conj(sinh((1+eta)w)) is c_eta cosh(2u)/2,
and |sinh(w)|^2=cosh(2u)/2. Replacing sinh by cosh gives exactly the
same two quantities since cos(2 Im w)=0. Dividing by the common denominator
proves (5). The exceptional endpoints have measure0.

On Hardy space, the analytic kernel

 C_j(z,u)=[r_j(z)+conj(r_j(u))-2c_eta h_j(z)conj(h_j(u))]
                                                     /(1-z conj(u))

represents the operator

 M_rj+M_rj^*-2c_eta M_hj M_hj^*
      =2c_eta(M_hj^*M_hj-M_hj M_hj^*)>=0.               (6)

The equality is the Toeplitz boundary identity (5), and positivity follows
from analytic-multiplier hyponormality:
||M_hj^*F||=||P_+(conj(h_j)F)||<=||h_j F||=||M_hj F||.
Thus each C_j is PSD on arbitrary finite disk point sets.

For real x, put z=tanh(x/2) and let
g_s(z)=sinh((1+eta)atanh(z))/sinh(atanh(z)),
g_c(z)=cosh((1+eta)atanh(z))/cosh(atanh(z)).
These are finite, real, positive and nonzero; g_s(0)=1+eta. Using

 sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)/cosh((x-y)/2)=zu/(1-zu),
 cosh(x/2)cosh(y/2)/cosh((x-y)/2)=1/(1-zu),

the first kernel in (4) is the diagonal congruence of C_s by z g_s(z),
and the second by g_c(z). Signed/zero sinh factors pose no problem: the
zero node has a zero row there; the cosh kernel includes zero nodes directly.
This proves both full real PSD assertions in (4).
