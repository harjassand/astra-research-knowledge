# Decision: preserve the validated cuts, but stop promoting them as a foundational advance

**The numerical improvement survives rigorous computational validation. The reciprocal inequalities, however, are explicitly captured by bounded-order moment machinery. Their practical advantage over the strongest implemented methods remains unresolved—not demonstrated, and not computationally defeated.**

This continuation closes the input-identity and numerical-certification gaps and establishes a precise mathematical containment result. It does **not** complete the matched strongest-incumbent comparison or establish performance across additional difficult instances.

The resulting classification is:

> **A validated, compact family of projected moment inequalities, with an unproven computational-efficiency advantage—not an established new global-optimization capability.**

## 2. The mathematical comparison is now explicit

The central result is stronger than saying that a sufficiently large moment hierarchy eventually captures every valid inequality. **There are explicit low-degree certificates for these particular inequalities.**

Here, *order two* means a full real moment relaxation containing polynomials through degree four; *order three* means through degree six.

| Formulation | What is established about reciprocal coherence |
|---|---|
| Full real order-two relaxation of the original voltage formulation | Implies every **upper** reciprocal inequality |
| Full real order-three relaxation of the original formulation | Implies **both sides** |
| Full real order-two relaxation augmented with valid current upper bounds **and their localizing constraints** | Implies **both sides** |
| Unaugmented order-two relaxation, lower inequality | Not settled |
| A particular QC/RLT or sparse-moment implementation | No blanket implication or independence result established |

The qualifications in the last two rows are essential.

### The explicit certificates

For two fixed-injection buses, write

\[
x=V_i,\qquad y=V_j,\qquad
u=J_i=B_iV,\qquad v=J_j=B_jV.
\]

The fixed-power equations become the quadratic equalities

\[
h_i=\overline{x}u-1=0,
\qquad
h_j=\overline{y}v-1=0.
\]

Assume the original voltage bounds

\[
a_i\le |x|^2\le b_i,
\qquad
a_j\le |y|^2\le b_j.
\]

For arbitrary complex constants \(\alpha,\beta\), define

\[
z=\alpha u+\beta v,
\qquad
q=\alpha\overline y+\beta\overline x,
\]

and

\[
\delta=\overline x\,\overline y\,z-q
       =\alpha\overline y\,h_i+\beta\overline x\,h_j.
\]

The quantities \(|z|^2\) and \(|q|^2\) are the scalar quadratic forms associated with \(K_{ij}\) and \(C_{ij}\).

#### Upper inequality: degree four suffices

The following identity holds even away from the power-equality set:

\[
\begin{aligned}
b_i b_j|z|^2-|q|^2
={}&
\frac{1}{b_i}
\left|b_i\overline y\,z-xq\right|^2\\
&+b_i(b_j-|y|^2)|z|^2\\
&+\frac{b_i-|x|^2}{b_i}|q|^2
+2\operatorname{Re}(\overline q\,\delta).
\end{aligned}
\]

The first term is a squared modulus of a quadratic polynomial. The next two are voltage-bound constraints multiplied by squared moduli of linear polynomials. The last term is a polynomial combination of the fixed-power equalities and therefore vanishes under the corresponding moment constraints.

Every term has degree at most four. Thus a full order-two relaxation implies

\[
\boxed{C_{ij}(W)\preceq b_i b_jK_{ij}(W).}
\]

#### Lower inequality: degree six suffices

Similarly,

\[
\begin{aligned}
|q|^2-a_i a_j|z|^2
={}&
(|x|^2-a_i)|\overline y\,z|^2\\
&+a_i(|y|^2-a_j)|z|^2\\
&-2\operatorname{Re}(\overline q\,\delta)-|\delta|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

The first term is a degree-six voltage-bound localizer. The second has degree four.

The negative square is **not** being treated as nonnegative. It belongs to the equality ideal, because

\[
-|\delta|^2
=
-\operatorname{Re}(\overline\delta\,\alpha\overline y\,h_i)
-\operatorname{Re}(\overline\delta\,\beta\overline x\,h_j).
\]

A full order-three relaxation includes the necessary equality multipliers and annihilates these terms. Therefore it implies

\[
\boxed{a_i a_jK_{ij}(W)\preceq C_{ij}(W).}
\]

Both identities and the equality-ideal decomposition passed **exact symbolic polynomial checks**, without numerical substitutions.

### Why the augmented order-two result matters

The physical equations also imply

\[
|J_i|^2\le \frac1{a_i},
\qquad
|J_j|^2\le \frac1{a_j}.
\]

Once these valid quadratic bounds are explicitly included **and localized**, exchanging the voltage and reciprocal-current roles in the upper certificate gives a degree-four certificate for the original lower inequality.

But merely imposing scalar current bounds at first order is not equivalent to imposing their localizing matrices. That distinction prevents an unjustified claim that ordinary node envelopes already imply all reciprocal cuts.

The MOMENT_CONTAINMENT.md[complete containment proof](sandbox:/mnt/data/astra_reciprocal_decision/proofs/MOMENT_CONTAINMENT.md) gives the substitution and degree accounting.

### What this does—and does not—defeat

This establishes that reciprocal coherence does **not** impose consistency beyond suitably supported, bounded-order moment formulations.

It does **not** establish that the exact projected inequalities were previously published. Nor does it establish that constructing a large moment relaxation is as cheap as adding the projected cuts.

Sparsity remains consequential. The saved cuts involve unions of **4–24 original complex-voltage variables**, not necessarily one existing branch clique. A sparse implementation may omit the monomials or equality multipliers used by the proof. Minimal-sparsity research explicitly seeks tractable subsets of higher-order moment structure; it cannot be assumed to contain every consequence of the full relaxation. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19232)

Also, **148 of the 160 saved cuts are lower-side cuts; only 12 are upper-side cuts**. Proving only the unaugmented order-two upper implication would therefore have left most of the executed construction unclassified.

**The surviving mathematical interpretation is an analytic projection of low-order moment consistency. Whether that projection is computationally valuable remains a separate question.**

## 6. Research disposition

The conclusion should not be overstated in either direction.

**What survives:** the inequalities are valid; the selected benchmark is now exactly identified; the numerical gain is reproducible and computationally certified; and the projected constraints can be imposed without introducing the complete higher-order moment representation.

**What does not survive as a justification:** treating reciprocal coherence as consistency beyond established moment machinery. The explicit degree-four and degree-six certificates show where that consistency already resides.

**What remains unresolved:** whether this particular projection delivers a better certificate-versus-total-cost tradeoff than the strongest available implementations, and whether it does so across consequential hard instances.

The only defensible continuation for this mechanism is therefore a **matched original-implementation comparison with an incremental-cut ablation**. Its purpose would be to establish an engineering advantage of the projection—not to manufacture a foundational claim by generating more pairwise cuts.

Until that comparison succeeds, this mechanism should remain reusable optimization machinery rather than an Astra flagship discovery. **The numerical effect is established for the pinned case; the claim of new global-optimization power beyond the strongest existing solutions is not.**
