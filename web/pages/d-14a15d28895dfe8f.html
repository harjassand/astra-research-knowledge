# Nearest primary prior and an effective curvature certificate

Status7October2026: prior analytic sections read, full prior moduli/existence proofs not independently audited. The quantitative inequality below is our elementary deduction from the explicitly stated adjoint witness. It is submitted for independent programme review and makes no priority claim.

## What the prior changes

Du Yun-Heng and Xie Song-Yan, *Ample vector bundles without Griffiths-semipositive metrics*, arXiv2609.26504v1, submitted22September2026 14:36:01UTC, prove an adjoint Levi obstruction and claim an ample stable rank-two example on an abelian surface (Theorem1.1). Their §2.1–2.3 was read technically, especially Propositions2.1–2.4 and Corollary2.5. Their irreducible-moduli bridge in §4 was sampled for scope, not fully independently reconstructed.

Their curvature obstruction is already **strict and uniform**: a fixed positive separable tensor Q tests horizontal Levi forms, integration by parts gives an adjoint coefficient S_J, and S_J≤−ηId excludes every seminegative Hermitian metric on F. Corollary2.5 supplies η=5π/8 throughout one C² neighbourhood of a split holomorphic structure. Therefore the general phenomenon “positive curvature-error floor” is not plausibly novel merely because the main theorem states exact semipositivity exclusion. The explicit quadric graph bundle and its all-power/all-twist consequences remain different objects; novelty of those exact formulations is UNKNOWN.

## A directly usable quantitative witness

Let X be compact, F a holomorphic bundle with fixed smooth background k, ω a fixed tangent Hermitian form, dV a fixed positive volume, and Q a smooth separable positive tensor on T_X⊗F. The horizontal lift is defined using the fixed Chern connection of k. For a candidate Hermitian metric h'=kA, let

    I_J(h')=∫⟨Q, horizontal-Levi_J(h')⟩dV=∫tr(S_J A)dV.

Suppose a checked witness gives S_J≤−ηId with η>0. Contract Q in the tangent factor with ω to obtain a positive fibre endomorphism W_Q. In a decomposition Q=Σν_i P_{u_i}⊗P_{v_i},

    W_Q=Σν_i ω(u_i,u_i) P_{v_i}.

This definition is independent of the separable decomposition because it is a linear tensor contraction. Let C=sup_X λ_max(W_Q), measured in k. C is finite on the compact base and positive for a nonzero strict witness.

If the Chern curvature of h' on F satisfies R_h'≤εωId in the Griffiths sense, the squared-norm Schur-complement identity gives, for **any** chosen fibre-coordinate lift w,

    Levi(||v||²_h')(u,w)
      = ||w+h'⁻¹(h')_u v||²_h' − h'(R_h'(u,ū)v,v)
      ≥ −ε ω(u,u) h'(v,v).

In particular it holds on the fixed background horizontal lift used in I_J. Pairing with separable Q gives

    I_J(h') ≥ −ε∫tr(W_Q A)dV ≥ −ε C∫tr(A)dV.

The strict adjoint inequality gives the other bound I_J(h')≤−η∫tr(A)dV. Positive-definiteness makes ∫tr(A)>0. Hence **ε≥η/C**. By duality, every smooth metric on F* has Griffiths lower-curvature error at least η/C relative to ω. Equality is not excluded by this non-strict estimate; all ε<η/C are excluded.

This is an effective certificate **when Q,S_J,η and C are supplied or rigorously acquired**. It does not produce such a Q for the quadric graph bundle merely from its compactness obstruction.

## Literal Du–Xie constants and scope

In their §2.3 use the tangent Hermitian form given by H=diag(4,5), and define

    A₁=[[8,1+13i],[1−13i,15]],
    A₂=[[12,−1−13i],[−1+13i,10]],
    C_s=H^(−1/2) A_s H^(−1/2),
    Q_s=(23/4)Id−C_s.

The source's exact identities are tr(C_s)=5, det(C_s)=−5/2, tr(Q_s)=13/2, det(Q_s)=29/16 and tr(C_sQ_s)=−5/4. Q_s is positive-definite. With split line projections P_s, Q₀=Q₁⊗P₁+Q₂⊗P₂, so W_Q₀=(13/2)Id. Proposition2.4 supplies S_J₀=−(5π/4)Id and Corollary2.5 supplies S_J≤−(5π/8)Id in a C² neighbourhood.

Thus, with **their unscaled curvature convention and the tangent Hermitian form H**:

* the split model excludes every lower-curvature error ε<5π/26 on its dual;
* every holomorphic structure in the stated witness neighbourhood excludes ε<5π/52.

These numerical ratios follow from the certificate above. The split model is not ample, so the first number is not an explicit ample example. The source claims ample points in the witness neighbourhood via a moduli-density argument; identifying a concrete algebraic ample point there and certifying its connection-distance bound remains a separate acquisition problem. The bound must be rescaled if one changes the curvature normalization or tangent form. No transfer of these particular numbers to the quadric graph bundle is claimed.

## Downstream decision

The family050 compactness proof establishes a positive but ineffective gap for an explicit algebraic bundle. The prior adjoint proof identifies exactly what an effective replacement would need: a positive separable differential-test tensor whose formal adjoint has a certified strict negative upper bound. A meaningful next construction is to acquire that witness for the explicit graph bundle, or show why its obstruction is not represented by such a smooth finite certificate. Searching more numerical metrics without acquiring this witness would not address the missing lemma.

No result in these prior statements repairs the absent properness/cocompactness or polynomial critical-envelope hypotheses in the accumulated Keller/Hessian routes.
