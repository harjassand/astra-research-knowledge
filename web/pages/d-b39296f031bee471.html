# Cycle3: implicit exact full-transcript converter and finite expected-budget certificate

Independent checker: check_selection. 2026-10-06. Prior cycle artifacts preserved. No subworkers. Mathematical and exact-code audit passed under the specified ideal finite source/randomness interface. This is an explicit symmetric supplied-law construction, not autonomous learning or external validation.

## Exact interface

There are three unknown states i=0,1,2 and three measurement actions a. A source reading X is Bernoulli with P_i^a(X=1)=1/10 if a=i and 9/10 otherwise; source action costs are one. The target is the full length-n iid ternary transcript, with per-reading law 4/5 on symbol i and 1/10 on each other symbol. Target measurement cost is n. Source outputs the entire transcript, exactly in law in every state. Auxiliary independent random bits and ordinary computation are allowed and counted separately from source measurements. Only expected source measurement cost is constrained; individual runs may use more than n readings.

For histogram c=(c_0,c_1,c_2), Σc_i=n,

    H_i(c)=n!/(c_0!c_1!c_2!) * 8^{c_i}/10^n.

Thus H_i/H_k=8^{c_i-c_k} and all ratio calculations omit the multinomial coefficient. A source converter need not enumerate O(n^2) histograms, still less 3^n complete transcripts. Generate a proposal from H_k with n iid ternary draws and count its symbols; all verifier/acceptance calculations depend only on c. Keeping the accepted proposed transcript produces a uniform conditional multiset ordering automatically. If only histograms are generated, perform a separate state-independent uniform multiset ordering. Either method yields the full trace, not just a compressed statistic.

## Initial classification and scalar verifier

For r readings per source action, classify the action with the smallest success count, breaking ties uniformly. Its confusion matrix is permutation-equivariant with diagonal 1-2b and common off-diagonal b. Under true state i, pairwise competitor event C_i≥C_j has probability at most (9/25)^r: the exponential Markov factor at e^t=9 is

    [(.9+.1*9)(.1+.9/9)]^r=(.36)^r.

Consequently total classification error is at most 2(9/25)^r and b≤(9/25)^r. Uniform ties are essential for the stated symmetry.

Set η=1/(30n), g=log(1/η). Choose r_0 so total preliminary error≤η. Classify candidate k, propose c from H_k, and measure only action k. Every candidate-versus-alternative log likelihood moves simultaneously by ±log9: upward probability .9 under true k and .1 under every other state. Let

    L=ceil(log_9(1/η)),
    U_k(c)=ceil(log_9(max_{j≠k}max(1,H_k(c)/H_j(c))/η)).

Reject on score reaching -L; accept on reaching +U_k(c). This enforces the positive-part output-specific success thresholds and reverse rejection. All barriers can be computed by integer comparisons to powers of 8 and 9, without logarithmic floating-point errors.

For any U,L≥1, exact probabilities are

    p_correct(U,L)=9^U(9^L-1)/(9^{U+L}-1),
    p_wrong(U,L)=(9^L-1)/(9^{U+L}-1).

Their expected reading counts are respectively

    [(U+L)p_correct-L]/(4/5) ≤(5/4)U,
    [L-(U+L)p_wrong]/(4/5) ≤(5/4)L.

These formulas are the scalar gambler-ruin solution; the earlier exact finite certificate verified its harmonic equations. They are rational and integrable.

Let A_0 be the preliminary confusion matrix. Thin accepted proposals by a=(1-η)/(1+2η). The accepted histogram submass is

    Q_i(c)=a Σ_k A_0(i,k) H_k(c) p_i(k,c),

using the appropriate correct/wrong probability. Define T_i(c)=H_i(c)-Q_i(c). The verified own/cross likelihood inequalities imply pointwise

    ηH_i(c)≤T_i(c)≤6ηH_i(c).

The upper bound follows from retaining only the own-state proposal contribution, whose classification success is ≥1-η and verifier success is ≥1-2η. The usual algebra gives missing mass at most 6η, and the same pointwise own-contribution argument gives the claimed stronger bound.

## Common residual mass is load bearing

Write δ_i=Σ_c T_i(c). Target histogram laws, uniform-tie classifier, scalar barriers, verifier probabilities and thinning all commute with every permutation of state/action/symbol labels. Therefore T_{π(i)}(π(c))=T_i(c). Permuting the finite histogram space proves δ_i=δ for every state. Also η≤δ≤6η.

It is not enough that the target alone is symmetric; asymmetric tie breaking, label-dependent thresholds, classifier designs or thinning can destroy this equality. Without common δ the implicit row-wise rejection sampler below normalizes rows by different factors and the inverse-confusion repair no longer reproduces the required residual experiment.

## Exact repair without evaluating δ or a histogram table

Take r_R balanced readings per action, again uniformly breaking ties. Its confusion matrix is A_R=(1-3b)I+bJ, where J is the all-ones matrix. Choose r_R so

    b≤(9/25)^{r_R}≤1/(18*8^n),   b≤1/6.

Its exact inverse acting on unnormalized residual columns is

    W_k(c)=(T_k(c)-b Σ_i T_i(c))/(1-3b).

Positivity follows from

    b Σ_i T_i(c)≤6bη Σ_i H_i(c)
                  ≤18bη8^n H_k(c)≤ηH_k(c)≤T_k(c).

Here every H_i/H_k≤8^n. Also W_k≤T_k/(1-3b)≤12ηH_k. All rows W_k have total mass δ, since each T_i row has mass δ. Given repair classification k, propose c~H_k and accept with probability

    W_k(c)/(12ηH_k(c)).

This probability lies in [0,1] and is computable from exact rational ratios H_i/H_k and the exact finite classifier parameter b. Its average is δ/(12η), which lies in [1/12,1/2]. Repeated proposals therefore terminate almost surely with expected proposal count at most 12 and accepted histogram law W_k/δ, without computing δ.

The fresh repair classifier under true state i chooses row k with probability A_R(i,k). Therefore the repaired law is (A_R W)_i/δ=T_i/δ. Multiplying by the rejection/thinning branch probability δ and adding accepted submass gives Q_i+T_i=H_i exactly. Fresh source measurements and auxiliary bits after the flag are required; conditioning on the flag must not alter the repair classifier's law.

All acceptance factors depend only on histograms. Conditional transcript ordering remains uniform, so the final full length-n transcript law is exactly the target's in every state. No target-state posterior or target histogram table is required.

## Computational and scientific scope

The implicit construction removes the cycle2 exponential transcript table and the need to evaluate the global missing mass. Histogram proposals take O(n) target auxiliary draws; a repair branch needs at most 12 such proposals in expectation. Classifier probability b is a finite binomial/tie sum with r_R=O(n), computable exactly. Powers/acceptance fractions have O(n) bit length for this fixed-law family, but exact compiler CPU, peak memory and auxiliary random bits must be measured rather than equated with source observations.

Uniform categorical proposals and Bernoulli acceptance from exact rational probabilities can use unbiased random-bit rejection; their bit cost is finite but not zero as a computational resource. A seeded pseudorandom implementation is evidence of the arithmetic algorithm, not independent physical sampling validation. This is a concrete executable-statistical route for a special symmetric model. It does not give an efficient universal converter for arbitrary known menus, nor unknown-law acquisition or broad scientific capability.

Nearest prior art remains the exact fixed-source conversion mechanism in Li–Liu arXiv:2608.19897v2 and classical controlled sensing. The implicit symmetric residual normalization and finite-budget certificate are our deductions; novelty against all Bernoulli-factory/simulation literature is unverified. Next falsifiable checks: inspect the sampler's exact barrier comparisons and classifier b; verify small-n full transcript laws exhaustively; test extreme histograms at n=1024 for nonnegative acceptance; count the actual auxiliary bits, maximum arithmetic size, compiler CPU and realized source measurements.

## General δ-free repair: symmetry can be removed

Parent proposed a stronger repair loop, independently verified here. Suppose ηH_i≤T_i≤CηH_i pointwise but residual row masses δ_i differ. Choose a classifier matrix A near identity with

    ||A^{-1}-I||∞≤e^{-Δ(H)}/(2C).

Then W=A^{-1}T obeys (η/2)H_k≤W_k≤(C+1/2)ηH_k. Choose M≥C+1/2. On EVERY repair attempt, draw a fresh source classifier k with law A_i, then propose y~H_k and accept with W_k(y)/(MηH_k(y)). The state-i one-attempt output submass is

    Σ_k A_i(k)H_k(y) W_k(y)/(MηH_k(y))
      =T_i(y)/(Mη).

Its acceptance probability is δ_i/(Mη)≥1/M, and repetition gives the required residual law T_i/δ_i without computing any δ_i. The classification must be repeated on every attempt; classifying once and normalizing each W row separately is generally wrong when δ_i differ.

If each classifier takes N_A source readings, the unconditional original-failure repair cost is exactly

    δ_i * (Mη/δ_i) * N_A = MηN_A.

Thus the inverse correction still costs O(ηΔ(H)) measurements even though physical classification repeats; the common-mass symmetry assumption is unnecessary for the joint proposal mechanism. The remaining computational requirement is pointwise evaluation of T_i. Current symmetric source scalar verifiers support exact evaluation for arbitrary supplied rational iid categorical target rows using histogram probability ratios, so the implicit approach extends beyond symmetric targets.

## Additional equality-boundary obstruction audited

Let F be symmetric binary(.1,.9), with both directed KL d_9=.8log9 and every stopped-record likelihood ratio in9^Z. Let G reveal which branch was selected: symmetric binary(.2,.8) with probability α, or symmetric binary(1/17,16/17) otherwise. Set d_4=.6log4, d_16=(15/17)log16 and α=(d_16-d_9)/(d_16-d_4), strictly between0 and1. Both directed G KLs equal d_9 exactly, while G stopped-record likelihood ratios lie in4^Z. All probabilities have full support.

If F exactly reproduces G^n with E_iN≤n, stopped-record KL plus data processing gives n d_9≤d_9 E_iN≤n d_9. Equality of KL data processing forces the source likelihood ratio to equal the terminal-output likelihood ratio almost surely (strict conditional Jensen). A positive G^n output consisting of n branch4 outcomes favoring state0 has ratio4^n, impossible in9^Z by prime factorization. Conversely, G cannot reproduce F^n within the same expected budget because positive F outputs have ratio9^n outside4^Z. State-independent auxiliary randomization does not change the stopped-record likelihood lattice.

Therefore the universal first-order coefficient equals1 in both directions but exact same-budget conversion fails for every positive integer n in both directions. The strict-coefficient generic characterization cannot be extended by replacing '<1' with'≤1'. This is an analytic obstruction, not a finite test; broader novelty of the example remains unverified.
