Worker c01_l01. Date: 2026-10-07 UTC.

I checked an additional primary source directly: Kathuria and Deshpande, “On Sampling and Greedy MAP Inference of Constrained Determinantal Point Processes,” arXiv:1607.01551v1 (2016), §§2–3.1, https://arxiv.org/html/1607.01551v1; conference version: Celis et al., “On the Complexity of Constrained Determinantal Point Processes,” LIPIcs APPROX/RANDOM 2017, https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.APPROX-RANDOM.2017.36.

Their Partition-DPP conditions on a fixed quota vector (k_1,...,k_p) over a partition P_1⊔...⊔P_p of the DPP ground set, i.e. |S∩P_i|=k_i for every i. They derive conditional probabilities from a multivariate characteristic polynomial and give exact sampling in time O(m^{p+4} n k / p^p) in the stated algorithm, hence polynomial for a fixed constant number p of parts. This is a determinant-volume sampler under constraints, but it is a sequential exact sampler based on computing partition-polynomial coefficients, not a Markov pair-energy theorem.

For c_k(F), the natural ground set is the 2n columns (e_i,f_i), partitioned into n coordinate-pair blocks. The target samples only 2k-subsets that are unions of k whole blocks. This is a mixture over quota vectors with k_i∈{0,2} and Σ_i k_i=2k; there are binomial(n,k) such vectors, and the mixture weights are exactly the block-complete Gram minors det((V*V)[P(I)]). The cited algorithm handles one fixed quota vector; taking p=n leaves its runtime exponential in n, and the paper does not give an efficient way to sample the quota vector from those unknown polynomial coefficients. Using two parts for all e-columns and all f-columns would impose only k selections in each half and would permit mismatched coordinates, so it has the wrong support.

Therefore constrained-DPP literature provides a closer algebraic model and a useful exact algorithm at constant partition count, but does not close the hard-BCS target's n-block all-or-none mixture or produce the desired microscopic pair kernel. The support counterexample from INITIAL still blocks the homogeneous strongly-Rayleigh exchange route. No hardness theorem from the constrained-DPP paper is being transferred to c_k(F); their general constraint hardness is a separate result with a different interface.
