## Outcome and escalation status

The surviving result is modest: population span prediction can be admitted without ambient latent-rank diversity. This changes the correct task-admission boundary and supplies a direct observed-law comparator, but the proof is elementary and nearby prior art is substantial. Do not promote it as a foundational breakthrough.

The tail lower bound is routine and is paired with a relative-quantile control matching its leading exponential rate. A supplied bounded-degree polynomial decoder class makes the dose dependence polynomial using only unpaired observed source samples. This is a standard conditional closure baseline; acquiring or validating that global decoder class remains unsolved. Both results are recorded in COVERAGE_AND_CLOSURE.md, including a finite negative fixture.

Unexplored alternatives preserved: a quantitative mean-point graph theorem under finite-dimensional mechanism complexity; characterization of BlockExo's separation margins from scientific error models; acquisition of trustworthy action embeddings; exact factor-model identification beyond the older paper's restricted product-ratio transformations. No claim that these are new open problems in the literature or that they are resolved here.

Next useful gate is independent proof audit plus prior-art checking of the partial-rank corollary. If retained, a frozen rank-deficient benchmark should compare against density-ratio/likelihood fitting and direct scalar quantile extrapolation with the same source observations, labels, class information, computation and coverage. There is currently no architecture-specific win.
