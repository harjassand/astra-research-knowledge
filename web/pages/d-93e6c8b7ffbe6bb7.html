T02 — Multi-copy inverse-multiplicity coupling: exact extension and obstruction
Version: v1 (frozen mathematical record, 2026-10-09)
Status: internally derived theorem; small-family computation finite-tested; Frankl target unresolved. No novelty or priority claim.

Target
------
For every finite union-closed family F of distinct subsets with at least one nonempty member, prove max_i P(i in A) >= 1/2 for A uniform on F. The construction below is an exact k-copy extension of the inverse-union-multiplicity projection used in Jiang, arXiv:2609.08291v1, §10. It is valid for all k>=2, but the derived lower bound degenerates with family size and the natural residual can decay exponentially in k even at the Boolean-cube equality example. It does not resolve the target.

Definitions
-----------
Write N=|F|>=2 and T=union(F), which belongs to F by finite union closure. For k>=2 and S in F, define

  r_k(S) = |{(A_1,...,A_k) in F^k : A_1 union ... union A_k = S}|.

Let P_0 be the product-uniform law on F^k. Let U=A_1 union ... union A_k and set

  Q_k(A_1,...,A_k) = 1/[N r_k(U)].

This is a probability law: summing over the r_k(S) tuples in each fiber gives mass 1/N to each S. Thus under Q_k, U is uniform on F. Let C_k be all joint laws on F^k whose k coordinate marginals are uniform on F. It contains P_0. Let P_* minimize D(P||Q_k) on C_k, and write nu_* and nu_0 for the laws of U under P_* and P_0.

Theorem (k-copy balanced entropy gain)
---------------------------------------
Define, under P_0,

  c = log r_k(U),  g_j(A_j) = E_0[c | A_j],  mu = E_0 c,
  R_k = c - sum_{j=1}^k g_j(A_j) + (k-1)mu,
  V_k = E_0[R_k^2].

Then P_* exists, is unique and strictly positive, and has the form

  P_*(A_1,...,A_k) = (product_j a_j(A_j))/r_k(U)

for positive scaling factors a_j chosen to give uniform coordinate marginals. Moreover,

  H(nu_*) - H(nu_0) >= D(P_0||P_*)
                    >= [1/(2 k^2 log N)] [1 - 1/(2 k^2 log N)] V_k
                    >= V_k/(4 k^2 log N)
                    >= log^2(N^k-(N-1)^k)/(2^(k+2) k^2 N^k log N) > 0.

All logarithms are natural. The bound is uniform over F but tends to zero with N for every fixed k. The stronger quantity V_k is family-dependent; no frequency-sensitive lower bound for it is established here.

Proof
-----
The feasible set C_k is a nonempty compact polytope, and D(P||Q_k) is continuous and strictly convex because Q_k is positive. Thus it has a unique minimizer. The minimizer is positive: if a cell had zero mass, moving a small amount toward the positive feasible law P_0 would make the directional derivative of the t log t term at that cell tend to minus infinity. Lagrange multipliers for the k marginal constraints then give log(P_*/Q_k) as a constant plus a sum of k one-coordinate functions. Since log Q_k = -log N - log r_k(U), exponentiation gives the asserted scaling form.

For any P in C_k, let nu_P be the union law. Decomposing relative entropy by the fibers U=S gives

  D(P||Q_k) = D(nu_P||Unif(F))
      + sum_S nu_P(S) D(P(.|U=S)||Unif({tuples with union S})).

Consequently log N-H(nu_P) <= D(P||Q_k). Under P_0, every conditional fiber is uniform, so

  D(P_0||Q_k) = log N-H(nu_0).

The multiplier formula implies the projection identity

  D(P_0||Q_k) = D(P_0||P_*) + D(P_*||Q_k).

Indeed, the difference between the two sides is the integral of log(P_*/Q_k), a sum of one-coordinate functions and a constant, against P_0-P_*; all such terms vanish because the marginals agree. Combining the last three displays gives

  H(nu_*)-H(nu_0) >= D(P_0||P_*).

Under the product law, the coordinates A_j are independent. For each j, E_0[R_k|A_j]=0: the conditional term g_j cancels, and each other g_l averages to mu. Hence E_0 R_k=0 and E_0(R_k c)=E_0(R_k^2)=V_k, since c-R_k is a sum of one-coordinate functions and a constant. Also 0<=c,g_j,mu<=k log N. Therefore

  -k^2 log N <= R_k <= k^2 log N.

Put epsilon=1/(2 k^2 log N), which is below 1/2 for k>=2,N>=2, and define

  P_epsilon = P_0(1-epsilon R_k).

This is a positive probability law with the same k uniform marginals as P_0. Using D(P||P_0)<=chi^2(P,P_0),

  D(P_epsilon||P_0) <= epsilon^2 V_k.

Since log(P_0/Q_k)=c-(k-1)log N and E_epsilon c=mu-epsilon V_k,

  D(P_epsilon||Q_k)
    <= D(P_0||Q_k)-epsilon(1-epsilon)V_k.

Minimality gives D(P_*||Q_k)<=D(P_epsilon||Q_k); the projection identity then yields the stated gain. Since epsilon<=1/2, epsilon(1-epsilon)>=epsilon/2=1/(4k^2 log N).

It remains to lower-bound V_k. Choose a minimal member M of F. A tuple has union M only if each coordinate is a member contained in M; minimality forces every coordinate to equal M. Thus r_k(M)=1. Every tuple with at least one coordinate equal to T has union T, so

  r_k(T) >= N^k-(N-1)^k.

Here M and T are distinct because N>=2. In the 2^k corners with each coordinate chosen from {M,T}, c is zero at (M,...,M) and equals log r_k(T) at every other corner. The full alternating difference over these corners therefore has magnitude log r_k(T). It annihilates every constant and every one-coordinate function, so it has the same value on R_k. Cauchy-Schwarz gives

  log^2 r_k(T) <= 2^k sum_corners R_k^2 <= 2^k N^k V_k.

Substitution proves the final lower bound. This completes the derivation.

Exact Boolean-cube obstruction to increasing k
-----------------------------------------------
Take F=2^[n], so N=2^n and every coordinate has frequency 1/2. For each S,

  r_k(S)=(2^k-1)^|S|,
  c = log(2^k-1) |U|.

Under P_0, the union bit at a fixed ground element is the OR Y of k independent fair bits. Its mean is 1-2^-k. Its one-slot conditional expectation has first-order component 2^-k(2X_j-1). Thus the one-coordinate part removed from Y is its mean plus these k first-order terms, and its residual has variance

  Var(Y - E Y - 2^-k sum_j(2X_j-1))
    = 2^-k-(k+1)2^-2k.

Different ground elements are independent, so exactly

  V_k = n log^2(2^k-1) [2^-k-(k+1)2^-2k],
  V_k/log N = log^2(2^k-1)[2^-k-(k+1)2^-2k]/log 2.

The second quantity tends to zero like k^2 log(2)/2^k. Hence the natural first-order-balanced log-multiplicity residual does not become stronger with more replicas, even on the sharp equality family. This is not a counterexample to Frankl or to a different multi-copy invariant; it refutes the specific hope that V_k/log N grows without bound or stays bounded below by a positive constant as k increases. The all-corners lower bound above is also visibly too weak in this example.

Prior-art boundary and current status (checked 2026-10-09)
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Yunjiang Jiang, “Entropy bounds and global couplings for union-closed families,” arXiv:2609.08291v1, submitted 2026-09-08, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08291. Sections 10.1–10.7 define the pair multiplicity r(S), the inverse-multiplicity law Q(A,B)=1/[N r(A union B)], the projection P_*(A,B)=a(A)a(B)/r(A union B), the exact KL projection identity, and the double-centered statistic R_AB. Theorem 10.2 proves H(nu_*)-H(nu_0)>=V/(16 log N)>=log^2(2N-1)/(64N^2 log N). Proposition 10.3 gives a positive size-dependent frequency correction above 0.38288525 and §10.7 states that the bound does not determine whether gain stays comparable to log N at small maximum frequency. The pair case k=2 of the theorem here reproduces the same order and the same explicit multiplicity lower bound; it is prior art, not a discovery of this worker.
2. Simone Costa and Ankan Sadhu, “Beyond Liu’s 0.382709 threshold for the union-closed sets conjecture,” arXiv:2610.02295v1, submitted 2026-10-01, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02295. Theorem 1.2 gives c_UC>=0.3828852549667978; §7 bounds the same product-relaxation method by 0.3828852599667.... This is a more recent pointwise/protocol boundary. It does not rule out a global invariant, but makes another scalar product optimization an inadequate route to the target.
3. Gilmer, “A constant lower bound for the union-closed sets conjecture,” arXiv:2211.09055v2, https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09055, is the independent-sampling starting point, and its proposed KL strengthening was subsequently refuted; the current proof status should not be inferred from that early manuscript alone.

The two 2026 sources above still present Frankl’s conjecture as open and establish lower bounds below 1/2. No source located here establishes the target. The k-copy projection itself is a direct finite-dimensional extension of Jiang’s pair construction; no priority claim is made. The new candidate is only the interaction residual R_k and its exact Boolean-cube profile as an avenue to examine, not a claimed new frequency theorem.

Remaining obstruction and next mathematical gate
------------------------------------------------
The derived theorem controls the entropy gain by V_k, but its universal corner certificate is of order log^2(N^k-(N-1)^k)/(2^k N^k log N), which vanishes with N. The exact cube formula further shows the normalized residual can vanish exponentially in k. To reach 1/2, one needs a frequency-sensitive inequality that uses more than this scalar log-multiplicity residual; for example, a lower bound on a different coupling invariant conditioned on p<1/2, or a chain-level construction whose useful entropy survives after union multiplicities concentrate at T. No such inequality is proved in v1. The assignment objective remains unresolved.
