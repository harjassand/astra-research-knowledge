## Load-bearing premises

The law mu_h=<v,E^h v> has a positive spectral measure only because E is supplied to be self-adjoint, 0<=E<=I, and the known preparation/readout use the same normalized v in the declared inner product. Correct resets, pulse units, linearity, stationarity, iid Gaussian errors, protocol-independent noise variance and known assay costs are also supplied. Neither moment fitting nor zero-noise calibration learns these assumptions. In particular, using zero-protocol variance to calibrate all pulse means would be unsound under unrestricted heteroskedasticity. The source report explicitly declares the common-variance Gaussian model, so this is a scope boundary rather than an error.

## Two-moment closure and approximation

Spectral calculus gives mu_h=int x^h dnu(x) for a probability measure on[0,1]. Then r=mu2-mu1^2=int(x-a)^2 dnu is nonnegative. For integer h>=2, Taylor's integral remainder about a with f'' in[0,h(h-1)] and zero first centred moment proves 0<=mu_h-a^h<=binom(h,2)r. Positivity is essential. Exact r=0 implies support at a (or Ev=av), giving closure for all integer h; the converse follows already from h1,2.

Clipping estimated moments to[0,1] is nonexpansive because true moments lie there. The rL/rU intervals and prediction bound h*w+binom(h,2)rU are valid on the shared joint confidence event. The claimed rU<=r+6w bound is conservative but sound, including the a<2w clipping case. Therefore w=tau/[2(H+6C)] with r<=tau/(2C) is a sufficient certification condition. Failure of that condition or an UNKNOWN output is not evidence that the target approximation is infeasible.

## Four-moment positive quadrature

For r>0, the compression of multiplication by x to span{1,(x-a)/sqrt(r)} is the symmetric matrix [[a,sqrt(r)],[sqrt(r),e]], with e=(mu3-2a mu2+a^3)/r. Its spectrum lies in[0,1] by compression of a positive contraction. Its off-diagonal entry is positive, so its two nodes are distinct and its e1 spectral weights are positive. Trace T=(mu3-a mu2)/r and determinant D=T*a-mu2 are correct. The compressed measure matches moments0 through3.

For p(x)=x^2-Tx+D, orthogonality int p=int xp=0 gives

    int p^2 = int x^2 p = mu4-T mu3+D mu2 = rho >=0.

At the two nodes p=0. Cubic Hermite interpolation of x^h yields a pointwise remainder f''''(xi)p(x)^2/24. Its derivative is nonnegative and bounded by24*binom(h,4) on[0,1] for h>=4. Integration and degree3 matching prove 0<=mu_h-muhat_h<=binom(h,4)rho. At rho=0 the original measure is supported on the roots of p, yielding exact global output recurrence without a microscopic dimension bound. This conclusion is valid in the promised positive spectral-measure class; it does not acquire that class.

The independent code generated80 weighted rational measures with up to6 support points, including endpoints. It separately computed residual squares and the trace/determinant recurrence, checked positive-contraction principal minors, moment matching0 through3, and both approximation bounds through h12 using exact fractions. All passed. These finite checks supplement the universal Hermite derivation; they do not establish it by enumeration.

## Noisy four-moment extension

rhat>=rL>0, |r-rhat|<=3w, and the numerator error is at most8w. Since e is in[0,1], division by rhat and clipping give |e-ehat|<=11w/rL. The stated square-root error bound3w/sqrt(rL) is sound (a factor2 improvement is possible because both variances are at least rL, but unnecessary).

Spectral clipping onto the convex set of symmetric positive contractions is Frobenius projection. Its nonexpansiveness, followed by operator norm<=Frobenius norm, gives the proposed kappa. Telescoping powers of contractions yields h*kappa. Combining this with the mu4 confidence interval gives a valid upper rhoU; triangle inequality then gives h*kappa+binom(h,4)rhoU. No four-noisy-moment acquisition algorithm or optimizer is implemented in the audited code. The report explicitly labels this a mathematical extension, correctly.

## Supplementary audit: arbitrary finite integer-lag checklist

The later cyclic extension is sound for every n=q+1>=3. This supplementary review read the added report section, the `cyclic_family()` implementation and its saved result; it did not rerun or broaden the experiment. Earlier snapshots remain preserved. New source snapshots and hashes are recorded separately in `sources/check_causal/cycle3/cyclic_extension_sources.json`.

In the cyclic orthonormal basis, D0=diag(3/5,1/2,...,1/2) and N is the cyclic permutation. Expand (D0+epsilon*N)^h as noncommuting words. A word containing ell factors N takes the first basis vector to basis vector ell modulo n; intervening D0 factors scale without changing that index. For h<n only the word with ell=0 contributes to the first-coordinate readout, giving mu_h=(3/5)^h. At h=n, the sole additional contributing word is epsilon^n*N^n, giving mu_n=(3/5)^n+epsilon^n. This proves the universal moment identities without enumerating dimensions.

For physical admissibility, use the induced Euclidean operator norm. The physically rotated N is orthogonal, so its norm is1 and each entry has absolute value at most1. Physical off-diagonal entries of E are therefore at least (1/10n)-(1/100n)=9/(100n)>0; diagonal entries are positive too. Since ||I-E||<=1/2+epsilon<1, the real logarithm series converges and exponentiating it recovers E.

The log perturbation bound does not require D0 and N to commute. With A=I-E, B=I-D0, telescoping A^k-B^k gives

    ||A^k-B^k|| <= (1/2+epsilon)^k-(1/2)^k.

Summing the log series yields ||log E-log D0||<=-log(1-2epsilon). With epsilon=1/(100n), this is strictly below1/(50n-1), which is below1/(6n). Meanwhile log(6/5)>1/6, so the baseline off-diagonals log(6/5)/n remain strictly positive. The baseline column sums are log(3/5). Their perturbation is at most sqrt(n)/(50n-1)<1/49: squaring reduces the latter inequality to (n-1)(2500n-1)>0. Finally log(5/3)>2/5>1/49. Every column sum of log E is consequently negative. Thus both comparators are genuine finite-rate interconversion/degradation systems on the same n-species apparatus with the same normalized uniform pulse and aggregate readout.

The precise obstruction is: given any predetermined finite set of nonnegative integer lags, choose q at least its maximum and2. Two admissible systems agree at every requested lag, one with exact one-rate aggregate closure and one without it. The construction does not prove failure for arbitrary noninteger waits, an adaptive assay scheme with a supplied dimension bound, or species-resolved access. Lag q+1 separates this particular pair. Its gap (100n)^(-n) also becomes tiny, so this is an exact finite-menu nonidentifiability argument rather than an efficient noisy discrimination result or a uniform separation-margin theorem. No additional scientific novelty claim is made.
