STATEMENT
For an initial permutation-invariant thermal state, scalar on every spin
multiplicity space, the stationary state of zero-temperature or finite-nu
collective decay is exactly2-producible: a classical mixture of disjoint
singlet pairs and one-qubit product factors. When the R/E witness proves
entanglement, its stationary entanglement depth is EXACTLY2. For N>=3 this
rules out genuine N-partite entanglement; at N2 depth2 is ordinary bipartite
entanglement. No such upper bound is inferred for arbitrary non-PI inputs.

1. EXACT SECTOR TWIRL
Let b=N/2-j, K=N-2b=2j and d_b=binom(N,b)-binom(N,b-1). Place b singlets
on the disjoint labeled pairs(1,2),(3,4),...,(2b-1,2b), and put any density
sigma_K on the symmetric spin-K/2 subspace of the remaining K qubits.
Each singlet has total spin0. This canonical embedding therefore has only
spin j, with density sigma_j tensor |v_b><v_b| in V_j tensor M_j. The same
normalized multiplicity vector v_b is independent of m because the embedding
intertwines the collective SU2 action.

Permutations commute with that SU2 action and act on M_j as the irreducible
two-row S_N representation. Schur's lemma therefore gives the EXACT identity
 (1/N!)sum_(pi in S_N) U_pi
       [(|singlet><singlet|)^tensor b tensor sigma_K] U_pi^dagger
 =sigma_j tensor I_(d_b)/d_b,                             (1)
embedded in the original N-qubit space. No spin sector is lost, and the
normalization is fixed by trace1. In particular a uniform permutation of
canonical disjoint pairs gives the required uniform multiplicity operator.

2. STATIONARY SPIN FACTOR IS IID SEPARABLE
For the finite thermal bath set z=nu/(nu+1) in(0,1); the spin factor is

It is a common local filter of the symmetric identity: F=|g><g|+sqrtz|e><e|,

identity is the positive uniform coherent resolution, so sigma_K(z) is
exactly an iid-product coherent mixture. At zero temperature z0 use
|g><g|^tensor K. K0 is scalar. The z1 limiting law is Pi_sym,K/(K+1).

An explicit probability density for the excited population x is
 f_(K,z)(x)=(K+1)z^(K+1)/[S_K(z)(z+(1-z)x)^(K+2)], x0..1.  (2)
Its positive Bernstein integrals are exactly z^r/S_K. One direct proof starts
from the uniform coherent population x0, applies F, and sets

The CDF and inverse are elementary:

3. WHOLE STATIONARY STATE AND DEPTH
The thermal initial state has the scalar sector weights q_b derived in
COLLECTIVE_DECAY_ENTANGLEMENT.txt. Collective dynamics keeps these weights
and the uniform multiplicity operators. Its stationary state is therefore
 rho_infty=sum_b q_b [sigma_(K,z) tensor I_(d_b)/d_b].       (4)
Substitute (1)-(2). Every pure component is a product of b two-qubit singlets
