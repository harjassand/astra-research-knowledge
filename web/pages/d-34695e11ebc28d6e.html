# Failed and narrowed routes

## 1. Attributing arbitrary edge-varying channels to the released N49 theorem

**Failure:** the N49 card title/formula suggested the released paper proves arbitrary known `lambda_e` and no uniform degree bound. The pinned OpenAI/math manuscript's abstract and Theorem 1.1 instead assume a single common `0<lambda<1` and a fixed finite outdegree bound `B`.

**Repair:** keep the two claims separate. The internal Astra page derives a heterogeneous/unbounded-degree extension conditional on the companion posterior-cone proposition. A hostile reread found no algebraic gap in its stated proof, but source replay was partial, external review absent, and priority UNKNOWN. The finite DP itself needs no reconstruction theorem; any Bayes guarantee built on the weighted heterogeneous iff remains conditional.

Evidence: `prior_art/N49_finite_depth_networked_inference.md`, `mechanism_attack/N49_SOURCE_PROOF_AND_HOSTILE_AUDIT.md`, and pinned sources in `source/SOURCES.md`.

## 2. Call capacity-optimal sensor placement Bayes-optimal

**Refuted:** a root-only cost-2 q=4 fixture reverses the design ranking. One `P_0.7` channel has capacity `0.5621055` but exact MAP success `31/40=0.775`; two independent `P_0.6` channels have capacity `0.7717436` but MAP success `7/10=0.700`. Thus the capacity DP maximizes a theorem proxy and can choose a lower-accuracy design.

**Repair:** label the DP objective “capacity-certificate optimal.” Exact Bayes-optimal sensor placement on a finite menu can be found by exhaustive plan enumeration plus exact BP/outcome aggregation, but worst-case runtime is exponential in menu or outcome count. No polynomial exact design algorithm, submodularity, or approximation ratio is claimed.

Evidence: `proof/FINITE_SENSOR_TREE_CAPACITY.md`; `code/finite_tree_capacity.py`; `code/RESULTS.json`.

## 3. Use unweighted flow energy for edge-varying reliability

**Refuted:** on a single ray with `lambda_n=1-2^(-n-1)`, the product of channels is positive, so exact root reconstruction remains positive. The constant nonzero ray flow has divergent unweighted energy. The weighted increment `(1-lambda_e^4)*(theta_e/R_w)^2` telescopes correctly; the unweighted heterogeneous criterion does not.

Evidence: the pinned internal weighted-capacity page and `mechanism_attack/N49_SOURCE_PROOF_AND_HOSTILE_AUDIT.md`.

## 4. Turn posterior computation into an engineering safety result

**Rejected:** exact tree BP returns a Bayes-optimal root class under the supplied finite probabilistic model. It says nothing by itself about how an actuator behaves after that classification. A physical controller needs a state-transition/safety model, intervention costs, delayed/failed-message behavior, and closed-loop validation, none of which is supplied by the Potts reconstruction theorem.

**Repair:** present only a distributed sensing/inference emulator test with an explicit chance baseline, best-sensor, census, centralized-BP, and distributed-BP comparator. Do not claim closed-loop control or energy/safety benefit.

## 5. Claim a practical prediction from the explicit cone constant alone

**Narrowed:** with `C=460`, the explicit TV floor is `b/(1600*sqrt(b^2+2))`. At the sample capacity `b≈0.49235`, this is only `2.05e-4` expected TV, or roughly `6.85e-5` MAP advantage. A finite experiment may have no power to distinguish this bound from chance without a large sample. This lower bound is a qualitative positive certificate rendered quantitative; it is not a useful high-accuracy guarantee.

**Repair:** use it as a theorem/interface falsifier, while measuring actual held-out MAP and posterior TV against exact BP. If the engineering decision needs a larger practical margin, the present result does not supply one.
