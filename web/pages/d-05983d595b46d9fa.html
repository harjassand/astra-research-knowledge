CARDINALITY-FREE POSTERIOR GATE: ATTEMPT AND OBSTRUCTION
8 October 2026

STATUS

I did not derive a cardinality-free common-EB bound from Holevo capacity and
an actual pair broadcaster. The strongest exact obstruction found is to
the existing information-extraction route: neither Gamma nor the selected
label Helstrom gain can be controlled by C alone, even when the family has
an exact full-domain broadcaster and zero EB error. A balanced-sign
classical family gives explicit formulas. It is not a counterexample to
broadcast-to-EB; it shows that the label-wise posterior test is an
inappropriate sufficient statistic in this legal compatible case. The
unrestricted target remains UNKNOWN.

1. CANDIDATE GATE

For a finite net R with prior p, the finite-label route uses

  Gamma(R)=max_p (sum_i sqrt(p_i))^2 chi_p

inside the common-decoder bound. A tempting improvement is a uniform
estimate Gamma(R)<=F(C(R),beta_R), or a posterior inequality replacing the
effective-support factor in the selected-label Helstrom step by a function
of C and compatibility alone. Such an estimate would make the net bound
independent of N. The example below disproves the Gamma estimate and the
corresponding selected-label gain inequality, even at beta_R=0. It does not
disprove another argument that constructs a better common measurement.

2. EXACT COMPATIBLE CONTROL

Let m be even, 0<a<1 fixed (rational if a rational input is desired), and
let S_m={s in {+1,-1}^m: sum_i s_i=0}. On H=C^m define

  rho_s=diag((1+a s_1)/m,...,(1+a s_m)/m),
  sigma=I/m,  M=|S_m|=binom(m,m/2).

Each rho_s is a density matrix. Its relative entropy to sigma is

  D(rho_s||sigma)=c(a)
   =[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2.

The uniform prior has mean sigma and Holevo information c(a). Donald's
identity gives chi_p=c(a)-D(rho_bar||sigma)<=c(a) for every prior, so
C(E_m)=c(a), independent of m. The center is exact and
rho_s<=(1+a)sigma.

The full-domain channel

  B(X)=sum_(i=1)^m <i|X|i> |ii><ii|

is CPTP and has both marginals equal to the input dephasing channel. Thus
it broadcasts every rho_s exactly: beta_R=0. Computational-basis
dephasing is a full-domain EB channel fixing all rho_s, so e_EB(E_m)=0.

Nevertheless, for the uniform prior,

  N_(1/2)(p)=M,  chi_p=c(a),  Gamma(E_m)>=M c(a),

which grows exponentially in m. Any fixed-radius net of E_m below a/8
also has exponentially many points: a constant-rate code in the balanced
sign slice has exp(c_0 m) codewords with Hamming distance greater than
m/4, and T(rho_s,rho_t)=a d_H(s,t)/m. This is an exact compatible,
bounded-capacity witness against compressing Gamma or net cardinality into
a function of C and beta.

3. WHERE THE LABEL-HELSTROM STEP LOSES THE CLASSICAL STRUCTURE

For every s,

  T(rho_s,sigma)=a/2,
  S_0:=sum_s p_s T(rho_s,sigma)=a/2.

Fix one candidate label s_0. The Helstrom test for rho_(s_0) against
sigma is the diagonal projector onto P={i:s_(0),i=+1}. Its probability
under a prior label t is

  q_t=1/2+(a/m) Z_t,
  Z_t=sum_(i in P) t_i.

The balanced-sign prior gives E Z_t=0. For |P|=m/2, its coordinate
covariances are E t_i^2=1 and E t_i t_j=-1/(m-1) for i!=j, hence

  Var(Z_t)=m^2/[4(m-1)].

The binary information gain of this particular test is

  g=ln 2-E_t h(q_t)
   <= a^2/[2(1-a^2)(m-1)],                                 (1)

because q_t lies in [(1-a)/2,(1+a)/2] and
ln2-h(1/2+x)<=2x^2/(1-a^2). Therefore

  S_0^2/g >= (1-a^2)(m-1)/2.                               (2)

No constant depending only on C=c(a) can replace the growing factor in
this selected-label inequality. The elementary Helstrom/Pinsker lower
bound g>=2 p_(s_0)T(rho_(s_0),sigma)^2 is only a lower bound; (1) shows
that even the actual gain of this particular binary test vanishes as
1/m while the posterior trace spread stays constant.

The lost structure is simple. The computational-basis POVM has

  I(S;I)=sum_s p_s D(rho_s||sigma)=c(a),

so it attains the full Holevo information. Measuring that POVM and
preparing |i><i| on outcome i reconstructs every rho_s exactly. The
label-selected binary test asks whether the unknown label is s_0; the
useful decoder instead reads the common classical coordinate. Thus
Holevo information and compatibility do not repair the chosen-test
argument; they permit a different, collective common measurement here.

The exact broadcaster above also yields K perfectly correlated outputs
when iterated. One can read the same coordinate from a receiver and
prepare it; no label identification is required. This example therefore
does not falsify posterior methods in general. It falsifies only the
attempt to make the existing max-label Helstrom-to-spread step
cardinality-free without changing the measurement objective.

4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW

The example cannot refute a bound e_EB<=f(C,beta) with f(C,0)=0: its EB
error is already zero. The bad intermediate factor Gamma is not a
necessary measure of reconstruction difficulty. The N93 Pauli/Clifford
controls supply the opposite caution: bounded C and low accessible
information can coexist with large EB error, but those controls keep
beta bounded away from zero. Neither family decides the joint target.

A valid positive route still needs a compatibility-sensitive common-POVM
inequality, for example a dimension-free modulus in the fixed-sandwich
score formulation

  Delta(p)<=F_(C,theta)(sum_x p_x E_Phi(H_x)),
  F_(C,theta)(u)->0 as u->0,

or a different minimax construction that chooses one measurement for
the entire family. Quantum conditional Holevo quantities cannot simply
replace the classical gains in the adaptive chain: their separate sum
does not have the measured-outcome chain-rule bound. Any such replacement
needs an explicit monogamy/redundancy theorem for the actual compatible
extension, including the effect of the prefix instrument.

5. INPUTS AND COSTS

This obstruction is finite and explicit. The Hilbert dimension is m; the
listed ensemble has M=binom(m,m/2) states, each a rational diagonal
matrix for rational a. An explicit fixed-radius net/ensemble therefore
costs Theta(M m p) stored rational entries at p bits per entry (or a
succinct generator plus a separate verification oracle, which is a
different input model). The broadcaster has m sparse Kraus operators
V_i=|ii><i| and a succinct O(m)-size description. The EB decoder has m
measurement outcomes and is exact. The code-packing and Gamma calculations
are analytic; no simulation or numerical checker is needed.

For a supplied finite net, the broadcaster can be applied to its N
centers at the charged cost in acquired_complexity_transfer.txt. This
family shows that replacing N/Gamma there by capacity alone cannot be
justified from the data, even with zero net broadcast error. It does not
show the net-based reconstruction gate is necessary: here the optimal EB
channel is far cheaper than the explicit net.

FALSIFIERS AND NEXT DECISIVE GATE

The stated obstruction would be falsified by a dimension-independent
upper bound on Gamma(E_m) for this family; the exact uniform-prior value
M c(a) already rules that out. The broader positive target would be
resolved by proving a dimension-free common-POVM modulus using actual
compatibility, or by an experiment sequence with bounded C, beta->0,
and e_EB bounded below. Neither is supplied here. Verdict: UNKNOWN.
