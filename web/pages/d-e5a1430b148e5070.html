\section{A robust marked-line obstruction to soficity}\label{sec:groups}

\subsection{Attribution and statement}
The finite marked-line incidence construction, triangle wiring,
integral rank reduction, and interpolation identity come from OpenAI's
released family 252, \emph{A torsion-free hyperbolic group that is not
residually finite} \cite{family252}. The released conclusion is
non-residual-finiteness. The new step is quantitative survival of its
rank contradiction under Hamming errors, which rejects sofic
approximation. The metric below reconstructs the geometric premises
without assuming the source's long global geometry argument.

For permutations of $D$ points write
$d_H(\sigma,\tau)=D^{-1}|\{a:\sigma(a)\ne\tau(a)\}|$.
This metric is invariant under multiplication on either side.
Permutation matrices act on column vectors, and their traces count
fixed points.

\begin{theorem}[Internally reconstructed extension]\label{thm:main}
Fix $h=20$, $c=100^{-20}=10^{-40}$, $r=100/c=10^{42}$, and
$q=2^{521}-1$. There exist finite marked-line data over $\F_q^{20}$
and a finite connected two-dimensional triangle complex $K$, constructed
below, with these properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ admits a locally CAT$(-1)$ metric, its universal cover is a
proper CAT$(-1)$ space, and $G=\pi_1(K)$ is torsion-free and
word-hyperbolic. In particular $K$ is a finite two-dimensional $K(G,1)$.
\item The specified finite rectangle family $\mathcal R$ consists
entirely of nonidentity elements.
\item If $N$ is the number of retained line labels, $m=q^{20}$,
$E$ is the exceptional point set, and
\[
 \Delta=\frac{Nr}{4}-2m-r|E|>0,\qquad
 S_{\rm inc}=\sum_{z\notin E}|I(z)|\le N(q-1),
\]
then every assignment of $D$-point edge permutations, in any $D\ge1$,
with triangle defects at most $\delta$ and rectangle fixed-point
fractions at most $\lambda$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:robust}
 \boxed{\Delta\le r^2(S_{\rm inc}\delta+N\lambda/8).}
\end{equation}
Simultaneous $\delta,\lambda\le\eps_*$ are impossible, where
\[
 \eps_*=\min\left\{\frac12,\frac{\Delta}
 {2r^2(S_{\rm inc}+N/8)}\right\}>0.
\]
For the finite data below, the explicit smaller tolerance
$\eps_{\rm explicit}=1/(100rq)$ also suffices.
\item $G$ is nonsofic. Some fixed $w\in\mathcal R$ is killed by
every homomorphism from $G$ to every sofic group, although the argument
does not identify its index. The construction below also specifies a
combined nonidentity word $W$ in the same common kernel, without
selecting that rectangle.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\subsection{A specific finite incidence construction}
The number $q=2^{521}-1$ is a Mersenne prime. The local exact check
recomputes its Lucas--Lehmer certificate: $s_0=4$,
$s_{a+1}=s_a^2-2\pmod q$, and $s_{519}=0$; the exponent 521 is prime.
The classical criterion \cite{gimps} certifies primality. Also
$q>10^{156}>64r^2/c$, since $2^{10}>10^3$.

Put $P=\F_q^h$, $m=q^h$, and choose
$S\subset\F_q$ of size $\lfloor q/100\rfloor$. Initially label every
mark $a\in S^h$ and independently choose a uniform projective direction
for its line. There are
$M_0=|S|^h$ marks and $J=(q^h-1)/(q-1)$ directions.
A label is incident with every point on its line except its own mark.
Distinct labels may initially name the same geometric line.

\begin{lemma}[Finite alteration]\label{lem:incidence}
Deleting short-cycle labels in a suitable finite outcome leaves
$N$ lines $\ell_i$ with distinct marks $a_i\in S^h$ such that the
punctured bipartite graph on $P\sqcup I$ has girth at least 14 and
\[
 |I(z)|\ge4r^2\ (z\notin E),\qquad
 |E|\le m/(2r),\qquad N\ge cm/4.
\]
Consequently $\Delta\ge15m/4$. Exact rejection sampling takes at most
two trials in expectation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Bernoulli's inequality gives
$M_0\ge cm(1-100h/q)\ge cm/2$.
Every point's initial degree is binomial with mean
$(M_0-\mathbf1_{\{z\in S^h\}})/J\ge cq/8\ge8r^2$ and variance
at most its mean. If $B$ counts points of degree below $4r^2$,
Chebyshev therefore gives $\E B\le32m/(cq)$.

A simple cycle with $s$ point vertices and $s$ distinct labels,
$2\le s\le6$, has at most $m^s$ ordered point choices.
Consecutive distinct points force a line with at most $q$ possible
marks. The distinct labels' prescribed directions occur independently
with probability $J^{-s}$. Its expected count is at most
$m^sq^sJ^{-s}\le q^{2s}$. Coincident geometric lines and puncture-
forbidden choices merely enlarge this upper bound.
For the number $C$ of all simple cycles of length at most 12,
$\E C\le5q^{12}$.

Accept an outcome if $B\le m/(4r)$ and $C\le m/(24rq)$.
Markov and a union bound give rejection probability at most
$128r/(cq)+120r/q^7<1/2$. Delete every label on a short cycle,
at most $6C$ labels. Let $E$ contain the old low-degree points and
all points on discarded lines. No surviving degree changes outside
$E$, and deletion creates no cycle. Hence
\[
 |E|\le B+6qC\le m/(2r),\qquad
 N\ge M_0-6C\ge cm/2-m/(4rq)\ge cm/4.
\]
Bipartiteness gives girth at least 14. Since $cr=100$,
$\Delta\ge(cr/16-2-1/2)m=15m/4$.
Independent trials have success probability at least $1/2$.
\end{proof}

All choices are finite. A deterministic alternative takes the first
acceptable assignment in a fixed enumeration. Neither acquisition is
practical at these parameters.

\subsection{Cartesian blocks and a finite presentation}
For $z\notin E$ let $d=|I(z)|\ge4r^2$.
Choose $t\in[r,2r-1]$ congruent to $d$ modulo $r$ and write
$d=rs+(r+1)t$, where
$s=[d-(r+1)t]/r\ge r$. Thus $I(z)$ has two Cartesian blocks of
sizes $s_bt_b$ with $s_b,t_b\ge r$. Index blocks at different points
separately and choose bijections
\[
 i\longmapsto(\alpha_b(i),\beta_b(i))
 \in\mathbb Z/s_b\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/t_b\mathbb Z.
\]
Exceptional points have no blocks, but their incidences remain in
$I(z)$.

The complex has vertices $O,V,W$ and, with $[r]=\{0,\ldots,r-1\}$,
edges
\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 x_{iuv}:V\to W &(i\in I,\ u,v\in[r]),\\
 L_{b,u,j}:O\to V &(u\in[r],\ j\in\mathbb Z/s_b\mathbb Z),\\
 R_{b,v,k}:O\to W &(v\in[r],\ k\in\mathbb Z/t_b\mathbb Z).
 \end{array}
\]
For $i\in I_b$ and $u,v\in[r]$, attach a triangle with boundary
$L_{b,u,\alpha_b(i)+v}\,x_{iuv}\,R_{b,v,\beta_b(i)+u}^{-1}$.
Coordinates are cyclic. Three distinct vertices make every closed
cell embed. There is a regular point, hence a block, so $K$ is connected.
Collapsing a maximal tree gives a genuine ordinary presentation with
tree-edge symbols set to identity. Equivalently use connecting paths
from $O$ to interpret each edge as a based loop. The triangle relations
are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:triangles}
 x_{iuv}=L_{b,u,\alpha_b(i)+v}^{-1}R_{b,v,\beta_b(i)+u}.
\end{equation}
Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rectangles}
 w_{i,u,u',v,v'}=
 x_{iuv}x_{iu'v}^{-1}x_{iu'v'}x_{iuv'}^{-1},
 \qquad u\ne u',\quad v\ne v'.
\end{equation}
There are $r^2S_{\rm inc}$ triangles.

\subsection{Reconstructed negative-curvature geometry}
Use the hyperbolic triangle with angles
$\angle O=27\pi/50$ and $\angle V=\angle W=9\pi/50$.
Their sum is $9\pi/10<\pi$.
Equal $V,W$ angles give compatible $L,R$ side lengths; all $x$ sides
have the same third length. Assign the corresponding lengths also to
unused edges. The topology and group remain the source's.

At $O$, a block's link is a simple complete bipartite graph:
prescribed $L_{b,u,j}$ and $R_{b,v,k}$ directions determine exactly one
label by $\alpha_b(i)=j-v$, $\beta_b(i)=k-u$.
Every nonempty cyclically reduced walk has at least four corners and
length at least $108\pi/50>2\pi$.

At $V$, send an $x_{iuv}$ direction to label $i$ in the incidence graph,
and an $L_{b,u,j}$ direction to the block's point. This link map is
locally injective: at $x$, a label belongs to at most one block at a
point; at $L$, $j=\alpha_b(i)+v$ has at most one $v\in[r]$ because
$s_b\ge r$. The $W$ argument uses $t_b\ge r$.
Thus a cyclically reduced link walk has a reduced incidence image
of at least 14 edges and angular length at least $126\pi/50>2\pi$.

A reduced path between distinct same-label $x$ directions maps to
a positive closed incidence walk without internal reversal. Such a
walk contains a simple cycle: a shortest positive subwalk with repeated
endpoints cannot have length two, which would be an internal reversal.
This remains true if the original closing seam reverses.
Every such link path has at least 14 edges, or none exists. Hence
all four turns of every rectangle have link distance at least
$126\pi/50>\pi$.

The polyhedral link criterion and Cartan--Hadamard
\cite{BH,bridson} make the universal cover complete CAT$(-1)$.
It is proper and the deck action is proper and cocompact because the
complex and its cell-shape list are finite. CAT$(-1)$ hyperbolicity
and this action give word hyperbolicity.
A finite-order deck isometry has a fixed point by the complete CAT(0)
fixed-point theorem; a nonidentity deck transformation acts freely.
Thus $G$ is torsion-free.

Each rectangle is a positive closed local geodesic. If nullhomotopic,
it would lift to a closed local geodesic in the CAT$(-1)$ cover,
where local geodesics are global geodesics. This is impossible.
Thus $1\notin\mathcal R$. The cover is contractible, so this same
finite $K$ is a two-dimensional classifying complex.

\subsection{Robust rank lower bound}
Assign arbitrary permutation matrices $X_{iuv},L_{b,u,j},R_{b,v,k}$
of size $D$, without assuming a homomorphism. Triangle products have
Hamming defect at most $\delta$; nondegenerate rectangles fix at most
$\lambda D$ points. Set
$Y_i=(X_{iuv})_{u,v\in[r]}\in\mathbb Z^{rD\times rD}$.
Every row has exactly $r$ ones in disjoint block columns. Expansion
gives
\[
 \tr(Y_iY_i^{\mathsf T})=r^2D,\qquad
 \tr((Y_iY_i^{\mathsf T})^2)
 \le[2r^3-r^2+r^2(r-1)^2\lambda]D.
\]
The second expansion has $2r^3-r^2$ choices with $u=u'$ or $v=v'$,
each contributing $D$. Every other trace counts rectangle fixed
points. Cauchy--Schwarz on positive eigenvalues therefore yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:realrank}
 \rank_\R Y_i\ge\frac{r^2D}{2r-1+(r-1)^2\lambda}.
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Integral row budget]\label{lem:reduction}
An integer matrix with row norms at most $\sqrt r$ satisfies
$\rank_{\F_q}A\ge\frac12\rank_\R A$ for every prime $q>r$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $d=\rank_\R A>0$ choose a nonzero $d$ by $d$ minor $B$.
Hadamard gives $1\le|\det B|\le r^{d/2}$.
If $d'=\rank_{\F_q}B$, select $d'$ rows spanning modulo $q$ and
subtract integer lifts of their combinations from the other rows.
These determinant-preserving operations make $d-d'$ rows divisible
by $q$. Thus $q^{d-d'}\mid\det B$ and
$q^{d-d'}\le r^{d/2}$. Since $q>r$, $d'\ge d/2$.
The zero-rank case is immediate.
\end{proof}

Combining this with \eqref{eq:realrank},
\[
 \rank_{\F_q}Y_i\ge\alpha(\lambda)D,\qquad
 \alpha(\lambda)=\frac{r^2}{2(2r-1+(r-1)^2\lambda)}.
\]
For $a=2r-1$, $b=(r-1)^2$, we have $\alpha(0)\ge r/4$ and
\[
 \alpha(0)-\alpha(\lambda)
 =\frac{r^2b\lambda}{2a(a+b\lambda)}
 \le\frac{r^2\lambda}{8},
\]
since $b/a^2\le1/4$. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:alphaloss}
 \alpha(\lambda)\ge r/4-r^2\lambda/8.
\end{equation}
$D$ is only a numerical dimension; the proof never divides by $D$
in $\F_q$. In particular $q\mid D$ is harmless.

\subsection{Triangle defects give the rank upper bound}
Hamming bi-invariance turns a triangle defect into
\[
 d_H\bigl(X_{iuv},
 L_{b,u,\alpha_b(i)+v}^{-1}R_{b,v,\beta_b(i)+u}\bigr)\le\delta.
\]
The difference has at most $\delta D$ nonzero columns, hence rank
at most $\delta D$ over every field. Within each Cartesian block,
the ideal sum has the exact factorization
\[
 \left(\sum_jL_{b,u,j}^{-1}\right)_{u\in[r]}
 \left(\sum_kR_{b,v,k}\right)_{v\in[r]}.
\]
For fixed $u,v$, shifted coordinates run over all $(j,k)$ exactly
once. These are a block column and row with inner dimension $D$.
The matrix order is preserved; no commutativity is assumed.

Let $Z(z)=\sum_{i\in I(z)}Y_i$. Embed each triangle-error difference
in its single matrix block and use rank subadditivity. The two blocks
at each regular point give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:localrank}
 \rank_{\F_q}Z(z)\le
 \begin{cases}
 2D+r^2|I(z)|\delta D,&z\notin E,\\
 rD,&z\in E.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
The exceptional bound uses the ambient dimension, including its
actual incidences. No approximate action has been repaired.

\subsection{Exact representation and fixed-envelope coarsening}

\begin{lemma}[Finite MLR envelope]\label{lem:envelope}
Let $p_t$ be positive laws on a finite ordered support with
$p_t(x)/p_s(x)$ nondecreasing in $x$ for $t\ge s$.
Put $h(x)=\max_{0\le s\le R}p_s(x)$ and
$H_{\rm env}=\sum_xh(x)$.
For each $\delta>0$ there is a contiguous partition of at most
$2H_{\rm env}/\delta+2$ bins such that retaining the bin and
resampling from $h$ conditional on it gives uniform TV error at most
$\delta$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For fixed $t$,
\[
 g_t(x)=p_t(x)/h(x)
 =\min\left\{\inf_{s\le t}\frac{p_t(x)}{p_s(x)},
 \inf_{s\ge t}\frac{p_t(x)}{p_s(x)}\right\}.
\]
The first infimum increases and the second decreases. Their minimum is
unimodal, with a possible plateau, lies in $(0,1]$, and has discrete
variation at most 2.
Isolate atoms with $h(x)>\delta$ and make greedy maximal bins of
raw $h$-mass at most $\delta$ on the remaining light blocks.
Adjacent light-bin masses sum to more than $\delta$; a light block
of mass $H_b$ uses at most $2H_b/\delta+1$ bins. Heavy atoms
separate at most one more light block than their number, which is
at most their mass divided by $\delta$. This gives the count.

For a bin $B$, let $\bar g_B=p_t(B)/h(B)$. Its contribution to
twice TV is at most $h(B)\operatorname{osc}_B(g_t)$.
Singletons contribute zero; the other $h(B)$ are at most $\delta$
and their oscillations sum to at most 2.
\end{proof}

The threshold uses raw $h$-mass. With normalized reference
$h/H_{\rm env}$ it becomes $\delta/H_{\rm env}$.
The likelihood envelope itself is a standard universal-coding object
\cite{shtarkov}; the reconstruction inequality above is proved directly.

\begin{theorem}[Bounded Gaussian memory law]\label{thm:gaussian}
For every fixed bounded $\Theta$ of affine dimension $s\ge1$,
there are $0<c_\Theta\le C_\Theta<\infty$ such that, for all $D\ge1$,
\[
 c_\Theta D^{-2/s}\le
 e_D:=\inf_{\mathcal E,\mathcal D}
 \sup_{\theta\in\Theta}\TV(\mathcal D\mathcal E P_\theta,P_\theta)
 \le C_\Theta D^{-2/s}.
\]
The upper is wholly classical. The lower includes arbitrary quantum
or hybrid memory and independent shared seeds. No continuum, interior,
or manifold hypothesis on $\Theta$ is required; $s+1$ affine-independent
means suffice. Constants are not uniform when the spanning geometry
collapses.
\end{theorem}

Posterior quantization and finite reductions of experiments are
established decision theory \cite{strasser,potzelberger}.
The dimension-versus-squared-distortion exponent is also familiar
from Shannon and Holevo bounds \cite{koch,holevo,datta}.
The precise uniform-TV bottleneck theorem and positive no-tail-logarithm
construction below were reconstructed in this run. A bounded primary
search did not locate an exact prior statement, but historical
novelty remains unresolved. This theorem does not identify equality
of optimal quantum and classical constants.

\subsection{Posterior geometry and the dimension converse}
Choose $\theta_0,\ldots,\theta_s\in\Theta$ affine-independent with
positive prior $\pi_i$. Draw $\theta$ from that prior and
$X\mid\theta\sim N(\theta,I_s)$. Its marginal density is $p(x)$,
and its posterior mean is
\[
 m(x)=
 \frac{\sum_i\pi_i\theta_i e^{\theta_i\cdot x-\|\theta_i\|^2/2}}
 {\sum_i\pi_i e^{\theta_i\cdot x-\|\theta_i\|^2/2}}
 =\nabla\log\sum_i\pi_i e^{\theta_i\cdot x-\|\theta_i\|^2/2}.
\]
$Dm(x)=\Cov(\theta\mid X=x)$ is positive definite for all finite
$x$: posterior weights are positive and a zero variance direction
would have equal inner product with all $\theta_i$, contradicting
affine independence. Integrating this positive Hessian along any
segment also makes $m$ globally injective.

Fix $C=[-L,L]^s$ with $L>0$. Compactness gives
$Dm(x)\ge vI$ on $C$, $v>0$; let $M=\Pr(X\in C)>0$ and
$p_{\max}\le(2\pi)^{-s/2}$. Injectivity, the local inverse theorem,
and change of variables imply, for all $z,h$,
\[
 \operatorname{Vol}\{x\in C:\|m(x)-z\|\le h\}
 \le v^{-s}\operatorname{Vol}(B_s)h^s.
\]

Condition on a shared seed $R$. At $x$ the memory state
$\omega_{R,x}\le I$; its output POVM $F_R$ has total trace at most $D$.
Tonelli's theorem and the preceding volume estimate give
\begin{align*}
 &\Pr\{X\in C,\ \|m(Y)-m(X)\|\le h\mid R\}\\
 &\quad\le
 p_{\max}\tr\int F_R(dy)
 \operatorname{Vol}\{x\in C:\|m(x)-m(y)\|\le h\}\\
 &\quad\le C_*Dh^s,\qquad
 C_*=p_{\max}\operatorname{Vol}(B_s)/v^s.
\end{align*}
The seed is independent of $\theta,X$, so $p,M$ are unchanged.
Averaging this inequality works for an arbitrary seed distribution,
without a finite seed alphabet.
For $h=(M/(2C_*D))^{1/s}$, at least $M/2$ probability has
posterior displacement exceeding $h$. Hence
\[
 \E\|m(Y)-m(X)\|^2
 \ge\frac M2\left(\frac{M}{2C_*D}\right)^{2/s}.
\]

Choose $B>0$ with $\|\theta_i\|\le B$.
$m(y)$ lies in their convex hull, so
$\|\theta-m(y)\|^2/(4B^2)\in[0,1]$.
Uniform parameterwise TV error at most $e$ makes its Bayes-risk
increase at most $e$. Since $\theta\to X\to(R,\text{memory})\to Y$
is a Markov chain and $R$ is independent, posterior orthogonality
gives the exact identity
\[
 \E\|\theta-m(Y)\|^2-\E\|\theta-m(X)\|^2
 =\E\|m(Y)-m(X)\|^2\le4B^2e.
\]
Therefore
\[
 e\ge\frac{M}{8B^2}
 \left(\frac{M}{2C_*}\right)^{2/s}D^{-2/s}.
\]
This is a direct posterior-risk and dimension-trace proof. In
particular, a finite mean set still has a continuously varying
injective posterior statistic; finite hypothesis count is not a
finite sufficient-observation alphabet.

\subsection{A positive upper kernel without tail truncation}
After translation put $\Theta\subset[-A,A]^s$. In one coordinate
write $f_\theta=N(\theta,1)$ and choose the public broad reference
\[
 g(x)=\frac1{2\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-x^2/8},\qquad
 u=G(x)=\Phi(x/2),\qquad q_\theta(G(x))=f_\theta(x)/g(x).
\]
$q_\theta$ is a density on $(0,1)$.
With $a=g'/g=-x/4$ and $d/du=g^{-1}d/dx$,
\[
 q_\theta'=(f_\theta'-af_\theta)/g^2,\quad
 q_\theta''=
 \left(\tfrac38x^2-\tfrac54\theta x+\theta^2-\tfrac34\right)
 f_\theta/g^3.
\]
$q_\theta,q_\theta'$ tend to zero at both endpoints, uniformly for
bounded $\theta$. Moreover
\[
 \|q_\theta''\|_1
 =\int_\R\left|\tfrac38x^2-\tfrac54\theta x+\theta^2-\tfrac34\right|
 f_\theta(x)/g(x)^2\,dx
 \le M_A,
\]
where one explicit finite constant is
\[
 M_A=8\sqrt2\pi e^{A^2/2}
 \left(\tfrac32+\frac{A}{2\sqrt\pi}\right).
\]
Indeed $f_\theta/g^2=
4\sqrt{2\pi}e^{-(x-2\theta)^2/4+\theta^2/2}$.
Putting $z=x-2\theta$ makes the polynomial
$3z^2/8+\theta z/4-3/4$; Gaussian integrals give the constant.
Thus the periodic extension lies in $W^{2,1}(\R/\mathbb Z)$.
The broader variance-four reference is essential to this integrability.

For $J\ge5$, $h=1/J$, and circle vertices $kh$, use hats
\[
 a_k(u)=\left(1-\frac{\operatorname{dist}_{\R/\mathbb Z}(u,kh)}h\right)_+.
\]
They satisfy $a_k\ge0$, $\sum_ka_k=1$, and $\int a_k=h$.
Encode $k$ with probability $a_k(u)$; decode with density $a_k(v)/h$.
The resulting symmetric Markov kernel is
\[
 K_h(u,v)=h^{-1}\sum_ka_k(u)a_k(v),\qquad
 R_hq(v)=\int K_h(u,v)q(u)\,du.
\]
It is at most $1/h$ and supported at circle distance at most $2h$.
On the unique local unwrapping its first moment about $v$ is zero:
each hat has mean its vertex and the two active weights interpolate
that vertex mean to $v$. $J\ge5$ avoids antipodal ambiguity.

Taylor's formula with integral remainder cancels constants and this
first moment. Translating on the circle yields
\[
 \|R_hq-q\|_1
 \le h^{-1}\int_{-2h}^{2h}t^2dt
 \int_0^1(1-r)\,dr\,\|q''\|_1
 =\tfrac83h^2\|q''\|_1.
\]
Tensor the $s$ independent coordinate kernels and telescope by
$L^1$ contraction:
$\TV(R_h^{\otimes s}q_\theta,q_\theta)
\le(4/3)sM_AJ^{-2}$.
The memory alphabet has $J^s$ labels; the decoder returns
$x_j=2\Phi^{-1}(v_j)$. Its unbounded real draws are fresh output
randomness. Null endpoints may be assigned arbitrarily.
The fixed invertible transform preserves TV.
Take $J=\lfloor D^{1/s}\rfloor$ for $D\ge5^s$; enlarge the
constant for smaller dimensions. This proves the upper bound.

These are measurable stochastic channels, not a finite-bit
running-time theorem. A deterministic digital output alphabet would
be atomic and have TV one against a continuous Gaussian unless an
output discretization interface is declared.

\subsection{Conditional quantum critical precision}

positive block-scalar center
$\rho_0=\bigoplus_ba_bI_{m_b}$.
The traceless block-Hermitian mean space has
$s=\sum_bm_b^2-1$ dimensions and covariance
\[
 \langle H,V_0L\rangle
 =\tr(\rho_0HL)-\tr(\rho_0H)\tr(\rho_0L).
\]
The current exact block/count construction supplies two Gaussian
comparison channels with error
$\eta_n=C_{\rho_0}(\delta_n+n^{-1/2})$ on the entire known algebra,
\emph{conditional on} the inherited tracial comparison in each block.
Unknown eigenvalues and directions within each block are allowed;
the block projectors are public. Its complete derivation is in
\path{work/quantum_collision.md}.

In the critical full-algebra envelope
$n\delta_n^2$ is bounded above and away from zero. The Gaussian mean
set is bounded and contains a fixed $s$-dimensional cube.
The positive kernel above gives
$D\le C\eps^{-s/2}$ and $e\le\eta_n+\eps$.
Conversely, place the Gaussian-to-quantum comparison before any
quantum archive and its reverse comparison after it. It is a Gaussian
code through the same $D$, so
$e+2\eta_n\ge cD^{-2/s}$, with harmless constant absorption.
For prescribed $\eps_n\to0$ satisfying $\eps_n\sqrt n\to\infty$,
\[
 D_n^{\rm opt}(\eps_n)=\Theta(\eps_n^{-s/2}),\qquad
 \log_2D_n^{\rm opt}(\eps_n)
 =\frac s2\log_2(1/\eps_n)+O(1).
\]
The regime below the comparison error and the simultaneous
growing-signal/precision law remain unsettled.

For a critical known-spectrum tracial orbit,
$\sqrt n(\diag(p_n)-I/d)\to A\ne0$ with $A$ traceless.

Permutations give all diagonal differences; two-dimensional Hadamard
and phase rotations give all real and imaginary offdiagonal units.

therefore affinely spans, with uniform lower constants for large $n$

\section{Useful deductions and failed transfers}\label{sec:negative}

\subsection{Stable laws cannot be imposed on arbitrary short-edge supports}
A proposed transfer from the Heisenberg pin mechanism to general
mean-preserving rounding needs a stable law on the legal vertices.
The support
\[
 V'=\{000111,110001,101010,011100\},\qquad y=(1/2)^6
\]
refutes this added premise. It is a three-dimensional simplex,
every atom has cardinality three, and every edge changes four
coordinates. Its exact-mean law is uniquely uniform. The event
containing coordinates $\{2,3,4\}$ has probability $1/4$, exceeding
the product of marginals $1/8$, despite nonpositive pairwise
covariances.

Every real-stable law on this support is a point mass.
If two atoms have positive weights $p,q$, they share a coordinate
$c$; the two other supports share a coordinate $d$ absent from
their union. Setting $z_d=0$ leaves
$z_c(pz_uz_v+qz_rz_s)$, with disjoint exclusive pairs.
Set $z_c=i$, $z_u=1+i$, $z_v=q(1+i)$,
$z_r=-1+i$, $z_s=p(-1+i)$: the two terms cancel.
Restore $z_d=i\eta$. The root in the affine variable $z_u$
remains in the upper half-plane for small $\eta>0$ by continuity,
contradicting stability. Therefore any stable law has marginal
error at least $1/2$ and TV distance $3/4$ from the uniform law.

Cartesian products retain edge support four and the block obstruction.
With identity features, every legal output nevertheless has
discrepancy $1/2$, and ordinary uniform block sampling solves the
mean task with two fair bits per block.
This is a failure of an added stability interface, not a failure of
rounding or a disproof of the source's physical pin construction.

\subsection{Physics, geometry, and sampler boundaries}
The exactness section of family 279 supplies a further reusable,
but conditional, finite-gate interface \cite{exactfactoring}.
Suppose an output has a unique polynomial-time verifiable encoding,
a polynomial-size coherent preparation produces it with probability
$p\ge1/2$, and that verified output permits polynomial-time exact
computation of the dyadic $p$. Assume a public polynomial denominator
bound and fixed gates supporting reversible classical computation,
Hadamards, signs, and the preparation and its inverse.
Then certainty can be compiled without arbitrary-angle rotations.

Here is a direct one-stage derivation. Pad the unique output to
$W$ bits, with $W\ge1$ at least the denominator exponent of $p$.
Mix the ordinary preparation and a uniform padded guess with equal
probabilities. This does not require controlled preparation gates:
run the preparation unconditionally, also generate the independent
guess, and reversibly select the proposed output using a branch bit,
while retaining both outputs and all work. On a verified output compute
\[
 k=\left\lfloor\frac{2^{W-1}}p\right\rfloor,\qquad
 l=2^{2W-1}-2^Wpk.
\]
These are integers with $0\le k\le2^W$ and $0\le l<2^W$.
Retain the ordinary successful branch with probability $k/2^W$
and the unique successful guess with probability $l/2^W$.
The total retained probability is exactly
\[
 \tfrac12p\frac{k}{2^W}
 +\tfrac12 2^{-W}\frac{l}{2^W}=\frac14.
\]
All sampled probabilities are dyadic; verification and integer
division are reversible polynomial computations. Incorrect outputs
are rejected before their proposed mass values matter. Keep all
workspace and branch registers coherent. If the resulting state is
$\psi$ and $g=\Pi\psi$ its retained component, then
$\|g\|^2=1/4$ and
$(2|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|-I)(I-2\Pi)\psi=2g$.
The two exact reflections therefore give certain correct output.

Additive probability adjustment and exact amplification already
appear in perfect-completeness literature \cite{qcmaperfect}.
The useful extracted contract computes the mass only after generating
and verifying its output. The ability to compute this exact mass
is an additional service, not a consequence of efficient verification.
It is absent for the current learning interfaces and for our many-output
incidence rejection sampler. The argument above does not adopt the
unread factoring-process proof or claim a general BQP-to-exact compiler.

The physics sweep identifies a precise failed complete entropy transfer.
Use vacuum covariance $I/2$ and natural logarithms. For a finite-energy
Gaussian state with covariance
\[
 V_{AM}=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 160I_2&\sqrt{25536}\,\diag(1,-1)\\
 \sqrt{25536}\,\diag(1,-1)&160I_2
 \end{pmatrix},
\]
the symplectic eigenvalues are $8,8$.
Mix $A$ with an independent thermal $B$ of photon number one at
transmissivity $1/2$; then $I(A:B\mid M)=0$.
For $g(n)=(n+1)\log(n+1)-n\log n$, the actual conditional output
entropy is less than $0.936570948716$, while
$g((g^{-1}(S(A\mid M))+1)/2)>0.963517784888$.
Exact rational logarithm and square-root enclosures certify a
violation exceeding $0.0269$ nats.
This refutes the conditional photon-number interpolation; the
unconditioned entropy photon-number law is an equality here.
The established conditional exponential qEPI remains compatible
\cite{conditionalepi}.
The same sweep repairs one Gaussian gauge diagnostic with charge

For planar diamond graphs at depth $k$, the classical
Lee--Naor bound is
$\operatorname{distortion}(G_k\to L_p)^2\ge1+(p-1)k$
\cite{leenaor}. Norm comparison gives the endpoint-tuning consequence

 d\ge2^{k/(4\Delta^2-1)}

for any $\Delta$-distortion embedding into $\ell_p^d$,

\section{Research mechanisms tested beyond their source setting}
The released reasoning accounts suggest mathematical operations, not a
controlled theorem-discovery algorithm. Their transferable content was
tested against the old programme's exact interfaces. Several apparent
shortcuts fail when the required input, positivity, or conditioning is
made explicit.

The most productive operation was to retain quantitative slack in a
finite certificate. In family 252, the rank contradiction has a fixed
gap while each Hamming defect has an explicit rank cost. This upgrades
an exact finite-quotient obstruction to rejection of approximate
permutation models without a repair theorem. The same step fails for
unitaries and general linear matrices, for the counterexamples already
given. Changing the triangle metric separately reduces the global
geometric obligation to local link inequalities.

The recurring warning in accounts 007, 102, 221, and 271 is that
exposing a witness changes the conditional law. In the Gibbs archive,

The family-271 stable-polynomial mechanism was tested directly on

added as an automatic consequence of short edges. Accounts 017 and
