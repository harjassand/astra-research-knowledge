## Gray ordering resolves the extra imaginary constraints

Use the same rational orthogonal V,W and real Bell basis as cycle2, but order its 64 columns by g(k)=k xor (k>>1). Each Bell matrix is a tensor product of I,X,Z,J with J=iY=[[0,1],[-1,0]], indexed by six binary bits. Adjacent Gray indices differ in one bit. With symbols indexed I=00,X=01,Z=10,J=11, either bit flip gives a relative factor proportional to X or Z. Consequently both P_i P_j^T and P_i^T P_j are real symmetric for adjacent Bell columns.

For any real skew operator L on the left half, the relevant Bell matrix entry is Tr(P_i^T L P_j)/8 = Tr(P_j P_i^T L)/8 = 0. The right-half expression similarly contracts the symmetric P_i^T P_j against a skew matrix. Real orthogonal conjugation preserves skewness. Thus every physical J_i=iY_i has zero adjacent entries B_J,k,k+1 at this basepoint exactly over the rationals, not merely over a finite field. Bell maximal entanglement also gives zero diagonal for every local X,Z.

Let C_s,ij=2s^|i-j|/(1+s^(2|i-j|)) off diagonal and C_s,ii=1. For X,Z use the same 63 real common-w eigenvector constraints as cycle2. For J set B_J=U^T J U, a real skew matrix, and define 63 prefix-flow constraints

G_J,k(U,s) = (1/s) sum_{i<=k<j} C_s,ij B_J,ij,   k=0,...,62.

Every term is analytic at zero because the off-diagonal filter is divisible by s. At zero only the single adjacent edge crossing the cut survives: G_J,k(U,0)=2 B_J,k,k+1. These values vanish by the Gray argument. For s>0, G=0 is equivalent to (C_s Hadamard B_J)w=0: summing the first k+1 row sums of a skew matrix cancels all internal edges and leaves precisely the prefix flow. Successive differences recover every row, and their total is zero. Since the dressed Y=-iJ is purely imaginary Hermitian, any common real-w eigenvalue must be zero. Thus these 378 real equations account for all imaginary Y constraints; no complex equations were discarded.

With U=U_0 exp(Omega), Omega real skew, the derivative at zero is 2[B_J,Omega]_(k,k+1). There are 756 X,Z diagonal equations plus 378 J flow equations, total 1134, against 2016 orthogonal coordinates.
