INDEPENDENT CYCLE04 VARIANCE THEOREM
Status: internally derived exact proof; global C2 remains UNKNOWN.
Attribution: tensor_frame_sol, theorem-only reconstruction. No other cycle04
worker derivations were read. Earlier C4 is a supplied verified premise only.

1. Interface and normalization

Let tau(X)=Tr(X)/d on M_d and H the real Hermitian Hilbert space with inner
product tau(XY). Let Phi be a unital trace-preserving completely positive map
with a CPTP broadcaster B:M_d -> M_d tensor M_d having both marginals Phi.
Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjointness is NOT needed for the theorem below.
For a finite Hermitian frame A_1,...,A_r set
 V=sum_i tau(A_i^2), s=sum_i tau(A_i Phi(A_i)),
 k=sup_{p_j,pi_j} sum_j p_j sum_i Tr(pi_j A_i)^2,
where pi_j are rank-one projections, p_j>=0, sum p_j pi_j=I/d.
The barycenter and the common ensemble are part of this definition.
Equivalently V-k is the minimum over the SAME ensembles of
 sum_j p_j sum_i Var_{pi_j}(A_i).

2. Exact two-receiver identity

Assume r<=2. Measure the spectral PVM of A_1 on receiver 1 and of A_2 on
receiver 2. Missing observables when r<2 are simply omitted. If y denotes
the joint outcome, z_i(y) its eigenvalues and E_y the product PVM effects,
pull this single joint measurement to input effects M_y=B^*(E_y). Set
 p_y=Tr(M_y)/d, rho_y=M_y/Tr(M_y), mu_i(y)=Tr(rho_y A_i),
omitting zero-trace effects. Positivity and unitality of B^* give
 sum_y M_y=I, sum_y p_y rho_y=I/d.
The unitality of the marginal Phi, together with its being trace preserving,
gives the three exact moment identities
 sum_y p_y sum_i Tr(rho_y A_i^2)=V,
 sum_y p_y sum_i z_i(y)^2=V,
 sum_y p_y sum_i mu_i(y) z_i(y)=s.
For the last identity the intermediate expression is
 sum_i tau(A_i Phi^*(A_i)); this equals s by adjointness of the inner
product, whether or not Phi is self-adjoint.
Expanding the squared terms therefore proves
 2(V-s)=sum_y p_y [sum_i Var_{rho_y}(A_i)
                          +sum_i (mu_i(y)-z_i(y))^2].       (1)
No independent-output assumption occurs: the two output measurements only
need to commute because they act on different tensor factors.

3. Pure refinement and conclusion for each rank-two frame

Refine every rho_y spectrally, rho_y=sum_a q_{a|y} pi_{ya}. The resulting
one pure ensemble with weights p_y q_{a|y} has barycenter I/d. Variance is
concave in the state: explicitly
 Var_{rho_y}(A)-sum_a q_{a|y} Var_{pi_{ya}}(A)
   =sum_a q_{a|y} Tr(pi_{ya} A)^2-Tr(rho_y A)^2 >=0.
Thus (1) gives
 V-k <= sum_{y,a} p_y q_{a|y} sum_i Var_{pi_{ya}}(A_i)
      <= sum_y p_y sum_i Var_{rho_y}(A_i)
      <= 2(V-s).                                            (2)
This proves the exact C2 inequality for any frame of rank at most two;
after diagonalizing a frame Gram operator, any redundant list whose Gram
rank is <=2 is included.
The construction is finite: if A_1,A_2 have at most d distinct eigenvalues,
there are <=d^2 joint effects and <=d^3 pure refinement atoms. This is a
loose atom count, not an efficient construction claim.

4. One comparator on a fixed two-dimensional span

Fix a real Hermitian subspace S of dimension <=2. Let P_S be its orthogonal
projection and define T=P_S (Phi+Phi^*)/2 |_S. Let K_S be the compact convex
set of all compressions P_S Psi|_S, where
 Psi(X)=d sum_j p_j Tr(pi_j X) pi_j,
 sum_j p_j pi_j=I/d.
Every element of K_S is the compression of ONE physical canonical pure EB
channel. Compactness follows by taking the compact convex hull of the
finite-dimensional moment pairs (pi, |pi><pi|), fixing the barycenter,
and projecting; finite pure ensembles suffice by Caratheodory.
For every positive operator Q on S, write Q=sum_{i=1}^r |A_i><A_i| with
r<=2. Equation (2) says
 h_{K_S}(Q)=k(Q) >= Tr_H[Q(2T-I_S)].
If K_S failed to meet (2T-I_S)+PSD(S), strict finite-dimensional separation
of a compact convex set from this closed cone translate would give a
separator Q>=0 with the reverse strict inequality. The condition Q>=0
follows because the infimum on the cone translate must be finite.
Consequently there exists ONE canonical pure EB channel Psi such that
 P_S Psi|_S >= 2T-I_S.                                       (3)
Leakage of Phi(S) outside S is permitted; no invariance of S is assumed.
When Phi is self-adjoint, T is simply the compressed Phi.
The per-frame measurements in Section 2 are used to prove all support
inequalities before separation; they are not relabeled as one channel.

5. Exact fixed-frame extensions and limits

If the frame partitions into two sets that are each internally commuting,
measure their joint spectral PVMs on the two receivers. The same moment
identity (1) and conclusion (2) hold for that entire fixed frame. This does
not imply a common comparator on its whole span: arbitrary positive Gram
operators may require noncommuting linear combinations that admit no such
partition. More generally, r receivers with a common marginal give (2)
for a rank-r frame by measuring one spectral PVM per receiver.

The proof does not establish global C2 for a merely two-compatible channel
when arbitrary frame rank is allowed. Separating scalar convex roofs is
invalid: at the tracial qubit state, the individual variance roofs for X
and Z are both zero, but their common-ensemble variance roof is one
(V=2 and pure Bloch vectors obey x^2+z^2<=1, attained by an unbiased X
eigenbasis ensemble). In particular sums of separately optimized scalar
Fisher/variance roofs do not equal the multiparameter common roof here.
Similarly, support functions are subadditive, so rank-two inequalities
cannot simply be added to prove all higher-rank inequalities.

6. Falsifier and evidence status

A violation for a rank<=2 frame or a two-commuting-set frame, with one
genuine broadcaster and exact unital marginal, would refute (1) or the
pure variance refinement. These are direct finite algebraic identities.
No source literature, novelty, external validation, or general C2 claim is
being inferred from this derivation. The remaining global gate is rank>2
with no two-set commuting measurement realization.
