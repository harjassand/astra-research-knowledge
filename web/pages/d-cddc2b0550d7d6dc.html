## Audit target and verdict

The assigned question was whether a common-POVM minimax / all-frame Dirichlet comparison is being confused with per-label recovery, fixed-support bounds, or an unweighted norm. Reconstructing the relevant arguments and cross-checking peer artifacts confirms three material boundaries:

1. One POVM must serve the entire label set. The common-map minimax in `INITIAL.txt` handles that quantifier exactly; the qubit pair `{X,Z}` is a minimal strict gap against per-label optimizers.
2. Finite-dimensional separation gives support bounds that grow with algebra dimension. It does not turn every fixed-label theorem into a statement for growing label families.
3. Faithful central block weights belong inside the physical trace. For observables confined to a block of weight `w`, the displayed Pauli loss scales as `w theta^2/2`; it does not introduce `1/w`. No claim follows for an unweighted block norm.

The following peer work gives scoped subclasses, exact calibrations, or useful obstructions. None resolves the unrestricted broadcasting-to-EB / universal Dirichlet gate. One qutrit sharpness example and the qubit factor-two lower bound are calibrations of a threshold already present in the supplied baseline, not new counterexamples.

## Stronger quantifier audit from this assignment

For a fixed finite algebra, the measurement kernel is compact and convex when the POVM coin is retained as part of the outcome. The all-frame comparison `D_M <= cG` can be checked by finite-dimensional cone separation. Its separator is PSD on the real Hermitian test space of dimension `D`; spectral decomposition needs at most `D` arbitrary Hermitian test vectors. For an experiment-specific optimal prior, the prior enters only through `Y_p=sum_x p_x |H_x><H_x|` in `Sym(R^D)`; Carathéodory gives a support bound no larger than `D(D+1)/2+1`. The two bounds answer different questions, and each grows with `D`. In particular, neither yields a fixed-support theorem for a sequence of increasing algebras.

Therefore a uniform all-frame operator comparison is sufficient to cover growing label sets, while a fixed-support route needs a covering/entropy hypothesis or a modulus uniform over the actual sequence. The supplied N46/R01 route's growing-entropy sufficient condition is `b_n log N_n(a_n) -> 0` for some `a_n -> 0`; it should remain explicit. The N61/R16 copy ladder addresses a different, unrestricted experiment interface and is not a counterexample under the current fixed-sandwich hypotheses.

The bridge from an arbitrary EB factorization to one POVM is also sensitive to the reference input. Pinching effects and outputs into the promised block algebra preserves probabilities/targets and does not increase trace error. Sandwiched-2 data processing for the measurement followed by preparation, together with the supplied pair-continuity estimate on `(Lambda rho_H, Lambda sigma)`, gives the stated loss bound with both errors `<=2e`; it does not require `Lambda(sigma)=sigma` or an inverse smallest eigenvalue. Conversely, a canonical map with `q(E)` has half-trace error at most `sqrt(q(E))/2`, using `0 <= I-Psi_M <= I` and normalized `L1 <= L2`. This is a single shared map in both directions.
