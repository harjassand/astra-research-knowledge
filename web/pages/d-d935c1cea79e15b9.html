# r01_discovery — acquiring proof-relevant representations from scientific evidence

**Outcome:** recent primary-source mechanisms investigated; a sharp universal graph bound independently derived; a general exact-counterfactual identifiability obstruction constructed. Neither bibliographic novelty nor a broad autonomous-discovery advantage is established. The graph result is mathematically complete at the written-proof level; the main foundational acquisition question remains open.

## Exact research question and access boundary

Can a discovery process propose a new reusable variable/relaxation that explains finite observed reversals, then supply a verifiable universal derivation under explicit cost and access constraints? Concrete test case: start with graph invariants a (annihilation), ec (edge cover), nu (matching), graph degrees and the bipartite/regular examples; hide fractional matching nu_f from the initial dictionary. The desired endpoint is an acquired, executable bridge

    ec(G)-a(G) <= ceil(nu_f(G)-nu(G)) <= ceil(oct(G)/2)

for every finite simple isolate-free graph. The generation/selection/proof/verification costs and every failed candidate must count. Nu_f was proposed by this investigator; no tested system has acquired it in this run.

## Complete derivation and decisive obstruction

`notes/r01_discovery/fractional_annihilation_derivation.md` gives the full proof: fractional independent vectors satisfy the summed degree budget; the continuous degree-knapsack cardinality optimum rounds down to a; fractional vertex cover/matching duality yields a>=floor(n-nu_f); Gallai's identity yields the first bound; an independently reconstructed extreme-point proof decomposes a fractional matching into unit edges and half-weight odd cycles, yielding nu_f-nu<=oct/2. Disjoint q triangles attain both bounds. Assumptions and isolates/zero-regularity boundary cases are explicit.

`notes/r01_discovery/novelty_cone_obstruction.md` proves that same-target convex aggregation is incomplete even for a supplied consistent linear library: f<=g+h, g<=u, h<=v entail f<=u+v, while the restricted target cone misses it. Complete real-polyhedron implication is classical Farkas theory; it still does not certify graph realizability, integer rounding or global scientific novelty.

`notes/r01_discovery/identifiability_obstruction.md` gives the load-bearing foundational obstruction. A known increment/reset dynamical representation with an unknown monotone instrument admits two transforms identical on every budget-q transcript but different at the unobserved target state q+1. Therefore exact transfer can remain information-theoretically impossible even with the representation disclosed. This is not an actual ULTRADISCOVERY flaw: its generated readout class/code is required to decide that.

## Falsification and evidence

- All 28,263 isolate-free labelled simple graphs of orders 2–6 satisfy the OCT bound in the exhaustive diagnostic.
- All 814 such graphs of orders 2–5 satisfy the fractional bound when fractional cover is computed by exact half-integral enumeration. Independent brute edge-subset matching and covering agree with the DP/Gallai implementation.
- Toy monotone twins have identical transcripts for all action words of length at most q, q=1,...,8. The universal impossibility uses an explicit induction, not the finite enumeration.
- A rational symbolic certificate checker verifies the unrestricted three-constraint novelty example.

## Nearest prior art, novelty, downstream value

The proof tools are classical: degree-knapsack relaxation, LP duality, Gallai identities and fractional matching extreme points. The combined bound was reconstructed here. Targeted primary-literature searches and the inspected July paper did not locate the exact combined statement, but this is insufficient to certify novelty. Pepper's original annihilation literature and fractional degree-sequence bounds remain the next bibliographic targets. The current theorem is a small, potentially useful result rather than a consequential foundational breakthrough.

For t=ec-a>0, the theorem certifies oct>=2t-1 from readily computable degree/matching data. More generally, it provides a realistic supervised test of acquisition of a proof-relevant relaxation. A system that merely replays our supplied variable or caches a proof has not demonstrated the acquisition capability.

The deeper research route is target-specific model-identifiability certification: return either a target prediction invariant across compatible models or two models plus a separating experiment. The unresolved bottleneck is constructing the admissible model family and economical discrimination procedure from raw scientific interfaces. Ordinary finite version-space/decision-tree theory supplies the endpoint only when that family is already supplied.
