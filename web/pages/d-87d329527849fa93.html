## 2. A local Gibbs lower bound with no false exponential order

Let R be any r-qubit region, write H=I_R tensor H_out+T_R with ||T_R||<=v, and let P_R be a nonzero local projector of rank k. Then

    Tr rho(P_R tensor I)>=k 2^(-r) exp(-2 beta v).             (3)

Proof: compression to P_R gives K_P=P_R H P_R. Choose its orthonormal eigenbasis u_j. Scalar Jensen for the spectral measure of H yields

    sum_j <u_j,exp(-beta H)u_j> >= sum_j exp(-beta<u_j,H u_j>)
      =Tr_(P_R) exp(-beta K_P).

Minmax on K_P gives this compressed trace at least exp(-beta v) k Z_out. Minmax on the FULL H gives Z_H<=exp(beta v)2^r Z_out. Dividing proves(3). P_R need not reduce H, and T_R need not commute with H_out. This is a trace proof, not exp(A+B)>=exp(A)exp(B).

## 4. Exact unpaired-frequency lower bound for logical leakage

For each fixed X-check sector s, let B_(s,eta,omega) be the restriction of A_omega to its L=eta block. Say omega is UNPAIRED in s when precisely one of these two operators is nonzero. This operator-support definition is weaker and more useful than requiring all intersector Bohr gaps to differ. Let

    mu_unpaired(I)=sum_(s,omega in I unpaired) Tr rho_s A_(s,omega)^* A_(s,omega).

Then the actual full-frequency Gibbs-weighted logical leakage obeys

    k_O=sum_omega gamma(omega)Tr rho[O,A_omega]^*[O,A_omega]
       >=gamma_min(I) mu_unpaired(I).                       (8)

Proof within s: O has off-diagonal unitary block T between eta=+ andeta=-. If A_omega=diag(B,0), [O,A_omega]^*[O,A_omega] has one block B^*B and its opposite unitary conjugate; both are positive. Its trace in the first Gibbs block is exactly the corresponding bath outflow mass, and the second contributes nonnegatively. Swap signs for diag(0,B). Blocks for different s cannot interfere since O preserves s. No comparison between rho_+ and T rho_- T^*, and no modular right-multiplication estimate, enters.

In particular, if every frequency in I is unpaired wherever it acts, then

    k_O>=gamma_min(I)m_*.                                   (9)

This rules out a vanishing-in-N leakage bound for that O and bath. It applies to any whole-H logical involution anticommuting L and preserving the X-check sectors, not merely the old decoder sign convention. It does not rule out every changed encoding or prove actual memory failure.

Conversely, a proposed exponentially small k_O must acquire an exact shared-frequency outflow mass of at least

    mu_paired(I)>=m_*-k_O/gamma_min(I).                     (10)
