For spin j, d=2j+1, the all-mode covariant c2 target is controlled by

  W_w=sum_(l=1..2j) w_l sum_a B_l,a^T tensor(B_l,a^A+B_l,a^B),
  V(w)=sum_l(2l+1)w_l,
  C_j(w)=max_(pure psi) sum_l w_l sum_a<psi|B_l,a|psi>^2,

for EVERY w_l>=0. The covariant pure orbit has mean I/d, so unlike a
generic noncovariant experiment this pure maximum is exactly attainable
as a reference ensemble capture. A sufficient all-j lemma is

  W_w <= [V(w)+C_j(w)]I.                                 (G)

It is stronger than needed for the positive-compatible channel interface.
If (G) fails, a maximal spectral state twirled over SU(2) has tracial input
and output marginals. Resolving its output-swap parity gives a common-
marginal compatible channel. Its OTHER multipole eigenvalues must then
be checked for L2 positivity before claiming a positive-class falsifier.
A failure of c2 does not refute the weaker dimension-free N93 modulus.

What made spin 2 manageable: three explicitly achievable pure-seed capture
vectors yield three positive polar cones with only ten extreme rays. Their
exact total-spin blocks have size at most five, and all ten PSD bounds
pass. Six mixed rays, not just the four individual multipoles, are needed.
Two mixed rays (0,8,5,1) and (8,0,3,7) have constant pure capture 14 and18.
This is explained by the symmetric square of spin 2 having only total
spins 0,2,4, so the invariant quartic functions have a small linear span.
The proof itself does NOT need those seeds to exhaust the achievable set.

A general-j exact recoupling representation is available. For the scalar
pair interaction K_l=sum_a B_l,a tensor B_l,a, its eigenvalue at pair
spin k=0,...,2j is

  beta_l(k)=d(2l+1)(-1)^(2j+k){j j k; j j l},              (1)

where braces are a Wigner 6j symbol. In total spin f, change from output
pair spin L to input-output pair spin k with

  R_Lk=sqrt[(2L+1)(2k+1)]{j j L; j f k},

up to harmless row/column phases. The output-swap parity is
S_LL=(-1)^(2j-L). After spin-flip conjugation on the Choi input,

  W_l,f=(-1)^l[Q_l,f+S Q_l,f S],
  Q_l,f=R diag(beta_l(k)) R^T.                            (2)

These small finite matrices make exact higher-spin star analysis possible;
they do not acquire the pure-state moment support C_j.

IMPORTANT RELAXATION FAILURE

Because psi tensor psi lies in Sym^2(C^d), one can upper-bound the genuine
capture by the spectral maximum of K_w on that symmetric subspace:

  C_j(w)<=C_sym(w)=max_(k congruent 2j mod2) sum_l w_l beta_l(k).

Replacing C_j by C_sym may vastly weaken (G). For spin 1 and only the
quadrupole frame, beta_2(0)=5 and beta_2(2)=1/2, so C_sym=5. But the true
maximum pure capture is 2, by S_2=2-S_1 and attainable S_1=0. The spin-0
spectral state is maximally entangled and cannot be psi tensor psi; its
allowed pure-product singlet probability is at most 1/d. Thus arbitrary
probability vectors over symmetric total spins are not realizable moments.
Neither a polytope of all spectral phase vertices nor a symmetric-state
optimization is a substitute for pure reference ensembles.
