## Established-theorem deduction 1: no hyperbolic direct-finiteness witness
For an arbitrary unital ring R, ab=1 implies e=ba idempotent. Also e!=0 (otherwise a(ba)b=(ab)^2=1 would equal 0), so if R has no idempotents except 0,1, e=1 and R is directly finite. Hence, for every torsion-free word-hyperbolic K and **every field F**, F[K] is directly finite. This excludes all candidate F_2[hyperbolic K] witnesses ab=1, ba!=1, independently of the complexity of any proposed geometric construction.

## Deduction 2: finite multiplication-pattern obstruction (elementary)
Assume H has a,b in F[H] with ab=1 but ba!=1. Let A=supp(a), B=supp(b), T={1} union A union B union AB union BA (finite). There exists NO injective map f:T->K, K any torsion-free hyperbolic group, such that f(1)=1 and f(xy)=f(x)f(y) for all x in A,y in B or x in B,y in A. Indeed the induced finite sums a',b' in F[K] obey a'b'=1, while b'a' !=1 since f is injective on BA union {1}, contradiction. Thus H is not locally embeddable into the class of torsion-free hyperbolic groups (an explicit finite partial multiplication table obstructs it). ## Deduction 3: universal nontrivial residual kernel (elementary, stronger than individual obstruction)
Fix characteristic p>0. Let C_p be torsion-free groups in which NO nonidentity element is conjugate to its p^k-th power for any k>=1. By Formanek, F_p[K] has only trivial idempotents for every K in C_p. The class C_p is closed under finite direct products, although such a product need not be word-hyperbolic.
Let c=1-ba !=0 in F_p[H], S=supp(c) finite. For every hom phi:H->K in C_p, phi_*(c)=0. Suppose every pair s!=t in S could be separated by some such hom phi_st. The finite product of the target K_st again belongs to C_p; the product hom is injective on S, hence its pushforward of c is nonzero, contradiction. Therefore there exist DISTINCT s,t in S with phi(s)=phi(t) for EVERY hom phi:H->K in C_p; the nontrivial group element s^(-1)t is in the common kernel. In particular no counterexample group H is residually torsion-free hyperbolic. Exact pair/elements are not explicitly acquired.

## Deduction 4: no escape through torsion-free hyperbolic direct limits
Let G be any directed colimit (injective connecting maps NOT required) of torsion-free word-hyperbolic groups G_i. For each field F, F[G] is the directed colimit of F[G_i]. If a nontrivial idempotent e existed in F[G], lift its finite coefficients to some stage i. The finite equality e^2=e holds at a later stage j; its image remains nontrivial at every stage since its colimit is not 0 or 1, contradicting the trivial-idempotent theorem for F[G_j]. Therefore F[G] has only trivial idempotents and is directly finite. This blocks importing the OA197 non-direct-finite witness even via a graded/direct-limit tower with torsion-free hyperbolic stages. **Do not generalize to arbitrary hyperbolic-stage towers with torsion or to every lacunary hyperbolic group without a torsion-free-stage representation.** Olshanskii-Osin-Sapir 2007 describes lacunary hyperbolic groups as special hyperbolic direct limits: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701365
