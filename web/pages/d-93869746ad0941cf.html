Abstract.

We construct a rank-two ample holomorphic vector bundle on an abelian surface that admits no smooth Griffiths-semipositive Hermitian metric, thereby giving a counterexample to the Griffiths conjecture. The proof combines a curvature obstruction that persists under deformations with the openness of ampleness in an irreducible moduli space of stable sheaves.

Key words and phrases: Griffiths positivity, ample vector bundle, Levi form, abelian surface, stable sheaf, extensions of line bundles

Griffiths [16] proved that Griffiths positivity implies ampleness and conjectured the converse. For line bundles, this equivalence is the celebrated Kodaira embedding theorem [21].
