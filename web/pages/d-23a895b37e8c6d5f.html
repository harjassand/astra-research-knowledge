# A04 Phase II update: good-path Knill–Laflamme closure

Date: 2026-10-09  
Status: conditional internal lemma. This is a new append-only version; see `v1.txt` for the frozen source/interface audit and `v2.txt` for the decoder compilation and pathwise recurrence.

## Main added observation

If the recurrence in `v2.txt` holds pathwise for every fault path with at most `tau n` faults in each QTC recovery cycle, then all such good paths are jointly correctable as a coherent sum. This is not limited to stochastic or Pauli-diagonal noise.

Let the QTC family have distance `d_n >= d_0 n`, and let `r_* = K tau n/(1-alpha) < d_n/2` be the residual bound from (3)–(4) in `v2.txt`. Include measurement outcomes, classical decoder records, and the shared bath in the environment. Expand each local fault operator on its bounded system support in a Pauli basis, leaving operator-valued coefficients on the bath. For each Pauli term and each measurement branch, the QTC extraction circuit is Clifford and the decoder's classical output is fixed; hence the final data operator on the encoded input is a Pauli residual `R` with stabilizer-reduced weight at most `r_*`.

By the definition of stabilizer-reduced weight, for every such `R` there is a stabilizer `S_R` and a Pauli representative `\widetilde R` with physical weight at most `r_*` such that
\[
R P = \omega_R\widetilde R P,
\]
where `P` projects onto the code and `\omega_R` is a phase. Let `\mathcal E` be the set of all physical Pauli errors of weight at most `floor(r_*)`. For any `E_a,E_b in \mathcal E`, `E_a^\dagger E_b` has weight at most `2 floor(r_*)<d_n`, so the distance property gives
\[
P E_a^\dagger E_b P = c_{ab}P.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus the entire error set `\mathcal E`, not just each path separately, satisfies Knill–Laflamme. This gives one recovery map that corrects every path residual simultaneously.

Write `G` for the sum of all fault paths that are good in every cycle and `B` for the remaining paths in the global unitary dilation. After applying a Stinespring form of the common exact recovery from (1), the good operator has the form
\[
(\mathcal R\otimes I_{bath}),G P
   = P_L\otimes C,
\tag{2}
\]
for an operator `C` on the recovery workspace, records, and bath that is independent of the logical input. This follows because the recovery maps each `E_aP` to the same logical state tensored with an error-syndrome/workspace vector; all path and bath coefficients are absorbed into `C`. Therefore the subnormalized logical channel induced by `G` is `p_G id_L`, with `p_G` independent of the input state. Coherent cross terms among good fault paths cannot carry logical information through the bath.

If the global bad-path operator obeys `||B||<=epsilon`, then `U=G+B` is unitary and `G=U-B`. Before or after any CPTP recovery, the channel perturbation satisfies
\[
\|\Phi_U-\Phi_G\|_\diamond
\leq 2\epsilon+\epsilon^2,
\tag{3}
\]
by expanding `U rho U^dagger-(U-B)rho(U-B)^dagger` and using `||U||=1`. Since `\Phi_G=p_G id_L` and `\Phi_U` is trace preserving, (3) also gives `|1-p_G|<=2epsilon+epsilon^2`; consequently
\[
\|\Phi_U-id_L\|_\diamond\leq4\epsilon+2\epsilon^2.
\tag{4}
\]
Here the norm comparison assumes a final recovery on the data plus its records; it does not yet assert that this final recovery runs under the same noisy hardware model.

## Conditional repeated-round consequence

Suppose the complete round has `N=N_0 n` macro-locations, the local aggregate fault-path condition `||E(S)||<=eta^{|S|}` holds for every specified set of locations in any round (with the *same shared bath* and no condition elsewhere), and the exact-set Möbius inversion in the director's message applies to this location convention. With `k=ceil(tau n)`, the per-round bad operator is bounded by
\[
\epsilon_n\leq {N\choose k}\eta^k(1+\eta)^{N-k}
\leq\left({eN_0\eta\over\tau}\right)^{\tau n}e^{N_0\eta n}.
\tag{5}
\]
Choose a fixed `eta>0` small enough that
\[
c(\eta):=-\tau\log(eN_0\eta/\tau)-N_0\eta>0.
\tag{6}
\]
For `T` cycles, a union expansion over the `T` possible bad-cycle positions gives `epsilon_T<=T epsilon_n`, without assuming bath reset or temporal independence. If `T<=exp(c(eta)n/2)`, then `epsilon_T<=exp(-c(eta)n/2)` up to the ceiling/constant factors. Substituting `epsilon_T` into (4) would give an exponentially small logical diamond error after an exact final recovery.

This is a *conditional theorem skeleton*: the nontrivial source/noise-model check is whether the relevant physically selected shared-bath dynamics yield the aggregate cylinder bound for every complete-circuit macro-location, including readout, reset, classical logic and correction control. The adjacent worker is auditing this global fault-path step. The proof also needs the per-cycle fault threshold convention to agree between the cylinder sets `S` and the pathwise recurrence's count of extraction plus decoder locations.

## Remaining implementation gate

The repeated storage cycle itself has constant depth, `O(n)` physical operations, `O(n)` check ancillas and measurement bits, plus `O(n)` decoder bits/messages, all on the bounded-degree QTC graph (`v2.txt`). The exact recovery invoked in (2) is an abstract final QEC recovery. The QTC paper provides a parallel algorithm whose `alpha_k` decays exponentially in `k`; `k=O(log n)` makes a constant-fraction input error exactly correctable, but the straightforward local circuit then has `O(log n)` depth and `O(n log n)` locations. Under the crude global Möbius tail, `N=Theta(n log n)` with a threshold `k=Theta(n)` does not give a fixed-eta exponential tail. Therefore a final physically noisy decoder/readout protocol still needs either a robust implementation/recursive protection argument or a different constant-overhead output construction. Until that is supplied, (4) is a logical-memory statement with an ideal final recovery, not a full end-to-end physical logical-output theorem.

## Claim ledger

- **Internally proved conditional:** if each good fault path leaves a residual representative of weight `<d/2`, all such paths, including their operator-valued common-bath amplitudes, lie in one Knill–Laflamme-correctable span; one recovery removes their logical effect.
- **Internally derived conditional:** a global bad-path norm `epsilon` plus the good-path property implies logical diamond error at most `4 epsilon+2 epsilon^2` after an exact recovery.
- **Still open:** derive the aggregate local fault-path condition for the complete physical cycle and final noisy output, show exact correspondence of fault sets to support counts, and build/count the final recovery/readout under that model.
- **No discovery claim:** this version closes the coherent good-path algebra conditionally; the full mechanism remains unproved.

## Clarification on the CSS two-sector weight

In `v2.txt` and the equations above, the scalar residual weight `r_j` means the sum of the stabilizer-reduced weights of the X and Z components, `r_j:=|e_X|_R+|e_Z|_R`. Apply Theorem 4.20 separately to the two CSS sectors and add the inequalities; define `a_ext,b_ext,c_dec` as the corresponding summed support constants. The single-shot input promise for each sector follows from the stronger summed promise in (4). Each sector then has a representative of weight at most `r_*`, and the product Pauli has a representative of total support at most the sum `r_*`. Thus `r_*<d_n/2` gives the ordinary distance-based Knill–Laflamme condition (1) for the joint X/Z Pauli set. This convention removes an ambiguity in the phrase “stabilizer-reduced support of the residual Pauli” in the preceding version.
