STRONGEST CLAIM

Let E be finite and
  h(x)=sum_{U subset E, |U| even} f(U) x^U
be a nonzero multiaffine polynomial with f(U)>=0 that is nonvanishing whenever every Re(x_i)>0. For disjoint forced-hole and forbidden-hole sets A,B, and arbitrary nonnegative activities w_i on the remaining coordinates, define
  Z_{A,B,w}(z)=sum_{W subset E\(A union B)} f(A union W) w^W z^((|A|+|W|)/2),
where odd-cardinality terms are zero. If this polynomial is not identically zero, then
  Z_{A,B,w}(z)=z^ceil(|A|/2) R_{A,B,w}(z),
where every zero of R is real and nonpositive. The statement allows exact zeros in f, zero activities, and boundary localizations; an empty fiber gives Z=0.

Thus every nonempty, coordinate-localized ambient-hole count distribution, under any positive global hole fugacity, is a shifted Poisson-binomial law at the level of the total number of holes. In particular, after removing its deterministic initial zero-root shift, the sector coefficients have no internal zeros and are ultra-log-concave. The one-dimensional log-fugacity potential has variance at most half the number of free coordinates. This applies directly to the signed ambient-hole coefficients f(U)=Z_{E\U}(F), and continues to hold after the audited coordinate-pair auxiliary additions. It concerns only the cardinality marginal, not a sampler for hole configurations.

FULL DERIVATION

1. Localization. Since h is multiaffine,
  g_{A,B}(x)= (partial_A h)(x) |_{x_j=0, j in B}
           = sum_{W subset E\(A union B)} f(A union W) x^W.
The derivative of a multiaffine stable polynomial is stable or zero: writing h=a+x_i b, the stable polynomials h(...,R,...)/R converge locally uniformly as R->+infinity to b=partial_i h, so multivariate Hurwitz gives the claim; iterate over A. Substitution x_j=0 is a limit of substitutions x_j=epsilon>0 and is likewise stable or zero. These limits are essential: they do not divide by coefficients that might vanish.

Every monomial in g has |W| congruent to |A| modulo 2. Put epsilon=|A| mod 2 and let m=|E\(A union B)|. For strictly positive w, p(t)=g(t w) is a real univariate polynomial, is zero-free for Re(t)>0, and satisfies p(-t)=(-1)^epsilon p(t). If some w_i=0, approximate w by w+delta*1 and take delta down to zero; the resulting p is zero or remains zero-free in the open right half-plane by Hurwitz. If p is nonzero, its parity and right-half-plane zero-freeness force every root onto the imaginary axis: a root with negative real part would have its reflected root in the forbidden positive half-plane. Hence p(t)=t^epsilon R(t^2) for a real polynomial R whose roots are all in (-infinity,0].

Writing R(z)=sum_l c_l z^l gives
  c_l=sum_{W: |W|=2l+epsilon} f(A union W) w^W.
The total hole count is (|A|+|W|)/2=ceil(|A|/2)+l, proving the stated Z=z^ceil(|A|/2)R identity. If R is nonzero, its exact factorization is C z^q product_{j=1}^d(z+lambda_j), with C>0 and lambda_j>0. Therefore its coefficients are positive exactly from degree q through q+d; zero sectors can occur only before q or after the degree, never internally. Newton's inequalities make that shifted coefficient sequence ultra-log-concave.

2. Count law and log-fugacity geometry. At tau>0, normalize the terms defining Z to a probability law on W, and write K=(|A|+|W|)/2. If R=C z^q product_j(z+lambda_j), then
  E[s^(K-ceil(|A|/2))] = R(tau*s)/R(tau)
                           = s^q product_j[(1-p_j)+p_j*s],
where p_j=tau/(lambda_j+tau). Consequently K has the distribution of the constant ceil(|A|/2)+q plus a sum of d independent Bernoulli variables. This is a representation of the one-dimensional count law only; it gives no independence statement about the coordinates of W.

For Psi(theta)=log Z(exp(theta)),
  Psi'=E[K],
  Psi''=Var(K)=sum_j p_j(1-p_j) <= d/4 <= m/8,
  |Psi'''|=|sum_j p_j(1-p_j)(1-2p_j)| <= Psi''.
These identities hold on every nonempty fiber, including fibers with zero coefficient sectors. If d=0 the count is deterministic. The monotone fugacity response may help calibrate a count-sector annealing schedule if evaluations of this localized partition are independently available; no such evaluation oracle is supplied here.

3. Local deletion/inclusion pencil. For any additional free coordinate i, varying its nonnegative activity s writes the localized sector polynomial as A(z)+s z B(z) when |A| is even, and as A(z)+s B(z) when |A| is odd, using the natural total-hole-count exponent. Every member of either pencil is real-rooted with nonpositive roots, including the s=0 boundary or the zero polynomial. This is a local compatibility statement across deletion and inclusion; it does not assert that the full multivariate h is log-concave.

4. Actual signed-hole instance and auxiliary closure. The cycle-4 derivation constructs the actual signed real-stable Q_F from the chiral determinant and directional derivatives, with
  Q_F(x)=sum_{U even} (-1)^((n-|U|)/2) Z_{E\U}(F) x^U.
Rotation gives h_F(x)=(-1)^(n/2)Q_F(i x)=sum_U Z_{E\U}(F)x^U, which is right-half-plane stable with nonnegative even coefficients. The existing coordinate-pair auxiliary update is Q_new=Q-w partial_i partial_j Q; after rotation it is h_new=h+w partial_i partial_j h. Its stability preservation follows from the stable algebraic symbol already audited in cycle4. The localization theorem therefore applies to the exact determinant-hole representation and these auxiliaries, with no positivity/full-support perturbation of the hole coefficients.

BOUNDARIES AND COUNTEREXAMPLE TO A BROADER TRANSFER

The result does not extend to arbitrary joint log-concavity. For n=2 and F_12=1, F_21=0, the actual ambient-hole polynomial is h(x,y)=1+xy. Its logarithm has
  det Hess(log h)=(x^2 y^2-1)/(1+xy)^4,
which is negative when xy<1. Thus the stable representation does not yield the standard multivariate log-concave density or its sampler machinery. What survives is the exact one-dimensional radial/fiber geometry above.

That geometry also does not reveal microscopic hole locations. For example, F with only F_12=F_34=1 and F' with only F'_13=F'_24=1 give different labeled supports but the same unit-field radial sector polynomial (1+z)^2. So count concentration alone cannot supply the missing determinant-weighted two-replica flow or conditional hole sampler. It is not a hardness result.
