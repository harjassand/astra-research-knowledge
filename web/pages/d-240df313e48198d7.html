1. INTERFACE AND STATEMENT

Supply one exact carrier V_(a,b), integer a>=b>=1, N=a+b, B=b+1,
d=(a+1)B(N+2)/2. Fix a traceless Hermitian fundamental H with three
distinct eigenvalues. Write H=sum x_A t_A, Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2,
T_A=d pi(t_A), and B_H=T_H/N. The unknown-g input family is

 rho_g=U_g exp(h B_H) U_g^dagger/Tr exp(h B_H).

The single value h is supplied and lies in a fixed positive compact
interval contained in(0,h_*(H)]. The threshold h_*(H)>0 is the explicit,
carrier-independent threshold of WEIGHTED_POSTERIOR_CONVERSE Section4.
Alternatively one may fix any one h in that interval. Constants can
depend on H and this interval, but are fixed before a,b,N,Q,g.

The blind encoder has arbitrary free classical labels and at most Q
dimensions in each quantum branch, with no quantum side channel or
entanglement assistance. The decoder returns a state on the same V.
Let e*_Q be the infimum of worst-orbit half-trace error. The same orders
hold for Haar-average half-trace error. The resource model is equivalently
the channels with a rank<=Q Kraus decomposition, an established prior
characterization rather than a new resource theorem.

With D_A=d_ABC T_B T_C, alpha=C3/C2, S_A=(D_A-alpha T_A)/N,
C_S=Delta/N^2, set

 E_w(A)=E_T(A)/N^2+E_S(A)/(N sqrt(C_S)),
 Lambda_w(Q)=min_(rank A<=Q,||A||HS=1) E_w(A).

The candidate theorem, uniform over ALL positive labels, is

 e*_Q ≍ Lambda_w(Q) ≍ f_Q,
 f_Q=(1/N)min{Q^(-1/3),sqrt(B/Q)}, 1<=Q<=d/2;

 e*_(d-k) ≍ Lambda_w(d-k) ≍ g_k,
 g_k=(1/(Nd))min{k^(2/3),sqrt(Bk)}, 1<=k<=d/2;

 e*_d=Lambda_w(d)=0.                                     (1.1)

Integer Q and k are understood. These overlapping half-dimension
formulas give every memory size. Swapping a,b by duality gives the
mirrored result. Exact pure walls are covered separately by the banked

rank<=Q matrix has Schmidt rank<=Q, hence

 ||Pi A||HS^2<=Q Tr(Pi)/d.                               (3.1)

This is valid for arbitrary nonpositive or nonnormal A. Choose
t=f_Q/1000. Then t<=1/(100N), and by(2.1), Q<=d/2,

              Lambda_w(Q)>=f_Q/4000.                   (3.2)

4. ARBITRARY RIGHT-KERNEL LOWER, WITH EXPLICIT CONSTANT

Let rank A<=d-k and choose a rank-k orthogonal projection P inside
its RIGHT kernel. Write A=zI+X, TrX=0. AP=0 gives Tr(PX)=-kz,
including complex z and arbitrary nonnormal A. Set Y=P-kI/d and
G_P=<Y,L_w^+Y>. Spectral Cauchy--Schwarz and the gap give

 k^2|z|^2<=G_P E_w(A),
 ||X||HS^2<=N^2 E_w(A)/3,
 E_w(A)/||A||HS^2>=[dG_P/k^2+N^2/3]^(-1).              (4.1)

The vectorized projection P has rank k and squared norm k, so its
nontrivial spectral cumulative mass obeys

 M_P(t)=||1_(0,t](L_w)Y||HS^2
       <=min{k,k^2 D_+(t)/d}.                            (4.2)

Subtracting the trivial mode BEFORE integration is essential. The
exact layer cake is G_P=int_0^infty M_P(t)t^(-2)dt. Put

 K=(1/(100N))min{k^(-1/3),sqrt(B/k)}.

Applying(2.1) up to K and only M_P<=k above it gives

 G_P<=100000 k^2[(N^2/B)K+(N^3/2)K^2]+k/K
     <=1105 N max{k^(4/3),k^(3/2)/sqrt(B)}.              (4.3)

The coefficient is 100+1000+5=1105. For k<=B^3, every term
is bounded by the first power; for k>=B^3, by the second. Thus
dG_P/k^2<=1105/g_k. Dimension comparisons BN^2/4<=d<=2BN^2
and k<=d/2 give N^2 g_k<=sqrt(2). From(4.1),

            Lambda_w(d-k)>=g_k/1106.                    (4.4)

No positivity replacement, kernel-orientation optimizer or spectral
