THEOREM

For every finite d>=1, let Phi:M_d->M_d be unital, trace-preserving,
completely positive, Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint, and self-compatible:
one supplied CPTP B:M_d->M_d tensor M_d has both marginals Phi.
Then there is ONE finite canonical rank-one entanglement-breaking channel

  Psi(X)=d sum_j p_j Tr(pi_j X) pi_j,
  pi_j rank-one pure, p_j>=0, sum p_j=1,
  sum_j p_j pi_j=I/d,

such that, as operators on the ENTIRE real Hermitian HS space,

  Psi >= (2 Phi^2-I)_+.                                     (U1)

In particular,

  I-Psi <=2(I-Phi^2)<=4(I-Phi).                              (U2)

The comparator is independent of every observable and frame. For every
positive frame with V=sum tau(A_i^2), s=sum tau[A_i Phi(A_i)], and
unrestricted pure-ensemble score k with exact barycenter I/d,

  V-k<=4(V-s).                                               (U3)

No efficient exact ensemble-acquisition algorithm is asserted.

0. CONVENTIONS AND ELEMENTARY CHANNEL FACTS

tau=Tr/d, <X,Y>=tau(XY) for Hermitian X,Y. Identify real Hermitian HS
space with its real orthonormal coordinates. The identity coordinate
is orthogonal to the traceless subspace H0 of dimension D=d^2-1.

A unital CP map satisfies Phi(X)^2<=Phi(X^2) for Hermitian X: apply
its two-positive amplification to the positive block
[[X^2,X],[X,I]], then take the Schur complement of I. Taking tau and
using trace preservation shows ||Phi(X)||_2<=||X||_2. Since Phi is
HS self-adjoint, its eigenvalues are real and lie in [-1,1].
Unitality and TP make Phi block diagonal as 1 plus its H0 restriction.

Replace B by (B+Swap B)/2. This is CPTP, retains both marginals Phi,
and is output-swap symmetric. This symmetrization is a legal construction,
not a covariance assumption on Phi.

The d=1 case is Psi=Phi=identity. Below d>=2.

1. A SYMMETRIC OUTPUT CORRELATION IS A CONTRACTION

Let S=B(I/d), a normalized two-register state with both one-register
marginals I/d. Define the real bilinear form

  gamma(X,Y)=Tr[S(X tensor Y)].

Swap symmetry makes it symmetric, so it defines a self-adjoint real
HS operator Gamma by <X,Gamma Y>=gamma(X,Y). It fixes identity and
does not mix identity with traceless operators. For every Hermitian X,

  0<=Tr[S(X tensor I +/- I tensor X)^2]
    =2 tau(X^2) +/-2 gamma(X,X).

Therefore -I<=Gamma<=I. No CP or channel interpretation of Gamma is
required. In particular Gamma need NOT commute with Phi.

2. A LEGAL BINARY BROADCAST TREE AND EXACT SECOND MOMENTS

Set T_0=identity and
  T_(n+1)=(T_n tensor T_n) B.
This is CPTP from one d-dimensional input to N=2^n output registers.
Every leaf marginal is Phi^n. Under input I/d every leaf is I/d.
These facts follow inductively from the equal marginal of B and unital Phi.

For any output Hermitian X define the mean over depth-n leaves
  M_n(X)=(1/2^n) sum_(ell=1)^(2^n) X^(ell).
Let Q_n be its symmetric second-moment HS operator under
S_n=T_n(I/d): <X,Q_nY> is the expectation of
[M_n(X)M_n(Y)+M_n(Y)M_n(X)]/2.

The two child subtrees each receive marginal I/d. Their Heisenberg
mean is Phi^(n-1)X, using HS-selfadjoint Phi. The cross-subtree term
is gamma(Phi^(n-1)X,Phi^(n-1)Y). Thus

  Q_0=I,
  Q_n=(1/2)Q_(n-1)+(1/2)Phi^(n-1)Gamma Phi^(n-1),

and induction gives the EXACT operator identity

  Q_n=2^(-n)[I+sum_(k=0)^(n-1)2^k Phi^k Gamma Phi^k].        (U4)

This includes every cross term; Gamma and Phi were not commuted.

3. FAST MODES AND ONE ACTUAL JOINT CLASSICAL MEASUREMENT

Let P be the spectral projection in H0 onto modes with |lambda|>1/sqrt2.
If its rank m is zero, the traceless target in U1 is zero and any
orthonormal-basis pure canonical channel suffices.

Otherwise choose tau-orthonormal Hermitian eigenoperators E_1,...,E_m
spanning this fast space, with Phi(E_a)=lambda_a E_a. Set
F=diag(lambda_a) and H=2F^2-I_m>0. F is invertible and may have both signs.

At EACH depth-n leaf, independently choose a label a uniformly in
{1,...,m} and measure the spectral PVM of lambda_a^(-n) E_a.
A spectral refinement with at most d outcomes is also allowed. Label
selection is part of the POVM, not an uncharged oracle. Measurements
on different leaves form a single legitimate product POVM. If leaf ell
has selected label a_ell and measured eigenvalue t_ell, define the
classical scores

  Z_a=(m/2^n) sum_ell 1_(a_ell=a) t_ell.

The output first-moment operator of Z_a is M_n(lambda_a^(-n)E_a).
Pulling back through T_n gives the EXACT input first moment E_a,
because T_n^dagger M_n(X)=Phi^n X. The signs of lambda_a cause no
difficulty: the rescaled observables remain Hermitian.

Let K_n be the m by m real classical second-moment matrix E[ZZ^T]
under input I/d. The labels on distinct leaves are independent. All
different-leaf second moments therefore equal those of the quantum
mean. On a single leaf, mutually exclusive labels have moment
m delta_ab tau[(lambda_a^(-n)E_a)^2], rather than the symmetric
quantum product moment delta_ab lambda_a^(-2n).
There is no off-diagonal correction, by orthonormality and the tracial
leaf marginal. Consequently, restricting Gamma to the fast space,

  K_n=m(2F^2)^(-n)
       +sum_(r=1)^n 2^(-r)F^(-r)Gamma_fast F^(-r).             (U5)

This is a finite exact measurement identity. No almost-commuting
measurement theorem, tomography theorem, or limiting output observable
is imported.

Since Gamma_fast<=I and congruence preserves positive order,

  K_n <= R_n := m(2F^2)^(-n)+sum_(r=1)^n(2F^2)^(-r).          (U6)

Each R_n is a positive diagonal operator. On the fast space,

  R_n -> H^(-1).                                             (U7)

Indeed each spectral number 2lambda_a^2 is strictly greater than one,
and the geometric sum converges to 1/(2lambda_a^2-1).

4. FULL INPUT GRAM DOMINATION, WITHOUT A PINCHING ASSUMPTION

Pull the entire output POVM back through T_n and denote its input
effects by M_y>=0, sum_y M_y=I. Omitting zero-probability effects, put

  p_y=tau(M_y), rho_y=M_y/Tr(M_y).

Then sum_y p_y rho_y=I/d exactly. Let E_1,...,E_D be any completion
of the fast basis to a tau-orthonormal traceless Hermitian basis,
x_alpha(y)=Tr[rho_y E_alpha], and z(y)=Z(y).
Their means vanish. Their exact cross moment is

  sum_y p_y x_alpha(y) z_a(y)
    =tau[E_alpha sum_y z_a(y)M_y]=delta_(alpha,a).             (U8)

Write J:R^m->R^D for this basis inclusion and
C_n=sum_y p_y x(y)x(y)^T. The classical Gram matrix is

  [[C_n,J],[J^T,K_n]]>=0.                                    (U9)

K_n>=I_m. An elementary proof for v is to set
G=sum_y(v dot z(y))M_y=sum_a v_a E_a. Positivity of
sum_y[(v dot z(y))I-G] M_y [(v dot z(y))I-G] gives
sum_y(v dot z(y))^2 M_y>=G^2. Taking tau proves v^T K_n v>=||v||^2.

The Schur complement in U9 therefore yields

  C_n >= J K_n^(-1) J^T >= J R_n^(-1) J^T.                   (U10)

This is an inequality on the ENTIRE traceless input space, not just
its compression to P. Cross terms with slow modes have not been
discarded or set to zero by an unphysical projection.

5. PURE REFINEMENT AND COMPACTNESS GIVE ONE EXACT FINITE COMPARATOR

The coarse canonical channel d sum_y p_y rho_y Tr(rho_y .) has
traceless HS covariance C_n, but its atoms may be mixed. Refine every
input effect as M_y=sum_l c_(yl) pi_(yl), with c_(yl)>=0 and rank-one
pure pi_(yl), and use p_(yl)=c_(yl)/d. These probabilities sum to one
and retain barycenter I/d.

For every Hermitian X, weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives
  tau(M_yX)^2/tau(M_y)
     <=(1/d)sum_l c_(yl)[Tr(pi_(yl)X)]^2.
Thus the actual pure canonical channel Psi_n has HS operator

  Psi_n|H0 >= C_n >= J R_n^(-1)J^T.                           (U11)

The set of such pure canonical channels with barycenter I/d is compact.
For completeness, map each pure pi to
(x(pi), x(pi)x(pi)^T) in
R^q, q=D+D(D+1)/2=D(D+3)/2. Its image is compact. Every convex
combination can be reduced to at most q+1 points by eliminating an
affine dependence, so its convex hull is the continuous image of
a compact simplex times (q+1) copies of the pure-state set. Imposing
first moment zero is a closed condition. This proves compactness and
also a finite atom bound q+1 for every limiting barycenter/covariance.

Take a convergent subsequence Psi_n->Psi. Positive order is closed.
Using U7 and U11 gives

  Psi|H0 >= J H J^T = (2 Phi^2-I)_+|H0.                     (U12)

Every Psi_n and Psi fixes I and has zero identity/traceless cross terms.
Since Phi(I)=I, the target on I is also one. Hence U12 proves U1 on
the entire Hermitian space. The limit has a representation using at most
1+(d^2-1)(d^2+2)/2 pure atoms, retaining exact barycenter I/d.

The resulting map is genuinely rank-one EB and CPTP: Kraus operators
sqrt(d p_j)pi_j give Psi(X)=d sum p_j pi_j X pi_j, and their squared
sum is d sum p_j pi_j=I. Unitality follows from the same barycenter.

6. FACTOR FOUR AND THE UNRESTRICTED SUPPORT INTERFACE

Positive part dominates its underlying self-adjoint operator, so
Psi>=2Phi^2-I. Rearranging gives the first inequality U2. The exact
identity

  4(I-Phi)-2(I-Phi^2)=2(I-Phi)^2>=0                           (U13)

gives its second inequality. For every frame, the canonical pure
ensemble contributes s_Psi<=k, with k optimized over ALL pure ensembles
having the tracial barycenter. Thus V-k<=V-s_Psi<=4(V-s_Phi).
The same Psi works for all frames simultaneously.

7. PREMISES, COSTS, AND FALSIFICATION GATES

Supplied: exact channel Phi and one actual joint CPTP B with both
marginals Phi. No selected-observable compatibility is substituted.
All source claims in competing cycle03 proofs remain unconsumed.
Only elementary finite-dimensional spectral/linear algebra and the
CPTP/barycenter hypotheses are used above; the proof of the crucial
measurement second moment, full input Gram and compactness is included.

A depth-n tree uses 2^n-1 copies of B and d^(2^n) total output dimension.
The local randomized PVM has at most md labeled outcomes, so a fully
listed joint POVM has at most (md)^(2^n) outcomes; root effect refinement
multiplies this by at most d. Channel/broadcaster acquisition, dense data,
spectral decomposition, exact arithmetic, physical implementation,
shot counts, and large rescaled outcomes are separately charged.

For a fixed admitted finite-dimensional channel let
g=min_fast(2lambda^2-1)>0. Then
||R_n-H^(-1)|| <=(m+1/g)(1+g)^(-n).
As K_n>=I and R_n>=I, this also bounds
||R_n^(-1)-H||. Hence an explicit finite pure comparator has
Psi_n|H0 >= target-epsilon I once that bound is <=epsilon.
There is no uniform depth bound as g approaches zero, and compactness
does not supply an efficient exact limiting-ensemble algorithm.
Caratheodory's atom count is an existence bound, not extraction cost.
