1. Symmetrization is legal, but changes the entropy map

Write s=sqrt(sigma), u=sqrt(s), Psi(X)=uXu. Since L(I)=0 and L_*sigma=0,

  Hs=0, H* s=0.

The KMS-adjoint generator L_sharp=Gamma^(-1)L_*Gamma is legal: its semigroup
is Gamma^(-1)P_t*Gamma, a composition of CP filters and a CP adjoint. It is
unital because P_t*sigma=sigma, and its dual fixes sigma. Thus

  L_sym=(L+L_sharp)/2

is a legal KMS-symmetric QMS generator, by finite Trotter products. Its
weighted implementation is H_sym=(H+H*)/2. Consequently

  E_L(rho)=Tr[sqrt(rho)H_sym(sqrt(rho))],
  J_(L_sym)(rho)=[J_L(rho)+J_(L_sharp)(rho)]/2.

A KMS entropy/root lower controls this AVERAGE. Replacing the actual
same-map J_L by it is not justified. The skew weighted component need not
be a physical Hamiltonian commuting with sigma and need not vanish in J_L.

The actual KT positive endpoint at f=Gamma^(-1)rho is

  E_1(f)=(1/2)Tr[Gamma L Gamma^(-1)(rho)(log rho-log sigma)]
        =J_(L_sharp)(rho)/2,

not J_L/2 unless the relevant adjoint maps agree. This matches the task's
map warning and the previously checked primary endpoint convention.

2. Correct stationary Hessian without H selfadjointness

Let rho_e=s^2+e(sY+Ys), Y=Y*, Tr(sY)=0. Define

  delta=ad(log s), C=cosh(delta/2), A=(delta/2)/sinh(delta/2).

The first-order density and log increments are

  sY+Ys=2Psi(CY),
  Dlog_(s^2)(sY+Ys)=2Psi^(-1)(AY).

The physical positive Schrödinger generator is L_*=Psi H*Psi^(-1).
Hence the coefficient of e^2 in the TRUE same-map J is

  J_(L,2)=4 Re<CY,H AY>,

while the root coefficient is Re<Y,HY>=<Y,H_symY>. The KMS-specialized
expression with A and C interchanged across H cannot be reused when H
is not selfadjoint. The skew contribution is a genuine remaining term.
These identities expose the unresolved mechanism, rather than proving
that its contribution has either sign.

3. One exact legal stationary driven-qubit family

Fix a real k>1, with sigma=diag(k^2,1)/(k^2+1). Define

  K=[[1,-sqrt(k)],[1/sqrt(k),-1]],
  Q=[[0, i q],[-i q,0]], q=(k+1)^2/[2sqrt(k)(k-1)].

Q is Hermitian. The negative physical Lindblad generator is

  -L_*(rho)=K rho K* -{K*K,rho}/2-i[Q,rho].          (Q1)

This is a full-domain CPTP generator: the noise exponential, drift filter,
and unitary Hamiltonian exponentials are CP, finite Trotter products give
CP, and the displayed generator is trace preserving. No indefinite
kernel is substituted for legality.

For a real Bloch state rho=(I+x sigma_x+z sigma_z)/2, put

  m=(k^2-1)/(k^2+1), d=sqrt(1-m^2)=2k/(k^2+1),
  B0=4sqrt(k)/(k-1).

Direct matrix multiplication in(Q1) gives

  xdot=-(3+1/d)x-(B0/d)(z-m),
  zdot=B0 x-(2/d)(z-m).                             (Q2)

In particular x=0,z=m is exactly stationary and sigma is faithful.
These two polynomial/rational Bloch expressions were also checked by a
single symbolic matrix multiplication; that finite diagnostic is not a
general universal-inequality test.

Weighted transport has W=u K*u^(-1)=[[1,1],[-1,-1]]. Its Hermitian real
and imaginary parts are sigma_z and sigma_y. The averaged drift is
diag(1+1/k,1+k), so H_sym is exactly the legal sum of their two Hermitian
noise generators. H is not selfadjoint: (H-H*)(I)=4sigma_x.

Take the faithful real state rho_r=(I+r sigma_x)/2 with 0<r<1. Set
v_r=sqrt(1-r^2), a_r=atanh(r)/r, n_m=atanh(m). Direct evaluation gives

  E_L(rho_r)=1+1/d-2v_r>0,                          (Q3)
  J_L(rho_r)=a_r r^2(3+1/d)-a_r r B0 m/d
                              +n_m B0 r+2n_m m/d.  (Q4)

Equation(Q4) follows from J=-atanh(r)xdot+atanh(m)zdot at z=0. It includes
the Hamiltonian compensation and both reference-log terms.

4. Exact upper limit, with no universal lower conclusion

As k decreases to1 at fixed r, d->1, B0 m->4, B0 n_m->4 and n_m m->0.
Thus

  lim_(k->1+) J_L(rho_r)/E_L(rho_r)
    =2[r+(r-1)atanh(r)]/[1-sqrt(1-r^2)].             (Q5)

As r increases to1, (r-1)atanh(r)->0, so(Q5) tends to2. For every real
target c>2, choose a finite rational r<1 sufficiently close to1 and then
a finite rational k>1 sufficiently close to1 to obtain J_L<c E_L with
E_L>0. Sigma and rho have rational entries; K and Q have algebraic entries.

Primitivity may be enforced at the fixed strict instance by adding

  L_(eta),*(rho)=L_*(rho)+eta(rho-Tr(rho)sigma), eta>0.

It is legal, stationary at sigma and positivity improving. Exact commuting
replacement dynamics is exp(-eta t)exp(-tL_*)rho+(1-exp(-eta t))sigma for
density rho. At the fixed rho, J and E change affinely with eta; a positive
rational eta small enough preserves J<cE and E>0. Thus the upper result
uses finite faithful primitive systems, not a limiting nonprimitive model.
