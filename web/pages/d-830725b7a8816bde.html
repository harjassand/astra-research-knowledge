# Close prior on information lost by SLD

Li Gao, Haojian Li, Iman Marvian, Cambyse Rouze, *Sufficient statistic and recoverability via Quantum Fisher Information metrics*, arXiv:2302.02341v1, submitted 5 February 2023 09:02:36 UTC. Primary metadata and Sections 1.2–1.5 and 2 independently read. Recoverable HTML saved in sources/b_prior/cycle2/2302.02341v1.html.

Proposition 1.1 and Section 2 equations (2.7)–(2.9) give faithful qubit families with a fixed dephasing channel preserving the SLD and SLD Fisher information but allowing no common recovery channel. Theorem 1.2 distinguishes regular Fisher metrics that do characterize sufficiency. Theorem 1.5 states a Petz recovery bound from symmetric-inverse chi-squared divergence defect without a smallest-eigenvalue factor. This is a channel/recoverability interface, not a score-matching measurement or local-frame theorem.

Independent simplification of their explicit qubit family: the ODE for r is r'/r=p'(1-2p)/(2p(1-p)), hence r proportional to sqrt(p(1-p)). Thus

rho_k(p)=[[p,k sqrt(p(1-p))],[k sqrt(p(1-p)),1-p]], 0<|k|<1,

has parameter SLD S_p=diag(1/p,-1/(1-p)), independent of k. Directly, its offdiagonal derivative is k(1-2p)/(2sqrt(p(1-p))), exactly the offdiagonal of {rho_k,S_p}/2; diagonal entries also agree. Determinant p(1-p)(1-k^2)>0. The general parameter derivative is essential: this family changes eigenvalues and is not the fixed-generator unitary-orbit interface of the current quantum score paper. Its existence therefore does not resolve fixed local Xi,Zi dressed-frame irreducibility, and it is compatible with the qubit ordinary-frame irreducibility result.

Novelty consequence: a broad claim that SLD information can miss faithful-state distinctions is already established. Candidate new reflection witness is substantially narrower: the particular modular filter converts an irreducible norm-one reflection frame into a reducible one, with exact equal unitary-orbit scores. This priority remains unresolved and should not be sold as discovery of general SLD information loss.

Potential constructive direction: source's symmetric-inverse chi-squared recoverability controls a physical trace norm without global sigma_min, but one would need a channel whose divergence defect is bounded by our acquired local score discrepancy. No such comparison is supplied by the source or proved here. Merely replacing harmonic SLD metric by a stronger metric does not preserve the original acquisition interface.
