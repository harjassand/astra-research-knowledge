1. Conventions and physical stationarity

Let sigma=s^2>0, Tr sigma=1, u=log s. Positive physical generator is
 L_*(rho)=sum_j [{K_j* K_j,rho}/2-K_j rho K_j*]+i[Q,rho],
Q=Q*. Its negative exponential is CPTP and assume L_*(sigma)=0. Positive
Heisenberg L is its trace adjoint. Set Gamma(X)=sXs,
 H=Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2), B_j=s^(-1/2)K_j s^(1/2).
Both H(s)=H*(s)=0. Define
 J_L(rho)=Tr L_*(rho)(logrho-logsigma),
 E_L(rho)=Re Tr sqrt(rho) H(sqrt(rho)).
All rho are faithful finite states in this note. Finite untouched references
can be incorporated by replacing sigma with tau_R tensor sigma and noise
with I_R tensor local noise; the same local frequencies then remain.

2. Eliminate the physical Hamiltonian without assuming KMS

Put W=sum_j[K_j sigma K_j* - {K_j*K_j,sigma}/2]. Stationarity gives
 i[Q,sigma]=W. Thus W has zero blocks within every repeated eigenvalue
of sigma. Let Dlog_sigma be the Frechet derivative of logarithm; its divided
differences are continuous also on degenerate blocks. Then
 -i[Q,logsigma]=-Dlog_sigma(W).
This identity holds on the full algebra: on degenerate blocks both sides
vanish. The component of Q commuting with sigma contributes nothing.
Consequently, exactly,
 L(logsigma)=sum_j[{K_j*K_j,logsigma}/2-K_j*logsigma K_j]
             -Dlog_sigma(sum_j[K_j sigma K_j* - {K_j*K_j,sigma}/2]).
This equality uses stationarity only after summing the noises. Individual
noises need not satisfy a diagonal-flow stationarity constraint.

3. Modular-frequency coefficients

For one B decompose B=sum_omega B_omega with [u,B_omega]=omega B_omega.
Unlike the KMS Hermitian-noise argument, B need not be Hermitian and no
relation B_omega*=B_-omega is imposed. Put m=(omega+nu)/2,
delta=nu-omega. Since K_omega=exp(omega/2)B_omega,
 K*K=sum exp(m) B_omega*B_nu,
 K sigma K*=sum exp(-m) s B_omega B_nu* s.
The first elementary logarithmic drift has coefficient -2m exp(m) on
B_omega*B_nu. The Frechet terms have coefficients
 Dlog_sigma({K*K,sigma}/2): exp(m) delta coth(delta),
 Dlog_sigma(K sigma K*): exp(-m) delta csch(delta).
Here delta coth(delta) and delta csch(delta) both have value1 at delta0.
Therefore the sum over noises of L(logsigma) is obtained from the elementary
expression
 sum_(omega,nu) exp(m)[delta coth(delta)-2m] B_omega*B_nu
               -exp(-m) delta csch(delta) B_omega B_nu*.
An elementary expression alone may have spurious degenerate stationary
blocks; those cancel exactly in the summed stationarity relation above.

4. Exact proposed ordered-pair Gram

Diagonalize the FULL rho=sum_i q_i^2 |i><i|, q_i>0. Put
 beta_ik=log(q_i/q_k), c_omega(i,k)=<i|B_omega|k>,
 x=beta_ik-omega, y=beta_ik-nu,
 z=(x+y)/2, h=(x-y)/2.
The physical log-rho entropy term has coefficient
 -2 beta_ik q_k^2 exp(m) conj(c_omega)c_nu.
Combining with the preceding exact potential gives the candidate identity
 J_L(rho)=sum_j sum_(i,k) q_i q_k
                 sum_(omega,nu) conj(c_omega(i,k))
                     Jker(beta_ik-omega,beta_ik-nu)c_nu(i,k),
 Jker(x,y)=2 exp(-z)[-z-h coth(2h)]+2 exp(z)h csch(2h).
At h0, h coth(2h)=h csch(2h)=1/2. Thus
 Jker(x,x)=exp(x)-(1+2x)exp(-x).
All pairs are ordered, diagonal pairs included, and all complex phases
are retained. There is NO averaging with a nonexistent Hermitian mirror.

The HS symmetric part of H has drift determined by
 V s+s V=sum_j(B_j*sB_j+B_j sB_j*).
Its two elementary potential pieces have respective coefficients
 exp(m)/(2cosh((omega-nu)/2)) B_omega*B_nu and
 exp(-m)/(2cosh((omega-nu)/2)) B_omega B_nu*.
Therefore the root-energy identity in the same conventions is
 E_L(rho)=sum_j sum_(i,k) q_i q_k sum_(omega,nu)
          conj(c_omega(i,k)) L0(beta_ik-omega,beta_ik-nu)c_nu(i,k),
 L0(x,y)=cosh((x+y)/2)/cosh((x-y)/2)-1.
The identical L0 occurs in the KMS case, but its elementary amplitude
vectors now come from non-Hermitian B with no reflected pairing.

5. Reduction of the lower gate

The sufficient scalar gate for ANY stationary finite QMS, including finite
references, is that Jker-kappa L0 be PSD on EVERY finite real node set.
The endpoint candidate is kappa=pi/2, based on a separately proposed and
now worker-reconstructed physical upper family. No lower positivity proof
has been established here. Necessity for every kernel direction also has
not been established; a non-PSD Gram alone is not yet a physical counter.

For kappa0 the entropy kernel has a useful Loewner representation. Algebra
gives, with continuous values when arguments coincide or vanish,
 Jker(x,y)=2 exp[-(x+y)/2] sinh(x)sinh(y)
                    [f(x)-f(y)]/sinh(x-y),
 f(x)=x+x coth(x)=g(exp(2x)), g(v)=v log(v)/(v-1).
The divided difference is
 [g(v)-g(w)]/(v-w)
       =integral_0^infinity t/[(1+t)(v+t)(w+t)] dt.
Hence, putting r=exp(x), t0=exp(y),
 Jker(x,y)=[(r^2-1)(t0^2-1)/sqrt(r t0)]
       integral_0^infinity a/[(1+a)(r^2+a)(t0^2+a)] da,
 L0(x,y)=(r-1)(t0-1)/(r+t0).
This integral explicitly proves Jker PSD by a real diagonal congruence.
The desired quantitative improvement is the kernel domination
 [(r+1)(t0+1)/sqrt(r t0)]
    integral_0^infinity a/[(1+a)(r^2+a)(t0^2+a)] da
          >=_PSD (pi/2)/(r+t0), r,t0>0.
That is still an OPEN comparison in this freeze, not an accepted inequality.
