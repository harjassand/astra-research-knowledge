BINARY-LARGE SINGLE-HERALD GAUSSIAN SAMPLING AND ITS ACQUISITION BARRIER
Research date: 10 October 2026. Internal mathematical and computational result; historical novelty and external/formal validation remain unestablished. No historic-scale breakthrough is claimed.

MAIN FINDING
For a supplied exact nonnegative rational Gaussian kernel, zero displacement and ONE herald mode, the numerical herald count h can be removed from the count sampler's complexity: the remaining count vector can be sampled using work polynomial in its binary encoding length. The matching distribution is classical and generic log-concave sampling is established; the useful construction here is the complete finite-bit Gaussian interface and implementation.
The same conclusion is false when exact kernel acquisition is hidden: an explicit pair of uniformly physical, bounded-energy Gaussian inputs requires Omega(h) raw copies to produce even one approximate conditional count sample when the branch is unknown. For that two-hypothesis family, simple photon detection gives a matching O(h) acquisition algorithm at fixed accuracy. Thus the strongest result is a precise separation between an encoded simulator and acquiring the data it requires.

1. INPUT AND OUTPUT CONTRACT
Input: rational symmetric entrywise nonnegative K on 2m ket/bra coordinates, invariant under ket/bra exchange; rho(XK)<1; displacement exactly zero; one herald mode H={j}; integer h>=0 given in binary; nonzero herald-event probability; rational 0<epsilon<1/4. For a quantum interpretation K must represent a positive density operator. The general constructor checks symmetry, nonnegativity and convergence, not quantum physicality. The pure B=B^T>=0, ||B||<1 subclass is checked exactly and physically valid.
Output: m-1 binary-encoded photon counts, epsilon-close in total variation to their conditional law. No photon trajectories, complete state vectors, physical prepared state, laboratory herald success, or tomography are output. Arbitrary many herald modes and nonzero displacement are excluded.

2. AGGREGATE MATCHING IDENTITY
Eliminate the doubled unobserved coordinates by rational Schur complements. The two remaining herald token types have effective pairing matrix S=[[a,b],[b,a]], a,b>=0, and no singleton weights. Among h tokens of each type, let k be the cross pairs. Then k=h mod2, 0<=k<=h, with q=(h-k)/2 own-type pairs on each side.
The number of labeled pairings with these counts is
  binom(h,k)^2 k! [(h-k)!/(2^q q!)]^2
  = h!^2/[k! 2^(h-k) q!^2].
Therefore w(k)=h!^2 a^(h-k)b^k/[k! 2^(h-k)q!^2].
For positive a,b,
  w(k+2)/w(k)=(b/a)^2 (h-k)^2/[(k+1)(k+2)].
This decreases strictly across nonempty transitions. On j=(k-(h mod2))/2, it is a unimodal finite integer law. Find a mode with O(log(h+1)) exact rational comparisons. The mode denominator is irrelevant to its computation.
Degeneracies: h=0 gives no edges; a=0<b gives k=h; b=0<a gives k=0 when h is even and rejects odd h; a=b=0 rejects h>0.

3. NORMALIZATION-FREE FINITE-BIT MATCHING SAMPLER
Write g(j)=w(j)/w(mode), so g(mode)=1 and 0<=g<=1. On either side of the mode, dyadic distance blocks [1],[2,3],[4,7],... use their nearest-to-mode height. There are O(log(h+1)) blocks. Monotonicity bounds their total real-valued envelope mass by 3 sum_j g(j). The proof charges each noninitial block to twice the preceding closer block's mass; the two initial singleton blocks are charged directly.
Let M=floor(h/2) and choose scale=2^b with 2(M+1)/scale<eta/32. Rigorous interval log-gamma/log/exp evaluations supply integers l_j<=scale*g(j)<=u_j with u_j-l_j<=2. Weight each block by length times its upper endpoint height, choose a uniform integer position, and accept under l_j. The accepted untruncated distribution is proportional to l_j. Its TV distance from the target is at most 2(M+1)/scale<eta/32. The acceptance probability exceeds 1/5. Capping proposals at T=4*(bitlength(denominator(eta))+4) loses at most eta/16 since (4/5)^4<1/2.
No h! or partition function is formed. Log ratios use
  (k-k0)log(2b/a)+logGamma(k0+1)-logGamma(k+1)
  +2[logGamma(q0+1)-logGamma(q+1)].
Log-gamma cancellation requires working precision increasing with log h, not numerical h. Standard shifted Stirling expansions with signed remainder yield bit-polynomial enclosures. The executable uses FLINT/Arb 0.8.0 bindings with rigorous ball enclosures, refining precision until endpoint integer widths are <=2. This is ordinary numerical software, not a formal proof kernel. The bit-polynomial theorem is an algorithmic existence statement using explicit rigorous shifted-Stirling elementary-function algorithms. We have NOT proved a uniform worst-case runtime bound for this particular FLINT/Arb implementation or its adaptive precision refinements; finite timings and successful enclosures do not supply that theorem. Correctness of returned enclosures relies on the documented ball-arithmetic library contract and ordinary software assumptions.

4. REVERSE SCHUR COMPOSITION
Seed only aggregate directed boundary counts C00=C11=q, C01=k, C10=0. The Astra reverse-Schur theorem routes each multiplicity through an eliminated vertex via Binomial(Cij,thetaij) and adds self loops NegBin(1/2+K_v,p_v). The extra 1/2 loop immigration is indispensable. The fixed cross-edge orientation is valid after identifying physical ket/bra variables. No token list of length h is built.
The graph has O(m^3) scalar calls and stored rational routing entries. Rational Schur data have polynomial bit length via determinant/minor bounds. At each reverse stage, the largest count's bit length grows by O(log m + log(1/p_v) + log(1/eta)); there are O(m) stages. Negative-binomial scalar truncation at ceil(4*mean/eta) costs at most eta/4 in TV. All encoded count lengths are polynomial in complete input length, m, log(h+1), and log(1/epsilon).
The endpoint assigns 3epsilon/8 to aggregate matching, 3epsilon/(8S) to each of at most S graph scalar calls, and epsilon/4 to all uniform-integer generation. This totals at most epsilon, including adaptive parameters by coupling and a union bound.

5. BOUNDED RANDOM-BIT GENERATION
Every envelope proposal makes at most three integer-ticket draws. If T bounds each law's proposal count and there are <=S+1 laws, there are <=R=3(S+1)T tickets. For a ticket of width n, draw k=bitlength(n-1) bits; its success probability is >=1/2. The attempt cap is bitlength(ceil(4R/epsilon)), so the union probability of any ticket exhaustion is <=epsilon/4. A specific exception returns a fixed zero output vector and records last_ticket_fallback. No generic numerical exception is silently treated as a sample. All other random decisions are exact integer comparisons.
The mathematical theorem uses independent unbiased bits. Experiments use a seeded Python generator for reproducibility, which is not a demonstration of physical random-bit independence. The endpoint records ticket count, random bits requested, and cap. Standalone inherited scalar routines still accept an external rng.randrange interface; the capped theorem endpoint is LargeHeraldGaussian.sample.

6. EXACT ACQUISITION LOWER BOUND
Let h=4r^2, r>=1, a=1/4, t=1/(4r). Consider the normalized pure Gaussian states with
  B0=[[a,0],[0,0]],  B1=[[a,t],[t,0]].
Their norms are uniformly <=(1+sqrt(5))/8<1, so unconditioned energy stays bounded. Herald h photons in mode1. Under B0 the output mode2 has K=0 certainly. Under B1 its matching parameter is b=t^2=a/h, and w(2)/w(0)=1/2. Thus P1(K>=2)>=1/3 and conditional-law TV separation is >=1/3.
The exact squared state overlap is
  F=|<psi0|psi1>|^2=sqrt(1-(8h-1)/(15h^2)).
This follows either from Gaussian determinant normalization or directly from Fock coefficient summation; see acquisition_proof.txt for an independent reconstruction. For N raw independent copies, even arbitrary collective or adaptive measurement has distinguishing TV <=sqrt(1-F^N)<=sqrt(8N/(15h)).
An algorithm that always outputs a count and is within epsilon of each conditional target has output separation >=1/3-2epsilon. Data processing therefore gives
  N >= (15h/8)(1/3-2epsilon)^2.
In particular epsilon=1/12 requires N>=5h/96.
The selector is unknown and the exact true kernel is NOT supplied to this acquisition interface. The theorem permits a known promise that the state is one of these two models. It excludes a free branch label, free postselected copies, and silently discarding failed runs. The bound applies to an unconditional sampler with a fixed worst-case copy budget.
Matching upper bound: detect mode2 photons on N copies; B0 never clicks. Under B1 the no-click probability on one copy is F. Choose B1 after any click and B0 otherwise. Since 1-F>=(8h-1)/(30h^2)>=7/(30h), error<=exp(-7N/(30h)). Follow with the known-kernel sampler. This yields O(h log(1/delta)) copies, and Theta(h) at fixed accuracy. The root's separate exact two-model family sampler supplies an elementary direct comparator as well. Its independently audited stronger result identifies the exact optimal fixed-copy count for exact unconditional target laws: N_star=ceil(log(Z_h)/[-log F]), where Z_h=sum_{j=0}^{h/2} product_{r=0}^{j-1}(1-2r/h)^2/(2j)!. Exact sufficiency/minimality uses (D1/D0)^N<=1/Z_h^2; the full channel and its ordinary polynomial(h) setup accounting are in the separate calibration_audit/audit.txt. This is not a poly(log h) exact-acquisition setup claim.

8. PRIOR ART AND NOVELTY STATUS
The aggregate distribution is a symmetric trinomial bridge and generalized central-trinomial/Legendre coefficient law. Generic unnormalized log-concave sampling already covers its oracle model: Chewi et al., 'Rejection sampling from shape-constrained distributions in sublinear time', arXiv:2105.14166. Schur elimination and binomial/negative-binomial path reconstruction trace to Athènes-Bulatov2014; determinant occupation laws to loop-soup theory. The source N417 already supplied the Gaussian composition and finite-bit route, while leaving numerical herald cost in its executable matching DP.
No directly matching published full finite-bit poly(log h) single-herald count-sampling theorem was located. That absence from this bounded search does not prove novelty. Collision-aware Bulmer et al.2022 algorithms, and Budinger et al.2026 photon-subtraction representations, are stronger relevant comparators than naive Fock enumeration. The 2020 nonnegative-kernel GBS approximate-determinant approach is not a demonstrated uniform FPRAS: Uvarov-Vinichenko exhibits exponential estimator variance. See prior_art.txt and distribution_prior_art.txt for exact primary-source URLs and scoped comparisons.
The acquisition bound uses standard trace-distance data processing. Its explicit Gaussian pair is useful for this interface, not asserted as a new quantum-information principle.

9. FAILED/UNRESOLVED EXTENSION
With displacement c>0, aggregate cross count has w(k) proportional to b^k/k! times H_(h-k)(a,c)^2. Computing H_n by its natural recurrence costs O(n), restoring the unresolved binary-count gate. The k marginal need not be unimodal: h=2,a=1/4,b=7/40,c=1/100 has weights 6255001/400000000,7/400000,49/3200, with both endpoints more than800 times the middle. Thus the present dyadic proof cannot simply be reused. Parity splitting or higher-dimensional conditional-Poisson sampling remains possible but unproved. See displaced_gate.txt.
General signed/complex kernels, efficient physical calibration for rare conditionals, many-mode binary-large heralds, and displaced binary-large heralds remain open in this work.
