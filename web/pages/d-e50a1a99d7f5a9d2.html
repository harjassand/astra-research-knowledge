PRIMARY SOURCE RECORD — CYCLE 04

1. Giulio Chiribella, “On quantum estimation, quantum cloning and finite quantum de Finetti theorems,” arXiv:1010.1875v4 (2010; LNCS 6519, 2011).
   URL: https://arxiv.org/html/1010.1875
   Exact statement: §2.1, Theorem 2.1, Eqs. (15)–(16), HTML lines 197–218: symmetric-output-subspace broadcast channel has M&P approximation with k-output diamond error <= 2kd/M. §2.1, Theorem 2.2, HTML lines 218–234: arbitrary permutation-invariant-output broadcast channel has M&P approximation with the bound after d -> d^2. Its one-site marginal therefore has diamond error <= 2d^2/M. The explicit coherent-state POVM and universal M&P channel are defined in §1, Eqs. (1)–(5), lines 49–83; the arbitrary-output theorem proceeds by Stinespring dilation and symmetric purification, lines 221–234. This is the finite common-measurement mechanism used in C4.1.

2. Teiko Heinosaari and Takayuki Miyadera, “Incompatibility of quantum channels,” arXiv:1608.01794v2 (2017).
   URL: https://arxiv.org/html/1608.01794
   Exact statement: §4.3, Definition 4 and Proposition 12, HTML lines 529–558. N-self-compatible means N identical marginals have a joint channel (Definition 4, lines 533–536). All-N self-compatibility iff EB (Proposition 12, lines 544–558); the proof cites a symmetric broadcast-channel EB approximation with diamond error 2d^2/N (Eq. 60, lines 555–557). This is not a theorem that a merely two-self-compatible Phi is EB-close at fixed M.

3. David Sutter, Volkher B. Scholz, Andreas Winter, and Renato Renner, “Approximate Degradable Quantum Channels,” arXiv:1412.0980v3 (2017; IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 63 (2017), 7832–7844).
   URL: https://arxiv.org/html/1412.0980
   Exact statement: §3, Definition 3.7, HTML lines 309–317: epsilon-antidegradable means there is a channel Xi with ||Phi-Xi circ Phi^c||_diamond <= epsilon. Proposition 3.6 (lines 274–304) expresses the analogous epsilon-degradability optimum as an SDP; the text at lines 328–332 identifies the anti-degradable parameter with the complementary channel's degradability parameter, hence also SDP-computable. This optimizes antidegradability residual, not EB approximation or Dirichlet deficit.

4. Liuhang Ye, Bjarne Bergh, and Nilanjana Datta, “Quantum de Finetti theorems for states and channels in any distance measure,” arXiv:2609.40343v1 (submitted 2026-09-30; preprint).
   URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.40343
   Exact statement: §5.1, Eqs. (60)–(63), HTML lines 470–503: permutation-covariant n-slot channels A^n -> B^n and no-signalling from discarded input slots. §5.2, Choi operator-order representation (Eq. 91), lines 630–640, and diamond estimate (Eq. 92), lines 647–659. Its CP domination is a different order, and its replicated-input hypothesis does not match one-input broadcasting.

5. Fernando Granha Jeronimo, Pei Wu, and Haochen Xu, “Optimal Quantum de Finetti Theorems via Argmax Rounding,” arXiv:2608.02590v1 (2026-08-03; preprint).
   URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02590
   Exact statement: Theorem 1.2, HTML lines 195–200 (equivalently Corollary 3.7, lines 962–985): arbitrary exchangeable N-site states have a two-site tensor-power-mixture approximation with trace distance <= sqrt(d^2-1)/(2(N-1)), and the rectangular Werner examples give the matching Omega(d/N) lower-bound order. Theorem 6.1, lines 1458–1464, gives a dimension-free Hilbert–Schmidt approximation <= min{sqrt(d-1)/(N-1), 8/sqrt(N-1)} only for states supported on Sym^N(C^d). Theorem B.6, lines 2223–2299, gives the matching Omega(N^-1/2) lower bound as d grows. The authors expressly note at lines 304–306 that purification does not extend the HS result to arbitrary exchangeable states because partial trace need not contract HS norm. This isolates the support/reference mismatch for the channel-tree application.

6. Toby S. Cubitt, Mary Beth Ruskai, and Graeme Smith, “The structure of degradable quantum channels,” arXiv:0802.1360v2 (2009; J. Math. Phys. 49, 102104 (2008)).
   URL: https://arxiv.org/html/0802.1360
   Exact statement: §3 gives necessary and sufficient conditions for antidegradability of unital qubit channels; Appendix B.5 treats the depolarizing channel. The depolarizing map at Bloch multiplier lambda=2/3 is antidegradable. Antidegradability is equivalent to two-self-compatibility of a channel via the standard symmetric-extension characterization. For the norm-gap example, the normalized Choi state at lambda=2/3 is isotropic with maximally entangled overlap 3/4; any separable two-qubit state has overlap <=1/2, so its trace norm distance from every separable Choi state is >=1/4. The diamond norm dominates this Choi-input trace norm. The overlap bound follows directly because a product pure state has squared overlap with a two-qubit maximally entangled vector at most 1/2, then by convexity for separable states. This derived example only rules out uniform diamond closeness to EB; it does not address the deficit inequality.
