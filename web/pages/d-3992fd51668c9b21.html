# Technology development, loaded on demand

Start here only for a technological capability or an engineering obstruction.
Ordinary mathematics continues through `00_START_HERE.txt`, current dossiers and
the existing proof gates. This layer adds no live invention, model trial or
experimental result. The worked example is explicitly synthetic.

The programme seeks consequential scientific and technological capabilities,
including energy abundance, automation and space infrastructure. Potential
civilisation-scale importance is a selection consideration, never an evidence
status. Independently important mathematics remains a valid objective.

## Use the smallest record that answers the next decision

Copy [templates/LEAD.json](templates/LEAD.json) for an opportunity. Record the
capability, sourced best-known comparator (or `UNKNOWN`), limitation, proposed
mechanism, measurable target and next unresolved obligation. Four assessments
remain separate: potential significance, scientific evidence, feasibility and
readiness, and next-step value. Each carries a rationale, reference IDs and limits;
there is no weighted score or reward for confident wording.

Use [templates/DEVELOPMENT.json](templates/DEVELOPMENT.json) when modelling or
testing begins. Add only relevant sections. [schema.json](schema.json) describes
their shapes; [validate.py](validate.py) additionally checks hashes, references,
line ranges, lineage, transfer states and stage requirements. Text `UNKNOWN` or
numeric `null` means unassessed, never zero or an available free oracle. An
essential unknown remains an unresolved gate even if other fields are complete.

Live records use `technology/records/T-<stable-id>/v0001.json`. Subsequent versions
must retain the ID, increment the version and hash-pin the previous record.
Published versions and evidence are immutable; append a new version for changed
assumptions, negative evidence, reassessment or abandonment. `--immutable-base`
checks committed and working-tree edits against a pinned PR base. Without it,
current bytes alone cannot establish historical immutability.

## Evidence and development are separate

`stage` is the highest completed development gate, not the work being attempted.
`next_obligation` names the next decisive work; evidence can be recorded at any
stage without promoting the record. Gates need scoped references and explicit
decisions. A receipt is an attestation, not proof that its author is independent
or its scientific conclusion is true.

| Completed gate | Required scoped evidence |
| --- | --- |
| opportunity | Precise capability and comparator status; an untested lead is allowed |
| mechanism | Hypothesis or derivation identifying the proposed causal/computational operation |
| model | Derivation and scientific/engineering contract; source correctness remains separately scoped |
| simulation | Executed simulation with exact output references and a decisive-test contract |
| experiment | Independent physical measurement for physical/hybrid work; independent computational benchmark for software |
| prototype | Independently attested prototype validation in the claimed environment |
| scaling | Demonstrated system in that environment plus an explicit scaling assessment |

All prior gates must be recorded. Reproduced simulations require an independence
receipt, yet remain simulations. Failed and unknown outcomes cannot close a gate.
AI narratives, internal model agreement, successful compilation, source hashes
and planned tests cannot become experimental or prototype evidence. Software
benchmarks cannot validate a physical device. The schema records evidence
*claims*; scientific review must assess their scope, controls and credibility.

These gates are Astra development milestones, **not NASA or other numerical
Technology Readiness Levels**. V1 assigns no TRL. Any future TRL assessment must
identify its standard, relevant environment, acceptance criteria and exact
evidence; confidence, significance and a simulation count cannot assign it.

The engineering contract records governing equations, assumptions, conservation
and thermodynamics, material constraints, units/tolerances, input acquisition,
error budget, energy balance, reliability and resource requirements. Use an
explicit reason when a field is inapplicable. A proposal relying on physical
efficiency must include its complete preparation, control, loss and output energy
boundary; arithmetic counts are not joules. Scaling adds manufacturability,
supply chain, energy/material demands, economic comparator, maintenance,
environmental constraints and limiting regimes. These sections are optional for
leads and required only at the appropriate development gate.

## Mathematics to technology, and back

A `bridges` entry pairs the exact source and destination interfaces: objects,
regime/quantifiers, supplied/acquired information, legal access, guarantee,
units/norm/precision and costs. Record the actually established source scope,
possible capability, implementation, missing operations, resource feasibility
and decisive validation experiment. Cite source IDs when available, current card
IDs and open proof-gate IDs; preserve their status rather than copying a theorem
into a new, stronger authority. The five states and proof obligations in
[`agent/COMPOSITION.txt`](../agent/COMPOSITION.txt) apply unchanged. Shared topics
are insufficient. A scoped transfer proof does not certify its source theorem.

A reverse `handoffs` entry starts from an engineering obstruction or anomaly and
states the precise requested mathematical capability, information interface and
acceptance test. Forward handoffs carry a mechanism to implementation/validation.
[routes.tsv](routes.tsv) routes both directions. An accepted handoff is permission
to pursue a separate opportunity, not an edit to another branch's active objective
or checkpoint. A branch may decline or preserve the problem for later.

Theoretical Pro and Primitive Genesis retain independent invention. Computational
Pro receives algorithm and acquisition contracts; current computational instances
are already represented by `worker-*` checkpoints. Natural Sciences Pro handles
physical hypotheses and measurements. Sol Ultra uses the `ultra` role for dynamic
cross-disciplinary synthesis and worker allocation; this architecture fixes no
technology sectors or worker quotas. Technology Foundry reuses
[`foundry/pilots`](../foundry/pilots) for public software pilots and engineering
test artifacts. No additional checkpoint branch is registered automatically.

## Commands and sharing

From the repository root, using Python 3.10+ and only the standard library:

```sh
python3 technology/validate.py validate --include-fixtures --immutable-base <full-PR-base-SHA>
python3 technology/validate.py route --card N654
python3 technology/validate.py route --role theoretical-pro --include-fixtures
python3 frontier/retrieve.py technology N654
python3 technology/fixtures/contraction/demo.py --check
python3 -m unittest discover -s technology -p 'test_*.py'
```

Routes exclude fixtures by default and return the latest version only. Empty
routes mean no public connection has been recorded, not technological
impossibility. Static readers can follow record paths and `routes.tsv` directly;
no database, model calls, service, private index or paid dependency is required.

Publish public records and exact artifacts through a dedicated Git branch/PR.
Use existing [`state/CONTINUITY.md`](../state/CONTINUITY.md) for actual branch
handoffs, including stale-parent and concurrent-head handling. A technology
handoff does not bypass that publisher. The pinned baseline has three malformed
historical checkpoints that currently block repository-wide checkpoint validation
and publication; see [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). This layer preserves them and
does not invent ancestry to repair them. Technology records can still be reviewed
through Git while that separate source-preserving repair is pending.

Follow [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) for stage decisions, the synthetic example and
public/confidential separation. Research-workflow observations belong in
[`evaluation/workflows`](../evaluation/workflows/README.md); the existing matched
evaluation protocol remains the authority for comparative claims.
