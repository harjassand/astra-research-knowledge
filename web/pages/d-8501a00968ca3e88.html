3. CENTRAL BLOCK GLUING LEMMA

Let a traceless Hermitian frame Bi live on an m-dimensional block S, with exact
tracial envelope Gs>=0 and Ls=sum Bi^2. Suppose there are PSD local
operators Xs,Ys satisfying

 Ws=sum Bi^T(A)(Bi(B)+Bi(C))<=Xs_A^T+Ys_B+Ys_C,
 tau_m(Xs+2Ys)=Vs+ks.                                           (O3)

Extend every Bi by zero to H=S direct-sum T. Add the single observable
D=cI_S direct-sum D_T, where D_T is real diagonal, and choose its trace
so D is traceless. Let Delta=min_{eigenvalues d of D_T}|d-c|. If

 Delta^2/2 >= ||P_s||,   P_s=sum_i Bi^T tensor Bi,                (O4)

then an exact full-frame envelope and a trace-optimal C=2 local dual are

 Gfull=(Gs direct-sum 0_T)+D^2,
 X=(Xs direct-sum 0_T)+D^2,
 Y=(Ys direct-sum 0_T)+D^2/2.                                   (O5)

The envelope proof and matching block ensemble are the argument in
Section 1. In particular Vfull=(m/d)Vs+tau_d(D^2) and
kfull=(m/d)ks+tau_d(D^2), so tau_d(X+2Y)=Vfull+kfull.

For positivity, split the triple tensor product into S/T label sectors.
The D contribution to X_A^T+Y_B+Y_C-W is exactly

 (1/2)[(D_A^T-D_B)^2+(D_A^T-D_C)^2]>=0.                          (O6)

On SSS it vanishes and (O3) applies. If the reference is in T, or if
both outputs are in T, the Bi star vanishes and all remaining local
operators are PSD. The only other case has reference in S and exactly
one output in S. Its Bi interaction is P_s, while its positive local
terms can be discarded. The other output is in T, so (O6)>=Delta^2/2.
Condition (O4) then makes the total deficit PSD. These sectors exhaust
H^tensor3. This proves Wfull<=X_A^T+Y_B+Y_C.

Consequently every state with all three marginals I/d satisfies the
C=2 star bound. Equal pair marginals or HS self-adjointness are not
required for this stronger tracial statement. The PSD hypotheses on
Xs,Ys and the explicit gap condition are essential sufficient assumptions.
This is a block-extension lemma, not a reduction of arbitrary frames.
