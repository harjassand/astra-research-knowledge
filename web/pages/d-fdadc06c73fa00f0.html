D. ACQUIRED RESAMPLING GAP FOR THE ACTUAL DETYPED WIRES

Let a(X),b(Y) be centered local real functions. Let m_a,m_b and Var_a,Var_b be their means and variances on valid-prefix ordinary wires. On invalid wires they may vary with their private salts. Expanding the four branches and using global centering cancels all mean products:

Thus the maximal local correlation is at most q. For centered pair f, set a=P_X f,b=P_Y f and S=||a||^2+||b||^2. Then
