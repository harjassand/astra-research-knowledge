1. What changed from the previous approach
The previous conditional-envelope construction correctly retained hidden source mass and internal residence, but independently combining their worst cases could make the result unusable. Placing the 100-fold interaction uncertainty in its dominant trap produced an interval wider than five million-fold.    RESEARCH_NOTE
The replacement is to derive which part of the response can actually remain slow, while bounding everything orthogonal to it. The energy law determines equilibrium weights; the same law, together with the microscopic mobility rule, determines local transition rates. Those quantities are no longer independently adjustable.
I developed three mechanisms before concentrating on the successful one.
Local inverse-response moments. Splitting constants from mean-zero functions gives an exact Schur expression for the mean lifetime:
\[
\tau_{\mathrm{eq}}
=
\frac{1}{\mu-\langle g,C^{-1}g\rangle},
\]
where \(\mu\) is the mean reaction hazard and \(g\) its centered fluctuation. Locality permits acquiring several moments of \(C\) without trajectories. However, in the cooperative-trap test, four moment levels still allowed the denominator to approach zero. This did not produce a finite upper bound. Inverse-form quadrature is established prior art, not a new operation by itself. arXiv
Polynomial response fitting. Linear, quadratic and iteratively refined trial functions produced valid but broad certificates. Eight refinements of one trial reached only a ratio of approximately 1.483 while increasing its polynomial representation to 1,472 terms. Improving the fitted function alone was not sufficient.
Energy-derived slow structure plus a complete remainder bound. This succeeded in the rare cooperative regime. The crucial information was not a list of transition paths. It was an algebraically identifiable collective mode, a globally valid relaxation bound for the remaining modes, and an energy-based representation whose omitted source contribution could be calculated.
2. The physical family and the information actually acquired
The benchmark uses the classical ferromagnetic Ising ring with Glauber dynamics, supplemented by a tagged local reaction. The interacting spin dynamics is established physics; the tagged reaction is an authored kinetic diagnostic, not a calibrated molecular application. AIP Publishing
Let \(x_i\in\{0,1\}\) describe elementary closed/open coordinates. Before acquisition, the learner does not receive their ring ordering or interaction coefficient. Within the declared pairwise class,
\[
w(x)
=
r^{\sum_i x_i}
t^{\sum_{(i,j)\in E}\mathbf 1[x_i=x_j]},
\qquad t>1,
\]
where \(E\) is the unknown cycle. The successful dominant-trap construction uses the recovered symmetric case \(r=1\).
The reaction hazard is
\[
k(x)=\kappa x_0.
\]
Thus the physical target coordinate is known, but the collective conformational mechanism controlling its activation is not supplied as a pathway.
Recovering the interaction structure
The prototype queries the energies of the reference configuration, every single-coordinate perturbation and every double-coordinate perturbation. Using their Boltzmann weights,
\[
\frac{w(e_i+e_j)w(0)}{w(e_i)w(e_j)}
=
\begin{cases}
t^2,&(i,j)\in E,\\
1,&(i,j)\notin E.
\end{cases}
\]
These contrasts recover the graph and coupling. The single-coordinate contrasts then recover the uniform field parameter \(r\).
The count is
\[
1+n+\binom n2,
\]
or 301 interrogations for \(n=24\). Each synthetic energy evaluation inspects the model’s local interactions; that work is included.
No equilibrium trajectories, normalized conditional densities or metastable escape-rate queries are used. The prototype returns exact rational Boltzmann weights to keep the subsequent audit exact. Finite-error energy observations are treated by a separate robustness calculation below.
The global pairwise law remains a premise. These queries cannot establish that an unobserved higher-order interaction is absent. The algorithm reconstructs the physical model within that class; it does not certify the class from sparse observations.
The kinetic clock is separate information
With \(\sigma_i=2x_i-1\), define
\[
\eta=\frac{t^2-1}{t^2+1}.
\]
The specified microscopic heat-bath rate is
\[
q_i(\sigma)
=
\frac{\nu}{2}
\left[
1-\frac{\eta}{2}\sigma_i(\sigma_{i-1}+\sigma_{i+1})
\right].
\]
Creating a defect in an aligned configuration therefore has rate
\[
\frac{\nu}{t^2+1}.
\]
This rare rate is calculated from the acquired energy and the microscopic law; it is not measured by waiting. But \(\nu\) and \(\kappa\) are physical model coefficients, not consequences of equilibrium energies. The same Gibbs distribution can support different dynamics. arXiv
No laboratory calibration of those clocks was performed.
3. The missing global information can be derived in this family
Let \(L\) be the positive, un-killed generator in the true equilibrium Hilbert space. Define collective magnetization
\[
m(\sigma)=\frac1n\sum_i\sigma_i.
\]
Applying the recovered local generator gives
\[
L1=0,
\qquad
Lm=\lambda m,
\qquad
\lambda=\frac{2\nu}{t^2+1}.
\]
The magnetization identity is classical Glauber structure. It is not claimed as a new invariant. arXiv
The useful construction is to separate that genuinely slow direction from every other mode. I derive
\[
\boxed{
L
\succeq
\lambda P_m+
\gamma\bigl(I-P_1-P_m\bigr),
\qquad
\gamma=
\nu\left[(1-\eta)+\frac{4\eta}{n^2}\right].
}
\tag{1}
\]
Here \(P_1\) and \(P_m\) are orthogonal projectors onto the constant and magnetization directions.
As \(t\) increases, the collective relaxation rate \(\lambda\) becomes arbitrarily small. The complementary bound \(\gamma\), however, remains of order \(\nu/n^2\).
Why the complementary bound is global
The derivation uses the action of \(L\) on spin monomials. Their degree either stays unchanged or decreases by two. On each fixed-degree quotient, the generator becomes a system of nearest-neighbor walkers killed when they collide.
For degree at least two, retaining the energy of just two walkers gives a collision bound. The degree-one nonuniform modes are bounded separately. Together, these establish (1) for the entire configuration space—not merely for configurations observed during acquisition.
The full argument and exact small-matrix checks are in the report. This is an internally derived specialization of known Glauber structure; external proof review and historical-priority assessment remain outstanding.
This is the acquisition step that was missing previously: the bound on the unrepresented response is obtained from the recovered microscopic law, rather than assumed to be available.
4. Source-complete closure with only a two-dimensional global inverse
Write
\[
K=L+k.
\]
Equation (1) gives
\[
K\succeq
C:=
\lambda P_m+
\gamma(I-P_1-P_m)+k.
\]
For any trial mean-time field \(v\), define the full residual
\[
r=1-Kv
\]
and the variational lower bound
\[
L_v=2\langle1,v\rangle-\langle v,Kv\rangle.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
L_v\le\tau_{\mathrm{eq}}
\le L_v+\langle r,C^{-1}r\rangle.
}
\tag{2}
\]
The residual identity is established mathematics. The leverage comes from constructing an informative, cheaply invertible \(C\). Functional error-majorant approaches are important prior art here. arXiv
Because \(C\) differs from multiplication by \(\gamma+k\) only on the two-dimensional span of \(1\) and \(m\), its inverse requires a \(2\times2\) matrix calculation. The required static moments are derived from the acquired interaction law through exact finite-ring partition formulas.
This does not mean the physical response has two coefficients. Only the global remainder bound has this small representation.
Which configurations are represented?
The recovered energy identifies configurations with zero or two domain walls: the aligned states and every single contiguous reversed domain.
Their number is
\[
|S|=2+n(n-1).
\]
The compiler includes every nucleation location and both growth directions in that energy sector. It does not select one supplied pathway.
A sparse equation is solved on \(S\), with a trial value of zero outside it. Crucially, the final certificate does not regard leaving \(S\) as physical absorption.
Outside the one-flip neighborhood of \(S\),
\[
Kv=0,\qquad r=1.
\]
That constant residual represents all unlisted source states. Its contribution is included analytically; it is not discarded because their equilibrium probability is small.
At \(n=24\), the calculation uses:
- 554 trial configurations;
- their 10,178-configuration residual neighborhood;
- analytic source and spectral contributions covering all 16,777,216 configurations.
This is polynomial-size selective enumeration, not an assertion that no configurations are ever represented.

5. A guarantee in the genuinely rare regime
The numerical success has a corresponding finite-rarity bound.
For the exact truncated trial, \(n\ge4\), \(t\ge n\), and
\[
D_n=1+n\left(1+\frac{\nu}{\kappa}\right),
\]
the source-complete certificate satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\frac{U-L}{L}
\le
\frac{D_n^2}{t^2}
\left[
\frac{63}{4}n^7+
\frac94\frac{\nu}{\kappa}n^5
\right].
}
\tag{3}
\]
The constants are conservative—far weaker than the actual computed intervals—but polynomial in dimension.
The proof accounts for the omitted region rather than assigning it zero residence. Configurations with at least four walls have total mass \(O(n^4/t^4)\). Their residual can grow like \(t^2\), so small omitted mass alone is insufficient. Separating the slow mode makes the complete inverse-weighted residual controllable, yielding relative error \(O(t^{-2})\) at fixed \(n,\nu,\kappa\).
Thus, within this class, making the dominant gate rarer need not make acquisition or validation scale with its physical waiting time. Exact arithmetic precision still grows with the input bit length, including \(\log t\).
This is not a guarantee for arbitrary dimensions at fixed rarity, arbitrary fields, or arbitrary molecular interactions.

7. Coupled uncertainty and a distinguishable kinetic decision
For heat-bath dynamics, edge conductance is
\[
c_{ij}=\nu\frac{m_i m_j}{m_i+m_j}.
\]
Consequently, an energy perturbation changes source mass and transport conductance through the same physical parameters. They cannot independently take arbitrary favorable or unfavorable values.
If the acquired parameter uncertainty implies an energy-discrepancy oscillation at most \(\Omega\), and the microscopic clocks lie between relative factors \(a\) and \(b\), the report derives
\[
\boxed{
\frac{e^{-2\Omega}}{b}\,\tau_0
\le\tau
\le
\frac{e^{2\Omega}}{a}\,\tau_0.
}
\tag{4}
\]
For the uniform family, errors in coupling and field give
\[
\Omega\le n\bigl(|\delta\log t|+|\delta h|\bigr).
\]
Four-energy contrasts propagate a per-energy error \(\delta\) into coupling error at most \(2\delta\), and field error at most \(6\delta\), within the accepted model class.

8. Comparison with strong alternatives
The local-window comparison is unfavorable to the window—but not a universal separation
I implemented an attractive-coupling window solver with the same recovered law and the true initial window marginal. Frozen-open exterior coordinates provide a lower lifetime; frozen-closed coordinates provide an upper lifetime.
At \(n=24,t=1000\), the 11-bit window still gave a ratio exceeding 15,756. Its two solves and exact residual checks took about 2.16 seconds but did not reach useful accuracy.
That comparison shows why a purely local boundary envelope fails for this collective gate. It does not prove that every improved window method fails.
Full sparse solvers were also checked at \(n=6,8,10,12\). They agree with the certificates and are competitive or faster at the smallest sizes. Their larger-size reference intervals are substantially tighter, so their timings do not establish a matched-accuracy speed advantage for the candidate.
Close prior art must be credited
The trial calculation is an energy-based finite-state projection. Exit-time finite-state projection already addresses first-passage distributions and occupation measures; adaptive truncation and rigorous error control are not new by themselves. arXiv
Tensor master-equation methods already avoid exponential state arrays, and tensor methods have been applied to rare dynamical trajectories. Neither compact representation nor avoiding real-time rare waiting is a sufficient novelty claim. PLOS
Optimized weighted ensemble already uses local first-passage information for trajectory allocation. TAMMBER already directs atomistic exploration using missing-transition uncertainty. Descriptor-free AIMMD supplies learned committor and path-sampling machinery for molecular kinetics. arXiv
No optimized-WE, TAMMBER, AIMMD or strong tensor head-to-head was executed. This run does not establish superiority over them.
The candidate contribution is narrower: deriving a useful source-complete complementary response bound from an interrogated local law, then using it to certify an energy-truncated dominant-trap calculation. An exhaustive novelty review has not been completed.
9. What is established, and the exact remaining obstruction
The final audit executed 6,818 assertions, including detailed balance, generator action, rational small-matrix checks, exact inverse identities, interaction-graph recovery, full sparse references and eight witness replays. The larger small-matrix spectral check was numerical; the report distinguishes it from the rational checks. This is internal validation, not external review or formal verification.
The substantive advance is that the useful coupled constraints are constructed:
- equilibrium mass follows from the acquired interaction law;
- rare elementary rates follow from that law and the microscopic clock model;
- the collective slow direction follows from local generator algebra;
- every omitted mode receives a global bound;
- omitted source mass and residence remain in the certificate.
But the successful algebra relies on a symmetric, homogeneous one-dimensional family. A hidden higher-order well or an unmodeled kinetic prefactor can preserve every energy-query answer while invalidating the premises.
The next missing operation is therefore not another larger ring or another fitted slow mode. It is constructing a global complementary response bound when the acquired molecular law does not close an exact low-dimensional slow space.
This investigation supplies a concrete example of the information that resolves a dominant hidden trap—and a verified procedure for acquiring that information in a restricted physical family. It does not yet establish how to acquire it for a generic molecular gate.
