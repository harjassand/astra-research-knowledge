## Family 332: a constructive ambient nonlinear smoother

Input: finite stochastic `A`, stationary `π`, integer `t≥1`, real ambient
`L1(μ)` data `x_i`. Define `W` as the stationary average of squared original
distances over walk lengths `1,…,t`, and

`J_t(y)=Σ_iπ_i‖x_i−y_i‖₁²+tΣ_ijπ_i a_ij‖y_i−y_j‖₁²`.

Author claim: `J_t(y)≤3024W` for real ℓ1, using expected coordinatewise
medians of three independent endpoints from
`G=[I+t(I−A)]⁻¹`. The source combines a finite binary cut lift, cubic
boundary flattening, a quartic martingale potential, and geometric killing.

Our reconstructed improvements and consequences, with full proofs in
[MEDIAN_LIFT.md](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/Literature Digestion/notes/frontier_functional/cycle19/MEDIAN_LIFT.md):

- The local cubic/quartic inequality has sharp constant 18. A separate
  stationary geometric/Cesàro comparison is less than 18. Together they
  give `J_t(y)≤324W`, hence `N₂≤18`, in every real ambient `L1(μ)`.
  Reversibility is unnecessary for the construction. The global constant
  18 is an upper bound; only the local inequality was shown sharp.
- Sorting coordinates and applying cubic threshold probabilities avoids
  exponential cut enumeration. Exact rational finite input has dense
  arithmetic cost `O(n³+n²d+nd log n)`, storage `O(n²+nd)`, and polynomial
  bit cost with appropriate elimination. All acquisition/output work is charged.
- A shared reference probability gives a globally 2-Lipschitz simplex
  retraction, preserving common support faces. Thus `N₂(Δ)≤36`; the known
  Hilbert→ℓ1 extension consequence followed by this repair extends into `Δ`
  with another factor at most two. Efficient arbitrary-domain extension
  remains separate.
- For hypercube walk data `x_v=e_v`, the prescribed linear-resolvent
  ratio is at least `d/9` at `t=d`. This proves that that particular
  linear smoother cannot have a universal constant. Constant-zero and
  stationary-mean outputs have bounded ratios on the same family, so
  the example proves no advantage over all competent smoothers or over
  the minimum objective. The revised computation includes those controls.

The independent checker reconstructed the full source assembly and all these

The confidence version replaces `L` by `Lδ` inside the square root; simplex
