Quantum Entropy Contraction and Factorization from Hypercontractivity
LI GAO AND LIJUN WANG
Abstract.
We prove that hypercontractivity implies entropy contraction for a single quantum channel, without a detailed balance condition. For primitive quantum Markov semigroups that are KMS-symmetric with respect to a faithful invariant state σ\sigma, we obtain the modified Log-Sobolev bound
α1≥λ(2+log⁡‖σ−1‖∞)\alpha_{1}\geq\frac{\lambda}{(2+\log\|\sigma^{-1}\|_{\infty})}
where λ\lambda is the spectral gap. This removes the assumption of LpL_{p}-regularity for the comparison through the log-Sobolev constant. As an application, we show that the hypercontractivity of an average of two conditional expectations implies the approximate tensorization of relative entropy.
1. Introduction
Quantitative convergence to equilibrium can be studied through either norm estimates
or through entropy inequalities. Hypercontractivity describes the improvement
of weighted LpL_{p} norms under a Markov evolution, while entropy contraction
measures the decrease of relative entropy. In this work, we study the implications from hypercontractive estimate to entropy contraction in finite-dimensional quantum systems.
We note that the passage from hypercontractivity (HC) to entropy contraction (EC) already
makes sense for a single channel. This is inspired by the work of Salez [20]
in the classical setting, without reversibility or
regularity assumptions. In the quantum setting, the corresponding statement
follows from Hirche–Rouzé–França [12, Proposition 5.4] through
KMS duality. We combine it
with a centering criterion that removes the multiplicative defect in a hyperbounded
(HB) estimate using strict L2L_{2}-contraction on centered elements.

2. Entropy contraction via Hypercontractivity
We discuss the entropy contraction from hypercontractivity via a dual form of [12, Proposition 5.4]. The hypercontractivity can be obtained from a hyperbounded endpoint and strict L2L_{2} contraction on centered observables, using a weighted version of the method in [25, Theorem 1.6].
The centered criterion will be used directly for semigroups in Section 3.
2.1. Weighted norms and state-preserving channels
Throughout the paper, let B⁡(ℋ)B(\mathcal{H}) denote the algebra of linear operators on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space ℋ\mathcal{H} and Tr\Tr be the standard matrix trace. We identify states with density matrices, and fix a faithful state σ>0\sigma>0, Tr⁡σ=1\Tr\sigma=1. All logarithms are natural.

2.2. From norm estimates to entropy contraction
For two states ρ,ω\rho,\omega, their Umegaki relative entropy [23] is
D(ρ∥ω)=Trρ(logρ−logω)D(\rho\|\omega)=\Tr\rho(\log\rho-\log\omega)
if supp⁡ρ⊆supp⁡ω\supp\rho\subseteq\supp\omega, and is +∞+\infty otherwise.
We recall the three functional inequalities of a state preserving quantum channel.

‖Φ‖p→q,σ≤M\|\Phi\|_{p\to q,\sigma}\leq M

D(Φ∗ρ∥σ)≤ηD(ρ∥σ),∀ state ρ.\displaystyle D(\Phi_{*}\rho\|\sigma)\leq\eta D(\rho\|\sigma),\qquad\forall\ \text{ state }\rho.

Hypercontractivity is the case M=1M=1, which can hold only if Φ\Phi is primitive, i.e. Φk\Phi^{k} converges to EσE_{\sigma}. Theorem 4.1 (AT from EC).
Let Ei:B⁡(ℋ)→𝒩iE_{i}:B(\mathcal{H})\to\mathcal{N}_{i}, i=1,2i=1,2 be
conditional expectations preserving a common faithful state σ\sigma, and E𝒩E_{\mathcal{N}} be the σ\sigma-preserving conditional expectation onto the intersection 𝒩=𝒩1∩𝒩2\mathcal{N}=\mathcal{N}_{1}\cap\mathcal{N}_{2}.
