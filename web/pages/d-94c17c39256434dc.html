BOUNDED HOLEVO BROADCASTING: MIXED-FAMILY ADVERSARIAL ATTACK
7 October 2026. Independent cycle-2 work.

STATUS
The broad target remains OPEN. No genuinely mixed family satisfying all three
C(E_n)<=C, b_2(E_n)->0, and liminf e_EB(E_n)>0 was obtained.
The strongest concrete result below is an exact obstruction for a genuinely
mixed, constant-capacity bosonic family: e_EB=2 b_2, indeed
b_K=(1-1/K)e_EB for every K>=2. It is an internally derived corollary of
established universal cloning/state-estimation results, not a novelty claim.
A mixed dyadic ladder of these states also has a bounded-capacity
classicalization bound O(sqrt(C/L)); thus it does not rescue the N61 mechanism.

INTERFACE
For any supplied experiment E on H, T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2,
C(E)=sup_{finite priors pi} [S(sum pi rho)-sum pi S(rho)], using natural logs.
b_K is the infimum over CPTP B:B(H)->B(H^tensor K) of the worst unknown-state,
worst-receiver marginal T error. e_EB is the analogous infimum over one EB
channel B(H)->B(H). Channels act on the full input algebra; correlated outputs
are allowed. No purification or product-clone preservation is required.
Read N46,N61,N67,N69 and their decisive sources; the pure-copy and Pauli
failures recorded there were not counted as new attempts.

1. EXACT MIXED BOSONIC FAMILY

Let d>=2,k>=1, H_k=Sym^k(C^d), D_k=binomial(k+d-1,k), omega_k=I/D_k.
For a pure one-particle direction P_psi=|psi><psi|, write
N_psi=sum_{r=1}^k P_psi^(r), restricted to H_k, and define

 rho_psi,k = d N_psi/(k D_k).

These are the output states of the optimal universal 1->k cloner. They are
highly mixed for k>1, not pure tensor powers. Their Haar mean is omega_k.
For 0<a<=1 also allow rho^a_psi,k=omega_k+a(rho_psi,k-omega_k).
The family is faithful for every fixed 0<a<1. All formulas below acquire the
factor a where an error appears. Set t_k,d=T(rho_psi,k,omega_k), independent
of psi. Then, for the FULL orbit E_k,d^a,

 e_EB(E_k,d^a) = a [d/(k+d)] t_k,d,
 b_K(E_k,d^a)  = a [(K-1)d/(K(k+d))] t_k,d
                = (1-1/K)e_EB(E_k,d^a).                 (1)

Hence any sequence of these mixed experiments with b_2->0 has e_EB->0,
irrespective of dimension or capacity. Conversely k=d->infinity gives

 t_d,d=(d-1)/(2d-1),
 e_EB -> a/4,          b_2 -> a/8.                       (2)

Thus bounded Holevo capacity coexists with robust nonclassicality in an
honestly mixed family, but its broadcasting obstruction remains constant.

2. PROOF OF THE EXACT ERROR FORMULAS

Twirling a proposed channel over U(d), and in the broadcasting case over
receiver permutations, cannot increase its uniform trace error and preserves
EB/CPTP status. The representation of U(d) on H_k is irreducible; the adjoint
representation in B(H_k) has multiplicity one. Therefore a covariant marginal
channel T fixes omega_k and acts on the span of
J_A=sum_r A^(r), Tr A=0, by a real scalar lambda. Its Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint
acts by the same scalar. Consequently

 T(rho^a_psi,k)=omega_k+a lambda(rho_psi,k-omega_k),
 uniform error = a |1-lambda| t_k,d.                    (3)

The multiplicity-one assertion is exactly Keyl-Werner Lemma 3/Appendix A.4,
not an assumption that arbitrary covariant representations are nondegenerate.
Its proof decomposes Sym^k(C^d) tensor its dual and checks that the adjoint
highest weight occurs once.

Compose this full-domain channel with the one-particle decoder R:H_k->C^d,
which traces out k-1 particles. R^*(A)=J_A/k for traceless A. On the legitimate
full-domain input P_psi^tensor k (outside the mixed promised experiment),

 R T(P_psi^tensor k)=lambda P_psi+(1-lambda)I/d.           (4)

If T is EB, R T is EB. The established optimal k-copy pure-state-estimation
fidelity bound gives [1+(d-1)lambda]/d <= (k+1)/(k+d), hence
lambda<=k/(k+d). This supplies the lower EB bound in (1).

If B has K equal covariant marginals T, embed each H_k output in k physical
qudits. The result is an admissible k->Kk pure-state cloner with arbitrary
output correlations. Every one-qudit marginal on P_psi^tensor k is (4).
Keyl-Werner's UNRESTRICTED-output single-clone theorem gives

 lambda <= (Kk+d)/(K(k+d)).                              (5)

The pure-output test also implies lambda<=1, so there is no alternative
lambda>1 branch hidden by the absolute value in (3). Equations (3),(5) prove
the broadcasting lower bound. This use of inputs outside E is legitimate
and essential: the original broadcaster must be CPTP on all B(H_k).

For EB achievability use the covariant POVM
D_k P_phi^tensor k dmu(phi), preparing P_phi^tensor k. The symmetric Haar
moment identity yields, exactly,

 E_k(rho_psi,k)
  = d int |<psi,phi>|^2 P_phi^tensor k dmu(phi)
  = d(I+N_psi)/[(k+d)D_k]
  = [k/(k+d)]rho_psi,k+[d/(k+d)]omega_k.                 (6)

For broadcasting achievability apply the optimal universal k->Kk cloner

 X -> (D_k/D_Kk) P_sym,Kk (X tensor I^tensor(Kk-k)) P_sym,Kk,

then group the Kk particles into K groups of k. Sym^(Kk) embeds isometrically
in H_k^tensor K. This is CPTP on all B(H_k). Its marginal scalar is the RHS
of (5), yielding equality in (1). The channel fixes omega_k and therefore
also gives the stated faithful a<1 formulas.

3. CAPACITY ACCOUNTING AND AN EXPLICIT CONSTANT-CAPACITY REGIME

Every orbit state has the same entropy and omega_k maximizes entropy.
Thus the Haar prior attains C(E_k,d)=D(rho_psi,k||omega_k); the same value is
attained by any finite one-particle design averaging P_psi to I/d, although
such a finite restriction need not retain the uniform operational lower bound.
Let n be the occupation number of mode psi under omega_k. Its distribution is

 p_r=binomial(k-r+d-2,d-2)/D_k,  0<=r<=k;
 E n=k/d,       E n^2=k(2k+d-1)/[d(d+1)].

Therefore

 C(E_k,d)=sum_r p_r (dr/k) ln(dr/k),
 C(E_k,d) <= ln[d(2k+d-1)/(k(d+1))].                    (7)

The upper bound is Jensen applied to ln under rho, equivalently the order-2
relative-entropy bound. In particular k>=d gives C<=ln3. More generally the
faithful family has

 C(E_k,d^a) <= ln{1+a^2[d(2k+d-1)/(k(d+1))-1]}
             <=ln(1+2a^2)  when k>=d.                   (8)

For k=d, dr/k=r and the only negative part of rho-omega is occupation n=0.
Its reference mass is (d-1)/(2d-1), proving (2). This directly verifies that
the lack of a counterexample is caused by a broadcast cost, not failure to
keep Holevo capacity bounded or failure to use mixed states.

4. MIXED DYADIC-LADDER ATTEMPT AND ITS QUANTITATIVE OBSTRUCTION

Take H=direct_sum_{j=0}^{L-1} H_(2^j), and
rho_psi,L=(1/L)direct_sum_j rho_psi,2^j, with the FULL direction orbit.
Its capacity is exactly the average of the sector capacities. Each sector
rho_psi,k is supported where n>=1; that subspace has omega mass
k/(k+d-1). Relative entropy to omega therefore gives

 C_k >= ln(1+(d-1)/k),
 C_L >= (1/L)sum_j ln(1+(d-1)/2^j).                     (9)

Let r=log_2(d-1). If r>=L then C_L>=L ln2/2. Otherwise the left Riemann sum
of (r-x)_+ gives C_L >= (ln2)r^2/(2L). Thus for C_L<=C and L>2C/ln2,

 r<=sqrt(2CL/ln2).

Measure the flag and apply E_k separately, preserving the flag. Using (6),

 e_EB(E_L) <= (1/L)sum_j [d/(2^j+d)]t_(2^j),d
            <= sqrt(2C/(L ln2))+4/L ->0.                (10)

The last step uses sum_j d/(2^j+d) <=log_2 d+3<=r+4.
This blocks the mixed universal-cloner replacement of the N61 pure ladder.
For fixed a<1 faithful sector perturbations, joint-mixture relative entropy
gives C_L^a >= a C_L-h_2(a), so (10) becomes
e_EB<=sqrt[2a(C+h_2(a))/(L ln2)]+4a/L. It still vanishes.

The physical splitter is also explicitly understood. Taking the r-particle
marginal of rho_psi,k gives

 Tr_(k-r)rho_psi,k
  = alpha rho_psi,r+(1-alpha)omega_r,
 alpha=r(k+d)/[k(r+d)].                                (11)

This follows by tracing (6), since Tr omega_k=omega_r and Tr P_phi^k=P_phi^r.
For k=2r, signal loss is d/[2(r+d)]. A dyadic flag splitter therefore has
error <=1/L+(1/(2L))sum_{j=0}^{L-2} d/(2^j+d), and (9) makes this small
precisely in a regime in which the displayed EB error is already small.

SCOPE LIMIT: (9)-(10) are for the full U(d) orbit, not arbitrary restricted
direction sets. An arbitrary restriction may have much lower capacity than
the orbit, and (9) cannot be assigned to it. Formula (1) also relies on the
full orbit. These observations are scoped obstructions, not the broad theorem.

5. OTHER ATTACKS REJECTED WITH EXACT REASONS

Fixed catalytic/embezzling register: replacing E by {rho_x tensor tau}, for
any label-independent fixed density tau of arbitrary dimension, leaves C,
e_EB and b_K EXACTLY unchanged. One direction appends the fixed register;
the other prepares tau before the candidate channel and traces it out after.
Both constructions are full-domain CPTP; EB is preserved by composition.
The original model already allows arbitrary known ancillas in a global CPTP
map. A parameter-dependent or label-correlated catalyst changes the experiment
and its information budget, so its return error alone proves no counterexample.
van Dam-Hayden entanglement embezzlement does not evade this interface.

Standard antisymmetric/Werner extendibility: a normalized antisymmetric
two-party state has Choi channel W_d(X)=[Tr(X)I-X^T]/(d-1). Its whole output
experiment obeys

 T(W_d(rho),I/d)=T(rho^T,I/d)/(d-1)<=1/d.

The replacement EB channel therefore has error <=1/d. Large extendibility
and constant entangled-Choi separation do not give constant experiment
reconstruction error; the latter tests unassisted density inputs rather than
arbitrary entangled references. This is an exact norm-interface obstruction.

Locking: the standard small-key examples keep a large Holevo quantity while
reducing accessible information. Tracing or diluting them does not on its own
supply a small-error broadcaster. A bounded-Holevo data-hiding construction
must still pass the broadcasting test; neither this route nor a quantum code
with an approximately shareable Choi state supplied that missing channel.
No no-go theorem for all locking constructions is asserted.

Perfect-matchings restricted algebra: coordinated with quantum_classicality.
That worker independently proved exact b_2=a(1-1/sqrt(2))/2 for its proposed
faithful matching family, via a joint-measurement witness allowing arbitrary
output flag rearrangement. Its constant error rules out that candidate. See
that worker's report for the proof; it is not an independently replayed result
of the present branch.
