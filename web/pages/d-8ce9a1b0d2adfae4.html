## 1. Finite-dimensional theorem

Let rho=exp(-beta H)/Tr exp(-beta H), beta>0 finite. Let O_1,O_2,O_3 be
Hermitian unitaries forming a full-space qubit Pauli algebra. Let L generate
a CPTP semigroup and satisfy L(rho)=0 EXACTLY. Its complex trace-induced
norm has a known upper bound Lambda. Define

    c_j = (Tr rho [H,O_j]*[H,O_j])^(1/2).

Suppose a local approximation to the SAME generator is

    L_R(X) = -i[H+sum_a B_(a,R), X]
       + sum_a integral (J_(a,z,R) X J_(a,z,R)*
                     -1/2{J_(a,z,R)*J_(a,z,R),X}) dz,   (4)

where B_(a,R) are Hermitian. The original H is left untruncated. Integrals
may be finite sums. Require

    ||L-L_R||_(complex 1->1) <= F_R,
    b_R = sum_a ||B_(a,R)|| < infinity,
    s_R = sum_a integral ||J_(a,z,R)||^2 dz < infinity.

Assume a projection P_R protects each actual truncated support:

    [B_(a,R),O_j] P_R = [J_(a,z,R),O_j] P_R
       = [J_(a,z,R)*J_(a,z,R),O_j] P_R = 0.             (5)

Let p_R=Tr rho(I-P_R). There is no assumption that [P_R,H]=0, that rho is
syndrome diagonal, or that L_R fixes rho. Define

    d_j = c_j + (2 b_R+3 s_R) sqrt(p_R) + 2 F_R,
    delta <= (beta/4) sum_j c_j,
    E_ref = sum_j d_j + 4 Lambda delta.                (6)

Then explicit CPTP Enc,Dec with Dec Enc=id satisfy, including every reference,

    ||(Dec exp(tL) Enc tensor id)(tau_LR)-tau_LR||_1
       <= t E_ref.                                    (7)

Values above2 can of course be replaced by the trivial trace-distance bound.

## 2. Product rule without modular right-multiplication

Write Q=I-P_R. If C P_R=0, then C=CQ and Hilbert--Schmidt Holder gives

    ||C rho||_1 <= ||C|| ||Q rho||_1
                <= ||C|| ||Q sqrt(rho)||_2 ||sqrt(rho)||_2
                = ||C|| sqrt(p_R).                    (8)

One must not replace this step by a false bounded-right-multiplication
inequality in the Gibbs-weighted Hilbert norm. All subsequent right
multiplications use ordinary trace norm and operator norm.

Direct expansion of (4) gives

    L_R(O rho)-O L_R(rho)
      = -i[H+sum B_R,O]rho
         + sum integral [J_R,O]rho J_R*
         -1/2 sum integral [J_R*J_R,O]rho.             (9)

The H term is at most c_j by HS Holder. Equation(8) and commutator norms
<=2 times the relevant operator norm bound the remaining terms by
(2b_R+3s_R)sqrt(p_R). Indeed the gain term costs2||J||^2 and the
anticommutator term costs||J||^2. Integrability is explicitly s_R.

Set E=L-L_R. Exact stationarity gives

    L(O rho)= L_R(O rho)-O L_R(rho)+E(O rho)-O E(rho).

Since ||O rho||_1=||rho||_1=1, the two E terms cost2F_R. Therefore
||L(O_j rho)||_1<=d_j. This argument uses global exact stationarity only;
it never invokes stationarity of a truncated dissipator or individual jump.
