1. CHECKED SIMPLIFICATION OF W
Let s>0, q>0, rho=q^2, r=s^(-1/2)rho s^(-1/2), and
  z=s^(1/2)(logrho-2logs)s^(1/2),
  W=2Lyap_s^(-1)((rz+zr)/2-2rho).
Diagonalize s with entries s_i>0 and write u_i=logs_i. Since rho commutes
with logrho, direct multiplication gives
  W_ij=(rho logrho)_ij/sqrt(s_i s_j)
       -[2(s_i u_i+s_j u_j)/(sqrt(s_i s_j)(s_i+s_j))
          +4/(s_i+s_j)]rho_ij.
Define
  k(x)=x sinhx-coshx+1,
  psi(h)=h tanhh-1+sechh=k(h)/coshh.
Because 2(s_i u_i+s_j u_j)/(s_i+s_j)
  =u_i+u_j+(u_i-u_j)tanh((u_i-u_j)/2),
the equivalent exact matrix formula is
  W=s^(-1/2)[rho logrho-{logs,rho}-2rho
        -2(psi((u_i-u_j)/2) circ rho)]s^(-1/2),
where circ is the Schur product in the s eigenbasis. At rho=s^2 this gives
W=-2s, confirming all constants.

2. THE POTENTIAL AND ITS PSD
Write the spectral decomposition q=sum_a q_a P_a, q_a>0, P_a orthogonal
spectral projections, sum_a P_a=I. In the s eigenbasis set
  x_ai=log(q_a)-u_i,
  (K_a)_ij=K(x_ai,x_aj),
  K(x,y)=[x sinhy+y sinhx-4sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)]
          /[2cosh((x-y)/2)],
  R_K=sum_a q_a(K_a circ P_a).
Each K_a is a scalar positive Gram matrix by the supplied audited kernel
theorem, while P_a>=0. The Schur product theorem (equivalently tensoring
the two Gram systems) gives K_a circ P_a>=0. Hence R_K>=0. This includes
degenerate q eigenvalues and all noncommuting q,s; there is no simultaneous
diagonalization of q and s.

3. COMPLETE IDENTITY, WITH THE REQUIRED RESIDUAL CANCELLATION
For any real x,y put m=(x+y)/2, h=(x-y)/2. Elementary hyperbolic algebra gives
  K(x,y)=k(m)-coshm psi(h)
        =m sinhm-coshm(1+psi(h))+1,
and therefore the EXACT scalar identity
  2e^(2m)(m-1-psi(h))
   =4e^mK(x,y)+2(m+1+psi(h))-4e^m.                  (*)
For x=x_ai,y=x_aj, m=log(q_a)-(u_i+u_j)/2, while h=(u_j-u_i)/2.
The contribution of q_a^2P_a to W_ij is
  sqrt(s_i s_j) 2e^(2m)(m-1-psi(h))(P_a)_ij.
Using (*) and SUMMING a gives
  W=4R_K-4q+z+2s.                                  (R)
Specifically, sum_a m(P_a)_ij=(logq)_ij
  -(u_i+u_j)delta_ij/2, which gives z. The remaining extra term
  2sqrt(s_i s_j)psi(h) sum_a(P_a)_ij
vanishes because sum_aP_a=I and psi(0)=0 on the diagonal. The constant
term gives 2s. This cancellation is load-bearing.

There is NO per-projector assertion W_a=z_a+2s_a-4q_a+4R_a without a
residual. If 2s is distributed per projector as (s_i+s_j)(P_a)_ij, as in
the parent's independent audit convention, the residual RHS_a-W_a is
  2sqrt(s_i s_j)[coshh-1-psi(h)](P_a)_ij
   =2sqrt(s_i s_j)sinhh(sinhh-h)/coshh (P_a)_ij.
It is independent of a and cancels after the same spectral sum. Under
the convention 2sqrt(s_i s_j)(P_a)_ij instead, the residual is
-2sqrt(s_i s_j)psi(h)(P_a)_ij and cancels likewise. Both distributions
sum to 2s. The complete identity (R), rather than either false uncorrected
per-projector statement, is the proved result.

Consequently the exact Loewner bound is
  W>=z+2s-4q.
This is a substantive all-dimensional operator link supplied by the scalar
Hardy Gram, but it is only a link for this ONE potential, not for the full
entropy operator M.

          +Tr[B(s-r)Bz]+2Tr[B(q-s)B(q-s)].             (F)
Only the first term is manifestly nonnegative. The sum of the last two
can be negative even when J=E=0, so it cannot be discarded or claimed
