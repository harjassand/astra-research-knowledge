# Single rich current identifies known-partition scalar EIT: PDE rank bridge

**Status:** substantive mathematical deduction, scoped theorem candidate passed independent adversarial checks of the rank bridge. It depends on established localized-potential/sign-patch results and the independently checked generic vector-input theorem. Novelty unresolved. No publication/contact.

**Recent starting point:** Alberti–Poletti–Sanna–Santacesaria, [arXiv2608.14324v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14324v1),14 Aug2026, Section1.5 asks whether full vector-valued responses can lower physical experiment count to one generic excitation. The paper proves2d+1 random scalar observations; it does not claim the PDE result below.

**Question/interface:** Ω smooth bounded connected in R^n,n≥2; Σ open nonempty boundary part; real mean-zero L² current; full boundary voltage response on Σ. Known fixed finite-dimensional V⊂L∞ has only piecewise analytic elements, e.g. fixed-partition piecewise polynomials. Positive scalar conductivity σ_a=σ₀+Σa_jφ_j, independent basis. Does every distinct pair and nonzero parameter tangent give an infinitely ranked ND operator?

**Result:** yes, from an N+1 disjoint-ball construction that preserves localized potentials through N arbitrary linear current constraints. Every nonzero contrast has a visible definite-sign patch, even when globally sign-changing. Monotonicity then forces every secant and derivative to be nonzero on some vector in every finite-codimension subspace, hence infinite rank. Analyticity follows from the coercive variational inverse and its local Neumann series. The generic vector-input theorem consequently gives a.s. injectivity and immersion from one full-support Gaussian current, plus a sample-dependent inverse Lipschitz estimate on every compact parameter subset.

**Complete derivation:** [notes/rank_pde/proof.md](../notes/rank_pde/proof.md). check_vector_measurements and team_a_inverse independently reconstructed/audited the geometry, finite constraints and monotonicity signs; r07_analysis also passed the rank argument; team_a_inverse verified the original hull definition and partial-access extension in notes/team_a_inverse/vector_rank_audit.md. Novelty remains unresolved.

**Independent elementary PDE witness:** [notes/rank_pde/radial_derivation.md](../notes/rank_pde/radial_derivation.md) proves the all-pair/all-tangent rank statements for two-layer radial disk conductivity directly from exact Fourier eigenvalues. It gives explicit single rich-current reconstruction and a counterexample to replacing the rich current by any nonzero current: a pure-frequency excitation collapses two parameters to one scalar. [work/rank_pde/radial_check.py](../work/rank_pde/radial_check.py) produced [results/rank_pde/radial_check.json](../results/rank_pde/radial_check.json),100 exact rational modes. Those checks are finite diagnostics; universal statements follow from analytic formulas and asymptotics.

**Nearest prior art:** Harrach1808.04737v3 Lemmas2.5–2.8, Theorems2.3–2.4, and Harrach–Ullrich2013 underpin the conductivity proof. Harrach–Pohjola–Salo2019 APDE Theorems4.1/5.1 independently prove the analogue of infinite rank for bounded Helmholtz ND maps with nonresonance and sign-visible contrasts. Existing full-map/Galerkin finite-data Lipschitz results do not by themselves prove the single-current result. Targeted searches did not resolve novelty; radial symmetry single-input reconstruction is elementary and not claimed novel.

**Assumptions/decisive obstruction:** shared fixed V essential; sums of arbitrarily individually piecewise analytic states cannot silently be assumed piecewise analytic. For rough infinitely sign-oscillating contrasts this argument lacks the visible-sign patch. Anisotropic boundary-fixing gauge can give distinct parameters with identical operators. Unknown interfaces need separate derivative analysis. Full-support Gaussian covariance must be injective and trace-class. An individual input can fail.

**Cost/downstream use:** one excitation acquires a function, not a scalar. Noise precision, input/voltage spatial modes, electrode count, conditioning and computation remain charged. Radial interior sensitivity decays exponentially: the exact secant at mode32 with R=1/2 is approximately9e−22. A compact finite-dimensional current/output realization may exist nonconstructively by the additional finite-minor-cover lemma being audited by r07_analysis; no explicit counts or practical stability constant result here. This is a design principle for fixed-partition EIT and a concrete PDE answer to the2026 paper's single-experiment question within a meaningful subclass.
