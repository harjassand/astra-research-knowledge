GRASSMANNIAN CARTAN-HALF SPLITTERS: SCOPED EB DIRICHLET THEOREM
Worker c04_l07 | 7 October 2026

RESULT AND SCOPE

For every d>=2, 1<=k<d, and integer m>=1, the natural Cartan-half splitter
on the Grassmannian irrep V_(2m omega_r), r=min(k,d-k), is a genuine
self-compatible tracial channel and has an explicit coherent-state EB
rounder satisfying

  I-Psi_(2m) <= [2(m+d)/(2m+d)] (I-Phi_(m,d,k)).

The constant is less than 2 and is attained by the first nonconstant
Grassmann harmonic for this proposed coherent EB map. This establishes an
all-parameter result for this Cartan-split family, including rank r>1; it
does not establish a universal result for arbitrary compatible channels.
The constant's optimality among all EB rounders is not claimed.

The general Grassmann Berezin spectrum is grounded here in the primary
spectral theorem of Genkai Zhang, “Berezin transform on compact Hermitian
symmetric spaces,” Manuscripta Mathematica 97 (1998), 371-388, DOI
10.1007/s002290050109. Publisher page:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s002290050109. The full theorem
text was read in the author's public copy of the primary paper at
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/225423870_Berezin_transform_oncompact_Hermitian_symmetric_spaces.
The latter is a host for the primary article, not a second mathematical
source. The ingredients are the Berezin/Wick definitions in Sections 1.1-1.2,
Proposition 3.1, Theorem 3.6 and Theorem 4.2.

1. BEREZIN EIGENVALUES FROM THE SOURCE

Let X=Gr(r,d), the compact type-I Hermitian symmetric space. Its bounded
dual has rank r, root multiplicities a=2 and b=d-2r, genus p=d, and complex
dimension n=r(d-r). For integer s>=0, let H_s=A^(s,2)(X), the holomorphic
line-bundle representation of highest weight s omega_r. Its dimension is

  D_s = product_(i=1)^r product_(j=r+1)^d (s+j-i)/(j-i).

The coherent rank-one projectors p_(s,x) are indexed by x in X. Normalize
invariant probability measure mu so that D_s integral_X p_(s,x)dmu(x)=I.
The coherent measure-and-prepare map is

  Psi_s(A) = D_s integral_X Tr(p_(s,x)A) p_(s,x) dmu(x).

It is EB, unital, trace preserving, positive and selfadjoint for normalized
trace. Zhang defines Wick quantization W_s and its covariant-symbol adjoint
W_s*. The Berezin transform on functions is B_s=W_s*W_s; the operator map
Psi_s=W_s W_s* has the same nonzero eigenvalues by polar decomposition.

Zhang's Proposition 3.1 gives the multiplicity-one decomposition of L2(X)
by Grassmann harmonics indexed by partitions

  lambda_1 >= ... >= lambda_r >= 0.

His Theorem 3.6 gives the Berezin eigenvalue. Specializing a=2, b=d-2r,
p=d, n/r=d-r, and reversing the source's increasing weight tuple gives

  b_s(m_1,...,m_r) = product_(j=1)^r
       Gamma(s+j) Gamma(s+d-j+1)
       / [Gamma(s+d-r+j+m_j) Gamma(s+r-j+1-m_j)].

Here 0<=m_1<=...<=m_r are Zhang's weight coordinates. Set
lambda_i=m_(r+1-i) and reindex j=r+1-i. The denominator becomes
Gamma(s+d-i+1+lambda_i) Gamma(s+i-lambda_i). The product of numerator
factors is unchanged by reordering: product_i Gamma(s+r+1-i)Gamma(s+d-r+i)
= product_i Gamma(s+i)Gamma(s+d-i+1). Thus

  mu_s(lambda) = product_(i=1)^r
                   Gamma(s+i) Gamma(s+d-i+1)
                 / Gamma(s+i-lambda_i)
                   Gamma(s+d-i+1+lambda_i)

for lambda_1<=s, equivalently

  mu_s(lambda) = product_(i=1)^r product_(t=0)^(lambda_i-1)
                   (s+i-1-t)/(s+d-i+1+t).                 (1)

Zhang's Theorem 4.2, together with the Section 1.2 diagonal-symbol
identification H tensor conjugate(H) inside L2(X), gives exactly the
partitions in the s-rectangle, each once. Thus (1) is the full spectrum of
Psi_s on End(H_s), not only its action on selected highest vectors. This is
the candidate formula tested in the d=4,5 matrix audit, now sourced to the
complete primary spectral theorem with its type-I hypotheses matched.

2. ACTUAL COMPATIBLE PETZ SPLITTER

The Cartan component H_(2m)=V_(2m omega_r) embeds isometrically as

  J : H_(2m) -> H_m tensor H_m,

with a highest coherent vector sent to the tensor square of its level-m
coherent vector. Its image lies in the swap-symmetric subspace. Define

  T(X) = Tr_2(J X J*),
  T*(Y) = J*(Y tensor I_(H_m))J,
  R(Y) = (D_m/D_(2m)) T*(Y),
  Phi = R T.

Here T* is the ordinary Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint. Irreducibility gives
T(I_(H_(2m)))=(D_(2m)/D_m)I_(H_m), while T*(I_(H_m))=I_(H_(2m)); hence R
is completely positive and trace preserving. Since J's image is swap
symmetric, the two physical marginals of J(.)J* are both T. Therefore

  B_split = (R tensor R) o (X -> J X J*)

is an actual full-domain broadcast channel with both marginals Phi. The map
Phi=(D_m/D_(2m))T*T is tracial-selfadjoint, completely positive, unital
and reference preserving. This is an actual compatibility construction,
not a claimed PPT or Choi relaxation.

3. TRANSFER OF THE BEREZIN SPECTRUM TO PHI

Let sigma_s(A)(x)=Tr(p_(s,x)A) be the coherent symbol. On the Cartan
component, coherent vectors tensor exactly, so for every Y in End(H_m),

  sigma_(2m)(T*Y) = sigma_m(Y).                           (2)

This follows by evaluating on a coherent vector: its expectation of
J*(Y tensor I)J is its level-m expectation of Y. Equivariance gives the
identity throughout X.

For normalized trace tau_s=Tr/D_s, the coherent-symbol identity and the
measure-and-prepare formula give

  ||sigma_s(A)||_(L2(mu))^2 = <A,Psi_s(A)>_(tau_s).

On the irreducible lambda-module, Psi_s has eigenvalue mu_s(lambda). For
lambda_1<=m, apply (2) to obtain

  ||T*Y||_(tau_(2m))^2
       = [mu_m(lambda)/mu_(2m)(lambda)] ||Y||_(tau_m)^2.

Converting normalized to unnormalized Hilbert-Schmidt norms shows TT* on
this module has eigenvalue [D_(2m)/D_m][mu_m/mu_(2m)]. The nonzero spectra
of TT* and T*T agree, so Phi=(D_m/D_(2m))T*T has eigenvalue

  phi_lambda = mu_m(lambda)/mu_(2m)(lambda), lambda_1<=m. (3)

For m<lambda_1<=2m, End(H_m) has no such module by the source's support
theorem. Equivariance forces T and Phi to vanish there, which agrees with
setting mu_m(lambda)=0. The coherent EB map Psi_(2m) has eigenvalue
mu_(2m)(lambda) on every module in End(H_(2m)). Equations (1)-(3) establish
the full spectra of the two actual maps.

4. ALL-PARTITION DEFICIT BOUND

The scalar inequality is proved in GRASSMANN_SCALAR_LEMMA.txt. For every
nonempty partition in the 2m-rectangle it gives

  1 - phi_lambda >= [(2m+d)/(2(m+d))] [1-mu_(2m)(lambda)].

For lambda_1<=m, the proof groups boxes by columns, proves an affine bound
for each column product, then combines columns by concavity. For lambda_1>m,
phi_lambda=0 and the inequality is immediate. The empty partition is fixed
by both maps. The source decomposition is orthogonal and multiplicity free,
so the scalar inequalities are equivalent to

  I-Psi_(2m) <= [2(m+d)/(2m+d)] (I-Phi)

on all of End(H_(2m)). At lambda=(1), equality holds. There is no limiting
argument, finite-dimensional extrapolation or numerical optimizer.

5. EXACT FINITE MATRIX AUDIT

For k=2,m=1,d=4,5, grassmann_split_exact.py constructs
V_(2 omega_2)=ker(wedge:Sym^2(Lambda^2 C^d)->Lambda^4 C^d), forms the actual
partial-trace Petz map over Q, and checks the full six-module decomposition.
The exact eigenvalues are

  d=4: 1, 3/5, 1/2, 0, 0, 0;
  d=5: 1, 7/12, 7/15, 0, 0, 0.

The Weyl dimensions sum to 400 and 2500, respectively; all highest-weight
equations and channel actions are checked exactly. Replay output is
PASS_EXACT_RATIONAL in about 2.52 seconds. This verifies finite instances;
the general theorem follows from the cited source and derivations above,
not by extrapolating these two cases.

PROVENANCE / LIMITS

The Grassmann spectrum is established prior art in Zhang's theorem. The
all-partition scalar comparison and its transfer to this compatible channel
are derivations in this worker's audit; external priority for the combined
Dirichlet statement is not checked. The d=4,5 code checks only finite
instances. No optimality among all EB maps, efficient finite compilation of
the continuous coherent POVM, or theorem for arbitrary positive tracial-
selfadjoint self-compatible channels is claimed. The unrestricted
broadcasting-rounding objective remains open outside this Cartan-split family.
