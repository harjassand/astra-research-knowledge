# Recent scientific-discovery mechanisms: source notes

Retrieval date: 2026-10-06. Exact versions and recoverable snapshots are in `sources/r01_discovery/metadata.json`. Each author claim below is a short paraphrase; reconstructed results are in separate derivation files.

## AutoGraphForge — Ján Pastorek, 2609.03478v1, 3 September 2026

Read the substantive architecture/results/discussion sections, especially 3.2–3.5 and 4.2. Author claims: finite invariant tables support candidate generation; class-safe known bounds feed a convex-combination LP; counterexamples enlarge the table; surviving statements pass to deterministic Lean export. The proof stage was integrated and sanity-checked, with controlled evaluation over survivors still pending at this version. The example inequalities connect annihilation and edge cover on bipartite/regular graphs. Section 4.2 explicitly calibrates these examples as shallow combinations of classical results.

Our deductions: a trusted known-theorem cone is a useful conservative critic but not a certificate of global novelty. The regular derivation needs positive common degree; ordinary edge cover needs absence of isolates. The current public code at a pinned September 13 commit restricts its LP to the candidate's left-hand invariant. Our fractional-matching theorem quantitatively continues the example and is not attributed to the author.

## ULTRADISCOVERY — Li, Zheng, Song, Wong, See, 2610.03092v1, 2 October 2026

Deeply read mechanism/target/validation/limitations: A.6–A.11, B, C and relevant main results. Author claims: a controlled factorial benchmark separates representation disclosure from evidence alignment, while holding the latent world fixed. Its exact unseen-intervention endpoint and workspace metrics expose failures to compose evidence into a transferable account. Appendix C.3's route takes 39 actions and uses a reference oracle. The source promises a later code release.

Our deductions: the reference calculation is not an operational identifiability certificate. An unrestricted monotone instrument class can retain target ambiguity even after the dynamical representation is supplied. This is a general theorem, not evidence that the particular benchmark generator is ambiguous. The raw-model-class acquisition bottleneck remains open.

## TxGraffiti — Randy Davila, 2409.19379v2, 11 May 2026

Original v1 dates to 28 September 2024; the v2 revision lies inside the starting window. Read v2 discussion of table truth, significance/touch, optimization and filtering; preserve v1 as an older prerequisite snapshot. Author claims: restricted symbolic expression search or optimization generates candidate invariant relations; Dalmatian-style filters suppress statements uninformative on the current finite table. Examples are candidates for subsequent human proof/refutation.

Our deduction: table significance concerns a finite evaluation set and cannot certify universal validity, novelty, or that the initial invariant representation was acquired. The reusable representation supplied to a program must be accounted separately from successful theorem search.

## Annihilation, Independence, and Residue — Ohr Kadrawi and Vadim E. Levit, 2607.01438v1, 1 July 2026

Read abstract/introduction/contribution calibration, annihilating-decomposition lemmas and statements of main matching-gap results. Author claims: matching-number-dependent control of a-alpha, sharpened integer bounds/equality constructions, and a proof of a TxGraffiti residue inequality. The paper acknowledges prior related work and an independent stronger proof of its final conjecture by another route.

Our deduction: this is close prior art for any claim built from annihilation and matching. Our target ec-a has a different direction and is controlled by fractional matching, but no newness follows from that distinction alone. We did not independently audit the paper's whole extremal theorem. Full text was searched for fractional terminology without a match.

## Substantive unresolved alternatives

1. Kernel verification plus faithful invariant definitions could turn a surviving candidate into a trustworthy theorem. Statement elaboration and successful trivial proofs alone do not do so.
2. Conjecture generation can improve future research when it proposes a useful new variable or relaxation, but the reported graph systems largely begin with the invariant dictionary supplied. The fractional relaxation in our continuation was supplied by this investigator.
3. A controlled benchmark can discriminate source of failures without resolving arbitrary scientific model acquisition. Target-specific identifiability, economical experiment selection and acquisition of the admissible family are separate tasks.
4. Greater compute or richer grammars may improve search. No matched experiment here shows an acquisition advantage or a broad intelligence result.
