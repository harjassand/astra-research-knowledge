REVISION 02 — positive determinant-volume analogue and its boundary
Worker c01_l01. Date: 2026-10-07 UTC.
INITIAL.txt remains unchanged; this corrects and sharpens its prior-art conclusion.

The primary DPP result in Anari–Oveis Gharan–Rezaei (2016) does yield an exact additive two-replica inequality for ordinary k-volume sampling by a simple product-kernel lift. Thus the safe conclusion is not “no determinant-volume analogue exists.” Rather: an analogue exists for standard k-DPP/volume sampling because a known single-replica Poincare gap is stronger, while no matching source theorem or direct reduction was found for the pair-restricted hard-BCS law c_k(F).

Derivation. Let μ be any k-homogeneous strongly-Rayleigh law on k-subsets, including a k-DPP. Let P_μ be the lazy single-state exchange chain from the paper, with Poincare constant λ_μ≥C_μ≥1/(2kn). On Ω² use stationary μ⊗μ and define ~P by choosing x or y with probability 1/2 and applying P_μ to that coordinate only. This kernel is reversible. For F(x,y)=f1(x)+f2(y), its Dirichlet energy is exactly

    E_{~P}(F) = (1/2) E_{Pμ}(f1) + (1/2) E_{Pμ}(f2).

The ordinary Poincare inequality therefore gives

    Var_μ(f1) ≤ λ_μ^−1 E_{Pμ}(f1)
                ≤ 2 λ_μ^−1 E_{~P}(F)
                ≤ 4kn E_{~P}(F).

This holds for all real f1,f2 and is a valid computational pair-energy interface when the volume-sampling weights and exchange ratios are available. It is not the OA113 mechanism: it uses a full one-coordinate exchange gap and has no cycle-swap/repair encoding. The bound is in k,n for the DPP ground set. A uniform k-volume sample from rows of X is the k-DPP with ensemble L=XX* as described in the paper.

Why the lift stops at the hard-BCS target. That law is indexed by I with weight Σ_{J disjoint I, |J|=k}|det F[I,J]|², equivalently the 2k-column Gram volume on only block-complete column sets P(I)={e_i,f_i:i∈I}. It is not the unrestricted 2k-DPP on 2n columns. Moreover, the exact directed-4-cycle instance in INITIAL has pair-index support {{0,2},{1,3}}, which fails the basis-exchange axiom required of homogeneous strongly-Rayleigh support. Therefore Anari's hypothesis, and hence the preceding Poincare-to-pair-energy lift, cannot be invoked uniformly on c_k(F). The 2020 log-concave-polynomial down-up result is likewise a single-state result for its stated log-concave distributions; the pair-complete restriction's eligibility/irreducibility would need separate proof and is refuted for the direct one-element exchange graph by this example.

Separate exact warning about copying OA113's proof: the family in det_reassignment_ratio.py has line activities all one but a union-preserving two-copy reassignment with determinant-weight ratio δ^4. Taking δ=2^−b gives a 2^−4b Metropolis capacity ratio despite O(b)-bit input. Thus the DPP product-kernel route is real for ordinary volume sampling, but it does not supply OA113's missing pair energy for the block-restricted target; nor can one inherit OA113's safe-switch estimate from line-activity bounds alone.

Primary-source references:
- Anari, Oveis Gharan, Rezaei, “Monte Carlo Markov Chain Algorithms for Sampling Strongly Rayleigh Distributions and Determinantal Point Processes,” PMLR 49 (2016), Theorem 2 and §1.1: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v49/anari16.pdf
- Anari et al., “Log-Concave Polynomials IV: Approximate Exchange, Tight Mixing Times, and Near-Optimal Sampling of Forests,” https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07220

This result is a deduction from the cited single-replica theorem, not a theorem quoted verbatim from the paper. No need to invoke a local computation: the energy identity follows directly by splitting the random-scan product Dirichlet form by coordinate.
