Sampling noisy compressed linear
programs
Proof draft • 12 October 2026
This construction samples the full endpoint of a deterministic linear program with independent
compound-Poisson polynomial noise, even when the program is given by a short grammar with
an enormous expansion. The key is to use sparse parity summaries only for counting, while
drawing every actual noise mark from the supplied model.
Status. This is a mathematical proof candidate. It has not been formally verified, and neither
novelty nor practical speed is established.
1 Model and theorem
Let V = F₂D. The input is an acyclic concatenation grammar whose leaves are either a supplied
linear map C : V → V, or additive noise formed by XORing N independent copies of F(U), where
N has Poisson mean λ, U is uniform on b supplied bits, and F is an explicit multilinear vector
polynomial of degree at most d. All noise occurrences use fresh Poisson and latent
randomness, including occurrences of a reused node.
The means λ are nonnegative binary rationals. Binary-encoded repetitions are compiled by
repeated squaring and concatenation using O(log N) additional nodes for a repetition count N.
Matrix dimensions, explicit monomial lists, latent inputs, and all integer bit lengths are
charged to the input. Write G for the compiled grammar size and H ≥ 1 for its maximum depth.
Theorem. For every 0 < ε < 1, the construction samples the full final state to total variation
error at most ε in time polynomial in the input length, D, 2d, and ε−1. For fixed d, the cost is
independent of the expanded control length and numerical Poisson means, apart from their bit
lengths. The clean output is computed by matrix composition and added to the sampled error
frame. The maps need not be invertible.
Final frame intensity
For a node A, let CA be its clean map and νA its mark intensity measure in A’s output
coordinates. A noise leaf has νA = λ Law(F(U)); a control leaf has zero intensity. If A is followed
by B, independence and linear pushforward give
Thus the final error has compound-Poisson intensity νroot. Marks equal to zero can be
discarded throughout. No expanded fault history is required.

one with probability at least 2−d. To see this, choose a monomial of maximum degree r. After

4 True conditional marks and rank growth
Descent through the original grammar
To acquire a true root mark conditioned on lying outside W, descend the actual grammar.
Maintain a context matrix L from the current node’s output to root coordinates. At a
concatenation A followed by B, the two active masses are
Query the two summaries and select a branch in proportion to the estimated active masses. On
the left update L to LCB; on the right keep L. On successful preprocessing, true zero masses
are estimated as zero. Exact span tests can also guard zero cases.
If each estimate is within e±ρ, normalizing changes branch probabilities by O(ρ) in total
variation. There are at most H branches. Hence the selected leaf and context differ from the
true active conditional selection by O(Hρ).
At a selected polynomial noise leaf, draw fresh uniform latent inputs until LF(U) lies outside
W. These inputs are independent of preprocessing samples. If acceptance is possible, some
functional annihilating W makes ℓ(LF(U)) a nonzero degree-at-most-d polynomial. Acceptance
probability is therefore at least 2−d. Rejection sampling gives the exact conditional law at that
leaf in at most 2d expected proposals. A cap of O(2d log(D/ε)) proposals adds a chosen small
total failure probability.
Distribution preservation. Only the branch selection is approximate. Given the selected leaf
and context, the acquired mark has its true conditional distribution. The sparse summary is
never substituted for the physical jump law.
A finite rank representation
For a jump by mark v of intensity ν(v), replace it with a rate-2ν(v) proposal that adds either
zero or v, each with probability one half. The generator is unchanged:
Conditional on the proposed marks, the accumulated error is uniform on their linear span W: it
is the image of independent fair bits under a linear map. A proposal inside W leaves this
conditional law unchanged and can be discarded. A proposal outside W enlarges the span by

Thus at most D active stochastic transitions are needed to describe the final error law. This is a
law-level representation of the endpoint; it does not enumerate, reconstruct, or preserve the

5 The endpoint sampler and its error
The exact auxiliary sampler
Run the following process for a time horizon of one, with ν = νroot.
1. Start with W = {0} and elapsed time t = 0.
2. Set a = 2ν(V ∖ W). If a = 0, stop.
3. Draw an exponential waiting time T with rate a. If T > 1 − t, stop; otherwise set t ← t + T.
4. Draw a true mark conditional on being outside W, and adjoin it to W.
5. Repeat. On stopping, output a uniform vector of W.
The previous page proves this produces the exact compound-Poisson endpoint. Uniform
sampling from W means choosing fair coefficients for any basis. Add the clean deterministic
output to obtain the full final state. Each accepted transition increases dimension, so there are
at most D transitions and at most D clock draws.

Why parity preservation is not enough on its own
This argument makes no spectral-to-endpoint-total-variation inference. Preserving all Fourier
exponents by a small generator sparsifier does not, by itself, preserve the full finite-time law in
total variation. Here the summary estimates only clock rates and branch masses. Every
sampled mark comes from the original input model, which is the essential extra step.
On any explicitly bounded failure event, the implementation may return a fixed vector; its
probability is charged directly to the error budget. All success guarantees remain statements
about the unconditional output distribution.

6 Bit complexity and scope
Rational data and finite clocks
All summaries have polynomially many vectors. Basis peeling adds rational factors k/α with
polynomial bit descriptions; numerator and denominator lengths grow only polynomially across
H levels. Expanded occurrence counts are never stored in unary. Matrices, spans, coefficient
evaluation, outside-subspace tests, and branch weights use exact finite-field or rational
arithmetic. Categorical choices can use integer rejection sampling or a bounded approximation
whose error is budgeted.
Real-valued clock primitives are unnecessary. Let A be twice the total positive weight in the
root summary. Every approximate active rate is at most A, and every nonzero rate is at least
twice the smallest positive stored weight. Both extremes have polynomial bit descriptions. The
zero-summary case stops immediately.
Generate each clock to absolute error Δ, treating overflow beyond two as a stop. With at most
D clocks, rounding can change the stopping index only if a cumulative arrival is within DΔ of
time one. Conditional on its preceding history, an arrival has density at most A. A union bound
gives probability O(D²AΔ); choose
For T = −log(U)/a, exclude a tiny-U event of probability O(ε/D), truncate uniform bits, and
evaluate the logarithm to the needed precision using the known positive lower rate bound. The
precision is polynomial in log A, the logarithm of the smallest positive stored rate, and log(1/ε).
It never scales with A itself. This accounts for binary-encoded huge rates rather than assuming
an ideal real-clock oracle.
What the construction supplies
The output is one full joint endpoint error frame. It may be passed through a supplied
polynomial-time syndrome or decoder computation, retaining an additive distributional
guarantee. Nested repetitions, heterogeneous tiny or huge means, and directions appearing in
only an exponentially rare expanded occurrence are included. Clifford schedules are a physical
application when their supplied matrices are the binary symplectic actions on Pauli frames.
Assumptions and unresolved claims
The control grammar, explicit polynomial noise maps, and rate calibration must be supplied or
acquired separately. The theorem does not cover coherent noise, state-dependent rates,
adaptive measurement feedback, or the expanded measurement trajectory. It gives no relative
accuracy guarantee for exponentially rare decoder failures. The noise interface is an
assumption, not a tomography or physical-validation result.
The proposed combination is uniformly reusable parity-based active-mass counting, conditional
descent through the original compressed program, and finite-rank heatbath innovations. The
argument does not establish a priority claim. Its polynomial exponents may be large, and no
implementation benchmark or practical-speed claim is provided.
Discrete finite-alphabet extensions are being developed separately and are not used in this proof.
