1. DENSITY, GENERATOR AND PARTNER FACTORIZATION

Write q_a for its density, V_a=-log q_a, K_a=V_a'', and A_a=-d^2+V_a'd.

In detail V_a'=(x-E[X_a|W=x])/tau and
V_a''=1/tau-Var(X_a|W=x)/tau^2; the posterior is a Gaussian restricted to

the lower K bound and nonnegative posterior variance gives the upper bound.

Let U:f->f sqrt(q_a) be the unitary change from L2(q_a) to L2(dx), and set

A useful direct bound avoids relying on spectral folklore. If W_a^+>=c>0,
