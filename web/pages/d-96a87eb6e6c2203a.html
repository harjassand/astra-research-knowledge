PRIMARY SOURCES CHECKED (2026-10-08)

1. Gorjan Alagic and Alexander Russell, “Uncertainty Principles for Compact
   Groups,” arXiv:math/0608702v2 (last revised 2008-08-29); journal version,
   Illinois Journal of Mathematics 52(4) (2008), 1315–1324.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608702
   https://arxiv.org/html/math/0608702v2
   Theorem 1: exact operator uncertainty identity for multiplication-local
   P and left-equivariant R; Theorem 2: compact-group support/Fourier-rank
   uncertainty μ(supp f)Σ_ρ d_ρ rank(fhat(ρ))≥1. This is closest prior art.

2. Chunlan Jiang, Zhengwei Liu, and Jinsong Wu, “Noncommutative Uncertainty
   Principles,” arXiv:1408.1165 (submitted 2014-08-06); Journal of Functional
   Analysis 270 (2016), 264–311.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1165
   Abstract/source record covers finite-index subfactors, finite groups,
   finite-dimensional Kac algebras, Hausdorff–Young/Young and
   Hirschman–Beckner/Donoho–Stark uncertainty. This is related uncertainty
   literature; the bounded abstract check does not establish novelty.

3. Cédric Arhancet, “A reverse Riesz estimate combined with a spectral gap
   implies a Poincaré inequality,” arXiv:2607.08322v3 (submitted
   2026-07-09; v3 revised 2026-10-02).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08322
   General Banach-space Poincaré result from reverse Riesz plus reduced
   spectral gap, with commutative and noncommutative examples. Relevant
   Dirichlet/Poincaré antecedent; not a rank-profile theorem as stated in
   the abstract.

4. Marius Junge and Jia Wang, “Generalized Poincaré inequality for quantum
   Markov semigroups,” arXiv:2601.06005v2 (submitted 2026-01-09; v2 revised
   2026-08-31).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06005
   Noncommutative p-Poincaré inequalities for detailed-balance QMSs under
   spectral gaps, including non-tracial algebras. Related functional
   inequality literature; the bounded source check does not establish
   absence or novelty of the present result.

5. Pierre Bérard and Daniel Meyer, “Inégalités isopérimétriques et
   applications,” Annales scientifiques de l'École Normale Supérieure 15(3)
   (1982), 513–541, Theorem 1.5.
   https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.1435.pdf
   Volume-constrained Dirichlet Faber–Krahn inequality under positive
   Ricci-curvature assumptions. It has no matrix-rank parameter; a lower
   support-volume bound alone has the wrong direction for inserting it.

6. Hajime Urakawa, “Spectra of some Domains in Compact Lie Groups and their
   Applications,” MPIM Preprint 1984 (56), published in Proc. Amer. Math.
   Soc. 97(4) (1986), 717–723.
   https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/883/1/preprint_1984_56.pdf
   https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1986-0845995-X
   Computes zonal Dirichlet spectra for a particular family of domains in
   simply connected compact simple groups. This is direct Casimir/domain
   spectral prior art, but it does not state a rank-Q matrix profile.

COMPARISON LIMIT

The first paper already has rank-sensitive compact-group uncertainty and a
projection-overlap mechanism. Its operator theorem also directly yields a
support-constrained low-Casimir energy profile by layer cake. Do not describe
the abstract overlap argument or generic support-energy profile as novel.
The present packet explicitly derives the matrix/Schmidt-rank profile on an
irreducible carrier and matching Casimir tapers in SU(m) symmetric powers
and SU(2)^r products. The search above is targeted rather than exhaustive,
and priority/novelty remain UNKNOWN.
