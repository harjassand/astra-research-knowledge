## 3. Exact conditional pin first moment, with two obstructions

For a random permutation Π satisfying every exterior-minor identity E∧^kΠ=∧^kK, K=EΠ, partition labels J⊔Q and condition every cycle to hit J. Exact block determinants give

    Pr(G)=det(I−K_QQ),
    E[Σ|G]=K_JJ+K_JQ(I−K_QQ)^{-1}K_QJ.

The stronger arbitrary-W determinant identity proves every entry of the next-pin mean. Independent Bernoulli transpositions satisfy the exterior premise by rank-one affinity and independence. Given rational K, one determinant and linear solve compute the conditional mean in O(n³) rational operations and polynomial bit complexity; rejection of a rare event is unnecessary.

Two valid counterexamples delimit this service. Uniform S4 and (1+χ_(2,2)/2)/24 have identical exterior moments but different identity probabilities1/24 and1/12: exterior information does not identify the law. One shared physical X flip on two spins is (12)(34); at probability1/3 it has E detΠ=1 but detEΠ=1/9, violating the premise. This refutes the literal computational-configuration transfer, not passive quantum memory generally.
