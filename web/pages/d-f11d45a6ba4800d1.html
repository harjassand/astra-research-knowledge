I checked Mohammadi--Stevens, arXiv:2102.01655v2 (25 March 2021),
Low-energy decomposition results over finite fields, in the author report:
https://www.ricam.oeaw.ac.at/files/reports/21/rep21-20.pdf
Section1.1 explicitly separates large ambient-field subsets from subsets
small relative to the characteristic, and cites Li--Roche-Newton for
the subfield-intersection regime. Its notation assumes odd characteristic.
Theorem1 and its arbitrary-field extension retain |A|<=p^(5/8).
Theorem3's large-field factor includes |A|/sqrt(q) (up to logs), which
is ineffective for our |A|<q^.472 even without the characteristic issue.
These results therefore do not replace the applicable 12/11 comparison.

Roche-Newton--Rudnev--Shkredov, New sum-product type estimates over finite
fields (2016), author's university record:
https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/publications/new-sum-product-type-estimates-over-finite-fields/
The displayed 6/5 theorem requires positive odd characteristic p and
|A|<p^(5/8). It does not apply to our growing characteristic-two sets.

The bounded search also found arXiv:1407.1654 (sum-ratio),
arXiv:1807.11065 (A(A+1)), and arXiv:2604.17117 (dense prime-field sets).
These have different operations or regimes; no stronger theorem for our
exact fixed-characteristic sum/product hypotheses was established by
this check. This is an applicability comparison, not exhaustive priority
verification or a certified statement of the current global record.
