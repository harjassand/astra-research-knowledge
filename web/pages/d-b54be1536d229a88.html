R2 — NONPRODUCT FULL-DIMENSIONAL INSTANCE OF THE THICK-POSTERIOR GATE
Worker c09_l06; independent verification of the construction first stated in
c09_l03/INITIAL.txt. This is attributed to c09_l03, not a new-origin claim.

CLAIM AND INTERFACE

For every finite d>=3, fix 0<lambda<1/4 and let nu_d be product uniform
measure on [-1,1]^d. With cyclic indices define
  V_d(z)=lambda sum_{i=1}^d z_i^2 z_{i+1}^2,
  dmu_d(z)= exp(V_d(z)) dnu_d(z) / Z_d.
This is a strictly positive analytic, full-dimensional reference law with
nonproduct dependence. It is invariant under each coordinate sign flip and
cyclic permutations. Therefore E_mu Z_i=0, E_mu Z_i Z_j=0 for i!=j, and
E_mu Z_i^2=sigma_d^2 for every i.

Choose positive s_i=a exp(-b i) with sum_{i>=1}s_i^2<rho^2<1 and define
for v in the Euclidean unit ball in R^d
  dP_v/dmu_d(z)=1+sum_{i=1}^d s_i v_i z_i.
This is a normalized blind one-observation family. Since |z_i|<=1,
|sum_i s_i v_i z_i| <= ||s||_2 ||(v_i z_i)_i||_2 <= ||s||_2 ||v||_2 < rho;
thus every likelihood is positive and bounded below by 1-rho. At v=0 its
Fisher matrix is sigma_d^2 diag(s_i^2). The reference scores are pairwise
orthogonal, but not independent: the cyclic Gibbs density couples adjacent
squares, and the posterior feature law is full-dimensional rather than a
product law.

UNIFORM JOINT-DENSITY LOG-GRADIENT BOUND

The full log density relative to Lebesgue measure has derivative
  partial_i log g_[d](z)=2 lambda z_i(z_{i-1}^2+z_{i+1}^2),
so |partial_i log g_[d]|<=4lambda everywhere, uniformly in d. For any
coordinate subset I and i in I, integrate the full density over z_(I^c).
Differentiation under the integral is valid on the compact cube and gives
  partial_i log g_I(z_I)
    = E_mu[partial_i log g_[d](Z) | Z_I=z_I].
Hence |partial_i log g_I|<=4lambda for every I and d. Each marginal is
strictly positive and C^1 (indeed analytic in the interior and continuously
differentiable on the compact cube). This verifies the exact joint-posterior
thickness premise of c09_l03 with L=4lambda; it does not infer that premise
from covariance or Fisher data.

A dimension-uniform lower bound on each feature variance follows from the
single-site conditional law. Given all coordinates other than i, its density
relative to uniform on [-1,1] is
  q_i(t)= exp(a_i t^2) / integral exp(a_i u^2) dnu_1(u),
where a_i=lambda(z_(i-1)^2+z_(i+1)^2) lies in [0,2lambda]. Since the
denominator is at most exp(a_i), q_i(t)>=exp(-a_i)>=exp(-2lambda).
Therefore
  sigma_d^2=E_mu Z_i^2 >= exp(-2lambda)/3.
The upper bound sigma_d^2<=1 is immediate. So the Fisher singular values
have the same exponential envelope as s_i with constants independent of d,
although their common variance factor may depend on d.

TRANSFER TO ARCHIVE RATES AND REQUIRED QUANTIFIERS

Subject to the c09_l03 codec theorem, the above law meets its assumptions
with c0=1 and L=4lambda. Thus the positive product-hat encoder/decoder has
uniform sup_v TV error at most epsilon with
  log_2 D <= (1/2) sum_{i<=m} [log_2(s_i/epsilon)]_+
             + O_{a,b,lambda}(m log m),
where m is chosen so ||s_(>m)||_2<=epsilon/4. Its arbitrary-hybrid-memory
converse applies to every finite active set I and yields
  ln D >= (1/2) sum_{i in I} ln(s_i/(K_(lambda) epsilon)),
for s_i>=K_(lambda)epsilon, with finite K_(lambda) determined by the
joint entropy lower bound and the score-regret constants. Consequently for
s_i=a exp(-bi), the matched leading log-squared order follows only in the
triangular family regime d>=b^-1 ln(a/(K_(lambda)epsilon))+O(1). For each
fixed finite d, the correct claim is the dimension-truncated sum; it is not a
log-squared asymptotic as epsilon->0 with d held fixed.

The proof uses a single encoder and decoder blind to v, one output sample,
seed independence from (v,X), and a total hybrid dimension capped for every
seed. Shared public randomness may be averaged in the accuracy criterion;
per-seed accuracy is not needed. The upper's conditional reference measures
are
  mu_j(dx)=q_j^-1 product_i phi_(i,j_i)(z_i(x)) mu_d(dx),
  q_j=integral product_i phi_(i,j_i)(z_i(x))dmu_d(x).
Each q_j>0 from the positive full-dimensional density. This is a valid
measure-prepare codec on paper; evaluating q_j and sampling mu_j are supplied
reference/sampler costs, not acquired for free by the memory theorem.
Finite-bit realization, precision, reference acquisition, and output runtime
remain open costs. No claim here supplies an efficient sampler for this
Gibbs reference.

STATUS AND ATTRIBUTION

Exact analytic derivation of the Gibbs marginal score bound, variance lower
bound, likelihood positivity, and finite-d quantifier performed in this
revision. The positive codec and archive theorem are c09_l03-origin and were
only interface-audited here; their full proof has not been independently
externalized or peer-reviewed. This instance establishes that the stated
joint-thickness class includes a genuinely correlated, full-dimensional
family with diagonal Fisher at the reference. It does not imply a theorem for
singular shared-scalar families, arbitrary Fisher-orthogonal features,
N75's absent likelihood field, or an end-to-end resource-bounded archive.
