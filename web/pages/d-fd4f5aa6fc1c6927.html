# What the strongest audited coarse-graining certificates do and do not acquire

7 October 2026. rounding_spectral, verified Sol/max; no nested workers.
Author interface deductions from pinned primary sources. These are failures
of a proposed CERTIFICATE composition, not lower bounds on microscopic error
and not a no-go theorem for the physical memory.

## C1. Exact original-product service and strongest time-uniform comparator

The unresolved original service is a family-uniform comparison between
the exact microscopic reduced evolution from rho_S tensor rho_B,eq and a
same-bath finite-resolution generator, over a storage interval stretched
exponential in the actual recursive code size. It must also pay native
stationarity/Lamb-shift corrections. Neither a stationary engineered
Gibbs generator nor the interacting JOINT initial resource of the separate
candidate note is that service.

Fresh primary: Teruhiro Ikeuchi and Takashi Mori, "Time-Uniform Error Bound
for Temporal Coarse Graining in Markovian Open Quantum Systems",
arXiv2604.21366v2, 28May2026 (v1 23Apr2026). Official version history and
recoverable source were acquired. Directly read TeX142--end, including
the main theorem/proof337--499 and both End Matter derivations. This is
a real time-uniform finite-system theorem, and it supersedes an overly
broad claim that all known CG errors must grow with time.

The source compares Redfield exp(tL_R) to a temporally coarse-grained,
diagonalizable GKLS exp(tL), not directly a microscopic unitary in its
main theorem. It uses dimension d, coefficient-sum conditions on slow
and fast Bohr pairs, bath amplitude gamma and correlation time tau_B,
and a unique stationary state with gap g>0. Its theorem states

    E_CG=kappa[S]{c1(gamma/g)(tau_B/Delta_t)^alpha
          +[c2+c3(gamma/g)]gamma Delta_t}<=1/2
    =>sup_(t>=0)||exp(tL)rho-exp(tL_R)rho||_1<=2E_CG.   (C1)

Here kappa is the trace-induced diagonalization condition number and
the c_i may depend on d. The discussion375--376 and495--497 explicitly
leaves the many-body time-AND-size-uniform extension open. Linear free
bosons are discussed as a route to a finite-system Born--Markov error,
with reference to the older thermodynamic-limit paper; this is not an
explicit family-uniform all-orders theorem for our code.

One intermediate displayed integration-by-parts bound uses1-exp(-gt)
where its two endpoint norms naturally give1+exp(-gt). Replacing that
line by the latter only changes an unspecified constant c2. No source
refutation or failure of the stated scaling is claimed from that typo.

## C2. Quantitative memory-versus-global-mixing deduction

Suppose a CPTP evolution Phi_T has a CPTP encoder/decoder Dec Enc=id
and recovers TWO orthogonal encoded inputs with trace error<=eta<1.
Their logical trace distance is2. The recovered outputs have distance
at least2-2eta by the triangle inequality. Since Dec contracts trace
norm and Enc preserves these states' distance (Dec Enc=id),

    ||Phi_T[Enc(rho0)-Enc(rho1)]||_1>=2(1-eta).        (C2)

If its ALL-traceless mixing bound is the one used in C1,

    ||Phi_T(X)||_1<=kappa exp(-gT)||X||_1,

then C2 forces

    gT<=log kappa-log(1-eta).                         (C3)

Thus polynomially conditioned global rapid mixing cannot hold on a
stretched-exponential storage interval for the same protected qubit.
The ratio gamma/g and the diagonalization constants in C1 cannot be
replaced by dimension-free O(1) values merely because the bath intensity
is local. A large kappa can formally evade C3 but worsens C1 instead.
This is a universal conditional deduction, not an acquisition of g or
kappa for the actual code. The source could be improved by isolating
fast error/auxiliary sectors while retaining the slow logical sector;
that is precisely a new projection-resolved microscopic lemma, not the
main finite-system theorem as stated.

## C3. Scandi--Alhambra time-window tradeoff, including weak coupling

Primary pinned by rounding_prior: Matteo Scandi and Alvaro M. Alhambra,
"Thermalization in Open Many-Body Systems and KMS Detailed Balance",
arXiv2505.20064v2. The official historical metadata was already verified
in cycle16; this turn directly read source560--660,812--900,1580--1692.
The full unoptimized proof certificate app:f34 is

    E_SA(s;alpha,T)=K1+K2 s+K3 log(1+Gamma0 s/alpha^2),
    K1=4Gamma0 alpha^2 T/sqrt(pi),
    K2=6alpha^2 Gamma0^2 tau0 +(2+3Gamma0 tau0)/T
                                      +4alpha^2 Gamma0^2 T,
    K3=2alpha^2 Gamma0(1+Gamma0 tau0).                 (C4)

s=alpha^2 t_physical is the slow/kinetic time. T is the PHYSICAL Gaussian
observation window of the same-bath temporal averaging, not s or the
requested storage interval. Gamma0 sums absolute correlations over ALL
channels. Independent baths with fixed per-site intensity make it O(N),
not O(1) by a notational redefinition. The constants and nonoptimal
simplifications are retained as the source's proved certificate.

For this explicit certificate to certify E_SA<=eta, its positive term
alone necessarily requires

    T>=(2+3Gamma0 tau0)s/eta.                         (C5)

This is a necessary condition for THIS upper bound to be small, not
for the physical error to be small. The sharper earlier expression
app:31 has a term2Gamma0(1+Gamma0 tau0)s/(2+Gamma0 T), so at large s
the linear-in-s window demand is already present before the simplification
used in C4. It is not created solely by discarding a denominator.

The optimized window T*=sqrt(2+3Gamma0 tau0)/(2alpha Gamma0) can make
the error small by shrinking alpha. However it grows at the same time.
The existing local-protection method uses physical operator evolution
within T, an LR cone of size roughly vT plus a tail margin, and actual
code ball volumes O(R^3). Its Gibbs prefix ledger pays exp(const R^3)
against exp(-const Q). It therefore certifies only R=O(Q^(1/3)) and,
with this method, a polynomial physical observation window T_loc(N).
Any required Gaussian-tail precision can restrict T further; it cannot
increase the certificate's permitted T. If one combines C4 with such
an acquired restriction T<=T_loc(N), C5 yields

    s<=eta T_loc(N)/(2+3Gamma0 tau0).                 (C6)

This excludes a stretched-exponential KINETIC storage proof by this
particular global-error-plus-LR-prefix composition, independently of how
alpha is chosen. Small alpha can produce a huge PHYSICAL time with only
polynomial kinetic evolution, which is the trivial weak-rate slowdown;
it is not a uniform finite-intensity thermal-memory guarantee. A method
controlling only logical error, bad occupancy or stationary local sectors
could avoid C5. None is acquired by algebraically inverting C4.
