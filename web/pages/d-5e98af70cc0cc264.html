4. EXACT FIXED ALGEBRA IS ABELIAN; ITS SCHRODINGER PROJECTION IS EB

and Phi=S*. Let J:B(H tensor H)->B(H) be the UCP Heisenberg adjoint of

For Hermitian fixed A,C, the output-register factors commute, hence

  AC=J[(A tensor I)(I tensor C)]
    =J[(I tensor C)(A tensor I)]=CA.

The fixed space of Phi is consequently an abelian C*-algebra. This is a
self-contained specialization of the usual faithful-fixed-state argument.

10. ENTIRE CP RANGES BECOME UNIFORMLY APPROXIMATELY COMMUTATIVE

This is a stronger scoped consequence than (14) for selected likelihoods.
Work in the tracial interface (25). For ANY Hermitian contractions A,C,
put A_m=Phi^m(A), C_m=Phi^m(C), m>=1, and

  d_m=m^m/(m+1)^(m+1)<=1/(m+1).

Since Phi is a positive selfadjoint contraction on L^2(A,tau), spectral
calculus and ||A||_(2,tau)<=1 give

  ||Phi(A_m)-A_m||_(2,tau)<=d_m,
  tau[Phi(A_m^2)-Phi(A_m)^2]
      =||A_m||_2^2-||Phi(A_m)||_2^2
      <=max_(0<=lambda<=1)(1-lambda^2)lambda^(2m)=d_m.         (26)

For J's two commuting receiver embeddings x=A_m tensor I,
y=I tensor C_m, its multiplicative defect is bounded in L^2(tau)
by the square roots of these Schwarz expectations. For a Stinespring
isometry V, it is V*pi(x)(I-VV*)pi(y)V; taking the L^2(tau) norm bounds
it by ||x|| times the square root of y's Schwarz expectation, and the
adjoint estimate handles the other ordering. Thus

  ||[Phi(A_m),Phi(C_m)]||_(2,tau)<=2sqrt(d_m).

Replacing each Phi(A_m) by A_m costs at most4d_m (tracial left/right
multiplication by contractions preserves the norm). Hence

  sup_(A,C Hermitian,||A||,||C||<=1)
      ||[Phi^m(A),Phi^m(C)]||_(2,tau)
         <=2sqrt(d_m)+4d_m
         <=2/sqrt(m+1)+4/(m+1).                              (27)

Both (26) and (27) are uniform over the ENTIRE operator-norm unit ball,
all dimensions, all trace block weights, and all such Phi. No selected
label set, spectral-gap bound, or scalar reference spectrum is used.
Phi^m is itself 2^m-selfcompatible by the binary broadcaster tree.

A sufficient missing lemma can therefore be isolated in channel norm.
Let EB_tau denote CP tau-preserving measure-and-prepare maps on A, of form

  E(A)=sum_z tau(E_z A) f_z,
  E_z>=0, sum_z E_z=I, f_z>=0, tau(f_z)=1.

These are ordinary full-domain EB channels after the central-reference
identification rho=sigma_F A and composition with D_A. Define

  beta_m= sup_(A,tau,Phi as in25) inf_(E in EB_tau)
                 sup_(A=A*,||A||<=1)||E(A)-Phi^m(A)||_(1,tau).

Proving beta_m->0 would suffice. Indeed for an included theta-sandwich
state I+H with residual<=r in L^1(tau), channel contraction gives

  ||Phi^m(H)-H||_(1,tau)<=m r,
  T(E(rho),rho)<= (1/2)[(1+theta)beta_m+m r].                 (28)

Choose m->infinity slowly enough that m r->0, and apply Sections3,9.
More generally a dimension-free theorem rounding UCP maps whose ENTIRE
range has the uniform commutator bound (27) to EB in infinity->1 norm
would settle this sufficient lemma. No such theorem is imported/proved.

This range condition is not supplied by the flagged Clifford experiment's
small pair gaps/commutators: the experiment controls only a chosen family,
whereas (27) controls images of EVERY bounded input under ONE CP map.
Nevertheless it would be invalid to assume that this stronger condition
automatically permits growing-family simultaneous commuting approximation.
That exact uniform rounding implication is the load-bearing gap.

An ultraproduct argument identifies an abelian fixed/range algebra in a
limit, but pointwise finite-partition approximations of its expectation
do not by themselves provide the uniformly bounded-input approximation
needed in beta_m. Moving from finite sets to the supremum in (28) needs
an actual lifting/rounding argument. No ultraproduct compactness assertion
is being counted as a solution.
