1. PRECISE COEFFICIENT THEOREM

Let E_1,...,E_s be disjoint blocks, q_j=|E_j|>=1, m=sum q_j. Each nonnegative
table b_j(0),...,b_j(q_j) has interval positive support, or is identically zero.
Set beta_j(k)=b_j(k)/k!. Suppose every beta_j is logconcave, equivalently

  (k+1)b_j(k)^2 >= k b_j(k-1)b_j(k+1),   1<=k<q_j.             (1)

Then for EVERY t=0,...,m the homogeneous multiaffine polynomial

  g_t(x)=sum_[|T|=t] product_j b_j(q_j-|T intersect E_j|) x^T

is Lorentzian (zero allowed), hence logconcave on the positive orthant.
The statement has no Slater-matrix or spin-separation assumption.

Short proof and prior-art identity. Collapse all variables of E_j to y_j:

  G_t(y)=sum_[|h|=t] product_j [binom(q_j,h_j)b_j(q_j-h_j)] y^h
        =(product_j q_j!) sum_[|h|=t] product_j beta_j(q_j-h_j) y^h/h!.

The log of the normalized coefficient is a sum of one-dimensional concave
functions on a box intersected with |h|=t. For h_i>k_i choose j with h_j<k_j.
Moving one unit from i to j in h and back in k balances both affected pairs;
concavity increases their summed logs. Interval support preserves both moved
vectors. This is precisely M-concavity. BH Corollary 3.16 gives Lorentzian G_t,
and BH Proposition 3.1 polarization divides its coefficients by the binomial
factors and returns exactly g_t. BH Theorem 2.30 gives logconcavity.

Zeros require no regularization in this proof. Independently, the requested
regularization is valid: if beta is positive on [l,u], retain those values and
extend with beta_K(k)=beta(l)K^(k-l) for k<l and beta(u)K^(u-k) for k>u.
Take integer K larger than all positive neighboring beta ratios and their
reciprocals. These full positive tables are logconcave, and converge to beta
as K tends to infinity. Lorentzian closedness then gives the same result.
Dividing by b(N) or b(N-1) at a zero endpoint is invalid; the direct proof or
this regularization avoids that division. An identically zero local table
makes every g_t zero and gives filtered norm zero.

2. INDEPENDENT HESSIAN AND SHARPNESS CHECK

First assume all table entries positive. For t>=2 differentiate g_t in a set
R of t-2 distinct variables. Put N_j=q_j-|R intersect E_j|. Blocks N_j=0 are
omitted. Let C=product_j b_j(N_j), r_j=b_j(N_j-1)/b_j(N_j).
Within-block pair coefficient is C b_j(N_j-2)/b_j(N_j), while cross-block pair
coefficient is C r_j r_k. Congruence by the reciprocal r_j on each block and
division by C yields cross coefficient 1 and within-block coefficient

  rho_j=b_j(N_j)b_j(N_j-2)/b_j(N_j-1)^2,   N_j>=2.

The within-block contrast eigenvalues are -rho_j. On normalized block means,
the Hessian quotient is exactly

  Q=vv^T+diag(delta_j),  v_j=sqrt(N_j),
  delta_j=(N_j-1)rho_j-N_j (N_j>=2), delta_j=-1 (N_j=1).      (2)

No rho is needed for N=1. Condition (1) at k=N-1 makes delta<=0, so the
rank-one positive term can create at most one positive eigenvalue.

Necessity in the correct universal sense: if a q-mode table violates (1) at
k, take TWO identical copies and physical rank 2k (hole degree 2q-2k).
Differentiate q-k-1 holes per copy. The two surviving blocks have N=k+1;
their difference-direction quotient eigenvalue is k*rho-(k+1)>0, in addition
to a positive sum-direction eigenvalue. Thus the derivative has two positive
eigenvalues. The original multiaffine g is not logconcave: derivatives of a
multiaffine logconcave polynomial are logconcave by sending differentiated
coordinates to infinity and dividing by their product.

Interval support is also necessary for this universal two-copy/all-total
property if the starting table is not assumed to have it. If b(k)>0,
b(k+1)=0, and b(l)>0 for l>k+1, choose two supported hole sets with nested
block occupancies (k,l) and (l,k). Every basis exchange from the excess holes
in the first block to the second changes (k,l) to (k+1,l-1), of zero weight.
The support is not M-convex, excluding Lorentzian/logconcave g.

These are source-hypothesis failures, not hardness or preparation lower bounds.

3. THE TWO NECESSITY QUALIFICATIONS

A single block gives a scalar multiple of an elementary symmetric polynomial
at every degree, for arbitrary b. More strongly, two heterogeneous q=2 blocks
b1=(1,1,4), b2=(1,1,1) have every g_t Lorentzian despite violation in b1.
Only g2 is nontrivial; its normalized quotient [[4,2],[2,1]] has eigenvalues
5,0 and negative contrast eigenvalues. Compensation invalidates any claim
that local (1) is necessary for a fixed collection/all totals.

Complement orientation matters. The DIRECT homogeneous polynomial

  h_r(x)=sum_[|S|=r] product_j b_j(|S intersect E_j|) x^S

has the mirror universal sufficient/sharp local condition
  b_j(k)/(q_j-k)! logconcave,
  b_j(k)^2 >= (q_j-k)/(q_j-k+1) b_j(k-1)b_j(k+1).
This follows by reversing each table. Two q=3 blocks with b(k)=k! pass the
complementary condition, but direct h2 has Hessian eigenvalues
7,1,-2,-2,-2,-2. Substituting h_r for g_(m-r) without this check is false.

4. EXACT POLYNOMIAL RECOGNITION FOR POSITIVE FIXED-RANK TABLES

Interface: explicitly supplied strictly positive rational tables, q_j>=1,
physical particle rank 0<=r<=m. The zero-table extension of this recognizer is
NOT claimed. Zero-mode blocks can be removed as constants. If d=m-r<=1,
g_d is trivially Lorentzian. Otherwise every quadratic derivative corresponds
exactly to residual counts
  0<=N_j<=q_j, sum_j N_j=r+2.                                (3)
All such count patterns are realizable by some derivative set R.

For an active residual pattern, let p be the number of positive delta entries
in (2), and z indicate an active zero entry (N=0 does not count as zero).
The EXACT inertia failure rule for Q is:
  p>=2: fail;
  p=1 and z=true: fail;
  p=1, z=false: fail exactly when 1+sum_j N_j/delta_j<0;
  p=0: pass.

Proof: two positive diagonal entries survive adding vv^T. With one positive
and a zero, the corresponding 2x2 principal determinant is delta_p*N_z>0
with positive trace. With one positive and no zero, D=diag(delta) is
invertible. The bordered matrix [[D,v],[v^T,-1]] is congruent both to
diag(Q,-1) and to diag(D,-1-v^TD^-1v), so
  positive(Q)=1+1_[1+sum N_j/delta_j<0].
With D<=0, a rank-one positive update has at most one positive eigenvalue.
Equality in the scalar condition passes and may create a zero eigenvalue.

Dynamic program: each block has options N=0,...,q. Store the minimum additive
cost sum N/delta at each state (total N, positive-count capped at2, zero-flag).
An omitted N=0 option has cost0; active delta=0 has cost0 because cost is
irrelevant in zero-flag rejection states. N=1 has delta=-1 and cost=-1.
Reject final total r+2 if either first two failure types is present or the
minimum cost in state (r+2,1,false) is less than -1. Otherwise every Hessian
passes, and uniform full support plus BH Theorem 2.25 proves Lorentzian g_d.
Store predecessors to return a rejected residual pattern and hence R.

There are O(m) states per layer, and sum_j(q_j+1)<=2m. Thus O(m^2) rational
operations suffice. Input-rational ratios and at most s-term rational sums
have polynomial bit length. Predecessors use O(sm) memory; Boolean admission
alone needs two O(m)-state layers.

5. PREFIX AND FERMION CONSEQUENCE

For physical forced inclusions F and exclusions E, remaining local size is
q'_j=q_j-|F_j|-|E_j| and b'_j(k)=b_j(k+|F_j|). For the local sufficient class,
the original ratio factor (k+|F_j|)/(k+|F_j|+1) is at least k/(k+1), so the
shifted/truncated table still satisfies (1), including interval zeros.

For the larger fixed-rank recognized class, prefix source is obtained by
differentiating g_d on forced-empty modes and specializing forced-occupied
variables to0. Lorentzian derivative/specialization closure handles EVERY
prefix; no further DP recognition or exponential union check is needed.
Direct h_r has the mirrored operation. This is stronger than proving only
initial norm logconcavity.

With explicit full-rank A in Q(i)^(m x r),
  f_A=det(A*diag(x)A)=sum_|S|r |det A_S|^2 x^S
is homogeneous multiaffine real stable, hence Lorentzian/logconcave. The norm
  Z=sum_|S|r |det A_S|^2 product_j b_j(|S_j|)
is [x_all]f_A*g_(m-r). If the complementary DP accepts, or local (1) accepts,
Chen–Liu Theorem5.1 supplies its FPRAS with acquired minor/product coefficient
oracles, positive support witnesses, and polynomial-bit coefficient ranges.

The direct orientation is also legal: f_A^*=x_all*f_A(1/x) remains real stable
and has coefficient |det A_(U\T)|^2 at T. Then Z=[x_all]f_A^*h_r. This is
algebraic full complementation; it requires no spin-separation or physical
partial particle–hole transformation. The union of DP-accepted orientations
therefore feeds the same count-to-amplitude compiler.

For interval-zero tables, physical supported r-sets are a matroid base family.
An independence oracle checks |I_j|<=u_j and sum_j max(l_j,|I_j|)<=r. Exact
matroid intersection with the represented row matroid of A decides Z=0 and
acquires witnesses, also at prefixes. With positive tables only, all local
occupancies are supported and the feasibility acquisition is simpler.
Squared nonzero rational minors have polynomial-bit lower bounds after
denominator clearing; products of supplied table entries have polynomial-bit
range bounds. Thus B=m+log(R_fR_g) is polynomial in input length.

The imported per-count cost is
  O(m^10 B^2 eta^-2 log^2(2B/eta)) arithmetic/oracle operations,
on every execution, plus log(1/beta) amplification and poly-bit arithmetic.
The DP's O(m^2) rational acquisition is additional. The existing N63 shared
depth-tape compiler uses eta=O(eps/m), beta=O(delta eps^2/m^2) and available
determinant/local phase routines. It yields a randomized polynomial-size
coherent circuit with the stated seed success/trace error, without inverse Z.
This consequence remains conditional on that imported FPRAS/compiler; they
were not run here. b alone specifies Born weights; desired local phases must
also be explicitly efficiently computable. Matrix/weight acquisition and
variational optimization are outside this interface.

In particular b=(1,1,g^2) on each two-mode site satisfies (1) for g^2<=2.
This includes weak attraction 1<g<=sqrt2 and arbitrary complex Slater spin
mixing. Two identical sites at physical rank2 give quotient eigenvalue
g^2-2, making this source threshold universally sharp. It is not a barrier to
other representations/algorithms, nor a claim about arbitrary BCS states or
interacting Gibbs sampling.
