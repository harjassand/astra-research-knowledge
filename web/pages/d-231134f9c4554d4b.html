ACQUIRED NET: OPERATIONAL BROADCAST-TO-EB TRANSFER
8 October 2026

STATUS

For a genuinely supplied finite trace net and a full-domain broadcaster,
there is an explicit common-EB reconstruction bound. It depends on the
measured broadcaster error on the net and a finite-label information term.
The strongest bound from the audited adaptive-extraction argument is

  e_EB(E) <= min{1, 2 eps + inf_(K>=1) [ phi(K) beta_R
                                  + sqrt(Gamma(R)/(2K)) ]}.      (1)

Here R is the rational net, beta_R is computed by applying the supplied
channel to its net states, and Gamma(R) is defined below. If only a
capacity certificate is available, Gamma(R)<=N C(R); without one,
Gamma(R)<=N ln N. A derived information-radius center gives a different
constant-preparation bound, eps+sqrt(C(R)/2), but neither the center nor
bounded capacity removes N from (1). Bounded capacity does not control the
covering size: a balanced-sign commuting family has constant capacity,
exact broadcasting, exponential fixed-radius packing, and exact EB error
zero. This is a complexity obstruction, not a counterexample to
broadcasting-implies-EB.

1. LEGAL INPUT AND PRECISION

Let H=C^d and E be any state family, finite or infinite. A supplied list
R={r_1,...,r_N} consists of valid rational density matrices, with a
certified trace-distance cover

  sup_(rho in E) min_i T(rho,r_i) <= eps,  T=||.||_1/2.          (2)

The list need not be contained in E. If the exact cover centers are only
represented by rational surrogates within trace distance eta, replace eps
everywhere by epsbar=eps+eta, provided the cover certificate refers to
those exact centers. If the supplied certificate already bounds distance
to the rational matrices themselves, epsbar=eps. Rational bit precision
is an input-size charge; it does not by itself certify (2).

The supplied B:B(H)->B(H tensor H) is one full-domain CPTP channel. It can
have arbitrary correlated outputs. No independence, covariance, reference,
rank, or label promise is used. Define the directly measurable net error

  beta_R=max_(i=1,...,N; j=1,2) T(B_j(r_i),r_i).                (3)

Using beta_R avoids assuming a separate bound on B over E. If a global
bound b_E is supplied and R is an exact subset of E, beta_R<=b_E. For
surrogate centers near E, beta_R<=b_E+2 eta, but (1) uses the computed
beta_R and needs no b_E certificate.

2. EXACT COMMON-CHANNEL BOUND

For a finite family R with N distinct states, and a prior p on R, write

  chi_p=S(sum_i p_i r_i)-sum_i p_i S(r_i),
  N_(1/2)(p)=(sum_i sqrt(p_i))^2,
  Gamma(R)=max_p N_(1/2)(p) chi_p.

Set phi(1)=0 and, if 2^s<=K<=2^(s+1),

  phi(K)=s+2-2^(s+1)/K.

For every integer K>=1, finite-label extraction and minimax give one
unknown-label-independent full-domain EB channel Lambda_R satisfying

  max_i T(Lambda_R(r_i),r_i)
       <= phi(K) beta_R + sqrt(Gamma(R)/(2K)).                 (4)

This is a common decoder: prior-specific decoders are only the minimax
proof device. The finite family is assumed distinct; exact duplicates are
merged. Since N_(1/2)(p)<=N and chi_p<=C(R)<=ln N,

  Gamma(R)<=N C(R)<=N ln N.                               (5)

If no optimized prior quantity is computed, substitute N ln N in (4).
If a certified capacity upper bound Cbar_R is available, substitute
N Cbar_R. These replacements retain N.
If all net states are members of E, C(R)<=C(E). If each rational
surrogate is within eta of a member of E but is not itself promised to be
in E, the direct substitute is the dimension-dependent continuity bound
C(R)<=C(E)+2 f_d(eta), where f_d(t)=h_2(t)+t ln(d-1) in its usual
Fannes-Audenaert range. No dimension-free capacity transfer to surrogate
centers is asserted.

Proof sketch with the charged error. Recursively apply B on a binary tree
with K leaves, using K-1 calls. A leaf at depth t has marginal error at
most t beta_R: for any state omega and promised net state r_i,

  T(B_j(omega),r_i)<=T(omega,r_i)+T(B_j(r_i),r_i).

Uniformly permute leaves. Every output marginal is then the average leaf
marginal, and a full K-leaf tree of minimum mean depth has mean phi(K).
Thus each output is within phi(K) beta_R of every r_i.

For any prior p, sequentially measure receivers adaptively. At history h,
let p_i(h) be the posterior, sigma_i^h the conditional state of the
current receiver, tau_h=sum_i p_i(h)sigma_i^h, and
S_h=sum_i p_i(h)T(sigma_i^h,tau_h). Choose i_h maximizing
p_i(h)T(sigma_i^h,tau_h)^2 and apply the binary Helstrom test against
tau_h. Classical Pinsker in nats gives conditional gain
g_h>=2 p_(i_h)(h)T(sigma_(i_h)^h,tau_h)^2>=2S_h^2/N_(1/2)(p(h)).
Bayes' rule gives E_h N_(1/2)(p(h))<=N_(1/2)(p). Over K receivers,
the classical gains sum to at most chi_p by Holevo data processing; hence
some receiver j has expected posterior trace spread at most
sqrt(N_(1/2)(p)chi_p/(2K)). Measuring only its prefix and preparing tau_h
is an EB channel. Its prior-average error adds at most phi(K) beta_R.
Finite-dimensional minimax over the compact convex EB channel set and
prior simplex produces one common channel and yields (4). The exact
derivation is recorded in
work/programmes/classicality/leaves/followup_finite_label_bound.txt.

Exact-zero special case. If rational arithmetic certifies beta_R=0, the
finite set R is exactly broadcastable and hence its states commute (the
finite-dimensional no-broadcasting criterion). Simultaneous diagonalization
then gives an exact measure-and-prepare channel fixing every r_i, with at
most d basis outcomes. Its error on E is at most 2 epsbar by (2). This
avoids the K->infinity limit in (4). The criterion is not inferred from
small nonzero beta_R.

For any rho in E, choose r_i with T(rho,r_i)<=epsbar. Trace-distance
contractivity of Lambda_R gives

  T(Lambda_R(rho),rho)
    <=T(Lambda_R(rho),Lambda_R(r_i))
      +T(Lambda_R(r_i),r_i)+T(r_i,rho)
    <=2 epsbar+T(Lambda_R(r_i),r_i).

This proves (1) with epsbar in place of eps. No state outside R needs to
be sampled or measured to evaluate beta_R. If one has both a net-error
certificate beta_R and a separate global channel certificate b_E, use
the smaller valid input for the tree term (beta_R for a net decoder; b_E
also works on R only when its promise covers the relevant centers).

3. INFORMATION-RADIUS CENTER: WHAT IT BUYS

The finite net has an information-radius center omega_R with
max_i D(r_i||omega_R)=C(R) (or an eta-optimal center). A constant EB
channel preparing omega_R therefore obeys

  max_(rho in E) T(omega_R,rho)
      <= epsbar+sqrt(C(R)/2).                                (6)

Consequently combine (1) and (6) by taking their minimum. Pinsker uses
natural logarithms. At fixed positive C(R), (6) does not vanish with the
broadcast error. Finding omega_R is not free: its matrix must be
represented and a relative-entropy-radius optimization over N states
must be solved to certified precision. A trace-accurate center alone
does not certify the radius when its smallest eigenvalues are unresolved.
The center adds no covering information.

4. N46 GATE AND THE COVERING-COMPLEXITY COST

If only the universal finite-label capacity C(R)<=ln N is used, choose K
so sqrt(N ln N/(2K))<=delta, for example

  K=max{1,ceil(N ln N/(2 delta^2))}.

Then (1) gives

  e_EB(E)<=2 epsbar+phi(K)beta_R+delta,
  phi(K)<=log_2 K+1.                                        (7)

When C(R)<=Cbar, the sufficient choice
K=max{1,ceil(N Cbar/(2 delta^2))}
replaces ln N by Cbar inside K, but phi(K) beta_R still has leading
order beta_R log_2 N. Taking delta comparable to beta_R makes the total
error scale

  2 epsbar + O(beta_R log N + beta_R log(1/beta_R))

for bounded Cbar. Thus eps_n->0 and beta_(R_n) ln N_n->0 are sufficient.
Without capacity, the N ln N version has the same gate, plus a lower-order
beta ln ln N contribution. These are sufficient conditions, not
necessary conditions on each family.

N46's source-reported finite-cover estimate is the dyadic version

  e_EB(E)<=2 epsbar+inf_(L>=0)
       [L beta_R+sqrt(N ln N/(2*2^L))].

It yields the same b log N gate. Equation (1) is sharper in two exact
ways already supported by the finite-label proof: arbitrary K uses the
minimum mean tree depth phi(K), and Gamma(R) replaces N ln N. N46's
uniform fixed-scale covering theorem also gives a qualitative conclusion
without an explicit mixture modulus; (1) is the operational finite-net
bound with explicit receiver, outcome, and information costs. The direct
bound bypasses the pair fidelity/affinity step; small pair gap alone does
not specify a decoder.

5. FULL ACQUISITION AND ALGORITHM COST

Input acquisition and validation:

* Store N rational d-by-d Hermitian matrices. At p bits per real entry,
  this takes O(N d^2 p) bits, plus the epsilon/eta and cover certificate.
  Verify positivity and trace one by exact rational arithmetic or a
  certified eigensolver. A list alone is not a net certificate: for
  infinite/opaque E, verifying (2) requires a supplied analytic cover
  proof or a separation/membership oracle. For explicit finite E it can
  be checked by trace-distance calculations.
* Store B, for example as r_B Kraus matrices of size d^2-by-d
  (O(r_B d^3) entries) or a d^3-by-d^3 Choi matrix (O(d^6) entries),
  and certify trace preservation and complete positivity on the full
  input algebra. Evaluate both marginal outputs on all N net states.
  Dense Kraus evaluation is O(N r_B d^5) arithmetic operations before
  trace-norm eigensolves; the N marginal trace norms cost O(N d^3).
  Certified trace-norm upper bounds add a chosen numerical slack nu_B to
  beta_R.
  If B is supplied as a Choi matrix, the matrix is d^3-by-d^3; a dense
  positivity check costs O(d^9) arithmetic by eigensolve/LDL, and the
  partial-trace TP check costs O(d^6). A Kraus list has CP built in and
  needs only the completeness check.

Decoder compilation for a chosen K:

* Build the balanced K-leaf tree and its uniform leaf permutation. It
  uses K-1 B calls per use of the decoder and K d-dimensional output
  registers. Exact permutation symmetrization is a mixture over K!
  permutations (or a random seed with entropy log_2(K!) bits).
* For each prior, at most 2^K-1 history-conditioned Helstrom tests are
  needed; each is an eigensolve of a d-by-d Hermitian matrix, O(d^3)
  dense arithmetic. A decoder measuring a prefix has at most
  2^(K-1) classical outcomes. Directly propagating joint receiver states
  uses d^K-dimensional matrices, d^(2K) storage, and exponential
  arithmetic in K in the dense representation. A circuit description
  avoids storing the whole channel but still requires K-1 invocations of
  B and may have exponentially many adaptive branches.
* Exact minimax proves existence but is not by itself an algorithm. One
  explicit approximation uses an l1 xi-net of the prior simplex with
  denominator M>=N/xi. It has
  J=binom(M+N-1,N-1) priors. Build one Helstrom decoder per grid prior,
  compute their N-component worst-member loss vectors, and solve the
  finite zero-sum LP to mix them. The simplex grid gives at most xi extra
  loss; Caratheodory reduces the final mixture to at most N+1 decoders.
  For each grid prior, the constructed decoder has average loss at most
  q_K from (4); finite minimax over the grid therefore gives a mixture
  with loss at most q_K on every grid prior. Any prior is within xi in
  l1-distance of a grid point, and each label loss lies in [0,1], so the
  worst-member loss is at most q_K+xi (plus LP/numerical slack).
  Thus the compiled EB channel has at most (N+1)2^(K-1) outcomes and
  preparation states/measurement effects on H. The offline work is at
  least proportional to J decoder constructions and a polynomial-size
  LP in J,N and the chosen arithmetic precision. J is exponential in N;
  no polynomial-time or dimension-free acquisition claim follows.
  The exact Gamma(R) is a mathematical refinement; unless a certified
  upper bound is supplied, use N ln N. Optimizing Gamma over priors is an
  additional global optimization task and is not included in the stated
  base algorithm cost.
* Finite-precision decoder synthesis adds explicit chosen tolerances:
  if the LP slack is xi+nu_LP and the implemented EB circuit differs in
  output trace distance by at most nu_impl on every net input, the bound
  is (1) plus xi+nu_LP+nu_impl. Numerical error in beta is charged as
  phi(K)nu_B. State-sampling error is avoided because the supplied net is
  explicit; any unknown-family cover verification cost is charged above.

The cost is not N*K-1 physical calls per input: the final mixed decoder
selects one branch and invokes the K-output circuit once, using K-1 calls
to B. Offline compilation repeats the branch analysis across the J prior
grid. State preparation and implementation of algebraic Helstrom
projectors are charged to the stated numerical precision; condition
numbers and input bit length can make them expensive.

6. DECISIVE CONTROL: CAPACITY DOES NOT BUY THE COVER

Let m be even and take all balanced sign vectors
s in {+1,-1}^m with sum s_i=0. On C^m define commuting rational states
(take rational 0<a<1)

  rho_s=diag((1+a s_1)/m,...,(1+a s_m)/m),
  sigma=I/m.

Every state has D(rho_s||sigma)=c(a), where
c(a)=((1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a))/2. The uniform prior has mean sigma,
and Donald's identity gives C(E_m)=c(a)<=ln 2. The center is exact and
also satisfies rho_s<=(1+a)sigma. Reading the diagonal and preparing
the same basis label defines a full-domain CPTP broadcaster with both
marginals rho_s exactly; dephasing is an EB channel with zero error.

Nevertheless, a binary-code subset of balanced signs has exp(c_0 m)
members with pairwise trace distance greater than a/4. Therefore every
net of radius below a/8 has N>=exp(c_0 m). For the uniform prior on all
M=binom(m,m/2) states, chi=c(a) and N_(1/2)=M, so
Gamma(E_m)>=M c(a) grows exponentially. This is a legal bounded-capacity,
exact-broadcasting control: neither C nor its exact information center
bounds the cover size or Gamma. It does not refute EB reconstruction—the
family is already exactly classical. It shows why replacing N in a
general complexity estimate by a function of C alone is invalid, without
making any rank inference from capacity.

N61 is the complementary obstruction to deleting the complexity gate:
its copy ladder has b_2->0 and e_EB->1 with b_2 ln N(a) bounded below,
but its Holevo capacity diverges. It calibrates (7), not the bounded-C
target. N93's bounded-capacity Pauli/Clifford controls have positive
b_2 and likewise do not refute that target. The broad implication remains
UNKNOWN; this artifact proves only the explicit finite-net transfer and
the capacity-versus-cover boundary.
