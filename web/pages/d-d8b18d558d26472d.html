proper-score potential F by F'(p)=clipped h_i^-1((2p-1)/r_i), with endpoint
extension -1,+1. The derivative is bounded [-1,1] and Lipschitz <=2/(c r_i).
The Savage losses ell(s,p)=F(s)-F(p)-(s-p)F'(p), s=0,1, lie in [0,2]. Convex

where y is clipped to [-1,1]. Average binary TV <=epsilon thus forces
