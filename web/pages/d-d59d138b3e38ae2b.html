Scope and verdict
-----------------
I audited only §§4-8: the normalized Husimi comparison, finite-statistic
binning and decoder, trace/TV comparisons, conditional-sampling charge,
Caratheodory refinement, and the finite-precision/resource boundary. I did
not re-audit §§2-3 or the variance theorem. Conditional on their stated
pointwise bounds and on the supplied irreducible coherent POVM identity,
I found no mathematical flaw in §§4-8. The result is an ideal finite-outcome
existence theorem with a common parameter-independent decoder; it is not an
efficient sampler/compiler or a total-machine O(log n) memory theorem.

§4: normalized classical comparison
-----------------------------------
With q(x)=Tr(P_x exp(P_theta))/z_theta and
p(x)=exp(f_theta(x))/Z_theta, r(x)=Tr(P_x exp(P_theta))/exp(f_theta(x)),
direct normalization gives

  E_p r = Tr(exp(P_theta))/(d Z_theta) = z_theta/Z_theta,
  q/p = r/(E_p r).

Thus the normalization on both sides is essential. If |r-1|<=u=kappa/N
and u<=1/2, then E_p r>=1-u and
|q/p-1|<=2u/(1-u)<=4u. Therefore

  D(q||p)<=chi^2(q||p)<=16 kappa^2/N^2.

The first inequality follows from log t<=t-1; the second follows by
squaring the density-ratio deviation and integrating under p. Product
additivity and Pinsker in the convention TV=(1/2)||.||_1 give
TV(q^n,p^n)<=sqrt(8) kappa sqrt(n)/N. Constants in (11)-(12) check.

§5: labels, exact effects, and the two latent-law charges
---------------------------------------------------------
There are M=binom(g+D,D)-1 nonconstant monomials. On each coordinate
S_alpha in [-n,n], width h=epsilon/A_eff needs at most
ceil(2n A_eff/epsilon) intervals; the displayed +2 bound safely covers
endpoints/degenerate partitions. Since sum_alpha |gamma_alpha|<=A_eff,
the log likelihood gamma.S varies by at most A_eff h=epsilon within a bin.

For C_s of base mass b_s>0, the effects
E_s=d^n integral_Cs P_x^product dmu^n are positive and sum to I because
integral P_x dmu=I/d. The decoder states
tau_s=b_s^-1 integral_Cs P_x^product dmu^n are normalized states, and
E_s=d^n b_s tau_s. Both are independent of theta. Zero-mass cells have
zero effect and may be assigned any state. The resulting POVM followed by
preparation of tau_s is exactly a legal finite-alphabet EB channel.

The tiny-bin estimate is uniform in b_s: under nu=mu^n(.|C_s), the
conditional p^n density is w/E_nu w with w=exp(gamma.S). The range ratio
of w is at most e^epsilon, so the normalized density lies in
[e^-epsilon,e^epsilon]. Consequently its TV from nu is at most
(e^epsilon-1)/2<=epsilon for 0<epsilon<=1. No division by a small bin
mass appears.

All three terms in (15) are needed and their stated bounds are valid:

  (1/2)||sigma_q-sigma_p||_1 <= TV(q^n,p^n),
  (1/2)||eta_Q-eta_P||_1 <= TV(Q,P) <= TV(q^n,p^n),
  (1/2)||sigma_p-eta_P||_1 <= epsilon.

The first is continuous preparation of coherent product states. The
second is the distinct label-law term: the map x->s is a classical
postprocessing, and then s->tau_s is another stochastic-to-quantum map.
The third compares, bin by bin, p^n conditional states against the base
conditional mixtures defining tau_s. Omitting the second q^n-to-p^n
charge would leave the actual decoder weights Q_s uncontrolled.

Combining these with (16), the coefficient of sqrt(n)/N is
e^h0 K_infinity/sqrt(2) + 2 sqrt(8) kappa
= e^h0 K_infinity/sqrt(2) + 4 sqrt(2) kappa, as in (2)-(3).
The small-N fallback is also sound: if N<2kappa, the second term alone
in C_arch sqrt(n)/N exceeds 2sqrt(2) for n>=1, hence the trivial
half-trace bound by 1 suffices.

The number of retained labels is at most
(ceil(2n A_eff/epsilon)+2)^M and contains no separate log N factor.
This relies on the common parameter and the shared public finite-degree
family; it says nothing about independently varying copy parameters.

§7: conditional decoder sampling and its costs
-----------------------------------------------
Rejection sampling from the known base mu^n, accepting exactly C_s,
produces mu^n(.|C_s), hence tau_s after preparing P_x^product. Its mean
candidate count is 1/b_s. Since ||P_theta||<=h0, the normalized Husimi
density obeys q_theta<=e^(2h0), so Q_theta(s)<=e^(2h0 n)b_s. Averaging
over the encoder label gives

  sum_{b_s>0} Q_theta(s)/b_s <= B_arch e^(2h0 n).

This is finite and uniform but exponential in n; no efficient or
polynomial worst-case sampler is implied. Haar/orbit sampling, coherent
preparation, exact membership/precision, arithmetic, scratch statistics,
and output preparation are charged as separate resources. It is valid to
defer coherent preparation until acceptance; the stated streaming cost is
a conservative implementation option. The decoder uses only s and public
data, not theta.

§8: finite refinement and precision boundary
--------------------------------------------
For b_s>0, tau_s lies in the closed convex hull of the compact image of
closure(C_s) under x->P_x^product. The trace-one Hermitian affine space
has real dimension d^(2n)-1, so Caratheodory gives an exact ensemble with
at most d^(2n) atoms; the proof's looser d^(2n)+1 bound is valid. Scaling
that same ensemble by d^n b_s refines E_s into positive rank-one
product-coherent effects. Summing the exact E_s identities gives exact
completeness, with at most B_arch(d^(2n)+1) fine effects and B_arch
grouped retained outcomes. Nodes on closure boundaries need not be
reclassified by the half-open bin rule: they are effects in a positive
decomposition of the already defined E_s, not a claim that quadrature
nodes implement the original pointwise classifier.

The finite refinement is existential, not an effective finite-bit
construction. Exact nodes, weights, matrix data, conditional sampling,
and apparatus description may be expensive or lack short encodings; the
proof correctly excludes them from the archive payload. Approximate
encoder outcome laws and decoder states contribute at most eta_E+eta_D
in half trace under the stated uniform guarantees. Coordinate precision
O_G(eta_D/(nN)) for preparing an n-output product to total eta_D is
consistent with generator norm O_G(N); normalized statistic precision
O(epsilon/n) is consistent at fixed G,D. Bin-boundary and conditional
mass errors need separate certificates, which the proof explicitly leaves
to an implementation.
