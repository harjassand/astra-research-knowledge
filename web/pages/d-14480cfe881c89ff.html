1. ORIENTATION ERRATUM

In PURE_CANONICAL_ENTROPY_OBSTRUCTION.txt, the sentence saying that Psi_t
"fixes sigma" refers to its Schrodinger map. The notation there defines
Psi_t as Heisenberg. Read the statement as

  Psi_t^*(sigma)=sigma.

Its Heisenberg map instead satisfies Psi_t(I)=I. All displayed formulas,
the KMS identity, P1, P2 and the counterexamples use the correct convention.
The exact replay verifies Schrodinger stationarity explicitly. This is a
literal prose erratum, not an inequality or channel construction repair.

2. PURE REFINEMENT IN THE NONTRACIAL KMS C4 PROOF

The tree POVM in NONTRACIAL_KMS_C4.txt can have mixed root effects, even
though the leaf POVM is rank one. Before its compactness passage, refine
the root POVM as follows. For any effect M_z choose a finite rank-one
decomposition M_z=sum_j M_(z,j). Let

  p_z=Tr(sigma M_z), p_(z,j)=Tr(sigma M_(z,j)),
  pi_z=S M_z S/p_z,
  pi_(z,j)=S M_(z,j) S/p_(z,j).

Discard zero effects. Faithfulness gives positive weights for all remaining
effects, each fine pi is pure, and the coarse posterior is exactly its
conditional mean:

  pi_z=sum_j [p_(z,j)/p_z] pi_(z,j).

If a_z denotes the full centered KMS coordinate vector of pi_z, then the
fine coordinates obey

  E[a_fine | z]=a_z,
  S_fine-S_coarse=E[Cov(a_fine | z)]>=0.

Assign the SAME coarse estimator x_z to every suboutcome (z,j). Its mean,
second moment N_m and full crossmoment E[a_fine x_z^T] are unchanged.
Consequently the exact full block moment/Schur lower bound survives the
refinement and the C4 order can only improve. The refined effects still
sum to I, and their pure posterior ensemble has mean sigma.

Now the compactness argument in the frozen proof applies literally to
the probability measures on pure posteriors. It yields a finite pure
sigma-canonical comparator by Caratheodory, as claimed. This is the same
rank-one refinement mechanism as the supplied tracial C4 proof; there is
no additional no-cloning premise or efficient extraction assumption.
