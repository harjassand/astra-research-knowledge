UNIVERSAL CLONER EXCLUDES EVERY SEPARABLE-OUTPUT BROADCASTER
Independent extension by c05_s02, 7 October 2026. INITIAL unchanged.
Status: complete author derivation; independent verification/priority open.
This is a broader EXACT representation obstruction, not a dimension-uniform
EB power-range counterexample or a Dirichlet-majorization refutation.

CLAIM
For d>=2, let Phi_d be the universal-cloner marginal from INITIAL,
 Phi_d(rho)=[(d+2)rho+(Tr rho)I]/[2(d+1)].
Every CPTP broadcaster B with BOTH marginals exactly Phi_d is the standard
universal cloner W_d(rho)=2/(d+1)P+(rho tensor I)P+.
For every pure input its outputs are entangled. Consequently Phi_d has no
broadcaster that outputs a separable two-receiver state for every input.
In particular it cannot be produced by ANY symmetric instrument-append
broadcaster, even with arbitrary prepared states and multi-Kraus outcome
instruments, followed by arbitrary local recovery channels.

FULL ELEMENTARY PROOF OF BROADCASTER UNIQUENESS
Use normalized Choi state J=(id_R tensor B)(|Omega><Omega|),
|Omega>=d^(-1/2)sum_i |i,i>. Its input-reference marginal is I_R/d.
Let
 P_1=|Omega><Omega|_(R,1) tensor I_2,
 P_2=|Omega><Omega|_(R,2) tensor I_1.
Each marginal channel has entanglement fidelity

 F_e= lambda_d+(1-lambda_d)/d^2=(d+1)/(2d).

Therefore Tr[(P_1+P_2)J]=2F_e=1+1/d.
The range of P_1 has orthonormal basis u_k=|Omega>_(R,1)|k>_2;
the range of P_2 has orthonormal basis v_k=|Omega>_(R,2)|k>_1.
Directly <u_k,v_l>=delta_(k,l)/d. Thus the normalized vectors

 w_k^+=(u_k+v_k)/sqrt(2(1+1/d)),
 w_k^-=(u_k-v_k)/sqrt(2(1-1/d))

are orthonormal across both signs. P_1+P_2 has eigenvalues 1+1/d on the
span of w_k^+, 1-1/d on the span of w_k^-, and 0 on its orthogonal
complement. Since J>=0 with trace 1 attains the largest eigenvalue,
its support lies entirely in the top d-dimensional space. Write

 J=sum_(k,l) theta_(k,l) |w_k^+><w_l^+|,
 theta>=0, Tr theta=1.

Partial trace of the displayed vectors is an exact calculation:

 Tr_(1,2)[|w_k^+><w_l^+|]
   =[delta_(k,l) I_R+|l><k|_R]/(d+1).

The two diagonal terms each contribute delta_(k,l) I/d; the two cross
terms each contribute |l><k|/d; divide their sum by 2(1+1/d).
It follows that J_R=(I+theta^T)/(d+1). Trace preservation requires
J_R=I/d, so theta=I/d. This fixes J uniquely. The explicit W_d is CPTP
and has the required two marginals by INITIAL, so it is this unique map.
No assumption of covariance, optimal cloning import or numerical SDP
uniqueness is needed for the reconstruction.

ENTANGLED OUTPUT OF THE UNIQUE BROADCASTER
For input |0><0| set |s_(0,j)>=(|0,j>+|j,0>)/sqrt(2), j!=0. Then

 W_d(|0><0|)=2/(d+1)|0,0><0,0|
             +1/(d+1)sum_(j!=0)|s_(0,j)><s_(0,j)|.

Partially transpose receiver 2. The principal 2x2 submatrix on the span
of |0,0>,|1,1> is

 [ [2/(d+1), 1/(2(d+1))],
   [1/(2(d+1)), 0] ].

Its determinant is -1/[4(d+1)^2]<0. A separable state has positive partial
transpose, because each positive product term stays positive under a
partial transpose. Therefore this output is entangled. Unitary covariance
transfers the conclusion to every pure input.

WHY THIS EXCLUDES GENERAL INSTRUMENT APPENDING
Let I_y be arbitrary CP outcome instruments with sum_y I_y TP, and let
omega_y be arbitrary positive trace-one prepared states. The symmetrized
append broadcaster

 B_app(rho)=(1/2)sum_y [I_y(rho) tensor omega_y
                        +omega_y tensor I_y(rho)]

outputs a separable state for every density rho. This is a positive convex
combination of product states, after normalizing nonzero I_y(rho). Applying
any local channel R_1 tensor R_2 retains separability. Convex mixtures and
local unitary conjugations retain it as well. Such a map cannot have both
marginals Phi_d, by uniqueness and the negative principal minor above.

This argument DOES NOT assume the canonical prepared state M_y/Tr M_y,
bistochastic Kraus normalization, L2 positivity, or a single Kraus operator
per outcome. The counterexample marginal itself satisfies all those
relevant target properties: CP, UCP, faithful-reference fixation, positive
selfadjoint L2 action, and actual selfcompatibility, as proved in INITIAL.

IMPORTANT CLOSURE BOUNDARY
Tensor products of channels that individually have separable outputs need
NOT have separable outputs on arbitrary entangled inputs across factors.
For example, routing the two halves of an entangled input to different
receivers can create receiver entanglement. The broader exact separable-
output obstruction is therefore NOT asserted for an arbitrary tensor
closure of instrument-append maps. The separate corrected-canonical
supertrace obstruction in INITIAL DOES handle that tensor closure.

WHY THIS STILL DOES NOT REFUTE EB ROUNDING
The raw half-route channel has B_half(rho)=(rho tensor I/d+I/d tensor rho)/2,
which is a separable-output broadcaster and has marginal with depolarizing
parameter 1/2. Its parameter differs from lambda_d by 1/[2(d+1)] only.
Thus this exact broader instrument exclusion can disappear in normalized
map distance as d grows. The constant corrected-canonical norm separation
from INITIAL should not be silently transferred to this broader class.
Indeed Phi_d itself has the sharp c=2 Dirichlet EB comparator with parameter
1/(d+1), and its powers approach EB uniformly by INITIAL equation (8).
