## 1. Exact input and local dynamics

Take the rational squared-contrast matrix

    Q = [[1, 0, 1, 1],
         [0, 1, 1, 1],
         [1, 1, 23/10, 11/5],
         [1, 1, 11/5, 23/10]].

Its leading minors are 1,1,3/10,1/20, so Q>0. Equivalently its symmetric and antisymmetric pair blocks are [[1,2],[2,9/2]] and diag(1,1/10), both positive definite. Let f=(y^2)^TQ(y^2), H=Hess f, h=det H and K=h/f^2 on H>0.

At p=(1,1,0,0), f=2, H=diag(12,12,8,8)>0 and grad log K=0. Direct differentiation of log det H gives

    Hess log K(p) = [[-6,6,0,0],[6,-6,0,0],
                    [0,0,-7/30,0],[0,0,0,-7/30]].

On the tangent basis (1,-1,0,0), e_3,e_4 of f=const, the induced Hessian metric is diag(24,8,8) and the curvature Hessian is diag(-24,-7/30,-7/30). Thus the projective V=F/h^2 flow has three strictly negative rates

    -1, -7/240, -7/240.

The ray p is an isolated strict attracting curvature maximum with an open projective basin. Multiplication by h^2>0 preserves attraction for F. The maximum has support2.

## 2. A complete global calculation

Use squared-variable sums and differences

    S=m_1+m_2, A=m_1-m_2,
    T=m_3+m_4, B=m_3-m_4,   m_i=y_i^2.

For real y, S,T>=0, |A|<=S and |B|<=T. Direct expansion gives

    f = A^2/2+B^2/20+S^2/2+2ST+9T^2/4,

    h = 576 S^4+(22368/5)S^3T+11748 S^2T^2
        +(60528/5)ST^3+(20304/5)T^4
        +A^2[(4068/25)B^2-576 S^2-(18528/5)ST-(27636/5)T^2]
        -B^2[(4068/25)S^2+(9264/25)ST+(4368/25)T^2].

The determinant can be computed without square roots in m as det[4diag(Qm)+8Qdiag(m)], similar to H when all y_i!=0 and equal polynomially everywhere. Its two imbalance derivatives are

    partial h/partial A^2
       =(12/25)[339 B^2-1200 S^2-7720 ST-11515 T^2] <0,

    partial h/partial B^2
       =(12/25)[339 A^2-339 S^2-772 ST-364 T^2] <=0,

for nonzero S,T; the second is strict when T>0. At fixed S,T, balancing A=B=0 therefore increases h and decreases f. Whenever h>0, it increases K=h/f^2. Consequently every positive global curvature maximum is balanced within each occupied pair. At a full-support critical point S,T>0, these strict derivatives force A=B=0: the derivative of K in either nonzero imbalance has the opposite sign, and the squared-variable-to-y change is locally invertible.

For A=B=0 and S>0 put t=T/S. The curvature is

    K(t) = [(6+4t)(4+47t/5)(24+114t+108t^2)]
           /[1/2+2t+9t^2/4]^2.

If N is the numerator and D=1/2+2t+9t^2/4, exact differentiation gives

    N' D-2 N D'
      =-(6/5)[9162t^4+17111t^3+10032t^2+1986t+56] <0

for all t>=0. Thus K is strictly decreasing, with K(0)=2304. The pure second-pair endpoint S=0 has the smaller limiting value. It follows that

    h/f^2 <= 2304 whenever h>0,

and equality is attained only at m_1=m_2>0, m_3=m_4=0. These rays have H>0. In particular the global positive-Hessian curvature maxima all have support exactly2, and **there is no full-support critical point anywhere in H>0**. An H>0 full-support attractor cannot rescue the proposed universal proof.

The inequalities about h>0 are stronger than needed for the H>0 region. We do not conflate positive determinant with a positive-definite Hessian; the local dynamical conclusion explicitly uses H(p)>0.
