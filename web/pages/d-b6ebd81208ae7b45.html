GROUPS / OPERATOR ALGEBRAS — CYCLE 5
Date: 2026-10-08
Scope: fixed-characteristic rank models and global compatibility in the N65 triangle system.

STATUS
======

This cycle isolates a decisive positive-characteristic interface and a distinct
local obstruction to extending the permutation argument to arbitrary matrices.
It does not prove a group-level breakthrough.

1. PROVED-CANDIDATE SUFFICIENT THEOREM. If G admits an injective rank-metric
   ultraproduct model by finite-dimensional general linear groups whose fields
   all have one fixed characteristic p (their degrees may vary), then K[G] is
   stably finite for every coefficient field K of characteristic p. The
   proof reduces any hypothetical K-counterexample to one over a finite field,
   extends the coordinate fields to a common finite subfield, restricts
   scalars, and applies Arzhantseva–Păunescu's fixed-field group-algebra
   embedding theorem plus stable finiteness of rank ultraproducts.
2. FINITE-STAGE MODULAR SPECIALIZATION. For any one complex finite-stage rank
   model with finitely generated coefficient ring R and finite nonzero rank
   minors c, p-safe reduction holds for all but finitely many primes. This
   pointwise statement does not yield one fixed p along an infinite sequence.
   An exact algebra-separating rank model of Z has coefficient rings
   Z[1/P_k], with P_k the product of the first k primes; every fixed prime is
   inverted at all sufficiently late stages. Z itself has a separate
   characteristic-p permutation model for every p.
3. EXACT LOCAL GL CONSTRUCTION. On one complete r=s=t=2 wiring block, an
   incidence-dependent GL_D(F_q) assignment over any odd q satisfies all 16
   triangles and has W=-I_D on every ordered nondegenerate rectangle. Thus
   rank(I-W)=D is maximal. This refutes local triangle-only incompatibility
   claims. It does not extend to the global N65 complex, where each X_{iuv}
   is shared across many point blocks and interpolation couples all labels.

The resulting central gates are now sharp: (i) produce a fixed-characteristic
rank model from a complex linear-sofic model (or otherwise detect
characteristic-p one-sided inverses), and (ii) for N65, understand the
cross-block consistency imposed on the shared X_{iuv}. Neither gate is closed.

1. SAME-CHARACTERISTIC RANK MODELS IMPLY STABLE FINITENESS
=========================================================

Theorem. Fix a prime p. Let G be countable and suppose there are finite fields
F_i of characteristic p, dimensions n_i tending to infinity along an
ultrafilter omega, and an injective group homomorphism

  G -> prod_{i->omega} GL_{n_i}(F_i) / d_omega,
  d_omega(A,B) = lim_{i->omega} rank(A_i-B_i)/n_i.

Then K[G] is stably finite for every field K with char(K)=p. If the
characteristic equals p only on an omega-large subset, restrict to that subset.

Proof. First let k0=F_{p^d} be any finite extension of F_p. Write
F_i=F_{p^{e_i}} and put L_i=F_{p^{lcm(d,e_i)}}. Then L_i contains both F_i and
k0. Extend the coordinate matrices from F_i to L_i and restrict scalars from
L_i to k0. An n_i-by-n_i matrix over L_i becomes a k0-linear matrix of size

  N_i = n_i [L_i:k0].

For every A in M_{n_i}(L_i),

  rank_{k0}(Res(A)) = [L_i:k0] rank_{L_i}(A).

This follows because the L_i-image of A has L_i-dimension rank_{L_i}(A),
and each L_i-dimension contributes [L_i:k0] dimensions over k0. Applying this
to A-B shows normalized rank is exactly preserved. The group embedding
therefore yields an injective rank-metric group embedding over the single
fixed field k0; N_i tends to infinity since N_i>=n_i.

Arzhantseva–Păunescu, Theorem 7.4, then gives an injective unital k0-algebra
map k0[G] into a rank ultraproduct of matrix algebras over k0. Proposition 2.8
shows this target is stably directly finite. Concretely, the field-independent
identity rank(I-AB)=rank(I-BA) implies that AB=I in the quotient exactly when
BA=I; it also applies at each fixed matrix amplification. Injectivity pulls
the equality back to k0[G]. In fact this proves stable finiteness of k0[G],
because the target is stably finite and the embedding remains injective after
every fixed matrix amplification.

It remains to pass from finite k0 to arbitrary K of characteristic p. Fix
any matrix size r and suppose A,B in M_r(K[G]) satisfy AB=I but BA!=I. There
are only finitely many group coefficients among all entries of A and B. Give
each such coefficient a variable over F_p; the equations AB=I give finitely
many polynomial equations. Choose one nonzero group coefficient of one entry
of BA-I and impose Z times that coefficient equals 1. The resulting finite
polynomial system over F_p has a solution in K, so its finitely generated
coordinate algebra is nonzero. A maximal ideal has finite residue field k0
by Zariski's lemma. The reduced matrices give a counterexample to stable
finiteness in M_r(k0[G]), contradicting the preceding paragraph. Since r was
arbitrary, K[G] is stably finite, and in particular directly finite.

For a possibly uncountable G with an embedding of the stated kind, the same
conclusion follows locally: any finite-matrix witness lies in M_r(k[H]) for
the countable finitely generated subgroup H generated by the union of its
entry supports, and H inherits the rank-ultraproduct embedding.

Exact qualification. This theorem is conditional on a rank-metric group model
whose coordinate fields have one fixed characteristic. It does not obtain
such a model from hyperlinearity, and it does not promote AP's finite-field
reduction with unspecified characteristics to a fixed-p model.

2. P-SAFE SPECIALIZATION AND A DIAGONAL MODEL FAILURE
=====================================================

At one finite stage, suppose the matrices and their inverses lie in a
finitely generated Z-subalgebra R of C. For each matrix in the finite list
of generator invertibility, nonidentity separation, and multiplicativity
defects, let r_X be its rank over C and choose one nonzero r_X-by-r_X minor
when r_X>0. Let c be the product of these minors (empty product 1).
If c is not nilpotent modulo pR, then there is a maximal ideal m of R
containing p but not c, and R/m is a finite field of characteristic p.
Indeed (R/pR)[1/c] is a nonzero finite-type F_p-algebra; a maximal ideal of
the localization gives a finite residue field and contracts to a maximal
ideal avoiding c. All larger minors already vanish in R, and the selected
minors remain nonzero, so every listed rank is preserved exactly. Inverse
entries in R ensure the reduced generator matrices remain invertible.

Lead refinement: for any fixed stage with R a domain of characteristic zero
and c!=0, this condition holds for all but finitely many primes p. The
finite-type morphism Spec(R[1/c]) -> Spec(Z) is dominant because R embeds in
C and R[1/c] is nonzero. By constructibility of its image, it contains a
nonempty open subset of Spec(Z), hence all but finitely many prime points.
Equivalently, for all but finitely many p the fiber (R/pR)[1/c] is nonzero,
so c is not nilpotent modulo pR. This is only a finite-stage, pointwise-in-p
statement: the exceptional finite set can depend on the stage.

Exact adversarial sequence. Let p_1,p_2,... be the primes in increasing
order, P_k=product_{j<=k} p_j, and n_k=k. Set

  D_k = diag(P_k, P_k^2, ..., P_k^k) in GL_k(Q),
  theta_k(m) = D_k^m for m in Z.

Each theta_k is an exact group homomorphism. If m!=0, none of the diagonal
entries P_k^{jm} equals 1, so rank(I-D_k^m)=k. Thus Z embeds faithfully in
the rank-metric ultraproduct, with separation 1 on every nonidentity element.
For any nonzero Laurent polynomial f in Q[t,t^{-1}], f(D_k) is diagonal
with entries f(P_k^j); a nonzero polynomial has finitely many integer roots,
and P_k tends to infinity, so rank(f(D_k))/k=1 for all sufficiently large k.
This even gives an injective algebra map Q[Z] into the rank ultraproduct.

The coordinate coefficient ring is R_k=Z[1/P_k]. For any fixed prime p_j
and every k>=j, p_j is a unit in R_k. No unital ring map R_k to any nonzero
characteristic-p_j ring can exist: the equality p_j*(1/p_j)=1 would map to
0=1. Yet each fixed stage has only finitely many excluded primes (including
the finitely many divisors of its chosen rank minors), so it has p-safe
reductions at all but finitely many primes. The sequence demonstrates why
stagewise cofinite goodness does not imply one fixed prime works eventually.
It is a failure of this selected model's reduction, not a group-level
obstruction: the usual n-cycle permutation matrices over F_p give a separate
rank model of Z for every p, since rank(I-S_n^m)=n-gcd(n,m), which equals
n-1 for primes n>|m|.

The cost is explicit but not optimized. The rational diagonal model has k
entries at stage k and bit length j*log2(P_k) for P_k^j; the exact model
does not require approximate relation error. The p-safe criterion needs
minor and ideal computations whose bit-height and running time are not
bounded here. For a stable-finiteness witness in M_r(K[G]), specialization
uses one variable for each coefficient in the finitely supported entries of
A and B, plus one inverse-defect variable. The resulting finite system has no
residue-degree bound from Zariski's lemma.

3. EXACT WIRED MATRIX MODEL ON ONE COMPLETE BLOCK
================================================

Take r=s=t=2, I=Z/2 x Z/2 with i=(alpha,beta), any odd finite field F_q,
and any D>=1. Indices alpha+v and beta+u are read modulo 2, with u,v in
{1,2}. Put

  L_{u,j}=I_D for every u,j;
  R_{1,0}=R_{1,1}=R_{2,0}=I_D; R_{2,1}=-I_D;
  X_{iuv}=R_{v,beta+u mod 2}.

Then every exact triangle equation holds:

  L_{u,alpha+v} X_{iuv} R_{v,beta+u}^{-1}=I_D.

For beta=0, the 2-by-2 block array Y_i=(X_iuv) is [[I,-I],[I,I]]; for
beta=1 it is [[I,I],[I,-I]]. Direct multiplication gives

  X_i11 X_i21^{-1} X_i22 X_i12^{-1} = -I_D.

Reversing either row or column pair inverts this rectangle, and (-I_D)^-1
=-I_D, so all 16 ordered nondegenerate rectangles have W=-I_D. Since q is
odd, rank(I_D-W)=rank(2I_D)=D. The sum over the four labels in this one block
is Z=[[4I_D,0],[4I_D,0]], of rank D, exactly at the local factor bound.

An exact checker at
`work/programmes/groups_operator/leaves/d5_simplex_candidate/cycle5_wired_GL_model/check_wired_gl_model.py`
verifies all 16 triangle equations, all 16 ordered rectangle slots, and the
local ranks over F_3 and F_7 for D=1. Tensoring by I_D multiplies ranks by D,
so the symbolic proof covers all D and odd q. In the conservative N65 local
rank interface r=2 requires q>r; q=7 is a legal field size for that inequality
(but this toy block is not the actual large-r construction).

This construction refutes the failed lemma “the full local triangle wiring
alone forces some rectangle defect below D.” It does not refute the global
N65 nonsoficity theorem or establish linear soficity. In the full system,
the same X_{iuv} is shared among all point blocks containing label i. The
exact cross-block condition is

  L_{b,u,alpha_b(i)+v}^{-1} R_{b,v,beta_b(i)+u}
   = L_{b',u,alpha_{b'}(i)+v}^{-1} R_{b',v,beta_{b'}(i)+u}

whenever i occurs in both b and b'. These global equations, together with
the interpolation matrices H(z), are not tested by the isolated block.
The finite block sum has rank D and is consistent with the existing local
rank-factor estimate. No huge graph or presentation was acquired.

4. SOURCE STATUS, BASELINE, AND NEXT GATES
==========================================

The primary AP source is Arzhantseva–Păunescu, “Linear sofic groups and
algebras,” arXiv:1212.6780, submitted 2012-12-30 and published in Trans. AMS
369(4) (2017), 2285–2310, DOI 10.1090/tran/6706. Its Proposition 2.8 states
stable finiteness of rank ultraproducts; Theorem 7.4 embeds F[G] into such an
ultraproduct from a rank model over one fixed field; Theorem 8.2 reduces
complex linear-soficity to finite-field models without prescribing their
characteristics. The source's Question 8.6 asks for a model over a prescribed
finite field and says a positive answer would imply Kaplansky direct
finiteness for linear-sofic groups. This is the source's 2012 question; no
claim is made here about its present global status. Full theorem locators and
the inspected primary URL are in READ_LEDGER.txt.

Strongest baseline: sofic groups satisfy direct finiteness over every field
by Elek–Szabó. AP's fixed-field rank theorem already supplies the algebra
embedding for a fixed rank model. The theorem here adds an explicit
varying-degree, same-characteristic-to-fixed-field reduction and a proof
that any arbitrary-char-p coefficient-field counterexample reduces to a
finite extension. It remains a conditional sufficient interface; it does
not supply that model for a given hyperlinear or complex-linear-sofic group.

Falsifiers: (a) an exact same-characteristic rank-model group with a checked
one-sided inverse in a same-characteristic group algebra would break the
AP/restriction/specialization chain; (b) a p-safe finite-stage reduction
where any selected listed rank changes would break the minor argument; (c) a
failure among the 16 explicit triangle or rectangle checks would break the
local GL construction. A model-specific lack of reduction does not falsify
the existence of another model for the same group.

Next decisive positive-characteristic gate: for each fixed p, derive a
rank-metric model over finite fields of characteristic p from a complex
linear-sofic model, or find a different characteristic-p detector of
one-sided inverses. For the N65 system, acquire only a small source-legal
shared-label incidence subconfiguration if available; solve or obstruct its
coupled GL equations exactly. A successful finite local model is not enough:
global consistency over all shared incidences is the live gate. Hyperlinear
to rank transfer remains blocked by the exact HS-small/full-rank example in
the Cycle 4 leaf, and no conclusion is drawn for hyperlinearity,
nonhyperlinearity, or direct finiteness of N129.

No novelty, priority, full N65 presentation acquisition, group-level linear
soficity conclusion, or current-global-open claim is made.
