Findings
========

The proposal normalizer factors as

    Z_q=product_j s_+^(k_j) mu_(j,k_j),

because `X_j=sqrt(2s_+G_j) B_j^(1/2)u_j`, the homogeneous intensity weight
changes the Gamma radius to shape `k_j+1`, and the independent angular
weight is `(u_j^T B_j u_j)^k_j`. In the eigenbasis, the arcsine moment
`E[T^ell]=binom(2ell,ell)/4^ell` gives the stated nonnegative mixture
weights and the Beta `(ell+1/2,1/2)` component. The rational moment formula
for `mu` is consistent with direct angular integration.

Whitening by `B^(-1/2)` gives the target/proposal precision difference
`S'^(-1)-s_+^(-1)I`, whose operator norm is at most
`s_-^(-1)-s_+^(-1)`. Under the proposal,
`E||B^(-1/2)X||^2=2s_+(m+kappa)`, so Jensen yields
`alpha>=exp[-(m+kappa)(s_+/s_- -1)]`. The dyadic `Lambda` envelope is valid.
The exact-real rejection identity normalizes to the desired conditional
core law without a herald-probability oracle.

For the Beta ratio, on `A+D>=zeta_B`, the numerator and total-denominator
errors are respectively bounded by

    e_A <= kappa eta_E+(R_N+1)eta_Z,
    e_F <= kappa eta_E+2(R_N+1)eta_Z.

Since the computed nonnegative numerator does not exceed its computed
denominator, the ratio error is at most `(e_A+e_F)/(A+D)`. The chosen
parameters give

    (e_A+e_F)/zeta_B
      <= delta_T [kappa/(32(kappa+1))+3/256] < delta_T.

Also `e_F<3 zeta_B delta_T/128`, so the `zeta_B/2` fallback cannot fire on
the good event. The Gamma `(ell+1)` small-denominator probability is at most
`zeta_B` per positive herald mode; summing over `m` modes is the stated
`m zeta_B` failure budget.

The angular/eigenbasis error has no missing factor of `sqrt(m)`: on the good
radial event `||Z||^2=2 sum_j G_j<=T_Z`, while every transformed block has
operator norm at most `sqrt(s_+ b_max)`. Thus the aggregate direction error
is bounded by `sqrt(s_+ b_max T_Z) delta_u <= B_X delta_u < tau/64`.
Radial exponential and square-root errors separately include the required
`s_+ b_max`, `kappa+m`, and `sqrt(m)` factors. The second `eta_E` cap leaves
the perturbed whitened radius within the slack used in the `gamma_A` matrix
root approximation.

Eigenvalue, eigenvector, and square-root intervals have constant algebraic
degree; near-isotropic or nearly diagonal cases do not require a numerical
condition oracle. A nonzero rational off-diagonal entry has magnitude at
least `2^(-O(L))`, and the angular normalizer lower bound
`(det B_j/tr B_j)^(k_j)` costs `O(k_j L)` bits. These are polynomial in
input length and numeric `kappa`; they do not create a condition-number
rejection factor. The conditional output-energy trace bound is valid.

Scope and remaining gates
=========================

The audit found no fatal gap in the sampler derivation or the audited
finite-bit coupling. The exact fixture checks the angular normalizers,
relative sandwich, acceptance identity, covariance reconstruction, and
energy bound; it does not execute the finite-bit coupling algorithm. It is
finite evidence only. The Section 8 rare-herald/high-condition comparison
is supported by the separate exact finite fixture and its displayed
closed-form derivation, but has not received a separate sibling review.

This is internal peer review, not external mathematical validation, a
novelty search, or priority evidence. The theorem still requires a supplied
positive rational local-block sandwich; a poor standardized
inter-mode-correlation ratio can make `Lambda` large. It is not a general
displaced or non-positive-P sampler, and physical repeat-until-herald
preparation continues to cost `1/p_H`.

Two precision notes were incorporated into the theorem file: (i) compute the
rational upper Frobenius bound for A's interval-centre matrix within additive

projection, round the inward scaling down so the finite rational core is
certified to remain in the Ymax ball. The latter adds at most tau/64 to the
coupling error and preserves the stated tau/4 budget.

Scope: this is an internal adversarial review, not external validation and
