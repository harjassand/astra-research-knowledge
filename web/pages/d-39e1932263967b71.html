STRONGEST CLAIM

An explicit ordered complex-rational pair matrix F has a deterministically
acquired exact MPS with bond dimensions

 D_p=2^(rank F[[0,p),[p,n)] + rank F[[p,n),[0,p)]).

Consequently, if the maximum displayed rank sum c is O(log n), every canonical
coefficient, every physical occupation prefix norm, exact zero detection, and
independent exact Born sampling of arbitrary nonnegative rational onsite
squared filters are polynomial-bit tasks. Hard projection is included, as are
arbitrary attraction t>2, cancellations, singular F, and rare sectors. The
class is recognized from the supplied order by ordinary exact rational rank,
with no numerical threshold, supplied norm, singular-value promise, or Chen–Liu
FPRAS dependency. Order optimization is not supplied or claimed.

This admits dense nonbipartite full-rank matrices: F_ij=1 for i<j, F_ij=2 for
i>j, F_ii=0 has c=2 and determinant (-1)^(n-1)(2^n-2), n>=2. It is neither
monomial nor cross-bipartite. General quasiseparable matrices, bounded bandwidth
matrices, and low-rank matrices also enter. The unrestricted parity FPRAS is
still open. Gaussian MPS plus local projections is established prior art;
novelty of this exact rational gauge and costed admission theorem is UNKNOWN.

EXACT INPUT AND OUTPUT

Input F in Q(i)^(n x n), in a given site order; t_i>=0 rational; optional pair
count k; optional site restrictions X_i subset {0,u,d,ud}. Let

 C_k = sum_{|I|=|J|=k} |det F[I,J]|^2 product_{i in I intersection J} t_i.

At t_i=0 use 0^0=1. Hard t=0 gives precisely the supplied frontier's disjoint
minor partition. An occupation restriction simply removes forbidden terms.
The delivered code returns all C_k exactly as rationals, rejects zero sectors,
and samples occupations with their exactly normalized squared-minor weights.
It prepares no quantum circuit or hardware state. Acquiring or optimizing the
physical pair matrix lies outside the explicit-input interface.

FULL CONSTRUCTIVE DERIVATION

1. Work in the site-interleaved fermionic mode order
 u_0,d_0,u_1,d_1,..., with exterior multiplication tracking every sign. Write
 G=sum_ij F_ij u_i^dagger d_j^dagger. All quadratic creation monomials commute
and square to zero, so exp G=product_ij(1+F_ij u_i^dagger d_j^dagger).
Its coefficient on I,J equals (-1)^(k(k-1)/2) det F[I,J] in up-then-down order.
Changing to site order adds the explicit inversion parity sum_{i in I,j in J}
1_(i>j). Thus squared coefficients are the required determinant weights.

2. At cut p let L=[0,p), R=[p,n), a=rank F[L,R], b=rank F[R,L]. Factor the
two cross blocks into full rank factors. Their cross generator is a sum of a
products (left-up linear form)*(right-down linear form), and b products
(right-up linear form)*(left-down linear form). Expanding its commuting
nilpotent factors gives 2^(a+b) tensor products. Left factors, before the
invertible local exp G_LL, are all exterior products of subsets of a independent
up forms and b independent down forms. These are linearly independent because
the two spin spaces are disjoint. The right factors have the same property.
Multiplication by exp G_LL and exp G_RR is invertible, with inverse exp(-G).
Therefore the exact unfiltered Schmidt rank is 2^(a+b), and its left Schmidt
support has the following explicit rational basis:

 L_p[S,T] = exp(G_LL) product_{alpha in S} U_alpha
                         product_{beta in T} V_beta.

U is any column basis of F[L,R], viewed as up creation forms. V is any column
basis of F[R,L]^T, viewed as down creation forms. The implementation chooses
actual columns using exact Gaussian elimination, introducing no irrational
normalization. Labels S,T are ordered subsets, with up forms first.

3. Acquire a small, invertible evaluation matrix for this left basis. Select
a physical up rows of U forming a nonsingular a x a minor and b physical down
rows of V forming a nonsingular b x b minor. Let P_p be these c_p=a+b modes.
Evaluate every basis column on every occupation subset Y of P_p, with all
other left modes empty. The resulting square matrix K_p has size D_p=2^c_p.

K_p is invertible: restriction to P_p sends U and V to invertible linear
changes of their respective a- and b-mode spaces. Their induced maps on the
full exterior algebra are invertible. Restriction of exp G_LL acts on this
same algebra by exp(G restricted to P_p), which is invertible. K_p is their
matrix product in occupation coordinates. Overlap between the selected site
indices causes no issue: up and down are distinct modes. The endpoint K_0 is
(1), and at p=n the basis has the single column exp G with vacuum amplitude 1.

4. Consecutive Schmidt supports satisfy
 span L_p subset span L_(p-1) tensor C^4.
Indeed, expand the global pure coefficient tensor first across p and then
across p-1. Its right Schmidt factors at p are independent; applying their
dual linear functionals proves the inclusion for every L_p column. Therefore
there exist unique rational matrices A_p^x, x in {0,u,d,ud}, such that

 L_p[b] = sum_{a,x} L_(p-1)[a] tensor |x> (A_p^x)_[a,b].

Evaluate on each previous pivot occupation Y and new site occupation x. Let
H_p^x[Y,b] be that coefficient of L_p[b]. Then K_(p-1) A_p^x=H_p^x, giving
the acquired formula A_p^x=K_(p-1)^(-1) H_p^x. Every query uses at most
c_(p-1)+2 physical modes, and is computed by exterior multiplication after
erasing all unqueried modes. No full Fock vector is constructed. Induction
from L_0=(1) proves that A_1^x1 ... A_n^xn is the exact BCS amplitude, including
its phase. The code implements precisely this formula.

5. Set w_i(x)=1 for empty/up/down and w_i(ud)=t_i, and set w_i(x)=0 for a
forbidden site occupation. The suffix matrices satisfy

 B_n[0]=(1), B_n[q]=(0) for q!=0,
 B_i[q]=sum_x w_(i+1)(x) A_(i+1)^x B_(i+1)[q-u(x)] (A_(i+1)^x)^*,

where u(x)=1 if the up mode is occupied, otherwise zero. Induction shows
B_i[q] is the Gram matrix of all suffix amplitudes of up charge q. It is PSD,
although its entries can be complex rationals. Therefore C_k=B_0[k]_[0,0].
Global unfiltered amplitudes already have equal up/down number, so tracking
the up count alone gives the exact pair sector, with no scalar tilt or sector
rejection. Local filtering and prefix restriction occur inside this exact
contraction. The final scalar is exactly real and nonnegative; code asserts
this invariant.

6. At a chosen physical prefix, retain its unfiltered virtual amplitude row v
and remaining up count q. The four next-state masses are exactly

 m_x=w_i(x) (v A_i^x) B_i[q-u(x)] (v A_i^x)^*.

Previously accumulated filter weights cancel from the conditional law. These
four nonnegative rational masses sum to the current completion norm. Clear
their denominators and draw an integer uniformly below their total, using
unbiased random bits and rejection of out-of-range integers. Choosing x with
probability m_x/sum m and updating v,q proves the exact Born law by the chain
rule. Each integer attempt has success probability at least 1/2. Exact zero
sector detection precedes any sampling, and positive conditional completions
remain positive. There is no inverse projection or sector probability cost.

BIT AND COMPUTATION COSTS

Let L be the total binary input length and D=max D_p. Deterministic cut ranks,
column bases, and physical pivot rows cost O(n^4) rational field operations by
naive elimination. Their bit cost is polynomial in n,L. Each local exterior
query uses at most c+2 modes and O((c+2)^2 2^(c+2)) arithmetic operations.
There are O(n D^2) such queries. Pivot inversions and tensor multiplications
cost O(n D^3). Thus acquisition has the conservative arithmetic bound
 O(n^4 + n D^3 (c+2)^2).

All coefficient/suffix contractions cost O(n^2 D^3) rational operations and
O(n^2 D^2) rational storage. A reused suffix table gives one sample in
O(n D^2) additional rational operations plus random integer draws. The simple
reference sample() recomputes this table on each call; this remains polynomial
but is not the optimized multiple-sample interface.

For bit heights, clear all F denominators by one Gaussian-rational denominator
of O(L) bits. A queried exterior amplitude has degree at most c+2 in input
entries, with numerator/denominator heights O((c+2)(L+log(c+2))) up to a fixed
constant. A common denominator for K_p has the same polynomial height.
Adjugate/Hadamard bounds make K_p^(-1) and A_p entries have
O(D (c+2)(L+c+2)) bits conservatively. Common denominators for a complete
contraction are products of the per-site tensor denominators and t denominators.
Each term contains only O(n) factors; the number of virtual and physical paths
adds O(n log(4D)) numerator bits. Hence all stored exact rationals have bit
length polynomial in n,D,L. No conditioning/singular-value assumption is
needed for these exact arithmetic bounds.

Uniform c<=C log_2(n+1) for fixed public C makes D<=(n+1)^C and every displayed
time/space/bit bound polynomial in binary input. The implementation has an
explicit max_dimension cap and rejects an exceeded cap; it silently performs
no truncation. Sampling is exact expected polynomial time. Limiting the
integer retries at each site to ceil(log_2(n/delta)) gives an explicit
polynomial worst-time approximate sampler with total failure probability
<=delta; on failure a positive-completion path is acquired by always taking
a positive mass. This truncation wrapper is proved here but not implemented.

STRICT EXTENSIONS AND A SECOND EXACT FAMILY

If rank F=r then c<=2r, admitting r=O(log n). If F has bandwidth b, both
cross blocks have rank<=b, admitting b=O(log n). More broadly an r-quasiseparable
matrix has both cross ranks<=r even with full rank and dense nonbipartite
support. This is not an approximation to arbitrary matrices of low entropy:
rank tests are exact and a tiny nonzero singular direction is retained.

For the dense matrix F_ij=alpha if i<j, beta if i>j, and arbitrary diagonal,
hard minors on disjoint I,J vanish unless the colors I,J alternate in their
merged ordered support: consecutive same-color rows or columns are identical
and force a zero minor. For an alternating 2k-site support, row subtraction
gives determinant alpha(alpha-beta)^(k-1) in one orientation and
beta(beta-alpha)^(k-1) in the other, up to the already specified permutation
phase. Consequently, for k>=1,

 C_k=binomial(n,2k)(|alpha|^2+|beta|^2)|alpha-beta|^(2k-2), C_0=1.

For alpha=1,beta=2 this is 5 binomial(n,2k). Row differences
2e_i-e_(i+1) also yield det F=(-1)^(n-1)(2^n-2) at zero diagonal. This fixture
establishes a full-rank, dense, all-size family with c=2 and direct canonical
counting. It does not identify the generic c=2 family with a positive graph
matching representation.
