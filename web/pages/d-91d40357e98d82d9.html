Date: 2026-10-08
Status: no counterexample or failed step found in the scoped candidate.
This is an internal proof audit, not external validation or a novelty claim.

TARGET AND SCOPE

Target: outputs/research/spectral_geometry/su3_regular/rank_profile_allq.txt,
with its dependencies principal_cartan_filter.txt,
profile_asymptotics.txt, and spectrum_exact.txt.
The claim is for V_(a,b), N<=a,b<=2N, N>=16, with d=dim(V_(a,b)) and
E(X)=sum_A ||[T_A,X]||_HS^2 in the stated fundamental-generator
normalization. It concerns the infimum over all complex matrices with a
rank cap; the upper witnesses are explicit positive diagonal matrices.

CLAIM CHECKED

The proposed profile is

  Lambda(q) ~= (d/q)^(1/3),       1<=q<=d/2,
  Lambda(d-k) ~= (k/d)^(2/3),    1<=k<=d/2,
  Lambda(d)=0.

The file gives a single loose pair of constants 1/10000 and 20000. The
lower and upper mechanisms are distinct: a stable low-Casimir projector
count proves the low-rank lower; a right-kernel Green functional proves the
near-full lower for arbitrary matrices; principal-Cartan caps prove the
low-rank upper and small-deficit near-full upper; a clipped linear Cartan
function supplies the moderate-deficit upper.

AUDIT FINDINGS

1. Low-rank lower. The rank-q vectorization/partial-trace inequality gives
  Lambda(q)>=integral_0^infinity [1-qD(T)/d]_+ dT.
The first nontrivial conjugation Casimir is 3, so D(T)=1 on [0,3). In the
strict stable range T<Delta^2, Delta=min(a+1,b+1), the multiplicity-aware
count has D(T)<=729 T^3 for T>=1. With H=(d/q)^(1/3), the split H<12 and
H>=12 gives the claimed lower; the cutoff alpha H remains strictly below
Delta^2 for the stated N range. I checked the constants alpha=1458^(-1/3),
the bounded-T branch, and the cutoff dependence.

2. Near-full lower. For any rank<=d-k matrix A, choose a rank-k projection
P in ker(A), write A=aI+X with Tr X=0, and P0=P-(k/d)I. Then
  k^2 |a|^2 <= G(P) E(A),   ||X||_HS^2 <= E(A)/3,
  G(P)=<P0,L^+P0>.
This remains valid for nonnormal A: AP=0 implies Tr(PA)=0, and the
argument uses only the scalar consequence of the kernel constraint.
For each cumulative nontrivial Casimir projector Pi_(0,T],
  ||Pi_(0,T] P0||_HS^2 <= k^2 D(T)/d,
because Pi kills the identity and the vectorized rank-k projection has
Schmidt rank k. Integrating the cumulative spectral mass against T^(-2)
gives G(P). The cutoff K=max(3,(d/k)^(1/3)) is below Delta^2 for
N>=16; the low- and high-spectrum terms give
  G(P)<=3286 k^(4/3)d^(-1/3),
and hence Lambda(d-k)>=(1/3287)(k/d)^(2/3). The orientation-free bound is
not an inference from a spectrum alone: it uses the rank-k right-kernel
projection and its shellwise Schmidt-rank uncertainty.

3. Principal-Cartan shell construction. For H0=diag(1,0,-1), the GT depth
ell=2x+y+z has shell size g_ell=floor((ell+2)^2/4) through ell<=m,
m=min(a,b), and cap volume V_R=sum_(ell<=R)g_ell. The shell traces are
  s12=(a-ell/2)g_ell, s23=(b-ell/2)g_ell,
  s13=(a+b-ell)g_ell.
The trace recurrence from [Eij,Eji]=Eii-Ejj yields one-step conductances
c12,c23 and two-step c13. In particular c=c12+c23 is increasing,
c(q)<=5m q^3 for q<=m, and c13(q)<=c(q-1) for 2<=q<=m+1.
For a one-step profile with increments Delta_q, the long-root difference
is Delta_(q-1)+Delta_q; monotonicity plus (u+v)^2<=2u^2+2v^2 gives
E13<=4E1. I checked the endpoint q=M+1 in the near-full taper: the
required two-step conductance is available because M<=m.

4. Low-rank upper. The ramp F_R(ell)=(1-ell/R)_+ has rank V_(R-1),
||F_R||_HS^2>=R^3/384, and E(F_R)<=25mR^2. Thus its normalized energy is
at most 9600m/R. Rounding to the largest R with V_(R-1)<=q, followed by
the fixed cap R0=floor(2m/3) for the larger low-rank budgets, covers every
1<=q<=d/2 without extending the exact shell formula beyond ell=m. The
rank rounding is one-sided as required by rank<=q. The constants fit the
stated 20000(d/q)^(1/3) upper.

5. Small and moderate near-full upper. For k<=floor(d/512), the harmonic
taper is 1 on q<=R and zero at M=2(R+1), where V_R>=k. The cutoff proof
uses D<=3.5m^3, V_(R-1)>=R^3/12, and minimality of R; it yields M<=m.
Its rank is d-V_R<=d-k, its norm squared is at least 7d/8, and its
energy is at most 60m(R+1)^2. Converting R to k yields the claimed
743(k/d)^(2/3) upper. For k>floor(d/512), the matrix
X=(-T_(H0)/(a+b))_+ has rank<=d/2<=d-k by Weyl symmetry, norm squared
>=d/16, and energy<=12d; the ratio is at most 192, which is bounded by
12288(k/d)^(2/3). This fills the moderate-deficit interval that the
small-cap taper alone does not cover.
