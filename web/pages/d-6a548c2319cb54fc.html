# Primary source and prior boundary

## Directly used prior result

Correggi, Giuliani, and Seiringer, “Validity of the spin-wave approximation for the free energy of the Heisenberg ferromagnet,” arXiv:1312.7873v2 (2019; published in *Communications in Mathematical Physics* 339, 2015), [official arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.7873), [official HTML text](https://arxiv.org/html/1312.7873).

Relevant primary ranges:

- Proposition 5.2, Eqs. (5.5)–(5.12): open-cube total-spin sector gap (H_{\Lambda_\ell}\ge C S\ell^{-2}(S\ell^3-S^T)). It proves the three-spin operator inequality (5.6), the path inequality (5.9)–(5.10), then uses a geometrically balanced family of paths to control edge traffic.
- Eqs. (5.14)–(5.16): finite-block total-spin multiplicity and trace tail from bounded bosonic slots.
- Eqs. (5.22)–(5.42): reduction of the finite-volume trace to low-energy total-spin sectors, an interacting-boson correction, and a truncated free-boson mode partition bound. These estimates support their low-temperature free-energy result; they do not state a W1 rate for the total-spin distribution at fixed temperature.
- Theorem 2.1 concerns the thermodynamic free-energy asymptotic as β→∞, not a finite-torus fixed-β magnetization-radius estimate.

The exact periodic routing count, weighted comparison by (J_-\), and the finite Gibbs-tail/W1 corollary in this folder are reconstructed consequences. They are not claimed as new source results or as independently externally validated mathematics.
