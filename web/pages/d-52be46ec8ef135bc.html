# Exact services and costs of the cycle18 preparation route

The scientific artifact is a conditional analytical cutoff theorem plus
a fully specified finite circuit recipe. There is no executed bath,
quantum circuit, thermal sample or exponential matrix in this result.
The effective recipe is the base proof PLUS its mandatory computable-mode
box/control addendum. A circuit description does not acquire inaccessible
environmental control.

| Service | What is acquired mathematically | Remaining access/cost |
|---|---|---|
| Actual Hamiltonian | Exact finite-mode polaron identity; bounded spin perturbation | N checks/qubits and every mode/coupling/frequency/temperature description; coefficient precision |
| Oscillator truncation | Polynomial second moment and explicit full-to-compressed Gibbs trace error | Dyadic positive-frequency brackets; Dbox=2^N product(K_l+1); no finite mode count alone implies a small register |

| Storage | Exact original Htot and original decoder after active controls stop | No generated Davies/window channel; finite-mode gate accessibility and physical implementation remain stated premises |

Let L=sum_checks h+sum eps, Lambda=sum_Zchecks h+sum eps, and m the mode
count. The cutoff moment M2 is O[(L+m/beta)^2+beta^-2]. At target resource
error delta the box boundary in the addendum is an explicit rational upper
bound on O[(beta L sqrt(M2)+beta M2)/delta^2+
sqrt(M2(c0+2beta sqrt(M2)))/delta]. Here
c0=N log2-sum log(1-exp(-beta omega/2)); all low-frequency data are paid.
Choose K_l=ceil(R/omega_l^-) with certified rational omega_l^->0.
The output box uses R_out>=(2Ebox^++2Eshift)/eW^2 and its corresponding
Kout_l. This is a sufficient cutoff, not an optimized or necessary one.

For herald precision delta_f, p*=exp(-2beta Lambda) and epsK=delta_f
p*/16, the explicit Euler step prescription has
nsteps=O[1+beta Lambda+beta^2 Lambda^2/epsK]. Finite time integration
uses T=(beta/pi)log(16Lambda/xi+4), xi=epsK/(16beta), h<=xi/(8E Lambda),
and E=Ebox^++L+Lambda. The specified dense scalar operation bound is
O(nsteps Ktime Kexp Dbox^3), with Ktime and Kexp as in P9 using E.
Per fresh trial, the finite-register gate count is conservatively
O(nsteps Dbox^2 nbox^2), plus input sampling/reset. A capped trial count
4 exp(2beta Lambda)log(1/zeta) gives failure probability at most zeta;
ceilings and at least one trial are understood. The final displacement
isometry costs O(Dout^2 nout^2) gates in this compilation, or its explicitly
supplied native controlled-displacement implementation cost.

Physical wall time requires the actual gate, number-state and reset rates;
none is assumed dimension-independent for an environmental oscillator.
Working precision includes log Dbox, beta Lambda, log(1/delta_f), all
step/mesh factors, coefficient bits and the frequency-bracketing oracle
cost. Computational N/time claims must include these inputs. There is
no paid/cloud service or physical access acquired through tooling here.

The equally informed control is dense finite Gibbs preparation of the
SAME compressed target, followed by the SAME displacement/output cutoff
and original encoder. It may avoid QBP retries. No superiority over this
control, or generic Gibbs preparation, has been established. The declared
dense acquisition has matrices with dimension at least2^N and pays

The construction's upper costs are not lower bounds on any other algorithm.
