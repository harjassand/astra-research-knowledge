R2 — A fixed-dimension, reference-preserving codec for bounded-spectrum Gaussian laws

Status and scope
----------------
This is a proved, one-observation codec for the family {N(0,Sigma): I <= Sigma <= A I} in a fixed dimension m. The encoder and decoder depend only on (m,A,epsilon), not on Sigma. It gives a uniform total-variation approximation to each conditional observation law and hence to the joint law with any latent Y whose conditional covariance belongs to the class. It does NOT bound I(Y;Z), the information in a family of scale-indexed observations, or the N75 scale integral. It is constructive partial progress on the assigned representation/codec question, not a resolution of N75.

The N75 card available to this worker describes a Gaussian observation with covariance I+f_L(T R(Y)^2). If that matrix is positive semidefinite and has the advertised uniform spectral cap, the theorem below applies separately at each fixed T with A equal to that cap. The full N75 source packet was unavailable here, so this is an explicitly conditional interface-level connection. The codec does not retain correlations among multiple T values. Conditional TV closeness alone cannot be promoted to mutual-information closeness for continuous Y; the scalar analytic Gaussian variance-field witnesses in c09_s01 and c09_s02 are a direct warning against that inference.

Reference transport and encoder/decoder
---------------------------------------
Let g(x)=prod_{i=1}^m [pi(1+x_i^2)]^{-1}, the product standard-Cauchy density on R^m. Map each coordinate to the unit circle by

  u_i = F_C(x_i) = 1/2 + arctan(x_i)/pi,   x_i = tan(pi(u_i-1/2)).

Thus the reference law g dx becomes uniform du on the torus T^m. For P_Sigma=N(0,Sigma), define its likelihood density relative to this uniform reference by

  q_Sigma(u) = p_Sigma(x(u))/g(x(u)).

For an integer J>=4 set h=1/J and let phi_j be the periodic triangular hat centered at jh, of height one and support radius h, for j in {0,...,J-1}. The hats are nonnegative, sum to one, and each integrates to h. In one coordinate the encoder emits label j with probability phi_j(u); the decoder emits v with density phi_j(v)/h. For m coordinates use the product channel and label vector (j_1,...,j_m), which has J^m possible labels. Equivalently, the decoded-reference Markov operator is

  K q(v) = sum_{j in {0,...,J-1}^m} [ integral_T^m q(u) prod_i phi_{j_i}(u_i) du / h^m ] prod_i phi_{j_i}(v_i).

This is positive, preserves total mass, and maps uniform measure exactly to uniform measure. After decoding, apply the inverse Cauchy map x(v). The output reference on R^m is therefore exactly g, regardless of the input law. Neither encoder nor decoder uses Sigma.

Uniform smoothness bound
------------------------
Write r=||x||_2 and ell=log q_Sigma. Since g is a product reference,

  a_i := partial_{x_i} ell = -(Sigma^{-1}x)_i + 2x_i/(1+x_i^2),
  a_i' := partial_{x_i} a_i = -Sigma^{-1}_{ii} + 2(1-x_i^2)/(1+x_i^2)^2.

The spectral assumption I<=Sigma<=A I gives |a_i|<=3r and |a_i'|<=3. With D_i=partial_{u_i}=pi(1+x_i^2)partial_{x_i},

  |D_i ell|^2 <= 9 pi^2(r^2+2r^4+r^6),
  |D_i^2 ell| <= pi^2(3+12r^2+9r^4).

Using D_i^2 q/q=(D_i ell)^2+D_i^2 ell,

  |D_i^2 q_Sigma|/q_Sigma <= pi^2(3+21r^2+27r^4+9r^6).

At every coordinate face u_i=0 or 1, q_Sigma and its first u_i derivative tend to zero: the Gaussian exponential tail dominates the polynomial Cauchy denominator and the factors from D_i. Thus q_Sigma has a periodic W^{2,1} extension coordinatewise. Also q_Sigma du=p_Sigma dx. For a centered Gaussian with covariance at most A I,

  E r^2 <= A m,
  E r^4 <= A^2 m(m+2),
  E r^6 <= A^3 m(m+2)(m+4).

Consequently, for every coordinate i,

  ||partial_{u_i}^2 q_Sigma||_1 <= M(A,m)
  := pi^2[3+21 A m+27 A^2 m(m+2)+9 A^3 m(m+2)(m+4)].

Approximation bound (including a conservative explicit constant)
---------------------------------------------------------------
In one coordinate K has symmetric kernel k(u,v)=h^{-1} sum_j phi_j(u)phi_j(v). For J>=4, its support has circular distance |v-u|<=2h, its first signed displacement moment is zero (the hats reproduce the local linear function), and k<=3/h because at most three centers contribute to a pair (u,v). Taylor's formula with integral remainder, followed by integration in u and the bound on k, gives

  ||K_i q-q||_1 <= (3/h) ||partial_i^2 q||_1 integral_{-2h}^{2h} delta^2 d delta
                  = 16 h^2 ||partial_i^2 q||_1.

The tensor product operators are L1 contractions. Telescoping their product therefore gives

  TV(K q_Sigma,q_Sigma) <= 8 J^{-2} sum_i ||partial_{u_i}^2 q_Sigma||_1
                         <= 8 m M(A,m)/J^2.

Choosing J=max(4, ceil(sqrt(8m M(A,m)/epsilon))) proves TV<=epsilon simultaneously for every Sigma in the spectral class. A single discrete label vector carries

  log_2 D = m log_2 J = (m/2)log_2(1/epsilon)+O_{A,m}(1)

bits for fixed A,m. This is an ideal-real-arithmetic construction: it assumes exact Cauchy CDF/inverse, exact hat probabilities, and decoder randomness. It counts the entire label vector; it does not omit any input-dependent labels.

Finite arithmetic check
-----------------------
`cauchy_gaussian_codec_bound.py` checks a parameter instance using only rational arithmetic and pi^2<484/49. Its JSON result records the exact integer J and verifies 8m M_upper/J^2<=epsilon. This check validates the label-count arithmetic only; the analytic derivative and Markov-kernel argument above are the proof. The resulting J can be large and the bound is not offered as a practical implementation cost.

Relation to the frozen work and cross-exposure
----------------------------------------------
The frozen INITIAL and R1 are unchanged. R2 is an independent conditional-law codec construction for bounded-spectrum Gaussian observations. The frozen c09 Sol baselines c09_s01/c09_s02 establish that generic scalar Gaussian variance channels can obstruct the broad covariance-observation integrated-MI step; this theorem does not make that step. c09_s03's admitted integrated bound concerns a different measure/interface. Available Luna baselines describe (i) a full-dimensional affine-likelihood posterior codec, (ii) an audit of the earlier codec interface, (iii) a shared-scalar Fourier witness, and (iv) an N75 plateau reconstruction with the exact source field still missing. R2 does not subsume those statements and claims no novelty or priority. Cross-exposure is recorded solely as provenance; no peer outputs are copied into the proof.

Next decisive gap
-----------------
To attack N75, one still needs a valid bound on information retained jointly across T, under the exact N75 channel and acquisition costs, or a precise obstruction for that exact field. This artifact supplies neither. A useful next experiment would test whether a multiscale version of the positive kernel can preserve a bounded collection of scale-conditioned laws at total label cost while controlling a joint metric strong enough to imply the desired mutual-information bound; that implication itself must be proved and must not be inferred from TV alone.
