# Passive physical realization of an entire symmetric response ball

Worker r09_algebra, 2026-10-06. Status: elementary universal construction. This is a finite resistor graph with a continuous finite-dimensional parameter family, not a finite model list and not a continuum PDE realization. It connects r02's statistical sphere-prior theorem to physical networks; that statistical proof is separate.

## Construction

Let input dimension d≥2 and n=d+1 terminals. Let U:R^d→H=1^⊥⊂R^n be a fixed isometry. Choose R>0 and τ>0 with

τR≤1/(4n²).

For every A∈Sym(d), ||A||_F≤R, define

F_A=n⁻¹I+τA,
Λ_A=U F_A⁻¹ Uᵀ.

The response F_A is SPD because τ||A||_op≤τR<n⁻¹. Λ_A annihilates constants, is symmetric, and is positive definite on H. At A=0,

Λ_0=nP_H=nI−11ᵀ,

whose off-diagonal entries are −1. The resolvent identity gives

||Λ_A−Λ_0||_op
 ≤τn²R/(1−τnR)
 ≤[1/4]/[1−1/(4n)]<1/3.

Consequently every off-diagonal entry of Λ_A is below −2/3. Assign the complete graph edge conductances

c_ij(A)=−(Λ_A)_ij>2/3  (i<j).

Since Λ_A has zero row sums, its diagonal entries equal the sum of incident conductances, so it is exactly this passive graph's Kirchhoff/Dirichlet-to-Neumann matrix. Its inverse on H is the current-to-voltage map, and in the chosen orthonormal coordinates it is exactly F_A. Physical boundary currents Ug have zero total current and norm ||g||; the zero-mean voltage detector corresponding to h is Uh and is unit whenever h is unit.

Thus the full symmetric response ball is realized with all positive conductances, known complete topology, n terminals, and n(n−1)/2 edges. It is generally nonplanar and has no hidden interior nodes. Baseline permutation symmetry does not restrict the unknown A; alternatives are fully nonradial.

## Connection to the statistical experiment

For a current g and unit detector h, subtracting the known baseline leaves the Gaussian scalar experiment

y−n⁻¹hᵀg=τhᵀAg+σz.

This is exactly the finite Sym(d) experiment of r02's `notes/r02_causal/cycle2/statistical_comparator.md`. Its sphere prior lies in the realized ball. Single-current lower bounds based on unobserved symmetric directions therefore concern actual networks here, under the supplied topology, response-contrast parameter metric, detector/noise and acquisition contracts. A current's orthogonal null space persists under arbitrary repeated detectors because every response depends on A only through Ag.

The construction does not automatically transfer a stated risk bound to Euclidean error in the raw conductance table. The response metric and conductance metric are related, but the constants depend on dimension and the response scale; they must be charged explicitly.

## Metric comparison if conductance error is required

Put b=1/n. Matrix inversion gives, for any A,B in the ball,

τ||A−B||_F/(b+τR)²
 ≤||Λ_A−Λ_B||_F
 ≤τ||A−B||_F/(b−τR)².

For any edge-conductance difference vector δc on the complete graph,

√2||δc||₂≤||δΛ||_F≤√(2n)||δc||₂.

The lower bound counts the two off-diagonal copies. For the upper bound, each diagonal entry is a sum of n−1 incident edge changes; Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the total diagonal squared norm by 2(n−1)||δc||₂², and the off-diagonal squared norm is 2||δc||₂². Therefore

τ||A−B||_F/[√(2n)(b+τR)²]
 ≤||c(A)−c(B)||₂
 ≤τ||A−B||_F/[√2(b−τR)²].

These are universal, explicit bi-Lipschitz bounds, not a dimension-independent metric equivalence. Define their lower and upper constants as l and L. Statistical risks in raw conductance geometry must charge these factors.

## Direct Bayes-risk transfer without a projection loss

For any observed data Y and independent posterior copies A,A' conditional on Y,

tr Cov(c(A)|Y)=½ E[||c(A)−c(A')||²|Y]
 ≥l²·½ E[||A−A'||_F²|Y]
 =l² tr Cov(A|Y).

Thus conductance Bayes risk is at least l² times A Bayes risk, without constructing an inverse estimator or incurring a projection factor four. This identity was pointed out and reconstructed by the inverse-team lead. In particular, grant any known random single-current design the noiseless exact vector Ag, an experiment more informative than noisy repeated detector readings of that same response. For the independent uniform sphere prior in Sym(d), parameter dimension p=d(d+1)/2, the unseen posterior variance is at least R²(p−d)/p. Consequently the raw conductance Bayes and minimax risks are at least

l²R²(p−d)/p.

The lower bound holds for a design sampled independently of A, with the realized design known to the estimator. A design chosen after observing A is outside that statistical interface. Conversely, a multi-current A estimator projected onto the supplied Frobenius ball can be mapped through c; its conductance risk is at most L² times its A-risk bound. All source, detector, noise and energy costs from r02's experiment remain charged. This is an actual finite-network random-design obstruction under explicit geometry, not a PDE realization.

The supplied model ball is an exact structural promise. The construction gives no acquisition algorithm for that model promise, no unknown-topology recovery, no hidden-interior uniqueness theorem, and no circular-planar or continuum conductivity transfer. Those are separate falsifiable extensions.
