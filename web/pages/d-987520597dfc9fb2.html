every fixed compact simple group. It assumes a supplied regular cone
a,b >= eta(a+b), a supplied degree-k (or conjugation-support) score promise,
and an actually compatible covariant bistochastic marginal on the same
V_(a,b), or a supplied SU(3)-closed experiment that permits group twirling.
It compares the score's own coherent-EB loss with the Hermitian Dirichlet

INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE TWO SU(3) WEYL-RATIO MOMENTS

Write N=a+b, d_(a,b)=(a+1)(b+1)(N+2)/2, and let v be the normalized
highest vector. In the Cartan-product realization,

  <v,U(g)v> = g_11^a det(g_[12],[12])^b
             = g_11^a conjugate(g_33)^b,       g in SU(3).

Thus the probability kernel k(g)=d_(a,b)|<v,U(g)v>|^2 is normalized by
Schur orthogonality. Multiplying its unnormalized density by |g_11|^2
is the squared highest matrix coefficient of the Cartan component
V_(a+1,b) in V_(a,b) tensor C^3. Multiplication by |g_33|^2 is the
corresponding highest coefficient in V_(a,b) tensor (C^3)^*, with top
weight (a,b+1). Schur orthogonality therefore gives exactly

  E_k |g_11|^2 = d_(a,b)/d_(a+1,b)
                 = (a+1)(N+2)/((a+2)(N+3)),
  E_k |g_33|^2 = d_(a,b)/d_(a,b+1)
                 = (b+1)(N+2)/((b+2)(N+3)).

Subtracting from one yields the displayed deficits
  (a+N+4)/((a+2)(N+3)) and (b+N+4)/((b+2)(N+3)).
Each is at most 1/(a+2)+1/(N+3), respectively
1/(b+2)+1/(N+3), since N+2 <= N+3. Hence

  E_k Delta <= J_(a,b)
  Delta=(1-|g_11|^2)+(1-|g_33|^2),
  J_(a,b)=1/(a+2)+1/(b+2)+2/(N+3).

On a supplied cone a,b>=eta N this is at most
2(1+1/eta)/N. These are exact finite identities and bounds. The ratio
moments are the candidate's derivation from the Weyl dimension formula and
Schur orthogonality; the primary Berezin source below supplies the general
kernel framework, not these two explicit SU(3) ratios.

INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE PROJECTIVE/BEREZIN TRANSFER

With Haar probability on G/T, L(A)(g)=Tr(P_g A) and normalized trace
tau=Tr/d, the adjoint is L^*f=d integral f(g)P_g dg. Therefore

  L^*L(A)=d integral Tr(P_g A)P_g dg = Psi(A),
  (LL^*f)(g)=d integral Tr(P_g P_h)f(h)dh,
  d Tr(P_g P_h)=d |<g v,h v>|^2.

This fixes the factors without an unstated sqrt(d). Positivity of Psi and
LL^*, and equality of their nonzero spectra, are then the ordinary
L^*L/LL^* identities. For a regular SU(3) weight, the projective orbit
stabilizer is T. The candidate's direct big-cell polarization argument
for injectivity is sound: lower-unipotent orbit vectors are polynomial in
three complex coordinates and span V_(a,b); QR/Iwasawa factorization makes
each a nonzero scalar multiple of a G-coherent vector. If
<v(z),A v(z)>=0 for all z, uniqueness of the real-analytic polynomial
coefficients polarizes it to <v(w),A v(z)>=0 for independent w,z. Since
the coordinate functions of v(z) are linearly independent, their separate
products are linearly independent and A=0. This is the needed injectivity
of L, not an inverse-norm estimate; it gives no quantitative conditioning.

For the full W-isotypic Berezin block, Peter-Weyl identifies the target
multiplicity space with W^T. Since k is bi-T-invariant,
K_W=int k(g) rho_W(g) dg acts there. Inversion symmetry makes it
selfadjoint; LL^* positivity makes it positive. A unit zero-weight vector
z is fixed by diagonal phase D. The 3x3 unitary identities give
1-|g_22|^2=|g_21|^2+|g_23|^2<=Delta and
||g-D||_HS^2=2 sum_i(1-|g_ii|)<=4 Delta. For W in End(V_(a,b)), the
SU(3) center acts trivially, so its highest weight has a U(3) lift with
integer coordinates summing to zero. Thus D acts trivially on W. For a
principal Hermitian logarithm gD^*=exp(iY), removing the scalar trace part
does not enlarge its Hilbert-Schmidt norm, and the Casimir bound gives
||d rho_W(Y_0)||_op^2<=2 c_W ||Y||_HS^2. Consequently

  (1/2)||rho_W(g)z-z||^2 <= pi^2 c_W Delta(g),
  0 <= I-K_W <= pi^2 c_W J_(a,b) I.

Because L is injective and intertwines the representations, every eigenvalue
of Psi on the entire W-isotypic domain is an eigenvalue of LL^* on the
whole W^T multiplicity space. This is the step that charges all domain
multiplicity embeddings and possible leakage; a scalar-on-one-copy
argument would be insufficient. The Casimir/logarithm constants and
regular-cone arithmetic reproduce the stated C_W and C_eta(k). I found no
factor error in these bounds.

BOUNDARY RESULT

The closest exact prior is the flag-manifold Berezin/Töplitz interface,
with the normalization caveat above. The exact Weyl-ratio moments, the
quantitative torus-distance bound on every coherent eigenvalue, and the
actual-compatible Dirichlet closure are distinct steps in the current
candidate. The targeted primary-source check found no theorem combining
actual two-output compatibility with this one fixed coherent EB map and a
finite-carrier, all-isotypic-multiplicity own-target comparison. This is
a bounded search finding, not a novelty or priority conclusion. No claim
for arbitrary simple G follows: the explicit two-entry/minor kernel,
3x3 torus geometry, and cone constant are SU(3)-specific. Historical
equivalence and external validation remain UNKNOWN.
