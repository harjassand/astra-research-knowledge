CLOSED CONDITIONAL COMPOSITION

The former GNS/modular restriction and qubit-only restriction are removed.
Faithfulness of tested rho is removed by finite regularization; faithfulness
of the fixed local sigma is retained. No minimum-eigenvalue factor is charged.

For joint states with B marginal sigma, genuine D becomes mutual information.
For varying B marginals, the extra D(rho_B||sigma) remains EXACTLY. Choosing
the untouched reference tau_R=rho_R on its support for each entropy calculation
does not choose a different comparator: both J and E cancel those reference
weights, while the local sigma/Phi/Psi are fixed throughout.

The simultaneous family theorem is in beta_sigma, the infimum over fixed-sigma
stationary KMS self-compatible channel marginals, and epsilon_sigma, the

The bound can alternatively be proved by near-optimal witnesses without
claiming any attainment. The C4 comparator of a beta-optimal channel is not
asserted to be epsilon-optimal.

GENUINE D CANNOT BE REPLACED BY OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS
The old commuting flagged example supplies an exact interface separation.

  rho_x=p0 vac+directsum_(n=1)^N p_n |x_n><x_n|.

argument gives R(E_N)<=4ln2. Each state's genuine D to sigma0 is

all these states commute and both raw errors are exactly zero.
