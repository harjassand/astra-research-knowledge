# Support-compression audit for CYCLE2

This is a linked addendum to [CYCLE2_REPORT.md](CYCLE2_REPORT.md), with a separate [claim record](SUPPORT_COMPRESSION_CLAIMS.json). The original report is unchanged. It supersedes only that report's implementation-status sentence that efficient explicit-support extraction was unknown: the sampler tree can be explored *support-synchronously*, keeping at most `m+1` atoms throughout. The feature guarantee, assumptions, and Li-signing dependency remain exactly those in CYCLE2.

## Audited conclusion

The proposed upgrade is valid. For `m=|E|`, it deterministically outputs at most `m+1` actual perfect matchings with nonnegative rational weights summing to one and exact edge-marginal vector `x`. Every output atom is retained from an actually generated branch, so the original pathwise feature certificate applies to it. The complete support construction takes polynomial bit time in the listed graph, feature, marginal, and level input sizes. It requires neither optimization nor separation. Constructing the law consumes no random bits; sampling from its output law can be done with exact rejection sampling in expected polynomially many fair bits.

The claim is conditional on the same source-reported Li signing algorithm used by CYCLE2. This audit does not validate that external signing implementation. It makes no novelty or priority claim: affine-dependence elimination is the standard constructive Carathéodory reduction, and the CYCLE2 source ledger remains the prior-art/source record.

## Synchronous residual cycle phase

Set `b=2^T`, `q=bx`, `ell=floor(q)`, and `r=q-ell`. Let `Q` be a common denominator of the input marginals. Every residual coordinate in the cycle process lies in `(1/Q)Z`, is in `[0,1]`, and every residual vertex degree is integral. Maintain a weighted support `S` of pairs `(r,lambda)`, initially the single pair `(r,1)`.

In one round, let `K` be the maximum number of fractional edges in any state in `S`. If `K=0`, stop. For each state with fractional edges, find a simple cycle in its fractional-support graph and branch to its two exact endpoint moves with probabilities `p=t_+/(t_+-t_-)` and `1-p`; leave an integral state fixed. Each child is feasible and its conditional mean is its parent. At least one fractional edge becomes integral at each endpoint. Thus, **before compression**, every child has at most `K-1` fractional edges: parents with `K` fractional edges lose one, parents with fewer already have at most `K-1`, and integral parents remain integral. The candidate list has at most `2(m+1)` entries.

Now compress that list by exact affine dependence, retaining only candidate states and preserving its total mass and vector mean. Retention cannot increase the maximum fractional-edge count. Repeating decreases that maximum by at least one per round, so there are at most `m` rounds. At termination `y_T=ell+r` is integral, has degree `b`, is edgewise floor/ceiling of `bx`, and the compressed law still has mean `bx`.

## Synchronous dyadic coarsening

At level `t`, each retained state `y` is an integer degree-`2^t` bipartite b-matching. Its odd-multiplicity edge graph is Eulerian, hence decomposes into edge-disjoint even cycles `C`. Choose the CYCLE2 alternating vector `delta_C`, put `w_C=A delta_C`, and set `U_t(y)=ceil(max_C ||w_C||_2)` (zero if there are no cycles). For nonzero scale, call the rational Li signer on the columns `w_C/U_t(y)`; for zero scale use any fixed signs. Put `v(y)=sum_C s_C delta_C`. Branch to both states

`y^+ = (y+v(y))/2` and `y^- = (y-v(y))/2`, each with half the parent's mass.

Both are nonnegative integer degree-`2^(t-1)` b-matchings. Indeed every odd edge is on exactly one cycle and changes by `+1` or `-1`, remaining nonnegative and even before division; even edges are unchanged; and each alternating cycle has zero degree change at every vertex. Their mean is `y/2`. The normalized feature increment on either branch is bounded by `99*2^-t*U_t(y)` in infinity norm. After branching **all** retained atoms, there are at most `2(m+1)` feasible children; exact affine compression returns to at most `m+1` existing children while preserving the global mean `2^(t-1)x`. Iterate to `t=0`. Degree-one integer outputs are actual perfect matchings.

The compression is applied to matching/b-matching **edge-coordinate vectors only**, in ambient dimension `m`; no feature rows need be added. It preserves the law's mean, not the original conditional coupling, which is unnecessary: each next transition is feasible and mean-preserving state by state. Every final atom has a concrete branch history because compression only deletes/reweights existing candidates. Therefore every retained atom obeys

`||A(1_M-x)||_infinity <= m*2^-T + 99*sum_{t=1}^T 2^-t U_t(path)`.

In particular its error is at most `m*2^-T + 99*max_path sum_t 2^-t U_t(path)` for the full, uncompressed sampler tree, or the coarser `m*2^-T+99m` under unit-norm actual-edge columns. Compression cannot worsen this all-atom bound. No graph-independent constant is inferred; CYCLE2's even-cycle obstruction still applies.

## Exact compression and bit-height proof

For a candidate list of `N>m+1` rational edge vectors `z_i` and positive rational weights `lambda_i`, form augmented columns `(1,z_i)`. Find a nonzero exact rational kernel vector; clear denominators to obtain a nonzero integer vector `c` satisfying `sum_i c_i=0` and `sum_i c_i z_i=0`. The first identity guarantees both signs occur. Choose a positive coordinate `j` minimizing `lambda_j/c_j`, and replace

`lambda_i <- lambda_i - (lambda_j/c_j)c_i`.

All weights remain nonnegative, their sum and weighted vector mean are unchanged, and at least one weight becomes zero. Delete zero-weight entries and repeat. This terminates with at most `m+1` **existing** candidate vectors, all with their original feasibility and path certificates.

Here is a conservative bit bound. Let `L_x` be the total binary input length of `x`; choose `Q` as the product of its denominators if an lcm is inconvenient, so `log Q <= L_x`. A residual endpoint is an integer multiple of `1/Q` in `[-1,1]`; its width has numerator at most `2Q`. Consequently each endpoint branch probability has reduced denominator at most `2Q`. With a common weight denominator `D`, branching at most `m+1` support states can use common denominator `D*product_i d_i`, adding at most `O(m(L_x+1))` bits per initial round and `O(m^2(L_x+1))` over at most `m` rounds.

For a compression, scale initial residual vectors by `Q`; their augmented integer matrix entries have magnitude at most `Q`. At dyadic level `t`, use the integer edge vectors `y` with entries at most `2^t`. A minimal dependent subset has at most `m+2` columns; its integer dependence can be taken from minors of an `(m+1)`-row matrix. Hadamard's determinant bound gives coefficient bit size `O(m(log H+log(m+1)))`, where `H=Q` in the initial phase and `H=2^t` at level `t`. Exact fraction-free elimination finds such a dependence in polynomial bit time. One elimination multiplies the common weight denominator by at most the largest dependence coefficient. There are at most `m+1` eliminations per compression, at most `m` initial compressions, and `T` dyadic compressions. Branching at dyadic levels adds only one denominator bit per level. Thus one conservative final common-denominator bit bound is

`O(m^3*(L_x+log(m+1)) + m^2*(T^2+T*log(m+1)) + T)`.

Since all output weights lie in `[0,1]`, their numerators have no more bits than this common denominator. This is polynomial in `m,L_x,T`; exact compression is not hiding exponentially large rational weights. The bound is deliberately conservative and is a bit-height bound, not a tight runtime estimate.

There are at most `m` residual rounds and `T` coarsening levels, with at most `m+1` active atoms, at most `2(m+1)` candidates per round, and at most `m+1` eliminations per compression. Cycle finding/decomposition is polynomial in `|V|+m`. The signer is called at most `(m+1)T` times, on rational cycle columns of bit length polynomial in `(m,d,L_A)`; Li's source theorem reports polynomial bit complexity. Hence total bit time is polynomial in `(m,|V|,d,L_x,L_A,T)`. The support-list output has at most `m+1` edge-ID matchings plus rational weights and exact atom certificates. To sample from that law, use `randbelow(D)` for a common denominator `D`; fair-bit rejection has expected `O(log D)` bits and unbounded worst-case bits.

## K2,2 vector-block corollary

For `r` disjoint `K2,2` blocks, let `z_i=1` denote the diagonal matching in block `i`, and assign feature column `+v_i/2` to each diagonal edge and `-v_i/2` to each off-diagonal edge, where `||v_i||_2<=1`. The four edge marginals in a block are `(p_i,p_i,1-p_i,1-p_i)`, and the actual-edge feature discrepancy is exactly

`A(1_M-x)=2 sum_i (z_i-p_i)v_i`.

At any dyadic state in one block, the four integer edge numerators have equal parity: the four vertex degree equations modulo two imply this. Thus its odd support is either empty or the whole 4-cycle. With alternating orientation chosen to agree with the edge-feature signs, `A delta_C=2v_i`; in either orientation its norm is at most 2. Every active cycle signing call can therefore use `U_t=2`, and Li gives per-level actual-edge feature increment `<198*2^-t`. The normalized block feature `sum_i(z_i-p_i)v_i` has half this error, hence per-level increment `<99*2^-t`.

For the initial residual phase, each of the four columns in block `i` has norm `||v_i||_2/2<=1/2`, so the direct coordinate-rounding estimate gives actual-edge error at most `2r*2^-T`; after dividing by two, the block-feature error is at most `r*2^-T`. Choose `T=ceil(log2(2r))`, which makes this at most `1/2`. The complete block-feature error is therefore strictly below `1/2+99*sum_{t=1}^T2^-t<99.5` for every atom.

The support can be compressed in the `r` block coordinates `z`, not the `4r` ambient edge coordinates: every feasible dyadic point in a block is determined by its diagonal numerator, with the off-diagonal numerator its complement. Affine elimination in `Q^r` preserves `E z=p`, and hence all four edge marginals, while retaining only safe generated candidates. The synchronous residual phase needs at most `r` rounds here because a fractional block is exactly one 4-cycle and one endpoint move makes that block integral; each coarsening level uses one compression. The resulting explicit law has at most `r+1` matching atoms. This is a proof-only specialization of the present audit: it is conditional on Li's signing theorem and is not implemented or finite-tested here.

## Scope and checks

The algorithm uses exact degree/nonnegativity checks, graph searches, rational cycle arithmetic, rational signing, and exact affine elimination. It uses no matching optimization or separation oracle; for a bipartite graph, the supplied degree-one feasibility constraints are the complete perfect-matching-polytope description. It outputs explicit atoms, not a compressed sampler oracle.

Prior-art boundaries and primary checks for the residual cycle rounder and Li signing primitive are recorded in [CYCLE2_SOURCES.md](CYCLE2_SOURCES.md). The cited dependent-rounding work is context for the cycle phase; the support-compression extension is an elementary exact Carathéodory implementation and no novelty claim is made. The result-113 source distinction belongs to the separate release audit and is not used as support for this claim.

Finite tests in `cycle2_smoke.py` test only the earlier single-path wrapper; they do not test this explicit-support implementation, which is specified/proved here but not yet coded. A direct implementation test remains a validation gate, separate from the mathematical bit-height argument.
