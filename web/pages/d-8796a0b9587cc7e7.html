## Primary kinetic-mechanism paper and code

Katherine M. Xiang et al., “Mechanism of Giant Magnetic Field Effect in a Red Fluorescent Protein,” *Journal of the American Chemical Society* 147 (2025), 18088–18099, [doi:10.1021/jacs.5c03997](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c03997). Author-hosted article PDF: [Xiang et al. 2025 JACS PDF](https://cohenweb.rc.fas.harvard.edu/Publications/Xiang_MFE_mechanism_JACS_2025.pdf).

- Figure 6 and its mechanism caption (printed p. 18093 / PDF p. 6): triplet SCRP formation, singlet recombination, triplet separation to a nonfluorescent radical, and FMN reoxidation. The accompanying text defines `β` as the separation fraction and `1−β` as the recombination fraction.
- Table 1 (printed p. 18094 / PDF p. 7): model rate parameters including `kox=3375 M^-1 s^-1`, `kred=5×10^7 M^-1 s^-1`, `kdis=5×10^9 M^-1 s^-1`, and in-vitro `β0=.81`, `βsat=.93`. The paper's Supporting Information (printed SI p. 17 / PDF p. 29) states that the radical anion is nonfluorescent under 561-nm excitation and reports a 3% constant fluorescence offset.
- The authors' public supporting-code repository is [adamcohenlab/xiang2025mfe](https://github.com/adamcohenlab/xiang2025mfe), cloned at commit [`74c48845428f8d0340a88968080cb4fecec4d94e`](https://github.com/adamcohenlab/xiang2025mfe/tree/74c48845428f8d0340a88968080cb4fecec4d94e). `MFE_kinetic_model.m` lines 99–104 give the six-state ODE; its parameter comments label both β values as separation fractions. `SCRP_spin_and_chem_sim.m` lines 176–177 computes β as separated events divided by separated plus recombined events. Local copies are under `sources/xiang2025mfe/`.
