LEAF 06 | Fermat ceiling for degree/smoothness determinant bounds
Date: 2026-10-08
Work label: reconstructed (direct determinant identity); no external review

Outcome
-------
For every r,d >= 2 over C, the smooth Fermat form F_{r,d}=sum_{i=1}^d x_i^r has an exact affine-linear determinant representation of order 1+d(r-1). The proof and sparse matrix are in proof.md.

Therefore degree, projective smoothness, and essential dimension alone cannot give a universal smooth-form lower bound omega(rd). Applied to an affine restriction of Perm_m, degree <= m and essential dimension <= m^2, so this route has a cubic ceiling. This supports N79's no-supercubic warning. It does NOT refute a cubic lower bound: the September 2026 Family 108 preprint claims exactly an rd-scale bound and is consistent with this example.

Candidate result and validation status
--------------------------------------
Family 108 claims bdc(Perm_m) >= m^3/(5,529,600 e) for m >= 1408, and the same exact lower bound, by extracting a smooth degree-Theta(m) form in Theta(m^2) variables as an affine projection. Its general smooth-form estimate is (r-1)(d-1)/(4e).

The candidate is not independently certified here. The release page says no expert review and no independent Lean build. At pinned release commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a, both public Lean declarations end in `by sorry`:

Falsifier / boundary
--------------------
For a proposed universal smooth-form estimate L(r,d), the displayed determinant falsifies any claim L(r,d) > 1+d(r-1) on this family; in particular it blocks omega(rd). It does not falsify L(r,d)=c rd for a fixed constant c, nor the cubic permanent claim. The direct determinant identity is exact over C and has O(rd) nonzero coefficients of height 1; determinant evaluation time is outside the determinantal-complexity measure.
