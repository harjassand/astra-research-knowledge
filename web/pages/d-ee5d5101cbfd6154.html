# Cell lineage graph/control lane — final report

## Verdict

A one-cell transition graph and even the mean offspring matrix do not, in general, identify clone-level regeneration reachability or finite-horizon safety. The missing state is the joint offspring law at a division. I derived an exact two-type Galton–Watson counterexample: with marginal probability `p=0.6` that each daughter remains a regenerative progenitor, two models with the same per-daughter graph and the same expected 1.2 progenitor daughters have eventual progenitor-clone survival probabilities `1/3` and `5/9`. Their one-division probability of any progenitor daughter is `0.60` versus `0.84`. If the other fate is unsafe, probability of at least one unsafe daughter is `0.40` versus `0.64`.

This is rigorous as a theorem about the stated branching models. It says nothing by itself about real organoids, tumors, or clinical control. The mechanism is standard branching-process theory, so I make no novelty claim. The potentially useful application result is a quantitative model-contract/falsification test: acquire and compare sister-pair offspring distributions, rather than treating a cell-state Markov matrix as sufficient.

## Proposed quantitative assay

Use the published human cerebral-organoid depletion/rescue system: barcode wild-type and puromycin-resistant cells, use untreated mixed organoids and day-11 puromycin at 10% and 20% resistant inputs, and classify both daughters at each tracked progenitor division alongside barcode, clone size, total tissue size, and progenitor/neuron composition. The cited study reports that ~10% resistant cells nearly restored organoid size, and shows lineage-output plasticity under WT depletion. Add live lineage imaging or a validated paired-daughter measurement; snapshot barcode/scRNA-seq alone does not establish sister outcomes.

Pre-register a finite-horizon safety endpoint and require a measured cap `H` on at-risk divisions. Given an upper bound `epsilon_U` on unsafe fate per daughter and `b=2`, a dependence-robust sufficient gate is `H b epsilon_U <= eta`. In the toy p=.6 contrast, independent daughters predict mixed-sister probability .48; common-fate sisters predict zero. Five independent divisions give a 96.2% chance of observing at least one mixed pair under the independent model, but this is only a toy-model design floor, not a powered organoid study. Cluster inference by clone and organoid.

## Prior art and status

The mathematical mechanism is not novel: CLiNC (PNAS 2020) formulates lineage branching with a joint daughter-state distribution; single-cell B-cell pedigrees report strong within-family dependence; 2024 organoid work demonstrates broad clone-size heterogeneity and replenishment after depletion; 2025 cancer lineage modelling infers distinct resistance routes from barcode/population time series. A decisive literature gate remains if root wants a novelty claim for the control consequence.

Astra N35/N34/N50 offer adjacent reaction-network certificate machinery, not a cell-lineage theorem. N35 is a finite-horizon result under supplied bounded molecular jump/rate/polytope assumptions; it is explicitly not infinite-time permanence and does not encode the daughter joint law automatically. The lineage counterexample was derived independently from the offspring PGFs.
