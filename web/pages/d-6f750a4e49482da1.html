## Constants and computability

Use C,T,η,R from GENERAL_GRAPH_CANDIDATE.md, with T=C^L e^C/L!, L=d0(u,v)≥2. Choose a positive dyadic rational κ satisfying

κ≤min{1,log(5/4)/C,T^(−1/(L−1))}.

The finite known constants allow such a κ to be computed; taking a smaller dyadic κ changes constants, not exponents. For β_k=2^(−k−1), choose the exactly dyadic calibrated lag

t_k=κ 2^(−ceil((k+1)/(L−1))).

Then (κ/2)β_k^(1/(L−1))≤t_k≤κβ_k^(1/(L−1)). The established bounds imply, uniformly for every actual b∈[0,1],

r_b(t)=P_b(t)uv≤bt+β_k t_k,

r′_b(t_k)≥t_k/2.

The first uses T t_k^(L−1)≤β_k; the second uses Ct_k≤log(5/4). These inequalities do not require b to belong to the current stage's scale bracket.

The threshold r_(1.5β_k)(t_k) is computable from supplied Q0. Use a certified approximation c_k with error at most β_k t_k/32. This turns every decision into a finite comparison between an observed rational frequency and a certified numerical number; exact equality does not require an unbounded real-number comparison. If Q0 is given as exact rational rates, uniformization gives a direct effective evaluator: Q=ρ(A−I) with nonnegative stochastic A, rational ρ≥max escape; P(t)=e^(−ρt)Σ_j(ρt)^jA^j/j!. Rational partial sums and a certified exponential interval bound each entry, and the Poisson tail bounds the remainder. Increasing truncation/precision until interval width≤β_k t_k/32 terminates. Computable rates with certified precision oracles also suffice. An unverified float matrix is not a probability certificate.

## Observable stopping policy

Fix 0<δ≤1/2 and set δ_k=δ/[4(k+1)²]. At stage k:

1. Continue the actual process at fixed lag t_k, collecting the next

   m_k=ceil[2048 log(1/δ_k)/(β_k t_k)]

   transitions whose observed initial label is u. Every intervening endpoint reading is counted.
2. Let z_k be the proportion ending at v.
3. If z_k>c_k, stop the scale search and return the bracket

   B_k=[β_k,min{4β_k,1}].

   Otherwise proceed to k+1. All source transitions at the next stage are fresh.

The policy uses only endpoint labels, calibrated known lags and certified model probabilities. It does not use the unknown b, unseen jumps, a hidden clock record, or free state resets. Given the retained history before a stage, its source-u transition outcomes are iid Bernoulli(r_b(t_k)), by the outgoing-stack argument.

## Uniform classification bounds

If b≤β_k, then r_b≤2β_k t_k, while

c_k−r_b≥(1.5β_k−b)t_k/2−β_k t_k/32≥β_k t_k/8.

Bernoulli Bernstein with this conservative margin gives false stopping probability at most

exp[−3m_k β_k t_k/784]≤δ_k.

If b≥2β_k, then r_b≤1.5b t_k and

r_b−c_k≥(b−1.5β_k)t_k/2−β_k t_k/32≥b t_k/16.

Here c_k>0, so the downward margin is at most r_b; using 2r_b+(2/3)margin≤(8/3)r_b≤4bt_k in Bernstein yields

Pr(continue at stage k | its retained starting history)≤exp[−m_k b t_k/1024]≤δ_k^(2b/β_k).

In particular this is ≤δ_k. No correctness claim is required in the indifference region β_k<b<2β_k.

By a union bound, all required out-of-indifference classifications are correct with probability at least

1−Σ_k δ_k=1−δπ²/24>1−δ/2.

On this event, stopping at K implies b>β_K. If K>0, the previous continuation implies b<2β_(K−1)=4β_K. If K=0, the initial envelope b≤1 already supplies the upper endpoint. Thus the returned B_K contains b. This is a scale bracket acquired from observations, not side information supplied to the final estimator.

## Almost sure termination and expected full cost

Let k_* be the first index with β_k≤b/2. It exists for every b>0, and β_k* lies in (b/4,b/2], except the endpoint b=1 where k_*=0 and β_0=b/2. For j≥1, reaching stage k_*+j requires continuing at the preceding stage, so the uniform conditional bound gives

Pr(reach k_*+j)≤(δ/4)^(2^(j+1)).

This bound does not condition on scale-search correctness. The tail is doubly exponential in j, while stage cost grows only geometrically; it proves almost sure stopping and controls failed-scale paths in expectation.

Source acquisition at a fixed stage has the same uniform bound as before. Let A=1+2(R+1)/η. From an arbitrary starting endpoint label,

E[stage readings | its starting history]≤ceil(1/t_k)/η+A m_k.

All waits are t_k, so physical time is this count multiplied by t_k. Summing costs before k_* and using the above tail afterwards gives finite constants depending only on the supplied baseline such that

E_b N_scale≤C_Q b^(−L/(L−1)) [log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)],

E_b T_scale≤C'_Q b^−1 [log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)].

Proof of summability: t_k is within a fixed factor of β_k^(1/(L−1)); m_k is bounded by a constant times β_k^(−L/(L−1))[log(4/δ)+2log(k+1)]. The geometric sum through k_* is dominated by the last term. For tail j, multiply this stage cost by (δ/4)^(2^(j+1)); sums of 2^(pj) times that tail and log(j+1) converge for every fixed p=L/(L−1). The first-hit term is O(β_k^(−1/(L−1))), of lower order. Multiplying by t_k leaves O(β_k^−1) stage time plus a bounded startup term, and the same summation applies. Constants are not claimed practical or uniform in n, graph rates or mixing.

The local scale was acquired at an extra log log(1/b) confidence cost. This is an upper bound; the logarithm's necessity is not established. The procedure can stop incorrectly on its δ event, but those paths are included in the unconditional expected-cost bound. A fixed hard cap independent of b cannot succeed uniformly over all b>0, consistently with the prior lower bound.

## Final rate confidence interval

After stopping, keep its β=β_K,t=t_K and collect a fresh source-u block. Conditional on the scale bracket being correct, r_b≤5βt on [β,min{4β,1}] and r′_b≥t/2. For chosen 0<ε_rel≤1, put u=ε_rel βt/2 and collect

m_final=ceil[42 log(4/δ)/(ε_rel² βt)].

Bernstein gives |z_final−r_b|≤u with conditional probability at least1−δ/2. Define the observable confidence set

C_b={x∈[β,min{4β,1}]: |r_x(t)−z_final|≤u}.

It is an interval because r_x is strictly increasing. Its endpoints follow by monotone inversion of z_final±u with clipping to the bracket. On the joint good event it contains b; its diameter is at most4u/t=2ε_relβ. Any midpoint therefore has error at most ε_relβ≤ε_rel b. The coverage, including scale search, exceeds1−δ. If the set is empty, report the empty confidence set and the observed inconsistency; do not silently claim a point estimate. A practical interval evaluator can enlarge u by a certified probability-evaluation tolerance and increase m_final by the corresponding constant safety factor; the read/time orders persist. The displayed42 constant uses exact arithmetic for this final interval, whereas the scale stopping threshold already permits explicit approximation error.

Summing final-block costs over the random K with the same doubly exponential tail yields

E_b N_total≤C_Q ε_rel^(−2)b^(−L/(L−1)) [log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)],

E_b T_total≤C'_Q ε_rel^(−2)b^−1 [log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)].

Thus no local rate bracket remains supplied. The known baseline, support, passive full-state sensor and calibrated time remain supplied. This is matching polynomial order, up to the displayed logarithm, rather than an optimal-constant result.

## Observable entropy-production stopping

Under the reciprocal-support hypotheses in GENERAL_GRAPH_CANDIDATE.md, stationary EP is a known computable function σ(Q_x) of x>0. For rational Q0, stationary π(x) is a rational function of x (e.g. matrix-tree formula), and σ is a finite sum of rational functions times logarithms. The acquired interval C_b stays away from zero. Certified interval arithmetic can therefore produce an enclosure C_σ of {σ(Q_x):x∈C_b}. Report this enclosure as the data-supported result.

For a specified additive EP tolerance ε, use a refinement confidence schedule **instead of** the fixed final-block δ/2 allocation above, starting with δ_final,j=δ/[4(j+1)²] at j=0. If the intersection of confidence sets becomes empty, terminate immediately with the explicit result `INCONSISTENT/EMPTY`; do not keep sampling indefinitely on this failure event. Otherwise stop final refinement when the computed enclosure has width≤2ε. If still wider, double the fresh/accumulated source sample count, constructing C_b,j by the corresponding Bernoulli bound and intersecting valid confidence sets. The scale and refinement union budgets total δπ²/12<δ, preserving coverage; do not add this schedule on top of the fixed-block δ/2 budget. Interval computations must be refined too; arithmetic looseness is not evidence of physical uncertainty. This is an observable stopping rule requiring neither an unknown b nor a supplied entropy derivative bound. The explicit empty-set exit was added following r08's adversarial check; without it positive-probability inconsistent intersections could lead to infinite unconditional sampling cost.
