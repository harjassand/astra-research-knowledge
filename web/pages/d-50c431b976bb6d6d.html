## Independently reconstructed log-mass argument

    H=4diag(Qm)+8YQY,
    K=Y^-1 H Y^-1
      =12I-8B-4diag(sum_j B_ij exp(z_j-z_i)),
    h=(prod_i m_i)det K.

For every fixed direction u, K'' is diagonal with entries

    -4 sum_j B_ij exp(z_j-z_i)(u_j-u_i)^2<=0.

Thus K is Loewner-concave and the strict domain K>0 is convex. On it

    d^2 logdet K=tr(K^-1 K'')
                 -tr(K^-1 K' K^-1 K')<=0.

The first term is nonpositive by congruence; the second is the negative square of the Frobenius norm of K^-1/2 K' K^-1/2. Therefore log h(e^z)=sum z_i+logdet K is concave. This is a log-mass assertion. It does not revive the false unrestricted linear-mass root-concavity route.

    Hess_z log f=2(C+diag(c))/f-4cc^T/f^2.

    (c^T u)^2<=f u^T C u,
    (c^T u)^2<=f sum_i c_i u_i^2.

For the first, c=C1 and ||1||_C^2=f; equality forces u constant because C>0. The second uses the positive weighted diagonal inner product. Adding them proves positive semidefiniteness with kernel precisely span{1}. Consequently
