BLIND BASELINE NOTATION CLARIFICATION
2026-10-09. Saved with the first baseline freeze; no competing proof read.

For rectangular channels B and T_n, the symbol dagger in the baseline
means the standard UNNORMALIZED-TRACE Heisenberg dual:
Tr[T_n(X)Y]=Tr[X T_n^dagger(Y)]. Thus T_n^dagger is unital CP for
CPTP T_n. It is not the adjoint using different normalized input/output
traces. For Phi:M_d->M_d, normalized and unnormalized HS adjoints agree,
so HS self-adjointness gives the required Phi^dagger=Phi. All normalized
traces tau in the covariance proof refer to the one-register input or
one-register leaf M_d. Global output moments use Tr[S_n .] with S_n
an ordinary trace-one state. There is no suppressed output-dimension
factor in U4-U10.
