Primary sources opened 2026-10-08

- Raz and Shpilka, “Deterministic Polynomial Identity Testing in Non-Commutative Models” (2005), author PDF https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~shpilka/publications/RazShpilka_PIT.pdf: abstract; introduction on formal commutative versus noncommutative identity, lines 36–45; noncommutative ABP definition/path order, lines 133–144; deterministic NC ABP PIT claim, abstract lines 4–13 and section 2. This establishes the algorithm used by the restricted bridge.
- Derksen and Makam, “On non-commutative rank and tensor rank” (arXiv:1606.06701, 2016), https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06701: introduction lines 11–22 defines commutative rank over the rational function field and noncommutative rank over the free skew field, and gives an odd-skew example with different ranks; abstract lines 3–4 states ratios approaching 2.
- Fortin and Reutenauer, “Commutative/Noncommutative Rank of Linear Matrices and Subspaces of Matrices of Low Rank” (2004), https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/s52reut.pdf: author-hosted paper, sections 2–3. Read the free-field definition and rank characterization (PDF pp. 3–5), the inequality crank ≤ ncrank ≤ 2 crank (p. 6), and the infinite-field link between commutative rank and maximum scalar-substitution rank (p. 9). This is the primary rank-comparison framework.
- Lakhani and Mukhopadhyay, “Hitting point for sparse noncommutative polynomials,” ECCC TR26-120 revision 2 (2026-07-20), https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/120/: abstract and revision record, lines 26–42 and 63–78. States the sparse NC hitting tuple, matrix dimension s, entry-bit bound O(s log n), and matrix-algebra one-query interface. N68 is routed through this theorem, not used outside its sparse free-algebra interface.
