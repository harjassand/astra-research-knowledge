## 2. Discrete filtered high tail, including its diagonal

The series is absolutely convergent because sum_l (2l+1)(1+lambda_l)^(-a)<infinity. Every diagonal pair is present. There is no replacement by a continuum operator.

Put R_j=2^j L, chi_0(u)=h(u/(2L)), and for j>=1 put

    |a_j^(q)(u)|<=D_a R_j^(-2a-q).                    (G9)
