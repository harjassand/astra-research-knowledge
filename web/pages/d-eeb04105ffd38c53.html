## Supplied interface and decisive outcome

Let A=C[x,y]. Allow finite words in the two fixed-axis generators

`(rho,eta)->(rho+F(b,eta),eta)` and
`(rho,eta)->(rho,eta+H(b,rho))`,

where each tail has polynomial base coefficients and globally vanishing
constant and first fibre coefficients. Delete zero tails and add adjacent
same-axis tails, including exact cancellation. A nonempty reduced word
alternates axes and each remaining tail has fibre degree at least two.
These polynomial operations do not introduce an inverse or divide by a
base coefficient.

For the prescribed acquired D2, `G=(2y,4x)D2`, every potential

`psi=h(b)+G1(b)Theta1+G2(b)Theta2`

has nonconstant full four-dimensional Hessian determinant, for every
such reduced word, including zero and one letter, and arbitrary base h.
The proof is all-degree and all finite lengths in this exact subgroup.
It does not classify all polynomial fibre automorphisms, words with
intervening arbitrary linear/conjugation maps, changed first jets,
base/pivot-moving charts, or arbitrary direct potential repairs.

## Independent leading-degree argument for arbitrary length

Suppose the first tail is rho+=F(b,eta), and undo it by the global map
`Phi(b,s,eta)=(b,s-F(b,eta),eta)`. The next letter updates eta to
`Z=eta+H2(b,s)`, with degree d2>=2 in s. After this, every remaining
coordinate is polynomial in (s,Z), with weights wt(s)=1, wt(Z)=d2.
At each subsequent alternating letter, the tail degree multiplies the
dominant coordinate degree; the old coordinate added has strictly
smaller degree and cannot cancel the leading term. Over the polynomial
domain, nonzero leading base coefficients remain nonzero under products
and powers. Vanishing on some special base fibre is irrelevant to this
global coefficient identity and is not excluded by fiat.

If the last updated degree is D, it is the product of all degrees after
the first letter, and D>=2. The other output has strictly smaller degree.
Its coefficient cannot cancel the dominant output in the potential,
because G1,G2 are nonzero polynomials. Thus, for the transformed
potential V,

`V=E(b)s^D+Q(b)s^(D-1)+...`,

where E is nonzero and is divisible by the coefficient G_i of the last
updated output. The derivative operator partial_eta is partial_Z after
the substitution and drops weighted degree by d2. Hence

`deg_s V_eta<=D-d2`, `deg_s V_etaeta<=D-2d2`.

The zero polynomial has degree minus infinity. These inequalities also
show that the first two coefficients E,Q are independent of eta.
Base derivatives do not increase s degree. This is a uniform induction,
including zero/subleading coefficients; it does not rely on a selected
word fixture or an automorphism normal-form theorem over C(x,y).

## Full nonlinear chain rule and unique highest determinant term

The exact congruence is

`JPhi^T Hess_old(psi) JPhi=Hess_new(V)+V_s Hess_(b,eta)(F)`.

The correction has zero s row and column. In the order (x,y,s,eta),
the leading degrees of the corrected matrix are

```
base-base: D,      base-s: D-1,       s-s: D-2,
base-eta: <=D-1,  s-eta: <=D-d2-1,   eta-eta: D-1.
```

The eta-eta leading coefficient is D E F_etaeta. All determinant
permutations not fixing eta have degree at most4D-4. The only terms at
degree4D-3 fix eta and use the (b,s)3-by-3 block. Its generic leading
coefficient matrix is

```
[[E_xx,E_xy,D E_x],
 [E_xy,E_yy,D E_y],
 [D E_x,D E_y,D(D-1)E]].
```

Its determinant is D M_D(E), where

`M_D(E)=(D-1)E det Hess_b(E)-D gradE^T adj(Hess_b(E))gradE`.

Therefore the literal highest coefficient is

`[s^(4D-3)]detH=D^2 E F_etaeta M_D(E)`.

All factors E and F_etaeta are nonzero. The independently proven
cycle15 cylinder lemma says M_D(E)=0 iff E is a polynomial in one
affine-linear form. Every divisor of such an E is a product of parallel
affine linear factors, so G_i itself would be cylindrical. No machine
factorization is needed: coefficient vectors of grad G_i have rank at
most one precisely in the cylindrical case.

For the fixed D2, coefficients at x^2 and xy give minor -2 for G1
and -8 for G2. Both fail this necessary condition. This proves the
theorem for every reduced word of length at least two. If the word
begins with the other axis, a constant fibre permutation gives the same
argument, without a rational coordinate substitution.

One letter gives coefficient `-G_i tail'' K_(G_i)` on the affine
residual coordinate, where
`K_Q=Q_y^2 Q_xx-2Q_xQ_yQ_xy+Q_x^2Q_yy`.
At the origin K_G1=-3700 and K_G2=9248; neither polynomial is zero.
The empty word gives `(JacG)^2`, with

`JacG=-4[(x^2+8x+17)y+x^3+10x^2+25x+2]`,

which is nonconstant. These cover every cancellation/degenerate reduced
length, rather than applying the D>=2 argument to a zero tail.

## The next coefficient when the cylinder filter passes

For a changed first jet, suppose the last coefficient E is a nonconstant
cylindrical polynomial. Use only a constant affine base frame to write
E=E(x), and keep Q=[s^(D-1)]V independent of eta. Define

`J_D(E)=D(E')^2-(D-1)E E''`.

If e=deg_x E>0, its highest coefficient has nonzero factor
e(e+D-1). At degree4D-4, only the leading (x,s) block and the
next (y,eta) block can contribute. The former determinant coefficient
is -D J_D(E). The latter is

`D E F_etaeta(Q_yy+D E F_yy)-D^2E^2(F_yeta)^2`.

Thus the independently reconstructed coefficient is

`-D^2 E J_D(E) [D E detHess_(y,eta)(F)+F_etaeta Q_yy]`.

Generic lower x/y/s mixed jets and s-eta terms enter only at lower
degree. A separate generic formal-degree checker tests this exact
coefficient including arbitrary such jets; it is not inferred from a
finite-degree word.

Since F and F_eta vanish identically at eta=0, let r>=2 be the
least eta power of F. In the identity forced by this coefficient,
detHess_(y,eta)F has eta order at least2r-2, whereas
F_etaeta Q_yy/(D E) has order r-2 if Q_yy is nonzero. Hence Q_yy=0
over C(x), and detHess_(y,eta)F=0.

To conclude F_y=0 without a Hesse prerequisite, suppose m=deg_y F>=1
and write its leading y coefficient as A(eta). It has positive eta
degree n>=2. The coefficient of y^(2m-2) in the zero determinant is

`m[(m-1)AA''-m(A')^2]`.

Its highest eta coefficient has nonzero factor -m n(n+m-1), a
contradiction. Thus the first shear is independent of the transverse
base y, and Q is affine in y. This is necessary only, requires
nonconstant E, and makes no assertion when E is a constant unit.
