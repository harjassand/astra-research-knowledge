7 October 2026. These are internal derivations. No novelty or external validation claim.

1. TAIL-REPAIRED SINGULAR OSGOOD COMPARISON

Let V:[0,T] -> [0,M0] be absolutely continuous, V(0)=0. At each 0<t<t0,
let Y_t,Y'_t be arbitrarily coupled real random variables. Assume constants
alpha,a,K,C,M>0, 0<=gamma<1, independent of t, such that

  E|Y_t|^p + E|Y'_t|^p <= 2 exp(K p^2) t^(alpha p), all integers p>=1;
  |Y_t-Y'_t|<=M a.s.; E|Y_t-Y'_t|^2<=C V(t).

Let g be globally Lipschitz and satisfy |g'(y)|<=C |y|^a for |y|<=1.
Write S(t)=E|g(Y_t)-g(Y'_t)|^2. Suppose, a.e. on the positivity set,

  V'(t) <= C[(1+t^(-gamma)) V(t)+S(t)/t],

and that V'<=C_T V[1+log(K0/V)] at later positive times, for K0>max(1,M0).
Then V identically vanishes. No independence
between Y and Y' is needed.

Proof. Set L=log(1/t), choose p=floor(alpha L/(4K)), and apply Markov:

  P(|Y|>t^(alpha/2)) <= exp(Kp^2-alpha Lp/2) <= exp(-c L^2)

for sufficiently small t; likewise Y'. On the event where both fields are
at most t^(alpha/2), the squared derivative of g is at most C t^(alpha a),
so that S_good<=C t^(alpha a) V. On its complement, Lipschitzness gives
CV while the deterministic difference bound and the marginal tail give
C exp(-c L^2). Thus

  S <= C t^(alpha a) V + min(CV,C exp(-cL^2)).

Fix K0>max(1,M0) and put I=1+log(K0/V)>1 when V>0. Splitting at
V=exp(-cL^2/2) yields min(CV,C exp(-cL^2))<=C V I/L^2. Consequently

  V'<=k(t)V I,
  k(t)=C[1+t^(-gamma)+t^(alpha a-1)+1/(t log^2(1/t))].

All four terms are integrable at zero. On a positivity interval,
(log I)'=-V'/(VI)>=-k. Taking its left endpoint, where V approaches zero,
contradicts boundedness of log I. This proves the conclusion. For an initial
error V(s)<=delta>0, the same comparison yields

  V(t) <= (e K0)^(1-eta) delta^eta,
  eta=exp(-integral_s^t k(u)du).

This is a quantitative stability consequence once k is acquired. Flatness
is substantive: without a>0, V(t)=t^c solves V'=cV/t and starts from zero.
Taking Y=0,Y'=t^(c/2),g(y)=y meets the moment/difference assumptions but
allows this nonzero discrepancy. The singular comparison alone is insufficient.

Family 236 uses alpha=1/4,a=1,gamma=3/4, with an additional field/site
comparison. Its main infinite-tree theorem is NOT imported by this lemma.

2. POTTS EDGE/FUSION COORDINATE OBSTRUCTION AND EXACT EDGE REPAIR

Fix q>=2 and 0<theta<1. Set T=1^perp in R^q, P=I-11^T/q,
K_theta=theta I+(1-theta)11^T/q. The log-message update is

  psi(h)=P log(K_theta exp(h)), h in T,

where exp and log act coordinatewise. Taylor expansion at zero gives

  psi(h)=theta h + theta(1-theta)/2 P(h elementwise h)+O(|h|^3),
  Dpsi(th)=theta I_T+theta(1-theta)t P diag(h)P|T+O(t^2).

At q=3, h=(2,-1,-1), P diag(h)P has tangent eigenvectors h and
v=(0,1,-1), of eigenvalues +1 and -1. Thus Dpsi(th)'s singular values
split to first order as theta[1 +/- (1-theta)t]+O(t^2). The normalized
Gram matrix has first variation 2(1-theta)P diag(h)P. At q=2 this
first variation is identically zero on T.

An edge alone can nevertheless be exactly repaired. Let

  p(h)=exp(h)/sum_i exp(h_i), F(h)=q[p(h)-1/q],
  J(h)=Dp(h)=diag(p(h))-p(h)p(h)^T,
  G(h)=q^2 J(h)^T J(h) on T.

Then softmax(psi(h))=K_theta p(h), hence F(psi(h))=theta F(h).
F is a local diffeomorphism, DF(0)=I_T, and differentiation gives the
exact positive-metric identity

  Dpsi(h)^T G(psi(h)) Dpsi(h)=theta^2 G(h).

G is positive definite on T for finite h, but need not be uniformly
conditioned as a posterior approaches the simplex boundary.

The remaining fusion defect cannot simultaneously be removed by any C^2
coordinate choice. Suppose a local F:T->T has F(0)=0,DF(0)=I and
F(h)=h+1/2 A(h,h)+o(|h|^2), where A is symmetric bilinear.
In F coordinates the edge's quadratic term is

  theta(1-theta)/2 [P(z elementwise z)-A(z,z)].

Making it vanish forces A(u,v)=P(u elementwise v) by polarization.
But additive field fusion, in the same coordinates, becomes

  F(F^(-1)(u)+F^(-1)(v))=u+v+A(u,v)+o((|u|+|v|)^2).

Making its mixed quadratic term vanish forces A=0. For q>=3,
P(u elementwise v) is not identically zero: at q=3 the preceding h,v
give P(h elementwise v)=-v. For all q>=3, u=(q-1,-1,...,-1) and
v=(0,1,-1,0,...,0) give the same nonzero example. For q=2, every
u,v in T has constant coordinatewise product, so P(u elementwise v)=0.

Thus the scalar Ising second-order flatness is exceptional. A Potts
innovation/FIID extension needs probabilistic or pathwise cancellation
of the unavoidable fusion term, not a new choice of local coordinates alone.
This disproves that particular shortcut, not a Potts FIID theorem.

3. COSET CAPS TO FOURIER CONTRACTION FOR ANY FINITE GROUP

Let G be finite, with an internal direct-product subgroup H=H1 x ... x Hk.
Let nu be a probability on G such that nu(gHa)<=B |Ha|/|G| for all a,g.
For an irreducible unitary rho of dimension D, define

  R_rho=sum_a sum_{pi irreducible of Ha, dim(pi)<=D^(1/k)} dim(pi)^2.

Then ||sum_g nu(g)rho(g)||_op^2<=B R_rho/D.

Proof. Restrict rho to H. Every tensor type has product degree at most D,
so assign it to a factor with degree <=D^(1/k). This partitions rho's
space orthogonally into E_a. For u_a in E_a, its Ha-orbit spans W_a of
dimension at most the corresponding sum of degree squares. Multiplicities
are harmless: on V_pi tensor A_pi, a single vector's orbit is the image
of End(V_pi), of dimension <=dim(pi)^2. Projection onto rho(g)W_a is
constant on gHa. The cap and Schur averaging bound its nu-expectation by
B dim(W_a)/D. Cauchy-Schwarz in the representation space, expectation,
and index a gives the stated operator bound.

If a law mu has aggregate complementary-coset TV error delta<1/2, simultaneous
trimming removes mass epsilon<=2delta and obtains B=2/(1-epsilon).
The convex decomposition, rather than a generic TV estimate, gives

  ||mu_hat(rho)|| <= sqrt(2 R_rho/D)+2delta.

For convolution powers, TV(mu^t,U_G)<=1/2 sqrt(sum_{rho!=1}
D_rho^2 ||mu_hat(rho)||_op^(2t)). This is an acquired-cap reduction:
no sampler, cap testing, efficient group representation, or bit bound is supplied.
The argument of family 238 already proves the symmetric-group instance;
the stated arbitrary-group version is a direct independently checked transfer.

An exact obstruction: uniform A_n has exactly uniform ordered image laws
for any list missing at least two cards, since the two remaining images
admit an odd transposition. Nevertheless TV(U_A_n,U_S_n)=1/2 and all
convolution powers equal U_A_n. The one-dimensional sign representation
has Fourier value 1. Large marginals never remove the low-dimensional gate.

4. COMPATIBLE FLAGS CERTIFY EXPANSION AND FAIL ON FINITE ACTIONS

Consider just one stage of family 251, with finite nonempty X,Y, steps
s(x,y) in a discrete group G, and flags E/F at departure w. Assume

  for every w,x, fraction_y F(w;x,y)<=beta;
  for every v,y, fraction_x E(v s(x,y)^(-1);x,y)<=beta.

For every nonempty finite A subset G, counting E edges departing A
and E edges arriving A gives

  average_(x,y) |A s(x,y) minus A| >= (1-2beta)|A|.

Indeed at least (1-beta)|A||X||Y| E edges depart A, whereas at most
beta|A||X||Y| arrive A. Their difference is bounded by all exiting
labelled edges. Stage compatibility F1 is unnecessary for this consequence.

For beta<1/2, this is an explicit multiset Følner expansion certificate.
For P_S the average right-translation operator, Q=(P_S+P_S*)/2, and
lazy Q_l=(I+Q)/2 on l2(G), the standard coarea/Cauchy-Schwarz Cheeger
proof gives ||Q_l||<=1-(1-2beta)^2/4. Hence Q_l^t(e,e) is at most
[1-(1-2beta)^2/4]^t. This conclusion is conditional only on the two
supplied flag inequalities, not on the release's strong-Ulam circuit proof.

On a finite permutation action, total departure and arrival E counts
are exactly equal. Therefore beta<1/2 is impossible. Allowing additive
defects delta_out,delta_in in the two inequalities requires

  delta_out+delta_in >= 1-2beta.

This is an obstruction to finite realization of the DECORATED action.
It says nothing about soficity of the underlying group: the boundary
flags do not carry an invariant probability law. Generic finite-dimensional
unitary-instability claims also do not follow; nonamenable higher-rank
lattices with finite-dimensional Ulam stability are in the checked prior source.

Acquisition gate: source 251 obtains the finite lists through a compact
strongly proximal boundary and Hahn-Banach separation. It gives no
presentation-to-list compiler or bounds on list size/word lengths. The
spectral certificate becomes effective only after those data and their
global inequalities are actually acquired.
