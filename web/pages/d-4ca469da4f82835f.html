Status: independent exact derivation, prior to any tensor_frame_sol candidate

VERDICT

TRUE: Every finite tensor product of unital, TP, HS-self-adjoint,
self-compatible qubit CPTP channels has ONE canonical EB comparator with
constant C=2 on the entire Hermitian matrix space of the composite system.
Entangled observables and negative local/global HS eigenvalues are included.
The construction uses specially chosen local absolute-spectrum comparators.
Products of arbitrary valid local comparators can fail for signed channels.

LEMMA A: POSITIVE HS PRODUCT CLOSURE, SAME CONSTANT

Let H_j be finite real Hilbert spaces and 0<=F_j<=I positive self-adjoint
contractions on H_j. Let 0<=P_j<=I be positive self-adjoint contractions
(in the application, canonical POVM EB channels). Assume for one C>=1
  I-P_j <= C(I-F_j)   for every j.
Then
  I-(tensor_j P_j) <= C[I-(tensor_j F_j)].
No commutation between F_j and P_j on the same factor is required.

Proof. Write a=1-1/C>=0 and b=1/C in (0,1], so a+b=1. The local
inequality is equivalent to F_j<=R_j=aI+bP_j. The F_j and R_j are positive.
Monotonicity of tensor products of positive operators gives
  tensor_j F_j <= tensor_j R_j.
For example, if 0<=F_1<=R_1 and 0<=F_2<=R_2, then
  R_1 tensor R_2-F_1 tensor F_2
   =(R_1-F_1) tensor R_2+F_1 tensor (R_2-F_2)>=0;
induction handles all factors.

For x,y in [0,1],
  (a+bx)(a+by)=a+bxy-ab(1-x)(1-y)<=a+bxy.
Induction gives product_j(a+b x_j)<=a+b product_j x_j. Diagonalize each
P_j separately; their lifted copies on distinct factors commute and their
eigenvalues lie in [0,1]. Applying the scalar inequality on the product
eigenbasis yields
  tensor_j R_j <= aI+b(tensor_j P_j).
Combining the two orders and rearranging proves the lemma. This also covers
C=1, where a=0. The comparison is on all vectors of the full Hilbert tensor
product, including entangled vectors.

CANONICAL TENSOR PRODUCT FACT

If P_j is the canonical channel for a POVM {M_(j,y)}, then tensor_j P_j
is the canonical channel for the product POVM
  {tensor_j M_(j,y_j)}_(y_1,...,y_n).
The effects are positive and sum to I. Their traces are products of the
local traces. On arbitrary A in the full matrix space, the canonical map is
  sum_(y_1,...,y_n) Tr[(tensor_j M_(j,y_j))A]
                     (tensor_j M_(j,y_j))/product_j Tr[M_(j,y_j)].
It equals tensor_j P_j first on simple matrix tensors and hence on every
matrix by linearity. Zero effects are omitted. It is one full-domain
canonical EB channel, not a collection of separate observable-specific maps.

LEMMA B: ABSOLUTE-SPECTRUM LOCAL QUBIT COMPARATOR

Let F:M_2->M_2 be CPTP, unital, TP, HS-self-adjoint and self-compatible.
There is a canonical EB P, diagonal in an orthonormal Pauli basis for F,
such that
  I-P <= 2(I-|F|)
on the full local Hermitian space. Here |F| is the absolute value as an HS
operator; no claim that |F| is a CPTP quantum channel is used or required.

Proof. Use normalized HS inner product tau(AB)=Tr(AB)/2. Unitality and
TP fix I and preserve its traceless orthogonal complement. Self-adjointness
makes the 3-by-3 real Bloch matrix symmetric. Its real orthonormal eigenbasis
G_1,G_2,G_3 consists of traceless Hermitian involutions with
  {G_i,G_j}=2delta_ij I,  F(G_i)=lambda_i G_i.
Kadison-Schwarz and TP make F an HS contraction, so lambda_i in [-1,1].
Take s_i in {+1,-1} with s_i lambda_i=|lambda_i|, choosing either sign
when lambda_i=0. The receiver observables B_i=s_i G_i are again a Clifford
family. Let J be the normalized Choi state of a supplied joint broadcaster
for F. Then, for any subset S of generators,
  Tr[J sum_(i in S) G_i^T tensor (B_i tensor I+I tensor B_i)]
       =2 sum_(i in S) |lambda_i|.

The representation-independent star bound applies to the three families
G_i^T, B_i, B_i. To check its hypotheses directly, set
  X_i=G_i^T tensor B_i tensor I,
  Y_i=G_i^T tensor I tensor B_i,
  Z_i=I tensor B_i tensor B_i.
The Z_i commute, X_i commutes with Z_i and anticommutes with Z_j for j!=i,
and X_i+Y_i=X_i(I+Z_i). These are exactly the verified sign-sector relations.
For r=|S|, the star norm is at most r+1 (including r=1 with bound 2).
Thus 2 sum_(i in S)|lambda_i|<=r+1.

Choose S={i:|lambda_i|>1/2} and put
  mu_i=(2|lambda_i|-1)_+,  s=sum_i mu_i.
The preceding inequality gives s<=1, including S empty. Use the two-outcome
Pauli spectral PVM P_i^+/-=(I+/-G_i)/2, whose canonical channel is
  D_i(X)=tau(X)I+tau(G_i X)G_i.
Also use D_0(X)=tau(X)I. Define
  P=sum_i mu_i D_i+(1-s)D_0.
It is canonical, with at most seven local effects
  mu_i P_i^+, mu_i P_i^-, (1-s)I,
omitting zeros. Its eigenvalues are 1 on I and mu_i on G_i. The local
absolute HS operator |F| has eigenvalues 1 on I and |lambda_i| on G_i.
Since mu_i>=2|lambda_i|-1, I-P<=2(I-|F|) on the entire four-dimensional
real Hermitian space. This proves Lemma B.

THEOREM: FULL SIGNED QUBIT TENSOR COMPARATOR

For every integer n>=1 and every supplied collection F_j:M_2->M_2 as
in Lemma B, define F=tensor_(j=1)^n F_j acting on M_(2^n). Then there
exists one canonical EB P such that, for EVERY Hermitian A in M_(2^n),
  Tr[A(I-P)(A)] <= 2 Tr[A(I-F)(A)].
No HS-positive-spectrum premise is imposed on any F_j or on F.

Proof. Choose the canonical local P_j from Lemma B and set P=tensor_j P_j.
It is canonical by the product POVM fact. The positive HS contractions
|F_j| satisfy I-P_j<=2(I-|F_j|), so Lemma A with C=2 gives
  I-P <= 2[I-(tensor_j |F_j|)].
In the product Pauli eigenbasis, F has eigenvalues product_j lambda_(j,a_j),
including lambda_(j,0)=1 on I. Its absolute HS value therefore obeys
  |F|=tensor_j |F_j|.
Spectral order gives F<=|F|, hence I-|F|<=I-F. Consequently
  I-P <= 2(I-|F|) <= 2(I-F).
This is a full HS operator order on the entire real Hermitian space. It
includes arbitrary real combinations of Pauli strings, hence all entangled
Hermitian observables, rather than just simple observable tensors.

Equivalent scalar check. On any product Pauli string put
  x_j=|lambda_(j,a_j)| in [0,1],  y_j=mu_(j,a_j),
with y_j=1 on the identity. The local bound is y_j>=2x_j-1. Lemma A gives
product_j y_j>=2product_j x_j-1. If the signed product eigenvalue is
nonnegative, this is exactly the desired string comparison. If it is
negative, 1-product_j y_j<=1<=2(1-product_j lambda_(j,a_j)). No expansion
in the number of factors changes the constant.

SIGNED EDGE CASE: ARBITRARY LOCAL COMPARATORS FAIL TO TENSOR

Let Z be a Pauli observable and P_+=(I+Z)/2, P_-=(I-Z)/2. The qubit channel
  F(X)=Tr(P_+ X)P_-+Tr(P_- X)P_+
has Bloch eigenvalues (0,0,-1). It is CPTP, EB, unital, TP, HS-self-adjoint
and self-compatible, with the explicit broadcaster
  B(X)=Tr(P_+ X)(P_- tensor P_-)+Tr(P_- X)(P_+ tensor P_+).
The depolarizing canonical D_0 has I-D_0<=I-F, so it is a local comparator
even with C=1. But F tensor F fixes Z tensor Z exactly, while D_0 tensor D_0
annihilates it. Therefore
  I-(D_0 tensor D_0) <= C[I-(F tensor F)]
fails on Z tensor Z for EVERY finite C. This refutes unrestricted signed
product closure for arbitrary chosen local comparators.

The main theorem avoids this obstruction: Lemma B chooses mu_Z=1 for F,
so its comparator is Z-dephasing and its product preserves all diagonal
Z strings, including Z tensor Z.

SCOPE AND CHARGED COSTS

The construction is specific to tensor products of the admitted qubit
channels. It does not prove the arbitrary-dimensional self-compatible
channel theorem: a general channel on M_(2^n) need not factor into qubit
channels, and higher-dimensional Clifford spans omit other local directions.
The given local maps/broadcasters and tensor decomposition are supplied.
Three-by-three local score eigendecompositions yield at most seven effects
per factor and at most 7^n product effects before omission/coalescence.
The product representation is succinct, but dense channel/POVM materialization,
physical measurements, precision and acquisition remain separately charged.
