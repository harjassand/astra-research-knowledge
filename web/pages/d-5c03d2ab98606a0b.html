## 4. Attractor support is also a retained conditional mechanism

If the coordinate extactic vanishes, the rational span W of the coordinate vector and its successive flow derivatives has rank<n. Along a nonsingular trajectory this subspace is fixed; poles of a chosen rational basis can be handled by continuation of the fixed linear identities. An open attracting basin to a fixed projective point p implies p∈W(x) on that open set, and therefore rationally for generic x everywhere. Sign covariance then puts every sign flip of p in W. A full-support p would force W=V; more generally the union of supports of several such fixed attractors enters W.

This is exactly the earlier cycle10 support-orbit argument. A continuum of maxima with an initial-point-dependent limit does not supply a fixed p on an open basin.
