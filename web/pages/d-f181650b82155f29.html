## k-source optimum

Let C=sum_j,l w_l² f_jl f_jl^T and alpha=lambda_min(C)>0. With x_jl=w_l C^-1/2 f_jl, sum x_jl x_jl^T=I. Hence each column norm²≤1, grouped leverage q_l≤k and total leverage n. Hard total input energy bounds alpha sum_l w_l^-2 q_l. Filling at most k slots in increasing cost order gives sum costs≥S_k. Countably many nonnegative terms do not impair the argument; the first n slots give the finite optimum. Thus alpha≤E/S_k.

For the attaining design f_jl=sqrt(E/S_k)w_l^-1 e_i on the first n slots, C=(E/S_k)I. Exactly n nonzero source-block vectors occur, with n coordinate voltage tests each: n² scalar acquisitions, even though a source may occupy several physical blocks. No extra source is needed to read multiple blocks. The signal columns all have coefficient tau sqrt(E/S_k). Fisher minimum on Frobenius-unit Sym(n) is tau² alpha/sigma², since tr(V²C)≥alpha tr(V²) and V=uu^T attains equality.

Symmetrizing the n noisy columns gives n diagonal variance sigma²/(tau²alpha), and n(n-1)/2 off-diagonal pairs each contribute the same amount to Frobenius risk: each averaged off-diagonal has variance sigma²/(2tau²alpha) and is counted twice. Total is Dsigma²/(tau²alpha), exactly as claimed. Ball projection decreases error when the admitted truth lies inside the ball.

Interface conditions matter: currents are selected without unknown-dependent adaptation; energy means squared input norm; noise is independent equal-variance in retained orthonormal coordinate tests; full-output information is an upper bound within this detector/noise interface. Arbitrary repeated/nonorthogonal detectors have a different finite-reading Fisher resource and are correctly excluded from the exact equality. Full infinite-coordinate white noise is properly interpreted as a product Gaussian sequence, not an ell²-valued random vector.

Minor correction suggested: the operational paragraph says the family is positive symmetric, whereas the formal interface presently admits all A in Sym(n).
