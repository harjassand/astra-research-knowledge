## Reproduction depth

`reconstruction/anchor_fourier_check.py` is new, locally written independent code. It constructs the finite periodic Hessian, solves the covariance Sylvester equation by eigendecomposition, compares against the analytic Fourier expression, and records residuals and input parameters in `reconstruction/anchor_fourier_results.json`. This is a finite equation-level check with double-precision arithmetic; it does not replay PR source code, estimate a Markov-chain mixing rate, simulate their λ=24 model, prove the incoming theorem, or validate a laboratory/device model.
