LEGAL INTERFACE AND CLAIM

Fix d>=2, N>=1. Supply rational q_lambda>=0 summing EXACTLY to one, indexed
by partitions lambda of N with at most d rows. Input length L includes all
weights/labels. Let P_lambda be the full Schur-Weyl isotypic projector and
 r_lambda=dim U_lambda, f_lambda=dim S_lambda,
 rho=sum_lambda q_lambda P_lambda/(r_lambda f_lambda).        (C1)
For rational 0<eps<1, the algorithm below emits a labeled partition of the
N sites into clusters of size <=d and an EXACT rational pure density matrix
on each cluster. Its mean rho_out satisfies T(rho_out,rho)<eps, with
deterministic time, output size and fair-bit count polynomial in
 N,L,log(1/eps), for FIXED d.                                (C2)
Cluster dimension is d^h<=d^d, a fixed constant here. No polynomial-in-d
claim, flat ensemble enumeration, physical gate execution or arbitrary
permutation-invariant-state theorem is made. There is no acquisition oracle
for q_lambda: they are supplied in this statement.

1. WHY THIS CONTINUOUS ENSEMBLE IS THE CORRECT TARGET

Schur-Weyl duality gives H=direct_sum_lambda U_lambda tensor S_lambda,
with multiplicity one for the joint U(d) x S_N action. Commutation with
both collective U and site permutations gives exactly (C1). For a Young
column of height h define the normalized wedge a_h of e_1,...,e_h. Put
 w_lambda=tensor_over_columns a_h.
Its number weight is lambda; every raising operator E_ab, a<b, kills
each column because b is absent or replacement by a repeats a wedge label.
Highest-weight structure therefore puts w_lambda entirely in the lambda
isotypic space. Haar conjugation by U^tensorN and uniform permutation
conjugation, applied to |w_lambda><w_lambda|, give
 P_lambda/(r_lambda f_lambda).                              (C3)
The same U is used at EVERY site and column. Independent rotations for
different columns would be a different state. Each sample remains a
product of column clusters, relabeled by the permutation.

The column vector itself has direct prior art: Reuvers1805.00364v2,
Lemma13 Eq61, read directly. Its double-twirl consequence and representation
facts are also reconstructed in the independent C04_S03/C04_S02 proofs.
This compiler claims no novelty for the column vector or central upper bound.
No matching lower bound on a mixed-sector state is used by the sampler.

2. HAAR FROM d^2 INDEPENDENT UNIFORMS, WITH A STABLE SECTION

Induct on m=1,...,d. At m=1 choose U_1=e^(2pi i u), u uniform[0,1].
At m>=2, take m-1 independent uniforms u_j and set
 t_j=1-(1-u_j)^(1/(m-j)),
 x_j=t_j product_(i<j)(1-t_i),
 x_m=product_(i<m)(1-t_i).                                 (C4)
The independent t_j have Beta(1,m-j) densities. Their change-of-variables
Jacobian gives the constant (m-1)! density on the simplex: this is
Dirichlet(1,...,1). With m-1 additional independent uniform phases set
 v_1=sqrt(x_1)>=0, v_j=sqrt(x_j)e^(2pi i u'_j), j>1.
Take one further independent uniform phase a, and an independent Haar
U_(m-1). The first column a v is uniform on the complex unit sphere.
For w=e_1+v set
 R(v)=-I+2 w w*/||w||^2,
 U_m=R(v) diag(a,U_(m-1)).                                (C5)
Here ||w||^2=2+2v_1>=2, R is unitary and R e_1=v. Conditional on its first
column, the remaining frame is Haar on its stabilizer U(m-1), so U_m is
Haar on U(m). This follows from invariance of sphere measure and uniqueness
of the Haar probability on each stabilizer fiber. The number of uniforms
is 1+sum_(m=2)^d(2m-1)=d^2. No Gaussian tails, QR pivot conditioning,+unbounded rejection or division by a small first coordinate is involved.

3. A UNIFORM COUPLING BOUND, NOT A FALSE HAAR-TV CLAIM

Replace each uniform u by the midpoint of its P-bit cell. Under the natural
coupling its error is <=delta=2^-P. For r<=d-1,
 |z^(1/r)-z'^(1/r)|<=|z-z'|^(1/r), 0<=z,z'<=1.
Telescoping products in(C4), then the square-root Holder bound, give
 ||v-v'||<=8d^2 delta^(1/[2(d-1)]).
The section is uniformly stable: ||R(v)-R(v')||<=8||v-v'||, because both
||e_1+v||^2 denominators are >=2. Recursive matrix telescoping gives
 ||U-U_grid||op<=C_d delta^a,
 C_d=100d^3, a=1/[2(d-1)].                                (C6)
These conservative constants dominate all levels and phase errors.
A discrete matrix law is mutually singular with continuous Haar; no small
TV distance between those two laws is asserted. Coupling controls their
quantum-state means. Tensor telescoping gives state error <=N||U-U_grid||.

4. CERTIFIED RATIONAL ARITHMETIC CAN PRESERVE UNITARITY EXACTLY

Acquire roots in[0,1] by rational bisection of y^r against rational input;
r<=d is fixed. Compute inner roots to twice the desired output accuracy,+so product error followed by a square root remains controlled by the
Holder bound. Sin/cos of arguments in[0,2pi] use rational Taylor series
with an explicit factorial remainder and polynomial degree in accuracy
bits. A rational interval for pi is obtained from Machin's identity and
geometric alternating-arctangent remainders. No exact-real evaluator is free.

Let rational c,s approximate a unit phase cos(theta),sin(theta). Pick the
sign eta=+1 if c>=0 and -1 otherwise. Set t=(eta s)/(1+eta c), round t to
a sufficiently fine dyadic, and use the EXACT Gaussian-rational phase
 a_hat=eta(1-t^2+2it)/(1+t^2).                             (C7)
Its modulus is exactly one. The chosen denominator is >=1; if numerical
chart choice differs from the true cosine sign, both charts still have
uniformly bounded denominator near the boundary. The map is Lipschitz in
the input phase accuracy and t rounding. This avoids the unstable tangent
chart at angle pi. Relative phases and global phases both use(C7).

Approximate v rationally and use rational w=e_1+v_hat in(C5). A nonzero
rational Householder is EXACTLY unitary even when v_hat is not unit length.
At sufficient precision its denominator is >=1. All recursive products
therefore yield an EXACT Gaussian-rational unitary U_hat. By the stable
expressions above, for requested xi>0 one acquires
 ||U_hat-U_grid||op<=xi                                   (C8)
with arithmetic/output bits polynomial in P+log(1/xi), for fixed d.
Rational bisection, series, rounding and fixed-depth rational operations
provide this bit algorithm. Huge internal rational sums may be rounded
to dyadics with certified interval error before each phase/reflection step;
fixed expression depth controls both errors and denominator bits.

5. PURE CLUSTER OUTPUT IS EXACTLY PHYSICAL

For h<=d and an ordered local string (j_1,...,j_h), define the rational
ray coordinate
 z_h(j_1,...,j_h)=det[(U_hat)_(j_a,b)]_(a,b=1)^h.           (C9)
Repeated rows give zero. Since U_hat is unitary, Cauchy-Binet or the
normalized wedge identity gives ||z_h||^2=h!. Emit
 rho_h=z_h z_h*/h!,                                      (C10)
an exact rational rank-one positive trace-one matrix. Its phase need not
be represented by a normalized rational vector. Only h<=d determinants
and d^h<=d^d ray coordinates are involved; those are constant-size objects
for fixed d. Compute only the at most d distinct heights needed.

At a column, trace distance from the ideal rotated wedge is <=h||U_hat-U||.
Summing the heights over columns gives at most N||U_hat-U||. Thus exact
unitarity removes any cluster-normalization or small-Gram precision gap
in the UNFILTERED core. The samples remain in their lambda sector too,+since a collective invertible map preserves that space, but exact central
invariance of the finite sampler is unnecessary for the error guarantee.

6. LABELS, PERMUTATIONS AND CONCRETE PRECISION

There are at most M<=(N+1)^(d-1) supplied partitions. Round all but one
positive q down to a common b_q-bit dyadic grid, assigning residual to
a valid positive label. The law TV is <=M 2^-b_q. For a permutation use
Fisher-Yates: at stage j draw b_pi bits V and choose floor(jV/2^b_pi),+a valid integer in{0,...,j-1}. Its stage TV is <=j 2^-b_pi; adaptive
coupling sums this to <=N^2 2^-b_pi. No list of N! permutations is formed.
After generating the permutation, assign its consecutive indices to the
column clusters and return their matrices(C10).

For example choose
 b_q=ceil log2(32M/eps), b_pi=ceil log2(32N^2/eps),
 P=ceil[2(d-1) log2(32C_d N/eps)], xi=eps/(32N).             (C11)
Then label, permutation, Haar-cell and arithmetic-unitary errors each
contribute <=eps/32. The mean trace error is <=eps/8, with ample remaining
budget for optional approximate physical cluster preparation. Fair-bit
count is b_q+(N-1)b_pi+d^2 P; every loop has a deterministic bound.
All numerical logarithm choices can be replaced by integer bit-length/
doubling tests on the rational arguments. Time and emitted description
length are polynomial in N,L,log(1/eps), for fixed d.
