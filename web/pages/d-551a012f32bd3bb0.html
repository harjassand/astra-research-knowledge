1. EXACT THEOREM AND RESOURCE INTERFACE

Let H be finite dimensional. Let rho_x be trace-one positive states with
mean sigma>0 and suppose [rho_x,sigma]=0 for every x. Let Phi:H->H be ANY
CPTP reconstruction, with Kraus A_i, rho'_x=Phi(rho_x), xi=Phi(sigma),
and average HALF-trace reconstruction error

  e=int (1/2)||rho_x-rho'_x||_1 dx.

For a PUBLIC scalar A>0 put

  L_x=sigma^(-1/2)rho_x sigma^(-1/2),
  G_x=L_x(A+L_x)^(-1), 0<=G_x<=I,
  B_i=A_i sqrt(sigma).

Then

  A sum_i int||[G_x,B_i]||_HS^2 dx <=(5/2)e,
  e>=(2A/5)sum_i int||[G_x,B_i]||_HS^2 dx.             (1.1)

No output commutation, covariance, twirl, central output support, largest
likelihood, dimension or condition-number factor appears in (1.1).
The source commutation assumption is essential to THIS proof and is not
silently extended to arbitrary noncommuting means.

The theorem applies to a hybrid archive whose composite Kraus operators
have rank<=Q; then rank B_i<=Q. Every encoder/decoder composition through
a conditional register of dimension <=Q has such a refinement. Arbitrary
classical outcomes are allowed via the closed finite-dimensional Choi
Schmidt-number-<=Q cone. This is a lower-bound interface, not a finite-label
upper. All retained records remain charged in any upper construction.

2. SINGULAR OUTPUT SUPPORT AND THE PROPER SLD OPTIMIZER

Fix x and abbreviate rho=rho_x, rho'=rho'_x. Define

  M=rho+A sigma, M'=rho'+A xi, E=supp(xi).

sigma>0 makes every A_i output range lie inside E: if y lies in E^perp,
0=<y,xi y>=sum_i||sqrt(sigma)A_i^dag y||^2 implies A_i^dag y=0.
Also rho<=||L_x|| sigma gives rho'<=||L_x|| xi. Thus M' has support E
and is strictly positive there. No uniform ||L_x|| enters the final bound.

On E define the unique Hermitian Sylvester optimizer

  G'_x=2 int_(t=0..infinity) exp(-tM')rho'exp(-tM')dt,
  M'G'_x+G'_x M'=2rho'.                               (2.1)

Extend G'_x by ZERO on E^perp. The equation is then valid on the WHOLE H.
The integral and 0<=rho'<=M' show
0<=G'_x<=2int exp(-tM')M'exp(-tM')dt=I_E<=I_H.
It is an analytic witness, not an acquired oracle used by the archive.
It need not commute with rho',xi or the source G_x.

Source commutation implies

  MG_x=G_xM=rho,
  V_x:=Tr rho G_x=Tr M G_x^2.

The output Sylvester equation and cyclic trace give

  V'_x:=Tr rho'G'_x=Tr M'G'^2_x.                      (2.2)

3. EXACT POSITIVE DATA-PROCESSING DEFICIT

Let D_i=G'_x A_i-A_i G_x. Direct expansion gives

  sum_i Tr D_i M D_i^dag
    =Tr M'G'^2_x+Tr M G_x^2
                      -Tr G'_x Phi(MG_x+G_xM)
    =V'_x+V_x-2Tr G'_x rho'
    =V_x-V'_x=:J_x>=0.                               (3.1)

The first term uses Phi(M)=M'; the second uses TP; the cross terms use
MG_x+G_xM=2rho. No output commutation is used. Since M>=A sigma and
G_x commutes with sqrt(sigma),

  J_x>=A sum_i||D_i sqrt(sigma)||_HS^2
       =A sum_i||G'_x B_i-B_i G_x||_HS^2.              (3.2)

This is the exact quadratic-data-processing remainder. It is proved
here directly rather than imported from a general monotone-metric result.

4. FULL-SPACE PROPER RISK; KEEP SUPPORT CROSS TERMS

For ANY Hermitian H on the WHOLE space define

  q'_x(H)=2Tr H rho'-Tr H^2 M'.

Using the FULL-space Sylvester equation, cyclic trace gives

  V'_x-q'_x(H)=Tr M'(G'_x-H)^2>=0.                    (4.1)

In particular

  R_x:=V'_x-q'_x(G_x)
       =Tr M'(G'_x-G_x)^2
       >=A Tr xi(G'_x-G_x)^2.                         (4.2)

When xi is singular, do NOT replace G_x by E G_x E. The square in
(4.1)-(4.2) retains G_x's cross terms between E and E^perp, which are
positive and needed for the triangle estimate. There is no target-support
crop or central-support assumption.

Input optimality is q_x(G_x)=V_x for
q_x(H)=2Tr H rho-Tr H^2(rho+A sigma). Direct subtraction therefore gives

  J_x+R_x
   =Tr(2G_x-G_x^2)(rho-rho')+A Tr G_x^2(xi-sigma).      (4.3)

Both J_x,R_x are nonnegative. The first factor is an effect. Scalar
A[t/(A+t)]^2<=t/4, functional calculus and int L_x dx=I imply

  0<=A int G_x^2 dx<=I/4.                             (4.4)

The actual mean error satisfies T(xi,sigma)<=e. Effect expectation bounds
on trace-zero state differences, in HALF-trace normalization, yield

  0<=int(J_x+R_x)dx<=e+e/4=5e/4.                     (4.5)

Finally,

  [G_x,B_i]=(G_x-G'_x)B_i+(G'_x B_i-B_i G_x),
  sum_i||(G_x-G'_x)B_i||^2=Tr xi(G_x-G'_x)^2.

The squared Hilbert-space triangle inequality with (3.2),(4.2) gives
A sum_i||[G_x,B_i]||^2<=2(J_x+R_x). Integrating proves (1.1).
All target mass, arbitrary output coherences and zero-xi sectors have
been retained in this derivation.
