# Two-gate force reserve and a conditional robust-propagation threshold

## Source interface

The relevant physical event is cell detachment from the vitelline membrane (VM), not just threshold crossing of a chemical profile. Collinet et al. (2024) depict a MyoII-generated de-adhesion force `F_d` applied at detachment angle `theta`; its component normal to the VM is `F_d sin(theta)`, which must overcome adhesion. They measure that local increases in beta-PS focal-complex signal accompany reduced detachment-front speed, and that the angle changes during detachment (the mean cross-correlation between derivatives of angle and detachment speed was `-0.30 +/- 0.02` SEM). They do not directly measure absolute adhesion or contractile force.

Mundhe et al. (2026) supplies the upstream two-gate structure. Fog-dependent Smog brightness/oligomerization can remain high or increase when alphaPS3 integrin is depleted, even while MyoII output falls; integrin loss suppresses the broadened/high MyoII phenotype induced by Gprk2 depletion. Integrins therefore gate downstream GPCR-to-MyoII output as well as carrying adhesion. The FCS brightness, beta-PS fluorescence, and MyoII intensity are proxies, not force-calibrated quantities. All sources and detailed data boundaries are recorded in `../primary_audit.md`.

## Cell-level detachment reserve

Index successive PZ cells by `i`. Let `O_i >= 0` denote the effective Fog-activated receptor-cluster signal, `I_i >= 0` the active integrin/cofactor state, `theta_i` the measured detachment angle with `sin(theta_i)>0`, and `B_i` an adhesion-bearing focal-complex state. Assume `K>0` and positive force coefficients. A minimal two-gate model is

```text
M_i = M0 + Gamma O_i I_i/(K + I_i)
F_d,i = alpha M_i
F_a,i = beta B_i
Delta_i = F_d,i sin(theta_i) - F_a,i.
```

`Gamma>0` implements integrin-dependent signaling downstream of clustering; `beta>0` represents the adhesion load carried by integrin complexes. Saturation `I/(K+I)` is a candidate closure for cofactor action, not a fitted biochemical law. If contact activation `I_i` is proportional to adhesion proxy `B_i`, absorb the proportionality into `beta` and write `B_i=I_i`.

Under the explicit quasi-static peeling assumption that detachment occurs exactly when the normal de-adhesion component exceeds the adhesion load, cell `i` detaches iff `Delta_i>0`. Under a strictly serial, no-bypass relay in which detachment of cell `i` exposes/contact-activates the next row, propagation through N cells occurs iff

```text
min_{1 <= i <= N} Delta_i > 0.
```

**Proof.** If every margin is positive, the force rule detaches cell 1; serial relay exposes cell 2, whose positive margin detaches; induction gives all N. If any margin is nonpositive, the first such row cannot detach under the stipulated force rule, so the no-bypass relay cannot recruit any row beyond it. This is a sharp deterministic condition for the named discrete model. It is not established as the embryo's full force law.

This immediately attacks a common mean-profile shortcut: positive mean MyoII or positive mean force margin does not guarantee propagation. For example, in normalized force units with fixed `theta` absorbed into `M`, `beta=alpha=1`, these two two-cell arrays have identical means `(mean M, mean B)=(1.2,1.0)` and mean reserve `0.2`:

```text
uniform:    (M,B) = (1.2,1.0), (1.2,1.0)    -> Delta = +0.2, +0.2; both detach
heterogeneous: (M,B) = (2.2,0.5), (0.2,1.5) -> Delta = +1.7, -1.3; row 2 stalls
```

Thus spatially averaged MyoII, beta-PS and contact profiles can agree while serial propagation differs. The 2024 study's discrete focal-complex dynamics and local speed/adhesion associations make the per-cell distribution and covariance biologically material; they do not provide the missing force calibration.

## Integrin sign switch: signaling benefit versus adhesion cost

With `B=I`, fixed measured `theta`, and the model above,

```text
Delta(I;O,theta) = alpha sin(theta)[M0 + Gamma O I/(K+I)] - beta I.
```

The marginal effect of stronger integrin state on the detachment reserve is

```text
dDelta/dI = alpha Gamma O K sin(theta)/(K+I)^2 - beta.
```

Therefore added integrin raises the cell's detachment reserve exactly when

```text
O > O_c(I,theta) = beta (K+I)^2/[alpha Gamma K sin(theta)].
```

Below this cluster/cofactor threshold, extra integrin adds more adhesion load than signaling-supported de-adhesion force; above it, the signaling increment dominates locally. For fixed `O`, the reserve is maximized at

```text
I_star = sqrt(alpha Gamma O K sin(theta)/beta) - K,
```

when `alpha Gamma O sin(theta) > beta K`; otherwise reserve decreases with all increases in `I`. This is a mathematically sharp, conditional sign-switch prediction using the actual dual role of integrins in the 2024/2026 mechanism. It does not predict a numerical `O_c` because none of `alpha`, `beta`, `Gamma`, `K`, or force-calibrated `O,I` is identified in the sources. Geometry cannot be silently fixed in an experiment: `theta_i` is directly measured and its feedback must be stratified or included in the model.

The discriminating prediction is not “more integrin helps” or “more integrin slows.” At matched angle, Fog/Smog state and source drive, an integrin titration should change the measured per-cell detachment reserve with a sign that switches at `O_c(I,theta)`. A monotone receptor-only model cannot generate the sign switch, and a uniform integrin-amplifier model omits the adhesion term.

## Reliability law for a finite-row relay

The deterministic criterion makes the weakest cell decisive. If, as an additional statistical model, the N cell reserves are independent and identically `Normal(mu,sigma^2)`, then one-row success is `Phi(mu/sigma)` and N-row success is exactly

```text
P_N = Phi(mu/sigma)^N.
```

For a target failure probability `epsilon`, the corresponding sharp Gaussian reliability condition is

```text
mu/sigma >= Phi^{-1}((1-epsilon)^(1/N)).
```

For a seven-row target and 95% propagation reliability, this requires `mu/sigma >= 2.4421`; for twenty rows it requires `mu/sigma >= 2.7992`. Seven rows is motivated only as a target comparable to the 2026 WT mean of about 7.1 propagated rows; the existing row count is not evidence for Gaussian iid cell margins. If cell reserves are correlated, the product formula fails and the joint lower tail must be measured/modelled. The value of this result is to make a per-cell noise/force-reserve acquisition requirement explicit; it is not a fitted statement about current embryos.

If the positive-margin force law is overdamped, one possible local kinetic closure is `u_i = mu_i Delta_i` for `Delta_i>0`. Then the time for a fixed number of row crossings is a sum of local delays, and the tissue speed depends on the distribution of `mu_i Delta_i`, not the mean MyoII amplitude alone. Other laws (e.g. Bell-like bond rupture or nonlinear peeling) have the same threshold but different speeds. The existing angle/fluorescence data do not select among them.

## What this advances, and what remains unknown

This supplies a stronger model interface than the passive `ell <= D/v` bound: a Fog/Smog cluster is an upstream permissive signal, integrin is simultaneously a signaling gate and an adhesive load, geometry converts contractility into normal peeling force, and propagation is a serial weakest-link event. It yields (i) an exact model propagation condition, (ii) an explicit conditional integrin sign-switch, (iii) a same-mean/different-outcome counterexample, and (iv) a reliability threshold once local reserve statistics are acquired.

Nature is not identified with these equations. The primary sources do not measure force units, the active integrin state per cell, local mobility/clearance, or a speed-versus-force constitutive law. They also leave extracellular Fog modification/cofactor mechanisms open. In addition, the 2026 source's statement that larger amplitude/range slows the wave is in its discussion and invokes the 2024 adhesion work; the 2026 Fig. 3 profile experiment itself does not report a matched speed curve. Bailles et al. (2019) directly found that its extra-Fog condition raised MyoII but did not change wave speed. Thus a quantitative Fog-to-speed law is not a source-supported result. Do not publish a numerical integrin/Fog speed optimum until local forces and matched front speeds identify the law.

## Falsifiers and model boundaries

- The serial-margin model is false if measured cells detach and propagate despite repeated `Delta_i <= 0` under an independently calibrated force and geometry measurement, or if bypass/nonlocal transmission crosses such a cell.
- The proposed integrin sign switch is false for this closure if a force-calibrated titration at fixed geometry and receptor state has a single sign for `dDelta/dI` across the full range, with no saturation or sign crossing.
- The Gaussian reliability formula is false if cell-reserve distributions are non-Gaussian/iid or correlated; use the measured joint distribution instead.
- The mean-profile counterexample only demonstrates structural non-identifiability: no numerical force values in its example are presented as Drosophila data.
- A measured mean signal profile is not a robustness certificate. A quantitative intervention interface requires per-cell `(O_i,I_i,B_i,theta_i)` and a force or detachment-time calibration, with embryo-level replication.
