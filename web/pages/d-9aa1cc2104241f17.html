# Full tangent curvature trace and a non-reflection exact exclusion

2026-10-07. Mathematical reconstruction; historical priority unverified. A restricted landscape obstruction, not axis acquisition or the full weighted strict-saddle theorem.

For orthogonal W, M=W^{circ2}, and real symmetric G, let F_G(W)=tr(GMM^T). For each i<j let A_ij=E_ij−E_ji (Frobenius norm squared2). Then

  sum_i<j F_G''(W;A_ij)
   =tr(G [8I+4J−4(n+2)MM^T]).                         (1)

This is an ambient identity on the orthogonal group, with no stationarity assumption. Adding1v^T+v1^T to G only changes F by a constant. Thus write P=I−J/n and G0=PGP; the identity is equivalently

  sum_i<j F_G''=8tr(G0)−4(n+2)tr(G0 MM^T).             (2)

## Direct derivation

For a tangent generator A write V=WA, D=2W circ V and D2=2V^{circ2}+2W circ WA². The full second derivative is tr(GK_A), K_A=2DD^T+MD2^T+D2M^T.

The coordinate skew basis satisfies sum_A A²=−(n−1)I. At a fixed column j its velocity covariance is sum_A V[:,j]V[:,j]^T=I−W[:,j]W[:,j]^T. Consequently

  sum_A DD^T=4(I−MM^T),
  sum_A D2=2J−2nM.

Substitution gives (1). All operations are finite exact sums; no numerical limiting argument is used. The coefficient matrix kills1, consistent with the gauge boundary. `curvature_trace.py` independently constructs every bilinear Hessian coefficient and verifies (1) at both the rational weighing matrix and a rational nonuniform reflection. These are code checks of the universal derivation.

## A falsifiable exclusion criterion

If on1-perp, MM^T has maximum eigenvalue rho<2/(n+2), then

  sum_A F'' >= [8−4(n+2)rho] tr(G0)>0

for every contrast-positive weight G0 (G0 positive definite on1-perp). Hence W cannot be a local maximum for any such G. Some normalized coordinate tangent has curvature at least [8−4(n+2)rho]tr(G0)/[n(n−1)]. This is a sufficient exclusion criterion, not a necessary characterization. Near signed permutations rho=1 and it does not exclude true maxima.

A necessary condition for any local maximum is

  F_{G0}(W)>=2tr(G0)/(n+2).

This lower bound is much weaker than the global target tr(G0); it does not prove an algorithm reaches or recognizes the target. A weight-agnostic trace identity is a familiar type of Laplacian/Casimir computation, so novelty is not assumed.

## Exact non-Householder structured instance

Let C be circulant with first row (−1,1,1,0,1,0,0), W=C/2. Direct integer multiplication gives CC^T=4I. W is rational, has zeros, and is not a uniform reflection. Its support incidence S=4M has four ones per row and two common ones per pair of different rows. Hence

  MM^T=(I+J)/8,
  sum_i<j K_Aij=(7/2)P,
  sum_i<j F_G''=(7/2)tr(PG)>0.                        (3)

Thus no positive definite weight, or merely contrast-positive weight, makes this W a local maximum. At G=I it is stationary because W^{circ3}=W/4; its stationarity map on the21-dimensional centered symmetric weight space has exact rank8 and13-dimensional kernel. This is a structured stationary locus, rather than a generic matrix at which no PD stationary weight can exist.

An independently constructed sum of the28 mixed pivots A=x_i e_j^T−e_j x_i^T at nonzero entries gives Kaggregate=(35/4)P (`weighing_curvature.py`). The simpler trace certificate (3) suffices and avoids reliance on the stationarity-based Jin pivot formula.

## Interface, prior and unresolved gap

G,W are supplied for this landscape calculation. In the intended discovery problem, the learner receives expanded rational quartic coefficients and an acquired lower-term metric. It does not receive latent G, W, axes, zeros or the commuting matrices of the equivalent OJD problem. Landscape obstructions inform synthesis but do not erase this distinction.

Jin2026 v2 settles the unweighted G=I case and attributes qualitative priority to Boumal2024. Our weighted full theorem remains unproved: for arbitrary contrast-positive G, every non-permutation stationary W has a positive tangent direction. Even proving it would leave quantitative acquisition, polynomial bit/iteration bounds and exact rational reconstruction unresolved. No scientific breakthrough or historical-priority claim is made for (1) or the weighing-family exclusion.

Generic exact Cayley matrices have an invertible contrast-stationarity map and are an inappropriate broad search distribution.
