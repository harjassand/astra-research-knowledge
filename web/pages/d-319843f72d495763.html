## F1. Literal general-abelian guard: subtraction, not addition

SUBTRACTED from the reference label before postselecting zero. Adding

## F2. Polar and character proof, independently reconstructed

Let Phi have normalized GU columns psi_c, G=Phi*Phi>0 and
Mu=Phi G^(-1/2). Then Mu*Mu=I. Translation invariance of G makes every
character chi an eigenvector with eigenvalue lambda_chi>0, and makes
diag sqrt(G) constant s=Tr sqrt(G)/M. Therefore

    <mu_c,psi_c>=s,
    Phi chi=sqrt(lambda_chi)Mu chi,
    ||Mu chi||=sqrt(M), ||Phi chi||=sqrt(M lambda_chi).

A reversible dilation of the projective PGM maps mu_c to some unit
vector v_c with deterministic label c. These v_c are orthonormal
because the dilation is an isometry. Correct-label postselection
retains s M^(-1/2)sum chi_c v_c, of norm s, independently of chi.
Undoing maps v_c back to mu_c and resets the ancilla. The normalized
output is exactly Phi chi/sqrt(M lambda_chi). The inverse is necessary;
measurement error or a success rate alone does not specify this map.

The optimality/success lower bound can also be checked without assuming
a theorem from a snippet. On the ensemble span let S=(Phi Phi*)^(1/2)
and Y=s S/M. The rank-one domination criterion gives

    psi_c*(sS)^(-1)psi_c=G^(1/2)_(c,c)/s=1,

so Y>=psi_c psi_c*/M. This is a feasible state-discrimination SDP dual.
Its trace is s^2, which equals the PGM primal success. Thus s^2 is
optimal for the equiprobable linearly independent GU ensemble. Any
decoder with failure pfail gives s^2>=1-pfail. It does NOT give a circuit
for this optimal PGM. Also s<=1 by trace Cauchy--Schwarz.

## F4. Coherent precision and actual primary acquisition costs

Suppose U I0 approximates the desired projective-PGM isometry V within
xi in operator norm on the input subspace. Then
||U*V-I0||=||V-U I0||<=xi. First decoding changes the accepted vector
by at most xi. The ideal accepted vector is in ran(V), so undoing
adds at most s xi<=xi. Total vector error<=2xi. With xi<=s/4 its norm
is at least s-2xi, and normalized pure-state trace error<=8xi/s.
No uncontrolled reference garbage or arbitrary unitary completion is
inferred from a measurement-channel diamond estimate.

For a unitary whose designated block approximates the isometry V
within epsilon, the omitted-block squared norm is<=2epsilon+epsilon^2
by unitarity and V*V=I. Thus its INPUT-SUBSPACE isometry error is at
most epsilon+sqrt(2epsilon+epsilon^2). Charge epsilon=O(xi^2).
The actual Gilyen--Lloyd--Marvian--Quek--Wilde proof constructs an
approximate Petz isometry and an amplified unitary block encoding,
not merely a classical measurement probability. That is sufficient
in principle for this coherent interface at its stated precision.
