## Fixed-H diamond estimate and Lamb shift

For the SAME finite H, set E=A-B. The weighted spectral column for A
has operator norm squared
||sum_omega gamma_omega A_omega^dagger A_omega||
<=Gamma||A||^2, and likewise for E. The bilinear gain map has a
Stinespring representation with those two columns, so its diamond
norm is at most Gamma||A||||E||. This includes ancilla inputs and
does not count frequencies.

Expand the gain difference as Phi_(A,E)+Phi_(E,B). Expand the loss
matrix difference as sum gamma(A_omega^dagger E_omega+
E_omega^dagger B_omega). Column Cauchy–Schwarz gives the same bound
for the latter matrix. Its half-anticommutator has superoperator
diamond norm no larger than its operator norm. Consequently

    ||D_A-D_B||_diamond
       <=2Gamma(||A||+||B||)||A-B||.                  (1)

For a norm-contracting truncation ||B||<=||A||, and summing the
physical bath intensity gives the peer's4N r_bath f(R). The anchor
remains the original physical bath support for this rate inventory;
the pulled-back spatial support is not inserted into r_bath.

Signed bounded Lamb-shift coefficients insert a diagonal contraction
between the spectral columns, hence

    ||K_A-K_B||<=Lambda_LS(||A||+||B||)||A-B||.         (2)

Multiplying by2 for the commutator gives the peer's4N r_LS f(R).
Both channels being compared are genuine CPTP generators at fixed H,
with the same Gibbs state. Duhamel and diamond contraction therefore
charge4tN(r_bath+r_LS)f(R). This is not a statement about changing H;
the actual counterexample in SECULAR_LIMIT_OBSTRUCTION.md rules out
that latter continuity claim.

## Protection, support entropy and the time interval

The eroded safe projector is the intersection of all actual Pauli
prefix translates of the single-site safe projector. A Pauli on q
sites has pre-factor prefixes of weight<=q-1. Requiring those
syndromes to be safe makes its logical commutator vanish by exact
telescoping. The family of all Paulis in every radius-R ball contains
all these prefixes; distinct prefixes can be counted once.

For G_R<=v(1+R)^3 sites per ball, M_R<=2N4^G_R is a safe inventory.
Every prefix translation is a valid syndrome bijection and changes
the commuting energy by at most g times its weight. Thus

    p_R<=M_R exp(beta g(G_R-1))p_1.                    (3)
