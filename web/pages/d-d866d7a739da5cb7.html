REACTION DYNAMICS — CYCLE 2, LEAF: CLASSWISE VS UNIFORM TIGHTNESS
Date: 2026-10-08

STATUS

INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE: a distinct order-three weakly reversible mass-action example gives a sequence of positive recurrent classes whose stationary laws escape in unscaled molecule-count space. It also gives an exact physical-time return-period divergence. This is only a cross-class result: the classes have different values of the conserved total A+B. It does not reproduce or strengthen the cycle-1 same-stoichiometric-plane construction. No novelty or external-priority claim is made.

EXACT INTERFACE AND THEOREM

Fix the two-species reaction network

  2A+B  <->  A+2B

with both deterministic rate constants equal to 1 and the standard stochastic mass-action propensities

  lambda_+(a,b)=(a)_2(b)_1=a(a-1)b,
  lambda_-(a,b)=(a)_1(b)_2=ab(b-1),

where (x)_r=x(x-1)...(x-r+1), interpreted as zero when x<r. Both reactant complexes have molecularity 3. The network is reversible, hence weakly reversible; it has two complexes, one linkage class, stoichiometric rank 1, and deficiency 2-1-1=0. The exact integer class invariant is

  N=A+B.

For every integer N>=3, let

  Gamma_N={(k,N-k): k=1,...,N-1}.

Then Gamma_N is a finite closed irreducible CTMC class. Its unique stationary law is

  pi_N(k,N-k)=binom(N,k)/(2^N-2),   1<=k<=N-1.

For every compact K subset R^2, pi_N(K)=0 once N>max_{(a,b) in K}(a+b). Thus the family {pi_N:N>=3} is not uniformly tight in unscaled counts, although each member is positive recurrent. More strongly, for z_N=(1,N-1) and tau^+_{z_N}=inf{t>0:X(t)=z_N},

  E_{z_N}[tau^+_{z_N}] = (2^N-2)/(N(N-1)(N-2)) ~ 2^N/N^3.

Consequently, there is no uniform bound on mean continuous-time return periods over all states and all classes. This is an explicit negative answer to the uniform-return claim with that precise metric; it does not rule out faster mixing to a class-dependent bulk equilibrium or uniformity after rescaling to concentrations.

STOICHIOMETRY AND DETERMINISTIC DYNAMICS

The two reaction vectors are (-1,+1) and (+1,-1), so S=span{(-1,+1)} and the conserved total is A+B=N. The positive deterministic class is

  P_N={(a,b):a>0,b>0,a+b=N}.

It is a bounded line segment for each N, but the family of such segments is not contained in one bounded subset as N varies. At c=(1,1), both complex monomials are 1, so the equilibrium is complex balanced. In fact every (r,r), r>0, is complex balanced. The mass-action ODE is

  dot A = -A^2 B + A B^2 = AB(B-A),
  dot B = -dot A.

On P_N, 

  dot A = A(N-A)(N-2A).

The right-hand side is positive for 0<A<N/2 and negative for N/2<A<N. Hence every positive deterministic trajectory in P_N converges monotonically to (N/2,N/2). The class attractor depends on N and escapes as N grows. There is no one common deterministic equilibrium or one common compact absorber across this sequence of classes. This matches the per-class quantifier in N33 and is not a counterexample to it.

CLASS CLOSURE AND CONTINUOUS-TIME RECURRENCE

At state (k,N-k), the reaction 2A+B -> A+2B sends k to k-1 at rate

  q(k,k-1)=k(k-1)(N-k),

and the reverse reaction sends k to k+1 at rate

  q(k,k+1)=k(N-k)(N-k-1).

For 2<=k<=N-1 the downward rate is positive. For 1<=k<=N-2 the upward rate is positive. Thus every adjacent pair in {1,...,N-1} communicates, and no jump from this interval can reach k=0 or k=N. The endpoints (0,N) and (N,0) are separate absorbing singleton classes; they are not part of Gamma_N. Every rate on Gamma_N is finite and Gamma_N is finite, so the CTMC is conservative and nonexplosive in physical time. The detailed-balance law below is a normalizable invariant probability; irreducibility then gives positive recurrence and uniqueness on Gamma_N.

EXACT STATIONARY LAW

For neighboring states k-1 and k, the detailed-balance ratio required by the continuous-time generator is

  pi_N(k)/pi_N(k-1)
    = q(k-1,k)/q(k,k-1)
    = [(k-1)(N-k+1)(N-k)]/[k(k-1)(N-k)]
    = (N-k+1)/k.

This is precisely binom(N,k)/binom(N,k-1). Since

  sum_{k=1}^{N-1} binom(N,k)=2^N-2,

normalization gives the stated pi_N. This is the product-Poisson law with means (1,1), conditioned onto the closed class Gamma_N; equivalently it is Binomial(N,1/2) conditioned to exclude the two endpoints. Direct detailed balance proves the formula without relying on that general theorem.

STATIONARY ESCAPE AND PHYSICAL-TIME RETURN PERIOD

A compact K in R^2 has bounded A+B. But every state in Gamma_N has A+B=N. Therefore for all N larger than that bound, Gamma_N intersect K is empty and pi_N(K)=0. The sequence is not tight. This is a simple linear-conservation obstruction: it is necessarily across distinct deterministic compatibility classes, and it disappears if one uses concentration coordinates (A/N,B/N), whose supports all lie in the compact unit simplex.

For the physical-time result choose z_N=(1,N-1). Its only enabled reaction is A+2B -> 2A+B, so its total exit rate is

  q_N(z_N)=(N-1)(N-2).

Its stationary mass is pi_N(z_N)=N/(2^N-2). To compute the mean continuous-time return period, start at an entrance to z_N and split the path into successive entrance-to-entrance cycles. The mean holding time at z_N per cycle is 1/q_N(z_N). The renewal-reward theorem says the stationary fraction of time at z_N is that mean holding time divided by the mean cycle length. Therefore

  pi_N(z_N) = [1/q_N(z_N)] / E_{z_N}[tau^+_{z_N}],

which gives the exact formula in the statement. It diverges exponentially. This checks a physical-time recurrence quantity directly; no embedded-chain clock conversion is being used. The uniformity failure is not caused by slow reaction rates (the exit rate at z_N grows quadratically); it comes from exponentially small stationary mass at this near-boundary state.

QUANTIFIERS AND LIMITS

- For every fixed N>=3, Gamma_N is finite, irreducible, nonexplosive, and positive recurrent, with invariant law pi_N.
- For the family across all N, no common compact captures a fixed positive fraction of all pi_N.
- More strongly, sup_{N>=3} E_{z_N}[tau^+_{z_N}]=infinity in physical time.
- The network has different deterministic compatibility classes P_N. This example does not refute uniform tightness among stochastic components within one fixed P_N; here Gamma_N is the only positive lattice component in P_N.
- It does not imply failure of ergodicity or nonexplosion in any Gamma_N. It does not show that the class-dependent concentration laws fail to be tight.

COSTS AND STRONGEST COMPARATOR

The input is a two-species, two-arrow, order-three network with fixed unit rates. The proof uses exact integer products and binomial coefficients; it has no numerical precision, solver, simulation, or oracle-acquisition cost. The invariant law is represented by a closed formula. Producing its full table for a given N takes N-1 entries; each probability has O(N) bits in a direct exact encoding, so full tabulation uses O(N^2) bits up to constant factors. The return-time formula is exact, and its time unit is fixed by the unit rate constants.

The strongest equally informed prior-art comparator is Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz's product-form theorem for complex-balanced stochastic mass-action systems: it supplies an invariant product-Poisson law restricted to each closed irreducible class. The present derivation is its elementary reversible two-species specialization. The deficiency-zero structure guarantees complex balance for all positive rate constants; our proof only needs the explicit unit-rate balance. N33's deterministic theorem is per positive compatibility class and permits class-dependent compact sets. N34's global-recognition gap and N35's finite-horizon safety result do not assert a cross-class stationary bound. No conflict with these statements follows.

FALSIFIERS AND DECISIVE CHECKS

The construction would fail if any rate above were incorrect, if an enabled reaction took an interior state to an endpoint, or if one neighboring detailed-balance ratio differed from (N-k+1)/k. Those checks are explicit for every N and k. The physical-time conclusion would fail if the exit rate at z_N or the stationary mass there were miscomputed; both are given exactly. A stronger uniform-return claim with a different target or normalization needs its own metric. In particular, replacing molecule counts by concentrations removes this escape, and a theorem restricted to one fixed N is unaffected.

UNRESOLVED GATE / RESTART

This example settles the elementary cross-class question under unscaled molecule counts: classwise positive recurrence does not give uniform stationary tightness or a uniform physical-time mean return bound over all initial-state/class pairs. It is not a counterexample to a stronger fixed-class claim, and the example's uniformity failure is predictable from the unbounded conserved total. To continue toward a less elementary obstruction, require all candidate classes to lie in one fixed deterministic compatibility class or normalize counts by the class total; then search for a genuinely uniform obstruction rather than treating conservation-level escape as hidden structure. Any novelty claim requires a separate primary-literature search; no such claim is made here.
