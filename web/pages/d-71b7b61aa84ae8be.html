# SLD sign recovery for commuting conjugates

Worker r09_algebra; 2026-10-06. Exact finite-dimensional operator-valued SLD interface. This proves a positive subclass theorem, not universal physical XYZ identifiability for arbitrary faithful states.

## Interface and source boundary

Let σ>0, Tr σ=1. For Hermitian A, define its unitary-orbit SLD L_A by −i[A,σ]=(σL_A+L_Aσ)/2. The dressed Hermitian generator K_A=T_σ(A) has entries 2√(p_i p_j) A_ij/(p_i+p_j) in a σ-eigenbasis, equivalently it uniquely solves

σK_A+K_Aσ=2√σ A√σ.

Dong–Leng, arXiv:2609.28391v1 (23 September 2026), Lemma 6 and Theorem 15 use scalar joint commutant of these dressed generators as an identifiability condition. They do not assert ordinary irreducibility suffices generally. Their source condition and its exact faithful-state converse were reconstructed by b_math. We independently checked the source locations and reconstructed the following algebraic result.

## Sign theorem

**Theorem.** Let A=A† and A²=I. If [σ,AσA]=0, then

K_A=D_A A,

D_A=2(σ·AσA)^(1/2)(σ+AσA)^−1>0,

[D_A,A]=0, and sign(K_A)=A.

**Proof.** Put σ'=AσA. By assumption σ and σ' commute, so all displayed scalar functional calculus expressions are legitimate. Conjugation by A interchanges σ and σ'; the symmetric expression defining D_A is consequently A-invariant. Thus D_A commutes with A. Furthermore Aσ=σ'A and A√σ=√σ' A. Therefore

σ(D_AA)+(D_AA)σ=D_A(σ+σ')A=2√σ√σ' A=2√σ A√σ.

The faithful Lyapunov equation has a unique solution, proving K_A=D_AA. Simultaneous diagonalization of positive D_A and involution A gives eigenvalues ±d with d>0; their signs are precisely the eigenvalues of A. Hence sign(K_A)=A. □

Consequently {K_a}'⊆{A_a}' for any involution frame satisfying [σ,A_aσA_a]=0 for every a. Ordinary irreducibility implies scalar dressed commutant and singleton exact SLD fiber. No perturbation or temperature limit is used.

The commuting-conjugate assumption is substantive. A arbitrary faithful state need not satisfy it, including for physical local Paulis. General CP positivity, involution spectra, or Lie closure does not prove it.

## Stabilizer-diagonal and commuting Pauli consequences

For n qubits, suppose σ is diagonal in any complete stabilizer basis, equivalently a Clifford conjugate of a computational-basis diagonal faithful state. Every physical Pauli A conjugates the corresponding maximal Abelian Pauli algebra into itself: it changes signs of its generators. Thus σ and AσA lie in the same Abelian algebra and commute. The fixed physical local X/Z frame and the XYZ frame have scalar ordinary commutant; the theorem proves their SLD scores identify every such σ against every faithful competitor, not merely against competitors in the stabilizer-diagonal subclass.

More generally, let H=Σ_P J_P P be an arbitrary commuting Hermitian Pauli Hamiltonian with real coefficients. Its Gibbs state σ=e^−βH/Tr(e^−βH) is faithful at every finite real β. For every physical Pauli A, H'=AHA is in the same Abelian algebra. The sign theorem applies, including highly entangled Gibbs eigenbases and arbitrary interaction order.

This covers graph-state/cluster stabilizer Hamiltonians and other commuting Pauli models, not only Hamiltonians diagonal in the physical computational basis. It imposes no claim about unknown model acquisition, copy complexity, circuit efficiency, or robust score estimation.

## Quantitative Frobenius commutator gap

Let r_A=λ_min(D_A)>0. For any complex Q,

||[Q,K_A]||_F²≥r_A²||[Q,A]||_F².

**Proof.** In a joint eigenbasis of D_A,A, write eigenvalues k_i=a_i d_i with a_i∈{−1,1}, d_i≥r_A. The commutator entries are (k_j−k_i)Q_ij and (a_j−a_i)Q_ij respectively. If a_i=a_j the second vanishes. Otherwise |k_j−k_i|=d_i+d_j≥2r_A=r_A|a_j−a_i|. Sum the entrywise inequalities. □

For the commuting Pauli Gibbs case,

D_A=sech(β(H−AHA)/2),

and H−AHA=2Σ_{P:AP=−PA}J_P P. Therefore, with J_A=Σ_{P:AP=−PA}|J_P|,

r_A≥sech(|β|J_A).

Take J_loc=max_A J_A over the selected physical local frame. Then, for every complex traceless Q,

Σ_{i=1}^n Σ_{A∈{X_i,Y_i,Z_i}} ||[Q,T_σ(A)]||_F²
 ≥8 sech²(|β|J_loc)||Q||_F²,

and for physical X/Z alone the corresponding bound is

Σ_{i=1}^n (||[Q,T_σ(X_i)]||_F²+||[Q,T_σ(Z_i)]||_F²)
 ≥4 sech²(|β|J_loc)||Q||_F².

The undressed constants follow exactly by expanding Q in the orthogonal Pauli basis: a nonidentity Pauli string anticommutes with at least two local XYZ labels, or at least one local X/Z label, and an anticommutator contribution is 4 times its squared Frobenius coefficient. Identity alone is excluded by Tr Q=0. The constants 8 and 4 are attained in the undressed case by suitable weight-one Paulis.

If each site meets at most Δ interaction terms of magnitude≤J, then J_loc≤ΔJ. The resulting lower bound is independent of n for every fixed finite β, Δ and J. It can be exponentially small as |β|ΔJ grows; no uniformly temperature-independent stability is claimed. This is a Frobenius dressed commutator gap. Transfer to the source's harmonic SLD metric requires its separate metric comparison; that transfer is not silently asserted here.

## Costs, prior art and remaining question

Input promise: exact physical Pauli derivations and a faithful commuting-Pauli/stabilizer-diagonal target. Exact full score operators are supplied; acquiring them is not free in a real experiment. The sign theorem is a functional-calculus certificate and does not require estimating the target eigensystem, but using it as a computational recovery algorithm would still require score acquisition and numerical conditioning analysis.

Nearest verified source claims are Dong–Leng's dressed-commutant criterion and high-temperature local theorem. b_math's earlier diagonal all-temperature argument covers physical computational-basis diagonal states. The present sign recovery extends that mechanism to commuting conjugates and entangled stabilizer bases. Functional calculus and commuting Pauli models are classical; a literature search has not established scientific novelty for this deduction.

Universal physical XYZ question remains open here. The load-bearing missing implication is that [Q,T_σ(X_i)]=[Q,T_σ(Y_i)]=[Q,T_σ(Z_i)]=0 forces Q scalar for an arbitrary faithful σ. Local SU(2) Lie closure alone is not a proof: T_σ need not preserve commutators. A falsifiable next step is either a proof using additional Lie/Casimir structure without commuting-conjugate assumptions, or an exact full-XYZ counterexample with genuinely noncommuting σ-conjugates.
