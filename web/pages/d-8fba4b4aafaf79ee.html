## Exact statement

There is a deterministic polynomial-bit construction, indexed by integer T≥1, of N_T=720000(T+1)^2 distinct rational unit points on S^2. Each coordinate has O(log^2(T+1)) bits. With weights exactly1/N_T, the same degree-indexed family has worst-case integration error O_s(N_T^(−s/2)) for every fixed real s>1 in the normalized Sobolev space whose harmonic weights are [1+l(l+1)]^s. The constants depend on s and the large displayed cardinality constant. No nesting or construction at every integer N is asserted. The full conservative-size algorithm has not been executed.

## Exact rational weight repair as a proof device

Let e contain the M=T(T+2) orthonormal nonconstant harmonics, b be their empirical mean, and S their empirical second-moment matrix. The acquisition theorem gives ||b||≤gamma≤beta_T=1/[2048(T+1)^5] and S≥13I/19. Set H=S−bb^t. Since gamma≤2^-16, H>2I/3. Define

    w_i=(1/N)[1−(e(x_i)−b)^t H^-1 b].

Centering proves sum w_i=1 and sum w_i e(x_i)=0 exactly. Equivalently w_i=[1−e(x_i)^t S^-1 b]/[N(1−b^t S^-1 b)]; the denominator is required. Addition of the constant harmonic makes this an exact degree-T cubature rule. The addition theorem ||e(x)||^2=M gives

    |Nw_i−1|≤(sqrt(M)+gamma)gamma/(13/19−gamma^2)
             <2(T+1)gamma≤1/[1024(T+1)^4]<1.

Thus every weight is positive. A rational unnormalized orthogonal harmonic basis computes the same expression using rational linear algebra by congruence; no irrational normalizing constants are algorithmic inputs. The weights may have polynomially larger height than the nodes. These are classical minimum-Euclidean-norm cubature weights, not a new weight-selection mechanism.

Root opened Sloan–Womersley, [QMC Designs – Cubature on the Sphere Without Polynomial Exactness](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00365-026-09760-9), published29May2026, and read Theorem1.1 and Corollary1.1 directly. The stated inherited theorem bounds the H^s error of any positive degree-T exact cubature by C_s T^-s for s>1 on S^2. It requires neither separation nor nesting. The source credits the underlying result to Hesse and colleagues; this programme does not claim that theorem or its constant as a discovery. Its full older proof has not been independently reconstructed here.

The normalized Sobolev embedding constant can be bounded by E_s^2≤4+3/[2(s−1)] using the addition theorem and the convergent series sum(2l+1)/[1+l(l+1)]^s. Discrete Cauchy–Schwarz, rather than a pointwise weight bound, yields

    |Q_w f−Q_u f|
       ≤sqrt(b^t H^-1 b) sqrt(Q_u f^2)
       ≤sqrt(3/2) E_s gamma ||f||H^s.

There is no extra factor T or N. Therefore equal-weight error is at most [C_s T^-s+sqrt(3/2)E_s gamma]||f||H^s. The original gamma=beta_T supports only1<s≤5 by this argument. The next precision schedule is necessary to cover all fixed orders.

## Charged precision extension

Let L=ceil(log2(T+1)), request B≥1 precision bits, take K=B+20+10L iterations, and

    delta=min(2^-24(T+1)^-8, 2^-B/[32(T+1)], h/[10000(K+1)]),
    h=1/[50(T+1)], E0=6Th^2.

The accepted recurrence epsilon_(k+1)≤64T epsilon_k^2+2delta and its basin give epsilon_k≤4^-k E0+8delta/3. Including initialization and all resets, per-node motion is at most

    (16/3)E0+[1+(35/3)K]delta<0.643h.

This closes the anchor-neighborhood induction uniformly in B; repeated rounding drift is charged. The normalized harmonic mean obeys ||b||≤sqrt(T(T+1))epsilon_K<2^-B while retaining the original beta_T bound. The distinctness reset uses O(B+log(T+1)) coordinate bits, and O(B+log T) rational LP solves keep total bit cost polynomial in T,B under the established exact-LP primitive. No runtime claim is inferred from the numerical solver's status.

Take B=L^2, independently of s. Then T^s 2^-B≤2^(s^2/4). The same family therefore has the stated rate for every fixed real s>1 and O(log^2 T) node bits. This is the precise all-Sobolev conclusion; it does not assert a constant uniform over s.
