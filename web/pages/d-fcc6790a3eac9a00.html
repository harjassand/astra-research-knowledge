RARE-SECTOR DATA-HIDING TENSOR AMPLIFICATION -- EXACT FAILED GATE
2026-10-09. foundational_transfer_sol. Independent Cycle07 addendum.

PURPOSE AND STATUS

This attacks the plausible idea that a rare high-dimensional entangled
sector has very small CMI, while sufficiently many copies amplify its
trace entanglement. The sector is an exterior-power state, but the NEW
mechanism is UNFLAGGED dilution against its exact-marginal product state
followed by arbitrary tensor amplification; its losses cannot be inferred
by weighted-flag intuition. The computation is exact. It rules out this
whole finite family, including unbounded-information parameters, as a
counterexample to two-small-CMI stability. It does not rule out arbitrary
private/data-hiding constructions.

1. FINITE FAMILY AND ALL MARGINALS

Let m>=1, k=2m+1 and d>=4m. Put
 omega=P_wedge^k/binom(d,k)
on k particles, A oneparticle, B and C m each. Its BC marginal is
 kappa=P_wedge^(2m)/binom(d,2m), and A marginal is I_d/d.
For 0<=q<=1 define
 rho_q=(1-q)(I_A/d tensor kappa)+q omega.              (1)
All states are positive normalized; A and BC marginals are fixed exactly.
The mixture is NOT flagged by a classical register.

For r=m or r=2m, put
 Q_r=I_A tensor P_wedge^r,
 D_r=d binom(d,r), R_r=binom(d,r+1),
 a_r=R_r/D_r=(d-r)/(d(r+1)).
On Q_r, the A:(r-particle) marginal equals
 rho_(q,r)=(1-q)Q_r/D_r+q P_wedge^(r+1)/R_r.
It has two uniform eigenvalue blocks, the fully antisymmetric block of
probability v_r=a_r+q(1-a_r) and its orthogonal complement of probability
1-v_r. Its product marginal Q_r/D_r has block probabilities a_r,1-a_r.
Therefore exactly
 I(A:r particles)=F(q,a_r),
 F(q,a)=D_Ber(a+q(1-a)||a).                          (2)
No asymptotic entropy continuity or approximate block orthogonality enters.

Set a=a_(2m), A0=a_m. Both conditional mutual informations are equal:
 delta_B=delta_C=g(q)=F(q,a)-F(q,A0).                 (3)
I(A:B)=F(q,A0) may become arbitrarily large as m,d grow, even for q->0.
Thus the old fixed-information theorem alone is not the exclusion below.

2. AN EXPLICIT SEPARABLE STATE AT THE PROJECTOR BOUNDARY

For general r<d, choose orthonormal a,e_1,...,e_r in C^d. Haar-twirl the
PRODUCT A:(r-particle) state
 |a><a| tensor |e_1 wedge...wedge e_r><e_1 wedge...wedge e_r|
under the same U(d) rotation on all r+1 particles. The result S_r is
separable and preserves the original uniform A and wedge-r marginals.
Its antisymmetric-projector probability is
 s_r=1/(r+1).
Its exact form is
 S_r=s_r P_wedge^(r+1)/R_r
      +(1-s_r)(Q_r-P_wedge^(r+1))/(D_r-R_r).          (4)

Why the two uniform blocks in (4) are rigorous: on Q_r the commutant of
U(d) is spanned by Q_r and P_wedge^(r+1). Here is a finite direct proof.

An operator commuting with U(d)^(tensor (r+1)) also commutes with all
GL(d,C) tensor powers: differentiate uniform unitary actions, complexify
the Hermitian Lie generators and exponentiate. On e_1 tensor...tensor
e_(r+1), diagonal phase invariance permits only permuted tuples, so
the operator agrees there with a linear combination of permutation
operators. By GL(d) invariance it agrees on every tuple of linearly
independent vectors; these are dense in all tuples because d>=r+1.
Continuity and spanning then prove the full permutation-commutant
statement. An operator on Q_r commuting with U can be extended by zero
to the full tensor space, so its commutant is the compression of that
permutation span. Since Q_r antisymmetrizes the r record particles,
compressed permutations in a fixed double coset of S_r within S_(r+1)
differ only by a sign. There are TWO double cosets, according as the A
slot stays fixed or is exchanged with a record slot. Hence the compressed
commutant has dimension at most2; Q_r and the full antisymmetrizer are
two linearly independent elements. The Haar-twirled density is uniform
on those two projector blocks. Its probabilities are fixed by the
overlap 1/(r+1), obtained by expanding the normalized Slater vector
of r+1 distinct modes. This proves (4) without an unexplained
representation multiplicity or an external source-specific lemma.

The product state Q_r/D_r has probability a_r<s_r. The probability
v_r in (1) reaches s_r at EXACTLY q=1/(d+1). Thus rho_(q,r) is separable
for 0<=q<=1/(d+1), by mixing the product state and S_r on that interval.
For larger q, the explicit separable approximation S_r has
 D(rho_(q,r)||S_r)=D_Ber(v_r||s_r).                 (5)
Both its original marginals are still exact.

3. THE TWO-CMI PRICE -- A UNIFORM LOWER BOUND

For d>=4m,
 1.5 a<=A0<=3a,  1/(2k)<=a<=1/k.
For q<1, direct differentiation of (2) gives
 g''(q)=(A0-a)/
 [(1-q)(a+q(1-a))(A0+q(1-A0))].                    (6)
Also g(0)=g'(0)=0. In particular g is convex.

When 0<=q<=a, every integration point u<=q has
 a+u(1-a)<=2a, A0+u(1-A0)<=4a, A0-a>=a/2.
Hence g''(u)>=1/(16a), and
 g(q)>=q^2/(32a)>=q^2 k/32.                         (7)
When q>=a, (7) at a and g'(a)>=1/16, together with convexity, give
 g(q)>=g(a)+(q-a)g'(a)>=q/32.                        (8)
The endpoint q=1 follows by continuity. These deliberately conservative
constants suffice; no limiting logarithm is used to discard a regime.

4. ARBITRARY TENSOR AMPLIFICATION CANNOT MAKE A COUNTEREXAMPLE

Take N>=1 copies and group the systems as A^N:(B^N C^N). Both conditional
mutual informations are EXACTLY N g(q). There are two legal separable
approximations preserving A^N and (BC)^N exactly.

Low-likelihood regime q<=a:
Below q=1/(d+1), rho_q itself is separable. Otherwise use S_(2m) from
(4), where s=1/k. The classical chi-square upper bound on relative
entropy gives
 D(rho_q||S_(2m))
 <=(v-s)^2/[s(1-s)]<=1.5 q^2 k<=48g(q).
Its tensor power is separable with the same marginals. Relative-entropy
additivity and Pinsker imply
 dist_Sep_exact_marginals(rho_q^(tensor N))
 <=sqrt(24 N g(q)).                                 (9)

High-likelihood regime q>=a:
Use the product state (I_A/d tensor kappa)^(tensor N). One-copy half-
trace error is q(1-a)<=q; tensor telescoping gives error <=Nq.
Equation (8) therefore yields
 dist_Sep_exact_marginals(rho_q^(tensor N))
 <=32 N g(q).                                       (10)
Cap (9)-(10) at1. Their maximum is a dimension-free function of Ng(q)
tending to zero. Any sequence with BOTH total CMIs tending to zero
thus has separable distance tending to zero, even if its total mutual
information diverges. No product-overlap multiplicativity, regularized
entanglement faithfulness, LOCC norm, or independent cloning premise
is used.

5. HOW THE TEMPTING COUNTERMECHANISM FAILS

At large q/a, the losses cost order q, while the entangled-sector weight
can only be amplified at cost Nq. At small q/a, the sector sits close
in relative entropy to the explicit separable twirl, at cost order q^2 k;
the CMI has the SAME scale, so tensor Pinsker defeats that regime too.
Replacing this calculation by a rare-flag average would miss the
quadratic regime; treating that quadratic regime as an undiluted
constant entanglement signal would falsely announce a counterexample.
