# Entropy hidden at arbitrarily deep scales: mechanism and acquisition boundary

Status: full technical read and independent reconstruction of the release148 argument; no decisive gap found in this first pass, but no independent external validation or transitive Lean build was performed. The strongest reusable step is the finite-law pair-capacity estimate plus adapted disjoint-window packing. The observation obstruction below is a separate exact construction, relying only on an older algebraic-contraction theorem for its nonzero-parameter dimension statement.

## Exact source question and interface

For a finite indexed family φ_i(x)=r_i x+t_i on R, 0<|r_i|<1, and strictly positive weights p_i, let μ be the self-similar measure, G_n the complete random affine composition, h=lim_n H(G_n)/n, and χ=−Σ_i p_i log2|r_i|. The authors claim

    dim_H μ=min(1,h/χ),

without separation or arithmetic assumptions and allowing signed unequal ratios, exact overlaps, and repeated symbols. The source uses lower Hausdorff measure dimension, which agrees with the almost-sure local dimension by Feng–Hu exact dimensionality. Complete map entropy rather than address entropy is essential when collisions occur. This is a statement about dimension, not absolute continuity, effective separation, or a finite membership algorithm.

## Reconstructed dependency chain

1. Upper bound: group n symbols into one map-valued block with entropy H(G_n) and mean contraction nχ. Typical-address coverings give dim≤H(G_n)/(nχ). Infimum over n and ambient dimension give min(1,h/χ). This needs no new entropy estimate.

2. Translation-averaged scale entropy H_s is the entropy of a randomly shifted grid, equal to h_differential(X+Uniform[0,s])−log2 s. For integer t/s, G_(s,t)=H_s−H_t is a conditional nested-grid entropy, lies in [0,log2(t/s)], is concave in the law, and does not decrease under independent convolution. The integer ratio is explicitly retained throughout the proof.

3. If μ has d<1, its one-bit deficits Δ_v=1−G_(v,2v)(μ) have a positive infimum over v>0. To reconstruct this: Δ_v equals mutual information about a fair translation by v after uniform smoothing. Small Δ_v makes the two smoothed translates close in total variation. A fixed K-way translated mixture consequently carries negligible label information, so G_(v,Kv) is almost log2 K. Stop the coding at absolute contraction approximately s/v; the stopped law is a finite mixture of signed affine copies of μ. Concavity transfers the K-window gain to every smaller s, losing a constant independent of K. Choosing K with (1−d)log2 K larger than that loss contradicts the entropy-dimension upper bound after telescoping. This works with negative and unequal ratios.

4. A fair pair with separation v∈(s,t) increases G_(s,t) by at least Δ_v−s/v−v/t. The two errors are precisely the fractions of random grid shifts for which a fine cell crosses a v-boundary or the pair crosses a coarse t-boundary. This is a mutual-information estimate, not a separation hypothesis.

5. For a finite law ν on F, the pair capacity c_ℓ is the largest mass of equal-weight pairs at distances [2^-ℓ,2^(1−ℓ)) whose endpoint masses fit beneath ν. Source Lemma3.2 proves, for every integer k≥1,

    k(H(ν)−H^0_ρ(ν))
      ≤log2|kF| + C_k Σ_(2^-ℓ≤2ρ)c_ℓ(ν),
    C_k=60000 k^4 is permitted.                    (1)

Independent reconstruction of the key charge: condition k independent coordinates on their current cells and their exact sum. Split cells by randomly perturbed cuts. If at most one coordinate has a large deviation from its conditional mean, its changed child label forces the sum to reveal this; remaining uncertainty can be charged to two simultaneous deviations. The flag entropy is bounded by a constant times the total exceptional probability because the two-deviation probability is quadratic. Random cuts bound exceptional probability by mean pair distance divided by parent scale. A symmetric within-cell coupling provides admissible pair mass; summing geometric parent scales charges each distance band by a bounded total, independent of the terminal separating depth. The finer grid need only eventually separate the finite support; its depth cancels from(1).

6. Condition blocks of b symbols on symbol counts. Counts fix the signed slope while costing at most |Λ|log2(b+1) bits per block. Conditional translation entropy therefore retains at least bh−|Λ|log2(b+1) bits per block even with exact collisions. A dimension deficit dχ<h allows b and a prescribed observation depth An to be chosen so the retained exact entropy exceeds grid entropy by εn. The support S of a length-n conditional translation satisfies

    log2|kS|≤n |Λ|^b log2(k+1).

Choose k after b so this sumset term is smaller than kεn/2. Equation(1) supplies expected pair capacity at least cn in a deterministic finite band [a_n,B_n], on the event E_n that the segment contraction depth is ≤A'n, where A'<A. Each B_n is a maximum over finitely many support-pair distances and type prefixes. No growth estimate on B_n is needed or asserted.

7. Conditional future is important: individual type-conditioned tails need not be nonsaturated. The unobserved type environment after j+n averages the tail back to a scaled copy of μ. Since Δ_v is convex, Jensen gives the average deficit≥δ. The pair decomposition at the retained segment is fixed by the prefix sigma-field F_(j+n); conditional gain is then at least (δ/2)c_(L−ceil σ_j) in a bounded entropy window [r/M,Mr]. Retention indicators belong to that same sigma-field. Conditioning on the entire infinite environment too early would destroy this justified use of μ's nonsaturation.

8. Choose segment lengths n_1<…<n_q only after all earlier B endpoints are known, with

    a_(n_i)−A'n_i−1>B_(n_h) for h<i.

For a fixed target depth L, adjusted depth L−ceil σ_j decreases with j. A retained fine-band window cannot reach the earlier coarse band before it ends. Retained index windows are therefore disjoint, so their entropy gains telescope to at most 2log2 M. Sum over target depths L=1,…,T. Each segment length n has at least T/(2Dn) candidate starts and capacity cn per start; n cancels. Every band supplies a fixed multiple of T, contradicting the bounded total for arbitrarily many bands. The argument needs finitely many prior endpoints, not a uniform separation rate.

## Reusable abstract packing lemma

Suppose a stationary marked sequence has positive increments bounded by D, cumulative depth σ_j, finite candidate bands [a_n,B_n], and adapted segment events E_n with depth increment≤A'n. Assume a_n−A'n→∞ and

    E[1_(E_n) Σ_(ℓ=a_n)^B_n c_(n,ℓ)]≥cn.

For every target L suppose monotone g_j(L)∈[0,C] obeys

    E[g_j−g_(j+n) | F_(j+n)] ≥ κ c_(n,L−ceil σ_j).

All c's, retention events, and σ_j involved must be prefix-measurable. Select q lengths so a_(n_i)−A'n_i−1 exceeds all earlier endpoints. Start windows at multiples of their respective length. At fixed L the retained windows are disjoint by the adjusted-depth order, hence total gain≤C. For a sufficiently large target range, every band index occurs once per candidate start. Summing gives

    CT ≥ κ c q T/(2D),     hence q≤2DC/(κc).

This contradicts arbitrary q. This is a reconstruction of the authors' mechanism, not an independently novel theorem. It can reopen arguments previously blocked on uniform scale endpoints if they supply the exact adapted gain and linear capacity hypotheses. The difficult new task in any application is proving those hypotheses; no general transfer to other entropy objects is claimed.

## Explicit finite-observation obstruction

Consider equal weights on the indexed maps

    φ0(x)=x/2,     φ1(x)=x/2+1,
    φ2,ε(x)=x/2+1+ε.

At ε=0, the last two symbols are the same map. The collapsed binary digit probabilities are (1/3,2/3), so h=H2(1/3)=0.918295834… and χ=1. Their open-interval pieces are disjoint apart from an endpoint, giving dim μ0=H2(1/3).

If ε is irrational, a length-n translation has the form

    A(w)+(1+ε)B(w),

where A and B are dyadic binary masks identifying positions occupied by the symbols1 and2. Equality of two translations forces (B−B')ε to be rational, hence B=B', then A=A'. A fixed-length binary mask is unique: its first nonzero coefficient exceeds the sum of all later coefficients in absolute value. Thus equal masks mean identical words. There are no exact overlaps at any length; different lengths have different slopes 2^-n. Rapaport's older Theorem2 applies because contraction1/2 is algebraic, giving dim με=1. This dimension contrast does not rely on the new release theorem.

Couple the coding of με and μ0 with identical symbols. Every digit displacement is either0 or ε, so

    |Xε−X0|≤|ε|Σ_(j≥0)2^-j=2|ε|.                (2)

Let observation be a grid cell at scale s, using an independent public uniform dither U∈[0,1). Under the common coding and common U, the labels differ on at most min(1,|Xε−X0|/s) of the dither range. Thus the joint law including the public U satisfies

    TV(observationε,observation0)≤min(1,2|ε|/s).

For N such samples at scales s≥2^-K, a union coupling gives joint TV≤2N|ε|2^K. Any fixed finite N and K can therefore be defeated by a sufficiently small irrational ε: the dimensions differ by 1−H2(1/3)>0.0817 while the data laws are arbitrarily close. Under equal priors, every classifier between the two parameters has error at least(1−TV)/2. This is an exact obstruction for that finite-depth observation interface, not a statement about exact symbolic parameter input or noiseless real oracles.

A concrete bound: ε=√2·2^-100, K=20, N=10^6 gives joint mismatch strictly below 3·10^6·2^-80, approximately2.49·10^-18, despite the fixed dimension gap. Repeated generators and nearly repeated generators are analytically and computationally different.

Executable evidence: work/frontier_dynamics/cycle19/overlap_observation.py enumerates exact dyadic masks through length8 and obtains 3^n generic maps versus2^n collapsed maps, with per-symbol entropies log2 3 and H2(1/3). It records the exact observation bound in results/frontier_dynamics/cycle19/OVERLAP_OBSERVATION_CHECKS.json. The all-length proof is the mask argument above; finite enumeration is only a check.

## Complete cost boundary and decisive remaining work

The new dimension proof has no supplied algorithmic input representation for arbitrary real translations. Its band endpoint can be found only after exact support construction and distinction of every pair. Block-type enumeration, conditional support generation, pair-capacity linear programs, endpoint bit precision, and increasing segment lengths are all uncharged by a purely qualitative theorem. Even for rational/algebraic inputs with decidable equality, no bit-complexity bound for the proof route is given. No claim of local tractability follows.

The observation obstruction is not a refutation of the formula or packing lemma. It shows why an effective application needs additional separation/encoding promises or explicit symbolic structural knowledge. The true universal mathematical dependency to recheck is finite-law Lemma3.2, especially the sum-conditioned two-exception entropy charge and shared-parent cuts, together with the adapted sigma-field in conditional gain. An independent proof reader plus an adversarial finite-law search is a more useful next allocation than further overview collection.

An ambient-dimension extension is also not automatic. Embedding a line-supported self-similar measure into R² can give h/χ>1 while dimension remains1; min(2,h/χ) would be false. A multidimensional analogue needs a hypothesis ensuring pair directions hit the unsaturated entropy directions. The one-dimensional nonsaturation gain cannot simply be copied unchanged.

Concrete downstream use: rigorous inverse-entropy arguments with arbitrarily fine near-collisions, and an exact benchmark separating qualitative dimension theorems from information acquisition. Novelty remains the authors' unseparated entropy-rate formula; our contribution is its reconstructed reusable interface and a complete finite-observation counterexample to an unsupported effective consequence.
