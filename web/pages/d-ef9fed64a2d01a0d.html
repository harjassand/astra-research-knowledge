RESULT

The baseline's universal factor-three four-hole bound is SHARP in its exact
canonical matrix class, including Q(i) inputs with all activities equal to1.
An explicit eight-by-eight rational-complex family has ratios approaching3.
At a fixed complex algebraic limit the ratio is exactly3. This rules out every
universal improvement C<3; it does not refute the coefficient-one square-root
exchange or any microscopic algorithm.

A separate exact gadget embeds an arbitrary complex vector-pair full-basis
partition, including every old ambient-hole coefficient, into a canonical
hard-BCS matrix of dimension n+2m WITHOUT extra coordinate-pair lines. This
is an exact polynomial-size reduction, and also an alternative way to prove
INITIAL's arbitrary-vector-pair signed stability from the baseline chiral
stability. The signed rank-two extraction proof remains a direct proof.

A. TWO-ROW CANONICAL EMBEDDING: COMPLETE PROOF

Supply arbitrary pairs (u_l,v_l) in C^n with nonnegative activities lambda_l,
l=1,...,m. In the theorem's algebraic existence proof absorb sqrt(lambda_l)
into v_l. In rational algorithms retain the activity on the second new pair
so no irrational square root is needed. For each l append two private ambient
coordinates s_l,t_l. Set every old-coordinate row of F' to zero and put
 row_{s_l}(F') = u_l^T+e_{t_l}^T,
 row_{t_l}(F') = v_l^T.
All other private entries vanish. The original ambient rows and columns are
in the first n positions, and u_l,v_l are extended by zeros in private rows.
The new canonical pairs are (e_i,row_i(F')^T). Give s_l pair activity1 and
t_l pair activity lambda_l (or row-scale v_l for an existence argument).

Consider a full paired determinant in the ambient row set R union all private
rows, where R is any retained subset of the old coordinates. Any nonzero
configuration must select every s_l pair, since row s_l has a nonzero entry
only in its identity column e_{s_l}; no distributed-vector column has an
s_l component. For each l there are exactly two remaining cases:

1. t_l pair absent. The only nonzero t_l-row component is the1 in the
   distributed column u_l+e_{t_l} belonging to the s_l pair. Expanding that
   row removes this column and e_{s_l}, contributing absolute factor1.
   The u_l component is irrelevant in that expansion.
2. t_l pair present. Expand along e_{s_l},e_{t_l}. The e_{t_l} component of
   u_l+e_{t_l} can be removed by a column operation against e_{t_l}, leaving
   u_l,v_l as the two old-row columns.

These row expansions commute over distinct private coordinates. Thus the
nonzero determinant configurations correspond bijectively to subsets A of
old lines, selecting the t_l pair precisely for l in A. The remaining old-row
matrix is V_A[R]. Full cardinality forces 2|A|=|R|. Squared determinant and
activity product are preserved exactly; row-order signs disappear in the norm.
No extra unwanted full configurations survive. Hence
 Z_{R union private}(F') = sum_{|A|=|R|/2} lambda_A |det V_A[R]|^2.
The old ambient-hole signature is exactly reproduced when all private
coordinates are kept occupied. In the signed polynomial, specializing private
hole variables to0 gives (-1)^m Q_old(x), owing to the2m extra ambient rows.
Signed stability follows from the baseline and real boundary specialization.

For Q(i) input and rational activities the matrix and activities are explicit
with O(nm+m) nonzero entries and polynomial bit length; zeros and dependencies
are retained. The two-row gadget preserves microscopic line configurations
bijectively at full private occupancy. It is not a coefficient oracle or a
sampler for the reduced instance. Fixed partial cardinality can first use the
baseline dummy-coordinate homogenization, with its exact factorial factor.

B. TWO DECOMPOSABLE BIVECTORS SATURATE THE FOUR-HOLE BOUND

Let rho have unit modulus and put
 u=(1,0,-rho^2,rho)^T,
 v=(0,1,rho,rho^2)^T.
Use the two arbitrary vector pairs (u,v) and (conj(u),conj(v)) in ambient
dimension4, with unit activities. Their bivector coordinates, ordered
(12,13,14,23,24,34), are
 a=(1,rho,rho^2,rho^2,-rho,-rho^4-rho^2).
They are decomposable by construction; the explicit Plucker identity is
 a12*a34-a13*a24+a14*a23=0.
Put c=Re rho and r=c^2. Since |rho|=1,
 |a34|^2=4r, and the other five |aij|^2 equal1.
The second pair has the conjugate bivector. For retained old-row pairs,
the sum of the two minor-square weights is therefore2, except retained34,
whose sum is8r. The empty retained set has weight1.

For all four old rows the only full paired configuration uses both lines.
Its determinant amplitude is the wedge product
 D=a12*conj(a34)+a34*conj(a12)
    -a13*conj(a24)-a24*conj(a13)
    +a14*conj(a23)+a23*conj(a14)
   =4+2Re(-rho^4-rho^2)
   =4+12r-16r^2.
Thus the full squared weight is D^2. Embedding A into a canonical eight-site
matrix gives the following f(U)=Z_[8]\U for holes among the FIRST FOUR sites:
 f(empty)=(4+12r-16r^2)^2,
 f(1234)=1,
 f(12)=8r,
 f(13)=f(14)=f(23)=f(24)=f(34)=2.
The FOUR private sites constitute a shared occupied core throughout.

Its three pairing products are16r,4,4. Consequently the four-hole ratio is
 R(r)=(4+12r-16r^2)^2/(16r+8).
The exact identity
 3(16r+8)-(4+12r-16r^2)^2
    =8(4r-1)^2(1+2r-2r^2)
shows R(r)<=3 for0<=r<=1 and equality at r=1/4. Choosing
rho=exp(2pi i/3) has r=1/4, f(empty)=36, all two-hole f=2, f(1234)=1.
The signed stable four-hole slice is, up to its global sign,
 36-2 sum_{i<j}x_i x_j+x_1x_2x_3x_4.
The factor-three squared inequality is equality36=3(4+4+4), and the
coefficient-one square-root inequality is equality6=2+2+2.
This is an actual F' matrix, not just an abstract stable signature.

Explicit rows of F' on old sites0,...,3 and private sites4,...,7:
 F'[4,0:4]=u^T, F'[4,5]=1,
 F'[5,0:4]=v^T,
 F'[6,0:4]=conj(u)^T, F'[6,7]=1,
 F'[7,0:4]=conj(v)^T;
all other entries zero. Every line activity is1. F' is zero-diagonal.

C. RATIONAL-COMPLEX SHARPNESS WITH BOUNDED ENTRIES

For rational t=a/b define
 rho=((b^2-a^2)+2ab i)/(a^2+b^2).
Then rho is in Q(i), has unit modulus and every nonzero entry of F' has
absolute value1. Let a,b be positive Pell solutions a^2-3b^2=1 obtained from
(a,b)=(2,1) by a'=2a+3b,b'=a+2b. Their t tends to sqrt3, so Re rho tends

violating the factor-C bound. The universal sharp constant is exactly3,

D. WHY THIS DEEPER ATTACK DOES NOT CLOSE SAMPLING

The embedding expands the class available for proof/auxiliary design and
shows that the canonical class already contains arbitrary paired-volume
representations. The sharp factor removes the hope that determinant-specific
structure would restore an improved universal squared four-hole inequality.
Neither result bounds a microscopic two-replica energy, gives balanced hole
partitions, or acquires conditional weighted samples. The bad natural-line-
label sector/FLC law, non-windability, and the precise existing sewing failures
remain consistent with this construction. No universal sampler hardness is
proved, and no FPRAS run or coherent preparation is reported.
