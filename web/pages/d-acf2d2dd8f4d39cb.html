Q04 result: the finite-n (g_L) bound is exact up to an explicit positive
remainder, and the current bounded spin-window witness cannot remove its
sqrt(log(1/rho)) cutoff loss.

Use ρ for the Bloch radius and q=(1-ρ)/(1+ρ). With
f(s)=Z_s(q)/(s+1), t_L=1/f(L), T_L=Z_L(q^2)/Z_L(q), and
g_L=T_L-t_L f(2L), for every L>=1,

  g_L = ρ/2 - 1/[2(2L+1)]
        + q^(L+1)[L(1+q^L)-sum_(s=1)^(L-1)q^s]
          /[(2L+1)(1+q)Z_L(q)].

The bracket is >=1, so the requested g_L bound holds for all 0<=ρ<=1;
L=0 is immediate. Also, for the exact Schur weights P_L,
E[L(L+2)]=3n+n(n-1)ρ^2 and
E[(L-nρ)^2]<=n(1-ρ)(3+ρ). The adjacent-sector ratio is

  P_(L+2)/P_L = [(n-L)(L+3)]/[(n+L+4)(L+1)q] * Z_(L+2)(q)/Z_L(q).

It decreases with L; whenever it is below 1 at J, it gives the exact
geometric tail certificate P(L>J)<=P_J R_J/(1-R_J).

Moment log-convexity f(L+K)^2<=f(2L)f(2K) proves the sector-transport
inequality t_L t_K f(L+K)<=(b_L+b_K)/2, b_L=t_L^2 f(2L)<=t_L. For the
effect with output blocks (t_K/C)q^(K/2-J_v) on L<=J, this controls every
transition inside the window and yields the exact master bound

  (3/2)e >= C^(-1) sum_(L<=J) P_L t_L g_L - P(L>J).

In the critical scaling ρ=c/sqrt(n), the exact sector law has the
noncentral-Maxwell limit

  p_c(x)=0.5 sqrt(2/pi)(x/c)[exp(-(x-c)^2/2)-exp(-(x+c)^2/2)].

A fixed window J=A sqrt(n) therefore leaves a positive tail as n grows.
Making that tail small enough for an error-scale-ρ lower bound requires
A=c+sqrt(2 log(1/ρ)+O(log log(1/ρ))); then C=t_J is
Theta_c(sqrt(log(1/ρ))). A fixed bulk with c x>1/2 contributes
Omega_c(ρ/C), giving the current Omega_c(ρ/sqrt(log(1/ρ))) scale.
Thus a bounded spin window cannot remove the loss within this witness.

The transport loss is real: for any finite support S, i in S, i>=1, and
sufficiently large j outside S,
t_j f(i+j)>t_i f(2i), since the left side tends to 1 and the right side is
f(2i)/f(i)<1. The corresponding feasible coupling flow raises the
transport-bound score, so this proof cannot simply omit the tail. This does
not rule out a different full-support witness or assert that the relaxed
coupling is attained by an EB channel.

Exact evidence is in `work/programmes/combinatorics_optimization/cycle3/qubit_compression/q04_moments/exact_moments.py` (all assertions use Fraction):
n=100, ρ=1/5, J=20 has P(L>J)=0.6218367168658; its feasible 20<->40
cycle raises the bounded-window coupling score. For n=361, ρ=1/19, an exact
five-cycle refutes diagonal dominance for the different unweighted
full-support kernel. These are finite checks, not an asymptotic proof.
