# C9 noncommutative Dirichlet lead

## Verdict

The Stinespring route provides an internally proved dimension-free estimate connecting the supplied broadcaster's compression defect to both approximate multiplicativity and pairwise commutators of observables. It preserves the exact input interface, includes negative HS spectrum, and exposes every operator-norm factor. The output is a useful structural certificate, not a dimension-free quantum-to-EB theorem.

The decisive gap remains the common-measurement step: for each positive HS dual Q, one canonical POVM must control the entire weighted frame with the homogeneous all-mode inequality. Pairwise low-energy commutation does not select that POVM. The estimates lose their dimension-free character on HS-unit directions whose operator norms reach order sqrt(d). ## Proven statements

For Phi CPTP, unital and HS-self-adjoint, and a supplied CPTP two-output broadcaster with both marginals Phi, let Gamma=B^*. In a Stinespring dilation of Gamma, compression defects satisfy

\[
\tau(K_{A\otimes I}^*K_{A\otimes I})
=\langle A,(I-\Phi^2)A\rangle_\tau
\le2\langle A,(I-\Phi)A\rangle_\tau.
\]

The inequality holds on the full spectrum [-1,1]; no HS-positivity assumption is used. For commuting-copy observables this yields, for all Hermitian A,B,

\[
\|[\Phi(A),\Phi(B)]\|_{2,\tau}
\le \sqrt2(\|A\|_\infty\sqrt{e(B)}+\|B\|_\infty\sqrt{e(A)}),
\]

and consequently

\[
\|[A,B]\|_{2,\tau}\le3\sqrt2(\|B\|_\infty\sqrt{e(A)}+\|A\|_\infty\sqrt{e(B)}).
\]

The same Stinespring defect yields

\[
\|\Phi(AB)-\Phi(A)\Phi(B)\|_{2,\tau}
\le\min\{\|A\|_\infty\sqrt{2e(B)},\|B\|_\infty\sqrt{2e(A)}\}
\]

for arbitrary A,B, with \(e(X)=\tau(X^*(I-\Phi)X)\). Complete proofs and assumptions are in PROOF_AND_GATE.md.

## Failed shortcuts and gates

Two fixed covariant POVM shortcuts fail exactly: independent product-Haar measurements under the universal qubit cloner have Z-score \(4(2-2\ln2)/9<1/3\); the symmetric-subspace coherent POVM under a copying Z-dephasing broadcaster has Z-score \(3-3\pi/4<1\). These only reject named fixed measurements. The true POVM is channel- and frame-adaptive, so neither result refutes the theorem.

The \(L^2\) multiplicativity estimate is not a CP-square representation. A prior exact qubit counterexample already rules out universal bistochastic \(\Phi=C^*C\) factorization while leaving EB domination true. That prior is cited rather than repeated.

The rank-one dual support gate is already handled by measuring that observable on the two outputs. This branch does not combine a growing mixed frame into one POVM. It does not claim that fixed-tuple almost-commuting theorems, square-root energy estimates, or dimension-dependent operator norms solve the all-Q gate.
