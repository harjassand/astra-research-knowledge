Date: 7 October 2026. Status: direct mathematical derivations with independent internal audit; no external correctness or priority certification. Completed results include interacting collective-experiment transfer, informative-scale classicalization under asymptotically conserved spin, a charged polynomial-time 1D quantum archive, sharp logarithmic Gibbs continuity order, an exact unequal-mode Gaussian reconstruction threshold, and finite near-tracial/pairwise-compatible families with maximal classical-reconstruction failure. ## 1. The changed premise and the two serious targets

The current primary preprint [Pilatowsky-Cameo, Styliaris, Bakshi, Malz, arXiv:2609.35973v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35973v1), Theorems 1–2 and Corollary 1, gives an exact block-product mixture for every bounded-range 1D Gibbs state at any fixed positive temperature and a polynomial-time approximate sampler of the pure-state mixture. The stated block/depth bound is double exponential in inverse temperature, with a range constant itself exponential in range. This is a real changed preparation premise. Its mixture and sampling circuit depend on the supplied Hamiltonian. It does not provide a parameter-independent measurement that preserves an unknown-Hamiltonian experiment.

Target A: a strong, full-density statistical reduction for interacting local Gibbs families, rather than another archive exponent. Exact SU(2) conservation permits a complete proof for collective fields and anisotropies. Approximate conservation and local-intensive perturbation replacement are the substantive escalation mechanisms below.

Target B: the exact boundary between thermal/preparation simplicity and inferential classicalization. A connected cluster-chain example obstructs inference from locally maximally mixed marginals. Growing-dimension accumulation was completed using an exact collective multimode Gaussian EB benchmark, followed by published fixed-d LAN and an existential diagonal sequence; see Section 6B.

## 2. Whole-experiment transfer from any SU(2)-invariant reference

Let the known faithful reference on a finite spin system be

    omega_N = direct_sum_j q_N(j) [I_(2j+1)/(2j+1) tensor xi_(N,j)].

The multiplicity state xi_(N,j) can be arbitrarily entangled. Permutation symmetry, locality, dimension of the multiplicity spaces and an identity multiplicity state are NOT assumptions. The representation and conditional reference states are supplied public data; acquiring or preparing them is charged separately.

Let a_N tend to infinity, R_N=j/a_N converge weakly under q_N to a known law q on [0,infinity), and for some delta>0 assume

    sup_N E_qN exp[(M+delta) R_N^2 + (B+delta) R_N] < infinity.

Unknown parameters range over real symmetric A with ||A||op<=M and vectors b with |b|<=B. Put

    H_N(A,b)=Q_A(J)/a_N^2 + b.J/a_N,
    Q_A(J)=sum_(i,k) A_ik (J_i J_k+J_k J_i)/2,
    rho_N(A,b)=omega_N exp(H_N(A,b))/Tr[omega_N exp(H_N(A,b))].

The product is valid because exact reference invariance implies [omega_N,H_N]=0. The entire experiment is uniformly equivalent, via fixed parameter-independent channels in both directions, to one sample (R,U) with reference q(dR) times uniform sphere measure and likelihood

    exp[R^2 U^T A U + R b.U] / Z(A,b).

Atoms, singular q, q=delta_m, and mass at R=0 are allowed. The reverse channel reconstructs the entire quantum density including xi_(N,j), not only magnetization moments. Error is O(a_N^(-1/2)) + o(1), where the unspecified o(1) is radial-coupling error; no rate follows from weak radial convergence alone.

### Proof: quantum reconstruction

In each spin-j factor apply the coherent measure-and-prepare channel B_j, and act as identity on multiplicity. The block-pinched map Bcal is unital and trace-preserving, preserves log omega_N, and acts on collective tensor ranks 1,2 with exact multipliers

    lambda_1=j/(j+1),
    lambda_2=j(2j-1)/[(j+1)(2j+3)].

The actual classical encoder discards multiplicity and its decoder resets it to known xi_j. That physical composition need not be unital on arbitrary input matrices. It agrees with Bcal ON THE GIVEN FAMILY; this distinction is essential.

For every unital trace-preserving CP map Bcal and tau=e^K/Tr e^K, operator Jensen for log gives

    D(tau||Bcal(tau)) <= Tr tau(K-Bcal(K)).

There is no exponentiation of a noncommuting operator inequality. With A0=A-(tr A)I/3, the exact tensor multipliers and ||Q_A0||<=||A0||j(j+1) imply

    D(rho_N||Bcal(rho_N))
      <= 3||A0|| E_rhoN[j]/a_N^2 + |b|/a_N
      = O(1/a_N).

The exponential slack gives the required uniformly bounded tilted E[j/a_N]. Pinsker yields trace distance O(a_N^(-1/2)).

### Proof: encoded law and radial coupling

The coherent measurement density in sector j is q_N(j) <j,U|e^Hj|j,U>/Z. Exact lower/upper symbols are

    h_j^- = c j(j+1)/a_N^2
             + j(j-1/2) U^T A0 U/a_N^2 + j b.U/a_N,
    h_j^+ = c j(j+1)/a_N^2
             + (j+1)(j+3/2) U^T A0 U/a_N^2
             + (j+1)b.U/a_N,
    c=tr A/3.

Scalar Jensen gives <U|e^Hj|U> >= e^(h_j^-); scalar Jensen applied to each eigenvector with the coherent resolution gives Tr e^Hj/(2j+1) <= integral e^(h_j^+). Both symbols differ from h(R_N,U)=R_N^2 U^TAU+R_N b.U by O((1+R_N)/a_N). Summation with exponential slack gives uniform O(1/a_N) TV approximation of the encoded law by its ideal classical tilt.

Choose a parameter-independent monotone/quantile coupling Gamma_N between q_N and q. Weak convergence implies R_N-R tends to zero in probability under this coupling. Exponential slack implies uniform integrability of the weights and, by compactness/equicontinuity of the parameter class,

    sup_(A,b) E_GammaN,mu |e^h(R_N,U)-e^h(R,U)| -> 0.

Use the two disintegrations of Gamma_N as the forward/backward radial Markov kernels. Atoms require this two-kernel construction; exact recovery of j after transport is not claimed. Preparing P_(j,U) tensor xi_j after the backward kernel completes the reverse channel. Normalizers stay bounded away from zero, so the weight L1 discrepancy bounds both TV deficiencies.

## 3. Approximate conservation: no total-Hamiltonian norm restriction

For omega=e^(-beta H)/Z and Hermitian A,

    ||[sqrt(omega),A]||HS^2
       <= (beta^2/4) Tr omega [H,A]^dagger[H,A].

Proof in the H eigenbasis: with p_i=e^(-beta E_i)/Z,

    (sqrt(p_i)-sqrt(p_j))^2/(p_i+p_j)
       = 1-sech(beta(E_i-E_j)/2)
       <= beta^2(E_i-E_j)^2/8.

The symmetric sum over p_i+p_j equals twice the omega-weighted squared commutator norm. This is an elementary Wigner–Yanase/energy estimate; historical priority is not asserted.

For a lattice spin system H_N=H_N^inv + lambda_N sum_x v_x, where H_N^inv is SU(2)-invariant, each v_x has uniformly bounded norm and support, and bounded overlap holds, set A_i=J_i/(NS) (spin S fixed). Then

    ||[H_N,A_i]||op = O(lambda_N),
    ||[A_i,A_j]||_(2,omegaN) = O(1/N).

Thus N39's near-modular commuting-grid archive sufficiency (or the independently reconstructed general_classicality theorem) applies to the bounded unknown macro-field Gibbs family exp(log omega_N+sum b_i A_i), fixed |b|_1<=R, whenever lambda_N->0. This allows N lambda_N->infinity; no trace-distance closeness between the known anisotropic and invariant references is required. The criterion is sufficient and not necessary. At the informative J/sqrt(N) fluctuation scale, one additionally needs uniform thermal tail/concentration and connected-correlation control; Section 6A proves these at sufficiently high temperature rather than inferring them from this macro-scale estimate.

## 4. Local intensive perturbations can be replaced in reference L2

Here is a useful exact Gibbs transfer lemma. Let omega be faithful, [B,omega]=0, and A,B be Hermitian with ||A||,||B||<=C. Define rho_A=e^(log omega+A)/Z_A and rho_B analog, D=A-B, epsilon=Tr omega D^2. Then

    D(rho_B||rho_A) <= e^(2C) k(C) epsilon,
    T(rho_A,rho_B) <= sqrt[e^(2C) k(C) epsilon/2],
    k(C)=(e^(2C)-1-2C)/(4C^2), with k(0)=1/2.

Proof: relative entropy equals log Tr exp(log rho_B+D) - Tr rho_B D. Golden–Thompson bounds the trace by Tr rho_B e^D. Scalar spectral calculus gives e^D<=I+D+k(C)D^2 for ||D||<=2C, and log y<=y-1 gives D(rho_B||rho_A)<=k(C)Tr rho_B D^2. Commutation and bounded B imply rho_B<=e^(2C)omega. Pinsker finishes. Neither A nor D need commute with omega or B. The constant can be optimized by centering D; it is already independent of Hilbert dimension and the least reference eigenvalue.

Therefore unknown physical weak local perturbations A_N(theta)=sum_x local_v_(theta,x)/N admit full-experiment replacement by a collective central polynomial B_N(theta)=f_theta(J/N), provided the following explicit load-bearing condition is proved:

    sup_theta Tr omega_N [A_N(theta)-B_N(theta)]^2 -> 0.

Combined with Section 2 this transfers a local Hamiltonian perturbation experiment to the classical order-parameter experiment. If this conditional self-averaging variance is O(1/N), the additional trace error is O(N^(-1/2)), even though the generator difference need not tend to zero in operator norm. Spherical magnetization moments alone do not prove this L2 condition for local anisotropy; phase uniqueness/completeness and local observable self-averaging remain separate hypotheses.

Independent direct proof and general noncentral logarithmic alternative: gibbs_l2_audit/CONTINUITY_PROOF.md. The central comparison is the relevant strongest version for a reference-commuting approximant.

For arbitrary noncentral A,B, the same audit derived and independently checked the sharp dimension-independent DECAY ORDER. Put L=log(4C^2/epsilon). Then

    T(rho_A,rho_B)
      <=min{1, e^(3C/2)(1+C/4)sqrt(epsilon)
                +2C^2(C+2)e^(2C)/(L+C)^2}.

Weighted Schatten interpolation and a telescoped Dyson expansion provide the upper bound. A faithful qubit reference diag(1-p,p), common off-diagonal perturbation C/sqrt(3), and rare diagonals +/-2C/3 give T~[4C^2/(3sqrt(3))]/log^2(1/epsilon), so no positive-power uniform Holder modulus in reference L2 exists generally. A qutrit improves the lower coefficient to C^2; the sharp coefficient across all dimensions remains open. The exact general modulus priority is unresolved, and standard machinery is explicitly cited in that audit.

## 6. Failure ledger and consequence limits

* Exact block separability, constant-depth known-Hamiltonian preparation and thermal area laws do not imply strong classical sufficiency for an unknown Hamiltonian. Nontracial product qubits already obstruct that inference; the boundary audit has a geometrically connected three-site cluster-interaction variant with every one-site marginal I/2.
* Vanishing pairwise tangent symplectic form alone does not imply whole-experiment classicalization: modularly generated conjugate observables can retain quantum information. N39 explicitly includes this obstruction.
* Direct raw TV convergence of discrete sector radius to a continuous radius is impossible. The two classical transport kernels solve the correct experiment deficiency problem.
* The multiplicity-reset decoder is not generally unital; use the spin-only unital map for Jensen and establish equality on the family.
* Scalar anisotropy at a deterministic radius is an ancillary normalization and cannot create another inference coordinate.
* Full-density approximation transfers every bounded decision problem and later POVM with additive trace-error bounds. It does not clone independent samples of the limiting classical experiment. Marginal broadcasting is possible; iid cloning is a different task.
* No circuit/runtime benefit follows from abstract CPTP channels when the known multiplicity reference states and radial couplings are supplied without efficient acquisition/preparation.
* Fixed-d near-tracial results are not dimension-uniform. The growing-d Gaussian-EB accumulation lower was subsequently completed and independently audited; see the addendum below. It remains an internally derived theorem, not external certification.

Strongest next attack: prove the local intensive anisotropy reference-L2 reduction in the released low-temperature Heisenberg model, or exhibit two ergodic equilibrium phases with the same magnetization direction but different local anisotropy means. That exact observation decides whether Section 4 yields a new full local-field experiment or only a conditional transfer theorem.

## 6E. Exact unequal thermal threshold and intrinsic pairwise boundary

UNEQUAL_THERMAL_THRESHOLD.md and independent gibbs_l2_audit/UNEQUAL_THERMAL_BENCHMARK_AUDIT.md extend the collective Gaussian benchmark to arbitrary known unequal thermal means n_i>=0. The exact minimax EB/PPT reconstruction error is TV(tensor_i Geom(n_i),tensor_i Geom(n_i+1)). The optimal witness is the finite-rank weighted photon likelihood region, which is coordinatewise decreasing. For arbitrary correlated/entangled seeds, phase twirling followed by a simultaneous coordinatewise Fock-amplifier coupling shows that this region's probability is maximized by vacuum. This closes the collective lower bound without a one-mode tensorization assumption.

For s=sum_i(n_i+1)^(-2), the exact bounds are

    1-exp(-s/8)<=error<=sqrt(1-2^(-s)).

Hence error->0 iff s->0, and error->1 iff s->infinity. If min_i n_i->infinity and s->c in (0,infinity), a direct triangular-array likelihood CLT gives error->2Phi(sqrt(c)/2)-1. A fixed cold mode invalidates that critical Gaussian profile even though the exact TV formula/bounds remain true. In fixed-d qudit LAN, s=sum_(i<j)[(p_i-p_j)/p_i]^2; near-tracial p_i=(1+u_i)/d give s~d sum_i u_i^2. This applies to the LAN oscillator experiment; any finite-n or growing-d qudit consequence separately charges LAN/localization errors and cannot assume uniform LAN rates.

boundary_audit/PAIRWISE_ENTROPY_BOUNDARY.md completes finite-dimensional FINITE experiments of faithful states with sup_pair[F_root-Tr(sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma))]->0 but arbitrary collective EB reconstruction error->1. For equal thermal means n and m=n^3, the exact supremum pair gap is (kappa-1)kappa^[-kappa/(kappa-1)]<=1/(eD^2)~1/(16en^2), D=(sqrt(n+1)+sqrt(n))^2, kappa=1+D^(-2), independent of m. Finite-box localization, Fock leakage n^-12 and finite 1/n nets preserve both limits. An explicit binary displacement code remains exponentially trace-distance separated after projection. Thus compactness alone does not suffice; uniform covering entropy matters. This provides a physical smooth thermal counterpart to general_classicality's mutually-unbiased-basis obstruction, and is compatible with its intrinsic criterion under uniform metric entropy.

For unequal thermal means the exact pairwise defect likewise depends only on kappa_*=max_i[1+(sqrt(n_i+1)+sqrt(n_i))^(-4)]: its supremum is (kappa_*-1)kappa_*^[-kappa_*/(kappa_*-1)]. If the coldest mode heats, this is asymptotic to max_i(n_i+1)^(-2)/(16e). Independent audit confirmed this last optimization. The full experiment therefore tests the SUM of inverse squared thermal means, while any single pair tests only their MAXIMUM.
