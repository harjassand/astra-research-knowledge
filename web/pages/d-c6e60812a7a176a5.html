## 1. Question, scope and answer

Can fields only on qubits having an ODD number of original Z-check
neighbors generate virtual check weights that make every old syndrome
energy simple? **No for the actual RX/RZ replacement family.** An interior
two-face patch gives an exact degeneracy inside the conserved original
X-check and logical-X sectors, for every choice and size of these odd-site
fields. Its CURRENT Gibbs mass has an N-independent positive lower bound.

This answers a calibration subproblem. It does not prove memory failure,
does not rule out a matrix argument within degenerate energy shells, and
does not classify the Hamiltonian with positive fields on EVERY qubit.
The latter includes the even-incidence qubits that our calibration
subfamily leaves unperturbed.

The authors' primary construction supplies the local cochain complexes
and connecting maps. The spectral symmetry and Gibbs estimate below are
our deductions, not claims attributed to the authors.

## 2. Literal carriers in the source construction

### RX carrier

Use an RX level and one parent square incident to a parent X-check x.
The primary source defines C_x=C^bullet(Y_x), where each parent square
is subdivided into N by N cells, N=(ell-1)/2, and two copies are joined
by the cylinder on the boundary of the entire square star. In the full
RX degree-one space, the C_x qubits are the EDGES of this grid; its
degree-two Z-checks are the FACES. The other degree-two rows are C_q
graph-edge checks and the C_z endpoint checks.

For an adjacent parent pair x,q, g_xq is restriction to the embedded
q-to-z graph Y_q. In each parent square these graph arms lie on the
parent boundary, with its doubled boundary cylinder; they do not traverse
the interior of a subdivided parent square. The square-based replacement
gives the same specification. Thus an edge strictly inside ONE parent
square and one sheet has precisely two C_x face-check neighbors and NO
C_q restriction row. There is no g_xz term.

This is read from paper.tex:1460-1475,1530-1667 and the literal primary
figures replacement-Cx.pdf and replacement-full.pdf. Both figures have
been rendered and inspected this cycle. This is an abstract cell
statement, unaffected by the legal physical flattening or coincident
embedded cells. It does not replace the actual inset mesh by a continuum.

Take N>=4 (ell>=9), as certainly holds in the admitted recursive family.
Label the subdivided faces by (i,j), 0<=i,j<N. Choose the two adjacent
faces f=(1,1), g=(2,1). Every edge of their union is strictly interior
to the parent square. Their seven edge qubits form R. Their common
edge is e. Put

    C_f=product_(a in boundary f) Z_a,
    C_g=product_(a in boundary g) Z_a,
    K=X_e,
    P=(I-C_f C_g)/2.

C_f and C_g have distinct supports, their product is a nonidentity
six-edge Z operator, and K anticommutes with each separately. Every
qubit of R has exactly TWO original Z-check neighbors. In particular,
no qubit of R belongs to the set ODD of odd-incidence qubits.

K commutes with every original check other than C_f,C_g. In particular,
K commutes with every original X-check. As a pure-X Pauli it also
commutes with any bare pure-X logical L. The existence of both CSS
colors elsewhere is a named inherited premise; no claim about every
corner is needed for this strictly interior patch.

### RZ carrier, including the final even levels

The source final family is C_(2k)=(RZ RX)^k C_(0); thus a result only on
RX intermediate levels would not automatically cover its final levels.
The literal dual carrier closes this scope gap. In RZ, C_z is the
TRANSPOSE cochain complex of the doubled square star Y_z. Degree-one
CZ qubits are its edges, and degree-two Z checks are its vertices.
The additional CQ-to-CZ map sends graph vertices to the corresponding
vertices in Y_z along the embedded q-to-x boundary arms and their
boundary cylinder. It has no support at strictly interior vertices of
a subdivided parent square.

Choose two adjacent interior grid vertices f=(1,1), g=(2,1), again
with N>=4. The corresponding original Z checks are the four-edge stars
at these vertices, with no CQ image contribution. Their supports share
exactly the edge e between f and g and have a seven-qubit union R.
Every such CZ edge maps under the local transpose differential to
exactly its two distinct vertex endpoints, and there is no other
degree-two target for a CZ edge. Hence every qubit of R has Z incidence
TWO. The same C_f,C_g,K,P definitions and all the operator identities
below apply, with vertex stars replacing face boundaries.

This carrier is read directly from primary paper.tex:1936-2000,
2028-2088 and the rendered primary replacement-Cz-RZ.pdf and
replacement-full-RZ.pdf figures. Transposition, not an assumed CSS
duality of an arbitrary boundary patch, supplies its exact rows.
It therefore exists at the FINAL even RZ levels of the source family.

## 3. Exact spectral symmetry, not an asymptotic self-energy

Let

    H0=sum_a (I-C_a)/2,
    H_odd=H0-sum_(i in ODD) epsilon_i X_i,
    epsilon_i>=0.

Amplitudes may be unequal, generic, or large. Every field acts outside
R. Therefore C_f,C_g and P commute with H_odd. Also K commutes with all
fields. On P the two faces have opposite signs, so their combined unit
penalty equals one:

    [(I-C_f)/2+(I-C_g)/2]P=P.

All other checks commute with K, and hence

    [H_odd,K]P=0,       [K,P]=0.                         (1)

Define the conditional flip

    T=KP+(I-P).

It is a Hermitian involution. Equation (1) gives [T,H_odd]=0. It
commutes with all original X-checks and L. Within P it interchanges
the C_f and C_g signs. Choose a current eigenvector psi in P that is
also a C_f eigenvector; T psi is an orthogonal current eigenvector with
the SAME energy and the SAME original X-check/L labels, but the opposite
C_f sign. Thus every eigenvalue of the P block has even multiplicity.

The P block is nonzero in every nonempty joint original-X-check/L
sector. Indeed those sector projectors are sums of pure-X Paulis,
whereas C_f C_g is a nonidentity pure-Z Pauli; trace orthogonality
shows that P has half the sector rank. No arbitrary assignment of an
illegal syndrome is used. Alternatively, an interior edge adjacent to
f on its opposite side flips f and a third face, making f,g have opposite
signs starting from a legal ground state.

Consequently odd-site virtual weights cannot acquire the ALL-syndrome
simple-spectrum premise used in the independently weighted cycle17
proof. This conclusion is exact at a finite level and persists over both
RX and RZ levels possessing a parent square with N>=4, including the
source's final even levels.

## 4. Arbitrarily weak even-edge fields rotate the degenerate pair

For j>0, keep the same odd-site fields and add only -jK. P still reduces
the Hamiltonian. For the paired eigenvectors just constructed, the
restriction is exactly

    H_odd-jK = E I - j [[0,1],[1,0]].                    (2)

The two eigenvectors are (psi+Tpsi)/sqrt(2) and
(psi-Tpsi)/sqrt(2), with eigenvalues E-j and E+j. Their overlap with
either C_f-labeled state is 1/sqrt(2), independently of how small j is.
This is exact pair mixing, not an omitted order-j^2 effect.

This subfamily has zero fields on most even-incidence sites; it does
not satisfy a strictly full-density positive-field promise. The separate
transport shell proof treats the all-positive finite-N direction
-tX_e-t^2 sum_(i!=e)X_i. Our symmetry alone does not pay that restoration.
Large syndrome rotation can be logically harmless: the original decoder
can assign the SAME logical label to these two locally correctable
configurations. Equation (2) therefore is not a logical leakage bound.

## 5. Current Gibbs mass without a field-norm penalty

Let rho_odd=e^(-beta H_odd)/Z_odd, beta>=0. Split

    H_odd=I_R tensor H_out+H_touch,

where H_touch is the sum of the original unit check penalties whose
supports meet R. Fields are outside R. The original code has at most
seven incident checks per qubit (primary bounded-degree premise), so
there are at most 7|R|=49 touching checks, including both CSS sectors.
Thus

    0<=H_touch<=M I,          M<=49.                     (3)

P commutes with every term. On the P block, (3) and eigenvalue minmax
give

    Z_odd,P >= e^(-beta M) Tr(P e^(-beta(I_R tensor H_out))).

On the full space H_odd>=I_R tensor H_out, so the SAME trace-eigenvalue
comparison gives

    Z_odd <= Tr e^(-beta(I_R tensor H_out)).

P is a local rank-half projector on R. Dividing yields

    Tr(rho_odd P) >= (1/2)e^(-beta M)
                  >= (1/2)e^(-49 beta).                 (4)

No operator-monotonicity claim about the exponential is used. These are
trace comparisons from ordered eigenvalues on reducing blocks. The
outside fields cancel in the rank ratio; no sum_i epsilon_i, intensive
field bound, or global partition-function comparison is charged.
At fixed beta this is a nonzero constant independent of the level,
number of qubits, and odd-site amplitudes. It may be astronomically small
at the historically calibrated large beta. It is sufficient to show that
the obstructing degenerate block is not an asymptotically vanishing
thermal event in this SUBFAMILY. Conditional Gibbs weights after fixing
all original X-check labels are not covered by (4).
