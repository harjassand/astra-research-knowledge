THEOREM
Let B_1,...,B_r be Hermitian tau-orthonormal operators in M_d,
tau=Tr/d, with sum_i B_i^2=rI. Let Phi be bistochastic UCP and have
BOTH marginals of an ACTUAL UCP J:M_d tensor M_d->M_d equal Phi.
Neither covariance, scalar action on the frame, normality, L2 positivity
nor L2 self-adjointness of Phi is needed. Define

  epsilon=1-(1/r)sum_i tau(B_i Phi(B_i)),
  q(B)=(1/(2r^2))sum_ij ||[B_i,B_j]||_(2,tau)^2.

Then

  q(B)<=36 epsilon.                                       (1)

The input normalization and actual extension are essential. This is a
floor on the averaged marginal Dirichlet energy, not an EB reconstruction
theorem by itself. It remains valid for frames mixing multiplicity copies.

PROOF
Write the supplied joint Stinespring representation as J(Z)=V^* pi(Z)V,
P=VV^*, with V isometric. Put

  F_i=pi(B_i tensor I), G_j=pi(I tensor B_j),
  M_i=V^*F_i V=V^*G_i V=Phi(B_i),
  L_i=(I-P)F_iV, R_j=(I-P)G_jV.

The output operators commute, and their square sums are rI. Define

  delta=r-sum_i tau(M_i^2),
  h=sum_i ||B_i-M_i||_2^2.

Kadison--Schwarz and trace preservation give sum_i M_i^2<=rI and
delta>=0. Direct expansion gives

  h=2r epsilon-delta>=0,
  0<=delta<=2r epsilon, 0<=h<=2r epsilon.                  (2)

On the enlarged Stinespring space,

  sum_i L_i L_i^*
   =(I-P)sum_i F_i P F_i(I-P)<=r(I-P),

and likewise for R. Normalized input traces give
sum_i||L_i||_2^2=sum_j||R_j||_2^2=delta. Consequently

  sum_ij ||L_i^*R_j||_2^2<=r delta,
  sum_ij ||R_j^*L_i||_2^2<=r delta.

The exact commuting-output identity is

  [M_i,M_j]=-L_i^*R_j+R_j^*L_i.

Thus

  sum_ij||[M_i,M_j]||_2^2<=4r delta<=8r^2 epsilon.          (3)

Now put C_i=B_i-M_i. Exact subtraction gives

  [B_i,B_j]-[M_i,M_j]=[C_i,B_j]+[M_i,C_j].

The square-sum identities and operator Kadison bound imply

  sum_ij||[C_i,B_j]||_2^2<=4r h,
  sum_ij||[M_i,C_j]||_2^2<=4r h.

For example expand each commutator into two terms and use
sum_j B_j^2=rI on both product orders; for the second use
sum_i M_i^2<=rI. Hence the subtraction error has squared direct-sum norm
at most16rh<=32r^2epsilon. Minkowski in the direct sum over i,j, with (3),
gives

  (sum_ij||[B_i,B_j]||_2^2)^(1/2)
   <=sqrt(8r^2epsilon)+sqrt(32r^2epsilon)
    =6sqrt(2)r sqrt(epsilon).

Square and divide by2r^2 to obtain (1). Every trace normalization refers
to the original input dimension d; there is no ambient Stinespring
dimension factor. An optimization using h+delta=2r epsilon can improve
36, but no such numerical improvement is needed for the continuation.

MULTIPLICITY USE WITHOUT COMPATIBILITY OF THE HERMITIAN PART
For a supplied irreducible compact-group carrier, any invariant Hermitian
tau-orthonormal frame has sum B_i^2=rI by Schur. Let Phi additionally be
covariant and S=(Phi+Phi^dagger)/2. Covariance of S permits diagonalizing
its self-adjoint multiplicity blocks. If B_i spans one real irreducible
eigencopy with S eigenvalue lambda, then epsilon=1-lambda in (1).
It is the ORIGINAL actually compatible Phi to which the Stinespring
proof is applied; S need not be CP or compatible. Thus a quantitative
lower q(B) on every such eigencopy gives an operator lower bound on I-S.

LIE PROJECTION ON AN ARBITRARY ADJOINT COPY
Let the carrier be a nontrivial irreducible representation of a compact
simple group with Hermitian Lie generators T_a and
tau(T_a T_b)=kappa_T delta_ab, kappa_T=C_Lambda/dim(g).
Take ANY real adjoint-copy frame B_i, indexed in the same orthonormal
Lie basis, with tau(B_i B_j)=delta_ij. Covariance gives
[T_a,B_i]=i f_ai j B_j. Let Q_a=T_a/sqrt(kappa_T). Cyclicity gives

  tau(Q_a[B_i,B_j])
    =tau([Q_a,B_i]B_j)=i f_ai j/sqrt(kappa_T).

Project the commutator onto the generator copy Q_a. Its squared norm
alone supplies

  q(B)>= C_adjoint/(2 C_Lambda).                          (4)

Indeed sum_aij f_ai j^2=dim(g) C_adjoint, and r=dim(g).
The bound is independent of which adjoint multiplicity copy was used,
even if its operators are nonmonotone quadratic expressions in T.
For SU3 in Tr_fund(t_a t_b)=delta_ab/2 normalization,
C_adjoint=3 and (4) is3/(2C2).
