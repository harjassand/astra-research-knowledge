# Systems and metabolic biology: research result and claim boundary

## Strongest result

The best constructive result from this lane is a finite-horizon CRN safety certificate that keeps *coupled* enzyme/resource rate constraints instead of replacing them with independent per-reaction rate intervals. For a facet (g_i(x)=a_i^Tx+b_i\ge0), define the controlled exponential generator

\[
H_i(x,k;\theta)=\sum_r k_r x^{y_r}\bigl(e^{-\theta a_i^T\nu_r}-1\bigr).
\]

If a supplied state-dependent allocation set (K(x)) gives a strict MGF margin \(\sup_{x\in Q,\,0\le g_i\le\ell_i,\,k\in K(x)}H_i(x,k;\theta_i)\le-\eta_i<0\), then the stopped molecule-count CTMC obeys an explicit exit bound after adding the exact (O(1/V)) falling-factorial correction. The complete theorem, proof, and certificate algorithm are in [the coupled-flux proof](resource_allocation/proofs/flux_coupled_exit.md). This is an internally reconstructed conditional refinement of Astra P/N35; it is not externally reviewed and no novelty claim is made.

The toy comparison is exact at the model level. In

\[
2A\xrightarrow{k_A} A+B,\qquad 2B\xrightarrow{k_B} A+B,\qquad A+B=V,
\]

a shared allocation (k_A=k_B=q\in[1,16]) points inward on (Q=[1/4,3/4]) with collar margin (7/16). The coupled MGF certificate uses \(\theta=1/2\), \(\eta=3/20\), (c=1/64), and (V_0=223). At (V=3000), it certifies exit probability below (2.169\times10^{-6}) up to (T=e^{23.4375}\approx1.5093\times10^{10}) model time units. If the same allocation is widened to independent ([1,16]^2), the drift at (x=1/4,k_A=16,k_B=1) is (-7/16), so the inward certificate fails. This establishes why preserving joint resource coupling matters in this example; it does not establish biological calibration.

The price of this bound is substantial. The global activity bound is \(\Lambda_Q\le10\), so exact thinning allows up to (V\Lambda_QT\approx4.53\times10^{14}) candidate events per run. The toy rates and coupling are stipulated, not measured. A real application still needs absolute count/volume calibration, integer stoichiometry and conserved-pool verification, enzyme/(k_{cat}) and isotope-flux data defining (K(x)), an acquired collar certificate, initial lattice compatibility, and a fair finite-state CTMDP comparator. Open feed, dilution, turnover, controller, and assay costs must be charged.

## Exact counterexample to a stochastic overreach

An equally load-bearing result is a precise obstruction to inferring stochastic long-run conclusions from deterministic attraction. For the network

\[
2C\rightleftarrows A+B+C\rightleftarrows3A+2B\rightleftarrows2C,
\qquad 2C\rightleftarrows2C+D,
\]

with all effective rates one, the open internal model is detailed balanced at ((1,1,1,1)) in the positive class (B+C=2). Standard complex-balanced global-attractor theory gives the deterministic conclusion. Yet for every (n\ge0), the count CTMC has a closed irreducible positive-recurrent class

\[
\Gamma_n=\{(n,0,2,d),(n+1,1,1,d),(n+3,2,0,d):d\ge0\}.
\]

Its exact stationary law is the product-Poisson law restricted to that class; (A\ge n) almost surely, so the family over (n) is not tight even though every class lies in the same deterministic stoichiometric plane. The count invariant (A-B(B+1)/2=n) is nonlinear and pathwise; it is not a deterministic conservation law. The complete reconstruction, exact balance checks, and chemostat bookkeeping are in [the candidate proof](metabolic_thermo/CANDIDATE_PROOF.md) and [report](metabolic_thermo/FINAL_REPORT.md). This strengthens source-summary U/N77 by making the three core links reversible, but historical novelty and physical realizability remain unknown. Another lane additionally showed that the reaction-vector lattice is saturated, so lattice saturation alone does not eliminate these catalyst/availability-separated communicating classes.

The same obstruction holds under the standard density-dependent propensities at each fixed integer volume (V): the deterministic plane is (b+c=2/V), its common equilibrium is ((1,1/V,1/V,1)), and the class stationary laws have (A/V\ge n/V). This does not contradict a fixed-positive-concentration law of large numbers because the conserved concentration plane approaches the boundary as (V\to\infty). The source U/N77 archive, proof, and code were unavailable at the pinned snapshot; our exact proof is an independent reconstruction, not a replay of its diagnostics.

## N33 deterministic bridge and its unresolved acquisition debt

P/N33 is the broad deterministic candidate examined here: finite actual exponent-arrow endotactic diagrams, all rates in a common compact positive interval, arbitrary measurable time paths, and one compact convex absorber per positive stoichiometric class. The key reconstructed step is a weighted essential-source inequality: an active affine slope (r\notin S^\perp) selects a lowest (r\)-source tier with positive projected drift; every negative projected reaction lies at a source at least (h^H) smaller in monomial weight. This produces (r\cdot f\ge\kappa Hh^M>0). The finite affine-minimum family then forms a compact plateau and a nonnested scale chain yields finite classwise entry.

The bridge to N35 is mathematically conditional and clear. N33's plateau is an intersection of finitely many affine halfspaces inside the positive class. Each proper relative facet has a nonneutral supporting slope. N33's drift margin on that facet, uniform over the rate box, extends by compactness and continuity to a positive collar. N35 then gives finite-horizon stochastic safety *if* integer molecular complexes, the lattice-compatible buffered start, and actual collar data are supplied. The N33 proof supplies no useful numeric bounds for the scale, affine-family size/precision, collar width, margin, or acquisition time. N34 preserves `UNKNOWN` when a finite checker cannot finish; R/N50's all-scale QE recognition is conditional and lacks an implemented general CAD engine or complexity bound.

The line-by-line internal attack and explicit collar/acquisition boundary are recorded in [N33 hostile audit](N33_HOSTILE_AUDIT.md). No proof defect was found in the supplied candidate's affine construction, weighted source comparison, measurable-rate chain rule, or nonnested scale chain; this is not external validation. A critical scope limit is that N35 starts with a buffered count already inside the certified set: deterministic N33 absorption does not supply a stochastic entry theorem.

The substantive possible N33 advance is arbitrary-dimensional all-endotactic permanence under measurable positive rate controls, not merely nonautonomy. Craciun–Nazarov–Pantea already prove a two-species time-varying endotactic permanence theorem. The N33 candidate survived our internal weighted-drift/tie/scale audit, but the Astra status remains source-derived and externally unverified. The separate OpenAI/math OA149 proof is weakly reversible and cannot stand in for the all-endotactic weighted essential-source argument.

## Applied stress tests and rejected routes

The ecological/chemostat lane reconstructed Hofbauer–Schreiber's invasion-graph theorem and found a source discrepancy in its periodic Monod example. With the caption's β=(1,+0.3,+0.2), ordinary RK4 gives two rare-invader amplitude roots near 0.3031558122 and 0.2949399516, but these are not interval-certified. It also finds an additional species-2-only candidate boundary orbit at amplitude 0.3 and a text/caption sign conflict for β₂. The extra community is invadable, so this does not overturn permanence; it means the article's worked example is not a turnkey certificate until its boundary table and parameter transcription are resolved. The necessary invasion-graph acyclicity condition has an exact May–Leonard counterexample when removed. Full details and scripts are in [chemostat_control](chemostat_control/FINAL_REPORT.md).

The enzyme-constraint lane derived the exact finite-horizon capacity comparison

\[
E(t)\le E_0e^{-\Gamma(t)}+\int_0^t\bar p(s)e^{-[\Gamma(t)-\Gamma(s)]}\,ds,
\qquad \int_0^T v_j\,dt\le\int_0^T\bar k(t)\bar E(t)\,dt.
\]

It exposes the transient gap between a static eFBA/TFA optimum and available enzyme reserve, but deFBA/r-deFBA already model that broad effect. It is an audit lemma, not a new dynamic-FBA framework. See [enzyme constraints](enzyme_constraints/FINAL_REPORT.md).

The reaction-inference lane proves that finite low-count observations cannot uniformly recover an unbounded reaction order: an immigration–death model and a model with a high-order autocatalytic reaction have exactly identical generators below the hidden threshold, while the latter can explode at high count. The construction is deliberately non-endotactic and does not refute N33. An acquired order bound plus exact generator data has established identifiability results, but no uniform noisy trajectory guarantee follows. See [reaction inference](reaction_inference/FINAL_REPORT.md).

The initial broad metabolic-affinity route was rejected: the proposed mass-balance/affinity “bridge” reduces to (v^TS^T\mu=\mu^TSv) and established thermodynamic FBA constraints. The stronger reversible N77 construction is a separate stationary-class counterexample; it does not establish a new general flux theorem. See [metabolic thermodynamics](metabolic_thermo/FINAL_REPORT.md).

The stochastic-deterministic lane also reconstructs the established finite-volume extinction mechanism: (A+B\to2B), (B\to A) has a stable positive ODE equilibrium for \(\beta>\delta\), while every finite count chain reaches extinction almost surely. The extinction action is \(\Delta=\log(\beta/\delta)-1+\delta/\beta\); at ratio 2, \(\Delta\approx0.193147\). Exponentially vanishing nucleation changes the stationary limit while leaving each fixed-time fluid limit unchanged. This is known Keizer-paradox territory and is not claimed as novel. See [the report and exact recurrence script](stochastic_deterministic/REPORT.md).

## Quantitative tests and next gates

For the coupled-flux certificate, a mathematical falsifier is one feasible measured state/allocation in a collar where the asserted (H_i\le-\eta_i) fails. An experimental falsifier is a replicated first-exit frequency with a valid lower confidence bound above the theorem's upper bound, after checking initial count, volume, propensity law, allocation membership, observation error, and model mismatch. Before any experiment, the decisive computational gate is: choose a real closed or explicitly budgeted metabolic subnetwork; fit a joint feasible flux polytope from matched isotope/proteomics data; certify stoichiometry/coset and strict collars; then compare the certificate with the same-information independent-box and finite-state CTMDP baselines on tractable volumes. No such measured system was available or run in this cycle.

For the reversible stationary-class example, prepare ((n+1,1,1,1)) in Γ_n, calibrate every propensity, and verify (B+C=2). The model predicts (A\ge n) at stationarity and exact phase weights; any forbidden transition, a reproducible stationary observation with (A<n), or a mismatched calibrated phase flux falsifies this model realization. Rare phase probabilities and cubic high-order rates make long-run sampling costly; no physical implementation was tested.
