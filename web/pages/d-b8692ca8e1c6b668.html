## N68: sparse NC polynomials

- Official primary source: Foram Lakhani and Partha Mukhopadhyay, “Hitting point for sparse noncommutative polynomials,” ECCC TR26-120 revision 2 (20 July 2026), official record: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/120/ . The record states a deterministic tuple in M_s(Z)^n, generated in poly(n,s) time, with O(s log n)-bit entries, testing zero versus nonzero for every polynomial with at most s words over C, under a matrix-algebra query. This is the source theorem. The proof was not independently audited in this leaf.

That acquisition/decoder is source-derived/unreviewed; do not identify it with the ECCC paper's dimension-s PIT theorem.
