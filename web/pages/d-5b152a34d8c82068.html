3. BRANCHING AND THE NORMALIZED-CHARACTER GAP VIA PIERI

Let A=diag(p_(n,1),...,p_(n,p)), with smallest eigenvalue p_min>b.
Write lambda_top=(m+mu1,...,m+mup). Under U(p) on the first p coordinates,
the lambda' carrier branches multiplicity one into dominant integral nu
with lambda'_i>=nu_i>=lambda'_(i+1), i=1,...,p. The last coordinate weight
is N=|lambda_top|-|nu|>=0. This follows from semistandard tableaux by
removing all entries d: their boxes form a horizontal strip. In particular
lambda_top/nu is a horizontal strip of N boxes.

The positive carrier seed is pi_lambda'(diag(A,b))/s_lambda'(A,b). Right
U(p) twirling makes it scalar c_nu on each U(p) component. Its vacuum
scalar is c0=s_lambda_top(A)/(r s_lambda'(A,b)), and

  c_nu/c0=b^N [s_nu(A)/dim V_nu]/[s_lambda_top(A)/r].      (3.1)

Here every physical nu is nonnegative. The following exact trace inequality
closes the required gap at arbitrary positive A, not only near aI.

Pieri says V_lambda_top is an invariant subspace of V_nu tensor Sym^N(C^p)
because lambda_top/nu is horizontal. Let P be its invariant orthogonal
projector. Its partial trace over Sym^N is (r/dim V_nu)I_nu by Schur's lemma.
Since Sym^N(A)>=p_min^N I,

 s_lambda_top(A)=Tr P[pi_nu(A) tensor Sym^N(A)]
 >=p_min^N Tr P[pi_nu(A) tensor I]
 =p_min^N (r/dim V_nu)s_nu(A).                            (3.2)

The positive difference need not commute with P for the trace inequality;
Tr of a positive operator against a positive projector is nonnegative.
The complexified representation commutes with its invariant P, giving the
first equality. Combining (3.1),(3.2) proves the EXACT outside gap

  0<c_nu<=c0 qbar^N, qbar=b/p_min<1, N>0.                (3.3)

The standard Pieri identity can be checked at the character level:
s_nu(x)prod_i(1-t x_i)^-1 equals the sum of s_lambda(x)t^(|lambda|-|nu|)
over horizontal strips. It follows from the Weyl alternant by summing the
geometric series in its interlacing row intervals; complete reducibility
and character independence then give the displayed invariant embedding.
Thus the normalized-character inequality is not a conjectured monotonicity
of Schur probabilities or a higher-dimensional floor-coupling analogue.
