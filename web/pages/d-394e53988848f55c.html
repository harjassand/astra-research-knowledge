## Input and exact linear lift

Let the observed rational homogeneous quartic have coefficient vector fhat. Suppose an acquired certificate consists of rational M>0, Ghat, lambda, w, alpha with A(Ghat)=fhat, Shat=Ghat-v(M)v(M)^T>=0, K=A*lambda>=0, and dual block [[K,-w],[-w^T,alpha]]>=0. Require 2w.v(H)=tr(M^-1 H) for every symmetric H. The exact observed gap is deltahat=lambda.fhat+alpha-n.

The unknown true quartic f has coefficient error e=f-fhat with ||e||_2<=epsilon. This is a declared deterministic uncertainty premise, or a confidence event acquired separately with its own costs. It is not estimated for free from the polynomial.

Construct one fixed rational right inverse R of A: for each degree-four monomial choose one unordered pair of degree-two monomials. Put its error coefficient in the corresponding diagonal Gram entry, or half the coefficient in each of the two symmetric off-diagonal entries. Different quartic monomials use disjoint Gram entries. Then

    A(R(e))=e,
    ||R(e)||_F^2=sum_diagonal e_a^2+(1/2)sum_offdiag e_a^2
                 <=||e||_2^2.

In particular ||R(e)||_op<=epsilon. The lift is selected entirely from the observed monomial convention, with no hidden frame.
