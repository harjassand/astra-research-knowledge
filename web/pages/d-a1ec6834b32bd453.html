# Primary-source notes and collision boundary

This is a scoped audit, not a comprehensive novelty search. The sources below are primary papers; page anchors refer to their arXiv-hosted text.

## Definitions matched to the reduction

1. **Craciun, Nazarov, Pantea (2011), “Persistence and permanence of mass-action and power-law dynamical systems.”** [arXiv:1010.3050](https://arxiv.org/html/1010.3050). Definition 13(ii) defines the (v)-essential subnetwork by deleting reactions whose reaction vectors are orthogonal to (v); Definition 13(iii) takes its essential support; Definition 14 gives endotacticity by the (v)-support condition (text around lines 228–254). This is the two-dimensional CRNT source for the ordinary essential-source convention reflected by RG-05. The paper proves permanence of two-species endotactic systems and does not classify the computational complexity of sparse rational-rate recognition.

2. **Gopalkrishnan, Miller, Shiu (2013), “A geometric approach to the Global Attractor Conjecture.”** [arXiv:1305.5303v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1305.5303v2). Definition 3.9 defines (w)-essential reactions and support among their reactants (lines 269–294); Remark 3.10 explicitly relates the authors' maximum-score orientation to the usual convention (lines 295–297). Definition 3.14(1)–(3) states ordinary endotacticity, while Definition 3.14(4) adds standard strong endotacticity: for each (w) not orthogonal to the stoichiometric subspace, an inward reaction must lie at a maximum among **all** reactants (lines 318–345). This is the decisive source for rejecting the child report's nonstandard essential-top-face label as standard strong endotacticity.

## Recognition / complexity neighbors

3. **Johnston, Pantea, Donnell (2014), “A computational approach to persistence, permanence, and endotacticity of biochemical reaction systems.”** [arXiv:1412.4662](https://arxiv.org/html/1412.4662). The abstract states that their MILP framework determines endotactic and strongly endotactic status for a chemical reaction network (lines 36–41). The formulation uses explicit stoichiometric and source-complex matrices and introduces direction/sign variables in its MILP section (lines 295–338); the paper closes with algorithmic recognition claims (around lines 600–601). This is relevant prior art for explicit finite diagrams. It does not state a complexity classification for the quotient-support encoding (P_e/Q_e). Materializing the RG-05 common-denominator lift can cause a Cartesian support expansion, so explicit-diagram input size need not be polynomial in the sparse rational description.

4. **Samal, Grigoriev, Fröhlich, Radulescu (2015), “Analysis of Reaction Network Systems Using Tropical Geometry.”** [arXiv:1508.05717](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05717). The abstract expressly covers polynomial or rational rate functions and computes tropical equilibrations by requiring dominant monomials of opposite signs to balance in each ODE coordinate (abstract, lines 9–17). It is a close rational/tropical analysis neighbor, but its endpoint is tropical-equilibration branch enumeration, not the all-directions active-source endotactic property.
