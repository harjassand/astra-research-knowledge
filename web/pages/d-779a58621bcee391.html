SU(3) degree-two 27 sector for regular carriers
Date: 2026-10-08

Status: exact normalization and one-copy coherent coefficient derived;
full 27 multiplicity-block comparison UNKNOWN. This note isolates the
Cartan-product compatible Choi witness on V_(a,b), a,b>=1. It does not
revisit the already settled V_(1,1) P27 full-space example, and it makes no
full-space or dimension-free conclusion.

1. Conventions and first low-degree copies
------------------------------------------

Let G=SU(3), H=V_(a,b), a,b>=1, and

  D=dim(H)=(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)/2,
  C2=(a^2+ab+b^2+3a+3b)/3.

Use Hermitian generators J_c=d pi(t_c), with t_c=lambda_c/2,
Tr_3(t_ct_d)=delta_cd/2, and the standard real SU(3) symmetric tensor
d_abc. The normalized Hilbert-Schmidt inner product on End(H) is
tau(X,Y)=Tr(X^dagger Y)/D.

The generator operators J_c form one adjoint copy in End(H). A quadratic
adjoint copy is K_a=d_abc J_b J_c. For regular (a,b), the orthogonalized
copy K_a-(C3/C2)J_a is independent of J_a; here
C3=(a-b)(2a+b+3)(a+2b+3)/18. In a highest-weight state |a,b>,

  <J_3>=a/2,   <J_8>=(a+2b)/(2 sqrt(3)),
  <K_3>=a(2a+4b+3)/12,
  <K_8>=(2a^2-4ab-4b^2+3a-6b)/(12 sqrt(3)).

The determinant of these two Cartan expectation vectors is
-ab(a+b+1)/(2 sqrt(3)), so the highest-weight seed has nonzero
projections onto both adjoint copies. This is a useful calibration, but the
target below is the 27 sector.

The multiplicity of V_(2,2) in End(V_(a,b)) is

  m_27=1                  if (a,b)=(1,1),
       2                  if min(a,b)=1<max(a,b),
       3                  if a,b>=2.

This is the LR multiplicity in V_(a,b) tensor V_(b,a). One copy is supplied
by the quadratic 27 component of Sym^2(8): for an orthonormal real tensor
basis E_A (A=1,...,27) of that component, define

  S_bc=(J_b J_c+J_c J_b)/2,
  Q_A=sum_bc E_(A,bc) S_bc.

The other copies when m_27>1 are not in this quadratic construction. Thus a
calculation on Q_A alone is not a calculation on the full 27 isotypic
space.

2. Exact coherent-orbit Gram normalization
-------------------------------------------

Put P=|a,b><a,b| for a unit highest-weight vector and define the
same-seed coherent EB channel

  Psi(X)=D integral_G Tr(P_g X) P_g dg,
  P_g=U_g P U_g^dagger.

Schur's lemma gives integral P_g dg=I/D, so Psi is trace preserving,
unital, and entanglement breaking. Choose a real Hermitian
tau-orthonormal tensor-operator frame B_(alpha,s) for the 27 isotypic
space, where alpha=1,...,m_27 labels copies and s=1,...,27 labels a basis
inside each copy. Define q_(alpha,s)=tau(B_(alpha,s)P). Haar orthogonality
gives the exact multiplicity transfer matrix

  M_(beta,alpha) = (D^2/27) sum_(s=1)^27
                       q_(beta,s) q_(alpha,s).              (1)

Thus M is a positive semidefinite Gram matrix. Formula (1) fixes the
normalization for every copy and allows coherent leakage/mixing between
copies. It does not imply rank one: the q_alpha are vectors in the
27-dimensional representation space, and the Gram rank can be as large as
m_27.

An exact diagonal entry of (1) is available for the quadratic copy Q_A.
SU(3) projector identities give

  sum_(b,c) tau(S_bc^2) = C2^2 - 3 C2/4,
  sum_a tau(K_a^2)       = C2^2/3 + C2/4,
  kappa_27 := tau(Q_A^2) = C2(3 C2-4)/120.               (2)

For the last line, the singlet and 8_s projectors in Sym^2(8) are
P_1=delta_ab delta_cd/8 and
(P_8s)_(ab,cd)=(3/5)d_abe d_cde; subtracting their squared norms from
the total S_bc norm leaves 27 kappa_27. The identities in the first two
lines follow from sum J_a^2=C2 I, the adjoint Casimir identity
sum_b J_b J_c J_b=(C2-3/2)J_c, and the SU(3) d-tensor contraction.

In the highest-weight state, the nonzero entries of s_bc=<S_bc> are

  s_33=a^2/4,  s_88=(a+2b)^2/12,
  s_38=s_83=a(a+2b)/(4 sqrt(3)),
  s_11=s_22=a/4,
  s_44=s_55=(a+b)/4,
  s_66=s_77=b/4.

Project this real symmetric tensor with P_27=I_sym-P_1-P_8s. Its squared
Frobenius norm is

  p_27 := ||P_27 s||_F^2 = N(a,b)/40,

  N(a,b)=3(a^4+b^4)+6ab(a^2+b^2)+9a^2b^2
         -6(a^3+b^3)-8ab(a+b)+3(a^2+b^2)+2ab.            (3)

For B_A=Q_A/sqrt(kappa_27), equation (1) therefore has the exact
quadratic-copy diagonal (Rayleigh quotient)

  M_(quad,quad)=p_27/(27 kappa_27)
                =N(a,b)/[3 A(A-4)],
  A=a^2+ab+b^2+3a+3b.                                    (4)

This is an eigenvalue when m_27=1, but only a diagonal/Rayleigh entry when
m_27>1. For example, at (1,1), (4) gives 7/135. On the regular wall
b=1 it reduces to a(3a^2+4)/[3(a+2)^2(a+4)], so the coherent capture on
this quadratic direction tends to 1 with deficit asymptotic to 8/a. For
a=b=n, it is n(27n^2-28n+8)/[9(n+2)(3n^2+6n-4)], with deficit
asymptotic to 136/(27n). These are properties of the coherent block only.

3. The actual Cartan-product compatible Choi state
--------------------------------------------------

The Cartan component V_(2a,2b) occurs once in Sym^2(H); call its
isometric embedding V:V_(2a,2b)->H tensor H. Frobenius reciprocity gives a
unique copy of V_(a,b) in bar(H) tensor V_(2a,2b). Let W:H->bar(H) tensor
V_(2a,2b) be an isometric intertwiner onto that copy, and let
Pi=WW^dagger. Embed the second factor through V and set

  Omega_RAB=(I_R tensor V) (Pi/D) (I_R tensor V^dagger).             (5)

This is a normalized positive invariant state. Its reference marginal is
I_bar(H)/D by irreducibility, so (5) is the normalized Choi state of an
actual CPTP map from H to H tensor H. Its outputs are swap symmetric
because V_(2a,2b) lies in Sym^2(H); tracing either output gives the same
covariant bistochastic marginal Phi. This is an actual compatibility
witness, not only a positive Choi-necessary star constraint.

In an orthonormal basis, if
W|r>=sum_(i,nu) C_(i,nu;r)|bar i> tensor |nu>, then
Pi_(i,nu;j,mu)=sum_r C_(i,nu;r) overline{C_(j,mu;r)}. This fixes the
CG phase-independent projector needed in (6); changing the phase of W
does not change Pi. The specific CG coefficient array has not been
tabulated here.

For any real Hermitian tau-orthonormal tensor frames B_(mu,alpha,s) and
B_(mu,beta,s) in End(H), the exact marginal transfer block of this
Cartan-product witness is

  L_(mu;beta,alpha) = (1/d_mu) sum_s
       Tr[ Omega_RAB (B_(mu,alpha,s)^T tensor
                       B_(mu,beta,s) tensor I_C) ].       (6)

Here d_mu is the irrep dimension. Equation (6) follows from the normalized
Choi identity Tr[J(Phi)(B_in^T tensor B_out)]
=tau(B_out Phi(B_in)); covariance makes the result independent of s.
In particular, taking mu=(2,2) gives an exact m_27-by-m_27 block L_27.
No self-adjointness of Phi is assumed; the quadratic-form comparison uses
L_27^sym=(L_27+L_27^T)/2.

4. The precise unresolved comparison
------------------------------------

For the root-seed coherent EB channel, the requested squared comparison on
the full 27 isotypic space is exactly

  (I-M_27)^2 <= C (I-L_27^sym),                            (7)

in Loewner order on the multiplicity space, with M_27 given by the full
Gram formula (1) and L_27 by the compatible Choi contraction (6). All
m_27 copies and their off-diagonal entries matter. Formula (4) only
controls one Rayleigh entry of M_27; it does not determine the eigenvectors
or eigenvalues of M_27, and it says nothing by itself about the matching
quadratic form of L_27. The Cartan-product Choi formula (6) specifies that
matching block exactly but its general-(a,b) SU(3) recoupling coefficients
have not been evaluated here.

Therefore the gate is unresolved: no uniform C for (7) has been proved,
and no sequence violating every fixed C has been constructed. In
particular, the near-one coherent Rayleigh quotient (4) is not a
counterexample: its squared deficit is of order (1/a)^2 on b=1, while the
compatible block and its mixing with the other copies still need to be
computed. Conversely, checking only this copy cannot establish (7) for the
full multiplicity block.

The existing SU(3) adjoint-copy C=2 result applies to the generator copy
and allows leakage to the other adjoint copy; it does not settle (7).
High-sector dimension bounds likewise do not cover d_mu=27 as D grows.
This note makes no inference about unrestricted full-space EB comparison,
bounded-Holevo experiments, or the global classicality implication.
