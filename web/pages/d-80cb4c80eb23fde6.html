STRONGEST CLAIM AND STATUS

REFUTED mechanism: even when a real rational hard-BCS pair matrix has zero
diagonal, every off-diagonal entry positive, every fixed-half-filling hard
configuration positive, and condition number at most 65/63, there is no
uniform polynomial-bit mixing guarantee for stationary Markov kernels which
replace fewer than all q selected pairs per step. A quantified exact family
below has reversible spectral gap at most 9*2^n*4^(-L), and arbitrary
stationary kernels in the same move class need at least
floor(4^L/(32*2^n)) steps to mix from a specified positive state to TV 1/4.

This strengthens the baseline disconnected permutation-cycle obstruction
to a dense, irreducible hard law. It excludes every stationary local kernel
in the stated move class, not just one unfavorable Metropolis proposal or a
single tiny determinant ratio. The construction itself admits an acquired
two-endpoint approximation when the displayed error bound is small, so it
is explicitly NOT approximation hardness for general parity counting.

UNRESOLVED main gate: no AP-hardness reduction, generic parity FPRAS,
microscopic defect/auxiliary energy theorem, or external novelty claim is
established here. Exact #P hardness and tiny acceptance probabilities have
not been used as substitutes for approximate hardness.

EXACT THEOREM

Let n=2q>=4, L>=n+2, with sites 0,...,n-1. Let P be the directed-cycle
permutation matrix, P[i,j]=1 exactly when j=i+1 modulo n. Define

 C[i,j]=(i+1)^j for i!=j, and C[i,i]=0,
 M=n^(n-1), H=n! (1+M)^n, delta=2^(-L)/H,
 F=P+delta C, a=2^(-L), r=2^n 4^(-L).

For every q-set I, define its complement J=V\I and

 w(I)=det(F[I,J])^2,
 Z=sum_(|I|=q) w(I)=c_q(F), pi(I)=w(I)/Z.

The identity with the legal c_q is direct: disjoint q-sets cover all n
sites, so J is forced to be I's complement. All pair activities are one.
Let E={0,2,...,n-2}, O={1,3,...,n-1} and B=Omega\{E,O}.

Then:
 (i) F has zero diagonal and strictly positive off-diagonal entries.
 (ii) Every w(I)>0. Thus ordinary one-pair Metropolis on q-sets is
      irreducible; the obstruction does not rely on disconnected support.
 (iii) Every singular value of F is in [1-a,1+a], hence
       kappa_2(F)<=(1+a)/(1-a)<=65/63.
 (iv) w(E),w(O) are in [(1-a)^2,(1+a)^2], and
      R=sum_(I in B)w(I)<=r. In particular pi(E),pi(O)>=1/3 and pi(B)<=r.
 (v) If a stationary kernel K on Omega obeys K(E,O)=0, then
      K(E,B)<=2r. If K is also reversible,
      gap(K)<=9r.
 (vi) For every stationary such K and every integer
      0<=t<=floor(1/(32r)), its distribution from E after t steps has
      TV distance greater than 1/4 from pi.

The condition K(E,O)=0 follows whenever each transition replaces at most
b<q selected pair labels: E and O are disjoint q-sets. No restriction on
how other local probabilities are computed is needed for the theorem.
The result also covers a kernel with extra retained registers if its
stationary marginal is pi and one step cannot move the pair label directly
from E to O: apply the same stationary-flow argument to the entire E fiber.
It does not cover auxiliary states with positive stationary mass outside
Omega, coordinated whole-cycle jumps, or a transition whose local word
contains q pair replacements before being counted as one step.

FULL DERIVATION

1. A rational perturbation with no complementary zero minors.

For ordered, distinct positive numbers x_1,...,x_s and strictly increasing
nonnegative integer exponents j_1,...,j_s, det(x_i^(j_l)) is nonzero. If it
were zero, a nonzero real polynomial with at most s distinct monomials
would vanish at the s distinct positive x_i. Such a polynomial has at most
s-1 distinct positive roots: factor out its smallest power, then apply
Rolle's theorem to its derivative, which has at most s-1 monomials, and
induct. The base case of one monomial has no positive root. Therefore every
square generalized Vandermonde minor is nonzero. For disjoint row and
column sets, none of C's deleted diagonal positions occurs. In particular
det(C[I,J]) is a nonzero integer for every disjoint pair I,J of equal size.

For any square s-minor, s<=n, the polynomial
 p_IJ(t)=det(P[I,J]+t C[I,J])
has integer coefficients. Its sum of absolute coefficient values is at
most s!(1+M)^s<=H, by expanding each of the s! determinant permutations
and each product of s entries. For disjoint I,J it is not identically zero,
since its top coefficient is det(C[I,J])!=0.

Write d for its first nonzero degree. That coefficient is a nonzero integer,
so has absolute value at least 1. At t=delta<=1, the remaining tail is
bounded by delta^(d+1) H. Since delta H=a<=1/64<1,

 |p_IJ(delta)| >= delta^d (1-delta H)>0.

This proves positivity of every hard configuration without random-generic
arguments or uncharged testing of exponentially many supports.

2. The original cycle has exactly two full-basis hard configurations.

P[I,J] has a nonzero determinant iff the cycle successor maps I onto J.
For J=V\I this means site membership alternates along the entire n-cycle.
Because n is even, the only possibilities are I=E and I=O. Their minors
are permutation matrices and their determinants have absolute value 1.
Every other I has p_IJ(0)=0. The coefficient-sum bound now implies

 |p_E(delta)-p_E(0)|<=delta H=a,
 |p_O(delta)-p_O(0)|<=delta H=a,
 |p_I(delta)|<=delta H=a for I outside {E,O}.

Consequently endpoint squared weights lie between (1-a)^2 and (1+a)^2,
and R<=binom(n,q)a^2<=2^n a^2=r.

3. Good conditioning does not prevent the bottleneck.

||C||_2<=||C||_F<=nM and H>=nM. Therefore
||F-P||_2<=delta nM<=a. As P is orthogonal, the triangle and reverse
triangle inequalities on ||Fx|| give singular values in [1-a,1+a].
Since n>=4 and L>=n+2, a<=1/64. Thus the condition-number assertion follows.
The graph Slater V=[I,F^T] is likewise full rank and well conditioned; its
nonzero singular values are sqrt(1+sigma_i(F)^2).

Also a<=1/16 and r<=1/16. Hence

 Z>=2(15/16)^2>1,
 Z<=2(17/16)^2+1/16=594/256,
 pi(E),pi(O)>=(225/256)/(594/256)=225/594>1/3,
 pi(B)=R/Z<=r.

4. A stationary-flow bound, rather than acceptance heuristics.

Stationarity gives
 pi(E) K(E,B) <= sum_x pi(x)K(x,B)=pi(B).
Because K(E,O)=0, the only exit from E is into B. Thus

 e=K(E,Omega\{E})<=pi(B)/pi(E)=R/w(E)<=2r.

For reversible K, test its Dirichlet variational characterization with
h=1_E. Then

 E_K(h,h)=pi(E)K(E,B)<=pi(B),
 Var_pi(h)=pi(E)(1-pi(E))>=pi(E)pi(O)>=1/9,
 gap(K)<=E_K(h,h)/Var_pi(h)<=9r.

This is the usual 1-lambda_2 gap; periodicity cannot improve the claimed
worst-case TV mixing guarantee. Laziness is not needed for the upper bound.

For arbitrary stationary K, starting at E, the probability of never leaving
E in t steps is (1-e)^t>=1-te>=1-2tr. Therefore

 TV(K^t(E,.),pi)>=K^t(E,E)-pi(E)
                 >=1-pi(E)-2tr>=pi(O)-2tr>=1/3-2tr.

At t<=1/(32r), this is at least 1/3-1/16=13/48>1/4. This proves (vi)
without reversibility. For an extra-register chain, initialize from its
stationary conditional law on the E fiber; the union bound on the stationary
exit flux gives the same fiber escape estimate. Therefore at least one
actual starting register state has the claimed worst-case slow mixing.

5. Explicit easy counting/sampling boundary for the same family.

Compute the two endpoint determinants exactly and output
 Z0=w(E)+w(O). Then 0<=Z-Z0=R<=r and
 |Z-Z0|/Z=pi(B)<=r. Independently sample E or O with their exact two weights.
The output law is pi conditioned on {E,O}, so its TV distance from pi is
exactly pi(B)<=r. Rational Bernoulli sampling can use integer rejection
with expected O(log(1+numerator denominator)) random bits, or a capped
O(log(1/eta)) extra-bit approximation with charged additive eta failure.
The output law requires only two polynomial-bit determinants and one
rational random-bit decision; no unknown partition, balancing ratio or
inverse rare-event probability is supplied for free.

This admits prescribed accuracy eps whenever the explicit bound r<=eps;
the theorem does not promise arbitrary-accuracy counting for every fixed
member, nor arbitrary conditioned-prefix sampling when both endpoints are
excluded. For fixed n, full enumeration is an alternative exact algorithm,
which makes the absence of any complexity-hardness conclusion particularly
transparent.

COSTS / SUPPLIED-ACQUIRED BOUNDARIES

The family is explicit from n and L. log_2 M=O(n log n),
log_2 H=O(n^2 log n), and each rational entry has
O(L+n^2 log n) bits. Writing the n-by-n matrix costs
O(n^2(L+n^2 log n)) bit output, up to standard encoding overhead. Exact
determinants of rational q-minors are polynomial bit computations. There
is no unbounded precision, unknown support oracle, free coefficient oracle,
free balancing data, or free sampler in the obstruction.

For any fixed move radius b, choose fixed n=2(b+1), then let L grow. The
input length is Theta(L) for that dimension, while the mixing lower bound
is exp(Omega(L)). If n also grows, the precise bound 4^L/(32*2^n) and the
full matrix encoding cost above must be retained rather than calling every
growth regime exponential in the entire encoding length.
