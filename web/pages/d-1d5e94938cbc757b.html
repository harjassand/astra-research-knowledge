## Independent reconstruction of the load-bearing argument

1. The word series h_ε=1, h_iw=J_i h_w are independent for every finite word set over characteristic zero. In the truncated free algebra of words of length≤D, analytic continuation around the pole−i yields Q_i=1+2πi X_i modulo degree≥2. Ordered products of (Q_i−1) have leading term (2πi)^|w|X_w. They form a triangular basis and lie in the span of loop values. A constant functional annihilating the original germ therefore annihilates the entire truncated free algebra. A nonzero rational Taylor minor transfers the result from C to every characteristic-zero field.
2. The usual acyclic path representation gives f_w=uB_wv. Scalar-edge elimination keeps B_i strictly upper triangular. Hence G=Σ_|w|<m B_w h_w is finite, G(0)=I, and G'=AG with A=Σ B_i/(z+i)=P/q, degq=n, degP≤n−1. The polynomial output h_f=uGv is nonzero by step1.
3. For h=uG, define rational derivative rows R₀=u and R_{j+1}=R_j'+R_jP/q. R_j=Z_j/q^j has numerator degree≤j(n−1). If the component span of h has dimension k≤m, the minimum orderδ of a nonzero k-row minor is at most(n−1)binom(k,2). Multiplication by G preserves the ideal of those minors over F[[z]]. A distinct-order basis a₁<⋯<a_k of the component span has Wronskian orderδ=Σ(a_j−j+1). Thus any nonzero scalar output has order≤a_k≤δ+k−1≤(n−1)binom(m,2)+m−1.
4. The explicit integration matrices retain that first nonzero coefficient. Their denominators are nonzero in characteristic zero and have polynomial bit length. This establishes the claimed native finite-acyclic guarantee without invoking the companion rational-formula theorem.

The authors themselves credit word independence / hyperlogarithms and Moura’s2003 multiplicity bound. The apparent new ingredient is their exact compilation into a uniformly sized noncommutative black-box hitting tuple. This is not a new multiplicity theorem from this investigation.

## Derived obstruction1: exponential shuffle closure

The obvious commutative repair replaces products by shuffle products. With x₁,…,x_n distinct, the commutative monomial p=x₁⋯x_n has a formula of2n−1 gates. Its shuffle lift is

$$ \mathrm{Sh}(p)=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}x_{\sigma(1)}\cdots x_{\sigma(n)}. $$

For any k, select one increasing word u_S for each k-subset S⊆[n] and one increasing word v_[n]\\T for each k-subset T. The coefficient of u_S v_[n]\\T is1 exactly when S=T. Consequently the degree-k / degree-(n−k) coefficient flattening contains an identity matrix of order binom(n,k).

Every m-dimensional coefficient representation f_w=uB_wv factors this flattening through F^m, so

$$ m\ge \binom{n}{k},\qquad m\ge\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}. $$

This applies even to cyclic weighted automata. A formula-to-acyclic conversion then also forces exponential formula size up to its constant factor. The full shuffle lift of an easy commutative formula cannot in general stay inside the polynomial-size acyclic representation required by116. This is a standard Nisan-rank style obstruction, newly applied here to the proposed transfer; no new general lower-bound claim.

The handoff’s commutator repair illustrates the complementary issue: for g=qz+ab−ba, a faithful noncommutative hitting point intentionally retains ab−ba. A zero abelianization of q cannot make g a noncommutative identity. Scalar evaluations of ab and ba agree at every point, yet their matrix evaluations differ. Ordinary scalar black-box access therefore does not provide the native ordered matrix oracle either.

What remains open: an efficient direct representation of the commutative quotient, or a different quotient-sensitive verifier. The exponential result excludes this full-shuffle implementation, not every commutative PIT approach.

## Derived obstruction2: the cyclic first-column extension is false

Consider the nonzero rational series

$$ f=\sum_{k\ge1}(x_1^k-2x_2^k)
   =x_1(1-x_1)^{-1}-2x_2(1-x_2)^{-1}. $$

It has a3-state coefficient representation with u=(1,0,0), v=(0,1,2)^T and

$$ B_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 B_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&-1\\0&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}. $$

The diagonal entries make this representation cyclic. Mixed words vanish; uB₁ᵏv=1 and uB₂ᵏv=−2 for every k≥1. Nevertheless its scalar hyperlog encoding is identically zero:

$$ \sum_{k\ge1}h_{1^k}-2\sum_{k\ge1}h_{2^k}
 =e^{\log(1+z)}-1-2(e^{\log(1+z/2)}-1)=0. $$

Equivalently, the differential-system column b(z)=(0,z+1,z+2)^T solves b'=(B₁/(z+1)+B₂/(z+2))b with b(0)=v, and ub≡0 although the rational series is nonzero.

For **every** finite source integration tuple, all inverses exist by nilpotence and f(T)e₀=0. This is an all-dimension mathematical identity, not a inference from the finite replay. It does not refute116’s acyclic polynomial theorem. Nor does it refute its rational-formula companion or assert f(T)=0 as a full matrix: for d≥4, the(r,q)=(3,1) entry of f(T) is−1/12. It specifically defeats the proposed direct extension of the scalar-output/first-column mechanism to cyclic weighted automata.

## Positive finite-state repair and complete access accounting

Let f_w=uA_wv be a supplied R-state weighted rational series. If f is nonzero, some word of length≤R−1 has nonzero coefficient: the reachable row spaces span{uA_w:|w|≤j} grow by at most R dimensions; once stable, they are stable under every A_i. Orthogonality to v through lengthR−1 would imply orthogonality for every word.

Unroll layers0,…,R−1, each with R states. B_i shifts one layer with block A_i, u starts on layer0 and v is copied to every layer. This is an acyclic representation with m=R² for the truncated polynomial f_≤R−1. Family116 therefore gives a single universal matrix tuple of dimension

$$ d=(n-1)\binom{R^2}{2}+R^2=O(nR^4) $$

detecting all-word inequivalence of the supplied models through one exact evaluation of that truncation. This repair avoids the false cyclic extension. The matrix construction is polynomial bit cost; evaluation is a polynomial number of rational matrix operations if the rational transition descriptions are supplied with finite bit size. Complete implementation needs the chosen multiplication and rational arithmetic costs; this note does not claim a superior white-box algorithm. Existing reachable-space equivalence tests already run in polynomial time on supplied models.

If only a matrix black box is supplied, its promise must include the bounded acyclic unrolling or an exact truncated-series evaluation. An infinite generating-function black box does not automatically return its truncation at the desired horizon. Ordinary statistical HMM samples do not provide exact evaluations. This is a changed interface, not a compiler for the original approximate arbitrary-rank teacher.

The stronger residual-feature learner below uses a direct proper-series matrix query, so it supersedes the truncation-access requirement for that specified interface.

## Stronger reopening: length marking makes cyclic recognizable series faithful

This composition was identified by `metric_prior` / `frontier_check_hitting` during this cycle and independently reconstructed here. Let F have characteristic zero, n,m≥1, and

$$ f_w=uA_wv,\quad A_i\in\operatorname{Mat}_m(F),\qquad
 d=(n-1)\binom m2+m. $$

No acyclicity is assumed. The interface is a **recognizable-series matrix oracle defined on every jointly nilpotent tuple**. Its semantics are the finite sum Σ_w f_w X_w there. An arbitrary syntactic rational formula can have additional domain restrictions, so properness of the represented series alone is not being used as a promise about every inverse gate of an arbitrary formula.

Introduce a formal length variable λ and the series

$$ G(z,\lambda)=\sum_w \lambda^{|w|}A_w h_w(z),\qquad
 y(z,\lambda)=uG(z,\lambda)v. $$

Every coefficient of z^r is a polynomial in λ of degree≤r, because h_w has vanishing order≥|w|. Thus these series are well-defined over F(λ)[[z]]. We have G(0,λ)=I and

$$ G'=\left(\sum_i\frac{\lambda A_i}{z+i}\right)G. $$

If f is nonzero, one homogeneous slice f_k=Σ_|w|=k f_wx_w is nonzero. Its finite hyperlog image is nonzero by the checked word-independence argument. This image is exactly the coefficient of λ^k in y, so y is nonzero over F(λ). The general Moura estimate applies to the same m-dimensional system (it never required nilpotent residues) and gives ord_z y≤d−1.

Let S_d be the nilpotent shift on coefficient columns in λ, with(S_d)_{r,q}=1 for r=q+1. Let T_i be the source z-jet matrices of size d. Put

$$ W_i=S_d\otimes T_i\in\operatorname{Mat}_{d^2}(\mathbb Q). $$

Every product of d W_i vanishes, so oracle evaluation is a finite well-defined sum. Its first column, indexed by the coefficient tensor basis λ^a z^b, equals the double jet of y at λ=z=0. Some z^r coefficient with r<d is nonzero, and that coefficient has λ-degree≤r<d; it survives both truncations. Therefore

$$ f\ne0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(W_1,\ldots,W_n)\ne0,
 \qquad f\ne0\Longrightarrow f(W)e_0\ne0. $$

The tuple dimension is d²=O(n²m⁴), independent of the coefficient values and the series horizon. Its entries are0 or a rational source entry, hence have polynomial bit length; constructing the dense tuple in n d⁴ entries is polynomial. Over an arbitrary coefficient field, evaluation remains an exact matrix oracle. Rational bit costs for the response require an explicit coefficient-height promise or a supplied rational realization; they are not acquired from the zero/nonzero guarantee.

This is a concrete use of the new primitive: it removes the cyclic analytic collision that blocked reuse of its scalar differential-system proof. The crucial added ingredient is word-length marking, which turns finite-word independence into faithfulness for the infinite recognizable series before applying multiplicity. It avoids the R²-state horizon unrolling and gives a universal query directly at the proper series interface. It does not remove the exponential full-shuffle rank obstruction or the passive-sample barrier.

For the3-state counterexample above, m=3,n=2 gives d=6 and36×36 matrices. The marked first-column entry at(λ-degree,z-degree)=(1,2) is−1/4, so the previously invisible cyclic series is detected. The exact replay checks this convention independently.

## Audited exact acquisition from output-only matrix queries

The peer `frontier_check_hitting` derives the following consequence in its `CHARZERO_AUDIT_AND_MATRIX_ORACLE_ACQUISITION.md`, §5. I independently read that proof and the entire learner code. Let r≤m be the true left-residual/Hankel rank, and D=d² in the cyclic case (D=d for a promised acyclic representation). An output-only oracle for exact values on rational jointly nilpotent tuples permits an equivalent minimal r-state weighted representation in at most 1+nr queries of dimension≤D+r. The learner receives only n,m and the callable oracle.

For f_w(s)=f(ws), take the feature φ(f_w)=e₀ᵀ f_w(W₁ᵀ,…,W_nᵀ). Transposition is essential: φ(g)=(rev(g)(W)e₀)ᵀ. The reversed residual has initial row vᵀ, transitions A_iᵀ and final column (uA_w)ᵀ, all in the same m-dimensional common representation. Every linear combination of residuals still has this form with a different final column. The universal first-column detector therefore makes φ injective on the entire residual span. In the acyclic case, anti-diagonal simultaneous conjugation restores strict upper triangularity of the reversed representation.

To query a prefix w of length k, place k scalar chain states before the suffix block C_i=W_iᵀ. For each required letter, include its unit edge to the next prefix state; the final prefix edge enters suffix coordinate e₀. The source row of the output's suffix block is exactly e₀ᵀ f_w(C). A path reaching that block must traverse the complete specified prefix first, then any suffix word. The query dimension is D+k and the entire tuple is strictly upper triangular. For the empty prefix, use f(C)'s first row.

Breadth-first exploration retains independent features, then queries the n children of every retained prefix. A feature dependency is an actual residual dependency by injectivity; applying each left derivative preserves it. Thus children of a dependent prefix need not be queried. Closure of the retained span under these derivatives and inclusion of f proves it contains every residual. At most r prefixes are retained. Each retained prefix has retained ancestors, so its length≤r−1; children have length≤r. This proves the query and dimension bounds. Set A_i[p,q] to the coordinates of f_(p i) in the retained residual basis, α to those of f, and β_q to each basis residual's constant term, which is the feature's first coordinate. Induction gives f(w)=αA_wβ for every word. Independence and spanning prove minimality, including the zero-state zero series.

Costs remain explicit. The returned matrix has (D+k)² field entries, even though only one row is retained. Query construction and Gaussian coordinate processing are polynomial; repeated solves can be charged O(nr³D) scalar operations plus full response/query work. Rational processing is polynomial in n,m and the total encoded response length. A bounded hidden realization height H supplies a sufficient polynomial response-height bound: common denominators have polynomial bit length, query nilpotence cuts off after at most d+k letters, and rational determinant bounds control coordinate elimination. Without bounded/encoded responses there is no uniform rational bit bound. Oracle execution and latency are separate charged costs. The acquired realization need not be nonnegative, stochastic or stable for filtering.

Independent code evidence: `audit_output_only_acquisition.py` imports only the peer learner and gives it a separate triangular block-resolvent evaluator. It independently checks exact all-word equality by reachable-space closure and minimality by the finite prefix/suffix Hankel matrix through length m−1. Eight fresh adversarial fixtures (45 queries) pass: delayed nonzero initial coefficients, unreachable modes, reachable unobservable modes, a nonzero realization of the zero series, generic cycles, a positive stochastic source, an acyclic hidden null state, and an oriented degree-three polynomial. The positive stochastic source's learned minimal model has three negative transition entries, concretely illustrating the realization limitation. These certificates prove equality for each fixture; the finite collection does not establish the universal acquisition theorem.

## Why exact teacher acquisition still misses the approximate compact target

There is an additional exact obstruction even when the teacher’s matrix queries are implementable. Let K=2^L. A horizon2L target P consists of2L iid fair bits. Split the word at L: its joint prefix/suffix matrix is P_{ij}=1/K² and has rank1. Let R choose a uniformly random L-bit prefix and repeat it as its suffix; its matrix is R_{ij}=δ_{ij}/K and has rankK. Both have explicit finite HMM constructions (R stores the prefix and replay position). For0<ε<1 put

$$ Q=(1-\varepsilon)P+\varepsilon R,\qquad
 Q_{ij}=\frac{1-\varepsilon}{K^2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{K}\delta_{ij}. $$

Since Q≥(1−ε)P pointwise, KL(P||Q)≤−log(1−ε), uniformly in K. Yet Q’s cut matrix has eigenvalue1/K on the uniform vector and ε/K on each of its K−1 orthogonal directions. Hence rankQ=K, and every exact weighted-state realization of this cut needs at least K states. Its determinant is ε^(K−1)/K^K>0.

This gives a rank1 statistical target arbitrarily close in global KL to a teacher with arbitrarily large exact predictive rank. The new marked fingerprint and exact residual learner correctly retain those small but nonzero teacher directions. They therefore cannot, by themselves, provide the promised compact **approximation** based on the target’s rank. This is a standard contamination/rank phenomenon, not a new information-theoretic impossibility: discarding R already gives the useful rank1 approximation P. What is missing is a stable, task-loss compatible approximate residual quotient with affordable acquisition.

In particular, “matrix queries can be computed from the supplied teacher” does not finish the original target. It changes the access issue but leaves the approximation geometry, tolerance, conditioning and state closure obligations intact.

## Robustness boundaries

There is no coefficient-independent positive gap. Already at degree1, two laws P_t(1)=1/2+t and P_t(2)=1/2−t have a fingerprint difference proportional to t. To decide exactly whether t=0 or t=2^−B from iid words with success≥2/3 needs at least1/(18t²) observations: χ²(P_t,P₀)=4t², KL≤4t², tensorization and Pinsker give TV(P_t^N,P₀^N)≤sqrt(2Nt²), while such a test needs TV≥1/3. Thus exact identity-query simulation has exponential worst-case sample cost in the B-bit parameter description. This does not obstruct approximate prediction that can ignore a discrepancy below its tolerance.

The native integration tuple also has small absolute dynamic range at high degrees. For T₁, absolute values of entries of T₁^k are bounded by

$$ \max_{r,q}|(T_1^k)_{r,q}|\le\frac{H_{d-1}^{k-1}}{(k-1)!}. $$

Indeed each increasing-index path contributes1/(r r₁⋯r_{k−1}); ignoring the fixed1/r and summing distinct intermediate indices bounds the sum by the corresponding elementary symmetric function, then by H_{d−1}^{k−1}/(k−1)!. For formula x₁^k, source dimension is O(k²), making this upper bound exp(−k logk+k loglogk+O(k)). This is a limitation for a fixed **absolute-error** matrix sensor, not a sample lower bound: a sample estimator’s variance can shrink with its signal. Rescaling or clearing denominators can restore absolute signal but its oracle dynamic range and physical cost must then be charged.
