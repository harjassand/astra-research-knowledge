## Theorem and precise scope

Let the physical frame on six qubits be exactly {Xᵢ,Zᵢ: i=1,…,6}, and define the whole weighted unitary-orbit score by

    −i[A,ω]=(1−λ)ωS_A+λS_Aω.

For every fixed λ∈(0,1), there exist unequal faithful density matrices σ,ρ with identical entire scores for all twelve generators in this **unchanged physical frame**. The constructed states depend on h=λ(1−λ) and a sufficiently small positive parameter s; this is not one state pair colliding for all weights. The pair for a chosen λ also works for 1−λ. Scores may be non-Hermitian. No practical temperature, conditioning, copy-cost or efficiently acquired-score claim is made.

For λ=1/2 this is an additional fixed-X/Z SLD construction. For asymmetric λ it defeats the proposed asymmetric-weight repair for this fixed frame. It is separate from r03's λ-dependent dense-frame theorem and from the cycle2 Y repair of a different Bell ordering/exponential-chart witness. No full XYZ conclusion follows here.

## Weighted fiber algebra, independently derived

For σ=U diag(p)U† faithful, D=diag√p and B=U†AU, the score entries are

    Sᵢⱼ=i(pᵢ−pⱼ)Bᵢⱼ/[(1−λ)pᵢ+λpⱼ].

Put K=D⁻¹(B−iλS)D=D(B+i(1−λ)S)D⁻¹, so

    Kᵢⱼ=√(pᵢpⱼ) Bᵢⱼ/[(1−λ)pᵢ+λpⱼ].

The common-score equation for ρ=UDQD U† is equivalent to [Q,K]=0: the target equation is (B−iλS)D²=D²(B+i(1−λ)S); conjugating its candidate counterpart gives KQ=QK. Q is Hermitian, so the adjoint commutation condition follows as well. Positive denominators give uniqueness of each entire score. We use real U and Q=I+P_w−I/64, w=1/8·1. A real K commutes with P_w precisely when both K1 and Kᵀ1 are the same scalar multiple of 1.

## Bell ordering kills both singular-limit constraints

Use the same exact rational Cayley half rotations V,W specified in `sources/r11_logic/cycle3/ift_integer_inputs.json`; these rotations build U₀ only. The subsequent IFT chart is **exponential**, U(y)=U₀exp Ω(y), not the main lead's cycle2 Cayley continuation chart.

Real three-qubit Pauli products in I,X,Z,J, J=iY=[[0,1],[-1,0]], have labels 0,…,63 in lexicographic base-four order. XOR of labels is multiplication up to a real sign. Select the six GF(2)-independent labels

    48=JII, 12=IJI, 3=IIJ, 52=JXI, 49=JIX, 28=XJI.

Their independence follows because adding the first three to the last three yields the single-site X labels on all three sites, and J+X yields Z. Each generator is skew-symmetric. Define the 64-label ordering by XOR of these generators according to the bits of the binary Gray code k XOR (k>>1), k=0,…,63. Consecutive labels differ by one generator; therefore every P_k P_{k+1}ᵀ is skew-symmetric.

Let U_Bell have columns vec(P_k)/√8 in this order, and U₀=(V⊗W)U_Bell. Its columns are maximally entangled, so every physical Xᵢ,Zᵢ has zero diagonal in Bₐ⁰=U₀ᵀAₐU₀. Moreover every consecutive offdiagonal entry is zero. For a generator on the first half it is a trace pairing of VᵀAₐV, symmetric, with P_{k+1}P_kᵀ, skew; for the second half the analogous pairing uses WᵀAₐW and P_kᵀP_{k+1}, again skew. Thus

    diag Bₐ⁰=0 and (Bₐ⁰)_{k,k+1}=0 for all twelve a and all k.

These are exact real identities, not modular-only observations. The matrices Bₐ⁰ are rational because the two Bell normalizations combine to 1/8.

## Analytic desingularization of row and column constraints

Fix h=λ(1−λ)>0. Use populations pᵢ=s²ⁱ/Σⱼs²ʲ for s>0. For i<j, Δ=j−i, the weighted filter entries are

    Kᵢⱼ=s^Δ Bᵢⱼ/[(1−λ)+λs^(2Δ)],
    Kⱼᵢ=s^Δ Bᵢⱼ/[λ+(1−λ)s^(2Δ)],

where B(y)=exp(−Ω(y))B⁰exp Ω(y) is real symmetric. Define the common positive denominator polynomial

    d_Δ(h,s)=h+(1−2h)s^(2Δ)+h s^(4Δ).

The symmetric part has diagonal Bᵢᵢ and offdiagonal multiplier

    a_Δ(h,s)=s^Δ(1+s^(2Δ))/(2d_Δ(h,s)).

For λ≠1/2, the antisymmetric difference K−Kᵀ divided by ((2λ−1)/h)s has, above the diagonal, multiplier

    e_Δ(h,s)=h s^(Δ−1)(1−s^(2Δ))/d_Δ(h,s),

and its negative below the diagonal. Crucially this expression is analytic at s=0 for fixed h>0; e₁(h,0)=1 and e_Δ(h,0)=0 for Δ>1. The same analytic auxiliary constraint can be imposed at λ=1/2, even though K−Kᵀ is then already zero.

For each generator define F₁ as the first63 coordinates of (I−11ᵀ/64) times the symmetric-part row-sum vector. Define F₂ as the first63 coordinates of the normalized antisymmetric matrix times1. Both are real analytic in (y,s) near zero. F₁(0,0)=0 by hollow diagonals; F₂(0,0)=0 by the vanishing adjacent entries proved above. Concatenating twelve F₁ blocks and twelve F₂ blocks gives1512 equations.

At s=0, D_yF₁ is the derivative of diag B(y). Its mean is zero because Tr B(y)=0. D_yF₂ is the path divergence of the derivative of the63 adjacent entries. In the first63 coordinates that divergence map sends edge vector t to (t₀,t₁−t₀,…,t₆₂−t₆₁), a lower triangular matrix with determinant1. Thus full rank of the **diagonal-plus-adjacent** derivative is exactly full rank of this analytic constraint derivative. This Jacobian is independent of h.

## IFT and the physical collision

Restrict Ω(y) to the selected1512 coordinates. For each fixed h>0 the real analytic1512-equation map has a nonsingular derivative at (y,s)=(0,0). The real analytic implicit-function theorem gives y_h(s) for all sufficiently small |s|, with F₁=F₂=0. Choose any sufficiently small positive s; then every pᵢ>0, so σ=U(y_h(s))diag(p)U(y_h(s))ᵀ is faithful.

F₁=0 forces symmetric-part row sums constant. The symmetric part is symmetric, so column sums agree. F₂=0 forces the entire normalized antisymmetric row-sum vector to vanish: its full coordinate sum is zero, so its final coordinate follows from the first63. For asymmetric λ this gives (K−Kᵀ)1=0; at λ=1/2 this is automatic. Hence K1=Kᵀ1=c1, and P_w commutes with every weighted K. The weighted fiber algebra proves equality of the entire physical X/Z score tuple for

    ρ=U D(I+P_w−I/64)D Uᵀ ≠ σ.

Q has eigenvalues127/64 and63/64, so ρ is faithful. Since diag P_w=1/64, Tr(D²Q)=1 exactly. D is invertible and Q≠I, proving distinction. The physical X/Z frame never changes; its ordinary commutant remains scalar because tensor Pauli products generate the entire matrix algebra.

This is an interior collision, not one at the singular s=0 limit. The radius depends on h and may deteriorate near λ=0 or1; no endpoint or uniform endpoint-radius claim is made.
