# Failure boundary and restart gates

## What would falsify this conditional finding

Any of the following would remove the claimed public-specification mismatch:

1. Nature's fit code or explicit equations show that its fitted `β` is the recombination fraction, with the ODE branch terms swapped accordingly.
2. The model's actual camera observable contains a documented RF-dependent contribution large enough to reverse both sign and the observed ≈4.8% scale; a fixed constant background or weak radical brightness is insufficient under the source states.
3. Nature's exact rates, prehistory, and raw time-series, when replayed with an explicitly supplied observation map, show a positive RF-on trace under the source separation-fraction convention. That would imply the present source-adjacent replay omitted a load-bearing term.

## Current stop line

The public record suffices for the conditional branch-sign conclusion and the corrected-slope counterexample. It does not provide enough information to classify the discrepancy as (a) a prose sign error, (b) a private complement convention, (c) an omitted β-dependent process, or (d) a true implementation error. Do not promote the conditional model-interface finding to “the Nature simulation is wrong” without one of the missing implementation artifacts.

## Useful next evidence

- Exact Nature simulation source or a full equation listing, including the definition of the fitted state variable and fluorescence output map.
- The precise mapping from the 520-nm illumination schedule to `Iσ`, and the simulated state at RF onset after blue and green pre-exposure.
- The raw time-resolved series used in Supplementary Data Fig. 6, with the baseline/normalization definition and matching parameter file.
- Product-resolved measurement of radical-anion yield during the same resonant RF pulse sequence, together with the red-emission trace, to determine whether the experimental response is an increase in recombination yield or another optical channel.

## Prior boundary

The 2025 JACS paper already specifies the redox photocycle and β-controlled SCRP branching; the 2026 Nature paper already reports positive RYDMR from mScarlet–flavin samples and in vivo. This audit only checks consistency at their shared model interface. No novel capability, mechanism, or experimental priority is claimed.
