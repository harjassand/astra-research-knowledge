## 1. Interface and the missing quantity

For a finite actual one-logical-qubit CSS replacement code, use

    H0 = sum_a (I-C_a)/2,
    H = H0+V,       V = -sum_i epsilon_i X_i,
    epsilon_i>0,    v=||V||=sum_i epsilon_i <= 1/64,
    rho = exp(-beta H)/Tr exp(-beta H).

The equality in the definition of v uses the common +1 eigenvector of all
physical X_i. Fix the bare pure-X logical L. H preserves L and each
original X check. The ORIGINAL decoder Z or Y observable O obeys

    O*=O, O^2=I, [O,C_a]=0, {O,L}=0.

Keep the unchanged single physical bath A=X_q, its FULL-H frequency
components [H,A_omega]=-omega A_omega, and the original fixed Gaussian KMS
rate

    gamma(omega)=exp(-omega^2)/(1+exp(-beta omega)).

Thus gamma(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma(omega), gamma<=Gamma with
Gamma=1 (Gamma=2 is also a safe historical cap). No reservoir model or
microscopic approximation is changed or acquired here. Define

    k_F = sum_omega gamma(omega) Tr rho [F,A_omega]*[F,A_omega].   (1)

The reduction constructs a Hermitian involution U, preserving original
X-check labels and anticommuting with L, which maps corresponding CURRENT
integer-energy shells exactly. Put

    b=4v/(1-2v),   c=1-sqrt(1-b^2),   d_v=sqrt(b^2+c^2).

Then

    ||O-U||_(rho,2) <= d_v,
    sqrt(k_O) >= sqrt(k_U)-2 sqrt(Gamma) d_v.                   (2)

At small v the cost is O(v), independent of code size, shell rank,
syndrome degeneracy and Bohr-frequency count. U is a comparison frame;
the encoder and recovery still use the ORIGINAL O. U need not commute H.

**Load-bearing remaining question:** acquire a nonvanishing lower bound
for k_U from the actual degenerate shell matrices of the unit-check,
positive-X Hamiltonian. Simple spectra alone, or splitting a chosen
counterpart scalar energy, does not supply this matrix bound.

## 2. Riesz projectors with no rank factor

Work in one joint original-X-check sector g and one L sector eta. Old
energies E are integers (the X-check contribution is a fixed integer in
this sector). Let P^0_(g,E,eta) be the old E eigenspace. The original O
maps the eta=+ and eta=- old spaces unitarily, so their ranks agree.

The current spectral projector P_(g,E,eta) is the contour integral on
|z-E|=1/2. Weyl's norm bound puts its eigenvalues in [E-v,E+v] and
preserves its rank. On this circle,

    ||(z-H0)^-1|| <= 2,
    ||(z-H)^-1|| <= 1/(1/2-v).

The resolvent identity and contour length pi give

    ||P_(g,E,eta)-P^0_(g,E,eta)|| <= 2v/(1-2v)=a.              (3)

This proof uses the whole old shell, including all its degeneracies.
It does not choose individual current eigenvectors in it.

On the plus sector abbreviate

    P=P_(g,E,+),       Q=O P_(g,E,-) O.

Since the old corresponding projectors are conjugate by O, (3) gives
||P-Q||<=2a=b<1. Set

    T_(g,E)=P_(g,E,-) O P_(g,E,+).

For x in Ran P,

    ||(I-Q)x||<=b||x||,
    T* T=PQP >= (1-b^2)P.

T therefore has a unitary polar factor between the equal-rank current
spaces:

    U_(g,E)=T_(g,E)(T_(g,E)*T_(g,E))^-1/2.

Its singular values before normalization lie in [sqrt(1-b^2),1], hence

    ||U_(g,E)-T_(g,E)|| <= c.

For every unit x in the plus current shell, the two parts of

    Ox-U_(g,E)x
      = (I-P_(g,E,-))Ox + (T_(g,E)-U_(g,E))x

are orthogonal. Their squared norm is at most b^2+c^2. The inverse polar
factor gives the same estimate on the minus shell. Define

    U=sum_(g,E) (U_(g,E)+U_(g,E)*),

as the direct sum of these off-diagonal blocks. It is a Hermitian
involution, anticommutes L, and preserves g. Evaluate the weighted norm
in a CURRENT Gibbs eigenbasis; each vector belongs to one current shell.
The squared bounds average with probabilities summing to one, proving
the first statement of (2). No global operator-norm summation over E is
claimed. A naive sum of projector errors would lose a rank/level factor;
the Gibbs-basis argument avoids that loss.

## 3. KMS transfers the weighted error without modular inflation

For Hermitian A, A_omega*=A_-omega. Put

    R=sum_omega gamma(omega) A_omega* A_omega.

Frequency pinching implies

    sum_omega A_omega* A_omega = pinch_H(A^2)=I,
    0<=R<=Gamma I,       [R,rho]=0.

The lowering convention gives A_omega rho=exp(-beta omega)rho A_omega.
KMS reindexing thus proves the exact identity

    sum_omega gamma(omega) A_omega rho A_omega* = rho R.        (4)

Take D=O-U, which is Hermitian. The squared triangle bound gives

    k_D <= 2 sum_omega gamma Tr rho A_omega* D^2 A_omega
           +2 sum_omega gamma Tr rho D A_omega* A_omega D
         =2 Tr D^2 rho R +2 Tr rho D R D
         <=4 Gamma Tr rho D^2.                                (5)

The first term uses (4), the second uses R<=Gamma I. There is no
uncontrolled right multiplication of a weighted norm, no transport of O
through rho, and no assumption [O,H]=0. Equation (5) and the Hilbert-space
triangle inequality for the direct sum of frequency commutators prove
the second statement of (2).

For several independent Hermitian bath channels the same argument gives
4 sum_alpha Gamma_alpha ||A_alpha||^2 as the comparison coefficient.
That extensive intensity must be charged; it is not the one-channel
constant in (2).

## 4. The exact degenerate-shell acquisition target

Restrict to g, and identify minus shells with plus shells using U. In the
plus reference space define

    H_plus = H|_(g,+),
    Hhat_minus = U* H|_(g,-) U,
    A_plus = A|_(g,+),
    Ahat_minus = U* A|_(g,-) U.

For each exact FULL-H frequency omega, compute the spectral components
of A_plus with H_plus and Ahat_minus with Hhat_minus separately. On a plus
input the commutator [U,A_omega], after applying U* to its output, is
exactly

    A_plus,omega - Ahat_minus,omega.                           (6)

The analogous minus-input term completes k_U. Formula (6) is the actual
matrix-valued matching problem. Corresponding integer shell dimensions
agree, but individual branches inside them need not have the same
eigenvectors. Distinct eigenvalues inside each sector do not imply a
large difference of the two frequency-resolved operators. Shared atoms
from different branches can contribute with nontrivial matrix overlaps.

In particular, a sufficient next lemma would supply a positive-weight
set of CURRENT inputs on which the operators in (6) have squared norm
at least a fixed constant in a band where the same fixed gamma has a
positive lower bound. An equivalent directly computed defect is the
weighted norm of their exact spectral pinching difference. This lemma
must retain the within-shell eigenspaces and actual bath matrix elements;
a scalar trace or eigenvalue discriminant is insufficient.

If it supplies k_U>=K>0, choose v small enough that
2 sqrt(Gamma)d_v<=sqrt(K)/2. Then k_O>=K/4. The already gated cycle17
GNS chord/channel argument applies to the ORIGINAL O, original unit-H
encoder/thermal auxiliary, and unchanged recovery after charging the
static unit-H/current-Gibbs auxiliary change <=2 beta v. No encoder
using U is silently substituted. At present K is **UNACQUIRED** for the

## 5. Actual local falsifier of individual old-label acquisition

An RX deep interior CY_x edge q touches exactly two distinct CY_x face
Z checks C_f,C_g and no CQ restriction. A valid syndrome with their
eigenvalues opposite exists: an adjacent interior edge flips C_f and a
third face while leaving C_g unchanged. On its q-paired old states,

    H0|_(pair)=E I,
    X_q|_(pair)=[[0,1],[1,0]].

For the strictly positive field direction

    V(t)=-t X_q -t^2 sum_(i!=q) X_i,       t>0,

the leading degenerate block is -t P_E X_q P_E. On the opposite-sign
two-face subspace this has eigenvalues +/-t. An old syndrome vector has
weight exactly 1/2 in each band. For fixed finite N, the remaining field
norm and the second-order inter-shell correction are O(N t^2), so the
current eigenvectors in either band have overlap at most
1/sqrt(2)+O(Nt) with that old vector. Current zero-band eigenvectors
have overlap O(Nt). Thus individual-label eigenvector distance cannot
be O(||V||), however tiny every positive field is made:

    liminf_(t->0) min_phase ||psi(t)-phase phi_s||
        >= sqrt(2-sqrt(2)).                                   (7)

This is an actual-family obstruction to recycling cycle17's simple-label
proof, not an obstruction to memory or to the shell reduction. The
decoder can act identically on the two corrected error configurations;
large syndrome rotations do not by themselves imply logical leakage.
