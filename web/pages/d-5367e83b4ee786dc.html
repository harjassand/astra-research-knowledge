Abstract
Sandwiched (quantum) α\alpha-Rényi divergence has been recently defined in the independent works of Wilde et al. (arXiv:1306.1586) and Müller-Lennert et al (arXiv:1306.3142v1). This new quantum divergence has already found applications in quantum information theory. Here we further investigate properties of this new quantum divergence. In particular we show that sandwiched α\alpha-Rényi divergence satisfies the data processing inequality for all values of α>1\alpha>1. Moreover we prove that α\alpha-Holevo information, a variant of Holevo information defined in terms of sandwiched α\alpha-Rényi divergence, is super-additive. Our results are based on Hölder’s inequality, the Riesz-Thorin theorem and ideas from the theory of complex interpolation. We also employ Sion’s minimax theorem.
I A non-commutative Rényi divergence
Several entropic quantities that have been shown to be useful in information theory belong to the family of α\alpha-Rényi entropies. For a random variable XX with distribution {pi}\{p_{i}\}, the α\alpha-Rényi entropy of XX, for α>0\alpha>0 and α≠1\alpha\neq 1, is defined by
Hα​(X)=11−α​log⁡(∑ipiα).H_{\alpha}(X)=\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\log\left(\sum_{i}p_{i}^{\alpha}\right).
In the quantum setting where states are represented by density matrices (positive semi-definite matrices with normalized trace)
α\alpha-Rényi entropy can be defined by
Hα​(ρ)=11−α​log⁡(tr​ρα),H_{\alpha}(\rho)=\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\log\left(\text{tr}\rho^{\alpha}\right),
which reduces to the classical Rényi entropy when ρ\rho is a diagonal matrix. The limiting cases of Rényi entropy when α→1\alpha\rightarrow 1 and α→∞\alpha\rightarrow\infty are known to be equal to the Shannon entropy and min-entropy respectively.
Likewise a family of Rényi divergences can be defined. For two random variables X,YX,Y with distributions {pi}\{p_{i}\} and {qi}\{q_{i}\} respectively, their α\alpha-Rényi divergence for α>0\alpha>0 and α≠1\alpha\neq 1 is defined as follows. If there exists ii such that qi=0q_{i}=0 but pi≠0p_{i}\neq 0 then Dα(X||Y)=∞D_{\alpha}(X||Y)=\infty. Otherwise,
Dα(X||Y)=1α−1log(∑ipiαqi1−α),\displaystyle D_{\alpha}(X||Y)=\frac{1}{\alpha-1}\log\left(\sum_{i}p_{i}^{\alpha}q_{i}^{1-\alpha}\right),
(1)
which is equal to
1α−1​log⁡(tr​(ρα​σ1−α)),\displaystyle\frac{1}{\alpha-1}\log\left(\text{tr}(\rho^{\alpha}\sigma^{1-\alpha})\right),
(2)
if we let ρ\rho and σ\sigma be the diagonal matrices with diagonal entries {pi}\{p_{i}\} and {qi}\{q_{i}\} respectively.
Rényi divergence had been generalized to the quantum setting based on the above equation, and appears in the quantum Chernoff bound [1] for the range of 0<α<10<\alpha<1. See also [2] for other applications of this Rényi divergence.
To capture the non-commutative essence of the quantum theory, another generalization of Rényi divergence to the quantum setting was recently proposed in [3] and [4] (and before that in the talks by Tomamichel [5] and Fehr [6]):
Dα(ρ||σ)={1α−1​log⁡(tr​(σ1−α2​α​ρ​σ1−α2​α)α)supp​(ρ)⊆supp​(σ)∞otherwise,\displaystyle D_{\alpha}(\rho||\sigma)=\begin{cases}\frac{1}{\alpha-1}\log\left(\text{tr}(\sigma^{\frac{1-\alpha}{2\alpha}}\rho\sigma^{\frac{1-\alpha}{2\alpha}})^{\alpha}\right)&\quad\quad\text{supp}(\rho)\subseteq\text{supp}(\sigma)\\
\infty&\quad\quad\quad\quad\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
(3)
where ρ,σ\rho,\sigma are density matrices and supp​(ρ)\text{supp}(\rho) is the support of ρ\rho, i.e., the span of eigenvectors of ρ\rho corresponding to non-zero eigenvalues. This divergence reduces to (2) when ρ\rho and σ\sigma commute.
This new divergence is called sandwiched Rényi relative entropy in [3] and quantum Rényi divergence in [4]. To avoid confusions with the previous Rényi divergence we prefer to employ the first name in this paper and call it sandwiched Rényi relative entropy or sandwiched Rényi divergence.
Sandwiched Rényi divergence has already been shown to be useful in quantum information theory. Based on the framework of Sharma and Warsi [7] (which itself is based on [8]), sandwiched Rényi divergence is used in [3] to prove a strong converse for the classical capacity of entanglement-breaking channels.
To be a useful divergence, one would expect that sandwiched Rényi divergence satisfies some properties including the data processing inequality. Some of these properties have been studied in [3, 4] and are proved to hold especially when α=1/2\alpha=1/2, 1<α≤21<\alpha\leq 2 and α=∞\alpha=\infty. These properties include
(a)
Positivity and equality condition: Dα(ρ||σ)≥0D_{\alpha}(\rho||\sigma)\geq 0 and equality holds if and only if ρ=σ\rho=\sigma.
(b)
Data processing inequality: for a quantum channel Φ\Phi, Dα(ρ||σ)≥Dα(Φ(ρ)||Φ(σ))D_{\alpha}(\rho||\sigma)\geq D_{\alpha}(\Phi(\rho)||\Phi(\sigma)).
The data processing inequality is proved in [3, 4] for the range of 1<α≤21<\alpha\leq 2 using Lieb’s concavity theorem. Moreover the equality condition is shown to hold for the same range of α\alpha. These two properties for other values of α\alpha are conjectured to hold in [4].
In this paper we prove the equality and positivity condition for all positive α≠1\alpha\neq 1 (see Theorem 5) and the data processing inequality for all values of α>1\alpha>1 (see Theorem 6).
There are two more conjectures in [4] about sandwiched Rényi divergence, the first of which is monotonicity in α\alpha:
(c)
Monotonicity in α\alpha: α↦Dα(ρ||σ)\alpha\mapsto D_{\alpha}(\rho||\sigma) is increasing.
This conjecture is proved in [4] in the special case where ρ\rho is rank-one.
Here we prove monotonicity in the general case for α>1\alpha>1 (see Theorem 7).
From the definitions it is clear that α\alpha-Rényi entropy can be expressed in terms of sandwiched Rényi divergence
Hα(ρ)=−Dα(ρ||I),H_{\alpha}(\rho)=-D_{\alpha}(\rho||I),
where II denotes the identity operator. Moreover, the conditional (von Neumann) entropy can be defined in terms of KL-divergence as follows. For a bipartite state ρA​B\rho_{AB} we have
H(A|B)ρ=−infσBD(ρA​B||I⊗σB).H(A|B)_{\rho}=-\inf_{\sigma_{B}}D(\rho_{AB}||I\otimes\sigma_{B}).
A similar equality holds for conditional min-entropy in terms of quantum relative max-entropy. Based on these observations quantum conditional Rényi entropy is defined in [4] by
Hα(A|B)ρ:=−infσBDα(ρA​B||I⊗σB).\displaystyle H_{\alpha}(A|B)_{\rho}:=-\inf_{\sigma_{B}}D_{\alpha}(\rho_{AB}||I\otimes\sigma_{B}).
(4)
Then the following is conjectured.
(d)
Duality: For all 1/2≤α,β≤∞1/2\leq\alpha,\beta\leq\infty, α,β≠1\alpha,\beta\neq 1, such that 1α+1β=2\frac{1}{\alpha}+\frac{1}{\beta}=2, and all tripartite pure states ρA​B​C\rho_{ABC} we have Hα​(A|B)=−Hβ​(A|C)H_{\alpha}(A|B)=-H_{\beta}(A|C).
The special case of this conjecture when ρA​B\rho_{AB} is pure, is proved in [4]. Here we give a proof for the general case (see Theorem 9).
We also answer an open question raised in [3] about the super-additivity of a quantity called α\alpha-Holevo information (see Theorem 12).
To prove these results we mostly employ properties of Schatten norms. In particular we use Hölder’s inequality and its generalizations for proving (a). For (b) and (c), we use the Riesz-Thorin theorem and ideas from the theory of complex interpolation. Finally (d) is proved based on Hölder’s duality and Sion’s minimax theorem.
In the following two sections we review Hölder’s inequalities and prove the Riesz-Thorin theorem. The main results are stated and proved in Sections IV and V.
Note: After completion of this work we discovered that Frank and Lieb [9] have also proved the data processing inequality (property (b)). Their proof (that is different from ours) works for all values of α≥1/2\alpha\geq 1/2 unlike ours which works only for α>1\alpha>1. Moreover, monotonicity (property (c)) and duality (property (d)) have been proved in the updated paper of Müller-Lennert et al [10]. Their proof of duality is similar to ours, but they have a different proof for monotonicity.
