# Historical finite-species continuation audit

Checked 2026-10-07. Scope: the named historical sources only. No new-release proof was inspected.

## Outcome

For a **fixed finite number of species with nonnegative densities, fixed positive masses, and fixed real charges**, continuation methods based on the Glassey–Strauss representations, positive energy/null flux, and positive momentum moments extend by specieswise estimates and finite positive sums. Charge signs do not destroy energy positivity. This is an adaptation checked below, not a literal application of a scalar theorem to the signed charge density.

Multi-species continuation was explicitly part of Glassey's authored 1996 presentation, and Luk–Strain explicitly recognized a multi-species extension in 2014. Arbitrary masses/signed charges are not a separately stated theorem in the three checked arXiv papers. Original 1986 full wording remains **UNVERIFIED**: its publisher page is subscription-only and the attempted PDF URL redirects to that preview. The 1996 authored reproduction is directly verified and materially stronger evidence than a secondary citation.

## Checked mathematical adaptation (independent reconstruction)

Set c=1 and let a=1,...,S, S<infinity. Use physical momentum p, epsilon_a(p)=sqrt(m_a^2+|p|^2), v_a=p/epsilon_a, m_a>0, and q_a in R. The equations are

    (partial_t+v_a.partial_x+q_a(E+v_a cross B).partial_p) f_a=0,
    rho=4pi sum_a q_a integral f_a dp,
    j=4pi sum_a q_a integral v_a f_a dp.

The Gauss constraints use these signed sources; the f_a themselves remain nonnegative. Introduce u=p/m_a, g_a(t,x,u)=m_a^3 f_a(t,x,m_a u), u0=sqrt(1+|u|^2), lambda_a=q_a/m_a. All velocities become u/u0 and each transport force gets the fixed coefficient lambda_a. Its flow preserves phase volume and the individual Lp norms.

**Positive energy:** direct integration by parts gives

    H = (1/2) integral_x (|E|^2+|B|^2)
        +4pi sum_a m_a integral_(x,u) u0 g_a,
    dH/dt=0.

The particle work is E.j, because the magnetic force is orthogonal to velocity. It cancels the Maxwell work -E.j regardless of signs of q_a. The local matter energy/flux is (4pi sum_a integral epsilon_a f_a, 4pi sum_a integral p f_a). The null-cone matter term is

    4pi sum_a m_a integral_u u0(1+u_hat.omega) g_a >=0.

Thus all energy and flux estimates survive, with constants depending on the fixed species parameters.

**Absolute field sources:** after the above normalization, retarded T contributions have coefficient q_a; retarded S contributions have coefficient q_a*lambda_a=q_a^2/m_a. Choose

    c_a=1+|q_a|+q_a^2/m_a,   G=sum_a c_a g_a >=0.

Triangle inequalities bound both kinds of field contribution by the same positive kernel functionals of G used in the scalar estimates. Also ||G||infinity <= sum_a c_a||g_a0||infinity, and its first moment is controlled by H times max_a(c_a/m_a). Spatial interpolation, Holder/Minkowski, wave Strichartz estimates, angular denominators and compact-support geometry therefore keep their exponents. With fixed finite masses, physical epsilon_a moments and normalized u0 moments are equivalent up to fixed constants.

**Moment and continuation steps:** differentiate M_N=sum_a c_a integral u0^N g_a specieswise. Magnetic work vanishes and

    |dM_N/dt| <= N max_a|lambda_a| integral_x |E|
                     sum_a c_a integral_u u0^(N-1) g_a.

The usual scalar interpolation uses the boundedness of positive G and yields the same moment exponent/closure inequality. For characteristic criteria, the supremum must range over all species' characteristic flows; each satisfies |dU_a/dt| <= |lambda_a|(|E|+|B|). The finite-species local weighted-Sobolev argument and differentiated field/iteration estimates use sums over all a. A bound on every species' continuation quantity is consequently sufficient.

**Important non-identity:** G usually does not solve one scalar Vlasov equation because the lambda_a differ. Neither does sum_a q_a g_a. The proof must apply transport/derivative bounds specieswise and sum afterward. Signed charge-density cancellation cannot replace positive species-moment control in these estimates.

## Limits and failure ledger

- No uniform theorem over growing species number, masses tending to zero, unbounded charge-to-mass ratios, or infinitely many species has been established by this check. The displayed constants make the missing uniformity explicit.
- Massless species are outside this normalization and the strict subluminal kernel argument.
- For common-plane criteria, the same plane/envelope must control the union of species supports. Different uncontrolled planes cannot be substituted without a fresh geometric argument.
- All initial regularity, finite energy and tail assumptions in the cited theorem must be imposed specieswise (or in a finite equivalent positive sum). Their omission is not justified by species counting.
- Original 1986 access failed legitimately; no institutional login, paid acquisition or people messages were attempted. The accessible authored reproduction was used with its distinct provenance preserved.
