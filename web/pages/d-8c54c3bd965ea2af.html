# Failed routes and retained limitations

1. **“More EdU+ Paneth cells proves more functional niche repair.” — Rejected.** EdU reports recent DNA synthesis/label incorporation. The aggregate fraction does not say how renewal is allocated across crypts or whether a renewed PC gives sufficient local Wnt support. Exact spatial counterexample: [aggregate_alias.md](counterexamples/aggregate_alias.md).
2. **“Lower Notum in sorted renewed PCs proves dm-αKG improves 5-FU recovery through PC replacement.” — Not identified.** The PC-renewal/Notum and 5-FU outcome cohorts are separate; no selective block of the proposed mediator is reported in that endpoint experiment. The prior Notum study supports the plausibility and importance of the mediator, but not this particular intervention’s mediation. Exact causal alias: [mediation_alias.md](counterexamples/mediation_alias.md).
3. **“Fifteen percent asymmetric mitochondrial segregation gives a numerical Paneth commitment rate.” — Rejected.** The denominator is sampled position-specific divisions, not a reported per-crypt daily event flux jointly measured with Paneth birth/death. (D), residence-time distribution, and output multiplier are missing. Preserve the symbolic mass-balance bound rather than insert a guessed rate.
4. **“Use 57 days as the mean Paneth lifetime.” — Rejected.** Ireland et al. report up to 57 days to reporter inheritance and use that to challenge earlier shorter estimates. It is not, without an explicit survival model, a mean exponential residence time.
5. **“Old organelles promote differentiation across adult stem cells.” — Rejected.** The 2025 peroxisome/G6PD primary study reports old-peroxisome inheritance supporting self-renewal in mammary and epidermal cells. The effects cannot be generalized across organelles or tissues.
6. **Numerical source fitting — Not run.** Source-data spreadsheets are linked but were not downloaded, and figures do not provide the joint per-crypt lineage data needed for the theorem’s thresholds/rates. No biological effect size is inferred from the synthetic script.
7. **Specific threshold model — Uncalibrated.** A Paneth/Notum or Wnt threshold per crypt is a modelling choice. Notum is secreted and crypts may be coupled. No threshold was fit to the paper or treated as measured.
