STATIONARY CONCENTRATION BEYOND N35: EXACT OBSTRUCTION AND TWO CONDITIONAL POSITIVE ENDPOINTS
Date: 2026-10-07
Status: derived mathematical audit; internal reasoning, no external correctness or novelty review.
Scope: autonomous factorial mass-action molecular CTMCs. N35 also permits predictable history-dependent controls; those need not define a stationary Markov process at all. Any stationary theorem must first supply an autonomous Markov interface (or an explicitly included finite-state controller).

MAIN CONCLUSION
An acquired positive, full-dimensional polytope with strict inward facet collars does NOT imply a stationary probability law, stochastic permanence, or stationary concentration. This remains false for actual strongly endotactic reaction networks. The missing bridge is global stochastic recurrence/return after a rare exit. A valid quantitative return certificate turns N35 into a true infinite-time stationary occupation theorem. Complex balance gives a different, stronger, existing algebraic route through the Anderson-Craciun-Kurtz stationary law.

2. ACTUAL STRONGLY ENDOTACTIC COUNTEREXAMPLE WITH AN EXPLICIT N35 POLYTOPE
The primary-paper precedent is Anderson-Cappelletti-Kim-Nguyen, “Tier structure of strongly endotactic reaction networks,” SPA 130 (2020), 7218–7259, Examples 3.1 and 3.2. Example 3.1 is
  R0: empty -> 2A+B,
  R1: 2A+B -> 4A+4B,
  R2: 4A+4B -> A.
The paper establishes strong endotacticity and stochastic transience for every positive choice of constants. Example 3.2 supplies a related explosive network. Verified primary sources:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.05328  (v2, pp. 5–7)
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spa.2020.07.012

The following concrete strict inward certificate is derived here, rather than assumed from deterministic permanence. Choose deterministic constants k=(1,5,4). With concentrations (a,b),
  f_a(a,b) = 2+10a^2 b-12a^4 b^4,
  f_b(a,b) = 1+15a^2 b-16a^4 b^4.
The equilibrium is c=(1,1), and
  Df(c) = [[-28,-38],[-34,-49]].
Let epsilon=1/10000, ell=epsilon/1000=1/10000000, and
  Q = { |a-1| <= 7 epsilon/5, |b-1| <= epsilon }.
This is a rational, positive, full-dimensional rectangle. The stoichiometric space is R^2, since det((2,1),(2,3))=4. Set every facet margin gamma=4epsilon/5=1/12500.

Exact verification of the collars:
On [99/100,101/100]^2, every second partial derivative of either component of f has absolute value less than 310. For example the largest simple conservative bound is
  |partial_ab f_b| <= 30(101/100)+256(101/100)^6 < 310.
The other bounds follow from
  f_a,aa=20b-144a^2b^4, f_a,ab=20a-192a^3b^3,
  f_a,bb=-144a^4b^2,
  f_b,aa=30b-192a^2b^4, f_b,ab=30a-256a^3b^3,
  f_b,bb=-192a^4b^2.
Writing u=a-1,v=b-1, Taylor's formula gives
  f_a=-28u-38v+R_a, f_b=-34u-49v+R_b.
To avoid any ambiguity about collars inside Q versus ambient neighborhoods, extend the tangential coordinates of every collar by ell as well. Throughout the resulting expanded rectangle,
  |u|<=1.401 epsilon, |v|<=1.001 epsilon,
  |R_a|,|R_b| <= (310/2)(|u|+|v|)^2
                 <= 894.28862 epsilon^2 = 0.089428862 epsilon.
In the upper-a collar, u>=7epsilon/5-ell and v>=-epsilon-ell, so
  f_a <= -6epsilon/5+66ell+894.28862epsilon^2
       = -1.044571138 epsilon <= -gamma.
The lower-a collar reverses the inequality. In the upper-b collar,
  f_b <= -7epsilon/5+83ell+894.28862epsilon^2
       = -1.227571138 epsilon <= -gamma.
Again the lower-b collar reverses the inequality. Every facet therefore has the required strict inward collar, even with this stronger ambient-neighborhood convention, with an explicit rational width and margin. The controls in this certificate are the SINGLETON fixed autonomous vector k=(1,5,4); K=5 is only its envelope, not an independent common [1,5] box. For every integer V, (A,B)=(V,V) is an exactly compatible central starting state; N35's stated volume threshold can subsequently be evaluated. The narrow collar gives a small certificate exponent, but that does not affect the logical obstruction.

The classically scaled molecular rates at volume V are
  lambda_0=V,
  lambda_1=(5/V^2) A(A-1)B,
  lambda_2=(4/V^7) A(A-1)(A-2)(A-3) B(B-1)(B-2)(B-3).
They are actual factorial mass-action rates, with positive fixed constants at every fixed V. The primary transience statement therefore applies at every V, including V beyond any finite certificate threshold.

Independent escape-path verification of the obstruction:
From an arbitrary count state (A,B), choose a finite number j of R0 reactions so that B+j is divisible by 4 and A+2j>=B+j. Choosing j still larger makes the final A count below arbitrarily large. Then repeat R2 until B=0. Every reaction in this finite path is enabled and has positive probability. The final state is (n,0), where n=A+2j-3(B+j)/4. The intermediate finite path does not revisit its original state: a return after k R2 steps would require 2j=3k and j=4k, impossible for j>0.
At (n,0), R0 is the only enabled channel. The sequence R0,R1,R2 takes
  (n,0) -> (n+2,1) -> (n+4,4) -> (n+1,0).
The probability that R1 is selected at the second state and R2 at the third state is
  p_n = [k1(n+2)(n+1)/(k1(n+2)(n+1)+k0)]
        [24k2(n+4)(n+3)(n+2)(n+1)/
         {24k2(n+4)(n+3)(n+2)(n+1)+4k1(n+4)(n+3)+k0}],
where k0=V,k1=5/V^2,k2=4/V^7. For fixed V, 1-p_n=O(n^-2), so the infinite product product_{n>=n0}p_n is strictly positive. Along that event, the base A count increases by one every cycle and never returns. Its calendar time is infinite: each cycle includes an independent exponential holding time with rate k0 at B=0, whose infinite sum diverges almost surely. Choose n0 beyond the original A count; every count state consequently has positive probability of never returning after departure. Each is transient. A stationary probability on finite count states would give recurrence to at least one state of positive stationary mass, contradicting this. Adding a cemetery state in an explosive model would only produce a cemetery invariant law, not molecular safety concentration.

Strong endotacticity can also be checked without relying on the prose assertion: the source hull has vertices (0,0),(2,1),(4,4). Its outward edge normals are (-1,1),(1,-2),(3,-2). At each maximal source all outgoing normal components are nonpositive, and at least one is negative; the interior vertex cones inherit these inequalities. For the cone at (4,4), (3,4)=(1,1)+(2,3) is a positive combination of its cone-defining forms, so R2 has a strictly negative normal component for every nonzero vector in that cone. This proves the definition directly.

IMPORTANT SCOPE: the simpler logistic or epidemic extinction examples are not endotactic rebuttals. The example above really is strongly endotactic and is the relevant obstruction to transporting deterministic endotacticity into stochastic recurrence. It does not contradict deterministic permanence or finite-horizon N35.

3. POSITIVE THEOREM: N35 PLUS AN AUDITABLE RETURN CERTIFICATE
Fix V. Let X be an autonomous, finite-state, irreducible CTMC on a closed molecular class Gamma_V, with its exact factorial mass-action generator L_V. Let Q_V=Q intersect Gamma_V/V be nonempty and proper, and H_V be a nonempty buffered subset of Q_V. All statements are in concentration coordinates. Suppose:
 (a) From every h in H_V, the acquired N35 certificate gives
     P_h(tau_Q<=t)<=alpha_V+beta_V t for all t>=0, with alpha_V<1.
 (b) For every possible first-exit state y from Q_V,
     E_y[tau_H]<=M_V<infinity.
These are additional assumptions; condition (b) is not inferred from inward drift.
Then the unique stationary distribution pi_V satisfies
  pi_V(Q_V^c) <= M_V/(L_V^exit+M_V),
  L_V^exit := (1-alpha_V)^2/(2beta_V).
Consequently
  pi_V(Q_V^c) <= 2 beta_V M_V/(1-alpha_V)^2.
If alpha_V<=1/2 and M_V<=C V^p, then
  pi_V(Q_V^c) <= 16m Lambda C V^(p+1) exp[-c_* V].
This is an invariant probability and infinite-time occupation statement, not an exponentially long finite-horizon relabeling.

Proof:
Integrating P_h(tau_Q>t)>=(1-alpha_V-beta_V t)_+ gives E_h tau_Q >= L_V^exit uniformly. Define cycles starting on entrance to H, ending at the first next entrance to H after an exit from Q. Let P be the finite entrance-state transition matrix on H, and choose its invariant law mu. For a cycle C starting from h, Dynkin's identity gives
  E_h integral_0^C Lf(X_t)dt = E_h[f(X_C)-f(h)].
Averaging over mu makes the right side zero. Thus the cycle occupation measure, normalized by mean cycle length, is invariant; irreducibility identifies it with pi_V. Write A for the mu-averaged time before Q exit and B for the mu-averaged time from exit until H return. We have A>=L_V^exit and B<=M_V. Time outside Q in the cycle is at most B. Therefore
  pi_V(Q^c) <= B/(A+B) <= M_V/(L_V^exit+M_V).
All cycle expectations are finite because the class is finite and irreducible. The argument admits countable-state extensions, but this report makes no such extension without separately certifying nonexplosion and finite mean cycles.

An exact way to acquire condition (b): provide a nonnegative table or symbolic function W_V on Gamma_V such that
  W_V=0 on H_V,
  L_V W_V <= -1 on Gamma_V\H_V,
  max_{possible Q-exit y}W_V(y)<=M_V.
Stopped Dynkin and Fatou give E_y tau_H<=W_V(y). On a finite class, generator inequalities and irreducibility are exact finite checks. A succinct symbolic certificate may reduce the checking cost. A brute-force table/class enumeration can itself be enormous and must be charged. Existence of positive recurrence alone does not bound M_V polynomially: an exponentially slow return can cancel the rare-exit exponent.

This theorem is a transparent renewal-occupation consequence, not a claim of a new general theorem about endotactic networks. The new task-specific bridge is the return-certificate acquisition problem. A finite conserved class makes existence auditable, but does not rule out absorbing boundary classes; irreducibility must be checked on the actual class reached from the proposed start.

4. POSITIVE ALGEBRAIC THEOREM: COMPLEX BALANCE + COMPATIBLE CLASS WITNESS
For an autonomous classically scaled mass-action network, supply a positive c satisfying, at every complex y,
  sum_{r:source=y} k_r c^y = sum_{r:product=y} k_r c^{source(r)}.
Supply the actual closed irreducible count class Gamma_V. Existing ACK theory gives
  pi_V(n) = P(Z_V=n | Z_V in Gamma_V),
  Z_{V,j} independent Poisson(V c_j).
The scaled form is ACK Theorem 4.3. Complex-balanced mass-action networks are nonexplosive; their polynomial rates have finite expectation under the Poisson law, and the later Anderson-Cappelletti-Koyama-Kurtz result makes the nonexplosion/positive-recurrence point explicit. Verified primary sources:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.3042  (Theorem 4.3, pp. 9–10)
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.09356 (Theorem 2 and complex-balance corollary)
https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-010-9517-4
https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-018-0473-8

The following concentration bound is derived directly from that exact stationary law. Let Q={x:a_i dot x<=b_i, i=1,...,m}, and suppose delta_i=b_i-a_i dot c>0. For a supplied t_i>0, compute
  I_i=t_i delta_i-sum_j c_j[exp(t_i a_ij)-1-t_i a_ij].
Choose t_i with a rigorously checked I_i>0; such a choice exists because the derivative at zero of the expression is delta_i. If n_V is a supplied member of Gamma_V, write
  p_V=exp[-V sum_j c_j] product_j (Vc_j)^(n_V,j)/(n_V,j!).
Then exactly
  pi_V(Q^c) <= p_V^(-1) sum_i exp[-V I_i].
Proof: the centered product-Poisson mgf gives the Chernoff bound for each facet; a union bound controls unconditioned Q^c; conditioning divides by P(Z_V in Gamma_V), which is at least p_V.
If d is fixed and |n_V,j-Vc_j|<=C uniformly in V, Stirling bounds give p_V>=C_0 V^(-d/2) for sufficiently large V. Hence the stationary tail is at most
  (m/C_0) V^(d/2) exp[-V min_i I_i].
The theorem does not need inward collars. It needs the stronger algebraic balance and a compatible lattice/class witness. Using an arbitrary equilibrium from the wrong conserved class, or silently dropping the conditioning factor, is invalid. The exact p_V formula can be interval-checked rather than estimated asymptotically.
The large-volume connection between product-form stationary potential and the standard relative-entropy Lyapunov function is established prior work:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8408763/

5. FINITE-SPATIAL-GRAPH EXTENSION, INCLUDING UNEQUAL SPECIES DIFFUSIVITIES
Create species A_{s,j} for site s and molecular species j. At each site supply local complex balance at c_s. Add hopping reactions A_{s,j}->A_{t,j} with coefficients d_{s->t,j}. If
  sum_t d_{s->t,j} c_{s,j} = sum_t d_{t->s,j} c_{t,j}
for every s,j, then the augmented network is complex balanced at (c_s). This follows by adding balanced local and hopping contributions at each complex; singleton complexes shared by the two parts remain balanced after addition.
The exact stationary law is therefore the product of Poisson(V c_{s,j}), conditioned on the actual closed class. On a symmetric finite graph with common local c, arbitrary species-dependent coefficients D_j are allowed. This route does not require all species to diffuse at one common rate. It also does not assert a deterministic rectangular invariant region under unequal diffusion.
For q sites, common local c, and unconditioned irreducible class, simultaneous violation of local Q is bounded by
  q sum_i exp[-V I_i] <= qm exp[-V I_min].
For a conditioned class divide by its explicitly certified Poisson class-mass lower bound. The crude single-point bound has dimension qd and may be poor for large q. Site count, local volume, total counts, hopping activity, and class-witness acquisition are all charged. No continuum limit or classwide graph acquisition result is claimed. This extension is standard augmented-network ACK reasoning, useful as a sharply scoped endpoint rather than a novelty claim.

6. OTHER PRIMARY LITERATURE CHECKS / PRIORITY LIMITS
Anderson-Enciso-Johnston (2014) establishes deterministic robust positive equilibria coexisting with stochastic extinction, and analyzes particular quasi-stationary limits. That is a source precedent for the gap, not a generic QSD theorem from inward collars:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.3761
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3928931/
Anderson-Kim gives structural sufficient conditions for positive recurrence in selected network classes; positive recurrence is a separate stochastic analysis input:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11263
https://doi.org/10.1137/17M1161427

A generic QSD-concentration claim is not established here. Inward collars do not select a killed irreducible class, prove QSD uniqueness, or control its eigenfunction/conditioning weights. Boundary extinction can leave a long-lived interior QSD, but a theorem requires additional irreducibility and spectral/return hypotheses. Product-Poisson concentration and renewal occupation are existing mathematical mechanisms. The useful auditable conclusion is the exact bridge: local inward acquisition alone fails even under strong endotacticity; a stationary conclusion requires global return or complex balance, with class compatibility and costs explicit.
