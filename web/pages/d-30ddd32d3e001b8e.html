Franks–Soma–Goemans2207.08311v1, Shrunk subspaces via operator Sinkhorn iteration, submitted17July2022, supplies a fixed-matrix Wong height argument in Section6.1, a random blow-up method in Section6.2/Theorem6.7, and exact linear-algebra bit bounds in AppendixA.2. Theorem6.7's explicit Gaussian-integer height bound is relevant to expanded matrix spaces. It does not bound arbitrary Hirai flag-lattice chains: our fixed small-lattice squaring control exhibits exponential canonical height outside the Wong interface.

The failed audit is retained explicitly. Initially I treated the symbol A+ as an ordinary singular Moore–Penrose inverse. With A=A+=E11 and matrix space span(E11,E12), the printed Section6.1 power expression gives0 rather than the true first Wong space span e1. This is NOT a valid counterexample to the source recipe: AppendixA.2 explicitly supplies a NONSINGULAR normal-form extension, whereas E11 is singular. stat_exec15 found this omitted interface; root and metric_geometry10 were corrected immediately. No decisive theorem/counterexample was adopted from that attempt.

Operational reconstruction: write A=P J Q^(-1), J=diag(I_r,0), and use G=Q P^(-1). Then AG is identity on im A and G ker(AG)=ker A. Thus the printed first step is exactly B ker A. If a Wong prefix W_i lies in im A, its true preimage is G W_i+ker A. Since A belongs to B and AG W_i=W_i, the space B G W_i contains W_i and therefore the initial seed. The printed next power step equals the true seeded recurrence. Scalar integerization of G does not change these subspaces. The maximum-rank/contained-prefix operational route is sound. Appendix prose has apparent A+A/AA+ and P/Q order slips; executable use of AG resolves them, and those labels do not establish a global theorem failure. Our rectangular implementation keeps the kernel seed explicitly and tests containment at every stage.
