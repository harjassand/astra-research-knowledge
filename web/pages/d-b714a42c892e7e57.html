Title:A new continuation criterion for the relativistic Vlasov-Maxwell system

Authors:Jonathan Luk, Robert M. Strain
            
View a PDF of the paper titled A new continuation criterion for the relativistic Vlasov-Maxwell system, by Jonathan Luk and Robert M. Strain

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:The global existence of solutions to the relativistic Vlasov-Maxwell system given sufficiently regular finite energy initial data is a longstanding open problem. The main result of Glassey-Strauss (1986) shows that a solution $(f, E, B)$ remains $C^1$ as long as the momentum support of $f$ remains bounded. Alternate proofs were later given by Bouchut-Golse-Pallard (2003) and Klainerman-Staffilani (2002). We show that only the boundedness of the momentum support of $f$ after projecting to any two dimensional plane is needed for $(f, E, B)$ to remain $C^1$.
