1. Genuine stationary block reference and exact physical secants

Let sigma,rho be arbitrary faithful density matrices on C^d. Put
 s=sqrt(sigma), q=sqrt(rho), D=s-q, Delta=sigma-rho,
 Omega=(sigma directsum rho)/2,
 S=sqrt(Omega)=(s directsum q)/sqrt(2),
 Y=(1/sqrt(2))[[0,D],[D,0]].
Y is Hermitian and Tr(SY)=0. The faithful linear perturbation is
 Omega_e=Omega+e(SY+YS)
        =(1/2)[[sigma,e Delta],[e Delta,rho]].
It remains faithful for all sufficiently small real e; explicitly it is
positive when e^2 ||sigma^(-1/2)Delta rho^(-1/2)||^2<1.
The exact tangent root derivative is Y because
 sD+Dq=s^2-q^2=Delta.

The Fréchet log derivative has the exact cross block
 [Dlog_Omega(SY+YS)]_(1,2)=logsigma-logrho.
Indeed the unnormalized resolvent identity is
 (sigma+t)^(-1)(sigma-rho)(rho+t)^(-1)
   =(rho+t)^(-1)-(sigma+t)^(-1).
Integrating over t>=0 gives logsigma-logrho. Uniform factor1/2 in both
reference blocks and tangent cancels under t rescaling. This identity
does NOT assume sigma and rho commute. Thus the secant embedding gets
both desired physical square-root and logarithmic differences exactly.

2. A legal shared-noise extension and its exact energy

For a fixed Hermitian B, use the UNNORMALIZED Lyapunov convention
 Lyap_v(X)=vX+Xv,
 V_s=2Lyap_s^(-1)(BsB), H_s(X)={V_s,X}/2-BXB,
 V_q=2Lyap_q^(-1)(BqB), H_q(X)={V_q,X}/2-BXB.
Each is a legal HS-symmetric weighted KMS noise generator fixing its
respective stationary root. With the block noise B_ext=B directsum B,
the stationarity-forced drift at S is exactly V_ext=V_s directsum V_q.
Consequently
 H_ext(X)={V_ext,X}/2-B_ext X B_ext
is HS self-adjoint, fixes S, and its bounded drift/noise Dyson expansion
gives a CP semigroup. Transport by S^(1/2) gives a full-domain unital CP
Omega-stationary KMS semigroup. This is an ACTUAL legal extension, not a
formal ordered positive operator.

On an upper-right cross block its weighted action is
 H_cross(X)=(V_s X+X V_q)/2-BXB.
For our tangent, the exact stationary root Hessian is
 E2_ext=<Y,H_ext Y>
       =[E_s(rho)+E_q(sigma)]/2,
where E_s(rho)=Tr[q H_s(q)] and E_q(sigma)=Tr[s H_q(s)].
To verify, use H_s(s)=0,H_q(q)=0 and self-adjointness to write each as
<D,H_s D> or <D,H_q D>. The Hermitian mirror blocks of Y contribute
Tr[D H_cross(D)], which is exactly their average. The reverse energy
term is a real nonnegative cost of the legal second block, not an
implicitly cancelled duplicate of the original energy.

3. Exact physical entropy Hessian: mixed dynamics, not original J

Set d_s=s^(1/2), d_q=q^(1/2),
 K_s=d_s B d_s^(-1), C_s=d_s V_s d_s^(-1),
 K_q=d_q B d_q^(-1), C_q=d_q V_q d_q^(-1).
The physical upper-right Schrodinger action of the legal block generator
is exactly
 L_cross(X)=(C_s X+X C_q^*)/2-K_s X K_q^*.
The actual entropy-production Hessian at Omega is therefore
 J2_ext=Re Tr[L_cross(Delta)(logsigma-logrho)].
This follows from the tangent block Delta/2, its exact log derivative
and the two Hermitian mirror blocks. The mixed action can produce a
non-Hermitian cross block; the explicit real part is essential.

The supplied stationary-pi theorem yields the new exact comparison
 Re Tr[L_cross(sigma-rho)(logsigma-logrho)]
   >=(pi/2)[E_s(rho)+E_q(sigma)].
It is an inequality for the MIXED two-reference action L_cross. It is
not the original entropy production
 J_s(rho)=Tr[L_s,*(rho)(logrho-logsigma)],
 L_s,*(X)=(C_s X+X C_s^*)/2-K_s X K_s^*.
Replacing C_q,K_q by C_s,K_s would generally violate the stationary
extension interface. No comparison in the direction needed to recover
J_s(rho)>=cE_s(rho) is proved by this construction.

4. Exact two-dimensional commuting control of the mismatch

Take sigma=I_2/2, rho=diag(4/5,1/5), B=[[0,1],[1,0]]. Then
 s=I/sqrt(2), q=diag(2,1)/sqrt(5),
 V_s=I, V_q=diag(1/2,2),
 K_s=B, K_q=[[0,sqrt(2)],[1/sqrt(2),0]],
 C_s=I, C_q=diag(1/2,2).
The original forms and the extension energy are exactly
 E_s(rho)=1/5, E_q(sigma)=1/4, E2_ext=9/40,
 J_s(rho)=(3/5)log4=(6/5)log2.
For Delta=diag(-3/10,3/10), the mixed physical image is
 L_cross(Delta)=diag(-9/40-3sqrt(2)/10,
                         9/20+3sqrt(2)/20).
The entropy Hessian is
 J2_ext=[(9-6sqrt(2))log5+(9+30sqrt(2))log2]/40.
In particular
 J2_ext-J_s(rho)
  =[(9-6sqrt(2))log5+(30sqrt(2)-39)log2]/40>0.
Both coefficients are strictly positive because 13/10<sqrt(2)<3/2.
Thus both exact identities required for direct transfer fail already in
a commuting qubit example. The extension's entropy is larger, so its
lower bound does not supply a lower bound for the original entropy by
identifying or discarding that cost. This is NOT a counterexample to
any proposed original nonlinear entropy/root-energy inequality.

5. Specified no-go for copying/intertwining the original dynamics

If instead one copies the ORIGINAL WEIGHTED H_s into both flag blocks,
including the original cross action, its shared drift is V_s directsum
V_s. It fixes S if and only if H_s(q)=0, since H_s(s)=0 already.
Because H_s is PSD, that condition is equivalent to E_s(rho)=0.
Therefore this exact form-preserving copied weighted extension is not
stationary whenever the target root energy is positive. It cannot be
used for the nontrivial transfer without changing the generator.

A separate general marginal/intertwining obstruction is also elementary.
If an extended stationary reference Omega has a CP compression R with
R(Omega)=c rho (c>0), and its generator intertwines on that compression,
 R L_ext,*=L_s,* R,
then stationarity forces 0=L_s,*(c rho). Thus an enlargement retaining
autonomous original dynamics on a flag/reference marginal equal to rho
is impossible unless rho is already stationary. This covers direct
physical copies and local marginal-preserving dilations, not every
possible nonlocal extension or tangent encoding.
