STRONGEST CLAIM

The sharp transport inequality has no uniform global geometric compactness or
curvature modulus controlled by its entropy-power deficit alone. This fails
even in explicit smooth, steady, divergence-free, connected, closed
three-manifold suspensions. Three exact mechanisms are available:

1. On one fixed mapping torus, there are intermittent smooth metrics for which
   the normalized deficit tends to zero while the pointwise strain gap stays
   bounded below and sectional curvature diverges. A second, explicit
   sinusoidal family has strain converging uniformly to the equality strain,
   but unbounded curvature. Thus an L2 deficit cannot imply uniform strain
   control, and even uniform strain control does not imply C2/bounded-curvature
   rigidity without derivative or bounded-geometry hypotheses.
2. At fixed volume and fixed entropy H=1, exact equality examples collapse:
   a sequence of Sol mapping tori has injectivity radius tending to zero and
   curvature tending to infinity. Exact equality also admits finite covers
   with fixed H and diameter tending to infinity. Therefore equality itself
   gives no uniform injectivity-radius, curvature, or diameter bounds across
   the admitted class.
3. If disconnected manifolds are admitted, a Sol component plus a zero-field
   component gives the exact support-fraction defect. Absolute deficit can
   tend to zero while almost all volume is stagnant; the relative deficit
   records the sharp support loss. Time rescaling separately shows that an
   absolute defect cannot imply a positive entropy or work scale.

These are counterexamples to the named uniform consequences, not to the
sharp inequality, its exact local-equality classification, or a weaker
L2/measure-theoretic stability statement. No unspecified theorem is called
false: a useful stability theorem can still hold after fixing H, bounding
geometry/derivatives, measuring distance modulo the whole equality class, or
using only the L2 quantities in the existing deficit identity.

EXACT INTERFACE AND COMMON SUSPENSION CALCULATION

Let B be a hyperbolic matrix in SL(2,Z) with eigenvalues exp(a), exp(-a),
a>0. In eigen-coordinates (x,y), take its invariant lattice and form the
mapping torus
  M_B = (R^2 x R)/((v,t) ~ (Bv,t-1)).
For any smooth u:R->R with u(t+1)=u(t)+a, the metric
  g_u = dt^2 + exp(2u(t)) dx^2 + exp(-2u(t)) dy^2
descends: the two exp(+-a) eigenvalues of B cancel the change in u under the
gluing. Its volume form is dx dy dt, independent of u. The vector field
X=partial_t is smooth, divergence-free, and has unit length. Its time-one map
on each cross-section is B; Lebesgue entropy is H=h_mu(B)=a. This is the
standard entropy of a hyperbolic toral automorphism (Adler-Weiss 1967; the
cat-map instance and direct cylinder/Ruelle check are also in the saved
cycle-1 proof).

The strain endomorphism in the orthonormal frame
  e1=exp(-u) partial_x, e2=exp(u) partial_y, e3=partial_t
is diag(u',-u',0). When u'>0, its normal strain parameter is s=u', while the
log-speed and cross-strain defects q,b vanish. The established stationary
strain-viscosity identity gives W=2 nu integral |Def X|^2, hence, with V the
volume of M_B,
  W/(4 nu V) - H^2
    = integral_0^1 (u'(t))^2 dt - a^2
    = integral_0^1 (u'(t)-a)^2 dt.                         (1)
This exact identity reduces all examples below to a one-dimensional
calculation. Entropy is supplied by the explicit return map; no entropy
estimation algorithm is being claimed.

INTERMITTENT COUNTEREXAMPLE: SMALL L2 DEFICIT, LARGE POINTWISE DEFECT

Choose a smooth nonnegative bump b supported in (-1/2,1/2), with max b=1,
and write B1=int b, B2=int b^2. On the t-circle let
  b_eps(t)=b((t-1/2)/eps),  m_eps=int_0^1 b_eps(t)dt=eps B1,
  delta_eps(t)=c(b_eps(t)-m_eps),
where 0<c<a/2 is fixed. This is smooth and has zero mean. Define the periodic
psi_eps by psi'_eps=delta_eps and set u_eps(t)=a t+psi_eps(t). For small eps,
  u'_eps=a+delta_eps >= a-c m_eps > 0,
  ||u'_eps-a||_infinity >= c(1-m_eps) -> c,
  Delta_eps := W/(4 nu V)-H^2
       = c^2(eps B2-eps^2 B1^2) -> 0.                     (2)
Thus no uniform modulus Delta->0 => ||s-H||_infinity->0 follows from the
deficit. The region carrying order-c strain deviation has width O(eps).

For this metric the sectional curvatures, verified directly from its
Christoffel symbols, are
  K(e1,e3)=-(u''+(u')^2),
  K(e2,e3)= u''-(u')^2,
  K(e1,e2)= (u')^2.                                      (3)
At a point where b' is nonzero, u''=c eps^(-1)b'((t-1/2)/eps), while u'
stays bounded by a+c. Therefore sup |K| grows at least linearly in eps^(-1).
The manifold, entropy H=a, and volume are fixed throughout.

HIGH-FREQUENCY COUNTEREXAMPLE: EVEN UNIFORM STRAIN CONTROL IS NOT C2 CONTROL

Set
  u_n(t)=a t + n^(-3/2) sin(2 pi n t),
  u'_n(t)=a+2 pi n^(-1/2) cos(2 pi n t).
For n large enough u'_n>0, and (1) gives the exact gap
  Delta_n=2 pi^2/n -> 0,
while ||u'_n-a||_infinity=2 pi/sqrt(n)->0. But
  u''_n(t)=-4 pi^2 sqrt(n) sin(2 pi n t).
At t=1/(4n), formula (3) yields
  K(e1,e3)=4 pi^2 sqrt(n)-a^2,
  K(e2,e3)=-4 pi^2 sqrt(n)-a^2.
Thus curvature has no uniform bound even with fixed M_B, fixed H, and uniform
convergence of the strain to its equality value. A C2 conclusion needs an
additional derivative/regularity premise. The exact symbolic script checks
the curvature formulas and these identities for n=100,1000,10000.

EXACT EQUALITY COLLAPSE AT FIXED VOLUME AND ENTROPY

For each integer n>=2 define
  A_n = [[n,1],[n^2-1,n]],
  d_n=sqrt(n^2-1), lambda_n=n+d_n, a_n=log(lambda_n).
Then det A_n=1 and its eigenvalues are lambda_n and lambda_n^(-1). Let
  P_n=(1/n)[[1,1],[d_n,-d_n]],
  D_n=diag(lambda_n,lambda_n^(-1)),
so A_n=P_n D_n P_n^(-1) and |det P_n|=2d_n/n^2. The lattice
  Lambda_n=sqrt(|det P_n|) P_n^(-1) Z^2
has covolume one and is preserved by D_n, since A_n preserves Z^2. Use it as
the fiber lattice in the Sol mapping torus with u(t)=a_n t. The total volume
is one. The vector from the integer vector (0,1) has squared fiber length
  |sqrt(|det P_n|) P_n^(-1)(0,1)|^2 = 1/d_n.
It is a noncontractible fiber loop, so the global injectivity radius is at
most half its length, 1/(2 sqrt(d_n)) -> 0. The Sol sectional curvature has
magnitude a_n^2 -> infinity.

Now set X_n=partial_t/a_n. Entropy scales linearly under constant time
rescaling, so H_n=1. The strain eigenvalues are (+1,-1,0), hence
  |Def X_n|^2=2, W_n=4 nu, V_n=1,
and the sharp inequality holds with equality for every n. This is a connected
closed, volume-normalized, fixed-entropy exact-collapse sequence; no deficit
modulus can force a uniform injectivity radius or curvature bound over the
full class. The changing monodromy and metric are essential; the example does
not contradict a theorem restricted to one fixed manifold and bounded
geometry.

GLOBAL DIAMETER, ZERO-SET, AND TIME-SCALE BOUNDARIES

For the m-fold cover of one fixed Sol suspension, the base circle has length
m and monodromy B^m. With X=partial_t, H=h(B^m)/m=a, s=a, and Delta=0. The
1-Lipschitz projection to that circle gives diam(M_m)>=m/2. Equality therefore
does not imply a uniform diameter bound even when local geometry and H are
fixed; volume grows like m.

If disconnected closed manifolds are allowed, put the Sol field with rate a
on a component occupying volume fraction alpha and set X=0 on the remaining
component. The identity on the zero component contributes no entropy, so
  H=alpha a,  W/(4 nu V)=alpha a^2,
  Delta=alpha(1-alpha)a^2,
  Delta/H^2=(1-alpha)/alpha.                              (4)
This exactly realizes the support factor in the prior zero-set extension.
For alpha->0, absolute Delta->0 while the entropy itself tends to zero and
the relative gap diverges; an absolute-gap hypothesis alone is vacuous at
small scale. For alpha->1, relative gap is asymptotic to the zero-volume
fraction, so this example confirms rather than defeats the sharp support
dependence. It proves no connected positive-volume-zero construction.

Finally X->cX gives H->cH and W->c^2W. Exact equality persists for every
c>0, including c->0. A dimensionful statement about minimum work, throughput,
or positive entropy needs a fixed clock/entropy normalization. This is not a
new objection; it preserves the previously audited scaling boundary.
