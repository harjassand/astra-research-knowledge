Title:Rapid mixing of quantum spin chains at any finite temperature

Authors:Leeseok Kim
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Rapid mixing of quantum spin chains at any finite temperature, by Leeseok Kim

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:We prove that a quasi-local quantum Gibbs sampler rapidly mixes to the Gibbs state of any 1D finite-range Hamiltonian at all finite temperatures in $\mathcal{O}\left(\log (n/\varepsilon)\right)$ time. Each update traces out a contiguous block of constant length and reconstructs it using the Petz recovery map, following early proposals for quantum heat-bath Gibbs samplers by Kastoryano and Brandão~\cite{kastoryano2016quantum}. Our proof directly bounds the distance between the updated state and the target Gibbs state using quantum Wasserstein distance of order $1$. We further provide $\mathcal{O}(\text{polylog}(n/\varepsilon))$ depth of quantum circuits to prepare these Gibbs states. We hope these techniques will facilitate the analysis and design of other Gibbs samplers.
