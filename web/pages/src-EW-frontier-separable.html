1. Why the next representation must accommodate genuinely nonlocal corrections
There is now a concrete reason not to pursue ordinary exponential support locality.
On the three-particle exclusion gap graph, with coordinates \(a,b\ge0\), the polynomial
\[
h(a,b)=(a-b)(2a+b+1)(a+2b+1)
\]
is harmonic, including at the reflecting boundaries. For the local source
\[
b=\delta_{(0,1)}-\delta_{(1,0)},
\]
its harmonic moment is
\[
\langle b,h\rangle=-12.
\]
If the corresponding pressure gradient decayed exponentially, summation by parts against \(h\) would give \(-12=0\). Thus that projection cannot have an exponentially local tail.
This is not an unrelated pathological source. With \(H_{\mathrm{diag}}=U_z\sum_iZ_iZ_{i+1}\),
\[
A_{\mathrm{diag}}^2J=-8U_z^2(J-F_{\mathrm{mid}}),
\qquad
F_{\mathrm{mid}}=\sum_i Z_{i-1}j_iZ_{i+2},
\]
and the population divergence of \(F_{\mathrm{mid}}\) is precisely the displayed gap-lattice dipole, up to the known hopping factor. Astra’s N754 source identified this obstruction; I independently recalculated its harmonic and current identities in the new code. The convention matters: the previous centered-occupation parameter \(U=4\) corresponds to \(U_z=1\). 
The correct response is not to assume that the tail is negligible. It is to represent and certify the tail without listing its coordinates.
Fermionic rather than Pauli degree remains useful for this purpose, but it is not a new representation choice: FDAOE already preserves widely separated quadratic fermionic operators. The new work below concerns exact conservation and error-controlled computation within a nonlocal representation. arXiv
2. The central operation: conservation repair with at most twice the tensor rank
At degree \(k\), write the population positions as \(x_1<\cdots<x_k\) and their relative gaps as
\[
g_i=x_{i+1}-x_i-1,\qquad i=1,\ldots,m,\qquad m=k-1.
\]
The domain is the orthant \(\mathbb N_0^m\). The earlier construction enumerated a large portion of this domain and solved a multidimensional Poisson equation.
Instead, represent the conservation defect as
\[
b(g_1,\ldots,g_m)
=
B_1(g_1)B_2(g_2)\cdots B_m(g_m),
\]
where each \(B_i(g_i)\) is a small matrix and the complete product is a scalar. This is a tensor train: its bond dimensions measure how much information must pass between successive gap coordinates.
The representation is explicit input to the operation. It is not an assumed representation of the unknown exact response.
Construct the correction from one-dimensional sums
Choose normalized reference sequences \(\rho_i\). Define
\[
M_i=\sum_{s\ge0}B_i(s),
\qquad
R_i(s)=\rho_i(s)M_i,
\]
\[
H_i(s)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{s}
\big[B_i(\ell)-\rho_i(\ell)M_i\big].
\]
Now set
\[
F_i(g)
=
B_1(g_1)\cdots B_{i-1}(g_{i-1})
H_i(g_i)
R_{i+1}(g_{i+1})\cdots R_m(g_m).
\]
With outgoing-minus-incoming grid divergence,
\[
\partial_\square F(g)
=
\sum_i\big[F_i(g)-F_i(g-e_i)\big],
\]
the ordered matrix products telescope:
\[
\boxed{
\partial_\square F
=
b-\left(M_1\cdots M_m\right)\prod_i\rho_i.
}
\]
Therefore, whenever the source has zero total mass,
\[
\boxed{\partial_\square F=b.}
\]
No matrices need to commute. Each \(F_i\) retains the original tensor bond dimensions.
For the geometric cores used in the implementation,
\[
B_i(s)=\sum_p A_{ip}q_{ip}^{s},\qquad |q_{ip}|<1,
\]
the sums and primitives have closed rational expressions. For a geometric reference \(\rho_i(s)=(1-r_i)r_i^s\),
\[
M_i=\sum_p\frac{A_{ip}}{1-q_{ip}},
\]
\[
H_i(s)
=
M_i r_i^{s+1}
-
\sum_p\frac{A_{ip}q_{ip}^{s+1}}{1-q_{ip}}.
\]
At most one additional pole is introduced into each core. The resulting fields are square summable. General summable sequences require a separate square-summability check on their primitives.
Route through the original exclusion moves
Cartesian grid edges are not all individual physical exclusion moves. Replace
\[
g\longrightarrow g+e_i
\]
by the legal path
\[
g\longrightarrow g+e_1
\longrightarrow g+e_2
\longrightarrow\cdots
\longrightarrow g+e_i.
\]
The first move creates a vacancy gap; subsequent moves transfer it. Divergence telescopes exactly, and the routing norm obeys
\[
\|\mathcal RF\|_{\mathrm{edge}}
\le m\|F\|_{\mathrm{grid}}.
\]
The routed edge fields involve sums \(F_j+\cdots+F_m\). Naively adding tensor trains would increase the rank once per summand. That is unnecessary: use the block core
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
B_i&H_i\\
0&R_i
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its upper-right product block sums all terms containing exactly one \(H_i\).
Consequently:
\[
\boxed{
\text{Each routed edge-family field has bond dimension at most }2r_i.
}
\]
This is an explicit construction, not a rank bound supplied as a hypothesis. It preserves the actual unrestricted hopping graph, including the appropriate physical-edge labels. The known factor relating graph divergence to quantum population balance is restored when converting the flow into a quantum correction.
A crucial limitation: this produces a feasible correction, not necessarily the minimum-energy correction. A compact feasible flow can still have a large norm.
3. A multiscale completion controls that energy
The reference profile in the construction is not innocuous. Sending a spatially spread source directly to a point can concentrate too much flow.
I derived a multiscale alternative for
\[
b_J=\mu_J-\delta_0,
\]
where
\[
\rho_j(s)=2^{-j}(1-2^{-j})^s,
\qquad
\mu_j(g)=\prod_{i=1}^{m}\rho_j(g_i).
\]
The length scale is \(2^J\). Decompose
\[
b_J=\sum_{j=1}^{J}(\mu_j-\mu_{j-1}),
\]
and repair each difference using the previous scale as reference.
The one-dimensional norms can be calculated explicitly. Combining them with the legal routing bound gives
\[
\|w_J\|
\le
m\,2^{(m-1)/2}
\sum_{j=1}^{J}2^{j(1-m/2)}.
\]
For \(m\ge3\),
\[
\boxed{
\|w_J\|^2
\le
\frac{m^2 2^{m-1}}
{\left(2^{m/2-1}-1\right)^2}
<24m^2,
}
\]
independently of the length scale.
The representation requires \(O(m^2J)\) separated terms, rather than \((2^J)^m\) coordinates. Pole and coefficient precision remain charged.
The executed four-dimensional example represents length scale
\[
2^{40}=1{,}099{,}511{,}627{,}776
\]
using 478 routed terms, with exact divergence and the analytic energy bound
\[
\boxed{\|w_J\|^2\le128.}
\]
No grid of that radius was constructed. The complete forty-level energy was not numerically evaluated; the bound follows from the proof. Smaller cases were evaluated exactly.
This is an explicit source-class theorem, not a statement that every interacting response defect has this form. It nevertheless shows that large relative extent need not cause either a large representation or a large completion energy.
4. The infinite domain can be solved and verified without truncating its tail
For a shifted geometric sequence
\[
u_{q,a}(g)=1_{g\ge a}q^{g-a},
\]
the exact overlap is
\[
\boxed{
\langle u_{q,a},u_{r,b}\rangle
=
\frac{q^{c-a}r^{c-b}}{1-qr},
\qquad c=\max(a,b).
}
\]
Products give separated-field overlaps. Tensor-train overlaps are finite products of small transfer matrices. Thus norms, Gram matrices and complete residuals are evaluated over the whole infinite lattice using finite rational arithmetic.
Finite-range quantum commutators also remain computable. They move Majorana endpoints and distinguish a finite number of near-contact cases. A long gap is retained as a shifted functional argument, not visited site by site.
The implemented algebra retains nonzero nearest- and next-nearest-neighbour diagonal interactions and all generated commutator terms. It does not truncate outputs merely because they leave the trial dictionary.
Certifying the minimum-energy projection
Let \(\nabla\) be the graph gradient and \(\partial=\nabla^*\). Given a source \(b=\partial F\), let \(g_*\) be the gradient component of \(F\), and define
\[
E_b=\|g_*\|^2.
\]
For any potential \(\phi\) and any explicitly constructed flow \(w\) satisfying \(\partial w=b\),
\[
\boxed{
2\langle b,\phi\rangle-\|\nabla\phi\|^2
\le E_b\le\|w\|^2.
}
\]
Their gap is exactly
\[
\boxed{
\Gamma=\|w-\nabla\phi\|^2.
}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\boxed{
F-w\text{ is exactly balanced},\qquad
\|F-w-\Pi F\|\le\sqrt{\Gamma}.
}
\]
This converts a feasible compressed repair into an accuracy-certified approximation of the orthogonal balance projection, without an inverse spectral-gap constant.
The solver starts with a feasible flow, adds explicitly divergence-free cycle fields, and optimizes finite potential and cycle dictionaries. Final coefficients are rounded, and the full infinite residual is recomputed exactly. A poor dictionary returns a wide interval rather than an unsupported prediction.

6. How the operation enters the full interacting solve
The construction is more than a compact final-output format. It supplies the projection operation needed during an iterative solve.
At degree at most \(N\), retain the unprojected skew operator \(W_N\), with the previous explicit bound
\[
\|W_N\|\le M_N=\frac{4Nt}{d}+v.
\]
The constrained response satisfies
\[
(I+\Pi W_N\Pi)x_N=z.
\]
Choose
\[
h=\frac1{1+M_N^2}.
\]
For an exactly balanced iterate \(x_j\), compute an exactly balanced approximation \(q_j\) to
\[
\Pi(z-W_Nx_j)
\]
with certified norm error at most \(\eta\). Then update
\[
x_{j+1}=(1-h)x_j+hq_j.
\]
Skew-adjointness gives a contraction factor \(\sqrt{1-h}\), yielding
\[
\boxed{
\|x_j-x_N\|
\le
(1-h)^{j/2}\|z\|+2\eta.
}
\]
Thus the number of outer iterations is
\[
O\!\left((1+M_N^2)\log\epsilon^{-1}\right).
\]
This count has no relative-coordinate radius or unknown physical relaxation time. The standard iterative method is not claimed as new; the new component is its exactly balanced, norm-certified nonlocal projection interface.
A direct stopping certificate avoids reconstructing the exact pressure
For a balanced trial \(x\), set
\[
r=z-(I+W_N)x.
\]
If \(L_{\mathrm{pressure}}\) is a certified lower bound on the gradient-component energy of its visible residual, then
\[
\boxed{
|\kappa_N-\langle z,x\rangle|
\le
\|z\|
\sqrt{\|r\|^2-L_{\mathrm{pressure}}}.
}
\]
The exact unknown pressure is not required. The earlier degree-truncation allowance must still be added before claiming accuracy for full \(D\).
Every approximation of \(W_Nx_j\), every pole approximation and every rank reduction is charged to the error budget. Tensor rounding may break conservation; the actual defect must be repaired, and the combined change checked. A small uncorrected tensor-rounding error is insufficient.
What remains unbounded is the work required to close each inner projection certificate at the desired rank. The polynomial outer iteration count does not conceal or remove that obligation.

8. Complete cost accounting
For a represented geometric tensor source, conservation repair and verification have polynomial cost in the number of gap coordinates, tensor ranks, pole counts and rational bit lengths. The routed rank overhead is at most two per edge family.
There are several boundaries to that statement.
The spatial decay length can be enormous without requiring a corresponding grid, but poles close to one still affect coefficient magnitudes and arithmetic precision. Large delayed point masses can also have long exact representations; the prototype explicitly charges those delays rather than claiming logarithmic work when its fractions themselves contain many bits.
A fixed finite dictionary requires finite Gram assembly, a finite quadratic solve and complete residual verification. Those operations are charged. The dictionary is not presumed to contain an accurate answer: the resulting interval decides whether it is adequate.
Finite-range interacting commutators can branch into additional endpoint types and contact cases. Their cost is bounded in terms of the explicit representation and \(C_{\mathrm{physical}}^N\), but repeated rank growth is not assumed away.
The physical inputs remain the local Hamiltonian coefficients and the specified pure-dephasing structure. This calculation acquires neither those coefficients nor proof of exact locality, translation invariance or Markovianity in a device. No Gibbs specimens, quantum hardware, additional physical noise or many-body spectrum were used.
The checks include exact matrix-valued telescoping, legal routing, factor-two rank construction, infinite contractions, 514 independently expanded infinite-tail commutator coefficients, the physical current identities, and separate reconstruction of the entire pressure residual. All standalone reruns passed. They are not independent mathematical review or experimental validation.
9. The exact remaining theorem
The geometric grid is no longer mandatory for either conservation repair or certificate evaluation. But low-rank input does not imply a low-rank inverse.
Even the elementary separable equation
\[
\Lambda X+X\Lambda=\mathbf1\mathbf1^T
\]
has
\[
X_{ij}=\frac1{\lambda_i+\lambda_j},
\]
a full-rank Cauchy matrix for distinct positive \(\lambda_i\). This rejects exact rank preservation. It is not a lower bound against accurate low-rank approximation, and it does not rule out the desired quantum algorithm.
The decisive theorem is now:
Construct, along the actual degree-\(N\) interacting response calculation, near-minimum-energy Hodge corrections and coefficient fields with certified errors, tensor ranks, pole/state counts, rational precision, and total discovery/solve/verification cost bounded by
\[
C_{\mathrm{physical}}^N\epsilon^{-c}.
\]

This must be a constructive statement about the fields generated by the physical operator—not a supplied compact-witness assumption.
The new result makes that theorem more specific. Exact population balance itself can be maintained without geometric enumeration and without an exponential rank penalty. What needs further mathematics is the rank needed to be near optimal, and how interactions propagate that rank across successive corrections.
Operator-backflow and related transport methods remain strong comparators; no superiority over optimized implementations follows from the present work. arXiv
The retained advance is a compressed conservation operation with an energy-controlled multiscale extension, an exact infinite-domain solver for a physically generated projection, and an error-accounted route into the full interacting calculation. The remaining barrier is now accurate low-rank inversion—not merely representing or enforcing conservation over a large domain.
