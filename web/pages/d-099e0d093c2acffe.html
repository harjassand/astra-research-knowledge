COORDINATOR DECODER TRANSFER THROUGH A SUPPORT-BOUNDED CHAIN HOMOTOPY
Frozen v1, 2026-10-09. Status: internally derived elementary algebraic lemma; not a claimed novel theorem or completed qLTC decoder. Construction of the required maps for the GGJ family remains the central gate.

Let C and K be finite chain complexes over a field, with boundary maps d_i:C_i→C_{i-1} and e_i:K_i→K_{i-1}. Fix degree2 errors. Let P_i:C_i→K_i and G_i:K_i→C_i be chain maps (e_iP_i=P_{i-1}d_i, d_iG_i=G_{i-1}e_i). Suppose H_i:C_i→C_{i+1} obeys
    id_{C_2}−G_2P_2=d_3H_2+H_1d_2.
Choose block support weights with uniform bounds |P_i v|≤L_{Pi}|v|, |G_i w|≤L_{Gi}|w| and |H_1v|≤L_H|v|. Reduced weight is min_{b∈im d_3}|z+b| in C_2 and analogously in K_2. Chain-map identities imply |P_2z|red≤L_P2|z|red and |G_2r|red≤L_G2|r|red: change the representative by a boundary and use P_2d_3=e_3P_3 or G_2e_3=d_3G_3.

Assume a supplied-syndrome K decoder D takes η=e_2w+ε and returns c' such that |w−c'|red≤γ|ε|, whenever |w|red≤a and |ε|≤b. No knowledge of w or ε is supplied to D.

Given C syndrome y=d_2z+m, compute η=P_1y=e_2P_2z+P_1m. If L_P2|z|red≤a and L_P1|m|≤b, apply D to obtain c'=D(η). Output the C correction
    c=G_2c'+H_1 y.
The exact identity is
    z−c = G_2(P_2z−c')+d_3H_2z−H_1m.
Indeed substitute the homotopy formula for z−G_2P_2z and y=d_2z+m. Modulo im d_3 the middle term vanishes. Hence
    |z−c|red ≤ L_G2 γ L_P1 |m| + L_H |m|.
In characteristic2, minus and plus coincide, matching binary Pauli correction. This proof includes arbitrary adversarial data and syndrome errors and does not use a stochastic noise model.

The decoder only accesses y, not z. H_2z occurs solely in the correctness proof. If P_1,G_2,H_1 can be applied in O(N) time and memory and the K decoder uses O(N), then the transferred decoder uses O(N) with fixed constants. Physical-qubit versus stalk support conversion contributes the fixed local block-size bounds; it must be included separately in a code-family application.

This does not prove the GGJ application. The remaining task is to construct actual P,G,H from the anchored-array bicomplex, with the chain and homotopy identities and support/cost bounds uniform in quotient size. Vector-space quasi-isomorphism or existence of arbitrary linear inverses is insufficient: those maps may have global support. The local Δ/T exactness and bounded horizontal primitives suggest a finite-depth zigzag, but that zigzag must be written and verified, especially in the presence of noisy noncycle syndromes.
