Target and exact stochastic interface
--------------------------------------
Use the definitions in outputs/research/sol_ground_xxz/RESULT.txt: S=L-1
sites, H_L=Hcrit-(kappa/L^2)W-(lambda/L^2)N, beta=tau L^2. In the occupation
basis, -Hcrit is the sub-Markov generator

  (Gkill f)(X)=2J sum_{Y: X<->Y by one exclusion hop}[f(Y)-f(X)]
                 -2J(n_1(X)+n_S(X))f(X).

The endpoint term is killing at rate 2J for each occupied endpoint. It has no
off-diagonal transition to the sector with one fewer particle. This is not the
usual zero-density reservoir generator, which does have those death jumps.
However, that reservoir generator, ordered by particle number, is block
triangular; its diagonal N-block is exactly Gkill restricted to N. Adding the
diagonal Feynman-Kac potential kappa W+lambda N preserves triangularity.
Consequently its eigenvalue multiset is the union of the tilted killed-block
eigenvalue multisets, and its exponential trace is the sum of their block
traces. This gives an exact finite-L spectral/tracial bridge to a zero-density
open-SSEP formulation, but does not itself justify importing a continuum
large-deviation theorem whose assumptions exclude zero reservoirs.

The target limiting variational problem is equivalently

  e*(kappa,lambda) = inf_{rho}
      { I_DV(rho) - kappa int rho^2 - lambda int rho },
  I_DV(rho) = (J/2) int |theta'|^2,
  rho=sin^2(theta/2), theta in H^1_0(0,1), 0<=theta<=pi.

The local attraction term changes the angle Euler-Lagrange equation to

  J theta'' + ((kappa+lambda)/2) sin(theta)
                 - (kappa/4) sin(2 theta) = 0,
  theta(0)=theta(1)=0.

Thus for kappa>0 the equation is double-sine-Gordon; the ordinary
sine-Gordon survival equation is only a neighboring special case, not the
same variational problem.

Closest primary sources and exact scope
---------------------------------------

1. Bodineau--Dagallier, “Large deviations for out of equilibrium
   correlations in the symmetric simple exclusion process,” arXiv:2212.11561v2
   (published EJP 29 (2024), paper 63),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11561 .
   Section 2.2.1, equations (2.13)--(2.22), is a particularly direct prior
   for the Fisher cost. At equal reservoir density rho in (0,1), the finite-N
   reversible DV functional is the Dirichlet form. For the specific test law
   mu^N=product_i Ber(rhohat(i/N)), with rhohat smooth and equal to rho in
   boundary neighborhoods, their exact computation gives

      I_DV^N(mu^N)/N -> (1/8) int rhohat(1-rhohat)
                                      |partial_x logit(rhohat)|^2 dx
                         = (1/8) int |theta'|^2 dx.

   This is the same interior angle-Fisher integrand (up to the chain-rate
   normalization) as the proposed limit. It is a special product-profile
   asymptotic/recovery computation, not the arbitrary-state-law Gamma-liminf
   used in the target proof. Their reservoir density is strictly between 0
   and 1 and the tested profile agrees with it near endpoints; the target has
   zero-density killing/Dirichlet traces, a local rho^2 tilt, variable total
   number, and a full-Fock trace. Their main Theorem 2.3 concerns two-point
   correlations of the positive-density-reservoir process, not this target.

2. Agranov--Meerson--Vilenkin, “Survival of interacting diffusing particles
   inside a domain with absorbing boundary,” arXiv:1507.04460v2,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.04460 .
   Sections II and V.1 give the closest boundary-condition and profile
   parameterization: SSEP macroscopic fluctuation theory for no absorption,
   with rho=sin^2(U/2), U=0 at the absorbing boundary, and the stationary
   sine-Gordon equation U''+C sin U=0 on an interval. Their survival action is
   proportional to int |U'|^2, and the interval solution is in elliptic
   functions. This strongly anticipates the boundary-pinned Fisher geometry.
   The result is an MFT saddle calculation for fixed initial total density
   and a no-absorption event; it does not prove a microscopic Gamma limit,
   does not add the local kappa int rho^2 potential, and does not establish
   the grand-canonical all-sector ground-energy or speed-L trace theorem.
   With kappa=0, a Legendre transform over fixed mass is a close formal
   connection; for kappa>0 the fixed-mass survival action alone does not
   supply the profile-resolved cost needed for the rho^2 tilt.

3. Bertini--Gabrielli--Landim, “Concurrent Donsker--Varadhan and
   hydrodynamical large deviations,” arXiv:2111.05892v1; Ann. Probab. 51
   (2023), 1298--1341, https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05892,
   DOI 10.1214/22-AOP1619.
   This proves joint time/system-size LDP and variational convergence for
   weakly asymmetric exclusion on a d-dimensional torus, for time-averaged
   empirical density and current. It is a rigorous ancestor for the DV/
   hydrodynamic Gamma-convergence method and Fisher form. Its periodic domain,
   conserved fixed-density sectors, and current/density interface do not
   provide the target's open zero-trace boundary or its local tilted
   grand-canonical energy. It is not a direct implication without a new
   boundary/sector theorem.

4. Lecomte--Garrahan--van Wijland, “Inactive dynamical phase of a symmetric
   exclusion process on a ring,” arXiv:1203.1600v1,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.1600 .
   This maps activity bias to a ferromagnetic XXZ ground-state problem and
   studies continuum density profiles through fluctuating-hydrodynamics/MFT
   and integrability. It is periodic, fixed particle number, and activity
   tilt, so the boundary and variable-mass target are not covered. There is
   an exact bulk-potential relation:

      -kappa rho^2-lambda rho
        = kappa rho(1-rho) - (kappa+lambda)rho.

   Thus the target potential is an SSEP activity/easy-axis-anisotropy
   density term plus a longitudinal field. This is a strong mechanism-level
   prior, but the source's ring, fixed-mass interface and periodic geometry
   do not yield the open killed-chain theorem.

5. Miao--Ilievski--Gamayun, “Semi-classical quantisation of magnetic
   solitons in the anisotropic Heisenberg quantum chain,” arXiv:2010.07232v3,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07232 .
   Its Section 3 treats the periodic ferromagnetic spin-1/2 XXZ chain,
   fixed M-magnon Bethe sectors, and weak-anisotropy continuum scaling
   eta=epsilon ell/L+O(L^-2), Delta-1=O(L^-2), leading to a classical
   anisotropic LL Hamiltonian. Writing S^z=cos(theta)=1-2rho, the target's
   bulk potential is kappa(1-(S^z)^2)/4 minus
   (kappa+lambda)(1-S^z)/2, i.e. the standard easy-axis anisotropy plus a
   longitudinal field (up to a constant). This makes both the spin-coherent
   continuum mechanism and weak-anisotropy scale established prior physics.
   The paper treats periodic chains and fixed M-magnon Bethe sectors; it does
   not state an open-chain Dirichlet Gamma-limit, an arbitrary-state lower
   bound, or the grand-canonical full-Fock trace limit. Its asymptotic
   Bethe/LL spectral analysis is not the target's variational proof.

6. Napiórkowski--Seiringer, “Free energy asymptotics of the quantum
   Heisenberg spin chain,” arXiv:1912.01967v2,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01967 .
   Theorem 2.1 is a rigorous low-temperature asymptotic of the thermodynamic
   free energy for the homogeneous isotropic nearest-neighbor ferromagnet:
   first L->infinity at fixed beta, then beta->infinity. No weak XXZ
   perturbation, chemical field, killed endpoints, local contact tilt, or
   joint beta=tau L^2 open-chain limit appears. It is an important rigorous
   baseline but does not imply this result.

7. Ruijsenaars, “The continuum limit of the infinite isotropic Heisenberg
   chain in its ground state representation,” J. Funct. Anal. 39 (1980),
   75--84, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/002212368090021X .
   This is an infinite-chain isotropic strong-resolvent limit to free bosons
   in a ground-state representation. It is adjacent low-excitation continuum
   physics, not a finite open-chain, weakly attractive, macroscopic-density
   variational or full-Fock result.

Safe novelty boundary
---------------------
The Fisher/DV functional, angle variable, absorbing-boundary pinning,
weak-anisotropy continuum scale, and coherent-profile Euler--Lagrange
mechanism all have strong prior lineages. The exact target is not directly
covered by the checked source theorems. The candidate's load-bearing
mathematical increment, if the independent audit confirms it, is the
microscopic arbitrary-state-law lower bound for the open killed chain that
simultaneously (i) enforces the zero endpoint trace, (ii) identifies the
full local contact observable as int rho^2, and (iii) permits variable total
mass / all Fock sectors, followed by the full trace passage. This is a
specific Gamma-limit/tilted-ground-state theorem. Claim it as internally
reconstructed candidate mathematics only; novelty and priority remain
unknown pending a broader source and expert review.
