P13 noisy-vacuum thermal reference | 2026-10-08

Under [q,p]=i, E|alpha|^2=nu, and power loss transmissivity eta, the normalized additive displacement channel maps vacuum to thermal occupancy nu; applying loss afterward gives t=eta nu. Vacuum is invariant under the passive fanout, and independent noise/loss on the R+1 modes therefore gives tau_t^(tensor R+1), where P(n)=t^n/(1+t)^(n+1).

For the labeled all-one pattern on the R idlers, each thermal mode contributes P(1)=t/(1+t)^2, so the exact vacuum-reference herald mass is p0=[t/(1+t)^2]^R. The unheralded signal factor remains exactly thermal. Its count law is geometric and its total variation distance from vacuum is t/(1+t).

With eta=1/R and nu=R^(-a), t=R^(-(a+1)), p0=R^((a+1)R)/(R^(a+1)+1)^(2R), and T(signal,vacuum)=1/(R^(a+1)+1). All are rational. For the TMSV input lambda=2^(-R^2), lambda/p0=2^(-R^2+(a+1)R log2 R+o(1))->0; postselection contractivity then bounds the actual conditioned signal's half-trace distance to tau_t by lambda/(p0-lambda).
