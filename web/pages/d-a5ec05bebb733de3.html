# Quantitative unknown-baseline dyadic-stage classification

6 October2026. Root is composing a separate unknown-scale theorem with r17's staged protocol. This note audits only the load-bearing classification margins and the selected final-bracket nuisance budget. Complete stopped-stage tail/cost analysis is owned by root/r17; no ungated global theorem is asserted here.

Use all constants from unknown_baseline_rare_edge.md. At stage beta (starting at beta=1/2 and halving), t=kappa beta^(1/(L-1)). Uniform P/t calibration needs only the global bound b<=1, not a local bracket. Counts are fixed in advance at that stage and each fresh row block obeys the same conditional concentration bound from arbitrary prior retained history.

Define r_A(c)=[exp(t(A+cB))]uv, and decide to stop when the fresh rare endpoint frequency phat exceeds the acquired threshold r_Ahat(1.5beta). On successful ordinary calibration, the support-sensitive bound at the supplied threshold rate c=1.5beta gives

    |r_Ahat(1.5beta)-r_A(1.5beta)|
      <=Delta[Astar t^L+3D beta t²]
      <=Delta(Astar+3D) beta t.

Thus Delta<=1/[64(Astar+3D)] makes the threshold error at most beta t/64. This comparison never evaluates an unknown b in the algorithm; the true-baseline threshold is only a proof object.

For every c in[0,1], the already-proved monotonicity r_A'(c)>=t/4 holds at the chosen lag. If b<=beta, the acquired threshold minus the true mean is at least

    (1.5beta-b)t/4-beta t/64>=7beta t/64.

The true mean is at most bt+(Rt)^L/L!<=2beta t. Hence Bernstein bounds false stop by exp(-c0 m beta t) for a supplied constant c0>0.

If b>=2beta, the true mean minus the acquired threshold is at least

    (b-1.5beta)t/4-beta t/64
       >=bt/16-bt/128=7bt/128.

Its mean is at most bt+beta t<=1.5bt. Hence Bernstein bounds false continuation by exp(-c1 m bt). A failed calibration adds its conditional failure allocation delta_k. Taking m proportional to log(1/delta_k)/(beta t) gives the desired low-b classification and an exponentially stronger high-b classification. The strong high-b exponent does not by itself remove the additive calibration failure probability from the late-stage tail; root/r17 must retain that term in their expectation analysis.

On simultaneous good classification events, stopping at beta implies b>beta. If a previous stage exists, its beta was2beta and continuation implies b<4beta. At the first stage, the supplied global b<=1 already supplies an upper bound. Therefore the selected final bracket is [beta,min(4beta,1)]. No stationary initial law or unknown rate is supplied to the clock.

For this wider final bracket, nuisance error is bounded by

    Delta(Astar+8D) beta t.

Choosing Delta<=e/[8(Astar+8D)] gives e beta t/8. The true rare endpoint mean is at most5beta t; with rare frequency threshold e beta t/8, Bernstein's sufficient coefficient is640+(16/3)e, so650 log(6/delta)/(e² beta t) samples is safe for e<=1. The same acquired-model inversion and no-reset charge apply. A fresh final calibration block avoids any need to infer parameter-conditioned independence at the selected stopping history; the stage-conditioned concentration is already uniform.

The fixed-deadtime nuisance case remains separate. In particular, this classification lemma relies on shrinking t to control the first-order ordinary-rate calibration bias.
