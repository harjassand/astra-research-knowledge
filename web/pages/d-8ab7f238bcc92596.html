# Prior-art boundary: all-dimensional endotactic permanence

Checked 2026-10-08 against primary papers. This is a focused theorem-scope audit, not a comprehensive priority search. The target being compared is the N33 card at `work/agents/systems_metabolism/reaction_graphs/sources/cards/N33-endotactic-permanence.txt`.

## Theorems and exact scope

**Two species, all endotactic (Craciun–Nazarov–Pantea).** In *Persistence and permanence of mass-action and power-law dynamical systems*, Theorem 19 in arXiv v2 (published as Theorem 6.4) states that every two-species endotactic κ-variable reaction system is permanent. Here “two species” is ambient dimension two; permanence is on each stoichiometric compatibility class, with a compact positive set eventually entered by every positive solution. The paper defines κ(t) to be piecewise differentiable and uniformly bounded above and away from zero: κ(t) ∈ (η,1/η)^R. It states the extended permanence conjecture for any number of species and presents the two-species theorem as its proved case. Do not cite this theorem, as written, for merely measurable controls without separately checking an a.e./Carathéodory extension.

Primary sources: [arXiv:1010.3050](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.3050) (definitions at §2.2 and permanence at §2.3; two-species result, Theorem 19), [published SIAM article](https://doi.org/10.1137/100812355).

**Two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace, broader ambient dimension (Pantea).** Theorem 5.1 of *On the persistence and global stability of mass-action systems* proves persistence for κ-variable systems with bounded trajectories, two-dimensional compatibility classes, and lower-endotactic stoichiometric subnetworks. The rates vary in compact positive intervals, with the paper's κ convention. This is a persistence theorem conditional on trajectory boundedness, not a classwise permanence theorem. It does not settle N33's all-dimensional claim.

Primary sources: [arXiv:1103.0603](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.0603), Theorem 5.1 and rate definition 6; [published SIAM article](https://doi.org/10.1137/110840509).

**All dimensions, strongly endotactic (Gopalkrishnan–Miller–Shiu).** Theorem 1.1 of *A geometric approach to the Global Attractor Conjecture* proves permanence for every strongly endotactic network in the mass-action differential-inclusion model. Its tempering assigns each reaction an arbitrary nonempty compact interval contained in (0,∞); the inclusion fiber independently permits each reaction's coefficient to take any value in its interval. Thus the theorem is robust to arbitrary time variation of bounded, uniformly positive rates (under the standard a.e. solution interpretation of differential inclusions), in every finite dimension. It is stronger than a fixed-rate theorem and is direct prior art for the time-varying part of N33 on this subclass.

The exact class boundary matters: their Definition 3.14 defines strong endotacticity as endotacticity plus, for every w not orthogonal to the stoichiometric subspace, at least one reaction from a w-maximal reactant whose flux points strictly inward. This extra “strong” condition is absent from N33. They explicitly exhibit endotactic-but-not-strongly-endotactic networks (Example 3.17), and a 3D weakly reversible, non-strongly-endotactic network in Example 9.6; the paper says persistence/permanence for that example remained open there. Their Definitions 3.8 and 3.23 give the positive compact per-reaction rate intervals and full interval-valued differential inclusion. The paper allows arbitrary real complexes (Remark 3.3), so real/negative exponents alone do not distinguish N33 from this prior theorem on the strongly endotactic subfamily.

All-dimensional theorem: [arXiv:1305.5303](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.5303), Definitions 3.8, 3.14, 3.23; Theorems 1.1 and 1.3. Published as [SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst. 13 (2014), 758–797](https://doi.org/10.1137/130928170). For weak reversibility, Corollary 3.20 shows every one-linkage-class network is strongly endotactic; Corollary 3.19 covers weakly reversible networks whose linkage classes all have the full network's stoichiometric subspace. Hence these classes inherit all-dimensional interval-rate permanence. General weak reversibility alone does not imply strong endotacticity.

**Nearby but not a substitute.** Pantea's 3D Global Attractor result is for complex-balanced mass-action systems, not all endotactic networks with independently varying rates. Brunner–Craciun's later robust permanence paper treats a planar tropically endotactic polynomial/power-law class with possibly time-dependent parameters; it does not supply the general all-dimensional endotactic theorem. A 2025 revision of Xu's first-order endotactic paper concerns first-order linear systems and selected asymptotically autonomous examples, not arbitrary all-dimensional time-varying rate boxes. Sources: [Pantea](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.0603), [Brunner–Craciun](https://doi.org/10.1137/17M1133762), [Xu v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01598).

## Novelty boundary for N33

The all-endotactic, any-dimension, bounded-positive-rate permanence statement is exactly the Extended Permanence Conjecture stated by Craciun–Nazarov–Pantea; Gopalkrishnan–Miller–Shiu prove it only for the strongly endotactic subclass. If N33's proof is valid, it would close that conjecture for its stated actual-diagram class and extend the established all-dimensional time-varying theorem to endotactic networks that are not strongly endotactic. The 2-species theorem is already established, and full-dimensional strongly endotactic/selected weakly reversible cases are already established.

N33 also states stronger/formulation-specific features that are not asserted in these cited theorems: arbitrary measurable rate paths in an explicit common box; one compact **convex**, positively contained, forward-invariant common absorber for each positive stoichiometric class; and an entry-time bound independent of the chosen rate path (but depending on the initial point/class). Existing permanence is a common eventual compact containment per fixed class and tempering; it should not be described as proving N33's convexity or rate-path-independent entry bound absent a direct theorem comparison. The CNP paper writes “piecewise differentiable”; the GMS paper gives full interval fibers and a differential-inclusion trajectory definition, but its displayed definition says “differentiable curve.” For exact priority wording on merely measurable rates, reconcile these solution conventions rather than silently equating them.

## Next decisive source/priority question

Has any post-2014 primary paper proved the Extended Permanence Conjecture for all endotactic networks under per-reaction compact positive rate intervals (or an equivalent all-dimensional, non-strong-endotactic class)? The 2014 computational paper still calls the general case conjectured, and the checked 2025 first-order paper is narrower, but that is not a complete citation-chain audit. Search forward citations and author bibliographies from CNP/GMS, especially post-2014 work on variable-κ endotactic systems; then ask a domain expert or author whether the conjecture is regarded as open and whether measurable-rate and classwise uniformity strengthen the classical statement.

Primary status indicators checked: [Johnston–Pantea–Donnell, arXiv:1412.4662](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.4662) (abstract says general endotactic permanence remains conjectured); [Xu, arXiv:2409.01598v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01598) (first-order focus). These support a provisional boundary, not a definitive priority claim.
