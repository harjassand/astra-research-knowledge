CYCLE 5 EXTENSION — MIXED-COFACTOR TOGGLE NETWORKS
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE under the exact rate inequality below;
finite checker is FINITE-EVIDENCE only. External validation and priority
remain UNKNOWN.

1. EXACT CLASS
--------------
Fix finite r>=1 catalyst species C_1,...,C_r and one species each A,B. Let
there be a finite nonempty list of nonzero multiindices nu_j in N_0^r,
j=1,...,m. Put phi_j(c)=product_i (c_i)_(nu_ji), where falling factorials
are zero below their order. Include positive stochastic mass-action pairs

  0 <-> A                         alpha, delta a
  0 <-> C_i                       gamma_i, mu_i c_i
  A <-> A+B                       p a, q a b
  A+nu_j.C <-> nu_j.C             eta_j a phi_j(c), zeta_j phi_j(c).

Every pair is reversible, hence the network is weakly reversible. The last
pair removes/adds only A, preserving B and all C counts. No restriction is
placed on overlap among the nu_j supports, their orders, or linkage count.
All rates are fixed positive finite numbers.

Define

  Q(c)=sum_j zeta_j phi_j(c),       E(c)=sum_j eta_j phi_j(c),
  rho=min_j eta_j/zeta_j,           Lambda=max_j eta_j/zeta_j,
  M_i=max_j nu_ji (zero if species i occurs in no cofactor),
  h(0)=0, h(1)=1, h(M)=M+M^2/4 for M>=2,
  H0=sum_i mu_i h(M_i),             kappa=min(delta,rho)-H0.

Assume the explicit strict condition

                         kappa>0.                                      (1)

For any positive weights w_i and K>=1, set x=1+Q, u=1/x,
g(a,Q)=x/(x+a), W=R^b, and

  V(a,c,b)=1+sum_i w_i c_i+a u(Q)
                   +W[1+epsilon g(a,Q)]
                   +K 1_{a+sum_i c_i=0}.                              (2)

There are rational epsilon>0 and R>1 for rational input rates, and a finite
integer N, such that V is norm-like and LV<=C-cV for finite C,c>0. The
minimal CTMC is nonexplosive in physical time and positive recurrent on its
irreducible state space.

2. LOAD-BEARING MIXED-ORDER BOUND
--------------------------------
For c_i>0 put Q_i=Q(c-e_i), Delta_i=Q-Q_i>=0, and

  H(c)=sum_i mu_i c_i Delta_i/[(1+Q_i)(1+Q)].

Then, for every c, H(c)<=H0. To see this fix i and let M=M_i. If c_i>M,
every active monomial satisfies
  phi_j(c-e_i)/phi_j(c)=(c_i-nu_ji)/c_i,
so Delta_i<=M Q_i/(c_i-M). Since Q>=Q_i,
  c_i Delta_i/[(1+Q_i)(1+Q)]
      <=[c_i M/(c_i-M)] Q_i/(1+Q_i)^2
      <=M(M+1)/4.
If 1<=c_i<=M, split Delta_i into terms with nu_ji<c_i and nu_ji=c_i.
The former sum is at most M Q_i because c_i-nu_ji>=1; the latter is at
most Delta_i. Their normalized contributions are at most c_i M/4<=M^2/4
and c_i<=M, respectively. If M=1, the low case has no safe term
(nu_ji<c_i=1 forces nu_ji=0), so its total is at most 1; the high case is
at most 1/2. If M>=2, M^2/4+M dominates M(M+1)/4. Thus the i summand
without mu_i is at most h(M); summing proves the bound. If M_i=0 its
summand vanishes.

This bound retains the potentially nonzero limiting cost of a death jump
that disables a mixed cofactor at a threshold. It does not assume H tends
to zero along every escaping ray.

3. EXACT GENERATOR CONTROL
--------------------------
The a u(Q) contribution is exactly

  u[alpha+Q-(delta+E)a] + a H
     + a sum_i gamma_i[u(Q(c+e_i))-u(Q)].

The immigration sum is nonpositive. Since E>=rho Q,
  (delta+E)/(1+Q)>=min(delta,rho),
and (1) gives

  L(a u(Q)) <= max(alpha,1)-kappa a.                                 (3)

The correction g is designed to have g(0,Q)=1 independently of Q while
its one-step change in a is order 1/Q at large Q. Write x=1+Q. For a>=1,

  g(a-1,Q)-g(a,Q)=x/[(x+a-1)(x+a)]=:D_a.

For toggles and A death, the positive part of L(W g), divided by W, is at
most

  delta a D_a + a E D_a + Gamma,
  Gamma=sum_i gamma_i.

Here C immigrations can increase g by at most one each, C deaths decrease
it, and A immigration and reverse toggles increase a and decrease g.
The elementary bounds x+a-1>=(x+a)/2, 2ax/(x+a)^2<=1/2, and E<=Lambda Q
give

  delta a D_a <= delta/2,
  a E D_a <= 2 Lambda a.                                               (4)

At a=0, C jumps leave g unchanged, while A immigration and reverse toggles
have total rate alpha+Q and change g by
  g(1,Q)-g(0,Q)=-1/(2+Q).
Thus

  L[epsilon W g](0,c,b)
       =-epsilon W (alpha+Q)/(2+Q)
       <=-epsilon tau W,   tau=min(alpha/2,1)>0.                       (5)

This continuous-in-a corrector avoids the mixed-threshold death hazard in
the inactive W term. Its active toggle gain (4) is only O(a W), which the
substrate consumption term can absorb for large b.

The A<->A+B contribution to W[1+epsilon g] is
  a[1+epsilon g(a,Q)]G_R(b),
  G_R(n)=[p(R-1)-q(1-R^{-1})n]R^n.
Let g_+(R)=sup_{n>=0} max(G_R(n),0); for rational R this is an exact finite
maximum, and g_+(R)->0 as R down to one.

Let C1=delta/2+2 Lambda+Gamma and cB=epsilon tau/2. Choose N>=1 so

  p(R-1)<=q(1-R^{-1})N/2,
  q(1-R^{-1})N/2>=epsilon C1+cB,
  (1+epsilon)g_+(R)+2epsilon Lambda R^(N-1)<=kappa/2.                 (6)

Such a triple is effective for rational rates: enumerate epsilon=1/k and
R=1+1/k^2, form the least N satisfying the first two rational inequalities,
and test the third. As k->infinity, N=O(k), R^(N-1)->1, and g_+(R)->0,
so the search terminates.

For a>=1,b>=N, (6) gives

  a[1+epsilon g]G_R(b)
       +epsilon W(delta/2+2Lambda a+Gamma)
       <=-cB W.                                                       (7)

For a>=1,b<N, W<=R^(N-1); (6) and (4) leave at most
  -kappa a/2 +epsilon R^(N-1)(delta/2+Gamma)
from the A-weight and W terms. For a=0, (5) supplies -epsilon tau W.
The weighted C immigration/death term is
  sum_i w_i gamma_i-sum_i w_i mu_i c_i
       <=sum_i w_i gamma_i-(min_i mu_i)sum_i w_i c_i.

The boundary indicator has positive generator part at most
K max(delta,max_i mu_i). At the origin an A immigration jump has full V
increment 1-K-epsilon W/2<=0; a C_i immigration jump has increment
w_i-K, whose positive part contributes at most gamma_i(w_i-K)^+.
Combining these estimates, and adding cB R^(N-1) to the constant on the
low-b region, gives

  LV<=C0-(kappa/2)a-(min_i mu_i)sum_i w_i c_i-cB R^b.                 (8)

Since V<=1+sum_i w_i c_i+a+(1+epsilon)R^b+K and is norm-like, (8) implies
LV<=C-cV. The chain is irreducible: immigration/death adjusts A and every
C_i, and while A>0 the A<->A+B pair adjusts B in either direction. The
localized stopped-Dynkin argument on finite V sublevels proves physical-time
nonexplosion. The countable-state Foster criterion then gives positive
recurrence.

4. RATIONAL NON-COMPLEX-BALANCED FIXTURE
----------------------------------------
Take two co-catalysts C,D and toggles

  A+2C+D <-> 2C+D       eta_1=zeta_1=1,
  A+C+2D <-> C+2D       eta_2=2, zeta_2=1.

Use alpha=2, delta=p=q=1, gamma_C=gamma_D=1,
mu_C=mu_D=1/100, w_C=w_D=1, epsilon=1/100, R=11/10, K=1.
Then

  Q=(C)_2 D+C(D)_2,       E=(C)_2 D+2C(D)_2,
  rho=1, Lambda=2, M_C=M_D=2, H0=3/50, kappa=47/50,
  tau=1, C1=13/2, N=3, g_+(11/10)=1/10.

The low-b inequality is exactly
  (101/100)(1/10)+(2/100)(2)(11/10)^2=747/5000 < 47/100=kappa/2.
The fixture therefore meets every analytic hypothesis. It is not complex
balanced: the isolated reversible edge at complex A+2C+D requires activity
a_A=zeta_1/eta_1=1, while the edge at A+C+2D requires a_A=1/2. No positive
activity vector can satisfy both. The full network has four species and
multiple linkage classes.

The exact checker `verify_mixed_fixture.py` compares direct channel sums to
the closed generator on 7,200 states, checks the universal H bound and all
rational inequalities, and verifies LV<=7-V/250 on its finite grid. The same
explicit constants follow analytically for this fixture by combining (8)
with C0<6, the drift coefficients 22/25, 1/100, and 1/200, and
V<=2+a+C+D+(101/100)R^b. This finite replay is diagnostic evidence only;
the all-state proof is the analytic derivation above.

5. SOURCE BOUNDARY AND LIMITS
-----------------------------
This is a fixed-rate theorem for the displayed grammar under (1), not an
all-rate theorem for weakly reversible networks. It uses independent
immigration/death for each co-catalyst, toggles that preserve all C and B
counts, and one A-controlled substrate B. It does not cover conversions,
multiple substrates, general competing reaction graphs, or PDE systems.
No ODE permanence implication is used.

The bounded primary comparison is documented in `LITERATURE_AUDIT.txt`. In
addition to the usual binary single-linkage and rank-one restrictions, the
fixture fails the general Anderson--Kim tier sufficient condition on the
explicit sequence (A,B,C,D)=(0,n,n,2), and fails the higher-order pure-drain
hypotheses in the stochastic strongly endotactic theorem. These are failures
of sufficient criteria only. Product-Poisson and nonexplosion theorems for
complex-balanced systems cover only their exact complex-balanced parameter
slice. An author/project page now states that the two-species all-WR
recurrence conjecture has a complete proof and summarizes boundary
coarse-graining plus a patched chemical Lyapunov strategy; its publication
list still places the manuscript under “In preparation.” The conference
abstract makes the same 2D theorem claim, but no full proof manuscript was
inspected. Treat this as source-reported, not as a proof reconstructed here.
The present example has four species. This bounded source search establishes
noncoverage of these listed results, not novelty or absence of other
literature.
