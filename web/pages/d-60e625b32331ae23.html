In an energy basis in increasing order, E=T* T with upper Cholesky T, B=T^{-1}, A=B*B. Triangularity implies both T^tensor n and B^tensor n preserve EVERY cumulative energy cutoff. Their restrictions there are inverse. Therefore
  p_n(Q)=lambda_min(P E^tensor n P|Ran P)
        =||B^tensor n P||^{-2}.
For any legal unit y, chi=B^tensor n y/||B^tensor n y|| is legal and has miss ||B^tensor n y||^{-2}. This exact inverse identity is the decisive proof step; no discarded energy tail is ignored.

Put C_s=e^{-sH/2} A e^{-sH/2}, phi(s)=log||C_s||, s_H=inf{s>=0:A<=e^{sH}}, and
  g(c)=inf_{s>=0}[phi(s)+sc].
Tensor order gives F_n(c)=-log p_n(nc)<=n g(c). The tilt norm is log-convex, has subgradient equal to minus the energy interval on its top eigenspace, and vanishes for s>=s_H. A top vector can therefore be selected with mean energy c' at an interior optimum, or at most c' at s=0.

This yields the supplied simultaneous bound
  n g(c)-2s_H K-D log(n+1)-C_D <= F_n(c)<=n g(c),
  C_D=(D-1)+D(D-1)^2.

DETERMINISTIC COMPILER THEOREM — PRECISE INTERFACE

Fix D. Supply H=diag(h_0,...,h_{D-1}), 0=h_0<h_1<=... with rational entries, rational-complex E satisfying the above promises, and rational Q>=0. The ordered energy basis and its physical state/rotation interface are SUPPLIED, not learned. Let L count these matrix/budget encoding bits. For n parallel uses let
  M=binom(n+D-1,D-1),
  K_n,Q={k in Z_+^D:sum k_j=n, sum k_j h_j<=Q},
  Z_k=||B^tensor n |D_k>||^2,
where |D_k> is the normalized equal-amplitude type vector.

There is a deterministic classical algorithm to enumerate K_n,Q and approximate its scores. There is a unitary preparation circuit, with digital rotation approximations but EXACT hard support, producing miss at most 2/max_k Z_k. Thus at Q=nc,
  -log miss >= n g(c)-2s_H K-D log(n+1)-C_D-log 2.
The optimizing type, its amplitudes, and a controlled-count preparation program are acquired by the algorithm. No optimizing spectral vector, successful physical filter, or tiny preparation probability is supplied as an oracle.

PROOF AND ALGORITHM

(a) Compute occupation amplitudes directly. If k!=prod_j k_j! and ell!=prod_a ell_a!, define
  F_k(z)=prod_j (sum_{a<=j} B_aj z_a)^{k_j}.
Then the exact symmetric-tensor identity is
  B^tensor n |D_k> = sum_{|ell|=n} c_ell |D_ell>,
  c_ell=sqrt(ell!/k!) [z^ell]F_k(z).
It follows by expanding creation operators, or by counting the equal amplitudes of each word. Repeated multiplication by linear forms computes all coefficients with O(D n M) arithmetic operations. Repeat for <=M candidate types: O(D n M^2) arithmetic operations. Rational energy tests decide legality exactly.

(b) Triangularity makes every reachable output occupation legal whenever k is legal. One can enforce the combinatorial support explicitly: for every suffix j, sum_{a>=j} ell_a<=sum_{a>=j}k_a. Because energies are ordered, this implies sum ell_a h_a<=sum k_a h_a. Structural triangular zeros and illegal occupations are kept EXACTLY zero throughout classical approximation and the quantum circuit. Extra physical leakage from imperfect gates is outside this ideal controlled-rotation model.

(c) Prepare the normalized occupation vector sum_ell c_ell/ sqrt(Z_k) |ell> on O(D log(n+1)) counter qubits. A finite binary tree of conditional two-level rotations and phases loads this table. Then, for m=n,n-1,...,1, prepare an output category a with amplitude sqrt(ell_a/m), coherently decrease that counter, and continue. The product along a word is sqrt(prod ell_a!/n!), independent of order; thus |ell> maps to |D_ell>. All counters finish at zero. Categories with zero remaining count are omitted; deterministic branches use exact identity/permutation gates. Angle rounding therefore changes amplitudes but cannot create a wrong occupation or violate Q. Preparing the count table only on its legal support has the same property. Approximate two-level rotations act inside their selected subspace; exact classical controls and exact inverses preserve inactive branches.

(d) Precision is charged. E<=I implies the least singular value of B is >=1, so Z_k>=1. Meanwhile Z_k<=||A||^n. For fixed D, rational positive E has log||B||=poly_D(L), and its Cholesky radicals are computable with polynomial bit effort. Polynomial coefficient computation can be carried out to absolute vector error delta using
  b=O_D(n poly_D(L)+n log(n+1)+log(1/delta))
precision bits; this overcounts intermediate factorial/combinatorial growth. Cancellation is handled by absolute, rather than relative, coefficient precision. Approximate scores to a fixed multiplicative factor and choose a factor-close winner. Recompute its coefficients more accurately for loading. If G controlled rotations are used, choose rotation error O(delta/G). A telescoping operator-norm bound gives final trace distance O(delta). Taking delta<=1/(100 Z_k) makes the additive miss error a constant fraction of 1/Z_k. Since log Z_k<=n log||A||, exponentially small miss needs O(n poly_D(L)) precision bits, not exponentially many bits or gates. Constants can be adjusted to the displayed factor 2.

(e) Conservative resource bounds: O(D n M^2) classical algebraic arithmetic operations at polynomial precision; O(D n M poly(b,log n)) elementary gates using explicit multiplexed count rotations; n input qudits plus O(D log n) control/work qubits if rotation tables are precompiled. These are polynomial in n,L,log(1/delta) for fixed D, but have dimension-dependent exponents. This is NOT efficient uniformly in D. Free-use search enumerates n<=N=floor(Q/h_min), so the resource bound is polynomial in the numerical N, not in log Q or log(1/h_min). For a fixed nontrivial (E,H,D), optimal Q and n are O(log(1/epsilon)); the compiler cost is polynomial in log(1/epsilon).

This removes probabilistic preparation attempts. It does not charge an energy-conserving battery, coherent reference, analog control noise, driving work, channel acquisition, or receiver engineering. The compiler does charge input registers, scratch registers, table size, classical arithmetic, gates, and exponentially relevant digital precision. There is no theorem about total thermodynamic advantage. A matched separable comparator also knows E,H and has its best cutoff/product preparations available.

  W_{r,i}=2^{r-i} sum_{t=max(0,2i-r)}^i binom(i,t)binom(r-i,i-t)3^t.
Choose the best W over ceil(Q/2)<=r<=Q, i=Q-r. Load the i+1 count amplitudes, apply the exact parity phase (-1)^j, and coherently expand each count using sqrt(j/m) rotations and controlled decrement. There are O(r^2) simple controlled rotation-table entries. Zero/forced branches are exact, so the channel input support stays <=r+i under angle rounding. It is enough to specify b>=log_2 W+log_2 G+O(1) rotation precision to preserve a constant multiple of 1/W.

The generating function sum_Q sum_{r+i=Q}W_{r,i}u^Q = 1/[(1-u)(1-u-4u^2)] proves the optimal exponent s_H without optimization or a spectral solver. GENERAL NULL SUPPORT / PARALLEL IDLER LEMMA

Let the null be R_sigma(rho)=Tr(rho) sigma, with S=supp sigma and projector Pi_S. Let Phi(|0><0|)=sigma and E=Phi*(Pi_S)>0. With arbitrary parallel input rho_AR, the null output is sigma^tensor n tensor rho_R, whose support projector is Pi_S^tensor n tensor Pi_{rho_R}. Zero false alarm forces the null-acceptance effect to be identity on this support. The alternative output has reference support contained in supp rho_R. Its minimum miss therefore equals
  Tr[(Pi_S^tensor n tensor Pi_{rho_R})(Phi^tensor n tensor id)(rho_AR)]
  =Tr rho_A E^tensor n.
The projector test attains equality. A retained idler cannot improve the input-marginal optimization. Every hard/expected theorem for E consequently transfers unchanged. If sigma is faithful, Pi_S=I and E=I, so every miss is one. A ground/null family not equal to a replacer requires another interface.

FINITE FALSE ALARM: SAME-E COUNTEREXAMPLE

Accept-null effect is M; false alarm is 1-Tr(null M), miss is Tr(alternative M).

Take spectral E=sum_j e_j |e_j><e_j| including |0>, e_0=1. Choose two orthogonal output basis states v,u.
  Phi_bin(rho)=Tr(E rho)|v><v|+Tr((I-E)rho)|u><u|.
  Phi_coh(rho)=sum_j <e_j|rho|e_j> |psi_j><psi_j|,
  |psi_j>=sqrt(e_j)|v>+sqrt(1-e_j)|u>.
Both are CPTP, both send |0> to |v>, and both satisfy Phi*(|v><v|)=E. Their zero-false-alarm hard/expected rates coincide exactly.

For Phi_bin with legal input marginal, the all-vacuum reference block is
  tau_R=Tr_A[(E^tensor n tensor I)rho_AR].
Since P E^tensor n P>=p_n(Q)P, tau_R>=p_n(Q)rho_R. Null acceptance >=1-delta implies miss >=p_n(Q)(1-delta); other classical output blocks contribute nonnegatively. An optimal no-idler input and M=(1-delta)|v^n><v^n| attain it. Thus even arbitrary idlers cannot change the hard exponent.

For Phi_coh choose an E eigenvector w with eigenvalue e<1 and positive cost. Its input support is below h_max. Send w^tensor n: alternative output is pure psi^tensor n. M=I-|psi^n><psi^n| gives MISS ZERO and false alarm e^n. For any delta>0, n=ceil(log(delta)/log(e)) and hard ceiling Q=n h_max suffice. Every finite delta therefore allows zero miss at finite energy, while Phi_bin requires Q growing logarithmically as miss approaches zero. This rules out any full finite-FA rate formula from E,H alone.

For the supplied qutrit use e=1/4, n=3, Q=6, delta=1/64. Phi_coh has zero miss. Phi_bin has miss >=(63/64)e^{-6s_H}=0.003484501768... . The construction uses supplied channel knowledge; it is not a calibration-robust claim.

EXPECTED BUDGET WITH ONE RETAINED FLAG

For Phi_bin permit averaged mean input energy Q, arbitrary finite parallel uses, and a retained classical preparation flag. Put t=1-delta. For any idler-assisted test, let M_R be its all-vacuum block and tau_A the positive input substate obtained by applying sqrt(M_R) on R. Then Tr tau_A=t'>=t and Tr H_n tau_A<=Q. Scalar Jensen and L_n<=alpha H_n give
  miss >=Tr E^tensor n tau_A >=t' exp(-alpha Q/t') >=t exp(-alpha Q/t).
To attain its exponent, with probability delta prepare vacuum and reject the null solely using the retained flag; with probability t use an N18-optimal high-cost pulse of mean Q/t and its zero-FA test. Overall FA=delta, mean cost Q, and miss t times the high-cost miss. The optimal exponent per averaged Q is alpha/t. Only one classical bit is retained. If the controller forbids retained flags/idlers and requires a deterministic input with a scalar output test, the binary realization instead gives the factor (1-delta) times its zero-FA miss. The operational difference must be recorded, not averaged away.

Adaptive discrimination was explored, but no unrestricted adaptive s_H collapse was proved. Generic coherent feedback needs the channel beyond E and an explicit path/battery budget.
