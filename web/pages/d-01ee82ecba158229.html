1. Contract and theorem
Fix 0<alpha<1. The model class consists of finite irreducible reciprocal-support CTMCs, initialized at their actual stationary distribution, with arbitrary hidden state count and fixed visible alphabet. A deterministic observation map may duplicate symbols. It is enough that the class include the extensions below. The target is the dimensionless stationary entropy-production rate
  sigma(Q,rho)=sum_{i!=j} rho_i q_ij log[(rho_i q_ij)/(rho_j q_ji)].
Absent edges are omitted. Rates and entropy use physical elapsed time. This is the identity-state-reversal convention; physical odd variables require a separately specified involution and target.

Let L_T be any measurable function of the observed record on [0,T], with optional independent algorithm randomization. Uniform honesty means
  P_M(L_T<=sigma_M)>=1-alpha
for every admissible model M and every finite T at which the procedure is used. Then for every base A with sigma_A>0 and every c in (0,1),
  P_A(L_T>=c*sigma_A)<=min(1,alpha/c).                  (B1)
If the data are n independent stationary records of finite lengths T_1,...,T_n, replace the right-hand side by min(1,alpha/c^n).

2. Minimal one-extra-state proof
Let pi be A's stationary law, choose a base state a, and adjoin b with h(b)=h(a). For p in (0,1) and epsilon>0 leave all old off-diagonal rates unchanged and set
  q_ab=epsilon*(1-p)/(p*pi_a), q_ba=epsilon.
All other new off-diagonal rates are zero; diagonals make rows sum to zero. Put rho_i=p*pi_i on old states and rho_b=1-p. Internal balances are scaled old balances; the new bridge is detailed-balanced with equal directional flux epsilon*(1-p). Hence rho is stationary. The chain is finite, irreducible, and reciprocal-support, with just one additional hidden state.

Every old entropy term is multiplied by p, while the bridge's term is zero. Thus sigma_new=p*sigma_A exactly. Conditional on its stationary initial state lying in A, the initial law is pi. If O_T is the time spent at a by a base path and r=epsilon*(1-p)/(p*pi_a), the joint probability of an observed event E and remaining in A throughout the record is
  p E_A[1_E exp(-r O_T)] >= p exp(-rT) P_A(E).         (B2)
This is the ordinary CTMC path density with an additional killing clock; it holds for the complete visible jump record and all measurable coarsenings.

Choose p<c and E={L_T>=c*sigma_A}. E is a coverage failure in the extension because sigma_new=p*sigma_A<c*sigma_A. Therefore alpha>=p exp(-rT)P_A(E). First send epsilon down to zero with p fixed, then p up to c. This proves (B1). Only positive epsilon chains are used; the reducible limiting chain is not assumed admissible. For n independent stationary starts, use the joint event that all n starts lie in A and no record crosses the bridge. The domination coefficient becomes p^n exp[-r sum_k T_k], proving alpha/c^n.

3. Strengthening: preserve the exact visible stationary marginal
Add a dormant copy b_i of every base state i. The new state set is S x {active,dormant}, with h(i,active)=h(i,dormant)=h(i). Keep Q_A in the active layer, put no transitions within the dormant layer, and set
  q[(i,active),(i,dormant)]=r=epsilon*(1-p)/p,
  q[(i,dormant),(i,active)]=epsilon.
The stationary law is rho(i,active)=p*pi_i, rho(i,dormant)=(1-p)*pi_i. Every bridge has directional flux epsilon*(1-p)*pi_i in both directions. Internal active balances remain p times the base balances. Thus stationarity is exact, and sigma_new=p*sigma_A exactly.

Irreducibility holds because every dormant copy is linked to its active state and the active layer is irreducible. Every positive edge has a positive reverse edge. The hidden count is 2|S|, an overhead of exactly |S| states and |S| reciprocal bridge pairs; the observation alphabet is unchanged. The stationary visible marginal is
  sum_{i:h(i)=z} [rho(i,active)+rho(i,dormant)]
  =sum_{i:h(i)=z} pi_i,
which equals A's marginal for every visible symbol z. Consequently the theorem still holds if that exact visible marginal is supplied as extra information.

Active-to-dormant exit rate is constant r, independent of the active base state. Thus the no-bridge event produces the EXACT submeasure
  P_new(E, initially active, no bridge by T)
  =p exp(-rT)P_A(E).                                  (B3)
The preceding coverage proof applies verbatim. The macrosector itself is a two-state CTMC with active exit r and dormant exit epsilon; its nonzero eigenvalue is -(r+epsilon)=-epsilon/p. The family therefore contains arbitrarily slow mixing despite exact stationarity and a fixed hidden-count overhead.

4. Strengthening: there need be no entropy loss from observation
Suppose the base observation distinguishes every base state, h(i)=i, and the added copies duplicate these labels. For a stationary microscopic path, let R reverse the state sequence and reflect all interior jump times about T. Holding-time factors cancel. Its full path log likelihood ratio equals
  log[rho(X_0)/rho(X_T)]+sum_jumps log[q_forward/q_backward]
  =sum_jumps log[(rho_i q_ij)/(rho_j q_ji)].             (B4)
The equality telescopes the rho terms. Every bridge term in (B4) is zero, and each active-layer jump i->j contributes log[(pi_i q_ij)/(pi_j q_ji)]. Dormant states have no internal jumps. Consequently (B4) is the observable functional
  F(Z)=sum_VISIBLE_jumps_i_to_j log[(pi_i q_ij)/(pi_j q_ji)].
It is not a functional the estimator knows numerically; it is a measurability statement about the true laws.

Let P_full and Q_full=R_*P_full be the full path laws. Their RN derivative is exp(F(Z)). Because this derivative is measurable with respect to Z, pushing both measures through the observation map gives dP_Z/d(R_*P_Z)=exp(F(Z)). Therefore
  D(P_Z||R_*P_Z)=D(P_full||R_*P_full)=T*p*sigma_A.       (B5)
This applies to both the one-trap and dormant-copy extensions. Thus the obstruction can concern the actual observed path irreversibility rate as well as microscopic EP; it is not forced by loss of irreversible information under coarse observation. Further lossy coarsening need not preserve equality.

5. Quantifier audit and consequences
Inclusive threshold: choose p<c, so {L>=c sigma_A} implies strict violation L>p sigma_A. Taking p up to c after epsilon down to zero establishes the inclusive >= statement with no atom issue.

Fixed finite T: the alternative may depend on T. This is permitted by uniform coverage over the model class, and is exactly why ergodicity of each fixed alternative does not help. For alpha fixed and c>alpha, (B1) precludes L_T converging in probability to sigma_A as T grows. A plug-in estimator can still be pointwise consistent; what fails is finite-time uniform honesty together with that consistency.

Anytime: under simultaneous coverage, apply the domination to the event that any bound on a finite observed prefix exceeds c sigma_A. Then let the prefix length grow. This proves the alpha/c ceiling for ever crossing the threshold at any finite member of a countable schedule. A continuously indexed procedure requires the ordinary adaptedness and measurability of the supremum event. Arbitrary unbounded stopping can be handled by intersecting its event with {stopping time<=H}, using domination through H, then H up to infinity, provided the corresponding coverage contract applies to that stopping procedure under every alternative.

Replicates: genuine independent hidden stationary starts contribute p^n. A collection of marginally stationary but dependent segments from one finite trajectory contributes the single-sector p construction. Splitting one record or allowing finite gaps does not manufacture independent starts.

Useful must be quantified: (B1) does not say all honest lower bounds are zero. It forbids reliable certification of a substantial fraction of the rate. For replicate power beta>alpha at fraction c, n>=ceil(log(beta/alpha)/[-log c]) is necessary, not sufficient. Known full hidden topology/count excluding extensions, externally certified mixing, interventions, or a known generator can change the statistical experiment.

6. Independent-sample bounded-critic repair
For a genuinely independent stationary observed block of duration tau, with reversal-compatible observation and no odd variables, full path KL is tau*sigma, so D(P||R_*P)<=tau*sigma. Let f be fixed independently of the evaluated block, antisymmetric f(Rz)=-f(z), with |f|<=B. Define m=E_P f, z=E_P exp(-f). The ordinary variational KL inequality gives
  tau*sigma >= D(P||R_*P) >= m-log z.                  (B6)
A direct proof tilts R_*P by exp(f)/z and uses nonnegativity of KL. There is no unknown path likelihood oracle in evaluating f.

With n iid stationary blocks and r_n=sqrt(log(2/alpha)/(2n)), bounded Hoeffding gives, jointly with probability >=1-alpha,
  m>=m_hat-2B*r_n,
  z<=z_hat+(exp(B)-exp(-B))*r_n.
Thus
  L=[m_hat-2B*r_n-log(z_hat+(exp(B)-exp(-B))*r_n)]_+/tau
is an honest lower bound. Clipping the z upper bound at exp(B) is valid and may help. A finite fixed or independently trained family of M critics permits maximizing their certificates with log(2M/alpha) in r_n. Adaptive reuse of evaluation blocks does not satisfy this argument unless a separate uniform-concentration or predictable-betting contract is supplied.

Bounded antisymmetric critics are population-complete for observed KL: f*=log[dP/d(R_*P)] is antisymmetric under the reversal involution; its symmetric clipping f_B is bounded and antisymmetric. When KL is finite, E f_B tends to KL and E exp(-f_B) tends to 1 by dominated convergence (exp(-f_B)<=1+exp(-f*)). Consequently the variational objective approaches observed KL. This does not assert microscopic identifiability from coarse observations or efficient acquisition of the critic.

7. Cross-audit of the originating adaptive betting repair
The originating report uses the equivalent NWJ score G=f+1-exp(-f). Because log z<=z-1, E_P G<=D(P||R_*P)<=T*sigma. Its lower bound ell=-B+1-exp(B) is negative for B>0, and its range r=2B+exp(B)-exp(-B)=2(B+sinh B) is exact.

Under independent stationary next blocks, past-trained critics are fixed conditional on past data and E[G_i|past]<=T_i*sigma. Under a supplied uniform hidden-state TV envelope beta(g), the Markov property and conditional hidden posterior imply that the new block's conditional path law is within beta(g_i) of the stationary block law. Bounded score expectation changes by at most r_i*beta(g_i), using TV=sup_event. Therefore the stated drift bound R1 is correct. The envelope must be supplied externally or otherwise proved; an observed autocorrelation estimate is insufficient.

For s>=sigma the factor
  1-eta_i+eta_i*(G_i-ell_i)/(T_i*s+r_i*beta(g_i)-ell_i)
is nonnegative and has conditional mean <=1. Its denominator is strictly positive because T_i>0, s>=0, beta>=0, and B_i>0. Predictable eta_i in [0,1] independent of s makes every factor nonincreasing in s. Products and fixed convex mixtures are therefore nonnegative supermartingales with monotone rate dependence.
