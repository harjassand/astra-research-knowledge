For a finite two-player game G with classical question law mu, finite answer-product d, and commuting-operator value v<1, let sigma be the minimum positive resampling-chain gap over support components, with the convention1 for an entirely singleton support. Every commuting k-repetition strategy satisfies

    Pr[W_k >= ceil((v+delta)k)]
      <= exp[-sigma delta^3 k/(2048(1+log d))],

for every k>=1 and0<delta<1-v. Connected support improves2048 to1024. The statement permits arbitrary Hilbert spaces and commuting von Neumann algebras, not merely finite tensor products. The precise full proof is `notes/frontier_check_repetition/cycle19/COMMUTING_THRESHOLD_EXTENSION.md`.

Its second-resolvent-identity Jensen estimate is a norm-convergent bounded-operator argument after adding epsilon I; ||F_(T+epsilon)-F_T||<=sqrt(epsilon) permits norm passage to arbitrary spectra meeting zero. * For G_a>=0 summing to T, the form on ran sqrt(T) defines a bounded C_a with G_a=sqrt(T) C_a sqrt(T). The regularizations converge strongly to C_a in the original von Neumann algebra. The polar isometry V_T defines complete transported POVMs V_T C_a V_T* plus the complementary default outcome. Separate scalar L2 ancillas for Alice and Bob preserve ALL cross-input commutators. Applying both pullbacks gives the exact joint law, rather than only matching marginals.
* Mixed question exposure uses the original product law across repetitions. Conditional on one local input at each coordinate, the unrevealed opposite inputs are independent. Finer effects may therefore be averaged separately before postselection. The answer-mask weights alone are not probabilities; the vector norm supplies their remaining mass. The entropy potential uses a positive normal vector functional, not a density matrix or trace. Two operator Jensen telescopes cost at most2p log(d^|C|/p).
* Hilbert-valued Poincare controls variance of the common purified vectors. Their component barycenters produce a legal question-independent commuting state with a central shared flag. The exact mass loss is the variance; Cauchy–Schwarz compares success without dividing by a small barycenter norm. The disconnected case charges the drift in component frequencies via product relative entropy and Pinsker.
* The resulting conditional excess is at most sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m))+2sqrt(tau/sigma). Taking tau<=sigma delta^2/128 gives excess<delta/4. The source's purely probabilistic tested-set selection yields the displayed cubic rate. Tensoring independent copies of any fixed short strategy removes the large-k restriction; all-input commutation is preserved under those copies.

These steps do not approximate a commuting strategy by a finite-dimensional tensor strategy. They explicitly construct another legal commuting strategy, allowed to use the enlarged Hilbert space. Restriction to a fixed factor, fixed hardware or fixed representation is outside the theorem.
