Title:A solution to Crouzeix's conjecture

Authors:Emiel Lorist, Felix Schwenninger

    View PDF

            Abstract:We provide a proof of Crouzeix's conjecture, which combines the tools developed previously for weaker estimates with a simple perturbation lemma for $2$-dilations. Applying the lemma to the iterates $f^{n}$ in the double-layer potential representation yields the conjectured bound.
