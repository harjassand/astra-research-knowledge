\subsection{Related Work}

\paragraph{Uniform Matroid:} \DetMax{} is NP-hard even for uniform matroid ~\cite{Welch82}. Koutis~\cite{Koutis06} showed that there exists a constant $c>0$ such that it is NP-hard to approximate better than a factor of $(1+c)^d$~\cite{Koutis06}.  Let $k$ be the rank of the uniform matroid. Bouhtou \etal ~\cite{BouhtouGS10} gave a $\pr{\frac{n}{k}}^d$-approximation algorithm based on rounding the solution of a natural concave relaxation. Nikolov~\cite{Nikolov15} improved the result to a $e^{k}$-approximation when $k \leq d$. Wang \etal~\cite{WangYS16} improved the approximation ratio of $(1+\epsilon)^d$ when $k \geq \frac{d^2}{\epsilon}$. Allen-Zhu \etal~\cite{Allen-Zhu2017} improved the bound on $k$ to give $(1+\epsilon)^d$-approximation when $k = \Omega\pr{\frac{d}{\epsilon^2}}$. This was improved by Singh \etal~\cite{SinghX18} who gave a $(1+\epsilon)^d$-approximation when $k = \Omega\pr{\frac{d}{\epsilon} + \frac{1}{\epsilon^2} \log \frac{1}{\epsilon}}$. Recently, this was improved by Madan\etal~\cite{MadanSTU19} who gave a $(1+\epsilon)^d$-approximation when $k \geq d + \frac{d}{\epsilon}$.

\paragraph{General Matroid:} Nikolov and Singh~\cite{NikolovS16} gave a $e^d$-approximation for \DetMax{} with partition matroid of rank $d$. Let $k$ be the rank of a general matroid. Anari and Gharan~\cite{anari2017generalization} gave a $e^{2k}$-approximation when the generating polynomial for the matroid is real-stable. This corresponds to the Strongly Rayleigh matroids which includes both uniform and partition matroids. This was generalized by Anari, Gharan, and Vinzant~\cite{AnariGV18} who gave a $e^{2k}$-approximation for any matroid. While the result in~\cite{AnariGV18} is not stated for $k>d$, it can be easily deduced from the analysis. Algorithms in~\cite{NikolovS16,anari2017generalization,AnariGV18} are estimation algorithms as they estimate the optimum value up to a certain approximation factor, but do not yield a solution in polynomial time. Straszak and Vishnoi~\cite{straszak2017real} gave a polynomial time algorithm without output a $O(\sqrt{k} e^k)$-approximate solution for partition matroid.
