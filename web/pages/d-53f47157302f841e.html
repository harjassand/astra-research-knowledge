CLAIM AND STATUS

The cycle-1 same-Fisher-spectrum archive lower bound has a consistent blind
channel interface and its uniform-TV-to-posterior-distortion step survives
arbitrary hybrid memories, shared input-independent seeds, and nonuniform
seed-by-seed error. Its proof is valid under one explicit condition that should
be written into the definition: both encoder and decoder are fixed independently
of the unknown parameter v. The cycle-1 prose says that v is unknown and the
encoder is blind, but does not state decoder-independence in the same sentence.
If the decoder receives v, D=1 suffices by sampling P_v, so that omission is
load-bearing at the interface level. This is a formalization gap, not a flaw in
the intended blind-code theorem.

The present result is an audit of an already-derived exact counterexample to
Fisher-spectral universality, not a new field-level theorem. I find no defect in
its normalization or hybrid-register accounting under the fixed-code contract.
The N75 covariance-only heat-flow summary does not fill a joint-information
gap: its own status says Poincare control does not imply the needed likelihood
log-Sobolev or additive mutual-information estimate. The supplied N75 packet is
summary-only, so I make no stronger transfer claim.

EXPLICIT CHANNEL AND QUANTIFIERS

For each parameter v in the declared set, let X have law P_v. The encoder is a
single parameter-independent channel E_s from X to a block-diagonal quantum
state Q in algebra direct-sum_j M_(d_j); s is a shared seed independent of
(v,X), and the same decoder channel R_s maps Q to a fresh classical output Y.
All input-dependent records are in Q, with total dimension
D = sum_j d_j. D is bounded for every seed. There is no free receiver
entanglement, feedback, or other input-dependent side channel. The guarantee is
sup_v TV(R_s E_s P_v averaged over s, P_v) <= epsilon, with TV = half L1.
The decoder knows the model and epsilon but not v. It preserves the experiment's
output distribution, not the realized X or its external correlations.

For any finite parameter prior pi, couple T~pi, X~P_T, Q~E_S(X), and
Y~R_S(Q). The joint laws of (T,X) and (T,Y) have total variation at most
sum_t pi(t) TV(P_t, Law(Y|T=t)) <= epsilon. Therefore every fixed loss
g(t,z) in [0,1] has absolute prior-average risk change at most epsilon. This
uses the uniform guarantee only to bound each term; no per-seed error guarantee
is needed. If only one prior-average channel error is assumed, the same
conclusion holds for that prior alone, not for other priors used by a lower
bound.

PROPER-SCORE DISTORTION DERIVATION

For a binary subexperiment with equal prior T in {0,1} and densities
dP_1/dmu = 1+sU and dP_0/dmu = 1-sU, |U|<=c0 and s c0<=1, the mixture
law of X is exactly mu and its posterior is r(X)=(1+sU(X))/2. Use the
bounded Savage loss
ell(t,p)=F(t)-F(p)-(t-p)F'(p), where
F'(p)=clip((2p-1)/(s c0),[-1,1]). Then F(0)=F(1), ell lies in [0,1],
and on the posterior interval F''=2/(s c0). Because T-X-Q-S-Y is Markov,
the conditional law of T given X,Y is r(X). For the output action r(Y),

  E[ell(T,r(Y))-ell(T,r(X)) | X,Y]
    = F(r(X))-F(r(Y))-(r(X)-r(Y))F'(r(Y))
    = s (U(X)-U(Y))^2/(4c0).

The joint-law TV bound makes the expectation of this fixed bounded loss differ
by at most epsilon between Y and X. Hence

  E_mu,S,E,R[(U(X)-U(Y))^2] <= 4 c0 epsilon/s.

The reference expectation is valid because the binary mixture is exactly mu.
The score and feature are seed-independent; averaging over a seed is harmless.
The same argument applies separately to each pair v=+/-e_i because each pair
has the same reference mu. A single blind channel must work for all such pairs,
so the same reference encoder state is reused in each coordinate lower bound.

ALL HYBRID REGISTERS AND RANDOM-ACCESS STEP

Condition on S=s. The encoder's output lives in a Hilbert space of dimension
D=sum_j d_j, including every classical block label. Holevo/data processing
gives I(X;Y|S=s) <= I(X;Q|S=s) <= ln D. Averaging gives

  I(X;Y,S) = I(X;Y|S) <= ln D,

since S is independent of X under each common-reference mixture. No entropy
budget for S is needed, but allowing D to vary only on average would be a
different model. For U(X) and U(Y), data processing and the scalar entropy
distortion inequality give

  ln D >= I(U(X);U(Y),S)
       >= h(U(X)) - (1/2) ln(2 pi e E[(U(X)-U(Y))^2]).

For the product model, under mu the X_i are independent. For a finite active
set I, the same Q is used for every i, although the coordinate lower test may
read a different marginal/POVM. Data processing, conditional quantum
subadditivity, and Holevo give

  sum_(i in I) I(X_i;Y_i,S)
    <= sum_(i in I) I(X_i;Q|S)
    <= I(X_I;Q|S) <= ln D.

This is a finite-subset argument; taking the set of modes with s_i above the
cutoff yields the stated spectral lower. It needs independent reference
coordinates, not just orthogonal score functions. In the shared-scalar model,
all scores are functions of one scalar, and the single-feature entropy lower
matches its positive periodic-hat upper instead.

UNIFORM VERSUS AVERAGE: EXACT SCOPE

The uniform sup_v guarantee implies the binary-prior bound for every
v=+/-e_i. It does not require one global prior that gives every infinitely many
coordinate pair appreciable mass. Replacing sup_v by an average under one
chosen prior cannot recover these pairwise guarantees. Exact common-support
example: P0=Bernoulli(1/2), P1=Bernoulli(3/4), and prior mass eta=1/100 on
P1. A D=1 decoder that always emits P0 has prior-average TV 1/400 but worst
case TV 1/4. Thus the cycle-1 lower is properly a worst-case result; a Bayesian
average formulation would need its own prior and cannot be silently substituted.

NORMALIZATION AUDIT

For the periodic uniform-reference codec, hats obey sum_j phi_j(t)=1 and
integral phi_j=1/J. Encoder probabilities are phi_j(t), decoder density is
J phi_j(y), and the induced density-ratio operator is

  Kf(y) = J sum_j phi_j(y) integral phi_j(t) f(t) dt.

So K1=1 and integral Kf=integral f; its kernel is nonnegative and symmetric,
and its quadratic form is a sum of squares. For graded hats on
mu=Unif[-1,1], m_j=integral phi_j dmu; decoder density with respect to
Lebesgue measure is phi_j(y)/(2m_j), which integrates to one. The induced
reference-ratio operator is

  Kf(y) = sum_j phi_j(y) integral phi_j(x) f(x) dmu(x)/m_j,

again with K1=1 and exact reference preservation. These formulas keep the
factor 1/2 in the Lebesgue decoder; no normalization defect was found.

I independently implemented exact finite checks in
work/cycle6/c09_l06/interface_audit.py. The J=4, N=16 periodic grid has
reference hat masses 1/4, stochastic row and column sums one, a symmetric
Gram kernel, and a nonnegative exact quadratic form 19/32. A three-point
binary mixture with s=1/2 has TV 1/6 at each endpoint, expected squared
feature error 4/3, exact Bregman regret 1/6, and score range [0,1]; this checks
the 4 c0 epsilon/s coefficient. The first harness attempt double-weighted
the risk by the 1/2 prior after already using the mixture law mu; that factor
was removed, after which exact risk difference and regret agree. This is a
diagnostic, not an asymptotic or quantum proof.

COSTS, LIMITS, AND NEXT GATE

The claim charges log2 D where D is the sum of hybrid block dimensions;
classical labels count as d_j=1 blocks. The shared seed is free only because it
is independent of data/parameter, and D is bounded at every seed. There is no
free receiver entanglement, feedback, or other input-dependent record. The
mathematical output may be continuous and generated with ideal real randomness.
Acquiring the model, computing spectrum cutoffs, finite-bit simulation,
reference sampling, and physically producing outputs are separate costs.
Atomic floating-point output is not TV-close to a continuous law.

No external correctness or novelty review was performed. The first decisive
repair is to state common parameter-independent encoder AND decoder channels
in the theorem interface. The next scientific gate is to formulate any
joint-posterior condition using the actual joint posterior law or controlled
conditional entropies, then prove a positive dimension-uniform reconstruction
operator; Fisher covariance, score orthogonality, or the N75 Poincare plateau
alone does not supply it. N75's specific scale-integrated likelihood-information
bound remains unproved.
