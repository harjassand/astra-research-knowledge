## Exact interface and source scope

The primary is Balasubramanian, Davydova and Lin, *A passive self-correcting
quantum memory in three dimensions*, arXiv2605.10943v1, 11 May 2026.
Source paper.tex §§3.3–3.5 defines the alternating actual replacements
R_X and R_Z, their local cochain complexes, gluing, and physical
piecewise-linear folding. Its outlook leaves TQO-2 and general local
Hamiltonian perturbation stability for future work. We do not substitute
a Tanner graph assembled after a geometric crossing, an unrestricted
synthetic patch, or a code with omitted original checks.

Fix the legal auxiliary **X–Z diagonal** of every parent square
(x,q,z,q'), as in the source's replacement-fold figure, before acquiring
the perturbed embedding. This makes every triangle in an X-star contain
its X apex and every triangle in a Z-star contain its Z apex. Our proof
does not classify a different Q–Q auxiliary diagonal. Incident abstract
cells are glued exactly as the source prescribes. Unrelated sheets may
physically cross or coincide and are never identified by that fact.

The geometric premise is an admitted actual realization, with old and
fine Tanner edges of length at most1, the source's separated cap
perturbations, alpha=1000, alpha2=139/2, lambda>=10^24, and ell the least
odd integer greater than2001lambda. Density acquisition remains charged.
The filling proof uses no density constant. For downstream applications
one must use a valid density-admitted parameter choice; the source's
illustrative numerical density is not independently certified here after
the conservative auxiliary-diagonal bookkeeping below. Fine unit Tanner
edges do follow directly from the template: an axis step has normalized
length1/ell and each affine triangle's axis derivative is an original
parent Tanner-side vector of length<ell. A step crossing the diagonal is
the sum of such pieces; collapsed doubling steps have length0. An
auxiliary diagonal itself need not have old length1.

Explicitly, put the normalized corners at z=(0,0), q=(0,1),
q'=(1,0), x=(1,1). The interpolation is

    u<=v: I(u,v)=z+v(q-z)+u(x-q),
    u>=v: I(u,v)=z+u(q'-z)+v(x-q').                 (0a)

Every coordinate derivative is a parent Tanner-side vector. Splitting
an axis step at u=v therefore retains the unit-length bound even when
the diagonal |x-z| exceeds ell. This exact formula also checks the
inset carrier correspondence used in Section5.

Write A0 --d0--> A1 --d1--> A2 for the old binary cochain complex and
F0 --D0--> F1 --D1--> F2 for the fine one. X global stabilizers are
im D0; Z global stabilizers are im D1^T. A filling radius always bounds
the **whole support of every original selected check**, not its center.

The conclusion is: a constant C independent of recursion depth generates
every global stabilizer supported in B(x,r), r>=1, using original checks
wholly supported in B(x,Cr). For r<1 the literal bound is C max(r,1).
This gives reduced-ground-kernel equality with a proportional buffer.
It is weaker than the fixed one-cell collar in BHM1001.0344v1 §2.2.

## 1. Reconstructing both degree-one retracts

The actual RX local blocks are cellular cochains CYx of two subdivided
cones over the old connected link, joined by the cylinder over that link;
CQq=C(Yq)[-1] for the doubled q-to-z star; and CZz two points in degree2.
The maps X->Q and Q->Z are literal cellular restrictions. The RZ blocks
and gluing are the color-swapped degree-reversing transposes. Source
paper.tex lines1530–1667 and1950–2078 specify them; they are not merely
blocks with matching global Betti numbers.

For each finite block choose Cj=Bj direct-sum Hj direct-sum Lj, with
d:Lj->B(j+1) an isomorphism. Set i to inclusion of Hj, p to projection
onto Hj, and h to the inverse on B and zero on H+L. Over F2,

    pi=1, ip=1+dh+hd, hi=ph=h²=0.                     (1)

This is executable binary linear algebra inside complete actual blocks,
whose dimensions are O(ell²) after bounded parent degree is fixed.
Naive Gaussian contractions cost O(ell^6) binary operations per local
type. No such astronomical matrix was executed, and no free homology
oracle or efficient finite-coordinate acquisition is asserted.

Let g be the gluing differential. Its tag path is X->Q->Z, while h
does not change tags. Thus (gh)^3=(hg)^3=0 and the finite HPL formulas are

    i'=i+hgi+hghgi, p'=p+pgh+pghgh,
    h'=h+hgh+hghgh,
    i'p'=1+D h'+h'D.                                (2)

There is a load-bearing extra check: nilpotence by itself does NOT
identify the transferred differential with pgi. The possible term
pghgi vanishes here. In RX, the degree0 X-H class enters CQ1, but h
would have to enter CQ0=0; a Q-H1 class enters CZ2, whose h target
CZ1 is zero. In RZ, a degree0 X class can pass through CQ1->CQ0->CZ1,
but pCZ1=0 because H1(CZ)=0; every other two-tag path stops. Hence the
source's induced complexes are indeed the literal transferred complexes:

    RX: E0=A0, E1=A1, E2=(A2+A2)+M2,
        dE0=d0, dE1=(d1,d1,0).
    RZ: E0=(A0+A0)+M0, E1=A1, E2=A2,
        dE0(a,b,m)=d0(a+b), dE1=d1.                  (3)

The extra endpoint metachecks have zero degree-one maps. Define J:A->E
and Q:E->A by RX J2z=(z,z,0), Q2(z,w,m)=z and identities in degrees0,1;
RZ J0x=(x,0,0), Q0(x,y,m)=x+y and identities in degrees1,2.
Then QJ=1, J1Q1=1 and both selectors are chain maps.

For X set P1=Q1p'1, P0=Q0p'0, I1=i'1J1, I0=i'0J0, K=h'1.
For D1e=0 one obtains

    e+I1P1e=D0Ke,
    P1D0=d0P0, D0I0=I1d0.                          (4)

For Z, take the degree-reversing transposed complex. The exact maps are

    P1Z=J1^T i'1^T, P2Z=J2^T i'2^T,
    I1Z=p'1^T Q1^T, I2Z=p'2^T Q2^T, KZ=h'2^T.

If D0^T e=0 then

    e+I1Z P1Z e=D1^T KZ e,
    P1Z D1^T=d1^T P2Z, D1^T I2Z=I1Z d1^T.         (5)

This dual calculation is required independently of (4); full old/fine
equivalence in all degrees is neither true nor needed.

## 2. Physical support constants and the radius floor

A new parent position is lambda v+c(v), |c(v)|<=alpha lambda. A local
block lies in convex hulls of squares incident to its tag, within2ell
of that perturbed tag. Every term of (2) crosses at most two parent
Tanner edges. Thus, for either sector,

    P: B(x,r) -> B(x/lambda,r/lambda+a),
    K: B(x,r) -> whole-check B(x,r+b),
    I: coarse whole-check B(x/lambda,s)
         -> fine whole-check B(x,lambda s+b),
    a=alpha+2ell/lambda+2, b=6ell+2.                 (6)

The projection pays input local offset2ell, perturbation alpha lambda
and at most two old unit hops. The homotopy pays input/output offsets
2ell each, two perturbed tag hops2ell, and whole-check completion1.
For injection, the parent check CENTER can be1 beyond the coarse
whole-qubit-support ball. Paying this extra lambda gives

    (alpha+3)lambda+2ell+1 <= 6ell+2=b.              (6a)

This corrects a center/whole-support ambiguity without changing b.
Transpose reverses tag paths and preserves the same bounds.

For f_i(r) denoting whole-check filling radius for global boundaries,
(4) or (5) gives

    f_(i+1)(r) <= max(r+b,lambda f_i(r/lambda+a)+b).  (7)

Projection stops shrinking at r*=a lambda/(lambda-1); it cannot be
iterated until the boundary is empty. Set r0=r*+1. The uniform anchor
at this exact radius is proved next, rather than inferred from finite
local types, global distance, or global first homology.

## 3. Reachable-family invariant, including connected links

At every actual level the following hold: every qubit has an X and a Z
neighbor; every X/Z pair overlaps on either0 or2 qubits; the graph on a
check's adjacent qubits, joining each pair shared with an opposite check,
is connected and has an edge. Nonzero overlap2 makes each parent X/Z
face unique.

From the source's actual five-qubit seed figure, in order NW,NE,C,SW,SE,

    HX=[11100;00111], HZ=[10110;01101].              (8)

All four opposite overlaps are2. Each check link is a path of length2.
This base calculation is a reconstruction of the displayed seed, not
an arbitrary CSS starting point.

For RX, all fine X checks are CYx vertices. Fine Z checks are CYx faces,
CQq edges, or a layer copy of inherited z. A vertex and its incident
regular face share the two boundary-edge qubits, else share0. A vertex
and a CQq edge share its CYx edge and the corresponding CQq endpoint
qubit, else share0. A vertex and an inherited z copy share the old
overlap at the unique old z corner and layer. This is0 or2 by induction.
All other block pairs are0. Old square corners are distinct, ell>=5,
and two abstract layers are distinct even when physically collapsed;
there are no self-identifications of a small face's vertices or edges.

For connectivity, CYx is the doubled cone over its connected old link
with a subdivided equatorial cylinder. Its vertex links are connected:
an apex link is the old link; a vertex on a cone edge or equator has
the suspension of a nonempty incident set; a face-interior mesh vertex
has a subdivided circle. Every vertex is incident to a2-cell. This
connects all incident CYx edge qubits by CYx face checks. Each added CQq
vertex qubit at that X check is attached through an incident CQq edge
check to a CYx edge qubit, preserving connectedness. A CYx face-check
link is its boundary cycle. A CQq edge-check link has its two endpoint
CQq qubits joined by a two-edge path through the corresponding CYx
edge qubit for each old X neighbor of q; there is at least one. An
inherited z check has exactly its old connected link at that layer.

Every CYx edge has endpoint X checks and an incident face Z check;
every CQq vertex has a restriction X neighbor and an incident CQq edge
Z check. Thus qubit nonisolation also persists. An old X-Q edge always
has a face: take any Z neighbor of Q; CSS even overlap supplies its
other common qubit. This pays the nonempty sets used above. The RZ
case is the exact color-swapped transpose of this case analysis.

This is an induction on the recursively reachable source family,
not the false claim that all bounded-degree CSS links are connected.
In particular the restriction at an old z corner is unambiguous because
there is only one parent face for the nonzero pair(x,z).

## 4. Geometry with folded overlaps retained

An auxiliary diagonal can have old length2, while Tanner edges have
length1. Using triangle inequalities and the cap separation/projection
argument gives the conservative perturbed-parent bounds

    L=2002lambda (side upper bound),
    l=(135/2)lambda (side lower bound),
    w=(2/5)lambda (minimum vertex-to-opposite-side distance). (9)

The width follows from
1000[1-cos(139/2000)]-2>2/5; the lower Taylor value is exactly
159034698959/384000000000. No asserted old auxiliary length1 is used.

For a ball of radius s, set R=(1+2L/w)s=10011s. A ball meeting two
sides of one triangle places their common vertex within R. Indeed
sin(theta)>=w/L, two encounter points are at most2s apart, and the
distance along the first side to the common vertex is at most2sL/w.
If2R<l, an incidence-connected set of triangles meeting the ball is
contained in one triangle, one abstract edge star, or one vertex star
whose vertex is within R. To see this, switching side labels in a
triangle creates a near vertex. Every later switch not containing that
vertex would place the other endpoint of an edge within R too, contrary
to its length>=l. Paths that never switch use one edge label.

Only identified shared sides or vertices meeting the ball permit such
an adjacency. A physical crossing of unrelated simplices creates none.
For a fine error supported in B(x,r), its interaction graph connects
qubits sharing a syndrome check. That check and all relevant fine
Tanner edges lie within B(x,r+1). Their intrinsic parent carriers give
exactly the foregoing incidence paths, including auxiliary-diagonal
splits and collapsed cylinder cells. Each connected error component
is itself closed if the whole error is closed: a check contributing
to two components would already join them. It is NOT automatically a
global boundary; the anchor proof below avoids that false inference.

For 0<=r<=r0 choose

    s=r+128, R=10011s, M=R+8ell+100.                 (10)

The least-odd choice gives ell<=2001lambda+2 and
r0<=5005+5008/(lambda-1), hence
s<5134, R<51,396,474. In particular2(R+65)<l-16 and w>R+s. The
finer interaction classification at r+1 also applies. M is constant
independent of depth and center; its large magnitude is charged.

## 5. RX X sector: compact potential on the actual inset mesh

Map the same abstract CYx to an ideal full parent X-star: both cones
map directly onto that star and the equatorial cylinder collapses to
its old link. The physical map is still the source's inset one. With
z at normalized square origin and N=(ell-1)/2, an ideal blue coordinate
i/N is physically (1+2i)/ell, so the coordinate difference is at most
1/ell in each direction. Both maps use the X–Z diagonal. Their affine
slopes are at most2L and L/ell<2; each actual blue vertex is within8
of its ideal location. This also covers collapsed cylinder vertices.
Uniqueness of each old(x,z) face guarantees compatible old z corners;
radial X-Q boundaries have the same interpolation on incident squares.

Any CYx graph-edge endpoints are at actual distance<=2 through their
qubit; any two vertices of a fine face are at distance<=4 through its
face check. Their ideal displacements add at most16. The ideal image
of a cell is its piecewise-affine hull in the relevant old triangles,
so its diameter is at most32, safely including diagonal splits and
collapsed cylinder cells. An edge's actual qubit is within64 of its
ideal cell (in fact much less). Thus the128 mesh collar in (10) pays
both cell diameter and carrier offset without an inverse-geometric
condition number.

In a parent triangle, the ball B(x,s) meets at most two sides: meeting
three would place adjacent vertices within R, violating2R<l. Every
component of its complement contains a triangle vertex. One can move
radially away from the planar disk to a side and follow that side's
remaining interval to an endpoint. If two sides are crossed, the only
possibly isolated corner cap is at their shared near vertex and is
contained in conv(vertex, the two side encounters), hence in B(x,R).
Consequently all triangle points outside B(x,R) join every far vertex
of that triangle through paths avoiding B(x,s).

Each parent X-star triangle contains its apex under our diagonal choice.
If the apex is within R, every link vertex is far, and every link EDGE
avoids B(x,s): its distance from the apex is at least w, hence distance
from x at least w-R>s. Connectedness of the full link joins both cones'
outer pieces, and its cylinder joins the sheets. Far endpoints alone
would not justify this step. If the apex is outside R, every triangle
has a far link vertex, since the two endpoints of its link edge cannot
both be within R. That vertex joins the far apex on either sheet, and
the cylinder over one far link vertex joins the two apex copies.
Therefore all ideal CYx points outside B(x,R) lie in one abstract
component avoiding B(x,s), despite folding of unrelated images.

Lift a continuous path in this component to edges through the finite
fine cells it meets. Selected ideal cells stay within32 of the path,
and their actual qubits within64 of those cells. Since128>32+64,
these edge qubits are outside B(x,r). Every actual vertex outside
B(x,R+64) starts at an ideal point outside B(x,R) and joins the same
unaffected graph component. A narrow wedge really can isolate a cap;
the cap is inside the enlarged ball. We never claim the complement of
the original small ball is connected, nor cross sheets at a physical
overlap without an abstract gluing edge.

For a closed X error e, its CYx component e_x obeys d_x1e_x=0. The
source's H1(CYx)=0, independently recovered from the suspension of the
connected link, yields e_x=d_x0 f_x. On an unaffected graph edge f_x
has equal endpoint values. Choose its constant gauge to be0 on the
unique outside component. Then supp f_x lies in B(x,R+64); select
f_x=0 for an empty e_x. Summing these original fine X checks gives
whole support in B(x,R+65).

After subtracting D0f, only CQq vertex qubits remain. Their closure
under d_q1 says they are constant on each connected Yq. Each Yq
contains a whole parent Q-Z side with actual endpoint displacement
at most8; its actual diameter is at least l-16>2(R+65). A nonzero
constant vector cannot fit the residual support ball. Thus all vanish.
This is a whole-original-X-check filling, with radius R+65.

## 6. RX Z sector: exact relative coarse image, not global membership

In RX the injection of a degree-one Q cohomology class is literally
the all-ones vector on Yq. Its possible HPL correction would go from
CQ1 to CZ2 and then h to CZ1=0. Consequently (5)'s projection is

    b_q = parity of e on the CQq vertex qubits.       (11)

It has no CYx-edge contribution. For any closed fine Z component,
(5) implies d0^T b=0, independently of whether that component was a
global stabilizer.

Use its actual interaction/carrier path in B(x,r+1). CQq qubits have
Q-Z side carriers. In an isolated triangle there is at most one such
q label. In a parent Q-Z or X-Q edge star there is again only its q;
in a parent Q vertex star only that Q label can occur. In a parent X
vertex star there is none: Q-Z is the opposite side of the X triangle,
at distance>=w from the X vertex, while the vertex is within R and
the ball radius is< w-R. In a parent Z star all labels are in N(z).

There is an important auxiliary X-Z-edge exception. Its two triangles
in the unique parent face can carry the two different q labels. Folded
Q-Z sides can lie physically near each other far from Z, so mere
spatial proximity is insufficient. An actual connected carrier path
between the two sides crosses their identified X-Z seam within the
ball. In the triangle on that path, the Q-Z and X-Z sides both meet
B(x,r+1); their shared Z is within10011(r+1)<=R. Thus this case also
has supp b subset N(z) for a near old z. If only one q label occurs,
then b=0 because that q has an X neighbor and d0^T b=0.

In the remaining case supp b subset N(z), every nonzero old X row of
d0^T b is the equality of the pair shared with z, by invariant(8).
The old Z link is connected; hence b is constant on N(z) and

    b=t d1^T z, t in F2.                            (12)

This is the exact relative boundary-image computation. It does not
follow from global homology or a freely capped star. Apply (5):

    e=D1^T [KZ e + I2Z(tz)].                        (13)

The first term uses whole checks inside B(x,r+b). The injected z
uses at most two parent tag hops and a local block, so lies within
4ell+1 of the perturbed old z, which is within R. Both fit (10)'s M.
Equivalently, subtract the inherited layer0 CZ check with parity image
d1^T z, use full connected Yq edge paths to eliminate even parities,
and fill the remaining CYx1 cycles with CYx2 faces. This uses only
original source generators, never newly added coarse checks. The
complete touched blocks have diameters at most2ell and4ell and fit
the deliberately loose8ell budget.

Linear superposition over the closed interaction components preserves
the common whole-support bound, even when their filling blocks overlap.
This proves the RX opposite-sector anchor. Swapping colors and
transposing the actual RZ template exchanges Sections5 and6; therefore
both CSS sectors hold in both alternating replacement steps.

## 7. Uniform anchor, proportional filling, and exact boundary

At every fine level i>=1, every closed fine X or Z error in B(x,r0)
admits the foregoing original-check filling in B(x,M0), where
M0=10011(r0+128)+8ell+100. At the seed we claim this only for global
boundaries; the seed's logical errors are not fillable. Its fixed
whole-support diameter is absorbed in M0.

Combining the uniform boundary anchor with (7), stop after
j=ceil(log_lambda max(r,1)) projections or at the seed if earlier.
The radius is r_j=r/lambda^j+r*(1-lambda^(-j))<=r*+1. Lift its
anchor filling, retaining all earlier homotopy terms. Since
lambda^j<=lambda max(r,1), a conservative final constant is

    C=2+lambda[M0+b+(a lambda+2b)/(lambda-1)].       (14)

This proves the stated Cr result for all global boundaries at all
finite recursion depths. CSS components of a mixed stabilizer have
support contained in the mixed support, so both fillings suffice.

BHM §2.3's signed-stabilizer criterion then gives equality of the
reduced ground-state kernels on A when local checks are imposed in
the proportional containing ball. This does NOT acquire BHM's
one-unit collar by notation, a generic perturbation block-diagonalization,
a perturbed thermal memory lifetime, or efficient thermal encoding.

## Prior, costs, falsifiers, and unresolved interfaces

Source author claims are the actual recursively local CSS construction,
global degree-one homology preservation, and the unperturbed thermal
memory theorem. Our scoped deduction is controlled support plus the
relative small-ball anchor and proportional generator localization.
The stabilizer reduced-kernel criterion is prior BHM. No novelty claim
for the geometric component lemma, local homotopy technique, or this
proportional filling result has been established by a full prior search.

Construction parameters and constants are astronomical. Density/cap
admission and the Moser–Tardos geometric acquisition algorithm remain
source premises, with their computational and precision costs charged.
Our proof constructs finite contractions but executes no physical-size
patch matrix. A small exact template check, if added, is transcription
evidence and cannot certify the universal induction or random geometry.

Decisive falsifiers are an actual reachable vertex/face overlap other
than0/2, a disconnected actual link, a failure of the ideal/actual
mesh correspondence or path lifting, or an actual connected small-ball
carrier yielding a coarse parity outside the singleton/near-Z cases.
These are the retained falsifiers; the separate hostile gate reconstructs
these steps with PASS. Another diagonal choice,
an arbitrary artificial patch or a graph glued at a geometric crossing
would test a different, unclaimed interface.

Remaining exact obstacle after this gate: acquire a perturbative theorem
whose graph/buffer and locality hypotheses are proved for this actual
proportional-filling family. Fixed-collar BHM TQO-2 and a change to the
original Tanner graph in a De Roeck theorem cannot simply be inferred.
Those downstream questions, and thermal dynamics under perturbation,
remain open here after adoption of the scoped mathematical anchor.
