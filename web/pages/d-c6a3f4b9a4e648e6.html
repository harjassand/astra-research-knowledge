EXPOSED GENERAL-DIMENSIONAL KERNEL IDENTITY AUDIT

Status: the supplied exact identity, its PSD conclusion, and the entropy
decomposition PASS analytic reconstruction in every finite dimension for
faithful sigma and rho. This is NOT a proof that J>=2E in general dimension.
The remaining terms can have a strictly negative sum, even at J=E=0.

1. Precise normalization and hypotheses

Let sigma,rho be faithful density matrices on C^n; the algebraic identities
also hold without trace-one normalization. Set
 s=sqrt(sigma), q=sqrt(rho), d=s^(1/2),
 r=s^(-1/2)rho s^(-1/2),
 z=s^(1/2)(logrho-2logs)s^(1/2).
ALL logarithms are natural. Define the UNNORMALIZED Lyapunov operator
 Lyap_s(X)=sX+Xs,
and C={r,z}/2-2rho. The candidate is
 W=2 Lyap_s^(-1)(C).
This equals ell_s^(-1)(C) for ell_s(X)={s,X}/2, the convention used in
the earlier quantum audit. Using 2ell_s^(-1) instead would double W and
make the displayed identity false already for commuting scalar entries.

In an orthonormal s eigenbasis write s_i>0, u_i=log s_i, and use the
spectral resolution q=sum_a q_a P_a, q_a>0, sum_a P_a=I. Define
 v_a=log q_a, x_ai=v_a-u_i,
 T(x,y)=x sinh y+y sinh x-4sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2),
 K(x,y)=T(x,y)/(2cosh((x-y)/2)),
 R_K=sum_a q_a ( [K(x_ai,x_aj)]_ij Schur P_a ).
P_a may have arbitrary finite rank. Repeated eigenvalues and degeneracies
cause no difficulty, and splitting an eigenspace leaves the sum unchanged.
The PSD kernel statement applies to ALL real x_ai, including zero and
opposite signs. This audit makes no logarithmic claim for singular rho.

2. Entrywise reconstruction, including the completeness cancellation

The commutation of rho with logrho gives
 C_ij=sum_a q_a^2 [sqrt(s_i/s_j)x_ai
                         +sqrt(s_j/s_i)x_aj-2](P_a)_ij.
Since Lyap_s^(-1) multiplies the ij entry by 1/(s_i+s_j), its contribution
to W for a fixed a has coefficient
 w_a(i,j)=2q_a^2/(s_i+s_j)
                 [sqrt(s_i/s_j)x_ai+sqrt(s_j/s_i)x_aj-2].
Put x=x_ai, y=x_aj, m=(x+y)/2, h=(x-y)/2. Then
 s_i=q_a exp(-x), s_j=q_a exp(-y), and
 w_a(i,j)=q_a [x exp(y)+y exp(x)-2exp(m)]/cosh(h).

The remaining matrices admit the complete-projector expressions
 z_ij=sum_a q_a exp(-m)(x+y)(P_a)_ij,
 (2s)_ij=sum_a q_a [exp(-x)+exp(-y)](P_a)_ij,
 q_ij=sum_a q_a(P_a)_ij.
For z, symmetrizing u_i and u_j is legitimate because sum_a P_a=I.
For 2s, (s_i+s_j)sum_a(P_a)_ij=2s_i delta_ij.

Let b_a(i,j) be the coefficient of z+2s-4q+4R_K with these expressions.
Directly expanding the exponentials yields
 b_a(i,j)-w_a(i,j)
  =2q_a exp(-m) sinh(h)[sinh(h)-h]/cosh(h)
  =2sqrt(s_i s_j) sinh(h)[sinh(h)-h]/cosh(h).
One convenient verification multiplies the first equality by cosh(h)/q_a,
uses 4sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)=2cosh(m)-2cosh(h), and then uses
 x exp(-y)+y exp(-x)
   =exp(-m)[(x+y)cosh(h)+(x-y)sinh(h)].

This residual depends only on i,j, not a, because h=(u_j-u_i)/2.
Consequently its sum times (P_a)_ij is its value times delta_ij.
At i=j, h=0 and the residual vanishes. Thus the residual is zero after
the spectral sum, proving the EXACT identity
 W=z+2s-4q+4R_K.
In general it is not an identity for an isolated spectral summand.

3. Positive kernel term and covariance

For each a, the finite real-point kernel matrix
 K_a=[K(x_ai,x_aj)]_ij is PSD by the independently audited Hardy proof.
P_a is PSD, so the Schur product theorem gives K_a Schur P_a>=0.
Every q_a is positive, hence R_K>=0. This proves the operator bound
 W>=z+2s-4q.

There is no need for a special choice of rho eigenvectors or phases.
Inside degenerate s eigenspaces the chosen basis can change; the exact
identity expresses R_K=(W-z-2s+4q)/4 in basis-free matrices and therefore
shows the answer is independent of that choice. It is also independent
of refinements of repeated q eigenspaces, by the linearity of the Schur
product for each fixed eigenvalue.

4. Exact entropy decomposition for every Hermitian noise B

For a Hermitian B define
 V_B=2Lyap_s^(-1)(B s B),
 H_B(X)={V_B,X}/2-B X B.
This is the previously audited KMS noise representation: H_B(s)=0 and
its CP semigroup transports to a sigma-preserving KMS Markov semigroup.
No modular covariance, positive channel spectrum, primitivity or
self-compatibility is assumed in this identity.

Let L_* be the transported positive Schrodinger generator,
 L_*(X)=d H_B(d^(-1)X d^(-1))d.
Then
 J_B=Tr[L_*(rho)(logrho-logsigma)]=Tr[H_B(r)z],
 E_B=Tr[q H_B(q)]=Tr[V_B rho]-Tr[B q B q].
The HS self-adjointness of Lyap_s^(-1) and trace cyclicity give
 F_B=J_B-2E_B
    =Tr[B s B W]-Tr[B r B z]+2Tr[B q B q].
Substituting the proved W identity and collecting the two equal cyclic
cross terms proves EXACTLY
 F_B=4Tr[B s B R_K]
       +Tr[B(s-r)B z]
       +2Tr[B(q-s)B(q-s)].
Every trace here is real. Since B s B>=0 and R_K>=0, the first term is
nonnegative: it equals 4||s^(1/2)B R_K^(1/2)||_2^2.
The third term is not a squared HS norm when q-s is indefinite. Neither
the last two terms individually nor their sum have a universal positive
sign. Summing this identity over a legal Hermitian noise decomposition
is legitimate, but does not supply the missing global sign argument.

5. Exact legal control: the remaining sum may be negative at F=0

Take n=3,
 sigma=I_3/3, rho=diag(4/9,4/9,1/9), B=E_12+E_21.
Both states are faithful; B is Hermitian and traceless. Here s=cI with
c=1/sqrt(3), V_B=B^2, and q=diag(a,a,b) with a=2/3,b=1/3.
The noise generator H_B annihilates both q and rho because their first
two diagonal entries are equal. Thus E_B=J_B=F_B=0 exactly.
For this tracial reference, R_K is diagonal and its first two entries
are a k(t), where
 t=log(a/c)=log(2/sqrt(3))>0,
 k(t)=t sinh(t)-cosh(t)+1>0.
Consequently
 4Tr[B s B R_K]=8ca k(t)>0,
whereas the other two terms have sum
 Tr[B(s-r)B z]+2Tr[B(q-s)B(q-s)]
  =-(2/9)log(4/3)+4(2/3-1/sqrt(3))^2
  =-8ca k(t)<0.
This is a legal KMS noise control showing that the first PSD term can
be essential to cancel a negative remainder. It is not a counterexample
to J>=2E. Positivity of the complete decomposition in arbitrary dimension
or with arbitrary untouched quantum references remains OPEN here.
