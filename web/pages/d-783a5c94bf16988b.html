ATTRACTIVE FINITE-RANGE COUNTEREXAMPLE: PHASE COEXISTENCE BREAKS THE XX COUNT TAIL
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact theorem and archive below;
REFUTED for a universal transfer of the XX cubic occupation-tail bound to all
finite-range stable spin interactions under beta=tau L^2 and field detuning
lambda/L^2. No claim is made against the scoped free-XX or N20 theorems.

Result
======

A periodic spin-1/2 Ising chain with a nearest-neighbor attractive interaction
has two phase-separated ground states at its coexistence/saturation point.
At beta=tau L^2, every configuration with both spin values pays at least two
domain walls, so its total Gibbs weight is at most exp[L log 2-2 tau U L^2]
relative to the two pure phases. At zero detuning, the empty and full phases
each have probability tending to 1/2. Consequently every occupation tail
P(M>=d), 1<=d<=L, remains at least 1/2 asymptotically: the XX cubic tail is
not universal.

The unknown field still enters only through exp(tau lambda M), so the exact
canonical sector channel survives. A phase-aware two-label archive measures
whether M=0 and decodes the other label to the full phase. Its whole-state
trace error is exactly the mixed-configuration probability and is uniformly
at most exp[L log 2-2 tau U L^2]. For any fixed nontrivial public field window
[-B,B], B>0, zero retained bits have asymptotic minimax error 1/2, while one
bit suffices for vanishing error. Thus the tail obstruction is not an
ensemble-complexity obstruction: the archive becomes simpler once it tracks
the two phases instead of treating a large count as an undifferentiated
overflow.

1. Exact model and theorem quantifiers
======================================

Let L>=3 be a periodic ring of L spin-1/2 sites, n_i=(I-Z_i)/2,
M=sum_i n_i, n_(L+1)=n_1, and W=sum_i |n_i-n_(i+1)|. Fix U>0, tau>0,
and a public B>=0. The unknown scalar lambda is supplied only through its
promise |lambda|<=B. Define

  H_(L,lambda) = U W - (lambda/L^2) M,
  beta_L = tau L^2,
  rho_(L,lambda) = exp(-beta_L H_(L,lambda)) / Z_L(lambda).

Equivalently,

  H_(L,lambda) = 2U M - 2U sum_i n_i n_(i+1) - (lambda/L^2)M
                 = (U/2) sum_i (I-Z_i Z_(i+1)) - (lambda/L^2)M.

This is a translation-invariant range-one attractive lattice gas (pair
coupling -2U) with the chemical potential tuned to empty/full coexistence.
It is stable: H_(L,0)>=0 and H_(L,lambda)>=-B/L. The all-empty and all-full
configurations are the only zero-detuning ground configurations. This is a
first-order Ising coexistence point, not the gapless one-magnon z=2 point of
the free XX chain. That distinction is a scope boundary, not a defect in the
counterexample to a claim covering all finite-range stable interactions.

Write a=tau U>0, q_L=exp(-a L^2), and z=exp(tau lambda). For a bitstring n,
its exact unnormalized Gibbs weight is

  q_L^W z^M.

Theorem (exact phase archive and failure of a uniform count tail). For every
L>=3, U,tau>0 and |lambda|<=B:

  (a) Z_L(lambda)=Tr(T_L^L),
      T_L=[[1,q_L],[q_L z,z]],
      mu_±=[1+z ± sqrt((1-z)^2+4q_L^2 z)]/2,
      Z_L(lambda)=mu_+^L+mu_-^L.

  (b) If p_mix is the Gibbs probability of 0<M<L, then
      p_mix <= 2^L q_L^2 = exp(L log 2-2 tau U L^2), uniformly in lambda.

  (c) At lambda=0, Z_L(0)=(1+q_L)^L+(1-q_L)^L
      =2+L(L-1)q_L^2+O(L^4 q_L^4), and for every 1<=d<=L,
      P_(L,0)(M>=d)>=P_(L,0)(M=L)=1/Z_L(0)->1/2.
      Therefore no bound tending to zero with d, in particular no uniform
      exp(-c d^3+O(d^2)) count-tail bound, holds over this family.

  (d) Define the parameter-independent conditional sector states
      omega_(L,m)=Z_(L,m)^(-1) sum_(M=m) q_L^W |n><n|,
      Z_(L,m)=sum_(M=m)q_L^W. Then
      rho_(L,lambda)=sum_(m=0)^L [z^m Z_(L,m)/Z_L(lambda)] omega_(L,m).
      So M remains an exact canonical sufficient statistic for the unknown
      field; only its tail changes.

  (e) The two-label channel E measures M=0 versus M>0 and prepares respectively
      |0^L><0^L| or |1^L><1^L|. It is independent of lambda and
      T(E(rho_(L,lambda)),rho_(L,lambda))=p_mix<=2^L q_L^2.

  (f) If B>0 and e_(L,D) is the minimax whole-state trace error over all
      parameter-independent measure-and-prepare channels with at most D
      orthogonal labels (equivalently, a D-dimensional classical memory),
      then with epsilon_L=2^L q_L^2 and
      delta_L=exp(-tau B L)+epsilon_L,
      1/2-delta_L <= e_(L,1) <= 1/2+epsilon_L,
      e_(L,2) <= epsilon_L.
      Hence e_(L,1)->1/2 and e_(L,2)->0. If B=0, a fixed decoder can prepare
      rho_(L,0), so this one-bit lower bound appropriately does not apply.

All probabilities and trace distances here refer to the entire L-spin state.
The result is exact for every finite L except the displayed asymptotic
expansion, which follows directly from the exact binomial formula.

2. Proof
========

Transfer matrix. For neighboring occupancies a,b in {0,1}, set
T_(a,b)=q_L^|a-b| z^a. On a cyclic product, each site contributes its z^n
factor once and each bond contributes q_L^|n_i-n_(i+1)| once. Therefore the
configuration sum is Tr(T_L^L). The characteristic polynomial is
x^2-(1+z)x+z(1-q_L^2), giving the eigenvalues in (a).

Mixed-phase bound. Every cyclic bitstring with 0<M<L has at least two domain
walls. Since q_L<1, q_L^W<=q_L^2. Also z^M<=max(1,z^L), and there are at most
2^L bitstrings. Thus the mixed unnormalized weight R obeys

  R <= 2^L q_L^2 max(1,z^L).

The empty and full bitstrings each have W=0, so Z>=1+z^L. Since
max(1,z^L)/(1+z^L)<=1, R/Z<=2^L q_L^2. This proves (b) uniformly, even
without the compact field promise.

Zero-field asymptotics and tail. At z=1, the transfer eigenvalues are
1+q_L and 1-q_L, which gives the exact formula in (c). In a ring, a set of
2r domain-wall bonds determines a bitstring in exactly two ways, so

  Z_L(0)=2 sum_(r>=0, 2r<=L) binom(L,2r) q_L^(2r).

The r=0 and r=1 terms give 2 and L(L-1)q_L^2; the remaining terms are
O(L^4q_L^4). The full configuration has probability 1/Z_L(0) and is in
every event {M>=d} with d<=L. Since q_L=exp(-aL^2), Z_L(0)->2. This proves
the nonvanishing lower bound on every count tail. More explicitly, for any
integer d>=1, taking L>=d and then L->infinity gives
sup_(L>=d, |lambda|<=B) P_(L,lambda)(M>=d) >= 1/2.

Exact sector channel. The detuning term is a scalar on each M-sector, and
beta_L(lambda/L^2)=tau lambda. Thus the sector Gibbs weight is multiplied by
z^m, while the normalized conditional state omega_(L,m) has no lambda
dependence. This proves (d) without measuring or estimating lambda.

Archive error. The channel leaves the empty configuration unchanged and maps
every nonempty configuration to the full configuration. On the diagonal
probability simplex, its output distribution differs from the Gibbs
distribution by moving exactly the mass of mixed strings to the full string.
The total variation distance, hence the quantum trace distance, is exactly
p_mix. This proves (e). The encoder needs one bit: it measures the binary
predicate M=0; it does not need to retain M.

One-bit threshold. At lambda=+B, the empty phase has probability at most
z^-L=exp(-tau B L), and at lambda=-B the full phase has probability at most
exp(-tau B L). Part (b) bounds the mixed mass by epsilon_L, so rho_(L,+B) is
within delta_L of |1^L><1^L| and rho_(L,-B) is within delta_L of
|0^L><0^L|. Their trace distance is at least 1-2delta_L. Every D=1
encoder-decoder pair outputs one fixed state, so the triangle inequality
forces worst-case error at least (1-2delta_L)/2. For the upper bound choose
the fixed midpoint sigma=(|0^L><0^L|+|1^L><1^L|)/2. For any lambda, remove the
mixed configurations and renormalize the two endpoint weights. This changes
rho by exactly p_mix; the resulting two-point mixture is within 1/2 of
sigma. Hence sup_lambda T(rho,sigma)<=1/2+epsilon_L. Part (e) supplies the
D=2 upper bound.

3. Exact spectrum, crossover, and physical mechanism
====================================================

The transfer-matrix eigenvalues are the exact finite-L spectrum controlling
the partition function. At coexistence, the correlation length extracted
from their ratio is

  xi_L^(-1)=log(mu_+/mu_-)=log((1+q_L)/(1-q_L))=2q_L+O(q_L^3),
  xi_L=(1+o(1))/(2q_L)=(1+o(1)) exp(tau U L^2)/2.

Thus the finite ring is much shorter than its domain-wall correlation
length. This is phase separation, not a collection of spatially resolved
low-momentum critical modes. The interface cost is 2U; its Boltzmann weight
is q_L^2=exp(-2 tau U L^2), while the number of configurations grows only
as exp(O(L)).

The two pure-phase weights are 1 and z^L=exp(tau lambda L). For fixed
lambda>0 the full phase wins; for fixed lambda<0 the empty phase wins; at
lambda=0 they coexist equally. The actual finite-size crossover is
lambda=xi/L, since then z^L=exp(tau xi). In Hamiltonian units, the crossover
field detuning is lambda/L^2=xi/L^3. The nominal supplied window
lambda/L^2 with fixed lambda in [-B,B] is broader than this coexistence
window, but contains it, including lambda=0. The uniformly bad count tail
and uniformly good two-phase archive both result.

The exact two-wall contribution at arbitrary z is

  R_(W=2)=L q_L^2 sum_(m=1)^(L-1) z^m,

because a two-wall configuration is one cyclic block of m occupied sites,
with L translations. At lambda=0 this is L(L-1)q_L^2. This gives the leading
mixed partition correction shown above.

Diagnosis: the failed implication is the use of a one-sided count tail as a
proxy for archive error. Count M is still sufficient, but its law is
bimodal at the first-order coexistence point. The archive should preserve the
two phase labels, not send all large M to a generic state. There is no
ensemble-complexity obstruction here: the input is classical-diagonal and
two labels are enough. The scale mismatch (field exponent tau lambda L)
sets a narrower L^-3 field crossover, and phase separation removes the
gapless XX mode mechanism. A hypothesis restricting to a continuous z=2
one-magnon saturation transition might support a different uniform theorem;
this result does not decide that narrower class.

4. Supplied/acquired information and complete costs
===================================================

Supplied: ring size L, U, tau, the domain-wall Hamiltonian, beta_L=tau L^2,
and the field window [-B,B]. Unknown: lambda. There is no parameter oracle,
field estimate, side register, or entanglement assistance. The encoder
measures the local occupation bits (or their total-zero predicate) and stores
one binary outcome. This requires O(L) local readout and O(L) classical
predicate/OR work, with one retained bit. Decoder output preparation is a
product state and costs O(L) one-site resets/preparations. Those output and
hardware costs are stated separately from retained memory. Input Gibbs
state generation and noise are not part of the theorem.

The strongest equally informed exact baseline stores M in L+1 labels and
decodes omega_(L,m), giving zero error with log2(L+1) retained bits. The
phase-aware two-label code uses the same supplied U,tau,L,B and no lambda,
and improves this to a vanishing-error one-bit code. A no-message baseline
cannot have vanishing worst-case error when B>0, by the endpoint argument.
This is an analytic memory statement, not an efficient thermal sampler or
experimental preparation protocol.

6. Falsifier, limitations, and next gate
========================================

Direct falsifier: for some L>=3, a mixed bitstring with fewer than two cyclic
domain walls, a transfer trace not equal to the configuration sum, or a
phase-archive trace error exceeding p_mix would break the theorem. A uniform
cubic occupation-tail claim for this model is already ruled out by the exact
lambda=0 full-state event. The rational replay found no such failure for
L=3,...,8, and the general proof is analytic.

Scope boundary: this is diagonal (zero transverse hopping), first-order
coexistence. It is deliberately a counterexample to universality over the
class of finite-range stable interactions. It does not prove that every
attractive XXZ/XXZ-like chain loses the XX cubic tail, nor that the phase
archive survives arbitrary noncommuting perturbations. For positive hopping,
the nearest-neighbor attractive hard-core-boson model enters the easy-axis
ferromagnetic XXZ/droplet regime; cited XXZ papers establish relevant gap and
droplet structure, but their relation to the finite-size beta=tau L^2 archive
requires a separate proof.

Next gate: fix nonzero hopping t and attraction g in
  H=-t sum_i(b_i^* b_(i+1)+h.c.)-g sum_i n_i n_(i+1)+mu_L M,
identify the actual few-magnon saturation threshold (including bound pairs),
then prove or disprove a uniform finite-ring estimate on the Gibbs mass
outside the vacuum/droplet phases at beta=tau L^2. The decisive lemma must
control all sector multiplicities and droplet dispersion, not only the
two-particle energy. A threshold resonance at g=2t in the infinite-chain
two-body problem is a candidate alternate scaling gate, but is not asserted
as part of this result. This is the necessary next step before claiming the
same archive for a genuinely noncommuting XX chain with attraction.
