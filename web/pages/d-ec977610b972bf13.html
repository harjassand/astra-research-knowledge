## 1. Question, exact source, and prior

Can the recent constructive connexive calculus inherit actual intuitionistic Frege/EF hard formulas with all line, extension, and atom-renaming costs charged?

The source is exactly Niki, *Correspondence of Contradictions in the Constructive Connexive Calculus C*, [publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00153-026-01013-7), published **2 May 2026**, Archive for Mathematical Logic65:593–629. Definition1, p595/PDF3, gives language `{and,or,weak->,primitive~}`, fourteen cases A1–A12 (A8/A9 each have two cases), and **MP only**. `C_arrow` uses `{weak->,~}` and A1–A6+MP. There are no primitive zero, fusion, strong implication, or bang constructors here.

The intended old comparator is Kamide–Wansing, *Completeness of Connexive Heyting-Brouwer Logic*, IFCoLog3(3):441–466 (2016), [publisher PDF](https://www.collegepublications.co.uk/downloads/ifcolog00007.pdf). Definition3.1 p450 gives precisely the signed normalizer below; Theorems3.2–3.3 pp451–454 embed BCL and its implication-containing fragment ICL into the positive bi-intuitionistic/intuitionistic calculi. The displayed forward proof replaces each logical inference by one corresponding inference, and deletes double-negation inferences. Our finite Hilbert macros make its quantitative consequence explicit. Wansing's 2005 original was not fully acquired: the author-linked AIML endpoint failed, and the indexed author PDF mirror was unavailable. Do not claim absence of an older quantitative statement from that failure.

## 2. Signed normalizer and universal proof

For each original atom p reserve independent coordinates p+ and p−. Positive names will ultimately be preserved: p+=p. Define two formula coordinates simultaneously:

| source constructor | positive | negative |
|---|---|---|
| `~A` | A− | A+ |
| `A and B` | A+ and B+ | A− or B− |
| `A or B` | A+ or B+ | A− and B− |
| `A weak-> B` | A+ -> B+ | **A+ -> B−** |

The last negative clause is essential. It is the connexive clause, whereas Nelson/N4's negative weak implication is A+ **and** B−. Neither clause can be silently used for the other system. No Pair or atomic consistency obligation is assumed or acquired for C.

Take the explicit positive intuitionistic Hilbert basis consisting of A1,A2,A7,A8_l,A8_r,A9_l,A9_r,A10 and MP (the usual S,K,and/or schemata). For arbitrary independent signed slots a,u,b,v,c,w:

- A1,A2,A7–A10 translate to single instances of those positive axioms.
- A3/A4 translate to `(a->v)->(a->v)`.
- A5/A6 translate to `a->a`.
- A11 translates to `(g->g) and (g->g)` with `g=u or v`; A12 similarly with `g=u and v`.

There is a five-row K/S proof of `g->g`. Its conjunction with itself uses one conjunction-introduction axiom and two MP rows, giving eight rows. An MP step translates to one MP step because positive implication is homomorphic. Concatenating those fixed proofs proves, uniformly for every finite source proof π:

`k_IPC(F+) <= 8 k_C(F)` and `k_IPC_arrow(F+) <= 5 k_C_arrow(F)`.

These are ordinary proof **lines**; each target Hilbert line contains one formula. No subformula-cache term, unknown Pair proof, or size-preserving completeness assumption is hidden. Substitution of arbitrary compound signed slots into the stored constant proofs is legal intuitionistic Frege substitution. Additional merely admissible rules, including a substitution rule, are outside this source basis.

Each coordinate of a source formula tree has at most its original node count: at a binary node each coordinate chooses one coordinate from each child and adds one node; primitive negation swaps coordinates and adds none. Consequently this gives a linear formula-node simulation of **ordinary** C-Frege. On a common source DAG with V nodes at most2V coordinate nodes suffice. Under a fixed finite-alphabet encoding, the explicit arbitrary-name allocator below costs O(log(S+2)) per new negative name, so the safe encoded-symbol bound is O(S log(S+2)), where S is input symbol size. These are proofs by structural induction and encoding accounting, not extrapolations from finite samples.

## 3. Direct EF and representation-stable atom interface

All source extension bodies must omit their current fresh alias, use only earlier aliases, and have aliases absent from the final conclusion. The only other rules remain the stated source axioms and MP.

Preserve every positive original atom name. Construct each negative name outside the **entire** finite input atom-name set, which includes all extension names, and make the negative names mutually distinct. The code scans that complete name set and checks every candidate. For a current source alias e, its body and earlier source formulas omit e. Injectivity then makes both e coordinates absent from both translated bodies and all earlier translated rows. This proves actual target extension freshness.

The naive rule `minus(p)=p+'-'` is invalid for arbitrary-name EF. A legal source proof of A5(~p) can precede an alias whose name is `p-`; the naive translated prefix already contains `p-`, so target freshness rejects its definition. The saved negative control reproduces this failure. The corrected allocator also handles an original atom deliberately named `signed.negative.0`.

For a **weak** fresh definition `(e->F) and (F->e)`, introduce the target EF pair `e+->F+`, `F+->e+`. Negative e− remains an unconstrained free atom. Its occurrences cannot invalidate the universal axiom/MP translation. One positive conjunction acquisition adds three rows, giving five rows including the two actual extension rows.

For a **strong** definition, let the strong arrow be the fixed abbreviation

`A => B := (A->B) and (~B->~A)`.

The two-way source strong definition has positive image

`[(e+->F+) and (F−->e−)] and [(F+->e+) and (e−->F−)]`.

Introduce two actual target definitions, one for e+ and one for e−. Neither body contains either current coordinate. Four extension rows and nine conjunction-acquisition rows yield the literal image in **13** rows. This is an explicit direct IPC-EF simulation, not a raw-Chu extension-atom claim. All translated bodies are tree-linear and each fixed macro has node cost a constant multiple of the corresponding supplied source formula/body. Charging the arbitrary-name encoding gives the explicit symbol bound **O(S log(S+2))**, hence a polynomial-time simulation. An unqualified linear encoded-symbol claim would hide the atom-name cost.

For C_arrow, conjunction-form definitions are outside its primitive signature. Use two separate weak directional clauses, or four separate strong directional clauses `{e->F,F->e,~e->~F,~F->~e}`. Their images are precisely one or two target IPC_arrow EF pairs; no source conjunction is silently admitted. The lower bound holds for these explicit chronological interfaces. Calling every arbitrary abbreviation convention “standard EF” without specifying these clauses would conceal a language change.

As an independent existence check, global substitution of e:=F changes a weak conjunction-form definition to `id(F) and id(F)`, provable in eight source rows. The strong diagonal needs16 rows. The corresponding source proofs are serialized too. For full C, this gives the looser unfold-first bound at most136 target rows per original source line; direct EF uses at most13.

## 4. Actual hard-family theorem

Primary Jeřábek, [arXiv1512.05667v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.05667v3), *Proof complexity of intuitionistic implicational formulas*, archive revision **18 September2016** (the retrieved PDF's printed title date is **14 June2018**, both recorded), supplies the exact quantitative input:

- Theorem3.2 p9 and Corollary3.7 p13 give a polynomially constructible family of **pure implicational** intuitionistic tautologies φ_n of polynomial size requiring IPC-EF symbol size `2^{n^{Omega(1)}}`.
- Proposition2.5 p8 states `s_IPC-EF(A)=O(k_IPC-F(A)+|A|^2)` and `k_IPC-F(A)=O(k_IPC-EF(A))`. These are primary source claims, not our new circuit-compression result.

Every φ_n is a C_arrow theorem because its positive K/S fragment is exactly positive intuitionistic implicational logic; it is also a full C theorem. Moreover φ_n+=φ_n, with every original positive atom name preserved. Combining the exact bounds gives

`k_C-F(φ_n), k_C_arrow-F(φ_n) >= 2^{n^{Omega(1)}}`.

For each of the specified fresh EF interfaces, the direct simulation gives IPC-EF proofs with O(L) lines and O(S log(S+2)) encoded-symbol size. Applying Proposition2.5's line relation and the same hard family gives exponential **EF line** lower bounds, as well as exponential EF **symbol-size** lower bounds. The source EF is definition-only; source SF/substitution-rule systems are excluded. The theorem is about these nonclassical calculi; it gives no unrestricted classical Frege/EF lower bound and no same-logic Frege-versus-EF separation.

This is a proof by composition of the old signed embedding and old intuitionistic hard family, with our explicit finite basis and EF allocator. No bounded search can establish first priority. A further lower bound for the *Peirce extension* is expressly unsupported: its positive fragment becomes classical, so the IPC hard-family inference fails.
