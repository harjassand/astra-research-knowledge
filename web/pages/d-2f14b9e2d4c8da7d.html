MIXED-AUGMENTATION OBSTRUCTION FOR MATCHED FACTOR REPRESENTATIONS
2026-10-08

Question and exact interface

A proposed repair of the finite-quotient construction removes all sectors
trivial on either factor and uses the mixed augmentation carrier
H=V tensor V, with V=ell^2_0(Q)=1^perp in the left regular representation
of one finite quotient q:F_65 -> Q. The two free factors use the same quotient
map and the same real permutation representation pi=lambda_Q|V. Each factor
has k=2d=130 inverse-closed branch labels. The total K=260 branches act as

  W_(1,g)=pi(q(g)) tensor I_V,
  W_(2,g)=I_V tensor pi(q(g)),

for g in the formal inverse-closed set S={x_i^(+/-1):1<=i<=65}.

The score radius is

  Gamma_K(pi tensor pi)=sup_{A=A*, Tr A=0, ||A||_HS=1}
          || sum_(s,t in S_1 union S_2) a_st W_s^* W_t ||.

This representation has no global constant vector: V contains only mean-zero
vectors. Nevertheless it fails the selected gate by an exact Bell-vector
witness.

Theorem

For every nontrivial finite quotient Q and every q:F_65 -> Q, if the two
factors use the matched real regular-minus-constants representation above,
then

  Gamma_260 >= sqrt(2k)=sqrt(260)>16,

whereas the selected target is sqrt(50)+0.165<7.237. Therefore the matched
mixed-augmentation lift cannot realize the cycle4 score gate, independent of
|Q|, girth, or quotient collisions.

Proof

Let D=|Q|-1>0 and let

  Omega=D^(-1/2) sum_(x in an orthonormal basis of V) |x> tensor |x>

be a maximally entangled unit vector on V tensor V. For real orthogonal
matrices A,B on V,

  <Omega| A tensor B |Omega> = D^(-1) Tr(A^T B).

Fix any formal letter g in S_1. The second factor's inverse-branch alphabet
contains the formal letter g^(-1) in S_2, even if the quotient identifies
some letters. For the relative operator W_(1,g)^* W_(2,g^(-1)), the two local
matrices are pi(q(g))^T and pi(q(g^(-1))). Thus

  <Omega| W_(1,g)^* W_(2,g^(-1)) |Omega>
     = D^(-1) Tr(pi(q(g)) pi(q(g^(-1)))) = 1.

There are k such matched cross-factor unordered branch pairs, one for each
formal g in S. Their reverse ordered pairs also have expectation one. Define
A on the 2k corresponding ordered entries by setting the two entries of each
matched pair to 1/sqrt(2k), and all other entries to zero. Then A is real
symmetric, traceless, and ||A||_HS^2=2k/(2k)=1. Its score polynomial obeys

  <Omega|P_A|Omega> = 2k/sqrt(2k)=sqrt(2k),

so ||P_A||>=sqrt(2k). At k=130 this is sqrt(260), which strictly exceeds
sqrt(50)+33/200. The proof uses no quotient injectivity and remains valid if
formal branch images collide, since the branch labels remain separate entries
of A and the corresponding permutation products still equal identity.

Scope and consequence

This closes the natural same-quotient/same-generator mixed-augmentation repair.
It explains why merely deleting axial fixed sectors is insufficient: when the
two tensor factors carry equivalent real representations, a maximally entangled
input correlates inverse-matched branch pairs perfectly. The result does not
cover inequivalent quotient representations Q_1,Q_2, unequal-dimensional
carriers, nonregular representations, nonreal pairs without the relevant
conjugate equivalence, or independently chosen algebraic unitaries. Those
routes would need an explicit bound on the full coefficient sphere, not just a
scalar Ramanujan adjacency estimate.

Cost/status

The argument is exact finite-dimensional linear algebra. It needs no matrix
materialization, quotient enumeration, or numerical computation. Status:
INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the matched real regular mixed-augmentation
architecture. It is not a falsifier of the finite Haar existence result or of
unmatched/nonregular constructions. No entropy-violation significance claim is
