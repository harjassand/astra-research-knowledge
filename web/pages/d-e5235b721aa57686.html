1. THE EXACT CLASSICAL DENSITY GENERATOR

Write q=N^-1/4, epsilon=N^-1/2. Use scaled BLOCH coordinates
 m=2q x, |x|<R_N=1/(2q), k_N(x)=((I+2q x.sigma)/2)^tensor N.
These coordinates differ from the decoder's exponential coordinate y.

A sandwich e^(alpha n.J) has local filter e^(alpha n.sigma/2).
Its normalized Bloch flow and trace weight obey
 dm/dalpha=n-(n.m)m,  d log w/dalpha=N(n.m).
Differentiating w(alpha) k_N(m(alpha)) gives exactly {n.J,k_N}.
Consequently, with u_n=n.x and a_n(x)=n-4epsilon u_n x,
 B_n=s D_n, D_n=2u_n+(epsilon/2)a_n.grad,
 and B_n k_N={n.J,k_N}.
Iterating this identity gives B_n^2 k_N={n.J,{n.J,k_N}}, without an
approximation. The field uses (b.J)/(2s), so its generator is D_b/2.

On densities, formal adjoints with respect to d^3x are
 T_n f=-(1/2)div(a_n f), D_n^*=2u_n+epsilon T_n.
The exact positive trace-WEIGHTED classical filter semigroup Q_t has generator
 G_N^* f=sum_i (lambda_i/4)(D_ni^*)^2 f+(1/2)D_b^* f.       (1)
Its integration against k_N intertwines with e^(tL). Positivity is supplied
by the actual random normalized-filter flows and their positive trace weights;
it is not inferred merely from the displayed differential expression.

Define h(x)=x^TCx+b.x. Expanding (1) gives EXACTLY

 epsilon sum_i (lambda_i/4)[2u_i T_i f+T_i(2u_i f)]
 +(epsilon/2)T_b f
 +epsilon^2 sum_i (lambda_i/4)T_i^2 f.                     (2)
Every coefficient of (2) and of its first two derivative terms is bounded
by a fixed polynomial in |x|, uniformly epsilon<=1. Lambda_i<=2M.
The leading operation is multiplication by h. The existing branchwise
N^-1/4 bound misses this density-level cancellation.

The vector fields are tangent to the Bloch-ball boundary:

Invertible filters preserve the open ball.

2. UNIFORM WEIGHTED SOBOLEV CONTROL OF THE EXACT SEED

The exact normalized seed in exponential coordinates y has density
 q_N,M(y) proportional to
 sinch(2q r) exp[N logcosh(2q r)-2sqrt(N)r^2/d0],
 r=|y|, d0=1-M/(2sqrt(N)).
It is mapped to Bloch coordinates by
 F_N(y)=tanh(2q r)/(2q) y/r.
Let f_N=F_N#q_N,M. Its unnormalized radial density is explicitly
 ftilde_N(x)= (u/t) cosh(u)^3
             exp[N(logcosh u-u^2/(2d0))],
 t=2q|x|<1, u=atanh t, u/t=1 at zero.                    (3)
Indeed the radial Jacobian is (u/t)^2/(1-t^2), and multiplying
sinch u=t/(u sqrt(1-t^2)) produces (3).

For every fixed K,L>=0 and integer k, and |alpha|<=2,
 sup_N int_(|x|<R_N) e^(K|x|^2+L|x|)(1+|x|)^k
                      |partial^alpha f_N(x)| d^3x <infinity             (4)
for all sufficiently large N. Here is the full elementary control.

On 0<=u<=1, t and u are comparable, du/d|x|=2q cosh^2u<=Cq.
Let ell=log ftilde_N. Since
 u-tanh u<=u^3/3,
 d0^-1-1=M/(2sqrt(N)d0),
 and log(u/t)+3logcosh u is even analytic with bounded first two
derivatives on [0,1], one has
 |ell_u|<=C[N u^3+M sqrt(N)u+u],
 |ell_uu|<=C[N u^2+M sqrt(N)+1].
Also u_rr=8q^2 sinh u cosh^3u<=Cq^2u.
These imply
 |ell_r|<=C_M(r^3+r),
 |ell_rr|+|ell_r|/r<=C_M(1+r^4), r=|x|.
The r=0 value is the continuous even limit. Thus first/second Cartesian
derivatives of ftilde_N are bounded by C_M(1+r)^8 ftilde_N.
For f(u)=u^2/2-logcosh u, f(u)>=u^4/48 on [0,1].
Since u>=2qr, N f(u)>=r^4/3. The additional M term is nonpositive.
Hence ftilde_N<=C exp(-r^4/3) on this entire region.

On u>1, f(u)>=delta u^2 with delta=1/2-logcosh1>0;
the ratio logcosh u/u^2 is decreasing. Formula (3) then gives
 ftilde_N<=C(u+1)e^(3u-delta N u^2).
Differentiating at most twice with respect to x introduces at most
C N^2(1+u)^k exp(4u), times a fixed power of q; these factors follow
from du/dr=2q cosh^2u and u_rr=8q^2 sinh u cosh^3u.
Volume measure is C q^-3 tanh^2u sech^2u du, while r<=1/(2q).
Any weight in (4) is bounded here by
C N^(k/4) exp(Ksqrt(N)/4+L N^1/4/2).
The resulting integral is bounded by a polynomial in N times that last
subexponential factor times int_1^infinity (1+u)^k
 exp(Cu-delta N u^2)du. It is O(exp(-cN)), for sufficiently large N.
Finally (3) converges on a fixed ball to e^(-4r^4/3-Mr^2); its normalizer
is bounded above and below by positive constants. This proves (4).
At the boundary the log-Gaussian factor beats every power of distance:
f_N and all fixed-order derivatives extend by zero smoothly.
These facts justify integrations by parts and the generator domain below.

5. SHARP ASYMPTOTICS FOR THIS DECODER AT THE ISOTROPIC POINT

At A=b=0, the exact heat identity gives rho_N=S_N q_N,0, without approximation.
Write p0(y) proportional to e^(-4r^4/3). Taylor expansion gives in L1
 sqrt(N)(q_N,0-p0) -> p0(y)(H1(r)-mu1),
 H1(r)=(2/3)r^2+(64/45)r^6,
 mu1=E_p0 H1=2 E_p0 r^2
     =2(4/3)^-1/2 Gamma(5/4)/Gamma(3/4).                 (11)
The first contribution is log sinch(2qr)=2epsilon r^2/3+O(epsilon^2 r^4);
the second is Nlogcosh(2qr)-2sqrt(N)r^2
=-4r^4/3+(64/45)epsilon r^6+O(epsilon^2 r^8).
Uniform scaled L1 convergence follows by the same interior Taylor envelope
and exterior tail split. E r^6=(15/16)E r^2 proves the stated mean.

H1 is strictly increasing for r>0, so H1(r)-mu1 has one zero r_*.
Sector measurement J^2 recovers the sign asymptotically. Conditional on an
input branch with direction u, J^2 and J_u commute and
 E(J^2-J_u^2)=N/2,
 Var(J_u)=N(1-|m|^2)/4<=N/4,
 E J_u/s=[N^1/4/2]tanh(2qr) ->r.
Because j>=|J_u| and (j-|J_u|)^2<=j(j+1)-J_u^2,
 E[(j/s-|J_u|/s)^2]<=1/(2sqrt(N)).
Therefore j/s->r in probability, for every fixed r and direction.
The actual distinguishing measurement is just the PARAMETER-INDEPENDENT
event j/s>r_*, so it does not use the unknown direction.

Trace-norm contractivity gives limsup sqrt(N)T<=E|H1-mu1|/2.
Applying that event to (11) and dominated convergence gives liminf equal
to int_(r>r_*) p0(H1-mu1)=E|H1-mu1|/2.
Thus the exact full-state asymptotic is
 T(rho_N(0,0),S_N p0)=c_iso/sqrt(N)+o(N^-1/2),
 c_iso=(1/2)E_p0|H1(r)-mu1|>0.                           (12)
This disproves o(N^-1/2) for this unchanged decoder. It does not disprove
other N-dependent decoders or establish an optimal operational deficiency.
