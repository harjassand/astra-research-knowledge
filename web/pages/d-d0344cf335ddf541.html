# A positive contrast dual certificate for all Johnson reflections

2026-10-07. Exact universal family theorem and quantitative curvature margin. Historical priority unverified; the full arbitrary-W weighted strict-saddle/acquisition lemma remains open. This is a substantive positive aggregate beyond the previously excluded uniform Householder and block-rotation families, not a finite sampling conclusion.

## Question and interface

For F_G(W)=tr(GMM^T), M=W^{circ2}, can the weighted mixed-pivot coefficient matrices be combined into a positive contrast matrix? Such a matrix certifies an escape for every contrast-positive G, even if G is not itself acquired. The intended synthesizer receives expanded rational quartic coefficients and an acquired metric, not latent G,W or an explicit commuting diagonal family. The theorem below is a landscape theorem. It does not produce hidden axes or a rational SOS certificate from that input.

Let m>=5 and N=binom(m,2), with coordinates indexed by the edges of K_m. Let B be the N-by-m unsigned incidence matrix, E0=J/N,

  E1=(BB^T−4J/m)/(m−2), E2=I−E0−E1,
  W=I−2E1.                                             (1)

E1,E2 are rational orthogonal projectors of ranks m−1 and m(m−3)/2. W is a nonuniform reflection of rank m−1, rather than a rank-one uniform Householder reflection. Write P=I−E0. For i an edge-coordinate, set

  x_i=W^T e_i, A_i=x_i e_i^T−e_i x_i^T,
  F''_G(W;A_i)=tr(G K_i).

For m>=6, the universal exact aggregate is

  Ksum=sum_i K_i=λ1 E1+λ2 E2,
  λ1=(m/2)h1, λ2=((m−1)/(m−3))h2,                     (2)

where

  h1=128[3m^7−28m^6+4m^5+592m^4−1536m^3+768m+4096]
             /[m^4(m−2)^6],
  h2=256(m−4)(m−3)[3m^4−49m^3+296m^2−732m+528]
             /[m^2(m−2)^6].                          (3)

Both λ1,λ2 are positive. In fact λ1,λ2>=16/m². Therefore

  sum_i F''_G(W;A_i)>=16 tr(PG)/m²>0                 (4)

for every symmetric G positive definite on the contrast1-perp. No stationarity assumption is needed for (2)–(4). In particular no arbitrary positive definite metric, including one that breaks all S_m symmetry, can make W a local maximum.

For m5, the diagonal pivot is not uniformly positive. The orbit of pivots x_i e_j^T−e_j x_i^T with disjoint edges i,j gives a positive aggregate instead: each representative has E1 curvature773104/1366875 and E2 curvature344152/273375, both positive. There are30 ordered disjoint-edge pairs, so the aggregate eigenvalues are30 times these coefficients divided by the respective ranks4 and5. At m4, W is the permutation sending each edge to its complementary edge; a no-ascent conclusion there would be false. The theorem deliberately begins m5.

## Projection and stationarity reconstruction

B^TB=(m−2)I+J. Centering its columns annihilates1, so (1) gives E1²=E1 and E1 E0=0. Their ranks follow from the nonzero centered incidence columns. E2 is the orthogonal complementary projector. Hence W^TW=I.

The S_m permutation action on edge coordinates has exactly three ordered-pair orbits: equal edges, edges sharing one vertex, and disjoint edges. Its commutant consists of matrices constant on those three orbits. These matrices are symmetric, and form the commutative Johnson association algebra generated by I,BB^T,J. Thus its primitive eigenspaces are precisely E0,E1,E2.

If G=bE1+cE2+aE0, all of W,M,GM and W circ(GM) lie in that algebra (it is also closed under Hadamard products). Their commuting symmetric products give S=W^T[W circ(GM)] symmetric. Such G makes W fully stationary, not merely stationary along an invariant one-parameter family. If b,c,a>0, G is PD. This provides a genuine structured stationary locus of PD metrics rather than a generic fixed W with no PD stationary weight.

More generally, averaging any PD metric over S_m leaves it PD and in this algebra. If an arbitrary metric made this W stationary with nonpositive full Hessian, averaging would preserve these properties. Consequently inverse trap feasibility reduces exactly to the two positive contrast parameters b,c; this reduction does not supply a metric to the learner.

## Complete aggregate derivation

For any skew A, set V=WA, D=2W circ V and D2=2V^{circ2}+2W circ WA². Direct differentiation gives

  K_A=2DD^T+MD2^T+D2M^T.                              (5)

Each K_A kills1 because a gauge weight1v^T+v1^T changes F by a constant. Under S_m conjugation, W is fixed and the A_i are permuted. Thus Ksum is in the commutant and, as Ksum1=0, equals λ1E1+λ2E2. It remains to calculate two scalar coefficients at a representative diagonal pivot; this is a universal orbit-count calculation, not interpolation from finite m.

Let i=(0,1). Matrix entries W_ai have three values

  d=(m−4)/m for a=i,
  h=−2(m−4)/[m(m−2)] for |a intersection i|=1,
  k=8/[m(m−2)] for disjoint a,i.

The eigenvalues of M on E1,E2 are

  μ1=d²+(m−4)h²−(m−3)k²,
  μ2=d²−2h²+k²,

and (E1)_ii=2/m, (E2)_ii=(m−3)/(m−1). For the pivot i, write z_j=W[:,j] circ W[:,i] and q_r(j)=z_j^T E_r z_j. This depends only on the pair orbit of i,j. The stationary weighted-pivot identity applies to G=E_r because those association-algebra weights are stationary (ambient PD is not needed for the identity). It gives

  h_r=4{[(μ_r−1)μ_r²+2d²+(1−4d²)μ_r−μ_r²](E_r)_ii
       +2[d² q_r(equal)+2(m−2)h² q_r(shared)
                    +(m−2)(m−3)k² q_r(disjoint)/2]}.

The q_r values are obtained without matrices of symbolic size. Let D be the distinguished vertices in i union j, q=|D|, and o,o' two representative outside vertices. Put z(a)=W_ai W_aj. For each v in D and a representative outside vertex define

  t_v=sum_w in D,w!=v z({v,w})+(m−q)z({v,o}),
  t_o=sum_w in D z({o,w})+(m−q−1)z({o,o'}).

Then

  ||B^Tz_j||²=sum_v in D t_v²+(m−q)t_o²,
  ||z_j||²=sum_{a subset D,|a|2}z(a)²
        +(m−q)sum_v in D z({v,o})²
        +(m−q)(m−q−1)z({o,o'})²/2,
  q_1=(||B^Tz_j||²−4 delta_ij/m)/(m−2),
  q_2=||z_j||²−delta_ij/N−q_1.

The delta terms use1^Tz_j=delta_ij, by orthogonality. These finite combinatorial formulas also correctly handle the last case m5 with only one outside vertex: its pair count is zero. Substituting the entry values gives exactly (3). `johnson_symbolic.py` preserves this complete orbit-count derivation and the resulting q values; no other worker's code is imported. Explicit matrices for m5..10 check the formulas and stationarity with exact rational arithmetic in `johnson_stationary.py`. These replays check implementation conventions, while the orbit-count calculation proves all sizes.

Since each edge is equivalent, tr(E_r Ksum)=N h_r. Dividing by rank(E_r) yields (2).

## Positivity and a polynomial margin

Put x=m−6>=0. The h1 numerator polynomial becomes

  3x^7+98x^6+1264x^5+8272x^4+29232x^3
      +54432x²+45696x+8704,

and the quartic in h2 becomes

  3x^4+23x³+62x²+120x+96.

All coefficients are positive, proving strict positivity for every real m>=6, hence every allowed integer size. For the stronger bounds h1>=32/m³ and h2>=16/m², respectively subtract the required numerator terms. The resulting polynomials in x are

  4p1−m(m−2)^6 =11x^7+362x^6+4672x^5+30368x^4
      +105408x³+188544x²+141824x+10240,
  16(m−4)(m−3)p2−(m−2)^6
      =47x^6+584x^5+2880x^4+7808x³+13248x²+13056x+5120,

where p1 is the degree7 numerator and p2 the quartic. Again every coefficient is positive. Formula (2) now implies λ1,λ2>=16/m².

All diagonal pivots have ||A_i||_F²=2(1−d²). Consequently some nonzero normalized pivot has

  F''/||A_i||_F² >= tr(PG)/[N(m−2)]
                   >= μ(N−1)/[N(m−2)]

if PGP>=μP with μ>0. This is an explicit polynomial-dimensional margin, useful for robust full-Hessian escape detection at this family. The pivots themselves use latent W rows; a coefficient-only learner instead has access to its own intrinsic full Hessian, whose top eigenvalue is at least this bound under an orthogonal chart transformation.
