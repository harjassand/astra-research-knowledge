# Small Gibbs commutator does not control exact secular leakage

7 October 2026. Independent finite-interface counterexample, pde_spectrum9 Sol/max; no nested workers. This is not an actual BDL thermal-failure theorem.

Let H_delta=delta Z on one qubit, O=A=X, P=I and rho_delta proportional to exp(-beta delta Z). Keep the SAME Hermitian one-site bath coupling A=X at every delta. Use bounded smooth logistic KMS rates gamma(omega)=1/[1+exp(-beta omega)] with convention [H,A_omega]=-omega A_omega. The envelopes are uniformly at most1; gamma(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma(omega).

For delta>0 the ACTUAL components are A_(2delta)=|1><0| and A_(-2delta)=|0><1|, where Z|0>=|0>. Their commutators with X are respectively -Z and +Z. Hence the Gibbs weighted full-frequency leakage is EXACTLY

    k_plus(delta)=sum_omega gamma(omega)
                  Tr rho_delta [A_omega,O]^*[A_omega,O]
                 =gamma(2delta)+gamma(-2delta)=1.

By contrast [A,O]=0, p_bad=0, and

    ||[H_delta,O]||_(rho_delta,2)=2delta ->0.

At delta=0, the only component is A_0=X, so k_plus(0)=0. Thus no universal vanishing upper bound on k_plus follows from Gibbs approximate Hamiltonian conservation, bare bath commutation and small bad mass. This remains true with an unchanged local bath and smooth bounded rates; the frequency projections, not a rate singularity or freely chosen jump inventory, cause the discontinuity.

For comparison the dissipator at delta=0 is (1/2)D[X], whereas for every delta>0 it is gamma(2delta)D[|1><0|]+gamma(-2delta)D[|0><1|]. In the delta->0+ limit its Heisenberg action on X is -X/2, while the delta=0 dissipator action on X is0. The coherent Heisenberg drift 2delta Y tends to0 and does not remove this finite dephasing. A full Pauli logical algebra can be taken as X,Y,Z; all Hamiltonian commutators are O(delta), but this bath protects only the X observable before secularization. The example targets the proposed bound for that observable, not exact protection of a whole encoded qubit by its bare bath.

The earlier cycle14 gate already embeds a distinct degeneracy-splitting secular discontinuity using actual local BDL plaquette/edge data. Neither finite example asserts that every actual full-density transverse-field BDL memory fails. A model-specific bridge must instead acquire exact full-frequency protection, a quantitative clustered/finite-resolution approximation with a time/error budget, or some additional spectral/logical structure strong enough to exclude this mechanism. A small Gibbs witness tail and an exponentially small [H,O] bound alone leave that bridge open.

An exact rational-rate check is included in check_secular_obstruction.py. The rates at +/-2delta can be parameterized by the positive variable z=exp(2beta delta); then gamma_+=z/(1+z), gamma_-=1/(1+z), making the k_plus identity independent of approximation. This algebra check is supporting evidence; the universal no-go above is the displayed exact two-level family.
