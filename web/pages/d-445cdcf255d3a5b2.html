# Noncommutative Dirichlet route: compression bounds and the all-frame gate

## Verdict

This independent Stinespring/Dirichlet representation yields a dimension-free quantitative bound on noncommutativity: for any supplied symmetric two-output broadcaster, the marginal's Dirichlet energy controls the normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt commutator of every pair of Hermitian observables, with operator norms stated explicitly. It also gives an L2(tau) approximate-multiplicativity bound for the marginal itself. The proof permits negative eigenvalues of the self-adjoint channel.

These estimates do **not** construct the required common POVM and do not imply a dimension-free entanglement-breaking (EB) comparator. The all-positive-dual, one-POVM support condition remains the unresolved gate. In particular, the estimates cannot be applied to an HS-unit frame without charging possible √(d) operator norms. No theorem or counterexample closing universal compatible-channel classicality was found in this branch.

## Exact interface

Let d>=2 and tau=Tr/d. Let Phi: M_d -> M_d be CPTP, unital, and self-adjoint for the normalized-trace HS inner product on Hermitian matrices. A supplied CPTP broadcaster B: M_d -> M_d tensor M_d has both partial-trace marginals equal to Phi. If B is not output-swap symmetric, replace it by B_s=(B+swap∘B)/2; this preserves both marginals. No HS-positivity assumption is made. Set Gamma=B^*, using the trace-dual map, which is UCP because B is trace-preserving. The two marginal adjoints are Phi^*, and HS self-adjointness identifies Phi^*=Phi, so

\[
\Gamma(A\otimes I)=\Gamma(I\otimes A)=\Phi(A).
\]

For Hermitian (A), write

\[
 e(A)=\langle A,(I-\Phi)A\rangle_\tau.
\]

Kadison--Schwarz and trace preservation make Φ an HS contraction; its HS self-adjointness therefore gives spectrum in [−1,1], hence (e(A)\ge0). The exact comparison target in the existing all-(Q) gate is one canonical EB map Ψ with

\[
\Psi\succeq 2\Phi-I \quad\text{on the entire Hermitian HS space},
\]

equivalently, for every (Q\succeq0) on the traceless space, the POVM support inequality in `work/agents/c5_quantum_almost_abelian/cycle7_all_Q/FINAL_REPORT.md` must hold. A test-dependent family of POVMs is insufficient; the support condition must hold for each whole finite weighted frame before convex separation extracts one full-domain comparator.

## Stinespring-compression theorem

Take a Stinespring representation Γ(X)=(V^*\pi(X)V), with (V^*V=I), (P=VV^*), and define

\[
K_X=(I-P)\pi(X)V.
\]

For Hermitian (X),

\[
\Gamma(X^2)-\Gamma(X)^2=K_X^*K_X.\tag{1}
\]

For either copy (X=A\otimes I) or (I\otimes A), partial-trace compatibility and trace preservation give

\[
\begin{aligned}
\delta(A)&:=\tau(K_X^*K_X)
 =\tau(A^2)-\tau(\Phi(A)^2)\\
 &=\langle A,(I-\Phi^2)A\rangle_\tau
 \le 2\langle A,(I-\Phi)A\rangle_\tau=2e(A).\tag{2}
\end{aligned}
\]

The last step is the scalar inequality (1-\lambda^2\le2(1-\lambda)) for every λ∈[−1,1]. It explicitly covers the negative spectrum; replacing it by a positive-spectrum assumption would narrow the supplied interface unnecessarily.

If (X,Y) are commuting Hermitian observables, then

\[
[\Gamma(X),\Gamma(Y)]=-K_X^*K_Y+K_Y^*K_X.\tag{3}
\]

Indeed, insert (P+(I-P)) between π(X) and π(Y) in their products and cancel Γ(XY)=Γ(YX). Also ‖K_X‖∞≤‖X‖∞ and ‖K_Y‖₂,τ=√δ(Y), so (2)–(3), with (X=A\otimes I), (Y=I\otimes B), yield

\[
\|[\Phi(A),\Phi(B)]\|_{2,\tau}
\le \sqrt2\left(\|A\|_\infty\sqrt{e(B)}
                   +\|B\|_\infty\sqrt{e(A)}\right).
\tag{4}
\]

Since ((I-\Phi)^2\preceq2(I-\Phi)),

\[
\|A-\Phi(A)\|_{2,\tau}\le\sqrt{2e(A)}.
\tag{5}
\]

Using UCP contractivity ‖Φ(A)‖∞≤‖A‖∞ and expanding ([A,B]-[\Phi(A),\Phi(B)]) gives the main estimate

\[
\boxed{\ \|[A,B]\|_{2,\tau}
\le 3\sqrt2\left(
       \|B\|_\infty\sqrt{e(A)}+
       \|A\|_\infty\sqrt{e(B)}\right).\ }\tag{6}
\]

For Hermitian contractions this is (3\sqrt2(\sqrt{e(A)}+\sqrt{e(B)})), also trivially capped by 2. Every factor in (6) comes from (3), two commutator product bounds, and (5); no dimension or hidden norm conversion is suppressed.

### Arbitrary-operator approximate multiplicativity

The same compression gives a direct, separate statement for arbitrary A,B in M_d. In this paragraph, K_A abbreviates K_{A tensor I}:

\[
\Phi(AB)-\Phi(A)\Phi(B)=K_{A^*}^*K_B,
\]

where the notation (K_X=(I-P)\pi(X)V) is extended linearly. The tracial Schwarz defect obeys

\[
\tau(K_B^*K_B)=\|B\|_{2,\tau}^2-\|\Phi(B)\|_{2,\tau}^2
\le 2\,\tau\bigl(B^*(I-\Phi)B\bigr).
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{\ \|\Phi(AB)-\Phi(A)\Phi(B)\|_{2,\tau}
\le \min\!\left\{
  \|A\|_\infty\sqrt{2e(B)},\;
  \|B\|_\infty\sqrt{2e(A)}
  \right\},\ }
\tag{7}
\]

where (e(X)=\tau(X^*(I-\Phi)X)\). Self-adjointness of Φ makes (I-\Phi\) a positive HS operator, so this definition is nonnegative. This is approximate multiplicativity in normalized (L^2); it is not operator-norm multiplicativity and it is not a CP-square factorization.

For completeness, the Hermitian-Kraus representation Φ(X)=∑ₘHₘXHₘ gives the exact Dirichlet identity (e(A)=\frac12\sum_j\|[H_j,A]\|_{2,\tau}^2), and the corresponding product seminorm satisfies

\[
\sqrt{e(AB)}\le\|B\|_\infty\sqrt{e(A)}+
                  \|A\|_\infty\sqrt{e(B)}.
\tag{8}
\]

This is consistent with (7), but the derivation of (1)–(7) uses the supplied broadcaster and is the independent representation pursued here. Formula (8) and fixed-algebra abelianness already appear in the C5/C7 Markov-form work and are not claimed as new here.

## Exact normalization boundary

Let (A) be HS-unit and traceless. In general ‖A‖∞ can be √(d-1) (for example, a normalized rank-one contrast). Therefore (6) gives, for HS-unit (A,B),

\[
\|[A,B]\|_{2,\tau}\le3\sqrt2\left(
  \|B\|_\infty\sqrt{e(A)}+\|A\|_\infty\sqrt{e(B)}
\right),
\]

which may grow as √(d). It does **not** give a dimension-free all-frame commutator estimate on a normalized HS basis. Scaling (A\mapsto A/\|A\|_\infty) also scales its Dirichlet and target contributions quadratically and cannot be treated as cost-free. Any further approximate-joint-diagonalization step must expose frame size, operator norms, energy margins, rounding exponents, and the loss when converting the rounded family to one POVM.

## Why this misses the decisive gate

The broadcaster implies approximate commutativity of two low-energy observables, and (7) shows the marginal approximately preserves products of low-energy inputs in normalized (L^2). To reach the channel theorem one still needs a *single* canonical POVM for a full positive weighted frame, with a homogeneous operator-order inequality. Pointwise control of commutators or multiplicative defects is not that result. In particular:

1. Fixed-pair or fixed-tuple normalized-HS rounding has constants depending on the tuple size; the frame rank in the support gate can grow to (d^2-1).
2. A square-root energy bound cannot be substituted into a claimed linear all-(Q) energy comparison without a proved conversion and a stated margin.
3. For an HS-unit frame, the operator norms in (6) can cost √(d). Contraction normalization avoids that cost only for inputs with an independently supplied operator-norm bound.
4. An approximate *-homomorphism conclusion for low-energy modes would still require an explicit common abelian/POVM rounding with a dimension-free modulus, then a separate extension to all modes.
5. An inference Φ=C⁺C for a bistochastic CP map C is invalid: C5 cycle8 has an exact qubit canonical-EB counterexample to that representation, while its desired EB domination is trivial. This branch uses no CP-square inference.

For rank-one dual (Q=|A\rangle\langle A|), the broadcaster-generated joint POVM that measures the spectral PVM of (A) on both outputs proves the support inequality exactly on that one direction. This is the known rank-one gate. The open step is the whole weighted frame (Q=\sum_iw_i|A_i\rangle\langle A_i|): no estimate here combines those individually optimal measurements into one measurement with the exact all-(Q) bound. This is exactly the nonduplicated C5/C7 canonical-moment gate, not a new claim that the universal theorem is false.

## Natural measurement shortcuts that fail (exact scoped controls)

One tempting fixed measurement is independent Haar-covariant rank-one measurement on the two broadcaster outputs. It fails even for the symmetric universal qubit cloner, so it cannot be the missing universal POVM. For that cloner, Φ is the depolarizing marginal with traceless eigenvalue λ=2/3; hence the target (2\Phi-I) has eigenvalue (1/3) on (Z). The two-output states are

\[
\mathcal B(I/2)=P_{\rm sym}/3,
\qquad \mathcal B(Z/2)=\frac13(|00\rangle\langle00|-|11\rangle\langle11|).
\]

For the product Haar POVM (4P_n\otimes P_m\,d\mu(n)d\mu(m)), with unit Bloch vectors (n,m\), the maximally-mixed outcome density and the (Z)-tangent density are respectively

\[
p_0(n,m)=1+\frac{n\cdot m}{3},\qquad
\dot p_Z(n,m)=\frac23(n_z+m_z).
\]

Its canonical score on (Z) is exactly its classical Fisher information:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle Z,\Psi Z\rangle_\tau
&=\frac49\int\!\int
    \frac{(n_z+m_z)^2}{1+n\cdot m/3}\,d\mu(n)d\mu(m)\\
&=\frac49\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{1+u}{3+u}\,du
=\frac49(2-2\ln2)
\approx0.2727581<\frac13.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]

In the second line, at fixed (u=n\cdot m), rotational averaging gives (\mathbb E[(n_z+m_z)^2\mid u]=2(1+u)/3). This exact failure rejects only this fixed product-Haar POVM, not a channel-adaptive POVM or the target theorem.

A different fixed coherent-state POVM on the symmetric two-output subspace is adapted to the universal cloner but fails on a classical copying broadcaster: for Φ the qubit Z-dephasing channel, Φ(Z)=Z so the target eigenvalue is 1, while the POVM (3|\psi\psi\rangle\langle\psi\psi|,d\psi) has Z-score (3-3\pi/4\approx0.6438<1) (direct one-variable Haar integral). This rejects a universal fixed covariant POVM across both quantum and classical extremes. The required POVM is allowed to depend on the supplied channel and test frame, so neither scoped failure is a legal counterexample to the all-(Q) theorem.
