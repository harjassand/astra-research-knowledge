## Exact question and legal interface

Can one construct a norm solution using only bounded linear algebra and roots in finite reduced algebras, without receiving a factorization of the support or a splitting isomorphism?

Inputs are an explicitly represented finite field k of characteristic p, an odd prime q, a primitive qth root ζ in k, and a generator ω of the full q-primary subgroup of k*. Also supplied are monic square-free F of positive degree N divisible by q, and nonzero G=A/B in k(X), reduced, with D=max(degA,degB). Assume p>(N+2D+1)^2 and promise that G is a norm from L=k(X)[Y]/(Y^q−F). Output h in L* must satisfy ∏ρ^j(h)=G, where ρ(Y)=ζY.

The existence promise and primary generator are charged inputs. A norm solution, roots of F, support factors, vector-bundle splitting, fiber basis, conjugating matrix, rank-one idempotent and local orders are acquired. The manuscript supplies the primary data elsewhere for its larger application; this note does not make them free or reconstruct the full prime-field learner.

## Independently reconstructed algebraic bridge

Form E=⊕_{b=0}^{q−1} L V^b with VY=ζYV and V^q=G. A place with v(F)=1 shows Y^q−F is Eisenstein, so L is a field of degree q. The norm promise implies E≅M_q(k(X)): an unspecified promised h0 would represent V by h0ρ, and the characters ζ^{bj} prove independence. This establishes existence only; h0 is not passed to the construction.

For each place of the rational base, acquire a maximal order from the following local models.

* If v(F)=0 and q divides w=v(G), use the integral span of Y^a(T^{−w/q}V)^b. All its basis monomials and inverses have unit qth powers. The matrix-trace coordinate formula c_ab=q^{−1}tr(a W_ab^{−1}) makes this order self-dual and maximal.
* If v(F)=1, set V'=Y^{−w}V. Its qth power G/F^w is a unit whose residue is a qth power by the norm promise. Lift a residue root R. Let Y be the shift with wrap F, and V' diagonal with entries ζ^jR.
* If v(F)=0 and q does not divide w, the residue of F is a qth power: otherwise the unramified degree-q extension forces norm valuations divisible by q. Lift its root S. Let Y be diagonal with entries ζ^{−j}S and V the shift with wrap G.
* At infinity z=1/X, use z^{N/q}Y, whose qth power z^NF(1/z) has constant term1. Its root with constant term1 is acquired by Hensel lifting. V is the same weighted shift, including when v∞(G) is divisible by q.

Each matrix model is full: distinct diagonal eigenvalues produce diagonal matrix units, and the invertible shift gives the other units. Integral matrix entries define a maximal order. The abstract identification is never computed.

Intersect these orders to obtain S, the global sections, and evaluate at infinity to obtain B⊂M_q(k). Local lattices descend from completions and glue to a vector bundle V on P1_k. For an existence argument only, S=H0(P1,EndV). Projective-line splitting holds over k itself: choose the maximal line subbundle O(d); after twisting by −d−1, H1(O(−1))=0 forces all quotient degrees ≤d, and all extension groups vanish. Thus V is a sum of line bundles. Grouping equal degrees shows that B is conjugate to the full block upper triangular algebra.

This geometric statement supplies a shape promise, not a computed splitting. From only a basis of B, take N to be the trace-pairing kernel. Matrix-unit pairing on a full matrix algebra is nondegenerate in every characteristic, including when the characteristic divides a block size; hence N is exactly the strictly block upper triangular part. Its common column kernel U is the first block. Any nonzero v∈U and row w with wv=1 give a0=vw∈B, a rank-one idempotent.

Lift a0 linearly to b∈S. The characteristic-polynomial coefficients of a global endomorphism are everywhere regular on P1 and therefore constant. At infinity they equal T^{q−1}(T−1). Consequently e=b^q kills the nilpotent zero-primary space and equals1 on the one-dimensional other primary space. The lift b need not be idempotent; taking the power is necessary.

The left ideal Ee has dimension q. The map L→Ee, a↦ae, is injective because L is a field, hence an isomorphism. Solve Ve=he. Repeated multiplication by V yields Ge=(∏ρ^j(h))e; e≠0 proves the desired norm identity.

## Derived improvement: M=N+D is enough

The producer uses M_old=10q²(N+D+1) in equations6.12–6.13. Its actual Lemma6.5 estimates prove the smaller complete bound

    M=N+D.

This deduction is uniform in the varying odd prime q; it is not inferred from the fixtures.

At a ramified finite place, put D_R=diag(ζ^jR). The exact monomial model is

    W_ab=ζ^{wb(b−1)/2} Y^{a+wb} D_R^b.

For any integer t, the sole nonzero entry of Y^t in column j has valuation floor((j+t)/q). For 0≤a,b,j<q and |w|≤D, both W_ab and its inverse have nonzero-entry valuations of absolute value at most D+2. At an unramified matrix place, Y is a unit diagonal matrix and V^b has valuations0 or w; inverse entries have opposite valuations. At infinity, the scalar z^{−aN/q} contributes less than N, and V^b contributes at most D, so both matrices have absolute valuations at most N+D. In the scaled-basis order the coordinate-change exponent bw/q has absolute value at most D.

Since N is positive and divisible by the odd q, N≥3. Thus N+D≥D+2. The trace-coordinate formula proves every coefficient of any local integral-order element has valuation at least −M. At ordinary finite places it is integral.

Let H be the product of the distinct finite bad support polynomials; degH=d_H≤N+2D. Square-free decomposition and gcd refinement acquire H and its valuation groups without irreducible factors. Every global section is in the ansatz

    c_ab=P_ab/H^M,  degP_ab≤M(d_H+1).

Indeed this denominator clears every finite pole, and the remaining possible pole at infinity has order≤M. Imposing local integrality gives necessary and sufficient linear conditions on this complete space. Computing T^M c_ab and T^M W_ab to order2M+1, and requiring coefficients below2M to vanish, tests exactly the original order membership. Hensel precision3M+3 still suffices for all basis entries. The coefficient at2M in the infinity calculation yields the evaluation map. No condition has been weakened when M is reduced.

Therefore the exact linear system has at most

    U=q²[(N+D)(N+2D+1)+1]

unknowns and O(q²(N+D)(N+2D+1)) scalar conditions. The old width was q²[10q²(N+D+1)(N+2D+1)+1]. The improvement removes a q² factor in the section width (and q4 in dense squared storage), up to the displayed degree constants. This is an algebraic resource refinement of the existing construction, not a new general norm method or a claimed improvement of its full headline exponent.

All quotient rings have dimension at most (3M+3)d_H. Subsequent products b^s have denominator H^{Ms}B^{s−1} and numerator degree at most sM(d_H+1)+(s−1)(N+D). The rank-q final solve has polynomial-degree minor formulas. Dense exact linear algebra therefore remains polynomial in q,N,D and log|k|. It can still be expensive.

## Executed acquisition, controls and retained failures

Own work/norm_fiber_reconstruction.py implements the actual local constraints and fiber step for two q=3 prime-field examples. It receives only the declared input; no global section, idempotent, norm solution or support factor is passed. The residue-root subroutine uses gcd degree layers, Frobenius kernels, unit scalar searches, q-primary digit partitions and CRT, followed by Hensel lifting. The standard irreducible F has degree3, so even the ramified residue calculation occurs in an extension algebra. Inputs are our instantiations of the published class, not published numerical examples.

* p127,q3,F=X³−X+1,G=2X³−X+1,ζ107,ω22: M6 instead of630,387 unknowns and432 conditions; S and B dimensions9; h=107(X+Y) modulo127. Acquired norm reconstruction:1.322s,7.40MB Python-traced peak.
* Same F, G=X−1: M4 instead of450,189 unknowns and216 conditions; infinity cancellation is handled. Acquired h is a scalar multiple of X−Y, with exact norm X−1;0.488s,2.86MB traced peak. The filename contains “unbalanced”, but its evaluated fiber is M3; it is not evidence of an unbalanced bundle splitting. A pre-refinement M6 run is retained separately.

The terminal checker rebuilds multiplication by Y and h over F127(X), verifies det(mult_h)=G, constructs V=mult_h diag(ζ^j), checks VY=ζYV and V³=G I, and verifies the nine Y^aV^b images have rank9. This is an explicit acquired full matrix-algebra isomorphism. It imports no producer module.

The same checker tests dense-conjugated full parabolics with block sizes(1,1) in characteristic2,(2,3) in characteristic3, and(2,1,4) in characteristic127. It also tests dual numbers: the proposed fiber procedure returns UNKNOWN, as its shape promise is false. Global lifts with a nilpotent zero-primary block confirm b²≠b while b^n is rank-one idempotent.

Norm equality first failed because a library fraction compared different structural representations of equal rational functions; exact subtraction was zero. The repair uses an exact zero residual, with unchanged input. The separate checker first had the same expanded-versus-factored characteristic-polynomial comparison, then a JSON-only SymPy-integer serialization error after all assertions passed. All failures and repairs are retained. There is no claim of an independent implementation of all local cases for arbitrary q.

NumPy int64 modular elimination is safe only for these bounded p127 executions. The universal construction must use appropriately sized exact modular arithmetic. Reported custom arithmetic counters omit uninstrumented operations and are not complete bit-operation counts. The finite search used to obtain ω is charged; the theorem takes ω as supplied.
