EXACT LEMMA

For any densities nu,sigma on the same finite B, put
 delta=T(nu,sigma), T=HALF trace norm.
There is an actual full-domain CPTP R:B->B, depending only on nu,sigma,
with R(nu)=sigma and with the simultaneous reference guarantee
 T((id_A tensor R)zeta_AB,zeta_AB)
 <=sqrt(2delta-delta^2)<=sqrt(2delta)                  (L)
for EVERY finite quantum A and EVERY extension zeta_B=nu.
No minimum eigenvalue, commutation, state label, or A operation is assumed.

CONSTRUCTION AND SUPPORT

Let positive part refer to Hermitian spectral positive part and put
 M=nu+(sigma-nu)_+=sigma+(nu-sigma)_+.
The identity follows from X_+-(-X)_+=X. Thus M>=nu and M>=sigma,
TrM=1+delta. Its support contains supp(nu) and supp(sigma); inverse square
roots are support inverses. Define K=sigma^(1/2) M^(-1/2). Then
 K*K=M^(-1/2)sigma M^(-1/2)<=P_M<=I,
 K M K*=sigma,
 KnuK*<=sigma.
Let q=1-Tr(KnuK*)>=0. Since K*M? Only cyclic trace is used:
 q=Tr[K(M-nu)K*]<=Tr(M-nu)=delta.                     (1)
The inequality follows from K*K<=I and M-nu>=0, without commuting any
positive factors. If q>0, set chi=(sigma-KnuK*)/q, a density, and take
 R(Y)=K Y K*+Tr[(I-K*K)Y]chi.                         (2)
If q=0, choose any density chi for the same completion; sigma=KnuK*
by positivity and equality of trace. Both cases give CP and TP since
Tr(KYK*)+Tr[(I-K*K)Y]=TrY, and give R(nu)=sigma. On ker(M) K=0 and
the completion makes R an actual full-domain channel. No inverse exists
or is required there. If delta=0 then M=nu=sigma and the map fixes all
extensions on their B support exactly, regardless of chosen completion.

THE CRUCIAL GENTLENESS ESTIMATE

Define epsilon=||(I-K)nu^(1/2)||_2^2. The exact factorization is
 (I-K)nu^(1/2)=(M^(1/2)-sigma^(1/2))M^(-1/2)nu^(1/2).
Since M^(-1/2)nu M^(-1/2)<=I,
 epsilon<=||M^(1/2)-sigma^(1/2)||_2^2.                (3)
The square-root function is operator monotone, so M>=sigma implies
M^(1/2)>=sigma^(1/2). Consequently
 ||M^(1/2)-sigma^(1/2)||_2^2
 =Tr(M)+Tr(sigma)-2Tr(M^(1/2)sigma^(1/2))
 <=Tr(M)-Tr(sigma)=delta.                            (4)
The trace product in (4) is real, and its excess above Tr(sigma) is
Tr[(M^(1/2)-sigma^(1/2))sigma^(1/2)]>=0. There is no assertion that
the square roots commute.

Expanding the norm in epsilon gives
 epsilon=1+Tr(KnuK*)-2ReTr(nu K)=2-q-2ReTr(nu K).
Combining (1), (3), (4),
 ReTr(nu K)>=1-(q+epsilon)/2>=1-delta.                 (5)
Because 0<=delta<=1, the right side is nonnegative. For ANY purification
of nu, applying R to B gives entanglement fidelity at least the single
Kraus contribution |Tr(nu K)|^2, hence at least (1-delta)^2. Purify a
general extension zeta_AB with one additional E and trace E after this
bound. The pure-input trace/fidelity inequality gives (L), with exactly
HALF trace convention. This proves preservation of an arbitrary quantum
reference, not just classical conditional ensembles.

LOCAL BROADCASTING INTERFACE

Given an actual common local marginal T_B and fixed sigma_B=rho_B, let
nu=T_B sigma_B. Its actual joint residual b controls delta by partial
trace. Thus U_B=R T_B fixes sigma_B exactly, is still actually compatible
by applying R to both outputs, and has JOINT residual at most
 min{1,b+sqrt(2b-b^2)}.
The same R works for every tested reference state with that fixed B
marginal. It depends only on T_B,sigma_B. This closes the physical
stationarity repair for arbitrary nontracial sigma_B. It does not make
U_B unital, HS reversible, or its standard adjoint TP when sigma_B is
nontracial. Those are separate algebra/entropy hypotheses.

FALSIFIERS / FINITE AUDIT CONTRACT

Noncommuting qubit/three-level nu,sigma should test M>=nu,sigma, K*K<=I,
q<=delta, epsilon<=delta, and fidelity on a purification. Rank-deficient
and q=0 cases test full-domain completion. A trace-one check alone would
not validate arbitrary-reference gentleness; (5) supplies that premise.
The tempting replacement M=min(nu,sigma) is meaningless as a common
Loewner lower bound for noncommuting states and is NOT used.
