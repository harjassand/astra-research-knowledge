## Exact systems and resources

Let F be the canonical Frege system for classical affine linear logic LLW
in Tabatabai–Jalali, arXiv2601.22393v2, Frege Tables6–8, identified without
numbering ambiguity by the TeX labels `Fig: FL_e`, `Fig: linear`, and
`Fig: contraction-free logics`. It has the FL_e schemas and rules, double
negation elimination, bounded constants, all five linear-exponential
schemas, theorem necessitation, and unrestricted weakening. It has no
unrestricted contraction. Source proofs are closed DAG-like sequences.

A shared syntax graph is a finite acyclic constructor graph whose unfolding
is the actual ordinary formula. Its edges denote identical syntax, not
semantic equivalence or permission to identify linear assumptions. Count m
nodes across all formula roots and annotated substitution operands of a
source proof, and L source axiom/rule steps. Count target lines as total
formula occurrences in its sequents, exactly the source's definition of l.
All antecedents are multisets with multiplicities, including coincident
formulas after substitution.

The target is the ordinary intuitionistic affine linear sequent calculus
ILLW with cut. Target formulas are ordinary unfolded formulas: shared storage
is an encoding of their syntax, and is not an extension rule in that logic.
Set the Chu parameters D=N=bottom.

## Theorem 1: the shared compiler

Every such F-proof of A admits an ILLW-proof of `=>A^t` with

    l <= 13m + 93L.

The proof and its formula labels admit a common syntax graph with O(m+L)
nodes. A conservative final formula A admits an appended repair, giving

    l(=>A) <= 13m + 93L + 30m_A + 1,

where m_A counts distinct source subformulas of A and m_A<=m. Using only the
recent source's original repair proof instead would give the weaker but
sufficient additional O(|A|^2) charge.

**Proof.** Process source formula nodes topologically, acquiring both Chu
components. Implication creates at most four new nodes; all other
constructors create boundedly many. Cache the compatibility sequent
`P_X: T_X,S_X=>bottom` for each node X. Exact per-constructor proofs cost at
most13 formula occurrences per node. In the implication case, identity on
S_C, P_B, left implication on `S_C->S_B`, left additive projection, and left
fusion prove P_(B->C). The context contains the actual separate occurrences
of T_B and S_C throughout. Additive constructors use permitted common-context
branching; multiplicative constructors combine both context multisets.

For each source inference, install its fixed schematic certificate on the
already acquired operand roots. There are23 axiom certificates and3 rule
certificates. The largest is93 formula occurrences, including declared input
rows. Replace compatibility inputs by references to the corresponding cached
earlier endpoint and rule-premise inputs by their already proved closed
theorems. Reference reuse is legal under the source's explicit proof-DAG
convention. It supplies no extra antecedent resource. Uniform substitution
preserves every primitive rule and its side conditions: promotion contexts
remain entirely exponential, and all contraction principals remain `!T_X`.
Topological induction produces the claimed closed endpoint and line bound.

All templates are finite fixed objects. Their substitution operands are
source graph roots; they do not create a recursive family of distinct
substitutions. The certified direct double-negation template needs only P_A.
Following the source's negation-duality proof literally would add at most two
negation-wrapper graph nodes per use, still O(L). The printed adj_u slip is
repaired by combining the already proved `=>T_A` and `=>1` using right AND;
necessitation promotes the already proved `=>T_A`.

For a conservative target, simultaneously cache `T_X=>X`, `X=>T_X`, and, for
fully conservative X, `S_X=>bottom`. The source's conservative grammar makes
each constructor step bounded. The independent local compiler supplies the
30m_A upper bound. Append the repair `T_A=>A` and its single cut conclusion.
The same cache and fixed-template induction bound shared syntax as well.
This constructs a legal ordinary proof, although its unfolded symbol size
can be exponential. QED.

## Theorem 2: line normalization

For this fixed finite F, if A has an L-line proof with arbitrarily large
intermediate formula trees, it has an L-line proof with a common syntax graph
of O(L+|A|) nodes, including schema operands.

**Proof.** Retain every inference label and earlier-premise reference. Use a
fresh copy of that fixed rule schema's metavariables and constructors, and
equate each premise root with its referenced line root. Equate the last root
with the rigid tree for A. There are O(L+|A|) initial term nodes. The original
proof gives an actual finite ground solution.

Merge equation classes. A merge of constructors requires matching labels and
arities and adds their child equations. Label clashes cannot occur for this
solvable input. The quotient's constructor graph has no directed cycle: along
every child edge the original solution's finite term height strictly
decreases. Ground constructor-free classes by a single fresh ordinary atom.
The quotient has at most the initial node count plus one, satisfies all rule
equations, and leaves the rigid goal exactly A. This changes intermediate
formula labels honestly. It is not an object-level substitution inference.

The fixed F rules have no atomicity/freshness side conditions; closed theorem
necessitation remains valid. Exact quotient construction and checking cost
polynomial arithmetic in the compact skeleton. Reading or validating an
original huge explicit proof still costs its full supplied byte length. The
line lower-bound application is existential normalization. QED.

Generic circuit/Frege/EF compression is prior art. Jeřábek's2008 author version,
published2009, Propositions3.2–3.3 already provides closely related relations
for modal and superintuitionistic logics. The lemma above reconstructs why
the finite schematic argument also applies to this particular affine system.

## Theorem 3: standard acyclic definitional extensions

Let EF add defining axioms `(e_i->B_i) AND (B_i->e_i)`, where e_i is absent
from its own body, all earlier proof lines/hypotheses, and the final target.
All defining bodies are ordinary finite formulas. Then a size-S EF proof of
an original-language target has an unfolded ordinary F-proof encoded by
O(S) common syntax nodes and O(S) source steps.

**Proof.** A later extension atom cannot occur in an earlier defining body,
by its later freshness condition. Expand definitions in chronological order
by redirecting extension leaves to acquired earlier body roots. This creates
no constructor cycle or recursive copying. Each defining axiom becomes
`(B_exp->B_exp) AND (B_exp->B_exp)`, with a fixed-length derivation from identity
and the source's derived ordinary adjunction. All remaining source axioms and
rules are preserved by the one global substitution; the final target remains
unchanged. Apply Theorem1 to the resulting ordinary proof graph. QED.

The same argument gives O(E) ordinary source lines from E EF lines, regardless
of expanded formula sizes; combined with Theorem2 it also inherits the stated
hard-family line lower bound under this exact EF definition. The principal
robustness assertion here is the total certificate-size lower bound. Neither
unrestricted substitution Frege nor semantic circuit-equivalence rules are
included. The executable definition-redirection primitive is not a complete
arbitrary EF freshness/trace checker; these hypotheses are explicit theorem
assumptions.

## Corollary: the authors' hard family remains hard after naming

Let Theta*_n be the conservative polynomial-size clique/color formula family

occurrences, or O(L+|Theta*_n|^2) using the original source repair. Thus

    L >= 2^(n^Omega(1)).

Theorem3 gives the same exponential lower bound on total size of standard
acyclic definitional LLW-EF certificates. The recent source already provides

is restored. Fixed finite standard Frege presentations for the same logic
simulate one another at constant line overhead by substituting fixed rule

including the specified extensions, not about the general classical Frege or
EF lower-bound problem.

## Decisive obstruction and discriminators

Directly translating an extension atom as an ordinary atom loses its negative
component. In the exact affine Łukasiewicz model, p=3/4 and e=1/2 satisfy the
definition e equivalent to p*p with value1, while `t(e->p*p)` has value3/4.
This invalidates raw extension translation and requires unfold-first handling.
The full fractions and a crisp exponential interpretation are retained in
the primitive and independent gate artifacts.

The exact tests also reject unrestricted linear contraction, promotion with
an unmodalized antecedent, constructor mismatch, quotient cycles, forward
proof references, and self/future definitions. A depth96 implication comb has
small input syntax but exponential translated tree size; its pair proofs
still require385 rows. A64-definition chain has65 shared body nodes and
`2^65-1` unfolded nodes. These are concrete syntax/resource discriminators;
they are not a finite proof of the asymptotic lower bound.

## Costs, uses and unresolved scope

Constructing the shared compiler from a supplied valid compact proof takes
polynomial time, and its output references have O(log(m+L)) bits. Add the total
input atom-name encoding length separately. Certificate encoding is therefore
`O((m+L)log(m+L)+atom_bytes)`. Explicitly printing unfolded formula trees can
take exponential time and bytes; this is excluded from the compact output
claim. Acquiring a short source proof is not supplied by the compiler.

The strongest equally informed older comparator already has generic circuit
and extension sharing. The new load-bearing step is preserving that sharing
through exact Chu proofs and conservative repair while respecting affine
contexts. No speed advantage, discovery algorithm, or measured theorem-prover
improvement is asserted. A concrete research use is a stronger starting
separation for studying how much controlled contraction is needed to regain
efficient proofs: naming and linear exponentials alone do not evade it.
