NEXT HIGH-CEILING ATTACK

Do not return to direct scalar Foster domination, factorial-Lyapunov repair,
or deterministic permanence. The remaining mathematical bottleneck is what
happens when the stochastic top tier is not contained in the deterministic
top tier because a source complex is disabled on a boundary stratum while
still winning the deterministic order. For each such LP witness, distinguish
unreachable strata from strata visited by an infinite communicating-class
sequence. Then either:

- build a rate-weighted graph of tier/path states that produces a finite-step
  Lyapunov decrease despite the tier mismatch, with exact control of the
  lowest path probability and the physical-time total-rate clock; or
- construct a genuinely WR counterexample with a positive-probability,
  nonperiodic escape mechanism and a proved finite sum of physical holding
  times (for explosion), or a recurrence/transience classification.

The direct closure of the known non-WR explosive word is blocked: its added
return reaction is order n^3 against order n^2 escape and makes the intended
word's probability zero. Avoid repeating that route. A failure of the tier
condition is not itself evidence of nonrecurrence.
