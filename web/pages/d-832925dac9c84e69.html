# Root reconstruction of the logarithmic regularity extension

7 October 2026. Internal mathematical audit, not external validation or historical priority. The candidate is in `notes/r15_geometric_analysis/cycle6/LOG_FREQUENCY_REGULARITY.md`; its base weighted construction has separate full reconstructions by r07 and r12. I read and reconstructed both complete notes. No failed implication was found. The following records the load-bearing all-order points rather than treating agreement or finite tests as proof.

Fix a dimension n>=3. Write R=(K^2+|x|^2)^(1/2), a=log R, ell=(R/a)^(1/2), kappa=a^(3/2)R^(-1/2), eta=(Ra)^(-1/2), and Phi=R log R-R-(K log K-K). The parameter K is chosen once from the zeroth-order geometry, sparsity, and repair estimates. All later derivative constants may depend on their order and this fixed K. No derivative-order-dependent choice of the constructed functions is permitted or needed.

## Jet estimates and the cancellation that must be preserved

For every multiindex nu, the radial chain rule gives derivatives of a and ell bounded by C_nu times their value times R^(-|nu|). Derivatives of Phi of positive order j are bounded by C_j a R^(1-j). Consequently D^j exp(-Phi) is bounded by C_j a^j exp(-Phi). These are estimates with unrestricted finite constants, not analytic or Gevrey bounds.

In the locally finite patch sum, differentiation of cutoffs costs ell^(-1)<=a and differentiation of waves costs a_i comparable to a. One must first cancel the a^2 q term against the radial exponential. The residual Delta q+a^2 q has base terms ell^(-2), a/ell, and a^2 ell/R, all at most C kappa. For k>=1, the frequency mismatch coefficient has derivatives bounded by C_k a^2/R^k <= C_k kappa a^k.

The remaining tangential coefficient is h_i=a_i(a/R)(x dot t_i). Tangency at the patch center makes its undifferentiated size O(a^2 ell/R)=O(kappa). Its kth derivatives have sizes at most C_k a^2(ell/R^(k+1)+1/R^k), both at most C_k kappa a^k. The second summand has ratio (a R)^(1/2-k) to the target. Keeping the regularized-origin residual -K^2 a^2/R^2, instead of discarding it, introduces only a finite C_K after K has been fixed. Thus all derivatives of Delta f have the required bound C_(m,K) kappa a^m E.

## Smoothing with one fixed construction

The weighted repair supplies |W|<=C_K kappa a^(-2)E for a possibly nonsmooth zero extension. Its derivatives are never taken. For the variable smoothing kernel t^n psi(t(x-y)), t=a/sigma, each x derivative of total order j has size at most C_j a^(n+j) on support of volume O(a^(-n)). Integration against W proves the order-j bound C_(j,K) kappa a^(j-2)E. Taking j=m+2 bounds the mth derivative of its Laplacian.

For the smooth part, let Y=x-epsilon(x)z. For j>=2, |D^jY|<=C_j/(a R^j)<=C_j a^(j-1), while DY is bounded. A Faà di Bruno term containing k derivatives of f and jets of Y of total order m therefore costs at most a^k a^(m-k)=a^m. Applying this accounting to the canceled Delta f and to each of the exact Laplacian commutators retains kappa. The three commutator base scales are a/R, R^(-2), R^(-2), all dominated by kappa. This gives |D^m F|<=C_(m,K)a^m E and |D^m Delta F|<=C_(m,K)kappa a^m E.

The finite-order constants never enter a smallness requirement. A fixed smooth cutoff/mollifier has finite constants for every order; the already fixed K, tau, rho and sigma therefore define a single pair of smooth functions satisfying all of these estimates.

## Quotient, scope, and a useful finite-jet consequence

On the open small-value region the smoothed function remains harmonic. On its complement, |F|>=c tau E. The definitions V=0 on the first open set and V=Delta F/F wherever F is nonzero agree on the overlap. Induction in the multiindex Leibniz identity VF=Delta F yields |D^mV|<=C_(m,K)kappa a^m. This includes the interface, where the denominator is bounded below. Thus every fixed-order derivative tends to zero. The solution decays as exp(-c r log r), faster than every fixed exponential; this statement alone does not supply decay exp(-c r^(1+epsilon)).

The same derivative bookkeeping applies to the power construction a=R^alpha, kappa=R^(-beta), alpha=(1-2beta)/3. Hence D^mV is bounded by C_(m,K)R^(m alpha-beta). For any specified finite N, choosing N/(2N+3)<beta<1/2 makes all derivatives through order N tend to zero while preserving fixed superlinear-power decay. Equivalently one can choose any 1<p<1+1/(2N+3), with beta=(4-3p)/2. This corollary does not interchange 'for every N there exists a construction' with 'there exists one power-decay construction for every N.'
