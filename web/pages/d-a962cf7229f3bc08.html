## Source-backed prescribed treatment allocation

Yamin–Kennedy–Wilder, UAI 2025/PMLR 286:4681–4700, Section 3, asks for binary assignments with an exact integer treatment count and prescribed marginal probabilities p_i. Their Section 4.1/Proposition 1 explains why this keeps the IPW estimate of the sample treatment effect unbiased. They study infant-health and public-housing semi-synthetic examples in Section 6.4. These are actual published allocation interfaces, not labels invented for D. The publisher PDF and arXiv:2506.12677v1 are both saved; the publisher page confirms the conference date 21–25 July 2025.

For known rational covariates a_i and target p, define P as the rank-B uniform matroid base polytope. Normalize each feature column in Euclidean norm, charging preprocessing. A short-edge exact-marginal law adds the deterministic promise

    sum_i Z_i=B,    E Z_i=p_i,
    |sum_i a_ji(Z_i-p_i)|<hard feature radius for every realized allocation.

For 0<p_i<1 and fixed potential outcomes, the usual IPW statistic is linear in Z, so E Z=p alone gives exact design-based unbiasedness. The covariate guarantee controls chosen known covariate sums on each allocation; it makes no bound on unknown outcomes without an additional outcome model.

The comparison decisively weakens D's priority on this interface. The constraint is uniform, hence has an efficient all-subset exchange construction. The cycle-9 transfer of Li's finite-bit signing theorem already yields a favorable hard radius <198 and an exact law via the standard extractor. The general kT floating-LP method gives 2T; its original-face quotient LP can reduce T further. D's improved arbitrary-matroid radius 737 is weaker. Swap/pipage rounding also preserves marginals and the count with negative correlation, a property neither the D nor the transferred-Li hard-law interface promises. These algorithms should be compared on the property needed by the allocation study, with the same covariates and target p.

### Source pseudocode rejection and corrected comparator

The source's stated interface must be separated from its written implementation. Algorithm 1, Case 2, in both arXiv v1 and the publisher PDF (printed p4683), uses (1-p_i)/(2-p_i-p_j) as the probability of setting coordinate i to one when p_i+p_j>1. The marginal-preserving probability is instead (1-p_j)/(2-p_i-p_j), obtained by solving the two-endpoint mean equation. The published expression exchanges the two coordinates' conditional means.

For p=(4/5,2/5,4/5), budget 2, always choose the first two fractional coordinates. The as-written algorithm's complete exact law is: mass 1/5 on (1,1,0), 1/5 on (1,0,1), and 3/5 on (0,1,1). Its mean is (2/5,4/5,4/5), violating the prescribed first two marginals. With the corrected probability the masses are 1/5,3/5,1/5 and the mean equals p. Both outcome trees are saved in the exact fixture output. This is a specific versioned pseudocode rejection, not a claim about the authors' unretrieved experiment implementation. The competent comparator remains standard pipage with the independently verified endpoint probabilities from Harvey–Olver's Appendix A; it does not inherit this source error.

### A decisive variance rejection, independently derived

Take n=2r units, p_i=1/2, and exactly r treatments. Let a law put probability 1/2 on treating the first r units and 1/2 on treating the last r units. It has the prescribed marginals, the exact budget, and zero discrepancy for the unit-column budget feature a_i=1. Set Y_i(1)=1 for the first block and zero for the last, and Y_i(0)=0 for all units. The IPW statistic is sum_first Z_i/r. Its mean is 1/2 and variance is 1/4. Independent Bernoulli assignments have the same mean and variance 1/(4r). The variance ratio is r, unbounded as r grows.

This rejects the implication “exact means plus outcome-wise feature balance gives swap-rounding variance improvement.” It is not a counterexample to swap rounding or to the existence of some better D law. Exact Fraction fixtures r=2,8,64 are saved in `results/rounding_prior/cycle10/exact_checks.json`; the formulas above prove the whole family. No clinical outcome claim follows from D alone.

## Source-backed general matroid measurement design

Madan–Nikolov–Singh–Tantipongpipat, arXiv:2004.07886v1, Introduction “Experimental Design,” studies measurements with known v_e, independent unit-variance Gaussian noise, and a feasible matroid basis S. The least-squares covariance is M_S^(-1), M_S=sum_{e in S}v_e v_e^T. Their main estimation and deterministic approximation claims depend on feature dimension d rather than matroid rank. The deterministic proof is in `deterministic.tex`; their rounding does not promise a law with a prescribed mean. We use this established measurement model, adding a clearly specified prescribed-inclusion law interface of our own.

### Exact downstream deduction and its margin

Input rational v_e in Q^d, the same ordinary matroid independence oracle, and rational y in its base polytope. Put M_y=sum y_e v_e v_e^T. Choose a rational invertible preconditioner W, or simply W=I, with all acquisition and computation charged equally to the comparators. Let

    u_e=W v_e,  C_e=u_e u_e^T,
    L=max_e ||u_e||_2²,
    S_y=W M_y W^T.

Use full d² entry vectorization. Each feature column vec(C_e) has Euclidean norm ||u_e||²<=L, a rational normalization. The original-face quotient can instead provide a certified representation X with maximum column norm rho, preserving vec(M_b-M_y) for every b in F(y). It may remove information components fixed by the quota/face constraints.

Under the improved arbitrary-matroid theorem, every atom of the exact-mean law then satisfies

    ||W(M_b-M_y)W^T||_op <=737 d rho.

The factor d is a conservative consequence of bounding all d² entries. Suppose an exact certificate establishes S_y>=mu I, with rational mu>0, and

    epsilon=737 d rho/mu<1.

Then the perturbation is >=-epsilon S_y. Congruence by W^(-1) gives, on **every** law atom,

    M_b >=(1-epsilon) M_y,
    M_b^(-1) <=(1-epsilon)^(-1) M_y^(-1),
    det M_b >=(1-epsilon)^d det M_y.

Also E M_b=M_y exactly. This converts a hard additive feature law into a robust covariance/information guarantee under a verified margin. Exact LP decomposition supplies the explicit law; no independence claim about its items is used. This is a concrete downstream use of short-edge Euclidean rounding, conditional on the margin and with a prescribed-mean requirement. It is not a general approximation to optimal A/E/D design.

### Matched comparators and costs

| Method | Same supplied interface | Relevant guarantee / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Original-face quotient kT | Exact matroid optimization from independence, same y and information columns | Physical entry radius 2 Lambda(F,vec C), with Lambda the explicitly optimized max-column l1 norm; operator radius <=2d Lambda. Hard exact law is obtained by the same extractor. |
| Li transfer for an efficient exchange class | Same instance plus an actually available all-subset complementary exchange procedure | Hard entry radius <198 rho and exact law. Uniform/explicit partition applications already have this stronger comparator. Independence alone does not supply the procedure for all matroids. |
| Matrix pipage | Matroid independence and the same PSD C_e,y | Harvey–Olver, arXiv:1307.2274v1, gives exact marginals and a matrix upper-tail bound; a deterministic good basis is available when the pessimistic bound is below one. This is a tail/one-basis guarantee, rather than a hard guarantee on every atom of an exact-mean law. Only the inspected upper-tail result is adopted here. |
| Design-specific approximation | Same measurement vectors and matroid | The MNS estimation/approximation interface optimizes the determinant. It should be preferred if optimal design, rather than an exact prescribed-inclusion hard law, is the actual objective. Its stated factors were verified in the primary statement and relevant proofs were read; the complete convex-program numerical implementation was not audited. |
| Direct row box / structure-specific optimization | Same original face, y, and input structure | Often makes the spectral construction unnecessary. The n134 trace and the six-item centering fixture are in this category. |

Since Gamma<=Lambda<=d Gamma for the d² information features, an exact Gamma certificate can beat the quotient-kT **guarantee** only when Lambda>737 Gamma/2. Necessarily d>737/2, so d>=369. Using the conservative 25/16 Gamma acquisition guarantee raises this necessary dimension to 576. These are necessary conditions for this particular guarantee comparison, not proof that D wins in that dimension; actual Lambda, row boxes, matrix tails, covariance margin, and running costs remain decisive. At low d or sparse information features the old kT method is stronger. No superiority was observed on an application data set.

## Recent hardness prevents an unqualified application claim

Bansal–Xu, arXiv:2608.05468v1, 5 August 2026, Theorem 1.1, proves that assuming P!=NP, A/E-design under partition constraints has no polynomial-time approximation factor 2^(poly(d)+(1-epsilon)B) for any fixed epsilon in (0,1), where B is coordinate bit length. I read the full reduction and inverse/gap proof, not only the abstract.

Their construction has one two-choice part for each 3-DM triple plus forced element and special vectors. For each Boolean choice x its inverse contains R²(Ax-1), giving an inverse norm gap between YES and NO instances. All bases are nonsingular, so the obstruction is not merely infinite costs. This reinforces why the positive information margin above must be checked; an additive discrepancy theorem cannot be advertised as an unconditional efficient A/E approximation.

## Decision and next falsifiable step

The published fixed-budget treatment-allocation interface establishes real value for exact marginals and hard covariate balance, but already has stronger short-edge priors than D. Additionally, the energy/screening calculation in `AFFINE_RADIUS.md` proves that screened kT already meets radius 737 whenever n<737²/2=271584.5. Both inspected published cohorts (747 infant-health units and 3163 public-housing individuals) lie below that threshold. They cannot demonstrate a guaranteed-radius advantage of D at the current constants. The general-matroid measurement interface supplies a precise conditional covariance benefit; its strong margin and matched quotient-kT comparison substantially restrict a credible advantage. Retain it as a downstream gate, not as an established application breakthrough.

A discriminating experiment would freeze public covariates/vectors, rational target y, its constraint oracle and acquisition costs; compute Gamma certificates, the exact Lambda LP, direct row ranges and an information-margin certificate before running any spectral path. Admit D only if a relevant hard-law guarantee beats those controls and all charged complexity fits local resources. No such full data/target package or high-spectral execution has been acquired in this cycle. The immediate practical obstacle remains the fallback step size; decreasing K only changes its enormous fixed-step count by a polynomial constant factor.
