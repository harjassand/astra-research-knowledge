# Independent gate of the finite prime-capacity PIT composition

## Reconstructed assumptions

A fixed input is sum_j f_j(X)exp(g_j(X)/h_j(X)), with integer polynomial
data of total degree<=d, nonzero h_j and distinct rational fractions.
The latter means every D_jt=g_jh_t-g_th_j is a nonzero integer polynomial.
An integer w>=2 bounds coefficients of f_j,h_j,D_jt. k>=1; n=0 is legal
for a constant instance. The promised height/degree and condensation are
part of the interface, not acquired from a general circuit for free.

The exact oracle must accept changing primes q,p, order-q element a,
v in F_q^n and u in F_p^n. Inner fraction arithmetic is modulo q and
outer coefficient arithmetic modulo p. Its answer is
sum f_j(u)a^[g_j(v)/h_j(v) modq], or undefined on a zero denominator.
It is a specialized algebraic interface. Ordinary real exponentials or
a fixed-field-only implementation do not supply it.

Outer characteristics Q are distinct certified primes q>k. Each q has
a fixed, acquired set S_q of distinct certified primes p=1 modq, plus
an order-q generator. Each pool may depend on q and promised parameters;
it is fixed independently of v and the hidden coefficient witness.
q is sampled with weight floorlog2q, p with weight floorlog2p, v,u
uniformly in their fields, and a uniformly among the q-1 nonidentity
qth roots. Input is fixed before those random choices. The pools may
be reused in fresh independent trials.

## Integer determinant and root-multiplicity budget: PASS

For an integer matrix A of modular nullity r at prime p, pick rank(A modp)
independent rows and put them first. Subtract their integer-lifted modular
linear combinations from the other rows. Those r rows become multiples
of p, preserving detA. Thus p^r divides detA. This proof needs no computed
Smith form and covers complete modular annihilation.

For nonzero C in Z[z] of degree<=q-1, support<q and l1 norm<=W, irreducibility
of Phi_q makes Res(Phi_q,C) a nonzero integer R. The degree condition
forces any nonzero multiple of Phi_q to have all q coefficients, contrary
to support<q. Its complex-root product gives |R|<=W^(q-1).

For p=1 modq, p!=q and Phi_q has q-1 distinct linear factors over F_p.
Multiplication by C on Z[z]/Phi_q has determinant R and modular nullity
equal to r_p, the number of nonidentity qth roots at which C vanishes.
Consequently p^r_p divides R. Distinct primes within a pool give

    sum_p r_p log2p <= log2|R| <= (q-1)log2W.

Weighted prime sampling then has exact root failure
sum_p floorlog2p*r_p/[T_p(q)(q-1)]<=ceil(log2W)/T_p(q).
The root at1 is excluded, and whole-polynomial annihilation modulo p is
included with r_p=q-1. Neither a prime-density theorem nor computing R
is required for this soundness argument.

## Characteristic selection and conditioning: PASS

For each h_j and each D_jt choose one fixed nonzero integer coefficient
before choosing q. A coefficient c with |c|<=w has
sum_{q in Q,q|c} floorlog2q<=log2|c|. There are
L=k+binom(k,2) such witnesses, so bad characteristic mass is at most
L*ceil(log2w)/T_q. These witnesses are existential proof data, not queries
the tester must secretly obtain.

For each good q, independent random v incurs at most
[kd+2d*binom(k,2)]/q=dk^2/q for a denominator zero or two exponent
residues colliding. On the complement, alpha_j=g_j(v)/h_j(v) are distinct.

Fix one monomial whose coefficient vector eta_j=[X^mu]f_j is nonzero.
Its integer l1 norm is at most kw. The polynomial
C_(q,v)(z)=sum eta_j z^alpha_j has distinct residue positions, degree<=q-1,
support<=k<q and norm<=kw. This is a **coefficient extraction before u
evaluation**. Integer f_j(u) values would not satisfy this norm bound.
The vector eta is independent of p; q,v only change its exponent positions.

p may be drawn before v in the written algorithm because, conditional
on q, their random choices are independent. We can equivalently condition
on q,v and then integrate over the unchanged weighted p pool. If the
outer polynomial H_(a,v)(X)=sum f_j(X)a^alpha_j vanishes identically over
F_p, its selected coefficient C_(q,v)(a) vanishes. The preceding resultant
budget therefore bounds that event by ceil(log2kw)/T_p(q). Otherwise its
degree<=d and independent random u costs at most d/p. This remains valid
if many f coefficients disappear modulo p; no p-dependent coefficient
witness was chosen.

Combining the events yields exactly

    error <= L*b_w/T_q + dk^2/q_min + d/p_min
                                  + b_(kw)/T_p,min.

With q_min>max(k,6d(k^2+1)), T_q>=6L*b_w and every T_p(q)>=6b_(kw),
the two capacity terms are <=1/6 each. Since p>q, the two degree terms
sum to <1/6. Thus error<1/2, and fresh independent repetitions give
error<2^-t for a fixed input. Positive capacities/pools are necessary.

For k>=1 and w>=2, the large-q corollary also checks: q>2kw implies each
used p>2kw, so floorlog2p>=ceil(log2kw); three distinct progression primes
give root error<=1/3 and degree error<1/6.

## Perfect completeness and prior boundary: PASS

If an exponential-polynomial identity with distinct rational fractions
had a nonzero f, choose a rational point avoiding all h_j,D_jt and that
f. The finite union of nonzero-polynomial zero sets cannot contain all
rational points. The evaluated rational exponents are distinct, and the
algebraic coefficients are not all zero. Lindemann--Weierstrass linear
independence contradicts the identity. Hence all f_j are zero and every
defined algebraic query returns zero; undefined queries are accepted.
The zero-characterization prerequisite is explicit, rather than replaced
by numerical testing of real exponentials.

## Unadopted implementation claims and decisive falsifiers

This proof gate supplies no pool-acquisition time bound. Enumerating and
certifying progression primes, modular generator construction, reading
large constants, parameterized-oracle evaluation and query traffic all
remain charged. A polynomial-in-bit-height acquisition claim requires
its separate number-theoretic proof and actual algorithm. I have not
read/imported the producer's code or independently replayed the candidate
logs in this gate. No compiler or end-to-end speedup follows.

Repeated primes cannot add capacity; a composite q can have a sparse
polynomial divisible by its cyclotomic polynomial; choosing C after p
can annihilate it at every prime; and replacing coefficient extraction
by a large integer evaluation breaks W<=kw. These are actual mathematical
falsifiers of stronger unsupported statements. They are excluded by the
checked interface, not treated as mere implementation details.
