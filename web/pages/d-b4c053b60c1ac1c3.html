## Reconstructed confidence proof and predictable data use

With past filtration H_(k−1), t_k and current state x_k known before Y_k, let p_Q,k(y)=[exp(t_k Q)]_(x_k,y). Let q_k be any past-measurable positive probability vector. Define M_K=∏q_k(Y_k), L_K(Q)=∏p_Q,k(Y_k). All positive-lag transition probabilities are positive because the baseline is strongly connected, including b=0. Thus

    E_Q[(M_K/L_K)/(M_(K−1)/L_(K−1)) | H_(k−1)]
      = Σ_y p_Q,k(y) q_k(y)/p_Q,k(y) = 1.

The common parameter-independent adaptive action law cancels. Ville therefore bounds ever crossing 1/δ by δ; inversion C_K={Q:L_K(Q)≥δM_K} gives simultaneous-in-time coverage of true Q. There is no finite-grid approximation and no union bound over Q: this is a pointwise-in-truth probability statement inverted into a random set.

Dirichlet(1,1,1) separately per (starting state, chosen lag) cell predicts (C_y+1)/(N+3). Its ordered-sequence product per cell is 2!∏C_y!/(N+2)!, even when cell visits are adaptively interleaved. Consequently M=1/D with exact integer D=∏[binom(N+2,2) multinomial(N;C)]. The multinomial factor is part of the inverse-predictor identity; the observed sequence likelihood has no such factor.

The structured implementation uses the fitted generator from the **preceding** 1024-reading block. The previous-fit object remains fixed while the current block is collected; its rational prediction is reconstructed from that object, the lag and current state. Floating fitted probabilities are floored at scale 2^52, each integer weight is at least one, then divided by the exact row sum. They are normalized, positive and predictable even if the fit or floating semigroup is inaccurate. Current-block fitting creates the next block's predictor. Retrospective normalized vectors chosen after an endpoint would fail this proof; no such step appears in the checked recursion. The fixed half-mixture of two sequence predictors has a martingale ratio too; post-hoc maximization without the mixture penalty would not.

Historical structured-predictor comparisons are deterministic width audits of a specified recursion. They are not an independent confidence-validation population; data-driven selection of a new recursion would require a fresh predeclared experiment or an appropriate mixture/selection penalty.

## Global continuous-Q projection and n=3 path bounds

For each visited cell use empirical row p̂, N observations, and separate-cell saturated likelihood L_sat. Exactly

    log L_sat − log L(Q) = Σ_cells N KL(p̂ || P_Q).

Accepted Q makes this at most B=log L_sat−log M+log(1/δ). Each term is nonnegative. Grouping nonsuccess outcomes gives N_0 kl(p̂_02||P_Q,02)≤B for each starting-0 cell. The code uses an outward upper budget (upper log L_sat, lower log M and lower log δ); this enlarges the necessary set. Bisection moves a boundary inward only after certifying exclusion by a lower KL bound. Ambiguous arithmetic stops with outer endpoints. Endpoint q=0 or 1 is handled separately; zero-count cells impose no restriction. An empty relaxation is a confidence failure/UNKNOWN event, not proof that the physical class is impossible.

Let λ=13/5=2.6 bound all escape rates. A path consisting of exactly the rare jump and no others has probability ≥b t e^(−λt). The disjoint path consisting of exactly 0→1→2 and no other jumps has probability ≥(7/10)^2 t² e^(−λt)/2. The two ordered jump-time integrals are simplex volumes t and t²/2. Therefore

    P_Q,02(t) ≥ e^(−λt)[bt + 49t²/200].

For an upper bound couple the process to a global added-edge clock of rate b. Its probability of ringing by t is ≤bt. Without such a clock event, a baseline path ending at 2 must contain an ordered 0→1 then 1→2 pair; bounding both intensities by 13/10 gives expected pair count ≤169t²/200, including loops. Thus

    P_Q,02(t) ≤ bt + 169t²/200.

If certified binary KL inversion gives P_Q,02∈[l_t,u_t], every accepted b lies in

    [max(0,(l_t−169t²/200)/t),
     min(1,e^(λt)u_t/t−49t/200)].

Intersection across visited lags remains an outer projection over all continuous ordinary rates. The code uses the lower enclosure of survival e^(−λt) in the reciprocal for the upper b endpoint, and outward rounding of final b bounds. This is a graph/envelope-specific necessary condition, not a profile-optimizer approximation or iid-binomial confidence claim.

For 0<L≤U, the harmonic point h=2LU/(L+U) satisfies max_(b∈[L,U]) |h/b−1|=(U−L)/(U+L), attained at endpoints. The report test L>0 and width ratio≤1/4 therefore proves its protected claim on the confidence event. At b=0 a positive report is already outside that event; the assertion should be understood through the all-b relative inequality |h−b|≤b/4, rather than dividing by zero. Budget exhaustion and empty intervals return UNKNOWN. A final incomplete batch retains every observed count/cost but may return the last interval from a preceding completed check; that earlier confidence interval remains valid at its earlier time, so the statement is safe without pretending to use all final observations.

## Reconstructed arithmetic certificate

The load-bearing factorial enclosure is not merely a truncated asymptotic expansion. Binet's positive-real formula, NIST DLMF 5.9.10, and

    atan x = x−x³/3 + ∫_0^x u^4/(1+u²)du,
    0≤remainder≤x^5/5   (all x≥0)

give, after integration against 2/(e^(2πs)−1),

    S_2(n) ≤ log(n!) ≤ S_2(n)+1/(1260n^5),
    S_2(n)=(n+1/2)log n−n+(1/2)log(2π)+1/(12n)−1/(360n³).

The needed positive moments are ∫s^r/(e^(2πs)−1)ds=r!ζ(r+1)/(2π)^(r+1), for r=1,3,5 giving 1/24,1/240,1/504 before prefactors. This independently checks sign and constant. Code uses n≥32; below 32 factorials are exact integers. Machin's π identity and forty alternating atan terms have the correct signed next-term enclosure; positive coefficients and subtraction reverse endpoints correctly.

For log x, write x=2^k y, 1≤y<2. Outward 96-bit y endpoints stay in [1,2]. With z=(y−1)/(y+1)∈[0,1/3], forty-eight odd terms end at z^95/95 and leave a positive tail ≤2z^97/[97(1−z²)]. At integer scale S=2^100, floor/ceiling multiplication bounds each nonnegative power inductively. After the last multiplication the upper integer p_h bounds z^97; the integer tail ceil(2 p_h S/[97(S−z2_h)]) has the right units and positive denominator. Multiplying the enclosed log2 by negative k correctly swaps endpoints. Final 60-bit rounding is outward. This proves inclusion for every positive rational input, not only the finite reference comparisons.

Candidate semigroup witnesses use rational uniformization at rate 4, which dominates λ. Positive matrix-series partial sums, a geometrically bounded positive tail, and an alternating exp(−x/16) enclosure raised to the sixteenth power certify transition entries for the stated lag range. The audit did not promote numerical witness exclusions to global b exclusions.
