STRONGEST CLAIM
There is an explicit sparse rational family of the actual hard-BCS matrix class whose signed ambient-hole polynomial cannot be represented by one constant-kernel determinant with each physical variable entering through a rank-one update, regardless of the number of fixed auxiliary modes or whether the kernel is Hermitian. A positive mixture of constant-parity Hermitian determinant boundary signatures preserving ALL hole coefficients requires at least ceil((4/3)^q) components for q independent four-site blocks. Thus the known chiral mixed derivative cannot generally be replaced by a polynomial-size positive mixture of those directly evaluable Gaussian/determinantal primitives.

This excludes a representation class distinct from the prior nonnegative matching/windability and shared-core-cut obstructions. It does not exclude a scalar-only reduction, a kernel with variable-dependent rational entries, higher-rank/cancellation determinant gadgets, an interacting auxiliary law, a localized flow, mismatch-register purification, or a general determinant-parity FPRAS. The block instances themselves are easy.

INTERFACE AND EXPLICIT WITNESS
For F in C^(n x n) and even R define Z_R(F)=sum_{I subset R, |I|=|R|/2}|det F[I,R\I]|^2, with Z_empty=1 and odd R zero. Write Q_F(x)=sum_R (-1)^(|R|/2) Z_R x^(V\R). Baseline proves Q_F is multiaffine real stable via a chiral determinant and positive mixed derivatives; that stability is not newly claimed here.
Take n=4 and F0=J4-I4: all twelve off-diagonal entries are one; diagonal entries zero. Every two-retained partition equals 2. Every complementary two-by-two minor on all four sites is the all-ones matrix and has determinant zero. Consequently Z_[4]=0 and
 Q0=x1x2x3x4-2 sum_{i<j}x_i x_j.
(The complement of a two-set is another two-set, so no indexing ambiguity remains.)
This uses unit entries; failure is not precision or a large-weight effect.

LEMMA 1: NO CONSTANT COMPLEX KERNEL
Suppose Q0=det(diag(x1,x2,x3,x4)+A) for an arbitrary complex 4x4 A. Comparing principal-minor coefficients gives Aii=0, Aij Aji=2 for every pair, every principal three-minor zero, and det A=0.
Diagonal similarity preserves all principal minors. Normalize A1j=i sqrt(2), Aj1=-i sqrt(2) for j=2,3,4; this is legal because every pair product is nonzero. For the principal triangle {1,i,j}, its determinant is
 A1i Aij Aj1+A1j Aji Ai1=2(Aij+Aji).
Thus Aji=-Aij. The pair products then give Aij^2=-2, so Aij=+/- i sqrt(2). All entries now have the form A=iK with K real skew-symmetric and every upper off-diagonal entry +/-sqrt(2). Therefore
 det A=det K=Pf(K)^2
 = [K12 K34-K13 K24+K14 K23]^2
 =4 or 36,
because the bracket is twice the sum of three numbers in {+1,-1}. This contradicts det A=0. The argument is elementary and excludes non-Hermitian constant kernels as well.

POSITIVE NONZERO ROBUST VARIANT
Put F_delta=F0+delta E12, rational 0<delta<=1/4. Exact enumeration gives
 Z_[4]=2 delta^2,
 Z_12=2+2delta+delta^2,
 Z_ij=2 for the other five pairs,
 and every odd Z_R=0.
A hypothetical constant complex determinant kernel is again diagonally similar to iK real skew, now |Kij|=sqrt(Z_ij). Its three Pfaffian-term magnitudes are
 p=sqrt(4+4delta+2delta^2), 2, 2.
As 2<=p<=2+2delta, every signed sum of those magnitudes has absolute value at least 4-p>=2-2delta. Hence det A >=(2-2delta)^2>2delta^2, contradiction. Choosing delta=2^-L gives a positive requested full norm and an O(L)-bit input, still with no exact such representation. No asymptotic numerical simulation is used.

LEMMA 2: FIXED AUXILIARY MODES CANNOT REPAIR A RANK-ONE PHYSICAL DETERMINANT
Consider any finite-dimensional representation
 Q(x)=c det(A+sum_{i=1}^n x_i u_i v_i^T),
where A,u_i,v_i are fixed complex data and [x1...xn]Q=1. The top coefficient being nonzero forces the u_i to be linearly independent and independently the v_i to be linearly independent: the coefficient is alternating multilinear in each family. By invertible left/right transformations place u_i and v_i^T on the first n coordinate axes. The transformed pencil becomes
 [ X+B  C ; D  E ], X=diag(x).
Its x1...xn coefficient is a nonzero scalar times det E, so E is invertible. Schur complement reduces the polynomial, after normalization of its top coefficient, to
 Q(x)=det(X+B-C E^-1 D).
Thus any number of fixed auxiliary modes and any nonorthogonal rank-one boundary directions reduce to a constant n-by-n kernel. Lemma 1 rules them out for F0 and F_delta. The Hermitian/PSD rank-one case u_i v_i^T=b_i b_i* is a special case. Empty auxiliary block has determinant one.
This lemma is not a no-go for arbitrary linear determinant circuit representations: higher-rank physical coefficients can exploit cancellations and are outside this interface.

LEMMA 3: EXPONENTIAL POSITIVE DETERMINANT-MIXTURE SIZE
For q>=1 take F^(q)=diag(F0,...,F0) on q blocks V_b of size four. Its partitions factor:
 Z_R=product_b Z_(R intersect V_b)(F0).
Indeed block-diagonal complementary minors vanish unless each block has equal selected row/column counts, then determinants and squared sums multiply. Thus a retained block may be empty (weight1), a two-set (weight2), or full/odd (weight0).
Assume its full hole signature is a positive mixture
 Z_R=sum_{ell=1}^s c_ell a_ell(R), c_ell>0,
with each a_ell(R)=(-1)^(|R|/2)det A_ell[R] for even R, each odd principal minor zero, each even a_ell(R)>=0, and A_ell Hermitian. Components are monic at R=empty; sum c_ell=1. These are the exact constant-parity positive Hermitian determinant primitives under consideration.
For i,j in different blocks, target Z_ij=0. Since a_ell(ij)=|A_ell,ij|^2>=0, every A_ell,ij=0. Each A_ell is block diagonal. Target Z_(V_b)=0 and nonnegative component weights give det A_ell[V_b]=0. Odd principal minors zero give trace zero and all size-three principal minors zero. The characteristic polynomial of the Hermitian four-by-four block is therefore
 t^4 - S t^2, S=sum_{i<j}|A_ell,ij|^2>=0.
Hence each block has eigenvalues sqrt(S),-sqrt(S),0,0 and rank at most two.
For any such Hermitian rank-two zero-diagonal block write A=u u*-v v*. The zero diagonal implies |u_i|^2=|v_i|^2=p_i. Its pair matrix W_ij=|Aij|^2 has the decomposition
 W=2 p p^T - w bar(w)^T - bar(w) w^T, w_i=u_i bar(v_i).
Consequently rank W<=3, including missing-edge/zero-block cases. This derivation does not require every component support to be complete or a real-skew gauge.
Now make a 4^q-by-4^q matrix T indexed by row tuple i=(i1,...,iq) and column tuple j=(j1,...,jq). If all ib!=jb, its entry is the partition on the retained two-set {ib,jb} in each block; if some ib=jb use the corresponding odd retained block and entry0. For the target,
 T=W0 tensor ... tensor W0, W0=2(J4-I4),
 so rank T=4^q (W0 eigenvalues6,-2,-2,-2). For component ell its matrix is the tensor product of its q pair matrices, each of rank<=3, hence rank<=3^q. Rank subadditivity in the positive mixture yields
 4^q=rank T<=s 3^q, so s>=ceil((4/3)^q).
The bound permits arbitrarily large fixed auxiliary determinants in every component by Lemma 2 in the Hermitian rank-one boundary case. It excludes exact polynomial mixtures preserving all coefficients. Signed mixtures, components with nonzero odd coefficients cancelling across components, or nonlinear output relabelings are not covered.
