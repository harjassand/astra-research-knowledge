# r14: degree-preserving compression of weak demi-hardness

## Exact question

Can a barely nontrivial hard-string set for a supplied circuit G:F2^n->F2^N be compressed to an expanding range-avoidance instance without raising GF(2) algebraic degree? The sampler receives G's circuit description and dimensions, not hard-set membership/certificates. Proof hardness/security of G is assumed, not constructed.

## Main mathematical construction

For every fixed support S subset F2^N of size at least 2^k, a uniform surjective linear L:F2^N->F2^m covers S with probability at least 1-epsilon if

    k >= m + ceil(log2 m) + ceil(log2(32/epsilon^2)).

The elementary complete proof uses affine pairwise independence, random-span growth, conditional density squaring with summable failure probabilities, and an equivariant uniform kernel completion. See [complete derivation](../notes/r14_computation/LINEAR_DISPERSER_PROOF.md), Propositions A–C. A has established asymptotic prior art (Alon et al. 1999, Theorem 7); explicit reconstruction and application are our work. The generic zero-error-disperser hardness transfer is Li–Ren–Zhong, September 2026, Theorem 4.1.

**Finite proof-system consequence:** if at least 2^k strings admit no refutation of G(x)=z at a specified transformed length bound b(ell,N,m,|G|), then with probability 1-epsilon every compressed equation LG(x)=y has no refutation of length <=ell. Requires closure under the specified parity proof reduction; semantic implication alone does not suffice. This is fixed-P/fixed-threshold, not one secure generator family for all proof systems and all thresholds.

**Uniform SearchNP consequence:** under the precisely quantified weak demi-hardness assumption in Proposition C, any uniform errorless nondeterministic Avoid heuristic succeeds on at most epsilon of sampled compositions. For m=n+1, epsilon=1/2, the sufficient conservative hard-set count is 1024*n*2^n (when the ambient space is large enough). No hard-set recognition oracle appears in the construction or transfer.

Linear composition preserves GF(2) degree d exactly, and fixed-d NC0 outputs expand into XOR-of-AND_<=d functions. It can destroy NC0 locality. Matrix description/randomness mN bits, <=m(N−1) XOR2 gates, O(logN) extra balanced depth, O(mN) bit evaluation, polynomial rank check, exact rejection sampler <3.5 expected trials. Security verification is neither performed nor supplied.

An r06 refinement retains the unused error-budget slack: the same parameters work for a uniform arbitrary matrix, charging rank deficiency within epsilon. Thus the disperser and conditional transfers also admit exactly mN random bits and worst-case O(mN) matrix preparation/evaluation without rank tests or retries. The distinction between this family and the exact uniform-surjective distribution is explicit in the proof note.

## Decisive obstructions and actual computations

Proposition D: any distribution of m-output maps, each output using <=w input coordinates, that covers every k-coordinate bit-fixing support with success >=1-epsilon must have w>=(1-epsilon)N/k. This prevents retaining constant locality when k≈m and N/m is unbounded. It covers nonlinear maps and assumes no independent rows.

Proposition E: A={0,1,2,3,4,5,8,10} has A+A=F2^4. Hence S_m=A×F2^(m−3), m>=3, has size2^m in F2^(m+1) and full difference set. Every linear compression to m bits has a collision on S_m, so no such map covers. The finite seed certificate supports a universal product argument, not an extrapolated finite check.
