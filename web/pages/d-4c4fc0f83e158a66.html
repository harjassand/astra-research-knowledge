# Comparable density does not put the completion target in the uniform basin

7 October 2026. Independent reconstruction by pde_gate10, verified Sol/max; no nested workers. **PASS for the obstruction below.** It rules out a particular inference about the previously proved initializer. It does not prove that Newton iterations fail, or that retaining the prescribed nodes is impossible.

The interface is the sphere with probability area measure sigma. A prescribed set F has N distinct rational points. The new m equal-weight points must approximate w sigma, with w=1-g_L/m and

    g_L(z)=sum_(x in F) Phi_L(x,z)-N,
    Phi_L(x,z)=sum_(l=0)^(2L) h(l/L)(2l+1)P_l(x dot z).

Assume 0<=h<=1, h=1 on [0,1] and h=0 on [2,infinity). This is the low-frequency completion target in Wu–Zhuang, arXiv2609.15767v1, Lemma 3.2 and Theorem 3.1. Their theorem chooses a density-adapted cubature, not a uniform initializer. Our question is whether positivity/comparability of w alone lets one substitute the existing uniform shifted Euclidean initializer into its O(T^-2) H1-dual Newton basin.

For harmonics e_lk orthonormal in L2(sigma), lambda_l=l(l+1), define the nonconstant H1-dual norm by

    zeta(b)^2=sum_(l>=1,k) |b_lk|^2/lambda_l.

The target moments are b_lk=-h(l/L)sum_(x in F)e_lk(x)/m. Take

    u_j=j/(100NL),
    x_j=(2u_j/(1+u_j^2), 0, (1-u_j^2)/(1+u_j^2)),  1<=j<=N.

These are distinct rational unit vectors, all at distance at most 1/(50L) from the north pole. Their coordinates have O(log(NL+1)) bits. Since |P_l'|<=lambda_l/2 on [-1,1] and 1-cos theta<=theta^2/2,

    P_l(x_j dot north)>=1-lambda_l/(10000L^2)>=0.99,  1<=l<=L.

The normalized zonal harmonic e_l0=sqrt(2l+1)P_l therefore gives

    zeta(b)>=0.99(N/m) sqrt(V_L),
    V_L=sum_(l=1)^L (2l+1)/[l(l+1)]
       =2H_L+1/(L+1)-1.

Conversely, the addition theorem gives the evaluation-vector norm sqrt(V_(2L)) at every point. Triangle inequality and |h|<=1 give

    zeta(b)<= (N/m) sqrt(V_(2L)).

Thus the order (N/m)sqrt(log L) is sharp for this prescribed-node interface. This is the actual moment right-hand side, not a continuum midpoint or a surrogate integral.

Comparability remains legal. Addition-theorem Cauchy–Schwarz gives |Phi_L(x,z)-1|<=4L(L+1), hence

    ||g_L||_infinity<=4NL(L+1).

In particular m>=8NL(L+1) gives 1/2<=w<=3/2, and integral w d sigma=1 exactly. For the universal uniform construction take T=L ceil(sqrt(N)), N>=4, q=600(T+1), and m=2q^2. Then T>=2L and m>=720000NL^2, so actually |w-1|<=1/90000.

The existing rational uniform initializer has zeta(mu_start)<=12/q^2=24/m; its certified basin for a target is zeta(mu_start-b)<=1/[8(T+1)^2]=90000/m. But

    zeta(mu_start-b)>= [0.99N sqrt(V_L)-24]/m.

Whenever 0.99N sqrt(V_L)>90024, the certified basin hypothesis fails. The density can remain within 1/90000 of uniform throughout this counterexample family. The derivative sampling theorem for the uniform nodes does not change this right-hand-side mismatch.

The failed implication is therefore exact: comparable density plus the old uniform initializer does not imply the old H1-dual basin condition, at m=O(T^2). Increasing a fixed cardinality constant cannot repair the uniform implication for all prescribed F. This also does not say that a more effective inverse, a different target continuation, or an initializer depending on w cannot work.
