The Exponential Power of Cut in LK
In terms of provability LK and LK−, (i.e., LK without(cut)) are the same.
But in terms of proof complexity:
Theorem (Krajíček)
LKhas an exponential speed-up overLK −.
Inspired by this separation and in order to understand how LK or
equivalently Frege systems get their huge power, one may get interested to
capture the power of the other two structural rules, i.e., contraction and
weakening.
Our Main Theorem (informal)
Exponential separation{cut,w,c}>{cut,w}.
Sub-exponential separation{cut,w,c}>{cut,c}.
Exponential "separation"{cut}>{w,c}.

Some Extensions of the Base
We use the following abbreviations:
Proof means proof in MALL− (i.e., no structural rules).
For anyX⊆ {c,w,cut},X-proof means proof in MALL − +X(i.e.,
just the structural rules inX).
An intuitionisticX-proof means a single-conclusionX-proof.
Note that{w,c}-proof means LK −-proof and{w,c,cut}-proof means
LK-proof.

Main Theorem (formal)
(i)There is a sequence{A n}n∈N of provable formulas such that every
{cut,w}-proof ofA n has size exponential in|An|, whereasA n has
polynomial-size proofs in LK.
(ii)For every monotone sub-exponential functionf:N→N, there exists
a sequence{A n}n∈N of{c}-provable formulas with polynomial-size
LK-proofs, while the size of every{cut,c}-proof ofAn exceedsf(|A n|).
(iii)There is a sequence{S n}n∈N of provable sequents such thatSn has a
polynomial-size{cut}-proof, but requiring exponentially long
{w,c}-proofs.
Proof.
(i): feasible conservativity
(ii): vector addition system
(iii): feasible interpolation.

The First Goal
For any{cut,w}-proofπofA, there is an intuitionistic{cut,w}-proofσ
ofA t such that|σ| ≤ |π| O(1).
It is impossible as the translation may grow exponentially:
(A→B) t := (At →B t)∧(B s →A s)
(A∗B) s := (At →B s)∧(B t →A s)
involveA t,A s,B t, andB s, which in effect cause the size to roughly double
at each translation of an implication (resp. multiplication).The good news
is that this is the only problem. Hence, we can change the main goal to the
following:
Theorem
For any{cut,w}-proofπofA, there is an intuitionistic{cut,w}-proofσ
ofA t such thatl(σ)≤ |π| O(1).

Feasible Conservativity
Lemma
LetBbe a fully conservative andCbe a conservative formula. Then, there
are intuitionistic{cut,w}-proofsπ,σ, andτof the sequents
B t ⇔B B s ⇔ ⊥C t ⇔C
respectively, such thatl(π),l(σ)≤O(|B|)andl(τ)≤O(|C|).
Finally, combining the two ingredients:
Theorem
The provable sequents(⇒Θ ∗
n)haveLK-proofs of sizen O(1) , while any of
their{cut,w}-proofs has size at least2 nΩ(1)
.
