EXACT TARGET

Let H be the finite-dimensional observable OS Hilbert space of a stationary finite-alphabet path law, with unit Ω, orthogonal observation projections E_a, and self-adjoint positive semigroup T_t=e^{-tA}. A finite reversible deterministic-emission CTMC lift would give finitely many x_i∈H, positive π_i, labels a_i, and symmetric conductances w_ij≥0 such that

  <Ω,x_i>=1,             Σ_i π_i x_i x_i^T=I_H,
  E_a x_i=1_{a_i=a}x_i,   Σ_j w_ij(x_j−x_i)=−π_i A x_i.      (F1–F4)

The question was whether complete positivity of all reflected word tables, with full time consistency, forces some such reversible frame and common generator. It does not: the data below have observable rank eight, are generated by a ten-state ordinary CTMC, have a single globally consistent nonnegative factorization of every reflected word table, yet admit no F1–F4 frame.

The full exact rational matrices are frozen in `work/sol/hidden_realization/exact_certificate_v1.json`. My independent standard-library checker is `work/natural/n22_reversible_frame/verify_frame_obstruction.py`; it reads that frozen certificate and verifies the exact finite identities used here. Both ordinary and optimized Python executions return PASS. The checker is exact arithmetic evidence for matrix/geometric/Farkas identities, not a substitute for the analytic CP or closure arguments.

WHY EVERY REFLECTED WORD TABLE IS CP

For any future cylinder F, let h_F(s) be its conditional probability given hidden state X_0=s under Q. The vector h_F is nonnegative and belongs to ran(H), by QH=HL and invariance under the emission projections. Let Q* be the stationary adjoint, equal to Q^T because π is uniform, and let g_F be the conditional probability of the reflected-past word given X_0.

Relative to ran(H)⊕ran(H)^⊥, invariance gives

  Q = [[L, B_leak],[0,C]],        Q* = [[L,0],[B_leak*,C*]],

where the first block L is self-adjoint in the M metric. Every E_a is block diagonal because it is an orthogonal projection that preserves ran(H). Starting from Ω and recursively applying the reversed-past semigroup/emission operators, any component that leaks into ran(H)^⊥ under Q* cannot return to ran(H); projecting at each stage therefore gives Π_H g_F=h_F. This is a projected equality, not a pointwise equality of hidden past and future conditional laws.

Conditional independence of past and future given X_0 now yields, for every pair of future cylinders F,G,

  P(F reflected past, G future)
    = <g_F,h_G>_π
    = <h_F,h_G>_π
    = (1/10) Σ_{s=0}^9 h_F(s) h_G(s).                    (2)

The ten factors are actual nonnegative conditional word probabilities and are shared across every time list and every finite table. Thus the entire reflected cylinder kernel is completely positive, with one common ten-atom factorization. This also proves ordinary time-reversal invariance of the observed law: every full observed word probability is computed in the self-adjoint observable semigroup using self-adjoint E_a and Ω, so taking adjoints reverses the word and leaves its value unchanged. No additive-reversibilization claim is used; Q* leaks outside the observable subspace.

STATIC POSITIVE FACTORS ARE RIGID

Let C be the cone generated by nonnegative future-cylinder classes in the observable Hilbert space. A normalized positive factor point is x with <Ω,x>=1, <x,c>≥0 for c∈C, and a deterministic boundary label satisfying E_a x=x. A static factor measure μ has covariance ∫xx^T dμ=I_H.   ξ_α=<φ_α,x>.

Then ∫ξξ^T dμ=M. The boundary label and normalization force

  internal label:  ξ=(1,v.x,v.y,0^5),
  marker k label:  ξ=(0,0,0,e_k).

The marker points are unique. To force internal v into the pentagon, use positivity on the future cylinder that the observation at rational time t is marker k. Its class has coefficient vector e^{tL}e_{3+k}; for an internal ξ,

  ξ e^{tL}e_{3+k} ≥ 0,       ξ e_{3+k}=0.

Divide by t>0 and let rational t↓0. The derivative is the affine marker rate ηℓ_k(v), so ℓ_k(v)≥0 for every k and v lies in the pentagon. This uses the positive dual-cone condition, not an assumption of F4.

The second moments give μ(internal)=M_00=1/2 and

  ∫_{internal} ||v||² dμ = M_xx+M_yy = 1/2.

Every point of the inscribed pentagon has ||v||²≤1, with equality only at a vertex: for a convex combination v=Σα_i p_i,

  1−||v||² = Σ_{i<j} α_i α_j ||p_i−p_j||².

EXACT FULL-SPACE FARKAS OBSTRUCTION

  Σ_i π_i <Z_i,A x_i> ≥ 0

Indeed, pairing F4 with Z_i and summing gives the left side as Σ_{i<j}w_ij<Z_i−Z_j,x_i−x_j>. The finite-dimensional cone alternative makes (3) sufficient as well for a fixed frame, but only necessity is needed here.

FINITE-SUPPORT CYLINDER-CLOSURE AUDIT

The following closure statement is scoped to the finite-rank OS setting above. Let

  D={x∈H: <Ω,x>=1, E_a x=x for one label a, <x,c>≥0 for all c∈C},
  Γ={μ∈Prob(D): ∫xx^T dμ=I_H}.

If one finite set S⊂D supports every μ∈Γ, then target cylinder law is in the rational-cylinder closure of finite stationary reversible CTMC outputs iff a frame on S admits the symmetric flows F4. The forward implication, which excludes unbounded comparison state counts/rates, is audited as follows.

Take a sequence P_n of finite reversible outputs converging on all rational cylinders. Choose a finite cylinder basis of H and an orthonormal basis e_1,...,e_r. For each approximating hidden model, let j_n v be the same linear combination of conditional future probabilities of the basis cylinders given the hidden state. These features are uniformly bounded, since the basis is fixed and every conditional event probability lies in [0,1]. Define x_n(i) by (j_n v)(i)=<x_n(i),v>. The distributions μ_n of (x_n(i), hidden output label) live in a fixed compact box times a finite alphabet.

For any fixed cylinder F, choose v_F=[F]. The squared reflection norm of F minus its basis combination is a finite linear combination of observed reflected-cylinder probabilities, so convergence gives

  ||h_F^n−j_n v_F||_{L²(π_n)}→0.                         (4)

Here h_F^n is the actual conditional future probability in model n; reversibility and conditional past/future independence identify the reflection norm with its hidden L² norm. The same argument applies to 1, boundary-multiplied cylinders, and rationally shifted cylinders. Hence the feature distributions have subsequential limit μ with covariance I; the constant and output relations pass to the limit; and μ is supported in D. For positivity, h_F^n≥0 and (4) imply that the squared negative part of <x_n,v_F> has vanishing expectation. The negative-part map is continuous on the compact feature box. A countable family of rational cylinder atoms suffices to obtain μ(D)=1. Thus μ∈Γ, so the rigidity hypothesis forces supp μ⊂S.

Fix a rational t>0. The stationary two-time feature coupling κ_{n,t} of (x_n(X_0),x_n(X_t)) is symmetric by reversibility and has both marginals μ_n. Compactness gives a subsequential limit κ_t with marginals μ and support S×S. For every continuous f on the feature box and v∈H, the shifted-cylinder version of (4), the Markov property, and Cauchy–Schwarz give

  ∫ f(x)<y,v> dκ_t(x,y) = ∫ f(x)<x,T_t v> dμ(x).

Since S is finite and distinct, continuous functions can select each row point. Using self-adjointness of T_t yields

  Σ_j κ_t(i,j)x_j = π_i T_t x_i.                        (5)

Symmetry and row regression imply, for any pairwise monotone field Z_i,

  Σ_i π_i<Z_i,x_i−T_t x_i>
    = (1/2)Σ_{i,j}κ_t(i,j)<Z_i−Z_j,x_i−x_j> ≥ 0.

Divide by t and take t↓0 along rationals. Since T_t=e^{-tA}, the Farkas inequality (3) follows on S, giving F4 by finite-cone separation. Equivalently, one may use the exact energy identity

  Σ_{i,j} κ_t(i,j)||x_i−x_j||² = 2 tr(I−T_t) ≤ 2t tr(A).

As S is finite with positive minimum separation, κ_t(i,j)/t is bounded off the diagonal; a subsequential limit supplies symmetric nonnegative conductances directly from (5). Neither route assumes convergence or boundedness of approximating generator derivatives, rates, activities, or state counts.

In this example Γ has only the one forced support/weight pattern, and the exact Farkas field violates (3). Therefore the target is outside the rational-cylinder closure of all finite reversible CTMC output laws. As a direct fixed-lag variant, the continuous function

  F(t)=(1/10)Σ_i d_i·(ξ_i−ξ_i e^{tL})

has F(0)=0 and exact F'(0)<0 from the full pairing above, so F(t)<0 for all sufficiently small t>0 and for some rational t. Any reversible two-time coupling on S forces F(t)≥0, giving the same closure contradiction at a fixed lag.
