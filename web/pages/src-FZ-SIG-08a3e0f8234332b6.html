## Zero rates and locality

For gamma_i >= 0, finite-rate CPTP uniformization requires h_i = 0 wherever
gamma_i = 0. The exact formula is Gamma + h^T diag(gamma)^+ h, with the
additional range condition h in range diag(gamma). A zero-over-zero summand is
zero only at the actual zero pair. Joint limits do not determine that value:
h = sqrt(gamma) keeps h^2/gamma = 1 as gamma decreases to zero, whereas
h = gamma makes it tend to zero. Identity generators admit zero proposals.
The minimality statement also covers arbitrary finite-total-rate measures of
CPTP proposals, since their normalized average is itself CPTP.

A one-patch total proposal budget is not automatically a maximum local touching
budget. In the receiving sweep proof, nu is the type-summed rate at a specified
center. A guard can contain many centers, so its charged rate is the sum of
their budgets. Patch supports and guard incidence must be retained. Native
Pauli noise supplies a floor only in catalog directions with a strictly
positive, justified rate; allowed zero-rate source baths do not supply it.
Completion of the ideal correction channel remains a separate necessary input.

## Exact allocation for independently proposed Pauli modes

Let i index distinct permitted Pauli modes with known supports. Let c index
the receiving cells/guards, and let S_c be the catalog modes whose proposal
must be charged to c. Use the single-mode unitary proposals of v0001. Their
rates are lambda_i = gamma_i + h_i^2/gamma_i, and the touching rate is

    R_c(h,gamma) = sum_(i in S_c) lambda_i.

These are rates of an exact mathematical Poisson representation of the
specified generator. Implementing the unknown unitary is not an extra physical
control assumption, but acquiring/justifying the coefficient and support bounds,
the deliberate floors, the corrector and the geometry is still required.

For the coefficient box |h_i| <= epsilon_i, the fixed floors gamma_i = epsilon_i
simultaneously minimize every cell's worst rate:

    inf_gamma max_c sup_h R_c = 2 max_c sum_(i in S_c) epsilon_i.

Proof: every coordinate obeys gamma_i + epsilon_i^2/gamma_i >= 2 epsilon_i,
and the displayed choice attains equality at every cell. This is exact within
the stated modewise proposal/floor family, not optimization over every physical
noise intervention.

For the acquired ellipsoid sum_i (h_i/b_i)^2 <= 1, b_i > 0, each cell's exact
worst rate is

    F_c(gamma) = sum_(i in S_c) gamma_i
                    + max_(i in S_c) b_i^2/gamma_i.

The maximum is attained by putting the ellipsoid mass in a largest-ratio
coordinate of that cell. Empty cells have rate zero. Hence the sharp modewise
spatial allocation is the finite convex problem

    q_* = inf_(gamma_i > 0) max_c F_c(gamma).

It admits the following explicit equivalent feasibility test, retaining all
overlaps rather than pretending the cells are independent. For any candidate
budget q, there must exist z_c > 0 such that, with

    gamma_i(z) = max_(c: i in S_c) b_i^2/z_c,

one has sum_(i in S_c) gamma_i(z) + z_c <= q for every nonempty cell.
For necessity take z_c = max_(i in S_c) b_i^2/gamma_i from any feasible floors;
then gamma_i(z) <= gamma_i. For sufficiency the displayed floors obey
b_i^2/gamma_i(z) <= z_c at every incident cell, which proves the budget.
Modes charged nowhere can be omitted. This finite optimization is not an
oracle for unknown physical supports or an exact finite-bit real solver.

In particular q_* >= 2 max_c (sum_(i in S_c) b_i^2)^(1/2), by minimizing each
cell separately. For disjoint nonempty cells this bound is attained: write
B_c = (sum_(i in S_c) b_i^2)^(1/2) and choose gamma_i = b_i^2/B_c in its cell.
Thus q_* = 2 max_c B_c for disjoint supports under the stated charge convention.

The total-rate ellipsoid optimum in v0001 need not optimize spatial rate.
For two modes in disjoint receiving cells with b=(3,4), the total-optimal floors
(9/5,16/5) give worst touching rate 41/5. Floors (3,4) give the spatial optimum
8, although their global worst total rate is 11 rather than 10. These are
different receiving objectives. A proof that all possible correlated supported
proposal decompositions share this optimum has not been supplied.

## What this changes

when the actual admitted fault catalog and its local incidence are bounded.
It also exposes a nonuniform/native-bath route and a precise convex acquisition
contract for floors. Dense arbitrary patch errors and continuous shared-bath
Hamiltonians retain their separate range/dimension obstructions. The receiving
lifetime tail and its external probability imports are not proved anew here.
