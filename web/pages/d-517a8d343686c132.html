Title:A construction of spherical $5$-designs with $O(d^2)$ points

Authors:Andrii Arman, Andriy Bondarenko, Andriy Prymak, Danylo Radchenko
            
View a PDF of the paper titled A construction of spherical $5$-designs with $O(d^2)$ points, by Andrii Arman and 3 other authors

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:For every $d\geq1$ we give an explicit equal-weight spherical $5$-design in $\mathbb{S}^{d-1}\subset\mathbb{R}^d$ with at most $72d^2$ points. Our approach utilizes recent construction of complex projective $2$-designs based on Sidon sets.
