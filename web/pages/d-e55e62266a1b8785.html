CYCLE 3 HIGH-CEILING ROUTE AUDIT: GAMES, BMVZ FRAMES, AND ENERGY
Date: 2026-10-08

STATUS

INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE: exact full-list diagonal B / common-isometry
compression for the augmented BMVZ term list; exact constant-gap reversible
frame connector with an exponential row-support obstruction in the direct
layer-frame architecture; and the stated coordinate-walk gap/row tradeoff.
SOURCE-REPORTED: BMVZ and BJSW construction facts, Lin's MIPco=coRE theorem,
and the JNVWY strict C_qa subset C_qc result. CONDITIONAL: the amplified Lin
no-instance operator bound uses the inherited N11 commuting-repetition
theorem, whose source proof was not externally audited in this run. UNKNOWN:
whether an active/non-Markovian encoding or another C outside the direct
layer-frame architecture gives the complete BMVZ family a polynomial-row,
constant all-state gap.

DECISION

There is a finite, polynomially described common isometry V and one diagonal
positive contraction B for the *complete augmented BMVZ output* with exact
compression V*BV = H_out. The acceptance/test side has alpha=beta=1, row
support at most one, and a polynomial row/entry procedure. The known gap
obstruction sits entirely in C. A reversible gauge-Laplacian C can have exact
kernel ran(V) and gap 1/2, but its cross-color blocks are exponentially
row-dense. If the branch graph is kept within colors, its kernel instead
contains one independent logical copy per color, and its gap relative to
ran(V) is zero. In the natural q-coordinate interpolation between the two
BJSW seed frames, polynomial row support forces gap O(log n/T).

This closes the energy/consistency accounting for this route, not the general
common-positive-encoding problem. The lower bound below is for the direct
common-layer-frame isometry and reversible unitary-connection graph
Laplacians. It does not exclude active workspace maps, non-Markovian C, or a
different V.

1. THE TARGET AND ITS FINITE INTERFACE

Let the complete final augmented output be

    H_out = sum_{j in J} pi_j h_j,  pi_j>0, sum_j pi_j=1,

where j=(chi,f) indexes every BMVZ term, including the two appended singleton
seed colors. The augmented starting family is

    H_aug = (1/2) H_base tensor I + (1/4) P_phi + (1/4) P_psi,

with the two five-qubit BJSW projectors on fresh registers. They have a common
kernel of dimension 30, so the ground energy and promise gap of H_base are
rescaled by 1/2. This is a hardness-preserving, constant-color augmentation;
the seed colors remain separate singleton layers at every BMVZ iteration.
After T repeated copies their terms are

    Q_(v,T) = I - (I-|v><v|)^{tensor T},  v in {phi_5,psi_5}.

The BMVZ source defines each layer as an average of commuting projections and
amplifies a layer by path clauses

    h_(chi,f) = I - tensor_(a=1)^t (I-Pi^chi_(f(a))).

Thus every clause in a fixed output color is diagonal in a common joint basis.
For the BJSW stabilizer-projector start, a joint Clifford diagonalizer is
constructible by symplectic Gaussian elimination; iteration tensor-powers
these decoders over the independent copies. For the singleton seed layers,
take the fixed factorwise Clifford diagonalizers U_phi,U_psi. This is the
canonical layer-frame route audited below.

The standard quantum-PCP target is an explicit finite Hamiltonian with
constant-qubit-local terms and a constant relative ground-energy promise gap.
BMVZ Theorem 1.4 instead gives a polynomial list of constant-gap terms that
are O(n)-fold tensor products of O(1)-local projectors: the term count and
measurement are efficient, but term locality is not constant. BMVZ also
states that black-box sparsification does not yield sparse quantum PCP and
leaves exploitation of the tensor structure open. The present B is a sparse
matrix, but its diagonal block predicates are global on the T copies and
cannot replace the missing all-state consistency operator C.

2. FULL-LIST COMMON V AND ONE DIAGONAL B

For every output branch j=(chi,f), choose a unitary U_j=U_chi such that

    h_j = U_j* D_j U_j,

where D_j is diagonal. (For the repeated seed clauses, U_j is the T-fold
tensor power of the selected five-qubit Clifford decoder and D_j is the
diagonal OR projector.) Define

    V|psi> = sum_(j in J) sqrt(pi_j) |j> tensor U_j|psi>,
    B      = sum_(j in J) |j><j| tensor D_j.

Orthogonality of the branch register gives V*V=I. B is a positive diagonal
contraction and, in the branch/data computational basis, has at most one
nonzero per row. Direct calculation gives the operator identity

    V* B V = sum_j pi_j U_j*D_jU_j = H_out.

This is exact compression, alpha=beta=1. It is not merely a ground-state
expectation statement: it holds for every logical vector and hence every
logical density matrix.

Representation ledger for the standard BMVZ hard family:

* The output has M=|J|=poly(n) branches and N=poly(n) data qubits; the branch
  register uses ceil(log_2 M) qubits, with invalid binary labels assigned
  zero B and a positive diagonal penalty in C.
* V has a polynomial-size circuit description: prepare the layer/path label
  superposition and apply the color-controlled Clifford U_chi. Exact
  equal-weight path preparation can be done with polynomially many gates;
  arbitrary polynomial-bit weights require the corresponding controlled
  amplitude precision. More generally, if an implemented isometry V_tilde is
  within operator norm epsilon of V, then
  ||V_tilde* B V_tilde - V* B V|| <= 2 epsilon because ||B||<=1. This is a
  circuit representation, not a sparse-matrix representation of V.
* For a row (j,z), B's entry is D_j(z,z) in {0,1}. In the stabilizer BMVZ
  family it is computed by evaluating the O(T) diagonal syndrome predicates
  in the path clause. Row enumeration is O(1); entry computation is
  polynomial in the input/output description. The D_j block acts on up to
  the whole N-qubit register (seed locality 5T), so row sparsity does not
  imply constant locality.
* The branch list is succinctly generated in polynomial time by the BMVZ
  path generator; there are polynomially many terms at the stated iterated
  parameters. Full term-table storage is polynomial, but the data Hilbert
  dimension 2^N is of course exponential; an oracle is essential.

3. EXACT ALL-STATE C IN THE DIRECT FRAME MODEL, AND WHY ITS ROWS BLOW UP

Let pi=(pi_j), S=|sqrt(pi)><sqrt(pi)| on the valid branch space, and
G=sum_j |j><j| tensor U_j. Define

    C = (1/2) G ((I-S) tensor I_data) G*.

Extend C by (1/2)I on invalid branch labels. This is a positive contraction;
its kernel is exactly ran(V), and every nonzero eigenvalue on valid labels
is 1/2. Consequently

    C >= (1/2)(I-VV*),   ||C||=1/2.

So the direct-frame route has the desired *all-state* constant image gap.
For j != k its block is

    C_(j,k) = -(1/2) sqrt(pi_j pi_k) U_j U_k*.

The nonzero support of this block is precisely the support of the relative
decoder. An independent-resampling branch chain gives an explicit constant
gap, but is dense in branch space; its branch degree M is polynomial while
its data-block row support may be exponential. A sparse reversible branch
graph can lower branch degree, but it must still connect the color sectors.
For an arbitrary supported graph, the exact row bound at branch j is

    rs_row(C at (j,z)) <= 1 + sum_(k: P_jk>0) rs_row(U_j U_k*)(z,.) .

Thus evaluating one requested entry can be polynomial even when listing all
nonzeros in the row is exponential.

Here is the route-specific lower bound. Let g be the (constant) number of
output colors, including base and the two seed colors. Contract all path
vertices of each color: within one color the frame U_chi is the same, so
within-color transport is identity on data. If the support graph of a
reversible connection is disconnected after this contraction, C has one
logical kernel copy per component, not ran(V), and its gap against
I-VV* is zero. Otherwise there is a simple color path from the phi seed
frame to the psi seed frame of length ell<=g-1.

For the canonical BJSW Clifford decoders, the one-block relative unitary

    W = U_phi U_psi*

has exactly 8 nonzeros in every row and every column; this follows by exact
multiplication of the circuits preparing phi_5 and psi_5 from |00000>.
Tensoring the T independent seed copies gives

    rs_row(W^{tensor T}) = rs_col(W^{tensor T}) = 8^T.

For a matrix A write rho(A)=max(max-row-support(A),max-column-support(A)).
For matrices with the relevant compatible dimensions,

    rho(AB) <= rho(A) rho(B),

because each row (respectively column) of AB is supported on at most the
product of the two support bounds. Relative decoders along the color path
telescope:

    W_phi W_psi* = (W_phi W_(chi_1)*) (W_(chi_1)W_(chi_2)*) ...
                   (W_(chi_(ell-1)) W_psi*).

If every graph-edge block had rho at most s, then 8^T<=s^ell and therefore

    s >= 8^(T/ell) >= 8^(T/(g-1)).

Because C is Hermitian, both orientations of each edge occur, so a maximum
row-support bound on C also bounds rho of every relative edge block. Hence
every connected direct layer-frame reversible enforcer for this fixed seed
decoder choice has a row with at least 8^(T/(g-1)) nonzeros. For polynomial
row support n^a, this excludes the route once

    T > (g-1) a log_8(n).

In the BMVZ high-gap iteration T is polynomial in the original input size,
so this is superpolynomial. The complete direct-frame construction thus
exhibits the exact tradeoff: exact B and a 1/2 all-state C gap are available,
but not polynomial row support in C.

The lower bound is scoped to the specified factorwise seed frames and
reversible unitary-connection Laplacian. It is not a lower bound on arbitrary
positive C. Rechoosing both seed diagonalizers globally, adding a growing
number of intermediate frames, allowing a branch-dependent active workspace,
or using non-connection off-diagonal blocks changes the hypotheses and is
not settled here.

Cost of the direct C: its matrix entries are a branch coefficient times a
Clifford relative-matrix entry, hence exact algebraic numbers of polynomial
bit length when queried at specified indices. This does not make row access
polynomial: a row oracle must enumerate 8^(Omega(T)) nonzeros on the seed
connection. The canonical phi/psi relative unitary acts on the three
propagation qubits per block and is identity on their two common clock-suffix
qubits, so a direct phi/psi edge has data locality 3T plus the branch
register. More generally, the path proof forces an edge block with row
support at least 2^(3T/(g-1)); a unitary acting on only ell data qubits has
row support at most 2^ell, so that edge has locality at least 3T/(g-1)
qubits, plus the branch register. Since g is constant this is Omega(T), not
constant. Within-color path edges act only on branch labels, but they do not
remove the need for cross-color consistency.

4. LOCAL FRAME INTERPOLATION: THE EXPANDER-WALK ESCAPE ALSO LOSES GAP

One proposed way around a dense direct seed edge is to insert a frame bit for
each seed block. For x in {0,1}^T let U_x=tensor_i U_(x_i), with
U_0=U_phi and U_1=U_psi, and V_seed|psi>=2^(-T/2) sum_x |x>U_x|psi>.
For an edge x~y changing h coordinates, the relative block has exactly
8^h nonzeros per row. Let P be any reversible uniform-stationary Markov
kernel on this cube, with maximum degree d and jump radius q (every edge
changes at most q bits). The gauge Laplacian

    C_P = (1/2) G ((I-P) tensor I) G*,    G=direct-sum_x U_x,

has kernel ran(V_seed), norm at most one, and gap one half the Markov
Poincare gap. Its row support is at most 1+d 8^q.

For completeness, the Poincare upper bound is elementary and uses no
expander assumption. For independent signs epsilon_i, put

    F_epsilon(x)=sum_(i=1)^T epsilon_i (-1)^(x_i).

It has mean zero and variance T. For a transition changing h bits,
independently averaging over epsilon gives
E_epsilon[(F(x)-F(y))^2]=4h. The Dirichlet form has the factor 1/2, so
averaging over epsilon and stationary transitions gives at most 2q.
Some choice of epsilon therefore has Rayleigh quotient at most 2q/T:

    gap(P) <= 2q/T,       gap(C_P) <= q/T.

Combining row and gap bounds, if d<=poly(n) and row support of C_P is
S<=poly(n), then 8^q <= S and q=O(log n), hence gap(C_P)=O(log n/T).
Conversely a constant gap forces q=Omega(T), making a single connection
block have 8^(Omega(T)) row entries. The bit-flip walk gives the sparse
endpoint: q=1, degree T, row support <=1+8T, and gap(C_P)=1/T.

An h-coordinate edge has row support in at most 5h seed qubits and, for the
canonical common-suffix Clifford decoders, acts nontrivially on the three
propagation qubits in each changed block; it also acts on the branch
selector. Keeping constant data locality forces h=O(1), which by the same
Rayleigh bound gives gap O(1/T). The cube register uses T qubits (its 2^T
basis labels are not themselves a qubit-count blowup). This interpolation is a valid seed-pair
bridge calculation, not a completed C for the full base BMVZ colors: the
base-color coherence gate remains. It shows that coordinate-local
reversible expander walks do not repair the direct route at constant gap.

5. NAMED GAME VERIFIER: WHAT ACCEPTANCE DOES AND DOES NOT REPRESENT

Named verifier. Lin, “MIPco=coRE,” Theorem 1.1 (arXiv:2510.07162v1,
8 Oct 2025) gives a two-prover, one-round classical verifier for a
coRE-complete promised game-value problem. For input z of length n, its
question sampler and predicate run in polynomial time; question and answer
alphabets are binary strings of polynomial length x(n),a(n). In the
commuting-operator strategy model the promised value is 1 in the yes case
and at most 1/2 in the no case. The full source proof is not re-audited
here; use is SOURCE-REPORTED. This is an explicit verifier interface, not a
finite-dimensional strategy representation.

The named proof package at
work/astra_prior/web/pages/d-37c1f60482b460b2.html adds a one-sided erasure
wrapper and the inherited N11 commuting repetition theorem. The wrapper
sends an erased Alice question with probability 1/4 and has the exact value
identity

    omega(G_skip) = 1/4 + (3/4) omega(G),

so its promise is 1 versus at most 5/8. With d_skip=(|A|+1)|B| and
L>=1, the inherited repetition bound yields

    omega_co(G_skip^(k)) <= exp(-L),
    k = ceil(7864320 (2a(n)+2)L / 27),

for the binary alphabets in that source package; yes value remains supremum
1. The exponent/constant is conditional on the inherited N11 theorem, which
is source-derived and internally reconstructed but externally unvalidated.
The original Lin 1-versus-1/2 promise already suffices for the computability
obstruction below; no repetition assumption is needed for that part.

Communication and verifier costs of the amplified family: it remains two
provers and one round. For an original sampler using s(n) random bits, the
wrapper/repetition use k(s(n)+2) bits; Alice receives k(x(n)+1) question
bits and returns k(a(n)+1) answer bits, while Bob receives kx(n) and returns
ka(n). The verifier runs k predicates in time
O(k[T_Q+T_D+x+a]) and accepts only if every copy accepts. These are
polynomial in n and L when L is polynomial. The whole-string test checks k
copies; it is not a constant-qubit query. Writing the full question-answer
acceptance table costs up to

    (|X|+1)^k |Y|^k (|A|+1)^k |B|^k

entries, exponential in the verifier's polynomial bit lengths. The sampler
and predicate are succinct, but do not output prover measurement matrices.

For a fixed repeated game, let P_(a|x), Q_(b|y) be the complete projective
measurements in the two prover algebras, with cross-player generators
commuting. Its acceptance element is

    R_k = sum_(x,y) nu^k(x,y) sum_(a,b accepted) P_(a|x) Q_(b|y).

Each joint product is positive and the full joint outcome sum is identity,
so 0<=R_k<=I in the universal commuting-measurement C*-algebra. Thus the
penalty K_k=I-R_k is positive. In the amplified no case,

    K_k >= (1-exp(-L)) I

in every commuting representation; in the yes case its universal spectral
infimum is zero. This is a genuine uniform energy inequality in that
operator algebra. It does *not* supply a finite matrix, a computational
basis, an efficient row oracle, a fixed number of qubits, or constant
qubit-local terms. A prover effect can act on the prover's entire
strategy-dependent Hilbert space. Positivity and a succinct referee sampler
do not supply matrix locality or sparse rows.

Exact route obstruction. If a total computable compiler mapped every game in
Lin's coRE-complete promised family to a finite effectively described
Hermitian matrix K_G and effectively separated thresholds alpha<beta with

    yes: lambda_min(K_G)<=alpha,   no: lambda_min(K_G)>=beta,

then one could decide the coRE-complete language: run the reduction and
compiler, approximate the finite matrix entries to operator error less than
(beta-alpha)/4, compute/approximate its minimum eigenvalue, and compare to
the midpoint. This always terminates, contradicting coRE-completeness.
The argument allows arbitrary finite dimension and no polynomial resource
bound; it only requires effective finite dimension, certified entry access,
and an effective positive threshold separation. A polynomial-row compiler
is a special case. Therefore the repeated commuting-game acceptance
element cannot be uniformly converted into the requested finite spectral
object for this whole family.

This no-go is not a no-go for the BMVZ QMA family. The latter is a decidable
finite-Hamiltonian family, and the common-positive-encoding target is
restricted to those instances. A game route for BMVZ would still need to
give a finite-dimensional spatial strategy representation, polynomial
dimension/precision and a common V with positive sparse B,C protecting all
auxiliary states; no such map is provided by the Lin verifier.

There is a second model-separation warning. Ji–Natarajan–Vidick–Wright–Yuen,
“MIP*=RE,” prove C_qa strictly contained in C_qc for a finite Bell scenario.
Since C_qa is compact in that finite-dimensional probability space, strict
separation gives a Bell functional with positive margin; shifting and
rescaling its coefficients, then rationally approximating them, produces a
finite one-round game G_sep with

    omega_qc(G_sep) >= omega_qa(G_sep) + Delta,   Delta>0.

More quantitatively, if the separating functional c is normalized by
||c||_infinity=1 and has margin delta, a uniform question law and payoff
W=(c+1)/2 give margin delta/(2|X||Y|). A rational table c' within epsilon
infinity norm changes the unnormalized margin by at most
2|X||Y|epsilon; choosing epsilon<delta/(4|X||Y|) preserves a positive gap.
The source establishes the strict inclusion/finite scenario (and describes
an explicit example), but this audit does not extract its concrete alphabet
sizes or numerical Delta. For its loss effect, the
commuting optimum is at most 1-omega_qc while every finite tensor-product
strategy has loss at least 1-omega_qa, separated by Delta. A finite
Hamiltonian state is a spatial finite-dimensional state; it cannot be
identified with the commuting optimum in general. This proves a precise
model gap, but does not construct a BMVZ reduction or rule out a game whose
soundness is separately proved in the spatial model.

6. QPCP / SPARSE-QPCP COMPARISON AND GATE STATUS

BMVZ's own formulation of the quantum-PCP conjecture asks for constant
qubit locality and a constant ground-energy promise gap. Their Theorem 1.4
has the gap but terms are O(n)-fold tensor products; each term can be
measured efficiently, not queried on a constant number of proof qubits.
Their sparse-QPCP discussion defines the alternative as efficient sparse
matrix access and explicitly leaves it open whether their tensor structure
can yield it. The route construction above sharpens the interface split:

* B is a one-sparse positive matrix and compresses every term exactly, but
  its diagonal block is a global OR predicate on T copies, not a
  constant-local term.
* The full-refresh C is positive with exact image kernel and gap 1/2, but
  its direct seed/base relative blocks have exponential row support and
  act on Omega(T) data qubits.
* Restricting the connector to within-color path expansion gives sparse
  rows but leaves one zero-energy logical sector per color. The
  all-state gap against the single common image is exactly zero.
* A q-local coordinate interpolation has a proved O(q/T) gap ceiling;
  poly-row coordinate steps yield O(log n/T), not a constant.
* The verifier route supplies a positive acceptance/loss element only in
  an abstract commuting algebra. It does not bridge to finite row access;
  for Lin's full coRE family, any total computable finite spectral bridge
  is impossible. JNVWY additionally separates commuting from spatial
  optimum values by a positive game gap.

Thus this audit changes the route choice, not the general target verdict.
The direct color-frame expander can meet exact compression and constant
all-state gap, but fails the polynomial-row gate. The generic commuting-game
route cannot be used as a finite Hamiltonian compiler. A decisive positive
result must change at least one explicit premise: the encoding frame,
the type of C, the verifier's strategy model, or the way full BMVZ clauses
are represented. No global impossibility has been proved.

FALSIFIERS / NEXT DECISIVE OBJECT

Any of the following falsifies the corresponding scoped negative claim:

1. A direct layer-frame reversible connection for the fixed factorwise
   BJSW seed decoders with polynomial row support and connected color
   support. The telescoping 8^T support product would then be contradicted.
2. A coordinate-frame reversible walk changing q=o(T) bits per edge but
   with constant Poincare gap. The sign-function Rayleigh bound rules this
   out under uniform stationarity.
3. A total computable finite matrix/threshold compiler for all of Lin's
   promised coRE games. It would decide a coRE-complete language.

These falsifiers do not concern arbitrary active encodings or QMA-only
constructions. The next positive object is a polynomial-row positive C for
the full term-index image whose off-diagonal branch action is not a
reversible covariant transport of the noncommuting layer decoders, together
with the exact B compression above and a verifier/model ledger.
