## Exact prefix law and the sign estimator

For an explicitly given rational-complex n × n matrix F, fix sector k and pairwise disjoint site sets U,D,H, prescribed up, down, and empty. Write a=|U|, b=|D|, and

\[
 \mu(U,D,H)=\sum_{\substack{|I|=|J|=k,\ I\cap J=\varnothing\\
                  U\subseteq I,\ D\subseteq J,\ (I\cup J)\cap H=\varnothing}}
                  |\det F[I,J]|^2.
\]

Let V=(u₁,v₁,…,uₙ,vₙ), where uᵢ=eᵢ and vᵢ is row i of F, transposed into a column. For real site activities tᵢ, form the complex skew matrix

\[
 A(t)=\sum_i t_i(u_iv_i^T-v_iu_i^T)=\operatorname{diag}(t)F-F^T\operatorname{diag}(t).
\]

The transpose is essential here; the Hermitian adjoint A* A appears later only in the norm coefficient computation. Let ω=Σᵢ tᵢ uᵢ∧vᵢ. Since the degree-two summands commute and each squares to zero,

\[
 \omega^k/k!=\sum_{|S|=k}\Bigl(\prod_{i\in S}t_i\Bigr)
        \bigwedge_{i\in S}(u_i\wedge v_i).
\]

The coordinate indexed by a 2k-set R is Pf(A[R]). Its summand indexed by S is zero unless S⊆R, because the selected identity columns uᵢ=eᵢ force those row indices into R. When S⊆R, the coefficient d(R,S) has squared modulus

\[
 |d_{R,S}|^2=|\det F[S,R\setminus S]|^2.
\]

Choose independent signs sᵢ∈{−1,+1} on free sites; set tᵢ=z on U, tᵢ=sᵢ on unforced sites, and tᵢ=0 on D∪H. For R avoiding H, the coefficient of zᵃ in p_R(z)=Pf(A(t)[R]) is

\[
 h_R(s)=\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq R,\ |S|=k\\
                        U\subseteq S,\ S\cap(D\cup H)=\varnothing}}
                   d_{R,S}\,\chi_{S\setminus U}(s),
 \qquad \chi_Q(s)=\prod_{i\in Q}s_i.
\]

Because z is real, the coefficient of z²ᵃ in |p_R(z)|² is |h_R(s)|², hence nonnegative. For each T⊆D, let W_T=[n]∖(H∪T), and set

\[
 Q_T(z)=X_k(A(t)[W_T]),\qquad
 X_k(B)=\sum_{|R|=2k}|\operatorname{Pf}(B[R])|^2,
 \quad \operatorname{top}_T=[z^{2a}]Q_T(z).
\]

The physical-prefix random variable is

\[
 Y(s)=\sum_{T\subseteq D}(-1)^{|T|}\operatorname{top}_T.
\]

Expanding each top coefficient into its row sets shows that a fixed row set R avoiding H receives multiplier

\[
 \sum_{T\subseteq D\setminus R}(-1)^{|T|}
   =\begin{cases}1&D\subseteq R,\\0&D\nsubseteq R.\end{cases}
\]

Thus the signed deletion formula is exactly

\[
 Y(s)=\sum_{\substack{|R|=2k,\ R\cap H=\varnothing,\ D\subseteq R}}
                         |h_R(s)|^2\ge0.
\]

This is the critical sign check: inclusion-exclusion is only an exact way to acquire the row-set filter; it is not a numerically subtracted estimate. For each eligible R, the characters indexed by S∖U are distinct as S varies, so Boolean orthogonality removes cross terms. The surviving term is exactly the ordered pair I=S, J=R∖S in the definition of μ. Therefore

\[
 \mathbb E_s Y(s)=\mu(U,D,H).
\]

This is also an exact positive-support identity for the estimator: since Y≥0 on every sign word and the sign space is finite,

\[
 \mu(U,D,H)=0\iff Y(s)=0\text{ for every }s,
 \qquad
 \mu(U,D,H)>0\iff Y(s)>0\text{ for at least one }s.
\]

A single zero draw does **not** certify a zero prefix when the mean is positive.

A separate exact positivity identity is used by self-reduction. If a site x remains free, every physical pair pattern puts it in exactly one of three disjoint cases: up, down, or empty. Since the minor weights are nonnegative,

\[
 \mu(U,D,H)=\mu(U\cup\{x\},D,H)+\mu(U,D\cup\{x\},H)
                                  +\mu(U,D,H\cup\{x\}).
\]

Consequently a positive parent has at least one positive child, and a zero parent has three zero children. This identity concerns exact masses; it does not say every random sign draw detects a positive child.

## Pointwise bound and sampling cost

For each eligible R, the row set contains U∪D and has 2k−a−b other sites. The number of possible up-sets S is exactly

\[
 C_{a,b}=\binom{2k-a-b}{k-a}.
\]

Complex Cauchy–Schwarz applied to h_R(s), followed by summing over eligible R, gives the pointwise and second-moment bounds

\[
 0\le Y(s)\le C_{a,b}\mu,\qquad
 \mathbb E Y=\mu,\qquad
 \mathbb E Y^2\le C_{a,b}\mu^2.
\]

The last inequality follows from Y²≤Cₐ,ᵦ μY and expectation; it remains valid at μ=0. Moreover

\[
 2^b C_{a,b}\le 2^b\binom{2k-b}{k}\le\binom{2k}{k}.
\]

For the first inequality, Cₐ,ᵦ counts the k-subsets containing a designated sites among 2k−b sites. For the second, fᵦ=2ᵇ binom(2k−b,k) is nonincreasing because fᵦ₊₁/fᵦ=2(k−b)/(2k−b)≤1. Thus the 2ᵇ deletion cost does not exceed the central-binomial sector bound.

The forward difference

\[
 [z^{2a}]Q_T(z)=\frac1{(2a)!}\sum_{j=0}^{2a}(-1)^{2a-j}\binom{2a}{j}Q_T(j)
\]

This is exact since each Pfaffian has degree at most a and Q_T has degree at most 2a. One Y-draw uses at most 2ᵇ(2a+1) evaluations of X_k. For the implementation’s X_k evaluation, the standard skew-matrix unitary-congruence form gives

\[
 \sum_{m\ge0}X_m(A)z^m=\sqrt{\det(I+zA^*A)}.
\]

With Tⱼ=Tr((A* A)ʲ), the coefficient recurrence is

\[
 2m q_m=\sum_{j=1}^m(-1)^{j-1}T_jq_{m-j},\quad q_0=1.
\]

The recurrence requires O(kn³+k²) exact arithmetic operations with ordinary matrix multiplication. Clearing rational denominators by one positive common D gives Gaussian-integer samples; each physical sector and prefix scales by D⁻²ᵏ. The z values are at most 2a≤2k, trace powers stop at k, and finite-difference/inclusion-exclusion coefficients have polynomial bit length. Thus these steps have polynomial bit cost in n and input bit length L, besides at most 2ᵇ(2a+1) coefficient evaluations. The matrix-normal-form identity is the standard imported Youla theorem cited in c03_s02’s report; I did not perform an independent priority search.

For μ>0, a bounded nonnegative variable Y≤Cₐ,ᵦ μ supports a multiplicative Chernoff estimate with O(Cₐ,ᵦ η⁻² log(1/δ)) independent draws. Alternatively, c03_s02 uses Chebyshev per batch followed by a median. The viewed c01_s03 code uses the Chernoff form with

\[
 m=\left\lceil\frac{3C_{a,b}\,\lceil\log_2(2/\delta)\rceil}{\eta^2}\right\rceil,
\]

The code exact-enumerates the sign space only when it is no larger than that cap. Because log₂(2/δ)≥ln(2/δ), the standard bounded-variable Chernoff inequality gives failure at most δ. At μ=0 every sample is exactly zero; at positive μ, a capped empirical estimate can still be zero on its charged failure event. Memoization caches function values for repeated iid sign words without changing the iid sampling law.

## Cross-comparison with c03_s01 phase matching

The authorized extension uses phased site signs (real signs on even sites, imaginary signs on odd sites) and a deterministic real-matching baseline G. Character orthogonality gives an unbiased global count estimator; its additional route is a certificate that supplied F is sufficiently close in Frobenius norm to G, making the baseline count a pointwise scale for the sampled Pfaffian norm. The read JSON reports exact fixture checks (96 sign words, 352 coefficient comparisons, 10 unbiased-sector checks) and one 144-sample count smoke test; these are finite checks, not a proof that the certificate is valid for every accepted input. The extension itself says it does not implement an arbitrary physical-prefix counter or Born sampler. It is therefore complementary: phase matching seeks a constant-factor estimator on a certified near-matching family, while the prefix construction here estimates every physical prefix for arbitrary exact F with a central-binomial dependence on k. I did not execute the extension or inspect its imported support modules.

## Independent exact finite check

`phase2_lowk_prefix_audit.py` implements its own exact Gaussian-rational determinant, recursive Pfaffian, finite differences, deletion sum, and direct disjoint-minor prefix mass. It imports and executes no peer code. On one dense complex rational 4 × 4 matrix, it exhausts every forced-up/down/empty/free assignment, every legal k∈{0,1,2}, and every free-site sign word. The recorded result in `phase2_lowk_prefix_audit.json` is:

- 768 prefix/sector cases and 1,875 exact sign-prefix/sector evaluations;
- 194 positive prefix/sector cases;
- all first-moment, nonnegativity, pointwise-bound, second-moment, zero/positive-support, and Cₐ,ᵦ=1 assertions passed;
- 768 exact parent-equals-three-children partition checks passed.

This validates finite instances of the identities, not their proof for arbitrary n,k,F; the derivation above is the general argument.

## Remaining gaps and boundaries

The estimator is exponential in k through binom(2k,k) in general; it is a low-sector FPRAS, polynomial for k=O(log n), not a general dense-sector FPRAS or hardness theorem. The viewed Born sampler explicitly has an OK/FAIL outcome and a positive-norm promise. Its exact child partition is sound; an end-to-end total-variation guarantee still needs the stated estimator-failure and rounding accounting, and a one-draw zero must never be exposed as an exact-zero certificate. F is supplied exact rational input: this audit establishes no acquisition from noisy measurements, microscopic-energy guarantee, executed quantum synthesis, hardware runtime, novelty, or priority claim.
