1. SHARP LYAPUNOV-RATE AND HYPERBOLIC-VOLUME STABILITY

  E_mu(chi-H)^2<=Delta.                                 (1)
  beta>=H^2/(H^2+Delta).                                (2)

Proof. The normal estimate gives chi<=sbar, the continuous-flow time
average of s. Jensen/Fatou, or conditional-expectation contraction,
give E chi^2<=E sbar^2<=E s^2<=H^2+Delta. Thus
  E(chi-H)^2<=Delta-2H(J-H)<=Delta.
The invariant A={chi>0} satisfies

This uses invariance and time averages; chi<=s is not asserted pointwise.
Cauchy on A gives H^2<=J^2<=beta integral_A s^2<=beta(H^2+Delta).

Both constants are sharp on disconnected closed manifolds: put a Sol
suspension with rate H/beta on a component of volume fraction beta and
