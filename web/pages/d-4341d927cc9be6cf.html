1. FULL FINITE PROFILE AND OPERATIONAL CONSEQUENCE

For m>=1 let H_m=Sym^m(C^3), d=(m+1)(m+2)/2. The conjugate carrier obeys
the same result. On End(H_m) use the orthogonal SU(3) components H_(l,l),
0<=l<=m, of dimension (l+1)^3. Put

  beta_l=m!(m+2)!/[(m-l)!(m+l+2)!],
  a_l=1-beta_l,
  E_B(A)=sum_(l=1..m) a_l ||A_l||_HS^2,
  Lambda_m(Q)=min_(rank A<=Q, ||A||_HS=1) E_B(A).

The exact all-Q variational capacity and finite-temperature comparison were
proved in COLD_CARRIER.txt. This file gives its complete explicit order:

  Q<=d/2:          Lambda_m(Q)=Theta(Q^(-1/2));
  d/2<Q<d, h=d-Q: Lambda_m(Q)=Theta(sqrt(h)/d);
  Q=d:            Lambda_m(d)=0.                            (1.1)

Every constant is independent of m,Q,h. Equivalently, with

  f_d(Q)=sqrt(1/Q-1/d), 1<=Q<=d,
  c=(1-exp(-1))/256,

one may take the deliberately loose finite bounds

  c f_d(Q)<=Lambda_m(Q)<=64 f_d(Q).                         (1.2)

For the unknown pure coherent full orbit P_v=|v^tensor m><v^tensor m|,
the optimal worst-case whole-carrier half-trace error is exactly Lambda.
For a known anisotropic thermal seed

  rho=Z^(-1) q1^n1 q2^n2,
  0<=q1,q2<=qbar<1,

with entirely unknown U(3) rotation, its optimal error obeys

  (1-qbar)^4 c f_d(Q)<=e_Q^thermal<=64 f_d(Q).              (1.3)

Every intermediate quantum branch has dimension <=Q; unlimited classical
labels are allowed in the converse. The explicit upper below uses at most
[(2m+1)(m+1)]^2 positive frame labels, and total hybrid dimension <=that
number times Q. Every frame label is charged. No reference plane, direction,
purifying reference, entanglement or additional carrier is supplied. The
carrier itself is supplied; its iid occurrence probability is not acquired
by this theorem.

For near-full h>=1 the proposed alternatives h/d and
m^(-1)sqrt(h/d) reduce, in order, to sqrt(h)/d: the latter is comparable
to sqrt(h)/d, while h/d is larger. There is no extra linear-deficit regime
among integer deficits. At h=1 the answer is Theta(d^(-1)); at h=0 it is
exactly zero. A global leading constant and optimal hole geometry are UNKNOWN.

2. ELEMENTARY BEREZIN AND CASIMIR COMPARISONS

The exact coefficient derivation in COLD_CARRIER.txt Section 2 gives

  beta_l=prod_(a=0..l-1) (m-a)/(m+3+a),
  beta_l<=exp[-l(l+2)/(m+l+2)],
  gamma:=min_(l>=1) a_l=a_1=3/(m+3).                      (2.1)

Let c_l=l(l+2), and let E_C(A)=sum_l c_l||A_l||_HS^2 be the
SU(3) Casimir energy, with Tr_fund(t_a t_b)=delta_ab/2. The union bound
for the product yields a_l<=c_l/m. Also, with c0=1-exp(-1),
1-exp(-x)>=c0 min{x,1}. Since m+l+2<=4m for 1<=l<=m,

  a_l>=(c0/4) min{c_l/m,1},
  1/a_l<=(4/c0)(1+m/c_l).                                (2.2)

The factor 1 in the inverse bound is essential: the Berezin energy
saturates at high harmonics instead of continuing quadratically. No
unsaturated Casimir extrapolation is used in the converse. For every A,

  E_B(A)<=E_C(A)/m.                                      (2.3)

This upper comparison will transfer explicit resistance filters, not an
operational theorem about a different Gibbs experiment.

3. GREEN INEQUALITY FOR AN ARBITRARY RIGHT KERNEL

Take any complex A with ||A||_HS=1, rank A<=d-h, and choose a rank-h
orthogonal projection P in its RIGHT kernel, AP=0. Write

  alpha=Tr A/d, X=A-alpha I, P0=P-(h/d)I,
  G_B(P)=sum_(l=1..m) ||P_l||_HS^2/a_l.

Trace cyclicity gives <P0,X>=Tr(PX)=-h alpha, including when alpha is
complex or A is nonnormal. Spectral Cauchy-Schwarz on traceless matrices
and the first gap give

  h^2 |alpha|^2<=G_B(P) E_B(A),
  ||X||_HS^2<=E_B(A)/gamma,
  1=d|alpha|^2+||X||_HS^2.

Consequently

  E_B(A)>=1/[d G_B(P)/h^2+1/gamma].                       (3.1)

This uses a necessary scalar consequence of AP=0. It does not claim that
the scalar constraint is sufficient for a kernel, nor assume that a best
kernel is a coherent cap. The inequality holds for every permitted kernel
orientation and directly for all complex matrices in Lambda.

4. RANK-SENSITIVE GREEN BOUND FOR THE SATURATED ENERGY

Let Pi_l denote the invariant projector onto H_(l,l) in vectorized matrix
space. Irreducibility gives both partial traces

  Tr_2 Pi_l=[(l+1)^3/d] I.

The vector vec(P) has squared norm h and first support P, so
|vec(P)><vec(P)|<=h(P tensor I). Set u_l=||P_l||_HS^2. Then

  u_0=h^2/d, sum_(l>=1)u_l=h-h^2/d<=h,
  u_l<=h^2(l+1)^3/d.                                    (4.1)

Define G_C(P)=sum_(l>=1)u_l/c_l. Let R=(d/h)^(1/4),
L=min{m,floor R}. The cutoff controls its low mass and its high total mass:

  G_C(P)<=(h^2/d)sum_(l=1..L)(l+1)^3/[l(l+2)]
                           +h/[(L+1)(L+3)].             (4.2)

The last term is omitted when L=m. For l>=1,

  (l+1)^3/[l(l+2)]<=(8/3)l,

because 8l^2(l+2)-3(l+1)^3=(l-1)(5l^2+12l+3)>=0.
Thus the sum is <=(8/3)L^2<=(8/3)R^2. If a tail is present,
L+1>R, and its bound is <h/R^2. Therefore, for every P,

  G_C(P)<=(11/3) h^(3/2)/sqrt d.                         (4.3)

Now (2.2), the total mass bound, and m/sqrt d<=sqrt 2 imply

  G_B(P)<=(4/c0)[h+m G_C(P)]
         <=G0 h^(3/2),
  G0=(4/c0)[1+11sqrt(2)/3]<52.                           (4.4)

The harmless h term is absorbed because h>=1. This explains why no separate
linear-deficit regime survives for integer h; it would matter in a formal
continuous mass interpolation below one dimension, which is not this task.

For 1<=h<d/2, 1/gamma=(m+3)/3<=m+1<=sqrt(2d)<d/sqrt h.
Substituting (4.4) into (3.1) gives the explicit all-matrix lower

  Lambda_m(d-h)>=sqrt h/[(G0+1)d]>=sqrt h/(53d).          (4.5)

No positivity replacement is needed in this lower proof. The same rank-
projector Green architecture occurs in the campaign's near_full.txt for
the Casimir energy, but (4.4) separately treats the actual saturated
Berezin spectrum. That distinction is load bearing here.

5. EXPLICIT RADIAL RESISTANCE FILTER FOR SMALL DEFICITS

The occupation layer ell=m-n3 has ell+1 states. Let

  D_ell=(ell+1)(ell+2)/2,
  C_ell=(m-ell)(ell+1)(ell+2), 0<=ell<m.

For the positive diagonal radial filter F=f(ell), exact summation of the
two external root edge conductances gives

  ||F||_HS^2=sum_(ell=0..m)(ell+1)f_ell^2,
  E_C(F)=sum_(ell=0..m-1) C_ell(f_(ell+1)-f_ell)^2.       (5.1)

Roots within the other two modes preserve ell and contribute zero. The
paired Hermitian fundamental generators give exactly one root conductance
per undirected occupation edge under the normalization in Section 2.

Suppose m>=8 and 1<=h<=m^2/128. Put

  r=ceil(sqrt(2h)), B=2r,
  R_rB=sum_(j=r-1..B-1)1/C_j.

Since r<=m/8+1<=m/4, B<=m/2. Define

  f_ell=0, ell<r;
  f_ell=[sum_(j=r-1..ell-1)1/C_j]/R_rB, r<=ell<B;
  f_ell=1, ell>=B.                                      (5.2)

The number of zero occupations is D_(r-1)=r(r+1)/2>=h, so rank F<=d-h.
Overshooting h holes is legal because Q is a maximum branch dimension.
The increments on the intervening edges are (1/C_j)/R_rB. Hence

  E_C(F)=1/R_rB.

There are r+1 summands, each with C_j<=m(2r)(2r+1)<=6mr^2. Therefore
R_rB>=1/(6mr). The unit-valued region contributes

  ||F||_HS^2>=d-D_(B-1),
  D_(B-1)/d=B(B+1)/[(m+1)(m+2)]<=m/[4(m+1)]<1/4.

Using (2.3), then r<=(sqrt 2+1)sqrt h, proves

  E_B(F)/||F||_HS^2<=8r/d
                  <=8(sqrt 2+1)sqrt h/d<20sqrt h/d.       (5.3)

This explicit filter is a polynomial in one occupation observable of degree
<=m, with completely specified rational values. No optimizer or coherent-
hole extremality oracle is used. It is a comparator to every arbitrary
matrix in the lower, rather than an asserted unique minimizer.

6. MODERATE DEFICITS AND EVERY SMALL CARRIER

For any 1<=Q<d let K be the largest integer with D_K<=Q. The positive
occupation cap taper

  f_ell=(K+1-ell)/(K+1), ell<=K; f_ell=0 otherwise

has rank D_K<=Q. Direct summation in (5.1) gives

  E_C(F)/||F||_HS^2=(4m-3K)/(K+2),
  E_B(F)/||F||_HS^2<=4/(K+2)<=4/sqrt Q.                 (6.1)

Here Q<D_(K+1)<=(K+2)^2, since Q<d implies K<m. This is also an
explicit upper for the entire small-Q range, without variational acquisition.

If m>=8, Q=d-h>d/2 and h>m^2/128, then

  Ebar_B(F)<=4sqrt(2)/sqrt d
          <64 (sqrt d/m) sqrt h/d<=64sqrt h/d,            (6.2)

using sqrt d/m<=1 for m>=8. For 1<=m<8, d<=36. Any legal positive
rank-one filter has normalized Berezin energy <=1, while sqrt h/d>=1/36.
Thus Lambda_m(d-h)<=36sqrt h/d in all of those remaining finite cases.
This proves the near-full upper in (1.1) with uniform constant 64.

For Q<=d/32, COLD_CARRIER.txt's harmonic cutoff proves

  Lambda_m(Q)>=(1-exp(-1))/(256sqrt Q).                   (6.3)

For d/32<Q<=d/2, the trace/Schatten inequality
|Tr A|<=||A||_1<=sqrt Q||A||_HS gives scalar mass <=Q/d<=1/2.
Consequently

  E_B(A)>=gamma/2=3/[2(m+3)]
          >=[3sqrt(3)/(8sqrt(32))]Q^(-1/2).               (6.4)

The last step uses Q>d/32 and sqrt d/(m+3)>=sqrt 3/4. The latter follows
from 16d-3(m+3)^2=(m-1)(5m+11)>=0. It also handles finite m for which
the d/32 interval contains no positive integer. Equations (6.1),(6.3),(6.4)
prove the small-Q order up to d/2, with the constant c in Section 1.

To obtain (1.2), for Q<=d/2, f_d(Q) is between Q^(-1/2)/sqrt2 and
Q^(-1/2). For Q>d/2, f_d(Q) is between sqrt h/d and sqrt(2)sqrt h/d.
The small-Q lower constant c and (4.5) cover both ranges, and the upper
constants 4sqrt2 and 64 cover them. At Q=d use F=I and exact storage.

7. COMPLETE SATURATION AND PRECISION INTERFACE

Let Q_min(epsilon) denote the minimum legal quantum branch dimension at
whole-state error <=epsilon. For the pure orbit, a necessary real bound is

  Q_min(epsilon)>=1/[epsilon^2/c^2+1/d],                 (7.1)

while the explicit filters give a sufficient integer choice

  Q_try=min{d, ceil(1/[epsilon^2/64^2+1/d])}.            (7.2)

For thermal seeds replace c in (7.1) by (1-qbar)^4 c; the same upper
choice works. These inequalities include the rounding and exact-storage
endpoint. They should not be abbreviated to epsilon^(-2) at all accuracies.

For any budget Q>d/2, its missing quantum dimensions h=d-Q produce
Theta(sqrt h/d) error. Thus the deficit has order d^2 epsilon^2 in the
near-full precision range, subject to integer endpoints and the constants
in (7.1)-(7.2). Error below c/d (or the thermal constant times c/d) forces
Q=d, because every Q<=d-1 has error at least c/sqrt[d(d-1)]>c/d.
At epsilon well above d^(-1/2) the familiar Q=Theta(epsilon^(-2)) order
applies. Around and below that boundary the rank approaches the physical
dimension; exact error zero is achieved at Q=d.

An explicit endpoint comparator is F=I-P_v, rank d-1:

  E_B(F)/||F||_HS^2=[1-d/d_(2m)]/(d-1),
  d_(2m)=(2m+1)(2m+2)/2.

Its error is asymptotic to 3/(4d). The lower here establishes the same
order, NOT that this constant is optimal or that its one-dimensional hole
is extremal.

8. FINITE CHANNEL, COSTS, CONTROLS AND SCOPE

For any explicit positive radial F above, let s=Tr F^2 and
Phi_F(X)=(d/s)int F_U X F_U dU. Haar averaging makes this unital TP.
The unchanged COLD_CARRIER.txt argument makes pure coherent whole-trace
error exactly E_B(F)/s; positive coherent mixtures give the thermal upper.

The conjugated radial filter depends only on a CP^2 direction and is a
degree-<=m polynomial in the rank-one fundamental projector. The composed
channel therefore has fundamental direction bidegree <=(2m,2m).
Write |v1|^2=t1, |v2|^2=(1-t1)t2, |v3|^2=(1-t1)(1-t2), with Haar density
2(1-t1)dt1 dt2 and two independent 2pi phases. Each phase grid of 2m+1
nodes exactly kills its nonzero frequencies up to 2m. After phase averaging,
the integrand, including the density, has degree <=2m+1 in t1 and <=2m in
t2. Positive (m+1)-node Gauss-Legendre rules thus integrate it exactly.
This is an explicit positive rule with

  C_frame=[(2m+1)(m+1)]^2.

Its exact Kraus operators are sqrt(d w_U/s) F_U. They have rank <=Q.
Factor each through a Q-dimensional branch, store the public frame node,
and decode by the corresponding embedding. The instrument acquires its
frame from the supplied state itself. No classical direction is given for
free, and none of the node indices is omitted from memory accounting.
Maximum branch Q and alphabet C_frame give D<=C_frame Q. At Q=d, exact
full-carrier storage uses one label and D=d. Runtime, finite circuit gates,
conditioning, and synthesis precision of ideal algebraic nodes/operators
remain unproved; a finite CPTP implementation is the scope of the resource
claim. No optimal classical-label or total-memory theorem is inferred.
