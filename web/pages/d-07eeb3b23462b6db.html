# Team D: controlled acquisition of reusable experiment information

Status (2026-10-06): a finite known-model controlled-source extension of Li–Liu's uniform exact-conversion theorem has survived independent reconstruction by team lead, d_math, and check_selection. External validation and a comprehensive novelty search remain outstanding. Classical adaptive hypothesis-testing gains are not claimed as discoveries. The exact arbitrary-output extension is the substantive provisional result. Computational implementation of the exact simulator is not supplied.

## Exact interface

There is a fixed finite hidden state set Θ. The experiment catalogue consists of finitely many known channels F^a_i(x), each on a finite alphabet with strictly positive probabilities, with positive fixed costs c_a. An action may leave some state pairs indistinguishable, but the catalogue jointly distinguishes every pair. At each step the source policy chooses action a using past observations and state-independent randomization, pays c_a, and receives a fresh conditional sample F^a_i. No state transitions, unknown parameters, drift, or interventions that change the hypothesis are allowed.

The target G is one fixed known finite full-support pairwise-identified channel. A target policy repeatedly samples G, stops at T with E_i T finite in every state, and reports a finite-valued Z. Source reproduction means P_i(Y=z)=P_i^G(Z=z) exactly, simultaneously for every i,z. Cost is C_N=Σ_{n≤N}c_{A_n}; it is not runtime, memory, or total scientific acquisition expense.

Define d^a_ij=KL(F^a_i||F^a_j), g_ij=KL(G_i||G_j). For each i define the linear programme

    α_i = min Σ_a c_a w_a
          subject to w_a≥0 and Σ_a w_a d^a_ij ≥ g_ij for all j≠i.

The dual is max Σ_j λ_j g_ij subject to λ_j≥0 and Σ_j λ_j d^a_ij≤c_a for every action a. These are row-specific joint constraints, not individually maximal pairwise divergences. A minimizing w_i corresponds to q_i(a)=w_i(a)/Σ_aw_i(a), and α_i= c(q_i) max_j g_ij/d_ij(q_i). This follows by homogeneity; minimization is finite and attained because the costs are positive and a uniformly sampled catalogue distinguishes every pair.

## Provisional controlled-source theorem

There is C<∞, depending on the known catalogue, costs, target G, and chosen minimizing mixtures, such that EVERY finite-output integrable stopping G-policy has one controlled-source exact reproduction satisfying

    E_i C_N ≤ α_i E_i^G T + C sqrt(E_i^G T),  for every i.

The coefficients are sharp: exact reproduction of the full n-sample G transcript by any adaptive source policy satisfies E_i C_N ≥ n α_i. Consequently, if every α_i<1, the controlled catalogue eventually reproduces every target policy available under a common statewise budget within that same budget. If any α_i>1, it cannot reproduce sufficiently long target transcripts at the same statewise budget. Equality α_i=1 requires lower-order analysis and is unresolved by this argument.

This generalizes the fixed-source theorem in arXiv2608.19897v2, Theorem 2, by allowing the source experiment selected during verification to depend on a candidate state. Its coefficients reduce to classical controlled-testing allocation coefficients when the terminal task is state identification; exact preservation of arbitrary finite terminal experiments is the additional conclusion. This is our deduction, not the authors' claim.

## Independent proof reconstruction: finite terminal experiment

Fix any finite common-support terminal experiment H with h_i(y)>0. Write λ_ij(y)=log(h_i(y)/h_j(y)), Δ(H)=max_ijy|λ_ij(y)|, d=|Θ|-1, η≤min(1/2,1/(2d)), and g=log(1/η). Choose a fixed globally identifying catalogue mixture q_0. Every selected verifier mixture q_k distinguishes k from each j≠k. It need not distinguish alternatives from one another.

1. Sample q_0 for O(g) steps and use maximum likelihood to choose K. Bounded increments and Hoeffding give P_i(K≠i)≤η simultaneously. Given K=k, independently propose y from H_k.

2. On a fresh q_k stream accumulate S_kj(n)=Σ_{t≤n}log(F^{q_k}_k(X_t)/F^{q_k}_j(X_t)); the label of the chosen action is included in X. Stop when either all S_kj≥λ_kj(y)+g (success), or any S_jk≥g (rejection). Give success a fixed tie convention. Under true k, each forward process has positive drift. Under any wrong true i, the single reverse S_ik process has positive drift, so rejection occurs in mean O(g), uniformly in y. This direct rejection criterion is the essential change from Li–Liu's all-alternative failure region.

3. Under k, exp(S_jk) is a nonnegative mean-one martingale, so Ville plus union bound gives own-state rejection probability ≤dη. Thus p_k(k,y)≥1-dη. Under wrong i, the stopped likelihood density gives

       p_i(k,y) ≤ η h_i(y)/h_k(y) · p_k(k,y).

   Both stopping times are integrable under every relevant state; the likelihood identity follows by summing finite stopped histories. The chosen q_k is fixed conditional on observed k,y, and all future sampling is fresh, so this conditioning does not depend on the hidden state.

4. Let π_i(k)=P_i(K=k). Before thinning, accepted mass is Σ_k π_i(k)h_k(y)p_i(k,y). Its own-state part is at most h_i(y); each cross-state part is at most ηh_i(y). Independently thin success by a_η=(1-η)/(1+dη). Then accepted mass Q_i(y)≤(1-η)h_i(y), while its total mass is at least a_η(1-η)(1-dη). Failure probabilities satisfy

       η ≤ δ_i=1-Σ_yQ_i(y) ≤ 2(d+1)η.

5. Define R_i(y)=(h_i(y)-Q_i(y))/δ_i. This is a full common-support probability law, and

       Δ(R) ≤ Δ(H)+log(2(d+1)/η).

   On failure run an exact fixed-length reproduction of R using q_0. The construction in Li–Liu Lemma 8 uses a sufficiently accurate finite-state classifier confusion matrix A and output kernel A^{-1}R. Its length is O(1+Δ(R)); invertibility and nonnegativity follow from sufficiently small classifier error relative to the smallest residual probability ratio. Its contribution to expected source cost is O(η[1+Δ(H)+g]). Final mass Q_i+δ_iR_i is exactly H_i. This step requires exact probability computations; no efficiency guarantee is inferred.

6. Under true i, the correct verifier's simultaneous-passage benchmark is

       b_i(y)=max_{j≠i} [λ_ij(y)]_+/d_ij(q_i).

   Independent bounded positive-drift random walks yield E_iτ≤t+C√(1+t)+C for t=max_j boundary_j/drift_j: after n_0=ceil(t), Hoeffding bounds the tail by d exp(-γs²/(n_0+s)); splitting s≤n_0 and s>n_0 sums to O(√n_0)+O(1). This uses only the true-i row of q_i. Adding g to each threshold costs O(g). Wald's identity turns verifier sample count into c(q_i) times expected cost. Wrong-candidate verifiers cost O(g) uniformly in y; classifier cost is O(g). Therefore finite-H reproduction costs

       E_i C_N ≤ B_i(H)+C[√(1+B_i(H))+g+η(1+Δ(H))],
       B_i(H)=E_{Y~H_i} max_j c(q_i)[λ_ij(Y)]_+/d_ij(q_i).

All constants are uniform over H and its finite output size. A finite number of q_i makes the constants simultaneous across states.

## From finite H to arbitrary integrable target policies

For bounded T≤m, stopped target likelihoods satisfy

    λ_ij(Z) ≤ E_i[S^G_ij(T)|Z],
    S^G_ij(T)=g_ij T+M_ij(T),
    E_i M_ij(T)^2=v_ij E_iT.

Conditional Jensen, positive-part convexity and the finite maximum give

    B_i(H) ≤ α_i E_iT + C sqrt(E_iT).

Bounded log increments also give Δ(H)≤mL_G. Set η=η_0/m in the finite-H bound, obtaining exact reproduction cost α_i E_iT+C√m, independent of the policy and output range.

For unbounded integrable T, preserve the state-independent target stop/continue randomization at time zero. For the continuing branch split its target signal path at s_k=2^k. For every live finite target history at a stage boundary, apply the bounded conversion to the finite continuation that reports either its terminal output or the next live target history. Restart from fresh source experiments with the selected continuation policy. Induction gives exactly the target law of reaching each live history, hence terminal output law. The cost-leading terms sum to α_i E_iT by the target tail-sum identity. The remainder is bounded by a constant times

    Σ_k P_i(T>s_{k-1}) √s_k ≤ C sqrt(E_iT).

To see the bound, split at the dyadic scale closest to μ=E_iT: earlier terms sum geometrically to O(√μ), later terms use Markov P(T>s)≤μ/s and also sum geometrically to O(√μ). If μ<1, the initial continuation probability is at most μ and remaining scales begin at 1, giving O(μ)≤O(√μ). Exact reach probabilities make the probability of infinitely many entered stages zero; total expected cost is finite. The source cost is bounded below by c_min>0 per observation, so finite expected cost implies finite expected source duration. All policies are pasted at observed stopping times; selected future action kernels are state-independent functions of past outputs.

## Exact sharpness and dual certificates

Under any adaptive controlled source, the likelihood ratio of its stopped transcript between i and j is Σ_{t≤N}log(F_i^{A_t}(X_t)/F_j^{A_t}(X_t)); the action-selection factors cancel because the policy is state independent. With finite E_iN and bounded increments, martingale optional stopping gives

    KL(stopped law_i||stopped law_j)=Σ_a E_iN_a d^a_ij.

Output data processing for exact reproduction of n independent target outputs yields Σ_a E_iN_a d^a_ij≥ng_ij for all j. Setting w_a=E_iN_a/n proves E_iC_N≥nα_i. No testing-specific decision argument is used. Dual λ certifies the same lower bound directly, by multiplying and summing the directional constraints.

For one frozen iid mixture q, action labels give pairwise KL d_ij(q)=Σ_aq_a d^a_ij and expected cost c(q)E_iN. Its minimax first-order conversion coefficient is R(q)=max_ij c(q)g_ij/d_ij(q); sharpness again uses full target transcripts, while sufficiency is Li–Liu Theorem 2. Minimizing R over q is one joint LP

    max t: Σ_a c_aw_a=1, w_a≥0, Σ_aw_ad^a_ij/g_ij≥t for every ordered i≠j;
    R*=1/t*.

Its dual is an adversarial probability distribution λ over ordered pairs with Σλ=1 and Σ_ijλ_ij d^a_ij/g_ij≤νc_a for every a, minimizing ν. These are technology comparison certificates conditional on a specified known state class. A KL matrix is not a record of observed scientific facts and does not replace the likelihood laws required by the exact simulator.

## Decisive example: pairwise maxima fail even with adaptation

Independently checked numerical construction supplied by check_selection: two Bernoulli source rows (.9,.1,.899) and (.9,.899,.1); target Bernoulli row (.88,.12,.13), all costs one. Every ordered pair has max_a d^a_ij>g_ij. Yet state 0 has source rates (a,b) and (b,a), a=1.757779661868976,b=.000005522882104565. Summing the two constraints gives

    E_0N/n ≥ (g_01+g_02)/(a+b)=1.6836117045179895>1.

Thus separate per-pair best sources cannot establish same-budget dominance even for arbitrary adaptive sampling. The standard-library script work/team_d_experiments/design_certificate.py solves the two-source fixed-mixture LP by enumerating affine-line intersections and prints a primal/dual certificate. Its fixed-iid minimax coefficient is 1.7004966508759267. The numerical primal/dual gap is 1.1e-16; this is a numerical check, while the two-constraint analytic argument is the universal proof. A simpler exact symbolic coordinate-sensor obstruction and a stronger positive all-output adaptive-versus-fixed separation are documented in the independent checker and d_math artifacts.

## Positive reversal: adaptation changes universal same-budget convertibility

Use three states and three unit-cost binary actions a=0,1,2. Action a returns 1 with probability .1 under state a and .9 under the other two states. The target is the ternary channel with probability .8 on the true-state symbol and .1 on each alternative symbol. All laws are full support. For each source action, every pair involving its named state has directed KL D=.8log9; other pairs have KL zero. All target directions have g=.7log8.

The optimal candidate-state mixture puts all verifier observations on action i. It distinguishes true i from both alternatives at rate D. Every LP optimum is therefore α_i=g/D=.8280953015625377: feasibility follows from that single action, and no action can give any relevant pair more than D, proving the converse. The controlled exact-conversion theorem gives source expected cost ≤.8281 E_iT+C√E_iT simultaneously in every state for every target terminal policy. At every sufficiently large common statewise budget n, this is ≤n.

A fixed iid action mixture q supplies i/j KL D(q_i+q_j). At least one pair has q_i+q_j≤2/3. Thus every such mixture has worst-state first-order conversion coefficient at least 3g/(2D)=1.2421429523438066>1; uniform q attains the minimax bound. Some pair of statewise target n-transcript output laws demands more than n expected source observations, for every n. No amount of stopping flexibility or terminal randomization rescues that frozen iid mixture. This comparison retains the full target-output law, and the fixed source control is equally informed and allowed arbitrary adaptive stopping. It excludes arbitrary predetermined time-varying action schedules and state-independent mixtures of entire schedules; those stronger controls require their own converse.

This source model itself already appears in Nitinawarat–Atia–Veeravalli (2013), Section V, Table I, with binary outcomes interchanged, as d_example verified. The candidate new content is the exact uniform all-terminal-output conversion conclusion, not inventing this sensor family or adaptive classification. ## Novelty, costs and scientific route

Li–Liu v2 (28 Aug 2026) Theorem 2 / Appendix C is the base exact-conversion result. The paper explicitly fixes the experiment within each policy; its conclusion identifies adaptive source choice as an extension. Nitinawarat–Atia–Veeravalli (2013) and Chernoff (1958/1959) already establish controlled finite-hypothesis testing rates and gains, so the allocation LP and a gain in correct classification are not new. Closest sequential sufficiency precedents include Greenshtein (1996) and Greenshtein–Torgersen (1997); full-text access remains unavailable in the prior-art worker, so historical novelty is provisional. See sources/d_prior and results/d_prior for verified primary-source metadata and failed accesses.

Charged by theorem: all source samples and their stated action costs, including classifier, wrong verifiers, residual repair and dyadic restarts. Uncharged and unresolved: specifying/learning Θ and every F,G, computing all output probabilities/continuation kernels, exact verifier acceptance probabilities, residual inverse kernels, infinite family of continuation policies, numerical precision, runtime, RAM, storage and changed-world recalibration. Recoverable source snapshots are preserved. No claim of an efficient runnable exact simulator or real scientific discovery system is made.

Concrete downstream use: compare a calibrated diagnostic or measurement catalogue against a standard fixed instrument while preserving every later finite decision law, rather than one classification metric. The first falsifiable next step is to implement exact finite rational channels and residual repair for small bounded target policies, compare realized laws by exact enumeration, and measure the compiler/precision costs. The next theoretical task is to turn the existence construction into an effective compiler with stated rational-bit complexity. Applying it to actual research requires an independently justified finite model class and likelihood calibration; those are the acquisition bottleneck, not supplied by this theorem.
