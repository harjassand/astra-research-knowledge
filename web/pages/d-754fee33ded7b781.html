1. EXACT LOCAL CHANNEL INTERFACE AND CONCLUSION

B is finite dimensional. Sigma>0 is a density on B. Phi is a Schrodinger
CPTP channel, Phi(sigma)=sigma, and ONE full-domain CPTP broadcaster
mathcal B:B->B tensor B has both marginals Phi. Let H=Phi^dagger denote
the Heisenberg UCP channel. Require either of the equivalent sufficient
interfaces:
 (i) H is self-adjoint in the GNS inner product Tr[sigma A^dagger C];
 (ii) H is KMS self-adjoint in Tr[A^dagger sigma^(1/2)C sigma^(1/2)]
      AND commutes with every modular conjugation sigma^(it)(.)sigma^(-it).
Modular covariance by itself, without reversibility, is not the interface.
Hilbert-space-positive spectrum, primitivity and a spectral gap are absent.

The weighted C4 theorem supplies ONE finite PURE sigma-Petz canonical EB
Schrodinger comparator Psi, independent of the tested state and reference:

 sigma=sum_i p_i pi_i, p_i>0, pi_i pure,
 M_i=p_i sigma^(-1/2)pi_i sigma^(-1/2),
 Psi(X)=sum_i Tr(M_i X)pi_i.                              (1)

Thus sum_i M_i=I, Psi is CPTP and fixes sigma. Define
 Gamma(X)=sigma^(1/2)X sigma^(1/2),
 T_Phi=Gamma^(-1/2)Phi Gamma^(1/2),
 T_Psi=Gamma^(-1/2)Psi Gamma^(1/2),
 where Gamma^(+/-1/2)(X)=sigma^(+/-1/4)X sigma^(+/-1/4).
Weighted C4 becomes the ordinary Hilbert-Schmidt order

 I-T_Psi <=4(I-T_Phi).                                  (2)

For EVERY density rho on B, using natural logarithms and HALF trace errors,

 e(rho)=||rho-Psi(rho)||_1/2,
 b(rho)=||rho-Phi(rho)||_1/2,
 e(rho)^3 <=(6+3sqrt(2))D(rho||sigma)b(rho).              (3)

For EVERY finite reference R, EVERY density rho_RB, and EVERY faithful
reference tau_R, put omega=tau_R tensor sigma,
 e_R=||rho_RB-(id_R tensor Psi)(rho_RB)||_1/2,
 b_R=||rho_RB-(id_R tensor Phi)(rho_RB)||_1/2.
The SAME local Psi satisfies simultaneously

 e_R^3 <=(12+6sqrt(2))D(rho_RB||omega)b_R.                (4)

If tau_R is nonfaithful, the statement remains valid when rho_RB is
supported on supp(tau_R) tensor B, by restricting R to that support.
In particular, when rho_B=sigma, choose tau_R=rho_R on its support. Then

 e_R^3 <=(12+6sqrt(2))I(R:B)_rho b_R.                    (5)

Equations (4)-(5) also hold for normal densities on a separable reference
Hilbert space with finite displayed relative entropy/mutual information;
the proof in section 6 requires no infinite-reference inverse or entropy
derivative. Phi and Psi still act only on the fixed finite system B.

2. WHY THE GNS AND WEIGHTED INTERFACES MATCH

For completeness, GNS self-adjointness alone in this finite CP interface
implies KMS self-adjointness and modular covariance. In HS space let
R_sigma(X)=X sigma and L_sigma(X)=sigma X. GNS symmetry says
 R_sigma H=H^dagger R_sigma.
Both H and its HS adjoint are dagger preserving. Conjugate this equality
by the antiunitary X->X^dagger to obtain
 L_sigma H=H^dagger L_sigma.
Eliminating H^dagger shows H commutes with
 Delta=L_sigma R_sigma^(-1), hence with Delta^(it). Since
 Gamma=R_sigma Delta^(1/2), it follows that
 Gamma H=H^dagger Gamma, the KMS symmetry relation. Conversely KMS
symmetry plus commutation with Delta gives GNS symmetry by the same
identity. Adjoints have the same modular covariance property.

KMS self-adjointness yields HS self-adjoint T_Phi and T_Psi. The weighted
C4 order on observables is transported by the isometry
A->Gamma^(1/2)A, because
 Gamma^(1/2)H Gamma^(-1/2)=Gamma^(-1/2)Phi Gamma^(1/2).
This proves (2) in the actual Schrodinger implementation. The pure
refinement in the old C4 erratum is essential for (1); a coarse mixed
posterior comparator cannot simply be assigned the pure trace constant.

3. FINITE SCALAR AND COMPLETE ENERGY GATES

For a faithful rho define q=sqrt(rho),
 E_Phi(rho)=Tr[q(I-T_Phi)(q)],
 E_Psi(rho)=Tr[q(I-T_Psi)(q)],
 J_Phi,sigma(rho)=Tr[(rho-Phi(rho))(log rho-log sigma)].

The already audited PURE canonical scalar gate gives
 e(rho)^2 <=c E_Psi(rho), c=1+1/sqrt(2).
The finite GNS/modular Kraus argument gives the GENUINE entropy-production
gate J_Phi,sigma(rho)>=4E_Phi(rho), for every faithful physical rho.
Together with (2), these imply

 e(rho)^2 <=c J_Phi,sigma(rho).                          (6)

For a finite reference, the weight factors cancel through the idle map:
 Gamma_(tau tensor sigma)^(-1/2)(id_R tensor Theta)
                  Gamma_(tau tensor sigma)^(1/2)
       =id_HS(R) tensor T_Theta, Theta=Phi or Psi.         (7)
No commutation of rho with either marginal/reference is assumed.
Thus the energy depends on sigma and the physical joint rho, but not tau.

Order (2) was stated on Hermitian HS vectors. It extends to the COMPLEX
HS space: for X=A+iC with Hermitian A,C, a HS-self-adjoint dagger-preserving
form Q has <X,QX>=<A,QA>+<C,QC>; the imaginary cross terms cancel.
Tensoring its PSD remainder with the identity on HS(R) therefore proves
 E_Psi,R(rho_RB)<=4 E_Phi,R(rho_RB)                     (8)
on every joint root q=sqrt(rho_RB), including entangled states.

The canonical BLOCK-COLUMN gate, rather than a global-purity assertion,
gives e_R^2<=c_R E_Psi,R with c_R=2+sqrt(2). The complete GNS entropy
gate holds for every faithful joint state and every faithful product
reference, J_Phi,R>=4E_Phi,R. Hence

 e_R^2 <=c_R J_Phi,R.                                  (9)

There is no modular averaging of Psi in (6) or (9). The trace gate uses
the original pure canonical Psi; the entropy gate uses Phi. Psi need not
be GNS symmetric or modular covariant. Nor is id_R tensor Psi globally
pure canonical or globally EB on RB. Its action is EB on system B, and
only the local self-compatibility of Phi was needed to construct it.

4. THE ORBIT INTEGRAL AND COEFFICIENTS

Use the SAME fixed comparator along the whole Poisson orbit
 P_t=e^(t(Phi-I))=e^(-t)sum_{n>=0}t^n Phi^n/n!.
It is CPTP and fixes sigma. For faithful rho, rho_t=P_t(rho)>=e^(-t)rho>0.
The exact finite entropy derivative is D'(rho_t||sigma)=-J(rho_t).
If e(x)^2<=A J(x) holds for EVERY faithful state with one fixed Psi, then
 integral_0^infinity e(rho_t)^2 dt<=A D(rho||sigma).
Indeed integrate to finite time first and use D(rho_T||sigma)>=0;
no ergodicity or orbit limit is required.

Trace contraction and Phi-I commuting with P_t give
 T(rho_t,rho)<=t b(rho).
For any CPTP Psi, |e(x)-e(y)|<=2T(x,y). Therefore
 e(rho_t)>=(e(rho)-2t b(rho))_+.
For b>0 its entire squared lower cone has integral e(rho)^3/(6b).
Consequently e(rho)^3<=6A D(rho||sigma)b(rho).

Equation (6) has A=c and gives (3), since 6c=6+3sqrt(2).
For (9) run precisely the same argument with local maps on RB and
stationary reference omega. It has A=c_R and gives (4), since

This also checks the complete GNS convention and full/half trace factors.

6. SEPARABLE NORMAL REFERENCES: COMPRESSION PLUS FLAG

Let R be separable, rho a normal density on R tensor B and tau a normal
density with D_0=D(rho||tau tensor sigma)<infinity. Finite D ensures support
inside supp(tau) tensor B. Restrict to supp(tau), on which tau is faithful.
The finite-support case is already proved. Otherwise choose increasing
finite-rank spectral projections P_n of tau tending strongly to I. Let
R_n=P_n R direct-sum C f_n and define the NORMAL CPTP channel

 Q_n(X)=P_n X P_n direct-sum Tr[(I-P_n)X]|f_n><f_n|.

It has Kraus P_n (with the natural inclusion into R_n) and
|f_n><u_j| over an ONB of the tail. Put
 rho_n=(Q_n tensor id_B)(rho), tau_n=Q_n(tau).
Tau_n is a faithful finite density; the tail flag has positive weight.
This is a trace-preserving compression, with no normalization or
postselection on a rare event. It commutes exactly with either local map:
 (Q_n tensor id_B)(id_R tensor Theta)
        =(id_Rn tensor Theta)(Q_n tensor id_B), Theta=Phi,Psi.

Normal relative-entropy data processing gives the EXACT bound

 D(rho_n||tau_n tensor sigma)<=D_0.                     (10)

For explicit trace convergence embed R_n into R by mapping the flag to
any unit vector w_n in the tail. Write a_n=Tr[(I-P_n)rho_R]->0 and
chi_n=Tr_R[((I-P_n)tensor I)rho((I-P_n)tensor I)]. The embedded output is
 bar(rho_n)=(P_n tensor I)rho(P_n tensor I)+|w_n><w_n|tensor chi_n.
The tail blocks each have trace a_n, and Hilbert-Schmidt Holder gives
||P_n rho(I-P_n)||_1<=sqrt(a_n(1-a_n)). Hence

 ||bar(rho_n)-rho||_1<=2a_n+2sqrt(a_n(1-a_n))->0.        (11)

All projections in this estimate act on R and are tensored with I_B.
Embeddings commute with the local maps and preserve trace norms. The
2-Lipschitz estimate for the HALF error then proves e_n->e and b_n->b.
Apply the finite theorem and (10):
 e_n^3<=(12+6sqrt(2))D_0 b_n.
Taking the limit proves (4) for normal R with finite D_0. No convergence of
the compressed entropies, infinite-dimensional log derivative, unbounded
Gamma_tau inverse, or infinite-reference C4 construction was used.

If tau=rho_R, Q_n(tau) is EXACTLY the marginal of rho_n. If additionally
rho_B=sigma, trace preservation of Q_n leaves the B marginal exactly sigma.
Thus D(rho_n||tau_n tensor sigma)=I(R_n:B)_rho_n and (10) bounds it by
I(R:B)_rho; this proves the normal version of (5), with the same fixed Psi.
Finite D_0 is part of this normal statement, including the zero-b endpoint.

The established normal DPI used in (10) was checked in the actual primary
research text: A.S. Holevo and M.E. Shirokov, Mutual and Coherent Information
