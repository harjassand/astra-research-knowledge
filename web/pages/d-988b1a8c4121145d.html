1. SUPPLIED STRUCTURE AND EXACT ARCHIVE THEOREM
Fix a compact connected SIMPLE group G in the physics metric of
GENERAL_COHERENT_FIRST_BIAS_PROOF.txt (long-root squared1). Supply any
nontrivial dominant carrier H_Lambda, Lambda=sum n_i omega_i,
N=sum n_i>=1, known represented generators and highestweight orbit.
An unknown member theta determines a Hermitian polynomial P_theta
of degree<=D in the NATURAL generators T_i/N, with coefficient sum
at most A and ||P_theta||_infinity<=h0. The degree, coefficient and
norm bounds are fixed public structure, not inferred from capacity.
Supply n IDENTICAL copies of the SAME unknown member

  rho_theta=exp(P_theta)/Tr exp(P_theta).

The copies share the same coefficients/parameter; an independent
unknown member on each copy is OUTSIDE this archive theorem. No
broadcaster, calibration or side channel is assumed. Set

  g=dimG, L_G=max(1,max_i||omega_i||),
  A_eff=A L_G^D, B_i=T_i/(L_GN),
  v_G=2(max_i||omega_i||)||rho||/L_G^2,
  M=binom(g+D,D)-1.                                    (1)

The contractions B_i have symbols a_i(x)=Tr(P_x B_i) in[-1,1].
Let K_infinity be the explicit uniform full-likelihood source constant
from GENERAL_COHERENT_FIRST_BIAS_PROOF.txt Eq(19), and put

  H_star=max(h0,A_eff),
  kappa=(v_G/2){A_eff D(D-1)
                         +exp(H_star+A_eff)A_eff^2D^2},
  C_arch=exp(h0)K_infinity/sqrt(2)+4sqrt(2)kappa.          (2)

For every 0<epsilon<=1 and n>=1 there is ONE parameter-independent
classical encode/decode channel on H_Lambda^tensor n, using at most

  B_arch <=(ceil(2n A_eff/epsilon)+2)^M

classical labels, such that uniformly over all supplied members,

  (1/2)||Decoder Encoder(rho_theta^tensor n)
                            -rho_theta^tensor n||_1
       <= C_arch sqrt(n)/N+epsilon.                     (3)

The physical trace norm in (3) is on the FULL n-copy output, without
first-harmonic restriction. Risk may be capped by1. The retained
classical archive payload is at most

  ceil[M log2(ceil(2n A_eff/epsilon)+2)] bits.             (4)

Thus, at fixed G,D,A,h0, the retained payload is O(log n+log1/epsilon),
with no n-tuple of labels and no required logN factor in the LABEL
count. Public carrier/apparatus description, sampling/preparation
precision, construction time, scratch space and decoder output space
are separately charged in Sections7--8. This is an ideal finite-POVM
existence/explicit-integral theorem, not an efficient finite-bit compiler.
In particular n=o(N^2) allows vanishing full-state error with epsilon->0.

If carrier labels, n and the chosen precision are not shared public
metadata, an archive must also store their fixed-rank integer/rational
descriptions. This adds O_G(logN+logn+log1/epsilon) bits for a fixed
standard precision coding, rather than silently treating those data
as known to a later decoder. Real apparatus-node tables are NOT
short metadata and are charged separately below.

If A_eff=0, or the whole family has only scalar Hamiltonians, every
state is I/d and a zero-bit constant decoder is available separately.
The trivial N=0 carrier is also zero-bit. No group-rank uniformity,
arbitrary degree/conditioning-free Gibbs family, independent-per-copy
parameter compression, all-Q optimality or unrestricted N93 is claimed.

2. POINTWISE COHERENT VARIANCE, NOT AN AVERAGED SURROGATE
For EVERY unit coherent vector v_x, its mean vector for the raw
generators is Ad_x Lambda. Therefore

  sum_i Var_x(B_i)
    =[C_Lambda-||Lambda||^2]/(L_G^2N^2)
    =2(Lambda,rho)/(L_G^2N^2)<=v_G/N.                    (5)

Each individual variance is at most v_G/N, uniformly in the point,
carrier face and label ratios. This is distinct from the earlier
Haar-averaged per-generator energy and requires no multiplicity
eigenbasis or selected-copy invariance.

For a contraction word W=B_(i_1)...B_(i_l), let
w(x)=a_(i_1)(x)...a_(i_l)(x). Exact telescoping gives

  W-wI=sum_(j=1..l)
       (B_(i_1)...B_(i_(j-1)))
       (B_(i_j)-a_(i_j)I) product_(k>j)a_(i_k).

Apply it to v_x. Each centered trailing factor has vector norm at
most sqrt(v_G/N), and every prefix/scalar suffix is a contraction.
Thus

  ||(W-wI)v_x||<=l sqrt(v_G/N).                          (6)

There is a stronger EXPECTATION bias. In the term with j, center the
adjoint prefix before pairing with (B_(i_j)-a_(i_j))v_x; its constant
part vanishes because that factor has mean zero. The same telescoping
applied to the adjoint prefix bounds its centered vector norm by
(j-1)sqrt(v_G/N). Hence that expectation term is at most
(j-1)v_G/N. Sum over j:

  |Tr(P_x W)-w(x)|<=l(l-1)v_G/(2N).                     (7)

No commutation of the word factors is used. This polynomial l^2
estimate is sharper than summing all centered-word subsets.

3. HAMILTONIAN SYMBOL AND UNIFORM POINTWISE EXPONENTIAL ESTIMATE
Re-express the supplied polynomial in contractions B_i, charging
coefficient sum A_eff. Since the represented operator is Hermitian,
replace its formal expression by half itself plus its formal adjoint.
This reverses words and conjugates coefficients, preserves P exactly,
and does not increase the coefficient sum or degree. Its COMMUTATIVE
symbol f_theta(x) is then real, |f_theta|<=A_eff.

Equations(6)--(7) and coefficient triangle/Minkowski give pointwise

  |Tr(P_x P_theta)-f_theta(x)|
                   <=A_eff D(D-1)v_G/(2N),
  Tr(P_x(P_theta-f_theta(x)I)^2)
                   <=A_eff^2D^2v_G/N.                  (8)

For the second line, P_theta and f_theta are Hermitian/real, so it
is exactly the squared vector norm, including all cancellation and
noncommutative order. It is a second moment about f, not an assumed
variance about an equal quantum expectation.

Scalar Taylor of exp on[-H_star,H_star], applied to the spectral
measure of the Hermitian P_theta in v_x, gives

  |Tr(P_x exp(P_theta))-exp(f_theta(x))|
   <=exp(f_theta(x))|Tr(P_xP_theta)-f_theta(x)|
      +(exp(H_star)/2)Tr(P_x(P_theta-f_theta(x)I)^2).

Dividing by exp(f_theta)>=exp(-A_eff) proves the UNIFORM relative bound

  r_theta(x)=Tr(P_x exp(P_theta))/exp(f_theta(x)),
  |r_theta(x)-1|<=kappa/N.                               (9)

This full pointwise exponential estimate is the decisive quantum-to-
classical interface. A mere first-score or averaged variance estimate
would not justify the latent exponential family below.

4. NORMALIZED HUSIMI LAW VERSUS A FINITE EXPONENTIAL FAMILY
Let mu be Haar probability on the coherent orbit, z_theta=tau exp(P_theta).
The product coherent POVM applied to one rho has density

  q_theta(x)=Tr(P_x exp(P_theta))/z_theta.

It is normalized because integral P_x=I/d. Define the genuinely
CLASSICAL comparison density

  p_theta(x)=exp(f_theta(x))/Z_theta,
  Z_theta=integral exp(f_theta(x))dmu(x).                 (10)

Then q_theta/p_theta=r_theta/E_(p_theta)r_theta. If N>=2kappa,
u=kappa/N<=1/2, so |q_theta/p_theta-1|<=2u/(1-u)<=4u.
The scalar log bound log t<=t-1 yields

  D(q_theta||p_theta)<=chi^2(q_theta||p_theta)
                     <=16kappa^2/N^2.                  (11)

This uses normalization on BOTH sides; (9) alone would only give a
linear upper without the cancellation. For n common-parameter copies,
classical relative entropy adds and Pinsker gives

  TV(q_theta^tensor n,p_theta^tensor n)
                           <=sqrt(8)kappa sqrt(n)/N.     (12)

The classical Pinsker constant can be checked by log-sum reduction to
a binary optimal set and the second derivative of binary entropy,
1/[x(1-x)]>=4. No source-reported large-n approximation is used.

Merge word coefficients with the SAME commutative monomial, discarding
the scalar parameter that cancels in (10). With t_alpha(x)=a(x)^alpha,
1<=|alpha|<=D, the count is M in(1), and

  f_theta(x)=gamma_(theta,0)+sum_alpha gamma_(theta,alpha)t_alpha(x),
  sum_alpha|gamma_(theta,alpha)|<=A_eff.                  (13)

For a latent tuple x=(x_1,...,x_n), S_alpha(x)=sum_j t_alpha(x_j) lies
in[-n,n]. The comparison density is proportional to exp(gamma_theta.S).
These M sums are the standard sufficient statistic for the classical
exponential family; the parameter is not acquired by the encoder.

5. FINITE STATISTIC BINS AND A COMMON QUANTUM DECODER
Partition each[-n,n] coordinate into half-open intervals of width
h=epsilon/A_eff, with the last endpoint included. Bin the vector S.
There are at most B_arch bins as in Section1. No logarithmic N
factor enters this bin alphabet. Let C_s be the preimage of a bin,
b_s=mu^tensor n(C_s), and P_x^product=P_(x_1)tensor...tensorP_(x_n).
For b_s>0 define

  E_s=d^n integral_(C_s) P_x^product dmu^tensor n(x),
  tau_s=(1/b_s) integral_(C_s) P_x^product dmu^tensor n(x). (14)

For zero-mass cells E_s=0 and any fixed tau_s can be chosen. The E_s
are positive, sum to I on the n-copy carrier, and do not depend on
theta. The tau_s are states, mixtures of product coherent states,
and do not depend on theta. They satisfy E_s=d^n b_s tau_s, but no
assumption of equal cell masses or algorithmic knowledge is made.
Encoder performs this POVM and retains only s. Decoder prepares tau_s.
This is a LEGAL EB encode/decode channel with finite classical alphabet.

For the supplied input, the probability of s is Q_theta(s)=integral_Cs q^n.
Define the comparison weights P_theta(s)=integral_Cs p^n. Within each
bin, gamma.S varies by at most A_eff h=epsilon. The classical p^n
conditional density relative to the BASE conditional mu^n|C_s is
therefore between exp(-epsilon) and exp(epsilon). Its TV distance
from that base conditional is at most (exp(epsilon)-1)/2<=epsilon
for 0<epsilon<=1. The last bound is uniform even for tiny b_s.

Let sigma_q=integral q^n P_x^product=Psi^tensor n(rho^tensor n),
sigma_p=integral p^n P_x^product, and let
eta_Q=sum_s Q_theta(s)tau_s, eta_P=sum_s P_theta(s)tau_s.
Preparing a density matrix is a contraction from classical TV to
physical half-trace. Thus

  (1/2)||sigma_q-sigma_p||_1<=TV(q^n,p^n),
  (1/2)||eta_Q-eta_P||_1<=TV(Q,P)<=TV(q^n,p^n),
  (1/2)||sigma_p-eta_P||_1<=epsilon.                     (15)

Both latent TV terms are charged. Replacing the actual encoder weights
by the comparison weights without the second line would be an error.

7. CONDITIONAL SAMPLING IS CHARGED, NOT A FREE DISINTEGRATION
The decoder in(14) has an explicit ideal implementation: draw a
Haar-product tuple from the KNOWN base mu^n, compute S, and accept
only if it lands in C_s. On acceptance prepare its product coherent
state. This gives precisely tau_s, independent of theta. For b_s>0
the number of candidate tuples has geometric mean1/b_s. That time
can be enormous; no polynomial worst-case sampler is claimed.

Since each q_theta lies in[exp(-2h0),exp(2h0)],
Q_theta(s)<=exp(2h0 n)b_s. Averaged over the actual encoder label,

  E_(Q_theta)[number of decoder candidate tuples]
    =sum_(b_s>0)Q_theta(s)/b_s
    <=B_arch exp(2h0 n).                                (17)

This uniform expectation bound is finite but EXPONENTIAL in n. Each
tuple needs n Haar draws, n coherent preparations if preparing as
one streams the candidate, and O(nMD) arithmetic for its statistics
(a standard monomial recurrence can reduce that count). Scratch
statistic accumulators require their own finite-precision bits.
Preparing a candidate as it is generated allows classical coordinates
to be discarded; on rejection the candidate output systems are
discarded. On acceptance the n systems are the demanded output.
The decoder's n-system output/workspace is not quantum information
retained from the encoder.

A Haar/orbit sampler, coherent POVM apparatus and known-carrier U_xv_hw
preparator must be SUPPLIED or ACQUIRED. Their precision/time is not
given for free by (17). The compressed label contains no hidden
unknown-parameter-dependent preparator, node choice or normalizer.

8. FINITE DISCRETIZATION / APPARATUS SIZE / PRECISION BOUNDARY
Each tau_s belongs to the closed convex hull of the continuous product
coherent states from the closure of C_s. That image is compact. Real
Caratheodory therefore gives an exact positive ensemble for tau_s
with at most d^(2n)+1 atoms. Using the same ensemble in
E_s=d^n b_s tau_s gives an exact finite positive POVM refinement,
with at most B_arch(d^(2n)+1) rank-one product-coherent effects and
B_arch GROUPED retained outcomes. Completeness follows from the exact
integrals, not a numerical quadrature guess. Closure-boundary nodes
are permitted; the resulting E_s and tau_s remain exactly the same
operators, and the proof uses their integral identities.

This is an EXISTENTIAL finite discretization, not an efficient node
acquisition algorithm. Algebraic/real nodes and masses need not have
short finite descriptions, and exact matrix data have exponential
size. Arbitrary grouped effects can instead be synthesized as a joint
n-copy POVM; such synthesis is also separately charged. No apparatus-
description or scratch-space cost is included in the small ARCHIVE
payload(4). It would be false to advertise total-machine O(log n)
bits including all those resources.

For a physically approximate implementation, if the actual encoder
has outcome-law TV error<=eta_E uniformly on the supplied inputs and
each prepared tau_s has physical half-trace error<=eta_D, then(3)
has the additional eta_E+eta_D. These must be certified/charged,
not inferred from the existence of cubature or disintegration.
Approximate conditional sampling must likewise pay its conditional
TV/preparation error; tiny b_s can amplify base-measure discretization.
No uncharged condition-number bound is asserted.

Known-carrier coherent state variation is Lipschitz with generator
norm O_G(N), so obtaining per-copy state accuracy eta_D/n requires
group-coordinate precision O_G(eta_D/(nN)), before conditioning costs.
Thus a group-coordinate bit budget has at least the usual
log N+log n+log1/eta_D dependence. The sufficient statistic symbols
use normalized moments and have fixed-G,D derivative bounds; their
sum requires per-coordinate precision O(epsilon/n) for ordinary
bin evaluation. Bin-boundary ambiguity and conditional mass accuracy
need a separate discretization/sampling certificate. The resource
audit must not identify these requirements with the retained label
count or claim that ideal continuous Haar sampling is a finite-bit
routine already executed.
