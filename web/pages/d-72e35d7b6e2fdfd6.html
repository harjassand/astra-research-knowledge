# A robust permanence deduction from the affine-envelope mechanism

Status: independently reconstructed consequence, conditional on the release's affine-approximation lemma. The main manuscript was read in full; no external validation or transitive Lean build was performed. The current family149 Lean documentation covers the September25 initial-condition-dependent predecessor, not the October5 common-absorbing-set theorem. This note is an original deduction and proof organization; the load-bearing envelope construction is the authors' result.

## Exact question and interface

Can the fixed-rate permanence mechanism be made uniform over changing rates, and does it actually require integer nonnegative complexes?

Input: a finite set C ⊂ R^d of distinct complex vectors, a finite weakly reversible directed reaction graph R ⊂ C×C, a positive representative c, the subspace S=span{y'-y:(y,y')∈R}, and numbers 0<k_-≤k_+<∞. For every collection of measurable functions k_e:[0,∞)→[k_-,k_+], consider

    x'(t)=Σ_(y→y') k_(y→y')(t) x(t)^y (y'-y),
    x^y=Π_i x_i^(y_i),     P=(c+S)∩R^d_(>0).

Here kinetic exponents are exactly the source complex coordinates. This does not cover generalized kinetics with independently assigned kinetic orders. Negative or nonintegral complex coordinates are allowed because the vector field is smooth on the positive orthant; solutions are understood in the Carathéodory sense. Bounded coefficients give a uniform local Lipschitz constant in x on every compact positive box, so local existence and uniqueness hold.

Claim deduced: there is one compact convex K_P⊂P, depending on C,R,c,k_-,k_+ but on neither the selected rate functions nor the initial point, such that every positive solution is global, enters K_P in finite time, and stays in it. Entry time can depend on the initial point; the construction below even supplies bounds uniform over rate functions once the envelope coefficients are supplied.

Author scope: October5 Theorem1.1 assumes C⊂Z^d_(≥0) and fixed positive rates. Our two extensions are measurable bounded kinetics and real (including signed) complex exponents. No claim of novelty over all literature is made. The bounded-kinetics formulation already occurs in Boros–Hofbauer for one linkage class; the all-linkage-class extension is the consequence being isolated here.

## Load-bearing source input

Uniform activity Corollary2.2, derived from affine approximation Lemma2.1: given any positive, possibly discontinuous tolerance E on [-1,1]^d, a finite concave affine minimum

    F_h(x)=min_λ (r_λ·x+b_λ(h)),      r_λ∈[-1,1]^d,

has, for every sufficiently small h, the property that an active slope at x=(h^p_i) satisfies ||r_λ-p||∞<E(r_λ), uniformly for p∈[-1,1]^d. Slopes are fixed before the common h threshold is selected. The lemma's proof uses dimension induction, dominance of powers of h across fixed exponent gaps, local compactness of limiting active sets, and a finite interval cover. It is the decisive dependency; subsequent flux arguments alone cannot manufacture such envelopes.

Exact source: sources/frontier_dynamics/cycle19/149/build/sections/affine.tex, labels lem:affine and cor:activity. Main input reproduces the September25 construction rather than taking an unproved external black box.

## Complete proof of the extension

1. Form D={e_i}∪{z-y:y,z∈C,z≠y}. It is finite even when complexes are real. Set

    E(r)=min({1/2}∪{|r·v|/(2||v||1):v∈D,r·v≠0}).

Every E(r)>0; zero comparisons are omitted. Apply the activity input. From its finite slope family define

    H=min({1}∪{|r_λ·v|/2:λ,v∈D,r_λ·v≠0})>0.

For any active λ at x=h^p∈B_h=[h,h^-1]^d, every nonzero comparison has sign(p·v)=sign(r_λ·v) and |p·v|≥H. Consequently r·y<r·z implies x^z/x^y=h^(p·(z-y))≤h^H. Integer exponents are unnecessary.

2. Choose h_P∈(0,1) so small that activity holds for all h≤h_P, ||log(c)/log(h)||∞<H, and

    m_R k_+ h^H < k_-,        m_R=|R|.

At c, any active slope with a nonzero coordinate would imply the corresponding |p_c,i|≥H. Hence all active slopes at c are zero. A zero-slope label gives the global maximum M_h=F_h(c). Its plateau K_h={F_h=M_h} is an intersection of affine halfspaces, contains c, and is closed and convex. If x∈K_h∩B_h, the constant label is active; E(0)=1/2 forces ||p||∞<1/2, hence x∈int B_(sqrt h). Convexity then excludes any point of K_h outside B_h: a segment from c to it would cross ∂B_h inside K_h. Therefore K_h is compact and K_h⊂int B_(sqrt h).

3. For an active r, order the distinct levels r·y as a_1<…<a_q. Define Q_j as total upward minus downward flux through the cut a_j. If a downward reaction crosses a cut, a directed return path has an upward crossing w→w'. Every downward source u has r·u>r·w, so its monomial is ≤h^H x^w. Thus, for every admissible instantaneous rate vector,

    Q_j ≥ (k_- - m_R k_+ h^H)x^w >0.

If only upward edges cross, Q_j≥k_-x^w; if none cross it is zero. Let

    M=max_(y∈C)||y||1,       κ_h=k_- - m_R k_+ h^H>0.

For x∈B_h and real y, x^y≥h^(||y||1)≥h^M. Every positive level gap is ≥2H, by the definition of H. The exact decomposition

    r·f(t,x)=Σ_j(a_(j+1)-a_j)Q_j(t,x)

therefore proves r·f(t,x)≥0, and, whenever r∉S⊥,

    r·f(t,x)≥δ_h:=2H κ_h h^M>0.                 (1)

This explicit bound replaces the manuscript's compactness minimum. It is uniform over x in the active set and every rate choice in the box. Source trapping Proposition3.2 uses fixed rates only in the same comparison; no balance identity is needed.

4. At x∈P\K_h, no slope in S⊥ can be active: its affine value is constant on c+S and at least M_h, whereas F_h(x)<M_h. Thus every active nonconstant label has derivative≥δ_h. Along any absolutely continuous solution, F_h(x(t)) is absolutely continuous. At almost every t its derivative is an active affine derivative; at ties, derivatives of equal absolutely continuous branches agree almost everywhere on their equality set. Equivalently,

    d/dt F_h(x(t))=min_(λ active) r_λ·f(t,x(t))     a.e.

Hence F_h is nondecreasing while the solution lies in B_h, and increases at least δ_h while outside K_h in its class. This a.e. chain-rule argument is the required repair for merely measurable rates; a pointwise right derivative of x(t) need not exist.

Since F_h≤M_h and K_h⊂int B_h, the usual first-exit argument gives forward invariance of K_h, uniformly for all rates and all start times. A solution confined there stays in a compact subset of the positive domain, so its Carathéodory continuation is global. A global solution staying in B_h must enter K_h within

    (M_h-F_h(x(t_0)))/δ_h

after t_0. Since F_h is 1-Lipschitz in ||·||1, the numerator is at most d(h^-1-h) for x(t_0),c∈B_h.

5. For any positive initial x0∈P choose small h0≤h_P with ||log(x0)/log(h0)||∞<H. Its active slopes are zero, hence x0∈K_h0; global positivity follows. Define h_(j+1)=min(sqrt(h_j),h_P). Invariance of K_hj and K_hj⊂B_(sqrt h_j) put the trajectory in B_h(j+1) for all later times. Apply the finite-entry bound at the next scale. After at most

    J=max(0,ceil(log2(log(h0)/log(h_P))))

scale transitions, the capped sequence reaches h_P. The one set K_P=K_hP∩(c+S) is compact, convex, and absorbing for all initial states and all admissible rates. This proves the deduction. If R is empty or S={0}, the class is a singleton and the claim is immediate.

## What the result enables, and what it does not yet acquire

The robust polytope is a potential certificate for uncertainty in kinetic rates, switched reaction controllers, and model reductions that preserve the reaction form. A controller can choose rates arbitrarily within the stated bounds without changing the absorbing concentration bounds. For real exponents, this also covers directly coupled power-law networks.

The family construction is not a polynomial-time certificate compiler. Its offsets, finite subcover, minimum H, and common scale h_P have no complexity estimates. Time bound (1) is explicit only after those data are obtained. This is an acquisition obstacle, not a counterexample to the mathematical implication.

A supplied rational certificate can be checked separately: on each active affine region intersected with a rational box and rational stoichiometric class, verify a_λ·f_κ(x)≥δ for all rate-box vertices κ. Dependence on κ is linear, so vertex checks suffice. In integer/rational-complex cases these are semialgebraic universal inequalities and admit quantifier-elimination verification. This gives a finite verifier and a search specification; no implemented general quantifier-elimination compiler or complexity bound is claimed.

## Adversarial boundaries

The lower rate bound is essential: on the weakly reversible graph 0⇄A, x'=e^-t-x has x(t)=e^-t(x0+t)→0. The upper rate bound is essential: x'=t+1-x has x(t)=t+x0e^-t→∞. Arbitrary kinetic orders independent of reaction complexes invalidate the monomial-order comparison. Deterministic permanence supplies no theorem about stochastic reaction counts, numerical integrators, convergence to equilibrium, or chaos/entropy. No such consequences are adopted.

## Independently certified small construction

work/frontier_dynamics/cycle19/robust_scalar_certificate.py checks exact rational inequalities for the two-linkage-class network 0⇄A and 2A⇄3A with all four rates varying measurably in [1,2]. The shared interval [1/4,4] is invariant and absorbing. For 0<x≤1/4,

    f(t,x)≥1-2x-2x³≥15/32;

for x≥4,

    f(t,x)≤2-x+2x²-x³≤-x³/2.

The interval is entered from below within (1/4-x0)32/15<8/15, and from above within 1/16-x0^-2<1/16. This example is proved directly, independently of the release. Rate-box vertex enumeration verifies facet signs exactly, rather than a trajectory sample.

## Prior art and novelty status

Boros–Hofbauer, arXiv:1903.03071v2, 13March2019, Section3 equations(1), Definition1 and Theorem2, already use rates bounded above and away from zero and prove a common invariant absorbing set for one linkage class. Their Section7 explicitly exhibits the failure of the standard entropy function for multiple linkage classes. The October5 source explicitly contrasts its fixed-rate all-linkage-class theorem with these older bounded-kinetics results. Craciun's arXiv:1501.02860v3, 23September2026, states bounded-solution persistence in the abstract and Section8 final remark; this is weaker than a classwise absorbing-set conclusion on unbounded classes.

The general robust theorem above is not claimed as an independent new breakthrough. It is a stronger scoped consequence of the authors' affine lemma and proof mechanism, with an explicit flux bound and measurable-rate chain-rule repair. A complete novelty audit and an independent proof of the affine lemma remain necessary before publication-grade adoption.
