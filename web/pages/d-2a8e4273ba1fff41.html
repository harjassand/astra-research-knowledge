## Strongest reusable mechanism

For a compact group acting orthogonally on a real inner-product space, keep the relation between a supplied action g and its stochastic transition P_g by using the transfer

(A_P z)_i = integral rho(g)^(-1) sum_j P_g(i,j) z_j dmu(g).

Its dual propagates unnormalized conditional means. Positivity gives a one-step orbit-quantization contraction; independent actions compose the actual transfers. Uniform small-ball bounds must hold for **all normalized conditional means**, not just the initial orbit.

For sphere rotations the exact coefficient is eta_(m,d), and maximum retained directional information is eta_(m,d)^T. For unitary conjugation on traceless Hermitian matrices, a uniform eigenvalue-gap-to-Grassmann argument gives 1-kappa_(m,D) of order m^(-1/(D-1)). It does not prove exact attainment of kappa^T. The separate polytope construction gives the matched state complexity T^(D-1).

## Exact theorem contract

Fix D>=2, delta in (0,1/(D^2-1)], epsilon in (0,delta/[2(D^2-1)]). Preparations are all density matrices, queries are the displayed weak binary effects, and gates are arbitrary supplied unitaries. Simulation is sequential, finite-state, Markov, and uniformly accurate for all words through T. Transitions may depend arbitrarily and measurably on time and current gate. They cannot use an uncharged persistent seed, correlated external noise, earlier inputs or a stored continuous state.

Minimum classical states: Theta_(D,delta,epsilon)(T^(D-1)). Static size: exactly D^2. Quantum Hilbert-space dimension: D. Classical bits: (D-1)log_2 T + O_(D,delta,epsilon)(1).

## Single next mathematical obligation

Prove or refute the sharp linear state law for the fixed qubit rotation alphabet

{I,R_x,R_x^(-1),R_z,R_z^(-1)},

where the two nonidentity rotations have cosine 3/5 and sine 4/5 about the named axes, at delta=1/3 and epsilon=1/72.

The supplied finite-alphabet block profile is a proved interface, not a solution. Do not silently use the continuous-Haar one-step coefficient for this finite alphabet: it becomes one once the state count exceeds the alphabet size. The target is unresolved in this investigation, without any claim that it is globally new or open in the literature.

The alphabet is dense in SO(3): if its rotation angle were a rational multiple of pi, then 2cos(theta)=6/5 would be a rational algebraic integer, hence an integer, a contradiction. Its powers are therefore dense in each of two perpendicular axis circles; those circles generate SO(3). This establishes density, not a quantitative simulation lower bound.
