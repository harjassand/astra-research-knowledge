Claim: Let q be a prime power, A_1,…,A_m∈F_q^{p×n}, c_i∈F_q^n, and let g:F_q^p→F_q^m be efficiently evaluable. Suppose a certified integer R satisfies rank(Σ_i λ_i A_i)≥R for every nonzero λ∈F_q^m. For F(x) with rows xᵀA_i+c_iᵀ, the source derives an exact rejection sampler whose output conditioned on success is uniform over every pair in S_g={(x,y):F(x)y=g(x)}, including singular fibers. For R≥m+1, its expected number of trials is at most (1+α)/(1−α/(q−1))<3, where α=(q^m−1)q^(−R). In the pure bilinear fixed-target case c_i=0 and g(x)=b, R≥m suffices for fewer than four expected trials. For x=x_0+Pu of codimension a and a graph restriction Dy=h(x) with k independent rows, the source proves a transformed rank bound R−a−k; the <3 guarantee persists when R−a−k≥m+1.
Interface: Finite field F_q; matrices A_i and offsets c_i are supplied; g is evaluated as a supplied circuit/algorithm. The exact output domain is all satisfying joint pairs, sampled uniformly, not a chosen affine chart or only full-rank fibers. A valid collective-rank lower bound R must be certified. The stated closure covers affine restriction of x and a full-row-rank affine graph constraint on y with efficiently evaluable h; arbitrary added polynomial constraints are outside the interface.
Proof spine: For each dual λ and affine-kernel point x, the proposal/kernel factors give each admissible pair (x,y) the same one-trial probability q^(−p)/(1+α); counting the dual annihilator of F(x) yields the identical mass q^(m−p−n)/(1+α) for every solution pair. A projective union bound controls row-deficient x and supplies the expected-trial bounds. Under the stated conditioning, substitution through a right inverse and a basis for ker D transforms A_i to PᵀA_iQ and loses at most a+k rank.
Costs: Per trial: polynomially many finite-field operations in (m,p,n), one evaluation of g, exact linear-system solves, and O((m+p+n)log q) expected unbiased bits; the certified regimes give fewer than three or four expected trials. For square n×n matrices, the source gives a structured Moore-interpolation certificate R=n−s using O(n³+mn²) extension-field operations after a verified F_{q^n} representation. Field construction/search and verification are charged; the certificate is coordinate-dependent, and finding a favorable hidden basis is not included.
Failure: A high collective-rank certificate is a strong promise; individual A_i ranks do not suffice, and general minimum-combination-rank acquisition is not solved. The Moore certificate can weaken under an unknown basis scramble. Uncharged nonlinear identification can make a conditioned system empty (the alternating-matrix y=x example); rank loss and added constraints must be charged. Outside the certified regimes rejection may fail to terminate on an empty solution set, so this is not a general decision procedure or generic constraint solver.
