## 1. An actual Tanner star has at most seven qubits

The primary definition at1560–1571 is

    X_x=closure{parent squares containing x}.

Every square has boundary (x,q,z,q'). Both qubits in it are adjacent
to x. Taking closures adds check vertices and Tanner edges but no
new qubits. Hence, at every level i,

    X_(i),x intersect V_Q,(i) subset N_T(i)(x),
    |X_(i),x intersect V_Q,(i)|<=z_v<=7.             (1)

This X_x is the closed star in the **current Tanner complex**. It
is not the subdivided/doubled replacement Y_x, whose size is
O(ell²). The dual Z-star has the same bound.

## 2. Coarse error comparison, with gauges paired locally

For each ordinary step i>=3, choose all decoder correction rules
deterministically, with f_{i,v}=0 for zero local input. The source's
string-pairing rules allow this normalization. Mere local
computability would not imply it: a rule could add a fixed closed
string even on zero input.

Write e for any error realizing sigma and tilde e for another
realizing tilde sigma. Suppose their difference h=e+tilde e is
supported on the qubits in one current X-check star. We construct
coarse representatives such that h'=e'+tilde e' is supported on one
coarse X-check star. No assumption on the size of the background
e is made.

### R_Z step

The actual direct-sum degrees are

    C_X^0 only; C_Q^0 -> C_Q^1; C_Z^0 -> C_Z^1 -> C_Z^2.

The coarse error is the literal parity

    e'_q=sum_{a in C_q^1} e_a.                       (2)

The decoder's f is supported only in C_Z^1, so (2) is unchanged by
f. This is primary2525–2652. The full cochain projection in degree
one is also (2): any later h/g transfer into the Z block dies under
the degree-one Z projection. The coarse inclusion F has PF=id.
The cochain identity supplies

    e+f=F(e')+delta^0 s.                             (3)

For completeness, the difference on the right is closed by the
syndrome relation and has zero coarse projection; the actual
homotopy then makes it a coboundary. Thus (3) preserves the logical
class, rather than merely preserving the syndrome.

There are exactly three possible types of a fine X-check in the
actual replacement:

* An old-x cap, in C_X^0: its qubit neighbors in C_Q^1 belong only
  to Q blocks q adjacent to that old x, since g_XQ is zero for
  nonadjacent parents.
* A dual edge check of Y_q, in C_Q^0: its neighbors in C_Q^1 are
  vertices of that same Y_q. Other qubit neighbors lie in C_Z^1
  via g_QZ and are invisible to (2). The coarse difference is
  therefore supported on the single old q. Every actual qubit has
  an X neighbor, so that q lies in an old X-star.
* A dual face check of Y_z, in C_Z^0: all its neighbors lie in
  C_Z^1. Its coarse difference is zero.

These exhaust the actual degrees; there is no g_XZ term in this
template. In every case h' is zero, a single old q, or supported in
N(old x). No assumption about a subdivided ideal star is used.

### R_X step

All fine X-checks are vertices in C_X^0. A check in Y_x has qubit
neighbors only in C_x^1 and in C_q^1 for old q adjacent to x. This
is the literal restriction map g_xq at1630–1660.

The first decoder cleaning f_X has one component f_y for each
old y. Because the two input syndromes have identical C_y^2 data
for y!=x, the deterministic corrections agree outside x. Therefore
e_1+tilde e_1 is supported in C_x^1 plus the Q blocks q~x.

The auxiliary gauge step in primary3135–3186 chooses s_y with
delta_y^0 s_y=e_{1,y}. It is not a decoder step. Choose a fixed
right inverse of delta_y^0 on its image, so the two gauges agree
whenever the local errors agree. Their difference is zero for
y!=x. Applying delta^0 to the remaining s_x adds qubits only in
C_x^1 and the blocks q~x. Thus the X-cleaned error difference,
which has no C_X^1 component, is supported only in those Q blocks.

After first cleaning, C_q^2 syndrome data differ only for q~x.
The second decoder cleaning f_q therefore agrees for every other
q. The final error difference remains supported in Q blocks q~x.
The source's final C_Q^1 error is a local cocycle on every Y_q,
hence a constant 0-cochain on that connected graph. Its inverse
under F is supported on the corresponding old qubit q. Consequently
the coarse difference is supported in N(old x), proving the
claimed invariant. The exact identity (3) here follows directly
from the source's cleaning formula at3285–3347.

Iterating these two cases preserves a single-star comparison down
to level2. A single-qubit initial difference can be placed in one
X-star because every qubit has an X neighbor. The dual argument
interchanges X and Z and transposes the maps; both sector parities
and both actual replacement steps are retained.

## 3. One fixed minimum-weight lookup suffices

For every valid level-2 syndrome s, choose a global minimum-weight
Pauli-sector correction f2(s) with delta_2^1 f2(s)=s, using fixed
ties. In particular f2(0)=0. No locality of this lookup is claimed.

Suppose sigma2=0. Section2 constructs h2=e2+tilde e2 supported in
one X-star, with delta h2=tilde sigma2. By (1),

    |h2|<=7,  |f2(tilde sigma2)|<=|h2|<=7.

Therefore h2+f2(tilde sigma2) is closed and has weight<=14. If
d(C2)>14 it is a stabilizer. The actual cycle13 admitted-family
distance bound at level2 is vastly larger than14; use the proved
bound, not the primary informal O(ell) notation.

Telescoping (3) and lifting by the actual cochain maps makes the
level-2 trivial class equal to the full decoded difference

    e_single + dec(sigma) + dec(sigma+delta e_single).

The same fixed f2 is used for every comparison. Thus sigma2=0
implies single-Pauli stability. Three factual source issues are
repaired:

* "corrects all errors whose syndrome fits inside radius2" is
  impossible literally, because a logical has zero syndrome. The
  required promise is a small **support representative** h2.
* An existential local correction does not imply that an arbitrary
  chosen lookup applies it. The minimum-weight lookup above fixes
  this quantifier.
* f2 need not remain in the star. Distance bounds the closed
  difference globally by weight.

## 4. Ordinary witness components and the bottom exception

    C_low=sum_{j<=2}|V(G_j)|,  K_dom=2(1+Delta_level²).
