## Falsification and failed-route ledger

* An initial possible “new” all-time depth-two insight was found already in N120. It was discarded as a novelty claim.
* Surviving entanglement in each hidden noisy pair does NOT prove that their unrecorded mixture is entangled. The fluctuation/tail argument is the missing step.
* The old unfiltered variance witness loses the Maxwell radius variance. Its 0.626657 threshold was only sufficient. It cannot refute entanglement in (1/sqrt3,0.626657].
* At the exact boundary tr(TT^T)=1, an explicit full-separable decomposition exists. The proof uses a strict trace deficit and makes no contrary claim there. Finite t filter diagnostics remain on the separable side at that boundary.
* Taking a filter parameter to infinity at fixed N is not the asymptotic argument. The order is fixed t, then large N.
* A filtered variance violation alone does not imply a nonvanishing unfiltered trace-distance gap, because filter success may vanish. The gap is separately proved with bounded local measurements.
* Weak convergence of discrete counts is not convergence in TV to a continuous Gaussian density. TV in the theorem compares two finite-N measurement laws on the same sample space.
* Separable alternatives may have arbitrary classical correlations and heterogeneous product factors. The proof keeps a COMMON mixture of Gaussian means and variances; a single-Gaussian approximation or independent axis-specific mixtures would be invalid.
* Characteristic limits are not simultaneous quantum measurements. Only three separately chosen one-axis measurements are used operationally.
* The stationary state is selected by the input, not the generator's unique invariant state for arbitrary input.
* Nonunital extension is not automatic. Extensive mean, limiting commutators, and filtering-induced spin-sector tilts change. No theorem for that case is claimed.
* A local-noise-during-preparation perturbation breaks the conserved-sector interface. Post-preparation robustness does not solve it.
* Numerical work used dense small-N matrices and sector formulas, not physical N=10^8 states or a proof assistant.
