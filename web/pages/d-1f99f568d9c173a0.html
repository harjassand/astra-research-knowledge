# Scientific report — cycle 5 and early 6

**Strongest supported advance:** a near-optimal acquisition theorem for rare transitions from passive state observations. If the shortest ordinary path replacing a rare edge has L jumps, as b tends to zero the rare rate can be learned with expected readings O(b^[-L/(L-1)] loglog(1/b)) and duration O(b^-1 loglog(1/b)), at fixed accuracy/confidence. The procedure acquires both ordinary rate values and the unknown rare scale. Graph support, a positive ordinary-rate gap, full state labels and calibrated timing remain supplied. The polynomial exponents match independently proved adaptive lower bounds; necessity of the logarithmic overhead is unresolved. [Complete composed proof](../notes/root_cycle5/ACQUIRED_RATES_AND_SCALE.md), [independent full-text audit](../notes/r08_information/cycle5/combined_acquisition_gate.md).

**Why this can improve future research:** the proof identifies which nuisance information must be acquired accurately, and separates sensor readings from physical experiment duration. A separate [sensor-resolution theorem](../notes/root_cycle5/SENSOR_RESOLUTION_THEOREM.md) shows a fixed positive minimum sampling interval changes duration to quadratic order in1/b. This gives a concrete experimental-design test rather than an unqualified rare-event penalty. [Code](../work/root_cycle5/sensor_resolution_checks.py) passes110 exact graph/edge fixtures and9133 support identities; numerical checks support the predicted crossover. They do not replace the universal proof or constitute an executed composite experiment.

**Prior art and significance:** [Barendregt–Webb–Kilpatrick2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10371v2) already provides adaptive endpoint inference, including directed rings. Standard likelihood/Fisher design predicts the same exponent. The candidate contribution is the rigorous topology-dependent adaptive cost law and charged acquisition extension, not a new adaptive learner. [Primary-source audit and remaining access gaps](../notes/r04_statphys/cycle5/PRIOR_ART_AUDIT.md). Historical novelty is unverified; no foundational or revolutionary breakthrough is claimed.

The full composite algorithm has not yet been implemented; conservative constants may be impractical. These limits constrain any claim of general scientific discovery.
