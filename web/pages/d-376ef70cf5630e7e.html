CYCLE10 SAME-BANK DISCRETE EXIT-LABEL TRANSPORT
Status: exact conditional marked-exit lemma; actual-exploration hypotheses remain open.
Scope: one candidate lemma, one concrete verification route, one slit counterexample.
No global Sobolev extension is used. No GFF-to-SLE implication is claimed.

DECISION

Physical NN-walk exit labels can identify which limiting prime-end bank was
hit, provided two separate limits are proved in a common prime-end coordinate:
(1) the discrete exit measures converge to the limiting harmonic measure, and
(2) the physical sign attached to the last boundary half-edge agrees with the
limiting bank label with probability tending to one. Caratheodory kernel
convergence alone is only an interior conformal-map limit; it neither tracks
the two banks over a slit impression nor transports a sign label.

The square lattice is isoradial. Chelkak--Smirnov define discrete harmonic
measure as the exit distribution of the corresponding nearest-neighbor walk
and use convergence of discrete harmonic measures in the bulk for simply
connected isoradial domains. This is the relevant fixed-domain exit-law
precedent. The random square exploration still must supply marked-domain
convergence and access-label stability; those are not consequences of the
continuum bounded-harmonic-mean premise.

1. SAME-BANK MARKED EXIT THEOREM

For each n let E_n be a random finite simply connected square-lattice domain
embedded in the plane, with a bulk vertex x_n. Let S^n be simple random walk,
independent of E_n conditionally on E_n, stopped at its first exit. Record the
last directed NN half-edge e_n through which it exits, rather than only the
Euclidean exit point. This access record determines the discrete prime end
P_n even when several accesses share a physical boundary point. Give e_n a
physical label L_n in {+, -, 0}; the two signs are assigned by the oriented
exploration/crosscut and 0 denotes the remaining boundary.

Normalize a conformal map f_n:D->E_n by f_n(0)=x_n and a fixed orientation.
Map the access-selected prime end P_n to Theta_n in the prime-end circle
partial D. Let nu_n be the conditional law of Theta_n given E_n. On the
limiting side let E be a simply connected domain, f:D->E the corresponding
normalization, and ell:partial D->{+,-,0} the limiting label of each prime
end. Put I_+={theta:ell(theta)=+} and I_- analogously. The limiting harmonic
measure in this coordinate is lambda(dtheta)=dtheta/(2 pi).

Assume along a coupling of the random environments that, in probability (or
almost surely after a Skorokhod coupling):

  (D1) the pointed domains converge in the Caratheodory kernel sense, so
       f_n -> f locally uniformly in D;
  (D2) the conditional exit-angle measures nu_n converge weakly to lambda;
  (D3) the access labels are stable:
       P(L_n != ell(Theta_n) | E_n) -> 0;
  (D4) lambda(partial I_+) = lambda(partial I_-) = 0.

Then for every continuous psi on the circle and sigma in {+,-},

  E[ psi(Theta_n) 1_{L_n=sigma} | E_n ]
      -> integral_{I_sigma} psi(theta) lambda(dtheta).

The convergence is in probability over the environments; if (D1)-(D4) hold
almost surely in the coupling, the conditional conclusion is almost sure.
The same conclusion holds for bounded measurable psi whose discontinuity set
is lambda-null.

Proof. Replacing 1_{L_n=sigma} by 1_{ell(Theta_n)=sigma} changes the
conditional integral by at most ||psi||_infinity times the mismatch
probability in (D3). The replaced integral is integral psi 1_{I_sigma} dnu_n.
By weak convergence and (D4), psi 1_{I_sigma} is a lambda-continuity test,
so this converges to integral_{I_sigma} psi d lambda. This proves the claim.

This is a sufficient, directly falsifiable marked-exit criterion. It is
minimal for this two-step proof in the sense that (D2) controls the exit
location while (D3) controls the mark; neither can be dropped and recovered
from the other. It is not a theorem that (D1) implies (D2) for arbitrary rough
moving domains. A stopped-walk invariance principle or a discrete harmonic
measure convergence theorem with the required boundary hypotheses must
establish (D2).

OPTIONAL-STOPPING COROLLARY AND SCOPE LIMIT

If H_n is the discrete harmonic extension in E_n of bounded boundary data
g(L_n), optional stopping gives H_n(x_n)=E[g(L_n)|E_n]. The lemma identifies
the limit of these values as integral g(ell(theta)) d lambda. If this holds
for every interior pole, the locally uniform limit is the Brownian harmonic
extension of g(ell), and its prime-end trace is g(ell) harmonic-measure-a.e.

This corollary applies to discrete harmonic extensions. It does not assert
that the hard-Lipschitz conditional mean is itself a discrete harmonic
extension of the sign labels. The current continuum theorem says its limit
is harmonic; a separate representation/approximation bridge would be needed
to identify that mean's trace numerically from the exit-label payoff. The
lemma's present conclusion is the exact transfer of the actual physical
positive/negative labels to the correct prime-end banks.

2. ONE CONCRETE ROUTE FOR THE ACTUAL EXPLORATION

For a mesh crosscut Gamma_n, form the two component domains with their marked
boundary arcs. Define each sign on the boundary half-edge by the sign of its
incident interior NN vertex and the orientation/order of Gamma_n. Do not
label by Euclidean projection onto Gamma_n: that projection collapses the
two bank accesses.

On a coupled subsequence, verify the following chain for this exploration:

  (a) The oriented crosscuts Gamma_n converge uniformly under a fixed
      parametrization to an access-resolved simple Gamma, with endpoints
      converging. The two side domains and their marked boundary arcs then
      converge as marked prime-end domains, not just as unmarked physical
      sets.
  (b) For each fixed compact bank subarc away from the two endpoints and any
      prescribed ambiguity set, the last-NN-edge sign is eventually the
      limiting bank sign. This uses the oriented path order and local
      separation of the two sides.
  (c) The exit probability of the discarded endpoint/ambiguity collars
      tends to zero uniformly in n as the collar shrinks. A concrete way to
      prove this is a square-lattice discrete Beurling/barrier bound on the
      access-resolved crosscut, paired with nonatomic Brownian harmonic
      measure at the finitely many marked endpoints. If the geometry only
      gives bounded-away-from-endpoint local separation, use (b) there and
      charge all remaining hits to (c).
  (d) Apply square-lattice discrete harmonic-measure convergence to each
      marked bank arc. This supplies (D2); (b)-(c) supply (D3).

For fixed Jordan or suitably regular marked approximants this route is
classical in form: discrete harmonic measure is the SRW exit law, and its
convergence to continuous harmonic measure is available on isoradial
approximations. For the random rough square crosscut, the new work is to make
(a)-(c) uniform in the exploration environment. In particular, neither
uniform path convergence alone nor a Caratheodory kernel limit proves the
collar estimate (c) if a sequence can hide narrow fjords or unresolved
self-approaches with nonvanishing exit mass. This route uses walk exits and
marked boundary geometry, not an ambient Sobolev extension.

3. EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO UNMARKED CONVERGENCE

Let E be the unit disk with the radial slit [0,1) removed, and take E_n=E
for every n. Thus the pointed domains, normalized conformal maps, physical
boundary sets, unmarked random-walk exit law, and limiting Brownian harmonic
measure are all constant in n. The upper and lower slit banks are distinct
prime-end arcs with the same Euclidean impression. On even n label an exit
on the upper slit bank + and the lower bank -; on odd n reverse the two
labels. Both bank arcs have positive harmonic measure. A continuous test
function supported in the conformal-angle interval of an upper-bank compact
subarc gives a positive plus-labeled exit integral on even n and zero on odd
n. Therefore the marked exit laws do not converge, although every unmarked
domain and exit-law convergence statement holds exactly.

This is an exact falsifier of the weaker inference “unmarked Caratheodory,
Hausdorff, or random-walk exit convergence implies same-bank label
convergence.” The failed hypothesis is precisely (D3), not Brownian
harmonicity or exit-law convergence. It also shows why the last directed NN
half-edge/access record must be retained rather than collapsing an exit to
its Euclidean point on the slit.

STATUS, COSTS, AND DECISIVE NEXT CHECK

Proved here: the conditional same-bank exit-law implication (D1)-(D4) and
the discrete-harmonic optional-stopping corollary. Counterexample: fixed
slit with alternating bank labels. Primary-source comparison: fixed-domain
discrete harmonic measure convergence is established in isoradial settings;
Caratheodory kernel convergence alone is known to leave marked-boundary
observables unresolved.

Not proved: (D2)-(D3) for the actual random square exploration; discrete
Beurling/collar control in its rough crosscut; identification of the
hard-Lipschitz conditional mean with a discrete harmonic boundary extension;
common bank values; field/curve joint convergence; GFF or SLE.

The decisive next model-specific check is one joint theorem for the actual
oriented Gamma_n: conditional exit-angle convergence for NN walk and
vanishing probability of any exit whose last-edge sign disagrees with the
limiting prime-end bank. If that theorem fails, the explicit missed-event
type must carry nonvanishing harmonic measure; if it passes, the physical
labels transport without needing a global ambient mean extension.
