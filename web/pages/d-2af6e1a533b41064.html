# Perceptron continuity does not supply a uniform finite-precision optimizer

Status: the local approximation and obstruction below are independently
reconstructed. Family 222's full all-density pressure theorem is not adopted

## 1. Author interface and an independently checked reusable bound

The Ising manuscript, OpenAI, dated 24 September 2026, considers Gaussian
patterns, M_N=floor(alpha*N), uniform probability prior on {-1,1}^N, and a
bounded Borel real log-potential f. Its model:thm:main claims all-density
pressure convergence to inf_q{alpha*V_f(q)+S_I(q)}, where S_I is itself a
supremum over nonnegative step field paths. A variational expression is not
an acquired finite optimizer: both overlap approximation and the entropy
supremum need charged interfaces.

The local bound in extensions:ext:path-approx is valid independently of the
global pressure proof. If |f|,|g|<=K, then

    |E p_N(f)-E p_N(g)| <= alpha*e^(2K)*||f-g||_(L1 Gaussian),
    sup_q |V_f(q)-V_g(q)| <= e^(2K)*||f-g||_(L1 Gaussian).

For the first bound, delete one independent pattern before interpolating its
potential. Its fresh projection at every spin has standard Gaussian law;
restoring a factor bounded between e^(-K) and e^K costs at most e^(2K).
Sum over M_N rows and divide by N. For the second, terminal interpolation
propagates f-g by normalized tilted Gaussian kernels. The full path density
relative to the independent increments telescopes to terminal f_t minus a
convex weighted average of intermediate recursion values. Each lies in
[-K,K], so this density is <=e^(2K). The untilted terminal variance is one.
These arguments do not depend on partition size or covariance degeneracy.

Consequently a certified Gaussian L1 surrogate error transfers uniformly to
all finite pressures and all pattern trials. Since the entropy is unchanged,
it transfers to the variational infimum whenever that expression is used.
This is a reusable activation-approximation certificate, not a pressure oracle.

## 2. No modulus depending only on the amplitude bound

Let f_n(x)=cos(n*x), q_n(u)=1-1/n and p_n(u)=1-1/n^3, for n>=2. These are
bounded smooth even activations with K=1, and constant trial paths with top
values strictly below one. Their L1 path distance tends to zero.

For a constant path r, the exact pattern term is

    V_f(r)=E_G log E_Z exp(f(sqrt(r)*G+sqrt(1-r)*Z)).

At r=1, V_f_n(1)=E cos(nG)=exp(-n^2/2). Write
exp(cos u)=sum_{ell in Z} a_ell exp(i*ell*u). Expanding
exp((exp(iu)+exp(-iu))/2) gives nonnegative coefficients,
a_0=sum_{j>=0}1/(4^j*(j!)^2)>=5/4 and sum_ell a_ell=e.
Gaussian averaging over the residual variance 1/n damps the ell-th
coefficient by exp(-ell^2*n/2). Therefore, uniformly in the outer field,

    |E_Z exp(f_n(sqrt(q_n)*G+sqrt(1-q_n)*Z))-a_0|
       <= e*exp(-n/2).

Both quantities inside logarithms are >=e^(-1), so
|V_f_n(q_n)-log a_0|<=e^2*exp(-n/2). The smooth first-variation bound gives
|V_f_n(p_n)-V_f_n(1)|<=n^2*(1-p_n)/2=1/(2*n). This bound extends from
compact smooth terminals to bounded smooth terminals with bounded derivatives
by the same dominated Gaussian differentiation; f_n meets those conditions.
Hence

    V_f_n(q_n)-V_f_n(p_n)
      >= log(5/4)-e^2*exp(-n/2)-exp(-n^2/2)-1/(2*n).

The right side tends to the positive constant log(5/4), despite the path
distance tending to zero. No uniform L1 path modulus follows from K alone,
even on these smooth even activations and top<1 finite trials. A high-frequency
input can invalidate a path grid chosen solely from amplitude and accuracy.
This is not a counterexample to continuity for each fixed f, nor a general
algorithmic hardness lower bound. The varying activation is essential.

## 3. Acquired modulus for explicitly encoded interval activations

An effective surrogate certificate repairs the missing interface. Let f be a
bounded step activation with J explicitly listed jump thresholds and |f|<=K.
For s>0, let g_s(x)=E f(x+sZ). Crossing any listed threshold is necessary for
f(G)!=f(G+sZ). Conditional on Z, the Gaussian probability of crossing a
threshold is at most s*|Z|/sqrt(2*pi). Summing thresholds and using
E|Z|=sqrt(2/pi) gives

    ||f-g_s||_(L1 Gaussian) <= 2*K*J*s/pi <= K*J*s.

The derivative of the Gaussian convolution satisfies ||g_s'||_infinity<=K/s.
Combining the uniform terminal approximation with the smooth path variation
yields the acquired explicit modulus

    |V_f(p)-V_f(q)|
      <= 2*e^(2K)*K*J*s + K^2*||p-q||_1/(2*s^2).

For K,J>0 and tolerance epsilon, choose s<=epsilon/(4*e^(2K)*K*J) and
||p-q||_1<=epsilon*s^2/K^2. This makes the bound <=epsilon. Cases K=0 or
J=0 are constant and immediate. Listed thresholds and rational Gaussian
convolution/CDF evaluations have finite computable representations; all
evaluation precision and the potentially large e^(2K) factor remain charged.
This acquires the pattern-term grid precision. It does not acquire the entropy
supremum, prove a polynomial-bit global optimizer, or replace the contact/cavity
proof of equality to the physical pressure.
