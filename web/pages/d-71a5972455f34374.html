# Exact negative-form discriminator using the real harmonic collar

7 October 2026. **Our variational reconstruction; not an asserted infinite-index result.** Scalar H=−Δ+V on R^n, our real smooth u≠0 with Hu=0, bounded V. The repaired construction supplies an open neighborhood O of the entire zero set where V=0 and u is harmonic. This criterion uses the actual repaired u,V; it never replaces V by Δf/f.

## Why obvious oscillatory trial functions cannot work

For any smooth compactly supported real h,

    q_H(hu)=∫u²|∇h|²≥0.                            (1)

Test Hu=0 with h²u, expand the gradient and subtract from the expanded q_H(hu). This uses no positivity of u. In particular normalized-wave trials h=e^Φχ, which produce χ(u/E), still have nonnegative form. Oscillatory trials inheriting u's nodal trace cannot exhibit negative spectrum. This is an exact obstruction, not a numerical failure of a chosen cutoff.

Also |∇|u||=|∇u| almost everywhere, so for χ∈C_c∞,

    q_H(χ|u|)=q_H(χu)=∫u²|∇χ|²=:B_χ.              (2)

Any negative test built from |u| must gain enough energy by filling its zero trace to beat the cutoff boundary cost B_χ.

## Legal harmonic filling and exact comparison

Choose a bounded Lipschitz domain D compactly contained in O, and χ∈C_c∞ equal to1 on a neighborhood of closure(D). Let w=χ|u|. Let p∈H¹(D) be the unique harmonic extension with p−w∈H¹_0(D), and define v=w outside D and v=p inside D. This is a compactly supported H¹ function with support contained in support(χ)∪closure(D).

Since V=0 on D, harmonic orthogonality gives

    ∫_D|∇w|²=∫_D|∇p|²+∫_D|∇(w−p)|².

Thus the ACTUAL Schrödinger form obeys exactly

    q_H(v)=B_χ−G_D,
    G_D=∫_D|∇(|u|−p)|².                            (3)

There is no unspecified quotient error, discarded collar contribution, or mean-potential approximation in (3). Smooth compact approximations of v preserve strict negativity when it exists, because V is bounded and the support may be kept in a fixed compact neighborhood.

If D crosses a regular nodal point, then |u| is not harmonic there. In distributional terms Δ|u|=2|∇u| times hypersurface measure at regular points, with the corresponding nonnegative distribution at singular points. Therefore G_D>0. But strict positivity of G_D alone is not enough: it must exceed B_χ.

Write z=p−|u|∈H¹_0(D). Then −Δz=Δ|u|≥0, z≥0, and

    G_D=∫_D|∇z|²=〈Δ|u|,z〉.

This is an exact Dirichlet Green-energy interface for the nodal measure. It identifies the first new quantitative estimate needed for this route: choose escaping, mutually disjoint compact supports with G_(D_j)>B_(χ_j). Such a sequence gives arbitrarily large negative-definite form spans by disjointness and hence infinitely many negative eigenvalues. The current sparse-repair and jet bounds do not prove this inequality.

## What is and is not controlled

The existing estimate |V|≤Cκ does not give the sign of ∫Vχ² or of a low-frequency local spectral cell. Likewise |W|≤C_Kκa^−2E controls correction amplitude, not the sign of the Green-energy surplus in (3). The collar where V=0 is useful because (3) is exact, but its width and the exponential radial variation of u make the competing cutoff cost substantial. A heuristic microscopic gain of order τa²u² per volume cannot be compared to that cost without integrating the same legal cutoff and the true u.

The raw local free model u0=e^(−as)cos(at), V0=0, already has high tangential frequency and harmonic zero collars, while every compact test has q=∫|∇v|²≥0. Therefore oscillation, a strict positive nodal-filling gain, and shrinking wavelength alone cannot imply G_D>B_χ. A successful estimate must detect the construction's radial frequency variation or coupling between patches beyond this free model.
