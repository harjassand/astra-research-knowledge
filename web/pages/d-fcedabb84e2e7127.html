# Independent gate: recovery-law information changes proof-search gradients

7 October2026. Root read arXiv2609.25575v1 primary metadata and source main.tex
325–365 and941–1012, then reconstructed the exact models in
notes/rounding_prior/cycle17/SEARCH_RECOVERY_GRADIENT_BOUNDARY.md. PASS for
its two heterogeneous-cost counterexamples and common-iid renewal correction.
The paper's common deterministic same-state-return theorem is not refuted.
Its broader stochastic/restart interpretation cannot be used without extra
assumptions. The primary source is Ousherovitch–Tewari, *Direct Optimization of
Generators for Search in Automated Theorem Proving*, submitted22September2026,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25575v1. Exact version and read depth matter.

For L ordered successful steps, p_t in(0,1), q_t=1-p_t and deterministic
step-specific miss costs k_t>=1, completion by integer budget N has

    J=(product_t p_t) sum_(sum k_t m_t<=N-L) product_t q_t^(m_t).

The elasticity in the source's proportional-probability direction is
w_t=1-(p_t/q_t)E[m_t | completion]. Recovery laws and other probabilities are
held fixed; this is not an arbitrary LLM parameter-gradient identity.

At L=2, p1=p2=1/2, N=4, k=(2,1), the finite sum is
S=1+q1+q2+q2²=9/4, and E[m1|success]=2/9. Thus J=9/16 and w1=7/9.
The common-cost-two process has effective unit budget3 and w1=3/4, which is
strictly smaller. At N=5, k=(1,2), S=1+q1+q1²+q1³+q2+q1q2=21/8;
p1*dS/dq1=13/8, giving w1=8/21. The unit-cost budget5 weight is5/13,
strictly larger. Both success probabilities still lie between the unit-cost
and common-maximum-cost probabilities. Such objective ordering cannot simply
be differentiated to obtain ordering of logarithmic derivatives.

A common IID cost law independent of p DOES admit a corrected theorem. Draw
an infinite iid miss-cost sequence K_j independently of all success trials.
Let M be its renewal capacity within B=N-L. Conditional on M=m, completion
is exactly the unit-cost event with at most m misses. Therefore

    J_N=sum_m alpha_m Jhat_(L+m), alpha_m=P(M=m),
    w_t(J_N)=sum_m [alpha_m Jhat_(L+m)/J_N] what_t(L+m).

The mixture weights are posterior weights conditional on success. They are
not the prior renewal weights. If 1<=K<=kmax, M lies between floor(B/kmax)
and B. Unit-cost weights decrease in budget: the geometric generating function
G(z)=pz/(1-qz) obeys p*dG/dp=G-G², giving
what_t(n)=1-P(S_L+G'_t<=n)/P(S_L<=n). Geometric pmfs, their convolution and
its cumulative sum are log-concave, so the shifted-CDF ratios increase with n.
This proves the stated bracket for the common-iid interface.

The mean alone does not determine the renewal law. For L=1,N=3,p=1/2,
iid miss cost equally likely1 or3 gives J=p(1+q/2+q²/4)=21/32 and w=5/7.
A deterministic cost2 has the same mean but J=3/4 and w=2/3.

A global root restart is also a different process. Two length-two blocks give
J=1-(1-p1 p2)² and weight6/7 at p1=p2=1/2, while same-state retained-prefix
retry at total budget4 has weight6/11. A bound on one block's length does not
bound the cost to reacquire an already reached later trace state: repeated
root failures have positive probability. Nor is that reacquisition law
independent of the demonstrated probabilities.

The source's exact general configuration Bellman recurrence is already an
appropriate comparator. Its reported trained-prover gains were not tested
here, and the derivative boundary does not refute them. Our concrete downstream
use is to require recovery-law acquisition before treating trace probabilities
or a scalar mean-cost prior as an exact compute-aware learning objective.
The worker's exact DP is runnable evidence; no training/held-out advantage or
historical novelty is established by these small models.
