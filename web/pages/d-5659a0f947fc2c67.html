Exact generator theorem:
Let E be totally real with e=[E:Q]<=4. Let V_i=T(S_i)(1), 1<=i<=m, be any finite list of projective complex K3 transcendental structures with End_Hdg(V_i)=E, E-rank three, a specified common E-identification, the same active embedding sigma0, and full independent joint Hodge group G=product_i Res_(E/Q) SO(V_i,q_i). Let D_i=C_E^+(V_i,q_i), and let m_D count the labels in each E-Brauer class. For ANY H in their rational tensor category whose representation of G is faithful, the minimum number of actual auxiliary projective K3 transcendental structures whose tensor category contains H is
    r_min(H)=sum_D ceil(m_D/2).
This also counts generators for the whole original tensor category because a faithful representation tensor-generates Rep(G). No new HC or KS release premise enters.
Lower bound: the derived Lie algebra of a joint auxiliary Hodge group is subdirect in the product of individual auxiliary groups. Its original target simple ideals can be assigned injectively to auxiliary simple slots. Zarhin allows at most two rational simple slots for one K3, and two occur only for RM rank four with square discriminant. Both such slots use one quaternion algebra over one field. Centroids and the common unique split real embedding force any two original labels covered by that auxiliary to have the SAME specified D. Thus slots give bins of size<=2 monochromatic in D, requiring sum_D ceil(m_D/2) bins.
Upper bound: pair originals within each D class. The common quaternion norm module U_ik=D has E-rank four; pairing the lifted original Hodge circles gives K3 type with h20=1 and trace signature(2,4e-2). Independence makes it simple with End_Hdg=E. Its rational rank4e<=16 admits a K3-lattice complement of rank22-4e>=6 by local quadratic-form classification, reciprocity, and Hasse-Minkowski; the period theorem realizes an actual projective K3. Then wedge^2_E U_ik=V_i direct-sum V_k as rational Hodge modules, inside wedge^2_Q U_ik. One auxiliary therefore tensor-generates each common-D pair; unpaired labels use their original surface.
Higher-degree scope: for e=5 the same lower bound holds but pair acquisition has a genuine rank-two rational-lattice embedding gate; absent certificates only r_min<=m is automatic. The theorem for arbitrary finite m does not require m<=e; that restriction belongs only to the level-two distinct-embedding application.

Interface/proof core:
Let V_i=T(S_i)(1) be E-rank-three K3 transcendental structures, with End_Hdg=E, specified shared field identification, same unique active real embedding sigma0, and full independent joint Hodge group product_i Res_(E/Q) SO(V_i,q_i). For two factors with distinct even-Clifford quaternion classes D1,D2, choose any rational simple off-diagonal constituent H_O selected by an orbit idempotent in E tensor E. Every projection of a Galois orbit of distinct embedding pairs hits all embeddings, so H_O is faithful for the full product group; it has normalized level exactly two. A single-K3 tensor carrier would force a Hodge-group quotient onto this product. Zarhin classification makes every K3 derived rational group simple, except RM dimension four and square discriminant. That exception has two adjoint factors from the SAME quaternion norm algebra D/F. Centroids force F isomorphic to E, and comparison gives D2=alpha(D1) for a field automorphism alpha. Its preservation of the singleton real split place forces sigma0 composed alpha=sigma0, hence alpha=id. This contradicts D1!=D2. Therefore H_O belongs to the tensor category of no single K3, of any rank or endomorphism field; all Hodge maps from its tensor words or exterior powers vanish.
For 2<=m<=e independent factors, ANY Galois orbit of pairwise-distinct embedding m-tuples likewise gives a rationally simple faithful H_O of normalized level two; untwisted in H^(2m), its exact Hodge coniveau is m-1. No K3 Hodge group has three rational simple derived quotients, so m>=3 excludes any single-K3 carrier even when all quaternion classes agree. More generally r>=ceil(m/2), refined to the exact count above when e<=4. The generic c-return cycle account gives only support N^(m-c), while this H_O needs N^(m-1); c>=2 therefore leaves a support deficit. This does NOT bound actual geometric coniveau, disprove GHC, or exclude one H2 carrier on an abelian/other variety. The exact missing object remains an algebraic crossed-spin return cycle from H2 of such a variety to the original cross tensor. This is a strategy obstruction, not the requested foundational breakthrough.
