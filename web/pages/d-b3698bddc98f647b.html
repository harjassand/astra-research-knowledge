# Complement-LCU image: exact entry access and a stabilizer-image obstruction

Date: 2026-10-08  
Status: **exact projection/gap and entry formulas proved; no polynomial-row enforcer obtained.** A valid exact preparation-unitary completion can be chosen so the LCU image is not a stabilizer code, even for the actual BMVZ clause list. This rules out applying a stabilizer-code orbit-expander enforcer to that completion. It does not rule out a different completion, nor any non-stabilizer sparse enforcer.

## Actual BMVZ clause family and the LCU interface

For a weighted commuting-layer input $H_0=\sum_\chi w_\chi H_\chi$, with $H_\chi=m_\chi^{-1}\sum_i\Pi_i^\chi$, BMVZ's one-round path generator emits the actual clauses

\[
h_{\chi,f}=I-\bigotimes_{s=1}^{t}(I-\Pi_{f(s)}^\chi),
\]

with the path $f$ drawn from the layer's constant-degree expander walk. The Hamiltonian is the weighted average over these path labels. For a layer too small for the expander rule, BMVZ repeats its clause; in particular, a singleton layer with rank-one seed $P_v=|v\rangle\langle v|$ produces exactly

\[
h_{v,R}=I-(I-P_v)^{\otimes R},\qquad R=(2t)^\ell
\]

after the ℓ iterated rounds. The augmented hard input in the cycle-2 audit includes both BJSW five-qubit seeds

\[
|\phi_5\rangle=(|00010\rangle-|10010\rangle)/\sqrt2,\qquad
|\psi_5\rangle=(|00010\rangle-|01110\rangle-|10110\rangle-|11010\rangle)/2
\]

as separate positive-weight singleton layers, alongside all original BMVZ clauses. Thus the complete target remains a polynomially weighted sum of the generated clauses, not just the seed pair. See `../../cycle2/bmvz_bjsw_source_audit.md` for the source-level augmentation and exact occurrence audit.

Write the complete, normalized sum (including any positive-weight zero-projector padding branches) as

\[
H=\sum_{j=1}^{L}\pi_j h_j,\quad 0<\pi_j,\quad\sum_j\pi_j=1,
\]

where the $h_j$ are the full BMVZ clauses, dummy projectors if present, and zero projectors. Put $R_j=I-2h_j$. The complement identity is

\[
I-H=\tfrac12I+\tfrac12\sum_j\pi_jR_j.
\]

Use one additional identity sentinel $j=0$, and prepare

\[
|\chi\rangle=2^{-1/2}|0\rangle+\sum_{j=1}^{L}\sqrt{\pi_j/2}\,|j\rangle.
\]

Pad the label alphabet to dimension $m=2^q=\mathrm{poly}(n)$ by splitting a silent zero-projector weight if needed. Let $A|0^q\rangle=|\chi\rangle$, let

\[
\mathrm{SELECT}=|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes I+\sum_{j=1}^{L}|j\rangle\langle j|\otimes R_j,
\quad U=(A^*\otimes I)\,\mathrm{SELECT}\,(A\otimes I),
\quad V|z\rangle=U(|0^q\rangle|z\rangle).
\]

Then $V$ is an isometry and its top-left block is $Y=I-H$. The diagonal failure-flag contraction $B=(I-|0^q\rangle\langle0^q|)\otimes I$ has at most one nonzero per row and

\[
V^*BV=I-Y^*Y=2H-H^2,
\qquad H\le V^*BV\le2H.
\]

The canonical image enforcer $C=U B U^*=I-VV^*$ is an exact positive projection with

\[
\ker C=\operatorname{ran}V,\qquad C\ge I-VV^*,\qquad \text{gap}(C|_{(\operatorname{ran}V)^\perp})=1.
\]

This establishes all algebraic kernel/gap requirements. It does not establish row sparsity.

## Exact matrix-entry and row-cost ledger

Let $K_a=\langle a|V$ be the data operator in output label row $a$. For every completion $A$,

\[
K_a=\sum_j\overline{A_{j,a}}\chi_jR_j,
\qquad
(VV^*)_{a,b}=K_aK_b^*.
\]

Consequently the exact physical entry is

\[
C_{(a,x),(b,y)}=\delta_{ab}\delta_{xy}
-\sum_{j,k}\overline{A_{j,a}}\chi_j A_{k,b}\overline{\chi_k}
  (R_jR_k)_{x,y},
\]

and, since $R_j=2Q_j-I$ with $Q_j=I-h_j$,

\[
R_jR_k=4Q_jQ_k-2Q_j-2Q_k+I.
\]

For the BMVZ clauses, $Q_j$ is a tensor product of complements of the original constant-local projectors over disjoint amplified copies. Thus each requested $Q_jQ_k$ entry factors into constant-size entries on the union of the two clauses' supports per copy. If every factor is five-local and there are $R$ copies, a product row has at most $2^{10R}$ candidate columns. With $m$ labels, the direct bound for a row of $C$ is

\[
\max_x |\operatorname{supp} C_{(a,x),\cdot}|\le O(m^3 2^{10R}).
\]

This is only an exponential upper bound, not a lower bound: different pairs $(j,k)$ may cancel. The exact entry formula takes $O(m^2R)$ local-factor operations per output label block, and polynomially many label-matrix operations because $m=\mathrm{poly}(n)$. With the term table and $A$ supplied as polynomial-size algebraic circuits, a $b$-bit approximation is computable in time polynomial in $m,R,b$. Exact algebraic entry expressions are as displayed. Enumerating every candidate column is exponential in $R$; no polynomial row-list algorithm or polynomial maximum-row-support bound follows from circuit or entry access. The full BMVZ clause itself is already nonconstant-local (support can be $5R$); the failure flag $B$'s $q=O(\log n)$ label locality does not transfer to $C$.

For the forced singleton seed clauses, the actual data factors are explicit. If $S_v=\operatorname{supp}(v)$, then

\[
(Q_{v,R})_{x,y}=\prod_{r=1}^R
  (\delta_{x_r,y_r}-v_{x_r}\overline{v_{y_r}}),\qquad Q_{v,R}=(I-P_v)^{\otimes R}.
\]

The support sizes are $|S_\phi|=2$, $|S_\psi|=4$. A row of $Q_{v,R}$ with $a$ blocks in $S_v$ has exactly $|S_v|^a$ nonzeros, so its maximum is $2^R$ or $4^R$. The reflection row is obtained exactly as $R_{v,R}=2Q_{v,R}-I$, and any pair product in $C$ obeys the explicit $4Q_jQ_k-2Q_j-2Q_k+I$ formula above. The local seed entries and all these tensor products lie in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt2)$, with $O(R)$-bit exact product coefficients. This confirms termwise exact access while leaving the LCU image row list potentially exponential.

## The clause reflections are not Pauli/Clifford orbit actions

For either BJSW seed there is a five-qubit Clifford (D_v) taking (|v\rangle) to (|00000\rangle). In this decoded basis, the repeated reflection (R_{v,R}=2(I-P_v)^{\otimes R}-I) is diagonal with phase

\[
(-1)^{g_R(x)},\qquad
g_R(x)=1+\prod_{r=1}^{R}\left(1+\prod_{b=1}^{5}(1+x_{r,b})\right)
\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_2.
\]

The factor in parentheses is the indicator that block $r$ is nonzero. The algebraic-normal-form degree of $g_R$ is $5R$, since the product has a nonzero top-degree monomial on all $5R$ bits. A diagonal Clifford phase has degree at most two. Hence $R_{v,R}$ is non-Clifford for every $R\ge1$, even though $P_v$ is a stabilizer projector. Averaging its Pauli orbit does not produce the complement-LCU image projector by the stabilizer normal-form argument; the selected reflection is a nonlinear phase oracle on all amplified syndrome blocks.

This observation alone is not a no-go for a stabilizer-code *range*: a non-Clifford unitary can map one stabilizer code to another. The next argument gives a more direct, but completion-specific, range obstruction.

## Explicit LCU completion whose image is not a stabilizer code

There is freedom in $A$ beyond its first column $\chi$, and $VV^*$ depends on that completion. I construct one allowed exact-algebraic completion for which no stabilizer-code description exists. This prevents applying the cycle-2 stabilizer-orbit enforcer to this particular LCU frame; it does not show that every completion fails.

Pair the $m$ labels arbitrarily, with one designated pair $(0,j)$ consisting of the identity sentinel and any nontrivial BMVZ clause of weight $0<\pi_j<1$. For one pair with positive amplitudes $a,b$, define a real $2\times2$ block of $O$ as follows:

\[
O_{ab}=\begin{cases}
\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&s\\s&-\alpha\end{pmatrix},\quad
s=\dfrac{|a^2-b^2|}{a^2+b^2},\quad
\alpha=-\operatorname{sgn}(a^2-b^2)\dfrac{2ab}{a^2+b^2},&a\ne b,\\[6pt]
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},&a=b.
\end{cases}
\]

Take $O$ to be the direct sum over pairs. Then $O=O^*=O^{-1}$, $\operatorname{Tr}(O)=0$, and each pair has zero $\chi$-expectation. Its $+1$ and $-1$ eigenspaces both have dimension $m/2$. Since $\langle\chi|O|\chi\rangle=0$, an exact unitary $A$ can be chosen with

\[
A|0^q\rangle=|\chi\rangle,\qquad A X_1 A^*=O.
\]

To construct it, map the $+1$ and $-1$ eigenspaces of $X_1$ onto those of $O$; $\chi$ has norm $1/\sqrt2$ in each eigenspace, so the map can be chosen to send $|0^q\rangle$ to $\chi$. Complete orthonormal bases within the eigenspaces. The label dimension $m$ is polynomial, and the weights are rational, so this completion has a polynomial-size exact algebraic matrix/circuit description (for an exact algebraic-gate model). Approximating it over a fixed finite gate set would not preserve the exact range identity without an additional kernel-preserving argument.

Let $P=VV^*$, $D=2^N$ be the data dimension, and $\rho$ be the label marginal of $P/D$ in the SELECT basis. Put

\[
\tau_{uv}=D^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(R_uR_v).
\]

The normalized Pauli coefficient of the physical label Pauli $X_1$ is

\[
\mu=D^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(P X_1)
=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho O)
=\sum_{(u,v)}\left[\alpha_{uv}(a^2-b^2)+2ab s_{uv}\tau_{uv}\right].
\]

For $a\ne b$, the zero-expectation choice of $\alpha,s$ makes that pair's contribution exactly $2ab s(\tau_{uv}-1)\le0$: the normalized trace of a product of two Hermitian reflections is real and at most one. Equal-amplitude pairs contribute zero. The designated pair has strict negative contribution. Indeed, $R_0=I$, while any nontrivial projection $h_j$ satisfies

\[
-1<\tau_{0j}=D^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(R_j)=1-2\,\operatorname{rank}(h_j)/D<1.
\]

Thus μ<0. Moreover the principal (2\times2) block of ρ on labels (0,j) is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}a^2&ab\tau_{0j}\\ab\tau_{0j}&b^2\end{pmatrix},
\]

which is positive definite since $|\tau_{0j}|<1$. More explicitly, $\rho_{uv}=\chi_u\overline{\chi_v}\tau_{uv}$. The $O$-block has both signs on this pair, so $\rho$ has positive weight in both eigenspaces and $\mu>-1$. Therefore $\mu\in(-1,0)$.

If (P) were a stabilizer-code projector of rank (2^N) on (N+q) qubits, then (P/2^N) would be the normalized stabilizer projector and every Pauli expectation would lie in \(\{0,+1,-1\}\). The exact fractional coefficient μ contradicts this. So this explicit valid LCU completion has a range that is not a stabilizer code of any description size.

### Finite exact check

Take (R=1), the actual five-qubit seed projectors (P_\phi,P_\psi), and a silent zero-projector branch, each with weight (1/3). The three Hamiltonian terms have total weight one, and the label dimension is (m=4). Pair the identity branch (a=1/\sqrt2) with the φ branch (b=1/\sqrt6); pair the equal-amplitude ψ and zero branches. For the first pair,

\[
\tau_{0,\phi}=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(I-2P_\phi)}{32}=\frac{15}{16},
\quad s=\frac12,\quad \alpha=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2},
\quad \mu=2ab s(\tau_{0,\phi}-1)=-\frac{1}{32\sqrt3}.
\]

Choose (O=O_{0,\phi}\oplus\mathrm{diag}(1,-1)_{\psi,0}) and the exact (A) above. Then (P=VV^*) has rank 32 on 128 physical dimensions, while (\operatorname{Tr}(P X_1)/32=-1/(32\sqrt3)\notin\{0,\pm1\}\). This is an exact finite certificate that the chosen LCU image is not a stabilizer code. It is a finite example, not a stand-alone QMA-hard input.

## Result boundary

The complement-LCU gives an exact positive compression and a gap-one image projector. Its entries are explicitly computable, but the proved row bound remains exponential in the BMVZ repetition parameter. The full BMVZ clauses are tensor products of local complements, not Pauli permutations; in the forced seed sector their decoded phase has degree (5R). The constructed valid completion additionally has a fractional Pauli projector coefficient and therefore no stabilizer-code normal form.

The obstruction is deliberately scoped. A different completion (A) might produce a stabilizer-code image; this proof does not test the standard circuit completion. Even if no completion is stabilizer, a sparse non-stabilizer positive (C) could still exist. I have not proved a lower bound on row support of every exact positive enforcer for the full multi-clause LCU image, and the canonical (C=I-VV^*) has no polynomial-row proof. Thus the high-ceiling task is **unresolved** at the sparse-enforcer gate.
