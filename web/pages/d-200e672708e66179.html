## Primary theorem actually used

The May2026 source, paper.tex1210–1270 and3380–3823, constructs
decoder-dressed logical Pauli operators OX,OZ and a syndrome projector P.
For a Gibbs-stationary GKSL generator it invokes Chesi–Loss–Bravyi–Terhal
Theorem1 to bound the state-storage error by

    8 t ||D||_(1->1) Tr[(1-P) rho_beta].                    (1)

The prerequisites are an exact Pauli algebra, protection
[L_a,Oj]P=0 for the actual jumps, and full commutation of P,OX,OZ
with H. Its encoder is a logical state tensor the thermal syndrome
state in that Pauli algebra's virtual tensor factorization. Detailed
balance is not required for this abstract theorem. The source's
single-flip decoder argument and Gibbs witness erasure yield, after
retaining the fixed top-level witness-count prefactor,

    Tr[(1-P) rho_beta] <= C_base * 2^(-2^(k-1)),
    C_base=2(|V_2^X|+|V_2^Z|),
    Q_k=2^(k-1)>=2C_low,
    C_low=sum_(j=0,1,2)|V(G_j)|.                          (2)

Use a fixed global minimum-weight level-2 correction with fixed ties
and f2(0)=0. No locality of that bottom lookup is assumed. Paired
actual replacement gauges preserve a one-star difference, of at most7
qubits, down to level2; its minimum-weight correction also has weight
at most7. The closed difference has weight at most14, below the
independently proved admitted-family distance, so it is a stabilizer.
For ordinary levels i>=3 choose deterministic local correction rules
normalized to zero on zero input. The domination estimate excludes
levels0,1,2 and uses K_dom=2(1+Delta_level^2), with Q_k>=2C_low.
These repairs are reconstructed in the peer's

## Concrete Davies assumptions

For each finite N, specify a system Hamiltonian H_N, a reservoir in a
KMS state at fixed beta>0, and interaction

    H_int = g sum_alpha A_alpha tensor B_alpha.

We use Hermitian A_alpha with uniformly bounded norm, bounded local
support and K=O(N) independent reservoir channels. The reservoir
autocorrelations must satisfy the decay hypotheses of the weak-coupling
limit. Their Fourier rates gamma_alpha are nonnegative and obey

    gamma_alpha(-omega) = exp(-beta omega) gamma_alpha(omega),
    sup_omega gamma_alpha(omega) <= Gamma_alpha = O(1).     (3)

With [H,A_alpha,omega]=-omega A_alpha,omega, the dissipator is

    D_H(rho)=sum_(alpha,omega) gamma_alpha(omega)
      [A_alpha,omega rho A_alpha,omega^dagger
       - {A_alpha,omega^dagger A_alpha,omega,rho}/2].        (4)

The spectral components in (4) are rebuilt from the FULL perturbed H.
Locality of A_alpha is not locality of A_alpha,omega for a generic H.
A Lamb-shift Hamiltonian K_LS commutes with H and can be retained in
the coherent generator. The actual source writes the coherent part as H;
an application retaining K_LS must pay its logical commutators as well.

The finite-N weak-coupling result concerns g->0 and the rescaled time
t=g^2 t_physical with a bounded rescaled-time interval. It does not by
itself give a uniform approximation at t=exp(c N^eta), or uniform
resolution of many-body frequencies as N grows. These are different
limits. A mathematical exact-Davies lifetime theorem can be stated
without claiming that uniform microscopic approximation.

The exact older comparator is Trushechkin2103.12042v3 (25 July2021),
SecsII–III and AppendixA, Eq(EqTh). Its clustered derivation treats
H_S=H_S^(0)+g^2 delta H_S with commuting reference/correction and
bounded delta H_S. AppendixA assumes bounded CAR reservoir couplings
and weighted integrability integral |C_alpha,beta(t)|(1+t)^d dt<infinity,
d>0. It proves trace-norm convergence uniformly on every fixed bounded
rescaled-time interval, for each fixed system. Clustered KMS stationarity
is with respect to H_S^(0), and its Lamb shift need not commute with
H_S; this is a different interface from exact full-H Davies and cannot
be substituted into (4) or the new-Gibbs encoder silently.

For each finite N, such pointwise convergence can in principle choose
g_N small enough for any finite target interval, including a numerically
exponential one. It gives no acquired N-uniform lower bound on the
allowed g_N, no explicit cost for choosing it, and no fixed physical
bath-strength claim. Letting the noise strength vanish arbitrarily with
N is a changed interface. A uniform storage theorem must pay that
scaling and the physical conversion t_physical=t/g_N^2.

For every finite H, pinching gives

    sum_omega A_omega^dagger A_omega
      = time-average_t A(t)^dagger A(t) <= ||A||^2 I,
    ||D_H||_(1->1) <= 2 sum_alpha Gamma_alpha ||A_alpha||^2. (5)

Thus exponentially many frequencies do not force an exponentially
large generator norm. This does not control the CHANGE of that generator
under a Hamiltonian perturbation: the actual local counterexample in
SECULAR_LIMIT_OBSTRUCTION.md disproves such norm continuity.

## What spectral dressing supplies and what it does not

A ground-band spectral flow U can preserve the ground code and dress
local A_alpha quasi-locally. It need not conjugate the entire H into
I_log tensor H_aux. It need not map the Gibbs state to the old Gibbs
state, nor make UOXU^dagger and UOZU^dagger commute with H in the
thermally occupied excited sectors. Consequently neither the encoder
in (1) nor the bare-coupling-to-frequency protection step follows
from the ground-band conclusion alone.

At an exact full isospectral orbit H'=UH0U^dagger, Davies covariance
does hold with the same rates after pulling the physical bath back to
U^dagger A_alpha U. pde_gate10 independently handles finite-depth
orbits and their enlarged-support decoder protection. That restricted
route is distinct from the generic H0+V question.

The same actual Davies-discontinuous family has a restricted positive
thermal theorem in ACTUAL_LOCAL_NONISOSPECTRAL_THERMAL_FAMILY.md: one
commuting check shift, followed by a one-qubit rotation, changes the
spectrum while keeping a protected full logical algebra and a directly
controlled new Gibbs state. Its lifetime proof uses the new generator's
protection rather than continuity of the old generator. The final
rotated weighted checks still commute, so the example does not acquire
a generic noncommuting-neighborhood theorem.

An approximate full-H route is stated and proved conditionally in
THERMAL_TWIRL_AND_WEIGHTED_LEAKAGE.md. It explicitly pays both coherent
logical drift and frequency-resolved thermal leakage. Neither input
is replaced by a spectral-gap assertion.

## Recent thermal-phase alternative

Ma–Khemani–Sang2509.15204v2 (24 December2025; PRX Quantum7,033052,
published10 September2026, verified from APS-deposited metadata)
gives a different conditional bridge: stable clustering along a Gibbs
path from a macroscopic zero-temperature code, plus a quasi-local
Lindbladian whose individual terms fix the target Gibbs state.
Its channel encoder is not merely the ground spectral-flow unitary.
Approaching the zero-temperature endpoint also charges a suitable
low-energy density-of-states bound, rather than using a gap alone.
Theorem3 and its preceding lifetime theorem in main.tex553–691 are
the decisive statements; definitions263–286 fix the strong annular
and perturbation-uniform correlation condition.

For the actual recursive memory, that stable-clustering path and
termwise Gibbs-stationary quasi-local bath decomposition are not
acquired. Generic exact Davies components need not have a
system-size-independent spatial decay rate. The coherent local terms
-i[h_Z,.] need not individually fix rho_beta either; an admissible
termwise decomposition of the FULL dynamics must be supplied. A
Gibbs-preserving channel
circuit between two finite-temperature states would also not by itself
prove reversibility on all encoded logical states. The source theorem
pays local reversibility and code indistinguishability explicitly.

## Decisive missing estimates and costs

The exact next generic-H question is whether the actual decoder Pauli
algebra can have both exponentially small thermal coherent drift and
exponentially small frequency-resolved bath leakage in rho_beta(H),
or whether a new reversible thermal channel construction supplies them.
The protected-witness Gibbs bound for H0 is not a bound for H0+V.

Fixed-temperature Gibbs preparation, open-system rate normalization,
frequency resolution, finite-bit geometry and exponentially long
weak-coupling approximation remain charged. None of the cited sources
or our zero-temperature result closes those costs. The Chesi theorem
and Davies norm identity are primary prior; novelty of our scoped
conditional extension or model-specific obstruction is unestablished.

is performed.
