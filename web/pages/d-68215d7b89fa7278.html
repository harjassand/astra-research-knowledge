# Independent special-case Q height bound for contained Wong prefixes

7 October2026. **PASS in expanded dimensions**, for the fixed-matrix, contained Wong recurrence used by the bounded oracle. This is a separate source/code bit interface, not a compressed binary-weight bound or a height guarantee for arbitrary lattice programs, Hirai Q trajectories or modular canonical lifting.

Let integer generators B_i have shape Nt by Ns and absolute entries at most2^hB. Let the sampled integer A in their span have rank r and absolute entries at most2^hA. Coefficient sampling and the sum over generators are included when determining hA. Choose an invertible r by r minor J=A_(I,K), determinant D. Put G=embed_K J^-1 select_I, so AG is the identity on im A. For r=0, A=0 and containment either terminates immediately or rejects after the initial image, so there is no long prefix to bound.

For r>=1 set

    g=r(hA+ceil(log2(r))+1).

Hadamard bounds every r by r minor and every adjugate entry by2^g. Therefore Gbar=D G is integer with entries bounded by2^g. A kernel basis can be chosen with the same bound: put D in each free coordinate and negative adjugate-times-column values in pivot coordinates, each of which is an r by r minor by Cramer's rule. No exhaustive minor search is needed; ordinary elimination selects a rank minor.

Put C0=B ker A. While Wi subset im A, the full recurrence is

    W_(i+1)=C0+B G Wi=C0+B Gbar Wi.

The second equality is equality of spans, since D is nonzero. A generating seed for C0 has entry height at most

    h0=hB+g+ceil(log2(max(1,Ns))).

Every fixed matrix B_i Gbar has the same bound. Hence every generator obtained by at most k such products applied to a seed has entry height at most h0+k[h0+ceil(log2(max(1,Nt)))]. A strict Wong chain has at most Nt positive dimension increases. All contained prefixes therefore have generators with height at most

    hword=(Nt+1)h0+Nt ceil(log2(max(1,Nt))).

This is a height existence bound; the exponentially many formal words are NOT enumerated. The executable recurrence repeatedly computes a polynomial-size image and basis. A canonical basis of a subspace of K^Nt is ratios of minors of a selected generator basis, so its numerator and denominator heights are at most Nt[hword+ceil(log2(max(1,Nt)))], a conservative Hadamard bound. Preimage/nullspace computations concatenate A with that rational basis; clearing the finitely many entry denominators and applying another determinant bound gives a further polynomial factor in Ns,Nt,hA,hB. Repeated normalization cannot invalidate this intrinsic coordinate bound because every state is one of the same fixed-word spans.

Thus the admitted fixed trial schedule has rational bit cost polynomial in expanded copy/blowup dimensions, generator count, input/coefficient heights and number of trials while its Wong prefixes remain contained. Numeric signed weights can make those expanded dimensions exponential in their binary bit length; the explicit preflights are necessary. This special fixed-word argument does not rescue the separate general meet/join squaring example, whose repeatedly acquired matrices depend on the evolving state. Nor does it bound an unacquired Q target merely from a modular Jacobian.
