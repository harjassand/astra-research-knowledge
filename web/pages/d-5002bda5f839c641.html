# Adversarial audit: pure-family classicalization

## Verdict and scope

No fatal gap found in the stated finite-dimensional theorem or its separable-infinite-dimensional extension. The infinite extension is valid for a separable physical Hilbert space and an arbitrary, possibly uncountable, labeled family, provided the broadcaster is a normal channel and the finite/algebraic core in the proof is accepted. It does not establish the same result for a nonseparable physical Hilbert space. For an empty family the conclusion is vacuous.

## Checks

- **Broadcaster to cb-Schur estimate:** For each label, project its Stinespring output onto the joint target ray and normalize the environment component. The two marginals give joint overlap at least `1−2b`, hence vector error at most `2√b`. The diagonal Stinespring maps on `ℓ²(I)` have difference norm `sup_i ||u_i−v_i||`; telescoping at every amplification gives the cb bound `δ=4√b`, with no label-cardinality factor. Schur composition by the environment Gram multiplier gives `||S_L−S_L²||cb≤δ`.
- **Idempotent correction:** The convergent binomial series is valid because `||4(T²−T)||cb≤4δ<1`. The normal cb-Schur multipliers form a cb-norm closed unital Banach algebra, so the corrected map remains normal Schur. It is self-adjoint, fixes the diagonal, and is idempotent entrywise, hence its symbol is Hermitian with entries 0 or 1. A nontransitive triple gives the three-vertex path mask, whose Schur multiplier norm is at least `(1+2√2)/3`; this contradicts `||Q||cb≤1+η≤√3−1/2`. Thus Q is a partition mask.
- **KSW applicability/alignment:** KSW Theorem 1 applies to CP maps from any C*-algebra into `B(K)` for arbitrary Hilbert space K; its nonzero condition is automatic here, and it allows an attaining common representation equal to the direct sum of minimal representations. Each Schur channel has an explicit minimal normal dilation `x↦x⊗I` on `ℓ²(I)⊗H₀` or `ℓ²(I)⊗F`. Their direct sum remains normal. By Stinespring uniqueness, the aligned dilations are induced by environment isometries `J_C,J_D`; intertwiners of these standard amplifications are `I⊗J`. Therefore the columnwise vector discrepancy is bounded by `√a`, where `a=||S_C−S_CQ||cb`. This does not rely on equal environment dimensions. KSW source: [arXiv:0710.2495, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.2495).
- **Countability and normal measurement:** For distinct Q-block representatives, the off-block overlap is at most `a<1`; their projective rays are uniformly separated by `√(2(1−a))`. A separated subset of the projective unit sphere of separable H is countable. The block environment subspaces are orthogonal; their projections plus the residual projection lumped into one outcome form a countable POVM. Extending its complement to the whole input space and preparing block representatives gives a normal trace-preserving measure-and-prepare map. For an input in block g, its wrong-outcome probability is at most `||(I−P_g)J_Cψ_i||²≤||J_Cψ_i−J_Dφ_i||²≤a`. Together with within-block pure-state distance `≤√(2η)`, this yields the claimed error `a+√(2η)`.

The cb defect, correction, and path-mask reasoning are dimension-free for arbitrary label sets. The infinite-dimensional extension uses separability only to make the block POVM countable. KSW itself is not a normal-category theorem, but the selected minimal representations and their direct sum are explicitly normal. One methodological caveat: KSW's published proof uses compactness/minimax; if the project forbids relying on those ingredients even through an established theorem, that restriction would need separate review.

## Finite-ensemble corollary check

The claimed `64 e^(1/3)` bound is sound if each label's badness is **global** cloning infidelity `h_i=1−⟨ψ_i⊗ψ_i|B(P_i)|ψ_i⊗ψ_i⟩`. With `t=e^(2/3)`, Markov gives discarded mass `w≤e/t`; on good labels, target projection gives Stinespring vector error `≤√(2t)` and thus `δ=2√(2t)`. The conditional good-set EB infidelity is at most `δ+3η` (wrong-class probability `≤δ+η`, within-class infidelity `≤2η`). Charging discarded labels loss at most 1 gives total `≤e/t+δ+3η`. For `0<e<64^(−3)`, `δ≤1/16` and `η≤4δ`, so this is `(1+26√2)e^(1/3)<64e^(1/3)`; for `e≥64^(−3)` the bound capped at 1 is trivial. If an optimal cloner is not attained (e.g. infinite-dimensional setting), use ε-optimal cloners and pass to the supremum EB fidelity; this also handles `e=0` as a limiting statement. If a zero-error optimum is attained, `e=0` is the exact case. The phrase “individual clone infidelity” should be clarified as global per-label 1→2 infidelity; marginal infidelity alone does not give the stated `δ` scaling by this argument.
