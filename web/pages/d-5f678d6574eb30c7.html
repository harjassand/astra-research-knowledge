# Entropy-production separation gives a genuine measurement lower bound

2026-10-07. Root deduction, independently reconstructed with an effective parameter choice in `notes/r11_logic/cycle6/EP_SEPARATED_LOWER_AUDIT.md`. This supplies an EP-specific lower reduction rather than assuming that every accurate EP estimate identifies a rate. Historical novelty remains unverified.

Fix one admitted irreducible baseline Q0 from the known-support/positive-ordinary-rate-envelope class. It lacks u->v, has v->u at rate a>0, and has reciprocal support for all other edges. Let Qb=Q0+b e_u(e_v-e_u)^T, with b>0. The shortest baseline replacement path length is L>=2. Full state labels and parameter-independent calibrated observation clocks are as in the audited endpoint theorems; initialization is parameter-independent. EP uses the standard stationary dimensionless rate convention.

Matrix-tree cofactors show that the stationary law is positive and analytic at b=0. Separating the exceptional edge pair gives

    sigma(b)=J(b)log(1/b)+G(b),
    J(b)=a pi_v(b)-b pi_u(b), J(0)=a pi_v(0)>0,

where J and G are analytic near0. For any desired FIXED additive accuracy epsilon>0, choose a fixed K>=2 with J(0)log K>4epsilon. Then

    sigma(b/K)-sigma(b) -> J(0)log K

as b decreases to0. For sufficiently small b the two EP values differ by more than2epsilon. Any estimator within epsilon with probability at least.9 under each hypothesis therefore gives a binary test with both errors at most.1. Its complete stopped-transcript laws have TV at least.8. This inference uses only separation of two parameter points; it does not claim global injectivity of sigma or reduce arbitrary EP accuracy to relative-rate estimation everywhere.

Let alpha=(L-2)/(L-1), p=2-alpha=L/(L-1). The independently proved endpoint information bound on every initial state and every chosen lag is I_t,x(c)<=C c^-alpha. Throughout c in[b/K,b] it is at most C K^alpha b^-alpha. Square-root likelihood integration and the stopped-prefix affinity telescope give

    TV^2 <= [C K^alpha(1-1/K)^2/4]
                b^p sqrt(E_b N E_(b/K) N).

Consequently

    sqrt(E_b N E_(b/K) N)
       >=2.56/[C K^alpha(1-1/K)^2] b^-p.       (1)

Thus at least one expected count has the stated order, equivalently a minimax lower bound on constant-ratio windows[beta,Kbeta]. It is not a pointwise lower bound on both expected reading counts. K depends on epsilon and the fixed baseline. An arbitrary fixed epsilon need not be separated on the narrower[beta,2beta] window, so that window must not be substituted without checking its EP gap.

If every lag is at least tau, 0<tau<=t0<=1, the additional uniform bound I<=C' tau^[-(L-2)] gives the stronger window-worst-case reading lower order

    max(b^-p,b^-2 tau^[L-2]).                    (2)

This retains the small-tau domain of the sensor theorem. No tau-polynomial bound for arbitrarily large tau is claimed.

For physical duration, even a complete jump-resolved path stopped at an integrable time T has directed divergence

    KL(P_b||P_(b/K))
       =b(log K-1+1/K) E_b A_u(T)
       <=b(log K-1+1/K) E_b T.

Here A_u is time spent at u and the equality follows from the rare-edge compensator. Binary data processing for the .9/.9 test gives KL>=.8log9. Hence E_b T>=.8log9/[b(logK-1+1/K)]. Combining with T>=tau N yields window-worst-case duration lower order

    max(b^-1,b^-2 tau^[L-1]).                    (3)

The fixed-baseline family is a subfamily of the unknown-ordinary-rate model, so these lower bounds also constrain learners which must work uniformly in that larger class. The audited acquired-rates/scale EP upper in `notes/r17_nonequilibrium/UNKNOWN_BASELINE_EP_ENVELOPE.md` has unrestricted shrinking lags and matches the read/time polynomial exponents, with a loglog overhead. This does not automatically compose that upper with a minimum sensor lag. A separate joint empirical-log theorem has acquired-rate guarantees under a minimum lag, but its EP and unknown-scale versions need their own error/stopping accounting.

Effectiveness and verification: choose K=2^m with J(0)m/2>4epsilon, using log2>1/2. Exact affine stationary cofactors and rational derivative/log bounds give a computable dyadic threshold below which EP separation holds. r11 supplies that calculation and exact rational-separated fixtures for L2,3,4 and two epsilon choices. The fixtures are supporting diagnostics; the analytic two-point argument proves the stated scope.

This is a measurement-resource consequence of an established likelihood/EP interface. It does not establish a new thermodynamic principle, practical sensor advantage, unknown-structure acquisition or historical priority.
