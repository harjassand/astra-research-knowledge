## Mechanism and tradeoffs

- John J. Hopfield, “Kinetic Proofreading: A New Mechanism for Reducing Errors in Biosynthetic Processes Requiring High Specificity,” *PNAS* 71(10), 4135–4139 (1974), [doi:10.1073/pnas.71.10.4135](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.71.10.4135). Original driven multistep discrimination mechanism; the paper's error and energetic accounting is specific to its scheme.
- Davide Chiuchiu, Shrabani Mondal, Simone Pigolotti, “Pareto optimal fronts of kinetic proofreading,” *New Journal of Physics* 25, 043007 (2023), [doi:10.1088/1367-2630/acc757](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/acc757). Multi-step Pareto analysis of speed, error, and dissipation; this precludes novelty claims for broad speed/accuracy tradeoffs.
- “Reliable ligand discrimination in stochastic multistep kinetic proofreading: First passage time vs. product counting strategies,” *PLOS Computational Biology* (2024), [PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11192422/). The measured output statistic changes the conclusions; the present report therefore states its endpoint and path interfaces explicitly.
- Arup Biswas and L. Mahadevan, “When proofreading improves both speed and accuracy,” arXiv:2606.12795 (2026), [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12795). Non-Markovian renewal/stall-time regime; outside the exponential CTMC theorem and a warning against extrapolating the theorem to nature.
- Tomer Berg, Ofer Shayevitz, Or Ordentlich, “Binary Hypothesis Testing with Deterministic Finite-Memory Decision Rules,” arXiv:2005.07445 (2020), [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.07445). Prior finite-memory testing results for iid Bernoulli inputs and time-invariant machines.

## CTMC likelihood and hidden-state identifiability

- Lucas Maystre, Tiffany Wu, Roberto Sanchis-Ojeda, Tony Jebara, “Multistate Analysis with Infinite Mixtures of Markov Chains,” UAI 2022, PMLR 180:1350–1359, [PMLR page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v180/maystre22a.html). The continuous-time likelihood is expressed through transition counts and state sojourn times; supports the standard sufficient-statistic boundary, not the theorem's novelty.
- David Blackwell and Lambert Koopmans, “On the Identifiability Problem for Functions of Finite Markov Chains,” *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 28(4), 1011–1015 (1957), [doi:10.1214/aoms/1177706802](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177706802).
- Tobias Rydén, “On identifiability and order of continuous-time aggregated Markov chains, Markov-modulated Poisson processes, and phase-type distributions,” *Journal of Applied Probability* 33(3), 640–653 (1996), [doi:10.2307/3215346](https://doi.org/10.2307/3215346).
- Elizabeth S. Allman, Catherine Matias, John A. Rhodes, “Identifiability of Parameters in Latent Structure Models with Many Observed Variables,” *Annals of Statistics* 37(6A), 3099–3132 (2009), [doi:10.1214/09-AOS689](https://doi.org/10.1214/09-AOS689).
- Prony's exponential-sum reconstruction is classical; the exact Hankel pencil is proved directly in `immune_memory/PROOF.md`. No priority claim is attached.

The retrieved material establishes the comparator and novelty boundary only. It does not externally certify the new derivations or determine priority.

## Matched finite-stage Markov discriminator

- David Blackwell, “Comparison of Experiments” (1951), [Project Euclid](https://projecteuclid.org/euclid.bsmsp/1200500222), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1525/9780520411586-009): primary prior art for experiment garbling and decision-risk ordering.
- Jerzy Neyman and Egon S. Pearson, “The testing of statistical hypotheses in relation to probabilities a priori” (1933), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S030500410001152X): likelihood-ratio optimality for simple hypotheses; yields the Bernoulli-count threshold.
- Jean-René Chazottes, Cristian Giardina, and Frank Redig, “Relative entropy and waiting times for continuous-time Markov processes” (2006), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/EJP.v11-374): path-space relative entropy and waiting-time likelihood prior art.
- Paul Dupuis, Markos Katsoulakis, Yannis Pantazis, and Petr Plecháč, “Path-Space Information Bounds for Uncertainty Quantification and Sensitivity Analysis of Stochastic Dynamics” (2016), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/15M1025645): path-space KL/Fisher information formulas including CTMCs.
- Marta Casanellas, Jesús Fernández-Sánchez, and Jordi Roca-Lacostena, “The embedding problem for Markov matrices” (2020), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00818), and Robert B. Israel, Jeffrey S. Rosenthal, and Jason Z. Wei, “Finding Generators for Markov Chains via Empirical Transition Matrices” (2001), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9965.00114): prior work on fixed-time matrix generator existence and nonuniqueness.
