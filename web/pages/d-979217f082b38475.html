SHARP ARBITRARY-DIMENSION STAR BOUND FOR CONSTANT PURE CAPTURE
8 October 2026. Selective Sol /root/sol_classicality.
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE, analytic proof below.
External correctness / historical priority UNKNOWN.
This does not solve the general SU(2) positive-weight gate or N93.

THEOREM

Let H_1,...,H_N be traceless Hermitian d-by-d matrices. Assume, for every
unit vector psi in C^d,

  sum_i <psi|H_i|psi>^2 = c.                              (1)

Set K=sum_i H_i tensor H_i and define the two-output Choi star

  W=sum_i H_i^T_R tensor (H_i^A tensor I_B+I_A tensor H_i^B).

Then c>=0, sum_i H_i^2=(d+1)c I, and

  W <= (d+2)c I = [(d+1)c+c] I.                           (2)

For c>0 and d>=2 the bound is sharp: every vector

  z_v = sum_r |r r>_RA tensor v_B
                + sum_r |r>_R tensor v_A tensor |r>_B      (3)

is an eigenvector with eigenvalue (d+2)c. The normalized projector onto
this d-dimensional subspace has every single-system marginal I/d and
is an actual compatible normalized Choi state. Thus the bound is not a
mere relaxation of the legal broadcaster interface.

The proof uses Hermitian-frame Gram positivity. It does not assume a
group, covariance, commuting matrices or dimension fixed in advance.
For d=1 tracelessness forces c=0 and everything is zero. Below take d>=2.

1. POLARIZATION AND THE EXACT CONSTANT-CAPTURE IDENTITY

Let S be the swap on C^d tensor C^d, and Pi_sym=(I+S)/2. Since K commutes
with S and psi tensor psi is symmetric, (1) implies

  K Pi_sym = c Pi_sym.                                   (4)

Here is a precise justification, including why there is no larger space
of linear identities on all complex pure moments than on Sym^2. For the
Hermitian operator A=Pi_sym(K-cI)Pi_sym, the polynomial
<z tensor z|A|z tensor z> vanishes for every z in C^d by homogeneity.
Writing it in symmetric monomial coordinates gives coefficients for all
degree-(2,2) monomials z_a z_b conjugate(z_c z_d). Those monomials are
linearly independent as polynomials in the real and imaginary parts of z.
All the matrix coefficients of A are therefore zero. Equivalently,
Veronese coherent projectors span the Hermitian operators on Sym^2.

Taking the second-system partial trace of K(I+S)=c(I+S) gives

  sum_i H_i Tr(H_i) + sum_i H_i^2 = c(d+1)I.

Tracelessness proves the square-sum assertion. In entry notation (4) says

  sum_i [(H_i)_(sr)(H_i)_(ac)+(H_i)_(sc)(H_i)_(ar)]
     = c[delta_sr delta_ac+delta_sc delta_ar]              (5)

for every r,a,s,c. This coefficient identity is the load-bearing fact.
The letter c in an index is only an index; the scalar on the right is the
constant from (1).

2. POSITIVE GRAM DECOMPOSITION OF THE STAR

Put e=sum_r |r r> (UNNORMALIZED) and E=|e><e|. Define

  F=sum_i |vec(H_i^T)><vec(H_i^T)| >=0,
  vec(H_i^T)=sum_(r,a) (H_i)_(ar)|r a>.

Hermiticity makes its entries
F_(ra;sc)=sum_i (H_i)_(ar)(H_i)_(sc). The single-arm star has entries
(W_RA)_(ra;sc)=sum_i (H_i)_(sr)(H_i)_(ac). Equation (5) is exactly

  W_RA + F_RA = c(I+E_RA),
  W_RB + F_RB = c(I+E_RB).                                (6)

Thus with E_sum=E_RA+E_RB and F_sum=F_RA+F_RB,

  W=2cI+c E_sum-F_sum,       F_sum>=0.                     (7)

No partial transpose is applied to a positive inequality here. Both (6)
are exact entry identities, and the positivity in (7) is Gram positivity.

3. THE UNIVERSAL SYMMETRIC-CLONER SUBSPACE AND ITS COMPLEMENT

For v in C^d write

  x_v=e_RA tensor v_B,   y_v=sum_r |r>_R tensor v_A tensor |r>_B,
  Z_+=span{x_v+y_v},     Z_-=span{x_v-y_v}.

The output swap S_AB interchanges x_v and y_v. The spaces Z_+ and Z_-
are orthogonal, each of dimension d. Indeed <x_v,x_u>=<y_v,y_u>=d<v,u>
and <x_v,y_u>=<v,u>. Direct contraction gives

  E_sum x_v=d x_v+y_v,   E_sum y_v=x_v+d y_v,

so

  E_sum=(d+1)Pi_Z+ + (d-1)Pi_Z-.                          (8)

Its range is Z_+ direct-sum Z_-. Each summand in F_sum is positive and
F_sum commutes with S_AB. Tracelessness gives F_RA x_v=F_RB y_v=0.
Contracting (5) with v in the remaining index gives

  F_RB x_v+F_RA y_v=c(x_v+y_v).

Consequently

  F_sum z=c z for every z in Z_+.                          (9)

Since F_sum is Hermitian, Z_+ is a reducing subspace. On Z_+ the operator
c E_sum-F_sum equals c(d+1)-c=cd. On its orthogonal complement within
the S_AB-symmetric output sector, E_sum=0 and -F_sum<=0. In the
S_AB-antisymmetric output sector, E_sum<=(d-1)I and F_sum>=0, so
c E_sum-F_sum<=c(d-1)I. These three orthogonal pieces exhaust RAB.
Therefore c E_sum-F_sum<=cdI, proving (2) by (7). Equation (9) also
proves the stated equality on Z_+.

4. EXACT BROADCASTER INTERFACE AND FRAME VARIANCE CONSEQUENCE

Let omega_RAB be the normalized Choi state of any actual broadcaster with
identical marginal Phi, in the normalized trace tau=Tr/d. Then

  Tr(omega W)=2 sum_i tau[H_i Phi(H_i)].

Put V=sum_i tau(H_i^2)=(d+1)c and
E_Phi=sum_i tau[H_i(I-Phi)H_i]. Applying (2) yields

  2(V-E_Phi)<=V+c,
  V-c <=2 E_Phi.                                         (10)

Every pure-state ensemble with barycenter I/d captures exactly c under
(1), so the canonical reference-ensemble convex roof has variance V-c.
Thus (10) is the all-frame factor-two variance comparison for this exact
constant-capture frame, with no group or dimension dependence. It remains
a comparison for THIS frame; it does not furnish one EB channel dominating
the entire Dirichlet form for arbitrary frames. The unrestricted minimax
problem requires (10) for all admitted prior-weighted frames.

The equality state is omega_+=Pi_Z+/d. Its representation is invariant
under conjugate U on R and U on A,B, so irreducibility makes all its
single-system marginals I/d. Explicitly its RA marginal is

  omega_RA=[(d+2)E_RA+I]/[2d(d+1)]
            (E here is the unnormalized rank-one projector).

This has trace one, marginal I/d, and depolarizing eigenvalue
lambda=(d+2)/[2(d+1)]. Therefore omega_+ is the normalized Choi state of
the standard compatible symmetric cloner and attains (10).

5. ARBITRARY-SPIN CONSEQUENCE AND WHAT REMAINS

For irreducible spin j, d=2j+1, choose tau-orthonormal Hermitian multipoles
B_l,a and nonnegative w_l. Set H_l,a=sqrt(w_l)B_l,a. Their frame capture is
sum_l w_l S_l(psi). The exact constant-capture cone is

  w>=0,  sum_l w_l beta_l(k)=c
       for every k congruent 2j modulo 2,
  beta_l(k)=d(2l+1)(-1)^(2j+k){j j k; j j l}.             (11)

The theorem proves W_w<=V(w)+C_j(w) for EVERY j and EVERY w in this cone,
with C_j(w)=c and V(w)=(d+1)c. This is an arbitrary-spin analytic result,
not an extrapolation of low-spin numerical certificates.

The cone spans a linear space of dimension ceil(j). There are 2j
nonconstant multipoles and floor(j)+1 symmetric pair sectors. Their
floor(j) independent differences impose exactly floor(j) linear
constraints: the full beta transform including l=0 is invertible because
both K_l and the pair-spin projectors are bases of the invariant two-spin
commutant; beta_0(k)=1 makes sector differences independent even after
removing l=0. Thus dimension 2j-floor(j)=ceil(j). The all-ones weight
vector is strictly positive and has constant capture d-1, so the cone has
nonempty relative interior in this space.

Examples recovered without finite diagonalization:
spin 3/2: rays (0,1,0), c=1, and (1,0,1), c=2;
spin 2: rays (0,8,5,1), c=14, and (8,0,3,7), c=18.
For spin 2 the k=2 beta row is (-3/2,-15/14,4,18/7); both displayed rays
are exactly constant on k=0,2,4. The earlier SPIN2_PROOF ten-ray comparator
still requires its eight OTHER ray bounds: only these two are explained
by this theorem. Individual multipole polars and nonconstant mixed rays
are outside (11), and this cone cannot be presumed to generate the full
positive support fan of actual pure moments.

No acquired covariance, channel coefficients or pure moment maxima are

exact structural subcone, not an error modulus for approximate constant
capture: an approximate identity would need its actual operator/Gram
residual charged, not merely a pointwise polynomial residual in growing d.

Decisive falsifier for this scoped theorem: traceless Hermitian matrices
satisfying the exact polynomial identity (1) for which (2) fails. The
displayed entry identities and orthogonal reducing-space proof exclude
that under the stated interface. The general-spin remaining falsifier is
an actual compatible positive-L2 covariant channel with a nonconstant
positive weight w whose certified realizable pure capture violates the
corresponding factor-two roof. Such a failure would refute c=2, not every
finite c or the qualitative bounded-Holevo N93 implication.
