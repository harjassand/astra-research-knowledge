Claim: The broad claim E_pi |N_2(v)| >= E_pi d^+(v), for the stationary law of the uniform-outneighbor walk on every strongly connected oriented graph, is false. One four-vertex witness has arcs 0->1,0->3,1->2,2->0,3->1,3->2, stationary law (4,3,4,2)/13, outdegrees (2,1,1,2), and exact second-neighborhood sizes (1,1,2,1), giving expectations 19/13 and 17/13. A separate strongly connected triangle-free eight-vertex cyclic-block graph with variable outdegree has E_pi d^+=17/7 but E_pi |N_2|=2. These examples obstruct broad stationary-weighted extensions; neither refutes the existential Seymour conjecture, the minimum-outdegree formulation, or a row-regular triangle-free statement.
Interface: Finite strongly connected oriented digraphs; pi is stationary for the uniform-outneighbor transition. In the first witness outdegree is variable and directed triangles exist. The second witness is triangle-free but variable-degree, with exact second neighborhoods defined to exclude the start and first outneighbors.
Proof spine: For the four-vertex graph, multiply the stated rational row vector by the transition matrix and enumerate exact two-step neighborhoods. For the triangle-free example, arrange four two-vertex blocks cyclically, put one internal arc in each block and all arcs to the next block; the exact stationary weights are 3/28 on each u_i and 4/28 on each v_i, while each exact second neighborhood has size two.
Costs: The examples have explicit adjacency lists and exact rational stationary laws. The package retains finite-discovery and exact-check scripts; they were not executed during intake.
Failure: These witnesses do not test or falsify the regular triangle-free averaging route covered separately by N618, do not refute Seymour’s existential conjecture, and do not imply a general obstruction to stationary methods. They are scoped counterexamples to the stated weighted inequalities only.
Closest comparator: The report makes no novelty claim. Its purpose is to prevent importing a broad stationary-weighted theorem without proof.
