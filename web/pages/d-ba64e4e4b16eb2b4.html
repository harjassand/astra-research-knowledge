# Controlled exact experiment conversion

Independent checker: check_selection. 2026-10-06.

**Question/interface.** Can an adaptive catalogue of finite known sensors exactly reproduce every finite-output integrable policy from a fixed target experiment, with optimal statewise expected measurement cost and a policy-uniform remainder?

**Result.** Reconstructed candidate theorem: finite full-support collectively identified source menu, deterministic positive action costs, fixed identified target G; define R_i by min Σc_aw_a subject to Σd_ij^a w_a≥D(G_i||G_j) for all j≠i. Long target transcripts establish matching LP first-order necessity even against fully adaptive converters. Full derivation: notes/check_selection/technical_verdict.md.

**Decisive mechanism.** Classify a candidate k, propose its target-law output, and run a candidate-specific mixture q_k. Verify all candidate-versus-alternative output likelihood thresholds, but reject on any reverse alternative-versus-candidate threshold. This makes wrong-candidate cost independent of the target likelihood diameter without requiring q_k to distinguish the other states. Thinning preserves a strictly positive residual, repaired using a separate baseline mixture. Dyadic target continuations give the uniform square-root remainder.
