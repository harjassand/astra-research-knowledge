  https://doi.org/10.1080/00411459208203923
  The cited result is in a linearized grazing-asymptotics setting.

* L. Desvillettes (2015), “Entropy dissipation estimates for the Landau
  equation in the Coulomb case and applications,” J. Funct. Anal. 269,
  1359–1403, arXiv:1408.6025:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.6025

  weighted H^1 norm of sqrt(f); consequences include homogeneous-solution
  estimates. This is separate Landau-operator coercivity prior art, not a
  consequence of the N58 rank map.

  physics,” Ann. Inst. H. Poincare Anal. Non Lineaire 21, 61–95:
  https://ems.press/journals/aihpc/articles/4077202
  Read journal abstract/metadata: inhomogeneous grazing approximation under
  finite mass, energy, entropy and entropy-production estimates, on finite
  time intervals.
