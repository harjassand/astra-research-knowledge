# Deeper attack: fixed-power microcopy obstruction for noisy trajectory acquisition

Date: 2026-10-07. Worker: c08_l09. This is a post-INITIAL derivation. The
immutable independent snapshot remains `INITIAL.txt` (SHA-256

## Result and attribution

This extends the hidden-island idea in c08_l08, but changes the scaling. The
c08_l08 single-island alternatives keep the target volume entropy fixed by
speeding up one shrinking gadget, so their strain energy grows with the number
of possible locations. Here I split a fixed active-volume and fixed entropy
budget among arbitrarily many shrinking copies, keeping the strain energy
constant. The resulting no-go is for a fixed, finite-resolution noisy
trajectory interface; it does not contradict c08_l08's noiseless result.

Take one imported smooth divergence-free compactly supported three-dimensional
gadget `Y` with positive volume metric entropy `h0` per unit of its own
normalized volume and normalized strain energy `e0`. c08_l08 supplies one
possible seed via a suspension of the Berger--Turaev positive-area disk map.
No finite-precision implementation of that seed is claimed here.

There are constants `alpha0>0` and `C0=e0/(alpha0*h0^2)` depending only on
this gadget and its embedding such that, for every `eta>0` and every fixed
strain-energy cap `B >= C0*eta^2`, the smooth divergence-free fields below
have either entropy 0 or entropy exactly `eta`, while satisfying
`integral |Def X|^2 dmu <= B`. Under the observation model below, for every
finite total observation budget `T`, no estimator can distinguish these two
entropy values uniformly with success probability 2/3 once the copy scale is
small enough. Thus even a supplied strain-power cap does not yield any finite
uniform noisy-path observation-time bound over this smooth class.

This statement is conditional on the imported entropy gadget and the stated
noise channel. It is an internal construction, not a priority claim. It only
charges the integrated strain-viscous work; derivative, actuator, and sensor
costs need separate budgets.

## Construction with exact scaling

Let `G` be a compact support container for `Y`, of Euclidean volume `v0` and
diameter `D0`, strictly inside the unit cube. Write `nu0` for normalized
Lebesgue measure on `G`,

    h0 = h_nu0(phi^Y_1) > 0,
    e0 = integral_G |Def Y|^2 dnu0 > 0.

Choose a fixed `alpha0` small enough that a homothetic copy of `G` with volume
`alpha0/M` fits inside each of the `M=n^3` congruent cells of the unit flat
three-torus. Fix `c=eta/(alpha0*h0)`. If `r_n=(alpha0/(n^3*v0))^(1/3)`, place one copy in every cell and define in its local Euclidean coordinates

    X_n(a_j + r_n z) = r_n*c*Y(z),   z in G,

with `X_n=0` elsewhere. The field vanishes in a collar of every copy, so the
extension is smooth and the copies are invariant. Spatial dilation preserves
divergence-freeness. In scaled coordinates the flow is conjugate to the base
flow with time multiplied by `c`, because `z_dot=cY(z)`. Entropy of a smooth
flow scales linearly in time. Each copy has normalized-volume weight
`r_n^3*v0=alpha0/n^3`, hence

    h_mu(phi^{X_n}_1) = n^3*(alpha0/n^3)*c*h0 = eta.

The zero field has entropy zero. Since
`Def X_n(a_j+r_n z)=c*Def Y(z)`, the total normalized strain energy is

    integral |Def X_n|^2 dmu
      = n^3*(alpha0/n^3)*c^2*e0
      = eta^2*e0/(alpha0*h0^2) = C0*eta^2,

independent of `n`. The strain-viscous work on the unit-volume torus is
`W_n=2*nu*C0*eta^2`, also independent of `n`.

For every starting point `x` and all times `t`,

    dist(phi^{X_n}_t(x),x) <= D0*r_n.

Outside the copy containers the path is stationary. Inside one container it
cannot leave that container, whose diameter is `D0*r_n`. This all-time bound
is the key difference from merely having a small speed: a long but finite
trajectory remains inside a shrinking cell.

The pointwise scales are also explicit:

    ||X_n||_infinity = O(r_n),
    ||nabla X_n||_infinity = O(1),
    ||nabla^k X_n||_infinity = O(r_n^(1-k)) for k>=2.

Thus the family has a uniform C1 bound but no uniform C2 bound or modulus of
continuity for `nabla X_n`. In fact, if `g0=integral_G|nabla Def Y|^2 dnu0`,
then `g0>0` for a nonzero compactly supported gadget and
`integral|nabla Def X_n|^2 dmu = alpha0*c^2*g0/r_n^2`, which grows as `n^2`.
This derivative cost is not included in `W`.

## Noisy observation theorem

Use the following fixed sensor. A queried path starting at `x` is observed over
its chosen time interval by continuous white-noise drift data

    dZ_t = disp_X(t;x) dt + sigma dW_t,
    disp_X(t;x) = the lifted displacement phi^X_t(x)-x,

where `sigma>0` is fixed and `W_t` is standard three-dimensional Brownian
motion, independently for separate exposures. The algorithm may choose
starting points and intervals adaptively, and may launch any finite number of
paths, but the total exposure time is at most `T`. This channel is a
finite-resolution, finite-information model; direct derivative queries and
noiseless path access are excluded.

For each conditional path record, the Cameron--Martin formula gives

    KL(P_X || P_0 | chosen x, interval I)
       = (1/(2*sigma^2))*integral_I |disp_X(t;x)|^2 dt
       <= D0^2*r_n^2*|I|/(2*sigma^2).

The inequality is uniform in the chosen point and the entire chosen interval.
By the chain rule for relative entropy it continues to hold under adaptive
queries; the query-selection kernels themselves are shared conditional on
the previous transcript. Summing over exposures gives

    KL(P_{X_n}^{transcript} || P_0^{transcript})
       <= D0^2*r_n^2*T/(2*sigma^2).

Pinsker therefore yields

    TV(P_{X_n}^{transcript},P_0^{transcript})
       <= D0*r_n*sqrt(T)/(2*sigma).

Given any finite `T`, choose `n` large enough that the right side is below
`1/3`. The equal-prior optimal zero-versus-`eta` test then has error strictly
above `1/3`. An estimator uniformly accurate to `eta/3` with probability at
least `2/3` would induce a test with error at most `1/3` by thresholding at
`eta/2`, a contradiction. The same proof applies to any adaptive finite list
of independent Gaussian position samples: with `K` total samples the KL bound
is `K*D0^2*r_n^2/(2*sigma^2)`.

## Exact boundary of the result

1. Unlike c08_l08's noiseless hidden-location family, this family has a fixed
   active-volume fraction `alpha0`; uniform random starts hit an active copy
   with constant probability. Its obstruction is that every measured
   displacement is smaller than fixed sensor noise, not that all starts miss
   the active set. With exact noiseless path access, a start inside an active
   copy reveals a nonstationary path, so this argument gives no lower bound
   against a finite number of ideal exact starts.
2. It does not show impossibility under a supplied C2 bound, a lower bound on
   spatial scale, direct velocity-gradient readout, or a sensor whose
   resolution improves with `r_n`. A uniform C1 bound alone does not remove
   this example when the observation is noisy position data.
3. The force required by the strain-viscous equation is allowed to vary with
   `n`. Although its integrated work is fixed, the `nabla Def X_n` scale
   diverges. No bound on `||F||_2`, force-gradient, actuator precision, or
   force synthesis cost follows. A physical device that charges any of these
   may exclude the construction.
4. The seed entropy is imported from Berger--Turaev through the suspension
   route described in c08_l08. It is not independently implemented or
   numerically certified here. The conclusion is conditional on that standard
   smooth positive-entropy seed and standard time-scaling/entropy-decomposition
   facts.
