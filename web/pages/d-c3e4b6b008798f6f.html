THEOREM

Fix a finite B, a density sigma_B tracial on its support P, and an ACTUAL
local CPTP broadcaster C:B->B_1 tensor B_2 with B_j isomorphic to B. There
exists ONE full-domain EB CPTP Lambda_B depending ONLY on C,sigma_B,
preserving sigma_B, such that simultaneously for EVERY quantum finite A
and EVERY rho_AB with rho_B=sigma_B,
 e=T((id_A tensor Lambda_B)rho_AB,rho_AB)
obeys
 e^3 <=54 I(A:B)_rho min{1,b+sqrt(2b-b^2)},              (T1)
where b=max_j T((id_A tensor C_j)rho_AB,rho_AB) and T is HALF trace norm.
All information quantities are in NATS. In particular,
 e<=min{1,[54(1+sqrt2)I]^(1/3)b^(1/6)}.                (T2)
If the support-reduced common marginal T_B of C already preserves sigma_B,
the SAME construction can omit marginal repair and gives e^3<=54 I b.
At b=0 the fixed selected Lambda_B reconstructs every such rho_AB exactly.
At I=0 every target is a product and is reconstructed exactly.

The map is chosen independently of rho_AB,A,b,I. This is a joint quantum-
reference guarantee with a fixed B marginal, not a diamond-norm bound.
No short classical alphabet, acquisition/efficiency bound, independent
output copies, or proximity to a prescribed classical basis is asserted.

1. SUPPORT AND SYMMETRY

Use the physical support map R_P(X)=PXP+Tr[(I-P)X]sigma_B to compress both
outputs and restrict input to P. It fixes every target joint state and
cannot increase either joint marginal error. Extend the final channel
by composition with R_P. Rank one is the trivial product case. On the
compressed B, write sigma_B=tau_B=I/r. Symmetrize C by a random swap and
let T_B be its common marginal. It is actually selfcompatible, and
 T((id_A tensor T_B)rho,rho)<=b,
 delta=T(nu,tau)<=b, nu=T_B(tau).
The latter inequality is partial trace over the quantum A.

2. ONE LOCAL COMMUTING MARGINAL REPAIR

Because tau is tracial, nu and tau commute. Put M=min(nu,tau) by spectral
functional calculus of nu, and define
 K=M^(1/2)nu^(-1/2) on supp(nu), K=0 on ker(nu).
Then 0<=K<=I, KnuK=M, and Tr(nu-M)=Tr(tau-M)=delta. If delta>0, set
 chi=(tau-M)/delta,
 R_0(Y)=K Y K+Tr[(I-K^2)Y]chi.                         (R)
This is an actual full-domain CPTP channel and R_0(nu)=tau. If delta=0,
take R_0=id. This choice depends only on T_B and tau.

For EVERY extension zeta_AB with zeta_B=nu,
 T((id_A tensor R_0)zeta,zeta)<=sqrt(2delta-delta^2).    (G)
Proof: purify zeta using an additional reference E. The purified input's
entanglement fidelity under R_0 is at least the contribution of its Kraus
operator K, namely (Tr nu K)^2. Since 0<=K<=I,
 Tr nu K>=Tr nu K^2=1-delta.
The pure-input trace/fidelity inequality gives the upper bound
sqrt[1-(1-delta)^2]. Tracing E cannot increase HALF trace distance.
This is a reference-preserving bound on every extension; no A measurement
or state-family reconstruction is substituted.

Define U_B=R_0 T_B. It is CPTP and fixes tau, hence bistochastic. Applying
R_0 to BOTH outputs of the actual joint broadcaster proves that U_B is
selfcompatible. By triangle inequality and (G), its joint residual is
 b_0=T((id_A tensor U_B)rho,rho)
 <=min{1,b+sqrt(2delta-delta^2)}
 <=min{1,b+sqrt(2b-b^2)}.                              (B)
The monotonicity used in the last step holds on delta,b in [0,1].

3. FIXED REVERSIBLE CHANNEL AND ITS COMPARATOR

Since U_B is bistochastic, its HS adjoint U_B* is also bistochastic CPTP.
The channel S_B=U_B*U_B is bistochastic, HS-self-adjoint and positive,
and applying U_B* to both outputs witnessing U_B's selfcompatibility
proves S_B is actually selfcompatible. Choose ONE Psi_B from U for S_B.
This is the final EB channel on the compressed B.

HS contractivity of bistochastic U_B follows from CP Schwarz followed
by trace preservation. Algebra gives
 I-S_B <=2[I-(U_B+U_B*)/2],                            (O)
because the difference is (I-U_B)*(I-U_B)>=0. For Hermitian X put
 E_U(X)=Tr[X^2]-Tr[X U_B(X)]
       =<X,[I-(U_B+U_B*)/2]X>_HS>=0.
Thus E_Psi(X)<=4E_S(X)<=8E_U(X). The order amplifies to Hermitian AB
using a Hermitian orthonormal A basis. Both amplified maps leave A alone.

4. NONREVERSIBLE UNITAL ENTROPY PRODUCTION

Let F=id_A tensor U_B, and let rho be faithful on supp(rho_A) tensor B
temporarily. Diagonalize rho=sum_i p_i|i><i| in the FULL AB space. Set
 w_ij=<i|F(|j><j|)|i>.
Complete positivity gives w_ij>=0, trace preservation gives column sums
one, and unitality gives row sums one. Therefore
 J_F(rho):=Tr[(rho-F rho)log rho]
          =sum_ij w_ij p_j(log p_j-log p_i).
For a,b>0,
 a(log a-log b)-a+b >=(sqrt a-sqrt b)^2.               (KL)
Indeed the difference equals 2b x[x log x-x+1]>=0, x=sqrt(a/b).
The terms -a+b cancel in the double sum by bistochasticity. Also
 sum_ij w_ij(sqrt p_j-sqrt p_i)^2
 =2[1-Tr(sqrt rho F(sqrt rho))]=2E_F(sqrt rho).
Consequently
 J_F(rho)>=2E_F(sqrt rho).                            (H)
This does NOT assume U_B is reversible. The constant two is enough;
no unproved nontracial strong-Lp inequality enters.

5. EB ERROR IS CONTROLLED BY THE SAME ROOT ENERGY

Let Z=id_A tensor Psi_B and e(rho)=T(Zrho,rho). Since Z is CP and unital,
operator Jensen for the square-root function gives
 sqrt(Zrho)>=Z(sqrt rho).
Root fidelity consequently obeys
 f(rho,Zrho)=||sqrt rho sqrt(Zrho)||_1
 >=Tr[sqrt rho sqrt(Zrho)]
 >=Tr[sqrt rho Z(sqrt rho)]=1-E_Z(sqrt rho).
Using e^2<=1-f^2<=2(1-f), amplified (O), U's C4, and (H),
 e(rho)^2<=2E_Z(sqrt rho)
          <=8E_{id tensor S}(sqrt rho)
          <=16E_F(sqrt rho)
          <=8J_F(rho).                               (E)
All these are global AB trace/fidelity estimates of a physical local
channel. The quantum reference A is neither copied nor replaced.

6. POISSON ORBIT AND MUTUAL-INFORMATION BUDGET

Define Gamma_t=exp[t(U_B-id_B)], a CPTP bistochastic Poisson mixture,
 rho_t=(id_A tensor Gamma_t)rho,
 omega=rho_A tensor tau_B.
Every rho_t has the SAME two marginal states rho_A,tau_B. Thus
 D_t=D(rho_t||omega)=I(A:B)_{rho_t}>=0, D_0=I.
Differentiating, since A is preserved and log(tau_B) is scalar,
 -D_t'=Tr[(rho_t-F rho_t)log rho_t]=J_F(rho_t).
Also the Duhamel formula and trace contraction give
 T(rho_t,rho)<=t b_0.
By two uses of contractivity, e(rho)<=e(rho_t)+2t b_0. Average on [0,L]:
 e(rho)<=b_0 L+(1/L)integral_0^L e(rho_t)dt
        <=b_0 L+sqrt[(8/L)integral_0^L (-D_t')dt]
        <=b_0 L+sqrt(8I/L).                           (A)
Here the integral Schwarz inequality is dimension independent. It uses
I, not the entropy of B or the full AB relative entropy to a maximally
mixed state. The log(rho_A) term is conserved by every local map.

If I,b_0>0 choose L=(2I/b_0^2)^(1/3). Then
 e(rho)<=3(2I b_0)^(1/3), so e(rho)^3<=54I b_0.
For b_0=0 let L go to infinity in (A); for I=0 let L go to zero or
observe that rho is a product and Psi_B fixes tau_B. The selected map
does not depend on L. Finally substitute (B), proving (T1) and (T2).

Nonfaithful joint rho is handled by rho^(epsilon)=(1-epsilon)rho+epsilon
omega. It has the same fixed marginals, is faithful on the analytic
support, has I^(epsilon)<=I by convexity, and Fomega=Zomega=omega.
Its original residual and EB error both equal (1-epsilon) times the
corresponding residual and error of rho. Let epsilon down to zero.
No dimensions or inverse eigenvalues enter this limiting step.

7. PETZ-STABILITY GATE: WHAT THIS PROOF DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW

This proof bypasses rather than assumes small joint error of the ORIGINAL
Petz correction R_{tau,T_B} T_B. It instead creates U_B with an actual
gentle local marginal repair and applies C4 to S_B=U_B*U_B. A dimension-
free correction bound for the latter S_B ALSO follows from the same orbit:
CP Jensen gives T(Srho_t,rho_t)^2<=2E_S(sqrt rho_t)<=4E_F<=2J_F.
Repeating (A) with 2 in place of 8 gives
 T((id tensor S_B)rho,rho)^3 <=(27/2) I b_0.            (P)
This is an actual fixed positive/reversible S_B with small joint residual
at fixed I. It does not claim the same estimate for an unrelated original
transpose correction before marginal repair. Combining (P) with a second
reversible power theorem is valid but loses exponents compared with the
direct estimate (T1).
