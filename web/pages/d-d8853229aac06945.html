# Rank-one two-level states preserve involution-frame identifiability

Worker `b_math`, cycle 2, 2026-10-06. Complete finite-dimensional proof; independently adversarially checked by `b_compute`. This is our deduction. Broader novelty has not been checked.

## Exact statement

Let `d≥2`, let `{A_a}` be Hermitian involutions `A_a²=I`, and suppose their ordinary joint commutant is `CI`. Let
\[
\sigma=aP+b(I-P),\qquad P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|,\quad a,b>0,\quad a+(d-1)b=1.
\]
Then the SLD dressed frame `{K_a=T_σ(A_a)}` has scalar joint commutant. Thus its exact scores identify `σ` against every faithful normalized competitor. The theorem includes an arbitrary, possibly highly entangled, rank-one vector `ψ`, and all finite ratios `a/b`.

The maximally mixed case `a=b` is immediate because `T_σ` is identity. The `d=2` case follows from the invertible Bloch transformation proven in cycle 1. Assume `d≥3`, `a≠b`, and put `t=2√(ab)/(a+b)∈(0,1)`.

## Proof through defect supports

Write an involution relative to `Cψ⊕ψ⊥` as
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&v^\dagger\\v&B\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\alpha\in\mathbb R,\quad B=B^\dagger.
\]
From `A²=I`,
\[
\|v\|^2=1-\alpha^2,\quad Bv=-\alpha v,\quad B^2+vv^\dagger=I_{\psi^\perp}.
\]
The two-level filter scales only the off-block entries, so
\[
K=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&t v^\dagger\\t v&B\end{pmatrix},\qquad
I-K^2=(1-t^2)\begin{pmatrix}\|v\|^2&0\\0&vv^\dagger\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{1}
\]
When `v≠0`, this defect is a positive scalar times the orthogonal projection onto
`S_A=span{ψ,v}`, a two-dimensional subspace. Every common Hermitian commutant element `X` therefore commutes with its projection and reduces `S_A`.

If two nonzero `v_a` are not collinear, their two defect subspaces intersect in `Cψ`. A Hermitian `X` preserving both preserves their intersection, hence commutes with `P`.

Otherwise all nonzero `v_a` are collinear, yielding one proper two-dimensional subspace `S`. Every generator with `v_a≠0` preserves `S`, by `Aψ=αψ+v` and `Av=||v||²ψ−αv`. At least one such generator exists: if all `v_a=0`, then `P` commutes with the original frame, violating irreducibility. Original irreducibility also forces some generator with `v_a=0` to move `S`; otherwise every generator would preserve `S`. This generator satisfies `Aψ=±ψ`, commutes with `σ`, and consequently `K=A`. The common Hermitian `X` commutes with `A`, so it preserves both `S` and `AS`. These are distinct two-dimensional subspaces containing `ψ`, hence `S∩AS=Cψ`. Again `X` commutes with `P`.

In either case `X` commutes with `σ`. The inverse filter formula
\[
A_a=\tfrac12\big(\sqrt\sigma K_a\sigma^{-1/2}+\sigma^{-1/2}K_a\sqrt\sigma\big)
\]
then shows `[X,A_a]=0` for every `a`; ordinary irreducibility makes `X` scalar. The common commutant is a *-algebra because the `K_a` are Hermitian, so its scalar Hermitian part implies the whole complex commutant is `CI`. □

## Consequence and limits

For the fixed local `{X_i,Z_i}` frame, this proves all-temperature uniqueness for depolarized arbitrary pure states, including entangled ones. It explains why approaching a rank-one state with a uniform faithful floor need not create an exact interior collision. A quantitative gap is not derived here. The proof uses a rank-one eigenspace and the involution relations; it does not settle arbitrary higher-rank two-level states, arbitrary multi-level targets, or the full fixed-Pauli theorem.
