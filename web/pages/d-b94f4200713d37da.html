Strongest defensible claim

A primary-source preprint, Bizeul–Klartag–Lehec, arXiv:2610.05474v1 (posted 2026-10-04), explicitly claims a proof of the dimension-free Kannan–Lovasz–Simonovits (KLS) inequality for every isotropic log-concave probability measure. Its central source-reported theorem is CP(mu) <= C with universal C, equivalently CP(mu) <= C ||Cov(mu)||op for every log-concave law. The KLS proof is claimed, not certified by this audit: I checked the stated theorem, dependency chain and the cumulant-energy induction algebra, but have not completed an independent proof-level verification of the stochastic-localization regularity or every imported lemma. The preprint itself states that most proofs and mathematical ideas were found by ChatGPT, with suspension as a notable author-suggested idea; it says the authors' role was largely understanding and improving exposition. This is provenance, not a correctness verdict.

This claimed KLS resolution changes the status of the older U/N75 card: its covariance-only heat-flow argument remains a reported partial mechanism, and its missing scale-integrated mutual-information bound remains a gap in that particular reduction. That MI bound is not thereby established and may no longer be necessary for KLS. It is also not the same theorem as a uniform joint-posterior acquisition guarantee beyond Fisher spectra. The exact U target inequality cannot be stated more formally from supplied evidence: only the summary was supplied, and the linked technical note/code/verification packet is absent.

Independent algebra audit of BKL cumulant-energy induction

The proof's quantifiers appear correctly uniform at the algebraic level. Fix a deterministic unit vector u and define E_m(t)=|kappa_m(t)(u)|_t^2 using the posterior covariance metric A_t^{-1} on each free tensor slot. At every finite posterior state with positive-definite covariance, whitening by A_t^{-1/2} makes nu_t isotropic log-concave, so the inductive pointwise bounds apply with dimension-free constants, not a dimension-dependent eigenvalue cutoff. The base m=2 energy is <A_t u,u>, and E E_2(t)=e^(-t)|u|^2 from E A_t=e^(-t)Id, giving integral 1.

For m>=3, differentiating the log-Laplace SDE gives d kappa_m = kappa_(m+1)(..., A_t^(-1/2)dB_t) - (m kappa_m + L_m)dt. Applying Ito to E_m gives the paper's drift lower bound
  drift E_m >= (1/2)E_(m+1) - C m^2 E_m - 2<kappa_m(u), L_m(u)>_t.
The negative curvature cross-terms are controlled using the whitened third-cumulant matrix inequality sum_i H_i A_t^(-1) H_i <= 8 A_t, which follows from R_3(nu_t)<=8Id. The factor and tensor-slot count are consistent: after whitening, sum_i Htilde_i^2<=8Id, and Cauchy–Schwarz over m-1 tensor slots costs at most 8(m-1)^2 E_m.

For the nonlinear drift L_m, the size-a subset terms P_a satisfy integral E|P_a|_t^2 <= 2 b_a b_(m-a+1), assuming earlier pointwise and integrated induction bounds. There are binom(m-1,a-1) terms at size a, and with b_r=K^(r-1)((r-1)!)^2 each binomial-weighted square root equals sqrt(b_m). There are m-3 sizes, giving the stated L2 bound sqrt(2)(m-3)sqrt(b_m). Substitution gives relative induction error 2C m^2/[K(m-1)^2] + 4(m-3)/[(m-1)sqrt(K)], which is <=1 for the paper's sufficient choice K>=max{5,9C,64}. No dimension or covariance-condition-number enters these constants. I did not find an algebraic contradiction in this induction.

One exposition-level gap in Lemma 5.2 is repairable: integrability of t -> E E_m(t) does not imply E E_m(T)->0 for every T. It does imply a sequence T_j -> infinity along which that nonnegative integrable function tends to zero (otherwise its tail integral diverges). Apply the finite-T inequality on that sequence; monotone convergence handles the nonnegative E_(m+1) integral and the two integrable drift terms. This repairs the limit if the finite-T Ito/expectation identity is justified.

Regularity and inverse-covariance audit boundary

BKL first reduces to compactly supported full-dimensional log-concave measures. Its text states that compact support ensures Eldan's localization SDE is well-defined with a unique strong solution and bounded posterior moments; it cites Eldan and Chen for this. For finite parameters the posterior is a strictly positive exponential reweighting on the original full-dimensional support, hence its covariance is positive definite. The whitening argument is invariant and needs no uniform lower bound on lambda_min(A_t); so a condition-number blowup is not itself an identified obstruction. However, A_t^(-1) occurs in the SDE, energy metric and Ito derivatives. A complete audit still needs a stopping/localization argument proving finite-horizon Ito expectation identities and safe removal of stopping under the inverse-covariance coefficients. Compact support bounds raw moments but does not, by itself, bound whitened energies or A_t^(-1) uniformly. The paper cites stochastic-localization foundations but the exact pathwise/integrability proof has not been independently reconstructed here. Approximation from compact support to arbitrary log-concave laws is stated by BKL and appears standard; I have not checked all convergence details for every cumulant order.

Heat flow and information/acquisition gap

The Klartag–Putterman source verifies that Gaussian convolution preserves log-concavity and that Poincare constants/eigenvalues are monotone. The N75 summary additionally reports the bound, for compact centered log-concave mu with P0=CP(mu), sigma^2=||Cov(mu)||op and mu_s=mu*N(0,sI,
  P0 <= CP(mu_s) <= P0 + sigma^2 s/(P0-s), 0<s<P0.
It sketches a gradient-eigenfunction/heat-contact derivation and explicitly says the proposed subgaussian LSI is not needed. But its full technical note is unavailable, so I have not verified the sharp derivative identity or domain/approximation steps that produce this upper bound. If valid, it gives a fixed-s plateau CP(mu_s)->1 when P0=1 and sigma^2->0; it does not contradict KLS or prove a covariance-only information bound.

The missing U/N75 bridge is described only at interface level: an upper bound on scale-integrated mutual information for matrix-field Gaussian observations Z_T|Y ~ N(0, I+f_L(T R(Y)^2)), replacing the source route's LSI control of observation likelihoods with covariance-only input. Poincare control of square-root likelihoods yields an affinity/Hellinger estimate, but not the claimed additive MI over diffuse spectral blocks; raw likelihood manipulations risk uncontrolled ratios. A centered exponential law has finite Poincare constant but fails LSI, so Poincare cannot be silently promoted to LSI. The actual inequality, constants, admissible f_L/R, and cost accounting are absent; no exact formal theorem can be certified from the supplied summary.

Statistical-memory relevance and decision

The older same-Fisher-spectrum counterexample in work/cycle1/statistical_memory.txt (an allowed prior-cycle source, summarized in baseline) separates product-sensor acquisition Θ(log^2(1/epsilon)) from shared-scalar acquisition Θ(log(1/epsilon)), even with arbitrary unassisted quantum memory. Therefore tangent Fisher spectra alone do not characterize joint-posterior acquisition. The current KLS proof is a geometric Poincare result, not a coding/acquisition theorem and does not erase that counterexample. A real sufficient theorem for the target would need explicit joint posterior assumptions, observation channel class, archive/register accounting and a reconstruction or information guarantee; none follows from Fisher spectral monotonicity or the claimed KLS theorem alone.
