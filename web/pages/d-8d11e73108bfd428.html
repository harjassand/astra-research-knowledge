GLOBAL-DENOMINATOR OBSTRUCTION — EXACT GENERATOR RAY
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: REFUTED for the named global-denominator Foster template; no
recurrence or explosion conclusion about the CTMC.

Fix an integer D>=1, R=6/5, epsilon>0, and take disjoint base edges
(A1,B1),(A2,B2), with alpha=delta=p=u=v=1, q_11=2 and all other paired
rates equal to one. Include the positive reversible, substrate-preserving
deactivation pair A2+B1 <-> B1. Let h_1=1_{a1=a2=0} and h_2=1_{a2=0}. Test the
global potential

  V=A+R^(b1)+R^(b2)[1+epsilon h_2/(1+b1+b2)^D],

with the corresponding global h_1 correction omitted or retained at any
fixed finite weight (it vanishes on the ray below). At

  x_n=(a1,a2,b1,b2)=(N,0,1,n),  N=n^(D+2),

we have h_1=0,h_2=1. The B1 birth/death pair changes the denominator of the
untouched B2 correction. Its contribution is exactly

  N[p_11 R(R-1)+q_11(1-R)]
  +epsilon N R^n{p_11[(n+3)^(-D)-(n+2)^(-D)]
                   +q_11[(n+1)^(-D)-(n+2)^(-D)]}.

The second line is

  epsilon D(q_11-p_11) N R^n n^(-D-1)(1+o(1))
  = epsilon D R^n n(1+o(1)),

because q_11-p_11=1 and N=n^(D+2). All remaining contributions are
polynomial in n (including the A1 factorial reverse-autocatalysis),
bounded exponentials from B1, or the negative B2 inactive-face switch
O(epsilon R^n/n^D). They are lower order than the positive
epsilon D nR^n term. Hence LV(x_n)->+infinity. No inequality LV<=C-cV
or finite-set negative drift can hold for this potential.
