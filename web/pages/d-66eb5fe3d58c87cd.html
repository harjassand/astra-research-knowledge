1. LEGAL INPUT, GEOMETRY, AND THE EXACT DUAL

Fix d>=2 and a rational 0<=theta<1/d. N is the numerical system size,
not log N. Input R is rational Hermitian, trace one, permutation invariant,
and ||R||_op<=2; it may be indefinite. L includes all rational encodings.
Set
 D=binom(N+d^2-1,d^2-1),
 C_theta=conv{sigma^tensorN: Tr sigma=1,sigma>=theta I},
 v_theta(R)=min_(S in C_theta) (1/2)||R-S||_1.
The local domain is compact and nonempty; the finite-dimensional convex
hull is compact. Trace-norm duality and compact bilinear minimax yield
 v_theta=max_(||W||op<=1/2)[Tr(W R)-max_sigma Tr(W sigma^tensorN)].
W can be permutation-averaged: R and each iid product are invariant,
pairings are unchanged, and operator norm cannot increase.

Introduce a scalar a and the dual body K_theta:
 ||W||op<=1/2, -1<=a<=1,
 a>=Tr(W sigma^tensorN) for every admitted sigma.
The box is harmless, since all state pairings lie in [-1/2,1/2], and at
an optimum one may set a equal to their supremum. The objective is
c(W,a)=Tr(W R)-a. The body is full-dimensional in D+1 real coordinates,
even when the primal moment cone is extremely thin. This avoids a missing
interior-radius oracle for C_theta itself.

2. FULL PHYSICAL TRACE AND PSD DATA ARE POLYNOMIALLY ACQUIRED

The author's B_m basis consists of sums of distinct tensor matrix units
with d-by-d count table m of total N. It spans the entire permutation
commutant and has dimension D. Distinct orbits have disjoint matrix-unit
support, so its Gram entry is s_m=N!/prod m_ab!, with 1<=s_m<=d^(2N).
Adjoint transposes the count table. Physical trace is s_m on all-diagonal
tables and zero otherwise. It must not be replaced with the trace of
left multiplication, which has different representation multiplicities.

The three-index table formula for B_m B_k counts intermediate letters for
each fixed input/output word. At given output pair table p, the coefficient
is sum_t prod_ac p_ac!/prod_abc t_abc!, constrained by its three margins.
There are binom(N+d^3-1,d^3-1) tables; for fixed d the full structure tensor
is polynomial-sized and each coefficient counts at most d^N words.
The arithmetic and coefficient bit lengths are therefore polynomial.

For Hermitian X, G L_X is Hermitian and
 y^* G L_X y=Tr(Y^* X Y).
Physical X>=0 implies that this form is nonnegative. Conversely, the
negative spectral projector of X is a polynomial in X and belongs to the
same algebra, so its coefficients supply a negative value if X has a
negative physical eigenvalue. Thus X>=0 iff G L_X>=0. Complex rational
Hermitian matrices have the usual realification of size2D. No Schur basis
or exponentially large physical matrix is needed for these PSD tests.

For a product node, its B_m coefficient is prod_ab sigma_ab^m_ab, WITHOUT
a multinomial factor. The physical pairing is the Gram-weighted pairing.
These facts make both the local support polynomial and finite-primal SDP
exact rational data, not an uncharged block-state oracle.

4. FIXED-VARIABLE EXACT DECISION ORACLE

Parametrize a one-site trace-one Hermitian tau by its first d-1 diagonals
and real/imaginary upper entries, p=d^2-1 real variables, all in [-1,1].
Trace fixes the last diagonal. Positivity is equivalent to its 2^d-1
nonempty principal minors being nonnegative; their degrees are at most d.
Set sigma=theta I+(1-d theta)tau. This maps the ordinary state domain onto
the entire restricted domain sigma>=theta I.

For numerical rational W supplied by the outer algorithm, the objective
 F_W(tau)=Tr[W (theta I+(1-d theta)tau)^tensorN]
is a REAL rational polynomial of degree at most N in ONLY p variables.
W coordinates are data, not another block of quantified variables. The
dense polynomial has at most binom(N+p,p) monomials. Expanding its fixed
number of affine factors and Gram-weighted orbit sum takes polynomial
time, with bits polynomial in N,bits(W),bits(theta).

After clearing rational denominators, the predicate
 exists tau in a rational coordinate box:
       all principal minors>=0 and F_W(tau)>=u
has a fixed number of variables and polynomial degree/input bits. The
imported BPR theorem gives a fixed-power operation bound, and intermediate
integer bit length bounded by input_bits*degree^(fixed exponent). This is
a bit algorithm, not just a unit-cost real computation. It permits weak
inequalities and degenerate/boundary sets; no optimizer separation gap or
irrational-witness oracle is needed.
