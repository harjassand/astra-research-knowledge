assuming the exact normalized mode identity (4)/(5). The separate
fixed_domain_geometry audit covers that coefficient/phase interface. The
already banked SU(2) argument covers m=2. No dimension-uniform-in-m

Checks made:

1. The diagonal 4F3 term ratio is exactly
   t_(s+1)/t_s = z^2/[(s+1)(s+m-1)] * a_s, with a_s as (7). The proof
   that 0<=a_s<=1 uses T>=L and the stated l>=s+1 inequality correctly.
   For z^2<=m the terms from t_1 onward decrease and t_2/t_1<=1/2,
   giving 1-eta>=z^2/[2(m-1)]. The product bound delta<=L/(N+m)<=z
   has the right direction.

2. On compact positive z intervals, dominated convergence in the exact
   series gives 0F1(;m-1;-z^2). This is the characteristic function of
   a continuous coordinate distribution on S^(2m-3), so it is strictly
   below 1 for z>0. Together with the small-z estimate, this excludes
   every bounded-z sequence from a vanishing-ratio sequence.

3. The recurrence (10) and its Sturm form are consistent with the exact
   normalized Racah column. I independently checked (10) by exact
   rational substitution for m=3,...,6, N=2,...,12, all modes; the
   author's bounded exact checks are also recorded in the source file.
   The factorization (13) follows from p_k=sqrt(b_k d_(k+1)) and
   A_k=b_k+d_k. The error terms in (16) give q/L->1 uniformly from
   k=l through floor(5N/8), and p'<0 there because N^2/l^4->0.

4. In the only residual scale l/N->0 and l^2/N->infinity, the stated
   p-range gives total log variation at most log 4. Since q~L and
   |q'|<=K_m(D^2+1)/x^3, its total log variation is
   O_m(N^2/l^4)=o(1). The full interpolating parameter rectangle obeys
   q/p<=16L/N^2<1. The frozen quadratic H is invariant under the exact
   one-step recurrence; its two log-parameter derivatives are bounded
   by 3H. Thus the transport factor F=exp(3(log 4+1))=64e^3 is valid
   uniformly over the order-N interval.

5. The cutoff identity (19) has the correct plus sign and uses no endpoint
   recurrence. On the support, q=L+O_m(1); the cross term is bounded in
   absolute value using sum |u_k u_(k+1)|<=1, yielding G<=3L eventually.
   On J the energy estimate is <=21L, while transport gives at least
   N E_l/(8F). Hence E_l<=168F L/N and q_l>=L/2 yields
   u_l^2<=672F/N. Since u_l=(r_l/d_N)eta_l(l), this implies
   |eta_l(l)|^2<=C_m(N/l^2)^(2m-3), which tends to zero in the residual
   regime, contradicting eta_l(l)->1.

6. Finite strictness plus the sequence contradiction proves a_m>0 for
   fixed m. The eventual thresholds and constants are non-effective.
