C05_L05 FINAL — WEIGHTED REVERSIBLE CENTER / QUANTUM FIBER
7 October 2026. Internal mathematical candidate; external proof and priority review remain open.

RESULT

For every finite reversible Markov kernel P with faithful stationary weights pi and equal q-dimensional quantum fibers, the direct-sum algebra

  A=direct_sum_(z=1)^r M_q,       tau(A)=sum_z pi_z tr_q(A_z)

has a concrete positive, tau-selfadjoint, selfcompatible channel Phi whose powers admit one EB approximation preserving every classical center mode exactly:

  ||Phi^m-E Phi^m||_(infinity->1,tau) <= 4^(-m),   m>=1.

Here L(A)_w=sum_z P_wz A_z, E(A)_z=tr_q(A_z)I_q, C=(L+E)/2, and Phi=C^2. The symmetrized instrument-append channel for the CP components L_wz(A)_w=P_wz A_z has marginals L and E; postprocessing both outputs by C gives the actual broadcaster for Phi. Detailed balance supplies the normalized effects M_wz=P_zw P_z and prepared states P_z/pi_z. The exact SOS is

  2(I-Phi)-(I-E)=(I-L^*L)+(E-E^2)+(L-E)^*(L-E)/2 >=0.

On a center eigenvector with P-eigenvalue lambda, Phi has eigenvalue ((1+lambda)/2)^2. On fiber-traceless modes it has eigenvalue lambda^2/4. Thus the EB comparator matches slow center dynamics, even as the Markov gap vanishes, while its difference from Phi^m is confined to a fiber with L2 contraction at most 4^-m. The induced norm conclusion follows from normalized-trace L2/L1 inequalities; it is not an ancilla-assisted norm result.

For q=4 the first marginal can be non-EB: the uniform-fiber restriction decodes to depolarizing parameter 1/4, whose normalized Choi partial transpose has eigenvalue -1/64. Exact-rational checks cover the q=4 witness arithmetic and a nonuniform (2/3,1/3) two-sector fixture. The latter checks stationarity, detailed balance, likelihood similarity, weighted selfadjointness, instrument marginals, symmetrized broadcaster marginals, and C-squared similarity on every algebra matrix unit.
