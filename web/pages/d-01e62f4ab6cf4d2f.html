REVISION R2 — BALANCE ACQUISITION INTERFACE AND ADMITTED EXCEPTION
Worker c02_l06. 7 October 2026. No peer artifacts read.

QUESTION
Can the row maxima r_i=max_j f(ij)/f(empty), required as input to the aggregate capacity calibration, be obtained from determinant leverage scores or a convex program without a numerical hard-parity count interface?

1. WHAT THE CONVEX FUNCTION WOULD NEED
For h(z)=sum_{U even} f(U) z^U, all coefficients are nonnegative and h(0)=f(empty). Since odd-hole coefficients vanish, for i!=j
  partial_i partial_j log h(0) = f(ij)/f(empty) = g(ij).
Thus the wanted ratios are entries of the log-Hessian at the zero-field boundary. The fact that log h(exp x) is convex follows from its log-sum-exp form, but h is presented by exponentially many unknown coefficients. A convex optimizer needs value/gradient queries, or a separate certified evaluation algorithm. Stability of h and exact support do not supply those queries.

By contrast, the available standard determinant DPP has partition det(VV*) and diagonal leverage scores v_a*(VV*)^-1 v_a. These are computable from a positive Gram matrix, but they belong to the unfiltered distribution. INITIAL gives an exact unitary example where they stay fixed while the needed log-Hessian grows exponentially. The direct Rademacher determinant identity in R1 is a possible value oracle, but has 2^n terms and exponentially ill-conditioned signed averaging on the weak-block example.

2. TWO ROUTES FALSIFIED AT THEIR ACTUAL INTERFACE
- Leverage-only/logdet balancing: refuted by the rational orthogonal weak block in INITIAL. Its full Gram VV*=2I and all column leverage scores are 1/2, yet r_2=r_3=1/(2s^2). Varying u changes r by 2^(2L) without changing these data.
- Rejection or independent signed-term Monte Carlo: the hard-pair event has probability Theta(2^(-2L)) in the weak block, and the signed-character estimator's optimal importance-sampling relative variance is Theta(2^(4L)); see R1.
- Softened convex balancing: the inherited cycle4 exact six-site counterexample has all 12 ordinary column marginals exactly 1/2 and zero gradient at its global soft-dual minimum, while the hard-pair overlap is asymptotic to s^2/(6 epsilon^2), exponentially small for s=Theta(2^-L) and inverse-polynomial epsilon. This is work/cycle4/parity_counting_gate_attack.txt, §3. It refutes the inference from a balanced soft DPP to reliable hard-event information; it does not refute every convex program over the actual hole polynomial.

The weak-block profile extends over a nonconstant-weight parity core. Let G be the six-dimensional rational orthogonal cycle-rotation core in the executable diagnostic; its nonzero complementary-minor squares take the two distinct values 1296/15625 and 4096/15625. For F=G direct-sum R(c,s), with the final two coordinates the weak block, block factorization gives
  f_F(empty)=2s^2 f_G(empty),  f_F({last two})=f_G(empty),
so g(last pair)=1/(2s^2), while all ordinary column leverages are still 1/2. The core's nonconstant minor weights make the full instance not an automatic constant-determinant Pfaffian parity. For every positive s the pair-support pattern is fixed as s varies. The exact n=8 instance and all hole ratios are reproduced by orthogonal_balance_probe.py/.out. This is still a family counterexample to leverage-only inference, not a complexity lower bound: its block structure reveals s directly.

3. POSITIVE EXCEPTION: STRUCTURALLY PFAFFIAN SUBCLASSES
A no-black-box-count route does exist when additional algebraic structure makes all feasible parity-basis determinants equal to a known nonzero constant (after an admitted orientation/representation). Then f(U)=N_U |Delta_U|^2, with N_U the number of feasible parity bases. If the same constant-determinant certificate is available for U=empty and every pair-hole instance U={i,j}, the counts N_U can be computed by the associated Pfaffian formula, yielding exact g(ij), after which the existing scale update applies. The necessary certificate must cover each restricted instance; the property for U=empty alone is insufficient. Arithmetic-field complexity and rational bit costs must be charged separately.

Matoya–Oki define Pfaffian parities by the constant-determinant condition and retrieve the number of parity bases from an associated Pfaffian; their weighted result counts minimum-weight solutions within that restricted class. This is a real admitted exception, not a general count oracle for sums of varying squared determinants: [Matoya and Oki, arXiv:1912.00620](https://arxiv.org/html/1912.00620). The cycle4/5 reports separately record the supported bipartite pair-matrix subclass and monomial/permutation exact DP; these also do not cover arbitrary F.

4. EXACT SCOPE AND NEXT GATE
Established: (a) ordinary diagonal leverage/logdet and the obvious soft-pair convex dual cannot certify the needed balance; (b) a stable hole polynomial gives the desired ratios as a Hessian, but its determinant-derivative/Rademacher representations do not give a polynomial-cost positive oracle; (c) constant-determinant Pfaffian instances admit direct count-derived balance, conditionally on certificates for all required hole restrictions.

Unresolved: whether a more specialized polynomial-time algorithm on arbitrary rational-complex F can return multiplicative estimates of all g(ij) without a count oracle. The counterexamples refute specific acquisition mechanisms, not the existence of such an algorithm. No worst-case complexity reduction was proved. The next test is to either construct a polynomial-bit evaluation of the log-Hessian of h (or an equivalent balancing certificate), or isolate a hardness reduction for the pair-ratio task. A complete proposal must account for zero ratios, numerical precision, all n^2 pair queries, and the later balance-scaling error budget; support witnesses, maximum parity, or agreement of leverage scores do not meet that requirement.
