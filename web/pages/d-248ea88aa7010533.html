## 1. Actual schedule and preserved interface

    U=sum_(old blocks) ceil sqrt(block size),
    S_k=k+1,
    L=ceil sqrt(U^2 k^(k-1)),
    t=min {v integer>=1: M v^2>=A k L^2},
    T=t-1, m=M t^2,
    B=(k+1)ceil log2(m+1)+4,
    A=2^90, M=200000000.

These are the actual fixed-endpoint scales with K=k and S=S_k, with no inferred continuum target. The rational C6 filter, actual density g and w=1-g/m, all latitude/longitude quantiles and ambient centroids, target moments and current-node product-box corrections are the already independently gated acquisitions. The compiled density's comparability follows from the same fixed kernel/occupancy constant and A; it does not deteriorate with S_k. Every old node is forbidden at every short rational reset. Thus every prior coordinate is retained exactly and every new node avoids both old and previously reset new nodes.

The cap occupancy invariant has the same constant15000 at every stage. It is a block count bound at the current total N, rather than newly asserted separation. As before U^2<=4N, since

    U_next=U+ceil sqrt(m)<2sqrt(N_next)

## 2. Fixed-r constants do not grow with the endpoint

Fix a real r>1 for the proof, and let k_r=max(1,ceil(r)-1). For k>=k_r we have S_k>=r. The old-tail estimate can be reconstructed at this r directly, rather than substituting a growing S into a compact-interval constant. Its band multipliers are

    a_j(u)=chi_j(u)(1-h(u/L))^2[1+u+u^2]^(-r),
    R_j=2^jL,
    kappa_j(v)=R_j^(2r)a_j(R_j|v|-1/2).

Only six derivatives are needed. Derivatives of p(u)^(-r), p=1+u+u^2, are finite sums in p'=2u+1 and p''=2, with coefficients depending on this fixed r. On u>=1 they are bounded by C_r u^(-2r-q), q<=6. The fixed filter coefficients, scaled cutoff derivatives and shift then give a C6 kernel constant depending on r, uniformly in j,L,k and S_k. The actual pair rows, including the diagonal, are bounded by the unchanged cap invariant. Summing the geometric factors2^(j(2-2r)) yields

    ||D_old-g sigma||_(H^-r)<=F_r H L^(1-r),
    H=sum_(blocks) sqrt(block size),                 (D-G2)

where F_r is independent of S_k. The mathematical multiplier R_j^(2r) is used for analysis, not evaluated by the algorithm or supplied as a real oracle.

For the acquired new block, the independently reconstructed direct-occupancy Sobolev estimate gives

    ||D_new+g sigma||_(H^-r)
      <=m[e_w+C_r T^(-r)]
      <=E_r m^(1-r/2).                              (D-G3)

The weighted error constant C_r depends only on r and the fixed geometry constants. T>=t/2 and m=M t^2 contribute at most C_r(2sqrt(M))^r to E_r. The charged precision contributes at most

    m 2^(-B)/4 <=m^(1-S_k)/4 <=m^(1-r/2)/4

once S_k>=r. Thus E_r is independent of the changing endpoint too. This step uses the primitive's uniform polynomial-density interface and actual arbitrary-B tolerance; it does not pretend precision is free.

The scale matching also retains a constant independent of S_k. Put d=r/2-1 and z=m/L^2 in[Ak,4Ak]. Then

    H L^(1-r)/m^(1-r/2)=(H/L)z^d.

For d<0 use z>=Ak, giving z^d<=(Ak)^d<=k^d. For d>=0 use z<=4Ak. Since H/L<=k^(-(S_k-2)/2), both cases give

    H L^(1-r)<= (4A)^max(d,0)
                   k^((r-S_k)/2)m^(1-r/2)
              <=(4A)^max(d,0)m^(1-r/2).             (D-G4)

No growing compact-endpoint derivative bound appears in this inequality. With the exact discrepancy identity and N_k/m in[1,2], (D-G2)–(D-G4) give

    E_r(X_k)<=Q_r N_k^(-r/2),
    Q_r=2^max(d,0)[F_r(4A)^max(d,0)+E_r],           (D-G5)

for all k>=k_r. Constants are also uniform on any fixed analysis interval[r0,R] inside(1,infinity), using that fixed interval in the derivative and geometric-sum estimates. They are not uniform as r approaches1 or across unbounded r.

## 3. The finitely many early prefixes

For a single point x, the H^-r norm of delta_x-sigma has square

    sum_(l>=1) (2l+1)/(1+l(l+1))^r
       <=3 sum_(l>=1)l^(1-2r)
       <=3[1+1/(2r-2)].                             (D-G6)

This is the addition theorem in the exact probability-area normalization. Constants vanish because delta_x-sigma has mass zero. Triangle inequality for an average gives the same bound for the normalized discrepancy of every finite equal-weight set, regardless of its geometry.

There are only finitely many k<k_r. Enlarging Q_r by

    sqrt(3[1+1/(2r-2)]) max_(0<=k<k_r) N_k^(r/2)

therefore covers them. This is a finite constant depending on r and the already specified finite early prefixes, independent of later k. It is not an algorithmic selector or input; the family has already been fixed. The correct quantifiers are one acquired family, then every fixed real r>1, then a constant Q_r, then every prefix k. The inequality is not vacuous despite possibly huge early constants: the same constant governs arbitrarily late prefixes.

## 4. Growing powers, precision and total bit cost

The lower-level weighted primitive has a polynomial bit bound in numerical degree T, density degree R and height H, precision B, forbidden-node count and encoded old coordinates. Its algorithmic polynomial is uniform in these inputs; it is not a hidden fixed-S algorithm whose exponent can increase at each stage. S_k only changes the acquired integer scales and B.

The integer k^(k-1) is at most L^2 and hence at most N_k. Repeated squaring has O(log k) multiplications of O(log N_k)-bit integers, up to a harmless constant overshoot in an unused square. U^2 k^(k-1), the integer root arguments and the mass scale A k L^2 all have O(log N_k) bits. Integer bisection or standard exact square roots have polynomial bit cost. The formula t=ceil sqrt(ceil(A k L^2/M)) is exact: for integer v, v^2>=A k L^2/M is equivalent to v^2>=ceil(A k L^2/M).

Since k<=log2(N_k)/90 and m<=N_k,

    B_k=O(k log N_k)=O(log^2 N_k),
    T_k=O(sqrt(N_k)),  R_k=2L_k=O(sqrt(N_k)).         (D-G7)

T_k and R_k are separately bounded; they are not equal to each other. The input/correction count I=I0+ceil log2(B+1) is O(log N_k+log(B+1)). The reset tolerance remains the minimum of its original precision bound,2^(-B)/(32(T+1)), and h/[10000(1+67I/3)]. Thus both arbitrarily many precision iterations and their resets are charged within the unchanged travel budget. The final new-node coordinate height is

    O(B_k+log(T_k+N_(k-1)+1))=O(log^2 N_k).

Every earlier coordinate keeps its original value and shorter acquired encoding. For the north-pole start all stored heights at prefix N_J are O(log^2 N_J).

The density compilation uses every actual old denominator. The common denominator from the independent fixed-S gate divides

    m L^13 2^(2L) product_(old x,a) D_(x,a)^(2L).

Its bit height is O(L E_old+L+log m), where E_old is total inherited coordinate encoding. At prefix N_J, E_old=O(N_J log^2 N_J). With O(L^3) ambient monomials and L=O(sqrt(N_J)), the entire density coefficient encoding is polynomial in N_J. The temporary heights of exact targets, LP inputs/solutions and rational retractions can be much larger than final node heights but are still bounded by that uniform polynomial input ledger; resets restore short coordinates. All root/centroid guards and all failed collision candidates are included. Actual f evaluations, if desired for integration, are performed only at newly added nodes and total exactly N_J, apart from their separately charged evaluation cost.

There are O(log N_J) stages by (D-G1). Every intermediate count, degree, precision and coefficient/denominator encoding is polynomially bounded in N_J. The uniform per-stage bit bounds therefore sum to a deterministic polynomial in actual prefix output size. This is not polynomial work only in log N_J and is not a practical run at A=2^90.
