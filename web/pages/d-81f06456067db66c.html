In v2, Theorem 2 needs an approximately Gibbs-producing, locally reversible
channel circuit from a diameter-l code, with range<=l/4. Each Lindbladian
term must separately fix Gibbs and have uniform quasi-local norm/tail bounds.
Theorem 3 supplies a stretched-exponential guarantee from a stable-clustering

Neither that path nor termwise quasi-local stationarity follows from a spectral
stability theorem or from arbitrary Davies dynamics. At the end of the v2

choosing any smaller positive power preserves the intended weaker conclusion.
This is a bookkeeping correction, not a disproof of the theorem.

The useful contrast is an exact interface: the present restricted orbit uses

For the PARTICULAR finite-range commuting-algebra orbit proved here, there
is a useful stronger observation about the dynamics premise. Split H' into
its commuting local terms touching an original S_alpha and the remaining
terms. The latter commute with S_alpha, so every S_alpha,omega stays in the
fixed touching-term neighborhood. The complete independent Hermitian-channel

fixes Gibbs under its commutator. Thus this restricted model does meet

The missing MKS input is then the acquired locally reversible code-to-Gibbs
channel or stable-clustering path. This observation does not supply that

## Consequence, obstruction and next falsifiable step

The exact paid corollary provides a structured physical deformation family:
nonuniform/check-centralizer energies with a positive erasure margin, followed
by local non-Clifford gates. Hamiltonian differences can fail to commute with
the base H, while all new terms remain in a conjugated commuting algebra.
Finite-depth gates preserve the source lifetime exponent under fixed local
bath couplings. A quasi-local exact full-spectrum orbit with uniform tail
exp(-mu R^a) has the proved exponent min(eta,eta a/3), with the whole support,
global rate maxima and generation time charged. No optimality is claimed.

The finite exact replay shows why two attempted shortcuts fail: independently
protected singles need not have a protected product; a full decoding unitary
can preserve the spectrum while making an original local bath a zero-frequency
logical dephaser. Neither shortcut refutes the actual memory theorem. A low-band
spectral dressing supplies no exact full-spectrum thermal encoder. Generic
Hamiltonian perturbations can also change secular Davies generators by order1
at arbitrarily small perturbation, as the independent transport/prior fixtures
show; those are generator-comparison obstructions, not lifetime counterexamples.
