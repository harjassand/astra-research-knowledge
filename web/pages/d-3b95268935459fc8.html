## Reconstruction of the certificate

For each selected principal triple introduce six independent variables m_i,s_i. Set U_i=1−2m_i s_i, V_i=q_ii−s_i^2, A_i=U_i V_i, and H_ij,B as in the producer note. Every choice of one U_i or V_i for each i defines one of eight branches. Its relations have disjoint leading monomials m_i s_i or s_i^2 under a graded order; their leading terms are pairwise coprime, and replacing them by constants decreases degree. Thus zero remainders have lifts with no degree growth. This property is essential to charge the certificate degree.

On a branch define ell_i=2m_i for a U choice and ell_i=s_i/q_ii for a V choice. Then s_i ell_i=1 modulo the selected relations. Put d_i=q_ii ell_i^2, r_ij=q_ij ell_i ell_j, kappa_i=2−d_i. For a U index,

    e_ij=kappa_i−r_ij = H_ij ell_i ell_j modulo the branch,
    e_B=3−r_12−r_13−r_23 = B ell_1 ell_2 ell_3 modulo the branch.

Both lifts have product degree at most six. For a V index d_i−1=−V_i/q_ii. The identity r_ij^2=alpha_ij d_i d_j is an identity before quotienting, with alpha_ij=q_ij^2/(q_ii q_jj)<1.

With at least two U indices, relabel them 1,2. Direct collection of coefficients gives

    c=(2e_12+e_13+e_23−e_21−e_B)/3=kappa_1−1,
    d_1−1=−c, d_2−1=−c+e_12−e_21, r_12−1=c−e_12.

Consequently

    1−alpha_12=−(r_12−1)(r_12+1)
                 +alpha_12[d_2(d_1−1)+(d_2−1)]

has product degree at most eight, and division by its positive rational left side gives a unit certificate. This covers four branches, including zero and negative off-diagonal q.

With one U index 1, let r=r_23, alpha=alpha_12, gamma=alpha_23, kbar=(3−r)/2, dbar=(1+r)/2, and a=(e_12+e_13−e_B)/2. Here a=kappa_1−kbar and d_1−dbar=−a. Expansion, without eliminating a variable by a square root, gives

    E(r)=(3−r)^2−2alpha(1+r)
        =4[e_12(kappa_1+r_12)+alpha d_1(d_2−1)
            −a(kappa_1+kbar+alpha)].

This has certificate degree eight. Also r^2−gamma=gamma[d_3(d_2−1)+(d_3−1)] has degree four. Writing C=9+gamma−2alpha and D=6+2alpha gives

    C^2−D^2 gamma=(C+Dr)[E(r)−(r^2−gamma)]+D^2(r^2−gamma).

Its certificate degree is ten. Its rational constant is strictly positive: it equals E(sqrt(gamma))E(−sqrt(gamma)); E decreases on [−1,1] and E(1)=4(1−alpha)>0. Square roots establish this sign only and are absent from the constructed certificate. This covers three branches.

For the all-V branch, the quotient by s_i^2−q_ii has the eight squarefree monomials as a rational basis and is reduced. At all eight real roots, B/(s_1 s_2 s_3)=3−sum r_ij>0 since |r_ij|<1. Thus multiplication by B is an invertible rational 8-by-8 matrix. Its inverse on 1 gives a polynomial b of degree at most three. Dividing 1−bB by the three quadratics supplies a product-degree-six lift.

Write the branch certificates as 1=S_sigma+T_sigma with S in its three selected U/V relations and T in (H,B). The telescoping identity expresses 1−product S_sigma in (H,B).
