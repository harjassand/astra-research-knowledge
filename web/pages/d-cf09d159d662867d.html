# Conditional whole-unitary tail transfer and an uncontrolled-depth obstruction

## Q1. Exact interface, with uniform tails rather than a qualitative label

Use H, rho_beta, decoder logicals and p_1 from F1--F4 of
FINITE_DEPTH_FIXED_BATH_THERMAL_TRANSFER.md. Suppose

    p_1<=C_0 N^b exp(-c N^eta),   0<eta<=1, c>0.         (Q1)

Take an exact unitary U_N on the entire Hilbert space and H'=U_N H U_N*.
For each original Hermitian bath coupling S_alpha anchored at x_alpha,
suppose A_alpha=U_N* S_alpha U_N has a Hermitian truncation B_alpha,R
supported on B(x_alpha,R), with

    ||B_alpha,R||<=||S_alpha||,
    ||A_alpha-B_alpha,R||<=||S_alpha|| f(R).              (Q2)

A conditional expectation onto the ball algebra is norm-contracting and
Hermiticity-preserving, so it supplies these first properties if an explicit
operator tail bound is available. Require uniformly in N and alpha
f(R)<=C exp(-mu R^a), a,mu,C>0. The full original bath rates, including
their maxima over every relevant frequency of H, must be bounded as charged
in F4. A quasi-local global A can have frequencies outside the finite-cone
band, so local-band rate maxima are insufficient. At most polynomial N
growth is permitted for the asymptotic consequence below.

There is no bath conjugation in the physical model: (Q2) is solely an
analysis of the pulled-back original couplings. All rates are exactly the
same functions at each step. The truncated comparison Davies generator
for H and B_alpha,R is Gibbs-stationary by the same KMS identity; it need
not be the original physical device.

## Q2. Fixed-H continuity pays a tail without a frequency-count factor

Let D_A,D_B be the independent-channel Davies dissipators at the SAME H
and rates. Write E_alpha=A_alpha-B_alpha. For columns of spectral
components, pinching gives sum_omega A_omega* A_omega<=||A||^2 I.
The completely bounded norm of the bilinear jump map
sum_omega gamma(omega) A_omega (.) E_omega* is at most
Gamma ||A|| ||E||, by the Stinespring column Cauchy--Schwarz inequality.
Expand each quadratic jump and anticommutator difference into two cross
terms. Their identical norm charges yield

    ||D_A-D_B||_diamond
       <=2 sum_alpha Gamma_alpha (||A_alpha||+||B_alpha||)
                                      ||A_alpha-B_alpha||
       <=4 N r_bath f(R).                              (Q3)

This is a fixed-H extension of Chesi's pinching argument. rounding_prior
independently reconstructed the same bound. It is not continuity under
perturbing H: secular frequency splitting can make that different problem
discontinuous even on a two-qubit system.

If retaining Lamb shifts K_A,K_B with the real bounded coefficients of
F6, the same column Cauchy--Schwarz argument with |ell|<=Lambda gives

    ||K_A-K_B||<=2 N r_LS f(R),
    ||-i[K_A-K_B,.]||_diamond<=4 N r_LS f(R).             (Q4)

Both K's commute with H and rho_beta. Their local bare Pauli representation
times syndrome functions allows the original truncation support in the
protection bound, as in F6. Duhamel between the two CPTP semigroups gives
an accumulated comparison error at most
4 t N(r_bath+r_LS)f(R). This term cannot be declared negligible merely
because f(R) is small at fixed R.

## Q3. The growing support and time costs

Assume the actual bounded-density geometry gives
G_R=max_x #B(x,R)<=v(1+R)^3. Admit all Paulis in these N balls as prefixes;
their total number is at most M_R<=1+N(4^G_R-1)<=2N4^G_R. The F3 argument
with q=G_R gives

    p_R<=M_R exp(beta g(G_R-1)) p_1.                    (Q5)

For the truncated Gibbs-stationary model Chesi and the optional Lamb-shift
input drift give tN(16 r_bath+4 r_LS)p_R. Combining with (Q3)--(Q4), the
ACTUAL exact-unitary model obeys, uniformly over logical input states,

    ||Phi'_t Enc'(tau)-Enc'(tau)||_1
      <=t N [(16 r_bath+4 r_LS) M_R exp(beta g(G_R-1))p_1
                       +4(r_bath+r_LS) f(R)].          (Q6)

Here Enc'=Ad_U_N Enc, Dec'=Dec Ad_U_N*, and decoded error is at most the
same bound. The full-spectrum thermal factor is retained. If implementing
U_N or thermally preparing its auxiliary state is costly, that cost is
not solved by (Q6); no finite-depth encoder is inferred from a tail bound.

Let D=log4+beta g. Set R=floor(b_0 N^(eta/3)) for any fixed b_0>0 with
8Dv b_0^3<=c/2. For large enough N, G_R<=8v b_0^3 N^eta, so the first
term of (Q6) is a polynomial in N times exp(-c N^eta/2). The second is
a polynomial times exp(-c_tail N^(eta a/3)), with c_tail>0; the floor
can be absorbed into its fixed constants. Thus for

    zeta=min(eta,eta a/3),
    0<=t<=delta exp(c_* N^zeta)/poly(N)                 (Q7)

one obtains error<=delta, for a sufficiently small fixed c_*>0 and an
explicit polynomial covering the rate and source prefactors. For ordinary
exponential tails a=1 this is exponent eta/3, not eta. A fixed-dimensional
volume bound R^p would instead give min(eta,eta a/p). The ordinary fixed
finite-depth orbit has zero tails beyond a fixed cone and keeps eta by F15.

This is a conditional positive result, not a claimed optimal exponent.
No counterexample to preserving eta under every uniformly exponentially
quasi-local exact U has been proved here. The equation shows that this
prefix/Duhamel proof does not establish that stronger conclusion.

For a tail of the form C exp[-mu(R-v_LR |tau_U|)^a], require the actual
integer radius condition v_LR |tau_U|<=R/2. For all sufficiently large N,
the strict sufficient choice v_LR |tau_U|<=b_0 N^(eta/3)/4 pays the floor
in R. A fixed local
generation time satisfies this; a growing preparation/dressing time must
be included quantitatively. U_N is fixed during the storage interval.
Neither a time-dependent U during storage nor an unbounded hidden
Lieb--Robinson prefactor is covered.

## Q4. Exact obstruction to unitary equivalence without controlled locality

The full virtual logical factor in F5 has a decoding unitary W_N mapping
its decoder-dressed Pauli pair into X_1,Z_1. Since H commutes with that
algebra, W_N H W_N*=I_1 tensor H_aux, and its conjugated thermal encoding
is tau_1 tensor eta_aux. For a stabilizer decoder W_N can be constructed
by a Clifford syndrome extraction followed by the decoder's conditional
logical corrections; the latter need not be Clifford. It is incorrect to
assert that an arbitrary syndrome-decoder-dressed pair has a Clifford
decoding unitary. Keep an original physical bath coupling Z_1.
At the decoded Hamiltonian Z_1 is a zero-frequency logical operator. A
KMS-compatible rate gamma(0)=gamma_0>0 gives gamma_0 D[Z_1], and starting
from |+><+| tensor eta_aux yields

    ||Phi_t Enc'(|+><+|)-Enc'(|+><+|)||_1
                  =1-exp(-2gamma_0 t).                 (Q8)

The lifetime to fixed error delta is
-log(1-delta)/(2gamma_0), independent of N. The spectrum and Gibbs
partition function are exactly those of the original memory. Thus
unitary equivalence alone cannot transfer thermal lifetime under the
original bath. A finite-dimensional decoding unitary can be compiled into
bounded-range nearest-neighbor gates on a connected geometric network using
universal gates and swaps, with its actual possibly exponential cost retained.
Its depth grows with N and has no uniformly bounded cone or uniform
tail condition of (Q2). Calling every finite-size circuit 'local' without
charging its growing depth would be an invalid extension. This is not
a counterexample to the fixed-depth theorem or to uniform quasi-local
tails; it is an explicit obstruction to the uncontrolled extension.

A complete exact finite realization is saved in
work/pde_gate10/cycle14/check_prefix_and_decoding_obstruction.py and
results/pde_gate10/cycle14/PREFIX_AND_DECODING_OBSTRUCTION.json. For the
five-qubit perfect code with checks XZZXI,IXZZX,XIXZZ,ZXIXZ it reconstructs
both full dressed logicals and their 32-dimensional decoding unitary over
(1/4)Z[i], verifies its unitarity and the full Hamiltonian factorization,
and retains every unitary matrix entry. All 15 singles are protected on
the code projector, but XYIII is decoded as IIXII and leaves the logical
XYXII; the squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm of its protected commutator is 8.
This exact finite replay confirms the algebraic prefix obstruction and
decoding-unitary construction, not an asymptotic memory-family theorem.

## Status and downstream utility

Q2--Q7 give an explicit quantitative test for a proposed exact full-spectrum
dressing: supply uniform whole-operator tails and global rate bounds, then
balance the support-volume cost against the source barrier. A ground-band
spectral-flow theorem supplies neither premise. Q8 separately disproves
unqualified unitary-covariance transfer with a fixed bath. The memory's
general noncommuting thermal stability remains unresolved. Primary generic
mechanisms are prior; novelty is not claimed for the pinching/Duhamel or
decoding-unitary counterexample. The useful output is the paid exact
interface for the new concrete memory family.
