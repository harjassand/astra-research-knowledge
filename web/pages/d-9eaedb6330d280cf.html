# Cycle 3: a two-world obstruction to uniform fresh-prefix prediction

**Result.** Complete-law KL closeness to an exactly evaluable teacher cannot support a *uniform-over-prefixes* conditional-prediction recurrence with sample complexity polynomial in horizon and independent of inverse prefix mass. This remains true when both candidate sources have O(n) states and rational probabilities, and the learner may make arbitrarily many exact teacher calls. The proof is a two-world indistinguishability argument, not an appeal to an inherited rare-prefix result.

The obstruction does **not** refute Astra A06’s canonical objective, which measures task error on actual P-path prefixes and permits O(ζ) error. In this example the teacher Q itself has actual-path squared conditional-prediction risk at most ζ/(4 ln 2). The result rules out a stronger, uniform fresh-prefix guarantee; it identifies exactly where an inverse-mass condition would be needed.

## Exact A06 interface and the strengthened target tested

A06’s pinned target gives independent complete P trajectories and a fixed polynomially evaluable teacher Q that returns exact rational probabilities at any explicitly chosen prefix, with complete-law KL(P||Q)≤ζ. It asks for polynomial acquisition without inverse ζ, inverse singular-margin, or inverse latent-mass dependence, followed by a bounded-memory finite-precision recurrence and simultaneous bounded-task readouts with actual-path MSE O(ζ)+ε². It forbids full-prefix storage or post-acquisition Q calls. This is the interface stated in A06 lines 18–31 of the pinned source [page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/web/pages/d-01db0c364a53b02c.html).

The strengthened target tested here is different and explicit: after acquisition, for every explicitly supplied prefix h, the recurrence must return a next-symbol distribution whose TV distance from P(·|h) is at most 1/3, with success probability at least 2/3. It is enough to require this at a single cut and for the bounded task g=1[last symbol is 1]. Any recurrence satisfying the all-prefix target must satisfy this special case. A06 does not require that uniform conditional guarantee.

## Two sources and one identical teacher

Fix n≥2. Let X₁,…,Xₙ be independent fair bits and let Y be one terminal bit. For b∈{0,1}, define source P_b by

\[
P_b(Y=1\mid X=x)=
\begin{cases}
b,&x=0^n,\\
1/2,&x\ne0^n.
\end{cases}
\]

Thus the first n symbols are uniform; only on the rare prefix h*=0ⁿ is the final bit deterministic, with its value equal to b. Set Q to be the uniform law on all n+1 bits. Q is the same teacher in both worlds. It is positive and exactly evaluable: each next-symbol conditional is 1/2 and each word probability is 2^{-(n+1)}. Conditional evaluation costs O(1); full-word probability evaluation reads O(n) input bits and returns O(n)-bit output; one Q trajectory costs O(n) fair-bit draws.

Both P₀ and P₁ are finite-state sources. A two-valued flag records whether all bits seen so far are zero, and a clock records position; the final emission consults the flag. The flag update is an edge-emitting finite automaton and has a standard state-emitting HMM lift with O(n) states. All probabilities belong to {0,1/2,1}; coefficient precision and hidden-state count are small. The two sources and Q therefore fit the A06 finite-horizon alphabet q=2, horizon H=n+1, state bound r=O(n), and polynomial teacher-description bound L=O(n).

Write ρ=2⁻ⁿ. Since the only conditional difference from Q is at x=0ⁿ,

\[
\mathrm{KL}(P_b\Vert Q)=\rho\,\mathrm{KL}(\delta_b\Vert\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2))=\rho\ln2=:\zeta_n.
\]

This is finite because Q has full support. Also TV(P_b,Q)=ρ/2, while TV(P₀,P₁)=ρ. Both worlds satisfy the same complete-law KL promise with the same exact teacher Q and the same ζₙ.

## Uniform fresh-prefix lower bound

**Theorem.** Let an arbitrary randomized acquisition procedure receive m independent complete P_b trajectories and exact evaluation access to Q at any chosen prefixes. If m≤2^{n−2}, no procedure can, in both worlds, output a recurrence whose conditional terminal-bit prediction at h*=0ⁿ has TV error at most 1/3 with success probability at least 2/3.

**Proof.** A single P-trajectory reaches h* with probability ρ. P₀ and P₁ agree on every trajectory that does not reach h*. If h* occurs, their terminal symbols are opposite and deterministic, so the two trajectory supports are disjoint on that event. Therefore

\[
\mathrm{TV}(P_0^{\otimes m},P_1^{\otimes m})=1-(1-\rho)^m\le m\rho\le1/4.
\]

All possible Q evaluator answers are identical in both worlds; granting unlimited exact Q queries is just adjoining the same deterministic transcript and cannot increase this TV distance. Any recurrence meeting the stated conditional-TV target at h* can be converted into a test for b: predict b=1 if its output probability for Y=1 is above 1/2, and b=0 otherwise. Error at most 1/3 makes that test correct, because the true conditional probability is 0 or 1. The assumed guarantee would thus give a test with equal-prior success at least 2/3. But the optimal test’s success is at most (1+TV)/2≤5/8, a contradiction. Hence m>2^{n−2} samples are necessary. The bound is Ω(2ⁿ)=Ω(1/ζₙ) complete trajectories, or Ω(n2ⁿ) observed symbols. ∎

The theorem allows unrestricted computation and arbitrary recurrence size. It is an information bound for the supplied access interface, not a computational hardness claim. It also allows unlimited exact Q calls, so making the teacher evaluator cheaper cannot remove the obstruction. It directly concerns acquisition from P-samples plus Q, rather than conditional sampling from P.

## Matched access controls and full costs

| Access | What happens at h*=0ⁿ | Cost to distinguish P₀ from P₁ |
|---|---|---|
| A06 passive access | Complete P trajectories; each reaches h* with probability ρ. Q returns the same exact 1/2 answer in both worlds at every prefix. | More than 2^{n−2} trajectories are necessary for the 2/3, 1/3 uniform guarantee; each trajectory has n+1 symbols. Any number of O-evaluable teacher calls is uninformative about b. |
| Chosen-prefix P-conditional sample | Query the source at h*; its terminal bit equals b deterministically. | One chosen-prefix conditional sample, returning one symbol, recovers b exactly. Prefix specification costs n bits. |
| Exact P conditional-probability oracle | Query P(Y=1|h*), which is 0 or 1. | One scalar query recovers b exactly. |
| Teacher-only / Q samples | Q is the same uniform law under both P₀ and P₁. | No number of Q calls or samples distinguishes the worlds. |

The chosen-prefix P oracle is strictly stronger than A06’s teacher: it conditions the *unknown source* at the rare prefix. The fact that h* itself has positive mass does not help passive sampling when that mass is 2⁻ⁿ. The distinction is exactly the one in the conditional-query literature: a chosen prefix can be queried directly even when natural sampling almost never reaches it.

## Why this does not refute A06’s canonical actual-path loss

The same pair shows why the counterexample must not be overstated. Q predicts the wrong conditional law only on the rare branch. For any bounded future task g∈[0,1], the difference in its conditional expectation is at most the conditional TV distance. At a cut after t≤n input bits, the only differing histories are h=0ᵗ. Their P mass is 2⁻ᵗ, and their conditional TV is

\[
\mathrm{TV}(P_b(\cdot\mid 0^t),Q(\cdot\mid 0^t))=\tfrac12 2^{-(n-t)}=2^{-(n-t+1)}.
\]

Consequently, at every such cut,

\[
\mathbb E_{h\sim P_b}\big[\mathrm{TV}(P_b(\cdot\mid h),Q(\cdot\mid h))^2\big]
=2^{-t}\,2^{-2(n-t+1)}=2^{-2n+t-2}\le2^{-n}/4=\zeta_n/(4\ln2).
\]

The one-dimensional constant-state recurrence that compiles Q is therefore already an O(ζₙ) actual-path predictor, simultaneously for all bounded tasks at each cut. It can ignore the rare branch and still meet the form of A06’s loss guarantee for an appropriate universal constant. This aligns with A06’s stopped-prefix squared-TV/KL accounting lemma (lines 82–125 of the same pinned page): that lemma controls error averaged on source-generated prefixes, not the supremum over every explicitly supplied prefix.

The lower bound thus establishes a precise limitation: **complete-law KL cannot yield a mass-free uniform fresh-prefix prediction guarantee.** It does not establish a failure of A06’s expected actual-path recurrence proposition, and it does not show that A06’s static-to-sequential construction is impossible. The Q recurrence meets this pair’s actual-path loss bound whenever the universal coefficient C is at least 1/(4 ln 2). A constructive resolution of the canonical gap still needs stable features and updates meeting the actual-path objective, not uniform conditional accuracy.
