acquired from an unknown physical state. The delivered implementation admits
0<=n<=64, 0<=delta<=1 and 0<epsilon<1. Default numerical acquisition has at
most three attempts. Failed acquisition/certification returns failure.

2. ORDINARY MOMENTS AND EXACT QUADRATURE

For a probability measure mu on[0,1] representing the populations,
 p_k = C(n,k) integral x^k(1-x)^(n-k) dmu(x).
The ordinary moments are
 u_l = sum_{k>=l} C(k,l)/C(n,l) p_k.
Indeed C(n,k)C(k,l)=C(n,l)C(n-l,k-l), and the remaining binomial sum is1.
These u_l are not the logit moments p_k/C(n,k).

For odd n=2m+1 set d=m+1,
 H_ij=u_(i+j), K_ij=u_(i+j+1), H=LL^T,
 J=L^-1 K L^-T.
Positivity of the coherent measure with a uniform component gives 0<K<H,
so J has spectrum in(0,1). Since u0=1, L^T e0=e0. For j<m,
 K e_j=H e_(j+1), hence J^j e0=L^T e_j through j=m.
Splitting ell=i+j proves e0^T J^ell e0=u_ell through2m; using K on the two
last Krylov vectors proves the remaining moment2m+1. The Krylov vectors span,
so the d spectral nodes are distinct and all first-component squared weights
are positive. Their sum is1. This is the exact Gaussian rule.

For even n=2m, all K entries are known except the bottom-right. With
 A_ij=u_(i+j+1), i,j<m, and v_i=u_(i+m+1), choose
 K_mm=v^T A^-1 v.
Then K is positive semidefinite of rank m. The unknown true next moment has
nonnegative Schur complement, so K_true>=K; consequently
 H-K>=H-K_true>0.
The same Krylov proof reproduces moments through2m. J has one prescribed node0
and m interior nodes, with positive spectral weights. This is the left-endpoint
Radau rule. n1 needs only x=u1 and one weight; n0 uses an empty product.

For the numerical variant H,K and J are evaluated in mpmath. J is symmetrized,
the known Radau zero is imposed, and the computed nodes/weights are quantized
to nonnegative dyadic values. Support, positivity and normalization of the final
values are exact. Numerical residuals and condition estimates are separately
marked FLOATING_DIAGNOSTICS_ONLY; no numerical eigenvalue is an interval proof.

3. ZERO-FIELD REUSE AND LARGE BINARY FIELDS

Acquire the rule at h=0. For each node x define
 a=1-x+exp(h)x, y=exp(h)x/a, and w'=w a^n / sum_i w_i a_i^n.
Then
 C(n,k) w' y^k(1-y)^(n-k)
 =exp(hk) C(n,k)w x^k(1-x)^(n-k) / sum_i w_i a_i^n.
Thus every target population is tilted exactly. The denominator is correct
because a^n=sum_k C(n,k)exp(hk)x^k(1-x)^(n-k), a degree-n polynomial.
This avoids acquiring a nearly singular field-concentrated moment matrix.
The larger whole-Gibbs implementation caches one zero-field rule per needed
(subset size,effective delta,working precision), and reuses it for every u.
Every tilted block still gets its own exact posterior certificate.

If |h|-delta>=b, then for r>=1 the ratio to the favored endpoint obeys
 p_(endpoint-r)/p_endpoint <= exp[-(|h|-delta)r] <=2^(-br),
using e>=2. Hence the total off-endpoint mass, and the trace distance to its
pure product state, are at most 1/(2^b-1). Choosing b by exact rational
comparison to epsilon gives a product branch without evaluating enormous exp(h).

4. EXACT POSTERIOR POPULATION CERTIFICATE

Final nodes x_i=X_i/D and weights w_i=W_i/D, D=2^B, satisfy
 0<=X_i<=D, W_i>=0, sum_i W_i=D.
The delivered ideal phase-averaged populations are computed independently by
 q_k = C(n,k) sum_i W_i X_i^k(D-X_i)^(n-k) / D^(n+1).
Integer arithmetic proves q>=0 and sum q=1; acquired moments/eigenvectors are
not used by this checker.

For every target rational exponent subtract its exact maximum, leaving exp(-r)
with r>=0. To enclose it, range-reduce r/2^s=y<=1. The positive Taylor sum S
for exp(y) has remainder bounded by next_term/(1-y/(degree+2)). Reciprocate
the positive lower/upper bounds, round outwards to a fine dyadic grid, and square
s times with outward rounding. Because these intervals lie in[0,1], width grows
by at most a factor2 per square plus2 ulps. The implementation uses
working bits=accuracy_bits+s+8 and a stricter Taylor cutoff; its asserted final
width is at most2^(-accuracy_bits). Every operation uses exact rationals.

If each target unnormalized weight lies in[a_k,b_k], then
 p_k lies in[a_k/sum b,b_k/sum a], with sum a>=1 after shifting the maximum.
Let E_k=max(|q_k-p_k_lower|,|q_k-p_k_upper|). The exact diagonal-state trace
distance is bounded by (1/2)sum E_k. Stored bounds are rounded UP to dyadics.
This certifies the final mixture independently of whether numerical acquisition
was accurate, well conditioned, or produced the expected nodes.

5. ROOT-OF-UNITY PHASES, BOUNDED RANDOMNESS, FINITE LOCAL OUTPUT

For each x use the pure local vector
 sqrt(1-x)|0>+exp(2*pi*i*j/(n+1))*sqrt(x)|1>.
The ideal uniform j=0,...,n kills every off-diagonal Dicke entry because a
nonzero population-index difference has magnitude at most n. Thus the exact
uniform-phase ideal state has the certified q_k populations.

Weights are dyadic, so one B-bit uniform integer samples the node exactly.
Uniform roots can be sampled exactly by rejection, with finite expected cost.
The default actual sampler has a predetermined bit budget: map a phase integer
R uniform in0..2^b-1 to floor(R(n+1)/2^b). Each phase mass differs from uniform
by at most2^-b, giving TV at most(n+1)/2^(b+1). If n+1 is a power of two, use
its exact logarithmic number of bits and charge zero phase error. This is a
classical distribution bound, hence also a quantum trace-distance bound between
the two mixtures. It is included in the total error, not silently called uniform.

The sampler supplies both a finite algebraic descriptor of the pure local
vector and an exactly rational 2x2 PSD matrix for every site. Displayed polar
angles are FLOATING DISPLAY ONLY. For the finite density matrix, retain the
exact diagonal(1-x,x). Bound sqrt(x(1-x)) by integer square roots. Bound pi by
the Machin identity16 atan(1/5)-4 atan(1/239) with alternating rational tails;
the repaired tail cutoff is bits+14. Bound sin/cos of the exact root phase by
rational Taylor sums and their Lagrange remainder on |angle|<7, plus the pi
interval uncertainty. Each off-diagonal real/imag component is rounded TOWARD
ZERO from the interval endpoint nearest zero. Its component magnitude is no
larger than the true component magnitude, so
 re^2+im^2 <=x(1-x)
exactly. The local matrix has trace1 and is PSD exactly, including endpoints.
The sum of the component error bounds is <=8*2^(-density_bits), and equals
an upper bound on local trace distance because diagonals agree. Telescoping

the population and bounded-phase budgets. A label is a compact classical

6. WHOLE FINITE-ALPHA STATE WITHOUT A DENSE MATRIX

Use Jz=k-N/2 as the sign convention. The target is proportional to

columns K_k, k=0,...,N. Their physical spin-sector probabilities are

Spin j_b=N/2-b has multiplicity d_b=C(N,b)-C(N,b-1) and dimension

simplex: w0=qhat_lower/A_lower,0 and wN=1-w0; the other weights vanish. N1
has only one sector and K0 suffices. The resulting bounded small CLI directly

For larger N, root proposed normalized filtered-column fitting. Let scaled

The filtered target sector distribution is proportional to

7. THE LOAD-BEARING BRIDGE TO THE ACTUAL WHOLE SAMPLER

Global fhat_t are POSITIVE DYADIC approximations. Let a_b=(Aw)_b. The exact
ideal filtered candidate Fhat(K_w) has joint spin/magnetization probabilities
 s_b,t = [a_b fhat_t/(N-2b+1)] / sum_c a_c chat_c,
 b<=t<=N-b.
The true target joint probabilities are proportional to
 d_b exp[-alpha*b*(N+1-b)/N] f_t.
There are only sum_b(N-2b+1)=O(N^2) entries. Rigorous scalar exponential
intervals enclose every true joint probability; the candidate entries are
exact rationals. Their TV bounds the FULL physical trace distance, because
both states are scalar within the same joint projectors. No2^N matrix occurs.
If the exact filter were shared, the conditional magnetization laws would
agree and the distance would reduce exactly to sector TV. The implemented
joint certificate ALSO charges the actual rounded filter discrepancy.

Disintegrate Fhat(K_w) by type(k,u): the raw mass is
 w_k C(N-k,u) sum_{l=0}^k fhat_(u+l)/[(k+1)2^(N-k)].
Choose the k-subset and complementary u-one string uniformly. On the subset,
the ideal block populations are EXACTLY
 phat_l=fhat_(u+l)/sum_{a=0}^k fhat_(u+a).
The sum of raw masses is independently checked equal to sum_b a_b chat_b.
Normalize and round type probabilities to dyadics; compute their exact TV
rounding error. Each actual quadrature block is acquired using
 delta'=delta*k/N, h'=h-delta*(2u-(N-k))/N.
Its own true-Gaussian certificate alone does NOT bridge the rounded fhat input.
We additionally compute the EXACT rational TV between its recovered q_l and
the intended phat_l, for EVERY positive type. Weighted errors give the inner
state bound. The scaling version rounds each weighted nonnegative block error
UP to160 dyadic bits before summing, avoiding an exponential denominator LCM;
the added upper-bound slack is <=branch_count*2^-160.

Finally charge the finite phase/density budgets, plus bounded near-uniform
subset and complement choices. A rank chosen by floor(R*C/2^128) has TV to
uniform at most C/2^129. Lexicographic combinatorial unranking needs no list
of all subsets. Every actual output is a product of explicitly rational PSD
one-qubit matrices. Branch-dependent unused random bits are padded, giving a
known predetermined total bit count. The triangle sum is the reported total
target trace-distance bound, and acceptance requires it<=requested epsilon.
