The prior potential is \(A^2+13A+2B\), rather than the v1 \(+11A\). Its degree14, five-variable constant-Hessian example and collision are author results. **New relevant primary correction.** Masoud Gharahi, arXiv2608.11182v2, On the Kronecker Products of Symmetric Persistent Tensors, was submitted27 August2026 17:45:39UTC. The v1 was submitted11 August17:43UTC. The revised record explicitly replaces the broad closure assertion with refined positive cases and a counterexample. Section5.1 equations40–42 derives normalized nilpotence from a determinant identity; Proposition26/Corollary27 impose simultaneous strict triangularizability as an additional sufficient condition. Section6/Remark36 provides a persistent cubic in dimension12 whose Kronecker square is not persistent. This is a useful warning against inferring a uniform flag from pointwise nilpotence. It is **not** a counterexample in our four-dimensional relative-Hessian pencil class, nor an adopted classification of that class. Read: abstract/introduction and these sections only.

**Remaining alternatives.** Direct target repairs at a nonzero level are now legal automatically; they still need an actual constant nonzero reduced Hessian determinant and retained gradient collision. Full-source inheritance requires the unchanged zero-level determinant and all pencil coefficients. Three-or-more residual shears, nonlinear pivot terms vanishing at the \(c=0\) base fibre, and base-moving charts remain outside the present no-go results. No HC4 counterexample or all-degree closure is claimed.
