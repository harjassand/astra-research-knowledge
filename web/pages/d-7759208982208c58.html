## Exact identity and source normalization

Use the source half-normalized per-direction observable

    Oλ=½[λSλ†Sλ+(1−λ)SλSλ†−i[A,Sλ+Sλ†]],

where −i[A,ρ]=(1−λ)ρSλ+λSλρ. Assume A=A† and A²=I. Set K=βH_phys, q=e⁻ᴷ and ρ=q/Trq. Direct integration gives

    Obar=K−AKA−I+Dlog_q(AqA)
        =K−AKA−I+k(ad_K)(BB†),
    B=eᴷᐟ²Ae⁻ᴷᐟ², k(ν)=ν/[2sinh(ν/2)], k(0)=1.

The root's no-half convention doubles this observable. No log partition function is needed: scalar normalization cancels in K−AKA and Dlog_{cq}(cX)=Dlog_q(X).

Independent coefficient derivation checks signs and factors. In a ρ eigenbasis put a=pᵢ/p_k, b=p_k/pⱼ. The half-normalized quadratic coefficient integrates to

    I(a,b)=−∫₀¹ (1−λ)(a−1)(b−1)
                   /[(a−(a−1)λ)(1+(b−1)λ)] dλ
          =[(b−1)log a−b(a−1)log b]/(ab−1),

with continuous diagonal limit I(a,1/a)=log a−1+1/a. The half-normalized derivative term averages to2(K−AKA), since ∫Sλ=i(log pᵢ−log pⱼ)Aᵢⱼ and −i[A,∫(Sλ+Sλ†)]/2=2(K−AKA).

The desired quadratic coefficient is

    ½(log a−log b)−1+b log(ab)/(ab−1).

Its difference from I(a,b) depends only on r=ab=pᵢ/pⱼ:

    I−desired=1−[(r+1)/(2(r−1))]log r.

Summing over k multiplies this by (A²)ᵢⱼ. For i≠j it vanishes because A²=I; at i=j its continuous limit is0. This proves the identity for arbitrary finite dimension, not just the numerical examples.

`work/r11_logic/cycle4/check_uniform_mixture.py` independently verifies the whole4-dimensional matrix identity exactly for rational populations and a dense rational Hermitian involution, plus direct symbolic rational integrals. This finite implementation check supports, rather than replaces, the preceding universal derivation. It also preserves the scope counterexample: norm-one projector A=diag(1,0), K=diag(0,−log2) has all scores zero, while the involution formula falsely gives diag(0,−1−log2). Norm one alone is insufficient.

The Fréchet derivative term has matrix entries log(pᵢ/pⱼ)/(pᵢ−pⱼ)·(AqA)ᵢⱼ, with diagonal1/pᵢ. Since (BB†)ᵢⱼ=(AqA)ᵢⱼ/√(pᵢpⱼ), the even k multiplier follows exactly.

## Positive real-time kernel independently checked

The density η(t)=(π/2)sech²(πt) is nonnegative and integrates to1. Substitution u=e²πᵗ gives

    ∫η(t)eⁱνᵗdt=∫₀∞uⁱνᐟ²π/(1+u)²du
                =Γ(1+iν/2π)Γ(1−iν/2π)
                =ν/[2sinh(ν/2)].

Hence k(ad_K)(W)=∫η(t)eⁱᵗᴷWe⁻ⁱᵗᴷdt is a unital completely positive average, contractive in operator norm, and positive for W≥0. Its two-sided time-tail probability is1−tanh(πT)≤2e⁻²πᵀ. Also E|t|=log2/π. These facts justify both the norm and the continuity bounds without global candidate evolution.

## Localization: complete source-to-constant reconstruction

Assume an OPEN finite interval of a1D chain, interaction diameter at most integer R≥1, J=supₓΣ_{X∋x}||Φ_X||, and A supported in an interval of length q_A. Extend interactions by zero outside the chain. This preserves J,R and lets the source's theorems on Z apply at either endpoint. No translation invariance is needed. A periodic wrap bond can have large diameter under this embedding; periodic chains are not covered by this argument without an additional boundary treatment.

Source Eq19 gives Σ_kΩ*_k(x)z^k=exp[xΣ_{j≥1}Ω_jz^j], where Ω_j≤J and Ω_j=0 for j>R. At imaginary time s=−iβ/2, Theorem2.3 has prefactor e^{βJq_A}, coefficient Ω*_k(2β) and factor e^{2βJk}. Taking z=e^{2βJ+μ} gives

    Mμ=exp[βJq_A+2βJR exp((2βJ+μ)R)], C=M₀,
    ||B||,||Bℓ||≤C,
    ||B−Bℓ||≤Mμ e^{−μ(ℓ+1)}.

This uses the finite-range coefficient generating function; the paper's exponential-tail strip does not establish arbitrary-temperature localization for fixed exponential decay rate. Source bounds are used at norm-one A, so their printed normalization omission in Eq16 does not affect this application.

For a W supported in the enlarged interval of length w=q_A+2ℓ, Theorem2.4 and z=e give

    ||Γ_{βt}^{H_phys}(W)−Γ_{βt}^{H_L}(W)||
       ≤||W||w exp[βv|t|−(p+1)], v=4J(1+R e^R),

when L pads that interval by p sites. The bound follows from Ω*_k(4β|t|), the source's e^{4β|t|J} prefactor and Σ_{j=1}^RΩ_je^j≤JR e^R. The β multiplying real filter time is essential and is retained.

For exact Bℓ and Wℓ this yields the independent aggregate bound

    ||Obar−Olocal||≤2C M₁e^{−(ℓ+1)}
        +C²w e^{βvT−(p+1)}+4C²e^{−2πT}.

The completed c_priorart construction additionally budgets finite arithmetic:

    η₀=ε/(64C), ℓ=ceil log(2M₁/η₀),
    T=log(256C²/ε)/(2π),
    p=ceil[βvT+log(64C²w/ε)].

These choices are sound. Araki truncation≤η₀/2; computed Bhat error≤η₀/2; ||Bhat||≤2C and ||Bhat Bhat†−BB†||≤3Cη₀<ε/16. The inner real-time discrepancy is≤4C²w e^{βvT−p−1}≤ε/(16e); the integrated outer discrepancy is≤16C²e^{−2πT}=ε/16. Add another certified ε/16 for local matrix arithmetic: total operator error≤ε/4. Enlarging L if necessary to include the exact K−AKA term only improves the padding bound.

K−AKA contains only interactions meeting A, so its support is inside the original interval padded by R and its norm≤2βq_AJ. Final support is at most q_A+2max(R,ℓ+p), clipped by actual chain boundaries. For short chains this window can be the entire chain; the algorithm bounds the size of its computed window, not a literal ban on ever diagonalizing all sites of a short system.

## Computation, measurements, and continuity

At fixed admitted β,J,R,q_A and onsite dimension, support grows O(log1/ε) with constants depending on those parameters. Thus local dimension D=d₀^support is polynomial in1/ε. Dense matrix construction and certified finite-precision matrix functions are polynomial in D and precision; spectral degeneracies require stable function evaluation, not an eigenvalue-gap assumption. Candidate terms and coefficients in these windows must be explicitly accessible and their precision charged. This is not an exact-real or global modular oracle. Temperature dependence is severe: log Mμ is exponential in βJR, and Mμ double exponential; the theorem is not polynomial in variable β.

The computed local Hermitian observable has norm at most M=2βq_AJ+1+4C²+ε. In qubits, its Pauli coefficient mass satisfies Σ|c_P|≤D||O||op≤DM, by Frobenius orthogonality and Cauchy–Schwarz. Sampling a string with probability proportional to |c_P| and measuring its local single-qubit Pauli factors gives an unbiased estimator bounded by DM. Hoeffding gives O(D²M²ε⁻²log1/δ) independent target copies. Finite stored coefficients can be treated as rational numbers; sampling/arithmetic bias must stay within the existing numerical budget. No candidate Gibbs preparation or candidate logZ is needed. Target copies, local basis measurements and processing of O(D²) coefficients are still real charged resources.

The exact finite-window coefficient Lipschitz bound also passes. If each Pauli coefficient changes by at most δ, with m_A,m_w,m_L counts in the three relevant windows and a straight path remaining inside the admitted J bound, Duhamel gives

    ||Bℓ(θ)−Bℓ(θ')||≤βe^{βJw}m_wδ,

because each half exponential has norm≤e^{βJw/2}. Then W changes by≤2Cβe^{βJw}m_wδ. Real-time conjugation changes by at most2β|t|C²m_Lδ, whose integral is (2βlog2/π)C²m_Lδ. The exact local difference contributes2βm_Aδ. Their sum is precisely c_priorart's L_window. A bounded per-site support-list count is needed for system-independent windows; duplicate or huge explicit encodings still need reading/aggregation costs. Numerical evaluation errors are separate from this exact-window continuity statement.

## Practical limit and disposition

The acquisition gate is mathematically constructive for each candidate and accuracy, under ideal charged local target measurements. It establishes neither sample-optimal fitting nor efficient optimization of the Hamiltonian objective. Known1D all-temperature learners and endpoint-score acquisition receive the same locality tools as competent comparators. Novelty or total research advantage does not follow from this local oracle.

The conservative constants are operationally enormous. An independent descriptive floating evaluation of the exact aggregate bound at J=R=q_A=1, ε=.01 gives support35 qubits for β=.1,97 for β=.5,393 for β=1, and6793 for β=2. These are loose bound illustrations, not lower bounds or interval-certified numerical conclusions. A practical claim needs sharper bounds and a matched implementation, rather than polynomial-in-accuracy notation alone.
