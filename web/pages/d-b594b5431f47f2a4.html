N01 — Cosmic inference, version 4 (v3 scope correction)
2026-10-09

This append-only note corrects the theorem scope in v3; v1-v3 are preserved. All action-matching claims below concern the linear scalar sector only. The expansion, scalar matter growth, and weak-lensing observables do not reconstruct vector or tensor effective stress, so the construction does not claim matching those sectors. Stress conservation and full scalar-component matching imply the longitudinal material-field equation; they do not establish the two transverse/vector equations or the tensor response.

Corrected theorem
=================
Fix constant M_Pl, separately conserved known matter, flat FRW, k != 0, and one first-derivative isotropic three-scalar action F(X,Y,Z) with phi^I=x^I on the background. Work at linear scalar order, below the EFT cutoff, in Poisson gauge. Let T^E=M_Pl^2G-T^known. For h_E=rho_E+p_E>0 and a smooth background, define

  S_rho(k,N)=delta rho_E/h_E - 3Phi,
  U_E=-theta_E/Hcal,
  (delta T_E^i{}_j)_TF=Pi_E Q^i_j,
  c_a^2=p_E,N/rho_E,N.

A necessary-and-sufficient condition for matching the reconstructed *linear scalar-sector stress* to one such solid is:

  rho_E,N=-3 h_E,
  S_rho,N=U_E,
  delta p_E=c_a^2 delta rho_E,
  Pi_E=2 mu(N) S_rho,

for every k and every independently identified scalar transfer solution, with one smooth mu(N) common to all modes, together with

  0 <= mu/h_E <= 1,
  0 <= c_a^2 + 4mu/(3h_E) <= 1.

For strict propagating gradient stability, require the sound-speed lower bounds to be strict; zero-speed endpoints are marginal limits. The derivative-only test Pi_N=a Pi+K U from v2 is a consequence when K=2mu>0, but is not sufficient: it contains no density/pressure closure and loses the initial displacement constant.

Sufficiency proof and explicit action
=====================================
Set X=3a^-2, so X_N=-2X, and define the smooth functions along the background by

  rho_0(X)=rho_E(N(X)),
  h_0(X)=h_E(N(X)),
  mu_0(X)=mu(N(X)),
  G(X)=9[h_0(X)-mu_0(X)]/4,
  F(X,Y,Z)=-rho_0(X)+G(X)(Y-1/3).

Separate conservation gives rho_0,X=3h_0/(2X)>0. Endlich et al. (arXiv:1210.0569v1, eqs. 2.16 and 3.3-3.4) give rho=-F, p=F-(2/3)XF_X, and

  c_T^2=1+(2/3)(F_Y+F_Z)/(F_X X),
  c_L^2=1+(2/3)F_XX X^2/(F_X X)
                       +(8/9)(F_Y+F_Z)/(F_X X).

The constructed F has F_X=-rho_0,X, F_Y=G and F_Z=0 on Y=1/3,Z=1/9. Therefore background rho,p match, the phonon kinetic coefficient is

  -F_X X/3 = h_0/2 > 0,

and the sound speeds are c_T^2=mu_0/h_0 and c_L^2=c_a^2+4mu_0/(3h_0). Because delta Y=delta Z=0 at first order around an isotropic B^{IJ}, F_0 supplies the background-determined density/pressure perturbations and G supplies the shear modulus. The scalar stress components then equal the target by the four closure equations above. Since both stresses are conserved, the longitudinal scalar EOM follows from the scalar-action identity nabla_mu T_F^{mu nu}=E_I partial^nu phi^I and the invertible background spatial matrix partial_i phi^I=delta_i^I.

No further mixed-derivative integrability condition exists along this one-dimensional path: the explicit smooth extension fixes F_XY=G_X, while delta Y begins at second order and does not enter the linear isotropic pressure response. This is sufficiency at background plus linear scalar-stress order only; it does not match vector/tensor transfer functions, nonlinear interactions, or UV completion.

Singular cases and nuisances
============================
- mu=0: use Pi_E=0 and the full stress equations; do not define a=K_N/K. The transverse speed is zero. An accelerating barotropic component with c_a^2<0 then has a longitudinal gradient instability, so a nonzero shear modulus is required in this class. At an isolated zero use Pi_N=2mu_N S+2mu U.
- h_E=0: conservation gives rho_E,N=0 and F_X=0, so the quadratic phonon kinetic coefficient vanishes. A nonzero mu is not a healthy finite-speed realization in this truncated action; causal bounds force mu->0 with h_E. A constant F cosmological constant is allowed but its three material scalars are nondynamical at quadratic order; the solid response variables are undefined.
- h_E<0: the phonon kinetic coefficient is negative. A single such solid is ghostly; a crossing passes through the degenerate h_E=0 point.
- S_rho=0: require Pi_E=0 for finite mu; never divide by S_rho.
- rho_E,N=0: for separately conserved T^E this is h_E=0, so c_a^2 by division is undefined.
- Unknown Omega_m0 changes rho_E,p_E,h_E,delta rho_E and U_E after ordinary matter is subtracted. The test must profile over externally allowed matter parameters; the v1 slip ratio’s Omega_m0 cancellation does not carry over.
- A single adiabatic primordial seed multiplies every transfer component, so the homogeneous closure equations cancel its common amplitude. Unknown mixtures of independent primordial modes do not identify individual transfer columns from auto/cross powers alone; each mode must be resolved or a covariance model supplied. Unknown galaxy bias can be canceled for the metric-slip reconstruction, but full scalar stress reconstruction also needs matter density/momentum subtraction and exact relativistic continuity.

Claim boundary
==============
This result gives an explicit local, minimally coupled action for every compatible positive-enthalpy background and shear profile, and an iff test for whether a fully reconstructed linear scalar stress belongs to that single-solid class. It is not a universal DE/MG discriminator: multi-field solids, entropy variables, higher derivatives, dissipation, vector/tensor observations, and other local DE actions lie outside the class. No real data or named MG target has been tested. The low-energy positive kinetic and sound-speed calculation is not a proof of strong-coupling control or UV completion.
