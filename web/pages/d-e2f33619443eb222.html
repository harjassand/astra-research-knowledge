# Exact rational repair: checked implementation and representation boundary

Status: a supplied finite experiment converter is executable and independently checked. This is an implementation and proof audit of an existing construction, not a foundational discovery, structure acquisition mechanism, or proof of the sharp controlled-menu coefficient. Cycle1 Rzk conservativity findings remain unchanged.

## Question and interface

Given m≥2 states, a finite menu F of rational full-support finite observation laws, with some action distinguishing every pair of states, and an explicitly supplied positive rational target table H, construct a finite observation schedule, classifier confusion matrix A and state-independent stochastic output kernel K satisfying AK=H exactly. Source observations are conditionally independent given the fixed state. Randomization is ideal unbiased independent bits. No state label is available to the converter at execution. H and F are supplied, not learned.

`work/r11_logic/cycle2/rational_repair.py` implements two schedules, exact Fraction inversion, an adaptive *compilation* search over doubling horizons, and a sampler. Its execution schedule is fixed length, not adaptive sensing. A failed resource cap is `BudgetExceeded`/UNKNOWN, not infeasibility. Floats are forbidden.

`check_certificate.py` independently reconstructs probabilities by a single-observation count-distribution recurrence, never importing the compiler. It checks the declared classifier, stochasticity and every AK entry. The certificate establishes behavior under the supplied laws; it cannot establish those laws are true of a physical source. Python fractions and this checker are trusted arithmetic code, not a proof-assistant kernel.

## Complete marker proof and effective bound

For every ordered pair (k,j), choose an action a and symbol x maximizing |Fₖᵃ(x)−Fⱼᵃ(x)|. Let pₖ,pⱼ denote the two probabilities and γ>0 the smallest selected difference. Take L independent observations for each ordered pair in its own block. A test favors k exactly when the observed frequency is strictly closer to pₖ across the midpoint: count > L(pₖ+pⱼ)/2 if pₖ>pⱼ; count < midpoint otherwise. Reject ties. Candidate k passes if all its m−1 tests pass. Output the first passing candidate; default to state 0 if all fail. Every block is sampled even if a decision could be made earlier, making N=m(m−1)L exact.

Hoeffding's inequality gives both errors for a (k,j) block at most q=exp(−Lγ²/2). Under true state i, candidate i fails with probability at most (m−1)q. Any wrong candidate k has its test against i and passes with probability at most q. Regardless of default or candidate ordering, classifier error ≤2(m−1)q. Consequently δ=||A−I||∞≤4(m−1)q.

Let C=maxᵢⱼᵧ Hⱼ(y)/Hᵢ(y), finite by positivity. If δ<1, Neumann inversion gives ||A⁻¹−I||∞≤δ/(1−δ). Thus, entry by entry,

Kᵢ(y) = Hᵢ(y)+Σⱼ(A⁻¹−I)ᵢⱼHⱼ(y) ≥ Hᵢ(y)[1−Cδ/(1−δ)].

If δ≤1/(C+1), K is nonnegative. A1=1 and H1=1 imply K1=A⁻¹H1=1. The execution law is ΣₖAᵢₖKₖ(y)=Hᵢ(y), exactly.

The existence bound can be computed without logarithms or floating-point comparisons. Choose the least integer M such that 2ᴹ≥4(m−1)(C+1), by integer doubling; put L=ceil(2M/γ²), by rational division. Since exp(−M)<2⁻ᴹ for M>0, δ<1/(C+1); hence this horizon suffices. `sufficient_marker_horizon` implements this. For the symmetric fixture γ=4/5, C=8, M=7, it gives L=22/N=132, with AK=H checked; compilation discovers a smaller L=4/N=24. A doubling search eventually reaches a sufficient horizon, so without imposed resource caps this algorithm terminates for every admitted input.

For unequal known action costs cₐ, execution cost is exactly LΣ₍ₖ,ⱼ₎cₐ₍ₖ,ⱼ₎. This is not a claim of optimal cost or Li–Liu's first-order coefficient.

## Exact confusion computation and bit costs

Each pair test has an exact binomial tail. Let bᵢᵏ be the product of its tails under state i. Dedicated independent blocks make Aᵢₖ=bᵢᵏΠₗ<ₖ(1−bᵢˡ), with Πₗ(1−bᵢˡ) additionally assigned to output 0. No joint pass/fail pattern enumeration is needed. The compiler uses integer binomial formulas; the checker uses repeated convolution, an independently reconstructed calculation.

Let D be the lcm of all source probability denominators, J the lcm of all target denominators. Every A entry has denominator dividing Dᴺ, so write A=M₀/Dᴺ. Hadamard or the determinant expansion bounds |det M₀| by m!Dᴺᵐ; the adjugate gives a representation of every K entry whose numerator and denominator have O(mN log D + log J + m log m) bits. Reduced fractions cannot be larger. Binomial tails and exact elimination have polynomial arithmetic and bit cost in the admitted table sizes, m, L and their bit lengths. This is polynomial in the numerical horizon, not necessarily in the bit length of the source: γ can be exponentially small in its rational encoding. The source conditioning dependence is load-bearing.

The ML option samples L observations per action and enumerates count vectors instead of raw transcripts. Its number of types is Πₐ binom(L+rₐ−1,rₐ−1). This may be exponential in the number of actions or alphabet dimensions; calling it generically polynomial would be wrong. Rational integer likelihood weights and uniform ties are used. Uniform ties reproduce the independent d_example classifier convention; the paper's selected tie convention need not match it.

`rational_choice` draws enough unbiased bits to cover a common denominator and rejects integers outside range, then uses integer cumulative weights. Acceptance exceeds 1/2, with expected fewer than two proposals. This exact-law argument assumes ideal randomness; using a deterministic seeded PRNG for execution does not turn the PRNG into such a source.

## Reuse and downstream use without exaggerated discovery claims

The rare-output experiment discriminates minimum mass from likelihood-ratio spread. A state-independent split of a target column can make minimum probabilities arbitrarily small while leaving C unchanged. If H=B W for a supplied state-independent stochastic postprocessing W and A⁻¹B is stochastic, then K=(A⁻¹B)W is stochastic and AK=H. All such postprocessings reuse the same classifier. This is ordinary Markov-kernel composition and the existing diameter logic, not a new theorem.

The implementation includes a product-bit postprocessing sampler: first simulate the base report, then append h independent supplied Bernoulli bits. This samples a report with |Y|2ʰ possible outputs using one base repair and O(h) extra rational draws; the target law is Bᵢ(y)Πₜpₜᵇᵗ(1−pₜ)¹⁻ᵇᵗ. No exponential target table need be extracted for this supplied factorization. An explicit three-bit fixture verifies this factor identity entrywise; a 64-bit execution smoke check uses three source calls and a 64-bit tuple but proves only program behavior, not a PRNG's ideal randomness. Arbitrary state-dependent branching reports need not have this factorization.

For future research, this primitive makes finite, supplied-law conversion claims auditable and exposes certificate size/cost before invoking them inside a larger theorem or experiment. It provides neither unknown-likelihood acquisition nor a reason to expect a fundamental research acceleration.

## Obstruction and next falsifiable step

The separation penalty is physically necessary in some cases, not just an inefficient marker choice. Independently reconstructing r05's conditioning obstruction: take two source states Bernoulli(1/2) and Bernoulli(1/2+ε), ε=2⁻ᴮ≤1/4, and desired target Bernoulli(1/4) versus Bernoulli(3/4). For any exact converter with an integrable stopping time T and independent state-free randomization, stopped-history KL is E₀T·D(F₀||F₁), because the bounded log-likelihood increments allow interchange of expectation and the stopped sum, and each continuation decision is made before the next sample. Data processing to the terminal bit implies this is at least D(H₀||H₁)=(log 3)/2. Here

D(F₀||F₁)=−(1/2)log(1−4ε²)≤(8/3)ε²,

using −log(1−x)≤x/(1−x) and x=4ε²≤1/4. Therefore E₀T≥(3 log 3/16)ε⁻²=(3 log 3/16)4ᴮ. This applies even to implicit exact samplers and adaptive stopping in this single-action family. It does not say all instances with B-bit probabilities require exponential work. The stopped-likelihood/data-processing prerequisites were checked directly in Li–Liu's TeX proof, lines 1178–1208 and 1320–1344. This is a standard information lower-bound specialization, not a new discovery.

The interface takes an explicit H or a supplied factorization. Extracting arbitrary exact terminal probabilities from a succinct branching policy is a separate counting task; r05 gives a #P-hardness construction, and also a direct-sampling bypass demonstrating why that does not prove every implicit converter hard. Unbounded stopping probabilities can be noncomputable without stronger assumptions. Those are representation boundaries, not impossibility claims for every sampling implementation.

The next discriminating test is a bounded state-dependent branching-policy family with an explicitly supplied *compressed* residual representation that remains closed under correction. Compare exact certificate/compilation/observation costs against direct simulation and a competent single-step garbling control. A closed polynomial-size representation or a proved obstruction is the actual missing contribution; table-based repairs and common state-independent postprocessing do not supply it.
