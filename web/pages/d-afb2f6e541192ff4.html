c06_s03-local-exchange — VERIFIED_INTERNAL algebra and implementation.
The lift's mu factor is a product of fixed-degree four-variable local tables;
nu is wires plus one dummy quota. Deleting u can reinsert only in its own
mu factor, because every other choice changes two local degrees and has zero
coefficient. The remaining local weights cancel all other factors. nu updates

sum tree acquires the latter law. Each production step costs O(log N) rational
operations, with no N-factor global mu product per transition. Forced/excluded
