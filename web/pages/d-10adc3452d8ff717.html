The existing cyclic harmonic function is

    U_k(x)=cos(kx_1)cos(kx_2)cosh(sqrt(2)kx_3)
          +cos(kx_2)cos(kx_3)cosh(sqrt(2)kx_1)

Set m>=1, k=4m, D=64k. The supplied rational polynomial P_m is its degree-D Taylor truncation; the independent grid gate is `notes/harmonic_grid_audit/audit.md`. At the old 8m^3 centers, p_i=(j_i+1/2)pi/k, j_i=-m,...,m-1, every cosine vanishes. The earlier frozen-Hessian/third-derivative proof gave only exponential witness radii.

Retain the centers satisfying all three triangle inequalities

    |p_i| <= |p_j|+|p_l|,    {i,j,l}={1,2,3}.                  (T1)

There are exactly 4m^3+4m such centers. The claim proved below is: if R is C^2 on each specified chart and

    ||grad_x R||_2 <= k/20000,
    ||D_x^2 R||_op <= k^2/1000,                              (T2)

then U_k+R has exactly one critical point in each ball B(p,1/(200k)). It is nondegenerate, has index two, and its Hessian at that point has smallest singular value at least k^2/16. This is not a Hessian estimate throughout the whole witness ball, nor a frozen-Hessian contraction there.

The proposed equal-unit composition keeps n_m=720000(D+1)^2 sources but uses

    rho=7/16,
    A=2^(-7k)/[3072(2D+1)(D+1)^5],

It yields at least 4m^3+4m index-two equilibria with physical witness radius 7/(3200k), rather than exp(-7k)/(40000k) in cycle12. ## 1. Exact triangle count and charts

Each positive absolute coordinate corresponds to an index a in {0,...,m-1}, with magnitude (a+1/2)pi/k. A triangle inequality fails precisely when its largest index a exceeds the sum of the two others b+c. The largest coordinate is then unique. For a>=1, the number of ordered (b,c) with b+c<=a-1 is a(a+1)/2. Thus the count of invalid positive triples is

    3 sum_(a=1)^(m-1) a(a+1)/2 = (m^3-m)/2.

Valid positive triples number (m^3+m)/2. Each has eight independent sign choices, proving the exact count in (T1).

Put delta=1/100 and s_i=sin(kp_i) in {+1,-1}. On the cube |c_i|<=delta set

    x_i(c_i)=p_i-s_i*arcsin(c_i)/k,
    A_i(c_i)=cosh(sqrt(2)k*x_i(c_i)).                        (T4)

Then c_i=cos(kx_i), and the function in these coordinates is exactly

    F(c)=A_3(c_3)c_1c_2+A_1(c_1)c_2c_3+A_2(c_2)c_3c_1.      (T5)

Every A_i>=1. Direct differentiation gives on the closed chart

    |A_i'/A_i| <=sqrt(2)/sqrt(1-delta^2)<2,
    |A_i''/A_i| <=2/(1-delta^2)
              +sqrt(2)*delta/(1-delta^2)^(3/2)<4,
    |x_i'| <=2/k,  |x_i''| <=2delta/k.                      (T6)

For nonnegative u,v, cosh(u)cosh(v)=[cosh(u+v)+cosh(u-v)]/2 >=cosh(u+v)/2. At a selected center, (T1) therefore implies A_i(0)/(A_j(0)A_l(0))<=2. The logarithmic derivative bound in (T6) transports this to the entire chart:

    A_i(c_i)/(A_j(c_j)A_l(c_l)) <=2*exp(6delta)<3.           (T7)

The charts lie in B_(3/2): center norm <sqrt(3)pi/4<11/8, and chart displacement norm is at most 2sqrt(3)delta/k<1/8 for k>=4. The physical observation ball under (T3) is B_(21/32), and chart balls are mutually disjoint since center separation is pi/k.

## 2. Small-gradient elimination without exponential conditioning loss

Fix an arbitrary point in a chart. Relabel the coordinates so A_1 is the largest of A_1,A_2,A_3 at that point. Write A=A_1, B=A_2, C=A_3, t=c_1, b=c_2, z=c_3, and a=A_1'/A. The following are pointwise algebraic equalities; no change of maximizer is differentiated. From (T5),

    g_1=C b+B z+A_1' b z,
    g_2=C t+(A+B' t)z,
    g_3=B t+(A+C' t)b.                                     (T8)

Suppose |g_i|<=epsilon with 0<=epsilon<=delta. Set u=1+B't/A and v=1+C't/A. Since B,C<=A,

    1-2delta <=u,v<=1+2delta,
    z=(g_2-Ct)/(A u),    b=(g_3-Bt)/(A v).                 (T9)

With b_0=-Bt/(Av) and z_0=-Ct/(Au), the corresponding value of g_1 is

    g_10=-(BC/A)*t*(u+v-a t)/(u v).

By (T7), BC/A>=1/3; by (T6) and (T9), the last fraction is positive and at least (2-6delta)/(1+2delta)^2. Hence

    |g_10| >= |t|/2.                                      (T10)

The differences b-b_0 and z-z_0 have magnitude at most epsilon/[A(1-2delta)]. The two linear terms and the three product errors in g_1 consequently obey

    |g_1-g_10| <=2epsilon/(1-2delta)
                +2(epsilon^2+2delta*epsilon)/(1-2delta)^2
              <3epsilon.                                 (T11)

The inequality is also valid as <= at epsilon=0, which directly forces t=0. For epsilon>0, (T10)--(T11) give |t|<=8epsilon and then (T9) gives

    |b|,|z| <=9epsilon/(1-2delta)<=10epsilon,
    ||c||_infty <=10epsilon.                               (T12)

This is the load-bearing localization statement. Large individual A_i values and large ratios A_1/A_2 do not enter it. A global third-derivative bound would reintroduce those losses.

## 3. Balanced Hessian only at small-gradient points

Define the positive diagonal matrix D by

    D_i^2=A_i/(A_jA_l),    D_i D_j=1/A_l,
    max_i D_i^2<=3,
    K=[0 1 1; 1 0 1; 1 1 0].                              (T13)

K has eigenvalues 2,-1,-1. The diagonal Hessian entries of F are A_i'' c_j c_l. The off-diagonal entries are, for example, H_12=C+(A_1'+B')z. At a point satisfying (T8) with |g|_infty<=epsilon, (T9) and |t|<=8epsilon imply

    |(D H_F D)_12-1|,
    |(D H_F D)_13-1| <=36epsilon/(1-2delta)<40epsilon,
    |(D H_F D)_23-1| <=32epsilon<40epsilon.                 (T14)

For the first diagonal entry, |A b/B| and |A z/C| are at most 9epsilon/(1-2delta)<10epsilon, hence |(D H_F D)_11|<=400epsilon^2. For the second and third entries, (T6), B,C<=A and (T9) give at most 288epsilon^2/(1-2delta)<400epsilon^2. Thus

    ||D H_F D-K||_op <=80epsilon+400epsilon^2.              (T15)

This estimate is not asserted at every point of the chart. In particular, arbitrary c_2,c_3 may make D_1^2 H_11 large when A_1 is huge. Pointwise small-gradient localization is what permits (T14)--(T15).

## 4. C^2 persistence by degree and inertia

Let R_c(c)=R(x(c)). From (T2) and (T6),

    ||grad_c R_c||_infty <=2(k/20000)/k=1/10000=:epsilon_0,

        <=12*(k^2/1000)/k^2+6delta*(k/20000)/k
         =12003/1000000 <1/32.                             (T16)

The Hessian transformation used here is

    H_(R_c)=X' H_xR X'+diag(x_i'' partial_i R).

Consider the homotopy F+tau R_c, 0<=tau<=1. Any of its critical points has |grad F|_infty<=epsilon_0, so (T12) places it in |c_i|<=delta/10. Thus no homotopy gradient vanishes on the boundary of the chart cube. For F alone, (T12) with epsilon=0 proves that its only critical point is c=0, whose Hessian determinant is 2A_1(0)A_2(0)A_3(0)>0. Its local degree is +1; hence the total gradient degree is +1 throughout the homotopy.

At every zero of F+tau R_c, (T15) gives 80epsilon_0+400epsilon_0^2=2001/250000<1/32. Together with (T16), the balanced Hessian is within 1/16 of K. Consequently it is invertible with index two and smallest singular value >3/4. Congruence preserves index, and max D_i^2<=3 implies

Each zero is isolated; their set is compact inside the cube, so it is finite. Every local gradient degree is +1 because the Hessian has two negative eigenvalues. ## 5. A precise obstruction to extending the same budget to all centers

The triangle restriction has a mathematical purpose. Take p_1=(m-1/2)pi/k and p_2=p_3=pi/(2k). At its center write A_0=cosh(sqrt(2)pi(m-1/2)), B_0=C_0=cosh(pi/sqrt(2)). For m>=8, A_1 remains the largest throughout the chart. The same elimination with g_2=g_3=0 gives, throughout any possible zero,

    |g_1| <= (B_0 C_0/A_0)*exp(6delta)
             *delta*(2+6delta)/(1-2delta)^2
           <3delta*(B_0 C_0/A_0).                         (T19)

Choose the harmonic linear perturbation R(x)=-beta(x_1-p_1), with beta=4delta*k*(B_0 C_0/A_0). Its c-gradient first component has magnitude beta/[k sqrt(1-c_1^2)]>=4delta*(B_0 C_0/A_0), incompatible with (T19). There is no critical point anywhere in this chart. Yet ||grad_xR||=beta and H_xR=0. The elementary bounds B_0,C_0<e^3<27, A_0>=2^(3m-4), and m>=8 give beta<k/20000. This is a harmonic perturbation satisfying (T2) that destroys the witness at an unbalanced center. It rules out extending this unrestricted C^2-budget persistence statement to all 8m^3 centers. It does not rule out a compiler exploiting extra structure of its errors or a different grid.
