COLD DERIVATION — MIXED COFACTOR TOGGLES
=======================================
Scope: toggles A+nu_j.C <-> nu_j.C with phi_j(c)=prod_k(c_k)_(nu_jk),
independent C_i immigration/death, Q=sum_j zeta_j phi_j,
E=sum_j eta_j phi_j, u=1/(1+Q). This report is a separate leaf result;
no source theorem was edited.

Conclusion
----------
The pure-cofactor argument H(c)->0 does not extend to mixed monomials.
A death at a bounded threshold coordinate can annihilate every growing
toggle term, leaving a nonzero face-limit penalty in H. There are exact
rational examples where H->1/2 while both theta_* and kappa_* stay
positive, and examples where H->2 and both margins tend to -1. Thus a
mixed theorem must retain the face-kill penalties in its margins; no
parameter-free uniform H tail is available.

I derive the exact limit on each fixed escaping face and give a separate
sound finite rational certificate under the additional conservative
condition B<min(1,rho). This certificate is not necessary and is not a
complete decision procedure for arbitrary mixed rates; it is a finite
sufficient certificate. The face formula identifies what a sharper exact
certificate would need to check.

1. Exact generator margins
--------------------------
Set Q_i^-(c)=Q(c-e_i), Q_i^+(c)=Q(c+e_i),
Delta_i=Q-Q_i^-, Delta_i^+=Q_i^+-Q. Then

  u(c-e_i)-u(c)=Delta_i/[(1+Q_i^-)(1+Q)],
  u(c+e_i)-u(c)=-Delta_i^+/[(1+Q)(1+Q_i^+)].

Consequently

  H(c)=sum_i mu_i c_i [u(c-e_i)-u(c)],
  J(c)=sum_i gamma_i [u(c)-u(c+e_i)],
  theta(c)=(alpha+Q)/(1+Q)+J(c)-H(c),
  kappa_A(c)=(delta+E)/(1+Q)-H(c).

The correction eps R^b 1{a=0}u(c) has generator exactly
-eps R^b theta(c) on a=0. The A u(c) term has generator

  (alpha+Q)u + a[-(delta+E)u+H-J]
  <= alpha+1-kappa_A(c)a,

since (alpha+Q)u<=alpha+1 and J>=0. On a>=1, the inactive correction
can rise only when a=1 and A dies or a forward toggle fires; the rate of
those events times the jump is eps R^b u(delta+E). Since
E<=rho_max Q with rho_max=max_j eta_j/zeta_j, this is at most

eps R^b (delta+rho_max).

These identities transfer unchanged to mixed multi-indices. The issue is
tail behavior of H, not the generator algebra.

2. Exact escaping-face formula
------------------------------
Take a sequence for which coordinates in a nonempty set U tend to infinity
and all remaining coordinates are fixed at t_k. Define

  G(U,t)={j: prod_{k notin U}(t_k)_(nu_jk)>0
             and sum_{k in U} nu_jk>0}.

If G(U,t) is nonempty, Q(c)->infinity. For a bounded coordinate i notin U
with t_i>0, define

  G_i^-(U,t)={j in G(U,t):
       prod_{k notin U}(t_k-1{k=i})_(nu_jk)>0}.

If G_i^- is nonempty, Q(c-e_i)->infinity and that death term in H tends
to zero. If G_i^- is empty, the decrement kills every growing cofactor;
put

  q_i^0(U,t)=sum_{j: sum_{k in U}nu_jk=0}
       zeta_j prod_{k notin U}(t_k-1{k=i})_(nu_jk).

Then the i-th death term tends exactly mu_i t_i/(1+q_i^0). For i in U,
the death term tends zero. Also J(c)->0 because each summand is at most
gamma_i/(1+Q). Therefore the theta limit on this face is exactly

  theta_infty(U,t)=1-
       sum_{i notin U: t_i>0, G_i^-(U,t)=empty}
                 mu_i t_i/(1+q_i^0(U,t)).                    (F-theta)

For kappa, any subsequential limit of E/Q is a weighted average of
eta_j/zeta_j over the growing terms; hence with
rho(U,t)=min_{j in G(U,t)} eta_j/zeta_j,

  liminf kappa_A >= rho(U,t)-
       sum_{i notin U: t_i>0, G_i^-(U,t)=empty}
                 mu_i t_i/(1+q_i^0(U,t)).                    (F-kappa)

The lower bound is exact as a uniform sufficient face test; the exact
E/Q tier may be larger than rho(U,t) if a low-ratio monomial cannot
asymptotically dominate. This is precisely where a sharper tier/Newton
face refinement could improve the test.

This shows why mixed cofactor faces differ from pure (c_i)_d: at
c_i=nu_ji, death of C_i can set that whole toggle monomial to zero even
while its other cofactor counts diverge. Then q_i^0 can be zero and the
death contributes the nonzero limit mu_i nu_ji.

3. Explicit rational examples
------------------------------
Use r=2 and one mixed toggle with nu=(1,1), so phi(c)=c_1 c_2 and
Q=E=c_1c_2 when eta=zeta=1. Take alpha=delta=gamma_1=gamma_2=1.

(a) H fails to vanish even when both margins stay positive. Let
mu_1=1/2, mu_2=1/10. For c_1,c_2>=1, Q=c_1c_2 and

  H_1=(1/2) Q/[(1+(c_1-1)c_2)(1+Q)] <=1/2,
  H_2=(1/10)Q/[(1+c_1(c_2-1))(1+Q)] <=1/10.

If either coordinate is zero, H=0. Since alpha=delta=1 and E=Q,

  theta=1+J-H >= 2/5,
  kappa_A=1-H >=2/5.

But at c=(1,n),

  H_1=(1/2)n/(n+1),  H_2=(1/10)/(n+1),
  H(c)->1/2.

So no assertion H(c)->0 is valid even throughout a region where both
margins are uniformly positive.

(b) A rational face obstruction when the margins are not imposed. Keep
the same toggle, alpha=delta=gamma_i=eta=zeta=1, but take mu_1=2,
mu_2=1. At c=(1,n),

  H(c)=2n/(n+1)+1/(n+1)->2,
  J(c)=n/[(n+1)(2n+1)]+1/[(n+1)(n+2)]->0,
  theta(c)->-1,  kappa_A(c)->-1.

All rates are positive rationals. Along a=0,c=(1,n),b=n the correction
drift is -eps R^n theta(c), hence is positive of order R^n, while all
remaining terms are at most linear in n or bounded. The proposed V cannot
have negative Foster drift there. This is an explicit obstruction to
transferring a pure-cofactor uniform-tail argument without checking mixed
face margins.

4. A sound finite rational certificate (conservative)
------------------------------------------------------
Let s be the number of toggles, m_i=max_j nu_ji (0 if no toggle uses C_i),

  B=sum_i mu_i m_i,
  rho=min_j eta_j/zeta_j,
  C_H=sum_i mu_i m_i(m_i+1)^2.

If there are no toggles, Q=E=H=0 and the margins reduce to theta=alpha,
kappa_A=delta; handle that case directly. Otherwise rho is well-defined.
For c_i>m_i, every active monomial survives its i-death with ratio
(c_i-nu_ji)/c_i >=1/(m_i+1). Moreover, for an active monomial,
Delta_i^j=nu_ji*phi_j(c)/c_i, so

  c_i Delta_i <= m_i(m_i+1) Q.

Thus H_i<=mu_i m_i(m_i+1)^2/Q. For c_i<=m_i,
H_i<=mu_i c_i/(1+Q_i^-)<=mu_i m_i. Therefore for Q>0,

  H(c)<=B+C_H/Q.                                             (H-tail)

Also E>=rho Q. For Q>=Q0>=1, this yields

  theta(c)>=1-B-[(1-alpha)^+ + C_H]/Q0,
  kappa_A(c)>=rho-B-[(rho-delta)^+ + C_H]/Q0.

If B<min(1,rho), choose rational Q0 strictly above the two resulting
thresholds. Both margins then have explicit positive tail slack.

For Q<Q0, all active monomials are bounded. For each i set

  N_i=max( {0} union
      { n: nu_ji>0 for some j and zeta_j(n)_(nu_ji)<Q0 }).

If c_i>N_i, no monomial involving i is active; lowering c_i to zero
preserves Q,E,H (inactive monomials cannot become active under deaths).
Thus project each such coordinate to zero and enumerate at most
prod_i(N_i+1) representatives. Check exactly, using rational arithmetic,

  (alpha+Q)/(1+Q)-H > 0,       (delta+E)/(1+Q)-H > 0.

The first is a lower bound on theta because J>=0. Passing the finite box
and tail conditions certifies theta_*,kappa_*>0 for the mixed family.
This is sufficient, not necessary: B can exceed min(1,rho) while the
face-kill denominators q_i^0 still make every face margin positive. The
exact face formulas (F-theta/F-kappa) are the route to a less conservative
certificate.

Cost: computing m_i,B,C_H and rho is O(sr) rational operations. Q0 can be
found by exact integer search. Each N_i is found by monotone search on a
falling factorial. The representative enumeration is at most
prod_i(N_i+1) states; each state costs O(sr) rational arithmetic for Q,E,H.
Bit lengths grow with the input rates, exponents, and cutoff. This is finite
but generally exponential in r and has no polynomial-time claim. A sharper
face-tier check has at most 2^r structural escape subsets, but must also
handle the exposed/tied monomial tiers for E/Q; no complete complexity
bound for that refinement is claimed here.

5. Gate
-------
The pure-cofactor tail H->0 is falsified. For mixed cofactors, any theorem
must either assume a uniform face margin such as (F-theta/F-kappa), or use
a finite certificate that charges threshold-coordinate deaths. The
conservative rational certificate above is sound under B<min(1,rho), but
is not a complete decision procedure for all positive mixed rate vectors.
No claim beyond this family or deterministic-to-jump implication is made.
