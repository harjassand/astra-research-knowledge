2. Exact reduction of the source channel cube

The source channel lives on H0=C10, H1=C10, Hstar=C. It discards off-diagonal
input blocks, moves H0->H1 through c1 Phi1, H1->H0 through c2 Phi2, and sends
missing trace to the absorbing one-dimensional flag. Write
Ai=Phi_i*(I), ci=1/(1+Tr Ai), Fi=I-ci Ai.
Since Ai>=0 and Ai <= (Tr Ai)I, Fi >= ci I>0. Hence the missing-trace branches
are CP rank-one-output maps; the flag is fixed. This proves CP and TP. Output
transpose replaces each active map by its co-CP map and fixes the flag, proving
PPT. The exact source effects have Tr A1=Tr A2=1485979, so
c1=c2=1/1485980.

  H1 -> H0: c2^2 c1 Phi2 Phi1 Phi2 = c2^2 c1 B S B Ad_E*.

The target was a PPT TP channel with non-EB cube, or unbounded EB index.

active Choi matrices have full rank 60; exact modular rank certificates and
