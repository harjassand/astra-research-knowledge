Strongest completed results

1. Exact benchmark. For every d>=2 the universal 1-to-2 qudit cloner
  B_d(X)=2/(d+1) P_+(X tensor I) P_+,
  P_+=(I+F)/2,
has depolarizing marginals
  Phi_d(X)=p_d X+(1-p_d)Tr(X)I/d,
  p_d=(d+2)/(2(d+1)).
The optimal EB Dirichlet comparison constant for this channel is exactly 2:
  I-Psi_d <= 2(I-Phi_d),
where Psi_d is Haar measure-and-prepare at the depolarizing EB threshold
1/(d+1). The matching lower bound follows by U(d) twirling any EB
comparator and the separable-Choi overlap bound with |Omega>. Its normalized
Choi state has exact minimum EB trace distance (d-1)/(2d). That is an
entangled-input channel certificate, not a lower bound for the campaign's
fixed-sandwich reconstruction problem. Full derivation and source context
are in immutable INITIAL.txt.

2. Exact instrument fixture. The qutrit cyclic-shift instrument and its
Petz-corrected marginal give a positive selfadjoint selfcompatible channel.
The full instrument class has c=2 by the noncommutative SOS in
work/cycle4/detailed_balance_broadcast_attack.txt. In this fixture, the
canonical depolarizing comparator is sharper with c=4/3, tight on the
Hermitian shift mode. Exact rational checks cover d=2,3,4,5 matrix units and
the qutrit SOS in qudit_channel_certificates.py / exact_checks.json.

Post-release results

3. Power-range cloner sharpening. c05_s02 independently derived the exact
finite-d EB distance formula for Phi_d^m; I attribute that result to them.
I sharpened the dimension-uniform supremum to exactly 2^(-m), approached
along even d tending to infinity, and recorded the non-EB growing-dimension
sequence. Thus this candidate does not refute CP-range lifting. Proof and
exact rational diagnostic are in revisions/CP_RANGE_SHARPENING.txt and
revisions/power_range_cloner.py.

4. Conditional all-powers lift. If Phi and an EB map Psi are positive
selfadjoint L2 contractions with spectrum in [0,1] and
I-Psi<=c(I-Phi), then E_m=Psi o Phi^m is EB and
  ||E_m-Phi^m||_(Hermitian infinity-to-normalized-one)
    <= sqrt(c) (2m)^m/(2m+1)^(m+1/2), m>=1.
This is dimension-free, has no spectral-gap dependence, and decays as
sqrt(c/(2em)). It applies to the exact instrument subclass already supplied
with c=2. The proof is in revisions/DIRICHLET_POWER_LIFT.txt, and rational
values of the scalar bound are generated by dirichlet_power_bound.py. This
does not produce a universal EB comparator for arbitrary compatible Phi.

5. Rate sharpness for the prescribed lift. An exact qubit family with
I-Psi<=2(I-Phi) has
  ||Phi^m-Psi o Phi^m||_(Hermitian infinity-to-normalized-one)
    =(1-delta)^m sin(alpha),
and a Pythagorean sequence gives sqrt(m) times this error tending to
1/sqrt(2e). Thus the square-root rate cannot be improved for the
post-composition construction using only the stated c=2 certificate.
The example's Phi is itself EB, so its optimal EB approximation error is
zero; this is not a lower bound against choosing another EB map. Commuting
Phi,Psi instead give an O(1/m) bound. Proof and exact Fraction checks are in
revisions/POSTCOMPOSITION_RATE_SHARPNESS.txt and
revisions/postcomposition_rate_sharpness.py.
