# FJ contact-compensation audit

## Finding

The main harmonic-analysis estimate is internally consistent, and I found no fatal algebraic counterexample to the incoming argument. The strongest result it supports is a compatible-gradient cubic estimate at each fixed acoustic contact. It yields strict Jensen positivity for backgrounds $tA+uR$ with arbitrary $t,u$ when the gradient is relatively close to the contact line in both $L^2$ and $L^4$.

That is a real, useful local escaping-background theorem. It does not close N803's open gates. The full compatible positive-Hessian Jensen inequality, control of general correlated residuals after the nonlinear shear, and full strong-$L^4$ continuity of the optimized Schur loss remain unresolved.

## What the proof establishes

For every compatible mean-zero gradient $X$ and fixed non-polyconvex rank-one-convex quartic $F$ in the full nine-coefficient left-$SO(2)$ cone, the proof establishes
$$
|\mathbb E DF(X)[A]|
\le K\{\mathbb E D^2F(X)[A,A]\}^{1/2}\|X\|_4\|X-(X:R)R\|_4,
$$
and
$$
|\mathbb E D^2F(X)[A,R]|
\le K\{\mathbb E D^2F(X)[A,A]\}^{1/2}\|X-(X:R)R\|_2.
$$
The constant depends on $F,A,R$. Determinant balance first makes the averaged Hessian direction nonnegative. The contact classification then leaves Type I, where the cubic is algebraically transverse to $R$, and Type II, where a single $a^3$ term survives on the kernel plane. For Type II the curl-correcting Hodge projection converts that term into a mixed second derivative. Lemma 3.1 gives the required $L^{8/3}$ estimate by interpolation between $L^2$ and $L^4$ endpoints.

I checked the interpolation exponents and endpoint multipliers. At the midpoint the reciprocal exponent is $3/8$, and two Hilbert transforms recover the mixed derivative. The constant has no frequency-bandwidth or frequency-aspect term. The incoming note's sentence about extending to every flat periodic lattice via large-box cutoffs is abbreviated; a precise write-up should name the standard transference step. This is a proof-presentation issue, not an observed failure of the estimate on the stated periodic or Euclidean settings.

The contact-plane Jensen result uses both relative norms:
$$
\|Y\|_2\le\delta\|s\|_2,\qquad
\|Y\|_4\le\delta\|s\|_4.
$$
Under these conditions, for every $t,u\in\mathbb R$,
$$
\mathbb E[F(tA+uR+X)-F(tA+uR)]
\ge\eta(\|s\|_4^4+u^2\|s\|_2^2)>0
$$
unless $s=0$. The coefficients may escape to infinity; the closeness condition on $X$ stays essential.

## Adversarial compatible fields

The supplied piecewise profile proves the $L^4$ transverse factor is sharp, but the original base and profile are not smooth. I upgraded the counterexample to smooth compatible fields. Smooth compactly supported $W^{1,4}$ approximants of the seed retain positive $\beta_\psi$ and $h_\psi$ by continuity. A smooth periodic base derivative can be chosen to vanish on an open rectangle; its gradient is always on the contact line, where both directional moments vanish pointwise. Inserting $\rho^{1/2}\psi((x-x_*)/\rho)$ into that rectangle gives smooth periodic gradients with
$$
\frac{\beta_\rho^2}{2h_\rho}=\frac{\beta_\psi^2}{2h_\psi}>0,\qquad
\|Y_\rho\|_p=\rho^{2/p-1/2}\|Y_\psi\|_p\to0\quad(p<4),
$$
and uniformly bounded $L^4$ norms. So every replacement of the transverse $L^4$ norm by $L^p$, $p<4$, fails even on smooth compatible fields. The sequence has growing pointwise gradients and does not furnish negative Jensen gaps.

A separate exact smooth three-frequency compatible field has nonzero cubic mean $85/18$. It directly defeats any argument that assumes compatibility cancels cubic resonances; the estimate must use the Hodge/interpolation mechanism.
