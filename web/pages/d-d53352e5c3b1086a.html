# A rational, seven-critical-point unit-charge fixture

2026-10-07. **Exact rational outward verifier PASS; independent audit pending.** This is a finite implementation check of critical-pattern transfer, not a large-N equilibrium advantage: it uses 192 sources to certify seven critical points.

The target harmonic polynomial is

    P=x^4+y^4+z^4-3(x^2y^2+y^2z^2+z^2x^2)+x^2+y^2-2z^2.

Its seven explicit critical centers are the origin, (0,0,+/-1), and (+/-1,+/-1,0). Their Hessian spectra are respectively {2,2,-4}, {-4,-4,8}, and {20,-4,-16}. All have minimum absolute Hessian eigenvalue at least 2. The origin has Morse index 1; the other six have index 2.

`build_quartic_fixture.py` proposes rational unit directions by rounding an icosahedron through stereographic charts and taking sixteen rational quaternion rotations. It uses a 200-digit floating radial inverse at epsilon=2^-40 and rho=2^-96, then rounds all radii to dyadics of denominator 2^520. No floating claim is relied upon by the verifier. Generation took 1.52 seconds in the recorded run.

`verify_quartic_fixture.py` uses Python's standard library and exact Fraction arithmetic. It directly verifies unit directions, source distinctness and radius bounds. It checks harmonicity, dimension and all 576 exact spherical inner products of the complete degree-1-through-4 basis; it reconstructs P exactly. For every multipole moment it rounds each exact rational summand outwards to 560 binary places, including the actual saved radii. These intervals bound every coefficient error through degree four. The infinite Coulomb tail is controlled on B3 by the Legendre series and then in C2 on B2 by the unit-ball Poisson derivative formula. There is no replacement of the physical potential by a finite Taylor approximation without its tail.

A third-derivative bound 1296 for P, witness radius 10^-5, inverse-Hessian norm at most 1/2, and the certified C2 error give a contraction below 2^-6 in each disjoint witness ball. Center displacement is below 2^-43. Hence the full physical equal-charge potential has at least seven nondegenerate critical points at rho times those witness balls. The interval calculation took 0.13 seconds, with maximum coordinate numerator/denominator bit lengths 639/640.
