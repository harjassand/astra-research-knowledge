STRONGER GATE: REMOVE THE MUTUAL-INFORMATION BUDGET
2026-10-09. foundational_transfer_sol. Separate from the frozen baseline.

EXACT AMBITIOUS TARGET -- STATUS UNKNOWN

Does there exist a dimension-free h:[0,infinity)->[0,1] with h(t)->0
as t->0 such that every finite-dimensional tau_ABC satisfies
 inf_{sigma separable across A:(BC)} T(tau,sigma)
 <=h(I(A:C|B)_tau+I(A:B|C)_tau)?                    (G)
No I(A:B), entropy, rank, dimension or spectrum budget is imposed.
The target is trace distance to Sep(A:BC), not proximity to a common
locally readable PVM-label state. The latter would be a strictly stronger
approximate structural target and is not established by this work.

The supplied local theorem and the baseline prove (G) only on classes
with bounded I(A:B), through an actual two-recovery broadcaster. They
do NOT establish (G) without that bound. No high-I family with BOTH CMIs
tending to zero and persistent separable distance was found here. Thus
(G) is neither proved nor refuted in this report. The known broadcasting
ladders cannot be substituted without checking the two entropic losses.

1. A VALID I-FREE BOUNDARY RESULT: ONE CMI EXACTLY ZERO

For finite-dimensional tau_ABC with I(A:C|B)=0, there is a separable
sigma_A:BC with sigma_A=tau_A and sigma_BC=tau_BC satisfying
 D(tau||sigma)<=I(A:B|C),
 T(tau,sigma)<=sqrt[I(A:B|C)/2].                     (1)
Information is in nats. No I cap is needed. This is a direct consequence
of the standard HJPW equality structure, not a claimed new exact endpoint.

PROOF

HJPW Theorem 6 (quant-ph/0304007v2, 22 Aug 2003) gives
 B=direct_sum_j B_L,j tensor B_R,j,
 tau=direct_sum_j p_j tau_A B_L,j tensor theta_B_R,j C.
Define
 sigma=direct_sum_j p_j tau_A,j tensor tau_B_L,j
                       tensor theta_B_R,j C.        (2)
It is separable across A:(BC) and preserves both marginals exactly.
Direct-sum relative entropy and the conditional product expression give
 D(tau||sigma)=sum_j p_j I(A:B_L,j)_{tau_j}.          (3)
The classical label J is physically encoded orthogonally in B. Applying
the chain rule, then evaluating the conditional product blocks, gives
 I(A:B|C)=I(A:J|C)+sum_j p_j I(A:B_L,j)_{tau_j}.
The first term is nonnegative, so (3) proves the relative-entropy bound.
Quantum Pinsker in nats, D>=2T^2, proves (1).

There is also an actual EB pipeline producing (2) from tau_AB: measure
the block projectors P_j on B, and prepare
 tau_B_L,j tensor theta_B_R,j C.
Its marginal input tau_B gives exactly tau_BC. This channel may be
selected from the tested state's equality decomposition; no stronger
fixed-kappa simultaneous selector is inferred by this boundary argument.
The opposite exact-CMI direction is obtained by swapping B,C.

Why it does not solve (G): small but nonzero first CMI supplies recovery
closeness, not trace proximity to a state with the HJPW decomposition.
Substituting an exact Markov decomposition into such a state is a false
step in general. The new local theorem circumvents it with the information
budget; the I-free approximate-intersection step remains missing.

2. BALANCED ANTISYMMETRIC RECORDS ARE NOT AN I-FREE COUNTEREXAMPLE

Let m>=1, d>=2m+1, and take the normalized projector onto wedge^(2m+1)
(C^d). Let A be one particle, B the next m, C the final m. All particle
subset entropies are f(r)=ln binom(d,r), so
 I(A:B)=ln[d(m+1)/(d-m)],
 delta_B=delta_C
 =ln[(d-m)(2m+1)/((m+1)(d-2m))].                    (4)
Since (d-m)/(d-2m)>=1,
 delta_B>=ln[(2m+1)/(m+1)]>=ln(3/2).                 (5)
For large m and d/m^2->infinity the split cost tends to ln2; it is NOT
at least ln2 for every finite m. This exact qualification corrects the
coarse asymptotic shorthand used in an earlier coordinator message.

These states are highly entangled across A:(BC). For any unit a in A,
 <a|P_wedge^k|a>=(1/k)P_wedge^(k-1)(a^perp), k=2m+1.
Indeed rotate a to the first basis vector and expand each normalized
Slater vector: its a component has amplitude 1/sqrt(k). Thus every
normal product state across A:(BC) has projector expectation <=1/k.
The normalized projector state has expectation one. Therefore
 inf_Sep T(tau,Sep)>=1-1/(2m+1).                    (6)
The witness survives the trace-closed convex hull. Taking, for example,
d=m^3+2m+1 gives I(A:B)~ln m and distance approaching one, but the TWO
CMIs approach ln2 each, not zero. This family therefore does not refute
(G) and does not discharge the new stronger gate.

3. A REAL FAILED SHORTCUT: NO UNIVERSAL LINEAR RELATIVE-ENTROPY BOUND

The same balanced family DOES disprove an apparently stronger entropic
shortcut of the form
 E_R(A:BC)_tau<=K[delta_B+delta_C]
with a finite dimension-independent K. Measurement of the effect P_wedge^k
has outcome one with probability one on tau and probability <=1/k on
every separable sigma. Relative-entropy data processing gives
 D(tau||sigma)>=ln k,
hence E_R(A:BC)>=ln(2m+1), whereas delta_B+delta_C->2ln2 in the preceding
large-d regime. The ratio diverges. This is a precise obstruction to
that stronger entropy estimate, not a counterexample to a TRACE modulus
h(t)->0. A dimension-free trace theorem could cap at one for constant t.

The one-sided wedge example in the baseline independently shows that
small I(A:B|C) alone, even I(A:B)<=ln4, cannot yield Sep(A:BC) closeness.
The standard Christandl--Schuch--Winter antisymmetric construction has
exactly that same one-sided interface; it is not a novelty claim here.

4. REMAINING FOUNDATIONAL INTERFACE

The concrete unresolved step is a dimension-free stability principle
for simultaneous near equality of the two relative-entropy data-processing
inequalities under Tr_C and Tr_B. Equality gives the common locally
readable PVM structure in Hayashi--Zhao Theorem 1; a recovery estimate in
each direction supplies only the actual compatible channel used in the
baseline. The current quadratic-orbit transfer still spends an I-dependent
substate cap and does not control that step without an information budget.

A valid solution to (G), or an explicit high-I double-small-CMI trace
counterexample, would change the foundational assessment. Neither follows
from small broadcasting error alone, small squashed entanglement, a weak
LOCC distance estimate, or the existing exact equality classification.
This report leaves that gate UNKNOWN and does not rename the bounded-I
corollary as a major-problem resolution.
