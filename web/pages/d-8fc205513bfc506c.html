Current candidate
-----------------
`RESULT.txt` proves a restricted all-state geometric Foster theorem for one
immigrating/degrading A, one A-controlled substrate B, independent C_i
immigration/death, and arbitrary finite mixed cofactor toggles
A+nu_j.C <-> nu_j.C. The key correction is g(a,Q)=(1+Q)/(1+Q+a), with
Q=sum zeta_j product_i(c_i)_(nu_ji). It equals one on a=0 independently of
the cofactor counts, so C jumps create no inactive W hazard; its A-death and
toggle increments are O(1/Q), and the substrate b-sink absorbs their O(aW)
active contribution. The separate a/(1+Q) term handles the A drift.

The exact mixed-order death bound is H<=H0=sum_i mu_i h(M_i), where
M_i=max_j nu_ji, h(0)=0, h(1)=1, and h(M)=M+M^2/4 for M>=2. Under
kappa=min(delta,min eta_j/zeta_j)-H0>0, the proof
gives LV<=C-cV. A rational four-species non-complex-balanced example is
included. No blanket weak-reversibility or all-rate claim is made.
