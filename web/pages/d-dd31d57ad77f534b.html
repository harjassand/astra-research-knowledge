It has the uniformly smooth extension at delta=0 supplied by rationalizing the difference of square roots. Its first weights are sigma u1+sigma sum_i mu_i u_(i+1). The shifted t_i/R equals mu_i-sigma delta u_(i+1) through weight two. Normalized radial-shape coefficients add -2delta sum_i beta_i cos(Theta_i-sigma u_(i+1)) to (20) at that order. The leading radial harmonic average of every cosine is one, so this shape contribution is EXACTLY -2delta sum_i beta_i=0; it is not bounded away by an unproved small coefficient assumption.

With h_sigma=sigma< u_(i+1)sin(sigma u_(i+1)) > under the tilted measure psi e^(sigma u1), the first crest equations and full second residual are

    (aE)^(-1)G_(ti)=-ci Theta_i-(1-h_sigma ci)mu_i
                       +terms of weight>=3,
    (a^2E)^(-1)Delta G
        =2sum_i ci mu_i Theta_i-2h_sigma sum_i ci mu_i^2
          +(2h_sigma-n+1)delta-qK-2delta sum_i beta_i
                       +terms of weight>=3.          (A1)

The absence of weight-two terms in the first line follows also from simultaneous active reflection (mu,Theta)->(-mu,-Theta), with radial coefficients and delta,qK fixed. Substitution of its critical phases gives the universal -2sum_i mu_i^2 in the second line. Multiplying the order-two numerator by the order-two denominator correction only changes order four. The origin term qK and every first radial shape term have been retained.

Uniform Taylor remainders and their nominal derivatives follow from this smooth finite-kernel representation on its compact support, bounded normalized radial jets, and the radial profile's required derivative bounds. Differentiating at FIXED Theta acts only on these soft parameters: D_(tc)mu=O(1/r), D_(tc)delta=0, D_(tc)qK=0, and coefficient/radial-jet dependence through R adds bounded derivatives no larger than C/L. A remainder of weight three therefore has nominal derivative at most C eta^3/L. Radial derivatives at fixed nominal t_c cost 1/R_j. The stable backward crest integral preserves those bounds as stated after (22).

## Appendix B. Differentiated matrix-stable crest, without a terminal-scale loss

Let Theta_crit(r;t_c) be the instantaneous phase crest from (21), and put w=a[y-y_crit(r;t_c)] on the backward invariant crest. In its fixed small tube the exact equation has the form

    w'=N(r,w;t_c)+f(r;t_c),
    N(r,0;t_c)=0, Sym D_wN>=c a I,
    f=-partial_rTheta_crit=O(eta/R_j),
    w(r_*)=0.

The exact soft field gives |partial_rN|<=Ca|w|/R_j, |D_(tc)N|<=Ca|w|/L, |f_r|<=C eta/R_j^2, and |D_(tc)f|<=C eta/(R_j L). Backward coercivity first yields |w|<=C eta/(aR_j)=C eta^3. The nominal derivative satisfies

    (D_(tc)w)'=D_wN D_(tc)w+D_(tc)N+D_(tc)f,
    D_(tc)w(r_*)=0,

so |D_(tc)w|<=C eta^3/L. For z=w_r, differentiation gives

    z'=D_wN z+partial_rN+f_r,
    z(r_*)=f(r_*)=O(eta/R_j).

Consequently

    |w_r(r)|<=C eta^3/R_j
                  +C(eta/R_j)exp[-ca(r_*-r)].        (B1)

The reference neck and all return gates are a fixed macro distance from r_*, so the last term is exponential there. Bounding w_r only from its original equation would lose two powers of eta; (B1) uses the differentiated stable equation. Its fundamental matrix norm is controlled by the symmetric part of D_wN, so neither diagonal commutation nor a constant weight vector is assumed. These estimates provide the invariant-crest derivative margin required in (23)-(24).
