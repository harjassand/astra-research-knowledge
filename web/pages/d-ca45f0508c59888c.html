C04_S02 -- FIXED ANISOTROPY OPEN NEIGHBORHOOD
7 October 2026. Addendum to QUDIT_POSITIVITY_LOCALIZATION.txt; no INITIAL
change. Internal mathematical derivation, external validation/priority UNKNOWN.
Root proposed the fixed-anisotropy probe after reading the shrinking-cutoff
result. I reconstructed it directly from the general localization identities
and coordinated the explicit constants with c04_s01. The c07 stopped
construction and c04_s01 direct logdet route retain their attribution.

EXACT CLAIM

Fix d>=2, beta>0 and B0>=0. Use the complete fundamental SU(d) basis
Tr T_a T_b=delta_ab/2 and collective J_a,Q_C as in the parent proof. Define

 D0=3 beta(d-1)+d B0,
 H0=2 beta(d-1)/d+B0,
 L=sqrt(4 beta d(d-1)(H0+2)),
 eta=exp[-d log d-D0-L],
 a_star=beta eta^2/10.                                                  (F1)

This is a positive open-neighborhood radius independent of N. For every
N>=d+1, every known real symmetric A_N with ||A_N||<=a_star, and every
known traceless Hermitian B_N with ||B_N||<=B0, the whole Gibbs density

 rho_N=exp(K_N)/Tr exp(K_N),
 K_N=(beta Q_I+Q_{A_N})/N+J_{B_N},

has an actual positive iid-product mixture sigma_N such that

 T(rho_N,sigma_N)<=min{1,H0 N exp(-2N)}.                                (F2)

Thus a fixed anisotropy neighborhood of the isotropic model admits an
exponentially accurate whole-density product-mixture representation. A_N
need not decay with N and may change eigenaxes arbitrarily within the bound.
The uniform one-body field is bounded but need not be scaled to zero. All
constants depend on fixed public d,beta,B0. Radius a_star is generally tiny;
the result is not a claim about arbitrary fixed anisotropic ferromagnets.

COMPLETE DERIVATION FROM THE ACQUIRED GENERATOR

The parent Sections1-3 derive the full product-kernel identity
 M_K k=(L_C+V_C)k, k=rho^{tensor N}, with epsilon=1/N,C=betaI+A_N,
eta_field=1 and B=B_N, including exact principal D_C and drift. Those
identities hold before any positivity assumption and so also apply here.
This addendum uses a fixed full-rank cutoff, rather than the parent's e_N.

Since eta<1/d, a_star<beta/(10d^2)<beta/2. Hence
 (beta/2)I<=C<=2betaI.
On the compact density domain rho>=etaI, the proven principal bounds give

 D_C=betaD_I+D_A >=(2beta eta^2-10||A||)I
                     >=beta eta^2 I>0.                               (F3)

Therefore the SDE is real and smooth on this compact region, has a strictly
positive known covariance margin, and starts in its interior at rho_0=I/d.
Stop at its first boundary hit tau=lambda_min(rho)=eta, freezing both rho
and its weight. Its normalized weighted endpoint sigma_N is automatically
a positive iid-product mixture. No external reference/seed is needed.

The exact logdet identities (parent7), C<=2betaI and N>=d+1 prove

 L_C logdet rho>=-D0,
 Gamma(logdet rho)<=2beta d(d-1)/N.

At the boundary, det rho<=eta because the remaining eigenvalues are<=1.
The initial logdet is -dlogd. Thus exit forces a martingale drop at least
 log(1/eta)-dlogd-D0=L. The exponential-martingale bound gives

 P(tau<=1)<=exp[-N L^2/(4beta d(d-1))]
            =exp[-(H0+2)N].                                          (F4)

The parent completeness identity and C<=2betaI give
 ||K_N||<=h_N=beta(d-1)(N+d)/d+N B0<=H0N.
The stopped Duhamel estimate is exactly
 ||exp(K_N)/d^N-W_1||_1<=h_N exp(h_N)P(tau<=1).
The improved exp(h_N), rather than exp(2h_N), uses
w_tau exp[(1-t)h_N]<=exp(h_N) for tau<=t in the integral. Scalar Jensen
gives the target trace>=1, since C is positive and B is traceless. Therefore
normalized half trace distance<=the unnormalized trace error. Substitution
of (F4) proves
 T<=h_N exp[h_N-(H0+2)N]<=H0N exp(-2N), as claimed.

All steps are uniform over the displayed fixed anisotropy ball and over all
N>=d+1. No claim of central concentration, unproven heat integral, pressure
asymptotics, or extrapolation from a small matrix is made.

COST / LIMITS

Known inputs d,beta,A_N,B_N and B0 are supplied. The radius and cutoff are
explicit computable real constants. They can be replaced by smaller rational
certified bounds to make an input comparison reviewable; doing so narrows the
radius and changes the exit rate explicitly. The resulting stochastic law is
specified, but Brownian path generation, stopping, weighting/normalization
and physical one-site preparation at certified finite precision are not
implemented. A rejection envelope still permits exp(O(N)) cost. Exact
finite-outcome existence by ambient Caratheodory can use up to d^{2N} atoms,
which is not a practical synthesized decomposition. No polynomial-time
algorithm, finite-bit runtime, reference acquisition or external priority
claim is established. Local exact generator diagnostic is inherited from
the parent and verifies signs/coefficient normalization only.

The independently assigned audit of the full generator/rate is ongoing in
c07_l05. Internal agreements are not external mathematical validation.
The generic broadcasting-to-EB Dirichlet gate remains OPEN.
