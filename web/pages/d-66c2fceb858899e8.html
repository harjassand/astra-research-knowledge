CYCLE07 INDEPENDENT BASELINE -- MARGINAL-COHERENCE GATE
2026-10-09. foundational_transfer_sol. No Cycle07 sibling proof exposure.

TARGET AND STATUS

For arbitrary finite-dimensional rho_ABC, let
 delta_B=I(A:C|B), delta_C=I(A:B|C), t=delta_B+delta_C.
The information-free target is a dimension-free f(t)->0 such that
 dist_Sep(A:BC)(rho)<=f(t).
A stronger version preserves rho_A and rho_BC exactly. Neither full
version is proved or refuted in this baseline. The theorem below removes
the I(A:B) budget on a substantial explicit marginal class and identifies
one precise entropy cost blocking the same proof in general. It does not
turn that scoped advance into a resolution of the unrestricted target.

All logarithms are NATURAL. T is HALF trace distance. Spaces are finite
and arbitrary; all entropies are finite. No upstream Cycle05 theorem,
universal recovery, numerical evidence or new model-generated source
claim is used in the proof below.

1. EXACT GENERAL DEPHASING IDENTITY

Choose ANY orthonormal bases on B,C. Let Delta_B,Delta_C be their
rank-one projective pinching channels and Delta=Delta_B tensor Delta_C.
Set
 rho'= (id_A tensor Delta)rho, kappa=rho_BC, kappa'=Delta(kappa).
Define coherence costs
 C_rho=D(rho||rho')=S(rho')-S(rho),
 C_kappa=D(kappa||kappa')=S(kappa')-S(kappa),
 C_B=D(kappa_B||Delta_B kappa_B),
 C_C=D(kappa_C||Delta_C kappa_C).
Support containment and the pinching conditional-expectation identity
give each equality, including singular inputs. Put
 t'=I(A:C|B)_{rho'}+I(A:B|C)_{rho'},
 c=I(B:C|A)_rho-I(B:C|A)_{rho'}>=0.
The last inequality is ordinary CMI monotonicity under local operations
on its TWO unconditioned systems B,C; A remains untouched.

Then the EXACT identity is
 C_rho = t-t' + 2C_kappa-C_B-C_C-c.                   (1)
Consequently
 D(rho||rho')<=t+2C_kappa-C_B-C_C<=t+2C_kappa,         (2)
and rho' is separable across A:(BC). In particular
 T(rho,rho')<=sqrt((t+2C_kappa-C_B-C_C)/2)
            <=sqrt(t/2+C_kappa).                    (3)
The total cap1 may be applied. This is an approximation preserving A
exactly, with BC marginal kappa'. The prepared B,C states are classical
basis states. No small alphabet or efficient basis acquisition is claimed.

FULL ALGEBRA

Let J=I(A:BC)_rho, K=I(A:B)_rho, L=I(A:C)_rho and use primes for rho'.
Then t=2J-K-L. Define losses x=J-J', y=K-K', z=L-L'.
Pinching affects no A marginal, so
 x=C_rho-C_kappa,
 y=C_{rho_AB}-C_B, z=C_{rho_AC}-C_C.
All three mutual-information losses are nonnegative by data processing.

The chain identity, valid for ANY tripartite state, is
 K+L-J=I(B:C)-I(B:C|A).
Subtract its primed version:
 y+z-x = [I(B:C)_kappa-I(B:C)_kappa']-c.             (4)
The marginal mutual-information loss is exactly
 I(B:C)_kappa-I(B:C)_kappa'=C_kappa-C_B-C_C.
Thus x-y-z=c-C_kappa+C_B+C_C. Also
 t-t'=2x-y-z=x+c-C_kappa+C_B+C_C.
Inserting x=C_rho-C_kappa gives (1). Nonnegativity of t', c, C_B,C_C
gives (2). Quantum Pinsker D>=2T^2 gives (3).

There is no general assertion x>=y+z without fixing the BC marginal.
Its correction in (4) is essential. Omitting that correction would
silently assume the disputed general information-free theorem.

2. INFORMATION-FREE FIXED-MARGINAL COROLLARY

If kappa is diagonal in the chosen product basis, then Delta kappa=kappa
and C_kappa=C_B=C_C=0. Equation (1) becomes
 D(rho||rho')=t-t'-c<=t.
Therefore for EVERY extension of this fixed classical-classical kappa,
 sigma=(id_A tensor Delta)rho is separable across A:(BC),
 sigma_A=rho_A and sigma_BC=kappa EXACTLY, and
 D(rho||sigma)<=delta_B+delta_C,
 T(rho,sigma)<=sqrt((delta_B+delta_C)/2).             (5)

There is no restriction on I(A:B), rank, nonzero eigenvalues, dimension
ratios or amount of classical mutual information in kappa. The pinching
on BC is chosen from its diagonalizing product bases BEFORE the reference
A or tested extension is supplied. It is an actual joint EB channel
BC->BC. It is NOT claimed to be a B->BC reconstruction channel; those
interfaces differ. It reconstructs from the supplied whole rho_ABC by
local pinching of both records, which suffices for the requested Sep
distance and exact-marginal strengthening.

This excludes all counterfamilies with classical-classical BC marginal,
including extensions with high quantum reference dimension and arbitrary
classical correlations between the records. Such a family cannot retain
a positive Sep(A:BC) gap while both CMIs tend to zero.

3. APPROXIMATE MARGINAL CLASS AND EXACT MARGINAL REPAIR

For general kappa, (3) gives the unconstrained-separable bound
 dist_Sep(A:BC)(rho)<=sqrt(t/2+C_kappa).
Optimizing over the product bases defines the usual relative-entropy
product-basis coherence quantity
 Q_CC(kappa)=inf_bases D(kappa||Delta_B Delta_C kappa).
Only its definition is used; historical terminology/priority is not
assumed. Thus
 dist_Sep(A:BC)(rho)<=min(1,sqrt(t/2+Q_CC(kappa))).     (6)
Finite dimensions make the infimum attainable by compactness and
continuity of dephased entropy. A proof of that attainment is not needed
for the bound; an approximating sequence suffices.

To preserve the ORIGINAL BC marginal as well, apply a CPTP repair on
the local BC side to rho'. The following normal/finite gentle repair
was already independently audited in Cycle05; its exact physical
construction and half-trace constant are re-stated here.

For densities nu=kappa', sigma=kappa and delta=T(nu,sigma), let
 M=nu+(sigma-nu)_+=sigma+(nu-sigma)_+,
 K=sqrt(sigma) M^{-1/2} on supp(M), zero on its complement.
Then K^dagger K<=I, K nu K^dagger<=sigma, and
 q=1-Tr(K nu K^dagger)<=delta.
For q>0 set
 R(Y)=K Y K^dagger+
      Tr[(I-K^dagger K)Y](sigma-K nu K^dagger)/q.
It is CPTP, R(nu)=sigma and EVERY extension omega_A:BC of nu obeys
 T((id_A tensor R)omega,omega)<=sqrt(2delta).
For q=0 use an arbitrary positive completion on the zero-weight failure
support; its action on every nu extension is the displayed success term.
This is a CHANNEL on BC, so it preserves Sep(A:BC), not B:C separability.

Therefore sigma_repaired=(id_A tensor R)rho' is separable, has EXACT
A and BC marginals, and
 T(rho,sigma_repaired)
 <=sqrt(t/2+C_kappa)+sqrt(2T(kappa',kappa))
 <=sqrt(t/2+C_kappa)+2^(1/4) C_kappa^(1/4).           (7)
Pinsker gives T(kappa',kappa)<=sqrt(C_kappa/2), with the orientation
D(kappa||kappa') used in (2); trace distance is symmetric. The repair
depends only on kappa and the chosen bases, not A or rho. Bound7 may
be capped at1. At Q_CC(kappa)=0 it reduces to the exact theorem (5).

PREMISE SCOPE: (7) uses the previously audited marginal-repair lemma.
Equations (1)-(6) are self-contained finite entropy/Pinsker derivations.
No Cycle07 sibling theorem is imported.

4. NECESSARY OBSTRUCTION FOR ANY COUNTERFAMILY

For ANY fixed positive sep gap eta and t->0, (6) requires
 liminf Q_CC(kappa)>=eta^2.
Thus a genuine high-I both-small-CMI trace counterexample cannot merely
inflate redundant classical labels, use a classical-classical record
marginal, or hide a quantum reference in a marginal tending to that
class in relative entropy. It needs substantial record nonclassicality.
This is a necessary condition, not a sufficient counterconstruction.

The information-free target is still UNKNOWN when Q_CC is unrestricted.
The current identity spends 2C_kappa. There is no argument that the two
reference CMIs bound that cost. For example an arbitrary entangled BC
state tensored with independent A has t=0 and can have large C_kappa;
it is already separable A:(BC). Consequently the desired extra cost is
not simply all nonclassicality in kappa, but nonclassicality linked to A.

5. EARLY FALSIFIERS AND ATTEMPTED MECHANISMS

A. Pure ABC: t=2S(A) exactly. Pinsker with a product approximation or
the largest Schmidt coefficient gives information-free closeness; a
pure counterfamily cannot retain persistent A:BC entanglement.

B. Product BC marginal: since I(B:C)=0, the chain identity gives
 K+L<=J and t>=J. Hence rho_A tensor kappa is an exact-marginal product
approximation with D=J<=t and T<=sqrt(t/2). This is a stricter special
case, without product-basis acquisition.

C. Classical dephasing records: (5) rules out genuine quantum reference
extensions even if rho itself has off-diagonal coherences. One must not
assume rho is already a classical state from classicality of its marginal;
the pinching/Pinsker proof is the actual needed step.

D. Balanced-wedge and its dilute product mixture: no inference that
small broadcasting error gives small CMI is made. Balanced wedge has
both losses tending to ln2. Diluting a locally detectable entangled
sector costs its weight times that constant; copying such sectors does
not remove the total loss while maintaining their total detectable
weight. The exact unflagged likelihood calculation remains separate,
not asserted from intuition. No successful counterfamily is claimed.

E. Direct entropy-to-C4 proposal: recovery maps chosen for small CMI
are fixed by kappa, but trace closeness of the recovered state does NOT
control its mutual information without dimension/information budgets.
It is invalid to treat the actual composed broadcaster's entropy loss
as <=delta_B+delta_C from trace closeness alone. A theorem controlling
this particular loss would be a genuinely new gate; it is not assumed.

6. NEXT DECISIVE GATE

Either exhibit a family with t->0, persistent Sep(A:BC) gap, and
Q_CC(kappa) bounded away from zero, or replace the marginal cost in (1)
by a reference-sensitive term uniformly controlled by t. The dephasing
identity is a concrete exact new mechanism/check, not the missing general
theorem. Priority of this identity/corollary is UNKNOWN until primary
comparison; standard SSA, CMI monotonicity and Pinsker are imports.
