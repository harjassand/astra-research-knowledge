## A precise easy-source obstruction already in the transfer prior

Let the target list contain all independent commuting physical Xi and a check C that anticommutes with at least two of them. Suppose all source list entries act on one qubit, and the source/target satisfy equal pairwise commutation matrices and kerA subset kerB. Any source representative of C must share its one physical qubit with the two field representatives it anticommutes with. Those two source fields commute. Nonidentity commuting one-qubit Paulis have the same label, so their sum lies in kerA. Their target labels Xi and Xj are independent, so this sum is not in kerB: contradiction. Signed phases do not remove this binary-label obstruction.

This rules out the most immediate independent-one-qubit source for a nontrivial actual CSS check/field star under that exact homomorphism interface. It does NOT rule out larger-block sources, different markings, efficient interacting sources, polaron controls, or other Gibbs algorithms. The mechanism is already captured by2610.06667v1's reduced-Pauli one-local structural bounds (`qip_gibbs_qip__3_.tex4190–4300`), not a new foundational discovery here.
