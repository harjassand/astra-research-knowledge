# Conditional continuation: renewal selection through later irradiation

15 October 2026. New internal continuation of PR92 N845, not an amendment to its preserved producer claims. Historical novelty and physical validity UNKNOWN. No measured tail kernel, same-atom record, reset law, useful lifetime or physical advantage has been acquired.

## Supplied contract and exact dose transform

Use PR92's ideal independent fresh draws: H probability p, L probability 1-p; constant ejection cross sections h=σ_H and l=σ_L with 0≤h<l. An attempt accepts after surviving fluence d; an ejection before d causes a fresh independent reset and a new attempt. No missed events, same-atom returns, changes of rates, collateral damage, reset irradiation or assay irradiation are hidden. Let D be the total irradiation fluence through acceptance. With

    a = p exp(-hd) + (1-p) exp(-ld),
    q = p exp(-hd)/a,
    g(t) = p h exp(-ht) + (1-p) l exp(-lt),  0≤t<d,

one attempt succeeds with probability a and duration d, or fails with density g and restarts independently. Its total-dose Laplace transform therefore satisfies

    L_D(s) = a exp(-sd) + L_D(s) ∫[0,d] exp(-st)g(t) dt,

and, for s≥0,

    L_D(s) = a exp(-sd) /
      {1 - p h/(h+s)[1-exp(-(h+s)d)]
           - (1-p) l/(l+s)[1-exp(-(l+s)d)]}.

A zero-rate failure term is zero, including its continuous value at s=0. The denominator is positive because the integral is at most 1-a. In particular L_D(0)=1, and differentiation gives

    E D = {p[1-exp(-hd)]/h + (1-p)[1-exp(-ld)]/l}/a,

with [1-exp(-hd)]/h=d when h=0. This is exactly the mean already present in PR92. The transform retains the retry distribution that the mean alone discards. The isotope on the last successful attempt is independent of D: each failed history is independent of the last fresh draw and survival event, and every successful attempt has the same duration d and conditional composition q. This assertion uses the exact iid renewal contract; it fails as a free assumption for rate drift, correlated refills or history-dependent acceptance.

## Retained endpoint under an exogenous later dose

An accepted site i has H probability q_i. A later nonnegative incident fluence δ_i is independent of that site's isotope and future ejection/refill randomness; its source is otherwise arbitrary. Keep the same rates for the accepted site. These are indispensable added premises.

If an ejected atom is immediately replaced by a neutral independent draw with H probability p_i, define

    b_i = p_i l_i + (1-p_i) h_i,
    q*_i = p_i l_i/b_i.

Then the two-state forward equation is Q'=b_i(q*_i-Q), Q(0)=q_i. Consequently

    P(final isotope is H | δ_i) = q*_i + (q_i-q*_i) exp(-b_i δ_i).

This is the final-species endpoint, not survival of the accepted atom. If the endpoint instead requires that the accepted H atom itself remain, then

    P(accepted atom is H and remains | δ_i) = q_i exp(-h_i δ_i).

A lost atom may be refilled and leave the final position H while failing the same-atom endpoint. Without refill, conditioning on survival can increase measured H fraction while reducing occupied yield; those denominators must not be interchanged.

For deterministic later dose and q_i>q_target>q*_i, the neutral-refill final-purity criterion is exactly

    δ_i ≤ log[(q_i-q*_i)/(q_target-q*_i)]/b_i.

## Composition with a supplied beam-tail kernel

Suppose later writing attempts j have independent ideal renewal totals D_j, and a measured nonnegative cross-dose coefficient η_ij makes δ_i=Σ_j η_ij D_j. Require exogenous coupling: damage or isotope changes at i do not change any later writer's stopping, rate, reset, beam delivery or acceptance. Independence of D_j and of the earlier site's future jump noise must be established rather than inferred from separate marginal calibrations. Then

    E exp(-s δ_i) = ∏_j L_{D_j}(s η_ij),

so the marginal final-species probability and accepted-H-atom retention are respectively

    q*_i + (q_i-q*_i) ∏_j L_{D_j}(b_i η_ij),
    q_i ∏_j L_{D_j}(h_i η_ij).

Without independent later doses, the product must be replaced by their joint multivariate Laplace transform. Without exogenous dose/specimen independence, even that substitution need not describe the accepted site's conditional evolution. These formulas do not authorize a feedback-coupled dense pattern, missed-event process, chemical alteration, shielding scheme or neutral immediate refill model by analogy.

Jensen gives E exp(-bδ)≥exp(-b Eδ). For q_i≥q*_i, inserting the mean dose gives a conservative lower bound on expected final-species purity in this supplied model, not its exact value, a pathwise dose cap, a high-probability guarantee or the probability of an entirely correct pattern. For q_i<q*_i the purity inequality reverses. Shared later doses correlate retained-site events; products of unconditional marginal purities cannot be asserted as pattern yield. Costs include every rejected attempt, all later dose, calibration, shielding, refill, verification and abort. Finite attempt caps require their own conditioned/censored dose transform; the uncapped transform above cannot be reused without modification.

## Exact falsifying toy for free lifetime transfer

Set p=1/2,h=1,l=2,d=log4. Exact arithmetic gives

    a=5/32, q=4/5, E D=39/10, L_D(1)=5/56, q*=2/3.

For an earlier accepted site with the same parameters and a later independent writer, choose bη=1. Its final H probability becomes

    2/3 + (4/5-2/3)(5/56) = 19/28,

rather than 4/5. For the distinct same-atom endpoint with hη=1, retained H yield is (4/5)(5/56)=1/14. These use different η choices and are separate examples. Both are exact normalized model calculations, not physical beam predictions. They show why a correct acceptance posterior alone cannot be transferred into a retained pattern.
