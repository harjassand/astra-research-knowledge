## Decisive recent primary

Xiaojin Lin, **When is a polynomial in three variables cylindrical?**, arXiv2609.06465v1, submitted6 September2026 at08:25:55UTC. The exact version's abstract page, full HTML and source archive are recovered. The complete main proof and substantive discussion/counterexamples were read.

Theorem1 states that a nonconstant complex polynomial \(Q\) in three variables satisfies

\[
 U(Q)=\nabla Q^T\operatorname{adj}(HQ)\nabla Q=0
\]

identically if and only if a nonzero constant direction \(v\) has \(D_vQ=0\). No hypothesis on its critical locus or polynomial degree is imposed. Corollary2 gives the real form. Proposition9 gives counterexamples in every dimension at least four; the three-variable conclusion is not extrapolated there.

The load-bearing main-proof steps are: an indecomposable generator \(Q=g\circ h\); the exact composition identity \(U(g\circ h)=g'(h)^4U(h)\); an irreducible smooth generic fibre by the named Bertini–Krull and generic-smoothness results; rank-at-most-one developable geometry; Piontkowski's cylinder theorem; and the final degree divisibility \((h-c)\mid D_vh\), forcing \(D_vh=0\). A single suitable fibre suffices after its translation axis is established.

In the HC4 use, the bordered Hessian's last row forces \(\nabla Q\) nowhere zero. This additionally makes \(Q\) indecomposable: if \(Q=g\circ h\) and \(\deg g>1\), a root of \(g'\) is attained by the nonconstant complex polynomial \(h\), giving \(\nabla Q=0\). Thus the composition step is unnecessary in that specific application. This simplification does not replace the named global geometric prerequisites.

## All-degree zero-pivot consequence belongs to root's synthesis

For \(f=P(x,y,z)+wQ(x,y,z)\), the four-variable Hessian determinant is

\[
 -\nabla Q^T\operatorname{adj}(HP+wHQ)\nabla Q.
\]

The highest possible power of \(w\) is **two**, with coefficient \(-U(Q)\). A scratch message initially said three and was corrected; all saved exact scripts use two. Constant determinant therefore forces \(U(Q)=0\); Lin makes \(Q\) binary after a constant linear change. The exact binary determinant then has its \(w\)-coefficient \(-P_{zz}K_Q\), where

\[
 K_Q=Q_y^2Q_{xx}-2Q_xQ_yQ_{xy}+Q_x^2Q_{yy}.
\]

Root's global polynomial dichotomy gives \(P_{zz}=0\), an exact plane Keller lift, or \(Q\) affine-linear and the established HC2 inverse. Together with the prior nonzero-pivot HC3 branch, this makes constant-pivot HC4 invertibility equivalent to JC2. The exact scope and coefficient-descent proof are in `notes/root_cycle13/CONSTANT_PIVOT_HESSIAN_REDUCTION.md`; root owns that synthesis.
