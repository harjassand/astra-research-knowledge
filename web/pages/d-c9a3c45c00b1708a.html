# Further exact restrictions on fixed-local-Pauli collisions

Worker `b_math`, cycle 2, 2026-10-06. These are independent deductions; broader novelty unverified. The six-qubit IFT construction in `REAL_IFT_AUDIT.md` now settles the unrestricted all-state question negatively, so these characterize positive subcases and constrain candidate counterexamples.

## No Pauli commuting symmetry at any faithful state

For every faithful `σ`, the dressed map is strictly positive as a Hilbert–Schmidt superoperator:
\[
\operatorname{Tr}(A^\dagger T_\sigma(A))
=\sum_{i,j}\frac{2\sqrt{p_i p_j}}{p_i+p_j}|A_{ij}|^2>0\quad(A\ne0).
\]
Suppose a Hermitian involution `P` commutes with the whole dressed frame. If any nonzero Hermitian generator in the real span of the frame anticommutes with `P`, then
`Tr(A T_σ(A))=Tr((PAP)(PT_σ(A)P))=−Tr(A T_σ(A))=0`, a contradiction.

In particular the common dressed commutant for the physical local `{X_i,Z_i}` frame contains **no nonidentity Pauli operator**: the local labels span the full symplectic label space, so every nonidentity Pauli anticommutes with at least one local generator. A nonscalar commutant can still consist of dense operators containing no Pauli symmetry, exactly as the IFT witness does. This lemma cannot be substituted for irreducibility.

## Arbitrary faithful product targets

Let `σ=⊗_i ρ_i`, `ρ_i=(I+r_i·Pauli)/2`, with `|r_i|<1`. Spectator population factors cancel in the local modular-frequency ratios, hence
`T_σ(A_i)=T_ρi(A_i)⊗I_rest`. Put `t_i=√(1−|r_i|²)>0` and `u_i=r_i/|r_i|` when nonzero. On Bloch vectors,
\[
T_{\rho_i}=t_i I+(1-t_i)u_i u_i^T.
\]
Its restriction to the physical `xz` plane has Gram eigenvalues
`t_i²` and `t_i²+(1−t_i²)|u_i,xz|²`. For two resulting local Bloch vectors `k_x,k_z`, the local commutator quadratic form on a traceless Bloch vector `v` is
`4[(||k_x||²+||k_z||²)||v||²−|v·k_x|²−|v·k_z|²]`. Its smallest eigenvalue is exactly `4t_i²`. Tensor-summing the independent local superoperators therefore gives
\[
\inf_{\operatorname{Tr}M=0}
\frac{\sum_i(\|[M,T_\sigma(X_i)]\|_F^2+\|[M,T_\sigma(Z_i)]\|_F^2)}{\|M\|_F^2}
=4\min_i(1-|r_i|^2).
\]
Thus the fixed local frame identifies every faithful product target against arbitrary faithful competitors, with an exact Frobenius gap independent of system size at fixed local purities. These product targets need not be diagonal in the physical computational basis.

For a single qubit, its unitary-orbit SLD itself is `S_A=2(a×r)·Pauli`, for `A=a·Pauli`; this follows because the Bloch score vector is perpendicular to `r`. Local product scores tensorize. This is exact matrix-score identifiability, not an economical score-acquisition protocol.
