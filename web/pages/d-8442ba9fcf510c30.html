Here is a further conditional bridge that closes one concrete precision gap.
Use the convention V_vac=I/2, and let V and Vhat be centered physical
covariances on M modes. Apply the same passive W and uniform vacuum loss eta
to both, 0<eta<=1/10:

  V_eta    = eta W V W^T + (1-eta) I/2,
  Vhat_eta = eta W Vhat W^T + (1-eta) I/2.

If both output states have mean photon number at most N, Mele et al.,
arXiv:2405.01431v4, Theorem 10, gives in its covariance convention
V_paper=2 V_here

  d_tr(rho_eta,rhohat_eta)
       <= f(N) ( ||Delta m||_2 + sqrt(2) sqrt(||Delta V_paper||_1) )
       = 2 f(N) sqrt(eta ||V-Vhat||_1),
  f(N)=(sqrt(N)+sqrt(N+1))/sqrt(2),

because both means vanish and orthogonal W preserves trace norm. PNR
measurement is a POVM, so its total variation distance is no larger than
d_tr. Combining this with the conditional N72 reset theorem on the rational
physical covariance Vhat gives

  TV(PNR(V_eta), Reset(Vhat,eta))
      <= 2 f(N) sqrt(eta ||V-Vhat||_1)
         + (5184/25) eta^3 sum_j xhat_j^2
                                    (xhat_j+1/(1-eta)),

where xhat_j are the ordinary eigenvalues of Vhat-I/2. A finite-bit
implementation adds its own stated sampler error. This is an additive
distributional guarantee; it gives no relative accuracy for a rare pattern.

Physical rationalization is explicit. If a symmetric rational raw matrix V0
has certified ||V0-V||_op<=delta, define Vhat=V0+2 delta I. Since
V+i Omega/2 >=0 and 2 delta I+(V0-V)>=delta I, Vhat+i Omega/2>=delta I>0.
Its trace-norm covariance error is at most 6 M delta and its mean photon
energy is at most E+3 M delta. The strict slack is intended to remove
vacuum-only inactive modes; the N72 active-support condition must still be
checked in its exact notation when instantiating the sampler. The covariance
precision needed for total PNR error eps is
  ||V-Vhat||_1 <= eps^2/[4 eta f(N)^2], after reserving the other error
budgets. The bound is dimension independent except through N and the chosen
trace-norm precision.

INDEPENDENT CHECKS, LIMITS, AND DECISIVE REMAINDER

The exact rational script passes. Its physicality check is the one-mode
uncertainty determinant; its 10,000-mode covariance trace norm, energy, reset
expression, and final arithmetic are exact Fractions. The external Gaussian
continuity inequality is sourced to Mele et al. The N71/N72 theorem remains
an internally reconstructed candidate from the pinned Astra package, not
externally certified here. Lim's source paper is a recent preprint; its
full high-precision sampler engine remains unexecuted. The construction does
not include displacement, an interacting/nonquadratic Gibbs state, a
nonvacuum loss environment, a partition function, state trace-norm error
from Reset, or a relative Fock-pattern estimate.

The remaining algorithmic bottleneck is to compute a certified rational
physical covariance approximation from the supplied rational quadratic
Hamiltonian with its numerical cost charged, then feed that representation
to the finite-bit reset sampler. The Gaussian continuity lemma now converts
that matrix error into a PNR-TV budget, but it does not itself provide the
covariance compiler or validate T73. The exact example has rational covariance
and is only an algebra/budget diagnostic.
