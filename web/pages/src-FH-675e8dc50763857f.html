## Specialized comparator replay

The source `comparators.py` accepts an explicit output path, so it was replayed to `replay/independently_replayed_comparators.json`; no `/mnt/data` destination was used. Its five n=24,t=1000 attractive-coupling local-window intervals remain extremely wide: width ratios are 185,172 (w=3), 72,437 (w=5), 38,330 (w=7), 23,514 (w=9), and 15,756 (w=11). The w=11 pair of solves/residual checks took about 0.356 s. This defeats the local boundary comparator for the 10% target but is not a claim that all window methods fail.

The full sparse reference solves for n=6,8,10,12 have ratios 1.0000000006, 1.0000000020, 1.0000000043, and 1.0000000179, respectively. The n=12 full-state solve used 4,096 states and took 2.01 s in this environment; it is accurate but only demonstrated up to n=12. This is not a same-size or matched-accuracy superiority comparison. The source's `audit.py` was not run because its fixed destination is `/mnt/data/astra_source_escape`, outside the permitted output path; independent exact checks and guarded replays write under this workspace. The comparator comparison receipt is `replay/comparator_difference_summary.json`.

## Query-tree replay

- `replay/query_tree_exact_replay.json`: exact rational `W(S)=r^{|S|}t^{n-b(S)}` oracle, `t=3/2`, `r=5/3`, 101 n=24 cycle orderings, plus 25 source-like `t=1000,r=1` orderings. All recovered edge sets equal the planted cycles. For the first fixture set, unique-mask counts are min 173, median 209, max 220; natural order 173. For the source-like set, min/median/max are 189/208/218.
- `replay/query_tree_audit.json`: 5,000 seeded n=24 labelings, exact cache-mask accounting and planted-cycle recovery. Counts min 183, p10 200, median 208, mean 207.585, p90 214, max 223; every sample is below the pairwise 301 baseline. This is a randomized empirical distribution plus a separate deterministic upper bound of 289; not an exact enumeration of all cycle orderings.

## Wall-cut trial-size extension

The existing small `w=2` and `w=4` results are archived in `replay/wallcut_n16_t{10,100}_w{2,4}.json` and summarized by the source JSON. At n=16,t=100, increasing the wall cutoff from two to four changes the interval ratio from `2.20645` to `1.00118`, while increasing trial entries from 242 to 3,882 and residual neighborhood from 2,562 to 19,226. At n=16,t=10, the ratio falls from `23.5971` to `6.06186` but still fails. A subsequent w=6 trial grew to about 1 GiB RSS and was terminated after roughly three minutes; it produced no result. No larger-size run was attempted after the parent requested audit priority.
