## Statement

Let f be a proper recognizable noncommutative formal series with at most m states:

    f_w=uB_wv,   f(x)=u(I-sum_i B_i x_i)^-1 v.

There are no inverses of singular constant terms. For every n,m>=1 let T_i be the same explicit lower triangular Volterra matrices of dimension d=2nm used in the acyclic theorem. Then

    f!=0  =>  f(ell T_1,...,ell T_n)!=0 for some ell in {1,...,m}.

Every evaluation exists, because the T_i are jointly strictly lower triangular, so the series truncates regardless of cyclic B_i. The single block diagonal tuple direct_sum_(ell=1)^m ell T_i therefore detects all such series at dimension2nm^2. This replaces the previous universal length marker of dimension about n^2 m^4. It does not turn an unmarked single scalar tuple into a faithful cyclic model: an explicit five-state alias shows that is false.

## Derivation

Introduce a scalar indeterminate lambda, put A_lambda(z)=lambda P(z)/q(z), q(z)=prod_i(z+i), degP<=n-1, and solve G'=A_lambda G,G(0)=I in F(lambda)[[z]]. Acyclicity is unnecessary for this formal solution. Volterra operators J_i increase z-order, so all infinite sums at a fixed coefficient are finite. If uv!=0, every evaluated matrix detects the nonzero diagonal immediately. Otherwise the formal kernel is

    K_lambda(z,t)=uG(z)G(t)^-1 A_lambda(t)v,
    f(lambda J)a(z)=int_0^z K_lambda(z,t)a(t)dt.

It is nonzero: choose a nonzero homogeneous word-length part f_h. Finite-word hyperlogarithm independence makes the coefficient of lambda^h in f(lambda J)1 nonzero. This uses only finite faithfulness, and does not assume that an arbitrary infinite unmarked series is faithful.

Over F(lambda,z), define R_0=u, R_(j+1)=R_j'+R_j A_lambda, and R_j=Z_j/q^j. The same invariant rational row-span argument gives some j<m for which

    b_j(lambda,t) = (partial_z^j K_lambda)(t,t)
                  = Z_j(lambda,t) lambda P(t)v / q(t)^(j+1)

is nonzero. Induction on the numerator recurrence gives BOTH bounds

    deg_t Z_j <= j(n-1),       deg_lambda Z_j <= j.

Consequently its nonzero numerator N(lambda,t) has lambda-degree at most j+1<=m, is divisible by lambda, and has t-degree at most(j+1)(n-1). Select any nonzero coefficient of t in N/lambda. It is a polynomial in lambda of degree at most m-1, so it cannot vanish at all m distinct nonzero integers1,...,m. For some ell in that set, b_j(ell,t) is nonzero and has t-order at most(j+1)(n-1).

Now specialize the entire formal differential equation to that integer ell. Its coefficients are regular at z=0 because q(0)=n! is nonzero; no rational row-dependence coefficient is specialized, so their possible lambda-poles are irrelevant. The actual derivative identity remains valid. As in the acyclic argument, K_ell(t+x,t) has a nonzero term of total degree at most n(j+1)-1<=nm-1. Its lowest homogeneous part is recovered by the Cauchy moments of inputs1,z,...,z^(ell0-1), where ell0<=nm. The corresponding entry lies inside dimension2nm. Therefore f(ell T)!=0.

This proves the statement subject to the checked finite-word faithfulness prerequisite. An alternative is a nilpotent lambda-marker S_(m+1): select a nonzero coefficient lambda^h of N, h<=m. Its kernel coefficient has the same degree bound, so f(S_(m+1) tensor T) is nonzero at dimension2nm(m+1). The scalar grid gives slightly smaller direct-sum dimension and permits m separate smaller queries.

## Acquisition and cost obligations

The parameters n,m alone construct all queries; coefficients and hidden state matrices are not read. There are m tuples of dimension2nm, or one tuple of dimension2nm^2. Scalar multiplication by ell adds O(log m) bits. Full outputs from separate tuples contain m(2nm)^2=4n^2m^3 field elements before any structural compression; a dense direct-sum response has larger nominal size and should not be used without charging it.

In the residual-span learner, every linear combination of left residuals shares the same hidden transition matrices B_i and changes only its initial row, so the SAME bound m suffices. Frontier_crossfield independently supplied this useful correction. A comparison against an unrelated external m-state hypothesis instead needs bound2m. Prefix extraction has two legal implementations: width-d chains of length|p| for full-matrix residuals, or d individual seed queries with scalar prefix chains. These trade matrix dimension against query count. With r<=m actual minimal rank, the grid/full-width version uses at most m(1+nr) exact output-only queries, each of dimension at most2nm(r+1), and residual feature length4n^2m^3. Full query/response matrices, exact linear algebra and coefficient heights must all be charged. The theorem does not bound response numerator height from m alone and does not preserve positivity of a stochastic representation.
