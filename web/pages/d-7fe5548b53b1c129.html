# What the fixed-frame collision does and does not hide

2026-10-06. This note uses the exact implicit six-qubit construction in `EXACT_LOCAL_PAULI_CERTIFICATE.md`; it adds no numerical existence claim.

Let sigma=U diag(p_i) U^T, `p_i=s^(2i)/Z`, d=64, and take the nontrivial collision rho_t from that construction. In the **sigma eigenbasis**, both states have identical diagonal populations and

    (rho_t-sigma)_ij = t sqrt(p_i p_j)/64 for i!=j.

All scores for the fixed local Xi,Zi frame match **exactly**, so their target-weighted score discrepancy is zero for every admitted nonzero t. Full-state identifiability fails even though sigma and rho_t are faithful and the original frame has scalar commutant.

The original local frame's unweighted Hilbert--Schmidt commutator gap is at least2 in singular-value units: every nonidentity Pauli string anticommutes with at least one Xi or Zi, hence

    sum_i ||[M,X_i]||_HS^2+||[M,Z_i]||_HS^2 >=4||M||_HS^2

for traceless M. The transformed frame's gap is zero at the constructed target. Thus ordinary spectral-gap certification does not certify the transformed gap, even for this actual fixed tensor frame.

The target's minimum eigenvalue is `s^126/Z>0` and condition number is `s^-126`. The conservative explicit s_star from the certificate is extraordinarily small. This is a low-temperature, ill-conditioned existence obstruction; it is not evidence of a high-temperature failure or an experimentally convenient collision.

The copies of the two states remain distinguishable. For example, consider the bounded observable

    B=U(|0><1|+|1><0|)U^T, ||B||_op=1.

It has target expectation0 and candidate expectation

    Tr[B rho_t] =2t sqrt(p_0 p_1)/64 = t s/(32Z).

Thus a **global**, target-dependent coherence measurement distinguishes the displayed pair with finitely many copies. A direct bounded-outcome mean estimator needs at most a constant times `(32Z/(|t|s))^2 log(1/delta)` copies for fixed-confidence discrimination by this observable. This is only an upper bound for one specified measurement, not an optimal hypothesis-testing lower bound. Implementing B also requires the constructed global basis U, whose exact root is given by the contraction definition; its preparation cost has not been bounded practically.

This distinguishes three claims: the copies themselves contain the information; local SLD scores for the supplied fixed frame lose it; and the explicit proof construction does not supply an efficient new measurement or learning algorithm. Adding candidate model moments can restore distinctions but may require normalization or candidate-state preparation, as recorded in the Cycle1 repair audit.

Both states are Gibbs states for finite6-qubit Hamiltonians. Taking the complete nonidentity Pauli basis on each isolated6-site block gives locality6 and at most3072 terms incident on a site. A sufficiently large common inverse temperature puts both Hamiltonian coefficient vectors in [-1,1]. Repeating such isolated blocks or adding maximally mixed ancillas preserves bounded locality/interaction degree and extends the fixed-local-frame obstruction to all n>=6. The paper's high-temperature guarantees remain outside this construction's regime.

Unresolved: minimal qubit count, a useful lower bound on the largest faithful-collision separation, practical finite-parameter constructions at modest condition number, and novelty relative to earlier quantum score/Fisher identifiability literature. None is silently inferred from the exact certificate.
