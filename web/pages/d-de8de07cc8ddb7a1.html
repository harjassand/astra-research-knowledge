REACTION DYNAMICS — CYCLE 3 RESTART
===================================
Checkpoint: 2026-10-08

Current strongest result
------------------------
Read RESULT.txt first. The complete catalyst-substrate-pair family with
positive immigration/death, substrate production/removal, and reversible
higher-order autocatalysis is irreducible, nonexplosive in physical time,
and positive recurrent at every fixed positive finite rate vector by the
exponential Foster proof in
leaves/exponential_foster_outside_gain/RESULT.txt. This covers an exact
three-species non-complex-balanced fixture outside the old strict linear
small-gain LP.

The higher-ceiling extension is
leaves/geometric_rate_box.txt: the multiplicative potential
V=A+(1+1_{A=0})sum_i R^b_i has a common LV<=C-cV drift over any compact
rate box bounded away from zero. The claimed consequence is uniform
stationary tightness, uniform substrate exponential moments, and a uniform
first catalyst moment. No uniform mixing claim is made. The additive
indicator theorem alone does not give those exponential stationary
moments.
