T27 Frobenius / evaluation-place nonconcentration — frozen Phase-IV record v1
2026-10-09

Status: internally derived conditional theorem, independently audited against the source-reported BSSZ function-field construction and T09's selected-place argument. The source construction, curve sequence, and numerical rates remain source-reported; no independent audit of BSSZ's long proof or novelty/priority search is claimed. This record freezes one structural principle and its complete finite-field specialization. It is not a result about arbitrary near-extremizers.

1. Target and theorem

Use Riemann–Roch evaluation at a selected closed point to strengthen the affine-subfield nonconcentration of the BSSZ q=1024 construction past the scalar-action bound. The selected place is chosen by averaging rational-function ratio incidences, not by assuming its evaluation code is generic.

Conditional on BSSZ arXiv:2605.28781v1, Theorem 7.8 and its curve sequence, there is a sequence of prime degrees m_i, curves C_i/F_q, and finite sets B_i⊂F_{q^{m_i}}, q=1024, such that, for all sufficiently large i, writing g_i=g(C_i), n_i=|B_i|, Q_i=q^{m_i},

  m_i/g_i → 85,
  Q_i^0.45 ≤ n_i ≤ Q_i^0.48,
  |B_i+B_i| ≤ n_i^1.89,     |B_iB_i| ≤ n_i^1.91,

and for every proper subfield K⊊F_{q^{m_i}}, every a∈F_{q^{m_i}}, and every ξ∈F_{q^{m_i}}^×,

  |B_i ∩ (a+ξK)| ≤ n_i^{2/3}.                         (1)

The exponents 0.45 and 0.48 are deliberately rounded inward from the source rate interval 0.4594399… to 0.4712561…. The affine-coset exponent 2/3 is also conservative: the incidence exponents below give 0.662958… with an exponential margin. The base subfield F_q has cosets of size at most q=1024, so its fixed scalar orbit is compatible with (1) for large i. In particular this does not assert |B∩(a+ξK)|≤|K|^{1/2} for K=F_q.

2. Source input and parameter ledger

BSSZ, T. F. Bloom, W. Sawin, C. Schildkraut, D. Zhelezov, “The sum-product conjecture is false for real numbers,” arXiv:2605.28781v1 (27 May 2026), HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28781v1, §7.3, Theorem 7.8, equations (7.18)–(7.23), states the function-field input used here. For q=1024, α=34, β=40.53 satisfy α>√q+1=33; the theorem's characteristic-2 threshold (7.20) gives strict bounds a_*<1.89 and b_*<1.91. Its construction uses curves with |C_i(F_q)|/g_i→31 and sets A_i=P_iG_i in H^0(C_i,L_i), then identifies their sections with rational functions using a common nonzero section.

Put L=log_q(1−q^{-1}) and F_q(x,y)=(x+y)log_q(x+y)−xlog_q x−ylog_q y. The source's size rates, with the stated parameters, are

  ρ_- = F_q(β,31)+α+62L−2
      = 39.05239901777024556466578652655…,
  ρ_+ = α−1+31L+F_q(β,31)
      = 40.05676868555972676064218719802… .

Thus log_q n_i/g_i lies eventually between ρ_-−o(1) and ρ_++o(1). The source thresholds evaluate to

  a_* = 1.8828548783018735… < 1.89,
  b_* = 1.9083157208793110… < 1.91.

The common pole divisor D_i has degree d_i=(α+β+o(1))g_i=(74.53+o(1))g_i. A prime m_i with m_i/g_i→85 exists by the prime number theorem; for large i, m_i>d_i. A degree-m_i closed point has residue field F_{q^{m_i}}. It is not in supp(D_i), and evaluation is injective on L(D_i): a nonzero section/function vanishing there would have at least m_i zeros while its pole divisor has degree at most d_i<m_i.

For m_i prime and >5, the proper subfields of E_i=F_{2^{10m_i}}=F_{q^{m_i}} are F_{2^e} for e∈{1,2,5,10,m_i,2m_i,5m_i}. The fields for e=1,2,5 lie in F_q and have size at most 32; e=10 is F_q and has size q. The three proper fields not contained in F_q are

  K_1=F_{2^{m_i}}, K_2=F_{2^{2m_i}}, K_5=F_{2^{5m_i}},
  |K_j|=q^{jm_i/10},   K_j∩F_q=F_{2^j}   (j=1,2,5).

This field-lattice enumeration is essential: treating E_i only as a prime-degree extension of F_q would omit K_1,K_2,K_5.

3. Selected-place incidence lemma

Let C/F_q be smooth, projective, geometrically connected of genus g, let D be an effective divisor of degree d, and let A be a set of n distinct elements of L(D)⊂F_q(C). Let m be prime, coprime to 10, with m>d, and let S_m be the degree-m closed points of C. For v∈S_m evaluation is injective on L(D). For an ordered triple (f_1,f_2,f_3)∈A^3 with f_1≠f_2 and f_3≠f_1, define

  r_{123}=(f_3−f_1)/(f_2−f_1) ∈ F_q(C).

If r_{123} is nonconstant, its morphism C→P^1 has degree at most 2d. Indeed, each difference is in L(D); the pole divisor of a quotient u/v is bounded in degree by deg(u)_∞+deg(v)_0≤2d. (For a common line-bundle presentation the sharper bound d also follows, but is not needed.)

For any finite subfield K⊂F_{q^m}, the number of v∈S_m for which r_{123}(v)∈K is at most

  2d |K|/m.                                           (2)

Proof: for each y∈K the geometric fiber r_{123}^{−1}(y) has at most deg(r_{123})≤2d points. Hence at most 2d|K| geometric points above K. Every degree-m closed point contributes exactly m geometric Frobenius-conjugate points, and K is stable under q-Frobenius, so dividing by m gives (2). Poles do not contribute because only finite y∈K are counted. The degree-m place is outside supp(D), so the denominator f_2−f_1 does not vanish at v by evaluation injectivity.

Let Z(v) count incidences (K_j,(f_1,f_2,f_3)) for j=1,2,5 where r_{123} is nonconstant and r_{123}(v)∈K_j. There are at most n^3 ordered triples. From (2),

  Σ_{v∈S_m} Z(v) ≤ (2d n^3/m)(|K_1|+|K_2|+|K_5|)
                   ≤ (6d n^3/m) q^{m/2}.

Since m is prime, Weil's bound gives

  m|S_m|=|C(F_{q^m})|−|C(F_q)|
       ≥ q^m−q−2g(q^{m/2}+q^{1/2}) ≥ q^m/2

for all sufficiently large i when m/g→85. Therefore |S_m|≥q^m/(2m), and averaging yields some v∈S_m with

  Z(v) ≤ 12 d n^3 q^{−m/2}.                         (3)

This is the Frobenius/Riemann–Roch place-selection principle: large affine-subfield intersections create many bounded-degree rational-function values in proper subfields; each such value can occur at only a small fraction of degree-m places. It applies simultaneously to all affine translates and scalar dilates, without a generic-code assumption.

4. From incidences to affine-coset intersections

Fix one v satisfying (3), put B=ev_v(A), and let K be one of K_1,K_2,K_5. Write I=F_q∩K and h=|I|=2,4,32 respectively. Suppose C_0=B∩(a+ξK) has t elements. Every ordered pair x_1≠x_2 in C_0 determines the I-affine line x_1+(x_2−x_1)I, which contains at most h elements. For any x_3 outside that line,

  (x_3−x_1)/(x_2−x_1) ∈ K\I.

Lift x_j=ev_v(f_j) uniquely to f_j∈A. If r_{123} were a constant c∈F_q, then c=r_{123}(v)∈K forces c∈I, contradicting x_3 outside the I-line. Thus r_{123} is nonconstant and is counted by Z(v). Distinct ordered triples are distinct incidences. Consequently

  t(t−1)(t−h) ≤ Z(v) ≤ 12d n^3 q^{−m/2}.             (4)

This also handles the potentially problematic constant ratios exactly: constants in F_q can belong to K only through I and only generate the h-point I-line already removed. If t≤h there is nothing to prove.

For K=F_q, every affine coset has exactly q elements, so |B∩(a+ξF_q)|≤q directly. Subfields inside F_q have coset size ≤32. There are no other proper subfields by the enumeration in §2.

5. Exponent consequence at m/g→85

With d/g→74.53 and n≤q^{(ρ_++o(1))g}, the right side of (4) is at most

  q^{(3ρ_+−85/2+o(1))g}=q^{(77.6703060567…+o(1))g}.

Meanwhile n^2≥q^{(2ρ_-−o(1))g}=q^{(78.1047980355…−o(1))g}. The gap is positive, 0.4344919789… in the exponent. Thus for sufficiently large i, the right side of (4) is <n_i^2. If t>n_i^{2/3}, then t(t−1)(t−h)>n_i^2 for large i, a contradiction. More precisely the exponential rates allow any

  γ > (ρ_+−85/6)/ρ_- = 0.6629580428…

as an eventual exponent in t≤n^γ (subject to choosing a fixed γ strictly above the displayed rate). We record the simpler γ=2/3 in (1).

The size interval from the same rates is

  ρ_-/85=0.4594399884… ≤ liminf (log_Q n),
  limsup (log_Q n) ≤ ρ_+/85=0.4712561022… .

Hence Q^0.45≤n≤Q^0.48 eventually. The original low-growth exponents remain a=1.89 and b=1.91. This is a substantial improvement over the elementary scalar-action bound n^{1.91/2}=n^0.955, while leaving a near-half-density extension-field construction.

There is a continuous tradeoff. If m/g→λ>d/g, the same estimate gives t≲q^{(ρ_+−λ/6+o(1))g} when the exponent is positive; when λ>6ρ_+=240.3406121…, (3) is <1 and all non-base proper affine-subfield cosets have at most h≤32 points. This stronger constant-intersection regime costs ambient extension degree λ≈242 and reduces n to roughly Q^{0.16}. The λ=85 choice gives the useful balance (1).

6. Evaluation preserves the construction

Every A_i and A_i+A_i lies in L(D_i). Since m_i>d_i, evaluation is injective there, so |B_i|=|A_i| and |B_i+B_i|=|A_i+A_i|≤n_i^{1.89}. The product map only needs to be a homomorphism: |B_iB_i|≤|A_iA_i|≤n_i^{1.91}; no injectivity on L(2D_i) is asserted. Since A_i is F_q^×-stable, F_q^×B_i=B_i. The base-field coset bound q is therefore sharp up to one point for nonzero b∈B_i, and (1) correctly leaves that fixed-size obstruction in place.

7. Scope, checks, and unresolved points

• This is a conditional specialization of the source function-field construction, with an internally derived selected-place argument. BSSZ Theorem 7.8 and the asymptotically optimal curve sequence are source-reported here, not independently re-proved.
• The proof accounts for all proper subfields of F_{2^{10m}}, and does not infer nonconcentration merely from prime degree over F_q.
• The averaging counts all nonconstant ratios at once; no union bound over affine cosets is needed. The ordered-triple count handles their continuum-of-descriptions issue exactly.
• The conclusion (1) is stronger than the scalar-symmetry square-root estimate for the three large non-base subfields. For the base field F_q, the scalar orbit of a nonzero element has 1023 points, so a universal |K|^{1/2} conclusion is impossible.
• T09's earlier m/g≈79, α=33.01 cardinality setup targeted a different size interval; for the present α=34 construction m/g=85 is required for the stated n^{2/3} incidence consequence. The condition follows from the exact exponent gap above, not from the threshold m/2>ρ_+ alone.
• No claim is made that this bounds intersections of arbitrary sets with small sum/product growth; it uses the bounded-degree function-field representation.
• The incidence method can be used at λ≈242 to force |B∩(a+ξK)|≤32 for non-base K. That regime is mathematically valid but carries a large ambient-field cost and is not the main theorem.
• No novelty, priority, or external-validation claim is made. BSSZ §7.2–7.3 explicitly observes that a finite-field reduction in small characteristic would require checking distance from subfields (Theorem 7.8 discussion, around HTML lines 577–579); this record supplies a candidate quantitative bridge for the stated construction, subject to full source verification and priority search.
