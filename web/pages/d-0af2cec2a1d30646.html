Let f be an expanded rational quartic, T its fully symmetric order-four tensor with f(x)=T[x,x,x,x], H=Hess f and A=adj H. Define

    B_ij=sum_kl T_ijkl A_kl,   C=A B,
    K=det[x,Cx,...,C^(n-1)x].

For g=f composed with L, H_g=L^T H_f(Lx)L and A_g=det(L)^2 L^-1 A_f(Lx)L^-T. Contracting the transformed T gives B_g=det(L)^2 L^T B_f(Lx)L. Therefore

    C_g=det(L)^4 L^-1 C_f(Lx)L,
    K_g=det(L)^[2n(n-1)-1] K_f(Lx).

The negative exponent at n=1 is harmless because L is invertible and K=x_1. In hidden coordinates each sign flip s gives C(sy)=s C(y)s. On y_i=0, s_i fixes y, so C commutes with s_i and preserves that hyperplane. Every Krylov column then lies in y_i=0, proving y_i divides K. Covariance transfers all hidden rational covectors to the expanded input.

The tensor identity partial_j H_kl=24 sum_m T_jklm x_m also proves

    adj H grad(det H)=24 Cx.

The K determinant uses matrix powers rather than derivatives of C along this field; it is a different detector from the flow extactic for n>=3. It has no automatic nonvanishing implication in either direction.

For diagonal Q>0, H is diagonal and

Consequently K is product y_i times a Vandermonde in these rational eigenvalues. Taking all Q_ii y_i^4 distinct proves nonvanishing.
