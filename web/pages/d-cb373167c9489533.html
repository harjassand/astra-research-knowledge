DIRECT CHECKS OF patterns.tex
-----------------------------

1. Bounded image rank, chains and multiplicities (lines63-151).
After leaf deletion and degree-two suppression, the edge-stationary
nonbacktracking walk on a min-degree-three multigraph has individual walk
probability <=(2r)^(-1)2^(-(k-1)). At least half its mass is on inherited
length <=2kCL/r. With k=ceil(log2(8r))+1, there are >4r^2 short walks but
at most4r^2 endpoint pairs. Two distinct expanded reduced paths sharing
endpoints yield a cycle <=4kCL/r. The resulting r<=4Ck bounds r.

Every leaf of the actual path image is an endpoint of the optional
interval path, so there are <=2. Degree-sum accounting then bounds branch
vertices and total branch degree. Indexed path starts mark every circle
component. The decomposition into finitely many chains is legitimate.

Successive traversals of one oriented edge along one indexed immersed
path are separated by a nonempty reduced closed walk, which contains a
cycle of length>=L. Both orientations and all path starts produce the
stated m_e<=2(H/L+K0). Each chain therefore has bounded total traversals.
Lengths and comparison endpoints in [0,CL] give polynomial pattern count.

2. Multiplicity incidence (lines165-191).
At a vertex z, half-edge traversal incidences are paired by all immersed
turns except the a(z) interval endpoints. Any one half-edge cannot be
paired with itself. Hence 2 max_{e incident z}m_e <= sum_{e incident z}m_e
+a(z). Looplessness holds because L>2. A vertex appears in exactly
max_{e incident z}m_e multiplicity levels. Summing gives
sum_j V_j <= H+iota and sum_j E_j=H, so the root factor at j=1 exactly
pays the aggregate surplus: sum_j(V_j-E_j-k_j)<=0. No stagewise sign of
V_j-E_j is asserted or required.

3. Common grid and occurrence matching (lines228-302).
Pigeonhole approximation uses only the bounded number d1 of integer
offsets. The difference of two index vectors gives 1<=u<=N^d1 with
dist(ua/L,Z)<=1/N. For s=L/(uR), therefore dist(a/s,Z)<=R/N.
The lower bound s>=L/[N^d1 sqrt(N)] tends to infinity, while s<=L/R=o(L).
All estimates are uniform once K0,C,I are fixed.

Reflection maps [ks,(k+1)s) to the adjacent grid interval (with endpoint
convention reversed), and integer rounding loses at most two positions.
Choosing kappa to dominate R/N and10/s suffices. A matched portion occupies
more than half of both block occurrences; two proposed partners for one
occurrence would overlap compared positions, contradicting disjointness.
Outside a bounded set of occurrences within O(s) of chain/comparison ends,
an unmatched full block is wholly unpaired. Thus sb_*<=b0+O(s+L/s).

4. Link-word counting and self-links (lines321-380).
Given a word on a parent block, one spanning-tree link fixes >=(1-kappa)s
letters on the child. At most kappa s+1 child letters remain free, yielding
|T|^(kappa s+1) possibilities. Multiplying this conditional bound over a
spanning tree is valid even if many links reuse information.

A translation self-link has nonzero integer step a. Its equality edges
{x,x+a} are distinct: a duplicate reversed equality edge would force2a=0.
Each residue class modulo|a| is a line, so the equality graph is a forest.
At least(1-kappa)s equality edges among h_i<=s+1 positions leave at most
kappa s+1 letter classes. A reflection self-link compares inverse labels
on two intervals whose intersection is nonempty and reflection-invariant.
Its central fixed position forces a self-inverse letter, or its central
adjacent pair forces an unreduced turn. Both are prohibited.

Without a self-link, the block carrying maximum multiplicity z has at
least z-b unmatched-budget-adjusted partners among S-z OTHER occurrences;
each occurrence has at most one partner. Hence2z<=S+b. No assumption about
simple links or comparison independence is present in this argument.

5. Word bins and projected stage labels (lines384-420,474-501).
The square-mass estimate gives both d_i>=delta s/2 and the bin count
exp(2d_i-delta s), uniformly for h_i=s+O(1). The minimum positive turn
weight is1/129, which yields the explicit admissible a0=log129 (see below).
There are O(L/s) blocks and O(s) bins each, so the bin entropy is o(L).

Stage labels are explicitly RESTRICTIONS OF FULL STRING ASSIGNMENTS,
not arbitrary assignments satisfying only the surviving stage constraints.
This exact definition at lines424-433 makes the use of a minimum-bin
block ABSENT from stage j legal: projection cannot increase cardinality.
One may bound all full assignments in its represented link component
using that absent block and then project. Different components can be
counted independently for an upper bound, because cross-component graph
immersion/turn constraints only reduce possibilities. End fragments have
O(s) positions and cost exp(o(L)).

6. Fixed-stage graph expectations (lines435-472).
An active chain of length ell has exactly ell-1 unmarked internal vertices;
the total is E_j-Q_j, including a chain with coincident marked endpoints.
At these vertices, reduced-turn counts are np w+O(1). Marked unfixed
vertices have <=n choices and the marked stage-one root has one choice.
Choosing vertices independently is an upper bound; subsequent injectivity
and type compatibility restrictions reduce the candidate set.

Injective realizations and immersion of the abstract labelled graph ensure
that different abstract undirected edges prescribe distinct matched slot
pairs. Thus the conditioned E_j-edge prescription bound applies. It leaves
n^(V_j-E_j-k_j) p^(-Q_j) times internal turn weights. Q_j is bounded.
Deleting other turn weights increases this product because every positive
weight<=1. Each retained block contributes P(W_i)<=exp(-d_i). This gives
the claimed fixed-stage expectation.

7. Deterministic product, minimum and uniform epsilon (lines503-593).
Every original realization has Z_j>=1 at every stage, so
P(original realized)<=min_j E Z_j<=min_j B_j. No independence of Z_j is
needed. The incidence lemma cancels the aggregate positive n exponent.
For a no-self-link component with d_min=min_i d_i, its summed exponent is
at most (2z-S)d_min-delta s z.
If2z-S<=0, d_min>=delta s/2 gives<=-delta s S/2.
If2z-S>0, use2z-S<=b, d_min<=a0s and z>S/2, giving
<=a0sb-delta s S/2. Self-linked components have no initial-word term.
Summing yields log product B_j <= -delta H/2+a0b0+o(L).
The fixed alphabet and weights therefore permit epsilon chosen before
K0,C,I. The number M of stages is fixed after these parameters are fixed;
the min_j bound exp[-delta H/(4M)] dominates exp(o(L)) patterns.
