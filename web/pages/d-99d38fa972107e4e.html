VERDICT AND SCOPE

No mathematical defect was found in the stated fixed-m regular-cone SU(m)
theorem or in the separately supplied reference-weighted self-score lemma,
under their exact interfaces. This is an independent internal audit with
finite diagnostics, not external validation. The SU(m) conclusion is scoped
to fixed m, supplied nonzero H, fixed 0<c<=C and 0<h0<=h1, and regular labels
cN<=a_i<=CN. A legal encoder/decoder has only a classical label plus one
quantum branch of dimension at most Q, and no uncharged shared quantum side
system. Risk is same-carrier full-state half trace distance, uniformly over
the supplied orbit and h interval.

The checked SU(m) claim is e*_Q comparable to Lambda_a(Q)/N^2 for every
1<=Q<=d and every N>=1, with constants depending on the fixed supplied
interface. Lambda minimizes the full complex-matrix Casimir commutator
energy over rank<=Q, not only sector-preserving or normal matrices. At Q=d,
both sides vanish. No SU(m) rank-profile formula follows from d alone.

WHAT WAS CHECKED

1. Normalization and concentration. With Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2,
the highest-vector mean matrix is xi=diag(l_i/2), |mu|^2=2Tr(xi^2), and
the exact Casimir is C_N=N^2|mu|^2+N beta(l). The root-generator sum on the
highest vector gives the linear term directly. Therefore
sum_A||(T_A/N-mu_A)psi||^2=beta(l)/N exactly. Bounds on l, beta and the
Casimir give the uniform operator bound b used for both X and xi.

2. Matrix-power frame. For X=sum_A t_A tensor T_A/N and
Y_(s,A)=2Tr_fund[(t_A tensor I)X^s], fundamental Parseval gives
sum_A||2Tr_fund[(t_A tensor I)Z]||_2^2<=2||Z||_2^2. Since
||[X,I tensor F]||_2^2=E(F)/(2N^2), ordered commutator telescoping yields
E_(Y_s)(F)<=s^2 b^(2s-2)E(F)/N^2 for every complex matrix F. The Y's are
Hermitian and transform as adjoint tensors; the finite-N Y_2 linear
correction is -m T_A/(4N^2), and cannot be dropped.

3. Central Gram and conditioning. The symmetrized contraction
Z_st=1/2 sum_A(Y_sA Y_tA+Y_tA Y_sA) commutes with the irreducible carrier,
so q G_(N,st)=<psi,Z_st psi> exactly. Highest-vector variance plus an
ordered (noncommuting) power telescoping gives G_N=H/q+O(N^-1/2) with the
displayed fixed constants. H=2 V^T(I-11^T/m)V is the centered
Vandermonde Gram at eta_i=l_i/2. The determinant is exactly
det H=(2^(m-1)/m) product_(i<j)(eta_i-eta_j)^2; regular adjacent gaps give
a uniform positive lower bound. The determinant/trace bound gives a
positive minimum eigenvalue, and the explicit N0 perturbation then yields
G_N>=g0 I and Tr G_N<=g1. No normalized-trace semiclassical limit is
assumed.

4. Full adjoint multiplicity. For every regular integral label a_i>=1,
V_a tensor C^m is multiplicity-free with m distinct summands, by the
one-box/Pieri or shifted-Weyl-alternant argument. Frobenius reciprocity and
C^m tensor (C^m)*=1 plus ad imply adjoint multiplicity m-1 in End(V_a).
The positive (m-1)-by-(m-1) Gram proves the polynomial families are
independent for N>=N0; their count then fills the complete adjoint
isotypic component. No small-N independence is needed.

5. Finite-N patch. Every nontrivial SU(m) irrep has Casimir at least
C_F=(m^2-1)/(2m). The scalar part of a rank-Q matrix obeys
|Tr A|^2<=Q||A||_2^2, so Lambda_a(Q)>=C_F(1-Q/d)>=C_F/d for Q<d.
The Weyl dimension product gives d<=(CN+1)^(m(m-1)/2). Thus the reset
channel's error<=1 is covered by the stated C_small for all N<N0, while
the identity channel handles Q=d. This patch does not assume a conditioned
small-N frame.

6. Operational transfer. A rank-Q Kraus family is exactly the channel
class with unrestricted classical labels and per-branch quantum dimension
Q, provided no other quantum side system is free. The arbitrary-code
converse uses the nonunital-safe self-score identity and Gibbs
divided-difference lower bound; it charges every Kraus rank and no
sector-preservation. For the upper, a positive Casimir minimizer exists by
the two-modulus SVD identity. The covariant double-commutator channel uses
the complete adjoint frame; its coefficient solve is qd G theta=v, and
the normalized Gram gives trace-norm change linear in E(F), not
sqrt(E(F)). Applying the derivation rule twice to ordered Gibbs powers
counts k double-derivative terms and k(k-1) cross terms. Since fixed
C_B powers pull outside the Haar integral in the first class, their trace
norms are bounded by ||D_F(C_B)||_1; cross terms use Schatten Holder.
The exponential series therefore controls the full density, including
noncommuting higher terms. This is existential: it does not give an
algorithm to find the positive rank-energy minimizer.

7. Generalizations and boundaries. Section 13 is a proved conditional
principle once p homogeneous equivariant polynomial maps with a supplied
uniformly positive classical value Gram are given. It does not supply
those maps for every simple type. Section 14's fixed product-of-SU-factors
extension requires a nonzero Gibbs component in every factor; H=(H1,0)
gives exact preparation of the maximally mixed second factor at Q=d1 while
the total Casimir rank minimum is positive, falsifying unweighted transfer
without that condition. No semisimple profile is inferred.

REFERENCE-WEIGHTED SELF-SCORE LEMMA

The separate short lemma ROOT_REFERENCE_WEIGHTED_SELF_SCORE.txt also
survives independent algebraic audit. For [rho_x,sigma]=0, sigma>0,
L_x=sigma^(-1/2)rho_x sigma^(-1/2)<=M I, B_i=A_i sigma^(1/2), and
C=int L_x^2, direct Kraus expansion gives

  D/2 = int Tr[L_x(rho_x-Phi(rho_x))]
          + (1/2)Tr[C(Phi(sigma)-sigma)],
  D=sum_i int ||[L_x,B_i]||_2^2.

Here I<=C<=MI. The effect L_x/M bounds the first term by M e; the effect
(C-I)/(M-1), trace-distance contraction, and averaging bound the
reference term by (M-1)e/2. Thus e>=D/(3M-1). If Phi(sigma)=sigma or C
is scalar, e>=D/(2M). M=1 forces a constant ensemble. The derivation uses
neither unitality nor sector preservation; the variational rank minimum
must include all cross-sector blocks. The resource conclusion needs the
code to factor through the charged Q-dimensional branch alone. Exact
two-sector rational fixtures confirm a nonunital cross-sector reset and
a nonunital channel preserving non-scalar sigma.

LIMITATIONS / NEXT DECISIVE GATES

The proof constants are exceptionally loose. One receipt estimates
N0 about 1.37e19 at m=3,c=C=1; this is illustrative floating-point
evaluation, not an exact ceiling. The theorem is eventually asymptotic at
fixed interface, but this explicit cutoff is not a practical compiler
threshold. The finite Choi cubature has <=d^4+1 nodes only existentially;
optimizer, nodes, weights, circuit precision, acquisition, and bit costs
are not supplied. The all-types polynomial-frame input, any growing-m
uniformity, wall crossover, iid-sector transfer, and general SU(m) rank
profile remain open. Classical tensor-operator/matrix-power machinery is
prior art (Gould 1978; Isaac-Werry-Gould 2014); the quantum-memory/Kraus
rank criterion is prior work (Bisio et al. 2012). No priority claim is
made for the internal theorem increment.
