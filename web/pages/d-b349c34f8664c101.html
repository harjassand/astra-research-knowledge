# Paneth-niche renewal in aged intestine: quantitative gate, not a new biological result

## Verdict

The strongest distinct aging/repair target found is the 2025 primary study of old-mitochondria-enriched intestinal stem cells and α-ketoglutarate (αKG)-driven Paneth-cell renewal. It is a strong, recent published mechanism, but this audit establishes no new biological finding, no priority, and no general rejuvenation result. The work product is a quantitative obstruction to inferring tissue repair from pooled cell-renewal readouts, plus a symbolic homeostatic flux bound and a causal-mediation rejection for the current aggregate design.

The high-ceiling question is whether a rare, metabolically defined ISC subpopulation can replace enough aged Paneth-cell niche function across the *crypts that need repair*, while keeping the stem-cell support system intact. This is distinct from the prior 53BP1 lesion-partition and IFNγ/chromatin-selection routes.

## Primary mechanism and actual interface

Andersson et al. (2025) report that about 9% of Lgr5-high mouse ISCs are enriched for old mitochondria, commonly 1–2 cells per crypt. Mitochondria-age-selective asymmetric apportioning occurs in about 15% of observed divisions and is localized to positions +3/+4. The old-mitochondria-enriched cells have higher αKG and TET-linked methylation changes, and show greater early niche-independent organoid initiation. Their advantage is transient in the organoid assay: at the early Paneth-cell bottleneck there were about three times as many Paneth-containing organoids, while by day 10 mature organoids had similar Paneth counts. The paper explicitly argues that deterministic Paneth fate after every age-selective division would exceed the observed slow Paneth turnover; its interpretation is biased, stochastic fate, not a fixed asymmetric division outcome.

In aged mice, the paper reports that repeated dm-αKG increased the EdU-positive Paneth-cell fraction after three weeks; Notum transcript was lower in newly made EdU-positive Paneth cells than in pre-existing EdU-negative cells. In a separate cohort, two weeks of prophylactic dm-αKG before one 100 mg/kg 5-FU challenge reduced weight loss and restored ileal villus-cell density. These are source-reported results in aged mice, not human data. The intervention is systemic; αKG can affect cells outside the ISC-to-Paneth path.

EdU is a DNA-synthesis/renewal reporter. Lgr5-EGFP identifies an ISC-enriched population; mitochondrial SNAP substrates operationally label age classes as ≥48-hour versus ≤8-hour. Paneth markers and Notum qPCR are state/function proxies. None is a direct measure of functional support delivered by every crypt. The 5-FU injection is the acute insult; weight and villus-cell density are outcomes, with the latter more proximal to epithelial repair. The paper does not quantify local 5-FU injury burden and repair as a same-crypt trajectory.

## Reconstructed quantitative obstruction

The source’s deterministic-fate objection can be written as a one-crypt event budget. Let (D) be the daily number of relevant +3/+4 ISC divisions per crypt; (a\approx0.15) the observed asymmetric-apportionment fraction; (P) the mature Paneth pool per crypt; and \(\tau_P\) the mean Paneth residence time under a stationary replacement model. If an age-biased daughter has probability (p) of producing mature Paneth cells and output (c) conditional on that fate, then

\[
  aDpc\leq P/\tau_P,
  \qquad p\leq P/(aDc\tau_P).
\]

This is a necessary mass-balance condition. If the upper bound is below one, deterministic commitment is impossible. It does not produce a numerical commitment probability because the focal paper does not report the position-specific division flux, Paneth residence-time distribution, and lineage output (c) together. Inserting “57 days” as an exponential mean would be unjustified: the cited primary Ireland et al. paper reports up to 57 days to inherit a reporter, and older nucleotide-label studies give shorter turnover estimates.

The second obstruction is fully constructive. Suppose a crypt is functionally supported once at least a fraction \(\tau\) of its Paneth cells has been replaced. If (R_i\in[0,1]) is the per-crypt replacement fraction and only the pooled mean \(q=\mathbb E R_i\) is known, then the fraction \(\pi=\Pr(R_i\geq\tau)\) is bounded only by

\[
  \max(0,(q-\tau)/(1-\tau))\leq\pi\leq\min(1,q/\tau),
\]

up to threshold-boundary attainment. For (q=\tau=1/2), the closure of the identified range is ([0,1]). A 100-crypt, equal-size exact toy counterexample keeps the pooled EdU-positive Paneth fraction, total cell count, mean Notum, and mean Notum in sorted EdU-positive and EdU-negative pools identical, but changes the fraction crossing a local Notum threshold from 100% to 50%. The normalized old/new Notum values and threshold are synthetic; they are not extracted from the paper. The calculation shows exactly what pooled readouts fail to identify.

Under a constant-hazard model, the pooled EdU fraction over a window (T) is \(\mathbb E[1-e^{-\lambda_iT}]\), one Laplace-transform value for the heterogeneous crypt renewal-rate distribution. It is not the mean rate. In a synthetic 21-day fixture, the same pooled label fraction 0.5 is compatible with mean hazards 0.0330/day and 0.0512/day. Jensen gives a lower bound on mean hazard, not a rate estimate.

The exact derivations and finite arithmetic are in [quantitative_obstructions.md](proofs/quantitative_obstructions.md) and [crypt_repair_identifiability.py](models/crypt_repair_identifiability.py). The generated JSON labels all figures as synthetic, not biological data.

## Mediation and decisive intervention

The source measures EdU/Notum in one set of animals and 5-FU recovery in another; it does not report a selective block of the proposed mediator during the recovery experiment. Two causal models therefore remain compatible with the reported treatment contrasts: (i) dm-αKG improves repair through ISC αKG/TET activity, new low-Notum PCs, and local stem-cell Wnt support; or (ii) it increases the same PC-renewal readout while a direct epithelial or systemic dm-αKG effect independently improves recovery. The exact group-mean alias is in [mediation_alias.md](counterexamples/mediation_alias.md).

The strongest next experiment is a matched aged-mouse factorial test of dm-αKG versus vehicle and a validated, selective mechanism block or clamp—such as blocking the ISC-to-PC TET-dependent route, or holding Notum high in new Paneth cells—followed by the same 5-FU challenge. It must measure target engagement, renewal, Notum/Wnt activity, and epithelial injury/recovery per crypt. The prior Notum study already establishes that manipulating the Paneth–Notum–Wnt axis can affect aged intestinal regeneration; the missing link is whether this particular dm-αKG benefit requires the renewal mechanism.

Use vehicle plus the same blocker as the equal-information control, with matched drug handling, age, injury dose, sampling, and reporter acquisition. If a selective blockade prevents the proposed PC/Notum change but leaves the dm-αKG recovery advantage intact, the route is not necessary for that benefit. If renewed low-Notum PCs appear but do not predict per-crypt Wnt/stem activity or epithelial recovery, the simple local-threshold model fails. A direct loss-of-crypt or barrier measure should accompany body weight because weight is affected by hydration and intake as well as epithelial repair.

Costs include aged mice (the paper defines old as ≥18 months), repeated high-dose dm-αKG injections, 5-FU, EdU, and lineage/spatial measurements. The focal study used 600 mg/kg intraperitoneal dm-αKG, with the injury figure specifying once-daily prophylaxis for two weeks; the general methods specify twice daily unless a figure states otherwise. The injury dose was 100 mg/kg i.p. once. The additional mechanism-block arms and per-crypt assays raise animal and measurement costs; no reliable money, duration, or sample-size estimate can be made without pilot variance and clustering data.

## Prior boundary and status

Pentinmikko et al. (2019) already report that aged Paneth-cell Notum suppresses Wnt and that Notum targeting improves aged intestinal regeneration. The broad Paneth/Notum aging mechanism is prior art. The 2025 αKG/organelle-age results are also published primary results. This report does not claim that the focal study is wrong; it narrows the unmeasured link between its aggregate renewal readouts and mechanism-specific recovery. A 2025 primary study of old peroxisomes and G6PD supports self-renewal in mammary and epidermal stem cells, so organelle-age fate effects cannot be generalized across tissues or organelles.

**Final status:** conditional model theorem plus exact synthetic counterexample; mediation of the published 5-FU benefit by Paneth renewal remains `UNKNOWN`; biological novelty and priority remain `UNKNOWN`. This is acute mouse injury recovery, not proof of baseline rejuvenation, lifespan extension, clinical benefit, or tissue-wide restoration.
