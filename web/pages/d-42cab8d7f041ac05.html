CYCLE 5 — HOSTILE MIXED-COFACTOR THRESHOLD-FACE AUDIT
======================================================
Status: exact counterexample to the proposed Q-weighted Lyapunov template's
strict margin conditions and Foster drift. This is a certificate failure,
not a recurrence counterexample: the exhibited network is detailed balanced
and has an irreducible product-Poisson stationary law.

Runnable exact fixture:
  verify_threshold_ray.py

Run from the repository root with:
  python3 outputs/research/reaction_dynamics/cycle5/mixed_cofactor_extension/leaf_hostile/verify_threshold_ray.py

The fixture uses Fraction arithmetic, directly evaluates the mass-action
generator of the displayed potential, independently evaluates the closed
form margins, and asserts exact equality and positive LV at n=16 and n=32.

1. NETWORK AND PROPOSED MARGINS
------------------------------
Species are A, B, C1, C2. Use these reversible mass-action pairs:

  0 <-> A                         rates 1, 1*a
  0 <-> C1                        rates 1, 1*c1
  0 <-> C2                        rates 2, 2*c2
  A <-> A+B                       rates 1*a, 1*a*b
  A+2C1+2C2 <-> 2C1+2C2           rates 1*a*(c1)_2*(c2)_2,
                                         1*(c1)_2*(c2)_2
  A+C2 <-> C2                     rates 1*a*c2, 1*c2

Here (x)_r=x(x-1)...(x-r+1). Every rate constant is strictly positive and
rational. The two toggle cofactor vectors are nu_1=(2,2) and nu_2=(0,1).
The mixed reverse-toggle intensity and its forward-A counterpart are

  Q(c1,c2)=(c1)_2(c2)_2+c2,
  E(c1,c2)=(c1)_2(c2)_2+c2=Q(c1,c2),
  u(c)=1/(1+Q(c)).

For independent C immigration/death, use the mixed versions of the parent
margin definitions:

  H(c)=sum_{k:c_k>0} mu_k c_k [Q(c)-Q(c-e_k)]
       /[(1+Q(c-e_k))(1+Q(c))],
  J_+(c)=sum_k gamma_k [Q(c+e_k)-Q(c)]
       /[(1+Q(c))(1+Q(c+e_k))],
  theta(c)=[alpha+Q(c)]/(1+Q(c))+J_+(c)-H(c),
  kappa_A(c)=[delta+E(c)]/(1+Q(c))-H(c).

The proposed potential is the mixed-cofactor analogue

  V=1+c1+c2+a*u(c)+R^b[1+1_{a=0}u(c)]+1_{a+c1+c2=0},
  R=6/5.

2. THRESHOLD-FACE RAY AND EXACT LIMIT
-------------------------------------
Take x_n=(a,b,c1,c2)=(0,n,n,2), n>=2. Then

  Q(x_n)=2n(n-1)+2 -> infinity,
  Q(x_n-e_C2)=Q(n,1)=1.

Thus one C2 death changes Q from quadratic growth to the fixed value 1.
Writing Q_n=2n(n-1)+2 and Delta_2=Q_n-1, its H contribution is exactly

  H_2(x_n)
    = 2*2*Delta_2 / [(1+1)(1+Q_n)]
    = 2 Delta_2/(1+Q_n)
    -> 2.

The other death contribution vanishes:

  Q(n-1,2)=2(n-1)(n-2)+2,
  Delta_1=4(n-1),
  H_1=n*4(n-1)/[(1+Q(n-1,2))(1+Q_n)] -> 0.

Both immigration contributions vanish. In particular,

  Q(n+1,2)-Q(n,2)=4n,
  Q(n,3)-Q(n,2)=4n(n-1)+1,

and each corresponding J denominator is quadratic in Q_n, hence
J_+(x_n)->0. Since alpha=delta=1 and E=Q,

  [alpha+Q_n]/(1+Q_n)=1,
  [delta+E]/(1+Q_n)=1,
  theta(x_n)->-1,  kappa_A(x_n)->-1.

Consequently the proposed hypotheses inf(theta)>0 and inf(kappa_A)>0 fail.
This falsifies the direct mixed-cofactor extension of the separate-catalyst
margin theorem.

3. FULL GENERATOR FALSIFIER, NOT JUST A BAD MARGIN
-------------------------------------------------
At x_n the A-production channels have total rate alpha+Q_n. Each such jump
changes a*u(c) by u(c), contributing (alpha+Q_n)u(c)=1. The inactive
correction 1_{a=0}u(c) has generator exactly -theta(c), so its contribution
to V is -theta(x_n) R^n. The weighted C drift is

  (gamma1-mu1*n)+(gamma2-mu2*2)=(1-n)+(2-4)=-n-1.

The A-to-A+B edge is disabled at a=0. The origin indicator is zero and no
one-step jump from x_n reaches its support. Therefore the exact full drift is

  LV(x_n)=1-theta(x_n)R^n-n-1
         =-n-theta(x_n)(6/5)^n.

As theta(x_n)->-1, LV(x_n)~(6/5)^n-n -> +infinity. The script directly
enumerates every reaction propensity and potential jump and confirms this
identity with exact Fractions. For example it verifies theta,kappa_A<0 at
n=8,16,32 and LV>0 at n=16,32. This is an all-rate, exact failure of Foster
drift for the proposed V, not merely failure of a conservative margin
bound.

The network is detailed balanced at unit activities: every displayed
forward/reverse pair has equal equilibrium flux at (a,b,c1,c2)=(1,1,1,1).
The independent immigration/death pairs make the class irreducible, and the
product-Poisson law with all means 1 is stationary. Nonexplosion also follows
directly by conditioning on the nonexplosive immigration/death C paths: on
each finite time interval C is bounded, A is a linear birth-death chain with
bounded coefficients, and conditional on A, B has finite time-varying
immigration and linear death rates. Thus the example attacks the candidate
Lyapunov correction, not recurrence or nonexplosion.

4. WHAT A FINITE FACE/TIER CERTIFICATE MUST TRACK
------------------------------------------------
A single max-coordinate cutoff and the separable bound H=O(M^{-d}) are
unsound for mixed cofactors: here M=max(c1,c2)=n but H->2. The decisive
feature is not a large-coordinate approximation error; C2 remains at the
threshold c2=2 while its death deletes the only monomial growing in c1.

For a fixed finite list nu_j, define N_k=max_j nu_{jk}. There are finitely
many one-jump support patterns. They must be computed separately for Q(c),
Q(c-e_k), and Q(c+e_k), not inferred from Q(c) alone:

  * death thresholds c_k=t in 1,...,N_k can delete every currently large
    monomial containing k (this fixture has t=2);
  * immigration thresholds c_k=t-1 can activate a previously dormant large
    monomial, making J_+ have a nonzero limit even if Q(c) is bounded;
  * for c_k>N_k, no monomial is switched on/off by one jump. For an active
    monomial, phi_j(c-e_k)/phi_j(c)=(c_k-nu_jk)/c_k and
    Delta_k/phi_j=nu_jk/c_k. Thus its death correction vanishes
    on an escaping ray whenever that active monomial makes Q grow; if no
    active monomial uses k, Delta_k=0.

For fixed finite input, there is a finite threshold/support decomposition
that can support an exact rational certificate, but it is richer than a
single max-count cutoff. Let N_k=max_j nu_jk. When c_k>N_k, every active
monomial survives a C_k death and the falling-factorial identities give

  Delta_k(c) <= N_k Q(c)/c_k,
  Q(c-e_k) >= Q(c)/(N_k+1),
  H_k(c) <= mu_k N_k(N_k+1)/(1+Q(c)).

When 1<=c_k<=N_k, instead use the exact threshold bound

  H_k(c) <= mu_k c_k/(1+Q(c-e_k)).

Thus high-coordinate death terms vanish when Q grows. A bounded-coordinate
death term vanishes if its post-death Q grows; otherwise its surviving
monomials avoid all escaping coordinates and its limit is a rational
threshold-face expression. Immigration terms satisfy

  0 <= J_k(c) <= gamma_k/(1+Q(c)),

so they vanish when Q grows. When Q stays bounded but Q(c+e_k) grows, the
only nonzero immigration limit occurs when c_k=nu_jk-1 activates a previously
inactive channel, and equals gamma_k/(1+Q_face). This is why both
post-death and post-immigration support patterns must be kept.

The remaining base terms have exact bounds

  [alpha+Q]/(1+Q) >= min(alpha,1),
  [delta+E]/(1+Q) >= min(delta, min_j eta_j/zeta_j).

For a fixed finite grammar, enumerate the finite threshold values, active
channel subsets under c and c+/-e_k, and which shifted Q polynomials are
bounded or grow on each escaping-coordinate face. A shifted Q grows exactly
when one of its active positive monomials contains an escaping coordinate;
otherwise its value depends only on bounded gate coordinates. If a bounded
shifted Q is below a chosen rational cutoff, positivity of its active
monomials bounds every coordinate used by those monomials; inactive channels
are governed by the finite gate thresholds. This leaves a finite enumeration.
If the shifted Q exceeds the cutoff, the displayed H bound makes its
contribution as small as prescribed. For Q->infinity, E/Q is a weighted
average of eta_j/zeta_j; the minimum over a face is found by its active
channels, with a finite Newton-fan refinement if one needs to exclude
unrealizable dominant subsets. These steps produce finitely many rational
face limits/bounds, followed by an exact finite-box check. Strict positive
face margins permit choosing rational tail cutoffs and give a finite
sufficient certificate.

Answer to the decomposition question: yes, a finite pattern/tier scheme
can suffice for exact rational *sufficient margin certification* for fixed
finite species and reaction vectors, provided it tracks the one-step
support changes and supplies explicit tail bounds. The bare threshold
pattern without shifted-Q analysis is insufficient, and the old generic
all-large H=O(M^{-d}) estimate is falsified here. This leaf has not
implemented or independently proved a general terminating compiler for
arbitrary mixed inputs; it records the finite strata and the inequalities
such a compiler must check. Failure of this V or of a conservative
certificate is not itself a recurrence obstruction.
