2. EXACT PRODUCT-ORBIT TRANSPORT OPERATOR

For a unit vector n apply g_u=exp(u n.sigma/2) to a normalized qubit.
If its Bloch vector is m=tanh(|y|)y/|y|, then
dm/du=n-(n.m)m.
Therefore its natural parameter y evolves with velocity B(y)n, where
B(y)=beta(|y|)I+[1-beta(|y|)]ee^T,
beta(z)=z coth z, e=y/|y|, B(0)=I.
For x=y/a, define v_n(x)=a^-1 B(ax)n and
s_n(x)=N tanh(ar)n.x/r.
The unnormalized N-fold branch trace has logarithmic derivative s_n.
Consequently the EXACT matrix identity is
 {F_n,P_N(x)}=v_n(x).grad P_N(x)+s_n(x)P_N(x).

The density adjoint is
T_n f=s_n f-div(v_n f).
Integration by parts gives
integral P_N T_n f= {F_n,integral P_N f}.
Thus the exact mixing-density semigroup has generator
mathcal L_N f=sum_i lambda_i/(4N^(3/2)) T_(n_i)^2 f
                 +(1/(2N^(3/4)))T_b f,                 (1)
where v_b,s_b,T_b are linear in b. Its positive action is not an assumed
P-representation: push each density through the smooth diffeomorphism
x -> a^-1 log-natural-coordinate(g tau_N(x)g*) and multiply by the branch
trace weight [Tr(g tau_N(x)g*)]^N, then average the Gaussian filter paths.
The local boost vector fields are globally Lipschitz and have linear growth.
The local two-by-two linear Stratonovich filter SDE exists on every finite
time interval. Each path is invertible and gives a smooth interior Bloch-ball
diffeomorphism, so the push-forward density and the positive semigroup exist.
Finite Gaussian products and their stochastic limit give (1), including
degenerate C; no ellipticity or eigenvalue gap is required.

Useful explicit bounds follow from elementary coth inequalities:
0<=beta-1<=min(z,z^2/3), 0<=beta'<=1, 0<=beta''<=2/3.
The z^2/3 bound follows by differentiating
(z^2+3)sinh z-3z cosh z; the derivative is
z(z cosh z-sinh z)>=0. The z bound follows from e^(2z)-1>=2z.
beta'=coth z-z csch^2 z is between 0 and 1, and
beta''=2 csch^2 z(beta-1)<=2/3.
Differentiating B=I+(beta-1)(I-ee^T) gives
||B||<=1+z, ||DB||<=3, ||D^2 B||<=7; the more conservative 16
is used for the last two bounds below. For the second derivative use
||D ee^T||<=2/z, ||D^2 ee^T||<=8/z^2,
beta'/z<=2/3 and (beta-1)/z^2<=1/3. Origin limits are smooth.
Hence
|v_n|<=N^(1/4)(r+1/2), ||Dv_n||<=16,
|div v_n|<=48, |grad div v_n|<=48a,
|s_n|<=2N^(3/4)r, |grad s_n|<=2N^(3/4).
The exact divergence is -2(beta(ar)-1)(n.x/r)/(ar), which also directly
checks the removable origin behavior.

|grad f_t|<=G f_t, ||Hess f_t||<=(H0+G^2)f_t.
