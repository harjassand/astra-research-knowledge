BOSON COLLISION ROBUSTNESS IN THE DIRICHLET CRITICAL WINDOW
===========================================================

Date: 2026-10-08. Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE, conditional only on
the explicit closed-form and trace-class hypotheses below. This note gives
the d>=3 hard-projection application and the collective number-sufficient
EB archive. It does not certify external validity, priority, decoder
efficiency, or the separate d=2 Jastrow extension being audited.

1. INTERFACE AND MAIN CLAIM

Fix d>=1, J,tau>0, and an integer collision range R>=0. Let
Lambda_L={1,...,L}^d, with Dirichlet ghosts, and

  h_L=J sum_{a=1}^d (2I-T_a-T_a^*),
  K_L=dGamma(h_L),  N=dGamma(I),  beta_L=tau L^2,
  G_{0,L,lambda}=K_L-(lambda/L^2)N,  lambda<=0,
  rho_{0,L,lambda}=Z_0^{-1} exp(-beta_L G_{0,L,lambda}).

The one-particle energy is

  eps_k=2J sum_a(1-cos(pi k_a/(L+1))),  k in {1,...,L}^d.

For occupation numbers (n_x)_{x in Lambda_L}, define

  C_R=sum_x binom(n_x,2)
      +sum_{x<y: 0<||x-y||_infinity<=R} n_x n_y,
  P_R=1_{C_R=0},  Q_R=I-P_R.

Thus P_R excludes on-site double occupancy and every pair at distinct
lattice distance at most R. For each L, suppose W_L is a nonnegative closed
quadratic form on the bosonic Fock space such that:

  (i) G_{0,L,lambda}+W_L is a well-defined closed form sum;
  (ii) every vector in Ran(P_R) belongs to the form domain of W_L and has
       W_L-form value zero;
  (iii) its Gibbs operator at beta_L is trace class.

No bound on W_L is imposed, and W_L need not commute with N. The domain
condition is essential for unbounded penalties; the shorthand W_L P_R=0
means precisely the zero-form condition on Ran(P_R), with the cross terms
therefore zero by positivity. Write rho_{W,L,lambda} for the Gibbs state of
G_{0,L,lambda}+W_L.

THEOREM. For fixed d>=3, J,tau,R there is C<infinity, independent of L,
lambda<=0, and every such W_L, for which

  D(rho_{W,L,lambda} || rho_{0,L,lambda})
      <= C [L^(2-d)+L^(-d) log(L+1)],

and consequently

  ||rho_{W,L,lambda}-rho_{0,L,lambda}||_1
      <= C [L^(2-d)+L^(-d) log(L+1)]^(1/2).

This is a full-Fock trace-norm statement, uniform over arbitrary positive
collision-supported penalties. The proof below uses a trial state on the
allowed subspace, not a number-conserving interaction decomposition.

2. FREE-STATE ESTIMATES

The normalized sine eigenvectors satisfy

  |phi_k(x)|^2 <= 2^d L^(-d),
  L^2 eps_k >= J |k|^2.

Indeed 1-cos t>=2t^2/pi^2 and L^2/(L+1)^2>=1/4. The gauge-invariant
quasi-free covariance is

  C_L=(exp[tau(L^2 h_L-lambda)]-I)^(-1),
  (C_L)_{xy}=sum_k phi_k(x) overline(phi_k(y))
                    /(exp[tau(L^2 eps_k-lambda)]-1).

Since lambda<=0, the summands are bounded by a summable Gaussian in k.
There are constants c,C depending only on d,J,tau such that, uniformly in
L,lambda,

  Z_0=prod_k(1-exp[-tau(L^2 eps_k-lambda)])^(-1) <= C,
  (C_L)_{xx}<=C L^(-d),
  |(C_L)_{xy}|<=C L^(-d),
  beta_L Tr(rho_0 G_0)<=C,
  Tr(rho_0 exp(aN))<=C_a   for every fixed 0<a<tau Jd.

The covariance off-diagonal bound follows from positivity and Cauchy-Schwarz.
The exponential number moment follows by multiplying the one-mode geometric
generating functions; the Gaussian majorant is summable. It also gives
uniform exponential tails for N and N-weighted tails. These facts guarantee
finite entropy of rho_0 and justify number cutoffs in the arguments below.

Bosonic Wick expansion gives, for each fixed k,

  <a*(x_1)...a*(x_k)a(y_k)...a(y_1)>
      = per[(C_L)_{y_j x_i}]_{i,j=1}^k
      <= k! C^k L^(-dk).

Let Q_{R+1} denote the analogous forbidden-pair projection at range R+1.
The diagonal pair count C_R obeys Q_R<=C_R. There are O_R(L^d) close
distinct pairs. Wick's two-point identity gives

  q_R:=Tr(rho_0 Q_R)<=Tr(rho_0 C_R)<=C L^(-d).

For the number-weighted event, write N=sum_z n_z. If the extra site z is
distinct from a close pair x,y, the three-point normal-ordered expectation
is O(L^(-3d)); summing z gives O(L^(-2d)) per close pair. If z=x or y,
use n_x^2=n_x(n_x-1)+n_x and Wick again; these repeated-index terms are
O(L^(-2d)) per pair. The on-site factorial term is treated identically.
Therefore

  r_{R+1}:=Tr(rho_0 N Q_{R+1})<=C L^(-d).

The constants depend on R but not on L or lambda. The same Wick calculation
shows Tr(rho_0 N C_R)=O(L^-d), which is an alternative upper bound for the
event-weighted quantity.

3. LOCAL KINETIC FORM COST

In a fixed m-particle sector, realize K_L as the sum of m coordinate
Dirichlet graph Laplacians, restricted to symmetric vectors. Let an edge of
the configuration graph join configurations differing by one nearest-

neighbor particle hop. A hop from an allowed configuration into a forbidden
one can only create a pair at distance <=R by moving one particle one step.
Its allowed endpoint therefore lies in Q_{R+1}. Every configuration has at
most 2dm directed one-coordinate hops. For a diagonal filter F with values
in [0,1], the exact commutator entries are

  [F,[K_L,F]]_{u,v}=-(K_L)_{u,v}(F(u)-F(v))^2  (u!=v),
  [F,[K_L,F]]_{u,u}=0.

On each labeled coordinate edge (K_L)_{u,v}=-J, so the entry is
J(F(u)-F(v))^2. The free heat kernel in each fixed-particle labeled
sector is entrywise nonnegative. Positivity of rho_0 and
|rho_0(u,v)|<=sqrt(rho_0(u,u)rho_0(v,v))<=(rho_0(u,u)+rho_0(v,v))/2
give

  Dir(F):=(1/2)Tr(rho_0 [F,[K_L,F]])
      <=(J/2) sum_{unordered {u,v} edge}
                (rho_0(u,u)+rho_0(v,v))(F(u)-F(v))^2.

For the hard projection F=P_R the squared difference is one exactly on
crossing edges. Counting the incident coordinate hops at both endpoints
gives

  Dir(P_R)<=C Jd Tr(rho_0 N Q_{R+1})<=C L^(-d).

No bosonic hopping coefficient is omitted here: the count is in the labeled
coordinate representation on symmetric vectors, where repeated occupation
edges retain their labeled multiplicity. The Wick bound above controls the
same multiplicities through the occupation-number observables.

Since [rho_0,K_L]=0, the exact double-commutator identity yields

  Tr(rho_0 P_R K_L P_R)
      =Tr(rho_0 P_R K_L)+Dir(P_R)
      <=Tr(rho_0 K_L)+Dir(P_R).

For the final inequality, K_L rho_0 is positive and P_R<=I, so
Tr(K_L rho_0 P_R)<=Tr(K_L rho_0). The chemical term
(-lambda/L^2)N is nonnegative and commutes with P_R, so its compressed
expectation is no larger than its free expectation either.

Let p=1-q_R and sigma=P_R rho_0 P_R/p. For all sufficiently large L,
p>=1/2, and

  beta_L[Tr(sigma G_0)-Tr(rho_0 G_0)]
      <= [q_R beta_L Tr(rho_0 G_0)+beta_L Dir(P_R)]/p
      <= C [L^(-d)+L^(2-d)].

4. INFINITE-FOCK ENTROPY COST

Pinch rho_0 by {P_R,Q_R}. Entropy increases under pinching, and the
entropy-of-mixture bound gives

  S(rho_0)-S(sigma)<=h_2(q_R)+q_R S(omega_Q),
  omega_Q=Q_R rho_0 Q_R/q_R.

If q_R=0 this is interpreted by continuity. For S_sites=L^d bosonic modes,
take s=log(S_sites+1). The number reference state

  omega_s=Z_s^(-1) exp(-sN),
  Z_s=Tr exp(-sN)=(1-exp(-s))^(-S_sites)
      =(1+1/S_sites)^(S_sites)<=e

has finite partition function. Positivity of relative entropy, first on the
cutoff N<=M and then by monotone/entropy truncation using the exponential
number moment, gives

  q_R S(omega_Q)<=s Tr(rho_0 N Q_R)+q_R log Z_s
                 <=C L^(-d) log(L+1).

This is a direct Gibbs-variational bound for the entropy of the forbidden
conditional state. It does not use a finite-dimensional Fannes inequality
on infinite Fock space. Combining with the energy bound,

  D(sigma||rho_0)
      =S(rho_0)-S(sigma)
          +beta_L[Tr(sigma G_0)-Tr(rho_0 G_0)]
      <=C [L^(2-d)+L^(-d)log(L+1)].

5. TRANSFER TO AN ARBITRARY UNBOUNDED POSITIVE PENALTY

The form hypotheses ensure that sigma is an admissible trial state for
G_0+W_L and has zero W_L-form expectation. The Gibbs variational principle
therefore gives

  log Z_W >= S(sigma)-beta_L Tr(sigma G_0).

Equivalently, log(Z_0/Z_W)<=D(sigma||rho_0). Also G_0+W_L>=G_0, so
Z_W<=Z_0. To justify the relative-entropy identity at an unbounded form,
min-max gives Z_W(beta_L/2)<=Z_0(beta_L/2)<infinity; then
x exp(-beta_L x)<=C_beta exp(-beta_L x/2) implies finite thermal
expectation of G_0+W_L and of each nonnegative summand. Spectral truncation
then yields

  D(rho_W||rho_0)
      =-beta_L Tr(rho_W W_L)+log(Z_0/Z_W)
      <=log(Z_0/Z_W)
      <=D(sigma||rho_0).

The argument allows arbitrary L-dependent penalty strength and does not
require [W_L,N]=0. It requires a genuine positive closed form sum and a
zero-form allowed subspace; an informal infinite on-site coefficient is
handled as a hard-constraint limit, not as an operator with an unspecified
domain. Pinsker's inequality proves the theorem in Section 1.

6. COLLECTIVE NUMBER-SUFFICIENT EB RECONSTRUCTION

For n copies with a common unknown lambda<=0 and possibly different
admissible W_{L,j}, additivity and the preceding relative-entropy bound give

  D(tensor_j rho_{W_j} || rho_0(lambda)^(tensor n))<=n G_L,
  ||tensor_j rho_{W_j}-rho_0(lambda)^(tensor n)||_1<=sqrt(2 n G_L),

where G_L=C[L^(2-d)+L^(-d)log(L+1)].

Let T=sum_{j=1}^n N_j and K^(n)=sum_j K_{L,j}. The free product state is
proportional to exp[-beta_L K^(n)+tau lambda T]. Conditional on T=t it is
the normalized restriction

  omega_{L,n,t}=P_t exp(-beta_L K^(n))P_t / z_{L,n,t},

which is independent of lambda. Therefore

  Phi_n(X)=sum_{t>=0} Tr(P_t X) omega_{L,n,t}

is one fixed entanglement-breaking measure-and-prepare channel and fixes the
entire free family. Contractivity gives

  ||Phi_n(tensor_j rho_{W_j})-tensor_j rho_{W_j}||_1
       <=2 sqrt(2n G_L).

The free one-copy exponential number moment is uniformly bounded: for any
fixed 0<a<tau Jd,

  C_a:=sup_{L,lambda<=0} Tr(rho_0 exp(aN))<infinity.

Thus Pr_{rho_0^tensor n}(T>R)<=C_a^n exp[-a(R+1)]. Replacing all outcomes
t>R by one overflow symbol and a fixed output state adds at most twice this
free tail to the EB error. It suffices to take

  R=ceil[(n log C_a+log(2/delta))/a].

The classical register has R+2 possible outcomes and uses
ceil(log_2(R+2))=O(log(n+1)+loglog(1/delta)) bits at fixed public constants;
it uses zero persistent quantum memory. The input measurement, preparation
of omega_{L,n,t}, finite-precision compilation, output size, and runtime
are not proved efficient and must be charged separately. If lambda differs
across copies, total T is not sufficient; that interface is excluded.

For fixed epsilon, the full state has vanishing EB reconstruction error
whenever n=o(L^(d-2)) for d>=3. This is a sufficient condition, not a
necessary or optimal distinguishability threshold.

7. LOWER-DIMENSION BOUNDARY AND CLAIM LIMITS

The hard projection estimate yields beta Dir=O(L^(2-d)); it proves no
vanishing relative-entropy bound at d=2 and cannot be used for d=1. This
failure is a limitation of that witness, not a nonconvergence result. A
smoothed logarithmic-capacity witness may improve d=2; its exact discrete
double-commutator, positive-kernel and infinite-Fock entropy estimates are
being independently audited in a separate file. No claim about that branch
is used above.

The theorem compares a low-density critical-window free boson Gibbs state
with arbitrary positive penalties supported on a fixed collision set. It
does not apply to hard-core spins in d>1 via Jordan-Wigner, to attractive
interactions, to positive chemical potentials at or above the first scaled
one-particle energy, or to a growing collision range without changed
constants. No energy-accuracy, state-preparation, circuit-size or novelty
claim follows from trace-norm EB reconstruction.

8. PRIMARY COMPARISON

The argument uses established free-boson quasi-free/Wick and Gibbs
variational tools. Araki and Woods (1963) construct CCR representations for
the infinite free Bose gas; that is not the present finite-box, low-density
critical-scale trace-norm stability theorem:
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1704002 . Shirai and Takahashi (2003) develop
bosonic permanental point fields and their correlation identities; they do
not treat the lattice collision projection or arbitrary-penalty Gibbs
comparison here:
https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-1236(03)00171-X . Schick (1971) studies the
two-dimensional hard-core Bose gas at zero temperature and supplies
physical context for logarithmic 2D behavior, not this finite-temperature
trace-norm bound:
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.3.1067 . The primary-source comparison is
bounded; priority and novelty remain UNKNOWN.
