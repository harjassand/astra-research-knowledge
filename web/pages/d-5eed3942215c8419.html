## Exact interface

Let C_a be commuting Hermitian Pauli checks with nonempty codespace, acting on finitely many qubits positioned in R^3. Set H0=sum_a (I-C_a)/2. The stabilizer group contains no -I. Logical distance d means every Pauli commuting with all checks and of weight below d is a stabilizer up to phase. Valid syndrome strings s label the nonzero simultaneous eigenspaces, C_a=(-1)^(s_a); these form a binary linear space even with redundant checks, and have equal dimension.

Assume every global stabilizer supported in B(x,r) can be expressed using original checks whose **whole supports** lie in B(x,C max(r,1)). Assume each physical ball of radius L contains at most B(L) qubits, uniformly in code size. The cycle13 admitted family has these properties with an enormous fixed C.

Let V=sum_X V_X, with V_X Hermitian, nonempty support X of physical diameter at most R, |X|<d, and supplied intensive strength

    J=sup_q sum_(X containing q) ||V_X||.

Scalar identity terms may be discarded. The crucial algebraic premise is

    [V,C_a]=0 for EVERY original check a.

Individual terms need not commute before the reduction below. Commutation only with H0 is insufficient. With fixed R and bounded physical density, |X|<d eventually holds for the power-distance family. Define L=C max(R,1) and B=B(L).

## A local conditional expectation avoids a Pauli-coefficient loss

Average conjugation over the finite stabilizer group: E(O)=|S|^-1 sum_(g in S) gOg. Each Pauli conjugation preserves the exact support region X, E is norm contractive, and E(O) commutes with every check. Thus W_X=E(V_X) has support in X and norm at most ||V_X||. The total per-check commutation premise implies sum_X W_X=E(V)=V.

Expand W_X in the Pauli basis only for the mathematical argument. Each surviving Pauli commutes with all checks. Its weight is below d, so it is a stabilizer up to scalar phase. Pick an anchor q_X in X. Since X lies in B(q_X,R), local filling puts **every** such Pauli in the algebra generated by the same set A_X of original checks wholly supported in B(q_X,L). Consequently W_X is a function of those checks. On any valid syndrome sector it equals a scalar v_X(s) times the identity, with

    |v_X(s)| <= ||V_X||.

If s and s' agree on A_X then v_X(s)=v_X(s'). Otherwise their difference is at most 2||V_X||. Only realizable syndromes are used; an arbitrary extension of v_X to inconsistent check signs is neither needed nor justified.

This estimate uses the operator norm directly. Summing absolute Pauli coefficients would introduce an unnecessary 2^|X| factor. No efficient large-system twirl or generator-finding algorithm is inferred from the algebraic existence statement.

## Congestion is controlled by physical locality and input strength

For each check a fix one qubit q_a in its support. If a belongs to A_X, then q_a lies within distance L of q_X. Charge the term to its single chosen anchor. It follows that

    sum_(X:a in A_X) ||V_X||
       <= sum_(q in B(q_a,L)) sum_(X:q_X=q) ||V_X||
       <= B J.

No uniqueness or independence of the check representation is required. The entire local-check algebra A_X gives a safe overcount; whole-support localization ensures the geometric charging step. Thus, for any two valid syndromes,

    |v(s)-v(s')| <= 2 B J |s XOR s'|,       (1)
    V|sector_s = v(s) I.

In particular H0+V acts identically on the logical factor at every syndrome, not only in its ground band. This full-spectrum logical degeneracy is stronger than what a generic spectral-stability theorem supplies.

## Valid witness erasure and exact Gibbs comparison

Let tau be a valid syndrome and let E_tau be any event contained in {s:tau subset s}. For s in this event, T_tau(s)=s XOR tau=s minus tau is valid and the map is injective. The unperturbed energy decreases by |tau|. From (1), with kappa=1-2BJ>0,

    E_V(s)-E_V(T_tau(s)) >= kappa |tau|.

Because all syndrome sectors have equal dimension, the Gibbs distribution is proportional to exp(-beta E_V(s)). Therefore

    Pr_V(E_tau)
       <= exp(-beta kappa |tau|)
          Z_V^-1 sum_(s in E_tau) exp(-beta E_V(T_tau(s)))
       <= exp(-beta kappa |tau|).           (2)

This is a local comparison with the SAME perturbed partition function. A global Radon–Nikodym estimate exp(O(beta J N)) would destroy the desired thermodynamic bound and is not used. Mixed X/Z-sector check interactions are allowed: an eraser changing only one sector still obeys (1).
