1. MODEL, KERNEL AND FORM DOMAINS

The edge convention here is that of the continuum 4D candidate; the 2D

All statements below retain every Fock number sector and the vacuum.

Let P_R be the diagonal projection onto configurations with no pair
having sup-distance <=R, for a fixed integer R>=1. The proof also covers

Hard compression onto a closed subspace P H containing P_R H is also

 [1296+20480(3+log r0)]KH^2/t

5. EXACT PRODUCT LAPLACIAN: NO EXPONENTIAL PARTICLE FACTOR

Fix coordinate i, write n=m-1, u_j=f(x_i-x_j). The factors in F_m not

 B(u+d)-B(u)=sum_j d_j product_{l!=j}u_l
       +2 sum_{j<k}d_j d_k integral_0^1(1-s)
                         product_{l!=j,k}(u_l+s d_l)ds. (A.6)

Each cross coefficient has absolute value <=1. Summing directions, the

       +sum_{j<k;j,k!=i}sum_e
           |D_e f(x_i-x_j)|^2|D_e f(x_i-x_k)|^2].      (A.7)

For first differences, integral first-order Taylor similarly gives
 |D_{i,e}F_m|^2<=m sum_{j!=i}|D_e f(x_i-x_j)|^2.       (A.8)
This proof does not expand all subsets of the product; there is no 2^m

6. LABELED/FIELD NORMALIZATION, INCLUDING GRADIENTS AND GHOSTS

Use the usual isometry from physical occupation basis into labeled
normalized symmetric m-particle coordinate space. If n_x are a site's
occupations, its ordered-coordinate representatives have count
m!/product_x n_x!, and each has amplitude divided by the square root
of that count. The annihilation kernel is exactly

 (a_x psi_m)(Z)=sqrt(m) psi_m(x,Z).

Therefore, for arbitrary normalized symmetric psi_m and a direction e,

 (a_y b_{x,e}psi_m)(Z)
 =sqrt(m(m-1))[psi_m(x+e,y,Z)-psi_m(x,y,Z)].            (A.9)

If x+e is a ghost, its wavefunction and annihilator are both zero. If y
coincides with x or x+e, the same identity holds: annihilators commute
and yield factorial rather than ordinary occupation moments. There is
no after-hop shift of the weight evaluated at the output source x.

For any nonnegative fixed weight w(x,y,e), symmetry and (A.9) imply the
EXACT identity

 sum_{i!=j,e,X}w(x_i,x_j,e)|D_{i,e}psi_m(X)|^2
       =sum_{x,y,e}w(x,y,e)||a_y b_{x,e}psi_m||^2.       (A.10)

The two sides both contain m(m-1) times the same residual-coordinate
sum. Ordinary pair and ordered-triple diagonal sums likewise equal
Gamma2 and Gamma3. Summing over the free state's positive spectral
decomposition proves these identities for its labeled Gibbs blocks.
The derivative images need not themselves be symmetric in all m
coordinates; only the input symmetry and the displayed norm identity
are used. Number tilt preserves the same symmetry and normalization.
