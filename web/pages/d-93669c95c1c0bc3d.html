## Stronger cost consequence for a coded invariant region

Let `E` be invariant under every `phi_t`, `alpha=mu(E)>0`, and `E subset U={X!=0}` modulo null sets. The already-frozen normal-cocycle proof applies to the invariant probability `mu_E=mu|E/alpha`, not just to ambient volume. Thus

`h_{mu_E}(phi_1) <= integral_E s dmu_E`,

and Cauchy plus `|S|^2/2 >= s^2` on `U` gives

`integral_E |S|^2/2 dmu = alpha integral |S|^2/2 dmu_E >= alpha h_{mu_E}(phi_1)^2`.  (1)

Because the stationary work identity is `W=2 nu V integral_M |S|^2 dmu`, (1) yields the localized bound

`W >= 4 nu V alpha h_{mu_E}(phi_1)^2`.  (2)

Suppose an ideal `q`-ary iid process is a factor of `(E,mu_E,phi_tau)`, and an equivariant decoded process has each coordinate error at most `epsilon <= 1-1/q`. Block Fano gives entropy rate at least

`r_epsilon = log(q)-h_2(epsilon)-epsilon log(q-1)`

(with zero substituted if this is negative). The decoded process is a factor, so `h_{mu_E}(phi_tau)>=r_epsilon`; entropy scaling gives `h_{mu_E}(phi_1)>=r_epsilon/tau`. Therefore

`W >= 4 nu V alpha (r_epsilon/tau)^2`.  (3)

This improves the c08_l10 INITIAL's `4 nu V alpha^2 r_epsilon^2/(beta tau^2)` for `alpha<=beta`: the conditional active measure and the work integral on E retain one factor of alpha rather than first lower-bounding ambient entropy and then paying the global active-volume Cauchy factor. It applies only when the symbol process/readout is actually an equivariant factor on the positive-volume invariant region. It does not establish that such a region exists for a proposed machine, nor charge construction, acquisition, sensing, clock, force-generation, or decoding costs. If E is only invariant under `T=phi_tau`, the same linear-alpha result follows from the frozen componentwise deficit below; the direct `mu_E` argument in (1) is stated only for full-flow invariance.

## Explicit recurrence step behind the normal exponent estimate

A proof detail worth making explicit in the original is the cancellation of the `q=<Se,e>` term when the invariant components are only time-one ergodic. Fix `tau>0`, `T=phi_tau`, and a T-ergodic invariant probability `lambda` with `lambda(U)=1`. On U,

`q=X(log |X|)`, and `g_tau(x)=integral_0^tau q(phi_t x)dt=log|X(Tx)|-log|X(x)|`.

The function `g_tau` is bounded because `q` is bounded. Birkhoff gives a limit for its T-averages. Since `lambda(U)=1`, some set `{|X|>=1/m}` has positive lambda-measure. Poincare recurrence returns almost every point of that set to it infinitely often; compactness also gives a global upper bound on `|X|`. Along this subsequence,

`(1/n) sum_{j=0}^{n-1} g_tau(T^j x)=(log|X(T^n x)|-log|X(x)|)/n -> 0`.

The Birkhoff limit is therefore zero, so `integral g_tau d lambda=0`. This is the required cancellation even when `log|X|` is not lambda-integrable near Z. The same recurrence proves the flow-direction Lyapunov exponent is zero. No assumption of flow-ergodicity for lambda is needed.

For completeness, with `P=I-e tensor e`, `v(t)=Dphi_t v_0=w+a e`, and `w=P v`, projection of the variational equation gives

`P nabla_X w = P nabla_w X`.

The two moving-frame terms cancel since `P nabla_X e = P nabla_e X`. Hence

`d/dt log|w| = <S w,w>/|w|^2 <= s-q/2`.

Integrating over a T-step, applying Oseledets to the two-dimensional quotient cocycle, using `integral g_tau=0`, and applying Ruelle componentwise yields

`h_lambda(T)/tau <= integral s_tau d lambda`, where `s_tau=(1/tau)integral_0^tau s(phi_t x)dt`.

For a nonergodic T-invariant measure, disintegrate into T-ergodic components. This is the exact scope needed by the original T=phi_1 deficit; it does not silently assume its components are invariant under every flow time.

## Time-scale form and localized version of the deficit

The whole decomposition in INITIAL extends to every `tau>0`. Put `T=phi_tau`, `s_tau=(1/tau)integral_0^tau s(phi_t x)dt`, `H_tau=h_mu(T)/tau`, and

`Gamma_tau=E_mu[(1/tau)integral_0^tau s(phi_t x)^2dt - s_tau^2] >= 0`.

For the T-ergodic decomposition `mu=integral lambda_omega d eta`, set `h_omega=h_{lambda_omega}(T)/tau` and `m_omega=integral s_tau d lambda_omega`. Then exactly

`W/(4 nu V)-H_tau^2 = Gamma_tau + integral Var_{lambda_omega}(s_tau)deta + integral(m_omega^2-h_omega^2)deta + Var_eta(h_omega) + (3/4)E_U q^2 + E_U|b|^2 + (1/2)E_Z|S|^2`.

The proof is the same algebra as (A), with Jensen over a tau-window and the recurrence/Ruelle lemma above. In particular for any T-invariant measurable `A subset U` modulo null sets, `alpha_A=mu(A)>0`,

`W >= 4 nu V alpha_A [h_{mu|A/alpha_A}(T)/tau]^2`.

This covers a discrete-sampling invariant code region even if it is not invariant at intermediate times. All entropy quantities are metric entropies under the admitted invariant measures; a finite observed word, topological entropy, or a null-volume code is not enough.

## Equality proof audit

The positive-entropy equality chain remains internally consistent under the stated compact smooth, closed, divergence-free 3D assumptions. The zero-field step is load-bearing: `W/(4 nu V)>=H^2/alpha` forces alpha=1 when `Delta=0` and `H>0`; then U is dense, and continuity extends `SX=0` and `spec(S)={H,0,-H}` across Z. At a putative zero p, differentiating `SX=0` gives `S(p)DX_p=0`; since `ker S(p)` is a line, `DX_p=k tensor w`, while `S(p)=sym DX_p` and `S(p)k=0` force w=0, contradicting rank(S(p))=2. Thus X has no zeros.

With SX=0, `X-perp` is invariant under Dphi_t, so the normal quotient is the actual derivative there. Ruelle and the cocycle bound force the top normal exponent to equal H. The measurable Oseledets top line is flow-invariant; along it the Rayleigh quotient `a=<Sv,v>/|v|^2` is at most H. The averages `n^-1 log|Dphi_n v|/|v|` converge to H and are bounded in absolute value by H. Invariance of mu makes their integrals equal to `integral a dmu`; dominated convergence gives `integral a=H`, hence `a=H` a.e. Equality in Rayleigh's bound gives the smooth eigenspace E+ a.e.; full support and continuity extend its invariance to every point. For a vector from E- transported alongside E+, the derivative of their inner product is `2H` times that inner product, so initial orthogonality persists and E- is invariant as well. Exact rates then make the flow Anosov.

The rest of the coframe proof is sound as an internal derivation: smooth first integrals are constant by the stable/unstable expansion; r=|X| is constant; the oriented joint eigenline cover has degree at most four; the global frame has constant brackets `[X,U+]=-H U+`, `[X,U-]=H U-`, `[U+,U-]=cX`; Jacobi and compactness make c constant; Cartan integration plus completeness identifies the universal cover with the simply connected Sol or `SL(2,R)` group carrying the corresponding homogeneous metric. The dynamical classification substantially overlaps Ghys (1987), Theorem 3.1 and §5; this is not a novelty claim. External review and priority for the exact work identity remain absent.
