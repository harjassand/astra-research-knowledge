### c10_s01 — two-layer reciprocal boundary correction

I independently checked the displayed generator identity and boundary cases.
For a child with parent count S, `g(S)=1+c/(S+1)`, root immigration contributes
`-c B/((S+1)(S+2))`; root death contributes
`c sum_i delta_i r_i/(S(S+1))` only when S>=1. At S=0 no death term exists,
and the exact value is `-cB/2`. For S>=1, the latter term is at most
`c D/(S+1)`, and `cD<=beta` gives
`L_root g - a g <= beta/(S+1)-beta S <= -beta/2`. Adding child births and
deaths and the acquired root weights gives the stated `LV<=C-lambda V`.
The linear count non-explosion bound `LN<=b+A N`, the irreducibility path,
and the finite-core stopping argument are consistent with the network.
I found no algebraic defect in this derivation. Its exact class is narrow;
rates are fixed in the displayed statement, and priority overlaps substantially
with environment-modulated immigration/death work (notably arXiv:1912.00401),
which the Sol initial itself correctly leaves for a full comparison.

### c10_s02 — a recurrent bimolecular counterexample to two certificate forms

The specified six-reaction network is reversible and has all complexes as
sources. At `(a,0,1)`, the only enabled state changes that increase the exact
N76 residual-factorial minimum are `C->0`, from `(a-1)!` to `a!`; the other

At `(0,n,0)`, the only jumps are `B->A+B` and `0->C`; both increase every

Its two species with no unary drain also put it outside c10_s03's class.

## New independent extension: robust quadratic boundary recovery

Consider the eight reversible reactions

    0 <-> A,   0 <-> B,   A <-> A+B,   B <-> A+B,

with positive rational lower/upper bounds on each independent reaction rate.
Every rate may be an arbitrary predictable, history-dependent process in its
supplied interval. Propensities are exactly the stochastic mass-action
falling-factorial propensities. Let `a,b` denote the counts and
`V(a,b)=a^2+b^2`.

Write `lambda_A^+,lambda_B^+` for the upper birth rates; `delta_A^-,delta_B^-`
for the lower unary death rates; `alpha_A^+,alpha_B^+` for the upper catalytic
rates `A->A+B`,`B->A+B`; and `eta_A^-,eta_B^-` for the lower rates
`A+B->B`,`A+B->A`, respectively. Set

    delta = min(delta_A^-, delta_B^-),
    beta  = min(eta_A^-, eta_B^-),
    c0    = lambda_A^+ + lambda_B^+,
    cA    = 2 lambda_A^+ + delta_A^- + alpha_A^+,
    cB    = 2 lambda_B^+ + delta_B^- + alpha_B^+,
    D     = 2 alpha_A^+ + 2 alpha_B^+ + eta_A^- + eta_B^-,
    S0    = ceil(D/(2 beta)),
    B_L   = c0 + (cA^2+cB^2)/(2 delta),
    B_S   = c0 + max(cA,cB)(S0-1) + D(S0-1)^2/4,
    B     = max(B_L,B_S).

Every quantity is an acquired rational except the integer ceiling `S0`.
For a birth, unary death, catalyst creation and pair reversal, the increments
of V are respectively `2a+1`, `a(-2a+1)`, `a(2b+1)`, and `ab(-2b+1)`;
the B-symmetric increments follow by swapping coordinates. Because the death
and pair-reversal increments are nonpositive whenever enabled, the upper
generator over the rate box is

    LV <= c0 + cA*a + cB*b - 2 delta V + D ab - 2 beta ab(a+b).

Let `s=a+b`, `p=ab`. If `s>=S0`, then `Dp-2 beta p s<=0` and
`cA*a+cB*b <= delta V/2 + (cA^2+cB^2)/(2delta)`, so
`LV<=B_L-(3delta/2)V<=B-delta V`. If `s<S0`, then `s<=S0-1`,
`p<=s^2/4`, and adding `delta V` to the displayed upper bound leaves at most
`c0+max(cA,cB)(S0-1)+D(S0-1)^2/4=B_S`. Thus, on every count state and for
every permitted control,

    LV <= B - delta V.                                      (QF)

For the uniform box `[1,2]` on all eight channels,
`delta=beta=1`, `c0=4`, `cA=cB=7`, `D=10`, `S0=5`,
`B_L=53`, `B_S=72`, and hence `LV<=72-(a^2+b^2)`. This fixture includes the
unit-rate c10_s03 balanced-cross rejection.

`V` is coercive and has finite sublevel sets. From `LV<=B`, stop at
`V>=m`: `P_x(tau_m<=t)<=(V(x)+Bt)/m`. Local rates are bounded on each finite
sublevel set, so this proves non-explosion under every allowed predictable
control. With `R=ceil((B+1)/delta)` and `K={V<=R}`, (QF) gives `LV<=-1`
outside K; stopped Dynkin and Fatou give `E_x tau_K<=V(x)`.

There is also an explicit global boundary-return bound. For any q>=1, from a
state in K, use unary deaths `A->0`,`B->0` to reach 0 in at most
`N0=ceil(sqrt(2R))` reactions; then fire `0->A` and `0->B` q times each.
This is an actually enabled word ending in `G_q={(a,b):a,b>=q}`, of length
at most `H=N0+2q`. Its count never exceeds `Ncap=max(N0,2q)`. Set

    U=sum_r u_r, Q=U(Ncap+1)^2, ell=min_r l_r,
    alpha=ell/(2Q), p=alpha^H, t0=H/Q.

At each desired step, while no event has yet occurred, its intensity is at
least ell and the total intensity is at most Q. Therefore the probability
the desired next event occurs within `1/Q` is at least
`ell integral_0^(1/Q) exp(-Qs) ds >= alpha`. Conditioning step by step shows
`P_x(tau_Gq<=t0)>=p` uniformly over all past histories and controls. After an
attempt from K, `E V(X_t0)<=R+B t0`; a failed attempt costs at most its
duration plus a return-to-K expectation of `V(X_t0)`. Geometric retries yield

    E_x tau_Gq <= V(x) + [R+(B+1)t0]/p.                     (QR)

For `[1,2]^8` and q=2, `R=73`, `N0=Ncap=13`, `U=16`, `Q=3136`,
`ell=1`, `alpha=1/6272`, `H=17`, `t0=17/3136`, and
`E_(a,b) tau_G2 <= a^2+b^2 + (230169/3136) 6272^17`.
The bound is intentionally crude but finite, rational and fully charged.

For a fixed rate vector in the box the chain is irreducible: unary deaths
reach zero and zero-source births reach every count state. (QF) therefore also
gives non-explosion and positive recurrence for each such fixed-rate chain.
The unit-rate instance is detailed-balanced with product-Poisson law, but the
whole interval family is not complex-balanced. For example the fixed rate
vector `(1,1,1,1,1,1,2,1)` in channel order as listed satisfies the complex
balance equations at 0,A,B only if `c_A+c_B=2`, `2c_A=1+c_Ac_B`, and
`3c_B=1+c_Ac_B`. The last two imply `2c_A=3c_B`, forcing `(c_A,c_B)=(6/5,4/5)`,
which violates the A equation by `11/25`. This is a concrete non-complex-balanced
member covered by the common drift and recurrence proof.

No common positive linear weight `w_A a+w_B b` works uniformly for the full
`[1,2]^8` box, since that box contains the unit-rate member. For that member,
the coefficient of a on `(a,0)` is `-w_A+w_B`, while the coefficient of b on
`(0,b)` is `w_A-w_B`; these cannot both be negative. This is the exact
contradiction behind c10_s03's failed affine condition on this fixture. The
quadratic certificate is not concave and does not contradict c10_s02's
distinct recurrent-network obstruction to globally coercive concave
potentials.

## Executed evidence, attribution, prior art and remaining gate

The independent standard-library script
`work/cycle6/c10_l09/revisions/quadratic_cross_foster.py` uses exact
`fractions.Fraction` arithmetic. It checked the direct generator at all 256
rate-box corners for each of 169 states `(a,b) in {0,...,12}^2`, totaling
43,264 inequalities, and checked the acquired drain/production word on all
67 states in `V<=73`. It also checked the rational recovery constants and the
non-complex-balance contradiction. Exit status was 0; the machine-readable
output is `quadratic_cross_foster_checks.json`. This grid is a transcription
diagnostic, not a proof for all states; the symbolic polynomial split proves
(QF) for the whole count lattice and the explicit path/hazard derivation
proves (QR). No stochastic simulation or heavy computation ran.

Prior art is substantial. Gupta, Briat and Khammash, *A Scalable Computational
Framework for Establishing Long-Term Behavior of Stochastic Reaction
Networks*, arXiv:1304.5404 / PLOS Computational Biology (2014), includes
constructive Foster-Lyapunov criteria for bimolecular/quadratic networks
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.5404; https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003669).
Anderson and Kim, *Some Network Conditions for Positive Recurrence of
Stochastically Modeled Reaction Networks*, arXiv:1710.11263, gives structural
fixed-rate positive-recurrence criteria (https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11263).
Xu, *On the Regularity of Reaction Systems*, arXiv:2409.05340v2, Theorem 4.6,
establishes non-explosivity for second-order endotactic networks
(https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2); that theorem alone does not supply recurrence.
These sources block any novelty claim for quadratic Foster reasoning,
fixed-rate recurrence, or non-explosion. The exact simple family, arbitrary
predictable `[1,2]`-box quantifier, and explicit recovery word above have not
received external priority review; no novelty claim is made.

The cross-phase assignment's main general gate remains open. This explicit
quadratic family and c10_s01's reciprocal corrector each solve selected
boundary geometries, while c10_s02 shows two tempting potential architectures
fail elsewhere. The next useful step is a broader effective synthesis language
for polynomial or finite-step Lyapunov certificates with uniform rate boxes,
plus an exact proof that its acquired boundary paths stay in the target closed
communicating class. There is no general bimolecular-endotactic recurrence
theorem or counterexample in this audit.
