The p>2 witness in lp_orientation_exchange_boundary has a strict l-infinity exchange failure that persists with every coordinate-pair auxiliary present at positive activity 10^-4.

Let F be the second 6x6 matrix in lp_orientation_exchange_boundary.txt, S={1,5}, T={2,6}, a=1. For the unaugmented instance, each endpoint has one orientation of amplitude 2. The j=2 and j=6 exchanged coefficients each have two orientations of amplitude 1, while the j=5 term vanishes. Hence the l-infinity comparison is 4 > 1+0+1=2.

Add all 15 auxiliary coordinate pairs (e_i,e_j) with activity w=10^-4, so each selected pair multiplies an amplitude by t=sqrt(w)=1/100. The selected auxiliary pairs must form a matching. Different selected matchings are distinct microscopic labels; therefore the extended maximum amplitude is

  m_t(U)=max_{K matching disjoint from U} t^|K| m_0(U union V(K)),

where m_0(W) is the largest absolute original complementary minor. Exact enumeration gives max_W m_0(W)=2. The endpoints remain m_t(S)=m_t(T)=2. The three exchange-side products are exactly 1, 1/5000, and 1, so

  m_t(S)m_t(T)=4 > 10001/5000 = 2.0002.

The strict gap is 9999/5000. This is a positive-activity counterexample in the fully populated coordinate auxiliary extension, not merely a limiting w=0 obstruction. The matching formula follows by expanding the added coordinate columns; any overlapping auxiliary pair choices repeat a coordinate and have determinant zero.
