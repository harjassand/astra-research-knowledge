1. PROMISE AND UNIVERSAL FLOOR -- PASS

For a positive fully separable state supported on Sym^n, every positive pure
product term is itself supported on Sym^n. Swap invariance then forces identical
one-site pure factors. Dicke diagonality permits a common phase twirl, yielding
pi_k=integral binom(n,k)x^k(1-x)^(n-k) mu(dx) for a positive probability mu.
The converse follows from this coherent representation. This is the precise
promise; an arbitrary positive Dicke-diagonal state need not satisfy it.

Uniform dx represents P_sym/(n+1), since each Bernstein integral is 1/(n+1).
For eta=epsilon/8, mu_reg=(1-eta)mu+eta dx >=eta dx and
rho_reg=(1-eta)rho+eta P_sym/(n+1) has D(rho,rho_reg)<=eta. This regularizes
atomic or singular measures without a model-specific density floor. The added
state is the symmetric white state, not white noise on the whole 2^n space.
