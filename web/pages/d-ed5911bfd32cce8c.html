Remark 1.8. The non-degeneracy requirement that γ is not contained in W is easily

and f is the indicator function of F n−1.
Remark 1.9. Theorem 1.6 is stated for V equal to all of projective or aﬃne space,
but one can trivially restrict V to any smaller set (e.g. some intermediate subvariety
between W and PN or AN ) and obtain the same estimate for f ∈ ℓn(V (F )). Indeed, the
most interesting case in practice is that where V is an n-dimensional variety containing
W .
Theorem 1.3 follows immediately from Theorem 1.6 by taking V = Pn and W the
hyperplane at inﬁnity, and setting d = 1; then the set of projective lines through a
given point of W (F ) is naturally identiﬁed with the set of lines in F n parallel to some
ﬁxed line.
