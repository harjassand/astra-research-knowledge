NEW MECHANISM: MIDPOINT FACTORIZATION GIVES LINEAR WITNESS BOUNDS

Use v1's stationary finite CTMC Q with all-even hidden variables, positive stationary pi and finite entropy production sigma. Let P_s=exp(sQ), and P_s*=exp(sQ*) in L2(pi).

For a centered real state observable f with range R=max f-min f and t=2s,
 C_Q(t)=<f,P_t f>_pi=<P_s* f,P_s f>_pi.
Set u=P_s f, v=P_s* f. The parallelogram identity gives
 C_Q(t)=||u+v||_pi^2/4-||u-v||_pi^2/4 >=-||u-v||_pi^2/4.
The stationary average of the conditional path KLs from Q to Q*, starting at the same hidden i and run for s, is s sigma. For each i, the expectations of f at time s differ by at most R times the corresponding conditional path TV. Pinsker therefore gives
 ||u-v||_pi^2 <= R^2 s sigma/2.
Consequently
                 sigma >= 16[-C_Q(t)]_+/(R^2 t).       (8)
For binary Y in {0,1}, its centered observable still has range 1, so sigma>=16[-Cov(Y0,Yt)]_+/t. This is linear in the negative covariance, improving the earlier quadratic Pinsker witness in its small-signal regime. The earlier KL-projection inequality remains independently valid; no global comparison between their numerical strengths is claimed.

No positivity of the nonsymmetric semigroup as an operator on L2 is assumed. Only its Markov nature, stationarity, its adjoint and P_s P_s=P_t enter. The bounded-range expectation inequality applies despite centering. Conditional path KL is used before observation; its average is the full stationary entropy production on duration s.

GENERAL REFLECTED WORDS AND COPOSITIVE WITNESSES

Use the mirrored observation windows of v1 with full span T=2s. Conditional on X0=i, let u_i be the probability vector of the future word and v_i that of the reversed-past word. Past and future are independent given X0, so the actual observed table satisfies
                         P=sum_i pi_i v_i u_i^T.
Under Q, the reversed past has conditional law Q* run forward from i. Therefore
 sum_i pi_i D(u_i||v_i)<=s sigma, and likewise
 sum_i pi_i D(v_i||u_i)<=s sigma.
Let W be a symmetric copositive matrix and M=max_uv |W_uv|>0. Then u_i+v_i is nonnegative and
 v_i^T W u_i
 =[(u_i+v_i)^T W(u_i+v_i)-(u_i-v_i)^T W(u_i-v_i)]/4
 >=-M ||u_i-v_i||_1^2/4
 =-M TV(u_i,v_i)^2.
Average and use Pinsker to obtain
                  sigma >= 4[-<W,P>]_+/(T M).           (9)
This strengthens v1's quadratic copositive witness. It can be nonzero even when P=P^T and the entire observed path law is time reversible. The matrix W is copositive in the classical nonnegative-orthant sense, not a quantum channel.

STRONGER VARIATIONAL MIDPOINT BOUND

For any finite probability table P define the extended nonnegative quantity
 K(P)=inf sum_r lambda_r [D(p_r||q_r)+D(q_r||p_r)]
 over probability weights lambda_r, probability vectors p_r,q_r on the same word alphabet, with P=sum_r lambda_r p_r q_r^T.
The actual hidden midpoint factorization is feasible, so
                           sigma >= K(P)/T.            (10)
This formula does not assert efficient computability or optimality among all compatible CTMCs. K is a convex roof over probability-table atoms; all infinite divergences remain explicit.

K(P)=0 exactly when P is completely positive (with total mass one).
The easy direction: write P=sum_r b_r b_r^T with b_r>=0, set lambda_r=(1^T b_r)^2, p_r=q_r=b_r/(1^T b_r). Total mass ensures sum lambda_r=1, and cost zero.
Conversely, if a sequence of factorizations has cost tending to zero, Pinsker bounds the average ||p_r-q_r||_1^2 by that cost. Replace each atom by m_r m_r^T where m_r=(p_r+q_r)/2. For the tensor l1 norm,
 ||p q^T-m m^T||_1 <= ||p-m||_1+||q-m||_1=||p-q||_1.
The averaged difference is at most square root of the cost by Cauchy-Schwarz and Jeffreys>=||p-q||_1^2. Thus P is a limit of normalized completely positive tables. That set is closed in finite dimension, so P is completely positive.

For symmetric P a sharper relation follows after cancellation of antisymmetric terms:
 P=sum lambda_r [m_r m_r^T - (p_r-q_r)(p_r-q_r)^T/4].
Hence the l1 distance of P to normalized CP tables is <=cost/4, and every normalized copositive witness yields K(P)>=4[-<W,P>]_+/M, agreeing with (9).

IMPORTANT LIMIT
This characterizes zero cost of a single latent-midpoint factorization, not compatibility with a reversible Markov process across all times. A collection of separate factorizations is not a common generator. That is the current deeper obstruction, alongside focused priority review and numerical/statistical acquisition of useful witnesses.
