## C2. Exact oracle still does not turn an incremental predictor into a parent test

Let all noises be independent, let A be fair binary, and set

\[
M=A\oplus N_1,\qquad Y=M\oplus N_2,
\qquad N_1,N_2\sim\operatorname{Ber}(1/4).
\]

The observed, causally sufficient DAG is A→M→Y. All eight cell probabilities are positive: in lexicographic(A,M,Y) order they are(9,3,1,3,3,1,3,9)/32. Independent irrelevant event positions can be added to match the source's endpoint indexing. Y is a leaf and has no spouses. Time order is A before M before Y. This positive distribution is faithful: the only nontrivial pairwise CI is A independent of Y given M. Direct structural parents are unambiguous.

An exact predictor has zero error. The main§5.2 query for the earliest candidate, with empty earlier history, equals

\[
I(Y;A)=\log2-h_b(3/8)>0,
\qquad
I(Y;M\mid A)=h_b(3/8)-h_b(1/4)>0.
\]

Indeed P(Y=1|A=1)=5/8 and P(Y=1|A=0)=3/8. Thus threshold0 includes A→Y although A is not a direct parent. Conditioning on earlier events blocks backdoors, but does not block the later directed mediator A→M→Y. Being a leaf removes Y's children and spouses; it does not remove Y's ancestors.

The false implication in AppendixC.4 is: nonparent + causal sufficiency + conditioning on the pre-candidate history implies zero CMI. The chain disproves that implication. Correctly conditioning on M yields I(Y;A|M)=0.

There is also a literal indexing defect: AppendixB.1 defines strong faithfulness using I(T_t;E_(t−ell)|X_<t), although E_(t−ell) is already measurable in X_<t. That CMI is identically zero. Accordingly, this is NOT asserted to be a counterexample satisfying an unsatisfiable literal strong-faithfulness definition. Under the charitable non-self-conditioning interpretation of the main query, the chain satisfies the intended perfect-oracle/positive-margin assumptions and direct-parent soundness fails. If an implementation instead randomizes/conditions later mediators for Y, this particular implementation conclusion does not follow.

Status: FAIL for the main pre-candidate-CMI→direct-parent implication; malformed literal indexing must be repaired before Theorem2 is a well-posed nonempty-graph theorem. Software behavior UNKNOWN.

## C3. Model-history CMI can drift while every local kernel error vanishes

This counterexample targets Theorem1 independently of causal faithfulness and avoids the ambiguous Y notation.

Take an odd m, fair independent history bits H_1,…,H_m, an independent fair candidate A, and define F(H)=1 when a majority of the history bits is1. The binary next target T has kernel

\[
p(1\mid A,H)=
\begin{cases}
1/2,&F(H)=0,\\
1/4+A/2,&F(H)=1.
\end{cases}
\]

The model makes each history bit Ber(1/2+δ), but uses the EXACT candidate and target kernels. At every possible prefix, its per-step true→model KL is at most

\[
\epsilon=-\tfrac12\log(1-4\delta^2).
\]

The target approximation error is exactly ε_T=0, pointwise, and its no-candidate target marginal is also exactly1/2. Put c=log2−h_b(1/4)>0. The bounded per-history CMI integrand is c F(H). Consequently

\[
I_P(T;A\mid H)=c/2,
\qquad I_\theta(T;A\mid H)=c\,\Pr_\theta(F(H)=1).
\]

The main estimator and AppendixC.1 converge to the second expression, not the first. For m=3 and δ=1/16, the model majority probability is1215/2048; the difference is(191/2048)c>0. The claimed left bound2Φ(sqrt(ε_T/2)) is zero.

Even the claimed right bound in ε fails for long histories. Choose δ=1/500 and m=1000001. Chebyshev gives

\[
\Pr_\theta(F=0)\le\frac1{4m\delta^2}<\frac1{16},
\quad |I_\theta-I_P|>\frac7{16}c\ge\frac7{128}.
\]

The last inequality is binary Pinsker: c≥2(1/4)^2. Also ε≤1/124998, hence sqrt(ε/2)<1/499. Since

\[
\Phi(x)=x\log2-x\log x+(1+x)\log(1+x),
\]

it is increasing, and log998<7 gives

\[
2\Phi(\sqrt{\epsilon/2})
<2\left(\frac8{499}+\frac1{499^2}\right)
<\frac7{128}.
\]

The inequality log998<7 can be certified by the rational partial sum of exp7; it is checked in the owned exact replay. This is a finite analytic counterexample, not a million-position model-training run. More generally mδ²→infinity, δ→0 yields a nonzero limiting CMI error while ε→0.

Status: FAIL for Theorem1 applied to the source's main model-generated-history estimator, even under the stronger uniform pointwise kernel-error interpretation. The teacher-forced/common-history version in AppendixC.3 avoids this particular objection, but is a different estimator. A generic joint-law repair pays history length: chain-rule KL of a t-step joint law is at most tε, before conditional-entropy continuity is invoked. Target-only error ε_T cannot cover history-law drift.

## C4. Marginalizing a randomized mediator can erase a direct edge

Let A,M be independent Ber(1/2), let N be independent Ber(1/4), and T=A xor M xor N. Both A and M directly enter the target mechanism. For each fixed M, changing A changes P(T=1) from1/4 to3/4 or conversely. After averaging over a uniform M, P(T=1|do(A=a),do(M uniform))=1/2 for both a.

Thus the KL AFTER averaging mediator kernels, as in the literal AppendixF.1 lagged definition, is zero. Retaining each mediator assignment and averaging its KL gives

\[
I(T;A\mid M)=\log2-h_b(1/4)>0.
\]

Under randomized A independent of M, the exact difference is

\[
I(T;A\mid M)-I(T;A)=I(A;M\mid T)\ge0.
\]

This XOR example is not ordinary observational-faithful: I(T;A)=0 despite the edge. It establishes the need to distinguish observational, averaged-interventional and conditional-mechanism faithfulness; it does not refute a theorem that explicitly assumes a margin for its actual averaged-interventional query. The seq2cause strong-faithfulness notation does not specify that repaired margin.

Important nearest comparator: Chadyuk–Zhang–Kucukates, *Replicating TRACE*,2609.01108v1,1September2026, §3, describes paired mediator draws shared between two arms and a particle average of mediator-specific contrasts. That reported instrument can avoid this cancellation. Their empirical results were not replayed here; no claim that TRACE's actual software has this flaw is made.

## C5. Observational predictive loss does not buy intervention coverage

Let R~Ber(p), A~Ber(1/2) be independent. The true binary target probability is2/5 when R=0 and1/4+A/2 when R=1. The model is exact at R=0 and returns1/2 at R=1. All cells are positive for0<p<1; the observational structural graph is causally sufficient and time ordered.

Its average target KL is

\[
\epsilon_{\rm obs}=p c\longrightarrow0,
\quad c=\log2-h_b(1/4),
\]

but under do(R=1), target TV error is1/4 in either arm and the true paired effect score is1/8 while the model score is0. The query/observation density ratio is κ=1/p. This is a failure of an AVERAGE observed predictive error as an intervention certificate. A supplied uniform all-context kernel bound would cover it; the objection is that held-out observational cross-entropy does not acquire that bound.

Precisely, let ν be the observational full-prefix law, let ρ be the acquired reference query law, and suppose ρ absolutely continuous with respect to ν and dρ/dν≤κ. Then

\[
\mathbb E_\rho\mathrm{KL}(k\|\hat k)
\le\kappa\mathbb E_\nu\mathrm{KL}(k\|\hat k).
\]

Absent coverage, ν-null query contexts have unconstrained kernels even with perfect observational fit. With small positive observational mass, the preceding example makes the coverage cost explicit. This is established distribution-shift/positivity machinery, not a new impossibility result.

The source's entropy-floor surrogate is not an upper certificate either: at the population level CE(current)−CE(best)=KL(current)−KL(best)≤KL(current). Treating an imperfect best model as the entropy floor can underestimate the actual oracle error. An independently acquired upper bound or the experimental protocol below is needed.

## C6. Exact replacement: retain the mediator and certify a kernel variance

Fix a binary target T and one candidate categorical A. Let Z contain every earlier event other than A, including later mediators. Assume a known valid order, causal sufficiency with independent structural noises, and observational positivity on all queried full-prefix assignments. Thus k(1|a,z) is the actual target mechanism. Fix an acquired reference law ρ(a,z)=λ(z)π(a|z) whose support witnesses every target-kernel dependence under consideration; π has full candidate support there. Sampling λ and π is an explicit resource.

Draw A,A' independently from π given the SAME Z, and define

\[
D(k)=\mathbb E[(k(1|A,Z)-k(1|A',Z))^2]
=2\mathbb E_Z\operatorname{Var}_{\pi(\cdot|Z)} k(1|A,Z).
\]

D=0 iff k is constant in A at almost every queried Z. In a minimal fully observed target-kernel DAG with a full-support witness law, D>0 iff A is a direct target parent. Here minimality is an explicit assumption: an SCM arrow whose distributional effect is exactly erased by independent target noise need not be visible in k. To verify the claim, the ordered Markov factorization gives P(T|all pre-target variables)=P(T|target parents). A nonparent does not enter this kernel. A parent in a causally minimal positive distribution has different target conditionals at some assignments of that parent and the other parents (Proposition6.36). Positivity permits extending that assignment to all remaining pre-target variables, and full support of λ and π puts positive weight on its nonzero paired contrast. This is a kernel-level parent statement, not a claim about an individual's realized counterfactual cause. No sign assumption is needed, and XOR cannot cancel because the squared contrast is formed before mediator averaging.

Let e=hat k−k. If E_ρ KL(k||hat k)≤ε_ρ, binary Pinsker and independent pairing give

\[
\|(e(A,Z)-e(A',Z))\|_2^2
=2\mathbb E_Z\operatorname{Var}_\pi e
\le2\mathbb E_\rho e^2\le\epsilon_\rho.
\]

The triangle inequality in L2 therefore proves

\[
|\sqrt{D(k)}-\sqrt{D(\hat k)}|
\le\sqrt{\epsilon_\rho}
\le\sqrt{\kappa\epsilon_{\rm obs}}.
\tag{C6}
\]

This is the useful replacement equation. In particular, a nonparent has D(hat k)≤κ ε_obs. A parent with sqrtD>2sqrt(κ ε_obs) clears that ideal threshold. Common sampled query contexts eliminate model-history drift; retaining Z eliminates the ancestor and cancellation errors.

The constant and overlap dependence in C6 are sharp under this interface. On a query-only rare context R=1 with observational mass p, put k(1|a,1)=1/2+(2a−1)h and hat k(1|a,1)=1/2, with uniform a; outside that context the model is exact. Then κ=1/p, D(k)=2h², D(hat k)=0 and ε_obs=p KL(Ber(1/2+h)||Ber(1/2)). As h tends to0, that KL is2h²+O(h⁴), so sqrtD/sqrt(κ ε_obs) tends to1. This demonstrates optimal dependence, not a new generic information inequality.

The statistic is exactly twice a conditional total-effect variance. Sobol2001, §10, Theorem3, Eq(7), already supplies the paired squared-difference identity (visual PDF page277 checked). This is a straightforward known sensitivity estimator plus Pinsker and the change-of-measure inequality, not a claim of new generic causal discovery. Peters–Janzing–Schölkopf2017, Proposition6.36 and §6.6 Eq(6.7), supply the older causal-minimality/mechanism-autonomy comparator: graph support needs minimality and actual conditional kernels, not marginal prediction increments. The source theorem repair and explicit acquisition interface are the consequence.

An even closer approximation comparator is Panin1912.11070v2 (6January2021), published EJS15(2021):235–281. Its §3.1.1 Theorem1 controls normalized Sobol/total-effect errors by relative L2 model error; §3.1.2 explicitly discusses transfer between sampling measures through the norm domination d(μ,ν) and thresholding positive total effects. Its holdout error example is explicitly noiseless, and it states a separate Monte Carlo-calculation premise. Those passages were read from the primary TeX. Therefore do NOT claim the C6 approximation/measure-transfer/thresholding mechanism is generically novel. Our version specializes an unnormalized binary probability-kernel contrast and spells out causal and noisy conditional-experiment inputs.

## C7. Finite confidence and acquisition costs

For J candidates, N iid paired-query trials per candidate, the model squared contrast lies in[0,1]. With probability≥1−α, all model estimates obey

\[
|\overline D_{\hat k}-D(\hat k)|\le
u_N:=\sqrt{\log(2J/\alpha)/(2N)}.
\]

Accepting a parent only when overlineD_hat−u_N>κ ε_obs has no false positives on that event, provided the error and coverage upper bounds are actually acquired. A probability query rounded and clipped to absolute accuracy u contributes at most2u to the root-score error (two query errors), or4u to the squared score. For rounded scores use the paid acceptance margin sqrt(max(0,overlineD_rounded−u_N))>sqrt(κ ε_obs)+2u. Round all queried probabilities on one common B-bit dyadic grid, with B=O(log(1/u)). Exact summation/squaring then uses O(B+logN)-bit accumulators and O(N) arithmetic updates, each polynomial in B+logN; model query/context costs are additional. No accumulating-denominator claim is made for unrelated arbitrary rationals. All statistical trials require context generation and two model probabilities, not two magical do-oracles.

`ACQUIRED_PAIRED_EXPERIMENT_CERTIFICATE.md` supplies a separate conditional-experiment validation method that avoids the inaccessible entropy floor. It pays repeatable target experiments and does not make offline observations magically intervenable.

Full support alone supplies no efficient discovery promise. Here is a sharp black-box query obstruction. Let Z range over2^m binary contexts, let A be fair, and consider an exact kernel oracle. The no-parent kernel is identically1/2. A parent alternative indexed by an unknown w∈{0,1}^m returns1/2+(2a−1)h at z=w and1/2 elsewhere, with fixed0<h<1/2. All joint cells remain positive; the resulting target kernel is causally minimal. An exact query at a non-w context has precisely the baseline transcript. Before hitting w, an adaptive randomized procedure querying at most K distinct contexts can hit a uniformly random w with probability at most K/2^m. If its no-parent false-positive probability is≤α, its average acceptance probability under this mixture of alternatives is≤α+K/2^m. Uniform sensitivity≥1−α consequently requires

\[
K\ge(1-2\alpha)2^m.
\tag{C7a}
\]

This is a black-box conditional-probability-query lower bound, not a computational lower bound for inspecting the weights of a particular neural architecture. No ordinary faithfulness promise is imposed here; it demonstrates the gap between kernel minimality/full support and a quantitatively useful effect margin. Under a uniform context law, the squared effect is D=2h²/2^m. A promised effect lower bound, structural restrictions or acquired witnessing contexts are needed for efficient support recovery. Giving an exact predictive oracle does not by itself supply any of them.
