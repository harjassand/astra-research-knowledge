# Independent gate of direct DAG disjunction extraction

Status: PASS for the universal construction under the assumptions below; PASS
for data-only replay of five bounded whole proofs. The universal structural-
presentation theorem is mathematical, not an implemented arbitrary-rule
compiler. This repairs an existing author claim; no new FDP priority is claimed.

## Exact assumptions and result

Fix a finite structural calculus G as in arXiv:2609.00422v1 Definitions2/4,
pp8–10. G extends FL_e, IMALL, or ILL in the corresponding signature. Assume
every structural rule is valid in the classical target LK_! (the paper's
Corollary7(ii) supplies this from L_G⊆LK_!). In particular it is Boolean-sound
when *=∧ and bang is interpreted as identity. Schematic rule slots accept
arbitrary formulas; there are no extra atomicity/freshness/context side conditions.

Input: an actual valid closed annotated **DAG** G-proof π of ⇒A∨B, with L total
formula occurrences including every antecedent multiplicity. Output: an actual
ordinary DAG G-proof τ of ⇒A or ⇒B, with

    l(τ) ≤ L²+20L+1.

No source contraction rule is added. No theorem search, conclusion-size
assumption, tree normalization, EF alias rule, or uniform-substitution inference
is introduced. Closed earlier source proofs can be reused as DAG endpoints;
that is an explicit premise of this proof model.

The recent paper claims FDP in Theorems15/16, p19. Theorem13, pp12–15, first
appeals to Theorem8 to replace π by a small tree. Our independent reconstruction
shows that this step is unnecessary. TREE_RESOURCE_BOUNDARY.md explains why it
cannot be used freely for genuine occurrence trees under the stated line metric.

## Independent Boolean preservation derivation

Associate a distinct angled atom a_F to every needed source formula F. These
atoms are in a disjoint namespace outside the entire original atom set.
Equality is actual syntactic formula equality after canonical interning, not an
unverified formula-id collision. Conceptual angled names are not EF aliases.
Let t(p)=a_p, t(c)=c∧a_c, t(F◦H)=(t(F)◦t(H))∧a_(F◦H), and
t(!F)=!t(F)∧a_(!F), exactly Definition9 of the primary.

For each distinct source row add its one local Horn clause:

| Rule | Clause |
|---|---|
| ⇒1 | ⇒a_1 |
| right top | ⇒a_top |
| R* | a_F,a_H⇒a_(F*H) |
| R∧ | a_F∧a_H⇒a_(F∧H) |
| R∨ | a_selected_child⇒a_result |
| R→ | names of its actual antecedent multiset ⇒a_(F→H) |
| R! | names of its actual !-antecedent multiset ⇒a_(!F) |

All other rules add none. Modal promotion assumptions a_(!γ)⇒!a_(!γ) in
Theorem13 are identities in the Boolean interpretation, so they are not needed
in the Horn extraction program T.

For every Boolean valuation satisfying T, each translated original row is true.
This is a topological induction, independent of tree conversion:

* t(F) always implies a_F. Identity and bottom/zero-left are immediate. Unit and
  top-right use their Horn heads. L1 strengthens the antecedent; R0 follows
  from an already false antecedent conjunction.
* Left ∧/∨/* follow because the translated principal contains the required
  child Boolean combination. Right ∧/* obtain the child names from their two
  earlier rows and then the added Horn head. Right ∨ uses its selected child.
* Left implication follows pointwise from the two translated premises.
  Right implication gets its logical implication from the earlier premise and
  its new name from the named antecedent Horn clause.
* Cut uses exactly the same t(F) in both premise slots. Bang-left/W!/C! are
  pointwise valid under bang-as-identity. R! follows from its earlier premise
  and its named !-context Horn clause.
* A structural rule applies with each schematic formula replaced by its
  translated formula and each multiset context translated with multiplicities.
  The fixed rule's assumed classical soundness gives its conclusion.

At the root, t(A∨B) implies a_A∨a_B. Thus T entails that disjunction. Every
definite positive Horn program has a least Boolean model obtained by forward
chaining its body atoms, ignoring literal1. That least model must contain a_A
or a_B. This reasoning avoids constructing the potentially huge t-formula
classical proof and does not silently assume IPC behavior after adding Peirce.

## Actual source proofs for every fired Horn clause

Replace a_F by F (standard substitution). Cache a **closed** source proof of
⇒I(h^s) for every Horn h, with I(Γ⇒C)=(product Γ)→C and empty product1.

Unit/top use their fixed 3-occurrence closed proofs. R*/R∧ use compound identity
followed by R→, charging3. R∨ uses identity, its R∨ inference, and R→, charging5.
For R→ and R!, use the **existing actual source conclusion row**. Fold its k
antecedent formulas by L* in a chosen consistent order, then close with R→.
For k=0 first apply L1, obtaining 1⇒C, and then R→. There is no copy of that
row's earlier derivation. No implication is acquired from a mere claimed
equivalence or theorem oracle.

While forward chaining, each known atom already has a proved closed ⇒F endpoint.
Build the fired clause's antecedent conjunctions by closed R∧, then its product
by closed R*. Repeated body atoms cite the same closed endpoint in the appropriate
slots; this is legal DAG sharing, not antecedent contraction. Empty body uses
a shared actual unit axiom ⇒1. From ⇒P and cached ⇒P→C append

    C⇒C;  (P→C)⇒C by L→;  ⇒C by cut.

These rows charge exactly2+2+1=5 occurrences. The final head a_A or a_B gives
the exact desired source disjunct. If one additionally wants Theorem13(iii),
append the fixed modal4 implications and combine the closed cached implications
by R∧. This also remains polynomial; it is unnecessary for direct extraction.

## Independently audited charge

Classical soundness excludes empty⇒empty, so every original row charges at
least one formula and the row count is ≤L.

There are at most L Horn clauses. Their total atom incidence is ≤3L: R*/R∧
use two body atoms plus one head, R∨ uses two total, and a context-width k
R→/R! row uses k+1, already charged in π. Only R∧ adds a conjunction connector.
The original DAG costs L and is retained once.

Closing one nonempty context of width k costs exactly k(k+1)/2 occurrences:
L* rows have widths k,k−1,...,2, followed by the one-formula R→ row. Empty
contexts cost3. Summing over selected distinct rows gives at most L²/2+3L.
Fixed Horn implications cost at most5L. Fired bodies add at most3L; the final
three-row eliminations at most5L, and the shared unit adds1. Therefore the
coarse bound L²+20L+1 safely covers even the bound L²/2+17L+1 obtained here.
All original structural-rule costs stay in the one retained source prefix.

Representation matters. For a tree-encoded ordinary source proof of encoded
size S, every generated formula has O(S) symbols and there are O(L²) charged
roots, so a coarse O(S³) output/time bound is available. For **common-formula-DAG
input**, a compact output has O(V+L) formula nodes and O(L²) row occurrences,
with indices/name/integer bits charged. Its ordinary unfolded printing can be
exponential in the compact input length. Polynomial time in L alone, or ordinary
symbol-size polynomial in an uncharged compact formula input, is not claimed.

Jalali prerequisite has not been obtained; its differing preprint numbering is
