BOUNDED PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISON, 2026-10-08
No exhaustive priority search; absence of a match is not evidence of novelty.

1. Exact chain solvability: Lieb, Schultz and Mattis, Two soluble models
of an antiferromagnetic chain, Ann. Phys. 16, 407-466 (1961),
https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(61)90115-4. Exact Jordan-Wigner/XY
solvability is established. The original full source was not re-audited;
the finite convention is reconstructed directly and independently checked.

2. Critical physical setting: Blosser et al., arXiv
https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.05243 (2017-07-17), Phys. Rev. B 96, 134406
(2017-10-03), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.96.134406.
The prior spin leaf supplies the z=2 saturation context. This source does
not prove our whole normalized density-operator trace experiment.

3. Quasi-free CAR foundations: Huzihiro Araki, On Quasifree States of CAR
and Bogoliubov Automorphisms, PRIMS 6, 385-442 (1970), publisher metadata
https://ems.press/journals/prims/articles/2511,
https://doi.org/10.2977/PRIMS/1195193913 (publisher date 1970-12-31).
Publisher metadata read; original full proof not audited. Quasi-free state
representations and implementability are established theory. Our summable
occupation-residual criterion and normal trace-limit application are not
claims to a new Shale criterion.

4. Gaussian trace distance/purification: Bittel, Mele, Eisert and Leone,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17953 (2024-09-26; v2 2025-01-14, v3
2025-10-09), PRX Quantum 6, 030341 (2025),
https://doi.org/10.1103/pzx6-nkfb.
Primary HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2409.17953v2 read around VIII.2,
Lemma 10 (partial reduction) and Lemma 11 (Gaussian purification),
parsed lines 551-567. Those finite Gaussian operations/continuity tools
are pre-existing. We give the explicit Majorana block purification and
ancillary particle-hole choice needed for the uniform number MGF.

5. Supplied-model Gaussian circuits: Nusseler, Dhand, Huelga and Plenio,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.05243 (2020-08-12; v2 2020-08-31),
Phys. Rev. A 104, 012415 (2021-07-16),
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.104.012415.
Primary HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2008.05243v2 read in the construction
and Appendix B.5-B.6: passive rotations, paired/blocked pure Gaussian form
and thermal-state construction. Known-Hamiltonian/state Gaussian circuits
are an equally informed preparation baseline. The present archive uses one
public sine embedding for unknown lambda,kappa, whose storage claim is
different from a state-dependent preparation circuit.

6. Supplied-state Gaussian MPS: Fishman and White,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.07701 (2015-04-29), PRB 92, 075132
(2015-08-21), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.92.075132.
Primary abstract/publisher scope read. Correlation-matrix-based transforms
are a strong informed baseline. A transform computed from each supplied
state is not an unknown-parameter common encoder/decoder, and its cost
must not be silently granted to the latter task.

7. Quantum experiment limits: Guta and Kahn,
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0512075 (2005-12-09), PRA 73, 052108
(2006). Primary abstract read. Quantum local asymptotic normality gives
uniform local channel comparisons for iid qubits and oscillator limits.
Our object is one correlated critical chain in a finite-size crossover;
experiment-level convergence and public recovery are established concepts,
not a new statistical viewpoint.

8. Compression and classical error floors: Yang, Bai, Chiribella and
Hayashi, https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03372 (2017-01-12; v3 2017-02-24).
Primary HTML https://arxiv.org/html/1701.03372v3 read in I-II and V,
parsed lines 601-655, and the projection/overflow channel around Eq. (11).
It already proves a nonvanishing measure-and-prepare benchmark for iid
qudit/displaced-thermal families via LAN and uses coherent cutoffs.
Thus a need for quantum memory and an overflow channel are not discoveries
here. Our candidate contribution is their explicitly certified application
to the open critical XY crossover, with a dimension-independent F-A
witness and local pairing response. Priority of the general F-A inequality
was not established by this bounded comparison.

9. Trace affinity concavity: Elliott H. Lieb, Convex trace functions and
the Wigner-Yanase-Dyson conjecture, Adv. Math. 11(3), 267-288 (1973-12),
https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(73)90011-X.
Primary publisher/author-record abstract read; original full proof not
audited. Joint concavity of affinity is a named established input in the
EB witness, not a new lemma credited to this work.
