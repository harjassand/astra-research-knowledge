## A2. Exact reflected Gaussian coefficient

The real Gaussian Fourier amplitudes give

    alpha_T(omega_1,omega_2)
       =exp[-T^2(omega_1-omega_2)^2/4]
                          r_T((omega_1+omega_2)/2),
    r_T(x)=Gamma sqrt[2sigma^2T^2/(1+2sigma^2T^2)]
       exp[-T^2(x-beta sigma^2/2)^2/(1+2sigma^2T^2)].

Consequently

    beta_e=beta*2sigma^2T^2/(1+2sigma^2T^2),
    alpha_T(-omega_2,-omega_1)
       =exp[-beta_e(omega_1+omega_2)/2]alpha_T(omega_1,omega_2). (A1)

For rho_e=exp(-beta_e H)/Z_e, spectral reindexing using A_omega*=A_-omega and A_omega rho_e=exp(-beta_e omega)rho_e A_omega proves

    integral J_nu rho_e J_nu* dnu=rho_e^(1/2)Q rho_e^(1/2),
    D_T(rho_e)=rho_e^(1/2)Q rho_e^(1/2)-(Q rho_e+rho_e Q)/2. (A2)

This is the same coefficient mechanism used in the primary Gaussian KMS sampler, but here the coherent term is NOT added and the equilibrium defect is retained.

## A3. Gaussian center-time averaging bounds the double commutator

Let C(s)=(2pi)^(-1)integral gamma(nu)e^(-i nu s)dnu. Explicitly

    C(s)=Gamma sigma/sqrt(2pi)
             exp[-sigma^2s^2/2]exp[-i beta sigma^2s/2],
    integral |C(s)|ds=Gamma.

Change variables v=(t+t')/2 and s=t-t' in the exact Q integral. Since f_T(t)f_T(t')=g_T(v)exp[-s^2/(4T^2)],

    g_T(v)=exp[-v^2/T^2]/(sqrt(pi)T),
    Q=integral g_T(v) exp(iHv) Q_0 exp(-iHv)dv,
    Q_0=integral exp[-s^2/(4T^2)]C(-s)
                        A(-s/2)*A(s/2)ds,
    ||Q_0||<=Gamma a^2.                                (A3)

The sign C(-s) follows the plus Fourier exponent in J; its modulus bound is unchanged. Twice integrating by parts, with Gaussian boundary terms zero, gives

    ||[H,[H,Q]]||<=||Q_0|| integral |g_T''(v)|dv
                          <=4Gamma a^2/T^2.            (A4)

The last inequality uses |g_T''|<=(4v^2/T^4+2/T^2)g_T and its second moment T^2/2. It is an ordinary operator-norm estimate, independent of dimension, Bohr-gap separation or the magnitude of H. No forbidden Gibbs right-multiplication bound is used.

## A4. Thermal midpoint quadrature gives the actual trace defect
