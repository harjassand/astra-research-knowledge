## Results

The original initial audit stands: the supplied N81 rare-herald reduction
survives its stated positive-herald, constant-TV decision interface
conditionally, but does not transfer to determinant-weighted hard-parity
counting. Exact parity support is not a weighted normalizer; graph support,
normalized sampling, and rare-event relative counting remain distinct.

The strongest attributed cross-review result is c03_l10's low-sector
random-sign Pfaffian estimator. I independently derived its expectation and
second-moment bounds for arbitrary paired rational-complex vectors. It gives
a relative approximation to `c_k(F)` in
`O(9^k epsilon^-2 log(1/delta))` exact polynomial-bit sample work, hence a
polynomial-time FPRAS when `k=O(log n)`. The formal square-root determinant
identity computes each sample exactly. At zero target every sample is zero;
on positive targets the FPRAS, not an individual zero observation, controls
false-zero probability.

I derived a row-prefix extension. For forced row sites `A`, excluded sites
`B`, `C=V_A`, `d=det(C^*C)`, and the orthogonal projector `P` off `span(C)`,

    W_k(A,B) = d * Z_(k-|A|)(P V_[n minus (A union B)]).

This is a positive projected-volume representation, avoiding
inclusion-exclusion cancellation. Applying the peer estimator to each child
prefix and choosing by estimated mass ratios gives a row-marginal sampler
with total variation at most epsilon and cost
`O(9^k n^3 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon))` polynomial-bit sample work, for
`c_k(F)>0`. Given the row set `I`, the allowed columns have exact
volume-sampling weights from `M=F[I,I^c]`; determinant-coefficient
self-reduction samples them exactly in expected polynomial bit time. Together
these provide a derived low-sector canonical hard-BCS TV sampler, without
inverting a sector probability. This is a mathematical construction, not an
implemented end-to-end sampler. An exact zero-sector report can use the
existing deterministic parity-support route; the randomized estimator alone
has bounded-error false-zero probability on positive input.

The result does not extend to `k=Theta(n)` with the current bound, establish
approximation hardness, imply a PP collapse, or validate novelty. Attributed
peer reports on treewidth, cut-rank, low-rank/sparse structure, functional
digraphs, and fermionic KLM gadget failures are compatible structural or
route-specific results; none closes the unrestricted high-sector gate.
