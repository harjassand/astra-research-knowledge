## A precise logical no-transfer from the degree criterion

Knowing a continuous coefficient map F satisfies <p,F(p)> >0 on a bounded domain's boundary is sufficient for zero existence. It does not certify a convex gradient, a monotone map, or a contracting residual iteration.

For example, in even-dimensional Euclidean space let J be block diagonal with blocks [[0,-1],[1,0]], and put

    F(x)=(||x||²-1/4)x+Jx,      ||x||≤1.

Then <x,F(x)>=3/4 on the unit sphere, but DF(0)=−I/4+J has negative symmetric part and is nonsymmetric. F cannot be the gradient of a C² convex potential. For every lambda>0, the derivative at zero of x↦x−lambda F(x) has singular values

    sqrt[(1+lambda/4)²+lambda²] >1.

The most direct residual descent therefore fails the local contraction test at the root, despite strict boundary positivity and a polynomial formula. This is a counterexample to the inference from the criterion, not a counterexample to the specialized Xu–Xu map or a hardness theorem for spherical designs. To transfer its proof efficiently one must independently obtain an algorithmic property of that specialized map: a quantitative monotonicity condition, a controlled continuation path, or another global acquisition invariant.

## New scoped obstruction: iid sampling with only O(T²) nodes

**Theorem.** Let T≥8, C>0 fixed, and 1≤N≤CT². Sample N independent uniform sphere points in one attempt. The probability that their mean satisfies the more permissive condition

    ||b||₂ ≤2 beta_T                           (B)

is at most

    6 exp[-(T²/128)exp(-64C)].                  (C)

Therefore a procedure that repeatedly draws an independent N-node iid set and accepts only (A) has expected retry count at least

    (1/6) exp[(T²/128)exp(-64C)],

whenever an attempt can succeed. For every fixed cardinality constant this is exponential in T² asymptotically. The constants are conservative and may make the quantitative onset enormous; the theorem is not a claim about a moderate-T optimizer's performance.

The conclusion survives deterministic rational rounding of each iid point within the existing delta_T mesh. It does not apply to correlated/stratified sampling, repulsion, gradient-based optimization, adaptive rejection at individual nodes, or a supplied design.

### 1. A low mean forces caps of radius O(1/T) to be occupied

Let n=floor((T−1)/2), q=n+2, and s=1−16/q². Since T≥8, q≥5 and 0<s<1. Under normalized sphere measure z=omega·u is uniform on [−1,1] for any fixed u. Define the orthonormal zonal polynomials

    phi_l(z)=sqrt(2l+1)P_l(z),       0≤l≤n.

The Legendre recurrence makes the matrix of multiplication by z on this basis tridiagonal. Its off-diagonal coefficient between l and l+1 is

    a_l=(l+1)/sqrt[(2l+1)(2l+3)] >1/2,         (1)

because (l+1)²−(2l+1)(2l+3)/4=1/4.

Choose the positive unit sine vector

    v_l=sqrt(2/q) sin[pi(l+1)/q],   0≤l≤n,
    p(z)=sum_(l=0)^n v_l phi_l(z).

The identity sum_(j=1)^(q−1) sin²(pi j/q)=q/2 normalizes it. The tridiagonal matrix with all off-diagonals equal to 1/2 sends v to cos(pi/q)v. Since v has positive coordinates and every a_l>1/2,

    integral z p(z)² d sigma >cos(pi/q)
                            ≥1−pi²/(2q²)
                            >1−8/q².          (2)

Now form the spherical polynomial

    Q_u(omega)=(omega·u−s)p(omega·u)².

Its degree is 2n+1≤T, it is nonpositive outside the open cap omega·u>s, and its mean is

    mu_u=integral Q_u d sigma >8/q².           (3)

For every z∈[−1,1], |P_l(z)|≤1. Therefore Cauchy–Schwarz gives |p(z)|≤n+1, and

    ||Q_u||₂ ≤2||p²||₂ ≤2(n+1).              (4)

Expand Q_u in the constant harmonic and our harmonics through T. Its nonconstant coefficient norm is at most (4). For a configuration satisfying (B),

    N^(-1)sum_i Q_u(omega_i)
       ≥mu_u−2(n+1)||b||₂
       >8/q²−4(n+1)beta_T >0.                 (5)

The last inequality follows from q≤T+1 and 4(n+1)beta_T≤1/[1024(T+1)^4]. If that cap contained no sample point, every summand on the left would be nonpositive. Thus every open cap with threshold s must contain a sample point.

This argument uses only the mean test, without the Gram test. It is a quantitative positive-polynomial coverage obstruction; its exact-design ancestors are established design prior, and novelty of this approximate formulation is UNKNOWN.

### 2. Pack many disjoint required caps

Write r=arccos s<pi/2. Such a cap has normalized area

    p_cap=(1−s)/2=8/q².

Take a maximal finite set of cap centers separated by at least 2r. Their radius-r open caps are disjoint up to boundaries. The radius-2r caps cover the sphere by maximality. A radius-2r cap has area

    sin²r=1−s²≤32/q².

If K is the number of centers, covering implies

    K≥q²/32≥T²/128.                          (6)

Every configuration satisfying (B) must occupy all K disjoint caps. The cap radius is O(1/T) since 1−cos r=16/q²; the argument needs their exact area rather than a numerical geodesic approximation.

### 3. Poissonize the iid occupancy problem

Let Z be Poisson with mean 2N, and draw Z iid uniform points. Counts in the K disjoint caps are independent Poisson variables, each of mean 2Np_cap. Hence

    Pr(all caps occupied in the Poisson experiment)
        =(1−exp[-2Np_cap])^K
        ≤exp[-K exp(-2Np_cap)].               (7)

Let f(j) be the probability all caps are occupied by j iid points. It increases with j. Paley–Zygmund for the nonnegative variable Z gives

    Pr(Z≥N) ≥N²/(4N²+2N)≥1/6.

Consequently E f(Z)≥f(N)/6. Combining this with (7), N≤CT², q≥T/2, and (6) gives

    Pr((B)) ≤f(N)
       ≤6 exp[-K exp(-16N/q²)]
       ≤6 exp[-(T²/128)exp(-64C)],

which proves (C). Independence of the Poisson cap counts follows directly by summing the multinomial probabilities against the Poisson mass; no geometric independence assumption is made about the fixed-N occupancies.

### 4. Rational rounding does not remove the obstruction

For the existing delta_T=2^(-14)(T+1)^(-7) rational stereographic approximation, the harmonic vector changes by at most

    2(T+1)² delta_T
        =1/[8192(T+1)^5]=beta_T/4.

If the rounded configuration passes (A), the underlying iid real configuration has mean norm at most 5beta_T/4<2beta_T. It is therefore included in (B), and the same probability upper bound applies. Collisions or a failed exact Gram check can only reduce acceptance.

## Consequence, remaining alternatives, and falsifiable next steps

The explicit design theorem changes the node-count constant but does not close acquisition complexity. Plain iid whole-set retry is asymptotically excluded at optimal order by the mean interface alone. The result does not claim all randomized design algorithms are inefficient.

A plausible surviving route is controlled descent from an equal-area stratification, maintaining a mesh or sampling invariant until the mean is tiny. A numerical stationary-point theorem alone would need a quantitative bound keeping that invariant during acquisition; favorable local iteration costs cannot substitute for it. Another surviving route is a computable non-topological consequence of the specialized cubed-sphere map. Neither is established here.

The largest substantive progress is the independently gated fixed-scale/linear-bit compiler. The iid theorem supplies a specific rejected comparator and focuses future acquisition work on correlated constructions and certified continuation. It does not improve source count, evade the near-shell barrier, or make a historical priority claim.
