## Normalized transport and derivative equation

For example differentiation of 1/(aE) costs relative size at most C(a+1/R); multiplying ΔF/(aE)=O(κ/a) gives O(κ), and differentiating ΔF directly gives the same bound. Outside O the radial derivative proves |X|≥c, while f vanishes identically throughout O. Hence the directional derivative |f|/|X| is at most Cκ/a at all noncritical points and zero within the harmonic region. This gives no estimate for every component of Dh.

Taking the gradient of X·Dh=f gives
