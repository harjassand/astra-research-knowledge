# Unique polynomial line ruling of the displayed Alpöge map

Cycle17, 7 October2026; hessian_bridge12, root-verified GPT-6.1 Sol/max. **Provisional programme deduction pending independent hostile gate.** No historical priority or external validation is claimed. This theorem classifies all globally polynomial source flows with affine target images; it is not a degree-bounded chart search or a claim about all five-dimensional source automorphisms.

## Question and precise source interface

Can a genuinely base/pivot-moving global three-dimensional source automorphism make the actual published Keller map affine in a new scalar coordinate, so that the published doubling/Schur construction has a new polynomial ruling to use?

Use the literal map in Meng–Yang arXiv2607.22198v2, Section6 and AppendixA:
\[
 F=h+t g,\qquad g=((1+xy)^3,3x(1+xy)^2,-x^3),\quad\det JF=-2.
\]
The map, counterexample and published Schur construction are prior. The author bridge assumes an affine named source coordinate; a new global source automorphism would retain the Keller Jacobian, but would have to make all three pulled-back outputs affine in the new scalar variable. This is exactly the hypothesis classified here.

**Theorem.** Let \(\delta\) be any locally nilpotent derivation of \(R=\mathbb C[x,y,t]\). Then
\[
 \delta^2F_i=0\ (i=1,2,3)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \delta=p(x,y)\partial_t\quad\text{for some }p\in\mathbb C[x,y]. \tag{1}
\]
In particular, every polynomial automorphism \(\Phi(B_1,B_2,\tau)\) for which \(F\circ\Phi\) is affine in \(\tau\) has the form
\[
 \Phi(B,\tau)=(\phi(B),a\tau+H(B)), \tag{2}
\]
where \(\phi\) is a polynomial automorphism of the old two-dimensional base and \(a\in\mathbb C^\times\) is constant. Thus this broad source-coordinate strategy cannot acquire a genuinely new affine pencil.

## Rational field certificate and the exact quadratic factor

Work first in the generic source fraction field \(K=\mathbb C(x,y,t)\). Set
\[
 w=y+1/x,\qquad v=1/x,\qquad c=F_3.
\]
They are algebraically independent and generate K: the rational inverse is
\[
 x=1/v,\quad y=w-v,\quad t=5v^2-3wv-cv^3.
\]
The literal map becomes
\[
 a=F_1=-cw^3+w^2+wv,\qquad
 b=F_2=-3cw^2+4w+2v,\qquad F_3=c. \tag{3}
\]
All these are exact, independently runnable substitution identities. Its reciprocal-variable cubic is
\[
 P_0(W)=cW^3-2W^2+bW-2a=(W-w)T_2(W), \tag{4}
\]
where
\[
 T_2(W)=cW^2+(cw-2)W-2cw^2+2w+2v.
\]
The discriminant is \((3cw-2)^2-8cv\). Over \(\mathbb C(c,w)(v)\), this is a linear polynomial in v with nonzero coefficient−8c, hence has an odd valuation at its irreducible zero and is not a rational square. Therefore T2 is irreducible over K. Also \(T_2(w)=2v\ne0\), so (4) is the complete coprime factorization of P0 into a linear and an irreducible quadratic factor. Finally
\[
 x\,[b-4w+3cw^2]=2. \tag{5}
\]

## Polynomial source flow and exhaustive gcd cases

Suppose \(\delta\) is locally nilpotent and \(\delta^2F=0\). The exponential gives a polynomial source flow \((x(s),y(s),t(s))\in R[s]^3\), and
\[
 F(s)=F+s q,\qquad q=\delta F\in R^3.
\]
If q=0, the invertible JF gives \(\delta=0\), already covered by (1). Otherwise
\[
 w(s)=y(s)+1/x(s)\in K(s),\quad w(0)=w,
\]
satisfies
\[
 P_0(w(s))+sQ(w(s))=0,\quad
 Q(W)=q_3W^3+q_2W-2q_1\ne0. \tag{6}
\]
Here the q entries need not be constants: they are fixed elements of K when considering the flow parameter s. Their constancy along the flow follows from \(\delta^2F=0\); the displayed polynomial flow formula is exact.

Let \(G=\gcd(P_0,Q)\) in K[W]. The only possibilities from (4) are 1, W−w, T2 and P0. The last is impossible: a nonzero scalar multiple of P0 has nonzero W² coefficient, whereas Q has W² coefficient0. After dividing by G, the remaining pair is coprime. Its polynomial \(\widetilde P+s\widetilde Q\) is primitive and linear in s, hence irreducible in K[W,s]. If it has positive W degree it is also primitive over K[s], so Gauss makes it irreducible over K(s). This is an exact algebraic irreducibility argument, not a finite factorization extrapolation.

If G=1, the remaining W degree is3 and no rational root exists. If G=W−w, the remaining W degree is2 and has no rational root; the only rational root of the full polynomial is the fixed root w. The quadratic T2 remains irreducible under the purely transcendental extension K(s). Thus a moving rational w(s) can arise only when G=T2, from its remaining linear factor.

## The only moving rational branch is not a polynomial source flow

If T2 divides Q, then q3!=0 because Q otherwise has degree at most1. Write \(q_3=c^2k\), with \(k\in K^\times\). Its zero W² coefficient forces
\[
 Q(W)=ck\,T_2(W)(W+2/c-w),
\]
and therefore
\[
 w(s)=w-\frac{2sk}{1+sck}. \tag{7}
\]
Comparing W coefficients also gives
\[
 q_2=k(-3c^2w^2+6cw+2cv-4),
\]
\[
 q_1=k(cw^2-w-v)(2-cw).
\]
Apply the exact source identity (5) along this proposed flow. With \(c(s)=c+sc^2k\), \(b(s)=b+s q_2\), direct simplification gives
\[
 b(s)-4w(s)+3c(s)w(s)^2
 =\frac{2N(s)}{1+sck},
\]
\[
 N(s)=v+sk(2cv-3cw+2)
 +s^2ck^2\,[c(v-3w)+4].
\]
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{x(s)=\frac{1+sck}{N(s)}.} \tag{8}
\]
The leading coefficient of N is nonzero: c,k are nonzero in K, and \(c(v-3w)+4\ne0\) by algebraic independence of c,w,v. Thus N has degree2 in s while the numerator has degree1. A polynomial x(s) would require N to divide that numerator in K[s], impossible by degree. Cancellation cannot alter this divisibility conclusion. The proposed moving branch is rational but cannot be the polynomial source flow of a locally nilpotent derivation.

For scope control, its y(s) does happen to be polynomial:
\[
 y(s)=w-v+sk(-cv+3cw-4).
\]
One polynomial recovered coordinate does not certify a global source flow; (8) is the decisive obstruction.

## Fixed reciprocal variable forces the original base foliation

We have proved w(s)=w. Differentiate at s=0:
\[
 \delta x=x^2\delta y\in(x).
\]
For a locally nilpotent derivation on a characteristic-zero domain, \(\delta f\in(f)\) implies \(\delta f=0\). Its finite nilpotence degree is additive on products; a nonzero \(\delta f=fk\) would have degree both \(\deg_\delta f-1\) and at least \(\deg_\delta f\). Applying this to x gives \(\delta x=0\), hence \(\delta y=0\).

It follows that \(\delta=p(x,y,t)\partial_t\). Passing to \(\mathbb C(x,y)[t]\), a locally nilpotent one-variable derivation has coefficient independent of t: if its coefficient has t degree at least1, repeated differentiation never vanishes. Therefore p belongs to \(\mathbb C[x,y]\). Conversely each such p gives a locally nilpotent derivation and \(\delta^2F=0\), since F is affine in t. This proves (1).

The use of x!=0 and c!=0 was only inside the generic fraction field. The resulting identities \(\delta x=\delta y=0\) are polynomial identities in R, and hence hold on the special loci x=0 or c=0 as well. No fibre or point on those loci has been discarded.

## Global source-automorphism consequence and downstream use

For a global source automorphism \(\Phi\) satisfying the affine-pencil hypothesis, push forward \(\partial_\tau\). It is a locally nilpotent derivation with a polynomial slice, and (1) says it equals p(x,y)∂t. A slice implies p is a unit of R, so p=a is a nonzero constant. Old x,y are independent of τ and old t has the form aτ+H(B). The inverse new-base coordinates are invariant under a∂t and are therefore independent of old t; hence the base map has a polynomial inverse. This proves (2).
