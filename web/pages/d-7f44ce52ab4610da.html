# A stationary rational weight with a restricted maximum and a full escape

2026-10-07. Universal exclusion for a structured n5 family, with an exact rational instance. Not a full weighted OJD strict-saddle theorem or coefficient-only acquisition algorithm. Historical priority unverified.

## Family, symmetry and exact stationarity

Let n5, P=I−J5/5, PB=diag(0,I4−J4/4), e=e0 and z=(0,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2). Let W(c,s) rotate the span of e,z and fix its orthogonal complement:

  W00=c, W0B=(s/2)1^T, WB0=−(s/2)1,
  WBB=I4+(c−1)J4/4, c²+s²=1.

This is a non-reflection one-parameter orthogonal family. It commutes with every permutation of the last four coordinates. For any contrast-positive G, average PGP over this S4. The result is b PB+gamma(P−PB), with b,gamma>0. Any full stationary point for the original weight remains stationary for the average because conjugation fixes W. On the whole invariant W(c,s) family its objective is exactly equal to that for the average.

The matrix M=W^{circ2} acts on block contrasts by (1+c)/2 and on the one-dimensional contrast of the two group means by (5c²−1)/4. Thus, modulo an additive gauge constant,

  F(c)=3b(1+c)²/4+gamma(5c²−1)²/16.

For s nonzero, full stationarity implies F'(c)=0, hence

  b/gamma=−5c(5c²−1)/[6(1+c)].                       (1)

As b,gamma>0 and −1<c<1, (1) permits exactly

  −1<c<−1/sqrt5, or 0<c<1/sqrt5.                    (2)

## A full tangent direction that excludes every permitted point

Put u=(0,1,−1,0,0), A=e u^T−u e^T. Exact second differentiation along W exp(tA), using the general Hessian matrix formula in `exact_sdp_landscape.py`, gives for the averaged weight

  F''(A)=−[16bc²+6bc−10b+35gamma c⁴−15gamma c²]/2.

Substituting (1) simplifies this to

  F''(A)/gamma=5c(c−1)(19c²−6c−5)/6.                (3)

For 0<c<1/sqrt5, the final quadratic is less than19/5−5−6c<0 while c(c−1)<0. For −1<c<−1/sqrt5, the quadratic is greater than19/5+6/sqrt5−5>0 while c(c−1)>0. Thus (3) is positive throughout (2).

For the original, possibly non-S4-invariant G, equation (3) is the average curvature over the S4-conjugates of A. Positivity of the average proves at least one of those exact tangent directions has positive curvature. It does NOT assert the particular displayed A always escapes for an arbitrary unaveraged G.

At c1,s0, W=I is a true axis solution. At c−1,s0, W is diag(−1,H4), and the block uniform-reflection curvature exclusion applies. Hence every non-axis stationary point in this whole family has a full tangent escape for every contrast-positive weight.

## A rational instance where the restricted family gives the wrong impression

Choose c13/85,s84/85, gamma1, b4147/42483 and

  G=b PB+(P−PB)+J5/5.

All entries are rational. Its eigenvalues are1 on1 and the between-group contrast, and b (multiplicity3), so G is PD. Exact stationarity coefficients all vanish. Nevertheless

  d²F(c)/dc²=−9417/14161<0.

The stationary point is therefore a strict maximum along the one-dimensional mean-rotation family, and F'' in its theta tangent is this coefficient times s². It is not a full local maximum. The transverse direction A in (3) gives

  F''(A)=6168864/10440125>0.

`block_rotation_escape.py` retains the full rational W,G and checks stationarity and the exact transverse Hessian. Its 2x2 transverse block has positive determinant38193725087232/42726514326125 and positive trace1587313808/306939675. Floating full-Hessian eigenvalues are exploratory only. The exact positive diagonal suffices to refute a full maximum.

The full-coefficient inverse SDP returned numerical infeasible for this W and four other block-rotation inputs. This result is supporting evidence; the mathematical exclusion is (3), rather than a solver status or finite sampling.
