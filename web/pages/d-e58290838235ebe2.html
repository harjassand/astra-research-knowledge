CYCLE 6 REVISION — THE ONE-SWAP CROSS-REPLICA WORM STILL BOTTLENECKS
Worker: c01_l04. Work continued after the immutable INITIAL checkpoint.

CLAIM
The epsilon-regularized six-cycle also defeats a natural paired cross-replica repair. Add to the initial kernel an exact Metropolis proposal that exchanges one mismatched coordinate between replicas. The resulting chain remains irreducible, but the pair state (O,O) has conductance exactly half the one-replica mode conductance. For the additive test G(A,B)=1[A=O]+1[B=O], exact Fraction computations at b=4,8 yield required L values 767.63 and 196607.27. A direct mass bound proves L=Omega(epsilon^-2) for this augmented kernel. The same singleton-cut proof applies to any symmetric-proposal product Metropolis kernel whose moves out of (O,O) change each replica by at most two coordinate exchanges; the alternate heavy mode E is three exchanges away.

CROSS-REPLICA MOVE
For A,B in Omega_+, if A!=B choose i uniformly from A\\B and j uniformly from B\\A; replace
  (A,B) by ((A\\{i}) union {j}, (B\\{j}) union {i}).
If A=B, hold. The proposal is symmetric: both difference sets have the same size r, and the reverse move chooses the same swapped pair with probability 1/r^2. Accept by Metropolis using product weight w(A)w(B). Call this kernel C. The augmented pair kernel is
  P_cross = (1/4)Swap + (1/4)(M tensor Id) + (1/4)(Id tensor M) + (1/4)C.
All four components are reversible for pi tensor pi. The local M moves retain irreducibility on the positive product support.

PAIR CONDUCTANCE
At x0=(O,O), Swap and C hold. The two local updates are selected with total probability 1/2, each exits O with probability Phi_M({O}); hence
  P_cross(x0,x0^c)=Phi_M({O})/2
     =(epsilon^2+epsilon^4)/(6(1+epsilon^3)^2).
Since pi(O)^2<1/4, {x0} is the smaller side, so this is the exact singleton conductance. It is Theta(epsilon^2), with pair mass tending to 1/4. The defect-exchange move does not help because the identical-replica state has no mismatch to exchange.

ADDITIVE-ENERGY UPPER BOUND FOR THE AUGMENTED KERNEL
Let H={O,E}, let q=pi(Omega_+\\H), and f=1[O]. The minor table gives
  q=6epsilon^2(1+epsilon^2)/Z <=3epsilon^2(1+epsilon^2),
  Var_pi f=p(1-p), p=pi(O), and p<1/2.
For epsilon<=1/4, p>1/4, so Var_pi f>1/8.
The local M components contribute p Phi/2 <=5epsilon^2/24 to the Dirichlet energy of G=f(A)+f(B); Swap contributes zero. For C, starting pairs outside HxH have probability at most 2q and |Delta G|<=2, so its unit-kernel energy there is at most 4q. On HxH, equal replicas hold; unequal mode pairs are (O,E) or (E,O), and every cross-exchange destination has both replicas one-swap neighbors of modes, each of weight at most epsilon^2. Their product Metropolis acceptance is at most epsilon^4, so that part of the unit-kernel energy is at most epsilon^4/2. After its mixture weight 1/4,
  E_Pcross(G) <=5epsilon^2/24 + q + epsilon^4/8 <4epsilon^2
for epsilon<=1/4. Therefore the claimed inequality Var_pi f <= L E_Pcross(G) requires L>1/(32epsilon^2). This is a proved exponential-in-b lower bound, though the constant is deliberately loose. The exact enumerator gives the near-asymptotic constants below.

LOCALITY THRESHOLD / REPAIR
Any non-mode state within Johnson distance 1 or 2 of O has amplitude in {0,epsilon,epsilon^2}; E is at distance 3. Thus any symmetric-proposal product Metropolis move from (O,O) that changes each replica by at most two exchanges has acceptance at most epsilon^2 unless it leaves (O,O) unchanged. Its singleton conductance is at most epsilon^2. This covers local updates in either replica, one-swap cross-replica moves, and pair updates bounded to two exchanges per replica. A direct complement proposal O<->E has acceptance one and constant proposal mass, so it removes this particular barrier. Constructing such a nonlocal, acquired cycle proposal uniformly for arbitrary rational F is precisely outside this counterexample.
