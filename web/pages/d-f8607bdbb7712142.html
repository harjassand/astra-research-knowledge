# Distinct quaternion classes obstruct every single-K3 tensor carrier

Status: a representation/Hodge-theoretic obstruction derived below. It uses the established Zarhin classification, not any new HC or KS manuscript. It is not a disproof of generalized Hodge, nor an algebraicity theorem for the crossed spin construction.

## Exact interface

Let E be a totally real number field of degree e>=2, with fixed active real embedding sigma0 and embedding set Sigma. For actual projective K3 surfaces in the rank-three interface below, 3e<=21, hence 2<=e<=7. Let V_i=T(S_i)(1), i=1,2, be normalized transcendental Hodge structures of projective complex K3 surfaces with End_Hdg(V_i)=E and dim_E V_i=3. Assume both are active at sigma0, and

    Hg(V_1 direct-sum V_2) = G_1 times G_2,
    G_i = Res_(E/Q) SO(V_i,q_i).

Write D_i=C_E^+(V_i,q_i). Thus D_i is split at sigma0 and quaternionic at every other real embedding. Assume [D_1] != [D_2] in Br(E).

Choose ANY absolute-Galois orbit O in the set of ordered DISTINCT embedding pairs (sigma,tau) in Sigma times Sigma. Let H=H_O be the rational summand of V_1 tensor_Q V_2 selected by the corresponding primitive idempotent of the finite etale algebra E tensor_Q E. Then

    H_C = direct-sum_(sigma,tau in O)
          (V_1,sigma tensor V_2,tau).

Every coordinate projection of O is all of Sigma: its image is nonempty and Galois-stable, and Galois acts transitively on Sigma. No pair has both coordinates sigma0. Consequently H has normalized Hodge level EXACTLY two. The corresponding substructure of H^4(S_1 times S_2) has Hodge coniveau exactly one.

The previous quadratic case is recovered by Sigma={sigma0,sigma1}; its sole off-diagonal orbit gives the two displayed crossed summands from the original report.

**Claim.** For every projective complex K3 surface S, H is absent from the rational tensor category generated by U=T(S)(1). In particular Hom_Hdg(wedge^k U,H)=0 for every k, and no direct sum of exterior powers of the normalized H^2 of one S surjects onto H. The same statement holds for arbitrary finite sums of tensor words in U and its duals, with weight-compatible Tate twists.

The claim does not assume that U belongs to the tensor category generated by V_1,V_2. Thus it also rules out an auxiliary K3 constructed by any other method, provided the proposed transport is an actual rational Hodge map from a tensor object generated by that single K3.

## Proof

### 1. The cross summand remembers the whole original product group

Over Qbar, each G_i has e adjoint A1 factors. There is no hidden assumption that E tensor E splits as copies of E. Instead

    (E tensor_Q E) tensor_Q Qbar = product_(Sigma times Sigma) Qbar.

The indicator of O is Galois-invariant and therefore descends to a rational primitive idempotent. Its image H is the displayed direct sum. Every summand is an irreducible standard tensor standard representation of the split original product. The summands belonging to different ordered pairs are pairwise inequivalent, and each occurs ONCE. A rational sub-Hodge structure of H becomes a sum of some of these modules; the set selected is Galois-stable. Since O is one orbit, that set is empty or all of O. Thus H is rationally simple. Multiplicity one in this explicit rational realization also avoids any Schur-index or rational-projector gap.

The representation of G_1 times G_2 on H is faithful. Indeed the standard representation of SO_3 is faithful and its only scalar element is the identity. If A tensor B acts as the identity, A and B are reciprocal scalar matrices; hence both are identity. Since both coordinate projections of O hit every embedding, the summands detect every simple factor of both original groups. Consequently

    Hg(H) = G_1 times G_2.

This conclusion uses the full product hypothesis. It is false in general when the two original Hodge groups are linked diagonally.

### 2. A single-K3 tensor carrier would give a product quotient

Suppose H belongs to the rational tensor category generated by U. The associated Mumford-Tate/Hodge representation then induces a surjection

    Hg(U) -> Hg(H) = G_1 times G_2.

For weight-zero normalizations this is directly the Hodge-group quotient. If Tate objects are included, their central torus has trivial image in the adjoint semisimple target, so the same necessary surjection exists from the derived group. Adding the algebraic part of H^2(S)(1), which is a trivial Hodge representation, changes nothing.

### 3. Zarhin leaves only the real-multiplication SO_4 case

Put F=End_Hdg(T(S)) and m=dim_F T(S). Zarhin's classification gives:

* If F is totally real, Hg(U)=Res_(F/Q) SO(U,Phi), with m>=3.
* If F is CM with maximal totally real subfield F0, Hg(U)=Res_(F0/Q) U(U,Phi). Its derived group is Res_(F0/Q) SU(U,Phi), trivial for m=1 and Q-simple for m>=2.

Here Q-simple means that its Lie algebra has no proper nonzero rational ideal. Restriction of scalars of an absolutely simple group is Q-simple: its absolute simple factors are indexed by embeddings of the field and Galois acts transitively on them. Thus a CM K3 cannot have a quotient equal to the product of the two nontrivial Q-simple groups G_1,G_2. Its central torus cannot supply either target factor.

For totally real F, SO_m is absolutely simple for m=3 or m>=5, so the same exclusion applies. For m=4 with nonsquare discriminant, the two absolute A1 factors are exchanged over the discriminant quadratic extension; after restriction to Q all absolute factors form one Galois orbit. More explicitly, they are indexed by embeddings into Qbar of the discriminant extension L=F(sqrt(disc(Phi))). Since L is a field, this set is Galois-transitive. A rational Lie ideal extends to a Galois-stable sum of absolute simple ideals, so it is zero or the whole Lie algebra. This is again Q-simple.

Therefore the only possible case is m=4 and square discriminant over F. Its adjoint group is

    Hg(U)^ad = B times B,
    B = Res_(F/Q) PGL_1(D)

for one quaternion algebra D/F.

For completeness, equality of the two quaternion classes needs no appeal to a hidden representation-descent assumption. A four-dimensional quadratic form with square discriminant is, up to similarity, diagonal <1,x,y,xy>, hence the reduced norm of D=(-x,-y). Left and right multiplication give Spin(q) = SL_1(D) times SL_1(D), with the usual diagonal central kernel in its action on D. Both adjoint components therefore involve the same D. Equivalently the two even Clifford components have the same Brauer class.

### 4. A product quotient forces equal original quaternion classes

Each B is Q-simple. A surjection B times B -> G_1 times G_2 induces a bijection between the two rational simple Lie ideals: the image of a domain simple ideal is a normal simple ideal of the target, and neither target ideal can be omitted. Thus, after possibly exchanging the two factors,

    Lie(B) ~= Lie(G_1),  Lie(B) ~= Lie(G_2).

The centroid of Lie(Res_(F/Q) PGL_1(D)) is F. The centroid of Lie(G_i) is E. Lie isomorphisms consequently supply field isomorphisms phi_i:F -> E; in particular [F:Q]=e. Classification of forms of A1 (or reconstruction of the quaternion algebra from its trace-zero Lie algebra and Killing form) gives

    [D_i] = phi_i([D]).

Put alpha=phi_2 phi_1^(-1), an automorphism of E. Then [D_2]=alpha([D_1]). Both D_i have the same UNIQUE split real embedding sigma0. The split place of alpha(D_1) is sigma0 composed with alpha^(-1); equality of the singleton split-place sets therefore gives sigma0 composed with alpha=sigma0. Since sigma0:E->R is injective, this equality implies alpha(x)=x for every x in E. Thus alpha is the identity, and [D_1]=[D_2], contradicting the hypothesis. QED.

## What the obstruction resolves

It excludes every higher-rank or higher-spin auxiliary **single K3** as a tensor carrier for the full rational cross H, not just the previously tested same-embedding spin_1 tensor spin_2 character. An arbitrary exterior degree, a larger real endomorphism field, a CM auxiliary, or a larger transcendental dimension cannot avoid the quotient argument. In fact the proof does not use the K3 rank bound or lattice realizability: it applies to any simple polarizable rational Hodge structure of K3 type with no rational (0,0) part after normalization.

The proof does not exclude an H^2 carrier on an arbitrary abelian or other smooth projective variety. In particular the crossed weight-one spin realization can still exist. Its algebraic return to H remains the separate crossed-spin shuffle problem. Nor does this show that two or more exterior returns are mathematically necessary in every possible geometric-coniveau argument; it establishes that the advertised one-K3 exterior-return mechanism has no carrier here.

The real-place condition is essential to the last step. Without a common active embedding, the general necessary conclusion is only that the two quaternion classes agree after a field automorphism. The full product Hodge-group condition is also essential.
