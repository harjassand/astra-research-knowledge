## Why the scores are exactly equal

The unitary-orbit SLD convention is `-i[A,tau]=(tau S_A(tau)+S_A(tau)tau)/2`. A faithful tau gives a unique Hermitian solution. In sigma's eigenbasis the filtered generator is `K_A,ij=2sqrt(p_i p_j)/(p_i+p_j) B_A,ij`, exactly the matrix `c elementwise B_A` used in F. The exact zero makes the all-ones vector a common eigenvector. Thus all K_A commute with `Pw=11^T/64`.

The state-dependent fibre identity follows by substituting `rho=sigma^(1/2)Q sigma^(1/2)` into the SLD equation fixed at sigma. Multiplying on both sides by sigma^(-1/2) makes this equation exactly `[Q,K_A]=0`: in sigma's eigenbasis the two factors `sigma^(-1/2)A sigma^(1/2)-(i/2)sigma^(-1/2)S_A(sigma)sigma^(1/2)` and its corresponding right factor both equal K_A.

Choose `Q=I/2+32Pw`. It has diagonal one, eigenvalues 1/2 and 65/2, and commutes with every K_A. Hence rho is normalized, faithful, different from sigma, and `S_A(rho)=S_A(sigma)` for all 12 fixed physical local X/Z operators. This is equality of full pointwise SLD operators, not merely equal expectations. It does not claim equality along every many-body unitary direction.
