SU3 WALL CROSSOVER: A RIGOROUS OBSTRUCTION TO THE GENERIC-FILTER UPPER
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE. Optimal wall-uniform law is UNKNOWN.

This note disproves the inference that the regular SU3 energy-to-full-
Gibbs upper for ANY positive filter extends with constants independent
of the ratio b/a. It does not disprove the optimal e*_Q≈Lambda(Q)/N^2
law: a different rank-Q filter or conditional decoder might attain it.
The arbitrary-code lower Lambda/N^2 remains uniform for a+b≈N.

1. EXACT MIXED-ADJOINT OPERATOR

Use SU3 Hermitian generators with Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2,
carrier T_A, D_A=d_ABC T_BT_C, quadratic/cubic Casimirs C2,C3,
alpha=C3/C2 and

  S_A=(D_A-alpha T_A)/N,
  Delta=C2(C2/3+1/4)-C3^2/C2,
  C_S=Delta/N^2,
  Tr(T_AS_B)=0,    Tr(S_AS_B)=d C_S delta_AB/8.                (1.1)

Let L=sum_A ad(T_A)^2 and M=sum_A ad(T_A)ad(S_A). Covariance and
Jacobi give exact adjoint block identities

  L(T_B)=3T_B,                 L(S_B)=3S_B,
  M(T_B)=3S_B,
  M(S_B)=3(C_S/C2)T_B-(6alpha/N)S_B.                         (1.2)

In particular, for every B,

  E_TS(S_B)/E_T(S_B)=-2alpha/N,
  E_TS(F)=sum_A Tr([S_A,F]^dagger[T_A,F]).                    (1.3)

The diagonal coefficient in the second adjoint is not small when
a≈N and b=o(N). Orthogonal Gram conditioning alone misses this term.

2. PROOF OF THE LOAD-BEARING UNIVERSAL VECTOR IDENTITY

The nontrivial identity behind (1.2) is

       sum_A[T_A,[D_A,D_B]]=(C2+3/4)T_B.                    (2.1)

Here is a finite PBW proof, rather than an extrapolation from matrices.
The left side is an adjoint covariant of filtration degree at most3.
The exact symmetric-power decompositions of the adjoint8 are

  Sym^1(8)=8,
  Sym^2(8)=27+8+1,
  Sym^3(8)=64+27+10+10bar+8+1.                              (2.2)

They follow by expanding the adjoint weight character (six roots and
two zero weights) and the Weyl characters; verify_wall_su3.py checks
those finite Laurent-character equalities with integers. Each degree
1,2,3 has exactly one adjoint copy. PBW symmetrization is equivariant,
so all universal adjoint covariants up to degree3 lie in the span of
T_B,D_B,C2 T_B, distinguished by their nonzero leading symbols.

Under the SU3 outer conjugation automorphism, T has one adjoint parity
and D the opposite parity (the defining cubic tensor d changes sign).
The left side of (2.1) involves one T and two D, so it has the parity
of T. Its D coefficient is zero. It equals u C2 T_B+w T_B.

Use the conventional Gell--Mann ordering in this one scalar-symbol
calculation (the identity itself is basis independent). Evaluate the
top symbol at x_A=0 except x_8=t. The symbol of D_A is
d_Aij x_i x_j. Commutator symbols are i times Lie--Poisson bracket,
and covariance makes the top symbol of the left side

  -4t^3 sum_(A,C,j,l) f_A8C f_jl8 d_Aj8 d_Cl8.

The d_Aj8 matrix is diagonal. The only relevant ordered root pairs
(A,C) are (4,5),(5,4),(6,7),(7,6), with f_AC8^2=3/4 and
d_AA8=d_CC8=-1/(2sqrt3). Since f_A8C=-f_AC8, the sum is t^3.
Thus u=1 in the generator normalization of (1.1). In the defining
carrier D_B=(5/6)T_B, C2=4/3 and L(T_B)=3T_B. The left side is then
(25/36)*3T_B=25T_B/12, giving w=25/12-4/3=3/4. This proves (2.1)
as a universal enveloping-algebra identity in EVERY SU3 carrier.

For (1.2), also sum_A[T_A,[D_A,T_B]]=3D_B. Insert
S=(D-alpha T)/N, use (2.1), L(T)=3T,L(D)=3D and
Delta=C2(C2/3+1/4)-alpha^2 C2. Direct expansion gives
M(S)=3(C_S/C2)T-6alpha S/N. Taking trace against S_B proves (1.3).

3. A RANK-DEFICIENT POSITIVE FILTER WITH ORDER 1/a GIBBS ERROR

Take the regular carrier (a,b)=(a,1), N=a, integer a>=144. Its exact
parameters simplify to

  d=(a+1)(a+3),
  C2=(a+2)^2/3,
  alpha=(a+2)/3-3/(a+2),
  C_S=3/4+3/a.                                               (3.1)

Thus 3/4<=C_S<=9/4 and 1/4<=alpha/a<=1/2 for a>=2.
Fix one normalized fundamental generator H=t_B with three distinct
eigenvalues, e.g. a root-offdiagonal generator with eigenvalues1/2,
-1/2,0. This is a fixed regular H, not a varying Gibbs direction.
Let c=-lambda_min(S_B)>0 and put

                         F=S_B+cI.                          (3.2)

S_B is nonzero traceless Hermitian, so c>0. F is positive and singular.
Set Q_a=rank F<d and S_F=Tr F^2. Exact Gram and scalar sum S_A^2=C_S I
give

  C_S/8<=S_F/d=C_S/8+c^2<=9C_S/8<=81/32,
  E_T(F)=3d C_S/8,
  1/3<=E_T(F)/S_F<=3.                                       (3.3)

The rank constraint is actual, not a truncation of a full-rank witness.
The channel Phi_F=(d/S_F)int F_g(.)F_g dg has legal branches Q_a and
can be exactly realized with finitely many labels by Choi cubature.

Let D_F(X)=int[F_g,[F_g,X]]dg. The exact two-copy coefficient formula
for arbitrary Hermitian F is

  D_F(T_A)=uT_A+vS_A,
  u=E_T(F)/(d C2),     v=E_TS(F)/(d C_S).

Scalar shifts do not affect any commutator. By (1.3) for the filter(3.2),

  u=3C_S/(8C2),      v=-6alpha/(8a),
  D_F(B_e)=3C_S T_B/(8C2 a)-6alpha S_B/(8a^2),
  B_e=T_B/a.                                               (3.4)

For every component, ||S_B||op<=sqrt C_S and
Tr(S_B^2)/d=C_S/8, so ||S_B||1/d>=sqrt(C_S)/8. Also
||T_B||1/d<=sqrt C2. The triangle inequality and (3.1) give

  ||D_F(B_e)||1/d
      >=3sqrt3/(256a)-27sqrt3/(32a^2)
      >=L0/a,       L0=3sqrt3/512,      a>=144.              (3.5)

This already rules out a uniform bound ||D_F(B)||1<=const E(F)/a^2
for every positive deficient F. It is a factor-a discrepancy.

The failure persists for a full positive Gibbs state, not just its
linearization. One has ||B_e||op<=2. The ORDERED derivation product
rule, now with ||B||<=2 rather than shifting to a contraction, gives

 ||D_F(exp(hB))-hD_F(B)||1
    <=h(exp(2h)-1)||D_F(B)||1
       +h^2 exp(2h)(1/8)E_T(F)/a^2.                        (3.6)

Each same-factor second derivative uses its exact averaged D_F(B);
cross terms use int||[F_g,B]||HS^2=(1/8)E_T(F)/a^2. Thus no
commutation or uncontrolled higher-order remainder is being assumed.
For 0<h<=1/8, exp(2h)<=4/3. Since E_T(F)/d<=27/32 and a>=144,
(3.5)--(3.6) yield

  ||D_F(exp(hB))||1 >= (h/2)||D_F(B)||1
                           >=h L0 d/(2a).                  (3.7)

Finally Z_h<=d exp(2h)<=4d/3 and Phi_F-I=-dD_F/(2S_F), so the
FULL half-trace risk satisfies

  (1/2)||Phi_F(tau_h)-tau_h||1
      >= h L0/(27a) = h sqrt3/(4608a).                      (3.8)

It is also bounded above by a constant/a by the same ordered estimates
and S_F/d>=C_S/8. Hence this actual legal filter has error Theta(1/a)
uniformly, for example on h in [1/16,1/8], while E(F)/(S_F a^2)=
Theta(1/a^2). All constants are fixed before a or Q_a.

4. WHAT IS AND IS NOT REFUTED

Refuted: applying the regular-cone estimate uniformly to arbitrary
positive low-energy filters as one highest-weight label approaches a
wall. The cross-adjoint term does not cancel generically, even on a
regular Gibbs conjugacy orbit. Raw Gram conditioning cannot be replaced
by the assertion that normalized E_S and E_T are uniformly comparable.

NOT REFUTED: the optimal task theorem e*_Q≈Lambda(Q)/N^2 with constants
uniform over a+b≈N. The filter above need not minimize either task
error or Lambda. A fiber-preserving cap or a different covariant
conditional decoder could avoid its cross-adjoint defect. Producing
one bad filter is not a minimax lower bound on every legal Q-code.

The lower c Lambda(Q)/N^2 remains uniform across the entire scale
a+b≈N: bounded scalar Casimir, adjoint orthogonality and the nonunital
Gibbs self-score do not require an invertible second-copy Gram.
Pure-wall carriers have the separate already proved symmetric-carrier
upper. The genuine bridge at a≈N,b≈N^beta (0<beta<1) remains open.
Its necessary gate is an actual good code/matching spectral profile,
or an arbitrary-code lower stronger than the scalar Casimir score.

Subsequent distinct progress is now saved in WEIGHTED_POSTERIOR_CONVERSE.txt:
the exact wall-uniform second-adjoint Taylor factor and two independent
orbit directions prove an arbitrary-code weighted converse at one small
supplied temperature. FIBER_PRESERVING_FILTERS.txt gives an explicit good
fiber-preserving class and a uniform ALL-wall Q=1 theorem. Neither packet
decides the all-Q optimal wall law. This original obstruction is preserved.
