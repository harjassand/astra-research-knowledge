# Hostile audit: Nature mScarlet–flavin β sign

## Finding

The conditional sign discrepancy survives an independent source and equation audit. Nature S8's fit raises `β` with RF amplitude and describes `β` as the radical-pair separation fraction. Xiang et al.'s 2025 Table 1/model and public MATLAB source use the same named fraction to route triplet products into a nonfluorescent radical anion; the complementary branch returns fluorescent ground-state mScarlet. Under that literal transfer, `β_RF > β_off` predicts less fluorescence, while the 2026 RF-on traces show more.

This is a conditional inconsistency in the **published model interface**, not evidence that the observed RF-associated fluorescence increase is false. Nature does not publish its fitted equations, state-to-camera output map, or exact 520-nm excitation-rate conversion. A private complement convention or a sign-label error can rescue the model. The sign-corrected source-equivalent simulation below is a concrete counterexample to any inference against the experiment.

## Load-bearing reconstruction

I independently downloaded the official Nature supplement and the Xiang authors' public code. The downloaded supplement is byte-identical to the earlier saved copy; the authors' GitHub repository is at commit `74c48845428f8d0340a88968080cb4fecec4d94e`. Nature S8 reports `β=.68+.117 B1` (B1 in mT), initial concentrations `(mScarlet, FMN, FMNH2)=(50,20,330) μM`, and a joint fit to the positive RF-on data. The plotted high-intensity signal at `B1=.3 mT` is about +4.8%.

In the source ODE, with ground-state mScarlet `G`, triplet `T`, radical anion `R`, FMN `F`, FMNH2 `H`, and FMNH radical `Q`, the two β-sensitive branches are

```text
Gdot includes +(1−β) kred H T
Rdot includes +β kred H T
```

All other rates, including total triplet quenching `kred H T`, are β-independent. Thus an upward β step has the immediate sign `ΔGdot = −Δβ kred H T` for any pre-step state with `H,T>0`. At the exact S8 initial state `T=0`, the first derivative difference is zero, but the second derivative is negative once green excitation creates triplets. An exact stationary reduction using the model's three conserved quantities also gives `dG/dβ<0` over the fitted interval.

With JACS Table 1 rates correctly converted to μM units and the S8 concentrations, the stationary change for `β=.68→.7151` is `−2.87%`, `−3.94%`, and `−4.40%` at modelled green intensities 0.5, 1.7, and 3.8 W cm^-2. Full transient integration of the 0–5 s RF-on interval gives `−4.41%` at 5 s from the S8 initial state and `−4.40%` from the illuminated steady state. Across tested green prehistories from 0 to 10 s, the first RF-on pulse remains negative.

The output-map rescue is also tightly bounded. For `Fdet=G+ρR+B` with fixed nonnegative emissivities and constant background, a positive high-intensity signal requires the radical to be at least as bright as mScarlet, contrary to Xiang's explicit nonfluorescent-radical model. Even in the radical-only limit the 5-s fractional increase is only about 0.35%, far below the plotted ≈4.8%; a positive background cannot increase that limit.

Changing only the sign of the separation-fraction increment, `β=.68→.6449`, instead gives `+4.85%` at 5 s from the S8 initial state. With a 2-s green prehistory, the simulated signal rises from +0.87% at 0.1 s to +4.82% at 5 s. That is not a refit of all panels, but it shows a single sign correction can produce the observed direction and scale under the same network. Interpreting Nature's fitted parameter as the recombination fraction is another directional rescue, although it changes the parameter semantics and the example amplitude overshoots.

## Limits and handoff

The exact quantitative transient is conditional: Nature transfers mScarlet3 rates to mScarlet-I, uses 520-nm green light while the JACS intensity reference is 561 nm, and describes its fit as semi-quantitative. S8 does not state the conversion from `I520` to `Iσ` or the complete prehistory. I therefore use the transient magnitudes as source-adjacent checks; the immediate branch sign and exact steady sign are load-bearing and robust to the modest repository-default/Table 1 differences.

The published statements that Nature's fitted β increases as a *separation fraction* and that the resulting traces match positive fluorescence cannot both follow the cited Xiang equations with `F∝G`. Without Nature's private implementation and output map, this establishes a mismatch in the public specification, not necessarily in the code the authors ran. No new biological mechanism, capability, experimental claim, or priority is asserted.
