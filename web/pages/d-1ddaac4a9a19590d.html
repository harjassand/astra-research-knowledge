## Interface and reduction

Let G be the 48 signed coordinate permutations of R³. Take S rational exact-unit representatives z_i whose G-orbits are free and pairwise disjoint, and use all N=48S points with equal weights. The cyclic harmonic grid U_k, either of its declared Taylor truncations, and its moment target are G-invariant. The method also applies to other G-invariant targets; a nonsymmetric target is outside this reduced interface.

The Reynolds map R_G f=|G|^-1 sum_g f(gx) commutes with the Laplacian and preserves sphere integration. For every feature f,

    N^-1 sum_(i,g) f(g z_i)=S^-1 sum_i (R_G f)(z_i).

Thus all noninvariant harmonic components of the empirical moment vector vanish exactly. For an orthogonal rational basis q_a of the invariant nonconstant harmonics, with nu_a=integral q_a², the remaining moments and tangent operator are

    mu_a=S^-1 sum_i q_a(z_i),
    L_(a,ia)=S^-1 [(I-z_i z_i^t) grad q_a(z_i)]_a.

The invariant homogeneous polynomial ring has generators of degrees2,4,6 (symmetric polynomials in x²,y²,z²). Harmonic decomposition removes its degree2 radial generator. Its harmonic Hilbert series is therefore 1/[(1-t^4)(1-t^6)]. The number of nonconstant invariant rows through degree R is the number of nonnegative pairs (a,b) with4a+6b<=R, minus one, asymptotically R²/48. Through16 it is9, at degrees4,6,8,10,12,12,14,16,16. This is a fixed-group factor reduction, not a new asymptotic exponent.

For any feasible full tangent correction cancelling a G-invariant moment error through R, average it under G. The equations remain feasible and its squared Euclidean norm cannot increase. The unique minimum-Euclidean correction is consequently equivariant: v_(i,g)=g v_i. Conversely an equivariant correction satisfying L v=-mu satisfies every original harmonic equation, because Reynolds annihilates the noninvariant rows. Thus the exact seed solve

    lambda=(L L^t)^-1(-mu),  v=L^t lambda

when the actual sampled L has full row rank, equals the full minimum-Euclidean correction through R. Its full objective is48 sum_i|v_i|². For the all-point invariant derivative J, J J^t=(L L^t)/48 and its multiplier is48lambda. These are statements about the actual sampled operator. A continuum Gram cannot replace L L^t. Singular sampled L causes a rejected/UNKNOWN proposal unless independently repaired; symmetry alone supplies no rank or acquisition guarantee.

Sphere retraction is equivariant, so applying rational Cayley maps to representatives and expanding the orbits preserves every exact group identity. The finite run uses rational hemisphere chart resets; it checks free/disjoint orbits after every reset. It does not use a general collision-repair theorem or assume any minimum separation for arbitrary seeds.
