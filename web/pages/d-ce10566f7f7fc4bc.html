# Independent audit: unrestricted EB to canonical POVM domination

## Verdict

**Pass, with a domain condition.** The proposed reduction is correct when the quadratic-form inequality is required for every Hermitian input (equivalently, the self-adjoint part of the difference is positive on the full real Hilbert space). Testing the slack at $I$ forces every feasible trace-preserving EB comparator to be unital. Its Holevo decomposition then supplies two POVMs, and their canonical maps have an exact positive Gram slack over the symmetrized comparator. This preserves the exact domination constant and yields a canonical comparator with at most twice as many outcomes as the selected Holevo representation.

For $K=2\Phi-I$, this makes the canonical all-positive-dual support gate necessary and sufficient for the existence of any full-domain TP EB comparator. It does **not** establish the same reduction if feasibility is imposed only on traceless inputs: that condition does not test the slack at $I$, so it does not force unitality. No failure of existential canonical domination on that restricted domain is asserted here; the stated proof does not cover it.

This is an algebraic bridge, not a proof or disproof of the universal factor-two claim, and it gives no efficient compiler or priority clearance.

## Proof audit

Work on the real space of Hermitian $d\times d$ matrices with
\[
\langle A,B\rangle_\tau=\tau(AB),\qquad \tau=\operatorname{Tr}/d.
\]
Let $K=K^*$ and $K(I)=I$. Let $\Lambda$ be a TP EB map and suppose
\[
\langle A,\Lambda(A)\rangle_\tau\geq\langle A,K(A)\rangle_\tau
\quad\text{for every Hermitian }A.\tag{1}
\]
The quadratic form sees only $T=(\Lambda+\Lambda^*)/2$, hence $P=T-K\succeq0$. TP gives $\Lambda^*(I)=I$, while TP also gives $\tau(\Lambda(I))=1$. Thus
\[
\langle I,PI\rangle_\tau=\tau(T(I)-I)=0.
\]
For a positive operator $P$, zero quadratic form at $I$ implies $P^{1/2}I=0$, hence $PI=0$. Therefore $T(I)=I$, and $T(I)=(\Lambda(I)+I)/2$ forces $\Lambda(I)=I$. This identity slack is the load-bearing step; it uses the full-domain inequality and $K(I)=I$.

Write a finite Holevo form, omitting zero effects,
\[
\Lambda(X)=\sum_y\operatorname{Tr}(M_yX)\sigma_y,\quad
M_y\succeq0,\quad\sum_yM_y=I,\quad \sigma_y\succeq0,\quad\operatorname{Tr}\sigma_y=1.
\]
Set $p_y=\operatorname{Tr}(M_y)/d>0$, $\rho_y=M_y/\operatorname{Tr}(M_y)$, and $N_y=\operatorname{Tr}(M_y)\sigma_y=d p_y\sigma_y$. The input effects $M_y$ sum to $I$; unitality gives
\[
\sum_yN_y=\sum_y\operatorname{Tr}(M_y)\sigma_y=\Lambda(I)=I,
\]
so $N_y$ is a second POVM. The omitted-zero-effect convention is necessary before dividing by $\operatorname{Tr}M_y$; for PSD $M_y$, trace zero means $M_y=0$, and its channel contribution and $N_y$ are both zero.

In the normalized-τ Hilbert space put $u_y=d\sqrt{p_y}\rho_y$ and $v_y=d\sqrt{p_y}\sigma_y$. Since
\[
\langle u_y,X\rangle_\tau=\sqrt{p_y}\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_yX),
\]
we have, with no missing factor of $d$,
\[
\Lambda=\sum_y|v_y\rangle\langle u_y|,\quad
\Lambda^*=\sum_y|u_y\rangle\langle v_y|,\quad
\Gamma_M=\sum_y|u_y\rangle\langle u_y|,\quad
\Gamma_N=\sum_y|v_y\rangle\langle v_y|.
\]
Here $\Gamma_M(X)=\sum_y\operatorname{Tr}(M_yX)\rho_y$, and similarly $\Gamma_N$ prepares $\sigma_y$. Expanding the outer products verifies
\[
\frac{\Gamma_M+\Gamma_N}{2}-\frac{\Lambda+\Lambda^*}{2}
=\frac12\sum_y|u_y-v_y\rangle\langle u_y-v_y|\succeq0.\tag{2}
\]
The left average is itself one canonical channel: use the union POVM with effects $M_y/2$ and $N_y/2$, and retain which half produced the outcome. Thus $\Gamma\succeq T\succeq K$. The exact comparison constant is preserved.

The adjoint is CPTP after the unitality step: it is CP as the trace adjoint of a CP map, TP because $\operatorname{Tr}\Lambda^*(X)=\operatorname{Tr}(X\Lambda(I))=\operatorname{Tr}X$, and unital because $\Lambda$ is TP. Its EB property can be seen without assuming a general closure theorem: the same decomposition gives
\[
\Lambda^*(X)=\sum_y\operatorname{Tr}(N_yX)\rho_y,
\]
with $N_y$ a POVM and $\rho_y$ states. This also checks the sometimes hidden normalization in the informal claim that the adjoint is another channel in Holevo form.

## Exact all-target statement and scope

For $K=2\Phi-I$, with $\Phi$ unital, TP, and Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint, $K=K^*$ and $K(I)=I$. Let $\mathcal C_d$ be the set of canonical POVM maps restricted to traceless Hermitians and define
\[
S_d(Q)=\sup_{\Gamma\in\mathcal C_d}\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Gamma),\qquad Q\succeq0.
\]
The full canonical-map set is compact and convex. For any POVM, write $p_y=\operatorname{Tr}(M_y)/d$ and $\rho_y=M_y/\operatorname{Tr}(M_y)$. Then $\sum_y p_y\rho_y=I/d$, and the channel is
\[
\Gamma_\mu(X)=d\int\operatorname{Tr}(\rho X)\rho\,d\mu(\rho),
\]
for the finite-support probability measure $\mu=\sum_y p_y\delta_{\rho_y}$ on the compact density-matrix space. Conversely, every finite-support measure with barycenter $I/d$ gives a POVM by $M_y=d p_y\rho_y$. Allow all probability measures with that barycenter; this is a compact convex set, and the second-moment map above is continuous with finite-dimensional range. Carathéodory, applied to the combined barycenter and second-moment coordinates, gives a finite-support representative for every image point. Mixture of measures implements a retained classical coin. Conic separation then gives
\[
\exists\Gamma\in\mathcal C_d:\Gamma\succeq K|_{\mathcal H_0}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
S_d(Q)\geq\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(QK)
\quad\forall Q\succeq0.\tag{3}
\]
In finite dimensions, the separating functional must be positive because its infimum over $K+\{R:R\succeq0\}$ is finite only for $Q\succeq0$. Since both $K$ and a canonical map fix $I$ and are self-adjoint, domination on $\mathcal H_0$ extends to the full Hermitian space. Combining (1)--(3), the same support gate is necessary and sufficient for **any TP EB comparator satisfying full-domain quadratic-form domination**.

There is also a valid rank-one reduction, but it must not be confused with equality of channel sets. Spectrally refining an effect $M_y=\sum_jM_{yj}$ into rank-one effects gives, for every Hermitian $A$,
\[
\sum_j\frac{(\operatorname{Tr}M_{yj}A)^2}{\operatorname{Tr}M_{yj}}
\geq\frac{(\operatorname{Tr}M_yA)^2}{\operatorname{Tr}M_y}
\]
by weighted Cauchy--Schwarz. Therefore the refined canonical map PSD-dominates the original one. The support supremum over $Q\succeq0$ is attained among rank-one POVMs, and a canonical dominator exists iff a rank-one canonical dominator exists. But arbitrary canonical maps are not themselves rank-one POVM moments: the one-outcome POVM $\{I\}$ gives the depolarizing canonical map, which is not a rank-one POVM map. The c5 moment note's compactness justification should be read with this repair: use the compact density-matrix measure space for the full canonical set, or restrict to rank-one maps only after invoking refinement domination. The support/extraction gate remains valid.

If the proposed problem instead asks only for $\langle A,\Lambda A\rangle\geq\langle A,KA\rangle$ for traceless $A$, the proof of forced unitality is unavailable. For example, with $d=2$, take $\Phi(X)=\operatorname{Tr}(X)I/2$, so $K=2\Phi-I$ equals $-I$ on traceless matrices; the pure-state replacer $\Lambda(X)=\operatorname{Tr}(X)|0\rangle\langle0|$ is TP and EB, nonunital, and satisfies the restricted inequality. This example does not refute existence of a canonical comparator (the completely depolarizing canonical channel works); it shows only that identity-slack unitalization cannot be invoked from an H0-only premise.

## Representation and outcome accounting

Given an $r$-term Holevo form, (2) produces at most $2r$ POVM outcomes. In finite dimension a separable normalized Choi state lies in the convex hull of pure product states in a real affine space of dimension $d^4-1$; Carathéodory therefore gives a representation with at most $d^4$ product terms and hence a canonical majorant with at most $2d^4$ outcomes. This is only an existence/outcome-count statement. It does not imply the decomposition is efficiently found, that the comparator can be compiled from black-box channel access, or that the original EB comparator's outcome count is minimal. A direct implementation of the output comparator measures the union POVM and prepares the normalized effect state; no channel-use advantage or physical acquisition protocol follows from the algebra.

## Primary-prior boundary

Horodecki--Shor--Ruskai establish the standard finite-dimensional Holevo measure-and-prepare form, its TP iff POVM normalization, and equivalence with separable Choi states. Those facts supply the representation and finite outcome reduction. The sources checked do not state the present full-domain order reduction or the exact PSD identity (2).
