SU(3) WALL FIBERS: EXACT CPV JACOBI MODEL, INTERPOLATING SPECTRUM
DIAGNOSTIC, AND FAILURE OF THE NAIVE INDEX-DIAGONAL FORM
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: exact full-multiplicity block reduction and bounded spectral
falsification tests. The uniform sorted-spectrum estimate (★) is neither
proved nor refuted here.

1. CLAIM AND QUANTIFIERS UNDER TEST

Let a,b be integers with a>=b>=1, N=a+b, and define on End(V_(a,b))

  T_A=d pi(t_A),
  S_A=(D_A-alpha T_A)/N,
  L_S=sum_A ad(S_A)^2,
  K^S_(p,q)=L_S|_(M_(p,q)),

using Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2 and

  C2(lambda)=(a^2+ab+b^2+3a+3b)/3,
  C3(lambda)=(a-b)(2a+b+3)(a+2b+3)/18,
  alpha=C3(lambda)/C2(lambda).

The proposed sorted-spectrum statement is the existence of an absolute
c>0 such that, for every a>=b>=1 and every root-lattice output (p,q)
with p,q>=0, p+q<=a, and nonzero multiplicity m_(p,q), the eigenvalues
of K^S_(p,q), ordered increasingly as kappa_1<=...<=kappa_m, satisfy

  kappa_l >= c [ j_l + eta j_l^2 ],
  eta=(a-b)/N,
  j_l=delta+l-1,
  delta=|p-q|/3,
  1<=l<=m_(p,q).                                           (★)

The scalar slot j_1=0 is allowed when p=q. This is an eigenvalue
inequality; it does not assert that any particular CPV or GT basis
vector is the eigenvector paired with j_l.

For exact formulas, assume p<=q (the opposite order is obtained by
exchanging the two CPV cases). Root-lattice support is p congruent q
mod 3, so delta is an integer. Put

  h=(2p+q)/3=p+delta,
  m=min(h,b)-delta+1,
  J_max=min(h,b).

For p+q<=a these are precisely the nonempty truncated blocks, with
CPV multiplicity dimension m and the GT zero-weight index set
J=delta,delta+1,...,J_max. The rank-one scalar output has m=1,j_1=0.
No claim here covers p+q>a.

2. EXACT CPV PARAMETER REDUCTION ON THE FULL TRUNCATED RANGE

Use the exact quartic identity from
`outputs/research/sol_semiclassical_memory/wall_su3/MISSING_LABEL_MAPPING.txt`
§4. Set c_(p,q)=C2(p,q)=(p^2+pq+q^2+3p+3q)/3 and

  sigma_(p,q)=-c_(p,q)^2/12
    +(C2(lambda)/3+1/4+alpha^2)c_(p,q)
    -(4/3)alpha C3(lambda).

The CPV six parameters simplify throughout p+q<=a to

  (xi_1,xi_2,xi_3)=(N+1-h-delta, b+1, b+1-delta),
  (xi_4,xi_5,xi_6)=(b-h-delta, b-h, 0),
  xi_b=max(b-h,0),
  xi_a=b+1-delta,
  m=xi_a-xi_b.

The CPV basis indices are xi_b,...,xi_a-1. Reversing their order
by J=b-index gives exactly J=J_max,...,delta. This is a cardinality
and index correspondence only. It does not identify the CPV basis
vectors with orthonormal GT spin vectors.

The remaining source parameters are

  Lambda=N+h+delta+3,
  lambda_+=2(a+2b+3)/3,
  lambda_-=(-a+b-3(h+delta)-3)/3.

Here the CPV matrix index t=1,...,m corresponds to
J=J_max-t+1. Define

  x(J)=(a+1/2-h-delta+J,
        J+1/2,
        J+1/2-delta,
        J-h-1/2,
        J-h-delta-1/2,
        J-b-1/2),
  E_k(J)=sum_(i=1)^6 x_i(J)^k,  k=1,2,3,4.

The exact CPV diagonal entries are

  X_J=-[(7/2)E_1^3-18E_1E_2+18E_3]/108
       -E_1(Lambda^2+2)/24,

  Y_J=[(5/2)E_1^4+32E_1E_3
       +6(E_2^2-3E_1^2E_2-4E_4)
       +6E_2(Lambda^2+2)-3E_1^2(Lambda^2-2)
       -(3/2)Lambda^4+6Lambda^2-36]/288.

Thus the full bracket H=N^2 K^S has

  H_(J,J)=sigma_(p,q)-Y_J-2alpha X_J.

For each edge J<->J-1, J=delta+1,...,J_max, the two directed
off-diagonal entries are exactly

  H_(J,J-1)=(a-h-delta+J)J(J-delta)
       [(a+2b)/3+h+delta+2-J+2alpha],

  H_(J-1,J)=(h-J+1)(h+delta-J+1)(b-J+1)
       [(2a+b+3)/3+J-2alpha].

All other entries vanish. These formulas retain the quartic Y term;
dropping it would change the fiber spectrum. They are obtained by
substitution into CPV's exact X,Y tridiagonal formulas, not by assuming
GT-spin diagonalization.

Both off-diagonal entries are positive on this truncated range. The
first bracket is positive since J<=h and alpha>=0. For the second,
the identity

  (2a+b+3)/3-2alpha
      =(2a+b+3)b(b+2)/(a^2+ab+b^2+3N)>0

shows positivity even before adding J. The multiplicity matrix is
therefore diagonally similar to a real symmetric Jacobi matrix with
edge magnitudes

  g_J=N^(-2) sqrt(H_(J,J-1) H_(J-1,J))>0.

This symmetric Jacobi matrix has exactly the spectrum of K^S. Its
alternating-sign conjugate has negative off-diagonals and can be
written exactly as

  L_g + diag(v_J),
  L_g=sum_edges g_J (e_J-e_(J-1))(e_J-e_(J-1))^*,
  v_J=d_J-g_J-g_(J+1),  d_J=H_(J,J)/N^2,

with missing boundary conductances set to zero. This is an exact
operator-form representation in an isospectral Jacobi model. The
CPV index used to write it is not an orthonormal GT-spin coordinate.

3. A CONCRETE FAILURE OF THE NAIVE DIAGONAL LOEWNER COMPARISON

Take the regular truncated family

  (a,b)=(n,n), (p,q)=(2,2), n>=4.

Then eta=delta=0, m=3 and J labels are 2,1,0 in CPV row order. After
diagonal symmetrization, the exact K^S matrix is

  [ d_o  g_n   0  ]
  [ g_n  d_c  g_n ]
  [ 0    g_n  d_o ],

  d_o=(3n^2+6n+1)/(2n^2),
  d_c=(2n^2+4n-3)/(2n^2),
  g_n=sqrt((n-1)(n+2)(n+3))/(2 n^(3/2)).

Consequently the central CPV-coordinate Rayleigh quotient after the
alternating-sign conjugation is

  v_1=d_c-2g_n
      =1+2/n-3/(2n^2)
        -sqrt((1-1/n)(1+2/n)(1+3/n))
      =9/(8n^4)-9/(4n^5)+O(n^-6).

Since the CPV row is labeled J=1 by the index map, this rules out any
uniform direct Loewner estimate in that coordinate of the form

  K^S_(2,2) >= c diag(0,1,2), c>0,

or any diagonal-Gershgorin proof that treats the CPV row label as an
orthonormal GT-spin mode. It does not refute (★): the actual sorted
eigenvalues of the displayed matrix tend to (1/2,3/2,2), consistent
with j=(0,1,2). The failure is specifically of a basis-diagonal
operator comparison; the off-diagonal path mixing is essential.

A second obstruction to the simple decomposition L_g+diag(v) as a
nonnegative potential plus path Laplacian is that some v_J are
negative. For example (a,b,p,q)=(3,1,1,1) gives v_0 approximately
-0.1098177 in the same symmetrized model. Thus proving (★) by separately
lower-bounding a nonnegative diagonal potential and the path Laplacian
also fails without a sharper joint estimate.

4. BOUNDED FULL-BLOCK FALSIFICATION SEARCH

The reproducible wrapper is
`outputs/research/spectral_geometry/wall_lower/operator_interpolating_spectrum_check.py`.
It instantiates the exact CPV block, symmetrizes by diagonal
similarity, and tests (★) numerically for every truncated block with
1<=b<=20, b<=a<=60, p<=q, p+q<=a, root-lattice support, and m>0.
It tests every nonzero j_l; the j=0 slot is skipped exactly as allowed
by the statement. It recounts rather than hard-coding the number of
blocks. Result:

  truncated blocks:                       112,251
  nonzero-spin eigenvalue comparisons:    652,918
  largest multiplicity:                   21
  minimum computed kappa_l/(j_l+eta*j_l^2): 0.777557969307
  minimizer: (a,b,p,q,j,kappa)
            (25,20,1,1,1,0.8639532992301167)

The same checker uses symbolic arithmetic for the exact n-family
3-by-3 characteristic polynomial and row-potential expansion above.
The bounded scan finds no failure of sorted (★). Its eigensolver uses
floating coefficients; only the displayed three-by-three family is
checked symbolically. Therefore neither this scan nor the special
family supplies a uniform lower bound.

5. STATUS AND THE REMAINING UNIFORM GATE

The exact quartic block now gives a precise, all-parameters truncated
Jacobi problem for (★), including the j=0 mode and the boundary
truncation at J_max=min(h,b). It neither diagonalizes K^S in GT spin
nor proves sorted eigenvalue growth. No concrete violation of sorted
(★) was found in the bounded or selected large-carrier diagnostics.

The sorted statement remains open uniformly over all integers
a>=b>=1, all root-lattice (p,q) with p+q<=a and m>0, and every
1<=l<=m. A proof must control the spectrum of L_g+diag(v_J) jointly;
the row potential alone can be tiny or negative, while the path term
can lift those CPV-coordinate modes. Establishing such a min-max,
Hardy/capacity, or comparison estimate with a constant independent of
a,b,p,q is the next decisive step. Even a proof on this truncated
range would not automatically control outputs p+q>a in the full
low-Casimir window when b is small.
