XXZ THERMAL TRANSFER: INDEPENDENT AUDIT
7 October 2026. Root-assigned audit; coordinates with tensor_contraction_audit.

DECISION AND STRONGEST RESULT

The proposed transfer survives the orientation, norm-error, nonhomogeneous-field,
and rational-bit audits. More strongly, the one-qubit field gates admit a direct
homogeneous LC lift. Thus the result needs Chen--Liu's homogeneous Theorem 5.1
and its rational-bit implementation, already independently reconstructed in
work/cycle2/counting_gate_audit.txt; it does not need the generic homogeneous-layer
closure in their Theorem 1.6. This is a conditional mathematical corollary with a
complete explicit reduction, not an implemented counting algorithm, external
proof certification, or certified-priority claim.

Let G be ANY loopless graph, including nonbipartite graphs, on n qubits, and let

 H = sum_(e=uv) [alpha_e(X_u X_v+Y_u Y_v)+gamma_e Z_u Z_v]
       + sum_v [b_v X_v+c_v Z_v],
 alpha_e >= |gamma_e|, b_v >= 0, c_v arbitrary,

with rational data. The physical Hamiltonian is -H. Set
 C=3 sum_e alpha_e+sum_v(b_v+|c_v|).
Conditional on Chen--Liu, the following classical algorithms exist:
 (i) relative partition-function approximation for Tr exp(beta H) in bit time
     polynomial in input length, numerical beta C, and inverse error;
 (ii) a standard bit-FPRAS for lambda_max(H), with no numerical norm overhead;
 (iii) sampling the computational-basis measurement law of the Gibbs state in
     total variation epsilon in the same numerical-parameter polynomial time;
 (iv) reconstruction, to trace-norm epsilon, of its reduced density matrix on
     any specified k=O(log n) qubits, and additive approximation of supplied
     norm-bounded observables on those qubits.
The sampling claim is for classical measurement outcomes. No global quantum
Gibbs-state preparation circuit is obtained.

1. LOCAL GATES AND ORIENTATION

Put r_v=b_v+|c_v| and define PSD local terms
 A_e=3 alpha_e I+alpha_e(XX+YY)+gamma_e ZZ,
 A_v=r_v I+b_v X+c_v Z.
The eigenvalues of A_e are 3alpha+gamma (twice), 5alpha-gamma,
and alpha-gamma. Hence A_e>=0 and ||A_e||<=6alpha.
Also A_v>=0 and ||A_v||<=2r. Therefore sum_i||A_i||<=D:=2C and
 sum_i A_i=H+CI.
If C=0, H=0 and all conclusions are trivial.

For any rational s>=0 use F_i=I+sA_i. The two-qubit table, in row/column
order 00,01,10,11, is

 [ a 0 0 0 ]
 [ 0 b c 0 ],  a=1+s(3alpha+gamma),
 [ 0 c b 0 ]   b=1+s(3alpha-gamma), c=2s alpha.
 [ 0 0 0 a ]

At every gate keep the row bits and complement the column bits. With row
variables x1,x2 and complemented-column variables x3,x4, the generating
polynomial is
 q2=a(x1x2+x3x4)+b(x1x4+x2x3)+c(x1x3+x2x4).
Its Hessian eigenvalues are a+b+c, a-b-c, -a+b-c, -a-b+c. The latter three
are 2s(gamma-alpha), -2s(gamma+alpha), -2-4salpha, all nonpositive.
Thus q2 is LC, including alpha=0 and gamma=+-alpha. Equivalently, the
triangle condition is |a-b|<=c<=a+b, which holds for every s>=0.

The one-qubit table is [[v,d],[d,u]], with
 d=sb, u=1+s(r-c), v=1+s(r+c).
The same bit convention yields
 q1=d(1+xy)+ux+vy.
Here uv-d^2=1+2sr+s^2(r^2-b^2-c^2)>=1. In general, the log-Hessian of
 a+ux+vy+dxy has diagonals -(u+dy)^2/q^2, -(v+dx)^2/q^2 and off-diagonal
 (ad-uv)/q^2. It is negative semidefinite iff ad<=2uv. Our gate satisfies
the stronger d^2<=uv. Its reciprocal also satisfies this condition; q2 is
self-complement symmetric. Nevertheless, reciprocal-LC is not assumed.

For a trace circuit, each physical wire joins a column end to the next row
end. Original equality of their bits becomes exactly one selected end under
the convention above. Give every halfedge a distinct variable. The partner
polynomial for the wires is product_(wire h,h') (xh+xh'), the basis polynomial
of a partition matroid. A full-squarefree coefficient enforces exactly once
on every wire. No spatial-graph bipartition or planar embedding is needed.
Keeping both endpoint bits unchanged would impose equality rather than
exactly once and would be the wrong reduction.

2. HOMOGENEOUS FIELD LIFT (NEW INTERNAL CONSTRUCTION)

For each field gate introduce two distinct dummy variables z1,z2 and lift q1 to

 q1_tilde = d z1z2+(u/2)x(z1+z2)+(v/2)y(z1+z2)+dxy.

This has degree two and is multiaffine. Its specialization z1=z2=1 is q1.
To prove LC, positively rescale x,y to balance u,v. The three complementary
pair weights then become d, sqrt(uv)/2, sqrt(uv)/2, and their triangle condition
is exactly d^2<=uv. The case d=0 is the product
 (ux+vy)(z1+z2)/2 and is immediate. The square root is a proof device; the
algorithm uses only the displayed rational coefficients.

Consider any circuit with e two-qubit gates and f field gates. It has
 W=2e+f physical wires and 2f dummy variables. Define

 P=product_(two-qubit gates)q2 * product_(field gates)q1_tilde,
 Q=product_(physical wires h,h')(xh+xh') * e_f(all 2f dummy variables).

Both are homogeneous LC on N=4(e+f) variables, each of degree N/2. Q is
a product of partition-matroid and uniform-matroid basis polynomials, with
all positive coefficients equal to one. Then
 [x^U](P Q)=Tr(circuit).

Indeed, exactly once forces P to choose W physical coordinates. Since P has
degree 2e+2f, it chooses exactly f dummy coordinates, so Q's complementary
dummy subset has size f. Summing all local dummy choices gives q1 for each
field gate (the two u/2 choices sum to u and the two v/2 choices to v).
An unused physical qubit contributes a separate known factor 2.

Coefficient queries for P are products of at most e+f local entries: there is
no polynomial expansion or decomposition to acquire. Positive starting sets
are obtained locally from positive diagonal choices, and Q starts by picking
one incidence per wire and any f dummy coordinates. Hence Theorem 5.1 applies
directly. The construction also avoids reliance on general reciprocal-LC.

3. SYMMETRIC EULER APPROXIMATION, WITHOUT EXPONENTIAL TRACE LOSS

Let tau=beta D and
 m=ceil max{1,4tau,20tau^2/epsilon}, delta=tau/m<=1/4,
 s=beta/(2m), W=product_i(I+sA_i), B=WW*.
The second half of B is the reversed gate order. Put Q0=(H+CI)/D.
Every F_i>=I, so the least singular value of W is >=1 and B>=I.
The total sum of the norms of the increments in B is <=delta, and their
linear sum is delta Q0. Expanding this finite ordered product yields

 ||B-I-delta Q0|| <= exp(delta)-1-delta <= delta^2,
 ||B-I|| <= exp(delta)-1 <=2delta.

For E=B-I>=0, scalar functional calculus gives
 ||log(I+E)-E|| <=||E||^2/2 <=2delta^2.
Thus ||log B-delta Q0||<=3delta^2<=5delta^2, and
 ||m log B-beta(H+CI)||<=5tau^2/m<=epsilon/4.

The two generators are Hermitian. The min--max principle bounds each ordered
eigenvalue difference by epsilon/4. Exponentiating those SCALAR eigenvalue
bounds and summing gives

 exp(-epsilon/4) Tr exp(beta(H+CI))
   <=Tr(B^m)<=exp(epsilon/4) Tr exp(beta(H+CI)).

This argument does not assert operator monotonicity of the exponential and
does not first obtain an additive trace error divided by Z. Count Tr(B^m)
at relative epsilon/8, approximate exp(-beta C) at relative epsilon/8, and
multiply. The upper ratio is <=exp(epsilon/2)<=1+epsilon for 0<epsilon<=1;
the lower ratio is >=1-epsilon/2. The scalar exponential is not silently
treated as an exact real-arithmetic output.

4. BIT COST AND EXACT INTERFACE

If p is the number of retained local terms, B^m has 2mp gates; after the lift
N=8mp variables. Each local coefficient has O(L+log m) bits, where L includes
the rational beta and coupling descriptions. P's value oracle returns a
product of O(mp) such rationals, of polynomial bit length. A supplied crude
range bound has log R_P=O(mp(L+log m)); R_Q=1. No minimum positive coefficient
is paid inversely. With B0=N+log R_P, the homogeneous imported arithmetic bound
is O(N^10 B0^2 eta^-2 log^2(2B0/eta)), on every execution; rational-oracle and
bounded-coin details give polynomial bit time. Initial support acquisition is
local and explicit. Full matrices of dimension 2^n are proof objects, not
algorithmic inputs or storage.

The runtime is polynomial in NUMERICAL beta C, not uniformly in binary log beta.
It is a conventional bit-FPRAS for partition functions only when beta C is
polynomially bounded in input length. Otherwise it is a parameter-polynomial
scheme. This is the same type of low-temperature limitation carried by the
named Bravyi--Gosset and Chen--Liu C.17 statements. The rescaling factor can
need O(beta C) output-precision bits, already within that charged regime.

5. EIGENVALUE CONSEQUENCE WITH A FULL BIT-FPRAS

Let A=sum alpha_e, B1=sum b_v, T=sum |c_v|; C=3A+B1+T. The product state
|+>^n has energy A+B1. A computational-basis product state whose Z signs match
c_v has energy >=T-A. Therefore

 lambda_max(H)>=max(A+B1,T-A)
   >=(4/5)(A+B1)+(1/5)(T-A)
   =(3A+4B1+T)/5>=C/5.

Also ||H||<=C, so C/5<=lambda_max(H)<=C when C>0. Choose rational
 beta=20(n+1)/(epsilon C).
Now beta D=40(n+1)/epsilon is automatically polynomial. Constant-relative-error
counting of Z, and evaluation of log Z to constant additive accuracy, suffice:
 lambda_max(H)<=log Z/beta<=lambda_max(H)+n log 2/beta.
The estimator's extra log error contributes only O(1/beta); together the
error is smaller than epsilon C/5<=epsilon lambda_max(H). On a failed tape
with zero count, define the output as zero. This yields a standard relative
bit-FPRAS for lambda_max(H), extending the EPR lower-bound trick in C.17.
Equivalently, it approximates the negative ground energy of physical -H.

6. GIBBS STATE ERROR AND OPEN-BOUNDARY COUNTS

For Hermitian K,L with ||K-L||<=kappa, let rho=exp(K)/Tr exp(K) and
sigma=exp(L)/Tr exp(L), and let t=||rho-sigma||_1/2. Their Jeffreys divergence is
 J=D(rho||sigma)+D(sigma||rho)=Tr[(rho-sigma)(K-L)]<=2 kappa t.
Measure the positive spectral subspace of rho-sigma. The two resulting binary
laws have total variation t. Relative-entropy data processing gives
 J>=J_binary>=4t artanh(t).
For the last inequality, write p=q+t: the binary Jeffreys divergence is
 t log[((q+t)(1-q))/(q(1-q-t))], minimized at q=(1-t)/2.
Thus t<=tanh(kappa/2). This is dimension independent and sharp for
K=(kappa/2)Z, L=-(kappa/2)Z. Standard imported facts are the Helstrom measurement
and relative-entropy data processing, verified in Watrous Theorems 3.4/5.35.
Applied to K=beta(H+CI), L=m log B, it controls the entire Gibbs state, not just Z.

For an entry on k chosen qubits, open their last-to-first circuit wires, pin
the boundary row bits to a and the original column bits to b (hence selected
column bits to 1-b), and exact-once all other physical wires. This counts
 N_(a,b)=Tr_rest <a|B^m|b>, a nonnegative rational quantity.

CRUCIAL FIELD-LIFT CORRECTION: put Delta=|a|-|b|. The partner's dummy factor
must be e_(f+Delta), not e_f. P chooses f-Delta dummy coordinates because its
selected physical count becomes W+Delta. If f+Delta lies outside [0,2f], the
entry is zero. After pinning the P degree is N/2-k-Delta and the Q degree is
N/2-k+Delta; their sum is exactly the remaining ground-set size N-2k.
They remain homogeneous LC after derivative/zero-variable pinnings. The
uniform-matroid rank is changed as displayed. Local positive support and
range oracles remain explicit. Keeping e_f for all entries would incorrectly
annihilate nonzero off-charge entries produced by X fields.

7. SAMPLING AND LOCAL OBSERVABLES, WITHOUT RARE-MARGINAL COST

For a diagonal prefix a=b, Delta=0, so the dummy rank stays f. Estimate the
two child unnormalized masses with relative eta=epsilon/(8n) and failure
epsilon/(16n) each, using independent tapes. When both estimates are good,
their normalized Bernoulli probability differs by at most eta from the exact
one, regardless of the prefix's probability. Zero child masses are returned
as zero on every execution. If both estimates are zero on a bad tape, choose
any fixed completion; its contribution is absorbed in the failure allocation.
Round each conditional Bernoulli probability to O(log(n/epsilon)) random bits,
adding another eta per step. Coupling/telescoping plus the union bound and the
Gibbs error proves total variation <=epsilon after allocating kappa<=epsilon/2.
This uses 2n coefficient estimates, with no inverse prefix probability.

For a k-qubit reduced density matrix, d=2^k, estimate all d^2=4^k entries N_(a,b)
and the normalizer at relative eta=epsilon/(100d^2), boosting total failure
below 1/4. On the good event, entrywise normalization error is <=3eta because
0<=sigma_(a,b)<=1. The raw matrix trace-norm error is at most
sum_(a,b)|error_(a,b)|<=3eta d^2. Symmetrize, clip negative eigenvalues and
normalize. If the raw trace-norm error is E, negative eigenvalue mass is <=E,
and clipping/normalization raises the error to at most 4E. Additive spectral
arithmetic can be run with a small extra epsilon allowance. With kappa<=epsilon/4,
the final reduced-state trace-norm error is <=epsilon. The cost is polynomial
in 4^k, N, input length, numerical beta C, and epsilon^-1. Hence k=O(log n)
is allowed. Any supplied observable O on those qubits has expectation error
<=||O|| times the trace-norm error, even when its entries have mixed signs.
Relative approximation of a tiny observable expectation is not claimed.

8. PRIMARY PRIOR-ART COMPARISON AND LIMITS

Chen--Liu, arXiv:2610.06724v1, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06724 and
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06724v1. Decisive dependency: homogeneous Theorem 5.1,
Remark A.4 (in Appendix A.5) for rational bits. C.16 is the triangle six-vertex
application; C.17 already supplies a symmetric-Euler reduction for EPR and
bipartite Quantum Max-Cut. Local formula-preserving source:
work/cycle1/slater_chen_liu_math.txt, lines 1126 onward, 2484 onward, 3248 onward,
3355 onward. The Euler mechanism is an extension of that source construction.
The homogeneous field lift and broader Hamiltonian/state consequences were
independently reconstructed here in coordination with the tensor/soft workers.

Bravyi--Gosset, arXiv:1612.05602, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05602, Eq.1/Theorem1:
physical interactions -b_ij XX+c_ij YY with |c_ij|<=b_ij and arbitrary Z fields.
Their class has no ZZ interaction in that displayed basis. Global axis changes
cover other two-axis versions, but an edge with alpha,gamma both nonzero has
full-rank coupling tensor diag(alpha,alpha,gamma), so local basis rotations do
not turn it into their two-axis form. Their proof uses matching gadgets plus
a model-specific near-perfect/perfect ratio bound. Our count uses no such ratio.

Harrow--Mehraban--Soleimanifar, arXiv:1910.09071,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.09071, Theorem50/Section7.2: deterministic
quasipolynomial approximation for Jzz>=|Jxy| and a uniform constant field mu.
That is the opposite easy-axis regime, touching ours only at its positive
isotropic boundary. It does not cover arbitrary mixed spatial Z/X fields here.

Cai--Liu--Lu, https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.05880, prior six-vertex FPRAS uses
squared triangle inequalities. For our infinitesimal gates and gamma>0,
 a^2-b^2=4s gamma(1+3salpha), c^2=4s^2alpha^2,
so a^2<=b^2+c^2 fails for sufficiently small s. For gamma<0 the b inequality
fails symmetrically. Thus the old squared-triangle result cannot substitute
for the imported linear-triangle improvement away from gamma=0.

Ryan Mann, arXiv:2608.19489, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19489, and author page
https://www.ryanmann.org/posts/15/fast-algorithms-for-stoquastic-spin-systems:
recent faster sampling/counting is explicitly at high temperature; its
ferromagnetic-Heisenberg statement assumes beta<=1/(e^3 Delta). It does not
remove our numerical-temperature restriction or supersede the low-temperature
parameter-polynomial result. Wider novelty searches found no exact matching
classical theorem, but this is not a comprehensive priority certification.

State-error standards: https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/TQI.3.pdf, Theorem3.4;
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/TQI.5.pdf, Theorem5.35. Natural logarithms
are used in the Jeffreys argument, translating the textbook's base-2 convention.

The local LC boundary is real: at alpha=1, s=1/10, gamma=+-11/10 the Hessian
of log q2 at all variables one has a positive eigenvalue 1/280. The present
reduction fails beyond |gamma|<=alpha; this is not a hardness theorem there.
Mixed-sign X fields are outside the stated common-basis class, as are Y fields,
generic XYZ interactions, arbitrary coherent dynamics and full quantum-state
preparation. Equalities and zero coefficients inside the stated cone are covered.
