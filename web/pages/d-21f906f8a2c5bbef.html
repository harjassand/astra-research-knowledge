# Effective stationary entropy-production certificate for known rational Q0

2026-10-06. Own derivation and exact replay; independent gate requested. This closes the effective derivative/finite-arithmetic gap left in ACQUIRED_BRACKET.md for the **known rational baseline**. Unknown ordinary-rate acquisition, and its error multiplied by log(1/b), are separate.

Assume the general theorem's fixed finite irreducible Q0, missing u→v, reverse v→u of known rate a>0, and reciprocal support for every other nonzero ordinary edge. All baseline rates are supplied exact positive rationals. Let Q_b=Q0+bB for 0<b≤1. The rare b is acquired, not supplied.

## Stationary law is an explicit linear-fractional function

The directed matrix-tree theorem gives π_i(b)=τ_i(b)/Σ_jτ_j(b), where τ_i is the sum of products of edge rates of spanning trees directed toward root i. A tree uses the single rare edge at most once. Thus

τ_i(b)=A_i+bB_i, A_i>0, B_i≥0.

These coefficients can be computed without tree enumeration: A_i is the determinant of the i-deleted principal minor of −Q0, and B_i is the corresponding cofactor at Q_1 minus A_i. Rank-one perturbation also directly proves affine dependence. Irreducibility of Q0 gives A_i>0; tree-weight positivity gives B_i≥0. Define A=ΣA_i>0,B=ΣB_i≥0. Then

π_i(b)=(A_i+bB_i)/(A+bB),

π_i′(b)=(AB_i−A_iB)/(A+bB)².

The rational constants K_i=|AB_i−A_iB|/A² therefore bound |π_i′(b)| on [0,1]. Exact cofactor calculations require polynomial rational matrix arithmetic, not stochastic source observations; these computational costs are separate from sensor readings.

## Computable global EP derivative bound

For each state define the fixed ordinary contribution

c_i=Σ_{j:ordinary pair} q0_ij log(q0_ij/q0_ji),

excluding both u→v and v→u and omitting zero edges. The stationary rate identity Σπ_iq_ij log(q_ij/q_ji) gives

σ(b)=Σ_iπ_i(b)c_i+J(b)log(a/b), J(b)=aπ_v(b)−bπ_u(b).

Let C_i be the rational upper bound obtained by replacing each |log r| with max(r,1/r)−1 and summing q0_ij times that bound. Thus |c_i|≤C_i. Put H=aK_v+1+K_u, and L_a=max(a,1/a)−1≥|log a|. Since π_i≤1,

|J′|≤H, |J|≤a+1.

Differentiate the explicit EP formula. For 0<b≤1, b|log b|≤1, so the fully rational computable constant

**C_EP=Σ_iK_i C_i+H(L_a+1)+(a+1)**

satisfies

**b|σ′(b)|≤C_EP.**

This is conservative. It may depend badly on the baseline's stationary conditioning; neither a useful practical constant nor dimension-independent stability is claimed. It is nevertheless an effective certificate, rather than an existence assertion about an analytic derivative. Every quantity is computed from supplied rates and finite rational determinants.

Consequently for x,y in an acquired bracket with lower endpoint β>0,

|σ(x)−σ(y)|≤C_EP|log(x/y)|≤C_EP|x−y|/β.

## Finite arithmetic and an EP-accuracy protocol

For target ε>0 choose a relative-rate target ε_rel=min{1,ε/(4C_EP)}. Apply the acquired-scale protocol, retaining its β,t and bracket [β,min{4β,1}]. Collect a fresh source-u block of

m=ceil[200 log(4/δ)/(ε_rel² βt)]

outcomes and let z be the endpoint-v frequency. On a correct scale bracket, r_b≤5βt. Bernoulli Bernstein with s=ε_rel βt/4 gives

Pr(|z−r_b|>s)≤2exp[−m ε_rel²βt/(160+8ε_rel/3)]≤δ/2.

This block replaces the ideal exact-probability final block in ACQUIRED_BRACKET.md. Let τ=ε_rel βt/32 and w=ε_rel β/4. Use certified probability intervals of radius at most τ at rational bisection midpoints. If z lies inside an interval, return that midpoint. Otherwise its certified sign determines the next half-interval. If the current parameter interval has width≤w, return its midpoint. This terminates after at most ceil(log2(12/ε_rel))+1 comparisons. To see the error bound, let x_* be the inverse of z clipped to the bracket. Reliable signs preserve x_* in the current interval. At a tolerance-triggered return, both r_x and z lie in an interval of width2τ, so |r_x−z|≤2τ, giving |x−b|≤2(s+2τ)/t. At a width-triggered return, |x−x_*|≤w/2 and |x_*−b|≤2s/t. Thus, on the joint success event,

|b̂−b|≤max{2(s+2τ)/t,2s/t+w/2}=5ε_relβ/8<2ε_relβ.

If a certified interval of width 2τ is represented by its center, the stated “lies inside” condition is evaluated against that interval itself, not an unverified floating value. Equality at a rational boundary is an ordinary finite comparison. All evaluations are at positive rational rates. The scale and fresh-block failure budgets together are less than δ.

The conservative bound |b̂−b|≤2ε_relβ therefore gives statistical EP error≤2C_EPε_rel≤ε/2. Evaluate σ(b̂) with certified arithmetic error at most ε/2; the final EP estimate then has additive error≤ε at the same covered confidence. This fixed-precision point protocol has no empty-set refinement loop and terminates on failure events too.

A fully finite evaluator follows directly. π_i(b̂) is rational. Each logarithm of a positive rational y is evaluated by binary range reduction y=2^k x with 1≤x<2, then log y=k log2+log x. The identity log x=2Σ_{j≥0}z^(2j+1)/(2j+1), z=(x−1)/(x+1)∈[0,1/3), has explicit tail bound

2|z|^(2m+1)/[(2m+1)(1−z²)]

after m terms; log2 uses z=1/3. Rational partial sums and this tail provide certified intervals. Allocate arithmetic tolerances according to the rational coefficients in σ(b̂); geometric convergence ensures termination. Range reduction avoids an iteration count polynomial in1/b simply to evaluate log b. Arithmetic work/bit sizes depend on n, supplied rational coefficient lengths, log(1/b), log(1/ε), and conditioning constants; sensor-count theorems do not absorb these costs.

With ε,δ fixed and known Q0, the full unknown-b EP protocol has expected endpoint readings

O_Q[(C_EP/ε)² b^(−L/(L−1)) {log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)}],

and expected physical time

O_Q[(C_EP/ε)² b^−1 {log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)}],

up to harmless replacement of (C_EP/ε)² by max{1,(C_EP/ε)²}. Arithmetic uses the certified threshold/inversion scheme and rational-log evaluator above. The displayed rate orders do not imply small finite constants. Endpoint count is near the lower-bound polynomial exponent; necessity of the loglog factor is not established.

For interval rather than point output, propagate the covered rate interval through σ using the explicit C_EP modulus and a certified center evaluation. Empty statistical intersections terminate with `INCONSISTENT/EMPTY` as in the corrected acquired-bracket note. No pathological failure-event infinite refinement is needed for this fixed final precision protocol.
