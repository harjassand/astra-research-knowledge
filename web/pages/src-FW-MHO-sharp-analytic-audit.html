## 1. Parameter positivity and pointwise identity

Write I=[-21/8,-5/2], alpha_*=3r/[8(r+2)], and

    F_r=alpha_* H+r(r-1)u^2/6+j^2+r u j.

The independent algebra check establishes

    Q_r(A,z)=Z^T C_r S_r C_r^T Z
       =d1 D2F_r(A)[B1,B1]+d2 D2F_r(A)[B2,B2]
         +omega_r(A)[B1,B2],

with omega_r closed and homogeneous quadratic. The diffusion satisfies

    d1 in [114/100,117/100], d2 in [83/100,86/100], d1+d2=2.

Every relevant denominator stays away from zero on the closed interval:
9(r+1)(2r+1)>0, and -(r+2) is in [1/2,5/8]. The six Bernstein matrices
are positive definite and their nonnegative weights sum to one. Thus S_N

strict positivity on all bilinear features.

## 2. Heat monotonicity at every affine background

Fix any r in I, any B in R^(2x2), and phi in C_c^infinity(R2;R2). Set

    phi_tau=exp(tau(d1 partial_xx+d2 partial_yy)) phi,
    q_tau=Dphi_tau, A_tau=B+q_tau,
    E(tau)=integral [F_r(B+q_tau)-F_r(B)-DF_r(B):q_tau].

This is finite relative energy, even if F_r(B)<0. A quartic expansion gives

    |E(tau)| <= C_r[|B|^2 ||q_tau||_2^2
                      +|B| ||q_tau||_3^3+||q_tau||_4^4].

All differentiated heat perturbations are Schwartz for tau>0. The polynomial
primitive eta=(1/4)i_A omega has cubic coefficients; evaluated at B+q_tau,
its coefficients stay bounded at infinity. Its pulled-back one-form contains
D2phi_tau, so its flux decays rapidly. Stokes on expanding balls therefore
gives integral omega(A_tau)[B1,B2]=0 despite the arbitrary affine B.

Differentiating relative energy and integrating by parts is justified by
these decay bounds. Constant scalar input diffusion commutes with spatial
derivatives, so the exact identity yields

    E'(tau)=-integral Q_r(A_tau,D2phi_tau)<=0.

The Gaussian convolution estimate in two dimensions is

    ||q_tau||_p <= C(d1,d2,p) tau^(-(1-1/p)) ||Dphi||_1,
    p=2,3,4.

Thus the displayed quartic growth bound tends to zero as tau tends to
infinity. Strong L2, L3, and L4 continuity at zero gives E(0)>=0.
The integrated linear term vanishes because integral Dphi=0. Hence

    integral [F_r(B+Dphi)-F_r(B)]>=0

for every affine background B. Negative pointwise values of F_r on general
matrices are consistent with this gradient-constrained inequality.

## 3. W1,4 and periodic extension

For any bounded Lipschitz Omega and phi in W0^(1,4)(Omega;R2), choose
phi_n in C_c^infinity(Omega;R2) converging strongly in W1,4 and apply the
whole-plane inequality to each zero extension. Quartic polynomial growth
gives

    |F(B+X)-F(B+Y)| <= C_F(|B|+|X|+|Y|)^3 |X-Y|.

Holder's inequality and bounded-domain L4 control show convergence of the
energy integrals. The affine Jensen inequality therefore passes to phi.
No parameter- or duration-dependent domain constant is needed for this
qualitative inequality. For periodic data, the constant heat flow decays to
the mean and Stokes on the torus cancels the exact form; the same proof
applies. No coercive quantitative gap is inferred in either case.

For alpha>alpha_*, add (alpha-alpha_*)H, which is convex as the fourth power
of the norm of a linear map. Below alpha_*, the report's explicit e11
second-variation witness is negative. Consequently the stated equivalence
QC iff ROC iff alpha>=alpha_* is valid for every r in I.

    alpha H+beta u^2+gamma j^2+delta u j

## 5. The new boundary rules out the previous H-subtraction route

For A0=[[0,(2r+1)/3],[1,0]],

    D2(F_r-epsilon H)(A0)[e11,e11]
       =-16(r+2)^2 epsilon/9<0, epsilon>0.

The earlier anisotropic subtraction epsilon(a-d)^4 is also impossible at

    average (a'-d')^4 = (3/8)[(a-d)^2+(b+c)^2]^2=(3/8)H

## 6. Additional internally derived extremality result

The author report does not claim extremality. Combining its new sharp
quasiconvexity with the independently reconstructed contact face yields a
stronger, precisely restricted result:

    For r in I, F_r spans an extreme ray of the full nine-dimensional
    left-SO(2)-invariant rank-one-convex homogeneous quartic cone.

It is then also extreme in the left-SO(2)-invariant quasiconvex cone.
This is not a claim about the unrestricted noninvariant ROC cone, and
exposedness has not been established here.

Proof. Let F_r=G1+G2, with both Gi in the invariant ROC cone. The linear
acoustic evaluation at (I,e12) is nonnegative on that cone and vanishes
at F_r, so it vanishes at both Gi. Write a generic invariant quartic in
the basis

    (u^2,v^2,uv,w^2,uw,vw,u j,v j,w j),

with coefficients p0,...,p8. Its acoustic form is nonnegative in every
background and direction. At the zero (I,e12), its first derivatives in
the four background entries and the two spatial direction entries vanish.
Their exact coefficient matrix has rank five and forces

    p1=-p7/4, p2+p3=-p7/2, p4=p5=p8=0.

Thus each Gi lies in the four-dimensional face
alpha_i H+beta_i u^2+gamma_i j^2+delta_i u j. For every ROC polynomial in
this face, the two matrices

    M1=[[6beta+delta,delta],[delta,gamma]],
    M2=[[8alpha,delta],[delta,2beta+delta]]

are positive semidefinite. Necessity is elementary: the conformal acoustic
curve A_t=[[1,-kt],[kt,1]], direction e1 tensor (t,1), has t^2 coefficient

    2[(6beta+delta)-2delta(k-1)+gamma(k-1)^2],

forcing M1>=0 for all k. The test A=[[0,y-c],[c,0]], direction e11, has
acoustic value 4alpha y^2-2delta c y+2(2beta+delta)c^2, forcing M2>=0
by a diagonal congruence. Sufficiency of this criterion is not needed for
the extremality argument.

At the sharp polynomial these are

    M1(F_r)=[[r^2,r],[r,1]],
    M2(F_r)=[[3r/(r+2),r],[r,r(r+2)/3]],

both nonzero rank-one PSD matrices. Any PSD summand of a rank-one PSD
matrix is a nonnegative scalar multiple. Hence

    M1(Gi)=lambda_i M1(F_r), M2(Gi)=mu_i M2(F_r).

The shared off-diagonal delta=r is nonzero on I, so lambda_i=mu_i.
M1 determines beta_i,gamma_i,delta_i and M2 determines alpha_i. Therefore
Gi=lambda_i F_r, which proves the claimed invariant extremality. QED.
