## Reconstruction

For nonnegative weights, the intersection defect is supermodular on the subspace lattice. Its finitely many dimension profiles ensure an attained maximum; closure of maximizers under joins gives a unique largest maximizer. Fixing its profile gives a constructible rational locus with an algebraic point. A point with that maximal dimension and profile must be the same largest maximizer. Galois invariance of its unique reduced row echelon matrix descends it to Q. The identical Frobenius argument works over finite fields. Thus complex/real extension does not hide a worse characteristic-zero subspace.

For every rational W, the lattices W intersect Z^r and S_i intersect W intersect Z^r are saturated. Their reductions inject into the ambient F_p^r and preserve their own ranks. The reduced intersection lattice lies in the intersection of the reduced lattices. Hence intersection dimensions can only increase modulo p. Nonnegative weights give P_Fp subset P_Q. For supplied unsaturated columns, rank preservation ensures equality with the saturated reduction: Smith normal form excludes p from their lattice index.
