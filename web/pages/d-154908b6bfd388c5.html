QUANTUM CAPABILITY — CYCLE 2 LITERATURE LEDGER
Checked live on 2026-10-08

Current explicit minimum-output-entropy frontier

1. Guocheng Zhen, Ranyiliu Chen, Xin Wang, “Closed-Form Arbitrary-Gap
Rényi Nonadditivity Above Any Positive Order via Direct Products of Free
Groups and High-Girth Graphs,” arXiv:2610.06533v1, submitted 2026-10-05.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06533
Primary items inspected: Theorem 2.1, Corollary 2.2, Proposition 4.8,
Proposition 5.4, Proposition 6.5, and Sections 5–7. The Corollary 2.2
specialization gives a real-algebraic channel with 256-dimensional output,
input dimension 2^37,608,898, and Bell-state von Neumann additivity gap
greater than 1/1024 nats. Source-reported; this programme has replayed the
arithmetic substitutions but not the entire proof.

2. Laura Shou and Alexey V. Gorshkov, “A constructive violation of additivity
of minimum output von Neumann entropy,” arXiv:2609.23946v2, 2026-09-24.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23946
Theorem 1.1 gives a deterministic explicit channel with output dimension
2^32, input dimension 3*2^(5268390106224805281791998), and positive gap.
Its proof architecture and the parameter inequalities were inspected in the
earlier portion of this cycle. The source-conditional parameter retuning in
RESULT.txt is now superseded in scale by arXiv:2610.06533v1.

3. Debbie Leung, Benjamin Lovitz, Peixue Wu, “Counterexamples to additivity of
minimum output p-Rényi entropy of quantum channels for p>3/4 and
0<=p<1/4,” arXiv:2607.15210, 2026-07-16.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15210
Primary source says finite-dimensional projection-induced counterexamples
cover p>3/4 and 0<=p<1/4. It is relevant surrounding frontier, but does not
replace the p=1 closed-form specialization above.

Historical explicit-violation statement

4. Marcin Aschieri, Daniel Ruba, Jan Philip Solovej, “The landscape of
minimum output entropy of SU(2)-irreducibly covariant channels,”
Communications in Mathematical Physics 405 (2024), article 298.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04133
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-024-05178-1
The 2024 discussion that no explicit von Neumann MOE additivity
counterexample was known is superseded by the 2026 preprints above. This
paper remains relevant to the separate irreducible SU(2) channel family.

5. Peter W. Shor, “Additivity of the classical capacity of entanglement-
breaking quantum channels,” Journal of Mathematical Physics 43 (2002),
4334–4340. https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0201149
Theorem 1 supplies MOE additivity when one tensor factor is entanglement
breaking; used in the SU(2) obstruction derivation in PROOF.txt.

6. S. Al Nuwairan, “SU(2)-irreducibly covariant and EPOSIC channels,”
arXiv:1306.5321 (2013). https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.5321
Its complementary-channel and Clebsch–Gordan formulas may overlap the
minimum-coupling SU(2) observation in PROOF.txt. Priority of that observation
is UNKNOWN.

7. Felix Lazebnik, Vasiliy A. Ustimenko, Andrew J. Woldar, “A new series of
dense graphs of high girth,” Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society
32(1) (1995), 73–79. https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-1995-00569-0
The current preprint cites Proposition 2.1(iii) for the girth lower bound of
its coordinate graph D(nu,Q), and the proof of Lemma 3.1 for the edge-invariant
coordinates used to take an invariant fibre. These are load-bearing external
graph-theoretic inputs; they were identified but not independently reproved
in this programme.

Frontier statement: as of the live check on 2026-10-08, arXiv:2610.06533v1 is
the strongest high-ceiling source located for an explicit closed-form
von Neumann MOE violation in this bounded search. This is not a claim that it
is globally dimension-optimal or independently externally validated.
