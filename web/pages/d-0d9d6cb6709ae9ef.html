## Odd-qubit logical factorization

For odd n>=3, put N=n-1 (even). Target basis labels are (ell,r_1,...,r_N). Map them to physical computational bits by z_n=ell+sum r_i modulo two and z_i=z_n+r_i for i<n. This is an invertible bit permutation: r_i=z_i+z_n and ell=sum_i z_i. Let R be its real permutation matrix.

For i<n let a_i=e_i and b_i=1+e_i in F_2^N; for i=n let a_n=b_n=1. Write M_Xi=X(a_i), M_Zi=Z(b_i), M_Yi=M_Xi M_Zi. Direct bit action gives

\[
R^T X_iR=X\otimes M_{Xi},\quad
R^T Z_iR=Z\otimes M_{Zi},\quad
R^T Y_iR=Y\otimes M_{Yi}.
\]

Because a_i dot b_i=0, all three residual matrices are real symmetric involutions. All are traceless. For i!=j, a_i dot b_j=1, so residual M_Xi and M_Zj anticommute; this compensates the anticommuting logical factors and preserves the exact physical tensor relations. The residual frame itself generates the full matrix algebra: individual residual X_i appear directly, and M_Zn M_Zi is individual residual Z_i.

If tau and eta are two residual faithful states with the same SLD score tuple for this exact 3n-element residual frame, then sigma=R(I_2/2 tensor tau)R^T and rho=R(I_2/2 tensor eta)R^T have the same **full physical local XYZ** score tuple. This follows by substitution into the defining Lyapunov equations: the physical score is R(axis tensor S_M(tau))R^T. The logical imaginary Y factor is retained exactly; no imaginary constraint is dropped.

Equivalently the modular filter on paired equal logical populations acts as axis tensor the residual filter. A common residual projector becomes a physical rank-two sector I_2 tensor P. ## Gray adjacency removes the Y singularity in the six-qubit Bell route

Real half-Paulis on three qubits are P(x,z), with x,z in F_2^3 and transpose parity q(x,z)=x dot z. For two labels u,v, P(u)P(v)^T and P(u)^T P(v) are both signed copies of P(u+v). They are symmetric exactly when q(u+v)=0.

Index the 64 labels by six binary bits, pairing bits into the X/Z labels I,X,Z,J on each site. Binary reflected Gray order g(j)=j xor (j>>1) has adjacent labels differing in exactly one bit. Their relative Pauli therefore has only one X or Z factor, and q=0. Both relative matrices are symmetric.

Let U_0=(V tensor W) Bell_Gray, where V,W are any real orthogonal 8-by-8 matrices. Each column remains maximally entangled, so every physical local X or Z has zero diagonal in U_0. For physical Y=iJ, the transformed half generator V^TJV or W^TJW is real skew. An adjacent Bell matrix element is a trace pairing of that skew matrix with one of the symmetric relative matrices just described, hence zero. Therefore every imaginary-Y adjacent entry vanishes for **all** such half rotations, not just at the unrotated Clifford seed.

For geometric populations p_j proportional to s^(2j), j=0,...,63, the filter is C_ij=2s^|i-j|/(1+s^(2|i-j|)). If B is the real skew part of a Y generator, flat-vector commutation means row sums of C elementwise B vanish. Since the matrix is skew, this is equivalent to all 63 prefix cuts

\[
F_k(s)=\sum_{i\le k<j} C_{ij}(s)B_{ij}=0.
\]

For s>0 divide by 2s; the resulting function extends analytically to s=0 with value B_(k,k+1). Gray adjacency supplies its zero at the base. This desingularization includes all Y conditions and avoids the invalid inference from a derivative that vanishes identically at s=0. The team combines 378 such divided-Y constraints with 756 XZ diagonal constraints, then certifies rank1134 of the resulting Jacobian.
