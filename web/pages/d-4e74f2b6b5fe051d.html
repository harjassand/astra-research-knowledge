7 October 2026. AFTER peer exposure and after the finite-table codec proof.

The exact reference mean need not be supplied. The covariance-observation

1. GENERAL-REFERENCE INTEGRAL

For any PSD A and positive B, define the matrix relative entropy
 D(A||B)=tr[A(log A-log B)-A+B].
For f_L(S)=S(I+S/L)^-1 and C_A(T)=I+f_L(TA),

 int_0^infinity KL(N(0,C_A(T))||N(0,C_B(T))) dT/T^2
       <=D(A||B)/2.                                     (R)

Proof. Write R_A=(LI+TA)^-1, R_B=(LI+TB)^-1 and Delta=A-B.
The exact inverse remainder is
 R_A-R_B+T R_B Delta R_B=T^2 R_B Delta R_A Delta R_B>=0.
It gives the operator-concave tangent inequality
 f_L(TA)<=f_L(TB)+L^2 T R_B Delta R_B.
The Gaussian KL formula then yields, with g_T(X)=log(I+f_L(TX)),
 KL<=.5[tr g_T(B)+tr g_T'(B)(A-B)-tr g_T(A)].              (R1)
The derivative is of the scalar functional calculus; trace cyclicity makes
tr(C_B^-1 L^2T R_B Delta R_B)=tr(g_T'(B)Delta). No commuting values are
assumed. The bracket is nonnegative by trace concavity.

For c>0 the log tangent gap obeys
 int [tr log(I+cTB)+cT tr((I+cTB)^-1(A-B))
                    -tr log(I+cTA)]dT/T^2=c D(A||B).
One can verify this directly by the scalar spectral primitive. On an
epsilon-to-infinity interval, the trace functional has the expansion
 int_epsilon^infinity tr log(I+TX)dT/T^2
 =(1-log epsilon)tr X-tr(X log X)+o(1).
The linear trace terms cancel against the tangent; differentiation at the
positive B is uniform locally, leaving precisely D(A||B). Zero eigenvalues
of A have the continuous convention 0log0. Finite nonnegative tangent
integrals allow subtraction for c=b=1+1/L and c=a=1/L. Since b-a=1,
integrating (R1) proves (R). B is fixed in this calculation, not a hidden
observation-dependent oracle.

There is an exact uncapped identity behind the upper:
 int_0^infinity KL(N(0,I+TA)||N(0,I+TB)) dT/T^2
       =D(A||B)/2.                                      (R0)
Here the covariance is affine in A, so the tangent step is an equality and
the c=1 log identity gives the result directly, without commutation. For
fixed A,B, capped covariances converge pointwise to the uncapped ones as
L->infinity. Fatou and (R) therefore imply convergence of the capped KL
integrals to D(A||B)/2. Thus the coefficient 1/2 in (R) cannot be improved
uniformly over all caps. This is an internally derived exact divergence
representation; its historical priority has not been established. It is
not an identity for the actual mutual information of an arbitrary mixture.

For random A within a cell and an arbitrary codebook B_j>0, (R) and Tonelli
give the same decoder as the earlier theorem, but now with the acquired risk
 Delta_code=E_mu D(A(X)||B_{J(X)})
in place of H_J. In fact Delta_code=H_J+sum_j p_j D(M_j||B_j).
Thus mean-calibration error is explicitly charged, and the guarantee becomes

 sup_v TV(k target outputs,k decoded outputs)
       <=sqrt(k C c_w Delta_code/4).                     (R2)

The independent s02 square-root-field bound also has a constructive reading:
its proof controls E D(A||S^2), not merely H(A). Therefore B=S^2 can be the
actual decoder reference in (R), conditionally in each cell, if S=E sqrt A
and the needed range/mean certificate are acquired. Its b(r/s) variance
condition is attributed to c09_s02 revision03, not to this worker.

2. TRAIN-THEN-VALIDATE ACQUISITION CONTRACT

Choose any D-cell classifier J and PSD codebook A-estimates using n reference
draws, then freeze both. Clipping the codebook eigenvalues into [alpha,beta],
for known 0<alpha<=beta, makes each B_j positive. Empty training cells may use
alpha I. The actual source X is not used in codebook training; global model
calibration precedes the ONE archived observation and its n draws count.

Here D denotes the per-observation LABEL alphabet after the global model is
frozen. The learned codebook is an additional retained record and is never
free in a total-memory budget. If all calibration-derived state is counted
in that budget, add its storage bits (and any retained validation records or
certificate) to ceil(log2 D). A public independent reference-calibration tape
may be conditioned on in the archive theorem, but obtaining and retaining it
remain explicit costs. The calibration source is mu, independently of the
unknown v; additional P_v observations would be a different interface.

Assume the declared field certificate alpha I<=A(X)<=beta I. Matrix convexity
and the divided-difference Hessian of tr(X log X) give
 0<=D(A||B)<=||A-B||_F^2/(2alpha)
             <=R0=m(beta-alpha)^2/(2alpha).
For the last inequality, - (beta-alpha)I<=A-B<=(beta-alpha)I, so its
Frobenius square is <=m(beta-alpha)^2. This is dimension-dependent cost,
not an uncharged dimension-free calibration claim.

Acquire N fresh iid reference draws, independent of all training. At each
draw evaluate d_i=D(A(X_i)||B_{J(X_i)}) with a certified upper numerical value
u_i. Conditional on the frozen training, Hoeffding yields, with probability
at least 1-delta,
 Delta_code <=mean_i u_i+R0 sqrt(log(1/delta)/(2N)).        (A)
The same one event implies (R2) for EVERY v with dP_v/dmu<=C; there is no
union bound over parameters or cells. The model-class domination and field
range are acquired/supplied certificates and are not inferred from the same
finite validation records. If the right side fails the desired threshold,
return UNKNOWN rather than assert accuracy. Adaptive reuse of validation
data to choose a new J or B invalidates this event; use new held-out draws.

Changing kernels can also be admitted: if only 0<=A<=beta I is known and
alpha I<=B<=beta I, then
 D(A||B)<=m beta[1+log(beta/alpha)].
This follows by log B>=log alpha I, log A<=log beta I on its support, and
tr B<=m beta. Use this larger R0 in (A); it exposes the calibration cost
of small mean/range and rare large fields. The tighter quadratic bound above
does require the positive source floor.

If the empirical loss and log rounding are <=theta/2, N>=
2 R0^2 log(1/delta)/theta^2 suffices for Delta_code<=theta. For epsilon-TV
use theta=4epsilon^2/(k C c_w). The sample cost therefore has explicit
epsilon^-4, k^2, C^2 and field-range dependence. No rare-tail calibration
or covariance-parameter knowledge is free. This is a valid acquired upper,
not necessarily a sharp sample complexity or optimal memory rate.

3. IMPLEMENTED SCOPE

acquired_decoder_certificate.py consumes separate rational scalar training
and validation records, a fixed two-interval classifier, alpha,beta,C,k,
the scale window and delta. It trains rational cell means, uses alpha in
an empty cell, computes exact rational log intervals for validation losses,
and takes an upward rational square-root enclosure of the confidence radius.
It returns CERTIFIED_UNDER_IID_CONTRACT only if the entropy/risk threshold
is met. All n+N records and the interval-classifier/codebook are charged.

The demo records are generated from a declared four-state rational fixture
with independent pseudorandom tapes to exercise the pipeline. They are a
software diagnostic, not evidence of a physical source or statistically
certified random-generator independence. The fixture's exact reference risk
is separately computed by rational interval arithmetic and checked against
the returned bound. The concentration theorem supplies its stated iid
contract; the observed fixed-tape run does not prove a probability theorem.

For this fixture the two learned rational means are 44/85 and 68/75. A
prefix-free Elias-gamma encoding of each nonnegative numerator plus one and
each positive denominator uses 50 bits, in addition to the 1-bit label and
fixed model/classifier metadata. If those learned means are part of the
counted retained state, this is at least 51 bits, not a 1-bit total system.
Keeping all 544 four-state calibration records adds 1088 payload bits plus
framing. They can be discarded after freezing and validating the codebook;
retaining audit receipts is another stated storage choice. Continuous scale
and Gaussian-output generation still have their separately declared costs.

This closes a finite scalar calibration/simulation gate after all range,
domination, reference-access and ideal Gaussian-output conditions are made
explicit. General dense matrix logarithms, learning the model class from
the archived observation, unknown range/tail acquisition, original-X TV
archives and digital continuous Gaussian output remain outside this result.
