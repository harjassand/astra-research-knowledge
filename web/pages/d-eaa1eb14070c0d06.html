SU3 WALL CONTROLS: EXACT FIBER-PRESERVING FILTERS AND UNIFORM RANK ONE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; independent audit requested.
No all-Q wall rank profile or optimal wall-memory equivalence is claimed.

The wall generic-filter obstruction does not apply to every filter.
This note proves a wall-uniform full-Gibbs upper for an explicit class
preserving entire U(2) branch fibers, and proves e*_1=Theta(1/N) uniformly
over all SU3 highest weights a+b=N, including the exact pure walls.
It complements the weighted arbitrary-code converse; it does not yet
match that converse at every Q when b=N^beta.

1. SUPPLIED CARRIER AND OPERATIONAL INTERFACE

Use the exact tensor notation of WEIGHTED_POSTERIOR_CONVERSE.txt and
../regular_su3/RESULT.txt. For integers a>=b>=1, N=a+b, supply V_(a,b)
and a fixed nonzero fundamental H with ||x||=r. The weak Gibbs orbit is
tau_(h,g)=exp[(h/N)T_H,g]/Z_h, with h in any fixed positive compact
interval [h0,h1]. Unlike the weighted-converse proof, this upper does
NOT require small h or three distinct fundamental eigenvalues.
The output is the same carrier and risk is full half-trace distance.
Quantum memory is maximum branch dimension Q with free classical labels,
no entanglement assistance or side channel. Exact positive Choi cubature
gives finite labels for each filter, existentially; no efficient node
acquisition or all-Q energy optimizer is assumed.

The U(3) lift has highest weight (a+b,b,0), center L=a+2b, and
q0=L/3. Write E_ij for its standard matrix-unit generators. The
traceless T_0 corresponding to Z0=diag(2/3,-1/3,-1/3) is E_11-q0 I.
Let H_R denote the entire E_11 shell of eigenvalue N-R, starting from
the maximum. A function F=f(R)I on each shell commutes with the
represented U(2) on indices2,3 and preserves its whole branch fibers.

2. EXACT SHELL DIMENSIONS FOR ALL 0<=R<=a

U(3) to U(2) branching is multiplicity free, with middle row
(u,v) satisfying b<=u<=N, 0<=v<=b and branch dimension u-v+1.
At the top E_11 shell set u=b+s,v=t, so s+t=R. For 0<=R<=a,
t runs from0 to min(R,b), with dimension b+R-2t+1. Summing gives
in BOTH regimes R<=b and R>=b exactly

  g_R=dim H_R=(b+1)(R+1),
  V_R=sum_(t=0)^R g_t=(b+1)(R+1)(R+2)/2.                  (2.1)

This formula does not stop at R=b. It is valid up to R=a, which is
essential for a thin highest-weight label.

3. ORDINARY ROOT CONDUCTANCE

For 0<=R<=a, let w_R be the sum of squared T matrix elements crossing
from H_R to H_(R+1) via E_21,E_31. The standard Hermitian generator
normalization makes the two real generators of a root pair contribute
one weight per undirected edge. Set w_(-1)=0. The commutator identity

  sum_(j=2,3)[E_1j,E_j1]=3E_11-LI

implies after shell trace

  w_R-w_(R-1)=(2a+b-3R)g_R.                              (3.1)

Indeed downward norm on shell R is w_R and upward norm is w_(R-1).
Summing (3.1) using (2.1) yields

  w_R=(b+1)(R+1)(R+2)(2a+b-2R)/2.                       (3.2)

For a real shell filter whose only varying edges have R<=a,

  E_T(F)=sum_R w_R(f_R-f_(R+1))^2.                       (3.3)

The exact GT-root identities are classical representation machinery;
the normalization and energy application are stated here in full.

4. EXACT SECOND-ADJOINT SHELL MEAN

In the U(3) lift define Q_i=sum_j E_ji E_ij. The quadratic Casimir
sum_i Q_i acts by c_U3=N(N+2)+b^2. The defining-matrix characteristic
identity, or direct expansion of the t_A completeness relation, gives

  D_Z=sum_i z_i Q_i+(3/2-2q0)T_Z                         (4.1)

for diagonal traceless fundamental Z=diag(z_i). To verify the constant,
write X=sum t_A tensor T_A=(E_ji-q0 delta_ij)/2. The exact identity
2Tr_fund(Z X^2)=D_Z/2-3T_Z/4 gives (4.1). On the defining carrier it
reduces to D_Z=(5/6)T_Z as required.

For Z0 and shell R, Q_1=E_11^2+sum_(j=2,3)E_j1 E_1j. The second term
has trace w_(R-1), while E_11=N-R. Thus

 Tr_(H_R) D_0/g_R
   =(N-R)^2+w_(R-1)/g_R-c_U3/3
                     +(3/2-2q0)((2a+b)/3-R).             (4.2)

Since w_(R-1)/g_R=R(2a+b-2R+2)/2, the quadratic R terms cancel.
Using alpha=C3/C2, one obtains

 alpha-(2a+b+3)/6=-b(b+2)(2a+b+3)/(6C2),

 Tr_(H_R) S_0/g_R
   =b(b+2)[a(N+1)-(2a+b+3)R]/(6N C2),   0<=R<=a.         (4.3)

This is an exact trace over the full fiber, not a statement about each
individual GT basis vector. It is of order b^2/N near the top for b<<a;
the generic operator norm of S_0 is of order b and cannot replace it.

5. EXACT MIXED ROOT CONDUCTANCE AND CANCELLATION

Let S_ij denote the complex adjoint tensor corresponding to E_ij, and
define c_R as the REAL mixed S/T weight on edges H_R to H_(R+1):

  c_R=sum_(j=2,3) Re Tr_(H_R)(S_1j E_j1), c_(-1)=0.        (5.1)

For the opposite crossing use cyclicity between the two shell spaces:
c_(R-1)=sum_j Re Tr_(H_R)(E_j1 S_1j). Covariance gives
[S_1j,E_j1]=S_11-S_jj. The traceless diagonal tensors sum to zero.
Therefore

  c_R-c_(R-1)=3 Tr_(H_R) S_0.                             (5.2)

This also fixes the mixed normalization: the two Hermitian generators
of each root pair give one real mixed weight per undirected edge.
Summing (5.2) using (2.1),(4.3) gives exactly

 c_R=(b+1)(R+1)(R+2)b(b+2)
          [3a(N+1)-2(2a+b+3)R]/(12N C2).                 (5.3)

For real shell profiles,

  E_TS(F)=sum_R c_R(f_R-f_(R+1))^2.                       (5.4)

If every varying edge is in 0<=R<=a/2, its c_R is nonnegative and

  c_R/w_R <= b(b+2)a(N+1)/(2N^2 C2)
           <=6 C_S/C2.                                  (5.5)

For the second inequality, divide by the exact C_S formula in (1.1)
of the weighted-converse note; the ratio is at most
6C2/[(a+2)(N+3)]<=6. Here a>=b gives N<=2a, and
C2<=N(N+3)/3<=(a+2)(N+3). Consequently the ENTIRE filter class has

  0<=E_TS(F)<=6(C_S/C2)E_T(F).                             (5.6)

This is an actual cancellation from preservation of whole fibers.
It is not uniform comparability of E_S and E_T for arbitrary filters.

6. FULL GIBBS UPPER, INCLUDING NONCOMMUTING HIGHER POWERS

Let D_F(X)=int[F_g,[F_g,X]]dg and S_F=Tr F^2. The exact two-adjoint
block says D_F(T_A)=uT_A+vS_A, with
u=E_T(F)/(d C2), v=E_TS(F)/(d C_S). The scalar sums of squares imply
||T_H||1<=dr sqrt(C2), ||S_H||1<=dr sqrt(C_S), hence

  ||D_F(B)||1<=r E_T(F)/(N sqrt(C2))
                            +r E_TS(F)/(N sqrt(C_S))
             <=8r E_T(F)/N^2.                            (6.1)

The last step uses sqrt(C2)>=N/2, (5.6), and
sqrt(C_S)<=C2/N. The latter follows from
Delta<=Omega=C2(C2/3+1/4)<=C2^2 for a nontrivial carrier.

The legal covariant channel Phi_F=d/S_F int F_g(.)F_g satisfies
Phi_F-I=-dD_F/(2S_F). The ordered double-derivation exponential
identity from the independent regular_su3 proof applies without
commutativity assumptions. With ||B||<=2r, gamma=r^2/8 it gives

 (1/2)||Phi_F(tau_h)-tau_h||1
   <=[exp(4rh)/(4S_F)] [8rh+gamma h^2]E_T(F)/N^2.         (6.2)

Every fixed positive h interval is allowed, with constants independent
of a/b. This is a full-state error, not a bound on the first harmonic.
The mixed cancellation in (5.6) is used only at the first generator;
the ordered power expansion controls all higher products.

7. EXPLICIT LINEAR TAPER AND MEMORY COST

For integer 1<=R<=floor(a/2), define F_R on shell t by

  f_t=(R-t) for 0<=t<R, f_t=0 for t>=R.                   (7.1)

It is positive with exact rank, norm and ordinary energy

  Q_R=(b+1)R(R+1)/2,
  S_F=(b+1)R(R+1)^2(R+2)/12,
  E_T(F_R)/S_F=(4a+2b-3R+3)/(R+1).                       (7.2)

These follow by summing (2.1),(3.2), with unit layer differences on
0<=t<R. This is a polynomial-description filter on supplied matrices.
Its largest R is imposed to ensure the mixed cancellation (5.5), not
an inferred cap validity beyond that range.

Equation (6.2) gives e<=C_(H,h1) a/[N^2(R+1)], or equivalently a
constant times sqrt(b+1)/(N sqrt(Q_R)). For a prescribed Q>=b+1 choose
the largest R<=floor(a/2) with Q_R<=Q. In the range Q<(b+1)
floor(a/2)(floor(a/2)+1)/2 this yields, up to fixed factors,

  e*_Q<=C_(H,h1) sqrt(b+1)/(N sqrt(Q)).                    (7.3)

For larger Q this one taper only gives the error at its largest R;
monotonicity does not establish the near-full-rank optimum. If b stays
bounded, (7.3) has a constant depending on that bound, rather than a
wall-uniform all-Q result for b=N^beta. It is a concrete comparator,
not a proof that Lambda_T is attained within this filter class.

8. EXACT COHERENT MIXED ENERGY AND THE UNIFORM Q=1 THEOREM

Let P be the highest-weight rank-one projector. The ordinary all-matrix
rank-one minimum is exactly E_T(P)=Lambda_T(1)=2N. The proof by the
weight-polytope moment bound and scalar-Casimir two-modulus identity
does not require a regular cone.

There is also an exact mixed identity

  E_TS(P)=-ab(a-b)(N+1)/(3N C2).                          (8.1)

One direct derivation is as follows. Use the highest U(3) weight
n=(N,b,0), q0=(a+2b)/3 and

  ell_i=n_i^2+sum_(j<i)(n_j-n_i)+(3/2-2q0)n_i.

Raising E_ij kills the highest vector for i<j; lowering/raising for
i>j has squared norm n_j-n_i. Thus (4.1) gives the D expectation
ell. The mean-vector inner products are

  ||m_T||^2=(1/2)sum_i(n_i-q0)^2=C2-N,
  m_T dot m_S=(1/(2N))sum_i(n_i-q0)
                            [ell_i-mean(ell)-alpha(n_i-q0)].

Expansion reduces the second expression to minus one half of (8.1).
Since sum T_AS_A=0, expanding the mixed rank-one commutator square
gives E_TS(P)=-2m_T dot m_S, proving (8.1).

For a,b>0 its ratio to sqrt(C_S) satisfies

 |E_TS(P)|/sqrt(C_S)
   =(2/sqrt3) sqrt[ab/((a+2)(b+2))]
       |a-b|/sqrt(C2) sqrt[(N+1)/(N+3)] <=2.               (8.2)

On exact pure walls S=0 and E_TS(P)=0. Inserting (8.2) into the
generic first-block bound (6.3) of the weighted note gives
||D_P(B)||1<=6r/N, while E_T(P)/N^2=2/N. The full ordered Gibbs
series then gives for every highest weight a,b>=0,N=a+b>=1

  e*_1(h)<=[exp(4rh)/4][6rh+2gamma h^2]/N.                 (8.3)

The independent self-score lower at Lambda_T(1)=2N gives

  e*_1(h)>=[h^2 r^2 exp(-12rh)/8]/N.                      (8.4)

Thus e*_1=Theta_(H,h0,h1)(1/N) uniformly over all SU3 walls, crossover
scales and interiors, for any fixed nonzero H and positive h interval.
The same highest-weight measure-and-prepare code works for every h.
No optimizer is acquired. This is a scoped classical-only consequence,
not an all-Q wall bridge or an iid qutrit statement.

8A. EXACT PURE WALL: ALL-Q CONTROL FOR GENERAL NONZERO H

The earlier symmetric packet specified a rank-one fundamental orbit.
For clarity, the pure-wall control here can be extended to ANY fixed
nonzero H directly, without importing that orbit restriction. On
V_(N,0) or V_(0,N), C2=N(N+3)/3 and the adjoint multiplicity is one.
Thus a positive filter has exactly

  D_F(T_A)=E_T(F)T_A/(d C2),
  ||D_F(B)||1<=sqrt3 r E_T(F)/N^2.                         (8A.1)

The first assertion follows from covariance and contraction with the
scalar T-square; no second tensor exists. The norm bound uses
sqrt(C2)>=N/sqrt3. The same full ordered exponential proof yields
an upper constant times E_T(F)/(S_F N^2), uniformly for every N,Q
and fixed h interval. The independent nonunital self-score supplies
the lower and the two-modulus lemma supplies a positive rank minimizer.
Consequently pure-wall e*_Q≈Lambda_T(Q)/N^2 holds for all Q even when
H has three distinct eigenvalues. The claimed unknown bridge concerns
small positive b or a, not this exact pure-wall case.
