D-DIMENSIONAL FREE-FERMION REPULSIVE UNIVERSALITY: INDEPENDENT AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally proved candidate; independent hostile audit passed. Small-box
checks are floating-point diagnostics, not exact arithmetic. This is a spinless
fermion Fock-space statement, not a d-dimensional hard-core-spin/JW theorem.

1. FINITE MODEL AND CONTRACT

Let Lambda_L={1,...,L-1}^d, S_L=(L-1)^d, with d fixed, and let F_L be the
spinless fermion Fock space over ell^2(Lambda_L), dim F_L=2^{S_L}. For each
site x let c_x,c_x^dagger obey the CAR and n_x=c_x^dagger c_x. Put
M=sum_x n_x. The one-particle Dirichlet hopping matrix is
 h_L=4Jd I-2J A_L,
where A_L is the nearest-neighbour adjacency matrix of the box. Its sine
eigenvectors and eigenvalues are
 phi_k(x)=(2/L)^{d/2} product_{a=1}^d sin(pi k_a x_a/L),
 eps_k=4J sum_a(1-cos(pi k_a/L)), k in {1,...,L-1}^d.
The second-quantized positive kinetic energy is K=dGamma(h_L), so
H_0=K-lambda M/L^2, beta=tau L^2, with J,tau>0 and |lambda|<=B.

Fix a finite list Z of nonzero integer displacements. For each z include the
unordered/ordered pairs (x,x+z) for which both endpoints are in Lambda_L;
overcounting pairs is permitted. Let Q be the diagonal Fock-space projector
onto configurations containing at least one such pair, and P=I-Q. Add any
finite-dimensional W_L>=0 satisfying W_L P=0. W need not preserve M or be
local, and can be arbitrarily large and L-dependent. This is a real
spinless-free-fermion model. No tensor-product local-spin representation in
d>1 is asserted: the Jordan-Wigner map used for a chain is not local here.
For a physical fermion Hamiltonian one can additionally require W to commute
with fermion parity; the mathematical theorem does not need that condition.

Write rho_0 and rho_W for the normalized beta-Gibbs states of H_0 and H_0+W.
Define the finite constants
 c_0=e^(tau B) sum_{k in N^d} exp(-8 tau J |k|^2),
 c_2=e^(tau B) sum_{k in N^d} |k|^2 exp(-8 tau J |k|^2),
 A=2*4^d*pi^2*c_0*c_2*sum_{z in Z}|z|^2,
 a_L=A/L^(d+2), and s_L=log S_L.
For L>=2 and a_L<=1/2, the candidate conclusion is
 ||rho_W-rho_0||_1 <= sqrt(2 G_L),
 G_L=[h_2(a_L)+a_L+(2+c_0)a_L log S_L
      +tau*a_L{2J*pi^2*c_2+8Jd L^2(2+c_0)+B(2+2c_0)}]/(1-a_L).
Thus G_L=O(L^-d), and the whole-state trace distance is O(L^-d/2),
uniformly over lambda and over every W>=0 with W P=0. Constants may depend on
d,J,tau,B,Z, not on the W magnitude. For finitely many L with a_L>1/2 use
the trivial trace-distance bound 2. This is a candidate theorem; novelty is
not established.

2. DILUTE DETERMINANT ESTIMATE

Second quantization makes rho_0 a gauge-invariant quasi-free state. In the
energy sine basis its occupations are independent Bernoulli variables with
 f_k=(1+exp(tau(L^2 eps_k-lambda)))^-1.
The elementary cosine inequalities on [0,pi] give
 8J|k|^2 <= L^2 eps_k <= 2J*pi^2 |k|^2,
so f_k<=exp(tau B-8tau J|k|^2). Consequently Tr(rho_0 M)<=c_0 and
 L^2 Tr(rho_0 K)<=2J*pi^2 c_2.
In the position basis the occupation process is determinantal with kernel
 C_xy=sum_k f_k phi_k(x)phi_k(y). In particular, for distinct x,y,
 Tr(rho_0 n_x n_y)=sum_{k<l} f_k f_l
       [phi_k(x)phi_l(y)-phi_l(x)phi_k(y)]^2.

For y=x+z, the product sine function g_k(x)=product_a sin(pi k_a x_a/L)
satisfies |g_k(x+z)-g_k(x)|<=pi |k||z|/L. This follows by telescoping
the d factors, using |sin u-sin v|<=|u-v| in each coordinate and
Cauchy-Schwarz. Since |g_k|<=1,
 |g_k(x)g_l(x+z)-g_l(x)g_k(x+z)|
 <= |g_k(x)-g_k(x+z)|+|g_l(x)-g_l(x+z)|
 <= pi |z|(|k|+|l|)/L.
Restoring the orbital normalization yields
 [phi_k(x)phi_l(x+z)-phi_l(x)phi_k(x+z)]^2
 <=4^d*pi^2 |z|^2(|k|+|l|)^2/L^(2d+2).
Further,
 sum_{k<l} f_k f_l (|k|+|l|)^2
 <=2 sum_{k<l} f_k f_l(|k|^2+|l|^2)
 <=2 (sum_k f_k)(sum_k |k|^2 f_k)<=2c_0 c_2.
Summing at most S_L<=L^d starting sites for each z gives
 delta=Tr(rho_0 Q)<=sum_{x,z} Tr(rho_0 n_x n_{x+z})<=a_L.

The weighted bad mass needed for the energy estimate is
 q_M=Tr(rho_0 M Q)<= (2+c_0) a_L.
Indeed Q<=sum_{x,z}n_x n_{x+z} pointwise. For each distinct pair,
 M n_x n_y=2n_xn_y+sum_{u notin{x,y}}n_xn_y n_u. Wick's determinant
formula gives E(n_xn_yn_u)=det C_{x,y,u}. The positive semidefinite Schur
complement inequality det C_{x,y,u}<=det C_{x,y} C_uu, valid also for
singular principal blocks by continuity. Summing over u bounds the triple
contribution by Tr(C) E(n_xn_y)<=c_0 E(n_xn_y). Sum over forbidden pairs.
This is a determinant bound; it does not assume independent site occupations.

3. COHERENT ALLOWED-SPACE PROJECTION AND ENERGY

Let delta=Tr(rho_0 Q), p=1-delta, and sigma=P rho_0 P/p. If delta=0 the
claim is immediate. Otherwise define omega=Q rho_0 Q/delta. The gentle
measurement bound gives ||sigma-rho_0||_1<=2 sqrt(delta).
Since W P=0, W sigma=0. Let h_L^F denote second quantization as above. The
Fock operators obey 0<=K<=8Jd M and [K,rho_0]=0. No commutator [K,Q]=0 is
needed. Expanding PKP and dropping the nonnegative cross traces gives
 Tr(rho_0 P K P)<=Tr(rho_0 K)+8Jd q_M.
For clarity, Tr(rho_0 QK)=Tr(rho_0 K Q)>=0: rho_0 and K commute, so
rho_0 K is positive semidefinite; its trace against Q is nonnegative.
Also QKQ<=8Jd QMQ=8Jd MQ because Q commutes with M. Therefore
 L^2[Tr(sigma K)-Tr(rho_0 K)]
 <=[2J*pi^2 c_2 delta+8Jd L^2 q_M]/p.
As [P,M]=0, exactly
 Tr(sigma M)-Tr(rho_0 M)=(delta Tr(rho_0 M)-q_M)/p,
whose absolute value is <=[c_0 delta+q_M]/p. These inequalities use finite
matrix traces and hold even when W later breaks number conservation.

4. ENTROPY AND GIBBS RELATIVE ENTROPY

Binary pinching by P,Q cannot decrease entropy:
 S(rho_0)<=h_2(delta)+p S(sigma)+delta S(omega).
Subtract S(sigma) and use p-1=-delta and S(sigma)>=0 to get the
stronger direct bound
 S(rho_0)-S(sigma)<=h_2(delta)+delta[S(omega)-S(sigma)]
                    <=h_2(delta)+delta S(omega).
The Gibbs variational inequality D(omega||exp(-sM)/Z_s)>=0 gives
 S(omega)<=s Tr(omega M)+log Z_s,
 Z_s=Tr_F exp(-sM)=(1+e^-s)^(S_L).
Choose s=log S_L (s=0 if S_L=1). Since log Z_s<=1,
 delta S(omega)<=s q_M+delta. Thus
 S(rho_0)-S(sigma)<=h_2(delta)+q_M log S_L+delta.
This removes the crude delta log dim(F_L)=delta S_L log2 term. It is valid
even though omega need not be gauge invariant or number diagonal: the entropy
variational bound uses only its expected M.

For finite W, min-max eigenvalue monotonicity H_0+W>=H_0 implies Z_W<=Z_0;
this is not an operator order of the exponentials. Because W sigma=0,
 D(sigma||rho_W)=-S(sigma)+beta Tr(sigma H_0)+log Z_W.
Subtract D(rho_0||rho_0)=0 and use the bounds above. For delta<=a_L<=1/2,
h_2(delta)<=h_2(a_L), q_M<=(2+c_0)a_L, and p>=1-a_L. The K term costs
tau a_L[2J*pi^2 c_2+8Jd L^2(2+c_0)]/p; the field costs at most
tau B a_L(2+2c_0)/p. Combining with the entropy bound gives
D(sigma||rho_W)<=G_L as displayed; the same calculation gives
D(sigma||rho_0)<=G_L. The direct reverse-KL comparison in Section 7
strengthens the resulting gentle-projection trace bound to
||rho_W-rho_0||_1<=sqrt(2G_L). Since L^2 a_L=L^-d times a constant,
G_L=O(L^-d) (the entropy terms are O(L^-(d+2)log L)).

5. SINGLE-COPY STATISTICAL CHANNEL CONSEQUENCE AND SCOPE

For each m let rho^0_{L,m} be the normalized restriction of exp(-beta H_0)
to the m-particle sector. Define Phi_L(X)=sum_m Tr(P_m X) rho^0_{L,m}. It is
CPTP, entanglement breaking, independent of lambda and W, and fixes rho_0
exactly. Hence ||Phi_L(rho_W)-rho_W||_1<=2||rho_W-rho_0||_1. The sufficient
classical statistic M is therefore an approximate EB reconstruction for the
whole repulsive family, including W that does not commute with M. This is not
exact sufficiency for the interacting states and does not imply a
reference-preserving/diamond-norm bound.

The result concerns a spinless fermion box with free Dirichlet hopping. A
d-dimensional hard-core spin model is not equivalent by a local JW circuit
in d>1 and is outside this claim. The theorem also does not cover attractive
or indefinite W, generic positive W that acts on P, displacement sets growing
with L unless their sum of squared lengths is charged, or a thermodynamic
limit with d itself growing. No efficient physical preparation of rho_W is
provided. Novelty/prior-art status remains UNKNOWN pending primary-source
comparison and external mathematical review.

6. DIRECT REVERSE-KL COMPARISON AND COLLECTIVE ARCHIVE AT FIXED TEMPERATURE

The projection estimate also gives D(sigma||rho_0)<=G_L. For the interacting
state, the Gibbs formula gives the exact identity
 D(rho_W||rho_0)=-beta Tr(rho_W W)+log(Z_0/Z_W).
Let Z_P=Tr_{Ran P} exp(-beta P H_0 P). Since W>=0 and W P=0, the restriction
of H_0+W to Ran P is P H_0 P. The min-max principle implies
 Z_W=Tr exp[-beta(H_0+W)] >= Z_P.
Also the Gibbs variational principle on Ran P gives
 log Z_P >= S(sigma)-beta Tr(sigma H_0), hence
 log(Z_0/Z_P)<=D(sigma||rho_0)<=G_L.
Dropping the nonpositive W term therefore yields
 D(rho_W||rho_0)<=G_L,
and quantum Pinsker gives ||rho_W-rho_0||_1<=sqrt(2G_L). This works when
[H_0,W]!=0 and [M,W]!=0. It uses eigenvalue min-max, not an operator
monotonicity assertion for exponentials.

For n independent copies with the same public L,J,tau and unknown common
lambda,W, relative entropy tensorizes:
 D(rho_W^{tensor n}||rho_0(lambda)^{tensor n})<=nG_L,
so the product-state trace distance is at most sqrt(2 n G_L). Define the
single collective statistic T=sum_{i=1}^n M_i and the channel
 Phi_{L,n}(X)=sum_t Tr(P_t X) rho^0_{L,n,t},
where P_t is the total-number projector and rho^0_{L,n,t} is the free
n-copy Gibbs state conditioned on T=t. The conditional state is independent
of lambda because the field contributes only the scalar exp(tau lambda t).
Thus this is an entanglement-breaking channel that fixes every free product
state exactly. For the interacting product family,
 ||Phi_{L,n}(rho_W^{tensor n})-rho_W^{tensor n}||_1
 <=2 sqrt(2 n G_L).
The archive label can be truncated: E[2^T]<=exp(n c_0), so
 Pr{T>t_*}<=2^(-t_*) exp(n c_0). Choosing
 t_*=ceil((n c_0+log(1/delta))/log 2)+1 and replacing overflow by vacuum
 adds at most 2 delta trace norm. The retained alphabet uses
ceil(log_2(t_*+2))=O(log(n+2)+log(2+log(1/delta))) bits, independent of L.
For this fixed-tau encoder, T can be measured in the site occupation basis
using nS_L occupation readouts and classical summation; the fine site record
is discarded and only T is retained, which implements the same EB channel.
For fixed known tau the conditional decoder is implementable by a weighted
subset sampler: on Q=nS mode slots with weights w_q=exp(-tau L^2 eps_q),
the suffix elementary-symmetric recurrence
 E[q,j]=E[q+1,j]+w_q E[q+1,j-1]
and backward sampling produce the exact T-subset. This takes O(Q t_*)
arithmetic operations and table entries, followed by Slater-state preparation.
For finite-bit sampling with requested sampler variation eta=eta_DP in
(0,1), retain each positive mode weight/suffix sum to relative error
eta/O(Q). Since the
largest one-mode energy is O(JL^2) and
there are at most t_* selected modes, a conservative binary precision is
O(t_* tau J L^2+t_* log(Q+1)+log(Q t_*/eta)) bits per positive table value.
Certified sine/exponential evaluation and threshold sampling add polynomial
bit work and O(p+log(Q/eta)) random bits per transition. These are
sampler/decoder costs, not archive-label bits.
It is not a claim of free input acquisition or interacting-state preparation.
Here eta_DP is total variation distance of the classical subset sampler, so
its contribution to the output trace norm is at most 2 eta_DP. Add the
Givens synthesis/measurement implementation error eta_gate separately.

7. UNKNOWN TEMPERATURE IN A COMPACT POSITIVE INTERVAL

Here is a separate approximate-sufficiency corollary, not needed by the
fixed-tau theorem. Assume public 0<tau_-<=tau<=tau_+<infinity, unknown
tau and lambda with |lambda|<=B, and otherwise the same finite displacement
family and positive W P=0. Uniformly replace c_0,c_2,tau in the previous
proof by
 c_0^*=exp(tau_+ B) sum_{k in N^d} exp(-8 tau_- J |k|^2),
 c_2^*=exp(tau_+ B) sum_{k in N^d}|k|^2 exp(-8 tau_- J |k|^2),
 tau_+ in the explicit prefactor of G, and
 A^*=2*4^d*pi^2*c_0^* c_2^* sum_z |z|^2.
The resulting a_L^*=A^*/L^(d+2), G_L^*=O(L^-d) are uniform over the
compact parameter box for sufficiently large L with a_L^*<=1/2; smaller L
use the trivial trace bound. The relative-entropy comparison remains
 D(rho_{tau,lambda,W}||rho_{tau,lambda,0})<=G_L^*.

For n copies put T=sum_i M_i and E=sum_i L^2 K_i. The free family is
 proportional to exp(-tau E+tau lambda T). Conditional on T=t and an energy
 bin I of width h, lambda cancels. For any two temperatures in the interval,
the log likelihood ratio between the conditional laws varies by at most
(tau_+-tau_-)h across the bin. Hence a fixed reference temperature
tau_ref in [tau_-,tau_+] gives conditional trace distance at most
 exp((tau_+-tau_-)h)-1. Measuring the pair (T, floor(E/h)) and preparing
the free reference Gibbs state conditioned on that pair therefore gives an
EB reconstruction of the free family up to this shape error. Applied to the
interacting family, its trace error is at most
 2 sqrt(2 n G_L^*) + exp((tau_+-tau_-)h)-1 + 2 delta,
when the combined number-or-energy overflow probability under the free
family is at most delta (for example, budget delta/2 to each tail).
If tau_- = tau_+, omit the energy label and use Section 6.

The tails have explicit dimension-independent cutoffs. Set
 t_* = ceil((n c_0^*+log(2/delta))/log 2),
 R = (n log C_E+log(2/delta))/a.
Then the number and energy tail probabilities sum to at most delta. From
the occupation bound, E exp((log 2)T)<=exp(n c_0^*), so
 Pr(T>t_*)<=2^(-t_*) exp(n c_0^*).
For energy let a= tau_-/2 and
 C_E=product_{k in N^d}(1+exp(tau_+B-4 tau_- J |k|^2))<infinity.
The free Bernoulli product and L^2 eps_k>=8J|k|^2 imply
 E exp(a E)<=C_E^n, hence Pr(E>R)<=C_E^n exp(-aR).
For a finite-bit implementation, use certified rational upper bounds
c_0_bar>=c_0^* and C_E_bar>=C_E. Such bounds follow by truncating the
separable Gaussian sums and bounding the decreasing tail by its Gaussian
integral; for example, sum_{k>m} exp(-c k^2)<=exp(-c m^2)/(2cm). Compute
an integer t_* with 2^(-t_*)exp(n c_0_bar)<=delta/2 and a rational grid
cutoff R_g>= (n log C_E_bar+log(2/delta))/a by directed interval
arithmetic, rounding upward to the energy grid. Use these rounded thresholds
in place of the ideal real cutoffs above. Then the actual two tail
probabilities are still at most delta. This slack avoids any equality test
on real-valued logarithms.
Taking t_*,R_g=O(n+log(1/delta)) and h=Theta(epsilon/(tau_+-tau_-))
(or any h with the displayed shape error <=epsilon) leaves at most
 (t_*+2)(ceil(R_g/h)+2) labels. The number of classical bits is
 O(log(n+2)+log(2+log(1/delta))+log(1/epsilon)), independent of L. Truncating
the combined overflow branch to vacuum costs at most 2 delta, as charged in
the displayed error.

Finite-bit conditional preparation can be made explicit, though it is not
fast in the box volume. Choose a rational grid spacing g0>0 and compute for each one-particle
energy e_k a certified rational interval 0<=ell_k<=e_k<=u_k with
u_k-ell_k<=g0. Set tilde_e_k=g0 floor(ell_k/g0). Then
0<=tilde_e_k<=e_k and e_k-tilde_e_k<=2g0; write zeta=2g0. Thus the
quantized energies are nonnegative exact grid multiples and never round
upward. Choose zeta so
 t_* zeta <= c h/(1+tau_+),
for a sufficiently small fixed c. Use rational bin width h=H g0 for an
integer H, selected by certified arithmetic to meet the desired shape budget,
and a suffix DP over Q'=nU slots, particle count <=t_*, and energy-grid
index m<=G_max=O((R_g+t_* zeta)/g0). The recurrence is
 D[q,j,m]=D[q+1,j,m]+exp(-tau_ref tilde_e_q)
                              D[q+1,j-1,m-m_q],
where tilde_e_q=g0*m_q. Summing DP weights over grid indices in the reported
bin and backtracking samples the reference conditional Slater mixture. A
retained label has quantized total energy tilde_E<=R_g, so its exact energy
is E<=R_g+t_*zeta and each occupied mode has e_k<=R_g+t_*zeta. Thus the
number U of candidate modes per copy is at most
O((1+(R_g+t_*zeta)/J)^(d/2)), independent of L. Conversely, an overflow
label tilde_E>R_g implies E>R_g, so the free overflow probability is bounded
by the Chernoff tail at R_g.
This preserves the energy-cutoff estimate without an upward-rounding error.
The grid sum and bin indices use exact integer/rational arithmetic; interval
endpoints are computed with directed rounding and refined until width <=g0.
No comparison oracle for equality of a transcendental energy and a bin
boundary is needed. With quantized-bin labels, the actual energy diameter in
one bin is at most h+2t_*zeta. Hence the within-label temperature-shape error
is at most exp((tau_+-tau_-)(h+2t_*zeta))-1. Replacing actual Boltzmann
weights by quantized-energy weights adds at most
exp(2 tau_ref t_* zeta)-1 in conditional trace norm. Choose zeta so both
quantization contributions fit the requested error budget. For a finite-bit
implementation, approximate each positive DP weight/sum with relative error
eta_DP/O(Q' t_* G_max); a conservative fixed-point precision is
p=O(tau_+ R_g+t_* log(Q'+1)+log(Q' t_* G_max/eta_DP)+log L)
bits per stored number, with certified sine/exponential evaluation for
public rational parameter inputs. Random thresholds use
O(p+log(Q'/eta_DP)) bits per transition, with total sampler variation
error budget eta_DP. Add the synthesis error eta_gate for encoder/decoder
Givens rotations. Since eta_DP is total variation, its trace-norm cost is
2eta_DP. The complete finite-channel bound is at most
 2 sqrt(2 n G_L^*) + 2 delta
 + exp((tau_+-tau_-)(h+2 t_* zeta))-1
 + exp(2 tau_ref t_* zeta)-1 + 2 eta_DP + eta_gate.
The second exponential is the error from replacing actual by quantized
Boltzmann weights. For any target excess epsilon, allocate fixed fractions
of epsilon to both displayed exponential terms, arithmetic/sampling and
gate synthesis. The table costs
O(Q' t_* G_max) entries and arithmetic operations (each poly(p) bit
operations); backtracking costs O(Q'). These are polynomial in n,1/epsilon,
log(1/delta), and independent of L except for gate synthesis precision.

The encoder can implement the energy/number observation by a fermionic
linear-optical transform from site to product-sine modes, measuring the
occupation bits, summing T and quantized energies classically, and discarding
the occupation record. A conservative generic implementation uses
O(n S_L^2) two-mode Givens rotations and n S_L occupation measurements.
The decoder samples a retained mode subset and prepares the corresponding
Slater basis vector in the site basis, again with O(n S_L^2) Givens rotations
and separately charged gate synthesis/routing. This outputs n copies and
requires at least the output-sized work/physical register. The information
archive itself retains only the label and uses zero persistent quantum
memory. Thermal preparation/acquisition at beta=tau L^2, decoder access to
the public one-particle transform, and the high-degree DP cost are not free.
These are abstract logical Givens counts. If a circuit has G=O(nS_L^2)
primitive rotations and target implementation error eta_gate in trace norm,
it suffices to synthesize each rotation to operator-norm error
O(eta_gate/G), by a telescoping unitary-product bound. The gate count/bit
cost of that synthesis and routing depends on the chosen finite gate set and
connectivity and is not claimed here. For the finite-bit sampler, J, tau
endpoints, and other public parameters are supplied as rational numbers to
the requested precision; encoding/acquiring those parameter descriptions is
also an external input cost.
