# Independent gate: weak local centralizer terms preserve witness energy

7 October2026. **PASS for the following algebraic/congestion lemma.** This is an independent reconstruction of root's proposed centralizer route, not independent adoption of every subsequent decoder/Peierls theorem or a general noncommuting thermal-stability claim.

Assume commuting Pauli checks C_a, minimum logical weight d, bounded physical qubit density and check incidence, and uniform original-check filling: every stabilizer in a radius-r ball is generated by checks whose whole supports lie in a radius-C max(r,1) ball. C is fixed for the family.

Let V=sum_X V_X be Hermitian, each nonempty X have bounded physical diameter R_v and |X|<d. Suppose the TOTAL V commutes with every original check, and J=sup_q sum_(X containing q)||V_X||. Individual input terms need not commute. The bound on |X| is eventual for a bounded-density/range family with growing d; it must be stated for finite levels.

## Twirling is local and norm contracting

Let T be the stabilizer-group conjugation average. Conjugation by a Pauli check preserves actual support X: factors outside X cancel, and every Pauli on X only acquires a sign. T(V_X) is therefore still supported in X, Hermitian, norm at most||V_X|| and commutes with every check. Moreover

    sum_X T(V_X)=T(V)=V.

One may apply only the commuting elementary conjugation averages for checks meeting X; all other checks act trivially on every intermediate term. The number of such elementary averages is at most w_q|X|. This is a finite local matrix operation for bounded |X|, though no whole-family implementation is executed here.

The Pauli expansion of T(V_X) consists of centralizer Paulis of weight below d. Each is a stabilizer up to scalar phase. Choose a ball of radius max(R_v,1) centered at an actual input site q_X. The assumed whole-check filling puts every one of those Paulis in the algebra of the SAME set F_X of original nonidentity checks wholly inside its fixed expanded ball.

Thus on each realizable full syndrome sigma,

    T(V_X) P_sigma=f_X(sigma) P_sigma,
    |f_X(sigma)|<=||T(V_X)||<=||V_X||.

The value depends only on signs of F_X. Its variation between any two realizable syndrome sectors is at most2||V_X||. No Pauli coefficient l1 bound, expansion cardinality or unconstrained fictitious sign assignment is used.

## Congestion depends on the input intensive norm

For a fixed changed check a, choose one actual site q_a in its support. If a belongs to F_X, its WHOLE support is in the fixed filling ball centered at q_X. Therefore q_X is within a fixed radius R_* of q_a. With B a uniform upper bound on the number of physical qubits in such a ball,

    sum_(X:a in F_X)||V_X||
      <=sum_(q within R_* of q_a) sum_(X containing q)||V_X||
      <=B J.

The first inequality assigns each X to its chosen q_X. It does not require a canonical or efficiently acquired generator representation. B explicitly includes the filling/range/density constants and can be enormous.

Writing v(sigma)=sum_X f_X(sigma) gives

    |v(sigma)-v(sigma')|<=2 B J |sigma symmetric-difference sigma'|.  (1)

This is a variation estimate between realizable sectors. A mere bound||V||<=NJ would not give it.

## Valid erasers retain an energy margin

For H0=sum_a (I-C_a)/2, a witness eraser tau subset sigma must produce a realizable syndrome sigma'=sigma minus tau. Its unperturbed energy drop is |tau|. Equation(1) gives

    E_(H0+V)(sigma)-E_(H0+V)(sigma')
                         >=(1-2 B J)|tau|.             (2)

Choose2BJ<1. Stabilizer syndrome sectors all have the same nonzero dimension2^k, and V is scalar on them, so their Gibbs weight ratio is bounded by exp[-beta(1-2BJ)|tau|]. A prior Peierls injection/eraser counting argument may consequently use that reduced energy margin, PROVIDED its same eraser is valid in the mixed syndrome interface. Validity and the prior count are separate gates, not inferred here.

This proves exactly the lemma requested by root. Commuting only with H0 is insufficient: its degenerate energy sectors permit operators transferring excitations between individual check signs. Full per-check commutation is necessary for the scalar syndrome argument. Physically nonlocal bounded-body supports are also insufficient for a fixed local congestion radius; the stated physical-diameter condition is paid.
