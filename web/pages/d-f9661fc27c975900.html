2. EXACT FAITHFUL RADIUS AND CAPACITY: REVIEWER'S EXPOSED EXTENSION

For the origin family let D_j=2^j+1, D_H=2^K+K, delta=w/K,
rho'_psi=(1-eta)rho_psi+eta I_H/D_H, 0<eta<1. Put
 u=eta/D_H,
 c=(1-eta)delta+u,
 q_j=(1-eta)delta/D_j+u.
The reviewer proves the EXACT convex-minimax radius

 R(E')=max_(0<=r<K) (K-r)c
    ln[(K-r)c/((1-eta)delta sum_(j=r)^(K-1)1/D_j+u(K-r))]. (F1)

At the design mean an effect's vacuum and orthogonal-ray components have
target density <=center density, so their removal cannot decrease any
positive divergence. On the remaining rays p=c sum f_j and q=sum q_j f_j.
Convexity gives a vertex maximum; highest flags minimize q for fixed
number of selected rays. For any convex center, the same exact-design
average of tail projectors and Jensen give the matching lower. Thus(F1)
is a minimax formula, not merely an upper at a selected center. I checked
these steps after exposure; the derivation remains credited to the reviewer.

The reviewer also proves the EXACT MAX-PRIOR capacity

 C(E')=K c ln c+u sum_j(D_j-1)ln u-sum_j D_j q_j ln q_j. (F2)

Each target has vacuum eigenvalue v=(1-eta)(1-w)+u, ray eigenvalue c,
and orthogonal eigenvalue u. Every mean has fixed block trace D_j q_j.
The design mean simultaneously maximizes block entropies; equal vacuum
entropies cancel, giving(F2). This is not the unperturbed capacity formula.
The origin's binary-tag upper/lower bounds remain correct.

For eta=delta^2 the reviewer's exact formulas prove:
 - RegimeA,w=1/K: R(E')->ln2/4 and C(E')->ln2/2;
 - RegimeB,w=2^(-K): R(E')/R(E)->1 and C(E')/C(E)->1,
   with both quantities tending to zero.

For eta>0, R(E')<=(1-eta)R(E)<R(E), since R(E)>0. The exact faithful
radius is therefore literally DIFFERENT from w R_K. This is the precise
correction needed by ancillary Section4. The power obstruction requires
only the origin's upper R(E')<1, so no new dependency is inserted there.
