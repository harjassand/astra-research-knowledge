SUPPLIED PREMISE P

Let E be any nonempty, possibly infinite, family of density matrices on one
fixed finite-dimensional H. Put
 C(E)=sup_finite_priors [S(sum_theta pi_theta rho_theta)
                         -sum_theta pi_theta S(rho_theta)]
in nats. Define b(E) as the infimum over ONE full-domain CPTP map
B:M_d -> M_d tensor M_d of the maximum over rho in E and output j=1,2
of (1/2)||B_j(rho)-rho||_1. Output correlation is unrestricted.
Let e(E) be the analogous infimum reconstruction error over ONE EB CPTP
Gamma:M_d -> M_d. The supplied candidate theorem says
 e <= f(C,b):=min(1,400 sqrt((C+1)/ln(1/b)))     for 0<b<1,
 e=0 for b=0; for b=1 use f=1.
In each fixed finite dimension the optimum e is attained: the EB CPTP set
is compact and the supremum reconstruction error is a continuous function
of Gamma. Thus one Gamma has error epsilon=f(C,b), with no state label
supplied at runtime. This does not assert closeness on entangled references.

COMMON NOTATION AND FINITE DECOMPOSITION

Fix any EB Gamma with sup_E (1/2)||Gamma(rho)-rho||_1 <= epsilon. In finite
dimension it has a finite measure-and-prepare representation
 Gamma(X)=sum_{z=1}^K Tr(M_z X) sigma_z,
 M_z>=0, sum_z M_z=I, sigma_z density matrices.
One can take K<=d^4: the normalized separable Choi state belongs to the
convex hull of pure product states in the real affine space of trace-one
Hermitian d^2 by d^2 matrices, dimension d^4-1. Caratheodory gives at most
d^4 products; its fixed input marginal I/d makes their input effects a
POVM (with the Choi transpose). This is dimension-dependent, existential
support reduction; it is no capacity-only bound or acquisition algorithm.
Write M for the quantum-to-classical POVM channel and R for the preparation
channel. Gamma=R M. Trace distance and TV below both include the factor 1/2.

TARGET A: ONE CLASSICAL STATISTICAL EXPERIMENT FOR ALL ORDINARY DECISIONS

Theorem A1. For E={rho_theta}, define the single classical experiment
 p_theta(z)=Tr(M_z rho_theta). It is exactly obtainable from E by M and
E is recovered from it by R with worst statewise error epsilon. For every
finite-supported prior pi, finite action set, and loss l(theta,a) in [0,1],
their optimal Bayes risks satisfy
 R_Q(pi,l) <= R_C(pi,l) <= R_Q(pi,l)+epsilon.
The same p_theta, M, R work for ALL these priors, losses, and quantum POVMs.
Moreover every exact matrix preparation relation sum_theta u_theta
rho_theta=0 is preserved as sum_theta u_theta p_theta=0, and the classical
experiment's capacity sup_pi I(Theta;Z) is at most C(E).

Proof. Any classical randomized decision d(a|z) induces the quantum POVM
A_a=sum_z d(a|z)M_z, proving R_Q<=R_C. Conversely, for any quantum POVM
{A_a}, use the classical decision d(a|z)=Tr(A_a sigma_z). Its distribution
on theta is Tr(A_a Gamma(rho_theta)), within TV epsilon of the original
POVM distribution by trace-distance contraction. For each theta the loss
is in [0,1], so its expectation changes by at most epsilon. Average under
pi and optimize. Linearity of M preserves every stated relation exactly.
The Holevo information of the cq ensemble cannot increase under M, giving
I(Theta;Z)<=chi(pi,E)<=C(E). The same reconstruction bound extends by
convexity to conv(E), but it still has no arbitrary-reference promise. QED.

This is the forward randomization/deficiency implication with explicit
maps. Ordinary classical decision comparisons do NOT generally have a
quantum-channel converse, even with a classical experiment. Jenčova's
quantum randomization theorem uses the appropriate enlarged decision
tests. We claim no new Blackwell theorem. Nor does I(Theta;Z)<=C imply
H(Z)<=C or K<=exp(C): Theorem B2 below gives a decisive counterexample.

Corollary A2 (equivalence-constrained preparation witness). Consider a
finite prepare-and-measure task with a [0,1] score, preparations in E,
and specified preparation equivalences that are exact matrix identities.
Suppose EVERY classical encoding satisfying these equivalences, with an
unrestricted latent alphabet, and EVERY allowed response rule has score
at most beta. If the given quantum task achieves beta+Delta, Delta>0, then
 e(E)>=Delta.
Under P this implies, for all b including the endpoints,
 b(E) >= exp[-160000(C(E)+1)/Delta^2].

Proof. The common POVM encoding respects the stipulated equivalences and
responses Tr(A_a sigma_z) produce the Gamma-reconstructed score. Its score
differs by at most epsilon from the quantum score, so every such Gamma
has epsilon>=Delta. Take the infimum. Substitute e>=Delta into P; if
0<b<1, rearrange ln(1/b)<=160000(C+1)/Delta^2. b=0 is impossible, b=1
is trivial. QED.

Qualification: an operational equivalence relative only to a RESTRICTED
measurement set need not be a matrix identity. Likewise, effects agreeing
only on E can disagree on decoder states sigma_z. A1/A2 do not silently
preserve those weaker subtheory equivalences or assert full preparation-
and-measurement noncontextuality. Constraints actually imposed on the
classical benchmark must be checked. No latent-cardinality restriction
may be imported into beta. These are early falsifiers of broader claims.

Concrete check: two-bit parity-oblivious multiplexing. For x in {0,1}^2
let
 rho_x=(I+[(-1)^x1 sigma_x+(-1)^x2 sigma_z]/sqrt(2))/2.
The even and odd parity mixtures are both I/2. Uniform x and query y=1,2,
with measurement sigma_x or sigma_z, yield quantum success
 (1+1/sqrt(2))/2.
Any parity-blind classical message has success at most 3/4. Indeed for a
message z of positive probability, its posterior over x has the form
 [1+a(-1)^x1+b(-1)^x2]/4 because the parity Fourier coefficient vanishes.
Positivity gives |a|+|b|<=1, so the best averaged bit guess has success
1/2+(|a|+|b|)/4<=3/4. Thus Delta=(sqrt(2)-1)/4 and C=ln 2, since these
are pure qubit states with an I/2 average. A2 gives a very weak exponential
lower bound on broadcasting error. The task, classical bound and quantum
advantage are prior results of Spekkens et al.; their proof is reproduced
here only to check the compatibility and constants. The broad exact
contextuality/no-broadcasting link is also prior (Jokinen et al.). Only
the quantitative supplied-modulus composition is at issue, with priority
UNKNOWN and no claim of sharpness or practical cloning certification.

TARGET B: ONE FIXED CLASSICAL PREDICTIVE KERNEL

Theorem B1. Let {L_a}_{a in A} be a supplied finite-output quantum
instrument on M_d: each L_a is completely positive, and sum_a L_a is TP.
Let rho_0 be a supplied initial state. Suppose E contains rho_0 and every
normalized conditional quantum memory state reached after every original
positive-probability finite output history. If ONE common Gamma has error
epsilon on E, the following edge-emitting classical HMM is well defined:
 q_0(z)=Tr(M_z rho_0),
 T(a,z'|z)=Tr(M_z' L_a(sigma_z)).
One fixed time-homogeneous kernel works for all horizons, and its n-output
law Q_n obeys
 TV(P_n,Q_n)<=min(1,n epsilon),
where P_n is the original quantum instrument's n-output law.

Proof of the kernel. Positivity gives T>=0 and
 sum_{a,z'} T(a,z'|z)=Tr(sum_a L_a(sigma_z))=1.
Starting with q_0, preparation gives Gamma(rho_0). After an output a,
measuring the postinstrument memory gives z', and preparing sigma_z'
implements Gamma L_a. Thus the classical HMM output law is exactly the
quantum process with Gamma inserted before the first instrument use and
after each output, before the next use. A final Gamma has no effect on
the output probabilities.

Proof of the horizon bound, including the reachable-family gate. For
j=0,...,n define a hybrid using the original instrument for its first j
outputs, inserting Gamma immediately thereafter and before each remaining
instrument use. The j=n hybrid has the original output law; j=0 has Q_n.
For adjacent hybrids j and j+1, the first j histories have their ORIGINAL
probabilities. At history h of positive original probability the memory
rho_h belongs to E. The only differing local insertion before the next
instrument is replacing rho_h by Gamma(rho_h). The associated cq prefix
states therefore have half trace distance at most
 sum_h P(h) (1/2)||rho_h-Gamma(rho_h)||_1 <= epsilon.
The next instrument and all subsequent resetting, instruments, and final
classical readout are CPTP, so contract this cq trace distance. Hence
adjacent output laws differ by at most epsilon. Sum n such comparisons.
Crucially the promise is invoked only on ORIGINAL conditional states;
modified trajectories can leave E without causing a gap. QED.

Controlled version. If supplied instruments L_{a|u} are indexed by
classical controls, E must contain every original reachable memory under
every admitted classical feedback policy. Set
 T(a,z'|u,z)=Tr(M_z' L_{a|u}(sigma_z)).
The same hybrid proof, with u chosen from the shared classical prefix,
gives n epsilon for every such policy. This requires the uniform union of
reachable-state families, rather than a policy-specific Gamma. Coherent
controls or entangled quantum references are outside the statement.

Broadcasting composition. Under P, the same HMM satisfies
 TV(P_n,Q_n)<=min(1,400 n sqrt((C+1)/ln(1/b))) for 0<b<1.
For fixed output error delta, ln(1/b)>=160000 n^2(C+1)/delta^2 suffices.
For varying families, horizons n_j can diverge with vanishing TV if
n_j sqrt((C_j+1)/ln(1/b_j)) ->0. At b=0 an exact classical realization
exists. This is a finite-horizon simulation theorem. It provides no
infinite-path TV bound, stationary entropy bound, efficient learned
kernel, or guarantee that one can acquire the family/POVM from observations.
The supplied complete instrument and EB decomposition determine the
kernel mathematically. Finding them or certifying the all-history family
is separately charged. The finite support K<=d^4 still depends on d.

THE FATAL MEMORY-SIZE GATE: CAPACITY DOES NOT BOUND CLASSICAL ALPHABET

Theorem B2. Let N be a multiple of 8, and E_N contain all diagonal
 rho_S=(2/N) sum_{i in S}|i><i|,
where S ranges over subsets of [N] of size N/2. Then C(E_N)=ln 2 and E_N
is exactly broadcastable by one full-domain channel. Yet any fixed
classical K-symbol encoder and fixed quantum preparation decoder that
reconstruct all rho_S with error <=1/16 must satisfy
 K >= L_N^all := [(1-h_2(1/4))N ln 2 - ln(N+1)]/ln 9.
Here h_2 denotes binary entropy in BITS. In particular K=Omega(N), whereas
capacity and b are fixed. Exact reconstruction needs K>=N and K=N works.

Proof of capacity and broadcasting. D(rho_S||I/N)=ln 2. For every prior,
chi=ln 2-D(mean rho||I/N)<=ln 2. A uniform prior over balanced S has
mean I/N and attains ln 2. The channel
 B(X)=sum_i <i|X|i> |ii><ii|
is CPTP on the full algebra and each marginal fixes every rho_S. Its
outputs are correlated; it is not an independent cloning assertion.

Proof of the robust support lower bound. A greedy code of balanced S
with pairwise symmetric-difference size at least N/4 has cardinality
 M_code >= 2^{N(1-h_2(1/4))}/(N+1).
There are at least 2^N/(N+1) balanced subsets. Each greedy selection
deletes at most sum_{j<=N/4} binom(N,j)<=2^{N h_2(1/4)} subsets, even
when all, rather than only balanced, subsets are counted in its ball.
For selected S,T, TV(rho_S,rho_T)=|S symmetric_difference T|/N>=1/4.
Let q_S in Delta_K be their encoded distributions. The triangle
inequality and contraction under the preparation decoder imply
 TV(q_S,q_T)>=1/4-2/16=1/8.
Thus open l_1 balls of radius 1/8 centered at q_S are pairwise disjoint
in R^K. They lie in the l_1 ball of radius 9/8 centered at 0 since each
q_S has l_1 norm 1. Volume comparison gives M_code<=9^K. Taking logs
proves the bound. This permits arbitrary quantum decoder states, not
merely diagonal or basis states. For exact reconstruction, the target
states have affine span dimension N-1 (swapping i,j in balanced S gives
every e_i-e_j direction). K prepared states have affine span <=K-1,
so K>=N. Measuring the basis and preparing it attains K=N. QED.

STATIONARY PROCESS STRENGTHENING, INCLUDING SHANNON MEMORY ENTROPY

Theorem B3. There is a stationary supplied quantum instrument of dimension
N+2, whose entire original reachable memory family has C=ln 4 and b=0,
such that every exact classical HMM realization has stationary hidden
memory entropy at least
 ln 3+(ln N)/3,
and this minimum is attained by an N+2-state classical HMM. Moreover
every classical HMM whose TWO-OUTPUT law approximates the given stationary
law within TV 1/192 needs
 K >= L_N := [(1-h_2(1/4))N ln 2-ln(2(N+1))]/ln 9.
If the approximation error is <=1/384, its intermediate hidden memory
distribution pi must have entropy
 H(pi)>=(1/384) ln(L_N/2)
whenever L_N>2. Thus even fixed-error classical MEMORY ENTROPY cannot
be bounded by a function of this capacity and broadcasting error alone.

Construction and charged costs. Let M=binom(N,N/2), with basis states
|init>, |1>,...,|N>, |done>. The output alphabet is the DISJOINT union
of all M labels S, the N labels i, and a blank symbol bottom. Its size
is exponential in N; we do NOT claim a fixed-alphabet example. Define
 L_S(X)=<init|X|init> rho_S/M,
 L_i(X)=<i|X|i> |done><done|,
 L_bottom(X)=<done|X|done> |init><init|.
These are CP measure-and-prepare operations and their sum is TP. The
state
 rho_*=(|init><init|+I_N/N+|done><done|)/3
is fixed by sum_a L_a, hence defines a stationary output process. The
outputs cycle through S, i uniform in S, bottom, with uniform random
stationary phase and independent new choices on each cycle. It is
periodic, so no mixing hypothesis is being hidden.

Reachable family and capacity. After any positive-probability output
history the memory is init, done, or rho_S, determined by the last output
type, and before observation it is rho_*. All these states commute and
are fixed by the basis-copy broadcaster. For
 sigma=(1/4)|init><init|+(1/2)I_N/N+(1/4)|done><done|,
each of init, done, rho_S has relative entropy ln 4 from sigma; rho_*
is in their convex hull and has no larger relative entropy. Consequently
every prior on the full reachable family has chi<=ln 4 by the center
identity. The prior giving masses 1/4 to init and done and total mass
1/2 uniformly to rho_S averages to sigma and attains chi=ln 4. Thus the
ALL-PRIORS capacity is exactly ln 4, rather than only the stationary
prior's information. The family has b=0 without using supplied premise P.

Lemma B3a (balanced incidence has large Wyner common information).
Let S be uniform among balanced subsets, and i uniform in S. Then
 I(S;i)=ln 2,
 C_W(S;i):=inf_{W: S independent i | W} I(S,i;W)=ln N.
This statement is about Wyner's common information, not C(E_N).

Proof. The joint law is the constant 2/(MN) on all incident pairs and
zero elsewhere. For each conditional-product component w with positive
weight, let T be the support of its i distribution and t=|T|. Its S
distribution is supported only on balanced S containing T; otherwise
the positive product would give positive mass to a forbidden pair.
Thus 1<=t<=N/2 and
 H(S,i|w)=H(S|w)+H(i|w)
          <=ln binom(N-t,N/2-t)+ln t.
As the original joint law is constant on its support,
 D(P_{S,i|w}||P_{S,i})
 >=ln[MN/(2t binom(N-t,N/2-t))]
 >=ln N+(t-1)ln 2-ln t
 >=ln N.
The second inequality uses
 binom(N,N/2)/binom(N-t,N/2-t)
 =product_{j=0}^{t-1}(N-j)/(N/2-j)>=2^t,
and the last uses 2^{t-1}>=t for integral t>=1. Average in w to get
I(S,i;W)>=ln N. Choosing W=i attains it. The uniform marginal of i
and H(i|S)=ln(N/2) give I(S;i)=ln 2. QED.

Exact HMM entropy bound and attainment. Let A_1,A_2 be consecutive
outputs in an exact classical HMM and W its hidden state after A_1.
The Markov property makes A_1 and A_2 independent conditional on W.
There are exactly three disjoint allowed pairs of output TYPES:
 subset->index, index->bottom, bottom->subset.
Any positive conditional-product component can occupy just ONE such
pair: two distinct past types have disjoint sets of allowed future
types, so a product rectangle spanning them would create a forbidden
pair. The phase type is therefore a deterministic function of W and
has entropy ln 3. Conditioned on phase subset->index, the joint law is
exactly the balanced-incidence law of B3a; hence H(W|that phase)>=ln N.
The other two conditional entropies are nonnegative. Consequently
 H(W)>=ln 3+(ln N)/3.
This proof applies to every edge-emitting or equivalent state-emitting
finite HMM, including nonunifilar HMMs. It does not merely lower bound
the number of causal states. If the HMM is stationary, W has its
stationary hidden distribution. If it is not stationary, the same
bound applies to this intermediate hidden distribution.
The basis classical HMM has transitions
 T(S,i|init)=2/(MN) for i in S,
 T(i,done|i)=1, T(bottom,init|done)=1,
and stationary distribution (1/3,1/(3N),...,1/(3N),1/3).
Its entropy is exactly ln 3+(ln N)/3, attaining the lower bound.
Equivalently the Wyner common information of the full two-output law
equals ln 3+(ln N)/3. Its mutual information equals ln 3+(ln 2)/3.
The same value is the past-future predictive mutual information: phase
is determined on both sides, and only the subset->index phase carries
an additional dependence, of ln 2, across that boundary.

Robust two-output cardinality bound. Let P be this stationary two-output
law and let Q be any HMM two-output law with TV(P,Q)<=delta. For first
output S, P(S)=1/(3M), and p_S, its conditional next-output distribution,
is uniform on the index labels in S. An HMM's conditional next-output
distribution q_S is a mixture of the SAME K hidden-state output
distributions, using its posterior hidden distribution after S. If
Q(S)=0 choose an arbitrary posterior; the inequality below still holds.
For any finite joint laws the conditional-distance inequality gives
 sum_S P(S)TV(p_S,q_S)
 <=TV(P,Q)+TV(P_first,Q_first)<=2delta.
Indeed P(S)(p_S-q_S)=(P(S)p_S-Q(S)q_S)+(Q(S)-P(S))q_S;
sum half l_1 norms, including the zero-Q(S) case. Here summing over just
the subset output labels is no larger than summing all first outputs.
The uniform average over S is thus <=6delta. At delta<=1/192 it is
<=1/32, so at least half of all S have TV(p_S,q_S)<=1/16. Apply the
greedy code of B2 to those good subsets. Its size is at least
 2^{N(1-h_2(1/4))}/[2(N+1)].
Their target distributions have separation >=1/4; decoder contraction
therefore makes their hidden posteriors separated by >=1/8 in TV.
The same l_1 packing gives code size <=9^K and hence K>=L_N. This is
a lower bound for any K-component conditional-product factorization
of Q, so in particular for every K-hidden-state HMM. QED.

Robust entropy bound by truncation. Let pi be the HMM's intermediate
hidden distribution and H=H(pi). For eta=1/384, retain those w with
-ln pi(w)<=H/eta. By Markov's inequality their complement has mass
<=eta, and at most exp(H/eta) states are retained. The intermediate
hidden variable gives a product mixture
 Q(A_1,A_2)=sum_w pi(w)u_w(A_1)v_w(A_2).
Keep the retained components. Merge the tail into ONE component with
its original weighted-average u_w and any fixed future distribution v_0.
The resulting Q' has TV(Q,Q')<=eta and at most exp(H/eta)+1 components.
If TV(P,Q)<=1/384, then TV(P,Q')<=1/192, so the preceding packing
bound implies L_N<=exp(H/eta)+1<=2exp(H/eta). Rearrangement proves
H>=(1/384)ln(L_N/2). The truncation needs only the one-boundary product
factorization, not preservation of an entire HMM under that truncation.
If H is infinite the lower bound is trivial; all finite HMMs have finite
H. This charges Shannon entropy, not just log-cardinality. QED.

DECISION ON FOUNDATIONAL UPGRADE

A removes the per-decision choice obstruction and produces an ordinary
classical sufficient experiment up to statewise error, with exact matrix
preparation identities. B1 removes the per-history choice obstruction and
produces one fixed transition kernel. These are concrete consequences of
the supplied common-map theorem, not independent resolutions of the
classical randomization problem, contextuality characterization, or
teacher-free predictive-memory acquisition. B2/B3 kill the stronger
capacity-only alphabet and memory-entropy upgrade, even at exact copying
and stationarity. The relevant latent-resource invariant is conditional-
product/common-information complexity, which capacity does not control.
Existing primary literature already distinguishes these invariants.
No named major open problem is claimed resolved. New statistical physics
objectivity, approximate commutative algebras, purification preservation,
quantum reference extensions, independent copies, and efficient learning
would need additional hypotheses and are not inferred here.
