# Cycle 8: symplectic defining-representation mixed-dual theorem

## Result

For every compact symplectic defining representation \(V=\mathbb C^d\), \(d=2n\geq4\), every \(G=\mathrm{USp}(2n)\)-covariant, unital, trace-preserving, Hilbert--Schmidt-self-adjoint channel with a two-output CPTP extension admits the one fixed canonical EB comparator

\[
\Psi_d(X)=\frac{X+\operatorname{Tr}(X)I}{d+1},
\qquad
\operatorname{id}-\Psi_d\preceq_{\rm HS}2(\operatorname{id}-\Phi).
\]

The proof establishes the exact support identity for every positive invariant dual with arbitrary nonnegative weights on the adjoint and primitive-two-form sectors. It explicitly includes the full output-antisymmetric block. For general positive duals, group twirling is justified by covariance. The maximizing star vector can itself be twirled into a valid symmetric broadcaster Choi state with every one-party marginal \(I/d\).

The detailed derivation is in [USp_fundamental_all_mixed_Q.md](USp_fundamental_all_mixed_Q.md). The essential calculation reduces the three-copy \(V\)-isotypic sector to three symplectic contractions. The output-symmetric eigenvalue \(h_+\) dominates the antisymmetric \(2\times2\) contraction block by exact trace and determinant, and dominates the remaining space by \(\|F_{01}+F_{02}\|\leq2\).

## Prior-art and novelty boundary

At \(d=4\), the exact symmetric broadcaster is the standard \(U(4)\) universal \(1\to2\) cloner, restricted to Spin(5), and the comparator is standard pure-state estimation. The cloner reaches \(3/5\) on both irreducible sectors and is extreme even in the unrestricted CPTP set; the orbit POVM yields \((X+\operatorname{Tr}(X)I)/5\). These constructions are recorded with exact derivations and primary-source links in [SOURCE_AUDIT.md](SOURCE_AUDIT.md). Prudhoe's symplectic channel work is adjacent one-channel CP/PPT/EB structure, not a two-output compatibility theorem. The bounded audit found no source stating this all-mixed support inequality, but the search is not exhaustive. No novelty or priority claim is made.
