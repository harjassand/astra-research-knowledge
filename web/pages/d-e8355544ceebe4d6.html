C02_L07 REVISION 02 — EXACT FOUR-SITE EVEN-WINDABILITY RECOGNIZER
7 October 2026. Exact finite theorem and construction; internal status.

RECOGNIZER
Let f be a nonnegative rational function on subsets of V={0,1,2,3}, supported on even sets. Let the three pairings be M_1=(01|23), M_2=(02|13), M_3=(03|12). Define

  A = f(empty) f(V),
  h_i = f(P_i)f(V\P_i),
  B = (h_1+h_2+h_3-A)/2,
  L_i = max(0,h_i-B).

Then f is even-windable on four sites iff

  B >= 0   and   L_1+L_2+L_3 <= A.

Proof. For the ordered pair (empty,V), a witness has one orbit mass a_i for each matching M_i; EW1 gives sum_i a_i=A. For the ordered pair (P_i,V\P_i), matching M_i lies in its aligned pair-flip orbit, while the other two matchings lie in their crossed orbits. Let b_i be the mass for the crossed orbit of M_i. EW1 gives h_i=a_i+sum_{j!=i}b_j. Summing gives sum_i b_i=B. Solving yields b_i=a_i+B-h_i. Thus nonnegativity is exactly a_i in [max(0,h_i-B),h_i], with sum_i a_i=A. Such a choice exists iff B>=0 and sum_i L_i<=A<=sum_i h_i; the upper bound is B>=0. Conversely, choose any a_i in these intervals with sum A and put b_i=a_i+B-h_i. These masses satisfy all empty/all and complementary-pair equations. Assign them to the two flip orbits for each M_i; differences of size zero or two have a unique matching and receive the full product mass. Odd-difference products vanish. This constructs a witness for every assignment pair and makes it invariant under flipping either matched pair. Hence the criterion is necessary and sufficient.

The usual four-hole defect condition Delta=sum_i h_i-A>=0 is only B>=0 and is not sufficient. The extra lower-bound test is substantive. A simple abstract even signature has f(empty)=f(V)=1, f(01)=3, f(23)=1, and all other pair coefficients zero. Then h=(3,0,0), A=1, B=1, and sum L=2>A, so it is not even-windable despite Delta=2. This synthetic function is only a recognizer boundary test; no determinant realization is claimed.

CROSS-COORDINATE FIXTURE
Applying the criterion to the seven-edge K_{4,2} extension from REVISION_01 gives

  A=1897/64,
  h=(98515,467625,433225)/32768,
  B=28101/65536,
  L=(168929,907149,838349)/65536,
  sum L=1914427/65536 < A=1942528/65536.

A concrete feasible choice is

  a=(98515/32768, 907149/65536, 838349/65536),
  b=(28101/65536, 0, 0).

For each i, h_i=a_i+sum_{j!=i}b_j; and sum_i a_i=A. The orbit assignment described in the proof is therefore an explicit even-windability witness. The script checks all ordered pairs and flips exactly. The top-left 4x4 block of F6 is the original F4 matrix, but nonzero cross-block entries change the pinned marginal already before auxiliaries; the construction is a coupled completion, not a signature-preserving embedding.

SCIENTIFIC CONSEQUENCE / LIMITS
The local auxiliary invariant is an exact no-go only when the base four-site signature is the object being transformed on those same four coordinates. Coupled external coordinates contribute values f(S union {i,a}) and f(S union {i,j,a,b}) to the pinned marginal; these terms can change Delta and even restore an exact four-site witness. This clarifies the limitation of the earlier obstruction, and supplies one concrete route worth testing in a larger construction.

The result does not show that the full six-site signature is even-windable. Even-windability of this pinned four-site slice does not by itself construct a polynomial-size matching circuit or a sampler. The F6 completion is hand-selected, no uniform extension rule for arbitrary input F is supplied, and no coefficient-acquisition, microscopic two-replica energy, congestion, FPRAS, external correctness, or priority claim is made. The next gate is to determine whether a conservative, efficiently constructed extension can preserve the needed old marginal and control all unpinned states.
