## Decisive calculation

After a proof-only real diagonal change, set y_i^2=1/a_i. Put A=sum_i1/a_i, d0=4(3+bA)>0, rho=12(1+bA)>0. Then

    H=d0 I+8b yy^T,
    H^-1=d0^-1I-[8b/(d0rho)]yy^T.

Direct logarithmic determinant differentiation yields V=c diag(a)y+ey with c=24/d0^2>0 and a common e. Polarization/unit identities then give

    L_V=gamma diag(a)+delta I+E,
    gamma=288/d0^3>0,
    image(E) subset span{y,diag(a)y},
    L_V y=V.

The initial-vector Krylov columns are triangular in w_k=diag(a)^k y, with leading coefficients c gamma^(k-1). Their exact determinant is

    c^(n-1) gamma^[(n-1)(n-2)/2]
      (product_i y_i) product_(i<j)(a_j-a_i) !=0.

This nonzero leading tau coefficient proves the pencil claim. Unknown normalized blocks compose by the already independently gated invertibility/exact block-scaling/minimal-polynomial CRT argument. None of these real witnesses, scales or spectra enters computation.
