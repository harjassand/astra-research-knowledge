# All-endotactic permanence: source and priority audit

**Cutoff:** 2026-10-08. **Purpose:** compare the N33 candidate with primary literature, including post-2014 forward-citation work. This is a bounded literature audit, not a proof review, exhaustive search, or novelty certificate.

## Claim compared

N33 concerns a finite actual exponent-arrow diagram \(y_e\to y'_e\) in a fixed finite dimension \(d\), with real source exponents (including negative entries), reaction vectors \(\nu_e=y'_e-y_e\), and the usual all-direction endotactic condition. Each edge coefficient is an arbitrary measurable function with one common fixed box \(0<\kappa\le k_e(t)\le K<\infty\); solutions are Carathéodory (absolutely continuous, satisfying the equation a.e.). For every positive stoichiometric class \(P=(c+S)\cap\mathbb R^d_{>0}\), the claimed conclusion is one compact convex \(K_P\subset P\), independent of initial state and rate path, that is forward invariant and is entered in finite time by every \(x_0\in P\) under every admissible rate path. The entry time may depend on \(x_0\); no uniform entry time over an unbounded class or uniformity over different classes is claimed.

### Rate and trajectory convention check

The target’s arbitrary measurable rates and Carathéodory trajectories should not be silently attributed to every source. CNP and Pantea say piecewise differentiable rates. GMS gives compact per-edge interval fibers but defines trajectories as differentiable curves with pointwise tangent inclusion. Boros–Hofbauer says bounded kinetics without an explicit measurable/AC clause. Craciun–Deshpande and Craciun 2026 describe variable coefficients as bounded, but the passages checked do not specify an a.e. solution convention. A standard differential-inclusion extension to absolutely continuous solutions may be straightforward, but that is a proof bridge, not the verbatim theorem statement.

## Priority boundary and next decisive observation

No preempting all-dimensional theorem was located that combines: arbitrary endotacticity (not strong endotacticity), arbitrary finite dimension, a common positive compact per-edge rate box, arbitrary real/negative exponents, and permanence for all trajectories without assuming their boundedness. The broad claim is explicitly named the **Extended Permanence Conjecture** by CNP; Craciun–Deshpande (2020) and Ding–Deshpande–Craciun (2023) still state it as a conjecture while recording the dimension-two, rank-two, and strongly endotactic cases; the latest found Craciun source (2026-09-23) adds persistence only for already bounded endotactic trajectories. Thus the candidate, if independently verified and if its solution convention is justified, appears to target the missing boundedness/permanence step in that conjecture.

This is provisional priority evidence, not blanket novelty clearance. The strongest next priority observation is an author/expert check of the exact statement against post-2026-10-08 unpublished/preprint/proceedings work, asking specifically whether anyone has proved **boundedness of every trajectory** for non-strongly-endotactic networks of rank/dimension above two under independent compact-box time-varying rates. That is the load-bearing gap not closed by the conditional persistence result. Separately, settle whether the literature’s interval-valued differential-inclusion theorems are accepted in the absolutely-continuous/a.e. sense needed for N33’s measurable controls, and whether their permanence constants are uniform over all selectors.
