The outcome-rank bound in ACQUISITION_AND_COST.txt admits a stronger direct
classical-channel proof and a sharp leading expected-bit result. These are
cost boundaries for an already classical family, not counterexamples to EB
reconstructibility. Standard elementary Fano reasoning is fully derived here;
no measurement-to-trace upgrade or entropy-label shortcut is imported.

For even d, 0<theta<1, take the finite balanced-sign family
 E={rho_h=diag(1+theta h_i)/d: h_i=+/-1, sum_i h_i=0}.
Its Holevo capacity is EXACTLY
 c(theta)=((1+theta)ln(1+theta)+(1-theta)ln(1-theta))/2.
Every prior has chi<=c(theta) by the relative-entropy decomposition in the
previous revision; the uniform balanced-sign prior has mean I/d and attains
c(theta). All states commute, share the fixed sandwich, and are exactly fixed
by the positive selfadjoint EB dephaser with an actual exact broadcaster.

Let ANY EB reconstruction have an m-outcome factorization
 Lambda(rho)=sum_y Tr(M_y rho) rho_y,
with uniform half-trace error <=epsilon on E. Reference fixation by Lambda
is not assumed. Pinch outputs in the target basis; this cannot increase error
on E. Define column-stochastic classical matrices
 Q[y,i]=<i|M_y|i>, S[j,y]=<j|rho_y|j>, R=S Q.
Under the reference sigma=I/d the outcome law is p_y=d^-1 sum_i Q[y,i].
This is also the outcome law averaged under the uniform prior on E.

Write rho_+ and rho_- for the pair indexed by h,-h. Their two error vectors
have L1 norms <=2epsilon. Their difference equals
  (2theta/d)(Rh-h).
Therefore for EVERY balanced sign h,
  ||Rh-h||_1/d <=2epsilon/theta=:eta.                   (1)
This cancellation removes any output bias at the reference exactly.

Since h_i=+/-1,
  1-h^T R h/d <=||h-Rh||_1/d <=eta.                    (2)
Under the uniform balanced-sign prior E[h_i h_j]=-1/(d-1) for i!=j and
E[h_i^2]=1. Using sum_ij R_ij=d gives
  E[h^T R h/d]=(Tr R-1)/(d-1).
Thus
  Tr R >= d-eta(d-1).                                 (3)
For an m-outcome factorization,
  Tr R=sum_y sum_i S[i,y]Q[y,i] <=sum_y 1=m.
Consequently the stronger alphabet bound is
  m >= d-(2epsilon/theta)(d-1).                        (4)
This improves the preceding kernel-polytope bound slightly. No dimension
bound on the preparation span, POVM rank or nonzero-outcome weight is needed.

EXPECTED BITS, FOR THE ACTUAL MEASUREMENT RECORD
Introduce a classical latent input index I uniform on {1,...,d}, pass it
through Q to obtain Y, then through S to obtain J. Equation (3) says
 Pr[J!=I]=1-Tr R/d <=eta(d-1)/d=:a_d.
The optimal estimator of I from Y makes error no larger than this stochastic
postprocessing: conditional on Y, choosing the largest posterior probability
is at least as successful as averaging that posterior against S[.,Y].
Let that estimator's error indicator be Z. Then
 H(I|Y)<=H(Z|Y)+H(I|Z,Y)
        <=h2(a_d)+a_d log2(d-1).                       (5)
For 0<=eta<1, a_d<=(d-1)/d. The RHS h2(a)+a log2(d-1) is increasing up to
(d-1)/d, so its use of the upper bound a_d is valid even when eta>1/2.
By data processing and H(I)=log2 d,
 H(Y)>=I(I;Y)
      >=log2 d-h2(a_d)-a_d log2(d-1).                  (6)

This is ordinary stored-record cost under the uniform label prior. It is not

EXPLICIT MATCHING CONSTRUCTION
Let q be even, 0<=q<d. Partition the basis into one group G of size q and
d-q singleton groups. Measure the group index, and prepare the uniform state
within the measured group. For q=0 use d singleton groups. This is an ordinary
EB channel fixing sigma. Singleton coordinates are retained exactly.
For balanced h put z=q^-1 sum_(i in G) h_i. Its full-trace error is
  (theta/d) sum_(i in G)|h_i-z|=(theta q/d)(1-z^2)
  <=theta q/d.
The half-trace error is therefore <=theta q/(2d), and equality is attained
by a balanced sign assignment within G (and within its even complement).
Hence epsilon_q=theta q/(2d) EXACTLY. The measurement has m=d-q+1 outcomes
and reference probabilities 1/d for the d-q singletons, and q/d for G.
Its outcome entropy is
 H(Y)=(1-q/d)log2 d-(q/d)log2(q/d).                     (7)
For q/d->eta in [0,1), this is (1-eta)log2 d+O(1). Together with (6), the
leading expected-bit coefficient 1-2epsilon/theta is sharp for this sequence
of explicit finite experiments and a single full-domain EB decoder.
