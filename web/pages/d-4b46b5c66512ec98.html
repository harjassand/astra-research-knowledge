# W03 — dependency and correction graph audit

## Finding

The snapshot has useful graph coverage, but its current graph is a retrieval and navigation graph rather than a sound proof-composition graph. It contains 1,639 edges: 535 `supported_by`, 868 `links_to_not_logical_dependency`, and 236 edges spread over dozens of other relation strings. The curated relations show careful mathematical scope work, yet their semantics live in per-edge labels and prose; a generic traversal cannot know which edges compose, in what direction, or under what assumptions. Treating all adjacency as evidence would create false transfers.

The index helps preserve claim status: 186 claim records carry a status, and 58 external prerequisites have scopes. Of 186 claims, 114 have structured `scoped_dependencies`; only three use nonempty `depends_on`. This is a promising foundation, but the dependency relation currently has only `id` and `relation` at claim level, while `edges.jsonl` uses `from/to/type/source` and does not carry assumptions, scope, evidence locator, direction semantics, or validation status. 159 evidence edges to `d-*` identifiers look “unknown” if one checks only claims and external deps, because evidence documents are a third node class. The graph needs a unified node registry, not a rule that every endpoint must be a claim.

## What the current graph does well

- The dominant `links_to_not_logical_dependency` label explicitly blocks accidental proof inference for many corpus links.
- Curated edge labels distinguish proof prerequisites from conceptual relationship: e.g. `N02-correlated-sphere -> external:OA271_spin_concentration` is `requires_for_physical_application_only`, while `N03-bounded-score -> N01-qudit-gibbs` is `distinct_scope`.
- Refinement and failure preservation are visible in labels. `N24-acquired-rational-rounding -> N14-small-mass-rounding` says it refines acquisition with a weaker log factor, and `N99-X-checkpoint-ledger -> N98-positive-kernel-transfer-limits` preserves failed transfers.
- External prerequisites can carry sharply delimited roles. `external:OA277` is a prerequisite for the stated extension in L02, while its record explicitly says not to identify arbitrary commuting algebras with finite tensor strategies. L02 itself keeps the question-chain gap as a separately supplied parameter and says efficient sampling does not certify that gap.

These are valuable distinctions to promote into typed fields rather than encode in an expanding vocabulary of edge strings.

## Failure modes and examples

1. **Free-form edge types are not safe traversal rules.** The index has 868 explicitly nonlogical links, but also labels such as `requires`, `conditional_dependency`, `related_prior_interface`, `refines_*`, `distinct_scope`, and `does_not_require`. The edge tuple does not say whether `from` depends on `to`, whether `to` supports only a corollary, or whether the label is merely a note. Many relation names occur once. A shortest path through arbitrary edges is not a derivation.

2. **A refinement can narrow scope or worsen a bound.** N24 is not a strict improvement of N14: it adds an executable rational rounder under a stated interface, while the sharper mass-log bound is conditional on a certified signing engine. The graph label hints at this; a bare `refines` edge would wrongly allow bound substitution.

3. **Shared terminology/interfaces do not imply theorem transfer.** N30 addresses changing-radius Gaussian experiments and expressly says it does not establish the quantum comparison in N08; N31 is scoped to weak ellipsoids with growing dimension. N98 states that integer-q moment control does not transfer to S1 entropy and that its arbitrary-phase barrier is a different class from the positive-word result. The labels `addresses_*_only_not_*`, `integer_q_scope_no_S1_transfer`, and `distinct_*_not_contradiction` are exactly the safeguards that need machine-readable fields.

4. **Contradiction and supersession are distinct.** N53 is marked as superseding a dimension-blind transfer with a collective boundary; N83 explicitly says a positive subclass is not contradicted; N98 says its arbitrary-phase interface is not a contradiction of N81's positive-word scope. A correction can invalidate a broad inference while leaving a restricted theorem intact. A single “contradicts” or “supersedes” relation is insufficient without target scope and surviving scope.

5. **Provenance/status is easy to overread.** L01 and L02 are `source_derived_unreviewed`. L01's evidence includes a source report, notes, and a reconstruction script; L02's OA277 prerequisite is external while the extension proof remains an internally reconstructed candidate. N99 explicitly says diagnostics and PDF compilation are source-reported, not replayed. Edges to source documents must not promote an imported or source-reported result into a reviewed theorem.

6. **Historical and current scope can coexist.** Claims include curated updates and verbatim prior records. The top-level card status alone cannot represent a proposition that has been corrected, partially retained, or superseded by a later scoped claim. Keep versions and correction target spans explicit; do not overwrite old nodes or silently inherit their status.

## Minimal typed graph proposal

Retain current files as a source layer; materialize a normalized graph with three node kinds: `claim`, `evidence`, and `dependency` (the last includes external theorems, interfaces, supplied services/parameters). Every claim node should have an immutable `claim_version`, exact `statement`, `assumptions`/`interface`, `conclusion`, `scope_tags`, `evidence_state`, and `provenance`. Evidence nodes need locator plus evidence role (`proof_text`, `source_report`, `code`, `finite_check`, `external_publication`) and review/replay status. Dependency nodes need exact scope and whether supplied, imported, acquired, or unresolved.

An edge should minimally contain:

```json
{
  "from": "claim-version-id",
  "to": "claim-version-id-or-dependency-id-or-evidence-id",
  "relation": "requires | supports | refines | contradicts | supersedes | conceptual_link | sourced_by",
  "direction": "from_requires_to | to_supports_from | ...",
  "scope": {"applies_to": "named conclusion/corollary", "assumption_delta": []},
  "evidence": [{"node": "evidence-id", "locator": "section/lines/page"}],
  "status": "candidate | source_reported | reconstructed | reviewed | formal_checked | externally_verified",
  "composition": "allowed | conditional | forbidden",
  "reason": "short explanation"
}
```

The vocabulary must be closed and relation-specific. `requires` is a directed proof obligation, not similarity. `supports` must identify the precise proposition/span and support level. `conceptual_link` is never composable. `refines` records whether it strengthens, weakens, narrows, widens, or changes interface, and gives the exact old/new scope. `contradicts` points to mutually incompatible proposition spans under a common scope. `supersedes` records the invalidated inference and the retained restricted statement. `sourced_by` is provenance only and never proof support by itself.

A path counts as proof evidence only if every edge is composition-allowed, every prerequisite is discharged at adequate status, and the source conclusion's assumptions entail the next node's assumptions with compatible interfaces and costs. Otherwise return a typed explanation of the blocker, not a positive “connection.”

## False-transfer prevention

Use exact scope matching before path composition: object class, parameter regime, oracle/information access, quantifiers, conclusion type, and charged costs must be explicit. Require a human-readable bridge obligation when any field changes. In particular:

- Mark every non-proof relation as `composition=forbidden`; do not infer from shared topic, source, or terminology.
- Treat conditional and application-only dependencies as gates for only the named consequence.
- Keep “source supports claim” separate from “claim is externally verified.”
- Preserve failed transfer edges and counterexamples as first-class nodes, with the exact interface they refute.
- Version corrections; supersede a claim only on overlapping scope and retain unaffected subclaims.
- Refuse transfer when costs, supplied services, adaptive access, dimension dependence, or probability quantifiers are missing or differ.

## Next decisive lemma representation

Represent a proposed connection as a *lemma obligation* node before trying to link result cards. It should include: (i) target claim and exact conclusion; (ii) source result and exact cited subclaim; (iii) source assumptions and target assumptions as structured fields; (iv) the missing bridge statement, with quantifiers and constants; (v) which source hypotheses discharge which target hypotheses; (vi) interface/cost/probability deltas; (vii) known counterexamples or failed transfers; (viii) evidence required to close it and current status. For L02, a useful next obligation is not “find a verifier application,” but: *for a named verifier and input length, prove a lower bound on the minimum resampling gap σ under the actual question-generation law, state its dependence on input length, and charge sampling/execution costs; then substitute that bound into the L02 threshold theorem.* That representation cleanly separates the candidate repetition lemma from the missing application premise, and makes the next decisive observation auditable.
