ABSTRACT MULTIPLICITY-SAFE GIBBS MEMORY TRANSFER
Root derivation, 2026-10-08. CONDITIONAL THEOREM / internally derived candidate.
This consolidates the regular-SU3 mechanism; it is not a new verified group
application, a novelty claim, or a solution of the representation hypotheses.

CONTRACT
Let G be compact with real irreducible adjoint representation of dimension q,
and U_N an irreducible finite-dimensional unitary carrier of dimension d.
Let Hermitian generators T_a obey sum_a T_a^2=C_N I. Put B_a=T_a/N.
Fix a nonzero real vector x, r=|x|, and B_x=sum x_a B_a. Assume
||B_x||<=B0 uniformly in N. The supplied family is the whole orbit of
exp(h B_x)/Tr exp(h B_x), h in [h0,h1], 0<h0<=h1<infinity.
The legal code has arbitrary classical labels and maximum quantum branch Q;
its output is the same carrier, and error is full-state half trace distance.
There is no shared entanglement or uncharged quantum side register. All retained
input-dependent quantum information is in the declared branch memory.
Define E_T(F)=sum_a ||[T_a,F]||_HS^2 and Lambda(Q)=min_rank F<=Q,||F||HS=1 E_T(F).

FINITE ADJOINT-FRAME HYPOTHESES
For every N there are p Hermitian covariant tensor families Y_(nu,a),
nu=1,...,p, a=1,...,q, whose span contains the ENTIRE adjoint isotypic
component in End(V_N). The integer p is fixed, and
 (1/d)Tr(Y_(mu,a)Y_(nu,b))=G_(mu,nu) delta_ab,
where the real positive Gram G obeys lambda_min(G)>=g0>0, Tr G<=g1.
For all matrices F suppose
 E_(Y_nu)(F):=sum_a||[Y_(nu,a),F]||HS^2 <= C_nu E_T(F)/N²,
with sum_nu C_nu<=C0 uniformly. All hypotheses are finite exact statements
or uniform inequalities; do not replace the Gram bound by covariance alone.
Their verification near a weight wall can fail and remains a separate gate.

CONCLUSION
There are constants independent of N,Q such that
 c Lambda(Q)/N² <= e*_Q <= C Lambda(Q)/N².
The same upper code works for the full supplied h interval. One explicit pair is
 c=(h0² r²/(2q)) exp(-6h1 B0),
 C=(1/4)exp(2h1 B0)[h1 rho+(r²/q)h1²],
 rho=r sqrt(g1 C0)/(q g0).
The all-Q upper is existential unless a minimizing positive filter and its
finite orbit quadrature are actually acquired. It supplies no efficient
optimizer, circuit, total-memory optimum or transport across different carriers.

PROOF OF THE MULTIPLICITY STEP
For positive F set S=Tr F² and
 D_F(X)=int_G [F_g,[F_g,X]] dg, F_g=U_g F U_g*.
This is covariant, self-adjoint and Hermiticity preserving. The complete
adjoint frame gives D_F(B_a)=sum_nu c_nu Y_(nu,a) with real coefficients c.
Taking the trace inner product against all q components of Y_mu yields
 q d sum_nu G_(mu,nu)c_nu=v_mu,
 v_mu=sum_a Tr([Y_(mu,a),F]*[B_a,F]).
The group integral disappears by simultaneous orthogonal adjoint rotation;
this identity does not identify an individual posterior with B_a.
By Cauchy-Schwarz,
 |v_mu|<=sqrt(C_mu) E_T(F)/N².
Thus ||c||_2 <= sqrt(C0) E_T(F)/(q d g0 N²).
The normalized HS Gram also gives
 ||Y_(nu,x)||_1 <= sqrt(d)||Y_(nu,x)||HS = d r sqrt(G_(nu,nu)).
Cauchy-Schwarz over nu proves the load-bearing LINEAR energy bound
 ||D_F(B_x)||_1 <= rho E_T(F)/N².
No operator-norm bound on each higher tensor is needed for this last step.

FULL DENSITY UPPER
Scalar Casimir and the SVD identity
 E_T(A)-[E_T(|A|)+E_T(|A*|)]/2
 =sum_(a,i,j) sigma_i sigma_j |(U*T_aU)_(ij)-(V*T_aV)_(ij)|² >=0
show some positive rank-Q filter attains Lambda. For any such F,
 Phi_F(X)=(d/S)int F_g X F_g dg is CPTP and factors through rank(F), and
 Phi_F-I=-(d/(2S))D_F.
Set C_B=(B_x+B0 I)/(2B0), ell=2hB0. Then 0<=C_B<=I and
 int||[F_g,C_B]||HS² dg = [r²/(4qB0²)] E_T(F)/N²,
 ||D_F(C_B)||_1 <= [rho/(2B0)] E_T(F)/N².
The noncommutative derivation rule applied twice to C_B^k gives
 ||D_F(C_B^k)||_1 <= [k rho/(2B0)+k(k-1)r²/(4qB0²)] E_T(F)/N².
Each cross term uses Schatten Holder and ||C_B||<=1; ordered factors
cannot be commuted. Summing the exponential series and dividing by
Tr exp(ell C_B)>=d proves the stated upper constant, including the 1/2
trace-distance factor.

ARBITRARY-CODE LOWER
Each encoder/decoder branch gives a composite Kraus A_i of rank<=Q,
with sum_i A_i*A_i=I and sum_i||A_i||HS²=d. Let W_g=d tau_g.
Irreducibility makes int W_g² dg scalar, so even for NONUNITAL codes,
 Tr W_e²-int Tr(W_g Phi(W_g))dg=(1/2)sum_i int||[W_g,A_i]||HS² dg.
The witness effect W_g/exp(2h1B0) and scalar Gibbs divided differences
bound the average half-trace risk below by
 [h0² exp(-6h1B0)/(2d)] sum_i int||[B_g,A_i]||HS² dg.
Adjoint orthogonality gives int||[B_g,A]||HS²=(r²/q)E_T(A)/N².
Use rank<=Q and weights ||A_i||HS²/d to obtain the lower constant.

RESOURCE AND PRIOR BOUNDARY
An exact finite Choi-orbit quadrature exists with <=d^4+1 positive nodes
by Caratheodory, but this is not an acquisition algorithm. It charges that
many labels and branch dimension Q if the nodes and filter are supplied.
Representation hypotheses are proved for the earlier symmetric carrier
packet and regular-SU3 packet by their own arguments, not by this abstract
statement. Higher SU(m), general simple groups, wall crossover, growing h,
implementation precision, and optimal total classical/quantum memory remain
open until separately proved. The self-score and multiplicity-two route are
internal campaign inputs. No external novelty search for this exact abstract
statement has yet been completed.
