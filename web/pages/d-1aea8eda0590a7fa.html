SAME-ZERO-BOUNDARY ANCHORED-TILT REFLECTED COMPARISON: FROZEN v1
Separate immutable addendum to the interior comparison proof v1.

0. EXACT MODEL AND STATUS

Let V_n be the interior free vertices of a square rectangle or a finite
simply connected polygon made of square-lattice cells, with its boundary
and exterior fixed to ZERO. Assume V_n lies in a box of diameter C_D n.
Its zero set E_n=Z^2 minus V_n is connected to infinity, and the chosen
cell triangulation has every triangle outside the polygon entirely in
E_n. Rectangles satisfy these hypotheses directly. No isolated internal
observed component is included. More general domains require these exact
deterministic hypotheses, not just the word 'smooth'.

Let P0_n be the uniform hard-Lipschitz law. Let beta_i be deterministic
coordinate profiles supported in V_n with sup|beta_i|<=A n^-2, and let
  P_i(dh)=exp(<beta_i,h>)/Z_i P0_n(dh), i=1,2.
The zero-field/one-tilt case is beta_1=0. Anchored macro premise S2 from
the supplied spin1 packet applies to all the spread tests below: choose
a connected zero-pin cycle on an exterior bounding square of side O(n).
Its diameter is at least n and it is within O(n) of the profiles. Thus
no unanchored nonzero-mass estimate is required.

Choose C0=16(C_D+1)^2, increasing only the fixed coordinate convention
constant if necessary, for all these supports/weights and the exterior
anchor cycle. Take the
corresponding supplied anchored exponential constants a,M:
  E0 exp(a|<q,h>|)<=M
when sum|q|<=C0, max|q|<=C0 n^-2 and the connected pin anchor is as above.
Suppose 0<=2A<=a.
Since E0<beta_i,h>=0 by evenness, Z_i>=1 and
  ||dP_i/dP0||_2<=sqrt(M).                                (0.1)
The profile beta_i/A lies in the fixed spread class. Constants can depend
on the fixed domain bounding ratio, a,M,A and testing parameters, not n.

Construct ANY stationary synchronous normal-reflected joining on the
SAME finite free set V_n, using variance-two Brownian motion and drift
beta_i. It has the tilted invariant marginals: the finite weighted
Neumann form is integral grad f dot grad g exp(<beta_i,h>), with generator
Delta+beta_i dot grad and the SAME normal reflection. Integration by
parts gives the stated drift and zero normal flux. The finite positive
smooth weight has the same H1 form domain as the uniform polytope; the
Bass--Hsu coordinate-martingale/normal-local-time construction applies
with that drift. Monotonicity gives pathwise uniqueness. Compact Feller
time averaging constructs the joining, with fresh common Brownian
innovations independent of its forward past.

We will prove FULL boundary-incident weighted coercivity, expected total
projection loss O(1), polynomial spatial/conditional moment bounds, and
a zero-exterior filling with expected squared W^(1,p)(R^2) norm O(1) and
normalized original/fill L1 error tending to zero. Interior volume-rooted
n-gradient/increment/step-displacement tightness also follows. The proof
uses published CAP and the inherited anchored macro premise; it does not
assume the triangular whole theorem or a boundary version of S1.

1. NEW ALL-BOX SPARSE TEST, INCLUDING ZERO-PIN COLLARS

Let C4>=1 be the CAP Theorem 1.1 constant at degree four. For any finite
set of distinct original square edges and 0<t<=1/8,
  P0(all k edges have |grad h|>1-t)<=(C4 t)^(k/C4).         (1.1)
The finite graph can be enlarged by fixed zero sites to a connected
bounding square; its law on the original free vertices is unchanged.
Edges with two fixed zero endpoints can never be witnesses. The free
degree remains four. Thus (1.1) is valid throughout pin collars.

Fix
  a0=min(1/8, 1/[C4 16^(2C4)]), delta0=a0/4,
  lambda=2(C4 a0)^(1/(2C4))<=1/8.
For any deterministic x,y with d=d1(x,y), choose a fixed shortest
Manhattan path of d edges, lying inside their coordinate rectangle.
If |h(x)-h(y)|>(1-delta0)d, then the sum of the ABSOLUTE edge gradients
on that path exceeds (1-delta0)d. At least ceil(d/2) path edges therefore
have |grad h|>1-a0: otherwise their deficit is at least a0 d/2,
contradicting delta0 d<=a0 d/4. Choosing that edge subset and applying
(1.1) gives
  P0(long-pair failure)<=2^d(C4 a0)^(d/(2C4))=lambda^d.    (1.2)
This argument has no free-patch requirement and no boundary contraction.

For a fixed integer enlargement m>=1, use patches
  P_z=L z+[-mL,(m+1)L]^2 intersect Z^2.
Their vertices/edges number at most C_m L^2 and their pairs at most
C_m L^4, with C_m=(2m+4)^4 a safe explicit choice. Choose k0>=4/delta0 and take L the least even integer satisfying
L>=8(k0+2)/delta0. Then L<=10(k0+2)/delta0, so the pair count is polynomial
in k0. For a separated collection of patches, all canonical witness
paths and local edges are disjoint. For k such long-failing patches,
sum the choices of endpoint pairs and near-edge subsets and use CAP on
the UNION of the disjoint selected edges. The resulting joint bound is
  [C_m L^4 lambda^k0]^k.                                 (1.3)
For k patches with a local slack-delta failure, the same calculation is
  [C_m L^2(C4 delta)^(1/C4)]^k.                           (1.4)
One must NOT multiply the two different-threshold bounds for mixed
types; CAP at a common maximum threshold would lose (1.4)'s small delta.

Given any target rho in (0,1), color the block indices into
J=(2m+4)^2 classes whose enlarged patches have disjoint vertices. Set
eta=(rho^J/2)^2. First choose k0 sufficiently large that
  C_m L(k0)^4 lambda^k0<=eta;
this exists since lambda<1 and L(k0) is at most linear in k0. Then choose
0<delta<=min(delta0,1/8) with
  C_m L^2(C4 delta)^(1/C4)<=eta.
For separated patches, assign each bad box its local or long witness.
One type occurs on at least k/2 boxes. Union over at most 2^k assignments
and use (1.3) or (1.4) on that one type to obtain
  P0(all k separated patches bad)<=[2 sqrt(eta)]^k
                                  =(rho^J)^k.
For an arbitrary finite collection choose its largest color class of
size>=k/J. Hence
  P0(A subset Bad)<=rho^|A|                              (1.5)
for EVERY finite A, including patches containing true zero pins.
Under either tilt (0.1), choose the zero-law target rho0=(rho/sqrt(M))^2;
Holder gives sqrt(M) rho0^(k/2)<=rho^k for k>=1. Fixed thickening is
handled by retuning before the fixed witness multiplicity is applied.
The parameter order k0,L,delta is explicit and contains no circular
choice of L after delta. Dynamic near-edge threshold s0 may subsequently
be decreased further after L, as in the frozen interior proof.

2. BOUNDARY FACET FORCING AND POSITIVE WEIGHTS

Consider a fixed enlarged testing patch, now allowed to contain true
zero pins. On its free coordinates y_i=h_i-h_i(0), all original edge
inequalities have residual cost in [delta,2-delta], including edges to
true pins. Contract true pins to a ground coordinate y_ground=0.
Artificial collar bounds |y_i|<=r0<s0/8 are added only on FREE outer
collar coordinates. Keep original edges to actual zero pins even when
the pin lies just outside the shell; cut only cross-shell free-free edges.

Every free component reaches the artificial collar or a true pin: follow
an original lattice path to the shell, stopping at its first true pin
if it leaves V_n. Relative coordinate zero is uniformly strict in all
free inequalities. The body therefore has a uniform interior ball and
bounded diameter, with at most a fixed N free coordinates. Its possible
free/pin patterns are finite.
For the compact parameter cover, normalize any local component with no
true pin by one of its collar heights. Its initial height differences
are bounded by its fixed site count; components with a true pin already
have an absolute local bound from that pin. Thus no ambient initial
height or ambient diameter enters the compactness argument. Long-pair goodness ensures both directed
original-edge residual costs from a tested central vertex to the free
outer collar exceed Lambda>2, by the chosen patch enlargement and L.

Merge identical free normals before deleting redundant inequalities.
Several zero pins adjacent to one free vertex give the SAME unit facet;
use its AGGREGATE normal impulse. Each original incident pin edge has the
same z jump and can be charged to that impulse with multiplicity at most
four. Do not assert a positive impulse on an arbitrarily named duplicate
edge. Then delete redundant inequalities and use shortest facet paths for each
tested original free-free or free-pin edge, whose cost is at most two.
A minimal path is simple since all costs are positive. If it passes
through ground, split it there. Each leg entering or leaving ground
must use a GENUINE pin constraint: a leg through an artificial collar
would first traverse an original-edge path of cost>2 to that collar,
contradicting the total budget. There cannot be a ground-to-ground
excursion, since it would be a positive cycle. Thus the nominated jump
telescopes through at most N+1 genuine free-free or free-ground facets.
The ground has actual difference z=0 in BOTH replicas.

The compact fixed-body forcing proof of interior v1 Section 4 applies to
these finitely many patterns. A selected genuine facet has a relative
interior point strict for other distinct normals and for artificial
collars. All true pins are fixed, and the source is constant beta_i.
Compensate the prescribed controlled driver by -beta_i t. The Brownian
tube probability stays positive uniformly since |beta_i|<=A and the
local dimension is fixed. No large-n drift approximation is needed for
this local probability. Graph projection monotonicity gives the same
parameter stability. Exterior quietness follows from the original
stationary short-step motion bound, then the conditional-failure lemma.
The cut process need not be stationary. The other replica need not keep
its microscopic collar quiet. This constructs an actual forced normal
contact with probability>=p0/2 on every retained good boundary box.

On a fixed-input-zero cluster, projection derivatives are orthogonal
averaging blocks OR zero blocks. Their convex mixtures remain symmetric
positive contractions with nonnegative entries and row/column sums<=1.
For signed z, add ZERO to the initial-level list. Jensen's variance and
column deficits give the same displacement bound C(H+1)sqrt(D_v) for
the source-free comparator; translation invariance is used only on
clusters with no pin input. This supplies the missing pinned analogue
of the conservative averaging argument, without arbitrary walls.

For the actual source, write each projection update z'=A(z+g dt),
g=beta_1-beta_2. Decompose z=v+d, v'=Av, d'=A(d+g dt), d0=0. Then
  sup|d|<=2AT n^-2.
For Q_A(u)=u^T(I-A^2)u, positivity gives
Q_A(v)<=2Q_A(z+gdt)+2Q_A(d+gdt). The energy balance for d yields
  sum Q_A(d+gdt)<=C H A^2 T^2 n^-4.
Consequently, with D_C the actual sum of nonnegative projection losses,
  sup_t max_C |z_t-z0|
      <=C(H+1)sqrt(D_C)+C A T(H+1)^(3/2)n^-2.             (2.1)
The forced-contact argument yields the previous squared-jump loss bound
with an additive C A^2 H^m n^-4 error for a fixed finite m. If the initial
jump is below the displacement error, this inequality is immediate;
otherwise either displacement or persistent positive contact gives the
old bound. Allocate d_B=sum_incident D_C/H_C as before. Conditional
Cauchy and the finite facet path give positive weights
  w_B=c/[1+E(H_B^m|I)]
and, after summing all tested edges in the bath,
  E E_n<=C E sum_C D_C+C A^2 n^-2.                       (2.2)
All clusters now have only common zero inputs or common free sites:
there are no uncontrolled noncommon boundary values and no missing
boundary collar weights. Edges between two zero pins contribute zero
energy and may be assigned weight one. This is the required boundary
coercivity input, not inferred from interior energy.

The CAP/tilt and stationary Lyons--Zheng arguments of interior v1 Section
2 apply throughout the finite bath, including free-pin edges. Four-type
witness counting gives arbitrarily sparse dynamic boxes; rooted animals
and factorial complete-component counting give all spatial moments.
Conditional truncation/Jensen supplies deterministic inverse-weight
moments outside any specified polynomial failure probability, with no
ambient-size factor or unspecified random multiplier.

3. EXACT GLOBAL BALANCE BYPASSES INTERIOR ABSORPTION

Common Brownian noise cancels in z, leaving the deterministic source g
plus finite-variation reflections. At an upper free edge contact in
replica 1, z_j-z_i=1-(h'_j-h'_i)>=0, so its inward normal dissipates
||z||^2. At the corresponding contact in replica 2 the oriented difference
is nonpositive and the sign in dz is reversed, also dissipating. A
free-pin contact has the same property because the two true pin heights
are IDENTICAL zero. Lower contacts are identical after reversing signs.
Corners are sums of these normal contributions. Thus pathwise
  ||z_T||_2^2-||z_0||_2^2
      =2 integral_0^T <z_t,g>dt-D_total, D_total>=0.       (3.1)
D_total is the normal-reflection loss, equivalently the projection-loss
limit in Section 2. This equivalence follows directly from the discrete
identity: the common Brownian increments cancel in z, the squared source
increments vanish with the mesh, the source Riemann sum converges to
the integral in (3.1), and the endpoint norms converge uniformly by the
Skorokhod approximation. The same argument on each fixed contact cluster
identifies its nonnegative local limit. Splitting contacts among their transmission clusters
gives D_total=sum_C D_C, including pinned clusters. It is not the signed
final norm deficit when g is nonzero.

At every fixed n the bath is finite and |h|<=C_D n, so stationarity
justifies cancellation of the expectations of the two norms in (3.1).
There is no need for a uniform point-height or uniform squared-norm bound
at this step. Let q=g/(2A) for A>0. It has spread bounds at scale n and
the fixed connected zero anchor. From (0.1) and
E0<q,h>^2<=2M/a^2, Holder gives
  E_i|<q,h>|<=sqrt(2) M/a.
Therefore
  E D_total=2T E<z0,g>
       <=8sqrt(2) T A M/a.                               (3.2)
For A=0 the balance gives E D_total=0. The signed drift average is the
controlled macro observable; replacing it by the sum of absolute
point heights would unnecessarily lose this bound.

Combining (2.2),(3.2) proves E full weighted energy<=C independently
of n, with boundary-incident free-pin edges included. No higher energy
moment, conditional macro MGF, or density transfer of an already proved
energy estimate is used. Instead the proof is rerun under the tilted
stationary joining and the density estimate transfers only macro
observable moments and probability events.

4. EXACT ZERO-ANCHOR FILLING, SQUARED SOBOLEV NORM, AND L1 ERROR

Thicken the sparse defective blocks by the fixed layer making every fine
rim edge a tested good edge. Retain outermost filled star-component
hulls, with good connected rims and polynomial-length simple rim paths.
For a hole H meeting E_n, follow a nearest-neighbor E_n path from its
zero vertex to infinity. H is finite. The first vertex outside H on that
path is a GOOD surrounding rim vertex still in E_n, and its z value is
zero. Use it as the anchor and fill all H by zero. For a hole avoiding
E_n use any fixed good rim anchor. Outside holes retain z. Hence every
original zero vertex stays zero, including the physical boundary.

The rim path gradient bound is independent of the anchor choice: every
entering edge jump telescopes along a simple good rim path of length
O((1+d)^2), with polynomial congestion and bounded rim overlap.
Consequently the SAME weighted Holder bound as interior v1 Section 6
gives ||grad U_n||_Lp^2<=C_p E_n outside deterministic geometry exceptions.
The affine fill is zero on every triangle outside the polygon by the
declared cell-triangulation hypothesis. It is thus zero outside that
polygon, not just 'near zero' at an internal observed set. Set the whole
fill to zero on exceptional initial geometry events if needed; this
preserves the exact zero exterior.

After scaling the support lies in a fixed bounding square. Poincare on
a slightly larger bounding square, where U_n vanishes on an outer open
strip, controls ||U_n||_Lp by ||grad U_n||_Lp independently of the shape
of the polygon. Equation (3.2) therefore yields
  sup_n E||U_n||_(W^(1,p)(R^2))^2<infinity, 1<p<2.         (4.1)
The initial-geometry diagonal construction of interior v1 gives ONE
extension sequence for all fixed p. Its deterministic zero exterior is
preserved by every test/zeroing decision.

For each defect refinement j, test the SAME joining with a new short
step and new weights. Global balance (3.2) and coercivity (2.2) give its
constant C_j independent of n. Hence the all-original-edge L1 and volume
tail estimates of interior v1 Section 7 apply, INCLUDING pin edges,
while outside-zero edges have zero jump. The defect refinement limit
gives E n^-2 sum_all |grad z0|->0. On fixed original holes of diameter<=M
telescope to the zero or nonzero selected anchor along full-lattice
paths with polynomial M-congestion. Larger holes use |U-z|<=C(1+d)
and higher spatial moments. Since |z|<=C_D n on the bath, exceptional
zeroing contributes o(1). Thus
  E n^-2 sum_(x in V_n)|U_n(x/n)-z0(x)|->0.               (4.2)
This fill preserves the common zero boundary exactly.

For independent uniform roots over a deterministic interior macrobox,
or over V_n when |V_n|>=c n^2, the n-gradient and fixed-increment tails
follow from the same refined weighted energy estimates. For one fixed
short T0, (2.1), the exponential H tail and (3.2) give
  P_vol(n sup_(t<=T0)|z_t-z0|>M, H<=K0)<=C(K0+1)^3/M^2,
apart from the source term C A T0(K0+1)^(3/2)/n tending to zero.
This proves the usual volume-rooted displacement tightness. Roots on
tiny random free sets remain excluded.

5. SCOPE OF THIS ADDENDUM

This closes the SAME-DOMAIN, COMMON-ZERO-BOUNDARY comparison with the
anchored small smooth exponential tilts used in the ordinary source
response experiment, subject to the inherited anchored macro premise
and the stated deterministic polygon/zero-exterior hypotheses. It does
not assert arbitrary nonzero pin vectors, unanchored nonzero-mass tilts
in an arbitrarily large bath, arbitrary adaptive sign-wall transcripts,
or zero-preserving fills at isolated internal observed components.
Those harder interfaces are unnecessary premises for the present claim.
No response/covariance identification, convergence of domains, positive
stiffness, tangent theorem, or square GFF limit follows solely from this
comparison. Those remain separate root/tensor gates and review duties.
