## Worker-by-worker attribution

### c08_l01 — source and terminology audit

The derivation of the sharp inequality, work identity, and factor 4 reproduces the Sol baseline and is not an incremental theorem. The useful addition is an adjacent-literature/interface audit: it separates volume KS entropy and Ruelle's inequality from turbulence papers using “topological entropy” for material-line growth or statistical stretching. It also records that entropy, field representation, forcing, and actuation remain supplied rather than acquired. This supports accurate scope and prior-art triage; it does not settle novelty.

### c08_l02 — Fano interface on a flow-invariant region

Adds a conditional theorem: on a positive-volume set A invariant under every flow time, a certified K-symbol entropy rate R for a reference process and one-coordinate mismatch probability at most δ imply `W >= 4 nu V alpha [R-F_K(delta)]_+^2` (with the stated conversion for sampled physical time). The proof uses the localized normal-cocycle/Ruelle bound and block Fano. This information-error interface and the exact de Bruijn finite-window counterexample are absent from the Sol snapshots. The latter correctly shows that uniform finite block statistics through any fixed N do not certify positive asymptotic entropy: a periodic finite-order torus rotation can have those block counts and zero entropy/work.

The theorem is conditional on acquiring/proving A, α, R, and δ; it does not supply a finite-sample entropy certificate. It overlaps c08_l03/l06/l09/l10 on localized entropy and robust readout. In particular c08_l06's sampled-map version is more general in invariance and zero-set interface.

### c08_l03 — zero-set decomposition and equality classification

Adds a componentwise deficit decomposition that permits zeros of X, separates active and zero-set contributions, and gives an active-volume strengthening `W >= 4 nu V H^2/alpha`; it also states a local invariant-code floor. Its positive-entropy equality argument excludes zeros and reaches the Sol/sl(2,R) frame classification. This extends the frozen Sol hypotheses, which assume X nowhere zero, and makes explicit how the zero set and time-one ergodic components enter.

The underlying projective defect identity and positive-entropy equality frame substantially duplicate c08_s02 (and quantitative projective/strain estimates in c08_s01). The localized code floor overlaps c08_l02 and the later c08_l06 revision. Its proof is an internal extension using Ruelle and ergodic decomposition; the classification itself is acknowledged as overlapping Ghys. No acquisition or external validation is added.

### c08_l04 — sharp boundary examples for deficit stability

The sinusoidal high-frequency example has the same fixed connected mapping-torus setting and near-equality curvature blowup as c08_s01/s02: that conclusion is a duplicate. The intermittent family is a distinct strengthening: L2 deficit tends to zero while the pointwise strain gap remains bounded away from zero, and curvature diverges. This shows why an L2 deficit cannot imply uniform strain control; the Sol snapshots already establish failure of C2/curvature control even while strain approaches equality uniformly.

The varying-geometry examples add equality-boundary information beyond the frozen Sol examples: exact-equality Sol mapping tori with fixed volume/entropy but collapsing injectivity radius and unbounded curvature, and finite covers with fixed entropy and unbounded diameter. They show that equality across an unrestricted geometry class does not give uniform injectivity-radius/diameter bounds. These are not counterexamples to the local equality classification or to stability under supplied bounded-geometry hypotheses. The disconnected support-fraction and time-rescaling observations are scope checks rather than new transport theorems.

### c08_l05 — CAT(0) path stability in a mapping-torus ansatz

This is a substantive positive result beyond the Sol baseline. For `g=dt^2+dx^T G_t dx`, `det G_t=1`, and the mapping-torus deck condition, the determinant-one fiber metrics form the hyperbolic plane. The power deficit controls path length excess and gives a `sqrt(Delta)` tube around the geodesic segment; with initial metric on the Sol axis, the whole path stays within `4 sqrt(Delta)+epsilon` of one fixed constant-speed Sol path in the supplied time gauge. Equality in this subclass forces the Sol path. The exact path identities were checked in the worker's artifacts.

The projective-defect identity in its INITIAL duplicates c08_s01/s02 and should not be counted as its increment. The CAT(0) reduction is a narrow mapping-torus result, not a general geometric stability theorem. It controls metric values/transverse length distortion, not derivatives or curvature; therefore it is compatible with c08_s01/s02 and c08_l04 high-frequency curvature blowups, whose metrics can be uniformly close while their derivatives diverge. Novelty remains unknown.

### c08_l06 — sampled-invariant code power, connected topological-entropy boundary

The post-resume `robust_sampled_code_power.md` is the strongest refinement of the Fano interfaces. For `T=phi_tau`, a positive-volume T-invariant set C supported a.e. on `{X != 0}`, and conditional entropy rate κ nats/sample, it derives `W >= 4 nu V alpha κ^2/tau^2`. With a stationary joining of a finite-alphabet source process of certified rate R and the physical decoded readout, whose one-coordinate mismatch probability is at most δ, κ can be replaced by `[R-F_K(δ)]_+`. An external source without such a stationary coupling does not imply anything about the flow. The localized cocycle argument only needs invariance under the sampled map; zeros outside C are allowed. The `tau^-2` follows because the sampled exponent is κ per τ units, while the time-integrated strain contributes one factor τ before the final Cauchy/Jensen step. This improves c08_l02's requirement of invariance under all real flow times. It matches c08_l03's `alpha`-linear local floor when C is its active code set, and is sharper than the global-entropy route giving `alpha^2`.

The added `connected_topological_power_counterexample.md` repairs the earlier disconnected Sol-plus-flat witness. It uses an imported positive-entropy disk map, embeds its suspension in a connected T3, and shrinks the metric volume of the entropy-bearing support while keeping a global nowhere-zero field and total volume bounded below. At the stated interface this refutes a uniform replacement of volume KS entropy by topological entropy in `W >= C nu V h_top^2` when metrics are allowed to vary. It does **not** refute the Sol metric-entropy inequality, does not give a counterexample for one fixed metric with uniform geometry bounds, and does not furnish actuator cost. The positive-entropy disk gadget and entropy source are imported; this is an internal construction and historical priority is unknown. At the cutoff these were revisions; no `FINAL.txt` or `SCIENCE_END.json` was present for c08_l06, so treat completion as pending.

### c08_l07 — explicit equality benchmark and curvature sequence

The compact PSL(2,R) equality family is a useful exact benchmark, but its equality frame duplicates c08_s02's Sol/sl(2,R) classification and classical Ghys territory. The resumed fixed-flow/fixed-volume/fixed-entropy/fixed-work sequence with scalar curvature tending to minus infinity is an explicit equality instability. Its conclusion overlaps c08_l04's exact-equality varying-geometry collapse/curvature examples, while the Sol baseline already has a stronger fixed-background near-equality curvature counterexample. This is a reproducible construction/check, not a new general stability obstruction; entropy still uses imported smooth-volume Pesin, and novelty is unknown.

### c08_l08 — hidden-region trajectory-only minimax lower bound

This is a substantial acquisition-side addition. Starting from an imported area-preserving disk map with a positive-area entropy island, it builds a compactly supported divergence-free flow gadget and hides one of M scaled copies in one of M disjoint containers in the same flat T3. The fields have the same target volume entropy η, while the normalized strain energy grows as `C_* M eta^2`. Against the zero field, an adaptive trajectory-only protocol with at most m chosen starting states has transcript total variation at most `m/M`; hence uniform constant-confidence entropy estimation requires `m=Omega(B/eta^2)` when a strain-energy cap B is supplied, and is unbounded without a uniform envelope. The coupling also works with arbitrarily fine common path noise, since noiseless observations already fail on the no-hit event.

This complements rather than contradicts c08_s03: a globally certified net/modulus can be sufficient, while trajectory samples alone without spatial coverage or a uniform derivative/energy envelope are insufficient. It also supplies a concrete instance of the acquisition gap explicitly left open in c08_s01/s02. Limits matter: direct global field/derivative oracles, a supplied regularity envelope, dynamical noise between regions, and asymptotic typical-orbit questions are outside the interface. The Berger–Turaev gadget is imported and its construction is not replayed; constants h0/e0 and a finite-bit compiler are absent, so the minimax theorem is not an executable finite-instance estimator lower bound with explicit numeric constants. Novelty and external review remain unknown.

### c08_l09 — equality-frame patching and alternative code interface

The equality classification and projective identity largely duplicate c08_s02 and c08_l03. The explicit finite-cover frame patching and the condition of asymptotic total-variation closeness of block laws to iid uniform words are useful interface details, but overlap with c08_l02's Fano readout and c08_l10's decoded-stream condition. The iid/TV premise is still an asymptotic or acquired certificate, not a procedure for proving a positive entropy rate from a finite trace. It does not advance the base power theorem.

### c08_l10 — decoded iid stream and source-specific computation conversion

The positive-volume decoded-stream/Fano bridge to a quadratic sustaining-power floor overlaps c08_l02 and c08_l06. Its distinct extension converts the cited Cardona–Miranda–Peralta-Salas H1 lower bound for a robust tape-bounded Beltrami simulator on flat T3 into a lower bound on the strain-viscous power needed to hold that same field stationary, using `W=nu lambda^2 ||v||_2^2 >= (nu/2)||v||_{H1}^2` for the source's eigenfield normalization. The cited result itself marks the H1 bound as construction-specific. This is a source-specific physical-cost conversion, not a universal work-per-bit or Turing-completeness theorem; it inherits the source theorem and exact simulator assumptions. The iid readout and rate remain supplied/acquired conditions. Primary source previously checked in the resume audit: https://arxiv.org/html/2111.03559v3.

## Cross-cutting gaps and recommended integration

1. Keep the theorem target as volume KS entropy under the exact strain-viscosity force law. Never merge the metric-varying topological-entropy counterexample into the base inequality.
2. Separate sufficient code-cost results from acquisition. The data needed for positive floors—an invariant region, its volume fraction, asymptotic entropy/readout rate and decoder error—are not acquired by c08_l02/l03/l06/l09/l10. c08_l08 makes one missing acquisition premise adversarially necessary under its trajectory-only interface; c08_s03 gives a sufficient net/modulus route when those global bounds are supplied.
3. Separate metric-value stability from derivative/geometric stability. c08_l05's CAT(0) path tube does not control curvature; c08_s01/s02 and c08_l04 remain decisive against deficit-only curvature claims. c08_l04/l07's varying-geometry examples do not invalidate exact equality classification at a fixed geometry.
4. Preserve imports and status. Ruelle/Pesin, Ghys, Berger–Turaev, and the Beltrami simulator lower bound are imported sources; local exact checks verify formulas/examples only. All novelty and external correctness statuses remain UNKNOWN. No Luna artifact supplies a measured/actuated finite-precision physical realization.
