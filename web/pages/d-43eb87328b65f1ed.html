THEOREM AND QUANTIFIERS

For every integer d>=2, every faithful density matrix sigma in M_d, and
every CPTP map Phi:M_d->M_d satisfying the following two assumptions:
  (i) Phi(sigma)=sigma, and H=Phi* is self-adjoint in the KMS inner product
      <A,B>_sigma=Tr[sigma^(1/2) A* sigma^(1/2) B];
  (ii) there is one CPTP B:M_d->M_d tensor M_d whose two marginals equal
       Phi on EVERY input matrix,
there is ONE finite pure-ensemble sigma-Petz entanglement-breaking CPTP
map Psi with Psi(sigma)=sigma whose Heisenberg map P=Psi* is KMS
self-adjoint and satisfies, in FULL KMS Hilbert-space operator order,
  0<=P<=I,
  P >= (2 H^2-I)_+,                                      (T1)
  I-P <= 2(I-H^2) <= 4(I-H).                             (T2)
The same physical Psi works for all Hermitian observables A, and by
complexification for all A in M_d. In particular
  <A,(I-P)A>_sigma <= 4 <A,(I-H)A>_sigma.                 (T3)
The constant four depends on neither d nor the spectrum of sigma. No
minimum-eigenvalue, modular-covariance, spectral-gap, or positive-spectrum
assumption is present. The plus in (T1) is the positive spectral part of
the KMS self-adjoint linear operator, NOT a purported CP map.

The compact-limit construction is an exact existence proof. It supplies
no uniform finite-depth algorithm, finite-precision stability theorem,
physical compiler, or direct nonlinear entropy/local-reference theorem.
The precisely assigned weighted comparator gate is the theorem here.

1. SCHRODINGER, HEISENBERG AND PETZ CONVERSIONS

All trace adjoints use the ordinary unnormalized trace pairing. Thus
Phi is Schrodinger CPTP and H=Phi* is Heisenberg UCP. Define the positive
invertible sandwich operator
  Gamma_sigma(X)=sigma^(1/2) X sigma^(1/2).
Then <A,B>_sigma=Tr[A* Gamma_sigma(B)]. The KMS adjoint of H is
  H^sharp=Gamma_sigma^(-1) Phi Gamma_sigma.               (1)
This is a Heisenberg CP map, since all three factors are CP sandwiches
or CP maps. Stationarity gives H^sharp(I)=I. Its ordinary trace adjoint
is the Schrodinger Petz recovery channel
  R_sigma,Phi=Gamma_sigma H Gamma_sigma^(-1).             (2)
It is CPTP and sigma-preserving. Thus KMS reversibility here means the
specific equation H=H^sharp; equivalently
  Phi=Gamma_sigma H Gamma_sigma^(-1).
It is not GNS reversibility and does not assume [H,Ad_sigma^(it)]=0.

We first need contractivity without reversibility. If U is ANY UCP map
whose trace adjoint T is CPTP with T(sigma)=sigma, its KMS adjoint
  U^sharp=Gamma_sigma^(-1) T Gamma_sigma
is also UCP. Therefore U^sharp U is UCP and is positive self-adjoint as
an operator on the KMS Hilbert space:
  <A,U^sharp U A>_sigma=||U(A)||_sigma^2>=0.
A UCP map contracts the operator norm, so every eigenvalue of this
particular UCP map has modulus at most one. Positivity/self-adjointness
in KMS therefore puts every eigenvalue of U^sharp U in [0,1]. This proves
  U^sharp U<=I,  ||U(A)||_sigma<=||A||_sigma               (3)
on the full complex matrix Hilbert space. This finite-dimensional proof
does not borrow a Jordan/GNS norm inequality. Applied to H, it puts the
KMS self-adjoint eigenvalues of H in [-1,1].

For any finite input POVM {M_y}, omit its zero effects. Faithfulness
makes p_y=Tr(sigma M_y)>0 for every remaining effect. The measurement
channel and its Petz recovery are explicitly
  M(X)=sum_y Tr(M_y X) |y><y|,
  R_sigma,M(|y><y|)=rho_y=Gamma_sigma(M_y)/p_y.            (4)
These rho_y are density matrices and sum_y p_y rho_y=sigma. Their
composition is the Schrodinger channel
  Psi_M(X)=sum_y rho_y Tr(M_y X),                         (5)
with Heisenberg map
  P_M(A)=sum_y M_y Tr(rho_y A).                           (6)
The map is EB and CPTP, Psi_M(sigma)=sigma, and P_M(I)=I. In general
Psi_M is NOT Schrodinger-unital; that is not needed or asserted.

The exact KMS pairing is
  <A,P_M(B)>_sigma
    =sum_y p_y overline[Tr(rho_y A)] Tr(rho_y B).         (7)
Consequently P_M is KMS self-adjoint and positive as a Hilbert-space
operator. Equivalently, directly Gamma_sigma P_M=Psi_M Gamma_sigma.
The contractivity lemma (3) makes 0<=P_M<=I. All positivity here is in
the metric requested by the task, not the different norm Tr(sigma A^2).

If the POVM effects are rank one, every rho_y is pure. Conversely, any
finite pure ensemble {p_y,pi_y} with sum_y p_y pi_y=sigma gives the POVM
  M_y=p_y sigma^(-1/2) pi_y sigma^(-1/2),
and the corresponding canonical physical channel
  Psi(X)=sum_y p_y pi_y
             Tr[sigma^(-1/2) pi_y sigma^(-1/2) X].       (8)
The inverse sandwiches occur in the INPUT measurement effects. The
OUTPUT states are pi_y. Reversing these roles would give the wrong map.

2. THE SIBLING KMS COVARIANCE LEMMA, INCLUDING TRANSPOSE

Claim: if omega in M_d tensor M_d is any bipartite density matrix with
BOTH marginals sigma, then for Hermitian A,B,
  |Tr[omega(A tensor B)]|<=||A||_sigma ||B||_sigma.        (9)
No separability, independence, or symmetry of omega is required.

Choose once an eigenbasis of sigma, so sigma=diag(s_1,...,s_d)>0 is real.
Transposition t below is in exactly this basis. Define
  |Omega_sigma>=sum_i sqrt(s_i)|i> tensor |i>,
  J_T=(sigma^(-1/2) tensor I) omega
                       (sigma^(-1/2) tensor I),
  T(X)=Tr_A[(X^t tensor I) J_T].                          (10)
Here J_T is the UNNORMALIZED standard Choi matrix of T, associated with
the unnormalized vector sum_i |ii>. Its positivity makes T CP. The
first marginal condition is precisely
  Tr_B J_T=sigma^(-1/2) omega_A sigma^(-1/2)=I,
so T is TP. The second marginal gives T(sigma)=sigma. Direct expansion
of the matrix units proves
  omega=(id tensor T)(|Omega_sigma><Omega_sigma|).        (11)
In particular the auxiliary T is a genuine supplied-by-omega stationary
Schrodinger channel. It is not asserted to equal Phi or B.

Put U=T*. For Hermitian A,B, the same matrix-unit expansion gives
  Tr[omega(A tensor B)]
      =Tr[sigma^(1/2) A^t sigma^(1/2) U(B)]
      =<A^t,U(B)>_sigma.                                (12)
A^t is Hermitian when A is Hermitian. Transposition is a KMS isometry
in this fixed eigenbasis, since
  ||A||_sigma^2=sum_ij sqrt(s_i s_j)|A_ij|^2
and this expression is invariant under i<->j. Thus Hilbert-space
Cauchy-Schwarz and (3) give (9). This explicitly identifies the
transpose convention and keeps the reference on the correct register.

If omega is swap-symmetric and E_1,...,E_q are KMS-orthonormal Hermitian
observables, C_ab=Tr[omega(E_a tensor E_b)] is real symmetric, and (9)
implies
  -I_q<=C<=I_q.                                         (13)
This is the needed FULL matrix covariance order. C may have negative
eigenvalues. Square positivity of A tensor I-I tensor A alone would
instead bound it by Tr(sigma A^2), and would not prove (13).

The lemma is sharp at every faithful sigma. Take omega to be the pure
reference purification in (11), so T=id. For a real symmetric A it gives
Tr[omega(A tensor A)]=||A||_sigma^2. For imaginary antisymmetric Hermitian
A it instead gives the negative of that norm. This also explains why
negative sibling correlations must not be discarded as impossible.

3. KMS EIGENBASIS AND EXACT LEAF SCORE QUANTITIES

The real vector space of Hermitian matrices has a real KMS inner product.
H preserves this space and is KMS self-adjoint. It fixes I, whose norm
is one. Stationarity makes the centered subspace
  H_0={A=A*:Tr(sigma A)=0}
invariant. Choose a full real KMS-orthonormal Hermitian eigenbasis
  I,E_1,...,E_n, n=d^2-1,
  H(E_a)=lambda_a E_a, lambda_a in [-1,1].
Only indices with |lambda_a|>1/sqrt(2) need amplification. Relabel them
1,...,q, and let Lambda=diag(lambda_1,...,lambda_q). If q=0, the
sigma-eigenbasis dephasing map from its spectral pure ensemble is a
canonical sigma-Petz EB map, and already proves (T1) on H_0; both sides
agree on I. Hence suppose q>=1.

Choose p_a>0, sum_a p_a=1, for example 1/q. On each leaf use the ONE
randomized POVM obtained by choosing a with probability p_a and measuring
the spectral PVM E_a=sum_y e_ay P_ay:
  F_ay=p_a P_ay,
  (x_0(a,y))_b=delta_ab e_ay/p_a.                        (14)
The real vector estimator has the FULL operator first moment
  sum_z (x_0(z))_a F_z=E_a.                             (15)
At reference sigma its mean is zero. Its covariance is exactly diagonal:
  N_0=diag(v_a/p_a), v_a=Tr(sigma E_a^2).                 (16)
The v_a are ordinary physical measurement second moments, NOT KMS
norms. In general v_a!=1 and can be arbitrarily large across faithful
references. They are finite for each fixed finite-dimensional instance.
No comparison of v_a to one will be used in the limiting theorem.
Off-diagonal entries of N_0 are exactly zero because each retained
outcome coin permits just one nonzero estimator coordinate.

4. EXACT TREE RECURSION IN THE KMS COVARIANCE

Output-symmetrize B by a random swap if necessary. This preserves CP/TP
and both marginal channels. Put T_0=id and
  T_m=(T_(m-1) tensor T_(m-1)) B.
There are 2^m receiver systems. Use the same leaf POVM (14) independently
on each receiver and retain every random coin and outcome. This is one
product POVM on the output, hence ONE pulled-back input POVM M^(m)_z.
For the complete outcome record define
  x_m(z)=Lambda^(-m) 2^(-m) sum_leaves x_0(z_leaf).        (17)
The one-leaf marginal is Phi^m. Thus signs of negative eigenvalues are
retained and the Heisenberg first moments obey exactly
  sum_z (x_m(z))_a M^(m)_z=E_a                          (18)
on the FULL matrix algebra. Equivalently the two top-subtree scores
satisfy x_m=Lambda^(-1)(x_(m-1)^B+x_(m-1)^C)/2.

At input sigma, each top child marginal is sigma. Set omega=B(sigma),
and define the constant symmetric matrix C from (13). If
N_m=E_sigma[x_m x_m^t], then each child's subtree covariance is N_(m-1).
The exact product first moments of the two subtree measurements give
  E[x_(m-1),a^B x_(m-1),b^C]
       =Tr[omega(E_a tensor E_b)]=C_ab.                 (19)
The subtree first moment is an operator identity, so (19) holds even for
entangled omega. It does not factor the joint state or its probabilities.
Therefore
  N_m=(1/2)Lambda^(-1)(N_(m-1)+C)Lambda^(-1).            (20)

By (13) and positivity of the real congruence
  R(X)=(1/2)Lambda^(-1)X Lambda^(-1),
even with signed Lambda, induction gives
  N_m<=R^m(N_0)+sum_(k=1)^m R^k(I_q)=D_m.              (21)
D_m is diagonal, with exact entries
  (D_m)_aa=(v_a/p_a)(2lambda_a^2)^(-m)
         +[1-(2lambda_a^2)^(-m)]/(2lambda_a^2-1).        (22)
Thus, because all selected modes have 2lambda_a^2>1,
  D_m -> diag[1/(2lambda_a^2-1)].                       (23)
The potentially large physical variances v_a disappear in this limit.
The sibling term is bounded by the KMS identity, not by those variances.
The +/-1 modes are covered with geometric factor 2^(-m); there is no
division by 1-|lambda_a|. Boundary |lambda_a|=1/sqrt(2) requires no
amplification because its target positive spectral part is zero.

5. PETZ POSTERIOR CROSS MOMENTS AND FULL OPERATOR ORDER

Apply (4)-(7) to the entire pulled-back input POVM M_y=M^(m)_y. Write
  r_y=Tr(sigma M_y), rho_y=Gamma_sigma(M_y)/r_y,
  a_y,b=Tr(rho_y E_b), b=1,...,n.
Let S_m=E_r[a_y a_y^t], the matrix of P_m=Psi_M* on the ENTIRE centered
KMS basis. Let J:R^q->R^n embed the selected coordinates. The unbiased
operator identity (18) gives the crucial full-domain cross moment:
  E_r[a_y,b (x_m(y))_a]
      =Tr[Gamma_sigma(E_b) sum_y (x_m(y))_a M_y]
      =Tr[Gamma_sigma(E_b) E_a]
      =delta_ba.
Consequently
  E_r[a_y x_m(y)^t]=J.                                 (24)
Nonselected coordinates have cross moment zero exactly. This controls
all slow/fast cross terms rather than only diagonal preservation scores.

The classical covariance block matrix is PSD:
  [[S_m,J],[J^t,N_m]]>=0.                              (25)
N_m is positive definite: a kernel vector v would give x_m^t v=0
almost surely, and then Jv=0 by (24), which forces v=0. Its Schur
complement and inverse monotonicity in (21) yield
  S_m>=J N_m^(-1)J^t>=J D_m^(-1)J^t.                   (26)
This is full KMS quadratic-form order, with zero on the complementary
modes, and not merely a collection of separate observable comparators.

Spectrally refine every input POVM effect into rank-one effects, retain
the old estimator on each refinement, and construct the Petz canonical
map from the refined POVM. The operator first moments, probabilities'
sums and estimator covariance N_m stay unchanged. Equation (24) and
the Schur bound still hold directly. Equivalently the covariance S_m
can only increase by classical conditional Jensen. Since sigma is
faithful, refined Petz posterior states are pure. We henceforth use this
pure canonical refinement of P_m, without weakening (26).

6. COMPACTNESS, ONE FIXED FINITE CHANNEL, AND THE CONSTANT FOUR

For fixed faithful sigma, the unnormalized Choi matrices of the pure
canonical channels (8) are
  J_Psi=sum_y p_y
       [sigma^(-1/2) pi_y sigma^(-1/2)]^t tensor pi_y,
  pi_y pure, sum_y p_y pi_y=sigma.                      (27)
The vertices in (27) vary continuously on the compact pure-state set.
Their convex hull is compact in finite-dimensional Hermitian space.
The ensemble barycenter condition is exactly the closed Choi marginal
constraint Tr_output J_Psi=I; it is not a second unrecorded restriction.
Indeed that marginal equals [sigma^(-1/2)(sum_y p_y pi_y)
sigma^(-1/2)]^t. Thus the pure-canonical channel set is compact.
Caratheodory gives a finite representation; d^4+1 pure atoms is a safe
bound. These represent a single channel, not different per-query maps.

Take a convergent subsequence of the pure canonical P_m. KMS operator
order is closed for fixed sigma. Equations (23),(26) therefore give its
physical canonical limit P with
  P|H_0 >= J diag(2lambda_a^2-1) J^t
          =(2H^2-I)_+|H_0.                             (28)
P fixes I and preserves Tr(sigma A); hence identity/centered cross terms
vanish. The spectral target also fixes I. This proves (T1) on the full
real Hermitian space. All the compared maps are KMS self-adjoint and
preserve Hermitian matrices, so writing A=X+iY extends the order to the
full complex KMS Hilbert space: the imaginary cross terms cancel.

Positive spectral part dominates its underlying self-adjoint operator,
so P>=2H^2-I. Moreover
  (2H^2-I)-(4H-3I)=2(I-H)^2>=0.
Therefore
  I-P<=2(I-H^2)<=4(I-H),
as claimed. By (7) and (3) the physical limit remains a positive KMS
contraction. All these orders use the SAME Gamma_sigma and the SAME
one channel P; no change of metric or comparator occurs at the end.

7. EXACT CONTROLS, FAILED NAIVE ROUTE, AND THE ENTROPY BOUNDARY

For sigma=diag(p,1-p), 0<p<1, and Pauli X,
  Tr(sigma X^2)=1,
  ||X||_sigma^2=2sqrt[p(1-p)].
The ratio tends to infinity as p approaches zero or one through faithful
states. This exactly refutes a dimension-free transplant that replaces
the KMS sibling bound by the broadcast-square/Jordan bound and silently
declares the two norms equal. It is a proof-mechanism failure, not a
counterexample to the weighted theorem. Lemma (9) bypasses the loss.

The fixed exact replay uses sigma=diag(9/10,1/10). The X-basis POVM
M_+=(I+X)/2,M_-=(I-X)/2 has p_+=p_-=1/2 and pure Petz outputs
  pi_+=[[9/10,3/10],[3/10,1/10]],
  pi_-=[[9/10,-3/10],[-3/10,1/10]].
It checks normalization, stationarity, KMS self-adjointness, the positive
contraction, and the physical Choi matrix exactly. In particular
  P(I)=I, P(X)=3X/5, P(Y)=0, P(Z)=4I/5,
whereas the Schrodinger channel has Psi(I)=2sigma, not I.

The pure coupling |Omega_sigma><Omega_sigma| is a legal sibling output
for the compatible reversible replacer channel Phi(rho)=Tr(rho)sigma,
with B(rho)=Tr(rho)|Omega_sigma><Omega_sigma|. Its normalized covariance
on X,Y,Z-Tr(sigma Z)I is diag(1,-1,1). This checks both transpose signs
and sharp KMS order using an actual legal broadcaster, not an arbitrary
state that lacks a channel realization. A separate exact dephasing
broadcaster provides a selected +1 mode and zero slow/fast cross moments.
There are no random or parameter scans, solver probes or floating spectra.

For complex matrices the GNS norm is Tr(sigma A* A); on Hermitian
observables the physical variance is the Jordan quadratic form
Q_sigma(A)=Tr(sigma A^2). Neither is the KMS form used throughout (T1-T3).
The relative-entropy Hessian is also generally a different form. The
theorem alone is not a proof of a nonlinear entropy-stability estimate
or of the high-upside fixed-I(A:B) reconstruction endpoint. Those require
their own precise state/function interface. It closes the specifically
assigned universal weighted Dirichlet comparator gate without importing
a condition number into its comparison constant.

8. SUPPLIED/ACQUIRED INFORMATION, COSTS, AND PROVENANCE

Supplied: exact finite-dimensional sigma, Phi, and one compatible B;
faithfulness, stationarity and KMS reversibility are promises. Acquired:
the exact KMS eigendecomposition, selected PVMs, tree-pulled POVMs, Petz
posterior channels, the covariance bound and compact existence argument.
No broadcaster/state/channel acquisition theorem is supplied.

Depth m uses 2^m-1 calls to B and 2^m receivers. The same leaf POVM has
at most qd outcomes including its random coin; its full raw joint record
has at most (qd)^(2^m) outcomes. Rank-one input refinement adds at most d
outcomes per old effect. Estimator amplitudes may grow as |lambda_a|^(-m).
Finite-depth convergence can be slow near the threshold or when physical
leaf variances are large. With b0=max_a(v_a/p_a) and
kappa=max_a 1/(2lambda_a^2)<1, one has the additive bound
  S_m >= (2H^2-I)_+|H_0 - b0 kappa^m I_H0.
This finite-depth expression is not the exact multiplicative theorem at
zero-deficit modes. A finite abstract Caratheodory representation after
taking the limit does not erase the costs of designing/implementing it.
