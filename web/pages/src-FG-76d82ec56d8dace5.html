2. Conditional-envelope theorem: reconstruction and exact prerequisites

Use a normalized tractable retained reference pi(dx), and a normalized hidden
reference nu(dy) that need not be evaluated. The actual physical equilibrium
measure is proportional to w(x,y) pi(dx) nu(dy). Supplied global bounds satisfy
0<ell(x)<=w(x,y)<=u(x). The physical weighted form operator A_theta solves
A_theta t=w in this reference Hilbert space. It is not automatically minus the
physical generator with its own invariant inner product. The target mean is

  tau=<w,A_theta^(-1)w>/<w,1>.

The source proof reconstructs under three exact conditions: the full form
dominates A_- acting fiberwise on retained coordinates; the full form on
retained-only tests is at most A_+; and the retained inverses are positive and
positivity preserving. These imply

  <ell,A_+^(-1)ell>/Z_u <= tau <= <u,A_-^(-1)u>/Z_ell.

For the upper bound, inverse form order first bounds the full response by the
fiberwise A_- response. Positivity preservation then permits replacing each
positive source w by u. This second step would fail for an arbitrary positive
definite inverse with negative kernel entries. For the lower bound, the exact
positive test A_+^(-1)ell and the retained-only form upper bound give the numerator
bound. The actual source normalizer is enclosed separately between Z_ell and
Z_u. No hidden source mass or residence offset has been dropped.

An unknown internal bath partition function is absorbed into the normalized
nu. This cancellation is legitimate because the physical source/form are scaled
together and w has independently valid interaction bounds relative to that
reference. It does not reveal an arbitrary conditional free energy or make
retained interacting moments tractable. Cross diffusion, an unknown reaction
surface, or hidden reaction channels need their own full-form bounds. Arbitrary
slow hidden kinetics is harmless only inside those declared conditions.

The enclosure is generally conservative; the separate source/form extrema may
not be simultaneously realizable. Constant envelopes permit arbitrary bath

the explicit 16-state retained graph. The large continuum backend handles the
