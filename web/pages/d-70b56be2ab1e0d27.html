NONINTEGRABLE FINITE-RANGE REPULSIONS SHARE THE OPEN-XX WHOLE-STATE CRITICAL LIMIT
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the form and whole-state trace-norm theorem below. The prior TG mapping and the cited nonintegrability classification are SOURCE-REPORTED in their stated scopes. Novelty/priority is UNKNOWN; no external proof review has occurred.

Result
------
At the open spin-half XX saturation point, adding any nonnegative density-density interaction of fixed finite lattice range does not change the fixed-particle low-energy continuum limit under beta=tau L^2 and field detuning lambda/L^2. Under the natural parameter-independent Jordan-Wigner/sine-mode embedding, each fixed-m Gibbs block converges in trace norm to the Dirichlet Tonks-Girardeau/free-fermion continuum block. A uniform cubic particle-number tail upgrades this to convergence of the entire grand-canonical Gibbs state, uniformly over the compact unknown-field interval and even over arbitrary nonnegative interaction arrays of that fixed range. This is a proof by positive-form convergence, not a Bethe-ansatz argument.

1. Exact family and quantifiers
-------------------------------
Let N=L-1 sites form an open path. Fix J>0, tau>0, B>=0 and integer R>=1, all supplied. Let b_i be hard-core boson operators, n_i=b_i^dagger b_i in {0,1}, and M=sum_i n_i. For each N, take any finite real V^(N)_(i,r)>=0, i=1,...,N-r, r=1,...,R, with V=0 for larger separation. The coefficients may be inhomogeneous and may grow without bound with N. Define

 H_(N,lambda)^V = -2J sum_(i=1)^(N-1) (b_i^dagger b_(i+1)+b_(i+1)^dagger b_i)
                    +4J M - (lambda/L^2)M + W_N,
 W_N=sum_(r=1)^R sum_(i=1)^(N-r) V^(N)_(i,r)n_i n_(i+r),
 beta_N=tau L^2,   rho_(N,lambda)^V = exp(-beta_N H)/Tr exp(-beta_N H),
 lambda in supplied interval [-B,B] and unknown to encoder/decoder.

The one-particle saturation point remains 4J since W_N vanishes for M<=1; positivity prevents multiparticle states from lowering it. A specific homogeneous range-two instance is V_(i,1)=U>0 and V_(i,2)=V>0. Its periodic bulk spin Hamiltonian has NN XX/YY, NN ZZ, NNN ZZ and a longitudinal field; the cited classification places it outside its classical and Bethe-solvable exceptions. Open boundaries are retained for the theorem.

The whole-state limit uses one common Hilbert space. Jordan-Wigner maps each open hard-core-boson sector unitarily to fermions. The N-site sine mode phi_(L,k)(i)=sqrt(2/L)sin(pi k i/L) is sent to phi_k(x)=sqrt(2)sin(pi kx) in L^2(0,1); lift this isometry to every exterior-power sector and extend by zero in the continuum fermion Fock space. The inverse isometry plus an orthogonal-complement-to-vacuum map is a parameter-blind reverse channel. The bosonic Tonks-Girardeau form is equivalent via the one-dimensional sign map. The statement concerns this global state embedding, not a fixed-site spin marginal.

Write T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2 for trace distance. Let u_N:C^N->L^2(0,1) be the one-particle sine-mode isometry, U_(N,m)=wedge^m u_N, and script-U_N=direct_sum_m U_(N,m) for the full-Fock embedding.

2. Theorem
----------
For every fixed m, the ordered m-sector eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity, converge to those of

 A_(infinity,m) = -2J sum_(a=1)^m partial_(x_a)^2

on the antisymmetric Dirichlet space over [0,1]^m (equivalently H_0^1 on the ordered simplex 0<x_1<...<x_m<1). Isolated spectral-cluster projectors converge. Individual eigenvectors inside a degenerate limiting cluster are not a canonical claim.

More strongly, write the finite embedded Gibbs block as

 G_(N,m)^V(lambda)=exp(tau lambda m) exp(-tau A_(N,m)^V),
 A_(N,m)^V=L^2[ -2J sum(b_i^dagger b_(i+1)+h.c.)+4JM+W_N ] restricted to M=m.

Then, for each fixed m,

 sup_(|lambda|<=B, V_N>=0, range(V_N)<=R)
 || U_(N,m)G_(N,m)^V(lambda)U_(N,m)^* - exp(tau lambda m)exp(-tau A_(infinity,m)) ||_1 -> 0.

The direct sum over all m also converges after normalization:

 sup_(|lambda|<=B, V_N>=0, range(V_N)<=R)
 T( script-U_N rho_(N,lambda)^V script-U_N^*, rho_infinity(lambda)) -> 0.

The limit is the trace-class free-fermion/Tonks-Girardeau Fock Gibbs state with mode energies a_k=2J pi^2 k^2 and Bernoulli occupations p_k(lambda)=1/[1+exp(tau(a_k-lambda))]. The two channels in the whole-state experiment are parameter independent. This is qualitative o(1) convergence; an explicit rate for the fixed-m semigroup term is not established.

3. Proof mechanism and whole-state error decomposition
------------------------------------------------------
The finite scaled free modes have energies a_(L,k)=4J L^2[1-cos(pi k/L)]->2J pi^2 k^2. The interaction form is L^2<W_N>, nonnegative and supported only when some pair separation is at most R lattice sites. In the common sine-Fock space:

- Liminf: W_N>=0, and every finite Slater-coordinate coefficient of a weakly convergent bounded-form sequence converges while its free eigenvalue tends to 2J pi^2 sum k^2. Exhausting finite mode sets gives the continuum free-form lower bound.
- Recovery: sample a smooth function compactly supported inside the ordered simplex. For all sufficiently large L, its sampled support has every pair gap >R, so W_N times the recovery vector is exactly zero even if the positive V coefficients diverge with N. The scaled discrete Dirichlet form converges to 2J times the continuum gradient form. Such functions are a form core.
- Compactness: a_(L,k)>=8Jk^2. Thus bounded-energy vectors are uniformly tight in mode space; adding W>=0 preserves this. Compact Mosco convergence gives the fixed-sector eigenvalue and spectral-cluster convergence.
- Heat trace: min-max gives mu_(N,m,j)^V>=mu_(N,m,j)^0. The free spectra are bounded below by the ordered list of 8J sum_(k in S)k^2 over m-element subsets S of positive integers. Its heat tail is summable, uniformly in N and V. Sector semigroups therefore converge in trace norm.
- Grand-canonical lift: for c=8J and any d>=1,

    P_(N,lambda)^V(M>=d)
      <= exp(-s d) product_(k>=1)[1+exp(tau B+s-tau c k^2)].

  With s=tau c d^2/4, a=sqrt(B/c), K=floor sqrt(d^2/4+B/c), and C_(tau,c)=sum_(r>=1)exp[-tau c(r^2-1)],

    log P(M>=d) <= -tau c d^3/6 + (tau c a/2)d^2
                    +(tau c a^2+log(2)/2)d
                    +2tau c a^3/3+a log(2)+C_(tau,c).

  Since c=8J the leading exponent is -(4/3)tau J d^3. This is a uniform tail bound, separate from the fixed-sector convergence.

For a cutoff d, the full unnormalized trace-norm error is bounded by

 sum_(m<d) eta_(N,m) + S_N(d)+S_infinity(d),

where eta_(N,m)->0 is the fixed-sector heat-semigroup error, while S_N,S_infinity are the unnormalized m>=d traces bounded by the displayed cubic tail (vacuum normalization is at least one). The normalized trace distance is at most this unnormalized bound because both partition functions are >=1. Taking N->infinity first and then d->infinity proves the uniform whole-state claim. No hidden error term is put into the explicit particle tail.

4. The separate number-sector archive still holds
------------------------------------------------
Every W_N commutes with M. The field detuning multiplies each m-sector by exp(tau lambda m), so

 rho_(N,lambda)^V=sum_m q_(N,lambda)^V(m) omega_(N,m)^V,
 omega_(N,m)^V=exp(-tau A_(N,m)^V)/Tr exp(-tau A_(N,m)^V),

and omega_(N,m)^V is independent of lambda. Thus M remains an exact parameter-blind sufficient statistic, unlike the anisotropic XY follow-up. Store m exactly below D-1 and send all larger m to overflow; decode exact sectors with omega_(N,m)^V. For D>=N+1 this is exact. For d=D-1, the error is bounded by the same exp[-(4/3)tau J d^3+O_(J,tau,B)(d^2)] tail, uniformly in N and in all allowed V arrays. Here D is total memory Hilbert dimension / number of orthogonal labels; classical storage is log_2 D bits.

This is only an information-theoretic archive. It charges no estimate of lambda, but the full V array is treated as supplied model data. The exact decoder must prepare an interacting sector Gibbs state. No runtime, gate count, finite-bit diagonalization, or sampler is certified. The homogeneous V_N -> continuum limit is independent of V, but that does not make exact finite-N sector preparation efficient.

Finite evidence: [check_m2.py](check_m2.py) verifies the exact N=3,m=2 free-sector spectrum {8-2sqrt(2),8,8+2sqrt(2)} and that the U=1,V=2 range-(1,2) interaction adds the positive diagonal matrix diag(1,2,1). It is only a transcription/form-order sanity check, not evidence for the N->infinity theorem.

5. Nonintegrability scope and source audit
------------------------------------------
For the homogeneous range-two example, n_i=(1-sigma_i^z)/2 gives bulk couplings J^1_XX=J^1_YY=-J, J^1_ZZ=U/4, J^2_ZZ=V/4, and a longitudinal field. Naoto Shiraishi's Theorem 2.1 treats periodic shift-invariant, inversion-symmetric spin-half chains with NN and NNN couplings. Its only integrable exceptions are a classical diagonal family and a Bethe-solvable cross-coupling family; the present bulk point lies outside them. This is a primary-source classification of the periodic bulk's local-conservation status. Because our finite systems have open ends, it is used as a bulk nonintegrability identifier, not as a theorem about each finite open chain. The form proof does not invoke integrability at all.

Positivity is load-bearing; growing range or attractive interactions can produce other continuum physics and are outside the theorem.
