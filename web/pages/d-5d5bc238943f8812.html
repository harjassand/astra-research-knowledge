RESULT
The bounded exact checks found no algebraic counterexample to the differential
identities (S2)-(S4) or the stated inner-ball matrix bound (S5). They do confirm
the report's warning that D_C is not globally positive: for C=diag(1,2,3) and
m=(0,0,1), exact arithmetic gives D_C=diag(-1,1,0). The proposed stopped-ball
construction is therefore a load-bearing part of the argument.

This is a finite fixture audit, not independent verification of the theorem
(S1), the exponential tail (S7)-(S12), or its priority. No finite-bit SDE
sampler, physical preparation, or hardware result was checked.

PROOF-LEVEL RECONSTRUCTION (SEPARATE FROM FIXTURES)
For scalar/differential action on k_N, B_n^2 k expands to
(n.J)^2 k+2(n.J)k(n.J)+k(n.J)^2. Since [n.J,k]=iR_n k and R_n commutes
with the constant matrix action, -R_n^2 k=[[n.J,[n.J,k]]]=(n.J)^2k
-2(n.J)k(n.J)+k(n.J)^2. Subtraction gives (S2) with the reported sign and
factor. Applying this to each eigenaxis of C, and using the anticommutator
identity for b.J, gives the generator in (S3).

In the expansion of B_n^2-R_n^2, the second-order coefficient is
vv^T-rr^T, the first-order coefficient is
2N u v+(v.grad)v-(r.grad)r, and the zeroth-order coefficient is
N^2u^2+N(1-u^2). Summing with eigenvalues gives (S4), including its potential
V_N=Tr(H k_N). Also
D_C=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_x C[m]_x^T. Since C>=I and C<=Lc I, for any z,

 z^T D_C z >= |(I-mm^T)z|^2-Lc |[m]_x^T z|^2
             >= ((1-|m|^2)^2-Lc|m|^2)|z|^2.

At r0^2=1/[16(Lc+1)] this coefficient exceeds 3/4. The upper bound
D_C<=Tr(C)I follows from |v_i|<=1. These analytic steps explain why (S5)
gives a smooth, uniformly elliptic diffusion on the closed stopped ball.

The drift estimate (S8) follows by bounding |u v|<=r0,
|(v.grad)v|<=2r0 and |(r.grad)r|<=r0, then summing eigenvalues. The covariance
of each stopped martingale coordinate is <=Tr(C)/(2s^2). If the process starts
within r0/2 and hits r0 by time one while drift displacement is <=r0/8, its
martingale vector has maximum norm >=3r0/8. A coordinate union bound and the
standard two-sided exponential martingale maximum inequality give the stronger
exponent 3 r0^2 s^2/(64 Tr(C)); the report uses a factor-four weaker exponent.
With Tr(C)<=3Lc this implies its stated kappa1=r0^2/(256Lc).

The stopped Ito residual has the stated sign: W'_t=M_H W_t-R_t, where R_t is
the expectation of the stopped weighted M_H k_N term. Duhamel against
exp(M_H) gives a norm at most h_N exp(2h_N) P(tau<=1). The initial replacement
adds at most 2 exp(h_N) p_bad. Since t0 J^2 commutes with H, the comparison
operator is exactly e^K/Z_base. The sector trace Jensen bound gives its trace
at least one. For positive G,W, normalization satisfies
T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG. These are consistent with (S10)-(S12).

CONSTANTS / OPEN AUDIT ITEMS
The stated heat-seed tail constants C_{Lambda,delta} are not numerically
instantiated in the target text, although they can be made explicit from its
bounds. One conservative derivation is: on [a0,2], sinch(r/2)<=sinh(1) and
r^2<=4, so the unnormalized tail is at most
8 sinh(1) exp(-N a0^4/768). For r>=2, if N delta>=2, the integrand is at most
r^2 exp(-N delta r^2/8). With a=N delta/8>=1/4, integration by parts and the
Gaussian tail bound give
int_2^infty r^2 exp(-a r^2)dr <= exp(-4a)(1/a+1/(8a^2))
                           <= 6 exp(-N delta/4).
The seed normalizer lower bound is N^-3/4 Lbase, so one may take
C_{Lambda,delta}=(8 sinh(1)+6)/Lbase for
kappa0=min(a0^4/768,delta/4). This closes only the numerical-constant
presentation gap; the underlying elementary inequalities still merit a
separate formal audit.

The exact fixtures do not verify (S7)'s all-r tail and normalizer inequalities,
the existence/uniqueness and Ito/Feynman-Kac justification for every admitted
parameter, or the final uniform absorption into C exp(-cN). The report gives a
plausible derivation for each, but they remain proof-level dependencies until
an independent argument checks their hypotheses and constants. N0 is chosen by
asymptotic inequalities in the report; it is not numerically compiled here.
The principal operational limit is explicit: the construction is a
known-parameter continuous Brownian/Feynman-Kac mixture. It is not a
finite-bit sampler, and the exp(O(sqrt(N))) worst-case weight/semigroup bound
precludes inferring polynomial preparation cost from this proof.
