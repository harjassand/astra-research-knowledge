Theorems 1.2/1.3 (PDF pp.2,5): for one finite matrix A and analytic convex neighborhood Omega of W(A), an attained metric H satisfies I<=H<=4I; S=H^(1/2) makes f(A) an operator-norm contraction. 1. EXACT CLOSURE METRIC AND DISSIPATIVITY TRANSFER (derived)
For fixed finite A, use analytic convex outer neighborhoods Omega_j descending in Hausdorff distance to K=W(A), and normalized metrics I<=H_j<=4I. A subsequence converges in the finite-dimensional Hermitian space to H, I<=H<=4I. For each finite m and each matrix-valued F holomorphic near K, eventually F is defined on Omega_j. Passing
 F[A]^*(H_j tensor I_m)F[A] <= (sup_{Omega_j}||F||)^2(H_j tensor I_m)
to the limit gives the same inequality on K with H. The sequence and limit were chosen independently of F,m, so this one H works for all of them. The condition number of H^(1/2) is at most2.

If Re F(z)<=-alpha I_m on K, then sup_K||exp(s(F(z)+alpha I_m))||<=1 for every s>=0. Analytic evaluation preserves multiplication and exponentials. The common-metric contraction inequality and differentiation at s=0 give
 F[A]^*(H tensor I_m)+(H tensor I_m)F[A] <= -2alpha(H tensor I_m).
Thus for any measurable time-varying family F_t, uniformly locally bounded in sup_K norm and with this scalar-fibre dissipativity almost everywhere, the finite-dimensional evolution x'=F_t[A]x satisfies
 ||U(t,s)|| <= 2 exp(-integral_s^t alpha(u)du).
This permits noncommuting coefficient matrices, arbitrary switching among the functions, and arbitrary coefficient dimension. It requires ONE FIXED A. The degree is unrestricted; rational poles must stay outside K.

Likewise, if sup_K||F_j(z)||<=q_j, ordered products satisfy
 ||F_l[A]...F_1[A]|| <= 2 product_j q_j.
All intermediate values contract in the same H tensor I norm. Exact product evaluation is the homomorphism identity, even for noncommuting coefficients.

2. EXACT DISCRETE OBSTRUCTION TO DISTINCT-SPATIAL SWITCHING (unconditional)
Let J=[[0,1],[0,0]], A_1=2J and A_2=2J*. Both have identical closed unit-disk numerical range and spectrum {0}. Yet
 A_2 A_1=diag(0,4), ||(A_2 A_1)^k||=4^k.
Taking the same scalar function F(z)=z gives fibre bound |F|<=1 on both numerical ranges, while alternating evaluations grow exponentially. There is no positive metric, of ANY condition number, making both A_1,A_2 contractions: their product has spectral radius4.
Even strict fibre contractions do not repair this: A_1=2rJ,A_2=2rJ*, 1/2<r<1, have W(A_j)=r disk and sup|z|=r<1 but rho(A_2 A_1)=4r^2>1.

3. EXACT CONTINUOUS OBSTRUCTION, IDENTICAL STABLE SCALAR MODEL (unconditional)
Use the same A_1=2J,A_2=2J*, and the SAME rational function
 F(z)=-(1+(3/4)z)/(1-(3/4)z).
Its only pole4/3 is outside the unit disk. For |z|<=1,
 Re F(z)=-(1-(9/16)|z|^2)/|1-(3/4)z|^2 <= -1/7.
However nilpotence gives K_1=F(A_1)=-I-3J and K_2=F(A_2)=-I-3J*. Each is Hurwitz, with both eigenvalues -1. Alternating for one half-unit per mode gives
 e^(K_2/2)e^(K_1/2)=e^(-1)[[1,-3/2],[-3/2,13/4]].
The symmetric matrix has exact eigenvalues4 and1/4. Consequently repeated one-unit periods grow as (4/e)^k>1. An equally informed comparator that retains the two full matrices sees this instability directly. Shared numerical range, shared scalar transfer function, and individual stability cannot replace a common metric.
Also (K_1+K_2)/2 has eigenvalues1/2 and-5/2, ruling out any common dissipativity metric even at zero margin.

6. COST AND ACQUISITION BOUNDARY
The structural SDP is min tau subject to I<=H<=tau I and T*HT<=H, T=f(A). It has n^2 real Hermitian variables plus tau and three n-by-n matrix inequalities. Once T and tolerances are supplied, standard SDP machinery applies. General conformal-map acquisition and rigorous evaluation of f(A) are additional costs; the source theorem supplies no universal bit-complexity bound for those.
For a finite already evaluated family K_j=F_j[A] on n*m_j states, a direct common-metric certificate is the SDP with I<=H<=4I and K_j*(H tensor I)+(H tensor I)K_j<=-2alpha_j(H tensor I), or the discrete contraction analogue. Variables remain n^2; constraint block sizes grow as n*m_j. Sharp boundary feasibility need not be strict, so finite precision, bit heights and eigenvalue slack must be charged. The domain-wise bounds alpha_j/q_j, pole exclusion, evaluation costs, and verification of residual margins must also be acquired or explicitly supplied. Infinite families are not validated by a finite grid.

DECISION
Valid conditional transfer: one spatial A admits one well-conditioned quadratic energy for every scalar-fibre dissipative matrix-valued analytic family. Exact discrete and continuous counterexamples sharply delimit the scope across distinct A_j and arbitrary spatial mixing. No nonroutine asymptotic consequence beyond equally informed complete-spectral-set prior art survived this attack. Parent's finite-horizon robust feedback route is the stronger application if its sharp threshold and uncertainty interface are kept explicit.
