Assume a compact base, positive candidate metric written (h'=kA), separable positive tangent–fibre test tensor (Q), and an acquired integration identity

\[
I(h')=\int\operatorname{tr}(SA),\qquad S\le-\eta I,quad\eta>0.
\]

Let (W_Q) be contraction of (Q) in the tangent metric, and let (C=\sup\lambda_{\max}(W_Q)>0) in the background fibre metric. For any fibre-coordinate lift, the squared-norm Levi identity is a nonnegative square minus curvature. Thus a Griffiths upper-curvature bound (R_{h'}\le\varepsilon\omega I), with \(\varepsilon\ge0\), gives

\[
I(h')\ge-\varepsilon\int\operatorname{tr}(W_Q A)
\ge-\varepsilon C\int\operatorname{tr}A.
\]

The supplied strict adjoint bound gives (I(h')\le-\eta\int\operatorname{tr}A). Since the latter integral is positive, compatibility forces \(\varepsilon\ge\eta/C\). A negative \(\varepsilon\) is also impossible: the first inequality would make (I(h')\ge0). Equality at \(\eta/C\) is not excluded by these inequalities. Duality changes the curvature sign, giving the corresponding lower-curvature-error gate on the dual bundle.

For the displayed Hermitian matrices (A_1,A_2) and tangent form (H=\operatorname{diag}(4,5)), set (C_s=H^{-1/2}A_sH^{-1/2}). Direct arithmetic gives \(\operatorname{tr}C_s=5\) and \(\det C_s=-50/20=-5/2\). For (Q_s=(23/4)I-C_s),

\[
\operatorname{tr}Q_s=13/2,\quad\det Q_s=(529-460-40)/16=29/16>0,
\]
\[
\operatorname{tr}(C_sQ_s)=115/4-(25+5)=-5/4.
\]

Each (Q_s) is positive definite. With the supplied split-line projections, the stated tangent contraction is (W_Q=(13/2)I). Therefore the supplied bounds (\eta=5\pi/4\) and (\eta=5\pi/8\) give respectively (5\pi/26\) and (5\pi/52\). These ratios depend on the stated normalization of curvature and tangent form.
