2. EXACT ENTROPY AND SHARPER TRACE BOUNDS IN ANY TOTAL IRREP

Set omega_nu=exp(T_theta^(nu)/N)/Z_nu and sigma_nu=Psi_nu(omega_nu).
They are full rank because Psi_nu is unital. Operator log Jensen gives

  log sigma_nu>=Psi_nu(log omega_nu).

Here is a finite direct proof of that standard inequality. For A>0,t>=0,
the two-by-two block [[A+tI,I],[I,(A+tI)^-1]] is PSD. Apply the
completely positive unital map entrywise and take a Schur complement:

  Psi((A+tI)^-1)>=(Psi(A)+tI)^-1.

Integrate the resolvent formula for log, whose scalar-I terms agree
by unitality. The result is log Psi(A)>=Psi(logA), with no commutation
assumption. Thus

  D(omega_nu||sigma_nu)
    <=Tr omega_nu(log omega_nu-Psi_nu(log omega_nu))
    =(1/N)Tr omega_nu (I-Psi_nu)T_theta^(nu)
    <=B_G||theta||/N.                                   (5)

The trivial total irrep has zero error. This is a bound at arbitrarily
large total highestweights and arbitrarily large ||T_theta^(nu)/N||.
Substituting a bounded-temperature single-copy theorem here would be
invalid; (4)--(5) are what remove that restriction.

For the sharper trace rate, use an exact additional property of this
LINEAR Gibbs input. It commutes with exp(it T_theta^(nu)) for every t.
Group covariance of Psi_nu therefore makes sigma_nu commute with these
same unitaries, hence with T_theta^(nu). Even when that operator has
degenerate eigenvalues, omega_nu is scalar on each of its eigenspaces,
so sigma_nu and omega_nu commute. Their logarithms commute as well.

The whole-output bound (4) and Jensen now give

  log sigma_nu>=log omega_nu-delta I,
  delta=B_G||theta||/N.

Simultaneous diagonalization of the two commuting logarithms yields

  sigma_nu>=exp(-delta)omega_nu.                         (5a)

This step does NOT claim that the matrix exponential is generally
operator monotone. The exact commutation just proved is necessary.
Because both states have trace1, if delta>0 then
tau=[sigma_nu-exp(-delta)omega_nu]/[1-exp(-delta)] is a density state.
Consequently

  (1/2)||omega_nu-sigma_nu||_1
       <=1-exp(-delta)<=delta.                          (5b)

For delta=0, (5a) and trace equality give equality of the two states.
This removes the Pinsker square root, with no dependence on nu or
effective block temperature. The covariance/commutation upgrade was
