GENERAL MICROSTATE INTERFACE

The initialization in INITIAL used n=2k to make each retained orientation a single complementary minor. The same lift works for every F in Q(i)^(n x n) and every k<=n/2 on the actual microscopic state space
  Omega_(n,k)={(I,J): I,J subset [n], |I|=|J|=k, I intersect J=empty}.
For x=(I,J), set w(x)=|det F[I,J]|^2 and R(x)=I union J. Then
  c_k(F)=sum_(x in Omega_(n,k)) w(x),
so pi(x)=w(x)/c_k(F) is exactly the determinant-weighted hard-pair micro law. Its site-pair marginal at I is
  sum_(J disjoint I, |J|=k) w(I,J),
which equals the Gram volume of the paired columns {e_i,f_i:i in I} in V=[I_n,F^T].

Proof of the Gram marginal: let V_I be the n-by-2k matrix of those paired columns. Cauchy--Binet gives
 det(V_I^*V_I)=sum_(K subset [n], |K|=2k)|det V[K,P(I)]|^2.
If I is not a subset of K the minor vanishes along the identity columns. Otherwise K=I union J for a unique J disjoint from I of size k, and expansion along the e_i columns leaves F[I,J]^T, up to row-order sign. Therefore the sum is exactly sum_J |det F[I,J]|^2. Summing over I proves the normalizer c_k identity. This matches the current fixed-k Cauchy--Binet interface, including k<n/2 and variable hole set U=[n]\(I union J).

For two states x=(I,J), y=(I',J'), define the common retained core C=R(x) intersect R(y) and mismatch
  Delta(x,y)=(I intersect C) symmetric-difference (I' intersect C).
The coherent two-replica lift
  Xi_k=sum_(x,y in Omega) det F[I,J] det F[I',J'] |x,y,Delta(x,y)>
has squared norm c_k(F)^2. The label map is injective, so it is an isometry; measurement and tracing out Delta gives pi tensor pi exactly. The common-cut projector is the extra event Delta=emptyset and may delete all mass on the n=8 sewing example. Keeping Delta avoids that projection. As before, Xi_k is a formal normalized state only after division by the unknown c_k(F); no physical circuit or coefficient computation is claimed.

EXACT TRANSITIONS AND ENERGY COEFFICIENT

The number of microscopic proposals is
  M_(n,k)=binom(n,k) binom(n-k,k).
A uniform proposal is generated by choosing I uniformly among k-subsets and then J uniformly among k-subsets of [n]\I. Propose independently for the two replicas and accept the product state y with
  min(1, w(x') w(y')/[w(x)w(y)]).
Zero-weight proposals reject. Each ratio is rational and exact for rational-complex F; all normalizers cancel. For an expected-polynomial-time exact implementation, use uniform-rank sampling plus combinatorial unranking for I and J, then exact Bernoulli rejection for the rational acceptance probability. Positive support is irreducible because every positive pair can be proposed and accepted from every other positive pair.

For a single replica, symmetric uniform proposal and MH yield
  E_K(g,g) >= Var_pi(g)/M_(n,k),
by pi(x)K(x,y)=min(pi(x),pi(y))/M_(n,k) and min(a,b)>=ab for a,b in [0,1]. A random-scan product update of the two replicas then gives, for all f_1,f_2,
  Var_(pi tensor pi)(f_1(x)+f_2(y))
    <= 2 M_(n,k) E_P(f_1(x)+f_2(y)).
This establishes a computable two-replica energy comparison on the full microstate interface without an aggregate hole oracle. The coefficient 2M_(n,k) is exponential near the central sector; it is not the requested polynomial energy bound.

The directed 2q-cycle has n=2q,k=q, M=binom(2q,q). Only the two alternating (I,J=I^c) microstates have positive weight, each one. The random-scan product kernel has gap exactly 1/M; an indicator of which alternating state the first replica occupies gives Var=1/4 and energy=1/(4M), so L>=M for this kernel. This recovers the exact exponential obstruction from the direct microstate view.

REMAINING GATE

This direct lift makes the orientation register and mismatch explicit on the true fixed-k law, and it gives polynomial-bit transition evaluation. What is still missing is a proposal law whose probability is concentrated on supported states, or a larger defect state space with a polynomial-congestion flow. The uniform proposal gives the wrong energy scale, and the standard full-column DPP rejects an exponentially rare pair event on the directed-cycle family. The result should be attributed as a representation plus an exponential-cost baseline, not as progress to a general FPRAS or field-breaking sampler.
