EXTERIOR HALF-SPLITTERS HAVE NO SLOW QUANTUM DIRECTIONS
c04_s02, independent post-INITIAL derivation. Main general gate unresolved.

For d>=2s+1, N=2s, H_N=wedge^N(C^d), D_N=binomial(d,N), define physical
partial trace T_Ns:H_N->wedge^s(C^d), Petz recovery R=(D_s/D_N)T_Ns*, and
Phi=R T_Ns. The grouping inclusion V:H_N->H_s tensor H_s is isometric;
its two marginals are both T_Ns on every input. Applying R on BOTH outputs
therefore gives an actual compatible endomorphism Phi, positive selfadjoint
and maximally-mixed preserving. The full-domain replacement EB channel
Psi_0(X)=Tr(X)I_N/D_N satisfies

 I-Psi_0 <= [2(d-s)/d](I-Phi) <=2(I-Phi).                  (F1)

Consequently every theta-bounded likelihood family with its actual Phi
residual r has replacement half-trace error <=(1/2)sqrt(2 theta r).

half-splitter map as a counterexample route, not every alternative channel
on the same Hilbert space. d=N is a one-dimensional trivial input space;
no nontrivial directions or lower-bound statement occurs there.

COMPLETE SPECTRAL DERIVATION

End(wedge^N C^d), under simultaneous U(d) conjugation, has the standard
multiplicity-one exterior harmonic decomposition ell=0,...,min(N,d-N).
The ell component has highest weight (1^ell,0,...,0,-1^ell) and dimension
 m_ell=binomial(d,ell)^2-binomial(d,ell-1)^2.
This follows by successive contractions of wedge^N V tensor wedge^N V*;
the trace-free contraction kernel at each step is generated by its highest
weight tensor. Dimensions telescope to D_N^2, with Hodge duality used when
N>d/2. All covariance scalars below can thus be determined on highest vectors.

Use fermionic creation/annihilation operators and let O_(N,ell) move ell
distinct occupied last modes to ell distinct empty first modes. On a Slater
occupation basis O*O is the projector onto configurations containing those
ell source modes and excluding the ell target modes. Thus exactly

 tau_N(O*O)=binomial(d-2ell,N-ell)/binomial(d,N)
           =(N)_ell(d-N)_ell/(d)_(2ell).                  (F2)

The adjoint partial trace sends the corresponding s-particle ell-body
operator to [(s)_ell/(N)_ell]O_(N,ell). This is the probability that a uniform
retained s-subset contains all ell specified particle positions; multilinear
matrix elements, or the antisymmetric permutation expansion, give exactly
the same coefficient for off-diagonal occupation moves. U(d) covariance
extends it to the complete ell component.

As in the symmetric derivation, likelihood partial trace Q=(D_s/D_N)T has
normalized-trace adjoint T*. Squaring the adjoint coefficient and taking the
norm ratio in (F2) gives

 lambda_ell=[(s)_ell/(N)_ell][(d-N)_ell/(d-s)_ell], ell<=s,
 lambda_ell=0, ell>s,                                     (F3)

where ell is restricted to the input decomposition. lambda_0=1. All factors
for ell>=1 are nonnegative and <=1. They decrease with the step index, and
therefore lambda_ell<=lambda_1=(d-2s)/[2(d-s)]<1/2.
Phi is scalar on the whole decomposition. The replacement map is identity
on ell=0 and zero on every ell>=1, so its comparison constant is exactly
1/(1-lambda_1)=2(d-s)/d. This proves (F1); it is optimal for this PARTICULAR
replacement map because ell=1 attains it. Optimality among all EB maps is
not asserted. The baseline L2-to-trace deficit calculation supplies the
operational bound, with no label or dimension factor.
