# Targeted prior-art and proof audit: irreducible reflection frame under SLD dressing

Date: 2026-10-06. Scope: focused validation of the parent/b_compute construction; no comprehensive priority claim.

## Exact question and interface

Can a faithful state sigma and Hermitian norm-one involutions A_j have ordinary commutant CI while their SLD-dressed operators T_sigma(A_j), with T_sigma(B)_ik = 2sqrt(p_i p_k)/(p_i+p_k) B_ik in the sigma eigenbasis, have a nontrivial common commutant? Parent's theorem constructs this in every dimension d>=3, with d rank-one reflections. See `notes/team_b_quantum/reflection_obstruction.md` for the full independent construction, including an explicit finite spectral-separation bound.

## Decisive exact prior

Dianne P. O'Leary, *Scaling symmetric positive definite matrices to prescribed row sums*, Linear Algebra and its Applications 370 (2003), 185–191, DOI 10.1016/S0024-3795(03)00387-2. Publisher primary abstract states existence and uniqueness for each specified sign pattern. Publication date shown on publisher record: 1 September 2003.

Primary author technical report: University of Maryland repository record https://drum.lib.umd.edu/items/10d813e9-b808-4f98-b4da-09be4bd75b1a. Read depth: seven-page report extracted by web tool, including full constructive proof in Section 2. The theorem and signed-scaling corollary are on printed PDF page 6. The theorem constructs a positive diagonal X with XWX 1=u for arbitrary real symmetric positive-definite W and u>0. The following signed extension conjugates by a diagonal sign matrix and establishes a solution in each orthant. This exactly supplies v_i(Cv)_i=1 by W=C, u=1, X=diag(v). The proof uses a homotopy from I to W and continuation of an ODE. Our strictly convex barrier proof is independent and simpler for the needed instance.

## Independent adversarial audit of parent's quantum theorem

No hidden flaw found in the written theorem. The checks below are derivations, not agreement-based external validation.

1. On a fixed orthant, f(v)=v^T C v/2-sum_i log|v_i| is strictly convex: its Hessian is C+diag(v_i^-2)>0. Coercivity at infinity follows from lambda_min(C)||v||^2/2 dominating d log ||v||; approach to a finite orthant boundary makes a logarithmic barrier diverge. Thus its unique minimizer exists, and stationary equations are exactly v_i(Cv)_i=1, even with signed offdiagonal products.
2. T_sigma(P_v)w = w/||v||^2 holds entry by entry for w=1 because (C Hadamard vv^T)w has i-th entry v_i(Cv)_i. Hermiticity makes the orthogonal rank-one projector P_w commute with every dressed operator.
3. Parent's explicit R=(16d^2+1)^2 gives maximum offdiagonal row sum at most epsilon=1/(4d^2). Therefore ||C-I||_2<=epsilon. Strong gradient monotonicity gives ||v_s-s||<=epsilon sqrt(d)/(1-epsilon). The stated inverse of the sign-column matrix V_0 and its Frobenius norm are correct. The perturbation norm is at most d/(4d^2-1), strictly below the lower singular-value bound 2/d, so the actual minimizers span.
4. The star overlaps are protected by |v_0^T v_j-(d-2)|<=d[(1-epsilon)^-2-1]<1 for every d>=3. Hence the nonorthogonality graph is connected.
5. Crucially the ordinary-commutant argument applies to arbitrary complex M. From MP_j=P_jM obtain Mv_j=lambda_j v_j and v_j^T M=lambda_j v_j^T, with the same scalar lambda_j. Nonzero overlap implies lambda_i=lambda_j by evaluating v_i^T Mv_j. Connectedness and spanning imply M=lambda I. There is no assumption that M is real or Hermitian.
6. X=(1-1/d)I+P_w is strictly positive. Its diagonal is exactly one, hence rho=sqrt(sigma)Xsqrt(sigma) is faithful with trace one and the same diagonal as sigma. It is distinct because its offdiagonal entries are sqrt(p_i p_j)/d. X commutes with the dressed frame, so the previously proved exact SLD fiber theorem gives identical scores.

The conclusion excludes a repair relying solely on original-frame irreducibility, norm bounds, rank-one projectors, or involutions. It does not establish failure for a fixed local tensor Pauli frame, balanced involutions, restricted Hamiltonian families or a high-temperature regime. Large spectral ratios are essential to this particular explicit proof.

## Targeted search and novelty limits

Queries included combinations of quantum score/score matching with irreducibility or counterexample; SLD/symmetric logarithmic derivative with commutant, irreducibility, reflection and identifiability; sech Schur multipliers with irreducible/commutant; and Cauchy Schur matrices with irreducibility. These bounded searches found no verified primary source stating this reflection-frame obstruction. This is negative search evidence only, not a novelty certificate.

Nearest verified quantum sources remain Dong–Leng 2026 Appendix A Theorem 15 (sufficient dressed commutant criterion, no implication that ordinary-frame irreducibility survives SLD dressing), and Carlen–Maas 2017 Theorem 5.3 (commutant/ergodicity for its detailed-balance semigroup interface). Neither read theorem supplies or excludes this reflection construction. Endpoint commutator-score algebra-generation identification from Shukla–Jayakumar–Lokhov 2026 and Dong–Leng Lemma 16 is a different dressing/interface.

Candidate novelty is the explicit quantum use of signed SPD scaling to create a shared SLD blind direction while preserving original-frame irreducibility, plus the finite R bound. Priority remains unresolved. Next falsifiable step: audit whether comparable failure is possible under the algebraic relations of fixed local X_i,Z_i or balanced involutions; direct substitution by arbitrary reflections would not preserve those relations.
