# A trace and sign-covariance obstruction to invisible logical flux

7 October 2026. Root deduction in cycle17. Candidate universal lemma with an
independent reconstruction requested from pde_spectrum9. This concerns exact
counterpart transition gaps. It does NOT prove disjoint frequency supports,
positive unpaired thermal mass, original Davies memory failure, or the
unit-check-weight model. No historical priority claim.

## Algebraic interface

Let v_1,...,v_d be nonzero binary syndrome vectors, d>=2, whose ONLY nonempty
linear dependence is v_1+...+v_d=0. Thus W=span(v_i) has dimension d-1. View
W as a subspace of F2^m, allowing redundant physical checks. Give the checks
independent variables h_1,...,h_m. For each syndrome s in any ambient binary
syndrome space U containing W, set E_s(h)=sum_a h_a s_a. For generic h all
these finitely many energies are distinct. Positive h makes E_0 the unique
minimum on W. All rational functions below are identities in Q(h).

In a fixed logical eta=+/- sector, let the commuting physical field operators
T_i act by translation v_i, with product_i T_i=eta I. Such a representation
exists by choosing the first d-1 translations as generators and the last as
eta times their product. Basis gauge choices do not change eigenvalues.
On each syndrome orbit s+W consider

    H_eta = diag(E_w(h)) - sum_i epsilon_i T_i.

This is the exact minimal-support field subfamily of a CSS Hamiltonian after
fixing independent X-check labels and a pure-X logical eigenvalue, provided
S is a nontrivial pure-X logical circuit. A minimum-support nontrivial logical
is one example: a proper logical subset contradicts minimum weight, and a
proper stabilizer subset can be removed to leave a smaller logical. Minimum
weight search is NOT required. As independently observed by pde_transport9,
fundamental circuits from a column basis of H_Z span ker H_Z; since the
X-stabilizer row space is a proper subspace of that kernel, one fundamental
circuit lies outside it. Gaussian elimination and quotient membership select
such a circuit in polynomial time from explicit check matrices. This is why
the circuit hypothesis, rather than a generic graph assumption, applies.
An additive fixed X-check-sector energy has no effect on the argument.

For each w in an orbit, the simple eigenvalue E_w at zero field has an analytic
branch lambda_(eta,w) near zero for a fixed nondegenerate h. The coefficient
of the squarefree monomial epsilon_1...epsilon_d is

    [epsilon_1...epsilon_d] lambda_(eta,w)
          = (-1)^d eta t_w(h),

    t_w(h) = sum_(permutations pi of {1,...,d})
               product_(j=1,...,d-1)
                  [E_w(h)-E_(w+v_pi(1)+...+v_pi(j))(h)]^(-1).       (1)

## Direct derivation of the coefficient

Use a normalized eigenvector with its w-coordinate equal to one. In the
other coordinates the eigenvalue equation recursively substitutes resolvents
(E_w-E_u)^(-1), fields, and the correction lambda-E_w. A squarefree closed
walk using a proper nonempty subset of labels would require the sum of those
v_i to be zero; this is excluded. Thus every lower-order squarefree correction
lambda-E_w vanishes, and normalization/correction terms cannot contribute to
the full squarefree monomial. Its only contributions are the d! walks using
each label once. Each intermediate proper prefix has nonzero syndrome, so
all resolvents exist generically. The closed product of T_i is eta; the d
minus signs from the fields supply (-1)^d. This proves (1) without assuming a
nondegenerate FULL Hamiltonian across logical sectors.

## Three identities

First, for positive h, every denominator in t_0 is strictly negative. Hence
all d! summands have the same sign (-1)^(d-1), and

    t_0(h) != 0.                                                 (2)

Second, put D_s h = ((-1)^(s_a) h_a)_a. The elementary identity

    E_(s+w)(h)-E_s(h)=E_w(D_s h)

implies

    t_s(h)=t_0(D_s h),
    t_(s+w)(h)=t_w(D_s h).                                       (3)

These are identities of rational functions. Sign flips need not remain in
the positive physical parameter region; they are an algebraic device. A
nonzero rational function cannot vanish on an open positive box.

Third, the trace of each finite orbit block is sum_(w in orbit) E_w and is
independent of every epsilon_i and eta. Comparing squarefree coefficients in
the sum of the simple eigenvalue branches gives

    sum_(w in W) t_w(h)=0.                                       (4)

## At least two visible physical sites

Define the subgroup

    K={w in W : t_0(D_w h)=t_0(h) as rational functions}.

Closure and inverses follow from D_u D_v=D_(u+v). If K=W, identities (3)-(4)
give |W|t_0=0, contradicting (2). Therefore K is a proper subgroup.
For a physical site q, define

    Delta_q(h)=t_0(h)-t_(v_q)(h).

Delta_q is nonzero exactly when v_q is outside K. At least one generator
must be outside K because the v_i span W. In fact at least TWO must be
outside: if only v_q were outside, the relation sum_i v_i=0 would express it
as a sum of elements of K, a contradiction. For each of these fixed q and
EVERY ambient syndrome s, equation (3) gives

    t_s(h)-t_(s+v_q)(h)=Delta_q(D_s h) != 0                         (5)

as a rational function. Thus at least two physical sites have a nonzero
counterpart-gap splitting coefficient simultaneously for all syndrome labels.
This is stronger than a ground-state splitting assertion and does not use an
unproved noncancelling dominant pole.

## Consequence and exact remaining obstacle

For any such q, the difference between the two counterpart sector gaps

    [lambda_(+,s)-lambda_(+,s+v_q)]
       -[lambda_(-,s)-lambda_(-,s+v_q)]

has the nonzero squarefree coefficient 2(-1)^d Delta_q(D_s h). Consequently
it is not the zero analytic function. For each fixed finite CSS code there
are positive independently weighted penalties and arbitrarily small positive
fields where every one of these finitely many counterpart gaps splits. The
other site fields may be restored: the identified nonzero Taylor monomial
on their zero-field subfamily remains a nonzero coefficient of the full
family. There is no uniform lower bound on splitting magnitude here, no
polynomial-cost parameter selector, and no guarantee at exactly equal check
weights. At zero field the physical X_q acts on each counterpart pair with
unit modulus; by continuity its counterpart matrix element stays nonzero in
a sufficiently small finite-system neighborhood, whose size is not uniform.

Crucially, an exact frequency from one counterpart pair may still equal a
frequency from a DIFFERENT pair in the other logical sector. Logical
observables can mix those eigenvectors. The trace argument provides neither
an obstruction to such global matching nor a lower bound on the unpaired
Gibbs mass required by cycle16's leakage criterion. A generic counterpart
splitting assertion is therefore insufficient to infer Davies leakage or
memory failure. This is the exact next spectral/matching obstacle.

Independent follow-up: pde_spectrum9 found that first-order visibility at
EVERY site is false on a tree incidence circuit; even-degree interior sites
can have Delta_q identically zero. Their separate derivation will determine
whether paths make the at-least-two bound sharp. Do not replace (5) with an
unqualified all-sites theorem.

## Downstream use and provenance

This changes the spectral investigation from an uncertain dominant-pole
argument to a proved algebraic reduction for counterpart gaps, while exposing
that OTHER-pair matching is the remaining issue. pde_transport9 is investigating
whether its weighted outflow criterion can use a weaker matching obstruction.
Inputs are the exact CSS circuit interface and the cycle16 source geometry,
not a new claim attributed to BDL. The trace, analytic perturbation and binary
sign identities are standard tools; the application is our deduction with
novelty UNKNOWN. No large recursive Hamiltonian was diagonalized.
