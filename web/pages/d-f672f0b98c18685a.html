# A canonical connected word exposes the missing cancellation rewrite

7 October 2026. Independent rounding_prior reconstruction, following root's simpler canonical candidate. This is an obstruction to a literal inference in arXiv2411.02384v3 Eq. (4.8), not a counterexample to spectral stability. The finite Hamiltonian below is manifestly stable under small perturbations. A source revision, unstated collection equivalence or controlled rewrite may repair the proof. None is claimed found here.

## Source rules being tested

Section 3.2 makes collection addition pointwise and commutators use word concatenation. Section 4.2 partitions a word into V+, V-, M or D by its check labels. A word with only + and g is assigned to V+ even if its underlying operator happens to kill the full ground space. Section 4.3 first solves a global P/off-P cancellation equation, then states in Eq. (4.8) that V at the next step receives only the listed higher commutators. Those higher-order bounds are load-bearing for the rapid KAM decay.

The test below follows the canonical Section 3.3 initialization; it is not an arbitrary unproved reachable collection. Every initial word is a connected singleton. It avoids the earlier rejected toy with independent checks Zi,Zj, whose two-check ghost was disconnected.

## Exact canonical input

Use two data qubits, checks

    C_i=Z1 Z2, C_j=Z2,
    G_i=(I+C_i)/2, E_i=(I-C_i)/2,
    G_j=(I+C_j)/2, E_j=(I-C_j)/2,
    H0=E_i+E_j,
    Z(0)=mu Z1 + epsilon X1.

The checks overlap at qubit 2 and are independent. The ground space is |00>. There are no logical qubits in this two-qubit factor; one may tensor it with an unrelated large-distance code if a finite-distance code family is desired. Its TQO-II is satisfied with radius one: C_j is sufficient to complete the stabilizer algebra on the support of C_i. No nontrivial assumption about the May2026 code is involved.

Both perturbation Paulis have actual support {qubit1}; the minimum connected cover is that singleton, whose incident check set is exactly {i}. The initial decomposition therefore gives

    M_i=mu G_i Z1 G_i=mu C_j G_i,
    D_i=mu E_i Z1 E_i=−mu C_j E_i,
    V_+=epsilon R_i,  V_−=epsilon R_i*,
    R_i=E_i X1 G_i.

All four words have underlying set {i}. The unsandwiched operators are supported on qubit1, as required by Eq. (3.7). All are legal connected words. The appearance of C_j in the equivalent displayed formulas is algebraic; it does not change the underlying qubit1 representation using Z1.

On the global ground space, M_i acts as mu and kills the raised-i state. Lemma 5.1's scalar is q=−mu, so Delta=−mu. Admissible D_i products act on R_i as −mu C_j R_i. The resummed source generator consequently is

    A_+ = i epsilon R_i (1−mu−mu C_j)^(-1),
    A_− = A_+*,
    A = i epsilon (R_i−R_i*) (1−mu−mu C_j)^(-1).

For 0<mu<1/2, the series converges and all denominators are positive. Since R_i C_j=E_i X1 Z1 G_i, the rational function of C_j can be represented by an underlying operator on qubit1. Thus A_+ remains in the legal singleton i+ class. This verifies class membership directly, rather than inferring it merely from global support.

## Exact residual

In the C_j=+1 sector the actual raising gap of H0+mu Z1 is 1−2mu and the source generator uses exactly that denominator. In the C_j=−1 sector the actual gap is 1+2mu, while the source denominator is 1. Therefore

    i[H0+D_i+M_i,A] + V = −2mu epsilon X1 E_j.

The raising component is −2mu epsilon R_i E_j and the lowering component is its adjoint. The residual has zero off-ground block, so the defining equations (4.4–4.5) are indeed satisfied. It is nevertheless nonzero as an operator and as the literal pure-word collection. In particular,

    R_i E_j = E_i [X1 (I−Z1)/2] G_i,

because C_j G_i=Z1 G_i. Its unsandwiched operator has support only qubit1; the prescribed word is still singleton i+. It is assigned to V+ by the source's stated label rule, not to D. Likewise its adjoint is a V- word. Thus global P-block cancellation is not enough to remove the linear contribution from the prescribed V collection.

The explicit rational test chooses mu=1/8, epsilon=1/1024. In computational order 00,01,10,11,

    H0+mu Z1 = diag(mu, 2+mu, 1−mu, 1−mu).

R_i has nonzero entries (row2,col0) and (row1,col3). Write A=iB with B real antisymmetric, use the source denominator on each j sector, and evaluate −[H,B]+epsilon X1. Its only nonzero entries are −1/4096 at (1,3) and (3,1), exactly −X1 E_j/4096. Column 0 is zero. The independent stdlib checker executes this matrix calculation using rational arithmetic.

## What this proves and the missing repair

This establishes a canonical initial, connected-word obstruction to the displayed Eq. (4.8) inference with the literal Section 3.2 collection operations and Section 4.2 classification. It does not show that the prior ground-band theorem is false. It does not violate the ghost scalar lemma: that lemma is a statement after right multiplication by P, precisely where the residual vanishes.

For this toy a repair is easy: write R_i E_j as a word on {i,j} with i+,j=e. It is then a D term and only costs one extra label. The source needs a general version with a uniform intensive-norm/locality estimate and a precise rule for replacing the literal collection before deriving the flow. A mere operator identity or existence of some representation is insufficient to preserve that flow.

Under the stated TQO-II, a filler is constrained to a check-graph radius ell|S|, but the number of checks in that ball may be exponential in |S|. Appending every buffer projector to turn a global annihilator into D can therefore increase a word's cardinality exponentially and lose the exponential word-norm budget. A norm-controlled rewrite must avoid that naive cost or explicitly strengthen/charge its premise. No such general repair is adopted here.

Consequently I certify the local support/ghost/norm transfers, and the root CZ bridge as a conditional reduction to the *published* theorem. I leave a full internally reconstructed KAM proof, and hence a new unconditional declared-incidence theorem derived solely from the displayed flow, OPEN. The next falsifiable step is to supply and test the missing rewrite on this canonical toy, then prove its family-uniform cost under exactly the existing radius hypothesis.
