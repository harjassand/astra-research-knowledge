EXACT ORTHOGONAL-SECTOR ENTROPY WITNESS
2026-10-08 | isolated reusable module | /root/sol_kls_consequences

Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE elementary lemma. The strengthening
in this task was proposed by root. Its orthogonal-block Jensen mechanism
is routine; primary priority was not searched and no novelty is claimed.
No individual-sector energy bound, thermodynamic approximation, or
commuting approximation to the within-sector Hamiltonian is required.

Let H be a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, B self-adjoint, and {P_m}
orthogonal projectors summing to I with [B,P_m]=0. Let psi be normalized.
Set p_m=||P_m psi||^2 and select any subset I of sectors with
q=sum_{m in I}p_m>0. Let P_I=sum_{m in I}P_m, psi_I=P_I psi/sqrt(q),
E_I=<psi_I,B psi_I>, and p_I,max=max_{m in I}p_m. For every tau>0,

 Tr_{P_I H} exp(-tau B)
       >=(q/p_I,max)exp(-tau E_I)
       >=(q/p_max)exp(-tau E_I), p_max=max_all_m p_m.     (W1)

Equivalently the prefactor is the reciprocal of the maximum CONDITIONAL
sector probability p'_max=p_I,max/q. Zero-probability sectors may simply
be omitted from the lower sum. A countably infinite direct-sum extension
is possible under domain/finite-energy conditions, but is not needed for
this finite theorem and no uncharged infinite-state input is asserted.

Proof. For p_m>0 let psi_m=P_m psi/sqrt(p_m) and E_m=<psi_m,B psi_m>.
The spectral measure of B in this normalized vector is a probability,
so convexity of exp gives
Tr_{P_m H}exp(-tau B) >= <psi_m,exp(-tau B)psi_m> >= exp(-tau E_m).
Sum these inequalities over m in I, then use p_m<=p_I,max and scalar
Jensen with probabilities p_m/q:

 sum exp(-tau E_m)
 >=p_I,max^{-1}sum p_m exp(-tau E_m)
 >=(q/p_I,max)exp[-tau sum p_m E_m/q].

Commutation is precisely what identifies sum p_m E_m/q with E_I; no
cross-sector contribution remains. This proves(W1). It works even when
some E_m are arbitrarily large or negative in the finite spectrum.

Corollary. If along a sequence q stays positive, E_I is uniformly bounded
above, and p_I,max->0, then the restricted partition diverges. A coherent
trial with vanishing total rate energy can therefore force a divergent
thermal trace when its conserved-sector probabilities spread, without
controlling the energy in every contributing sector.

In the XXZ application, q->1,E_I=O(1/L), and the Poisson-binomial Fourier
bound gives p_max<=sqrt(pi)/(2sqrt(2V_L)),V_L~vL>0. Hence the macroscopic
partition is at least c sqrt(L). All number projectors commute EXACTLY
with B_L. This is a trace witness, not a free conditioning operation on
an unknown encoder input or an efficiently prepared test-state claim.

Bias must be charged. At parameters shifted by O(1/L), E_I can have a
nonzero bounded limit and multiplies the lower bound by exp(-tau bias).
If E_I grows, divergence is not automatic: retain that exponential in(W1).
The lemma does not determine exact partition asymptotics, mixing weights,
sector eigenvectors, total-memory cost, labels, circuits or acquisition.
