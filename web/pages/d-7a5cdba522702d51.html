ROBUST COHERENT EB COMPARISON UNDER APPROXIMATE SU(2) COVARIANCE
8 October 2026

Status: proved transfer lemma, conditional on the internally proved-candidate
exact-covariance all-mode comparison in ALL_MODE_RACAH_PROOF.txt. The transfer
itself is elementary and dimension-explicit. The all-mode constant C is finite
but not numerically effective. Effective versions inherit C=9, 12, or 16 on
the corresponding already-proved multipole bands. External validation of the
all-mode input theorem remains UNKNOWN.

RESULT IN ONE LINE

Let d=n+1, n=2j>=1, and let Phi_*:M_d->M_d be a bistochastic channel with an
actual two-output CPTP extension Lambda_*:M_d->M_(d^2) whose two marginals
are exactly Phi_*. If the marginal is delta-covariant in diamond norm,

  delta = sup_g || R_g o Phi_* o R_(g^-1) - Phi_* ||_diamond,

then twirling Lambda_* gives an exactly SU(2)-covariant compatible marginal
barPhi_*, and the fixed spin-coherent EB channel Psi_coh satisfies

  (I-Psi_coh)^2 <= C (I-Phi_s) + C sqrt(2 delta) I,       (A)

as a quadratic-form inequality on normalized Hilbert-Schmidt space. Here
Phi_s=(Phi+Phi^dagger)/2 is the Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint part of the
Heisenberg marginal. More sharply, for any family F of Hermitian likelihoods

  rho_H=(I+H)/d,  tau(H)=0,  ||H||_infinity<=theta<1,
  r=sup_(H in F) ||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau),

the same single coherent EB measurement obeys

  sup_(H in F) T(Psi_coh,*(rho_H),rho_H)
       <= (1/2) sqrt( C theta r + C delta theta^2 ).       (B)

No factor depending on d occurs in (A) or (B) with the stated diamond-norm
defect. If instead the only joint defect supplied is an induced trace-norm
defect with normalized trace on the d^2-dimensional output, conversion to a
marginal defect costs a factor d; see Section 4. The coherent POVM itself
has an exact finite positive Euler realization with at most
d(2d-1)^2 outcomes. This is a growing implementation cost, not a free
decoder twirl.

1. INTERFACE AND THE JOINT TWIRL

Write R_g(X)=U_j(g) X U_j(g)^* for the spin-j conjugation channel. On two
outputs use R_g^(2)=R_g tensor R_g. Assume Lambda_* is CPTP and

  Tr_2 o Lambda_* = Phi_*,     Tr_1 o Lambda_* = Phi_*.

The extension need not be covariant or symmetric. Define the Haar averages

  barLambda_* = int_G R_g^(2) o Lambda_* o R_(g^-1) dg,
  barPhi_*    = int_G R_g o Phi_* o R_(g^-1) dg,

with normalized Haar measure. Each integrand is CPTP, so barLambda_* is a
CPTP map. Invariance of Haar measure makes it exactly covariant. Partial
trace commutes with the simultaneous output rotations, hence both marginals
of barLambda_* equal barPhi_*. Thus compatibility is retained exactly.
Since Phi_* is bistochastic, so is barPhi_*.

Define the marginal covariance defect

  delta_Phi = sup_g ||R_g o Phi_* o R_(g^-1)-Phi_*||_diamond.

The triangle inequality for the Bochner integral gives

  ||barPhi_*-Phi_*||_diamond <= delta_Phi.                 (1)

If instead the supplied quantity is the joint defect

  delta_Lambda = sup_g ||R_g^(2) o Lambda_* o R_(g^-1)-Lambda_*||_diamond,

then partial trace is a CPTP postprocessing and diamond norm is contractive,
and the same integral estimate gives

  ||barLambda_*-Lambda_*||_diamond <= delta_Lambda,
  delta_Phi<=delta_Lambda.

Therefore (B) remains valid with delta_Lambda in place of delta. A bound on
marginal covariance alone is sufficient; the
extension may have a larger defect (or no useful joint covariance bound).
Conversely, a joint defect bound always supplies the needed marginal bound.

The same statements hold with ordinary induced 1->1 trace norm in place of
diamond norm if no reference system is part of the promise. Diamond norm is
used above to make reference-system stability explicit. No inference from
capacity or broadcasting accuracy to delta_Phi is made.

2. TRANSFER OF THE EXACT-COVARIANT COMPARISON

Input theorem. For every exactly SU(2)-covariant, bistochastic, compatible
spin-j marginal barPhi, the canonical coherent measure/prepare channel

  Psi_coh(X)=d int_G Tr(P_g X) P_g dg,
  P_g=U_j(g)|j,j><j,j|U_j(g)^*,

satisfies on all of M_d

  (I-Psi_coh)^2 <= C (I-barPhi).                          (2)

This is the all-mode candidate of ALL_MODE_RACAH_PROOF.txt, with
C=max(4,1024/(81 inf_(n,l) Q_(n,l))). Its proof establishes that C is
finite but does not compute a usable numerical value. For the explicitly
admitted bands in RESULT.txt / COHERENT_RACAH_LIFT.txt, use their effective
constants instead. The transfer below does not strengthen or independently
validate this exact-covariance theorem.

Use normalized Hilbert-Schmidt inner product <X,Y>_tau=tau(X^*Y). The
diamond bound (1), by duality, implies for the Heisenberg maps

  ||barPhi-Phi||_(infinity->infinity) <= delta.            (3)

Both Heisenberg maps are unital, trace-preserving CP maps. Hence each is a
contraction on Schatten infinity and Schatten one: the first follows from
unital complete positivity; the second follows by duality because its
Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint is also unital CP. Therefore, for Delta=barPhi-Phi,

  ||Delta||_(infinity->infinity)<=delta,
  ||Delta||_(1->1)<=2.

Schatten Riesz-Thorin interpolation gives

  ||Delta||_(2->2,tau) <= sqrt(2 delta).                  (4)

Normalization by tau does not change this same-dimension 2->2 operator
norm. Exact covariance acts multiplicity-free on the spin-j operator space,
so barPhi is Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint (its sector scalars are real).
Let Phi_s=(Phi+Phi^dagger)/2. For every X,

  Re <X,(I-Phi)X>_tau = <X,(I-Phi_s)X>_tau.

Using (2)-(4), for every X,

  <X,(I-Psi_coh)^2 X>_tau
   <= C <X,(I-barPhi)X>_tau
   <= C <X,(I-Phi_s)X>_tau + C sqrt(2 delta)||X||_(2,tau)^2.

This is (A). I-Phi_s is positive semidefinite: Phi_s is self-adjoint and
the average of two Hilbert-Schmidt contractions, so its spectrum is in
[-1,1]. Thus (A) is a genuine self-adjoint operator inequality even though
the original approximately covariant Phi need not be self-adjoint.

For the sharper bounded-likelihood estimate, do not use (4). For Hermitian
H with ||H||_infinity<=theta, (3) directly gives

  |<H,(Phi-barPhi)H>_tau|
    <= ||H||_(1,tau) ||(Phi-barPhi)H||_infinity
    <= delta theta^2.                                    (5)

Also

  <H,(I-Phi)H>_tau
    <= ||H||_infinity ||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau)
    <= theta r.

Combining (2) and (5) yields

  ||(I-Psi_coh)H||_(2,tau)^2 <= C(theta r+delta theta^2).

Since ||X||_(1,tau)<=||X||_(2,tau), and Psi_coh is bistochastic,

  T(Psi_coh,*(rho_H),rho_H)
    = (1/2)||(I-Psi_coh)H||_(1,tau),

which proves (B). This also shows why a fixed bound on H is part of the
interface: the covariance perturbation enters as delta theta^2.

3. DIRECTED DEFECTS AND ACQUISITION

The proof can use a weaker, family-specific certificate. Define

  e_F = sup_(H in F) ||(barPhi-Phi)H||_(1,tau).

Then the same argument gives

  sup_F T(Psi_coh,*(rho_H),rho_H)
       <= (1/2)sqrt(C theta (r+e_F)).                     (6)

For example, if an explicit average commutator certificate is supplied,

  eta = int_G ||Phi o R_g - R_g o Phi||_(infinity->1,tau) dg,

then unitary invariance of normalized trace norm and the twirl formula imply
e_F<=theta eta. Equation (6) becomes

  error <= (1/2)sqrt(C theta r + C theta^2 eta).

This directional form can be materially better than a worst-case diamond
certificate. In all cases the certificate must be supplied or acquired;
the theorem gives no sample complexity for estimating a supremum over G,
and a bounded Holevo quantity does not imply a small covariance defect.

4. DIMENSION AND IMPLEMENTATION COSTS

(i) With a standard (unnormalized) diamond norm on the channel
M_d->M_d, (1)-(B) have no hidden d factor. The joint version uses the
standard diamond norm for maps M_d->M_(d^2); partial trace is contractive.
The representation dimension enters through d=2j+1 and through the
coherent measurement, not through the robustness constant.

(ii) If a joint covariance defect is instead reported in an induced map norm
with normalized trace norm on the joint output (and the same input norm in
the numerator and denominator), partial trace costs d. Indeed,
for Y in M_(d^2),

  ||Tr_2 Y||_(1,tau_d) <= d ||Y||_(1,tau_(d^2)).

Thus a joint normalized-output 1->1 defect delta_Lambda,norm implies only
delta_Phi,norm<=d delta_Lambda,norm. A marginal normalized 1->1 defect
does not incur this conversion. This is a norm-convention factor, not a
representation-theoretic loss in (B).

(iii) The comparator used here is the canonical coherent EB channel itself;
we never twirl an arbitrary EB decoder. The existing construction gives an
exact positive Euler cubature with at most

  d(2n+1)^2 = d(2d-1)^2

outcomes. This is O(d^3) outcomes and increasing apparatus/precision cost.
The theorem does not say the Haar twirl of Lambda_* is free to implement:
it is an existence witness preserving compatibility. If a physical
implementation tildePsi has diamond error at most xi from Psi_coh, its
output trace-distance contribution is at most xi/2 and is added to (B).
No cost for certifying delta, realizing arbitrary group rotations, or
compiling finite-precision effects is included.

5. EXACT SCOPE, LIMITS, AND FALSIFIERS

Required exactly: finite spin-j representation; bistochastic marginal;
an actual common two-output extension with both marginals exactly Phi;
the stated covariance-defect certificate; and the exact-covariant coherent
comparison (2). Covariance is the only relaxed structural promise here.
If compatibility itself is approximate, an additional marginal mismatch
must be charged before (2) applies. The coherent POVM is the same for every
state family and does not depend on Lambda_*.

For an effective version, restrict every H to one of the existing bands and
replace C by that band's explicit constant. For the all-mode statement, C
is universal but non-effective because the scalar infimum in the exact
input proof is not numerically bounded. Neither version establishes the
sharp comparison constant 2 or the unrestricted bounded-Holevo N93 claim.

The transfer is falsified by any error in: (a) covariance of the joint Haar
average, (b) compatibility of its marginals, (c) the diamond-to-marginal
contraction, (d) the exact input comparison, or (e) the perturbation bounds
(3)-(5). For a quantitative counterexample to the claimed linear delta
likelihood term, one would need a bistochastic channel pair with small
operator-norm difference but a bounded-H likelihood quadratic form larger
than delta theta^2; inequality (5) rules this out under the stated norm.
This is a conditional robust transfer result, not an independent audit of
the all-mode Racah proof or an empirical covariance-acquisition protocol.
