The contract distinctions matter throughout: N18 is expected input cost;
N32 is a two-use spectral-support witness; N40 is an expected-cost smooth
packet; N62 is a source-derived candidate theorem for parallel, pure-null,
zero-false-alarm detection with exact accepted-input support. N62 excludes
probe acquisition/preparation, postselection retries, drive/reference work,
precision, detector implementation, idlers, and general adaptation. The
N32 linked proof package was absent in the supplied archive. Leaves 01 and 02
reconstruct N62 internally but do not provide external validation.

Closest primary literature and the exact overlap

- Datta, “Min- and max-relative entropies and a new entanglement monotone,”
  arXiv:0803.2770 (submitted 2008-03-19; IEEE TIT 2009),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.2770. Relevant to one-shot support-based exact
  type-I-zero discrimination; it does not supply N18/N62's resource-normalized
  rates or all-separable hard-support comparison. (Leaf 14.)
- Huang and Lupo, “Quantum Hypothesis Testing for Exoplanet Detection,”
  arXiv:2106.00488 (submitted 2021-06-01; PRL 2021),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00488. A direct physical pure-null sensing
  example with an exact zero-false-positive spatial mode; it has a different
  imaging model and does not optimize hard-energy channel probes. (Leaf 14.)
- Hasenöhrl and Wolf, “‘Interaction-Free’ Channel Discrimination,”
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-022-01175-z (published 2022-04-01). Pure
  fixed-point channel structure and adaptive interaction-free protocols are
  close structural precedents, but the resource/objective differs from the
  fixed N62 support-rate contract. (Leaves 14 and 10.)
- Harrow, Hassidim, Leung, and Watrous, “Adaptive versus non-adaptive
  strategies for quantum channel discrimination,” arXiv:0909.0256 (posted
  2009-09-01; PRA 2010), https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.0256. Gives a general
  adaptive separation for another channel pair; it warns against a universal
  parallelization claim but does not refute the pure-null N62 flattening
  theorem or the leaf-10 commuting special case. (Leaf 10.)
- Cooney, Mosonyi, and Wilde, “Strong converse exponents for a quantum
  channel discrimination problem and quantum-feedback-assisted
  communication,” arXiv:1408.3373 (posted 2014-08-14; CMP 2016),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.3373. Asymptotic replacer-channel Stein/strong
  converse setting; it does not state the exact finite-use hard-support,
  zero-false-alarm contract. (Leaf 10.)
- Berta, Hirche, Kaur, and Wilde, “Composite hypothesis testing and its
  applications to quantum information theory,” arXiv:1709.07268 (submitted
  2017-09-21; CMP 2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07268. Composite testing
  framework relevant to nuisance sets; not the shared-group finite hard
  support minimax identity reconstructed here. (Leaves 05, 14, 17.)
- Suzuki, “Nuisance parameter problem in quantum estimation theory: General
  formulation and qubit examples,” arXiv:1905.04733 (submitted 2019-05-12;
  J. Phys. A 2020), https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04733. Nuisance estimation
  context, not an exact-null detection result. (Leaf 14.)
- Shiraiwa and Kukita, “Limits of independent and identical measurements for
  quantum illumination with an unknown return phase,” arXiv:2608.13997
  (submitted 2026-08-14), https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13997. An unknown-phase
  QI result in a restricted i.i.d.-measurement regime; it does not settle
  collective hard-support detection. (Leaf 14.)

Energy, coding, implementation, and error-correction context

- Becker, Datta, Lami, and Rouzé, “Energy-constrained discrimination of
  unitaries, quantum speed limits, and a Gaussian Solovay-Kitaev theorem,”
  arXiv:2006.06659 (submitted 2020-06-11; PRL 2021),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06659. Energy-constrained unitary discrimination
  is adjacent but differs from N62's nonunitary pure-null effect and exact
  support comparison. (Leaves 01, 06, 14, 18.)
- Bardhan, García-Patrón, Wilde, and Winter, “Strong converse for the
  classical capacity of optical quantum communication channels,”
  arXiv:1401.4161 (submitted 2014-01-16; IEEE TIT 2015),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.4161. Photon-number occupation constraints use
  cutoff/shadow control; they do not imply exact support for every accepted
  probe. (Leaves 01 and 14.)
- Shannon, “A Mathematical Theory of Communication,” Bell System Technical
  Journal 27 (1948), https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb00917.x.
  Standard DMC coding supports the outer Z-channel consequence in leaf 09;
  the resulting positive-rate code is not N62's reliability function.
- Bärtschi and Eidenbenz, “Deterministic Preparation of Dicke States,”
  arXiv:1904.07358 (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07358; and “Short-Depth
  Circuits for Dicke State Preparation,” arXiv:2207.09998 (2022),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.09998. Circuit-model primitives support the
  symmetric-state finite-depth construction in leaf 07; they do not supply
  energy-conserving drive/reference work or hardware performance.
- Marvian and Spekkens, “Modes of asymmetry: the application of harmonic
  analysis to symmetric quantum dynamics and quantum reference frames,”
  arXiv:1312.0680 (2013), https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0680. Covariant
  processing preserves the absence of time-translation coherence modes;
  leaf 18 supplies the detector-specific finite qutrit bound. (Leaf 18.)
- Kessler et al., “Quantum Error Correction for Metrology,” arXiv:1310.3260
  (submitted 2013-10-11; PRL 2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.3260; Zhou et
  al., “Achieving the Heisenberg limit in quantum metrology using quantum
  error correction,” Nature Communications 9, 78 (2018),
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-02510-3. These concern parameter
  estimation under stated noise/control assumptions; they do not establish a
  gain for N62's exact-null hard-support objective or a free syndrome/reset
  ledger. (Leaves 14 and 18.)
- Dooley, Munro, and Nemoto, “Quantum metrology including state preparation
  and readout times,” arXiv:1608.00721 (submitted 2016; PRA 2016),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.00721. Contains the dephasing GHZ-versus-product
  QFI formulas reconstructed in leaf 16. That leaf's model and control-cost
  threshold are explicitly scoped; no novelty is claimed.

interaction-free discrimination, nuisance/composite testing, QI, energy

bounded search did not identify a source matching the complete N18 or N62
interface. This is only a bounded search outcome. Correctness, historical
priority, physical feasibility, and external expert validation remain
separate unresolved questions.
