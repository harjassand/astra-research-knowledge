# Erasure-aware molecular-history comparison and its exact scope

## 1. One common physical experiment

Let rho be the fixed input density operator. Include initial motion, the physical
bath and any purification reference in the input description. V_A and V_B are
isometries into the SAME final space describing the two actual controlled
histories, with a fixed relative phase reference. The ideal controlled dilation
is |A><A| tensor V_A + |B><B| tensor V_B; the label starts in |+>.

Let P be one common survival/acceptance projector on the final non-label space.
The survival flag and final label X/Y measurement commute. A physical instrument
can use a Naimark space, but that common instrument is additional supplied
structure, not something determined by two channel marginals. Define

    p_j = Tr(P V_j rho V_j†),    q_j=1-p_j,
    z_s = Tr(P V_A rho V_B†),
    z_l = Tr((I-P) V_A rho V_B†),
    L = Tr(V_A rho V_B†) = z_s+z_l,
    S = (p_A+p_B)/2,    Q=1-S.

These are probabilities and complex amplitudes for the same input/instrument.
They are dimensionless. Branch channels calibrated with an unrelated bath
distribution or different reset cannot silently supply these quantities.

The probability of acceptance is S. For S>0 the accepted label coherence is

    m = 2 z_s/(p_A+p_B) = z_s/S.                         (1)

Thus accepted fringe contrast is not the unconditional overlap. In particular,
|m| <= 2 sqrt(p_A p_B)/(p_A+p_B); unequal survival alone can reduce it.

## 2. Sharp completion disk

For every finite-dimensional experiment above,

    |z_s| <= sqrt(p_A p_B),
    |L-z_s| <= sqrt(q_A q_B).                            (2)

Given only p_A,p_B,z_s satisfying the first inequality, the second inequality
describes the EXACT possible set of L, if each output sector may have dimension
at least two. It is not merely a one-sided loose error bound.

Proof. Use Hilbert-Schmidt vectors A_j=P V_j sqrt(rho) and
B_j=(I-P)V_j sqrt(rho). Their squared norms are p_j and q_j. Their inner products
are z_s and z_l, respectively. Cauchy-Schwarz proves (2). For sharpness take a
pure input and normalized branch output vectors in C² direct-sum C². For
0<p_A<1 and any z_l with |z_l|<=sqrt(q_A q_B), choose

    v_A=(sqrt(p_A),0,sqrt(q_A),0),
    v_B=(conj(z_s)/sqrt(p_A), sqrt(p_B-|z_s|²/p_A),
         conj(z_l)/sqrt(q_A), sqrt(q_B-|z_l|²/q_A)).

They have norm one, branch survival probabilities p_A,p_B, and overlaps z_s,z_l
in the respective sectors. Each one-vector isometry can be completed to a
unitary on a larger common space. Boundary cases follow directly or by limits.
The entire disk lies in |L|<=1 since
sqrt(p_A p_B)+sqrt(q_A q_B)<=1. QED.

For equal survival p_A=p_B=1-q, this reduces to

    L in (1-q)m + disk(radius q).                        (3)

The source target Im L is therefore only certified from the accepted fringe
once the missing coherence allowance q and apparatus errors are paid. For
unequal survival the geometric radius sqrt(q_A q_B) is smaller than Q.
There is no theorem that lost histories have zero, positive or molecularly
irrelevant coherence just because they were discarded.

Example: p_A=p_B=.99, z_s=.99 and z_l=-.01 give m=1 but L=.98 and full X-failure
probability .01. Perfect surviving fringes need not certify perfect full control.

Confidence conversion must optimize the union of these disks over ONE valid
joint confidence region for p_A,p_B,z_s. Empirical ratios, separately favorable
fits or a conditional confidence interval without the attempt count are not
such a region. No such experimental region is supplied by the packet.

## 3. Counting erasures as failures safely transfers the N791 bound

Define one outcome per attempted trial: Z=1 if the trial is erased OR if it
survives and gives label X=-1. Otherwise Z=0. Then

    Pr(Z=1)=Q+(S-Re z_s)/2,
    p_full=(1-Re L)/2,
    Pr(Z=1)-p_full=(Q+Re z_l)/2
                     >=(Q-sqrt(q_A q_B))/2>=0.           (4)

The flag and label observables commuting is essential to this joint instrument
calculation. It holds even for outcome-dependent, state-dependent erasure;
discarding those trials generally does not preserve the bound. For equal
branch survival, (4) is sharp. Branch-resolved calibration can instead use the
tighter disk (2), with its own joint statistical budget.

The conservative dominance itself also holds when actual label mixing prevents
the controlled-dilation description above: for ANY valid final joint state,
Z=1 is the union of X=-1 and erasure, and
Pr(Z=1)-Pr(X=-1)=Pr(erasure AND X=+1)>=0. In that more general case the
branch-rate disk is not inferred from separately calibrated histories. The
complete actual readout still needs the uniform comparison to the intended
ideal experiment below.

This statement alone bounds only the actual full fringe. To identify it with
N791's ideal echo, retain ALL of that result's original hypotheses:

* A finite collar S of dimension d and a genuine collar/outside tensor
  factorization in the intended preserved sector.
* A fixed compiled witness and intended Hermitian residual
  R=R_S tensor I, with true support/tails charged, ||R||<=Rcap and
  ||[H,R]||<=2J||R||. Lambda=2J+Rcap>0.
* The intended coherent echo E(t)=exp(iHt)exp(-i(H+R)t), with
  0<t<2/Lambda and the necessary coherent branches, label preparation/readout,
  gate implementations and phase/timing reference.
* A fresh full-support local replacement I_S/d tensor tau_j before every trial,
  or the N791 fresh independent local Pauli ensemble pooled BEFORE conditioning
  on its current key, under its sector-safe control assumptions.
* A calibrated uniform conditional model/apparatus error gamma such that the
  actual full X-failure probability differs from that intended echo probability
  by at most gamma, for EVERY allowed outside state and past record. A fitted
  average contrast or accepted-only infidelity does not supply gamma. Unrecorded
  loss, false flags, crosstalk and model discrepancy remain charged.

N791's internally derived proof gives

    p_ideal,j >= c(t)||R||²,
    c(t)=t²(1-Lambda t/2)²/(4d).

Combining it with (4) yields for every past history

    Pr(Z_j=1 | past) >= max(0,c(t)||R||²-gamma).           (5)

For predeclared N, the attempted-trial count sum Z_j lower-tail dominates a
Binomial(N,max(0,c(t)||R||²-gamma)). This follows by using fresh uniforms to
couple each conditional Bernoulli to its lower bound. No independence of the
retained outside state or of successive Z_j is assumed. Invert that binomial
lower tail exactly as in N791 to obtain a confidence endpoint q_up:

    ||R|| <= min(Rcap, sqrt((q_up+gamma)/c(t))).           (6)

At t=1/Lambda this is 4 Lambda sqrt(d(q_up+gamma)). Fixed-N coverage, source
selection rules and any additional confidence allocation remain essential.
This is not an unchecked anytime theorem. Nonzero erasure counts produce a
real precision floor; more successful postselected trials cannot erase it.

A direct N791 countermodel to discarding failures: take d=8, H=0,
R=|0><0|, Rcap=Lambda=t=1 and the required reset I/d. Flag |0> as erased at
the end. Every surviving X outcome passes although ||R||=1 and the true echo
failure probability is (1-cos(1))/16>0. The original full-support theorem
survives; only the attempted transfer to survivor-only counts is refuted.

The molecular packet DOES NOT supply this collar residual, reset/twirl,
compiled witness, sector/control access or uniform gamma. This conditional

## 4. Ordinary branch channels do not determine the cross-history fringe

For the common controlled jump

    J = |A><A| tensor J_A + |B><B| tensor J_B,

the off-diagonal block K obeys exactly

    K' = -i(H_A K-K H_B)
       + sum_l [J_lA K J_lB†
          -(J_lA†J_lA K+K J_lB†J_lB)/2].                (7)

This is the corresponding block of ONE physical GKSL generator. Separately
aligning arbitrary Kraus/jump labels of two reduced channels does not define it.

Exact countermodel: H_A=H_B=0, J_A=sqrt(gamma) I and J_B=-sqrt(gamma) I. Each
branch's reduced molecular generator is ZERO, so every molecular input and
output channel is exactly identity. Nevertheless K'=-2 gamma K and the fringe
is exp(-2 gamma t). Using J_B=+sqrt(gamma) I gives the same branch channels
and fringe 1. The environment can record the branch without changing the
molecular reduced state. Gamma here is a symbolic scattering-model parameter,
distinct from N791's apparatus error and not a CaOH+ rate.

More generally, V_j|psi>=U_j|psi> tensor |e_j> gives the same reduced unitary
channels for every choice of environmental vectors, while L is multiplied by
<e_B|e_A>. Even identity reduced channels permit any disk-valued factor.
This underlying limitation is established channel-interference prior art;
see Oi, *Interference of Quantum Channels*, arXiv:quant-ph/0303178v2, Sections
II-III, especially the environmental-mark example (18). Our countermodels are
explicit reconstructions for the present interface, not a first discovery.

## 5. Equal intensity suppresses backgrounds without calibrating contrast

In an ideal cat model, encode the label into two orthogonal spatial states
|A,a> and |B,b>, apply the histories according to the spatial state, and decode
by the inverse controlled displacement. A common atomic energy phase mixes
the |+>,|-> cat labels. Write it in that label basis as

    M=cos(chi/2) I-i sin(chi/2) X.

After decoding, the unchanged-label and swapped-label components occupy
orthogonal residual motional states. Tracing motion and molecule gives

    L_observed=cos²(chi/2)L+sin²(chi/2)L*,
    Re L_observed=Re L,
    Im L_observed=cos(chi) Im L.                         (8)

Proof: the decoded pure state is proportional to
c(|A,0>u_A+|B,0>u_B)-is(|B,1>u_A+|A,1>u_B), with c=cos(chi/2),s=sin(chi/2).
Cross terms between residual motion 0 and 1 vanish; the two surviving label
coherences are L and L*. Mixed input follows by linearity. QED.

This toy keeps the erroneously unrecombined motion; it is not a complete
finite-wavepacket calculation. It suffices to refute a universal scalar C
description of arbitrary common atomic mixing. A blank L=1 can retain unit
contrast while the sought imaginary quadrature is suppressed. It does not
predict chi from a static polarizability.

It is equally important not to invent a false positive: under EXACT matching,
if the entire unwanted Hamiltonian depends only on the identical instantaneous
intensity and has the same initial state, it is identical in both programme
orderings. With no molecular history difference the two output laws coincide,
regardless of that common background's strength. An order-odd blank signal
requires imperfect matching, phase-sensitive unwanted response, pulse overlap,
drift or some other additional violated hypothesis. Common label mixing alone
is a contrast/model-identification obstruction in (8).

## 6. The carrier identity needs a finite-pulse interface

The packet's f1/f2 identity is exact for its monochromatic plane-wave model,
or a separable common-envelope approximation. The compiler's beam amplitudes
can share a global phase with real unequal coefficients a,b; the other pattern
exchanges them. For actual counterpropagating replicas of common envelope G,

    F1(t,x)=a G(t-x/c)e^(ikx)+b G(t+x/c)e^(-ikx),
    F2(t,x)=b G(t-x/c)e^(ikx)+a G(t+x/c)e^(-ikx).

The cross terms are equal, but the diagonal terms give exactly

    |F1|²-|F2|²=(a²-b²)(|G(t-x/c)|²-|G(t+x/c)|²).        (9)

Thus equality of instantaneous intensity everywhere is generally lost. The
time-integrated difference at each FIXED x is exactly zero, by translation
invariance of the envelope integral. Fixed-position, commuting intensity-only
Stark phases can therefore still cancel. This does not imply cancellation of
arbitrary finite-time joint dynamics or a complex molecular pulse area.

The packet's inferred d≈0.335 micrometre gives d/c≈1.12 fs, whereas Wu's
116.2-fs duration is a Fourier-limited inference from bandwidth, not a measured
envelope. These scales suggest a small molecular branch-envelope effect in one
scenario, not a validated error bound. The atom's offset, spectral phase,
dispersion and pulse overlap are additional unacquired data. The source already
excludes optical dispersion; (9) makes a concrete missing interface explicit.

## 7. A connected fourth response is not an anharmonicity certificate

Let half the specimens/rotational sectors be harmonic Gaussian systems with
control variance 1 and half with variance 3. Then

    L(epsilon)=.5 exp(-epsilon²/2)+.5 exp(-3epsilon²/2)
              =exp(-epsilon²) cosh(epsilon²/2),
    log L=-epsilon²+epsilon⁴/8+O(epsilon⁸).

Its fourth derivative at zero is 3, although every conditional sector is
harmonic and Gaussian. More generally the derivative is 3 Var(variance).
The source explicitly warns about mixtures; this is an exact falsifier of any
later promotion from nonzero fourth connected response alone to intrinsic
anharmonicity or useful complex chemistry.
