MICROSCOPIC EXTENSION FORMULA

Let ell_0(U)=sum_I |det F[I,R\I]| and z_0(U)=sum_I |det F[I,R\I]|^2 for R=[6]\U. Add an auxiliary pair (e_i,e_j) of activity w for each edge {i,j} of the complete graph on [6]. If a collection K of auxiliary pairs is selected, any two selected pairs must be vertex-disjoint: shared coordinates produce repeated columns and determinant zero. Expanding along the coordinate columns of a matching K leaves the old determinant problem with holes U union V(K). Different K and old orientations are distinct microscopic labels, so absolute amplitudes add. Put t=sqrt(w). The exact lifted orientation L1 weight and coefficient are therefore

  ell_w(U) = sum_{K matching in [6]\U} t^|K| ell_0(U union V(K)),
  z_w(U)   = sum_{K matching in [6]\U} t^(2|K|) z_0(U union V(K)).

This is the orientation-level expansion of the coefficient recurrence z_new(U)=z_old(U)+w z_old(U union {i,j}) from the prior signed-stability audit. No signs are combined across distinct auxiliary-selection labels.

Use the same S=emptyset, T={2,4,5,6}, a=2 and all 15 auxiliary edges with t=1/100 (so w=1/10000>0). Exact Fraction arithmetic gives

  ell_w(S) = 418483/200000,
  ell_w(T) = 201/100,
  ell_w(S)ell_w(T) = 84115083/20000000 = 4.20575415.

The three exchange terms, in j=4,5,6 order, are

  j=4: (403/10000)^2 = 162409/100000000,
  j=5: (10403/10000)^2 = 108222409/100000000,
  j=6: (10503/10000)^2 = 110313009/100000000.

Thus the l1 / Renyi-1/2 exchange strengthening fails even with a complete, strictly positive coordinate auxiliary set. Aggregate signed stability and its square-root exchange remain available for these same positive auxiliaries; the failed conclusion is solely the orientation-entropy lift.
