# Residual coordinate legality survives the critical envelope

Worker hessian_bridge12, cycle14, 7 October 2026. This is an all-degree necessary condition for a specified source-coordinate orbit, together with exact exclusions of two displayed direct-potential repair branches. It is not a construction of a constant-Hessian potential and does not close the arbitrary degree-nine repair problem.

## Exact interface and theorem

Use the prior normalized Meng–Yang potential

\[
 f_0(x,y,t,R_0)=Lt^2+a(x,y)t+N(y\rho_0+2x\eta_0),\qquad LN\ne0,
\]

over \(\mathbb C\). Keep the old base coordinates \((x,y)\) and old pivot coordinate \(t\) fixed, and replace the residual pair by an arbitrary **global polynomial fibre automorphism**

\[
 R_0=\Theta(x,y,t,R),\qquad R=(\rho,\eta).
\]

The automorphism and its inverse must be polynomial over \(\mathbb C[x,y,t]\). Dependence on \(t\), arbitrary degree, translations, and nonzero quadratic fibre jets are allowed. Suppose there is a polynomial critical section \(t=s(x,y,R)\) satisfying

\[
 \partial_t(f_0\circ\Phi)=\sigma\in\mathbb C,
 \quad \Phi=(x,y,t,\Theta),
\]

and put \(\psi=(f_0\circ\Phi-\sigma t)|_{t=s}\). Then the following **ideal equality**, not only a pointwise nonvanishing condition, is necessary:

\[
 \boxed{(\psi_\rho,\psi_\eta)=(x,y)
       \quad\hbox{in }\mathbb C[x,y,\rho,\eta].}
\]

Proof: the derivative of the critical envelope cancels the derivative of its section, giving

\[
 (\psi_\rho,\psi_\eta)
 =N(y,2x)\,J_R\Theta\big|_{t=s}.
\]

The Jacobian \(J_R\Theta\) has a polynomial matrix inverse and a constant nonzero determinant \(\Delta\), because \(\Theta\) has a polynomial inverse over the base/pivot ring. These identities remain identities after substitution of the polynomial section. Multiplication by an invertible polynomial matrix preserves the ideal generated by the components of a row. For a direct certificate, write the specialized matrix as

\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 r_1=N(ya+2xc),\quad r_2=N(yb+2xd).
\]

Then

\[
 dr_1-cr_2=N\Delta y,\qquad -br_1+ar_2=2N\Delta x.
\]

The reverse containment follows from the displayed definitions of \(r_1,r_2\). This proves the theorem without invoking any Jacobian-conjecture assertion.

In particular, the residual fibre gradient cannot vanish at any \((x,y)\ne(0,0)\). The invariant says more than absence of such zeros: it also fixes the nonreduced ideal at the base origin. It is **necessary, not sufficient** for a legal chart or polynomial critical solution. Charts mixing the old base or old pivot with the residual variables lie outside this theorem.

A cheap stronger check at the origin follows immediately. Modulo \(I^2\), \(I=(x,y)\), the two fibre-gradient generators must form a basis of the free rank-two \(\mathbb C[\rho,\eta]\)-module \(I/I^2\). Hence their coefficient matrix in \((x,y)\) must have a **constant nonzero determinant** in \(\mathbb C[\rho,\eta]\). Vanishing of the fibre derivatives at the base origin is only ideal membership, not this necessary equality check.

The checker includes the genuinely \(t\)-dependent shear
\(\Theta=(\rho+t\eta^2,\eta)\). Its polynomial critical section is
\(s=(\sigma-a-Ny\eta^2)/(2L)\); direct differentiation of the resulting envelope reproduces the same ideal equality. Thus the theorem does not silently assume pivot independence.

## Exclusion of the displayed minimal degree-nine branch for every parameter

The cycle13 diagnostic candidate is

\[
 \psi_0=h_0+c_1(\rho+\eta^2)
       +c_2[\eta+(\rho+\eta^2)^2],
\]

where \((c_1,c_2)=2(y,2x)D(x)\), \(D(1)=I\), and
\(h_0=-(\beta+45/4)^2\),
\(\beta=y^2(6x^2y^2+15xy+4)\). The exact degree-nine leading equations admit the minimal free-\(\alpha\) repair written in `DEGREE9_GRADED_COMPATIBILITY.md`. Every member cancels determinant degrees35/34/33, preserves the published collision, and retains full positive Hessian span. Nevertheless every member violates the legal coordinate orbit.

At the fixed nonzero base \((x,y)=(1,0)\), its fibre, up to a constant, is exactly

\[
 F_\alpha=A(\eta)\rho^2+8\rho\eta^2+4\eta^4+4\eta,
 \quad A=\frac72-\frac43\alpha\eta-\frac43\alpha^2\eta^2.
\]

Solving \(F_\rho=0\) where \(A\ne0\) gives \(\rho=-4\eta^2/A\). The remaining derivative vanishes precisely when

\[
 P_\alpha(\eta)=4A^2+16\eta^3A^2-64\eta^3A+16\eta^4A'=0.
\]

For \(\alpha\ne0\), this polynomial has degree7 and leading coefficient
\(256\alpha^4/9\), while \(P_\alpha(0)=49\). The roots of \(A\) are simple and nonzero: its discriminant is \(184\alpha^2/9\) and its constant term is \(7/2\). At an \(A\)-root, \(P_\alpha=16\eta^4A'\ne0\). Equivalently the exact resultant is

\[
 \operatorname{Res}_\eta(A,P_\alpha)
 =\frac{452386816}{243}\alpha^8\ne0.
\]

The fundamental theorem of algebra supplies a root of \(P_\alpha\), and the preceding identities show it gives a genuine fibre critical point with \(A\ne0\). For \(\alpha=0\), the equations reduce explicitly to

\[
 \eta^3=7/4,\qquad \rho=-8\eta^2/7.
\]

Thus the entire displayed minimal branch, for every complex \(\alpha\), fails the coordinate-orbit condition. This obstruction is independent of its already nonconstant Hessian determinant.

### Why this does not exclude the general remaining repair

The allowed degree-five addition

\[
 R_{\rm low}=x\rho^2\left[\frac12+\frac43\alpha\eta
                          +\frac43\alpha^2\eta^2\right]
\]

restores the fibre at this **one** base to
\(4(\rho+\eta^2)^2+4\eta\). Its two derivatives satisfy
\(F_\eta-2\eta F_\rho=4\), so they cannot vanish together there. It preserves the chosen collision, origin ideal membership, and determinant degrees35/34/33. This is a precise countercheck against extrapolating a single-fibre test to all lower completions.

It still fails the **global** ideal condition, now by the origin conormal test. The minimal branch's conormal determinant is \(-8\). At \(\eta=0\), adding this lower control changes its determinant to a polynomial with the \(\rho\) coefficient \(21/8\), independent of \(\alpha\); it is therefore never a unit. Further unrestricted free terms can alter both conormal rows, so this result does not close the general repair problem. The base-independent degree-four variant would already violate origin ideal membership and is an even weaker control.

Multiplying the bracket instead by \(x^2\rho^2\) gives a degree-at-most-six control which fixes the selected fibre **and** keeps the origin conormal determinant \(-8\). It nevertheless has fibre critical points at the second base \((-1,0)\). There the exact fibre is
\(A\rho^2+8\rho\eta^2+4\eta^4+4\eta\),
\(A=5+(8/3)\alpha^2\eta^2\), so the same elimination gives a degree7 polynomial when \(\alpha\ne0\), with leading coefficient \(1024\alpha^4/9\), avoiding the simple nonzero roots of \(A\). When \(\alpha=0\), \(\eta^3=-5/4\) and \(\rho=-4\eta^2/5\) give critical points. Thus selected-fibre noncriticality plus the origin conormal test are still insufficient. No further sequence of lower-control adjustments was searched.

## The actual quartic completion is also outside the orbit

For any nonzero constant \(k\), the completion

\[
 \psi_{\rm comp}=h_0+c_1\rho+c_2\eta
                     -\frac{xy}{8}(x\eta-k\rho)^4
\]

is a degree-nine direct repair of \(\psi_0\), preserves the chosen collision, and has the further determinant obstruction proved in `PURE_POWER_COMPLETION.md`. It also fails the source-orbit condition before that calculation. At the nonzero base \((1,-2k)\), \(D(1)=I\) gives \(c_1=-4k,c_2=4\). With \(q=\eta-k\rho\), the fibre becomes

\[
 4q+(k/4)q^4.
\]

Every root of \(q^3=-4/k\) gives \(\psi_\rho=\psi_\eta=0\). The base is never the origin. The linear change \((\rho,\eta)\leftrightarrow(q,\eta)\) on this fixed fibre is invertible, so the critical point is a genuine one.

## Prior art, status and downstream use

The polynomial Meng–Yang example, toric completion and normalization are primary prior inputs. Jacobian-ideal covariance under polynomial automorphisms and critical-envelope differentiation are elementary standard algebra; no originality claim is made for those identities. Their combination into this precise residual-source-orbit gate, the all-parameter critical-point certificate, and its application to the two repaired branches are programme deductions. Priority has not been established by a comprehensive literature search.

This gate changes the search interface: a direct repair passing a few graded determinant faces and retaining a known collision can still be unavailable to the actual descent. Any future base/pivot-fixed chart search must enforce the global ideal equality, in addition to a legal polynomial inverse, polynomial critical equation, full Hessian chain rule and actual collision compatibility. Checking the ideal cannot replace those remaining gates.

The next falsifiable step is to construct a remaining degree-nine completion with its free \(A_7,B_9\) and lower terms that passes the **global** ideal condition and the full nonzero constant-determinant identity, or to prove these two necessary identities incompatible. Alternatively change the old base/pivot coordinates explicitly and recompute the envelope. The present theorem makes no claim about that different interface.
