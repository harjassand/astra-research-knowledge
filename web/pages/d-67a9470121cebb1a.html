11. WITHIN-ROUND-BLOCK HALF OF LEMMA 2.5: EVERY REMAINING ADJACENT PAIR

This section closes the parent's requested EVERY-adjacent-pair interface. It is independently checked algebra, not an inference from pairwise rank two.

At round let Y_l be the real degree-l eigenspace, dimension 2*l+1, and define the product map
  T_l:Sym^2(Y_l) -> direct_sum_{j=0}^l Y_{2j},
  T_l(u symmetric_product v)=u*v.
Products lie in the target by the homogeneous harmonic decomposition of a polynomial of degree 2*l. The image is rotation invariant, hence preserved by the round rotation Casimir Delta_0. It contains
  F_l(z)=(1-z^2)^l=(Re((x+i*y)^l))^2+(Im((x+i*y)^l))^2.
Each factor on the right is a degree-l harmonic polynomial. Direct differentiation yields
  (Delta_0+2*l*(2*l+1))*F_l=4*l^2*F_{l-1}.
Writing F_l=sum_{j=0}^l c_{l,j}*P_{2j}(z), the coefficient c_{l,l} is nonzero by degree. For j<l,
  c_{l,j}=4*l^2*c_{l-1,j}/[2*l*(2*l+1)-2*j*(2*j+1)].
The denominator is strictly positive. Starting with F_0=1 proves EVERY coefficient nonzero. Polynomial spectral projections in Delta_0 therefore put a nonzero zonal harmonic in every Y_{2j} into im(T_l).

Rotations of a nonzero zonal degree-2j harmonic span Y_{2j}. One elementary justification: it is proportional to the harmonic projection of z^(2j); rotations replace z by any unit linear form, powers of linear forms span homogeneous polynomials, and their harmonic projections span the harmonic subspace. Thus T_l is onto. The dimensions are
  dim Sym^2(Y_l)=(2*l+1)*(2*l+2)/2=(l+1)*(2*l+1),
