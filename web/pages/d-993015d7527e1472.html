Title:Sharp Concentration of Simple Random Tensors

Authors:Omar Al-Ghattas, Jiaheng Chen, Daniel Sanz-Alonso
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Sharp Concentration of Simple Random Tensors, by Omar Al-Ghattas and 2 other authors

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:This paper establishes sharp dimension-free concentration inequalities and expectation bounds for the deviation of the sum of simple random tensors from its expectation. As part of our analysis, we use generic chaining techniques to obtain a sharp high-probability upper bound on the suprema of $L_p$ empirical processes. In so doing, we generalize classical results for quadratic and product empirical processes to higher-order settings.
