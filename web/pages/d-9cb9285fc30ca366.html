## Interface to compare

Input: integer T>=1. Output: N=O(T²) distinct rational unit vectors on S², with normalized harmonic mean through degree T at most 1/[2048(T+1)^5] and normalized Gram matrix greater than I/2. Charge initialization, arithmetic, iterative correction, coordinate bits and validation. Our explicit construction uses N=720000(T+1)² and O(log(T+1)) coordinate bits, with polynomial bit time in T and an explicit standard exact rational-LP primitive.

This is an approximate equal-weight acquisition interface. An exact real design, a weighted cubature rule, a supplied candidate with a certificate, a numerical solver that may fail, and a nonconstructive optimal-order existence proof are relevant comparators but do not automatically implement this interface. Conversely, a genuine universal polynomial-bit exact-design acquisition theorem with O(T²) points would be a stronger comparator and would largely subsume it after controlled rational rounding. We have not established that no such prior theorem exists.

## Closest certified-root comparator: Chen-Womersley 2006

Verified source: Xiaojun Chen and Robert S. Womersley, *Existence of Solutions to Systems of Underdetermined Equations and Spherical Designs*, SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis 44(6), 2326-2341, DOI 10.1137/050626636. The primary PDF records electronic publication on 24 November 2006. Read depth: introduction, Theorem 2.1 and Section 4; full PDF saved, not a claim of every-line reading. [Author-hosted PDF](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/ama/staff/xjchen/ReprintsB5.pdf).

Theorem 2.1 starts from a computed approximate solution with full-row-rank derivative and an invertible selected Jacobian block. A local Lipschitz bound and enclosure inequality certify a nearby root. In Section 4, page 2337, the procedure first acquires an approximate solution from an extremal fundamental system, then tests equation (4.2), rho<=1/2. It uses several nearby starts and Gauss-Newton with line search because the residual has multiple local minimizers. Table 1, page 2338, reports degrees 2 through 10; the introduction separately points to computed designs through degree 50.

Our deduction from these hypotheses: this is a strong local certification and numerical acquisition comparator with optimal-order cardinality (T+1)². The sections read do not prove universally charged extremal initialization or uniform polynomial convergence for every T. The new local mathematical ingredient being evaluated here is the product-ball duality/coordinate-box LP that bounds every node's correction, joined to a certified centroid basin and rational resets. Neither spherical design equations nor Newton correction nor local root verification is new.

## Practical numerical baseline and recent intake

Robert S. Womersley's [*Efficient Spherical Designs with Good Geometric Properties*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.01624), arXiv:1709.01624v1, 5 September 2017, Sections 4.4-4.5, is the stronger practical baseline. Equations (36)-(38) give the harmonic residual and derivative information underlying Gauss-Newton/Levenberg-Marquardt/trust-region methods. The numerical workflow seeks efficient low-cardinality designs and depends on starts and extensive computation. The sections read do not provide the unconditional centroid basin and polynomial-bit acquisition theorem here. ## Deduction, use, and next discriminating step

Our internally gated deduction is the explicit finite-precision composition: equal-area normalized ambient centroids -> uniform neighborhood L1 sampling -> minimum-coordinate tangent LP -> rational Cayley retraction -> distinct bounded-height reset. The LP support-function proof gives a correction of maximum norm O(Th²), avoiding the need to bound an L2 pseudoinverse in L∞. With h=c/T this is O(c²/T), and the loop converges inside its sampling neighborhood. We have not proved a lower bound saying an inverse-gradient logarithm is unavoidable for every alternative solver.

Root separately adopted its exact interface into the equal-unit-charge coefficient compiler in [the composition gate](../../root_cycle10/SPHERICAL_ACQUISITION_AND_CHARGE_GATE.md). It removes exponential direction enumeration within that construction while retaining its N^(3/2) critical-point order and small-signal limitations. This is the concrete use; it is not a new general equilibrium exponent claim.

The strongest open comparison question is whether a prior universal design algorithm already proves the same acquired-basin and finite-bit guarantee, perhaps in a broader manifold setting. The strongest execution test is a complete modest-size rational LP/reset run with original primal, mean and Gram certificates. The bounded replay failed to provide that implementation; [the failure record](CENTROID_LP_EXECUTION_LIMIT.md) is retained.
