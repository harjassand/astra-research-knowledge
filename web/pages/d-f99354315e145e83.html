LOCAL CRITICAL XY: NORMAL QUANTUM EXPERIMENT AND PUBLIC ARCHIVE
2026-10-08. INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; external validity/priority UNKNOWN.

For the open N-site chain L=N+1, anisotropy gamma=kappa/L, inverse
temperature beta=tau L^2 and saturation detuning lambda/L^2, J,tau>0,
the exact finite Gibbs states converge in trace norm in the common
Dirichlet sine Fock embedding, uniformly on compact (lambda,kappa) sets.
The continuum BdG operator has Dirichlet diagonal
A=2tau J(-partial_x^2)-tau lambda and antisymmetric pairing
K=-4tau J kappa partial_x. Its sine pairing kernel is
-16tau J kappa kl/(k^2-l^2) for opposite parity and zero otherwise.

The decisive occupation residual estimate gives, above a fixed k0,
Q_kk<=4pi^2 kappa^2/k^2+exp(-2tau J k^2), uniformly at finite N.
Thus the high-mode tail is O(kappa^2/M) plus a Gaussian thermal tail.
Strong column/core convergence, uniform graph bounds, finite covariance
continuity and the tail projection construct the normal whole Fock limit.
Scalar UV ground-energy subtraction is linear in L when kappa!=0 and does
not change normalized states. The raw formal pair kernel is not HS and
cannot be applied to the bare vacuum as an unrenormalized Hamiltonian.
Implementability/standard quasi-free tools are established prior art.

The public parameter-blind quantum archive keeps low modes M and total
number at most r coherently, with an overflow flag. A proved Gaussian
number-generating bound yields r=O(log(1/epsilon)/loglog(1/epsilon)), while
M=O(kappa_max^2/epsilon^2+sqrt(log(1/epsilon))). For fixed nonzero public
kappa_max it achieves O(log^2(1/epsilon)/loglog(1/epsilon)) stored qubits,
independent of N. Precise constants, all-small-N choices, flags, reversible
ranking, O(N^2) sine/matchgate gates, working memory and angle accuracy are
in proof.txt Section 7. This is an achievable rate, not an optimality claim.

Unknown anisotropy changes the operational experiment. At lambda=0,
the limiting pair coherence has a nonzero controlled derivative in kappa.
The full limit state at kappa=0 and a sufficiently small nonzero kappa
do not commute. For any EB measure-and-prepare channel T and any pair,
max(||T rho-rho||_1,||T sigma-sigma||_1)>=(F-A)^2/4, where F is root
fidelity and A=Tr sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma). This dimension-free witness and
the exact response prove a strict positive asymptotic classical
reconstruction floor for a selected unknown-kappa subfamily. The explicit
analytic constant is very poor; no practical margin is claimed. A finite
commutator does not by itself prove the infinite experiment; the whole
trace theorem is used to pass fidelity/affinity to the limit.

The reusable quadratic criterion in proof.txt Section 8 requires common
core convergence plus summability of residual^2/diagonal^2 and thermal
tails. For Weyl dimension d, kinetic order z and pairing order r,
2(z-r)>d is a sufficient boundary. Independent BCS blocks give a sharp
counterexample to that class criterion at/below the boundary. This is not
a new Shale criterion and not a universal dimension-two XY theorem.

62 pairing/column norm cases L=3..64; 36 residual/tail cases through L=129;
45 random complex Gaussian MGF cases; finite EB and response diagnostics.
All pass. These checks are not external mathematical validation.
