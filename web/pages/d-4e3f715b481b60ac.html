# Weighted orthogonal quartic landscape: actual missing synthesis lemma

2026-10-06. Status: consequential question formulated, recent theorem/proof read, global identifiability derived, finite inverse probe preserved. No general landscape theorem, counterexample, polynomial exact acquisition guarantee or historical novelty has been established.

## Exact input and intended consequence

Input to a proof synthesizer: expanded rational coefficients of p(x)=q(Lx)^T Q q(Lx), q_i(y)=yi²−ai, where Q is rational PD, a rational positive, b=Qa entrywise positive, and L in GL_n(Q) unknown. No Q,a,L, commuting target matrices, zeros or Gram face is supplied. The intended output is a rational factor subspace and exact positive weighted-square certificate, or UNKNOWN under a declared budget. The arbitrary Q promise allows collisions between diagonal and offdiagonal tensor eigenspaces, so the cycle spectral filter is insufficient.

The degree2 part gives M=−Hess(p2)/4=L^T diag(b)L. An exact algebraic whitening R, R^TMR=I, is acquired from M. Write O=diag(sqrt(b))LR, so O is orthogonal, and G=diag(b)^−1 Q diag(b)^−1 is PD. Then

  p4(Rz)=(Oz)^{circ2,T} G (Oz)^{circ2}.

The synthesizer can evaluate the quartic, its gradient and full constrained Hessian directly from the coefficient list, without seeing G or O. Maximizing sum_k p4(Ru_k) over orthogonal U is equivalent in latent coordinates to maximizing

  F_G(W)=sum_k m_k^T G m_k, m_k=(W_1k²,...,W_nk²)^T.

Finding its axes would expose the promised zero-sign quadratics and permit the exact coefficient/LDL synthesis already implemented. This changes the task from a supplied certificate verifier to acquisition.

## Deduction: unique global axes for every G PD

Let W be orthogonal. Its entrywise-square matrix M is doubly stochastic. Define Pij=sum_k Wik² Wjk². Because sum_j Pij=1,

  tr(G)−F_G(W)
   =sum_{i<j} (Gii+Gjj−2Gij) Pij.

For i!=j, wij=Gii+Gjj−2Gij=(ei−ej)^T G(ei−ej)>0. Thus F_G(W)<=tr(G). Equality implies Pij=0 for all i!=j. Each column has nonzero support in only one row; orthogonality makes W a signed permutation. Conversely signed permutations attain equality. This is a complete global-identifiability derivation, not a local-landscape or efficient-discovery proof.

A quantitative consequence follows without solving the landscape. Since wij>=2lambda_min(G),

  tr(G)−F_G(W)>=lambda_min(G)[n−||W||_4^4].

For any orthogonal W, M=W^{circ2} is doubly stochastic. A Birkhoff decomposition M=sum theta_j P_j implies some permutation P satisfies <M,P>>=||M||_F²=||W||_4^4. Put the matching signs into P. Then

  ||W−P_signed||_F²
   =2n−2sum matched |Wik|
   <=2[n−sum matched Wik²]
   <=2[n−||W||_4^4]
   <=2[tr(G)−F_G(W)]/lambda_min(G).

Thus approximate GLOBAL optimality implies closeness to a hidden axis frame, with an explicit condition margin. This independently reconstructs the matching ingredient behind the unweighted Zhai et al.Lemma6 and extends its use to this weighted gap. It is not a method to attain/certify global optimality: tr(G) and lambda_min(G) are latent, and the missing acquisition/landscape theorem remains essential.

The w_ij are squared Euclidean distances between columns of a factor V with G=V^TV. They are not arbitrary positive edge weights. Any proposed counterexample or extension must preserve this constraint.

## Exact nearest comparator: commuting-target joint diagonalization

Factor G=V^TV for analysis only, and let D_a=diag(V_a1,...,V_an). Then

  F_G(W)=sum_a ||diag(W^T D_a W)||²,
  tr(G)−F_G(W)=sum_a ||offdiag(W^T D_a W)||_F².

The D_a commute. If the transformed family O^T D_a O were SUPPLIED, a generic linear combination and eigenvectors acquire axes by standard exact joint diagonalization. Our coefficient-only learner does not receive this family: reconstructing it is the missing information interface. A mathematical landscape theorem could be old OJD theory even if applying it to the compressed quartic input is new. Do not claim a separation from supplied-family OJD.

Primary prerequisite read: Bijan Afsari, ICASSP2007 'What Can Make Joint Diagonalization Difficult?', Sections2–4: same offdiagonal-energy objective, commutator stationarity equation, uniqueness via distinct diagonal profiles, and local perturbation/sensitivity theorem. It supplies no full all-stationary landscape classification in the text read. The 24-page sensitivity manuscript was retrieved but not fully read. The Deakin repository API then recovered the decisive prerequisite: Guoxu Zhou, Shengli Xie, Zuyuan Yang, Jun Zhang, 'Nonorthogonal approximate joint diagonalization with well-conditioned diagonalizers', IEEE TNN20(11)1810–1819 November2009, receivedNovember2008. PDF archived; SectionsIII A–C, Proposition3 and AppendixII read, with the theorem page1814 rendered to recover missing formula glyphs. Proposition3 says the AMBIENT derivative dF/dW vanishes iff offdiagonal energy F=0. This includes trivial and degenerate solutions. It does not establish the projected-orthogonal landscape: ambient scaling W->tW reduces this homogeneous degree4 objective, but is unavailable on O(n). Independently, Euler's identity <dF/dW,W>=4F explains why unconstrained nonzero-error stationary points are excluded. The exact prior therefore does not settle the candidate orthogonal lemma; broader historical novelty remains unverified.

## Missing lemma and second independent cost gap

Candidate lemma, UNPROVED: for every G PD and every stationary W not a signed permutation, some skew A satisfies d²F_G(W exp(tA))/dt² at0>0. Equivalently no nonzero-error local minimum exists for exact commuting-target OJD with this compressed input. Possible stronger claim: construct the escape direction using only quartic coefficients and the current W, with quantitative curvature polynomial in n and spectral margin of G. Counterexamples must make G PD exactly and verify the FULL Hessian, not just coordinate-pair curvatures.

Even this qualitative lemma would not prove polynomial-bit rational proof synthesis. A second missing step is quantitative acquisition: finite precision/iteration bounds, input-conditioned separation and rational reconstruction of hidden axes, charging every whitening/optimization/reconstruction operation. A zero-residual certificate on the boundary has no arbitrary interior rounding margin. Rational final expansion and PD LDL checks provide soundness; they do not acquire a certificate efficiently.

## Exact derivative interface and executable finite probe

For W(t)=W exp(tA), put u_k=W[:,k], v_k=(WA)[:,k], z_k=(WA²)[:,k], m_k=u_k^{circ2}, dm_k=2u_k circ v_k, ddm_k=2v_k^{circ2}+2u_k circ z_k. Then

  F'_G=sum_k 2 m_k^T G dm_k,
  F''_G=sum_k [2 dm_k^T G dm_k+2 m_k^T G ddm_k].

The Euclidean gradient is4 W circ (G M). Stationarity is symmetry of W^T[W circ(GM)]. The full constrained Hessian is obtained by polarizing the second-variation form over all skew basis matrices. Both stationarity and Hessian depend linearly on G for fixed W.

`work/r16_proof_methods/weighted_landscape_probe.py` implements exact rational stationarity constraints and exact Hessian coefficient matrices at rational Householder W=I−2J/n, then samples stationary G combinations numerically. 10000 samples each n3/4/5 gave439,10,0 PD cases respectively and no negative-definite full-Hessian case. This is weak finite evidence. Zero PD samples at n5 are an explicit coverage failure. These tests do not settle the weighted lemma, do not test generic stationary W, and do not prove absence of traps. Q/G are supplied for inverse landscape search; this experiment is not an acquisition result.

A subsidiary pairwise trap is retained in `jacobi_stationary.json`: for unweighted G=I and Householder W at n>=8, every coordinate rotation has negative second derivative although W is nonglobal stationary. At n8 F=11/4<8 and every pair curvature=−1/2. The July theorem explains that a mixed full-Hessian direction escapes; its Section7(g) already covers this mechanism. Thus our explicit fixture is a baseline diagnostic, not new landscape theory.
