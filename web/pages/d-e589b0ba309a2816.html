\begin{abstract}
We give counterexamples to Griffiths' conjecture in rank two on the quadric
surface $\PP^1\times\PP^1$. We construct an explicit bundle $G$ whose
coordinatewise power pullbacks, tensored with $\OO(1,1)$, are ample for every
positive power, but admit no smooth strictly Griffiths-positive Hermitian
metric for all sufficiently large powers.
\end{abstract}
