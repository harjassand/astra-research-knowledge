2. THE CLASSICAL MEMORY INTERFACE

An edge-emitting generator has finite memory W_t immediately after output
A_t and before output A_{t+1}. One step draws (A_{t+1},W_{t+1}) jointly
from a fixed kernel conditional only on W_t, using fresh independent
randomness. More generally the lower bounds need only that W_t screens
the past from future draws. Then
 P(A_t,A_{t+1})=sum_w pi_t(w)u_w(A_t)v_w(A_{t+1}).
Both ordinary HMM conventions obey such a product decomposition at an
appropriate cut. A persistent seed, accessible stored past, or memoryful
environment belongs in W_t. Free external access to the past changes the
task. Neither unifilarity nor hidden stationarity is needed for a
one-boundary lower bound. If the target is stationary the same observed
pair law occurs at every cut, so every exact generator must satisfy it
at every cut even if its hidden distribution is nonstationary.

Shannon memory is H(W_t), not I(W_t;past), H(output), or ln(number of
predictive coordinates). State count K implies H(W_t)<=ln K, but a rank
lower bound K>=N does not alone imply entropy Omega(ln N).

The edge-emitting convention is necessary when claiming the candidate's
N+2-state entropy attainment. A strict Moore HMM separately samples
A_t from its emission distribution given Z_t, and then samples Z_{t+1}
independently of that emission given Z_t. Its state count and entropy
can differ. Section 7 gives the exact difference for this example.

3. RECONSTRUCTION OF THE BALANCED-SUBSET PROCESS

Let N be a positive multiple of 8, M=binom(N,N/2), and let S range over
balanced subsets. The candidate instrument has quantum dimension N+2,
three disjoint output types (S, index i, blank), and transitions
 init --S, probability 1/M--> rho_S=(2/N)sum_{i in S}|i><i|,
 i --i, probability 1--> done,
 done --blank, probability 1--> init.
The first displayed transition is a quantum preparation; its basis
classical realization instead jointly samples S and i in S with
probability 2/(MN), storing i. Each CP map is measure-and-prepare, and
the sum preserves trace. The stationary density
 rho_*=(|init><init|+I_N/N+|done><done|)/3
is fixed exactly. This is a stationary, period-three process; it is not
mixing. The entire observed-history reachable family comprises rho_*,
init, done, and rho_S. Convexly forgotten histories do not raise capacity.

The center sigma has weights 1/4 on init, 1/4 on done, and total 1/2
uniformly on the N indices. Every extreme reachable state has relative
entropy ln4 from sigma. The center identity gives capacity <=ln4 for
EVERY prior; weights (1/4,1/4,1/2 uniform subsets) attain it. Hence the
max-prior capacity is exactly ln4. The single basis-copy channel is
full-domain CPTP and broadcasts all reachable states exactly. A single
basis-dephasing EB channel fixes them exactly. No supplied modulus or
approximation theorem is needed for any of these counterexample facts.

4. EXACT ENTROPY AND WYNER INFORMATION: VALID

For the balanced incidence law (uniform S, uniform i in S), the pair
probability is 2/(MN) on each incident pair. In a positive product
component, if T is the supported set of indices, t=|T|, the subset
support consists only of S containing T. Thus
 H(S,i|w)<=ln[t*binom(N-t,N/2-t)].
For 1<=t<=N/2,
 M/binom(N-t,N/2-t)=product_{j=0}^{t-1}(N-j)/(N/2-j)>=2^t,
and 2^(t-1)>=t. Therefore each component contributes at least ln N to
D(P_{S,i|w}||P_{S,i}), and averaging proves C_W(S;i)>=ln N. W=i attains
equality. Meanwhile I(S;i)=ln2. These quantities are distinct.

In the full stationary pair law, the only output-type edges are
 subset->index, index->blank, blank->subset.
A nonzero product rectangle can occupy only one edge because two past
types have disjoint future-type neighborhoods. Therefore the uniform
three-way phase is a deterministic function of W. In its subset->index
phase, the preceding lemma gives H(W|phase)>=ln N. Consequently
 H(W)>=ln3+(ln N)/3.
The explicit N+2-state edge-emitting realization has stationary weights
 1/3 on init, 1/3 on done, 1/(3N) on each index,
and attains this entropy. Its W is determined by the adjacent output
pair, so the full pair's Wyner information equals the same number.
The ordinary adjacent and past-future mutual informations equal
 ln3+(ln2)/3, which remain bounded. The common entropy of this full
pair also equals ln3+(ln N)/3; equality follows here from the matching
H(W) witness, not from conflating its definition with C_W.

5. SEPARATE UNCONDITIONAL EXACT STATE COUNT

This is an exposed audit extension, not a change to the independent
baseline. Let B be the M by N incidence matrix B_{S,i}=1_{i in S}.
Its Gram matrix is
 B^T B=(M/2-off)I+off J,
 off=binom(N-2,N/2-2)=M(N-2)/(4(N-1)),
 M/2-off=MN/(4(N-1))>0.
Thus rank B=N. After permuting the column output types, the full pair
matrix has three disjoint diagonal blocks: this incidence block, a
nonzero index-to-blank column of rank one, and a nonzero blank-to-subset
row of rank one. Its ordinary rank is exactly N+2.

Any K-component product decomposition has rank <=K. Hence EVERY exact
classical simulator at the specified cut has at least N+2 states.
The candidate's edge-emitting realization attains N+2. This conclusion
is unconditional and separate from the entropy proof above.

6. FIXED TV ERROR: THE CANDIDATE CONSTANTS ARE VALID

Write gamma=1-h_2(1/4), where h_2 is binary entropy in bits, and
 L_N=[gamma*N*ln2-ln(2(N+1))]/ln9.

If TV(P,Q)<=delta, the weighted conditional-distance identity gives
 sum_S P(S)TV(p_S,q_S)<=2delta.
Here P(S)=1/(3M), so the uniform subset average is <=6delta. The
same future-distribution decoder with K hidden-state distributions
represents every q_S, including an arbitrary choice when Q(S)=0.
At delta<=1/192, average error <=1/32; at least half of the subsets
have error <=1/16. A greedy Hamming code inside this good half has
size at least 2^(gamma*N)/[2(N+1)], with pairwise symmetric difference
at least N/4. Its target conditional distributions have TV separation
at least 1/4. Triangle inequality and decoder contraction force the
hidden posteriors to have TV separation >=1/8. Their open l1 balls
of radius 1/8 are disjoint and lie inside the origin ball of radius
9/8. Full R^K volume comparison gives code size <=9^K. Therefore
 K>=L_N=Omega(N).
The packing is valid even though its centers lie in an affine simplex:
the compared balls are full-dimensional ambient l1 balls.

For H=H(pi_t) and eta=1/384, retaining states with surprisal <=H/eta
discards probability <=eta and keeps at most exp(H/eta) states. Merging
the tail into one product component changes the adjacent pair law by
at most eta. If TV(P,Q)<=1/384, the truncated pair law is within
1/192 and hence needs at least L_N product components. Thus
 L_N<=exp(384H)+1<=2exp(384H),
 H>=ln(L_N/2)/384 whenever L_N>2.
This truncation does not pretend that the modified pair factorization
is an HMM generating a whole process. It is only a necessary pair-law
test, which suffices for the lower bound. No entropy/rank stability
assumption is hidden. If L_N is nonpositive the cardinality bound is
simply vacuous; the asymptotic claim remains valid.

7. STRICT MOORE CONVENTION: DIFFERENT EXACT ATTAINMENT

The lower bounds in Sections 4-6 remain true for a strict state-emitting
HMM, but claiming its entropy minimum is attained with the candidate's
edge kernel would be an interface error. The exact Moore answer here is
 K_min=2N+1,
 min H(Z_t)=ln3+(2/3)ln N.

Proof: every positive hidden state has one current output type, by the
support rectangle argument for (A_t,A_{t+1}) conditioned on Z_t. In the
current-subset phase, that pair law is balanced incidence, requiring
at least N hidden states and conditional entropy >=ln N. In the
current-index phase, apply the same incidence argument to
(A_{t-1},A_t) conditioned on Z_t; past and current emission are also
independent conditional on Z_t in a Moore HMM. This phase likewise
needs N states and entropy >=ln N. The blank phase needs one state.
The three phases have stationary probabilities 1/3. These statements
prove both lower bounds separately.

For attainment, use N subset-phase states s_i, N index-phase states
r_i, and one blank state b. State s_i emits a uniformly random balanced
S containing i (probability 2/M each), then moves deterministically
to r_i. State r_i emits i and moves to b. State b emits blank and moves
uniformly to one of the s_i. Each phase has mass 1/3 and the first two
phases are uniform over i. It generates exactly the candidate process.
This clarification does not falsify the source's explicitly edge-emitting
simulator; it prevents a cardinality-preserving identification of the
two HMM conventions. The quantum-instrument update naturally corresponds
to the edge-emitting convention.

8. SUPPLIED-INSTRUMENT SIMULATOR TRANSFER B1: VALID

Given ONE EB channel Gamma=R M with statewise error <=epsilon on the
initial state and every ORIGINAL positive-probability history state,
the finite kernel
 q_0(z)=Tr(M_z rho_0),
 T(a,z'|z)=Tr(M_z' L_a(sigma_z))
is positive and normalized. It implements Gamma before the first
instrument, and after each output before the next use. The final
Gamma is probability preserving and does not add an error term.

The source's reverse-order hybrid is the decisive valid argument. For
hybrid j, the first j outputs use the original instrument with no reset;
then Gamma is inserted and the remaining uses reset. Comparing j and
j+1 invokes the promise only on original-prefix conditional states.
The cq prefix trace distance is bounded by the original prefix-weighted
average of their statewise errors, <=epsilon. The entire future hybrid
is CPTP, so each output-law difference is <=epsilon. Summing exactly
n comparisons gives TV(P_n,Q_n)<=min(1,n epsilon).

The inserted Gamma can send trajectories outside E. No invariance
Gamma(E) subset E is required by this proof. The decoder states may
also lie outside E; full-domain supplied L_a is essential for defining
T on them. The kernel is the same for all horizons. It need not preserve
stationarity, offer infinite-path TV, be efficiently acquired, or have
capacity-controlled state count. At exact epsilon=0 it realizes every
finite output law exactly and thus the full process in the ordinary
cylinder-law sense. Classical feedback is allowed if the ONE common
channel controls the uniform union of reachable states under all
admitted policies. Entangled reference guarantees do not follow.

For TV<=1/100 its independent parity-entropy proof gives
 H(W_t)>=(m/2-4)ln2,

This is a weaker fixed-error STATE-COUNT rate than the balanced
construction's Omega(N), but a larger explicit entropy coefficient.
The orthogonality OUTPUT alphabet has only N symbols, compared with
binom(N,N/2)+N+1 for the balanced construction. Both charge the actual
alphabet, quantum dimension, memory entropy, and description/computation
costs. Neither is a constant-dimensional quantum memory advantage.

An exposed strengthening derived from the baseline's own Gram identity:
let Pi=J/N and beta=2^(m-2)/d^2. Then
 T^2=Pi+beta(I-Pi), with 0<beta<=2/9.
In particular T^2 has every entry positive; the chain is irreducible,
aperiodic, and geometrically mixing. Every two steps contract TV by
at most beta, since T^2=(1-beta)Pi+beta I. Thus this independent
capacity-versus-memory obstruction is not an artifact of the balanced
construction's periodic phase. This postexposure observation has not
been inserted into the frozen blind baseline.

it does not bound the common latent alphabet or Shannon memory. A
positive resource theorem must supply a separate bound on a compatible
common decoder/kernel's support or memory entropy, plus the cost of
acquiring it. A fixed-alphabet family, dimensional restriction, bound

hypotheses. Pair common information gives necessary lower bounds, not
a generic full-process construction. No such extra resource hypothesis
has been established here, and no foundational open problem is claimed
resolved by these transfer deductions or obstructions.
