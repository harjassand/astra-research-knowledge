## Legal interface and target

Let Phi(g)=Ag+psi(g)+b, where A:R^m→R^n is known, a=||A||op<1, b can be unknown, and the target is N(E Phi(G),I_n). Values of Phi and residual forward/reverse routines are supplied at the points required by the original cloud access model. Unknown b is never estimated. The legal assumption on the residual is stronger than final composite low Frobenius energy: its true derivative and the **realized direction derivatives** of both routines have operator≤ell, Frobenius≤sqrt(tau)ell, and nuclear≤tau ell uniformly, tau≥1. Conditional mean derivative errors and value errors must be charged separately as in source AppendixB.

Set C0=I-AAᵀ and propose Phi(B1)+C0^{1/2}Z, with ordinary Brownian B and independent standard Z. The proposal needs an evaluation or sample of Phi(B1); its affine part need not be subtracted from a hidden value to form this proposal. Full legal access to the residual action evaluation points is still required. This does not invent access to Gaussian labels or hidden centers that the source interface does not provide.

## Completion and signed correction

Clark–Ocone gives the centered Phi(B1) martingale with integrand A+J_t, where J_t=E[Dpsi(B1)|F_t]. Its covariance clock is

    Sigma=AAᵀ+Delta,
    Delta=integral_0^1 [A J_tᵀ+J_t Aᵀ+J_t J_tᵀ]dt.

Thus exact completing noise conditional on B is N(0,C0-Delta). Put D=C0^{-1/2}Delta C0^{-1/2}. Then D is symmetric but can be indefinite. Norm convexity and the residual budgets imply

    ||D||op≤beta=(2a ell+ell²)/(1-a²),
    ||D||_*≤tau beta.

Assume beta≤1/4. Conditional on B, the correct whitened covariance is I-D, and the conditional likelihood ratio versus standard Gaussian Z has logarithm

    w_infty=1/2 sum_{k≥1}[tr(D^k)/k-ZᵀD^kZ].

All signed eigenvalues lie in [-beta,beta], so the real series converges and the exact conditional normalizer is one. Brownian marginal is unchanged by exact tilting. The Gaussian completion proof then returns N(E Phi(G),I_n), including the unknown b.

At truncation K, let H_K=sum_{k=1}^K D^k. The tilted Gaussian covariance has eigenvalues

    C_K(lambda)=(1-lambda)/(1-lambda^{K+1}).

The truncated conditional normalizer and covariance errors are bounded by the same signed-series tail estimates, using sum_i |lambda_i|^{K+1}≤tau beta^{K+1}. Consequently the Brownian marginal error is O(tau beta^{K+1}) and the conditional Gaussian TV error is O(sqrt(tau) beta^{K+1}). Since tau≥1, total truncation is O(tau beta^{K+1}).

**Audit correction:** C_K is not necessarily a contraction: negative lambda can give C_K>1. Instead ||C_K||op≤(1+beta)/(1-beta^{K+1})≤2. The nonlinear moment argument under the truncated tilt therefore uses a universal covariance factor. Treating C_K≤I here would be incorrect, although it does not destroy the bound.

## Residual action and moment budget

At the original conditional Gaussian derivative points, form independent residual forward/reverse routines F,R and use

    S(w)=A R(w)+F(Aᵀw)+F(R(w)),

then congruence by C0^{-1/2}. This uses the **same** realized forward function F in its two terms; it is odd and its derivative operator, Frobenius and nuclear budgets are respectively beta, sqrt(tau)beta and tau beta. The cross-term bounds require the residual routines' own nuclear budgets; composite low Frobenius energy alone does not suffice. With exact mean actions, conditional expectation is D w; biased means are handled by an integrated error budget. Independent copies composed K times have scaled budgets beta^k, sqrt(tau)beta^k and tau beta^k.

The independently checked change-of-variables moment lemma in secant_transfer.md therefore gives

    e_p≤C beta[tau+sqrt(tau p)+p]/(1-beta), p≥2,

under both the proposal and the finite Gaussian tilt, allowing the universal factor for C_K. Source FORS clipping and the integrated bias comparison then apply. This gives a scoped operator with error O(tau beta^{K+1}+B(e_p/B)^p+charged bias/value errors). It is weaker than the exact-linear Frobenius refinement and is not a proof of replacing d in Theorem1.1.

## Concrete qI plus residual primitive

Let V(x)=q||x||²/2+U(x), q≥0 known, U convex, and assume uniformly ||Hessian U||op≤h and tr(Hessian U)≤tau h. The gradient U is evaluable as gradient V(x)-qx. Let

    p=prox_{eta V}(x+sqrt(theta)u), y=p+sqrt(eta)g,
    c=q eta/(1+q eta),
    N=[q sqrt(theta)/(1+q eta) I, q sqrt(eta) I].

The proximal identity yields the exact decomposition

    grad V(y)=qx/(1+q eta)+N(u,g)+psi(u,g),
    psi=grad U(y)-c grad U(p).

This avoids acquiring an unknown mean or a common residual projector. To preserve residual energy, implement this residual DAG directly. If D_U,y and D_U,p are source-style residual gradient secants and D_prox a proximal secant, the realized forward routine is

    J(u,g)=D_U,y(D_prox(sqrt(theta)u)+sqrt(eta)g)
           -c D_U,p(D_prox(sqrt(theta)u)).

For a fixed realization, each residual-gradient secant's direction derivative is the average of two residual Hessians, hence operator≤h, Frobenius≤sqrt(tau)h, nuclear≤tau h. The finite proximal approximation is 1-Lipschitz, so its secant's direction derivative has operator≤1 even if nonsymmetric. Splitting the two branches gives all three J budgets with

    ell=h[sqrt(theta+eta)+c sqrt(theta)].

The realized reverse routine is

    Jtranspose(w)=[sqrt(theta)D_prox(D_U,y(w)-cD_U,p(w)),
                   sqrt(eta)D_U,y(w)].

Its derivative splits into [sqrt(theta)P F1;sqrt(eta)F1] minus [c sqrt(theta)P F2;0], giving the same budgets. The two realized routines need not be matrix transposes. Mean accuracy follows the ideal symmetric primitive derivatives, while pathwise norm arguments require no such equality.

## Block selection preserves query accounting

Suppose Phi=b+sum_j A_j[N_j G_j+psi_j(G_j)] with disjoint Gaussian blocks. The baseline A is the horizontal concatenation of A_jN_j. Put B_j=C0^{-1/2}A_j and define

    q_j=||B_j||op²(2a_j ell_j+ell_j²), a_j=||N_j||op,
    beta_bar=sum_j q_j, p_j=q_j/beta_bar.

Zero q_j blocks can be omitted. The whitened covariance correction is a sum of block cross/residual corrections. Sample j with probability p_j and multiply its action by p_j^{-1}. Then each realized selected action has operator≤beta_bar, Frobenius≤sqrt(tau)beta_bar and nuclear≤tau beta_bar, and calls only that selected block's residual routines. This is a conservative parameter beta_bar rather than automatically the global beta above. Full values evaluate all blocks on accepted proposals; source call accounting remains recognizable.

For the paper's interior scalar-copy maps, the known covariance is a small J×J matrix tensored with I_d; endpoint covariance is 2×2 tensored with I_d. Generating the baseline Gaussian need not form a dense Jd covariance, although arithmetic, memory and precision remain charged separately from gradient queries.

## Consequence and obstruction

This merits a scoped affine-baseline operator: it explicitly avoids the full-rank known quadratic energy barrier while retaining the source's local gradient-query interface under a **uniform primitive residual trace budget**. The measure-change and covariance-completion ingredients are established theory; exact novelty of this combination is unverified. It does not rescue arbitrary composite energy or solve whole-sampler cost propagation. The next falsifiable step is to instantiate the main Picard cloud recursion with these known baselines and prove residual mean/RGO likelihood estimates and recursive subcriticality in the same charged interface. The original ambient-dimensional bounds still control those parts until new estimates are proved.
