EXPLICIT FINITE-GATE AND BIT-COST CLOSURE FOR AUDIT_REPORT SECTION 7
c03_s02, cross-phase continuation, 7 October 2026 UTC. INITIAL unchanged.

This appendix closes a standard-primitive detail in the mathematical compiler
consequence. It is a construction, not executed circuit synthesis. It uses a
fixed efficiently computable finite gate set; it makes no Clifford+T gate-
count or physical implementation claim. The central-binomial count factor
is retained. External correctness and priority remain UNKNOWN.

Why make the primitive explicit
------------------------------
Dawson--Nielsen, arXiv:quant-ph/0505030v2, Theorem 1 and its algorithm provide
the familiar fixed-dimension inverse-closed Solovay--Kitaev construction.
The paragraph at the end of Section 3 explicitly leaves finite-precision
arithmetic analysis aside. Therefore the paper's arithmetic-operation bound
alone is not used as certification of this audit's exact bit-cost claim.
The elementary polynomial-accuracy construction below suffices for our
weaker C_k poly(n,L,epsilon^-1,log(1/delta)) bound.

Fixed gate set
--------------
Let alpha=(sqrt(5)-1)/2. Include X, CNOT, Toffoli, Hadamard, the phase gate
P_alpha=diag(1,exp(2 pi i alpha)), and Y rotations by 4 pi alpha with zero,
one and two controls, together with the inverses of these finitely many
gates. All gates act on at most three qubits. Controls are positive; an X
conjugation implements a negative control. Every fixed gate entry can be
computed to b bits in poly(b) time by bisection for sqrt(5) and bounded-
argument Taylor series for sine and cosine. Density of powers around Y and
Z axes, plus CNOT, makes this a fixed universal gate set. Only these native
finite gates and exact classical reversible gates are used below.

Elementary uniform synthesis
----------------------------
For every accuracy tau in (0,1), find a Fibonacci denominator q=F_j with
64/tau<=q<128/tau, and p=F_(j-1). Adjacent Fibonacci numbers are coprime.
The identity F_j alpha-F_(j-1)=(-1)^(j-1) alpha^j and the Fibonacci formula
give |alpha-p/q|<1/q^2. The denominators increase by a factor at most two,
so j and the bit lengths of p,q are O(log(1/tau)).

For a desired angle theta modulo 4 pi, let t=theta/(4 pi) modulo one.
Approximate t within 1/(4q), round q times that approximation to an integer
h, and solve m p=h modulo q with 0<=m<q. The extended Euclidean algorithm
does this in poly(log q) bit time. The modular distance between m alpha and
t is at most

 m|alpha-p/q|+1/(2q)+1/(4q)<2/q.

Thus q-native-angle repetitions are not needed: m<q repetitions implement
the desired Y rotation, with the same controls, to operator norm at most
4 pi/q<16/q<=tau/4. Its maximum sequence length is O(1/tau). The same
argument for theta modulo 2 pi compiles a phase gate from P_alpha powers.
All relevant fixed target angles below are dyadic fractions of a turn, so
t itself is an exact rational. This synthesis has finite exact classical
bit work and no unbounded search or oracle.

Reversible conditional rotations without enumerating prefixes
-------------------------------------------------------------
The capped prefix program in AUDIT_REPORT computes three nonnegative exact
rational weights in polynomial-bit registers, from the occupation prefix
and the fixed depth tape. Boolean circuits for its bounded loops, integer
arithmetic, and comparisons can be implemented by Toffoli/CNOT/X while
retaining a history. Copy outputs; later reverse the history to erase it.
The circuit is unrolled from the structured integer-arithmetic program,
not from a simulation using a large randomly addressed memory. Each addition,
multiplication, division and comparison on b-bit registers has poly(b) exact
Toffoli/CNOT/X size; the number of such operations is the charged coefficient-
evaluation count times poly(n,k). One can retain the full operation history;
no reversible space improvement is assumed.

For clarity, remove prefix-dependent loop/tape addressing as follows. Build
one guarded program block for each cardinality class (a,b) in {0,...,k}^2.
The block is active exactly when the actual forced up/down cardinalities
match that class. Its sample counts, scalar z evaluations, and enumeration
of the 2^b down subsets are then fixed compile-time loop lengths. Extract
the b down-site labels from the prefix by an n-site scan and polynomial
selection circuits; a subset of those b labels is specified by its fixed
b-bit enumeration word. Allocate a separate fixed sample-sign array for
each class and child counter. Each array has an n-bit sign word per draw;
masking it by the actual forced/free roles preserves independent signs on
the free coordinates. All trace operations use fixed matrix indices.
There is no random lookup in an exponentially long seed array.

Each class obeys 2^b C_ab<=C_k. Summing over (k+1)^2 classes adds only a
polynomial factor. Inactive blocks may be controlled off using the computed
class flag; divisions are defined on all bit strings with harmless defaults
for zero inputs, and are exact on active valid prefixes. Invalid prefixes
return the already prescribed finite fallback. This construction gives
C_k poly(n,L,epsilon^-1,log(1/delta)) arithmetic gates, seed bits, scratch
and history, rather than an unspecified polynomial in an exponential runtime.

Encode a physical site by |00>,|01>,|10> for its three outcomes. For child
probabilities p0,p1,p2, rotate the first qubit by
theta_a=2 arcsin(sqrt(p2)), then rotate the second qubit conditional on the
first being zero by theta_b=2 arcsin(sqrt(p1/(p0+p1))). If p0+p1=0 take
theta_b=0. This gives exactly the vector (sqrt(p0),sqrt(p1),sqrt(p2)). The
all-zero weight case is the previously specified feasible-cardinality
fallback, with its angles chosen accordingly.

Exact endpoint angles 0 and pi are implemented exactly, rather than by
irrational-angle approximation. The pi rotation is XZ; its controlled and
two-controlled versions use controlled-X and controlled-Z (the latter from
Hadamard conjugation of CNOT or Toffoli). This keeps true zero child weights
exactly absent, including the cardinality-forbidden children. Interior
angles may be approximate because all affected children there are legal.
Boolean flags can be combined in scratch using Toffoli, so guarding an
endpoint/interior case does not require a prefix-sized native gate.

Compute each angle as a B-bit dyadic fraction of a turn. Each set angle bit
controls one constant rotation; the second conditional rotation has two
controls, one from the angle bit and one from the first site qubit. This
uses O(B) constant rotations per site, not one rotation for each possible
prefix. The angle registers can be uncomputed after rotating because their
input is the unchanged earlier prefix and the fixed tape. The current site
was initialized zero and is not an input to that depth's count program.

There is no inverse-small-probability arithmetic cost. A ratio of the exact
weights is a rational with polynomial bit length, even if its denominator
is tiny as a real number. At zero and one the angle is known exactly. On
the remaining bounded interval, sqrt and the angle may be computed by
interval bisection/Taylor evaluation. Near the endpoints the map arcsin
sqrt(x) has modulus of continuity O(sqrt(|Delta x|)); computing the function
values to O(2^(-2B)) therefore suffices for O(2^(-B)) angle accuracy. Those
extra B bits are polynomial. Ambiguous interval comparisons can stop with
an angle interval of the desired width. No analytically unstable relative
angle approximation is needed.

Phase kickback and cost
-----------------------
After amplitudes are prepared, compute the determinant and its prescribed
fermionic ordering sign exactly. On nonzero determinants scale by the
maximum absolute real/imaginary component. The resulting norm lies in
[1,sqrt(2)], so a B-bit argument is obtained by quadrant selection and
bounded atan bisection with polynomial bit work. Zero/invalid strings use
phase one. To apply a dyadic phase, apply its constant phase rotations to
the set bits of the angle register, then reverse the determinant/angle
computation. This gives the desired diagonal phase by kickback.

There are Q=O(nB+B) rotation/phase-bit gates. Choose
B=O(log(n/epsilon)) large enough that all angle-rounding operator errors
sum to at most epsilon/8, and synthesize each of these Q constant gates
with tau=epsilon/(8 max(1,Q)). Their operator errors sum to at most
epsilon/8, and their sequence length is O(Q^2/epsilon). This is polynomial
in n and epsilon^-1 and contains no multiplicative C_k^2: the vastly more
numerous prefix-arithmetic gates are EXACT native reversible gates.
Polynomial-bit classical arithmetic for the weights/determinants adds
C_k poly(n,L,epsilon^-1,log(1/delta)) total work and circuit size. Tape,
history, angle-computation and padding registers obey the same bound.

Together with the epsilon/2 weighted-hybrid error of AUDIT_REPORT Section7,
the total pure-state norm error is less than epsilon, hence so is trace
distance, for the stated probability over classical compiler seeds. This
constructs a finite description of a circuit over one fixed finite gate
set. It does not claim an efficiently recognized good seed, executed
synthesis, Clifford+T optimization, physical energy, or hardware accuracy.
