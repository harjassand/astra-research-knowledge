# Cycle3: gamma=1 does not imply eventual exact same-budget conversion

2026-10-06. Independent proof, finite known full-support binary-state experiments. This resolves a negative equality example, not the complete boundary characterization or novelty question. All observation costs are one; arbitrary state-independent auxiliary randomness and integrable stopping are allowed.

## Construction

Let F_0=(.9,.1), F_1=(.1,.9). Its two directed KL rates both equal d9=(4/5)log9. Every finite stopped F-record likelihood ratio is9^r for an integer r, since every observation contributes+/-log9 and auxiliary/stop decision factors cancel.

For a second FIXED experiment G, observe a branch label A or B as well as a binary outcome. The branch is randomly generated by the experiment, not selectable by the controller; each experiment is a singleton source menu. Conditional on A, the state laws are(.8,.2) and(.2,.8), with likelihood ratios4 or1/4 and directed KL d4=(3/5)log4. Conditional on B, the laws are(16/17,1/17) and(1/17,16/17), with likelihood ratios16 or1/16 and KL d16=(15/17)log16.

Choose the same branch probability under both states,
alpha=(d16-d9)/(d16-d4).
Since d4<d9<d16, alpha lies strictly in(0,1); all four G outcomes have positive probabilities. Branch labels carry no state information by themselves. Both directed G KL rates are alpha*d4+(1-alpha)*d16=d9, exactly. Thus the sharp first-order conversion coefficient gamma is one in BOTH directions.

## Equality of stopped and output KL forces likelihood equality

Suppose an integrable F-stopping policy outputs exactly n iid G outcomes and E_0 N<=n. Its stopped-record KL in direction0/1 is d9 E_0N, by bounded-increment optional expectation. Output KL is n d9. Data processing therefore gives
n d9<=d9 E_0 N<=n d9.
All equalities hold, including zero information loss from stopped record to finite terminal output Z.

For a direct equality argument, let S be the stopped source log likelihood and Lambda(Z)=log(H_0(Z)/H_1(Z)). Stopped likelihood gives
exp(-Lambda(Z))=E_0[exp(-S)|Z].
Conditional Jensen yields Lambda(Z)<=E_0[S|Z]. Equality of total KL implies equality for every positive-probability output. Strict convexity of-log implies exp(-S) is constant conditional on each such output, hence S=Lambda(Z) almost surely. This argument requires only integrability of S and a finite full-support terminal output.

The target output z consisting of n branch-A outcomes all favoring state0 has positive probability and likelihood ratio4^n. But the source stopped ratio can only be9^r. For n>=1,4^n cannot equal9^r for any integer r: negative/zero r are excluded by4^n>1, and positive r contradict unique prime factorization. Thus exact G^n reproduction within expected F-budget n is impossible, for EVERY integer n>=1.

## Reverse failure

Every G observation contributes+/-log4 or+/-log16=+/-2log4. Therefore every finite stopped G-record ratio is4^r. Exact reproduction of F^n within expected G-budget n likewise forces stopped/output KL equality and a stopped ratio9^n on the positive-probability all-favor0 output. Unique prime factorization again rules this out.

The two experiments have identical directed KL arrays and first-order coefficients one, yet neither eventually stopping-dominates the other under exact same expected-sample budgets. The strict-dominance theorem is not contradicted: a lower-order positive overhead may still permit exact conversion, but it cannot be removed altogether here. No positive uniform numerical lower bound on that overhead is asserted.
