AXIAL HANKEL COUNTEREXAMPLE AND EXACT PPT TRANSFER
8 October 2026 UTC. Owned by c07_l02. INITIAL remains immutable.

SCOPE

For N=72 and delta=81/50, the H1 Hankel matrix for the Gaussian Dicke
populations is not positive semidefinite. This is an exact finite counterexample
outside the conjectured universal interval delta<=2 log 2 (since 81/50>2log2).
It does not disprove positivity for delta<=2log2, and a single finite N does
not decide whether any fixed delta<2 is eventually positive. It also does not
modify the independently joined all-N theorem at delta<=5/4.

EXACT MATRIX AND CERTIFICATE

Let p_k=exp[-(81/50)(k-36)^2/72]=exp[-9(k-36)^2/400] and
q_k=p_k/binom(72,k). Then H1=(q_(i+j+1))_(0<=i,j<=35). Equivalently,
if q^*_k=k!(72-k)!p_k, then H1=(q^*_(i+j+1))/72!, so its sign agrees
with the factorial-scaled matrix in the task.

The reproducible candidate and exact sign certificate are:

  axial_hankel_N72_delta81_50_exact.py
  axial_hankel_N72_delta81_50_exact.json
  verify_axial_hankel_N72_delta81_50.py

The vector is specified exactly in the JSON as z_i=num_i/den_i,
 v_i=2^(e_i) z_i, i=0,...,35. The search for z used 110-digit arithmetic
only to select a candidate. The independent verifier uses only Fraction and
integer arithmetic. It reconstructs the coefficients c_k in

  v^T H1 v = sum_(k=1)^71 c_k exp[-9(k-36)^2/400]

and bounds each exponential outward on the dyadic grid 2^-256. Specifically,
range reduction by 2^10 puts y=9d^2/(400*2^10) in [0,1/16]; the degree-35
odd and degree-34 even alternating Taylor sums bound exp(-y), and ten
outward-rounded squarings enclose exp[-9d^2/400]. The exact accumulated
interval is

  [-46241866545697988360726457954046964247616281970153451945998894612283919703,
   -46241866545697988360726457954046964247616277437831429380292800007015267215]
  / 2^256,

which is strictly below -1/3000. The rational replay passes and takes under
one second in the local run. No floating-point sign decision enters the
certificate.

EXACT BALANCED-CUT PPT WITNESS

Let A and B be the two halves, each containing 36 qubits, and let
|D_r^36> denote the normalized r-excitation Dicke vector. The unnormalized
symmetric Gaussian state is
 S=sum_(k=0)^72 p_k |D_k^72><D_k^72|.
Using
 |D_k^72>=sum_i sqrt[binom(36,i)binom(36,k-i)/binom(72,k)]
                     |D_i^36>_A |D_(k-i)^36>_B,
the partial transpose on A preserves the difference-excitation block spanned
by |D_(i+1)^36>_A |D_i^36>_B, i=0,...,35. Its entries are

 B_(i,j)=d_i d_j q_(i+j+1),
 d_i=sqrt[binom(36,i+1)binom(36,i)]>0.

Thus B=D H1 D. With the exact rational vector v in the certificate, set
y_i=v_i/d_i. Then y is an explicit real algebraic vector and

 y^* S^(T_A) y = v^T H1 v < -1/3000.

So S, and its normalized version sigma=S/Tr(S), are NPT across 36|36.
Since 1<=Tr(S)<=73, the normalized witness expectation is at most
-1/219000<-2^-18. The witness definition and interval are exact; no
approximate eigenvector is used.

FINITE-ALPHA FULL GIBBS TRANSFER

For the 72-qubit state
 rho_alpha proportional to exp[(alpha J^2-delta J_z^2)/72],
with delta=81/50 and alpha=300 log 2, the same state is also NPT across
36|36. In total-spin sector l<=35, the Casimir factor relative to the top
l=36 sector is at most exp(-alpha), because
[36*37-35*36]/72=1. The axial factor is at most 1, and total Hilbert-space
dimension is 2^72. Hence the total lower-sector trace after removing the
common top-sector Casimir factor is at most 2^72 exp(-alpha). The top-sector
trace is Tr(S)>=1. Therefore
 ||rho_alpha-sigma||_1 <= 2^73 exp(-alpha).

For a bipartition with dim(A)=2^36, partial transpose obeys
||X^(T_A)||_1<=2^36||X||_1. From the certificate, max_i|z_i|<10^14,
max_i e_i=34, and d_i>=1, so ||y||_2^2<36*(2^81)^2<2^168. The change
in the witness expectation is consequently at most

 ||y||_2^2 ||(rho_alpha-sigma)^(T_A)||_1
 < 2^168 * 2^36 * 2^73 * exp(-300 log 2)=2^-23.

This is smaller than 2^-18, the certified magnitude lower bound for the
negative expectation of sigma. Thus rho_alpha remains NPT. This transfer is
only for the explicit finite parameters N=72, delta=81/50, alpha=300 log 2,
with no field term; it makes no all-N or sampler claim.

LOW-ORDER GATE AND FAILED ROUTE

For every k=0,...,N-2, define r_k=q_(k+1)/q_k. Then
 r_(k+1)/r_k
 = [1+(N+1)/((k+1)(N-k-1))] exp(-2delta/N).
The minimum of the bracket is attained at the central
integer(s). At delta<=2log2 this proves adjacent log-convexity for every N:
for even N the threshold is N log(1+2/N), with equality 2log2 only at N=2;
for odd N=2n+1 it is (2n+1)/2 log(1+2/n)>2log2. Hence all 2x2
principal minors of each Hankel matrix pass in the conjectured range. This
is only a necessary low-order gate; it does not establish higher Hankel PSD.

Rejected route: q_k is a beta-prime moment times exp[-delta(k-N/2)^2/N].
That Gaussian factor is inverse Gaussian smoothing in the log variable, not
a positive multiplicative Gaussian mixture. Continuous Gaussian
deconvolution therefore cannot be asserted as a positive measure. The exact
finite certificate above avoids that invalid inference.

EXPLORATORY DATA, NOT THEOREM

High-precision normalized LDL exploration (120-250 decimal digits) found
negative pivots at delta=1.8 for N=20,30,...,100 and at delta=1.62 beginning
at N=72. These floating computations motivated the N=72 witness but are not
used for the exact sign proof, and the finite list does not show persistence
for unbounded N. At delta=2log2, exploration through N=100 found no negative
pivot beyond the exact N=2 zero; that too is diagnostic only. The universal
all-N claim through 2log2 and the eventual fixed-delta<2 question remain
open in this audit.
