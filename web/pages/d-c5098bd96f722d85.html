Exact statement under review
----------------------------

For fixed J,tau>0, |lambda|<=B and g in a compact interval
[g_-,g_+] with g_->0, the finite open chain has S=L-1 sites, critical
sector form Hcrit, contact count W=sum_i n_i n_{i+1}, and Gibbs operator at
beta=tau L^2 for Hcrit+(g/L)W-(lambda/L^2)N. In the m-particle sector, the
gap coordinates y_i=x_i-i+1 are weakly ordered on {1,...,n}, n=L-m.
As L->infinity, fixed m and then the grand-canonical direct sum converge in
trace norm, uniformly on the stated compact parameter rectangle, to the
bosonic Fock Gibbs state for the repulsive Lieb-Liniger form

  q_{m,g}(F)=2J int_[0,1]^m |grad F|^2
              +2g sum_{a<b} int_{x_a=x_b}|F|^2 d(collapsed coordinates),

on symmetric H^1_0([0,1]^m). This is a full density-operator limit, not just
a scalar thermodynamic limit. The continuum embedding is an ideal public
Hilbert-space channel; it is not a finite-register implementation or a
Gibbs-state preparation algorithm.

Audit of load-bearing steps
---------------------------

1. The polar embedding is exact. If the tie multiplicities of y are r_v,
then its labeled orbit has w(y)=m!/prod_v r_v! elements. The map
U_m psi(z)=psi(sort z)/sqrt(w(sort z)) is an isometry onto the symmetric
subspace of the full labeled cube. Centered product cells of side
h=1/(L-m+1) around sites hz have volume h^m, are disjoint, and leave only
outer half-strips; assigning h^(-m/2)U_m psi on them gives an exact
isometry into continuum L^2. Zero ghost nodes at 0 and n+1 ensure the
multilinear interpolant has zero outer trace. I found no boundary-domain
or normalization error here.

2. The fixed-m kinetic lower bound has the sharp coefficient. Put
K_m psi(z)=psi(sort z)/sqrt(m!), u=h^(-m/2)K_m psi, and
E_h=h^(m-2) sum_edges |Delta u|^2 over the full labeled grid including
ghost-normal edges. The exact archive comparison
K_m^* dGamma(h_n) K_m <= Hcrit,m gives
2J(Lh)^2 E_h <= L^2 <psi,Hcrit psi>.
Tensor multilinear interpolation satisfies
int |grad I_h u|^2 <= E_h; weak lower semicontinuity therefore yields
2J int |grad F|^2. The local-star incidence 3^m in Sections 2-3 of the
final file concerns Q1 interpolation stencils only. It does not count
ordered-to-labeled orbit lifts; those are handled separately by the exact
factor m! in the archive comparison. This resolves the ambiguity in the
earlier draft/leaf audit.

3. The tie defect is controlled by the scaled contact energy. The exact
polar embedding and K-step satisfy
||U_m psi-K_m psi||^2
 = sum_y |psi(y)|^2 (1-sqrt(w(y)/m!))^2
 <= <psi,1_{W>0}psi> <= <psi,W psi>.
On a bounded scaled-form sequence, the latter is at most C/(g_- L).
Also ||I_hu-U_hu||^2 <= m 3^m h^2 E_h. Thus exact embedded vectors and
interpolants are close, giving collective compactness in L^2 and
H^1-weak subsequential limits.

4. The contact quadrature has the correct normalization. For each labeled
pair a<b, the centered collapsed-node step D_{h,ab} obeys
||D_{h,ab}||^2=h^(m-1)sum_{z_a=z_b}|u_z|^2
                   =h^(-1)sum_{z_a=z_b}|K_m psi(z)|^2.
The star/path estimate in the final file gives
||D_{h,ab}-Tr_{x_a=x_b}I_hu||^2 <= m 3^m h E_h.
The factor h follows because collapsed cells have volume h^(m-1),
whereas E_h weights an edge sum by h^(m-2). Compactness of the H^1 trace
into L^2 on the fixed diagonal and strong bulk L^2 convergence imply
strong diagonal trace convergence.

The exact multiplicity step is valid as written in final Section 4. For
tie clusters r_v, let t=prod_v r_v!, W=sum_v(r_v-1), and
P=sum_v binom(r_v,2). Since t>=r_v for every v,
t W >= sum_v r_v(r_v-1)=2P. Since w=m!/t, this is equivalent to
m!W>=2wP. Grouping the full labeled pair-diagonal sum over the w orbit
points proves
gL<psi,W psi> >= 2gL sum_z sum_{a<b}1_{z_a=z_b}|K_m psi(z)|^2.
This includes triple and higher coincidences with the correct direction;
it does not discard their lattice mass. The previous bbgky audit's concern
is resolved by the final file's explicit definition of w and the orbit
sum. It is also equivalent to the sharper displayed blockwise inequality
2P/prod_v r_v! <= W.
As Lh->1 at fixed m, the limiting full-cube coefficient is 2g. Restriction
to a symmetric ordered chamber gives reduced-face coefficient g and
Robin condition partial_r psi=g psi/(4J), r=x_(i+1)-x_i. The factor is
not g/(2J) or g/(8J).

5. Recovery handles the exceptional collision strata. For symmetric
F in C_c^infinity, the source vector
psi_L(y)=sqrt(m!) h^(m/2) F(hy)
has the intended norm and embedded L^2 limit. Away from ties the K kinetic
form and source exclusion kinetic form agree by the m! orbit count.
Edges incident to a tie are O_m(n^(m-1)); smooth increments are O(h),
so after L^2 scaling their discrepancy is O_m,F(h). A single double tie
has exact contact multiplicity. Multiple tie intersections have
O_m(n^(m-2)) nodes and contribute O_m,F,g(h). Smooth symmetric compact
functions are dense in symmetric H^1_0; the trace inequality makes this
form-norm dense, so a diagonal approximation gives recovery for every
form-domain vector. No operator-domain smoothness is being assumed.

6. The resolvent and trace upgrades are substantive and present. The exact
embeddings identify all finite sectors with subspaces of one continuum
Hilbert space. The liminf/recovery argument gives strong convergence of
the variational resolvent minimizers. Collective compactness plus
self-adjointness and compactness of the limiting resolvent upgrades this
to operator-norm resolvent convergence, uniformly for compact g. Continuous
functional calculus gives operator-norm convergence of the embedded heat
operators. The separate half-temperature SSEP trace bound supplies the
high-energy tail; positive-operator finite-block compression then yields
actual trace-norm heat-semigroup convergence. The proof does not rely on
Mosco convergence alone to infer trace convergence.

7. The infinite sector interchange is closed by a separate estimate. The
all-sector theorem in proof.txt bounds
Z_{L,m}(lambda,g) <= A^m exp(-tau c m^2)
with A,c independent of L,m over the stated parameter set. Fixed-m
trace-norm convergence passes this bound to continuum sectors. Splitting
the direct sum at m<=r and summing the Gaussian tails proves full Fock
trace-norm convergence; vacuum mass gives Z>=1 for normalization. The
public two-way channel is parameter-blind. This step depends essentially
on g_->0 and compact lambda; the separate zero/negative-coupling
obstructions must remain visible.
