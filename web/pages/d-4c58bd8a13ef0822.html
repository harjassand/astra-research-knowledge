# Independent Gram-structure boundary

Worker `metric_geometry10`, 7 October 2026, cycle13. **PASS for the original identities and for the two exact structural counterexamples.** These are obstructions to a proposed proof route, not acquisition-detector counterexamples. The reader should not infer universal cyclicity, a fixed aligned metric or a Jordan structure from positivity of the point-dependent Gram form.

## Interface and valid deductions

At an H-invertible point of a homogeneous quartic, let (T=f^{(4)}/24), (H=12T(x,x,\cdot,\cdot)),

\[
L_u=12H^{-1}T(x,u,\cdot,\cdot),\quad
S(u,v)=\operatorname{tr}(L_uL_v),\quad R=H^{-1}S,
\quad M=H^{-1}(T:H^{-1}),\quad V=H^{-1}\nabla\log\det H.
\]

The multiplication (u\star v=L_uv) is commutative by tensor symmetry, has unit x, and is H-metrized: \(HL_u\) is symmetric. The following reconstruction uses only those definitions:

\[
dH[u]=24T(x,u)=2HL_u,
\quad d\log\det H[u]=2\operatorname{tr}L_u,
\quad S(x,u)=\operatorname{tr}L_u.
\]

Consequently \(Rx=V/2\). Differentiating logdet once more gives

\[
\operatorname{Hess}\log\det H=24(T:H^{-1})-4S.
\]

Because \(dH[u]V=dH[V]u\),

\[
\operatorname{Jac}V=24M-4R-2L_V,
\qquad H\operatorname{Jac}V
=\operatorname{Hess}\log\det H-dH[V]
\]

is symmetric. Thus all three terms are H-selfadjoint. On an H-positive patch, every L_u is similar to a real symmetric matrix; for u nonzero the unit relation \(L_ux=u\) prevents \(L_u=0\). Therefore \(S(u,u)=\operatorname{tr}(L_u^2)>0\), and S and R are positive in the appropriate H sense. This is a point-dependent form, obtained from the coefficients at x, not a metric already aligned with the hidden axes. Positivity and invertibility do not alone make x cyclic.

The Levi-Civita Christoffel multiplication for the Hessian metric is 
\(\Gamma_u v=\tfrac12H^{-1}f'''(u,v,\cdot)=L_uv\). This relation is valid; it does not add an algebraic associativity or Jordan identity.

## Primary prior and excluded hypotheses

[Fox, *Commutative algebras with nondegenerate invariant trace form and trace-free multiplication endomorphisms*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12343v2), v2 dated 14 May 2020, introduction pp1–3, equations (1.1),(1.3),(1.4), separates an invariant metric from an invariant Killing-type trace form. Nondegeneracy/invariance of the latter are additional hypotheses; our unit also prevents the trace-free multiplication condition because \(L_x=I\). Those stated hypotheses cannot be imported into this product. This is an excerpt-level source comparison, not a full-paper review or a claim that Fox studies this quartic-acquisition interface.

[Hildebrand, *Hessian potentials with parallel derivatives*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.7366v1), 29 March 2013, Section2 equation (1), Section3 condition (3), Lemma3.3 and Theorem3.9, derives a metrised Jordan algebra under a Levi-Civita-parallel third-derivative condition. For a quartic it is impossible on an H-invertible patch: write \(C=f'''\) and X for the radial position field. Ordinary differentiation gives \(\partial_XC=C\), whereas \(\Gamma_X=I\), so

\[
\widehat\nabla_XC=C-3C=-2C.
\]

Parallel C would vanish, contradicting \(C(X,\cdot,\cdot)=2H\). Hildebrand's difference-tensor convention uses minus the Christoffel product; that convention does not remove the failed parallelism hypothesis. This radial exclusion is our deduction. ## Consequence and next falsifiable step

A general dense cyclicity theorem needs a property actually forced by the quartic/QPD promise. The valid H-selfadjoint, positive-Gram identities are available, but neither of the two stronger algebraic identities is available. The next mathematical discriminator is a direct spectral/Krylov argument, or a proved link from a proper joint word span to quartic symmetry.
