Verdict: I find no mathematical gap in the submitted variable-energy proof.
It proves the requested localization estimate in the only remaining
regime:

  n -> infinity, l/n -> 0, l^2/n -> infinity
       implies u_l^2 <= 147456/n
       and |eta_(n,l)|^2 <= 147456 n/l^2 -> 0.                 (A)

Since l^2/n -> infinity, the first estimate is

1. Normalization and the residual scale

For n=2j, the normalized four-spin column is

  u_k = sqrt((2k+1)(2l+1)) {j j k; j j l},
  sum_(k=0..n) u_k^2 = 1.

At k=l this gives the exact diagonal conversion

  u_l=(2l+1){j j l; j j l},
  |eta_(n,l)| = d |{j j l; j j l}|
              = d |u_l|/(2l+1),  d=n+1.                   (1)

Thus no d or (2l+1) factor is missing in (25). The exact recurrence used
in RACAH_MIDDLE_COMPACTNESS.txt is

  L u_k=A_k u_k+B_k u_(k+1)+B_(k-1)u_(k-1),
  L=l(l+1), N=n(n+2), d=n+1,
  A_k=[N-k(k+1)]/2,
  B_k=(k+1)[d^2-(k+1)^2]/[2 sqrt((2k+1)(2k+3))].          (2)

It has the stated normalization by 6j orthogonality. On the diagonal,
u_l has magnitude \((2l+1)|eta|/d\). The already audited fixed-fraction
Jacobi compactness argument and the coherent low/log and recoupling
endpoint bands reduce a hypothetical vanishing-ratio sequence to

  n -> infinity, l/n -> 0, z=l(l+1)/sqrt(n(n+2)) -> infinity,
  eta -> 1.                                                (3)

Here z -> infinity is equivalent to l^2/n -> infinity. Conditions
n>=64, l^2>=n, 8l<=n therefore hold eventually.

2. Exact alternating gauge and coefficients

Set v_k=(-1)^k u_k and p_k=B_k. Substitution into (2), with no boundary
recurrence used, gives for 1<=k<=n-1

  p_k(v_(k+1)-v_k)-p_(k-1)(v_k-v_(k-1))=-q_k v_k,
  q_k=L-A_k+p_k+p_(k-1).                                  (4)

For s>=1 put g(s)=(1-1/(4s^2))^(-1/2) and
e(s)=(d^2-s^2)(g(s)-1)/4. Direct substitution of B_k gives

  p_k=[d^2-(k+1)^2]g(k+1)/4,
  q_k=L+1/4+e(k)+e(k+1).                                  (5)

Both p_k and q_k are positive and nonincreasing on the used index
range: p is a product of positive decreasing factors, and e is also a
product of positive decreasing factors. Rationalizing g-1 gives

  0 <= g(s)-1 <= 1/(4s^2),
  0 <= e(s) <= d^2/(16s^2).                               (6)

I checked the gauge sign and indices: with
h_k=p_(k-1)(v_k-v_(k-1)), (4) is exactly

  h_(k+1)=h_k-q_k v_k,
  v_(k+1)=v_k+h_(k+1)/p_k.                                (7)

3. Parameter rectangle and action transport

Assume n>=64, l^2>=n, and 8l<=n; put K=floor(5n/8). For l<=k<=K,

  n^2/8 <= p_k <= n^2/2,
  L <= q_k <= 2L,
  q_k/p_k <=16L/n^2 <=1/4+2/n <1.                        (8)

For the lower p bound, k+1<=5n/8+1<=11n/16, so
p_k >=(1-121/256)n^2/4 > n^2/8. For the upper bound use
g(s)<=2/sqrt(3), d/n<=65/64, and p_k<d^2/(2sqrt(3))<n^2/2.
For q, (6) gives q_k<=L+1/4+d^2/(8k^2); k>=l and l^2>=n make
this at most 2L. Finally 8l<=n yields
16L/n^2<=1/4+2/n. These estimates hold throughout every rectangle
between consecutive parameter pairs (p_k,q_k),(p_(k+1),q_(k+1)).

For frozen p,q define

  H(p,q;f,h)=q f^2+h^2/p-(q/p)fh.                         (9)

The map h'=h-qf, f'=f+h'/p preserves H exactly. In variables
x=sqrt(q)f, y=h/sqrt(p), its quadratic form is
x^2+y^2-sqrt(q/p)xy. Therefore q/p<=1 implies

  (qf^2+h^2/p)/2 <= H <= 3(qf^2+h^2/p)/2.                (10)

At fixed state, the logarithmic parameter derivatives are

  d_(log q)H=qf^2-(q/p)fh,
  d_(log p)H=-h^2/p+(q/p)fh.

Each has absolute value at most 3H under (10). Let
E_k=H(p_k,q_k;v_k,h_k). Equation (7) first preserves H at fixed
(p_k,q_k); changing to (p_(k+1),q_(k+1)) then changes log H by at
most three times the sum of the absolute log changes in p and q.
Both sequences decrease, and (8) gives p_l/p_k<=4 and q_l/q_k<=2.
Consequently

  E_l/512 <= E_k <=512 E_l,       l<=k<=K,                (11)

because exp(3(log 4+log 2))=512. H is positive on a nonzero state.
If (v_k,h_k)=(0,0), the recurrence forces the whole column to vanish,
contradicting its unit norm; so the logarithmic comparison is legitimate.

4. Cutoff identity, sign, and boundary check

Use the stated piecewise-linear cutoff chi(k/n), zero off [1/8,3/4],
equal to one on [1/4,5/8], with |chi_(k+1)-chi_k|<=8/n. For n>=64,
chi_0=chi_1=chi_(n-1)=chi_n=0. Thus multiplying (4) by
chi_k^2 v_k and summing only over 1<=k<=n-1 uses no endpoint recurrence
or unestimated flux boundary term. Discrete summation by parts gives
the exact identity

  G := sum_(k=0..n-1) p_k(chi_(k+1)v_(k+1)-chi_kv_k)^2
     = sum_(k=1..n-1) q_k chi_k^2 v_k^2
       + sum_(k=0..n-1) p_k(chi_(k+1)-chi_k)^2 v_kv_(k+1). (12)

The last term has a PLUS sign. On an edge, the algebra is

  (b-a)(beta^2 b-alpha^2 a)
    =(beta b-alpha a)^2-(beta-alpha)^2 ab,

with a=v_k, b=v_(k+1), alpha=chi_k, beta=chi_(k+1); this confirms
the sign after moving terms in the summation-by-parts identity.

The cutoff transition edges reach past K, so the cross-term estimate
needs a global p bound. It follows directly from (5): p_k decreases,
and

  p_k<=p_0=N/(2sqrt(3))<=n^2/2   (n>=64).                 (13)

This supplies the global bound used in Sol's proof. On nonzero cutoff
support k>n/8, (6) gives q_k<=L+1/4+8(d/n)^2<L+10<=2L.
Since sum v_k^2=1 and sum |v_kv_(k+1)|<=1, (12) yields

  G <= 2L+(n^2/2)(64/n^2) =2L+32.                         (14)

Thus the cutoff transition error is charged explicitly, not discarded.

5. Propagated core and normalization

Take J={ceil(n/4)+1,...,floor(n/2)}. For n>=64,
|J|>=n/8; J lies in [l,K]; and chi_k=chi_(k-1)=1 for every k in J.
Hence the edge energies p_(k-1)(v_k-v_(k-1))^2 for k in J occur
inside G. Also p_(k-1)/p_k<=4 by the global upper bound (13) and the
core lower bound in (8). Therefore

  h_k^2/p_k <=4p_(k-1)(v_k-v_(k-1))^2.

Using (10), (14), and unit normalization,

  sum_(k in J) E_k
    <=(3/2)[2L+4(2L+32)]
    =15L+192 <=18L,                                      (15)

since L>=n>=64. The action transport (11) gives the opposite estimate

  sum_(k in J) E_k >= |J|E_l/512 >= n E_l/4096.           (16)

Thus E_l<=73728L/n. Since E_l>=L v_l^2/2, this proves

  u_l^2=v_l^2 <=147456/n.

Combining with (1), d<=2n, and 2l+1>=2l gives

  |eta_(n,l)|^2 <=147456 n/l^2.                           (17)

All constants and the core length have been checked. The core contains
at least n/8 consecutive sites, not merely one local wavelength; that
macroscopic length is what lets normalization turn the transported
energy into a pointwise bound.

6. Scalar closure and limitations

If Q_(n,l)=(1-eta)/(1-mu)^2 tended to zero, 0<1-mu<1 would imply
eta->1. The earlier exact recurrence compactness, endpoint bound, and
bounded-z Racah/Bessel estimates force n->infinity, l/n->0, and
l^2/n->infinity for any such sequence. Then (17) forces eta->0,
a contradiction. Each finite pair has Q_(n,l)>0 by the exact
commutator/connected-SU(2) argument in ALL_MODE_RACAH_PROOF §1.
Therefore inf_(n,l) Q_(n,l)>0: if the infimum were zero, a sequence
with Q->0 could be selected.

The resulting coherent comparison is a finite but non-effective
existence theorem: this audit does not compute a numerical a_* or C.
It is a squared comparison for the supplied SU(2)-covariant channel
with an actual common-marginal UCP extension. It does not prove sharp
c=2 domination, infer covariance/compatibility from bounded Holevo
capacity, or resolve unrestricted N93.
