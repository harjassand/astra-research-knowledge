# Cycle 2 — positive stochastic embedding and readout cost

**Result.** Every rational tuple (A_1,dots,A_5inmathbb Q^{d	imes d}), including a tuple selected from the N51/family-116 list, embeds exactly into five strictly positive doubly stochastic kernels on (m=d+1) states. This preserves the target formula’s inverse domain and nonzeroness. The construction can also be realized mathematically as five positive-rate CTMC pulses. Its signal scale, however, can be exponentially small in the tuple’s bit length; N51 does not yield a useful physical assay by itself.

## Exact construction

Let

\[
E=[e_1-e_m,\ldots,e_d-e_m],\quad
F=[I_d\ 0]-\frac1m\mathbf1_d\mathbf1_m^T,\quad
J=\frac1m\mathbf1_m\mathbf1_m^T,\quad S=[\mathbf1_m\ E].
\]

Then (FE=I_d), (EF=I_m-J), (F\mathbf1_m=0), (\mathbf1_m^TE=0), and (S^{-1}=[\mathbf1_m^T/m;F]). With entrywise absolute-sum norm, set

\[
K=1+\max_i\{\|A_i\|_{e,1},\|EA_iF\|_{e,1}\},\quad t=(64mK)^{-1},\quad c=1/2,
\]

\[
P_i=J+E(cI_d+tA_i)F=cI_m+(1-c)J+tEA_iF.
\]

The exact change of basis and decoder are

\[
S^{-1}P_iS=\operatorname{diag}(1,cI_d+tA_i),\qquad FP_iE=cI_d+tA_i,
\qquad A_i=t^{-1}(FP_iE-cI_d).
\]

Thus each (P_i) is doubly stochastic. Every perturbation entry is (<1/(64m)), while every off-diagonal baseline entry is (1/(2m)); hence every entry of (P_i) exceeds (31/(64m)).

The standard polynomial is multilinear and vanishes if any argument is a scalar identity. Consequently

\[
S^{-1}\Phi(P)S=
\operatorname{diag}\!\left(0,t^4s_4(A_1,A_2,A_3,A_4)(\tfrac32I_d+tA_5)^{-1}\right).
\]

The inverse exists because (t\|A_5\|_2<1/(64m)). If the N51 tuple is a hit, (Phi(A)) is invertible, so (s_4(A)=Phi(A)(I+A_5)) is invertible; the raw kernel output is therefore nonzero. The original value is recovered exactly by first decoding (A_i), or from the raw lower block (Y) using

\[
\Phi(A)=t^{-4}Y(\tfrac32I_d+tA_5)(I+A_5)^{-1}.
\]

## CTMC and molecular boundary

The (P_i) are close in operator norm to (P_0=\tfrac12 I+\tfrac12J). The path (P_0+u(P_i-P_0)) stays in the right half-plane. Its principal logarithm is real, and the resolvent integral gives

\[
\|\log P_i-\log P_0\|_2<4\|P_i-P_0\|_2<1/(16m).
\]

Since the off-diagonal entries of (\log P_0=\log(1/2)(I-J)) equal \(\log2/m>1/(2m)\), all off-diagonal rates of (Q_i=\log P_i) exceed (7/(16m)>0); also (Q_i\mathbf1=0). Hence (e^{Q_i}=P_i) are time-one kernels of valid dense CTMC generators.

This is a local sufficient case of the classical Markov embedding problem; arbitrary stochastic matrices need not be CTMC exponentials ([Casanellas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00818)). Receptor/allostery models and experiments provide finite-state biochemical precedent ([del Castillo–Katz 1957](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.1957.0018), [Monod–Wyman–Changeux 1965](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-2836(65)80285-6), [Guo et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1111/bph.14184), [Lu et al. 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25020-9)). They do not establish independent programming of these dense (m)-state rates. A physical test is conditional on resolving (m) states, preparing each start state, implementing five dense pulse generators, and classifying endpoints. It needs (5m^2) transition-probability readouts; direct (s_4) measurement can require (24m^2) sequence/start/end conditions. Compare against direct search over the same 24 schedules with equal calibration and assay budget.

## Precision and falsifier

If (H=\max_{i,a,b}|(A_i)_{ab}|), then (K\le1+4d^4H) and

\[
t\ge[64(d+1)(1+4d^4H)]^{-1}.
\]

For integer N51 matrices with nonzero (s_4(A)), the lower zero-sum output block satisfies \(\|Y\|_2>t^4/(2\sqrt d)\). Thus required binary precision can be polynomial in input bit length, but endpoint samples scale as inverse squared accuracy, (t^{-8}\operatorname{poly}(d)), for direct output readout. Since the source guarantees only polynomial *bit lengths*, (H) may be exponential in that input size; no inverse-polynomial experimental margin follows. Decoding integer (A_i) first improves the scale to (O(t)) accuracy and (t^{-2}\operatorname{poly}(d)) sampling, which can still be exponential in bit length. If (A_i) and the formula are already known, exact evaluation is offline and the assay supplies no new model information.

The source theorem is a coefficient-blind hitting-list claim for unrestricted matrix substitutions; its original inverse-node domains are preserved by its rational-formula reduction (see [cycle-one source reconstruction](FINAL_REPORT.md)). It supplies no biological parameters, controls, model acquisition, or selected practical tuple. Its package reports impractical constants, (>4\times10^{62}) tuples in an underlying (n=B=1) example, and an unexecuted full generator; external correctness/priority remain unresolved.

**Frozen falsifier for an assay proposal:** publish a materialized N51 tuple, compute (K,t), five kernels, and one nonzero output entry before data collection; then require a held-out confidence radius below half that exact entry and calibrated realization of all five (Q_i). An interval containing zero or failure to meet the frozen calibration/readout bound rejects that tuple’s assay bridge. The standard identity is prior art ([Amitsur–Levitzki 1950](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1950-0036751-9)); this is an internal encoding result, not an experimentally validated molecular result.
