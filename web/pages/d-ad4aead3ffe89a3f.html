# SNR spectral-flow corollary for the integrated Gaussian field

This is an independent analytic continuation of the conditional channel lemma in `INITIAL.txt`.

Let \(X\in\mathbb R^d\) be square-integrable, \(Y_\gamma=\sqrt\gamma BX+G\) with \(G\sim\mathcal N(0,I_m)\), and let \(C_\gamma=\operatorname{Cov}(X\mid Y_\gamma)\). For a fixed matrix \(B\), set the random field covariance

\[
A_\gamma(Y_\gamma)=B C_\gamma(Y_\gamma)B^*\succeq0,
\]

and conditionally generate \(Z_{\gamma,T}\sim\mathcal N(0,I_m+f_L(TA_\gamma))\). Assume \(J_L=\int_0^\infty f_L(u)^2u^{-2}du<\infty\), with the finite-moment conditions of (1). The matrix I-MMSE identity gives

\[
\frac{d}{d\gamma}I(X;Y_\gamma)
=\frac12\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}(B C_\gamma B^*).
\]

Applying (1) conditionally at each SNR and integrating this derivative yields, for every \(\Gamma>0\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^\Gamma\!\int_0^\infty
 I(Y_\gamma;Z_{\gamma,T})\,\frac{dT}{T^2}\,d\gamma
&\le\frac{J_L}{2}I(X;\sqrt\Gamma BX+G)\\
&\le\frac{J_L}{4}\log\det\!\left(I_m+\Gamma B\operatorname{Cov}(X)B^*\right).
\end{aligned}\tag{4}
\]

The last line is Gaussian maximum entropy for the additive Gaussian observation. If \(\|\operatorname{Cov}(X)\|_{op}\le\sigma^2\), then (4) is at most

\[
\frac{J_L}{4}\sum_{j=1}^m\log\bigl(1+\Gamma\sigma^2s_j(B)^2\bigr),
\]

where \(s_j(B)\) are singular values. This keeps the full spectral cost visible and does not assume that posterior modes are independent. With \(B=I_d\), the per-coordinate bound is at most \(J_L\log(1+\Gamma\sigma^2)/4\); the total remains proportional to the effective number of observed modes, consistent with the counterexample in `INTEGRATED_MI_NORMALIZATION.txt`.

This supplies a scale-integrated information-flow inequality for the explicit posterior-covariance Gaussian channel. It still does not identify the unavailable N75 field \(R(Y)\), recover the intended (f_L), or show that the N75 information functional has this two-parameter normalization. Those source-specific steps remain necessary before (4) can be used in a KLS argument.

**Status:** internally derived conditional corollary; I-MMSE and Gaussian maximum entropy are standard prior-art inputs. No numerical computation is involved. The SNR cutoff \(\Gamma\), profile integral \(J_L\), and singular-value budget are explicit costs.
