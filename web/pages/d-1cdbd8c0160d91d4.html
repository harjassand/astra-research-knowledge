1. EASY-PLANE SPIN SYSTEMS: CONDITIONAL CLASSICAL ALGORITHMS
Let
 H = sum_edges [alpha_e(X_u X_v+Y_u Y_v)+gamma_e Z_u Z_v]
        +sum_v [b_v X_v+c_v Z_v],
with rational alpha_e>=|gamma_e|, b_v>=0, arbitrary rational c_v, on an arbitrary
graph. The physical energy Hamiltonian is -H. Compatible signed gauges are
allowed when the signed-graph and transverse-field constraints are satisfied.

Conditional on Chen--Liu arXiv:2610.06724v1, homogeneous Theorem 5.1:
 - Z(beta)=Tr exp(beta H) has an FPRAS, polynomial in input bits, numerical
   beta times the total local norm bound, and inverse relative error.
 - lambda_max(H) has a relative-error FPRAS with polynomial BINARY-input cost.
   This is not a numerical-coupling loophole: C=3 sum alpha+sum b+sum |c|
   satisfies C/5<=lambda_max(H)<=C when C>0, and beta C=O(n/epsilon) suffices.
 - The related diagonal thermal sampler and logarithmic-size reduced-density
   reconstruction are separately audited extensions; see the final XXZ report.

Mechanism: shifted rational two-qubit Euler gates have six-vertex coefficients
inside the log-concavity triangle. One-qubit fields have an explicit homogeneous
two-dummy-variable lift. Wire constraints form a partition-matroid polynomial;
one additional uniform-matroid factor fixes the dummy degree. Thus only the
homogeneous counting theorem is required. Symmetric products B=W W* have a
controlled log-generator error, allowing relative trace estimates without a
factor exponential in system size or inverse partition function.

Why this could matter: it gives a precise candidate tractable region for quantum
thermodynamics and extremal energy on arbitrary graphs, including nonzero ZZ
couplings outside the named XY comparator. It does not solve general stoquastic
Hamiltonians, frustrated sign patterns, or prepare a quantum Gibbs state.
Named primary comparators include Bravyi--Gosset 1612.05602 and
Harrow--Mehraban--Soleimanifar 1910.09071; their parameter regimes differ.

Proof status: two independent derivations and root inspection of the homogeneous
lift; 120 small thermal matrix models and 7,200 local Hessian fixtures replayed
by root. No full Chen--Liu FPRAS was executed. Its polynomial exponents are very
large, so a practical speed claim would be unsupported.
Proofs: work/cycle2/tensor_contraction_audit.txt;
        work/cycle3/xxz_thermal_audit.txt (final audit).

2. FILTERED FERMIONS: ACQUIRED CLASSES AND CANONICAL BCS
For explicit rational complex Slater data and disjoint local occupation tables
b_j(k), factorial-normalized log-concavity b_j(k)/k! is a sufficient condition
for the COMPLEMENTARY counting polynomial. For two-mode sites it admits local
squared cross-ratio at most 2, including a bounded attractive regime.
This structural sufficiency follows established Branden--Huh theory and is not
presented as a new Lorentzian theorem. A different global orientation can enlarge
the admitted class; arbitrary partial complementation is invalid.

New executable acquisition: filter_rank_recognizer.py decides the exact
fixed-rank Lorentzian condition for strictly positive rational local tables,
using O(m^2) rational operations. It returns an explicit rejecting quadratic
derivative when the condition fails. It handles heterogeneous compensation that
simple per-site thresholds miss. Both global orientations are implemented.
Root replay: 1,146 decisions, 7,270 exact Hessians, 208 rejection witnesses;
a 120-mode instance required 40,553 dynamic-program transitions.
This is an implemented coefficient recognizer, not an implemented sampler.

Laminar repulsive occupation filters also admit exact prefix feasibility,
matroid-rank and common-base zero certificates. Crossing filters can fail:
an explicit four-mode example has two positive Hessian eigenvalues. Laminar
concavity itself has established Murota prior art.

Paired-state extension: particle-hole conversion maps opposite-spin paired BCS
states to graph Slaters, with explicit determinant phases. Squared onsite
Gutzwiller filters t_j>=1/2 fit the complementary counting interface. General
opposite-spin pairing is not automatically spin singlet (the latter needs the
appropriate symmetry). Generic spin-mixing/Pfaffian states are not included.

The strongest later step is an internally proved real-rooted charge polynomial for ALL
repulsive 0<=t_j<=1: split each pairing column into proportional forbidden and
allowed clones, then identify the charge polynomial with an expected principal
characteristic polynomial of a Hermitian chiral matrix. Stable directional
derivatives prove real-rootedness. For 1/2<=t_j<=1, rational fugacity search makes
a requested feasible particle number have inverse-polynomial probability.
Combining this with the grand-canonical counting/compiler gives canonical
preparation with no inverse ORIGINAL projection-probability cost. The original
attractive counterexample shows why this tilt step cannot be asserted for all
t_j>1. Final independent proof and prior-art verdict are recorded separately.

Following that theorem exposed a stronger hard-projection subclass. Suppose
the explicitly supplied pair matrix has F_AA=F_BB=0 for a site bipartition.
Particle-hole transformation of BOTH spins on B turns the state into a graph
Slater of rank 2|B|. Its local squared-filter tables are (1,1,t_i) on A and
(t_i,1,1) on B. Their factorial-normalized criterion admits every
0<=t_i<=2, including hard zero and a bounded attractive range.
Physical pair number k becomes an exact occupation quota on A. This is a
laminar/direct-sum constraint, giving a DIRECT canonical counting/preparation
reduction without a rare-number rejection step. Independent exact fermionic
phase and canonical-norm checks passed. At all-hard zero, diagonal F entries
can be discarded before checking the off-diagonal bipartition.
The coefficient proof uses separately homogeneous color factors; the raw
laminar-weight criterion alone would justify only t_i<=1.
For positive heterogeneous filters, the implemented recognizer can admit
more: t=(4,1,4,1) with two sites in each color and k=2 passes both exact
color tests. The runnable example is examples/check_canonical_filter.py.

This condition concerns the pair matrix, not every BCS state defined by a
bipartite mean-field Hamiltonian. The two classes differ; even familiar
nearest-neighbor BdG states need not satisfy this pair-matrix condition.
Prior art already contains particle-hole and Marshall-sign constructions.
The claimed increment is the conditional, acquired counting interface for
this precise class, including zero/prefix certificates and phase correction.

The general hard-projection norm has been reduced exactly to a determinant-
weighted linear-matroid-parity partition function. Support, zero detection and
witnesses admit a deterministic polynomial-bit reduction to Hamada--Iwata's
unweighted parity algorithm (2610.03030v1), using an explicit rational-complex
finite-field reduction. This support procedure is proved, not implemented
here; it does not count. Efficient general relative counting remains open.
Exponential-variance clone averages, shrinking fractional-log-concavity,
disconnected bounded-block walks, and failed parity-penalty balancing have
explicit counterexamples. Thus scalar real-rootedness alone does not close
the general algorithmic gap. A separate monomial/permutation-pairing subclass
admits exact rational transfer-matrix counting even on odd cycles.

A fifth attack cycle changed representation again, from selected pairs to
unoccupied ambient sites. Its signed generating polynomial is real stable.
This yields an all-size coefficient-one square-root symmetric-exchange
inequality, now independently reconstructed several ways. The shortest proof
uses reciprocal twists and an elementary odd-degree Hurwitz coefficient
inequality; it handles the common occupied core that initially blocked the
Pfaffian argument. The underlying homogeneous exchange mechanism has prior
art in Anari et al. (2004.07220), explicitly credited. The extension also
supports aggregate relocation/capacity bounds and an exact subdivision gadget.
However, nonnegative matching-overlay encodings have an exponential tensor
obstruction, and a shared-cut sewing map can annihilate positive input norm.
These are precise failed mechanisms, not a counting-hardness theorem. A new
microscopic determinant-weight sampling/energy comparison is still needed.

The general weak-filter and bipartite projected-state transfers retain the

The full FPRAS and quantum compiler are unimplemented. Conditional preparation
does not solve ground-state optimization or identify filtered free Gibbs states

3. CRITICAL QUANTUM SPINS: FULL SEPARABILITY AND A PRODUCT DECODER
Let J=(1/2)sum_a sigma^(a), and consider the known mean-field family
 rho_(N,A,b) proportional to
 exp[2J^2/N+Q_A(J)/N^(3/2)+b.J/N^(3/4)],
with uniformly bounded real symmetric A and vector b.

An internally derived construction approximates the WHOLE density operator by
a mixture of identical single-qubit products, with trace error O_(M,B)(N^-3/2).
An explicit finite-N binomial-sum certificate is supplied. This optimized
approximation depends on A,b. It uses an exact positive isotropic heat integral,
then stochastic collective filters whose trajectories are g^(tensor N).
Double-commutator Duhamel bounds control the error.

A distinct later result is a parameter-independent decoder from a quartic-limit
observation x:
 S_N(x) = [ exp(2 N^-1/4 x.sigma)/Tr exp(2 N^-1/4 x.sigma) ]^(tensor N).
Its error is uniformly O(N^-1/4); the separately audited weighted scalar
Laplace remainder is O(N^-1/2).
These two rates have DIFFERENT interfaces and must not be conflated. The latter
removes singlet preparation and entangling gates from the earlier reverse
experiment construction, at the level of the internally audited theorem.
The known-parameter importance sampler has O_(M,B)(eta^-3+N) expected work in
real arithmetic, plus public certificate costs. A separate finite-bit
existence construction passed an internal audit: rational enclosures, finite
normal approximations, a gap-free matrix square root, bounded weight selection
and charged local preparation give error E_sep,N+2eta+epsilon with conservative
polynomial classical bit cost for fixed M,B and admitted N with
2/N-M/N^(3/2)>0. Explicit small-N fallbacks have
large but charged constants. Neither construction is a certified implemented
sampler; no fixed-gate compiler or hardware run is supplied. Historical
priority, including the precise 1991 critical-fluctuation interface, is open.
