# Bounded scores and heterogeneous independent verifier tests

Status: COMPLETE_INTERNAL_DERIVATION, using the independently checked
spectral exposure construction. Historical novelty is unverified. This
is an additional interface deduction, not a claim that reward lifting or
heterogeneous concentration is a new principle.

## Exact interface

Let G_1,...,G_k be finite two-player one-round games, each with a finite
classical question law mu_i, finite nonempty answer sets A_i,B_i and
score V_i(x,y,a,b) in [0,1]. The referee samples the k question pairs
INDEPENDENTLY, sends each player the complete local question string,
and receives arbitrary joint local answer strings. The players can use
one joint strategy across all tests.

Fix one strategy model t: finite-dimensional tensor-product strategies
or arbitrary commuting-operator strategies. Put

    v_i=omega^t(G_i) for the expected score,
    d_max=max_i |A_i||B_i|,
    vbar=(1/k)sum_i v_i.

For every non-singleton component of every mu_i, let sigma_i,j be the
gap of I-(P_X+P_Y)/2 on L2(mu_i,j) off constants. Set
sigma=min_(i,j) sigma_i,j, using sigma=1 if all components are singletons.
Every test has finite support and sigma>0; a promised lower bound may
be used in place of its exact value. Dependence across coordinate
question pairs is excluded, even if the individual laws are correlated.

For any 0<delta<1-vbar, define S_k=sum_i V_i(X_i,Y_i,A_i,B_i).
Then every joint strategy satisfies

    Pr[S_k >= sum_i v_i + delta k]
       <=exp[-sigma delta^3 k/(2048(1+log d_max))].          (1)

The supremum obeys the same inequality. The result concerns the sampled
empirical score itself; the verifier does not need to toss the proof's
additional Bernoulli coins. All logarithms are natural.

## 1. Fractional tested masks preserve the exposure lemma

For analysis only, let the referee independently accept coordinate i
with probability V_i after observing its questions and answers, and let
I_i denote that Bernoulli accept indicator. This leaves one-game value
v_i and the question/answer alphabets unchanged.

Conditioning on accepts at C has tested mask

    theta(x_C,y_C,alpha,gamma)=prod_(j in C) V_j(x_j,y_j,alpha_j,gamma_j),

which lies in [0,1]. The exact coarse/fine conditional-answer identities
continue to hold after multiplying by theta; the independent coins have
simply been averaged out. With d_C=prod_(j in C)|A_j||B_j|, the only
source entropy step that explicitly used a Boolean mask is repaired by

    theta h0(h)<=h0(theta h),      h0(u)=-u log u,

since -theta h log theta>=0. Jensen over the flag and its d_C answer
pairs therefore still gives each potential's range
[-p log(d_C/p),0]. Nonnegative mask weights preserve both resolvent
Jensen increments.

The mixed flags also work under non-identical question laws: they fix
one local question at every live coordinate, and the remaining local
choices factor under product_i mu_i. At fresh coordinate i, the local
conditional averages use mu_i(x|y),mu_i(y|x). Thus the exact exposure
laws and total error budget remain

    sum_i,r,z w_ir mu_i(z)||Psi_ir(z)||^2=p,
    sum_i,r,z w_ir mu_i(z)c_ir(z)<=2p tau,
    tau=(log(1/p)+sum_(j in C)log d_j)/m.

The contribution of EACH live coordinate to the first sum is exactly
p/m. This individual mass identity matters when the v_i differ.

For the commuting model, the same assertions follow from
`COMMUTING_THRESHOLD_EXTENSION.md`: the positive normal functional
chi(A,B)=<psi,AB psi>, separate resolvent ancillas and commuting
transported POVMs handle arbitrary Hilbert spaces. The effect
W_ir(z)=sum_(a,b)V_i(z,a,b)Mtilde_(r,x)^a Ntilde_(r,y)^b lies in [0,I].

## 2. Per-coordinate barycenters and component drift

For each live i and support component j define
beta_ir,j=E_mu_i,j Psi_ir(z), component original mass P_i,j,
barycenter mass P_hat_i,j and variance cost E_i,j. Hilbert Poincare gives

    E=sum_i,j lambda_i,j E_i,j<=p tau/sigma,
    P_hat_i,j=P_i,j-E_i,j.

Construct the legal shared state using the r flag separately for each
fixed i,j. Do not mix different i and then compare the mixture to a
single game value: the winning predicates and values differ by i.
The component success obeys

    T_i,j<=v_i,j P_i,j+2sqrt(P_i,j E_i,j),

where v_i,j is the value of the i-th component game in model t.
Every local input identifies its component, and tensoring component
strategies gives sum_j lambda_i,j v_i,j<=v_i. The individual norm law
identifies

    lambda_tilde_i,j=m lambda_i,j P_i,j/p=Pr[J_i=j|E_C].

The unconditioned live-component vector has product law
product_(i notin C)lambda_i. Conditional likelihood ratio is at most
1/p, so the sum of its marginal relative entropies is at most log(1/p).
Since v_i,j lies in [0,1], Pinsker and Cauchy-Schwarz give

    sbar <= (1/m)sum_(i notin C) v_i
                  +sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m))+2sqrt(tau/sigma).     (2)

This accounts for the drift even when both component labels and their
payoffs differ among coordinates. At tau<=T=sigma delta^2/128 the
excess above the REMAINING value average is below delta/4.

## 3. The tail transfer still works when v_i vary

Apply the binary upper-tail sampling argument to W=sum_i I_i with

    q=vbar+delta, q0=vbar+delta/2, eta=delta/16,
    c=delta T/(16(1+log d_max)).

Its rate/budget arithmetic is unchanged after replacing log d by
log d_max. If Pr[W>=ceil(qk)]>=exp(-ck), sufficiently large length
selects C with h=|C|<=eta k and
Pr[W<q0k|E_C]<=eta, and with tau<T. On E_C all h tests are accepted.
Compared with the remaining average v_out=(sum_(i notin C)v_i)/(k-h),

    E[W-h|E_C]-(k-h)v_out
       >=(1-eta)(q0 k-h)-(k vbar-sum_(i in C)v_i)
       >=(delta/2-eta-eta)k
       =3delta k/8.

The last inequality uses q0<1, h<=eta k and v_i>=0. Hence conditional
remaining success exceeds v_out by at least 3delta/8, contradicting
(2) at budget T. This replaces the homogeneous source's cancellation;
using a global vbar in (2) without accounting for removed tests would
have been a gap.

The selected-set budget has numerator

    log(1/p_C)+sum_(i in C)log d_i
       <=log(1/p_H)+log 2+ell log(d_max/q),

so the source's arithmetic gives the same exponent c. To handle short
catalogues, tensor N independent copies of the entire k-test strategy
and catalogue. The catalogue mean vbar, d_max and sigma remain fixed.
The event that every copy crosses its block threshold implies the
global threshold. Taking roots removes the large-length restriction.
Thus Bernoulli accepts have the rate in (1) for every finite catalogue.

## 4. From randomized accepts to the actual bounded score

Fix a k-test strategy and let p_H=Pr[S_k>=sum v_i+delta k]. For any
0<delta'<delta, tensor N independent copies. On the event that every
block crosses the score threshold, the conditional mean of the total
Bernoulli accepts, given all questions and answers, is at least
Nk(vbar+delta). The independent analysis coins satisfy Hoeffding:

    Pr[total accepts <ceil(Nk(vbar+delta')) | all block score events]
       <=exp[-2(delta-delta')^2 Nk].

The Bernoulli result just proved therefore gives

    p_H^N(1-exp[-2(delta-delta')^2 Nk])
       <=exp[-sigma(delta')^3 Nk/(2048(1+log d_max))].

Take N-th roots and let N tend to infinity; the parenthesized factor
tends to one. Then let delta' increase to delta. This proves (1) with
exactly the same cubic rate, no prefactor or small-k exception. The
tensor-copy argument is allowed in both strategy models. It is a proof
device, not a change to the score verifier's protocol.

## Consequence and boundaries

A fixed public schedule can contain different finite tests, different
correlated samplers and fractional scores. If the mean completeness
and soundness bounds are cbar>sbar with Delta=cbar-sbar, a midpoint
threshold has completeness rejection at most exp(-Delta^2 k/8) under
independent honest component strategies, and joint-strategy soundness
at most exp[-sigma(Delta/2)^3 k/(2048(1+log d_max))]. The intended
single-catalogue honest strategies need only approach their suprema;
Hoeffding applies to independent non-identical bounded scores.

This gives one soundness statement for a verifier's empirical aggregate
score, even when its constituent tests have different values. Efficient
use needs an efficient sampler, efficiently represented score/rational
threshold and supplied or certified spectral/soundness promises. We
do not acquire those promises or compute game values. Question/answer
length and the number of elementary checks scale with the catalogue size.
No sparse Hamiltonian or constant-query quantum PCP is established.
