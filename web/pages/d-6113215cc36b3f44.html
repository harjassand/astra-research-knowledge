## 1. Escaping growth and the legal interface

Fix one a>=1, nondecreasing and unbounded, with |a^(m)(R)|<=C_m a(R)/R^m and a(R)=R^(o(1)). Define beta=R a'/a, eta=(Ra)^(-1/2). Then 0<=beta<=C_1. If beta/eta had a bounded tail, say <=M, then

    (sqrt(a))'=a'/(2sqrt(a))<=M/(2R^(3/2)).

The right side is integrable at infinity, contradicting unbounded a. Continuity bounds the ratio on every compact interval. Thus every tail contains arbitrarily large ratios. Recursively choose R_j with R_j/R_(j-1)>=2 and beta_j/eta_j>=j. One can impose any further fixed lower-radius requirement at the same time.

Fix K once through the original construction and set x_j=sqrt(R_j^2-K^2)e_1, A_j=a(R_j), ell_j=sqrt(R_j/A_j). Because eta_j=ell_j/R_j->0, the reserved macro cubes and their fixed enlargements can be made disjoint by deleting finitely many terms or passing to a further subsequence. Reserve b_j=1 on the 3B cubes and zero outside the 4B cubes, where B=4*pi*sqrt(n). The separate-family packing, derivative product rule, and preassigned tangent e_2 directions in the plateau gate do not use a'=0. They therefore give a legal partition with

    q=cos(A_j x_2)

exactly on each 3B cube. All cover, coloring, compact-family sparsity and weighted-repair constants remain uniform in j. Macro comparability follows from |a'|/a<=C_1/R; higher a derivatives are used only for the local Taylor estimates.

The order remains a,B,uniform geometry; theta,rho,tau; one K; one radial psi and one sufficiently small positive sigma; then the escaping subsequence and its asymptotic tests. Choosing R_j after K is harmless since every tail contains arbitrarily large beta/eta. The final cover can be realized at this K using constants specified uniformly beforehand. ## 4. First-order smoothing and a direct sign proof

The strict negative mean of alpha can be proved directly, without taking sigma to zero. In the open stripes A_c=0. Outside the stripes its regular part is -c.     alpha_sigma(zeta)<=0
