Title:Three-color van der Waerden numbers grow super-exponentially

Authors:Jacob Fox, Zach Hunter
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Three-color van der Waerden numbers grow super-exponentially, by Jacob Fox and Zach Hunter

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:For $k$ sufficiently large, we show that there is a three-coloring of the first $2^{k (\log^* k)/4}$ positive integers without any monochromatic $k$-term arithmetic progressions. Thus, the three-color van der Waerden number $w(k;3)$ grows faster than any exponential in $k$. We further prove a new lower bound on multicolor van der Waerden numbers which resolves a problem of Erdős and Graham on canonical van der Waerden numbers.
