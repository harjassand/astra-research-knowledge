RESULT

Under the fixed-d fundamental SU(d) hypotheses in
QUDIT_POSITIVITY_LOCALIZATION.txt, the weakly anisotropic Gibbs density

 exp[beta Q_I/N+Q_{A_N}/N^{1+delta}+J_{B_N}]/Tr exp[...]

is EXACTLY FULLY SEPARABLE for every sufficiently large N, with the explicit
scalar threshold below. This holds for every fixed beta>0, delta>0 and
uniform bounds ||A_N||<=M, ||B_N||<=B0, B_N traceless Hermitian. It covers
arbitrary axes and bounded unscaled uniform one-body fields. No assumption
of a high-temperature or central phase is needed.

An explicitly tiny, fixed anisotropy neighborhood is also fully separable:
there is a_star(d,beta,B0)>0 independent of N such that
 exp[(beta Q_I+Q_{A_N})/N+J_{B_N}]/Tr exp[...]
is exactly fully separable for every N>=d+1 and ||A_N||<=a_star. All
constants and the complete derivation are below.

This proves exact convex mixtures of general product densities, whose local
factors can differ from site to site. It DOES NOT upgrade the approximating
stopped law into an exact iid-product mixture. Permutation symmetry plus
full separability is not generally finite iid exchangeability. No exact
decomposition search or efficient physical implementation is asserted.

1. AN ELEMENTARY MULTIPARTITE FULL-SEPARABILITY BALL

Let T_0=I/sqrt(d) and choose traceless Hermitian T_1,...,T_(d^2-1) with
Tr T_aT_b=delta_ab. Each ||T_a||_op<=||T_a||_HS=1. For a tensor index
s=(s_1,...,s_N) not identically zero, let r_s count its nonzero entries and
let P_s have factor T_(s_i) when s_i!=0 and factor I otherwise. Then

 I/d^N +/- P_s/d^N                                                    (S1)

is fully separable. Indeed, at the r_s active sites use local states
(I+epsilon_i T_(s_i))/d, epsilon_i in {+1,-1}, and average uniformly over
signs conditioned on product epsilon_i=+1, or respectively -1. Expansion
and the parity character kill every nonempty proper subset, retaining
exactly I +/- the full product. At inactive sites use I/d. This proves
(S1) as an explicit finite mixture; r_s=1 is included by a single sign.

For any Hermitian E with Tr E=0, expand in the orthonormal product HS basis
 E=sum_(s!=0) delta_s (tensor_i T_(s_i)).
Parseval gives sum delta_s^2=||E||_HS^2. Since
 tensor_i T_(s_i)=P_s/d^{(N-r_s)/2}, put
 a_s=d^{(N+r_s)/2}delta_s. Then

 I/d^N+E = (1-sum|a_s|)I/d^N
              +sum|a_s|[I/d^N+sign(a_s)P_s/d^N].                       (S2)

This is a convex combination of fully separable states if sum|a_s|<=1.
Cauchy--Schwarz and a binomial count give

 sum|a_s| <= [sum_(s!=0) d^{N+r_s}]^{1/2}||E||_HS
 = {d^N[(1+d(d^2-1))^N-1]}^{1/2}||E||_HS
 <= d^{2N}||E||_HS <=d^{2N}||E||_1.                                  (S3)

The inequality uses 1+d(d^2-1)=d^3-d+1<=d^3. Therefore

 Tr E=0, E=E*, ||E||_1<=d^{-2N}
           ==> I/d^N+E is fully separable.                            (S4)

This loose but adequate exponential-radius ball is fully proved here;
there is no imported multipartite-ball theorem or hidden Hilbert-space
dimension convention. Its norm is ordinary full trace norm.

2. THE STOPPED MIXTURE HAS AN EXPLICIT INTERIOR COMPONENT

Every endpoint of a diffusion stopped at lambda_min rho=eta satisfies
rho>=eta I. Thus with p=d eta in (0,1),
 rho=p I/d+(1-p)rho',   rho'>=0, Tr rho'=1.
Tensor binomial expansion gives
 rho^{tensor N}=p^N I/d^N+(1-p^N)tau_rho,
where tau_rho is a positive mixture of general product states. Average with
the normalized positive stopped endpoint law to obtain

 sigma_N=(d eta)^N I/d^N+[1-(d eta)^N]tau_N, tau_N fully separable.       (S5)

For a target density R_N, let E=R_N-sigma_N, with Tr E=0. If

 ||E||_1 <=(d eta)^N d^{-2N}=(eta/d)^N,                                (S6)

then I/d^N+E/(d eta)^N is fully separable by (S4). Substituting into (S5)
shows R_N is exactly fully separable. Only the closeness bound needs a
finite threshold; the implication itself is exact.

3. WEAK ANISOTROPY: AN EXPLICIT EVENTUAL THRESHOLD

Use the full proof's definitions
 k=max(1,sqrt(10M/beta)), eta_N=kN^{-delta/2},
 D0=3beta(d-1)+dB0, H0=2beta(d-1)/d+B0,
 C0=log k+dlogd+D0,
 L_N=(delta/2)log N-C0,
 h_N=beta(d-1)(N+d)/d+NB0<=H0N.
The acquired normalized HALF trace-distance bound implies

 ||R_N-sigma_N||_1
 <=2h_N exp[h_N-N L_N^2/(4beta d(d-1))].                              (S7)

An exact public sufficient test for full separability is

 N>=d+1, N^delta>=2M/beta, eta_N<1/d, L_N>0,
 N L_N^2/(4beta d(d-1))
     >=h_N+N log(d/eta_N)+log(2h_N).                                  (S8)

No numerical root search is required for the following conservative explicit
threshold. Write log_+(u)=max(0,log u),
 a=delta^2/[64beta d(d-1)], b=delta/2,
 C_sep=H0+log d+log_+(2H0)+1,
 x_sep=[b+sqrt(b^2+4a C_sep)]/(2a).
For any integer N satisfying

 N>=d+1, N^delta>=2M/beta,
 logN>=max{4C0/delta,x_sep},                                          (S9)

we have L_N>=(delta/4)logN and eta_N<1/d. Also
 log(2h_N)/N<=log_+(2H0)+1,
 log(d/eta_N)<=log d+(delta/2)logN.
Thus (S8) follows from a(logN)^2>=b logN+C_sep. Combining with (S6)
proves exact full separability for every N at or above (S9), uniformly over
all supplied A_N,B_N within their stated bounds. The constants can be
enormous and this result does not guarantee useful moderate-N thresholds.

4. A FIXED OPEN ANISOTROPY BALL WITH EXACT SEPARABILITY

Fix d,beta,B0 and keep D0,H0 above. Let
 Q=4beta d(d-1),
 C=H0+(d+1)logd+D0+log_+(2H0)+1,
 L=[Q+sqrt(Q^2+4Q(C+1))]/2,

Then L^2/Q-L=C+1. The earlier FIXED_ANISOTROPY_NEIGHBORHOOD proof applies
with this smaller fixed cutoff/radius: C_N=betaI+A_N is positive and <=2betaI,
D_C>=beta eta^2 I, and logdet exit probability<=exp[-N L^2/Q]. For
N>=d+1 the full normalized trace error is bounded by
 2h_N exp[h_N-N L^2/Q], h_N<=H0N.
Because
 log(d/eta)=(d+1)logd+D0+L,
 log(2h_N)/N<=log_+(2H0)+1,
the choice L^2/Q-L=C+1 pays the entire white-noise/separable-ball cost
with positive slack. Explicitly
 N L^2/Q >=h_N+N log(d/eta)+log(2h_N).
Hence (S6) holds, proving exact full separability for all N>=d+1 and all
||A_N||<=a_star, uniformly over ||B_N||<=B0.

This positive radius is independent of N and is an actual open coupling
neighborhood. It is extremely small. It is not a claim that all fixed
anisotropic quantum mean-field models are separable or that any critical
temperature/geometric entanglement statement was externally certified.
