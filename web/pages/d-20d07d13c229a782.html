## 1. Fixed, fully acquired schedule

Start X_0 from one fixed rational exact-unit node, for example(0,0,1), as one block, N_0=1. At stage k>=1 there are exactly k nonempty disjoint blocks and total N_(k-1). Their cap occupancy is C(1+N_(k-1)r^2), preserved under increasing total count. Compute only exact integers:

    U_(k-1)=sum_(blocks A) ceil sqrt(|A|),
    S_k=k+1,
    L_k=ceil sqrt(U_(k-1)^2 k^(k-1)),
    t_k=min{integer t>=1: M t^2>=A k L_k^2},
    T_k=t_k-1, m_k=M t_k^2,
    B_k=(k+1)ceil log2(m_k+1)+4.                       (D1)

Acquire the actual rational polynomial g_k from all old nodes using the fixed degree13 C6 filter, set w_k=1-g_k/m_k, and invoke the gated weighted primitive with forbidden set X_(k-1), degree T_k and precision B_k. Its output Y_k is a distinct rational exact-unit m_k-point block, disjoint from all old nodes. Set X_k=X_(k-1) disjoint-union Y_k and N_k=N_(k-1)+m_k. Every final equal weight is positive1/N_k. All previous coordinates are literally retained. No prescribed integrand, real smoothness input, existence zero or uncomputed parameter chooses the nodes.

The scale proof uses integer square roots and repeated integer powers. It gives

    U_(k-1)^2<=4N_(k-1),
    1+A k^k <= N_k/N_(k-1) <=65A k^k.                (D2)

The lower bound follows from U^2>=N, L_k^2>=U^2 k^(k-1), m_k>=A k L_k^2. The upper bound is the fixed-S ledger with S=k+1. In particular N_k>A^k and k<=(log2 N_k)/90. ## 2. Uniform error for each fixed r>1

Fix any real r>1 for analysis. It is not an algorithmic input. From stage k_r=max(1,ceil(r)-1) onward, S_k=k+1>=r. Write H=sum_blocks sqrt(|A|). The actual discrete filtered pair-tail proof at this fixed r gives

    ||D_(X_(k-1))-g_k sigma||_(H^-r)
         <=F_r H L_k^(1-r),                         (D3)

where F_r is independent of k,S_k,L_k and density height. To see the endpoint independence explicitly, the six derivatives of p(u)^(-r), p=1+u+u^2, have finite coefficients depending on that r. The dyadic pair-row proof then uses only the fixed filter, C and sum_j2^((2-2r)j). Neither a changing compact endpoint nor its derivative majorant is required. The same argument is uniform in r on a fixed compact interval[r0,R], r0>1, with that interval fixed for analysis.

The acquired new block satisfies

    ||D_(Y_k)+g_k sigma||_(H^-r)<=E_r m_k^(1-r/2),    (D4)

where E_r is independent of S_k: T_k>=t_k/2 and m_k=M t_k^2, so its high-band term contributes at most C_r(2sqrt(M))^r m_k^(1-r/2). Precision contributes at most m_k2^(-B_k)/4<=m_k^(1-S_k)/4<=m_k^(1-r/2)/4. This explicitly charges a growing B instead of treating arbitrary precision as free.

For d=r/2-1 use m_k/L_k^2 in[Ak,4Ak]. Choosing the lower endpoint when d<0 and the upper when d>=0 gives

    H L_k^(1-r)
      <=(4A)^max(r/2-1,0) k^((r-S_k)/2)m_k^(1-r/2)

    ||D_(X_k)||_(H^-r)/N_k <=Q_r N_k^(-r/2),         (D6)

There are only finitely many earlier prefixes. They have finite error uniformly over all sphere points. Indeed the nonconstant point-functional norm obeys

    sum_(l>=1)(2l+1)(1+l(l+1))^(-r)
      <=3 sum_(l>=1)l^(1-2r)

The normalized discrepancy of any finite equal-weight set is at most the square root of this bound, by the triangle inequality. Thus enlarge Q_r by at most

    sqrt(3[1+1/(2r-2)]) max_(0<=k<k_r)N_k^(r/2).

This is a finite constant independent of later k. Consequently one fully specified acquired nested family (X_k) is a QMC family for every fixed H^r, r>1, at its sparse prescribed cardinalities. ## 3. Total bit acquisition, including variable endpoint powers

At stage k, k^(k-1) is computed exactly by integer exponentiation. Its bit length is at most log2(L_k^2), hence O(log N_k). Computing it by repeated squaring costs polynomially many bit operations in the actual output scale; no unit-cost compressed exponent operation is assumed. U and t are exact integer root choices, with N_k bits and sizes charged. A direct formula is t_k=ceil sqrt(ceil(A k L_k^2/M)).

From(D1)–(D2), B_k=O(k log N_k)=O(log^2 N_k), T_k=O(sqrt(N_k)), R_k=2L_k=O(sqrt(N_k)), and the primitive uses I=O(log N_k+log(B_k+1)) corrections. Its tolerance includes the travel cap h/[10000(1+67I/3)], so the increased precision does not spend unbounded reset travel. All newly acquired coordinates have O(B_k+log(T_k+N_(k-1)+1))=O(log^2 N_k) bits. The old coordinates remain exactly as acquired at earlier stages.

At a final prefix N_J, every stored old coordinate therefore has O(log^2 N_J) bits. The density common-denominator ledger in the independent gate has bit height O(L_k E_old+L_k+log m_k), with every different old denominator counted. E_old=O(N_J log^2 N_J). There are O(L_k^3) monomials. This can be very large, but its entire encoding is polynomial in N_J. Exact rational target sums, transient LP solution heights, roots/centroid interval guards and collision comparisons are likewise polynomially bounded by N_J through the primitive's proved encoded-input cost. All rejected collision candidates are included; the forbidden set is the entire prior X_(k-1).

There are at most(log2 N_J)/90 stages. Every intermediate count, degree, precision and denominator height is polynomially bounded by final output size. Summing the stage costs is therefore deterministic polynomial bit time in N_J. One may substitute a different rational exact-unit one-point initializer, preserving this exact stage/block indexing and charging its encoded coordinates; then the runtime is polynomial in N_J and that input encoding. This paragraph does not extend the k-block indexing to an arbitrary multi-point initializer. The total fresh integrand evaluations at nested prefixes is exactly N_J, because old values can be reused; this is an accounting fact, not a small-error guarantee for intermediate N or a practical evaluation-cost claim.
