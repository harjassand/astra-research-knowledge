1. The fixed channel and the question
-------------------------------------

Take V=V_(1,1), the 8-dimensional adjoint irrep of SU(3). Let P_27 be the
orthogonal projector onto the 27-dimensional summand in Sym^2(V), and define
the physical symmetric channel

  Gamma_27(X) = (4/27) P_27[(X tensor I)+(I tensor X)]P_27,
  Phi_27 = Tr_2 Gamma_27.

The exact SU(3) contractions in `cycle4/multiplicity_blocks.txt` verify that
Gamma_27 is CPTP and output-swap invariant and Phi_27 is bistochastic. In an
orthonormal Hermitian operator basis for the two adjoint copies
H_1=8_s direct-sum 8_a, the marginal transfer block is

  M(Phi_27)|H_1 = diag(14/25, 2/3).

We ask for the smallest coefficient C such that one EB comparator can obey
the linear Dirichlet order on the full normalized-trace operator space:

  I-Psi <= C (I-Phi_27).                              (1)

The comparator is a single full-domain EB channel on M_8. A Loewner order
uses its selfadjoint part: for a nonselfadjoint EB channel Psi, write
S_Psi=(Psi+Psi^dagger)/2 in the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product. We reduce
any such EB attempt to a bistochastic selfadjoint covariant EB channel
without losing the quadratic-form inequality. The conclusion is an exact
full-space optimum C=2 against unrestricted EB channels, rather than only
against one orbit ansatz.

2. Twirling reduces every EB attempt to convex orbit mixtures
-------------------------------------------------------------

Let U_g be the adjoint representation and T the Haar twirl of a channel:

  T(Psi)(X) = integral Ad_(U_g)^(-1) Psi Ad_(U_g)(X) dg.

Each conjugated channel and their average are EB; TP is preserved. Phi_27
and the full L2 operator space are SU(3)-invariant, so Haar averaging any
valid instance of (1) preserves the full-space quadratic-form order. Because
V is irreducible,
T(Psi)(I) commutes with every U_g and hence is scalar; its trace is 8, so
T(Psi) is unital. Thus it is bistochastic. The Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint of a
bistochastic EB channel is again a bistochastic EB channel: if
Psi(X)=sum_y Tr(F_y X) sigma_y, then its adjoint can be written as a
measure/prepare channel with effects (Tr F_y) sigma_y and preparations
F_y/Tr F_y (zero-trace terms omitted). Hence

  Psi_0 = [T(Psi)+T(Psi)^dagger]/2

is a bistochastic selfadjoint EB channel and retains any selfadjoint block
order of the form (1).

Use normalized Choi states. Every separable Choi state is a convex mixture
of pure product states; since T(Psi) is invariant, it is itself a mixture
of Haar twirls of product pure states. Under the standard input-conjugate,
output Choi convention, each such orbit is a seed channel

  E_(alpha,beta)(X) = 8 integral Tr(P_(alpha,g) X) P_(beta,g) dg,
  P_(alpha,g)=U_g |alpha><alpha| U_g^dagger,

with normalized pure seeds alpha,beta in V. Irreducibility makes both orbit
averages I/8, so each seed channel is bistochastic. Convex mixtures of
these orbits therefore include every covariant bistochastic EB comparator.

3. Pure-seed bound for the antisymmetric adjoint copy
-----------------------------------------------------

Let T_e be the Hermitian adjoint generators with
Tr(T_e T_f)=3 delta_ef and sum_e T_e^2=3 I. The normalized HS-orthonormal
anti-copy basis from the Gell-Mann structure constants is

  (B_(a,e))_(ac) = i sqrt(8/3) f_ace.

Up to a harmless global sign, B_(a,e)=sqrt(8/3) T_e. Define the real seed
moment vector

  q_(a,e)(alpha)=tau(B_(a,e) |alpha><alpha|)
               = (1/sqrt(24)) <alpha|T_e|alpha>,
  tau=Tr/8.

The SU(3) adjoint weights are the six roots and zero. With the generator
metric fixed above, each root has length one. For any unit real vector n,
the Hermitian generator T(n)=sum_e n_e T_e is conjugate to a Cartan
generator; its eigenvalues are projections of these weights, so
lambda_max(T(n))<=1. Therefore

  ||(<T_e>_alpha)_e||_2
    = sup_(||n||_2=1) <alpha|T(n)|alpha> <= 1,
  sum_e q_(a,e)(alpha)^2 <= 1/24.                     (2)

Equality is attained by a highest-root weight vector. The exact seed
alpha=(|1>+i|2>)/sqrt(2) used in the cycle-4 replay is such a vector.

For a covariant orbit seed pair, Schur orthogonality gives the transfer
matrix on the two adjoint copies as

  M_(v,u)=8 sum_e q_(v,e)(beta) q_(u,e)(alpha).

In particular, Cauchy-Schwarz and (2) imply

  M_(a,a) <= 8 ||q_a(beta)|| ||q_a(alpha)|| <= 1/3.   (3)

The same bound holds for any convex mixture of orbit channels. It applies to
the anti-copy diagonal coefficient of every covariant bistochastic EB
channel, by the twirl reduction above.

4. The unrestricted lower bound and its sharp adjoint block
----------------------------------------------

On the anti-copy, Phi_27 has eigenvalue 2/3. Applying (1) to a unit vector
in 8_a and using (3) gives

  1-M_(a,a) <= C(1-2/3),
  2/3 <= C/3,
  C >= 2.                                             (4)

This lower bound applies to every full-domain EB comparator, not just to a
fixed coherent orbit.

For the equality seed alpha=beta=(|1>+i|2>)/sqrt(2), the exact replay gives

  M(E_(alpha,alpha))|H1 = diag(1/5,1/3).

Then on 8_s,

  1-1/5=4/5 <= 2(1-14/25)=22/25,

and on 8_a equality holds:

  1-1/3=2/3 = 2(1-2/3).

Thus C=2 is achievable on H_1 and is a necessary coefficient for the
full-space inequality (1). It follows on H_1 that

  P_H1 (I-Psi)^2 P_H1 <= 2 P_H1 (I-Phi_27)P_H1,

for this selfadjoint orbit channel, since 0<=I-Psi<=I on H_1 and
(I-Psi)^2<=I-Psi there. This squared comparison is an upper bound, not
claimed optimal.

5. Exact extension to every SU(3) operator sector
-------------------------------------------------

As an SU(3)-module under conjugation, the full operator space is

  End(V_8) = 1 + 8_s + 8_a + 10 + 10bar + 27.

The exact P_27 marginal has scalar multipliers

  sector       multiplicity   dim total   lambda(Phi_27)
  1                 1              1       1
  8_s               1              8       14/25
  8_a               1              8       2/3
  10                1             10       28/75
  10bar             1             10       28/75
  27                1             27       218/675.

The existing exact marginal superoperator has precisely this spectrum; the
8 copies are separated by the already checked 2-by-2 multiplicity block.
For the coherent root seed alpha=(|1>+i|2>)/sqrt(2), put
P=|alpha><alpha| and let Pi_lambda be the corresponding orthogonal
isotypic projection in normalized-trace operator L2. Schur orthogonality for
the orbit channel Psi(X)=8 integral Tr(P_g X)P_g dg gives, on a
multiplicity-free irreducible sector of dimension r_lambda, the following
calculation. For a tau-orthonormal basis {B_i} of that sector let
q_i(g)=tau(B_i,P_g). Then

  tau(B_i,Psi(B_j))=64 integral overline(q_i(g))q_j(g) dg
       =(64/r_lambda) delta_ij sum_k |q_k(e)|^2.

Since sum_k |q_k(e)|^2=||Pi_lambda(P)||_(2,tau)^2, the multiplier is

  mu_lambda = 8^2/r_lambda * ||Pi_lambda(P)||_(2,tau)^2.

Exact projector contractions give

  sector       ||Pi_lambda(P)||_(2,tau)^2   mu(Psi)
  1                         1/64               1
  8_s                       1/40               1/5
  8_a                       1/24               1/3
  10+10bar                  1/48 total         1/15 each
  27                        7/320              7/135.

The two conjugate 10 sectors have equal multipliers because Psi is
Hermiticity-preserving and Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint. The two adjoint
copies are not multiplicity-free; their exact transfer matrix is
diag(1/5,1/3), with zero off-diagonal entries.

Comparing deficits gives

  sector       1-mu(Psi)      2(1-lambda(Phi_27))
  8_s             4/5                 22/25
  8_a             2/3                  2/3
  10              14/15                94/75
  10bar           14/15                94/75
  27              128/135             914/675.

Both maps fix the scalar identity. Hence on the entire L2(Tr/8),

  I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi_27),
  (I-Psi)^2 <= I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi_27).

The first inequality is sharp because of 8_a, and its lower bound remains
valid against every full-domain EB map by §2-§3. Therefore the optimal
full-space linear coefficient against unrestricted EB is exactly 2 for
this one compatible channel. The exact replay of the new sector norms and
ratios is `su3_full_space_check.py`.
