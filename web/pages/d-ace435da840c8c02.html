INTERFACE
F is an n-by-n complex matrix (rational complex for algorithms). For an even retained ambient set R, define
 Z_R(F)=sum_{I subset R, |I|=|R|/2} |det F[I,R\I]|^2,
and Z_empty=1. This is the full-paired-basis partition of the restricted pairs (e_i,row_i F), with ambient rows restricted to R. It equals the squared-Gram sum for these full paired bases. For n even and hole set U, put f(U)=Z_{[n]\U}; f vanishes on odd U.

SIGNED-HOLE STABILITY: FULL DERIVATION
Let H be the 2n-by-2n Hermitian chiral matrix
 H=[[0,2F],[2F*,0]],
with coordinate pairs (u_i,v_i). P(u,v)=det(diag(u,v)-H) is real stable: the imaginary part of the matrix is strictly positive definite when every coordinate has positive imaginary part, so the determinant is nonzero. Its coefficients are real because H is Hermitian.

Apply D=2^(-n) product_i (partial_{u_i}+partial_{v_i}), and then set u_i=v_i=x_i. A nonnegative directional derivative preserves stability: introduce a new upper-half-plane variable t by shifting both coordinates by t, differentiate t, and specialize t=0 by Hurwitz. Identification also preserves stability. The result Q_F(x) is nonzero (leading coefficient one), real stable, and multiaffine in the n remaining x_i.

Expand the derivative as the average of principal characteristic polynomials retaining exactly one of u_i,v_i at each site. A selected principal chiral minor with up indices I and down indices J has determinant zero unless |I|=|J|. If |I|=|J|=k and I,J are disjoint, its determinant is (-1)^k 2^(2k)|det F[I,J]|^2. It is retained with probability 2^(-2k). Hence
 Q_F(x)=sum_{U: n-|U| even} (-1)^((n-|U|)/2) Z_{[n]\U}(F) product_{i in U} x_i.  (A)
The factor 2 in H is essential: using sqrt2 would leave unwanted powers of two. This is the full multivariate version of the already proved scalar principal-characteristic average, not a claim that the pair-label polynomial is real stable.

FOUR-HOLE REPAIR
For four distinct indices a,b,c,d, set all other hole variables to zero and multiply Q_F by its constant global sign. The surviving stable polynomial is
 q=Z0 -sum_{ij subset {a,b,c,d}} Zij x_i x_j +Z4 x_a x_b x_c x_d,
where Z0=f(empty), Zij=f({i,j}), Z4=f({a,b,c,d}), all nonnegative. If a specialization is identically zero, use continuity; the desired inequality is immediate whenever Z0 Z4=0.

Real stability of a multiaffine real polynomial implies Rayleigh difference
 (partial_a q)(partial_b q)-q partial_ab q >=0
at all real values of the remaining variables. This follows directly from the bivariate real-affine stability/Mobius criterion bc-ad>=0, so no coefficient-sign assumption is used.

At x_a=x_b=0 its Rayleigh difference is
 Z0 Zab +Zac Zbc x_c^2 +Zad Zbd x_d^2
 +(Zac Zbd+Zad Zbc-Zab Zcd-Z0 Z4)x_c x_d
 +Zcd Z4 x_c^2 x_d^2.
Write A=Z0 Z4, P=Zab Zcd, Q=Zac Zbd, R=Zad Zbc. Set v=x_c x_d>=0 and minimize the two quadratic terms at fixed v. Their infimum is 2 sqrt(QR) v, with zero-coefficient cases obtained by limits. Nonnegativity of the resulting quadratic for all v>=0 gives
 A+P-Q-R <=2 sqrt(QR)+2 sqrt(AP).
Equivalently
 (sqrt A-sqrt P)^2 <=(sqrt Q+sqrt R)^2,
so
 sqrt(Z0 Z4) <=sqrt(Zab Zcd)+sqrt(Zac Zbd)+sqrt(Zad Zbc).  (B)
By Cauchy-Schwarz,
 Z0 Z4 <=3[Zab Zcd+Zac Zbd+Zad Zbc].                    (C)
All matrices F and all positive pair fields are covered: absorb sqrt(activity_i) in row_i F as a proof operation. Diagonal ambient scaling preserves the full-basis normalized law and maps back to the same e_i,f_i form after a common pair-vector rescaling.

ADDING COORDINATE-PAIR AUXILIARIES
Add a new paired line (e_i,e_j) of nonnegative activity w, distinct from all old lines. Expanding every full paired determinant along these two new columns gives exactly
 f_new(U)=f_old(U)+w f_old(U union {i,j}) if U disjoint {i,j},
with the added term zero otherwise. Thus its signed polynomial is
 Q_new=Q_old-w partial_i partial_j Q_old.               (D)
This operator is stability-preserving on multiaffine polynomials: its algebraic symbol is
 [(z_i+y_i)(z_j+y_j)-w] product_{l not i,j}(z_l+y_l),
which is stable because each sum is in the upper half-plane and two such numbers cannot multiply to the nonnegative real w. The Borcea-Branden algebraic-symbol theorem, Theorem1.1/1.2 of https://arxiv.org/html/0809.0401v3, supplies the implication. Iterating (D) closes all signed-hole stability consequences under the auxiliary coordinate-pair additions needed for defect probes. This does not assert closure under arbitrary new vector pairs.

GENERAL SQUARE-ROOT EXCHANGE: COMPLETE SELF-CONTAINED DERIVATION
Let h(x)=i^(-n)Q_F(ix)=sum_U f(U)x_U. It is right-half-plane (Hurwitz) stable, multiaffine, has nonnegative coefficients and definite even parity. The same conclusion holds after the coordinate-pair additions above.

For any even sets S,T and any a in D=S symmetric-difference T, the claim is
 sqrt(f(S)f(T)) <=sum_{j in D, j!=a}
                   sqrt(f(S xor {a,j})f(T xor {a,j})).    (E)
If f(S)f(T)=0 the claim is trivial. Otherwise twist ALL coordinates in T by reciprocal transformation:
 h_T(x)=product_{i in T}x_i h((1/x_i)_{i in T},(x_i)_{i not in T}).
Since 1/z remains in the right half-plane when z does, this preserves Hurwitz stability. Its coefficient on x_U is f(T xor U), and its parity is even because |T| is even. Specialize all coordinates outside D to0 using Hurwitz continuity. The result is nonzero because its constant coefficient is f(T)>0. Common holes and the common occupied complement are already incorporated by this full twist; no derivative or common-cut sewing is needed.

Set x_a=1 and x_j=w_j z for all other j in D, with arbitrary positive w_j. The resulting univariate p(z)=sum_l a_l z^l is Hurwitz stable, real, with nonnegative coefficients. Write m=|D| and d=m-1, which is odd. Its constant and leading coefficients are positive:
 a0=f(T), ad=f(S) product_{j!=a}w_j.
By parity, the two neighboring coefficients are
 a1=sum_{j!=a}f(T xor {a,j})w_j,
 a_(d-1)=(product_{j!=a}w_j)sum_{j!=a}f(S xor {a,j})/w_j.
For m=2 the two factors in the resulting inequality are simply the two endpoints, giving equality; the indexing still works with d=1.

Elementary endpoint-coefficient lemma: if an odd-degree real polynomial with positive constant and leading coefficients has no zeros in the open right half-plane, then a1 a_(d-1)>=a0 ad. Factor it into negative-real-root factors (z+r), r>0, and conjugate-root factors z^2+2s z+t, s>=0,t>0. Odd degree supplies at least one real factor. Then
 a1/a0=sum_real 1/r+sum_conjugate 2s/t,
 a_(d-1)/ad=sum_real r+sum_conjugate 2s.
All contributions are nonnegative; the contribution of any one real factor makes their product at least1. This also permits purely imaginary conjugate pairs. No Asner or Pick representation theorem is needed.

Apply the lemma, divide by the product of w's, and obtain for every positive field vector
 f(S)f(T) <=[sum_j f(T xor {a,j})w_j]
             [sum_j f(S xor {a,j})/w_j].                 (F)
The infimum of the right side is [sum_j sqrt(f(T xor {a,j})f(S xor {a,j}))]^2, by Cauchy-Schwarz, attained at w_j proportional to sqrt(f(S xor {a,j})/f(T xor {a,j})) when both coefficients are positive. Zero coefficients are handled by the same positive-field infimum/limits. Taking square roots proves (E).

 sqrt(g(S)/r_a)<=sqrt(g(S-a+z)/r_z)+192/sqrtB.  (G)

MICROSCOPIC TRANSFER: EXPONENTIAL POSITIVE-OVERLAY OBSTRUCTION
Successful relocation does not establish a sampler. Natural microscopic states for f(U) are disjoint I,J with I union J=[n]\U and |I|=|J|, point weight |det F[I,J]|^2. That point weight is computable in polynomial bit time for rational-complex F, but the aggregate f(U) is unknown. A Markov theorem requiring an f(U) coefficient oracle would supply exactly the counting information still being sought.

OA113's discrepancy-cycle swaps preserve product edge weights exactly, and its encoding changes at most four comparable edge activities to get a polynomial A0^4 ratio. This positive structure cannot hold for the determinant-hole signature. Define H(x)=f(x)f(complement x). An even-windable decomposition would assign nonnegative B(x,complement x,M) to pairings M of all differing indices, sum to H(x), and be invariant under flipping any pair of M in both inputs. (Primary definition: Huang-Lu-Zhang, Definition1, https://arxiv.org/html/1510.04099, following McQuillan https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.2880.)

For the factor-one F4 counter, H(0000)=4 while the three complementary two-hole products are0,1,1. Each B at0000 is bounded by the corresponding two-hole product after flipping a pair, so nonnegativity and invariance would force4<=2. No even-windable decomposition exists. In particular, no positive perfect-matching circuit with internal vertices realizes exactly all these terminal-hole coefficients, because its alternating-path overlay would supply such an inequality.

The obstruction tensorizes exponentially. Take q independent block-diagonal copies of F4. Then H(0)=4^q. Any pairing containing a cross-block pair has B(0,M)=0: flipping that pair makes odd holes in each touched block, so H is zero. Each block has three internal pairings; the pairing12|34 likewise forces zero. The other two pairings per block survive, so only2^q global pairings can have positive B. Each such pairing orbit contains an input that is two/two in every block and has H=1; pair-flip invariance bounds its B by1. Consequently sum_M B(0,M)<=2^q. If a proposed relaxed positive invariant encoding only achieves H(x)<=C sum_M B(x,M) while respecting output mass sum_M B(x,M)<=H(x), then it needs C>=2^q. A single globally paid polynomial congestion loss cannot rescue this pairing/overlay mechanism. Gaussian_photonic independently found the same tensor proof and is recording the sewing obstruction separately. This does not disprove another sampler, a noninvariant norm flow, or a construction retaining mismatch ancillas.

Even bounded line reassignment and comparable activities alone do not control microscopic determinant ratios. Let
 F=[[0,delta,1,0],[delta,0,0,1],[1,0,0,1],[0,1,1,0]],
with every line activity1. Full pair-label bases A=12,B=34 have determinant amplitudes1,1. The union-preserving reassignment A'=13,B'=24 has amplitudes delta,delta, hence product squared-weight ratio delta^4. Choosing delta=2^(-L) makes the ratio exponentially small in input bit length. Alternative swaps may be good; this is not a mixing lower bound, but it invalidates an unconditional analogue of the graph bounded-edge-ratio estimate. Coordinate-only switches covering the identical four ambient rows DO preserve the remaining determinant; original f_i lines have no such single coordinate endpoint.

The bottom unit-activity tree-bag refresh also uses actual graph pairings; setting pair activities to one does not erase intrinsic determinants. A different legal approximate refresh is elementary. On an r-dimensional system with M original-plus-coordinate lines, put activity1 on every coordinate pair and activity epsilon on original vector pairs. If all vector coordinates have absolute value at most R>=1, every squared full determinant is at most r^r R^(2r) by Hadamard. There are at most2^M full-basis line sets, and each set containing an original line has activity product at most epsilon. Their total weight is therefore at most epsilon 2^M r^r R^(2r). The coordinate-only partition is (r-1)!!>=1, and its uniform matching law can be sampled exactly by a uniform random permutation followed by adjacent pairing. Choosing epsilon<=eta/[2^M r^r R^(2r)] makes this explicit refresh law within total variation eta of the true positive base law. Its exponent has polynomial bit length. A fixed execution budget can charge eta divided by the number of refreshes. This avoids assuming an unknown within-fiber conditional sampler. It does not prove an energy inequality along the ensuing annealing path.

LEGAL VECTOR-PAIR SUBDIVISION AND FIXED-k HOMOGENIZATION
Subdivision itself is legal. Replace one original pair(v,w) of activity lambda in an r-dimensional full-basis problem by three pairs(v,e_s),(e_s,e_t),(e_t,w), with two fresh private ambient coordinates s,t. A nonzero full basis must cover both private rows. The only surviving choices are middle-only (old pair absent) or both-outers (old pair present); a middle plus outer repeats a private column. Expanding private rows gives the same old determinant in the appropriate case. The new partition is the old partition times the product of all middle activities, with effective old activity lambda=lambda_left lambda_right/lambda_middle. Iterating gives an odd path profile. For original v=e_i,w=f_i, the last outer line is again of canonical e_t,f_t form with F row_i transferred to t, and the other lines are coordinate pairs. Thus signed-hole stability also survives that construction. However, f_i remains a distributed vector rather than one ambient vertex endpoint, so the tree-bag discrepancy/energy argument is still missing; this gadget does not provide a positive graph representation.

For a fixed original particle number k with2k<=n, c_k=sum_(|I|=k)detGram({e_i,f_i:i in I}) equals sum over disjoint I,J of sizek of |det F[I,J]|^2 by Cauchy-Binet. Add t=n-2k fresh dummy ambient coordinates, embed old pairs with zeros there, and add coordinate lines(e_j,e_dummy) of activity1 for every old coordinate j and every dummy. Every nonzero full basis must select exactly t such lines, covering every dummy and distinct old coordinates. It therefore selects exactly k old pairs. For each contributing old minor I,J, its t remaining old coordinates can be matched to the dummies in exactly t! ways, and private-row expansion preserves the squared minor. The full-paired partition in dimension n+t is exactly t! c_k. This is an exact polynomial-size homogenization, not an acquired full-basis sampler.

Counting/zero separation: support of each paired partition can be acquired by randomized linear-matroid-parity/Pfaffian identity testing, or by a deterministic represented linear-parity algorithm. Neither is an approximate count oracle. Matoya-Oki 1912.00620v2 covers Pfaffian parity constant-determinant subclasses and support/optimization infrastructure, not arbitrary squared-Gram FPRAS:
https://arxiv.org/html/1912.00620v2
A randomized support oracle's possible false zero must be budgeted across visited calls or compiler-weighted prefixes. Exact zero with certainty is also available through deterministic algorithms: Hamada-Iwata, posted2 October2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03030v1, Theorem1.1, gives a deterministic O(Nr^2 log r) arithmetic bound and explicitly points to the polynomial finite-field reduction for rational input (Iwata-Kobayashi, Theorem11.3). Earlier deterministic algorithms suffice; the newer speed improvement is not load-bearing here.

For rational-complex input, clear denominators to Gaussian integers. A nonzero witness minor delta has positive integer norm bounded by2^B with B polynomial in matrix size and entry bit length, by Hadamard. Among B+1 distinct odd primes some does not divide that norm. Reduce the instance over F_p if -1 is square, or F_(p^2) otherwise; small primes and the square root/nonresidue can be found by deterministic enumeration in polynomial bit time. The maximum parity cardinality over these fields equals that over Q(i): field reduction cannot turn an identically zero Gaussian-integer minor into a nonzero one, and one prime preserves a fixed optimum witness. This compiles deterministic finite-field support to deterministic polynomial-bit support. It neither evaluates determinant-squared sums nor implements the unproved sampler.
