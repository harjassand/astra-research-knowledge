# Phase 2: exact matroid gates for a four-label transfer

Status: internally reconstructed finite obstruction and finite replay of an already
released stronger socle theorem. No justified scientific promotion.

## Interface

For a matroid M with basis set B, let S=k[y_B:B in B], let I be the kernel of
y_B -> product_{e in B} x_e, let K be generated by all degree-two binomials in I,
and let J be generated by single-element symmetric-exchange binomials.
The remaining quadratic White interface is I=K. It is NOT I=J: Larson's
July 2026 primary paper supplies a counterexample to the latter.

The release CM tensor proof's combinatorial reduction, hodge-02-tensors.tex
lines388–401, corrects independent sign columns by two-row swaps. Its
composition, lines418–455, contracts auxiliary tensor factors and divides by
nonzero scalar values q_i in Qbar*. Neither step requires treating the claimed
class-wide Hodge theorem as validated for the present examination.

Two candidate transfer gates were attacked:
(A) fixed-target greedy correction remains available when allowed rows are
matroid bases;
(B) legal paths after adding auxiliary rows can be cancelled back in the
polynomial ideal.

Both gates fail.

## A. An exact Fano greedy counterexample

Use the Fano matroid of rank3 on labels0,…,6, where label i is the nonzero
binary vector with integer code i+1. A triple is a basis exactly when its
three binary vectors are independent. It has28 bases.

Let A={013,024,156}, B={016,025,134}; notation013 means {0,1,3}.
Each is a multiset of three bases. Their ground-set incidence counts agree:
(2,2,1,1,1,1,1).

For multisets U,V of p bases, define
D(U,V)=min_permutations sum_i |U_i \ V_pi(i)|.
It allows free relabelling of rows, making the fixed-target greedy claim
stronger than a particular chosen row ordering. D(A,B)=3.

A legal quadratic move replaces ANY pair of bases by ANY other pair with the
same multiset ground-set union. Direct exact enumeration of all such moves
from A gives precisely the following seven distinct neighbors:

| Neighbor | D(neighbor,B) |
|---|---:|
| {013,014,256} |3|
| {013,015,246} |4|
| {013,026,145} |3|
| {013,045,126} |3|
| {013,046,125} |3|
| {014,023,156} |3|
| {015,024,136} |3|

Thus no legal arbitrary quadratic move strictly decreases D. This attacks
the remaining weak White interface, rather than only single-element moves.

There is nevertheless a path
{013,024,156} -> {013,045,126} -> {016,045,123} -> {016,025,134},
with deficit sequence3,3,2,0. Every arrow is a single symmetric exchange.
The example refutes a greedy proof gate; it does NOT refute I=K or I=J for
Fano. The generator search initially encountered a tuple-normalization error
on the dual model; sorting the basis list repaired it. Failure retained in
failures.json.

## B. An exact auxiliary-cancellation obstruction

Use precisely Larson's9x18 binary matrix in Example1.4, copied as ROWS in
buffer_attack.py and checked against the rendered original PDF page3.
Its complete set of14,848 bases is acquired locally by exact binary rank
enumeration, not assumed.

Let
B0={1,2,3,7,8,9,13,14,15},
D0={1,2,3,5,7,9,11,13,15},
and let superscript c denote complement in {1,…,18}.
All four sets are bases. Define
f=y_B0 y_B0c - y_D0 y_D0c.
It belongs to I, and is already an arbitrary quadratic replacement in K.

The complementary pair fiber has2,784 unordered states and exactly two
single-exchange components:864 states splitting3 distinguished pairs and
1,920 states splitting7 or9. The split statistic changes by at most2 under
a single exchange; split5 never occurs in this fiber. Consequently f is
outside J. The degree-two J relations are graph-edge differences, whose
linear span has zero coefficient sum in each component, over any field.

The buffer search supplies, for example, a five-exchange path from
{B0,B0,B0c} to {B0,D0,D0c}. Telescoping its five cubic monomial differences
gives y_B0 f in J, although f is outside J.

A stronger exact finite certificate proves y_N f in J for EVERY basis N:
1. Verify73 binary-code automorphisms, preserving the distinguished pair
partition.
2. Partition all14,848 bases into ten covered orbits.
3. For each orbit representative N, supply an explicit cubic single-exchange
path from {N,B0,B0c} to {N,D0,D0c}.
4. Apply the corresponding automorphisms. The transformed complementary
endpoints remain in their split3 and split7/9 components. Paths within these
ordinary pair components replace them by the fixed B0,D0 endpoints, while
leaving N as the third factor.

Therefore the nonzero class of f in S/J is killed by the irrelevant maximal
ideal m, and J:f=m. The finite checker uses dense binary elimination and
whole-codeword automorphism checks, independently of the generator's pivot
dictionary/RREF implementation. Its PASS validates this finite matrix,
orbit, component, and path certificate. It is not formal kernel replay or
external review.

An initial verification assert used2,808 instead of2,784, an arithmetic
reporting error. The complete certificate sets agreed; correction864+1920
restored the check. This failure is preserved.

This is exactly the obstruction to gate(B): a basis variable is a nonunit
and a zero divisor modulo J. CM tensor contraction produces a nonzero scalar
unit. A transfer that identifies these two interfaces is invalid.

## Prior art and stopping decision

Lasoń–Michałek, arXiv1302.5236, Theorem3 and its proof (PDFpp3–6), already
establish J:m^infinity=I. Their Claim4 records a supplied-buffer exponent;
for the displayed complementary degree-two relation, deg_N(f)=9 and
deg(f)=2, giving y_N^7 f in J. The present finite buffer test sharpens that
particular bound, not the general theorem.

Historical checking then found Taeseok Oh's July31,2026 primary note,
Zenodo21720652. I opened/downloaded its PDF and read Theorem1.1p2,
Proposition2.1p3, Lemma5.1p10, Lemma6.1pp10–11,
Theorem7.1pp12–13 and Corollary7.3p13. It already proves the stronger
all-parameter cyclic-family annihilation statement J_m:f_m=m_m, with the
Larson example as m=3. Its proof uses a gap cut and local exchange triangles,
not an unqualified auxiliary cancellation. This prior release is decisive
against promoting the finite socle result as new. Its all-parameter proof
was read selectively, not externally validated or formally replayed here.

The current weaker I=K interface survives both negative gates. No new
candidate proof mechanism was obtained. A next serious attempt would need
an unsaturated, basis-preserving global connectivity argument using arbitrary
two-base replacements; the CM full-hypercube switching argument and known
saturation theorem do not supply it. Further effort should not be allocated
to packaging either finite result as a foundational advance.
