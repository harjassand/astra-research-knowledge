INTERFACE AND CLAIM

Let g be an m-dimensional real subspace of traceless Hermitian d-by-d matrices,
closed under the bracket -i[T,S]. Supply an ordinary Tr-orthonormal basis T_a of g.
The trace metric is ad-invariant. Remove the physically irrelevant scalar
generator if the original group contains it. Reducible unitary representations
and a nontrivial traceless center are allowed. Fix alpha>0,gamma>0,M,B<infinity.
F_a=sum_i T_a^(i), C_N=alphaI+A N^-gamma with A=A^T real, ||A||<=M, and
b in R^m, ||b||<=B. For

               K_N=Q_(C_N)/N+F_b,

the direct stopped construction gives a positive mixture of identical local
d-dimensional density products sigma_N such that

 T(e^K_N/Tr e^K_N,sigma_N) <=C exp[-c N(logN)^2]          (L1)

for all sufficiently large N, uniformly over A,b. The mixing local densities
lie on the acquired filtering manifold rho=e^H/Tr e^H, H in g. Constants
depend on this fixed local representation and public parameters. No arbitrary
compact-group model, nonquadratic interaction, growing local dimension,
unknown-parameter decoder or entanglement-preserving reference is included.

Unlike FULL_RANK_STOPPING.txt, this theorem cannot use full-matrix Parseval.
The principal form is positive on an intrinsic filtering manifold instead.
