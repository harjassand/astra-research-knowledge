## Research result: a complete-reference entanglement bound

I obtained a candidate theorem that goes beyond comparing a quantum channel with an entanglement-breaking channel in a quadratic norm. It connects broadcastability, entropy production, and multipartite separability, including arbitrary quantum reference systems.

The key result is:

Theorem — Entropy-production bound on entanglement.

Let \\(T_i:M\_{d_i}\to M\_{d_i}\\) be bistochastic quantum channels, each admitting a physical two-output broadcaster with both marginals equal to \\(T_i\\). Define

\\[ H_i=\frac{T_i+T_i^\dagger}{2}, \qquad L=\sum\_{i=1}^m(I-H_i), \\]

where each operator acts on its corresponding subsystem \\(B_i\\), leaving an arbitrary quantum reference \\(R\\) untouched.

For every full-rank joint state \\(\rho\_{RB_1\cdots B_m}\\), define the entropy-production functional

\\[ J_L(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}\\!\left[L(\rho)\log\rho\right]. \\]

Then

\\[ \boxed{ J_L(\rho)\ge \frac14\\, \operatorname{dist}\_{1/2} \\!\left(\rho,\operatorname{SEP}(R:B_1:\cdots:B_m)\right)^2 } \\]

where \\(\operatorname{dist}\_{1/2}\\) denotes minimum half-trace distance to fully separable states.

If every \\(T_i\\) is Hilbert–Schmidt self-adjoint, the constant improves from \\(1/4\\) to \\(1/2\\).

The constants are independent of all subsystem dimensions, the reference dimension, and the number of subsystems.

### 1. The connecting mathematical mechanism

The positive theorem uses three steps.

First, reconstruct a physical classical comparator. For a reversible bistochastic channel \\(T\\) with a genuine two-output broadcaster, the broadcast-tree covariance argument constructs one entanglement-breaking channel \\(\Psi\\) satisfying

\\[ 0\preceq\Psi\preceq I,\qquad I-\Psi\preceq4(I-T). \\]

For nonreversible \\(T\\), applying the construction to \\(T^\dagger T\\) gives

\\[ I-\Psi\preceq8\left(I-\frac{T+T^\dagger}{2}\right). \\]

I reconstructed this argument in the tracial setting rather than assuming Astra's broader nontracial result.

Second, transfer the comparison to quantum-reference trace distance. Tensor the comparators and leave \\(R\\) untouched:

\\[ \Phi=\operatorname{id}\_R\otimes\Psi_1\otimes\cdots\otimes\Psi_m. \\]

The output \\(\Phi(\rho)\\) is fully separable. Positivity and commutation of the local comparator superoperators give

\\[ 0\preceq I-\Phi\preceq CL, \\]

where \\(C=8\\), or \\(4\\) in the reversible case.

Operator Jensen and the fidelity–trace-distance inequalities then yield

\\[ \delta(\rho)^2 \le2C\\,\operatorname{Tr}\\!\left[\sqrt\rho\\,L(\sqrt\rho)\right]. \\]

This step controls entangled reference states without asserting a diamond-norm bound on the channel.

Third, connect that quantity to entropy production. Diagonalizing \\(\rho\\) and using the symmetric stochastic transition matrix induced by each \\(H_i\\) reduces the comparison to

\\[ (a-b)(\log a-\log b)\ge4(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2. \\]

Consequently,

\\[ J_L(\rho)\ge4\operatorname{Tr}\\!\left[\sqrt\rho\\,L(\sqrt\rho)\right]. \\]

Combining the two inequalities proves the \\(1/4\\) and \\(1/2\\) constants stated above.

The proof uses only finite-dimensional matrix arguments and standard operator Jensen and fidelity inequalities. It does not depend on accepting Astra's unreviewed sharp nontracial \\(J\ge\pi E\\) result.

### 2. A second exact result: random-time evolution cannot repair the diamond-norm obstruction

I also extended the fixed-power obstruction recorded in Astra to arbitrary random-time mixtures.

Let \\(\pi\\) be any fixed probability distribution on nonnegative integers and write

\\[ F\_\pi(T)=\sum\_{k=0}^{\infty}\pi_kT^k. \\]

Then

\\[ \boxed{ \sup\_{\substack{d,\ T\text{ bistochastic,}\\\ T=T^\dagger,\ \text{two-self-compatible}}} \inf\_{\Lambda\in\mathrm{EB}} \\|F\_\pi(T)-\Lambda\\|\_\diamond=2. } \\]

This includes both finite Cesàro averages and Poissonized continuous-time evolution.

### 3. An exact entanglement-breaking cutoff

For the \\(q\\)-dimensional universal-cloner marginal

\\[ \mathcal D\_\lambda(X) =\lambda X+(1-\lambda)\operatorname{Tr}(X)\frac{I_q}{q}, \qquad \lambda=\frac{q+2}{2(q+1)}, \\]

consider the local quantum Markov semigroup

\\[ \mathcal P\_{q,t}^{(m)} =\left(e^{-t(I-\mathcal D\_\lambda)}\right)^{\otimes m}. \\]

Its critical time is

\\[ t_c(q)=\frac{2(q+1)}q\log(q+1). \\]

The research note proves the exact dichotomy

\\[ \boxed{ \lim\_{m\to\infty} \inf\_{\Lambda\in\mathrm{EB}} \\|\mathcal P\_{q,t}^{(m)}-\Lambda\\|\_\diamond = \begin{cases} 2,&t\<t_c(q),\\\ 0,&t\ge t_c(q). \end{cases} } \\]

Below the threshold, tensor amplification makes the channel maximally distinguishable from every entanglement-breaking channel. At and above the threshold, each local depolarizing factor is itself entanglement breaking, so the entire product is exactly entanglement breaking.

This gives a precise boundary between the positive statewise entropy theorem and the failure of uniform channel-level approximation.
