# Independent prior-art audit and lambda-score fiber

Worker b_prior, retrieved 2026-10-06. Status: proved finite-dimensional algebraic extension; publication novelty unverified; no general low-temperature efficient learner established.

## Exact question and interface

Given finite-dimensional faithful density matrices sigma,rho and known Hermitian directions A_a, define partial_a X=-i[A_a,X] and S_{lambda,a}(rho) by partial_a rho=(1-lambda)rho S+lambda S rho, 0<=lambda<=1. Which full-rank states have exactly the same scores as sigma? Distinguish unrestricted state space from a supplied restricted Gibbs family; an ambiguity state need not belong to that family. Known beta and supplied Hamiltonian support do not provide access to sigma's matrix, eigenbasis, modular operator or dressed frame. Copy access allows known-observable expectations only.

## Independent exact fiber theorem

Let Delta_sigma(X)=sigma X sigma^-1 and define
K_{lambda,a}=(lambda Delta_sigma^-1/2+(1-lambda)Delta_sigma^1/2)^-1(A_a).
Let C_lambda be the commutant of the union {K_{lambda,a},K_{lambda,a}^dagger}_a. This is a finite-dimensional *-algebra. Then the full-rank score fiber is exactly

{rho=sigma^1/2 R sigma^1/2 : R>0, R in C_lambda, Tr(sigma R)=1}.

Consequently sigma is identifiable among all faithful states iff C_lambda=C I. For lambda=1/2, K is Hermitian, so Dong–Leng's sufficient condition is also necessary on the unrestricted state space. For other lambda, their unstarred commutant condition remains sufficient but need not be necessary.

Proof reconstructed independently. Set L_a=S_{lambda,a}(sigma), U_a=A_a+i(1-lambda)L_a, V_a=A_a-i lambda L_a. The defining score equation is equivalent to V_a sigma=sigma U_a, since multiplying the derivative equation by i gives A sigma-sigma A=i[(1-lambda)sigma L+lambda L sigma]. Equality of rho's score with L_a is equivalent to V_a rho=rho U_a. Both score maps are invertible because all eigenvalues (1-lambda)p_i+lambda p_j of the multiplication superoperator are positive. Using V_a=sigma U_a sigma^-1 and writing rho=sigma^1/2 R sigma^1/2, the second intertwining equation is exactly [R,sigma^1/2 U_a sigma^-1/2]=0. Direct substitution of L_a=i(Delta_sigma-I)/((1-lambda)Delta_sigma+lambda I)(A_a) gives sigma^1/2 U_a sigma^-1/2=K_{lambda,a}. Every equivalence is reversible. Hermiticity of R then makes [R,K]=0 equivalent to simultaneous [R,K]=[R,K^dagger]=0. Positivity and trace impose precisely the displayed constraints.

If C_lambda=C I, normalization forces R=I. Conversely any non-scalar *-algebra C_lambda contains a non-scalar Hermitian B. Subtract Tr(sigma B)I to form nonzero Hermitian B_0 with weighted trace zero. R=I+t B_0 is strictly positive for sufficiently small nonzero real t, has weighted trace one, and yields a distinct score-equivalent state. This proves necessity. No matrix-size limit or finite test is used.

In sigma's eigenbasis, the entries are
(K_lambda)_ij = sqrt(p_i p_j) (A_a)_ij / ((1-lambda)p_i+lambda p_j).
Since A_a is Hermitian, K_lambda^dagger=K_{1-lambda}. Hence C_lambda is the joint commutant of both complementary dressed frames. It also follows that the zero sets at lambda and 1-lambda coincide. For a restricted Hamiltonian family, the exact ambiguity is the intersection of that family with the displayed fiber; C_lambda nontrivial alone does not establish restricted-family nonidentifiability.

## Decisive qubit comparison and stability obstruction

Take sigma=diag(p,q), p+q=1, p,q>0, p!=q and the one-element frame A=X. For lambda=1/2, K=2sqrt(pq)X, and every
rho_t = [[p,t sqrt(pq)],[t sqrt(pq),q]], |t|<1
has the same SLD score as sigma: choose R=I+tX. It is full rank and normalized. It is a Gibbs state of some Hermitian Hamiltonian; a claim about a more restricted known support needs checking separately.

For any lambda!=1/2, write K=[[0,a],[b,0]],
a=sqrt(pq)/((1-lambda)p+lambda q), b=sqrt(pq)/((1-lambda)q+lambda p).
Then a!=b. From K+K^dagger=(a+b)X and K-K^dagger=(a-b)iY, the *-algebra contains X and Y and therefore Z and all M_2. C_lambda=C I. Thus this one direction identifies sigma for every nonsymmetric score even though {X}' is nontrivial. This sharpens the sufficient algebra-generation rule; it does not contradict the source theorem.

On traceless Hermitian matrices with Frobenius norm, R-> [R,K] has singular values |a-b|, a+b, sqrt(2(a^2+b^2)), as follows by applying it to X/sqrt2,Y/sqrt2,Z/sqrt2: the images are respectively diagonal real, diagonal imaginary, offdiagonal real and pairwise orthogonal for the real Frobenius inner product. The smallest is
sqrt(pq)|1-2lambda||p-q| / (((1-lambda)p+lambda q)((1-lambda)q+lambda p)).
It vanishes linearly as lambda approaches 1/2. An injective score can therefore become arbitrarily poorly conditioned near the symmetric choice. This is a structural warning against identifying exact uniqueness with finite-sample usefulness. The derivation concerns commutator geometry, not a proved sample complexity bound.

work/b_prior/fiber_check.py independently computes scores in each state's eigenbasis, tests the p=.7,q=.3,t=.6 fiber, and reconstructs the real commutator singular values. results/b_prior/fiber_check.json: SLD score error 4.74e-16, eigenvalue minimum .16; nonsymmetric errors nonzero at the six tested lambda; smallest gap .014665 at lambda .49/.51 and .872872 at endpoints. Those are finite diagnostic checks supporting, not proving, the above general algebra.

## Novelty status, downstream use, falsifiable next step

The exact fiber and adjoint-completed necessary-and-sufficient criterion were not found explicitly in the relevant sources read. They follow by reversing the published sufficient-condition proof and imposing Hermiticity, so likely count as a modest mathematical sharpening; do not call them a consequential foundational breakthrough. The qubit asymmetry/conditioning example is an explicit consequence, novelty unverified. SLD tanh formula, BKM chain rule, commutant criterion and endpoint learning objective all have decisive prior art. Distinguish improved interpretation/diagnostics from a new efficient learning method.

Concrete use: choose directions using complementary dressed-frame gaps; detect hidden symmetry in a supplied low-dimensional model before claiming recoverability; understand why nonsymmetric score completion may need fewer directions yet amplify noise. A meaningful subsequent theorem would connect an accessible lower bound on the relevant frame gap to a copy-estimable stability inequality without requiring sigma's eigenbasis and without exponentially bad dependence on its smallest eigenvalue. No such theorem established here. First falsifiable step: exhibit a local many-body family where two proposed equal-information frame choices have certified different accessible score stability and matched copy/evolution costs, then compare to source high-temperature local X/Z learner and to existing Gibbs-learning methods. Reject a proposed advantage if computing its dressed gap requires tomography or candidate thermal-state preparation.

## Addendum: diagonal SLD bridge is classical ratio matching

The team lead derived the diagonal Gibbs-state SLD loss as a multiple of conditional Brier scoring. This worker verified the decisive classical sources. Hyvarinen's primary author-hosted published PDF Some extensions of score matching (2007), §2 Eqs (12)-(16), p2502/PDF4, uses g(u)=1/(1+u) and a symmetric squared difference of transformed single-bit flip ratios. Eq(13) is its population discrepancy. Theorem1 Eq(14) says it equals E_P sum_i g(Q(x)/Q(x^-i))² plus constant; Eq(16) is the sample mean. Algebra gives g(Q(x)/Q(x^-i))=1-Q(X_i=x_i|X_-i=x_-i), exactly scalar Bernoulli Brier at the observed outcome. Strict positivity matters for the population identification argument; do not inherit broad zero-probability assurances for proofs.

Dawid–Lauritzen–Parry arXiv:1104.2224v3 (verified v1 12Apr2011; v3 31May2012; Ann Stat40(1):593-608) Example4.4 Eq(14), PDF10, explicitly states conditional scalar Brier S(x,Q)=sum_i (x_i-Q(X_i=1|X_-i=x_-i))² is ratio matching. PDF11 writes its divergence as sum_i E_Pcontext (Pcond-Qcond)² and connects it to Hyvarinen2007 Eq(13). Footnote2 flags errors in that older paper's further analysis, so independent derivation and the 2012 formula are preferable to copying old gradients wholesale. Full two-outcome Brier =2 scalar Brier; the lead's factor4 full Brier is factor8 scalar Brier. The quantum-to-discrete identity may be a useful explanatory bridge, but the resulting diagonal partition-free all-temperature estimator is an established classical method.
