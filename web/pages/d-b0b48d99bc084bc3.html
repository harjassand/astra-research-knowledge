# A positive-definite coupling with no attracting coordinate axis for the canonical Hessian field

7 October2026. Our deduction. Independently testable obstruction to one universal-nonvanishing proof mechanism; **not** a counterexample to axis acquisition or E_F!=0. Root derived the general local formula independently; worker independently reconstructed and directly differentiated it. Root/peer gate still required for adoption.

Let f(y)=(y^2)^T Q(y^2), Q>0, H=Hess f, h=det H, F=adj H grad h. Suppose every q_ij!=0. At e_i write a=q_ii, b_j=q_ij for j!=i, and c=h(e_i)=12a product_(j!=i)4b_j. Hessian there is diagonal with entries12a,4b_j. Hence

    F_i(e_i)=n c^2/(6a),   F_j(e_i)=0.

The scaled transverse projective slope of coordinate ratio y_j/y_i is

    (partial_j F_j(e_i)-F_i(e_i))/(c^2/2)
     = 3q_jj/q_ij^2
       +sum_(k!=i,j)q_jk/(q_ik q_ij)
       -(n+1)/q_ii.                              (1)

For an independent derivation, A=adj H=h H^{-1} is valid near e_i. The first Hessian derivative H_j has only the symmetric(i,j) pair8b_j, while its second derivative H_jj is diagonal. Thus

    partial_j A_ji(e_i)=-c/(6a),
    partial_jj h(e_i)=c[-2b_j/a+6q_jj/b_j
                         +2sum_(k!=i,j)q_jk/b_k].

Substitute these into partial_jF_j=(partial_j A_ji)partial_i h+A_jj partial_jj h, with partial_i h=2nc and A_jj=c/(4b_j), and subtract F_i(e_i). This yields(1). GL-naturality of F multiplies the transported field by det(L)^4>0, so its projective stability conclusion is intrinsic to the hidden rays.

Take

    Q=[[1,9/10,1/20],
       [9/10,1,-3/10],
       [1/20,-3/10,1]].

Its positive leading principal minors are1,19/100,141/2000. Formula(1), ordered by the remaining coordinate indices, gives

    i=1: [-188/27, 3568/3],

    i=3: [1136, -92/3].

Each axis has one positive and one negative transverse projective slope. It is therefore a saddle for both F and -F, and neither orientation has any strictly attracting hidden coordinate axis. This refutes the proposed transfer of the earlier max-vertex/hereditary-attractor proof to this canonical field using only QPD. A more general trajectory/nonvanishing argument remains possible.
