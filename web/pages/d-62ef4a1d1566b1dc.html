HOLEVO INFORMATION RADIUS AS A SUBSTITUTE FOR TRACE COVERING
2026-10-07. Status: requested global implication remains UNRESOLVED in this run.

Target: for finite-dimensional experiments E, C(E)=sup_finite-priors chi(pi,E),
does e_EB(E)<=f(b_2(E),C(E)) hold dimension-independently with f(b,C)->0
for each fixed C as b->0? No proof or counterexample with b_2->0 was obtained.
Do not present the Pauli family below as a counterexample to that target: its
b_2 is bounded away from zero.

Actual derived results:
1. Existing finite-label conditioning argument improves rigorously to
   e_EB(E)<=inf_{L>=0}[L b_2(E)+sqrt(m C(E)/(2*2^L))] for m supplied states.
   The m factor remains. This is an elementary Holevo data-processing
   refinement of prior adaptive-conditioning machinery, not a novelty claim.
2. A finite, optionally faithful, explicit Pauli experiment has fixed bounded
   capacity, exact EB error tending to a positive constant, and accessible
   information tending to zero. It disproves any attempt to replace the
   measured-information-to-trace step by a capacity-only constant.
3. The capacity-only constant-preparation bound e_EB<=sqrt(C/2) is asymptotically
   sharp at small C, even for faithful finite experiments of growing dimension.
4. Descendant derivation, independently audited by its parent and again read
   here: arbitrary restrictions of the uniform PURE dyadic ladder with
   C<=K have e_EB=O(((K+1)/L)^(1/4))->0, without dimension or cardinality bounds.
   Full proof: counterexample/ladder_capacity/LADDER_CAPACITY_OBSTRUCTIONS.md.
   It uses capacity bounds on each lower pure sector, an entropy-center
   spectral cutoff of rank <=exp(K/(eta a)), and actual copies in the supplied
   higher input sector to estimate the whole lower-sector pure state. It does
   not import extra specimens or assume pure-state preparation from an unknown
   parameter. Thus parameter restriction cannot rescue this known pure ladder
   as a bounded-capacity counterexample.

Conventions: all logs natural; T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. e_EB is worst
state trace reconstruction loss by a single measure-and-prepare channel.

FINITE-LABEL REFINEMENT

Let E={rho_x}_{x=1}^m and let B have both marginal errors <=b on E. Its depth-L
binary tree C_L has K=2^L leaves and marginal errors <=Lb by contraction and
telescoping. For a fixed design prior pi, perform the adaptive binary Helstrom
measurements from GENERAL_M_BROADCAST_BOUND.md on all K leaves solely to select
a small-information stage. At stage j and previous history h, write posterior
p_x(h), conditional leaf state sigma_x^h, its mixture tau_h, and d_x(h)=
T(sigma_x^h,tau_h). Selecting the label maximizing p_x(h)d_x(h)^2 gives

 I_h(X:Y_j)>=2 max_x p_x(h)d_x(h)^2
             >=(2/m)[sum_x p_x(h)d_x(h)]^2.

The full adaptive measurement composed with C_L is a POVM on the original
input. Consequently its total classical information obeys

 sum_j I(X:Y_j|Y_{<j})=I(X:Y_1...Y_K)<=chi(pi,E)<=C(E).

This replaces the source proof's bound by H(pi)<=ln m. Some j has conditional
gain <=C(E)/K. Measure only its prefix and prepare tau_h on the selected leaf.
The resulting EB channel has average pi loss <=Lb+sqrt(m C(E)/(2K)). All
conditional states, posteriors, and preparations are fixed from supplied E,
B, pi, and history; the actual unknown label is never used. In fixed input/
output dimension EB channels form a compact convex set, and the average loss
is continuous, convex in the channel, and affine in pi. The same Sion minimax
step as the source proof produces one channel with worst-state loss bounded
by this prior-independent expression. Infima in B are handled by arbitrary
slack. For C=0 all states coincide and a constant preparation is exact.

The full source bound can therefore use min{C(E),ln m}. A finite alphabet is
retained by the source Caratheodory step: at most (m+1)2^(K-1) outcomes. C_L
still costs 2^L-1 applications of B and K output systems. This is an existence
bound; efficient matrix acquisition, prefix selection, posterior computation,
minimax optimization, and preparation are not supplied.

EXACT FINITE PAULI BENCHMARK

Let d=2^q, q>=1. Let P_1,...,P_N be the nonidentity Hermitian q-qubit Pauli
matrices, N=d^2-1. They satisfy P_j^2=I, Tr P_j=0,
Tr(P_jP_k)=d delta_jk, and together with I form an orthogonal matrix basis.
For fixed 0<a<=1 define m=2N states

 rho_{j,s}=(I+s a P_j)/d,  s in {+1,-1}.

At a<1 every state is faithful. Every state has eigenvalues (1+a)/d and
(1-a)/d, each with multiplicity d/2. Its entropy is ln d-c(a), where

 c(a)=[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2,

with 0 ln0=0. Therefore every prior has chi<=c(a). The uniform prior has
mean I/d and achieves chi=c(a), so C(E_d,a)=c(a)<=ln2 exactly. Also
T(rho_{j,s},I/d)=a/2 for every label.

Accessible information. Let {M_y} be any POVM. Ignore zero-trace effects.
For the uniform prior, average outcome probability is q_y=Tr M_y/d and

 p_y(j,s)=q_y+s a Tr(M_yP_j)/d.

Using ln u<=u-1, the classical relative entropy obeys
D(p||q)<=sum_y(p_y-q_y)^2/q_y. Averaging labels and using Pauli Parseval,

 I(X:Y)
 <=a^2/[dN] sum_y [sum_j(Tr M_yP_j)^2]/Tr M_y
 =a^2/[dN] sum_y [d Tr(M_y^2)/Tr M_y-Tr M_y]
 <=a^2/[dN] [d sum_y Tr M_y-sum_y Tr M_y]
 =a^2/(d+1).

The inequality Tr M_y^2<=(Tr M_y)^2 follows from positivity, and sum_y
Tr M_y=d. This holds for every POVM, even with an arbitrarily large alphabet.
Thus I_acc<=a^2/(d+1)->0 although C=c(a)>0 and average trace radius=a/2.
In particular no inequality of the form

 I_acc >= k(C)[sum_x pi_x T(rho_x,rho_bar)]^2

with k(c(a))>0 independent of dimension can hold. This is precisely the
false replacement needed to remove m in the current measured-conditioning
proof without additional broadcasting structure.

Exact EB reconstruction. For any density matrix eta and any Hermitian Pauli,
|Tr P(eta-rho)|<=||eta-rho||_1. Hence for any EB channel Lambda,

 T(Lambda(rho_{j,s}),rho_{j,s})
 >=[a-s Tr(P_j Lambda(rho_{j,s}))]/2.

This lower bound is valid even if its right side is negative. Averaging s
cancels the possible nonunital part Lambda(I), giving

 average_loss >=a/2-[a/(2dN)] sum_j Tr[P_j Lambda(P_j)].

Write Lambda(A)=sum_z Tr(M_z A)tau_z. Pauli Parseval and Tr tau_z=1 give

 sum_j Tr[P_j Lambda(P_j)]
 =sum_z {d Tr(M_z tau_z)-Tr M_z}
 <=d sum_z Tr M_z-sum_z Tr M_z=d(d-1),

since 0<=tau_z<=I and sum_z M_z=I. Therefore

 e_EB(E_d,a)>=a d/[2(d+1)].

The EB covariant pure-state measurement/preparation channel

 Lambda_*(rho)=d integral |psi><psi| <psi|rho|psi> dmu_Haar(psi)
             =(rho+I)/(d+1)
             =[rho+d(I/d)]/(d+1)

attains that bound on every rho_{j,s}: it contracts the Pauli coefficient by
1/(d+1), so the error is (a/2)(1-1/(d+1)). Haar integration is an existence
formula; an exact projective 2-design or finite convex decomposition gives
a finite POVM in fixed d. Thus

 e_EB(E_d,a)=a d/[2(d+1)] -> a/2.

Why this is NOT the requested counterexample. Choose two anticommuting Pauli
matrices P,Q and restrict to their positive-sign states. They are a fixed
qubit pair tensored with a maximally mixed ancillary system. Their root
fidelity and affinity are

 F=sqrt(1-a^2/2),
 A=(1+sqrt(1-a^2))/2,
 Delta=F-A>0 for every fixed a>0.

If b_2 for the growing Pauli experiment vanished, preparing the fixed mixed
ancilla and tracing it out at both outputs would broadcast this fixed
noncommuting qubit pair with vanishing error. Compactness of fixed-qubit
channels would then give an exact broadcaster, contradicting the exact
no-broadcasting theorem. Equivalently the source dimension-free pair modulus
already forces b_2 bounded away from zero. At a=1 Delta=1/sqrt2-1/2.

CAPACITY-ONLY PINSKER BOUND AND ITS SHARPNESS

The relative-entropy information radius identity is

 C(E)=inf_omega sup_{rho in E} D(rho||omega)

in finite dimension (closure can be taken for arbitrary E). Constant
preparation of an optimal center and quantum Pinsker D>=2T^2 give
e_EB(E)<=min{1,sqrt(C(E)/2)}. This is an established elementary consequence,
not a new capacity theorem. In the Pauli family c(a)=a^2/2+O(a^4), while
e_EB->a/2. Thus lim_{a->0}lim_{d->infinity} e_EB/sqrt(C/2)=1.

This exact benchmark also separates Holevo capacity from metric covering:
many labels at fixed trace separation can coexist at bounded capacity. An
even simpler commuting balanced-sign code on a growing finite alphabet has
the same phenomenon and exact EB error zero; bounded C alone cannot supply
the source uniform trace total-boundedness hypothesis.

FAILURES / OPEN BOTTLENECK

- Pure dyadic copy ladders retain growing information capacity. Common
  flagged dilution of fixed mass does not bound C; shrinking flagged mass
  also shrinks the worst EB error. Descendant independently proves a more
  general bounded-capacity restriction obstruction; see its own report.
- Antisymmetric k-extendible states with bounded mutual information are far
  from separable in trace norm, but their Werner-Holevo output state families
  are trace-close to I/d. They do not establish large reconstruction error.
- Random half-dimensional subspaces and the explicit Pauli family obstruct
  capacity-only measured-to-trace extraction but have nonvanishing b_2.
- Near-tracial many-coordinate product families and MUB hole states can have
  bounded C and small pair gaps; no common b_2->0 channel was constructed.
  Pair-gap smallness is insufficient to assert broadcasting.
- The full target requires exploiting dynamical broadcasting structure to
  bypass the measured-norm/data-hiding obstruction. A claim proved only for
  a finite-label bound, entropy radius relative to a supplied center, thermal
  displacement subclass, or a restricted ladder is not the global target.
