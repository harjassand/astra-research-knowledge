1. PIN THE DEFINITIONS BEFORE CHECKING SIGNS

For a fixed state rho and Hermitian frame {h_l}, set
T=sum_l Tr(rho h_l^2), a=sum_l Tr(rho h_l)^2, and Q=sum_l F_Q[rho,h_l]/4.
The supplement's common pure-decomposition definitions are:

 I  = min_ensemble sum_k p_k sum_l Var_psi_k(h_l),               S7
 R  = max_ensemble sum_k p_k sum_l Var_psi_k(h_l),               S8
 I' = T-I = max_ensemble sum_k p_k sum_l <h_l>_psi_k^2,           S17
 R' = T-R = min_ensemble sum_k p_k sum_l <h_l>_psi_k^2.           S18

One ensemble serves every h_l. By the single-observable QFI roof theorem,
min(sum_l averaged variance)>=sum_l min(averaged variance), so

 I>=Q.                                                           (A1)

Variance concavity gives averaged variance<=the state variance, so

 R<=T-a.                                                         (A2)

Consequently

 I'<=T-Q,   R'>=a.                                               (A3)

2. CONFIRMED DIRECTION ERRORS

Fetched source S11a instead displays I<=Q; its direction is reversed.
Fetched source S11b instead displays R>=T-a; its direction is reversed.
Fetched source S26a instead displays I'>=T-Q; its direction is reversed.
Fetched source S26b instead displays R'<=a; its direction is reversed.

There are exact Pauli witnesses rather than interpretive objections.
For rho=I/2 and frame {sigma_x,sigma_y}, every pure Bloch vector has
x^2+y^2<=1. The +/-x ensemble attains maximum score 1, and the +/-z
ensemble attains minimum score 0. Therefore

 T=2, a=0, Q=0, I=1, R=2, I'=1, R'=0.

Thus S11a would say 1<=0, and S26a would say 1>=2.
For rho=I/2 and frame {sigma_x,sigma_y,sigma_z}, every pure score is 1,
so

 T=3, a=0, Q=0, I=R=2, I'=R'=1.

Thus S11b would say 2>=3, and S26b would say 1<=0.

3. S24 SWAPS THE TWO ROOFS

The fetched S24 displays, for frame {sigma_x,sigma_y},

 I'=F_Q[rho,sigma_z]/4,
 R'=1-<sigma_z>_rho^2.

These assignments are swapped. For a qubit with Bloch vector r, exactly

 I'(rho,{sigma_x,sigma_y})=1-r_z^2,
 R'(rho,{sigma_x,sigma_y})=r_x^2+r_y^2=F_Q[rho,sigma_z]/4.          (A4)

For the maximum, pure Bloch vectors satisfy x^2+y^2=1-z^2.
Jensen gives the upper bound 1-r_z^2. An ensemble with every z=r_z
and with x,y averaged to r_x,r_y attains it, since the allowed circle
has radius sqrt(1-r_z^2) and contains the desired mean in its convex hull.
For the minimum, Jensen gives x^2+y^2>=r_x^2+r_y^2 on average.
An ensemble fixing x=r_x,y=r_y and mixing the two values
z=+/-sqrt(1-r_x^2-r_y^2) attains that bound.
The QFI equality in (A4) is also the single-observable sigma_z roof.

At I/2, (A4) gives I'=1 and R'=0, directly contrary to S24.
No transpose convention appears in this single-system calculation.
The common-roof versus sum-of-single-roofs distinction is retained.

The paragraph preceding S24 claims the left side of S21 is 1 for this
two-Pauli example at I/2. In fact that left side is R'=0, equal to the
mixed-decomposition minimum 0. Its claimed two-Pauli gap is therefore
false. The three-Pauli example in S25 is a valid pure/mixed gap:
the pure minimum is 1 and the mixed minimum is 0.

The saturation sentence immediately after S26 assigns L=1,2 equality
to S26a and only L=1 to S26b. After correcting the directions, the
L=2 saturation belongs to the R' inequality S26b, consistent with its
own correct S27c. The general I' equality is only justified for L=1;
the two-Pauli tracial example is already strictly below T-Q.

4. THE SEPARABLE SUPPORT IDENTITY SURVIVES INDEPENDENTLY

S28/S31 identifies I' with the maximum correlation
sum_l Tr(rho_AB h_l tensor h_l) over separable rho_AB with both
marginals equal to rho. This identity remains correct.

Indeed, refine a separable decomposition to pure products
rho_AB=sum_k p_k pi_k tensor eta_k and write their expectation vectors
m_k,n_k. Then

 sum_k p_k m_k dot n_k
 <= (1/2)sum_k p_k(||m_k||^2+||n_k||^2)
 <= I'(rho,{h_l}).

Both marginal ensembles have barycenter rho. Conversely, an optimal
ensemble for I' gives the identical-product coupling
sum_k p_k pi_k tensor pi_k attaining I'. It is symmetric and separable.
This proof uses the definitions only and none of the incorrect signs.

The unrestricted two-copy expression S29 / Table 1(c) is a single
observable, two-qubit Wigner--Yanase formula. It is not an arbitrary-frame
or arbitrary-dimension broadcasting statement.
