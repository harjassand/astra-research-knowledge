Claim boundaries
----------------
The lower bound concerns the random conditional KL R_n before X_{n+1} is
observed. Its mean over X^n is the expected terminal conditional-KL risk.
The realized log score Lambda_n is a different random variable whose
conditional mean given X^n is R_n; its proved lower-tail probability is only
Omega(n^-1/2). Independent-history risk is not covered. The minimax lower
prior depends on n, so no fixed-source anytime conclusion is claimed.
