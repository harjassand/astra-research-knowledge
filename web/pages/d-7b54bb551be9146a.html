## Saturation and exact full logical factor

Define

    Q_X = intersection_(F in Pauli(X)) F^* P1 F,
    P   = intersection_(E in B) E^* Q_X E.                         (2)

All factors are syndrome-diagonal projectors, so these are ordinary products.
Conjugation by any Pauli on X permutes the factors in Q_X. It also permutes
those in every E^*Q_XE because two Paulis commute up to phase. Thus Q_X and P
commute with the entire matrix algebra on X:

    Q_X=I_X tensor Q_out,   P=I_X tensor P_out.                    (3)

They reduce H0 and V_X and commute with the old dressed pair. Every Pauli
prefix on X sends Q_X into P1. Telescoping one-qubit commutators therefore
gives [O_j,F]Q_X=0 for all F on X, hence [O_j,V_X]Q_X=0 by linearity.
Similarly P<=Q_X and E P maps into Q_X for every bath Pauli E. Because B
contains every Pauli prefix on each bath support, telescoping gives
[O_j,E]P=0. This argument incurs no Pauli coefficient-l1 cost.

For clarity, restricted Hamiltonian commutation is insufficient for a full
thermal logical factor. Clean a bare logical pair off X using s<d, as follows.
Let S be the binary stabilizer space and V_X^bin the on-X symplectic space.
Distance implies S^perp intersect V_X^bin = S intersect V_X^bin. For a bare
logical l in S^perp, every w orthogonal to projection_X(S) belongs to this
intersection and is orthogonal to l. Nondegeneracy and double orthogonal
complement show projection_X(l) belongs to projection_X(S). Solve a binary
linear system for a stabilizer removing l's X support. Do this for both
logical generators. The resulting L_X,L_Z remain an anticommuting Pauli pair
and commute globally with H0+V_X.

Patch the pair by

    O'_j = Q_X O_j + (I-Q_X) L_j.                                 (4)

Each branch commutes with Q_X. The pair squares to identity, anticommutes,
and commutes with H0+V_X on the full Hilbert space. For bath E and P,
E P is in Q_X, so [O'_j,E]P=[O_j,E]P=0. Arbitrary normalized raw bath
operators follow by linearity, without expanding them for the rate bound.
All actual Bohr components also satisfy protection: O'_j and P commute
with H, so spectral projection of [O'_j,A_b]P=0 yields it componentwise.

Consequently the complete Hamiltonian is I_log tensor H_aux in the factor
specified by (4), and the thermal encoder uses the NEW Gibbs auxiliary

## Amplitude-independent Gibbs comparison

Let H_t=sum_(checks meeting X) h_a D_a and H_o=H0-H_t. Then H_o acts off X,
0<=H_t<=M I, and M=sum_(checks meeting X) h_a<=g0 s. H_t commutes with H_o,
P1 and P. Its individual terms need not commute with V_X.

A useful finite-dimensional fact: if a projector R reduces A and B, and
0<=B<=M I, then each ordered eigenvalue of A+B on either R block lies
between its counterpart for A and that counterpart plus M. Thus

    Tr_R exp[-beta(A+B)] <= Tr_R exp[-beta A],
    Tr   exp[-beta(A+B)] >= exp(-beta M) Tr exp[-beta A].           (5)

This is block minmax, not the false operator-monotonicity of exp.

First evaluate the union in the reference Gibbs state rho_o of H_o. It is
uniform on X, so each F_X conjugation leaves rho_o exactly invariant. An
E in B flips checks of H_o with total penalty at most g0 q; since H_o is
commuting, its Gibbs translation cost is at most exp(beta g0 q). A union
bound on (2) therefore gives

    p_P,o <= 4^s M_B exp(beta g0 q) p1,o.                          (6)

Positive comparison (5) on the P1 blocks gives p1,o<=exp(beta M)p1,0.
The Hamiltonian H_o+V_X factorizes, and (3) implies exact cancellation of
its on-X partition factor: p_P,(o+V)=p_P,o, for ANY strength V_X. Finally
P reduces H_o+V_X and H_t; applying (5) once more yields

    p_P,new <= 4^s M_B exp[beta(2M+g0 q)] p1,0.                   (7)

There is NO exp(beta g0 s) translation cost and no ||V_X|| in (7). Deleting
H_t before the Pauli saturation is the reason. The looser common-block
Weyl comparison p_new<=exp(2beta||V_X||)p_P,0 also holds, but is unnecessary
for the amplitude-independent statement. Strong fields can change the
actual rate envelope gamma and the cost of preparing the new Gibbs state;
those quantities are not controlled by (7).

## Costs, prior control, falsifier

The cleaning proof is constructive binary elimination on supplied original
check matrices. Explicit saturation uses4^s translations and at most
M_B such products; its formal existence does not furnish efficient physical
preparation of its patched factor. The decoder's level2 lookup, geometry
acquisition and the new Gibbs auxiliary preparation remain charged and
unexecuted. Raw-bath count K is retained: a Pauli expansion is used for
protection, never turned into4^q independent bath channels. Pinching controls
all spectral components without paying their cardinality. Optional Lamb
shift terms need their own protected-algebra condition and are not silently
included in (8).

The method is a model application of Pauli cleaning, invariant saturation,
finite-dimensional minmax, and Chesi's older criterion. It proves no robust
class of full-density arbitrary perturbations and no generic quantum
Peierls inequality. Novelty status UNKNOWN. The next falsifier is a claimed
bath support not included in B, failure of exact full-algebra patching, or
a support/rate/preparation budget violating the stated interface. Unlike
a bound involving total norm, (7) withstands arbitrarily strong local V_X.
