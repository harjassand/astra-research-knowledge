. Introduction

One significant focus in discrete geometry is the study of structured and optimal point arrangements. Finding point sets that minimize energy, form efficient packings or coverings, maximize the number of unit distances, or avoid convex sets, for example, each comprise a significant and long-standing research program in the area [17]. Many other famous problems in discrete geometry are point arrangement problems in disguise: The famous equiangular lines problem, for example, corresponds to finding a regular simplex with many vertices in real projective space; the sphere kissing problem corresponds to packing points on a sphere.
