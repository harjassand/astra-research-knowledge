## 2. General crossed-product MASA, before specializing the geometry

For a disturbed region X, let S_t be the Pauli group generated by original checks meeting X and let

    J_X = Alg(S_t, Pauli(X)).

Its support lies in R_X=union of whole touched-check supports, with X included. It commutes with each untouched check. Suppose X is erasure-correctable: every Pauli on X commuting with all original checks is a global stabilizer. Let L_X=J_X intersect Alg(S), where S is the full stabilizer group.

Every Pauli label in J_X can be written S P with S in S_t and P on X. If this label centralizes S_t, P centralizes the touched checks. It also centralizes all untouched checks because they avoid X. Correctability makes P, and hence S P, a global stabilizer. Orthogonality of the physical Pauli basis gives the operator identity

    J_X intersect Alg(S_t)' = L_X.

The algebra L_X is abelian and contains Alg(S_t). Therefore

    L_X subset J_X intersect L_X' subset
      J_X intersect Alg(S_t)' = L_X.

Thus L_X is a MASA in J_X, including its center. This is a statement inside a specified incident algebra; it does not assert that every operator on R_X is incident-check diagonalizable. For a general X, L_X can contain stabilizers on X beyond S_t, and their original-generator filling/congestion remains an additional cost.

For disjoint X_i,X_j touched by no common original check, [J_i,J_j]=0: stabilizers commute, the bare-region Paulis have disjoint supports, and every touched check for one avoids the other's region. This holds even if R_i and R_j overlap at spectator qubits. It fails as an automatic rule when an original check touches both regions.

## 3. Exact centers, spectators and sorted matching

Write any finite-dimensional faithful representation as

    J_i = direct_sum_c (M_(d_c) tensor I_(m_c)).

Because L_i is a MASA including the center, each of its summands is D_(d_c) tensor I_(m_c). Let H0_i be the sum of original penalties incident to X_i and H_i=H0_i+V_i. Within each central summand, sort the d_c eigenvalues of H_i and the diagonal entries of H0_i, counting degeneracies. Send the corresponding H_i eigenbasis to the sorted H0_i coordinate basis. The resulting block unitary U_i is in J_i. Minmax in that SAME block gives

    D_i = U_i H_i U_i^* - H0_i in L_i,
    ||D_i|| <= ||V_i||.

No eigenvalue moves to another center sector, and the multiplicity m_c is an unchanged identity factor. This is the required center/multiplicity audit. A ground-band argument would not prove it. U_i can be far from I for arbitrarily small fields at a high-energy degeneracy.

For an eligible singleton x_i, no nonidentity on-site Pauli centralizes the checks; hence the earlier Pauli argument gives L_i=Alg(S_t,i) exactly. Put p_i=rank_F2 S_t,i, after all incident dependencies. Both CSS colors imply 2<=p_i<=7. X_i,Z_i add two independent binary labels: an on-site Pauli belonging to S_t,i would centralize every check and so be identity.

The commutation form in this p_i+2 dimensional space is

    [ 0_(p_i)   B ]
    [ B^T       J2],  rank B=2.

The two columns of B remain independent after eliminating check dependencies: one detects nonempty Z incidences and the other nonempty X incidences. A vector in the radical first has zero on-site component since B has full column rank; its check component lies in ker B^T. The radical has dimension p_i-2 and the full form has rank4. Thus the algebra consists of at most2^(p_i-2)<=32 M4 blocks with spectator identities. Each incident-check MASA has four diagonal entries in each block. ## 4. Global reduction, positive density and exact local strength

    U(H0+sum_i V_i)U^* = Hd = H0+sum_i D_i.

    E_d(sigma)-E_d(sigma\tau)

This uses operator norms, not a Pauli coefficient l1 norm. It applies to joint-CSS check dependence; no Gibbs sector factorization is assumed.
