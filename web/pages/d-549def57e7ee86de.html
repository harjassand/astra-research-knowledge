Title:Optimal Concentration of Information Content For Log-Concave Densities

Authors:Matthieu Fradelizi, Mokshay Madiman, Liyao Wang
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Optimal Concentration of Information Content For Log-Concave Densities, by Matthieu Fradelizi and 1 other authors

    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)

            Abstract:An elementary proof is provided of sharp bounds for the varentropy of random vectors with log-concave densities, as well as for deviations of the information content from its mean. These bounds significantly improve on the bounds obtained by Bobkov and Madiman ({\it Ann. Probab.}, 39(4):1528--1543, 2011).

          Comments:
