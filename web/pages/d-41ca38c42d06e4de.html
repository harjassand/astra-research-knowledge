## Exact interface and load-bearing result

Input: iid exact rational X1,...,XN in R^n, a justified almost-sure envelope ||X||<=R, a failure budget alpha, and the actual scientific promise X=mu+A Z with unknown mu and invertible A, Cov(Z)=I, and reflectional fourth cumulant h_Q with nonnegative pair gaps in that standardized source gauge. QPD is a sufficient subclass. Population covariance, cumulant, error radii and frame are **not supplied**. The true-source promise and support envelope **are supplied**; fitted candidate positivity cannot certify either.

The population covariance is the aligned metric C=AA^T, since the quartic is h_Q(A^T x). This proves the metric orientation/link. Four raw tensor moments through order4 and four scalar norm moments through order8 supply one confidence event with failure <=12 exp(-t). An observed second-moment variance bound, Hilbert Bernstein and a fully expanded mean correction provide epsilon_C and epsilon_T. The proof explicitly permits estimation of these bounds from the same samples and performs concentration before empirical whitening.

For any adaptive invertible rational frame V, define the exact candidate metric Gc=V^-T V^-1. With observed rational bounds nu>=||V V^T||op and Delta>=||Chat-Gc||op, the one confidence event gives

    zeta=nu(epsilon_C+Delta),
    (1-zeta)Gc <= C <= (1+zeta)Gc,
    delta=nu^2 epsilon_T.

If zeta<1, the adopted cycle12 composition gives

    delta_eff=delta+[(1-zeta)^(-2)-1](||That4||B,Gc+delta),
    deficit <= 2(delta_eff+rho)^2/qc^2,

where rho is the exact residual of projecting the observed quartic into the proposed frame's even monomials, and qc is half its minimum pair gap. Add metric axis distortion zeta(1+zeta)/(1-zeta) and an explicit condition factor for V to report ordinary Euclidean axis angles. A small deficit acquires the permutation matching globally; no local branch is supplied. All adaptively selected V are covered simultaneously by the same confidence event. Unknown conditioning, nonpositive qc, singular V or broad ambiguity remain non-recovery outcomes.

    Cov(Z)=I, E tau^4=1009/625,
    Q=(1152/625)J+(1009/625)I,

The unknown calibration is A=D H, H=I-2J/n, D_ii=1+i/(8n), i=0,...,n-1; mu_i=(-1)^i/(32n). It is rational, invertible, dense and withheld from each proposer. The checker verifies the model-wide envelope, rather than checking only the sample maximum.

The learner receives observed vectors with **strictly positive** multiplicities, N, R, alpha and the true positive-common-variance subclass promise. It receives no latent frame, mean, population probabilities, exact Q or population covariance. Latent values are stored separately for the checker. These finite distributions/counts are our constructed examples of a published class, not an externally published dataset. ## Complete resource boundary

For generic raw measurements, acquiring/reading the observations costs Omega(Nn), before statistics or optimization. Computing symmetric moments through order4 costs O(Nn^4) arithmetic and O(n^4) streaming storage, with coefficient/input bit heights charged. Exact multiplicities permit O(Un^4) statistics **after** acquisition/compression. The implementation generates a compressed multinomial realization using floating approximations to the model probabilities and a finite-precision RNG with retained seed. Its exact count-data identities are checked, but exact random sampling from the rational population law is not certified; it never physically collects the indicated N observations. Constructing our finite source law itself enumerates2*3^n atoms and is also charged as fixture generation, not a generic learning algorithm.

Full compressed simulation pipelines, including shared statistics and both controls, take approximately0.050s,0.027s,5.202s and6.067s respectively. These are local arithmetic times over U unique vectors, not processing N independent rows. For the n8 high-count run, eigenmatrix's proposal-only time is about0.00020s, MaxKurt's about0.01224s, and their respective exact posterior checks about0.986s and0.971s. Proposal timings exclude the common covariance, whitening, tensor construction and data-generation costs; no algorithmic speed comparison is inferred. Eigenmatrix performs an O(n^6) dense eigendecomposition. The current dense tensor-action MaxKurt implementation uses at most30 sweeps over n(n-1)/2 planes and is not an optimized sparse update implementation. Stronger engineering of either control remains available to the comparator.

The positive confidence result closes the *supplied metric/error* gap only under this richer bounded statistical interface. It does not close metric acquisition from a single noisy quartic. It also does not improve the general exponential-net proposer, prove statistical validity after an arbitrary stopping rule, validate a true-source gap from the data, or show affordable recovery. Sample size is fixed in the theorem.

The saved input coordinates use at most12-bit integer numerators at n4 and14-bit numerators at n8, with lattice denominators640 and1280.
