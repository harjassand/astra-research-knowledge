## 2. Correct normalization

Let the species index be α=1,…,J, with fixed mα>0 and eα∈R. Physical momentum p has energy (mα²+|p|²)½. Set v=p/mα, q(v)=(1+|v|²)½, u=v/q, and gα(t,x,v)=mα³ fα(t,x,mαv). Put λα=eα/mα. Then

\[
\partial_tg_\alpha+u\cdot\nabla_xg_\alpha
 +\lambda_\alpha(E+u\times B)\cdot\nabla_vg_\alpha=0,
\qquad
\rho=\sum_\alpha e_\alpha\int g_\alpha\,dv,
\quad j=\sum_\alpha e_\alpha\int ug_\alpha\,dv.
\tag{1}
\]

Maxwell has ∂tE−curl B=−j, ∂tB+curl E=0 and the usual constraints. Each gα,0 is smooth, nonnegative, and compactly supported in phase space. E0,B0 have finite L² energy and all bounded smooth derivatives, and satisfy the signed constraints. All norms and cutoffs below are in v, not physical p; failing to rescale both the density and the force would give the wrong mass factors.

Each species has its own phase-volume-preserving flow. Its supported receiver has

\[
K_\beta=V_\beta'=\lambda_\beta(E+a\times B),
\qquad a=u(V_\beta),\qquad
a_t=(I-a\otimes a)K_\beta/q_X.
\tag{2}
\]

The last identity has **no extra λβ**. It differentiates u(Vβ) using the already charged momentum derivative Kβ. A neutral species has Kβ=0 and needs no force estimate.
