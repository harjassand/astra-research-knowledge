Title:Small Designs for Path Connected Spaces and Path Connected Homogeneous Spaces

Authors:Daniel M. Kane

            Abstract:We prove the existence of designs of small size in a number of contexts. In particular our techniques can be applied to prove the existence of $n$-designs on $S^{d}$ of size $O_d(n^{d}\log(n)^{d-1})$.
