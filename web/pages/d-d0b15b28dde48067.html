## Provenance

The companion source [R Markdown](../c8_morphogenesis_control/cycle9/deeper_attack/source_refs/plot_results_with_data.Rmd) assigns model cell starts at x = 0, −200, and −400 μm, labels the measured x displacement in micrometres, and uses hours for time. Its Fig. 3C code directly overlays the measured `Velocity` column against the simulated `V` profile at the first output time. Article Methods define measured cell speed as the magnitude of a two-dimensional endpoint displacement divided by six hours. The direct overlay is the source’s comparison convention; it does not by itself establish identity between a nonnegative 2D speed norm and signed 1D Eulerian tissue velocity.

For that plot, `Time=1` is the first stored simulation column: the Rmd maps table indices 1, 51, 101 to 0, 3, 6 h in its profile export. Thus the Fig. 3C velocity overlay uses the model's initial instantaneous field, while the measured values summarize six-hour endpoint displacement. This is a temporal as well as directional observable-map assumption.

## Experimental observable used for the conditional comparison

`data_ex_vivo_tracked_cells_all.csv` contains one mean x-displacement curve for each dataset and each of three locations, sampled from 0–6 h. At 6 h, the four independent dataset means (in μm) are:

| Location | Dataset 170902 | 190305 | 190314 | 200120 | Mean | Between-dataset SD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bone front | 82.55 | 34.43 | 47.05 | 31.74 | 48.94 | 23.38 |
| In bone | 74.47 | 36.34 | 45.57 | 33.55 | 47.48 | 18.71 |
| Further in bone | 43.66 | 27.82 | 35.18 | 30.99 | 34.41 | 6.86 |

These are four video-level/location means; repeated time rows and individual cell counts were not treated as independent replicates. The SD is between datasets, unlike the per-cell SD shown for the Fig. 3C speed magnitudes.
