## Proposed lemma

Retain the exact rational S,W and admitted spectral gap of the cycle18 rational spectral module. Let K>=max(1,kappa(W)), A=SW, Q0=A(A+gamma I/2)^-1 and p(X)=3X^2-2X^3. Every exact Qj has operator norm at most sqrt(K), because its whitened eigenvalues lie in [0,1].

For any X,Y with ||Y||<=sqrt(K) and ||X-Y||<=1, one has ||X||<=2sqrt(K). Expanding noncommutative differences gives

    ||X^2-Y^2|| <= (||X||+||Y||)||X-Y||,
    ||X^3-Y^3|| <= (||X||^2+||X||||Y||+||Y||^2)||X-Y||.

Consequently ||p(X)-p(Y)|| <= (9sqrt(K)+14K)||X-Y|| <=32K||X-Y||.

Choose a total target rounding error 0<e<=1/2 and a fixed dyadic grid spacing

    rho <= e/[N(64K)^(J+1)],

where J is the publicly selected number of exact purification iterations. Round Q0 and each newly computed p(Qtilde_j) entrywise to this grid. Each rounding has operator error at most N rho. If d_j=||Qtilde_j-Q_j||, then d0<=N rho and

    d_(j+1)<=32K d_j+N rho.

The geometric sum gives d_j<=e for every j<=J, closing the condition d_j<=1 used in the Lipschitz estimate. This error control does not require rounded iterates to remain PSD or exactly W-selfadjoint.
