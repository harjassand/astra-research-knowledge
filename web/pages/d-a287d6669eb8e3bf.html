Square-Sided Heron Triangles

Abstract
We study rational Heron triangles with two marked square sides using elliptic curves and K3 surfaces.
An explicit quartic-to-elliptic correspondence parametrizes marked similarity classes by rational points
satisfying a positivity condition, modulo(x, y)∼(x,−y). We determine the generic Mordell–Weil
group, prove that everyk∈Q\{0,±1}supports infinitely many scalene classes with exactly two
square sides, and construct a primitive family withN (X)≫X1/4. Requiring the third side to be
square gives a genus-three Ciani quartic whose Jacobian isQ-isogenous to a product of three elliptic
curves. Geometrically, the two constructions give inequivalent elliptic fibrations on a single singular
K3 surface, with geometric Mordell–Weil ranks2and0. The minimal resolution of the all-square
locus is a surface of general type with invariants(K 2, pg, q) = (2 ,3,0). Assuming weak Bombieri–Lang,
parameters yielding a nondegenerate all-square triangle form a thin subset ofP1(Q).
Keywords:Heron triangle, square side, elliptic curve, Ciani quartic, K3 surface.

1 Introduction and main results
