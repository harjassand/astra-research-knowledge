## Selected: weighted real Landis construction

Frank–Ivanisvili, **2608.00802v1**, submitted 1 August 2026. Read Theorem1, Sections2–10 and AppendixA. Authors construct smooth real bounded V and globally r^(4/3)-decaying u in n≥3. Their key reusable mechanism is harmonic replacement on a uniformly sparse neighborhood of zeros, with a weighted Dirichlet inverse estimate and variable-radius smoothing. Our derivation changes the macro patch radius, not just the envelope. The proposed extension to V=O(r^(−β)) is written fully in `WEIGHTED_REAL_LANDIS_CONSTRUCTION.md`; r12 independent hostile gate PASS, while novelty remains UNKNOWN.

Nearest prerequisites: Filonov–Krymskii **2311.14491v2**, submitted24Nov2023, revised18Jun2024. Read Theorem0.1 and abstract spectral-gap comparison, Sections4–6 especially Theorem5.5 and correction/smoothing. Its cylinder result has dimension threshold from transverse Laplace spectral gaps and does not give global radial decay on R^n. Sparse-domain Newton-potential contraction and mean-value-preserving smoothing are already there. Cruz-Sampedro **1999**, journal42(1),143–153, DOI10.1017/S0013091500020071: read Theorems1–2, CarlemanLemma2.1/proofTheorem1 and annularconstructionSection3. Theorem2 provides exactly p=(4−2β)/3 for complex power-decaying potentials on R² only; Theorem1 gives the qualitative restriction in all n≥2, including ε=1/2, δ=0; publisher's2009online-date is not the publication year. Davey **2512.09839v1**,10Dec2025: read Theorem1 and the introductory iteration/conformal interface, Section2harmonic punctured-domain estimates. For planar real V=O(r^(−N)), N∈(0,2), normalized |u(0)|1 and growth |u(w)|≤exp(c0|w|^(1−N/2)), gives every-epsilon exponent1−N/2+epsilon. This is a serious dimensional comparator, not a contradiction of the proposed n≥3 construction.

Why consequential: if our adaptation is correct, weak pointwise decay of a real potential still fails to improve the complex unique-continuation power in dimensions at least three. A future improved theorem needs more than reality and V→0. It also points toward a reusable sparse-domain weighted inverse interface and a concrete derivative-control question. This is not advertised as an independent resolution of Landis; the new real mechanism belongs to FI and prior harmonic repair to Filonov–Krymskii.

## Other investigated recent interfaces

**Zhu2610.05704v1**,5Oct2026. Read main statements, determinant sampling, transported finite-dimensional spaces, moving complement/Rayleigh estimate and interpolation/telescoping. Authors prove observability from every positive-measure spacetime set for 1D heat with bounded real spacetime potential, uniformly at prescribed L∞norm. The moving-space method is potentially reusable: E_N(t)=U(t,0)span first N modes avoids a time-independent spectral split. Our reconstruction of its complement calculation: a reversed adjoint path with terminal vector perpendicular to E_N(t) begins/ends perpendicular to initial low modes; Dirichlet quotient derivative Q'≤M²/2 transfers Q≥λ_(N+1)−M²t/2. For w=z−F(t)a(t), differentiation gives w'=−(A+b)w−F a'; the orthogonality w⊥rangeF kills the last term in the energy identity. This abstract bounded-perturbation extension looked routine unless paired with a genuinely new transported sampling theorem. We did not select it merely because its formulas are elegant. The special total-positivity determinant bridge is the likely bottleneck outside one dimension.

**Boulard–Hayat–Trélat2607.13279v1**,14Jul2026. Read assumptions/main barrier and spectral packet mechanism. Weighted observability with h(t)=exp(−γ/t) forces γ≥L(ω)²/2 under nonnegative self-adjointness, ultracontractivity, Davies–Gaffney and pointwise local Weyl law. Control-norm convention divides that by2. The packet (cosh(r√A)−1)e^(−tA) and finite wave propagation handle arbitrary measurable sets and infinite horizon. A nonautonomous/moving-actuator extension would require replacing the spectral packet and checking the correct geometry; we found no tractable consequential new theorem yet. Do not transfer the author result to arbitrary learned metric kernels missing those hypotheses.

**Liu–Shi–Zhang2608.11602v2**,v1 12Aug2026,v2 21Sep2026. Read main simplex/lattice UCP statements, Pascal/factorial normalization and localization discussion. Their oriented simplex recursion gives quantitative lower support scale ceil(n/2); the full lattice result uses e^(−CL²) threshold and support of order L^ceil(n/2)/logL. Authors themselves explain that the exponent pair a=2,b=ceil(n/2) does not satisfy the inequality needed for their hoped-for Anderson–Bernoulli localization application. Better norm bookkeeping alone would not close that downstream gap. Qualitative simplex antecedent Li2608.02707 was found but not technically read/archived; no claim based on it. A Pith critique was encountered, not adopted as a defect or research lead.

**Capistrano-Filho–Martinez–Nascimento2609.36204v1**,28Sep2026. Read introduction/UCP setup and fixed-region control/stabilization sections. Authors claim unconditional periodic BBM UCP in energy regularity via a weighted Hamiltonian identity, with polynomial flux extensions. UCP by itself does not supply a uniform observability rate. For linear periodic BBM the high-frequency dispersion frequency tends to0, so localized fixed observation/control has a familiar compact-generator obstruction to uniform finite-time damping. That discriminator is useful but too familiar to select as a foundational result; nonlinear/quantitative acquisition is still separate.
