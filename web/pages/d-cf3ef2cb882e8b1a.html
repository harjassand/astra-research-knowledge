1. GENERAL RARE-SUPPORT INFORMATION LEMMA

Let Y be any random variable, let R(Y)>=0 be a measurable scalar field with
E R^2=M<infinity, and suppose R=0 outside an event B with probability p<1.
Set f_L(q)=q/(1+q/L), L>=32, and independently conditionally sample
  Z_T|Y ~ N(0,1+f_L(T R(Y)^2)).
Write alpha=Pr(|G|>=1)=erfc(1/sqrt2). If
  S=min(L,4 log(1/p))>=32,
then EXACTLY
  integral_0^infinity I(Y;Z_T) dT/T^2
       >= (alpha/8) M log(S/32).                         (RS)
No regularity, Poincare or covariance assumption is needed for this lemma.

PROOF. Fix a y with r=R(y)>0 and use s=T r^2 in [32,S]. Test the output event
  A_s={|Z_T|>=sqrt(s/2)}.
At this particular y the covariance is >=s/2, since s<=L. Its conditional
event probability a_y is therefore >=alpha. Under the marginal law, the
contribution from B^c is a standard Gaussian, and the contribution from B
has mass at most p regardless of how large its variances are. Consequently
  q_s=Pr(A_s)<=2e^-s/4+p<=3e^-s/4,
where the last inequality follows from s<=4log(1/p). Binary-event relative
entropy data processing gives
  KL(P_(Z|Y=y)||P_Z)>=a_y log(1/q_s)-log2
      >=alpha(s/4-log3)-log2>=alpha s/8,
because s>=32 and alpha(4-log3)>=log2. This comparison uses a different
measurable event for each fixed y inside a KL divergence; it does not assert
a single y-dependent decoder or move a measurement supremum inside an
operational average. KL data processing is a pointwise analytic inequality.

Integrate this lower bound over the y-dependent T interval [32/r^2,S/r^2].
Tonelli is legitimate because conditional relative entropy is nonnegative.
The Jacobian is dT/T^2=r^2 ds/s^2, giving alpha r^2 log(S/32)/8. Finally
integrate in y. On R=0 both the weight and the claimed contribution vanish.
This proves (RS). For fixed cap all conditional/marginal informations are
finite, since the variance is in [1,1+L].

2. COMPACT LOG-CONCAVE, SHARPLY SATURATED FIELD

Let n,K>=1, a=n+K+1, and Z=1-e^-a. Let X have density e^-x/Z on [0,a], and
let Y=(X-E X)/2. Then
  P(Y)=1/(1+4pi^2/a^2)->1,
  Cov(Y)=[1-a^2 e^-a/Z^2]/4->1/4,
as a->infinity. The exact Neumann spectral derivation is in INITIAL section1.

For 0<=u<=1 put phi(u)=u^2(1-u)^2, and set it to zero outside [0,1].
Phi is nonnegative, globally C1 and belongs to H2. Its exact integrals are
  C0=integral phi^2=1/630,
  C1=integral (phi')^2=2/105,
  C2=integral (phi'')^2=4/5.
The first derivative vanishes at both support endpoints, so the weak second
derivative has no delta boundary term. Let
  A=sqrt(Z/(K C0)),
  R(Y)=A e^(X/2) phi((X-n)/K).
This field is C1, in the generator domain, and supported strictly inside the
law's support. Exact cancellation of the exponential reference gives
  E R^2=A^2 K C0/Z=1.
Since d/dY=2d/dX, an integration of phi phi'=0 gives EXACTLY
  E |D_Y R|^2=1+4 C1/(K^2 C0)=1+48/K^2.                (SAT)
The centered generator in Y coordinates is
  A_eta=-d_Y^2+2d_Y =4(-d_X^2+d_X).
Within its support the conjugated exponential calculation yields
  (A_eta-1)R= -4A e^(X/2) phi''((X-n)/K)/K^2.
The C1 endpoint conditions justify this as a weak/form-domain and L2 identity
globally, without a hidden boundary distribution. Therefore
  E |(A_eta-1)R|^2=16 C2/(K^4 C0)=8064/K^4.            (EIG)
Thus when n,K->infinity, R is normalized, nearly mean zero, sharply saturates
the law's optimal Poincare energy, and is an approximate gap eigenfunction.
Indeed E R<=const K^-1/2 e^-n/2->0, so Var(R)->1 while P->1.
The potential's interior curvature is zero on the support of R, hence the
weighted curvature contribution E R^2 Hess(-log eta) is exactly zero there.
Boundary singularities of the compact density occur where R vanishes.

It has the scalar Sylvester representation

on R>0, set H=0 on R=0. All commutators vanish identically. H need not be
uniformly bounded near its zeros; E|H R|^2 is exactly the finite energy (SAT).
The source's integrated-upper reduction of derivative entries uses only this
weighted identity and the commutator defect, not a uniform bound on the

The carrier event is B={n<X<n+K} and

for n>=1. Apply (RS) with M=1. Along any n,K->infinity and every fixed L>=32,
  liminf integral I(Y;Z_T)dT/T^2 >=alpha/8 log(L/32).   (DIV)
This is the SAME limit order as source Section7: sequence first, then cap.
Sharp energy saturation, approximate gap eigenfunction, small mean, zero

3. ALL FINITE DERIVATIVE ORDERS / SMOOTH VERSION

If C-infinity regularity is desired, choose the explicit fixed bump
  phi(u)=exp(-1/[u(1-u)]) on 0<u<1, zero otherwise.
All derivatives vanish at the endpoints. Repeat the same normalization with

Moreover, for every FIXED m,
  D_Y^m R=A e^(X/2)(1+(2/K)d/du)^m phi((X-n)/K).
The binomial expansion in L2([0,1]) implies
  E |D_Y^m R-R|^2 ->0, E |D_Y^m R|^2 ->1.
Each m is fixed BEFORE K->infinity; no uniform-in-m bound is claimed.
The same rare-support proof (DIV) holds unchanged. This smooth construction

4. EXACT BOUNDARY TO THE N75 KLS QUESTION

The anomaly premise would require P0=1 and Cov op->0, whereas our compact law
has Cov/P->1/4. Consequently (DIV) is NOT a KLS counterexample and does not
falsify an information theorem that substantially uses Cov/P->0 or the exact
first eigenfunction plus that small covariance. It DOES falsify replacements
using only the source's normalized field second moment, sharp energy, small
mean, commutator defects, small curvature on the field, or fixed-order
approximate eigenfunction/derivative consequences.

N75's fixed-heat plateau might impose a stronger *simultaneous* multi-time
condition: the same anomalous mode persists for each fixed noise time below
one while covariance stays small relative to its gap. Our current example
does not establish/refute such a condition. The full U note is unavailable.
