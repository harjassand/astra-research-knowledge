Begin independently. Do not default to Astra's previous ionic transport, catalytic oscillation, defect repair, phonon-assisted irradiation or graphene isotope-separation programmes.

## Discover what science cannot currently do

Investigate major limitations in energy, chemistry, materials, synthesis, computation, manufacturing and experimental measurement.

Work backward from exceptionally valuable capabilities blocked by present physical operations.

Also investigate experimentally demonstrated responses whose deeper consequences may not yet be recognised.

Choose an opportunity where the missing operation could change what humanity can actually manipulate, measure, produce or discover.

## Invent from physical evidence

Identify a real material or experimentally accessible physical system.

Determine which physical responses, interactions and control operations are already established.

Invent a new operation by challenging the existing apparatus, physical intervention or representation.

Derive competing processes from one consistent description.

Do not independently assign favourable rates, coupling strengths, fields, lifetimes, reaction selectivities or energy costs.

If the mechanism requires an unmeasured response, determine whether it can be acquired with a specific, realistic experiment.

## Pursue exceptional consequences

A new transient physical signal is not automatically a useful technology.

Determine what new capability the mechanism would enable and why the strongest existing method cannot provide it at comparable cost.

Prefer mechanisms that remove decisive limitations in scientific measurement, controllable chemistry, precision synthesis, energy or other foundational capabilities.

Investigate retention, competing processes, selectivity, throughput, stability and total energy where relevant.

## Develop the decisive experiment

Derive quantitative predictions distinguishing the proposed mechanism from established alternatives.

Identify the smallest accessible experiment that could establish or reject the new causal operation.

If the evidence rules out the needed parameter regime, investigate another mechanism instead of adjusting hypothetical assumptions.

When genuine experimental leverage appears, pursue it deeply rather than repeatedly switching among superficial ideas.

6. The smallest experiment worth doing
The first experiment should remain on the demonstrated small molecular-ion platform. Moving immediately to large biomolecules would introduce unnecessary and unquantified preparation, motion and survival assumptions.

Establish the optical and ancilla baseline
Build phase-related pulse replicas with counterpropagating access. Measure the actual temporal envelopes, relative phases, dispersion and spatial pattern.

Calibrate the full pulse sequence’s effect on atomic readout and recombination, including off-resonant molecular settings and atomic-background controls. The measurement must determine whether the new optical intervention leaves a usable interferometer at all.

Measure the order-odd response
Prepare the spatial cat near a position turning point and apply the two opposite-order programmes on a timescale short compared with external motion.

Interleave the two orderings with phase-randomised sequences, individual-pulse exposures, common programmes on both branches, and matched off-resonant exposures. Read both ancilla quadratures.

The decisive signal is a molecular-resonance-dependent order-odd response that persists beyond pulse overlap and follows the controlled phase and delay dependence.

Why this is not yet an exceptional technological advantage
Existing two-dimensional infrared methods already measure molecular couplings and coherent dynamics. The proposal must outperform or extend those methods for a specified molecule and specified observable; merely producing a two-dimensional spectrum would not suffice. 

Likewise, ancilla-assisted computational spectroscopy already measures related mathematical objects on quantum processors. The proposed distinction is querying the native physical molecule, rather than a programmed simulation. That distinction is meaningful, but it is not automatically a speedup.

9. Research outcome
The substantive result is the combination of:

A physical construction. Two counterpropagating fields can independently programme two molecular branches at a geometry compatible with existing experimental scales, with an explicit finite energy cost.

A discriminating experiment. Opposite-order programmes yield an exactly derived coherent quadrature while preserving equal excitation in the calibration limit and identical illumination intensity everywhere. Phase randomisation supplies an additional null prediction.

A bounded feasibility test. Finite-wavepacket effects are modest in the idealised calculation, but molecular coherence, atomic Stark-induced errors, actual throughput and survival remain experimentally unresolved.

The decisive missing measurement is therefore:
