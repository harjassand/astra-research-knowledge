CLAIM AND STATUS

For rational-complex F and even n=2k, the determinant-weighted hard-pair law has an exact two-replica purification that retains independent orientation registers and their common-core mismatch. Its marginals are exactly the desired product law, and a global-proposal Metropolis kernel has rational, polynomial-bit-computable transitions without a partition-function oracle. This lift removes the specific common-cut annihilation: in the supplied n=8 counterexample, all positive tensor mass lies in the nonzero mismatch sector and the equality projector alone kills it.

The construction is an exact representation and transition rule, not a polynomial-energy result. Its uniform global proposal has an exact exponentially small spectral gap on the directed-cycle family. Thus this is a reusable L1 representation/diagnostic with an explicit failure of its naive kernel; the microscopic polynomial two-replica energy gate remains unresolved. No FPRAS, sampler with polynomial mixing, novelty, or external validation is claimed.

INTERFACE

Take n=2k and F in Q(i)^(n x n). For S in Omega={S subset [n]: |S|=k}, put
  d(S)=det F[S, [n]\S],   w(S)=|d(S)|^2,   Z=sum_(S in Omega) w(S)=c_k(F).
The microscopic paired-basis state associated to S selects the two columns e_i and f_i=row_i(F)^T for each i in S from V=[I,F^T]. Its n-column determinant is, up to a fixed ordering sign, d(S); hence its squared volume is exactly w(S). This is the hard-pair microscopic law pi(S)=w(S)/Z. For general k<n/2 the existing dummy-coordinate homogenization reduces the partition to full paired bases; the construction below is stated on the direct n=2k sector and does not supply costs for an enlarged-chain energy theorem.

PURIFICATION WITH INDEPENDENT MISMATCH REGISTER

For two retained sets R_1,R_2 with common core C=R_1 intersect R_2, let
  Omega_R={I subset R: |I|=|R|/2},
  a_R(I)=det F[I,R\I],   Z_R=sum_(I in Omega_R)|a_R(I)|^2.
Define Delta_C(I,J)=(I intersect C) symmetric-difference (J intersect C). The lifted state space is
  Omega_R1 x Omega_R2 x {Delta_C(I,J)},
with weight |a_R1(I)|^2 |a_R2(J)|^2. Equivalently, the formal unnormalized pure vector is
  Xi=sum_(I,J) a_R1(I) a_R2(J) |I,J,Delta_C(I,J)>.
The map |I,J> -> |I,J,Delta_C(I,J)> is an injective basis isometry, so ||Xi||^2=Z_R1 Z_R2. Measuring the first two registers and summing over the flag gives the exact independent product law; summing out J and the flag gives |a_R1(I)|^2/Z_R1, and symmetrically for the second replica. The normalization is deliberately symbolic: Z_R1 Z_R2 may be the hard quantity, and this equation is not an efficient state-preparation circuit.

The old common-cut projector is exactly the additional restriction Delta_C=emptyset. It is not an identity. For the saved eight-site instance in work/cycle5/parity_wick_sewing.txt, the only positive orientations are I_1={0,2} on R_1={0,1,2,3} and I_2={1,4,5} on R_2={0,1,4,5,6,7}; C={0,1}. Thus Delta_C={0,1}, Z_R1=Z_R2=1, the lifted norm squared is 1, and the equality-projected norm squared is 0. Keeping the flag preserves the independent-replica mass instead of silently discarding it.

COMPUTABLE REVERSIBLE TRANSITIONS

On Omega_R1 x Omega_R2 let N=|Omega_R1||Omega_R2|. From a positive state x=(I,J), propose y=(I',J') uniformly from all of Omega_R1 x Omega_R2, including zero-weight candidates, and accept with
  alpha(x,y)=min(1, |a_R1(I')a_R2(J')|^2 / |a_R1(I)a_R2(J)|^2).
Rejected proposals stay at x; after an accepted move, recompute the mismatch tag. This is a reversible kernel for pi_2(I,J)=|a_R1(I)|^2|a_R2(J)|^2/(Z_R1 Z_R2), since for distinct positive x,y,
  pi_2(x) P(x,y) = (1/N) min(pi_2(x),pi_2(y)),
which is symmetric. The two marginals therefore remain the exact target marginals. The common normalizer cancels from every acceptance probability.

For rational-complex input, each determinant and its squared absolute value is exactly rational. Clearing denominators and applying Hadamard bounds gives polynomial bit length in matrix dimension and input bit length; fraction-free elimination computes each proposed weight in polynomial bit operations. Uniform k-subsets can be generated by uniform-rank sampling and combinatorial unranking using O(n) bit-length integers; rejection sampling for a rational acceptance probability has expected fewer than two trials. Hence each transition is exactly samplable in expected polynomial bit cost, without an aggregate hole-coefficient oracle. A positive starting state can be supplied by the existing represented-parity support/witness route when its imported theorem applies; that support result is an optimization/witness interface, not a norm sampler.

EXACT LIMIT OF THE UNIFORM PROPOSAL

Let F be the directed 2q-cycle permutation, F[i,i+1 mod 2q]=1, and k=q. For S in Omega, d(S) is nonzero iff the cyclic shift of S is its complement. The only solutions are the two alternating sets, and both have w=1. The one-replica state-space size is N_q=binom(2q,q). Under the two-replica independent-uniform Metropolis proposal, the positive support has four equiprobable states; each distinct positive state is proposed and accepted with probability 1/N_q^2. The restricted transition matrix has diagonal 1-3/N_q^2 and every off-diagonal entry 1/N_q^2, so its spectral gap is exactly 4/N_q^2=4/binom(2q,q)^2=exp(-Theta(q)). This is an exact exponential obstruction to this global-refresh kernel, despite polynomial-time pointwise determinant and acceptance computation. It does not obstruct a cycle-aware move, a defect lift, or another kernel.
