CYCLE 3 LEAF | RATIONAL RATE BOXES FOR THE MULTI-CATALYST FAMILY
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the uniform controlled safety and
non-explosion bounds below; EXTERNAL-ESTABLISHED for the homogeneous
detailed-balanced subfamily (15); UNKNOWN for general homogeneous
higher-dimensional positive recurrence. No theorem for arbitrary predictable
controls is claimed.

1. Network and input interface
------------------------------
There are r>=1 catalyst species A_1,...,A_r, s>=1 substrate species
B_1,...,B_s, and integer gains m_ji>=1 (the prompt's k_ji). For every j,i
the reversible pairs are

  0 <-> A_j,
  A_j <-> A_j+B_i,
  A_j+B_i <-> (m_ji+1)A_j+B_i.

Write a_j and b_i for the counts. The six directed propensities associated
with each indicated pair are, respectively,

  0 -> A_j:                                      alpha_j^+(t),
  A_j -> 0:                                      alpha_j^-(t) a_j,
  A_j -> A_j+B_i:                               beta_ji^+(t) a_j,
  A_j+B_i -> A_j:                               beta_ji^-(t) a_j b_i,
  A_j+B_i -> (m_ji+1)A_j+B_i:                   gamma_ji^+(t) a_j b_i,
  (m_ji+1)A_j+B_i -> A_j+B_i:                   gamma_ji^-(t)(a_j)_(m_ji+1)b_i.

Here (n)_q=n(n-1)...(n-q+1), zero if n<q. This is the exact unit-volume
stochastic mass-action convention; no monomial replacement is made at low
counts. The state space is N_0^(r+s). The family is irreducible on the full
lattice when all fixed rates are positive; the exact helper-catalyst path is
in the Cycle 3 class-reachability leaf cited below.

Supplied inputs: the finite diagram, integer m_ji, and rational closed,
nonnegative, bounded rate intervals for each directed channel. There is no
acquired geometry, observed rate, volume parameter, or measurement. A
predictable controller may select each rate using the pre-jump state, time,
and full past, provided its value always lies in its supplied interval. All
conclusions below are uniform over such policies. When a controller is
restricted to preserve an active-catalyst region, that additional mode
restriction is stated separately.

2. Uniform generator envelope for predictable switching
-------------------------------------------------------
Choose positive rational weights w_j for A_j and z_i for B_i and set

  V(a,b)=sum_j w_j a_j + sum_i z_i b_i.

For every pre-jump state and every predictable rate selection, the exact
generator is

  L_t V = sum_j w_j(alpha_j^+ - alpha_j^- a_j)
       + sum_ji z_i a_j(beta_ji^+ - beta_ji^- b_i)
       + sum_ji m_ji w_j b_i
                    [gamma_ji^+ a_j
                     - gamma_ji^- (a_j)_(m_ji+1)].             (1)

The final bracket has degree m_ji+1 in a_j, multiplied by b_i. This exact
falling-factorial term is the mechanism that prevents a positive quadratic
or higher-order growth term from invalidating a global linear upper drift,
provided its reverse rate has a uniform lower bound (or the separate
first-order margin below holds).

Let all interval endpoints below be rational and finite. Use bars and
underlines for upper and lower endpoints. For each pair define

  A_ji = m_ji w_j overline(gamma_ji^+)
                         - z_i underline(beta_ji^-),
  H_ji = m_ji w_j underline(gamma_ji^-),
  G_ji = max_{n in N_0} [A_ji n - H_ji (n)_(m_ji+1)]_+.         (2)

The notation [u]_+=max(u,0); since n=0 gives zero, G_ji is nonnegative.
If H_ji>0, the maximum is finite because the negative term has degree
m_ji+1>=2. It is an exact rational number obtainable by finite integer
checks. Specifically, if A_ji<=0, G_ji=0. If A_ji>0, choose the least
N_ji>=m_ji+1 such that

  H_ji product_(h=1)^m_ji (N_ji-h) >= A_ji.                    (3)

For n>=N_ji, (n)_(m_ji+1)=n product_(h=1)^m_ji(n-h), so the expression in
(2) is nonpositive. Thus it suffices to maximize over 0<=n<N_ji. Rational
rate boxes and rational weights make (2)--(3) exact; no floating-point
margin is used.

If H_ji=0, then G_ji is finite exactly when A_ji<=0, in which case it is
zero. If B_ji=0<A_ji, the upper envelope is unbounded. This gives the exact
box condition for this linear-growth proof:

  underline(gamma_ji^-)>0
  OR
  z_i underline(beta_ji^-) >= m_ji w_j overline(gamma_ji^+).   (4)

Condition (4) is sufficient and, for the pairwise polynomial upper envelope
(2), necessary for finiteness. It is automatically met by strictly positive
lower bounds on every high-order reverse channel.

Set

  C_0 = sum_j w_j overline(alpha_j^+),
  D_j = sum_i z_i overline(beta_ji^+)
                         - w_j underline(alpha_j^-),
  G_i = sum_j G_ji,
  C_A = max_j [D_j/w_j]_+,
  C_B = max_i G_i/z_i,
  C   = max(C_0,C_A,C_B).

Using (1), the worst-case rate endpoints, and (2), for every allowed
predictable controller,

  L_t V <= C_0 + sum_j D_j a_j + sum_i G_i b_i
        <= C(1+V).                                               (5)

No lower-rate ordering is needed for (5), except for condition (4). In the
particularly simple margin regime

  z_i underline(beta_ji^-) >= m_ji w_j overline(gamma_ji^+)
  for every j,i,                                                 (6)

all G_ji vanish. If also

  w_j underline(alpha_j^-) >= sum_i z_i overline(beta_ji^+) - M_j
  for chosen finite M_j>=0,                                       (7)

then (5) holds with C_A<=max_j M_j/w_j. Strict catalyst death dominance
therefore tightens the growth constant, but does not create negative drift
in the substrate coordinates on the catalyst-free face.

3. Time-inhomogeneous safety and non-explosion
----------------------------------------------
Let W=1+V and assume (4). Equation (5) gives L_t W<=C W pointwise, uniformly
over time, state, history, and controller. For R>V(x), let

  tau_R = inf{t>=0: V(X_t)>=R},
  w_min=min(min_j w_j,min_i z_i),
  J_V=max(max_j w_j, max_i z_i, max_ji m_ji w_j).

Every jump changes V by at most J_V. Before tau_R, V<R and the stopped
process lies in a finite set. Time-inhomogeneous Dynkin/Gronwall therefore
gives

  E_x W(X_(t wedge tau_R)) <= W(x) exp(Ct),
  P_x(tau_R<=T) <= W(x) exp(CT)/(1+R).                           (8)

The second inequality is the uniform finite-horizon Foster safety bound. Its
constants are explicit from the supplied rational box, weights, and finite
maxima (2). It is not an exponential-in-R tail estimate; it is sufficient to
show that no unbounded-state escape occurs by a fixed finite time.

For completeness, a finite V-ball has an explicit uniform total-rate bound.
Put A_R=floor(R/w_min), B_R=floor(R/z_min). Then

  q(X) <= Lambda_R,
  Lambda_R = sum_j overline(alpha_j^+)
          + A_R [sum_j overline(alpha_j^-)
                 + sum_ji overline(beta_ji^+)]
          + A_R B_R sum_ji[overline(beta_ji^-)
                            +overline(gamma_ji^+)]
          + B_R sum_ji overline(gamma_ji^-)(A_R)_(m_ji+1),     (9)

whenever V(X)<=R, where z_min=min_i z_i. Thus infinitely many jumps confined
to a finite V-ball are impossible: the stopped jump count is dominated by a
Poisson process of rate Lambda_R. Any finite-time explosion would either
remain in a finite V-ball, which (9) excludes, or reach arbitrarily large
V before that time, whose probability is zero by (8) as R tends to infinity.
This proves non-explosion for all initial states and every predictable
controller satisfying the stated rate-box condition.

Holding-time and jump-size costs are explicit locally. While V<=R, the total
predictable intensity is at most Lambda_R, so the first holding time is
stochastically no shorter than an exponential variable of rate Lambda_R
(mean 1/Lambda_R when Lambda_R>0). The l1 count jump is at most
J_count=max(1,max_ji m_ji); the weighted jump is at most J_V. The highest
source order is max_ji(m_ji+2), and the local total-rate bound is (9). Across
unbounded states this certificate gives no state-independent lower holding-
time bound; positive upper rates on high-order channels can make q unbounded,
and controlled holding-time laws need not be exponential. The proof only needs
finite-set rate bounds and the global escape estimate. For rational inputs,
computing (2) requires integer search for N_ji and at most N_ji exact rational evaluations;
N_ji can be large when the reverse lower endpoint is small. No polynomial
bit-cost or uniform-in-rate numerical conditioning claim is made.

4. A strict but conditional Foster margin
----------------------------------------
The generator bound (5) is an upper-growth/safety bound, not a negative
Foster drift. A useful strict certificate is available on a supplied
active-catalyst domain. For each substrate i choose a designated catalyst
j(i). Let

  D = {a_(j(i))>=1 for every i}.

Assume the robust pair margins

  z_i underline(beta_ji^-) >= m_ji w_j overline(gamma_ji^+)
       for every j,i,                                             (10)

and for each designated pair a strict margin

  eta_i = z_i underline(beta_(j(i),i)^-)
                - m_(j(i),i) w_(j(i)) overline(gamma_(j(i),i)^+) > 0. (11)

Also assume a catalyst-dissipation margin, on states a_j>=2,

  w_j underline(alpha_j^-) - sum_i z_i overline(beta_ji^+)
       >= epsilon_A w_j,   epsilon_A>0,                          (12)

uniformly for all j. If the policy is allowed to turn off alpha_j^- at
a_j=1, that boundary mode is treated as having rate zero; this is possible
within the stated box only when its lower endpoint is zero. The resulting
one-state exception is absorbed into the constant below.

On D, the B_i-changing plus amplifier contributions from pair (j,i) are
bounded by

  b_i[(m_ji w_j overline(gamma_ji^+)
          -z_i underline(beta_ji^-))a_j
       -m_ji w_j underline(gamma_ji^-)(a_j)_(m_ji+1)].

They are nonpositive by (10), and the designated pair is at most
-eta_i b_i by a_(j(i))>=1 and (11). The catalyst linear terms are at most
-epsilon_A w_j a_j for a_j>=2 by (12); a_j=0 contributes no beta term and
each possible a_j=1 exception is absorbed in a constant. For a controller
that can suppress catalyst death at one, the safe universal choice is

  C_D = C_0 + sum_(j=1)^r [sum_i z_i overline(beta_ji^+)
                            + epsilon_A w_j].

This is slightly larger than necessary when death remains uniformly positive
at one. Therefore, uniformly over all
predictable rate selections satisfying these margins,

  L_t V <= C_D - epsilon_* V on D,
  epsilon_* = min(epsilon_A, min_i eta_i/z_i)>0.                  (13)

If every designated catalyst-death reaction is disabled when its count is
one (and zero belongs to each corresponding rate interval), D is invariant
and (13) gives a genuine time-inhomogeneous Foster
moment bound

  E_x V(X_t) <= e^(-epsilon_* t)V(x)
               +(C_D/epsilon_*)(1-e^(-epsilon_* t)).             (14)

Without that boundary restriction, D can be exited by A_j->0 when
a_(j(i))=1. Then (13) is only a stopped/local certificate up to loss of a
designated catalyst. It does not prove global recurrence or give a uniform
bound on the exit time from D. This extra admissibility condition is a
controller restriction, not a consequence of weak reversibility.

5. What fails on the catalyst-free face and under arbitrary controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------
At a=(0,...,0), every B_i-changing reaction and every amplifier is disabled.
For the linear V above, the exact drift is

  L_t V(0,b) = sum_j w_j alpha_j^+(t),

independent of b. Letting b grow makes V unbounded while this drift stays
constant, so no global inequality L_t V<=C-epsilon V with epsilon>0 can hold
for this weighted linear count, regardless of rate-box margins.
This does not rule out a nonlinear or multi-step Lyapunov proof. It isolates
the precise obstruction to upgrading (5) to global linear Foster recurrence.

If (4) fails for a pair (j,i), then H_ji=0 and A_ji>0. Select the box
endpoints gamma_ji^+=overline(gamma_ji^+), beta_ji^-=underline(beta_ji^-),
and gamma_ji^-=0, and consider a_j=b_i=n with all other counts zero. The
selected pair contributes A_ji n^2 to L_t V; all remaining terms
are at most linear in n. Since V=O(n), no C can satisfy L_tV<=C(1+V) for
this allowed controller. This proves necessity of (4) for the pairwise
linear-envelope certificate; it does not, by itself, prove explosion. The
reverse-rate floor or compensating beta^- margin is the exact safety premise
used above.

State-feedback also prevents an automatic stationary-law argument. A fixed
homogeneous mode may satisfy detailed balance at c, but when the selected
rates depend on the current state, incoming flux to x uses the mode selected
at a predecessor state whereas outgoing flux uses the mode at x. Thus the
same product-Poisson law need not solve the global balance equations. For
example, even on 0<->A, multiplying both homogeneous rates by a factor s(A)
preserves the same equilibrium ratio in every frozen mode, but a
state-dependent s makes adjacent birth/death flux ratios contain
s(A)/s(A+1); the fixed Poisson law is no longer invariant in general.

6. Homogeneous stationary positive recurrence: separate claim
-------------------------------------------------------------
Positive recurrence and a stationary distribution are standard properties
of a time-homogeneous CTMC; they are not inferred for arbitrary predictable
switching from (5) or (8). For fixed homogeneous positive rates, the following
detailed-balanced (hence complex-balanced) subfamily is rigorously covered. A
product-Poisson mean vector
(c_1,...,c_r,d_1,...,d_s)>0 satisfies detailed balance for every reversible
pair exactly when

  c_j = alpha_j^+/alpha_j^-,
  d_i = beta_ji^+/beta_ji^-  (independent of j for each fixed i),
  gamma_ji^+/gamma_ji^- = c_j^(m_ji)  for every j,i.             (15)

Then pi(a,b)=product_j Pois(c_j) product_i Pois(d_i) is invariant. Its
stationary total jump flux is the finite sum

  sum_{directed r} kappa_r theta^(y_r),

where theta=(c_1,...,c_r,d_1,...,d_s) and y_r is the reactant complex of
directed channel r. This is the sum of the finite factorial moments of the
displayed propensities. The full lattice is irreducible under positive
homogeneous rates, so the invariant probability gives positive recurrence; ACKK's
finite-flux theorem also gives non-explosion. These are standard
product-form consequences, not a new recurrence theorem.

For arbitrary constant positive rate vectors not satisfying (15), the
rate-box argument proves non-explosion but not positive recurrence. For
r+s>=3, no general classwise stationary law or positive-recurrence conclusion
follows from this report. For switching controls,
there is generally no single stationary law; exogenous time variation with
a common complex-balanced c is a separate special case, while arbitrary
state feedback does not preserve the common balance equations.

7. Status, costs, and falsifiers
--------------------------------
INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE: under rational bounded predictable rates
satisfying (4), the exact envelope (5), stopped safety estimate (8), finite
ball rate bound (9), and global non-explosion conclusion. The proof charges
all finite reaction channels and their exact falling-factorial rates.

INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE: the strict estimate (13) under the explicit
active-catalyst, pairwise rate margins (10)--(12); global use of (14) also
requires the stated boundary mode restriction preserving D.

EXTERNAL-ESTABLISHED: homogeneous detailed-balance conditions (15) imply
product-Poisson invariance, finite stationary jump flux, non-explosion, and
positive recurrence on the irreducible full lattice. The source theorem is
prior art; novelty is not claimed.

UNKNOWN: arbitrary homogeneous positive rates in dimension r+s>=3;
uniform recurrence or mixing under switching; any global negative Foster
function beyond the stated active-catalyst condition; and explosion when the
reverse-floor/compensating condition (4) fails.

Costs: network size is O(r+s+rs); (1) and (2) require O(rs) formulas.
Computing all G_ji checks at most sum_ji N_ji integer values after the exact
cutoffs (3). The local rate bound (9) grows polynomially in R with degree
max_ji(m_ji+2). Weighted jumps are at most J_V; the count jump is at most
J_count. Constants deteriorate when reverse lower endpoints approach zero.
No rate-uniform mixing time, stationary tail, or polynomial algorithm in
the input bit length is claimed.

FINITE-EVIDENCE: `rate_box_smokecheck.py` passes exact rational one-pair
fixtures for m=1,...,5, checking the generator-envelope and corrected
finite-ball constants on finite count grids. These checks audit arithmetic;
they are not the quantified proof above.

Exact falsifiers: an enabled reaction whose jump is missing from (1); a sign
error in the coefficient A_ji; a state n>=N_ji for which (3) fails to make
the expression in (2) nonpositive; a finite-time escape probability
contradicting (8); or a product-Poisson balance failure under (15). The
catalyst-free face directly falsifies a global negative-drift claim for
linear V, and the zero-reverse mode above directly falsifies (5) when (4)
is removed.
