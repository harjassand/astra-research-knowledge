EARLY INDEPENDENT FORMULAS — c02_s01 axial attack
Initial derivation only; all-N positivity unresolved.

q_k=exp[-delta(k-N/2)^2/N]/binom(N,k).
H0=(q_(i+j)), size floor(N/2)+1;
H1=(q_(i+j+1)), size floor((N+1)/2).
This is the Bernstein/logit Stieltjes criterion, not q as Hausdorff moments.
For odd N the two matrices are reverse-index congruent by q_(N-k)=q_k.
For even N both are centrosymmetric, so parity block reduction is exact.

Each Hs is a positive diagonal congruence of the factorial kernel
 K_s(i,j)=(i+j+s)! (N-i-j-s)! exp[-2delta i j/N].
The exponential cross term has the unfavorable sign; Schur-PSD cannot be
assumed. Decreasing delta from a proved endpoint preserves positivity:
the extra kernel exp[2(delta0-delta)ij/N] is PSD by its positive power series,
with positive diagonal congruences. This agrees with Gaussian local filtering.

N=2m. Normalize centered values v_j=q_(m+j)/q_m. Then
 v_j=binom(2m,m)/binom(2m,m+j) exp[-delta j^2/(2m)].
Under a symmetric logit measure X, v_j=E cosh(jX)=E T_j(z), z=cosh X>=1.
Thus the same finite problem is an ordinary truncated moment problem in
z>=1 of DEGREE m, after a triangular Chebyshev transform. It does not turn
q_k itself into ordinary moments. At delta0=0, t=(z-1)/2 has beta-prime
parameters(1/2,m+1). Its deformed moments are
 s_l(delta)=4^-l sum_(j=-l)^l(-1)^(l-j)binom(2l,l+j)v_j.

For odd N=2m+1, weight the symmetric logit measure by cosh(X/2). Then
 v_j=q_(m+1+j)/q_(m+1)=E[cosh((j+1/2)X)/cosh(X/2)]
 is a polynomial of degree j in z=cosh X, with recurrence
 V_0=1,V_1=2z-1,V_(j+1)=2z V_j-V_(j-1).
Again the transformed finite moment degree is m.

Normalized centered beta-logit Laplace transform has Euler product
 Gamma(c+1+y)Gamma(c+1-y)/Gamma(c+1)^2
 =prod_(h>=0)(1-y^2/(c+1+h)^2)^-1.
It is E exp(T y^2), where T=sum independent exponentials with rates
(c+1+h)^2. Axial damping shifts T to T-delta/N; T has support down to zero.
This is a useful signed-variance representation, not a positive Gaussian
mixture after the shift. Continuous Gaussian deconvolution is not justified.
