1. THREE CONSEQUENTIAL TARGETS AND THEIR ORIGINAL GATES

Foundational obstruction — arbitrary rational linear programming.
Target: solve feasibility and bounded optimization in poly(m,n) arithmetic
operations/comparisons, independently of coefficient magnitudes, with
polynomial intermediate encoding length. The missing operation is acquisition
of a globally feasible improving multi-resource block with a permanent
polynomial progress certificate. Solving an already selected block is weaker.
Current primary baseline: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11820, oracle LP and
primal-dual proximity; general strong polynomiality remains open. Diagonal
scaling/circuit augmentation retain logarithmic condition dependencies
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.06252, https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07913).
Strongly polynomial generalized-flow methods exploit a restricted sparse
structure, not arbitrary LP. Decisive experiments retained arbitrary rational
coefficients, exact arithmetic and the global feasibility/objective gate.

Scientific capability — deterministic point-Coulomb collisional relaxation.
Target: for iid homogeneous smooth stable non-Maxwellian data in a neutral
3D weakly coupled plasma, derive the nonlinear collisional law on physical
time N/log N, including singular encounters and collective response acquired
from the dynamics. The missing operation is an energy/impulse-weighted
correlation state that controls microscopic pairs and nonlinear causal
histories for that entire time. It must be initialized from iid data.
Primary baseline https://arxiv.org/html/2408.03597v2 is a linearized
equilibrium-covariance result with truncated interactions and explicitly
avoids dynamic screening. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10778 treats a modified
hierarchy with inserted diffusion and simplified transport/response; these
are not premises for the point-Coulomb target. The long-time hard-sphere
Boltzmann result https://annals.math.princeton.edu/articles/22284 was
recognized as established and was not retained as an open target.

Unconstrained frontier — long-time local quantum observables.
Target: from explicit bounded-degree local H and product input alone,
compute local expectations to additive epsilon at polynomial times, with
total acquisition, memory and sampling cost polynomial in N,T,1/epsilon.
The missing operation is acquired cancellation/observable closure that
preserves arbitrarily delayed returns, without a supplied low-rank promise.
The unrestricted worst-case target would imply BQP=BPP; this barrier is
retained, not bypassed. Strong matched baselines include connected dynamical
clusters for short times, free-fermion covariance closure, Krylov memory,
influence functionals and operator backflow. Primary sources and their exact
interfaces: quantum_influence/BASELINES.md, especially
https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.11490 and https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.0886.

oracle closure. Scalar energy alone cannot bound force or grazing covariance.
