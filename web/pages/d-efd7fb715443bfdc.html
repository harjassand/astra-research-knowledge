## 2. Commuting-operator threshold repetition

For any finite two-player game with commuting value v<1, answer-product d and minimum component resampling gap sigma, the internally reconstructed theorem gives

    Pr[W_k >= ceil((v+delta)k)]
      <= exp[-sigma delta³ k/(2048(1+log d))]

for all k>=1 and0<delta<1-v. It permits arbitrary Hilbert spaces and commuting von Neumann algebras. The proof transports measurements inside separate scalar-ancilla amplifications of the original commuting algebras, handles continuous spectrum at zero by bounded forms/strong limits, and combines mixed exposure with spectral barycenters. It never identifies commuting and finite tensor strategies.

This extends release277's finite-dimensional machinery and replaces its minimum-atom path loss by an explicit spectral gap. Lin's verified prior theorem concerns anchored all-wins repetition with gap power17. Our bound gives fixed-law thresholds and, after half-erasure anchoring (sigma>=1/4), a cubic anchored bound. This is a potentially useful proof-system amplification primitive. Constant Hamiltonian locality, sparse positive enforcement and quantum PCP do not follow.
