GAMES / HAMILTONIANS — CYCLE 3: COMPLEMENT-LCU ROW GATE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: exact compression proved; canonical row lower proved for every PREPARE completion; general sparse-enforcer gate remains UNKNOWN.

DECISION

The complement-LCU construction gives one polynomial-circuit isometry V and
one diagonal one-sparse test B for the full noncommuting positive list, with

    H <= V* B V = 2H-H^2 <= 2H.

Its canonical image enforcer C0=I-VV* has exact kernel and gap one, and any
specified matrix entry can be evaluated from the term generator in polynomial
time. However, for the legal BMVZ-admissible hard family augmented by two
forced BJSW singleton seed layers, every PREPARE completion has a canonical
physical row with at least ceil(5^R/D) nonzeros. Here R is the BMVZ repetition
count and D is the polynomial LCU label dimension, so the row output is
superpolynomial. This is a lower bound for C0, not for all positive enforcers
of the same image.

The exact statement and derivation are in `complement_lcu/03_decisive_counterexample.md`.
The full entry formula is in `complement_lcu/00_INTERFACE_AND_TENSOR_FORMULA.md`.
The fixed-completion 5^R calculation and exact checker are in
`complement_lcu/01_canonical_C.md` and `complement_lcu/check_seed_block_rows.py`.

THEOREM CANDIDATE: COMPLETION-INDEPENDENT CANONICAL ROW LOWER

Take the fresh five-qubit seed states

    phi=(|00010>-|10010>)/sqrt(2),
    psi=(|00010>-|01110>-|10110>-|11010>)/2.

Append their rank-one tests as separate positive-weight singleton layers to a
BMVZ-admissible hard starting instance. The singleton rule emits the actual
repeated clauses h_v=I-Q_v with Q_v=(I-|v><v|)^{tensor R}; both seed weights
are positive. For z=00010 and y classified by counts a,b,c in respectively
{z},{10010},{01110,10110,11010}, the exact row of the relative reflections is

    <z^R|R_phi R_psi|y>
      =4(3/8)^a(1/2)^b(1/8)^c
       -2*1[c=0]*(1/2)^(a+b)
       -2*1[b=0]*(3/4)^a(1/4)^c
       +1[b=c=0].

For R>=2 this is nonzero at every one of the 5^R strings in the local
alphabet and zero outside it. The all-z entry is -1/16 at R=2 and positive
for R>=3; the two one-sided cases reduce to impossible rational equalities
2(3/4)^a=1 and 2^(1-R)=1. Thus the selected relative-reflection row has
exactly 5^R nonzeros. The exact rational fixture checked R=1,...,6; the
symbolic cases cover all R>=2.

Now let A be any unitary PREPARE completion, chi=A|0>, and let C0=I-VV* in
the physical output-label basis. Its entries are

    C0_(a,x),(b,y)=delta_ab delta_xy
       -sum_(p,q) conjugate(A_pa) chi_p A_qb conjugate(chi_q)
              (R_p R_q*)_(x,y).

For distinct term labels j,k, contract the actually combined entries against
the rows of A:

    T_jk(x,y)=sum_(a,b) A_ja conjugate(A_kb) C0_(a,x),(b,y)
            =-chi_j conjugate(chi_k) (R_j R_k*)_(x,y).

The identity term vanishes by row orthogonality, and unitarity isolates the
single (j,k) term. Taking j=phi,k=psi shows that for each of 5^R distinct y,
some one of the D physical source-label rows (a,x), x=z^R, has a nonzero
entry in one of the output label columns. If s is maximum physical row
support, these D rows cover at most Ds such output strings. Therefore

    s >= ceil(5^R/D).

This argument includes all other BMVZ and dummy terms and survives arbitrary
cancellations in the combined C0 entries. BMVZ uses polynomial D and
R=n^Omega(1) for inverse-polynomial input gap, so this fixed C0 cannot have a
polynomial-output row-list algorithm. The family is a hardness-preserving
legal augmentation, not a proved member of one named fixed ABN-to-BMVZ map.

FULL COST AND INTERFACE LEDGER

* Logical target: a normalized positive sum H=sum_j pi_j h_j of the full
  generated clauses (plus harmless positive-weight zero padding when needed).
* Isometry: prepare chi=(1/sqrt(2), sqrt(pi_1/2),...,sqrt(pi_M/2)); SELECT
  the identity and reflections R_j=I-2h_j; conjugate SELECT by A. V is the
  first-column isometry of this polynomial circuit.
* Test: B=(I-|0><0|)_label tensor I_data. It is diagonal, one-sparse per row,
  and O(log M)-local. The all-state order constants are alpha=1,beta=2.
* Enforcer: C0=UBU*=I-VV* is a positive projection with exact kernel and
  normalized gap one; its circuit is polynomial, but its row support lower
  bound above rules out polynomial row output on the augmented family.
* Entries: equation (1) of `complement_lcu/00_INTERFACE_AND_TENSOR_FORMULA.md`
  factors each pair of amplified terms into O(R) fixed-size calculations.
  Entry evaluation is polynomial in M,R, requested precision and local input
  bit length. This is not row enumeration.
* Locality: the explicit C0 sum touches up to O(R) data qubits per summand;
  no constant-locality claim is made. B's small label locality does not
  transfer to C0.
* Amplification parameters: BMVZ has constant t and logarithmic iteration
  count ell; R=(2t)^ell is polynomially large as a positive power of the
  source size, whereas 5^R/D is superpolynomial.
* Finite precision: algebraic rotations give exact statements in the stated
  algebraic gate model. Fixed-gate compilation is approximate; exact kernel
  preservation and its error budget are not inferred from approximate gates.

ALTERNATIVE IMAGES AND ENFORCERS TESTED

1. The direct term-frame Naimark isometry gives V*BV=H exactly. The active
   flag variant also gives exact H with a one-bit test. For the seed pair,
   both fixed images have a row-mass witness with m=1/16 and
   a=(1/16)8^(-R); the polynomial-row Chebyshev lemma forces their normalized
   positive-enforcer gap to vanish. These are scoped fixed-image arbitrary-C
   no-go theorems, not no-go results for complement LCU.
2. For complement LCU, the active-flag witness does not transfer: the
   nonzero-label branch blocks are combinations of h_j h_k, not the relative
   decoder unitaries from the term-frame/flag construction. For the fixed
   BJSW local alphabet, rows of VV* are nevertheless approximable in l2 to
   error epsilon using polynomial(M,1/epsilon) entries. Thus the earlier
   constant-mass Chebyshev witness cannot exclude every polynomial degree.
3. A new exact nonunitary incidence enforcer exists algebraically. Write
   Y=I-H=K_0, which is invertible when the positive zero padding guarantees
   H<=3I/4. Let Tz=(z_a-K_aY^{-1}z_0)_(a>0) and C_inc=T*T/16. Then
   ker C_inc=ran(V), 0<=C_inc<=I, and C_inc >= (1/16)(I-VV*). This closes
   the gap algebraically, but its entries/rows involve Y^-1; no polynomial
   row-list/access bound is established. It isolates the unresolved issue as
   explicit row access, not the existence of a constant-gap positive form.
4. Constant-gap unitary-frame expander connection enforcers are blocked by
   the same seed transition. A path between the phi and psi reflection frames
   telescopes to R_phi R_psi; if the clock has K=poly(n) vertices and gap
   bounded below, path length O(log K) forces an edge row support
   exp(Omega(R/log K)), even allowing global unitary intermediate frames.
   This covers only that connection-graph subclass.
5. Scalar polynomial/rational filters of C0 cannot change its support,
   because C0 is a projection and every exact-kernel-preserving such filter
   is just a nonzero scalar multiple. The standard Schur parent cannot turn
   an inverse-polynomial normalized gap into a constant one while remaining
   a contraction. Detectability/gap-amplification sources do not provide
   the target isometry-image interface.
6. A finite-field Pauli construction gives an alternative constant-gap
   polynomial-row enforcer for any Clifford image, so no universal obstacle
   follows from having a circuit isometry or active workspace. A selected
   LCU completion is not a stabilizer code, but that only excludes this
   stabilizer route for that completion. Separate binary-diagonal spectral
   obstructions apply to a dummy fine-spectrum augmentation, not to a
   general real-valued B or a comparison-only sandwich.

WHAT IS NOT CLOSED

There is still no positive contraction C with all three properties proved
for the complement-LCU image: exact kernel ran(V), a size-independent
all-state gap under ||C||<=1, and polynomial maximum row support with an
efficient row-list oracle. There is also no impossibility theorem covering
every such C. The sparse enforcer could use nonunitary/global relations
different from C0, a connection Laplacian, a history graph, or a stabilizer
ansatz. A minimum-distance obstruction fails because the LCU image has a
nonzero kernel relation supported on at most 2D physical entries. The exact
incidence construction provides one nonlocal candidate whose Y^-1 row cost
is the nearest concrete gate.

The primary-source audit in `complement_lcu/12_primary_routes.md` finds no
off-the-shelf theorem providing the missing row oracle: sparse block-encoding
results assume row oracles; local Hamiltonian sparsifiers require a fixed-
local PSD term list; qPCP is a constant promise-gap reduction and not an
exact image-kernel/all-state compression theorem; detectability amplification
does not itself produce this interface.

CONSEQUENTIALITY / RESTART

If a polynomial-row constant-gap C is found for the full-list LCU image, the
hardness-preserving BMVZ reduction can be converted to a sparse-matrix
positive Hamiltonian by adding a small B term and normalizing (see
`complement_lcu/11_full_family_scope.md`). This would address a sparse-QMA /
quantum-PCP-style gate only after the family-membership and exact row-oracle
details are discharged. The current result does not prove qPCP, a new
complexity-class identity, or a finite-dimensional realization of a
commuting-operator verifier.

Next attack: resolve whether the exact incidence enforcer has a polynomial
row enumerator, or prove a lower bound for every exact positive sparse C for
this LCU image using its full frame dependency geometry. Any such lower bound
must accommodate the l2-approximate sparsity of VV* rows and the explicit
O(D)-support kernel relation; repeating the active-flag row-mass argument is
not sufficient. Independently, keep the L02 commuting-operator repetition
proof as a separate branch; it is recorded in Cycle 2 and must not be used to
claim a finite tensor verifier or a Hamiltonian encoding.
