THEOREM

Fix d>=2, beta>0, epsilon>0 and finite public M,B. Put m=d^2-1 and choose
Tr(F_a F_b)=delta_ab, Tr F_a=0. Let T_a=sum_i F_a^(i), Q=sum_a T_a^2,
Q_A=sum_ab A_ab {T_a,T_b}/2. Here A is a KNOWN real symmetric matrix with
||A||_op<=M. To avoid index ambiguity, the local field is the matrix h=h*,
Tr h=0, ||h||_HS<=B, and its collective sum is T_h=sum_i h^(i).

For the WHOLE known-parameter Gibbs density

 rho_N=exp[beta Q/N+Q_A/N^(1+epsilon)+T_h]/Z_N,        (F1)

an explicitly defined positive identical-product mixture sigma_N satisfies

 T(rho_N,sigma_N)<=C exp(-cN)                         (F2)

uniformly over admitted A,h for all sufficiently large N. Dimension d,
temperature beta, anisotropy exponent epsilon and bounds M,B are fixed.
This includes a fixed bounded O(1) local field; it does not require that
the field vanish per site. The anisotropy coefficient is smaller than the
isotropic mean-field coefficient by N^-epsilon. This scale condition is
essential to this proof. It is not an arbitrary anisotropic quantum
mean-field theorem.

1. ACQUIRE THE ISOTROPIC POSITIVE LAW WITH A STRONGER BAD-MASS BUDGET

Use the global physical-state SDE (G7) and the exact log determinant
(G8) in GLOBAL_CASIMIR_AND_FULL_GIBBS.txt. Those statements were derived from
the full-basis product-kernel identity and KMS factorization, not imported
as a heat-kernel oracle. For q0 N<=beta, the unweighted local seed terminal
tail is bounded by exp[-s^2 N/(4beta d^2)], while its weight is at most
exp(beta m N) and its normalization is at least one.

Choose public constants

 Ldet=beta(d^2+2d),
 s=2d sqrt[beta(beta m+B+1)],
 mu0=d^-d exp[-Ldet-s],

Thus the normalized isotropic seed law puts bad mass

 p_bad<=exp[-(B+1)N]                                (F3)

on lambda_min(eta)<mu0. Replace those branches by I/d. The new normalized
product seed rho_0' differs from the exact seed rho_0 by at most 2p_bad in
trace norm; every new starting eta has eta>=mu0 I. Increasing the log-det
cutoff charges the additional exp(BN) quantum/field norm below.

 D_C>=nu u^2 I-8M I>=(nu u^2/2)I.                   (F5)

3. FOLLOW THE KNOWN LOCAL-FIELD FLOW INSTEAD OF ASSUMING SMALL FIELD DRIFT

For each retained starting eta, the EXACT deterministic normalized local
filter flow is

 chi_t=exp(th/2) eta exp(th/2)/Tr[exp(th)eta],
 chi'_t=v_h(chi_t)/2,
 v_h(rho)={h,rho}-2Tr(h rho)rho.

Since ||h||_op<=B and eta>=mu0 I,
 lambda_min(chi_t)>=mu0 exp(-2Bt)>=u,   0<=t<=1.       (F6)

For density matrices, the HS derivative bound is ||Dv_h||<=6B, so v_h/2
has Lipschitz constant at most 3B on their convex hull. No spectral
eigenvector choice or unknown reference motion is needed.

The perturbation diffusion is the generic product-state SDE with
drift a_quad+v_h/2 and covariance qD_C/2. Stop at its first local eigenvalue
u/2 and freeze state/positive weight then. From the generic exact formula,

 ||a_quad||_HS<=(4N+7)q Kstar<=11Kstar N^-epsilon,
 D_C<=16Kstar I.                                    (F7)

Up to the stopping time, for the martingale component M_t,
 rho_t-chi_t=int_0^t a_quad(rho_s)ds
             +int_0^t [v_h(rho_s)-v_h(chi_s)]/2 ds+M_t.

Taking suprema and ordinary integral Gronwall yields, for t<=tau wedge1,

 sup ||rho_t-chi_t||_HS
 <=exp(3B)[11Kstar N^-epsilon+sup ||M_t||_HS].         (F8)

An exit requires ||rho_tau-chi_tau||_HS>=u/2 by (F6) and eigenvalue
Lipschitz continuity. If

 11Kstar N^-epsilon<=u exp(-3B)/4,                    (F9)

the stopped martingale must reach HS norm at least u exp(-3B)/4. Each
of its m coordinates has quadratic variation at most 8qKstar, so

 P_exit<=2m exp[-kappa N^(1+epsilon)],
 kappa=u^2 exp(-6B)/(256mKstar)>0.                   (F10)

The martingale is stopped for the variance estimate; (F8) is needed only
before the exit. Continuing the deterministic chi_t after the exit is not
used. This avoids a hidden assumption that the field itself is small.

4. EXACT STOPPED RESIDUAL AND EXTENSIVE FIELD WEIGHT

The potential is Tr[H rho^tensor N], and

 ||H||_op<=Hbound_N:=Kstar N^(1-epsilon)+BN.           (F11)

The field is extensive; its exp(BN) norm is NOT discarded. Matrix Ito and

 <=2 exp[Kstar N^(1-epsilon)-N]

For every fixed epsilon>0 the first exponent is -N+o(N), and the second is

strict exponential lower distance. The theorem is an upper construction only.
