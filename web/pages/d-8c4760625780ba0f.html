# A bounded-bit implementation of the exact legal soft projector

7 October 2026, `rounding_spectral`. This is an elementary numerical
construction derived from Li's proved Newton--Schulz filter, composed with
the exact legal projector. It is a theorem-level algorithm and error proof;
no high-L trajectory, practical benchmark, or novelty claim is supplied.

## Why a further implementation construction is useful

Exact Newton--Schulz rational iterates have denominator degree up to 3^J.
For J=O(log(nK)) this is formally polynomial, yet its magnitude can defeat
a local implementation long before the update count matters. The entire
trajectory theorem must still charge that bit cost. The construction below
instead stores every iterate on a fixed dyadic grid, while restoring exact
legality, PSD contraction, and soft-projector leakage at the end. Rounding
does not need to preserve commutation with the Hessian at each iteration.

## Input and exact reference filter

Use the symmetric rational S and exact rational orthogonal projector P
from `LEGAL_FLAT_PROJECTOR_GATE.md`. They commute; S is positive on
J-perp and has at least r strongly negative eigenvalues in J. Let
`||S||<=M`, with known rational M, eta>0 and 1<=r<=N. Define

`theta=eta r/(16N)`,

`A_f=S+5eta I/4`, `D_f=A_f^2+theta^2 I`,

`Y0=I/(M+2eta)`.

The exact reference iteration is

`Y_(j+1)=Y_j(3I-D_f Y_j^2)/2`.

Choose J by Li's rational lower-bound/squaring procedure so that the exact
filter `Q=(I-A_f Y_J)/2` has norm error to the ideal scalar filter at
most half of

`eps_NS=eta r/[2^16 N(M+2eta)]`.

The source proof gives 0<=Q<=I, Q eigenvalue>7/8 on the r strongly
negative directions, and the sharper explicit leakage

`Q(S+eta I)Q <=3eta r/(2^17 N) I`.                  (1)

The coefficient is the sum of ideal leakage 1/2^17 and numerical-filter
error 1/2^16; we retain this slack rather than replacing it immediately
by the looser 1/4096 soft-projector allowance. All exact Y_j have norm
at most 1/theta.

## Rounded iteration and complete error recurrence

Set

`eps_Q=eta r/[2^20 N(M+2eta)]`,

`eps_Y=eps_Q/(M+2eta)`,

`A=2+6(M+2eta)^2/theta^2`.

Choose a fixed dyadic mesh

`0<delta<=eps_Y/(N A^J)`.

Starting with the exact rational Y0, compute

`Yhat_(j+1)=Round_delta(Sym[Yhat_j(3I-D_f Yhat_j^2)/2])`,

rounding each symmetric entry to nearest delta, with a fixed tie rule.
Sym means (X+X^T)/2. One rounding changes operator norm by at most
Ndelta/2. The exact iterates are symmetric, so symmetrization changes
no reference value.

If both inputs have norm at most 2/theta, the cubic iteration map is
A-Lipschitz in operator norm: telescope YD_fY^2 as three factors to
bound its difference by `3||D_f||(2/theta)^2` times input error, then
include its factor 1/2 and the linear factor 3/2. The displayed A is
larger than `3/2+6(M+2eta)^2/theta^2`. Symmetrization is a contraction.
Consequently, for E_j=||Yhat_j-Y_j||,

`E_(j+1)<=A E_j+Ndelta/2`, `E0=0`.

Induction gives `E_j<=Ndelta A^j<=eps_Y` for all j<=J. The norm
premise closes because eps_Y<=1/theta and ||Y_j||<=1/theta.
No machine floating-point or empirical convergence claim enters this
error certificate. A smaller dyadic mesh only improves the proof.

## Exact legality and soft-projector repair

Form the rational symmetric raw filter

`Qraw=I/2-(A_f Yhat_J+Yhat_J A_f)/4`.

Then `||Qraw-Q||<=eps_Q/2`. Let Q_J=PQP and define

`Qhat=[P Qraw P+eps_Q P]/(1+2eps_Q)`.

Qhat is exactly symmetric, maps into J, and commutes exactly with P.
On J, P Qraw P is between Q_J-eps_Q P/2 and
Q_J+eps_Q P/2. Since 0<=Q_J<=P, addition and division give

`0<=Qhat<=P<=I`.

The repair perturbs Q_J by less than 4eps_Q in norm. Exact commutation
with S is no longer needed: the output soft-projector conditions follow
by the certified norm comparison.

On the r-dimensional strongly negative spectral subspace, Q_J has
quadratic form at least 7/8. Min--max gives at least r eigenvalues of
Qhat greater than `7/8-4eps_Q>=111/128`; the final inequality follows
from eps_Q<=2^-21. Thus

`tr(Qhat^2)>r(111/128)^2>3r/4`.

Compress (1) by P and telescope the two quadratic products using both
contractions. The additional leakage is at most

`2(M+eta)||Qhat-Q_J||<=8(M+eta)eps_Q<=eta r/(2^17 N)`.

Total leakage is therefore at most `4eta r/(2^17 N)`, strictly below
the required `eta r/(4096N)`. Qhat is an exactly legal soft projector.
The rational conditional-moment/normalization proof applies without any
constraint residual, with the same flatness and curvature margins.

## Persistent and temporary encodings

Here M, theta^-1 and eta^-1 are bounded by polynomials in the rounding
parameters. Thus log A=O(log(nK)) and J=O(log(nK)). The dyadic mesh
requires

`log2(delta^-1)=O(J log A+log(eps_Y^-1)+log N)`

`=O(log^2(nK))`,

in addition to bits of the original rational data defining S, eta and P.
Every persistent rounded Yhat entry has this grid and norm at most
2/theta. A temporary iteration has one fixed-degree rational expansion
in D_f and that bounded Yhat, then is compressed again. The denominator
does not iterate by degree 3^J. The final projector multiplications add
only a fixed number of original-input bounded rational operations.
Conditional moments have degree O(log(nK)), so the final legal direction
still has polynomial bits and the global face reset can regenerate them.

This can replace the exact polynomial filter in the charged-quartic
construction without changing any direction, local progress, epoch, reset,
or output guarantees. It improves the explicit bit implementation of a
local primitive, while all dense matrix, exact geometry, row acquisition
and conditional-moment costs remain charged. It supplies neither Li's
sleeping-row complexity nor practical general-polytope runtime. Exact legal
filter rounding is standard numerical error propagation plus projector
compression; no independent priority claim is made.
