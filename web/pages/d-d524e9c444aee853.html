# d_prior: Controlled-source reusability needs coupled KL rate vectors

Status: rigorous negative result for naive pairwise-max criterion; classical gain prior art verified; exact controlled conversion novelty provisional and sufficiency unproved here.

Exact question/interface: can an adaptive known finite source menu exactly reproduce every finite-output stopping policy of a fixed target experiment under asymptotic statewise expected-sample budgets? Fixed-source Li-Liu v2 Theorem2 is relevant; NAV2013 testing asymptotics alone do not solve it.

Our construction: finite full-support Bernoulli source a=(.5,.01,.49), b=(.5,.49,.01), target G=(.5,.04,.041). All six maximized source pairwise KL rates strictly exceed target. Nonetheless stopped KL chain rule and data processing force E_0 N >=1.152920527679029 m to reproduce m full target samples. Thus pairwise-max dominance cannot characterize source-menu reusability, even allowing arbitrary adaptive policies. The necessary object is each state's downward convex hull of attainable directed rate vectors.

Prior-art verdict: Chernoff report42 June24,1958 (primary recovered) already gives adaptive finite-menu asymptotic optimality. NAV arXiv1205.0858v6 Theorem3 p6 gives full multihypothesis first-order rate and converse. Strict known-menu adaptivity benefits are not a discovery. Li-Liu arXiv2608.19897v2 Theorem1 p8/Theorem2 p9 instead provide fixed-source all-output exact conversion with policy-uniform sqrt remainder and explicitly exclude adaptive source choice. A controlled-source extension may be a substantive next theorem; no verified novelty claim yet because exact sequential-comparison antecedents lack accessible full reading.
