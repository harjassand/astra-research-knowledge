No clipping at normalized mass1 is performed, correctly allowing an approximate anchor to lie below the true mode.
The anchor search either certifies a usual ratio sign bracket or stops only when the whole log-ratio interval lies in[-delta,delta], delta=1/[4(h+1)]. Parity log-concavity then bounds any increase moving away from that anchor by exp(h delta)<2. Thus twice each dyadic block's nearest-point upper mass dominates the block. This avoids any hidden exact comparison of transcendental modal ratios. Adjacent ratios whose sign is extremely hard to decide are resolved by the explicit near-tie condition.
Predecessor-block charging bounds the ideal inflated envelope by12G; the conservative constant is safe. Integer upward rounding adds at most4N/Q. HISTORICAL OPTIONAL DIAGNOSTIC ISSUE — RESOLVED BY FINAL ADDENDUM

Default dispatch uses the finite sum and succeeds; DisplacedHeraldGaussian never forces this branch.
