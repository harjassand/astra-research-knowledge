RESULT

An exact two-mode adaptive instrument distinguishes an erased-record sequential acceptance event from a single same-register hard projection and from one positive Gaussian Kraus branch.

In Fock basis (|00>,|10>,|01>,|11>), let P=diag(1,0,1,0), the projector that mode 1 is empty. Let U fix vacuum/full occupancy and rotate the one-particle space by [[3/5,-4/5],[4/5,3/5]], and put Q=UPU*. Measure P versus I-P. If I-P occurs, accept immediately; if P occurs, continue by measuring Q and accept only Q. The accepted Kraus operators are K0=QP and K1=I-P, so the accepted-event effect is

  E=K0* K0+K1* K1=PQP+I-P=diag(1,1,9/25,1).

The event probability is tr(E rho), and for I/4 it is 21/25. E is a positive contraction but not a projection or scalar multiple of one: its eigenvalues include 1 and 9/25. Thus no single terminal orthogonal projection, even conjugated by arbitrary unitaries and rescaled, gives this same acceptance probability for every same-register input.

There is a second exact obstruction. Normalizing E by its trace gives a diagonal positive operator with <n1>=25/42, <n2>=17/42, <n1 n2>=25/84; all cross normal and anomalous contractions vanish. Wick's rule for a Gaussian state would require <n1 n2>=<n1><n2>=425/1764, whereas the actual value is 525/1764, a gap of 25/441. Therefore E cannot be G*G for one positive fermionic Gaussian Kraus branch G, since a normalized positive Gaussian operator would be a Gaussian state. Each accepted branch is Gaussian; their outcome-dependent coarse-grained sum is not one Gaussian Kraus branch.

The branch-map calculation independently gives K0* K0=diag(1,0,9/25,0), with nonzero singular values 1 and 3/5. This cannot equal alpha W R V for any unitaries W,V, orthogonal projection R and scalar alpha, because such a map has flat nonzero singular spectrum. For two projectors, the relevant eigenvalues are squared cosines of principal angles between their ranges; here those cosines are 1 and 3/5. This precisely sharpens the simple projector-order counterexample already present in c03_s01's frozen INITIAL.
