# Same-code control and physical acquisition boundary

7 October 2026. rounding_spectral. This retains a prior obstruction and
cost boundary for the new memory filling lead. The control is not a
counterexample to Balasubramanian--Davydova--Lin's construction.

## Prior same-ground-space control, independently reconstructed

Bravyi--Hastings--Michalakis, arXiv1001.0344v1 §2.4, replaces toric-code
plaquette checks B_p by adjacent products B_p B_q and one pinned check
B_p*. It retains star checks A_s. Use an even number N_p of plaquettes
on a square torus and projector normalization. Since the dual adjacency
graph is connected, the coefficient vectors of adjacent products span
the even-parity subspace. Adding one odd singleton generates every
plaquette coefficient vector, modulo the toric relation product B_p=I.
The global stabilizer group and ground code are therefore unchanged.
The code distance and global indistinguishability alone cannot detect
this change in the local generator presentation.

The unpinned product group cannot generate a single B_p. Its coefficient
parity is even, and the only relation between plaquettes is the all-ones
vector, which has even parity when N_p is even. Adding local star checks
cannot change this: a pure Z target requires the X component of their
product to vanish. Thus in any buffer omitting the pinned check, a global
plaquette stabilizer remains unavailable from local generators. The
stabilizer reduced-kernel criterion gives a TQO-2 failure.

The all-minus plaquette syndrome is consistent because N_p is even.
It violates just the pinned projector and has unperturbed energy1;
all-plus has energy0. Add the geometrically local perturbation
V=h sum_p B_p. The two sector energies become

    E_plus=h N_p,   E_minus=1-h N_p.

They cross at h=1/(2N_p), a local strength tending to zero. A nonuniform
plaquette assignment cuts at least4 edges of the square-torus dual graph,
so at the crossing its energy is at least4-1/2, above both1/2 sectors.
For completeness, a periodic row with both signs has at least2 horizontal
cuts. Two such rows give4. If only one row is nonconstant, its two vertical
interfaces together add at least2. If every row is constant but the array
is nonuniform, two vertical interfaces contribute at least2L>=4. Star
violations add nonnegative energy. Hence the crossing is a true ground
gap closure. This is prior, not a new instability discovery.

The recoverable script check_admission_and_prior_control.py independently
checks an L=8,128-qubit instance: original Z rank63, adjacent-product
rank62, pinned-product rank63; the same signed global space; missing
single plaquette; and exact normalized crossing h=1/128. The universal
argument above supplies the conclusion; this finite check is only a
diagnostic. A mere omitted redundant plaquette is a different control
and has not been conflated with this source's gap-closing construction.

## Costs and distinct physical claims

The May2026 paper's main memory theorem uses thermal encoding and a
particular class of thermal dynamics. It does not supply efficient passive
ground-space preparation, an isometric-ground-encoding lifetime theorem,
or perturbed-Davies lifetime preservation. Ground-band/gap stability
under small Hamiltonian perturbations would itself be a separate result.

Our actual anchor derivation is an analytic support proof on the source's
templates, with a source-pictured X--Z auxiliary diagonal. It constructs
linear maps/fillings by finite binary algebra and proves bounds; it does
not execute the enormous matrices, acquire physical couplings or simulate
a device. Local block basis dimension and sparse initial matrix nonzeros
are O(ell^2) after bounded degrees. Naive dense Gaussian contraction uses
O(ell^4) bits of storage and O(ell^6) binary operations. Those costs are
not a practical sparse-access claim and remain visible at fixed depth.

The source displays lambda approximately5.4e24 and density6.6e10. We have
not certified that literal calibration after paying auxiliary diagonal
length2. Our separately admitted variant in GEOMETRIC_READMISSION.md
uses lambda2^95, ell79267776595521469762340722311169 and
density2013265920000. Thus even one O(ell^2) block has a scale around
10^64, and naive elimination has a scale around10^191 operations.
The geometric cap/LLL admission is a mathematical existence proof with
exact parameter inequalities, not an executed embedding, an acquired
continuous event oracle, or a finite-bit Moser--Tardos implementation.
Three cap centers are rational coordinate axes and their labels are the
existing X/Q/Z tags; independent triangle coloring and local candidate
event enumeration still have actual acquisition/work costs.

An ambient local perturbation can connect qubits that are physically
close and intrinsically far in the Tanner graph. The current bounded
geometry does not silently convert it into a Tanner-local perturbation.
Root's proposed enlarged-graph/identity-support/ancilla bridge needs its
own theorem proof and overhead account. Decoupled grid ancillas would
cost their bounding-volume size and do not come free with an embedding.

Physical parent positions obey radius_i<=lambda radius_(i-1)+alpha lambda,
so a recursively constructed finite instance occupies a bounding region
of radius O(lambda^i). The code grows exponentially in i at a constant
rate depending on ell. Grid volume is consequently polynomial in that
code size. A simpler bound avoids any unverified growth exponent: the
actual fine Tanner graph is connected and unit range. Every check has
a qubit neighbor, and each qubit has total degree<=7, so n qubits imply
at most7n checks and8n total vertices. Its physical diameter is<=8n.
An integer-grid padding box therefore has O(n^3) volume, with density
at most4t original vertices per unit cell because each vertex belongs
to a Tanner face and a unit cube fits a unit ball. Both connectivity
and nonisolation follow from the actual seed/template/gluing induction.
This accounts for a potential padding overhead; it does not perform
coordinate acquisition, prove a finite-bit resampling implementation,
or establish the separate perturbative theorem on the padded graph.

Eq5 of the May paper writes a first-time lifetime condition with trace
distance<=1/10; at time0 that expression would be0. It is evidently
in tension with its intended lifetime use. This sign issue is retained
as a likely definition typo, and is not treated as a refutation of the
substantive Theorem1.1 trace-norm bound.
