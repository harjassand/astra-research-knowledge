Let b=0,...,floor(N/2), j_b=N/2-b, multiplicity d_b=binom(N,b)-binom(N,b-1),
and physical sector dimension D_b=(2j_b+1)d_b. Any state invariant under both
SU(2) and permutations has the form G(q)=sum_b (q_b/D_b)P_b, q a probability
vector. Its axially filtered state is Phi_F(G)=FGF/Tr(FGF), with
F^2=exp[-delta Jz^2/N+hJz]. Define positive scalars

 z_b=sum_(m=-j_b)^j_b exp[-delta*m^2/N+h*m], c_b=z_b/(2j_b+1).

Then the filtered sector probability is Q_b(q)=q_b*c_b/sum_a q_a*c_a.
Conditional on sector b, the normalized state is the SAME for every q:
its eigenvalue at each (m,multiplicity-label) is
 exp[-delta*m^2/N+h*m]/(d_b*z_b).
The sector supports are orthogonal. Hence for ANY two positive probability
vectors q and s,

 T(Phi_F(G(q)),Phi_F(G(s)))= (1/2) sum_b |Q_b(q)-Q_b(s)|.    (1)

This is exact physical trace distance, with all multiplicities included.
It is not merely an upper bound using exp(N|h|+delta*N/4), and it does not
require constructing a 2^N-dimensional matrix. The identity follows by
diagonalizing the commuting projectors and summing absolute eigenvalues;
the within-sector conditional distribution sums to one.

Apply this to the explicit coherent-subset states K_k. Their rational sector
columns A_bk are supplied and independently checked in c01_s03's whole compiler:
 A_bk=(N-2b+1)[binom(N-k,b)-binom(N-k,b-k-1)]/[(k+1)2^(N-k)].
Set Z_k=sum_b A_bk*c_b and C_bk=A_bk*c_b/Z_k. Then C is a positive stochastic
matrix of the sector probabilities of the NORMALIZED filtered K_k.
The exact Gibbs target has q_b proportional to D_b*exp[alpha*j_b(j_b+1)/N].
Because G(q) belongs to conv{K_k} for alpha>=0, Q(q) belongs to conv{C_.k}.

A numerical LP may propose nonnegative simplex weights v fitting C v to Q(q).
