Farrell-Jones/K0 issue — independent theorem-check result
--------------------------------------------------------
The K-theoretic FJ theorem for hyperbolic groups applies to F2 coefficients:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0701434v2
It gives K0(F2[G])=K0(F2)=Z for torsion-free hyperbolic G. That equality alone does NOT force direct finiteness. From ab=1, ba!=1, P=eR with R=F2[G] is a nonzero projective right module, and
  R -> R⊕P, y -> (ay,ey),
  R⊕P -> R, (x,z) -> bx+z
are inverses. Thus R≅R⊕P and [P]=0. This is perfectly consistent at the Grothendieck-group level with K0(R)=Z; one needs an additional positivity/cancellation conclusion.

Exact BLR applications theorem:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0703548
https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/blr2.pdf
arXiv Theorem 0.12 / published Theorem 1.12 needs RG stably finite, OR characteristic-zero field, OR skew-field+sofic, in addition to the rational K0 isomorphism and coefficient-ring trivial idempotents. None is acquired for this proposed F2 hyperbolic group. Its proof explicitly uses stable finiteness to turn [P]=0 into P=0.

A primary generic counterexample to the bare K0+augmentation inference is the Toeplitz-Jacobson algebra T=F2<x,y|xy=1>, augmented by x,y->1. Iovanov–Sistko identifies the projective S=T(1-yx) and T≅T⊕S, giving K0(T)=Z while xy=1!=yx:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.00109
This is NOT a group-ring counterexample and is used only to locate the missing algebraic step.

Printed contrary evidence is real and cannot be erased: the introductory prime-characteristic bullet in BLR math/0701434v2 literally claims trivial idempotents for commutative prime-characteristic fields under its FJ conclusion and finite-subgroup condition. Torsion-free hyperbolic G and F2 fit that wording. The later Lueck survey also asserts a broad implication:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.11337
Its universal regular-coefficient implication is not resolved by the present audit. Thus do not state that FJ cannot imply the modular conclusion by any stronger route, or that the exact hyperbolic modular question is established open. The current scope is that the available K0/trace proof route does not acquire the claimed contradiction.
