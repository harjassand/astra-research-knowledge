## Fixed nonzero level: direct repairs become source-realizable

Start with the already verified normalized source
`f0=Lt²+a(b)t+c(b)R0`, L!=0. Take any t-independent polynomial
residual fibre automorphism Theta over C[b], and any polynomial H(b,R).
Keep b fixed but move the **old pivot** by

`oldt=tau+H(b,R)`, `oldR0=Theta(b,R)`.

This is a global polynomial source automorphism. Given oldt,oldR0, first
recover R=Theta^-1(b,oldR0), then tau=oldt-H(b,R). The source Jacobian
determinant remains the residual unit. Its polynomial critical section is

`tau*=(sigma-a)/(2L)-H(b,R)`.

For V=cTheta the reduced critical envelope is exactly

`psi_sigma=V-(a-sigma)²/(4L)+sigma H`.              (1)

For any fixed design level sigma0!=0 and any direct potential repair R,
choosing H=R/sigma0 realizes that repair through this legal chart. This
does not by itself prove constant determinant or collision. At the
original reduced witness points, their tau coordinates shift separately;
their old coordinates remain on the old critical graph. The full new
source gradients there are(sigma0,grad psi_sigma0). Hence an already
retained collision remains exactly when the repair gradients agree at
the two points. Requiring the repair gradient to vanish is sufficient
but stronger than necessary.

The prior residual-gradient ideal `(psi_rho,psi_eta)=(x,y)` applied when
the **old base and old pivot were both fixed**. It does not apply to
(1): at the origin the new derivatives may include sigma0 grad_R H.
The cycle14 degree-six inverse theorem retains its explicit origin-fibre
membership hypothesis and therefore is not silently extended to all
these charts. Base-moving charts are yet another interface.

## Full five-dimensional constancy is a separate pencil condition

Use Sigma as a variable critical level, with H fixed at its design
value. Apart from its base-constant term -Sigma²/(4L),

`psi_Sigma=W+Sigma Z`,
`W=V-a²/(4L)`, `Z=H+a/(2L)`.

Thus `Hess psi_Sigma=M0+Sigma M1`. The full source pivot derivative
is f_tautau=2L. At its polynomial critical graph, ordinary Schur
congruence gives

`det Hess_5(fprime)=2L det(M0+Sigma M1)`.           (2)

The transformation `(b,tau,R)->(b,Sigma,R)` is itself polynomially
invertible because Sigma=2L(tau+H)+a. Hence the full five-dimensional
Hessian determinant is constant if and only if the determinant pencil
on the right is the same constant at **all** levels. Verifying only
Sigma=sigma0 cannot establish that property. For a fixed residual
chart whose Sigma0 potential already has nonconstant determinant,
no pivot translation can repair the full-source condition: H drops
out at that level. The prescribed bad D2/T chart has exactly this
obstruction. No unconditional flag or relative-pencil classification
is inferred.

These statements explain the legal target-only repair route without
transferring the original Meng--Yang constant determinant through a
nonlinear source map. A candidate must calculate its actual target
Hessian determinant, and must separately gate the full source if that
inheritance is part of the intended construction.

## General nonzero-level origin normal form

Now permit an arbitrary base-fixed **actual polynomial A3 automorphism**
`(tau,R)->(T,Theta1,Theta2)` over C[b], not just a pivot translation.
Assume a global polynomial critical section tau=s(b,R) at level sigma!=0.
At b=0 the residual coefficients c vanish, so the critical identity is

`(2L T|section+a0) T_tau|section=sigma`.

Both factors are polynomials in C[R]; a nonzero constant product makes
both units. Since L!=0, `T|section=t0` is constant and
`T_tau|section=lambda!=0` is constant. Specializing an actual polynomial
automorphism and its inverse at b=0 preserves the automorphism.
Its coordinate polynomial T-t0 is irreducible. It vanishes on the
graph tau=s0(R), so it is divisible by the monic irreducible coordinate
polynomial tau-s0(R). Irreducibility makes the quotient a constant unit.
Therefore the complete origin restriction is

`T(0,tau,R)=t0+lambda[tau-s0(R)]`.                 (3)

In particular coefficients of higher tau powers must belong to(x,y).
This is necessary at the origin only, not a global affine-pivot claim.
The section is the entire inverse image of the old hyperplane T=t0.
Restricting the specialized A3 automorphism and its polynomial inverse
to these isomorphic hyperplanes proves that
`R -> Theta(0,s0(R),R)` is a global polynomial A2 automorphism.
Every polynomial s0 is possible in a triangular realization of(3),
with constants chosen to meet `(2Lt0+a0)lambda=sigma`.

The hypothesis sigma!=0 is essential. At zero level take
`T=tau²+rho`, `Theta1=tau`, `Theta2=eta`. This is a polynomial
automorphism with inverse tau=Theta1,rho=T-Theta1²,eta=Theta2.
The section tau=0 is critical for LT²+a0T at sigma=0, but
T|section=rho is not constant and T is quadratic in tau. This is
a direct scope counterexample, not a failure of the nonzero-level
theorem. L=0, nonpolynomial sections, or a map whose inverse was
merely conjectured lie outside the proof.

## A jointly polynomial all-level section is much stronger

Let P(tau,u)=partial_tau f. If one polynomial S(Sigma,u) jointly
in the level and remaining coordinates satisfies
`P(S(Sigma,u),u)=Sigma`, degree multiplication over the domain C[u]
gives `deg_tau(P)*deg_Sigma(S)=1`. Both degrees are one. Their
leading coefficients multiply to1, hence are constant units. Therefore
P is affine in tau with constant nonzero slope, and f has a constant
nonzero quadratic tau pivot. This is a necessary restriction on the
joint all-level polynomial-critical-inverse subinterface, not on a
single selected-level graph or on full Hessian constancy alone.

Even individually polynomial sections at every complex level do not
imply that joint hypothesis: P=tau² has a constant-in-u section for
each Sigma after choosing a square root, yet has no polynomial section
jointly in Sigma. The bridge proposed the joint-level lemma; its
degree/unit argument and this quantifier control were reconstructed here.
