## R1. An exact global product-versus-joint obstruction

This is an ARTIFICIAL decoupled-spin model, not a reachable BDL decoder
state or a failure of the actual memory. Its purpose is to reject a generic
claim that arbitrarily weak local couplings make joint and product thermal
resources globally close uniformly in N.

Take N independent copies of one spin and one oscillator,

    H_i=omega a_i* a_i-g c X_i(a_i+a_i*),
    omega,c,g>0,
    phi_i=c(a_i+a_i*),
    chi=2c^2/omega,
    v=c^2 coth(beta omega/2).

Complete the oscillator square. In the spin-X sector s=+/-1 its thermal
state is a displacement of the free oscillator Gibbs state by sgc/omega,
and the two spin sectors have the same partition factor. Hence the actual
joint Gibbs state is

    tau_i=(1/2)sum_(s=+/-1)|s_X><s_X| tensor
                          D(sgc/omega)rho_B D(sgc/omega)*.

The system marginal is EXACTLY I/2. The conventional product resource is
pi_i=I/2 tensor rho_B. Thus even the reduced system Gibbs state does not
reveal the resource difference.

Measure spin X and oscillator phi jointly, and output Y=s phi. Under tau_i
this is a normal variable of mean m=g chi and variance v; under pi_i it is
normal of mean0 and variance v. The N outcomes are independent in each
resource. Testing their average against m/2 has each error probability
Phi[-sqrt(N)m/(2sqrt(v))]<=exp[-Nm^2/(8v)]. Data processing therefore gives

    ||tau_1 tensor ... tensor tau_N-pi_1 tensor ... tensor pi_N||_1
                         >=2-4 exp[-N g^2 chi^2/(8v)].         (R1)

At EVERY fixed g>0 the trace distance tends to its maximum2. A generic
global initial-resource error estimate tending to0 as g->0 uniformly in N
is therefore false. The fixed-N weak-coupling order and the growing-family
order are different. This does not rule out a local initial-layer or logical
error bound for the actual code; it makes that bound a separate theorem
rather than an appeal to small g.

For completeness the per-copy relative entropy is beta g^2 chi/2, since
the displaced Gibbs oscillator has mean energy shift g^2 c^2/omega.
The total resource correlation/thermodynamic cost is extensive, while a
logical subsystem SWAP remains a code-only operation once the resource
has already been prepared. An efficient preparation of this interacting
resource is not supplied by the microscopic memory inequality.

## R2. What can be uniformly sent to an infinite reservoir

The candidate's bounds use only nonnegative periodic free covariances,
v=C(0) and chi=integral_0^beta C. For spectral density j(omega)>=0,

    C(s)=integral_0^infinity j(omega)
       cosh[omega(beta/2-s)]/sinh(beta omega/2) d omega,
    v=integral j(omega)coth(beta omega/2)d omega,
    chi=2integral j(omega)/omega d omega.                        (R2)

Finite atomic quadratures preserving these bounds can approximate C(s)
uniformly on the imaginary-time circle if its integrand admits ordinary
dominated quadrature. A Gaussian beta-KMS spectrum has finite v,chi.
Its corresponding REAL-time covariance also admits finite quadrature
on every prescribed compact time interval. These are covariance services,
not trace-class infinite-reservoir Gibbs states.

For fixed finite N,beta and time T, a Gaussian contour expansion on one
imaginary preparation branch and two real branches can in principle
construct reduced correlation limits. Its Wick absolute majorant has
the form exp[const N g^2 v (beta+2T)^2], in addition to bounded-spin factors.
Consequently an elementary covariance replacement bound would typically
pay that factor times the quadrature error. Such a proof supplies a
fixed-time LIMIT but can demand exponentially finer data and a much larger
mode inventory on the requested stretched-exponential storage interval.
No size/time-uniform inexpensive bath approximation follows from it.

An infinite interacting beta-KMS state should be formulated as a state
on the code-plus-reservoir observable algebra, with an actual dynamics,
rather than the nonexistent trace-class exp(-beta H_B)/Z_B on an arbitrary
continuum Fock space. Passing the logical memory estimate requires:

1. convergence/existence of the correlated equilibrium state and finite
   code observable dynamics;
2. a code-only logical SWAP/reset preparation represented on that state;
3. convergence of the decoded reference channel, or an equivalent algebraic
   proof, on the ENTIRE requested storage interval;
4. paid finite-bath/quadrature, control and resource preparation precision
   if an executable reservoir approximation is claimed.

Uniform finite-mode chi,v and the candidate's all-time inequality are
valuable: any independently acquired limit preserving these services will
inherit the same memory upper error at each t. However a continuum result
is not declared proved here merely because the scalar constants are uniform.
The next falsifier is an explicit correlated-contour limit/initialization
bound with charged errors; the original product-bath alternative needs a
different dynamical occupancy or initial-layer theorem.

Nearest prior mechanisms are displaced oscillator thermal states, Gaussian
influence functionals and the distinction between a reduced Gibbs state
and the Hamiltonian-of-mean-force resource. R1 is an exact elementary
interface counterexample; no novelty or actual-code no-go is claimed.
