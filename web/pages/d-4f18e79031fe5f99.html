# Adopted: one rational nested prefix family for every fixed smoothness

Root, 7 October 2026. **Internal mathematical PASS with named prior prerequisites**, independently checked by two workers. This is a constructive consequence of the filtered completion mechanism, not a claim that the underlying existence theorem or historical priority is ours.

The complete acquired interface starts from one rational sphere point and produces nested equal-weight sets X_k on S². For every fixed real r>1, a constant C_r bounds their H^r worst-case integration error by C_r |X_k|^(-r/2). Each finite prefix is acquired in deterministic polynomial bit time in its output size, with O(log²|X_k|) bits per new coordinate. Every old point stays exactly fixed. These are approximate rational rules at sparse prescribed cardinalities, not exact rational designs or optimal rules for every integer count.

Root reconstructed the main scale calculation. With K blocks, H=sum sqrt(block size), exact U=sum ceil sqrt(block size), endpoint S>=2 and L² comparable to U²K^(S-2), take m comparable to A K L², A=2^90. The acquired comparable-density rule supplies weighted error O_r(m^(-r/2)). The old filtered discrepancy is bounded by C_r H L^(1-r), using the actual ordered-pair kernel and the cap-count invariant, including its diagonal. Dividing by m gives the same rate because

    H L^(1-r) / m^(1-r/2) <= C_(r,A) K^((r-S)/2).

Both signs of r/2-1 require the two-sided m/L² bounds. The rational C6 filter and its explicitly normalized NPW localization bound make the constructed density positive with a certified margin. Thus density positivity and the correction initializer are acquired rather than supplied as uncharged oracles. The weighted cell labels yield occupancy without needing separation; increasing the total count preserves each old block's bound.

For the single all-smoothness family choose S_k=k+1. For a fixed r, the weighted and kernel constants depend on r, not the changing endpoint. Once S_k>=r the displayed factor is at most one; finitely many earlier prefixes can be absorbed in C_r. The chosen integer precision is O(k log N_k). Since the count grows by at least A at every stage, k=O(log N_k), giving O(log² N_k) coordinate heights. Integer powers K^K have output-bounded encoding and do not turn the polynomial acquisition theorem into a compressed-exponent claim. Prefix work is polynomial in the final output size and original encoding.

The scientific limitation is severe and explicit: the first universal completion has more than10^27 points, and successive count ratios grow like K^K. No universal acquisition was executed at those counts. Finite symmetry-based acquisition elsewhere in this cycle does not verify this universal theorem or automatically improve its constants.
