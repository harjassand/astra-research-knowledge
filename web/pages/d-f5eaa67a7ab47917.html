## Primary mathematical references

1. Masao Ishikawa and Masato Wakayama, “Minor Summation Formula of Pfaffians,” *Linear and Multilinear Algebra* 39 (1995), 285–305. The paper establishes a Pfaffian expression for weighted sums of minors. Publisher record: https://doi.org/10.1080/03081089508818403. The general full-sector sign identity used as context in this leaf is also recorded in the cycle-2 derivation; the new block-family proof is checked directly and does not depend on an unverified theorem statement from this source.
2. Kazuki Matoya and Taihei Oki, “Pfaffian Pairs and Parities: Counting on Linear Matroid Intersection and Parity Problems,” arXiv:1912.00620 (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.00620. This is background for the paired-column / linear-matroid-parity support interface, not a weighted partition-function algorithm.
3. Julius Borcea, Petter Brändén, and Thomas M. Liggett, “Negative Dependence and the Geometry of Polynomials,” arXiv:0707.2340, https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2340. This is prior framework for the stability/strong-Rayleigh discussion already audited in cycle 2; the present quadrature theorem does not invoke stability.
