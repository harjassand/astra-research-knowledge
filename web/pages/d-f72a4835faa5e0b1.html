A Note on Deterministic PIT for $\Sigma^{[3]}\Pi\Sigma\Pi^{[\delta]}$ Circuits

TR26-168
		
		Authors:
		
			Zeyu Guo

		Publication: 6th September 2026 12:29

		Downloads: 232 

Keywords: 

		Algebraic circuits, 
		
		depth-4 circuits, 
		
		PIT, 
		
		polynomial identity testing

Abstract:

Guo and Wang gave a deterministic polynomial-time black-box identity test for $\Sigma^{[3]}\Pi\Sigma\Pi^{[\delta]}$ circuits over fields of arbitrary characteristic, for constant $\delta$, assuming that one product gate is squarefree. This note communicates an observation suggested by a large language model: the squarefreeness assumption can be removed by combining the normalization argument of Guo and Wang with the Mason--Stothers theorem.
