where 0<s<lambda_*(K)-lambda_max and C_s is a uniform auxiliary full

3. HOMOGENEOUS CONTACT AND AN INTEGRABLE SHORT-TIME HEAT BOUND
------------------------------------------------------------
The exact imported contact inequality for normalized m-sector vectors is

 <W><=a_m+b_m sqrt(<A>),
 a_m=4m(m-1)/(L-1), b_m=4(m-1)sqrt(m/(2J))/L.            (9)

For m=0,1 both terms are zero. For arbitrary v in that sector, homogeneity
makes it

 <v,Wv><=a_m||v||^2+b_m||v||sqrt(<v,Av>).                (10)

Write b_J=4/sqrt(2J). For L>=2,m>=1,
 a_m<=8m^2/L, b_m<=b_J m^2/L.

Let B=B_v at any allowed coupling. For any normalized phi and t>0,
set z=exp(-tB/2)phi. Since B>=aA>=0,
 ||z||<=1,
 <z,Az><=a^(-1)<phi,B exp(-tB)phi><=1/(e a t).
Substitution into (10) gives the exact norm estimate

 ||exp(-tB/2) W exp(-tB/2)||_infinity
            <=a_m+b_m/sqrt(e a t)
            <=(m^2/L)[8+b_J/sqrt(e a t)].              (11)

The square-root singularity is INTEGRABLE. Replacing the contact by the
weaker relative form W<=const*(A+I)/L before this step would produce a
nonintegrable t^(-1) bound and an avoidable logarithmic loss. We do not
make that replacement in (11). Equality of norms of XX* and X*X gives

 ||W^(1/2)exp(-tB)W^(1/2)||_infinity
   =||exp(-tB/2)W exp(-tB/2)||_infinity.                 (12)

There is no commutativity assumption on A,W,B.

For LONG time t>=u=tau/2 only, sqrt(x)<=(x+1)/2 in (9) gives the form

 W<=c_J(m^2/L)F, c_J=8+b_J/2.

Thus, by trace positivity and monotonicity in t for the SAME positive B,

 Tr_m[W exp(-tB)]<=c_J(m^2/L)Tr_m[F exp(-uB)], t>=u.     (13)

The F,B product need not commute. The trace bound uses positive F and
the positive difference exp(-uB)-exp(-tB), which is a spectral function
of one B. No general exponential operator monotonicity is asserted.
