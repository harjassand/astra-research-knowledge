STRONGEST RESULT
The strongest exact counterexample is the two-cycle soft perturbation in `revisions/global_complement_counter.txt`. Let F6(epsilon) have cyclic forward entries 1 and backward entries epsilon, with all other entries zero, and let F12=diag(F6,F6), epsilon=2^-b. In the fixed k=6 paired-basis sector, a state I has weight |det F12[I,I^c]|^2. The positive support factors into 14 states per block.

Use the symmetric Metropolis kernel M that exchanges one selected F-row line with one unselected coordinate line, plus the global complement involution R:I↦I^c. On two replicas set Q=(M+R)/2 and P=(Swap+Q_A+Q_B)/3. All proposals and acceptance ratios are computable from rational determinants; M connects the positive support. The four heavy orientations are OO, OE, EO, EE. Let S={OO,EE}; the global complement maps OO↔EE and OE↔EO, so it preserves S.

Write a=(1+epsilon^3)^2 and Z6=2a+6epsilon^2+6epsilon^4. The cut mass is s=pi(S)=2a^2/Z6^2, which is less than 1/2 and tends to 1/2. From each state in S, the 36 one-exchange proposals include 18 cross-block moves of determinant zero and, within the two blocks, six targets of squared weight epsilon^2 a, six of weight epsilon^4 a, and six zeros. Therefore
  M(I,S^c) = phi = (epsilon^2+epsilon^4)/(6a).
The one-replica Q conductance of S is phi/2; the pair cut S×S has mass s^2 and exact conductance phi/3.

For f=1_S and G(A,B)=f(A)+f(B), Var_pi(f)=s(1-s). The global complement and replica swap leave G unchanged; the local Metropolis components give E_P(G)=s phi/3. Hence any pair-energy constant must satisfy
  L >= 3(1-s)/phi = (9+o(1)) epsilon^-2.
Since epsilon=2^-b and F12 has fixed dimension with O(b) input bits, the required L grows exponentially in the input bit length. This refutes the polynomial-energy claim for this exact local-plus-global-complement kernel family.

The first checkpoint independently gives the six-site one-cycle obstruction for the single-exchange kernel, including an exact additive-energy witness. `revisions/cross_worm_radius2.txt` extends it to a symmetric cross-replica one-coordinate mismatch exchange and the radius-two symmetric-proposal Metropolis class. These are specified-kernel boundaries, not general no-go theorems.

REPAIRS AND LIMITS
A direct complement flip rescues the isolated six-cycle. Two cycles show why a single global complement is insufficient: it preserves the parity of the two mode bits. A componentwise complement proposal for each 6×6 block exits S with probability at least 1/2 and removes this particular bottleneck. The support components are explicit in this example; acquiring analogous cycle moves for arbitrary rational F, with computable transitions and polynomial energy, remains unresolved.

The result does not refute a worm with mismatch registers, individually detected alternating-cycle moves, coordinate-pair auxiliaries, or a non-Metropolis norm flow. It does not establish an FPRAS, full microscopic sampler, field-breaking advance, or literature priority. The campaign baseline already includes the hard directed-cycle support disconnection; this work gives its connected epsilon-regularization and shows that global complement alone still fails on two blocks.

PRIMARY-SOURCE BOUNDARY
The literature check found adjacent but distinct interfaces: Iwata–Kobayashi give weighted linear matroid parity optimization, Matoya–Oki count minimum-weight solutions under Pfaffian parity conditions, and Anari et al. give an FPRAS for matroid bases. These do not directly provide the full squared complementary-minor partition sampler studied here: [Iwata–Kobayashi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.13371), [Matoya–Oki](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.00620), [Anari et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.01816). The 6-site positive support fails the matroid basis-exchange axiom: with zero-based X={2,4,5}, Y={0,3,4}, and x=2, both exchanges X-x+0={0,4,5} and X-x+3={3,4,5} have zero weight. Thus the generic matroid-base theorem is not directly applicable. No novelty claim is made.
