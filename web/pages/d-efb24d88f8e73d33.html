CYCLE 5 — INFORMATION-CENTER SPECTRAL TRUNCATION AT THE COMMON-POVM GATE
Status: internally proved obstruction to rank/spectral truncation; not a
counterexample to the compatible-broadcasting theorem.

Question. Can a bounded information-radius / relative-entropy center reduce
the least-favourable-prior common-POVM variance problem to a dimension-free
number of score directions? If not, what exactly is missing?

1. INTERFACE

Let tau=Tr/d and sigma=I/d in A=M_d. A score H=H* obeys tau(H)=0 and
||H||_infinity<=theta<1; its state is rho_H=sigma(I+H). For a finite prior
p on scores, write

  Q_p = sum_x p_x |H_x><H_x|       (operator on centered L2(tau)),
  V(p) = Tr_L2 Q_p = sum_x p_x tau(H_x^2).

For one pure-state reference ensemble E={(w_y,psi_y):
sum_y w_y |psi_y><psi_y|=sigma}, set

  J_E(p)=sum_y w_y sum_x p_x |<psi_y,H_x psi_y>|^2,
  J(p)=max_E J_E(p),
  Delta(p)=V(p)-J(p).

This is the convex-roof/common-measurement quantity: Delta(p) is the minimum,
over one ensemble shared by all x, of the prior-averaged posterior pure-state
variance. Finite-dimensional convex minimax gives q(E_family)=sup_p Delta(p).
The question here is specifically whether the entropy center can make this
common measurement low-dimensional; no interchange of infimum and average
is used below.

2. WHAT THE CENTER CONTROLS

For sigma=I/d and bounded H, define

  c(theta)=1/2[(1+theta)ln(1+theta)+(1-theta)ln(1-theta)].

If every score in a family has eigenvalues +/-theta with equal multiplicity,
then D(rho_H||sigma)=c(theta), and D_max(rho_H||sigma)=ln(1+theta).
For any prior, Donald's identity gives

  chi(p) = sum_x p_x D(rho_x||sigma)-D(rho_bar||sigma) <= c(theta).

If the prior mean score is zero, equality holds. Thus sigma is an exact
information-radius center, not just a convenient reference. The local
quadratic consequence more generally is only a trace bound on Q_p, such as
V(p)<=2(1+theta) C for center radius C. It does not control rank, entropy of
the normalized score covariance, or the tail after a fixed-rank spectral
cutoff.

3. A MATCHED PAIR: SAME CENTER AND FULL COVARIANCE SPECTRUM, DIFFERENT Delta

Fix a in (0,1), take k=2^s>=4, let m=2k and d=2^k, and use the uniform
prior on x in {+1,-1}^m. This choice makes m divide d and d/m even, so the
two frames below can be put in the *same ambient matrix algebra* M_d.
One may adjoin the reference score H=0 to either family; it changes none of
the capacity, covariance-prior, variance, or compatibility statements.

(A) Commuting frame. Partition a basis of C^d into m blocks B_i of size
d/m. On B_i, let Z_i be +1 on half the basis vectors and -1 on the other
half, and set Z_i=0 outside B_i. Put

  H_x^D = a sum_(i=1)^m x_i Z_i.

Then ||H_x^D||_infinity=a, tau(H_x^D)=0, tau((H_x^D)^2)=a^2, and
rho_x^D=(I+H_x^D)/d has eigenvalues (1 +/- a)/d. The uniform sign
prior has mean state sigma_D=I/d. Every state has relative entropy c(a)
and max-relative entropy ln(1+a) from sigma_D. Donald's identity proves the
Holevo capacity is exactly c(a), hence sigma_D is an exact radius center.
Indeed, for any alternative center omega, the uniform prior gives
average_x D(rho_x^D||omega)=c(a)+D(sigma_D||omega)>=c(a), while sigma_D
itself has maximum divergence c(a). The same calculation applies to the
Clifford frame.

The vectors e_i=sqrt(m) Z_i are orthonormal in L2(tau), and
H_x^D=(a/sqrt(m)) sum_i x_i e_i. Therefore

  Q_D=(a^2/m) sum_i |e_i><e_i|,

with rank m, all nonzero eigenvalues a^2/m, and trace a^2. Yet the one
computational-basis PVM is a common measurement for every score: each H_x^D
is constant on each basis vector, so J_D(p)=V(p)=a^2 and Delta_D(p)=0.
Indeed the dephasing channel is an exact compatible marginal on this family;
the basis-copy channel rho -> sum_j <j|rho|j> |jj><jj| broadcasts it exactly.

(B) Clifford frame. On the same dimension d=2^k, take m=2k pairwise anticommuting
Hermitian Pauli strings Gamma_i with Gamma_i^2=I and tau(Gamma_i Gamma_j)
=delta_ij. For example, for j=1,...,k,

  Gamma_(2j-1)=Z^(tensor j-1) tensor X tensor I^(tensor k-j),
  Gamma_(2j)  =Z^(tensor j-1) tensor Y tensor I^(tensor k-j).

Put

  H_x^C = (a/sqrt(m)) sum_(i=1)^m x_i Gamma_i.

Anticommutation gives (H_x^C)^2=a^2 I, so these scores too are centered,
have operator norm a and variance a^2, and their states have eigenvalues
(1 +/- a)/d_C. Their uniform mean is sigma_C=I/d_C. Their exact Holevo
capacity, information radius, and max-relative-entropy radius are respectively
c(a), c(a), and ln(1+a), exactly as in (A). Their uniform-prior covariance
is

Q_C=(a^2/m) sum_i |Gamma_i><Gamma_i|,

again rank m, flat nonzero spectrum a^2/m, and trace a^2. In either frame,
the normalized covariance Q_p/a^2 has entropy ln(m), which diverges despite
the fixed exact Holevo radius and D_max bound. Both now act in
the same M_d, so the *entire* covariance spectra, including the multiplicity
of zero, match; this is stronger than matching only nonzero eigenvalues.

Nevertheless Delta_C(p)=a^2(1-1/m). To verify this, for a pure psi let
r_i=<psi,Gamma_i psi>. Since ||sum_i t_i Gamma_i||_infinity=||t||_2,
duality gives sum_i r_i^2<=1. Hence for every reference ensemble,

  J_E(p)= (a^2/m) sum_y w_y sum_i r_i(psi_y)^2 <= a^2/m.

Equality is attained by any orthonormal eigenbasis of Gamma_1: each basis
vector has r_1=+/-1 and all other r_i=0, and the uniform basis ensemble
resolves I/d_C. Therefore J(p)=a^2/m while V(p)=a^2, as claimed. In
particular q(E_family)>=Delta_C(p), so this frame has a dimension-free
positive common-POVM deficiency as m grows.

4. EXACT OBSTRUCTION AND ITS SCOPE

The matched pair proves all of the following.

  • Any universal upper-bound rule depending only on the exact
    relative-entropy center, its radius, the max-relative-entropy bound, and
    the full eigenvalue list of Q_p must assign at least a^2(1-1/m) to both
    examples, since these inputs agree. It therefore cannot certify small
    Delta even for (A), where the exact value is zero.

  • No fixed R=R(C,theta,epsilon) score-rank truncation can capture all but
    epsilon of V(p) from the center assumption. For either frame and any
    rank-R projection P onto score modes,

    Tr(P Q_p) <= a^2 min(R,m)/m,
    Tr((I-P)Q_p) >= a^2 max(0,1-R/m).

    Thus for m >> R the discarded tail retains essentially all score energy,
    despite the exact center and even the uniform D_max bound. The same
    example blocks fixed-rank Hilbert-space truncation: every rho_x has all
    d eigenvalues of order 1/d, so a rank-R compression retains only O(R/d)
    state mass.

  • The missing information is operator-algebraic orientation. The diffuse
    tail is harmless when it lies in one abelian algebra (A), but is costly
    for a common measurement in (B). A center/covariance truncation that
    discards the tail cannot distinguish these cases.

This is NOT an obstruction to the desired theorem under actual compatibility.
The Clifford frame is not approximately broadcastable with small uniform
residual. Indeed, for independent uniform x,z,

  E ||[H_x^C,H_z^C]||_(2,tau)^2
     = (a^4/m^2) sum_(i,j)||[Gamma_i,Gamma_j]||_2^2
     = 4a^4(1-1/m).

The established compatible-map estimate (15) in
outputs/research/classicality/cycle4/information_score.txt gives

  E ||[H_x,H_z]||_2^2
    <=4(2+sqrt(2))^2 theta^2 sum_x p_x E_Phi(H_x).

If Phi is an admissible actual compatible marginal with theta=a and uniform
residual r=sup_x ||Phi(H_x)-H_x||_1, then E_Phi(H_x)<=a r and consequently

  r >= a(1-1/m)/(2+sqrt(2))^2.

By contrast, the diagonal frame has an exact compatible broadcaster and r=0.
Thus compatibility/low broadcaster energy contains precisely the extra
structure that the center-only truncation omits. The Clifford example is a
control, not a sequence refuting the compatible common-POVM modulus.

5. CONCLUSION / OPEN INTERFACE

The information-radius route yields a rigorous *method obstruction*: neither
relative-entropy centering nor any truncation based solely on the score
covariance spectrum supplies a dimension-free effective score rank or a
common-POVM variance bound. The failure persists with exact centers,
constant Holevo capacity, and bounded D_max, and can be seen at the exact
convex-roof quantity Delta rather than only at outcome count.

What remains open is a structure-sensitive implication using the actual
compatible extension, for example a modulus

  Delta(p) <= F_(C,theta)(sum_x p_x E_Phi(H_x)),  F_(C,theta)(u)->0,

without reducing to fixed score rank. A plausible route would have to retain
the full diffuse score algebra (e.g. construct one conditional expectation /
POVM from compatibility and an operator-algebraic near-commutation argument).
No such route is proved here. This note does not claim that the common-POVM
theorem is false, does not transfer the Pauli/Clifford nonbroadcastability
control into a counterexample, and does not establish a dimension-free
approximate-abelianization theorem.
