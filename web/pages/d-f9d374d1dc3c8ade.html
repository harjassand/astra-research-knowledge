## Research finding: an exact Bernoulli splitting

I have identified a route that goes beyond the previous Haar decompositions and approximate codings. It gives a candidate theorem with a complete proof using a classical relative-isomorphism result.

Theorem. Let \\(G\\) be a countably infinite torsion-free group with elements \\(a,b,c\in\mathbb F_2[G]\\) satisfying \\(ab=1\\), \\(ac=0\\), and \\(c\ne0\\). Then, for every \\(h>0\\), there exists a probability-measure-preserving \\(G\\)-system \\(R_h\\) such that

\\[ \boxed{ (\mathbb F_2^G,\mathrm{Haar}) \cong_G (L_h^G,\lambda_h^G)\times R_h, \qquad H(\lambda_h)=h. } \\]

The product measure is independent, and the isomorphism is exact and \\(G\\)-equivariant.

In particular, the fair binary Bernoulli shift has infinite weak Pinsker entropy, in the sense defined by Bowen and Tucker-Drob.&#x20;

For \\(h=\log4\\), this gives an exact splitting

\\[ \boxed{2^G\cong_G4^G\times R\_{\log4}.} \\]

This is substantially stronger than mutual factor maps: the four-symbol shift has an independent measurable complement inside the binary shift. It does not yet prove \\(2^G\cong_G4^G\\), because the complement has not been eliminated.

### Proof mechanism

Write \\(K=\ker T_a\\), where \\(T_a\\) is the linear cellular automaton associated with \\(a\\). The established one-sided inverse gives \\(2^G\cong_G2^G\times K\\). The equation \\(ac=0\\) supplies a nonzero finitely supported configuration \\(d\in K\\).

The key new step is to use two independent Haar copies of \\(K\\). In the first copy, choose a positive-measure marker event whose translates identify well-separated positions. At those positions, the translates of \\(d\\) have disjoint supports. The second copy of \\(K\\) can therefore be decomposed exactly into a residual configuration and independent fair bits at the marked positions.

Conditional on the marker and residual configurations, these are genuinely independent bits—not approximately independent finite-window statistics. Their distribution is an inhomogeneous Bernoulli extension: a fair bit appears at each marked group element, and a deterministic symbol elsewhere.

Thouvenot's relative isomorphism theorem, in the orbit-equivalence-relation formulation proved in Section 3.3 of Daniel Hoff's dissertation, converts this extension into a homogeneous Bernoulli shift, independently of the residual base. Its entropy is

\\[ \delta=\mu_K(W)\log2>0, \\]

where \\(W\\) is the marker event.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://escholarship.org\&sz=32)

escholarship.org



Consequently,

\\[ K\times K\cong_G Z\times B\_\delta \\]

for a residual system \\(Z\\) and a Bernoulli shift \\(B\_\delta\\) of base entropy \\(\delta\\).

Iterating the original finite Haar splitting gives, for every integer \\(n\\),

\\[ 2^G\cong_G2^G\times K^{2n} \cong_G2^G\times Z^n\times B\_\delta^n. \\]

The final Bernoulli factor has base entropy \\(n\delta\\), which is arbitrarily large. Seward's equal-entropy isomorphism theorem allows it to be expressed using any prescribed Bernoulli base of entropy at most \\(n\delta\\).&#x20;

The important point is that this argument uses only finitely many independent defect factors at each stage. It does not assume that the previously identified infinite residual tail splits off.

### Verification of the exact splitting

The delicate part is proving that the extracted randomness is independent of the entire residual system, not merely independent across finitely many coordinates.

Choose \\(d\in K\\) with finite support \\(S\\), and \\(s_0\in S\\) with \\(d(s_0)=1\\).

Because \\(K\\) is a nontrivial factor of the binary Bernoulli shift, its Haar action is mixing. Since \\(G\\) is torsion-free, this action is essentially free. We can therefore choose a positive-measure clopen set \\(W\subseteq K\\) satisfying

\\[ W\cap fW=\varnothing \qquad (f\in SS^{-1}\setminus\\{e\\}). \\]

For independent Haar configurations \\(u,v\in K\\), define the equivariant marker set

\\[ M(u)=\\{g\in G:g^{-1}u\in W\\}. \\]

The sets \\(gS\\), for \\(g\in M(u)\\), are pairwise disjoint. Define

\\[ \beta_g=v(gs_0)\quad(g\in M(u)), \qquad r=v+\sum\_{g\in M(u)}\beta_g(gd). \\]

The sum is well-defined coordinatewise because the supports are disjoint. Moreover, \\(r\in K\\) and \\(r(gs_0)=0\\) at every marked position.

For fixed \\(u\\), the transformation

\\[ v\longmapsto\bigl(r,(\beta_g)\_{g\in M(u)}\bigr) \\]

is an isomorphism of compact Abelian groups onto the product of the residual subgroup and \\(\mathbb F_2^{M(u)}\\). Consequently, Haar measure becomes the independent product of residual Haar measure and fair bits at every marked position.

The base system \\(Z=(u,r)\\) is mixing and essentially free because it is a factor of \\(K^2\\). The resulting extension over \\(Z\\) is precisely the inhomogeneous Bernoulli extension covered by Hoff's Lemma 3.3.7. That lemma identifies it, over the same base, with an ordinary Bernoulli extension of entropy \\(\mu_K(W)\log2\\).&#x20;

### What remains unsolved

The newly established direct-product decomposition meets the formal definition of infinite weak Pinsker entropy given by Bowen and Tucker-Drob.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://ems.press\&sz=32)

ems.press



It does not determine \\(h\_{\sup}^{\mathrm{Rok}}(G)\\), prove universal Bernoulli collapse, or establish the binary–four-symbol isomorphism.

The central unresolved step can now be stated more sharply:

\\[ \boxed{ \text{Can the binary Bernoulli shift absorb a nontrivial Bernoulli direct factor without retaining a residual factor?} } \\]

For example, a proof of

\\[ K^2\cong_G K^2\times B\_\delta \qquad(\delta>0) \\]

would suffice. The established absorption \\(X\cong_G X\times K^2\\) would then imply \\(X\cong_G X\times B\_\delta\\). Iteration and Seward's theorem would yield \\(2^G\cong_G4^G\\).

But the proved result is only \\(K^2\cong_G Z\times B\_\delta\\). Identifying \\(Z\\) with \\(K^2\\), or otherwise eliminating it, is a substantial additional problem. Neither conditional independence nor the relative-isomorphism theorem provides that identification.

I also investigated whether making the underlying linear endomorphism exact would eliminate this obstruction. It does not: triviality of the endomorphism's measurable future tail does not force its independent innovations to generate the original system. Thus that approach currently supplies no valid promotion to a Bernoulli conjugacy.

Research status: The infinite weak Pinsker theorem is a concrete, proof-supported advance beyond the previous Haar splitting and approximate coding results. I did not find an earlier statement of this particular consequence, although its publication-level novelty still requires specialist verification. Bowen's original isomorphism question remains unresolved by this investigation.
