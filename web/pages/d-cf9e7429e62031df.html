## F1. Interface and primary premise

There are N qubits with commuting Pauli checks C_a, and

    H = sum_a e_a (I-C_a)/2,   e_a>0,
    rho_beta = exp(-beta H)/Z,
    g = max_i sum_{a:i in supp(C_a)} e_a.                 (F1)

The physical beta multiplies this displayed Hamiltonian. If H=-sum_a C_a,
the excitation penalties are e_a=2, up to an irrelevant additive constant.
If excitation projectors have unit penalty, e_a=1. The actual family has at
most seven incident checks per qubit, giving g<=7 for the latter normalization
and g<=14 for the former. No temperature normalization is silently changed.

Assume decoder-dressed Hermitian logicals Q_X,Q_Z satisfy Q_X^2=Q_Z^2=I,
Q_X Q_Z=-Q_Z Q_X, and commute with every C_a. A syndrome-diagonal projector
P_1 commutes with both logicals and protects all single-qubit Paulis:

    [E_i,Q_X]P_1=[E_i,Q_Z]P_1=0,
    p_1=Tr[(I-P_1)rho_beta].                             (F2)

These are the exact additional hypotheses needed from the decoder in
Balasubramanian--Davydova--Lin, arXiv2605.10943v1, review of memory lifetime
and Section6. Source authors: Shankar Balasubramanian, Margarita Davydova,
Ting-Chun Lin; first submitted 11 May 2026. The actual admitted family uses
the repaired interface independently reconstructed in pde_spectrum9's
FINITE_LOOKUP_AND_WITNESS_GATE.md: ordinary correction rules are deterministic
and zero on zero input; at level2 use one fixed minimum-weight correction
for each syndrome, with fixed ties. The actual coarse-star comparison has
weight<=7, the correction also has weight<=7, and d(C2)>14 makes their
closed difference a stabilizer. This supplies single-Pauli protection without
asserting locality of an arbitrary finite lookup. The complete recursive
ordinary string-cleaning contraction is inherited primary prior, not
recreated by the present note.

The corrected source-based Peierls tail, retaining the witness-count prefactor, is
p_1<=C_base 2^(-2^(k-1)), with C_base=2(|V_2^X|+|V_2^Z|), a fixed constant
in depth k. Its final displayed 4 omits those fixed factors. Exclude levels
0,1,2 from ordinary-level domination, because level2's global lookup need
not be local and the artificial root tags have no syndrome. Put

    C_low=sum_{i=0}^2 |V(G_i)|,
    K_dom=2(1+Delta_level^2),   2^(k-1)>=2C_low.

Every ordinary marked tag is within graph distance<=2 of a syndrome tag;
the actual degree bound gives ball size<=1+Delta_level^2. The low-level
exclusion leaves at least half the tags, hence K_dom. For each sector a
sufficient exact temperature condition is

    2(Delta+1)^2 [(1+exp(-beta_s/4))^M_ell-1]^
                        (1/K_dom) <=1/2.               (F3)

Here beta_s=beta e for uniform penalties e. This finite threshold follows,
for example, by taking qcrit=[1/(4(Delta+1)^2)]^K_dom and
beta_s>=4 log[M_ell/log(1+qcrit)]. Use (1+x)^M<=exp(Mx). The source's
displayed log(1+x)<=x log2 on (0,1) is not used. These corrections preserve
the stretched-exponential tail. pde_spectrum9's independently reconstructed
baseline records the exact witness premise and these bookkeeping corrections.

Physical independent Hermitian bath channels S_alpha are kept fixed; their
supports have size<=q_b and diameter<=R_b. Their Davies rates are gamma_alpha
(omega)>=0, obey KMS gamma_alpha(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma_alpha(omega)
in the convention where positive omega is energy sent to the bath. Define

    Gamma_alpha=sup_omega gamma_alpha(omega),
    r_bath=max_i sum_{alpha:i in supp(S_alpha)}
                                Gamma_alpha ||S_alpha||^2.  (F4)

Suprema are over relevant frequencies and must be charged. Channels with
scalar couplings can be removed. Independent channels are an exact interface;
correlated baths require a separate norm bound on their positive rate matrices.
No uncharged summation over bath entries is assumed.

## F2. The fixed-bath conjugation is exact but changes the pulled-back coupling

Let U have d layers of disjoint gates, maximum gate arity a and gate diameter
R_U in the physical metric. H'=UHU*. The physical bath remains S_alpha.
In the U* frame its couplings are

    A_alpha=U* S_alpha U,
    |supp A_alpha|<=q=a^d q_b,
    supp A_alpha subset B(x_alpha,R_b+d R_U),
    ||A_alpha||=||S_alpha||.                             (F5)

The support claim follows by pulling back one layer at a time: disjoint
gates meeting the current support multiply its cardinality by at most a
and enlarge its physical neighborhood by at most R_U. An original support
qubit can be chosen as x_alpha. This gives N possible anchors regardless
of how many duplicate bath channels are present.

For spectral components A_omega[H]=sum_{E'-E=omega}Pi_E A Pi_E',

    U* S_alpha,omega[H'] U = A_alpha,omega[H].             (F6)

The Bohr frequencies and original rate functions are exactly the same.
Thus the physical Davies semigroup for H' and S_alpha pulls back to the
Davies semigroup for H and A_alpha. It generally does not pull back to
the original semigroup for H and S_alpha. This is the fixed-bath interface
that unitary covariance alone leaves unresolved. A_alpha need not be Pauli.

## F3. Local prefix erosion proves protection for arbitrary enlarged operators

Let each radius-R*=R_b+d R_U ball contain at most G qubits. Admit all Paulis
F of weight<=q-1 whose support is contained in one such anchored ball,
including identity. There are at most

    M=1+N sum_{j=1}^{q-1} 3^j binom(G,j) <=1+N(4^G-1)     (F7)

distinct admitted prefixes (q can be capped at G). For q=1 use M=1.
The global few-body alternative has M=sum_{j=0}^{q-1}3^j binom(N,j).
Define

    P_q = intersection_F F* P_1 F.                      (F8)

All factors are syndrome-diagonal, so they commute and their intersection
is an orthogonal projector. Since Q_X,Q_Z commute with every check, they
commute with every factor in (F8). Hence P_q commutes with H and both
logicals. It is essential here to retain syndrome diagonality and logical
commutation with individual checks, beyond the bare abstract Chesi hypotheses.

Let E=P_w ... P_1 be a Pauli of weight w<=q, factored into single-qubit
Paulis at its distinct supported sites. Each pre-factor prefix
F_r=P_r ... P_1, 0<=r<=w-1, is admitted and maps ran(P_q) into ran(P_1).
The exact telescoping commutator

    [E,Q]P_q=sum_{r=1}^w P_w ... P_{r+1}
                            [P_r,Q] F_{r-1} P_q=0        (F9)

proves protection. Every operator on an admitted size-q support is a
linear combination of such Paulis. Thus [A_alpha,Q]P_q=0 with no Pauli
coefficient l1 factor. Products of individually protected singles are not
automatically protected: the intermediate syndromes in (F9) must be safe.

For a weight-w Pauli F, only checks intersecting its support can flip, so

    ||F H F*-H|| <=g w.

Both Hamiltonians are commuting check functions. Consequently
F rho_beta F*<=exp(beta g w)rho_beta, with the same partition function.
Union bound over the commuting factors in (F8) gives

    p_q=Tr[(I-P_q)rho_beta]
       <=min(1, M exp(beta g(q-1)) p_1).                 (F10)

This is a valid full joint CSS Gibbs argument. It neither assumes an
independent syndrome distribution nor silently replaces it by two unrelated
models. Syndrome translations are legal because they arise from actual
Pauli conjugations of the full Hilbert space.

## F4. Davies protection and rate norm incur no frequency-count loss

If [H,Q]=[H,P_q]=0, then

    [A_alpha(t),Q]P_q=exp(iHt)[A_alpha,Q]P_q exp(-iHt)=0.

Time Fourier projection proves the same identity for every A_alpha,omega.
This is the exact Chesi0907.2807v2 Davies specialization. Its Theorem 1 allows
non-Pauli jumps. It requires stationarity rho_beta and logical/protection
commutation; the abstract theorem does not require detailed balance, while
the stated KMS construction supplies stationarity here.

For any H, spectral pinching gives

    sum_omega A_omega* A_omega
       =sum_E Pi_E A* A Pi_E <=||A||^2 I.                (F11)

The weighted completely positive jump map has trace-norm and diamond norm
equal to the norm of its adjoint at I, bounded by sum_alpha Gamma_alpha
||A_alpha||^2. The anticommutator part has at most the same norm. Therefore

    ||D||_1 <=2 sum_alpha Gamma_alpha ||S_alpha||^2
             <=2 N r_bath.                              (F12)

This independently reconstructs Chesi Proposition 1 with variable operator
norms and rates. There is no Bohr-frequency or Pauli-expansion multiplicity.
The source norm ||.||_1 is the induced trace norm on Hermitian inputs; the
diamond estimate is a safe stronger upper bound.

## F5. Exact thermal encoding, decoder and guaranteed lifetime

The logical Pauli algebra generated by Q_X,Q_Z yields an exact full-Hilbert
space factor H_L tensor H_aux. Since H commutes with that algebra,
H=I_L tensor H_aux and rho_beta=I_L/2 tensor eta_aux. Chesi Section 6.1 gives

    Enc(tau)=tau tensor eta_aux,
    Dec=partial_trace_aux,
    Enc'=Ad_U Enc,
    Dec'=Dec Ad_U*.                                     (F13)

These are CPTP and Dec' Enc'=identity. Equivalently Enc(tau)=O_tau rho_beta,
O_tau=I+r_X Q_X+r_Y Q_Y+r_Z Q_Z, ||O_tau||<=2. It commutes with H and P_q.
The physical thermal state for H' is U rho_beta U*, so the encoding is
its corresponding thermal encoding. This is not a claim that a bare
ground-state encoding has the same lifetime or that thermal initialization
has been acquired efficiently. Applying U and U* adds d ideal local layers
to a supplied base encoding and decoding procedure; the decoder's classical
processing is retained after U*.

Chesi Theorem 1 and (F10)--(F12) give for every input state tau and all t>=0

    ||Phi'_t Enc'(tau)-Enc'(tau)||_1
       <=16 N r_bath t M exp(beta g(q-1)) p_1.            (F14)

Decoded error is bounded by the same quantity by trace-norm contraction.
Thus error<=delta throughout the guaranteed interval

    0<=t<=delta/[16 N r_bath M exp(beta g(q-1)) p_1].      (F15)

Use a first failure threshold or this whole guaranteed interval; the
memory paper's printed min{t:error<=1/10} includes t=0 and is not an
operational definition of lifetime. For fixed d,a,R_U,q_b,R_b,G,beta,g
and at most polynomial physical rates, (F15) preserves the source's
stretched-exponential lifetime exp(Omega(2^k))=exp(Omega(N^eta)). The local
prefix count costs at most an extra power N; the explicit physical rate
already contributes its separate N.

## F6. Optional Lamb shift, with its additional support and rate cost

The dissipative convention of Chesi/source may omit a Davies Lamb shift.
To retain one, assume the Hamiltonian part is H'+K', where
K'=sum_{alpha,omega} ell_alpha(omega) S_alpha,omega* S_alpha,omega,
ell_alpha real and Lambda_alpha=sup_omega|ell_alpha(omega)| finite. Define
r_LS=max_i sum_{alpha:i in supp S_alpha} Lambda_alpha||S_alpha||^2.

In the pulled-back frame K commutes with H and rho_beta. Pinching (F11)
and the order inequalities for each real bounded coefficient give
||K||<=sum_alpha Lambda_alpha||S_alpha||^2<=N r_LS. The local protection
can be proved without assigning the full spectral component a small support.
Write A_alpha=sum_E a_E E in Paulis on its fixed bare support X_alpha.
Because H is a function of the checks, its spectral projectors are syndrome
functions and Pauli E permutes the syndrome projectors. Consequently

    A_alpha,omega=sum_E a_E E D_{E,omega}(C),

where D_{E,omega} is a diagonal syndrome projector selecting the syndromes
whose energy change under E is the indicated frequency. In the product
A_alpha,omega* A_alpha,omega, move a diagonal factor through E*F by shifting
its syndrome argument. Every summand is then E*F times another syndrome
function. Both E and F are Paulis on the same X_alpha, so E*F is still on
X_alpha and has weight at most |X_alpha|<=q, not twice that support. All
syndrome functions commute with Q and P_q. Thus (F9) protects each summand:

    [E*F D(C),Q]P_q=[E*F,Q]D(C)P_q=0,
    [K,Q]P_q=0.

This sharper argument uses the full syndrome-diagonal H and individual
check commutation of Q. If only a commuting finite-range term description
is available, the touching-term neighborhood gives a conservative enlarged
support instead. No generic spectral projection locality is asserted. In
the present independent-channel interface the original P_q suffices; a
2q erosion is also a valid conservative bound. Cross-channel Lamb-shift
terms would require a union-support and rate-matrix analysis separately.

K need not commute with Q globally, so
Chesi Theorem 1 is not simply reapplied with H+K. Instead use

    [K,Enc(tau)]=[K,O_tau](I-P_q)rho_beta,
    ||[K,Enc(tau)]||_1<=4||K||p_q.                       (F16)

Stationarity D(rho_beta)=0 and [K,rho_beta]=0 remain exact. Chesi's
dissipator input bound and (F16), followed by CPTP semigroup contraction,
give the stronger physical-interface statement

    ||Phi'_t Enc'(tau)-Enc'(tau)||_1
        <=t N(16 r_bath+4 r_LS) p_q.                    (F17)

The Bohr frequency convention for the weak-coupling generator still uses
H', with K' its commuting Lamb-shift correction. If a different model
recomputes jumps using H'+K' as a new microscopic Hamiltonian, it is a
different interface and is not covered without a new support analysis.
The ideal Markovian weak-coupling model is supplied. This theorem does not
acquire a microscopic bath approximation uniformly valid up to its claimed
exponentially long storage times.

## F7. Intensive Hamiltonian perturbation cost

For U_g=exp(-i theta_g K_g), ||K_g||<=1, |theta_g|<=theta and original
check arity q_c, set q_H=a^d q_c. No more than d q_H gates can affect a
given check during its conjugation, by the same layer light-cone count.
The commutator bound for an energy-e_a projector is ||[K_g,h_a]||<=e_a,
so telescoping gate evolution and the difference of two projectors give

    ||U h_a U*-h_a||<=e_a min(1,d q_H theta).            (F18)

Label each difference on its original check's d-layer light cone, and put
v_d=max_x #B(x,d R_U). If a label contains x then some original check qubit
lies in B(x,d R_U). Thus the physically charged interaction strength obeys

    J_pert=max_x sum_{a:x in label(a)} ||U h_a U*-h_a||
          <=v_d g min(1,d q_H theta).                   (F19)

The bounded-density geometry makes v_d a fixed constant at fixed d,R_U.
This permits arbitrarily small intensive J by choosing theta small, and
the exact orbit theorem also permits finite angles. V=H'-H can fail to
commute with H, while the conjugated check terms commute with one another.
This restricted algebraic relation is decisive; no arbitrary small-V
thermal theorem follows.

## F8. Composition with the independently gated diagonal thermal neighborhood

The proof F2--F6 only needs H to be a function of the original checks,
the corrected single-error premise p_1, and the Pauli translation inequality
||FHF*-H||<=g_eff |F|. It is not restricted to the particular sum in F1.
For the already independently gated class in rounding_spectral's
THERMAL_ERASURE_EXTENSION.md, take

    H_d=sum_a J_a D_a+sum_T V_T(C),
    J_min<=J_a<=J_max,
    g_check=max_a sum_{T:a in T}||V_T||,
    kappa=J_min-2g_check>0,
    g_eff=7(J_max+2g_check).                            (F20)

The legal witness-erasure estimate supplies p_1<=C_base 2^(-2^(k-1)) at
beta kappa above the corrected threshold F3. Each one-qubit Pauli flips
at most seven checks, and each affected interaction changes energy by at
most twice its norm, proving the displayed g_eff on every realizable
syndrome. A weight-w Pauli uses w actual syndrome translations, so the
operator inequality and union bound F10 hold with this g_eff. The joint
CSS Gibbs distribution need not factor. Therefore F14--F17 apply verbatim
to H'=U H_d U*, with the ORIGINAL physical bath and the NEW thermal
auxiliary state for H_d. This is a composition of two separately checked
interfaces, not an inference from closeness of Davies generators.

The physical centralizer application in that note gives g_check<=B_R J_c
for total bounded-range [V,C_a]=0 and |supp V_X|<d_code, after norm-contracting
stabilizer twirling and the actual original-check filling theorem. Its

For an actually supplied bounded-range decomposition H_d=sum_X h_X with
|X|<=q_h and I_H=max_i sum_{X:i in X}||h_X||, the F7 telescoping argument
gives the conservative general physical cost

    J(U H_d U*-H_d)
       <=2 v_d I_H min(1,d a^d q_h theta).              (F21)

Here the factor2 is the general operator commutator/difference bound; F19
is sharper for pure excitation projectors. Relative to the unit-penalty
base H_0 and H_d=H_0+V_c, the total perturbation cost is at most
J_c+2v_d I_H min(1,d a^d q_h theta). Taking both J_c and theta small
therefore gives a concrete nonzero intensive neighborhood inside this
restricted diagonal-plus-circuit class. All transformed terms still belong
to a conjugated commuting algebra. Generic noncommuting interactions
outside that algebra are not included.
