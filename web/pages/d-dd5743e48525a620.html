PRIOR ART AND INTERPRETATION

The cycle-1 analytic identical-Fisher comparison already gives
log-squared versus logarithmic archive growth. c09_s02 independently gives
an even wider Gaussian fixed-q affine-dimension gap. c09_l05 supplies a
general low-dimensional latent codec, and c09_l03 supplies a full-dimensional
joint-score sufficient condition. The new c09_l07 pair is best treated as a
small, exactly checked example between these gates, with a rational codec
fixture, rather than as a new general result.

The Gaussian heat-flow quantity I(W;W+sqrt(tau)G) used in the post-release
derivation is a standard high-SNR / information-dimension diagnostic. Wu and
Verdú's 2011 paper “MMSE Dimension” (author-hosted version surfaced in the
current source search at
https://www.stat.yale.edu/~yw562/reprints/mmse_dim.pdf) connects Gaussian
high-SNR MMSE behavior and information dimension. The direct author-PDF open
returned a fetch error, so only the search-extracted title/abstract metadata
was checked; no full proof inspection is claimed. The heat-flow calculation
for this pair is given self-containedly in
revisions/PAIR_POSTERIOR_HEATFLOW.txt and is not presented as novel.

The primary sources actually opened earlier in this task remain:
Hayashi–Tan arXiv:1612.02542v2, whose iid sample-size / approximate-sufficient-
statistic setting differs from the one-draw epsilon->0 archive interface;
Nayak 1999 on quantum random-access entropy bounds. They support prior-art
context only; the finite trigonometric identities and codec constants are
proved directly.
