S01 INDEPENDENT REPAIR OF L04'S RANDOM-AXIS WITNESS
7 October 2026. Derived after reading the assigned L04 INITIAL.
The antiferromagnetic family and measurement originate with L04. This note
repairs one intermediate inequality and strengthens its constant.

For a pure product of N qubits let S=sum_i u_i u_i^T, |u_i|=1, and
V(n)=(N-n^T S n)/4. Fourier inversion gives every atom of the Bernoulli sum
probability <=min(1,c/sqrt(V(n))), c=sqrt(pi)/(2sqrt(2)). Indeed its
characteristic modulus is <=exp[-2V sin^2(t/2)], and sin(|t|/2)>=|t|/pi;
integrating the resulting Gaussian over the real line gives exactly c/sqrt V.
This atom bound is independent of which integer sum is requested.

L04's claim lambda1>N/2 implies lambda2<=N/4 is false: the PSD trace-N matrix
diag(51N/100,49N/100,0) is a counterexample. What is true is
lambda2<=N-lambda1<N/2. Write z=n.e1. If lambda1>N/2, then
n^T S n<=lambda1 z^2+(N-lambda1)(1-z^2), so
N-n^T S n >=(N-lambda1)z^2+lambda1(1-z^2)
             >=(N/2)(1-z^2).
Thus V(n)>=N(1-z^2)/8. Averaging the c/sqrt(V) upper bound over z uniform
on [-1,1] is legitimate: its polar singularities are integrable. It gives

 average_n Pr(any chosen atom |n)
 <= c sqrt(8/N)*(1/2) integral_-1^1 (1-z^2)^(-1/2)dz
 = pi^(3/2)/(2sqrt N)<3/sqrt N.

If lambda1<=N/2, then V>=N/8 and the corresponding bound is sqrt(pi/N),
which is smaller. Convexity extends the universal 3/sqrt N atom bound to
all fully separable states, including mixed local products and correlated
classical mixtures of nonidentical products. It is a bound on this local
randomized-axis measurement, not merely on identical-product mixtures.

For N even and L>=0, the event |J_n|<=L contains at most 2 floor(L)+1 atoms.
The same proof gives the more useful central-band inequality

 Pr_sigma(|J_n|<=L, axis averaged)
 <= min(1, 3(2floor(L)+1)/sqrt N)                       (W1)

for every fully separable sigma. Its implementation consists of a shared
random axis and N single-qubit measurements, with finite-axis precision a
separate implementation cost. The analytical witness is exact.

Consequently L04's sector limit Pr_rho(E)->c_*=0.6220455028... gives
inf_sep T(rho_N,sep)>=c_*-o(1) with the stronger finite-size separable error
3/sqrt N. The original 8/sqrt N conclusion survives. L04's noncompact
anisotropy, macroscopic ferromagnetic scaling, and sufficient compiler-cost
blowup are separate claims and are not converted into general lower bounds
on all growing-bound algorithms by this repair.
