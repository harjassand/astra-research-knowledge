CYCLE07 NONLINEAR REFERENCE ATTACK: EXACT GAP AND A FAILED SHORTCUT

This is separate from the immutable independently derived stationary C2
baseline and the exposed root-origin pi extension. It gives an exact nonlinear
partial-trace reduction and an exact counterexample to a tempting stronger
sufficient inequality. It does NOT refute the complete weak endpoint.

1. Exact entropy and energy decomposition
-----------------------------------------
Fix one legal qubit Hermitian noise N_0, s_B=sqrt(sigma_B),
V=ell_(s_B)^(-1)(N_0 s_B N_0), and H(X)={V,X}/2-N_0 X N_0. Its physical
Schrodinger jump and drift operators are

  K=s_B^(1/2)N_0 s_B^(-1/2),
  A=s_B^(1/2)V s_B^(-1/2).

The Lyapunov equation implies A+A^*=2K^*K, hence A=K^*K+iQ for Q=Q^*.
The positive Schrodinger generator is

  L_*(rho)={K^*K,rho}/2-K rho K^*+(i/2)[Q,rho].

Let rho_RB>0 and rho_B=Tr_R rho_RB. Use I(R:B)=S(rho_R)+S(rho_B)-S(rho_RB).
Since the reference marginal is untouched and its log stationary term cancels,

  J_RB(rho)-J_B(rho_B)
      =-d/dt I(R:B)_(id_R tensor exp(-tL_*))(rho)|_(t=0)
      =: I_diss(K;rho).                               (1)

The local Hamiltonian term contributes zero to mutual-information dissipation.
The remaining stationary-log terms are linear in rho_B and cancel between the
joint and marginal quantities. With q=sqrt(rho),

  E_RB(rho)=Tr[V rho_B]-Tr[(I_R tensor N_0)q(I_R tensor N_0)q].

Define Wigner-Yanase skew information

  WY_N(rho)=Tr[N^2rho]-Tr[N sqrt(rho)N sqrt(rho)]
           =(1/2)||[N,sqrt(rho)]||_HS^2.

The difference between E and WY is the linear potential Tr[(V-N_0^2)rho_B].
Consequently

  E_RB(rho)-E_B(rho_B)
     =WY_(I_R tensor N_0)(rho)-WY_(N_0)(rho_B)=:Delta WY.  (2)

Combining (1)-(2), with F=J-2E,

  F_RB(rho)=F_B(rho_B)+I_diss(K;rho)-2Delta WY.         (3)

This is an exact reference interface. The already proved scalar qubit theorem
makes F_B(rho_B)>=0, but it cannot simply be discarded from (3).

2. Exact partial-trace quasi-entropy interface
---------------------------------------------
For a fixed matrix C and positive A,B, use the primary-source normalization

  Q_1(C;A,B)=Tr[CC^* A log A]-Tr[C^* A C log B],
  Q_(1/2)(C;A,B)=4(Tr[C^*AC]-Tr[C^*sqrt(A)C sqrt(B)]).

Let Delta_R mean the joint value with I_R tensor C minus the marginal value
with C. Direct substitution gives

  I_diss(K;rho)=Delta_R Q_1(K^*;rho,rho),
  Delta WY=(1/4)Delta_R Q_(1/2)(N_0;rho,rho).           (4)

Thus the FULL one-noise reference target is exactly

  Delta_R Q_1(K^*;rho,rho)
      -(1/2)Delta_R Q_(1/2)(N_0;rho,rho)
                                        >=-F_B(rho_B). (5)

The inequality with zero on the right is stronger and is false below.
Joint convexity/partial-trace monotonicity of each Q_p alone does not compare
these two gaps, whose observables K^* and N_0 differ.

Primary source actually read for normalization and convexity context:
Anna Jencova and Mary Beth Ruskai, A Unified Treatment of Convexity of Relative
Entropy and Related Trace Functions, with Conditions for Equality,
arXiv:0903.2895v4, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.2895v4 . The defining formulas
are (6)-(7); Theorem 2 gives joint convexity; the partial-trace result is
Theorem 7 / formula (28). The reference reduction and counterexample here are
derived directly, not taken from that source. No historical novelty claim.

3. Exact faithful classical-reference counterexample to the stronger shortcut
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Choose

  sigma_B=diag(9/10,1/10), alpha=log(3),
  N_0=sigma_x,
  K=[[0,sqrt(3)],[1/sqrt(3),0]],
  n=(sqrt(3)/2,0,1/2),
  rho(r)=(I+r[(sqrt(3)/2)sigma_x+(1/2)sigma_z])/2,
  0<r<1.

The ray n is K's +1 pure eigenstate. The legal weighted H has eigenvalues
0,2/3,8/3,10/3, so the physical qubit QMS is primitive. Every rho(r) above is
faithful. Exact qubit formulas, or direct two-by-two calculation, give

  J(r)= (4/3)(r-1)atanh(r)+(log(3)/3)(8-5r),
  E(r)= (2/3)(1-r)+(1/4)(1-sqrt(1-r^2)),
  F(r)=J(r)-2E(r).

Its exact second derivative is

  F''(r)=8/[3(1-r)(1+r)^2]-1/[2(1-r^2)^(3/2)]
        =1/[2(1-r^2)^(3/2)]
                       [(16/3)sqrt((1-r)/(1+r))-1].   (6)

Therefore F''(r)<0 whenever r>247/265. Let a two-valued classical reference have
equal weights and conditional states rho(989/1000),rho(991/1000). Its marginal
is rho(99/100), and the entire closed interval between these two parameters
lies above 247/265. Strict concavity from (6) proves exactly

  [F(989/1000)+F(991/1000)]/2-F(99/100)<0.             (7)

For a classical reference, the left side of (7) equals
I_diss(K;rho_RB)-2Delta WY by (3). Thus the proposed stronger shortcut
I_diss>=2Delta WY is false with faithful states and a legal primitive qubit
generator. Equivalently, the zero-right-hand-side version of (5) fails.

This counterexample does NOT violate J_RB>=2E_RB. Its classical-reference
endpoint follows from the independently proved scalar theorem, and the
positive marginal buffer in (3) covers the negative additional term. Replacing
(5) by the stronger zero-buffer claim would discard an essential quantity.

4. Why the stationary theorem cannot simply be embedded at rho_RB
----------------------------------------------------------------
For a primitive local qubit generator, ker(L_*)=span{sigma_B}. Hence every
stationary joint matrix of id_R tensor L_* is A_R tensor sigma_B, and every
stationary joint density is tau_R tensor sigma_B. A general entangled rho_RB
is not in this stationary family. Replacing the product stationary state by
rho_RB would violate the premises of the complete local pi theorem.

An embedding with a genuinely new enlarged KMS generator might still be useful,
but it must prove stationarity and CP and identify its Hessian with the original
NONLINEAR F. Adding new generator components changes J and E; a local Hessian
bound alone supplies no such identification. The exact remaining obligation
is (5), or the equivalent local-noise quadratic block gate in the separate
NONLINEAR_BLOCK_REDUCTION.txt. Both remain UNRESOLVED.
