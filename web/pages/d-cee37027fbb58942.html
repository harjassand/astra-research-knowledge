## 2. Incident-check algebra: the sharper exact reduction

For a general disturbed region X let A_X be the checks touching X, S_t their generated Pauli subgroup, and

    J_X = Alg(S_t, Pauli(X)).                                  (4)

This algebra is supported in R_X=union_(a in A_X) supp(C_a), with X included if necessary. It commutes with every untouched original check. Let L_X be the algebra generated by all stabilizer Paulis belonging to J_X. Suppose X is erasure-correctable for the ORIGINAL stabilizer code, equivalently any Pauli supported on X that centralizes all checks is a stabilizer.

Every Pauli monomial in J_X is S P, S in S_t, P supported X. If it commutes with every member of S_t, P commutes with touched checks. P already commutes with untouched checks because their supports avoid X. Correctability therefore makes P a global stabilizer; hence S P is also a stabilizer. Pauli expansions and their orthogonality then prove

    J_X intersect Alg(S_t)' = L_X.                             (5)

L_X is abelian because all its Pauli generators are global stabilizers, and Alg(S_t) is a subalgebra of L_X. Thus

    J_X intersect L_X' = L_X.                                 (6)

So L_X is a MASA of the finite-dimensional C*-algebra J_X; in particular it contains the center of J_X. This is an algebra statement within J_X, not a claim that all operators on R_X can be diagonalized using only incident checks. For a general X, L_X need not equal Alg(S_t): an additional stabilizer supported on X may occur. That distinction is retained.

If X_i,X_j are disjoint and no original check touches both, every generator of J_i commutes with every generator of J_j: original checks commute globally, Pauli(X_i) and Pauli(X_j) are disjoint, and a touched check for one region avoids the other. Therefore

    [J_i,J_j]=0.                                              (7)

The enlarged supports R_i may overlap. This does not invalidate(7). Conversely a shared touched check can invalidate(7), so an overlapping all-site perturbation is outside the theorem.

For a selected singleton x_i in the ACTUAL family, any Pauli P on x_i centralizing all checks is identity, because that qubit has both CSS colors. Therefore L_i=Alg(S_t,i) exactly. This also proves singleton correctability directly, independently of a numerical global-distance exponent.

## 3. C*-blocks, eigenvalue matching and complete multiplicities

Let H0_i=sum_(a in A_i) J_a D_a and H_i=H0_i+V_i. Both belong to J_i and H0_i belongs to its MASA L_i. Every finite-dimensional C*-algebra has a faithful representation

    D_i := U_i H_i U_i^* - H0_i in L_i,
    ||D_i|| <= ||V_i|| <= epsilon.                             (8)

The last inequality is the finite-dimensional Weyl/min-max inequality INSIDE each central block; it is not an unrestricted permutation of eigenvalues across center labels. ## 9. Safe generalizations and precise remaining obstacle

For bounded general regions X_i, correctability of X_i and the no-shared-original-check condition still give(4)-(10). Their MASA can include additional stabilizers supported inside X_i; local ORIGINAL-generator filling then supplies a common check algebra and a finite congestion B, giving kappa=J_min-2B epsilon. The singletons avoid this toll. A separate safe proof below allows pairwise-disjoint enlarged R_i using the full restriction algebra.

The present theorem does NOT sequentially diagonalize overlapping quantum islands, does NOT cover a generic perturbation touching every check, and does NOT bound generator differences under a small H perturbation. The decisive next falsifier is an admissible region arrangement for which the incident algebras fail to commute, or a common-check congestion that grows with N. Extending this positive-density class to genuinely overlapping interactions requires a new compatible local-algebra/cluster argument, not a reuse of Weyl matching or ground-band dressing.

### Appendix A. Independently derived restriction-algebra theorem

Suppose enlarged regions R_i are pairwise disjoint AND each R_i is erasure-correctable. Let T_i be the algebra generated by restrictions to R_i of every untouched original check. These restricted Pauli operators can anticommute; T_i is NOT assumed abelian. H_i belongs to A_i=T_i'. Let B_i=Alg(S_Ri), the global stabilizers supported on R_i. B_i is abelian and lies A_i. A Pauli in A_i commuting with B_i centralizes untouched global checks by restriction and touched checks because each touched check is fully supported in R_i and belongs to S_Ri. Correctability of R_i makes that Pauli a member of S_Ri. Pauli expansion proves A_i intersect B_i'=B_i; hence B_i is a MASA of A_i including every center block. Blockwise sorted matching gives U_i in A_i, D_i in B_i and(8). Pairwise disjoint R_i yields exact global reduction(10). Local ORIGINAL-generator filling bounds the check congestion for D_i.

Correctability of X_i alone does NOT establish the full restriction-algebra claim in this appendix. The sharper crossed-product proof uses only J_i, which is why it needs the weaker X_i hypothesis. Noncommuting restricted untouched checks and all physical multiplicities are retained in both arguments.
