QUANTITATIVE CHARGE CERTIFICATE FOR CONJUGATE FEEDBACK
12 October 2026. Root theorem; internal proof, priority UNKNOWN.

This extends the independent inventor's phase response beyond positive individual
edge charges. It does not claim that real physical networks satisfy the certificate
or that the certificate can be acquired without inspecting a large graph.

Model. On a finite directed stochastic network P, let unit phases update at rate
one by x_v <- y^(c_vw) x_w, w~P(v,.), with integer edge charges c_vw, of either
sign. The common phase has Brownian generator gamma partial_theta^2. A local row
sampler returns both w and its integer charge. Rational initial phases and gamma
are assumed only for the exact-rational evaluator, not for the response theorem.

Certificate. Supply AND VERIFY rational numbers a_v and delta>0 such that

      c_vw+a_v-a_w >= delta       whenever P_vw>0.             (1)

Let B=max_v a_v-min_v a_v, N0=ceil(2B/delta), and choose the least positive integer
L with gamma delta^2 L^3>=4. Put R=N0+L. The actual costs of supplying/verifying
the support, potential, B and delta are C_cert, not zero.

Theorem. The integrated response h_v=integral_0^infinity E[x_v(t)]dt exists and
solves -L_generator h_v=x_v. Uniformly over the initial phases,

      |h_v| <=4R.

An exact rational complex unbiased evaluator uses fewer than 8R expected local
row calls and has second moment at most64R^2, under rational row/coordinate access.
Its exact arithmetic cost is charged separately. For bounded charge magnitude C,
it is polynomial in the declared row/phase bit bounds, C and R. It is not promised
polynomial in log C: a rational phase raised to an enormous binary-encoded charge
can have output bit length proportional to C. No global mixing bound is used.

Proof. For an algorithmic P-walk W, let K_0=0 and K_j=sum_(l<j)c_(W_l,W_(l+1)).
Summing (1) telescopes:

      K_j >= delta j-a_(W_0)+a_(W_j) >=delta j-B.

For j>=N0, K_j>=delta j/2. Define d(k)=1+gamma k^2 and
A_n=product_(j=0)^n d(K_j)^(-1). Partition j=1,...,n into full consecutive blocks
of length R. In any block, use only its final L indices j=qR+N0+l, 1<=l<=L.
Each such j is at least N0 and K_j>=delta j/2>=delta l/2. Therefore

 product_(j in block)d(K_j)
 >=1+(gamma delta^2/4)sum_(l=1)^L l^2
 >=1+gamma delta^2 L^3/12 >=4/3.

All omitted factors are at least one, so the deterministic pathwise bound is

                 A_n <=(3/4)^floor(n/R).                     (2)

Direct generator calculation gives

 L_generator(x_v y^k)=-(1+gamma k^2)x_v y^k
                      +sum_wP_vw x_w y^(k+c_vw).

Consequently

 h_v= sum_(n>=0)E[A_n y^(K_n)x_(W_n)]                        (3)

is a convergent weighted Neumann series. With finite maximum charge magnitude,
its first two theta derivatives converge uniformly by (2). The finite network's
jump operator is bounded. Telescoping partial sums prove -L_generator h_v=x_v;
the remainder is bounded by (2). The associated rate-one charge/P-walk killed at
rate gamma K^2 has mean lifetime sum_n E A_n<=4R and dominates the absolute
first moment. Duhamel iteration, followed by this finite lifetime bound, proves
the actual integrated-response interpretation. All stationary x_v means vanish.

For evaluation, independently draw M with P(M=m)=(1/8)(7/8)^m and J uniform on
{0,...,R-1}, set N=RM+J, and follow one P-walk of length N, retaining its charges
and A_N. Return A_N y^(K_N)x_(W_N) / [(1/(8R))(7/8)^M]. This is unbiased for (3).
By (2) its magnitude is at most8R(6/7)^M<=8R. Its second moment is at most64R^2,
and E N=7R+(R-1)/2<8R. For rational data it uses only exact integer/rational
operations, fair bits, rational row sampling and finite powers of rational unit
phases. This proves the claimed interface and leaves row/bit acquisition explicit.

Why negative edges are genuinely allowed. On two vertices, take the deterministic
cycle with charges +2 and -1. Set delta=1/2, a_0=0,a_1=3/2. Both inequalities (1)
hold with equality. B=3/2 and N0=6, so R=O(1+gamma^-1/3). This gives a cubic-root
response bound despite a conjugate edge. In contrast the (+1,-1) cycle has total
charge zero and cannot satisfy (1); its response is inverse-gamma.

Acquisition and counterexamples. For an explicitly enumerated finite graph,
condition (1) is the classical system of difference constraints
a_w-a_v<=c_vw-delta. It is feasible exactly when every directed cycle has mean
charge at least delta. Bellman--Ford can obtain/check a potential with polynomial
graph work; computing a positive lower mean-cycle bound is also a graph problem.
Neither operation is independent of graph size. A mere row sampler cannot verify
all positive-probability support edges from finitely many sampled transitions.
Support enumeration or a sound symbolic support/certificate verifier is required.
A succinct symbolic proof of (1) is a different acquisition interface and must be
supplied/derived explicitly.

Qualitative nonzero winding is insufficient. The auditor's deterministic cycle
with charges (+1,-1) repeated m times, followed by +1, has one simple cycle of
positive total charge. For its first2m steps charge alternates0/1. At m~1/gamma,
the response is Omega(1/gamma). Here delta can be at most1/(2m+1), and the cost
N0+L detects the long neutral transient; the theorem does not claim uniform
cubic-root acceleration for this family.
