THEOREM

 H_s=Sym^s(C^d), D_s=binomial(s+d-1,d-1), tau_s=Tr/D_s.
Let T_phys be the ordinary partial trace from H_n to H_k. Its output is
supported on H_k for EVERY input on H_n. Let R_phys be its tracial-reference
Petz map, namely the standard universal k->n cloning map

 R_phys(X)=(D_k/D_n) P_n(X tensor I^(n-k))P_n              (1)

restricted to H_n. Define Phi=R_phys T_phys. Then Phi is CP, UCP, TP,
positive selfadjoint in L2(tau_n), fixes I_n/D_n, and is actually
selfcompatible on its entire input algebra.

The coherent-state measure-prepare channel

 E_n(A)=D_n integral <psi^n|A|psi^n> |psi^n><psi^n| dmu(psi), (2)

where mu is normalized Haar projective measure, is EB, UCP, TP, positive
selfadjoint in L2(tau_n), and obeys

 I-E_n <= [n/(n-k)](I-Phi) <=2(I-Phi).                    (3)

No bound depends on d,n,number of promised states, or the number of POVM
outcomes. The displayed map is supplied by the explicit symmetric-sector
interface; an arbitrary selfcompatible channel is not represented by it.

Consequences:
(a) For every bounded-likelihood family rho_H=(I+H)/D_n, H=H*, tau_n H=0,
||H||<=theta, and r=sup_H ||Phi H-H||_(1,tau_n), a SINGLE E_n gives

 sup_H T(E_n(rho_H),rho_H) <=(1/2)sqrt([n/(n-k)] theta r). (4)

(b) For ALL Hermitian contractions A, let m>=1. The actual EB map E_n Phi^m

 <=sqrt([n/(n-k)] (2m)^(2m)/(2m+1)^(2m+1))

Hence the ENTIRE power range lifts uniformly to EB in this subclass,

(c) The same c<=2 persists under finite tensors, mixtures, conjugations and

1. ACTUAL CHANNEL, REFERENCE AND BROADCASTER
For a normalized psi in C^d write P_(s,psi)=|psi^s><psi^s|. An elementary
Haar moment calculation gives

 integral P_(s,psi) dmu(psi)=I_s/D_s.                     (6)

For completeness choose the occupation basis indexed by a=(a_1,...,a_d),
sum a_i=s. Coherent coefficients are sqrt(s!/prod a_i!) prod psi_i^a_i.
Independent phases kill off-diagonal occupation entries. For the diagonal,
Haar squared moduli t_i=|psi_i|^2 have Dirichlet(1,...,1) distribution and

 integral prod t_i^a_i dmu=(d-1)! prod a_i!/(s+d-1)!.

Multiplication by the multinomial coefficient leaves 1/D_s, proving (6).
Since T_phys(P_(n,psi))=P_(k,psi), (6) shows
 T_phys(I_n/D_n)=I_k/D_k exactly.

Define the likelihood-coordinate map T=(D_k/D_n)T_phys. It is CP, unital
and tau-preserving. Its L2(tau) adjoint is the ordinary Heisenberg map
 T^dagger(A)=P_n(A tensor I^(n-k))P_n.
This is UCP. The physical map associated with T^dagger is R_phys in (1),
which is CPTP because the references in (6) are exactly fixed. Thus
Phi=T^dagger T, an actual UCP TP positive selfadjoint endomorphism.

To exhibit selfcompatibility, split the n symmetric constituents into
k,k,n-2k groups and trace the last group. This is an all-input CPTP channel
 B_0:B(H_n)->B(H_k tensor H_k).
Every fully symmetric vector lies in H_k tensor H_k tensor H_(n-2k), so
there is no hidden postselection or support failure. Both marginals of B_0
are T_phys by permutation symmetry. Now use

 B=(R_phys tensor R_phys) B_0.                           (7)

Its two marginals are precisely Phi. This supplies actual selfcompatibility;
a Choi-extendibility relaxation is not substituted. The faithful reference
is I_n/D_n. On the endomorphism algebra physical and likelihood maps coincide.

2. COHERENT MEASUREMENT AND ITS COMPLETE SPECTRUM
Let S_s(A)(psi)=<psi^s|A|psi^s> and
 M_s(f)=D_s integral f(psi)P_(s,psi) dmu(psi).
In normalized Hilbert spaces S_s=M_s^dagger, and E_s=M_s S_s. Therefore
E_s is positive selfadjoint. Equation (6) makes it UCP and TP. Its physical
implementation is the POVM D_s P_(s,psi)dmu followed by preparation of
P_(s,psi), so it is EB. Schwarz gives 0<=E_s<=I.

The classical operator B_s=S_s M_s has the kernel

 (B_s f)(x)=D_s integral |<x,y>|^(2s) f(y)dmu(y).         (8)

We derive its COMPLETE spectrum, rather than using only a fixed angular
mode or a nonuniform semiclassical expansion.

Let P_l be the functions on complex projective space obtained by restricting
bihomogeneous polynomials of degree (l,l) in z and conjugate z to ||z||=1.
Its complex dimension is D_l^2. Restriction is injective, because vanishing
on the sphere plus homogeneity gives vanishing at every nonzero z. It contains
P_(l-1) via multiplication by ||z||^2. Put
 H_l=P_l orthogonal-complement P_(l-1) in L2(mu), P_(-1)={0}.
Then

 h_l=dim H_l=D_l^2-D_(l-1)^2.                            (9)

This definition avoids importing an unproved representation decomposition.
Each space is invariant under unitary transformations. Let K_l(x,y) be its
orthogonal-projection reproducing kernel. By unitary transitivity,
K_l(x,x)=h_l. Fix x=e_1. Its stabilizer U(1) times U(d-1) makes K_l(e_1,y)
a polynomial of degree at most l in t=|y_1|^2. It is orthogonal to every
lower-degree polynomial in t, because those belong to P_(l-1). The law of t
is (d-1)(1-t)^(d-2)dt on [0,1]. Thus the normalized kernel is the unique
normalized orthogonal polynomial for that weight:

 K_l(x,y)=h_l Z_l(|<x,y>|^2),
 Z_l(t)=P_l^(d-2,0)(2t-1)/P_l^(d-2,0)(1),              (10)

where the P on the right is a Jacobi polynomial (not the function space).
This also proves the needed projective Funk-Hecke identity directly.
Indeed, for fixed x, the polynomial kernel D_s t^s is in P_s and is invariant
under its stabilizer. Its expansion uses K_0(x,.),...,K_s(x,.), which form
an orthogonal basis of the invariant polynomials of degree <=s. Consequently
B_s acts as a scalar b_l(s) on H_l and vanishes on P_s-perpendicular, with

 b_l(s)=D_s(d-1) integral_0^1 t^s(1-t)^(d-2) Z_l(t)dt.  (11)

No growing-number-of-modes approximation is being used.

Here is an exact evaluation. Let alpha=d-2. The Rodrigues identity is

 (1-t)^alpha P_l^(alpha,0)(2t-1)
   =(-1)^l/l! [d^l/dt^l][t^l(1-t)^(l+alpha)].

For l<=s, integrate by parts l times in (11). Boundary terms vanish.
The resulting beta integral is

 integral t^s(1-t)^alpha P_l^(alpha,0)(2t-1)dt
  = [s!/(l!(s-l)!)] integral t^s(1-t)^(l+alpha)dt
  = (s!)^2(l+alpha)!/[l!(s-l)!(s+l+alpha+1)!].

Since P_l^(alpha,0)(1)=(l+alpha)!/[l!alpha!] and
D_s=(s+d-1)!/[s!(d-1)!], cancellations yield

 b_l(s)=s!(s+d-1)!/[(s-l)!(s+l+d-1)!]
       =prod_(j=0)^(l-1) (s-j)/(s+d+j), 0<=l<=s,        (12)
 b_l(s)=0 for l>s, b_0(s)=1.

The eigenvalues are positive for l<=s. Also
 <M_s f,M_s g>_(tau_s)=<f,B_s g>_(mu).
Thus M_s maps every H_l, l<=s, injectively into B(H_s), and different
images are orthogonal. Their total dimension telescopes by (9) to D_s^2,
the full operator-space dimension. They therefore give an EXACT complete
orthogonal decomposition of the entire input algebra, not just selected
coherent-state directions. On M_s H_l, E_s has eigenvalue b_l(s).

3. SPLIT/RECOVER SPECTRUM
T M_n=M_k holds directly by integrating T_phys(P_(n,psi))=P_(k,psi) and

 T^dagger M_k f=[b_l(k)/b_l(n)] M_n f.

 lambda_l=b_l(k)/b_l(n) for l<=k; lambda_l=0 for l>k.    (13)

Equations (12)-(13) reconstruct all singular values of the true partial

4. THE UNIFORM DIRICHLET INEQUALITY
The next argument actually works for any 0<k<n; selfcompatibility (7) uses
2k<=n. Put t=k/n in (0,1), r=n/k, and for 0<=j<l<=k let

 a_j=(k-j)/(k+d+j), p_j=(n-j)/(n+d+j).

A direct cross multiplication shows

 a_j <= p_j/[r-(r-1)p_j],                               (14)

because the difference of cross-multiplied positive denominators equals
(d+2j)(n-rk+(r-1)j)=(d+2j)(r-1)j>=0.
For 0<=p<=1,

 1/[r-(r-1)p] <= [1+(r-1)p]/r =t+(1-t)p,               (15)

since [r-(r-1)p][1+(r-1)p]-r=(r-1)^2 p(1-p)>=0.
For ANY finite sequence p_j in [0,1],

 prod_j [t+(1-t)p_j] <=t+(1-t)prod_j p_j.               (16)

Proof: the two-factor difference is exactly
 t(1-t)(1-p_1)(1-p_2)>=0, then induct.
Combining (12)-(16) gives

 lambda_l=prod_j (a_j/p_j) <=t+(1-t)b_l(n).

For l>k the left side is zero, so this is still valid. For l=0 both sides
are one. On the COMPLETE orthogonal decomposition from Section 2 this
proves Phi<=t I+(1-t)E_n as Hilbert-space operators, equivalently

 I-E_n <= [1/(1-t)](I-Phi).

This is (3). In particular it preserves the slow classical/semiclassical
modes and does not replace Phi by its exact fixed-point projection.
For l=1 the ratio of defects is exactly

 [1-b_1(n)]/[1-lambda_1]
       =n(k+d)/[(n+d)(n-k)] -> n/(n-k) as d->infinity.

Thus the comparator constant is uniform-sharp for this explicit E_n.

5. TRACE RECONSTRUCTION AND ENTIRE CP-RANGE LIFT
Because 0<=E_n<=I, (I-E_n)^2<=I-E_n. For bounded Hermitian H,

 ||(I-E_n)H||_2^2 <= c <H,(I-Phi)H>
                   <= c ||H||_infinity ||H-Phi H||_1,
 c=n/(n-k)<=2.

Normalized trace L1 is at most L2. Multiplying by 1/2 gives (4).
For A a Hermitian contraction, put X=Phi^m A. The same inequality gives

 ||(I-E_n)Phi^m A||_2^2
 <= c <A,Phi^m(I-Phi)Phi^m A>
 <= c max_(0<=lambda<=1) [(1-lambda)lambda^(2m)].

The maximum occurs at lambda=2m/(2m+1) and equals
(2m)^(2m)/(2m+1)^(2m+1). Since composition with E_n is EB, the map
E_n Phi^m is an actual full-domain EB comparator. This proves (5).
No growing-dimension trace-to-diamond conversion is used.

6. FINITE OUTCOMES, ACQUISITION AND COST BOUNDARIES
The integral channel E_n is finite-dimensional. Caratheodory's theorem on
the real vector space of Hermitian Choi matrices yields an exact finite
weighted coherent-state decomposition with at most D_n^4+1 nodes. This is
existence, not a free efficiently acquired design or a runnable compiler.

One explicit finite quadrature is also available, with a deliberately large
charged alphabet. Use stick-breaking coordinates
 t_1=u_1, t_2=(1-u_1)u_2,...,t_d=prod_(j<d)(1-u_j),
where u_j has the beta density (d-j)(1-u_j)^(d-j-1). Use 2n+1 equally spaced
phases independently in each of d-1 relative phases. In each u_j use the
positive (n+1)-node Gauss-Jacobi quadrature for its beta weight.
The number of nodes is

 N_quad=[(n+1)(2n+1)]^(d-1).                             (17)

Every matrix entry of the superoperator |P_(n,psi)><P_(n,psi)| has phase
frequencies at most 2n. Phase averaging is therefore EXACT with this grid.
Surviving terms have integer powers of the t_i, of total degree 2n.
After stick breaking they are polynomials of degree <=2n separately in
each u_j. The chosen Gauss-Jacobi rules integrate them exactly. Thus this
constructs the complete channel E_n with positive finite weights, not
merely a selected low harmonic. Completeness of the finite POVM follows by
applying the resulting exact map to I_n. For d=2 it uses O(n^2) outcomes;
for growing d (17) is exponential in d and is expressly NOT efficient in
the logarithm of Hilbert dimension. Algebraic root and preparation data,
exact or finite-bit approximation, must be computed and charged.

No certified root-isolation/quantum-gate/outcome compiler or hardware run

A finite-bit implementation would have to charge approximating N_quad
