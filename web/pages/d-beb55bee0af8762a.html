## 1. Recommended: all-dimensional uniform log-order permanence

### Exact candidate theorem

Let E be finite, with nonzero reaction vectors nu_e in R^d, S=span{nu_e}, and consider xdot=sum_e nu_e w_e(t,x) on the positive orthant. Assume Caratheodory well-posedness (measurable t, locally uniform Lipschitz control in x on positive compact sets). For every admitted rate path, suppose there are continuous tau_e:[-1,1]^d -> R and epsilon(h)->0 such that, uniformly over paths, t>=0, e, and p in [-1,1]^d,

  | log w_e(t,h^p)/log h - tau_e(p) | <= epsilon(h),  h downarrow 0.

For each r in [-1,1]^d, let E_r={e:r dot nu_e != 0}. Assume E_r nonempty implies every minimizer of tau_e(r) over e in E_r has r dot nu_e>0; include every lower-dimensional face, including directions where some reaction projections vanish. Candidate conclusion: in every positive class P=(c+S) intersect R^d_{>0}, there is one nonempty compact convex forward-invariant K_P subset P that absorbs every positive trajectory in finite time, with the entry time uniform over admitted rate paths (but dependent on the initial condition).

This includes ordinary weakly reversible mass-action systems: tau_e(p)=y_e dot p, and the minimum-source/return-path geometry supplies the inward-minimizer condition. More broadly it allows time-measurable rates and unbounded subpower distortions such as exp(c_e(1+sum_i log^2 x_i)^(1/4)); their log-order error is O((|log h|)^(-1/2)). The conclusion is a deterministic permanence/absorber theorem, not a stochastic or spatial result.

### Historical obstruction and primary prior art

The permanence conjecture asks whether every weakly reversible mass-action network is permanent in each positive compatibility class, with one eventual compact bound for all initial states in that class. Established special cases include two-species/endotactic networks (Craciun–Nazarov–Pantea, SIAM J. Appl. Math. 73 (2013), DOI 10.1137/100812355), strongly endotactic networks (Gopalkrishnan–Miller–Shiu, SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst. 13 (2014), DOI 10.1137/130928170), and weakly reversible networks with one linkage class (Boros–Hofbauer, SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst. 19 (2020), DOI 10.1137/19M1248431). These leave the multi-linkage arbitrary-dimensional interface historically consequential. The pinned OpenAI Oct. 5, 2026 preprint claims the fixed-positive-rate weakly-reversible theorem; it is a candidate source premise here, not external validation. Brunner–Craciun already prove two-dimensional tropically endotactic permanence even for time-dependent parameters (SIAM J. Appl. Math. 78 (2018), DOI 10.1137/17M1133762), so novelty must be isolated to the all-dimensional uniform log-order transfer and its precise rate class.

### Mechanism and critical missing lemma

The proof selects one finite, h-independent family of affine functions F_h(x)=min_lambda(r_lambda dot x+b_lambda(h)) whose active slope at x=h^p approximates p to any prescribed positive, possibly discontinuous slope-dependent tolerance E(r). The tolerance shrinks near reaction-normal hyperplanes, so sign and strict order survive even at ties and tangent faces. Uniform log-order gaps then make the lowest-order inward flux dominate every negative projected flux. F_h has a compact plateau around a class representative; its value increases at a uniformly positive scale-dependent rate outside that plateau. Nested boxes link the plateaux at successive scales (the plateaux themselves need not be nested), producing a common classwise absorber.

The load-bearing imported statement is the **arbitrary-tolerance affine-minimum lemma**: for any dimension m, interval [a,b], and completely arbitrary E:[a,b]^m -> (0,infinity), produce a fixed finite set of slopes and h-dependent offsets b_lambda(h)=o(h^a) such that every label remaining active along h_n->0 and p_n->p has ||p-r_lambda||_infinity < E(r_lambda). Audit ties, discontinuous E, p_n approaching a boundary from outside the interval, and uniform quantifiers. N173 reconstructs a full downstream proof conditional on this lemma; the pinned OpenAI source includes a long proof of the lemma, but neither has independent specialist/formal validation. A second exact proof obligation is to verify Caratheodory continuation and the classwise plateau viability from the vector field rather than assume it.

### Early falsification / Phase-B action

First, independently prove or break the affine lemma in d=2/3 with a discontinuous tolerance that collapses near coordinate/reaction hyperplanes; check sequences approaching every face from outside and all affine ties. Then audit the active-slope drift estimate and plateau scale-composition with arbitrary uniform time paths. A theorem-level counterexample must satisfy the stated UV bound and strict all-face inwardness while failing class permanence; the known 2D system 0->Y, 2Y->Y, X+Y->Y is **not** such a counterexample because it fails TE exactly on boundary direction r=(r_x>0,0). It is instead a sharp falsifier of generic-directions-only certificates: all open sectors pass although y(0)=1 gives x(t)=x(0)e^{-t}.

If the affine lemma survives, the highest value is an external exact-contract review and independent proof reconstruction, followed by a clear priority comparison with the cited general-kinetics literature. The ASTRA rational face checker is fixed-d only and certifies signs, not an absorber compiler or explicit entry cost.

## 2. Secondary, incremental: two-species order-four stochastic explosion boundary

### Exact candidate theorem

For every finite two-species stochastic mass-action reaction network whose source complexes satisfy |y|<=4 and whose embedded graph is endotactic in every direction, for every fixed positive rate vector and every finite initial state, the CTMC is nonexplosive. Combined with the source-derived order-five explosive/equality phase result, this would establish that the minimum source order for a two-species endotactic explosion is exactly 5. The alternative discovery is one explicit legal all-direction endotactic source-order-4 network with positive-probability explosion from a stated initial state; that would make the minimum 4. This is a crisp finite-order gate, but an incremental boundary result, not a general stochastic reaction-network solution.

Known primary context: Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen exhibit explosive strongly endotactic networks and explain why deterministic permanence does not imply stochastic regularity (SPA 130 (2020), DOI 10.1016/j.spa.2020.07.012; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05328). Xu’s May 2026 v2 proves every second-order endotactic system regular in both CTMC and ODE senses, while remarking the minimum explosive order remains open (arXiv: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2, Thm. 4.6 and Rem. 4.8). Do not conflate the global minimum over all dimensions with the narrower two-species 4-vs-5 gate.

ASTRA’s source-derived current bracket is: order <=3 two-species all-endotactic CTMCs nonexplosive (N228; proof text located, completeness not audited); a source-order-5 two-species strongly-endotactic example has exact rate phase, explosive iff mu>0 and nonexplosive also at equality mu=0 (N227; source proof located, not independently audited); a legal order-4 strongly-endotactic near-miss is nonexplosive (N229), with proper Lyapunov function V(a,b)=a+b+K/(b+1), K>4. N229 shows why the tempting cubic-to-quadratic feedback construction fails: the B-clearing term has cubic source order and an essential pair-loss is required for endotaxis. Removing that arrow violates the all-direction condition; taking its rate to zero is not a fixed-positive-rate counterexample.

### Critical missing lemma and falsifier

For the universal order-4 direction, the missing lemma is a **face-sensitive excursion/physical-clock estimate** valid for every finite product and rate tuple, including disabled factorial propensities on tangent lattice faces, that rules out infinitely many jumps in finite integrated holding time. A proof must control arbitrary finite products and cannot assume irreducibility, conservation, or interior stoichiometric rank. The first falsification check is exhaustive symbolic search over two-species source sets of degree <=4 with target cones constrained by all-direction endotacticity; for any candidate, construct a finite-state path/tree with positive probability and sum its exact upper holding-time bounds. One legal explosive diagram is decisive; a total-direction-only witness or a near-miss is not.

## Screened but not promoted

**Diophantine physical-computation compiler.** N206 constructs a degree-bounded, uniformly strongly convex polynomial gradient flow `xdot=-M grad V` whose positive-integer equilibria encode H10(Z), with bounded monotone trajectories. Multiplying each component by x_i makes negative terms consume species i; Hárs–Tóth’s classical kinetic realization then gives an ordinary mass-action realization and preserves positive equilibrium points. This standard kinetic transformation means the unrestricted equilibrium-decision corollary is not a new compiler discovery. It remains algebraic existential undecidability: no finite-precision, robust, stochastic, or experimentally readable computation follows. The only high-ceiling missing bridge is an effective auxiliary-species weakly-reversible/reversible compiler preserving integer-root equivalence and preventing lattice artifacts. The same-species weak-reversible obstruction in ASTRA’s kinetic gate rules out a trivial route. Boros–Craciun–Yu construct WR/reversible systems with infinitely many positive equilibria by multiplying a base field by a common scalar polynomial, but the construction only supplies a restricted common-factor polynomial, not an arbitrary SOS mobility M. A naive product of separate one-residual gadgets would create a union of zero sets, whereas M=0 encodes the required intersection. Do not promote this as a candidate without a concrete gadget.
