## Exact cyclic obstruction and repair

The nonzero proper free series

    R=x1*x2-x2*(1+x1)^(-1)*x1*(1+x2)

has leading commutator but evaluates to the zero full operator at J_1,J_2, and hence at every finite unmarked T tuple. The formal proof uses differentiation and zero initial values; dimensions 2 through 25 also replay exactly. This refutes the naive cyclic extension even when all matrix entries are read.

Root's length-grading deduction repairs this obstruction: every nonzero proper recognizable m-state series is hit by one of **m predetermined tuples ell*T_i, ell=1,...,m, each of dimension 2nm**. Each evaluation exists by joint nilpotence. The proof bounds a selected derivative numerator's lambda-degree after removing an explicit lambda factor, so it cannot vanish at all m scales; it does not bound the degree of the entire output. One direct-sum tuple has dimension 2nm^2. The two written peer audits reconstruct this theorem. This is a proper-series result and does not assume arbitrary rational-expression domains.

The preregistered exact discriminator executed all 14 scales across three cyclic realizations. A five-state alias at d=20 has the entire ell=1 response zero and diagnostic entry ell^2(ell-1)/24, giving the first hit 1/6 at ell=2. A dense three-state nonzero is detected already at ell=1. A six-state doubled dense cancellation zero remains zero at all six scales. Direct expression evaluation, a separate Neumann evaluator and supplied-realization coefficient truth independently check the main block solve.
