# Coordinate gauge rigidity for scientific equation discovery

Source audited: Trenta, Massidda, Bacciu, Magliacane, *Identifying ODEs from Unstructured Data with Causal Representation Learning*, arXiv:2609.37083v1, 29 September 2026. Exact primary locations: Lemma 3.1; Appendix A.3, Assumptions A.2/A.3, equations (34)–(38), especially equality inference following (37); Appendix G polynomial-training failures. Snapshot in sources/a_prior/cycle3. Status: exact mathematical counterexample and corrected sufficient theorem independently derived; historical novelty unestablished.

## Interface and authors' claim
Latent polynomial ODE f on an open product domain, unknown invertible observation map. A CRL stage supplies coordinates only up to permutation and componentwise diffeomorphisms. Additional componentwise polynomial h is assumed for the theorem, not obtained from CRL in general. For each component take maximal univariate factor q_i(z_i) and primitive remainder r_i. Their Lemma3.1 claims no-cancellation assumptions imply N(transformed r_i)>=N(r_i), and equality implies all h_i(t)=a_i t^{p_i}. This last conclusion is false as written. Their Appendix A.3 separately recognizes shifted powers when q_i has one root; that observation does not repair the explicit equality assertion in the lemma. Polynomial theorem is not directly applicable to MLP experiments: Appendix G says polynomial encoders failed training and MLPs were used.

## Exact counterexample, including all-variable coupling
On z1,z2>1, let f1=(z1−1)(1+z2+z2²), f2=(z2−1)(1+z1+z1²). Set z_i=h_i(t_i)=t_i²+1, t_i>0. These are polynomial diffeomorphisms of the specified open domains. Chain rule gives

 g1=t1(3+3t2²+t2⁴)/2, g2=t2(3+3t1²+t1⁴)/2.

Maximal factors: q_i=z_i−1; transformed q_i=t_i/2 up to irrelevant nonzero normalization. Original and transformed primitive remainders each have exactly3 monomials. Each variable appears in the other component. A.2 holds for all powers because coefficients of h are positive; A.3 holds because every expanded remainder coefficient is positive. Nevertheless h_i has2 monomials. The original *full* vector field has6 terms/component; transformed field has3. Thus optimizing ordinary full polynomial sparsity may actively prefer this alternative origin/power gauge.

Not a finite numerical check: displayed polynomial identities and positivity prove the counterexample for every t_i>0. work/a_prior/cycle3/check_gauge.py additionally verifies exact SymPy identities and term counts.

General family: q_i=z_i−c_i, c_i>0; r_i a strictly positive-coefficient dense polynomial over a rectangular exponent grid in other coordinates. h_i=t_i^{p_i}+c_i, p_i>=2. Composition leaves the grid cardinality unchanged (exponents become coordinatewise multiples of p_j), while q_i(h_i)/h_i'=t_i/p_i. Hence primitive remainder sparsity is unchanged but full sparsity halves. Every coordinate may influence every other coordinate. This is a robust open coefficient family for each support, not a finely tuned cancellation.

## Exact general criterion and independent reconstruction
Work over characteristic0 and extend polynomial identities to C. Write f_i=q_i(z_i)r_i with r_i having no nonconstant univariate factor in z_i; assume nonconstant univariate h_j. Composition r_i(h) remains primitive in t_i: if all its coefficient polynomials vanished at t_i=c, injectivity of substitution in the other variables would imply r_i(h_i(c),z_{−i})≡0, giving a forbidden linear factor. This argument includes complex roots, unlike a proof restricted to real critical points.

Therefore polynomial conjugacy holds iff h_i' divides q_i(h_i), for every i. Its maximal univariate factor is q_i(h_i)/h_i', and primitive remainder is exactly r_i(h), up to normalization.

Let E_i=supp(r_i), H_j=supp(h_j). Under noncancellation A.2/A.3, transformed support is exactly
 U_i=union over ell in E_i of Cartesian product over j of (ell_j H_j),
where ell_j H_j is the ell_j-fold Minkowski sum and 0H_j={0}. This is an exact set criterion, not a term-by-term count with multiplicities. Degree representatives (p_j ell_j)_j inject E_i into U_i, proving the weak sparsity inequality. Equality says |U_i|=|E_i|; it need not force |H_j|=1 because expanded monomials can merge without cancelling. This is the decisive erroneous implication in the author's proof.

## Corrected support-antichain theorem
Assume each E_i is an antichain under coordinatewise order, and union_i E_i covers every coordinate j (some ell_j>0). Then simultaneous equality |U_i|=|E_i| for all i forces every h_j monomial.

Proof. Each ell has representative p⊙ell. If h_j has a nonleading exponent and ell_j>0, expanding that term provides some v<=p⊙ell with v≠p⊙ell. If v were representative p⊙ell' of another original monomial, then ell'<=ell strictly, contradicting the antichain condition. By noncancellation v survives and is an extra term, so |U_i|>|E_i|. Coverage completes the argument. Conversely monomial maps give exactly the original cardinalities whenever polynomial conjugacy divisibility holds. This fully proves a corrected sufficient result for polynomial diffeomorphisms on their permitted domains.

A simple consequential subclass is homogeneous primitive remainders of any fixed total degree: their supports are antichains. This does not cover generic Lotka–Volterra remainders containing both constant and linear terms. For constant q_i, divisibility over C forces h_i' constant, hence h_i affine; together with antichain equality and coverage it forces zero translation. For monomial h_i=a_i t_i^{p_i} with p_i>1, admissibility further requires q_i(0)=0; multiplicity k gives p_i k >=p_i−1 automatically for k>=1. If a global R→R diffeomorphism with nonzero derivative everywhere is required, any such monomial must have p_i=1; domains matter.

## Downstream use and novelty boundary
Use the exact support criterion as a certificate for whether sparse learned equations fix a coordinate gauge. It makes ambiguity reportable before assigning physical meaning to latent variables. The correction is elementary polynomial/support algebra, not yet a foundational breakthrough. Potential larger programme: characterize all sparsity-preserving admissible coordinate maps for a finite polynomial library and design minimal external calibration interventions to distinguish them. Classical conjugacy, polynomial composition, sparse-polynomial powers, and CRL gauge results are prerequisites; no priority claim from absence.

Next falsifiable step: adversarially test support-antichain necessity/sufficiency for general overlapping supports, then prove a noise-stable version under bounded degree, coefficient margins, compact domain, and singular-value bounds. Pure l0 support certificates are discontinuous near zero coefficients, so no uniform stability is possible without a margin. Need charge the acquisition of componentwise CRL coordinates and any origin/scale calibration; do not grant them for free.

## Constructive operational alternative: known additive actuation
A second route avoids sparsity assumptions entirely. Suppose the disentangled latent chart z_i=h_i(t_i) has a C1 diffeomorphism on a product domain. Baseline vector field f and an actuated field f+u share the observation/chart, with known state-independent additive u and every u_i nonzero. Their transformed difference is Delta g_i(t)=u_i/h_i'(t_i). If this difference is constant c_i in the learned chart, h_i'=u_i/c_i is constant and h_i is affine. If the recovered actuation matches the same known physical u, then h_i'=1 and only translations remain. One known physical reference state fixes all translations. This is a direct proof of coordinate calibration, not a claim that one trajectory estimates full vector fields.

Quantitative version: if |Delta g_i(t)−c_i|<=epsilon_i<|c_i| uniformly, then
 |h_i'(t)−u_i/c_i| <= |u_i|epsilon_i/(|c_i|(|c_i|−epsilon_i)).
Integrating on an interval of length L_i yields uniform distance to the affine map anchored at one reference <=L_i times this bound. Thus bounded differential-actuation error transfers to gauge-calibration error with explicit units and failure margin.

One baseline/one simultaneous full-support actuation environment is structurally enough after componentwise disentanglement; coordinatewise actuation is unnecessary for this theorem. However the assumed ability to perturb all physical coordinates additively, estimate paired vector fields over a domain, retain a shared observation map, and know the actuator support/amplitudes is substantive experimental input. With u_i=0 the i-th gauge remains unrestricted by this contrast; this exactly locates the acquisition boundary. Historical novelty unresolved and likely overlaps nonlinear system-identification calibration/controlled CRL. Practical use: choose physically meaningful actuator contrasts to fix the gauge before sparse equation discovery, rather than declare the sparsest origin physically true.
