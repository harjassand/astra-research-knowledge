# Cold review of the acoustic-face obstruction

## Finding

The generalized statement in work/FO-root/ACOUSTIC_FACE_OBSTRUCTION.md is correct under its stated hypotheses. The only wording clarification is that “C² homogeneous quartic” should explicitly mean a homogeneous quartic polynomial; coefficient topology and coefficientwise closure are being used. I found no counterexample with an invertible background and nonzero probe vectors.

The specific F-star-to-G result in work/FO-profile-prior/cone_followup/REPORT.md is a correct specialization. Its exact verifier passed when run from a copy. Independent exact checks reproduced the target acoustic zero and lifted-barycenter witness, checked the F-star acoustic Gram, and verified the coercive constant with exact LDL pivots. The obstruction separates a positive right-orbit/polyconvex synthesis cone; it does not establish non-quasiconvexity of G.

Invertibility is essential. If B0=0, the older F-star law has zero acoustic Hessian there but a negative lifted Jensen gap. This is a counterexample only to a weakened theorem with singular background, not to the stated theorem.

## Proof audit

For an arbitrary coefficient-convergent approximating sequence write

$$
H_n=\sum_i c_{ni}h_{S_{ni}}+P_n,\qquad h_S(A)=F(AS),
$$

where c_ni are nonnegative and P_n is polyconvex. Quartic homogeneity lets us normalize every nonzero S_ni to Frobenius norm one by absorbing its fourth power into c_ni. Singular matrices occur only in the compact closure of this normalized orbit.

Define

$$
\ell(K)=D^2K(B_0)[a_0\otimes b_0,a_0\otimes b_0]
$$

and let Lambda(K) sum the two probes D²K(B0)[a0⊗e_j,a0⊗e_j] in an orthonormal input basis. The chain rule gives

$$
D^2h_S(B_0)[a_0\otimes b,a_0\otimes b]
=D^2F(B_0S)[a_0\otimes(S^Tb),a_0\otimes(S^Tb)].
$$

Since ||B0 S||_F >= sigma_min(B0)||S||_F, the strict bound gives, with m=c sigma_min(B0)^2 |a0|^2 > 0,

$$
\ell(h_S)\ge m|S^Tb_0|^2,\qquad
\Lambda(h_S)\ge m\sum_{j=1}^2|S^Te_j|^2=m.
$$

The same probes are nonnegative on every homogeneous polyconvex quartic: restriction to a rank-one line is convex, and the quartic is C². Positivity of all conic weights and summand probes, coefficient convergence, and ell(H)=0 imply

$$
\sum_i c_{ni}\le \Lambda(H_n)/m=O(1),\qquad
\sum_i c_{ni}|S_{ni}^Tb_0|^2\le \ell(H_n)/m\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus orbit mass is bounded and concentrates at

$$
Z=\{S:||S||_F=1,\ S^Tb_0=0\}.
$$

For any neighborhood U of Z, compactness gives eta_U=min_{S outside U, ||S||_F=1}|S^T b0|>0. Therefore mass outside U is at most ell(H_n)/(m eta_U²) and tends to zero. This quantitatively closes the possible escape to singular right transforms.

Every S in Z has rank one. Its columns lie in the one-dimensional space b0-perpendicular, and normalization excludes rank zero. Write S=v⊗w. Then AS=(Av)⊗w, and every difference of two such arguments has rank at most one. The strict Hessian assumption implies rank-one convexity of F. Its restriction to the subspace {z⊗w} is therefore ordinary convex, so h_S(A)=F(AS) is convex in A. For every finite law, its Jensen functional j_mu(S)=E_mu h_S(A)-h_S(Abar) is nonnegative on Z.

For fixed finite mu, j_mu is continuous and bounded on the compact normalized sphere. Given epsilon>0, choose U so its negative part is at most epsilon on U. Bounded total orbit mass controls the contribution from U; boundedness of j_mu and vanishing mass outside U control the complement. Hence liminf_n sum_i c_ni j_mu(S_ni)>=0.

For each polyconvex P_n, the lifted mean condition E A=Abar and E det A=det Abar allows ordinary convex Jensen in (A,det A), giving J_mu(P_n)>=0. Finally, J_mu is continuous in the finite-dimensional quartic coefficient space because mu has finite support. Passing to H_n -> H proves the claim for every such law. No compactness of the P_n or closedness of the polyconvex subcone is needed.

This checks the assignment's specific proof steps: the chain-rule direction is S^T b; invertible B0 supplies a uniform lower bound; the null set is rank one because the input dimension is two; limiting atoms are convex; and arbitrary finite lifted-barycenter laws enter by Jensen. The proof would not supply these estimates for signed orbit combinations, a zero probe, singular B0, or a generator lacking the strict estimate.

## Exact specialization and adversarial checks

The worker proof uses

$$
F_*(A)=u^2+v^2+4w^2+\tfrac72q^2-\tfrac{29}{10}q(u+v),
$$
$$
G(A)=3(u+v-2q)^2+\tfrac{35}{24}u^2+q^2-\tfrac52qu.
$$

The copied verifier differentiates F-star and checks the exact rational acoustic Gram. Replaying it from source-copy passed and reproduced all eight positive leading principal minors of Q-I/16. The separate check script verifies the same polynomial identity and uses an exact LDL factorization instead; all eight rational pivots are positive and saved in checks/independent_acoustic_face_checks.json. The certificate checks the first output direction. F-star depends on u,v,w,q, which are invariant under left SO(2), so output rotation extends the estimate to every output vector. The exact uniform constant is c=1/16; it is not sampled positivity.

At I, direct substitution gives G(I+t e_12)=-1/24+3t^4, so the acoustic probe is zero. For

$$
\mu=\frac{\delta_{36I}+\delta_{-36I}}{2\cdot1297}
+\frac{1296(\delta_J+\delta_{-J})}{2\cdot1297},
\qquad J=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1),
$$

the exact means are E A=0, E det A=0=det 0, and E G(A)=-1350/1297. This negative Jensen functional contradicts the inequality forced on every cone member by the general theorem. It is an unconstrained finite lifted-barycenter witness, not a compatible-gradient construction.

Independent checks exercise a non-diagonal probe at B0=diag(2,3) and invertible unit-Frobenius S=diag(3/5,4/5). The exact orbit probe is 432144/3125, above its predicted lower bound 73/50; the two-probe sum is 28368/625, above 1/2. The normalized invertible sequence S_n=diag((n²-1)/(n²+1),2n/(n²+1)) for n=2,3,10 approaches Z while the sum-probe lower bound stays 1/16. On the boundary atom S=e1⊗(3/5,4/5), the profile is exactly (913/625)|A e1|^4 and is convex.

The assumption stress test uses the N794 law on ±7I and ±J. It has both lifted means zero and F-star Jensen gap -833/250. If B0=0, H=F-star is already an orbit generator and the acoustic Hessian is zero, contradicting the conclusion of the theorem with that weakened background assumption. Allowing a0=0 or b0=0 likewise makes the probe condition vacuous and gives the same counterexample to that weakened statement. These cases do not challenge the stated theorem. Its bound is for each fixed invertible B0; it is not uniform over backgrounds whose smallest singular value tends to zero.

Negative monomial coefficients in G are not themselves a cone obstruction. The proof uses the acoustic zero, the strict generator bound, and a negative Jensen functional satisfying the lifted constraints. Signed orbit combinations and subtraction of polyconvex terms remain outside the asserted cone.

## Scope

The generalized statement is a positive-transfer obstruction for one strictly acoustic generator under positive right-orbit mixing and arbitrary homogeneous polyconvex quartic addends. It clarifies the constants in the F-star/G specialization and its compactness mechanism. It does not rule out a certificate built from other generators, and it does not turn an unconstrained finite law into a gradient field. Internal replay and exact algebra are not external mathematical review or a priority claim.

No third-party literature, measurements, controls, or physical resources were acquired. The copied verifier and symbolic checks each ran in under one second. Aggregate inference, token, and financial costs are unmeasured.
