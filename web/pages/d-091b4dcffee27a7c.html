STRONGEST RESULT

The proposed ordinary-logconcave local-table extension is valid. In fact the
complementary polynomial satisfies the required logconcavity under the strictly
weaker condition

    beta_j(k) := b_j(k)/k! is logconcave with interval support,
    b_j(k)^2 >= k/(k+1) b_j(k-1)b_j(k+1),  1<=k<q_j.          (C)

This is the maximal independently checkable LOCAL rule which works uniformly
over arbitrary copies of an admitted table and every total particle number.
It is NOT necessary for an individual collection of blocks, even if all total
particle numbers of that collection are considered. Exact fixed-instance
certification is given below.

For a two-mode site b=(1,1,g^2), (C) is g^2<=2, covering the full repulsive range
0<=g<=1 plus weak attraction 1<g<=sqrt(2). For q modes with a pair-count filter
b(k)=a^(k(k-1)/2), (C) is a<=q/(q-1). Thus amplitude g^(k(k-1)/2) is covered
when g^2<=q/(q-1). Both boundaries are sharp in the above universal sense.

The closure proof is a consequence of existing Lorentzian normalization and
polarization, not a new foundational logconcavity theorem. The application to
filtered determinant amplitudes and the exact weak-attraction boundary appear
to be additional corollaries to investigate; priority is not established.

EXACT INTERFACE

Input: explicitly supplied full-column-rank A in Q(i)^(m x r), ordered modes
U=[m], a partition U=disjoint union E_j, q_j=|E_j|, and rational tables
b_j(0),...,b_j(q_j)>=0. Each table is positive exactly on [l_j,u_j]. The total
binary input length L includes A and the tables. Require (C), or the stronger
ordinary logconcavity in the proposed claim. Empty table/support is returned
as Z=0. Matrix acquisition/variational optimization is outside the interface.

Define
  w(S)=1_[|S|=r] product_j b_j(|S intersect E_j|),
  a(S)=det(A_S) sqrt(w(S)),
  Z=sum_[|S|=r] |det(A_S)|^2 w(S).
When Z>0 the target state has amplitudes a(S)/sqrt(Z); its Born law is the
normalized summand. A full-rank A alone does not imply Z>0.

Set d=m-r and
  f_A(x)=det(A* diag(x) A)=sum_[|S|=r] |det(A_S)|^2 x^S,
  g_d(x)=sum_[|T|=d] product_j b_j(q_j-|T intersect E_j|) x^T.
Then [x_all] f_A g_d=Z. f_A is real stable/logconcave: for positive x, its
matrix is positive definite and logdet is concave under the positive affine
matrix map. Arbitrary complex spin/orbital mixing in A is allowed. No separate
up/down Slaters, spin conservation, or reality/sign-free amplitude premise.

COMPLEMENTARY CLOSURE PROOF (INCLUDING ZERO ENDPOINTS)

Let beta_j(k)=b_j(k)/k!, which has the same interval support as b_j. On the
integer box 0<=h_j<=q_j, define
  Omega(h)=1_[sum h_j=d] product_j beta_j(q_j-h_j).
Omega is log-M-concave. If x_i>y_i, some j has x_j<y_j because sums agree.
Exchange one unit from i to j in x and back in y. Each pair of arguments in
the two affected beta tables moves toward balance; logconcavity makes the
product weakly increase. Interval support ensures both exchanged vectors
remain supported. This proves the full weighted symmetric exchange rule.

By Brändén–Huh Corollary 3.16,
  F(y)=(product_j q_j!) sum_h Omega(h) y^h / product_j h_j!
is Lorentzian. Its coefficient at h is
  product_j binom(q_j,h_j) b_j(q_j-h_j).
Polarize y_j into the q_j variables of E_j; Proposition 3.1 divides each
coefficient by product_j binom(q_j,h_j). The result is exactly g_d, so it is
Lorentzian and hence logconcave by Theorem 2.30. Zeros require no limiting
argument. If the allowed load intervals cannot sum to r, g_d=0 and the count
is identically zero; otherwise the polynomial is nonzero.

Do NOT replace g_d by the direct occupancy polynomial without checking its
orientation. Two three-mode blocks with b(k)=k! satisfy (C). Their direct
rank-two polynomial has within-block pair coefficients 2 and cross coefficients
1; its Hessian has eigenvalues 7,1,-2,-2,-2,-2 and is not logconcave. The
complementary degree-four polynomial is Lorentzian by the proof above.

SHARPNESS AND EXACT FIXED-INSTANCE DOMAIN

For positive tables, a quadratic derivative of g_d is particularly transparent.
Force a set R of d-2 modes; t_j=|R intersect E_j| and N_j=q_j-t_j modes remain.
Put c_j(t)=b_j(q_j-t). Divide out product_j c_j(t_j), and rescale each surviving
mode in block j by c_j(t_j+1)/c_j(t_j). The normalized quadratic coefficients
are 1 for modes in different blocks and
  rho_j = c_j(t_j)c_j(t_j+2)/c_j(t_j+1)^2
        = b_j(N_j)b_j(N_j-2)/b_j(N_j-1)^2
for two modes in block j. The within-block contrast eigenspaces have eigenvalue
-rho_j. The quotient on normalized block means is
  H=vv^T + diag((N_j-1)rho_j-N_j),    v_j=sqrt(N_j),
with singleton-block diagonal correction -1. Condition (C), at k=N_j-1,
makes every diagonal correction nonpositive, giving at most one positive
eigenvalue. This independently checks the polarity/index convention.

If (C) fails at k in the positive interval of one q-mode table, duplicate that
table on two blocks, force q-k-1 complementary modes in each, and use physical
total r=2k (complementary degree 2(q-k-1)+2). The surviving quotient has
load-difference eigenvalue k*rho-(k+1)>0 and a positive sum-direction eigenvalue.
Hence that derivative, and consequently the original multiaffine g_d, is not
logconcave. This proves universal necessity. The implication to the original
polynomial follows because for a multiaffine logconcave polynomial, coordinate
derivatives are logconcave: send the differentiated coordinate to infinity,
divide by that coordinate, and take a limit.

Concrete q=2 obstruction: two identical sites, physical r=2, b=(1,1,a) give
  g_2=a(x1*x2+x3*x4)+(x1+x2)(x3+x4).
Hessian eigenvalues are a+2,a-2,-a,-a. At x=ones and v=(1,1,-1,-1),
  v^T Hess(log g_2) v = 4(a-2)/(2a+4).
Taking a=9/4 gives 2/17>0. This defeats the source logconcavity hypothesis;
it is NOT a complexity-hardness theorem. For identical positive a, n>=2
two-mode sites, and every interior physical total 2<=r<=2n-2, a<=2 is exactly
the source threshold: derivatives can leave two whole sites untouched.
The endpoint totals 0,1,2n-1,2n admit arbitrary positive a.

Fixed-instance compensation can exceed (C): q=2 blocks b1=(1,1,4),
b2=(1,1,1) have all their total-degree g polynomials Lorentzian. The only
nontrivial degree is 2; after the above congruence its quotient is
[[4,2],[2,1]], eigenvalues 5,0, with contrasts -4,-1.

For arbitrary interval tables and fixed d>=2, the EXACT criterion is: every
quadratic derivative Hessian H_R (|R|=d-2), with off-diagonal entry
  product_j b_j(q_j-| (R union {u,v}) intersect E_j |), u,v outside R,
and zero diagonal, has at most one positive eigenvalue. Interval load support
is a matroid base family, hence M-convex. BH Theorem 2.25 therefore supplies
necessity/sufficiency. Zero derivative matrices are allowed. This finite
criterion may have exponentially many residual patterns; no polynomial-time
recognizer for the entire maximal fixed-instance domain is claimed.

A useful mirror class also exists: the DIRECT polynomial is universally
Lorentzian if b_j(k)/(q_j-k)! is logconcave, equivalently
  b_j(k)^2 >= (q_j-k)/(q_j-k+1) b_j(k-1)b_j(k+1).
For counting one may switch to the dual determinant
  f_A^*(x)=x_all*f_A(1/x),
which remains real stable and has coefficients |det(A_{T^c})|^2. Then
[x_all] f_A^* H_r=Z. Thus the counting/preparation domain includes the union
of these two global orientations. Arbitrary independent per-block particle-hole
flips are not justified by this argument.

ACQUIRED ORACLES, EXACT ZERO, RANGES, AND PREFIX CLOSURE

The supported physical r-sets with l_j<=|S_j|<=u_j are bases of a bounded-load
matroid when sum l_j<=r<=sum u_j. Same-block exchange or moving a load unit
from an overrepresented block to an underrepresented block proves exchange.
Its explicit independence oracle is
  |I_j|<=u_j for all j, and sum_j max(l_j,|I_j|)<=r.
This is exactly the condition that I extends to an allowed r-set. Greedy load
allocation constructs a support witness. The complementary g support is its
dual. Intersect this matroid with the represented row-linear matroid of A;
polynomial exact rational rank/matroid intersection decides Z=0 and returns
a nonzero witness. The zero test is not a numerical determinant threshold.

After physical forced inclusions F and exclusions E, contract the row-linear
matroid by F (reject dependent F), delete E, and use remaining block capacities
q'_j=q_j-|F_j|-|E_j|, target r'=r-|F|, shifted table b'_j(k)=b_j(k+|F_j|).
Intersect again for exact prefix feasibility. In (C), the original factor
(k+|F_j|)/(k+|F_j|+1) is at least k/(k+1), so the shifted tables remain in the
class. Alternatively the source polynomial is differentiated on forced
complementary inclusions and evaluated at zero on forced complementary
exclusions, which preserves its Lorentzian property. The mirror class also
survives prefixes since deletions only weaken its required factor.

Value oracles: compute |det A_S|^2 exactly in polynomial time, and count block
loads/multiply rational table entries in polynomial time. The state uses square
roots of rational weights; these are efficiently approximable and have no new
phase. Determinant phase remains the N63 phase oracle; efficient local diagonal
phases can be added without changing counting.

Range bound R_g <= product_j (max positive b_j)/(min positive b_j). Its log is
polynomial in L. Clear all denominators of A with integer D (log D<=O(L)).
Any nonzero squared determinant is >=D^(-2r), while Hadamard gives a polynomial
bit upper bound; thus log R_f is polynomial in L,m,r. All prefix coefficients
retain such bounds. No singular-value, overlap, projection-probability, or
minimum-Z promise is required.

Chen–Liu Theorem 5.1 supplies per-run arithmetic/oracle cost
  O(m^10 B^2 eta^-2 log^2(2B/eta)), B=m+log(R_f R_g).
Remark A.4 supplies polynomial bit runtime when the rational oracles have
polynomial output length, with capped random choices/abort tapes. Median
amplification costs log(1/beta). This meets the exact interface required by
N63, conditional on the imported FPRAS's correctness. Shared depth tapes and
true-prefix weighted hybrid errors then give polynomial coherent preparation
with eta=O(eps/m), beta=O(delta eps^2/m^2). The resulting polynomial is very
large; no practical circuit-size claim or implementation is made here.

PHYSICAL SCOPE AND EXTENSIONS

Hard Gutzwiller: b=(1,1,0) per two-mode site satisfies (C). All fillings r<=n,
including doped r<n, are covered, subject to exact Z>0. r>n gives zero. At
r=n this forces one particle per site. Arbitrary spin mixing in A is harmless.
Soft repulsive g_j in [0,1] is covered; arbitrary site-specific g_j^2<=2 is
also covered. For positive rational asymmetric local amplitudes alpha00,
alpha10,alpha01,alpha11, rescale the two A rows by alpha10/alpha00 and
alpha01/alpha00. Apart from a global product alpha00, the remaining symmetric
table is (1,1,a), a=(alpha11*alpha00/(alpha10*alpha01))^2; hence a<=2 suffices.
General asymmetric zero patterns are not claimed.

Finite temperature: given rational B in Q(i)^(n x n), form graph Slater
A=[B;I_n] with n occupied orbitals on 2n modes. Trace the n ancillary modes.
The k-particle physical matrix element is, by Cauchy–Binet,
  sum_[|Q|=k] det B_{P,Q} conjugate(det B_{P',Q})
   = det((BB*)_{P,P'}).
Thus the reduced unnormalized state is Gamma(BB*). Applying a physical
occupation filter D gives D Gamma(BB*) D. A canonical physical r sector is
enforced by the ancillary quota n-r; this is one more disjoint hard block.
For B=e^(-beta h/2), the target is D e^(-beta dGamma(h)) D /Tr.

This is a FILTERED FREE Gibbs state. It is generally not
e^(-beta(H0+V))/Tr for an interacting H0+V when D=e^(-beta V/2).
Explicit down-spin hopping between two sites plus doublon V has [H0,V]!=0;
the two expressions have different beta^3 coefficients (exact fixture below).
No generic interacting-Hubbard Gibbs sampler is obtained.

For rational Hermitian h, scalar Taylor approximation to exp on
[-K,K], K=beta||h||/2, gives rational B_N of degree O(K+log(n/eps)) with relative
operator error delta=O(eps/n). If (1-delta)^2 e^-beta h <= B_N B_N* <=
(1+delta)^2 e^-beta h, exterior powers, direct sums, and D congruence preserve
the sandwich with exponents <=n. After normalization the trace distance is
O(n delta), independent of the filter's success weight. This acquisition is
polynomial in NUMERICAL beta||h||, not uniformly in its binary encoding length
when that numerical quantity is unbounded. Exact rational B as input avoids
this additional approximation/interface question.

Opposite-spin BCS corollary (coordinated with /root/slater_counting): its graph
Slater representation has occupations (u,h), physical double occupancy
u*(1-h), and local identity g^(u*(1-h))=g^u*(1/g)^(u*h). Row-scale the up rows
by g and use b=(1,1,1/g^2). Hence g^2>=1/2 is covered (g>0 rational) by (C),
including weak repulsion and all attraction. Particle-hole/sign details remain
in slater_counting's report. This covers bipartite/opposite-spin pairing,
not arbitrary same-spin Pfaffian BCS states. The local weight identity was
independently checked for 1360 configurations; no full BCS circuit run.
