C[i,j]=(i+1)^j for i!=j, C[i,i]=0, H=n!(1+n^(n-1))^n, and
F=P+2^(-L)C/H. At k=q, define

 w(I)=|det F[I,V\I]|^2, pi(I)=w(I)/c_q(F).

Every q-set has positive weight, all F off-diagonal entries are positive,
F has zero diagonal, all activities are one, and kappa_2(F)<=65/63. The two
alternating q-sets E,O each have stationary probability at least 1/3; all
other states together have mass at most r=2^n 4^(-L).

E and O, the mixing time from E to TV 1/4 is greater than
floor(4^L/(32*2^n)). If K is reversible, gap(K)<=9*2^n*4^(-L).
In particular this covers any move replacing fewer than q selected pairs.
Ordinary one-pair Metropolis is irreducible because the hard law has full
support, so disconnected support is not the explanation. The proof uses

2. FULL-SUPPORT PRECISION OBSTRUCTION, INCLUDING SYMMETRIC SPIN SINGLETS

Every off-diagonal entry is positive, every one of the six hard states has
positive weight, kappa(F_v),kappa(F_u)<=25/7<4, and

 ||F_v-F_u||_2=sqrt(3)delta,
 entrywise relative difference <=2delta,
 c_2(F_v)=12delta^2(1-delta+delta^2),
