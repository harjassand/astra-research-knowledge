# Screening and retained alternatives

Date: 2026-10-06. Primary metadata and recoverable HTML snapshots are in `sources/r06_combinatorics/`.

Priority lead: Xu's 11 May 2026 sharp asymmetric pair-sum basis theorem, arXiv:2605.10771v1. Read the complete short proof. The diagonal forest is genuinely essential for preserving a count after contraction; parity quotient separates the surviving basis pair sums. The rank quotient uses at most |X|-1 dimensions after translating one X element to zero. No flaw found in the reconstruction. The proof applies to exact independent coordinates over R and does not justify approximate feature compression.

Our continuation: an exact two-point cover theorem for every degree k, with a progression extension, reconstructed without requiring Xu's parity-contraction argument. Independent audit r09_algebra passed. The higher-r unrestricted extrapolation fails: a cubic three-point triangular dictionary in dimension three needs only four B elements whereas every progression needs five.

Author discussion: Boris Bukh's live problem page marks the pair-sum problem solved. Bukh attributes a first solution, to his knowledge, to ChatGPT Pro 5.4 on 8 April 2026 and the first public solution to Xu. This is the author's attribution, not an independently verified chronology. The problem-page publication/update date is not specified. Snapshot saved.

Alternative A: Carpenter, Defant and Kravitz, arXiv:2605.05498v2 (first 6 May 2026, version 24 August 2026). Read introduction, Section 3 and Section 5, not all of Sections 2 and 4. The main structural mechanism collapses a bounded-rank GAP to rank one when an n-point positive real set has O(n^2) subset sums, allowing O(1) exceptions. Section 5.2 asks about rank thresholds for support growth n^kappa, especially kappa=2.01 and 3. Section 5.3 Conjecture 5.1 predicts f_3(n,2)=Theta(n^5), while their theorem yields only Omega(n^4). Here f_3 prohibits three vectors in a proper linear subspace, and no-three-in-a-line constructions are lifted to an affine height-one plane. A direct fixed-dimension computational search is plausible, but finite support counts cannot prove the asymptotic conjecture. The rank-one proof relies on exact collisions and geometry of numbers; quantitative approximate collision robustness is an unexplored alternative, not a demonstrated consequence.

Alternative B: Sohail Sarkar, arXiv:2605.00918v1, submitted 30 April 2026. Abstract read only. Claims cubic-supported visibility clique covers with O_k(s+1) colors for s off-curve points. This could connect to graph colorings after explicit blocker removal, but full proof and all geometric exceptional cases remain unaudited. Do not adopt theorem claims on this screening alone.

Older prerequisite: Bukh, van Hintum and Keevash, arXiv:2409.07442v1, dated 11 September 2024. Read introduction and Section 2 excerpts. The residue-dictionary/carry reduction for higher-order rational versus integer bases must not be confused with the plain two-dictionary cover studied here. Our result does not resolve that domain-change question.
