## Reconstructed core implication

For a globally L-Lipschitz Φ with a.e. ||DΦ||F²≤rL², the central secant F_h(x,v)=[Φ(x+hv)−Φ(x−hv)]/(2h) is odd in v. Its weak direction derivative is the average of the two shifted Jacobians, so it is L-Lipschitz with uniform Frobenius derivative budget rL². Vector Gaussian Poincaré gives E||F_h(x,V)||²≤rL². Gaussian concentration of its norm gives the stated Lq bound L(sqrt(r)+sqrt(2q)). These arguments require no differentiability at the particular value-query points: the weak derivative and the affine Gaussian directions suffice. A standard measurable value oracle is implicitly needed.

Conditional on the **whole** proposal Brownian path B, a fresh T and shared V, the two X_j=B_T+sqrt(1−T)G_j are independent. Hence the mean rank-one action S_h=F_h(X_1,V)F_h(X_2,V)ᵀ is the V covariance of the averaged secant g_{B,T}(V). It is PSD despite nonsymmetric realizations. Oddness and scalar/vector Poincaré give op≤L² and trace≤rL² pathwise. Sharing V is necessary; drawing independent V_j makes the covariance estimate zero. Using X_1=X_2 changes the matrix being estimated. Independence conditional on already observed bridge points is not the argument; consistent lazy bridge revelation realizes conditional independence given the conceptual full path.

## No-H transfer is valid

Let u_t(x)=EΦ(x+sqrt(1−t)G). Averaging X_j first yields g_{B,t}=F_h[u_t](B_t,·), exactly. Gaussian integration by parts differentiates the bounded weak Jacobian and gives

||D²u_t(x)[a]||op≤sqrt(2/π)L||a||/sqrt(1−t).

This is an operator bound uniform in x and B, not an inferred modulus for raw Φ. Taylor's integral formula then bounds the L²(V) secant discrepancy by H_t h sqrt(m(m+2))/2. The nuclear norm covariance identity and Cauchy–Schwarz give

||E_V ggᵀ−A_tA_tᵀ||_*≤H_t h Lsqrt(r)sqrt(m(m+2)).

The apparent singularity is integrable: ∫_0^1(1−t)^−1/2dt=2. Thus the note's Δ_h=2sqrt(2/π)hL²sqrt(r)sqrt(m(m+2)) is correct. Both covariance complements lie between 3I/4 and I. Their relative covariance matrices consequently have spectra in [3/4,4/3], and the Gaussian KL formula bounds TV by C||Σ_h−Σ||F≤CΔ_h. The exact martingale completion applies to Σ, so this controls the actual cloud error. No endpoint t=1 bound is needed because uniform T assigns it zero probability.

Fresh factors independent conditional on full B imply the product-norm bound α_p^k. ## Uniform precision extension

For a deterministic approximate value function with uniform Euclidean error τ, central secant error is γ=τ/h. The approximate function need not be Lipschitz: Minkowski against the exact averaged secant gives directional L² norm ≤L+γ, vector L² norm ≤Lsqrt(r)+γ, and the stated nuclear covariance difference ≤2Lsqrt(r)γ+γ². The mean matrix remains PSD because it is still an outer product of a conditional mean shared-direction secant.

The Lp matrix bound can conservatively use Lhat=L+γ, since sqrt(r)+2sqrt(p)≥1. Covariance-complement stability requires the stated Lhat²≤1/4. Comparing to the original ideal Gaussian complement and then changing the returned center costs Cτ. Thus the precision extension is sound even for a discontinuous deterministic approximate value function; no unmentioned regularity is used. It does not cover fresh random oracle noise or floating-point perturbations of bridge simulation, dot products, or acceptance comparisons.

## Required comparison-work correction

The reviewed version states O(BK log(1+BK)) expected balanced bridge comparisons. This is not uniform in B∈(0,1]. With B=K^−2, the J=1 event alone contributes

Pr(J=1) K log(1+K)=2Be^{−2B}K log(1+K),

whereas BK log(1+BK) is O(K^−2). Their ratio diverges. The issue is rare blocks of K insertions, not the average number of inserted points. A safe bound is

O(e^{2B}BK log(1+K))=O(BK log(1+K)), B≤1.

Indeed J K log(1+JK)≤JK[log(1+K)+log(1+J)], and a Poisson mean ≤2 has E[J log(1+J)]≤C B. Summing over reached attempts gives the bound. This corrects only time-index bookkeeping; the TV theorem and all value/gradient query formulas are unaffected. I notified c_nonlinear and the parent before adoption.
