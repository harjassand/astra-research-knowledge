# A dimension-free repair of the blind-subspace spectral sums

## Exact interface and result

Let H have an orthonormal eigenbasis e_j and let a K-dimensional space D be spanned by some eigenvectors. For every proposal S_a and every basis vector e_j in D, require that a nonzero matrix entry (S_a)_ij implies E_i-E_j>=Delta_j>0. This is the operative blind/upward-only hypothesis. It is stronger than merely P_D S_a P_D=0; couplings to lower states outside D must also vanish.

Suppose the filtered jump is

    L_a=2pi sum_ij sqrt(gamma_a(E_i-E_j))(S_a)_ij |e_i><e_j|,

with 0<=gamma_a(omega)<=1 and gamma_a(omega)=e^(-beta omega)gamma_a(-omega). Define

    epsilon_a=2pi||S_a|| exp[-beta min_(j in D) Delta_j/2],
    ell_a=||L_a||.

For the exact coherent correction

    B_a=(i/2) sum_ij tanh[beta(E_i-E_j)/4](L_a*L_a)_ij |e_i><e_j|,

the generator -i[sum B_a,.]+sum D[L_a] obeys, for every input density matrix supported on D, including an arbitrary reference,

    ||(L tensor id)(rho_DR)||1
       <=2sqrt(K) sum_a ell_a epsilon_a +K sum_a epsilon_a².

Therefore the CPTP semigroup changes the encoded state by at most t times that bound. All ell_a must be explicitly bounded/acquired. This proof removes the source's spectral bulk-l1 premise, but does not remove the existence/upward gap/proposal-norm hypotheses. For fixed K=2 and O(N) bounded proposal channels it reproduces the source's lifetime scaling Omega(N^-1 exp(beta Delta/2)) under strictly fewer spectral-sum assumptions.

## Complete proof

Orthogonality of the energy eigenvectors gives exactly

    ||L_a e_j||²=(2pi)² sum_i gamma_a(E_i-E_j)|(S_a)_ij|²
       <=(2pi)² e^(-beta Delta_j)||S_a e_j||².

There is no sum of absolute matrix entries. This proves ||L_a e_j||<=epsilon_a independently of the ambient Hilbert dimension or number of upper spectral levels.

For a fixed COLUMN j, the factor tanh has absolute value at most one, so

    ||B_a e_j||²<=1/4 sum_i |(L_a*L_a)_ij|²
       <=ell_a² epsilon_a²/4.

This is a column Euclidean estimate. It does NOT assert that multiplication of an arbitrary matrix by the tanh kernel contracts operator norm. Such a global Schur-multiplier claim would need a separate proof.

Let P project onto D. Cauchy–Schwarz across its K columns gives

    ||L_a P||<=sqrt(K)epsilon_a,
    ||B_a P||<=sqrt(K)ell_a epsilon_a/2,
    ||L_a*L_a P||<=sqrt(K)ell_a epsilon_a.

For a density matrix supported on P tensor I_R, left/right multiplication and positivity give

    ||-i[B_a tensor I,rho]||1<=2||B_a P||,
    ||{L_a*L_a tensor I,rho}/2||1<=||L_a*L_a P||,
    ||(L_a tensor I)rho(L_a* tensor I)||1
          <=||L_a P||².

Summing proves the claimed drift including references. The derivative is Hermitian; complete trace-norm contraction and the integral formula for exp(tL) then prove the lifetime estimate. No equilibrium preparation is required for this particular blind-space encoding, but acquiring and preparing that eigenvector space is a separate capability.

## Source comparison and consequential boundary

The source main jump bound uses the triangle inequality over all upper eigenvectors, then posits an O(1) Boltzmann-weighted l1 bulk sum and supplies small-system numerical evidence in its large-system appendix. Its appendix similarly bounds B_a e_j by a sum of individual entries, whose ambient-dimension cost is not controlled by entrywise O(e^-beta Delta/2). Both steps can be replaced by the above exact l2 identities. Thus the bulk assumption and its finite numerical test are unnecessary for the stated conditional filtered-jump metastability theorem.

The source also cites a uniform ell_a=O(1) premise. Bounded scalar gamma alone does not automatically give a dimension-free operator-norm bound for arbitrary filtered matrices; the Fourier kernel or the particular filtered-sampler theorem must pay it. The admitted Gaussian CKG control has an explicit integrated norm ledger, while the source here uses a distinct finite-jump filter. These interfaces are kept separate.

Adding a physical -i[H,.] produces known coherent evolution inside D; it is not covered by the static identity-storage estimate unless that evolution is scalar on D or is explicitly undone. A system-size-dependent gap Delta, symmetry-breaking bath couplings or failure to acquire D can still destroy the conclusion. None of the actual full-density CSS arguments supplies this exact blind eigenvector subspace. Consequently this stronger comparator does not close the original-Davies programme.

Historical novelty is UNKNOWN: the repair is elementary orthogonality and fixed-column filtering, and is not claimed as a new memory mechanism. The useful downstream change is that searching for protected filtered-sampler eigenvector pairs should test their actual upward selection rule, gap and proposal norm instead of spending effort on a bulk-l1 spectral sum. Next falsifier: an acquired candidate with lower/outside couplings or uncontrolled ell_a, rather than a growing number of upper levels by itself.
