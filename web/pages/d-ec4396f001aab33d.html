ROUND 6 REVISION 1 — EXACT QUIBIT DEPOLARIZING RANGE
c04_l09 | 2026-10-07 | internal derivation; not externally reviewed

This strengthens, but does not edit, INITIAL.txt. The earlier all-d necessary cap remains valid. For d=2, the instrument-Petz class has a sharper exact classification:

  Delta_lambda is representable as Phi=C^*C, C=(L+Psi_M)/2
  from a finite bistochastic Kraus instrument iff 0<=lambda<=1/4.

Thus the symmetric-cloner marginal Delta_(2/3), and even Delta_(1/2), are exact selfcompatible positive selfadjoint channels outside this representation class. This is still only a representability obstruction; these channels do not obstruct EB rounding.

UPPER BOUND

Use the Pauli basis and write T for the 3x3 Bloch matrix of L. For M_y=p_y(I+a_y dot sigma), positivity gives 0<=|a_y|<=1, and completeness gives sum p_y=1 and sum p_y a_y=0. The canonical Psi has Bloch matrix

  G=sum_y p_y a_y a_y^T,
  g=Tr G=sum_y p_y |a_y|^2.

Suppose Phi=Delta_lambda and set s=sqrt(lambda). Then C has Bloch matrix Q with Q^T Q=lambda I_3, so Q=sO for an orthogonal O and T=2sO-G. If det O=-1, unitary input/output rotations reduce Q to diag(s,s,-s); its Choi eigenvalue (1-3s)/4 forces s<=1/3. This already lies below 1/2. It remains to treat det O=+1. Every O in SO(3) is the Bloch rotation of a qubit unitary U.

Let L have Kraus K_y and let F_e(U^* o L) denote entanglement fidelity relative to the maximally mixed qubit. Two exact identities are

  Tr(O^T T)=4 F_e(U^* o L)-1,
  F_e(U^* o L)=1/4 sum_y |Tr(U^* K_y)|^2.

For each y, polar decomposition and the trace inequality for a positive matrix give

  |Tr(U^*K_y)| <= Tr sqrt(M_y)
                 = sqrt(p_y(1+|a_y|))+sqrt(p_y(1-|a_y|)),

hence |Tr(U^*K_y)|^2<=2p_y(1+sqrt(1-|a_y|^2)). Therefore

  Tr(O^T T) <= 1+2 sum_y p_y sqrt(1-|a_y|^2).

Also Tr(O^T G)<=g because G>=0 and the symmetric part of O is <=I. Taking the trace of T=2sO-G against O gives

  6s = Tr(O^T T)+Tr(O^T G)
     <= 1 + sum_y p_y[2 sqrt(1-u_y)+u_y]
     <= 3,

where u_y=|a_y|^2 and 2sqrt(1-u)+u<=2 on [0,1]. Thus s<=1/2, so lambda<=1/4. Together with the det(O)=-1 case, this covers every orthogonal polar factor.

ATTAINMENT FOR EVERY 0<=lambda<=1/4

Set t=2sqrt(lambda) in [0,1] and take the Pauli random-unitary instrument for L_t=Delta_t, with Kraus operators sqrt(p_0)I,sqrt(p_1)X,sqrt(p_2)Y,sqrt(p_3)Z and

  p_0=(1+3t)/4,   p_1=p_2=p_3=(1-t)/4.

Both Kraus normalization sums equal I. Each effect is M_y=p_y I, so Psi_M=Delta_0. Therefore C=(Delta_t+Delta_0)/2=Delta_(t/2), and C^*C=Delta_(t^2/4)=Delta_lambda. This proves the iff claim, including both endpoints.

The script work/cycle6/c04_l09/qubit_exact_instrument_cap.py checks the Pauli probabilities and parameter identities on 101 exact rational t values, and the pointwise square identity behind 2sqrt(1-u)+u<=2 on 101 rational values of sqrt(1-u). It ran successfully. This is bounded consistency evidence only; the upper bound is the analytic argument above.

CONSEQUENCES AND SCOPE

1. The supplied bistochastic instrument class is not a converse for the compatible-channel problem even in dimension two. Its canonical depolarizing reach stops at lambda=1/4, while selfcompatible qubit depolarizing channels extend to lambda=2/3 by the explicit symmetric cloner in INITIAL.txt.
2. The factor-two SOS remains valid throughout the represented class. The obstruction does not disprove a universal EB Dirichlet comparison: the target Delta_(1/2) has other EB roundings, including the qubit construction already recorded in cycle4.
3. No external theorem, source acquisition, apparatus search, or runtime claim is used. The only inputs are finite Kraus operators supplied as part of the representation class. Output count does not enter the upper bound. The proof and exact construction are dimension two; the earlier general-d obstruction is separate.
4. This improves a candidate method's boundary but does not settle the general broadcasting-to-EB problem, collective-variance modulus, or unrestricted Dirichlet comparison. Priority and external correctness remain unassessed.
