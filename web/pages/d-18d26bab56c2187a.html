LEAF 03 — FINITE-RANGE REPULSIVE ARCHIVE-TRANSFER AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-08

Decision
========

The low-occupation sector archive theorem is valid uniformly in N for the
open XX chain with any lambda-independent diagonal potential W_N whose
vacuum is a global minimum. The potential may be inhomogeneous, N-dependent,
arbitrarily strong, and have any finite range R_N<=N. In each fixed
excitation sector, min-max gives Z^W_{N,m}<=Z^XX_{N,m}; this transfers the
free-XX Chernoff tail unchanged. The explicit full trace-norm bound is twice
the tail probability; trace distance is at most the tail probability.

The statement is false if “nonnegative W_N” means only W_N>=0 as an
operator. The finite-range hole penalty W_N=N^2 sum_i(1-n_i) is
operator-nonnegative but favors full occupancy, and makes P(M>=d)->1 for
every fixed d. Thus vacuum-minimizing repulsion is a necessary premise for
this particular low-m archive transfer. This does not rule out an archive
adapted to a known high-density minimum.

Exact theorem
=============

Let N sites form an open path, L=N+1, n_i=(I-Z_i)/2, M=sum_i n_i, J>0,
tau>0, beta_N=tau L^2, and lambda in a fixed interval [-B,B]. Define

 H_N(lambda)=-J sum_{i=1}^{N-1}(X_iX_{i+1}+Y_iY_{i+1})
             +4JM-lambda M/L^2+W_N.

For every N, take W_N diagonal in occupations, independent of lambda, with
W_N-W_N(0,...,0)I>=0. The parameters J,tau,B are fixed with N; W_N may have
arbitrary public finite-bit description and N-dependent range/strength.
Writing q=tau J and A=tau B, for every integer 1<=d<=N,

 P_{N,lambda}^W(M>=d)<=min(1,exp(F(q,A,d))).

 F=-2qd^3+(A+ln2)n+2qd^2n-(4q/3)n(n+1)(2n+1)
      +exp(A-4qd)/(1-exp(-16q)),    n=floor(d/2).

In particular,

 log P(M>=d)<=-(4/3)qd^3+[(A+ln2)/2+q/3]d
                  +exp(A-4qd)/(1-exp(-16q)).

For D>=2, set d=D-1, retain sector labels m<d exactly, and merge all m>=d
into one overflow label decoded to any fixed state sigma_N. Then

 ||rho_N,lambda^W-rho_tilde_N,lambda^W||_1 <=2 P(M>=d)<=2 min(1,exp(F)),
 T(rho_N,lambda^W,rho_tilde_N,lambda^W)<=P(M>=d)<=min(1,exp(F)).

If d>N the tail is zero. D=N+1 labels (all sectors) give zero error. The
complete derivation, including the min-max proof and product Chernoff bound,
is in [PROOF.txt](/Users/harjas/Documents/Codex/2026-10-08/use-the-astra-research-knowledge-repository-3/work/programmes/spin_statistical/cycle2/leaves/leaf03_exact_xxz/PROOF.txt).

Exact decomposition and computational evidence
===============================================

The Gibbs state decomposes exactly as rho=sum_m p_m omega_m with
omega_m=e^{-beta P_m(H_N(0))P_m}/Z_m independent of lambda and
p_m proportional to e^{tau lambda m}Z_m. This is a sector decomposition,
not a claim that the interacting W_N states are free-fermion Gibbs states.
The only use of XX solvability is the comparator spectrum
4J(1-cos(pi k/L)); the perturbation itself can be nonintegrable.

Run the SymPy/NumPy diagnostic at
[exact_sector_check.py](/Users/harjas/Documents/Codex/2026-10-08/use-the-astra-research-knowledge-repository-3/work/programmes/spin_statistical/cycle2/leaves/leaf03_exact_xxz/exact_sector_check.py).
It constructs exact rational computational-basis Pauli matrices, checks
[H,M]=0 and each sector's min-max/partition comparison numerically, computes
sector weights and one-/two-site reductions, and checks the full trace norm
of the D-label channel. N=4, L=5. The range-two test uses J=1, lambda=1/3,
tau=1/25, beta=1, W=(1/2)n_1n_3+(3/4)n_2n_4. At d=2 it gives
P_tail=0.01600525885772444 and full trace norm 0.03201051771544885,
matching 2P_tail to numerical precision. A second test combines inhomogeneous
one-body penalties with U_N=N^2=16 on a range R_N=3 pair; it has tail
0.007051646740387014. The adversarial hole model at N=4,J=tau=1,beta=25 has
P(M>=1)=1.0 to double precision and P(M<4)=7.03e-96.

Exact inputs and verification costs are recorded in
[finite_check.json](/Users/harjas/Documents/Codex/2026-10-08/use-the-astra-research-knowledge-repository-3/work/programmes/spin_statistical/cycle2/leaves/leaf03_exact_xxz/finite_check.json).
The largest diagnostic is a 16x16 matrix, with sector sizes at most 6; exact
matrix construction is O(N 2^N) arithmetic and dense eigensolving scales as
O(2^{3N}). The local run completed in under a second. These finite checks do
not establish the theorem; the proof is analytic. The archive stores
ceil(log_2 D) bits. A reversible total-count measurement can use O(N log N)
elementary gates and O(log N) scratch bits; local bit-by-bit measurement
would dephase within sectors and is not the claimed encoder. The decoder's
preparation of omega_m, finite-precision cost, and gate complexity are
unproved. A generic dense sector diagonalization costs
O(binomial(N,m)^3) arithmetic before state synthesis, exponential for central
m. This is not a practical quantum Gibbs-preparation result.

Adversarial scope
=================

The theorem handles positive inhomogeneous V_i,N n_i, unequal range-two
couplings U_i,N n_i n_{i+2}, and arbitrary N-dependent repulsive strengths
and ranges, since no W_N norm enters (1)-(4). It requires W_N independent of
lambda and the vacuum to minimize it. A range-two density term is quartic
under Jordan-Wigner, so the interacting model is not covered by the free
fermion diagonalization. Generic next-nearest-neighbor XXZ perturbations are
known integrability breakers, but the primary model cited below contains
transverse terms too; this audit does not assert every diagonal-only
coefficient choice is mathematically nonintegrable.

With W_N=N^2(N-M), every sector M=N-k has energy at least
4JN+(N^2-4J)k-2J(N-1). Therefore for k>=1 the gap above the full sector is
at least Delta_N=N^2-2JN-2J, and
P(M<N)<=(2^N-1)exp[-tau(N+1)^2 Delta_N]->0. This explicitly refutes the
operator-nonnegativity-only version. If J_N=N^-2 or B_N grows with N, the
fixed-q/fixed-A constants required by the claimed uniform tail disappear;
these are outside the theorem. No claim is made for arbitrary local terms
that do not commute with M.

Primary-source comparison
=========================

Lieb, Schultz, and Mattis (1961), “Two soluble models of an antiferromagnetic
chain,” Annals of Physics 16, 407–466, DOI
https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(61)90115-4, is the exact Jordan-Wigner
solution lineage. We use its free-fermion mechanism, derive the open-path
sine spectrum explicitly, and add only min-max plus a Chernoff/archive
argument. Novelty and priority are UNKNOWN.

Mastropietro (2013), arXiv:1303.5977v1,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.5977 (submitted 2013-03-24), studies a weak
next-nearest-neighbor XXZ perturbation and identifies it as breaking exact
integrability. Its perturbation includes XX, YY, and ZZ terms, unlike the
diagonal-only range-two density term used in our finite diagnostic.

The immutable N90 card claims a conditional classical counting/sampling
result and cites Chen–Liu 2610.06724v1. The live primary record at
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06724 (accessed 2026-10-08) instead identifies
“An FPRAS for Counting Common Bases of Two Matroids,” submitted 2026-10-05.
That identifier is not a spin-chain source. The N90 provenance needs
reconciliation; its classical output interface in any case differs from
this sector-state archive.
