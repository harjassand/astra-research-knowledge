On the Proof Complexity of Linear Logics

sciencesconf.org:proofsociety26:764142

Amir Akbar Tababatai 
                        1
                         

1 : University of Groningen

Proof complexity is a branch of proof theory that studies the length of proofs in different proof systems for a given logic. In this talk, we present an overview of recent developments in the proof complexity of substructural and linear logics, focusing on how structural rules influence proof length. We discuss several recent hardness results and proof-length separations, including exponential lower bounds for !-free formulas in the sequent calculus for Affine Linear Logic, as well as exponential separations between various linear sequent calculi and their cut-free counterparts.
