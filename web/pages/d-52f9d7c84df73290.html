STRONGER POSITIVE STATEMENT

The uniform-proposal Metropolis lift from INITIAL does satisfy an explicit two-replica additive energy inequality for every rational-complex F. For retained sets R_1,R_2, put m_r=binom(|R_r|,|R_r|/2), and let K_r be the single-replica global uniform-proposal MH kernel on all balanced orientations, with target pi_r(I) proportional to |det F[I,R_r\I]|^2. Use the random-scan product kernel
  P=(K_1 tensor I + I tensor K_2)/2.
For every f_1,f_2,
 Var_(pi_1 tensor pi_2)(f_1(I)+f_2(J))
   <= 2 max(m_1,m_2) E_(P,pi_1 tensor pi_2)(f_1(I)+f_2(J)).
All transition probabilities remain pointwise computable as in INITIAL. The mismatch register Delta_C is attached deterministically and does not change this kernel or inequality.

PROOF

For one fiber with m=|Omega_R| proposals, symmetric uniform q=1/m and MH acceptance give
  pi(x)K(x,y)=m^(-1) min(pi(x),pi(y))  (x!=y, x,y in support).
Since 0<=pi<=1, min(pi(x),pi(y))>=pi(x)pi(y). Therefore
  E_K(g,g)
    = (1/(2m)) sum_(x,y in supp) min(pi(x),pi(y))(g(x)-g(y))^2
    >= (1/(2m)) sum_(x,y) pi(x)pi(y)(g(x)-g(y))^2
    = Var_pi(g)/m.
For independent replicas and additive g=f_1+f_2, product variance is Var_pi1(f_1)+Var_pi2(f_2), while the random-scan Dirichlet form is
  E_P(g,g)=1/2 E_K1(f_1,f_1)+1/2 E_K2(f_2,f_2).
Substitution yields E_P>= [Var(f_1)+Var(f_2)]/[2 max(m_1,m_2)], proving the displayed inequality. This is a proof for the stated kernel, not a polynomial bound: m_r is exponential in |R_r| in the central sector.

The directed 2q-cycle makes the deficiency sharp up to a factor two. Its support has exactly the two alternating orientations, each with weight one. The single-replica global MH chain has off-diagonal probability 1/m and gap 2/m, where m=binom(2q,q). The random-scan product gap is 1/m. Taking f_1 to distinguish the two supported orientations and f_2=0 gives Var=1/4 and energy=1/(4m), so any additive constant for this P must satisfy L>=m. This is exact, not inferred from finite numerics.

DPP PURIFICATION ROUTE AND AN EXACT POSTSELECTION FAILURE

There is a natural bigger Slater/DPP purification. Let V=[I_n,F^T], with paired columns (e_i,f_i), and take the rank-n projection DPP on n-element column subsets B with weight |det V_B|^2. Cauchy--Binet gives its normalizer det(VV^*) (real case: det(VV^T)). The hard paired microstates are the event B=union_(i in S){e_i,f_i} for |S|=n/2. Expanding along the identity columns shows their DPP weights are exactly |det F[S,complement S]|^2. Thus this is a formal DPP lift, but sampling the unrestricted DPP and conditioning on the pair event is only useful if that event has nonnegligible mass.

On the directed 2q-cycle permutation, n=2q and F is orthogonal. Hence VV^*=I+F^T F=2I, so the unrestricted DPP normalizer is 2^n. Only the two alternating paired microstates survive, with unit weight, so hard-pair mass is 2 and the conditioning probability is exactly 2/2^n=2^(1-n). Rejection therefore costs expected 2^(n-1) full DPP samples. This exact obstruction is to the natural DPP-postselection route only; it is not a lower bound for every DPP-based compiler or every two-replica lift.

Celis et al., “On the Complexity of Constrained Determinantal Point Processes,” APPROX/RANDOM 2017, https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.APPROX-RANDOM.2017.36, establishes exact algorithms for the paper's unary/constant-number partition constraint regimes and links arbitrary partition-constrained sampling to mixed discriminants. The present event uses a number of local pair constraints growing with n, and each block is constrained to occupancy 0 or 2. The source therefore provides a prior-art boundary and a general warning about constrained conditioning, not a direct algorithm or hardness theorem for this determinant-parity subclass.

There is also an exact single-scalar encoding, but it lands in the binary-weight regime. Index the 2n columns as e_i,f_i and assign costs c(e_i)=C+2^i, c(f_i)=C-2^i, where C=2^n. Every n-column subset has base cost nC. Its excess is sum_i 2^i(x_i-y_i), where x_i,y_i in {0,1} indicate selected columns. Since the signed binary expansion with coefficients in {-1,0,1} has no nontrivial zero, the excess vanishes iff x_i=y_i for every i. Thus the single equality c(B)=nC, together with |B|=n, is exactly the union-of-whole-pairs event. The costs have O(n)-bit representations but exponential unary magnitude. This explains why unary-budget or constantly-many ordinary partition guarantees do not immediately apply. Celis et al.'s binary-constraint hardness concerns general DPP kernels; it does not transfer automatically to V=[I,F^T], so no hardness claim for the present F class follows.
