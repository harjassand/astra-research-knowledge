STATEMENT
Under exactly the hypotheses and conventions of the original analytic
lemma, for the SAME fixed bistochastic CPTP Psi and EVERY density rho,

  e^3 <= (27C/8) D b.                                      (A1)

Here Phi is bistochastic and HS-self-adjoint, Psi is any bistochastic
quantum channel satisfying E_Psi(A)<=C E_Phi(A) for every Hermitian A,
D=D(rho||I/d), b=T(rho,Phi rho), e=T(rho,Psi rho), T is HALF trace distance,
and logarithms are natural. Psi need not be self-adjoint or EB for the
analytic lemma. Singular rho and D=0/b=0 are included.

FULL AVERAGING ARGUMENT
First let rho>0. Reuse the legal Poisson flow Q_t=exp[-t(I-Phi)] from the

  integral_0^T E_Phi(sqrt(X_t)) dt <= D/4.                  (A4)

The CP Schwarz/canonical-purification argument applies at EVERY t and
uses the SAME global form comparator, giving

       <= 2 E_Psi(sqrt(X_t))

The original invariance proof gives T(rho_t,rho)<=t b. Trace contraction
of the SAME physical Psi therefore gives, for EVERY t in [0,T],

  e <= e_t+2 t b.                                          (A6)

Average (A6) with uniform weight dt/T and use scalar Cauchy-Schwarz:

  e <= (1/T) integral_0^T e_t dt + b T
    <= sqrt[(1/T) integral_0^T e_t^2 dt] + b T
    <= sqrt[C D/(2T)] + b T.                               (A7)

This avoids the earlier selection of one low-energy s with the coarser
distance estimate 2Tb. The time average is a scalar estimate on errors,
not a new channel and not an average over state-dependent EB choices.
The functions being integrated are continuous in finite dimension.

For D,b>0 choose

  T=(C D/(8 b^2))^(1/3).

Then sqrt[CD/(2T)]=2 b T, so e<=3 b T and

  e^3 <= 27 b^3 T^3 = (27C/8) D b.

No constraint on T is needed. If b=0, take T to infinity in (A7) to get
e=0. If D=0, rho=I/d and both channels fix rho, so e=0. In dimension one
