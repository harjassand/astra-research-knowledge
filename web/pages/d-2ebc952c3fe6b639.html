RESULT

The strongest new result is a uniform constructive archive for one fixed-dimensional Gaussian observation. For every centered Gaussian N(0,Sigma) with I<=Sigma<=A I in R^m, the same positive encoder/decoder, independent of Sigma, maps the observation through product-Cauchy CDF coordinates and a periodic triangular-hat Markov kernel. It preserves product-Cauchy reference exactly and achieves

  TV(output law, N(0,Sigma)) <= 8m M(A,m)/J^2,
  M(A,m)=pi^2[3+21Am+27A^2m(m+2)+9A^3m(m+2)(m+4)],
  D=J^m labels, J=max(4,ceil sqrt(8mM(A,m)/epsilon)).

For fixed A,m, this is (m/2)log_2(1/epsilon)+O_{A,m}(1) label bits. Full derivation and Markov-kernel proof are in `revisions/R2.txt`. The fixed arithmetic diagnostic at m=1,A=2,epsilon=1/1000 gives J=D=10,701; `revisions/R2_codec_bound.json` confirms the rational upper-bound inequality. The diagnostic checks label arithmetic only.

For finite latent Y with K states and conditional observations N(0,Sigma_y) in the same spectral class, the same codec gives a retained-information bound

  I(Y;L) >= I(Y;Z) - epsilon log_2 K - 2h_2(epsilon).

This follows from conditional TV, continuity of conditional entropy for finite Y, and data processing through the decoded observation. Derivation is in `revisions/R3.txt`; a numerical instance K=1000, epsilon=0.001 gives an information penalty below 0.033 bits and 13.39 worst-case label bits (`revisions/R3_mi_check.json`). This finite-latent bridge does not extend to continuous Y without a separate quantization/partition argument.

LIMITS AND COSTS

The Gaussian theorem is fixed-dimension and has explicit but large dependence on m and A. For growing m, D=J^m can be prohibitive. It assumes ideal real Cauchy CDF/inverse, exact stochastic probabilities, and continuous decoder randomness. It charges every label coordinate but not arithmetic precision, sampler time, covariance/source acquisition, or physical random bits. It approximates a one-scale law in TV; this metric alone is not a continuous-latent mutual-information continuity bound. The scale-integrated MI audit is a separate peer-originated lemma with the f(0)=0 repair recorded above. No field-breaking result, novelty clearance, external review, KLS transfer, or N75 closure is established.
