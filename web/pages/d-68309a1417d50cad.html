1. INTERFACE AND QUANTIFIERS

For any finite-dimensional H, E is a nonempty, possibly infinite subset of
density matrices. Define, using natural logarithms,

 T(rho,nu) = ||rho-nu||_1 / 2,
 C(E) = sup_finite_priors [S(sum_x p_x rho_x)-sum_x p_x S(rho_x)],
 b2(E) = inf_CPTP B:H->H tensor H sup_(rho in E,j=1,2) T(B_j rho,rho),
 e(E) = inf_EB Lambda:H->H sup_(rho in E) T(Lambda rho,rho).

ONE full-domain B is used on all labels; ONE Lambda is required on all
labels. The outputs of B may be correlated. Dimension, number of labels,
Kraus count, spectrum and minimum eigenvalue may vary without bound.
No supplied label, dimension bound, spectral gap or acquisition algorithm
is assumed. A channel may depend on the entire experiment and its supplied
broadcaster, but not on the unknown input label at run time.

The broad sequential target is:
 for every sequence E_n on arbitrary finite H_n, if sup_n C(E_n)<infinity
 and b2(E_n)->0, then e(E_n)->0.

For a fixed theta in (0,1), its fixed-sandwich version uses a reference
sigma_n in E_n, faithful on H_n, with
 (1-theta)sigma_n <= rho <= (1+theta)sigma_n for every rho in E_n.
Sections 2--6 prove equivalence of these two sequential targets. Section 7
proves equivalence of the sandwich target to its restriction to experiments
approximately fixed by exactly reference-preserving, positive KMS-selfadjoint
selfcompatible channels.

All estimates below are valid for an actual witness B with half marginal
error b. For an infimum b2(E_n), use a witness within 1/n of the infimum.
No unproved interchange of supremum, infimum or limits is required.

2. ELEMENTARY MATRIX FACTS AND THE TWO NONTRIVIAL IMPORTS

For a density sigma, define on its support
 Q_sigma(X)=||sigma^(-1/4) X sigma^(-1/4)||_2^2,
 f(rho,tau)=||sqrt(rho)sqrt(tau)||_1 (ROOT fidelity).

(a) For Hermitian X supported on sigma, ||X||_1^2<=Q_sigma(X).
Choose a sign unitary U for X, use trace cyclicity, and apply Schatten Holder:
 ||sigma^(1/4) U sigma^(1/4)||_2 <= ||sigma^(1/4)||_4^2=1.

(b) If 0<=tau<=M sigma and Tr tau<=1, then Q_sigma(tau)<=M Tr tau<=M.
For Y=sigma^(-1/4)tau sigma^(-1/4), 0<=Y<=M sqrt(sigma), so
 Tr[Y(M sqrt(sigma)-Y)]>=0. This is a TRACE inequality, not an assertion
that squaring preserves operator order.

(c) The finite-matrix square-root inequality used throughout is
 ||sqrt(a)-sqrt(b)||_2^2<=||a-b||_1 for positive a,b.
For A=sqrt(a), B=sqrt(b), X=A-B, and U=sign(X), cyclicity gives
 Tr[U(a-b)]=Tr[|X|(A+B)]. Moreover
 Tr[|X|(A+B)]-Tr X^2=2Tr[X_+ B]+2Tr[X_- A]>=0.
Norm duality gives the result. This reproduces the known Powers--Stormer
inequality in the finite setting; it is not a new theorem.

(d) Root fidelity is concave in each positive argument, continuous at
singular matrices, and nondecreasing under CPTP maps. One elementary
variational formula is
 2f(a,b)=inf_(Z>0) [Tr(aZ)+Tr(bZ^(-1))].
For faithful a, set X=sqrt(a) Z sqrt(a) and C=sqrt(a)b sqrt(a); Holder and
AM--GM give Tr X+Tr(C X^(-1))>=2Tr sqrt(C), with equality approached at
X=sqrt(C). Singular a follows by continuity. Concavity is infimum of affine
functions. For T*, applying its 2-positive unital amplification to
[[Z,I],[I,Z^(-1)]]>=0 gives T*(Z^(-1))>=(T*Z)^(-1); the same variational
formula proves fidelity monotonicity. Thus no trace-normalization of tau
is needed. For normalized states, T(rho,nu)<=sqrt(1-f(rho,nu)^2): choose
purifications attaining the polar-decomposition overlap f and use trace
contractivity on their pure-state distance. These facts also match Watrous,
TQI Chapter 3, Corollary 3.26 and Theorems 3.27/3.33.

Nontrivial primary imports:
 JN: For normalized rho,sigma, supp rho<=supp sigma and eta in (0,1),
 there exists normalized tau0 with f(rho,tau0)^2>=1-eta and
 tau0 <= (1-eta)^(-1) 2^(D_obs_bits(rho||sigma)/eta) sigma;
 D_obs_bits <= D_rel_bits+1. Jain--Nayak 1103.6067v2, Theorem 1, PDF p3.
 B2: Q_(T sigma)(T omega)<=Q_sigma(omega) for normalized omega supported
 on sigma. Beigi 1306.5920v6, Equation (3), Theorem 6, alpha=2, PDF pp8--9.
 Scaling by (Tr tau)^2 proves B2 for subnormalized positive tau; tau=0 is
trivial. All output inverses are restricted to their supports.

3. A FAITHFUL HOLEVO CENTER, WITHOUT A DIFFERENTIABILITY ASSUMPTION

First close E in trace norm. For finite dimension this leaves channel
errors and finite-prior capacity unchanged by continuity. The graph
{(rho,S(rho)):rho in E} is compact. Its convex hull has real affine
dimension at most d^2. Maximize the continuous functional
S(mean)-mean_entropy there. Caratheodory gives an attaining prior with
at most d^2+1 atoms, and its mean sigma lies in conv(E).

Let C0=C(E). For any rho in E, mix the maximizing prior with mass t on
rho, 0<t<1, and put sigma_t=(1-t)sigma+t rho. The exact relative-entropy
identity gives its Holevo value
 chi_t=(1-t)C0+(1-t)D(sigma||sigma_t)+t D(rho||sigma_t).
Since chi_t<=C0 and D>=0, D(rho||sigma_t)<=C0. Lower semicontinuity as
t down to zero proves D(rho||sigma)<=C0, including support inclusion.
This avoids the source's singular-reference derivative discussion.

Let P=supp sigma. Every promised state is supported on P. Use the actual
CPTP output compression with codomain B(PH)
 R_P(X)=PXP+Tr[(I-P)X]sigma.
It fixes all promised states and sigma. Replace B by (R_P tensor R_P)B
restricted to input B(PH); marginal errors cannot increase. Sigma is now
faithful. A channel Lambda on PH extends to H as Lambda composed with
R_P. If Lambda is EB, that composition is EB. Thus this support reduction
introduces no error or missing output-trace condition.

Because sigma is a finite mixture of closure states, both marginals of
the SAME B have error at most b on sigma. Add sigma to the experiment.
Its capacity stays at most C0, by the common relative-entropy center.
Adding sigma to the experiment is a mathematical comparison step, not
a procedure that reveals the unknown input label.

4. UNIQUE COMPATIBLE SMOOTHER AND ITS EXACT MODULUS

For M>=1 and lambda>0 minimize
 J_(rho,sigma)(tau)=-f(rho,tau)+lambda Q_sigma(tau)
on K_sigma={tau>=0:tau<=M sigma,Tr tau<=1}; call the unique minimizer G.
Compactness gives existence; strict positive definiteness of Q and fidelity
concavity give uniqueness. For feasible v the exact quadratic identity
and minimality on (1-t)G+t v give
 J(v)-J(G)>=lambda Q_sigma(v-G)>=lambda ||v-G||_1^2.       (4.1)
Let t down to zero; no differentiability of fidelity is used.

Transport between references sigma,nu by writing tau=sqrt(sigma)A sqrt(sigma),
0<=A<=MI, with A=0 outside supp sigma. Set tau'=sqrt(nu)A sqrt(nu),
kappa=tau'/max(1,Tr tau'). With d_s=||nu-sigma||_1, Section 2(c) gives
 ||tau'-tau||_1<=2M sqrt(d_s),
 |Tr tau'-Tr tau|=|Tr[A(nu-sigma)]|<=M d_s,
 ||kappa-tau||_1<=2M sqrt(d_s)+M d_s.                    (4.2)
Kappa belongs to K_nu even if the supports differ.

The exact bounded-likelihood representation of Q gives
 |Q_nu(tau')-Q_sigma(tau)|<=2M^2 sqrt(d_s).              (4.3)
Clipping only decreases Q. For fidelity, put W_sigma=sqrt(sigma)sqrt(A).
Since f(rho,WW*)=||sqrt(rho)W||_1, Holder directly gives
 |f(rho,tau')-f(rho,tau)|<=sqrt(M d_s).
If Tr tau'=t'>1, clipping decreases fidelity by at most
sqrt(t')-1<=(t'-1)/2<=M d_s/2. Changing a density rho to rho' adds
at most sqrt(d_r), where d_r=||rho-rho'||_1, because Tr kappa<=1.
Consequently transport in either direction bounds the minimum values by
 |h(rho,sigma)-h(rho',nu)|<=xi,
 xi=sqrt(d_r)+(sqrt(M)+2lambda M^2)sqrt(d_s)+(M/2)d_s.  (4.4)
Only one-way Q control is used before repeating in the opposite direction.

Let T be any CPTP endomorphism, nu=T sigma, rho'=T rho, tau=G(rho,sigma),
and tau1=G(rho',nu). T tau is feasible for the latter problem. Fidelity
monotonicity and B2 imply J_(rho',nu)(T tau)<=h(rho,sigma). By (4.1)--(4.4),
 ||T tau-tau1||_1<=sqrt(xi/lambda),
 ||kappa-tau1||_1<=sqrt(2xi/lambda).
Combining with (4.2) proves
 ||T G-G||_1 <= (1+sqrt(2))sqrt(xi/lambda)
                  +2M sqrt(d_s)+M d_s.                (4.5)

This argument is uniform over dimension, labels, condition numbers and
singular support changes. It is not a claim that G is a physical CPTP map.

5. SUBSTATE ACCURACY, NORMALIZATION, AND EXACT INCLUDED REFERENCE

Fix one capacity bound C>=0 and epsilon in (0,1). Put
 eta=epsilon^2/8,
 M=(1-eta)^(-1) exp((C+ln 2)/eta), lambda=eta/M.
If D(rho||sigma)<=C, JN gives a normalized tau0<=M sigma with
f(rho,tau0)>=1-eta. Section 2(b) and minimality yield
 f(rho,G)>=1-2eta,
 t=Tr G>=f(rho,G)^2>=1-4eta=1-epsilon^2/2>1/2.
Thus Ghat=G/t is a density with
 Ghat<=2M sigma, T(rho,Ghat)<=epsilon/sqrt(2)<=epsilon.   (5.1)
Linearity of T gives exactly ||T Ghat-Ghat||_1=||T G-G||_1/t.
No state-dependent physical channel has been selected.

For lambda<=1/4, f(sigma,tau)<=sqrt(Tr tau) and Q_sigma(tau)>=(Tr tau)^2.
The scalar -sqrt(t)+lambda t^2 decreases on [0,1], and sigma attains its
minimum. Uniqueness proves G(sigma,sigma)=sigma. Our lambda<1/8 qualifies.
Therefore sigma is EXACTLY an included state of the normalized smoothed
experiment Ehat, rather than a nearby replacement.

For an actual broadcaster with half residual b on E and sigma, (4.5)
and t>1/2 imply that the SAME B has half residual at most

 beta_(C,epsilon)(b)=(1+sqrt(2))sqrt(Xi(b)/lambda)
                         +2M sqrt(2b)+2M b,
 Xi(b)=(1+sqrt(M)+2lambda M^2)sqrt(2b)+M b.              (5.2)

At fixed C,epsilon, beta(b)=O(b^(1/4))->0. This contains no fixed epsilon
floor in the broadcast residual. For EVERY single EB Lambda, contractivity
and (5.1) give error_E(Lambda)<=error_Ehat(Lambda)+2epsilon.

6. FIXED-SANDWICH REDUCTION AND ONE-COMMON-CHANNEL TRANSFER

Fix ANY theta in (0,1), set U=2M and a=theta/(U-1). Then 0<a<1. Dilute
each rhohat in Ehat to u_rho=(1-a)sigma+a rhohat, and call the family F.
It contains sigma, all states are faithful, and
 (1-theta)sigma<=u_rho<=(1+theta)sigma.
The SAME B broadcasts F with half error <=beta(b), since it broadcasts
Ehat including sigma. Operator monotonicity of log gives
D(u_rho||sigma)<=ln(1+theta), and hence C(F)<=ln(1+theta).

For EVERY single EB Lambda, the exact affine identity gives
 T(Lambda rhohat,rhohat)
 <=[T(Lambda u_rho,u_rho)+(1-a)T(Lambda sigma,sigma)]/a
 <=((2-a)/a) error_F(Lambda).                          (6.1)
Sigma's inclusion is load-bearing. This is not a physical inverse-dilution
map and does not require a label-dependent reconstruction.
In particular
 e(E)<=2epsilon+((2-a)/a)e(F).                         (6.2)
Support-compressed EB maps extend by R_P without additional error.

The fixed-sandwich sequential implication now implies the bounded-capacity
one: choose ONE uniform C and fix epsilon BEFORE taking n->infinity. Then
M,lambda,a are independent of n, beta(b_n)->0, so e(F_n)->0 and
limsup e(E_n)<=2epsilon. Let epsilon down to zero. Conversely fixed-
sandwich families already have the common bounded capacity above.
For the contrapositive, first pass to a subsequence with e(E_n)>=c>0
and choose epsilon<c/4; (6.2) retains a positive lower bound on e(F_n).

One may select near-optimal EB channels on each original E_n or diagonalize
the fixed-epsilon choices. The conclusion is ONE common Lambda_n per n,
not one map per label. No common map across different n is demanded.

7. PETZ CORRECTION: EXACT SUPPORT, COMPATIBILITY, AND KMS CONVENTIONS

Assume sigma faithful and rho=Gamma_sigma(I+H), H=H*, ||H||<=theta,
Tr sigma H=0, Gamma_sigma(X)=sqrt(sigma)X sqrt(sigma).
For CPTP T, let nu=T sigma, P=supp nu, and define on the full output algebra
 R(Y)=Gamma_sigma T*(nu^(-1/2)PYP nu^(-1/2))+Tr[(I-P)Y]sigma.
Faithfulness implies T(X) is supported on P for every X, hence T*(P)=I.
The displayed R is CP and TP: its first term has trace Tr(PY), its second
Tr((I-P)Y). R(nu)=sigma. Put S=RT.

Let G_sigma(X)=sigma^(1/4)X sigma^(1/4), V=G_nu^(-1)T G_sigma with range
restricted to P. B2 extends from positive to Hermitian X by applying it
to X+c sigma and cancelling 2cTr X+c^2; real/imaginary splitting extends
it to complex X. Therefore V is an HS contraction, and direct adjoint
calculation gives
 K=G_sigma^(-1) S G_sigma=V*V, 0<=K<=I.
Thus S is positive and self-adjoint for Q_sigma, with S sigma=sigma.
The likelihood map Phi=S*=Gamma_sigma^(-1) S Gamma_sigma is UCP and
positive self-adjoint for the KMS inner product
 <A,C>_KMS=Tr[A*sqrt(sigma)C sqrt(sigma)].
It need not be self-adjoint for ordinary HS or for Tr(sigma A*C).

The recovery inequality follows without importing a second recovery theorem:
 Q_sigma(S rho-rho)=||(I-K)G_sigma^(-1)rho||_2^2
 <=Q_sigma(rho)-Q_nu(T rho).                           (7.1)
Here (I-K)^2<=I-K. Section 2(a) converts it to trace norm. This reproduces
the established Gao--Li--Marvian--Rouze chi-square/Petz recovery result;
their Theorem 1.5 / 4.10 is attribution, not an additional logical import.

For the uniform loss bound, let p=rho-sigma, q=T rho-nu, p'=Gamma_nu(H).
Then Q_sigma(rho)=1+Q_sigma(p), Q_nu(T rho)=1+Q_nu(q), and
-theta nu<=q,p'<=theta nu. With d_r=||T rho-rho||_1, d_s=||nu-sigma||_1,
 ||p'-p||_1<=2theta sqrt(d_s),
 |Q_nu(p')-Q_sigma(p)|<=2theta^2 sqrt(d_s),
 ||q-p'||_1<=d_r+d_s+2theta sqrt(d_s).
For u,v between -theta nu and theta nu, cyclicity gives
 |Q_nu(u)-Q_nu(v)|
 =|Tr[(u-v)nu^(-1/2)(u+v)nu^(-1/2)]|<=2theta||u-v||_1.
Together with (7.1) this proves
 ||S rho-rho||_1<=sqrt[2theta(d_r+d_s)+6theta^2 sqrt(d_s)]. (7.2)
There is no minimum-eigenvalue constant. If d_s=0 it improves to
sqrt(2theta d_r).

Symmetrize a supplied broadcaster so its common marginal is T=(B1+B2)/2.
Apply the SAME R to both outputs: B'=(R tensor R)B. This remains full-domain
CPTP with both marginals S. If the original half residual on F is at most u,
then corrected FULL residual on F is at most
 p_theta(u)=sqrt[8theta u+6theta^2 sqrt(2u)].             (7.3)
Its half residual is p_theta(u)/2. For theta=1/2, p_theta(u)=
sqrt[4u+(3/2)sqrt(2u)]. No factor of two is concealed.

Thus arbitrary sandwich broadcasting reduces to EXACT-reference-preserving,
positive KMS-selfadjoint selfcompatibility, with residual O(u^(1/4)). The
reverse reduction simply uses the joint channel in selfcompatibility.

9. THE PRECISE SURVIVING ENDPOINT OBLIGATIONS

For an arbitrary faithful sigma, Gamma_sigma conjugation is an order
isomorphism but is NOT a trace-preserving coordinate change. The KMS norm
is not ordinary HS. Merely saying 'Petz makes the map self-adjoint' and
applying C4 would therefore omit a necessary hypothesis.

The general chain needs a separately proved dimension-free centralization
encoding/decoding statement, uniform over ALL labels and with physical trace
residuals. If it uses N46 to turn broadcasting into a fidelity/affinity gap,
that bridge is a load-bearing theorem, not a source-card label. No N46
statement is used here without a proof and explicit status.

The encoded central-reference algebra is normally a finite direct sum with
arbitrary faithful tracial weights. C4 as supplied in Section 8 is a full
M_d theorem. If an encoded map mixes blocks, applying C4 separately in
each block is invalid. A weighted finite-tracial-algebra extension, plus a
full-domain EB lifting by physical pinching/encoding/decoding, is another
separate dependency. The coordinator reports a direct extension reconstructed
by spin1_anisotropic_sol and being independently audited by tensor_frame_sol;
this stage audit does not promote that report to a frozen theorem.

After those two endpoint obligations pass, Sections 2--7 provide every
capacity/smoothing/Petz part needed for a general qualitative theorem.
An imported complete-chain addendum may then record the exact residual
conventions, bridge proof path/hash, and one-channel transfer modulus.
