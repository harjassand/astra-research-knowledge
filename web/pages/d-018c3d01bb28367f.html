ENVIRONMENT / STINESPRING DECOUPLING ROUTE
Status: exact obstruction to the proposed state-family-to-environment step;
the global bounded-Holevo broadcasting-to-EB question remains UNKNOWN.

Question and interface
----------------------
Let A have dimension d, E be a family of states on A, and B:A -> A_1 A_2
be one full-domain CPTP broadcaster. Write Lambda_1=Tr_{A_2} B and
Lambda_2=Tr_{A_1} B. The state-family hypothesis is

  sup_{rho in E} (1/2)||Lambda_i(rho)-rho||_1 <= eps,   i=1,2.

The desired conclusion in the parent problem is one full-domain
entanglement-breaking (EB) channel Theta:A->A whose output approximates every
rho in E. The hypothesis above is only about unreferenced density operators.
It gives no bound on (id_R tensor Lambda_i)(psi_RA) for a purification psi of
rho, and hence no immediate Stinespring complement-decoupling statement.

Exact bounded-Holevo witness against purification/CMI shortcuts
---------------------------------------------------------------
Fix 0<a<1 and d divisible by four. Let S_d be all sign vectors
s in {+1,-1}^d with sum_j s_j=0, and set

  rho_s = diag(p_{s,1},...,p_{s,d}),   p_{s,j}=(1+a s_j)/d.

Its Holevo capacity (supremum over all priors on S_d) is exactly

  C(E_d)=c(a)=1/2[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)],

independent of d. Indeed S(rho_s)=ln d-c(a) for every s; every average
state has entropy at most ln d, so chi<=c(a), while the uniform prior on S_d
averages to I/d and attains equality.

Use the CPTP measure-and-copy broadcaster

  B(X)=sum_j <j|X|j> |jj><jj|,   K_j=|jj><j|.

It is full-domain because sum_j K_j^*K_j=I. Both marginals are computational-
basis dephasing Delta. Thus Lambda_i(rho_s)=rho_s for all s. Also Delta is
itself a full-domain EB measure-and-prepare channel fixing every rho_s.
Consequently b_2(E_d)=e_EB(E_d)=0 (with the worst-case half trace-distance
definitions above). This is not a counterexample to the target theorem.

Nevertheless, for the canonical purification

  |psi_s>_RA = sum_j sqrt(p_{s,j}) |j>_R |j>_A,

the receiver-reference marginal after either Lambda_i is

  omega_{R A_i}^s = sum_j p_{s,j}|jj><jj|,

not |psi_s><psi_s|. A measurement onto |psi_s> distinguishes these states
with probability gap

  (1/2)||omega_{R A_i}^s-|psi_s><psi_s|||_1
       >= 1-<psi_s|omega_{R A_i}^s|psi_s>
       = 1-sum_j p_{s,j}^2
       = 1-(1+a^2)/d  -> 1.

Here the first inequality is the variational characterization of trace
distance. Thus exact state-family marginal preservation, even with uniformly
bounded Holevo capacity and full-rank family states, does not yield even
statewise purification preservation. The reference dimension is d and
lambda_min(rho_s)=(1-a)/d; neither is controlled by C(E_d).

A concrete Stinespring isometry for B is

  V|j>_A = |j>_{A_1}|j>_{A_2}|j>_E.

It gives

  (id_R tensor V)|psi_s>
    = sum_j sqrt(p_{s,j}) |j>_R|j>_{A_1}|j>_{A_2}|j>_E.

After tracing A_1 A_2, R and E are perfectly classically correlated with
mutual information S(rho_s)=ln d-c(a), which diverges. For a uniform random
label X in S_d, the broadcaster's outputs also obey

  I(A_1:A_2 | X) = E_s H(p_s) = ln d-c(a).

This follows because conditioned on X=s the two registers are the same
classical random variable J with law p_s. Hence small pair-broadcast error
and bounded Holevo capacity do not force low receiver CMI, decoupling of the
Stinespring environment from purifying references, or a low-CMI premise for
an approximate-Markov recovery theorem. Meanwhile one common full-domain EB
simulator exists exactly. The broadcaster's chosen output correlations are
not an invariant of the family or a witness of its EB error.

The obstruction also shows why replacing family-relative trace error by
channel closeness is substantive: each marginal is Delta, which fixes every
state in E_d, while its action on the purification above has trace error
tending to one. An argument using continuity of Stinespring dilations must
first obtain a stabilized (cb/diamond) norm estimate, or an explicit
reference-sensitive estimate; the family-relative hypothesis supplies
neither. A single Choi/reference test in dimension d has to be charged as
reference data and cannot be inferred from the unreferenced tests on E_d.
The dilation environment is not part of the supplied broadcaster interface:
the physical outputs are A_1 A_2, and an implementation would need explicit
access to E to measure it. Here that access caveat does not weaken the
obstruction, since A_2 itself already carries the copied basis label J. The
finite family has |S_d|=binom(d,d/2) members; no efficient family or channel
acquisition claim is made.

What known recoverability statements do and do not supply
--------------------------------------------------------
Kretschmann-Schlingemann-Werner prove continuity of Stinespring dilations in
completely bounded norm: close channels in cb norm admit suitably aligned
close dilations, with a square-root modulus. This is a norm-level theorem;
it does not turn the statewise E_d bound into cb/diamond closeness. Their
information-disturbance formulation similarly starts from a channel norm.

Fawzi-Renner show that small I(A:C|B) for a specified tripartite state gives
a recovery operation B->BC approximating that state. This is a per-state
recovery conclusion. It is not, by itself, a fixed measurement-and-prepare
channel on the original full input algebra. In the witness above, the natural
receiver CMI I(A_1:A_2|X) is instead ln d-c(a), despite the exact EB simulator.
Any attempted recovery argument must identify (i) the exact tripartite state,
(ii) why its CMI is small from the supplied hypotheses, and (iii) how its
recovery is converted to one input-independent POVM and full-domain EB map.
The statewise broadcaster premise does not provide steps (i)-(iii).

Source boundary and status
--------------------------
The family, channel, Stinespring isometry, entropy identities, and trace-
distance witness above are internally derived exact calculations. They are
an obstruction to this decoupling route, not a counterexample to
bounded-Holevo broadcasting-to-EB and not an externally validated novelty
claim. The exact no-broadcasting theorem of Barnum, Caves, Fuchs, Jozsa, and
Schumacher concerns a channel that broadcasts a set of states; it is
consistent with this construction because E_d is commuting. It does not
assert a dimension-free approximate theorem for the present interface.

Reproducibility
---------------
Run from the repository root:

  python3 outputs/research/classicality/cycle4/check_environment_decoupling.py

The script uses only Python's standard library and exact Fraction arithmetic.
It checks the balanced-sign mean, positivity/normalization, purity identity,
both broadcast marginals, EB fixed-point property, and the exact lower bound
from the purification projector test for d=4,8 and a=1/2. The analytic
capacity and entropy identities are proved above; the finite script is a
diagnostic, not a proof of the asymptotic theorem.
