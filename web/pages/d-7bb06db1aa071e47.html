# Uniform EP nuisance certificate on a supplied support/rate envelope

2026-10-06. Own derivation, independent gate pending. This supplements root's acquired-rates-and-scale theorem and r10's ordinary calibration/rare inversion result. It does not claim that unknown support or hidden states can be acquired. Baseline graph and positive rate envelopes remain supplied; only numerical rates and the rare-rate scale are acquired.

## Interface

A known finite directed support on n≥3 labels is irreducible, lacks u→v, has v→u, and has reciprocal support for every other ordinary edge. Every ordinary rate lies in supplied positive rational [q_min,q_max]. Add rare u→v at b∈(0,1]. The fixed baseline A is unknown. All models with this support/envelope are irreducible, including at b=0. Full passive endpoint labels, calibrated lag choices and no free reset remain the interface. Let Ahat have the same support and envelope, and let Δ=max ordinary edges |a_ij−ahat_ij|.

## Uniform stationary sensitivity from trees

Put M=max(q_max,1), T=n^(n−1), a_min=q_min^(n−1), D_tree=T(n−1)M^(n−2). There are at most T rooted directed spanning trees at any fixed root (a deliberately loose finite combinatorial bound). Their products have degree n−1. Keeping b fixed, telescoping products gives |τ_i(A,b)−τ_i(Ahat,b)|≤D_treeΔ. The same D_tree bounds the absolute derivative of τ_i in b, because a tree uses the rare edge at most once. Each root has at least one wholly baseline tree, so τ_i≥a_min and S=Στ_i≥n a_min, uniformly in A,b.

For π_i=τ_i/S, the quotient difference is bounded by D_treeΔ/S+πhat_i |S−Shat|/S. Thus the rational envelope constant

K_π=2D_tree/a_min

satisfies |π_i(A,b)−π_i(Ahat,b)|≤K_πΔ. The same quotient derivative argument gives |∂_b π_i(A,b)|≤K_π. These are coarse but effective constants. Segments between admissible A and Ahat preserve the support/envelope; no conditioning or stationary-gap constant is being estimated from data.

## Explicit EP modulus

Write a=a_vu and

σ(A,b)=Σ_iπ_i c_i(A)+J(A,b)log(a/b),

where c_i sums ordinary a_ij log(a_ij/a_ji), excluding both rare-pair directions, and J=aπ_v−bπ_u. Define rational bounds

L_r=q_max/q_min−1,
L_a=max(q_max,1/q_min)−1,
C_c=(n−1)q_max L_r,
D_c=(n−1)(L_r+1+q_max/q_min),
C_J=1+(q_max+1)K_π.

Then |c_i|≤C_c; the mean-value derivative of each ordinary paired term gives |c_i(A)−c_i(Ahat)|≤D_cΔ. Also |J(A,b)−J(Ahat,b)|≤C_JΔ and |J|≤q_max+1. Since |log a|≤L_a and |log a−log ahat|≤Δ/q_min, direct subtraction yields

**|σ(A,b)−σ(Ahat,b)|≤[C_0+C_1 log(1/b)]Δ**, for 0<b≤1,

C_0=nK_π C_c+D_c+C_J L_a+(q_max+1)/q_min,
C_1=C_J.

For rare-rate sensitivity at a fixed admissible baseline, put H=(q_max+1)K_π+1. Exactly as in EFFECTIVE_EP_CERTIFICATE.md,

**b|∂_bσ(A,b)|≤C_EP**, uniformly in A,b,

C_EP=nK_π C_c+H(L_a+1)+(q_max+1).

All constants are computable rational expressions in the supplied graph size/envelopes. They may be very poor for large graphs or poorly conditioned rate envelopes. This is an effective uniform existence certificate, not a practical conditioning guarantee.

## Fresh calibration after acquiring the scale

Suppose root's acquired-rates-and-scale protocol, run with relative target e=min{1/4,ε/(4C_EP)} and search failure budgetδ/2, has stopped with a covered bracket [β,min(4β,1)]. Use Astar and D_mix=2d_max from root/r10's rare-probability nuisance bound; these are distinct from D_tree above. Acquire a fresh ordinary-rate model to precision

Δ≤min{1,e/[32(Astar+8D_mix)], ε/[4(C_0+C_1 log(1/β))]},

with calibration failure budgetδ/4. Choose a certified logarithm upper bound ℓ satisfying log(1/β)≤ℓ≤log(1/β)+1, replace the logarithm by ℓ, and choose dyadic Δ between half this displayed minimum and the minimum itself. This supplies a lower as well as upper accuracy scale, needed for the cost claim. Range-reduced rational log intervals provide ℓ. Certified finite upper bounds on Astar may likewise replace its exponential expression.

Collect a fresh rare source block of m_final=ceil[32768 log(8/δ)/(e²βt)] outcomes. Root's Bernstein ledger gives |z−r_A,b(t)|≤eβt/32 except with probabilityδ/4. On the fresh good calibration event, the probability nuisance bound gives |r_A,b(t)−r_Ahat,b(t)|≤Δ(Astar+8D_mix)βt≤eβt/32, using b≤4β. The uniform model slope is at leastt/4. The exact inverse of z, clipped to the bracket, therefore differs from b by at mosteβ/4. Compute an approximate clipped inverse x using probability intervals of radius≤eβt/64; return a midpoint if z lies in its probability interval, otherwise follow the certified sign, and stop once the parameter interval width≤eβ/2. A probability-tolerance return differs from the clipped inverse by at most8(eβt/64)/t=eβ/8; a width return differs by at mosteβ/4. The conservative numerical allowance eβ/2 therefore holds, giving |x−b|≤3eβ/4. This explicitly uses the acquired 4β bracket; no 2β local-bracket result is substituted.

On the joint good event,

|σ(A,b)−σ(Ahat,x)|≤C_EP |x−b|/β+[C_0+C_1 log(1/β)]Δ≤3ε/16+ε/4.

A certified σ(Ahat,x) evaluation to error ε/2 gives total error≤15ε/16<ε. Search/fresh-calibration/fresh-rare failure budgets sum toδ. Earlier selected calibrations are not silently reused for finer EP accuracy. Clipped inverse bisection and fixed EP arithmetic terminate even if the bracket/calibration/data event fails; no empty-set refinement loop or good-event-only expected-cost argument is used.

## Explicit ordinary calibration cost

Let R_*=(n−1)q_max+1 and J_*=2R_*². For any generator in the envelope, ||Q||_∞≤2R_*, and the integral Taylor remainder with stochastic semigroup norm1 gives

|P(s)_ij/s−q_ij|≤J_*s, i≠j.

Choose a fresh nuisance lag s=min(t,1,Δ/(2J_*)), where t is the final rare lag. Every ordinary endpoint probability is ≤R_*s. For each initial label, a fresh source-row block of

m_0≥[(8R_*+4Δ/3)/(Δ²s)] log(2n²/δ_0)

has all its ordinary rates within Δ/2 statistically after dividing by s, by Bernoulli Bernstein and a union bound. The deterministic remainder is ≤Δ/2. Clipping ordinary estimates to [q_min,q_max] preserves the error. The rare edge is not estimated as an ordinary one.

Root/r10's uniform no-reset source-hitting and return bounds apply because the supplied support/envelopes give a uniform minorization. Expected endpoint cost is therefore

O_support,env[log(n/δ_0)/(Δ² min(t,Δ))],

and expected physical time O_support,env[log(n/δ_0)/Δ²], including initial source acquisition. No unseen jumps or free initial rows are used.

At fixed ε,δ, Δ can be chosen of order1/(1+log(1/β)), while t has order β^(1/(L−1)). The additional read cost is bounded by constants times

β^(−1/(L−1))(1+log(1/β))²+(1+log(1/β))³,

and time by a constant times (1+log(1/β))². These are strictly lower polynomial orders than β^(−L/(L−1)) readings and β^−1 physical time. The comparison is asymptotic in b with all supplied envelope/target constants fixed; it does not assert uniformly small preasymptotic costs. Summing over the random selected stage is legitimate under root's unconditional factorial reach tail: every displayed nuisance cost is a fixed exponential in stage times a polynomial, and that tail is summable even on scale-search failures.

Thus the existing rare-rate read/time exponents need not worsen for additive EP estimation when ordinary values and local scale are acquired, **conditional on independent gates for this envelope modulus and the composed protocol**. Support, finite visible labels, positive ordinary envelopes, time calibration and arbitrarily short lags remain supplied. A finite dead-time sensor or hidden-state interface requires a separate theorem.

## Next falsifiable checks

The logarithmic nuisance factor is necessary in general, rather than just looseness of the envelope proof. A three-state witness has baseline q01=λ, q10=q12=q20=q21=1 and rare q02=b. At b=0 its tree cofactors are (3,2λ,λ), so π2=λ/[3(1+λ)] and the coefficient of log(1/b) in EP is π2. Changing the ordinary rate from λ=1 to λ=1+θ gives the exact limit

lim_(b→0) [σ(1+θ,b)−σ(1,b)]/log(1/b)=θ/[6(2+θ)]≠0.

All ordinary remaining terms are bounded and stationary laws are rational and continuous at b=0, proving this limit directly. Fixed nonzero ordinary-rate uncertainty can therefore imply unbounded EP uncertainty as the rare rate vanishes. Its acquisition cannot be silently omitted, even though the required refinement has only logarithmic accuracy in this scoped interface.
