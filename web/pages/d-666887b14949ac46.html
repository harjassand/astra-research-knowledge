## Question, source and strongest older comparison

Can the Chu translation in Tabatabai–Jalali, *Proof Complexity of Linear
Logics*, arXiv2601.22393v2 (9 July 2026 revision; first submission 29 January),
transfer an intuitionistic proof-line lower bound to classical affine
Frege **lines**, including acyclic definitional extensions? The source proves
an affine proof-symbol-size lower bound and names affine line/EF robustness
as future work. Its core issue is that translated formula trees may grow
exponentially. Source locations: §2.1/Frege Tables6–8 (TeX labels
`Fig: FL_e`, `Fig: linear`, `Fig: contraction-free logics`), §4.1 Chu
definition and dual lemma, §4.2 conservative repair, and §7's full proof of
the Chu theorem (TeX labels `Def: translation`, `Lem: iGD`,
`thm: CFLe to FL_e`, `lem: basic conservative`, `Thm: main`).

The strongest older comparison is Jeřábek, *Substitution Frege and extended
Frege proof systems in non-classical logics*, author version 24 November
2008, published 2009. §2.2 pp12–13 defines circuit similarity, circuit Frege
and fresh definitional EF; Propositions3.2–3.3 p14 already relate ordinary
Frege lines, circuit Frege and EF size. Theorem6.37 p76 supplies exponential
intuitionistic EF size lower bounds for the negation-free Hrubeš formulas,
with a monotone-circuit reduction. Thus neither generic syntax sharing nor
the Frege-line/EF connection is a new result here. Jalali,
arXiv2006.09705v2 (20 August 2020, published2021), Definition3.9 p21 defines
substructural EF by the same fresh equivalence axiom. Both precise pages
were extracted and visually checked from recovered primary PDFs.

The candidate new strengthening is the **size-independent accounting for
the Chu proof templates**, conditional on these older lower bounds. It is
not a classical EF lower bound, a new circuit lower bound or a theorem of
faster proof search. The recent source is a preprint; publication/priority
of any subsequent equivalent strengthening has not been exhaustively
checked.

## L1. Linear common syntax for both translations — proved

Represent each formula by an acyclic constructor graph. Propositional atom
names are constants; each binary connective has two ordered input wires.
Graph sharing means identical unfolded syntax, never Boolean equivalence.
For every original node A compute the pair (T_A,S_A) bottom-up using the
source's exact clauses, with D=N=bottom. An implication creates three
constructor nodes for T_A and one for S_A; fusion creates one for T_A and
three for S_A. AND/OR and ! each create at most two nodes. Constants and
atoms create only bounded many nodes. Hence m source nodes produce at most
4m+O(1) new constructor nodes. All wire IDs have O(log(m+1)) bits.

The output may unfold to an exponential tree. For F_0=p,
F_(i+1)=F_i -> p, exact tree sizes obey

    T_(i+1)=T_i+S_i+5, S_(i+1)=T_i+2, T_0=S_0=1.

This gives the golden-ratio growth after a constant shift. L1 controls
stored syntax, not unfolded ordinary symbol size.

## L2. One legal cached pair proof per source node — proved and executed

Cache P_A: T_A,S_A=>bottom. The target is intuitionistic MALL with cut;
none of these pair proofs needs unrestricted weakening or contraction.
Antecedents are multisets with multiplicities retained. For implication,
use P_B and identity S_C=>S_C in left implication on S_C->S_B, then left
AND projection and left fusion. For fusion use identity T_C=>T_C and P_B,
then left implication on T_C->S_B, left AND projection and left fusion.
AND and OR use both child pairs, the appropriate left-AND projections and
left-OR additive branching. For !A use identity !T_A=>!T_A and identity
bottom=>bottom in left implication. Atom/constant cases are bottom axioms.

Each source node costs at most four new sequent rows, at most13 formula
occurrences; a cached row may be cited by several later inferences under
the source's explicit DAG-like proof convention. Citing the same p=>p
twice in right fusion still produces p,p=>p*p. It does not produce
p=>p*p. Our exact checker rejects that mutation.

Negation-duality repairs, (negA)^t <=> S_A and (negA)^s <=> T_A, use P_A
and boundedly many identities, implication, fusion, additive and cut rules.
Cache these once per required A. Double-negation axiom calls require A
and negA=A->0; adding one/two missing negation wrappers per occurrence
costs O(L) nodes. There is no iterative family of distinct substitutions.

## L3. Complete finite Frege-template audit — proved and independently replayed

Use exactly the recent source's finite classical affine linear Frege
presentation. Each axiom is an instance of a fixed finite schema, and the
inference rules are modus ponens, adj_u and closed necessitation. The
source's full Chu appendix has the following dependency ledger:

| Source scheme | Nonconstant proof dependency |
| --- | --- |
| `star_and` | P_A and P_B |
| `one_imp` | P_A |
| double-negation elimination | direct certified template needs only P_A; literal source proof may use cached dual repairs for A and negA |
| `bang_weak`, `bang_T`, unrestricted weakening | P_A |
| id, pf, per, AND projections, imp_AND, OR injections, OR_imp, imp_star, star_imp, unit, top, bottom, bang_contract, bang_K, bang_4 | fixed rule templates only |
| modus ponens | fixed left-AND projection, implication and cuts |
| adj_u | closed output theorem plus unit theorem, then right AND |
| necessitation | promotion of the closed output theorem |

The finite templates operate on the already translated operand roots,
regardless of their unfolded tree lengths. Products are rearranged through
fixed associativity/exchange templates. Additive branching uses the same
context on each branch as permitted by its rule; multiplicative rules keep
each hypothesis occurrence. Exponential contraction appears only on !A.
Promotions have empty or fully modal antecedents, as required. A finite
template introduces only boundedly many new formula nodes and proof rows.
Combined with L1–L2, m common input syntax nodes and L Frege steps give
O(m+L) intuitionistic affine-linear formula occurrences.

Two source display slips are corrected explicitly. The appendix claims
T_A=>T_A AND1 for adj_u; in the nonweakening target this is not generally
derivable. The correct rule simulation starts with the already acquired
closed theorem =>T_A and the unit theorem =>1, then right AND. The
necessitation paragraph starts from =>A; it must start from =>T_A. These
repairs require only a constant number of additional rows.

## L4. Ground skeleton normalization — proved and executed

Let a closed ordinary Frege proof have L steps, arbitrary intermediate
formula sizes, and a fixed final formula A. Record only each rule name and
its earlier premise references. Freshen the finitely many metavariables in
each rule instance. Introduce equality equations between each schematic
premise and the referenced conclusion and between the final conclusion
and the ground target A. Atom names in A are fixed constructor labels.
There are O(L+|A|) initial term nodes.

An elementary exact unifier suffices. Merge the equation classes. Two
constructor classes can merge only if their labels/arity agree; enqueue
their child equalities. Select one constructor descriptor per resulting
class. The quotient's constructor-edge graph is acyclic: any actual finite
proof instantiation is a solution, and each constructor-child edge strictly
decreases that solution term's unfolded size. A cycle would contradict
strict decrease. Ground constructor-free classes to one fixed fresh atom.
The quotient gives common formula roots and axiom substitutions with
O(L+|A|) nodes. Every grounded rule instance and the target are preserved.

No input formula labels are reused merely because they denote equivalent
Boolean functions. No new substitution inference is added. All source
rules meet the finite-schematic premise: necessitation acts on a closed
proved formula; adj_u is A/(A AND1); no rule has atomicity/freshness side
conditions. This lemma is compatible with, and already conceptually
contained in, the older Frege/EF relationship. We claim an independent
explicit construction and verifier, not historical novelty for L4.

There are at most the initial-node-count minus one successful class merges.
Each matching constructor merge adds at most two equations. A naive union
and cycle implementation costs polynomial arithmetic in L+|A|; exact atom
name reads and reference bit lengths are additionally charged. If the
original proof is supplied as enormous explicit formulas, reading and
validating those formulas still costs their full input size. The lower-bound
argument is existential normalization; it does not claim that enormous
input bytes can be read in O(L) time.

## L5. Extensions must unfold before Chu — proved; obstruction retained

For a standard acyclic definitional EF proof, each extension e_i is fresh
in its body, earlier lines and final target. Redirect e_i to its previously
acquired body root after recursively redirecting earlier extension atoms.
An ordinary proof-certificate syntax graph of size s yields a common
expanded syntax graph of O(s) nodes, even when its trees have exponential
size. Each defining equivalence becomes F->F AND F->F and has a fixed-length
ordinary proof. All other finite rule instances are preserved by this
single acyclic substitution. This supplies the circuit proof input to L3.

Translating the extension atom while still treating its dual as bottom is
invalid. In the exact Łukasiewicz valuation p=3/4,e=1/2, the definition
e<=>p*p has value1 but t(e->p*p) has value3/4. This is a concrete obstruction
to a naive extended-axiom translation. It does not refute unfold-first L5.
The executable retains the fractions and separately rejects self/future
definition references. Its expansion primitive is not a whole EF
freshness/trace checker; full EF scope in this lemma includes all standard
freshness and final-goal conditions.

## L6. Conservative target and lower-bound consequence — internally gated, prior-based

The hard target Theta*_n from the source is fully conservative and has
polynomial explicit size. Its simultaneous conservative repair proofs
T_A=>A, A=>T_A and S_A=>bottom can be shared; each source node adds only
boundedly many rows. For the hard target, the source's original polynomial
repair bound would already suffice. Thus L3–L4 turn an L-line classical
affine Frege proof of Theta*_n into an intuitionistic affine-linear proof
with polynomially many formula occurrences in L+n. It is also a proof in
LJ_!. Erasing ! and interpreting fusion/unit in ordinary intuitionistic
logic costs polynomially many lines; the source's conversion
Theta*_n=>Theta_n likewise costs polynomially many lines in n.

The older intuitionistic line lower bound for Theta_n now implies

    L >= 2^(n^Omega(1)).

L3+L5 analogously give an exponential total-size lower bound for standard
acyclic definitional classical affine EF proofs. The hard formulas remain
FL_e-provable and have short full classical Frege proofs as in the recent
source, so this isolates unrestricted contraction's strength even in the
presence of the source's linear exponentials. It does not address
substitution Frege, circuit equivalence under arbitrary Boolean functions,
or classical Frege/EF lower bounds.

This consequence still depends on the cited earlier monotone-circuit
lower bound and its proof-complexity transfer; we have read the primary
theorem, relevant proof reduction and precise conventions, not independently
reproved Razborov/Alon–Boppana's entire monotone-circuit lower bound.

## Sequent-line scope: one remaining exact extension obligation

For an arbitrary affine *sequent* proof, total formula occurrences l can
also define an O(l+|target|) first-order skeleton: retain context-occurrence
wiring; each rule's equations add O(number of conclusion occurrences+1)
nodes. Additive branches equate corresponding context slots, multiplicative
rules keep separate slots, and promotion marks every premise context slot
as modal. A finite proof again supplies an acyclic solution. This compact
syntax lemma appears sound independently of the generic Frege result.

To transfer the present lower bound to arbitrary sequent **line counts**,
one must still account explicitly for sequent-to-Frege proof templates in
terms of l and shared syntax, especially modal-context promotion and
context fold/reassociation. A generic polynomial-in-symbol-size equivalence
does not establish that accounting. We leave that scope unadopted here;
canonical Frege lines and standard definitional-EF size are the precise
current consequence. This is an exact next obligation, not evidence that
sharing permits a sequent lower-bound bypass.
