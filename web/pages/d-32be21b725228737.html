STRONGEST CONCRETE RESULT — FULL SEPARABILITY, FINITE-N CERTIFICATE

For every fixed M,B<infinity, uniformly over real symmetric ||A||op<=M and |b|<=B,
the whole N-qubit critical Gibbs density

 rho_(N,A,b) = exp[2J^2/N + Q_A(J)/N^(3/2) + b.J/N^(3/4)] / Z,
 J=sum sigma/2, Q_A=sum A_ab(J_a J_b+J_b J_a)/2,

has an explicit convex mixture sigma_(N,A,b) of IDENTICAL single-qubit product
states tau^(tensor N), with

 T(rho,sigma) <= E_sep(N;M,B) = O_(M,B)(N^(-3/2)).

The proof and public finite-N formulas are in spin_full_separability_audit.txt,
equations (1)-(9). For every N with t0=2/N-M/N^(3/2)>0 the exact certificate is
finite, not only asymptotic. Set s=N^(3/4), x_j=j(j+1)/s^2, ell_j=j/s,
a_j=(2j+1)m_N(j) exp[2j(j+1)/N], and

 Zbase=sum_j a_j exp(-M x_j),
 Usharp=sum_j a_j exp(M x_j+B ell_j)
        [2M x_j+(B/2)ell_j+(2M x_j+B ell_j)^2/3].

Then E_sep=min{1,[3M/(2s^2)]Usharp/Zbase} suffices. The already audited binomial
quartic-tail envelope makes the ratio uniformly bounded for fixed M,B. For M=0
the state is EXACTLY fully separable, including arbitrary bounded field b.
The scalar-anisotropy subfamily likewise has an exact positive representation.

Here T=||.||_1/2. If ||W||op<=1 and Tr(W sigma)>=0 for every fully separable sigma,
then Tr(W rho)>=-2E_sep. This is a whole-density statement and a precise prediction
about bounded separability witnesses in this all-to-all critical window. It gives
no unrestricted local-lattice conclusion, reference-system recovery, or claim
that all finite-N anisotropic states are exactly separable.

For a witness with outcomes in [-1,1], Hoeffding gives a sufficient shot count
2 epsilon^-2 log(2/delta) for additive epsilon accuracy and confidence 1-delta.
Resolving the vanishing O(N^-3/2) witness scale therefore has a worst-case
sufficient O(N^3 log(1/delta)) measurement cost; the finite certificate itself
can be evaluated before choosing an experiment. A violation below -2E_sep
outside sampling uncertainty would contradict the modeled critical family.

The construction is elementary. A positive, exact radial identity on every spin
representation is

 exp(tJ^2)=int exp(x.J) (4pi t)^(-3/2) exp(-t/4-|x|^2/(4t))
                         sinh(|x|/2)/(|x|/2) d^3x.

Its own character/Gaussian proof is supplied. Writing C=A+MI>=0 with eigenvalues
lambda_i and axes n_i, F_i=n_i.J, c_i=lambda_i/s^2, v=b/s, put
H=sum c_i F_i^2+v.J. The separability-preserving positive semigroup

 L(X)={H,X}/2-(1/4)sum c_i[F_i,[F_i,X]]

is built from exact Gaussian LOCAL filters and a deterministic local field filter.
Sigma is exp(L)(exp(t0 J^2)) normalized by its trace. A Duhamel expansion bounds
its difference from exp(t0J^2+H) using double commutators. Their coefficients are
O(N^-3/2); critical sector moments control all remaining polynomial factors.
This proof has been independently reconstructed by /root/critical_separability_audit.
Internal multi-agent agreement is not external peer review or a priority result.

FINITE CLASSICAL COMPILER AND PHYSICAL PREPARATION

Finite Lie-Trotter products Phi_m are already positive iid-product mixtures.
Equation (10) of spin_full_separability_audit.txt gives an explicit public G_N
with T(sigma_m,sigma)<=G_N/m and G_N=O_(M,B)(1). Sampling their branches does
not require actually filtering quantum qubits or accepting rare quantum outcomes:
sample the exact isotropic product prior classically, multiply only 2-by-2
single-qubit filter matrices, weight each proposal by
 w=[Tr(g tau_x g^*)]^N,
and prepare the normalized single-qubit state directly N times.

The finite mixture has mu=E w>=1. A pN-replica central supersolution and the exact
radial Gaussian identity bound E w^2 uniformly for fixed M,B, independently of m,
for all sufficiently large N. The radial base prior is logconcave; an explicit
two-Maxwell-mixture rejection sampler also supplies a constant expected
proposal bound depending on M. Those are mathematical sampling constructions,
not a supplied oracle. Self-normalized importance resampling of R iid candidates
adds at most sqrt(Var(w))/(mu sqrt R) in averaged output trace distance.

Thus m=O_(M,B)(eta^-1), R=O_(M,B)(eta^-2) yield additional error <=2eta using
O_(M,B)(eta^-3) scalar Gaussian/filter steps and N single-qubit preparations.
Public m,R may be computed from the finite-N bounds. At fixed M,B their constants
can be very large; no practical speed advantage is claimed. Finite-bit sampler
certification and gate compilation have not been implemented. Their total errors
epsilon_sample+epsilon_gate must be added; real-arithmetic counts are not bit-time
or actual hardware timings. Full costs and derivations are in the child's final
addendum and the independent critical_separability_audit report.

The exact-real oracle is also removed at the mathematical level by the separately
saved spin_finite_precision_compiler.txt, independently audited PASS in
spin_finite_precision_audit.txt. For rational supplied data (or a charged
polynomial-time precision interface), dyadic eta,epsilon, fixed public M,B, and
every admitted N with t0>0, its averaged output error is <=E_sep+2eta+epsilon.
It truncates normal paths, approximates normal CDF inversion with certified
series/bisection, and controls the complete matrix/weight/state errors. Three
columns of sqrt(A+MI), computed by guarded regularized Newton iteration, avoid
an unknown eigenaxis gap. The explicit large-N threshold and a finite small-N
Maxwell fallback charge every rejected-proposal/moment/conditioning constant.

Classical bit work is conservatively polynomial in N, accuracy inverses, and
supplied rational input length for FIXED M,B; it is distinct from O(eta^-3)
unit-cost scalar work. The simple finite-bit selection retains O(eta^-2) local
qubit/weight descriptions at its declared bit precision. N native one-qubit
emissions have a separately charged total error. A fixed discrete quantum gate
compiler, implemented interval sampler, hardware timing, and practical speed
advantage are unestablished. This is a mathematical preparation construction,
not a claim that finite-bit certified code has been run.

PARAMETER-INDEPENDENT, ENTANGLEMENT-FREE REVERSE CHANNEL

the universal one-observation decoder

 Ssep_N(x)=[exp(2N^(-1/4)x.sigma)/Tr exp(2N^(-1/4)x.sigma)]^(tensor N),

for x with p_(A,b)(x) proportional to exp[-4|x|^4/3+x^T A x+b.x]. Equations
(11)-(20) of spin_full_separability_audit.txt give a public finite-N recovery
bound: O_(M,B)(N^-1/4) plus a weighted scalar prior remainder tending to zero.
The prior remainder is itself O_(M,B)(N^-1/2) by its sixth-order scalar Taylor
bound and exterior exponential tail (see the final addendum). Hence the universal
fully separable reverse error is O_(M,B)(N^-1/4). It needs no parameter estimator,
singlets, entangling gates, or Schur transform. It emits N local qubits; input
sampling and finite precision are charged separately. The stronger known-parameter
separability-distance rate must not be substituted into this statistical channel.

WHY THE RATE IMPROVES

In a spin-j sector, coherent measurement/preparation B_j has the exact random
rotation representation from Aschieri–Ruba–Solovej, Lemma 13:

 B_j(tau)=int F_j(g) U_j(g) tau U_j(g)^* dg,
 F_j(g)= (2j)!(2j+1)! 2^(4j)/(4j)! * cos^(4j)(alpha),
 eigenvalues(g)=exp(+-i alpha), alpha in[0,pi].

Under this probability density sin^2(alpha)~Beta(3/2,2j+1/2). The folded physical
rotation angle theta=2 min(alpha,pi-alpha) obeys
 E theta^2 <= 3pi^2/[4(j+1)]. Uniform rotation axes cancel the first derivative.

For a unitary Gibbs curve tau_theta=exp(K_theta)/Tr exp(K_theta), the normalizer
is constant. Twice differentiating the exponential with the ordered Duhamel
simplex and Schatten Holder gives

 ||tau_theta''||_1 <= ||K_theta''||op + ||K_theta'||op^2.            (2)

No commutation of K and K' is required. Set A0=A-(tr A)I/3, M0=||A0||, s=N^(3/4).
The scalar J^2 term drops out under rotations. For any axis,

 ||K'|| <= 2M0 j(j+1)/N^(3/2) + B j/s =: L1_j,
 ||K''|| <= 4M0 j(j+1)/N^(3/2) + B j/s =: L2_j.

Taylor with its integral remainder proves the ALL-SECTOR finite bound

 T(tau_j,B_j tau_j) <= 3pi^2/[16(j+1)] * (L2_j+L1_j^2).          (3)

The critical Gibbs sector moments satisfy E j^q=O(N^(3q/4)) for every fixed q,
uniformly over M,B. Averaging (3), rather than maximizing it or taking Pinsker,
gives beta_N=O(N^(-3/4)). A convenient explicit bound is

 beta_N <= 3pi^2/16 * [ (4M0+2B^2)E j/N^(3/2) + B/s
                       +8M0^2(E j^3+E j^2)/N^3 ].               (4)

The rate is an application of known quantitative Berezin approximation, with a
direct derivative proof and explicit constants. The 2024 paper already provides
the random-rotation formula and O(1/j) Schatten/Sobolev estimates; do not label
those ingredients new.

EXACT POSITIVE MIXTURE AND PUBLIC FINITE-N CERTIFICATE

Let k=j+1/2, r_j=k/s, L=N+1, d_j=2j+1, and

 m_N(j)=(2j+1)/(N+1) binom(N+1,N/2-j),
 a_j=d_j m_N(j) exp[2j(j+1)/N].

For coherent direction u, define the EXACT lower symbol

 h_j^-(u) = j(j-1/2) u^T A u/N^(3/2)
            +(j/2)tr A/N^(3/2) + j b.u/s.

Let q^-_(A,b)(j,u) be the probability law proportional to a_j exp[h_j^-(u)]
with normalized sphere measure. Prepare the singlet/permutation output at (j,u).
This is sigma in (1), with no fitted estimate of A,b substituted for an unknown
input. A,b are supplied for the direct known-state preparation algorithm; the
decoder itself only depends on (N,j,u).

Scalar Jensen gives R_j(u)=<j,u|exp(H_j)|j,u> >= exp(h_j^-), where H_j contains
the A,b terms. Integrated upper-symbol Jensen gives

 int R_j <= exp(delta_j) int exp(h_j^-),
 delta_j=3M0(j+1/2)/N^(3/2)+B/s.                                 (5)

These are the corrected noncommuting comparisons in N20, not operator monotonicity
of the exponential. Normalized actual and lower-symbol laws differ in TV by at
most E_q^- [exp(delta_j)-1]. It is O(N^(-3/4)) by critical moment control. Triangle
inequality with (4) proves (1).

For a certificate requiring only N and public M,B, use M0=2M, tilt_j=M r_j^2+B r_j,

 Z_- = sum_j a_j exp(-tilt_j),
 F_q = [sum_j j^q a_j exp(tilt_j+delta_j)]/Z_-, q=1,2,3,
 U = [sum_j a_j exp(tilt_j)(exp(delta_j)-1)]/Z_-.

Since |h_j^-|<=tilt_j, the actual E j^q<=F_q. Hence the fully explicit formula is

 E_prep=min{1, U+(3pi^2/16)[(4M0+2B^2)F1/N^(3/2)+B/s
                                      +8M0^2(F3+F2)/N^3]}.       (6)

All sums contain only N/2+1 positive scalar terms. Numerical evaluation in the
script is ordinary floating point, not an interval-certified enclosure.

CLASSICAL SAMPLING / PHYSICAL COST

With A,b supplied, a rejection sampler for q^- first samples j with envelope
weights a_j exp(tilt_j), then a uniform direction u, and accepts with probability
exp(h_j^-(u)-tilt_j). Its acceptance probability is uniformly bounded below for
fixed M,B: both numerator and envelope normalizers have quartic tails, and a
fixed radial annulus has a positive mass and bounded tilt. There is no inverse
rare-event probability depending on N. Preprocessing requires O(N) scalar
evaluations, O(N) table storage and scalar/log arithmetic; one can use a log
recurrence for a_j. This is a real-arithmetic/continuous-randomness construction.
I have not implemented a rigorously certified finite-bit spherical sampler.
Approximate sampling TV eta and circuit trace error zeta add eta+zeta to (6).

The singlet decoder avoids a Schur transform. Proof: its seed is a highest-weight
vector in spin j. In Schur-Weyl coordinates it is |j,u> tensor |v_j>. Permutations
act trivially on V_j and irreducibly on multiplicity M_j. Averaging the latter
pure projector gives I_Mj/m_N(j), by Schur's lemma. Fisher–Yates uses <=N-1 swaps;
uniform bounded-integer sampling has O(log N) expected random-bit cost per step.
The final random seed is discarded. Its randomness/entropy production is charged
as preparation work; it is not retained archive data. Direction gate precision
O(eta/N) gives an elementary conservative O(eta) circuit error by telescoping.

SHARP THERMODYNAMIC RADIAL FLOOR

On r=k/N^(3/4), the exact unnormalized zero-field weight obeys the weighted
Stirling expansion

 a_j/2^N = 8sqrt(2/pi) r^2 exp[-4r^4/3]
          *{1+N^(-1/2)[4r^2-(32/15)r^6]+O(N^(-1)P(r))}.         (7)

The remainder is controlled in weighted L1, not only pointwise on compact sets.
For 2k/(N+1)<=1/2, the binomial entropy series bounds its logarithm by
 C[N^-1+N^-1/2(r^2+r^6)+N^-1 r^4], with its positive part bounded by
 C[N^-1+N^-1/2 r^2+N^-1 r^4]. Multiplication by the quartic density and any fixed
exp(Mr^2+Br) is integrable. The exterior region is exp[-cN+O(Msqrt N+BN^(1/4))].
Riemann/jitter error is O(N^(-3/4)) by the weighted derivative L1 norm, including
the parity/origin cells. This proves uniform O(N^(-1/2)) tilted radial TV.

At A=b=0, L(r)=4r^2-(32/15)r^6, E L=2 E r^2, and

 sqrt(N)*TV(jittered exact radius, limiting quartic radius)
   -> .5 E|L-E L| = 0.3534463689560967... >0.                    (8)

Thus the rate is sharp for THESE channels and the uncorrected limiting quartic
experiment. It is not a minimax lower bound over all finite-N comparison maps.
The O(N^-3/4) symbol/channel errors are smaller than this radial floor. Conditional
on N20's separately unreviewed sharp memory theorem, the finite-N archive bound
becomes cD^-2/3-CN^-1/2 <= e_(N,D) <= CD^-2/3+CN^-1/2. I did not independently
reaudit that memory converse, so only the bridge rate is internally derived here.
