1. FIX AN EXACT CUTOFF AND ACQUIRE ITS SCALED CONSTANTS

Supply rational A=A^T,b, fixed public M,B>=0, ||A||<=M, |b|<=B. Let
Lambda=M+1, C=A+Lambda I, Lc=2M+1, Tc=Tr C<=3Lc,
r0^2=1/[16(Lc+1)], r*=3r0/4, s=N^(3/4). Use the S01 cutoff:
 chi=1 for |x|<=r*, chi=0 for |x|>=r0,
 chi=1-10t^3+15t^4-6t^5 between,
 t=(|x|^2-r*^2)/(r0^2-r*^2).
Its derivative satisfies |chi'(t)|<=15/8, so Lip(chi)<=60/(7r0)<9/r0.
The definition uses exact rational squared-radius comparisons at rational
nodes; no square-root comparison or outside-ball PSD oracle is necessary.
Let kchi=9/r0. In the inner ball write
 a=(N-1)/(2N^1.5)[Cx-(x^TCx)x]+[b-(b.x)x]/(2N^.75),
 D=(I-xx^T)C(I-xx^T)-[x]_cross C[x]_cross^T,
 V=N(N-1)x^TCx/(4N^1.5)+N Tc/(4N^1.5)+N b.x/(2N^.75).
Use A_N=chi a, Sigma_N=chi chol(D)/(sqrt(2)N^.75), U_N=chi V,
all zero outside r0. Cholesky denotes the unique lower triangular factor
with positive diagonal. Principal square roots are also possible, but the
same chosen factor must be used for the true/Euler strong coupling.

On |x|<=r0, 3I/4<=D<=Lc I and Tr D<=Tc. The upper operator bound follows
from D<=P C P<=Lc P^2<=Lc I, P=I-xx^T. The trace bound follows from
Tr(P C P)=Tr(C P^2)<=Tr C and the PSD subtracted term.
In operator norm Lip(D)<=6Lc r0. To see this, Lip(P)<=2r0 and ||P||<=1
give 4Lc r0 for P C P; the two cross-product factors give 2Lc r0.
Thus its Frobenius Lipschitz constant is <=6sqrt(3)Lc r0.

Differentiate L L^T=D. With E=L^-1(dD)L^-T, write dL=L Phi(E), where
Phi keeps the strict lower triangle and halves the diagonal. ||Phi(E)||F
<=||E||F, ||L||op<=sqrt(Lc), and ||L^-1||op<=2/sqrt(3). Consequently
 ||dL||F<=4sqrt(Lc)||dD||F/3,
 Lip(chol D)<=8sqrt(3)Lc^(3/2)r0.
This argument integrates along the segment of Bloch points; the ball is
convex and the spectral floor holds along it. It is independent of any
eigenvector or eigenaxis gap. Cholesky pivots have squared value >=3/4:
each is a scalar Schur complement of a matrix with that spectral floor.

Here are explicit public constants for every N>=1:
 a0=(Lc r0+B)/2,
 la0=Lc(1+3r0^2)/2+B r0+kchi a0,

They give
 ||A_N||infty<=a0 N^-1/2, Lip(A_N)<=la0 N^-1/2,
 ||Sigma_N||F,infty<=s0 N^-3/4, Lip_F(Sigma_N)<=ls0 N^-3/4,
 ||U_N||infty<=h0 sqrt(N), Lip(U_N)<=u0 sqrt(N).
For the drift derivative use ||C-(x^TCx)I-2x(Cx)^T||<=Lc(1+3r0^2)
and ||(b.x)I+x b^T||<=2B r0. For the potential use
||grad V||<=Lc r0 sqrt(N)/2+B N^1/4/2<=a0 sqrt(N).
The cutoff products add kchi times the respective suprema. Matching zero

Conservative rational upper enclosures may replace each displayed radical.

2. INDEPENDENT WEIGHT-MOMENT CHECK, INCLUDING THE ACTUAL EULER LAW

For N>=2 set y=N^1/4|x|. On the inner ball,
 V(x)>=(1-1/N)y^2/4-B y/2>=y^2/8-B y/2>=-B^2/2.
Multiplication by chi in [0,1] preserves this lower bound, as does the zero
extension. Thus every exact SDE/Euler path weight w>=exp(-B^2/2), and both
mean masses mu>=exp(-B^2/2). S01's imported target-trace mass lower bound
is unnecessary for this cutoff and its Euler law.

Let Q be the supremum of the martingale magnitude by time 1, continuous or
at Euler nodes, and Z=N^1/4 Q. Each coordinate quadratic variation (or sum
of conditional Gaussian variances) is <=Tc/(2N^1.5). The exponential
martingale maximal bound, optimized over its parameter and unioned over
three coordinates and signs, gives conditionally on EVERY initial seed
 P(Z>x | m0)<=6 exp[-N x^2/(3Tc)].
If N>=6Tc K, integration of this tail gives
 E[exp(K Z^2)|m0]<=1+6K/[N/(3Tc)-K]<=7.
Coordinate independence and independence from the seed are not assumed.

The drift obeys globally |A_N(x)|<=Lc|x|/(2sqrt N)+B/(2N^.75).
Continuous/discrete Gronwall then yields
 N^1/4 sup|X| or N^1/4 max|Y|
 <=exp(Lc/2)[Y0+B/2+Z], Y0=N^1/4|m0|.
The discrete version uses the cumulative martingale and (1+Lh)^m<=e^L.
Euler nodes may overshoot; these global coefficients are defined there.
The potential upper bound, valid also when chi V<0 and outside the ball,
 U_N(x)<=(Lc+2)y^2/4+Tc/4+B^2/8.
For fixed p>=1 put Dp=p(Lc+2)/4 and K=3Dp exp(Lc). The square of the
three-term path bound implies
 E w^p, E w_E^p
 <=7 exp[pTc/4+pB^2/8+K B^2/4] E exp(K Y0^2).

The heat seed, projected to radius r0/2, contracts the original magnitude.
Write R=N^1/4 r and y=R/(2N^1/4). Its unnormalized scaled radial density is
 R^2 sinch(y) exp[-Lambda R^2/(16d0)-N f(y)],
 d0=1-Lambda/(2sqrt N), f(y)=y^2/2-logcosh y.
For N>=Lambda^2 its normalizer is >=Lbase=(7/3)e^(-Lambda/2-1/12),
by R in [1,2], sinch>=1 and f(y)<=y^4/12. Also Y0<=R/2.
For y<=1 use sinch(y)<=e and f(y)>=y^4/48. For y>=1 use sinch<=e^y
and f(y)>=delta y^2, delta=1/2-logcosh(1)>0. If sqrt N>=8(d+1)/delta,
the tilted outer integrand is <=R^2 exp(-R^2): y<=R^2/(4sqrt N) there.
Completing the square gives
 -R^4/768+dR^2<=-R^4/1536+384d^2.
The integrals of R^2e^(-R^4/1536) and R^2e^(-R^2) are respectively <768
and <1/2. (Substitute R^4/1536; Gamma(3/4)<4/3+e^-1<2.) Therefore
 E_seed exp(dR^2)<=J(d):=[768 exp(1+384d^2)+1/2]/Lbase.
Taking d=K/4 supplies an explicit Kp independent of N and step count.
Use rational upper enclosures and replace Tc by 3Lc in thresholds as needed.
These constants may be astronomically large; fixed M,B does not mean cheap.
This independently confirms the structure of S03 A5-A12 and S01 Section 3,
with slightly weaker radial constants to simplify the audit.

3. RETAIN THE N-SCALING IN THE STRONG ESTIMATE

Take h=1/m. Let Y_t be the unprojected continuous Euler interpolation and
barY_t its preceding node. Couple it to the exact cutoff SDE X_t with the
same Brownian motion and initial seed. Conditional on a node, the Gaussian
mean cross term vanishes. It is enough to use the looser uniform lag bound
 l:=sup_t E|Y_t-barY_t|^2
 <=2a0^2 N^-1 h^2+2s0^2 N^-3/2 h.
Do NOT replace this by an N-independent constant times h prematurely.

Let d(t)=E sup_(u<=t)|X_u-Y_u|^2. Cauchy-Schwarz in time for the drift,
Doob L2/Ito isometry for the stochastic integral, and |v+w|^2<=2|v|^2+2|w|^2
give
 d(t)<=Q_N integral_0^t[d(u)+l]du,
 Q_N=4la0^2/N+16ls0^2/N^1.5.
The factors 4,16 arise by first splitting drift/noise (2 and 8 after Doob)
and then splitting X-barY into X-Y and Y-barY (factor 2). Gronwall gives
 d(1)<=Q_N e^(Q_N) l.
With q0=(4la0^2+16ls0^2) exp(4la0^2+16ls0^2),
 Q_N e^(Q_N)<=q0/N,
 d(1)<=2q0[a0^2 N^-2 h^2+s0^2 N^-5/2 h].
This is a proved upper bound, not a sharpness claim or path measurement.

Let u=int U_N(X_t)dt, u_E=h sum U_N(Y_j). Jensen in time gives
 E|u-u_E|^2<=u0^2 N integral E|X_t-barY_t|^2dt
 <=2u0^2 N[d(1)+l].
Using |e^u-e^v|<=(e^u+e^v)|u-v| and the uniform K2 yields
 E|w-w_E|<=2sqrt(K2)u0 sqrt(2N[d(1)+l]).

Final endpoints must be projected to the INNER ball: z=Pi_r0(Y_m).
The exact X_1 lies in that ball and projection does not enlarge |X_1-Y_m|.
Every local state tau_x=(I+x.sigma)/2 there has eigenvalues >=(1-r0)/2.
The Sylvester equation for square roots gives
 ||sqrt(tau_x)-sqrt(tau_z)||F<=|x-z|/[2sqrt(1-r0)].
Use these matrices as normalized purifications. If their overlap is c>=0,
1-c^2<=||sqrt(tau_x)-sqrt(tau_z)||F^2. Tensoring and
1-c^(2N)<=N(1-c^2) yield
 T(k_N(x),k_N(z))<=sqrt(N)|x-z|/[2sqrt(1-r0)].
This repairs use of a full-rank constant after projection only to the unit
ball. With the inner projection the exact full raw operator difference is
 <=2sqrt(K2)u0 sqrt(2N[d(1)+l])
       +sqrt(K2) sqrt(N d(1))/sqrt(1-r0).
No operator dimension factor or high-dimensional empirical norm is hidden.
Normalize using mu_cut>=e^(-B^2/2). Define a rational upper enclosure C>=1 of
 e^(B^2/2)sqrt(2K2) max(a0,s0)
       [2u0 sqrt(2(1+q0))+sqrt(q0)/sqrt(1-r0)].
Then the whole-state trace error obeys
 T(sigma_cut,sigma_E)<=C[h+N^-1/4 sqrt(h)].
It suffices to choose
 m=ceil max{1,2C/eta,4C^2/(sqrt N eta^2)}.
All ceilings/radicals can use rational upper enclosures; no asymptotic choice
oracle is necessary. Fixed M,B constants dominate any practical experiment.

conditional on that exact differential/heat identity and Duhamel theorem.
Those identities are audited separately by S01; this cost audit does not
pretend to be a new independent replay of the all-N noncommutative proof.

For R independent Euler proposals, select one proportional to positive w_i.
For every bounded quantum test f, |nu_hat(f)|<=mu_hat, so
 |E[nu_hat(f)/mu_hat]-E[w f]/mu|
 <=E|mu_hat-mu|/mu<=sqrt(K2/R) exp(B^2/2).

Real-arithmetic path updates are

5. FINITE-BIT GUARDS WHICH THE INTEGRATED CODE MUST ACTUALLY IMPLEMENT

(i) Heat-seed and Gaussian inverse-CDF routines need a declared COUPLING:
outside a capped bad event, seed/Brownian increments are pointwise xi-close
to ideal draws. Finite discrete variables cannot be TV-close to a continuous
normal. Let D be the full finite number of scalar draws. Choose a rational
L>=sqrt(2 log(2D/delta_bad)); the Gaussian union tail is <=delta_bad.
Within [-L,L], the density has a positive explicitly enclosable lower bound.
Use uniform-bit mesh and certified CDF intervals tighter than xi times that
bound, returning an xi interval when a comparison remains ambiguous. All
normal/seed caps and fallback outputs must be charged, including seed
rejection acceptance/normalization. This audit did not independently replay
the entire cycle1 seed compiler; that dependency remains DECLARED, not a
fresh execution. Our local fixture needs no such real-normal/seed oracle.

(ii) For retained normal coordinates |Z_j|<=L, each exact Euler node map has
Lipschitz factor <=1+la0 N^-1/2 h+ls0 N^-3/4 sqrt(3) L sqrt(h). The product
is bounded by
 S_N=exp[la0 N^-1/2+sqrt(3)ls0 N^-3/4 L sqrt(m)].
Every completed update must be enclosed, including roots, coefficient
errors, normal errors and final rounding. If its local error is <=xi and
the seed error <=xi, node error is <=(m+1)S_N xi. If primitive xi is used
instead, include the explicit coefficient/noise/update multipliers first.
Use a node tolerance <=eta/[100(1+u0+1/sqrt(1-r0))sqrt N], enough both for
the tensor output and integrated logweight. Potential evaluation and each
accumulator rounding also need allocated tolerances; m accumulated raw
rounding errors cannot be silently called one error.
An adequate log2 precision includes log((m+1)/node_tol)+log2 S_N and the
input length, tails and elementary-evaluation guards. The conservative S01
P=O_(M,B)(Lsqrt(m)+sqrt(N)+Lin+log(NmR/eta)) is safe polynomial. Keeping
the N factors reduces its stability term. hN=O(sqrt N) worst-weight guards
add O(sqrt N) BITS, not exp(sqrt N) bits.

(iii) Root residuals must imply FACTOR error for the factor actually used.
A principal-root error estimate cannot silently certify an arbitrary
Cholesky factor. If a rational lower triangular positive-diagonal F has
||F F^T-D||F<=epsilon_D<=1/4, F is the Cholesky factor of its own covariance.
Along the covariance segment the spectral floor is >=1/2 and operator
bound <=Lc+1/4. The derivative proof in Section 1 then gives
 ||F-chol(D)||F<=2sqrt(Lc+1/4)epsilon_D.
Divide by sqrt(2)N^.75 and multiply by chi for the Sigma error guard.
At rational nodes covariance and Frobenius residuals are exactly rational,
with rational square-root upper enclosures. No eigenvalue computation is
needed. Root pivot intervals and their denominators must stay positive.

(iv) Project final emitted endpoints to r0 with a certified norm upper bound
and inward rescaling, so the rational emitted vector is EXACTLY positive
and retains the full-rank margin. Approximate irrational radius r0 by a
certified lower radius, and charge their difference to node/output error.
Keep Euler nodes unprojected; modifying every node would change the scheme
and require a different strong-error proof.

(v) Compare approximate logweights before exponentiation. A uniform error
<=epsilon_l multiplies unnormalized odds by factors in e^[+-epsilon_l]
and changes normalized categorical law by <=e^(2epsilon_l)-1. Subtract max
logweight; at least one scaled weight is one and sum is >=1. Use certified
positive rational/dyadic weights; if rounding R-1 probabilities down at
q bits and assigning the residual to the last, TV <=(R-1)2^-q. A q-bit
uniform integer and exact cumulative comparisons realize the law exactly.
All proposals must be guarded before this selection; an empirical maximum
or unbounded exp/log call is not an enclosure.

(vi) Couple the ENTIRE R-proposal algorithm, selection and emitted state.
On the event every ideal/finite draw is retained, pointwise state and
relative-logweight tolerances above control the output. The complementary
event costs at most its probability because all fallback/emitted states
are valid. Do not apply this bare bad-event bound to weighted raw operators
without weight moments or an exp(hN) multiplier. The algorithmic coupling
avoids that error. Native per-qubit preparation/measurement errors and
finite-gate compilation remain separately charged interfaces.
