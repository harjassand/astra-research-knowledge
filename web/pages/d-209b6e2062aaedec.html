7 October 2026; requested gpt-6-luna/max, observed model UNKNOWN.

RESULT

I derived and checked an exact two-mode analytic example showing how joint
posterior geometry changes archive memory while Fisher data stays fixed. On
the common observation space T^2 with uniform reference, compare

  S_v(t_1,t_2)=1+(1/4)[v_1 cos(2 pi t_1)+v_2 cos(4 pi t_1)],
  P_v(t_1,t_2)=1+(1/4)[v_1 cos(2 pi t_1)+v_2 cos(2 pi t_2)],
  ||v||_2<=1.

Both densities are analytic and lie strictly between 5/8 and 11/8. Both have
the same tangent Fisher Gram (1/32)I_2, and for every v their full Fisher
metrics lie between (1/44)I_2 and (1/20)I_2. In S, the score features satisfy
V=2U^2-1 and their joint law lies on a curve. In P, the two reference features
are independent. Exact Laurent-polynomial arithmetic verifies the moments,
the deterministic relation and the Fisher matrix.

For S, a reference-preserving periodic triangular-hat codec stores one label
and regenerates the ancillary coordinate. Its exact error is at most
3025/(588 J^2). For P, two independent hat labels give an exact error bound
605/(392 J^2) with total memory dimension J^2. Proper-score regret and
entropy-distortion lower bounds match these upper orders, including for
unassisted hybrid/quantum memories. Thus

  B_q^S(epsilon)=(1/2)log_2(1/epsilon)+O(1),
  B_q^P(epsilon)=log_2(1/epsilon)+O(1).

At epsilon=1/1000, the rational certificate uses D=72 for S with TV at most
3025/3048192, and D=1600 for P with TV at most 121/125440. These certify the
continuous ideal codec, not a finite-bit output pipeline.

The same reference feature laws have Gaussian heat-flow information
I(W;W+sqrt(tau)G)=(1/2)ln(1/tau)+O(1) for the shared curve and
ln(1/tau)+O(1) for independent features, despite identical covariance
(1/2)I_2. The proof is in the revision report. This is a useful joint-posterior
diagnostic, not a new information-dimension theorem.
