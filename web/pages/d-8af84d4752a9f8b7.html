This avoids invalid operator order e^(logomega+bounded score)<=constant omega. WY defects contract under ordinary HS functional calculus. Weighted score commutators do not automatically contract under clipping without centrality; a separate SU2 total-spin cutoff and smooth functional calculus proof closes that gate.

## Exact interacting counterexample

dimer_counterexample.md gives a connected ring cluster-chain Hamiltonian

H_N(theta)=-sum_i [cos(theta) Z_(i-1)X_i Z_(i+1)+sin(theta) Z_(i-1)Y_i Z_(i+1)].

At fixed inverse temperature a>0, every one-site marginal is exactly I/2 for all theta. A supplied depth-two CZ circuit reduces the entire state to nontracial iid logical qubits. Under theta=u/sqrtN, strong qubit LAN and thermal no-broadcasting compactness imply a positive EB floor. This refutes inference from local marginal traciality or zero one-dimensional tangent symplectic form to strong classical experiment preservation. Those conceptual obstructions were already explained in 2006 primary LAN work; the cluster chain is an explicit local Gibbs corollary, not a claimed new no-broadcasting principle.

check_cluster.py independently verified the exact Gibbs/CZ factorization, commuting interaction terms, and vanishing one-site Pauli expectations at N=4,6 and three angles; errors were below 9e-16. The checks verify conventions only.

## Later escalation: pairwise intrinsic defect without uniform entropy

PAIRWISE_ENTROPY_BOUNDARY.md independently audits and strengthens the thermal example. For m modes of common thermal mean n, root fidelity is exp[-s/(4n+2)] and affinity is exp[-s/D], D=(sqrt(n+1)+sqrt(n))^2. The exact supremum pair gap is (kappa-1)kappa^[-kappa/(kappa-1)]<=1/(eD^2)=O(n^-2), kappa=1+D^-2, independent of m. Setting m=n^3 makes the arbitrary-collective EB minimax risk tend to one while the pair gap tends to zero.

Thermal localization gives bounded compact displacement boxes. A Fock cutoff with leakage n^-12 makes the experiments finite-dimensional: reconstruction lower bounds lose at most n^-12+n^-6 and pair-gap bounds change by at most 4sqrt(2)n^-3. Finite 1/n trace-distance nets then give FINITE faithful-state experiments with the same limits. A projected binary displacement packing contains exponentially many states separated by trace distance tending to one, making the failure of uniform covering entropy explicit.

This is a counterexample to a pairwise-fidelity/affinity criterion WITHOUT a uniform metric-entropy premise; it does not refute a criterion retaining that premise. All primary fidelity conventions were checked in Banchi et al equations (8)--(9); the affinity, maximization, cutoff transfer and finite-net steps were independently derived. Local scalar diagnostics checked n=0,1,2,10,100,1000 and do not substitute for the proof. External correctness/priority remains open.
