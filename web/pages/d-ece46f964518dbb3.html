## 1. The strongest general result: classical archives beyond qubits

Your supplied state already contains the finite-spin measure–prepare mechanism, uniform qubit bounds near the maximally mixed state, and explicit open directions involving qudits and correlated inputs. The result below extends those interfaces; it does not replace the sharper qubit memory bounds already recorded. Pasted text

Write
\[
T(\rho,\sigma)=\frac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1,
\qquad C_d=\frac{d(d-1)}2.
\]

### Derived theorem

For every \(n\ge1\) and \(d\ge2\), there are a **finite classical encoder** \(\mathcal E_{n,d}\) and a quantum decoder \(\mathcal R_{n,d}\), depending only on \(n,d\), such that every positive-definite \(d\)-dimensional state satisfies
\[
\boxed{
T\!\left(
\mathcal R_{n,d}\mathcal E_{n,d}(\rho^{\otimes n}),
\rho^{\otimes n}
\right)
\le
1-
\left(
\frac{\lambda_{\min}(\rho)}
{\lambda_{\max}(\rho)}
\right)^{C_d}.
}
\]

In particular,
\[
\boxed{
\sup_{\|d\rho-I\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\delta}
T\!\left(
\mathcal R_{n,d}\mathcal E_{n,d}(\rho^{\otimes n}),
\rho^{\otimes n}
\right)
\le
1-\left(\frac{1-\delta}{1+\delta}\right)^{C_d}
=O_d(\delta).
}
\]

The important quantifier is **uniformity in \(n\)**. Consequently, for every fixed \(d\) and every sequence \(\delta_n\to0\), the entire shrinking family admits vanishing-error classical storage, regardless of whether \(n\delta_n^2\) tends to zero, stays bounded, or diverges.

The construction does not require the unknown spectrum or eigenbasis. The same encoder and decoder work throughout the family.

A conservative finite-alphabet bound is
\[
M_{n,d}\le
2(n+d)^{(d-1)(2d+1)}.
\]
Thus the retained record uses \(O_d(\log n)\) classical bits and **zero retained qubits**.

### What this does—and does not—establish

This preserves the **whole \(n\)-system density matrix in trace distance**, not just individual marginals or a selected observable.

It does **not** establish optimal dependence on \(n\delta^2\), a Gaussian-equivalence theorem, or a polynomial-time finite-precision compiler. The alphabet bound is existential. It also does not preserve purification or correlations with an external reference.

The shrinking-family condition matters. Earlier quantum population-coding work establishes an obstruction to asymptotically faithful, entirely classical compression for fixed noncommuting families. The theorem above does not contradict that result: its family changes with \(n\), approaching full spectral degeneracy. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03372)

## 2. The proof mechanism: control the generator, preserve the Gibbs state

The central lemma is more reusable than the qudit application.

### Gibbs-preservation lemma

Let \(\mathcal B\) be a unital, trace-preserving, completely positive map. Let \(K\) be Hermitian, and suppose
\[
[\mathcal B(e^K),K]=0,
\qquad
K-\mathcal B(K)\preceq cI.
\]

Then
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B\!\left(\frac{e^K}{\operatorname{Tr}e^K}\right)
\succeq
e^{-c}\frac{e^K}{\operatorname{Tr}e^K},
}
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{
T\!\left(
\mathcal B(\tau_K),\tau_K
\right)\le1-e^{-c},
\qquad
\tau_K=\frac{e^K}{\operatorname{Tr}e^K}.
}
\]

There is **no assumption that \(\|K\|\) is small**.

The proof begins with the standard operator-Jensen inequality for the logarithm:
\[
\log\mathcal B(e^K)
\succeq
\mathcal B(K)
\succeq
K-cI.
\]
The operator-Jensen ingredient is pre-existing mathematics. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0204049)

Ordinarily, exponentiating an operator inequality would be invalid. Here the commutation hypothesis is decisive: restrict to an eigenspace of \(K\), where the right-hand side is scalar. This gives
\[
\mathcal B(e^K)\succeq e^{-c}e^K.
\]
After normalization,
\[
\mathcal B(\tau_K)
=e^{-c}\tau_K+(1-e^{-c})\sigma
\]
for a density matrix \(\sigma\), proving the trace-distance estimate.

**This is the useful primitive: exact symmetry lets a bounded generator defect control a potentially enormous Gibbs exponent.**

### Why it applies uniformly to all representation sizes

For an irreducible \(SU(d)\) representation \(V_\lambda\), use the coherent-state measurement and preparation channel
\[
\mathcal B_\lambda(X)
=
D_\lambda\int
\operatorname{Tr}(P_gX)P_g\,dg,
\]
where \(P_g\) are highest-weight coherent projectors and \(D_\lambda=\dim V_\lambda\).

The required older ingredient is an exact coadjoint-orbit quantization identity from Leung, Li and Ma. In consistent highest-weight conventions, the upper symbol of a Lie-algebra generator differs from its coherent lower symbol by the sum of the complementary positive roots. Their theorem is exact, not merely a large-representation approximation. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.14339v1)

For \(K=d\pi_\lambda(H)\), that identity yields
\[
\|K-\mathcal B_\lambda(K)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le
C_d\,\operatorname{osc}(H),
\]
where
\[
\operatorname{osc}(H)
=\lambda_{\max}(H)-\lambda_{\min}(H).
\]

Crucially, the bound does not grow with the highest weight \(\lambda\). Covariance supplies the commutation condition, so the lemma gives
\[
T(\mathcal B_\lambda(\tau_K),\tau_K)
\le
1-e^{-C_d\operatorname{osc}(H)}.
\]

For \(\rho^{\otimes n}\), Schur–Weyl decomposition reduces the problem to these blocks with
\[
H=\log\rho-\frac{\operatorname{Tr}\log\rho}{d}I.
\]
The block label is retained exactly, the coherent channel acts within the representation, and the known multiplicity state is prepared afresh.

Finally, the continuous coherent channel has an exact finite implementation: apply Carathéodory’s theorem to its Choi operator. At most \(D_\lambda^4+1\) coherent outcomes per block suffice. The Weyl dimension bound then gives the stated polynomial alphabet.

The full note specifies the root conventions, including the important distinction between a full flag and a partial flag. It also separates **existence of this finite channel** from **efficiently computing its implementation**.

### The additional conclusion derived here

Let \(\omega_N\) be a **known** rotation-invariant reference state, possibly strongly correlated, with total spin at most \(NS\). Consider the unknown weak-field family
\[
\rho_{N,b}
=
\frac{\omega_N e^{b\cdot J_N/N}}
{\operatorname{Tr}(\omega_N e^{b\cdot J_N/N})},
\qquad |b|\le B,
\]
for fixed \(B\).

For a Gibbs convention \(e^{-\beta H_0+\beta h\cdot J}\), this corresponds to the physical field
\[
h=\frac{b}{\beta N}.
\]

Suppose the zero-field total-spin law satisfies
\[
a_N:=\mathbb E_0\left|\frac{J_N}{N}-m\right|\longrightarrow0.
\]

Then the **whole quantum statistical experiment** is asymptotically equivalent to one classical \(U\in S^2\) with density
\[
\boxed{
q_b(u)=
\frac{e^{m b\cdot u}}
{\sinh(m|b|)/(m|b|)}
}
\]
relative to normalized sphere area.

This equivalence has channels in both directions, independent of the unknown \(b\).

The encoder measures total spin and a coherent direction, then **discards the total-spin label**. The decoder receives the direction, draws a fresh total-spin label from the known zero-field law, prepares the corresponding coherent state and known multiplicity state, and reconstructs the original representation.

The bounds are
\[
\sup_{|b|\le B}\operatorname{TV}(E_N\rho_{N,b},q_b)
\le s_N(B),
\]
\[
\sup_{|b|\le B}T(R_Nq_b,\rho_{N,b})
\le 1-e^{-B/N}+s_N(B),
\]
with
\[
s_N(B)=
e^{SB}\left(
Ba_N+\frac{SB^2}{4N}
\right).
\]

### Why this is stronger than moment convergence

A limiting magnetization law describes selected observables. The recovery channel above reconstructs the **entire density-state family**.

The proof uses the exact coherent likelihood
\[
\ell_{j,b}(u)=
\left[
\cosh\frac{|b|}{2N}
+
(\widehat b\cdot u)\sinh\frac{|b|}{2N}
\right]^{2j}.
\]
Its logarithm satisfies
\[
0\le
\log\ell_{j,b}(u)-\frac jN b\cdot u
\le\frac{SB^2}{4N}.
\]
Combining this with \(j/N\to m\) controls the **mixing law**. The coherent Gibbs-preservation estimate supplies the remaining quantum reconstruction error.

This implements the useful principle already present in your qubit work: preserve the distribution of latent preparations rather than demanding accurate reconstruction of each latent pure state. Pasted text

## 4. A second reusable result: when small commutators really imply classical storage

I also derived a criterion that does not require group symmetry.

Let \(\omega_n\) be known reference states and \(A_{n,1},\ldots,A_{n,k}\) a fixed number of Hermitian contractions satisfying
\[
[\omega_n,A_{n,i}]=0.
\]
Consider
\[
\rho_{n,b}
=
\frac{\omega_n e^{\sum_i b_iA_{n,i}}}
{\operatorname{Tr}\omega_n e^{\sum_i b_iA_{n,i}}},
\qquad \|b\|_1\le R,
\]
with fixed \(R>0\).

Define
\[
\eta_n=
\max_{i,j}
\left(
\operatorname{Tr}\omega_n
[A_{n,i},A_{n,j}]^\dagger
[A_{n,i},A_{n,j}]
\right)^{1/2}.
\]

### Derived criterion

Under these exact assumptions,
\[
\boxed{
\text{vanishing uniform classical reconstruction error}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\eta_n\to0.
}
\]

The sufficiency construction is explicit. Perform \(q\) repeated weak binary measurements per charge **on the same input system**, using effects
\[
E_{i,\pm}=\frac{I\pm A_i/2}{2},
\]
and retain only the \(k\) plus-counts. The alphabet has size \((q+1)^k\), and
\[
\boxed{
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{reconstruction}}
\le
2Re^{3R}
\sqrt{\frac4q+(k-1)q\eta}.
}
\]
Choosing \(q\asymp\eta^{-1/2}\) gives \(O_{k,R}(\eta^{1/4})\) error.

The recovery map is the reference-conditional state
\[
R(z)=
\frac{\omega^{1/2}F_z\omega^{1/2}}
{\operatorname{Tr}(\omega F_z)}.
\]
This is a Petz-type construction; quantum sufficiency and this recovery perspective are prior art, not inventions of this run. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0412093)

The new derivation bounds reconstruction directly by the mean-square error of the measurement pointers. It keeps positivity and normalization throughout.

For the converse, any classical encoder and quantum decoder with error at most \(\varepsilon\) on the reference and the states \(\rho_{te_i}\), \(0<t\le R\), must satisfy
\[
\boxed{
\varepsilon
\ge
2^{-17}t^4e^{-4t}\eta^4.
}
\]
The proof combines a quantitative fidelity deficit with a Naimark dilation: accurate classical recovery forces the charges to be close, in the state-weighted norm, to commuting pointer observables.

### An important negative result

The boundedness assumption cannot be dropped.

For spin \(j=N/2\), take
\[
A_i=\frac{J_i}{\sqrt N},
\qquad
\omega=\frac{I}{N+1}.
\]
Then
\[
\|[A_x,A_y]\|_{2,\omega}
\longrightarrow\frac1{2\sqrt3}>0,
\]
yet the bounded-\(u\) family proportional to
\[
e^{u\cdot J/\sqrt N}
\]
is classicalized with error \(O(N^{-1/2})\).

Thus “classicalization occurs exactly when commutators vanish” would be false without the bounded-score hypothesis. The symmetry-based theorem and the bounded-score theorem identify **different mechanisms**, not one unrestricted principle.

### Strongest relevant prior art

The provenance is substantial and should constrain any novelty claim.

Coherent measure–prepare benchmarks for unknown iid qubits and displaced thermal states already existed in Guță–Bowles–Adesso’s work. Quantum local asymptotic normality already supplies an experiment-level framework. [APS Journals](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.82.042310)

Likewise, Glebsky’s normalized Hilbert–Schmidt almost-commuting results and Ogata’s commuting approximants for macroscopic observables are close prior art for the bounded-score criterion. **The general idea that sufficiently classical observables enable classical descriptions is not new.** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.14339v1)

The recent Lahiry manuscript is also relevant. Its inspected statements concern sufficiency relative to invariant measurement classes and spectral or thermal functionals. That is not automatically the same as reconstructing the full density family with an unknown orientation, but its full scope still requires a deeper overlap check. ### Largest remaining obstacle

There are two distinct barriers.

**For a trustworthy new scientific claim**, the next requirement is an external proof and priority audit: especially the all-representation normalization in the qudit argument, the possible prior appearance of the uniform Gibbs comparison, and the hard concentration input in the released ferromagnet manuscript.

**For a substantially larger practical consequence**, the unresolved problem is an efficient, finite-precision archive with sharp memory–error tradeoffs in growing parameter windows, partial spectral collisions, or families with additional unknown background parameters. Reconstructing a known correlated Gibbs background can still be computationally hard. Preserving the statistics of arbitrary entangled measurements through a recovery channel is not the same as efficiently simulating those measurements classically.
