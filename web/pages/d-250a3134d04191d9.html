ATTRIBUTION AND SCOPE REPAIR

The immutable INITIAL used the pair X_0=0 and compactly supported alternatives. That proves a trajectory-query no-go in the class allowing zeros, but the baseline entropy-power interface often assumes X is nowhere zero. This revision repairs that scope gap: on one fixed flat T^3, the null and every alternative are smooth, divergence-free, and nowhere zero, and all alternatives agree with their null outside one hidden transverse disk. The result is still an internal analytic minimax bound, not a novelty or field-breaking claim.

THEOREM: FINITE STARTS CANNOT UNIFORMLY ACQUIRE POSITIVE VOLUME ENTROPY

Fix any target rate eta>0. Use the imported Berger–Turaev existence result to choose a smooth area-preserving disk diffeomorphism f, equal to identity on a boundary collar, with normalized-area metric entropy h0>0. Choose a smooth area-preserving isotopy g_s from id to f, supported in the disk and stationary near s=0,1; this uses the standard isotopy-to-Hamiltonian fact for area-preserving disk maps. Write v_s=(d g_s/ds) o g_s^{-1} for its divergence-free Eulerian generator.

Let the observation manifold be the fixed flat T^3=T^2 x S^1 with product volume dx_1 dx_2 dt of total volume one. Choose an integer q>4 eta/(pi h0). For each M=n^2, place M disjoint equal Euclidean disks D_1,...,D_M on an n-by-n grid in T^2, each of normalized area

    alpha = eta/(q M h0),       radius r = sqrt(alpha/pi).

The condition on q makes their diameters strictly smaller than the grid spacing 1/n, so this packing exists for every n. Let psi_j map the unit disk to D_j by the radius-r similarity and set g_{j,s}=psi_j g_s psi_j^{-1}. This is area-preserving for the standard area form, is stationary near the time endpoints, and is the identity on a collar of D_j. Its generator is

    v_{j,s}(x)=r v_s(psi_j^{-1}x).

On T^2 x S^1 define the autonomous three-dimensional field

    Y_j(x,t) = (v_{j,t}(x), 1),
    a_M = q M,
    X_0^(M) = a_M partial_t,
    X_j^(M) = a_M Y_j.

The time dependence of v_{j,t} is a dependence on the state coordinate t, so X_j is a steady autonomous field on T^3. Since div_x(v_{j,t})=0, div X_j=0. Its t component is a_M>0, so it is nowhere zero. The isotopy is identity near t=0,1, making the field smooth on S^1. Outside D_j x S^1, X_j=X_0^(M).

Equivalently, this is the suspension of f_j=psi_j f psi_j^{-1} on the mapping torus N_(f_j), trivialized by Psi_j([x,t])=(g_{j,t}(x),t). This is well-defined across (x,1)~(f_j(x),0), preserves dx_1 dx_2 dt because each g_{j,t} preserves area, and is the identity outside D_j x S^1. It sends the suspension field partial_t to Y_j. The unit-roof suspension entropy formula therefore gives h_mu(Phi^1_(Y_j))=h_area(f_j)=alpha h0.

On the section t=0, the return map is psi_j f psi_j^{-1} on D_j and the identity outside. Every other t-slice return map is conjugate to this one by the partial isotopy, so the unit-roof entropy value is the same on every slice.

The constant time change X_j=a_M Y_j yields

    h_mu(Phi^1_{X_0^(M)}) = 0,
    h_mu(Phi^1_{X_j^(M)}) = a_M alpha h0 = eta.

Here a_M=qM is an integer, so the entropy scaling is also the elementary iterate identity h(Phi^{a_M}_{Y_j})=a_M h(Phi^1_{Y_j}). The null time-one map is the identity; its continuous paths are vertical loops. No member of the pair has a zero of X.

For any randomized adaptive procedure choosing at most m initial points and observing each true path for any finite duration, form the uniform alternative mixture Pbar=M^{-1} sum_j P_j. Couple the procedure's random bits and common measurement-noise channel under X_0^(M) and X_J^(M), with J uniform. Under the null transcript each chosen initial point has a fixed T^2 coordinate; it belongs to at most one D_j. If J is not among the at most m disks visited by those null starts, every queried true path under X_J equals the null vertical path for all real times. By induction the adaptive transcripts coincide. Thus the coupling mismatch probability is at most m/M, and

    TV(P_0, Pbar) <= m/M.

This argument allows exact, arbitrarily high precision path observations and unbounded trajectory duration. A common noisy observation channel is only postprocessing and cannot increase TV. Le Cam's equal-prior bound gives minimum zero-versus-eta testing error at least (1-m/M)/2. If m<M/3 this exceeds 1/3. An estimator uniformly eta/3-accurate with success at least 2/3 would yield a test with error at most 1/3 by thresholding at eta/2, a contradiction. Hence there is no finite uniform trajectory-start budget over this nowhere-zero smooth class without a supplied regularity/energy envelope.

If the interface instead forces starts to be independent uniform-volume draws, each draw hits any specified D_J with probability alpha. The same coupling and a union bound give the stronger TV bound TV(P_0,Pbar)<=m alpha. Thus m alpha<1/3 is necessary for the same success guarantee. Combined with M=Theta(B/eta) under the energy cap above and alpha=eta/(qMh0), this random-start-only interface has the lower bound m=Omega(B/eta^2), with fixed gadget constants. This stronger rate is not claimed for algorithms allowed to place each start adversarially and strategically.

STRAIN-ENERGY / POWER COST OF THIS NOWHERE-ZERO FAMILY

In flat product coordinates, for Y_j=(r v_s(z),1),

    |Def Y_j|^2 = |sym D_z v_s|^2 + (r^2/2)|partial_s v_s|^2.

Let

    A0 = integral_0^1 integral_D |sym D v_s|^2 dz ds,
    B0 = (1/2) integral_0^1 integral_D |partial_s v_s|^2 dz ds.

Both are finite fixed-gadget constants, and A0>0: otherwise every compactly supported v_s would be a Euclidean Killing field and hence zero, forcing f=id and h0=0. Since dx=r^2 dz and alpha=pi r^2,

    integral_T3 |Def Y_j|^2 dmu = (alpha/pi) A0 + (alpha^2/pi^2) B0,
    integral_T3 |Def X_j|^2 dmu
       = [q A0/(pi h0)] M eta + [B0/(pi^2 h0^2)] eta^2.

Thus under a supplied normalized strain-energy cap B, for fixed eta and fixed gadget constants, choose M=n^2 below (B-C2 eta^2)/(C1 eta), where C1=q A0/(pi h0)>0 and C2=B0/(pi^2 h0^2). For large B this gives M=Theta(B/eta), up to the fixed constants and square rounding, and forces m=Omega(B/eta). Under the stated stationary strain-viscosity convention, choose the residual sustaining force F=nabla_X X-2nu div Def X (with pressure zero); then W=2nu V integral|Def X|^2. Since V=1 here, a power cap W_max corresponds to B=W_max/(2nu).

For comparison, the immutable compactly supported X_0=0 construction proves the stronger m=Omega(B/eta^2) scaling when zeros are allowed. The present nowhere-zero construction has the weaker but scope-matched O(M eta) energy cost. No claim is made that either lower bound is optimal.

ACQUISITION AND NOISE BOUNDARIES

The no-go charges the number of starting points. It does not rely on measurement noise, finite precision, or short observation horizons: on every unhit vertical orbit the complete exact path is identical under both hypotheses. It assumes starts can be selected but each queried trajectory has one actual initial state; pointwise field/derivative access, a continuum of simultaneous starts, or a supplied global certificate is outside the interface. Noise that perturbs the actual dynamics and transports points between invariant regions is also outside this measurement-channel model.

The energy cap alone does not bound pointwise speed: the common null field has speed a_M=qM but zero strain. The strain-viscosity work convention charges no cost for this uniform translation. A supplied speed cap, sensor bandwidth, or actuation cost can restrict the construction and must be included in a different acquisition interface; none is silently treated as free physical hardware here.

The baseline inequality H^2 <= (1/2) integral |Def X|^2 dmu is consistent with the construction: its fixed positive H=eta is paid for by growing strain energy as the hidden disk shrinks. The result is an acquisition lower bound, not a challenge to the continuum entropy-power inequality. The strain-cone certificate in c08_s03 is consistent because it assumes global derivative/Lipschitz enclosures and a strict cone margin; its spatial derivative-oracle interface is stronger than finite path starts. Conditional symbolic-cost theorems in c08_l02/l06/l09/l10 remain conditional on supplied positive-volume invariant coding/rate/error data. This minimax construction shows why those data cannot be inferred uniformly from a finite number of trajectories in the unrestricted smooth class.
