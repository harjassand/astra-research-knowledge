SPIN_STATISTICAL — LOCAL CRITICALITY, GIBBS PREPARATION, AND AXIAL DEPTH
Date: 2026-10-08

Decision
========

The strongest new mathematical result in this programme is a local, finite-range z=2 critical whole-state theorem for the open spin-1/2 XX chain. At the saturation field and the finite-size scaling beta_N=tau(N+1)^2, its exact Gibbs experiment converges in whole-state trace distance to a countable product of quadratic-energy fermion modes. Every fixed physical region instead converges to the polarized vacuum. A parameter-blind total-excitation encoder gives a uniform D-label archive error with leading tail exp[-(4/3) tau J D^3], uniformly in N. This is a model-specific theorem and a counterexample to transferring the all-to-all quartic-vector D^(-2/3) law as a universal local-critical law. It is not a theorem about arbitrary local critical lattices, and the archive bound is not known optimal.

The most promising noncommuting extension is the open anisotropic XY chain at gamma_N=kappa/(N+1). Its exact Jordan–Wigner pairing kernel has an order-one scaled limit between low modes, but the XX sufficient statistic is destroyed by vacuum/two-particle coherence. An exact N=2 calculation verifies this failure. The remaining high-ceiling gate is a uniform full-Fock trace-norm limit for the normalized BdG Gibbs states and a parameter-blind memory theorem; neither is yet proved in the Luna work. Sol has a promising mode-occupation tail route in a parallel independent audit, reported to the root as provisional.

A separate constructive result covers finite-range diagonal Gibbs chains with explicit finite-bit local-circuit costs. For genuinely noncommuting 1D Gibbs states, a strong fixed-beta whole-state block-MPS preparation theorem already exists in primary literature, but its bit-level arithmetic and gate-synthesis contract is incomplete and its beta-dependent depth is enormous. No N90 basis sampler should be described as quantum-state preparation.

The latest axial Gibbs package has an internally audited candidate for a sharp uniform full-separability interval, delta<=2 ln 2, on the whole N-qubit Hilbert space, and a replayed N=72 NPT witness above that interval. Independent audits found no surviving proof seam in the N117 analytic/prefix join, but they did not replay all 197 prefix cases or externally validate the theorem. The closest model predecessor is a 2026 all-sector collective XXZ study, so the theorem's priority remains unknown. The candidate N118 finite-bit compiler is not end-to-end practical at N=64 under the old 60-second cap, and the universal backend remains unexecuted.

1. Local saturation criticality: exact XX theorem
=================================================

Model and interface. Let P_N be the open path, L=N+1, J>0, tau>0, and lambda in a supplied compact interval [-B,B]. Put n_i=(I-Z_i)/2, M=sum_i n_i, beta_N=tau L^2, and

  H_(N,lambda) = -J sum_(i=1)^(N-1) (X_i X_(i+1)+Y_i Y_(i+1))
                  +(4J-lambda/L^2) M.

The chain, J, tau, N, field window, and zero-anisotropy coupling are supplied; lambda is unknown and is not queried. The one-particle dispersion at saturation is 4J(1-cos q)=2Jq^2+O(q^4), so this is the z=2 saturation window with detuning and temperature of order L^(-2).

Exact diagonalization. Jordan–Wigner followed by the parameter-independent open-chain sine transform gives

  H_(N,lambda)=sum_(k=1)^N [4J(1-cos(pi k/L))-lambda/L^2] d_k^dag d_k.

In the mode-Fock basis the Gibbs state is exactly a product of independent Bernoulli modes with

  p_(L,k)(lambda)=[1+exp(tau(4J L^2(1-cos(pi k/L))-lambda))]^(-1).

Embed the finite chain into infinite-mode Fock space with all k>N in vacuum. The limit is

  rho_(infinity,lambda)=tensor_(k>=1)[(1-p_k)|0><0|+p_k|1><1|],
  p_k(lambda)=[1+exp(tau(2J pi^2 k^2-lambda))]^(-1).

For q in [0,pi], 1-cos(q)>=2q^2/pi^2; Taylor's theorem gives
|4J L^2(1-cos(pi k/L))-2J pi^2 k^2|<=J pi^4 k^4/(6L^2). The logistic derivative and the quadratic lower energy bound make the sum of coordinate probability errors O_(J,tau,B)(L^-2); the omitted infinite-mode tail is O(exp(-c_(J,tau)L^2)). Coupling the Bernoulli coordinates and using equality of trace distance and total variation for diagonal states proves

  sup_(|lambda|<=B) T(rho_(N,lambda),rho_(infinity,lambda))=O_(J,tau,B)(N^-2).

The limit is a regular one-parameter exponential family with finite positive Fisher information tau^2 sum_k p_k(1-p_k). Distinct fixed lambdas have a strictly positive whole-state limiting distance. By contrast, for any fixed ell physical sites R, E M_R<=C ell/L and

  T(rho_(N,lambda)^R, |up...up><up...up|)<=C ell/L.

Thus the whole state retains field information in delocalized low modes while every fixed physical region loses it.

Parameter-blind archive. Conditional on total occupation m, the state

  omega_(N,m)=P_m exp[-beta_N H_(N,0)]P_m / Tr[P_m exp(-beta_N H_(N,0))]

is independent of lambda, and the unknown lambda changes only the weight of sector m by exp(tau lambda m). Measure M, store m=0,...,D-2 exactly, and map m>=D-1 to one overflow label; decode exact labels to omega_(N,m) and overflow to a fixed state. This uses D classical labels, i.e. log_2 D retained bits, with no side register. For d=D-1,

  e_(N,D)<=sup_lambda P_(N,lambda)(M>=d),
  log e_(N,D)<=-(4/3)tau J d^3+O_(J,tau,B)(d^2),

uniformly in N. The derivation uses Chernoff with s=2 tau J d^2 and the bound a_(L,k)>=8J k^2; the full explicit finite-N inequality is retained in leaf 01. Storing all N+1 sectors gives zero error. Encoding costs O(N) local Z measurements and additions. The decoder's finite-bit sampling, run time, gate count, and physical state preparation are not established. No matching lower bound on e_(N,D) is claimed.

A direct exact check for N=2,J=1,lambda=0 gives the Hamiltonian eigenvalues 0,2,6,8, matching the sine modes. Full derivation and test: `work/programmes/spin_statistical/leaves/leaf-01-local-critical-limit/RESULT.txt`, `n2_exact_check.py`, and `n2_exact_check_output.txt`.

This refutes a *universal transfer* of N20's complete-graph critical law: the N20 card's D^(-2/3) lower/upper law belongs to a known all-to-all permutation-invariant family, a nondegenerate three-dimensional unknown-field cube, and a quartic R^3 limit. The local XX family instead has a quadratic mode spectrum and a finite-mean excitation count. The local upper bound is much smaller than D^(-2/3) for large D, but it is only an upper bound and does not determine the local optimum.

A separate local obstruction prevents replacing this counterexample with a blanket positive theorem. For every odd open antiferromagnetic spin-1/2 Heisenberg chain, Lieb–Mattis gives a ground-state spin-1/2 doublet. The six Pauli eigenstates encoded in this doublet incur exact minimax whole-state trace error 1/3 under every classical measure-and-prepare channel, regardless of alphabet size; a six-outcome Pauli POVM attains the bound. This proof is an entanglement-breaking qubit Choi-fidelity argument. It concerns a coherent ground-sector family, not the finite-temperature Gibbs family or a unique-ground-state thermal experiment. See leaf 04 for proof, exact check, and the selector-field precision caveat. Primary sources: Lieb–Mattis (1962), https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1724276; des Cloizeaux–Pearson (1962), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.128.2131.

2. Noncommuting XY crossover: exact kernel and open limit
=========================================================

Use the standard anisotropy convention

  H_(N,lambda,gamma) = -J sum_i [(1+gamma) X_i X_(i+1)+(1-gamma)Y_i Y_(i+1)]
                         +(4J-lambda/L^2)M,
  gamma_N=kappa/L, beta_N=tau L^2.

For sine modes u_(ik)=sqrt(2/L)sin(pi k i/L), the exact Jordan–Wigner form is

  H=sum_k [4J(1-cos q_k)-lambda/L^2]d_k^dag d_k
       -2J gamma sum_(k<l) B_(L,kl)(d_k^dag d_l^dag+d_l d_k),
  B_(L,kl)=sum_i[u_(ik)u_((i+1)l)-u_((i+1)k)u_(il)].

For k+l odd,

  B_(L,kl)=-4/L * sin(q_k)sin(q_l)/(cos(q_k)-cos(q_l));

for k+l even it is zero. Consequently the beta-scaled pairing coefficient converges for fixed opposite-parity k,l to

  -2 tau J kappa L B_(L,kl) -> -16 tau J kappa k l/(k^2-l^2).

This is a boundary-mixed, first-derivative pairing kernel, not independent k,-k pairing. The diagonal entries converge to tau(2J pi^2 k^2-lambda). The mode basis that diagonalizes the finite BdG matrix depends on lambda, so measuring those quasiparticle occupations is not a parameter-blind encoder.

A two-site exact witness identifies the immediate archive failure. At N=2, L=3, h=4J-lambda/9 and g=2J gamma, the even block in basis |00>,|11> is [[0,-g],[-g,2h]], and the odd block in |01>,|10> is [[h,-2J],[-2J,h]]. With r=sqrt(h^2+g^2),

  Z=2 exp(-beta h)[cosh(beta r)+cosh(2 beta J)],
  c=<00|rho|11>=g sinh(beta r)/{2r[cosh(beta r)+cosh(2 beta J)]}.

For beta,J,gamma>0, c>0. Any output block diagonal in M has trace distance at least c from rho, by the norm-one witness X=|00><11|+|11><00|. Therefore the exact XX count-sector decoder fails for every nonzero anisotropy; this is not a lower bound against all classical encoders or quantum memories. The exact N=2 script passes and verifies the matrix, spectrum, commutator, and coherence formula.

Uniform whole-state limit. A separate internally reconstructed proof now closes this gate. Fix J,tau>0 and finite B,K. With L=N+1, lambda in [-B,B], kappa in [-K,K], gamma=kappa/L, and beta=tau L^2, embed the N sine-mode Fock space into F(ell^2(N)) with all modes k>=L in vacuum. The exact scaled BdG matrix is h_L=[[A_L,K_L],[-K_L,-A_L]], where A_Lk=4 tau J L^2(1-cos(pi k/L))-tau lambda and K_Lkl=-2 tau J kappa L B_Lkl. For fixed modes its limit is h=[[D-tau lambda,K],[-K,-D+tau lambda]], D=2 tau J(-partial_x^2) with Dirichlet boundary conditions and K=-4 tau J kappa partial_x.

The load-bearing uniform estimate is the normalized-state occupation bound. The exact sine-column norm is ||K_L e_k||^2=16 tau^2 J^2 kappa^2 L^2 sin^2(pi k/L)(1-2/L). The spectral residual split gives, for k>=k0=max(1,ceil(sqrt(B/(4J)))), Q_L,kk<=4 pi^2 kappa^2/k^2+exp(-2 tau J k^2), uniformly in N,lambda,kappa. Hence the tail above M is at most T_M=4 pi^2 K^2/M+sum_(k>M)exp(-2 tau J k^2), once M>=k0-1. Exact fixed-column convergence upgrades to strong convergence; relative-bound estimates give self-adjoint common-core strong-resolvent convergence, uniformly on compact parameters. Finite covariance blocks then converge uniformly. Gentle projection onto the first M modes plus T_M->0 yields uniform full Schatten-1 convergence of the normalized states in the common Fock embedding. No finite-L rate is inferred from this core argument. The proof also records a scalar scaled ground-energy shift of order -kappa^2 L; it cancels in normalized Gibbs states, so raw Hamiltonian convergence on the vacuum core is not claimed.

The same tail gives a parameter-blind quantum archive, not a classical archive. A first-M-mode cutoff has reconstruction error <=2 sqrt(T_M)+T_M and M=O(K^2/epsilon^2+sqrt(log(1/epsilon))) for fixed public J,tau,B,K. A coherent cutoff to at most r bare particles uses a Gaussian-purification number-MGF bound Tr rho exp(s N)<=exp(mu(exp(2s)-1)), together with mu<=k0+(2/3)pi^4 K^2+sum_k exp(-2 tau J k^2). Taking M=O(epsilon^-2), r=O(log(1/epsilon)/loglog(1/epsilon)) gives q=O(log^2(1/epsilon)/loglog(1/epsilon)) persistent qubits for fixed nonzero K. The encoder/decoder use O(N^2)+poly(M,r,log M) gates, with synthesis precision and at least O(N) output work charged separately. This is an upper bound only. Efficient acquisition or preparation of the low-temperature input at beta=tau L^2 is not established.

There is also a whole-family classicality obstruction. For any entanglement-breaking (measure-and-prepare) channel T and two density operators rho,sigma, affinity satisfies A(T rho,T sigma)>=F_root(rho,sigma), where A=Tr sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma) and F_root=||sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)||_1. If both reconstructions have full trace-norm error at most delta, Powers-Stormer continuity gives delta>=(F_root-A)^2/4. In the continuum XY family at lambda=0, the pair coherence has derivative c0=16(1-p1-p2)/(15 pi^2)>0 at kappa=0 and remainder at most tau J kappa^2, so rho_0 and rho_kappa fail to commute for every sufficiently small nonzero kappa. Thus their root-fidelity/affinity gap is strictly positive and every classical archive, regardless of alphabet size and even if chosen separately for each N, has a positive limiting worst-case error on this two-point subfamily. Uniform trace convergence transfers the bound to finite chains. A conservative explicit floor eta^4/65536 is also derived from a commutator estimate; it is extremely small and not a practical-margin claim. This excludes vanishing-error classicalization, not parameter-aware reconstruction, multi-copy acquisition, or restricted-observable prediction.

The reusable quadratic sufficient criterion in Sol's proof has a sharp UV boundary for independent BCS block examples: with diagonal energy order k^(z/d) and pairing order k^(r/d), the occupation-tail argument works when 2(z-r)>d and can fail sharply at or below the boundary. This is a criterion for the stated quadratic/common-core class, not every d-dimensional lattice. Araki/Shale-Stinespring quasi-free representation and implementability background is established prior art; novelty of this specific critical XY whole-state convergence, archive, and entanglement-breaking application remains unverified. Blosser et al. (2017) study z=2 saturation scaling of correlations, not this whole-state trace-norm experiment. Full proof, EB argument, and finite diagnostics are in `outputs/research/sol_xy_critical/proof.txt`, `eb_obstruction.txt`, and `checks.py`/`checks.json`.

3. Constructive finite-range thermal preparation
================================================

Two distinct positive results must not be merged.

First, an internally proved finite-bit circuit theorem covers *commuting diagonal* finite-range chains. For rational local log weights on binary windows of length r, a backwards transfer recursion computes conditional probabilities, then controlled rotations plus local copy gates produce a purification whose data marginal is within trace error epsilon of the whole Gibbs state. The exact MPS bond is at most 2^(r-1). For binary input length B and fixed r, the report gives preprocessing O(N 2^(r-1) poly(B+log N+log(1/epsilon))) bit operations, peak working memory including an O(N 2^(r-1) log(N 2^(r-1)/epsilon))-bit angle table, and O(N 2^(r-1) polylog(N 2^(r-1)/epsilon)) finite-universal-gate output after Solovay–Kitaev synthesis. Cost depends on encoded bit length rather than numerical coupling magnitude. Full proof and arithmetic policy are in leaf 18. An exact two-qubit XX Gibbs state has trace distance tanh(beta*g)/2 from its dephased state, falsifying extension by basis sampler plus copy, not all other quantum methods.

Second, Pilatowsky-Cameo, Styliaris, Bakshi, and Malz, arXiv:2609.35973v1 (2026-09-28), Theorems 1–2 and Corollary 1, source-report an exact decomposition and sampled constant-depth block-MPS preparation for arbitrary fixed-range 1D qubit Gibbs Hamiltonians at fixed finite beta. Their block size and bond dimension are bounded by exp(exp(O_r(beta))), independent of N; their classical sampler is stated poly(N,1/epsilon), and the nearest-neighbor circuit depth is exp(exp(O_r(beta))). This is already a general noncommuting finite-range fixed-beta whole-state preparation result in the paper's model. The paper does not fully specify rational input-bit complexity, matrix exponential/eigenvalue precision, probability acquisition, or fixed-gate synthesis. Its beta-dependent constant is enormous; at beta_N~N^2 the bound is useless. A separate high-temperature dissipative algorithm (Rouze, Stilck Franca, Alhambra, arXiv:2411.04885v2) gives O-tilde(N) quantum gate/simulation cost under an explicit very-high-temperature threshold. Source details and counterexamples: leaf 06. Do not call the N90 FPRAS a quantum preparation theorem.

5. Exact entanglement-depth boundaries
======================================

For the normalized full Schur projector omega_lambda=P_lambda/Tr(P_lambda) on (C^d)^tensor N, the reconstructed N121 statement is exact depth ell(lambda), the number of Young rows. Every state supported entirely in that sector has depth at least ell(lambda). The column-wedge/twirl construction gives the matching upper bound for the full projector. A state invariant under both collective U(d) and all permutations is a mixture of these projectors and has depth at most the largest occupied row count, but that maximum is not a lower bound for a mixture: I/d^N is separable while occupying all sectors. Reuvers 2019 Lemma 13/Eq. 61 and Maassen–Kümmerer notes supply prior art for the upper construction; novelty of the lower bound is unknown. Exact d=N=2 check passed.

The symmetry boundary is real. A two-spin spin-1 SU(2) singlet has depth 2 while lying in the one-row U(3) sector, so the full-U(d) exact equality cannot be transferred to the physical spin-1 SU(2) subgroup. N120's ideal collective thermal trajectory has exact depth at most 2 for its admitted collective model; its finite-bit N64 rounded ensemble is a different object and is not covered. A local XX perturbation can make a three-qubit state genuinely tripartite entangled at arbitrarily short positive times if finite h is allowed to become sufficiently negative as t shrinks. Therefore no stability window is uniform over all finite h; the threshold at fixed h remains open. Leaf 10–12 contain proofs and checks.

================================================
