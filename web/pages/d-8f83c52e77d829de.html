BAYESIAN / JOINT-MEASUREMENT BRIDGE: CORRECTED SUFFICIENT CONDITIONS
2026-10-09. Internally reconstructed lemmas and an exact failed-step witness.

1. THE PRIMARY FACTOR-TWO RESULT AND ITS EXTRA RESOURCE

Albarelli--Branford--Rubio, arXiv:2511.16645v4, primary source
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16645v4, Eqs. (14), (16), (21), (22),
(28)--(35), concerns a supplied classical--quantum parameter ensemble
{p_x,rho_x}. Put rho0=sum_x p_x rho_x, bar_rho_i=sum_x p_x x_i rho_x,
and Lambda=sum_x p_x ||x||^2. Their square-root/pretty-good measurement
has mean squared loss

 MSL_PGM=2[Lambda-sum_i Tr(bar_rho_i rho0^(-1/2)
                         bar_rho_i rho0^(-1/2))]
        <=2 MSL_SPM.

The parameter prior and its second moment Lambda are supplied data.
At rho0=I/d, write bar_rho_i=B_i/d. Then the ensemble is equivalently
a single POVM {M_x=d p_x rho_x} with real vector labels x and

 sum_x x_i M_x=B_i,   Lambda=sum_x tau(M_x)||x||^2.                 (B1)

The POVM has all first moments simultaneously. A broadcaster does not
automatically supply (B1) at an arbitrarily prescribed second-moment cost.
The PGM theorem is not a dimension-free broadcasting theorem.

2. AN EXACT ELEMENTARY MEASUREMENT LEMMA

Let {B_i} be Hermitian on C^d and let any finite POVM with real labels
satisfy (B1). Set U=sum_i tau(B_i^2). For each nonzero M_x define
p_x=tau(M_x), rho_x=M_x/Tr(M_x), and m_x,i=Tr(rho_x B_i).
Then {p_x,rho_x} has barycenter I/d, and

 U=sum_x p_x x dot m_x <= sqrt(Lambda sum_x p_x ||m_x||^2).

Refining the mixed rho_x into pure states can only increase the score
sum_i <B_i>^2, by convexity. Therefore the tracial support k(B) obeys

 k(B)>=U^2/Lambda >=2U-Lambda.                                    (B2)

If Lambda=0 then every label with nonzero POVM weight is zero, B_i=0,
and the assertion is interpreted as the trivial k(B)=U=0. The second
inequality for Lambda>0 is (U-Lambda)^2/Lambda>=0.
This proof needs no PGM theorem; it makes the charged resource explicit.

3. TWO SUFFICIENT CONDITIONS, NEITHER SUPPLIED BY GENERAL BROADCASTING

Use the target notation V=sum tau(A_i^2), s=sum tau(A_i Phi(A_i)),
delta=V-s and k=k(A). Assume Phi is legal as specified in the target.

Sufficient condition for C=2:
If a single labelled POVM has first moments A_i and
Lambda<=V+2delta=3V-2s, then (B2) gives

 k(A)>=2V-Lambda>=2s-V,
 V-k(A)<=2delta.

Sufficient condition for C=4:
If a single labelled POVM has first moments B_i=Phi(A_i) and
Lambda<=V, put s2=sum_i tau(Phi(A_i)^2). Then

 k(A)>=k(Phi(A))>=2s2-V,
 V-k(A)<=2(V-s2)<=4(V-s).                                        (B3)

To justify the first comparison, take any pure ensemble for I/d
optimizing k(Phi(A)). Apply the unital TP map Phi to its states and
refine the resulting mixed states. Their barycenter remains I/d and,
by HS self-adjointness, their A-score before refinement equals the
original Phi(A)-score. Refinement cannot decrease that score.

To justify the last inequality, Phi is a self-adjoint HS contraction:
the unital Schwarz inequality and trace preservation imply
||Phi(X)||_2<=||X||_2. Hence its real eigenvalues lie in [-1,1]. On each
eigencomponent, 1-lambda^2<=(2)(1-lambda), so V-s2<=2(V-s).

These are actual conditional theorems. The additional joint POVM and
its cost budget must be established independently; they are not endpoint
proofs and do not close the arbitrary-frame gate.

4. EXACT LEGAL-CHANNEL FAILURE OF THE TEMPTING PRIOR BUDGET

On C^4 choose five Clifford generators
 Gamma1=X tensor I, Gamma2=Y tensor I,
 Gamma3=Z tensor X, Gamma4=Z tensor Y, Gamma5=Z tensor Z,
where X,Y,Z are the usual Pauli matrices. They are Hermitian,
Gamma_i Gamma_j+Gamma_j Gamma_i=2 delta_ij I, and tau(Gamma_i^2)=1.
Every state rho has m_i=Tr(rho Gamma_i) satisfying ||m||<=1: for real u,
(sum_i u_i Gamma_i)^2=||u||^2 I, so u dot m<=||u||.

Use the explicit symmetric universal cloning broadcaster in dimension d:

 B(X)=[2/(d+1)] P_sym (X tensor I) P_sym.

It is CP, output symmetric and TP because
Tr[P_sym(X tensor I)]=(d+1)Tr(X)/2. Its marginal is

 Phi(X)=[(d+2)X+Tr(X)I]/[2(d+1)].

At d=4 this is Phi(X)=(3/5)X+(2/5)tau(X)I. It is unital, TP,
HS-self-adjoint and self-compatible, so it is fully legal.
For A_i=Gamma_i we have V=5, s=3, delta=2, B_i=(3/5)Gamma_i.

Any POVM with first moments B_i and labels x must satisfy

 3=sum_i tau(B_i Gamma_i)=sum_x p_x x dot m_x<=sqrt(Lambda),

and hence Lambda>=9>V=5. Thus the hypothesis used in the conditional
C=4 bridge is false even for an explicit legal broadcaster.
The lower bound is attained by

 M_{i,+}=(I+Gamma_i)/(10), M_{i,-}=(I-Gamma_i)/(10),
 x_{i,+}=+3 e_i, x_{i,-}=-3 e_i.

These effects sum to I, their first moments are (3/5)Gamma_i, and
their prior second moment is exactly 9.

For first moments A_i themselves, the same argument gives Lambda>=25.
The C=2 sufficient-condition budget here is V+2delta=9, so that route
also cannot be inferred from self-compatibility.

This is a failed-bridge witness, NOT a counterexample to the broadcast
gate. Clifford C=2 is a previously verified input to this task.
