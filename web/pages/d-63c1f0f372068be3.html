1. Exact endpoint versus the achieved theorem

The endpoint is: every finite-d unital TP CP HS-self-adjoint self-compatible
Phi admits ONE pure canonical EB Psi with Psi>=2Phi-I on the entire real
Hermitian HS space. The supplied verified universal C4 premise remains
the strongest global control in this worker's reasoning.

For H_0={A=A^*:tau A=0}, let K be the compact convex body of all canonical
pure-ensemble covariance operators with barycenter I/d. Cone separation
gives the exact equivalence
 [exists Psi in K with Psi>=2Phi-I]
 iff [for EVERY Q>=0 on H_0, k(Q)>=2s_Phi(Q)-V(Q)].
Factor Q=sum_i |A_i><A_i| with the tau inner product. Then
 V-k=min_{one pure ensemble bary I/d} sum_j p_j sum_i Var_{pi_j}(A_i).
This is one multiparameter convex roof, with the trace/barycenter constraint.
The single-frame inequality alone does not furnish a common Psi on a
higher-dimensional span. The rank-two theorem proves ALL positive support
directions on any fixed span of dimension <=2 before using separation.

An equivalent classical support dual uses a Hermitian potential H:
 k(Q)=min_H tau H subject to
  Tr(pi H)>=sum_i Tr(pi A_i)^2 for every pure pi.
One way to justify attainment is the concave ensemble roof at a general
state rho; it is finite on the compact state space and has a supporting
affine functional at the interior state I/d. Absorbing the affine constant
into H gives the displayed dual. Thus ordinary pure-state operator support
does not in general replace fixed-trace ensemble support.

3. Failed global transfers with exact reasons

Separate scalar variance/Fisher roofs do not aggregate. At the tracial
qubit state, the individual roofs for X and Z are zero via their separate
eigenbasis ensembles. Their common roof is one: V=2, while every pure
Bloch vector has x^2+z^2<=1, and one unbiased X eigenbasis attains k=1.
No normalization-specific quantum Fisher theorem was imported as a premise;
the explicit variance calculation already refutes this aggregation step.

Rank-two support inequalities cannot simply be added. Support functions
obey k(Q_1+Q_2)<=k(Q_1)+k(Q_2), in the wrong direction for this transfer.
The preceding qubit example gives k(Q_X)=k(Q_Z)=1 but k(Q_X+Q_Z)=1.
Measuring more noncommuting observables on the same receiver is also not a
legal extension of the exact joint-measurement identity. Additional tree
receivers have a composed marginal, rather than the supplied Phi, so they
cannot silently solve this C2 gate.

The C4 quadratic comparator does not imply the C2 linear comparator. For
example a legal qubit depolarizing marginal with eigenvalue 3/5 is the
9/10 mixture of the symmetric universal qubit cloner marginal (2/3) and
1/10 depolarizing marginal. A broadcaster is obtained by the same mixture
of B(X)=(2/3)P_sym(X tensor I)P_sym and the tracial-output channel.
On every traceless direction (2lambda^2-1)_+=0, while 2lambda-1=1/5.
The Z-dephasing canonical channel satisfies the quadratic domination and
C4 but fails the C2 comparison for X. Another comparator may and does
work for this channel; this is a counterexample to the proof transfer,
NOT to the existential C2 theorem.

Ordinary pure-state support cannot replace tracial ensemble support. For
A=diag(2/3,-1/3,-1/3) in M_3, max_pi Tr(pi A)^2=4/9, whereas
k(A)=tau A^2=2/9. The upper bound follows from scalar variance positivity
and the fixed barycenter; the eigenbasis ensemble attains it. Accordingly
a generic Grothendieck/operator-norm estimate would need a separately
proved fixed-trace comparison before it could be used here. None was
obtained. Likewise no monogamy claim for another convex-roof measure was
treated as a theorem about this specific common multiparameter variance.

Bare star spectra need not respect the legal marginal interface. The
rank-five MUB frame has V=5, exact k=2 and unconstrained star norm about
7.098585>7, but its all-singleton-tracial and HS-symmetric broadcaster
gate has an exact upper bound 6.75. Twirling a deleted-quadrature frame
without an appropriate preserving irreducible symmetry loses the score.
The rank-five certificate explicitly retains both physical marginals and
the shared marginal's HS symmetry.
