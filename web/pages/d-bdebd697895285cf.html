STRONGEST CONCRETE RESULTS

1. Internally proved candidate extension of N42/N64: physical mixed/displaced Gaussian kernels in a componentwise RELATIVE complex neighborhood of an explicitly positive kernel retain rare-herald photon-count stability. The tolerance is O(epsilon/[(h+1)(1+E)]), independently of herald probability. This permits magnitude errors as well as phases; the supplied S proof explicitly excluded general amplitude changes. The photon sampler consequence still imports N42's unimplemented OA113-based algorithm/reductions. The stability statement itself does not depend on OA113.

These are scoped theorem/counterexample candidates, not a field-breaking resolution. General phased sampling and unrestricted fixed-cardinality squared-hafnian sampling remain open in this work. External correctness and priority are unverified.

PRECISE MIXED/DISPLACED STABILITY INTERFACE

Let rho_0 be any finite-mode physical Gaussian density operator with explicit Bargmann kernel
 R_0(z,w)=c_0 exp(1/2 xi^T K xi+ell^T xi), xi=(z,w),
 K=[[A,C],[C,A]], ell=(g,g), with K,ell entrywise nonnegative real.
Let E=Tr rho_0 N. Specify any Fock pattern on any subset H, with total h photons and positive rho_0 probability. Let rho_1 be another physical Gaussian density operator, with kernel parameters K',ell'. Require
 |K'_{ij}-K_{ij}| <= eta K_{ij},  |ell'_i-ell_i| <= eta ell_i.
These inequalities REQUIRE exact preservation of zero support. Scalar normalizers c_0,c_1 are unrestricted and cancel upon conditioning. The mode basis/local gauge is supplied; this theorem does not acquire a positive representation from covariance data.

Put alpha=(h+1)/2, x=24 eta(1+2E), and
 d=exp(2 eta h)(1-x)^(-alpha)-1.
If x<1 and d<1, the rho_1 herald is feasible and the distributions of ALL remaining photon counts satisfy
 TV(P_1(.|H),P_0(.|H)) <= d/(1-d).
For 0<epsilon<=1, the sufficient condition
 eta <= epsilon/[192(h+1)(1+E)]
gives TV <= epsilon/4. No reciprocal herald probability occurs.

If the supplied N42 sampler is correct and OA113's finite-bit PM FPRAS is valid, run it on rho_0 with TV error epsilon/2. The resulting sample is within 3epsilon/4 of rho_1's conditional photon law. Worst-case bit cost remains poly(L,m,h,1+E,epsilon^-1), where L includes all input/gauge descriptions and exact relative-error certification. E and h are numerical parameters. The physical rho_1 promise and relative certificate are supplied/acquired inputs; tiny nonzero reference entries demand proportionally tiny absolute errors. No practical optical calibration procedure or FPRAS implementation was produced.

PROOF OF THE STABILITY STATEMENT

For a full photon pattern containing the fixed herald and N remaining photons, a diagonal density coefficient is a loop hafnian on 2(h+N) repeated ket/bra vertices, divided by positive factorials. Every term is a positive reference weight times a product of at most 2(h+N) kernel/linear entries. If each factor changes by a relative complex error <=eta,
 |product(1+delta_j)-1| <= (1+eta)^D-1 <= exp(eta D)-1.
Thus, with the common scalar normalizer omitted, the perturbed/reference diagonal coefficient ratio r_n satisfies
 |r_n-1| <= exp(2 eta(h+N))-1.
Physicality makes r_n real and nonnegative. Exact preserved support implies no perturbed mass on a zero reference coefficient.

Let q be the reference conditional photon law. Section below reconstructs the N64 majorant
 E_q exp(tN) <= (1-12(1+2E)t)^(-(h+1)/2).
Consequently E_q |r_n-1| <= d. Set M=E_q r_n. Then |M-1|<=d, so M>0 and the perturbed herald is feasible. The perturbed conditional law is q_n r_n/M; hence
 TV <= [E_q|r_n-1|+|M-1|]/(2M) <= d/(1-d).

For the simple epsilon contract, x<=epsilon/[4(h+1)] and
 log(1+d)=2eta h-alpha log(1-x)
             <=epsilon/96+epsilon/6=17epsilon/96.
Convexity gives d<=epsilon(exp(17/96)-1)<epsilon/5. Thus d/(1-d)<=epsilon/4.
All comparisons are made directly between UNNORMALIZED conditional coefficients; there is no additive estimate of the tiny herald probability.

PURE-STATE STRENGTHENING

For a pure reference wavefunction exp(1/2 z^T A z+g^T z), A,g>=0, ||A||<1, allow a physical perturbation B,f with |B-A|<=eta A and |f-g|<=eta g. Let S=(h+1)(1+E). If eta<=1/(96S), the entire remaining conditional pure states satisfy
 (1/2)||rho_1^H-rho_0^H||_1 <=20 eta S.
This controls every later channel/POVM, but does not supply an efficient sampler for arbitrary later measurements of the positive reference state.

Proof: normalize the reference conditional Fock vector z, and compare it to the unnormalized perturbed vector y_n=z_n r_n. The wavefunction loop-hafnian has at most h+N factors, so
 ||y-z||^2 <=eta^2 E[(h+N)^2 exp(2eta(h+N))].
The pure majorant has c=12(1+E), alpha=(h+1)/2, and E N^k<=c^k(alpha)_k. Termwise differentiation yields
 ||y-z|| <=eta exp(eta h)(1-2c eta)^(-alpha/2-1)
                  [h+c sqrt(alpha(alpha+1))].
Under the displayed eta contract, the exponential factor is <=exp(41/96), and the bracket is <=12S. Therefore the bound is <20eta S<1. Distance from unit z to the complex line spanned by y equals pure-state trace distance; it is at most ||y-z||. This also proves feasibility. This extends the existing S state-level phase theorem to relative magnitudes.

RECONSTRUCTION OF THE LOAD-BEARING N64 MOMENT MAJORANT

The full S proof is web/pages/d-a237a48c638c8fa7.html; its exact bytes are web/assets/77004627c5be6960a4169e92eb18310aabc53212499ce8f1ed640d672589d640.txt. The following derivation was checked, rather than assuming that finite source diagnostics prove it.

Let X exchange ket/bra coordinates and M=XK=[[C,A],[A,C]]. Physical normalizability and positivity imply rho(M)<1: the real coherent-amplitude trace direction gives I-A-C>0, and Perron-Frobenius identifies rho(M)=lambda_max(A+C). With D_t duplicating the photon variables in ket/bra, Gaussian integration gives
 log G(t)=log c + (1/2)sum_{j>=1}tr((D_tM)^j)/j
                  +(1/2)sum_{j>=0}ell^T(D_tM)^j D_t ell.
All closed/open-walk coefficients are nonnegative. Vacuum conditioning on H removes all walks touching H. The normalized remaining Gaussian factor sigma_0 therefore has energy E_0<=E. These are the standard Gaussian PGF/cluster identities, also behind established loop-soup simulation; no novelty is claimed for them.

Take a Gaussian purification of the original state. Fock differentiation to total herald degree h leaves P(z)G_0(z), deg P<=h. G_0 purifies sigma_0. A Gaussian unitary on the environment puts it in canonical purification form. Conjugating/normal-ordering affine ladder operators preserves polynomial degree. Undoing the Gaussian unitary U for that canonical factor leaves a normalized chi supported on total photons <=h. Thus the conditional output has a purification U chi.

The canonical environment energy is sum_j(nu_j-1/2), bounded by sigma_0's covariance energy. For V=S diag(nu_j,nu_j) S^T, each symplectic column pair obeys s_{2j-1}^T Omega s_{2j}=1, so its squared norms sum to >=2, proving tr V>=2sum_j nu_j. The total canonical pure factor energy e is therefore <=2E_0<=2E. In the original pure case no environment doubling is needed and e<=E.

Write U^dagger a U=u a+v a^dagger+beta, e_s=||v||_F^2, e_d=||beta||^2, e=e_s+e_d. Canonical relations give ||u||<=sqrt(1+e_s), ||v||<=sqrt(e_s). For N'=U^dagger N U, normal ordering and the pair/linear ladder estimates give
 ||N' P_{<=r}|| <=4r+3+(6r+5)e_s+3e_d <=6(r+1)(1+e).
The pair-annihilation bound is sqrt(r(r-1))||Q||_F and the creation bound sqrt((r+1)(r+2))||Q||_F; the latter follows by taking the adjoint of the full column of a_i a_j between fixed photon sectors. The quadratic operator raises support by at most two. Hence
 E N^k= <chi|(N')^k|chi>
       <=||(N')^k chi||
       <=[12(1+e)]^k ((h+1)/2)_k.
Every Gaussian times a finite polynomial lies in all photon-moment domains; finite-sector N' iteration is valid. Nonnegative exponential-series terms allow Tonelli. Summing the majorant proves the MGF used above. The only imported standard structural theorem is Gaussian purification/normal form, not a computational counting theorem.

EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE: GLOBAL PHASE COST DOES NOT CONTROL RARE CONDITIONAL OUTPUTS

For 0<r<1/2, define a four-mode real symmetric matrix A with edges
 A_12=A_13=A_24=A_34=r,
all other entries zero. Let B have the same magnitudes but B_24=B_42=-r. Give BOTH states Bargmann displacement g=r e_1. The reference A is positive. A has norm 2r; B^2=2r^2 I, so both are physical.

Herald exactly one photon on modes 1,2,3 (h=3), retain mode4. Extracting z1 z2 z3 from the wavefunctions gives EXACTLY
 P_A(z4)=2r^2 z4+r^3 z4^2,
 P_B(z4)=-r^3 z4^2.
No residual Gaussian factor remains. The conditional Fock states are
 |psi_A^H>=(sqrt(2)|1>+r|2>)/sqrt(2+r^2),
 |psi_B^H>=|2> up to phase.
Both heralds are feasible. Their conditional count-TV is 2/(2+r^2)->1; state trace distance is sqrt(2/(2+r^2))->1.

The original energies are
 E_A=(9r^2-34r^4+8r^6)/(1-4r^2)^2,
 E_B=(9r^2-14r^4)/(1-2r^2)^2,
both O(r^2). Since vacuum infidelity is <=mean photons, the INPUT-state trace distance is <=sqrt(E_A)+sqrt(E_B)->0.

For any real pure Bargmann matrix C and g, the squared unnormalized norm is
 Z(C,g)=det(I-C^2)^(-1/2) exp(g^T(I-C)^(-1)g).
Every B amplitude is dominated by its corresponding A amplitude, so the global phase-correction acceptance from the normalized absolute photon model is s=Z(B,g)/Z(A,g). Here
 s=((1-4r^2)/(1-2r^2)^2)
       exp[-4r^6/((1-2r^2)(1-4r^2))]
   =1-4r^4+O(r^6)->1.
Thus nu=-log s=4r^4+O(r^6)->0. Unconditional count-TV is <=1-s->0. Yet conditional absolute-model acceptance is
 s_H=r^2/(2+r^2)->0,
and conditional count-TV is 1-s_H->1. For example r=1/100 gives nu about 4.002e-8, while conditional count-TV is 20000/20001.

The normalized herald probabilities are p_A=(4r^4+2r^6)/Z(A,g) and p_B=2r^6/Z(B,g), both positive. This is not the earlier zero-herald counterexample and not a hardness claim: this four-mode instance is exactly easy. It disproves any promise-free transfer from a small GLOBAL average-phase/negativity cost, total energy, or unconditioned input-state distance to rare conditional stability. A herald-specific coherence/relative parameter is indispensable.

SIMPLER EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO ADDITIVE PARAMETER ROBUSTNESS

Three modes: A_12=delta, A_13=delta^2; B swaps the two weights; all other entries and displacement are zero. Both are positive with the SAME zero support. For delta small, both have energy 2(delta^2+delta^4)/(1-delta^2-delta^4), and matrix distance sqrt(2)(delta-delta^2)->0. Herald N1=1. Remaining conditional vectors are proportional to |10>+delta|01> and delta|10>+|01>; conditional count-TV AND state trace distance equal (1-delta^2)/(1+delta^2)->1. Input fidelity is [(1-delta^2-delta^4)/(1-2delta^3)]^2->1. Therefore even same support plus additive norm accuracy is insufficient; COMPONENTWISE relative control matters.

The supplied S work already proves much more than compact N45: parity-filtered positive selector gadget barriers; support feasibility; a bounded induced-PM-multiplicity subclass; K12 ordinary log-concavity/Hurwitz counterexamples; normalized submultiplicativity; first-envelope anti-concentration; and low-cycle-rank anti-concentration. I read the full reports d-2cfe987cf0427ed1, d-21ea4c16d598b257, d-5863ee93560c57aa. I did not redo their searches or upgrade their conjectured normalized coefficient log-concavity to a theorem.

A possible transfer from Chen–Liu's new log-concave Hadamard/subset-convolution theorem fails at the required interface. The vertex-set matching polynomial at fixed matching size need not be log-concave: on two disjoint edges at k=1 it is z1 z2+z3 z4; along (1+t,1+t,1-t,1-t), its log has strictly positive second derivative at t=0. Therefore simply squaring matching vertex weights does not satisfy their Theorem1.6 hypotheses. This does not disprove other lifted reductions.

Current closest primary prior: Anand/Chen/Cryan/Freifeld/Goldberg/Guo/Zhang, "Simulating Gaussian Boson Sampling on Graphs in Polynomial Time", https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/hguo/papers/BS-simulation.pdf. Theorem1.1 samples the VARIABLE-cardinality positive graph law on all graphs via a weighted prism. Their Lemma4.2 controls the prism's ordinary near-perfect/perfect matching ratio; the matching-size log-concavity is ordinary prism matching size, not number of vertical edges. It does not settle prescribed occupation cardinality or our rare-herald complex-error theorem. The full proof was read locally in gaussian_photonic_BS-simulation.txt.
