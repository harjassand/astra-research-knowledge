1. THE FAMILY AND EXACT CLAIM

Keep the INITIAL network
    A+C <-> A+B <-> B, C <-> 0,
but allow ANY supplied fixed strictly positive rational rates k1,...,k6 in the
INITIAL reaction order. Intensities are k1 ac,k2 ab,k3 ab,k4 b,k5 c,k6.
Every such system admits, by the formulas below, an ACQUIRED rational quadratic
W>=1 and a finite integer box K with
    L W(x)<=-epsilon for every x in Gamma outside K,
where Gamma={a+b>=1} and epsilon>0 is supplied explicitly. Certificate synthesis
uses a constant number of rational operations and integer ceilings; its bit
complexity and output bit length are polynomial in the rational input length.
No CAD, unbounded search, symbolic oracle or listing all points of K is required.
The negative result for globally concave potentials is rescued by a convex
quadratic rather than by changing the theorem's component quantifiers.

2. POTENTIAL AND EXACT GENERATOR IDENTITY

Set beta=k2, gamma=k1 and
    u=(beta+3 gamma)/(2 beta),
    W(a,b,c)=1+(a-1)^2+beta(b-u)^2+gamma(c-1)^2.           (1)
This is rational, >=1, and globally coercive. Define
    L_a=3k3+2k4+2k2(k1+k2),
    L_c=3k5+2k6,
    h(a)=-2k3 a^2+L_a a-k4.
Direct substitution gives the EXACT identity
    L W = b h(a)-2a(k2 b-k1 c)^2
                   +k1[-2k5 c^2+L_c c-k6].             (2)
Derivation: A birth/death changes (a-1)^2 by 2a-1 and -2a+3,
so it contributes b[-2k3a^2+(3k3+2k4)a-k4]. For C->B conversion
the B,C square difference is 2(k2 b-k1 c), because the constant
beta(1-2u)+gamma(1+2)=0. The reverse conversion difference is
-2(k2 b-k1 c)+2(k1+k2). Weighting by k1ac and k2ab yields
-2a(k2b-k1c)^2+2k2(k1+k2)ab. Finally C immigration/death
contributes k1[-2k5c^2+(3k5+2k6)c-k6]. Summing gives (2).

For unit rates, W=1+(a-1)^2+(b-2)^2+(c-1)^2 and
    L W=b(-2a^2+9a-1)-2a(b-c)^2-2c^2+5c-1.              (3)
On the two INITIAL obstruction rays,
    L W(a,0,1)=2-2a,
    L W(0,n,0)=-n-1,
and on the otherwise problematic ray (a,0,0), L W=-1.
Thus this correction actually handles the exact previously exposed states.

3. ACQUIRED THRESHOLDS AND FULL UNIVERSAL DRIFT PROOF

All quantities are rational except the integer ceilings:
    epsilon=min(1,k1,k1 k6),
    D=k1 L_c^2/(8k5),
    H=L_a^2/(8k3),
    delta=min(k2^2, 2k1k5/[1+2k1^2/k2^2]),
    A=max(1,ceil(L_a/k3),ceil((D+1)/k3)+1,
                    ceil((L_c+1)/(2k1))),
    T=ceil((H+k1 L_c+delta+1)/delta),
    B0=ceil((D+epsilon)/k4)+1,
    C0=max(1,ceil((L_c+1)/(2k5))),
    B=max(T,B0), C=max(T,C0).
Let K=Gamma intersect {a<A,b<B,c<C}. This box is finite.

Large A, with b>=1: a>=A implies L_a a<=k3a^2 and
k3a^2>=D+1. The latter follows from a>=r+1 with r=(D+1)/k3,
because (r+1)^2>=r. Hence h(a)<=-(D+1)-k4. Completing the
C quadratic bounds k1[-2k5c^2+L_cc-k6]<=D. Dropping the negative
conversion square in (2) gives L W<=-(D+1)b+D<=-1<=-epsilon.

Large A, with b=0: if c=0, L W=-k1k6<=-epsilon. If c>=1,
2a k1>=L_c+1 gives
    L W= -2a k1^2 c^2+k1[-2k5c^2+L_cc-k6]
         <=k1[-c-k6]<=-k1<=-epsilon.

For a>=1, set v=k2b-k1c. The elementary inequality
    b^2+c^2 <=2v^2/k2^2+(1+2k1^2/k2^2)c^2
and the chosen delta give
    2v^2+2k1k5c^2 >=delta(b^2+c^2).
Also h(a)<=H by completing its square. Thus (2) implies
    L W<=-delta(b^2+c^2)+H b+k1 L_c c-k1k6
        <=-(delta/2)(b^2+c^2)
             +[H^2+(k1L_c)^2]/(2delta)-k1k6.             (4)
If b>=T or c>=T, delta^2(b^2+c^2)>=[H+k1L_c+delta+1]^2.
The excess over H^2+(k1L_c)^2 is at least (delta+1)^2>=2delta.
Then (4)<=-1-k1k6<=-epsilon. This argument is valid for all a>=1,
not merely for the bounded A portion of the box.

For a=0, (2) is -k4b+k1[-2k5c^2+L_cc-k6]. If b>=B0,
the C part is <=D, giving L W<=-epsilon. If c>=C0,
2k5c>=L_c+1 gives the C part <=-k1(c+k6)<=-k1<=-epsilon.
These cases cover EVERY x in Gamma outside K. Nothing was inferred from
finite testing, numerical root finding, or a rate approximation.

4. A SMALLER UNIT-RATE BOX

For unit rates, a>=5 gives h(a)<=-6. With b>=1, (3)<=-6b+2<=-4,
because max_{integer c>=0}(-2c^2+5c-1)=2. With b=0, c=0 gives -1,
and c>=1 gives -(2a+2)c^2+5c-1<=-1. For a=0,
L W=-b-2c^2+5c-1, negative by at least one when b>=3 or c>=3.
For 1<=a<=4, h(a)<=9 and
2(b-c)^2+2c^2 >=(b^2+c^2)/2, so
    L W<=-(b^2+c^2)/2+9b+5c-1
         =52-[(b-9)^2+(c-5)^2]/2.
If b>=20 or c>=16 this is <=-17/2. Consequently the explicit smaller
box K={a<=4,b<=19,c<=15} intersect Gamma also gives L W<=-1 outside K.
Its number of points is <=1600; no minimum-box claim is made.

5. WHAT IS ACQUIRED, WHAT IS IMPORTED, AND COSTS

The formulas acquire W, epsilon and K from the six rational rates with a
constant number of additions, multiplications, divisions, comparisons and
ceilings. Squared coefficients, ratios, sums and fixed-degree products have
O(L) bit heights for total input bit length L; integer thresholds also have
O(L) bits. Standard rational arithmetic therefore gives polynomial BIT
synthesis cost. This is an actual implemented compiler for this family, not
a generic CAD algorithm or all-endotactic termination assertion.

The thresholds' NUMERICAL values may be exponential in L. Enumerating all
states in K or naive explicit boundary paths can consequently cost exponential
time/space in the binary input length. The global inequality verification
does not enumerate K. Generator evaluation at supplied binary counts uses
polynomially many bit operations because W and (2) are degree-two/three
polynomials with rational coefficients. A physical trajectory or simulator
still pays actual event counts; this report does not supply cheap long-time
simulation, thermodynamic work, or a generic energy-efficient implementation.

Standard stopped Dynkin with W>=1 and the proved drift gives
    E_x tau_K <= W(x)/epsilon,
using finite-ball localization then monotone/Fatou limits. On each localization
the integral of the negative drift until tau_K is bounded by W(x); count-ball
exit probabilities vanish by the regularity proof. Recurrence of Gamma also
follows from the standard Foster criterion plus the explicit irreducibility
paths, or independently from detailed balance as in INITIAL. This bound is
polynomial in numerical population, not its encoded logarithm; it is conservative.
It is recovery to a finite count box, NOT pathwise containment in a strict-
positive concentration core. The latter is disproved at each finite volume.

Every fixed-rate member is complex balanced: equilibrium means are
alpha=k4/k3, gamma0=k6/k5, beta0=k1 gamma0/k2. Product Poisson with these means,
conditioned on a+b>=1, satisfies pointwise detailed balance. That recurrence
mechanism is established prior art; this certificate does not solve a previously
open family recurrence theorem or earn a historical priority claim.

The claim requires FIXED rates. It does not yet cover arbitrary predictable
switching over a rate box. Cancellation in (2) uses beta/gamma=k2/k1, which
cannot be silently selected after observing a time-varying rate. A universal
fixed common quadratic or a properly charged rate-dependent correction must
be supplied for that interface.
