Singular kinetic matrices, gauge-constrained auxiliary fields, curvature couplings, higher derivatives and nonlocal/loop corrections are excluded. 4. Exact two-time tensor transparency lemma
===========================================
Fix any spatial Fourier mode. In a real sine/cosine basis, let q be the finitely many matter variables of tensor helicity, h the two metric tensor polarizations, and absorb the positive a^3 volume into coefficients. The most general quadratic matter Lagrangian in this sector is

 L_2=1/2 qdot^T K qdot+qdot^T A q-1/2 q^T C q
                       +h^T[E q+F qdot]-1/2 h^T M h,    (4.1)

where K>0, M=mu_T times the positive polarization/volume normalization, and A,C,E,F are smooth time-dependent finite matrices that may depend on the fixed spatial momentum. There is no hdot in the original first-derivative P action: B depends algebraically on g. E(p) has spatial polynomial degree at most one because linear stress contains field variations and at most first field derivatives. No stationarity or slow background change is assumed.

The bare mixed TT stress is the action-conjugate operator, up to the fixed positive polarization factor,

  Y=E q+F qdot-M h.                                     (4.2)

The common-transverse-gradient Ward calculation in v1 applies with all other multiplets held fixed and still gives mu_T=C_z(b_x,b_x)>=0. Therefore M>=0 even in this enlarged theory. Extra multiplets can induce memory but do not alter this bare-contact identity.

Let p_q=K qdot+A q+F^T h. Substitution into (4.2) gives

 Y=(E-F K^{-1}A)q+F K^{-1}p_q
                         -[M+F K^{-1}F^T]h.             (4.3)

Canonical retarded evolution of q,p_q from a compactly supported smooth h produces Volterra integrals, with no instantaneous feedthrough in q or p_q. Thus the displayed final matrix is the exact instantaneous/distributional contact coefficient of the retarded h->Y kernel. Equivalently one can consider a bounded step in h: q,p_q remain continuous across the step, and qdot jumps by -K^{-1}F^T delta h. This gives the same coefficient without invoking a frequency limit or exceeding the EFT cutoff.

If the complete retarded tensor response is zero, its contact coefficient is zero:

  M+F K^{-1}F^T=0.

Both terms are PSD. Hence M=0 and F=0 pointwise. With F identically zero, source enters the field EOM as E^T h. The second-order retarded field Green matrix has the universal equal-time expansion

  G_R(t'+s,t')=s K^{-1}(t')+O(s^2),  s->0+.

This follows by integrating K qddot+lower-order terms=delta(t-t')I: q is continuous with q(t'+)=0, qdot(t'+)=K^{-1}(t'). The regular stress kernel is consequently

  E(t'+s)G_R(t'+s,t')E^T(t')
                    =s E(t')K^{-1}(t')E^T(t')+O(s^2).   (4.4)

It can vanish identically only if E=0. This proves the finite positive-kinetic two-time transparency lemma:

  zero tensor susceptibility => mu_T=0, F=0, E(p)=0.      (4.5)

There is no possible cancellation between different masses, retarded poles, derivative couplings or field multiplicities. Positive K is the actual obstruction. The proof remains valid with arbitrary time-dependent coefficients and nonzero time-space mixing in the q equation. It uses exact local evolution, not frozen-background passivity or a stationary pole sign claim. The distinction between the bare contact and a statically relaxed shear modulus is essential: a relaxed zero alone does not imply (4.5).

5. Rotational completeness: the tensor test kills scalar spin-2 stress
====================================================================
On the background of section 3, the linear traceless spatial stress is an SO(3)-equivariant map into spin 2. A local first-derivative scalar action permits only three kinds of field vertex:

 (i) no derivative, or one time derivative, of an internal spin-2 field;
 (ii) one spatial derivative of internal spin j=1,2,3;
 (iii) the bare traceless metric contact.

This list is complete because tensoring a field spin j with a spatial derivative spin 1 contains spin 2 iff j=1,2,3. Other internal field spins cannot enter linear traceless stress at this derivative order. Time differentiation is a rotational scalar. Each allowed j-to-spin2 coupling has one reduced coefficient for each multiplicity, by the multiplicity-one decomposition of j tensor 1; arbitrary interspecies matrices are allowed within multiplicities.

For a wave along z, the derivative has magnetic label 0. Its coupling into a stress helicity m is proportional to the Clebsch-Gordan coefficient <j,m;1,0|2,m>. For the tensor helicities m=+/-2:

 j=2: <2,2;1,0|2,2>=sqrt(6)/3 !=0,

The signs depend on the standard phase convention and are immaterial; nonzero magnitude is what matters. For j=1 the field has no m=+/-2 component and therefore a one-derivative triplet vertex is invisible to TT stress. This is the exceptional channel that requires the Ward/gradient-positivity argument.

Equation (4.5), applied for both tensor polarizations and an open spatial momentum band, forces every constant/time spin-2 reduced vertex and every spatial spin-2/spin-3 reduced vertex to vanish. E(p) is a degree-one polynomial, so its constant and p coefficients vanish separately. 6. What is stronger, and what remains unresolved
================================================
The enlarged theorem removes the internal-spin-multiplet loophole within the declared P(phi,B), positive-K, positive-C class. Its two-time proof addresses causality on an expanding background directly. It does not require finite stationary poles or positive spectral weights for arbitrary cross observables. The stress is the operator conjugate to the tensor metric, which is why the K^{-1} quadratic coefficients have a definite sign.

The narrow v1 invariant for singlets/triplets needs only exact zero tensor MASS/CONTACT, because there are no tensor matter modes. The enlarged theorem needs the complete zero retarded tensor susceptibility over the momentum/time domain. A vanishing low-frequency/static relaxed shear, luminal wavefront, or on-shell dispersion is insufficient when extra tensor-helicity fields exist. Known initial conditions are needed only to define that tensor source response; the resulting scalar no-go holds for all scalar initial conditions.

The proof uses the mathematical equality of causal operators of a stipulated local EFT. Short-time kernel coefficients are derivative/contact invariants of that EFT, not a claim that arbitrarily fast physical impulses above its cutoff can be applied. A finite noise/instrument-band tensor experiment does not reconstruct these coefficients exactly without a model. No observed tensor or scalar response has been supplied, and no practical model-independent finite-error exclusion of the entire enlarged class is claimed.

Principal health K>0,C_n>=0 is indispensable. Singular constrained fields require a separate reduced-constraint proof; an auxiliary rewriting of KGB into first-derivative variables can have an indefinite or singular unreduced kinetic form, so it does not enter the admitted class by notation alone. Curvature/higher-derivative vertices can have hdot, hddot or second spatial derivatives, invalidate (4.1) or the j tensor1 selection, and can evade the invariant. Quantum/interacting response kernels are outside this exact classical quadratic statement. Neither nonlinear stability nor UV completion is established.
