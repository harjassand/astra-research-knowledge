# Scientific report — 7 October 2026, cycle 6

**Strongest supported contribution:** an internally audited extension approaching the critical potential-decay boundary. For every n>=3 and every prescribed positive L(r) tending to infinity, however slowly, there are real smooth V and nonzero u on R^n with -Delta u+Vu=0, |V|<=C r^(-1/2)L(r) eventually, every fixed-order derivative of V tending to zero, and u decaying faster than every fixed exponential. [Combined theorem and proof navigation](../notes/root_cycle6/CRITICAL_AMPLITUDE_SYNTHESIS.md), [full construction](../notes/r15_geometric_analysis/cycle6/ADMISSIBLE_SLOW_ENVELOPES.md), [independent full gate](../notes/r07_analysis/cycle6/ADMISSIBLE_FREQUENCY_ARBITRARY_LOSS_AUDIT.md). A concrete earlier instance gives

    |u(x)| <= C exp(-c r log(2+r)),
    |D^m V(x)| <= C_m (1+r)^(-1/2) log(2+r)^(m+3/2), every fixed m>=0.

One fixed pair satisfies all derivative orders. Thus the potential and every fixed-order derivative tend to zero, yet the solution decays faster than every fixed exponential. This supplies neither analytic bounds nor relative inverse-radius derivative control. That same example does not have fixed superlinear-power decay. [Complete derivation](../notes/r15_geometric_analysis/cycle6/LOG_FREQUENCY_REGULARITY.md), [independent reconstruction1](../notes/r07_analysis/cycle6/LOG_FREQUENCY_ALL_ORDER_AUDIT.md), [independent reconstruction2](../notes/r12_geometry/cycle6/LOG_ALL_ORDER_HOSTILE_AUDIT.md), [root audit](../notes/root_cycle6/LOG_REGULARITY_GATE.md).

**Why it could improve future research:** absolute smoothness and even decay of all derivatives are insufficient to restore exponential unique continuation in this interface. The proof gives a reusable weighted sparse-domain repair estimate and identifies surviving targets: relative spatial regularity, stronger amplitude decay and spectral structure.
