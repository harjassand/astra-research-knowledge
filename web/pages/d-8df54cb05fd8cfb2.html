1. T08 first-order tridisc jet (independent calculation).
Let V1=I2, V2=X=[[0,1],[1,0]], V3=Z=diag(1,-1).
For bounded analytic scalar f on the tridisc with ||f||∞<=1, write a=f(0), Bj=∂j f(0). For every ζ in T^3, the disc g(t)=f(tζ1,tζ2,tζ3) obeys |g'(0)|<=1-|a|². Choose phases to align Bj; hence sum |Bj|<=1-|a|². Therefore B=sum Bj Vj satisfies ||B||<=1-|a|². The upper-triangular block [[aI,B],[0,aI]] is contractive: with δ=1-|a|²>0 the Schur complement of I-T*T is δI-B*B/δ>=0; δ=0 follows by the derivative bound. The assignment is multiplicative because first derivatives obey Leibniz and upper triangular nilpotents multiply to zero.

For F(z)=z1I+z2X+z3Z, fix global phase z1=1, z2=exp(ia), z3=exp(ib). Direct Pauli algebra gives ||F||²=3+2 sqrt(cos²a+cos²b+sin²(a-b)). The 2-amplified derivative block is I⊗I+X⊗X+Z⊗Z; its norm is exactly3, by the triangle upper bound and maximally entangled vector (|00>+|11>)/sqrt2 as eigenvector3. Hence cb/scalar gap>=sqrt(3/2).

2. T05 aligned local stretching consistency.
A=[[-a/2,-γ,0],[γ,-a/2,0],[0,0,a]], a>0,γ≠0, has trace0, curl(Ax)=2γe3, symmetric part diag(-a/2,-a/2,a). Thus ω points constantly along e3 while (ω·∇)u=aω and Δω=0 on any ball where u=Ax. A smooth compactly supported divergence-free extension exists by choosing χ=1 on that ball and u=curl[-(χ/3)x×(Ax)]; curl[-x×(Ax)/3]=Ax by the homogeneous degree1 divergence-free identity. Smooth NS local existence then gives D_tω=aω initially in that ball. This refutes a local direction-gradient-only pointwise stretching bound. It does not refute nonlocal vorticity direction criteria or regularity itself.
