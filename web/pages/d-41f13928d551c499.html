PRODUCT-VECTOR PURITY AND A PAULI/SIDON ARCHITECTURE NO-GO
2026-10-08

Scope

This note pushes the purity-certificate gate for explicit von Neumann minimum-output-entropy (MOE) additivity violations. It proves an exact product-vector reformulation for any equal-weight unitary Stinespring branch family and an all-parameter obstruction for one concrete Pauli/Sidon strategy: its standard Fourier-max/purity certificate cannot certify a positive Bell-witness gap. The latter is not a no-go for Pauli channels, for tighter entropy methods, or for MOE nonadditivity itself.

1. Product-vector reformulation of the global purity gate

Let H=C^n, let W_1,...,W_K be unitaries, and define the isometry

  V psi = K^(-1/2) sum_s W_s psi tensor |s>.

Its two complementary channels are

  E(X)=K^(-1) sum_s W_s X W_s*,
  Phi(X)=K^(-1)[Tr(W_s X W_t*)]_{s,t}.

For every pure input rho=|psi><psi|, E(rho) and Phi(rho) have the same nonzero eigenvalues, as they are the two reduced states of V psi. Hence they have the same purity and entropy. Since minimum output entropy of a channel is attained on a pure input by concavity, a global lower bound for E on pure states certifies the global MOE of Phi.

Set S(X)=sum_s W_s X W_s*. With vec(X)=sum_ij X_ij |i> tensor |j>,

  vec(S(X)) = M vec(X),       M=sum_s W_s tensor conjugate(W_s),
  vec(|psi><psi|)=psi tensor conjugate(psi).

The complementary Gram formula and cyclicity of trace give, exactly,

  Tr(Phi(|psi><psi|)^2)
     = K^(-2) sum_{s,t} |<psi,W_s*W_t psi>|^2
     = K^(-2) Tr(S(|psi><psi|)^2)
     = K^(-2) ||M(psi tensor conjugate(psi))||_2^2.

Thus the needed operator estimate is only on the conjugate-product/Veronese vectors psi tensor conjugate(psi), not on the full doubled Hilbert space. If rho_0=rho-I/n, trace preservation and unitality of S give

  Tr(S(rho)^2)=K^2/n + ||S(rho_0)||_2^2.

For a target purity (K+C^2)/K^2, it is sufficient and necessary for this displayed decomposition that

  ||S(rho_0)||_2^2 <= K+C^2-K^2/n

for every pure rho. A full traceless 2-to-2 norm bound is a stronger sufficient condition, but it tests arbitrary traceless operators, rather than only rho-I/n. A product-vector/SOS certificate can in principle be weaker.

This is equivalent to the exact Gram-radius formulation in Zhen et al., arXiv:2609.18747v1, Lemma 4.1: if G_psi is the K-by-K Gram matrix of (W_s psi)_s, then Phi(|psi><psi|)=G_psi/K and max_psi Tr Phi(|psi><psi|)^2=1/K+Gamma_K(W)^2/K^2, where Gamma_K is the supremum of ||sum a_st W_s*W_t|| over traceless Hermitian coefficient matrices of Hilbert–Schmidt norm one. The source proves that identity and bounds Gamma_K uniformly in its free regular model. The present vectorized form makes clear that this is a conjugate-product norm, not an unrestricted doubled-space norm.

For the free direct-product branch model r=2,d=64,K=2rd=256, the regular doubled-space action, after removing the trivial identity mode present in finite representations, is the sum A_1 tensor I + I tensor A_2 of two 128-regular tree adjacency operators. Each tree adjacency has norm 2 sqrt(127), so the sum has norm 4 sqrt(127)>45. The source's p=1 purity target with C^2=103/2 requires a traceless-image norm on the scale sqrt(K+C^2)=sqrt(307.5)<17.54. Therefore a direct full-space 2-to-2 estimate is too weak to replace the product-vector argument in this model. In a finite representation the identity vector itself is fixed by M with eigenvalue K, which is an additional obstruction if it is not projected out. This norm comparison does not rule out a product-vector/SOS estimate or a finite construction using a different mechanism.

2. Exact Pauli purity bound

Let n=2^q, G=F_2^(2q), |G|=N=n^2, and let {P_g:g in G} be a Hermitian Pauli basis satisfying Tr(P_g P_h)=n 1_{g=h} and P_s P_g P_s*=(-1)^[s,g] P_g, where [.,.] is the symplectic form. For S subset G, |S|=K, define

  E_S(X)=K^(-1) sum_{s in S} P_s X P_s*,
  F(chi)=sum_{s in S} chi(s),
  beta=max_{chi nontrivial} |F(chi)|/K.

Write r_g=Tr(P_g rho). Pauli orthogonality gives, for pure rho,

  Tr(E_S(rho)^2)=n^(-1) sum_g |F(chi_g)/K|^2 |r_g|^2
                 <= 1/n +(1-1/n) beta^2.

The complementary channel has the same pure-input spectrum, so this gives a valid global one-copy entropy certificate for Phi_S. It is the standard max-Fourier-coefficient purity certificate considered below.

3. Sidon sets force the purity certificate to miss every positive gap

Assume S is Sidon in the elementary abelian 2-group: sums s+t of unordered pairs of distinct elements are all distinct. Then N>=N0:=1+binom(K,2). For the Bell input, the output of E_S tensor E_S is diagonal in the Pauli Bell basis. Its probability at label g is m_g/K^2, where m_g=# {(s,t) in S^2:s+t=g}. The Sidon condition gives m_0=K and exactly binom(K,2) nonzero labels of multiplicity 2. Hence

  H_B = (ln K)/K + ((K-1)/K) ln(K^2/2)
      = 2 ln K-ln 2-ln(K/2)/K.

The additive energy is

  E(S)=#{(a,b,c,d) in S^4:a+b=c+d}=K^2+4 binom(K,2)=3K^2-2K.

Character orthogonality yields

  sum_chi |F(chi)|^2 = NK,
  sum_chi |F(chi)|^4 = N(3K^2-2K).

Deleting the trivial character and taking a weighted average gives

  beta^2 >= R_K(N)/K^2,
  R_K(N)=[N(3K^2-2K)-K^4]/[K(N-K)]
       =[N(3K-2)-K^3]/(N-K).

For K>=3, R_K is increasing on [N0,infinity), since

  R_K'(N)=K(K-1)(K-2)/(N-K)^2 >=0,
  R_K(N0)=K-2,
  lim_(N->infinity) R_K(N)=3K-2.

Put r_K(N)=R_K(N)/K^2 and

  f_K(N)=N^(-1/2)+(1-N^(-1/2))r_K(N).

The purity certificate's argument P=1/n+(1-1/n) beta^2 is at least f_K(N). Therefore the certified Bell-witness lower bound obeys

  2 H_min(Phi_S)-H_B >= -2 ln P-H_B <= -2 ln f_K(N)-H_B.

I now show f_K(N)>exp(-H_B/2) for every real N>=N0 and every K>=3. The logarithmic derivative calculation is not needed: direct differentiation gives sign(f_K'(N))=sign(2K(sqrt(N)-1)-(N-K)). The latter changes from positive to negative exactly once on N>=N0: its roots in x=sqrt(N) are K +/- sqrt(K(K-1)), and the smaller root is below 1 while sqrt(N0)>=2. Thus f_K has no interior minimum and

  f_K(N) >= min{f_K(N0),(3K-2)/K^2}.

At the first endpoint, r_K(N0)=(K-2)/K^2 and 1/2>=N0^(-1/2)>=sqrt(2)/K. Thus

  f_K(N0) >= sqrt(2)/K +(K-2)/(2K^2)
           >= (sqrt(2)/K)(1+1/(6 sqrt(2))).

At the second endpoint, (3K-2)/K^2 >= 7/(3K). Meanwhile

  exp(-H_B/2)=(sqrt(2)/K) exp(ln(K/2)/(2K))
              < (sqrt(2)/K) exp(1/(4e))
              < 32 sqrt(2)/(29K) < 9/(4K) < 7/(3K).

For the first endpoint, e>8/3 implies exp(1/(4e))<32/29, and 32/29<1+1/(6sqrt(2)); the last inequality is equivalent to 18sqrt(2)<29, verified by 648<841. For the second, 32sqrt(2)/29<9/4 follows by squaring 128sqrt(2)<261, i.e. 32768<68121. All inequalities are strict. It follows that

  -2 ln(1/n +(1-1/n) beta^2)-H_B < 0.

For K=2, a nontrivial character orthogonal to the nonzero difference of the two branch labels exists because |G|=n^2>=4. It assigns the same sign to both labels, so |F|=2, beta=1, P=1, and H_B=ln 2; the certificate is again negative. K=1 is trivial.

Conclusion of this section: no Pauli/Sidon branch set, in any power-of-two input dimension that can contain it, can obtain a positive MOE gap from (i) the Bell output's exact Sidon spectrum and (ii) the max-Fourier purity upper bound above. This is an architecture-specific exact obstruction. It does not say the actual minimum output entropy is bounded by this purity certificate, and it does not exclude non-Sidon Pauli sets, stronger entropy inequalities, non-Pauli unitaries, or different two-copy witnesses.

4. Exact small fixture: Gold graph in GF(8)

Take G=GF(8) x GF(8), n=8, N=64, K=8, and

  S={(x,x^3):x in GF(8)},

using the irreducible polynomial z^3+z+1. For a nonzero difference u=x+y, the second component of the difference is u^3(1+t+t^2), with t=x/u. The map t->t^2+t has kernel {0,1}; therefore every nonzero difference labels exactly one unordered pair. Thus S is Sidon. Direct exact enumeration gives max nontrivial Walsh magnitude 4, exactly matching the energy lower bound R_8(64)=16. The Bell spectrum is 1/8 once and 1/32 twenty-eight times. The purity bound is 11/32, so its entropy certificate is -ln(11/32)=1.0678406300 nats. The Bell entropy is (19/4)ln2=3.2924491077 nats, leaving the purity-derived gap lower bound -1.1567678477 nats.

This small fixture is a diagnostic and failed candidate, not a channel additivity counterexample. No global minimum-output entropy was computed or claimed.

5. Costs, strongest baseline, and falsifiers

Cost: the general argument is symbolic and exact; its endpoint and derivative checks are O(1) arithmetic in K. The GF(8) replay enumerates 64 characters and 64^2 ordered pairs, with negligible time/memory. No channel matrices or optimization over input states were generated. The input space in the fixture is only n=8, but the purity method remains insufficient.

Baseline: the strongest same-task source found in the current primary scan is arXiv:2609.18747v1: a source-reported near-one-bit Bell witness simultaneously for all Renyi orders, output dimension O(epsilon^-3), with a finite K=320 high-probability Haar-orthogonal example and a non-random existence consequence from strong convergence; the non-random consequence supplies no closed-form matrices or algorithm. For von Neumann entropy specifically, arXiv:2610.06533v1 reports a closed-form real-algebraic example with output dimension 256, input dimension 2^37,608,898, and gap >1/1024 nats. These remain SOURCE-REPORTED here; I have not independently validated the complete theorems.

Falsifier for the Pauli/Sidon no-go: an algebraic error in the additive-energy moments, the derivative shape of f_K, an endpoint comparison, the Pauli purity formula, or the Sidon Bell multiplicities. The accompanying exact fixture checker covers the finite arithmetic, not the analytic proof. A positive-gap Pauli/Sidon channel certified by a stricter global purity bound would falsify the stated scope only if that bound also improved the max-Fourier bound; it would not contradict this result as worded.

Next gate for the positive route: produce a dimension-efficient finite operator inequality on the Veronese cone (or a compact SOS certificate) controlling ||M(psi tensor conjugate(psi))|| uniformly, while avoiding the coefficient-sphere net. The full doubled-space norm is demonstrably too strong in the free model. No decisive product-vector certificate has been found in this cycle.
