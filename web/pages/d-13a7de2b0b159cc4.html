# A characteristic repair score for genuine multiwave cells

7 October 2026, cycle11. **Our exact cell deduction; independent gate PENDING.** This is a characteristic integral of the full harmonic-repair defect. It is different from the Haar phase mean of a potential, and its use does not require replacing the actual repair by a potential-only model.

## Interface

Let m>=2, and let c lie in the compact normalized simplex

    c1 in [7/10,4/5], ci>=c_*>0 (i>=2), sum_i ci=1,
    0<c_*<=1/[5(m-1)].

The simplex is assumed nonempty; m is fixed. On T^m=(R/(2pi Z))^m put

    P_c(theta)=sum_i ci cos(theta_i), L0=Delta_theta+1,
    Omega_c={|P_c|<tau}, 0<tau<=1/100.

For each i solve

    L0 p_i=2partial_i P_c in Omega_c,
    p_i in H1_0(Omega_c), periodic in the remaining coordinates,
    R_i=zero extension of p_i.                         (1)

The extension has both its regular interior Laplacian and its boundary defect measure. In the actual plateau construction the leading curvature corrector is sum_i mu_i R_i, mu_i=t_i/R. No quotient of a raw nodal potential is used.

Let psi be the SAME fixed nonnegative normalized smooth radial kernel in R^n, n>=m+1, and 0<sigma<=tau/8. Its actual tilted fast smoothing is

    S_sigma T(theta)=integral psi(u)e^(sigma u1)
                                  T(theta-sigma u_active)du. (2)

It acts on periodic distributions, retaining every boundary measure. Radial harmonic averaging preserves P_c and each partial_i P_c because e^(-as)P_c(at) and its phase derivatives are harmonic.

The all-negative to all-positive characteristic is

    theta_i(v)=2 arctan exp(-ci v), v in R,
    theta_i'=partial_i P_c(theta)=-ci sin(theta_i),
    theta_i(-infinity)=pi, theta_i(+infinity)=0.        (3)

Define the full characteristic repair score

    K_i(c;tau,sigma)=integral_R [S_sigma L0 R_i](theta(v))dv. (4)

Unlike a phase mean, (4) follows a literal gradient characteristic. The cell result is

    |K_i|<=50tau, and K_i is C-infinity in c
    with every fixed derivative finite for fixed tau,sigma. (5)

The bound in (5) is uniform in the simplex, in psi subject to (2), and in sigma<=tau/8. Derivative bounds are only asserted after tau,sigma are fixed; no smallness of K_i's derivative is inferred from its small value.

## 1. Gap, smooth boundary, and exact defect bound

On |P_c|<=tau+sigma, dominance gives

    |partial_1 P_c|^2
       =c1^2-[P_c-sum_(j>=2)cj cos(theta_j)]^2
       >=(7/10)^2-(3/10+tau+sigma)^2> (3/5)^2.       (6)

Every fixed-other-phase slice of Omega_c consists of two theta1 intervals of length <=10tau/3. Slice-wise Dirichlet Poincare therefore supplies

    lambda_1(-Delta_theta,Omega_c)>=(3pi/(10tau))^2>16. (7)

The boundary graphs are the two appropriate arccos branches of

    cos(theta1)=[+-tau-sum_(j>=2)cj cos(theta_j)]/c1.

They are uniformly smooth, with no critical level. Periodic cell elliptic regularity makes p_i smooth up to these graphs. Let

    H=3(tau^2-P_c^2)>=0 in Omega_c.

Since Delta P_c=-P_c,

    -L0 H=3[2|grad P_c|^2-tau^2-P_c^2]>2,
    H=0 on boundary(Omega_c).                        (8)

The Dirichlet maximum principle for -L0, whose gap is (7), applied to H+-p_i gives

    |p_i|<=3(tau^2-P_c^2),
    |partial_n p_i|<=6tau |grad P_c| on the boundary. (9)

The normal estimate follows directly by taking the inward difference quotient of H+-p_i at their common zero boundary trace. It does not presume a sign for p_i. Zero extension gives the EXACT measure identity

    L0 R_i=2partial_i P_c 1_(Omega_c)+nu_i,
    nu_i=-partial_n p_i dS,
    |nu_i|<=6tau |grad P_c|dS
           =6tau |grad P_c|^2 sum_+- delta(P_c+-tau)dtheta. (10)

Both boundary pieces are present. Deleting them loses the cancellation needed by transport. (9)-(10) also give finite total variation uniformly over the fixed simplex.

## 2. Shifted-characteristic coarea, before any averaging

Fix u in the support of psi and put z(v)=theta(v)-sigma u_active. Wherever |P_c(z(v))|<=tau, the unshifted point has |P_c(theta(v))|<=tau+sigma. The gradient in (6) is bounded below and ||Hess P_c||<=4/5. Therefore

    d/dv P_c(z(v))
       =grad P_c(z(v)) dot grad P_c(theta(v))
       >=(3/5)^2-(4/5)sigma>1/3.                    (11)

The shifted path runs from a negative crest neighborhood to a positive one. It crosses each shifted boundary once, and spends at most 6tau units of v inside the collar. Multiple crossings would require a downward crossing somewhere within the band, which (11) forbids. The regular part in (10) consequently has path integral bounded by 12tau.

For either boundary the one-dimensional coarea identity gives

    integral_R delta(P_c(z(v))+-tau)|grad P_c(z(v))|^2dv
           <=3,

using |grad P_c|<=1. By (10) the TWO boundary contributions together are bounded by 36tau. Multiplying by the nonnegative tilted kernel and integrating in u gives

    integral_R |S_sigma L0 R_i(theta(v))|dv
        <=48tau integral psi e^(sigma u1)du
        <=48e^(1/800)tau<50tau.                     (12)

The distribution calculation may be made literal by replacing each delta in (10) by a positive approximation. (11) gives a uniform coarea bound, so Fubini and passage to the limit are justified. We have integrated the full boundary current; this is not a pointwise evaluation of a measure on a curve.

Only (12)'s absolute bound is needed for the actual obstruction. It allows the repair score to have either sign and does not extrapolate the one-wave positive formula to a higher-dimensional collar.

## 3. Parameter smoothness with fixed tau and sigma

The explicit graphs pull Omega_c back to a fixed periodic rectangle by interpolation between its two theta1 boundary graphs. The pulled-back operator is uniformly elliptic, has smooth c coefficients, and retains the fixed positive Dirichlet gap. Differentiating its weak equation, then applying ordinary boundary estimates, gives all finite parameter/coordinate derivatives of p_i for the ONCE-FIXED tau. Normal traces and the surface Jacobians have the same fixed finite bounds.

Convolving those volume and surface distributions with the fixed smooth fast kernel gives smooth parameter dependence, including the shifted surface position. The characteristic (3) also depends smoothly on c. The residual in (4) has support where |P_c(theta)|<=tau+sigma, which lies in one fixed bounded v interval, uniformly in c, by monotonicity and (6). Thus differentiating (4) gives finite bounds on D_c K_i of every fixed order. The constants may depend on tau,sigma and m. Claiming D_c K_i=O(tau) would require another estimate; we do NOT make that claim.

## 4. Exact return coefficient, not a potential mean

Take the phase gates theta1=rho and pi-rho. On the characteristic (3), write

    rho_i(c)=2 arctan[tan(rho/2)^(ci/c1)], rho1=rho.

Choose rho sufficiently small that every rho_i<=1/10. Both gates then lie outside every shifted collar, and the entire score (4) lies between them. The leading radial fold geometry is

    ar=a r_H+sum_i log sin(theta_i),                 (13)

because d(sum log sin theta_i)/dv=-P_c. For the leading curvature residual -2sum_i mu_i partial_iP_c and its FULL repair sum_i mu_i L0R_i, the oriented neck cocycle is

    C_neck^(1)=-sum_i B_(i,rho)(c)mu_i,
    B_(i,rho)=2(pi-2rho_i)+K_i.                     (14)

Here the unperturbed phase orientation is from all negative phases to all positive phases. The raw term is the exact displacement integral 2sum_i mu_i integral partial_iP_c dv=2sum_i mu_i Delta theta_i. The repair term is (4). This gives (14) without Haar averaging, a quasiperiodic inverse, or a quotient near P=0.

The full-extrema tail coefficient of the ACTUAL raw-smoothed envelope is acquired separately in the companion note. Its difference is -4pi sum_i t0_i/r^2; hence the return obstruction is not supplied by (14) alone. In particular the zeroth harmonic field has zero total cocycle despite its folded endpoint contraction.

## 5. Normalized radial-shape source has zero characteristic score

**Exact cell deduction, PENDING independent reconstruction.** Let d be any real coefficient direction with sum_i d_i=0, and set

    Q_d(theta)=sum_i d_i cos(theta_i),
    L0 r_d=Q_d in Omega_c, r_d=0 on boundary,
    R_d=zero extension of r_d, A_d=L0R_d-Q_d.

The same gap gives a unique Dirichlet solution. Define the all-phase reflection Ttheta=pi·1-theta. It preserves L0 and Omega_c, and P_c(Ttheta)=-P_c(theta), Q_d(Ttheta)=-Q_d(theta). Uniqueness consequently gives r_d(Ttheta)=-r_d(theta). Its zero extension and its FULL Laplacian defect obey the same odd reflection identity; this includes every normal boundary current. Thus A_d is odd as a distribution.

The fast marginal of psi(u)e^(sigma u1) is even under u_active->-u_active: that substitution leaves u1 and the radial psi unchanged. Therefore S_sigma commutes with T and S_sigma A_d is odd. The characteristic satisfies theta(-v)=Ttheta(v). It follows that

    [S_sigma A_d](theta(-v))=-[S_sigma A_d](theta(v)). (15)

This is an absolutely convergent integral, not a principal-value cancellation. The smoothed L0R_d part has support in a uniformly finite v interval. Outside it S_sigma A_d=-S_sigma Q_d=-Q_d, by radial harmonic averaging. On the characteristic,

    Q_d(theta(v))=sum_i d_i tanh(ci v),

which decays exponentially at both endpoints because sum_i d_i=0 and every ci>=c_*. Hence

    integral_R [S_sigma A_d](theta(v))dv=0,           (16)

and the integral is also zero on any symmetric pair of finite v gates. No zero-extension measure has been discarded.

For a frozen radial mixture derivative, the first shape residual is a multiple of A_d, so its characteristic transport score is zero. This does NOT prove that a macro mixture has no actual shape contribution: parameter motion perturbs the characteristic and moving collar, and the second-order interactions can enter the fine return derivative scale. Acquiring those ACTUAL derivative terms is a remaining gate, not an inference from (16).

The spectrum worker's divided-potential response uses a Haar mean of (S_sigma A_d)/P_c. Both numerator and denominator are odd under T, so that quotient is even and need not integrate to zero. Its independently gated nonzero fixed-smoothed curl is therefore consistent with (16). A spatial potential mean and an undivided characteristic source are different diagnostics; neither supplies the other's global compatibility condition.
