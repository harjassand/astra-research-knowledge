# R11: singleton encoding toward full Rzk conservativity

Status: a small dRzk construction is independently derived and checked; full conservativity remains unresolved. Priority: moderate, precise foundational lead with direct use for proof portability and synthetic category theory. No claim of a new theorem unknown to prior literature.

## Exact question and interface

Rzk paper arXiv:2607.12207v1, Theorem 5.8 / Conjecture 5.9: given RSTT context/type J and arbitrary declarative Rzk derivation D of M:tau(T) in tau(J), produce an RSTT inhabitant of T, keeping meta-theoretic parameters schematic. Published proof requires ext-style hypotheses and tail concluded types (Definition 5.7). Remove that requirement without silently strengthening RSTT, weakening judgmental equalities to propositional ones, or changing tau-image endpoints.

Author claim: conservativity for the restricted fragment; unrestricted result explicitly conjectured. Our deduction: their erased free-standing restriction loses the premise needed at a variable. New local construction below stores the premise in an ext-style singleton section instead of erasing it.

## Independently reconstructed small theorem

In dRzk, fix cube context Xi, tope context Phi, A:type, a:A under extra tope phi. Let R=A[phi↦a], and let E=Pi_{s:1} A[phi↦a], where s is fresh and phi,a,A do not depend on s. Define pack:R→E by pack(x)=lambda s.x and unpack:E→R by unpack(f)=f(star).

Formation: restriction boundary a has the required type; the unit shape is top, and phi entails top. Under phi, x≡a by Restr-comp, so lambda s.x satisfies E's boundary. Application f(star) has R by shape-Pi elimination.

First inverse: unpack(pack(x))=(lambda s.x)(star)≡x by beta. Second inverse: pack(unpack(f))=lambda s.f(star)≡lambda s.f(s)≡f, by unit-cube uniqueness (s≡star) and shape-Pi eta. Thus both inverses are judgmental and their identity proofs are refl. If A is ext-style, E is ext-style.

The strict isomorphism extends through a dependent function domain: for B:R→U, H=Pi_{x:R}B(x), H'=Pi_{f:E}B(unpack(f)), define F(h)(f)=h(unpack(f)), G(k)(x)=k(pack(x)). Beta/eta and the two strict inverse laws give G(F(h))≡h and F(G(k))≡k. Four additional declarations verify this. This is genuine dependent reuse, not a non-dependent cardinality argument.

Code: `work/r11_logic/singleton_restriction.rzk`, `work/r11_logic/dependent_singleton.rzk`. Five base and four dependent declarations pass released Rzk 0.11.3. The mathematical derivation above uses only the common restriction/Pi/unit-cube rules. Checker acceptance validates these encoded declarations, not full metatheoretic conservativity.

## Decisive unresolved obligations and adversarial checks

1. **Target-system shape scope.** RS17 §2.1 definition (2.1), Fig.4 p11 present shapes with topes in the singleton cube context, separate from ambient Xi. E above has a boundary depending on already ambient t in the executable example. It is legal in dRzk, but a literal RSTT back-translation requires proof that contextual shapes/boundaries are admissible, or a normalization eliminating the ambient dependency. Generalizing RSTT to accept them would change the claimed theorem. Abstracting over the whole ambient cube is not an immediate fix: RSTT extension formation requires the typed variable context Gamma to be independent of the new shape binder; existing Gamma may depend on ambient t.

2. **Identity conversion and coherence.** Strict isomorphisms for values/Pi domains do not automatically give strict inverse maps for identity proofs. The standard path action ap has ap(id,p)=p propositionally by J, but fails judgmentally for arbitrary stuck p. `identity_obstruction.rzk` proves the propositional law; `negative_identity_strictness.rzk` deliberately attempts refl and is rejected. A full translation must preserve dependent substitution, identity elimination, and type-directed definitional equality; replacing these with an equivalence does not suffice. Need a logical relation or a conversion-preserving derivation translation, with exact boundary equations.

3. **Version provenance.** Paper reports checker v0.7.8; GitHub release tag v0.7.8 returned 404. Retrieved current v0.11.3 release, published 2026-08-31 according to GitHub API. It adds features beyond the paper. This experiment does not claim reproduction on v0.7.8. Source and executable archive preserved, executable SHA256 and replay logs recorded.

Negative controls: an arbitrary x:A cannot be packed with promised phi↦a (checker rejects); replacing singleton with interval makes eta-to-constant invalid (checker rejects); replacing J proof of ap(id,p)=p with refl invalid (checker rejects). All five replay cases match expectation. These independently test the conditions used in the small theorem. They are not independent implementation validation of Rzk's kernel.

## Nearest prior art and novelty

Riehl-Shulman 2017 extension types already have boundary computation, beta/eta, and unit-cube uniqueness; the singleton construction follows directly from them wherever contextual formation is admitted. Rzk 2026 splits extension types and proves positive/tail conservativity by erasure. The local encoding may be elementary folklore; bounded primary search found no specific singleton repair of Conjecture 5.9, which does not establish novelty. Do not present this as a discovery of full conservativity.
