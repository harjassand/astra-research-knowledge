## Family 332: a constructive ambient nonlinear smoother

Input: finite stochastic `A`, stationary `π`, integer `t≥1`, real ambient
`L1(μ)` data `x_i`. Define `W` as the stationary average of squared original
distances over walk lengths `1,…,t`, and

`J_t(y)=Σ_iπ_i‖x_i−y_i‖₁²+tΣ_ijπ_i a_ij‖y_i−y_j‖₁²`.

Author claim: `J_t(y)≤3024W` for real ℓ1, using expected coordinatewise
medians of three independent endpoints from
`G=[I+t(I−A)]⁻¹`. The source combines a finite binary cut lift, cubic
boundary flattening, a quartic martingale potential, and geometric killing.

Our reconstructed improvements and consequences, with full proofs in

also derived a more useful acquisition interface, which we independently
rechecked: `E|med₃(X)|²≤3(E|X|)²`, needing only the first absolute moment.
For `L` independent triples per row this yields

`EJ_t(ŷ)≤[18+sqrt(54(1+4t)/L)]²W`.

The confidence version replaces `L` by `Lδ` inside the square root; simplex
repair multiplies objective bounds by four. A row query needs `3L`
geometric endpoint walks, expected `3Lt` transitions, data-vector reads,
and actual coordinate/output work. Supplied sparse probability vectors make
the query independent of the ambient dimension in sample count; arithmetic
and output size remain charged. A fixed random tape gives a consistent
implicit map. This estimates the sanctioned median functional, not the
original endpoint mean or arbitrary unknown distributions in ℓ1.
