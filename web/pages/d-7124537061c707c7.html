## Strongest claim established at this checkpoint

The literature supports three distinct, non-interchangeable statements. (1) Every fixed-constant stochastic mass-action system whose reactant molecularities are at most two and whose embedded network is ordinary endotactic is non-explosive; this is a regularity result, not a recurrence theorem. (2) In one stoichiometric dimension, fixed-constant mass-action endotactic systems have a stronger classwise theorem under explicit structural assumptions: on a stoichiometric class containing a positive irreducible communicating class, that class is exponentially ergodic; the theorem also separately covers open/quasi-irreducible classes. It is not a theorem for arbitrary multidimensional classes. (3) Weak reversibility plus deficiency zero yields a product-Poisson stationary probability for each closed irreducible stochastic component, and complex balance supplies non-explosion; together these imply positive recurrence componentwise, at any molecularity, but for this structural subclass and fixed stochastic mass-action kinetics. None gives recurrence for arbitrary second-order endotactic networks in dimension at least two, time-varying/predictably controlled rates, or a certificate-based boundary-recovery theorem.

The key scope error to prevent is upgrading “regular” to “recurrent.” In Xu's 2024 paper, regular means CTMC non-explosivity (alongside global ODE existence); recurrence and positive recurrence are defined separately. The proof of the second-order theorem is a linear drift/Lyapunov argument that rules out explosions through the total molecule count, not an inward drift or boundary return estimate.

## Exact theorem hypotheses and deductions

**Second-order non-explosivity.** Xu, arXiv:2409.05340v2, Theorem 4.6, proves every second-order 1-endotactic stochastic mass-action system is regular. Its corollary includes ordinary endotactic networks because they are 1-endotactic. “Second-order” means every *reactant* has total stoichiometric count at most two. The theorem uses standard stochastic mass-action propensities with fixed positive constants, `lambda_(y->y')(x)=kappa x^underline(y)` on integer states. For 1-endotactic networks, a bimolecular reaction cannot increase total molecule count. Reactions that can increase it have at most first-order propensity, so `V(x)=1+sum_i x_i` has generator drift bounded above by `C V(x)`; the CTMC Lyapunov criterion gives non-explosivity. The theorem does not assert positive recurrence, tightness, boundary avoidance, a stationary probability, or a return time bound. The paper explicitly leaves the minimal order for explosive endotactic systems open and supplies an order-10 strongly endotactic explosive example.

**One-dimensional recurrence result.** Wiuf–Xu, arXiv:2012.07954v2, Theorem 4.11, assumes standard fixed-constant stochastic mass-action kinetics and ordinary endotacticity, plus: H2, `dim S=1` and the first species is not a catalyst; H3, both positive and negative reaction subnetworks relative to `omega*=gcd(Omega)` are nonempty; H4, `omega*` lies in `N_0^d`, excluding conservative networks so the relevant classes can be infinite. H1 (propensity positive exactly when the reactant is available) holds automatically for standard stochastic mass action. Then the system is non-explosive on each `L_c`. If `P_c` is nonempty, the closed positive irreducible components in that class are exponentially ergodic. If `R>1` and `Q_c` is nonempty, the open quasi-irreducible components instead have uniform exponential quasi-ergodicity; this is not positive recurrence of those open classes. Corollary 4.12 further gives positive recurrence on every positive irreducible class for weakly reversible systems under H2 and H4; H3 is automatic there. The definitions are componentwise and distinguish `P_c` from `Q_c`; a claim “the whole class is ergodic” must not collapse their different communicating components.

**Deficiency zero.** Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz, arXiv:0803.3042, Theorems 4.1–4.2, state that deterministic complex balance gives a stationary product-Poisson solution of the stochastic master equation; for a reducible lattice, the probability on a closed irreducible component `Gamma` is the normalized restriction of `prod_i c_i^{x_i}/x_i!`. The deficiency-zero theorem supplies complex balance for every positive rate-constant choice when the finite network is weakly reversible and has `delta=|C|-ell-dim(S)=0`. Anderson–Cappelletti–Koyama–Kurtz, arXiv:1708.09356, Theorem 3, separately proves complex-balanced stochastic mass-action systems are non-explosive from every initial distribution. Therefore each such closed irreducible component has a genuine invariant probability and is positive recurrent. The class restriction and non-explosivity step matter: a formal stationary solution of the forward equation alone need not entail a non-explosive CTMC or positive recurrence. This is not a result for arbitrary endotactic networks; deficiency zero plus weak reversibility is a specific subclass.

**Stochastic failure of naive deterministic transfer.** Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen, arXiv:1808.05328v2, Example 3.2, gives a strongly endotactic network `0 -> 2A -> 4A+B -> 6A+4B -> 3A`. Its maximum reactant molecularity is 10. From any state, the source constructs a positive-probability route to the B-axis-free state and then repeats a cycle that increases A by one. At the cycle stages, the chosen propensity orders are 2, 5, and 10, while the active competitors are lower by at least two degrees. The route failure probabilities have summable order `O(n^-2)`, hence the infinite path has positive probability; its expected selected holding-time sum is bounded by `sum_n O(n^-2)`, so the CTMC explodes. This disproves “(strong) endotactic implies stochastic recurrence/non-explosion” with no order restriction, but is not a bimolecular counterexample. Xu 2024 independently notes the minimum explosive order remains open.

**Definitions that must stay separate.** Endotactic means u-endotactic for every direction u; u-endotactic for just `u=1` is a weaker one-direction property. “Strongly endotactic” is a subclass. CTMC non-explosion (regularity), recurrence, positive recurrence, existence of a formal stationary solution, and an actual invariant probability on a closed irreducible component are separate conclusions. Deterministic permanence supplies no stochastic implication without an additional stochastic argument.
