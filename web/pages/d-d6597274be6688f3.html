# Logical open loops and imaginary-time bath responses

## Question and exact interface

Can the accepted positive-X Gibbs path method say something about the logical
sectors themselves, rather than only a decoder's syndrome event? Yes: a
nontrivial logical insertion forces a large connected field component, giving
the bounds below. The decisive remaining issue is exact Bohr-line pairing.

Let a finite CSS stabilizer code on N qubits have unsigned pure-X checks
C_a and unsigned pure-Z checks C_b. Let S_X be the binary span of its original
X checks. Fix an unsigned pure-X logical L=X_l, with l in ker H_Z but not S_X.
Define d_X=min{|v|: v in ker H_Z, v not in S_X}. No single logical qubit or
independence of the supplied check list is required. Use

    H = sum_a h_a (I-C_a)/2 + sum_b h_b (I-C_b)/2
        -sum_B epsilon_B X_B,    epsilon_B>=0, |B|<=w,
    J = max_q sum_(B:q in B) epsilon_B.                     (1)

The positive check penalties can be nonuniform. Define G_Z on the qubits,
joining two sites if some original Z check contains both, and suppose its
maximum degree is D. For the actual admitted BDL family, D<=42 and
d_X>=d, with the previously audited conservative d_2k>2^(190k-17).
The source distance/admission remains
an explicit inherited premise; the proof below is independent of decoder
history contraction and its astronomical finite lookup.

Set, when beta*w*J<1,

    delta = min{1, N (D+1)^(2d_X)
                          (e beta w J)^(d_X/w)}.          (2)

For one-site fields with maximum epsilon, this is

    delta <= min{1, N [(D+1)^2 e beta epsilon]^d_X}.         (3)

All equations are at finite N and finite beta. No ground-only dressing,
sampling of paths, or supply of a spectral pairing is assumed.

## 1. Positive open paths and the necessary logical component

Expand H=A-sum_l c_l T_l in the computational Z basis, as in the cycle15
Gibbs witness proof. A is the diagonal Z-check energy plus the scalar
X-check contribution. Each T_l is an unsigned pure-X permutation, with
coefficient h_a/2 for an X check and epsilon_B for a field tag. Ordered
times retain their exact beta^n/n! simplex volumes. The insertion

    Z_L = Tr[L exp(-beta H)]                               (4)

is a nonnegative sum over paths whose final configuration is L times their
initial configuration. All jump tags and coefficients remain positive.
Since a nonidentity pure-X permutation changes every computational basis
configuration, the net binary jump word on every contributing path is l.
Writing v for the parity sum of FIELD supports and s for the parity sum of
X-check supports gives v+s=l. Consequently

    v in ker H_Z, v not in S_X.                            (5)

Decompose the nonzero support of v into G_Z-connected components. No Z
check intersects two components: two such sites would be adjacent in G_Z.
Since H_Z v=0, each component separately has zero Z syndrome. If every
component were in S_X, then v would be in S_X, contrary to (5). Thus one
component is itself a nontrivial pure-X logical and contains at least d_X
sites. It contains a connected subset S of exactly d_X sites, by pruning
a spanning tree. Every site of S was touched by a field event at least once.

This does not infer logical support from ambient proximity. G_Z is the
actual share-check graph; crossings or folded physical sheets add no edges.
It also does not charge the support of an arbitrary chosen representative l.

## 2. A local open-path tilt, with no extensive norm toll

For a fixed connected d_X-site S, let N_S count FIELD tags B meeting S.
The event in section1 has N_S>=d_X/w. Tilt only these coefficients by e^theta.
Writing H_theta for the tilted Hamiltonian gives the exact moment numerator

    sum_open paths weight(path) exp(theta N_S)
      = Tr[L exp(-beta H_theta)].                         (6)

The right side is nonnegative by the path representation. As an operator
inequality for the trace, |Tr(L T)|<=Tr(T) whenever T>=0 and ||L||=1.
Thus (6) is at most Z(H_theta), including when the loop endpoints are open.
The coefficient intensity yields

    ||H_theta-H|| <= (e^theta-1) J |S|.

Ordered-eigenvalue minmax gives

    Z(H_theta)/Z(H) <= exp[beta J d_X (e^theta-1)].          (7)

The bound is on the actual interacting, normalized open-path mass. No
independent Poisson approximation or classical syndrome-diagonal Gibbs
state is used. Markov's inequality at the REAL threshold d_X/w and
e^theta=1/(beta w J) gives

    mass_open[every site of S touched]/Z(H)
      <= (e beta w J)^(d_X/w).                            (8)

If J=0 this mass is zero. A canonical depth-first traversal of a spanning
tree of a connected d_X-site set has 2(d_X-1) steps. The walk determines its
visited set, so the number of such sets is at most N(D+1)^(2d_X), a deliberately
loose bound valid also for D=0. Union over S proves

    0 <= Tr(rho_H L) = Z_L/Z(H) <= delta.                  (9)

Equivalently, Z_+=(Z+Z_L)/2 and Z_-=(Z-Z_L)/2 differ by at most delta Z.
For delta<1 both logical parities have positive thermal weight and
Z_+/Z_- <= (1+delta)/(1-delta). A unique finite-N ground state is compatible
with this statement: beta is fixed in the activity window. At fixed N and
fixed positive one-site fields, beta->infinity eventually leaves that window
and the ground-state logical parity can have weight one.

## 3. The same bound for original pure-X bath autocorrelations

Take ANY fixed unsigned pure-X Pauli bath operator B=X_b, B^2=I, and
0<=tau<=beta. It need not be a single site. Define

    M_B(tau)=Tr[e^{-(beta-tau)H} B e^{-tau H} B],
    M_L,B(tau)=Tr[L e^{-(beta-tau)H} B e^{-tau H} B].        (10)

These are nonnegative in the computational basis, since all factors have
nonnegative entries. In the tagged path expansion the two prescribed bath
insertions contribute b+b=0 to the binary jump word. Equation(5) therefore
still holds for the FIELD word: the logical component must be formed by
field tags, rather than supplied by the bath insertions. The tilt identity
(6) holds with M_L,B in place of Z_L.

For any tilted H_theta, write
K=e^{-tau H_theta/2} B e^{-(beta-tau)H_theta/2}. Because L commutes with both
H_theta and B, the inserted trace is Tr[L K^*K]. It has absolute value at
most Tr[K^*K]. Schatten Holder with exponents 2beta/tau and
2beta/(beta-tau), and ||B||=1, gives

    Tr[K^*K] <= Tr[e^{-beta H_theta}].                     (11)

The endpoint cases tau=0,beta follow by continuity or B^2=I. Consequently
the exact same event bound and union imply, UNIFORMLY on this full interval,

    0<=M_L,B(tau)/Z(H)<=delta,
    0<=M_B(tau)/Z(H)<=1.                                 (12)

Let C_+/-(tau) be the NORMALIZED imaginary-time B autocorrelation within
the corresponding logical-L parity sector. Since [L,H]=[L,B]=0,

    C_+/-(tau) = (m(tau) +/- ell(tau))/(1 +/- r),
    r=Z_L/Z, m=M_B/Z, ell=M_L,B/Z.

Hence, for delta<1,

    |C_+(tau)-C_-(tau)| <= 4delta/(1-delta^2).              (13)

This is quantitative thermal/spectral-response structure for the UNCHANGED
pure-X bath, acquired from the actual full-density Hamiltonian. It supplies
neither a new coupling nor a convenient local sampler. Other Pauli bath
orientations and cross-channel products require a separate word/positivity
argument and are not silently included.

## 4. What spectral information is actually acquired

C_s(tau) is the Laplace transform of the sector's positive KMS transition
measure mu_s(omega), with omega=E_n-E_m and weights
e^{-beta E_m}|B_mn|^2/Z_s. Equation(13) controls those transforms at
0<=tau<=beta. It does NOT control total variation of the measures or paired
transition frequencies. Inverse Laplace recovery is ill-conditioned here.

An exact diagnostic needs no numerical fixture. For a two-level gap a>0,
the unit-X transition measure has masses

    p_a=1/(1+e^{-beta a}) at +a,
    1-p_a at -a.

Its normalized imaginary-time response is
cosh[(beta/2-tau)a]/cosh(beta a/2). Different positive gaps a and a' give
mutually disjoint atomic supports and therefore maximal total-variation
distance, although both responses approach one uniformly as a,a'->0.
Smooth bounded KMS rates do not merge those atoms in the exact Davies limit.
This is an inverse-problem obstruction, not an actual BDL failure theorem.

The globally conserved bare L leaves two signed syndrome-graph sectors.
Short FIELD words see no nontrivial holonomy, while a logical component can
eventually resolve the sectors. A distance-scale activity suppression is
therefore compatible with tiny NONZERO energy/frequency splittings. Exact
Davies secularization can distinguish such splittings. A successful dynamics
composition needs paired Bohr lines, a direct weighted jump estimate, or a
physically justified finite-resolution channel with its error/time budget.

## 5. Prior, assumptions, costs and next falsifier

The positive path and tilted partition methods are standard quantum
statistical-mechanical tools; no historical novelty is claimed. The new
candidate application here is the explicitly normalized logical-open-path
bound and its same-bath imaginary-time response consequence. BDL supplies
the actual CSS family and distance; cycle15 supplies the rigorous positive
path framework. The5Oct2026 Putterman--Zlokapa--Cotler near-commuting
decomposition has connected perturbative word structure and amplitude
bounds, but its random-factor coefficients are not automatically normalized
worldline-event probabilities or exact spectral pairing. Gibbs-cone
membership is not used as a dynamics premise.

No enumerator or finite simulation is required by (9)--(13). A naive exact
sum has exponentially many basis configurations and unbounded path order;
the symbolic proof does not assert that it is computationally cheap. Acquiring
the stabilizer span, an unsigned logical representative and the share-check
graph is binary linear algebra plus input reading. Canonical coefficient
bit length, actual geometry, new Gibbs preparation, thermal encoder and
physical bath rates remain charged. This note does not claim that preparing
the sector states is easy or that their correlated spectra are acquired.

The next falsifier is a positive logical-inserted path with no large connected
odd-field component, a fixed bath insertion whose two identical pure-X words
do not cancel, or a tilted inserted trace exceeding its partition bound.
If the literal proof survives independent reconstruction, the next dynamic
test is whether the actual BDL parity transition atoms pair exactly. A small
Laplace difference cannot substitute for that test.
