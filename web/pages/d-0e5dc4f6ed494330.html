1. ROOT'S UNIVERSAL LOWER PI BRIDGE, INDEPENDENTLY RECONSTRUCTED

Let P_t=exp(-tL) be unital CP and sigma-stationary. Schwarz and stationarity
give, for EVERY complex matrix X,
 Tr[sigma P_t(X)^*P_t(X)] <= Tr[sigma P_t(X^*X)]
                            =Tr[sigma X^*X].
No GNS self-adjointness is assumed. In the coordinate Y=Psi(X), this norm
is exactly
 Tr[sigma X^*X]=Tr[Y^*s^(-1)Ys]=<Y,e^(-delta)Y>,
where delta=ad(log s), and P_t corresponds to exp(-tH).
KMS symmetry gives H=H^* on the whole complex HS space. Differentiating
the norm contraction at t=0 therefore gives
 H e^(-delta)+e^(-delta)H >=0.
Congruence by e^(delta/2), divided by2, gives precisely
 G=[e^(delta/2)H e^(-delta/2)+e^(-delta/2)H e^(delta/2)]/2 >=0.

In an HS eigenbasis of delta write x_alpha=delta_alpha/2, with real
x_alpha. Then G_alpha,beta=H_alpha,beta cosh(x_alpha-x_beta).
Set A=(delta/2)/sinh(delta/2), C=cosh(delta/2), both with removable values
at zero. Direct symmetrization of the assigned J2 shows
 J2-kappa E2=2<Y,Q_kappa Y>,
 Q_kappa=A H C+C H A-(kappa/2)H.
The quotient of its entries by the corresponding entries of G is
 N_kappa(t,u)=[a(t)+a(u)-(kappa/2)b(t)b(u)]/(1-tu),
 t=tanh x_alpha, u=tanh x_beta,
 a(z)=(1-z^2)atanh(z)/z, a(0)=1, b(z)=sqrt(1-z^2).
Indeed cosh(x-y)=cosh(x)cosh(y)(1-tu), A(x)/C(x)=a(t),
and 1/C(x)=b(t). This quotient means an entrywise identity even when
an entry of G vanishes; no division by a zero matrix entry is needed.

For completeness, the Hardy identity is exact. The series
 a(z)=1-2 sum_(m>=1) z^(2m)/[(2m-1)(2m+1)]
is absolutely summable, so a is bounded analytic. The branch b analytic
in the disk is bounded with ||M_b||^2<=2. On the unit circle,
 2Re a(e^(i theta))=pi |sin theta|
                       =(pi/2)|b(e^(i theta))|^2.
For example atanh(e^(i theta)) has imaginary part pi/4 on(0,pi)
and -pi/4 on(pi,2pi); multiplying by -2i sin theta yields the identity.
Consequently their Toeplitz operators satisfy
 M_a+M_a^*=(pi/2)M_b^*M_b.
For every f in H^2,
 ||M_b^*f||=||P_(H^2)(conj(b)f)||<=||conj(b)f||=||M_b f||.
Thus M_b^*M_b-M_bM_b^*>=0, and
 D_pi=M_a+M_a^*-(pi/2)M_bM_b^*
       =(pi/2)(M_b^*M_b-M_bM_b^*)>=0.

The real Szego vectors k_t(z)=1/(1-tz), t in(-1,1), obey
M_g^*k_t=g(t)k_t for these real-coefficient functions. Therefore
<k_t,D_pi k_u>=N_pi(t,u). Every finite real-node N_pi matrix is PSD,
including zero and repeated nodes. The Schur product theorem gives
Q_pi=G Schur N_pi>=0. Hence J2>=pi E2. This is a proof on the full HS
space, so restricting to legal Hermitian trace-zero tangents is valid.
Root's statement for all kappa<=pi is also sound: its displayed
decomposition covers 0<=kappa<=pi; for kappa<0 both terms in
(pi/2)M_b^*M_b+(-kappa/2)M_bM_b^* are positive directly.
