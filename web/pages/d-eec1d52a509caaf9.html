## Why the representation is acquired rather than supplied

For a site i let S_i be the subgroup generated by its incident original checks, and

    A_i = Alg(S_i, X_i, Z_i).

Any Pauli monomial in this algebra is sP_i. If it commutes with all S_i, P_i commutes with every incident check and also with every other original check by disjoint support. Both CSS colors at i force P_i=I. Orthogonal Pauli expansion therefore shows

    A_i intersect Alg(S_i)' = Alg(S_i).

Thus Alg(S_i) is a maximal abelian subalgebra of A_i, INCLUDING its center. This is not a assertion about every operator on the enlarged43-qubit neighborhood.

If p<=7 is the number of independent incident checks, the combined binary Pauli span has dimension p+2. Its alternating commutation form has rank4, because the two single-qubit Pauli directions pair independently with the X/Z incidence characters. Hence A_i is a direct sum of at most2^(p-2)<=32 copies of M_4, with spectator multiplicities represented by identities.

Set H0_i=sum of the incident check penalties. Diagonalize H0_i+V_i within each of these4-dimensional blocks. Match sorted perturbed eigenvalues to sorted H0_i eigenvalues in the SAME central block. The min-max bound gives a unitary U_i in A_i and

    U_i(H0_i+V_i)U_i^dagger = H0_i+D_i,
    D_i in Alg(S_i),             ||D_i||<=epsilon.       (1)

No eigenvalue-gap estimate, near-identity choice, whole-system eigensolver or assumed spectral-flow oracle is used. Exact rational/algebraic inputs have bounded local algebraic dimension; arbitrary unrepresented reals are not declared computably exact. Compiling the total physical dressing and long-time finite-precision control still has separate costs.

Because no original check touches two selected sites, EVERY generator of A_i commutes with EVERY generator of A_j. Thus the local algebras and U_i commute even when enlarged physical supports overlap. With U=product U_i,

    U H U^dagger = H_d = H0+sum_i D_i.                (2)

This is generally not isospectral to H0: the diagonal interaction D is part of the constructed output. It answers a previously supplied-versus-acquired gap in the cycle14 unitary-orbit theorem.

## Scope and why it matters for future research

The constructive step is the small incident-check algebra. It replaces a previously supplied whole-spectrum dressing by at most32 local4x4 spectral problems per selected site. Together with the actual witness-erasure and fixed-bath theorems, it handles weak noncommuting fields on a fixed positive fraction of all sites. This is stronger than a perturbation confined to o(Q_k) qubits, although the two classes are incomparable in amplitude: the separate regional-saturation theorem permits arbitrarily strong fields on a small total region.

The decisive boundary is overlapping incident algebras. If two selected sites share a check, their local algebras can fail to commute. Sequentially applying these U_i then changes the neighboring input algebras, and equations(2)-(4) are not proved. A bounded coloring of all sites does not by itself repair this failure. Generic full-density interactions, affordable initial/new Gibbs preparation, optimal constants and robust finite-precision long-time simulation remain open.

For general correctable regions the same crossed-product argument produces a stabilizer MASA which can contain extra region-supported stabilizers. Original-generator filling then reappears in the energy-congestion bound. The singleton simplification is not valid if a qubit admits a one-site stabilizer outside its incident span: checks Z1Z2 and Z2 give the elementary caution Z1=(Z1Z2)Z2. The all-site CSS eligibility gate is therefore load-bearing.
