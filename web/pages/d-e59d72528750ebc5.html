# Autonomous endpoint temporal-order spectroscopy

## Result and status

**Constructive candidate:** a programmable ladder of coherent internal modes measures whether an unrecorded, possibly stationary classical signal has a preferred temporal order. The instrument requires preparation and readout only at the ends of a sensing window. It does not require replaying or reversing the signal, reconstructing its trajectory, or applying a rapid externally timed control waveform during sensing.

The central operation is to exchange the prepared and measured endpoint of a reciprocal ladder. This exactly substitutes for reversing the unknown signal inside a path-dependent measurement. A hierarchy of ladders is complete for bounded classical signal laws: every law that differs from its temporal reversal produces a nonzero signal at some finite ladder length and some finite coupling strength.

**Final capability gate: this does not currently meet the request for a genuinely new scientific capability.** Path signatures, sparse unitary developments, quantum computational sensing, and time-reversal-sensitive quantum sensing already exist. No new physical interaction was invented: the construction uses standard reciprocal coherent couplings and a Ramsey reference. The potentially distinct result is the particular autonomous, endpoint-swapped, complete reciprocity-test interface, with finite-strength certification. This may be a useful instrument architecture/theorem, but its acquisition advantage over existing native sensing has not been established. The rotating example is a spin-1 Rabi problem, not a discovery. Hardware feasibility remains hypothetical. No physical experiment was performed.

## 1. What access changes

Usual workflow: record a signal over time, then compute order-sensitive statistics.

Proposed workflow: couple signal channels directly to distinct links of a small coherent ladder; let its state evolve; read two endpoint observables in repeated realizations. The unknown signal itself supplies the time dependence. The wiring of channels to ladder links is fixed during each realization.

The useful setting is an inaccessible or high-bandwidth environment for which several observables can modulate calibrated probe couplings, but faithful trajectory recording is expensive or impossible. Direct applications to intracellular chemistry or microscopic material dynamics are hypotheses, not demonstrated implementation claims.

The mathematical result does not require a quantum computational advantage. A classical coherent-wave network implementing the same linear evolution can perform it. A quantum implementation conveniently supplies bounded measurement outcomes and a clear resource count.

## 2. Exact assumptions

1. The external classical signal is a random bounded measurable function a(t)=(a_1(t),...,a_d(t)) on [0,T], with |a_i(t)| <= M after normalization, taking M >= 1 to include the clock channel.
2. The probe does not appreciably change this signal law. Independent/reproducible windows can be obtained for different preparation settings.
3. A known constant channel a_0(t)=1 is available when completeness for the fully timed signal law is required. For X(t)=(t, integral_0^t a(s) ds), the induced reversal convention is X_rev(t)=X(T)-X(T-t). This is reversal of the signal's increments, not ordinary signed path inversion X(T-t)-X(T).
4. A selected input channel can modulate each ladder coupling linearly, natively and coherently, without first obtaining a full classical measurement record. Couplings are real, reciprocal, and calibrated. No unknown complex loop phase is permitted in the ideal theorem. If the couplings are synthesized from previously recorded data, the device is only postprocessing and establishes no new acquisition ability.
5. There is coherent evolution for duration T, calibrated endpoint preparation/readout, and a decoupled reference mode. The ideal theorem is unitary; errors require explicit bias bounds.
6. “Reversal” initially means reversing the order of values, a^R(t)=a(T-t). For a thermodynamic interpretation, time-reversal-odd variables must be assigned their physical parity. A nonzero statistical witness alone is not a calibrated entropy-production measurement.

These assumptions expose, rather than hide, the hard part: fabricating a probe with useful signal-to-coupling transduction, adequate bandwidth/coherence, and negligible backaction.

## 3. A word is compiled into a reciprocal ladder

Choose a word w=(i_1,...,i_r), where i_j belongs to {0,...,d}. Use active states |0>,...,|r>, and a reference state |R>.

The reference is dark. The active Hamiltonian is

H_w(t)/hbar = lambda sum_{j=1}^r a_{i_j}(t) (|j><j-1| + |j-1><j|).

Let U_w[a;lambda] be its time-ordered propagator. Define

D_w(lambda) = i^r E[ U_w[a;lambda]_{r0} - U_w[a;lambda]_{0r} ].

This quantity is real: the ladder is bipartite and all couplings are real, so an endpoint amplitude at graph distance r has the overall phase (-i)^r.

### Exact reciprocity identity

Every instantaneous Hamiltonian is real symmetric. Discretize time and write U=U_n...U_1. Each U_j is symmetric, so U^T=U_1...U_n, exactly the propagator for the reversed signal. Passing to the limit gives

U_w[a^R;lambda] = U_w[a;lambda]^T.

Consequently D_w changes sign under reversal and is exactly zero for every reversible signal law, at every coupling strength. This is not a weak-coupling statement.

### Why two endpoint preparations suffice

Prepare (|R>+|0>)/sqrt(2). After sensing, its reference-to-|r> coherence is U_{r0}/2. The appropriate Pauli-like X or Y observable on span{|R>,|r>} therefore has mean i^r U_{r0}; select the sign/quadrature according to r modulo 4. Its values are in {-1,0,1}.

Repeat with (|R>+|r>)/sqrt(2), reading the reference-to-|0> coherence. Subtract the two means. Thus each word/strength uses two preparation/readout settings and r+2 modes in total.

The reference is important. Ordinary transition probabilities give |U_{r0}|^2 and |U_{0r}|^2, which lose amplitude signs/phases and do not support the completeness argument below.

## 4. Constructive completeness theorem

Define the ordered integral

S_w[a] = integral_{0<t_1<...<t_r<T} product_j a_{i_j}(t_j) dt_1...dt_r.

Reversing the signal reverses the word: S_w[a^R]=S_rev(w)[a].

The first possible walk along the ladder from 0 to r takes exactly r steps and is unique. The Dyson expansion therefore gives

D_w(lambda) = lambda^r (E S_w[a] - E S_rev(w)[a]) + O(lambda^{r+2}).

There is no order r+1 correction because of bipartiteness. This remains true when a channel appears on several edges: the monotone r-step path is still unique.

**Theorem, ideal bounded-signal model.** With the clock channel, the signal law equals its reversed law if and only if D_w(lambda)=0 for all finite words w and all positive lambda.

**Proof route.** The forward implication is the transpose identity. Conversely, vanishing of every analytic D_w on positive lambda makes every leading coefficient vanish, hence all expected ordered integrals agree between the law and its reversal. Form the time-augmented integrated path X(t)=(t, integral_0^t a(s) ds). These paths lie in a compact uniformly Lipschitz family with fixed starting point. Their iterated-integral coordinates separate paths because the strictly increasing time coordinate removes reparametrization/tree-like ambiguity. The shuffle identity makes their linear span an algebra containing constants. Stone–Weierstrass then makes that algebra dense in the continuous functions on this compact path family. Equality of all expected coordinates therefore gives equality of the two path laws. The integrated path determines a almost everywhere. Contrapositively, any difference has a finite word with a nonzero coefficient, so the corresponding analytic D_w is nonzero at some arbitrarily small positive lambda. QED.

**Limits of the theorem:** this is an unbounded hierarchy, not a uniform finite-resource test. It supplies a complete family, not a universal finite ladder. It does not provide a small upper bound on the first distinguishing word length, a polynomial-time word search, or a uniformly large observable signal. Arbitrarily near-reversible laws preclude uniform finite statistical power without a separation promise. Full path-law tomography has not been made inexpensive.

## 5. A finite-strength capability separation

Take a stationary rotating signal

a_1(t)=cos(omega t+phi),  a_2(t)=sin(omega t+phi),

where phi is uniform on [0,2pi). Reversal changes the sign of omega after relabeling the uniform phase.

For any fixed T, the integrated input vector has the same distribution for +omega and -omega: it is uniformly distributed on a circle of radius |2 sin(omega T/2)/omega|. Therefore **every probe with constant mutually commuting input generators** has the same endpoint law in both cases, regardless of its number of modes or choice of endpoint measurement. Such a probe's propagator depends only on that integrated vector.

For T=2pi/|omega| this is even simpler: every channel integral vanishes pathwise, so every such commuting probe returns its initial state.

Now use the three-active-mode ladder for w=(1,2). A rotating-frame calculation gives, at a full period,

D_(1,2)(lambda) = [2 omega/sqrt(omega^2+lambda^2)] sin[T sqrt(omega^2+lambda^2)].

It is independent of phi. At omega>0 and lambda=0.75 omega, D=1.6. Reversing the rotation gives D=-1.6. This is a large finite-strength distinction from an autonomous four-mode probe, with no synchronization to the unknown initial phase.

The leading small-coupling coefficient is 2pi/omega^2, matching

E S_(1,2) - E S_(2,1) = T/omega - sin(omega T)/omega^2.

An even simpler deterministic example is two nonoverlapping A and B pulses of unit duration and amplitude. At lambda=pi/2 the word-(A,B) device gives D=+1 for AB and D=-1 for BA, despite identical channel integrals.

**The separation is deliberately bounded.** It is against autonomous commuting integrators. Time-resolved detectors, time-modulated dephasing probes, and existing cross-correlation spectroscopy can distinguish suitable examples too. There is no claim that conventional sensing in general is blind.

## 6. Finite data and finite coupling

For a fixed selected word and coupling, pair independent runs of the two settings. Their outcome difference Y lies in [-2,2] and E Y=D. With n pairs,

Pr(|D_hat-D| >= epsilon) <= 2 exp(-n epsilon^2/8).

Thus n >= 8 log(2/alpha)/epsilon^2 is sufficient for confidence 1-alpha. This certifies the actual finite-strength witness without estimating derivatives at zero.

If each setting has a known absolute systematic mean error at most b, require |D_hat| > 2b + epsilon before declaring detected temporal asymmetry. Unknown decoherence/preparation asymmetry cannot simply be assumed to cancel. Searching many words or strengths also requires held-out validation or simultaneous/anytime-valid error control.

Let V=integral_0^T ||H_w(t)/(hbar lambda)|| dt. Since a real weighted path has maximum absolute row sum at most 2M, V <= 2MT, independent of r. A conservative expansion error is

|D_w(lambda)-lambda^r Delta_w|
 <= 2 sum_{k>=1} (|lambda|V)^(r+2k)/(r+2k)!
 <= 2 exp(|lambda|V)(|lambda|V)^(r+2)/(r+2)!.

Here Delta_w=E S_w-E S_rev(w). This yields an explicit choice of sufficiently small lambda if a lower bound on |Delta_w| is available, but the resulting sample cost can be severe. At first detectable order r, D is approximately lambda^r Delta_w, so fixed-power shot cost scales as lambda^(-2r) |Delta_w|^(-2). For fixed r with small Delta_w, the naive choice lambda proportional to sqrt(|Delta_w|) gives a shot scaling of order |Delta_w|^(-(r+2)), up to dimensional constants. No good high-order sample-complexity claim is being hidden.

The hardware still needs adequate transduction bandwidth. Fast oscillations at fixed available coupling can produce a very small signal. This is not a route around bandwidth, signal-energy, or information limits.

## 7. Reproducible local checks

`verify_probe.py` performs only small deterministic matrix/ODE calculations. `verification_results.json` records:

- Reversal/transposition residual at most 3.34e-16 on random piecewise inputs, word lengths 2 through 5.
- Correct leading ordered-integral coefficients as lambda decreases.
- AB and BA witnesses exactly +1 and -1 to floating-point precision.
- Chiral closed-form formula matching numerical ODE integration within 5.94e-12, across four couplings and four initial phases.
- Dark-reference readout reproducing the signed endpoint amplitude.

These validate the algebraic examples, not novelty, physical robustness, or usefulness in a real material.

## 8. Closest primary sources and novelty boundary

1. **Li, Lyu, Ni, Tao, Restricted Path Characteristic Function Determines the Law of Stochastic Processes.** [arXiv:2404.18661](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18661), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.18661v2). Sparse tridiagonal path developments already give a constructive way to recover tensor coefficients and determine path laws. The ladder/uniqueness ingredients must be credited as prior art. The candidate adds the endpoint-swap reversal comparator and its physical sensing interpretation; this may be a corollary rather than a sufficiently novel theorem.

2. **Kuffer, Zwick, Alvarez, Sensing Out-of-Equilibrium and Quantum Non-Gaussian environments via induced Time-Reversal Symmetry Breaking on the quantum-probe dynamics.** [published PRX Quantum 6, 020320 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.6.020320), [arXiv:2405.04742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04742), [initial preprint full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.04742v1). SENSIT already exploits reversal-sensitive probe responses and includes NMR demonstrations. Its described construction compares time-dependent dynamical-decoupling waveforms on a dephasing qubit. The final published abstract explicitly includes intrinsic time-reversal breaking, so no distinction should be based on the initial preprint's narrower discussion of nonstationary/quantum environments. The candidate's intended distinction is a fixed coupling architecture, endpoint-only swapping, and a complete bounded-classical-signal hierarchy. Do not claim invention of time-reversal sensing.

3. **Khan, Prabhu, Wright, McMahon, Quantum computational sensing using quantum signal processing, quantum neural networks, and Hamiltonian engineering.** [arXiv:2507.15845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15845), [published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-026-01235-w). Task-specific computation before measurement, including time-varying inputs and Hamiltonian-engineered systems, is established. The general sensor-as-computer framing is therefore not new.

4. **Sen et al., Quantum sensors that compute: quantum computational magnetic-field sensing using a superconducting qubit.** [arXiv:2608.17400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17400). An experimental QCS demonstration further rules out presenting this broad direction as unexplored. It does not itself establish the present endpoint-reciprocity hierarchy.

5. **Sales et al., Three Dimensional Polarimetric Neutron Tomography of Magnetic Fields.** [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5797168/). Path-order sensitivity of noncommuting probe rotations is established in physical tomography. Here the proposed unknown is a signal's stochastic ordering, not a magnetic field reconstructed along known ray paths.

6. **Rabi, Space Quantization in a Gyrating Magnetic Field (1937).** [Phys. Rev. 51, 652](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.51.652). This treats rotating-field evolution for arbitrary angular momentum. The example's matrices A=E01+E10, B=E12+E21, C=E20-E02 close under commutators: [iA,iB]=C, [C,iA]=iB, [iB,C]=iA, and (iA)^2+(iB)^2+C^2=-2I. This is the spin-1 Lie algebra representation. Thus the closed-form example is squarely a Rabi-class calculation, including its handedness response. Also see [Kobe et al., exact precessing-field phases (1990)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.42.3744), [Berry's geometric-phase paper (1984)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1984.0023), and [Suter et al., experimental Berry phase in NMR (1987)](https://pines.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/publications/berrys_phase_in_magnetic_resonance.pdf).

7. **Meinel et al., Quantum nonlinear spectroscopy of single nuclear spins (2022).** [Nature Communications 13, 5318](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32610-8). Higher-order and different types of temporal correlations are already accessible through sequential weak quantum measurements, with a fourth-order experimental example. The ladder trades repeated in-window measurement/control for internal coherent modes. That is an interface tradeoff, not proof that the observable was previously inaccessible.

8. **Chen, Iterated Integrals and Exponential Homomorphisms (1954).** [original DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-4.1.502). The ordered-integral representation is classical prior art. **Jurdjevic and Sussmann, Control systems on Lie groups (1972)** [original DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0396(72)90035-6) is foundational context for noncommuting control and Lie-algebra generation. The protocol uses those ordinary noncommutative ingredients; it does not invent order encoding or commutator control.

9. **Loschmidt-echo comparison.** An echo compares perturbed forward/backward evolution or the corresponding state overlaps. This protocol does not implement U inverse or reverse the physical environment: transposition plus swapping endpoints provides a virtual reversed-signal comparator. That distinguishes the experimental sequence from an echo, but does not establish a new physical primitive. See [Peres, Stability of quantum motion in chaotic and regular systems (1984)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.30.1610) for the original fidelity/stability formulation.

## 9. What would make this worth a serious program

The first deliverable should be a carefully bounded autonomous instrument, not a universal microscopic computer: two calibrated channels, three coupled active modes plus a phase reference, and a known stationary chiral stimulus. Establish the opposite-sign finite-strength response while autonomous commuting probes remain exactly indistinguishable. Then test drift, dephasing, imperfect reciprocity, and input transduction with a preregistered calibration error budget.

The mathematical next step is to characterize low-order witnesses under realistic bandwidth and coherence constraints, and to find physically meaningful families in which a small ladder length is guaranteed. The technological next step is a platform where input-dependent real coupling is native, rather than a simulation fed by data that were already fully measured.

The potentially substantial capability is endpoint measurement of temporal order in environments that cannot supply trajectories. But existing native quantum/nonlinear sensors already acquire temporal-order information, and no specific environment has yet been identified where this ladder obtains a scientifically decisive observable that they cannot acquire. The honest disposition is to preserve the constructive theorem and tests as a narrower research lead, and continue searching for the requested new capability elsewhere.
