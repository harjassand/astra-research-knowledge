# What a linear-buffer filling theorem would already buy

Root, 7 October 2026. **Conditional lemma, independent gate requested.** This is a direct extension of the syndrome-box argument in Bravyi–Hastings–Michalakis1001.0344v1, Lemma3.2, to a scale-proportional buffer. It is not a full perturbative stability theorem or a claim of novelty. The actual recursive quantum memory has not yet been proved to satisfy its filling premise.

## Exact premise and conclusion

Consider a finite D-dimensional lattice of bounded local Hilbert dimension and a commuting-projector Hamiltonian H0=sum_j h_j, with a common zero ground space P. Terms have uniformly bounded range and density, and nonzero defect energy at least one. Grouping sites permits range at most one. Let P_B be the product of I-h_j over terms wholly supported in B.

Assume a constant C>=1, independent of total size, gives the following **local annihilation property** for cubes A of side q up to a stated scale: every operator O supported in A with OP=0 also obeys O P_(B(A))=0, where B(A) is the concentric cube of side at most C(q+2). This is an explicit hypothesis. For stabilizer Hamiltonians it follows from equality of the global and locally generated stabilizer groups on A in that enlarged cube; distance alone does not imply it.

Let W=sum_A W_A over translated q-cubes, with W_A Hermitian, W_A P=0 and norm(W_A)<=J. Then

    norm(W psi) <= c_D J [C(q+2)]^D norm(H0 psi).

The constant also charges the fixed check density and range. The same argument applies to a local Hamiltonian H0(Omega), provided every enlarged B(A) lies inside Omega. This latter inclusion is a necessary boundary condition, not a consequence of supp W_A subset Omega.

## Proof with the enlarged buffer charged

Put R=ceil(C(q+2)). Partition check anchors into disjoint boxes of side R; assign every check to one anchor, including checks crossing a geometric box boundary. The commuting-projector decomposition labels each joint eigenspace by its defect syndrome. Group syndromes by the set Y of boxes containing at least one defect. Write R_Y for the resulting orthogonal projectors and k=|Y|. On R_Y, H0 has energy at least k.

The local annihilation premise and Hermiticity give

    W_A=(I-P_(B(A))) W_A (I-P_(B(A))).

Consequently R_Y W_A R_Z can be nonzero only if Y and Z each have an occupied box whose anchor is within distance O(R) of A. A check disjoint from A commutes with W_A. Thus Y and Z can differ only in boxes containing anchors of checks touching A. Since q<=R and check range is bounded, at most d_D boxes can change; d_D is independent of C,q,total size.

For fixed Y with k occupied boxes, there are at most c_D k R^D translated cubes A close enough to an occupied box. Each A allows at most 2^(d_D) output occupancy sets. Hence, with M_YZ=norm(R_Y W R_Z),

    sum_Z M_YZ <= a_D J k R^D.

Whenever M_YZ is nonzero, |Z|<=k+d_D. M is symmetric because W is Hermitian. For p_Y=norm(R_Y psi), expand norm(W psi)^2 and use p_Y p_V<=(p_Y^2+p_V^2)/2. The coefficient at p_Y^2 is bounded by

    sum_Z M_YZ sum_V M_ZV
      <= a_D^2 J^2 R^(2D) k(k+d_D).

For k=0 every row vanishes by annihilation. For k>=1, k(k+d_D)<=(1+d_D)k^2. Summing over Y, and using sum_Y k^2 R_Y<=H0^2, proves the bound after absorbing ceil effects into c_D. Restricting the anchor partition and all enlarged support regions to Omega gives the stated local version.

For a sum over q with norm(W_(q,A))<=J exp(-mu q), triangle inequality gives a finite relative-bound coefficient c_D J C^D sum_q(q+2)^D exp(-mu q), for all scales on which the premise holds. Finite-size terms beyond that scale remain a separate norm tail. Small enough J therefore preserves the ground sector and a positive gap for these already ground-annihilating perturbations.

## Exact remaining obstacle

This removes one possible concern about a Cr filling radius: the relative-bound step needs only a fixed geometric multiplicative buffer, with an explicit polynomial C cost. It does **not** justify applying the full BHM theorem verbatim, whose stated local-consistency condition uses a fixed collar. A generic local perturbation is not already decomposed into operators annihilating P. The iterative local block-diagonalization, its boundary inclusions and repeated support dilation must still be proved with the new buffer; repeated loss of decay cannot be ignored.

For the May2026 recursive memory there is a second, earlier obstacle: a level-uniform local-filling anchor at the coarse-projection radius floor is still missing. The team's degree-one maps and support recurrence do not establish it. This note is a conditional downstream use of that prospective lemma and sharpens the remaining stability work without declaring the open premise true.
