Interface
---------
Keep the exact Hcrit, endpoint killing and S=L-1 chain of RESULT.txt.
Fix an integer r>=1, INDEPENDENT of L. Supply the finite table

 v:[0,1] times {0,1}^r -> R,

continuous in x for each of its 2^r entries, with |v(x,z)|<=M. Define

 H_L^v=Hcrit+L^-2 sum_{i=1}^{S-r+1}
                       v(i/L,n_i,...,n_{i+r-1}).         (C1)

For L<=r the sum may be empty; this finite exception is irrelevant. The
local variables commute and v is a diagonal operator, so (C1) is an
explicit finite-chain Hamiltonian. No L-growing range, hidden internal
state, off-diagonal interaction, or nonlocal density oracle is covered.

For p in [0,1], define its supplied Bernoulli polynomial

 barv(x,p)=sum_{z in {0,1}^r}v(x,z)p^{|z|}(1-p)^{r-|z|}.
                                                               (C2)

This is the expectation of v under r independent Bernoulli(p) variables.
For each x it is a degree<=r polynomial. It satisfies |barv|<=M and
|barv(x,p)-barv(x,q)|<=2rM|p-q|, by coupling the r Bernoulli coordinates.
The coefficient table is explicit; evaluating (C2) costs O(r 2^r) scalar
operations. Here r is fixed. This algebraic potential evaluation is not a
finite-chain ground-state or Gibbs sampler algorithm.

An exact local count replacement
--------------------------------
Take a block of ell>=r sites and its maximum-spin projection P_B. Condition
on K particles in the block. The compressed expectation of v at any r
specified distinct sites is the uniform population-sampling expectation

 barv_ell(x,K/ell)
 =sum_z v(x,z) (K)_{|z|}(ell-K)_{r-|z|}/(ell)_r.         (C5)

All falling factorials use integer K,ell; a term with insufficient particles
or holes is zero. This formula holds at K=0 and K=ell as well, with no
density floor. It is precisely P_B v P_B=barv_ell(x,K/ell)P_B.

Compare drawing r indices from the ell-site population with replacement
and without replacement. Couple them until a repeat occurs in the former;
its collision probability is at most r(r-1)/(2ell). Until then the binary
words agree. Therefore

 sup_{K=0,...,ell}|barv_ell(x,K/ell)-barv(x,K/ell)|
                              <=M r(r-1)/ell.            (C6)

This is a uniform equivalence-of-ensembles estimate including K near0 or
ell. It does not replace a microscopic occupation word by its expectation
inside a single configuration.

For a normalized low-energy vector, apply (14),(17) of RESULT.txt to the
operator v(i/L,n_i,...,n_{i+r-1})-barv(i/L,K_i/ell), with norm<=2M. Its
projection has norm at most M r(r-1)/ell by(C6). Thus its spatially
averaged expectation differs from the barv evaluated on the ell-block
density by at most

 M r(r-1)/ell +6M sqrt(ell^3 D/(4J L))+O_r(M ell/L).     (C7)

Use the two-block estimate (23) and the Lipschitz bound2rM in(C2) to replace
that ell-block density by a=floor(delta L) forward density. At fixed
ell,delta take L first, then ell=ceil(delta^(-2/3)) and delta->0. Weak
empirical-law convergence and continuity of smoothed box averages give

 lim (1/L)<sum_i v(i/L,n_i,...,n_{i+r-1})>
                        =E_Pi integral barv(x,rho(x)).   (C8)

Spatial continuity makes the grid-to-integral error vanish uniformly over
all bounded densities. The unnormalized version of(C7) uses sqrt(w D)
and therefore survives summation over the softmax localized pieces. No
rare local-count probability is divided out.

Ground bound and recovery
-------------------------
The perturbation has norm<=M/L. Vacuum has energy in[-M/L,M/L], so an
approximate ground vector satisfies D<=2M/L+o(1/L). Apply the core
random-law kinetic liminf (6) and(C8):

 liminf L E_min,L^v
   >=E_Pi[I(rho)+integral barv(x,rho)]
   >=the minimum in(C3).                               (C9)

For a smooth product-spin profile, the r nearby on-site occupations are
independent Bernoulli variables with parameters p(i/L),...,p((i+r-1)/L).
Uniform continuity of the profile implies their local potential expectation
differs from barv(i/L,p(i/L)) by o(1) uniformly in i. The exchange and
endpoint recovery in RESULT.txt §9 is unchanged. Thus the product state
has L-scaled energy tending to the functional in(C3). H^1 density gives
the full recovery and proves(C3). Its minimizer exists by the same
compactness as the core theorem, since barv is bounded and continuous.
