EXACT INTERFACE AND CORRECTIONS

D=(X tensor X)SWAP. Its 00,01,10,11 matrix has diagonal a,b,b,a,

GENERAL POSITIVE DETERMINANT EXPECTATION
For m gates with a_v>0 and c_v,d_v>=0, put real skew four-port blocks A_v in
out_i,out_j,in_i,in_j order. Their paired upper entries are sqrt(d/a) on 12,34;
sqrt(b/a) on 13,24; sqrt(c/a) on 14,23. Pf(A_v)=(d-b+c)/a=-1,
and each local tensor entry is a_v times a squared principal Pfaffian.
Add the external wire matching with entries eta_e xi_e, where xi are independent
uniform signs and eta are any fixed unit phases. For the resulting 4m matrix K,
  Z=(product_v a_v) E_xi |det K(eta xi)|,
with an additional factor 2 for every idle qubit.

I checked the local entry identity and the global coefficient argument: the
Pfaffian is multiaffine in matching variables; fixing chosen external edges leaves
disjoint local principal blocks. Its coefficient sign disappears upon squaring,
and sign-character orthogonality removes cross terms. Thus arbitrary topology,
repeated edges and interleavings are admitted without erasing physical parity.
Complex phases require |det K|; real signs give a nonnegative determinant.

The matrix and matching are acquired from the circuit: 4m dimension, 2m sign bits,
O(m^3) arithmetic per draw. Polynomial precision suffices for a stated absolute
per-draw error on rational inputs; this is not a bound on total samples. The exact
worker fixtures include the six-gate interleaved bowtie with all 4096 sign choices.
Integration inspected the analytic proof and fixture artifacts, without a new
full replay. Godsil–Gutman/Barvinok determinant-estimator and Pfaffian mechanisms
are inherited; the explicit critical EPR* factorization and phase analysis are new
relative to the supplied checkpoint, with historical priority UNKNOWN.

VARIANCE AND CONTROLS
At legal rational weights (a,b,c,d)=(4,9,4,1), the raw one-gate relative second
moment is 626/169. For independent k-fold components it becomes (626/169)^k.
A directly chosen phase product i along each worldline makes the identity-only
circuit estimator constant. For an interacting one-gate component, the tested
phase tuning still has relative second moment 194/169, again tensorizing
exponentially. These are failures of the stated estimators, not counting-hardness
claims: disjoint components themselves can be factored and evaluated easily.
Small repeated-triangle floating diagnostics do not control growing circuits.
An acquired polynomial relative-variance bound, justified annealing construction,
or a different global positive representation with usable sampling remains the
load-bearing algorithmic gate. Positivity and polynomial per-draw cost alone do
not close it. The older parity-erasing proposal followed by global rejection also
has an exact exponentially shrinking acceptance family; that excludes only that
proposal. The single-log-concave coordinate-lift obstruction has its stated
selector-preserving scope and does not exclude a two-global-factor encoding.

EXPLICIT MATROID CONSTRUCTION AND ITS BOUNDARY
With one gate per edge of an n-cycle, the checked common transfer eigenvalues give
  Z_n=product(2a+c+d)+[1+(-1)^n]product(c+d)+product(c-d).
Writing u=a+c, v=a+d, Z_n/2 is the even-parity product sum E_n(u,v), plus
product(c+d) for even n. Odd cycles have the explicit binary [I|J-I] matroid
and one-per-pair partition matroid: a transversal is a basis exactly when its
number of second columns is even. For every cycle, a positive parity DAG encodes
Z_n/2; even cycles add a disjoint positive chain. DAG source-target paths are in
weight-preserving bijection with bipartite matchings by adding unit selfloops at
off-path vertices. Acyclicity excludes every other matching component. This
gives two explicit partition matroids with local derived weights and unit weights.

For a simple cactus, require every block's gates to be consecutive in the circuit
word. The block product commutes with collective X and Z, so tracing a leaf block
except its articulation yields (Z_block/2)I. Only the consecutive-block schedule
licenses substituting that scalar inside the full product. Repeated pruning and
direct sums give
  Z_G=2^{components(G)} WCB(P_rows,P_columns),
with ground size <=8m, rank <=3m, explicit independence oracles and polynomial
rational bit/range costs. I checked the cycle contraction, DAG bijection and
partial-trace schedule argument. The earlier supplied triangle is inherited from
epr_parity/root; arbitrary cycles, the all-parity DAG compiler and the cactus
extension were independently derived by epr_matroid_gluing.

More decisively for significance, the displayed transfer/parity dynamic program
