bound and the separation argument “would imply feasibility” of the LP over
the finite family \(\psi_0,\ldots,\psi_n\) for every self-compatible
channel. This conflated continuous-support feasibility with feasibility in a
restricted finite seed hull. That implication was too strong and is withdrawn.

The corrected status distinguishes:

   of \(\psi_0,\ldots,\psi_n\), sufficient for the finite LP. Per the parent
   checkpoint, its finite Spin(15) test fails. This only blocks that sufficient
   argument; it does not prove infeasibility of the LP for any particular
   legal transfer vector.
3. Direct exact LP feasibility for supplied rational transfers. A feasible
   solution produces the stated comparator; infeasibility rules out only
   this finite seed family for those transfers.

The all-n exact signed-permutation seed-channel compiler theorem is unchanged.
