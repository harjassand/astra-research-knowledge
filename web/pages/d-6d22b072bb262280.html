2. COMPLETE DETERMINISTIC ABSORBER PROOF

The ODE is
    a'=b(1-a),  b'=a(c-b),  c'=a(b-c)+1-c.                 (D)
For a positive initial state, let l=min(a(0),1)>0, U=max(a(0),1).
Exactly a(t)-1=(a(0)-1)exp(-integral_0^t b), so l<=a(t)<=U.
Choose U/(U+1)<q<1 and H=b+qc. Then
    H'=-(1-q)ab+[(1-q)a-q]c+q
       <=-d_b b-d_c c+q,
with d_b=(1-q)l>0 and d_c=q-(1-q)U>0. Taking
delta=min(d_b,d_c/q)>0 gives H'<=-delta H+q. Thus b,c are bounded
and the positive solution is global. Since c'>=1-(U+1)c,
liminf c>=1/(U+1). Since b'>=l c-U b, b has an eventual positive
lower bound. Therefore integral b diverges and a->1 exponentially eventually.
For z=(b,c), z'=M(a)z+(0,1) with
    M(a)=[[-a,a],[a,-a-1]].
For e=z-(1,1),
    e'=M(1)e+(a-1)[[-1,1],[1,-1]]z.
M(1) has eigenvalues (-3+sqrt5)/2 and (-3-sqrt5)/2, both negative.
The forcing tends to zero because z is bounded; variation of constants implies
e->0. Every positive initial condition therefore converges to (1,1,1), without
importing a general global-attractor theorem or N33's candidate proof.

Q=[1/2,2]^3 is invariant. On A lower/upper facets a'=b/2>0 and -b<0.
On B lower/upper facets a(c-b)>=0 and <=0. On C lower facet
c'=a(b-1/2)+1/2>=1/2; on C upper facet c'=a(b-2)-1<=-1.

For V_0=a+b+c,

P(sup_{s<=t} V_0(X_s)>=N)<=(V_0(X_0)+1)e^t/(N+1).
Jumps change V_0 by at most one; finite balls have bounded total rates.
Explosion would require exits from all count balls, so N->infinity proves
nonexplosion. This is an explicit derivation, not an imported regularity claim.

5. NO COERCIVE CONCAVE FOSTER POTENTIAL

Let W be concave on D={a,b,c>=0:a+b>=1}, and coercive on Gamma, meaning
W(x)->infinity along every Gamma integer sequence with norm->infinity.
Along any coordinate ray staying in D, this forces strict increase across
every unit step: if a concave one-variable g has g(t+1)<=g(t), later integer
secants are no larger, and g remains bounded above, contradicting coercivity.
At z_n=(0,n,0), the only jumps are
    B->A+B, rate n, displacement +e_A;
    0->C, rate 1, displacement +e_C.
Both strictly increase W, so
    L W(z_n)=n[W(z_n+e_A)-W(z_n)]
              +[W(z_n+e_C)-W(z_n)]>0                    (C)
for every n>=1. Hence no such W satisfies L W<=-epsilon off a finite set,
nor a geometric Foster inequality L W<=-epsilon W+b 1_K.

For W=min_{j<=m}(alpha_j dot x+beta_j), a sharper elementary proof applies.
Gamma contains coordinate rays (a,0,0), (0,b,0), (1,0,c); each affine form
upper-bounds W, so every alpha_{j,A},alpha_{j,B},alpha_{j,C} must be positive
for W to be coercive. Let alpha_A=min_j alpha_{j,A}, alpha_C=min_j alpha_{j,C}.
Then L W(z_n)>=n alpha_A+alpha_C>0. This holds for arbitrary real/rational
coefficients and any finite facet count. More facets/bit precision/search cannot
