# A quantitative thermal encoder with approximate logical commutation

7 October 2026. pde_transport9, verified Sol/max; no nested workers.
Status: complete conditional derivation; independent rounding_prior
analytic gate PASS (WEIGHTED_LEAKAGE_AND_LAMB_SHIFT_GATE.md).
pde_spectrum9 separately reconstructed the same interface as PASS.
The thermal coherent/bath hypotheses below have not been proved for
generic perturbations of the actual memory. This is a replacement
criterion with explicit debts, not an application of Chesi under
weakened assumptions.

## Finite-system interface

Let H be any finite Hermitian Hamiltonian, beta>0 finite, and
rho=exp(-beta H)/Tr exp(-beta H). Let O1,O2,O3 be Hermitian unitaries
forming an exact full-Hilbert-space qubit Pauli algebra. Fix its virtual
factorization H_system=C^2 tensor H_A. The algebra can be the actual
memory's syndrome-decoder Pauli frame, or another explicitly acquired
one; a Pauli algebra only on the ground band is insufficient here.

Take independent Hermitian physical couplings A_alpha with norm
a_alpha, nonnegative KMS rates gamma_alpha<=Gamma_alpha, and their
FULL-H spectral components A_alpha,omega. Set

    G=-i[K, .]+D_H,  [K,H]=0,                         (1)

where K=H if the Lamb shift is omitted, and K=H+K_LS if included.
Assume D_H rho=0. Define

    c_j^H = (Tr rho [H,Oj]^dagger[H,Oj])^(1/2),
    c_j^K = (Tr rho [K,Oj]^dagger[K,Oj])^(1/2),
    C_(alpha,omega,j)=[Oj,A_alpha,omega],
    k_(alpha,j) = sum_omega gamma_alpha(omega)
                  Tr rho C_(alpha,omega,j)^dagger C_(alpha,omega,j),
    Lambda = 2||K||+2 sum_alpha Gamma_alpha a_alpha^2.    (2)

Subtracting an arbitrary scalar from K can improve Lambda. No number
of frequencies occurs in (2). The rates are those for H, not H0.

## Conditional theorem

There exist explicit CPTP encoder and decoder with Dec Enc=id such
that every one-qubit state tau satisfies

    ||exp(tG) Enc(tau)-Enc(tau)||_1 <= t E,             (3)
    E = sum_(j=1)^3 [c_j^K
          +sum_alpha sqrt(Gamma_alpha) a_alpha sqrt(k_(alpha,j))]
          +(beta Lambda/2) sum_(j=1)^3 c_j^H.          (4)

The same bound holds after Dec. In particular, polynomially bounded
Lambda,beta,K_channel_count and thermal quantities decaying as
poly(N)exp(-b N^eta) give an exponential-in-N^eta storage bound.
If only their squares have that decay, the exponent is halved but
remains a positive power. This is a finite-temperature estimate; its
beta->infinity limit requires a separate uniform analysis.

## Gibbs commutator and the exact product encoder

For Hermitian O, let C=[H,O], so C^dagger=-C. Duhamel gives

    [rho,O]=integral_0^beta rho^(s/beta) C
                         rho^(1-s/beta) ds             (5)

up to an irrelevant global sign. At the endpoints the trace norms
of rho C and C rho are at most
(Tr rho C^dagger C)^(1/2), by Hilbert–Schmidt factorization; anti-Hermiticity
makes the two endpoint expressions equal. Schatten three-lines
interpolation for z->rho^z C rho^(1-z) keeps that bound for every
0<=Re z<=1. Therefore

    ||[rho,O]||_1 <= beta (Tr rho C^dagger C)^(1/2).    (6)

Full rank of finite-temperature rho justifies the imaginary powers.
No lower bound on its smallest eigenvalue is inserted into the constant.

Logical Pauli twirling defines

    bar_rho=(rho+sum_(j=1)^3 Oj rho Oj)/4
           = I_L/2 tensor rho_A,
    rho_A=Tr_L rho,
    delta=||bar_rho-rho||_1
          <= (beta/4) sum_j c_j^H.                    (7)

Set Enc(tau)=tau tensor rho_A and Dec=Tr_A in the exact acquired Pauli
factorization. These are CPTP and compose to the identity. The logical
maximally mixed input maps to bar_rho, rather than asserting that rho
itself is an exact product. Preparing rho_A remains a charged thermal
preparation problem; the theorem only constructs the maps.

## Frequency-resolved dissipative product identity

Fix alpha, abbreviate S_omega=A_alpha,omega,
gamma_omega=gamma_alpha(omega), C_omega=[O,S_omega].
Hermiticity gives S_omega^dagger=S_(-omega), and KMS implies

    rho S_omega^dagger
      =exp(-beta omega) S_omega^dagger rho,
    sum_omega gamma_omega rho S_omega^dagger C_omega
      =-sum_omega gamma_omega S_omega rho C_omega^dagger. (8)

Equation (8) does not assume [O,H]=0. Each independent Davies channel
fixes rho. Expanding its product rule and then using (8) yields

    D_alpha(rho O)
      =1/2 [rho sum_omega gamma_omega C_omega^dagger S_omega
            -sum_omega gamma_omega S_omega rho C_omega^dagger]. (9)

For the second term in (9), Hilbert–Schmidt Cauchy–Schwarz across
frequencies gives

    ||sum_omega gamma_omega S_omega rho C_omega^dagger||_1
      <= (sum_omega gamma_omega Tr rho S_omega^dagger S_omega)^(1/2)
         (sum_omega gamma_omega Tr rho C_omega^dagger C_omega)^(1/2)
      <= sqrt(Gamma_alpha) a_alpha sqrt(k_(alpha,j)).  (10)

For the first term, use the rectangular row
R=[sqrt(gamma_omega) sqrt(rho) C_omega^dagger]_omega
and column T=[sqrt(gamma_omega) S_omega]_omega. Then

    rho sum_omega gamma_omega C_omega^dagger S_omega
       =sqrt(rho) R T,
    ||R||_2^2=k_(alpha,j),
    ||T||^2=||sum_omega gamma_omega S_omega^dagger S_omega||
       <=Gamma_alpha a_alpha^2.                       (11)

Schatten Hölder and ||sqrt(rho)||_2=1 prove the same bound as (10).
The two halves add to

    ||D_alpha(rho Oj)||_1
       <=sqrt(Gamma_alpha) a_alpha sqrt(k_(alpha,j)).  (12)

This reconstruction was independently suggested and checked by
rounding_prior. It improves our initial looser bound that charged both
weighted commutator orientations. The decisive hypothesis remains the
FULL-H frequency-resolved k, not a bare-coupling commutator.

## Coherent drift and contraction in time

Since [K,rho]=0,

    ||-i[K,rho Oj]||_1 <= c_j^K.                       (13)

Pinching gives ||D_H||_(1->1)<=2sum Gamma_alpha a_alpha^2 for all
complex inputs as well as Hermitian inputs: the completely positive
gain map has norm bounded by its adjoint at I, and its anticommutator
has the same bound. Thus ||G||_(1->1)<=Lambda.

Write tau=(I+sum_j b_j sigma_j)/2 and
F_b=I+sum_j b_j Oj, ||F_b||<=2. The physical encoded state is
bar_rho F_b, whereas rho F_b need not be Hermitian. Stationarity
G rho=0, (12),(13), and the full complex trace-norm bound imply

    ||G Enc(tau)||_1
      <= sum_j |b_j| [c_j^K+sum_alpha sqrt(Gamma_alpha)a_alpha sqrt(k_(alpha,j))]
         +2Lambda delta
      <= E.                                           (14)

Enc(tau) is Hermitian. Its derivative under the CPTP semigroup is
Hermitian too, so trace-norm contraction gives

    ||exp(tG)Enc(tau)-Enc(tau)||_1
      <=integral_0^t ||exp(sG)GEnc(tau)||_1 ds<=t E.    (15)

There is no unsupported Jensen bound for a coherent generator and no
replacement of a finite-temperature trace by a ground-band norm.

## Actual-memory debts and a usable restricted tail estimate

For H=H0+V and the original decoder algebra, [H0,Oj]=0. If every
local term of V is protected on an explicitly enlarged good syndrome
projector P, then [V,Oj]P=0. Its skew-Hermitian commutator also vanishes
on P from the left, so

    (c_j^H)^2<=4||V||^2 Tr rho(H)(1-P).                (16)

Equation (16) uses the NEW Gibbs state. The actual source proves no
bound on that trace for a generic noncommuting V. More importantly,
(16) does not bound k_(alpha,j): full spectral components can resolve
arbitrarily small splittings. SECULAR_LIMIT_OBSTRUCTION.md shows why
generator continuity cannot be used to supply that missing estimate.

There is also a direct exact counterexample to bounding k by small
thermal coherent drift plus a protected bare coupling. On C^2 take
H_delta=delta Z, O=S=X, P=I, and the bounded smooth KMS rates
exp(-omega^2)/(1+exp(-beta omega)). The bare commutator is identically
zero and c^H=2|delta| tends to zero. Nevertheless, for every delta>0,
S_(2delta)=sigma_-, S_(-2delta)=sigma_+, and
[O,S_(2delta)]=Z, [O,S_(-2delta)]=-Z. Hence

    k_delta=gamma(2delta)+gamma(-2delta)=exp(-4delta^2)
              ->1,  whereas k_0=0.                  (16a)

This rejects a universal estimate k<=f(c^H,||[O,S]||_(rho,2)) with
f(c,0)->0. The Pauli algebra is exact on the full space. It is a
finite-system interface obstruction, not a logical-error realization
in the actual recursive code. The actual code still needs its own
full-frequency estimate; the single-site fixture cannot classify it.

For the restricted full isospectral orbit, pull back to H0. If
[Oj,H0]=0, fixed-H pinching does give

    k_(alpha,j)<=Gamma_alpha ||[Oj,A_alpha]||_(rho0,2)^2. (17)

Suppose A has a truncation A^(R) supported on at most c R^3 actual
qubits, with norm tail f(R), and an independently proved prefix-decoder
bound p_bad,q<=poly(N)exp(b beta q)p_bad,1. Then

    sqrt(k)<=2sqrt(Gamma)
       [||A^(R)||sqrt(p_bad,cR^3)+f(R)].               (18)

With p_bad,1<=poly(N)exp(-a M), M=2^k, choose R^3<=aM/(2b beta c).
For a stretched-exponential tail f(R)<=Cexp(-mu R^s), s>0, (18)
gives a lifetime at least exp(Omega(M^min(1,s/3))) up to polynomial
prefactors, provided all norm/support constants are uniform. This is
a concrete conditional extension of the exact full-orbit route;
it is not an inference about a ground-only spectral flow. Merely
superpolynomial tail bounds need not supply a power-exponential lifetime.

The next falsifiable generic-H lemma is an actual full-frequency
thermal logical-leakage bound, together with the new Gibbs witness
bound and a globally defined Pauli algebra. A low-energy gap or
finite-radius local filling does not replace any of these. Thermal
preparation and uniform microscopic weak-coupling accuracy remain
independent costs. The Gibbs interpolation and Chesi approximate-
stationarity method are prior tools; historical novelty is not claimed.
