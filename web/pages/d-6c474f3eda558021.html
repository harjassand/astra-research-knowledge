1. DEFINITIONS AND EXACT CONDITIONAL THEOREM

All Hilbert spaces are separable. A state is a positive trace-class
operator of trace one. T(rho,tau)=||rho-tau||_1/2. A normal channel is a
CP TP bounded linear map on trace-class operators, equivalently a
normal unital CP adjoint on bounded operators. The state separable set is
 Sep(R:B)=closure_(trace norm) conv{alpha_R tensor beta_B},       (1)
where alpha_R,beta_B are NORMAL density operators. A normal channel is
EB if (id_R tensor Lambda)(xi) belongs to (1) for every separable
reference R and every normal joint state xi. No countable measure-prepare
decomposition of a limiting channel or state is assumed or asserted.

Use normal Umegaki mutual information
 I(R:B)_rho=D(rho_RB || rho_R tensor rho_B),
with extended value +infinity. Data processing under normal channels is
used in its relative-entropy form, which does not subtract infinite
marginal entropies. Write
 g(I,b)=min{1, 6 sqrt[(I+1)/ln(1/b)]} for 0<b<1,
 g(I,0)=0, g(I,1)=1,                                          (2)
when I<infinity. It is increasing in I and b and continuous in b on
[0,1] for each finite I.

2. SUPPORT REDUCTION WITHOUT LOSING THE FULL-DOMAIN OBLIGATION

Let S=supp(sigma). Every extension with B marginal sigma is supported in
A tensor S: its mass outside S is zero, and positivity kills cross terms.
The normal CP TP map
 R_S(X)=SXS+Tr[(I-S)X]sigma                                    (5)
has output in S and acts identically on the entire S input algebra.
Restrict the input of the actual broadcaster to S and postprocess its
two outputs with R_S. This is one normal broadcaster mathcal B^S on S.
For all target extensions of sigma, each joint marginal error of this
modified broadcaster is at most the original error, by trace contraction.

It therefore suffices to construct Lambda_S on S, where sigma is faithful.
Extend it to the original FULL B by
 Lambda(X)=Lambda_S(SXS)+Tr[(I-S)X]sigma.                        (6)
The map pinches cross-support coherences. It is normal, CP, TP and EB,
fixes sigma, and has exactly the desired action on every target extension.
EB in (6) follows from the EB image of the positive S-compression and
the product replacement image of the outside compression, using (1).
This step handles a possible kernel of sigma and unobserved input states
outside its support. We now assume S=B and sigma faithful.

3. SIGMA-EIGENBASIS COLLAPSE AND LIFT

If sigma has finite rank, Section 4 extends (3) to infinite references
directly, then Section 2 gives the full-domain map. Assume infinite rank.
Choose an eigenbasis sigma=sum_i p_i|i><i| with every p_i>0. Put
 P_n=sum_(i<=n)|i><i|, Q_n=I-P_n,
 delta_n=Tr(Q_n sigma)>0, tau_n=Q_n sigma Q_n/delta_n.
Then delta_n->0 with no entropy or energy assumption. Let B_n be the
n-dimensional head plus one orthogonal tail flag |*>. Define
 C_n(X)=P_n X P_n direct_sum Tr(Q_n X)|*><*|,
 J_n(Y)=P_n Y P_n+<*|Y|*>tau_n,                                (7)
with the natural head identifications. Both maps are normal CPTP on
their full domains and pinch the corresponding coherences. They obey
 sigma_n=C_n(sigma)=P_n sigma P_n direct_sum delta_n|*><*|,
 J_n(sigma_n)=sigma.                                          (8)
The finite sigma_n is faithful. Write K_n=J_n C_n.

For EVERY reference A and EVERY extension rho_AB of sigma,
 T((id tensor K_n)rho,rho)<=eta_n,
 eta_n=sqrt[delta_n(1-delta_n)]+delta_n <=2sqrt(delta_n).        (9)
To prove it, split rho into head, tail, and their off-diagonal blocks.
If X=(I tensor P_n)rho(I tensor Q_n), Schatten Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 ||X||_1<=sqrt[Tr(P_n rho_B)Tr(Q_n rho_B)]
         =sqrt[delta_n(1-delta_n)].
Deleting X+X^dagger costs at most ||X||_1 in half trace norm. The old
positive tail block and its replacement rho_A^tail tensor tau_n both
have trace delta_n, so replacing it costs at most delta_n. This proves
(9), uniformly over all references and all extensions of sigma.

6. COMPACTNESS OF BOTH-MARGINAL-FIXED WEIGHTED CHOI STATES

Let R be the conjugate eigenbasis copy of B and set
 |sqrt(sigma)>=sum_i sqrt(p_i)|i>_R|i>_B,
 theta=|sqrt(sigma)><sqrt(sigma)|,
 q_n=(id_R tensor L_n)(theta).                                (15)
These are normal density operators with fixed marginals sigma^T on R
and sigma on B. The family of ALL normal states with these marginals
is trace-norm compact. Indeed for T_m=P_m^R tensor P_m^B,
 Tr[(I-T_m)q_n]<=2delta_m,
 ||q_n-T_m q_n T_m||_1<=2sqrt(2delta_m).                        (16)
The second bound is the unnormalized projection gentle bound, obtainable
by purifying q_n and contracting trace distance after projection. The
compressions act on a finite-dimensional space and are positive with
trace at most one, hence form a relatively compact set. Uniform (16)
makes the whole family totally bounded in trace norm. Its positivity,
trace and both marginal constraints are closed in that norm, so it is
compact. No Hamiltonian or finite entropy was used.

Select ONE subsequence q_nk->q in trace norm. The limiting q is normal,
positive, trace one, and has the same two marginals. Each q_n is
separable under (1), so q is too. This latter fact alone is not treated
as an unproved infinite-dimensional Choi-to-channel theorem; the full
normal limit map and EB property are constructed next.

7. RECONSTRUCTION OF A FULL NORMAL CP TP LIMIT CHANNEL

Write q_ij=(<i| tensor I)q(|j> tensor I), a trace-class output block.
Define on finitely supported input matrix units
 Lambda(E_ij)=q_ij/sqrt(p_i p_j).                              (17)
Faithfulness makes every fixed denominator positive. For input supported
on P_m, its Choi operator is
 sum_(i,j<=m)|i><j| tensor Lambda(E_ij)
   =(D_m tensor I)(P_m^R tensor I)q(P_m^R tensor I)(D_m tensor I),
 D_m=sum_(i<=m)p_i^(-1/2)|i><i|.                               (18)
This finite-input operator is positive and its output partial trace is
I_m. Thus (17) is a CP TP map on each finite input algebra, with normal
trace-class output. If desired, a direct Kraus representation follows
by spectrally decomposing the positive trace-class operator (18) and
reshaping each eigenvector into an operator from C^m to B. The resulting
Kraus Gram sum is I_m. These maps agree on nested input algebras.

Every such finite-input channel is trace-norm contractive: its adjoint
is unital CP and hence operator-norm contractive. The finite-support
operators are dense in T_1(B), since P_m X P_m->X in trace norm. Therefore
(17) extends uniquely to a bounded contraction Lambda:T_1(B)->T_1(B).
Positivity, trace preservation, and CP at every finite matrix level pass
by continuity. Its adjoint is automatically NORMAL because Lambda is a
map on the predual trace class. This supplies a full-domain normal CPTP
channel, rather than a rule only for sigma-dominated inputs.

No unbounded global conjugation by sigma^(-1/2) is applied. Only the
finite matrices D_m enter (18), and trace-norm contractivity controls
their resulting maps uniformly. This removes the apparent inverse-
sigma obstruction. Moreover
 Lambda(sigma)=sum_i p_i Lambda(E_ii)=sum_i q_ii=sigma           (19)
in trace norm. Its actual weighted Choi state is q, by applying (17) to
finite truncations of theta and taking the trace-norm limit.

For each i,j, convergence q_nk->q implies
 L_nk(E_ij)->Lambda(E_ij) in trace norm.                        (20)
Together with contraction and finite-support density, this gives strong
trace-norm convergence on the full input trace class.

8. FULL EB PROPERTY, WITHOUT AN ASSUMED INFINITE EB CLOSURE THEOREM

Let xi_AB be ANY normal state with any B marginal, not necessarily sigma.

 (id_A tensor L_nk)(xi)->(id_A tensor Lambda)(xi) in trace norm. (21)
This holds for infinite A as well. Each approximating output belongs to
Sep(A:B), since L_nk is explicitly EB. Definition (1) is closed in this
norm, so the limit belongs to it. Thus Lambda is a FULL EB channel.
There is no premise that a limit state has a countable product-state
