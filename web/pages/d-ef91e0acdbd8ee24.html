1. Three metrics must remain distinct

KMS: <A,C>_sigma=Tr sigma^(1/2)A^*sigma^(1/2)C.
GNS: <A,C>_GNS=Tr sigma A^*C.
Hermitian physical/Jordan second moment: Q_sigma(A)=Tr sigma A^2.
KMS is real and symmetric on Hermitian matrices. The complex GNS pairing
on two Hermitians need not be real; its real part is the Jordan form.
For diagonal sigma and an off-diagonal Pauli X, Q_sigma(X)=1 whereas
||X||_KMS^2=2sqrt(sigma_1 sigma_2). Their ratio has no uniform bound over
faithful references. The tree proof does NOT replace one by the other.

The weighted support interface is
 V_KMS=sum_i ||A_i||_KMS^2,
 k_sigma=sup_{one pure ensemble bary sigma} sum_z p_z sum_i Tr(pi_z A_i)^2.
The theorem proves V_KMS-k_sigma<=4(V_KMS-s_H) via one actual canonical
map, not by declaring V_KMS-k_sigma an ordinary variance convex roof.
The ordinary common variance roof equals V_Jordan-k_sigma instead.
Indeed sum_z p_z Tr(pi_z A_i^2)=Tr sigma A_i^2. Thus the earlier tracial
rank-two variance identity cannot be relabeled as a weighted KMS C2
theorem without a further argument; its physical second moments differ.
This distinction changes neither the valid weighted tree proof nor the
proved universal KMS comparison.

2. Exact legal fast nontracial control, beyond a slow-mode test

Let sigma=diag(4/5,1/5) and pi_+/- be the projectors onto
 v_+=(2,1)/sqrt(5), v_-=(2,-1)/sqrt(5).
Their equally weighted barycenter is sigma. Their Petz effects are
 M_+/-=(I+/-X)/2. Define the supplied broadcaster
 B(A)=sum_{epsilon=+,-} pi_epsilon tensor pi_epsilon Tr(M_epsilon A).
This is one full-domain CPTP channel; positivity is explicit from its
rank-one Kraus construction and its effects sum to I. Both output channel
marginals are the same canonical Phi. Phi(sigma)=sigma but
Phi(I)=2sigma, so Schrodinger unitality must not be inserted.

In the KMS-orthonormal basis
 I, E_X=sqrt(5)X/2, E_Y=sqrt(5)Y/2, E_Z=diag(1/2,-2),
H=Phi* has matrix diag(1,4/5,0,0). Thus E_X is genuinely fast:
2(4/5)^2-1=7/25>0. Its physical leaf variance is 5/4. The sibling KMS
correlation matrix at B(sigma) is also diag(1,4/5,0,0), and every channel
and broadcaster requirement is actual, not mere single-state marginals.

Complex GNS detailed balance fails in this SAME legal model:
 <X,H(Y)>_GNS=0, but <H(X),Y>_GNS=12i/25.
This is an exact counterexample to identifying KMS self-adjointness with
GNS self-adjointness, not a counterexample to the weighted theorem.

3. Exact depth-one POVM, posterior and Gram normalization

Measure X on both outputs and use the rescaled fast estimator. The four
pulled effects are
 M_(++)=(81/100)M_+ +(1/100)M_-,
 M_(--)=(1/100)M_+ +(81/100)M_-,
 M_(+-)=M_(-+)=(9/100)I.
Their sigma-probabilities are 41/100,41/100,9/100,9/100. The estimator
values are +5sqrt(5)/8,-5sqrt(5)/8,0,0, respectively. Its exact input
first moment is E_X. The nonzero posterior off-diagonal entries are
 +/-16/41; all posteriors have diagonal (4/5,1/5), so their weighted mean
is exactly sigma.

The exact classical estimator variance and upper bound are
 N_1=(5/4+4/5)/(2*(4/5)^2)=205/128,
 R_1=(5/4+1)/(2*(4/5)^2)=225/128.
The full centered posterior Gram matrix is diag(128/205,0,0), and the
full cross moment is (1,0,0). In particular the Schur bound is exact here:
C_X N_1=1, and C_X>=R_1^(-1). Incorrectly setting the physical seed
variance to one would predict a different N_1, exposing that proof error.

4. Quantifiers and the remaining outside-scope transfers

The theorem uses one sigma fixed in advance and one comparator depending
on the supplied sigma and channel. It does not change the comparator
when the observable, frame, or input state changes. Dependence on the
fixed reference sigma itself is part of the requested Petz form.
The channel need not be Schrodinger-unital or modular-covariant. It must
be full-domain CPTP, stationary, KMS reversible and supplied compatible.
Without reversibility H^2 need not be self-adjoint, so the same spectral
target is not even the assigned ordered object. No such extension is
asserted. Faithfulness is used in the inverse sandwiches; support-boundary
or approximate-reference variants need separate precise treatment.

Fixed-instance physical variances and inverse sigma operations may be
costly, and fast modes can approach the threshold. Those are construction
costs, not factors in the comparison constant. The finite compact limiting
ensemble is an existence result, not an efficient acquisition theorem.
The weighted C4 dependency is closed internally; any downstream nonlinear
local-reference logarithmic conclusion still uses its separately verified
state/function reduction. No identification of KMS with another entropy
Hessian, no external priority claim, and no formal-kernel validation is
supplied by this packet.
