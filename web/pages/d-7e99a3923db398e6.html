# Cycle 3 complement-LCU audit interface

## Target and exact encoding

Let the full augmented BMVZ output, including any dummy projectors used by a
specific route, be a normalized positive sum of projections

\[
H=\sum_{j=1}^M \pi_j h_j,\qquad 0\le h_j\le I,\quad h_j^2=h_j,
\quad \pi_j>0,\quad \sum_j\pi_j=1.
\]

An optional zero projector may be added with positive weight if the supplied
weights do not sum to one. For an iterated BMVZ term, flatten its clause to
\(R\) local factors and write

\[
h_j=I-Q_j,\qquad Q_j=\bigotimes_{r=1}^R Q_{j,r},\qquad
Q_{j,r}=I-P_{j,r},
\]

where each \(P_{j,r}\) is a fixed-arity projector (arity \(k=5\) for the
BJSW family). Different clause labels can have different local supports
inside a copy. Identity/spectator coordinates are included in \(Q_{j,r}\).
Define the term reflection

\[
R_j=I-2h_j=2Q_j-I,
\]

and add an identity label \(j=0\) with \(R_0=I\). Prepare a label unitary
\(A\) with first column

\[
A|0\rangle=|\chi\rangle,
\qquad \chi_0=1/\sqrt2,\quad
\chi_j=\sqrt{\pi_j/2}\ (j\ge1).
\]

Let

\[
\mathrm{SELECT}=\sum_{j=0}^M |j\rangle\langle j|\otimes R_j,
\qquad U=(A^*\otimes I)\,\mathrm{SELECT}\,(A\otimes I).
\]

Then \(\langle0|U|0\rangle=I-H\). Define the active isometry

\[
V|\psi\rangle=U(|0\rangle\otimes|\psi\rangle),\qquad
B=(I-|0\rangle\langle0|)_{\rm label}\otimes I_{\rm data}.
\]

It is an exact isometry, \(B\) is a diagonal projection with one nonzero
per row, and

\[
V^*BV=I-(I-H)^2=2H-H^2,
\qquad H\le V^*BV\le2H.
\]

The canonical enforcer is \(C_0=UBU^*=I-VV^*\): an exact projection with
gap one. It is polynomial-circuit and has polynomial-time specified-entry
evaluation. These facts alone do not establish polynomial row enumeration.

## Physical-basis tensor-entry formula for the canonical projector

For output label \(a\), set

\[
K_a:=\langle a|U|0\rangle
   =\sum_{j=0}^M \overline{A_{j a}}\,\chi_j R_j.
\]

In the computational basis \(|a,x\rangle\) of label plus data,

\[
(VV^*)_{(a,x),(b,y)}=(K_aK_b^*)_{x,y}
 =\sum_{j,k=0}^M \overline{A_{j a}}\chi_j A_{k b}\overline{\chi_k}
       (R_jR_k^*)_{x,y}.
\]

Therefore

\[
(C_0)_{(a,x),(b,y)}=\mathbf1[a=b]\mathbf1[x=y]
 -\sum_{j,k=0}^M \overline{A_{j a}}\chi_j A_{k b}\overline{\chi_k}
       (R_jR_k^*)_{x,y}.
\tag{1}
\]

The order in \(R_jR_k^*\) is important; distinct clauses need not commute.
For \(j,k\ge1\), the exact identity is

\[
R_jR_k^*=4Q_jQ_k-2Q_j-2Q_k+I.
\tag{2}
\]

Because the repetition copies are disjoint,

\[
(Q_jQ_k)_{x,y}=\prod_{r=1}^R (Q_{j,r}Q_{k,r})_{x_r,y_r},\quad
(Q_j)_{x,y}=\prod_{r=1}^R (Q_{j,r})_{x_r,y_r},\quad
(Q_k)_{x,y}=\prod_{r=1}^R (Q_{k,r})_{x_r,y_r}.
\tag{3}
\]

For each copy, \((Q_{j,r}Q_{k,r})_{x_r,y_r}\) is evaluated on the union
of at most \(2k\) local supports, so it uses at most \(2^{2k}\) matrix
coordinates and constant-size exact algebra for fixed \(k\). Equations
(1)–(3) give a polynomial-time specified-entry formula: sum over
\((M+1)^2\) term pairs, with \(O(R)\) local factors per pair, plus
polynomial-bit label arithmetic. For fixed \(k=5\), the crude per-pair
row-support upper bound is \(2^{10R}\), before cancellations. Summing
over the \(M+1\) output labels and term pairs gives a safe overall bound
\(O(M^3 2^{10R})\) on a row. This is only an upper bound. It does not
prove that any row has exponentially many nonzeros, since exact cancellations
between term pairs and label amplitudes may occur.

To certify a sparse-row claim, one must either (i) list every distinct
\((b,y)\) with a nonzero value after combining all terms in (1), with a
polynomial total list length and a polynomial-time row algorithm, or (ii)
prove a lower bound on the number of nonzero combined values in a selected
row. A fast evaluation of (1) at a specified pair \((a,x),(b,y)\) is not
either certificate.

## Costs and legal family

For the BMVZ augmentation, the two five-qubit singleton seed colors have
weights \(1/4\) each and the original hard list is scaled by \(1/2\). Their
small-layer rule forces the repeated clauses into the actual amplified list;
no expander self-loop is assumed. Each clause reflection \(R_j\) is
implemented by decoding its \(R\) constant-size local factors, computing
the zero-syndrome AND, applying a phase, and uncomputing; gate count and
workspace are polynomial in \(R\) and the term-generator description.
There are \(M=\operatorname{poly}(n)\) labels. Exact preparation uses
algebraic amplitudes \(\sqrt{\pi_j/2}\) and an exact polynomial-size label
unitary in the algebraic-gate model; finite-gate-set synthesis is approximate
and must be charged separately. \(B\) acts only on \(O(\log M)\) label
qubits. \(C_0\)'s exact row and entry costs are the open gates of this cycle.

This is a hardness-preserving augmented legal BMVZ family. It does not
assert membership in any specific canonical reduction image beyond the
explicit term generator and singleton augmentation described above.

## Validation status

The displayed identities follow algebraically from the LCU construction.
The separate exact seed checker verifies the five-qubit BJSW relative
decoder support, not the combined full-row cancellation problem in (1).
All cycle-3 claims must state their exact isometry and legal family; do not
transfer the active-flag or history-image row-mass theorem to this LCU image
without a fresh row-mass derivation.
