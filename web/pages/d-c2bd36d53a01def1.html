NATIVE SOL C1 INTERFACE AND COMPLETE PROOF

Use global orthonormal fields E0,E+,E- with
 [E0,E+]=-aE+, [E0,E-]=aE-, [E+,E-]=0,
 a=log((3+sqrt(5))/2), and Y=sum_i y_i E_i. Let
  (Dy)_(i,j)=E_j(y_i),
  G_Y=Dy-ad_y.
For an advected vector V=sum_i v_i E_i, [Y,V]=0 expands to
  Y(v)=Dy v-ad_y v=G_Y v.                                  (14)
Thus along EVERY trajectory the derivative cocycle has this actual
coordinate generator. The frame is orthonormal at every point, so component
norms are geometric norms. For the reference Y=E0, G_Y=D.

Reorder components as expanding u along E+ and z in span(E0,E-). Then
  u'=a u+(E v)_u,
  z'=D_cs z+(E v)_cs,
with ||E||_op<=delta and D_cs=diag(0,-a). For any alpha>0 consider
  F=||z||^2-alpha^2 u^2.
At a cone boundary ||z||=alpha|u|,
  F'/2 <=-alpha^2 a u^2+<-alpha^2 u,z> . E(u,z)
         <=[-alpha^2 a+delta alpha(1+alpha^2)]u^2.
The second line uses the EXACT boundary norm product
  ||(-alpha^2 u,z)|| ||(u,z)||=alpha(1+alpha^2)u^2.
The cone is invariant whenever
  delta(1+alpha^2)<=a alpha.                               (15)
Inside it,
  d/dt log|u|>=a-delta sqrt(1+alpha^2).                     (16)
Choose alpha=1. If delta<=a/2, invariance holds and the rate is at least
a-sqrt(2)delta>0. Starting on E+ at any x bounds the largest derivative
Lyapunov exponent below by this rate. Because Y has a bounded nonzero speed
on compact M, its flow direction has exponent zero. Exact volume
preservation makes the full spectrum chi,0,-chi. The imported smooth
volume Pesin formula now gives (R1). No assertion that the eigenvectors of
the instantaneous strain persist is needed.

In this global orthonormal frame, the Lie-derivative metric formula gives
  S_Y=sym G_Y.
Indeed [Y,E_j]=-(G_Y)_j and Y< E_i,E_j >=0, so
(L_Y g)(E_i,E_j)=G_(i,j)+G_(j,i). Thus
  ||S_Y-S_E0||_HS<=delta_F, ||S_E0||_HS=sqrt(2)a.
Normalized L2 Minkowski and W=2nuV E|S_Y|^2 prove (R2); combining with
(R1) and the baseline sharp floor proves (R3).

For a primitive velocity coefficient error with
  sup|y-(1,0,0)|<=rho<1 and sup||Dy||_HS<=d,
speed is at least 1-rho, and the Sol bracket table gives
  ||ad_(y-y0)||_HS^2=a^2[2(y0-1)^2+y+^2+y-^2].
Consequently BOTH generator norm errors are at most
  delta,delta_F<=d+sqrt(2)a rho.                           (17)
This is a genuine uniform C1 input. Unknown second derivatives do not alter
the perturbation theorem, although acquiring a global C1 bound by finite
point sampling needs a supplied modulus for the derivative or an interval
finite representation. C1 measurement uncertainty is charged by adding
its generator error to delta; exact incompressibility remains a premise.

speed>=1, and
  G_Y=[[0,0,0],[g(t),a,0],[0,0,-a]],

FINITE-STEP NORMAL-COCYCLE CERTIFICATE

For a supplied smooth nowhere-zero incompressible flow on compact M, let
A_T(x) be the exact two-dimensional normal quotient map for a fixed T>0,
represented in supplied orthonormal input/output cone frames. Normalize it
by sqrt(r(phi_T x)/r(x)) if desired; that scalar telescopes and does not
change Lyapunov exponents. Cone frames must agree as cone fields on atlas
overlaps; this condition is an explicit admission check, not automatic.

Suppose acquired rational interval data give, for every x,
  |A11|>=a0>0, |A12|<=b0, |A21|<=c0, |A22|<=d0.
The A11 interval must exclude zero; its orientation sign may vary between
charts because the proof uses absolute values. For alpha>0, check
  c0+d0 alpha<=alpha(a0-b0 alpha),
  m<=a0-b0 alpha, m>1.                                    (20)
Then |v2|<=alpha|v1| maps into the same output cone, and
  |v1'|>=m|v1|.
Iteration gives top normal exponent>=log(m)/T, up to a bounded initial
norm factor. The neutral line and volume preservation give the same full
positive exponent. Imported Pesin therefore certifies
  H>=log(m)/T, W>=4nuV[log(m)/T]^2.                       (21)
Entry noise and cell interpolation errors are simply added to b0,c0,d0
and subtracted from a0. A supplied h-net and uniform matrix modulus L_A
add L_A h to every entry bound. If a whole A11 interval crosses zero or
(20) fails, return UNKNOWN. This cannot infer zero entropy from a failure.

For the reference Sol map A1=diag(lambda,lambda^-1), coefficientwise
error epsilon and alpha=1 satisfy (20) when
  4epsilon<=lambda-lambda^-1=sqrt(5),
  m<=lambda-2epsilon, m>1.                                (22)
In particular epsilon=1/2 gives m=lambda-1=(1+sqrt(5))/2 and
  H>=log((1+sqrt(5))/2)=a/2.
This is a robust normal-map error interface, not yet a velocity error
unless the derivative simulation and moving-normal projections are charged.

GENERAL SHORT-TIME DERIVATIVE SIMULATION COST, DERIVATION

Admit an explicit coordinate chart/tube with exact evaluators and bounds
  |Y|<=B0, ||DY||<=L, ||G||<=B, Lip(G)<=K.
The tube and every chart transition are part of the input. Bounds are valid
for true and approximated trajectories. Exact forward Euler with step h,
piecewise-linear interpolation, has trajectory residual <=LB0 h; hence
  |xhat(t)-x(t)|<=B0 h(exp(Lt)-1).                         (23)
The L=0 case gives zero error for constant coordinate velocity.
The derivative approximation uses
  Mhat_(j+1)=(I+hG(xhat_j))Mhat_j, Mhat_0=I.
At intermediate times ||Mhat(t)||<=exp(Bt). Its residual relative to
G(xhat(t)) is at most h(KB0+B^2)exp(Bt). Variation of constants for
E=M-Mhat therefore gives
  ||E(T)||<=exp(BT)[K integral_0^T |x-xhat|dt
                                  +hT(KB0+B^2)]
           <=h exp(BT)[KB0 (exp(LT)-1)/L+T B^2],           (24)
where (exp(LT)-1)/L is interpreted as T when L=0.
Thus, to obtain an admitted derivative error zeta>0, choose
  h<=zeta/{exp(BT)[KB0(exp(LT)-1)/L+T B^2]}.
The operation count is O(T/h) fixed-dimensional evaluations/matrix steps.
This naive bound is EXPONENTIAL in numerical (B+L)T, polynomial in
inverse zeta, and not a polynomial-bit complexity theorem for arbitrary
long-time entropy. A fixed short T with a positive cone margin avoids
waiting for asymptotic Lyapunov convergence.

With approximate primitive evaluators and rational rounding, use their
actual trajectory residual tau(t), matrix residual rho(t), an admitted
bound Mbar(t), and initial errors eta_x,eta_M. The sound error budget is
  e_x(t)<=eta_x exp(Lt)+integral_0^t exp(L(t-s))tau(s)ds,
  e_M(T)<=eta_M exp(BT)+integral_0^T exp(B(T-t))
                       [K e_x(t)Mbar(t)+rho(t)]dt.         (25)
No rounding or state-evaluation error is silently discarded. Sufficient
precision generally includes O((B+L)T+log(1/zeta)) bits plus input heights
and known conditioning factors. Normal projection depends on the speed
floor: a native vector error eta_X with both speeds>=r0 yields
||P-Phat||<=4eta_X/r0. Frame construction adds inverse-angle factors;
speed normalization adds its own r0-dependent error. These costs must be
included before admitting A_T enclosures to (20).

The generic chart pipeline in (23)--(25) has not been executed. It is a
complete costed sufficient simulation interface under supplied bounds.
It does not acquire those bounds or recognize incompressibility globally.

ACQUISITION FAILURE THAT THE CERTIFICATES RETAIN

Finite point samples without a supplied modulus cannot certify a uniform
C1 generator bound. Choose a volume chart disjoint from the sampled points,
with density m(x)>0. A compactly supported field
  B=m(x)^-1 curl A
is exactly divergence-free. Smooth high-frequency compact potentials A can
make |B| small while derivatives of B are arbitrarily large. All sampled
jets remain unchanged since the support avoids them. This refutes global
derivative/norm bounds inferred solely from those finite measurements.
It is not a proof of an entropy indistinguishability theorem: the entropy
of the perturbed example is not asserted. An actual uniform modulus or a
finite certified smooth representation is necessary for the stated route.
