## 2. Cell smoothness and a positive localization barrier

For lambda in [7/10,4/5] and tau<=1/100, the critical values of P_lambda are +-1 and +-(2lambda-1). Thus the boundaries |P_lambda|=tau are uniformly smooth.     -(partial_lambda P_lambda)/|grad P_lambda|^2 grad P_lambda

## 4. First-order compensation is explicit

Let m_P(lambda)=M_(tau,sigma)(P_lambda;P_lambda), and m_Q(lambda)=M_(tau,sigma)(P_lambda;Q_lambda). The uniform cell estimates in the companion note give

    -21/20<=m_P<-4/5, m_Q<-1.                          (9)

Because P_lambda is even in both fast coordinates, the smoothed derivative responses are odd and have zero mean. Thus the exact leading cell mean of (8) is

    bar V_1(Y)=-2beta S m_P(lambda)+2lambda'(S)m_Q(lambda).

On |S+1|<=h, the explicit legal profile has lambda'=2beta S. Therefore

    bar V_1(Y)=2beta S[-m_P(lambda)+2m_Q(lambda)]
                >= beta.                              (10)

Indeed S<-0.9 and the bracket is <=21/20-2=-19/20. The positive margin holds uniformly throughout the working box and the same once-fixed cell/mollifier choices. It is produced by the actual partition derivative, not an added comparison potential.

If lambda is constant, that partition term is absent and the common-frequency mean is -2beta S m_P<0 on an inward box. Section 6 proves that such boxes occur in the SAME construction, not only in a different choice of mixture profile. Full repair localization uses (5), so this is a genuine two-wave member rather than a one-wave replacement.

## 5. An actual positive-form certificate, not just a positive average

For each macro Y solve the ordinary mean-zero periodic Poisson problem on T^2

    Delta_zeta G(Y,zeta)=V_1(Y,zeta)-bar V_1(Y).

The smoothed quotient in (8) is smooth and vanishes safely near the zeros of P_lambda. Uniform parameter/coordinate bounds give fixed finite bounds on G and its required first and second derivatives. The Poisson inverse is on the SAME fixed torus for every j; no small-frequency or quasiperiodic inverse is assumed.

Define the local corrector g_j(x)=eta_j G(Y,zeta). Cartesian chain differentiation gives

    |g_j|<=C eta_j,
    |grad g_j|^2<=C A_j^2 eta_j^2=C mu_j,
    Delta g_j=kappa_j(V_1-bar V_1)+O(mu_j).             (11)

The cross fast/slow term costs A/ell times eta=A^2 eta^2=mu. The purely slow term is smaller. Using the full actual (8), (10) and (11),

    V-Delta g_j-|grad g_j|^2
         >=kappa_j beta_j-C mu_j
         >=(1/2)kappa_j beta_j>0                       (12)

for all late selected j, since mu/(kappa beta)=eta/beta->0.

For any compact H1 test chi in the working box, exact integration by parts gives

    q_H(e^(g_j)chi)
      =integral e^(2g_j)[|grad chi|^2
                      +(V-Delta g_j-|grad g_j|^2)chi^2].

Multiplication by e^(g_j) bijects compact H1 test spaces. Therefore (12) proves (1) for every such test. This is a strict local scalar certificate for the full potential, including all repair and smoothing contributions. It proves no global positive solution and permits negative cells outside these boxes.

## What this settles and what remains

The negative common-frequency coefficient is robust under a general cell's harmonic repair. It does NOT imply that every fixed-cover macro region has negative spectrum: a legal two-wave radial weight transition supplies an independent first-order channel that can overcompensate the frequency mismatch. The construction above certifies the resulting local form as strictly positive, rather than inferring it from a mean alone.

All choices remain within the same real scalar Euclidean all-exponential family. This particular compensating construction has infinite negative index by Section 6. A universal finite-index exclusion for arbitrary fixed covers and a finite-index all-exponential example are both still absent.

The next falsifiable step is a fixed-cover radial balance theorem that retains the mixture functional J_tau, component-frequency offsets, the actual cell response and the O(mu) corrector ledger across successive overlapping clusters.
