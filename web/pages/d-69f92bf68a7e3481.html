1. THE KNOWN LADDER DOES NOT MAKE THE LOGARITHMIC THEOREM SHARP

The independently reconstructed exact family in
HARD_FAMILY_AND_CAPACITY_PROOF.txt has
 e->1, b2=Theta(1/L), C=Theta(2^L ln L/L), R=Theta(C).
It disproves a complexity-free stability theorem. It does NOT disprove
e^2<=K C b2^alpha for any fixed positive alpha: on this sequence
C b2^alpha diverges. It does NOT supply exp[-Theta(C/e^2)] small b
at bounded e: ln(1/b)=Theta(ln L), while C is exponential in L.
It also does not refute replacing C by ln(C+1) in a suitable universal
bound, since ln C=Theta(L) is still much larger than ln(1/b)=Theta(ln L).
Inferring any of those impossibility claims from N61 would be invalid.

2. BINARY INFORMATION EXTRACTION: WHERE ACCESSIBLE INFORMATION FAILS

Iterating an actual compatible broadcaster to a finite output tree yields
a quantum joint state. Its TOTAL quantum Holevo information is bounded
by the input Holevo capacity by data processing. The quantum chain rule
uses terms I(X:Q_j | Q_<j), with a QUANTUM prefix. A small such term can
support quantum recovery from the prefix, which is not an EB reconstruction
from a classical record.

Measuring the prefix produces a classical record Y. Its classical chain
rule is valid, but the new increment is accessible/measured information.
There is no general inference from a small measured increment to a small
quantum trace posterior discrepancy. One may not silently switch between
these two chain rules, or average over prior-dependent measurements and
then claim a common physical decoder. The following exact finite family
already blocks the trace conversion before any long chain is formed.

3. THE PAULI BENCHMARK: EXACT CAPACITY, EB DEFECT AND MEASURED INFORMATION

Let d=2^q, q>=1, let P range over the d^2-1 nonidentity Hermitian tensor
Paulis, and s in {+1,-1}. Fix 0<a<1 and use all faithful states

 rho_(P,s)=(I+s aP)/d.

The uniform prior has mean sigma=I/d. Every state has d/2 eigenvalues
(1+a)/d and d/2 eigenvalues (1-a)/d. Every prior has mean entropy at
most ln d, so the MAX-PRIOR Holevo capacity is exactly

 C=h(a)=[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2<=ln 2.          (1)

For the UNIFORM prior ONLY, every POVM has accessible information at most
a^2/(d+1). Here is a direct proof. For outcome y with t_y=Tr M_y>0,
put omega_y=M_y/t_y and r_(P,y)=Tr(P omega_y). Its conditional probability
is (t_y/d)(1+s a r_(P,y)), and its unconditional probability is t_y/d.
The classical inequality relative entropy<=chi-square divergence gives

 I(X:Y)<=a^2 sum_y (t_y/d) [sum_(P!=I) r_(P,y)^2]/(d^2-1).

Pauli orthogonality gives sum_(P!=I) r_(P,y)^2=d Tr(omega_y^2)-1<=d-1,
and sum_y t_y/d=1. Hence

 I_acc(uniform prior)<=a^2/(d+1)->0.                  (2)

Nevertheless T(rho_(P,s),sigma)=a/2 for every label. More strongly,
the ONE-channel optimal uniform EB reconstruction error is exactly

 e(E)=a d/[2(d+1)] ->a/2.                            (3)

For the lower bound let any EB map have measure-prepare form
Lambda(X)=sum_y Tr(M_y X) omega_y. Its HS superoperator trace is
 sum_y Tr(M_y omega_y)<=sum_y Tr M_y=d.
In normalized Pauli basis I/sqrt(d),P/sqrt(d), its identity-basis term
is Tr Lambda(I)/d=1 by TP. Thus
 sum_(P!=I) Tr[P Lambda(P)]/d<=d-1.
Testing rho-Lambda(rho) against sP/2 and averaging signs cancels the
possibly nonunital center offset. Averaging all P then gives
 worst error>= (a/2)[1-(d-1)/(d^2-1)]
             =a d/[2(d+1)].
The Haar rank-one measure-and-prepare map
 Lambda_0(X)=(X+Tr(X)I)/(d+1)
is EB and attains this value on every promised state. Its Haar integral
can be replaced by an exact finite projective 2-design. This proves (3)
for arbitrary mixed prepared outputs and arbitrary nonunital EB channels.

Equations (2)--(3) do NOT bound the MAX-PRIOR accessible capacity. For
example choose one P and a balanced prior on rho_(P,+),rho_(P,-), then
measure P. This prior has a positive information h(a) independent of d.
Confusing a locked balanced-prior ensemble with a max-over-priors resource
would change the quantifiers. The benchmark was already source-reported
in pinned N69; the exact derivation here makes that prior distinction explicit.

The benchmark is also NOT a small-broadcast-error obstruction. Choose
anticommuting P,Q and the two positive-sign states. They decompose into
repeated identical qubit pairs, so their root fidelity and affinity are

 f=sqrt(1-a^2/2), A=(1+sqrt(1-a^2))/2.
Their positive gap f-A depends only on a. The supplied frozen Cycle03
pair proof bounds this gap by v(b), where v(b)->0 as b->0, and therefore
this family has b2 bounded away from zero. It cannot be inserted as a
counterexample to the bounded-capacity broadcasting theorem. The ordinary
cloning/EB tradeoff is visible already in a fixed two-state subfamily.

4. LOCKING DOES NOT BY ITSELF CLOSE A LEGAL SMALL-b LOWER FAMILY

Primary Koenig--Renner--Bariska--Maurer quant-ph/0512021v2, PDF pp1--3,
directly exhibits low accessible information that can increase after
classical side information is supplied. It supports the conceptual failure
of treating accessible information as a composable quantum-trace metric.
Its task provides or later reveals side information. Our broadcasting
task supplies no unknown label/key at run time and requires one common
full-domain B and one common EB Lambda. No theorem from that paper gives
small uniform marginal-broadcast error for a locking family.

The paper's explicit tensor-Pauli parity family and the benchmark above
can hide information from one chosen prior. Neither removes the fixed
noncommuting-pair broadcasting obstruction. Accordingly no legal
information-locking counterexample with b2->0 and e bounded below was
acquired by this bounded source check. This is a surviving gap, not a
proof that such an example is impossible. No unrestricted strong modulus
is inferred from failure to find one.

5. RESTRICTIONS ON LADDER VARIANTS AND WHY THEY ARE INCOMPLETE ROUTES

Restricted pure-copy ladders with fixed d become classically reconstructible:
shift a vanishing fraction of top flags, use higher-copy inputs to estimate
the underlying direction, and prepare the retained lower sectors. The
pinned S report asserts a stronger restricted-capacity estimate and
contains audits; this worker does not import its quantitative rate as a
new proof. A lower bound from the bottom sector alone has weight 1/L and
cannot retain a fixed global EB defect.

Replacing a Haar orbit by finitely many locked pure states does not
automatically work. An EB decoder receives all higher-copy sectors, and
these may reveal the label. A single-copy accessible-information lower
bound does not control their estimation power. Pairwise-near-orthogonality
can make a finite label code EASY to distinguish; actual uniform EB error
must be proved against the full allowed input, not a chosen measurement.

Taking independent tensor products of whole ladders multiplies the available
flag shift cost together with reconstruction error; this alone gives no
improved small-b/large-e separation. Synchronizing one ladder flag across
many parameters preserves the displayed shift cost, but each sector then
contains a growing number of copies across parameters. A fixed defect can
require exponentially many parameters in L, so its capacity does not
automatically improve the known family. These were analytic warnings,
not exhaustive nonexistence results and not broad numerical scans.
