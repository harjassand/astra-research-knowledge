## G1. Algebraic interface and why the problem reduces to a trace epigraph

For every homogeneous quadratic PSD map Q:R^m→Sym_r the general determinant wrapper in R1 of `RATIONAL_LIFT_AND_TRANSPORT.md` is hyperbolic. Its closed cone has an affine slice/projection equal to

    E_Q={ (A,t,y): A≥0, range Q(y)⊆range A,
                     t≥tr(A-dagger Q(y)) }.

The pointwise block determinant and singular Schur-complement criterion verify this exact equality, with no closure operation. Hence any finite affine SDP lift of the cone would lift E_Q. It remains to construct a PSD Q whose trace epigraph violates the necessary local consequences of a lift.

## G4. Finite normalized fibers and inverse directions

Orthogonalize b to this translation range, then normalize ||b||²+||a||²=1 by positive dilation. Translation and dilation cannot be simultaneously invisible: otherwise the chart kernel consists only of pure quadratics, and the Hankel rank is at most9, contrary to20. This removes exactly8 dimensions from the193-dimensional projective stratum, giving a semialgebraic normalized space M of dimension185.

The projected-kernel map pi to the Grassmannian has derivative rank185 at the seed. A zero derivative is equivalent to vanishing on JH0, whose codimension is9. The image therefore has dimension185.

The locus of positive-dimensional normalized fibers and the image of the frontier of M both have dimension≤184. ## G5. The uniformity issue in passing from arbitrary sequences to exact rank

This is a load-bearing point which cannot be replaced by compactness of all Hankel matrices. In W⊕K blocks let Y_i=[[A_i,B_i],[B_i^T,D_i]], with A_i invertible and its inverses bounded, F_i=A_i^-1 B_i→0, and Schur residual D_i-B_i^T A_i^-1 B_i→0. Restriction of Hankel variations to K×K is onto by Condition1. Choose a fixed linear right inverse E of this restriction.

For the Schur map S, its derivative on a variation (a,b,d) is

    d - b^T F - F^T b + F^T a F.

It approaches the restriction map. On a fixed small perturbation ball, A^-1 and the perturbed F remain uniformly bounded using (I+A_i^-1 a)^-1. Differentiating these two quantities yields uniform second-derivative bounds; neither A_i nor B_i need be bounded. For f_i(z)=S(Y_i+E z), f_i(0)→0 and Df_i→I. The map z-f_i(z) contracts a ball of radius2||f_i(0)||, producing a Hankel correction Delta_i→0 with exact rank20, unchanged limiting inverse, and kernel→K. This explicitly handles divergent matrix entries.

The source's spectral inverse-limit lemma compresses an arbitrary relevant Hankel sequence to W, performs this correction, and normalizes its exact-rank classes by translation/dilation. No frontier at K implies a convergent subsequence to one of the finite fiber members. Translation parameters tend to0; dilation tends to infinity. The rescaled inverse extracts precisely the member's nonzero R-block C. Hence every nonzero projective inverse limit occurring in the separation argument is one of the finite C classes. This is substantially stronger than a finite count of solutions without control at infinity.

## G6. Separation while preserving Q(y)≥0 for every y

Let h=36 and fix N>10h². Choose a unit tuple w∈W^N spanning W and avoiding sum_i w_i^T C w_i=0 for the finite C classes. Choose an r=20N-1 dimensional U⊂w-perp so its projection to w-perp∩W^N is an isomorphism. It can also avoid every nonzero tuple satisfying prescribed projections onto orthogonal spaces F_infty,F_0 of combined dimension j>20 and a symmetric operator on F_0. The bad incidence family has at most4h² parameters, and the tuple prescription has codimension(j-20)N in the Grassmannian incidence estimate. Since N>10h², all such exceptional images have smaller dimension. This is an existence proof of U, not a sampled random U with a known bit bound or margin.

Suppose (I_N⊗Y_i)u_i→w. Normalize u_i to unit norm by scaling Y_i. Split spectral directions into divergent, finite nonzero, and zero eigenvalue limits. ## G8. Why every finite affine lift is contradicted

A positive definite functional through degree4 can be extended through any finite even degree: adding one homogeneous layer leaves its mixed moments fixed, and a sufficiently large Gaussian top block dominates the Schur complement. If E_Q had an exact finite affine SDP lift, semialgebraic stratification of its Gram-factor relation supplies analytic lift coordinates and factors on a nonempty open subpatch. Finite Taylor truncation and positivity of the extended functional make the tested pencil PSD up to O(epsilon^(b+1)). The common kernel of all feasible matrices is annihilated exactly; mixing with a fixed maximum-rank feasible matrix by epsilon^b repairs the error on its orthogonal complement. The projected point is feasible and O(epsilon^b) from the tested point. No strict feasibility or closure of projection is assumed.
