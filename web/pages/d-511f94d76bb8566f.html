1. The theorem and exact scope

Fix a compact connected simple Lie group with root system Phi. For its
simply connected form every dominant integral label is allowed; for a
central quotient restrict to labels whose central character is trivial on
the quotient kernel. Normalize the invariant weight inner product so long
roots have squared length 2. Let rank(G)=ell, p=|Phi+|,
g=dim Lie(G)=ell+2p, hvee be the dual Coxeter number, rho the Weyl vector,
and omega_1,...,omega_ell the fundamental weights. For a dominant integral
highest weight

    Lambda = sum_i n_i omega_i,   N = sum_i n_i >= 1,

let I={i:n_i>0} be its nonempty active support and
n_* = min_(i in I)n_i. Write V_Lambda for the carrier, d_Lambda for its
dimension, tau=Tr/d_Lambda, and

    C_Lambda = (||Lambda||^2 + 2(Lambda,rho))/2,

so that the Hermitian Lie-generator basis T_1,...,T_g, scaled by
1/sqrt(2) relative to a basis orthonormal for the basic metric, satisfies
sum_a T_a^2=C_Lambda I and tau(T_a T_b)=(C_Lambda/g)delta_ab. The
half-Casimir conventions are C_Lambda=(||Lambda||^2+2(Lambda,rho))/2
and c_W=(||nu||^2+2(nu,rho))/2. The basic metric remains the one with
long roots of squared length 2; its unit tangent acts on W with norm at
most sqrt(2c_W), as used in Section 4.

There is one canonical coherent entanglement-breaking channel

    Psi_Lambda(A) = d_Lambda integral_G Tr(P_g A) P_g dg,
    P_g = U_Lambda(g)|v_Lambda><v_Lambda|U_Lambda(g)^*,

where Haar measure is probability measure and v_Lambda is a unit highest
weight vector. It does not depend on the experiment, its residual, or any
score coefficient.

Let Phi be a bistochastic UCP Heisenberg marginal which is G-covariant and
has an ACTUAL two-output compatible extension with equal marginals: a UCP map
J:M_d tensor M_d -> M_d with J(A tensor I)=J(I tensor A)=Phi(A).
The extension itself need not be covariant. Let Phi^sharp be the normalized
trace Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint and set S=(Phi+Phi^sharp)/2. For every
nontrivial real irreducible Hermitian conjugation type W occurring in
End(V_Lambda), let r_W=dim_R W and c_W its Casimir in the normalization
above. On the FULL W-isotypic subspace E_W, including every multiplicity
copy in End(V_Lambda), one has

    ||(I-Psi_Lambda)A||_(2,tau)^2
       <= C_W(Lambda) <A,(I-S)A>_tau,                         (1)

    C_W(Lambda)
       = [72 p^2/(g gamma_(G,I)^2)]
         [C_Lambda/(n_*+1)^2] r_W c_W.                         (2)

Here gamma_(G,I)>0 is the fixed partial-flag constant defined in Section 2.
This carrier-specific form holds for every nonzero dominant Lambda,
including weights on walls. It is an own-target inequality: the right side
is the energy of the actual compatible marginal being tested. S itself is
not claimed to be compatible.

For a supplied face-regular cone, assume only
n_i>=eta N for i in I, with fixed 0<eta<=1/ell; coefficients outside I
are exactly zero. Then the SAME channel satisfies the uniform-in-carrier
bound with

    C_(G,eta)(k)
       = [144 p^2 M_G hvee (1+a_G)^p/
          (g gamma_G^2 eta^2)] k^(p+2),                        (3)

where gamma_G=min_(nonempty I)gamma_(G,I)>0. Thus the result includes
proper weight faces whenever their active labels grow proportionally to N.
If n_* stays bounded while N grows, the carrier-specific constant (2)
can grow as N^2/(n_*+1)^2; no dimension-free fixed-degree operator
comparison of the form (3) is claimed there. Section 9 gives a separate
all-wall conclusion only for the stated natural polynomial-Gibbs class.

For every Hermitian score in the image of the enveloping-algebra PBW
filtration of degree at most k, the SAME Psi_Lambda obeys

    ||(I-Psi_Lambda)A||_2^2
       <= C_(G,eta)(k) <A,(I-S)A>,

where the explicit root-data quantities are

    Omega_G = max_i ||omega_i||,
    M_G = Omega_G^2/2 + Omega_G ||rho||,
    a_G = max_(alpha in Phi+) sqrt(2)||alpha^vee||/(rho,alpha^vee).

The carrier factor used here follows directly from
||Lambda||<=sum_i n_i||omega_i||<=N Omega_G and
(Lambda,rho)<=N Omega_G||rho||, giving
C_Lambda<=M_G N^2 for N>=1.

One may replace the k^(p+2) expression in (3) by the slightly sharper
144 p^2 M_G/[g gamma_G^2 eta^2] times
(1+a_G k)^p k(k+hvee-1). The only constants not written as elementary
root-data expressions are the partial-flag gamma_(G,I) and, if a numerical
value is desired, its certified far-set lower bound. They depend only on
fixed G and active support I; taking the minimum over the finite set of
nonempty supports gives a fixed-G constant.
The theorem makes no claim uniform over root systems, for score degrees
growing without control, or from bounded Holevo information alone.

2. Partial-flag geometry and the fixed positive constant gamma_(G,I)

Work on the simply connected cover Gtilde with maximal torus Ttilde.
For the active set I choose one unit highest vector v_i in each
V_(omega_i), i in I, and set

    a_i(g)=<v_i,U_(omega_i)(g)v_i>,
    F_I(g)=sum_(i in I)(1-|a_i(g)|^2).

Let L_I be the connected Levi subgroup generated by T and the root
subgroups whose positive-coroot support avoids I. The common projective
stabilizer of the selected lines is exactly L_I, including finite
components: any remaining stabilizer normalizes L_I; after conjugating its
maximal torus to T, the Weyl element fixes each omega_i for i in I, hence
lies in the parabolic Weyl subgroup generated by the inactive simple
roots. Therefore

    G/L_I -> product_(i in I) P(V_(omega_i)),
    gL_I |-> ([U_(omega_i)(g)v_i])_(i in I)

is an embedding. The highest line of V_Lambda has the same stabilizer.
Thus F_I vanishes exactly on L_I. For a central quotient, the fundamental
representations may only be projective, but central elements act by
scalars, so these projective maps and moduli descend. The partial flag
G/L_I is naturally the corresponding quotient of Gtilde/Ltilde_I.

Use the basic metric with long roots squared length 2. The normal tangent
space to L_I consists of root planes whose coroot support meets I. If
alpha=sum_j q_j alpha_j, the quadratic coefficient of its real root plane
in F_I(exp(tX)) is

    (1/4) sum_(j in I) q_j ||alpha_j||^2.

Consequently the least normal Hessian coefficient is exactly

    c_(G,I) = (1/4) min_(i in I)||alpha_i||^2.                 (4)

For a unit normal X, the fundamental generator in V_(omega_i) has spectrum
in [-||omega_i||,||omega_i||]. Expressing the matrix coefficient as a
convex combination of phases gives
|(1-|a_i(exp(tX))|^2)'''|<=8||omega_i||^3. Set

    B_(G,I)=8 sum_(i in I)||omega_i||^3,
    r_(G,I)=3c_(G,I)/B_(G,I).

Taylor's theorem gives F_I(g)>=(c_(G,I)/2)d(g,L_I)^2 for
d(g,L_I)<=r_(G,I).

On the compact far set d(g,L_I)>=r_(G,I), let m_(G,I)=min F_I(g), with
m_(G,I)=+infinity if the set is empty. The embedding implies m_(G,I)>0
when the set is nonempty. Let Dbar_G be any computed upper bound on
diam(G), obtainable from a bounded fundamental parallelepiped of the
cocharacter lattice, and define

    gamma_(G,I)=min(c_(G,I)/2, m_(G,I)/Dbar_G^2)>0.            (5)

Then F_I(g)>=gamma_(G,I)d(g,L_I)^2 globally. For each fixed root system
and active set, a certified root-coordinate net and the Lipschitz bound
2 sum_(i in I)||omega_i|| produce a positive rational lower bound on the
far minimum. This is an effective fixed-(G,I) constant; the higher-rank
far-set values were not numerically evaluated. Taking

    gamma_G = min_(nonempty I) gamma_(G,I)>0                  (6)

is valid because there are finitely many supports. For the full support A1,
the exact global value is gamma=2/pi^2.

3. Kernel concentration from exact Weyl-dimension moments

The highest line is the Cartan product of n_i copies of each active
fundamental highest line. Thus

    d_Lambda |<v_Lambda,U_Lambda(g)v_Lambda>|^2
       = d_Lambda product_(i in I) |a_i(g)|^(2 n_i) =: k_Lambda(g).

It is a probability density on G by Schur orthogonality. For each i,
multiplying its matrix coefficient by |a_i|^2 gives the highest matrix
coefficient of the Cartan product V_(Lambda+omega_i). Another application
of Schur orthogonality gives the exact moment

    E_(k_Lambda)|a_i|^2 = d_Lambda/d_(Lambda+omega_i).          (7)

For a positive coroot alpha^vee=sum_j m_(alpha,j)alpha_j^vee, put
x_alpha=sum_j m_(alpha,j)(n_j+1). The Weyl dimension formula yields

    d_Lambda/d_(Lambda+omega_i)
      = product_(alpha>0) x_alpha/(x_alpha+m_(alpha,i)).

For alpha whose coroot support misses I, all active m_(alpha,i) vanish.
Otherwise x_alpha >= (n_*+1)sum_(i in I)m_(alpha,i). Using
1-product(1-u_alpha)<=sum u_alpha and summing only over i in I gives

    sum_(i in I) [1-d_Lambda/d_(Lambda+omega_i)]
       <= sum_(alpha>0) [sum_(i in I)m_(alpha,i)]/x_alpha
       <= p/(n_*+1).                                          (8)

Combining (7)-(8),

    E_(k_Lambda) d(g,L_I)^2
       <= p/[gamma_(G,I)(n_*+1)].                             (9)

All moments are exact ratios; no semiclassical expansion or recoupling
asymptotic is used.

4. Coherent symbol, full multiplicities, and its Dirichlet defect

On End(V_Lambda) with normalized-trace Hilbert space define

    L(A)(gL_I)=Tr(P_g A),
    L^dagger f=d_Lambda integral_G f(g)P_g dg.

Direct calculation gives

    L^dagger L = Psi_Lambda,
    (LL^dagger f)(gL_I)
      = integral_G k_Lambda(g^(-1)h)f(h)dh.                   (10)

Both operators are positive self-adjoint contractions. The symbol map L is
injective for every dominant Lambda. Its coherent projective orbit is the
partial flag G_C/P_Lambda, with opposite dual flag G_C/P_Lambda^vee.
The complexified orbit of the pair of highest lines is an open dense
subset of their product, with stabilizer the complex Levi L_(I,C). The
compact real orbit G/L_I is maximal totally real in this open orbit. If
L(A) vanishes, the holomorphic bidegree-(1,1) section
ell(Ax), for line/covector pair (x,ell), vanishes on that maximal totally
real orbit. The identity theorem makes it zero on the open orbit and then
on the product. Both highest-line orbits span their irreducible carrier
and dual, so all matrix coefficients of A vanish and A=0. For Lambda=0
the carrier is one-dimensional and injectivity is immediate. This direct
polarization proof gives injectivity, not a lower bound on the smallest
symbol singular value.

Let W be a real irreducible conjugation type, with Casimir c_W, and
consider its block in L^2(G/L_I), namely W tensor W^(L_I), where
W^(L_I) is the L_I-fixed space. For a unit z in W^(L_I) and a nearest
ell in L_I to g, write g ell^(-1)=exp(X), ||X||=d(g,L_I). Here the
distance uses the basic metric with long roots of squared length 2,
whereas c_W is the half-Casimir corresponding to the physics-scaled
generators in Section 1. For X in the basic-metric Lie algebra, its
weight-functional norm is ||X||, so

    ||d rho_W(X)|| <= ||nu|| ||X|| <= sqrt(2 c_W)||X||,

because c_W=(||nu||^2+2(nu,rho))/2 >= ||nu||^2/2. Since ell fixes z,

    1-Re<z,rho_W(g)z>
       = (1/2)||rho_W(g)z-z||^2
       <= c_W d(g,L_I)^2.

Average this estimate with k_Lambda and use (9). The convolution block of
(10) then satisfies

    0 <= I-K_W
       <= [p c_W/(gamma_(G,I)(n_*+1))] I.                    (11)

The nonzero eigenvalues of L^dagger L and LL^dagger coincide. Injectivity
of L transfers (10) to every copy in the FULL W-isotypic space E_W of
End(V_Lambda), including embeddings not present in a chosen score
subspace. Since Psi is a positive contraction,

    ||(I-Psi_Lambda)|_(E_W)||_op
       <= p c_W/[gamma_(G,I)(n_*+1)].                         (12)

5. Actual compatibility supplies a floor on every full block

We give the Stinespring estimate used here to fix the actual-channel
interface. Let B_1,...,B_s be Hermitian, tau-orthonormal and satisfy
sum_i B_i^2=sI. Let Phi be bistochastic UCP with actual equal-marginal
joint J. Put

    epsilon=1-(1/s)sum_i tau(B_i Phi(B_i)),
    q(B)=(1/(2s^2))sum_ij ||[B_i,B_j]||_(2,tau)^2.

Then q(B)<=36 epsilon. To see this, take J(Z)=V^*pi(Z)V, P=VV^*,
F_i=pi(B_i tensor I), G_i=pi(I tensor B_i),
M_i=V^*F_iV=V^*G_iV=Phi(B_i), and
L_i=(I-P)F_iV, R_i=(I-P)G_iV. Set

    delta=s-sum_i tau(M_i^2),
    h=sum_i ||B_i-M_i||_2^2=2s epsilon-delta.

Kadison-Schwarz and trace preservation give 0<=delta,h<=2s epsilon.
The two output algebras commute, so
[M_i,M_j]=-L_i^*R_j+R_j^*L_i. Also
sum_i L_i L_i^*<=s(I-P), sum_i R_i R_i^*<=s(I-P), and the squared
Hilbert-Schmidt norms of each leakage family sum to delta. It follows that
the direct-sum squared norm of ([M_i,M_j])_ij is at most 4s delta.
Writing C_i=B_i-M_i, each of ([C_i,B_j])_ij and ([M_i,C_j])_ij has
squared norm at most 4s h, using sum B_i^2=sI and sum M_i^2<=sI.
Minkowski thus gives

    (sum_ij ||[B_i,B_j]||_2^2)^(1/2)
       <= sqrt(8)s sqrt(epsilon)+sqrt(32)s sqrt(epsilon).

Squaring and dividing by 2s^2 proves q(B)<=36 epsilon.

Now choose the physics-scaled Hermitian Lie-generator basis T_a from
Section 1 and put
kappa=C_Lambda/g, Q_a=T_a/sqrt(kappa). On one real irreducible copy
W with orthonormal Hermitian frame B_1,...,B_s, write

    [T_a,B_i]=i sum_j (L_a)_(ji) B_j,
    -sum_a L_a^2=c_W I_s.

The L_a are real skew matrices. Cyclicity gives
tau(Q_a[B_i,B_j])=i(L_a)_(ji)/sqrt(kappa). Bessel's inequality,
projecting each commutator onto the orthonormal Q_a, gives

    q(B)>=g c_W/(2s C_Lambda).                                (13)

The sum_i B_i^2 is invariant and hence scalar by carrier irreducibility;
its trace forces sum_i B_i^2=sI, so the preceding Stinespring bound
applies. Diagonalize only the self-adjoint covariant Dirichlet part
S=(Phi+Phi^sharp)/2, then decompose each eigenspace into real irreducible
copies. Applying q<=36(1-lambda) and (13) to each copy proves on the
entire W-isotypic block

    I-S >= [g c_W/(72 r_W C_Lambda)] I.                       (14)

The compatible map in the Stinespring argument is the original Phi.
Neither compatibility nor a joint extension is inferred for S. The
argument applies to realifications of complex and quaternionic types and
to all multiplicities.

Combining (12) squared with (14) proves (1)-(2). The two block matrices
need not commute: (12) bounds the operator norm of I-Psi on the whole
isotypic block, while (14) is a scalar lower bound there.

6. PBW growth and real/complex type bookkeeping

The complex adjoint representation is V_theta. Every constituent of
degree at most k in End(V_Lambda) occurs in some adjoint tensor power of
order at most k. Hence its highest weight nu lies in the root lattice and
satisfies nu<=k theta. Tensor weights also give ||nu||<=sqrt(2)k. With
the half-Casimir convention,

    c_W=c_2(nu)=(||nu||^2+2(nu,rho))/2
       <= k(k+hvee-1).

Weyl's dimension formula and Cauchy-Schwarz give

    dim_C V_nu <= (1+a_G k)^p,
    a_G=max_(alpha>0) sqrt(2)||alpha^vee||/(rho,alpha^vee).

The underlying real irreducible Hermitian type has dimension at most
2 dim_C V_nu; this includes a nonselfdual conjugate pair and quaternionic
realification. The Casimir is unchanged by realification. Therefore

    r_W c_W <= 2 hvee(1+a_G)^p k^(p+2).                       (15)

The trivial type is fixed by both channels and needs no compatibility
floor. Orthogonality of distinct isotypic spaces means the theorem's
constant is the maximum of the block constants, not their sum. This is
why the degree exponent is p+2 rather than the larger exponent obtained
by summing multiplicities. Formula (3) follows from
C_Lambda<=M_G N^2, n_*+1>=eta N, gamma_(G,I)>=gamma_G, and (15).
No multiplicity-free hypothesis is used.

7. Noncommutative polynomial-Gibbs consequence

Fix D>=1 and h0>=0. Suppose a family on the same face-regular carrier has

    rho_x = exp(X_x)/Tr exp(X_x),
    X_x=X_x^*,   X_x in pi_Lambda(U_<=D(g_C)),
    ||X_x||_infinity<=h0.

For a supplied actual covariant bistochastic compatible marginal, let

    b = sup_x (1/2)||Phi^sharp(rho_x)-rho_x||_1,
    theta=exp(2h0)-1,
    epsilon_K=exp(2h0) h0^(K+1)/(K+1)!.

The same coherent EB map, independent of K, x, residual, and polynomial
coefficients, satisfies for every integer K>=1

    e_EB <= sqrt(C_(G,eta)(D K))
             sqrt(theta b+epsilon_K^2)+epsilon_K/2.            (16)

Here e_EB=sup_x (1/2)||Psi_Lambda(rho_x)-rho_x||_1. In particular, for
fixed G, eta, D, h0 and b down to zero,

    e_EB = O_(G,eta,D,h0)(sqrt(b)
                    [log(1/b)/log log(1/b)]^((p+2)/2)).

This is a structured bounded-Holevo theorem, not the unrestricted
bounded-Holevo broadcasting conjecture: the face-regular, fixed-degree,
operator-norm and covariance/closure promises are essential supplied
structure. The family has Holevo capacity at most 2h0 because
D(rho_x||I/d)<=2h0, but capacity alone does not supply those promises.

Proof. Put z_x=tau exp(X_x), F_x=exp(X_x)/z_x and H_x=F_x-I, so
rho_x=F_x/d, tau H_x=0, ||H_x||_infinity<=theta. The norm bound gives
e^(-h0)<=z_x<=e^(h0) and
||exp(X_x)-sum_(j=0)^K X_x^j/j!||_infinity
<=e^(h0)h0^(K+1)/(K+1)!.
Divide by z_x and let p_K=sum_(j=0)^K X_x^j/(j! z_x),
T_K=F_x-p_K. Then ||T_K||_2<=epsilon_K and p_K is Hermitian of PBW
degree at most DK; the scalar component is allowed.

Since 0<=I-S<=2I and the physical residual gives
||F_x-Phi^sharp(F_x)||_(1,tau)<=2b, the Dirichlet energy satisfies
E_S(F_x):=<F_x,(I-S)F_x>_tau
=<H_x,(I-S)H_x>_tau <= 2 theta b.
The energy seminorm triangle inequality yields

    E_S(p_K)<=2E_S(F_x)+2E_S(T_K)
             <=4 theta b+4 epsilon_K^2.

Apply (3) to p_K, use ||I-Psi||_(2->2)<=1 on T_K, then
||Y||_(1,tau)<=||Y||_(2,tau). The physical half-trace contributes the
factor 1/2 and gives exactly (16).

For the asymptotic choice, choose the least n=K+1 with
e^(2h0)h0^n/n!<=sqrt(b). Stirling shows
n=O_(h0)(log(1/b)/log log(1/b)); then (3) and (16) give the displayed
rate. If b=0, send K to infinity in (16); if h0=0, all states are I/d.

If the original broadcaster is not covariant but the supplied experiment
is G-conjugation-closed, twirl the whole physical two-output map by
conjugating the input and both outputs. The family residual bound is
preserved by convexity and unitary invariance, the twirled map is still
an actual broadcaster, and irreducibility makes its covariant marginal
bistochastic. This yields the same result. No closure of a family is
inferred from its Holevo capacity.

8. Inputs, costs, controls, falsifiers, and limits

For Sections 1-7, supplied inputs are the compact simple root system and
normalization; eta and the face-regular highest-weight carrier; an actual
compatible broadcaster; covariance (or a supplied conjugation-closed
experiment); and the PBW degree and operator-norm bounds. These promises are
not acquired from data. The comparator is a continuous coherent POVM
followed by coherent preparation. Caratheodory gives an exact finite orbit
decomposition with at most d_Lambda^4+1 atoms, but no efficient quadrature,
representation evaluator, precision, state-preparation, memory, or physical
synthesis cost is claimed. The constants gamma_(G,I) are certifiable for
each fixed group and support by the net method in Section 2; no higher-rank
numeric certificate was produced. The truncation K in (16) is explicit
from h0 and b; no inverse symbol map or coefficient conditioning is used.

The strongest equally informed baseline is the established highest-weight
coherent/Berezin channel and exact dimension-ratio moments in (7). The
face-regular bridge combines those moments with the fixed-partial-flag
global overlap bound, the actual-compatible commutator floor, and PBW
full-isotypic support. Section 9 adds a separate all-wall consequence for
natural normalized-generator polynomial Gibbs probes, but it does not
extend the dimension-free fixed-degree comparison (3).

Falsifiers for the face-regular theorem include a normal positive-root
plane missed by the active fundamental highest-line family, failure of the
global lower bound (5), an incorrect Cartan-product moment (7), a coherent
convolution eigenvalue violating (11)-(12), or an actual joint violating
the Stinespring/Casimir bounds in Section 5. The corrected factor sqrt(2)
between the basic-metric tangent and physics-scaled generators is part of
the load-bearing normalization. A finite numerical check cannot replace
these analytic steps.

trace directly and do not import the paper's printed symbol-isometry

9. All-wall natural-polynomial Gibbs consequence

This separate consequence removes face regularity for a narrower, native
class of probes. Fix G and let N=sum_i n_i>=1 for any dominant carrier,
including arbitrary walls. Choose the physics-scaled Hermitian generators
T_1,...,T_g from Section 1 and set Xi_a=T_a/N. Define

    R_G=max(1,Omega_G/sqrt(2)),
    sigma_G=max(p-2,0),
    a'_G=max_(alpha>0)||alpha^vee||/(rho,alpha^vee),
    delta_G=min_(nu in the weight lattice, nu!=0)||nu||>0,
    c_min=delta_G^2/2,

    Rratio_G=max(2(1+a'_G)^p/c_min,
                 2^(p+2)(1+a'_G)^p (sqrt(2))^sigma_G),
    Gamma_G=72 M_G Rratio_G/g.

These constants depend only on fixed G. Suppose a supplied Hermitian
noncommutative polynomial

    X_x = sum_w c_(x,w) Xi_(w_1)...Xi_(w_|w|)

has degree at most D>=1 and coefficient l1 norm
sum_w|c_(x,w)|<=A; no separate operator-norm promise is imposed. Since
the represented-weight convex-hull bound gives
||T_X||_infinity<=||Lambda||_*||X||_* for a unit Lie direction X, and
||Lambda||_*<=N max_i||omega_i||_*. Hence
||Xi_a||_infinity<=max_i||omega_i||_*<=R_G, so these bounds imply
||X_x||_infinity<=A R_G^D=:h_pol uniformly in x and carrier. Set
rho_x=exp(X_x)/Tr exp(X_x). For a G-covariant bistochastic UCP marginal Phi
with an actual equal-marginal two-output extension, let
b=sup_x (1/2)||Phi^sharp(rho_x)-rho_x||_1. The same canonical coherent EB
channel Psi_Lambda, independent of x and b, obeys the following bounds,
where e_EB:=sup_x (1/2)||Psi_Lambda(rho_x)-rho_x||_1:

    e_EB <= K_src/N,                                             (17)

where K_src is the explicit fixed-G constant in (23) below, obtained
from the exact first-bias identity rather than the energy-only
N^(-1/2) estimate.

For every K>=1,

    e_EB <= sqrt(Gamma_G) N (1+DK)^(sigma_G/2)
                  sqrt(theta b+epsilon_K^2)+epsilon_K/2,       (18)

    theta=exp(2h_pol)-1,
    epsilon_K=exp(2h_pol) h_pol^(K+1)/(K+1)!.

For L_b=log(1/b)/loglog(1/b) and sufficiently small b, optimizing N and K
between (17) and (18) gives, uniformly over all dominant carriers,

    e_EB=O_(G,D,A)(b^(1/4) L_b^(sigma_G/4)).                  (19)

The family has Holevo capacity at most 2h_pol since
D(rho_x||I/d)<=log||d rho_x||_infinity<=2h_pol. Thus the operator-norm
promise follows from the supplied coefficient and degree bounds. Capacity
still does not infer the representation, polynomial coefficient, degree,
or covariance/closure promises. There is no uniformity over root systems.

9.1. All-wall target comparison from compatibility alone

The actual compatibility floor (14) applies to every carrier. Since the
coherent EB channel is a positive Hilbert-Schmidt contraction,
||(I-Psi)A_W||_2^2<=||A_W||_2^2. Hence on a nontrivial W-isotypic block,

    ||(I-Psi)A_W||_2^2
        <= [72 r_W C_Lambda/(g c_W)] E_S(A_W).                (20)

For a highest weight nu at PBW degree k, Weyl's formula gives
dim_C V_nu<= (1+a'_G||nu||)^p; r_W<=2dim_C V_nu,
c_W>=||nu||^2/2, and c_W>=c_min. If ||nu||<1, the c_min bound gives
r_W/c_W<=2(1+a'_G)^p/c_min. If ||nu||>=1, then

    r_W/c_W <= 4(1+a'_G)^p 2^p ||nu||^(p-2).

For p=1 the last power is negative and is at most 1; for p>=2,
||nu||<=sqrt(2)k bounds it by the second term in Rratio_G times
(1+k)^sigma_G. The trivial type is annihilated by I-Psi. Orthogonality of
full isotypic blocks and C_Lambda<=M_G N^2 therefore give

    ||(I-Psi)A||_2^2
        <= Gamma_G N^2(1+k)^sigma_G E_S(A)                  (21)

for every Hermitian PBW-degree-<=k score. This route uses neither gamma
nor a lower bound on n_*/N. Its N^2 loss is traded against the source
approximation (17); it does not replace the dimension-free face-regular
comparison (3).

9.2. All-wall coherent source estimate for natural polynomials

The first-bias input is the exact moment shift from
Leung--Li--Ma, arXiv:2010.14339v1, Theorem 2.4 and Eq. (2.5), independently
reconstructed in GENERAL_COHERENT_FIRST_BIAS_PROOF.txt. Fix the weight
metric for this source estimate to be half the basic root-length-2 metric,
so long roots have squared length 1 (short roots retain their usual
root-system-dependent lengths). This is the metric for the physical
Hermitian generators T_a used in Xi_a=T_a/N. Equivalently, all norms in
the source constants below are basic-metric norms divided by sqrt(2).
This convention does not alter Section 9.1, whose Weyl-dimension and
Casimir bounds were written in the basic metric.

For a face with active complementary positive roots R_F^+, let
beta_F=sum_(alpha in R_F^+) alpha. The exact identity is

    Delta T_X = d integral m_(beta_F,X)(P) P dmu(P),
    ||Delta T_X||_infinity <= ||beta_F||_* ||X||_*,

where * denotes the half-basic metric. It follows from the shifted
Toeplitz moment identity: the shift is positive and equals twice the
half-sum of complementary positive roots. In particular, for every
highest-weight face, including arbitrarily near walls, the operator
norm bias is bounded by B_G||X||_* with
B_G=sum_(alpha positive)||alpha||_*. This is a whole-output bound, so it
does not assume preservation of a selected adjoint copy.

Set

    w_G=max_i||omega_i||_*, L_G=max(1,w_G),
    delta_G=B_G/L_G,
    E_G=2 w_G||rho||_*/(g L_G^2), A_eff=A L_G^D.

The metric conversion gives L_G=R_G and A_eff=h_pol.

The carrier norm bound gives ||T_a/(L_G N)||_infinity<=1. The exact
coherent generator energy is

    sum_a E_Psi(T_a)=2(Lambda,rho)_*,
    E_Psi(T_a/(L_G N))<=E_G/N.

Thus for B_a=T_a/(L_G N), one has
||Delta B_a||_(1,tau)<=delta_G/N. For an ordered word W of length m in
the B_a, expanding the double-commutator formula

    Delta(Z)=(d/2) integral [P,[P,Z]] dmu(P)

gives one singleton term per position, costing delta_G/N, and two ordered
cross terms per unordered position pair, costing at most 2E_G/N by
normalized Schatten Holder and Cauchy--Schwarz. Hence, for the supplied
Hermitian polynomial P of degree at most D and coefficient l1 norm at
most A,

    ||Delta P||_(1,tau)
       <= A_eff[D delta_G+D(D-1)E_G]/N,
    E_Psi(P)<=A_eff^2 D^2 E_G/N.                              (22)

The second estimate follows from the direct-sum Kraus commutator
seminorm and its ordered Leibniz rule. It holds for noncommuting words
and complex coefficients whenever P is Hermitian; no coefficient norm
is inferred from ||P|| or Holevo capacity.

For F=exp(P)/tau(exp(P)), the full ordered-power expansion gives
||Delta exp(P)||_(1,tau)<=exp(h_pol)(||Delta P||_(1,tau)+E_Psi(P));
normalizing by tau(exp(P))>=exp(-h_pol) and taking physical half-trace
therefore yields

    K_src=(exp(2h_pol)/2) {
       A_eff[D delta_G+D(D-1)E_G]+A_eff^2D^2E_G },
    e_EB(F)<=K_src/N.                                          (23)

This is an unconditional structured source bound for the same coherent
EB channel, with no regular-face or large-label assumption. The exact
moment-shift identity is established in the cited primary source; the
whole-output norm and polynomial Gibbs estimate are the charged
quantitative consequence used here.

9.3. Actual-residual transfer and optimization

Taylor-truncate F_x at K to p_K, with T_K=F_x-p_K. The bound
||T_K||_2<=epsilon_K holds and p_K has PBW degree DK. As in Section 7,
E_S(F_x)<=2theta b and E_S(p_K)<=4(theta b+epsilon_K^2). Apply (21)
to p_K, use ||I-Psi||_(2->2)<=1 on T_K and normalized L1<=L2; the
physical half-trace yields (18). Choosing K with epsilon_K<=sqrt(b) gives
K=O(log(1/b)/loglog(1/b)). The minimum of (17), which decreases as
N^(-1), and (18), which is O(N sqrt(b)L_b^(sigma_G/2)), is at most a
constant times b^(1/4)L_b^(sigma_G/4), uniformly in N. This proves (19).

If the original broadcaster is not covariant but the supplied experiment
is G-conjugation-closed, twirl the two-output map by conjugating input and
outputs. The family residual is preserved and the new marginal is an
actual compatible covariant bistochastic channel. The class closure must be
supplied; bounded Holevo capacity alone does not give it. A continuous
coherent POVM is still the mathematical decoder; an exact finite
Caratheodory representation has at most d_Lambda^4+1 atoms, with no
polynomial-time or precision guarantee. Equations (17)-(19) are a
structured all-wall result, not the global N93 bounded-Holevo implication.
