# Concentration-to-entropic-chaos: targeted priority pass

**Version:** 2026-10-08, pass 3. **Status:** `read`—primary-source theorem statements and selected proof interiors inspected. This is a bounded comparison, not a proof audit, novelty finding, or priority claim.

## Supplied target

For each finite (N), the coordinator supplies a permutation-invariant (N)-qubit state ρᵢ and its one-site marginal τ=ρ₁⩾κI. For every Hermitian (H) with ‖H‖≤1 and every real (t), assume the uniform collective MGF bound

\[
\operatorname{Tr}\rho_N\exp\!\left[t\sum_{i=1}^N\bigl(H_i-\operatorname{Tr}(\tau H)I\bigr)\right]\leq A e^{BNt^2}
\]

with constants independent of (N,H,t). The supplied conclusion is centered Petz χ² and Umegaki relative entropy (D(\rho_r\|\tau^{\otimes r})=O((r/N)^2)), uniformly for (r=o(N)). The parent also supplied an operational entanglement-certification cost claim; it is outside this source pass and remains unverified here. The states are finite-(N) permutation-invariant states; no projective consistency or infinite extendibility is assumed.

## Finding

No opened source states this finite-state implication with the conjunction of (i) arbitrary permutation-invariant quantum states, (ii) all one-site observables and a uniform centered collective MGF premise, (iii) centering at the actual faithful marginal, and (iv) both Petz χ² and relative-entropy (O((r/N)^2)) for every sublinear (r). No contradiction was found. The result of this pass is an exact map of adjacent theorem interfaces, not evidence of novelty.

The closest route with matching classical rate is not a single theorem. Finite classical exchangeability identifies a block marginal as sampling without replacement conditional on the empirical type. The hypergeometric-versus-multinomial KL correction is (O(r^2/N^2)) for fixed alphabet and (r=o(N)), and convexity lifts that comparison to the empirical-i.i.d. mixture. This leaves the crucial step from the mixture to the product of the true one-site marginal; concentration of empirical frequencies could plausibly supply it classically, but no source inspected here states the target result from the given MGF premise. In the quantum setting, this decomposition is not itself a representation of an arbitrary finite PI state as a positive mixture of product states.
