# d=5 Weyl star twirl and an exact support lower certificate

This note closes two bookkeeping gaps in the d=5 Weyl-invariant dual route: the top star eigenvalue is attainable by a legal symmetric extension with a Hilbert--Schmidt-self-adjoint marginal, and the one-mode-per-line example is **not** a gate violation. The first result concerns self-adjointness; the twirled maximizer need not in general be positive as a Hilbert--Schmidt operator. The second gives an exact feasible-POVM lower bound for this one dual.

## Weyl/parity twirl makes the star maximum marginal-feasible

Let (d) be an odd prime, (W_g=X^uZ^v), and let (Q\succeq0) be Weyl-invariant on the real traceless-Hermitian Hilbert space. For any spectral frame (Q=\sum_iw_i|A_i\rangle\langle A_i|), set

\[
C_Q=\sum_iw_iA_i^T\otimes A_i,
\qquad
H_Q=C_Q\otimes I+\text{the same reference/output coupling on }R:B.
\]

The tensor $C_Q$ is independent of the chosen spectral frame. Weyl invariance of $Q$ gives

\[
(\overline W\otimes W)C_Q(\overline W\otimes W)^*=C_Q.
\]

Hence $H_Q$ commutes with the simultaneous representation

\[
U_W=\overline W_R\otimes W_A\otimes W_B.
\tag{1}
\]

If (|\psi\rangle) is any unit top eigenvector of $H_Q$, the finite displacement twirl

\[
\Omega_0=\frac1{d^2}\sum_{W\in\mathsf W_d}
       U_W|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|U_W^*
\tag{2}
\]

is positive, normalized, commutes with $H_Q$, and has the same star score (λ_{\max}(H_Q)). Each one-body marginal commutes with an irreducible Weyl representation, so Schur's lemma gives

\[
(\Omega_0)_R=(\Omega_0)_A=(\Omega_0)_B=I/d.
\tag{3}
\]

Because $Q=Q^*$ is a real self-adjoint Weyl commutant, it acts as a scalar on each real mode plane generated by (W_g,W_g^*). Indeed the commutant on such a plane is $aI+bJ$, and the self-adjoint condition forces $b=0$; inequivalent planes have no off-diagonal intertwiners. Phase-space parity (P|j\rangle=|-j\rangle) sends (g\mapsto-g), so (Q) and (H_Q) are parity invariant. Average (2) under \(\overline P_R\otimes P_A\otimes P_B\) and output swap. Both commute with $H_Q$. The resulting Ω is swap-symmetric, retains (3) and the same star score, and its $RA$ marginal is invariant under \(\overline W\otimes W\) and \(\overline P\otimes P\).

The normalized Choi inverse of this (RA) marginal is a unital, trace-preserving CP Weyl-covariant channel $\\Phi$. Weyl covariance diagonalizes it on the Weyl operators, $\\Phi(W_g)=\\lambda_gW_g$. Hermiticity preservation gives $\\lambda_{-g}=\\overline{\\lambda_g}$; parity covariance gives $\\lambda_{-g}=\\lambda_g$. Thus every $\\lambda_g$ is real and $\\Phi$ is Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint. Output swap supplies its symmetric two-output broadcaster. Consequently

\[
\max_{\Omega\text{ legal, HS-self-adjoint marginal}}
       \operatorname{Tr}(\Omega H_Q)
       =\lambda_{\max}(H_Q)
\tag{4}
\]

for every Weyl-invariant (Q\succeq0) in odd prime dimension. The reverse inequality in (4) is the variational bound for any density matrix; (2) and the parity/swap averages attain it. Equation (4) does **not in general** prove that the maximizing marginal is Hilbert--Schmidt-positive: parity forces real Weyl multipliers, not nonnegative ones. A qubit measure-and-prepare counterexample to automatic HS positivity is recorded in [`STAR_TWIRL_VERIFICATION.md`](../legal_sdp/STAR_TWIRL_VERIFICATION.md). For the specific six-mode $d=5$ dual below, the top-twirled marginal does pass this extra gate: the rational-interval residual/gap certificate in [`d5_twirl_top_positivity_certificate.py`](../legal_sdp/d5_twirl_top_positivity_certificate.py) proves every nonidentity transfer coefficient exceeds $0.472038$. Thus (4) is exact over the full target class for this one $Q$, while the general all-$Q$ star reduction applies only to the larger CP-self-adjoint class until HS positivity is separately established.

## The six-mode d=5 candidate passes its support gate

Take the six selected representatives

\[
S=\{(0,2),(1,0),(1,1),(2,4),(2,1),(2,3)\}\subset\mathbb Z_5^2,
\]

one real mode pair per projective line, and let $Q$ put unit weight on both normalized Hermitian modes for every (g\in S). Then (\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q=12), (Q) is Weyl invariant, and the full star maximum equals that of one 25-dimensional charge block. In computational indices ((r,a,b)), each star term preserves charge (c=a+b-r\pmod5): (W_g^T) on (R) and (W_g^*\) on its selected receiver shift their indices in the same direction. Simultaneous (X) displacement on (R,A,B) commutes with $H_Q$ and cyclically permutes the five charge sectors. The five block spectra are therefore equal.

For the charge-zero block the exact characteristic polynomial is

\[
\begin{aligned}
p(x)={}&(x+1)^8(x+1+\sqrt5)^4(x+1-\sqrt5)^4\\
&\cdot\left(x^2-8x-\frac{33}{2}+\frac{5\sqrt5}{2}\right)\\
&\cdot\left(x^2+2x-\frac{63}{2}-\frac{5\sqrt5}{2}\right)^2\\
&\cdot\left(x^3-12x^2+x\left(-\frac{169}{2}+\frac{5\sqrt5}{2}\right)-60\sqrt5+716\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]

Every non-cubic factor has all roots below 14. The cubic $r(x)$ has

\[
r(14)=-75-25\sqrt5<0,\qquad
r(15)=\frac{247-45\sqrt5}{2}>0,
\qquad r'(14)=\frac{335+5\sqrt5}{2}>0.
\]

Since (r''(x)=6x-24>0) for (x\ge14), its derivative stays positive there. Thus its only root in ([14,\infty)) lies in $(14,15)$, and

\[
14<\lambda_{\max}(H_Q)<15.
\tag{6}
\]

The exact block polynomial is recomputed in [`d5_selected_charpoly.py`](d5_selected_charpoly.py); the matrix is Hermitian and the off-charge entries vanish exactly. The identical block spectra follow from the displacement symmetry above.

Now use the rational seed

\[
z=(-0.453247-0.432812i,\ 0.272132+0.036691i,\ -0.198028+0.411862i,\ -0.552038-0.074431i,\ -0.081529-0.077853i),
\qquad \psi=z/\|z\|_2.
\tag{7}
\]

For this $Q$,

\[
f_Q(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)
 =2\sum_{g\in S}|\langle\psi|W_g|\psi\rangle|^2>3.
\tag{8}
\]

The exact interval checker [`d5_pure_state_lower_certificate.py`](d5_pure_state_lower_certificate.py) parses the coordinates as rationals, encloses fifth roots of unity using integer-verified square-root intervals, and proves (8). The 25-state Weyl orbit of $\\psi$ has barycenter $I/5$; since $Q$ is invariant, all its states have the same score. The orbit therefore defines one legal rank-one POVM and proves $S_5(Q)>3$.

Combining (6), (8), and \(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q=12\),

\[
\lambda_{\max}(H_Q)-\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q<3<S_5(Q).
\]

So this exact one-mode-per-line dual is not a counterexample, despite the failed line-PVM shortcut (\lambda_{\max}(H_Q)>14=\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q+2\max_L|S\cap L|). It is a useful control of both the star-twirl reduction and the barycenter-sensitive support; it does not establish the inequality for every Weyl-invariant $Q$, much less general (Q).
