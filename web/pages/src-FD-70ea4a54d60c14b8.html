FIXED-DEGREE EXTENSION AND EXACT TRANSLATION-STABILIZER FILTRATION
2026-10-13. Internal proof, using established local RM/NIS components.
Novelty, practical value, source transfer and external proof review UNKNOWN.

Contract. Replace quadratic coordinates by multilinear F2 coordinates of
degree at most fixed d>=1. Q is still a bijection, and a length h=n-r partial
translation flag exists but is not supplied. The persistent oracle agrees
with Q whenever both items avoid an unknown fixed B, density epsilon.
The general construction works whenever
  epsilon<2^(-d-2).
This statement is Monte Carlo exact recovery under promises, not recognition.
All 2^r residual work, degree/noise constants, coding preprocessing and source
costs remain charged. The fixed-degree extension is not an executed compiler.

1. Computing periods and own-toggles is polynomial for fixed d.
A tempting but unnecessary approach would solve the nonlinear coefficient
equations Delta_v f=0 or 1 directly in v. Instead use a filtration.

Given a known linear subspace S and degree-at-most-d polynomial f, start
with S_(d-1)=S. Every v in it has deg_x Delta_v f<=d-1. Inductively suppose
all v in S_t have deg_x Delta_v f<=t. The map
  A_t:S_t -> {homogeneous degree-t coefficient vectors},
  A_t(v)=degree-t part of Delta_v f,
is LINEAR. Indeed the translation cocycle is
  Delta_(v+w) f=Delta_v f + T_v Delta_w f.
Translation leaves a polynomial's highest homogeneous part unchanged, so
degree-t parts add for v,w in S_t. Also A_t(0)=0. Compute A_t on a basis of
S_t, solve its homogeneous linear kernel, and set S_(t-1)=ker A_t. This is
exactly the subspace where derivatives have degree at most t-1.

After t=d-1,...,1, every derivative on S_0 is constant. Its constant value
lambda(v) is linear by the same cocycle. The exact periods of f inside S
are ker lambda. A constant-one own-toggle exists iff lambda is nonzero;
then a basis vector with response one supplies it. Consequently:
* update an acquired-prefix stabilizer by applying this period algorithm to
  the newly acquired coordinate and the existing exact subspace;
* find a next own-toggle by the same filtration, selecting response one.
No generic multivariate nonlinear-equation solver is imported.

For sparse degree-d coefficients, one basis translation expands each
monomial into at most 2^d terms. At most n translations per level and d
levels, plus polynomial-size GF2 kernels, give polynomial n work at fixed d.
The known future original flag guarantees a response-one solution whenever
the newly acquired candidate is the true next coordinate. Arbitrary earlier
true own-toggles do not alter the true stabilizer or this existence argument.
The filtration also works starting inside an arbitrary computed subspace.

2. Robust labels and coding margins.
The deterministic fiber rank-threshold lemma and randomized median lemma
do not use degree two. They give raw next-bit global errors
  rho_direct<=2epsilon,
  rho_med<=2epsilon+eta_s,
  eta_s=binom(s,(s-1)/2)/2^(s+1), odd s with fair equality ties.
The degree-d RM minimum distance is a=2^-d and its unique radius is a/2.
Put
  gamma_direct=2^(-d-1)-2epsilon>0.
Choose odd s with eta_s<gamma_direct, for example <=gamma_direct/2, and
gamma_med=gamma_direct-eta_s. The pinned-cube correction proof is unchanged:
l>=d+1, K=2^l>=4/gamma^2, each cube has failure<=1/4, and R repetitions
give per-fixed-label failure<=exp(-R/8). Classical coefficient checksum
majority decoding proceeds through degrees d,d-1,...,0; every error point
spoils at most one parity among disjoint coordinate flats, so strictly less
than K*2^(-d-1) faulty positions is correctable. Noninjective random cube
maps and fresh randomized median labels are handled as before.

Dvir-Shpilka's primary theorem applies over F2 to multilinear functions of
any fixed d: construct an efficient degree-d NIS with L_n<=C_d*n^d positions
and fixed correction radius theta_d>0. Constant-confidence local labels
eta0=theta_d/4 and Markov give entire true-body recovery probability>=3/4.
Set R0 to the least odd integer >=8 ln(4/theta_d). No per-anchor log n is
needed in the body proposal. Generation/decoding remain charged polynomial
preprocessing/work; code-family constants and implementation are not supplied
by our executed scripts.

3. Fresh direct-label validation and capped full learner.
For any fixed degree-at-most-d candidate f, true q next and fresh uniform x,
the raw label discrepancy means satisfy
  f=q: mean<=rho,
  f!=q: mean>=2^-d-rho.
Accept below threshold 2^(-d-1). T=ceil(ln(1/delta)/(2 gamma^2)) fresh tests
bound both error probabilities by delta. Prefix tests use s comparisons
and the already acquired exact projection sampler. Residual tests use
L-1 comparisons after enumerating/filtering the acquired h-fiber; expected
fiber-size checks precede source comparisons and cap all bad histories.

At each of n stages propose/validate at most
  Rcap=ceil(2 ln(2n/beta)), delta=beta/(2n Rcap^2),
with 0<beta<=1/2. Prefix candidates also undergo the exact own-toggle
filtration gate; a true body passes it. True acceptance probability per
trial is >=(3/4)(1-delta)>=1/2. Stage caps contribute <=beta/2; first wrong
acceptance contributes <=beta/(2Rcap). All later histories stay capped.
Correct-entry expected proposals<=2; unconditional bad-entry charge adds
<=beta/2 per stage. Thus the full rank decoder, inverse and exact prefix
samplers are correct with probability at least 1-beta.

Let Tmed,Tdirect use gamma_med,gamma_direct, and Kmed,Kdirect their cubes.
Set S_r=2^(r+1)-r-2, with S_0=0, and
  B_d=h*(L_n*Kmed*R0*s + s*Tmed)
       +(L_n*Kdirect*R0+Tdirect)*S_r.
The precise comparison bounds are
  Qmax<=Rcap*B_d, E Q<=(2+beta/2)*B_d.
For fixed d, noise gap and coding family,
  E Q=O(n^(d+1)+2^r*n^d+(n+2^r)*ln(n/beta)),
  Qmax=O((n^(d+1)+2^r*n^d)*ln(n/beta)
         +(n+2^r)*ln(n/beta)^2).
For fixed r,beta, the expected count is O(n^(d+1)). This is a source-query
upper bound, not a runtime/bit-cost separation or optimality assertion.

The acquired toggles still give a triangular projection for every prefix
through h, independent of degree. After basis conversion,
  q_i(Vt)=t_i+H_i(t_<i,z), deg H_i<=d, i<=h.
The compact flagged coefficient payload is
  sum_(j=0)^d [binom(n,j+1)-binom(r,j+1)],
plus r*M_d(n) residual coefficients and n^2 basis bits, where
M_d(n)=sum_(j=0)^d binom(n,j). Conversion is source-free, but no acquisition
cost is erased. Residual inverse/prefix sampling still enumerates 2^r points.

4. Sharp uniform noise radius for r>=d (construction).
Let z have n-r-1 bits, u=(u1,...,ud), w have r-d bits. Define
  qb=x0+b*prod_(i=1)^d ui,
  Qb=(z,qb,u1+qb,...,ud+qb,w), b=0,1.
Both maps have degree<=d and are bijections: from outputs recover u by
adding qb, then x0=qb+b*prod ui. Their common length h=n-r partial flag is
the z coordinate directions, then e_x0. In every z block, A=(x0=0,u=1^d)
is the end of Q0's lower qb half; B=(x0=1,u=1^d) is the start of its upper
half. Q1 swaps these adjacent A,B blocks and preserves the rest. Total
|A|=N*2^(-d-1). Split A into equal Aearly,Alate, and observe Aearly,B,Alate.
Q0 is explained by bad Alate, Q1 by bad Aearly, each density 2^(-d-2), while
the last flagged prefix bit differs. Split within w when r>d; when r=d
split across z fibers, requiring n>=d+2. Thus this uniform family's threshold
cannot exceed the achieved strict radius. Special r<d/prefix classes may
have different thresholds; no stronger lower bound is claimed.

5. Finite evidence and implementation boundary.
degree_d_stabilizer.py exhaustively compares the symbolic filtration with
truth-table constant derivatives/periods for all 32768 four-variable degree
at-most-three polynomials. It also checks 500 random degree-three cases in
five variables and 500 degree-four cases in six variables, starting from
random restricted subspaces. Its independent bitset and sparse-coefficient
implementations agree throughout. The sparse implementation also completes
constructed n=64,d=3 and n=128,d=4 examples storing only three monomials,
with exact constant-derivative/period dimensions and toggles verified, and
without input-domain enumeration. The 2^n-bit reference encoding is used
ONLY in the small truth-table tests. These checks establish no dense large-n
runtime or source acquisition. Sharp general partial-prefix ambiguity at
(n,r,d)=(5,3,3),(6,4,3), epsilon=1/32, also passes every clean-pair, degree,
bijection and partial-flag check. The general direct NIS compiler has not
been implemented or executed here.
