2. AUXILIARY SPARSE-HOLE GEOMETRY (HOLE FILE LINES 5-68)

PASS. Star components with bounded nearest-neighbor complements filled
are contained in their original bounding rectangles. A hull site's
nearest-neighbor edge to the unbounded complement must start at an
original bad site: otherwise its supposed bounded complement would
connect to the unbounded one. At a diagonal contact of an exterior site
with a filled complementary site, both intermediate nearest-neighbor
sites must belong to the original component. Otherwise a two-step path
would give the same contradiction. Thus every exterior rim site is star
adjacent to the original bad component, and cannot itself be bad in a
different component. This is the exact point that justifies rim goodness.

Tracing the outer unit-square faces, with the exterior corner site at a
convex turn, connects all exterior entering-edge endpoints. Filling all
bounded nearest-neighbor faces eliminates inner boundary walks. Diagonal
touches are resolved along the outer face. The walk length is at most a
fixed multiple of the number of unit-square sides, hence O(|H|) and
O((d+1)^2). Loop-erasing gives SIMPLE rim paths with the same length
bound; this choice is important for the coefficient count below.

Distinct star components cannot cross an outer boundary without becoming
star adjacent. Their filled hulls therefore are disjoint or nested.
Retaining the outermost ones removes nesting, and any remaining diagonal
touch would also join the original components. Each rim site has an
original component site among its eight neighbors, so at most eight
retained components use it. Rim-edge overlap is uniformly bounded.

The tail proof needs no conditional rarity or independence. If d_z>=r,
an original component of m>=r/C sites lies within distance O(m+1) of z.
There are O((m+1)^2) possible roots and at most 64^m rooted star animals
using a canonical depth-first spanning-tree walk. Summing their joint
bad-site probabilities gives C exp(-cr) for sufficiently small fixed rho.
This applies also at deterministic pin collars, because collars are
excluded from B rather than assigned bad indicators. All fixed polynomial
one-site moments follow, uniformly in volume and site.

buffer-crossing bound O(N^2 exp(-cN)) gives a common event with

All needed paths stay in S+ there. Energy sums are over edges with BOTH
endpoints in the enlarged coarse square; the extra margin supplies all
rim edges used from S. No distant unintegrated energy is needed.

supremum <=C N^(-alpha)r^(alpha-1) and l1 norm <=Cr. Interpolation gives
the l^(p') bound
