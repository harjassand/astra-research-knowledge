C09_S01: NO FINITE IID ENTROPY CERTIFICATE WITHOUT A TAIL/RANGE INPUT
7 October 2026 UTC. Internal complete counterexample under the exact
sample-only interface below; external priority/correctness UNKNOWN.

The acquired decoder theorem uses a certified field range to validate its
matrix-relative-entropy loss. This is a substantive acquisition premise,
even if covariance, Poincare, field L2 norm and square-root energy are bounded.

INTERFACE

The reference mu is KNOWN Exp(1) on [0,infinity). The unknown scalar PSD
field A is accessible only through N fresh iid pairs (Y_i,A(Y_i)), Y_i~mu.
An algorithm may use independent random bits, the known density, and the
promises E A<=2 and E |(sqrt A)'|^2<=E0 below. It is not supplied the formula,
cutoff, tail range or source-code representation of A, and has no controlled
evaluation queries. Its task is a distribution-uniform confidence upper on
 H(A)=E[A log A]-(E A)log(E A).
The reference has covariance 1 and optimal Poincare constant 4. These are
fixed across fields and may be supplied freely to both algorithm and control.

THEOREM

For any finite N and delta<1/2, no algorithm that returns a finite upper with
probability >=1-delta on the constant field A=1 can guarantee
 Pr{H(A)<=returned upper}>=1-delta for every field in this smooth promised
class. A valid distribution-free algorithm must sometimes return infinity
or UNKNOWN on even the constant-field records, or acquire further range/tail
information. This is not a claim about an algorithm given the field formula.

CONSTRUCTION

Choose once a smooth step h: R->[0,1], zero on (-infinity,0], one on
[1,infinity), with bounded derivative. It can be C-infinity: take
 r(u)=exp(-1/u) for u>0, r(u)=0 otherwise and
 h(u)=r(u)/(r(u)+r(1-u)).
Put
 c0=int_0^infinity e^-u h(u)^2 du, e^-1<=c0<=1,
 c1=int_0^1 e^-u h'(u)^2 du<infinity,
 E0=c1/c0.
The promise can be a fixed numerical upper E0<=64e: r'(u)=u^-2 exp(-1/u)
is <=4e^-2; on (0,1) one of r(u),r(1-u) is >=e^-2, so h'<=8. Hence c1<=64
and c0>=e^-1. No unknown n-dependent gradient certificate is being supplied.
For n>0 set m_n=e^-n c0 and
 A_n(y)=1+h(y-n)^2/m_n, R_n=sqrt(A_n).
Every field is smooth, positive and constant 1 throughout y<=n. Exact
integration gives E A_n=2. Differentiating,
 |R_n'|^2=h^2 h'^2/[m_n^2(1+h^2/m_n)]<=h'^2/m_n,
so E|R_n'|^2<=c1/c0=E0 for EVERY n. Normalizing R by sqrt2 would give second
moment 1 with half this energy, but the unnormalized EA<=2 form is simpler.

On y>=n+1, h=1 and A_n=1+1/m_n. Its probability is e^-(n+1), giving
 E[A_n log A_n]>=e^-(n+1)(1+1/m_n)log(1+1/m_n)>=n/e.
The last bound uses c0<=1 and log(1+1/m_n)>=n. Therefore
 H(A_n)>=n/e-2log2 ->infinity.
All covariance, Poincare, field moment and energy promises stay bounded.

COUPLING PROOF OF THE ACQUISITION THEOREM

Run the algorithm on the constant field with the same iid Y_i and same
random tape as for A_n. Because its output is finite with probability
>=1-delta, choose a finite u for which Pr{upper_on_constant<=u}>1-delta-
epsilon, with an arbitrarily small epsilon>0. Choose n so large that
H(A_n)>u and N e^-n<epsilon.
With probability at least 1-N e^-n all sampled Y_i<=n. On that event the
ENTIRE observed records, including Y_i, are identical under A_n and A=1.
Thus Pr{upper_on_A_n<=u}>1-delta-2epsilon. Choose epsilon so that this is
>delta, possible since delta<1/2.
On the displayed event the purported upper is false. This contradicts the
distribution-uniform confidence requirement. The proof uses a known common
reference and exact smooth fields; hidden change of source law is unnecessary.

For a specified n, obtaining even one differing field evaluation has
probability <=N e^-n; constant-chance direct detection requires N=Omega(e^n).
The cutoff/range representation, if supplied, instead exposes the entropy
analytically. These are two different legal access models, not a disagreement
between equally informed algorithms. The held-out acquired decoder uses a
range certificate and returns UNKNOWN if its finite validation bound fails,
so it respects this obstruction. A field of bounded r/s in c09_s02 likewise
contains additional acquired tail information.

This lemma is a sample-acquisition obstruction for the whole field entropy,
not a KLS counterexample, not a quantum-memory lower and not a disproof of a
codec with an explicitly supplied model. It shows why replacing Fisher
spectra by a joint-field quantity must retain the cost of obtaining that
quantity. No finite-grid diagnostic or rare-region sample was used as proof.
