SYNTHESIS / CLAIM CORRECTION
============================
The justified lead conclusion is positive recurrence and physical-time
nonexplosion on each fixed isolated-substrate class, for the source-order-one
network grammar stated below. The proof establishes that LV(x)->-infinity
along every escaping sequence, hence LV<=-1 outside a finite set. It does
not, in this synthesis, claim a quantitative bound LV<=C-c[A+sum_i W_i],
a subgeometric rate, or a stationary V-moment. An earlier draft asserted
those stronger conclusions; it is preserved verbatim as
`RESULT_SUPERSEDED_STRONG_DRIFT_CLAIM.txt`. The independent cold audit at
`leaves/constructive/REPORT.txt` closes the finite-drift-set recurrence
argument and is the basis for the corrected claim. The stronger inequality
may be true, but it is an unresolved proof obligation here.

EXACT THEOREM INTERFACE
=======================
Let J,I be finite; E subset J x I; I_+={i:N(i) is nonempty}, with
N(i)={j:(j,i) in E}. For each j and each (j,i) in E, include positive
finite-rate stochastic mass-action channels

    0 <-> A_j                                  alpha_j, delta_j a_j
    A_j <-> A_j+B_i                            p_ji a_j, q_ji a_j b_i
    A_j+B_i <-> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i              u_ji a_j b_i,
                                                v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i,

where k_ji>=1. Add a finite list of reversible substrate-preserving
catalyst channels

    A_j+z_B <-> A_l+z_B,       A_j+z_B <-> z_B,

with catalyst source order one, finite substrate cofactor degree |z_B|<=m,
and positive finite rates. Fix the counts of isolated substrates i not in
I_+. The base paths make each active fixed-label slice irreducible (the
J-empty slice is trivial).

Choose a common R>1 close enough to 1 that

    chi_j=delta_j-(R-1)sum_{i:(j,i) in E}p_ji > 0

for every catalyst. For arbitrary finite epsilon_i>0 and K>=1, define

    V(a,b)=1+A+sum_{i in I_+} R^(b_i)
                [1+epsilon_i h_i(a)/(1+b_i)^D]+K 1_{A=0},
    A=sum_j a_j,   h_i(a)=1_{sum_{j in N(i)}a_j=0},

with integer D>=m. On each fixed isolated-substrate class, V is positive and
norm-like. The cold-audited claim is:

    LV(x) -> -infinity along every sequence escaping every finite subset.

Consequently F={x:LV(x)>-1} is finite. The minimal CTMC is nonexplosive in
physical time and positive recurrent on the active irreducible class.

PROOF SPINE
===========
The exact sparse-base generator and its catalyst escape split are recorded
in `leaves/constructive/REPORT.txt`, Sections 2 and 4. The local support
correction has no cross-face denominator change under a base B_i reaction:
that jump is enabled only when an A-neighbor is present, hence h_i=0 before
and after, and it leaves all other substrate coordinates unchanged.

For an added conversion/deactivation to increase any correction h_i W_i,
it must remove the unique molecule in N(i). Because its catalyst source is
order one, the source count is exactly a_j=1. Thus there is no unbounded
catalyst multiplier on a positive support-switch term. Activation has
increment at most 1-K<=0 at A=0 and cannot increase any h_i correction.

Let n=max_{i in I_+} b_i, using a common R. The finite added-channel
cofactors are O((1+n)^m). A positive support switch on a coordinate with
b_i>=n/2 is paid by that coordinate's q-removal term, of order
b_i R^(b_i); D>=m makes the correction surcharge at most O(R^(b_i)/(1+b_i)^0),
so its ratio to the q sink is O(1/b_i). A switch on b_i<n/2 is exponentially
smaller than the max-coordinate penalty. The max coordinate has an inactive
immigration penalty of order R^n/(1+n)^D when h=1, or an active q sink of
order nR^n when h=0. This also absorbs polynomial activation/K-boundary
terms. Base last-neighbor catalyst-death surcharges are paid by their same
active-edge q terms at high b_i and are bounded on low faces.

For catalyst escape with bounded substrates, each catalyst either has all
neighbor substrates zero, where chi_j gives a strict negative linear
coefficient, or has a fixed positive neighbor, where its reverse
falling-factorial term of degree k_ji+1>=2 dominates large a_j. If a
catalyst is large while an adjacent substrate diverges, its own edge
coefficient tends to minus infinity. Thus no catalyst group diverges
positively. For simultaneous substrate/catalyst escape, the max-face
penalty dominates all positive added-channel terms and each large catalyst
is controlled by its own high active edge, its zero-neighbor margin, or a
bounded-positive-neighbor factorial sink. The exact exhaustive escaping
sequence split, including heterogeneous supports, K=1, and fixed labels, is
in `leaves/constructive/REPORT.txt`.

At A=0, the exact base drift is

    alpha_sum(1-K)-sum_i alpha_i^* epsilon_i R^(b_i)/(1+b_i)^D,

and added activation jumps are nonpositive. So any escaping nonisolated
substrate coordinate forces LV->-infinity; if those counts are bounded,
only finitely many A=0 states remain. This covers K=1 without requiring a
uniform negative constant on the whole A=0 face.

Because LV<=-1 off a finite set, LV is globally bounded above by a finite C.
V-sublevel sets and their one-jump target sets are finite with bounded exit
rates. Stopped physical-time Dynkin gives
P_x(tau_L<=t)<=(V(x)+Ct)/L, which rules out explosion as L grows. The
countable-state continuous-time Foster criterion (Anderson–Kim, Theorem
3.1) then gives positive recurrence. This is a physical-time CTMC argument;
no event-chain clock substitution or ODE permanence implication is used.

verbatim, not recurrence, nonexplosion, or weakly reversible permanence.

A distinct catalyst-weighted correction now repairs a non-detailed-balanced
higher-order case. The scoped theorem, exact inactive-face condition, proof,

and `catalyst_weighted_extension/verify_catalyst_weighted_generator.py`;

For one substrate B, one catalyst A, and any finite number of independent
immigration-death co-catalysts C_k with reversible A+C_k<->C_k toggles, the
potential uses R^b[1+epsilon 1_{a=0}/(1+sum c_k)]. Under theta_*>0,
w_k mu_k>zeta_k, and delta>p(R-1), it has LV<=C-cV. The displayed
rational rate instance is weakly reversible but not complex balanced.
This is a concrete recurrence theorem beyond detailed balance, still for a
strictly restricted coupled-toggle family.

A further candidate covers source order d+1 for any fixed d>=2 in the
grammar A+dC_i<->dC_i with independent C_i immigration/death. It replaces
the denominator by 1+Q(c), Q=sum_i zeta_i(c_i)_d, and adds the catalyst
corrector a/(1+Q(c)); two exact margins control inactive-face substrate

Fixed isolated-substrate labels are essential for the sparse-graph result;

The sparse-graph result still has no promoted convergence-rate or stationary
