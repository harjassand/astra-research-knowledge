# Exact uniform base-schema certificates

7 October 2026. This supersedes the implementation limitation in the initial
Chu candidate/ledger for the seventeen nonmodal schemas assigned to this
worker. Root assigned the nine modal/weakening/inference cases to
`logic_gate14`, whose files remain separately owned.

`BASE_SCHEMA_CERTIFICATES.json` contains all seventeen base templates:
id, pf, per, fusion-to-AND, both AND projections, implication-to-AND,
both OR injections, OR-to-implication, fusion introduction/elimination,
unit, unit-to-identity, double-negation elimination, top and bottom.
Every template proves the exact Chu t-translation with D=N=bottom.

T_A,S_A,T_B,S_B,T_C,S_C are independent syntactic parameters. The only
extra initial sequents permitted by the template checker are the explicitly
declared Pair_A: T_A,S_A=>bottom and its B/C versions. They are not assumed
equal, identified with negations or treated as extra reusable hypotheses
inside a sequent. A pair used to derive a contradiction with additional
context is expanded into its named initial, the genuine bottom axiom with
that context, and one exact cut.

Search proposes a subformula proof; the separate exact multiset checker
then validates every row. Failed search branches may leave valid unused
rows. Their search-state counts and all-attempt row totals are charged;
the certificate retains the dependency subgraph of the accepted root.
Each completed finite template is a *schematic proof*: uniform formula
substitution preserves every checked rule. It therefore certifies all
instances once its pair inputs are discharged. This is stronger than a
finite collection of numerical valuations, and still does not independently
prove the cited monotone-circuit lower bound.

For an additional executable discharge check, A is a compound implication
with fusion/additives, B is a fusion of an implication and an additive
conjunction, and C is an exponential of a compound implication. Their true
Chu pairs are acquired by the ordinary recursive PairCompiler. Each named
input row is replaced by its exact cached pair proof. The whole substituted
certificate is then checked with **no named initial sequents permitted**.
The final root is compared against an independently recomputed genuine Chu
translation of the original axiom instance. Full actual operand, formula
and proof vectors are retained, not just residual/count assertions.

The largest retained base template has38 sequent rows and93 formula
occurrences. Double-negation elimination uses18 rows/36 occurrences and
one declared Pair_A; this certified implementation avoids the source's
extra negation-duality traversal. All templates are finite, fixed objects
that can be applied to arbitrary acquired operand pairs, so their stored
syntax/proof costs are bounded constants per source inference instance.
No unrestricted contraction or weakening appears in the base templates.
