## 1. Precise result and supplied boundary

There is a finite visible state space, a supplied irreducible directed baseline support, a missing u→v edge, a present reverse v→u, and reciprocal support for every other ordinary edge. Positive rational bounds q_min≤ordinary rates≤q_max and rare envelope 0<b≤1 are supplied. The baseline numerical rates and b are acquired from one passive homogeneous CTMC by full endpoint labels at calibrated adaptive lags. No resets, path jumps, supplied stationary samples, hidden clocks or unknown support are used. Arbitrarily short chosen lags are part of the ideal observation interface.

For fixed additive EP precision ε>0, fixed 0<δ≤.1, and fixed supplied support/envelopes, the revised construction gives a terminating point estimate with success probability ≥1−δ, and unconditional expected observation costs

    E N = O[b^(−L/(L−1)) {log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)}],
    E T = O[b^−1 {log(1/δ)+log log(e²/b)}],

where L≥2 is the shortest baseline u-to-v path length. Constants depend on support, rate bounds, ε and potentially very poor stationary/tree conditioning. The known-rational-baseline special case has an explicit baseline-dependent C_EP and costs multiplied by max{1,(C_EP/ε)²}. Unknown-baseline constants include its uniform counterpart and fresh nuisance calibration. These are acquisition **upper bounds**; the rare-rate lower-bound exponent is not automatically an independently gated EP minimax lower bound. A separate EP-separated two-point reduction is needed for such a claim.

This is not a hardware wallclock guarantee. Physical T is ideal CTMC observation elapsed time under the specified lag design. Arithmetic, streaming/counter work, finite sensor processing latency, actual minimal sampling time and control implementation are additional resources; a fixed positive detector dead time changes the theorem. The effective arithmetic argument establishes computability and finite termination, not free CPU time or instantaneous read processing.

## 2. EP identity and signs

Use row-generator convention πQ=0. For reciprocal positive fluxes, stationary entropy production is

    σ=Σ_i≠j π_i q_ij log[(π_i q_ij)/(π_j q_ji)].

Equivalently this is one half the oriented current-affinity sum. The stationary terms involving log π cancel: incoming and outgoing flux at each label agree. Hence σ=Σπ_iq_ij log(q_ij/q_ji). For the exceptional pair, its two directions give

    π_u b log(b/a)+π_v a log(a/b)
      =(aπ_v−bπ_u)log(a/b)=J log(a/b).

Thus the signs and normalization in both candidate notes are correct, and no missing factor two appears. Other ordinary pairs give c_i. All b>0 models have reciprocal support and finite EP. At b=0, EP itself can diverge; stationary probabilities and their sensitivities remain well defined.

## 3. Known rational baseline certificate

For Q_b=Q_0+bB, each in-tree uses the distinguished rare edge at most once. Matrix-tree weights therefore have τ_i=A_i+bB_i, A_i>0, B_i≥0. Principal cofactors of −Q_0 and −Q_1 compute A_i and A_i+B_i by finite rational determinant arithmetic. Rank-one determinant dependence supplies a separate algebraic check of affinity. Put A=ΣA_i, B=ΣB_i. Then

    π_i=(A_i+bB_i)/(A+bB),
    π_i'=(AB_i−A_iB)/(A+bB)²,
    |π_i'|≤K_i=|AB_i−A_iB|/A².

Irreducibility of Q_0 is essential to A_i>0. The claimed rational log bound is valid: for r>0, |log r|≤max(r,1/r)−1. Hence the constructed C_i≥|c_i| and L_a≥|log a|. Differentiating J=aπ_v−bπ_u gives |J'|≤H=aK_v+1+K_u and |J|≤a+1. Since b≤1 and b log(1/b)≤1,

    b|σ'|≤ΣK_iC_i+H(L_a+1)+(a+1)=C_EP.

Integrating gives |σ(x)−σ(y)|≤C_EP|log(x/y)|. If x,y≥β>0, the further bound C_EP|x−y|/β follows. Nothing requires σ to be monotone.

The known-baseline scale note proves slope r_b'(t)≥t/2 at its more restrictive lag. With s=e_relβt/4, m≥200 log(4/δ)/(e_rel²βt), p≤5βt, Bernstein's denominator is at most βt(10+e_rel/6). Its exponent is at least m e_rel²βt/(160+8e_rel/3), so the claimed δ/2 bound is conservative. Inversion probability radius τ=e_relβt/32 and parameter width e_relβ/4 give rate error at most 5e_relβ/8 on the good data event. Outside-image frequencies still lead to a positive clipped endpoint-near estimate in finitely many bisections. The easier 2e_relβ accuracy allowance yields EP statistical error ≤ε/2 for e_rel=min{1,ε/(4C_EP)}; certified EP arithmetic supplies the other ε/2.

This t/2 inverse guarantee belongs to the known-baseline construction. It is **not** transplanted into the unknown-baseline protocol, whose checked uniform slope is only t/4.

## 4. Uniform tree and nuisance constants

Let M=max(q_max,1), T_tree=n^(n−1), a_min=q_min^(n−1), and D_tree=T_tree(n−1)M^(n−2). At a fixed root there are at most T_tree candidate in-trees: each nonroot vertex chooses at most n possible outgoing destinations, and imposing the tree condition only reduces their number. Every root has at least one wholly baseline tree, hence τ_i≥a_min and S=Στ_i≥n a_min uniformly over all admitted rates and b∈[0,1].

For two admitted baselines with max ordinary-edge discrepancy Δ, telescope each tree product. Its n−1 factors are ≤M, and the rare factor is identical on both sides. Thus |τ_i−τhat_i|≤D_treeΔ. Differentiation in b removes at most one rare factor and gives |∂_bτ_i|≤D_tree. The exact quotient subtraction is

    π_i−πhat_i=(τ_i−τhat_i)/S + πhat_i(Shat−S)/S.

Using πhat_i≤1 and |Shat−S|≤nD_treeΔ gives |π_i−πhat_i|≤[(n+1)/n](D_tree/a_min)Δ≤K_πΔ for K_π=2D_tree/a_min. The same quotient derivative bound gives |∂_bπ_i|≤K_π. Interpolating ordinary rates keeps every support edge within the supplied positive envelope, so no estimated gap or hidden conditioning premise enters.

For f(x,y)=x log(x/y), |∂_xf|≤L_r+1 and |∂_yf|≤q_max/q_min, with L_r=q_max/q_min−1. Summing at most n−1 ordinary terms gives exactly the stated C_c and D_c. The current difference obeys

    |J−Jhat|≤[1+(q_max+1)K_π]Δ=C_JΔ.

For the exceptional logarithm, |log a|≤L_a and |log a−log ahat|≤Δ/q_min. Subtracting Jlog(a/b) and Jhatlog(ahat/b), and adding the ordinary contribution differences, yields

    |σ(A,b)−σ(Ahat,b)|≤[C_0+C_1 log(1/b)]Δ,

with exactly the candidate C_0,C_1. The derivative argument in §3 with K_i replaced by K_π and rates bounded by q_max gives the candidate uniform C_EP. These constants are deliberately loose but computable rational expressions. Their growth in n or q_min^−1 is not claimed efficient or benign.

The logarithmic amplification is genuine. Independently rebuilding the three-state witness with q01=λ, q10=q12=q20=q21=1, q02=b gives zero-b cofactors (3,2λ,λ). Therefore changing λ=1 to 1+θ changes the coefficient of log(1/b) by θ/[6(2+θ)]. The ordinary log terms stay bounded, and O(b) stationary coefficient changes times log(1/b) vanish. This proves the claimed limit, rather than merely observing it on a finite grid. The witness explains why reusing the earlier constant-accuracy calibration would not solve additive EP acquisition.

## 5. Revised final protocol and complete confidence ledger

Run the already gated acquired-rates-and-scale search with e=min{1/4,ε/(4C_EP)} and budget δ/2. It produces random K, β=2^(−K−1), t comparable to β^(1/(L−1)), and a covered bracket [β,min(4β,1)] except on its allocated failure event. Its unconditional stopping bound is retained, not conditioned on coverage.

Use D_mix=2d_max, distinct from D_tree, and a certified finite upper bound for Astar. Acquire **new** ordinary rates at precision

    Δ≤min{1, e/[32(Astar+8D_mix)],
                 ε/[4(C_0+C_1 log(1/β))]}.

Choose the dyadic Δ within a factor two of this minimum after replacing log(1/β) by a certified upper bound ℓ≤log(1/β)+1. This lower bound on Δ is important for the cost argument; an arbitrarily overprecise choice would not justify the stated upper complexity. A simpler valid cost-equivalent implementation can use the known integer K+1≥log(1/β), avoiding a logarithm calculation for this selection alone. Proper dyadic rounding then gives Δ=Θ(1/(K+1)) at fixed supplied/accuracy constants.

Allocate δ/4 to this fresh calibration. Fresh rare counts have m_final≥32768 log(8/δ)/(e²βt). On a covered bracket, p≤5βt. Bernstein at deviation eβt/32 requires coefficient at most 10240+(64/3)e; 32768 safely exceeds it. The rare-block error is ≤δ/4 conditional on the entire retained past.

On accurate fresh calibration, support-sensitive nuisance suppression and b≤4β give

    |r_A,b−r_Ahat,b|≤Δ(Astar+8D_mix)βt≤eβt/32.

The ideal clipped model inverse therefore has rate error ≤(4/t)(eβt/16)=eβ/4. With probability enclosure radius ξ=eβt/64, a tolerance-triggered return has residual ≤2ξ and lies within 8ξ/t=eβ/8 of the clipped inverse. Width stopping at eβ/2 gives at most eβ/4. The conservative eβ/2 numerical allowance is valid. Thus total rate error ≤3eβ/4. This is valid even if the empirical frequency is slightly outside the model image on a good event, since projection onto the image shrinks its distance to the true model value.

Since both returned x and true b are ≥β on the covered event, the EP error ledger is

    rare-rate error ≤3C_EP e/4≤3ε/16,
    nuisance error ≤[C_0+C_1 log(1/β)]Δ≤ε/4,
    final certified arithmetic ≤ε/2.

Total ≤15ε/16<ε. Failure budgets δ/2+δ/4+δ/4 sum to δ. These are history-uniform conditional guarantees plus a union bound; independence of the adaptively selected earlier calibration is neither asserted nor needed. Certified deterministic arithmetic consumes accuracy slack, not a statistical failure budget.

## 6. Fresh nuisance acquisition and no-reset costs

With R_*=(n−1)q_max+1 and J_*=2R_*², every admitted true generator has ||Q||_∞≤2R_* and stochastic semigroup norm one. The integral Taylor remainder gives |P(s)_ij/s−q_ij|≤J_*s. Choose s=min(t,1,Δ/(2J_*)). The deterministic ordinary-rate bias is at most Δ/2; the rare edge is excluded from ordinary estimates, not assumed absent from the actual data generator.

Every off-diagonal endpoint probability is ≤R_*s, by the chance of any actual jump. Bernstein at Δs/2 has exponent at least m_0Δ²s/(8R_*+4Δ/3). Taking the stated m_0 with log(2n²/δ_0) and union bounding the supplied ordinary edges gives the correct simultaneous precision. Clipping to the supplied bounds shrinks error and produces an admitted Ahat even on failure paths.

Choose baseline paths between every pair. The probability of exactly the prescribed path by time one and no extra jump is at least exp(−R_*)q_min^d/d!. A computable strictly positive minorization η exists uniformly over the supplied class. It gives π_i≥η, since π=πP(1), and P(h) entries≥η for h≥1 by semigroup composition. For a desired source at lag s, inspect blocks of ceil(1/s) reads; every block-end source-hit probability is at least η conditional on the past. Expected initial acquisition is at most ceil(1/s)/η reads.

Once at the source, complete each sampled return cycle before the next departure. Strong Markov makes the first endpoints iid row-law draws. Kac's mean cycle length is 1/π_i≤1/η, including one-step self-return cycles. A first endpoint can correlate with its cycle duration; linear expected-cost summation does not assume independence between endpoint and duration. m useful departures therefore cost at most m/η additional reads. All non-useful reads and complete returns are charged, and a new source acquisition is charged for each row block.

Consequently fresh ordinary calibration has expected reads

    O[log(n/δ_0)/(Δ² min(t,Δ))]

and physical time O[log(n/δ_0)/Δ²], uniformly conditional on every possible stopping history. The initial-hit time is bounded by a constant per row and is included. Neither stationary preparation nor a free reset is inserted.

## 7. Random stages, failed calibration and empty-set paths

Let k_* be the first scale stage β_k≤b/2. At later reached stages, inaccurate calibration costs at most δ_k, and continuation on accurate calibration costs at most δ_k^(2b/β_k). Thus the history-uniform unconditional continuation bound is ≤2δ_k, and iteration gives

    Pr(reach k_*+j)≤(δ_search/8)^j/(j!)², j≥1.

Wrong early stopping is a possible accuracy failure but ends at finite cost. Failed calibrations followed by continuation are explicitly in this tail. Conditional stage costs are bounded uniformly because empirical generators are always clipped into the envelope, regardless of their statistical accuracy.

At selected stage K, Δ^−1=O(K+1), t_K^−1=O(2^(K/(L−1))). The fresh nuisance read envelope is

    O[2^(K/(L−1))(K+1)²+(K+1)³],

and its time envelope is O((K+1)²). The fresh rare block has order 2^(KL/(L−1)) reads and 2^K time, with fixed e,δ constants. For tail j after k_*, all these fixed exponential-polynomial envelopes multiply the factorial reach tail and are summable. Before k_* their sums have at most the last-scale orders. Nuisance costs are lower polynomial order than rare reads/time as b→0, and can be absorbed into fixed-constant full upper bounds on 0<b≤1. This proves **unconditional** expectation bounds, including failure paths, not merely expectation conditioned on confidence coverage.

The adopted point protocol has no confidence-set intersection loop: its prescribed final blocks are finite, bracket nonempty, fitted model valid, inverse monotone, returned x≥β>0, and EP arithmetic terminates on every finite reached K. Hence bad calibration, a wrong bracket and z=0 or z=1 do not produce an infinite refinement demand. The separate interval-refinement variant exits immediately with `INCONSISTENT/EMPTY` if intersections become empty. Such an output is a declared failure, not a covered EP point estimate. The fixed point proof does not depend on that variant's stopping time.

## 8. Arithmetic and replay scope

Positive-rational logs are effectively enclosed by binary range reduction and the atanh series. For x∈[1,2), z=(x−1)/(x+1)∈[0,1/3), and after m terms the remaining positive tail is at most 2z^(2m+1)/[(2m+1)(1−z²)]. A signed k log 2 interval must reverse its endpoints when k<0; both the candidate and independent checker do so. Multiplying by rational current or other signed coefficients likewise reverses endpoints. Range reduction is logarithmic in the magnitude ratio, not polynomial in 1/b. Finite rational coefficients allow positive arithmetic tolerances to be allocated; geometric tails imply termination. The π computations are rational and exact for the empirical rational model.

The candidate uniformization enclosure is valid on its fixture. Its comment that exp(x)<2 for every x<1 is inaccurate, but its actual tail bound 2 times the next factorial term remains valid: after that next term the successive ratios are at most x/(j+2)<1/2. This is a comment-level issue, not a failed numerical certificate or theorem. The candidate's diagnostic caps of 10000 terms are fixture caps; universal effective evaluation uses increasing truncation until the certified tolerance is reached, not an unexplained fixed cap.

Our probability enclosure instead uses the direct signed matrix Taylor series with a rational infinity-norm factorial/geometric tail, avoiding the candidate uniformization implementation. Exact fixtures pass four affine cofactor/stationarity models (n=3,…,6), three perturbed-envelope models, rare sensitivity at b=1,1/8,10^−20, nuisance intervals down to b=10^−25, the symbolic logarithmic witness, and three clipped-inverse branches including frequencies zero and one. Signed log range reduction was exercised at 10^±1000. Interval comparisons use exact rational outward bounds; no sampled derivative grid is treated as a proof. Initial checker problems were symbolic expression-format equality and Python's integer decimal-printing cap; both were corrected without changing any claimed bound. Large log interval widths are recorded by numerator/denominator bit lengths to avoid giant decimal output.

## 9. Consequence, priors and remaining scientific gap

This closes the specified EP **upper acquisition interface**: numerical ordinary rates and rare scale are obtained from data, logarithmic EP nuisance amplification is explicitly paid for, and finite arithmetic/failure-path costs are supplied. Known support, a positive ordinary-rate gap, reciprocal ordinary pairs, visible labels, homogeneous Markov dynamics and calibrated arbitrarily short lags remain structural promises. No conclusion transfers to hidden states, unknown shortcut edges, a second vanishing ordinary rate, sensor label error, or fixed minimum lag.

Matrix-tree representation, Kac regeneration, log enclosure and stationary current-affinity EP are established tools. Primary prior-art witnesses are Schnakenberg (1976) for the master-equation network framework and Pitman–Tang (2016 v2) for the tree/Kac identities. Existing endpoint-generator inference is an equally informed competent comparator. Historical novelty of the support-dependent acquisition theorem remains unresolved; this audit proves no capability advantage over a competent estimator with the same interface.

The next falsifiable gap is an independently verified end-to-end adaptive passive trajectory implementation with all acquisition/read costs and its finite arithmetic ledger, followed by either a genuine same-interface advantage or a source-level prior-art identification. A separate two-point reduction would be required before declaring the EP upper exponents minimax optimal. Diagnostics, worker agreement and byte hashes do not supply either conclusion.
