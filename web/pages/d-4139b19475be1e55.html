## F1. Exact question and supplied interface

Keep the actual finite system Hamiltonian H, the physical Hermitian system couplings A_alpha, and the reservoir spectra gamma_alpha fixed. The old decoder gives a whole-Hilbert-space Pauli algebra O_j and a protected syndrome projector, but generally [H,O_j] is only small in the CURRENT Gibbs norm. Does replacing exact Bohr-frequency resolution by a positive finite-window kernel obtain useful logical leakage, while preserving Gibbs stationarity and a justified microscopic approximation on the desired storage interval?

Write rho=exp(-beta H)/Z and ||X||_(rho,2)^2=Tr rho X†X. Assume independent channels, gamma_alpha>=0, gamma_alpha<=Gamma_alpha, and the lowering-frequency convention

    [H,A_omega]=-omega A_omega,
    gamma(-omega)=exp(-beta omega)gamma(omega).

For any real time filter f in L1 and L2 with integral |f|²=1, define

    L_(alpha,nu) = sqrt(gamma_alpha(nu)/(2pi))
                  integral_R f(t) exp(i nu t) A_alpha(t) dt,
    A_alpha(t)=exp(iHt) A_alpha exp(-iHt).

The dissipator is the integral of D[L]=L(.)L†-{L†L,(.)}/2. A rectangular normalized f gives the standard dynamical coarse-graining jump formula. A Gaussian f gives a different smoothing prescription. Both are changed generators; neither is asserted equal to the original Davies dynamics or automatically a valid all-time microscopic approximation.

## F2. Positivity and two exact frequency-free bounds

The definition is a continuous positive sum of GKLS dissipators. Operator Plancherel, applied to every vector, gives

    integral L†L dnu <= Gamma ||A||² I.                  (F2a)

Indeed, drop gamma<=Gamma and use integral |f|² A(t)†A(t)dt <=||A||² I. Consequently the CP-map part has diamond norm at most Gamma||A||² and the anticommutator has the same bound. Thus

    ||D_f||_diamond <= 2 Gamma ||A||².                 (F2b)

No number of Bohr frequencies appears.

For a Hermitian O, applying ordinary Hilbert-space Plancherel to [O,A(t)]sqrt(rho) yields

    k_f^+ := integral Tr rho [O,L_nu]†[O,L_nu] dnu
         <= Gamma integral |f(t)|²
                       ||[O,A(t)]||_(rho,2)² dt.       (F2c)

The opposite orientation has the same upper bound. Instantaneously [O,A(t)] is anti-Hermitian; after weighting by the nonconstant gamma the Fourier jumps need not individually have equal orientations. The statement here only uses the common upper bound obtained by dropping gamma before Plancherel.

## F3. A rejected weighted propagation step and a legal conditional repair

The implication

    ||[O,A(t)]||_(rho,2)
       <= ||[O,A]||_(rho,2)+2||A|| |t| ||[H,O]||_(rho,2)

is NOT established. Left multiplication is bounded in this norm, but right multiplication generally is not. For rho=diag(1-p,p), X=diag(0,1), and A equal to Pauli X, ||XA||_(rho,2)^2=1-p while ||X||_(rho,2)^2=p.

A precise sufficient extra input is the acquired modular bound

    m_A=||rho^(-1/2) A rho^(1/2)||.

It gives A rho A†<=m_A²rho and ||XA||_(rho,2)<=m_A||X||_(rho,2). Conjugation by exp(iHt) is an isometry in this norm. Integrating the derivative of O(-t) therefore gives the valid conditional estimate

    ||[O,A(t)]||_(rho,2)
       <= c_A+(||A||+m_A)|t| c_H,                    (F3a)
    c_A=||[O,A]||_(rho,2), c_H=||[H,O]||_(rho,2).

For the Gaussian filter in F4, integral t²|f|²=T²/2. Minkowski then yields

    sqrt(k_f^+) <= sqrt(Gamma)
                [c_A+(||A||+m_A)T c_H/sqrt(2)],       (F3b)

and the same bound for the opposite orientation. This is conditional on m_A, not a new uniform estimate for the actual low-temperature model.

A bound on ||AHA-H|| alone does not acquire m_A by a noncommutative Gibbs-order argument. For E>1, take H=diag(0,E) and the Hermitian unitary

    A_E = 1/(E²+1) [[E²-1, 2E], [2E, 1-E²]].

Then ||A_E H A_E-H||=2E²/(E²+1)<2, but m_A is at least 2E exp(beta E/2)/(E²+1), which diverges. This example refutes that inference; it is not a fixed-local-intensity lattice construction.

An alternative is actual real-time locality plus an independently proved CURRENT-Gibbs eroded decoder tail for the entire local support. Root and rounding_spectral have now developed that latter tail via almost-excited witness sets, without a Gibbs-order comparison. It is not licensed merely by the old commuting-H translation inequality.

## F4. A physical thermal Gaussian reservoir with finite correlation time

Fix beta,sigma,Gamma,T>0. Keep the same physical coupling A and use

    f_T(t)=(pi T²)^(-1/4) exp[-t²/(2T²)],
    gamma(nu)=Gamma exp[-(nu-beta sigma²/2)²/(2sigma²)].

The spectrum is nonnegative and bounded by Gamma. Direct division gives gamma(-nu)=exp(-beta nu)gamma(nu). With C(t)=(2pi)^(-1)integral gamma(nu)exp(-i nu t)dnu,

    C(t)=Gamma sigma/sqrt(2pi)
         exp[-sigma²t²/2] exp[-i beta sigma²t/2].      (F4a)

Thus the correlation and all its time moments are integrable. One standard continuum free-boson realization has nonnegative spectral density J(nu)=gamma(nu)(1-exp(-beta nu))/(2pi) for nu>0, with a thermal KMS reservoir. This specifies a compatible physical two-point function; it is not a proof of the resulting reduced dynamics on arbitrary long times. A coupling strength g restores an overall g² in the second-order generator.

T is a Gaussian width, not a literal compact time cutoff. The exact filter has infinite time support; any finite implementation must explicitly truncate its Gaussian tail. This distinction matters when comparing to a microscopic finite observation interval.

The frequency window has unit mass

    |F_T(nu-omega)|²/(2pi)
       =(T/sqrt(pi)) exp[-T²(nu-omega)²].

Completing the square gives the exact smeared transition rate

    r_T(omega)=Gamma sqrt[2sigma²T²/(1+2sigma²T²)]
              exp[-T²(omega-beta sigma²/2)²/(1+2sigma²T²)].

Therefore

    r_T(-omega)/r_T(omega)=exp(-beta_eff omega),
    beta_eff=beta*2sigma²T²/(1+2sigma²T²)<beta.         (F4b)

The physical spectrum obeys beta-KMS, but its finite-window convolution does not. This is an exact algebraic loss of thermal stationarity.

## F5. Same logical seed as the accepted secular obstruction

On logical and gauge qubits, set

    B=Z_L tensor X_G, H_delta=delta B,
    A=I_L tensor Z_G, O=X_L tensor I_G,
    P_±=(I±B)/2, J_+=P_- A, J_-=P_+ A.

The old physical A commutes with the entire original logical Pauli algebra. J_+ lowers energy by omega=2delta. A(t)=exp(-i omega t)J_++exp(i omega t)J_-.

For delta>0, the filtered dissipator has coefficient matrix

    [[r_T(omega), c_T],[c_T, r_T(-omega)]],
    c_T=exp(-T²omega²) r_T(0).                       (F5a)

It is positive because it is the Gram integral of the actual filtered amplitudes. This is not a guessed interpolation between jump operators.

Let rho_beta=(I-tanh(beta delta)B)/4. The cross terms vanish on this B-diagonal state. Exact multiplication gives

    D_T(rho_beta)
      = [r_T(omega)+r_T(-omega)]
         [tanh(beta delta)-tanh(beta_eff delta)] B/4, (F5b)

and hence

    ||D_T(rho_beta)||_1
      = [r_T(omega)+r_T(-omega)]
         |tanh(beta delta)-tanh(beta_eff delta)|>0.   (F5c)

This defect is diagonal in the energy eigenbasis. For EVERY Hermitian K, every diagonal entry of [K,rho_beta] vanishes. Thus no Hamiltonian correction, including a proposed Lamb shift, can make rho_beta stationary while retaining this dissipator. Correcting only the coherent term is insufficient unless the transition part already satisfies the appropriate balance relation.

For the original logical O, [O,J_+] and [O,J_-] are opposite unitaries. Consequently the exact weighted leakage is scalar and independent of rho:

    k_T=r_T(omega)+r_T(-omega)-2exp(-T²omega²)r_T(0).

Since ||[O,A(t)]||_(rho,2)^2=4 sin²(omega t), F2 gives

    0<=k_T<=2Gamma[1-exp(-omega²T²)]
             <=8Gamma delta²T².                    (F5d)

This restores continuity as delta->0 at fixed T. At fixed delta≠0, sending T->infinity gives the exact frequency-resolved leakage r_infty(omega)+r_infty(-omega); sending delta->0 afterward gives 2gamma(0)>0. The opposite order gives zero. A smooth bounded KMS spectrum does not remove the order-of-limits distinction.

## F6. What this proves and what it does not

F2, F3 conditional on its modular input, and F4–F5 are analytic statements for all stated parameters. The included symbolic 4x4 replay verifies transcription only; it does not replace the derivation.

The counterexample proves that positivity, true reservoir KMS, finite correlation time, unchanged physical A and suppressed logical leakage do not collectively imply exact stationarity of the original Gibbs state. It does not prove failure of decoded memory: auxiliary motion can be irrelevant after recovery. A balanced-sector or logical-channel proof may use less than global approximate stationarity.

For the actual full-density positive-X Hamiltonian, the equilibrium witness is accepted. A physical microscopic bridge still owes: the full reservoir/coupling model; a justified generator including its actual coherent term; errors uniform in system size and on the requested storage interval; a logical estimate valid for the CURRENT Gibbs weights; and a charged preparation/recovery interface. Global generator norm continuity and a fixed finite-time weak-coupling limit do not supply these debts.

The engineered CKG generator separately addresses stationarity by CHANGING the transition weights and adding a specified coherent correction. In our lowering convention its basic gamma is exp[-(beta nu-1)²/2], which obeys a 2beta rate ratio before Gaussian smearing. With T=beta/sqrt(2), the smearing produces the target beta ratio. It is therefore materially different from a beta-thermal bath spectrum with the same filter.
