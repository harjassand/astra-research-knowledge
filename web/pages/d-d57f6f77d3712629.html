STATUS

Independently derived before reading any Cycle07 sibling proof or report.
The two proposed inequalities are true. The general case admits the
slightly stronger correction C_B(kappa)+C_C(kappa), rather than 2C_BC(kappa).
This is an internal theorem-only audit. No historical novelty, external
certification, implementation, or general both-small-CMI theorem is claimed.
Only finite-dimensional systems are considered in this baseline.

STATEMENT, QUANTIFIERS, AND CONVENTIONS

Fix finite A,B,C and fixed orthonormal product bases on B and C. Let E_B,
E_C be the corresponding full dephasing channels on their individual
systems, acting as identity on all other systems; they commute. E=E_B E_C.
For every density rho_ABC, put kappa=rho_BC, sigma=E(rho), and
 delta_B=I(A:C|B)_rho, delta_C=I(A:B|C)_rho.
All entropies and relative entropies use NATURAL logarithms, and
 T(x,y)=||x-y||_1/2.
Define C_X(omega)=D(omega||E_X omega)=S(E_X omega)-S(omega), X=B,C,BC.
These quantities are finite, including rank-deficient states.

Then
 D(rho||sigma)=C_BC(rho)
 <=delta_B+delta_C+C_B(kappa)+C_C(kappa)
 <=delta_B+delta_C+2D(kappa||E kappa).                (G)

If kappa is classical-classical in the fixed product basis, then
 C_B(kappa)=C_C(kappa)=0, sigma_BC=kappa, sigma_A=rho_A, and
 D(rho||sigma)<=delta_B+delta_C,
 T(rho,sigma)<=min(1,sqrt((delta_B+delta_C)/2)).      (CC)
Sigma is classical on both B and C and fully separable across A:B:C.
One fixed E works for all references and all extensions of a fixed
classical-classical kappa once its product basis is selected. The map
acts jointly on BC; this statement does not claim B-alone access.

PROOF

1. Pinching identity and support. For any finite pinching E_X, log(E_X
omega) lies in its block algebra, so Tr omega log(E_X omega)=Tr E_X omega
log(E_X omega), giving the stated entropy difference. This identity
remains valid on the support. Indeed, if v is in ker(E_X omega), each
nonnegative term <P_j v,omega P_j v> is zero. Thus omega^(1/2)P_j v=0
for every block, hence omega v=0; supp(omega) is contained in supp(E_X
omega). No inverse on a zero eigenspace or faithfulness assumption enters.

2. Exact CMI differences. E_C leaves rho_AB and rho_B unchanged. Direct
substitution into the finite entropy formula gives
 I(A:C|B)_rho-I(A:C|B)_(E_C rho)
 =C_C(rho)-C_C(kappa).                              (1)
Strong subadditivity says the second CMI is nonnegative, hence
 C_C(rho)<=delta_B+C_C(kappa).                       (2)
Similarly E_B leaves rho_AC and rho_C unchanged, and
 C_B(rho)<=delta_C+C_B(kappa).                       (3)
No assertion that dephasing a conditioning system decreases CMI is used.

3. Combine the coherences. Telescoping the entropy differences gives
 C_BC(rho)=C_C(rho)+C_B(E_C rho).
Relative-entropy data processing under E_C, together with E_B E_C=E_C E_B,
gives
 C_B(E_C rho)=D(E_C rho||E_C E_B rho)
            <=D(rho||E_B rho)=C_B(rho).
Therefore C_BC(rho)<=C_C(rho)+C_B(rho). Applying Eq2-3 proves the first
bound in (G). Each C_B(kappa), C_C(kappa) is at most C_BC(kappa), by
telescoping and nonnegativity of the remaining dephasing cost. This
proves the second bound. The argument is for every rho simultaneously.

4. Separability and exact marginals. Write
 a_bc=<b,c|rho|b,c>_BC>=0 as an operator on A. Then
 sigma=sum_bc a_bc tensor |b><b| tensor |c><c|,
 sum_bc a_bc=rho_A, Tr a_bc=<b,c|kappa|b,c>.
Nonzero a_bc can be normalized to density operators and their traces
sum to1, so sigma is a finite convex mixture of product states. Its A
marginal is exactly rho_A and its BC marginal is E kappa. Thus the BC
marginal is exactly kappa in (CC); it need not be exact in general (G).

5. Trace conversion. Quantum data processing to the two-outcome Helstrom
measurement gives D(rho||sigma)>=d_bin(p||q), where p-q=T(rho,sigma).
For 0<q<1, the binary relative entropy as a function of p has value0
and derivative0 at p=q and second derivative 1/[p(1-p)]>=4. Integrating
twice yields d_bin(p||q)>=2(p-q)^2. Endpoints follow by continuity (or
are infinite when supports disagree). Thus D>=2T^2 in nats, proving
(CC). The same calculation gives a trace bound from (G), but only its
classical-classical specialization preserves the whole BC marginal.

CONTROLS AND CLAIM BOUNDARY

GHZ control: rho=|000+111><000+111|/2. Kappa is diagonal on 00,11,
delta_B=delta_C=ln2, and C_BC(rho)=ln2, consistent with (CC).
Entangled-AB control: maximally entangled AB of rank d, tensor a fixed
C-basis state. Kappa is classical-classical, delta_B=0, delta_C=2ln d,
and C_BC(rho)=ln d. Rank-deficient kappa is legal.
General correction control: independent A tensor a pure Bell BC state
has both CMIs zero but C_BC(rho)=C_BC(kappa)=ln2>0. The correction cannot
simply be discarded for a nonclassical marginal. Here C_B(kappa)+C_C(kappa)
=2C_BC(kappa), so the local-coherence bound is not always smaller than
the advertised correction; no optimality claim is made.

Both CMIs zero and kappa classical-classical force rho=sigma exactly.
This remains a scoped finite classical-marginal statement. The output
dephasing consumes BC jointly, not solely B, and has no general exact
BC-marginal guarantee when kappa is nonclassical. General dimension-free
classicality, readable-label robustness, and historical priority are not
upgraded by this calculation. SSA and relative-entropy data processing
are standard imported mathematical facts; their published proofs are not
reproduced here. The entropy identities, support argument, combination,
quantifiers, controls, and trace normalization are reconstructed here.
