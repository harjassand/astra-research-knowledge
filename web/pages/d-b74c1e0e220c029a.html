CYCLE05 THEOREM-FIRST ANTISYMMETRIC LOCAL LADDER
2026-10-09. broadcasting_blind_review. Sol/max, no descendants.

VERDICT: PROVED independently, before candidate or local-reference proof
exposure. The quantifiers in both claims are met by one explicit family.
The joint local-EB error and conditional-state replacement error are
different quantities; their separation is mathematically consistent.
Historical priority and external validation remain UNKNOWN. No fixed-I

1. EXACT INTERFACE AND STATE

Use natural logarithms and T(sigma,tau)=||sigma-tau||_1/2. Given an integer
L>=2, put D=2^L, H=C^D, A=H and n_j=2^j for j=0,...,L-1. Define
 B_j=wedge^{n_j} H,   B=direct_sum_{j=0}^{L-1} B_j.
All spaces are finite. The direct-sum labels are mutually orthogonal and
form part of B, not a hidden supplied classical side register.

Use normalized determinant vectors |S> for subsets S of the fixed basis
of H. The ordinary antisymmetric subspace wedge^{n+1} H is isometrically
embedded in H tensor wedge^n H by separating the first particle from the
other n. Let P_{n+1} be its orthogonal projector in that ambient space and
 omega_n=P_{n+1}/binom(D,n+1).
Embed omega_{n_j} in A tensor B_j and define
 rho_AB=(1/L) direct_sum_j omega_{n_j}.                         (1)
This is a density operator of rank sum_j binom(D,n_j+1). Its marginals are
 rho_A=I_H/D,
 rho_B=(1/L) direct_sum_j I_{B_j}/binom(D,n_j).                 (2)

Here and below local broadcasting means one full-domain CPTP map
mathcal B:B->B tensor B, with both marginal output spaces the original B.
No state-dependent nonlinear operation or separate marginal choices are
allowed. Local EB error means infimum over all full-domain EB channels
Lambda:B->B. Both assertions below use exactly this interface.

2. ANTISYMMETRIC IDENTITIES, REPROVED

For disjoint ordered subsets T,U with union S, let epsilon(T,U) be the
sign of the permutation that reorders their concatenation into S. The
standard grouping isometry for N=s+t particles is
 |S> -> binom(N,s)^(-1/2)
        sum_{T subset S, |T|=s} epsilon(T,S\T)|T> tensor |S\T>. (3)
Its terms are orthogonal and each coefficient magnitude is the same.
Different S have disjoint unions of output labels, so (3) is isometric.

The reduced state of the uniform normalized N-particle projector on s
particles is the uniform normalized s-particle projector. Indeed each
fixed s-subset T occurs in binom(D-s,N-s) determinant basis states, and
 its weight is binom(D-s,N-s)/[binom(D,N)binom(N,s)]
              =1/binom(D,s).                                  (4)
This proves (2) and every reduction used below without assuming an
unproved representation-theoretic assertion.

For a unit vector psi in H, let Q_{psi,n} project onto wedge^n(psi-perp)
inside wedge^n H. Then the exact operator identity is
 ( <psi| tensor I ) P_{n+1} ( |psi> tensor I )
       =Q_{psi,n}/(n+1).                                      (5)
To check it, rotate psi to the first basis vector. A determinant |T> on
the other factor containing that vector is killed by antisymmetrizing.
For every T not containing it, the overlap with the normalized
(n+1)-particle determinant |{psi} union T> has magnitude 1/sqrt(n+1).
The resulting compression is diagonal in this determinant basis. Basis
rotation gives (5) for every unit psi. Its rank is binom(D-1,n).

3. ONE EXPLICIT FULL-DOMAIN BROADCASTER

For j>=1 write n_j=2m, where m=n_{j-1}. Let V_j:B_j->B_{j-1} tensor
B_{j-1} be the grouping isometry (3) with s=t=m, followed by the fixed
output block inclusions into B tensor B. Let Pi_j be the block projection
on the input B. Write sigma_0=I_{B_0}/D, included in the output block 0.
Define on every operator X in M(B)
 mathcal B(X)=sum_{j=1}^{L-1} V_j Pi_j X Pi_j V_j^dagger
                 +Tr(Pi_0 X)(sigma_0 tensor sigma_0).           (6)
Each term is CP. The isometries preserve each block trace and the final
term is a CP replacement channel, so their sum is TP on the FULL input
algebra. Off-diagonal block coherences are measured away. In particular,
the operation is defined on arbitrary input states, not just the listed
rho_B blocks. An explicit bottom-block Kraus representation has
operators D^(-1)|r,s><t| for r,s,t in the B_0 basis; the other Kraus
operators are V_j Pi_j. Their squared Gram sum is sum_j Pi_j=I_B.

Swapping the two outputs of V_j multiplies it by the scalar
(-1)^{m^2}; this scalar cancels in V_j X V_j^dagger. The bottom replacement
is also swap invariant. Thus both marginals mathcal B_1 and mathcal B_2
coincide as full-domain channels, not just on rho.

By (4), the output on rho's block j>=1, after retaining A and either
half, is exactly omega_{n_{j-1}} in A tensor output block j-1. On the
bottom input block the output is (I_A/D) tensor sigma_0. Hence for both
receivers ell=1,2,
 (id_A tensor mathcal B_ell)(rho)-rho
       =[(I_A/D) tensor sigma_0 - omega_{n_{L-1}}]/L,            (7)
where the positive term is in B block 0 and the negative term in block
L-1. These blocks differ because L>=2. Both terms have trace one and
orthogonal support, so the half trace norm in (7) is EXACTLY 1/L.
Consequently
 b_loc(rho)<=1/L.                                              (8)
No claim that this upper bound equals the infimum is needed.

4. JOINT LOCAL EB ERROR IS AT LEAST ONE HALF

Let P=direct_sum_j P_{n_j+1} on A tensor B. It is an effect, 0<=P<=I,
and Tr(P rho)=1. For every unit product vector a tensor b, decompose
b=direct_sum_j b_j. Identity (5) gives
 <a,b|P|a,b>=sum_j <b_j|Q_{a,n_j}|b_j>/(n_j+1)
             <=sum_j ||b_j||^2/(n_j+1)<=1/2.                   (9)
This includes product vectors coherent across B's block labels. By
convexity every separable state sigma on A tensor B has Tr(P sigma)<=1/2.

For every full-domain EB channel Lambda:B->B, the state
(id_A tensor Lambda)(rho) is separable. The variational trace-distance
bound with effect P therefore yields
 T((id_A tensor Lambda)(rho),rho)
       >=Tr[P(rho-(id_A tensor Lambda)(rho))]>=1/2.             (10)
Taking the infimum over all such Lambda proves
 e_loc(rho)>=1/2.                                              (11)
The witness applies even if Lambda creates output block coherences or
changes the block weights. No restriction to a finite EB dictionary or
to channels preserving the direct-sum sectors was imposed.

5. EXACT MUTUAL INFORMATION AND ITS ASYMPTOTIC

By the uniform projector spectra and orthogonal block labels,
 S(rho_A)=ln D,
 S(rho_B)=ln L+(1/L)sum_j ln binom(D,n_j),
 S(rho_AB)=ln L+(1/L)sum_j ln binom(D,n_j+1).
Using binom(D,n)/binom(D,n+1)=(n+1)/(D-n), this gives
 I(A:B)_rho=(1/L)sum_{j=0}^{L-1}
                     [ln(n_j+1)-ln(1-n_j/D)]
   =(L-1)ln 2/2 + delta_L,                                    (12)
 delta_L=(1/L)[sum_{j=0}^{L-1}ln(1+2^(-j))
                    +sum_{m=1}^{L}-ln(1-2^(-m))].             (13)
The correction is positive. Since ln(1+x)<=x for x>=0 and
-ln(1-x)<=x/(1-x)<=2x for 0<=x<=1/2, each sum in brackets is less
than two. Thus
 0<delta_L<4/L.                                               (14)
This proves the requested asymptotic with an explicit uniform constant.

For every alpha>1 and every fixed finite K>=0, (8), (11), and (14) give
 e_loc(rho)^2>=1/4,
 K(I(A:B)_rho+1)b_loc(rho)^alpha
  <= K[((L-1)ln2/2)+1+4/L]L^(-alpha) ->0.                      (15)
For sufficiently large L this violates any claimed universal inequality
e_loc^2<=K(I+1)b_loc^alpha. If b_loc vanishes, the violation is immediate.
This statement does NOT contradict a modulus with fixed information
I, nor decide the exponent-one case: this family's information grows
linearly with L, and its supplied broadcasting error is only an upper
bound on the infimum.

6. THE COMPLETE RANK-ONE A-CONDITIONAL STATE FAMILY

Conditioning rho on any nonzero rank-one A effect proportional to
|psi><psi| gives the normalized B state
 sigma_psi=(1/L) direct_sum_j
                    Q_{psi,n_j}/binom(D-1,n_j).                (16)
Indeed (5) and binom(D-1,n)=((n+1)/D)binom(D,n+1) show each normalized
block has rank-one outcome probability 1/D, independent of j and psi.
Thus conditioning keeps the block weights exactly 1/L. This includes
ALL unit psi, not just a fixed orthonormal basis or a selected ensemble.

Define the ONE fixed reconstruction state
 sigma_bar=(1/L) direct_sum_j I_{B_j}/binom(D,n_j),              (17)
and the replacement channel R(X)=Tr(X)sigma_bar on the full algebra M(B).
It is CPTP and EB. A uniform rank-r projector state differs from the
ambient uniform rank-R state by half trace norm 1-r/R. Here
 binom(D-1,n_j)/binom(D,n_j)=(D-n_j)/D,
so for every psi, including arbitrary phases and bases,
 T(sigma_psi,sigma_bar)
   =(1/L)sum_j n_j/D=(D-1)/(LD)<1/L.                           (18)
Hence this single replacement reconstruction has the requested uniform
conditional-state error. Applied to rho itself it instead produces the
product rho_A tensor sigma_bar, which is covered by the joint lower
bound (10). There is no implication from (18) to a trace-distance bound
with a quantum reference retained.

7. EXACT MAX-PRIOR HOLEVO CAPACITY

For every prior distribution on the whole family (16), all conditional
states have the same entropy
 S(sigma_psi)=ln L+(1/L)sum_j ln binom(D-1,n_j).
Every average conditional state has block weights 1/L, so its entropy is
at most ln L+(1/L)sum_j ln binom(D,n_j). Consequently the Holevo quantity
for any prior is at most
 c_L=(1/L)sum_j ln[binom(D,n_j)/binom(D-1,n_j)]
     =(1/L)sum_{m=1}^{L}-ln(1-2^(-m))<2/L.                    (19)

This bound is attained by the uniform prior on ANY fixed orthonormal
basis {e_a}_{a=1}^D of A. For the determinant basis on B_j,
 sum_a Q_{e_a,n_j}=(D-n_j)I_{B_j};
after normalizing this makes (1/D)sum_a sigma_{e_a}=sigma_bar. Thus
 max_prior chi({sigma_psi})=c_L<2/L.                          (20)
It is a genuine maximum attained by a finite legal A measurement, not
an upper bound for one chosen prior and not just a Haar-limit supremum.
