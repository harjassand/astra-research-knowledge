RESULT: THE UNIVERSAL TOTAL-VARIATION PREMISE IS FALSE
7 October 2026. Direct construction; independent algebra/geometry audit by chain_attack.

Claim refuted (even when C may depend on the entire fixed source):
  integral |T_mu-T_nu|^2 d rho <= C_rho TV(mu,nu)
for every compactly supported target pair, where rho is a fixed compactly
supported absolutely continuous probability with bounded density. TV is
sup_A |mu(A)-nu(A)| = half the L1 norm for discrete measures.

There is one fixed rho in R^2, and finite target pairs (mu_n,nu_n) supported
in Y=[-1,1]^2, for which
  integral |T_mu_n-T_nu_n|^2 d rho / TV(mu_n,nu_n)
    = 4n + 1/(4n) + 25/8 -> infinity.
Hence the premise fails under the proposed disconnected/nonconvex source
quantifiers. This does not refute the premise for connected convex sources
with a positive lower density bound, and does not refute a W_infinity bound.

1. ONE FIXED SOURCE

For every integer n>=2, set
  s_n=2^(-n), h_n=s_n/16, w_n=s_n/(4096 n^2),
  R_n=[-w_n,w_n] x [s_n-h_n,s_n+h_n].
The rectangles are pairwise disjoint. Their only accumulation point is
(0,0). Add the fixed base rectangle
  B=[-1/8,1/8] x [1/2,1],
which is disjoint from all R_n, and let
  A = area(B)+sum_(n>=2) 4 w_n h_n > 1/8.
Let
  rho = A^(-1) 1_(B union (union R_n)) dx dy, and c=A^(-1)<8.
Its support is compact, contained in [-1,1] x [0,1], and its density is
bounded by 8. No source parameter changes with n. The base rectangle is a
refinement proposed and checked by the parent to avoid a large density bound.

2. TARGET SITES AND CONVEX POTENTIALS

Fix n>=2 and abbreviate s=s_n,h=h_n,w=w_n. Put
  z_i=-1/4+i/(2n), sigma_i=(-1)^i, p_i=(sigma_i,z_i), i=0,...,n,
and two sentinel sites
  p_-=(0,-1), p_+=(0,1).
All n+3 sites are distinct and belong to Y=[-1,1]^2.
With v=y-s, define affine scores
  F_i(x,y)=sigma_i x+z_i v-h z_i^2,
  F_-(x,y)=-v-5h/8,
  F_+(x,y)= v-5h/8.
Let phi_n=max(F_-,F_0,...,F_n,F_+). For
  q=q_n=h/(100n),
define psi_n(x,y)=phi_n(x,y-q). This subtracts q times the vertical slope
of each affine score. Both potentials are convex finite polyhedral functions.
Let T_n=gradient phi_n and S_n=gradient psi_n where differentiable, with
arbitrary values on their finite union of tie lines (rho-null). Define
  mu_n=(T_n)#rho, nu_n=(S_n)#rho.

Both T_n and S_n are the unique quadratic optimal maps for their respective
pushforwards. This can be proved directly without appealing to a converse
Brenier theorem: Fenchel gives <a,b><=phi(a)+phi*(b), with equality on
b in subgradient phi(a). Thus the graph coupling maximizes correlation
among couplings with the same marginals. The quadratic cost differs from
-2 times correlation only by fixed marginal second moments. Any optimal
coupling must also saturate Fenchel, and at rho-a.e. differentiability point
the subgradient is a singleton, giving uniqueness. The same applies to psi.

3. ENVELOPE ON THE n-TH SOURCE RECTANGLE

At fixed x, the scores have strictly increasing vertical slopes in order
  -1,z_0,...,z_n,1.
Let b_- be the equality threshold of F_- and F_0, b_i the equality
threshold of F_i and F_(i+1), and b_+ that of F_n and F_+. Measured in v,
  b_-(x)=-3h/4-4x/3,
  b_i(x)=h(z_i+z_(i+1))
            -(sigma_(i+1)-sigma_i)x/(z_(i+1)-z_i), 0<=i<n,
  b_+(x)=3h/4+(4/3)sigma_n x.
Since z_(i+1)-z_i=1/(2n), the x coefficient of b_i has magnitude 4n.
Successive interior thresholds satisfy
  b_(i+1)(x)-b_i(x) >= h/n-8n w
    = 31h/(32n)>0,
because w=h/(256 n^2). The first and last interior thresholds lie within
(-h/2,h/2), since
  b_0 >= -h/2+h/(2n)-4nw > -h/2,
  b_(n-1) <= h/2-h/(2n)+4nw < h/2.
The sentinel thresholds lie near -3h/4 and +3h/4, so all n+2 thresholds
are strictly ordered and belong to (-h,h), for every |x|<=w.

For affine functions with increasing slopes and increasing consecutive
crossings, each score is the upper envelope on the interval between its
two neighboring thresholds: below a crossing the lower-slope score wins;
above it the higher-slope score wins; chaining comparisons excludes every
non-neighbor. Thus the cells on every vertical segment of R_n occur in
exactly the displayed site order. For psi_n every threshold is b(x)+q.
The shift q is smaller than all gaps, and all shifted thresholds remain
inside (-h,h). Consequently the n+2 swept bands are disjoint, and each
has area exactly 2wq.

4. ALL OTHER SOURCE RECTANGLES ARE UNCHANGED

For (x,y) in B and n>=2, v>=1/4, h_n<=1/64, and |x|<=1/8, so
  F_+-F_i >= (3/4)v-5h_n/8-|x|
    >= 3/16-5/512-1/8=27/512>0.
The shifted comparison loses at most (5/4)q<=1/10240 and remains positive.
Also F_+>F_- before and after the shift. Thus both maps send all B to p_+.

For m<n and (x,y) in R_m,
  v=y-s_n >= (15/16)s_m-s_m/2 = (7/16)s_m,
  h_n <= s_m/32.
For every internal i, since z_i<=1/4,
  F_+-F_i=(1-z_i)v-5h_n/8+h_n z_i^2-sigma_i x
    >= (3/4)v-5h_n/8-|x|
    >= (79/256)s_m-w_m >0.
Also F_+>F_- because v>0. The shifted scores replace v by v-q;
the same bound loses at most (5/4)q and remains strictly positive.
Thus R_m is entirely mapped to p_+ by both maps.

For m>n,
  v <= (17/16)s_m-s_n <= -15s_n/32.
Using z_i>=-1/4,
  F_--F_i >= (3/4)|v|-5h_n/8-|x|
    >= (5/16)s_n-w_m>0.
Again F_->F_+, and replacing v by v-q only strengthens these comparisons.
Thus R_m is entirely mapped to p_- by both maps.

5. EXACT TARGET MASS AND MAP DIFFERENCE

On R_n every bounded internal cell has both of its interfaces translated
up by q, preserving its area exactly. The bottom sentinel cell gains area
2wq and the top sentinel cell loses area 2wq. Section 4 shows there are no
other changes. Put delta_n=c*2w_n*q_n. Then
  nu_n-mu_n=delta_n delta_(p_-)-delta_n delta_(p_+),
  TV(mu_n,nu_n)=delta_n>0.

Each of the n+2 swept bands has rho-mass delta_n. At each internal boundary
the map changes between p_i and p_(i+1), with squared distance
  |p_(i+1)-p_i|^2=4+1/(4n^2).
At each sentinel boundary the squared jump is
  |p_0-p_-|^2=|p_+-p_n|^2=1+(3/4)^2=25/16.
Therefore
  integral |T_n-S_n|^2 d rho
   = delta_n [n(4+1/(4n^2))+25/8]
   = TV(mu_n,nu_n) [4n+1/(4n)+25/8].
This diverging ratio proves the announced refutation.

For every d>2, take the product of this source with uniform measure on
[-1/2,1/2]^(d-2), a volume-one cube, and append zero extra coordinates to
every target site. Use the same potentials independent of the extra source
coordinates. The density is still <8, and every calculation is unchanged.
In d=1 the premise is true: the common-source optimal maps induce the
monotone optimal coupling between mu and nu, hence their squared discrepancy
is W_2(mu,nu)^2, which is <=D_Y^2 TV(mu,nu) by maximal overlap coupling.

6. FAILURE LEDGER AND CONSEQUENCES

Initial attempt used only alternating rail sites with equally spaced slopes
up to their endpoints. It failed: on earlier fixed rectangles, endpoint
versus penultimate slope separation shrinks like 1/n, so no fixed nonzero
source width guarantees endpoint dominance for all n. Extra sentinel slopes
with a fixed gap 3/4 repair this issue. The exact external-block estimates
above are the load-bearing part of the fixed-source quantifiers.

The bad behavior is a length-n acyclic rerouting chain. An endpoint mass
change of delta sends delta across every adjacent pair of distant sites.
The number of target sites is therefore a necessary possible dependence
for a generic squared-map versus TV estimate. This fits the finite-site
D_Y^2(N-1) TV bound supplied by the parent via acyclic flow decomposition.

The proposed TV + W_infinity interpolation route to W_2 exponent 1/3 is
blocked under the general bounded-source quantifiers. This counterexample
does not decide whether the W_infinity squared-map bound holds, nor whether
the TV estimate becomes valid under log-concavity or positive density on a
convex domain. Those remain separate interfaces.

An independently derived two-rail endpoint-cap variant is recorded in
chain_attack/PROOF.txt, and the independent audit of this exact sentinel
construction is in chain_attack/SENTINEL_AUDIT.txt. The other construction
has exact E/TV=4k-4+4/k and W_infinity=4/k, but E/W_infinity tends to zero.
It explicitly confirms that failure of TV control alone does not refute the
separate W_infinity premise. For this sentinel family W_infinity>=5/4:
the gaining bottom sentinel must import positive mass from a distinct site,
and its nearest such site is p_0 at distance 5/4.
