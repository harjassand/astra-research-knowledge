## Precise positive result

Over any characteristic-zero field, let f be a noncommutative polynomial with an acyclic m-state linear representation f_w=uB_wv, where all B_i are strictly upper triangular. For n variables, the explicit rational matrices

    (T_i)_(r,q)=(-1)^(r-q-1)/(r i^(r-q)) for 0<=q<r<2nm

(and zero otherwise) satisfy f(T)!=0 whenever f!=0. The generator reads only n,m. It requires the FULL evaluated matrix. For a formula of source size s with m<=2s, dimension4ns suffices. This improves the source's quadratic-in-state first-column construction by changing its response interface.

Root read and independently derived every step of `notes/frontier_experiments/cycle19/LINEAR_DIMENSION_CANDIDATE.md`:

1. Set A(z)=sum B_i/(z+i)=P(z)/q(z), degP<=n-1, q(0)!=0, and G'=AG,G(0)=I. For zero constant term, f(J)a is the Volterra integral with kernel K(z,t)=uG(z)G(t)^-1 A(t)v. The left-letter order is essential and checks. Nonzero finite-word faithfulness implies K!=0.
2. Rows R_0=u,R_(j+1)=R_j'+R_jA have numerators Z_j of degree<=j(n-1) over q^j. The first rational dependence among at most m+1 rows makes their preceding span invariant under the covariant derivative, even when dependence coefficients have poles. It follows that some diagonal derivative b_j(t)=R_j(t)A(t)v is nonzero with j<m.
3. That derivative has order at most(j+1)(n-1). Thus K(t+x,t), and therefore K(z,t), has a nonzero homogeneous component of degree ell-1<=nm-1. The invertible linear substitution preserves total order; it does not assert any individual coefficient survives cancellation.
4. For K_(ell-1)=sum_b c_b z^(ell-1-b)t^b, the entry in row q+ell,column q of f(T) is sum_b c_b/(q+b+1). Taking q=0,...,ell-1 gives an invertible Cauchy matrix. The largest required row2ell-1 fits dimension2nm. This proves detection with no numerical rank argument or multiplicity estimate.

## A genuine boundary, reconstructed independently

Let A=J_1 and B=J_2. The proper rational noncommutative series

    R=AB-B(I+A)^-1 A(I+B)

is nonzero (degree-two term AB-BA), but evaluates to the ZERO operator on F[[z]]. Indeed put h=Ba and M(z)=(z+2)/(z+1). Direct differentiation and zero initial values give

    (I+A)(Mh)=A(I+B)a,       B(Mh)=ABa.

The first identity follows from M'=-1/(z+1)^2 and h'=a/(z+2); the derivative of its left side is (a+h)/(z+1). The second has derivative h/(z+1). Since I+A is invertible as a formal Volterra operator, these identities prove R(J)=0 on EVERY input. Every finite truncation consequently vanishes too. Thus enlarging the unmarked matrix, or reading all its entries, cannot fix cyclic faithfulness. The length marker is a structural repair, not merely an implementation choice. A concrete five-state realization and exact replay are being saved by frontier_check_hitting.

## Costs and significance

At fixed n, old first-column response length and new full-matrix response length are both order m^2. The improved matrix dimension reduces dense input descriptions from order m^4 to order m^2 field entries; it does not by itself reduce oracle computation, response bit height, learner query count, or total information cost. Integer conjugation is available but does not establish floating-point stability. An m-state acyclic class contains all binary homogeneous degree2t polynomials when m=3*2^t-2, forcing d^2>=4^t for any fixed complete-matrix feature. Thus linear dimension is optimal in order at fixed n for that interface.

The larger research opportunity is an economical exact representation-acquisition primitive. Existing white-box noncommutative PIT is polynomial, and Forbes–Shpilka already provide output-only deterministic quasipolynomial reconstruction. Historical priority of this explicit smaller construction is unresolved. The existing length-marked learner requires a strong matrix-evaluation oracle; neither a passive-sample learner nor an acquired positive stochastic realization follows. A newly smaller cyclic detector can increase prefix-extraction costs, so it requires a full comparison rather than automatic adoption as a faster learner.
