1. DETERMINANT-WEIGHTED LINEAR MATROID PARITY

For explicit complex rational F in C^(n x n), let
 c_k(F)=sum_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k} |det F[I,J]|^2.
This is the k-pair norm of a hard onsite-projected opposite-spin BCS state.
With f_i equal to the transpose of row i, V=[I,F^T], and P(I) selecting both
columns (e_i,f_i), Cauchy--Binet gives
 c_k(F)=sum_{|I|=k} det((V*V)[P(I)]).
Equivalently retain only exponents 0 or 2 in
 det(I+sum_i x_i(e_i e_i*+f_i f_i*)), then replace x_i^2 by z_i.

The scalar sum_k c_k z^k is real-rooted by the audited clone identity.
Support, exact zero detection and witnesses also have a deterministic
polynomial-bit reduction to the imported Hamada--Iwata unweighted linear
matroid-parity algorithm. Clear rational-complex denominators, bound the norm
of a nonzero Gaussian-integer minor, and test polynomially many finite fields;
one preserves an optimum witness and all modular witnesses lift soundly.
Forced-pair contraction and excluded-pair deletion handle prefixes. The
algorithmic import and reduction have not been implemented here. The earlier
randomized Pfaffian route remains valid. Neither supplies a counting oracle.

2. UNIFORM CLASSICAL RECONSTRUCTION FROM SELF-COMPATIBLE CHANNELS

The bounded-Holevo conjecture remains open: if b2(E_n)->0 while capacity is
bounded, must one entanglement-breaking channel approximate every state in
E_n uniformly in trace distance? Family size and Hilbert dimension can grow.

Compatible smoothing reduces it to sigma/2<=rho<=3sigma/2, sigma included.
An actual Petz correction makes the broadcaster marginal exactly reference
preserving, positive and KMS-selfadjoint, with O(b^1/4) loss. The underlying
Q-loss recovery inequality is established Gao--Li--Marvian--Rouze prior art.

Candidate decisive theorem on a finite-dimensional algebra with faithful trace:
for every positive selfadjoint selfcompatible UCP channel Phi, can an EB
channel Psi be chosen with a UNIVERSAL Dirichlet bound
 I-Psi <= c(I-Phi)
on the weighted Hilbert space? Positivity/selfadjointness of Psi, or an explicit
substitute controlling its squared displacement, is needed for the resulting
trace-error bound. A weaker bound only on the experiment's directions might
suffice. This is a proposed sufficient theorem, NOT proved in general.

For tracial qubits it is proved internally with c=2: diagonalize the Bloch
matrix, let mu_i=max(0,2lambda_i-1), and use the EB mixture of axis dephasings
with weights mu_i. Selfcompatibility gives pair and triple inequalities that
ensure sum mu_i<=1. The same c=2 survives tensor products, convex mixtures,
and unitary conjugation. This supplies a genuine growing-dimension subclass.
Depolarizing qudit factors are also covered. A complementary POVM construction
uses its Lueders channel L and canonical measure-prepare channel Psi, with
0<=Psi<=L<=I; the explicitly compatible (L+Psi)/2 obeys the same bound.

The instrument interface extends further. Supply Kraus matrices K_y with
sum K_y* K_y=sum K_y K_y*=I, let L(A)=sum K_y A K_y*, and let Psi be the
canonical measurement channel for M_y=K_y* K_y. The actual symmetrized
instrument-append broadcaster has marginal C=(L+Psi)/2. Correct it by C*
on both outputs to obtain the positive compatible marginal Phi=C* C.
The exact noncommutative identity
 2(I-Phi)-(I-Psi)
   =(I-L* L)+(Psi-Psi^2)+(L-Psi)*(L-Psi)/2 >=0
gives c=2 without assuming L selfadjoint or Psi<=Phi. Here stars on maps
denote their tracial adjoints. Inner Kraus families on the central block
algebra are also allowed. This is an acquired construction from supplied
instruments, not a representation of all compatible channels.
Tensor closure also needs no Psi<=Phi condition: for positive contractions
with Phi<=aI+bPsi, a=1-1/c, b=1/c, the identity
 aI+b(Psi1 tensor Psi2)-(aI+bPsi1) tensor(aI+bPsi2)
   =ab(I-Psi1) tensor(I-Psi2)>=0
retains the same c in every tensor power. Independent algebra audits agree.

Entire ranges of powers of a positive trace-selfadjoint selfcompatible Phi
have uniform commutator bound 2/sqrt(m+1)+4/(m+1) in normalized trace L2.
What is missing is dimension-free approximation of those CP maps by EB maps
in normalized infinity-to-one norm. An abelian ultraproduct limit does not
