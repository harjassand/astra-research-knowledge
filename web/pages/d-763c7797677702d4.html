EXACT THEOREM AND LEGAL INTERFACE

Fix K>=2 species, finite local dimensions d_p>=2, positive public
0<alpha_min<=alpha_p<=alpha_max, a fixed 0<lambda0<=1/K, and finite M,B>=0.
For every tuple of positive integers N_p with total N=sum_p N_p and

                         N_p/N>=lambda0,

let F_(p,a), a=1,...,m_p=d_p^2-1, be an HS-orthonormal traceless Hermitian
basis on species p; J_(p,a)=sum_(i in species p)F_(p,a)^(i),
Q_p=sum_a J_(p,a)^2. Supply arbitrary KNOWN real inter-species coefficient
blocks B_pq with B_qp=B_pq^T, B_pp=0, and full real symmetric block matrix
mathcal B having operator norm <=M. The block fields h_p are known,
h_p=h_p*, Tr h_p=0, ||h_p||_HS<=B. Different species may have different
dimensions, fields, temperatures alpha_p and populations.

There is an explicit positive delta_star, depending only on these fixed
public data and NOT on N or the admitted population tuple, such that the
WHOLE actual Gibbs density of

 H_N=sum_p alpha_p Q_p/N_p
      +(delta/N)sum_(p<q,ab)(B_pq)_ab J_(p,a)J_(q,b)
      +sum_p sum_(i in p)h_p^(i)                     (M1)

is EXACTLY fully separable across all N physical sites whenever
|delta|<=delta_star. Full separability permits different local factors in a
product term. An exact law of identical factors within each species is NOT
asserted after the final absorption. The stopped approximating law does
have identical local factors within each species before absorption.

The interaction is all-to-all but homogeneous within a fixed species pair.
This is not an arbitrary interaction graph or a growing-K theorem. The
coupling radius can be astronomically small; efficient preparation and
acquisition of unknown parameters are not claimed.

1. EXACT MULTISPECIES PRODUCT-KERNEL GENERATOR

Use k(rho_1,...,rho_K)=tensor_p rho_p^tensor N_p. For local F_(p,a), write
 f_(p,a)=Tr(F_(p,a)rho_p),
 v_(p,a)={F_(p,a),rho_p}-2 f_(p,a)rho_p,
 w_(p,a)=-i[F_(p,a),rho_p],
 B_(p,a)=2N_p f_(p,a)+v_(p,a).grad_(rho_p),
 R_(p,a)=w_(p,a).grad_(rho_p).
The exact identities {J,k}=Bk and [J,k]=iRk follow by differentiating one
species' tensor factors. For p!=q, the fields act on different variables
and therefore commute. Expanding constant matrix left/right actions gives

 {J_(p,a)J_(q,b),k}/2
       =(B_(p,a)B_(q,b)-R_(p,a)R_(q,b))k/4.          (M2)

The same identity for a squared local collective generator was derived in
QUDIT_STOPPED_DIFFUSION.txt; no asymptotic expansion enters (M2).

Introduce the full quadratic coefficient matrix
 mathcal G_pp=(alpha_p/N_p) I_(m_p),
 mathcal G_pq=(delta/(2N)) B_pq for p!=q.
The factor 1/2 is required because the symmetric coefficient sum counts
each cross pair twice. The product-space diffusion form is

 D=V mathcal G V^T-W mathcal G W^T,                  (M3)

where V,W are the direct sums of local filtering/rotation maps. The SDE
covariance is D/2. Its potential is exactly Tr(H_N k). The drift in species p is

 a_p=2(alpha_p/N_p)(N_p-1)(rho_p^2-purity_p rho_p)
               +(1/2)v_(h_p)(rho_p)+(1/2)v_(k_p)(rho_p),
 k_p=(delta/N)sum_(q!=p) N_q B_pq f_q.               (M4)

The last expression means the Hermitian traceless local matrix whose basis
coefficient vector is the displayed vector. It is an explicit polynomial
function of the known coefficients and current local states. There is no
cross derivative correction in (M4), since different species coordinates
are independent variables. The Brownian noises may be correlated; stochastic
independence of species is never assumed.

2. A PUBLIC POSITIVE DIFFUSION DOMAIN, INCLUDING UNEQUAL POPULATIONS

For the complete local basis, the earlier exact KMS identity gives

 <Z,D_(I,p)Z>=4Tr[rho_p(Z-Tr(rho_p Z)I)rho_p(Z-Tr(rho_p Z)I)].

Thus on rho_p>=eta I/2, D_(I,p)>=eta^2 I. Also the local filter has
HS operator norm <=4 and the rotation <=2; their direct sums have the
same bounds. Since N_p<=N, the diagonal isotropic diffusion obeys
 D_iso>=alpha_min eta^2 I/N.
The cross contribution has norm at most

 (|delta|/(2N)) M (||V||^2+||W||^2)<=10|delta|M/N.

Therefore if |delta|M<=alpha_min eta^2/20,

               D>=alpha_min eta^2 I/(2N).            (M5)

This is a positivity margin on a neighborhood of the stopping domain
rho_p>=eta I. The actual covariance has half this margin. A smooth matrix
square root exists there for each supplied N; its known gap scales as 1/N
and is not treated as a uniform numerical spectral gap.

3. EXACT MARGINAL LOGDETERMINANT BOUNDS SURVIVE THE CROSS COUPLING

The off-diagonal blocks in (M3) correlate species noises, but do NOT alter
any diagonal covariance block:
D_pp=(alpha_p/N_p)D_(I,p). The Hessian of log det rho_p involves only that
block, so the exact local determinant formula is

 d log det rho_p
 =[(alpha_p/N_p){2N_p-d_p^2-d_p(2N_p-1)purity_p}
              -d_p Tr(rho_p h_p)-d_p f_p^T k_p]dt+dM_p. (M6)

Its local martingale quadratic variation is EXACTLY

 d<M_p>_t=2(alpha_p/N_p)d_p^2(purity_p-1/d_p)dt.

For all density matrices ||f_p||_2^2=purity_p-1/d_p<=1. Each subblock
norm ||B_pq||<=M, and sum_q N_q/N<=1, so ||k_p||_2<=|delta|M.
For |delta|<=1 define dmax=max_p d_p and

 beta_det=alpha_max(dmax^2+2dmax)+dmax(B+M),
 kappa=4alpha_max dmax^2/lambda0.

Then every determinant drift is at least -beta_det and every stopped
local martingale has quadratic variation at most
 2alpha_max dmax^2/(lambda0 N).                       (M7)

This is where a FIXED positive population fraction is essential. Using total
N instead of N_p without lambda0 would give an invalid uniform tail.

4. STOPPING AND AN EXPLICIT UNIFORM ALL-N TAIL

Start each local state at I/d_p. Stop ALL states and the single positive
Feynman-Kac weight at the first time tau at which any local smallest
eigenvalue equals eta=e^-x. Until tau, (M5) gives existence and uniqueness
of the smooth finite-dimensional SDE. After tau every local state is frozen
and still has eigenvalues >=eta. The product output remains positive.

If species p hits eta, det rho_p<=eta, since its other eigenvalues are <=1.
From (M6)-(M7), its determinant martingale must then reach a negative value
at most -L, where

 L=x-dmax log dmax-beta_det>0.

Exponential-martingale maximal inequalities and a union over K species give

             P(tau<=1)<=K exp[-N L^2/kappa].          (M8)

No independence is used in this union bound. It is a whole-path event and
includes all population sizes promised in the theorem.

5. WHOLE QUANTUM GIBBS COMPARISON AND NORMALIZATION

Put mmax=max_p m_p. The total operator norm has a public extensive bound

 ||H_N||_op<=Hbar N,
 Hbar=alpha_max mmax+(M mmax)/2+B,                    (M9)

for |delta|<=1. Indeed ||Q_p||<=m_p N_p^2; the cross quadratic form
obeys -M sum_p Q_p<=Q_mathcalB<=M sum_p Q_p, and the
actual cross coefficient is delta/(2N). Thus its norm is at most
M mmax sum_p N_p^2/(2N)<=M mmax N/2. Fields cost at most BN.

Let W_t be the expected stopped positive weighted product. Matrix Ito with
(M2)-(M4) yields the exact stopped residual
 W'_t-M_H W_t=-E[1_(tau<=t)w_tau M_H k(rho_tau)].
All path weights and the quantum semigroup cost at most exp(Hbar N).
Duhamel therefore gives, with Dtot=product_p d_p^N_p,

 ||Dtot^-1 e^H_N-W_1||_1
   <=K Hbar N exp[-N(L^2/kappa-2Hbar)].              (M10)

The target normalization is >=1: its normalized trace mean is
 Tr(H_N)/Dtot=sum_p alpha_p(d_p-1/d_p)>=0,
because every cross and field term has zero trace. Spectral Jensen gives
Tr(e^H_N)/Dtot>=1. Therefore the normalized stopped product mixture
sigma=W_1/Tr W_1 and actual Gibbs target satisfy

 ||rho_Gibbs-sigma||_1
   <=2K Hbar N exp[-N(L^2/kappa-2Hbar)].              (M11)

The factor two from normalization is retained. The error concerns the WHOLE
d_1^N_1 ... d_K^N_K density, not only marginals, energy or free energy.

6. ELEMENTARY HETEROGENEOUS SEPARABLE BALL AND WHITE-NOISE ABSORPTION

On each physical site of dimension d_i use HS basis I/sqrt(d_i), F_a.
The traceless F_a have operator norm <=1. For any tensor-basis label v!=0,
let P_v be the tensor of the active F_a and identities elsewhere. The
operators (I+-P_v)/Dtot are fully separable: average local states
(I+s_i F_a)/d_i over signs with fixed product parity on the active support.
Every proper sign character vanishes and the full character is the chosen
parity. Local dimensions need not agree.

Any Hermitian trace-zero E expands as sum_(v!=0)e_v tensor_i T_(v_i), with
sum e_v^2=||E||_2^2. Its active tensor P_v has coefficient
c_v=product_(identity sites)d_i^-1/2<=1. The coefficients needed to express
I/Dtot+E as a convex combination of the parity states are
a_v=Dtot c_v e_v. Their absolute sum is bounded by

 sum|a_v|<=Dtot sqrt(Dtot^2-1)||E||_2<=Dtot^2||E||_1.

Thus ||E||_1<=Dtot^-2 is a sufficient fully separable ball at I/Dtot.
This is an elementary reconstruction of root/s01's ball for heterogeneous
dimensions, not an imported stronger Gurvits-Barnum radius.

Every stopped local state has rho_p>=eta I. Expanding each local density as
 d_p eta(I/d_p)+(1-d_p eta)rho'_p
extracts the same white component from every weighted product. The normalized
mixture has

 sigma=w I/Dtot+(1-w)tau_sep,   w=Dtot eta^N,

with tau_sep fully separable. Consequently if

 ||rho_Gibbs-sigma||_1<=w Dtot^-2=eta^N/Dtot,         (M12)

the ball absorbs the correction EXACTLY: rho_Gibbs is a convex combination
of a ball state and tau_sep. Since Dtot<=dmax^N, it suffices to make the
error <=(eta/dmax)^N. Exact iid or species-iid membership is not inferred.

7. PUBLIC CONSTANTS CLOSE ABSORPTION FOR EVERY ADMITTED N

Set Qbar=max(Hbar,1) and

 R=2Hbar+(dmax+1)log dmax+beta_det+3+log(2K Qbar),
 L=kappa/2+sqrt(kappa^2/4+kappa R),
 x=dmax log dmax+beta_det+L,
 eta=exp(-x),
 delta_star=min{1,alpha_min eta^2/[20 max(M,1)]}.      (M13)

These are all finite public constants, eta<1/dmax and delta_star>0. The
coefficient radius guarantees (M5). Also L^2/kappa-L=R exactly, so

 L^2/kappa-2Hbar=x+log dmax+3+log(2K Qbar).

For N>=1, 2K Hbar N<=exp[N(1+log(2K Qbar))]. Substituting into (M11) gives

 ||rho_Gibbs-sigma||_1
       <=(eta/dmax)^N exp(-2N)<=(eta/dmax)^N,

which closes (M12). The actual density is therefore EXACTLY fully separable
for every N and every population tuple satisfying the promise. There is no
large-N loophole or numerical separability inference.
