# D example: classical adaptive controlled-identification calibration

**Status:** scoped finite theorem and runnable reproduction. **Novelty:** exact model is already Table 1 in Nitinawarat–Atia–Veeravalli (2013); no foundational discovery or architecture advantage.

**Question/interface:** identify one of three known hypotheses with three known target actions. Bernoulli(4/5) on the true target and Bernoulli(1/5) elsewhere; full support, conditional independence, unit experiment cost; maximum error <=delta under every hypothesis. All models and action semantics are supplied equally.

**Result:** adaptive ordinary ML-target testing attains KL D=(3/5)log4, while the analytically optimized fixed IID frequencies attain 2D/3. The first-order experiment-count ratio is 3/2, not 2. At delta=10^-12, a self-contained occupation/optional-expectation proof gives adaptive expected samples <=1310/27=48.5185. Every fixed IID delta-correct test has uniform-prior mean >=49.8289. Every delta-correct test under balanced periodic actions has mean >=48.8289. This establishes strict finite gains over both comparator classes.

**Complete derivation:** notes/d_example/proof.md. **Runnable code:** work/d_example/experiment.py and work/d_example/verify.py. **Evidence:** results/d_example/exact_checks.json, aggregate.json, simulation.jsonl, cost_ledger.json, configuration.json, raw NPZs, verification.json. Run with `python3 work/d_example/experiment.py --trials 6000`, then `python3 work/d_example/verify.py`.

**Finite run:** 324,000 trials. At delta=10^-12, means are 58.2811 IID, 56.9572 balanced periodic, 36.5271 adaptive; zero errors and censoring at that tolerance. Tiny error bounds follow from proof, not those zero finite-run counts. Every wrong target experiment is charged; per-truth errors and uncertainties are saved.

**Assumptions/decisive obstruction:** pairwise likelihood increments and which actions distinguish hypotheses are known. Hide or misspecify those laws and the bounded wrong-target occupation proof stops applying. Supplied finite experiments do not acquire new structure. Arbitrary unbalanced predetermined schedules and initial mixtures of frequency vectors under unconditional risk are not covered by the strongest fixed-class theorem.
