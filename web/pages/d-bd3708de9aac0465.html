NONCOMMUTING_QUARTIC_FAMILY.txt, at public h_Q=h_D^2/128, beta=L^4,
chemical zero, with positive physical onsite strengths c/L^2 and 2c/L^2.
Put kappa=1/(1250*3^49), c=kappa/1000000, eta=kappa*c/162. For EVERY
normal measure-and-prepare channel T_L on the full physical Fock space,

 max_{z=c,2c}||T_L rho_L(z)-rho_L(z)||_1
                      >=e_*:=eta^4/65536>0, L>=4.      (E.1)

The classical alphabet, decoder, precision, runtime and active ancillary

restriction. Thus (E.1) is a single-copy full-state reconstruction lower
bound, including every classical archive in this interface. Normal EB
channels have a measure-and-prepare representation; finite/countable

The identical floor holds for the unique continuum contact Gibbs pair.
It does not need convergence to justify (E.1): the finite-L commutator
witness already has the uniform eta bound. A fixed public quantum

2. A DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT AFFINITY INEQUALITY FOR EB CHANNELS

For density operators rho,sigma on a separable Hilbert space define
 f(rho,sigma)=||sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)||_1,
 a(rho,sigma)=Tr sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma), d=f-a>=0.
Affinity a is real and nonnegative. Every measure-and-prepare channel
T obeys

 a(T rho,T sigma)>=f(rho,sigma).                       (E.2)

Here is a complete proof of the two inputs. For a POVM {E_x}, let
p_x=Tr rho E_x, q_x=Tr sigma E_x. Insert sum E_x=I between the square
roots in the polar variational formula for f. Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-
Schwarz, and the fact that the variational contraction has norm <=1,
give f<=sum_x sqrt(p_x q_x). This holds for countable measurements and
by integration for general POVMs. It is the usual measurement-fidelity
inequality; only this direction is needed.

The preparation step T rho=sum_x p_x eta_x, T sigma=sum_x q_x eta_x
has affinity at least sum_x sqrt(p_x q_x) by joint concavity and common
degree-one homogeneity of a(A,B)=Tr sqrt(A)sqrt(B). To avoid an imported
unproved concavity step, the p=1/2 case has the following direct proof.
For strictly positive X,Y on a finite Hilbert space, their parallel sum
(X^(-1)+Y^(-1))^(-1) has quadratic form
 inf_{u+v=w}(<u,Xu>+<v,Yv>),
so it is jointly operator concave. On the Hilbert-Schmidt space, left
and right multiplications L_A,R_B are commuting positive operators and

 L_A^(1/2)R_B^(1/2)
  =(1/pi)integral_0^infty t^(-1/2)
                    (L_A^(-1)+t R_B^(-1))^(-1)dt.    (E.3)

The scalar integral proves the identity by their joint spectral calculus.
Parallel-sum concavity, with Y=t^(-1)R_B, makes the right side jointly
operator concave in A,B. Evaluate its quadratic form at the identity
matrix to obtain a(A,B). Approximation handles singular matrices; finite
rank compression and the continuity estimate in Section 3 extend it to
trace class. Concavity and homogeneity imply superadditivity, which is
the displayed preparation bound. Continuous preparations follow by
simple-function approximation of the trace-class state-valued integral.
This proves (E.2). It is the p=1/2 instance of Lieb's established joint
trace concavity, retained here with proper attribution.

For a general outcome space, take the finite dominating measure
nu=mu_rho+mu_sigma and densities p,q. Finite-partition POVM fidelity
bounds tend to integral sqrt(pq)dnu by conditional-expectation
approximation and square-root L^2 continuity. Preparation Jensen with
nu/2, concavity and degree-one homogeneity gives the same integral
lower bound on affinity. Its state-valued Bochner integral is justified
by separability and simple-function approximation. Thus non-atomic
representations are covered as well as finite/countable alphabets.

For clarity, measurement and preparation alphabets need not have the
same finite size along L. No finite-output compactness assumption is
used in (E.2); the entire common Hilbert space is retained.

3. TRACE CONTINUITY, COMMUTATOR AND AN EXPLICIT ERROR CERTIFICATE

The square-root trace estimate is
 ||sqrt(rho)-sqrt(rho')||_2^2<=||rho-rho'||_1.           (E.4)
For a proof put X=sqrt(rho),Y=sqrt(rho'),C=X-Y,S=sign(C). Cyclicity and
the trace-norm dual bound give
 ||X^2-Y^2||_1>=Tr S(XC+CY)=Tr |C|(X+Y)>=Tr C^2.
The last difference equals 2Tr(C_+Y+C_-X)>=0. All products are trace
class by Hilbert-Schmidt bounds; finite-rank approximation justifies
the identities without invertibility. This is Powers--Stormer (1970).

If delta=max(||T rho-rho||_1,||T sigma-sigma||_1), (E.4) and Hilbert-
Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz yield
 |a(T rho,T sigma)-a(rho,sigma)|<=2sqrt(delta).
Together with (E.2) this implies

 delta>=d^2/4.                                       (E.5)

Now put X=sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)=U|X| in polar form. Since U^*U is the
support of |X|, the exact weighted squared norm is
 Tr |X|(U-I)^*(U-I)=2(f-Re Tr X)=2d.
The decomposition X-X^*=(U-I)|X|+|X|(I-U^*) and Hilbert-Schmidt
Cauchy-Schwarz give

 ||[sqrt(rho),sqrt(sigma)]||_1<=2sqrt(2 f d)<=2sqrt(2d).

Expanding [rho,sigma] as four products containing this square-root
commutator, using the square-root operator norms <=1, gives

 d>=||[rho,sigma]||_1^2/128,
 delta>=||[rho,sigma]||_1^4/65536.                     (E.6)

Neither finite dimension nor a thermal energy condition enters (E.4)-
(E.6). In particular a vanishing approximation by EB maps would force
commutation of the limiting pair, quantitatively and without a closure
argument. Errors using HALF trace norm have floor e_*/2 instead.

4. APPLICATION TO THE ACTUAL QUARTIC FAMILY AND THE COMMUTING CONTROL

NONCOMMUTING_QUARTIC_FAMILY Sections 2-5 prove
 ||[rho_L(c),rho_L(2c)]||_1>=eta
for every L>=4 by exact sine contact, uniform form perturbation and full
partition normalization. Insert this into (E.6) to prove (E.1). Its
Sections 6-7 prove full trace-norm Gibbs convergence, so the same eta
and e_* hold for the unique continuum contact pair.

This excludes a classical single-copy archive achieving arbitrarily
small error on the unknown positive-coupling interval [c,2c]. It does
not exclude classical reconstruction when the unknown coupling is
revealed, multiple-copy estimation, or approximation of a restricted
observable list. No lower bound on the optimal number of quantum qubits
is claimed beyond failure of an entirely classical archive at vanishing
error. The conservative e_* is extremely small; it is a mathematical
positive-floor certificate, not an optimized experimental margin.

The equally informed commuting control has one FIXED PUBLIC coupling z.
Varying a stable chemical parameter only multiplies each sector heat
operator by a scalar exp(tau lambda m). Measuring M and preparing the
conditional heat state at that public z is then an exact classical
decoder, before truncation. Preparing that interacting conditional state
has its own charged cost; it is not a free Gibbs oracle. In the unknown
interval, both couplings have identical public geometry, kinetic matrix
and time scale, and the one archive must reconstruct without reading z.

5. A PUBLIC QUANTUM ARCHIVE SUFFICES, WITH ITS COST EXPOSED

On occupation-mode Fock space set A_*=dGamma(|k|^4/2), vacuum energy zero.
For every z>=0 the dimensionless interacting Hamiltonian B_L(z) satisfies
B_L(z)>=A_* on its finite-mode subspace. Positivity and the vacuum imply
Z_L(z)>=1. Free half-time minmax and the elementary quartic partition
bound give Tr exp[-B_L(z)/2]<=exp(2). Since x exp(-x)<=(2/e)exp(-x/2),

 Tr rho_L(z) A_*<=Tr rho_L(z)B_L(z)<=2e.               (E.7)

The limit contact states obey the same estimate either by the identical
form argument or trace lower semicontinuity. These are derived input
Gibbs bounds; they do not restrict the lower-bound decoder output.

For requested fulltrace error 0<epsilon<=1 choose
 E=18e/epsilon^2, P_E=1_{A_*<=E}.
The tail delta_E<=2e/E. Encode the public P_E band coherently plus one
overflow flag and decode the flag as vacuum. Compression's gentle bound
and the overflow contribution give reconstruction error
 <=2sqrt(delta_E)+delta_E<=3sqrt(delta_E)<=epsilon.
The map is independent of z and L and works for the whole positive
interval, indeed for all z>=0. On finite L use only the available mode
labels; the same common band accommodates every lattice size.

Let N_E=floor(sqrt(2E)) and M_E=floor(E/2). Every occupied mode in P_E
has max k<=(2E)^(1/4), so there are at most N_E available two-dimensional
mode labels, and at most M_E particles. Thus its dimension is bounded by
 D_E<=binomial(N_E+M_E,M_E)<=(M_E+1)^N_E,
where the last bound enumerates all occupations 0,...,M_E separately.
Consequently ceil(log2(D_E+1))=O(epsilon^(-1)log(1/epsilon)) persistent
qubits are sufficient, independently of L. This is a crude upper bound,
not an optimal rate or a minimal-memory claim.

Energy-band projection, occupation encoding, sine transformations,
physical output preparation, arithmetic precision, runtime and active
working registers remain decoder/encoder costs. Enumeration of the
band can be exponential in its mode count; no efficient bound uniform
in epsilon is claimed. The lower bound allows these same unrestricted
active resources for classical maps. Only the type of persistent
register distinguishes the two reconstruction statements.
