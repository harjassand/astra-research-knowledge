## Theorem

Let \(\Phi:M_d\to M_d\) be unital, trace-preserving, completely positive,
and Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint. Suppose it has an actual symmetric
broadcaster: a UCP map \(\Gamma:M_d\otimes M_d\to M_d\) with
\(\Gamma(X\otimes I)=\Gamma(I\otimes X)=\Phi(X)\). Then
\[
\mathcal F=\operatorname{Fix}(\Phi)
\]
is an abelian *-subalgebra. The Hilbert--Schmidt orthogonal projection
\(E\) onto \(\mathcal F\) is a trace-preserving conditional expectation
and is entanglement breaking. Define the nontrivial spectral gap
\[
\gamma(\Phi)=\min\{1-\lambda:\lambda\in\operatorname{spec}(\Phi),\
                                      \lambda<1\}>0.
\]
If the set is empty, \(E=I\) and the comparison is trivial. Otherwise
\[
 I-E\preceq \gamma(\Phi)^{-1}(I-\Phi).                 \tag{1}
\]

The claim needs no positivity of \(\Phi\) as an operator on Hilbert--Schmidt
space. Unital CP plus trace preservation makes \(\Phi\) an L2 contraction,
so its self-adjoint spectrum lies in \([-1,1]\); the proof uses the Cesaro
means of \(\Phi\). Under the broadcaster assumption, the empty-set case
forces \(\operatorname{Fix}(\Phi)=M_d\) to be abelian and hence \(d=1\),
where \(E=I\) is EB. Under the campaign's stronger \(\Phi\succeq0\)
assumption, the ordinary powers \(\Phi^n\) themselves converge to \(E\).

## Proof that the fixed algebra is abelian

First let \(X\in\mathcal F\). Kadison--Schwarz gives
\[
 D_X=\Phi(X^*X)-\Phi(X)^*\Phi(X)\succeq0.
\]
Since \(\Phi(X)=X\) and \(\Phi\) preserves normalized trace,
\(\tau(D_X)=\tau(X^*X)-\tau(X^*X)=0\). Faithfulness of \(\tau\) implies
\(D_X=0\); applying the same argument to \(X^*\) gives the other
multiplicative-domain equality. Thus every fixed point lies in the
multiplicative domain of \(\Phi\), and \(\mathcal F\) is a *-subalgebra.

Let \(\mathcal B\) be the Schrödinger broadcaster dual to \(\Gamma\), and
let \(\omega=\mathcal B_*(I/d)\). Both marginals of \(\omega\) are \(I/d\).
For fixed \(X\), put \(Z=X\otimes I\). The UCP Schwarz defect
\[
 D=\Gamma(Z^*Z)-\Gamma(Z)^*\Gamma(Z)\succeq0
\]
has zero normalized-trace expectation:
\[
 \tau(\Gamma(Z^*Z))=\operatorname{Tr}(\omega Z^*Z)
 =\tau(X^*X)=\tau(\Gamma(Z)^*\Gamma(Z)),
\]
where the middle equality uses the first marginal of \(\omega\) and
\(\Gamma(Z)=\Phi(X)=X\). Hence \(D=0\), so \(X\otimes I\) lies in the
multiplicative domain of \(\Gamma\). Similarly, \(I\otimes Y\) lies in it
for each \(Y\in\mathcal F\). These two input operators commute, so
\[
 XY=\Gamma((X\otimes I)(I\otimes Y))
    =\Gamma((I\otimes Y)(X\otimes I))=YX.
\]
Therefore \(\mathcal F\) is abelian.

## Construction of the EB comparison map

Because \(\Phi\) is a self-adjoint Hilbert--Schmidt contraction, its
eigenvalues are real and lie in \([-1,1]\). Its Cesaro means
\[
 E_N=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\Phi^k
\]
converge spectrally to the orthogonal projection \(E\) onto the
\(\lambda=1\) eigenspace. Each \(E_N\) is UCP and trace-preserving, so the
finite-dimensional limit \(E\) is UCP and trace-preserving. Its range is
\(\mathcal F\). If \(P_1,\ldots,P_r\) are the minimal projections of this
abelian algebra, then the orthogonal projection is explicitly
\[
 E(X)=\sum_{j=1}^r \frac{\tau(P_jX)}{\tau(P_j)}P_j.
\]
This is a finite measurement-and-preparation representation (effects
\(P_j\), preparations \(P_j/\tau(P_j)\) in likelihood coordinates), so
\(E\) is EB.

On a \(\lambda=1\) eigenvector, both sides of (1) vanish. On every other
eigenspace, \(I-E\) has eigenvalue 1 and \(I-\Phi\) has eigenvalue
\(1-\lambda\ge\gamma(\Phi)\). This proves (1).

For a likelihood perturbation \(H=H^*\), \(\|H\|_\infty\le\theta\),
\(\tau(H)=0\), the same \(E\) gives
\[
 \|(I-E)H\|_{1,\tau}^2
 \le \langle H,(I-E)H\rangle_\tau
 \le \gamma^{-1}\langle H,(I-\Phi)H\rangle_\tau
 \le \gamma^{-1}\theta\|\Phi(H)-H\|_{1,\tau}.
\]
Thus the physical half-trace reconstruction error is at most
\(\tfrac12\sqrt{\theta r/\gamma}\) for a family with residual \(r\).
The comparator is one map for the full family.

For a positive self-adjoint \(\Phi\), each \(\Phi^m\) remains
selfcompatible (postprocess both outputs of a broadcaster), and has the
same fixed algebra. Its gap is
\(\gamma_m=\min_{\lambda<1}(1-\lambda^m)\); for each fixed finite channel,
\(\gamma_m\to1\). Hence this construction gives an EB comparison for every
power with a constant tending to 1 channel-by-channel. It gives no uniform
rate over growing dimensions or channels with eigenvalues arbitrarily close
to 1.

## Why this does not close the uniform gate

Attribution: c05_l03 independently proves, under the campaign's stronger
Hilbert--Schmidt-positive hypothesis, that the fixed-point space is abelian,
its spectral projection is EB, and a uniform gap gives exponential
\(\|\Phi^m-E\|_{\infty\to1}\) convergence. The fixed-point algebra and
gap-dependent approximation are therefore an explicit overlap, not a
separate discovery claim. This derivation also gives the direct quadratic
comparison (1) and drops Hilbert--Schmidt positivity by using Cesaro means;
those are elementary extensions and carry no novelty or priority claim.

The gap dependence cannot be removed from this *fixed-projection proof*.
For \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), the qubit channel that dephases in the \(Z\) basis
and applies the symmetric classical flip chain with flip probability
\(\epsilon\) is EB, self-adjoint and selfcompatible. Its Pauli eigenvalues
are \((1,0,0,1-2\epsilon)\), so \(\gamma=2\epsilon\to0\). Nevertheless the
channel itself is already an EB rounder with comparison constant 1. An
explicit broadcaster is
\[
 \mathcal B_\epsilon(\rho)=\sum_{i=0}^1\langle i|\rho|i\rangle
   \sigma_i\otimes\sigma_i,\qquad
 \sigma_i=(1-\epsilon)|i\rangle\langle i|
             +\epsilon|1-i\rangle\langle1-i|.
\]
Thus a small gap is a proof-route limitation, not a counterexample to EB
rounding.

The fixed algebra gives only block signs when its minimal projections have
rank greater than one. Those signs pinch between fixed sectors but do not
resolve the noncommutative matrix corners. This is exactly where the
complete-sign-frame/approximate-preimage condition in peer c05_s01 is not
automatically supplied by the fixed-point argument. Peer c05_s03 likewise
retains generator-count dependence for whole-range pinching. No missing
anchor or common measurement is manufactured here.

## Outward measurement-compatibility attack

An actual broadcaster does imply pairwise compatibility of every pair of
output POVMs after \(\Phi\). For POVMs \((A_i)\), \((B_j)\),
\[
 G_{ij}=\Gamma(A_i\otimes B_j)\succeq0,\quad
 \sum_{ij}G_{ij}=I,
\]
and the two marginals are \(\Phi(A_i)\), \(\Phi(B_j)\). This is a
pairwise statement: it does not create one parent POVM for an arbitrary
growing family.

An exact qubit illustration uses the depolarizing channel
\(\Delta_{3/5}\). It is selfcompatible: the symmetric cloner
\(\mathcal B(\rho)=\frac23P_+(\rho\otimes I)P_+\) has marginal
\(\Delta_{2/3}\), and mixing it with the replacement broadcaster
\(\rho\mapsto I/2\otimes I/2\) in weights \(9/10,1/10\) gives marginal
\(\Delta_{3/5}\). The three noisy Pauli binary POVMs
\(E_{\pm|j}=(I\pm(3/5)\sigma_j)/2\) are pairwise compatible: for each
pair \(i\ne j\),
\[
 G_{ab}^{ij}=\tfrac14(I+\tfrac35(a\sigma_i+b\sigma_j)),\quad a,b\in\{-1,1\},
\]
is a joint POVM, since \(2(3/5)^2=18/25<1\). But the triple is not
jointly measurable: averaging any putative parent over the Pauli octahedral
symmetry forces effects
\[
 G_{abc}=\tfrac18(I+\tfrac35(a\sigma_x+b\sigma_y+c\sigma_z)),
\]
which are positive only if \(3(3/5)^2\le1\); instead \(27/25>1\).
This exact pair/triple gap blocks a direct conversion from broadcaster-
induced pairwise measurement compatibility to the common-map variational
problem. It does **not** refute an EB Dirichlet comparator: \(\Delta_{3/5}\)
is 2-selfcompatible but not 3-selfcompatible (a 3-broadcaster would produce
a parent for the incompatible triple). It does have EB rounders, so this is
about the insufficiency of the pairwise-compatibility proof route, not a
counterexample to EB Dirichlet rounding.

The literature distinction is established: Heinosaari--Kiukas--Reitzner--
Schultz define finite \(n\)-incompatibility breaking and exhibit channels
that break finite families without being EB; all-family incompatibility
breaking is strictly stronger. Chitambar--Zhang's Oct 2026 preprint further
shows qubit incompatibility breaking is not tensor-stable. Neither source
provides an EB Dirichlet lift for an arbitrary selfcompatible channel.
