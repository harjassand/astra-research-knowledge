1. Exact conventions and the physical stationary expansion

Use the positive HEISENBERG generator L: P_t=exp(-tL) is unital CP.
Its trace adjoint is L_*, and P_t^*(sigma)=sigma, sigma>0. Let

KMS symmetry means Gamma L=L_* Gamma. Consequently
 H=Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2)
is Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint and H(s)=0. Here
 Gamma^(1/2)(X)=dXd,
 L_*(X)=d H(d^(-1)X d^(-1))d.
No assertion that L is GNS self-adjoint is being made.

On complex HS space, with <X,Y>=Tr(X^*Y), define
 delta(X)=[logs,X], C=cosh(delta/2),
 A=(delta/2)/sinh(delta/2), with A(0)=1.
In an s eigenbasis, delta(E_ij)=(u_i-u_j)E_ij, u_i=log s_i.
Thus delta,A,C are self-adjoint superoperators. A,C are real even functions
of delta, so they also preserve self-adjoint matrices.

Let Y=Y^* with Tr(sY)=0. Set
 rho_e=s^2+e(sY+Ys).
Its trace is1 exactly, and it is faithful for all sufficiently small real
e because sigma is faithful. The derivative of q_e=sqrt(rho_e) is Y:
the derivative equation s q'_0+q'_0 s=sY+Ys has the unique solution Y.
Hence q_e=s+eY+O(e^2). Define

 z_e=d(logrho_e-logsigma)d.
Then

The latter follows entrywise from the physical log derivative:

  =2(u_i-u_j)/(s_i-s_j) Y_ij,
with the continuous diagonal value 2Y_ii/s_i. Multiplication by
sqrt(s_i s_j) gives 2A(Y)_ij. No surrogate relative entropy is used.

The actual entropy production is

2. The genuinely physical input: GNS contraction, NOT GNS symmetry

For every complex observable X, Schwarz's inequality for the unital CP
map P_t and stationarity give
 Tr[sigma P_t(X)^*P_t(X)]
  <=Tr[sigma P_t(X^*X)]=Tr[sigma X^*X].
Thus P_t contracts the GNS norm ||X||_(sigma,GNS)^2=Tr(sigma X^*X).
This contractivity holds for EVERY stationary unital CP map; it does
not assert GNS self-adjointness or modular covariance.

Transport X to h=dXd. Cyclicity gives exactly
 Tr(sigma X^*X)=Tr[h^*s^(-1)hs]=<h,e^(-delta)h>.
The transported evolution is exp(-tH). Differentiating the norm
contraction at t=0, using H=H^*, proves the complex-HS operator inequality
 H e^(-delta)+e^(-delta)H>=0.
Congruence by e^(delta/2) proves
 G=(e^(delta/2)H e^(-delta/2)
                    +e^(-delta/2)H e^(delta/2))/2>=0.

Index the orthonormal matrix-unit HS basis by alpha=(i,j), and write

This is the load-bearing link between physical CP/stationarity and the
previously audited scalar Hardy kernel. Positivity of H alone would not
supply this transformed G inequality.

3. Exact Hardy quotient and the Schur factorization

Let a(x)=x/sinh(x), a(0)=1. Since A,C are diagonal in this HS basis,
 Q_(alpha,beta)=H_(alpha,beta)
 [cosh(x_alpha)a(x_beta)+cosh(x_beta)a(x_alpha)-1].

For real x,y put t=tanh(x), u=tanh(y). Define the audited disk functions
 a_disk(z)=(1-z^2)atanh(z)/z, a_disk(0)=1,
 b_disk(z)=sqrt(1-z^2), b_disk(0)=1.
Their analytic branches are the principal disk branches. Directly,
 cosh(x)=1/b_disk(t), a(x)=a_disk(t)/b_disk(t),
 cosh(x-y)=(1-tu)/(b_disk(t)b_disk(u)).
Therefore the EXACT quotient is
 [cosh(x)a(y)+cosh(y)a(x)-1]/cosh(x-y)
  =N(t,u)
  =[a_disk(t)+a_disk(u)-b_disk(t)b_disk(u)]/(1-tu).
There is no restriction on the signs of x,y, and t,u always belong to
(-1,1). Zero frequencies are covered by the analytic continuous values.
