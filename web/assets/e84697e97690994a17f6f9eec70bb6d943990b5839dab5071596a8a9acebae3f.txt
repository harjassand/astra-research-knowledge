Audited claims
--------------
Let s=m-1, d_N=binom(N+s,s), and use the stated su(m) generator normalization.
For 1<=k<d_N, define Lambda(d_N-k) as the minimum Casimir Rayleigh quotient
over all matrices of rank at most d_N-k. The finite arbitrary-kernel lower
bound is

  Lambda(d_N-k) >= [d_N B(k)/k^2 + 1/(s+1)]^(-1),
  B(k)=min_{0<=K<=N}{(k^2/d_N) sum_{j=1}^K b_j/[j(j+s)]
                         + k/[(K+1)(K+1+s)]},

with the last tail interpreted as zero for K=N. Here
  b_j=binom(j+s,s)^2-binom(j+s-1,s)^2.
The exact sharper choice replaces k in the tail by k-k^2/d_N. The
projector-overlap bound remains k^2 b_j/d_N in either formula.

Specializing the cutoff gives finite uniform near-full lower rates

  s=1: Lambda(d_N-k) >= 1/[7/2+log(d_N/k)];
  s>=2: Lambda(d_N-k) >= L_s (k/d_N)^((s-1)/s),

where one explicit choice for s>=2 is
  L_s=1/[A_s+1+1/(s+1)],
  A_s=2 s^(s-1)(s+1)^s/[(s-1)!s!].
The p=1 radial
construction has finite upper 12/log(e d_N/k) on its stated small-deficit
range. The s>=2 resistance taper has a finite explicit upper
U_s(k/d_N)^((s-1)/s) for k<=floor(32^(-s)d_N). Both lower bounds hold for
all 1<=k<d_N.

The integrated near_full.txt proof supplies the rest of the finite near-full
range using a positive occupation cap when the deficit is moderate, and the
small-rank branch when Q<=d_N/2. The two sides join at Q=d_N/2 at constant
order. Thus for fixed m and N>=max{s,8},

  Q<=d_N/2: Lambda(Q)=Theta_m(N Q^(-1/s));
  Q>d_N/2, s=1: Lambda(Q)=Theta(1/log(e d_N/(d_N-Q)));
  Q>d_N/2, s>=2: Lambda(Q)=Theta_m(((d_N-Q)/d_N)^((s-1)/s)).

For s=1, the exact harmonic resistance and norm show the sharper limit

  Lambda(d_N-k) ~ 1/log(d_N/k) whenever k=o(d_N),

including bounded or fixed k. For s>=2, the audit establishes order matching
only; the leading constant and optimal kernel orientation remain unknown.

Load-bearing checks
-------------------
1. For any admissible complex A, take a rank-k projection P into its right
   kernel. AP=0 implies Tr(PA)=Tr(AP)=0. With A=aI+X and
   P_0=P-(k/d_N)I, the identity ka=-<P_0,X> and weighted Cauchy-Schwarz
   give k^2|a|^2<=<P_0,L^(-1)P_0>E(A). No positivity or normality is used.
   Thus the near-full converse does not need the positive-replacement
   reduction in the integrated proof; it follows directly for every matrix.

2. Under vectorization, Tr_2(Pi_j)=(b_j/d_N)I. The unit vector vec(P)/sqrt(k)
   has Schmidt rank k, so its projection mass is at most k b_j/d_N.
   Restoring ||P||_HS^2=k gives ||Pi_j P||_HS^2<=k^2 b_j/d_N. The factor k
   from this restoration is essential. Also sum_j||Pi_jP||^2=||P_0||^2
   =k-k^2/d_N, sharpening every K<N tail.

3. K=0 has empty low sum and tail k/lambda_1 (or the sharper
   (k-k^2/d_N)/lambda_1); K=N has no high tail. For s>=2, the cutoff
   R=(d_N/k)^(1/(2s)), K=min(N,floor R), handles both K=N and K<N without
   assuming R<=N. For s=1, K=min(N,floor sqrt(d_N/k)); the exact harmonic
   sum plus the tail is bounded by (k^2/d_N)(3+log(d_N/k)) at every finite
   1<=k<d_N. Adding 1/lambda_1=1/2 gives the stated 7/2 denominator.

4. For a radial diagonal filter, summing root-edge ladder coefficients
   gives the exact conductance
     w_l=s(N-l)binom(l+s,s).
   The harmonic/resistance-linear taper has exact unnormalized energy
   (sum_j 1/w_j)^(-1). Its zero region contains V_R=binom(R+s,s)>=k states,
   so X=I-A has rank d_N-V_R<=d_N-k; the integer rounding is in the legal
   direction. For p=1 the nullity is exactly k. The outer unit region gives
   the stated Hilbert-Schmidt norm lower bound. The finite constant checks
   include the endpoint M=floor(N/2), odd/even N, and k=1.

5. The p=1 upper file's admissible range is empty when
   floor(d_N/32)=0; this is vacuous. In the integrated all-Q proof, the
   p=1 interval k>N/8 and the s>=2 moderate-deficit interval are covered
   by the occupation-cap upper, whose energy is constant while k/d_N is
   bounded below. No range is silently inferred from the small-deficit
   taper theorem.
