1. EXACT INTERFACE

A is an explicitly encoded full-column-rank m-by-r matrix over Q(i). The modes
are partitioned into disjoint E_j with integer quotas 0<=q_j<=|E_j| summing to r.
The target's amplitude, in increasing occupation-mode order, is
  a(S)=det A_S if |S intersect E_j|=q_j for every j, and zero otherwise.
Its norm is Z=sum_S |a(S)|^2. Test Z=0 exactly before attempting normalization.
The claimed output is a randomized classical compiler producing a polynomial
size QUANTUM circuit, to trace distance epsilon with probability >=1-delta over
the compiler's classical random tapes. It supplies no certificate identifying
which tape succeeds. It is not a classical wavefunction table or preparation
of the state on the user's laptop.

2. DETERMINANT AND PREFIX OBLIGATIONS VERIFIED

f(x)=det(A*diag(x)A)=sum_{|S|=r}|det A_S|^2 x^S by complex Cauchy-Binet.
For positive real x, its matrix is Hermitian positive definite and affine, so
logdet concavity proves logconcavity. f is homogeneous multi-affine with
nonnegative REAL RATIONAL coefficients, although A itself can be complex.
No complex PSD square root is an acquired input or computation.

g(x)=product_j e_(|E_j|-q_j)(x_(E_j)) is the dual partition-matroid basis
polynomial, homogeneous of degree m-r and logconcave. Thus [x_all] f g=Z.
Weights are exact squared rational-complex determinants and 0/1 quota tests.
Clearing the explicit A entry denominators gives a common Gaussian-integer
matrix; every nonzero minor has absolute value at least an inverse exponential
in the INPUT BIT LENGTH, and permutation/Hadamard bounds give an exponential
upper bound with polynomial logarithm. Hence a uniform polynomial-logarithmic
coefficient range bound is acquired from input bits, without numerical
conditioning or an inverse-amplitude promise.

For prefix forced-in I and forced-out J, take
  f_(I,J)=x^I [partial_I f evaluated at x_J=0],
  g_(I,J)=x^J [partial_J g evaluated at x_I=0].
They retain the original degrees r,m-r, ground set and original positive
coefficient values. For a nonzero multi-affine logconcave polynomial p,
  partial_i p(x)=lim_(t->infinity) p(x+t e_i)/t.
Translation, positive scaling and limits preserve logconcavity; exclusion
follows by x_i->0+, and multiplication by x_i adds concave log x_i. Therefore
every nonzero pinned factor meets the same counting interface. This direct
limit proof avoids assuming generic logconcavity is derivative-closed outside
the multi-affine setting.

Prefix feasibility is precisely common-base feasibility for A's row linear
matroid and the partition-quota matroid, with I contracted and J deleted.
Exact Gaussian-rational rank computations and polynomial-time matroid
intersection acquire a witness or certify zero. If I is dependent or exceeds
any quota, reject immediately. Feasible prefixes have feasible children and
separate positive initializing bases for both pinned factors. A count oracle
query is one original squared minor plus membership checks, not a new black-box
oracle. This also handles inputs with full column rank but zero projected norm.

3. RANDOMIZED CONDITIONAL FUNCTIONS ARE LEGITIMATE COHERENT FUNCTIONS

At every depth use a padded polynomial-length CLASSICAL random tape, fixed in
the circuit description and shared among all prefixes. Its segments implement
independent FPRAS repetitions for median amplification; fixed segments/padding
prevent any runtime-dependent shortage or accidental overlapping repetition
randomness. For each fixed prefix the tape still has the correct uniform
distribution. Independence BETWEEN different prefixes is unnecessary.

Exact feasibility overrides empty child estimates to zero. Estimates are
nonnegative, and a failed tape returning two zeros uses a deterministic known
feasible child. This guarantees support correctness on every tape, not only
on successful tapes. Define arbitrary sentinel rotations at infeasible
prefixes, and phase 1 at det=0 or wrong cardinality. All arithmetic and phase
functions are therefore total even on strings reached only by synthesis error.

Source Theorem5.1 gives all-execution arithmetic bounds. RemarkA.4 supplies
all-execution polynomial BIT bounds by capping exact fair-coin simulations and
returning zero on a capped failure. Thus a single uniform classical program
for (A,quotas,prefix,tape) can be converted to one polynomial-size reversible
computation. Each of m quantum stages evaluates it coherently on the prefix,
performs a rotation, then reverses ALL scratch computation. The prefix remains
unchanged during the rotation, so reversing the prefix-dependent function is
valid. This never precomputes a table of exponentially many prefixes.

4. STRONGER ERROR LEMMA — FULL DERIVATION

Let p be the true normalized Born law. At prefix h, child masses are estimated
with relative tolerance eta<=1/2 and individual failure probability beta.
Normalize the two estimates to conditional q_s(b|h), using the zero/fallback
rules above. Let bad_s(h) mean that either child estimate is inaccurate. Put
  B_(s,d)=sum_(|h|=d) p(h) bad_s(h),
  B_s=sum_(d=0)^(m-1) B_(s,d).
For EVERY fixed feasible h, P_s(bad_s(h))<=2beta. Since p(h) is independent
of the compiler tapes and each depth's prefix probabilities sum to one,
  E_s B_s<=2m beta.

At a good prefix and a child with p_b>0,
  q_b/p_b in [(1-eta)/(1+eta),(1+eta)/(1-eta)].
Consequently |sqrt(q_b/p_b)-1|<=4eta and
  sum_b (sqrt(q_b)-sqrt(p_b))^2 <=16eta^2.
An empty child has both probabilities zero. At a bad prefix, the squared
distance between the two nonnegative unit conditional vectors is at most 2.

Let T_d be the ideal conditional rotation defined by p at stage d, and G_d
the ideal conditional rotation defined by this fixed tape's q_s. Their products
obey the EXACT telescoping identity
  G_m...G_1-T_m...T_1
   =sum_d G_m...G_(d+1)(G_d-T_d)T_(d-1)...T_1.
Applied to |0...0>, the rightmost T stages supply TRUE prefix amplitudes
sqrt(p(h)); every later G stage is unitary and preserves the norm. Prefix
blocks are orthogonal. Therefore stage d's state-vector error is at most
  sqrt(16eta^2+2B_(s,d)),
and the total state-vector error is at most
  4m eta + sqrt(2m B_s).
This step uses p-prefix weights, so even a bad tape's concentration of q on
bad prefixes cannot invalidate the expectation bound.

Markov gives B_s<=2m beta/delta with probability >=1-delta. On that event,
  ||psi_q-psi_p|| <=4m eta+2m sqrt(beta/delta).
Choose
  eta=epsilon/(16m), beta=delta epsilon^2/(64m^2).
Then the ideal occupation-amplitude vector error is <=epsilon/2. Applying
the SAME determinant-phase diagonal to both states preserves this norm. Pure
state trace distance is at most vector distance, so epsilon/2 remains for
all finite gate/phase synthesis errors. This improves the earlier total-
variation proof without asserting a different random-seed certification model.

5. COSTS AND SYNTHESIS

Median amplification costs O(log(1/beta)) repetitions, not beta^-1.
Writing B=m+log(R_f R_g), source Theorem5.1's arithmetic/oracle workload for
the m-stage coherent compiler is bounded, up to constants and logs, by
  O(m^13 B^2 epsilon^-2
      log^2(Bm/epsilon) log(m/(delta epsilon))).
This is an arithmetic/oracle count, not a physical gate count. Multiply by
the polynomial exact bit cost of determinant/rank/partition queries and the
reversible arithmetic overhead. Polynomial workspace, fixed tape storage,
classical compilation, and gate synthesis are charged. No near-term feasible
resource bound or quantum execution evidence follows from this polynomial.

Rotation angles must have UNIFORM ADDITIVE operator/angle error O(epsilon/m),
including near conditional probabilities 0 and 1. Relative angle accuracy or
constant absolute probability precision would not suffice. Exact endpoints
are handled explicitly; square-root/arctangent computations at polynomial
bit precision suffice for the rest. Binary angle evaluation and controlled
rotations require polynomial gates, not a separate gate for every angle.
Determinant phases are computed from exact Gaussian-rational minors using the
acquired polynomial bit-height bound. Define their zero-case sentinel and
uncompute the phase arithmetic. Choosing per-stage synthesis error below
epsilon/(4m), plus final phase error epsilon/4, gives total trace <=epsilon.

The circuit uses general quantum computation. It does not assert locality,
number-conserving native hardware gates, fault-tolerance thresholds, a quantum
advantage for energy estimation, ground-state optimization, or gap certification.
If local-qudit rather than occupation-bit encoding is required, the quota
encoding is a further elementary reversible isometry with polynomial cost;
fermionic ordering conventions must be fixed in that isometry.
