RANK-ONE COUPLING OF SMALL BLOCKS — EXACT COUNTING AND SAMPLING
c02_l05, 7 October 2026. Revision to immutable INITIAL.

RESULT
Let G=diag(G_1,...,G_m) be block diagonal over rational complex entries, with block sizes at most b, and let F=G+u v^T be a rank-one (possibly dense) update with rational complex u,v. For the determinant-weighted parity coefficients
 c_k(F)=sum_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k} |det F[I,J]|^2,
there is an exact polynomial-bit coefficient algorithm and exact conditional sampler whenever b=O(log n). The algorithm uses phase-aware local determinants and at most O(m^2) positive defect categories. It does not assume stability, an FPRAS, or an oracle for smaller hole partitions. It is a new acquired subclass candidate; prior-art priority is unverified.

The class includes arbitrary dense rank-one interblock coupling, unlike the block-diagonal result in INITIAL. It remains a narrow structural subclass of general F and does not give the microscopic energy comparison for arbitrary matrices.

LOCAL DATA
For each block t, enumerate all disjoint I_t,J_t subset B_t. For balanced choices |I_t|=|J_t|=ell, put
 D_t=det G_t[I_t,J_t],
 Delta_t=v_{J_t}^T adj(G_t[I_t,J_t])u_{I_t}.
Here Delta_t=0 at ell=0. Define polynomials
 A_t(z)=sum_bal |D_t|^2 z^ell,
 B_t(z)=sum_bal D_t overline(Delta_t) z^ell,
 C_t(z)=sum_bal |Delta_t|^2 z^ell.
For row-surplus configurations |I_t|=ell+1, |J_t|=ell, define
 eta_t(I_t,J_t)=sum_{p=1}^{ell+1} (-1)^p u_{i_p} det G_t[I_t\{i_p},J_t],
 U_t(z)=sum_{row-surplus} |eta_t|^2 z^ell.
For column-surplus configurations |I_t|=ell, |J_t|=ell+1, define
 theta_t(I_t,J_t)=sum_{q=1}^{ell+1} (-1)^q v_{j_q} det G_t[I_t,J_t\{j_q}],
 V_t(z)=sum_{column-surplus} |theta_t|^2 z^ell.
A consistent 0-based cofactor convention changes only a common sign. All local sums use actual complex determinants/cofactors before taking squared magnitudes.

PROOF OF THE COUNT FORMULA
Fix disjoint global I,J with |I|=|J|=k. Write r_t=|I_t|, s_t=|J_t| and M=G[I,J]. Since M is block diagonal with possibly rectangular blocks,
 rank(M) <= sum_t min(r_t,s_t) = k - (1/2)sum_t |r_t-s_t|.
If det(M+u_I v_J^T) is nonzero, the rank-one update can increase rank by at most one, so rank(M)>=k-1. Therefore sum |r_t-s_t|<=2. Since sum(r_t-s_t)=0, there are only two possible count patterns:
(A) every block is balanced r_t=s_t; or
(B) exactly one ordered pair (a,b), a!=b, has r_a=s_a+1 and s_b=r_b+1, with every other block balanced.
All other row/column block patterns contribute zero.

In case (A), each M_t=G_t[I_t,J_t] is square and M is block diagonal. The matrix determinant lemma in its adjugate form, valid also for singular matrices, gives
 det(M+u_Iv_J^T)=det(M)+v_J^T adj(M)u_I
 = product_t D_t + sum_t Delta_t product_{s!=t}D_s.
Let G0=product_t D_t and G1=sum_t Delta_t product_{s!=t}D_s. Summing over all independent local balanced choices, define global moment polynomials P,Q,R by
 P'=P A_t,
 Q'=Q A_t + P B_t,
 R'=R A_t + P C_t + overline(Q)B_t + Q overline(B_t),
starting from P=1,Q=R=0. The recurrences follow by expanding respectively |G0|^2, G0 overline(G1), and |G1|^2 after appending one block. Thus the total balanced contribution to c_k is
 [z^k](P+Q+overline(Q)+R).
The final polynomial is coefficientwise real and nonnegative because it is the sum of |G0+G1|^2 over local choices; intermediate Q may be complex.

In case (B), det(M)=0. In v^T adj(M)u, a cofactor can be nonzero only when its deleted row is in the unique surplus block a and its deleted column is in the unique deficit block b: deleting any other row/column leaves at least one rectangular block imbalance. Deleting such a row and column makes every local block square. Cofactor expansion then factorizes the updated determinant, up to a global sign,
 det(M+u_Iv_J^T)= +/- eta_a(I_a,J_a) theta_b(I_b,J_b) product_{t!=a,b} D_t.
The sign disappears after squaring. The total weight for this count pattern is therefore
 [z^{k-1}] U_a(z)V_b(z) product_{t!=a,b} A_t(z).
Summing over ordered a!=b and adding case (A) proves the formula
 c_k(F)=[z^k](P+Q+overline Q+R)
       + sum_{a!=b}[z^{k-1}]U_a V_b product_{t!=a,b}A_t.
All endpoint categories are disjoint in (I,J), so no cross terms between them occur.

EXACT SAMPLER
First choose the balanced category or an ordered defect pair (a,b), with probabilities equal to their exact nonnegative weights in the count formula.

For a chosen defect pair, the local squared weights factor as U_a, V_b and A_t at other blocks. A standard suffix charge DP samples the local charge ell_t conditioned on total sum k-1, then samples each block configuration proportionally to |eta|^2, |theta|^2, or |D|^2. This returns a pair (I,J) with exactly the determinant-squared law.

For the balanced category, precompute suffix moment polynomials (P_s,Q_s,R_s) by the same recurrence. During sequential block sampling, maintain prefix scalars
 d=product_{t in prefix}D_t,
 e=sum_{t in prefix} Delta_t product_{r in prefix, r!=t}D_r,
 p=sum_{t in prefix} ell_t.
For a candidate next local balanced configuration with values (D,Delta) and charge ell, update d'=dD, e'=eD+dDelta, alpha=d'+e', beta=d'. Its exact completion mass is
 [z^{k-p-ell}](|alpha|^2 P_s + alpha overline(beta) Q_s
                         + overline(alpha) beta overline(Q_s)
                         + |beta|^2 R_s),
where s is the remaining suffix. This is a sum of squared determinants over all completions, so it is a nonnegative rational despite the cross-term form. These candidate masses sum to the current conditional mass. Sampling each local choice from these exact masses yields the balanced determinant-squared law. If the balanced category has zero mass it is skipped. Clearing rational denominators and rejection from a power-of-two interval gives an exact discrete draw with expected fewer than two trials.

COSTS AND INTERFACES
Each block has at most 3^b disjoint row/column assignments total across balanced and one-defect categories. Determinants and all deletion minors can be computed in poly(b,L) exact rational bit operations; a direct cofactors implementation remains polynomial. Local preprocessing is O(m 3^b poly(b,L)). Moment-polynomial convolution costs O(m n b) arithmetic operations for degree at most n/2. A naive leave-two-block-out DP for all m(m-1) defect pairs costs O(m^2 n b), still polynomial; polynomial products can be shared to improve it. Conditional suffix tables have polynomial size. For b=O(log n), 3^b is polynomial. Exact rational numerators/denominators remain polynomial-bit because each term is a product of at most O(n) input rationals and each local/global sum has polynomially many terms. Sampling has polynomial expected random-bit cost. Empty sectors are detected exactly by c_k=0.

This count/sample interface is not supplied by the general Hamada–Iwata support algorithm; that imported algorithm provides optimization/support, not the weighted coefficient sum used here. The baseline's unresolved dense-F energy comparison remains outside this theorem.
