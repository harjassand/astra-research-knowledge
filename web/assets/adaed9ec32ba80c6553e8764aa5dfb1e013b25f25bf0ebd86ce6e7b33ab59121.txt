FINITE U(N) WITNESS REPAIR: PRODUCT-CONJUGATE BELL OUTPUT
2026-10-08

Status

The cycle4 U(N) matrices do not justify applying the 2026 source's real-
orthogonal quotient to Phi_N tensor Phi_N. That self-tensor transfer is
withdrawn. The same U(N) construction does admit the quotient witness for
Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N), by a direct Stinespring/vectorization argument below.
This repairs the finite entropy comparison without changing the score net,
the U(N) random-matrix estimates, or N. The quotient-Gram majorization holds
for every finite representation, so no Bell trace approximation is needed.
It proves pairwise nonadditivity conditionally on the stated free-radius
input. A standard flagged channel
then converts the pair witness into a same-channel tensor-square witness,
at one extra input flag qubit per use. This extension is internally derived;
the cited 2026 source states the corresponding theorem only for real
orthogonal self-tensors.

1. Exact product-conjugate branch vectors and quotient

Let m=N^2, Gamma=F_65 x F_65, and let pi_N:Gamma -> U(m) be the tensor-leg
representation built from independent Haar U(N) generators. Write S=S^{-1}
for its K=260 branch labels and W_s=pi_N(s). Define

  E_N(X) = (1/K) sum_{s in S} W_s X W_s*,
  Phi_N(X)_{s,t} = (1/K) Tr(W_s X W_t*).

Then Phi_N is complementary to E_N, and bar(Phi_N) is complementary to the
complex-conjugate random-unitary channel bar(E_N). For the normalized Bell
vector Omega_m, the Stinespring vector for E_N tensor bar(E_N) is

  Psi_N = (1/K) sum_{s,t in S} eta_{s,t} tensor |s,t>,
  eta_{s,t} = (W_s tensor bar(W_t)) Omega_m
            = vec(W_s W_t*)/sqrt(m)
            = vec(pi_N(s t^{-1}))/sqrt(m).

The equality uses (A tensor bar(B))Omega=vec(AB*)/sqrt(m), valid for every
complex unitary B. Thus the branch pair (s,t) is labelled by the honest group
element nu(s,t)=s t^{-1}. The map nu has exactly the same fibre sizes as the
source map mu(a,b)=a^{-1}b: the bijection (a,b)=(s^{-1},t^{-1}) gives
mu(a,b)=s t^{-1}. This uses S=S^{-1}, and is valid at r=2,d=65.

For each distinct g in T=S S^{-1}, let n_g=|nu^{-1}(g)|,
q_g=n_g/K^2, and u_g=n_g^{-1/2} sum_{nu(s,t)=g}|s,t>. Define the isometry
U|g>=u_g and the complex Gram matrix

  R_N[g,h] = <pi_N(h),pi_N(g)>_HS/m
            = tau_m(pi_N(h^{-1}g)),
  B_N = D_q^(1/2) R_N D_q^(1/2).

The branch vectors are unit vectors, so R_N is Hermitian positive
semidefinite with diagonal 1. Grouping Psi_N by nu and tracing out the
system gives, up to the harmless branch-label permutation induced by the
inner-product convention,

  (Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N))(Omega_m Omega_m*) = U B_N U*.

This identity needs neither real matrices nor a self-conjugate channel. Since
diag(B_N)=q, Schur-Horn gives q majorized by the eigenvalue vector of B_N.
Therefore

  S((Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N))(Omega_m Omega_m*)) <= H(q).

For the free regular representation, R_F=I on distinct group labels and
B_F=D_q, so the quotient has eigenvalues q plus zeros. For r=2,d=65,
K=260, the fibre counts are: one size-K identity fibre; 16,900 size-2
mixed fibres; and 33,540 singleton fibres. Thus |T|=50,441 and

  q=(1/K once, 2/K^2 repeated K^2/4 times,
     1/K^2 repeated K^2/2-K times),
  H(q)=2 ln K - (ln K)/K - (ln 2)/2
      = 10.753402434708715... nats.

The vector q is an entropy-majorizing profile for finite representations;
it is not generally their exact finite Bell spectrum. The free regular
quotient has exactly this spectrum.

2. Optional finite trace approximation diagnostic

This section records a stronger-than-needed finite trace approximation to
the free Bell quotient. It is not used in the exact entropy bound or the
probabilistic existence theorem: the quotient-Gram Schur-Horn argument in
Section 1 already gives S(Bell)<=H(q) for every finite representation.

The product-conjugate output entry indexed by row (s,t) and column (u,v) is
the normalized trace

  Z_N[(s,t),(u,v)] = K^-2 tau_m(pi_N((u v^{-1})^{-1}(s t^{-1}))).

Equivalently, the word is a product of four branch letters, hence has total
reduced length at most four in F_65 x F_65. The free-regular Bell quotient
entry is K^-2 when nu(s,t)=nu(u,v), and zero otherwise. There are at most
K^4 labels. If each raw normalized trace is within delta, each Bell entry
is within delta/K^2. The full Frobenius error is then at most delta, so the
trace distance is at most K delta/2. The corrected tolerance is
delta=3.4766193e-7 for Bell entropy error 0.001 nats, and the corresponding
finite Bell threshold is N>=803,799,820; see rough_gate.py. The earlier
packet used a valid but unnecessarily conservative K^2-smaller raw-trace
tolerance.

For a nontrivial reduced word of length at most four in independent Haar
U(N) generators, phase invariance makes its expected normalized trace zero
unless every generator is balanced. The only nonidentity balanced reduced
words at these lengths are length-four commutators of two distinct
generators; Schur averaging gives expected trace 1/N^2. Tensor-leg traces
factor, so the same N^-2 bound holds for nontrivial words in Gamma. For
f(w)=tau_m(pi_N(w)), telescoping gives product-Hilbert-Schmidt Lipschitz
constant at most 4/sqrt(N). Meckes Theorem 5.17 and a union bound over K^4
labels give

  Pr(max_w |f(w)-tau_F(w)|>delta)
    <= 4 K^4 exp(-(N-2)N delta^2/3072),

provided N^-2<=delta/2; the displayed N gate in rough_gate.py makes this
failure probability at most 1/6. This U(N) trace argument must not be
transferred to Haar O(N): E tau_N(O^2)=1/N, not 1/N^2.

3. Additivity conclusions and the self-tensor flag

The one-copy score bound gives S_min(Phi_N)>=
-ln((K+(C+s)^2)/K^2), with C^2=50 and s=0.165. Also
S_min(bar(Phi_N))=S_min(Phi_N), since conjugation bijects input states and
preserves output entropy. The repaired Bell witness therefore gives

  S_min(Phi_N)+S_min(bar(Phi_N))
  - S((Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N))(Omega_m Omega_m*))
    >= 0.001007014607... nats

whenever the score event holds. At N=16(130)^14 p^836 from rough_gate.py,
with p=15,999,300,534, the score
event has probability at least 2/3, conditional on the free-radius bound
C^2=50. The Bell entropy upper bound is exact and incurs no continuity loss.

If a same-channel tensor-square statement is desired, define

  Xi_N(X) = Phi_N(X_00) + bar(Phi_N)(X_11),
  Xi_N: M_(2m) -> M_K,

for the block decomposition M_(2m)=M_m direct-sum M_m, discarding the
off-diagonal blocks. This is CPTP. Entropy concavity and
S_min(bar(Phi_N))=S_min(Phi_N) imply S_min(Xi_N)=S_min(Phi_N). The pure
input Omega_01=m^-1/2 sum_a |0,a> tensor |1,a> lies in flag-0 tensor
flag-1, and

  (Xi_N tensor Xi_N)(Omega_01 Omega_01*)
    = (Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N))(Omega_m Omega_m*).

Thus the same positive gap is a self-additivity witness for Xi_N. The flag
increases each input dimension from m to 2m, i.e. by one qubit-equivalent
dimension. This flag conversion is standard and is not a new mechanism.

4. Constant audit correction

The bound c_E<=64 exp(5/2)=779.6796...<780 survives. One missing proof
detail in the earlier draft is that a shifted multiplicity-three Gaussian
factor does not vanish: F_(2,1)(theta)=2 theta+theta^3. For total
multiplicity r=a+b, the exact coordinate factor is

  F_(a,b)(theta) = sum_{t=0}^{min(a,b)}
      binom(a,t) binom(b,t) t! theta^(r-2t).

For r=1 this is theta; for r=2,3 it is at most (1+theta)^r; for r>=4,
using t!<=r^(r/2) and binom(a,t)binom(b,t)<=binom(r,2t) gives
F_(a,b)<=r^(r/2)(1+theta)^r. Multiplying over coordinates recovers
theta^(e1)(1+theta)^q q^(m4/2) with no extra multiplier. The complete
path constant derivation is in luna_constant_audit.txt. Corollary 14's
q=1 theorem hypotheses should not be invoked as a direct shortcut.

5. Consequence for the prior cycle4 claim

The prior claim that the imported source quotient is unchanged for
Phi_N tensor Phi_N with complex Haar U(N) branches is false. In the source,
real orthogonality makes (W_s* tensor W_t*)Omega a vector labelled by one
relative group operation. For complex U(N), it instead vectorizes
W_s* overline(W_t), and mixed-fibre partners need not collide. The direct
U(N) self-tensor quotient is therefore not established. The product-
conjugate proof above is the precise repair; it does not prove the original
self-tensor assertion for Phi_N, though the flagged Xi_N construction gives
a valid self-tensor channel with a one-bit input-flag cost.
