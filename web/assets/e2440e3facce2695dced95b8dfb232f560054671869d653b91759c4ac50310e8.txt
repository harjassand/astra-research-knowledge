Strongest concrete consequence.
Positive binary tensor contractions with complementary edge couplers and polynomially evaluable locally log-concave signatures admit relative counting, arbitrarily rare evidence estimation, and conditional sampling in polynomial time, with no dependence on inverse evidence probability or treewidth. The same oracles give inverse maximum-entropy inference with an explicit interiority cost. This is a clean consequence of Chen–Liu's counting theorem, not a new counting mechanism. Equality-coupled tensor networks need an additional complement/gauge condition; quantum amplitude squaring can destroy the local premise.

2. Exact tensor/evidence reduction.
Interface. Let G be a finite loopless multigraph, with m edges, H its N=2m distinct halfedges, and H_v the halfedges incident at v. A local tensor is f_v:2^{H_v}->Q_{>=0}, supplied by a polynomial-bit evaluation algorithm. Require the NONZERO multiaffine polynomial
  p_v(x)=sum_A f_v(A) product_{h in A} x_h
to be log-concave on strictly positive variables. Supply one A_v with f_v(A_v)>0 and a bound R_v >= max_positive f_v/min_positive f_v. Write Lambda=sum_v log R_v. The local degree and graph treewidth may grow. Tensor tables of exponential size are not supplied for free; evaluation costs are charged. Global checking of arbitrary oracle log-concavity is NOT provided: the theorem uses a structural promise/certificate, e.g. a matroid basis polynomial, real-stable determinant polynomial, or a fully verified explicit signature.

Define
  Z_X = sum_{A subset H: |A intersect {h_e^0,h_e^1}|=1 for every e}
              product_v f_v(A intersect H_v).
Equivalently each edge contracts its two tensor bits through the nonnegative coupler X=[[0,1],[1,0]], rather than through equality delta.
Proof. P(x)=product_v p_v(x_{H_v}) is multiaffine, nonzero, log-concave: log P=sum_v log p_v on disjoint variables. Its coefficient omega(A)=product_v f_v(A_v) is evaluated with one local call per vertex, has seed union A_v, and R_omega <= product_v R_v. Let M_X be the partition matroid having the two halfedges of each edge as a capacity1 block, rank m. Then Z_X=sum_{A in B(M_X)} omega(A), exactly. Invoke LemmaC.1. Equivalently
  Z_X=[x^H] P(x) product_e (x_{h_e^0}+x_{h_e^1}).
The elementary reduction is proved; existence of the FPRAS is conditional on Chen–Liu.

Rare evidence. Choose any set D of edges and specify which halfedge is selected on every e in D. In M_X make the forbidden halfedge on each observed edge a loop; the allowed block is a singleton. Leave other blocks unchanged. The new matroid still has rank m. Its weighted basis sum Z_D is EXACTLY the unnormalized evidence mass. Critically, omega, its original support seed, and its coefficient-range bound are unchanged. No new positive seed conditioned on rare evidence is assumed. Greedy initialization of the dual matroid is immediate. Thus each pinned count has the same polynomial complexity as Z_X. If Z_X,Z_D>0, two relative estimates with error epsilon/4 give relative epsilon estimation of P(D)=Z_D/Z_X, however tiny that probability is. Zero results have the imported FPRAS confidence guarantee, not an unproved deterministic feasibility algorithm for arbitrary tensor oracles.

Conditional sampling. At each unobserved edge estimate the two further pinned masses to relative eta=epsilon/(8m), failure probability beta=delta/(4m); choose the halfedge using their normalized estimated masses. On the joint good event, each branching probability changes by <=O(eta), and sequential coupling gives TV <=O(m eta). The total bad-count probability is <=delta. Use smaller constants and delta<=epsilon/2 for TV epsilon. At most 2m amplified FPRAS calls are needed, with eta^-2 overhead and log(1/beta) repetition. Output may include infeasible configurations only on the bounded bad event. There is NO inverse Z_D/Z_X factor; the high polynomial in N and epsilon^-1 is fully retained. The source FPRAS/sampler was not implemented here.

Equality couplers. On bipartite G=(L,R), an ordinary tensor contraction
  Z_delta=sum_{bits on edges} product_v f_v(bits restricted at v)
is transformed to Z_X by complementing ALL local bits at each v in R. The required polynomials are p_v for v in L and
  p_v^*(x)=product_{h in H_v} x_h * p_v(1/x)
for v in R. Both sets must be log-concave. This follows for appropriate real-stable signatures, but cannot be inferred from arbitrary original log-concavity alone. A graph-specific endpoint-flip gauge is also allowed IF every resulting local polynomial is independently known log-concave. Bipartiteness avoids partial inversions; it does not remove the full-complement premise. The independent tensor_contraction_audit worker has an exact homogeneous cubic counterexample to full-complement closure and should be consulted for its detailed certificate.

3. Inverse inference: a complete finite-radius lemma and conditional algorithm.
Fix a positive instance above, possibly with pinned evidence. Encode each remaining edge's selected endpoint as X_e in {0,1}; let Omega be the set of positive configurations and w(x)>0 their base weights. Supply their affine hull x0+V, a rational target alpha, and a positive interiority promise eta such that alpha+{v in V:||v||2<=eta} lies in conv(Omega). These data/interiority are additional INPUTS, not silently acquired from noisy observations. Give a bound Lambda on log(max w/min w). There are at most 2^m configurations.

For theta in V define q_theta(x)=w(x)exp(theta dot x)/Z(theta), and Phi(theta)=log Z(theta)-theta dot alpha. Each tilt is implemented by positive variable rescaling at one designated endpoint per edge, which preserves local log-concavity. Thus the tensor FPRAS and sampler apply at every field. The field sizes add at most ||theta||1 to the log coefficient range.

Lemma (radius). There is a minimizing theta* in V satisfying
  ||theta*||2 <= R := (m log2 + Lambda)/eta.
Proof. Normalize min w=1 for the proof only. The interior-ball promise gives h_{conv(Omega)}(theta)-theta dot alpha >= eta||theta||2. Since Z(theta)>=exp h(theta), Phi(theta)>=eta||theta||2. Meanwhile Phi(0)=log sum w<=m log2+Lambda. Coercivity in V gives an attained minimum, and comparing the minimizer with theta=0 proves the bound. Normalization contributes only an irrelevant additive constant and need not be computed.

The weighted maximum-entropy solution with prescribed alpha is q*=q_theta*: stationarity gives E_q* X=alpha, and the Gibbs variational identity proves the weighted entropy optimum. Moreover,
  KL(q* || q_theta)=Phi(theta)-Phi(theta*).
This identity follows by substituting the two Gibbs densities and using E_q* X=alpha. Hence objective gap <=2 epsilon^2 implies TV(q*,q_theta)<=epsilon by Pinsker.

Concrete oracle algorithm. Project noisy gradient descent onto the radius2R ball in the supplied V and average iterates. True gradients E_qtheta X-alpha have norm <=sqrt(m). For approximate gradients of Euclidean error <=rho and norm <=2sqrt(m), the standard squared-distance update yields expected/conditional-good-event gap <=2R sqrt(m)/sqrt(T)+3R rho using a step of order R/(sqrt(mT)). Set T polynomial in R,sqrt(m),epsilon^-2 and rho=O(epsilon^2/R); then the gap is O(epsilon^2). Hoeffding estimates of all m marginal coordinates to rho/sqrt(m), using polynomially many independent approximate samples and a union bound over T, provide the gradient oracle. Choose each sampler TV error O(rho/sqrt(m)). Rational approximations to exp(theta_e) with per-coordinate log error O(rho/m) preserve the polynomial premise through exact positive variable rescaling and perturb the law by controlled TV. Their bit size is polynomial in the numerical radius R and requested log precision. Thus a polynomial-bit randomized inverse-inference algorithm follows conditionally, charging eta^-1, Lambda, epsilon^-1, confidence, local evaluations and the imported high counting degree. It is not polynomial in merely log(1/eta).

This general entropy/counting connection is prior art: Singh–Vishnoi, Entropy, Optimization and Counting, https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.8108 . The novel INPUT is the new countability theorem. The elementary radius proof and stated projected-gradient bounds are internally reconstructed here. No new identifiability/statistical sample-complexity result follows: directions orthogonal to V are gauges, boundary targets can require diverging fields, and empirical marginals may lack the required interiority. This is classical constrained maximum entropy, not generic quantum tomography.

4. More than two: distinguish convolution from common intersection.
POSITIVE consequence. A k-way subset convolution DOES reduce to the same two-factor theorem. Give each mu_i its own copy of every original coordinate, forming H=U x [k]. The product P=product_i g_mu_i(x_{U x {i}}) is multiaffine log-concave. An exact-once partition matroid across the k copies of each u counts disjoint S_1,...,S_k whose union is U. This counts [x^U] product_i g_mu_i(x), in polynomial time in k|U|, log coefficient ranges and accuracy. It does not count common bases. Chen–Liu C.18 already handles many-player matroid load constraints, so a blanket claim that all >2-factor consequences are blocked would be false.

Curvature check. For a k-copy block, psi_t(X)=t^{max(|X|-1,0)}. Its dual at Y is t^{max(k-|Y|-1,0)}. At a state with ell missing copies, the upward curvature1 matrix is: ell>=3, ((1-t)/t^2) I_ell; ell=2, ((1-t)/t^2) J_2; ell<=1, zero. Each is PSD. This generalizes the two-copy overlap block exactly; the broader matroid rank-penalty curvature statement is already in Chen–Liu LemmaD.2.

NEGATIVE consequence. Three COMMON bases encode perfect three-dimensional matching: ground elements are allowed triples, and the three partition matroids choose one triple for every vertex in their respective coordinate class. Existence is NP-complete; a general weighted sampler that either produces a verified common basis or correctly reports none, or an FPRAS combined with self-reduction/verification, would give NP=RP. Primary supporting source: Kashiwabara–Okamoto–Uno, Matroid representation of clique complexes, https://research.nii.ac.jp/~uno/joint/cliqueWEB.pdf , discussing the three-partition-matroid/3DM obstruction. The reduction itself is elementary and explicit above.

Tiny exact obstruction. Allowed triples are 000,011,101,110, with two vertices in each coordinate class. Each of the three partition matroids has four rank2 bases; the vector (1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2) lies in every base polytope. There is no common basis because no two allowed triples are coordinatewise complements. For the softened law on B(M1) x B(M2) x B(M3) with weight
  t^{|S1 symmetric_difference S2|+|S1 symmetric_difference S3|},
simultaneous complementation preserves all three supports and the weight. Every membership marginal is exactly1/2; the two equality-residual mean vectors are exactly0 at zero field for EVERY t>0, but exact agreement probability is0. Near stationarity alone cannot replace the uniform-gap/structural premise.

Local reason the natural equality penalty is outside the curvature class. On two binary copies, agreement weight a00=a11=1, a01=a10=t has curvature0 matrix at empty set
  [[t^2,t^2-1],[t^2-1,t^2]],
whose eigenvalue along (1,1) is 2t^2-1<0 for t<1/sqrt2. Equality introduces a ferromagnetic direction; the Chen–Liu overlap penalty supplies a repulsive rank penalty instead.

5. Additional exact failed transfers.
Partial inversion: the stable, homogeneous log-concave local polynomial p(x,y)=x+y becomes 1+xy after flipping x only. At x=y=1/2 its log Hessian is [[-4/25,16/25],[16/25,-4/25]], with positive eigenvalue12/25. Arbitrary edge-by-edge gauges therefore cannot preserve even this very small local premise.

Quantum amplitude squaring: let
 p=3(x1x2+x3x4)+2(x1x3+x2x4)+2(x1x4+x2x3).
Its constant Hessian has eigenvalues7,-1,-3,-3. A nonnegative homogeneous quadratic with only one positive Hessian eigenvalue is log-concave (the reverse-Cauchy/Lorentz signature criterion), so p satisfies the premise globally. Squaring the six tensor entries replaces 3,2,2 by9,4,4. Its Hessian has eigenvalues17,1,-9,-9; at all variables1, the log Hessian is positive along (1,1,-1,-1), eigenvalue1/34. Thus even real positive tensors can leave the tractable class when one forms Born/norm weights. General phases/cancellation, PEPS norm contraction, coherent preparation and tomography remain outside this result.

Continuous constraints: X~N(0,t) has zero mean, a Poincare constant t, and an arbitrarily strong inverse-gap1/t under the standard diffusion normalization, yet P(X=0)=0 for every t>0. Exact affine fibers have zero ambient probability; the lattice atom/conductor argument in Lemma2.5 cannot be applied. A tube of width delta has probability of order delta/sqrt(t) when delta<<sqrt(t). One needs a separate fiber/coarea/precision theorem; uniform curvature and small mean residual are insufficient. Standard continuous log-concave conditioning algorithms are existing literature, not a new consequence asserted here.
