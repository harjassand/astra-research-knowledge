# c09_l10 FINAL — finite-jet blindness and posterior-regret acquisition

**Status:** internally derived finite-jet counterexample, corrected after a

## Main result and interface

Fix finite dimension `m >= 1` and finite jet order `K >= 0`. Let
`lambda` be uniform probability on `[-1,1]`, `mu_c=lambda^m`, and `mu_d`
the product of `n=ceil((K+3)/2)`-point Gauss-Legendre probability rules. With
`tau=1/(4 sqrt(m))`, define under either reference measure

    dP_v^mu/dmu(x) = 1 + tau <v,x>,  ||v||_2 <= 1.

These are positive analytic likelihoods relative to their respective
references. The continuous reference has full cube support; the quadrature
reference is finite atomic. Tensor Gauss exactness through degree `K+2`
matches all such moments. The corrected derivative is

    partial^alpha F_ij(0)
      = (-1)^|alpha| |alpha|! tau^(|alpha|+2)
        E_mu[x_i x_j x^alpha],

with **no** `alpha!` denominator. Hence the Fisher metric `K`-jets agree,
`F(0)=(tau^2/3) I`, and score-product tensors through order `K+2` agree.
Both metrics remain between `(4/5)(tau^2/3)I` and
`(4/3)(tau^2/3)I` throughout the parameter ball.

For the finite model, storing the atom index and returning that atom is an
exact parameter-independent archive with `D=n^m` labels, so
`B_d(epsilon) <= m log_2 n` for every epsilon. For the continuous model, the
bounded proper-score proof in `revisions/001-erratum-posterior-regret.md`
gives, under the two-sided parameter-blind archive interface,

    B_q(epsilon) >= (m/2) log_2(tau/(2 pi e epsilon))

for sufficiently small epsilon. It allows arbitrary unassisted quantum
memory: the encoder/decoder channel first forces mean-square recovery of each
input coordinate under the common uniform reference, then differential
entropy and Holevo/data-processing give the bit bound.

Therefore no fixed finite Fisher jet, even with the indicated finite score
products, decides whether one-sample archive memory is bounded or diverges.
This is a sequence of pairs indexed by `K`; it does not match one pair's
infinite jet or entire Fisher field. The Carathéodory extension in the
revision gives at most `binom(m+K+2,K+2)` atoms for a general compact
reference with finite differential entropy and nonsingular covariance.

## Repaired derivation and evidence

Sol worker `c09_s01` identified that the INITIAL's Fisher-derivative display
had an extra `1/alpha!`; the exact moment-matching conclusion survives. The
frozen INITIAL remains unchanged. The initial script also displayed Taylor
coefficients as derivatives. The corrected check uses exact rational moments
and the actual derivative coefficient: `finite_jet_derivative_check.py`
passes 21 moment identities through degree five, 40 Fisher derivative
entries, and 62 score-product entries through order five, with zero displayed
residual. It is a finite equality diagnostic, not the proof. The proof uses
Gauss exactness and the posterior-regret/entropy argument.

The decoder must be independent of `v`; if it knows `v`, it can sample
`P_v` with `D=1`. The finite likelihood is analytic relative to its atomic
reference measure, not a Lebesgue density. Archive cost counts all
input-dependent memory, but does not charge model description, computation,
continuous decoder sampling, finite-bit generation, or the acquisition of
quadrature/reference-law data. No physical sensor or end-to-end finite-bit
codec is established.

## Prior art, novelty, and remaining gate

Hayashi–Tan formulate blind distribution-reconstruction codes with TV error,
but their main `d/2 log n` result is an `n`-iid asymptotic, distinct from this
fixed one-draw `epsilon -> 0` contrast: [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.02542v2).
Varshney–Varshney use mean Bayes-risk distortion for quantizing prior
probabilities, and Buscemi gives quantum decision-theoretic sufficiency;
these are direct antecedents to the regret viewpoint:
[Varshney–Varshney](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.4338),
[Buscemi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.3794v4).
Finite positive moment cubature is established Tchakaloff/Carathéodory
theory: [Curto–Fialkow](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-247X(02)00034-3).
The exact archive application did not receive exhaustive priority search.

**Remaining gate:** establish a weaker, broadly useful joint-posterior
condition that yields both a positive acquired codec and a matching lower
bound, with model/statistic acquisition, sampler, precision, random bits,
runtime, and output metric charged. This worker's result is a concrete
finite-jet obstruction and not that general theorem.
