1. EXACT K_k HULL AND PHYSICAL SECTOR FORMULA

Let K_k be the uniform k-subset average of P_sym,k/(k+1) tensored with white
complement sites. It is trace one, positive, permutation invariant, and SU(2)
invariant. A pure coherent Haar average is P_sym,k/(k+1). For a nonidentity
Pauli word supported on r sites, its support lies inside a uniform subset with
probability binom(N-r,k-r)/binom(N,k). The spherical moment is zero when an
exponent is odd and otherwise
 ((a-1)!!(b-1)!!(c-1)!!)/(r+1)!!.
The Pauli coefficient has the additional 2^-N, with NO word multiplicity in
the SUM-of-distinct-words coefficient. All numbers have polynomial bit length.
Fixed-four-letter orbit conversion has polynomially many type coefficients;
one may also bypass this optional certificate table and use sector columns.

The positive heat import expresses the isotropic G as a positive mixture of
angular averages of tau_(r n)^tensor N. The expansion
 tau_(r n)=r|n><n|+(1-r)I/2
has nonnegative Bernstein weights binom(N,k)r^k(1-r)^(N-k), summing to one.
Therefore G belongs to the finite hull of the K_k. This is a feasibility
argument only; acquiring a radial law is unnecessary.

For b=0,...,floor(N/2), j_b=N/2-b, multiplicity
 d_b=binom(N,b)-binom(N,b-1), dimension D_b=(N-2b+1)d_b.
The multiplicity follows by differencing adjacent magnetization dimensions.
Mutual SU(2)/permutation invariance makes K_k and G scalar on each joint block.

The whole magnetization-t probability of K_k is
 M_t=[(k+1)2^(N-k)]^-1 sum_(l=0)^k binom(N-k,t-l).
For t=b on the lower half, M_b-M_(b-1) is the sector's per-magnetization mass.
Consequently
 A_bk=(N-2b+1)[binom(N-k,b)-binom(N-k,b-k-1)]/
                          [(k+1)2^(N-k)].
The two binomials are the telescoped endpoint terms. They are not an added
spin multiplicity. Invariance also proves A_bk>=0 and sum_b A_bk=1.

With q_b the isotropic sector probabilities, the physical eigenvalue in
sector b is q_b/D_b, repeated D_b times. Thus EXACTLY
 T(G,sum_k w_k K_k)=1/2 sum_b |q_b-(Aw)_b|.
No trace in an unweighted regular/commutant representation is substituted.
Checks of this formula independently constructed J^2 and its spectral
projectors as full rational operators for N=1,...,4; see Section 11.

2. FILTER, CONDITIONAL BLOCKS, AND BRANCH DISINTEGRATION

F=exp[-delta Jz^2/(2N)+hJz/2]. Its squared condition number is at most e^C,
 C=delta N/4+N|h|.
For positive trace-one X,Y, the trace ideal and normalization inequalities give
 T(Phi_F(X),Phi_F(Y)) <= ||F(X-Y)F||_1/Tr(FXF)
                         <=2 e^C T(X,Y).
No commutation assumption is needed for this norm inequality.

For complement size n=N-k, a u-one string has m=u-n/2. On the subset,
 z=l-k/2, and the exponent is
 -delta(z+m)^2/N+h(z+m)
 =-delta z^2/N+(h-2delta m/N)z+(hm-delta m^2/N).
The scalar last term cancels in the normalized conditional block. Hence it is
the SAME one-site tensor filter of S_k(delta k/N), with effective
 delta'=delta k/N<=1, h'=h-2delta m/N.
This is the actual iid-cone admission; permutation symmetry alone would not
provide it. The original I1 measure remains positive under the local tilt.

Before filtering, the subset block has coefficient 1/(k+1) and each complement
string has coefficient 2^-n. Its conditional partition function is
 Z_ku=sum_(l=0)^k exp[-delta(l+u-N/2)^2/N+h(l+u-N/2)].
There are binom(n,u) such strings, and so the TYPE mass is precisely
 B_ku=w_k binom(n,u)Z_ku/[(k+1)2^n].
Uniformly selecting a k-subset and a u-one string after selecting this type
recovers Phi_F(sum w K_k). No subset or string multiplicity is missing.

There are M=(N+1)(N+2)/2 types, not exponentially many stored strings. All Z_ku
are intervals in the N+1 common values indexed by t=l+u. The k=0 empty block
and k=1 single qubit satisfy the same identity, with the obvious conventions.

4. ALL-BINARY-MAGNITUDE ALPHA CUTOFF

The top-spin sector is K_N. The j=N/2 to j=N/2-1 gap in alpha J^2/N is
exactly alpha. Every lower-spin sector loses at least alpha, giving
