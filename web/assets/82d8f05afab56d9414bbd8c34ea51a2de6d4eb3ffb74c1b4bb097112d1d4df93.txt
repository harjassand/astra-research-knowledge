CYCLE 3 / LEAF 19 — INDEPENDENT AUDIT: MULTICATALYST FOSTER CLOCK AND PERIODIC WORDS
Date: 2026-10-08 (Australia/Brisbane)
Status: no falsifier found for the conditional Foster proof in foster_multicatalyst.txt. The stopped-Dynkin and physical-time non-explosion steps are valid after the explicit localization below. A fixed periodic reaction-label word with positive coordinatewise growth cannot be followed forever with positive probability in a finite weakly reversible mass-action network. This is a route-specific obstruction, not a proof of general weakly reversible non-explosion. No shared output was edited.

1. AUDIT VERDICT AND INTERFACE

I audited outputs/research/reaction_dynamics/cycle3/leaves/foster_multicatalyst.txt and its scratch derivation. The source is the finite fixed-rate mass-action family stated there, with finite r,s>=1, positive constants, integer k_ji>=1, and strict margins
  gamma_j=lambda_j delta_j-sum_i w_i p_ji>0,
  eta_ji=w_i q_ji-lambda_j k_ji u_ji>0.
The candidate V=sum_j lambda_j a_j+sum_i w_i b_i+K 1_{A=0} is positive and proper. I found no incorrect reaction increment, indicator switch, finite-set bound, or clock estimate under these assumptions.

The leading-order interpretation is important: the inequalities are a sufficient conditional certificate for this named family. Their failure does not furnish an explosive path or refute recurrence.

2. INDEPENDENT GENERATOR CHECK

Write W=sum_j lambda_j a_j+sum_i w_i b_i, I0=1_{A=0}, and Ij=1_{a=e_j}. Reaction-by-reaction increments are:

  0->A_j:                  rate alpha_j;                 Delta W=lambda_j;
  A_j->0:                  rate delta_j a_j;              Delta W=-lambda_j;
  A_j->A_j+B_i:            rate p_ji a_j;                 Delta W=w_i;
  A_j+B_i->A_j:            rate q_ji a_j b_i;             Delta W=-w_i;
  A_j+B_i->(k_ji+1)A_j+B_i rate u_ji a_j b_i;             Delta W=k_ji lambda_j;
  (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i->A_j+B_i rate v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i;   Delta W=-k_ji lambda_j.

Only immigration from A=0 and death of the sole remaining catalyst change I0. The latter occurs exactly at catalyst vector e_j, regardless of b. Reverse autocatalysis leaves one A_j, so cannot switch I0 on. Therefore
  L I0=-alpha_sum I0+sum_j delta_j Ij
exactly, and
  L V = H -sum_j gamma_j a_j -sum_(j,i) eta_ji a_j b_i
        -sum_(j,i)lambda_j k_ji v_ji(a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i
        -K alpha_sum I0 +K sum_j delta_j Ij.                (A)
Every unavailable-reactant term is zero under the falling-factorial convention; no boundary continuation is being used.

The finite-set estimates also check:
- A=0: LV=H-K alpha_sum< -rho by the K condition.
- A=1, a=e_j: LV<=H-gamma+K delta_max-eta B.
- A>=2: the indicator term vanishes and sum_(j,i)eta_ji a_jb_i>=eta A B>=2 eta B, hence LV<=H-gamma A-2 eta B.
Thus the displayed F_rho is finite and LV<=-rho off it. V is proper since V>=min(lambda_j,w_i)(A+B) away from the origin and V(0,b)=K+sum_i w_i b_i.

The stated jump bound J=max{K+lambda_max,max_i w_i,k_max lambda_max} covers all six reaction types, including the two indicator-switching jumps. Equation (A) gives the global upper bound LV<=C_+=H+K delta_max. This upper bound is independent of the state; the negative terms need not be bounded below.

3. STOPPED-DYNKIN / PHYSICAL-TIME NON-EXPLOSION

The non-explosion inference is valid, but the clean rigorous version is to show the localization explicitly. Fix R>V(x) and define tau_R=inf{t:V(X_t)>=R}. The set {V<R} is finite by properness. Since all jumps of V are bounded by J, the pre-exit and overshoot states lie in the finite set {V<=R+J}; rates on that set are bounded. The stopped minimal chain is therefore an ordinary finite-rate chain up to tau_R, and Dynkin's formula is justified there:

  E_x[V(X_(t wedge tau_R))]
    = V(x)+E_x integral_0^(t wedge tau_R) LV(X_s) ds
    <= V(x)+C_+ t.

On {tau_R<=t}, V(X_(t wedge tau_R))>=R, so
  P_x(tau_R<=t) <= (V(x)+C_+t)/R.                         (B)

An explosion cannot occur while V stays bounded: bounded V confines the chain to a finite state set with uniformly bounded total exit rate, and such a chain cannot make infinitely many jumps in finite time. Therefore explosion by time t implies hitting every sufficiently large V-level by t. Letting R->infinity in (B) gives P_x(explosion by t)=0. This is a physical-time clock proof: the rates, and hence arbitrarily short holding times, are included through the generator bound.

There is no hidden issue from overshooting R: the bounded jump J puts the overshoot in a finite set, and the stopped Dynkin integral only uses the generator before tau_R. Equivalently, one can first stop at the finite set boundary and then pass to the minimal process. One must not infer this result from the embedded-chain increment LV/q(x); that increment divides by the unbounded exit rate q(x).

4. STOPPED HITTING-TIME DRIFT / RECURRENCE

For x outside F_rho let tau_F be the first entrance to F_rho. To avoid an unstated integrability step, stop at sigma_(R,t)=t wedge tau_F wedge tau_R. Before tau_F the drift is at most -rho, and Dynkin on the finite localized region gives
  E_x[V(X_sigma_(R,t))]
       <= V(x)-rho E_x[sigma_(R,t)].
Since V>=0, E_x[sigma_(R,t)]<=V(x)/rho. Non-explosion implies tau_R tends to infinity on each finite time interval as R tends to infinity. Monotone convergence first in R and then in t yields
  E_x[t wedge tau_F]<=V(x)/rho,   E_x[tau_F]<=V(x)/rho.   (C)
This is an elapsed-time bound, not a reaction-count bound.

The chain is irreducible on the full lattice: from any state, if A=0 immigrate a catalyst; with any active A_j, remove each B_i by A_j+B_i->A_j, then drain A_j->0. Conversely, from zero immigrate the target catalyst counts and make each target B_i using A_j->A_j+B_i; if the target has no catalysts, use and then remove a temporary catalyst. Every requested finite path has positive probability. Thus the state space is one closed irreducible component. The standard CTMC Foster criterion applies to V/rho and the finite set F_rho, and yields positive recurrence. Anderson–Kim Theorem 3.1 is an appropriate primary reference for the finite-set negative-generator-drift criterion; the present argument supplies its objects and independently establishes conservativity first. https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11263v3

5. PERIODIC REACTION-WORD TEST AGAINST WEAK REVERSIBILITY

Here “word” means a fixed finite list of reaction channels r_1,...,r_L repeated forever, not merely a state-space jump-vector pattern. Write r_j:y_j->y'_j and Delta=sum_j(y'_j-y_j). Along the prescribed word the cycle-start states are x_n=x_0+n Delta. Infinite feasibility forces Delta_i>=0 for every species: if some Delta_i<0, the count at cycle boundaries eventually becomes negative.

If Delta=0, every repeated cycle visits the same finite list of states. The total rates on that list have a finite maximum Q, so each conditional holding time is Exp(q_j) with q_j<=Q and can be coupled below by an independent Exp(Q) sequence; the sum diverges almost surely. Thus this case cannot explode.

Now assume Delta>=0 and Delta!=0, and put S={i:Delta_i>0}. After channel r_j fires, the state has the form z+y'_j with z>=0. Weak reversibility gives a directed path from y'_j back to y_j; its first reaction q_j has source y'_j and is enabled at this post-jump state. If the prescribed word is to continue, the next channel r_(j+1) (cyclically, with r_(L+1)=r_1) is also enabled there. At phase j of cycle n, mass-action rates for these two channels scale as n^{d'_j} and n^{d_(j+1)}, where
  d'_j=sum_(i in S)y'_(j,i),
  d_(j+1)=sum_(i in S)y_(j+1,i).
Coordinates outside S are fixed at each phase, and their factorial factors are positive constants whenever the word is feasible. Cyclic indexing gives the exact degree sum
  sum_j(d'_j-d_(j+1))
     =sum_j sum_(i in S)(y'_(j,i)-y_(j,i))
     =sum_(i in S)Delta_i=|Delta|_1>0.
Hence some phase j has m=d'_j-d_(j+1)>=1. At this phase q_j cannot be the prescribed next channel, since their source degrees differ. Its rate divided by the prescribed next-channel rate is Theta(n^m), so the conditional probability of the prescribed channel at that phase is O(n^-m). This phase recurs once per word, and the product of these bounds is zero. Therefore no fixed channel-labelled periodic word with positive coordinatewise increment can itself furnish a positive-probability explosion path in a weakly reversible finite mass-action network.

This covers the standard proof pattern of selecting a finite reaction sequence that is repeated with net molecule growth. For the published explosive chain 0->2A->4A+B->6A+4B->3A, the increment is +1 A per word, and adding reverse edges makes the boundary return source 3A compete at cubic order with the next 2A birth at quadratic order. The exact one-cycle bound from the 3-species closure test is
  P(2A->4A+B next at (n+1,0,0))
      <= kappa_1/[kappa_1+kappa_4^-(n-1)],
whose product is zero. The source example is Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen, Example 3.2; it is not weakly reversible. https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05328v2

Scope caveat: the argument is for a prescribed reaction-channel word. If distinct channels have the same net jump and an alternative channel can be counted as “success” for the state path, channel rates must be aggregated and the state-jump event audited separately. The degree argument above is not asserted as a theorem for every such enlarged state-path event, nor for nonperiodic or lengthening words. No general weakly reversible non-explosion theorem is claimed.

6. STRICT SMALL-GAIN IS NOT NECESSARY; NO EXPLOSION FALSIFIER FOUND

An exact outside-LP example confirms that failure of the margins is not evidence of explosion. Take r=s=2, all four pairs (j,i), k_ji=1, alpha_j=delta_j=1, p_ji=q_ji=1, and u_ji=v_ji=2. For any j, gamma_j>0 would require lambda_j>w_1+w_2; eta_j1,eta_j2>0 would require w_1>2lambda_j and w_2>2lambda_j. The latter imply w_1+w_2>4lambda_j, a contradiction. Thus the strict small-gain LP is infeasible.

Nevertheless the network is detailed balanced at c=(1,1,1,1): each reversible pair has equal forward and reverse rate constants and both monomials at c equal one. The product-Poisson law with four unit means is stationary. Its mean total exit rate is exactly 28: immigration 2, expected catalyst deaths 2, catalyst-to-substrate births 4, substrate removals 4, forward autocatalysis 8, reverse autocatalysis 8. Anderson–Cappelletti–Koyama–Kurtz Theorem 3 states that complex-balanced stochastic mass-action systems are non-explosive for every initial distribution. https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.09356v2

Thus the strict margins certify one Foster template, not a phase boundary. Standard finite periodic-word growth cannot bypass weak-reversibility return competition, even at rates outside this template. This does not determine behavior outside the LP for arbitrary nonperiodic routes or the wider weakly reversible class.

7. UNRESOLVED BOUNDARY

Checked/verified: the exact generator (including the only positive indicator switch), the finite exceptional-set drift, the bounded-jump stopped-Dynkin estimate, the physical-time non-explosion implication, the finite-set hitting-time bound after localization, and application of the irreducible CTMC Foster criterion.

Scoped negative result: no exact positive-probability explosion can be certified by repeating a fixed channel-labelled periodic word with positive coordinatewise net growth in a finite weakly reversible mass-action network. The standard non-WR explosive word loses its positive-probability path under direct weak-reversibility closure.
