CYCLE 2 PRIMARY-LITERATURE GATE — WEAK REVERSIBILITY / RECURRENCE
Access date: 2026-10-08
Status: EXTERNAL-ESTABLISHED for the candidate family by an existing generic Foster/tier theorem; no novelty claim.

QUESTION AND CANDIDATE INTERFACE

For fixed integer m >= 3, consider the two-species mass-action network

  0 -> A -> B -> mB -> A -> 0,

with rates alpha,beta,gamma,delta,epsilon > 0 in the displayed order. The stochastic propensity for y -> y' is kappa x^(falling factorial y). Its complexes are {0,A,B,mB}; it has one strongly connected linkage class. The stoichiometric vectors include (1,0) and (-1,1), so the stoichiometric rank is 2 and S=R^2. The network has n=4 complexes, l=1 linkage class, s=2, hence deficiency 1. It is weakly reversible, and all communicating classes are closed; in fact its stochastic chain is irreducible on N_0^2 (path proof below).

VERDICT

The family is outside the previously cited *restricted* recurrence results for rank-one, binary/second-order, and all-rate complex-balanced systems when m>=3 and rates are arbitrary. It is nevertheless already covered for all positive rate choices by Anderson–Kim (2018), Corollary 4.3, a general tier/Foster criterion. A short direct linear Foster function also proves nonexplosion and positive recurrence. Further, a 2026 two-species theorem is claimed in an official conference abstract/project report and would subsume it, but the full paper is listed as in preparation; classify that wider claim only SOURCE-REPORTED. Thus this candidate is a useful theorem-gate test, not an uncovered recurrence family.

1. EXACT GATE AGAINST THE NAMED THEOREMS

Rank-one theorem. Wiuf–Xu, arXiv:2012.07954v3 (last revised 24 Jan 2023), Corollary 4.10: if R is a weakly reversible mass-action SRN satisfying H2–H3, it is positive recurrent on every positive irreducible class (PIC), with an exponentially ergodic stationary distribution; H2 is dim(S)=1. Stochastic mass action fulfills H1, and for a nontrivial weakly reversible rank-one network H3 is the two-direction condition along the primitive stoichiometric line. The candidate has rank 2, so it is outside this theorem. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07954v3 ; theorem text: https://arxiv.org/html/2012.07954v3 (Corollary 4.10, H2 at Section 2, lines 129–143 in the HTML source).

Binary single-linkage theorem. Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim, arXiv:1904.08967v2 (last revised 16 Jan 2020), Theorem 4.1: for a weakly reversible binary mass-action system with one linkage class, if for each species S, {S,2S} intersects the complex set, then the associated CTMC is positive recurrent. “Binary” means every complex has total molecularity at most 2. Candidate has one linkage class and A,B themselves are complexes, but mB is a complex with molecularity m>=3, so it fails the binary assumption. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08967v2 ; exact theorem text: https://arxiv.org/html/1904.08967v2.

Anderson–Kim binary classes. Anderson–Kim, arXiv:1710.11263v3 (last revised 22 Aug 2018; published SIAM J. Appl. Math. 78 (2018), 2692–2713), Theorem 2.1 concerns a binary weakly reversible single-linkage core augmented by every species inflow and outflow. The candidate lacks B inflow/outflow and its mB source is nonbinary. Theorem 2.2 requires a binary double-full network and, from every double complex, a path to a unary or zero complex; the candidate is not binary and has no 2B complex for m>=3. These two structural results therefore do not cover it. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11263v3 ; theorem statements: https://arxiv.org/html/1710.11263v3.

Second-order/bimolecular nonexplosion. Xu, arXiv:2409.05340v2 (submitted 9 Sep 2024, last revised 9 May 2026), Theorem 4.6 states every second-order 1-endotactic mass-action system is regular; its listed consequences include every second-order weakly reversible mass-action system. Here regular means stochastic nonexplosion and deterministic global existence. Theorem 4.13 says every bimolecular weakly reversible stochastic mass-action system is positive recurrent iff a stationary probability exists in every communicating class. Candidate is weakly reversible and 1-endotactic, but its source mB has order m>=3, so it lies outside the second-order/bimolecular assumptions. Primary source/version: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05340v2 ; exact theorem statements: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2 (Theorems 4.6 and 4.13). The arXiv title is rendered “On the Regulary of Reaction Systems.”

Complex-balanced product form / deficiency zero. Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz, arXiv:0803.3042v4 (last revised 31 Jan 2010), prove that a positive complex-balanced equilibrium gives product-form stationary distributions on closed irreducible classes; deficiency zero plus weak reversibility supplies such balance for every positive rate vector. Candidate deficiency is 1, so the deficiency-zero result does not apply. For this candidate, complex balance holds only on a proper rate locus. The complex-balance equations at 0, B, and mB imply

  c_A = alpha/epsilon,
  c_B = beta*alpha/(gamma*epsilon),
  c_B^(m-1) = gamma/delta,

and the equation at A then follows from these. Thus the exact necessary and sufficient condition is

  delta * alpha^(m-1) * beta^(m-1) = gamma^m * epsilon^(m-1).

For example alpha=beta=gamma=delta=1, epsilon=2 is not complex-balanced. All rates equal to 1 are complex-balanced, so the family is partly covered by the complex-balanced theorem, but not for arbitrary rates. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3042v4 .

2. THE BROADER PUBLISHED FOSTER/TIER THEOREM THAT ALREADY COVERS IT

The decisive prior-art gate is Anderson–Kim (2018), not the restricted binary corollaries. Their general Theorem 3.1 states that for a CTMC on a countable state space, if a finite K and a positive V satisfy AV(x)<=-1 outside K, then each state in every closed irreducible component is positive recurrent, with finite expected time to enter the union of such components. Their Theorem 4.2 gives a network-level sufficient condition in terms of D-type and S-type tiers; Corollary 4.3 makes it: for every tier-sequence, the descending-source set D must be nonempty and the highest D-tier must equal the highest S-tier. It then gives classwise positive recurrence for every choice of positive rate constants. This is not restricted to binary networks. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11263v3 ; exact statements: https://arxiv.org/html/1710.11263v3 (Theorem 3.1, Theorem 4.2, Corollary 4.3).

Candidate-specific check of Corollary 4.3. A tier-sequence is x_n=(a_n,b_n) with |x_n|->infinity and each coordinate convergent in [0,infinity]. Its complexes are 0,A,B,mB. If b_n has a finite limit, divergence forces a_n->infinity; A is the unique highest D/S source tier and A->0 is descending. If b_n->infinity, then b_n^m/b_n->infinity, so B cannot be a top tier. The only possible highest D-tier sources are mB, A, or both tied. If only mB is top, mB->A is descending. If A is top (alone or tied with mB), A->0 is descending. Every top source is available along the sequence: a_n->infinity makes A available and b_n->infinity makes mB available. For available top complexes, falling factorial propensities satisfy (b_n)_m~b_n^m and (a_n)_1=a_n, so top D and top S tiers agree. Therefore D is nonempty and highest D = highest S for every tier-sequence. Corollary 4.3 applies and proves positive recurrence for every alpha,beta,gamma,delta,epsilon>0.

Independent direct Foster proof. The reaction graph is a directed cycle and the CTMC is irreducible on N_0^2: from (a,b), if b>0, apply B->mB, then mB->A, then A->0; the first two steps raise B by m-1 and then consume m, so the net effect is (a,b)->(a,b-1). Repeat to clear B, then remove A. Conversely, from 0, repeat 0->A->B to create any desired B count, then make the desired A count with 0->A. Every finite path has positive probability.

Set V(a,b)=1+a+b. The generator drift is exactly

  LV(a,b) = alpha + (m-1)*gamma*b - (m-1)*delta*(b)_m - epsilon*a.

For fixed positive rates and m>=3, the b-dependent polynomial tends to -infinity as b->infinity. Pick B0 such that alpha+(m-1)gamma*b-(m-1)delta(b)_m<=-1 for every b>=B0. On the finite range b<B0, choose A0 large enough that subtracting epsilon*a makes LV<=-1 whenever a>=A0. Thus LV<=-1 outside finite K={a<A0,b<B0}. In addition,

  LV(a,b) <= alpha+(m-1)*gamma*b <= C V(a,b)

for a finite network/rate-dependent C. This upper drift bound with norm-like V is a standard CTMC regularity criterion, so the CTMC is nonexplosive; the negative Foster drift gives positive recurrence. This direct proof is internally reconstructed, while the implication follows the cited standard Foster criteria. Thresholds B0,A0 depend on rates, especially delta and epsilon; no uniformity over rate vectors is claimed.

3. OTHER BROADER NETWORK THEOREMS CHECKED

Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen, “Tier structure of strongly endotactic reaction networks,” arXiv:1808.05328v2 (last revised 3 Oct 2019; journal Stochastic Processes and their Applications 130 (2020), 7218–7259), Theorem 7.2 covers a strongly endotactic core plus specified higher-order pure-species outflows and lower-order added reactions. In its notation, for every species S_i a nonempty R_i must be chosen from reactions aS_i -> y' with a>=M-1 and |y'|<=a-1, where M is the maximum source molecularity of the base network. The candidate has M=m; mB->A qualifies for B, but no pure-A source with a>=m-1 exists, so the per-species assumption fails. The binary fully-open Corollary 7.1 also fails. This theorem does not subsume the candidate as stated. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05328v2 ; Theorems 7.2/7.3 and Corollary 7.1: https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05328v2.

Two-species theorem (wider but not fully audited). The ECMTB 2026 contribution abstract states: for weakly reversible stochastic mass-action systems in two species, the CTMC is positive recurrent on each closed irreducible class. The official ConStRAINeD results page says every weakly reversible two-species SRN is positive recurrent regardless of rates, and lists the five-author proof by Agazzi, Anderson, Cappelletti, Laurence, and Mattingly as “In preparation.” The abstract is dated 14 Jul 2026; no proof manuscript or exact formal theorem was publicly linked on the checked page as of 8 Oct 2026. This claim covers the candidate in scope but remains SOURCE-REPORTED; exact edge cases and hypotheses cannot be checked from the available source. Sources: https://contributions.ecmtb2026.org/event/1/sessions/64/ ; https://constrained.polito.it/publications/ .

4. NEXT CONTROLLED CLASS FOR THE CANDIDATE'S DRIFT MECHANISM

The smallest dimensional lift is the three-species one-linkage cycle

  0 -> A -> B -> mB -> C -> A -> 0,   m>=3,

with arbitrary positive rates alpha,beta,gamma,delta,eta,epsilon. It has stoichiometric rank 3, maximum source order m, and generically is not complex-balanced. Let V=A+B+2C. Its generator drift is

  LV = alpha + (m-1)*gamma*B - (m-2)*delta*(B)_m - eta*C - epsilon*A.

For m>=3 this is at most -1 outside a finite set: high B is controlled by the negative degree-m term; bounded B with large A or C is controlled by the negative linear terms. Also LV<=alpha+(m-1)gamma*B<=C(1+V), so nonexplosion follows. The network is irreducible: B can be reduced one at a time by B->mB->C->A->0; C drains via C->A->0; A drains; from zero, repeated 0->A->B builds B, groups of m B produce C, and 0->A builds A. Thus the same Foster mechanism proves classwise positive recurrence in full stoichiometric rank three.

This is a useful validation lift, not a new literature gap: the same highest-tier check verifies Anderson–Kim Corollary 4.3 for this simple cycle. If b remains bounded, divergence is in a or c and the top source has A->0 or C->A descending (if both tie at top, A->0 descends). If b grows, mB dominates B; a top mB descends to C, while a top A or C has the preceding drain. In every case the available top source's falling-factorial propensity matches its dominant monomial. The reported 2D theorem is not needed. The 3D cycle is complex-balanced only when the same condition delta*alpha^(m-1)*beta^(m-1)=gamma^m*epsilon^(m-1) holds; when it does, c_C=delta*c_B^m/eta. A genuine next research gate beyond this candidate mechanism would have to involve competing boundary/high-order growth modes for which no single positive linear weight makes the generator strictly negative and the top D/S tier check fails; one would then need a patched or nonlinear Lyapunov construction. Do not market the 3D lift as novel before comparing against Corollary 4.3.

5. INPUTS, COSTS, STATUS, AND FALSIFIERS

Inputs: fixed finite network, integer m>=3, fixed positive finite rate constants. No rate estimate, oracle, numerical simulation, or acquired equilibrium is required for the direct Foster proof. Exact drift and communicating-class reachability are elementary. The finite Foster set depends on rates; as delta or epsilon approach zero, thresholds can diverge. No uniform mixing, return-time, spectral-gap, or stationary-tail bounds are proved. The complex-balance classification is exact and has no computational claim. No code/test run was needed.

Strongest baseline: (i) Anderson–Kim general tier/Foster Corollary 4.3 covers candidate for arbitrary positive rates (published baseline); (ii) Xu Theorem 4.13 reduces the missing invariant-law step for bimolecular WR systems, but not this m>=3 family; (iii) the asserted two-species theorem covers candidate broadly but is not available as a full proof. Candidate mechanism is reproduced by a direct rate-dependent linear Lyapunov function; its rate-independent theorem coverage is stronger evidence that no novelty is present.

Falsifiers to this report: an error in the state-dependent drift or tier-sequence case split; a version of Anderson–Kim Corollary 4.3 with assumptions not met by the candidate; or a positive-rate choice violating the displayed Foster inequalities. Algebraically, each is checkable. Conversely, a counterexample to candidate recurrence would contradict the stated Foster condition and standard CTMC criterion, so recheck the interface before proposing one.

Scientific label: EXTERNAL-ESTABLISHED for classwise positive recurrence under the published Anderson–Kim criterion, with an independent internally reconstructed drift proof. The 2D general theorem is SOURCE-REPORTED only. No novelty, field-breakthrough, or external-validation claim is made.
