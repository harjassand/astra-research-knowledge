# Cycle 3: bidual compactness audit

## Verdict

The Komlos route's separability restriction can be removed, conditional on its uniform finite-range global-extension premise. Index extensions of finite boundary restrictions by inclusion, use an anchor point for pointwise boundedness, take a cluster subnet in the product of weak-star compact balls of \(L_1^{**}\), and project through the norm-one L-projection back to \(L_1\). This gives the same extension constant and imposes no separability or common-support condition.

Rao's 2003 primary article states that \(L_1(\mu)\) is L-embedded for any positive measure. Mendel-Naor's 2013 extension theorem already identifies a retraction from \(Y^{**}\) to \(Y\) as the standard way to globalize finite extensions. Thus the compactness mechanism is not novel; it is a valid missing bridge from the finite-cut L1 premise.

Naor's [arXiv v2, revised 10 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07564v2) explicitly gives arbitrary-subset \(\ell_2\)-to-\(L_1[0,1]\) extension and says separability can be dropped. Its “Added in proof” reports forthcoming Mendel-Naor work proving \(L_1\) metric Markov cotype 2 and \(e(L_q;L_1)\lesssim\sqrt q\) for \(q\ge2\). Standard-Lebesgue conclusions are preempted at statement level; the forthcoming proof is not accessible or independently validated here. The arbitrary-positive-measure target scope is broader than the explicit \(L_1[0,1]\) convention, but priority remains unknown.
