1. EXACT EXPERIMENT AND THE CLAIM

Fix public J,tau>0, |lambda|<=B and 0<g_-<g_+<infinity. The continuum
experiment is the normalized grand-canonical Lieb-Liniger state

 rho(lambda,g)=Z(lambda,g)^-1 direct-sum_{m>=0}
                       exp[-tau(A_{m,g}-lambda m)],

with the forms and public finite-chain embeddings of continuum_limit.txt.
Select ANY two distinct public g1,g2 in [g_-,g_+], and fix lambda=0.
This document constructs a fully specified strictly positive number e_*
such that every entanglement-breaking (EB, measure-and-prepare) channel T
on the full infinite-dimensional Fock space satisfies

 max_{i=1,2} ||T rho(0,g_i)-rho(0,g_i)||_1 >= e_*>0.       (1.1)

The same lower bound holds as a liminf for arbitrary finite-chain EB
archives as L tends to infinity, including archives whose classical
alphabet and decoder depend on L. The already constructed quantum archive
achieves every requested error with O((1+log(1/epsilon))^(3/2)) persistent
qubits independently of L, without reading g or lambda.

This is not an optimal quantum-memory rate or a practical-sized classical
advantage. The explicit lower constant is deliberately extremely poor.
Its point is that no uncomputed positivity, unknown spectrum, approximate
no-broadcasting compactness theorem, or finite-dimensional output assumption
is needed. The quantitative witness is entirely in the m=2 sector.

2. DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT FIDELITY/AFFINITY CERTIFICATE

For density operators rho,sigma on any separable Hilbert space put

 f=||sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)||_1,
 a=Tr sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma), d=f-a>=0.

For every measure-and-prepare channel T,

                          a(T rho,T sigma)>=f(rho,sigma). (2.1)

Indeed its measurement laws p_x,q_x satisfy classical root fidelity
sum_x sqrt(p_x q_x)>=f(rho,sigma). Insert the POVM resolution of the
identity between the square roots in the polar variational formula and
apply Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz term by term. Preparation with states
eta_x has affinity >=sum_x sqrt(p_xq_x), by joint concavity and homogeneity
of Tr X^(1/2)Y^(1/2). This is Lieb's established trace-function theorem,
not a new channel result. Continuous alphabets follow by approximation in
trace class. It also suffices to restrict to arbitrary finite classical
archives; their alphabet size is unrestricted.

Here the needed p=1/2 concavity has a short self-contained verification.
For positive matrices X,Y the parallel sum (X^-1+Y^-1)^-1 is jointly
operator concave: its quadratic form at z is
inf_{u+v=z}(<u,Xu>+<v,Yv>), an infimum of affine functions of X,Y.
On the finite Hilbert-Schmidt space use commuting positive left and right
multiplication operators L_A,R_B. Scalar spectral integration gives

 L_A^(1/2)R_B^(1/2)
    =(1/pi)integral_0^infinity t^(-1/2)
                  (L_A^-1+t R_B^-1)^-1 dt.

The parallel-sum formula makes this jointly operator concave in A,B.
Its quadratic form at the identity matrix is Tr sqrt(A)sqrt(B).
Approximation handles singular matrices, and finite-rank compressions
plus (2.2) handle positive trace-class operators. Thus the exact affinity
concavity needed in (2.1) is proved here while retaining proper attribution
to Lieb's much broader established theorem.

The Powers-Stormer estimate

 ||sqrt(rho)-sqrt(rho')||_2^2<=||rho-rho'||_1             (2.2)

and Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz imply
|a(T rho,T sigma)-a(rho,sigma)|<=2sqrt(delta), where
delta=max(||T rho-rho||_1,||T sigma-sigma||_1). Thus

                    delta>=d^2/4.                     (2.3)

For completeness, (2.2) follows with X=sqrt(rho), Y=sqrt(rho'),
C=X-Y and S=sign(C):
||X^2-Y^2||_1>=Tr S(XC+CY)=Tr |C|(X+Y)>=Tr C^2.
The last difference is 2Tr(C_+Y+C_-X)>=0. Finite-rank approximation
justifies the identities in trace class.

A commutator gives another explicit certificate. With X=sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)
and polar X=U|X|,

 ||X-X^*||_1<=2||(U-I)|X|||_1<=2sqrt(2 f d)<=2sqrt(2d).

The middle bound is Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz and
Tr |X|(U-I)^*(U-I)=2(f-Re Tr X)=2d; a partial polar isometry can be
extended on a larger space, or completed by its defect without changing
this estimate. Expanding [rho,sigma] as four products containing
[sqrt(rho),sqrt(sigma)] and using square-root operator norms <=1 gives

 d>=||[rho,sigma]||_1^2/128,
 delta>=||[rho,sigma]||_1^4/65536.                     (2.4)

This applies directly in infinite dimension. Half-trace errors have
floor d^2/8, or commutator floor ||[rho,sigma]||_1^4/131072.

3. AN EXACT POSITIVE-COUPLING RESOLVENT IDENTITY

In m=2 let T0=-2J(partial_x^2+partial_y^2) on the symmetric Dirichlet
square, with form domain H^1_0 intersect L^2_sym. Let

 b(v,v)=2 integral_0^1 |v(t,t)|^2 dt,
 A_g=T0+g b                                      (form notation).

For s>0 set R0=(T0+s)^-1 and define the bounded positive operator C_s
by the normalized contact form

 <u,C_s v>=b(R0^(1/2)u,R0^(1/2)v).

continuum_limit.txt (3.3) gives
b(v,v)<=8||v||_2||grad v||_2
        <=[4/sqrt(2Js)](<v,T0v>+s||v||_2^2).
Consequently

                0<=C_s<=c_s I, c_s=4/sqrt(2Js).        (3.1)

The form resolvent equation, or its variational minimization, gives

 R_g=(A_g+s)^-1=R0^(1/2)(I+g C_s)^-1 R0^(1/2).          (3.2)

This is an exact identity for all g>=0. No operator product with an
unbounded bare delta potential is asserted. If a,b>=0 and
Q=(I+aC_s)^-1(I+bC_s)^-1, elementary bounded-operator multiplication gives

 [R_a,R_b]=(a-b)R0^(1/2) Q[R0,C_s]Q R0^(1/2).           (3.3)

To check the sign, multiply the middle difference on both sides by Q^-1.
It becomes (I+bC_s)R0(I+aC_s)-(I+aC_s)R0(I+bC_s)
=(a-b)[R0,C_s]. The inverse factors commute because they are functions
of the same C_s. Positivity also gives

 ||Q||<=1, ||Q-I||<=(a+b)c_s.

Therefore the difference from the undressed first-order expression obeys

 ||[R_a,R_b]/(a-b)-R0^(1/2)[R0,C_s]R0^(1/2)||
                           <=4(a+b)c_s^2/s^2.          (3.4)

This remainder does not require either coupling to be small: the spectral
scale s below is chosen from the public g_+.

Let phi_k(x)=sqrt(2)sin(pi kx) and u_k=phi_k tensor phi_k. These are

For k!=l, exact trigonometric integration gives

                        b(u_k,u_l)=2.                 (4.1)

 M=max(1,ceil(54400 g_+/J),ceil(sqrt(3/(4J pi^2 tau)))),

The vectors u,v have norm one and depend on no unknown state/eigenbasis.
With r_k=(s+e_k)^-1, the undressed operator in (3.4) has matrix element

 <u,R0^(1/2)[R0,C_s]R0^(1/2)v>
       =(2/M)sum_{k=M+1}^{2M}sum_{l=3M+1}^{4M}

For these labels,
r_k-r_l>=1/(40J pi^2 M^2),
r_k>=1/(20J pi^2 M^2), r_l>=1/(68J pi^2 M^2).

                    w_M=1/(27200 J^3 pi^6 M^5).        (4.3)

This already proves noncommutativity in the actual infinite-dimensional

5. EXPLICIT SHIFTED-LOG TRANSFER TO THERMAL OPERATORS

Put T_i=exp(-tau A_gi), positive trace class with ||T_i||<=1. Functional
calculus gives R_gi=psi(T_i), where

 psi(t)=1/[s-tau^-1 log t] for 0<t<exp(tau s), psi(0)=0.

Define fully explicit positive quantities

 gamma=min(1/(4s),eta_R s/32),
 delta=exp[-tau(gamma^-1-s)],
 s0=s-tau^-1 log(1+delta).                             (5.1)

The public choice (4.2) has tau s>=3. Consequently delta<=exp(-9)<1,
s0>=s/2, and tau s-log(t)>=2 for 0<t<=1+delta. Direct differentiation
shows

 psi''(t)=tau[2-(tau s-log t)]/[t^2(tau s-log t)^3]<=0.

Thus psi is increasing and concave on [0,1+delta], including its continuous
value at zero. Concavity bounds its increments by the increment from zero:
0<=psi(t+delta)-psi(t)<=psi(delta)=gamma for 0<=t<=1.
Set X_i=T_i+delta I and Rtilde_i=psi(X_i). Spectral calculus then gives

 ||Rtilde_i-R_gi||<=gamma, ||Rtilde_i||<=1/s0<=2/s.

Since ||R_gi||<=1/s, the difference of resolvent commutators is at most
2gamma/s+2gamma/s0<=6gamma/s<=3eta_R/16. In particular,

                   ||[Rtilde_1,Rtilde_2]||>=eta_R/2.    (5.2)

There is an elementary trace-norm bound for the shifted logarithms. For
bounded X>=delta I, the norm-convergent log integral and commutator identity
give, whenever [X,Y] is trace class,

 [log X,Y]=integral_0^infinity
                  (X+t)^-1[X,Y](X+t)^-1 dt,
 ||[log X,Y]||_1<=delta^-1||[X,Y]||_1.                 (5.3)

Indeed log X=integral_0^infinity[(1+t)^-1 I-(X+t)^-1]dt;
the scalar divergence cancels before integration. The integrand in the
commutator is bounded by ||[X,Y]||_1/(delta+t)^2, which is integrable.
Apply (5.3) twice to X_1,X_2. Their commutator is [T_1,T_2], trace class,
so

 ||[log X_1,log X_2]||_1<=delta^-2||[T_1,T_2]||_1.

Put P_i=s I-tau^-1 log X_i>=s0 I. The bounded inverse identity
[P_1^-1,P_2^-1]=P_1^-1 P_2^-1[P_1,P_2]P_2^-1 P_1^-1 implies

 ||[Rtilde_1,Rtilde_2]||_1
       <=tau^-2 s0^-4 delta^-2||[T_1,T_2]||_1.

Combining with (5.2), operator norm <=trace norm and s0>=s/2 yields

 ||[T_1,T_2]||_1>=eta_T,
 eta_T=eta_R tau^2 s^4 delta^2/32>0.                   (5.4)

Every quantity is specified by public J,tau,g_+ and the selected pair.
Shifted logs are an analytic comparison, not an acquired spectrum or an
operation that the archive must implement. The resulting constant is
exponentially tiny in the conservative public band scale and inverse
coupling separation; it is not a practical discrimination margin.

6. THE FULL FOCK DENSITY GAP AND EB FLOOR

Use proof.txt's all-m constants at the original public B,g_-:

 ||[rho(0,g1),rho(0,g2)]||_1>=eta,

Equations (2.3)-(2.4) provide both an intrinsic and a fully explicit floor:

 inf_EB T max_i||T rho(0,g_i)-rho(0,g_i)||_1
     >=[f(rho(0,g1),rho(0,g2))-a(rho(0,g1),rho(0,g2))]^2/4
     >=e_*:=eta^4/65536>0.                            (6.2)

There is no unacquired spectral or ground-state quantity in e_*. To use
finite expressions, one may replace C by Cbar=1+sqrt(pi/(4Jtau)); put
abar=1+log(Cbar)+tau(B+c), and replace S by the larger public bound

 Sbar=exp[abar^2/(4tau c)](2+sqrt(pi/(tau c))).

Complete the square in the m sum and bound a unimodal Gaussian sum by
its full-line integral plus at most two terms. This only weakens e_*.
Numerical evaluation should use logarithms; ordinary floating point will
usually underflow long before representing this constant.

7. FINITE-CHAIN TRANSPORT AND THE COMMUTING CONTROL

The exact public embeddings I_L of continuum_limit.txt give uniform full
trace-norm convergence of the normalized Gibbs states. Powers-Stormer
and Hilbert-Schmidt products make root fidelity and affinity continuous
in that topology. For the selected pair, d_L converges to d_infty>0.
Apply (2.3) directly to each finite-chain EB archive before taking L to
infinity. Equivalently extend it to continuum space by the public embedding
and its TP complement decoder. In either formulation,

 liminf_{L->infinity} inf_EB T_L
       max_i||T_L rho_L(0,g_i)-rho_L(0,g_i)||_1
                                      >=d_infty^2/4>=e_*. (7.1)

No useful explicit L threshold is claimed: the continuum proof establishes
convergence but does not provide a general rate. For sufficiently large L
one may also state the concrete floor e_*/4 using d_L>=d_infty/2.

For a fixed PUBLIC coupling g, varying lambda only multiplies each
number-sector heat block by exp(tau lambda m). Hence every pair in that
control family commutes. Measuring total number m and preparing the
parameter-independent conditional state exp(-tau A_L,m(g))/its trace is
an exact sufficient classical channel before number truncation. Tail
overflow gives error at most 2t_r, with r=O(sqrt(1+log(1/epsilon))) and
stored classical bits O(log(2+log(1/epsilon))). Preparing those conditional
interacting states is an explicitly separate cost, not a supplied free
Gibbs oracle. A decoder given the unknown g itself is also a different
interface and is not excluded by (7.1).

Thus the positive floor arises from UNKNOWN contact coupling, not from
chemical variation alone. A compact coupling interval with two distinct
values requires a quantum archive for vanishing full-state reconstruction
error, while the public finite quantum archive already supplies sufficiency.
The conclusion concerns one input copy and reconstruction of its full
state family, not multiple-copy tomography or a restricted observable list.

8. COSTS, FALSIFIERS, AND EVIDENCE STATUS

The band witness uses 4M public free sine modes. It is analytic, not a
claim that the encoded many-body memory must have that many modes. A
logarithmic representation of the scalar lower certificate avoids numerical
underflow; its required arithmetic precision depends on public inputs and
coupling separation. The shifted-log integral is proved and is not a
physical spectral oracle. A direct sharper numerical fidelity estimate
would require a certified continuum spectral/truncation approximation,
thermal tails and residual errors; no such oracle is supplied.
The finite quantum archive's waveform, Gram setup, gate, working-register
and critical-energy precision costs remain those of archive.txt and
energy_decoder.txt; the EB lower bound does not remove them.

Falsifiers are a wrong contact coefficient, failure of the bounded-form
resolvent identity, free-band signs/counts, the norm dressing estimate,
scalar concavity/shift bounds, logarithmic commutator integral, or the
EB affinity inequality. Finite diagnostics are separated in eb_checks.py.
The strict model-specific obstruction is an internally proved candidate;
fidelity monotonicity, Lieb concavity and compression of quantum statistical
families are established background. Priority and external validation are
UNKNOWN; internal cross-agent review is not specialist or formal validation.
