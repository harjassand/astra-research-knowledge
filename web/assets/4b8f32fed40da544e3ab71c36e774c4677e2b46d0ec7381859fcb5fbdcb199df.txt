Assume a supplied evaluator and range 0<=R<=rI, a frozen classifier J and
training root means 0<=Rhat_j<=rI. Let 0<gamma<=r, Q_j=Rhat_j+gamma I and
B_j=Q_j^2. The new pointwise assertion is

 D_matrix(R^2||Q_j^2)<=b(r/gamma)||R-Q_j||_F^2,
 b(t)=(2t^2 log t-t^2+1)/(t-1)^2, b(1)=2.

Independently checked derivation: diagonalize R and Q separately and let
w_ij be squared overlaps of their eigenvectors. Trace cyclicity gives
 D_matrix(R^2||Q^2)=sum_ij w_ij d_scalar(r_i^2||q_j^2),
 ||R-Q||_F^2=sum_ij w_ij(r_i-q_j)^2.
The scalar ratio is exactly b(r_i/q_j), including r_i=0 by continuity.
For t>0,t!=1,
 b'(t)=2(t^2-1-2t log t)/(t-1)^3>=0.
Indeed the numerator factor F(t)=t^2-1-2t log t has F(1)=0 and
F'(t)=2(t-1-log t)>=0, so its sign agrees with t-1. At t=1 the continuous
limit is b=2 and b'=2/3. Since r_i<=r and q_j>=gamma, termwise comparison
proves the pointwise inequality without any commutation or true-mean oracle.

The loss ell=||R-Q_J||_F^2 is bounded by W=m(r+gamma)^2, from
 -(r+gamma)I<=R-Q_J<=(r-gamma)I.
