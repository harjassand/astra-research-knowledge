INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE FIVE STEPS
Write g_k(x)=prod_a sin(pi k_a x_a/L), phi_k=(2/L)^(d/2)g_k, and y=x+z with both sites in the box. Telescoping the d factors, all of magnitude at most one, gives
  |g_k(x+z)-g_k(x)| <= (pi/L) sum_a |z_a| k_a
                        <= pi |z||k|/L.
The wedge has the exact rewrite
  g_k(x)[g_l(x+z)-g_l(x)]-g_l(x)[g_k(x+z)-g_k(x)],
so its magnitude is at most pi|z|(|k|+|l|)/L. Restoring both orbital normalizations and squaring gives exactly
  4^d pi^2 |z|^2 (|k|+|l|)^2/L^(2d+2).
This works for arbitrary signed finite integer z; no boundary correction is needed beyond requiring both endpoints lie in Lambda_L. Since (|k|+|l|)^2<=2(|k|^2+|l|^2), summing unordered mode pairs gives at most 2 c0 c2. There are at most S_L<=L^d starting sites per z. Thus the stated A=2*4^d*pi^2*c0*c2*sum_z|z|^2 and a_L=A/L^(d+2) are correct. Duplicate/oriented displacements only overcount and preserve the upper bound.

For C=sum_k f_k |phi_k><phi_k|, the product-Bernoulli Fock state has inclusion probabilities det C_X by Cauchy-Binet/Wick. Pointwise Q<=sum_(x,z)n_xn_(x+z) proves delta<=sum of pair intensities<=a_L. Also M Q<=sum_(x,z) M n_xn_(x+z). For each distinct pair,
  E[M n_x n_y]=2 det C_xy + sum_(u!=x,y) det C_xyu.
PSD Schur complementation gives det C_xyu<=det C_xy*C_uu, including singular blocks; sum_u C_uu=Tr C=sum_k f_k<=c0. Hence the pair-weighted contribution is at most (2+c0)det C_xy and q_M<= (2+c0)a_L. The proof does not mistake union probability for expected pair count.

The compression sign is valid even when [K,Q]!=0. Because rho0 is Gibbs of K-lambda M/L^2 and [K,M]=0, [K,rho0]=0 and K rho0 is PSD. Cyclicity plus this commutation gives
  Tr(rho0 QK)=Tr(QK rho0)=Tr(Q rho0 K)=Tr(rho0 KQ)>=0.
Thus both negative cross terms in PKP=K-QK-KQ+QKQ can be dropped. In d dimensions the one-body spectrum is in [0,8Jd], so 0<=K<=8Jd M. Congruence by Q and [Q,M]=0 give QKQ<=8Jd QMQ=8Jd MQ. These facts yield the displayed kinetic inequality with no illicit [K,Q] assumption.

For delta>0, omega=Q rho0 Q/delta has E_omega M=q_M/delta because Q commutes with M. Against the full-rank Fock Gibbs reference gamma_s=e^(-sM)/Z_s, nonnegativity of D(omega||gamma_s) gives S(omega)<=s E_omega M+log Z_s, whether or not omega is number diagonal. For S_L>=1, s=log S_L and Z_s=(1+1/S_L)^(S_L), so log Z_s<=1 and delta S(omega)<=q_M log S_L+delta. Pinching yields S(rho0)-S(sigma)<=h2(delta)+delta S(omega), hence the stated entropy term. At S_L=1 this remains valid with s=0.

For finite W>=0, H0+W>=H0 implies eigenvalue-by-eigenvalue min-max order and Z_W<=Z_0; no operator monotonicity of exp is used. WP=0 implies W sigma=0. Therefore D(sigma||rho_W)<=D(sigma||rho0), whose energy part is bounded by the projected K increment plus tau B |Delta M|. The exact number increment is (delta E_0 M-q_M)/p and |Delta M|<=(c0 delta+q_M)/p. Substituting delta<=a_L, q_M<=(2+c0)a_L, p>=1-a_L reproduces every coefficient in
  G_L=[h2(a_L)+a_L+(2+c0)a_L log S_L
       +tau*a_L{2J*pi^2*c2+8Jd L^2(2+c0)+B(2+2c0)}]/(1-a_L).
All numerator terms are nonnegative, so using the common denominator 1-a_L is a safe weakening of the entropy bound (which itself need not be divided by p). For fixed d,J,tau,B,Z, a_L=O(L^(-d-2)); the L^2 q_M kinetic term is O(L^-d), and the entropy terms are O(L^(-d-2)log L). Thus G_L=O(L^-d) and the stated whole-state trace-norm rate O(L^(-d/2)) follows within the assigned derivation.

FIXTURE AND EVIDENCE SCOPE
The supplied 2D/3D fixture checks the correct spinless CAR Fock Hamiltonians and has 16- and 256-dimensional Fock spaces. Its script uses float64 NumPy eigensolvers, tolerance assertions, and Gaussian sums truncated at 100 without an outward tail correction. Thus the saved JSON is useful finite numerical evidence, not an exact or certified inequality check; it is not a proof gap because the written claim labels finite checks diagnostic only. The 2D trace-theorem RHS is 2.33>2 and therefore vacuous there; the 3D fixture gives a nontrivial RHS about 0.958. It tests only positive-axis nearest-neighbour offsets and a parity-preserving number-changing W example, whereas the proof quantifies over arbitrary fixed finite displacement lists and arbitrary W>=0 supported on Q. That difference limits the fixture's scope but does not refute the proof.
