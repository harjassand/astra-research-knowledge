NEW REVIEW REPAIR: DIRECT PHYSICAL-COVARIANCE SCHUR CERTIFICATE.
This removes the need to acquire a TFD/Bloch--Messiah optical description
just to certify the covariance-input transfer, and settles the strict
active-support question in the rationalization construction.

Let J be an orthogonal complex structure and Sigma=I/2+K be a real physical
covariance: Sigma+iJ/2 >=0. Reality also gives Sigma-iJ/2 >=0. Write
  R=(K+J K J^T)/2, X=K-R.
R commutes with J and X anticommutes. In a complex orthonormal eigenbasis
of iJ, write R=diag(R_+,R_-) and X=[[0,M],[M*,0]]. The two uncertainty
matrices are, in one choice of block ordering,
  [[R_++I,M],[M*,R_-]], [[R_+,M],[M*,R_-+I]].
Their principal blocks imply R_+,R_- >=0. When R>0, the Schur complements
give
  M R_-^{-1} M* <= R_++I,
  M* R_+^{-1} M <= R_-+I.
These are exactly XR^{-1}X-R <= I in the original real coordinates.
Uniform vacuum loss K_eta=eta W K W^T scales D=XR^{-1}X-R to eta W D W^T,
so D_eta <= eta I. The passive choice B=eta/(1-eta) I bounds
D_eta(I-uD_eta)^{-1} for 0<=u<=1 by inverse/operator monotonicity and has
norm <=1/9 when eta<=1/10. N72 Lemmas 4--7 then give precisely l02's budget.
If Sigma+iJ/2 >= delta I with delta>0, both principal R blocks are >=delta I,
so R is strictly positive without deleting unidentified vacuum directions.
In particular Vhat=V0+2 delta I with ||V0-V||op<=delta has this property.
Finite-temperature stable finite-mode quadratic Gibbs states are strictly
physical, but acquiring their covariance and its numerical slack is still
a separate computation. Exact vacuum directions can instead be deleted.

SCOPE REPAIR: HOMOGENEOUS MULTIAFFINE LC IS ALREADY LORENTZIAN.
The report's suggested escape via a homogeneous, merely LC high-degree
ancilla cannot occur in the legal binary/multiaffine interface. If a
nonnegative multiaffine p is LC, write p=x_i g+h with g,h>=0. For positive
x,y in the remaining variables and t in [0,1], apply the defining LC
inequality to (R,x),(R,y), divide by R, and take R to infinity. This proves
g=partial_i p is LC unless zero. g is multiaffine, so the argument iterates
over every derivative. Repeated differentiation in the same coordinate is
zero. Thus p is strongly LC; if homogeneous, Branden--Huh Theorem 2.30
makes it Lorentzian. This proof also handles zero coefficients because a
nonzero nonnegative polynomial is positive on the positive orthant.
Consequently the no-go extends to homogeneous nonnegative multiaffine LC
vertex and bond polynomials, without separately promising Lorentzianity.
Any 'merely LC' escape must specify nonmultiaffine/nonhomogeneous resources
or a different representation. Nonhomogeneous LC can still be a legal
different Chen--Liu route, so no general impossibility is asserted.

The source closure theorem is established prior art. The network application
and this scope repair have score 2 as a reusable obstruction. They complement
s01's Lorentzian projector and s02's direct-insertion obstruction; s03's
exchange implementation does not widen the cone. External novelty UNKNOWN.

Product-error proof passes, with the following explicit noncommutative check.
Let G_i=log p2(h A_i)>=0, S=sum G_i, g=sum ||G_i||<=hD. The palindrome
contains each G_i twice. Its degree-two coefficient is 2 sum_i G_i^2 plus
2 sum_{i<j}(G_i G_j+G_j G_i)=2S^2. Thus both it and exp(2S) agree through
degree two. Each degree-k norm is bounded by (2g)^k/k!, so the two tails
total at most (8/3)g^3 exp(2g). Each factor has smallest singular value
>=1; hence W W*>=I. The resolvent integral gives the operator-norm log
Lipschitz bound on matrices >=I. The scalar positive-tail estimate gives
0<=x-log p2(x)<=x^3/5 for x<=1. Combining them yields the stated bound
  ||m log B-beta sum A_i|| <= (43/60) tau^3/m^2,
  tau=beta D, m>=2tau.
