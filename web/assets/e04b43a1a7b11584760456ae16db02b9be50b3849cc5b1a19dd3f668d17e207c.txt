PARAMETER-BLIND QUANTUM ARCHIVE FOR THE INTERACTING CRITICAL CHAIN
2026-10-08. INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; no external validity/priority claim.
This theorem does not assume or establish a Lieb-Liniger continuum limit.

1. THE ARCHIVE THEOREM AND A PUBLIC ENERGY CERTIFICATE

Use the exact model and all-sector estimate in proof.txt. All unknown
lambda,g satisfying |lambda|<=B, g>=g_->0 are allowed. Public inputs are
N=L-1, J,tau,B,g_- and the requested trace error 0<epsilon<=1.

THEOREM. There are public, parameter-blind CPTP encoders E_L and decoders
D_L, reading no lambda or g, such that their persistent register dimension
is bounded independently of L and

  sup_{L,lambda,g} ||D_L E_L(rho_{L,lambda,g})-rho_{L,lambda,g}||_1
      <=epsilon.                                        (1.1)

At fixed public constants the bound is

  q(epsilon)=O((1+log(1/epsilon))^(3/2)) qubits.            (1.2)

This is an explicit achievable upper bound, not an optimal rate. A
particle-number label/overflow flag, workspace and N output spins are
charged separately below. The family is non-Gaussian, and g is not supplied
to a model-dependent eigenbasis transform.

Let Hpos=Hcrit+(g/L)W and G0=tau L^2 Hpos>=0. The grand partition function
is at least one because the vacuum has zero energy. Apply the all-sector
theorem at tau'=tau/2, B'=2B. Write

  c=min(g_-/96,J/72),
  C_half=1+2 sum_{k>=1}exp(-2J tau k^2),
  A_half=e C_half exp[tau B+tau c/2],
  E_*=2 log sum_{m>=0} A_half^m exp[-(tau c/2)m^2].       (1.3)

A closed, simpler admissible substitute is

  E_bar=(log A_half)^2/(tau c)
             +2 log[1+sqrt(2pi/(tau c))] >=E_*.

Complete the square and bound a shifted lattice Gaussian sum by one plus
its whole-line integral to obtain this substitute. Any stated cutoff may
use E_bar in place of E_*.

Then the exact Gibbs state satisfies, uniformly in L,lambda,g,

  Tr(G0 rho)<=E_*,
  Tr(Hcrit rho)<=E_*/(tau L^2),
  Tr(W rho)<=E_*/(tau g_- L).                            (1.4)

Proof: Hpos commutes with number. Jensen under the exact Gibbs eigenvalue
distribution gives

  Tr(G0 rho)/2 <=log Tr(rho exp(G0/2))
    =log[Z(tau/2,2lambda,g)/Z(tau,lambda,g)]
    <=log sum_{m>=0}A_half^m exp[-(tau c/2)m^2].

Here Z denotes the same model with its displayed inverse-temperature and
detuning arguments; the chemical factor tau lambda m is unchanged in the
numerator. Use Z(tau,lambda,g)>=1 and the all-sector theorem. The lower
bounds Hpos>=Hcrit and Hpos>=(g_-/L)W finish (1.4). This logarithmic
partition estimate avoids an artificial exponential energy constant.

2. THE EXACT GAP-COMPRESSION CONTRACTION

Fix a sector 1<=m<=S. Ordered particle locations are
1<=x_1<...<x_m<=S. Set

  y_i=x_i-i+1,          1<=y_1<=...<=y_m<=n=L-m.

This is a bijection between configurations and weakly ordered m-tuples
on {1,...,n}. Occupied adjacent original sites correspond exactly to
equal consecutive y coordinates. If a value v has multiplicity r_v,
let w(y)=m!/prod_v r_v!, the number of distinct labeled permutations.

Define a contraction from the physical m-sector into the symmetric
subspace of (C^n) tensor-power m by

  (K_m psi)(z_1,...,z_m)=psi(sort(z))/sqrt(m!).            (2.1)

Its Gram matrix is diagonal in the original configuration basis:

  K_m^* K_m |x>= [w(y)/m!] |x>.

Thus 0<=K_m^*K_m<=I and

  I-K_m^*K_m <=1_{W>0}<=W.                              (2.2)

Away from original contacts the map is exactly isometric. This is not an
assumption of bosonic statistics for the spin input: it is an explicit
known linear map with a trace-preserving overflow completion.

Let h_n be the one-particle Dirichlet path operator

  <u,h_n u>=2J[sum_{v=1}^{n-1}|u(v+1)-u(v)|^2
                       +|u(1)|^2+|u(n)|^2],
  H^free_{n,m}=sum_{i=1}^m h_n^(i).

Then the crucial exact form comparison is

  K_m^* H^free_{n,m} K_m <= Hcrit,m.                    (2.3)

Proof with all multiplicities. A full labeled cube edge increments one
coordinate v to v+1. Sorting its two endpoints produces exactly one legal
weakly ordered hop, hence exactly one permitted original exclusion hop.
For a chamber edge whose source multiplicities are r_v,r_{v+1}, the
number of labeled preimage edges is

  w(y) r_v
    =m!/[(r_v-1)! r_{v+1}! prod_{u notin {v,v+1}}r_u!]
    <=m!.

After the normalization 1/m! in (2.1), its quadratic-form contribution
is at most the corresponding 2J exclusion edge. A cube ghost-boundary
edge at coordinate 1, or n, has respectively multiplicity w(y)r_1, or
w(y)r_n, also at most m!. The original boundary events are x_1=1 and
x_m=S, respectively. These are exactly the two endpoint-killing terms
in proof.txt (1.1). Sum all edges and both faces to prove (2.3).
The n=1 case includes both ghost faces and is also covered.

3. A PUBLIC LOW-MODE PROJECTION

The h_n eigenvectors are the known Dirichlet sine modes, and

  eps_{n,k}=4J[1-cos(pi k/(n+1))]
              >=8J k^2/(n+1)^2, 1<=k<=n.               (3.1)

Let P_{M,m,n} project onto symmetric m-particle states all of whose
one-particle sine labels are <=min(M,n). If n>M, a state outside this
projection has at least one particle in a label >=M+1, so

  I-P_{M,m,n} <= H^free_{n,m}/eps_{n,M+1}.

Since n+1=L-m+1<=L for m>=1, (2.3), (3.1) give

 Tr[K_m rho_m K_m^*(I-P_{M,m,n})]
    <= L^2 Tr(Hcrit,m rho_m)/(8J(M+1)^2).               (3.2)

If n<=M the left side is zero. Here rho_m may be unnormalized; no sector
probability is divided out. Sum over m to combine directly with (1.4).
The common low-mode archive spaces have dimensions

  d_m(M)=binom(M+m-1,m),
  sum_{m=0}^r d_m(M)=binom(M+r,r).                       (3.3)

Unused labels k>n are known to be unoccupied and may be padded by zero.

4. COMPLETELY POSITIVE ENCODE/DECODE AND ITS ERROR

Let t_r=Pr_rho(Npart>r). Retain m<=r and use the single successful encoder
operator K_m followed by P_{M,m,n}, expressed in the finite archive mode
basis. Give the encoder an orthogonal overflow flag whose weight is the
missing trace. On success the decoder applies K_m^* to that mode subspace;
complete its missing trace and the encoder overflow by a fixed physical
vacuum state. Both maps are CPTP, since every indicated operator is a
contraction. These direct sums can be implemented coherently; measuring
m and retaining its label is also sufficient for this number-conserving
Gibbs family. Coherence within a number sector is retained.

The combined successful operator is the positive contraction

  R= direct-sum_{0<=m<=r} K_m^* P_{M,m,n} K_m,

with K_0=P_0=1 and R=0 on m>r. The completed channel is

  rho -> R rho R+Tr[(I-R^2)rho] |vac><vac|.

Because 0<=R<=I, I-R^2<=2(I-R). Equations (2.2), (3.2) and (1.4) imply

 p:=Tr[(I-R^2)rho]
   <=2[t_r+E_*/(tau g_- L)+E_*/(8tau J(M+1)^2)].         (4.1)

For completeness, purify rho as |Psi>. Its unnormalized successful
purification (R tensor I)|Psi> has squared norm 1-p. The trace-norm
distance between the corresponding pure operators is at most 2sqrt(p):
the exact two-vector formula is sqrt[(1+(1-p))^2-4|<Psi,R Psi>|^2],
and <Psi,R Psi>>=1-p since R>=R^2. Partial trace cannot increase norm.
Adding the failure output of trace p gives

  ||decoded rho-rho||_1 <=2sqrt(p)+p<=3sqrt(p).           (4.2)

This argument does not replace the nonisometric K_m by a unitary, divide
by a possibly small success probability, or grant a free inverse map.

Choose integers r,M>=1 and L0>=2 such that

  sum_{m>r} A^m exp(-tau c m^2)<=epsilon^2/54,
  M+1>=sqrt[54 E_*/(8tau J epsilon^2)],
  L0>=54 E_*/(tau g_- epsilon^2).                        (4.3)

For L>=L0 the public K_m archive has error <=epsilon by (4.1)-(4.2).
The retained dimension, including one flag, is

  D_large=1+binom(M+r,r).

For L<L0 use only the number projection P_{<=r}, with an overflow flag,
and retain the physical occupation configuration coherently. Since the
Gibbs input is block diagonal in number, its decoded error is <=2t_r,
which is <=epsilon under (4.3). Its dimension is bounded by

  D_small=1+sum_{m=0}^{min(r,L0-2)}binom(L0-2,m).

A persistent register of dimension max(D_large,D_small) works for every
L. At fixed public constants, r=O(sqrt(log(1/epsilon))), M=O(epsilon^-1),
L0=O(epsilon^-2). The elementary bounds binom(M+r,r)<=(e(M+r)/r)^r and
sum_{m<=r}binom(L0-2,m)<=(r+1)max{1,L0^r} prove (1.2).
This small-L number cutoff is necessary to avoid charging L0 whole qubits;
whole-chain exact retention would give the weaker O(epsilon^-2) rate.

5. FINITE ALGORITHMS, ACQUISITION, PRECISION AND TOTAL COST

The encoder never acquires lambda or g. It uses only a public lower g_-,
upper B and exact site count. Constants (1.3), (4.3) can be certified by
outward-rounded finite Gaussian sums plus integral tails. Their numerical
size may be very large when g_- or tau is small; no benign conditioning
or practical tolerance constant is hidden in the asymptotic rate.

Small-L branch: coherent population counting followed by combinatorial
ranking stores each allowed occupation string and its m label in the
dimension above. Straight reversible scanning/ranking costs polynomial
in N,r,log N; a binomial table and arithmetic precision are charged as
encoding workspace, not persistent state. Decode by unranking to N spins.

Large-L branch: coherently extract the ordered occupied positions and
subtract i-1 to form y_i. An exact isometry U_m maps the ordered y basis
to its normalized symmetric labeled-cube vector, with amplitude 1/sqrt(w).
It can be realized by uniform multiset-permutation ranking/unranking:
prepare a uniform index of size w, form its labeled permutation, and
erase the index and ordered tuple by their reversible inverse ranks.
Factorial/binomial counts require O(r log r) bits and polynomial arithmetic
in r,log N. Approximate preparation of a uniform finite-range index uses
standard finite controlled rotations, with its synthesis error charged.

K_m=U_m diag(sqrt(w/m!)). Implement the diagonal contraction by a public
controlled rotation and a success flag. Apply the known n-site sine
transform independently to the m labeled registers; this preserves the
symmetric subspace. Dense one-particle unitary decompositions give the
conservative upper gate count O(r N^2 poly(log N)) in an ordinary encoded
register model. No fast sine transform is assumed. Project labels to
<=M, and rank the surviving multiset occupations into d_m(M) coordinates.
The m label and flag are included in the direct-sum dimension. Decoder
unranks, reverses the sine transform, performs the same public contraction
and U_m^*, expands x_i=y_i+i-1 and writes the N output spins.

Intermediate labeled position/mode registers require O(r log N) qubits,
in addition to the physical input/output and reversible arithmetic
workspace. Reading/writing the N spins costs at least order N. The result
is about persistent archive memory, not N-independent total time or peak
workspace. Each G-gate implementation may be synthesized to total
operator/channel error <=eta using angle accuracy O(eta/G), requiring
O(log(G/eta)) bits per angle and the gate-library's synthesis overhead.
Allocate eta separately within the requested error and replace epsilon in
(4.3) by the remaining analytic error budget. No exact uncharged continuum
oracle, permanent-evaluation oracle, or successful postselection is used.

Preparing beta=tau L^2 Gibbs inputs is not supplied. If the physical model
is programmed approximately, a worst-case finite Gibbs perturbation bound
||rho(G+Delta G)-rho(G)||_1<=2||Delta G|| charges errors separately.
For example |Delta g| gives ||Delta G||<=tau L^2|Delta g| (W<=L), and
|Delta lambda| gives <=tau L|Delta lambda|. A change Delta J in the
critical combination has norm <=4tau L^3|Delta J|. Thus conservative
fractional precisions include respectively log2(tau L^2/eta),
log2(tau L/eta), and log2(4tau L^3/eta), up to fixed budget constants.
The encoder does not estimate or use these unknown programmed values.

6. ENERGY METRIC AND THE LIMIT OF WHAT THE ARCHIVE PRESERVES

The input has bounded critical scaled energy by (1.4). The archive theorem
preserves the full density matrix in trace norm; it does not promise
uniform accuracy for the unbounded observable L^2 Hpos.

Indeed K_m^* recovery suppresses the original contact-node amplitude by
a factorial weight. Take two bosons in the first n=L-2 Dirichlet mode
f(v)=sqrt(2/(n+1))sin(pi v/(n+1)). Away from y_1=y_2, K_2^* multiplies
f(y_1)f(y_2) by sqrt(2), while at equality it multiplies by 1/sqrt(2).
Write eps_1=4J[1-cos(pi/(n+1))] and s=sum_v f(v)^4. For the resulting
unnormalized successful decoder vector psi, an exact form calculation is

  <psi,Hcrit psi>=2eps_1(1-s)+2J s.                    (6.1)

Start with chi_x=sqrt(2)f(y_1)f(y_2), for which
<chi,Hcrit chi>=2eps_1 and at contact (Hcrit chi)_x=eps_1 chi_x.
Then psi=chi-(1/2)1_{W>0}chi. The contact diagonal of Hcrit is 4J,
the contact squared norm of chi is 2s, and no one-hop edge connects two
contact configurations. These facts prove (6.1). For n>=2,
s=3/[2(n+1)], and consequently

  tau L^2<psi,Hcrit psi>/L ->3tau J>0.                  (6.2)

The vacuum overflow has zero energy. This legal archive input demonstrates
that the decoder has no uniform critical-energy bound on its fixed
low-mode output space. A scalar subtraction does not repair the issue;
this interacting vacuum already has zero energy. No claim is made here
that every actual Gibbs output has this same divergence coefficient.

This warning does not invalidate (1.1), because the modified strip has
vanishing probability. An energy-preserving decoder requires a contact
amplitude restoration/smoothing argument not supplied here. Physical
input generation, critical-energy fidelity, and a local continuum simulator
remain separate costs/gates.

7. STRONGEST BASELINES AND SCIENTIFIC STATUS

A supplied interacting Hamiltonian could be diagonalized in each retained
sector, but its spectral basis depends on unknown g and its full sector
dimension grows with N. A generic energy-eigenbasis truncation therefore
does not itself furnish this common public decoder. The free XX number
decoder is insufficient: within a fixed m-sector, the gW term changes
eigenvectors. Already for S=3,m=2, W=diag(1,0,1) in the ordered basis
(12,13,23) and Hcrit has nonzero off-diagonals between contact and
noncontact configurations; [Hcrit,W]!=0. Thus a single parameter-blind
conditional sector state cannot be the unknown-g sufficient decoder.

Here a public coordinate contraction and public bosonic sine basis replace
that parameter-dependent spectral acquisition. The model-specific uniform
number/contact/energy estimates make their error certified. General
energy-cutoff quantum compression is established technology; no claim
is made to discover projection channels or quantum compression.

The theorem gives a finite quantum archive for every L in an interacting
unknown-parameter family, without proving the limit state. It is a
stronger operational consequence than merely proving tight particle
number. Exact LL continuum trace convergence, a limiting classical/EB
error floor, optimal memory, efficient thermal input generation, and a
critical-energy-preserving decoder remain UNKNOWN. External proof validity
and priority remain UNKNOWN; finite checks are separately scoped evidence.
