Notation caveat
The paper writes span(A) as a Hamiltonian operator, apparently meaning the orthogonal projector onto range(A), as required by its projective-term setup. This audit uses that interpretation. For positive A, the support projector has the same kernel; the principal kernel conclusions do not require a normalization convention beyond this.

Inverse sandwich identity
For invertible Hermitian Q and orthogonal projector Pi,
  ker(Q^{-1} Pi Q^{-1}) = Q ker(Pi).
Thus the proposed split kernels are exactly those resulting from the §8.1 inverse-sandwich recipe followed by support projection:
  ker h_init = Q12(span{|0>}1 tensor C2 tensor C3),
  ker h_gate = Q12(C1 tensor span{|Phi>}23).
The inverse rather than forward sandwich is essential. The forward Q appears in the resulting kernel because it inverts the inverse sandwich.

Exact split separation
Let Q12=P_Phi12+delta(I-P_Phi12), delta>0, and h_out=P_0,3. Let the terms be projectors onto the orthogonal complements of the two displayed kernels. Their common parent kernel is the one-dimensional span of
  psi_delta = [(1+delta)|000>+2delta|011>+(1-delta)|110>]/sqrt(2(1+3delta^2)).
No nonzero vector satisfies all three checks. psi_delta satisfies both parent checks, so the equally weighted three-term Hamiltonian has combinatorial gap exactly 1/3.
For chi=|Phi>12|1>3,
  <chi|h_out|chi>=0,
  <chi|h_init|chi>=delta^2/(1+delta^2),
  <chi|h_gate|chi>=3delta^2/(1+3delta^2).
Consequently unnormalized ground energy <=4delta^2+O(delta^4), and normalized energy <=(4/3)delta^2+O(delta^4). This establishes failure of any delta-independent combinatorial-to-spectral lower bound for this split parent class. It does not establish failure for the §8.2 grouped class.

The §8.2 one-vertex grouping removes this mechanism
With initialization1 and local identity Choi23 grouped at one vertex, Eq.116 has the precheck I-|Omega><Omega|, Omega=|0>1|Phi>23. Its kernel is exactly span{psi_delta}, and its support projector is
  h_group = I-|psi_delta><psi_delta|.
This is also the support projector of h_init+h_gate, but not their sum. It retains their exact common kernel while replacing small nonzero eigenvalues by 1.
The exact minimum energy is
  lambda_min(h_group+h_out)=1-sqrt(2delta^2/(1+3delta^2)) -> 1.
More generally for fixed positive weights a,b,
  lambda_min(a h_group+b h_out)
    = [a+b-sqrt((a-b)^2+4ab*2delta^2/(1+3delta^2))]/2
    -> min(a,b)>0.
This is a local-term specialization, not a full Eq.117 instance on an edgeless graph, since Eq.117 contains 1/|E|. Positive constant normalization or dummy terms do not change this local obstruction to the proposed mechanism.
For one EPR location the paper's parameters satisfy delta=delta_vertex/sqrt(3), or delta=delta_edge/sqrt(3), when matched to this Q. For multiple locations, paper Eq.113 uses one grouped rank-one Bell-product projector plus a single uniform coefficient on its entire orthogonal complement, not a product of independent single-pair Q maps.

Bounded two-vertex allocation: grouping blocks energy, but literal source formula fails FF sanity check
Suggested allocation: initialize qubit1 at u; an identity transmission Choi23 has half2 at u and half3 at v; Qedge12 is the only nontrivial map; Qvertex are identity; there are no local computation Choi states. Reading Eq.115 literally gives h_e=h_group. Eq.116 gives h_u=|1><1|1 and h_v=0.
Then h_u psi_delta !=0: exact energy
  <psi_delta|h_u|psi_delta>=(1-delta)^2/[2(1+3delta^2)] ->1/2.
Hence these literal terms do not have the state asserted by Claim8.1 as a common zero-energy ground state. Indeed lambda_min(h_u+h_e) stays bounded away from zero, tending to 1-1/sqrt(2).
This is a source-consistency/placement warning, not a certified counterexample to an intended construction. Either this transmission ownership is ruled out by a convention not explicit in the displayed formulas, or the vertex inverse must include adjacent edge maps (or the vertex precheck must be changed). We found no explicit placement rule in §§8.1-8.2 or Definition3.20 resolving it.
The same issue arises on a naive 3-vertex identity-transmission chain: an edge-resource output half can be touched by the next edge's Q map, but Eq.115 only includes its own Qedge inverse and endpoint Qvertex inverses. Therefore a faithful chain audit must first settle which Bell pairs belong to which maps and which checked Choi resources those maps overlap. Inserting local identity gates alone does not resolve the vertex-term issue if Qedge still touches a checked local-resource output half.

Failure ledger and next attack
- Rejected: treating separate init/gate projectors as literal Eq.116 terms.
- Rejected: promoting split toy to a no-go theorem for the paper's final HDX/MPC construction.
- Rejected: treating the naive two-vertex allocation as validated faithful while ignoring its failure to satisfy Claim8.1.
- Strongest next step: construct a small explicitly owned distributed tensor network, list every Bell pair and every Choi/init check, and verify the stated common ground before searching for low energy versus output. An overlap-complete inverse sandwich may be a reasonable repaired parent to investigate, but that would require a new analysis and must be labeled separately from the displayed source construction.

Executable sanity checks
check_grouping.py and grouping_checks.json in this directory verify split witness and grouped eigenvalue formulas at six delta values, as well as the literal two-vertex FF failure. Exact derivations above carry the result; numerical agreement is only a sanity check.
