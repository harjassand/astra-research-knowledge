INFORMATION CONTRACTION VERSUS OPERATOR-SCORE GEOMETRY
8 October 2026

STATUS

The requested unrestricted dimension-free inequality remains UNKNOWN. I
derive a rank-free finite-label information bound, a direct all-frame score
formulation of the missing common-POVM step, and a new quantitative
near-commutator consequence of an actual compatible broadcaster. These do
not close the common-POVM lift. The Pauli and affine-plane Clifford controls
falsify the tempting shortcuts from Holevo information, accessible
information, or pairwise score commutators alone, but both retain a positive
two-output broadcasting obstruction. No qualifying counterexample with
bounded Holevo capacity, b_2->0, and e_EB bounded below was found. Internal
derivations and source-card results below are not external mathematical
validation or a priority claim.

1. LEGAL INTERFACE: NO LABEL-KNOWING MAP, COVARIANCE, OR SUPPLIED CENTER

For each finite-dimensional H let E be a compact set of density operators.
Use half trace distance T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2 and define

  C(E) = sup_(finite priors pi on E) chi(pi),
  b_2(E) = inf_B sup_(rho in E, i=1,2) T(B_i(rho),rho),
  e_EB(E) = inf_LambdaEB sup_(rho in E) T(Lambda(rho),rho).

B ranges over one CPTP map on the full input algebra with two outputs and
both stated marginals; its outputs can be correlated. Lambda is one full-
domain measure-and-prepare channel, independent of the unknown state label.
The dimension, label count, and channel descriptions may grow. No
covariance, rank, reference state, independent-copy output, or restricted
measurement promise is part of the target:

  sup_n C(E_n)<infinity and b_2(E_n)->0  =>  e_EB(E_n)->0.  (T)

In finite dimension the channel infimum defining b_2 is attained. An
approximate broadcaster must still be the same physical channel for every
rho in E; separate pairwise channels do not satisfy this interface.

2. FIRST ATTEMPT: EXTRACT ONE COMMON DECODER FROM MANY RECEIVERS

Fix a finite E={rho_x}_{x=1}^m, prior pi, and a broadcaster of marginal
error beta. Iterating its two-output map down a binary tree and uniformly
permuting the K leaves yields K correlated receivers with worst marginal
error at most phi(K) beta, where, for 2^s<=K<=2^(s+1),

  phi(K)=s+2-2^(s+1)/K.

The tree uses K-1 calls to the actual broadcaster; permutation symmetrizing
does not create independent copies. Sequentially measure these receivers.
At history h before receiver j, let p_x(h) be the posterior, sigma_x^h
the conditional state on receiver j, tau_h=sum_x p_x(h)sigma_x^h, and
d_x(h)=T(sigma_x^h,tau_h). The posterior trace spread relevant to a
measure-and-prepare decoder is

  S_h=sum_x p_x(h)d_x(h).

Choose x_h maximizing p_x(h)d_x(h)^2 and perform its binary Helstrom test
against tau_h. Its classical conditional information gain g_h obeys,
by the exact Helstrom total-variation identity and classical Pinsker in
nats,

  g_h >= 2 p_(x_h)(h)d_(x_h)(h)^2
      >= (2/m) sum_x p_x(h)d_x(h)^2
      >= (2/m) S_h^2.                                    (1)

There is a posterior-effective-support refinement. With
N_h=(sum_x sqrt(p_x(h)))^2, the same maximizing label gives

  g_h >= 2 S_h^2/N_h.                                     (2)

For the adaptive transcript, the gains g_j sum to at most the input
Holevo quantity: the classical chain rule gives

  sum_(j=1)^K g_j=I(X:Y_1,...,Y_K)
       <= chi(X:B_1...B_K) <= chi(pi) <= C(E).             (3)

The second inequality is data processing through the K-output channel.
Bayes' rule and Cauchy-Schwarz give E_h N_h<=N_(1/2)(pi), where
N_(1/2)(pi)=(sum_x sqrt(pi_x))^2. Thus for some receiver j,

  E_h S_h <= sqrt(N_(1/2)(pi) chi(pi)/(2K)).               (4)

Measuring the prefix and preparing tau_h is a legal EB map on the original
input: the broadcaster followed by the prefix instrument induces a POVM,
and each history has a fixed prepared state. The selected receiver's
marginal error adds phi(K)beta. This proves the prior-average bound

  inf_LambdaEB sum_x pi_x T(Lambda(rho_x),rho_x)
       <= phi(K) beta + sqrt(N_(1/2)(pi)chi(pi)/(2K)).      (5)

Finite-dimensional minimax over the compact convex EB channel set and the
prior simplex then supplies ONE channel for worst-member loss:

  e_EB(E) <= inf_(K>=1) [phi(K)b_2(E)+sqrt(Gamma(E)/(2K))],
  Gamma(E)=max_pi N_(1/2)(pi)chi(pi).                       (6)

This is a direct actual-broadcaster-to-common-POVM/reconstruction argument.
It needs no rank or covariance assumption. Its missing dimension-free step
is explicit: C(E) bounds chi(pi), but does not bound N_(1/2)(pi), the label
count, or Gamma(E). For m labels, Gamma<=m C. A dimension-free outcome of
(6) therefore requires a new way to replace effective support by a
quantity controlled by compatibility, or a different argument.

Costs: K outputs, K-1 broadcaster calls, and a uniform random permutation
of K leaves; prefix histories have at most 2^(K-1) outcomes. Conditional
states and Helstrom tests may be exponentially large to compute. Minimax
proves existence of a common channel but is not an acquisition algorithm;
an explicit finite-alphabet bound from the finite-family proof is at most
(m+1)2^(K-1) outcomes. No rank or label-count assumption is hidden in (5).

Why Holevo cannot simply replace accessible information. For history h,
the conditional quantum Holevo quantity

  chi_h = sum_x p_x(h) D(sigma_x^h || tau_h)

obeys quantum Pinsker chi_h>=2 sum_x p_x(h)d_x(h)^2>=2S_h^2. But the
chain-rule quantity in (3) is the information gained by actual classical
measurements, not chi_h. Holevo gives g_h<=chi_h, in the wrong direction
for replacing g_h in (1). Moreover, the conditional quantum chi values
after measuring earlier receivers are not known to sum to C(E). The
quantum chain rule sums conditionals on the unmeasured quantum prefixes;
measuring those prefixes can change the later conditional ensembles.
Approximate broadcasting may supply an extra monogamy/redundancy estimate,
but no such dimension-free estimate is established here.

3. INFORMATION RADIUS GIVES SCORE MASS, NOT A COMMON MEASUREMENT

The initial problem does not supply a center. The information-radius
identity supplies one: for every eta>0 there is a state sigma with

  sup_(rho in E) D(rho||sigma) <= C(E)+eta.

This is a derived center, not an extra promise. The N93 smoothing and
compatible Petz/flag reduction uses it to produce a bounded-likelihood
tracial problem with a central reference. It does not require a rank bound
or covariance. The center, its support/spectrum, smoothing maps and flags
must be acquired; their cost is charged in the N93 source record.

On the resulting fixed-sandwich interface write

  rho_H=sigma(I+H), H=H*, tau(H)=0, ||H||_infinity<=theta<1,
  tau=normalized trace on the reduced tracial algebra.

For the matrix case sigma=I/d and tau=Tr/d. The center relation gives the
dimension-free score-energy control

  D(rho_H||sigma)=tau[(I+H)log(I+H)]
       >= tau(H^2)/(2(1+theta)),
  tau(H^2)<=2(1+theta)C(E)                                (7)

when sigma is an information center for this family. This bounds the trace
of a score covariance but does not bound its rank or find a measurement.
After the sandwich reduction C(E)<=log(1+theta) is automatic; capacity
has done its work in producing the reduction and does not control the
number of weak score directions.

For a finite prior p on H_x define the covariance operator on centered
Hermitian L2 by

  Q_p=sum_x p_x |H_x><H_x|,
  V(p)=Tr_(L2)Q_p=sum_x p_x tau(H_x^2).

For one rank-one POVM/reference ensemble M={w_y,P_y} with
sum_y w_y P_y=sigma, its captured score is

  J_M(p)=sum_y w_y sum_x p_x [Tr(P_y H_x)]^2,
  h_1(Q_p)=sup_M J_M(p),
  Delta(p)=V(p)-h_1(Q_p).

The POVM must be common across x. Finite-dimensional convex minimax gives

  q(E)=inf_M max_x {tau(H_x^2)-J_M(H_x)}
      =sup_p Delta(p),
  e_EB(E) <= (1/2)sqrt(q(E)).                              (8)

The second inequality is the canonical reference-preserving EB decoder;
it follows from (I-Psi_M)^2<=I-Psi_M and normalized-trace
||.||_1<=||.||_2. This formulation keeps posterior trace reconstruction
and worst-member minimax distinct from information quantities.

The Holevo center bounds V(p), since (7) gives V(p)<=2(1+theta)C,
but Delta(p) is the *unexplained* part after the best common measurement.
A trace bound on Q_p gives no vanishing bound on Delta(p). For example,
the signed Pauli experiment with d=2^q and states

  rho_(P,s)=(I+s aP)/d,
  P nonidentity Hermitian Paulis, s in {+1,-1},

has uniform prior covariance Q_p=a^2 I_(L2_0)/(d^2-1): its trace is
a^2 while its rank is d^2-1. Its exact capacity is c(a)<=ln 2. Thus the
radius controls total score mass but cannot be silently replaced by a
finite-dimensional rank bound. This note does not truncate Q_p.

4. THE ACTUAL BROADCASTER SCORE GATE

After the N93 compatible Petz reduction, the map Phi is a trace-preserving
UCP likelihood channel whose L2(tau) superoperator is positive-semidefinite
and self-adjoint, with an actual
two-output compatible extension. If the corrected family residual is

  r=sup_x ||Phi(H_x)-H_x||_(1,tau),

then for every score

  0<=E_Phi(H):=<H,(I-Phi)H>_tau
       <= ||H||_infinity ||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau) <= theta r.  (9)

The original b_2 error yields such an r through the supplied fixed-sandwich
Petz bound gamma_theta(b)=sqrt(8 theta b+6 theta^2 sqrt(2b)); for an
unsandwiched bounded-capacity family the preceding smoothing/dilution adds
fixed-accuracy costs, with the source-recorded error transfer. No
covariance is used. A universal c=2 inequality

  I-Psi_M <= 2(I-Phi) on L2(tau),                        (10)

for one common canonical EB map Psi_M would combine (8)-(9) to give
q(E)<=2 theta r. The equivalent all-frame score test is

  Delta(p) <= 2 sum_x p_x E_Phi(H_x)                    (11)

for every finite score family and prior. More generally, for the target it
would suffice to prove a dimension-free modulus

  Delta(p) <= F_(C,theta)(sum_x p_x E_Phi(H_x)),
  F_(C,theta)(u)->0 as u->0,                             (12)

on the admitted families, followed by the N93 reduction. This is the
direct common-POVM/reconstruction inequality sought here. It places the
single measurement outside the whole prior/frame. Choosing a different
eigenbasis for each H_x is not valid.

There is an exact Choi formulation of the stronger (11). For
Q=sum_i |A_i><A_i| on centered Hermitian L2, let h_1(Q) be the maximum
captured score over pure-state 1-designs sum_y w_y P_y=I/d. If Omega_RAB
is the normalized Choi state of a Heisenberg joint extension with both
marginals Phi, then

  W_Q=sum_i A_(i,R)^T tensor (A_(i,A) tensor I_B+I_A tensor A_(i,B)),
  Tr(Omega W_Q)=2 sum_i tau[A_i Phi(A_i)].

Compact-convex separation shows (10) is equivalent to

  2 sum_i tau[A_i Phi(A_i)]-Tr_(L2_0)Q <= h_1(Q)          (13)

for all Q>=0. Equivalently, TrQ-h_1(Q), the minimum joint score variance
over one complete pure-state 1-design, is at most twice the broadcaster's
Dirichlet energy. This retains the actual joint marginal constraints and
the common first-moment constraint of the POVM. Dropping either constraint
and bounding only the largest eigenvalue of W_Q is not sufficient. A
rank-one Q test is also uninformative: its h_1 equals TrQ by an eigenbasis
1-design, reducing (13) to L2 contraction.

5. NEW CONSEQUENCE: SMALL SCORE ENERGY FORCES PAIRWISE HS COMMUTATION

The actual extension does yield a direct, dimension-free pairwise score
bound. This is useful evidence for (12), though it does not prove it.
Assume the tracial interface above and let H,K be centered Hermitian
scores. Symmetrize J under the output swap; this preserves both marginals
Phi, so it adds no premise. Take a minimal Stinespring isometry V for this
symmetric joint UCP map J, P=VV*, and representation pi. By uniqueness of
minimal Stinespring dilations, the swap automorphism is implemented by a
unitary U with UV=V and U pi(A tensor B) U*=pi(B tensor A). Put

  X_H=(I-P)pi(H tensor I)V,
  Y_K=(I-P)pi(I tensor K)V.

The two represented output algebras commute. Direct expansion gives

  X_H^*X_H=Phi(H^2)-Phi(H)^2,
  [Phi(H),Phi(K)]=-X_H^*Y_K+Y_K^*X_H
                 =-X_H^*Y_K+X_K^*Y_H.                  (14)

The first commutator formula uses the commuting represented operators
pi(H tensor I) and pi(I tensor K). The second equality uses the swap
unitary U; it is what permits a symmetric estimate in H and K. Without
swap covariance of the dilation, retain only the first formula.

Trace preservation, L2 self-adjointness, and positive-semidefiniteness as
an L2 operator put the eigenvalues of Phi in [0,1]. (Order positivity of a
CP map alone would not imply this spectral statement.) Consequently

  ||H-Phi(H)||_(2,tau)^2 <= E_Phi(H),
  tau(X_H^*X_H)=||H||_2^2-||Phi(H)||_2^2 <=2E_Phi(H).

Using both terms in the swap-symmetric formula (14),
||Y_K||_infinity<=||K||_infinity, ||Y_H||_infinity<=||H||_infinity,
and Schatten ideal bounds gives

  ||[H,K]||_(2,tau)
   <= (2+sqrt(2)) [ ||K||_infinity sqrt(E_Phi(H))
                    +||H||_infinity sqrt(E_Phi(K)) ].      (15)

For a family with ||H_x||_infinity<=theta, this implies

  sup_(x,z)||[H_x,H_z]||_(2,tau)
       <= 2(2+sqrt(2)) theta sqrt(theta r),                 (16)

and for any prior p,

  E_(x,z~p)||[H_x,H_z]||_(2,tau)^2
       <=4(2+sqrt(2))^2 theta^2 sum_x p_x E_Phi(H_x).

Thus small broadcaster energy controls a collective average of pairwise
score commutators without a factor equal to the family size. This uses the
actual compatible extension, not capacity alone. What it does not provide
is a single finite-dimensional POVM with uniformly small posterior
variance for an arbitrarily large family.

Why this geometric route stops. In the affine-plane Clifford control,
the centered scores have bounded capacity, and all pairwise normalized
Hilbert-Schmidt commutators tend to zero, yet their common-POVM deficiency
and EB error stay bounded below. Its actual pair-broadcast error is also
bounded below, so it is not a counterexample to (12); it shows that (16)
without the full score/compatibility inequality cannot finish. Glebsky's
normalized-Hilbert-Schmidt stability theorem supplies a commuting
approximation for a fixed number k of Hermitian matrices with a modulus
depending on k; it does not furnish the required modulus for a growing
label family. The 2026 Bansil-Kachkovskiy result supplies a sharp explicit
pair estimate, but only for two observables. Neither result removes the
family dependence here.

6. CONTROLS AND FAILURE BOUNDARIES

N46 (uniform trace compactness). Uniform trace-total-boundedness or a
uniform finite-radius net gives broadcasting-to-EB reconstruction without
a capacity premise. Its net-size/covering gate is a distinct complexity
assumption; a Holevo bound alone does not supply it. The affine-plane
Clifford family has growing fixed-radius covering number, so it does not
contradict N46.

N61 (copy ladder). The full-domain dyadic-copy broadcaster has b_2->0
and e_EB->1, but its Holevo capacity diverges at least
(2^(L-1)/L)ln L. It falsifies a capacity-free b_2->EB modulus, not (T).

N67 (receiver growth). The ladder's K-receiver error follows
min(log_2(K)/L,1) to the stated asymptotic error. This controls the tree
term phi(K)b in (6) and its cost; it does not remove the information term.

N69 (capacity boundaries). The finite-label bound (6), including the
posterior-effective-support refinement, is dimension-free for fixed
effective support. The bounded-capacity pure-ladder theorem does
classicalize its specified pure ladders. The signed Pauli family has
bounded capacity and constant EB error, but also b_2 bounded below. These
are controls on the precise missing premise, not counterexamples to (T).

N93 (global gate). The affine-plane Clifford family has C=c(a),
uniform-prior accessible information at most c(a)/r->0, and
e_EB=a(1-1/r)/2->a/2, while its exact compatible two-output witness gives
b_2>=a(1-1/sqrt(2))/2. The general bounded-Holevo plus vanishing-b_2
gate therefore remains open. N93's fixed-sandwich smoothing reduces the
unrestricted problem to the tracial score form (8)-(12); it does not prove
the common-POVM inequality.

The affine-plane family also shows why each notion must stay separate:

* Holevo information chi is the quantum relative-entropy information
  I(X:Q); it bounds all measurement information from above.
* Accessible information is the supremum of classical I(X:Y) over POVMs.
  It may vanish while chi stays fixed and the family remains hard for one
  common EB decoder.
* Posterior trace distance S_h is the reconstruction loss after an actual
  measurement history; it is not itself a mutual information.
* Worst-member e_EB is obtained from prior-average decoders only after a
  finite-dimensional minimax step. That step gives a common channel but
  not a uniform outcome count or an efficient way to find it.

7. COSTS, FALSIFIER, AND UNKNOWN

Supplied in the target: a compact state family, a full-domain pair
broadcaster or a certified upper bound b_2, and a capacity bound. Derived,
not assumed: an information-radius center. The N93 proof then uses a
compatible smoothing, normalization, dilution, and finite flagged
centralization. Its center/smoother precision and flags have potentially
large costs; no efficient black-box acquisition procedure is supplied.
The fixed-sandwich theorem pays no label or rank factor, but its general
common-POVM inequality is precisely what is missing. A finite minimax
POVM exists with dimension-dependent support; this is not a uniform
implementation result.

A decisive positive theorem is a dimension-free F_(C,theta) in (12), or
the stronger universal c=2 inequality (13), for every actual compatible
positive tracial selfadjoint UCP marginal. The c=2 statement is known only
for scoped instrument, low-dimensional, or exactly covariant classes; no
covariance is supplied in (T). The pairwise commutator bound (15) is a
proved consequence but does not imply (12) by itself.

A decisive counterexample to (T) must give E_n and one actual full-domain
B_n with C(E_n)<=C, b_2(E_n)->0, and e_EB(E_n)>=epsilon_0>0. In the
post-N93 tracial form it suffices to find least-favourable priors p_n with
the common-measurement deficit Delta_n(p_n)>=delta_0 while the corrected
residual r_n->0. A finite violation of c=2 alone, a growing ratio
Delta/E, pairwise near commutation, or low accessible information is not
enough. The known N61 and N93 controls each fail one required limit. No
qualifying sequence is established here. Verdict: UNKNOWN / OPEN.
