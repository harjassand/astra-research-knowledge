## Primary literature opened

1. Danial Senejohnny, Pietro Tesi, Claudio De Persis, “Self-triggered Coordination over a Shared Network under Denial-of-Service,” arXiv:1504.06810 (2015), https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06810. Original TeX saved at `sources/Senejohnny_Tesi_DePersis_2015.tex`. Reconstructed equations: sampled control and timer in Section II-A; shared-medium DoS model and duration/frequency assumptions in Section II-B; modified protocol and \(\Delta_*\) in Section III-A; PoC proposition (equations 19–20) and Theorem 2; proof of Proposition 2 in Section III-B. It assumes a shared medium where DoS prevents any node from sending or receiving. Its theorem is not used as a result for independent edge outages.

2. Heath J. LeBlanc, Haotian Zhang, Xenofon Koutsoukos, Shreyas Sundaram, “Resilient Asymptotic Consensus in Robust Networks,” IEEE JSAC 31(4), 2013, https://doi.org/10.1109/JSAC.2013.130413. The paper gives W-MSR resilience conditions under malicious-node models. Used only as the established baseline family for the next adversarial-node gate, not as a premise of the four-node finite witness.
