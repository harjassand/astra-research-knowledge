# Primary sources checked

1. Dinan, J. C. et al. “Protein Surface Site Determines the Evolutionary Accessibility of Allosteric Regulation.” bioRxiv, posted 3 July 2026. [DOI: 10.64898/2026.07.02.735819](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.02.735819). **Preprint, not peer reviewed** as of 8 October 2026. Supports the matched DL116/DL121 comparison, DMS counts, sector/bridge interpretation, thermal stability observation, and DHFR ProDomino analysis.
2. Southern, N. T. et al. “Phage-assisted evolution of allosteric protein switches.” *Nature Communications* 17, 3498 (2026). [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-71717-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71717-0). Supports POGO-PANCE/RAMPhaGE, the point/indel edit operators, selection results, LdelK phenotype and background dependence. Code/data archive: [Zenodo 15650047](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15650047).
3. Wolf, B. et al. “Rational engineering of allosteric protein switches by in silico prediction of domain insertion sites.” *Nature Methods* 22, 1698–1706 (2025). [DOI: 10.1038/s41592-025-02741-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-025-02741-z). Supports ProDomino’s sequence-based insertion-tolerance design prior. Code: [Niopek-Lab/ProDomino](https://github.com/Niopek-Lab/ProDomino).
4. Reynolds, K. A., McLaughlin, R. N. & Ranganathan, R. “Hot spots for allosteric regulation on protein surfaces.” *Cell* 147, 1564–1575 (2011). [DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2011.10.024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2011.10.024). Prior art for sector-connected allosteric surface hotspots.
5. Cambray, G. & Mazel, D. “Synonymous Genes Explore Different Evolutionary Landscapes.” *PLoS Genetics* 4(11), e1000256 (2008). [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pgen.1000256](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1000256). Prior art for synonymous coding-sequence recoding to alter accessible amino-acid neighborhoods.
6. Rozhoňová, H. et al. “Robust genetic codes enhance protein evolvability.” *PLOS Biology* 22(5), e3002594 (2024). [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pbio.3002594](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3002594). Prior art for evaluating empirical sequence-function landscapes under hundreds of thousands of rewired genetic codes.
