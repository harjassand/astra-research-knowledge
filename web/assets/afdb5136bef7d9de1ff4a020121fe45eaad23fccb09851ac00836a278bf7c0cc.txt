UNIFORM TRACE RATE FOR ARBITRARILY STRONG FINITE-RANGE REPULSION
8 October 2026. Root derivation; internal candidate, independent audits ongoing.

1. EXACT CONTRACT
Let L>=2 and use an open chain of L-1 spin-half sites. Put n_i=(I-Z_i)/2,
M=sum_i n_i, and
 K=-J sum_{i=1}^{L-2}(X_i X_{i+1}+Y_i Y_{i+1})+4J M,
 H0=K-lambda M/L², beta=tau L²,
where J,tau>0 and |lambda|<=B are fixed public bounds. Fix an integer R>=1
and let P project onto configurations with no occupied pair at separation
1,...,R. Add ANY positive semidefinite operator W with WP=0. It need not be
diagonal, number preserving, local, or bounded uniformly in L. This includes
 W=sum_{r=1}^R sum_{i:i+r<L} V_{i,r,L} n_i n_{i+r}, V_{i,r,L}>=0,
with arbitrary inhomogeneous, L-dependent, unbounded coefficients, and sums
of positive terms supported on windows of diameter at most R which annihilate
the local zero- and one-excitation spaces. Positivity makes PWP=0 equivalent
to WP=0. Denote normalized Gibbs states by rho0 and rhoW.

The candidate theorem is an explicit uniform bound
 ||rhoW-rho0||_1 <=2 sqrt(a_L)+sqrt(2 G_L)=O_{J,tau,B,R}(L^-1/2),
with a_L,G_L below, whenever a_L<=1/2. No integrability of H0+W is assumed.
The reference is the finite-chain FREE grand-canonical state, not a separately
acquired continuum spectrum. Every coefficient magnitude is absent from the
bound. The limit of infinite penalties is covered by finite-dimensional Gibbs
convergence on the allowed subspace, with the same bound. This is a whole-
state trace theorem, not just a partition-function or fixed-sector limit.
No claim about optimal quantum memory, microscopic energy accuracy or efficient
preparation of an arbitrary interacting Gibbs state is implicit.

2. FREE FERMIONS AND FORBIDDEN-PAIR MASS
The standard open-chain Jordan-Wigner/sine diagonalization gives
 epsilon_k=4J(1-cos(pi k/L)), phi_k(i)=sqrt(2/L) sin(pi k i/L), 1<=k<L.
Thus 0<=K<=8J M, [K,rho0]=[M,rho0]=0, and the free occupation process is a
fermion determinantal process with correlation kernel
 C_ij=sum_k f_k phi_k(i)phi_k(j),
 f_k=1/[1+exp(tau(L²epsilon_k-lambda))].
These identities follow directly by taking the product Bernoulli Gibbs state
in the diagonal fermion modes and applying Wick's determinant identity.
Since 8Jk²<=L²epsilon_k<=2Jpi²k²,
 f_k<=exp(tau B-8tau J k²).
Define finite public constants
 c0=exp(tau B) sum_{k>=1} exp(-8tau J k²),
 c2=exp(tau B) sum_{k>=1} k² exp(-8tau J k²),
 A=8pi² c0 c2 sum_{r=1}^R r², a_L=A/L³.
Gaussian tail bounds make these constants computable by finite sums and explicit
remainders. In particular E0 M<=c0 and E0(L²K)<=2Jpi²c2.

Let Q be the diagonal projector onto configurations containing ANY occupied
pair at separation 1,...,R; P=I-Q. Pointwise Q<=sum_{i,r}n_i n_{i+r}.
For j=i+r, the determinantal two-point expansion is
 E0(n_i n_j)=sum_{k<l} f_k f_l [phi_k(i)phi_l(j)-phi_l(i)phi_k(j)]².
The sine difference bound gives the bracket squared at most
 4pi² r²(k+l)²/L⁴.
Also sum_{k<l} f_k f_l(k+l)²<=2c0c2. Summing at most L positions for each r,
 delta:=Tr(rho0 Q)<=a_L.

The weighted bound is just as important:
 Tr(rho0 M Q)<=(2+c0)a_L.
Indeed E0(M n_i n_j)=2E0(n_i n_j)+sum_{z!=i,j}E0(n_i n_j n_z).
Positive-semidefinite determinant Schur complementation gives
 det C_{i,j,z}<=det C_{i,j} C_zz, including singular blocks by continuity.
Sum over z and then all forbidden pairs. This is a deterministic matrix
inequality; no independent-particle position approximation is used.

3. COHERENT PROJECTION AND KINETIC COST
Set p=1-delta and sigma=P rho0 P/p. This is a binary allowed-space projection;
measuring every occupation coordinate instead would not produce this state.
W sigma=0 by the common kernel condition. Moreover
 ||sigma-rho0||_1<=2 sqrt(delta)
by the gentle/purification fidelity bound for one successful projector.

Let qM=Tr(rho0 M Q). The kinetic projection cost satisfies
 Tr(rho0 P K P)<=Tr(rho0 K)+8J qM.
To prove it, expand PKP=K-QK-KQ+QKQ. As [rho0,K]=0 and K>=0,
Tr(rho0 QK)=Tr(Q K rho0)>=0, so the two subtracted terms can be dropped.
The operator bound K<=8JM and [Q,M]=0 give QKQ<=8J M Q. This is an
actual quadratic-form bound, not an assertion that exp(-beta H) is operator
monotone in H. Normalizing and using the free expectations,
 L²[Tr(sigma K)-Tr(rho0 K)]
 <=[delta(2Jpi²c2)+8J L² qM]/p.
Also, exactly,
 Tr(sigma M)-Tr(rho0 M)=[delta E0 M-qM]/p,
so its absolute value is at most [delta c0+qM]/p.

4. ENTROPY COST AND GIBBS VARIATIONAL BOUND
The two-block pinching P rho0 P+Q rho0 Q cannot decrease von Neumann entropy.
Writing omega=Q rho0 Q/delta (omit when delta=0) gives
 S(rho0)<=h2(delta)+p S(sigma)+delta S(omega).
As log dim=(L-1)log 2 and S(rho0)>=0,
 S(rho0)-S(sigma)<=[h2(delta)+delta(L-1)log2]/p.
Here h2 is the binary entropy in natural units. This step uses the exact full
finite-chain dimension, whose logarithm is only linear in L.

For the finite coefficient Gibbs state,
 D(sigma||rhoW)=-S(sigma)+beta Tr(sigma H0)+log ZW,
since sigma W=0. Variational free energy or eigenvalue min-max gives ZW<=Z0
when W>=0. (It does NOT imply an operator order of the heat matrices.) Using
0=-S(rho0)+beta Tr(rho0 H0)+log Z0 therefore bounds the relative entropy by
the entropy cost plus the energy cost from Section 3.

For a_L<=1/2, h2(delta)<=h2(a_L), p>=1-a_L, qM<=(2+c0)a_L. Thus
 D(sigma||rhoW)<=G_L,
 G_L={h2(a_L)+a_L(L-1)log2
       +tau a_L[2Jpi²c2+8J L²(2+c0)+B c0+B(2+c0)]}/(1-a_L).
Every term is explicit; G_L=O(1/L). Quantum Pinsker and the triangle inequality
give the claimed bound. For the finite omitted values of L, use trace norm<=2.
The constants can be loose but do not depend on any V_{i,r,L}. R must remain
fixed, or its growth must be charged through sum r² in A.

5. LIMIT AND COPIES
For fixed L, increasing any subset of penalties to infinity gives the Gibbs
state of the corresponding compressed H0 on the allowed subspace, with zero
outside. The same sigma is supported there and the displayed bounds pass by
finite-dimensional convergence. Thus arbitrarily strong fixed-range repulsion
has a uniformly controlled free-chain reconstruction at critical beta=tau L².
This can transport an existing public free Gibbs archive/decoder with an added
trace error given above. Section 6 gives one explicit classical channel.
For a joint n-copy comparison, telescope to at most n times this trace bound.
Consequently n=o(sqrt L) is sufficient at fixed parameters for vanishing error;
no statement with arbitrary n/L scaling is made.

6. ONE FIXED ENTANGLEMENT-BREAKING RECONSTRUCTION CHANNEL
Let P_m be the projector onto total particle number m and let
 sigma^0_{L,m}=P_m exp(-tau L²K)P_m / Tr(P_m exp(-tau L²K)).
The measure-prepare channel
 Phi_L(X)=sum_{m=0}^{L-1} Tr(P_m X) sigma^0_{L,m}
is CPTP, entanglement breaking, and independent of lambda and W. The chemical
term is scalar within each particle sector, so Phi_L(rho0(lambda))=rho0(lambda).
Contractivity therefore gives, with eta_L=min(2,2sqrt(a_L)+sqrt(2G_L)),
 ||Phi_L(rhoW(lambda))-rhoW(lambda)||_1 <= min(2,2eta_L).
This is an approximate reverse channel from the classical total-particle-number
experiment, uniformly over the entire perturbation family. It does NOT assert
exact particle-number sufficiency for rhoW, even when W preserves M. It remains
valid when [W,M] is nonzero. Phi_L may depend on public J,tau,L; they are not
simultaneously unknown nuisance parameters in this claim.

A finite alphabet independent of L at fixed accuracy is available. For an
integer cutoff m_*, keep outcomes 0,...,m_* and combine larger outcomes into
one symbol decoded as the vacuum. Define t_*=Pr_{rho0}(M>m_*). The free mode
occupations are independent Bernoulli variables and sum f_k<=c0, so for any
z>1,
 t_* <= z^(-m_*-1) exp((z-1)c0).
The truncated channel Phi_{L,m_*} has free-reference error at most 2t_*, and
 ||Phi_{L,m_*}(rhoW)-rhoW||_1 <=min(2,2eta_L+2t_*).
This uses the same triangle inequality and does not assume a tail bound for
the interacting particle measurement. It stores at most m_*+2 classical labels
per copy and no quantum register. Preparing each public free canonical state
is still a charged decoder operation, not an acquisition or runtime oracle.
A direct, deliberately nonoptimized implementation enumerates all subsets of
the L-1 sine modes of size m, samples weights exp(-tau L² sum epsilon_k), and
prepares the corresponding Slater state with a fermionic linear transformation.
Its classical enumeration costs O(L^{m_*}) at fixed m_*; numerical probability,
gate-synthesis, and output preparation errors must be added to the trace budget.
This establishes a channel/resource interface, not a practically optimized
uniform-time implementation. For n independently encoded copies, all single-
copy errors telescope with factor n, and the classical labels cost at most
n log2(m_*+2) bits before any additional lossless compression.

7. BOUNDARIES
The proof depends on positivity, the fixed-range allowed-space kernel condition,
open-chain hard-core free fermions and this critical scaling. It excludes
indefinite perturbations, generic positive operators charging the allowed space,
long-range exclusions with uncharged range, and higher spatial dimension.
The output is the whole mixed state; energy accuracy need not follow
from small trace norm when beta or microscopic operator norms grow.
