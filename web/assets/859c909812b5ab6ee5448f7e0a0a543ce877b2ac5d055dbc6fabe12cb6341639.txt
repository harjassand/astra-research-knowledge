SUPPLIED INTERFACE AND WHAT IT WOULD CHANGE

Candidate (as supplied by the coordinator): for a fixed bipartite state rho_AB with rho_B=sigma_B, an actual local CPTP map C:B->B1B2 has both output marginals within joint half-trace distance b of rho_AB. The candidate says that one B-local EB channel Lambda, selected with sigma_B and C, gives a joint-state half-trace approximation to rho_AB bounded by 6 sqrt((I(A:B)_rho+1)/ln(1/b)); an infinite-dimensional normal version is also supplied. The source audit does not verify that assertion. Its metric is on this fixed joint state, not diamond distance over all input states. “All references” does not turn it into a diamond-norm claim.

If valid, the immediate consequence is a dimension-free robust local-broadcasting-to-separability statement for a fixed state: an EB output is separable across A:B, so rho_AB lies close to Sep(A:B). This is consequential for the entanglement content of correlations that a physical local channel can almost duplicate. It does not, by itself, certify an objective record or a spectrum-broadcast state.

THE CLOSEST EXACT OBJECTIVITY STRUCTURE

Korbicz, Horodecki, and Horodecki, arXiv:1305.3247v2 (2013), define objectivity through many observers independently learning the same pointer-state spectrum without disturbing the state. Their derivation assumes full decoherence to a pointer basis, independent local projective measurements, Bohr non-disturbance of the full joint state, and agreement of observer outcomes. Under those assumptions, Eq. (9), p. 4, has the form

  rho_S:E = sum_i p_i |i><i|_S tensor rho_i^{E1} tensor ... tensor rho_i^{Ef},

with mutually orthogonal supports for the conditional states in every observed fragment. Their spectrum-broadcast definition, Eq. (10), p. 4, makes that local distinguishability explicit; Eq. (11), pp. 4-5, states the resulting equivalence to objective existence under the paper’s assumptions. The candidate has an actual two-output channel and two near-preserved marginals, but supplies neither a pointer measurement nor non-disturbing, agreeing outcomes, per-fragment orthogonality, or conditional product structure. A separable approximation cannot substitute for those hypotheses.

Le and Olaya-Castro, PRL 122, 010403 (2019), arXiv:1803.08936v2, give a state-structure characterization. Their strong-quantum-Darwinism condition, Eq. (5), p. 2, requires I(S:F)=I_acc(S:F)=chi_{Pi:F}=H(S), and for objectivity it must hold for multiple disjoint fragments. Their SBS definition, Eq. (8), p. 3, requires a pointer-diagonal mixture with perfectly distinguishable conditional states in each fragment; strong independence is I(E_j:E_k|S)=0, Eq. (9). Theorem, p. 4, says SBS iff strong quantum Darwinism plus strong independence; Corollaries 1-2 identify the bipartite SBS/strong-QD objectivity endpoint. The candidate bounds total mutual information and state reconstruction, but not accessible or Holevo information in any pointer basis, not the discord part, and not the multi-fragment conditions.

The separability-versus-record gap is strict. For example,

  rho_AB = (|0><0|_A tensor |0><0|_B + |1><1|_A tensor |+><+|_B)/2

is separable, but its conditional B states do not commute and cannot be perfectly distinguished by one B measurement. It is therefore not quantum-classical on B and cannot have the local orthogonal-record structure required by SBS. It is itself the output of an EB map that measures an orthogonal input label and prepares |0> or |+>. Thus EB reconstruction does not imply pointer-basis classicality, outcome agreement, or spectrum broadcasting.

THE NEAREST QUANTITATIVE SBS RESULT AND ITS MODEL GAP

Mironowicz, Korbicz, and Horodecki, PRL 118, 150501 (2017), arXiv:1607.02478v1, Proposition 1, Eq. (5), p. 2, construct an SBS state for a particular central-system decoherence model and bound its half-trace distance from the actual system-observed-environment state by a collective decoherence factor plus local discrimination errors. Corollary 1, Eq. (7), replaces those errors with weighted pairwise fidelities of conditional fragment states. The model starts from a product system-environment state and a measurement-limit interaction with a fixed pointer observable; the error is controlled by off-diagonal decoherence and fragment-state distinguishability. It is not a theorem from an arbitrary B-local two-output broadcasting channel plus I(A:B) alone. The candidate does not provide the decoherence and orthogonalization terms in that bound.

VERDICT AND DECISIVE MISSING BRIDGE

The genuinely consequential result that the supplied theorem could support is robust no-local-broadcasting-to-separability under a bounded state mutual information. Its relevance to objective records is that it would rule out substantial entanglement in correlations that a local process nearly duplicates, without a dimension cost. It does not solve the broader quantum-objectivity question: Sep(A:B) is weaker than quantum-classical structure, and two preserved marginals are weaker than independently readable, agreeing, non-disturbing records across many fragments.

The decisive missing bridge for an objectivity claim is a quantitative theorem converting the same physical hypotheses into a nearby full broadcast-structure state: a common pointer variable, high accessible/Holevo information in each record, locally distinguishable conditional states, agreement, and the needed conditional-independence/product structure. None of the checked primary statements supplies this bridge from the candidate’s EB/separability conclusion.
