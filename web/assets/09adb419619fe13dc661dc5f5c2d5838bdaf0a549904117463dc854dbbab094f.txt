CYCLE 5 — DIRECT RELATIVE MPO CONSUMER AND N125 COMPARATOR
Date: 8 October 2026
Decision: the positive-sector Loewner theorem and its rational bounded-bond
MPO postselection sampler are internally proved. A matched baseline now shows
that the explicit Gaussian-sector target's relative rational weights can be
constructed directly by certified rational exponentiation. This reproduces
the same consumer, with no Cycle-3/N125 premise or N>=40 ln(1/epsilon)
threshold, and gives a stronger raw relative error. Therefore no N125-specific
algorithmic gain is claimed. N125 remains a possible route to a product-state
separability certificate if that property is independently required by the
legal interface. The sampler does not classically simulate arbitrary quantum
circuits or verify the MPO's trace-nonincreasing promise.

1. Interface audited

The Chen–Liu primary is arXiv:2610.06724v1, posted 5 October 2026. Theorem 1.6
uses two nonnegative set-weight evaluation oracles whose multiaffine generating
polynomials are log-concave on the positive orthant, positive-support seed
sets, supplied coefficient-range upper bounds, and a specified
alpha in {0,1,2}^U. It returns a relative FPRAS for one coefficient
[x^alpha](g_mu g_nu), in ~O(m^11(m+log(R_mu R_nu))^2/eps^2) arithmetic and
oracle calls, with the paper's median amplification and rational-bit remark.
It is not a general-purpose oracle for tensor-network contractions, arbitrary
nonnegative weights, arbitrary quantum effects, or event-conditioned states.
The primary itself already gives mixed-discriminant/DPP, six-vertex,
bipartite Quantum Max-Cut/EPR, and further Appendix C applications. The
selective determinant Z_k encodings and their two exact Hessian failures are
already banked in Cycle 4; they are not repeated here.

This packet does not invoke Chen–Liu. Its main positive scalar-kernel input
is the explicit formula f_k=exp(-(k-N/2)^2/N), approximated directly by the
certified rational construction in §2. Thus the operator consumer below has
no Cycle-3 or N125 premise. The inherited N125 source card remains relevant
only as a possible product-state decomposition route; its source evidence is
unreviewed, and the infinite positive Newton series has a strict
survival-probability defect for every a>0. N117/N118/N123 already
source-report broader full-Schur or Dicke-diagonal positive-product
compilers; they remain unvalidated source claims and block an honest novelty
claim for another generic product/MPS consumer. N118's acquired PI-MPO
comparator already rules out “first product-measurement simulator” language.

2. Exact positive-sector composition lemma

Let f_k>0 be target sector weights for k=0,...,N, and suppose supplied
positive rationals g_k satisfy

    (1-delta) f_k <= g_k <= (1+delta) f_k,  0<delta<1.

For any finite outcome set T and any supplied nonnegative rational table
h(k,t), with at least one positive entry, define

    P(k,t) = f_k h(k,t) / Z,  Z=sum_{k,t} f_k h(k,t),
    Q(k,t) = g_k h(k,t) / Z', Z'=sum_{k,t} g_k h(k,t).

Writing r_k=g_k/f_k and R=Z'/Z=E_P[r_k], we have
R in [1-delta,1+delta] and Q(k,t)/P(k,t)=r_k/R on the common support.
Thus

    TV(P,Q) <= delta/(1-delta).

The bound is independent of Z, even when the positive filter has exponentially
small mass. All g_k h(k,t) and Z' are exact rationals. For a supplied
(N+1)-by-|T| rational table h, evaluating Q costs O((N+1)|T|) rational
products and additions after constructing the weights; the bit cost is
polynomial in N, the weight bits, the table bit length and output precision.
A single CDF grid over the
(N+1)|T| categories with

    b=ceil(log_2(16 (N+1)|T| / gamma))

fair bits adds at most gamma/8 TV using the conservative 2K/2^b bound. No
rejection loop and no inverse-Z runtime appear. This applies to a sector-only
filter, a rational classical response channel h(t|k), or any positive
likelihood table.

This sector-table lemma alone is not a high-ceiling consumer. Here
f_k=exp(-(k-N/2)^2/N) is explicit, and §2 constructs rational relative
approximants directly. The N125 mixture therefore removes no weight
representation or acquisition barrier for this target. The operator lift
below is a useful generic consequence of relative sector weights, but it is
not an N125-specific capability or a novelty claim.

Matched direct construction for the explicit Gaussian weights
---------------------------------------------------------------
This removes the N125 dependency from the operator consumer. Fix any rational
0<delta<1 and write

    u_k=(k-N/2)^2/N=(2k-N)^2/(4N),       0<=u_k<=N/4.

Choose the smallest power of two r_k with r_k>=1+u_k; then
r_k<2(1+u_k)=O(N), and v_k=u_k/r_k lies in [0,1). Let m_k be the smallest
odd integer such that 2^m_k>=6r_k/delta, and define the rational Taylor
partial sum

    t_k=sum_(j=0)^m_k (-v_k)^j/j!,       g_k=t_k^r_k.

For v in [0,1), the alternating-series theorem gives
0<T_m(v)<=exp(-v)<=T_(m+1)(v) when m is odd. The gap is the next term and
satisfies

    T_(m+1)(v)-T_m(v)
      = v^(m+1)/(m+1)! <= 2^(-m),

because (m+1)!>=2^m. Also exp(-v)>1/3. Thus
T_m(v)/T_(m+1)(v)>=1-3*2^(-m)>=1-delta/(2r). Raising to r and applying
Bernoulli's inequality gives

    1-delta <= 1-delta/2 <= (1-delta/(2r))^r
              <= g_k/exp(-u_k) <= 1.                         (12)

All quantities emitted are positive rationals. The power r is O(N); m is
O(log(N/delta)). The rational v has O(log N) bits. A common denominator for
T_(m+1) has O(m(log N+log(m+1))) bits, so g_k has
O(N m(log N+log(m+1))) numerator and denominator bits. Horner evaluation and
repeated squaring therefore construct all N+1 values in deterministic bit
time polynomial in N and the encoded delta (in particular in
N, log(1/delta), and its input bit length). This has no N lower-threshold.
The exact checker `verify_direct_relative.py` verifies positivity and the
rational inequalities between adjacent Taylor brackets used above on finite
N fixtures; it does not evaluate exp. The real sandwich follows from the
analytic argument.

Normalize these weights by G_tilde=sum_k g_k and put
sigma_tilde=sum_k (g_k/G_tilde)|D_k><D_k|. Using the product of the N+1
weight denominators as a common denominator gives a conservative normalized
weight bit height O(N^2 m_*(log N+log(m_*+1))), where
m_*=max_k m_k=O(1+log(N/delta)); division by binom(N,k) adds at most N bits to
an MPO boundary entry. Thus B_sigma=O(N^2 m_*(log N+log(m_*+1))+N) is an
explicit polynomial upper bound. Its entries are rational and its
ket/bra count-pair MPO has bond (N+1)^2, exactly as below. No separability
claim is needed for its classical MPO contraction. Thus direct exponentiation
is a stronger matched algorithmic baseline for this particular simulator:
it constructs only N+1 scalar weights and supports the same full-CP
postselection proof, without N125 atom isolation/quadrature or a theorem
dependency. It may lose if the legal task requires an actual positive
product-state ensemble or a separability certificate; this sampler's
mathematical interface does not. No practical runtime superiority is claimed
without a matched benchmark.

3. Generic Loewner lift and exact rational MPO consumer

Let

    rho = sum_(k=0)^N p_k |D_k><D_k|,  p_k=f_k/F_N,
    sigma = sum_(k=0)^N q_k |D_k><D_k|, q_k=g_k/G_N,

where |D_k> is the normalized k-excitation Dicke vector, F_N=sum f_k,
G_N=sum g_k. If supplied positive g_k satisfy

    a <= g_k/f_k <= b,         0<a<=b,

then G_N/F_N is a p-weighted average in [a,b]. Therefore
q_k/p_k=(g_k/f_k)/(G_N/F_N) lies in [a/b,b/a]. With L=b/a, the two
density operators are diagonal in the same Dicke basis, so

    L^-1 rho <= sigma <= L rho.                              (13)

Both operators are zero on Sym^N(C^2)^perp. Thus (13) is a Loewner
inequality on the full 2^N-dimensional Hilbert space, not just a promise about
inputs inside the symmetric subspace.

Here D(X,Y)=||X-Y||_1/2. For any completely positive
trace-nonincreasing map Phi (one Kraus map K(.)K* is enough), order
preservation gives L^-1 A<=B<=L A, where
A=Phi(rho), B=Phi(sigma). If P=Tr A>0 and Q=Tr B, then
L^-1<=Q/P<=L. Consequently the conditioned states tau=A/P and
upsilon=B/Q obey

    L^-2 tau <= upsilon <= L^2 tau.

For two trace-one positive operators under this sandwich,
D(tau,upsilon)<=L^2-1: on the positive spectral subspace of
upsilon-tau, the upper Loewner bound gives
Tr(upsilon-tau)_+ <= (L^2-1)Tr(tau) = L^2-1. Therefore

    D(Phi(rho)/P, Phi(sigma)/Q)
       <= L^2-1,                                               (14)

independently of P. Also P=0 iff Q=0, since (13) implies equality of
the states' supports after any completely positive map. For the N125-style
two-sided raw guarantee, a=1-delta,b=1+delta and (14) is
4 delta/(1-delta)^2. For the direct lower Taylor weights from §2,
a=1-delta,b=1, so (14) improves to

    (1-delta)^(-2)-1 = (2 delta-delta^2)/(1-delta)^2.        (15)

This all-CP-map stability theorem is the useful mechanism. It is a generic
consequence of relative sector weights; it is not specific to N125.

For rational target conditional error eps in (0,1/2], use the direct
construction with delta=eps/16. It applies for every N, has no finite-mixture threshold,
and (15) is at most 128 eps/961 <0.134 eps. The N125 mixture can also
instantiate (13) if an independently required product-state ensemble is
available, but it has no algorithmic advantage for the current classical
sampler.

Here is the end-to-end consumer using the direct weights. Put
\(\widetilde G=\sum_k g_k\), \(\widetilde q_k=g_k/\widetilde G\), and
\(\widetilde\sigma=\sum_k\widetilde q_k|D_k\rangle\langle D_k|\). Suppose
K is supplied as an open-boundary
rational 1D MPO Kraus operator of length N, maximum bond chi, and local entry
bit length B, with the promise K^*K<=I. (The same accounting works for local
Gaussian-rational entries in Q(i), using exact conjugation.) The output is an
N-qubit register whose
computational-basis string is sampled after the K-success event. The input
tilde-sigma has the exact rational matrix entries

    tilde-sigma[x,y] = tilde-q_k/binom(N,k) if |x|=|y|=k, and 0 otherwise.

It has a direct rational MPO with bond D=(N+1)^2: its bond state after a
prefix is the pair (a,b) of ket and bra Hamming counts; at site i, physical
pair (x_i,y_i) increments the two counters; the right boundary returns
tilde-q_a/binom(N,a) when a=b and zero otherwise. Thus the input MPO is
explicit, positive, and exactly rational. No separability property is used
or claimed for tilde-sigma.

Contracting K tilde-sigma K* gives a positive rational MPO A of bond at most
M=D chi^2. Its local tensor entries are formed by summing over the two input
physical bits, so the construction takes O(N M^2) rational operations under
the stated uniform-bond model. For each output prefix, compute the two exact
unnormalized branch masses a_0,a_1 by contracting the prefix against a
precomputed right suffix environment. The next bit has exact rational
conditional probability a_1/(a_0+a_1). Continue for N sites. At the root,
Tr A=0 is decided by exact rational arithmetic; if it is positive, the
sampler never rejects. It uses no inverse-success-probability factor.

The compiled bond and rational bit lengths are polynomial in N, chi, B,
and the direct rational weight output bit length. Let B_sigma bound the bit
height of the rational sigma-MPO entries. At each site, take the product of the
denominators of all entries in the local A tensor, including boundary data,
as a common denominator. If B_loc bounds both that denominator's bit length
and the scaled numerator height, then the crude count of O(M^2) tensor
entries at fixed physical dimension gives
B_loc=O(M^2(B+B_sigma+1)). A contraction rational then has height
H=O(N(B_loc+log(2M))): a term multiplies at most N local entries, and each
contraction sums at most (2M)^N paths (the factor 2 accounts for
physical-bit branches). Forming the local A tensors and all prefix/suffix
environments uses O(N M^2) rational operations under the stated fixed
physical dimension and open-boundary model; with schoolbook arithmetic this
is O(N M^2 H^2) bit operations, plus polynomial-time common-denominator
bookkeeping. The explicitly charged B_loc is polynomial in the complete
encoded local MPO tensors and mixture output. A dyadic threshold with
b=ceil(log2(4N/gamma)) bits at each of N steps incurs at most gamma/4 TV
from the exact sigma-conditional law (sum the N Bernoulli rounding errors).
For gamma=eps/2, rounding contributes at most eps/8; with (15), the total
output distance from the true rho-conditioned measurement law is at most
(128/961+1/8)eps < 0.259 eps. The random-bit cost is
O(N log(N/eps)). The cost depends polynomially on the encoded MPO and
mixture sizes, not on the numeric magnitude of P; the exact mass's bit
length is already included in the contraction arithmetic.

The nonzero-event decision is exact for this interface. Since rho and
tilde-sigma have the same support and K is supplied exactly,
Tr(K tilde-sigma K*)>0 iff Tr(K rho K*)>0. The former is a rational
contraction. This statement assumes
the MPO representation and its TNI promise are supplied; checking that
promise is outside the sampler. For multiple listed Kraus operators, the
same construction applies by summing their positive output MPOs, with cost
polynomial in the number and bond sizes of the Kraus MPOs.

This is a conditional classical sampler only for the specified low-bond
MPO postselection and computational-basis readout. It does not give an
efficient classical simulator for arbitrary polynomial-size quantum
circuits or arbitrary global POVMs. General MPS/MPO contraction itself is
standard. The all-CP-map relative-order transfer is a generic stability
lemma, not a new algorithmic capability by itself; the direct Taylor
construction removes the N125 dependency for this consumer. The route avoids
choosing state approximation accuracy as eps times the rare event
probability, but strict advantage over existing compilers remains
unestablished.

4. Sharp additive-error gate for general MPO/Kraus postselection

Let rho and sigma be input states with D(rho,sigma)=Delta. Let K be any
trace-nonincreasing Kraus map, A=K rho K*, B=K sigma K*, and
P=Tr A, Q=Tr B. If P>Delta, then Q>0 and

    D(A/P,B/Q)
      <= ||A-B||_1/(2P) + |P-Q|/(2P)
      <= 3 Delta/(2P).

The first inequality is triangle inequality after normalizing both
subnormalized outputs by P; the second uses trace-norm contraction for the
Hermitian difference under a trace-nonincreasing CP map and
|P-Q|<=Delta. Any later measurement can only reduce this bound. Thus an
epsilon-accurate conditional-output guarantee from an additive operator
approximation requires Delta=O(epsilon P), unless additional relative-order
structure is available. Equation (13) supplies that structure for the N125
approximant and makes the generic event-mass factor unnecessary here.

The scaling obstruction is exact. On two qubits, for 0<eta<1 define

    rho=(1-eta)|00><00| + eta|10><10|,
    sigma=(1-eta)|00><00| + eta|11><11|.

Then D(rho,sigma)=eta. Herald on the first qubit's outcome 1 and retain the
second qubit. Both herald probabilities equal eta, but the conditional outputs
are |0><0| and |1><1|, at TV distance 1. Hence trace distance alone cannot
give a uniform postselection guarantee when P is comparable to Delta.

For the specific target rho_N^sym, the computational all-zero outcome has
probability p_0=f_0/F_N, where f_0=exp(-N/4) and F_N=sum_k f_k. Since
1<=F_N<=N+1 for even N, and exp(-1/(4N))<=F_N<=N+1 for odd N,

    exp(-N/4)/(N+1) <= p_0 <= exp(-N/4+1/(4N)).

To turn a trace-error construction with error Delta into an epsilon-accurate
conditioned output at this event, the generic gate asks for Delta<=O(epsilon
p_0). The Cycle-3 mixture supplies an error parameter alpha only under
N>=40 ln(16/alpha). Taking alpha=epsilon p_0/4 would require

    N >= 40 ln(64/(epsilon p_0))
      >= 10N - 10/N + 40 ln(64/epsilon),

which is impossible for N>=2. This shows that the *generic additive-error
continuity route* cannot use the current Cycle-3 finite-truncation guarantee
for this rare event. The direct relative-weight route in §3 bypasses that
requirement, because its guarantee is multiplicative before and after any CP
map. This is not a no-go for the mixture or a claim that the event probability
is large; it only limits the stated Cycle-3 additive-accuracy route.

5. Exact technical delta and evidence boundary

The concrete consumer is a rational MPO postselection sampler on the explicit
symmetric Gaussian-sector input. It has polynomial tensor bond, finite bits,
exact zero-event detection, and conditional error independent of event mass.
The direct relative rational construction reproduces the same consumer
without N125. The operator sandwich applies to arbitrary CP maps, while the
end-to-end classical sampling cost is proved only for the supplied rational
bounded-bond MPO Kraus interface. Standard MPO contraction supplies the
computation once the relative-order representation is given.

N118 source-reports a broader whole-Gibbs product compiler and N123 a
universal compiler for promised truly separable Dicke-diagonal laws. They
are not independently validated in this cycle. N118's whole-Gibbs input is
not the same state, which is supported only on the maximal-spin symmetric
subspace. N123's exact-separability promise is met by the N125 approximant
sigma, but whether the target rho itself satisfies that promise is not
established here; moreover, N123's additive state-approximation interface
does not itself furnish this event-independent conditional guarantee. The
direct relative rational weights already produce the same input MPO and
consumer, so the N125-specific algorithmic-gain hypothesis is refuted for
this interface. N125 could still matter if a separate legal interface
requires an explicit positive product-state ensemble or separability
certificate; this sampler does not. No advantage over every possible N123
implementation on an overlapping promise is claimed. The present route also
does not establish that the target itself is nonseparable.

Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for direct rational weight construction,
the Loewner lift, and the rational MPO postselection sampler under the
supplied rational MPO/TNI interface. REFUTED for an N125-specific algorithmic
gain in this consumer, and REFUTED for the blanket claim that additive trace
error alone controls rare postselected outputs uniformly. UNKNOWN whether
this yields a strict advantage over a validated N118/N123 compiler on an
overlapping state promise. No Chen–Liu result is applied. No
general quantum-circuit classical simulation, postselection lower bound,
all-Schur theorem, hardware preparation claim, novelty, or priority is
asserted.

The N=4 dense rational replay and N=20 bond-2 CNOT filter fixture are now
included in `verify_mpo_postselection.py`. The latter checks a synthetic
sector law and its tiny nonzero event by exact combinatorial formulas; it is
not a generic MPO implementation benchmark.

Decisive next test: implement and replay a generic nontrivial bond>1 rational
MPO filter with exponentially small but nonzero success mass, include exact
zero/nonzero discrimination and full numerator/denominator bit accounting,
and compare its error/runtime against (i) an additive trace-accuracy compiler
that knows a success lower bound and (ii) N118/N123 on any overlapping
promise. A bare state-level MPS contraction does not pass this gate.
