DECISION

The fixed-model archive lower in the cycle-1 statistical-memory report is
valid under its intended blind-code contract. Its prose expressly says the
encoder is blind and that dimension D is bounded at every independent seed,
but it does not state decoder-independence from v as explicitly as it should.
That omission is load-bearing: if the decoder receives v, a one-dimensional
memory can discard X and sample P_v. It is a repair to the theorem interface,
not a counterexample to the repaired theorem.

The uniform-TV-to-posterior-distortion step, Holevo accounting for a hybrid
register, and positive reference-preserving codec normalizations survive this
cross-phase audit. The central distinctions are: one seed-averaged uniform
sup_v guarantee versus a chosen parameter prior; a shared seed independent
of both parameter and observation versus an input-dependent record; a common
per-seed dimension cap versus an average-only resource; and independent
reference coordinates versus merely orthogonal Fisher scores. The c09_l03
joint-posterior condition avoids the last independence requirement by using a
joint differential-entropy lower bound; it remains a conditional theorem, not
a general replacement for Fisher spectra.

EXACT ARCHIVE EXPERIMENT AND RISK-TRANSFER LEMMA

For each finite model family {P_v : v in V}, the defensible contract is:

* Draw V0~pi, X~P_V0. S is independent of (V0,X), with law rho.
* For every seed s, one encoder channel E_s maps X to a block-diagonal cq
  state Q_s in a direct-sum algebra with total Hilbert dimension
  D_s=sum_j d_{s,j}; the classical block labels are included in D_s.
* One decoder channel R_s maps Q_s to a fresh classical observation Y. E_s
  and R_s may depend on the declared family, epsilon, and public query, but
  neither receives V0 or the unknown v. No input-dependent register bypasses
  Q_s; there is no uncharged entangled receiver or feedback channel.
* The stated cost is a uniform bound D_s<=D for every seed, not an
  average-over-seeds assertion. The accuracy guarantee is
  sup_v TV(P^Y_v,P_v)<=epsilon, where
  P^Y_v = integral R_s E_s(P_v) rho(ds) and TV is half-L1.

For any finite prior pi, define the two joint laws
  A(dv,dx)=pi(dv)P_v(dx),
  B(dv,dy)=pi(dv)P^Y_v(dy).
Then TV(A,B)=sum_v pi(v)TV(P_v,P^Y_v)<=epsilon. Therefore every fixed
loss g(v,z) in [0,1] obeys |E_A g(V0,X)-E_B g(V0,Y)|<=epsilon. This is the
exact transfer used by a bounded proper score. It is seed-averaged: no
per-seed TV guarantee follows or is needed.

For a binary pair dP_±/dmu=1±sU, equal prior, |U|<=c0, sc0<=1, the
mixture of X is exactly mu and p(X)=Pr(T=+|X)=(1+sU(X))/2. Let F' be the
clipped linear derivative clip((2p-1)/(s c0),[-1,1]) and let ell be its
Savage loss. Since |U(Y)|<=c0 as well, r(Y)=(1+sU(Y))/2 remains in the
quadratic region of F. Conditional on X,Y, the Markov interface gives
Pr(T=+|X,Y)=p(X), and direct Bregman algebra gives

 E[ell(T,r(Y))-ell(T,r(X)) | X,Y]
       = s (U(X)-U(Y))^2/(4 c0).

The right side is nonnegative. Applying the joint-law risk bound to this
fixed loss gives E[(U(X)-U(Y))^2] <= 4 c0 epsilon/s under the common-reference
coupling. Expectation includes S; this neither asserts nor requires accuracy
for each S. For the affine family in c09_l03, v=+/-e_i is admissible for
every i, so the uniform guarantee supplies this bound for each coordinate
pair under the *same* reference-averaged channel. It does not require a
single prior that puts appreciable mass on infinitely many pairs.

Exact counterexample to replacing sup_v by one prior-average criterion:
P0=Bernoulli(1/2), P1=Bernoulli(3/4), and pi(P1)=1/100. A D=1 decoder
always emitting P0 has average TV 1/400 but worst-case TV 1/4. An average
criterion transfers risk for that chosen pi only. A finite prior putting
small mass on a hard pair weakens its bound by the reciprocal prior mass; it
cannot be silently read as the uniform guarantee needed for every i.

HYBRID REGISTER AND RANDOM-ACCESS AUDIT

Condition on each public seed. For the cq ensemble X->Q_s, Holevo and data
processing give I(W(X);Q_s)<=ln D_s for every measurable feature W(X), and
any subsequent decoder output obeys I(W(X);W(Y),S)<=ln D when D_s<=D and
S is independent. The block label count is at most D_s because every block
has dimension at least one. Thus `sum_j d_j`, rather than max_j d_j or only
the quantum subregister dimension, is the charged dimension. With a
seed-dependent cap the direct consequence is an average conditional
information bound by E[ln D_S]; it does not establish a worst-seed log D
claim. If S depends on X or v, the free-seed data-processing argument fails;
any information carried there must be included in the charged state.

For product-reference coordinates X_i, strong subadditivity plus their
independence gives
  sum_i I(X_i;Q|S) <= I(X_I;Q|S) <= ln D.
Each coordinate may use its own query-specific POVM after storage; the lower
does not require physically measuring all coordinate outputs at once. But
orthogonality or zero covariance alone is not enough for this sum inequality.
In c09_l03 the lower instead uses the vector Z_I jointly: Holevo yields
I(Z_I(X);Z_I(Y))<=ln D, and the entropy-thickness hypothesis supplies
h(Z_I)>=-H|I|. This is the right replacement for independence when the
joint posterior is full-dimensional with the stated entropy bound. It does
not cover singular shared-scalar score vectors.

An independent continuous seed has no entropy cost only because it is
independent and D is capped at every seed. A seed correlated with v can reveal
the parameter without passing through Q; an input-dependent seed can reveal
X. A receiver's output need only match the marginal experiment P_v; this
contract does not promise reconstruction of the realized sample or its
correlations with external records. Finite-bit generation of continuous Y,
reference-law acquisition, and conditional sampling remain outside D unless
explicitly added to the resource model.

NORMALIZATION CHECKS RETAINED

Uniform periodic hats satisfy sum_j phi_j(t)=1 and integral phi_j(t)dt=1/J.
The encoder sends j with probability phi_j(t); the decoder density is
J phi_j(y). The resulting density-ratio operator is
  Kf(y)=J sum_j phi_j(y) integral phi_j(t) f(t)dt.
It has K1=1 and integral Kf=integral f. Its kernel is symmetric positive
semidefinite because it is a Gram sum. For mu=Unif[-1,1], set
m_j=integral phi_j dmu=(1/2)integral phi_j dx. A decoder Lebesgue density
phi_j(y)/(2m_j) is normalized; relative to mu it is phi_j(y)/m_j, giving
  Kf(y)=sum_j phi_j(y) [integral phi_j(x) f(x)dmu(x)]/m_j.
Again K1=1 and mu is preserved. In the general product-label codec, with
q_j=integral product_i phi_{i,j_i}(Z_i(x))dmu(x), output
mu_j(dx)=q_j^-1 product_i phi_{i,j_i}(Z_i(x))dmu(x) is normalized and
sum_j q_j mu_j=mu. Positivity and normalization require q_j>0; the stated
strictly positive full-dimensional score density gives positive mass to every
interior grid cell.
