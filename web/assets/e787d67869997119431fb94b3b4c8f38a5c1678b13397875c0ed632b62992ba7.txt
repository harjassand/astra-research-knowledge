1. INTERFACE

Fix m>=2, N>=1, R=Sym^N(C^m), d=binom(N+m-1,N), and tau=Tr/d. Choose
Hermitian fundamental su(m) generators t_a with Tr(t_a t_b)=delta_ab/2,
where 1<=a<=r=m^2-1. Let

  T_a=dGamma(t_a)|_R,   kappa=tau(T_a^2)=N(N+m)/(2m(m+1)),
  B_a=T_a/sqrt(kappa),   H_1=span_R{B_1,...,B_r}.

The B_a are a Hermitian tau-orthonormal basis of the unique adjoint
subrepresentation in End(R). Assume Phi:M_d->M_d is UCP (Heisenberg
picture), tau-preserving, and SU(m)-covariant. Write Phi_* for its
trace-dual Schrödinger channel. Assume an ACTUAL physical broadcaster

  Bcal:M_d -> M_d tensor M_d

which is CPTP and whose two output marginals

  Bcal_A=Tr_B o Bcal=Phi_*,   Bcal_B=Tr_A o Bcal=Phi_*

on the entire input algebra. Equivalently, Bcal^*:M_d tensor M_d->M_d is
UCP and both of its restrictions to one output algebra equal Phi. No
covariance of Bcal is assumed. Multiplicity one and Hermiticity imply

  Phi(B_a)=lambda B_a for every a,   lambda real.

Bistochastic Kadison contraction gives |lambda|<=1. The trace-dual Phi_*
is covariant and preserves H_1. For B_a,B_b in the tau-orthonormal frame,

  tau(B_b Phi_*(B_a))=tau(Phi(B_b) B_a)=lambda delta_ab,

so Phi_* has the same scalar lambda there. Existence of Bcal is an
independent premise; this argument does not construct a broadcaster from
a small error.

2. THE SINGLE PURE 1-DESIGN AND ITS ADJOINT MULTIPLIER

For Haar-uniform projective unit vectors z in CP^(m-1), set
P_z=|z^tensor N><z^tensor N| and define

  Psi_N(X)=d integral Tr(P_z X) P_z dz.

Since integral P_z dz=I/d, this is a unital, trace-preserving,
self-adjoint EB channel. It is exactly the measure-and-prepare channel for
the one pure 1-design with effects d P_z dz and preparations P_z.
Covariance and multiplicity one make its action scalar on H_1; write that
eigenvalue as mu_1. It is

  mu_1=N/(N+m).                                         (1)

For the normalization check, fundamental completeness gives

  sum_a <z,t_a z>^2=(m-1)/(2m),
  Tr(P_z T_a)=N<z,t_a z>.

Thus, using tau(B_a^2)=1,

  r mu_1
    =sum_a tau(B_a Psi_N(B_a))
    =integral sum_a Tr(P_z B_a)^2 dz
    =N^2(m-1)/(2m kappa)
    =r N/(N+m),

which proves (1). This identifies the coherent-orbit capture and the
posterior score loss without a modewise recoupling coefficient.

3. A DIRECT CHOI-STAR OPERATOR INEQUALITY

On the Choi reference R-bar and outputs A,B, form

  W_ad=sum_a B_{a,R}^T tensor
             (B_{a,A} tensor I_B + I_A tensor B_{a,B}).

The exact operator inequality is

  W_ad <= r(1+mu_1) I.                                  (2)

Proof. Embed the symmetric powers isometrically in the N reference
fundamental legs and the 2N output fundamental legs. The collective
generator T_a is the sum of the fundamental t_a over its N legs; W_ad is
the compression to bar(R) tensor R tensor R of the corresponding ambient
operator. Let Omega=m^(-1/2) sum_q |q,q> and P_{ij}=|Omega><Omega| on
reference leg i and output leg j, tensored with identity on all other legs.
The fundamental Fierz identity, with this transpose convention, is

  sum_a t_a^T tensor t_a = (m P_Omega - I/m)/2.

Expanding the collective generators therefore gives the ambient identity

  W_ad=(1/(2 kappa))
        [m sum_(i=1..N) sum_(j=1..2N) P_ij - (2N^2/m) I]. (3)

For fixed reference leg i, the K=2N projections P_ij have pairwise
overlap norm 1/m: for j!=k, ||P_ij P_ik||=1/m. To bound their sum, map
the direct sum of the K ranges Ran(P_ij) into the ambient tensor space by
adding the component vectors. The Gram operator has identity diagonal
blocks and off-diagonal block norms 1/m. For vectors v_j in these ranges,

  <v,Gv> <= sum_j ||v_j||^2
       +(1/m) sum_(j!=k)||v_j||||v_k||
    <= [1+(K-1)/m] sum_j ||v_j||^2.

The nonzero spectra of that Gram operator and sum_j P_ij agree. Hence

  sum_(j=1..2N) P_ij <= [1+(2N-1)/m] I.

Summing this inequality over the N reference legs and inserting it in
(3) yields

  W_ad <= N(m+2N-1-2N/m)/(2 kappa) I
        = (m^2-1)(m+2N)/(N+m) I
        = r(1+mu_1) I,

as claimed. Compression preserves the inequality. The argument uses only
the pairwise overlap of ordinary maximally entangled projectors; there is
no decomposition into total-spin or Racah blocks.

4. USE OF THE ACTUAL COMMON-MARGINAL EXTENSION

Let

  omega_Bcal=(id_R tensor Bcal)(|Omega_d><Omega_d|_(R,in))

be the normalized Choi state of the physical broadcaster, where
|Omega_d> is maximally entangled between a reference copy R and the input.
Its two output Choi marginals are omega_Phi_*, the normalized Choi state
of Phi_*. With this convention,

  Tr[omega_Phi_* (X^T tensor Y)] = tau(Y Phi_*(X)).

Applying this relation to every summand of W_ad and using
Phi_*(B_a)=lambda B_a gives

  Tr(omega_Bcal W_ad)=2 sum_a tau(B_a Phi_*(B_a))=2r lambda.

Since omega_Bcal is a state, (2) implies

  lambda <= (1+mu_1)/2.                                  (4)

The two equal CPTP output marginals are essential in the factor 2r lambda.
The physical broadcaster need not be covariant; covariance is used only
for the Heisenberg marginal Phi to make its action scalar on H_1 (and hence
to give the same real scalar for Phi_* there). In particular, (4) is not
obtained by separately optimizing a measurement for each generator or by
assuming independent outputs.

Because mu_1 lies in [0,1], (4) is equivalent to the common-design
Dirichlet and squared comparisons

  I-Psi_N <= 2(I-Phi) on H_1,
  (I-Psi_N)^2 <= I-Psi_N <= 2(I-Phi) on H_1.              (5)

An equivalent exact posterior-score statement is

  sum_a integral Tr(P_z B_a)^2 dz = r mu_1,
  sum_a tau(B_a(I-Psi_N)B_a)=r(1-mu_1)
       <=2r(1-lambda)
        =2 sum_a tau(B_a(I-Phi)B_a).                     (6)

The same projective 1-design appears in every term. Equation (2), rather
than a list of separate scalar estimates, is the underlying operator
uncertainty bound.

5. CONSEQUENCE FOR A WHOLE ADJOINT LIKELIHOOD FAMILY

Let F be any family of Hermitian H in H_1 with ||H||_infinity<=theta<1,
and let rho_H=(I+H)/d. Define epsilon=sup_(H in F)
||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau). The same EB channel Psi_N obeys

  sup_(H in F) T(Psi_N(rho_H),rho_H)
      <= (1/2) sqrt(2 theta epsilon).                    (7)

Indeed, (5), Holder duality for normalized trace, and
||X||_(1,tau)<=||X||_(2,tau) give

  ||(I-Psi_N)H||_(1,tau)^2
    <= ||(I-Psi_N)H||_(2,tau)^2
    <= 2 tau(H (I-Phi)H)
    <= 2 ||H||_infinity ||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau).

The right side is nonnegative because (I-Phi) is the scalar
1-lambda>=0 on H_1. The physical half-trace distance contributes the
factor 1/2 in (7). No label-dependent design is used.

6. SCOPE, COMPARISON, AND COST

This supplies a uniform-in-m,N common design on the adjoint score span
whenever the stated Heisenberg marginal admits a physical CPTP broadcaster.
It bypasses the sufficient scalar gate based only on
lambda^4(1-eta_1(1))<=2(1-lambda^2): the Choi-star inequality uses the

The scope is strictly narrower than a full-algebra comparison. Higher
