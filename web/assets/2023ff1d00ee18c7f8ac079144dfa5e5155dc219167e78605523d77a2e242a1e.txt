The strongest positive construction consumes the Cycle 3 rational N125 binomial mixture at the operator level. 1. Rational operator consumer and product-measurement sampler
-------------------------------------------------------------
Let

    rho_N^sym = F_N^(-1) sum_(k=0)^N exp(-(k-N/2)^2/N) |D_k><D_k|,
    F_N = sum_(k=0)^N exp(-(k-N/2)^2/N).

For rational 0<epsilon<=1/2 and N>=40 ln(16/epsilon), import the Cycle 3 finite-support theorem: q=ceil((5N-1)/2) rational p_i in (0,1), positive rational mu_i, and

    g_k = sum_i mu_i binom(N,k) p_i^k(1-p_i)^(N-k),
    |g_k-f_k| <= (epsilon/4)f_k,  f_k=exp(-(k-N/2)^2/N).

The q(N+1)-state roots-of-unity twirl is exact: the uniform average over zeta_j=exp(2 pi i j/(N+1)) cancels every Dicke off-diagonal because excitation-number differences lie in [-N,N]. The resulting sector weight is g_k/G_N, G_N=sum_i mu_i=sum_k g_k. Relative error through normalization gives trace distance at most epsilon/(4-epsilon).

The Cycle 4 leaf replaces each p_i by a rational p_i'=4s_i^2/(1+s_i^2)^2 from exact dyadic bisection, with |p_i-p_i'|<=epsilon/(16N). A rational unit phase base z=(1-t^2+2it)/(1+t^2), with rational t approximating tan(pi/(N+1)) to epsilon/(16N^2), generates all phases z^j. Bernoulli coupling and tensor-product telescoping charge another epsilon/16 for amplitudes and epsilon/16 for phases. The exact rational-weight product ensemble is therefore within epsilon/(4-epsilon)+epsilon/8. Rounding the M=q(N+1) label intervals upward to a denominator 2^b, b=ceil(log2(8M/epsilon)), costs at most epsilon/4 in TV. The final dyadic product ensemble is within

    epsilon/(4-epsilon)+3epsilon/8 <= 37epsilon/56 < epsilon.

Every retained term is a normalized pure product state with rational complex local entries; the output has polynomial encoding length and stays exactly in the symmetric subspace. A label is sampled with exactly b fair bits. For any supplied nonadaptive product of at most s rational-complex single-qubit POVM outcomes per site, conditional outcomes factor over sites. With h=ceil(log2(8Ns/epsilon)) bits per site for CDF-grid sampling, the added TV is at most epsilon/8. Thus the full N-site outcome string can be sampled in exactly b+Nh bits and has TV at most 11epsilon/14 from measurement on rho_N^sym. The sample time is O(log M+N+Ns) rational arithmetic operations with polynomial bit lengths in Cycle 3 node heights, POVM input bits and N log(N/epsilon).

Scope: this is a symmetric-sector operator approximant, not a full exp(-J_z^2/N) all-Schur approximation. It does not evaluate arbitrary entangling POVMs efficiently or establish exact separability of the target. Cycle 3's root isolation, exponential cancellation, quadrature and normalization costs are inherited, not revalidated here. The exact N=3 Q(i) matrix replay and rational-grid tests pass; these are finite diagnostics. Full derivation and claims: `operator_consumer/`.

2. Chen–Liu coefficient FPRAS versus selective determinant Z_k
---------------------------------------------------------------
The live primary is Chen–Liu, *An FPRAS for Counting Common Bases of Two Matroids*, arXiv:2610.06724v1 (posted 5 October 2026), [Theorem 1.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06724v1). It estimates a supplied coefficient [x^alpha](g_mu g_nu), alpha in {0,1,2}^U, for two nonnegative multiaffine log-concave set-weight polynomials with positive support seeds and supplied coefficient-range upper bounds; the theorem gives an arithmetic/oracle bound and the paper's Remark A.4 reports a polynomial-bit rational implementation. The exact theorem assumptions and costs are recorded in `common_bases_zk/SOURCES.md` and `RESULT.md`.

For Gaussian-rational A, a row/column coefficient identity for

    Z_k(A)=sum_(|I|=|J|=k, I intersect J=empty) |det(A[I,J])|^2

is exact, and the natural site-cover selector is log-concave with range one. But the determinant-weight polynomial fails log-concavity already at n=2,k=1,A=[[0,1],[1,0]]: at the all-ones point, v=(1,-1,-1,1) gives v^T Hess(log g_mu)v=2>0. A second Cauchy–Binet Gram encoding moves the failure to its complete-pair selector, whose log-Hessian has the same positive-curvature witness. Exact finite replay passes 81 rational 2x2 matrices over {-1,0,1} plus the Hessian witnesses.

Conclusion: Theorem 1.6 does not give an FPRAS for general Z_k through either recorded factorization. This is a scoped failure of these encodings, not approximation hardness or an impossibility for other encodings. The arXiv identifier previously attached to an XXZ claim resolves to this matroid-counting paper; retain it as a possible imported counting lemma and correct the source mapping rather than discarding it.

3. Structured selected-column masks
-----------------------------------
For dense integer X in Z^(N x D), Y in Z^(D x N), and a succinct Cartesian mask I x J, tile selected rows and columns into D-sized blocks, pad to powers of two, and apply the explicit rank-7 Strassen recursion on each tile. The total exact arithmetic work is O(|I||J|D^(log_2 7-2)), including recombinations. With D=Theta(N^(1/4)), I=[N], and |J|=floor(N^(3/4)), this computes a mask of size Theta(N^(7/4)) in O(N^((5+log_2 7)/4))=O(N^(2-eta)), eta=(3-log_2 7)/4>0. For O(log N)-bit entries, schoolbook integer arithmetic remains subquadratic. Exact checks pass for (N,D,|J|)=(16,2,8) and (256,4,64).

The algorithm relies on the mask being one Cartesian product; it does not bound arbitrary sparse masks or improve the original unrestricted mask target. Full construction and bit ledger: `structured_mask/result.md`.

4. Hidden-code approximation bottleneck
---------------------------------------
The exact-hardness construction for hidden binary-code marginals uses cancellation-heavy interpolation; it is not approximation-preserving. A separate uniform relative-prefix FPRAS would, conditionally, transfer to the optimal O(n^(-1/2)) expected terminal-KL order via the root's smoothing lemma, with polynomial bit cost and no inverse-prefix-probability charge. The missing object is that relative oracle for every full projected prefix, including unused-row completions and the terminal strict-tail statistic.

Direct FKG/ferromagnetic sampling fails on a finite full-prefix counterexample. For q=2,N=5,prefix 1110, exact enumeration of all 164 compatible pools and all eight hidden paths gives unnormalized hidden masses lambda(000)=lambda(111)=7, lambda(001)=lambda(110)=8/3, and lambda(010)=lambda(011)=lambda(100)=lambda(101)=61/12. At x=010,y=100,

    lambda(x meet y)lambda(x join y)=56/3 < 3721/144=lambda(x)lambda(y).

The checker independently matches each path mass against sequential urn draws, and records Q_5(1110)=119/990, including separate contributions from unused rows and the terminal gap>1 condition. A full-span magnetization polynomial also violates coefficient log-concavity. These counterexamples rule out two direct structural shortcuts only; general FPRAS status remains UNKNOWN, and no approximation lower bound is proved. Full package: `hidden_code_approx/leaf01_fkg_counterexample/`.
