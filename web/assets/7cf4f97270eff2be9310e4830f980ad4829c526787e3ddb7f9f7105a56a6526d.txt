ADVERSARIAL AUDIT: SU(3) WALL WEIGHTED RANK PROFILE
Date: 2026-10-08
Scope: arbitrary complex matrices on V_(a,b), with a>=b>=1.
Status: exact reductions and unconditional lower bounds proved below;
the proposed 3-to-2 weighted spectral count remains unproved. No
counterexample to that count is found. Finite spectra are diagnostics only.

1. TARGET AND DISTINCTION BETWEEN THE TWO CLAIMS

Put N=a+b, B=b+1, d=(a+1)B(N+2)/2, C_S=Delta/N^2, and

  E_w(A)=E_T(A)/N^2+E_S(A)/(N sqrt(C_S)),
  Lambda_w(Q)=inf_{rank A<=Q, ||A||_HS=1} E_w(A).

The weighted-posterior note proves an operational converse in terms of
this all-complex-matrix Lambda_w. It does not prove a rank profile. The
candidate 3-to-2/fiber profile is the separate conditional hypothesis H
in `outputs/research/sol_semiclassical_memory/wall_su3/
ANISOTROPIC_SPECTRAL_GATE.txt`:

  D_w(t)-1 <= C d[(N^2/B)t^2+N^3 t^3],   0<t<=c0/N,       (H)

where L_w is the positive self-adjoint operator representing E_w and
D_w(t)=Tr 1_[0,t](L_w). The two terms in H are a conjectural weighted
spectral count, not a consequence of the joint-height/fiber upper
filters. The current audit finds no proof or contradiction to H.

2. EXACT POSITIVITY REDUCTION FOR EVERY RANK BUDGET

For a Hermitian matrix G define e_G(A)=||[G,A]||_HS^2. If
A=U diag(s_1,...,s_d) V^dagger is an SVD, set H_G=U^dagger G U and
K_G=V^dagger G V. Directly grouping the (i,j),(j,i) terms gives the
identity

  2 e_G(A)-e_G(|A|)-e_G(|A^dagger|)
    =2 sum_i s_i^2 |(H_G)_(ii)-(K_G)_(ii)|^2
       +4 sum_(i<j) s_i s_j |(H_G)_(ij)-(K_G)_(ij)|^2 >=0.          (2.1)

One can check (2.1) in the singular basis: the off-diagonal pair has
remainder 4s_i s_j|h_ij-k_ij|^2, and the diagonal term is
2s_i^2|h_ii-k_ii|^2. Thus the identity holds for complex, nonnormal A
and arbitrary degeneracies or zero singular values.

Apply (2.1) to each T_A and S_A and add with the positive coefficients
N^-2 and (N sqrt(C_S))^-1. Since |A| and |A^dagger| have the same
rank as A, at least one of them has weighted energy at most E_w(A).
Consequently, for every Q,

  Lambda_w(Q)=inf_{A>=0, rank A<=Q, ||A||_HS=1} E_w(A).              (2.2)

This is an exact finite-carrier theorem, not an asymptotic argument.
It removes a proposed adversary in which a nonpositive or nonnormal
matrix beats every positive filter. It does NOT say the minimizer is a
function of a Cartan element, preserves U(2) fibers, or has either
candidate scaling. A positive matrix that mixes the GT/branch basis
could still invalidate a conjectured fiber profile.

The combined generator family can be written
G^T_A=T_A/N and G^S_A=S_A/sqrt(N sqrt(C_S)); its squared sum is the
scalar (C2/N^2+sqrt(C_S)/N)I. This also gives the usual two-modulus
coercivity setup, but (2.1) itself does not need that scalar property.

3. EXACT RANK-ONE CHECK, INCLUDING NON-HERMITIAN VECTORS

If A=|u><v| with unit u,v, let m_T(u)_A=<u,T_Au> and
m_S(u)_A=<u,S_Au>. The scalar square identities give

  E_w(A)=2(C2/N^2+sqrt(C_S)/N)
       -2[m_T(u).m_T(v)/N^2
               +m_S(u).m_S(v)/(N sqrt(C_S))].                     (3.1)

The bracket is an inner product of two real vectors after positive
rescaling. Cauchy-Schwarz and 2xy<=x^2+y^2 show that its maximum over
u,v is attained with u=v at a maximizer of the corresponding squared
moment norm. Thus no off-diagonal rank-one |u><v| beats the best
positive rank-one projector. This is also a direct rank-one proof of
(2.2), independent of SVD moduli.

There is a uniform endpoint estimate on the wall regime. For the highest
weight vector P=|lambda><lambda|, the ordinary identity
E_T(P)=2N is exact. The S-moment can be evaluated from the U(3) weight
(N,b,0). Write

  R0=a^2+ab+b^2+3a+3b=3C2,
  R1=a^2+ab+b^2+6a+6b+12.

For traceless diagonal Z=diag(z_1,z_2,z_3), define s_i by
<lambda|S_Z|lambda>=sum_i z_i s_i. The exact values are

  s_1= ab(b+2)(N+1)/(2 N R0),
  s_2=-ab(N+1)(N+4)/(2 N R0),
  s_3= ab(a+2)(N+1)/(2 N R0),
  ||m_S(lambda)||^2=(1/2)sum_i s_i^2
    =a^2 b^2 (N+1)^2 R1/(4N^2 R0^2),
  C_S=ab(a+2)(b+2)(N+1)(N+3)/(4N^2 R0).                         (3.2)

Here the factor 1/2 follows from Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2 and
<S_Z>=sum z_A<S_A>. Hence

  E_S(P)=2(C_S-||m_S(lambda)||^2)
    = ab(N+1)/(2N^2 R0^2)
       [(a+2)(b+2)(N+3)R0-ab(N+1)R1].                            (3.3)

The bracket in (3.3) expands to

  2a^4+5a^3b+16a^3+4a^2b^2+29a^2b+42a^2
   +5ab^3+29ab^2+60ab+36a+2b^4+16b^3+42b^2+36b.

For a>=b>=1 this is at most 324a^4, while R0>=a^2 and N+1<=3a;
therefore E_S(P)<=486b. Also R0<=9a^2, N<=2a, and
(a+2)(b+2)(N+1)(N+3)>=abN^2, so sqrt(C_S)>=b/6. Combining these
estimates with the all-matrix rank-one minimum E_T(A)>=2N gives

  2/N <= Lambda_w(1) <= E_w(P) <= 2918/N,   a>=b>=1.              (3.4)

The upper constant is deliberately loose. This verifies the candidate
f_1=1/N order and rules out a rank-one nonnormal counterexample. It does
not establish that P minimizes E_w among positive rank-one projectors.

4. UNCONDITIONAL ALL-COMPLEX LOWER BOUNDS

Let L_T=sum_A ad(T_A)^2 and L_S=sum_A ad(S_A)^2, so
L_w=L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)) >= L_T/N^2 as positive operators.
Therefore

  Lambda_w(Q)>=Lambda_T(Q)/N^2                                  (4.1)

for every complex rank-Q matrix. There is a global SU(3) Casimir profile
bound that yields a quantitative, though generally weaker, consequence.
For output irrep (r,s), the multiplicity in End(V_(a,b)) is at most
its zero-weight multiplicity, which is zero unless r=s mod 3 and is
min(r,s)+1 otherwise. This is the standard stabilizer/cyclic-vector
multiplicity cap also recorded in the SU(3) source packet. If C2(r,s)<=T
and T>=1, then r+s<=2sqrt(T); hence there are at most 9T labels,
zero-weight multiplicity is at most 3sqrt(T), and dim V_(r,s) is at most
18T^(3/2). Thus, globally and independently of a,b,

  D_T(T)<=486 T^3, T>=1;       D_T(T)=1, 0<=T<3.                 (4.2)

Schur partial trace and the Schmidt-rank-Q Ky-Fan bound give
Lambda_T(Q)>=int_0^infty [1-QD_T(T)/d]_+ dT. For Q<=d/2 set
H=(d/Q)^(1/3)>=2^(1/3), epsilon=972^(-1/3). On 0<=T<=epsilon H,
(4.2) implies D_T(T)<=H^3/2 (for T<1 use D_T=1); thus

  Lambda_w(Q)>=epsilon/(2N^2) (d/Q)^(1/3),  1<=Q<=d/2.           (4.3)

No positivity of A is used in (4.1)--(4.3). For a>=b, d is comparable
to N^2 B, so (4.3) has order B^(1/3)/(N^(4/3)Q^(1/3)). This is weaker
than the candidate f_Q by a factor that can vanish as B/N->0 for
Q<=B^3. In the fiber range it is also weak except at a fixed fraction
of d. The ordinary Casimir term alone therefore cannot prove H or the
candidate profile.

There is a separate gap-only codimension estimate with an exact
right-kernel argument. For rank(A)<=d-k, choose a rank-k orthogonal
projection P onto ker(A), and put A=zI+X, Tr X=0,
Y=P-(k/d)I. Since AP=0, kz=-Tr(YX). The nontrivial gap of L_w is at
least 3/N^2, so with G=<Y,L_w^+Y>,

  k^2|z|^2<=G E_w(A),
  ||X||_HS^2<=N^2 E_w(A)/3,
  G<=N^2||Y||_HS^2/3=N^2(k-k^2/d)/3.

The scalar/traceless orthogonality then yields, with no assumption that A
is normal or positive,

  ||A||_HS^2 <= E_w(A)[dG/k^2+N^2/3]
               <= E_w(A) N^2 d/(3k),
  Lambda_w(d-k)>=3k/(N^2 d), 1<=k<d.                            (4.4)

The exact k-k^2/d mass matters: it cancels the added N^2/3 term. Bound
(4.4) is much weaker than the candidate near-full profile for small k,
but it is an unconditional all-complex lower and checks the kernel
orientation, complex scalar trace, and endpoint normalization.

5. AUDIT OF THE CONDITIONAL SPECTRAL-COUNT TO ALL-MATRIX BRIDGE

Assume H as stated in Section 1. For a unit rank-Q A, each invariant
spectral projector P_t of L_w has partial trace (D_w(t)/d)I. The
vectorization of A has Schmidt rank at most Q, whence
<A,P_t A><= QD_w(t)/d. Integrating the complementary spectral mass
gives the exact finite profile lower. For Q<=d/2 define

  f_Q=N^-1 min(Q^-1/3,sqrt(B/Q)),
  epsilon=min(c0,1,(8C)^-1/2,(8C)^-1/3), t=epsilon f_Q.

Then t<=c0/N. For Q<=B^3, the two terms in Q[D_w(t)-1]/d are at
most C epsilon^2 and C epsilon^3; for Q>=B^3 they are at most the same
two bounds in the opposite order. Also Q/d<=1/2. Thus QD_w(t)/d<=3/4
and

  Lambda_w(Q)>=epsilon f_Q/4.                                  (5.1)

This proof is for every complex matrix, not merely PSD filters. It is
valid conditional on H; (4.3) is the unconditional result available
without H.

For the near-full branch, the same right-kernel setup has the exact
Green layer cake
G=integral_0^infinity M_P(t)t^-2dt, where
M_P(t)=||1_(0,t](L_w)Y||^2. The scalar component must be removed:

  M_P(t)<=min(k,k^2[D_w(t)-1]/d).

Using H up to K=(eta/N)min(k^-1/3,sqrt(B/k)), eta=min(c0,1),
and M_P(t)<=k beyond K gives

  G<=C k^2[(N^2/B)K+(N^3/2)K^2]+k/K
    <= A_eta N max(k^(4/3),k^(3/2)/sqrt(B)),
  A_eta=eta^-1+C eta+C eta^2/2.

The first branch is active for k<=B^3 and the second for k>=B^3.
Substitution in the scalar/traceless inequality gives

  Lambda_w(d-k)>= [1/(N d) min(k^(2/3),sqrt(Bk))]
                    /(A_eta+sqrt(2)/3), 1<=k<=d/2,              (5.2)

under the gate's stated a>=8B dimension comparison. This is also an
all-complex lower conditional on H. The exact P0 mass k-k^2/d is needed
to avoid losing the N^2/3 term; the interval starts at positive spectral
gap 3/N^2, so no integral at zero is divergent.

I checked the algebraic bridge and its endpoints. The unresolved load-
bearing premise is H itself: it asks for the multiplicity-resolved
small-spectrum count of L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)). L_S acts inside the
growing multiplicity spaces, so a count for L_T alone, the adjoint
two-copy block, or a diagonal cap does not establish H.

6. FINITE-CARRIER SPECTRUM DIAGNOSTIC (NOT A UNIFORM RESULT)

I built the finite matrix L_w from the contraction-kernel realization
of V_(a,b), using vec([G,A])=(I tensor G-G^T tensor I)vec(A), then
diagonalized the full d^2 by d^2 Hermitian matrix. At thresholds
t=x/N^2 with integer x<=N (so t<=1/N), the observed values of
(D_w(t)-1)/{d[(N^2/B)t^2+N^3t^3]} were:

  (a,b)=(2,1), d=15: x=3 -> 0;
  (3,1), d=24: x=3 -> 0, x=4 -> 0.2222;
  (4,1), d=35: x=3 -> 0, x=4 -> 0.2747, x=5 -> 0.1524;
  (2,2), d=27: x=3 -> 0, x=4 -> 0;
  (3,2), d=42: x=3 -> 0, x=4 -> 0, x=5 -> 0.1429.

These use floating-point generator matrices and are outside H's required
large-b,a>=8B range; they only show no small-carrier gross normalization
failure. They neither verify a universal C nor test the asymptotic
exponent. Separately, (3.3) was compared to direct commutator evaluation
on highest-weight projectors for (a,b)=(1,1),(2,1),(3,1),(4,2),(6,3);
the largest absolute residual was 3.6e-15. This checks the displayed
normalization, not the proof or a uniform bound. The exact finite
rank-one identities and floating-point spectra are distinct evidence
types.

7. VERDICT

The arbitrary-complex aspect is not the bottleneck. The exact two-modulus
identity reduces the rank profile to positive semidefinite matrices, and
the partial-trace and right-kernel Green arguments genuinely cover
arbitrary complex/non-normal A. Rank one has the correct 1/N order, with
no off-diagonal rank-one improvement. The unconditional lower bounds
(4.3)--(4.4) are rigorous but do not match the 3-to-2 candidate except
at the fixed-fraction endpoint scales.

No counterexample to H or to the advertised profile is produced. Nor is
H proved: it remains a distinct spectral theorem about the multiplicity
matrices of L_S on low L_T isotypes. A bad generic filter (as in
GENERIC_FILTER_OBSTRUCTION.txt) only disproves a uniform upper estimate
for that filter class; it does not refute the rank-minimum profile.
Likewise, (2.2) eliminates nonpositive matrices as profile adversaries
but does not certify fiber-preserving filters as optimal. The current
mathematical status is therefore conditional profile, not an established
all-Q wall law.
