RANK-PROFILE CONSEQUENCE FOR THE RANK-ONE CLASSICAL TYPES B1 AND C1
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-COROLLARY from the separately banked SU(2)
profile; not a higher-rank B/C/D profile.

SETUP

For B1=Spin(3) and C1=Sp(1), an irreducible highest weight with Dynkin
label n has dimension d=n+1 and is the spin-j representation with
j=n/2. The regularity condition is n>=1; in the fixed-cone family
cN<=n<=CN, n and N are comparable. We use the normalized Casimir rank
minimum Lambda(Q)=min_{rank F<=Q,||F||_2=1} sum_A||[T_A,F]||_2^2 in the
classical_types metric and the same-carrier Gibbs recovery interface.

METRIC FACTORS

Write J_1,J_2,J_3 for standard angular momentum generators on spin-j,
[J_i,J_k]=i epsilon_ikm J_m. The SU(2) symmetric-power profile packet
uses these generators, with fundamental t_i=sigma_i/2, and energy
E_A(F)=sum_i||[J_i,F]||_2^2.

For B1, the defining real three-dimensional representation has the
Hermitian generators J_i^(1) with Tr_3(J_i^(1)J_k^(1))=2 delta_ik.
They are orthonormal for the B1 metric (1/2)Tr_3, and the B1 Lie algebra
representation sends them to J_i on spin n/2. Thus the B1 Casimir energy
is exactly E_A; no energy rescaling occurs.

For C1, the defining two-dimensional Hermitian generators are the Pauli
matrices sigma_i, orthonormal for (1/2)Tr_2. Since sigma_i=2(sigma_i/2),
their action on spin n/2 is 2J_i. Therefore
E_C(F)=4 E_A(F), and Lambda_C1(Q)=4 Lambda_A1(Q). The carrier dimensions,
ranks, and right kernels are unchanged. These factors also agree with
the defining Casimirs: B1 has n(n+2)/4, while C1 has n(n+2).

PROFILE AND GIBBS RISK

The separately proved SU(m) symmetric-power profile at m=2 (source:
outputs/research/sol_semiclassical_memory/near_full.txt, §§1-7) gives,
for n>=8 and d=n+1:

  1<=Q<=d/2:       Lambda_A1(Q)=Theta(n/Q),
  Q=d-k>d/2:       Lambda_A1(d-k)=Theta(1/log(e d/k)), 1<=k<d/2,
  Q=d:             Lambda_A1(d)=0.

Hence these same orders hold for B1. For C1 they hold as written with
the profile multiplied by exactly four, which is absorbed in Theta. The
classical_types transfer then yields, uniformly over fixed regular
rank-one cones and fixed nonzero supplied H,

  Q<=d/2:          e*_Q=Theta(1/(N Q)),
  Q=d-k>d/2:       e*_(d-k)=Theta(1/[N^2 log(e d/k)]),
  Q=d:             e*_d=0.

The first equivalence uses n=Theta(N). Hidden constants may depend on the
fixed cone, Gibbs interface, and type. For finitely many n<8, the constants
can be enlarged using the all-N transfer and the finite positive rank
profiles; this note only invokes the explicit near_full profile for n>=8.

STATUS AND LIMIT

This is a direct low-rank-rank-one corollary, not a newly proved profile:
B1 and C1 are Lie-algebra rank one, and their representations are the
already analyzed SU(2) symmetric powers with the metric factors above.
It supplies an explicit memory-error law for the rank-one cases of the
classical-types theorem. It provides no profile for B_l, C_l or D_l with
l>=2, and no asymptotics for any other regular highest-weight carrier.
The B/C/D polynomial-frame audit and the SU(2) profile each remain
internally proved candidates; external validation and novelty review are
separate gates.
