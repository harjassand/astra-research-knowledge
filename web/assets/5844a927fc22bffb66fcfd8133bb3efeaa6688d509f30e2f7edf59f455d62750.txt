REVISION — ANNEALING RATIO AND TOTAL-COST AUDIT
7 October 2026

This extends the immutable INITIAL checkpoint. It does not change its claim
or supply the missing energy inequality.

1. A uniform activity ramp has a computable density-ratio bound without a
partition-function oracle.

Fix positive coordinate-line activities and let every original line's
activity be theta_l(t)=t*lambda_l, with fixed positive rational lambda_l.
For a basis A let s(A) be its number of original lines; s(A)<=r/2. If
t'=c*t with c>=1, then

  w_(t')(A)/w_t(A)=c^s(A) in [1,c^(r/2)],
  Z(t')/Z(t)=E_(pi_t)[c^s] in [1,c^(r/2)],
  c^(-r/2) <= pi_(t')(A)/pi_t(A) <= c^(r/2).

The unknown normalizer ratio is trapped by the min/max of the known
pointwise ratio; it need not be queried. For c=1+1/r, the adjacent density
ratio lies in [(1+1/r)^(-r/2),(1+1/r)^(r/2)] subset [e^(-1/2),e^(1/2)].
From t0=epsilon=2^(-K) to 1, the number of stages is

  J=ceil(K*ln(2)/ln(1+1/r)) <= 2*r*K

for r>=2, using ln(1+1/r)>=1/(2r) and ln2<1. With M=O(r^2+N) and
R<=2^(O(ell)), K=O(r^2+r*ell+log(1/eta)+log Lambda), hence J is polynomial
in dimension and input bit length. The rational t_j=epsilon(1+1/r)^j has
O(K+j*log r) bits, which remains polynomial. No `1/epsilon` numeric loop is
needed for this schedule.

2. Partition-ratio estimation has polynomial sampling cost conditional on
the missing sampler.

For one stage,

  Z(t')/Z(t)=E_(pi_t)[R(A)],  R(A)=c^s(A) in [1,U], U=c^(r/2)<=e^(1/2).

Hoeffding therefore estimates this ratio to relative error xi with
O((U-1)^2 xi^(-2) log(1/rho)) independent pi_t samples; the factor is
dimension-independent for c=1+1/r. To make the product of all J ratios have
relative error at most epsilon, take xi=epsilon/(4J) (with a small constant
slack) and rho=delta/(2J). This costs O(J^3 epsilon^(-2) log(J/delta))
samples total. If each sample is produced within TV tau, the bias in each
stage's bounded expectation is at most (U-1)tau; choosing tau=O(epsilon/J)
keeps the product error budget polynomial. Each sample's transition coins
can be coupled with k=ceil(log2(Tmix/tau)) bits when an explicit worst-case
mixing-step bound Tmix is known. The sampling statement is CONDITIONAL:
neither Tmix nor a polynomial two-replica energy constant has been proved.

3. Total fixed-execution primitive cost conditional on that theorem.

For each proposed basis move, exact determinant/weight ratio computation is
poly(r,ell+K+j log r) bit operations. For a run capped at Tmix moves, use
an acceptance precision k=ceil(log2(4*Tmix/tau)) and proposal-selection
precision large enough that the per-step proposal TV error is at most
tau/(2*Tmix). Coupling gives total transition simulation error at most tau.
The coordinate matching refresh itself costs O(r log(r/eta)) random bits
and polynomial arithmetic, plus the K-bit rational activity description.
Across J stages and the above ratio sample count, the end-to-end cost is
polynomial *provided* the missing energy/mixing result gives an explicit
polynomial Tmix (and the same statement remains valid for the fixed-precision
trajectory coupling).

4. Where exponential dependencies can still hide.

- A particular local move may have acceptance 2^(-Omega(ell)), as shown by
  the exact delta^4 example in INITIAL. If a proposed route to connect two
  regions uses that edge as its only bridge, its conductance can be
  exponentially small. This is a bottleneck in the *kernel*, not a precision
  problem and not a lower bound against richer kernels.
- The sufficient refresh epsilon is numerically 2^(-K), but K is polynomial.
  An implementation with a loop count proportional to 1/epsilon would still
  cost 2^K; the refresh proof only bounds law error and encoding length.
- A move conditioned on all completions of a hole pattern has a denominator
  such as f(U)=sum_(I,J) |det F[I,J]|^2. Pointwise rational arithmetic does
  not evaluate this sum. Deterministic linear-parity support decides whether
  it vanishes, not its positive magnitude. Any such conditional must be
  replaced by an explicitly enumerable polynomial neighborhood or be
  charged as an unresolved counting oracle.
- If the target path sets line activities to exactly zero, support changes.
  The positive-ratio argument above applies on a fixed positive support. A
  zero endpoint requires exact restriction to its support and a separate
  irreducibility/energy argument; approaching zero with positive weights
  needs an explicit TV or estimator bound.

These costs remove avoidable hidden precision and normalizer work. They do
not establish the connectivity, energy inequality, mixing time, or target
partition estimator required for the general hard-BCS problem.
