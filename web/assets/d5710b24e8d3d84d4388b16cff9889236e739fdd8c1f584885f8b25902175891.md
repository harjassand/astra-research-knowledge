# Materials and energy theory — cycle-two synthesis

## Outcome

Eight specialized studies were run across ionic transport, phase nucleation, disordered homogenization, mechanical metamaterials, thermoelectric conversion, inverse materials design, dynamic materials, and many-body certification. The strongest completed quantitative result is an exact minimax design theorem for a **restricted one-dimensional iid resistor material**, where a full two-terminal boundary-response measurement identifies the number of high-conductivity cells. The most actionable systems result is an **acquired finite-horizon safety certificate** for a fixed reciprocal finite-volume reaction-diffusion graph, with time-uniform rate intervals feeding exact box-face checks. An independent battery model gives an exact collective-conductivity versus tracer-only Nernst–Einstein gap on a finite SSEP ring.

These are conditional model theorems and computational checks. No real material was measured, no DFT or molecular-dynamics fit was run, and no result is priority-cleared. The large scientific unknowns remain explicit in the linked reports.

## Best completed capabilities

**Restricted disorder-design theorem.** For iid two-phase one-dimensional series resistors with conductivities (1,K), cell length ℓ, and high-phase fraction (p\in[1/4,3/4]), the complete coupon Dirichlet-to-Neumann map identifies the high-cell count if resistance error is below the stated rounding threshold. With (M) independent cells, the minimax absolute error for the homogenized conductivity (A(p)=(1-(1-1/K)p)^{-1}) is between (D_K/(82\sqrt M)) and (D_K/(2\sqrt M)); the high-confidence count is (\Theta(D_K^2\varepsilon^{-2}\log(1/\delta))). This is a full-response sample-complexity result for that exact 1D model. The requested (d\ge2) lower bound for strict finite-range fields and complete local boundary maps remains `UNKNOWN`; infinite-range stripe examples do not prove it. Source upper bounds are conditional on periodization, ellipticity, iid/spectral-gap assumptions, and response accuracy. [Report and proof](disorder_homogenization/CYCLE2_REPORT.md), [claim ledger](disorder_homogenization/CLAIMS_CYCLE2.json), [arithmetic audit](disorder_homogenization/scripts/check_cycle2_design_arithmetic.py).

**Acquired stochastic graph-safety certificate.** Under the fixed reciprocal finite-volume reaction-diffusion model, Gamma-mixture counting-process confidence sequences acquire stationary per-mode rate intervals. Endpoint monotonicity computes conservative reaction margins on every box collar. Subtracting the heterogeneous-volume reciprocal-hop loss (C_{i,s}\ell_q/\alpha_i) gives a certified mode set; the inherited N35 jump-process argument then yields a finite-horizon exit bound that remains valid after pilot-data-dependent stopping and predictable mode switching. A data-dependent deployment gate converts it to joint failure risk at most calibration error δ plus a chosen operational target ε. This uses N35 only; it neither proves nor assumes N33 deterministic permanence. The path from rates to graph safety is model-conditional; it does not statistically establish mass action, reciprocity, complete event observation, or nature-level safety. The worked synthetic pipeline passed arithmetic checks, but its (V\Lambda T) event envelope is too large for brute-force validation. [Proof](lead_reaction_diffusion/PROOF.md), [acquisition report](lead_reaction_diffusion/CYCLE2_REPORT.md), [claims](lead_reaction_diffusion/CLAIMS_CYCLE2.json), [checker](lead_reaction_diffusion/certificate_checker.py), [synthetic end-to-end demo](lead_reaction_diffusion/acquired_graph_demo.py).

**Exact SSEP electrolyte counterexample and estimator.** In two independent periodic one-dimensional hard-core channels carrying (+q) and (-q), with (N) ions per channel and nearest-neighbor vacancy hops at rate γ, the finite-ring collective conductivity is (\sigma_L=\beta q^2a\gamma N(L-N)/(A L(L-1))), while the matched single-particle tracer diffusivity gives a self-only Nernst–Einstein estimate smaller by exactly (N). At fixed density, the latter tends to zero and the collective conductivity stays positive. The total unlike-bond activity is an exact local statistic for collective conductivity in this generator, with a reversible-gap/Chebyshev finite-time bound. This refutes any model-universal inference from same-state tracer diffusivity alone; it does not predict a real battery. Green–Kubo/Einstein–Helfand and correlation corrections to Nernst–Einstein are established prior art. [Report, derivation, and finite checks](battery_transport/CYCLE2_REPORT.md), [claims](battery_transport/CLAIMS_CYCLE2.json), [verifier](battery_transport/verify_cycle2.py).

## Prior art and verification boundary

The disorder report audits Fischer–Neukamm’s RVE fluctuation/bias theorem and Gloria–Neukamm–Otto’s iid discrete-network estimate against primary papers; it explicitly rejects transferring those rates to an arbitrary finite-range material. The transport report cites the established collective-current Green–Kubo/Einstein–Helfand interface and one-dimensional SSEP tagged-particle subdiffusion literature. The safety branch cites Howard et al. for confidence sequences, Stundzia–Lumsden for RDME finite-volume hopping, and Agazzi–Dembo–Eckmann for chemical-jump large deviations; those sources do not by themselves imply the acquired graph-safety composition. The thermoelectric report checks Maassen and Ding et al. for prior boxcar optimization. The inverse-design report checks CAL and GNoME while keeping phase-universe coverage distinct from panel stability. The mechanical source audit uses the full Shaat–Park paper, including its appendix slider description. Detailed source spans and confidence labels are in each linked report.

Validation was limited to internal arithmetic and exact finite fixtures: the SSEP checker covered 44 parameter pairs and 2,024 canonical states; the inverse-design A2B fixture used exact rationals; thermoelectric and many-body scripts reproduced finite illustrative outputs; the acquired-safety checker passed three arithmetic tests and its synthetic end-to-end certificate gate; the disorder script checks its lower/upper sample-count arithmetic. These are not formal proof checking, independent review, physical experiments, DFT validation, or novelty clearance. All priorities and model-to-nature transfers remain `UNKNOWN`.

## Recommended next gate

For a concrete materials result, select one named material and first measure whether the exact model interface is even plausible. In ionic transport, synchronize local occupancy/hop-event histories, tracer motion, and bulk conductivity on the same temperature, loading, and geometry; compare the local-activity predictor to collective-current data and reject the SSEP mechanism on rate heterogeneity or nonlocal interactions. For graph safety, preregister a small-volume held-out test in which pilot-derived intervals and the face-margin gate are frozen before an independent safety run, while independently testing reciprocal hopping and the factorial propensity law. For disordered conductivity, the deepest mathematical target is an estimator-independent (d\ge2) finite-range lower bound under the complete-DtN observation contract. No material should be recommended or certified from this research package alone.
