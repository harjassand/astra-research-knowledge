## Findings and disposition

**No Astra card supplies a reaction-selectivity mechanism.** The nearest mechanism worth carrying forward is U/N76’s exact falling-factorial potential for the embedded stochastic reaction-event chain. For each enabled jump, its supplied summary states

\[
F(x-y+y')-F(x)\leq \log\!\frac{M(x)}{x^{\underline y}},
\]

and says a currently maximizing available source cannot increase `F`, including at molecule-count boundaries. At fixed positive rates it reports an exponential upper-tail bound in *event count*. It does not provide negative drift, a physical-time nonexplosion/recurrence bound, an intervention policy, a product-yield ratio, or selectivity. Fast cycles can preserve `F`; controlling their transitions and holding-time costs is explicitly the open step. The underlying U technical note/code/log packet was not supplied, so this remains a source-reported summary, not a reconstructed proof. [N76 card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N76-factorial-reaction-boundary.txt) · [U availability and scope](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/web/U-sources.txt) · [supplied U summary, especially §§2–3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/web/pages/d-14513a2bef6ac3df.html).

The only defensible research direction I found that is distinct from the active H2/TST, DAR, flavin/RF, and generic deterministic-certificate lines is **a cycle-resolved stochastic selectivity problem**: for one explicitly specified finite driven molecular network, ask whether the event-chain boundary correction can be combined with an exact cycle/holding-time reduction to bound a competing-product first-passage or occupation-flux ratio under a stated intervention. N76 supplies only the boundary correction and identifies fast cycles/holding times as the missing gate. This proposal is an inference from the card, not an Astra result, and its relevance to any particular reaction is `UNKNOWN` until a network, propensities, controls, and product observable are fixed. A decisive falsifier would be a small exact network where the proposed cycle quotient changes the predicted branch ratio or loses the event-time bound.

## Excluded or preempted transfers

- **N33 all-endotactic permanence is not a selectivity result.** Its reconstructed theorem assumes a finite actual exponent-arrow diagram, a fixed positive stoichiometric class, supplied global bounds `0 < κ ≤ k_e(t) ≤ K`, and endotacticity under the essential-source convention. It establishes a common compact classwise absorber and path-independent finite entry, not a preferred product, branching fraction, rate optimum, or catalyst/photochemical/electrochemical response. The common-span switching extension allows arbitrary measurable mode weights and gives existence of a positive periodic solution for periodic inputs; it does not establish uniqueness, attraction, or a yield/selectivity law. Its proof package calls itself internally reconstructed and leaves external correctness/formal verification/priority open. Delays, stochastic jumps, and unrestricted forcing are explicitly outside the deterministic theorem. [N33 card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N33-endotactic-permanence.txt) · [full reconstructed source, interface and theorem](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/web/pages/d-326696af15b5e02b.html) (notably source lines 49–132, 694–724, 878–902, 990–1034).
- **N34/N50 are certificate-recognition work, not mechanistic design.** N34 accepts supplied rational diagrams, candidate affine-min functions, boxes, containment witnesses, and finite chains; a local facet certificate does not prove a global basin on an unbounded class, and search exhaustion is `UNKNOWN`. N50 conditionally upgrades the computability gap for promised rational endotactic inputs via rational all-scale families and real-closed-field obligations, but general CAD is unimplemented and no complexity bound is given. Neither finds a selective intervention or predicts a product ratio. [N34](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N34-reaction-certificates.txt) · [N50](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N50-global-reaction-recognizer.txt).
- **N35 gives a separate safety envelope but is still certificate-led and not a selectivity mechanism.** For integer-complex mass-action jumps with predictable bounded controls (not necessarily Markov), it requires a supplied compact positive polytope, strict inward facet collars uniform over every allowed mode, coordinate bounds, and a lattice-compatible initial count. It yields a finite-volume exit estimate through an exponentially long finite horizon. It does not prove infinite survival, nonexplosion, ergodicity, stochastic permanence, stationary product fractions, or optimal selectivity. The source explicitly leaves certificate-acquisition and physical control/energy costs outside the theorem. [N35 card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N35-controlled-chemical-safety.txt) · [proof appendix and exact input/conclusion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/web/pages/d-326696af15b5e02b.html) (lines 1093–1155, 1165–1238, 1297–1319).
- **N77 blocks a tempting deterministic-to-stochastic transfer.** Its supplied summary gives one deterministic stoichiometric class with a common complex-balanced attractor but infinitely many positive-recurrent stochastic classes whose stationary laws escape every common compact set. This refutes classwise-uniform stationary tightness, not componentwise positive recurrence. The complete proof/code/log packet is unavailable, so use it as a source-reported counterexample scope warning. [N77 card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N77-stationary-tightness-counterexample.txt) · [U summary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/web/pages/d-14513a2bef6ac3df.html) (lines 134–193).
- **N54 inherits N33’s permanence mechanism** and concerns common scalar diffusion/mean-class mixing, not molecular product selection. **N58 is not a catalytic-kinetics result:** it is a three-gain parameter-dimension/minimality result for a seven-coordinate pullback and disclaims a new global Landau/nonuniqueness or hydrodynamic theorem. [N54](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N54-spatial-permanence.txt) · [N58](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/8ede33c2f09a8abe07b430892b3ac4bb71c27c83/cards/N58-three-gain-kinetics.txt).
