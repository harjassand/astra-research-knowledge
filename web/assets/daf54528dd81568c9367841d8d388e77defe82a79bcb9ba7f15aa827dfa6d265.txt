STRONGEST CLAIM AT THIS CHECKPOINT

Assume M is a connected smooth closed Riemannian 3-manifold, X is a smooth
nowhere-zero divergence-free vector field, phi_t is its flow, mu is normalized
Riemannian volume, H=h_mu(phi_1)>0 uses natural logarithms, and
S=Def X=(nabla X+(nabla X)^*)/2. If

                 integral_M |S|^2 dVol = 2 Vol(M) H^2,                 (1)

then, after a cover of degree at most four, the two strain eigenline bundles
patch to global smooth unit fields U_+, U_-. The speed r=|X| is constant,
and with e=X/r there is a constant beta such that

       [e,U_+] = -(H/r) U_+,
       [e,U_-] =  (H/r) U_-,
       [U_+,U_-] = beta e.                                          (2)

The cover is a compact quotient Gamma\G by a cocompact lattice in the simply
connected Lie group with this algebra: G is Sol if beta=0, and the universal
cover of PSL(2,R) if beta is nonzero. Thus the equality frame patches globally
on a finite cover; there is no residual varying bracket coefficient. In the
nonzero-beta case e is the Reeb field of the contact form e-flat; in the
zero-beta case that form is closed.

This is an equality classification, not a near-equality theorem. It assumes
the sharp entropy–strain inequality and its Ruelle import stated below. The
global quotient conclusion is derived here by integrating the resulting
constant-bracket coframe; it is not a claim of external novelty.

INDEPENDENT DERIVATION

Put r=|X|, e=X/r, P=I-e tensor e, q=<Se,e>, b=PSe, and let B=PSP on the
normal plane e-perp. Since div X=0, tr B=-q. The trace-free part of B has
eigenvalues +s,-s for a smooth s>=0. Orthogonal block decomposition gives

        |S|^2 = q^2 + 2|b|^2 + |B|^2
              = 2s^2 + (3/2)q^2 + 2|b|^2.                          (3)

Here q=e(r)=X(log r). Define the normal cocycle
Q_t=P_(phi_t x) Dphi_t|_(e(x)-perp). It is invertible and obeys the cocycle
law because Dphi_t preserves the X-line and the final projection kills that
line. For v_t=Dphi_t v_0 write v_t=w_t+a_t e, w_t=P v_t. The variational
equation is nabla_X v_t=nabla_(v_t) X. Also

        nabla_X e = P nabla_e X,

because X=r e and q=e(r). Projecting the variational equation onto e-perp
therefore cancels the two a_t terms and yields

        P nabla_X w_t = P nabla_(w_t) X.

Consequently d(log|w_t|)/dt=<S w_t,w_t>/|w_t|^2. The largest eigenvalue of
B is s-q/2, so

 log ||Q_t(x)|| <= integral_0^t s(phi_u x)du
                       -(1/2)log(r(phi_t x)/r(x)).                  (4)

Since r is bounded above and below, the endpoint term is uniformly bounded.
The X-line exponent is zero: Dphi_t X=X o phi_t. Its one-dimensional
Jacobian is r(phi_t x)/r(x), so the normal determinant has reciprocal
Jacobian; the two normal exponents are chi(x),-chi(x), chi>=0. Oseledets,
Birkhoff applied to s_1(x)=integral_0^1 s(phi_u x)du, and (4) give

        H <= integral chi dmu <= integral s dmu.

The first inequality is Ruelle's entropy inequality for a C1 map of a
compact manifold. Cauchy–Schwarz and (3) give

        H^2 <= (integral s dmu)^2 <= integral s^2 dmu
             <= (1/2) integral |S|^2 dmu.                           (5)

The stationary strain-viscosity equation
nabla_X X - 2 nu div S + grad p = F on closed M has
W=int <F,X> dVol=2 nu int |S|^2 dVol, by incompressibility, pressure
orthogonality, and integration by parts. Thus W>=4 nu Vol(M) H^2. The same
calculation retains the deficit

  Delta := W/(4 nu Vol(M))-H^2
       >= Var_mu(s)+(3/4)E_mu(q^2)+E_mu(|b|^2).                      (6)

Indeed W/(4 nu Vol(M))=E_mu(s^2)+(3/4)E_mu(q^2)+E_mu(|b|^2), and
E_mu(s^2)-H^2=Var_mu(s)+(E_mu s)^2-H^2 >= Var_mu(s) by (5).
For equality in (1), (5)–(6) imply q=b=0 and s=H everywhere, by continuity.
Thus SX=0 and S has simple eigenvalues H,-H on e-perp. The identity

        2<SX,Y> = <nabla_X X,Y> + (1/2)Y(|X|^2)

shows that the Lie derivative L_X g vanishes when one argument is X.
Therefore for v_t=Dphi_t v_0,
d/dt <v_t,X(phi_t x)>=2<Sv_t,X>=0, and N=X-perp is an invariant smooth
subbundle. In N the derivative cocycle has exponents chi,-chi; (4) and s=H
give chi<=H a.e., while (5) gives integral chi=H. Hence chi=H a.e.

Let L_x be the measurable positive Oseledets line in N and set
A(x)=<S v,v>/|v|^2 for any nonzero v in L_x. For each fixed t, the maps
phi_t and phi_1 commute and have bounded derivative and inverse on compact M;
they preserve the simple Oseledets line almost everywhere. Along it,
d(log|Dphi_t v|)/dt=A(phi_t x), so invariance of mu gives

        integral [log(|Dphi_n v|/|v|)/n] dmu = integral A dmu.

The left side tends to H by Oseledets. It is bounded in absolute value by H,
since the eigenvalues of S are H,-H,0, so dominated convergence gives
integral A=H. As A<=H, equality forces L_x=E_+(x) almost everywhere, where
E_+,E_- are the smooth H and -H eigenline bundles of S|N. Full support and
continuity then make E_+ invariant everywhere under every phi_t. If v_+ and
v_- start in E_+ and E_-, respectively, they remain normal and

        d/dt <Dphi_t v_+,Dphi_t v_->
               =2<S Dphi_t v_+,Dphi_t v_->
               =2H <Dphi_t v_+,Dphi_t v_->.

An initially zero pairing stays zero, proving invariance of E_-; their norms
scale exactly by exp(+Ht) and exp(-Ht). This is a uniform Anosov splitting.

Every smooth first integral f (Xf=0) is constant: invariance of E_+ and
contraction under negative time make df vanish on E_+; positive time does the
same on E_-; Xf=0 handles the flow direction. These spaces span TM. Since
q=X(log r)=0, log r is a smooth first integral, hence r is constant.

Take the joint orientation cover of E_+,E_- and a connected component. Its
degree over M is at most four and it is compact. On it choose global unit
sections U_+,U_-. Exact derivative scaling and continuity in t give
[X,U_+]=-H U_+, [X,U_-]=H U_-. Write
[U_+,U_-]=c X+d U_+ + f U_- with smooth coefficients. Jacobi gives
[X,[U_+,U_-]]=0, hence

        Xc=0,       Xd=H d,       Xf=-H f.

The cover is compact and X is complete, so boundedness along all positive and
negative times forces d=f=0. The first-integral argument forces c constant.
Dividing by r gives (2) with beta=cr. This explicitly resolves the patching
question: line orientations are the only finite-cover obstruction, and after
removing it the bracket data are global constants.

For beta!=0 set h=-2e/(H/r), E=U_+, F=-2U_-/((H/r)beta). Then
[h,E]=2E, [h,F]=-2F, [E,F]=h, the standard split sl_2(R) relations. For
beta=0, span(U_+,U_-) is abelian and e acts diagonally with rates +/-H/r,
the Sol algebra. If alpha=e-flat, then d alpha(e,U_+)=d alpha(e,U_-)=0 and
d alpha(U_+,U_-)=-beta. Thus alpha is contact and e is its Reeb field for
beta!=0; d alpha=0 for beta=0.

GLOBAL INTEGRATION (CLOSING THE PATCHING GATE)

The orthonormal global frame (e,U_+,U_-) has constant brackets. Let g be the
simply connected Lie group with that Lie algebra and the left-invariant metric
making this basis orthonormal. Its Maurer–Cartan form omega and the dual
coframe theta on the universal cover of the equality cover satisfy the same
equations d theta + (1/2)[theta wedge theta]=0. Since the source is simply
connected, the Frobenius integration of this equation gives a developing map
dev with dev*omega=theta. It is a local isometry. The source is complete
(cover of compact M), and a left-invariant Riemannian metric on g is complete;
therefore dev is a covering. Both spaces are simply connected, so dev is a
global isometry. Deck transformations preserve theta and hence correspond to
left translations on g. The equality cover is Gamma\g, with Gamma discrete;
compactness makes it cocompact. This proves the global quotient statement
without relying on the Anosov-classification literature.

CONDITIONAL ROBUST COMPUTATIONAL-COST BRIDGE

There is a precise sufficient input for turning a robust bit stream into the
entropy required by (5). Let T=phi_tau for a physical instruction interval
tau>0. Suppose A is T-invariant with mu(A)=p>0, and under conditional volume
mu_A the readout rho:A->[m] has length-n word law P_n satisfying

        limsup_(n->infinity) TV(P_n, Unif([m]^n)) <= delta < 1.

With TV equal to half the l1 distance, entropy continuity on m^n words gives
H(P_n)>=n log(m)-TV(P_n,Unif) n log(m)-h2(TV(P_n,Unif)); hence the
readout process has entropy rate at least (1-delta)log(m). It is a factor of
(A,mu_A,T), so h_mu_A(T)>=(1-delta)log(m). Entropy decomposes over the
invariant sets A and A-complement, giving h_mu(T)>=p(1-delta)log(m).
Since h_mu(phi_tau)=tau h_mu(phi_1), the strain-viscosity theorem implies

        W >= 4 nu Vol(M) [p(1-delta) log(m)/tau]^2.                (7)

This is a robust distributional condition, not a consequence of universality
or finite-precision decoding alone. The mixture (1-epsilon)*delta_(all-0)
+ epsilon*Bernoulli(m) on the full shift has every finite word in its support,
but entropy rate only epsilon log(m), tending to zero. Thus mere
realizability of all finite words, even with positive probability, has no
uniform work consequence. To use (7), p and the block-law bound must be
certified from the actual physical encoding. No acquisition protocol for
those infinite-time statistics or actuator implementation is supplied.
