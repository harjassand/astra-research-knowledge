## Main theorem: interface and quantifiers

The claim is, in the correct order:

1. Fix any finite endotactic labelled diagram in dimension $d\geq1$, any supplied $0<\kappa\leq K<\infty$, and any positive class $P=(c+S)\cap\mathbb R^d_{>0}$.
2. Acquire one nonempty compact convex $\mathcal K_P\subset P$ using those data, before selecting an initial point or a rate path.
3. For every measurable rate path in the interval and every $x^0\in P$, there is a unique global positive Carathéodory solution. It enters $\mathcal K_P$ within a finite bound depending on $x^0$ and the supplied data, and independent of the rate path.
4. Its eventual coordinate bounds are independent of both the initial point and the rate path, within this fixed class.

No single entry bound for all initial states in an unbounded class is claimed. No bound uniform over different positive classes is claimed. Rates may be changed on null sets. A uniform entry bound on each compact initial subset of $P$ also follows by choosing one initial scale for that compact set, but this stronger corollary is not needed for the stated theorem. The argument is also uniform under arbitrary starting-time shifts, since shifting an admissible rate path retains the same interval.

Empty edge sets, repeated labelled edges, and self-loops are covered. In the zero-span case every velocity is zero and the class is a singleton; the manuscript handles it separately. The empty-edge source budget is explicitly $M=0$, and the auxiliary drift condition is automatic when $L=0$.

## Proof audit

### Affine lemma and global plateau

The full affine proof preserves the source lemma's sequential interface: the slope family is fixed before $h$, the tolerance may be discontinuous, and approximating exponent vectors may approach lower-dimensional cubes from outside. The local induction uses strict limiting exponent gaps, the type-$k$ comparison bounds raised coordinates below by $\beta_k$, and type-$k+1$ competitors bound unraised coordinates above. The closed limiting-active sets justify the compact bad-set separation without regularity of the tolerance. Shrinking the local radii also secures the offset decay needed for gluing.

The finite interval subcover, deletion of contained intervals, ordering of endpoints, overlapping consecutive intervals, interior cuts, and offset cancellations retain the required strict inequalities. The endpoint slice $t=b$, dimension-one case, ties, and negative $a$ are explicitly covered. I found no hidden assumption of chemical/integer complexes or weak reversibility in this part.

Including coordinate vectors in $D$ forces every label active at the sufficiently deep class representative to have exactly zero slope. Its constant value is a global upper bound for $F_h$. The zero-label activity estimate puts the plateau portion inside $B_h$ into $\operatorname{int}B_{\sqrt h}$. Convexity and the segment from the representative exclude any additional plateau points outside $B_h$. This establishes a compact positive global plateau rather than merely its intersection with a box.

### Weighted endotactic drift

For each active $r\notin S^\perp$, select a minimum-$r$ source among reactions with nonzero $r\cdot\nu$. Correct endotacticity applied to $-r$ makes all essential edges at that source tier positive. Every negative edge therefore has a strictly higher source tier. The finite comparison family contains the corresponding source differences and all nonzero reaction vectors, so the proof obtains

\[
x^{y_e}\leq h^H x^{y_*}\quad(r\cdot\nu_e<0),\qquad
r\cdot\nu_{e_*}\geq2H.
\]

The projection factors are accounted for by $L=\sum_e\|\nu_e\|_1$ and $\|r\|_\infty\leq1$. Consequently

\[
r\cdot f\geq x^{y_*}(2\kappa H-KLh^H)
\geq\kappa Hh^M=\delta_h>0
\]

when $KLh^H\leq\kappa H$. The coefficient budget controls the entire negative sum; the proof does not incorrectly compare only unweighted reaction fluxes. It uses the supplied two-sided rate interval pointwise and retains a rate-independent strict margin.

### Real powers, measurable controls, and continuation

For any real source exponent, including negative coordinates, $x^y$ is smooth on the positive domain. On $B_h$, writing $x=h^p$ gives $p\cdot y\leq\|y\|_1$ and hence $x^y\geq h^{\|y\|_1}$. Thus the absolute source norm budget $M$ is the correct one; the proof does not substitute the sum of source coordinates or assume nonnegative exponents.

The finite affine minimum composed with an absolutely continuous trajectory is absolutely continuous. At almost every differentiability time its derivative equals the minimum of the active affine derivatives. This includes all minimizers at a tie and justifies integrating the pointwise velocity inequalities for measurable rates. The source's smooth all-times right-derivative step is not silently reused.

Labels in $S^\perp$ cannot be active below the plateau in the fixed class. Strict drift therefore gives finite box-conditional entry. Starting in a plateau yields compact positive trapping and Carathéodory continuation; this avoids any boundary singularity from negative exponents. Every initial point belongs to a sufficiently deep plateau. The capped square-root scale sequence then reaches the class-fixed final scale in finitely many transitions. Only containment in the next box is needed; the proof never assumes that successive plateaux are nested.

## Switching and spatial compatibility

For switching/convex mixing, the common diagram span $S_j=S$ is exactly the hypothesis ensuring that each mode has a nonempty essential set for every active $r\notin S^\perp$. A union comparison family and common $L,M,H$ yield the same $\delta_h$ for every mode. Convex weights preserve it because their sum is one. No dwell time, lower bound on a particular weight, or requirement that all union edges stay active is used. The different-span counterexample correctly rules out replacing this hypothesis by the span of the union.

Without a common span, the separate compact-initial-range result uses only nonnegative projected drift and claims common invariance. It does not claim classwide absorption.

The transport corollary is also an invariance result for a supplied compact initial range. Its finite product minimum uses

\[
r_\lambda\cdot\sum_w m_{zw}(x_w-x_z)
=\sum_w m_{zw}(L_\lambda(x_w)-L_\lambda(x_z))\geq0
\]

at a jointly active compartment/label. The scalar coupling coefficient must be common to all species; nonnegative measurable locally bounded coefficients and a finite compartment count give the required local existence and continuation. The same one-compartment plateau works for every such finite topology. No classwise absorption from arbitrary configurations, convergence rate, species-dependent transport, infinite-compartment limit, or PDE theorem follows from this corollary.

Individual compartment stoichiometric classes are not asserted to be invariant. They would require compatible affine classes across compartments; the spatial corollary instead traps states in the common ambient positive plateau, so it does not need that additional assumption.

## Further consequences and scope witness

The compact convex class set gives a continuous time-$T$ map for periodic measurable rates and hence a positive periodic solution by Brouwer and uniqueness. This proves existence, with no entrainment, uniqueness, or attraction assertion. For an autonomous field, fixed points of the time-$1/n$ maps and the uniform small-time expansion produce an equilibrium; a single positive-period fixed point would not suffice, and the manuscript uses the correct argument.

The added three-species network

\[
2A\to2B\to2C\to2A,\qquad4A\rightleftarrows6B,\qquad A+B\to2B
\]

passes the advertised structural tests. If its added edge has positive $w$-projection, writing $w=(a,b,c)$ gives $b>a$ and $a+b<2b$. Either $2B$ is an essential cycle source when $c\ne b$, or $2C$ is one at the same level when $c=b$. The added outward edge cannot be at the maximal essential source tier. Negative projections point inward; zero projections are inessential. The cycle/reversible base is weakly reversible and endotactic. The displayed determinant is 8, proving full span. No path returns to $A+B$, and for $w=-(1,1,1)$ all overall maximal sources have degree two and only neutral outgoing edges. Thus it is neither weakly reversible nor strongly endotactic. The paragraph correctly restricts its prior-art conclusion to the cited hypotheses and makes no assertion about all alternate realizations.

## Certificate boundary

The certificate report and main manuscript are consistent:

- The implemented input interface is narrower: nonnegative rational complexes, rational class data, and rational bounds. It does not cover arbitrary negative or uncomputable real exponents.
- Rationalizing a fixed-scale real family must keep formerly orthogonal slopes in the rational subspace $S^\perp$. The report does this and uses strict inactivity gaps to prevent new active labels at locations with insufficient derivative margin. Compactness makes the common perturbation argument valid.
- An accepted local certificate proves compact positive invariance and entry conditional on remaining in its verified outer box. It does not certify that every point of an unbounded class eventually stays in that box.
- Exhaustive rational candidate search is conditionally complete for safety containing a supplied compact rational initial set. Its finite resource truncations may return `UNKNOWN`; no practical runtime bound is supplied.
- A finite checked chain with its first plateau containing the supplied initial polytope and each plateau strictly inside the next outer box proves eventual residence in the last plateau for that supplied initial set. This is not one finite certificate for every initial point of an arbitrary unbounded class.
- Independently establishing the classwide permanence theorem yields existence of a rational globally absorbing plateau: trajectories eventually stay in the original final box, where the rationalized plateau has box-entry drift. This existential argument does not make the first locally accepted candidate recognizable as globally absorbing.
- The finite bounded-class option separately proves entire-class containment and consuming source exponents at least one. Its Grönwall boundary-avoidance argument is sound for that narrower interface and is not substituted into the general real-power proof.

The report's scalar multistability example correctly refutes the inference from inward facets to global absorption. Its integer and fractional worked examples are within known smaller structural regimes and are identified as such. The checker’s exact arithmetic, vertex enumeration, termwise worst-rate selection, outward rational root bounds, and finite-chain containment logic are consistent with the claims made, but this is source inspection rather than a formal verification of the program.

The remaining algorithmic gap is explicit: no finite rational all-scale representation and no complete finite recognizer of classwide global absorption on arbitrary unbounded classes has been established. This gap does not invalidate the written mathematical permanence argument, and the mathematical argument does not erase this gap.
