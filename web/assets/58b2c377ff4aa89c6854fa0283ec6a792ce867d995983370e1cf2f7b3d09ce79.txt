1. INDEPENDENT RECONSTRUCTION OF THE OBSERVABLE INTERFACE

For bounded future cylinders, including time0, write

 B(F,G)=E[F(reversed past)G(future)].

Reflection positivity and finite rank give the real Hilbert quotient H.
Rational future-cylinder classes span H: finite-alphabet stochastic continuity
makes real-time cylinders ordinary-L2 limits of rational ones, and
|B(F,G)|<=||F||_ordinary-L2 ||G||_ordinary-L2. In finite dimension the rational
linear span is closed, so finitely many rational cylinders already form an
exact basis. Orthogonalizing that basis uses fixed coefficients, independent
of every approximating model.

The shift contractivity proof in v1 is sound. For h(s)=B(U_sF,U_sF), symmetry
of the shift gives h(s)=B(F,U_(2s)F), hence

 h(s)^2<=h(0)h(2s),   0<=h(s)<=E[F^2].

Iteration yields h(s)<=h(0); a null vector is therefore also shift-null.
Strong continuity follows from stochastic continuity on cylinders. The
finite-dimensional semigroup is T_t=exp(-tA), A=A^*>=0, and A Omega=0.
Boundary multiplication by a label indicator is a self-adjoint projection
E_a; the projections are mutually orthogonal and sum to I. These assertions
use the reflection boundary and do not infer an ambient Markov lattice.

The word formula follows by nesting E_a and T_t from the rightmost event.
Consequently an isometric feature embedding j with

 j Omega=1, j E_a=D_a j, Q j=-j A

reconstructs all word probabilities, not just two-time covariances. This is
the load-bearing sufficiency step of the exact frame theorem.

2. WHY UNBOUNDED-DIMENSION FEATURES REMAIN COMPACT

Assume for contradiction that reversible finite outputs P_n converge to P on
all rational cylinders. Remove stationary zero-mass states from each model;
reducibility of the remaining chain is harmless. Let f_1,...,f_r be fixed
bounded rational future cylinders representing an orthonormal target basis
e_1,...,e_r. Each f_l is a fixed finite linear combination of indicators.
For hidden state i in model n define

 x_n(i)_l=E_n[f_l|X0=i],
 (j_n v)(i)=sum_l v_l x_n(i)_l.

Every coordinate is bounded by ||f_l||_infinity. Thus ALL feature distributions
live in one fixed compact box K in R^r, regardless of hidden state count,
transition rates, spectral gaps, activities, or tiny stationary masses.
This compactness concerns feature values, not hidden generators or states.

For a fixed rational cylinder F, set v_F=[F] and
f_v=sum_l (v_F)_l f_l. The target reflection norm of F-f_v is zero. Its
reflection square in model n is a finite linear combination of rational
observed-cylinder probabilities and therefore tends to zero. Reversibility
and conditional independence of past/future given X0 imply exactly

 B_n(F-f_v,F-f_v)
   =||E_n[F|X0]-j_n v_F||^2_(L2(pi_n)).                 (R1)

This equality includes boundary events because the output at time0 is a
deterministic function of X0. It is an equilibrium identity, not a generic
identity for a nonreversible hidden process.

For the constant, boundary product, and shifted representative f_v, (R1)
gives

 ||j_n Omega-1||_2 ->0,
 ||j_n E_a v-D_a^n j_n v||_2 ->0,
 ||j_n T_t v-exp(t Q_n)j_n v||_2 ->0                 (R2)

for each fixed v and each fixed rational t. The last identity uses
E_n[U_t f_v|X0]=exp(tQ_n)E_n[f_v|X0] before taking limits. No derivative of
Q_n appears here. Since the space is finite-dimensional, convergence on the
fixed basis is enough for each fixed v.

Let mu_n be the joint equilibrium law of feature x_n and finite visible label.
Compactness gives a subsequential limit mu. Gram convergence gives
int xx^T dmu=I. The first two equations of (R2) imply normalization and label
block membership almost surely. For a nonnegative cylinder F,

 int (min(0,<x,v_F>))^2 dmu_n
   <=||j_n v_F-E_n[F|X0]||_2^2 ->0.

The negative part is continuous on K. Thus <x,v_F>>=0 mu-almost surely. A
countable collection of rational word indicators suffices: any nonnegative
rational cylinder function is a nonnegative combination of the atoms of its
finite visible partition. Their countable intersection gives all cone
inequalities. Therefore mu belongs to Gamma.

There is no loss of probability to arbitrarily many rare states: mu_n is a
probability law on a fixed compact box. There is no assumption that the
approximating features lie in the target D before the limit. Their convergence
into D follows from (R1)-(R2), rather than being imposed.

3. FIXED-LAG REVERSIBLE COUPLINGS AND THE COMMON-GENERATOR LIMIT

For each FIXED rational t>0, push the stationary hidden two-time law through
x_n to obtain kappa_n,t on K x K. Detailed balance makes this coupling
symmetric, and its two marginals are mu_n. A subsequence converges to a
symmetric kappa_t with marginals mu. A countable diagonal choice is possible
for all rational t; the concrete exclusion below needs only one lag.

For bounded continuous f on K, the Markov property and (R2) imply

 int f(x)<y,v> dkappa_t
   =int f(x)<x,T_t v> dmu.                              (R3)

The prelimit difference is bounded by ||f||_infinity times the L2 error in
(R2). All integrands are continuous and bounded. This avoids dependence on
hidden dimension and avoids any limit of exp(tQ_n) as an operator on a
changing hidden Hilbert space.

If every Gamma measure is supported on one finite set S={x_i}, continuous
functions can isolate each x_i. Self-adjointness of T_t turns (R3) into

 sum_j kappa_t(i,j)x_j=pi_i T_t x_i.                     (R4)

Every pairwise monotone field Z_i consequently satisfies

 sum_i pi_i<Z_i,x_i-T_t x_i>
  =1/2 sum_ij kappa_t(i,j)<Z_i-Z_j,x_i-x_j>>=0.           (R5)

Here pi_i=mu({x_i}); zero-weight points can be discarded. Dividing (R5) by
t and taking rational t down to0 differentiates ONLY the fixed target T_t.
It gives the finite Farkas inequalities for symmetric nonnegative flows.

An independent direct construction verifies the same conclusion. The target
covariance and (R4) give

 sum_ij kappa_t(i,j)||x_i-x_j||^2
   =2 tr(I-T_t)<=2t tr(A).                              (R6)

If distinct S points have minimum separation d_min>0, every off-diagonal
kappa_t(i,j)/t is bounded by 2 tr(A)/d_min^2. Along a small-rational-lag
subsequence these finitely many entries converge to symmetric w_ij>=0.
Subtract pi_i x_i from (R4), divide by t, and take the limit:

 sum_j w_ij(x_j-x_i)=-pi_i A x_i.                        (R7)

The finite generator Q_ij=w_ij/pi_i and Q_ii=-sum_j Q_ij is genuinely
reversible. Its feature embedding is isometric by the covariance, intertwines
boundary labels, and obeys Qj=-jA. Hence it realizes EVERY target word by the
word formula. This proves the general closure-to-exact-realization direction,
including its claimed <=|S| state bound, without importing a hidden rate
bound. Equality on rational words extends to all real times by stochastic
continuity of the target and constructed finite CTMC.

Fast transitions between near-identical prelimit features are not excluded
arbitrarily. They can survive before the limit, but their compact limit has
only the finitely many forced features. Transitions inside duplicate feature
classes are invisible; transitions between distinct limiting features satisfy
(R3)-(R7). This is why duplicate states or arbitrarily fast modes do not evade
the proof.

4. COORDINATE AUDIT: EUCLIDEAN FIELD PAIRING IS LEGITIMATE

The concrete certificate uses a nonorthonormal function basis phi, evaluation
rows xi_i, Gram M, and coefficient generator L defined by QH=HL. Here

 h_v(i)=xi_i v,  <v,w>_H=v^T M w,
 h_(T_t v)(i)=xi_i exp(tL)v.

Choose any invertible C with C^T M C=I. Orthogonal feature columns are
x_i=C^T xi_i^T. A certificate field in evaluation coordinates Z_i is dual:
choose its orthogonal field z_i so C z_i=Z_i. Then

 z_i dot(x_i-x_j)=Z_i dot(xi_i-xi_j),
 z_i dot[x_i-T_t x_i]
   =Z_i dot[xi_i-xi_i exp(tL)].

Thus the concrete monotonicity and drift pairings are invariant under the
coordinate change. No extra M in the certificate pairing is missing. M is
already accounted for by the dual transformation of the field. It would be
incorrect to interpret xi_i simultaneously as Riesz coefficients and to
keep the same primal field across that change; v2 does not need that error.

5. EXACT STATIC DOMAIN AND FORCING IN THE TEN-STATE EXAMPLE

Normalization and boundary projection membership give evaluation coordinates

 label0: xi=(1,v_x,v_y,0^5),
 label k+1: xi=(0,0,0,e_k).

At a label0 point, positivity of the rational future event Y_t=k+1 gives
xi exp(tL)e_k>=0. Divide by rational t and let t down to0 in the fixed finite
coefficient matrix. Because xi e_k=0, its limit is eta l_k(v). Therefore
every internal point in D obeys all five affine facet inequalities.

The ordered distinct unit-circle vertices form a convex pentagon, and these
five inward edge halfplanes have exactly that intersection. Conversely each
actual hidden-state evaluation vertex evaluates every nonnegative future
cylinder as its actual nonnegative conditional probability. The evaluation
is affine in v, so every convex combination of internal vertices also lies
in D. Each marker point is an actual conditional-probability vector. Hence

 D=(internal pentagon) disjoint union (five marker points).           (R8)

This exact domain identity does not rely on unknown future positivity outside
the facets: positivity on all vertices and linearity supply the converse.

In evaluation coordinates, the frame covariance becomes E[xi xi^T]=M. Its
normalization also gives E[xi]=the supplied mean, by applying the covariance
to the constant vector. In particular,

 Pr(label0)=1/2,
 E[1_label0 ||v||^2]=M_xx+M_yy=1/2.

Every pentagon point has ||v||^2<=1. Strict convexity shows equality only at
one of its vertices: a proper convex combination of distinct unit vectors
has norm strictly below1. Therefore the nonnegative deficit
1_label0(1-||v||^2) has expectation0 and vanishes almost surely. All internal
frame mass is on the five supplied vertices. The five marker points are
already forced separately.

The moments (1,x,y,x^2,xy) form a five-by-five coefficient matrix with exact
nonzero determinant

 -15163556658355961856/10160806025773960625.

They determine the five internal masses uniquely. The supplied uniform
weights solve the equations; hence all internal and marker atoms have
unconditional mass1/10. Gamma is therefore the singleton uniform ten-atom
measure, not merely a family supported somewhere near those points. Hidden
state duplications can only split these masses.

6. INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE ALL-WORD CP CONSTRUCTION

Q leaves H0 invariant, its restriction L is self-adjoint in Gram M, and the
label projections reduce H0. In orthogonal decomposition H0 plus H0^perp,

 Q=[[L,B],[0,C]],  Q^*=[[L,0],[B^*,C^*]].

Thus Pi Q^*=L Pi and Pi commutes with every label projection. Repeatedly
projecting the backward-word recursion gives Pi h_F^(Q^*)=h_F^Q, while every
forward-word conditional h_F^Q lies in H0. Given the hidden state at time0,
past and future are conditionally independent. Consequently

 B(F,G)=<h_F^(Q^*),h_G^Q>_pi
       =<h_F^Q,h_G^Q>_pi
       =(1/10)sum_i h_F^Q(i)h_G^Q(i).                   (R9)

These are genuine conditional probabilities, so every finite reflected word
kernel is CP under one common ten-atom factorization. This proof never claims
pointwise equality of backward and forward hidden conditional probabilities.
Taking adjoints of the word product on H0 proves ordinary time reversal of
the entire observed law.

Reachability is also complete. Boundary marker indicators are observable.
Their future conditional probabilities have derivative at0 equal to eta l_k
on the internal block plus a known marker-diagonal term. Subtracting that
term yields the five affine facets. Their coefficient rank is3, so they span
the internal affine coordinates. The finite-dimensional span of rational
future words is closed and contains these derivative limits. It is therefore
exactly H0, giving reflection rank8 rather than just an upper bound.

For the concrete theorem, no separate global-CP gluing result is necessary:
(R9) already provides an actual common factor and proves Gamma nonempty. The
general auxiliary statement in v2 that all finite reflection CP kernels imply
a static Gamma measure can also be reconstructed by compactness: refine
finite word lists to a common visible partition; normalize any CP factor by
its constant coordinate; enforce exact quotient relations on that finite
list; bounded normalized basis features have a weak subsequential limit as
the countable word list grows. Zero reflection norm enforces the quotient
relations pointwise, and boundary disjointness forces one-hot labels. This
static construction is compatible with, but not needed to rescue, the main
ten-state counterexample.

7. THE FIXED-LAG CONTRADICTION REALLY EXCLUDES THE WHOLE CLOSURE

The exact certificate field satisfies all ten internal-internal pairings
strictly positively, with minimum

 54355124699/139080625016250 >0.

Its extension to eight-coordinate fields Z_i places z_i dot v_i in all
marker coordinates and puts zero field at markers. The internal-marker
pairing is then identically zero; marker-marker pairings are zero. Hence the
extended field is monotone on every pair of the ten forced feature points.

The internal negative-generator drift is

 -(xi_i L)=(kappa,D(v_i-m)+kappa v_i,-eta l_0,...,-eta l_4).

The marker coordinates of Z_i cancel kappa z_i dot v_i because
eta sum_k l_k=kappa. Marker fields contribute zero. Thus

 J=(1/10)sum_i Z_i dot[-xi_i L]
   =(1/2)[(1/5)sum_(i=0)^4 z_i dot D(v_i-m)] <0,        (R10)

with exactly the negative rational in v2 Eq(9), divided by2.

Any putative reversible cylinder limit has, by (R1)-(R4), a symmetric
fixed-lag coupling on these precise ten points and their precise masses.
Using the dual-coordinate audit, (R5) requires

 F(t)=(1/10)sum_i Z_i dot[xi_i-xi_i exp(tL)]>=0          (R11)

for every rational t>0. But F is an analytic TARGET function, F(0)=0 and
F'(0)=J<0. Therefore F(t)<0 for every sufficiently small positive t. Pick
ONE positive rational t in that interval. This contradicts (R11).

Only after choosing that fixed target lag does one take the approximating
model subsequence. There is no interchange of the n-limit with t-derivatives
and no passage Q_n -> L. An exact nonzero derivative is enough for the
existential closure exclusion. An explicit lag/tolerance would require
quantitative matrix remainder bounds, which v2 accurately leaves to a
separate continuation.

8. DISSIPATION COROLLARY: WHAT THE TOPOLOGY DOES AND DOES NOT SUPPLY

Rational-cylinder laws form a countable-coordinate topology. Being outside
the CLOSED equilibrium set yields a basic neighborhood involving finitely
many word-event probabilities and positive tolerances. Refine these events
to the joint label word on their finite union of times, translate by
stationarity to one window [0,T], and take a smaller positive tolerance
epsilon. Every reversible comparison word law then differs in total
variation by at least epsilon. This finite-window conclusion does not
require a uniform bound on rates or hidden dimension.

For an arbitrary finite all-even exact completion of P, additive
reversibilization R preserves its stationary distribution. Per unordered
edge with flows a,b, its path relative-entropy-rate contribution is

 a log[2a/(a+b)]+b log[2b/(a+b)]
   <=(1/4)(a-b)log(a/b).

The inequality follows by differentiating the even scalar difference after
setting z=(a-b)/(a+b); its derivative is nonnegative for z>=0 because
log[(1+z)/(1-z)]<=2z/(1-z^2). Summing edges gives path KL<=T sigma/4.
Infinite-EPR completions obey the ensuing lower bound trivially. Data
processing to the visible word and Pinsker give

 T sigma/4 >= D(P_word||R_word)>=2 epsilon^2,
 sigma>=8 epsilon^2/T>0.

The supplied irreducible Q has all reverse edges and an asymmetric positive
internal flow, so its EPR is finite and positive. Thus the infimum over all
finite exact completions is indeed in (0,infinity). The separation argument
supplies existence of epsilon,T, not their numerical values or any claim that
the field pairing J equals the minimum physical dissipation.

9. ADVERSARIAL CHECKS AND EVIDENCE LIMITS

Potential escape routes checked analytically:

 - hidden dimension going to infinity: bounded fixed feature coordinates;
 - rates/activity going to infinity: fixed-lag couplings, never Q_n limits;
 - stationary masses tending to zero: compact probability laws of features;
 - duplicate states at one feature: flows aggregate symmetrically;
 - near-duplicate fast states: (R3) still passes at every fixed lag;
 - nonorthogonal coordinates: transform the field dually, as in Section4;
 - different positive frames: (R8) and norm saturation force the same frame;
 - pointwise past/future mismatch: projection, not equality, proves (R9);
 - boundary ambiguity: deterministic label blocks plus normalization;
 - only rational observation times: countability supports compact limits,
   and target analyticity supplies a small rational witness;
 - only two-time matching: the constructed frame generator intertwines all
   boundary projections and the common semigroup, so it matches all words.

Ran the supplied exact checker independently in this Sol context, normally
and with python3 -O; both passed. The checker uses explicit Fraction checks,
not assert statements. This supports the finite rational identities for Q,
H,M,L, stationary flows, rank/facet/determinant data and the field certificate.
It does NOT test compactness, all countably many cylinders, arbitrary hidden
models, a physical dissipation floor, or historical novelty. Those central
analytic assertions are supported here by the reconstruction above, subject
to ordinary independent scrutiny.
