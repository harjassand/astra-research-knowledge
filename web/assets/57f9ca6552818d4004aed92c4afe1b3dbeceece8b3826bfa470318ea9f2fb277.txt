PRIMARY SOURCES CHECKED

Girardeau, “Relationship between Systems of Impenetrable Bosons and
Fermions in One Dimension,” J. Math. Phys. 1 (1960), 516-523,

supports the continuum identification, not the discrete XXZ limit.

scaling at the Bethe/QTM and thermodynamic/correlation level.

J. Stat. Mech. P11017 (2011).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.6224 ; DOI
