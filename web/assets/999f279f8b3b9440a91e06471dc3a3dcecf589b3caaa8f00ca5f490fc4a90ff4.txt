FINAL EPR* DETERMINANT LIFT AND VARIANCE GATE
2026-10-08. Status: internally derived, exact finite checks; no independent external validation, priority clearance, generic sampler, FPRAS or hardness theorem.

Main retained advance
---------------------
Every finite closed critical EPR* gate network has an exact positive auxiliary expectation on a polynomial-size skew matrix. It retains all cycle parity and applies to arbitrary physical graphs, repeated gates and interleavings. Positivity is acquired globally, but polynomial relative variance/mixing is not. The determinant-estimator mechanism is closely related to established Godsil-Gutman/Barvinok ideas; the EPR* specialization and tuned gauge require separate priority review.

THEOREM D: exact global positive expectation
------------------------------------------
Let m four-port gates have nonnegative weights a_v,c_v,d_v, a_v>0, b_v=a_v+c_v+d_v, and uncomplemented matrix
 F_v=[[a,0,0,d],[0,b,c,0],[0,c,b,0],[d,0,0,a]].
Ports are (out_i,out_j,in_i,in_j). The physical closed circuit pairs output and subsequent input ports of every worldline with EQUALITY sewing. Idle qubits give a separate factor2 each.

Define a real skew matrix A_v by its upper entries
 A12=A34=sqrt(d_v/a_v),
 A13=A24=sqrt(b_v/a_v),
 A14=A23=sqrt(c_v/a_v).
Then Pf(A_v)=(d_v-b_v+c_v)/a_v=-1. For every subset S of the four ports,
 F_v(S)=a_v Pf((A_v)_S)^2,
with Pf(empty)=1 and odd principal Pfaffians0. Explicitly: empty/full coefficient a; 12/34 coefficient d; 13/24 coefficient b; 14/23 coefficient c. This is the local squared-Pfaffian identity.

Let E be the external wire matching on all4m ports (2m wire edges). Choose any fixed orientation of each external edge. For z in C^E let K(z) be blockdiag(A_1,...,A_m), plus z_e and -z_e in the two oriented positions of wire e. Overlapping internal and external entries are added.

For independent Rademacher xi_e and arbitrary fixed phases eta_e with |eta_e|=1,
 Z_circuit = (prod_v a_v) E_xi[ |Pf K(eta*xi)|^2 ]
           = (prod_v a_v) E_xi[ |det K(eta*xi)| ].              (D)
When every eta_e=1, K is real skew, detK=PfK^2>=0, so absolute values on det are unnecessary.

Proof. P(z)=PfK(z) is multiaffine in the external matching variables. For T subset E, its coefficient at z^T is epsilon_T times the product of local principal Pfaffians on the ports not covered by T; epsilon_T is a fixed sign from the Pfaffian expansion. Fixing T leaves exactly the disjoint local blocks, so the coefficient identity is exact even on nonplanar graphs. Equivalently, choose external edge e iff both its incident spin ports are0; remaining ports are1. Squaring a coefficient removes epsilon_T and gives the physical configuration weight divided by prod a_v. Rademacher characters are orthogonal: E xi^T xi^U=0 for T!=U and1 for T=U. Unit phases do not affect diagonal magnitudes. Thus Parseval gives (D). Finally detK=PfK^2 for every complex skew matrix, so |detK|=|PfK|^2.

No fermionic crossing signs were silently erased: they occur as epsilon_T and disappear only AFTER coefficient orthogonality. Equality sewing is essential to this statement. The initial COMPLEMENTED h polynomial has constant d, but its physical wires are disequalities. Using its A_v with equality sewing would be incorrect. This repair was sent to root before any composition inference. Our final matrix and scripts use the uncomplemented tensor and normalization prod a_v throughout.

Acquired size and per-draw cost
------------------------------
The matrix has dimension4m, 2m independent sign bits, six local square roots per gate, and a directly constructed matching. One determinant evaluation uses O(m^3) arithmetic operations; this is polynomial PER DRAW, not a total counting guarantee. All-zero-spin contribution gives Z/(prod a_v)>=1 (indeed the all-identity connection word gives2^{n_active}), so the expectation has an acquired positive floor. For rational input of B-bit ratios, the matrix norm is bounded by R with logR=O(B+log m). Rounding local roots to entry error delta changes the determinant by at most O(N^2 delta (R+1)^{N-1}), N=4m, by column multilinearity and Hadamard. Thus absolute accuracy epsilon per draw needs only O(m(B+log m)+log(1/epsilon)) precision bits. Exact rational/complex determinant computation on rounded entries followed by magnitude evaluation gives polynomial finite-bit per-draw work. The exact theorem is over algebraic entries; no inefficient exact global algebraic-field construction is required for an absolute-accuracy implementation. Total sample cost remains unbounded by a polynomial because of variance.

THEOREM V: raw determinant estimator has exponential variance
------------------------------------------------------------
For one gate at the rational-root weights above, external wires connect ports13 and24. With real signs xi1,xi2,
 PfK=-1-(3/2)(xi1+xi2)-xi1xi2.
Its squared values are25,0,0,1, each sign pair probability1/4. Thus E Y=13/2, E Y^2=313/2 and E Y^2/(E Y)^2=626/169. On k disjoint gates the matrix and phases factor and the relative second moment is exactly (626/169)^k. Averaging this raw positive estimator therefore lacks a uniform polynomial relative-variance bound. Component factorization can handle this easy family; it does not prove failure of every estimator or algorithm.

THEOREM G: tuned phase removes zero-time variance, but has a remaining exact obstruction
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At identity gates a=b1,c=d0, K consists of disjoint alternating internal/wire cycles, one per physical worldline. Each component has two perfect matchings, giving a Pfaffian factor 1+/-product_e z_e up to a harmless overall sign. Choose the unit wire phases so product_e eta_e=i on each physical worldline (one chosen edge phasei and others1 suffices). Each factor has squared magnitude |1+/-i product xi|^2=2. Therefore Y=|PfK|^2=2^{n_active} pointwise: zero variance. The phases are directly acquired, use only1,i, and need no bipartite graph or parity rejection.

For one nonidentity critical gate, choose both external phasesi. Then
 PfK=-1-i sqrt(b/a)(xi1+xi2)+xi1xi2.
Y takes4b/a for equal signs and4 for unequal signs, each with probability1/2. Its relative variance is exactly ((b-a)/(b+a))^2. At a4,b9 this is25/169. For k independent gates the relative second moment is (194/169)^k, still exponential. For a1,b1+t it is t^2/(2+t)^2, so the one-gate high-temperature behavior is quadratic in t; this alone does not control repeated interacting cooling circuits at fixed total beta.

Finite tuned diagnostics: 300 samples for repeated triangles at beta1,3,6 and up to24 gates. A single-i-per-worldline gauge had sample relative second moments roughly1.3-4.9; distributed phases roughly1.3-6.6. Identity beta0 was constant8. These are floating finite checks with no certified confidence bounds, no polynomial variance theorem, no annealing chain and no evidence of a BPP classification. Time subdivision did not demonstrate variance tending to1 at fixed beta. Diagnostic data are preserved as failures/unknowns.

Local LC interpretation and its limit
------------------------------------
The local matrix [I|A_v] gives a rank4 basis-volume polynomial with coefficients |det|. Its log-concavity follows from the volume polynomial of a zonotope and Brunn-Minkowski. Restricting to one column from each dual-rail pair produces det(A_S)=Pf(A_S)^2 and hence the local gate. This supplies a positive local matroid-volume lift, but sewing two ports to equal spin values requires paired column choices. Their allowed family {00,11} is not itself a matroid basis family or LC factor. Treating it as an ordinary partition constraint introduces invalid dual-rail choices. No construction reducing that sewing to only two global LC factors was acquired. The initial single-LC selector obstruction remains compatible with this two-factor local lift.

-----------------------------------------------
Random skew determinant/hafnian estimators and their exponential-variance examples are established. Barvinok's primary exposition includes the Gaussian skew determinant estimator for hafnians; Uvarov-Vinichenko arXiv2312.10143 studies Godsil-Gutman/Barvinok hafnian estimators and explicitly reports simple exponential-variance families. The local Pfaffian/Grassmann-Plucker identity and coefficient-orthogonality principle are standard. This run places the exact critical EPR* tensor into that representation with equality-wire conventions, supplies the stated polynomial per-draw interface, triangle matroid lift and exact raw/tuned variance examples. A bounded search did not identify this exact EPR* specialization, but priority remains UNKNOWN. We do not claim a new generic determinant-estimator invention.

RESULT 1: exact positive two-matroid lift for one physical triangle
----------------------------------------------------------------
Take arbitrary nonnegative per-gate coefficients a_i,c_i,d_i, i=1,2,3, on the three edges (01),(02),(12), one occurrence each. Then

 Tr(F_3 F_2 F_1)
  =2[(a1+c1)(a2+c2)(a3+c3)
     +(a1+c1)(a2+d2)(a3+d3)
     +(a1+d1)(a2+c2)(a3+d3)
     +(a1+d1)(a2+d2)(a3+c3)].                   (T)

Positive matroid representation: in K4, write C1=01,C2=02,C3=03 for spokes and D1=23,D2=13,D3=12 for opposite edges. Intersect the graphic rank-3 matroid of K4 with the partition matroid choosing exactly one element in each pair {Ci,Di}. The common bases are precisely CCC,CDD,DCD,DDC. Give Ci weight ai+ci and Di weight ai+di. The right side of (T) is twice the weighted common-basis count. Equivalently, identity selector a_i refines into either Ci or Di; c_i fixes Ci and d_i fixes Di.

Proof: a three-element choice one from each opposite pair forms a tree exactly for the four listed choices. Expanding their products, the number of allowed refinements times2 is: 8 for aaa; 4 for any word with exactly one non-a; 2 for any word with exactly two non-a; and, with no a, 2 for ccc,cdd,dcd,ddc and0 for ccd,cdc,dcc,ddd. The trace follows by two-coloring the signed closed degree-2 constraint graph. Identity connections impose equality on each site strand. C joins the two input ports and separately the two output ports with inequality; D joins input to opposite output with inequality. An even-parity component contributes2; an odd-parity component contributes0. All 27 coefficients were checked exactly by an independent graph-spanning-tree computation against a parity-DSU trace computation; script and JSON retained.

Algorithmic scope: this is a constant-size identity. A disjoint union of such triangles composes through direct sums of both matroids; the prefactor becomes2^k and the ground set has6k elements, all weights rational of input bit length plus O(1). Given Chen-Liu's weighted common-bases theorem/finite-bit implementation, its count would be polynomial, but disjoint constant-size components were already directly easy. This does NOT yet provide a polynomial lift for intersecting triangles or repeated/general gate circuits. Root has assigned an independent gluing worker the exact identity.

Load-bearing composition warning: merely adding two elements Ci,Di per gate and refining only identity selectors cannot be the general representation. On one pair of physical qubits D^2=I, so Tr(D^2)=4, while fixing both D tags provides only one partition-matroid choice and the single-component prefactor2 would give2. Any extension needs multiplicity at nonidentity tags, a variable prefactor with acquired evaluation, or a different encoding. This is a necessary interface warning, not an all-matroid obstruction.

RESULT 2: exponential parity projection cost, including continuous cooling
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let Z0 be the loop model obtained by erasing ALL inequality/flip labels from the above connection graphs. Each selector word then has weight 2^{number of loops}, whereas the actual trace is that weight times the indicator that every loop has even flip parity. Thus parity projection accepts with probability Z/Z0 under the normalized erased law.

For the triangle, with equal a,c,d,
 Z=8a^3+12a^2(c+d)+6a(c+d)^2+2c^3+6cd^2,
 Z0=Z+12c^2d+4d^3.
At a=1,c=1/4,d=3/4 (t=1,s=1/2), Z=215/8, Z0=233/8. This is a legal positive critical Euler gate, even positive definite. On k disjoint triangles the acceptance is exactly (215/233)^k. It follows that rejection from the erased-loop law, or any lift whose proposal marginal is that law and whose only repair is all-cycle parity rejection, has exponential expected cost. Projection is efficiently testable but exponentially rare.

The obstruction is NOT an artifact of taking a finite Euler step. In the continuous nonnegative series, erasing the cup-cap and twisted-swap labels replaces
 A_uv = u C_uv + v D_uv
by
 A0_uv = u C0_uv + v SWAP_uv,
where C0=2|phi+><phi+| is the equality cup-cap. Let A be the sum over triangle edges and A0 the erased sum. For s=1/2, u=1/4,v=3/4,
 A_uv=(I+XX-(1/2)YY-ZZ)/2,
 A0_uv=(I+XX+(1/2)YY+ZZ)/2.
The exact spectra are
 A: 7/4 (multiplicity4), 5/4+sqrt(13)/2 (multiplicity2), 5/4-sqrt(13)/2 (multiplicity2),
 A0: 1/4 (multiplicity4), 9/4 (multiplicity2), 13/4 (multiplicity2).
Therefore
 Z(beta)=4e^{7 beta/4}+4e^{5 beta/4}cosh(sqrt(13) beta/2),
 Z0(beta)=4e^{beta/4}+2e^{9 beta/4}+2e^{13 beta/4}.
The series difference is strictly positive at every beta>0: the nonnegative trace expansion is termwise dominated and the frustrated triangle terms appear with positive coefficient. In particular Z0-Z=(9/4)beta^3+O(beta^4). At beta1, the ratio is about0.8787845246, and k copies give its kth power. Exact rational minimal-polynomial identities and moments through degree6 were executed; displayed transcendental evaluations are only illustrative floats. The proof of strict exponential loss uses the nonnegative series and tensorization, not a floating comparison.

This does not forbid annealing, direct parity-aware sampling, a different proposal law, coupling between components, or a positive two-factor representation. RESULT1 expressly avoids rejection for the fixed triangle.

RESULT 3: no single log-concave coordinate lift retaining the selectors
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let G be the homogeneous multiaffine generating polynomial on the nine triangle connection selector variables a_i,c_i,d_i, with coefficients equal to their exact spin traces. Fix the first selector to c1 (differentiate c1 and remove a1,d1), then set a2=a3=0. The residual polynomial is
 2(c2 c3+d2 d3).
This is not log-concave. At all four variables1, v=(1,1,-1,-1) has zero directional first derivative, while v^T Hessian(log(c2 c3+d2 d3))v=2>0. Its two basis supports {c2,c3} and {d2,d3} also fail basis exchange.

If G were log-concave, this residual would be: coordinate restrictions preserve LC, and for a multiaffine polynomial the derivative in c1 is the limit G(c1=R)/R as R tends to infinity, so LC persists on its nonzero limit. Contradiction. Consequently no nonnegative multiaffine LC polynomial in these selectors plus auxiliary coordinates can project EXACTLY to G solely by auxiliary summation (auxiliary variables evaluated at1), coordinate pinning, and derivatives. This obstruction holds without a size restriction but depends on preserving the individual connection selectors and those projection operations. It does not exclude changing the selector encoding or a coefficient of a product of two LC polynomials. The two-matroid lift in RESULT1 is allowed precisely because it uses the latter kind of coupling.
