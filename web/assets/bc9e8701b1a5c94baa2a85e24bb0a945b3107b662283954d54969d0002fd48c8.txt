prefix mass W_k(A,B,E) has a DETERMINISTIC relative approximation in

 16^k poly(n,k,L,eta^-1)

bit time. Its output is zero if and only if the true mass is zero. On positive

 16^k poly(n,k,L,epsilon^-1)

bit time, with no estimator failure or abort. The sampler returns NO_SECTOR

For k=O(log n), this is polynomial in n, input bit length and requested inverse

baseline's imported unrestricted support theorem.

The preceding owned Pfaffian reconstruction costs binom(2k,k) poly(...) but

1. EXACT RECTANGULAR SQUARED-MINOR SUM

For disjoint site sets L,R let M=F[L,R]. Cauchy-Binet yields

 H_k(M) = [t^k] det(I+t M M*)
        = sum_{I subset L, J subset R, |I|=|J|=k}|det F[I,J]|^2.

All terms are nonnegative, and L intersect R=empty enforces the physical
up/down exclusion exactly. To compute this coefficient, form the Hermitian
Gram B=M M*, compute p_j=tr(B^j), j=1,...,k, and use Newton's recurrence

 q_0=1,
 q_d=(1/d)sum_{j=1}^d(-1)^(j-1)p_j q_{d-j}.

The production implementation computes it in polynomial bit time after a
common denominator is cleared. This recurrence has no formal square root.
It does not enumerate minors; minor enumeration is used only by independent
small comparators. Arbitrary signs and complex phases within a determinant
are retained by exact Gram arithmetic.

2. RANDOM BIPARTITION ESTIMATOR AND PHYSICAL PREFIXES

First, with no forced up/down sites, color each site not in E independently
left or right with equal probability. Compute both orientations

 X=2^(2k-1)[H_k(F[L,R])+H_k(F[R,L])]  (k>0).

A fixed ordered physical pair (I,J) survives in exactly one orientation when
I is entirely left and J entirely right, or vice versa. These disjoint events
have total probability 2^(1-2k). Thus E X=W_k(empty,empty,E). The two rectangle
sums together are a subset of the original positive terms, so
0<=X<=2^(2k-1)W. In particular E X^2<=2^(2k-1)W^2. The k=0 estimator is one.
An unsymmetrized single orientation with multiplier 4^k gives the same simple
bound up to a factor two.

For nonempty forced sets A or B, place A on the left and B on the right,
delete E, and independently color the remaining f=n-a-b-|E| sites. Let
r=2k-a-b be the number of occupied free sites. The exact constrained rectangle
mass is

 H_k^forced(L,R)=sum_{I subset L,J subset R,|I|=|J|=k,
                          A subset I,B subset J}|det F[I,J]|^2.

Every valid physical completion survives with probability 2^-r. Set
X=2^r H_k^forced. Then E X=W_k(A,B,E) and 0<=X<=2^r W. Therefore its second
moment is at most 2^r W^2. This uses positivity of the determinant-SQUARED
sum, not entry positivity or absence of cancellation in individual minors.

The forced rectangle coefficient is acquired without row/column enumeration.
Scale the rows in A by a real variable z and columns in B by a real variable
w. A selected squared minor has monomial z^(2|I intersect A|)w^(2|J intersect B|).
Hence the top coefficient z^(2a)w^(2b) of H_k of the scaled rectangle is exactly
H_k^forced. Its degrees are at most 2a and 2b. Apply the exact highest-degree
finite-difference formula separately to z and w: at most (2a+1)(2b+1) scalar
coefficient calls, followed by division by (2a)!(2b)!. Arithmetic is exact,
and the final forced coefficient is manifestly nonnegative by its minor-sum
identity. There is no ambient inclusion-exclusion factor 2^b in this control.
Impossible occupations give zero; r=0 is a single exact deterministic draw.

The exact-bit mean/categorical compiler from LOW_SECTOR_PROOF.txt applies
with the new range multiplier 2^r (or 2^(2k-1) in the empty-spin case). Thus
randomized low-k relative counts and TV samples cost 4^k poly(...). The color
control was written independently after reconstructing the attributed peer
Pfaffian approach; its ingredients are ordinary Cauchy-Binet and classical
random coloring. Specific target-family priority is not claimed.

3. EXPLICIT DETERMINISTIC SMALL-BIAS FAMILY

Let q=2^m and construct the field K=GF(q) as binary polynomials modulo an
acquired irreducible polynomial of degree m. Enumerate all q^2 seeds (x,y).
Identify x with an m-bit vector. For free site indices i=0,...,f-1 define

 b_i(x,y)=<x,y^i>_bits in GF(2),
 s_i(x,y)=(-1)^{b_i(x,y)}.

The ordinary coordinate dot product is used in code; the trace pairing
Tr(x y^i) gives the same family distribution after an invertible change of the
x-coordinate basis. Thus no trace table or algebraic-number representation is
needed. This is classical small-bias construction, not a new generator.

For every nonempty subset S of free indices, define P_S(Y)=sum_(i in S)Y^i.
It is a nonzero polynomial of degree at most f-1. Conditional on y,

 average_x (-1)^(sum_(i in S)b_i(x,y))
    = average_x (-1)^<x,P_S(y)> = 1 if P_S(y)=0, otherwise 0.

A nonzero vector defines a balanced linear functional on all m-bit x. The
field polynomial root bound therefore gives

 0 <= average_(x,y) product_(i in S)s_i <= (f-1)/q.

This is a universal proof for every nonempty S; no finite collection of parity
tests is assumed sufficient. For f=1 the only polynomial is the constant one,
so bias is exactly zero. The f=0/r=0 case is handled directly.

Fix any prescribed bit pattern v on a set T of r free sites. Expanding its
indicator as 2^-r product_(i in T)(1+(-1)^v_i s_i) shows

 |2^r Pr_family[b_T=v]-1| <= (2^r-1)(f-1)/q.

Choose q to be the least power of two at least

 max(2, (f-1)(2^r-1)/eta).

Then EVERY r-site prescribed pattern has probability between
(1-eta)2^-r and (1+eta)2^-r. This is relative pattern probability control,
not an additive error asserted to be useful on an unknown tiny target mass.
The family is averaged completely; it consumes no random bits.

4. DETERMINISTIC RELATIVE COUNT AND ZERO EQUIVALENCE

Evaluate the nonnegative color estimator X at every family seed and take its
exact mean. In the forced case, each physical (I,J) term contributes its
nonnegative exact weight times 2^r times a prescribed r-site pattern probability.
By section 3, that multiplicative factor lies in [1-eta,1+eta] independently
of the term's magnitude. In the unforced symmetrized case, both complementary
r=2k patterns have that bound, so their sum times 2^(r-1) has the same bound.
Summing positive terms proves

 (1-eta) W <= deterministic_mean <= (1+eta) W.

Because eta<1, the mean is strictly positive if W>0 and exactly zero if W=0.
This also proves no false zero, including sectors with cancellation scales
2^-L. A histogram of words with their EXACT seed multiplicities may replace
repeated evaluation; the average is over q^2 seeds, not uniformly over distinct
words. Losing those multiplicities would invalidate the proof.

This mechanism explains why a poor-conditioning or additive-precision
obstruction cannot refute the stated exact-rational algorithm: every tiny
nonzero term is kept exactly and receives relative, magnitude-independent
survival control. All coefficient cancellations are integer operations on
polynomial-bit operands, not floating evaluations.

5. ACQUIRING THE FIELD AND CHARGING ALL COSTS

No irreducible polynomial is supplied. Enumerate all at most q monic
constant-one degree-m binary polynomials. For a candidate p, compute repeated
Frobenius squares X^(2^j) mod p. Require gcd(X^(2^j)-X,p)=1 for all
j<=floor(m/2), and X^(2^m)=X mod p. Any reducible degree-m polynomial has
an irreducible divisor of degree at most m/2; that divisor divides
X^(2^d)-X at its degree d, so this test rejects it. Irreducible polynomials of
every degree over GF(2) exist; the bounded search finds one. Each test costs
poly(m) bit operations. Field construction costs O(q poly(m)), explicitly
within the advertised bound; no randomized search or logarithmic oracle occurs.

For fixed y, form powers y^i. Build the m basis words for x=(1<<j). Every
other x-word is their XOR, generated by deleting its lowest set bit. This
constructs the exact histogram in q^2 poly(n,m) bit operations. Cache keys
are (f,m), and the stored integer multiplicities sum to q^2. At most q^2 words
are stored; individual keys have f<=n bits and counts have O(log q) bits.
The complete sampler makes at most 3n count calls, so its cache storage is
bounded by a polynomial factor times the largest charged family. This is
explicit time/space accounting, not an assumption of free pseudorandomness.

For every prefix,
 q=O(n 2^r eta^-1) <= O(n 4^k eta^-1),
 q^2=O(n^2 16^k eta^-2),
 m=O(k+log n+log eta^-1).

One color evaluation costs at most (2a+1)(2b+1) rectangular coefficient calls,
each O(k n^3+k^2) Gaussian-integer operations. A conservative deterministic
count bound is

 O(16^k k^3 n^5 eta^-2)

Gaussian-integer operations on polynomial-bit operands, plus q^2 poly(n,m)
field/histogram bit work. Insert the corresponding integer-operation bit
cost for total bit time. In the sampler eta=epsilon/(8n); with 3n calls,
a conservative arithmetic bound is

 O(16^k k^3 n^8 epsilon^-2),

plus polynomial field and histogram factors. The field enumeration and mean
accumulation are charged even when duplicate-word memoization is effective.
This intentionally loose n-power is sufficient to establish the legal FPT
and logarithmic-k polynomial interface; it is not a claim of optimal runtime.

Clear a single common input denominator D, of bit length O(L), giving G=D F.
Rectangular minors have denominators D^k and squared norms D^(2k). Field-word
multiplicities introduce only denominator q^2. A mean is represented by one
integer numerator over q^2 D^(2k), with polynomial bit height. Row/column
scales in finite differences are integers at most 2k. Trace powers, factorial
exact divisions, seed weights and the final sum have polynomial bit height in
L,n,k,log eta^-1. There is no LCM of exponentially many unrelated denominators.

6. SUPPORTED, FINITE-BIT SAMPLING WITH NO CONFIDENCE ERROR

At each site compute three deterministic child counts, using eta=epsilon/(8n).
All nonzero child masses remain nonzero, and all zero child masses stay zero.
Normalize the child estimates and use Q=2^ceil(log_2(8n/epsilon)) dyadic
categorical draws by flooring cumulative probabilities. LOW_SECTOR_PROOF
section 5 gives per-step normalization TV <=eta/(1-eta), dyadic TV <=2/Q,
and zero-support preservation because equal zero-child endpoints stay equal.
No fresh randomness is used in the counts, so there is no adaptive confidence
union bound and no false-zero abort. On a positive prefix at least one child
estimate is strictly positive. Coupling the n prefix choices gives

 TV(output,target)<=n eta/(1-eta)+2n/Q
                  <=epsilon/7+epsilon/4=11epsilon/28<epsilon.

Every positive-sector execution completes a supported physical word. The only
randomness is n ceil(log_2(8n/epsilon)) fair categorical bits. If the initial
three child counts are all zero, then their true masses are all zero and
c_k=0; return NO_SECTOR. Such a zero cannot arise later on a positive path.
The classical normalized law is undefined at zero, and this status states
that fact exactly. This is not coherent quantum state preparation.
