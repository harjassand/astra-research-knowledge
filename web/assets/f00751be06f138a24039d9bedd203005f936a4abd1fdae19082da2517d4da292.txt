STRONGEST NEW THEOREM: COARSE BALANCE DOES NOT IMPLY SINGLE-PAIR ENERGY

For T=2^L and arbitrary fixed 0<sigma<=1, let

 F_T=[[0,T,T,1],[0,0,T,1],[0,0,0,1],[0,0,0,0]].

Use the four original canonical lines o_i=(e_i,row_i F_T transpose),
with unit activities, and add every coordinate line c_ij=(e_i,e_j)
at activity sigma. Each full-basis microscopic state contains two lines
and has weight activity product times squared determinant.

All original ambient-hole maxima are exactly r_i=1: the full partition
is T^2, the hole coefficients on12,13,23 are1, and those on14,24,34 are
T^2. Thus the original input is already balanced under the exact source
window. Choose sigma=1/[10^8*5^4] for the ordinary polynomial calibration
floor B_ij/D0 (the coordinate bottleneck is B_ij=1). The augmented maxima
remain within the balance window and its partition inflation is below2.

Nevertheless, EVERY stationary two-replica kernel whose moves are either
(a) at most one line replacement in each layer, or (b) arbitrary
multiset-union-preserving changes across the two layers, must have

   Var_pi f1 <= C E_P(f1(A)+f2(B)) for all f1,f2
   only if C>=T^2/240=4^L/240.

This is exponential in the binary vector-entry length. It is not caused
by an exponentially weak coordinate probe or an unknown coefficient oracle.

Complete proof: revisions/single_pair_no_go.txt. Its short mechanism is
as follows. Exactly four dominant states have weights proportional to T^2:

 {o1,o3}:T^2; {o1,c24}:sigma T^2;
 {o1,c34}:sigma T^2; {o2,c14}:sigma T^2.

The six remaining positive states have total weight3sigma(1+sigma).
Set h(A)=1_{o1 in A}. The dominant state without o1 shares no selected
line with any dominant state containing o1, so one-line updates preserve
h within the dominant set. Every union-preserving replica move preserves
h(A)+h(B) everywhere. Thus only pair states containing at least one rare
configuration can contribute energy to the equal-functions direction h+h.

With Z=T^2(1+3sigma)+3sigma(1+sigma), put

 beta=3sigma(1+sigma)/Z,
 p=[T^2(1+2sigma)+sigma]/Z.

Stationarity bounds total transition flow incident to a set of mass eta
by2eta. Since h+h lies in[0,2],

 E_P(h+h)<=4(2beta-beta^2),
 Var_pi h=p(1-p).

Hence C>=p(1-p)/[4(2beta-beta^2)]>=T^2/240. This proof includes arbitrary
proposal probabilities and even perfect partition knowledge. A locally
computed conditional normalizer does not avoid the bottleneck.

The explicit one-pair heat-bath probability compiler is implemented in
revisions/pair_heatbath_kernel.py. It takes real rational vector pairs,
positive rational activities and one positive full-basis state. It computes
each deletion fiber from at most M direct determinant weights and never
accepts or evaluates a full/ambient-hole partition oracle. A chosen deletion
costs O(M r^3) rational arithmetic operations using naive determinants;
entry, determinant, activity-product and finite-normalizer bit lengths are
polynomial in the explicit data. Exact random-bit sampling was not implemented.
The implemented complete transition-distribution function enumerates all
r/2 deletion choices and therefore costs O(M r^4) rational operations.

The small acquired kernel is checked for exact row sums and detailed
balance in revisions/heatbath_energy_checks.py/.json. Adding a complete
layer swap does not affect the energy of h+h. Its exact energy ratio
indeed grows exponentially with L. The general no-go comes from the
written stationary-flow proof, not this finite calculation.

BOUNDARIES AND AN EXPLICIT RESCUE

A TWO-line replacement {o1,o3}->{o2,c14} has weight ratio sigma and
crosses the dominant cut. Therefore the new no-go does NOT refute an
OA113-style two-edge kernel or a general two-pair energy compiler.

Nor does it refute every adaptive preconditioning/auxiliary design. For
square T, take D=diag(T^-1/2,T^-1/2,T^-1/2,T^1/2). Applying the common
ambient scale and determinant-one within-pair anchor normalization gives
F'=D F_T D=F_1. All original full-basis weights scale by T^-2 and the
original hard law is preserved. Both frames have r_i=1.

Preserving the ALREADY AUGMENTED law requires coordinate activities
sigma'_ij=sigma(d_i d_j)^2, so core probes become sigma/T^2. Every
microscopic target weight changes by the same factor and the energy
obstruction remains exactly unchanged. However, adding FRESH uniform
sigma probes in the new frame changes the auxiliary target law to the
T-independent F_1 system. This removes the particular barrier and is
acquired with O(L)-bit rational scales. The distinction is explicit.

Thus row-maxima balance and the default floor law alone are insufficient
for a universal single-pair energy theorem. A compiler might strengthen
its frame/auxiliary admission condition or use two-pair moves. I have not
proved that either route supplies the general needed capacity bound.
Scope check: revisions/preconditioning_rescue_checks.py/.json.

A useful acquired repair identity is also derived. For a canonical state
A containing original i, write D=det(R,e_i,f_i) and
D_j=det(R,e_i,e_j) with unchanged other columns R. Then

 D=sum_{j!=i}F_ij D_j,

All terms are locally computable with O(n) determinant queries. Aggregate

completion fiber with escape sigma/(T^2+sigma). This repair identity
supplies a precise positive local mechanism and its exact missing cost.
