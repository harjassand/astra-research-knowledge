LEAD RESULT: SHIFTS OF POWER-FREE POLYNOMIAL FAMILIES AND RECURRENCE

Let k≥2 and let F=(f_1,...,f_r) be a fixed finite family of nonzero integer polynomials. Assume each f_i is separable over Q and every nonconstant irreducible factor of each f_i has degree at most k+2. For each prime p, put

  Ω_p = {a mod p^k : p^k divides f_i(a) for at least one i}.

Assume |Ω_p|<p^k for every prime p. Let

  A_F = {n≥1 : every f_i(n) is k-power-free}.

Here k-power-free means no prime k-th power divides the value; zero is excluded. Conditional on the density theorem in the pinned 2026 OpenAI preprint, Corollary 1.3, the following exact criterion holds:

For every integer r, put A_{F,r}={n≥1:n+r∈A_F}. Then

  A_{F,r} is an averaging set of polynomial multiple recurrence
  iff every f_i(r) is k-power-free.

The unshifted criterion is the special case r=0. This extends the earlier quartic-only arithmetic-to-dynamics transfer to simultaneous families, arbitrary fixed shifts, and every admissible k in the 2026 family-density corollary.

The recurrence property is in the precise Bergelson–Kułaga-Przymus–Lemańczyk–Richter sense: for every invertible probability-preserving system, positive-measure set B, and finite list P_j∈Q[t] with P_j(Z)⊂Z and P_j(0)=0, the average of μ(B∩T^{-P_1(n)}B∩...∩T^{-P_ℓ(n)}B) over the increasing enumeration of n∈A_F has a positive limit.

Proof of the positive direction. The pinned density theorem gives

  d(A_F)=δ_F=∏_p(1−|Ω_p|/p^k)>0.

For a cutoff y, define S_y by imposing the k-free conditions only for p≤y. This is periodic modulo ∏_{p≤y}p^k, and the Chinese remainder theorem gives density δ_y=∏_{p≤y}(1−|Ω_p|/p^k). Since A_F⊂S_y and both natural densities exist,

  d(S_y\A_F)=δ_y−δ_F → 0.

Thus 1_{A_F} is a Besicovitch limit of periodic sequences, so A_F is rational in the definition used by the recurrence theorem.
For each fixed integer r, A_{F,r} is the translate of A_F by a fixed integer; finite shifts preserve natural density and Besicovitch rationality, since the periodic approximants can be shifted as well.

Now fix u≥1 and dilate around the shift r: g_i(x)=f_i(ux+r). This preserves separability and factor degrees. If p|u, then g_i(a)≡f_i(r) (mod p^k); when every f_i(r) is k-free, no a is jointly bad, so Ω'_p=∅. If p∤u, multiplication by u followed by addition of r is a bijection modulo p^k, hence Ω'_p=Ω_p under that bijection and |Ω'_p|<p^k. Corollary 1.3 therefore gives a positive natural density δ_{u,r} for m such that all f_i(um+r) are k-free. Consequently

  d(A_{F,r}∩uN)=δ_{u,r}/u>0

for every u. A_F is divisible. The primary recurrence theorem says a positive-density rational set is an averaging set of polynomial multiple recurrence exactly when it is divisible, proving the positive direction.

Proof of necessity. If q^k|f_i(r) for some prime q and component i, then for every q|n,

  f_i(n+r)≡f_i(r)≡0 (mod q^k),

so A_{F,r}∩qN=∅. More explicitly, on the q-cycle X=Z/qZ with uniform measure, T(x)=x+1, B={0}, and the polynomial P(t)=t, the return correlation μ(B∩T^{-n}B) is zero at every n∈A_{F,r}. The averaging limit is zero. This proves the converse without assuming the density theorem.

This is a transfer theorem, not a new proof of the number-theoretic density input. The crucial load-bearing observation is that the full-family local admissibility condition is preserved under every affine progression x↦ux+r exactly when all component values at the shift r are k-free. Positive density alone is insufficient.

EXACT EXAMPLES

Take k=2 and F^+=(x^4+3,x^4+5). Each polynomial is Eisenstein at its constant prime and separable. The joint local condition holds: modulo 4, x^4+5 has no roots and x^4+3 has only the two odd roots; modulo 9, x^4+3 has no roots and x^4+5 has at most four; modulo 25, x^4+5 has no roots and x^4+3 has at most four; for odd p≥7 each polynomial has at most four roots modulo p^2, so |Ω_p|≤8<p^2. Both values at zero are squarefree. Subject to the source density theorem, the set of n for which both values are squarefree, and every shift by a common squarefree input r, are averaging sets of polynomial multiple recurrence.

By the same paper’s Proposition 4.2, this recurrence conclusion has a combinatorial strengthening: for every positive-upper-density D⊂N and every fixed finite list of integer-valued zero-constant polynomials P_j, some β>0 exists such that a positive-lower-density subset of each good shift A_{F,r} consists of n with upper density of D∩(D−P_1(n))∩...∩(D−P_ℓ(n)) exceeding β. This application is imported recurrence machinery applied to the derived set theorem, not a new polynomial Szemerédi theorem.

There is also a distinct distribution consequence. Let Q_1,...,Q_m∈R[n] be fixed and suppose every nonzero h∈Z^m makes ∑h_jQ_j have an irrational nonconstant coefficient. For every good shift r and every nonzero h,

  lim_{N→∞} (1/N) Σ_{n≤N, n∈A_{F,r}} exp(2πi Σ_j h_jQ_j(n)) = 0.

Indeed, first test a periodic approximant for 1_{A_{F,r}}: split into residue classes modulo its period and apply Weyl equidistribution to the polynomial on each residue class. Affine substitution n↦qn+a is invertible over Q on coefficient vectors, so an irrational nonconstant coefficient remains. Then send the Besicovitch approximation error to zero. This gives orthogonality for each fixed irrational polynomial-phase system; it supplies no uniformity for changing phases, shrinking Bohr sets, or finite-prefix onset.

For the counterexample family F^-=(x^4+18,x^4+5), the same source theorem applies. Both polynomials are Eisenstein (at 2 and 5, respectively), and the joint local condition holds: modulo 4 neither has a root; modulo 9 the first has the three roots n≡0 mod 3 and the second has at most four unit roots, disjoint from those three; modulo 25 the second has no roots and the first at most four; for odd p≥7 the total is at most eight. At shift r=0, every n divisible by 3 makes n^4+18 divisible by 9, so the positive-density set excludes 3N and is not recurrent. At any shift r with 9|(r^4+18), the same obstruction applies. This exact example shows why the value at the shift is load-bearing.

FOUR FRONTIER MECHANISMS WITH OPPOSING ROUTES

1. Arithmetic values → polynomial recurrence (new derivation above). Full-proof route: density input → periodic finite-prime approximants → affine-progression divisibility → the rational-set recurrence theorem. Counterexample route: if any component f_i(r) is not k-free at a shift r, the q-cycle witness kills every multiple of q. The theorem gives an exact iff boundary for the stated class; the density input remains conditional.

2. Finite global torus rank → spectral endpoint (retained from Cycle 1). Full-proof route: rank k forces the Schrödinger coefficient a_k=4k/(k+1)<4 and a positive supersolution; the explicit complete proper warped cylinder in work/programmes/geometry_number/leaf10_global_rank/RESULT.txt has λ_4≥2 but λ_a=−∞ for every a<4. Opposing compact-local route: every exhaustion member has finite rank, but the required rank diverges. The publisher PDF of Gromov (2024), §1.B1, explicitly states an exhaustion identity after defining a single finite N globally (pp.1–4); under that printed interface the example is a serious apparent contradiction. The article’s intended convention and any correction remain unresolved. No external review/priority claim.

3. Pure-symbol predicate → height-one field-pair recovery (retained from Cycle 1). Full-proof route: relative p-basis Euler derivations give an alternating logarithmic-derivative pairing; a two-variable intersection calculation and projective lifting reconstruct K,H from a supplied global compatible bijection when relative p-rank≥2, up to F_p*. Counterexample/acquisition route: in relative p-rank one all degree-two pure symbols vanish, so nonisomorphic pairs can have identical predicates; finite opaque-handle transcripts can also be extended by fresh p-basis elements. The theorem is structural and does not imply finite-query recovery. See work/programmes/geometry_number/leaf05_field_reconstruction/RESULT.txt and leaf06.

4. Mixed K3 products → rational generalized-Hodge support (retained from Cycle 1). Full-proof route: conditional on the released ordinary-HC, exact Kuga–Satake, and exterior-return inputs, clusterwise highest-weight matching constructs carriers for Galois-independent real-quadratic blocks with a shared Clifford class only within each repeated-field block. Opposing obstruction route: pairwise field disjointness does not force full joint Galois action (the √2, √3, √6 orbit has parity coupling and can violate the token bound); distinct Brauer classes give an index-two product-spin Tits algebra that blocks the direct rank-four carrier. Neither obstruction refutes GHC. See work/programmes/geometry_number/leaf08_hodge_extension/RESULT.txt and leaf09.
