## Primary prior art checked

1. Robin Pemantle and Yuval Peres, [“The Critical Ising Model on Trees, Concave Recursions and Nonlinear Capacity”](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503137), *Annals of Probability* 38 (2010), 184–206, DOI [10.1214/09-AOP482](https://doi.org/10.1214/09-AOP482). The capacity manuscript cites Pemantle–Peres Lemma 3.1 and Corollary 3.4 for the nonlinear `p=3` capacity recursion. This is the central prior art for the finite scalar recursion; no novelty claim is made for the generic recursion.
2. Frank Kschischang, Brendan Frey, and Hans-Andrea Loeliger, [“Factor Graphs and the Sum-Product Algorithm”](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.910572), *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 47 (2001), 498–519. Tree message passing is established prior art; exact BP is not a novelty claim.
3. Wee Peng Tay, John Tsitsiklis, and Moe Win, [“Bayesian Detection in Bounded Height Tree Networks”](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/ebcbe0de-57d3-4f8f-9de0-fcdd9ea72b14), *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing* 57 (2009), 4042–4051, DOI [10.1109/TSP.2009.2023374](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSP.2009.2023374). It treats resource-limited tree detection with progressively compressed information, error exponents, and restricted-strategy design. The present candidate instead assumes a q=4 broadcast-tree model and full/upward likelihood messages; comparisons must be made at matched communication/cost interfaces.
4. Elchanan Mossel and Yuval Peres, [“Information Flow on Trees”](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0107033), *Annals of Applied Probability* 13 (2003), 817–844, DOI [10.1214/aoap/1060202822](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoap/1060202822). Broad tree reconstruction, channels, and census-versus-full-information distinctions.
5. Allan Sly, [“Reconstruction for the Potts Model”](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.1208), *Annals of Probability* 39 (2011), 1365–1406, DOI [10.1214/10-AOP578](https://doi.org/10.1214/10-AOP578), and Elchanan Mossel, Allan Sly, and Youngtak Sohn, [“Exact Phase Transitions for Stochastic Block Models and Reconstruction on Trees”](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-probability/volume-53/issue-3/Exact-phase-transitions-for-stochastic-block-models-and-reconstruction-on/10.1214/24-AOP1723.full), *Annals of Probability* 53 (2025). These establish q=3/4 threshold results in regular/Galton–Watson/SBM regimes under their stated assumptions, not the all-tree heterogeneous statement in the internal N49 derivation.

## Claims not established by these sources

- No source or calculation establishes safety/stability of a physical controller acting on a network.
- No source establishes finite-bit BP robustness, asynchronous update guarantees, packet-loss tolerance, or an advantage over a matched distributed detector.
- No source establishes a polynomial exact optimizer for actual Bayes sensor-placement reward.
- No priority search establishes that the heterogeneous q=4 flow theorem or the finite design wrapper is novel relative to all current work.
