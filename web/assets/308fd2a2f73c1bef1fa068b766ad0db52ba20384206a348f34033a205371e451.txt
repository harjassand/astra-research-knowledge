DEDUCTION/PROOF
1. Let H2 be a torsion-free cocompact quaternionic-hyperbolic lattice with property(T), e.g. in Sp(n,1),n>=2. COCOMPACT is needed to get a word-hyperbolic lattice; generic finite covolume alone is not used. Torsion-free finite-index choice is available by linearity, property(T) passes to finite-index subgroups.
2. Apply Theorem2.1 with H1=G and M={1} union R. G nonelementary because finite permutation obstruction excludes soficity, whereas torsion-free elementary hyperbolic groups are trivial or Z (both sofic); alternatively use displayed source free factor. Obtain torsion-free nonelementary hyperbolic Q, projection pi:G->Q injective on M, and H2->Q surjective. Thus Q has property(T). Every pi(w),w inR, remains nonidentity.
3. Put M_Q={1} union {pi(w):w inR}, a fixed finite subset of Q. Apply Corollary2.4 finite-set-preserving monster quotient to K=Q. Obtain nonabelian torsion-free T, quotient psi:Q->T injective on M_Q, and every proper subgroup of T cyclic. T inherits property(T) from Q. Every composite image psi(pi(w)) remains nonidentity.
4. If T were sofic, choose a sufficiently accurate finite-set approximation of the images of all original triangle generators, inverse elements, triangle products and rectangle prefixes. Pull it back through psi o pi. All original triangle equalities remain true as group equalities, while all rectangle words are nonidentity in T. The fixed sofic bookkeeping bounds triangle defects by eta and rectangle fixed fractions by8eta. Choosing eta<1/(800rq) produces permutation assignments forbidden by the exact rank gap. Contradiction. Thus T is nonsofic.
5. Every proper subgroup of T is cyclic and therefore sofic. T is infinite: nonabelian torsion-free group cannot be finite. It is finitely generated, as quotient of finitely generated Q; indeed every pair of noncommuting elements generates T, since their subgroup is noncyclic and cannot be proper. Thus the resulting T is an infinite torsion-free two-generated property(T) nonsofic group with every proper subgroup cyclic/sofic.

WHY THIS IS STRONGER THAN JUST NONSOFIC G
Nonsoficity alone need not survive arbitrary quotients. The finite quantitative obstruction supplies a fixed list of equations/nontrivial words; finite-set-preserving quotient machinery retains exactly that list. This converts the analytic/rank witness into a portable algebraic obstruction and permits property(T), then cyclic-proper-subgroup structure, without imposing link expansion on the original CAT(-1) core.
