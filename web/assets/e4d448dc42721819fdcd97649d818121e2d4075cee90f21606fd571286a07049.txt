SCALAR COMMUTATIVIZATION AND SELECTIVE READOUT: EXACT GATES
7 October 2026. Internal derivations; no historical novelty or external validation claimed.
Authoring scope: algebra_complexity/commutative_transfer/commutative_barrier.

MAIN RESULT
There is an unconditional Omega(n m^2) lower bound on individually returned
field elements for exact acquisition of arbitrary at-most-m-state recognizable
series, in the regime n >= 2m. It already holds for degree-three polynomial
series and for learning only their commutativizations. Arbitrarily large matrix
inputs do not evade it if one charges every returned scalar entry. This is a
polynomial information bound. A deterministic efficient scalar learner for the
unrestricted class would construct polynomial hitting sets for commutative
ABPs. No superpolynomial information lower bound is proved or implied.

INTERFACE
Fix a field K. Hidden series S has a presentation
  S = lambda (I - sum_i x_i A_i)^(-1) gamma,
where A_i are m by m matrices, lambda is a row and gamma a column over K.
The inverse is formal at zero; the scalar commutativization is the rational
function F(x)=lambda (I-sum_i x_i A_i)^(-1) gamma in K(x_1,...,x_n).
Scalar point queries and matrix entries are exact K elements. Any field
extension of degree e must be charged as e K coordinates per answer. An
uncharged arbitrary transcendental answer would change this interface and
invalidate the field-linear lower bound. No coefficient-height upper bound is
assumed. Matrix query inputs may be arbitrarily large, but each reported entry
or fixed K-linear functional of the matrix answer is one scalar answer.

THEOREM A: TRILINEAR FAMILY AND EXACT RESPONSE LOWER BOUND
For positive a,b,c with a+b+c <= n, let m=a+c+2. Split variables into disjoint
blocks X_1,...,X_a; Y_1,...,Y_b; Z_1,...,Z_c. For arbitrary tensor
T in K^(a*b*c), define
  P_T = sum_(i,j,k) T_(i,j,k) X_i Y_j Z_k.
The noncommutative polynomial P_T is recognizable by an m-state acyclic
presentation with layers {s}, {u_i}, {v_k}, {t}:
  A_(X_i)[s,u_i]=1,
  A_(Y_j)[u_i,v_k]=T_(i,j,k),
  A_(Z_k)[v_k,t]=1,
with all other entries zero, lambda=e_s^T and gamma=e_t. Only paths of length
three connect s to t, so this presentation realizes exactly P_T. Scalar
commutativization preserves the abc independent monomials X_i Y_j Z_k.

Every scalar query at x gives a K-linear functional of T. Every selected entry
of a matrix evaluation likewise gives
  sum_(i,j,k) T_(i,j,k) [X_i^query Y_j^query Z_k^query]_(r,s),
which is one K-linear functional of T, irrespective of matrix dimension.
Consider a deterministic adaptive learner making q<abc scalar-answer queries
on the zero tensor. Its queries induce q homogeneous equations on T. Their
joint kernel contains a nonzero T*. On T*, induction on the transcript shows
that exactly the same queries are asked and all answers equal zero. The learner
returns the same output, which cannot equal both 0 and P_(T*). Hence q>=abc.
The proof also applies to variable-sized batches, counting all returned entries.

If the promise excludes zero and requires exact minimal nc dimension m, use a
generic full-rank T0 instead of zero. Its transcript cuts out an affine fiber of
dimension at least abc-q. When a<=bc and c<=ab, the tensors whose two relevant
flattenings have ranks a,c form a nonempty Zariski-open set over infinite K.
The fiber through a point of this open set contains another such point if
q<abc, giving the same contradiction. The rank formula is
  rank(Hankel(P_T))=2+rank(T_(i;(j,k)))+rank(T_((i,j);k))
for nonzero T: residuals of lengths 3,2,1,0 respectively have disjoint remaining
degrees. This exact-minimal strengthening is only claimed over infinite fields.

Set a=floor((m-2)/2), c=ceil((m-2)/2), b=n-m+2. For m>=4 and b>=1,
  q >= floor((m-2)^2/4) (n-m+2).
For n>=2m this is Omega(nm^2). The bound is tight for this displayed tensor
family: abc queries with X=e_i, Y=e_j, Z=e_k recover its coefficients exactly.
It is a worst-case acquisition/response lower bound and also a deterministic
scalar PIT lower bound for this full tensor family: the zero and nonzero
kernel tensor have the same all-zero transcript. Randomized PIT for a fixed
nonzero tensor can use one generic/random point, with its usual error bound.

COEFFICIENT-HEIGHT CAVEAT. The K-linear nullspace proof permits unrestricted
coefficients in K. If the coefficients are promised in a known bounded finite
integer alphabet, a Kronecker/base-packing query with sufficiently large exact
coordinates may identify the tensor from a single very long scalar answer.
Thus the q>=abc statement is not asserted for that modified finite-alphabet
interface. In that interface, exact reconstruction of an alphabet C in each
of abc slots still requires Omega(abc log_2 |C|) input-dependent transcript
BITS in the worst case. This is a counting/entropy lower bound, charging the
whole exact answer encoding. It is not a comparable transcript-bit lower
bound for zero-testing.

THEOREM B: SCALAR NONIDENTIFIABILITY OF UNRESTRICTED NC SERIES
For m=4, use lambda=e_0^T, gamma=e_3 and
  A_x=E_01-E_23,  A_y=E_02+E_13.
Then lambda A_x A_y gamma=1 and lambda A_y A_x gamma=-1; all other accepted
words vanish. Thus S=xy-yx is nonzero and has identically zero scalar oracle.
Its Hankel rank is four: residuals xy-yx, y, -x, 1 are independent. The zero
series has the same scalar answers on every point. Therefore scalar evaluations
cannot recover the original arbitrary nc series, regardless of query count.
This does not refute learning its scalar commutativization, which is zero.

THEOREM C: DETERMINISTIC SCALAR LEARNING YIELDS COMMUTATIVE ABP HITTING
Let L be a deterministic learner that, for every scalar commutativization of an
at-most-m-state recognizable series, makes at most q(n,m) scalar queries and
returns an encoding of the exact commutative function. No canonical normal form
or efficient zero-test for its output is required for this reduction.
Run L on the identically zero oracle, and record all query points H. Then H is a
hitting set for every polynomial in this class. Otherwise a nonzero polynomial
P vanishing on H would generate the same adaptive transcript and final output
as zero, contradicting correctness on one of the two inputs. Running the zero
simulation computes H using the same time budget as L, without using L's output.

Every homogeneous linear-edge commutative ABP with <=m vertices belongs to this
class: lift its edge labels to nc variables, put each variable's coefficients
into a strictly upper-triangular transition matrix in topological order, and
choose source/sink lambda,gamma. Its scalar output is the ABP's commutative
polynomial. Thus a uniform poly(n,m)-time learner gives uniform polynomial-size
explicit hitting sets for general commutative ABPs. Affine edges do not weaken
the conclusion: homogenize to homogeneous linear edges with one extra variable,
or substitute x_0=1 after learning. The homogeneous subclass already supplies
the exact reduction. ABPs of large degree still have degree <=m-1 after this
vertex-count presentation. A learner restricted to a fixed degree-three class
does not supply general ABP PIT.

For rational functions, some zero-transcript points may be poles on another
hidden presentation. The direct hitting-set proof above deliberately uses
polynomials, so all queries are legal and there is no domain loophole.

THEOREM D: THE COMMUTING SUBCLASS IS PRECISELY PARIKH-INVARIANT SERIES
Let an nc presentation be minimal, meaning the columns A_v gamma span K^r and
the rows lambda A_u span its dual. Its matrices commute iff the coefficient of
each word depends only on the word's Parikh vector.
Forward direction: commuting products are invariant under adjacent swaps.
Converse: for all words u,v and symbols i,j, permutation invariance gives
  lambda A_u (A_i A_j-A_j A_i) A_v gamma=0.
Reachability and observability force A_i A_j-A_j A_i=0. The property is invariant
under minimal-realization similarity, although a nonminimal presentation can
contain invisible noncommuting blocks.

In characteristic zero, scalar commutativization is injective on this subclass.
If c_alpha is the common word coefficient for Parikh vector alpha, then
  [x^alpha]F = (|alpha|! / product_i alpha_i!) c_alpha.
The multinomial factor is nonzero, so all word coefficients are recovered by
division if the commutative coefficient data are known. This is an injectivity
statement; extracting all such coefficients is not itself an efficient learner.

Characteristic zero is essential. In characteristic 2 take
  A_x=E_01+E_23,  A_y=E_02+E_13.
These matrices commute, since both products equal E_03, and realize the nonzero
series xy+yx. Its scalar image is 2xy=0. Thus the all-field N51 import cannot
justify scalar injectivity even within commuting transitions.

NONEXPLICIT POLYNOMIAL-SIZE EXISTENCE CEILING (CHARACTERISTIC ZERO)
Write P=nm^2+2m. For a presentation, put
  D=det(I-sum x_i A_i), N=lambda adj(I-sum x_i A_i) gamma.
Degrees in x are at most m and m-1. Coefficients of ND are a polynomial map
of P presentation parameters of degree <=2m+1; deg_x(ND)<=2m-1. D(0)=1.
An elementary incidence-dimension argument shows that P+1 generic scalar points
hit every nonzero ND: the parameter space has dimension <=P, and each nonzero
polynomial cuts each point's n-dimensional space by at least one dimension.
Consequently some point is both defined and nonzero for F. This proves small
cardinality existence, without explicit coordinates or an efficient constructor.

The Heintz-Schnorr existence theorem in the primary restatement cited below also
gives an integer set of at most 10P points with coordinates in
  [ (2m+1)(2m)^2 ],
which hits this family. Its applicability follows directly from the stated
polynomial-map degree and polynomial-degree bounds. This is nonexplicit. For
pairwise separation, apply the same theorem to
  (N1 D2-N2 D1) D1 D2,
whose x-degree <=4m-1 and parameter degree <=4m+1 with 2P parameters. This gives
at most 20P integer points with coordinates in [(4m+1)(4m)^2], on one of which
every two different scalar functions are both defined and unequal. This excludes
a superpolynomial query-cardinality impossibility argument for unrestricted
exact coefficients over characteristic-zero fields. Efficiently constructing
such a set, finding a consistent hidden presentation, controlling bit heights,
and querying through poles remain separate tasks. Coefficient recovery is not
implied by mere identity detection.

THEOREM E: SCALAR ACQUISITION UNDER COMMUTING TRANSITIONS (POSITIVE RESULT)
Detailed proof and exact costs: commuting/REPORT.txt.
Suppose K has characteristic zero and the scalar oracle returns f(x) whenever
det(I-sum x_i A_i)!=0, with a recognized POLE flag otherwise. This is the
expression-domain interface; canceled/removable scalar singularities are not
queried as valid matrix resolvents. All scalar calls, including poles, count.
Then a randomized exact learner obtains a minimal commuting realization using
  m+(n+1)m^2(12m^2+8m+1)=O(nm^4)
scalar calls, with correctness probability >=1-delta for a finite random grid
of size >=2m^2/delta. It uses polynomial exact field arithmetic. Without a
height promise, this is not a coefficient-independent bit-time statement.

The construction uses the minimal reachable/observable quotient V of the
commutative transition algebra, with nondegenerate symmetric bilinear pairing
  B(p(A)gamma,q(A)gamma)=lambda p(A)q(A)gamma.
Regular resolvent vectors R(u)gamma span V: an annihilating functional would
kill every Taylor coefficient, which is a nonzero multinomial times its moment.
For regular u,v, x(t)=(1-t)u+tv, commutation gives the exact rational identity
  d/dt [t R(u)R(x(t))]=R(x(t))^2.
Thus scalar radial derivatives of s f(sx), and scalar coordinate derivatives
of f, yield the resolvent pairings
  H(u,v)=lambda R(u)R(v)gamma,
  K_i(u,v)=lambda R(u)A_iR(v)gamma
by rational antidifferentiation. Each derivative value is obtained by ordinary
univariate rational interpolation from <=3m scalar calls. Interpolating each
line integrand after pole tests uses at most 12m^2+8m+1 total calls. No derivative
oracle, analytic integral, logarithm evaluation, or matrix-output query is used.

Antidifferentiation is polynomial linear algebra: if the interpolated integrand
is p/q, the guaranteed rational primitive can be written U/q with deg U<=3m,
and one solves U'q-Uq'=pq, U(0)=0. Characteristic zero ensures every primitive
pole remains in its derivative, so its reduced denominator divides q.
Choose a nonsingular rank-r submatrix H0 of the sampled H. The matrices
  B_i=H0^-1 K_i, alpha=H0^-1 (f(u_i))_i, beta=(f(v_j))_j
give a minimal r-state commuting realization. Random endpoint spanning fails
with probability <=r(m-1)/|S|, and endpoint poles with <=m^2/|S|, yielding
the stated 2m^2/|S| failure bound. This is a Monte Carlo learner; the favorable
span event is not automatically a supplied success certificate.

SOURCE-CONDITIONED DETERMINISTIC COROLLARY. The current primary theorem
Bhardwaj-Saptharishi, arXiv:2609.34436v1 (28 Sep2026), gives an explicit hitting
generator for characteristic-zero polynomials with small total derivative
space. For every functional ell,
  T_ell(x)=sum_(k=0)^(m-1) ell (sum x_i A_i)^k gamma
has derivative-space dimension <=m^2: each derivative order spans <=m and
there are m orders. A nonzero ell on V has nonzero T_ell by reachability.
Importing their theorem gives explicit directions G_i(t)=sum_(k=1)^B(it)^k/k,
B=nm^4+m^2, sampled at (m-1)B+1 t values. At any direction hitting T_ell,
ell R(sG(t))gamma is a nonzero rational univariate. Among 2m scales, at most
m are poles and at most m-1 are zeros; hence a legal nonzero scale exists.
The legal vectors from this uniform explicit set span V. Building its H and
then only n r^2 K entries derandomizes the learner, with coarse query bound
  O(n^2 m^14+n m^4).
Primary URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34436v1 . The source discloses AI-assisted
proof provenance. Child read/audited the complete proof, while this report's
author independently checked the live theorem statement and reconstructed
the transfer. This is an imported theorem plus an internal new-interface
derivation, not a claim of external certification or priority.

ADDITIONAL COMMUTING LOWER BOUND. Every homogeneous cubic in n variables has
a commuting nilpotent realization of dimension 2n+2. On graded basis
(u0,v_1,...,v_n,w_1,...,w_n,u3), use a symmetric tensor C and define
  A_i u0=v_i; A_i v_j=sum_k C_ijk w_k; A_i w_j=delta_ij u3; A_i u3=0.
Full symmetry proves A_i A_j=A_j A_i, and lambda=u3*, gamma=u0 gives
f=sum_ijk C_ijk x_i x_j x_k. Dividing each desired monomial coefficient by
its multinomial factor realizes every cubic in characteristic zero. The
zero-transcript dimension argument therefore requires binom(n+2,3) scalar
answers, even for deterministic zero-testing. Along m=2n+2 this is again
Omega(nm^2), within the commuting subclass. Its proof is also recorded in
commuting/lower_bound/REPORT.txt. Univariate exact identification there requires
2m scalar calls for the unrestricted degree-(m-1)/degree-m rational class.

FAILED / INVALID TRANSFERS
- A scalar commutative query cannot simulate nc full matrix evaluation: xy-yx.
- Detecting the nc lift of a commutative ABP does not test its scalar image.
- A learned nc representation's nc zero-test is not automatically a zero-test
  for its commutativization; the zero-transcript reduction avoids that mistake.
- Polynomial-state parameter count does not establish polynomial computation or
  effective coordinate construction; it does preclude a naive dimension-based
  superpolynomial response lower bound.
- Characteristic-free nc hitting does not provide characteristic-free scalar
  reconstruction of commuting series; xy+yx in characteristic2 is a counterexample.
