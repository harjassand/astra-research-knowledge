The central seed is the normalized Gibbs state

  rho_N^* = exp[(d/(2N)) Q] / Z_N,
  Q = sum_{a=1}^{d^2-1} F_a^2,
  F_a = sum_{i=1}^N T_a^(i),   Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab,

with traceless Hermitian fundamental generators. This is the susceptibility
critical coefficient in the stated normalization. The question is whether a
positive iid-product decomposition of rho_N^* can have its one-site mixing
law concentrated in a shrinking Hilbert-Schmidt ball around I/d.

1. CUBIC FIRST-ORDER VARIATIONAL COUNTEREXAMPLE

Let eta be any one-site density, x_a=Tr(eta T_a), and
q(eta)=sum_a x_a^2=Tr(eta-I/d)^2. Completeness of a trace-orthonormal
traceless basis gives sum_a T_a^2=c_d I, c_d=(d^2-1)/d. On eta^tensor N,

  Tr(eta^tensor N Q)=N c_d+N(N-1)q(eta).

The Gibbs variational inequality, log Tr exp(K)>=S(omega)+Tr(omega K),
applied to omega=eta^tensor N, gives

  log Z_N >= N[S(eta)+(d/2)q(eta)] +(d/2)(c_d-q(eta)).       (1)

Thus the relevant one-site variational functional is
Phi_d(eta)=S(eta)+(d/2)Tr(eta-I/d)^2. Its quadratic entropy curvature
exactly cancels the Casimir energy at eta=I/d. The next term does not vanish.
For any traceless Hermitian X and sufficiently small real t with
eta_t=I/d+tX positive, Taylor expansion gives

  Phi_d(eta_t)-log d
    =(d^2/6)Tr(X^3)t^3 -(d^3/12)Tr(X^4)t^4 + O(t^5).         (2)

For every d>=3, choose X=diag(2,-1,-1,0,...,0). Then Tr X=0,
Tr X^2=6, Tr X^3=6, and Tr X^4=18, so

  Phi_d(eta_t)-log d = d^2 t^3 -(3/2)d^3 t^4+O(t^5)>0

for all sufficiently small t>0. The least eigenvalue is 1/d-t>0 and the
largest is 1/d+2t, so this is an interior perturbation. Therefore I/d is
not even a local maximum of the susceptibility-critical product variational
functional for d>=3. This is the first-order/cubic obstruction: a proof
which presumes that the critical seed is controlled by a Gaussian quadratic
well centered at I/d misses the positive cubic direction. For d=2 the
traceless spectrum is symmetric and Tr X^3=0; this particular obstruction
vanishes, consistent with the qubit-specific nature of the earlier route.

2. FIXED MACROSCOPIC WITNESS AND EXACT NO-CONCENTRATION CONSEQUENCE

The cubic calculation diagnoses local instability. A stronger fixed witness
gives an extensive finite-N free-energy gap. Take eta_* with eigenvalues

  (d-1)/d once, and 1/[d(d-1)] with multiplicity d-1.

Then

  q_* = Tr(eta_*-I/d)^2 = (d-2)^2/[d(d-1)],
  S(eta_*) = log d -(d-2)log(d-1)/d,
  Delta_d := Phi_d(eta_*)-log d
          = (d-2)^2/[2(d-1)] -(d-2)log(d-1)/d >0.          (3)

For the sign, put x=d-1>1 and rewrite

  Delta_d=(x-1)/(2(x+1)) [x-1/x-2log x].

The bracket is zero at x=1 and has derivative (x-1)^2/x^2>0 for x>1.
The witness is full rank for every fixed d, and its squared distance q_*
from I/d is a fixed positive constant.

Applying (1) to eta_* yields

  log Z_N - N log d >= N Delta_d +(d/2)(c_d-q_*).            (4)

Now set g(s)=log Tr exp(sQ). It is convex, g(0)=N log d, and
g'(d/(2N))=Tr(rho_N^* Q). The secant-slope inequality and (4) imply

  Tr(rho_N^* Q) >= 2N^2 Delta_d/d + N(c_d-q_*).              (5)

Suppose rho_N^* has any exact positive iid-product decomposition
rho_N^*=integral eta^tensor N mu_N(d eta). Linearity of the Q expectation
and the product identity then force

  integral q(eta) mu_N(d eta)
      >= [2N Delta_d/d - q_*]/(N-1).                         (6)

The right side tends to 2 Delta_d/d>0. This is about every exact positive
product decomposition, not just a particular coherent-state representation.
For an explicit fixed-ball consequence, if
N+1 >= d q_*/Delta_d, (6) is at least Delta_d/d. Set
epsilon^2=Delta_d/(2d), and let p_N=mu_N{q>=epsilon^2}. Since
0<=q<=1-1/d,

  integral q dmu_N <= epsilon^2 +(1-1/d) p_N,
  p_N >= Delta_d/[2(d-1)] >0.                              (7)

Hence no such mixing law can concentrate in any radius r_N->0 around I/d;
for all sufficiently large N it puts at least the fixed mass (7) outside
the fixed radius epsilon. In d=3,
Delta_3=1/4-log(2)/3>0, q_*=1/6, and the explicit condition in (7) holds
for N>=26. The inequality Delta_3>0 follows, for example, from
exp(3/4)>1+3/4+(3/4)^2/2+(3/4)^3/6>2, hence log2<3/4.

Crucial status boundary: this does NOT show rho_N^* is entangled. It rules
out central concentration of the local product factors. An exact positive
iid-product mixture may exist, but it must include macroscopically
noncentral factors. The distinction between existence of a positive product
mixture and concentration of its mixing law is load-bearing.

4. FINITE-N SEED CORRECTIONS CANNOT CANCEL THE EXTENSIVE GAP

This is not an artifact of replacing N(N-1) by N^2. For a nearby scalar
coefficient beta_N/N, the same exact variational calculation gives

  log Tr exp[(beta_N/N)Q]-N log d
    >= N[Delta_d+(beta_N-d/2)q_*] + beta_N(c_d-q_*).         (8)

Therefore every beta_N=d/2+o(1), including a first-order correction
beta_N=d/2+theta/N for fixed theta, retains an N Delta_d+o(N) gap. The exact
N-1 correction and bounded scalar shifts contribute only O(1); they cannot
restore central concentration.

More generally, let R_N be a Hermitian correction with ||R_N||=o(N),
independent of a scalar coupling u, and define
g_N(u)=log Tr exp[uQ/N+R_N]. Then
|g_N(0)-N log d|<=||R_N||. The eta_* variational trial gives
g_N(d/2)>=N log d+N Delta_d-O(1)-||R_N||. Convexity yields

  g_N'(d/2)=Tr(rho_{N,R} Q/N) >= [N Delta_d-o(N)]/(d/2).

Consequently any exact iid-product mixture of rho_{N,R} again has
E_mu q>=2 Delta_d/d-o(1). This covers bounded or subextensive finite-size
corrections that do not depend on u. It does not cover an extensive
correction, a nonvanishing change in the critical beta, or a correction
whose u-dependence changes the derivative; those are different interfaces
and need their own variational audit.

5. WHY THE c04_s02 DIRECT LOGDET ROUTE EVADES THE COUNTEREXAMPLE

c04_s02 uses Tr(T_aT_b)=delta_ab/2 and writes the target as
K_N=(beta/N)Q_I+N^(-(1+delta))Q_A+J_B. Under the normalization conversion
T_half=T_unit/sqrt(2), the central critical coefficient above corresponds
to beta=d in c04_s02's convention. Its direct construction begins the
one-site matrix SDE at rho_0=I/d, but it does not assert that the path or the
endpoint mixture remains near I/d. It stops only if
lambda_min(rho_t) reaches e_N=k N^(-delta/2), and its logdet martingale
controls that rank-boundary event. It places no shrinking Hilbert-Schmidt
ball around the center.

That change is exactly enough to avoid (7). The ordered witness eta_* has
lambda_min=1/[d(d-1)]>0, which lies above e_N for all sufficiently large N
at fixed d,delta,k. More generally, (7) forces the endpoint product law
certified by any exact representation to have noncentral mass, and the
logdet cutoff permits that mass. The SDE can move away from the central
starting point and explore ordered full-rank densities; the initial point
I/d is not a claim that its endpoint factors concentrate there. For the
isotropic case A=0, c04_s02's separate global-positive-diffusion argument
gives an exact positive mixture without a stop, so the counterexample is
particularly direct: an exact central-seed Gibbs state can be separable
while every exact iid-product mixing law is macroscopically noncentral.

The replacement does not erase the gate: growing d, beta, anisotropy bounds,
or fields require rechecking its explicit thresholds and costs. Its stated
asymptotic theorem fixes d,beta,delta,M,B0, and its finite-bit SDE sampler,
weight computation, endpoint resolution, and local-state preparation are
not implemented. Therefore the logdet proof evades the central-concentration
obstruction at the mathematical mixture level, not necessarily as a
practical preparation algorithm.

VERDICT

The central critical Casimir has an exact cubic variational instability for
every d>=3, plus a fixed macroscopic witness. Equations (6)-(7) are an exact
counterexample to a shrinking central-seed mixing law, with full-rank support
and explicit positive mass away from the center. This is a route obstruction,
not physical nonseparability. The c04_s02 direct full-rank/logdet stopped
diffusion bypasses the obstruction by localizing at the rank boundary rather
than at I/d; its permitted endpoint law can satisfy (7). A numerical costed
compiler and external proof/priority audit remain open.
