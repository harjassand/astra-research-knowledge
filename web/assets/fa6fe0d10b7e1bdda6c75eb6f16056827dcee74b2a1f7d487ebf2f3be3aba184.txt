The deterministic permanence certificate pipeline and the finite-volume stochastic safety theorem do not supply a global stochastic boundary-recovery or recurrence certificate. Their interfaces differ at three load-bearing points: N33 quantifies over positive real concentration classes and even permits real/negative source exponents; N35 requires integer nonnegative molecular complexes, a volume-scaled count lattice point initially inside a strictly positive polytope with fixed facet gaps, and proves only finite-horizon non-exit; N50 recognizes the deterministic absorber conditionally and gives no stochastic class or recurrence conclusion. This closes only the explosion gate, not recurrence.

CLAIM A — ROBUST NON-EXPLOSION UNDER PREDICTABLE CONTROL

Let a finite ordinary mass-action network have integer nonnegative source complexes of molecularity at most two and integer product complexes. Write \(\Delta_e=\mathbf1\cdot(y'_e-y_e)\), \(N(z)=\mathbf1\cdot z\), and use stochastic intensity
\[
\lambda_e(t,z)=k_e(t,\mathcal F_{t-})\,z^{\underline{y_e}},\qquad 0\le k_e\le K,
\]
where the controls are predictable and may depend on the full past and independent randomness. If \(\mathbf1\notin S^\perp\), ordinary endotacticity in direction \(w=\mathbf1\) says every essential edge out of a maximal-total source has \(\Delta_e<0\). Any order-two source is maximal among sources with nonzero \(\Delta\), unless its projection is zero; hence every order-two reaction has \(\Delta_e\le0\). If instead \(\mathbf1\in S^\perp\), every reaction conserves \(N\) and each fixed-total state space is finite.

For the nonconservative case, only order-zero and order-one sources can have positive total increment. Since their falling-factorial propensities are respectively 1 and \(z_i\),
\[
\mathcal A_tN(z)=\sum_e k_e(t)z^{\underline{y_e}}\Delta_e
 \le C_0+C_1N(z),
\quad C_0=K\sum_{|y_e|=0}(\Delta_e)_+,
\quad C_1=K\sum_{|y_e|=1}(\Delta_e)_+.
\]
The constants are finite and computable from the finite input; product complexes can have any finite size. For \(\tau_m=\inf\{t:N(Z_t)\ge m\}\), localization and the predictable compensator identity give
\[
\mathbb E N(Z_{t\wedge\tau_m})\le N(Z_0)+C_0t+C_1\int_0^t\mathbb E N(Z_{s\wedge\tau_m})\,ds,
\]
so Gronwall bounds the left side uniformly in \(m\) on every finite horizon. Thus \(\Pr(\tau_m\le T)\le \mathbb E N(Z_{T\wedge\tau_m})/m\to0\). On any bounded set the total propensity is bounded (finite network, bounded controls), so infinitely many jumps cannot occur there in finite time. Explosion would therefore require reaching arbitrarily large \(N\) before some finite \(T\), which the preceding bound excludes. The argument uses only the upper rate bound; a positive lower bound is not needed for non-explosion. It applies to time-inhomogeneous predictable feedback, unlike a stationary-distribution conclusion.

This is an internally derived extension of the fixed-rate regularity mechanism in Xu, arXiv:2409.05340v2, Theorem 4.6. That primary source proves non-explosion for every second-order \(\mathbf1\)-endotactic stochastic mass-action system, hence includes second-order endotactic systems; it does not assert positive recurrence. I claim no novelty or priority for the extension.

CLAIM B — EXACT BOUNDARY / STOICHIOMETRIC-CLASS OBSTRUCTION

Take the rational, bimolecular network
\[
2A\to A+B,\qquad 2B\to A+B.
\]
Its reaction vectors are \((-1,1)\) and \((1,-1)\), so \(S=\{(u,-u):u\in\mathbb R\}\). For every \(w\notin S^\perp\), if \(w_A>w_B\), source \(2A\) is maximal and its edge has projection \(w_B-w_A<0\); if \(w_B>w_A\), source \(2B\) is maximal and its edge has projection \(w_A-w_B<0\). Thus the network is strongly endotactic under the standard all-directions definition. Its deterministic positive class at total concentration \(c>0\) is the interval \(x_A+x_B=c,\ x_A,x_B>0\), with inward vector field \(\dot x_A=-k_Ax_A^2+k_Bx_B^2\) and a positive equilibrium.

For ordinary factorial count propensities, at total count 1 both states \((1,0),(0,1)\) have zero enabled reactions and are absorbing. At total count 2, the full integer stoichiometric coset from \((2,0)\) is \(\{(2,0),(1,1),(0,2)\}\), but \((2,0)\to(1,1)\), \((0,2)\to(1,1)\), and \((1,1)\) has no outgoing transition. Therefore the integer span coset is not a communicating class. At volume V=1 with total count one, the real positive concentration class is nonempty and deterministic trajectories are attracted to the interior, while the only corresponding lattice states are boundary states that never enter any strictly positive buffered target polytope. This is an exact counterexample to claiming boundary recovery from deterministic permanence for every lattice state in the real stoichiometric class. It does not refute recurrence of closed count classes: these particular closed classes are singletons or finite. Initial buffer and volume/coset compatibility in N35 are therefore load-bearing, not clerical.

The executable exact diagnostic is `work/cycle6/c10_l06/boundary_coset_check.py`; its saved output is `work/cycle6/c10_l06/boundary_coset_check.out`. The script checks enabled transitions and reachability for totals one and two and rational witnesses on both sides of the endotactic direction split. The universal all-direction endotactic proof is the two-case argument above; finite code is not used as its proof.

A second useful source audit concerns the apparent second-order transient counterexample in Xu, Example 4.15. The paper labels it (1,1)-endotactic, not standard endotactic. In its boundary class \(\{A=0\}\), the active subnetwork is \(0\to B\to2B\), which can be transient; but for \(w=(0,1)\), the source \(B\) is maximal among essential sources and its edge \(B\to2B\) has positive projection. So this example cannot refute recurrence for the standard endotactic target. It does show that replacing full endotacticity by only a \(\mathbf1\)-direction condition is insufficient.
