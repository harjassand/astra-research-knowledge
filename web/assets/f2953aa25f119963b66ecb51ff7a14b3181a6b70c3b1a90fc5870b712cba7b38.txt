INDEPENDENT DERIVATION AND EXACT BASELINE
For t>0 define the positive radial measure
  nu_t(dy)=(4*pi*t)^(-3/2) exp(-t/4-|y|^2/(4t)) sinh(|y|/2)/(|y|/2) d^3y.
On each spin-j irreducible representation, rotational invariance makes integral exp(y.J) nu_t(dy) a scalar. Evaluating the spin-j character and differentiating the Gaussian cosh integral gives that scalar exp(t*j(j+1)); hence
  exp(t J^2)=integral exp(y.J) nu_t(dy)
on the full N-qubit representation. Since exp(y.J)=[exp(y.sigma/2)]^(tensor N), normalizing each factor gives an exact mixture of identical one-qubit products, with branch law proportional to nu_t(dy)[2 cosh(|y|/2)]^N. In particular A=b=0 and t=2/N is fully separable for every finite N, not only asymptotically.

The critical quartic scale follows directly from this integral. Put y=4 N^(-1/4) x and r=|x|. With u=|y|/2=2N^(-1/4)r, the radial log weight, after dropping constants, is
  -2 sqrt(N) r^2 + N log cosh(u) + log(sinh(u)/u)
  = -4 r^4/3 + O(N^(-1/2)(r^2+r^6))
on bounded r. The quadratic terms cancel. The volume/Jacobian gives the three-dimensional radial factor; the limiting Lebesgue density is proportional to exp(-4|x|^4/3). Thus the quartic law has an elementary connection to the critical collective Gibbs state and is plausibly within the old fluctuation lineage. This calculation does not show that a 1991 local/fluctuation theorem controls the full N-body density.

EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO A COMMON INFERENCE

Both states have identical reduced states on every proper subsystem and the same full J_z outcome law, but rho_G-rho_C=(|0^N><1^N|+|1^N><0^N|)/2 has trace distance 1/2. This exact witness shows that local marginals plus a longitudinal fluctuation law do not entail whole-state trace-norm approximation or separability.
