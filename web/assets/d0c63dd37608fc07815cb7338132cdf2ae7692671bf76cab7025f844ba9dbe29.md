## 1. Spectral barycenters improve the given-law quantum repetition mechanism

**Exact question.** For a finite two-player one-round game G, let v be its finite-dimensional tensor-product entangled value, d=|A||B|, and let k independent question pairs be tested by an arbitrary joint strategy. How small is the probability of winning at least ceil((v+delta)k), for 0<delta<1-v?

**Author claim.** Family 277's main theorem has a universal fifth-power threshold exponent using Song TR26-199's postselection-stable rounding. Its separate fixed-law result has cubic exponent with the root/support coefficient `1/[16384(1+kappa_mu)(1+log d)]`, where kappa_mu uses component size and least atom. The complete substantive TeX was read and its resolvent/Jensen, answer transport, mixed exposure, two entropy telescopes and probability transfer were reconstructed. Song's external sampler was read only at the exact interface; our result does not use it. The release's Lean scope prose advertises delta^13 and explicitly excludes the cubic result. No Lean run or formal verification of our deduction is claimed.

**Our deduction.** On each support component j, use the reversible conditional-resampling chain

    P_j=(E[.|X]+E[.|Y])/2 on L2(mu_j),
    sigma_j=gap(I-P_j off constants), sigma=min_j sigma_j.

Singleton components have zero variance; take sigma=1 if all are singletons. Each finite component has positive gap. The source's mixed exposure yields common vectors f_ir(z), local anchors and exact transported answer laws with total anchor error at most 2p tau, where tau=(log(1/p)+|C|log d)/(k-|C|). Hilbert-valued Poincare gives total barycenter variance E<=p tau/sigma. Taking the actual component means as shared vectors gives the exact mass identity P_hat=P-E. The same local POVMs and a correlated classical flag form a legal strategy, and

    |T-T_hat| <=(sqrt P+sqrt P_hat)sqrt E <=2sqrt(PE).

Thus one need not pay a separate linear norm-loss term. Component drift is bounded by product relative entropy and Pinsker. The fully proved conditioning estimate is

    sbar <= v+sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m))+2sqrt(tau/sigma).

The purely probabilistic source transfer at T=sigma delta^2/128 gives

    Pr[W_k>=ceil((v+delta)k)]
       <=exp[-sigma delta^3 k/(2048(1+log d))].                 (Q1)

For connected support the denominator 2048 improves to 1024. The bound is uniform in finite strategy dimension and passes to the supremum.

**Decisive control and obstruction.** A product question law has sigma=1/2, independent of its least atom, so the mechanism eliminates the root coefficient's rare-atom penalty. This example is not a new free-game theorem: earlier specialized results can be stronger. For the nearly disconnected 2x2 law with diagonal masses (1-epsilon)/2 and off-diagonal masses epsilon/2, sigma=epsilon. A chain eigenvector attains the Poincare variance/anchor-cost ratio. Removing sigma from this generic mean-vector argument is therefore impossible; it does not exclude another universal cubic proof.

**Nearest prior art.** The exact resampling connection graph and Poincare tool are in Dinur--Harsha--Venkat--Yuen, arXiv1610.08349v2, Definitions 2/4 and Theorem 6. Their result is classical multiplayer all-wins with gap-squared/fifth-power dependence. The new release supplies the common quantum vectors and exact transport; our change combines them with means and the graph gap. Historical novelty requires a broader entangled-expander/fortification comparison.

## 2. The transport extends to arbitrary commuting algebras

This is a genuine change of strategy interface rather than an identification of commuting and finite tensor models. Frontier_check_repetition proposed separate resolvent ancillas for two commuting von Neumann algebras A,B on an arbitrary Hilbert space H. I independently reconstructed the operator gate, including the continuous-spectrum case, and then read and checked that worker's complete game-level proof.

**Missing ingredient removed.** For a bounded effect T, the common map F_T h(s)=T(T+sI)^-1h still satisfies F_T*F_T=T. The estimate

    ||F_(T+epsilon I)-F_T||<=sqrt epsilon

allows the inverse-resolvent Jensen inequality to pass to zero in operator norm. For finer effects G_a summing to T, the expression involving T^-1/2 is replaced by a bounded positive form contraction K_a on supp T. The strong limits

    (T+epsilon I)^-1/2 G_a(T+epsilon I)^-1/2 ->K_a,
    F_T(T+epsilon I)^-1/2 ->V_T

retain algebra membership. The transported POVM V_T K_a V_T* with default completion has exact pullback G_a. Alice uses the s ancilla, Bob the t ancilla; their amplified algebras commute for all inputs, and the double-resolvent vector has the exact joint fine-answer law. Both entropy telescopes use the positive vector functional <psi,FH psi>, requiring no finite-dimensional density matrix or trace. Arbitrary-H means and finite block flags then yield legal commuting strategies. Component tensoring and independent tensor copies close the game proof.

**Internally derived theorem.** Q1 holds with v=omega^co(G) for every arbitrary-Hilbert-space commuting strategy. Its all-wins limit is

      <=exp[-sigma(1-omega^co(G))^3 k/(2048(1+log d))].         (Q2)

Every finite question law has positive component gaps, so this gives qualitative exponential threshold and all-wins repetition whenever omega^co(G)<1. It is distribution-dependent and does not acquire the gap, value, state or a finite-dimensional realization. A third independent internal audit by frontier_functional is pending.

For independent finite games G_i with scores V_i in [0,1], values v_i in one fixed finite-tensor or commuting model, d_max=max_i |A_i||B_i| and sigma the minimum component gap,

## 3. Exact Fourier rows do not supply positive sparse image enforcement

**Target interface.** The imported sparse-PCP target is one efficient common isometry V for the actual amplified hard Hamiltonian H, with positive contractions C,B, P=VV*, ker C=ran V, C>=gamma(I-P), and alpha H<=V*BV<=beta H. Alpha,beta,gamma are independent of instance size and amplification depth. Polynomial row enumeration, entry computation, bits, norms, invalid encodings and all acquisition are charged. Existing compression inequalities remove the need for B to preserve the image, but do not construct B,C. Restricted commuting Clifford-involution constructions do not cover the general five-qubit rank-one Clifford projectors: for three or more qubits, such a projector's reflection is generally non-Clifford.

**Author mechanism.** Family 283 provides matched Fourier field encoders with at most L entries per row after contracting a small unitary block. A target-dependent unrestricted Boolean table supplies amplitudes, coefficients, support lists, recursion and phase words. The manuscript's polynomial oracle circuit does not give polynomial-time table acquisition, and row support of E does not bound row support of E*.

**Our exact quantitative obstruction.** For a unit vector c, a positive contraction K with Kc=0, p0=|c0|^2>0, a=K00 and row-neighbor mass p_N satisfies

    a<=p_N/(p0+p_N).                                         (row lemma)

The kernel equation plus Cauchy--Schwarz gives a^2p0<=p_N(a-a^2), using K^2<=K. A normalized gap K>=gamma(I-cc*) gives a>=gamma(1-p0). For c=(1/sqrt2,1/sqrt(2m),...,1/sqrt(2m)), a row with r tail neighbors therefore satisfies

    gamma<=2r/(m+r), hence r>=gamma m/(2-gamma).               (Q3)

The lemma allows arbitrary phase/sign patterns and couplings to coordinates where c vanishes. It still holds with additional completely occupied image sectors.

**Actual source realization.** Take the Hermitian unitary block

    U_c=[[I-cc*,c],[c*,0]].

Its internal D=0, so the exact forward Fourier encoder maps every port basis vector to frequency 0 and the internal basis vector to c at frequency -v1. It is an exact isometry with one nonzero per row. Its image projection is I on the zero-frequency ports and cc* in the chosen frequency sector. Q3 applies to every positive constant-gap enforcer for this image in these coordinates. For dimension 2^n, it forces an Omega(2^n) row, even with every oracle bit and coefficient supplied freely. The larger prescribed source partition is also realized by

    U_Q=[[I-Q*Q,Q*],[Q,0]],

where Q has orthonormal rows and its first row is c*. Distinct internal frequencies retain one nonzero per encoder row and the same peaked sector. Thus the obstruction is within admissible exact source field orders rather than an arbitrary unrelated sparse isometry.

**Robust variant.** If ||Kc||<=epsilon and K00>=gamma/2, then

    r>=8m(gamma/(2sqrt2)-epsilon)_+^2.

For epsilon<=gamma/(4sqrt2), r>=m gamma^2/4. Small enough residual does not remove the support requirement. The diagonal/gap hypothesis remains necessary.

**Competent control and alternatives.** K=I-P_E has exact kernel and gap one, with the required dense row. A changed basis sending c to a basis vector, or an auxiliary encoding spreading its large coordinate, evades the same-coordinate premise. Neither is ruled out. Their efficient shared acquisition, row/entry implementation and B compression must still be constructed. This is not a universal encoding no-go theorem, oracle lower bound or sparse-PCP impossibility.

**265 precise scope.** The PEPS companion assumes fixed local dimension, strength, a full excitation gap and unique ground state on an open square grid. It gives polynomial bond dimension and normalized global vector error at most 1/L. Its introduction supplies the ground state Omega to the initial party; the tensors may depend arbitrarily on Omega. The area-law positive replacement, section 3 Proposition `prop:positive`, was reconstructed: filter local h_i and subtract <Omega,h_i Omega>I to obtain M_i, then k_i=|M_i|/c*. This is useful positive conversion at a supplied gapped-ground-state interface. It does not remove the imported debt:

- The full excitation gap is distinct from a normalized ground-energy promise gap. Nonlocal or degenerate hard sparse-PCP inputs are outside the theorem's hypotheses.
- Ground expectations are charged input data; no efficient centering or PEPS tensor acquisition is provided.
