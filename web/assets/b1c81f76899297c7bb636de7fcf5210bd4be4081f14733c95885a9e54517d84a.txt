Independent audit of the constant-depth contraction addendum, 2026-10-09
Append-only. Read together with decoder_coordinator_audit_v1.txt.
Target: work/sol/local_chart_decoder/v2.txt, frozen Sol6 proof.

Exact checked interface: for any fixed alpha>0, a supplied GGJ code member
has a fixed classical circuit of O(N) gates, constant graph depth and
bounded fanout mapping its noisy CSS syndrome to a correction f, with
physical stabilizer-reduced residual <=alpha E+beta M under explicit
positive linear data/syndrome promises. Preprocessing the supplied code
incidences/local tables/coloring costs O(N). All constants can be enormous;
there is no claim of efficient discovery/acquisition of the code family,
Euclidean geometric locality, or realistic overhead.

1. Gain version of filling.
The local minimum on an ell-face u differs from the current local weight
by q_u. Every such correction is also a legal correction above any vertex
v of u, so q_u<=g_v. At ell=d the correction space is zero and q_u=0.
In the support recursion the extra term is r_(d,ell) q_u. Its coefficient
after unwinding is (d-ell)!/product_(h=ell+1)^d r_(d,h), exactly cancelling
the leading r. At most
binom(t,ell)binom(t-ell,d-ell)n_+^d
pairs (u,f) are assigned to any chosen vertex. Dropping support indicators
is safe. Thus the J_d formula and inequality (1.2) in Sol v2 follow without
a new local-code or spectral hypothesis.

2. Sweep charging.
The conflict graph uses full syndrome stars, and maximum degree is at
most (2^d-1)ell_d. Same-color flips have disjoint syndrome footprints.
Gain g_v is 2-Lipschitz in block Hamming distance: the current weight and
the minimum of translated weights are each 1-Lipschitz. A positive-gain
flip changes at most ell_d syndrome blocks and has integer gain at least
one. Thus changed support is bounded by ell_d times the actual gain.
Every changed d-face lies in 2^d vertex stars. Summing temporal triangle
inequalities for vertices when their colors are reached gives

 sum_v g_v(initial) <= (1+2^(d+1)ell_d) total_weight_decrease.

This is a valid charge even when an earlier flip destroys a later gain.
Zero-gain changes are explicitly forbidden; without that tie rule the
raw-support/gain charge would fail. No global best-color choice is used.

3. Noisy contraction and homology budget.
All ideal corrections are coboundaries, so delta b=delta m at every
stage. Measured syndrome weight decreases throughout each color sweep,
preserving the small-support premise. With C_B=M_d D_d/c_d and
tau=min(1/2,c_d/(J_d C_gain)), the available-gain bound yields

 |b_after| <= (1-tau)|b_before| + tau C_B M.

For |b|>=C_B M this weakens the available positive decrease; below that
threshold nonnegative decrease proves it directly. Iteration is therefore
valid on both sides, not only above a noise floor. Fixed q makes the
data coefficient D_j rho^q/eps_j as small as desired.

Across all q sweeps total positive gains are bounded by the initial
syndrome weight. The number of accepted flips is no larger than that
gain, and each correction has support at most ell_j. Therefore the raw
residual budget from v1 still holds. Its combination with the new small
same-syndrome representative lies below cohomological distance under
the displayed v2 promise (3.5)/(3.8). This preserves the correct
stabilizer-reduced metric and rules out a hidden logical-cycle error.

4. Transpose and binary factors.
The K decoder's residual w now has both data and noise terms. In the
unchanged reverse comparison, top data support alpha_K P_data E is
multiplied by H^L and J's factor G. This gives exactly
alpha_trans=G H^L P_data alpha_K. Choosing the K contraction smaller
compensates for these fixed factors; its linear input promises are
explicitly retained. No cancellation or H/T commutation beyond v1's
actual-stalk identities is used.

The maximum binary block size B enters the full physical theorem.
Choosing each sector's block contraction <=alpha/(2B) ensures that the
sum of the two output bit supports is <=alpha times the minimum incoming
physical Pauli support plus B max(beta_F,beta_trans) times syndrome-bit
fault support. Syndrome-block counts sum to at most the number of faulty
syndrome bits. The two sector data/noise budgets are intersected and
shrunk, giving positive constants independent of N.

5. Circuit and Sol3 interface.
Each local optimal-flip function has a fixed finite input/output space,
so exhaustive hardwired truth tables compile to fixed bounded-fanin,
bounded-fanout circuits. Fixed-color processing and fixed q produce
constant depth. K preprocessing, projections, H and J have finite
support and fixed t-stage ladders; their fanout is also bounded by a
constant independent of N. The circuit remains O(N) in size. Its static
code description, coloring and local tables are supplied/preprocessed.

This meets the deterministic contraction assumption used by
work/sol/quantum_faults/v1.txt through v3.txt: alpha can be chosen below
any fixed required threshold, the data/syndrome promises are positive,
and circuit faults can have bounded causal cones after a local reversible
compilation. The coherent shared-bath and aggregate-fault norm estimates
are separate assumptions/proofs; this audit establishes none of those.
The native CNOT/transfer theorem still has its own supplied encoded-state,
interblock edge, logical addressing, and final logical-decoding boundaries.

Conclusion: no gap found in Sol6 v2's displayed algebra, support budgets,
or fixed-parameter circuit accounting. This is an internal independent
audit, with T22 doing a separate source/finite stress audit. The GGJ good
qLTC construction remains source-reported; novelty and external
validation remain unknown. This proof improves the scoped candidate
from sequential decoding to constant-depth contraction and should be
assessed on that exact stronger interface.
