0. CONSTANT ORDER AND BOUNDED SCALES
Choose a fixed enlargement ratio, for example one block layer. Choose
the animal parameter below the fixed counting threshold in the auxiliary
proof, then choose delta,delta0,k0,even L by the admitted sparse theorem.
The admitted block constant c_*>0 is now fixed. This choice is independent
of V,R,q. All subsequent constants may depend on c0,C0 and these fixed
parameters, never on domain size, shape or component count.

If R/L is bounded by a fixed threshold, exact zero mass permits subtracting
h(x0) at any point of supp(q), giving
  |sum qh|<=sum|q| diam(Q)<=C0^2 R.
In the anchored alternative choose any pin in K; its distance to each
tested point is O(C0 R), and its zero height gives the same bound up to
a fixed factor. Thus bounded R/L has a deterministic uniform bound.
Empty weights/free set are immediate. Below assume N=ceil(R/L) is large.

1. ORDINARY BLOCKS, DETERMINISTIC PIN COLLARS AND COARSE WEIGHTS
Tile Q_z=Lz+{0,...,L}^2. An index is ordinary/eligible if its fixed square
enlargement Q_z^* contains no fixed vertex. Every other block is a
DETERMINISTIC pin/collar block, treated as a good zero block after the
truncation below, never as a member of the random bad set B(h).
Apply the sparse premise to the failures on ordinary blocks of the two
slack tests. B(h) is finite, since ordinary blocks lie in the finite free
set. Its retained filled hulls, d_z,d_e and geometry events are those in
SPARSE_HOLE_AND_TRACE_PROOF.txt.

Fix v_z=Lz+(0,L/2), the left-face midpoint, as representative. Partition
ambient vertices into HALF-OPEN cells Lz+{0,...,L-1}^2 and put
  w_z=sum_{x in cell z}q_x.
Then
  sum|w|<=C0,  max|w|<=C0 L^2/R^2<=4C0/N^2,
and the support fits in an O(N) coarse square. Moreover sum w=sum q
EXACTLY. No individual q-weight is dropped or renormalized.
If K is the connected physical pin set in the anchored alternative,
mapping each point to its half-open cell gives a nearest-neighbor
connected coarse pin set. Its coordinate diameter is at least c0 R/L-2,
hence comparable to N for large N. It lies together with supp(w) in an
O(N) square. These coarse sites are pin/collar blocks.

Choose a coarse square S of side O(N) covering weights and the coarse
anchor, and a fixed larger square S+ with separation of order N. Leave
another fixed microscopic margin for rims. Only blocks indexed in S+
will be integrated below. This localization prevents a spurious use of
energy or deficits at arbitrary distant blocks.

2. EXACT RETAINED DENSITY
Integrate the interiors of ALL ordinary Q_z indexed in S+. Retain every
other free coordinate in the entire finite volume, including the vertices
of pin/collar blocks and all outside vertices. Interiors of distinct
blocks have no connecting square edge; their shells are retained.
Writing s for retained heights, its EXACT unnormalized marginal is
  p(s)=1_A(s) product_{ordinary z in S+} exp(F_z(s|shell)),
  Z=int p(s) ds>0.                                  (12)
A consists of the remaining retained-edge constraints. Representatives
v_z are on block-grid lines and are always retained (or fixed).

The density p is even, log-concave and compactly supported. Log-concavity
follows either by Brunn-Minkowski for the factors and convexity of A,
or by marginal log-concavity of the uniform indicator of the full convex
polytope. Fiber volumes are upper semicontinuous: when s_n tends to s,
limsup of the filling indicators is bounded by the limiting indicator,
and all fibers lie in a common compact coordinate box. Reverse Fatou
gives the volume assertion; multiplying by the closed constraint indicator
preserves it. Thus p is upper semicontinuous as well.

The tested retained linear functional is
  ell(s)=sum_z w_z s(v_z),
where fixed zero representatives contribute zero. For every original
zero-pinned configuration the global 1-Lipschitz extension gives
  |sum_x q_x h(x)-ell(s)|<=2L sum|q|<=2L C0.          (13)

3. ENERGY OF EVERY FEASIBLE ENDPOINT DISPLACEMENT
Take ANY retained perturbation chi with p(s+chi)p(s-chi)>0. Choose any
full feasible endpoint fillings h+,h- over those shells. Their half
difference extends chi to a globally 1-Lipschitz field vanishing on ALL
pins: on an edge its increment has magnitude at most
(|grad h+|+|grad h-|)/2<=1. Thus
  X_z=chi(v_z)
is globally L-Lipschitz on the coarse nearest-neighbor grid.

Every representative and shared face center in or next to a pin/collar
block is within a fixed distance M0=O(L) of some physical pin. Fix M0
above that distance once and for all and put u=T_M0(X). Then u=0 on
all pin/collar blocks, including the coarse anchor. T_M0 is 1-Lipschitz.

Now fix a FULL midpoint configuration h with retained vector s whose
random-bad geometry is in the event from the auxiliary coercivity proof.
On an ordinary good block define
  g_z=F_z(s)-[F_z(s+chi)+F_z(s-chi)]/2.
The admitted block lemma applies: h supplies precisely the midpoint
slack tests, and (12) supplies positive endpoint fibers. For adjacent
ordinary good blocks z,w with shared face center f,
  |u_z-u_w|^2 <=2(|chi(v_z)-chi(f)|^2
                       +|chi(v_w)-chi(f)|^2)
               <=2c_*^(-1)(g_z+g_w).                (14)
If a good ordinary z neighbors a pin/collar block, |chi(f)|<=M0 and
  |u_z|<=|chi(v_z)-chi(f)|,
so |u_z|^2<=c_*^(-1)g_z. Edges between pin/collar sites contribute zero.
Good rim edges lie outside the filled hulls, hence their endpoints are
ordinary good sites or pin/collar sites. On the no-crossing-buffer event,
every energy edge needed to fill S lies inside S+. Bounded degree gives
  E=sum_{good edges in S+}|grad u|^2
       <=C sum_{ordinary z in S+}g_z.                (15)
All other g_z are nonnegative by convex-volume concavity. In particular
(15) does not presume a positive lower bound on every block deficit.

The auxiliary coercivity result now gives, on an event G of full-field
probability at least 1/2, SIMULTANEOUSLY for every such chi,
  |ell(chi)| <= Cadd+Cen sqrt(sum_z g_z).             (16)
The event G depends only on the midpoint bad geometry and deterministic
anchor measure, not on chi or its endpoint fillings. Choose Cen>=1.
It is crucial that no separate good event is chosen for each chi.

4. A CONDITIONAL-EVENT OVERLAP GAP
Take t=Cadd+Cen, so t>Cadd, and define
  A_t(s)=sup_{ell(chi)=t} sqrt(p(s+chi)p(s-chi)).
If the endpoints have positive density, the retained constraint indicator
is one at both and the midpoint, so the logarithmic density loss is
EXACTLY sum_z g_z. Consequently (16) implies on G
  sqrt(p(s+chi)p(s-chi)) <= exp(-1)p(s)
for every chi with ell(chi)=t. Zero-density endpoints are harmless.
Log-concavity always gives 0<=A_t(s)<=p(s).

Measurability is explicit: nonzero products restrict the endpoints to a
fixed compact support, hence chi to its compact half-difference set;
upper semicontinuity makes the corresponding compact supremum upper
semicontinuous (set it to zero if the level has no feasible pair).

G may depend on the interiors which were integrated out. Let
gamma(s)=P(G|S_retained=s), a regular conditional probability. It satisfies
0<=gamma<=1 and int gamma(s)p(s)ds/Z=P(G)>=1/2. For almost every s with
gamma(s)>0, some full midpoint filling over s is in G. The SIMULTANEOUS
inequality then bounds A_t(s), which depends only on s. Hence, almost
everywhere,
  p(s)-A_t(s) >=(1-exp(-1))p(s)gamma(s).
Integration gives
  int A_t(s)ds/Z <=1-rho*,
  rho*=(1-exp(-1))/2>0.                              (17)
This avoids an unjustified assertion that all conditional midpoint
fillings are good, or a nonmeasurable projection of G.

5. PREKOPA-LEINDLER AND EXPONENTIAL TAILS
If ell vanishes on all retained free coordinates, (13) is deterministic
and the proof is finished. Otherwise take linear coordinates s=(r,xi),
r=ell(s), absorbing the constant Jacobian into dxi. Let
  a(r)=Z^(-1) int p(r,xi) dxi
be its even log-concave probability density. For any xi1,xi2, the midpoint
xi=(xi1+xi2)/2, and chi=(t,(xi2-xi1)/2), the definition of A_t gives
  A_t(r,xi)>=sqrt(p(r-t,xi1)p(r+t,xi2)).
Functional Prekopa-Leindler on the complementary coordinates therefore
implies
  sqrt(a(r-t)a(r+t))<=Z^(-1) int A_t(r,xi) dxi.
Integrating and using (17) bounds the shifted geometric overlap by
1-rho*. An even log-concave density decreases with |r|, so
  int min(a(r-t),a(r+t))dr =2 int_t^infinity a(r)dr
                         =P(|ell(S_retained)|>t).
The geometric overlap is at least this minimum overlap. Thus
  P(|ell(S_retained)|<=t)>=rho*.                    (18)

By marginal log-concavity applied to a(t+z)1_{z>=0}, the survival
F(t)=P(|ell(S_retained)|>t)=2 int_0^infinity a(t+z)dz is log-concave
for t>=0. It has F(0)=1. Concavity of log F and (18) imply
  F(jt)<=(1-rho*)^j, j=1,2,... .                    (19)
Set a0=-log(1-rho*)/(2t)>0. Summing over intervals [jt,(j+1)t) gives
  E exp(a0 |ell(S_retained)|)
    <= exp(a0 t)/[1-exp(a0 t)(1-rho*)] <infinity.    (20)
Equation (13) transfers (20) to sum qh, multiplying the bound by
exp(2a0 L C0). Increase the constant to cover the bounded-N deterministic
case in step 0. This proves the target at zero pins, uniformly over ALL
finite volumes, shapes, components and admissible weights.
