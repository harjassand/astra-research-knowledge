# Source and prior-art notes

Scope: selective primary-source audit for a theoretical aging/regeneration branch. This is not a systematic review and does not establish novelty priority.

## Mechanism-level primary sources

**Sereda et al. (2026), Nature Aging.** “Decline of chaperone-mediated autophagy in aging impairs macrophage clearance of senescent cells.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-026-01240-w . Published 5 October 2026. Main source for the macrophage CMA/SIRPα line. The paper reports age- and secretome-associated CMA reduction, impaired phagocytosis/efferocytosis, altered SIRPα turnover and SHP-1, macrophage-Lamp2a conditional effects, delayed 6-mm wound repair, and systemic CA77.1 effects. Source data Fig. 4 XLSX downloaded from: https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs43587-026-01240-w/MediaObjects/43587_2026_1240_MOESM8_ESM.xlsx . Local SHA-256: `6dc9c342bf87f12e212f9f2b3e8349c7ee9640dbcdaa50dff61167d92e783f7f`.

Important exact interfaces from the article: (i) KFERQ-Dendra puncta report CMA cargo delivery; (ii) SA-β-gal, p21, HMGB1 and the RNA score are proxies for senescence/CMA; (iii) Fig. 4e efferocytosis is of staurosporine-killed targets; (iv) Fig. 4h is fluorescent IgG-bead uptake; (v) 23-month-old mice received systemic CA77.1 at 30 mg/kg, five days/week, for five months; (vi) the 6-mm wound is followed to a 15-day endpoint; (vii) LysM-Cre may also target monocytes and granulocytes; (viii) patient lung CMA scores are predictive/correlative, not direct clinical intervention evidence. Article passages and figure captions are indexed on its primary page.

**Schloesser et al. (2022), Journal of Cell Biology.** “Senescent cells suppress macrophage-mediated corpse removal via upregulation of the CD47-QPCT/L axis.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.202207097 . This preempts a generic anti-CD47/SIRPα or QPCT/L bypass claim: the paper reports contact-dependent suppression, reversal by checkpoint-axis/QPCT/L interference, and an important distinction—CD47 loss alone did not make senescent cells subject to macrophage engulfment.

**Ramírez-Pardo et al. (2025), Nature Metabolism.** “Chaperone-mediated autophagy sustains muscle stem cell regenerative functions but declines with age.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-025-01411-w . The primary report finds MuSC CMA loss impairs proliferation/regeneration and that CMA reactivation plus glycolysis enhancement improves aged mouse/human MuSC readouts. It is direct prior for generic CMA–MuSC rescue framing.

**Lee et al. (2026), Nature Aging.** “Glutamine-driven reductive TCA cycle metabolism supports aged muscle stem cell function via de novo lipogenesis.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-026-01120-3 ; public full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13284908/ . The study uses glutamine isotope labeling, targeted metabolomics, D2O labeling of palmitate, aged MuSC GLS restoration, fatty-acid bypass, transplantation and physical-function assays. The source supports the pathway and acute rescue under its protocol, but not repeated-injury reserve preservation or a joint single-cell transition matrix. Its own discussion identifies further lipidomic and metabolite-dynamics work as outstanding.

**Andersson et al. (2025), Nature Metabolism.** “Old mitochondria regulate niche renewal via α-ketoglutarate metabolism in stem cells.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-025-01325-7 . This is the tissue-repair child’s focal primary source for old-mitochondria-enriched intestinal stem-cell subpopulations, αKG, Paneth-cell fate bias, and aged regeneration readouts. The paper explicitly constrains deterministic fate by Paneth turnover considerations; the unmeasured position-specific division flux, residence time and lineage output prevent a numeric event-budget evaluation.

## Existing turnover, clearance and tissue models

**Ovadya et al. (2019), Journal of Gerontology A.** “Senescent cell turnover slows with age providing an explanation for the Gompertz law.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glz181 . Prior art for production/turnover/clearance feedback and age-dependent slowing; generic clearance-versus-production dynamics are therefore not a new result here.

**Pentinmikko et al. (2019), Nature.** DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1383-0 . Prior art for the intestinal Paneth-cell/Notum/Wnt niche and aged regeneration intervention. The tissue-repair child treats the newer mitochondrial-age/αKG mechanism as source-published, and only reconstructs conditional rate bounds.

**“Modelling the spatiotemporal dynamics of senescent cells in wound healing, chronic wounds, and fibrosis” (2024), PLOS Computational Biology.** DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012298 . Prior art for spatial source/clearance and wound/fibrosis phase dynamics; a broad spatial senescence feedback model is not claimed here.
