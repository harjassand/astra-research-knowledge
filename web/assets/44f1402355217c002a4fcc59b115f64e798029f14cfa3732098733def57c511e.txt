Exact quantum interface, using the source's maximum-energy sign convention:

 H = sum_{uv in E}[alpha_uv(XX+YY)+gamma_uv ZZ]

1. INDEPENDENT LOCAL FIELD PROOF
Take s>=0 rational, b>=0, c real rational, r=b+|c|. The shifted positive gate

 G=I+s(rI+bX+cZ) = [[u,d],[d,v]],
 u=1+s(r+c), v=1+s(r-c), d=sb.

Both diagonal entries are strictly positive. Complement the column index,
retain the row index, and assign x to the former, y to the latter. Its local
polynomial is

 q(x,y)=d+ux+vy+dxy.

The exact coefficient inequality is

 uv-d^2 = 1+2sr+s^2(r^2-c^2-b^2)
        = 1+2sr+2s^2 b|c| > 0.

For a general polynomial a+ux+vy+dxy with nonnegative coefficients and u,v>0,
direct log-Hessian computation gives log-concavity iff ad<=2uv. The field gate
therefore passes with extra margin; it even satisfies the bivariate stability
criterion ad<=uv, since here a=d. This stronger fact can be proved without a
stability theorem: for Im(x)>0, a potential root has
y=-(a+ux)/(v+dx), whose imaginary part is
-(uv-ad)Im(x)/|v+dx|^2<=0, hence cannot be in the upper half-plane.

The pair gate, already derived by the other auditor, has homogeneous polynomial

 q_e=a(x1x2+x3x4)+b(x1x3+x2x4)+c(x1x4+x2x3),
 a=1+s(3alpha+gamma), b=1+s(3alpha-gamma), c=2salpha.

Its Hessian eigenvalues are a+b+c, a-b-c, -a+b-c, -a-b+c. The last three are
nonpositive exactly in the required alpha>=|gamma| region. This is the same
local quadratic mechanism as source C.16, not a newly claimed six-vertex result.

2. HOMOGENEOUS FIELD LIFT LEMMA
For d>=0 and u,v>0 satisfying d^2<=uv, introduce two fresh variables z1,z2:

 F(x,y,z1,z2)=d z1z2+(u/2)x(z1+z2)+(v/2)y(z1+z2)+dxy.

F is nonnegative, rational, multiaffine and degree-two homogeneous, and
F(x,y,1,1)=q(x,y). It is log-concave on the positive orthant.

Proof for d>0: order the variables x,y,z1,z2. The x,y Hessian block
[[0,d],[d,0]] has inertia (one positive, one negative). Its Schur complement is

 [[-uv/(2d), d-uv/(2d)],
  [d-uv/(2d), -uv/(2d)]].

This has negative diagonal and determinant uv-d^2>=0, hence is negative
semidefinite. The full Hessian therefore has at most one positive eigenvalue.
A homogeneous nonnegative quadratic with this signature is log-concave:
writing F=1/2 x^T M x, the log-Hessian numerator is
F M-(Mx)(Mx)^T; the restriction of M to the M-orthogonal complement of the
positive vector x is nonpositive, and the x direction is strictly negative in
this numerator. For d=0, F=(ux+vy)(z1+z2)/2 is a product of positive linear
polynomials, so its logarithm is concave directly. This covers zero X fields,
identity gates, and boundary cases; no coefficient perturbation is needed.

3. EXACT GLOBAL LIFT AND SOURCE THEOREM 5.1 INTERFACE
Consider any finite trace circuit with e two-qubit pair gates and f one-qubit
field gates, counting repetitions. Each qubit's gates are connected in cyclic
time order. There are W=2e+f physical wires and 2W distinct physical half-edges.
Every wire joins one column end to one row end, even on a nonbipartite spatial
graph. Complement EVERY COLUMN index locally. Equality of the original wire
bits becomes exactly one selected half-edge per wire. This is an explicit
matrix-to-tensor transformation; it does not assume that arbitrary partial
complementation preserves log-concavity. The transformed local polynomials
were computed and checked above.

Replace each field factor by F, with its own two dummy variables. Let Ptilde be
the product of these and the unchanged pair factors. The factors have disjoint
variables, so Ptilde is multiaffine, homogeneous log-concave of degree 2e+2f.
On the enlarged set U of N=2W+2f=4e+4f variables, set

 Qtilde = product_{physical wire {h,h'}}(x_h+x_h')
                                     * e_f(all 2f dummy variables).

Here e_f is the elementary symmetric polynomial, including e_0=1. Qtilde is a
product on disjoint variables of partition- and uniform-matroid basis
polynomials, hence homogeneous log-concave of degree W+f=2e+2f. Every nonzero
coefficient is one. The degrees of Ptilde and Qtilde add to N.

Then

 Tr(circuit) = 2^{#inactive qubits} [x^U]Ptilde Qtilde.

Proof: a contributing Ptilde monomial selects exactly one physical endpoint
per wire, so its physical size is W. Its total degree forces exactly f selected
dummies. For every such dummy set the complementary dummy monomial in e_f has
coefficient one. Summing these choices is precisely setting all dummies to one
in the original product, which recovers the original local field factors.
Dummy choices are not individually pinned or assumed to have size one per
field: local dummy sizes zero and two are necessary and may compensate across
field gates. The global degree supplies exactly the required condition.

Theorem 5.1 requires homogeneous log-concave coefficient measures mu and nu
on a shared ground set, degree sum N, coefficient-range bounds and an
independent positive support seed for each. All are explicit here:
 - mu(S) is the product of local lifted table coefficients; returns zero if
   any local selection is absent. Its oracle needs O(e+f) table lookups and
   rational multiplications, with polynomial encoding length.
 - nu(S) tests one endpoint per wire and f dummies, and returns one or zero.
 - A mu seed chooses a positive diagonal term at each gate, with one dummy
   choice at each field gate. A nu seed chooses one endpoint per wire and any
   f dummies. They need not satisfy the two factors simultaneously.
 - R_nu=1. The exact R_mu is the product over local factors of their largest
   positive coefficient divided by their smallest positive coefficient,
   because the factors have disjoint variables. These six-entry extrema are
   computable from the input. If each local rational uses at most B0 bits,
   log R_mu=O((e+f)B0).

The imported homogeneous arithmetic bound is

 O(N^10 B^2 epsilon^-2 log^2(2B/epsilon)),
 B=N+log(R_mu R_nu),

on every execution, before charging rational bit/oracle costs and confidence
amplification. The source's Remark A.4 asserts polynomial rational-bit cost
for these oracles. The thermal construction in the other audit has size
polynomial in input length, n, numerical beta D and epsilon^-1, with
D=6 sum_e alpha_e+2 sum_v(b_v+|c_v|). It is not polynomial in log(beta D).
The homogeneous lift adds only two variables per field gate and a coefficient
one uniform-matroid oracle. No inverse trace or probability of a rare path is
introduced by the lift.

4. PRECISE THEOREM 1.6 / LAYER CLOSURE CHECK
Source Theorem 1.6 expressly allows NONHOMOGENEOUS multiaffine log-concave
polynomials. Thus the unlifted product P and physical partition polynomial Q
fit its stated assumptions. The algebraic field condition is not a reason to
exclude transverse fields from that theorem.

Its proof uses source Lemma A.3, citing RSJ19 Corollary 28, to assert that every
nonzero homogeneous component of a multiaffine log-concave polynomial is
log-concave. The published NeurIPS 2019 supplemental actually has Corollary 28
with precisely the homogeneous-component claim for strongly log-concave
polynomials; arXiv v1 numbers the same assertion Corollary 29. For multiaffine
polynomials, ordinary LC implies RSJ's coordinate-derivative SLC definition:
partial_i p is the pointwise limit p(x_i=t)/t as t tends to infinity; the
restrictions are LC, positive rescaling preserves LC, and iterating gives all
coordinate partials. Hence the statement-level hypotheses match.

A limited source-proof concern remains: that supplemental corollary's very
brief proof combines its Lemma 27 with main-text Theorem 2, whose statement
assumes independent-set matroid support. I have not independently repaired
that invocation for the full arbitrary-support nonhomogeneous interface or
produced a counterexample. This is an audit observation, not a disproof of
Lemma A.3. It is unnecessary for the present quantum theorem because Sections
2–3 directly satisfy homogeneous Theorem 5.1. Do not make it load-bearing merely
to save the two auxiliary variables per field gate.

5. PRIOR-ART AND PHYSICAL SCOPE
Bravyi–Gosset, arXiv:1612.05602, published PRL 119,100503 (2017), Eq.1/Thm.1:
 K_BG=sum(-b_ij XX+c_ij YY)+sum_i d_i(I+Z_i), |c_ij|<=b_ij,
 estimates Tr exp(-beta K_BG) in poly(n,beta,epsilon^-1), with coefficients
 normalized in magnitude to at most one. This already contains our gamma=0,
 b_v=0 XY-plus-Z-field slice. Generic gamma!=0 has interaction matrix rank
 three; the BG class has rank at most two, invariant under arbitrary product
 single-qubit unitaries. Also, the written BG field direction is orthogonal
 to its interaction plane; simultaneous X/Z fields do not follow from a
 single global rotation. These establish scope differences, not a claim that
 no alternative algorithm exists.

Harrow–Mehraban–Soleimanifar, arXiv:1910.09071, Thm.50 (2019/STOC2020):
 deterministic quasipolynomial approximation for an XXZ model in the regime
 Jzz>=|Jxy| with a uniform constant longitudinal field and no transverse X
 terms. Our |gamma|<=alpha cone is different and shares its isotropic positive
 boundary; arbitrary inhomogeneous c_v and b_v>=0 are additional interfaces.
 Their result must not be described as a general FPRAS for our cone.

Chen–Liu itself already contains source C.16 symmetric six-vertex counting and
C.17 EPR on arbitrary graphs / quantum MaxCut on bipartite graphs. The EPR
point gamma=-alpha with no fields is consequently already in the source.
The present derivation is an extension of an explicit application in that
preprint, with the counting innovation imported rather than independently new.

Marwaha–Sud, arXiv:2604.13026 (April2026), defines augmented EPR by conventional
energy terms

 K_b=(b+1)|psi+><psi+|+(b-1)|phi-><phi-|,
     =(b/2)I+(XX+bYY-ZZ)/2, -1<=b<=1.

On a bipartite graph, a Z rotation on one sublattice changes XX/YY signs, and
a common interchange of the Y/Z axes then turns -K_b, modulo its scalar, into
(XX+YY+bZZ)/2. Therefore bipartite augmented EPR is contained in the XXZ cone.
This does NOT cover generic augmented EPR on arbitrary graphs. The EPR endpoint
b=-1 can be mapped globally into our cone; b=+1 is trivial for ground-energy
estimation in their classification, which is not a partition-function claim.

There is an exact obstruction on odd cycles for |b|<1 even if one permits
arbitrary vertex product unitaries: the normalized interaction matrix for
-K_b is D=diag(-1,-b,+1). To map each edge to the positive-XY XXZ cone, its
singular values and determinant force A=diag(+1,+1,b), with a common axis
convention. The ordered product of edge interaction matrices around a cycle
transforms by orthogonal similarity under vertex rotations, since adjacent
R_v^T R_v cancel. For an odd cycle of length k, D^k has eigenvalues
{-1,-b^k,+1}, while A^k has {+1,+1,b^k}; these disagree for |b|<1. Thus no such
gauge exists. A nonlocal simulation reduction could still change scope; none
is supplied here. Do not claim that this exact XXZ corollary settles the
unrestricted augmented-EPR conjecture or the full Marwaha–Sud classification.
