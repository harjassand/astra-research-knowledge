1. THE CONTOUR IS COMBINATORIAL AND THE PHYSICAL GRAPH IS SQUARE

Take a finite Jordan square-cell domain with original boundary values
+lambda and-lambda on its two Dobrushin arcs,0<lambda<=1/2. Include
both endpoints of every original NN constraint in the graph; boundary
values are true original pins. The remaining height vector is uniform
on its hard NN Lipschitz body. The free configuration identically0
satisfies every constraint involving a free site strictly, so the body
has positive free-coordinate volume. Fixed-fixed boundary edges with
2lambda=1 do not remove any free-coordinate volume.

Draw the SAME NE diagonal in every elementary square solely to define
the ordered dual SIGN strand. Its primal NN constraints are unchanged.
At each triangle having mixed signs, exactly two of its three edges
are sign-changing. Join those edge midpoints by a combinatorial dual
segment. Follow the boundary-initiated strand, entering and leaving
each mixed triangle. Its geometric vertex locations are fixed triangle
centres/edge midpoints or another fixed combinatorial drawing; they do
NOT record the affine zero locations of the height interpolant.

Every free coordinate is nonzero almost surely by absolute continuity;
boundary coordinates are nonzero because lambda>0. Thus ties need not
be used to define the probabilistic kernel. In a finite domain the
boundary-initiated degree-two strand cannot enter a separate closed
cycle and has a finite number of visited triangles. Stop it at a
crossed-edge prefix, with the query response and exit decision completed.

2. EACH ACTUALLY QUERIED VERTEX HAS AN OPPOSITE SITE WITHIN DISTANCE2

The entry edge endpoints have known opposite signs. If the third
triangle vertex is new, query its SIGN. The exit edge joins it to the
known endpoint with the opposite sign. That edge is either an original
NN edge of distance1 or the NE diagonal of distance2 in the original
square NN graph. Hence for this newly queried vertex x there is a
known, oppositely signed vertex y with d1(x,y)<=2. It is either another
queried free vertex or an original boundary pin. This remains true at
all later times: the birth witness is retained in the queried record.

Let S be ALL free vertices actually queried in the completed prefix,
not an enlarged neighborhood guessed from the drawing. For any
configuration having the recorded signs, a positive queried x obeys
0<=h_x<=h_y+d1(x,y)<=2 and a negative one obeys-2<=h_x<=0.
The inequalities use the NN path of length at most2, not a fictitious
diagonal unit constraint. They hold throughout the closed sign fiber.
Original boundary heights have magnitude<=1/2. Thus the correct
temporary-freezing bound is2, with pair-coupling error4.

The oriented sequence of crossed edges and the starting orientation
determine each new queried sign inductively: the chosen exit identifies
which known entry endpoint has the opposite sign from the new vertex.
Conversely the queried signs determine the next edge. The actual
oriented combinatorial prefix therefore carries precisely this sign
query history, without additional numerical heights.

3. EXACT CONDITIONAL LAW FOR EVERY LEGAL STOPPED PREFIX

Write mu(dh)=1_K(h)dh/vol(K) for the original full finite law. A legal
exploration chooses the next query/exit from its previously recorded
signs, positions and an independent auxiliary random seed. Its stopping
time is adapted to that record. No height-based stop, affine crossing
ratio, off-prefix hidden probe or unrecorded height-value comparison
is included in this assertion. The complete auxiliary seed can be
recorded; all statements then hold for almost every seed, and for
every sign transcript of positive conditional probability for that seed.

Fix such a completed stopped transcript r, whose queried sites are
S(r) with signs epsilon_x. Conditional on the seed, induction on the
queries shows that the event of this stopped transcript is EXACTLY
the following subset of the original body:
   K_r = K intersect {epsilon_x h_x>=0 for all x in S(r)}.       (3.1)
Indeed all query locations, exits and previous non-stopping decisions
are already decided by those recorded signs. Every height vector in
the displayed sign fiber reproduces the same queries and stop. This
also checks the stopping decision, rather than conditioning only on
an event that the path happens to contain some unordered edges.

The strict inequalities or their closed versions differ by Lebesgue
null sets. Hence
   mu(dh | r) = 1_Kr(h)dh/vol(K_r).                            (3.2)
For auxiliary random choices recorded without the whole seed, the
record likelihood is constant on this fiber, since all their decisions
depend only on the same queried signs. Integrating the independent
seed therefore leaves(3.2) unchanged whenever that record has positive
probability. Null transcripts can receive an arbitrary kernel and do
not affect the assertion. All actual transcripts with positive volume
have the exact sign-interval law(3.2), regardless of how small their
probability is. In particular no lower bound on that probability is
used to divide a sparsity estimate.

The prefix may look like an absorbing set for an independent walk,
but its queried heights in(3.2) are MOVABLE, not fixed pins. The two
sides are not asserted independent: movable string heights may couple
them. Conditional on a PATCH EXTERIOR, separated patch interiors
factor for the elementary NN Gibbs reason used in the sparsity proof.

4. QUENCHED SIGN-PREFIX SPARSITY AND ITS EXACT SCOPE

The sites in(3.2) satisfy the opposition hypothesis by Section2, and
all their sign intervals contain0. Apply CONDITIONED_SPARSE_PROOF.txt
with its constants chosen only from rho,m and the degree4 zero CAP.
For every legal sign transcript r and every finite index set A,
   P_mu_r(all specified eligible blocks are bad)<=rho^|A|.      (4.1)
This is the STATIC bound and also its declared SHORT-TIME bound for
a stationary normally reflected variance-two motion on K_r itself.
The latter is a conditional motion with sign walls, not a conditional
version of the original unconditioned reflected motion's whole path.

It follows that a box collection chosen as a measurable function of
the legal SIGN transcript is allowed: condition on that transcript,
then A is fixed and(4.1) holds with the same constants. For random
cardinality the exact statement is the conditional bound rho^|A(r)|,
and annealing gives its expectation. Collections selected later from
unobserved heights to hunt for bad gradients are not covered.

An extra thin band[0,epsilon] on a positive queried site, or its negative
analogue, is a separately specified event. If conditioning leaves just
these coordinate intervals and the original NN body, the abstract
sign-interval theorem remains uniform for all positive widths. A
length-zero band is a true pin at0. Such optional extra interval tests
are not part of the ordinary sign-only prefix filtration above.

5. ANNEALED RESAMPLING: ONE-TIME FIELD LAW, NOT THE OLD PATH LAW

Let R be any legal transcript with regular conditional kernel K_R of
the original field H, including a legal sign record as above. Given R,
draw H' from that SAME conditional kernel, using any auxiliary
construction with exactly that marginal. For bounded measurable f,
   E f(H') = E[ integral f(h) K_R(dh) ] = E f(H).
More strongly,(R,H') and(R,H) have the same joint distribution. Thus
H' has the original ANNEALED one-time law. For a sign transcript,
H' re-explores the same stopped record if the auxiliary seed is retained,
since it has the same queried signs. Any stationary motion with
conditional invariant marginal K_R has this original annealed marginal
at each deterministic physical time after integrating R.

This identity does not equate the full path law to that of the original
unconditioned RBM. Conditioning on signs or values changes its walls
or fixed coordinates and hence its generator. Nor does it transfer an
unconditional probability bound to every realized value fiber. The
exact quenched conclusion(4.1) has its own proof because sign sites
remain movable and have the required bounded birth witnesses.

For a FIELD-ONLY event of a FIXED deterministic set of boxes, annealed
marginal preservation transfers any estimate of the original law.
A recomputed mask which only removes such events also preserves its
upper bound. Keeping flags computed from an old unobserved field while
testing a resampled field is a different event and is not silently
treated this way. An arbitrary set of boxes chosen from an exact-VALUE
transcript can select rare near contacts, so its uniform conditional
estimate is not supplied by the annealed identity.

6. EXACT VALUE RECORDS AND TWO-READING FREEZING

For clarity, a genuine adaptive exact-value transcript has a different
kernel. If each chosen next coordinate and stop are predictable from
the recorded earlier values/signs and independent seed, disintegration
of the finite uniform density gives the uniform remaining Gibbs body
with those recorded values FIXED and recorded signs enforced. Almost
every section has its proper volume density. The predictability check
ensures there is no hidden extra inequality in unrecorded coordinates.
For two originally independent fields, a FULL joint transcript of such
queries has the product of their conditional remaining Gibbs densities:
the choices are functions of the transcript, and hence introduce no
new cross-coordinate likelihood once the complete transcript is given.

Replacing either remaining field by ANY construction with that exact
conditional marginal retains its original individual annealed law by
Section5. Their new joint law need not remain a product. This is the
legitimate one-time adapted-freezing transfer. If each reading separately
has a fixed-set estimate rho^k, a union-of-two-readings bad event has
the elementary assignment bound
   P(all k boxes bad in at least one reading)
       <=2^k rho^(k/2),                                     (6.1)
using the larger assigned subset and the individual annealed estimates,
not independence of the coupled readings. Constants can be retuned.

This does NOT prove that every reading created by another field's sign
queries is itself a bounded/opposed sign string: it need not be. Nor
does it prove that newly fixed revealed values remain original small
pins. EXPLICIT_VALUE_REVEAL_FAILURE.txt gives a legal Dobrushin
sign prefix followed by one extra value reveal for which a near-edge
failure has conditional probability1. A patch protected from such
extra pins can use the local pin-distance criterion in the main proof,
but its long-shell bound and exact conditional law must still be checked.

7. WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN TRANSFERRED FROM THE SOURCE

Source interface.tex1-30 specifies the ordered dual sign strand rather
than affine zero ratios;1399-1402 says stopping adds no unobserved value
freeze. Source foundations.tex673-725 distinguishes conditional sparse
sign kernels from adaptive exact-value annealing. Source coupling.tex

identities above were independently derived for the finite square law.

The source's assertions of slit harmonic sampling, connected absorber
capacity/thickness, conditional covariance, trace height gap or curve
identification are not concluded here. In particular square NE contours
need their own geometric/harmonic inputs after this conditional-sparse
gate; the zero-field GFF result does not supply those interfaces.
