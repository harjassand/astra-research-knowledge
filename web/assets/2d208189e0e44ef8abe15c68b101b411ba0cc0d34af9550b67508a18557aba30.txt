SMOOTH FILTER WITNESSES FOR POSITIVE-PENALTY GIBBS ROBUSTNESS
8 October 2026. Root derivation; internal candidate, no novelty claim.

This is a reusable form of standard Gibbs variational and entropy arguments.
The scientific application requires an actual small-cost witness, not merely
this inequality. It extends PROJECTION_PRINCIPLE.txt beyond hard projections.

FINITE-DIMENSION THEOREM
Let K,M commute with 0<=K<=kappa M, H0=K-mu M,
rho0=exp(-beta H0)/Z0. Let 0<=F<=I commute with M, put
p=Tr rho0 F^2=1-delta>0, qM=Tr rho0 M(I-F^2),
sigma=F rho0 F/p, and
Dir=(1/2)Tr rho0 [F,[K,F]].
For s>0 and Z_M(s)=Tr exp(-sM), set
B=h2(delta)+s qM+delta log Z_M(s)
  +[beta delta E0K+beta Dir
    +beta abs(mu)(delta E0M+qM)]/p.
Then D(sigma||rho0)<=B.

For any W>=0 with WF=0, rhoW=exp[-beta(H0+W)]/ZW satisfies
D(rhoW||rho0)<=B, hence ||rhoW-rho0||_1<=sqrt(2B).
More generally if w=Tr sigma W is finite, the same conclusion holds with
B+beta w without requiring WF=0. A lower bound W>=-u I permits the bound
B+beta(w+u), assuming all Gibbs expressions exist. No operator monotonicity
of the exponential is used.

PROOF OF ENTROPY COST
Put G=(I-F^2)^(1/2) and omega=G rho0 G/delta if delta>0.
The map X->FXF+GXG is unital and trace preserving. Its output entropy is
at least S(rho0) and at most h2(delta)+p S(sigma)+delta S(omega).
Consequently S(rho0)-S(sigma)<=h2(delta)+delta S(omega), after dropping
-delta S(sigma)<=0. Since [G,M]=0, Tr omega M=qM/delta and the Gibbs
maximum-entropy bound gives delta S(omega)<=s qM+delta log Z_M(s).
The delta=0 case is the continuous interpretation and rho0 full rank then
forces F=I. This proof does not require F to be an orthogonal projection.

PROOF OF ENERGY COST
The exact double-commutator identity is
FKF=(F^2 K+K F^2)/2+[F,[K,F]]/2.
Because rho0 commutes with K, its expectation is
Tr rho0 FKF=Tr rho0 K F^2+Dir<=E0K+Dir.
The last inequality uses K rho0>=0 and I-F^2>=0, not [K,F]=0.
Thus E_sigma K-E0K<=(delta E0K+Dir)/p.
Also E_sigma M-E0M=(delta E0M-qM)/p.
Adding the entropy and energy bounds proves the trial relative entropy.
There is no assumed sign of Dir; an application supplies an upper bound.

DIRECT KL TRANSFER
Gibbs variationality at sigma gives
log(Z0/ZW)<=D(sigma||rho0)+beta Tr sigma W.
The exact identity
D(rhoW||rho0)=-beta Tr rhoW W+log(Z0/ZW)
then gives the asserted bound by positivity (or the lower bound -u).
This proof also covers an arbitrary trial density sigma; the filter is an
explicit way to construct a low-cost trial. It incurs no postselection in
the actual encoder. An interacting partition oracle is not supplied.

UNBOUNDED/Fock VERSION
The same proof requires actual domain, entropy and trace hypotheses: a
self-adjoint semibounded form sum H0+W; legal trial sigma with finite form
energy; finite Gibbs energies/entropies; a justified trace double-commutator
identity (for example sectorwise finite matrices with summable bounds);
and the entropy increase of the unital instrument justified by truncation.
Finite-mode stable bosons with number-preserving F can use number-sector
truncation. A positive H0 and finite half-temperature partition ensure the
interacting thermal energy and each positive form expectation are finite.
These conditions must be verified in each application, not inferred from
formal commutator notation. Arbitrary unbounded W is not automatically legal.

COLLECTIVE CONSEQUENCE AND CAPACITY GATE
If the witness bound is B_L uniformly over positive penalties, n independent
copies at common free parameters have relative entropy at most n B_L from
the n-copy free family. Their trace distance is at most sqrt(2n B_L).
The fixed total-number reverse channel in PROJECTION_PRINCIPLE.txt has
error <=2sqrt(2n B_L) and O(log n+loglog(1/delta_tail)) classical bits.
For an unknown interaction, no collective measurement can distinguish it
from the matched free state with success advantage above sqrt(2nB_L)/4
under equal priors. This is a sufficient invisibility regime, not a sharp
sample-complexity law.

The new analytic gate is to make the free double-commutator Dir small while
F vanishes on forbidden collisions and delta,qM remain small. On a positive
hopping kernel this is a weighted graph Dirichlet/capacity problem. The 2D
boson logarithmic Jastrow construction is being developed independently;
no lattice or fractional-dispersion rate follows from this abstract theorem
without its kernel and counting estimates.
