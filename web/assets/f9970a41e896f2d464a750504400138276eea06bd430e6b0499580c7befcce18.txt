Audited artifact
================

`outputs/research/photonic_complexity/conditional_gaussian_finite_bit_theorem.txt`
was reread after the finite-bit corrections: finite expected attempts are

Findings
========

The sibling adversarial audit checked the accepted-law normalization and its
constants. In particular, the probability both procedures accept is at
least alpha-eps_try>=1/(2 Lambda), and the claimed accepted-vector coupling
bound 3 Lambda eps_try+2 Lambda beta follows by summing the two conditional
marginal costs and the bad-distance probability under joint acceptance.

The proposal tail, small-uniform exclusion, log-midpoint error, gamma-radius
error, acceptance mismatch, deterministic rejection cap, and finite expected
attempt count were found consistent. The Taylor/Machin normal-CDF interval
routine has polynomial bit cost in the displayed parameters; the dyadic
mesh, endpoint ambiguity allowance, and inverse-CDF accuracy fit the stated
Gaussian coupling budget. The product-Poisson Hellinger bound, finite
Poisson/multinomial approximation, output-rate cap, and total TV allocation
were also found consistent. No fatal counterexample or missing cost factor
was found.

The audit identified one presentation omission in the N81 resolvent estimate:
the matrix A had not been defined. The proof now states A:=I+E before using
M=A^(-1)+tP_H. It also noticed the Gaussian precision parameters were
defined after their first use; these are now listed before the LDL and
inverse-CDF constructions. Both were presentation repairs, not changes to
the bounds.

Scope and limits
================

This is an internal analytic audit by the programme lead plus a distinct
sibling-leaf review. The rational fixtures remain finite diagnostics, not
theorem validation. No external proof review, novelty/priority determination,
or independent replay of the inherited N81 compiler was performed. The
sampler's polynomial-in-input-length corollary still requires numerical
kappa, Lambda, M_out, and delta^(-1) to be polynomially bounded; physical
repeat-until-herald generation still costs 1/p_H.
