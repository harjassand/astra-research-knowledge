Lead theorem: exact statement and proof locator

For m≥2 species on a bounded Neumann domain, let the reactions be Xi ⇌ 2Xi for each species and Xi→Xj along a fixed strongly connected directed transfer graph. The rates are positive, measurable, and bounded, and the diffusion coefficients Di may be arbitrary nonnegative numbers. Write A(t) for the Metzler linear reaction matrix. Suppose a supplied fixed vector w≫0 and constants α, Γ, b−, b+ satisfy almost everywhere

    0 < α ≤ (A(t)w)i/wi ≤ Γ,       0 < b− ≤ bi(t) ≤ b+.

With wmin=min wi and wmax=max wi, set K−=α/(b+ wmax), K+=Γ/(b− wmin), q0=min(i,x) u_i(0,x)/wi and Q0=max(i,x) u_i(0,x)/wi. For every nonnegative continuous initial condition, the mild solution is global. If q0>0, then

    q−(t) wi ≤ u_i(t,x) ≤ q+(t) wi,

where q− and q+ solve q−'=αq−−b+wmax q−² and q+'=Γq+−b−wmin q+², initialized at q0 and Q0. Thus after

    T*=max( α−1 max(0, log(K−/q0−1)), Γ−1 log 2 )

(with the first logarithm used only when its argument is positive), all species lie in

    [ wmin α/(2 b+ wmax), 2 wmax Γ/(b− wmin) ].

The box is independent of diffusion, domain/mesh geometry, and spatial dimension. The same comparison proof applies to conservative Markov diffusion generators with the maximum principle and L_i 1=0. The lower entry time depends on the positive weighted initial minimum; no positive lower bound follows for data that are allowed to vanish.

Proof in one line of mechanism: for a spatially constant vector qw, the reaction field obeys

    Fi(t,qw) ≥ wi(αq−b+wmax q²),
    Fi(t,qw) ≤ wi(Γq−b−wmin q²).

The constant spatial barriers have zero diffusion; cooperativity and the maximum principle compare the PDE with the two logistic ODEs. The upper barrier prevents finite-time norm blow-up, and the lower logistic barrier reaches K−/2. Full proof, exact quantifiers, costs, falsifier and primary comparison are in `LEAD_RESULT.txt`, `LEAD_CLAIMS.json`, and `LEAD_READ_LEDGER.txt`.

Frontier outcomes

1. Forced versus unforced Navier–Stokes. The inspected release states a forced finite-time result; the unforced alternatives require f=0. With zero initial data the energy identity for a nonzero forced trajectory requires positive work from Pf, while deleting the force leaves the zero unforced solution. Local blow-up scaling makes a smooth force small but does not create an unforced datum. The release theorem statement is source-reported and its proof validity was not independently established. See `leaves/01_forced_ns_transfer/`.

2. Hypocoercivity and response. Harmonic kinetic Fokker–Planck gives the exact gap ακ=(1−sqrt(1−4κ))/2∼κ, so a shifted Gaussian refutes any positive entropy-decay rate uniform as confinement κ↓0. For periodic kinetic Fokker–Planck, the exact Fourier eigenmode and collision tangent force λ≤2 min{ν,(2π/ℓ)²/ν} for an all-state KL estimate with finite prefactor; bare velocity Fisher dissipation does not control spatial entropy. The available modified-energy lower estimate is quadratic L², not a nonlinear KL theorem. For the homogeneous OU response, χγ(t)=e−γt/m and the equilibrium correlation obeys χγ=βCγ, while the static susceptibility 1/(mγ) diverges as the gap closes. See `leaves/02_kinetic_hypocoercivity_gap/`, `10_entropy_hypocoercivity_bridge/`, and `14_linear_response_kinetic/`.

3. Landau and non-cutoff Boltzmann. N58's three-gain rank lemma supports conditional qualitative nonconcentration; quantitative chart bounds need a Jacobian margin and an input decay rate. The positive-hot-generator grazing route has mass production of order δ−2, and the rank result supplies no Landau coercivity or hydrodynamic limit. Separately, Mouhot's source theorem controls local microscopic H^s_v for 0<s<1 after projecting off collision invariants. Its fixed homogeneous-equilibrium Fourier lift is independent of torus length, but the macro mode q=1 at k=2π/L has full-generator ratio √2π/L, excluding a torus-uniform full-generator lower bound. Neither statement alone is a nonlinear limit theorem. See `leaves/03_landau_three_gain/` and `19_noncutoff_regularization/`.

4. Unequal diffusion and mesh limits. The two-species network A⇌2A, B⇌2B, A⇌B has an exact [1/2,2]² box for arbitrary unequal diffusion. The lead generalizes the weighted diagonal-barrier mechanism to switched rates under a supplied common w and positive margins. At graph level, a common scalar species-transport coefficient preserves a fixed local affine barrier independent of graph size and edge-rate magnitude; a conditional finite-volume compactness argument gives a PDE limit when shape regularity, discrete energy/consistency, ellipticity and coefficient convergence are supplied. Unequal transport matrices require compatible facet normals. A two-site counterexample exits the mixed-facet polytope under rates 1/h² and 3/h² at time at most (log 3)h²/4 for h²<0.63720237; smooth PDE data produce derivative −3/5 at the same type of facet contact. This attacks the proposed barrier rather than the network's boundedness. The stochastic certificate instead requires V_h h⁴≫log N_h+log(V_h T), so it does not certify a fixed-density continuum limit. See `leaves/05_unequal_diffusion_pde/` and `06_mesh_limit_uniformity/`.

5. Transport and entropy. The exact N60 zigzag uses one fixed absolutely-continuous source with density <8 and targets in one square; it gives squared map error / target-TV ≥4n, refuting the proposed atom-independent linear-TV step in a W2^(1/3) proof. It does not refute the W2^(1/3) endpoint. The leaf separately reconstructs a fixed non-John room-and-passage source with no Hölder potential stability, a fixed uniform source showing that map exponents >1/3 fail, and a thin log-concave family showing a source-uniform one-third constant cannot hold without concentration dependence. The family-374 primary manuscript states the one-third map modulus for uniform sources on compact convex bodies; the broader compact-log-concave reconstruction remains internally audited, not an author-attributed theorem. A finite-entropy isotropic log-concave Laplace law also shows entropy does not imply subgaussian tails. For kinetic-to-fluid closure, a matched-moment Gaussian mixture has H(fε|M)=O(ε³)=o(ε²) while its cubic energy-flux pairing tends to a nonzero vector; entropy alone cannot provide the required uniform integrability. These are obstructions to specific proof steps, not evidence against the Euler limit. See `leaves/04_transport_entropy_counterexample/`, `07_boltzmann_hydrolimit/`, and `18_hydrolimit_entropy_obstruction/`.

6. Mean-field and hierarchy limits. Harmonic Vlasov gives fixed-time tagged-position error O_T(N−1), but at t=log N+c the error has a nonzero limit, so this model does not give uniform-in-time chaos. A quadratic quantum mean-field model has exact k-position error sqrt(1+kt²/N)−1: fixed-k convergence holds on bounded time windows but uniform-in-time fails at t∼sqrt(N); removing the center-of-mass mode restores a uniform fixed-k O(k/N) error. The Gross–Pitaevskii audit reconstructs a fixed-k theorem and cutoff dependencies; admissible product states refute uniform convergence through k=N and any universal algebraic rate under only the qualitative assumptions. The stable linear kinetic Langevin model gives an internal O(N−1/2) all-time bound for fixed Lipschitz tests when both drift matrices are Hurwitz; a neutral mode loses closeness at t∼N and an unstable mode at t∼(log N)/(2r). A nearby source theorem gives all-time scalar-test Gaussian approximation under bounded smooth interaction hypotheses, not the full measure-valued path CLT and not directly the unbounded mean-force model. See `leaves/08_vlasov_uniform_chaos/`, `09_kinetic_fluctuations/`, `16_bbgky_quantum_kinetic/`, and `17_gross_pitaevskii_hierarchy/`.

7. Navier–Stokes energy, boundary layers and Onsager flux. Energy/dissipation bounds alone do not imply scale-free critical L⁵ smallness; a continuation criterion is recorded without a blow-up or global-regularity claim. Kato's no-slip vanishing-viscosity criterion and corrector estimate are reconstructed, but no actual equal-data unforced violation example is known in this audit. The Euler triad calculation has flux scaling N^(1−3α), identifying the commutator method's threshold but not constructing an anomalous weak Euler solution. See `leaves/11_unforced_ns_energy/`, `12_vanishing_viscosity_boundary/`, and `13_onsager_flux_threshold/`.

8. Critical interacting-spin statistics. Curie–Weiss theory gives quartic critical fluctuations with Var(M_N)∼1.1708286566 N^(3/2) at β=1 and χ∼1.1708286566√N. At fixed β>1,h=0 the law is two-phase and χ is order N; conditioning on a phase restores Gaussian fluctuations. An internally derived compact finite-size window in β=1+a/√N, h=b/N^(3/4) is recorded separately from established core laws. The included float64 check is finite diagnostic evidence only. See `leaves/15_interacting_spin_thermodynamic/`.
