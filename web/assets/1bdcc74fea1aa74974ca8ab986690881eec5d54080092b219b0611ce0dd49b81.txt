INTERFACE CLARIFICATION — c09_l07

This supplements the frozen INITIAL and the later pair/heat-flow derivation;
the frozen artifact is not edited.

For every fixed epsilon and known family, the encoder channel E and decoder
channel R are chosen once and do not depend on the unknown v. The encoder sees
X only; the decoder knows the family, epsilon and public independent seed,
but receives no v, sign, or parameter-dependent side information. It outputs
a fresh classical point Y. The requirement is

  sup_{||v||_2<=1} TV(R E P_v, P_v) <= epsilon.

Every input-dependent register is included in the total hybrid dimension D;
an independent seed may be averaged with D bounded at every seed. No uniform
per-seed accuracy is assumed. This parameter-independent decoder condition is
load-bearing: if R were told v, D=1 could sample P_v directly.

All c09_l07 codec constructions satisfy this interface. The shared family
uses the same hat encoder for all v and a decoder that samples J phi_j plus a
fresh uniform ancillary coordinate. The product family uses the same pair of
hat-label encoders and product triangular decoder for every v. The lower
bounds use a single encoder/decoder pair across every sign alternative, not
separate memory states or decoders for different alternatives.

c09_l06 independently identified this parameter-independent decoder clause
as necessary when formalizing the cycle-1 archive theorem. This is an
explicit clarification of the c09_l07 interface, not a new result.
