N01 — Cosmic inference, version 2
2026-10-09

Status: internally derived necessary realizability test for a declared one-solid EFT class; no observational application, novelty claim, full action reconstruction, or external validation. Version 1 remains unchanged.

New target
==========
The bias-free slip estimator in v1 recovers a metric quantity. The first action-level counterexample showed that a stable subluminal GR elastic dark-energy solid can generate nonzero scalar slip, so slip alone cannot label modified gravity. This continuation asks a narrower, answerable question: after reconstructing the full linear effective dark stress, what consistency condition must it satisfy if it came from one minimally coupled, isotropic, nondissipative elastic solid described at leading derivative order by three material-coordinate scalars? The condition below removes the arbitrary initial displacement offset and yields a scale-independent constitutive coefficient with causal-stability bounds.

1. Hypothesis and conventions
=============================
Assume a constant Planck mass and minimally coupled known matter, so the metric can be rewritten as Einstein gravity with

  T^E_{mu nu} = M_Pl^2 G_{mu nu} - T^known_{mu nu}.

This is only a definition until a local action realizes T^E. Here the hypothesis being tested is stronger: T^E is the stress tensor of one isotropic elastic solid with action

  S_E = integral sqrt(-g) F(X,Y,Z) d^4x,
  B^{IJ}=g^{mu nu} partial_mu phi^I partial_nu phi^J,
  X=[B], Y=[B^2]/[B]^2, Z=[B^3]/[B]^3,
  background phi^I=x^I,

minimally coupled to Einstein gravity. We work at linear order, at k != 0, below the EFT cutoff, with no viscosity or higher-derivative operators and a homogeneous isotropic background. The solid has positive enthalpy h_E=rho_E+p_E>0. The known stress subtracted in T^E must include baryons, cold matter, photons, neutrinos, and any other separately conserved species relevant to the mode.

Use Poisson gauge, ds^2=a^2[-(1+2 Psi)d tau^2+(1-2 Phi)delta_ij dx^i dx^j]. This is a gauge choice, not a claim that displacement by itself is gauge invariant. It fixes the scalar shift and spatial shear to zero; the Bardeen potentials, the anisotropic-stress coefficient, and the Poisson-gauge velocity are the corresponding invariantly defined metric/velocity observables. In another gauge the displacement and spatial metric shear must be transformed together. A material-strain version of the equation is gauge invariant; in Poisson gauge it reduces to the scalar S defined below.

For each Fourier transfer solution define

  Q^i_j = k_hat^i k_hat_j - delta^i_j/3,
  xi^i(k,tau) = i k^i xi(k,tau),
  (delta T_E^i_j)_TF = Pi_E(k,N) Q^i_j,
  theta_E = i k_i v_E^i,
  U_E = -theta_E/Hcal,
  S_E = k^2 xi,
  N = ln a, Hcal=aH.

The subscript E on U_E and S_E denotes the effective dark component, not the expansion variable E=H/H0 from v1.

2. Exact leading-derivative solid response
==========================================
Battye & Moss (2007), arXiv:astro-ph/0703744v1, eqs. (34a)-(34c), derive the linear stress of an isotropic elastic medium in synchronous gauge in terms of its displacement xi^i, metric perturbation h^i_j, bulk modulus beta and shear modulus mu. Their momentum and spatial stress are

  delta T_E^i_0 = -h_E dot(xi^i),
  delta T_E^i_j = -delta^i_j (beta-2mu/3) D
                   - mu(2 partial_(j xi^{i)} + h^i_j),
  D = partial_i xi^i + h/2.

The constitutive stress law is tensorial; after fixing Poisson gauge, its scalar traceless part has h^i_j|TF=0. With the Fourier convention above,

  (2 partial_(j xi^{i)})_TF = -2 k^2 xi Q^i_j,
  Pi_E = 2 mu S_E.                                      (1)

In Poisson gauge the material velocity is v_E^i=dot(xi^i). Therefore theta_E=-k^2 dot(xi), and

  (S_E)_N = (k^2 dot(xi))/Hcal = -theta_E/Hcal = U_E.   (2)

Equations (1)-(2) are exact at linear order for this constitutive class; they do not use a quasi-static approximation, a Poisson growth law, a primordial-amplitude normalization, or a continuity substitution for matter growth. They do assume that the reconstructed stress and velocity belong to the same transfer solution.

Let K(N)=2mu(N). Since an isotropic local solid has one background shear modulus, K is independent of k and of which linear initial mode is excited. Differentiating (1) and using (2) gives

  Pi_E,N(k,N) = a(N) Pi_E(k,N) + K(N) U_E(k,N),
  a(N) = K_N/K,       K>0.                              (3)

This form eliminates the free constant in S_E(k,N)=C_k+integral U_E dN. Thus an arbitrary initial solid displacement/isocurvature offset cannot by itself defeat the test. If K=0 on an interval, the separate perfect-fluid condition is Pi_E=0 there; isolated zeros are handled by continuous extension.

A transfer-level necessary rank condition follows from (3): for any three independent (k, initial-mode) transfer solutions i=1,2,3,

  det | Pi_i   U_i   Pi_i,N | = 0.                       (4)

For two modes with D=Pi_1 U_2-U_1 Pi_2 != 0, the constitutive functions are reconstructible algebraically:

  K = (Pi_1 Pi_2,N - Pi_1,N Pi_2)/D,
  a = (Pi_1,N U_2 - U_1 Pi_2,N)/D.                       (5)

They must then satisfy K_N=aK and the same K,a must fit every other scale and every independent initial mode. If D=0, the algebraic inference is degenerate; it is not evidence for or against the solid. A nonzero determinant in (4), a scale-dependent inferred K, or failure of the differential integrability check refutes this one-solid constitutive hypothesis, subject to errors in the reconstructed transfer functions.

3. Causal and stability inequalities
====================================
Let beta be the isotropic bulk modulus. Battye & Moss eqs. (45)-(46) give the principal longitudinal and transverse characteristic speeds

  c_T^2 = mu/h_E = K/(2h_E),
  c_L^2 = (beta+4mu/3)/h_E = beta/h_E + 4 c_T^2/3.

For the restricted F(X,Y,Z) solid, a homogeneous FRW compression follows the one-dimensional background trajectory in X while Y,Z stay at their isotropic values; hence beta/h_E = c_a^2 := p_E,N/rho_E,N when rho_E,N != 0. Positive kinetic energy, no short-wavelength gradient instability, and subluminal characteristics require

  h_E>0,
  0 <= c_T^2 <= 1,
  0 <= c_a^2 + (4/3)c_T^2 <= 1.                         (6)

Equivalently,

  max(0,-3 c_a^2/4) <= K/(2h_E)
                         <= min(1, 3(1-c_a^2)/4).       (7)

This is a restricted-class inequality, not a theorem for all dark-energy fields. It concerns the low-energy phonon characteristic cone of the displayed first-derivative EFT; it does not prove a Lorentz-invariant UV completion, positivity bounds for all scattering amplitudes, nonlinear stability, or a cutoff above cosmological frequencies. If rho_E,N=0 or h_E=0, c_a^2 or the material rest frame is singular and this parameterization must be replaced rather than continued by division.

The measurable-reconstruction version of (6) is: infer K(N) from (5) or fit (3), compute h_E and c_a^2 from the background after specifying/subtracting known matter, and reject the one-solid model if the interval (7) is empty or the inferred K lies outside it. The broad slip estimator of v1 only uses ratios and cancels Omega_m0; this action test generally does not. Matter-background uncertainty must be propagated or profiled as a nuisance. It remains independent of galaxy bias and mode amplitude only at transfer level, because (3) is homogeneous in the common mode amplitude.

4. Check against the explicit local counterexample
=================================================
For the v1 action

  F=-A sqrt(X)+C sqrt(X)(Y-1/3),
  C=3A/16, A>0,

on X=3/a^2,Y=1/3, one has rho_E=A sqrt(X) proportional to a^-1, w=-2/3, h_E=rho_E/3, beta/h_E=c_a^2=-2/3, and c_T^2=3/4. Thus

  mu = h_E c_T^2 = rho_E/4,  K=rho_E/2,
  c_L^2 = -2/3 + (4/3)(3/4)=1/3.

The local action has positive quadratic phonon kinetic coefficient -F_X X/3=rho_E/6>0 and both sound speeds strictly between zero and one. For every solution of its linear equations, Pi_E=K S_E and therefore (3)-(7) hold. This verifies that the allowed set contains a nonzero-slip realization; the condition is not vacuous and nonzero slip does not fail its positivity test.

This construction does not match a named modified-gravity model across all (k,a), and no observed Pi_E,U_E transfer functions have been fitted here. The result is a candidate falsifiability criterion for one declared class, not an empirical DE/MG distinction yet.

5. Observability, degeneracies, and exact scope
==============================================
The variables needed for this test are components of T^E, not only eta. In principle, after reconstructing Poisson-gauge Psi from exact dust Euler/velocity data and Phi-Psi from deprojected lensing, time derivatives of the metric plus a known T^known determine T^E=M_Pl^2G-T^known. Its 0i component gives U_E (where h_E>0); its traceless ij component gives Pi_E. One may use the v1 transfer-amplitude ratios to cancel a common unknown primordial amplitude and galaxy bias in reconstructing metric responses, but computing T^E requires the standard matter perturbations and background density, not just the v1 slip ratio. Derivatives, lensing deprojection, relativistic number-count terms, massive-neutrino stress, and nonlinear corrections are serious observational limitations. The criterion is formulated for noiseless linear transfer functions; no forecast or data test is claimed.

Initial-condition handling is explicit but limited. The integration constant C_k disappears from (3); independent adiabatic and isocurvature transfer columns must each obey the same K(N). If observations provide only auto-power spectra with unknown correlated initial modes, the transfer columns may not be identifiable. An arbitrary mixture must not be treated as a measured transfer function. In the k range where all modes are proportional and D=0, there is no leverage to infer K from (5).

This does not establish a universal obstruction for all local minimally coupled dark fields. Two independent healthy solids give

  Pi_tot = 2 sum_A mu_A S_A,
  h_tot U_tot = sum_A h_A U_A,
  (S_A)_N = U_A,

and their relative-displacement mode generally prevents Pi_tot from being K(N) times a single integrated total velocity. Such a model can violate (3) while remaining a sum of local causal components. Higher-derivative operators, dissipative response, anisotropic media, extra internal fields, nonadiabatic constitutive variables, and non-Einstein tensor kinetic terms also lie outside the one-solid test. Therefore failure of (3)-(7) rejects the stated one-solid EFT, not dark energy in general. Conversely, satisfying the conditions is necessary but not sufficient for an F(X,Y,Z) action: the isotropic pressure response and every other stress component, field equations, strong-coupling regime, and background must still be matched.

Gauge note: Pi_E and metric potentials in the traceless Einstein equation are gauge-invariant at first order because the background traceless stress vanishes. The velocity entering U_E is the Poisson-gauge/Bardeen velocity; an arbitrary synchronous-coordinate velocity cannot be inserted. The material displacement itself is gauge-dependent. Equation (2) is used only after fixing Poisson gauge; in another gauge the metric spatial shear term in the constitutive strain must be retained, as it is in Battye & Moss eq. (34c). k=0 homogeneous displacements are excluded, since no scalar traceless direction Q^i_j exists there.

Tensor multimessenger status: the solid phonon speed c_T in (6) is not the metric gravitational-wave speed c_GW. In the action of v1, the tensor principal kinetic term is Einstein's, so c_GW=c at high frequency, while the solid can source tensor perturbations through stress at order H^2. GW170817's near-luminal tensor-speed result therefore does not constrain K/(2h_E) or remove this scalar counterexample. Standard-siren amplitude/friction constraints restrict specified modified-tensor-propagation models, not every scalar-sector MG model. No tensor observation was used to infer (3).

6. Prior-source comparison and claim ledger
===========================================
- Source-reported prior art: Amendola et al., arXiv:1902.06978v2, Sec. XI eqs. (122)-(137), already give the bias-, spectrum-amplitude-, and Omega_m0-canceling observable slip estimator recorded in v1. This note does not claim that estimator as new.
- Source-reported degeneracy: Kunz & Sapone, arXiv:astro-ph/0612452v1, Sec. IV eqs. (12)-(13), choose generalized-DE stress perturbations reproducing DGP metric perturbations. Their construction is phenomenological and does not establish a healthy local action.
- Primary constitutive source: Battye & Moss, arXiv:astro-ph/0703744v1, eqs. (34a)-(35) give linear isotropic-solid stress, displacement momentum, and equation of motion; eqs. (45)-(46) give c_L^2=beta/h+4c_T^2/3 and c_T^2=mu/h. The new step here is eliminating the material-displacement integration constant to get the cross-scale transfer identity (3)-(5) and combining its inferred K with (6)-(7). Their model is a continuum-medium description; the explicit F(X,Y,Z) action realization is separately sourced by Endlich, Nicolis & Wang, arXiv:1210.0569v1, Sec. 2 eqs. (2.3)-(2.17), as explained in v1.
- Candidate/internal derivation: equations (1)-(7), determinant test, two-mode inversion, and application to the explicit F action above.
- Finite test: none. No data have been analyzed; no numerical forecast or simulation has been run.
- Unresolved: whether any measured or named healthy MG transfer function violates the one-solid closure plus sound-speed interval over an observationally accessible (k,N) domain; whether a broader finite set of minimally coupled fields can realize that response; action-level sufficiency and a strong-coupling/UV analysis; transfer reconstruction with latent initial modes and realistic lensing/RSD kernels.

Claim boundary
==============
The strongest supported conclusion is a derived, initial-offset-independent necessary response test for a single leading-derivative isotropic elastic DE solid, with explicit positive-kinetic and subluminal speed bounds. It suggests how bias-free metric reconstructions could falsify that model class. It does not establish a universal DE/MG discriminator, a full realization theorem, a violation by actual data or a named MG target, novelty, priority, or a UV-complete dark-energy model.
