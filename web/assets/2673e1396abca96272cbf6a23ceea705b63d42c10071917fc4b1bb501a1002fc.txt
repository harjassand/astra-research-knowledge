INDEPENDENT BASELINE — CYCLE03 PERMANENCE
Frozen before reading foundational_transfer_sol/cycle03_affine or any source
proof. No numerical scan and no descendants. This is a partial exact derivation,
not validation of the full permanence theorem.

The supplied arrow-minimum condition is sufficient for a local directional
inward lemma. I have not independently constructed a single compact convex
invariant absorbing body in arbitrary dimension, nor found a counterexample.
The geometric passage from those local half-space normals to one body is the
load-bearing gap in this baseline. It cannot be replaced by pointwise
dominance or by entropy/V(x)=sum_i x_i(log x_i-1) alone, since that convex
potential has finite values on the coordinate boundary.

1. NECESSARY HOMOGENEITY OF THE EXPONENTS.
The uniform asymptotic statement forces tau_e(0)=0 and
tau_e(c p)=c tau_e(p) whenever c>0 and p,cp belong to [-1,1]^d.
Proof: h^(cp)=(h^c)^p. Compute log w(t,h^(cp))/log h using the estimate once
at (h,cp) and once at (h^c,p), then let h->0. The errors tend to zero.
At p=0, w(t,1)>0 is fixed at any fixed t, so the quotient tends to zero.
Thus genuinely arbitrary continuous functions on the cube are not realizable
exponents of a nonempty admitted class; they must be positively homogeneous.
This does not force affine linearity: sums of monomials can have piecewise
linear minimum exponents.

2. ALL-TIME BOUNDS ON POSITIVE COMPACTS.
Fix a compact C in the positive orthant. Choose h0>0 sufficiently small that
p(x)=log x/log h0 belongs to the cube for every x in C and the power estimate
holds at h0. Continuity bounds all tau_e(p(x)). The estimate therefore gives
strict positive lower and finite upper bounds for every w_e(t,x), uniformly
in t and admitted paths, on C. Hence the all-time assumption excludes the
usual time-change counterexample in which rates vanish with t or explode
with t on a fixed positive compact. The standard continuation alternative
can only fail through coordinate-boundary approach or unbounded states.

3. EXACT LOCAL DIRECTIONAL INWARD LEMMA.
Fix r in the cube with E_r nonempty. Partition E_r into I+={e:r.nu_e>0}
and I-={e:r.nu_e<0}. If I- is empty then r.f(t,x)>0 for every positive x.
Otherwise set alpha=min_(e in I+) tau_e(r) and
beta=min_(e in I-) tau_e(r). The supplied condition implies I+ nonempty and
beta>alpha. Let gamma=(beta-alpha)/4. Choose e+ attaining alpha. Continuity
gives a neighborhood U_r of r such that, for all p in U_r in the cube and
all e in I-, tau_e(p)-tau_(e+)(p)>=2 gamma. For h sufficiently small that
epsilon(h)<=gamma/2, the logarithmic estimate yields
      w_e(t,h^p)/w_(e+)(t,h^p) <= h^gamma.
Therefore
      r.f(t,h^p) >= w_(e+)(t,h^p)
            [r.nu_(e+) - h^gamma sum_(e in I-) |r.nu_e|] >0
for all sufficiently small h, uniformly in all admitted paths and times.
Arrows with r.nu_e=0 disappear exactly from this fixed-r dot product;
their potentially much smaller exponent does not invalidate this lemma.

Important quantifier: this proves positivity against the fixed normal r
in a neighborhood of r. It does not prove positivity against the moving
normal p; near r.nu_e=0, newly active rates can dominate that moving product.
A proof making that replacement requires additional justification.

4. COMPATIBILITY EXCLUDES LOGARITHMIC END DIRECTIONS IN S PERP.
Let c be a positive point of P. If x_n in P and rho_n=||log x_n||_infinity
tends to infinity, pass to p_n=-log x_n/rho_n -> r on the cube boundary.
Then r cannot belong to S perp.
Proof: if r in S perp, r.x_n=r.c for every n. Every r_i>0 corresponds to
x_(n,i)->0; every r_i<0 corresponds to x_(n,i)->infinity. If any negative
coefficient occurs then r.x_n -> -infinity, because positive-coefficient
terms tend to zero and zero coefficients contribute nothing. If none occur,
then r.x_n ->0, while r.c>0. Both contradict r.x_n=r.c. QED.
Thus every actual class-end direction has E_r nonempty. This is an exact
compatibility obligation, not an assumption of full stoichiometric rank.

5. FULL ONE-DIMENSIONAL CASE.
If S={0}, P is one point and the claim is immediate. Otherwise P=(0,infinity)
in one dimension. At r=1 the local directional lemma gives f(t,x)>0 for
0<x<=a with a sufficiently small. At r=-1 it gives f(t,x)<0 for x>=b with
b sufficiently large. Choose 0<a<b with strict inward inequalities also at
both endpoints. Then [a,b] is a compact convex forward invariant set.
For fixed x0<a, the explicit rate lower bound and the strict dominance above
give a uniform positive lower bound on f over [x0,a], valid for every t and
admitted path. The trajectory reaches a in time at most (a-x0)/lower_bound.
The corresponding negative upper bound over [b,x0] gives finite entry if
x0>b. The solution cannot leave [min(x0,a),max(x0,b)] and hence is global.
Thus the complete claim holds in dimension one with all stated uniformities.

6. FAILED INITIAL COUNTEREXAMPLES AND EXPOSED GAPS.
Time-dependent prefactors tending to zero or infinity violate the all-time
power estimate on a fixed positive compact. A common positive rate multiplier
cannot create finite-time blow-up once the trajectory lies in a positive
compact, because the estimate bounds it there.

The conservative cyclic autocatalytic rock-paper-scissors example
A+B->2B, B+C->2C, C+A->2A fails the supplied minimum condition: at r=(1,0,0)
an inward and an outward arrow tie at the minimal active source exponent.
It therefore cannot refute the theorem.

Rates with flat angular exponent gaps may defeat a direct proof of
p.f(t,h^p)>0 for moving p, but this does not yet refute the fixed-normal
lemma or the possibility of a suitable convex trapping body. No such
candidate is promoted to a counterexample.

The entropy potential has divergent boundary derivatives but finite boundary
values. Its high sublevels need not be compact inside P. Therefore an entropy
derivative calculation alone cannot prove the advertised positive compact
body or boundary exclusion.

7. BASELINE VERDICT.
No fatal counterexample has been obtained. The precise local inward mechanism,
compatibility exclusion and dimension-one theorem are proved above. The full
dimension-d compact convex body, its uniform finite entry, and boundary
continuation are unproved in this independent baseline. The proposed arbitrary-
gauge affine-minimum lemma was not imported and has not been assumed valid.
