PASSIVE HMM ACQUISITION: ONE STREAM VERSUS LENGTH-THREE RESETS
8 October 2026

RESULT

For the same stationary, noisy, two-state rare-switch pair used in the length-three reset lower bound, allowing one uninterrupted passive stream changes the fixed-accuracy training-symbol complexity for L-step endpoint prediction from Theta(L^2) to Theta(L). The matching one-stream prediction lower bound is information-theoretic; the upper bound uses long-lag products of noisy endpoint symbols in blocks of length about 0.85L.

This temporal-access improvement is specifically over independent episodes truncated to length three. If independent episodes of length Theta(L) are available at the same per-symbol price, they attain the same Theta(L) symbol order. A single stream mainly saves episode starts in that longer-episode comparison.

MODEL, LOSS, AND INTERFACE

For integer L>=126, hidden state Z_t is a stationary symmetric Markov chain on {−1,+1}, with flip rate r_i. Observations are Y_t=Z_t E_t, where the E_t are iid, independent of Z, and P(E_t=+1)=3/4. The candidate pair is known; only the model index is unknown:

  M0: r0=1/(8L);     M1: r1=1/(2L);     lambda_i=1-2r_i.

Training yields one uninterrupted stationary stream S=(Y_1,...,Y_T). Evaluation gives an independent stationary context H=(Y_1,Y_2,Y_3), and asks for the law of Y_(3+L), hiding all intervening symbols. A learner returns q(.|S,H); its model-i risk is

  R_i=E_i[TV(q(.|S,H), K_i(.|H))],
  K_i(.|H)=Law_i(Y_(3+L)|H).

The test context costs three symbols and one additional start. The output is one Bernoulli law, not a table. Computation is unrestricted for the lower bound.

MATCHING ONE-STREAM BOUNDS

Let c=(3/4−e^(−1))/8≈0.0477651. For target risk epsilon0=c/8≈0.0059706:

* Lower: if T−1≤(c^2/8)L≈0.0002852L, every predictor has max_i R_i≥c/4>epsilon0.
* Upper: a finite uniform construction has risk at most epsilon0 using T=5179(floor(0.85L)+1)=O(L) training symbols, one stream start, O(T) scan time, and constant working memory.

Thus minimax training-symbol cost is Theta(L) for this fixed pair and fixed accuracy. Constants are conservative; no optimal global leading constant is claimed.

UPPER CONSTRUCTION

For lag s, W=Y_tY_(t+s) has mean mu_i(s)=lambda_i^s/4. Its mean gap is

  D_(L,s)=1/4[(1−1/(4L))^s−(1−1/L)^s].

Take B adjacent, nonoverlapping blocks of s+1 symbols and multiply each block's first and last observations. These B products are iid: each is the product of its two endpoint emission noises and the hidden flip indicators internal to that block; the absolute hidden sign cancels, and all such noises/indicators are disjoint across blocks. Threshold the empirical mean midway between mu_0 and mu_1. Hoeffding bounds the model-index error by exp(−B D_(L,s)^2/8).

Choosing s=floor(0.85L), for L>=100, D_(L,s)>=d*=(0.7875−e^(−0.84))/4≈0.0889474. Taking B=ceil(8 log(8/c)/d*^2)=5179 makes the index error at most c/8. Forecast with the selected model's exact conditional law; wrong selection costs at most one in TV, so risk is at most c/8.

Within this block-product detector class, the asymptotic cost per log-confidence is minimized by maximizing d(x)^2/x where d(x)=(e^(−x/4)−e^(−x))/4 and x=s/L. The unique positive optimizer solves e^(3x/4)=(1+2x)/(1+x/2) and is x≈0.8543758. This gives d(x)^2/x≈0.0106819. The finite 0.85L choice supports a simple explicit bound.

LOWER ARGUMENT

The endpoint correlation gap is (lambda_0^L−lambda_1^L)/4≥(3/4−e^(−1))/4, so the joint laws of (H,Y_(3+L)) have TV at least c. The context marginals differ by at most 3/(2sqrt(2)L), hence the expected TV separation of the two forecast kernels under model-0 contexts is at least 3c/4.

The complete hidden path has KL at most (T−1)/(2L): each transition contributes kl(Ber(1/(8L))||Ber(1/(2L)))≤1/(2L), and emissions are identical. Observing the stream cannot increase KL. The independent length-three context has KL at most 9/(4L^2). For L>=126 and T−1<=c^2 L/8, Pinsker bounds the joint training/context data TV by c/4. The triangle inequality for the two forecast risks then gives R0+R1>=c/2, so max_i R_i>=c/4.

An independent model-index lower bound is also immediate: both-error-1/3 testing requires KL>=2/9, hence T−1>=4L/9. The lagged-product test gives O(L), matching identification order.

SAME-SYMBOL COMPARISON

For length-three reset episodes, the observed triple KL is O(L^−2), and the prior two-point result gives matching Theta(L^2) symbols for fixed-accuracy endpoint prediction. A one-stream detector spends Theta(L) symbols to expose lag-Theta(L) correlations. This is a factor-Theta(L) reduction compared with short length-three episodes at equal symbol budget.

It is not a gain over reset access to long episodes: B independent reset blocks of length s+1 produce the same iid endpoint products for the same B(s+1)=Theta(L) symbols. If each reset costs rho, long independent blocks cost B(s+1)+B rho, while one uninterrupted stream costs B(s+1)+rho. Thus the exact temporal advantage over the length-three interface is access to widely separated observations; reset savings are an additional implementation-cost effect only when episode starts are charged.

COSTS AND LIMITS

The learner scans T symbols in O(T) time, computes B products, and uses O(1) memory. Exact rational forecast output may need O(L log L) bits because lambda_i^L has that many bits; binary powering takes O(log L) integer multiplications on growing integers. For fixed target accuracy epsilon0, use B=5879 to make classification error at most epsilon0/2 and round the probability to error epsilon0/2; this keeps risk at epsilon0, uses constant output bits, and takes O(log L) controlled multiplications on O(log L+log(1/epsilon0))-bit fixed-point numbers. The input candidate HMMs are supplied, so no parameter-description or hidden-state-query cost is counted. This is a two-model result, not a rank-agnostic universal HMM bound; it does not identify the best leading constant among all stream predictors.
