# Operator form of the all-functions quantifier

A useful diagnostic for any proposed two-replica kernel is an exact Schur-complement characterization. Let H0=L²_0(π), and view the pair Dirichlet form restricted to additive observables as a positive semidefinite quadratic form on H0⊕H0:

  E(a(X)+b(Y)) = <a,Aa> + 2<a,Bb> + <b,Cb>.

Here A,C are self-adjoint positive semidefinite and the full block matrix [[A,B],[B*,C]] is positive semidefinite. Positivity implies B ker(C)=0. For each a, minimizing over every companion b gives

  inf_b E(a(X)+b(Y)) = <a,(A-B C† B*)a>,

where C† is the Moore-Penrose inverse on the range of C. Indeed, the minimizer is b=−C†B*a up to ker(C); completing the square yields the displayed minimum. Therefore the required all-a,b inequality is equivalent to

  A - B C† B* >= L^-1 I on H0.

A check with b fixed to zero only sees A>=L^-1 I and can miss arbitrarily complete cancellation through the cross block B. The involution witness in `additive_quantifier_counterexample.txt` has A=B=C=1, so its Schur complement is zero despite A=1.

The parity-conditional heatbath witness has A=C=1,B=0, so the Schur complement is 1 and the all-a,b inequality is valid with L=1. Yet its full pair kernel has a zero-energy parity interaction. Thus the Schur complement exactly audits additive coercivity, while a separate product/ANOVA argument is still needed to control arbitrary pair or global functions.

The script `work/cycle6/c01_l06/revisions/schur_complement_audit.py` computes these matrices using exact fractions from the finite kernels. This is an algebraic criterion, not an algorithm for constructing the determinant kernel or estimating its full operator matrix.
