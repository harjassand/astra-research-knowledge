480 T^2(1+beta T), and the away-from-wall ordinary count is at most
21600 beta T^3. Both are bounded by 24000[T^2+beta T^3]. Since
d=(a+1)B(N+2)/2 >= BN^2/4, this gives the stated H constant 96000;
rounding to 100000 is valid. For T<3 there is no positive output because

RIGHT-KERNEL GREEN TRANSFER

For a rank-k projection P in the right kernel of A, put P_0=P-(k/d)I.
The exact bridge is

 ||A||_HS^2 <= E_w(A)[d G(P)/k^2+N^2/3],
 G(P)=integral_0^infinity ||1_(0,t](L_w)P_0||_HS^2 dt/t^2.

The scalar mode is removed before applying layer cake. For the split point

 K=(100N)^-1 min{k^-1/3,sqrt(B/k)}
   =[100N max{k^1/3,sqrt(k/B)}]^-1,

the H estimate below K and the cumulative-mass bound <=k above K give

 G(P) <= 1000N k^2 B^-1 m + 5N k^2 m^2 + 100N k/m,
 m=min{k^-1/3,sqrt(B/k)}.

Separating k<=B^3 and k>=B^3 yields
G(P)<=1105N max{k^4/3,k^(3/2)/sqrt(B)}. Hence
dG(P)/k^2<=1105/g_k for
g_k=(Nd)^-1 min{k^2/3,sqrt(Bk)}. Also, using k<=d/2 and d>=BN^2/4,

 N^2 g_k <= (N/d)sqrt(Bk)
        <= N sqrt(B/(2d)) <= sqrt(2).

Thus the denominator in the bridge is below 1106/g_k, giving
Lambda_w(d-k)>=g_k/1106 for 1<=k<=d/2. The right-kernel argument
uses no positivity or normality assumption on A.
