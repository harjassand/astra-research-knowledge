# Basis-free two-output variance attempt

## Verdict and scope

This is an internally reconstructed reduction plus an exact obstruction to the direct two-output measurement strategy. It does **not** prove or refute the exceptional factor-two target. The surviving universal step is a weighted collective-variance inequality stated below.

Let $d\ge2$, let $\Phi:M_d\to M_d$ be CPTP, unital, trace-preserving, and Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint, and suppose a supplied CPTP broadcaster $\mathcal B:M_d\to M_d\otimes M_d$ has both marginals equal to $\Phi$. No positivity assumption on $\Phi$ as an operator on Hilbert--Schmidt space is imposed. Write $\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/d$, $\mathcal H_0=\{A=A^*: \tau(A)=0\}$, and $\langle A,B\rangle_\tau=\tau(AB)$. The comparison is on the full Hermitian domain. Any statement about unrestricted EB comparators below uses the full-domain identity-slack reduction in `cycle7_all_Q/CANONICAL_DOMINATION.md`; it is not licensed from an $\mathcal H_0$-only premise.

All channels and the two-output extension are treated as supplied exact finite-dimensional data. This note makes no acquisition, precision, runtime, or hardware claim and assumes no source-oracle-free construction.

## Exact all-positive-dual formulation

For a POVM $M=(M_y)$, set

\[
 \Psi_M(X)=\sum_{y:\tau(M_y)>0}
       \frac{\tau(M_yX)}{\tau(M_y)}M_y.
\]

This is a canonical unital, trace-preserving, HS-self-adjoint EB channel. If $Q\succeq0$ on $\mathcal H_0$ has a spectral frame decomposition

\[
 Q=\sum_{i=1}^r w_i|A_i\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A_i|,
 \qquad w_i\ge0,
\]

where the $A_i$ are HS-orthonormal. Define the canonical support

\[
 S_d(Q)=\sup_{M\ \mathrm{POVM}}\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Psi_M)
 =\sup_M\sum_iw_i\sum_y
       \frac{\tau(M_yA_i)^2}{\tau(M_y)}.
 \tag{1}
\]

Compact-convex separation of the canonical-map set from the translated PSD cone gives the equivalence

\[
 \exists\Psi\text{ canonical with }\Psi\succeq2\Phi-I
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 S_d(Q)\ge \operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q(2\Phi-I))
 \quad\forall Q\succeq0.
 \tag{2}
\]

This is an all-$Q$ condition: a different POVM may witness (2) for each $Q$; separation is the step that turns those support inequalities into one map independent of $Q$. The exact full-EB to canonical reduction, including its full-domain assumptions and outcome accounting, is in `outputs/research/cycle_checkpoint_04/evidence/c5_quantum_almost_abelian/cycle7_all_Q/CANONICAL_DOMINATION.md`.

## The variance form of the missing inequality

Write $p_y=\tau(M_y)$ and $\rho_y=M_y/\operatorname{Tr}M_y$. Then $\sum_y p_y\rho_y=I/d$, and

\[
 \frac{\tau(M_yA)^2}{\tau(M_y)}=p_y(\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_yA))^2.
\]

Rank-one refinement can only increase the score. Therefore the support deficit has the exact pure-ensemble form

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q-S_d(Q)
 =\inf_{\mu:\,\int\rho\,d\mu=I/d}
      \sum_iw_i\int \operatorname{Var}_{\rho}(A_i)\,d\mu(\rho).
 \tag{3}
\]

The barycenter constraint is essential; an unconstrained pure-state maximizer need not define a POVM ensemble.

Self-adjoint complete positivity gives Hermitian Kraus operators $H_j$ with
\[
 \Phi(A)=\sum_jH_jAH_j,\qquad \sum_jH_j^2=I.
\]
Indeed, starting with arbitrary Kraus operators $K_j$, self-adjointness gives $\Phi(A)=\tfrac12\sum_j(K_jAK_j^*+K_j^*AK_j)$; set $H_{j,+}=(K_j+K_j^*)/2$ and $H_{j,-}=i(K_j-K_j^*)/2$. Cyclicity of trace yields

\[
 e_\Phi(A):=\langle A,(I-\Phi)A\rangle_\tau
 =\frac12\sum_j\|[H_j,A]\|_{2,\tau}^2\ge0.
 \tag{4}
\]

Thus no separate HS-positivity assumption is needed for $I-\Phi\succeq0$. Combining (1)--(4), the exceptional factor-two gate is exactly the weighted variance assertion

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \inf_{\mu:\,\int\rho\,d\mu=I/d}
     \sum_iw_i\int\operatorname{Var}_{\rho}(A_i)\,d\mu(\rho)
 \ \le\ 2\sum_iw_ie_\Phi(A_i)
 \ =\sum_{i,j}w_i\|[H_j,A_i]\|_{2,\tau}^2.
 \quad}
 \tag{V}
\]

The infimum is over **one** ensemble for the entire frame. Separate minimization for each $A_i$ is not (V).

## What the supplied two-output extension proves exactly

Use the normalized maximally entangled state to form the broadcaster Choi state $\Omega_{RAB}$. Its reference, $A$, and $B$ marginals are all $I/d$, since $\Phi$ is unital and trace-preserving. With the usual transpose on $R$,

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\Omega_{RAB}
       (A^T_R\otimes A_A\otimes I_B)\right]
 =\langle A,\Phi(A)\rangle_\tau,
\]

and the same identity holds with $A$ and $B$ exchanged. Hence for every Hermitian $A$,

\[
 \mathbb E_\Omega[(A_R-A_A)^2]
 =2\|A\|_{2,\tau}^2-2\langle A,\Phi(A)\rangle_\tau
 =2e_\Phi(A).
 \tag{5}
\]

This is the natural basis-free “two-copy variance” supplied by the extension. It makes (V) equivalent to saying that the minimum common classical frame variance is at most the summed squared discrepancy of the reference observable and one broadcaster output:

\[
 \inf_{\mu:\,\int\rho\,d\mu=I/d}\sum_iw_i\int\operatorname{Var}_{\rho}(A_i)d\mu
 \le \sum_iw_i\mathbb E_\Omega[(A_i^R-A_i^A)^2].
 \tag{6}
\]

The right side is basis-free and records the correct reference/output marginals. The missing implication is from this quantum coupling cost to one classical ensemble for a growing, possibly noncommuting frame.

For one observable, the implication is an exact square identity. Let $A=\sum_y a_yP_y$ be its spectral PVM and pull it back from one output: $E_y=\Phi^*(P_y)=\Phi(P_y)$. Trace preservation gives $p_y=\tau(E_y)=\tau(P_y)>0$, and self-adjointness gives $\tau(E_yA)=\tau(P_y\Phi(A))$. The canonical score obeys

\[
 \sum_y\frac{\tau(E_yA)^2}{p_y}
 -\left(2\langle A,\Phi(A)\rangle_\tau-\|A\|_{2,\tau}^2\right)
 =\sum_y\frac{(\tau(E_yA)-a_yp_y)^2}{p_y}\ge0.
 \tag{7}
\]

Thus (V) holds for rank-one $Q$, without using the broadcaster.

It also holds for any frame of rank at most two. If $A_1,A_2$ have spectral PVMs $(P_y),(R_z)$, measure $P_y$ on output $A$ and $R_z$ on output $B$. The pulled-back effects

\[
 M_{yz}=\mathcal B^*(P_y\otimes R_z)
\]

form a full input POVM. Summing over $z$ gives marginal effects $\Phi(P_y)$; summing over $y$ gives $\Phi(R_z)$. Refinement from either marginal POVM to $(M_{yz})$ can only increase its canonical score, since

\[
 \sum_z\frac{\tau(M_{yz}A)^2}{\tau(M_{yz})}
 \ge\frac{\tau((\sum_zM_{yz})A)^2}{\tau(\sum_zM_{yz})}
\]

by scalar Cauchy--Schwarz. Apply (7) to each marginal and add with the frame weights. The product PVM has at most $d^2$ outcomes and uses one application of the supplied $\mathcal B^*$. No positivity assumption on $\Phi$ as an HS operator is used.

## Exact failure of the naive frame aggregation

The scalar identity (7) cannot simply be summed over $i$: it provides a different POVM for each observable. A concrete support-function check shows the loss. For a qubit, take the traceless Pauli observables $X,Z$. Their separate supports are $S_2(|X\rangle\langle X|)=S_2(|Z\rangle\langle Z|)=1$, attained by their eigenbasis PVMs. For the mixed dual $Q=|X\rangle\langle X|+|Z\rangle\langle Z|$, every pure state has Bloch coordinates satisfying $x^2+z^2\le1$, so every canonical ensemble scores at most $1$; an $X$-eigenstate ensemble attains $1$. Hence

\[
 S_2(Q)=1<2=S_2(|X\rangle\langle X|)+S_2(|Z\rangle\langle Z|).
 \tag{8}
\]

This is an exact failure of support additivity, not a legal channel counterexample. It shows why separately valid rank-one inequalities do not imply (2).

A second direct strategy is to randomize a pair of frame labels, measure one observable on each output, and retain the pair as a classical outcome. If pair $(i,j)$ is selected with probability $\alpha_{ij}$, the guaranteed diagonal contribution obtained from (7) is only

\[
 \sum_i w_i\pi_i\bigl(2\langle A_i,\Phi(A_i)\rangle_\tau-\|A_i\|_2^2\bigr),
 \qquad \pi_i=\Pr(i\text{ is selected}),\quad \sum_i\pi_i\le2.
 \tag{9}
\]

For a large frame with many positive diagonal targets, this bound loses the selection probabilities. The actual POVM may obtain useful cross-frame score as well, so (9) is only a precise failure of the diagonal-only estimate; it is **not** an impossibility theorem for all collective two-output measurements.

The prior full-tree proof avoids this bottleneck by assigning separate frame tests to a binary broadcaster tree. It yields the exact logarithmic comparator $I-\Psi\preceq2\lceil\log_2(d^2)\rceil(I-\Phi)$; its variable-depth form replaces rank by an energy-weighted entropy/spectral profile, but a flat profile still costs logarithmically. These are valid dimension-dependent controls, not the target. The C5 pairwise almost-commutation route also stops at a growing-frame/common-measurement gate: average pairwise commutator control is insufficient by itself (the Pauli $X_i,Z_i$ frame has mean squared commutator $2/n$ while every common POVM has mean variance at least $1/2$); that frame is not a legal channel witness. The C7 moment dual further rules out two tempting relaxations: an unconstrained pure-state maximizer need not satisfy the barycenter $I/d$, and an unconstrained star-Hamiltonian eigenvector need not have the reference/output marginals of a legal broadcaster. The cycle-eight Markov branch's CP-square/SOS identity proves the target for a supplied bistochastic $C$ with $\Phi=C^*C$, but its explicit qubit obstruction shows not every admissible marginal has such a representation. None closes (V).

## Exact remaining gate and evidence boundary

The general, basis-free all-$Q$ statement to prove is (V), equivalently (2) or (6), for every supplied legal $(\Phi,\mathcal B)$ and every finite positive weighted Hermitian frame. If it is proved, compact-convex separation gives one canonical finite-POVM EB map on the full Hermitian domain. If false, a counterexample must give a legal broadcaster state with the fixed reference and both output marginals, plus a positive $Q$ and a rigorous lower bound on the left side of (V) (equivalently a global moment-dual envelope); a pointwise observable test, an unconstrained star eigenvalue, or the nonadditivity example (8) is insufficient.

The two-output derivation resolves ranks one and two exactly and identifies the obstruction to the naive quantum-variance sum: no argument found here converts all the $A_i^R-A_i^A$ discrepancies into a *single barycenter-correct pure-state ensemble* without either a rank/entropy loss or an additional structural hypothesis. This is a route-level obstruction only. The universal factor-two theorem and a legal counterexample remain **UNKNOWN**.
