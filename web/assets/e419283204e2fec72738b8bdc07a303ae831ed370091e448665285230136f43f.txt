MARGINAL NEGATIVE WINDOW: COHERENT LOWER RATE AND A BEREZIN UPPER-SYMBOL FAILURE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: scoped internally proved lower bound and route obstruction. The exact
speed-L free-energy limit and the true phase boundary are UNKNOWN. No sampler
impossibility claim is made.

1. Interface and scaling
------------------------
There are S=L-1 open hard-core sites, with n_i=(1-Z_i)/2,

  N=sum_i n_i,   W=sum_{i=1}^{S-1}n_i n_{i+1},
  Hcrit=J sum_{i=1}^{S-1}(I-X_iX_{i+1}-Y_iY_{i+1}-Z_iZ_{i+1})
             +J(I-Z_1)+J(I-Z_S),

J,tau>0 are fixed, |lambda|<=B, and kappa>=0 is fixed. The marginal
negative coupling is

  V_L=-4J-kappa/L^2,
  H_L=Hcrit-kappa W/L^2-lambda N/L^2,
  beta_L=tau L^2.

Thus

  Z_L=Tr exp[-tau L^2 Hcrit+tau kappa W+tau lambda N].    (1)

Hcrit is the positive open ferromagnetic Heisenberg form. In the occupation
basis, its exact quadratic form is

  <f,Hcrit f>=2J sum_{unordered X~X'}|f(X)-f(X')|^2
                  +2J sum_X(n_1(X)+n_S(X))|f(X)|^2.       (2)

The local spin is fixed at 1/2 while L grows. This is not the large-spin
classical limit.

2. Coherent-symbol lower bound: the proposed functional is rigorous below
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let theta be any C^2 profile on [0,1] with theta(0)=theta(1)=0, and put

  p(x)=sin^2(theta(x)/2),
  |Psi_L(theta)>=tensor_{i=1}^{L-1}
       [cos(theta(i/L)/2)|0>+sin(theta(i/L)/2)|1>].

The state is normalized, product, and has zero entropy. The covariant spin
symbol is the unit vector s_i=(sin theta_i,0,cos theta_i). The exact bond
and endpoint expectations are

  <J(I-sigma_i.sigma_{i+1})>
       =J[1-cos(theta_{i+1}-theta_i)],
  <J(I-Z_1)> =J[1-cos(theta_1)],
  <J(I-Z_S)> =J[1-cos(theta_S)].

Because theta vanishes at the continuum endpoints, endpoint terms are
O(L^-2). Riemann sums give

  <Hcrit>= J/(2L) integral_0^1 |theta'|^2 dx + o(L^-1),
  <N>= L integral_0^1 p dx+o(L),
  <W>= L integral_0^1 p^2 dx+o(L).

The Gibbs variational principle therefore proves, for every such theta,

  liminf_{L->infinity} (1/L)log Z_L
    >= tau { kappa integral p^2 + lambda integral p
                        - (J/2) integral |theta'|^2 }.     (3)

Taking the supremum defines the coherent lower rate

  Phi_coh(kappa,lambda)=sup_{theta in H^1_0(0,1), 0<=theta<=pi}
       [ kappa integral p^2 + lambda integral p
                            - (J/2) integral |theta'|^2 ].  (4)

The restriction to [0,pi] loses no profile with the same p: replacing an
angle by its folded value in [0,pi] preserves p and does not increase its
Dirichlet energy. Smooth profiles are dense for this lower-bound supremum.
Since theta=0 is allowed, Phi_coh>=0. Equation (3) is a one-sided
variational theorem, not a formula for the full free energy.

3. A coherent sufficient threshold and what it proves
------------------------------------------------------
Define the coherent threshold

  lambda_coh(kappa)=inf_{theta not identically 0}
       [(J/2) integral |theta'|^2-kappa integral p^2]
                                  / integral p.           (5)

Then Phi_coh>0 whenever lambda>lambda_coh(kappa), and Phi_coh=0 for
lambda<=lambda_coh(kappa). The latter statement is only about the supremum
in (4), not about the true thermal phase boundary.

Poincare and p<=theta^2/4, p^2<=p imply

  2J pi^2-kappa <= lambda_coh(kappa) <=2J pi^2.            (6)

For kappa=0 the threshold is exactly 2J pi^2: the lower inequality follows
from Dirichlet Poincare, and theta=a sin(pi x), a->0, approaches equality.
For kappa>0 the precise variational value (5) is left unevaluated. The
attractive quartic can cause a nonlinear/first-order shift below the
small-amplitude value; no closed-form threshold is claimed.

If Phi_coh>0, equation (3) gives Z_L>=exp[tau Phi_coh L+o(L)]. This proves
macroscopic occupation, not merely escape from every fixed number sector.
One short conversion uses the independently established marginal sector
bound from the local exclusion heat trace: for this model

  Z_{L,m} <= A^m,  A=e C_tau exp[tau(kappa+B)],
  C_tau=1+2 sum_{r>=1}exp(-8J tau r^2),                (7)

uniformly in L and 0<=m<=L-1. Consequently
Z_{N<=epsilon L}<= (L+1)A^(epsilon L). Choose epsilon>0 with
epsilon log A<tau Phi_coh. Then

  P_L(N>=epsilon L)->1

exponentially fast in L. This use of (7) is only a conversion of the
coherent lower bound into a density statement; it does not provide the
coherent functional's matching upper bound. The source proof is
outputs/research/sol_attractive_critical/proof.txt, equations (5.3)-(5.6),
with the displayed constants specialized to this scale.

For fixed m, the marginal scaled block is
L^2 Hcrit-m-sector - kappa W - lambda m. Since W<=m-1, Golden--Thompson
and the fixed-time exclusion heat-trace bound (source proof.txt (5.3)--(5.6))
give Z_{L,m}=O_m(1), uniformly in L. In the fixed-m continuum scaling the
contact parameter is L(V_L+4J)=-kappa/L->0, so the corresponding LL sector,
if that fixed-sector limit is invoked, is the ideal-boson critical sector
with finite Dirichlet one-particle heat trace. This fixed-m statement does
not imply full-Fock tightness: the m~L sectors can dominate (1) when
Phi_coh>0.

4. Speed-L upper bound and the precise Berezin failure
------------------------------------------------------
There is an elementary full-Fock upper bound at the correct *scale*, but
not with the coefficient in (4). The product trial has zero von Neumann
entropy; it charges its profile energy and potential only. In contrast, the
full trace has configuration entropy at most (L-1)log 2, and this can
contribute at speed L. Since Hcrit>=0, W<=L-2, N<=L-1,

  0<=log Z_L <= (L-1)log 2 +tau(kappa+B)(L-1).            (8)

So log Z_L=O(L), with lower rate at least tau Phi_coh. Equation (8) charges
the full configuration entropy L log 2 and the worst-case potential energy.
It does not match the profile functional.

Here is the exact failed step for the most direct sitewise Berezin-Lieb
upper bound. Let n in S^2, P(n)=(I+n.sigma)/2, and d omega be normalized
spherical area measure. The spin-1/2 resolution is

  2 integral P(n)d omega=I.

The contravariant (upper) symbol of sigma^a is 3 n_a, since
2 integral 3 n_a P(n)d omega=sigma^a using
integral n_a n_b d omega=delta_ab/3. Product resolution then gives

  sigma_i.sigma_{i+1}  <->  9 n_i.n_{i+1},
  J(I-sigma_i.sigma_{i+1}) <-> J(1-9 n_i.n_{i+1}),
  J(I-Z_i) <-> J(1-3 n_{i,z}),
  n_i <-> (1-3 n_{i,z})/2,
  n_i n_j <-> (1-3 n_{i,z})(1-3 n_{j,z})/4.             (9)

Berezin-Lieb convexity would give

  Z_L <= 2^(L-1) integral exp[-tau L^2 h^U_L(n_1,...,n_S)]
                                      product_i d omega_i, (10)

where h^U_L is obtained by inserting the upper symbols (9) into H_L.
The covariant bond symbol is J(1-n_i.n_{i+1}), but the upper symbol is
J(1-9 n_i.n_{i+1}); their difference is order J per bond, not O(1/L).

An exact witness is the all-down coherent configuration n_i=-z. For S=3,
the actual Hcrit covariant symbol is 4J, while its contravariant upper
symbol is -8J. In general the latter is -8J(S-2), whereas the former is
4J. A fixed-radius product neighborhood of this point has measure
exp[-O(L)] and still has h^U_L<=-cJ L for a fixed c>0 and large L.
Consequently the right side of (10) contains contributions of size
exp[Omega(L^3)], even though the true partition has the elementary
exp[O(L)] bound (8). The standard sitewise upper-symbol estimate is thus
far too loose to establish a speed-L coefficient or (4) as a limit.

This is an obstruction to this specific Berezin-Lieb upper-symbol route,
not a no-go theorem for all possible contravariant symbols, block
coherent-state bounds, entropy inequalities, or controlled mean-field
arguments. Upper symbols are not unique, and an optimized nonlocal symbol
could behave differently. No matching upper bound for Phi_coh is proved.

5. Costs and finite checks
--------------------------
The state space has dimension 2^(L-1), requiring L-1 occupation qubits;
the m-sector dimension is binom(L-1,m). The explicit profile trial needs
L-1 local rotations and O(L) evaluations of theta,p and adjacent
differences. For theta(x)=2 pi x(1-x), each sampled angle is a rational
multiple of pi. A mesh Riemann estimate is O(L^-1) for a C^2 profile; a
certified value of (4) for a general optimized profile is not supplied.
If each rotation is synthesized to angle error O(delta/L), the product
state has trace error O(delta). Since ||H_L||=O(JL+kappa+B/L), beta||H_L||
=O(L^3); delta=O(L^-3) keeps the variational exponent error O(1), using
O(log L) bits per angle and O(L) rotations. This estimates a test-state
cost, not the Gibbs trace or a thermal sampler.

`checks.py`/`checks.json` verify exactly, by polynomial reduction modulo
c_i^2+s_i^2=1, the full occupation-matrix expectations of Hcrit,N,W against
the independent coherent bond/endpoint formulas for L=4,5,6. They also
check the spin-1/2 upper-symbol normalization and the S=3 all-down witness.
All checks pass. These are finite identities only.

6. Claim boundary, falsifier, sources, and restart
-------------------------------------------------
Established here: the profile functional is a rigorous Gibbs-variational
lower bound; Phi_coh>0 is a sufficient condition for a positive speed-L
partition lower bound and macroscopic occupation; log Z=O(L); and the
canonical sitewise Berezin upper-symbol substitution is unusable at this
scaling. Unknown: existence/value of lim L^-1 log Z, equality with
tau Phi_coh, and necessity or the true location/order of the thermal
occupation transition. Fixed-m LL limits alone do not resolve these
full-Fock questions.

Falsifier for the claimed coherent lower bound: an exact product-state
calculation contradicting (3), including its endpoint terms. Falsifier for
any future equality claim: a certified lower bound on L^-1 log Z strictly
above tau Phi_coh along a sequence. The next decisive gate is a *different*
upper mechanism controlling entropy and low-energy ferromagnetic modes at
speed L; the raw upper symbol (9) cannot be repaired by dropping its
negative bonds.

Source boundary: E. H. Lieb, “The Classical Limit of Quantum Spin Systems,”
Commun. Math. Phys. 31 (1973), 327-340,
https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01646493, proves classical/quantum partition
bounds in a large-spin limit (the spin parameter tends to infinity, after
the thermodynamic limit). That does not justify replacing a fixed spin-1/2
chain with this L-dependent inverse temperature by the static profile
integral. The local coherent identities and the failed upper-symbol
calculation above are derived directly; no external priority claim is
made.
