RESULT

OA113's source theorem is substantially narrower than a generic bounded-treewidth or DPP energy claim. For a finite rooted tree of labels, an even N-vertex set, each vertex in one label or two adjacent labels, and exactly one color-l edge for each vertex pair sharing label l, suppose rational activities satisfy h_l/D≤W_e≤3h_l with h_l=2^{level(l)} and integer D≥1. Assume N≥2 and at least one colored perfect matching. For the weighted matching law π and the two-replica kernel that chooses with probability 1/3 a two-edge switch in coordinate A, a color change in A, or an alternating-discrepancy-cycle layer swap of (A,B), followed by Metropolis for π⊗π, the pinned manuscript proves for every pair of real functions f1,f2:

    Var_π f1 ≤ (10^4 N D)^100 E_{π⊗π,P}[f1(A)+f2(B)].

The same coefficient holds under any common activity clamp. This is an additive-function inequality on two replicas, not a full Poincare inequality for arbitrary functions on M². The proof uses a tree-label-adapted quadrangulation of every alternating cycle, admissible odd-arc chords and good-arc leaf repairs, within-cell activity ratios ≤192D (bounded by A0=1000D), polynomially loaded two-matching encodings, and an involution toggling earlier discrepancy cycles in both demand layers. Long-arc conversions in the signed identity need not themselves be legal chain transitions. The final proof coefficient is bounded by 10^51 N^34 D^6 and then by the displayed L.

The tree-bag axioms are the exact sufficient hypotheses in the source, but I found no theorem that they are logically minimal. The least restrictive interface justified by this proof is: (1) algorithmic admissible-arc quadrangulation and good-arc local repairs for every discrepancy cycle; (2) polynomial local weight ratios, stable along clamps/annealing; (3) polynomial-preimage, polynomial-distortion demand encodings; and (4) a stationary-mass-preserving involution (or equivalent charge) converting context errors to legal pair energy. Another representation could satisfy those obligations differently. OA113 then adds a separate ladder theorem with q=32L copies per tier, adjacent laws within factor 2, exact tree-DP base refreshes and ordered ANOVA orthogonality, obtaining a full product-chain gap ≥3/(8M) and ≥1/(20LM), where M=Tq²+q and T=O(N²(K+log D+1)). The pair inequality alone does not yield that gap.

DETERMINANT-VOLUME PRIOR ART

There is a genuine direct analogue for ordinary k-volume sampling: Anari–Oveis Gharan–Rezaei (2016) prove that the lazy exchange chain for a k-homogeneous strongly-Rayleigh law, including k-DPPs, has single-replica Poincare constant λ≥1/(2kn). Random-scan independent updates of both replicas therefore satisfy the exact additive bound Var_μ(f1)≤4kn E(f1(x)+f2(y)) for every f1,f2. This is a deduction from their stronger single-replica theorem, not a special two-replica theorem quoted from the paper.

Kathuria–Deshpande's Partition-DPP work gives exact sampling under a fixed quota vector across p partition blocks, polynomial when p is a constant. The hard-BCS target c_k(F) is a mixture over binomial(n,k) quota vectors on n coordinate-pair blocks, with each block selected 0 or 2 times. Its weights are the block-complete Gram minors det((V*V)[P(I)]). The constant-p sampler does not sample this n-block mixture; using only two groups (all e-columns and all f-columns) permits mismatched coordinates and changes the support.

The hard-BCS pair law is not generally strongly Rayleigh on its pair index set. For the directed 4-cycle permutation matrix and k=2, its only supported pairs are {0,2} and {1,3}. They fail matroid basis exchange, so the usual homogeneous strongly-Rayleigh single-element exchange chain cannot cover this target. This exact obstruction does not refute all richer kernels. Separately, for a four-site rational matrix F_δ=[[0,δ,1,0],[δ,0,0,1],[1,0,0,1],[0,1,1,0]], a union-preserving pair reassignment changes determinant amplitudes (1,1) to (δ,δ), hence pair squared-weight ratio δ^4 despite all line activities equal to one. Taking δ=2^−b gives ratio 2^−4b with O(b)-bit input. This refutes direct transfer of OA113's local activity-comparability acceptance argument, not every possible transition graph or mixing theorem.
