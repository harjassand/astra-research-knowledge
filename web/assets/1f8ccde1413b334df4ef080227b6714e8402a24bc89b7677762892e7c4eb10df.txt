# Critical qubit branch-dimension gate

Date: 2026-10-08.  Status: **OPEN for every fixed Q>1; no counterexample found.**
This note isolates the quantum-branch question from the already distinct total
hybrid-dimension and entanglement-breaking (Q=1) results.  It records a
promising covariant-Casimir route, including a useful rank-Q estimate for one
irreducible spin block, and the still-missing step that prevents promoting it
to the iid full-orbit theorem.

## Question and exact interface

For each n let

    rho_(n,r,v) = ((I + r v·sigma)/2)^tensor n,
    v in S^2,     c0 <= r sqrt(n) <= c1,

where 0<c0<=c1<infinity are fixed and the known radius r=r_n is supplied to
encoder and decoder.  The unknown is the full Bloch direction v.  A blind
encoder E_n receives the density operator, not v, and outputs a dephased
hybrid algebra

    A_n = direct_sum_x M_(d_x),
    Q_n = max_x d_x,
    D_n = sum_x d_x.

Every input-dependent classical label is a one-dimensional block and is
included in D_n.  A Q-only lower may grant arbitrarily many such labels, but
that does not make them free in the total-D ledger.  There is no free
input-dependent side register, receiver entanglement, or feedback.  The
decoder is blind to v.  For the state-reconstruction interface it is a fixed
CPTP map R_n from A_n to n qubits, and

    eps_n = sup_v (1/2)||R_n E_n(rho_(n,r,v))-rho_(n,r,v)||_1.

This entails the same eps_n bound for every later POVM, including arbitrary
collective POVMs (and fixed ancillary systems), by trace-distance contraction.
Conversely, a requirement for all POVMs of one fixed decoded state is exactly
the trace-norm requirement.  This is not a requirement to preserve a
purification or an external record, nor does it allow the later query to leak
v to the decoder.  A query-specific decoder that receives a query containing
v would be a different interface and could trivialize the task.

The gate is whether, uniformly over every sequence r_n with
c0<=r_n sqrt(n)<=c1,

    Q_n <= Q fixed  ==>  eps_n >= c(Q,c0,c1) r_n,

or whether some fixed Q admits eps_n=o(r_n).  A proof of the displayed
inequality would imply Q_n->infinity for every eps_n=o(r_n) protocol, while
not yet giving a rate for Q_n.  No such result is established in this note.

## What is established and what it does not imply

The Q=1 case is the classical/entanglement-breaking boundary.  The local
critical-spin report proves an internally reconstructed candidate bound
e_EB=Theta_(c0,c1)(r): a bounded posterior-score witness gives a finite spin
sum, and a coherent measure-and-prepare map has error at most 2r.  The lower
allows arbitrary EB outcome spaces, all classical labels, arbitrary
input/output spin-sector transitions, and common seeds.  Its proof is not a
Q>1 proof.  In particular, a classical posterior-mean inequality does not
bound the extra noncommuting observables in a quantum branch.  See
`outputs/research/sol_collision_precision/RESULT.txt`, §§12–13, and the audit
boundary in `outputs/research/memory_information/prior_art_critical_eb.md`.

The critical comparison N08 does not decide this gate.  Its known-block
Gaussian archive law is conditional on the inherited bridge with remainder
C_(rho0)(r+n^(-1/2)); for this radius the remainder is O(r).  Its precision
law D=Theta(epsilon^(-3/2)) is certified only when epsilon->0 and
epsilon sqrt(n)->infinity.  The requested epsilon=o(r) lies strictly below
that window.  N07 is an arbitrary-quantum/hybrid **total-D** Gaussian-memory
converse, not a branch-Q theorem, and its constants assume a non-collapsing
mean set.  N36/N66 later-query Holevo converses likewise charge total D and
do not bound the largest quantum block separately.

The critical-qubit Vandermonde counterexample in MI-05 does establish that
bounded total D cannot give eps_n->0.  This does not force Q_n to diverge:
classical labels can make D_n diverge while Q_n=1.  In the present stronger
precision regime epsilon=o(r)->0 it only gives D_n->infinity.

The primary quantum-population-coding benchmark is Yang, Bai, Chiribella and
Hayashi, arXiv:1701.03372 (v1 submitted 2017-01-12, v8 2019-01-26,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03372).  Its Theorem 1 / §V says generic fixed
full-rank noncommuting models need some quantum memory for asymptotically
faithful compression, although the quantum/classical bit ratio can be made
arbitrarily small.  The reference spectrum is fixed and its Q-LAN limit is
not the triangular critical sequence r_n~n^(-1/2); it gives no fixed-Q
branch lower at error o(r).  This is a control, not a transfer.

N22 is a separate useful positive control: for the *pure* local coherent
qudit chart, Q_max=Theta((1+min(n,h_epsilon))^(d-1)) even with unlimited
dephased classical outcomes, where h_epsilon=ln(1/epsilon)/lnln(1/epsilon).
Its lower uses pure-state preservation and an entanglement-fidelity/
Schmidt-number argument.  The critical qubit inputs are mixed; their
purifications are not part of this state-only reconstruction contract, so
that argument cannot be applied to them.

N23 reinforces the need to separate Q from D and to specify acquisition.
Near the rank boundary it reports Q=Theta(1+min(n,h_epsilon)) for the
coherent coordinate even with free classical labels, while an unknown Poisson
intensity contributes an additional counted D factor.  Its finite-n
comparison has error C/n and its total-D lower below that scale is open.  It
does not cover the present fixed-temperature mixed-spin limit.  If radius,
eigenvalue gaps, or collision labels are not supplied, their acquisition
costs and any parameter-estimation error must be stated separately; neither
N08 nor this note silently supplies them.

## Covariant/Casimir attack

The following reduction is exact for a **single irreducible spin-j source**
and suggests a route for the iid family.  It is not yet the full iid proof.

Consider

    tau_(j,h,v) = exp((h/j) J_v) / Z_j(h),   v in S^2,
    0 < h0 <= h <= h1 < infinity,

where the radius-like spin parameter h is supplied.  A Q-branch archive,
with its classical labels arbitrary, induces a channel Lambda from V_j to
V_j whose normalized Choi state has Schmidt number at most Q: each classical
branch factors through M_(d_x), d_x<=Q, and local decoding cannot increase
Schmidt number.  Twirling Lambda over SU(2) preserves the Q bound and does
not increase its worst-case orbit error.  By irreducibility, a covariant
Lambda acts on the vector operators as

    Lambda^*(J_i) = eta_1 J_i,       i=x,y,z.

The bounded witness J_v/j has operator norm one.  Riemann sums give

    Tr(tau_(j,h,v) J_v/j) -> mu(h)=coth(h)-1/h > 0.

Thus, if e_j=sup_v T(Lambda(tau_(j,h,v)),tau_(j,h,v)), then

    2 e_j >= mu_j(h) |1-eta_1|,
    mu_j(h)=Tr(tau_(j,h,z)J_z/j),

so trace error directly controls the first-harmonic multiplier.  This is a
valid trace-to-witness conversion; it does not rely on continuity of QFI.

For the normalized Choi vector |A>> of a Kraus operator A with ||A||_2=1,
the identity-channel Choi overlap with the first-harmonic Casimir witness is
exactly related to the adjoint Casimir:

    defect(A) = sum_i ||[J_i,A]||_2^2
              = sum_(ell,m) ell(ell+1) |A_(ell,m)|^2,

where A_(ell,m) are its irreducible tensor-operator coefficients.  A
rank-Q Choi vector is a matrix A with rank(A)<=Q.  A sufficient estimate for
the one-spin claim is

    inf_{rank(A)<=Q, ||A||_2=1} defect(A) >= c_Q j
    for each fixed Q and all sufficiently large j.                 (C1)

Indeed, the covariant Choi expectation would then give

    1-eta_1 >= c'_Q/j,
    e_j >= c''_(Q,h0,h1)/j.

There is a concrete route to (C1), not just a QFI slogan.  Let D=2j+1 and
let T_(ell,m) be a Hilbert-Schmidt orthonormal tensor-operator basis.  Schur's
lemma and the trace give

    sum_m T_(ell,m) T_(ell,m)^* = ((2ell+1)/D) I.

Consequently, for any unit vectors u,v,

    sum_m |<u|T_(ell,m)|v>|^2 <= (2ell+1)/D.

By duality, every operator B supported on tensor ranks ell<=L obeys

    ||B||_op <= (L+1)/sqrt(D) ||B||_2.

For rank-Q A, ||A||_1<=sqrt(Q)||A||_2, hence its low-rank multipole mass
satisfies

    ||Pi_(ell<=L) A||_2^2 <= Q (L+1)^2/D ||A||_2^2.

Taking L=floor(sqrt(D/(4Q)))-1 when D>=64Q gives
Q(L+1)^2/D<=1/4 and L+1>=3sqrt(D)/(8sqrt(Q)).  At least 3/4 of the
Hilbert-Schmidt mass then lies at ell>L, so

    defect(A) >= (3/4)(L+1)^2 >= 27 D/(256 Q).

For the Choi normalization, with |A>> the vectorization of a normalized
Kraus operator,

    < <A| sum_i (J_i^T tensor J_i) |A> > = sum_i Tr(A^* J_i A J_i),
    sum_i ||[J_i,A]||_2^2
       = 2j(j+1) - 2 sum_i Tr(A^* J_i A J_i).

Thus, for a covariant channel whose Choi state has Schmidt number at most Q,
averaging the last identity over a decomposition into rank-at-most-Q Kraus
vectors yields

    1-eta_1 >= 27 D/[512 Q j(j+1)] = Omega(1/(Qj)),  D>=64Q.

Together with the bounded-witness conversion, this proves for the single
irreducible-spin family the internally derived lower

    e_j >= c_(h0,h1)/(Qj),  1<=Q<=D/64,

for all sufficiently large j.  If Q>D/64, the memory is already Omega(j).
In particular, fixed Q leaves an Omega(1/j) floor, and e_j=o(1/j) forces
Q_j->infinity.  This argument allows arbitrary classical labels and every
Q-branch instrument because it uses only the Schmidt-number-Q Choi
constraint.  It is an internally derived result, not externally reviewed.

An explicit covariant test instrument is also informative.  In a spin-j
block let P_Q project onto the top Q magnetic levels and set

    K_g = sqrt(D/Q) U_j(g) P_Q U_j(g)^*,
    Lambda_Q(X) = integral K_g X K_g^* dg.

The normalization follows from integral U_j(g)P_QU_j(g)^*dg=(Q/D)I.
Each outcome g is classical, the retained quantum branch has dimension Q,
and the decoder re-embeds the projected block.  Direct ladder-operator
calculation gives

    eta_1 = 1 - (2j-Q+1)/(2j(j+1)),       1<=Q<=2j+1.

For fixed Q this instrument still loses order 1/j in its vector moment; for
Q=1 it is the usual coherent spin measure-and-prepare map, and for Q=2j+1
it is the identity.  This is not an optimality proof.  It does show that a
natural constant-Q covariant correction attempt does not give an o(1/j)
counterexample.

### The iid reduction that remains missing

The qubit Schur decomposition is

    rho_(n,r,v) = direct_sum_j p_(n,j) tau_(j,h_(n,j),v) tensor I_(m_j)/m_j,
    h_(n,j) = 2 r j,

up to the harmless convention of J=one-half total Pauli spin.  In the
critical window, the mass of j=Theta(sqrt(n)) is bounded away from zero and
h_(n,j) lies in a compact positive interval on a suitable band.  Multiplicity
states are parameter-independent and can be regenerated.  The exact Schur
state-only compression therefore has maximum branch dimension n+1 and
total direct-sum dimension sum_j(2j+1)=O(n^2); it is an error-zero baseline
even if the spectrum/radius is not supplied.

However, applying the one-spin lower separately to each j is not yet valid.
An arbitrary decoder may send input sector j to output sector k and combine
contributions from multiple j in the same output block.  Measuring j before
encoding is nondisturbing, but it does **not** force the decoded output to
remain in that same sector; the target is a density matrix, not an
input-output record.  Twirling also allows intertwiners between shared
irreducible tensor ranks.  The EB report's completed-square score inequality
handles all such transitions only for Q=1.

The next sufficient lemma is a rectangular transition version: for every
covariant CP transition from spin j to spin k that factors through branch
dimension at most Q, bound its first-harmonic score by a term that is
strictly below the target score by at least c_Q/j on a critical positive-mass
band, uniformly in k, with a penalty that can be summed over arbitrary
stochastic sector couplings.  The rectangular Casimir defect

    sum_i ||J_i^(k) A - A J_i^(j)||_2^2

has spectrum ell(ell+1), ell=|j-k|,...,j+k; the same low-multipole/Schatten
argument is a plausible input to this bound.  One must then sum it against
the actual p_(n,j), preserve the target output-sector probabilities, and
convert the final bounded score gap to half trace distance.  This is the
load-bearing unsolved step.  Without it, C1 is only a one-spin lemma and is
not a proof for the original iid family.

## QFI/commutator controls and why they stop short

For the one-qubit Bloch family, tangent SLD QFI is order r^2 per copy, so
the n-copy directional QFI is order nr^2=Theta(1).  The mean SLD
commutator is order nr^3=Theta(r) in the critical window.  These scales
explain the classical local limit and the first nonclassical correction,
but neither is a branch-dimension converse.  QFI is not uniformly continuous
under trace norm: from eps control one only gets, for a bounded A,

    |Tr A(Lambda(rho_v^n)-rho_v^n)| <= 2 eps ||A||_op,

and for a finite difference at v,w the bound is 4 eps ||A||_op.  A derivative
or QFI comparison divides by the step size and also needs second-derivative
control; it cannot be inferred from trace closeness alone.  Even the O(r)
mean commutator can be represented by a qubit algebra, so merely observing a
nonzero commutator does not exclude Q=2.  A successful route needs a
dimension-sensitive multipole or instrument inequality such as C1 plus the
sector-transition summation above.

## Precision, unknown gaps, total-D ledger, and controls

* **Known radius (this task).** It is supplied to both parties.  Any
  acquisition procedure for r is excluded.  A finite-bit implementation
  would still have to specify the precision used by the cutoff, covariant
  instrument, or decoder; neither the branch-Q argument nor QFI supplies
  those bits.
* **Unknown radius.** This is a different source family, such as the full
  tracial ball ||2rho-I||_op<=delta_n.  N39's critical EB lower uses an
  admissible fixed critical sphere and is robust to not knowing the actual
  radius, but the minimum Q for error o(delta_n) remains open.  A radius-blind
  upper cannot invoke a decoder conditioned on the true radius.
* **Unknown qudit gaps.** For supplied known spectrum, the fixed-error
  collision product is B_n=product_(a<b)(1+n(p_a-p_b)^2), with all
  hybrid dimensions counted.  N08's critical precision exponent uses the
  full affine span only above its bridge remainder.  N39's EB floor does not
  determine Q>1.  When gaps are not supplied, spectrum acquisition and its
  precision must be charged separately; N23 shows this can change D even
  when Q is unchanged.
* **Total dimension.** Any future branch-Q theorem must report D=sum_x d_x
  separately.  The exact Schur archive is Q=n+1, D=O(n^2).  The critical
  Vandermonde lower only forces D->infinity when eps->0.  The known-radius
  classical Gaussian codec gives D=Theta(eps^(-3/2)) only in the N08 window
  eps sqrt(n)->infinity, and cannot be quoted for eps=o(r).  No matching
  total-D law below that precision is available here.
* **Later queries.** Full trace reconstruction controls every fixed later
  POVM.  If the interface weakens to a particular coordinate/query, the
  measurement description and query side information must be specified.
  N36 proves a sum-Holevo lower for independent Gaussian query features,
  not a generic SU(2) branch lower.
* **Multi-gap collisions.** A qudit analog would need transition bounds
  between all Schur-Weyl irreps/root planes, including gaps below, at, and
  above n^(-1/2).  Fixed-error N08/N39 orbit volume and Gaussian precision
  results have different regimes.  No fixed-Q conclusion follows by
  substituting a minimum gap into either formula.

## Falsifiers, status, and next gate

**Current theorem status:** the single irreducible-spin orbit has the
internally derived fixed-Q lower above, but the iid full-orbit question has
no fixed-Q>1 lower and no constant-Q o(r) construction.  The proof remains
incomplete for arbitrary inter-sector transitions in the iid output.  The
classical Q=1 floor is an independent control, never a proof for Q>1.  No new
numerical fixture is needed to support the unresolved status; the covariant
rank-Q instrument formula is an exact algebraic check, not evidence of
optimality.

**Decisive next work:** (1) independently formalize the tensor-rank/Schatten
bound and Choi normalization for rectangular j-to-k maps; (2) prove an
additive transition-aware posterior-score inequality for all Q-PEB hybrid
channels; (3) sum it over the critical Schur law with finite-n tail bounds;
(4) if the inequality fails, optimize the covariant rank-Q instrument over
Kraus operators and test whether Q=2 can cancel the O(1/j) term.  A finite
fixture showing positive first-harmonic loss is only diagnostic; the theorem
must be uniform in n and all protocols.

**Falsifier for a proposed fixed-Q lower:** an explicit CPTP instrument with
max branch dimension Q (all labels charged in D), no v-dependent side input,
and sup_v error o(r) on the full iid family.  **Falsifier for a proposed
constant-Q construction:** an audited uniform trace-norm lower 
liminf eps_n/r_n>0 for that fixed Q, including arbitrary sector changes.
