1. Every sigma-preserving KMS qubit CHANNEL is covered, including negative
   weighted spectrum and without requiring self-compatibility.

Let Phi be any full-domain qubit CPTP channel with faithful Phi(sigma)=sigma
and Heisenberg KMS symmetry Gamma Phi*=Phi Gamma. Define
 L=I−Phi*, L_*=I−Phi.
Then exp(−tL)=exp(−t) sum_{n>=0} t^n(Phi*)^n/n! is a unital CP semigroup
fixing sigma in the Schrödinger picture. It is KMS symmetric. The audited
faithful-qubit QMS theorem therefore applies. Its weighted positive generator
is exactly
 H=Gamma^(1/2)(I−Phi*)Gamma^(-1/2)
  =I−Gamma^(-1/2)Phi Gamma^(1/2)=I−T_Phi.
Thus for every faithful qubit rho,
 Tr[(rho−Phi(rho))(logrho−logsigma)]
    >=2 Tr[sqrt(rho)(I−T_Phi)(sqrt(rho))].
This is the original scalar channel gate with constant2 in dimension2.
No positivity of Phi's weighted spectrum is needed: Poissonization works for
any allowed KMS CPTP channel. The result includes positive-spectrum and
self-compatible/EB channels as subsets, without inferring compatibility for
all channels. Constant2 is proved sufficient, not optimal.

2. Singular qubit states: exact extended entropy-production limit

Let rho be a rank-one density P, Q=I−P, with faithful sigma. Let L_* be the
positive Schrödinger generator of a finite KMS QMS. Use
 rho_e=(1−e)rho+e sigma, 0<e<1.
It is faithful, L_*(rho_e)=(1−e)L_*(rho), and root energy converges to the
finite E(rho) because sqrt is continuous and H is a bounded matrix operator.

Positivity of exp(−tL_*)rho gives
 a_Q=Tr[Q L_*(rho)]<=0:
the Q population is zero at t=0 and its right derivative is −a_Q>=0.
The small eigenvalue of rho_e is e Tr(Q sigma)+O(e^2)>0, and its eigenprojector
is Q+O(e), since rho has a simple unit eigenvalue. Therefore:
 - If a_Q<0, the small-eigenvalue logarithm gives J(rho_e)->+infinity.
 - If a_Q=0, its coefficient is O(e); its O(e log e) contribution vanishes.
   The large-eigenvalue logarithm tends to zero, so
   J(rho_e)->−Tr[L_*(rho)logsigma], a finite value.
Define the extended entropy production J(rho) by these values, equivalently
the usual support interpretation of the log expression. The faithful
inequality passes to this finite/infinite limit and proves J(rho)>=2E(rho).
This covers all singular qubit density matrices; a trace-one qubit state
cannot have rank zero. The sigma reference remains faithful throughout.

For a discrete channel L_*=I−Phi, a_Q=−Tr[Q Phi(rho)]. If this is zero,
positivity of Phi(rho) forces its support to be the one-dimensional P, hence
Phi(rho)=rho and L_*(rho)=0. Then J(rho)=0 and the limiting inequality together
with E>=0 gives E(rho)=0. If Phi(rho) has any Q population, J(rho)=+infinity.
This explicitly handles the channel's pure fixed-state endpoint without an
unproved claim that physical sqrt(rho) must automatically be a weighted fixed
vector of every map fixing rho.

3. Untouched classical references, arbitrary alphabets, singular blocks

For rho_AR=sum_j p_j rho_j tensor |j><j|, active subsystem A a qubit, use
L_total=L_A tensor id_R, and any faithful product reference sigma_A tensor tau_R.
For every nonzero p_j, the logarithm in that block is logp_j+logrho_j.
The logp_j terms vanish because Tr L_A,*rho_j=0. The logtau_R term vanishes
globally because L_A(I)=0, independent of whether tau_R is diagonal in that
classical basis. The square root gives weighted block energies. Consequently
 J_total=sum_{p_j>0} p_j J_A(rho_j),
 E_total=sum_{p_j>0} p_j E_A(rho_j).
Singular rho_j are covered by Section2; zero p_j blocks remain zero under the
untouched-reference map and contribute nothing. Thus J_total>=2E_total with
the extended convention, with no alphabet-size bound.
No analogous block proof for a quantum reference is claimed.

tensor id_R and Tr[(L_A,* tensor id)rho(I tensor logtau_R)]=0. Thus J_total
and E_total are independent of the selected faithful product reference tau_R.
