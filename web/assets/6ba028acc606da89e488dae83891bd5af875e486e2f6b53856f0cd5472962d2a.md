# C8 implicit rational geometry: result

**Strongest result:** the exact essential-source recognition problem for sparse positive generalized-rational channels is coNP-complete when direction/species dimension is part of the input. The hardness reduction uses coefficient-1 positive integer polynomials with at most three monomials in each numerator and denominator. Thus the direct sparse-support representation avoids explicit common-denominator product expansion, but cannot yield a polynomial-time general recognizer unless P=NP. The delivered exact Z3 recognizer is compact and budget-aware; its worst-case solver work is not polynomial.

## Mathematical result

For channel `e`, let

\[
\rho_e=P_e/Q_e,\qquad
\tau_e(w)=\max_{a\in A_e}a\cdot w-\max_{b\in B_e}b\cdot w,
\]

with finite nonempty rational supports, strictly positive coefficients, and rational jump `nu_e`. The tested condition removes channels with `w dot nu_e = 0` before maximizing `tau`, and requires every active maximizer to point inward. Exact ties are retained: one outward member of an active top tie refutes the property.

The no-expansion identity is

\[
\operatorname{supp}\!\left(P_e\prod_{f\ne e}Q_f\right)
=A_e+\sum_{f\ne e}B_f,\qquad
h_{A_e+\sum_{f\ne e}B_f}(w)=h_D(w)+\tau_e(w),
\quad D=\prod_fQ_f.
\]

Because all coefficients are positive, ties and product collisions cannot cancel a top source. The common height `h_D(w)` leaves every cross-channel order and tie unchanged. This gives exact recognition from listed supports without materializing the potentially exponential Minkowski sums.

A real failure direction has a polynomial-bit rational witness: fix its support maximizers and channel sign/neutral branch; this gives a rational linear system with weak score comparisons and finitely many strict projection inequalities. Normalize a coordinate and optimize a positive margin for those strict inequalities. A rational basic solution has polynomial encoding size. Exact verification is polynomial in the input bits and sparse support count, so `NON-ENDOTACTIC` is in NP and `ENDOTACTIC` is in coNP.

For hardness, `PROOF.md` gives a many-one 3SAT reduction with coordinates `(u_1,...,u_n,z)`. Target, variable, clause, and paired guard channels encode the clause constraints. A satisfying assignment yields an outward target at an exact score-0 tie. The UNSAT proof checks all real directions by the three slices `z>0`, `z<0`, and `z=0`; the paired guards are essential on `z=0`. The construction has `3n+m+1` channels in `n+1` dimensions, constant support size and coefficient/exponent bit size. This proves coNP-hardness, and together with the rational-witness bound proves coNP-completeness for the declared sparse generalized-rational model.

A separate face-arrangement argument gives an `H^{O(d)}` exact algorithm for every fixed dimension `d`, where `H = m + sum_e [binom(|A_e|,2) + binom(|B_e|,2)]`. It is XP, not FPT. This is a mathematical bound, not the delivered backend.

## Executable decision interface

[`recognize_sparse_rational.py`](recognize_sparse_rational.py) uses one exact QF-LRA/ITE query per candidate outward edge. It encodes support maxima directly over the sparse terms; it never builds a product support or enumerates fan cells. `--property strong` first checks endotacticity, then checks the standard strong condition: an inward reaction must occur at the global maximum over *all* sources, with neutral source channels included. SAT directions are replayed using exact fractions in the same program and can be independently checked by [`verify_sparse_rational_counterdirection.py`](verify_sparse_rational_counterdirection.py), which uses only Python's `Fraction`.

Status contract:

- `REFUTED` includes a rational direction validated exactly for the selected property.
- `CERTIFIED` means all relevant exact solver queries returned `unsat`; no separately proof-checked UNSAT proof object is produced.
- `UNKNOWN` is returned for budget/timeout/resource exhaustion, missing solver, solver `unknown`, or an unreplayable model.

The input parser checks dimensions and strictly positive rational coefficients, collects duplicate support exponents, and preserves rational exponents/jumps. The main theorem covers arbitrary finite positive sparse generalized-rational rates. It does not claim coNP-hardness for every canonical Hill/Michaelis–Menten-only subfamily.

## Permanence and source boundary

For general endotacticity, permanence remains conditional on the internal N33 candidate and on the common-clock/forward-continuation hypotheses; this package does not prove N33 again. For the strongly endotactic lifted subfamily, the result is a structural transfer to established prior art, not a new permanence theorem: Gopalkrishnan, Miller, and Shiu state permanence for strongly endotactic networks and permit real complexes and compact positive reaction temperings ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1305.5303v2)). The standard strong definition uses the global all-reactant maximum, including neutral source channels.

Existing MILP recognition applies to finite explicit reaction diagrams ([Johnston, Pantea, and Donnell](https://arxiv.org/html/1412.4662)). It does not establish novelty or priority for quotient-support complexity. A multistationarity parameter-region paper was also checked as a neighboring but distinct endpoint ([Conradi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.03993)). Novelty/priority is `UNKNOWN`; see [`PRIMARY_SOURCES.md`](PRIMARY_SOURCES.md).

## Validation and artifacts

The exact SAT witness and tie, neutral-vs-strong distinction, positive endotactic/strong control, all-eight-clauses UNSAT fixture, and budget-to-`UNKNOWN` behavior were exercised. The inherited exact rational-lift regression also passed. See [`VALIDATION.md`](VALIDATION.md) for commands and scoped outputs. Corrections from the independent audit are itemized in [`CORRECTION_RECORD.md`](CORRECTION_RECORD.md).

The strong-hardness boundary has a concrete counterexample: for the UNSAT all-clauses fixture, direction `(0,0,0,-1)` puts all paired guards at neutral global score `2`, with no inward source at that maximum. Thus the weak coNP-hardness theorem does not imply strong-endotactic hardness. This is replayed in the standalone verifier and recorded in [`FAILURES_AND_RESTART.md`](FAILURES_AND_RESTART.md).
