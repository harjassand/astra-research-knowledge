2. FULL-SEPARABILITY WITNESS, INCLUDING MIXED PRODUCTS
For a product of arbitrary one-qubit states let p_i=<sigma_+sigma_->_i and
c_i=<sigma_->_i. Local positivity gives |c_i|^2<=p_i(1-p_i). Direct expansion
gives
 R=sum_i(p_i-|c_i|^2)+|sum_i c_i|^2
   >=sum_i p_i^2 >=(sum_i p_i)^2/N =<E>^2/N.
For a convex product mixture, linearity of R and Jensen's inequality for the
square give the SAME R>=<E>^2/N. This covers the entire full-SEP cone.

For any fixed beta>=0 it also supplies the linear entanglement witness
 W_beta=R_op-(2beta/N)E+(beta^2/N)I,
since every fully separable expectation is >=(<E>-beta)^2/N>=0. Thus the
later violation can be expressed by a single ordinary linear witness rather
than only a nonlinear inequality.

3. CONSERVED SPIN WEIGHTS, MULTIPLICITIES AND COHERENCES
Decompose H_N=direct sum_j (V_j tensor M_j), j=N/2-b,
b=0,...,floor(N/2), with multiplicity
d_(N,b)=binomial(N,b)-binomial(N,b-1)>0.
The jump is J_-^(j) tensor I_M. Consequently each spin-sector weight q_b is
conserved. For a full-rank initial state q_b>0 for every nonempty sector.
In particular q_1>0 when N>=2.

Define the conserved positive operator
 B=(N+1)I/2-sqrt(J^2+I/4).
Its value in spin j is b=N/2-j. Therefore
 beta=<B>_0=sum_b b*q_b>0
for every full-rank initial state at N>=2. Also E-B has eigenvalues n=j+m
in {0,...,2j}, so E>=B as an operator and <E>_s>=beta at every time.
This lower bound does not require the state to commute with J_z.

The populations p_(j,n)=Tr[rho (|j,m><j,m| tensor I_M)] obey a classical
pure-death equation even with initial spin/coherence/multiplicity correlations.
Neither coherences nor off-diagonal multiplicity entries enter these traces.
The rate out of n=j+m is
 lambda_(j,n)=n(2j-n+1).
It follows directly from the matrix element of J_-. The operator R_op is
diagonal in these same populations, so this population equation suffices for
all witness estimates. Initial finite axial fields alter positive sector and
magnetic weights but not the conserved-sector argument or rates.

For an axial Gibbs initial state the multiplicity block is scalar and magnetic

Hence R_infinity0 and E_infinity=beta>0. For arbitrary coherences the final

5. STRONGER SECTOR-UNIFORM BOUND
Put K=2j. For K>=1 and1<=n<=K,
 lambda_n-K=(n-1)(K-n)>=0.
There are at most K steps, each with rate at least K, so absorption is
dominated by Gamma(K,K). At parameter1/2 its exponential moment is at most

The last inequality is the integer Bernoulli inequality

This proves survival<=2exp(-s/2) independently of N and of the initial level.

The maximum ladder intensity is j(j+1) for integer j and j(j+1)+1/4 for
half-integer j. All allowed spins have the parity of N/2. Averaging the sector
bound therefore yields

with C conserved. For a full-rank N>=2 state C>0. Consequently
