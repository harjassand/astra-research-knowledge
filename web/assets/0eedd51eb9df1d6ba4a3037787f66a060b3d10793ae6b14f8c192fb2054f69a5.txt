1. WHAT IS CONSTRUCTIVE

The immutable INITIAL.txt proves a rational ambient-field identity and a
robust one-pass update. For rational-complex paired representation V and
positive rational row scaling D=diag(tau_i),

  Z_R(DV) = (product_{i in R} tau_i^2) Z_R(V),
  g_ij(DV) = g_ij(V)/(tau_i^2 tau_j^2).

The full-basis law is unchanged and the known partition correction is exact.
With one-sided estimates g_ij<=G_ij<=g_ij+2 alpha, alpha=10^-5, and a true
row maximum at least 1/8, the one-pass recurrence v_i <- max_j G_ij/v_j
(starting from 2 and updating in fixed order) has v_i in [1/16,2], keeps
all pair constraints, and leaves each row a tight edge. Rounding v_i upward
to q_i=(25/16)^m_i and setting tau_i=(5/4)^m_i is exact rational scaling.
Every scaled row maximum is at most one and the tight-edge lower bound is

  (256/625)(1-512 alpha) = 795904/1953125 > 1/4.

The initial F instance also has a deterministic coordinate-dominated base:
choose epsilon=2^-Q, Q=M+2ns+7 for M=n+binom(n,2), where 2^s bounds every
column norm. Hadamard and a 2^M subset bound make all mixed contributions at
most 1/128 of the coordinate-only partition, uniformly on every retained
even row set. Consequently the full law is within 1/128 TV of uniform
complete-graph matching, and a single rational uniform row scale puts all
hole ratios in [1/4,1]. Q=O(n^2+nL) for explicit input bit length L. This is
a valid initialization, not a sampler for later determinant-weighted stages.

The activity ramp epsilon to one needs O(nQ) stages at multiplicative step
1+1/n; reducing coordinate activities from one to D0^-1 needs O(n log n)
stages. Every full-basis weight changes by less than a factor two per stage.
In the coordinate-dominated case, the stated subdivision profile has p=2,
therefore nine edges per logical coordinate path. All activities and row
multipliers have polynomial binary descriptions. These identities and
conditioning facts are in INITIAL.txt and
revisions/conditioning_and_gauge_audit.txt.

2. ACQUISITION REDUCTION: NEAR-TIGHT FIELDS IMPLY A DETERMINISTIC FPTAS

Let G be any complete positive rationally weighted graph on an even set S,
Z(S) its weighted perfect-matching partition, and

  g_ij(S)=Z(S minus {i,j})/Z(S).

Suppose a deterministic polynomial-time routine works on every even residual
S and every 0<zeta<1, returning positive rational ambient row fields tau_i
and for every i a witness j_i with

  g_ij(S) <= tau_i^2 tau_j^2 for all j,
  g_i,j_i(S) >= (1-zeta) tau_i^2 tau_j_i^2.

Choose the least i, recurse on S minus {i,j_i}, and at each step set
q=tau_i^2 tau_j_i^2. Positivity of the witness lower bound keeps every
residual partition nonzero. Since Z(S)=Z(S-ij)/g_ij(S), the product
Y=product 1/q satisfies

  (1-zeta)^(n/2) Z(S) <= Y <= Z(S).

Taking zeta=epsilon/n gives (1-epsilon)Z(S)<=Y<=Z(S). The recursion uses
n/2 oracle calls and polynomial-bit rational products, so this hypothetical
near-tight acquisition routine is already a deterministic FPTAS for weighted
perfect-matching counting. The reduction is exact on the coordinate-pair
subfamily: assign edge activity w_ij to the pair (e_i,e_j), whose matching
minors have determinant +/-1.

This is not an impossibility theorem. It identifies the acquisition burden:
a deterministic near-tight scale procedure would settle a substantial
approximate-counting problem. The sparse unweighted benchmark follows by
padding missing edges with eta=epsilon/(4 n^(n/2)); bad matching weight is at
most eta (n-1)!! <= epsilon/4 when a matching exists. The padding has
polynomial bit length, and constant adjustment of the FPTAS tolerance yields
a sparse-graph approximation too.

3. THE CAPACITY WINDOW ALONE CANNOT COUNT

For K_n with unit edge weights, every even residual of size r has
Z(S)=(r-1)!! and g_ij=1/d for d=r-1. Let tau_star=sqrt(2)*d^(-1/4), so
 tau_star^4=4/d. Choose b=ceil(log2(4 sqrt(d))) and rational
 tau=2^-b floor(2^b tau_star), computable by an integer fourth root. Since
2^-b<=1/(4 sqrt(d))<=tau_star/4,

  (3/4)tau_star <= tau <= tau_star,
  81/(64d) <= tau^4 <= 4/d,
  1/4 <= g_ij/tau^4 <= 64/81 < 1.

Using tau on every row of each residual is a valid rational external field
with [1/4,1] row maxima and O(log n) bits. Yet the natural recursive estimate
q=tau^4 gives

  Y/Z(K_n) = product_{t=1}^{n/2} g_ij(S_t)/q_t
             <= (64/81)^(n/2).

Thus exact, uniformly valid fixed-window fields can give exponential
relative underestimation. This example concerns that recursive estimator; it
does not rule out unrelated deterministic estimators. It distinguishes the
imported fixed-window capacity condition from the near-tight condition needed
for an FPTAS reduction. The exact dyadic inequalities were checked for every
d=1,...,10000 in addition to the algebraic proof.

4. PRECISION, SAMPLE, AND MICROSCOPIC COSTS

A raw additive hole-ratio oracle cannot robustly choose a balance field when
hole ratios are exponentially small. For n=2 and F_t=[[0,t],[1,0]],
g=1/(t^2+1). Under a common absolute error allowance 2^-L, t=2^(L/2) and
t=2^L can both return zero, while their valid scale-product intervals
[g,4g] are disjoint for L>=4. This is only a limitation of that oracle
interface; these examples are easy from F itself.

Once estimates are supplied, the generalized rational update has polynomial
bit cost. If a row maximum is at least gamma and g<=G<=g+delta, the tight
edge ratio before rounding is at least 1-4delta/gamma^2. For a target
1-zeta, use beta=1+zeta/256 and round tau_i=beta^m_i upward so q_i=tau_i^2.
The pair denominator grows by at most beta^4<=1+zeta/32. Taking
delta=gamma^2*zeta/16 suffices; exponents and rational powers have polynomial
bit cost in 1/zeta and the input encoding. The exact initial coordinate base
can use contamination budget zeta/8. The missing operation is later
partition-ratio acquisition, not representation of the fields.

For a coordinate probe of activity w, the marked probability is w*g/I
with bag inflation I<=2. If a balanced row has some g>=1/4, this event has
probability at least w/8. Estimating g to additive alpha needs
O(w^-2 alpha^-2 log(nK/delta)) independent samples by Hoeffding. At the
weak-probe activity w=D0^-1 or D^-1 this is polynomial in the dimension and
precision, conditional on an actual sampler. This expectation/sample cost
does not provide the sampler.

All rational determinant weights and their pairwise ratios have polynomial
bit length: a selected n-minor has O(nL+n log n) bits by Hadamard, and at most
n/2 activities add O(nP) bits for activity length P. A specified weight is
computable by fraction-free elimination; summing exponentially many weights
is still not thereby polynomial. A rational transition can be simulated to
TV error at most T*2^-b over T transitions with b=ceil(log2(T/delta)), so
finite precision also is not the missing mixing theorem.

Finally, D multiplies every full determinant by the same det(D), so it
preserves every microscopic full-basis probability ratio. Exact subdivision
has a unique path-tiling lift per logical basis and multiplies every projected
basis weight by the same known factor. Neither identity improves the
conductance of a fixed projected chain or proves an energy bound; a different
kernel might still exploit the enlarged space. The delta^4 local ratio
example in the prior cycle4 report is a barrier for that specified
reassignment proposal, not a universal mixing lower bound.
