# Failure and limitation ledger

1. **No biological parameter fit.** The four-reaction calculation uses stipulated normalized rates in [0.9,1.1]; no source supplied measured rate constants for this exact network. It is a mathematical benchmark, not a biological prediction.
2. **The N33 proof is not externally certified.** The pinned Astra P package contains the full candidate manuscript `d-326696af15b5e02b`, unlike the static card alone, but this remains a source-derived candidate. Internal hostile reviews found no counterexample; those do not promote status to external proof or establish priority.
3. **OA149 is narrower.** The separate OpenAI/math OA149 proof is a weakly reversible theorem and its trapping proof uses weak reversibility. It cannot be substituted for N33's all-endotactic weighted essential-source step.
4. **Certificate acquisition is not quantitative.** N33's affine family exists after nonconstructive finite activity arguments but has no useful size, precision, scale, or waiting-time bound. N35 / N34 verify a supplied finite certificate; the general synthesis problem remains open/expensive. N50's real-closed-field enumeration is conditional and has no implemented general CAD engine or complexity bound.
5. **Strict collar is not automatic from a local certificate.** A strict inward collar implies finite-time stochastic safety after N35's volume correction; it says nothing about the basin of attraction outside Q. N34 explicitly distinguishes local safety from classwise global absorption.
6. **Finite horizon is not permanence.** N35's source manuscript gives a closed, finite-count CRN that exits its safe interval almost surely under fixed rates. N77 separately refutes classwise uniform tightness across stochastic classes inside a deterministic stoichiometric plane; it does not refute recurrence of each individual class.
7. **Stochastic class compatibility is separate.** A deterministic relative polytope is not automatically a molecule-count communicating class. Initial lattice point, conserved totals, coset, and concentration buffer must be supplied.
8. **Rates can fail the model contract.** Enzyme shutdown, irreversible washout, unmeasured feedback, or rates with no positive lower bound invalidate the all-endotactic N33 premise or the stochastic collar premise. The enzyme child identified vanishing enzyme capacity as an exact failure mode for uniform positive rate assumptions.
9. **No all-dimension literature priority claim.** The 2010 Craciun–Nazarov–Pantea result already proves a two-species time-varying endotactic permanence theorem; an arbitrary-dimensional N33 advance would be the relevant boundary, conditional on proof review and priority work.
10. **Simulation feasibility.** At the illustrative V and horizon, a crude exact thinning upper bound requires billions of candidate events across 200 replicates. The quantitative test is logically decisive but needs either optimized exact SSA, hardware/runtime budget, or a physical assay; none was performed.
