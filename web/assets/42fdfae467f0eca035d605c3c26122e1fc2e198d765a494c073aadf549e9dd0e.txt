MAIN RESULT

The baseline parameter-independent coherent-sector measurement T_N satisfies
 sup_(||A||<=M,|b|<=B) TV(T_N rho_N(A,b),p_(A,b))
       <=C_(M,B)N^-1/2.                                  (R1)
Combined with INITIAL.txt (10), the SAME quartic classical experiment and the
fully separable product reverse channel are bidirectionally O(N^-1/2)-close.
The quantum reverse has N local qubit emissions. The coherent-sector forward
measurement is mathematical; its Schur/POVM/finite-bit compiler is not supplied.

Conditional on the existing classical quartic archive law
 cD^-2/3<=e_D<=CD^-2/3, the quantum critical whole-state archive obeys
 cD^-2/3-C0 N^-1/2 <= e_(N,D) <= CD^-2/3+C0 N^-1/2.        (R2)
It is uniform in D, where D counts the TOTAL quantum/hybrid retained dimension.
Thus D=o(N^3/4) is a sufficient regime for the limiting precision exponent to
remain informative. The error-size theorem for the classical archive is a
separate internally derived import (N20); this revision does not certify its
full posterior converse or finite-bit implementation. Both all-retained-register
and no-entanglement-assistance conditions remain.

1. FORWARD CHANNEL AND SCALAR-SYMBOL COMPARISON

N,s,A,b,p have the same definitions as INITIAL.txt. The channel measures spin
sector j, performs the spin-j coherent POVM d_j |j,u><j,u| dmu(u), resets no
input-dependent classical data, and adds a uniform radial jitter in cell I_j.
The maximally mixed multiplicity is implicit in the Gibbs input; sector
multiplicity m_N(j) enters its actual measured law.

Put k=j+1/2, L=N+1, r_j=k/s and Delta=1/s. For even N,
j=0,1,...,N/2; for odd N, j=1/2,3/2,...,N/2.
Cells are
 I_min=[0,(k_min+1/2)/s),
 I_j=[(k-1/2)/s,(k+1/2)/s), j>j_min.
All widths are Delta except the first odd-N cell, of width 3Delta/2.
Their union is [0,(N+2)/(2s)). These details handle parity and the origin.

The unnormalized coherent law before jitter is
 a_N(j) R_j(u), a_N(j)=d_j m_N(j)e^(2j(j+1)/N),
 R_j(u)=<j,u|e^(H_j)|j,u>,
 H_j=Q_A(J)/s^2+b.J/s.
Let a=Tr A/3 and A0=A-aI. Its exact lower/upper coherent symbols are
 h_j^- = a j(j+1)/s^2+j(j-1/2)u^TA0u/s^2+j b.u/s,
 h_j^+ = a j(j+1)/s^2+(j+1)(j+3/2)u^TA0u/s^2+(j+1)b.u/s.

Scalar Jensen gives R_j>=e^(h_j^-).
For any eigenvector psi of H_j, d_j|<psi|j,u>|^2 dmu is a probability
measure whose expectation of h_j^+ is its eigenvalue. Jensen and summation
therefore give
 Tr e^(H_j)/d_j <=int e^(h_j^+)dmu
                <=e^(delta_j)int e^(h_j^-)dmu,
 delta_j=3(j+1/2)||A0||/s^2+B/s.
Since ||A0||<=4M/3,
 delta_*<=2M(N+1)/N^1.5+B N^-3/4=O_(M,B)(N^-1/2).
Pointwise lower domination and this integrated upper mass bound imply that
the normalized actual law differs from normalized a_N e^(h_j^-) by at most
1-e^-delta_* in TV. This is scalar Jensen; there is no erroneous
pointwise upper bound <u|e^H|u><=e^(upper symbol) assumed.

Set h(r,u)=r^2 u^TAu+r b.u. The alternate lower-symbol formula is
 h_j^-=j^2u^TAu/s^2+(j/2)(Tr A-u^TAu)/s^2+j b.u/s.
Since |Tr A-u^TAu|<=2M,
 |h_j^--h(r_j,u)|<=M(N+1/4)/N^1.5+B/(2s)=O(N^-1/2).
Normalized laws with this bounded log-density perturbation differ in TV by
O(N^-1/2). It remains to QUANTIFY the radial jitter remainder, which the
baseline supplied only qualitatively.

2. UNIFORM WEIGHTED RADIAL L1 RATE, WITH NO TAIL LOGARITHM

The exact identity
 m_N(j)=2k/L binom(L,L/2-k)
 gives
 a_N(j)=4k^2/L binom(L,L/2-k)e^(2(k^2-1/4)/N).             (R3)
Let I(z)=((1+z)log(1+z)+(1-z)log(1-z))/2.
For 0<=z<=1/2, its nonnegative series gives
 0<=I(z)-z^2/2-z^4/12<=z^6/[30(1-z^2)]<=2z^6/45.        (R4)
For k<=L/4, factorial Stirling with uniformly bounded remainders yields
 binom(L,L/2-k)=2^L sqrt(2/(pi L))(1-z^2)^-1/2
              exp[-L I(z)+O(1/L)], z=2k/L.              (R5)
The O(1/L) is uniform because both factorial arguments are >=L/4.
Insert (R5) into (R3) and set c0=8sqrt(2/pi). The exact log ratio is
 log[a_N(j)/(2^N c0 r_j^2)]
  =(3/2)log(N/L)-(1/2)log(1-z^2)
    +2k^2/N-L I(z)-1/(2N)+O(1/L).                       (R6)

The quadratic cancellation leaves 2k^2/(NL)<=2 N^-1/2 r_j^2.
The quartic term is -(4/3)(N/L)^3r_j^4.
Its difference from -4r_j^4/3 is O(N^-1 r_j^4).
The remainder in (R4) is O(k^6/L^5)=O(N^-1/2 r_j^6).
The prefactor terms in (R6) are O(N^-1+N^-1/2 r_j^2).
Consequently, throughout k<=L/4,
 |log[a_N(j)/(2^N c0 r_j^2)]+4r_j^4/3|
    <=C N^-1/2(1+r_j^2+r_j^4+r_j^6).                   (R7)
All radii are positive, including j=0, so the ratio is well defined.

Entropy I(z)>=z^2/2+z^4/12 gives independently, in this region,
 a_N(j)/2^N <=C r_j^2 e^-r_j^4/6.                       (R8)
This envelope is coarser than the limiting exponent but uniform.
Apply |e^x-e^y|<=|x-y|(e^x+e^y) to (R7). For every fixed M,B,
 sum_(k<=L/4) Delta e^(M r_j^2+B r_j)
   |a_N(j)/2^N-c0 r_j^2e^-4r_j^4/3|
       <=C_(M,B)N^-1/2.                               (R9)
Indeed (R7)-(R8) reduce the left side to N^-1/2 times a Riemann sum
of a fixed polynomial times e^-r^4/6+Mr^2+Br.
The sum is uniformly bounded by its finite integral plus a finite
total-variation bound, because Delta<=1. No growing truncation radius or
logarithmic multiplier is introduced.

For k>L/4, the crude entropy bound without a Stirling prefactor gives
 a_N(j)/2^N<=2L exp[1-L/192].
There are <=N sectors. Any fixed weighted polynomial factor is at most
poly(N)exp[O_M(sqrt(N))+O_B(N^1/4)] there. The weighted discrete exterior
mass, after multiplying by Delta, is exp(-cN).
The limiting quartic exterior beyond these radii is also exp(-cN).

On every ordinary cell I_j, |r-r_j|<=Delta/2.
The radial function
 g(r,u)=c0 r^2exp[-4r^4/3+h(r,u)]
 has a uniformly integrable radial derivative for ||A||<=M, |b|<=B.
Hence replacing its value at r_j by g(r,u) costs O_(M,B)(Delta) in
radial/angular L1: integrate the fundamental theorem of calculus over cells.
The radial-jitter density is (a_N(j)/2^N)exp[h(r_j,u)] on ordinary cells
after dividing the unnormalized sector weights by 2^N s.
For the first odd-N cell it is 2/3 of that value. Both it and g have
size O(Delta^2) there, so their integrated mismatch is O(Delta^3).
The missing limiting tail beyond the largest cell is exponentially small.
Equations (R9) and these cell bounds prove unnormalized L1 error
 O(N^-1/2)+O(N^-3/4)+O(N^-9/4)+exp(-cN)=O(N^-1/2).
Normalizers are uniformly bounded above and below on fixed compact radii.
Normalization therefore preserves the rate. Together with Section 1, this
proves (R1). It includes scalar A, arbitrary noncommuting bounded A,b, both
parities and the complete measured law.

3. FIRST RADIAL CORRECTION AND A CHECKABLE SIGN

On each fixed compact radial interval, expanding (R6) one order further gives
 a_N(j)/(2^N c0 r_j^2e^-4r_j^4/3)
    =1+N^-1/2[4r_j^2-(32/15)r_j^6]+O_R(N^-1).           (R10)
The two +2r^2 terms come separately from residual quadratic cancellation
and the binomial prefactor. The sixth entropy term has NEGATIVE sign.
This differs from the positive sixth-order term in the exponential-coordinate
heat prior of INITIAL (11). Confusing sector radius with the original heat
field radius gives an incorrect asymptotic correction.
The finite quadrature probe added below checks the zero-field radial rate.
(R10) is not required for the theorem (R1), but isolates a sign that a
proposed finite-size correction should reproduce.

4. MEMORY TRANSFER AND OPERATIONS

Let alpha_N,beta_N denote forward and reverse bounds in this revision and
INITIAL, both O_(M,B)(N^-1/2). Given a classical quartic archive of total
retained dimension D, compose T_N, that archive, and S_N. Contractivity
adds alpha_N+beta_N to its reconstruction error. Conversely, compose an
N-spin archive with S_N on input and T_N on output; the same sum converts it
to a classical quartic archive. Therefore, irrespective of the memory law,
 |e_(N,D)-e_D|<=alpha_N+beta_N                           (R11)
uniformly in D, under the same total-dimension/public-seed/no-assistance
conventions. This channel-composition statement is independently derived.

The N20 classical positive-hat upper and joint-posterior quantum converse,
if correct in their stated interface, give (R2). A nondegenerate field cube
is necessary for its positive lower constant; M=B=0 is a single known state
and has e_(N,D)=0 without retained memory. The sharp rate of this fixed
decoder at A=b=0 from INITIAL (12) is a different question.

A forward/reverse equivalence is not an efficient all-to-all simulation
compiler. T_N still needs Schur-sector/coherent measurement implementation;
S_N needs one observation to be supplied and emitted to N physical qubits.
Finite-bit input observation, local gate/coin precision, classical sampling
and all input-dependent side registers must be counted. The known-parameter
finite-bit importance sampler remains a separate construction with its own
costs. Nothing here compares actual hardware/energy costs to Davies mixing.
