After authorized peer exposure, the strongest cost refinement is the rational
quadratic gate p2(x)=1+x+x^2/2 used in a palindromic product. I independently
checked its in-cone edge and field log-concavity, the second-order product/log
bound 43 tau^3/(60 m^2), and the downstream relative-partition and
largest-eigenvalue cost schedules. No counterexample was found.

The refinement changes the large-tau single-partition arithmetic upper bound
from p^12 tau^24 epsilon^-14 (Euler) to p^12 tau^18 epsilon^-8 up to logs;
charging direct local coefficient-oracle construction adds a factor mp and
gives p^13 tau^(39/2) epsilon^(-17/2). At fixed tau this is epsilon^-8,
versus epsilon^-14, but the imported exponent remains too large to establish
practicality. The thermal partition route remains polynomial in numerical
beta*C, not generally in binary log(beta*C), because of both gate count and
relative scalar exp(-beta*C) output precision. For lambda_max, the positive
lower bound lambda_max>=C/5 permits beta=20(n+1)/(epsilon C), constant
relative counting accuracy, and m=O((n/epsilon)^(3/2)); this is a conditional
binary-input relative FPRAS. Dense-graph abstract count cost is O(n^42
 epsilon^-18) up to logs, with explicit local-oracle composition
O(n^45.5 epsilon^-19.5) up to logs.
