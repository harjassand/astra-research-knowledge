DECISIVE SYNTHESIS

1. There are three distinct bottlenecks: (i) obtain a uniform almost-commuting approximation for the whole range, (ii) turn that approximation into one EB map, and (iii) remove structural or acquisition assumptions. c05_s03/l03 chiefly address (i); s01 and l01/l02/l09/l10 illuminate (ii); s02 and l04/l05 identify representation and counterexample boundaries; l06/l08 keep the global reduction conditional. None closes all three.

2. The exact center/fiber subclass exposes why fixed-point projection is too coarse. Its EB comparator has the exact corrected center transition, not merely the fixed center algebra, and the quantum fiber contracts by 1/4 per corrected step in L2. This gives a channel-level power lift without an ergodic gap. It does not imply that an arbitrary approximately commuting CP range admits a measurable center/fiber decomposition.

3. The strongest near-term theoretical transfer is to characterize channels whose invariant/slow algebra is finite-dimensional and whose orthogonal complement admits a uniform fiber contraction, then construct a conditional expectation/power comparator. The missing load-bearing statement is a representation theorem with controlled map norm and no hidden dependence on dimension, center count, stationary weights, or a vanishing gap. Finite experiments or finite matrix checks do not provide that theorem.

4. The c05_s02 cloner separation must be retained when generalizing the instrument route: an arbitrary positive selfcompatible channel need not lie near the canonical corrected-instrument class. The present result deliberately asserts no such universality. Conversely, that separation is not a counterexample to EB lifting because cloner powers themselves contract rapidly toward an EB channel.
