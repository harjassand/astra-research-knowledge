LOCAL COMPLEMENT-INVARIANCE CHECK

An edge p2 table in four coordinates has its six nonzero entries in
complementary pairs:
  masks 3,12: a=p2(h(3alpha+gamma));
  masks 6,9: b=(p2(h(5alpha-gamma))+p2(h(alpha-gamma)))/2;
  masks 5,10: c=(p2(h(5alpha-gamma))-p2(h(alpha-gamma)))/2.
All entries are nonnegative under alpha>=|gamma|. Complement maps each
listed mask to its mate with equal weight. The two-qubit table is homogeneous
log-concave by the edge certificate in INITIAL.

For a field with c=0, write r=b and
  u=1+hb+h^2 b^2, d=hb+h^2 b^2.
The p2 matrix is [[u,d],[d,u]]. The homogeneous lift has coefficients d on
masks 3 and 12, and u/2 on masks 5,9,6,10. Every complementary pair has equal
weight. The field lift is log-concave by the determinant/Schur-complement
certificate in INITIAL (also derived independently in c06_l03/INITIAL.txt).

Every physical wire factor (x_i+x_j) has rank one on a two-element group;
the field dummy factor e_f has rank f on 2f coordinates. Both are
complement-invariant. Their product nu has degree N/2 and is invariant under
coordinatewise complement. Products preserve the gate-side invariance, so
mu is also invariant, with degree N/2. Inactive qubits contribute the exact
known factor 2 each and need no coordinates.

ACQUISITION-FREE HARD-EVENT ARGUMENT

At z=1, the soft-pair law is
  pi_t(S,T) proportional to mu(S) nu(T) t^{|S intersect T|}.
Because both measures have degree N/2 and are complement-invariant, the map
(S,T)->(U\S,U\T) preserves the law and swaps the events that a coordinate
has zero copies and two copies. Thus p_0=p_2 at every coordinate.

For a fixed coordinate u, let p_0,p_1,p_2 be its copy-count probabilities.
Use the source's uniform Poincare inequality for the exchange chain after
tilting the coordinate overlap penalty and external field so that the new
copy-count probabilities obey (q_0,q_1,q_2) proportional to
(p_0,s p_1,p_0), hence q_0=q_2<=1/2. The indicator that u is absent has
variance q_0(1-q_0). Its Dirichlet form is at most q_1 because the total
one-copy deletion rate is at most one and there is no two-to-zero jump.
The gap-one Poincare inequality gives q_0(1-q_0)<=q_1, hence
q_1/q_0>=1/2. Since q_1/q_0=s p_1/p_0 and s=sqrt(t) when p_0=p_2,
p_0<=2sqrt(t)p_1. Therefore p_0+p_2<=4sqrt(t). A union bound over N
coordinates gives
  Pr[T != U\S] <= 4N sqrt(t).
At t=1/(256N^2), the hard complementary event has probability at least
3/4. On that event the overlap penalty is one, so its unnormalized mass is
exactly the hard coefficient [x^U](g_mu g_nu). Therefore
  Z_hard = Z_soft Pr[T=U\S]
without acquiring any z field.

The p2 trace circuit satisfies the premises because each local gate table,
not just the original Euler table, has equal complementary coefficients.
This argument does not extend to arbitrary c_v: p2 field coefficients u
and v differ when c_v!=0, so mu loses complement invariance. Setting z=1
then has no derived marginal balance; the field-acquisition bottleneck
remains for the field-general class.

COUNT AND COST RECONSTRUCTION

Here is the bound from explicit subroutines, rather than substituting p2 into
Theorem 5.1's larger generic count bound.

1. The membership self-reduction has K=2N bits. Set alpha=rho/(128N),
   theta=rho/(256N), and draw
     M=ceil(2 alpha^-2 ln(8K/delta))
   conditional samples at each bit. The empirical majority branch has true
   conditional probability at least 1/2-alpha. Hoeffding plus the theta-TV
   sampling error bounds each selected probability to additive alpha with
   total failure at most delta/4. Its product estimates the complete soft
   normalizer within exp(+/-rho/16). There are
     Q=K M=O(N^3 rho^-2 log(N/delta))
   soft samples.

2. For every membership-conditioned law the measure degrees stay N/2; pinning
   only restricts supports, preserves log-concavity, and cannot increase the
   coefficient range. A supported initial pair is constructed gate-by-gate
   and group-by-group. Since the coordinate fields are all one, a valid
   lower bound for every such conditional stationary mass is
     pi_min >= t^(N/2)/(4^N R).
   The imported continuous gap is one. The lazy implementation has discrete
   gap at least t/(2N). Thus
     J=ceil[(2N/t)(H+ceil(log2(1/theta))+1)]
   steps suffices for TV at most theta from any valid initial state, where
   H is as displayed above. With t=1/(256N^2),
     J=O(N^3(H+log(N/rho))).

3. c06_s03's local cancellation is applicable to p2 because every local
   factor still has fixed degree two on four coordinates. Deleting a
   coordinate from a mu factor leaves at most three valid local insertions;
   all other factors cancel from the conditional ratio. A physical nu wire
   has two choices; the dummy quota is sampled using an exact integer-sum
   tree. One transition takes O(log N) arithmetic operations and never
   evaluates the global product mu(S). Therefore all Q conditional samples
   cost O(N^6(H+log(N/rho))rho^-2 log N log(N/delta)) arithmetic operations.

4. Independently estimate the hard-event probability to additive rho/64
   using Mc=O(rho^-2 log(1/delta)) more soft samples; since it is at least
   3/4, this is a relative O(rho) estimate. This term is dominated by Q for
   N>=2. Multiply by the soft-normalizer estimate. For bounded coin tapes,
   the global choice cap must count both phases:
   Nc=(Q+Mc)J(2 ceil(log2 N)+2),
   where Q denotes only the normalizer samples. This makes explicit the
   separate hard-event sampler's contribution; c06_s03's later Q notation
   could be read as already including both phases, so I use the conservative
   sum. Since Mc<=Q for N>=2 up to constants, this repair leaves the
   asymptotic bound unchanged. With this cap and the explicit sampling
   probabilities above, the product has relative error rho on the joint good
   event, bounded whole-run coin-tape abort probability, and bounded loops.

5. For p Hamiltonian terms, N=8mp and log R=O(mp ell). Consequently the
   specialized arithmetic bound is
     O((mp)^7 (ell+log(mp)) rho^-2 polylog(mp/rho,1/delta)).
   For one partition estimate, rho=Theta(epsilon) and, when tau>=epsilon,
     m=Theta(tau^(3/2) epsilon^(-1/2)), tau=beta D.
   Its leading dependence is therefore
     O(p^7 tau^(21/2) epsilon^(-11/2) (ell+log(mp)) polylog).
   This replaces the generic imported-count arithmetic core
   O(p^12 tau^18 epsilon^-8 ell^2 polylog) in my INITIAL; naive direct
   evaluation of each global product coefficient oracle adds another
   factor mp. The specialized local chain avoids that factor. This combined
   exponent is a conditional derived consequence, not a benchmark.

For a largest-eigenvalue reduction at constant generator and count error,
tau=Theta(n/epsilon), so the cost becomes
  O(p^7 (n/epsilon)^(21/2) polylog).
For dense p=O(n^2), this is O(n^(49/2) epsilon^(-21/2) polylog), versus
O(n^42 epsilon^-18) for the generic p2 arithmetic core. It remains a huge
polynomial and gives no useful machine-size threshold. For a fixed-tau
partition estimate the count-accuracy exponent is epsilon^(-11/2), rather
than epsilon^-8.

There is also a count-cap tuning available within the same proof. The union
bound gives p_hard>=1-4N sqrt(t). Instead of fixing p_hard>=3/4, demand
p_hard>=kappa_N=1/N and choose the rational penalty
  t=(1-kappa_N)^2/(16N^2).
Then estimating p_hard to relative error rho uses O(N^2 rho^-2 log(1/delta))
samples, below the normalizer's Q samples, while t^-1 approaches
16N^2 instead of 256N^2. This improves the dominant mixing constant by
about a factor of 16 for large N without changing exponents. This is an
internal schedule optimization and has not been implemented in a full
counter.

For scale, with n=20, p=60, beta*C=1 (so tau=2), and partition error
epsilon=0.1, the p2 gate schedule gives m=16 and N=7680. The factor N^7 alone
is about 1.58e27 arithmetic units; rho=epsilon/8 adds a factor 6400 before
the explicit logarithms, local bit-comparison multiplier, and hidden
constants. This is not a practical thermal count at that instance size.

PRECISION, BIT COST, AND PRACTICAL BOUNDARY

All local p2 entries are exact rationals. Selecting m needs only exact
integer comparisons against m>=2tau and
m^2>=43 tau^3/(15 epsilon_gen); no floating-point square root is needed.
Each local coefficient has encoding length ell=O(L+log m), and
log R_mu=O(mp ell). With z=1, dummy-tree masses are integers at most O(N),
and local transition probability numerators/denominators have O(ell+logN)
bits. A deterministic fair-bit cap r=ceil(log2(4Nc/delta)) on each rational
coin, aborting the whole output if unresolved, makes runtime/tape length
bounded; charge schoolbook rational comparisons separately. As in
c06_s03/INITIAL.txt, a conservative bit-work multiplier is
O(r(ell+logN+r)^3) per counted rational-operation unit, plus initialization
and output bit costs. Seeded PRNG diagnostics do not certify ideal random
bits.

Recovering the unshifted thermal partition function still multiplies by
exp(-beta C). A rational interval for this scalar at relative error
epsilon requires precision polynomial in numerical beta C and
log(1/epsilon); this is not polynomial in log(beta C) uniformly. The
largest-eigenvalue reduction subtracts beta C in log space and avoids
materializing this tiny scalar. Neither the p2 improvement nor symmetry
shortcuts demonstrate a practical many-body computation. No full wrapper
was executed, hidden constants remain unknown, and the generic bit-time
profile of the imported theorem has not been replaced by a measured one.
