1. EXACT BIPARTITE INTERFACE AND PARTICLE–HOLE MAP

Input: F in Q(i)^(n x n), partition of sites A,B, |A|=a, |B|=b, a+b=n,
F_AA=F_BB=0, rational t_i in [0,2], integer k if a canonical target is
requested, binary input length L, error epsilon and failure budget delta.
The partition can be acquired: scan all nonzero F_ij and test bipartiteness
of the undirected support graph by BFS. Any nonzero diagonal rejects this
class. One may reorder sites as A then B, charging its exact Fock permutation.

Write F=[[0,P],[Q,0]]. The physical state is

  |Psi> = product_i t_i^(n_ui n_di / 2)
          exp(sum_ij F_ij u_i^dagger d_j^dagger) |vac>.

At t_i=0 use 0^0=1; the filter kills double occupation. Its k-pair sector
has amplitudes, in canonical up-then-down Fock order,

  a_k(U,D)=(-1)^(k(k-1)/2) det F[U,D]
           product_(i in U intersect D) sqrt(t_i),
  |U|=|D|=k.

Apply particle–hole to BOTH spins on B. In grouped transformed mode order
(u_A,d_A,h_uB,h_dB), the pair generator becomes

  u_A^dagger P h_dB - d_A^dagger Q^T h_uB = c_A^dagger M h_B,
  M=[[0,P],[-Q^T,0]].

The transformed physical vacuum fills all 2b B-hole modes. Exponentiating
c_A^dagger M h_B replaces each occupied hole orbital by its graph orbital.
The exact transformed state is therefore the full-column-rank rank-2b
Slater matrix

  A_graph=[M; I_(2b)] in Q(i)^(2n x 2b).

No square root is introduced in its entries. Full rank follows from the
identity block, even when F, P, or Q is singular, or the colors are unequal.
If b=0 the only input in this class is F=0; handle its vacuum directly.

The local transformed squared filter tables are

  b_i(0),b_i(1),b_i(2) = (1,1,t_i) on A,
                        (t_i,1,1) on B.

Indeed, on B physical double occupation is transformed hole load zero.
For any nonzero transformed graph configuration S,

  N'_A+N'_B=2b,
  N_e=N'_A+(2b-N'_B)=2N'_A.

Thus the prescribed physical 2k-electron sector is exactly the quota
|S intersect E_A|=k, where E_A contains BOTH spin modes of the A sites.
This is why this quota can be handled directly; the single-spin Nambu map
for arbitrary F cuts across each local site block and lacks this interface.

2. EVERY SIGN IS EXPLICIT AND COMPUTABLE

In physical canonical mode order let B_modes be the 2b modes on B, sorted
ascending, and let gamma_j=c_j+c_j^dagger. The even unitary

  U_PH = Z_B product_(j in B_modes ascending) gamma_j,
  Z_B=product_(j in B_modes)(1-2 n_j)

satisfies U_PH c_j U_PH^dagger=c_j^dagger on B and leaves c_j on A.
The intermediate Majorana product negates B operators (there are 2b-1
other Majoranas) and leaves A operators (there are 2b); Z_B fixes the former.
U_PH|vac> is the filled-B state with phase +1 in canonical order.

For a physical occupation bitstring x its action is

  U_PH|x> = (-1)^h(x) |x xor 1_B>,
  h(x)=sum_(j in B_modes) sum_(l<j) x_l + sum_(j in B_modes) x_j mod 2.

Rightmost Majoranas act first, and flips of higher modes do not alter lower
parity sums. The final Z_B phase is the parity of the original occupied B
modes, since 2b is even. Let pi(S) be the inversion parity of the occupied
graph modes when their grouped order is mapped back to canonical order.
Then the physical unfiltered amplitude is exactly

  a(x)=(-1)^(h(x)+pi(S)) det A_graph[S].

For site order A then B, the only row-order inversions are an occupied d_A
mode preceding an occupied h_uB mode; pi(S)=N'_(d_A)N'_(h_uB) mod 2.
More general supplied site order has an O(n^2) inversion computation.
All corrections use bit permutations, X, Z, and CZ gates, with O(n^2)
overhead. Alternatively the compiler can compute the physical determinant
phase directly from a_k(U,D). Both are exact rational determinant interfaces,
with polynomial-bit evaluation even for very small nonzero minors.

3. DIRECT CANONICAL LOG-CONCAVE COUNTING: RANGE [0,2]

Put m=2n,r=2b. Let f(x)=det(A_graph^* diag(x) A_graph), a homogeneous
multiaffine real-stable/log-concave polynomial of degree r. Define the
complementary local-color polynomials

  g_A(x_A)=sum_(|T_A|=2a-k) product_(i in A)
             b_i(2-|T_A intersect E_i|) x^(T_A),
  g_B(x_B)=sum_(|T_B|=k) product_(i in B)
             b_i(2-|T_B intersect E_i|) x^(T_B).

The required squared sector norm is precisely

  c_k=[product_(j=1)^m x_j] f(x) g_A(x_A) g_B(x_B).

This uses TWO complementary-degree polynomials: the second is g=g_A*g_B,
of degree (2a-k)+k=2a=m-r. There is no third-filter theorem import.

The audited disjoint factorial-complement theorem says each color source
is Lorentzian/log-concave whenever b_i(j)/j! is log-concave with interval

  2 b_i(1)^2 >= b_i(0)b_i(2), equivalently t_i<=2.

  E_p Re E_L=<Psi|H|Psi>,

6. GENERAL HARD F: POSITIVE PARITY REPRESENTATION AND SUPPORT

For t=0 on every site,

  c_k=sum_(I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k) |det F[I,J]|^2.

Let f_i be row i of F transposed (no conjugation), and V=[I_n,F^T]. Group
its columns into pairs (e_i,f_i). For I of size k let V_I contain BOTH
columns of every selected pair. Cauchy–Binet, and the forced e_i rows, give

  det(V_I^* V_I)=sum_(J subset [n]\I, |J|=k)|det F[I,J]|^2,
  c_k=sum_(|I|=k)det(V_I^* V_I).

Equivalently these are the 0-or-2 exponents of
det(I+sum_i x_i(e_i e_i^*+f_i f_i^*)). This is a positive Gram/DPP lift
restricted to BOTH-OR-NONE from each pair. Its table (1,0,1) has disconnected
support and fails log-concavity. Positivity alone does not import Chen–Liu,
matching counting, or common-base counting for this parity restriction.

The support problem is linear matroid parity, because paired vectors for I
are independent iff rank F[I,[n]\I]=k. An explicit skew polynomial is

  K(z)=diag(z)F-F^T diag(z).

For ordered T of size 2k, its principal Pfaffian is

  Pf K[T]=sum_(I subset T,|I|=k)
           (-1)^(sum positions_T(I)) det F[I,T\I] product_(i in I)z_i.

Every I labels a DISTINCT monomial; there is no cross-orientation coefficient
cancellation. Thus generic rank K=2d where d is the largest feasible k, and
c_k>0 iff that generic rank is >=2k. This is the standard Lovasz/parity
Pfaffian mechanism, not a new polynomial identity.

Self-contained finite-bit randomized support/witness algorithm: specialize
independent z_i uniformly from {0,...,M-1}, M a power of 2 with
M>=k(n+1)/delta, and compute exact rational rank. A feasible k has some
nonzero degree-k Pfaffian, so Schwartz–Zippel bounds a false negative by k/M;
an infeasible k NEVER passes. Greedily attempt deleting each pair term
(setting its variable to zero), with fresh tests preserving rank>=2k. On all
tests succeeding correctly, the final active set has exactly k pairs: if
larger, a supported degree-k monomial omits a deletable active pair. Obtain J
by a column basis of F[I,complement I] and verify det exactly. If the final
size is larger than k, return UNKNOWN/retry, not a false witness. O(n) rank
tests cost O(n^4) field operations with polynomial rational bits in
n,L,log(1/delta). This is an explicit one-sided randomized decision program;
it is not a deterministic exact zero certificate. Established deterministic
linear parity algorithms can remove this randomness, but are an additional
import not implemented here. Nor did I establish every physical spin-prefix
support program for arbitrary hard F by this elementary argument.

Consequently an NP-hardness claim based only on existence of a disjoint
nonzero minor is unsupported. This says nothing about approximate partition
counting. General parity counting remains the specific acquired interface
gap identified here; no complete complexity dichotomy is proved.

7. EXACT EXPONENTIAL-VARIANCE COUNTEREXAMPLE

Take rational symmetric F block diagonal with q copies of [[0,1],[1,0]],
n=2q and t=0. The true norm is Z(s)=(1+2s)^q, so c_q=2^q.
At t=0 the cloned principal-deletion construction's sampled norm is

  X_A(s)=det(I+4s F[A,A^c]F[A,A^c]^*).

Each independent two-site block is split by A with probability 1/2. Its
factor is 1+4s if split and 1 otherwise. Therefore its top-sector coefficient
is 4^q only when ALL q blocks split (probability 2^-q), and is zero otherwise:

  E X_[q]=2^q,
  Var(X_[q])/(E X_[q])^2=2^q-1.

Even at grandcanonical s=1, E X_A=3^q but

  E X_A^2/(E X_A)^2=(13/9)^q.

Thus direct averaging of random principal characteristic/determinant values
is not a general polynomial relative-error estimator.

Likewise independent random +/-1 signs z_i obey

  c_k=E_z sum_(|T|=2k)|Pf K[T](z)|^2

by multilinear character orthogonality. For the same top sector its
estimator is product_j |z_(2j)-z_(2j+1)|^2, again zero with probability
1-2^-q and relative variance 2^q-1. Uniform circle phases reduce the relative
second moment to (3/2)^q, still exponential. These examples are easily
counted product states; they refute the estimators, NOT an FPRAS or a
physical hardness claim. Block-aware or worm methods could bypass them.

8. PRESERVED EXACT SPECIAL CLASSES / COST

Weighted permutation support provides a tractable class beyond bipartite
F: F_(i,p(i))=f_i and every other entry zero, for a permutation p. A selected
row set I has only one possible nonzero column set J=p(I), with squared
minor product_(i in I)|f_i|^2. Hence for arbitrary rational t_i>=0,

  Z(s)=sum_I s^|I| product_(i in I)|f_i|^2
                   product_(j in I intersect p(I)) t_j.

This is a binary nearest-neighbor model on disjoint permutation cycles. In
cycle order, transfer matrix T_i has entries

  T_i[a,b]=(s |f_i|^2)^b t_i^(a b), a,b in {0,1}.

Z is the product of traces of the cycle matrix products. Polynomial-valued
2x2 transfer DP gives all charge coefficients in O(n^2) rational arithmetic
operations with polynomial bits; canonical/prefix counts restrict a matrix
column to the required binary value. Physical down-prefix conditions are
row-I conditions at p^(-1)(j). Each prefix query costs O(n^2), so sequential
Born self-reduction costs O(n^3) arithmetic plus rotation/random precision.
The exact count program can be made reversible and used for coherent prefix
rotations in polynomial gate/bit cost, without Chen–Liu or a rare-event
factor. Phases are the selected f_i phases, the induced permutation sign,
and the BCS k(k-1)/2 sign. Odd permutation cycles are non-bipartite site
support and therefore extend the separate bipartite class. Attraction is
allowed at arbitrary rational magnitude with its binary range charged.

For constant selected pair rank k, exhaustive disjoint-minor enumeration
uses O(n^(2k) k^3) determinant arithmetic operations and gives exact count
and sampler. For fixed rank(F)=r, all k<=r and the total norm has
O(n^(2r)) enumeration cost; this is polynomial only when r is fixed.
Block-diagonal F with maximum site component w admits exact per-component
enumeration <=4^w, followed by polynomial charge-convolution/prefix DP.
This is polynomial for w=O(log n) with polynomial determinant bit cost,
and exponential for unbounded w. These are scope-preserving alternatives,
not general hard-state algorithms.
