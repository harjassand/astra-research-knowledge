1. EXACT LOCAL VIRTUAL-PROJECTION IDENTITY

Hypotheses: p is an integer >=1, j=p/2, D=p+1, |m|<1, n is a real unit
vector, and S is the spin-j irreducible representation with
[S_a,S_b]=i epsilon_abc S_c. Put x=|m|^2, u=n.m, v=n-u m, r=n cross m,

  rho(m)=(I+m.sigma)/2,
  q_p(m)=P_sym rho(m)^tensor p P_sym restricted to Sym^p(C^2),
  z_p(m)=Tr q_p(m)=2^(-p)F_p(x),
  R_j(m)=q_p(m)/z_p(m),
  F_p(x)=sum_(h=0)^floor(p/2) binom(p+1,2h+1)x^h,
  eta_p(x)=p+2(1-x)F_p'(x)/F_p(x).

The trace formula follows by rotating m to the z-axis and summing the p+1
symmetric weights
2^(-p)(1+|m|)^(p-k)(1-|m|)^k, k=0,...,p. In particular F_p(x)>=p+1
on [0,1], so R_j and eta_p are smooth there.

For p virtual qubits let J_n^v=1/2 sum_(a=1)^p n.sigma^(a). Direct Pauli
algebra gives

  {J_n^v,rho(m)^tensor p}=[p u+v.grad_m]rho(m)^tensor p,
  [J_n^v,rho(m)^tensor p]=i[r.grad_m]rho(m)^tensor p.

The symmetric projector commutes with every component of J^v. Projecting
therefore gives the same identities for q_p and spin-j S_n. Since
q_p=z_p R_j, the product rule adds

  v.grad log z_p = (v.2m)F_p'/F_p
                 =2u(1-x)F_p'/F_p

to the scalar coefficient, with a PLUS sign. Rotations leave z_p invariant.
Thus the exact local identities are

  {S_n,R_j(m)}=[eta_p(x)u+v.grad_m]R_j(m),
  [S_n,R_j(m)]=i[r.grad_m]R_j(m).                       (V1)

For k_N(m)=R_j(m)^tensor N and J_n=sum_i S_n^(i), this becomes

  {J_n,k_N}=[N eta_p(x)u+v.grad_m]k_N,
  [J_n,k_N]=i[r.grad_m]k_N,
  {J_n^2,k_N}/2=(B_n^2-R_n^2)k_N/4.                    (V2)

The square identity follows by writing left/right multiplication as L,R:
the anticommutator square is ((L+R)^2+(L-R)^2)/4, while the commutator is
iR_n, so (L-R)^2=-R_n^2. The angular term has a MINUS sign. This proof is
exact for every p,N and uses no approximation or physical projection.

I also verified (V1)-(V2) by an independent exact SymPy calculation for
p=1,2,3, N=1, symbolic m, and the rational unit axis n=(3/5,0,4/5).
All trace, boost, rotation, and square matrix differences simplify to zero.
The runnable transcription check is
work/cycle6/c07_l02/revisions/virtual_projection_exact_check.py. These three
fixtures do not replace the all-p proof above.

2. AUDIT OF c05_s02'S DIFFUSION MARGIN, TRACE, AND COST

For known C=A+Lambda I with ||A||<=M and C>=0, the V2 diffusion tensor is

  D_C=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T.

Writing C=Lambda I+A gives

  D_I=(1-x)(I-mm^T),
  ||D_A|| <= M||I-mm^T||^2+M||[m]_cross||^2
          <= M(1+x) <= 2M, 0<=x<=1.

On |m|<=r0<1, lambda_min(D_I)=(1-r0^2)^2. Hence

  Lambda=(2M+1)/(1-r0^2)^2  =>  D_C>=I.

Also D_C<= (I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)<=||C||I. These are correct. The first
bound is only local to the selected ball; it does not show global PSD.
For example, take m=r e_3 and C=diag(Lambda+epsilon,Lambda-epsilon,c3),
with 0<epsilon<=M. Then

  (D_C)_(e2,e2)=Lambda(1-r^2)-epsilon(1+r^2),

which is negative for r sufficiently close to 1, for every finite Lambda.
Thus a global anisotropic diffusion cannot be inferred from the virtual
projection identity. Stopping and an interior mass/exit estimate are
load-bearing.

The trace normalization also checks. For each total-spin-l block,

  Tr_l H_l/d_l = Tr(C) l(l+1)/(3 N^gamma) >=0,

where gamma=1+delta in the main theorem, or gamma=1 with the extra positive
epsilon factor in the addendum. The field trace is zero. Scalar Jensen gives
Tr_l exp(H_l)>=d_l. Since H commutes with J^2 and every heat-sector weight is
positive, Tr(exp(M_H)rho0)>=1 after division by Tr exp(t0 J^2). For positive
G,W, the normalization inequality used in both files is valid:

  T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG.

It follows from the triangle inequality and |TrG-TrW|<=||G-W||_1.
The stopped Ito residual is also charged with the right exponential factors:
||M_H k_N(m)||_1<=||H||=h_N, ||exp(t M_H)||_(1->1,Herm)<=exp(t h_N),
and w_t<=exp(h_N). Thus the displayed Duhamel term h_N exp(2h_N)P(exit)
is a valid worst-case bound, not a free weight.

For the all-temperature theorem with fixed delta>0, the stated exponents
are consistent: k=delta/8 gives Lambda_N=O(N^(delta/4)); the anisotropic
drift is O(N^(-3delta/4)); the field-only flow leaves gap g_N=Omega(N^(-delta/8));
the exit exponent is Omega(N^(1+delta/2)); and h_N=BjN+O(N^(1-3delta/4))=O(N).
The seed tail is exp[-N(a0+k log N)^2/(32 beta)]. Both terms in H15
therefore imply exp[-cN(log N)^2] for sufficiently large N. I found no
sign, trace, or exponent contradiction in H10-H15 under their stated fixed
j,beta,delta,M,B hypotheses. This is an internal audit, not external proof
certification.

For the fixed-cutoff addendum, the constants also pass independently. Put
||A||<=1, C_A=A+Lambda I, Lambda=3/(1-r0^2)^2, L=Lambda+1, and

  epsilon_* = min{ beta/(2Lambda),
    g/[2e^B L r0(U_p+1)],
    1/[L j(j+1)],
    3g^2/[16e^(2B)L(2Bj+4)] }.

For 0<epsilon<=epsilon_*, t0=(beta-epsilon Lambda)/N>=beta/(2N).
The actual diffusion is epsilon D_(C_A), so its coordinate quadratic
variation is at most epsilon L/(2N). The four constraints respectively
ensure: positive heat time; anisotropic drift at most g/(4e^B);
h_N<= (Bj+1)N; and

  kappa_exit=3g^2/[16e^(2B)epsilon L] >=2Bj+4.

The seed choice a^2/(32beta)>=Bj+2 makes the first raw error at most
2C_beta exp(-N). The Duhamel contribution is at most
6(Bj+1)N exp(-2N) <=6(Bj+1)e^(-1)exp(-N). Therefore F6 follows with
the stated C. At epsilon=0, the exact isotropic-plus-field construction
below handles the endpoint. The open-neighborhood radius is strictly
positive but can be extremely small (g and 1/L decay exponentially in B
and the chosen cutoff); the result is mathematical separability, not an
efficient preparation theorem.

3. AN EXACT SEPARABILITY CONSEQUENCE WITH NO ANISOTROPY

For any fixed spin-j local irrep, N, and t>0, the radial SU(2) heat identity
is exact on the full tensor-product representation:

  exp(t J^2)=c_t int_0^infinity r^2 sinhc(r/2) exp[-r^2/(4t)]
                     int_(S^2) exp(r u.J) du dr,
  c_t=[2 sqrt(pi)t^(3/2)exp(t/4)]^(-1).

On total spin l, the angular average is Z_l(r)/(2l+1) times identity,
Z_l(r)=sinh((2l+1)r/2)/sinh(r/2). The radial integral reduces to the
derivative of the Gaussian cosh integral and equals exp(t l(l+1)); the
identity extends over multiplicity spaces. Each exp(r u.J) is a product of
identical positive local exp(r u.S) factors. Thus the normalized Casimir
heat operator is exactly a positive mixture of identical spin-j thermal
products.

The exact isotropic-plus-uniform-field case follows for every real b:

  exp(tJ^2+b.J)=exp(b.J/2) exp(tJ^2) exp(b.J/2),

because [J^2,b.J]=0. On each heat branch the sandwich is
  (exp(b.S/2) exp(r u.S) exp(b.S/2))^tensor N.
The local factor is positive Hermitian, and in the spin-j representation is
an exp(y.S) for a real y (equivalently, use the positive Hermitian product
in the fundamental SL(2,C) representation and its spin-j representation).
Its trace is positive, so moving that trace into the mixing measure yields
an exact positive mixture of identical local spin-j thermal products. At
t=0 the identity is trivial. This is a separability theorem, not a sampling
algorithm. No anisotropic
Q_A is included in this exact case.

4. WHY VIRTUAL PROJECTION IS NOT AN AUTOMATIC TRANSFER OF ERROR BOUNDS

The virtual-qubit derivation of V1 is algebraic; it does not assert that a
physical postselection is performed. If one nevertheless tries to transfer
a qubit trace-distance guarantee by conditioning a full pN-qubit state on
P_sym at every p-qubit site, the conditioning success is

  s_N=Tr(P_sym^tensor N rho P_sym^tensor N),

and the normalized error is bounded only by the original error divided by
s_N. Indeed, for positive G,W, the conditional half-trace error is at most
||G-W||_1/Tr(PGP), after applying the contraction PG P-W projection.
There is no uniform lower bound on s_N: for maximally mixed virtual qubits
it is ((p+1)/2^p)^N. At a useful critical higher-spin test, the same
exponential issue persists. For p>1, set j=p/2 and beta_c=3/[2j(j+1)],
and take A=b=0. The target is supported in Sym^p at each site. As a full
virtual pN-qubit operator, its effective coupling is p beta_c=3/(j+1)<2,
below the qubit critical coefficient 2; its pressure per physical site is
p log 2. The spin-j critical heat trace has pressure log(p+1). Consequently

  (1/N)log s_N -> log(p+1)-p log 2 <0.

The pressure statements follow directly from the heat radial integrals:
for the virtual qubits use log cosh(r/2)<=r^2/8 and p beta_c<2; for
spin-j use the positive critical character defect, with a polynomial lower
normalizer near r=0. Therefore conditioning a virtual-qubit error bound
C exp(-c n) with n=pN is only useful as an exponential guarantee if
p c>p log2-log(p+1); the c07 qubit theorem does not supply that comparison
uniformly. c05_s02's direct R_j(m) kernel
and differential identity avoid this postselection step, but the SDE/error
proof is still required.

5. EXACT HYPOTHESES, COSTS, SOURCES, AND STATUS

The audited c05 theorem assumes a fixed irreducible local SU(2) spin j=p/2,
fixed beta>0, delta>0, M,B finite, known A,b with ||A||<=M, |b|<=B,
target exponent beta J^2/N+Q_A/N^(1+delta)+b.J, and identical local thermal
products R_j(m)^tensor N. Its fixed-neighborhood specialization assumes
||A||<=1 and perturbation epsilon Q_A/N with 0<=epsilon<=epsilon_*. The
whole-state half-trace bounds are as stated in H15 and F6. Cost accounting
includes the heat-seed tail, stopped-path exit probability, Feynman--Kac
weight exp(h_N), semigroup norm exp(h_N), and N local emissions. The law
depends on known A,b. No finite-bit heat/SDE/stopping/weighted-sampling
compiler is implemented, and no query, time, memory, acceptance, energy, or
hardware advantage is established. Virtual qubits are a proof device; a
physical virtual projection would carry the exponential success penalty
above.

6. INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE EXACT FULL-SEPARABILITY AMPLIFICATION

I additionally audited c05_s02/revisions/EXACT_FULL_SEPARABILITY.txt,
including its E3--E8 and all-N conclusion. The maximally-mixed ball step
attributed to the root is valid as written: in a Hermitian Hilbert--Schmidt
orthonormal local basis with T_0=I/sqrt(D) and traceless ||T_a||<=1,
each signed Pauli-word analogue I/D^N +/- P_s/D^N is the sign-conditioned
average of positive product states (I+z_i T_{a_i})/D. For a trace-zero
Hermitian X, its nonidentity tensor coefficients x_s are real. Rewriting
each tensor word as D^{-(N-r)/2}P_s gives coefficient a_s=D^{(N+r)/2}x_s
in X=sum_s a_s P_s/D^N. Since r<=N and there are at most D^{2N} words,
sum_s |a_s|<=D^{2N}||X||_2<=D^{2N}||X||_1. If ||X||_1<=D^{-2N},
the signed-word decomposition plus the remaining weight on I/D^N proves
I/D^N+X fully separable. This is a conservative, exponentially small
trace-norm radius, but it is sufficient for the stated amplification.

For the branch-local eigenvalue floor, if |m|=tanh(r/2)<=r0=1-g, then
r<=log((2-g)/g). Spin-j eigenvalues are e^{rh}/Z_j(r), h=-j,...,j;
Z_j(r)<=D e^{jr}, hence lambda_min(R_j(m))>=D^{-1}e^{-2jr}
>=eta=D^{-1}[g/(2-g)]^p. The floor is valid at m=0 and is no larger than
1/D. Expanding every branch tau^tensor N with tau>=eta I isolates the
common positive term eta^N I^tensor N, of trace weight (D eta)^N; all
remaining tensor terms are positive separable operators. Thus if the
target rho and this mixture sigma obey ||rho-sigma||_1<=(eta/D)^N,
then Delta=(rho-sigma)/(D eta)^N is trace zero and
||Delta||_1<=D^{-2N}; the ball result makes rho a convex combination of
separable states. This is fully separable; it does not claim a single-iid
mixture for rho.

The constants in E4 also close algebraically. With C=Lambda I+A,
||A||<=1 and Lambda=3/(1-r0^2)^2, the local diffusion matrix has
D_C>=I on |m|<=r0: D_I=(1-|m|^2)(I-mm^T), while ||D_A||<=2, and
Lambda(1-r0^2)^2=3. The chosen a is at least the positive root of
a^2-32 beta p a-32 beta R=0, so a^2/(32 beta)>=R+pa
=Hbar+W+S0. Also log((2-g)/g)<=a+B+log 3, which gives
W>=log(D/eta). The four positive terms in epsilon_exact therefore give
t0>=beta/(2N), the required drift and Feynman--Kac bounds, and
kappa1>=2 Hbar+W+1+log(24 Hbar). Since Hbar=Bj+1>=1 and
S0=log(max(8 C_beta,1)), the two E7 contributions after factoring
e^{-WN} are each at most 1/2 for every integer N>=1 (the second is
bounded using N e^{-N}<=1 and 12 Hbar exp[-N log(24 Hbar)]<=1/2).
Thus 2T<=e^{-WN}<=(eta/D)^N for all N>=1. I found no sign, trace-weight,
or endpoint error in E3--E8 or the all-N implication. The E9 eventual
corollary follows when epsilon_N=M N^{-delta} eventually lies below the
strictly positive fixed epsilon_exact; M=0 is the unperturbed case.
