## Primary literature checked 2026-10-08

1. Craciun, Nazarov, Pantea, *Persistence and permanence of mass-action and power-law dynamical systems*, arXiv:1010.3050 v2. [Primary arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.3050). The all-endotactic, bounded time-varying-rate permanence theorem used as the clean prior-art boundary is Theorem 19 and is specifically two species. Higher-dimensional all-endotactic permanence is the conjectured frontier in that paper.
2. Craciun, *Toric Differential Inclusions and a Proof of the Global Attractor Conjecture*, arXiv:1501.02860. [Primary arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.02860). Supports the established deterministic global-attractor premise for complex-balanced mass-action systems, under its precise class assumptions.
3. Anderson, Craciun, Kurtz, *Product-form stationary distributions for deficiency zero chemical reaction networks*, Bull. Math. Biol. 72 (2010), 1947–1970. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-010-9517-4), [primary arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3042). The theorem gives product-form stationary distributions on each closed irreducible class when the deterministic network has a complex-balanced equilibrium. It does not give uniform tightness across different classes.
4. Agazzi, Dembo, Eckmann, *Large deviations theory for Markov jump models of chemical reaction networks*, Ann. Appl. Probab. 28 (2018), 1821–1855. [Primary arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.02126), DOI 10.1214/17-AAP1344. Establishes a sample-path LDP and Wentzell–Freidlin asymptotics under specified hypotheses; it is prior art for chemical jump rare-event theory, not a supplied-polytope predictable-feedback bound with explicit facet constants.
5. Agazzi, Dembo, Eckmann, *On the geometry of chemical reaction networks: Lyapunov function and large deviations*. [Primary arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.07240). Geometric/lyapunov large-deviation antecedent.
6. Patel and Schreiber, *Indirect ecological feedbacks and the intermediate disturbance hypothesis*, Theor. Ecol. (2021/2022 version used by child), [primary source](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12080-021-00527-9). Chemostat child reconstructs the model and finds a sign inconsistency between prose and figure caption plus an extra candidate boundary orbit; numerical results are unvalidated RK4, not an overturning of the theorem.
7. Beard, Liang, Qian, *Energy balance for analysis of complex metabolic networks*, Biophys. J. 83 (2002), 79–86, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-3495(02)75150-3); Fleming et al., *A variational principle for complex biochemical systems*, [primary source](https://doi.org/10.1186/1742-4682-5-7). The broad proposed `flux × affinity = residual × chemical potential` bridge reduces to the identity (v^TS^T\mu=\mu^TSv\) plus established thermodynamic FBA constraints; the metabolic-thermo lane rejects it as a novelty route.
8. Craciun et al. / global attractor and AKC product-form theorems are established prior art for the detailed-balanced N77 completion. A targeted search did not establish whether the exact unbounded stationary-class family is known; historical novelty remains `UNKNOWN`.

## Evidence labels used

- `externally established`: theorem in a published primary source under its stated assumptions.
- `source-reported`: a claim in a pinned Astra card or imported report whose full linked packet was unavailable.
- `internally reconstructed candidate`: derivation checked in our work; not independent external verification.
- `conditional theorem`: follows if the stated premises/certificates are supplied.
- `finite computation`: exact rational arithmetic or floating numerical diagnostic for finitely many inputs.
- `UNKNOWN`: no sufficient source/replay/priority check.
