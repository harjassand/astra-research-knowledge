# Independent audit: finite-volume stochastic safety

7 October 2026. Audited the complete final derivations in `work/agents/pde_physics/chemical_jump_certificate.md` and `work/agents/statistical_mechanics/jump_safety.md`. No mathematical discrepancy was found in their stopped-process theorem, exact propensity correction, or worked examples. This is an internal proof audit of a supplied strict-polytope interface. It does not prove stochastic permanence or certify the universal deterministic affine construction externally.

## Exact theorem that survives

Let \(Q=\{x\in c+S:g_i(x)=a_i\cdot x+b_i\ge0\}\) be compact and strictly positive. Keep proper relative facets only. The concentration process jumps by \(\nu_r/V\), with \(\nu_r\in\mathbb Z^d\), and predictable intensity \(V A_r^V(t)\). Up to first exit from \(Q\), supply uniform bounds \(0\le A_r^V\le\bar A_r\) and positive collar drift

\[
\sum_r A_r^V(t)(a_i\cdot\nu_r)\ge\delta_i>0
\quad\text{when }0\le g_i(X_{t-})\le\eta_i.
\]

The bounds must hold for every allowed control or mode. The controls may depend on the full past and randomization but must be nonanticipating/predictable as intensities. No Markov property of the controlled process is needed.

Put \(s_{ir}=a_i\cdot\nu_r\), \(J_i=\max_r|s_{ir}|>0\), \(Q_i=\sum_r\bar A_r s_{ir}^2>0\), and \(\Lambda=\sum_r\bar A_r\). Choose \(\theta_i>0\) with

\[
\theta_iJ_i\le1,\qquad
\theta_i e^{\theta_iJ_i}Q_i/2\le\delta_i.
\]

For example, \(\theta_i=\min\{1/J_i,2\delta_i/(eQ_i)\}\) works. The statistical-mechanics choice \(\min\{1/J_i,\delta_i/(3Q_i)\}\) is more conservative and gives a strict negative collar generator.

For \(\tau_Q=\inf\{t:X_t\notin Q\}\), the exact result is

\[
\Pr(\tau_Q\le T)
\le \mathbb E\sum_i e^{-\theta_iVg_i(X_0)}
 +VT\sum_i\Lambda(e^{\theta_iJ_i}-1)e^{-\theta_iV\eta_i}
\tag{1}
\]

for every finite \(T\). Assume the initial state is lattice compatible in the fixed affine class and has gaps \(g_i(X_0)\ge d_i>0\) uniformly in \(V\). Set \(b=\min_i\theta_i d_i>0\), \(d=\min_i\theta_i\eta_i>0\), and \(C=\sum_i\Lambda(e^{\theta_iJ_i}-1)\). For any \(0<c<d\),

\[
\Pr(\tau_Q\le e^{cV})
\le m e^{-bV}+CV e^{-(d-c)V}.
\tag{2}
\]

The time exponent must be strictly below the collar exponent. Taking \(c=d\) does not give a small error. Starting a count-scale distance \(O(1/V)\) from a facet does not give an exponentially small initial error.

## Generator and stopping proof

For \(\phi_i(x)=e^{-\theta_iVg_i(x)}\), affinity of the gaps gives the exact jump multiplier \(e^{-\theta_is_{ir}}\), with no expansion in \(1/V\):

\[
\mathcal L_t\phi_i(x)
=V\phi_i(x)\sum_r A_r^V(t)(e^{-\theta_is_{ir}}-1).
\]

The Taylor inequality

\[
e^{-\theta s}-1\le-\theta s+\tfrac12\theta^2s^2e^{\theta J}
\]

makes this nonpositive in the collar. Elsewhere in \(Q\), \(\phi_i\le e^{-\theta_iV\eta_i}\), and every positive jump multiplier is at most \(e^{\theta_iJ_i}-1\). Thus the generator of \(\Phi=\sum_i\phi_i\) is bounded above by the second term's integrand in (1).

Overshoot helps the detection: at exit some gap is strictly negative, so \(\Phi(X_{\tau_Q})>1\). It causes no integrability loss. Every post-exit gap is at least \(-J_i/V\), because the pre-exit state is in \(Q\). Hence

\[
0\le\Phi(X_{t\wedge\tau_Q})\le\sum_i e^{\theta_iJ_i}.
\]

The pre-exit total hazard is at most \(V\Lambda\). For each finite \(T\), including \(T=e^{cV}\), the expected stopped reaction count is at most \(V\Lambda T\). There is no explosion before exit, and the compensated counting-process identity for this bounded stopped test is a genuine integrable martingale identity. Integrating the generator up to \(T\wedge\tau_Q\), then using nonnegativity on nonexit and \(\Phi\ge1\) on exit, proves (1). Anticipating controls would invalidate this compensator argument.

This proof also covers hitting a closed facet if safety is defined by \(g_i\le0\) rather than leaving the closed polytope. In that version \(\Phi\ge1\) at hitting.

## Exact falling-factorial error

For ordinary chemical reactions require \(y_r,y'_r\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^d\), \(\nu_r=y'_r-y_r\), and the source-matched convention

\[
A_r^V(x,k)=k_r V^{-|y_r|}\prod_j(Vx_j)_{y_{rj}},
\qquad\lambda_r^V=VA_r^V.
\]

Any binomial/factorial denominators must be absorbed into \(k_r\) consistently with the deterministic mass-action convention. Insufficient reactant counts give zero intensity and ensure count jumps remain nonnegative.

For \(0\le x_j\le U_j\), define

\[
E_y(U)=\sum_{j:y_j\ge2}\binom{y_j}{2}
 U_j^{y_j-1}\prod_{\ell\ne j}U_\ell^{y_\ell}.
\]

Then on every lattice point,

\[
0\le k_rx^{y_r}-A_r^V(x,k)\le K E_{y_r}(U)/V.
\tag{3}
\]

The insufficient-count case is covered, rather than silently discarded. One proof writes a lattice factorial product as \(\prod_{j,\ell<y_j}\max\{x_j-\ell/V,0\}\). When the count is insufficient, a zero factor makes this equal to the true falling factorial. Each clipped factor lies between zero and \(x_j\) and differs by at most \(\ell/V\). Telescoping nonnegative factors gives (3). Equivalently, in one variable with \(x=n/V\), \(n<q\) implies \(n\le q-1\le\binom q2\), which bounds the vanished factorial's discrepancy.

If the deterministic collar drift is at least \(\gamma_i>0\), put

\[
D_i=K\sum_r|s_{ir}|E_{y_r}(U).
\]

At \(V\ge\max_i2D_i/\gamma_i\), the actual stochastic drift is at least \(\gamma_i/2\). This charges the exact \(O(1/V)\) correction. The deterministic real-exponent theorem does not itself define ordinary molecular factorial propensities.

## Lattice and geometry checks

- Constraints constant on \(c+S\) have zero projected jumps and cannot be exit facets. Remove them; a strict collar drift cannot be claimed there.
- A rational representative \(c\) admits integer \(Vc\) along a denominator-compatible volume subsequence. Arbitrary coordinate rounding can change conserved totals. Actual initial counts and the communicating lattice must be supplied or checked.
- A deterministic inward condition only on the exact facet must first be extended to a positive-width collar using continuity and compactness. The stochastic proof needs that acquired width and margin.
- Every mode must satisfy the same collar inequalities. Taking a union rate box with independently disabled edges can destroy the hypothesis even if each intended graph mode has a certificate.
- An initial positive state outside the buffered core has no stochastic entrance guarantee merely because its ODE eventually enters. That is a separate finite-time approximation/entrance problem.

## Worked examples independently checked

The PDE worker's \(A\rightleftarrows B\), \(A+B=V\), rates in \([9/10,11/10]\), has \(Q=[1/10,9/10]\), collar width \(1/10\), and direct choice \(\theta=\log3\). The worst left/right collar generators are \(-1/25\); the 16 rate/endpoint corners cover the full bilinear inequalities. The direct check intentionally bypasses the generic Taylor restriction \(\theta J\le1\). From the midpoint at even \(V\),

\[
\Pr(\tau_Q\le T)\le2\,3^{-2V/5}+(22/5)VT\,3^{-V/10}.
\]

Its \(V=1000,T=3^{50}\) arithmetic is consistent.

The statistical-mechanics worker's conserved reactions \(2A\to A+B\), \(2B\to A+B\), rates in \([1,4]\), have \(Q=[1/4,3/4]\), collar width \(1/32\), and \(\theta=12/25\). At the left collar, the exact inward/outward intensity ratio is at least \(529/324\), since the falling factors improve the ratio when \(B>A\); a zero outward intensity only helps. The rational bound \(e^{12/25}\le283/175<529/324\) gives a nonpositive exact generator. With total scaled intensity at most \(5/2\),

\[
\Pr(\tau_Q\le T)\le2e^{-3V/25}+(108/35)VT e^{-3V/200}.
\]

All signs, projection factors, rational constants, and the horizon \(T=e^{3V/400}\) were checked. These two examples require no universal release theorem once their certificates are written explicitly.

## Global stochastic claims are false or unsupported

Even \(0\rightleftarrows X\) with fixed positive rates exits every finite count interval almost surely. From any state in the interval, sufficiently many consecutive births exit it with a positive probability bounded below over the finitely many states. Repeating Markov trials makes indefinite containment probability zero. Thus long finite safety and certain eventual exit coexist even for a weakly reversible network.

A stronger primary counterexample is Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen, [Example 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05328v2#S3), whose strongly endotactic network is

\[
0\to2A\to4A+B\to6A+4B\to3A.
\]

Its stochastic mass-action model has positive probability of finite-time explosion for every positive rate choice. The proof uses a repeatable three-reaction cycle increasing \(A\) by one, a positive infinite product of cycle-selection probabilities, and summable mean holding times. The complete relevant primary section was read; the HTML is retained at `sources/tier_structure_1808_05328v2.html`. Volume scaling only changes the positive rate coefficients for each fixed \(V\), so it does not remove that counterexample.

Therefore neither all-endotactic deterministic permanence nor (1) establishes global stochastic nonexplosion, positive recurrence, irreducibility, stationary-law concentration, ergodicity, indefinite survival, or a sharp quasipotential. The stopped local theorem remains compatible with these failures.

## Practical consequence and resource limits

A finite acquired certificate supplies a computable volume-versus-horizon reliability bound without simulating the full horizon. For example, after acquiring its constants, enough volume of order \(\log T+\log(1/\varepsilon)\), with certificate-dependent constants and a logarithmic prefactor correction, makes (1) at most \(\varepsilon\). This is a lower reliability exponent, not optimal large-deviation asymptotics.

The proof does not acquire actual reaction rates, a physical controller, or their validity for an exponential duration. Predictable controllers can be computationally unlimited; the theorem does not implement them. A simulation still executes \(O(VT)\) events within the intensity envelope. Mass conservation means no modeled molecular inflow is needed; it does not certify irreversible chemical implementation without energetic driving or account for unmodeled processes. Such physical inputs remain supplied assumptions.

The exponential stopping method has established primary antecedents: [Agazzi–Dembo–Eckmann](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.02126), as inspected in depth by the PDE worker. The surviving composition is an explicit finite-facet, control-uniform bound with charged propensity corrections, conditional on acquiring the strict inward certificate. No priority claim is established here.

An optional exact verification refinement is available for rational facet normals. Choose \(D_i\) so \(D_i s_{ir}\in\mathbb Z\) and \(\theta_i=D_i\log q_i\) for rational \(q_i>1\) close to one. Then the generator coefficients \(e^{-\theta_i s_{ir}}=q_i^{-D_i s_{ir}}\) are rational. Direct collar certification becomes rational polynomial inequalities and can improve the Taylor constants. Existence follows from strict drift near \(\theta=0\); no efficient search bound was proved.
