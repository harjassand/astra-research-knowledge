# Finite-bit and minimum-mass charges for a determinant-basis annealer

Assume a fixed rank-d matrix V∈Q(i)^{d×m} and positive rational line activities λ_j. The microscopic states are d-subsets A with nonzero determinant, and

  w(A) = (product_{j∈A} λ_j)|det V_A|²,   π(A)=w(A)/Z.

Let D be a common positive denominator for all real and imaginary parts of V, let R=max(1,max_{ij}|V_{ij}|), and write λ_min,λ_max for activity extrema. For nonzero det V_A, det(DV_A) is a nonzero Gaussian integer, so |det(DV_A)|²≥1. Hadamard gives |det V_A|²≤d^d R^(2d). There are at most 2^m states. Therefore

  π(A) >= [λ_min^d D^(-2d)]/[2^m λ_max^d d^d R^(2d)]

and

  log₂(1/min_A π(A))
    <= m + 2d log₂D + d log₂(λ_max/λ_min) + d log₂d + 2d log₂R.      (1)

Every term is polynomial in the encoded input length: the bit length of D is at most the sum of the input denominator bit lengths, and the activity ratio has polynomial bit length. Along a positive rational path with a uniform polynomial bound H on the right side of (1), q replicas at T+1 tiers satisfy

  Λ=log(1/min ν) <= q(T+1) H.

With q=32L, M=Tq²+q, and full-product gap at least 3/(8M), a chain started from any known state is within TV σ after

  s >= (8M/3)(Λ/2 + log(1/(2σ)))

updates. This is polynomial when L,T,H and σ^-1 are polynomially controlled (for FPRAS use σ with polynomially many bits, e.g. inverse polynomial in the sample-call count and failure budget).

A local proposal that changes a constant number of line indices needs only a constant number of d×d rational-complex determinant evaluations. Their bit lengths are polynomial by fraction-free or Gaussian elimination with exact rational arithmetic. Metropolis acceptance is a comparison of positive rational point weights; no partition coefficient is needed. For bounded worst-case randomness, replace each rational categorical decision by a fixed-k-bit threshold draw. With J total random decisions and at most A outcomes per decision, k=ceil(log₂(2AJ/δ)) makes the cumulative coupling error at most δ by a union bound. Rejection sampling with an unbounded number of draws would not establish a worst-case bit bound by itself.

For an adjacent annealing step, if every activity changes by a factor at most c and each state selects d lines, then unnormalized weights change by at most c^d, their partition functions also by at most c^d, and hence the normalized density ratio is at most c^(2d) in either direction. Taking rational c=1+1/(4d) gives c^(2d)<2. Thus a path through positive rational activities can keep adjacent laws within factor 2 using O(d log(λ_max/λ_min)) stages, up to endpoint-rounding details.

The importance-ratio product estimate has n=Θ(T/ε²) independent samples per stage and O(log(1/δ)) independent product repetitions, hence O(T² ε^-2 log(1/δ)) samples total. To implement each sample by worst-case mixing from a known state, use the displayed s with σ budgeted over all calls; multiply total sample calls by s and the polynomial per-update bit cost. An approximate base refresh changes the ideal kernel: if each refresh draw is within TV η of π_0, coupling bounds the whole trajectory discrepancy by at most (# refresh calls)η. Set η below the total simulation-error budget divided by that call count. It is not an exactly stationary update unless the refresh law is exact.

These estimates close only the algorithmic bookkeeping conditional on a polynomial uniform all-a,b local-energy theorem and a concrete kernel. They do not construct that kernel or prove its energy bound.
