Q-COLOUR OUTER LLL AND SPARSE REPAIR: SOURCE AUDIT AND DERIVATION
Date: 2026-10-07
Scope: local family160 primary TeX source, sections 02--06. Internal
mathematical derivation; no external novelty, formalization, practical
threshold, or efficient construction claim.

RESULT

The binary outer-colour dichotomy extends directly to a genuine
polychromatic Q-colouring. The geometric alternative and its witness are
colour-independent. A Q-way outer LLL supplies each colour in every test,
and rare shared-key shifts in Z/QZ repair the globally affine exception.
This is not the source's digit/vector product: every single k-progression
contains many occurrences of EVERY one of Q colours.

At the source dimension D=ceil(k^(1/10)) and M=ceil(k^(1/2)), fix
0<epsilon<1/10 and 0<rho<1/10. Uniformly for integers 2<=Q<=k^rho,
for all sufficiently large k there is a prime-power cyclic group
G=Z/NZ and a Q-colouring C such that

  N >= k^[ epsilon k / (320 Q) ],
  # { j<k : C(n_0+j d)=b } >= k^(1-epsilon)/(4(Q-1))

for EVERY n_0, EVERY nonzero d in G, and EVERY b in Z/QZ.
Real lower bounds on integer counts mean the corresponding ceiling.
One may instead advertise the floor as an integer robustness parameter.
The threshold depends on epsilon and rho, not on the individual Q in
this range. All k sampled terms are distinct, because the underlying
prime P exceeds k.

For fixed Q this is superexponential in k. For growing Q the displayed
bound has N^(1/k)>=exp[(epsilon/320)(log k)/Q], and is superexponential
when Q=o(log k). No superexponential conclusion follows from this bound
for Q comparable to or larger than log k.

The constant 320 is conservative and comes from a complete, explicit
rich-path union bound below. Replacing it by 1600 also works and agrees
with the earlier binary robust statement.

GENERAL PARAMETER REGION

The proof remains valid after replacing the dimension and return scale by

  D=ceil(k^a), M=ceil(k^b), h_0=D^2, p=k^(-epsilon),
  c_Q=epsilon/(320Q), H=k^(-2), eta=1/(1000D),
  q = least power of a prime P in (k,2k^2] with
      q >= k^(c_Q k/D), N=q^D,

and retaining the source definitions of lambda, H_x=H/lambda,
alpha=q^(-(D+1)), U, V, labels and keys.

For a fixed power envelope Q<=k^rho, the following strict inequalities
are sufficient:

  rho<a,
  epsilon<a,
  3a+rho<b<1,
  3a+epsilon+rho<1.

The last condition is essential: outer-LLL entropy small relative to M/Q
does not by itself make affine-signature entropy small relative to pk/Q.
These inequalities also imply a<1/3, 2a<b, a+rho<1, and h_0<2M<k.
They permit any rho<1/4 if epsilon is chosen sufficiently small. More
precisely, an a can be chosen if

  3 max{epsilon,rho}+epsilon+rho<1.

For fixed or sublogarithmic Q, varying a,b permits any epsilon<1/4;
the conservative source-dimension statement above needs epsilon<1/10.
Polynomial palettes give a valid polychromatic construction but the
displayed N lower bound is subexponential in k when rho>0 and Q~k^rho.

PROOF 1: THE Q-COLOUR OUTER LLL

Colour each full label beta independently and uniformly in Z/QZ. Use the
same event families and supports as source Section 04:

  (i) the cyclic rows of light positions, grouped so every row has
      distinct labels and l>=delta M-1, with delta=1/10;
  (ii) eligible recorded h-block patterns, with distinct regular labels
       and l>=h/2, for D^2<h<=2M.

A bad event says some colour has fewer than l/(2Q) occurrences. For each
target colour the count is Binomial(l,1/Q), so the ordinary multiplicative
Chernoff bound gives

  P(E) <= Q exp[-l(E)/(8Q)].

This bound can be proved directly by applying the exponential-moment
method to the independent 0/1 membership indicators; it does not require
independence between colour counts. Give E weight

  z_E=exp[-l(E)/(32Q)].

The source global word count is

  log T_glob = O[D(log k+log lambda)] = O(k^(3a) log k),

because log lambda<=D^2 log(2M). The number of local tests through one
specified label, including the choices of rational vector t, is at most
(CDh)^(6D). Their two incident-weight bounds become

  S_1 <= k T_glob exp[-(delta M-1)/(32Q)],
  S_2 <= sum_{h>D^2} (CDh)^(6D) exp[-h/(64Q)].

The first is o(1/Q) from 3a+rho<b. For the second, monotonicity of
log(CDh)/h at h>=D^2 gives

  sup_{h>D^2} 6D log(CDh)/h <= 6 log(CD^3)/D.

The condition rho<a makes this o(1/Q). Eventually each summand is at
most exp[-h/(128Q)], and hence

  S_2 <= O(Q) exp[-D^2/(128Q)] = o(1/Q).

Thus each label variable has total incident weight <=1/(500Q). Events
sharing a label are neighbours in the dependency graph, exactly as in
the source; an event using l labels has neighbouring weight <=l/(500Q).
For large k every z_E<1/2, and therefore

  z_E product_{F neighbour E}(1-z_F)
  >= exp[-(1/32+2/500)l/Q]
  >= Q exp[-l/(8Q)] >= P(E).

The middle inequality requires

  (1/8-1/32-2/500)l/Q >= log Q.

It holds uniformly because the smallest support is at least the minimum
of delta M-1 and D^2/2, both much larger than Q log Q under the displayed
parameter conditions. The finite asymmetric LLL in Section 04 supplies
one deterministic outer colouring c_* of all labels. Every tested row
or eligible pattern gives every colour at least 1/(2Q) of its positions.

No probability is attached to each occurrence separately. Repeated
full labels have one random outer colour, so the distinct-row splitting
and distinct regular labels are indispensable and retained verbatim.

PROOF 2: THE DICHOTOMY AND ITS LOAD-BEARING ELIGIBILITY CHECK

Pull back c_* by the full label map to c_0:G->Z/QZ. Every progression
either contains at least

  gamma_Q k = k/(20Q)

of every outer colour, or its entire centered y-representative sequence
is globally affine in the real vector space. Here is the exact audit.

If there are at least delta k light positions, sum the Q-balanced row
counts: each colour occurs at least delta k/(2Q)=k/(20Q). Otherwise
there is a heavy label, whose closest return gives h<=2M and the rational
path/drift decomposition of source Lemmas dichotomy:drift and
dichotomy:path. The gcd assertion and residue separation use only U
labels and circle geometry, not the outer colouring.

If h<=h_0, h divides lambda and the rational y-motion disappears. The
real step w_i=v_i/h belongs to q^(-i)Z. If a coordinate crosses a cut,
its nonzero step has |w_i|>=q^(-D)>=alpha, and the adaptive mesh widths
are <|w_i| throughout the progression. Distinct indices then have
distinct V labels in that coordinate, contradicting a heavy full label.
This gives the global affine lift. The proof accounts for closed-segment
cut hits, both directions, and the initial/final sample. The geometric
conditions used are M=o(k), 2kH<1 and alpha below the step lattice; all
hold in the generalized parameter region.

If h>h_0, use full h-blocks. Every rotating scalar coordinate has
rational denominator s_i>h_0 after dilation. The grid argument excludes
at most 4eta+1/s_i of its h positions. The union over D coordinates
excludes at most

  D(4eta+1/h_0)=4/1000+1/D<1/2.

Thus every full h-block has at least h/2 regular positions; its labels
are pairwise distinct by residue separation. The drift bounds supply
the absolute first-coordinate and rotating-coordinate eligibility
requirements.

For a full block containing ANY heavy index j_*, every stationary
coordinate uses the heavy label's own V interval I_*, whether or not
j_* is regular. The source relative-width lemma gives

  |v_i| <= (2M/k)|I_*|.

Every block point lies in the circular |I_*|-neighbourhood of I_*.
Source mesh:neighbors gives |V(y_i(n_j))|>=|I_*|/16. Since 2M/k<=1/16
eventually, this is >=|v_i|. This verifies exactly the relative-width
eligibility condition at every regular position. Merely citing a small
absolute drift would fail near the fine cut mesh; this is the decisive
check. The closures make endpoint equalities and cut crossings harmless.

A full block with a heavy index is therefore eligible and contributes
at least h/(4Q) positions of EACH colour. Every unused full block is
entirely light. With fewer than delta k light positions and fewer than h
positions in the remainder, the used blocks cover at least
k-h-delta k>=4k/5 positions. Each colour occurs at least k/(5Q), which
is stronger than gamma_Q k. This proves the claimed Q-colour dichotomy.

The entire geometric part is palette-independent. I found no specific
gap in these source steps; this is an internal adversarial reconstruction,
not an independent formal certificate for the imported full manuscript.

PROOF 3: THE SHARP SHARED-KEY MULTIPLICITY IS TWO

Retain the literal source key

  kappa(n)=((V(y_i(n)))_i, floor(||q tilde y(n)||_2^2)).

Source small-box affinity places every progression visit to one V-box
on one real affine line. Source separation gives Euclidean distance >=1
between any distinct scaled points q tilde y(n_j). Its proof is valid
for a prime POWER q: it only needs a coordinate with nonzero residue
u mod q, not a unit residue.

Let z_0 be the nearest point of this line to the origin and use unit
coordinate t along it. Squared norm equals C'+t^2. The key's half-open
band m<=C'+t^2<m+1 restricts each half-line to an interval of length
<=1. If its length equals one, its outer endpoint is EXCLUDED; otherwise
its length is strictly less than one. Hence each half-line contains at
most one of the >=1-separated sampled points. A key occurs at most two
times. The source's four-point bound arose by closing both intervals;
retaining the exact half-open band gives two. If t=0 is shared by the
two half-lines it cannot enlarge the bound.

A closed band really permits three separated collinear points -1,0,1;
the improvement relies on the half-open upper endpoint in the key.

PROOF 4: GENUINE Q-COLOUR SPARSE REPAIR

After fixing c_*, assign independent shifts E_K in Z/QZ to actual keys:

  P(E_K=0)=1-p,
  P(E_K=e)=p/(Q-1) for each e!=0,
  C(n)=c_0(n)+E_{kappa(n)} mod Q.

Each common key uses exactly ONE shift, including when occurrences have
different outer colours. Set a_0=p/(Q-1) and target threshold

  t=a_0 k/4.

For large k, p<=1/20, p<=1/16 and 1-p>=a_0. The first condition ensures
t<=gamma_Q k/2 uniformly over Q>=2.

Rich paths. Fix a progression with n_b>=gamma_Q k outer occurrences of
target b. If fewer than t final occurrences of b remain, more than half
its original b positions must change. Every changed original b position
requires E_K!=0. Multiplicity two therefore forces at least n_b/4
distinct keys appearing at original b positions to have nonzero shifts.
There are at most n_b such keys. Summing over subsets of these prescribed
keys gives

  P(final b-count<t) <= 2^(n_b) p^(n_b/4)
                       <= (2 p^(1/4))^(gamma_Q k).

This counts only keys at initial target-colour positions. A crude 2^k
factor would spoil the polynomial-Q range and must NOT be used.
There are fewer than N^2 cyclic progressions and Q targets. Thus

  log R <= log Q+2D log q+(k/(20Q))log2
           -(epsilon k/(80Q))log k
         <= -epsilon k log k/(160Q)
            +O(D log k)+O(k/Q)+log Q,

because D log q<=epsilon k log k/(320Q)+D log(2k^2).
This tends to -infinity uniformly under a+rho<1. The value 320 gives a
factor-two margin in the main logarithmic exponent.

Affine paths. Use the literal signature
((c_0(n_j),kappa(n_j)))_{j<k}. For each distinct key choose its first
occurrence, deterministically from the signature. There are s>=k/2 such
representatives. For any target b the representative's final success
probability is either 1-p or p/(Q-1), hence is at least a_0. These
indicators are independent across distinct keys. Their success count
stochastically dominates Binomial(s,a_0), and the full b-count dominates
the representative success count. Since t=a_0 k/4<=a_0 s/2, Chernoff gives

  P(final b-count<t) <= exp[-a_0 s/8] <= exp[-a_0 k/16].

The signature count is unchanged by the Q-valued outer colours: a full
label word determines all c_0 values once c_* is fixed. The source
three-coefficient quadratic arrangement count therefore gives

  T_aff <= C T_glob [k(Dq^2+2)]^3,
  log T_aff <= O(k^(3a) log k)+6 log q+O(log k)
            <= O(k^(3a) log k)
               +O(k^(1-a) log k/Q)+O(log k).

Compared with a_0 k~k^(1-epsilon)/Q, the first term is o(a_0 k) exactly
when 3a+epsilon+rho<1, and the second is o(a_0 k) when epsilon<a.
The rounding term and log Q are negligible as well. Identical literal
signatures define the same random event because neither keys nor colours
are renamed. Thus

  A <= Q T_aff exp[-p k/(16(Q-1))]=o(1).

The dichotomy covers every progression, so the total failure probability
R+A is o(1). A deterministic Q-colouring with all claimed counts exists.

PROOF 5: A SUBLOGARITHMIC PALETTE GIVES VANISHING-DENSITY ROBUST HITTERS

Fix 0<s<1 and take Q=floor((log k)^s). For sufficiently large k, Q>=2
and it satisfies the conservative power envelope. The theorem gives

  N >= exp[epsilon k (log k)^(1-s)/320],
  every colour class hits every nonzero-step k-cyclic-AP at least
  k^(1-epsilon)/(4(Q-1)) times.

All Q classes are disjoint robust hitters. The smallest class H has
|H|/N<=1/Q=o(1), while still hitting every progression at least t times.
Consequently its complement has density >=1-1/Q=1-o(1) and each such
progression has at least t points missing from that complement.
Double-counting the d=1 progressions also gives |H|/N>=t/k, if a lower
density bound is desired. This is an existential set, not an efficient
local acquisition procedure. This derivation makes no claim that the
corollary is absent from the worldwide literature.

ACTUAL OBSTRUCTION TO A DETERMINISTIC SHELL-RESIDUE SHORTCUT

The affine branch does NOT have a theorem saying squared-norm bands
cover residues modulo Q. Indeed consider the genuine cyclic progression

  n_j=j q^(D-1), 0<=j<k.

For q>8 lambda k^3, every U and V coordinate label is constant: all
coordinates below D vanish; x_D=j/q stays inside the first U interval;
y_D=lambda j/q stays inside the V interval just to the right of zero,
whose width is at least H/8 by the mesh-width proof. Thus c_0 is constant
and the global centered representatives are affine. Meanwhile

  ||q tilde y(n_j)||^2 = lambda^2 j^2

is exactly integral. Every Q<=h_0 divides lambda, so its floor is zero
modulo Q along this entire progression. The proposed colouring
c_0+floor(||q tilde y||^2) mod Q would therefore be constant here.

In the claimed parameter region, q>8lambda k^3 eventually because
log q has order k^(1-a)log k/Q, whereas log lambda=O(k^(2a)log k),
and 3a+rho<1. Also Q<=h_0 eventually. Thus this is an obstruction at
the ACTUAL source scales, not merely an abstract line or surrogate.
A deterministic shell construction would need an additional genuine
coverage argument or different colouring rule. The full-support random
shifts above resolve this exact exception.

FINITE CHECKS

check_shift_law.py enumerates finite modular-shift assignments with shared
keys and mixed outer colours using integer probability weights. It checks
that full counts dominate first-occurrence representatives and every
representative lower CDF is dominated by Binomial(s,p/(Q-1)). See
FINITE_CHECKS.json. The completed run passed 13,728 word/key interfaces,
3,711,748 exact shift assignments, and 246,310 exact lower-CDF checks.
Exhaustive four-position words cover Q=2,...,6 and p=1/8,1/20; curated
six-position words extend to Q=8 and include independent keys and paired
shared keys with differing outer colours. These checks validate the finite
interface, not the asymptotic construction.

UNRESOLVED / LIMITATIONS

No useful finite k threshold is derived; even p<=1/20 means k>=20^(1/epsilon).
No efficient method to acquire the outer LLL witness or final shift
assignment is supplied. The proof is existential. The complete imported
Lean development was not compiled or audited. Worldwide novelty and
comparison with robust-hitter literature require a separate primary-source
comparison; the local derivation alone does not establish priority.
