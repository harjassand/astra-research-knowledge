CYCLE06 COUNTERS, FAILED INFERENCES, AND OPEN GATES

1. Strong constant 4: the nonlinear optimized-noise qubit search found a legal
   primitive qubit generator with numerical J/E approximately 3.296149541.
   The sampled parameters were p=.9, r=.9968377223398316, an angle near
   .000018671810912919206, and real-plane noise coefficient a near 22262.794.
   qubit_search.json preserves the higher-precision diagnostic. It is not an
   exact symbolic disproof in this worker's baseline. The parent has separately
   supplied the information that an exact primitive canonical-EB counterexample
   exists. Its proof was not read here, so its detailed verification is outside
   this report.

2. Local-to-global: a local Hessian bound at rho=sigma cannot replace the
   nonlinear endpoint proof. The qubit theorem here uses the exact state
   dependence of sqrt(rho) and log(rho), angular concavity, and scalar endpoints.

3. Endpoint shortcut: when c tends to +-1, the minimizing noise coefficient a
   in (5) can diverge. Checking only fixed a at a diagonal state would miss the
   Schur-complement endpoint Q(+-1). The proof explicitly retains those limiting
   values and proves their exact factorization.

4. KMS versus GNS: KMS symmetry alone does not supply modular covariance. A
   modular-covariant noise decomposition would discard legal generators. The
   baseline's derivative-of-Kraus-coefficients argument avoids that assumption.
   A Hamiltonian correction can occur in the original Heisenberg L; it is
   absorbed by the correctly transformed Hermitian potential and is not dropped.

5. Noise-subclass versus all generators: proving positivity for (1) would only
   be a subclass result without the representation theorem. The coefficient-
   derivative representation is therefore part of the load-bearing proof, and
   the converse legality is used in the exact all-dimensional reduction (3).

6. Tensor shortcut: a theorem on the qubit alone does not tensor to an entangled
   reference. A joint rho can have noncommuting operator-valued Bloch components;
   scalar r,c and the logarithm/square-root formulas in (4) no longer describe
   it. The block argument proves only the classical-reference case. The full
   Hardy scalar kernel is PSD, but no general-dimensional operator lift has been
   established in this worker.

7. Finite no-counterexample search: 17,000 nonlinear state diagnostics in
   dimensions 3,4 and references 2x2,3x2 had nonnegative tested noise Gram
   matrices. This has no universal quantifier, is not proof of the exact
   quadratic gate, and is not independent external validation.

8. Primary-source conjectures: the 2013 weak/strong regularity conjecture is
   historical context. Its statement cannot establish current open status or
   the priority of a 2026 model-assisted candidate. No priority assertion is
   made by the frozen baseline.

9. Conditional broadcasting consequence: the single canonical EB channel and
   its uniform energy comparisons in POST_FREEZE_ADDENDUM.txt are supplied
   premises from the parent. The integral/cone derivation does not manufacture
   those premises or make them externally validated.

10. Remaining decisive gates: prove or refute (3) for all faithful sigma,rho and
    Hermitian B in dimensions >=3; separately prove or refute (3) for joint
    qubit-reference rho and noises B_A tensor I_R. Neither follows merely from
    numerical positivity or from the proven scalar PSD kernel.
