SPECTRAL LEARNING WITH UNSUPPLIED RANK: FINITE HANKEL CUTOFF, MARGIN, AND A NO-UNIFORMITY WITNESS
8 October 2026

STATUS
The finite-block theorem below is an INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE under an explicit reset-sampling, completeness, and singular-margin contract. It is an elementary Hoeffding-plus-singular-value perturbation result, not a new general spectral-learning theorem. The claim that exact Hankel rank can be recovered uniformly over all finite-state processes from finitely many noisy trajectories with no positive singular margin is REFUTED by the two-component Bernoulli mixture below. Full transition-operator recovery, positivity of the learned realization, adaptive basis acquisition, and advantage over data-retaining predictors remain UNKNOWN here.

1. ORACLE AND TARGET

Let Sigma be an alphabet of size a >= 2. A process P is a probability law on infinite strings X_1 X_2 ... over Sigma. A reset call of horizon L returns X_1...X_L from a fresh independent draw from P. Calls are independent. This is the only acquisition interface: no conditional history queries, hidden states, transition tables, parameter values, or matrix-response oracle are supplied. A learner may make N reset calls and observe N L symbols.

For a finite word w, set f(w)=P[X_1...X_|w|=w], with f(empty)=1. The infinite Hankel matrix is H_f(u,v)=f(uv). For finite prefix and suffix sets Pset=Sigma^{<=k} and Sset=Sigma^{<=ell}, define H in R^{p x q} by H[u,v]=f(uv), where p=sum_{i=0}^k a^i, q=sum_{j=0}^ell a^j, maximum queried word length L=k+ell, and D_L=sum_{i=0}^L a^i distinct word probabilities are relevant.

The population Hankel rank r is the minimal real linear realization dimension. The target block is COMPLETE when rank(H)=r. Completeness is not automatic for a short or poorly chosen block. A sufficient condition is a known upper bound d >= r and k,ell >= d-1: in a minimal r-dimensional representation, the spans of alpha^T A_u for |u|<=r-1 and A_v beta for |v|<=r-1 are full, since each span either grows at every step or has already become invariant. Thus the block on all words through depth d-1 has rank r. The rank r itself is not supplied.

2. NOISY HANKEL CONSTRUCTION

For each observed trajectory i and each word w with |w|<=L, let Z_i(w)=1{X^{(i)}_1...X^{(i)}_|w|=w}; these are independent Bernoulli samples across i with mean f(w). Define the empirical frequency fbar(w)=N^{-1} sum_i Z_i(w). Round each frequency to the nearest multiple of 2^{-b}, obtaining fhat(w), and set Hhat[u,v]=fhat(uv). Reusing the same rounded value for every split of a word preserves Hankel structure. With rho_b=2^{-(b+1)}, Hoeffding and a union bound over D_L words give, with probability at least 1-delta,

  max_{|w|<=L} |fhat(w)-f(w)| <= s_N + rho_b,
  s_N = sqrt(log(2 D_L/delta)/(2N)).

Consequently, ||Hhat-H||_op <= ||Hhat-H||_F <= sqrt(pq)(s_N+rho_b). Suppose the SVD routine returns singular values with certified absolute error at most zeta. Put

  R_data = sqrt(pq)(sqrt(log(2 D_L/delta)/(2N)) + 2^{-(b+1)}),
  R = R_data + zeta.

The numerical tolerance zeta is an explicit computation-precision input. The routine is required to return singular-value estimates within zeta and a rank-r truncation within zeta in operator norm of the exact rank-r truncation of Hhat. Counts can be stored exactly in ceil(log2(N+1)) bits; rounded frequencies use b fractional bits. No infinite-precision numeric oracle is being assumed.

3. CONDITIONAL UNKNOWN-RANK THEOREM

Theorem. Fix k, ell, N, b, zeta, delta as above. Assume the sampled block H is complete, has rank r>=1, and its least nonzero singular value lambda=sigma_r(H) satisfies lambda >= 4R. Form Hhat, compute the certified singular values, and return

  rhat = #{j : estimated_sigma_j(Hhat) > 2R}.

Then, with probability at least 1-delta, rhat=r. The rank r is not an algorithm input. The rank-r truncated SVD of Hhat has operator-norm error at most 2R from H when the truncation residual is certified to zeta; the same data directly estimate every queried f(w) to at most s_N+rho_b.

Proof. On the Hoeffding event, ||Hhat-H||_op<=R_data. Adding the certified singular-value computation error shows |estimated_sigma_j(Hhat)-sigma_j(H)|<=R_data+zeta=R. For j<=r, sigma_j(H)>=lambda>=4R, hence the estimate is at least 3R and is above 2R (if R=0, the positive singular value is still strictly above zero). For j>r, sigma_j(H)=0, hence the estimate is at most R and below 2R (again immediate if R=0). This proves exact rank recovery. For the approximation, the rank-r SVD residual of Hhat is its (r+1)-st singular value, at most R_data; adding the data perturbation again and the certified truncation residual gives at most 2R_data+zeta<=2R. The direct word estimate is the same uniform Hoeffding event. QED.

Here and below, `log` is the natural logarithm. For a target lower margin gamma with 0<gamma<=lambda, a transparent sufficient setting is

  N >= 72 p q gamma^{-2} log(2 D_L/delta),
  b >= ceil(log2(6 sqrt(pq)/gamma)),
  zeta <= gamma/12.

These give R<=gamma/4, so the unsupplied-rank cutoff succeeds. The algorithm itself needs N,b,zeta,delta and the declared horizon/basis, but not gamma or r. Gamma is a condition for the theorem, not a teacher-provided value. The bound is conservative: it uses uniform Hoeffding plus a Frobenius-to-operator bound, and is not claimed sample-optimal.

The result certifies the rank of the chosen finite block and, under completeness, the process's minimal linear dimension. It does not say a fitted rank-r weighted automaton is stochastic/positive, recover transition matrices stably, or predict past L. No stability bound is derived here for realization extraction from shifted Hankel blocks or for its pseudoinverse; those are separate obligations and cannot be inferred from rank recovery alone.

4. WHY THE MARGIN CANNOT BE REMOVED

Let P0 be iid Bernoulli(1/4). For 0<alpha<1, let Palpha first choose a latent theta once per reset episode, with theta=1/4 with probability 1-alpha and theta=3/4 with probability alpha, then emit iid Bernoulli(theta) forever. Palpha is a two-state hidden process (the selected theta is held forever); P0 has Hankel rank one. For Palpha, the complete two-by-two block indexed by {empty,1} is

  H_alpha = [[1, mu], [mu, nu]],
  mu = (1-alpha)/4 + 3alpha/4 = 1/4+alpha/2,
  nu = (1-alpha)/16 + 9alpha/16 = 1/16+alpha/2.

Its determinant is nu-mu^2 = alpha(1-alpha)/4>0, so its rank is two, while the corresponding P0 block has rank one. H_alpha is positive semidefinite and sigma_2(H_alpha)=det(H_alpha)/sigma_1(H_alpha)<=alpha/4 because sigma_1(H_alpha)>=1. Thus the smallest nonzero singular value vanishes with alpha.

One reset trajectory of any length has law (1-alpha)P0+alpha P_{3/4}, so its total variation distance from P0 is at most alpha, regardless of length. For N independent reset trajectories, the product-law distance is at most 1-(1-alpha)^N<=N alpha. Le Cam's two-point bound therefore gives minimax error at least (1-N alpha)/2 for deciding rank one versus rank two. Taking alpha=1/(10N) leaves error at least 0.45, for every N and every within-episode horizon. Hence no finite-sample rank method has a uniform success guarantee over all positive finite-state processes without a quantitative margin or another separation promise. This is an information lower bound, not an SVD-computation limitation.

There is a separate horizon/basis limit: if the chosen Pset,Sset do not span the reachable and observable directions, the block rank can be smaller than full Hankel rank. Increasing the singular threshold cannot repair missing rows or columns. With no supplied rank ceiling, a finite horizon certifies only the finite block; it cannot certify that all longer histories add no direction.

5. COMPETENT MODEL-SELECTION CONTROL

Use a data-matched nested-rank holdout baseline. Split the same N trajectories into train and validation halves. From the training sample, form the same complete-word Hankel block and compute truncated SVD candidates Hhat_k for every k=1,...,min(p,q). Map each word to one fixed prefix/suffix split and clip the candidate prediction to [0,1]. On validation trajectories, minimize the mean squared error averaged over all D_L word indicators; break ties toward smaller k. Conditional on the training data, each trajectory's averaged loss lies in [0,1], so a union-bound Hoeffding calculation gives simultaneous validation-risk error at most sqrt(log(2K/delta)/(2N_val)) over K=min(p,q) candidates. The selected candidate is within twice this quantity of the best candidate's population finite-horizon prediction risk.

This is a capable prediction-oriented comparator: it can choose a smaller rank when a weak direction contributes negligible finite-horizon error, while the singular cutoff asks the stricter exact-block-rank question. It needs the same reset trajectories and word basis, but reserves validation data and computes K SVDs instead of one; it needs no true rank or singular margin for its finite-class risk guarantee. It still cannot infer an arbitrarily low-mass component uniformly, and its selected predictive rank need not equal algebraic rank. A retained-sample lookup predictor is also a strong no-compression control for finite-horizon word estimates: store the trajectory/prefix counts and answer from empirical frequencies, avoiding SVD and rank assumptions. Compare predictive risk and charged memory, not just emitted state count.

6. COST LEDGER

Information cost: N independent reset episodes and NL observed symbols. One trajectory contributes an indicator for every prefix word, so the procedure does not issue N samples separately for each of the pq entries. The sufficient N bound nevertheless scales as pq/lambda^2 times a logarithm because the simple proof controls the full matrix in Frobenius norm. The q-mixture lower bound shows that removing all margin dependence is impossible, even when each episode can be arbitrarily long. This does not establish that the pq factor is necessary.

Representation and arithmetic: p rows, q columns, pq matrix entries, D_L distinct probability estimates, and O(pq) dense storage. Dense SVD arithmetic is O(pq min(p,q)); when k=ell=d-1 this is cubic in p, and p=(a^d-1)/(a-1), so the naive complete basis is exponential in the supplied ceiling d. Sample parsing/counting takes O(NL) symbol operations with incremental prefix hashing; filling the dense block is O(pq). Exact count storage costs O(D_L log N) bits, besides matrix storage. Numeric SVD work must return certified singular-value intervals of width at most zeta; this leaf charges that precision contract but does not claim a library-specific bit-runtime bound. These computational costs are distinct from NL acquired symbols and from N statistical episodes.

Baseline cost: the holdout version uses the same total N trajectories and NL symbols, roughly half for fitting and half for validation; it incurs K dense truncated SVDs and D_L-per-trajectory validation loss evaluation. The retained-sample control stores O(NL) symbols or O(D_L log N) aggregate counts, depending on the desired query interface. A claimed compression benefit must subtract the acquisition set, validation split, arithmetic, and output precision.
