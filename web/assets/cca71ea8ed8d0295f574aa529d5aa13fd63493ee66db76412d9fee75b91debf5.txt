AN INVERSE-DIAGONAL-ENERGY CRITERION FOR A WHOLE-GIBBS TRACE FLOOR
8 October 2026. INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; external validity is open.

1. LEGAL INTERFACE AND EXPLICIT CONCLUSION

Let h_L>0 act on S_L sites with a real orthonormal eigenbasis phi_L,k.
Let t_L>0 be a public critical time scale, a_L,k=t_L epsilon_L,k, and
K_L=dGamma(h_L). Let W_L=g sum_x n_x(n_x-1)/2 with FIXED g>0. The free
and interacting states are the full Fock Gibbs states at beta=tau t_L,
chemical parameter zero. Hard-core compression P0 K_L P0, embedded by
zero in the same full Fock space, is an alternative below. No decoder
energy condition is used.

Assume the following uniform public constants or certified bounds.

(i) There is one unique ground mode g with 0<a_L,g<=C_g, all other
    a_L,k-a_L,g>=Delta>0, and
    sum_{k!=g}exp[-(a_L,k-a_L,g)]<=R_0<infinity.
    The free grand partition at dimensionless inverse time 1 is at
    most Z_0, and the free canonical M=2 quarter-time partition is at
    most Z_2. Vacuum gives a lower grand partition of 1.

(ii) There is a normalized symmetric two-particle vector psi_L with
     psi_L(x,x)=0 EXACTLY, and <psi_L,t_L K_L psi_L><=C. The same psi_L
     is therefore a legal hard-core trial and annihilates W_L.

(iii) There is a fixed finite set J of mode labels, containing g, with
      |J|<=D, such that
        sup_L sum_{k notin J}1/(2a_L,k)<=1/(16 C_E),
      where C_E=2C+2+4 Z_2/e^2. Absent finite-L labels have zero terms.
      One can instead assume uniform inverse-energy tails tend to zero
      and select such a set; the acquisition of a certified tail and
      its resulting D is then an explicit input cost.

(iv) S_L/t_L tends to zero, or more precisely the lattice size in the
     conclusion satisfies S_L C_E/(g t_L)<=1/16.

Set eps=1/(16D), and choose the FIXED finite critical inverse parameter

 tau>=1+Delta^(-1)log(max(1,8 R_0/eps)).                (R.1)

Then for every lattice size satisfying (iv),

 ||rho_W,L-rho_0,L||_1 >= eps exp(-2 tau C_g)/(2 Z_0)>0. (R.2)

The same floor holds for the hard-core full state without needing (iv).
The constants depend on the public model/certificates and g, not on L.
An explicit model may permit a much smaller tau, as the quartic square
packet does at tau=1. This criterion is sufficient for separation; failure
of an assumption gives no universality theorem or impossibility result.

2. PROOF OF THE FULL THERMAL CLAIM

Work first in the normalized M=2 state. Let B=t_L(K_L+W_L)|_{M=2} or its
hard compression. The exact trial implies Z_B(tau)>=exp(-tau C).
Minmax implies Z_B(tau/4)<=Z_2 for tau>=1. Split its mean B energy at
Q=2C+2. The low part is <=Q. For E>Q,
 E exp(-tau E)<=(4/(e tau))exp(-tau Q/2)exp(-tau E/4).
After dividing by Z_B(tau), the high part is <=4 Z_2/e^2. Thus

 E_sigma B<=C_E, E_sigma(t_L K_L)<=C_E,
 E_sigma Q_D<=C_E/(g t_L) for finite onsite repulsion. (R.3)

Purify sigma. Let c_k be its ancillary vector coefficient on the physical
normalized pair |k,k>, and let u=||c_g||^2. Real orthogonality gives
 sum_x (purified wavefunction at x,x)=sum_k c_k exactly: distinct mode
pairs contribute zero and diagonal pairs contribute one. Cauchy-Schwarz
over sites and (R.3) give

 ||sum_k c_k||<=sqrt(S_L C_E/(g t_L))<=1/4.             (R.4)

For hard core the left side is zero. The high-mode sum is bounded by
 sqrt(sum_{k notin J}1/(2a_L,k))
       sqrt(sum_{k notin J}2a_L,k ||c_k||^2)<=1/4.
The remaining non-ground diagonal vectors have sum norm at most
sqrt(D-1)sqrt(1-u). Thus

 sqrt(u)<=sqrt(D-1)sqrt(1-u)+1/2.

If u>1-eps, its right side is <3/4 and its left side is >3/4, a
contradiction. Therefore u<=1-eps. This is a consequence of actual Gibbs
energy and coincidence estimates, not an imposed finite-energy restriction.

For the free canonical block define
 r_L(tau)=sum_{k!=g}exp[-tau(a_L,k-a_L,g)].
Assumption (i) gives r_L(tau)<=R_0 exp[-(tau-1)Delta]<=eps/8 under (R.1).
Its ground-pair probability is at least 1/(1+r+r^2)>=1-2r, hence at
least 1-eps/2. The normalized canonical trace difference is >=eps, since
the ground-pair effect probabilities differ by at least eps/2 and the
normalized-state difference is traceless, so an effect gap is bounded
by half the full trace norm.

The full free M=2 probability is at least exp(-2tau C_g)/Z_0. For any
two full states with normalized blocks rho_P,sigma_P and probabilities
p,q, the triangle/compression inequality gives
 p||rho_P-sigma_P||_1<=||p rho_P-q sigma_P||_1+|p-q|
                    <=2||rho-sigma||_1.
Use the FREE state's p. This proves (R.2) on the entire common Hilbert
space. Partitions, number sectors, and the interacting change in sector
weights are included; no replacement by a conditioned model is made.

3. COMPLETE APPLICATION TO POWERS OF THE DIRICHLET LAPLACIAN

On Lambda_L={1,...,L-1}^d let h_D have eigenvalues
 omega_L,k=4 sum_{j=1}^d(1-cos(pi k_j/L)), k in {1,...,L-1}^d.
Fix a positive exponent s and kappa>0, and set
 h_L=kappa h_D^s, t_L=L^(2s), S_L=(L-1)^d.
For EVERY d<2s, the preceding theorem gives a strictly positive full
thermal trace floor for fixed onsite strength g, at a sufficiently large
but fixed tau; the hard-state floor holds too. All hypotheses are proved
next. This is a dispersion-dependent boundary, not a claim that any
positive kinetic matrix of this dimension has the same behavior.

The product sine basis is real and orthonormal. For L>=4,

 kappa 8^s |k|^(2s)<=a_L,k
                      <=kappa(2pi^2)^s |k|^(2s).       (R.5)

Ground label g=(1,...,1) is unique and C_g=kappa(2pi^2 d)^s. Let
 c_0=kappa(8d)^s,
 X_q=sum_{k in N^d}exp[-q kappa 8^s |k|^(2s)].
These sums converge for every q>0 and have explicit shell-tail bounds.
One may take
 Z_2=X_(1/4)^2,
 Z_0=exp[X_1/(1-exp(-c_0))], R_0=exp(C_g) X_1.
Grand fugacity theta<c_0 is stable as well, giving uniformly finite free
number moments; positive number-conserving onsite repulsion and hard
compression have the same upper fugacity partition by block minmax.

For the first excited quadratic mode (2,1,...,1),
 L^2(omega_a-omega_g)
 =8L^2 sin(3pi/(2L))sin(pi/(2L))>=24.
Here each sine argument lies in [0,pi/2] for L>=4. Every other mode has
at least that increase. Since 8d<=L^2 omega_g<=2pi^2d, a uniform positive
ground gap is

 Delta=kappa[(8d+24)^s-(8d)^s] for s>=1,
 Delta=kappa[(2pi^2d+24)^s-(2pi^2d)^s] for 0<s<1.       (R.6)

Monotonicity in the base variable of (u+24)^s-u^s proves the appropriate
endpoint bound; the gap estimate is not assumed from a continuum limit.

The exact collision-free trial from QUARTIC_COUNTEREXAMPLE generalizes
in the first coordinate without change. Let a=(2,1,...,1), b=(3,1,...,1).
The normalized vector
 psi=sqrt(2/5)|g,g>-sqrt(2/5)|a,a>+sqrt(1/5)|g,b>
is proportional to [cos(pi x_1/L)-cos(pi y_1/L)]^2 phi_g(x)phi_g(y),
so it vanishes at x=y exactly. It has

 <psi,t_L K_L psi>=a_g+(4/5)a_a+(1/5)a_b
 <=C=kappa(2pi^2)^s[d^s+(4/5)(d+3)^s+(1/5)(d+8)^s].   (R.7)

Finally inverse diagonal energies have uniform summable tails:
 sum_{max k>N}1/(2a_L,k)
 <=[2kappa 8^s]^(-1)sum_{r>N}[r^d-(r-1)^d]/r^(2s).
The right side tends to zero when d<2s, explicitly bounded by a constant
times N^(d-2s). This supplies a finite cut J={max k<=N}, D=N^d, chosen
so its tail is <=1/(16C_E). Also
 S_L/t_L<=L^(d-2s)->0,
so (iv) holds once L^(2s-d)>=16 C_E/g. This proves every hypothesis and
the full trace floor (R.2). Its constant can be very small; choosing N
and tau is a charged mathematical acquisition, not a practical prediction.

The proof also applies at fixed positive chemical tilt in an admitted
stable range with adjusted constants, but no such extension is needed
or claimed in this record. The proven application fixes chemical zero.

4. DOMAIN, COMPLEXITY, PRIOR ART AND THE CRITICAL-DIMENSION GATE

At finite L, each positive onsite form has a dense closed positive sum
with K_L. Number-block minmax and a finite free fugacity partition imply
existence of full Gibbs states. Exact hard compression is defined directly
on its allowed physical Hilbert space, then embedded by zero. No equality
with an unproved infinite-strength limit is used.

For fixed positive INTEGER s and fixed d, h_D^s has range s and a bounded
number of nonzero entries per row, with rational entries if kappa is
rational. Fixed-strength onsite W is explicit diagonal. No hidden dense
row, exponentially precise-in-L coefficient, or eigenvector oracle is
required. For noninteger s, fractional spectral powers can be dense;
the theorem is a mathematical spectral-input statement and does not
grant sparse row access or efficient physical preparation for those powers.
The Gibbs input states are supplied in all cases. No efficient interacting
preparation, unrestricted EB error floor, or memory lower bound is claimed.

The inverse-energy sum diverges at d=2s, and the residual S_L/t_L does
not vanish there. Thus THIS criterion says nothing at the critical
dimension. The quadratic s=1,d=2 case has a separate proved logarithmic
filter universality theorem in PROOF.txt. For higher powers h_D^s,
nonstoquasticity and higher-order filter costs leave the critical case
unresolved; neither a blanket universality heuristic nor its negation is
justified by (R.2).

Qualitative Lifshitz contact-interaction relevance below dimension four
is already analyzed by Wu--Zhou--Wu (2017), arXiv:1509.03953. The object
proved here is a uniform finite-box full-Gibbs trace separation with exact
trial, tail and sector accounting. Its exact priority and external
validity remain UNKNOWN; no generic-physics discovery is asserted.
