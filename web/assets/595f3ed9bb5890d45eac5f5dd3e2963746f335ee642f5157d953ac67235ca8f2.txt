CYCLE 2 RESULT — STOCHASTIC WEAK REVERSIBILITY, CLOCKS, AND TIERS
Date: 2026-10-08
Programme: reaction_dynamics

OUTCOME

No general recurrence theorem, transient/null-recurrent example, or explosive
example was established for weakly reversible (WR) stochastic mass-action
networks with at least three species and higher molecularity. The general
classwise recurrence question remains UNKNOWN. In particular, no step in this
cycle transfers deterministic permanence to jump-chain or physical-time
recurrence.

The strongest positive result is an exact finite recognizer for one published
sufficient condition: the stochastic top tier is contained in the deterministic
top tier along every proper tier sequence. I reduce the universal sequence
quantifier to finitely many rational linear-feasibility tests and run an exact
Fraction-arithmetic implementation on a four-species, one-linkage, order-five,
non-complex-balanced WR network. The accepted fixture defeats two common
one-step drifts along a reachable unbounded ray, while its exact three-step
embedded entropy drift tends to minus infinity. Its classwise positive
recurrence follows from the published tier theorem, conditional on the internal
reduction/checker being correct. This is an effectivity result for a sufficient
condition, not a new general recurrence theorem and not a complete recurrence
decision procedure.

PUBLISHED THEOREM AND QUANTIFIERS

Let C be a finite complex set in N_0^d and R a finite weakly reversible
reaction graph with one linkage class. For each reaction y -> y', fix a
positive mass-action rate constant kappa_(y->y'). The CTMC has propensities

  lambda_(y->y')(x) = kappa_(y->y') product_i (x_i)_(y_i) 1{x_i >= y_i},
  (n)_(j) = n(n-1)...(n-j+1).

For a state sequence x_n, its deterministic tiers order (x_n vee 1)^y and
its stochastic tiers order lambda_y(x_n)=product_i (x_i)_(y_i)1{x_i>=y_i}.
Anderson, Cappelletti, and Kim, Theorem 6.1 (arXiv:1904.08967v2, 2020),
prove: if for every proper tier sequence of the CTMC the highest stochastic
tier T^{S,1} is contained in the highest deterministic tier T^{D,1}, then the
CTMC is positive recurrent from the specified initial state. The graph must
be weakly reversible and have one linkage class. Their proof selects a
favorable reaction word of length at most the number of reactions and applies
a Foster argument to a fixed-step embedded chain. The word probabilities are
rate-weighted. Their Theorem 3.1 converts embedded-chain positive recurrence
to physical-time nonexplosion and positive recurrence when the total rate has
a positive lower bound; the paper proves that lower bound on every
nonabsorbing class of a WR mass-action system. Absorbing singleton classes are
positive recurrent directly.

EXACT FINITE REDUCTION

The checker certifies a stronger, network-wide version of the theorem's
hypothesis by testing all lattice sequences, not only states reachable in one
particular class. For any candidate proper sequence, take a subsequence so
each coordinate is either fixed at an integer b_i or diverges to infinity.
Let I be the nonempty set of diverging coordinates. The enabled-source set is

  C_(I,b) = { y in C : y_i <= b_i for every fixed coordinate i not in I }.

For y in this enabled set, falling-factorial propensities have the same
asymptotic monomial order as product_(i in I) x_(n,i)^(y_i). The deterministic
monomials have that same order on I; fixed coordinates contribute only finite
positive factors. Because C is finite, there are only finitely many enabled
sets: for each bounded coordinate it suffices to use thresholds in
{0} union {y_i : y in C}.

For each stratum with at least one enabled source, a tier-inclusion failure
exists exactly when there are an enabled source y that can be stochastic-top
and a disabled source z outside C_(I,b) that is strictly above y in
deterministic order. A strictly higher enabled source would also be
stochastically higher, because both orders agree among enabled sources. The
asymptotic log-count
vector supplies positive weights on I. If multiple stochastic-top sources
tie only up to bounded log-ratios, projecting the log-count vector onto the
orthogonal complement of their exponent differences changes it by a bounded
amount, makes those ties exact, and preserves every diverging strict gap.
Approximation then gives rational weights. Conversely, a rational positive
weight vector w realizes the required tier comparison along
x_(n,i)=floor(n^(w_i)) for i in I, with the fixed coordinates held at b.

Thus each possible failure reduces to rational feasibility of

  sum_i w_i = 1,
  w_i >= delta,
  w dot (y-u) >= 0 for every u in C_(I,b),
  w dot (z-y) >= delta,
  0 <= delta <= 1,

with delta > 0. The common margin represents strict positivity of all weights
and the strict deterministic gap. A bounded rational polytope has positive
maximum delta iff an extreme point has delta > 0. Exact rational vertex
enumeration therefore decides this sufficient tier premise and can return an
explicit rational asymptotic witness when it fails. Failure to obtain the
certificate does not imply nonrecurrence; the witness may also be unreachable
from a particular class.

For m complexes and d species, the number of strata is at most
sum_(nonempty I subset [d]) (m+1)^(d-|I|), and there are at most m^2 candidate
(y,z) pairs per stratum. Each LP has |I|+1 variables and O(m) constraints.
Exact rational LP algorithms terminate, but the included implementation uses
vertex enumeration whose combinatorial cost grows rapidly with dimension and
constraint count. Bit complexity depends on exponent encodings and rational
intermediate heights. The implementation ignores positive rate values because
the tier premise does; rate values still affect the theorem's finite path
probabilities. It supplies no complexity bound useful for large networks,
return-time bound, stationary tail, or mixing rate.

EXACT FIXTURE AND RATE-WEIGHTED PATH MECHANISM

Species are A,B,C,D and the single directed cycle is

  A+3D -> A+B+3D -> A+C+3D -> C+3D -> 2B+3D -> A+3D,

with rate constants (1,1,2,1,1). The graph is one strongly connected linkage
class. It has five complexes, four species, stoichiometric rank 3, deficiency
1, and maximum source molecularity 5. It is not complex balanced: equality of
the directed cycle fluxes would force c_B=1 and c_C=1/2, while equality of
the last two fluxes forces c_C=c_B^2=1.

The exact checker examines 58 enabled-source strata and 220 rational LPs; it
accepts. On the D=3 class, every reaction propensity includes the fixed factor
(3)_3=6. The ray x_n=(n,1,0,3) is reachable and unbounded: reaction word
r1,r5,r1 maps x_n to x_(n+1). At x_n, exactly r1 and r2 are enabled, each at
rate 6n. Consequently, for W=A+B+C,

  L W(x_n)=6n,

and for V(x)=sum_i [x_i(log x_i-1)+1], with 0 log 0=0,

  L V(x_n)=6n(2 log 2 - 1)>0.

The exact three-jump calculation in the evidence script gives

  E_(x_n)[A(X_3)-n]
    = -(348 n^5 + 274 n^4 - 405 n^3 - 241 n^2 + 70 n + 24)
      / (2(2n+3)(3n-2)(3n+1)(3n+2)(4n+1)),
  limit = -29/36.

All three-step endpoints have bounded B,C coordinates and A=n+j for j in a
finite set. Since V_A(n+j)-V_A(n)=j log n+O(1),

  E_(x_n)[V(X_3)-V(x_n)] = -(29/36) log n + O(1) -> -infinity.

The particular word r1,r2,r3 has probability tending to 1/6, but the displayed
three-step expectation is summed over all endpoints and is verified
symbolically. The global recurrence conclusion is not inferred from this ray:
it follows from the all-sequence tier condition plus Theorem 6.1.

OTHER CYCLE-2 MECHANISMS AND NEGATIVE FINDINGS

The exact high-order rank-three family at
work/programmes/reaction_dynamics/cycle2/leaf16_wr_higher_order_clock/REPORT.txt
has mixed/zero top-tier total-mass changes and an explicit non-complex-balanced
positive-recurrent family of closed classes, with physical stationary laws
computed by a phase-rate/Poisson argument. It establishes classwise recurrence
for those classes only and exhibits stationary mass escaping as the class
index grows. It is a useful clock and class-quantifier control, not a
counterexample or general criterion.

The direct WR closure attempts do not yield an explosive example. In the
audited closure of a known non-WR escape word, a newly added reverse channel
has order n^3 while the desired birth has order n^2, so the probability of
following the escape word is zero; separate fixed-period escape constructions
also fail. These are obstructions to those constructions only. They do not
rule out nonperiodic WR escape, explosion, transience, or null recurrence.
Detailed records are linked in dispatch.json.

BASELINES, SOURCE STATUS, AND NOVELTY BOUNDARY

Complex-balanced stochastic mass-action systems have product-form
stationary laws and a nonexplosion theorem, so they provide a stronger
all-order baseline on that subclass; this fixture deliberately lies outside
complex balance. Anderson-Kim (2018), Corollary 4.3, gives a distinct general
one-step tier/Foster sufficient condition for arbitrary rate constants. The
present fixture's contribution is the exact compilation of the ACK 2020
path-tier condition and the ray calculation; neither improves the theorem's
coverage. The binary structural theorem in ACK 2020 is narrower.

Author/project pages and a 2026 conference abstract report a broader result
for all-rate two-species WR systems, while the project page lists the
manuscript as “In preparation.” I record that as SOURCE-REPORTED and do not
use it as a premise. The 2020 ACK paper calls arbitrary WR positive
recurrence a conjecture. The sources checked report no resolution for
arbitrary d>=3. This cycle makes no novelty or priority claim; independent
agent agreement and exact fixture checks are not external validation.
