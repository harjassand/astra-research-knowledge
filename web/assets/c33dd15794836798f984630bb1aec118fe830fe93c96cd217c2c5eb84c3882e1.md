# Independent counterattack: variance dual for the all-Q quantum gate

## Verdict and exact target

No legal counterexample to the exceptional factor-two claim was found in this pass. The universal claim remains **UNKNOWN**. The useful output is an independently derived certificate filter that makes the first viable witness rank and the required channel energy explicit.

The broad target tested here is: for every finite \(d\), every unital, trace-preserving, completely positive, Hilbert--Schmidt-self-adjoint channel \(\Phi:M_d\to M_d\) with a supplied symmetric two-output broadcaster \(\mathcal B\), there is one finite canonical EB map \(\Psi\) such that

\[
  \Psi\succeq 2\Phi-I
\]

on the **entire real Hermitian operator space**. Equivalently, \(I-\Psi\preceq2(I-\Phi)\). The cycle-8 subproblem additionally assumes that \(\Phi\succeq0\) as an HS operator. A counterexample under that extra assumption would refute both statements; this pass does not establish whether the extra assumption is needed.

Use \(\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/d\) and \(\langle A,B\rangle_\tau=\tau(AB)\). The normalized symmetric-extension state \(\Omega_{RAB}\) must satisfy

\[
\Omega\succeq0,\quad \operatorname{Tr}\Omega=1,\quad
\operatorname{Tr}_{AB}\Omega=I_R/d,\quad
\operatorname{Tr}_{RB}\Omega=\operatorname{Tr}_{RA}\Omega=I/d,
\]

output-swap invariance, and equal \(RA,RB\) Choi marginals for the same \(\Phi\). Thus reference trace preservation, both output unital marginals, and channel compatibility all remain explicit. A legal refutation must also defeat the full-domain canonical support, not just an \(H_0\)-only or fixed-frame surrogate.

## Independently derived global variance-dual filter

Take any positive dual on the traceless space and choose its spectral decomposition,

\[
Q=\sum_i w_i\,|A_i\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A_i|,
\qquad w_i\ge0,\quad \tau(A_i^2)=1,
\]

and set

\[
C_Q=\sum_iw_iA_i^2,\qquad
\beta(Q)=\inf_{\|\psi\|=1}\sum_iw_i\left(
\langle\psi|A_i^2|\psi\rangle-\langle\psi|A_i|\psi\rangle^2
\right).
\tag{1}
\]

For every pure projector \(P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\), its canonical support integrand obeys

\[
 f_Q(P)=\sum_iw_i(\operatorname{Tr}(PA_i))^2
 \le \operatorname{Tr}(PC_Q)-\beta(Q).
\]

Therefore \(Z_Q=C_Q-\beta(Q)I\) is a globally feasible moment-dual certificate, with

\[
 \tau(Z_Q)=\sum_iw_i-\beta(Q)
 =\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q-\beta(Q),\qquad
 S_d(Q)\le\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q-\beta(Q).
\tag{2}
\]

The barycenter \(\int P\,d\mu=I/d\) is used in the last step; the scalar shift costs exactly \(\beta(Q)\) under \(\tau\). In particular, if one can lower-bound \(\beta(Q)\) exactly and find a legal broadcaster satisfying

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q(I-\Phi))<\beta(Q)/2,
\tag{3}
\]

then the target fails: (3) implies
\(2\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Phi)-\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q>
\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q-\beta(Q)\ge S_d(Q)\).
This is a **sufficient** counterexample test. Failure of (3) says only that this particular dual \(Z_Q\) does not witness a deficit.

Equation (1) is the minimum weighted quantum variance over pure states. It isolates a direct counterattack: find a family with a positive incompatibility/variance gap while the compatible channel retains more than half that gap as HS Dirichlet energy. This pass found no such legal pair. The route avoids assuming that one pure seed can be twirled into a POVM and does not replace the marginal-feasible extension by an unconstrained star eigenvector.

## Exact controls that survive the attack

For rank-one \(Q=w|A\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A|\), \(\beta(Q)=0\), since \(A\) has an eigenvector. Its canonical support is exactly \(w\), attained by the spectral PVM of \(A\). HS contraction gives \(\langle A,\Phi(A)\rangle_\tau\le1\), so the support inequality holds for this dual without using compatibility. It cannot be a counterexample.

For any dual of rank at most two, let \(P_a\) and \(R_b\) be the spectral PVMs of its two Hermitian modes. The effects \(M_{ab}=\mathcal B^*(P_a\otimes R_b)\) form one input POVM. Each marginal is the pulled-back spectral measurement on one broadcaster output; Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjointness identifies those effects with \(\Phi(P_a)\) and \(\Phi(R_b)\). Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz under outcome refinement gives the canonical support inequality for both modes simultaneously. This is the depth-one instance of the existing C4 construction. Thus a successful support witness must have rank at least three.

For a full traceless HS-orthonormal Hermitian basis \(\{A_i\}_{i=1}^{d^2-1}\), completeness gives

\[
\sum_i A_i^2=(d^2-1)I,\qquad
\sum_i(\operatorname{Tr}(PA_i))^2=d-1
\quad\text{for every pure }P.
\]

Hence \(\beta(I_{H_0})=d(d-1)\) and \(S_d(I_{H_0})=d-1\) exactly; \(Z=(d-1)I\) is a matching global dual. The universal symmetric \(1\to2\) cloner has traceless eigenvalue \(\lambda=(d+2)/(2(d+1))\), so

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}\bigl(I_{H_0}(I-\Phi)\bigr)
=(d^2-1)(1-\lambda)=d(d-1)/2=\beta/2.
\]

It saturates rather than violates the filter: \(2\lambda-1=1/(d+1)\), and the Haar rank-one POVM has canonical transfer \(I/(d+1)\) on \(H_0\). At \(d=3\), the values are \(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q=8\), \(\beta=6\), \(S_3(Q)=2\), \(\lambda=5/8\), and Dirichlet trace \(3=\beta/2\). This checks the normalization and identifies an exact equality boundary.

Another exact support family is \(r\) pairwise anticommuting Hermitian involutions \(A_i\) with \(A_i^2=I\). For \(Q=\sum_i|A_i\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A_i|\), every pure state satisfies \(\sum_i\langle A_i\rangle^2\le1\): for each real unit vector \(x\), \(A(x)=\sum_ix_iA_i\) obeys \(A(x)^2=I\), hence \(|\langle A(x)\rangle|\le1\). An eigenbasis PVM of any one \(A_j\) attains one, so \(S_d(Q)=1\) exactly and \(\beta(Q)=r-1\). Any violation in this family would need

\[
\sum_i\langle A_i,\Phi(A_i)\rangle_\tau>(r+1)/2.
\]

The existing all-generator Clifford-vector audit covers this structured family, including anisotropic positive weights; this observation supplies a compact support-side filter, not a new result about arbitrary \(Q\).

## Prior attack audit and failure boundaries

- The C4 iterated-broadcaster construction is valid on the full operator domain but costs \(2\lceil\log_2(d^2-1)\rceil\); its variable-depth extension trades that rank loss for an energy-weighted spectral/commuting-cover entropy. Neither yields the constant two.
- The C5 fixed-algebra expectation cannot cover slow classical modes; a conditional expectation is also too narrow for the qubit cloner, whose equality comparator is non-idempotent.
- The proposed universal instrument/Petz CP-square factorization is false even for a qubit channel that is already canonical EB; this refutes that proof route only.
- An unconstrained star top eigenvalue or pure-seed maximum drops, respectively, the broadcaster marginals or POVM barycenter. The exact C8 \(d=5\) one-mode-per-line example defeats the MUB-line-PVM spectral shortcut, but its global support is safe after a 25-outcome orbit POVM certificate.
- The C8 cubic-phase qutrit is a legal positive channel after squaring and has one 12-outcome comparator passing the full operator order. It is a control, not a universal qutrit result.
- The prior two-qubit Pauli and \(d=4\) searches use finite random-state support lower bounds; their near-zero gaps cannot certify a global moment-dual deficit.

These failures imply that the remaining falsifier is still an exact tuple \((d,\Phi,\mathcal B,Q,Z)\): a positive dual \(Q\), a PSD/trace-preserving/output-symmetric extension with the stated marginals, and a global all-pure-state certificate \(\operatorname{Tr}(ZP)\ge f_Q(P)\) whose \(\tau(Z)\) is strictly below \(2\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Phi)-\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q\). The variance certificate (2) is one possible \(Z\), not guaranteed to be sharp.

It is a consistency check of the displayed formulas only. It does not solve an SDP, run an eigenvalue search, or test a new channel. Runtime is under one second on the present Python 3 / SymPy 1.14 environment; no data acquisition or hardware is assumed.
