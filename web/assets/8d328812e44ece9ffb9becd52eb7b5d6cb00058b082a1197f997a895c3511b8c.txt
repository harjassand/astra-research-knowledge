LEAF 05 — UNEQUAL-DIFFUSION CONTINUUM BRIDGE
Status: PROVED for one explicit, genuinely coupled, two-species complex-balanced/endotactic system; general N54 unequal-diffusion permanence remains UNKNOWN.
Date: 2026-10-08

RESULT

There is a mesh-uniform positive absorber for the following coupled reaction–diffusion system for every pair of unequal positive diffusivities. The proof is a cooperative parabolic comparison, with an exact entropy identity as a separate check. It uses no finite-graph permanence theorem, no stochastic recurrence statement, no N33 affine-family premise, and no heat-kernel constant.

Network and kinetic interface
-----------------------------

There are two species A and B and six actual mass-action arrows, each with rate constant λ>0:

    A ⇌ 2A,       B ⇌ 2B,       A ⇌ B.

Concentrations are nondimensionalized against the reference value 1, and time is scaled so that the six displayed rate constants all equal λ.

The ODE reaction vector is

    f_A(A,B)=λ(B−A²),       f_B(A,B)=λ(A−B²).

The stoichiometric vectors include (1,0) and (0,1), so S=R² and the only positive ODE stoichiometric class is the whole positive quadrant. At c*=(1,1), every arrow flux is λ. At complexes A and B, total incoming and outgoing fluxes are both 2λ; at 2A and 2B, both are λ. Thus c* is complex-balanced. In fact each reverse pair is individually balanced at c*. The diagram is weakly reversible. It is endotactic directly under the essential-source definition: every non-tangent arrow has its reverse; if an arrow from a maximal-height source pointed strictly up, its reverse would be an essential arrow from a strictly higher source, a contradiction.

Continuum theorem
-----------------

Let Ω be a bounded connected C² domain. Let D_A,D_B>0 with D_A≠D_B. Consider the no-flux problem

    ∂_t A = D_A ΔA + λ(B−A²),      ∂_ν A=0,
    ∂_t B = D_B ΔB + λ(A−B²),      ∂_ν B=0,
    A(·,0)=A₀, B(·,0)=B₀,

where A₀,B₀∈C(Ω̄) and

    0<m₀ := min(inf_Ω A₀, inf_Ω B₀),
    M₀ := max(sup_Ω A₀, sup_Ω B₀)<∞.

Then the unique global mild solution (classical for t>0) satisfies, for every t≥0, x∈Ω̄, and i∈{A,B},

    Q_λ(m₀,t) ≤ u_i(x,t) ≤ Q_λ(M₀,t),
    Q_λ(q,t) := q e^{λt}/(1+q(e^{λt}−1)).                 (1)

In particular K=[1/2,2]² is a forward-invariant pointwise concentration range. For all t≥T(m₀,M₀),

    1/2 ≤ A(x,t),B(x,t) ≤ 2 for every x∈Ω̄,

where the explicit entry time is

    T(m₀,M₀)=max{T₋(m₀),T₊(M₀)},
    T₋(m)=0                         if m≥1/2,
         =λ⁻¹ log(m⁻¹−1)            if 0<m<1/2,
    T₊(M)=0                         if M≤2,
         =λ⁻¹ log(2(1−M⁻¹))         if M>2.               (2)

The absorber K and the formula (2) do not depend on Ω, D_A, D_B, or any mesh. If a family of initial profiles has a supplied common box 0<a≤A₀,B₀≤b<∞, then the one entry bound T(a,b) works for the entire family. Without a supplied positive lower bound on initial data, no uniform entry time over all positive profiles is asserted.

Proof of (1), including the global-existence step
-------------------------------------------------

The reaction map is cooperative on the nonnegative quadrant because ∂f_A/∂B=λ and ∂f_B/∂A=λ. The scalar logistic solution q'=λq(1−q), q(0)=q₀, is explicit and is also a spatially constant solution of the two-component PDE along the diagonal A=B=q: indeed f_A(q,q)=f_B(q,q)=λ(q−q²), and both Neumann Laplacians vanish. The constant profiles q(t)≡Q_λ(m₀,t) and q(t)≡Q_λ(M₀,t) start respectively below and above the initial profile. The cooperative parabolic comparison principle therefore gives (1) on every interval of classical existence.

Since 0<Q_λ(M₀,t)≤max{M₀,1}, the solution cannot blow up in finite time. Quasipositivity and the lower comparison give strict positivity for every finite time. The standard semilinear continuation criterion then gives a global classical solution. For 0<m<1/2, solving Q_λ(m,t)=1/2 gives the first expression in (2); for M>2, solving Q_λ(M,t)=2 gives the second. Logistic monotonicity gives the bounds for every later time. The same comparison proves forward invariance when the initial profile already lies in K.

This argument uses separate positive scalar diffusivities, with no equality requirement. It only uses that diffusion preserves constants and satisfies the scalar maximum/comparison principle; the constants in (1)–(2) come entirely from the reaction network.

Mesh-uniform statement
----------------------

For each finite mesh h, let L_{A,h}, L_{B,h} act on its nodes V_h by

    (L_{i,h}z)_j = Σ_{k≠j} w^{(i,h)}_{jk}(z_k−z_j),
    w^{(i,h)}_{jk}≥0.

Thus L_{i,h}1=0 and the off-diagonal entries are nonnegative. For the stated no-flux/conservative interpretation, also assume a positive quadrature vector π_{i,h} with π_{i,h}ᵀL_{i,h}=0 (for an ordinary undirected graph, π can be the all-ones vector). The pointwise comparison proof only needs the sign and row-sum conditions.

The species may have different generators and arbitrary positive mesh-dependent multipliers D_{i,h}; no connectedness, spectral-gap, or uniform rate bound on the mesh edges is needed. Consider

    Ȧ_j = D_{A,h}(L_{A,h}A)_j + λ(B_j−A_j²),
    Ḃ_j = D_{B,h}(L_{B,h}B)_j + λ(A_j−B_j²).

Because L_{i,h}1=0, the same constant vectors Q_λ(m₀,t)1 and Q_λ(M₀,t)1 are respectively a subsolution and supersolution. The full 2|V_h|-dimensional ODE is cooperative: the off-diagonal entries of each diffusion generator and the local cross-species derivatives are nonnegative. Finite-dimensional comparison proves the very same bound (1) and entry time (2), with m₀ and M₀ the minimum and maximum over both species and all nodes. Thus every mesh in every such family has the same positive absorber K and the same entry bound from common initial bounds a,b. This is a direct uniform estimate, not a limit argument from fixed-mesh permanence.

Exact entropy/dissipation check
------------------------------

For positive classical solutions define

    H(A,B)=∫_Ω [A log A−A+1 + B log B−B+1] dx.

Integration by parts under the no-flux boundary condition gives the exact identity

    dH/dt = −∫_Ω [
        D_A |∇A|²/A + D_B |∇B|²/B
        + λ{A(A−1)log A + B(B−1)log B
             + (A−B)log(A/B)}
    ] dx ≤ 0.                                               (3)

Each reaction term in brackets is nonnegative: s(s−1)log s≥0 for s>0, and (A−B)log(A/B)≥0. Equation (3) is also obtained by summing the three reversible-pair flux-affinity products. It agrees with the complex-balance entropy structure, but (3) alone is not used to infer pointwise permanence; the explicit pointwise bound is (1). The network has the boundary equilibrium (0,0), so the general no-boundary-equilibrium hypothesis in Desvillettes–Fellner–Tang’s 2017 exponential PDE theorem is not met here.

Finite-dimensional comparison and exact rates
----------------------------------------------

Setting D_A=D_B=0 gives the homogeneous ODE, and the same logistic sandwich proves its permanence directly. Setting D_A=D_B=D>0 gives the equal-diffusion PDE comparator with identical K and T. The unequal-diffusion result has the same information interface: six arrows and their exact rate λ, positive initial lower/upper bounds (or the supplied profile itself), and the diffusion operators. It needs no acquired N33 affine coefficients, neutral heat-mixing time, domain heat-kernel bound, or mesh spectral gap. The input difference from the equal-diffusion comparator is only the supplied pair D_A,D_B; the conclusion does not depend on their numerical values.

Cost and acquisition accounting
-------------------------------

* Supplied model information: the six arrows, λ>0, positive diagonal diffusivities, Ω and Neumann boundary condition; for mesh h, the two conservative generators; and either certified bounds a,b on the initial profiles or the profile data from which m₀,M₀ are to be obtained.
* Exact network check: six reaction vectors/rates, four complexes, four incoming/outgoing flux equalities, and a rank-two check from the A→2A and B→2B arrows. This takes O(E d) arithmetic operations for E=6,d=2; with these fixed coefficients the check is constant size.
* Initial-bound acquisition: for a supplied finite mesh array, a single scan costs O(|V_h|) comparisons and O(1) working memory. For a continuum profile, certified m₀,M₀ are assumed supplied; no cost-free oracle for extrema is claimed.
* Certificate calculation: two scalar logarithms and a few comparisons for (2), O(1). No PDE solve or numerical optimization is charged or needed for the proof.
* Mesh verification: checking the stated conservative-generator interface costs O(nnz(L_{A,h})+nnz(L_{B,h})) from an explicit sparse matrix. No inverse power of mesh width, mesh-dependent lower bound, spectral gap, heat-kernel constant, or graph-size factor enters K or T.

Equal-information comparator
----------------------------

The matched comparator uses the same six reactions, the same λ, the same initial lower/upper information, and the same no-flux geometry. Replacing D_A≠D_B by D_A=D_B leaves (1)–(3) and K=[1/2,2]² unchanged. For the finite graph comparison, each species may retain its supplied generator, so the matched data need only satisfy the same generator conditions. N54’s common-scalar-diffusion argument is a broader source-derived result but depends on its finite-dimensional N33 affine mechanism and neutral-mode heat mixing. This leaf’s bound is narrower in network scope and stronger in mesh uniformity, with an explicit entry formula and no N33 dependency.

Falsifier and limits
--------------------

Decisive falsifier for this claim: an explicit positive classical solution, or a finite mesh solution under the stated generator assumptions, whose concentration exits [Q_λ(m₀,t),Q_λ(M₀,t)] at any x,j,t. Such an example would contradict either the diagonal logistic solution or cooperative comparison. A proof audit should first check quasipositivity, the off-diagonal signs, the row-sum-zero convention, and the comparison ordering; those are the load-bearing premises.

This establishes one useful unequal-diffusion continuum bridge, not a theorem for arbitrary complex-balanced, weakly reversible, or endotactic networks. It does not prove a mesh-uniform absorber for a general network whose reaction vector has no invariant cooperative diagonal comparison curve. It does not transfer fixed-mesh N33 absorbers to a PDE limit. It makes no stochastic recurrence, nonexplosion, invariant-law, or all-time survival claim. Those questions remain separate.
