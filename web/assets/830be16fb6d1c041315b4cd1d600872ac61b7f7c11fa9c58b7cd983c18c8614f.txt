1. Exact deterministic incidence certificate

Let q be an odd prime. Points are x=(t,y,z,w,v) in F_q^5 and labels are [a,b,c,d,e]. Incidence equations are b-y=at, c-z=bt, d-w=ay, e-v=az. Each label is an actual affine line
  (t,b-at,c-bt,d-ab+a^2t,e-ac+abt).
Match a point with its incident label having a=t. This is a bijection; the matched point of a label is obtained by substituting t=a. Every label is punctured only at its own matched point.

This graph is exactly D(5,q) under the sign changes point->(t,-y,-z,w,v), label->(a,b,c,-d,-e). The primary Gupta–Taranchuk paper states connectedness for odd q and Theorem 1.4 bounds the second adjacency eigenvalue by 2 sqrt(q). Since the graph is bipartite, its incidence matrix A has only one top singular direction (the constant vector), and ||A|_{1-perp}||<=2 sqrt(q).

For ANY prescribed mark set B of size N in the point side, retain its matched labels. Let P be the matching permutation, b=1_B, m=q^5 and rho=N/m. The degree after own-mark puncturing is exactly
  d=(AP-I)b,   mean(d)=mu=(q-1)rho.
Both P and A preserve the constant direction, so
  ||d-mu 1||_2 <= (2 sqrt(q)+1) sqrt(N(1-rho)).
Consequently, for E={x:d_x<4r^2} and mu>4r^2,
  |E|/m <= (2 sqrt(q)+1)^2 rho(1-rho)/(mu-4r^2)^2.       (S)
This is deterministic and independent of the structure of B. No sampling, affine-map search, or complete degree ledger is needed to certify the aggregate exceptional-set bound. A point's exceptional status remains a finite explicit degree predicate, not a cheap local lookup.

The old N27 grid now permits U=identity, with q=2^61-1 and r=1,200,000. Exact rational arithmetic, using isqrt(q)+1 as an upper bound for sqrt(q), gives |E|/m<=3.082280e-13 and Delta/m>=0.9999996301. This resolves the old unchecked-affine-map premise without trusting any random instance.

2. Denser simplex nodes and smaller deterministic input

Take s=(q-3)/2 and
  B={a=(a_1,...,a_5): nonnegative integer coordinates, |a|<=s},
viewed in F_q^5. Then N=binom(s+5,5). For each a in B define the polynomial
  f_a(X)=binom(s-sum_j X_j, s-|a|) product_j binom(X_j,a_j),
where binom(Y,k)=Y(Y-1)...(Y-k+1)/k!. Every denominator is invertible since k<=s<q. Total degree is s.

At a grid point b, introduce six barycentric counts b_0=s-|b|, b_j (j>=1), and similarly a_0=s-|a|, a_j. Both totals are s. If b!=a, some b_j<a_j, so one falling-factorial binomial vanishes; at b=a all six factors are one. Hence f_a(b)=delta_ab. No modular wrap occurs at these nodes, since all six integer counts lie in [0,s]. Thus H=f f^T has rank<=1, H(a)=E_aa and degree 2s=q-3<q-1. Summing along any full affine line gives zero in characteristic q; puncturing the label's own mark gives -E_aa exactly.

The mark count attains dim(polynomials in five variables of total degree<=s). Thus these are maximal-density nodes for this degree-bounded Lagrange mechanism: rho~1/3840, versus the old grid rho~1/100000. This is a dimension statement about the interpolation interface, not optimality among all possible obstruction mechanisms.

3. Where the group assertion comes from

The graph has girth at least ten. Every regular incidence set is split into two Cartesian blocks of dimensions >=r; the cyclic triangle wiring is the explicit source252 wiring. Give triangles angles (21pi/40,9pi/40,9pi/40). At O, link cycles have >=4 edges, angular length>=2.1pi. The V/W links immerse in the punctured incidence graph, so cycles have >=10 edges, length>=2.25pi. A path between distinct same-label directions projects to an internally reduced closed walk and contains a cycle even if its seam reverses. Thus all four turns of every rectangle have angle>pi. Standard CAT(-1) link and Cartan-Hadamard facts give a proper cocompact CAT(-1) universal cover, torsion-free hyperbolic group G, and nonidentity rectangle words.

The approximate-permutation proof itself is independent of the extraordinary global release252 theorem. For arbitrary D-point assigned permutations, form r-by-r block arrays Y_i. Real trace moments give
  rank_R Y_i>=r^2 D/[2r-1+(r-1)^2 lambda].
Bounded row norms in an integer minor permit fixed-prime rank reduction. Every failed triangle costs <=delta D rank over F_q because permutation differences vanish on good columns. The two Cartesian sums have inner dimension D. Tensoring these matrices with the rank-one interpolation certificate and summing gives (R) (or the original half-rank inequality). The scalar numerical cancellation of D is outside F_q; q dividing D is harmless. Triangle and rectangle defects <=1/(100rq) contradict the certified positive gap. A finite-set sofic approximation supplies them with eta<1/(800rq); inverse/prefix bookkeeping uses at most factor eight. No global stability theorem is assumed.

4. Higher-level consequence: property (T) is available through a quotient

Let F consist of identity and every nontrivial rectangle in G. Let H2 be any torsion-free non-elementary hyperbolic property-(T) group (for example a torsion-free cocompact quaternionic-hyperbolic lattice). Apply the standard finite-set-preserving quotient theorem to G*H2, with M=F. It yields a torsion-free non-elementary hyperbolic Q, injective on F, and a surjection H2->Q. Thus Q has property (T). If Q were sofic, composing G->Q with sofic approximation would keep EVERY rectangle nonidentity, contradicting the finite obstruction. Therefore Q is nonsofic.

The primary Arzhantseva–Steenbock 2023 paper states exactly this quotient theorem as Theorem 2.1, citing Olshanskii 1993 Theorem 2, and demonstrates its property-(T) use in Corollary 2.2. This corollary bypasses the inherited 'core property T needs new expansion' obstacle: it establishes property (T) in a different hyperbolic quotient, not in the original CAT(-1) core. The two-dimensional classifying-complex and CAT(-1) descriptions are not preserved by this argument. No actual quotient relators or effective parameter bound have been acquired.
