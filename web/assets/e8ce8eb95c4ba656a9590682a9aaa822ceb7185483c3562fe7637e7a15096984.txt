A. Complete tracial control, directly proved
--------------------------------------------
Let sigma_A=I_A/dim(A), and let L_A be any KMS-symmetric QMS generator. The
representation in the baseline has s_A scalar, so V_B=B^2 and

  H_B(X)=B^2 X/2+X B^2/2-BXB=[B,[B,X]]/2.

It follows that the strong endpoint J>=4E holds even after an arbitrary finite
untouched quantum reference, with any product stationary reference state.
This is a control, not a novelty claim. To prove it, diagonalize the faithful
joint density matrix rho with eigenvalues lambda_i. The reference log stationary
term contributes zero, and the tracial A term is scalar. For one noise
C=B_A tensor I_R,

  J_C=(1/2)sum_(i,j)|C_ij|^2
                  (lambda_i-lambda_j)(log lambda_i-log lambda_j),
  E_C=(1/2)sum_(i,j)|C_ij|^2(sqrt(lambda_i)-sqrt(lambda_j))^2.

For positive x,y,

  (x-y)(log x-log y)>=4(sqrt(x)-sqrt(y))^2.

For example, put u=sqrt(x/y). If u>=1, log u>=2(u-1)/(u+1), as its difference
has derivative (u-1)^2/[u(u+1)^2]>=0 and vanishes at one; u<=1 follows by
symmetry. Multiplying proves the displayed scalar inequality. Each summand
then gives J_C>=4E_C, and summation over the represented noises gives the
complete tracial control. This proof needs neither primitivity nor a reference
dimension bound. It does not prove the nontracial complete weak endpoint.

B. Parent-supplied qubit consequence; premises still require separate review
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The parent independently supplied this consequence after the qubit proof had
been frozen. Its external-channel premises are not proved or audited in this
worker's baseline. This section records the exact conditional interface and
checks the integral/cone algebra.

Let Phi be a sigma-stationary KMS qubit channel in the Schrodinger picture,
and P_t=exp[t(Phi-I)]. Write E_Phi(rho) for the baseline quadratic energy of
the Heisenberg generator I-Phi^*. Assume a single CPTP EB channel Psi is supplied
and satisfies, for every faithful qubit rho,

  E_Psi(rho)<=4E_Phi(rho),
  e_Psi(rho)^2<=c_0 E_Psi(rho),
  c_0=1+1/sqrt(2),
  e_Psi(rho)=T(Psi(rho),rho),
  T(rho,eta)=(1/2)||rho-eta||_1.

The assertion that a canonical single Psi with these premises exists for the
stated self-compatible-channel class is SUPPLIED BY PARENT and is not newly
established here. Under exactly these premises and the global qubit baseline,

  integral_0^infinity e_Psi(P_t(rho))^2 dt
                            <=2c_0 D(rho||sigma).     (A)

Indeed, d/dt D(P_t(rho)||sigma)=-J(P_t(rho)) and J>=2E_Phi. Integrating up to
any finite time and using nonnegativity of relative entropy gives
integral E_Phi<=D(rho||sigma)/2. The two assumed energy comparisons yield (A).
No convergence to sigma or primitivity is needed. Faithfulness along the
finite-time orbit follows, for this channel generator, from the positive
identity term exp(-t)rho in the Poisson expansion of P_t.

Set b=T(Phi(rho),rho). Since
P_t'=P_t(Phi-I), trace-norm contraction implies T(P_t(rho),rho)<=bt. The error
function of a CPTP Psi is 2-Lipschitz in T by the triangle inequality and
contractivity, so

  e_Psi(P_t(rho)) >= (e_Psi(rho)-2bt)_+.

For b>0, integration of this cone gives

  integral_0^infinity (e_Psi(rho)-2bt)_+^2 dt
                              =e_Psi(rho)^3/(6b).

Combining with (A) yields the conditional cubic estimate

  e_Psi(rho)^3 <= 12(1+1/sqrt(2))D(rho||sigma)b.       (B)

If b=0, the entire orbit is rho; the finite upper bound in (A) forces
e_Psi(rho)=0, so (B) also holds. The same supplied Psi works for all rho if the
premises are uniform. This conditional consequence has no arbitrary quantum-
   reference assertion: the baseline weak endpoint is only proved on the qubit
itself, apart from its separately stated classical-reference extension.

C. All-dimensional commuting-state strong endpoint, directly proved
-------------------------------------------------------------------
At a faithful state rho commuting with the faithful stationary sigma, every
finite KMS-symmetric QMS has J(rho)>=4E(rho), even if the generator lacks modular
covariance. This is a control, with no novelty claim. A general KMS generator
need not preserve the commuting-state set along its orbit, so this pointwise
control alone is not an orbit theorem.

Diagonalize rho and sigma simultaneously, with rho_i>0, sigma_i>0. Write
s_i=sqrt(sigma_i) and f_i=rho_i/sigma_i. In the representation (1),

  (V_B)_ii=sum_j |B_ij|^2 s_j/s_i,
  X_ii=s_i f_i,  Y_ii=s_i log(f_i).

Therefore identity-noise diagonal terms cancel, and symmetry B_ji=conj(B_ij)
gives the exact unordered-pair formulas

  E_B=sum_(i<j) |B_ij|^2 s_i s_j (sqrt(f_i)-sqrt(f_j))^2,
  J_B=sum_(i<j) |B_ij|^2 s_i s_j
                         (f_i-f_j)(log(f_i)-log(f_j)).

The positive scalar inequality in section A gives J_B>=4E_B; summing over
noises gives the stated all-dimensional pointwise control.

For an untouched quantum reference, choose its stationary state to be tracial
using the baseline's reference-independence identity. The same control applies
whenever [rho_AR,sigma_A tensor I_R]=0. For a nondegenerate qubit sigma_A, this
allows arbitrary positive quantum reference matrices C_0,C_1 in
rho_AR=|0><0| tensor C_0+|1><1| tensor C_1, even when C_0,C_1 do not commute.
Their joint diagonalization with sigma_A tensor I_R is by a controlled unitary,
so the pair formula remains valid. General joint states with coherences between
different sigma_A eigenspaces remain outside this control and UNRESOLVED.
