# Failure record and restart gates

## Failures that stop the current capability claim

1. **A likely sign incompatibility.** Nature S8's fitted response raises its
   stated radical-pair separation fraction as RF amplitude increases. The
   Xiang ODE uses that same separation-fraction label to route triplet
   encounters to nonfluorescent reduced mScarlet. Increasing the literal β
   lowers `G` immediately and at steady state. The fit is called
   semi-quantitative, so this is an unresolved model-interface mismatch, not a
   claim that the observation is false.
2. **Current-system spin inputs are unknown.** The published study states
   radical-pair partner identities and nuclear-spin distributions are unknown.
   A two-electron pseudofield model omits explicit nuclear spins, protein
   structure, partner assignment, exchange, orientation distribution, and
   molecular decoherence. Do not promote its output to a biological prediction.
3. **Frozen Floquet transfer fails.** After fitting the baseline separation
   yield and tuning static-field width toward the observed line, the
   source-adjacent Floquet model predicts negative response substantially
   larger than the fitted magnitude. It does not pass the held-out RF response
   test, so the dynamic extension is a negative control, not a capability.
4. **Observation-map uncertainty.** The ODE labels `G` as fluorescent
   mScarlet ground state, but the Nature supplement does not provide an
   independent calibration from this state variable to camera counts. That
   mapping must be established before asserting a contradiction beyond the
   model interface.
5. **Prior art bounds novelty.** RF spin-resonance control has been
   demonstrated in the 2026 Nature work; bounded optimal control of supplied
   radical-pair Hamiltonians is in 2024/2026 literature. Generic RYDMR,
   Floquet/Landau-Zener calculations, geometric phase, or pulse optimization
   cannot be claimed as new from this audit.

## Restart gates

- **Resolve the parameter interface first:** obtain/reconstruct the exact
  equations and fluorescence output used for Nature S8; trace its β symbol
  into the Xiang equations and check whether any complement/sign conversion or
  output transformation is applied. If no source supports that map, preserve
  the discrepancy as conditional and stop claims of mechanistic prediction.
- **Then freeze an equal-information prediction:** identify the radical pair
  and acquire the hyperfine/nuclear-spin/decoherence data by an explicitly
  charged transient EPR/ENDOR or equivalent protocol. Use that same information
  budget for an independently fitted empirical response control. Freeze both
  models before RF amplitude/frequency holdouts.
- **Decisive sample test:** simultaneously measure fluorescence and a direct
  radical/product observable while varying B1 from the lowest available field
  through 0.3 mT and sweeping through ESR. Include detuned RF with matched
  absorbed power and a measured temperature trace. A sign-correct, held-out
  joint prediction must beat both a same-data phenomenological fit and the
  matched heating/photobleaching control.
- **Costs to charge:** radical-pair/sample preparation, hyperfine acquisition,
  RF coil and B0 calibration, temperature matching, transient product
  measurement, model identification, and held-out replicates. No uncharged
  DFT/PES or free decoherence/hyperfine assumptions.
- **Allowed conclusion after passing:** predictive spin-dependent photochemical
  control for the measured pair and stated operating range. A wider biological
  or high-ceiling quantum-chemistry claim still needs the relevant independent
  validation.
