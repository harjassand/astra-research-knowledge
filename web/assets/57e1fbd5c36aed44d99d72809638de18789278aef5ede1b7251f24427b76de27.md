## Strong all-characteristic rational hitting

OpenAI Sept24 source `Polynomial Hitting Lists for Noncommutative Rational Formulas`, Theorem1.1, prints polynomial-bit rational-matrix lists for formulas over Q, with defined invertible hits and original tree domains. Its proof uses ordinary Wronskians and characteristic-zero Taylor reversal. The candidate here replaces that exact gate by folded q-Newton reversal and supplies prime-field specialization using a separable cyclotomic ring plus finite-field grid, counted across all embeddings. This is a further extension of that source mechanism.

Arvind–Chatterjee–Mukhopadhyay, *Black-Box Identity Testing of Noncommutative Rational Formulas in Deterministic Quasipolynomial Time*, arXiv:2309.15647 / ECCC TR23-147, is the prior general rational black-box comparator. ECCC's current Revision6, July11,2025, was acquired in full and inspected at Theorem2/Corollaries3–4 (page5), the division-generator field specification (page15), and Section6 (page37). Theorem2 outputs rational matrices and a quasipolynomial hitting list; its generator works over characteristic-zero extensions of Q(z). Section6 separately asks for polynomial-dimensional hitting tuples and black-box NSingular. Source https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2023/147/revision/6/download , local sources/acm2025r6.pdf and .txt. This is a direct comparator check, not a full proof audit or priority clearance against all related work.

Hrubeš–Wigderson2015, *Non-Commutative Arithmetic Circuits with Division*, Theorem2.1, Proposition7.1/Corollary7.2, and Proposition7.3 are the domain/free-field bridge. They apply to a field F without a characteristic-zero assumption. Primary full proof acquired at https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v011a014/v011a014.pdf , local sources/hw2015.txt. These are standard inherited facts, not new lemmas here.

Derksen–Makam2017, *Polynomial Degree Bounds for Matrix Semi-Invariants*, Theorem1.8, Proposition1.12 and Section6, gives generic bounded-size matrix witnesses and randomized rational PIT over infinite fields of arbitrary characteristic, preserving actual formula domains. Primary inspected at https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/hderksen/wp-content/uploads/sites/614/2018/05/A.I.a.54.pdf . Shell download was403; the web PDF reader supplied exact statements. Again, generic/random witness existence is not a universal deterministic list.

Chatterjee–Ghosh–Gurjar–Raj–Thierauf2026, *Bipartite Matching is in NC*, ECCC TR26-100 revision2, Theorem1.4 and Section5, gives deterministic NC noncommutative rank when matrix coefficients are supplied. Its folded-Wronskian reduction to a block matrix uses the given input A_i and does not immediately provide our coefficient-independent black-box tuples. Actual full source was read at these gates: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/100/ , local sources/nc2026.txt. Do not relabel our candidate as first efficient WHITE-BOX noncommutative rank.

ECCC TR24-084, current initial download, explicitly develops the Amitsur operational free-field model for Q or finite fields; Definition2.2 and Proposition2.5 confirm the finite-field domain distinction used here. Actual primary acquired at https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2024/084/download/ , local sources/rit2024.txt. Its main parallel/white-box result is an inherited comparator, not a black-box obstruction.

Guruswami–Kopparty, *Explicit Subspace Designs*, ECCC TR13-060 / Combinatorica2016, uses ordinary and folded Wronskians. Full source acquired at https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/060/download . Our folded Casoratian multiplicity idea is established prior technology; the q-Newton reversal and strong affine-pencil composition have not been cleared for priority. Primary abstract/technical context inspected at https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2018/017/download . A full theorem comparison remains required.
