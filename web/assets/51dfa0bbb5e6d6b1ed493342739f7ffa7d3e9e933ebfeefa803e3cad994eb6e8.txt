CYCLE03 TREE-PRIOR AUDIT
Status: narrow primary-source route check; no novelty or priority conclusion.

Question audited
-----------------
The candidate claim under proof audit is that every supplied unital, trace-
preserving, Hilbert-Schmidt-self-adjoint, self-compatible channel Phi admits a
single canonical pure-state measure-and-prepare channel Psi such that

    Psi >= (2 Phi^2 - I)_+

as positive quadratic forms on the full Hermitian operator space. This would
imply I-Psi <= 4(I-Phi), with a dimension-independent constant. The proposed
mechanism is a binary broadcasting tree: unbiased leaf estimators for modes
|lambda| > 1/sqrt(2), a bounded second-moment recursion above 2 lambda^2 > 1,
and a POVM/posterior-covariance step intended to produce one common Psi.

The audit asks whether primary literature already supplies that final
dimension-free, common-measurement, channel-order conclusion. The sources
below are close mechanism precedents; none of their checked theorem statements
is the target claim.

1. Exact scalar threshold: Peres--Roch / Kesten--Stigum
------------------------------------------------------
Yuval Peres and Sebastien Roch, “Reconstruction on Trees: Exponential Moment
Bounds for Linear Estimators,” Electronic Communications in Probability 16
(2011), 251–261, DOI 10.1214/ECP.v16-1630; arXiv:0908.2056v1.

Theorem 1 assumes an irreducible k-state stochastic edge matrix M with
stationary distribution pi, a real second-largest-in-modulus eigenvalue lambda,
and a corresponding real eigenvector nu normalized by sum_i pi_i nu_i^2=1.
On a complete b-ary tree with independent classical edge transitions, the
leaf statistic

    S_n = (b lambda)^(-n) sum_{x in L_n} nu_{xi_x}

is unbiased conditional on the root state. In the regime b lambda^2>1, the
second moments are uniformly bounded in depth; Theorem 1 further gives
E[exp(zeta S_n)|xi_root=i] <= exp(nu_i zeta + c(M) zeta^2), uniformly in n,
and Corollary 1 gives a uniform exponential-moment bound for S_n^2 near zero.
For b=2 the exact estimator threshold is 2|lambda|^2>1, matching the
candidate's scalar threshold.

What transfers: this is a direct primary-source precedent for the branching
second-moment mechanism and the 1/sqrt(2) threshold. What does not transfer:
the root and leaves are classical finite-alphabet random variables; all
observables are simultaneously readable in that classical sample space.
The bound is for each scalar eigendirection, its constants depend on M, and
there is no quantum broadcaster, noncommuting Hermitian frame, single POVM
posterior, EB channel, or operator-order inequality. The missing step is not
the scalar Kesten--Stigum recursion; it is joint operator-valued rounding of
all mode estimates into one quantum measurement with a dimension-free
posterior covariance lower bound.

2. Closest quantum tree channel: Yadavalli--Marvian
--------------------------------------------------
Shiv Akshar Yadavalli and Iman Marvian, “Noisy Quantum Trees: Infinite
Protection Without Correction,” npj Quantum Information 11, 151 (2025),
DOI 10.1038/s41534-025-00961-x; arXiv:2306.14294v3.

The setup (Eqs. (4)–(11)) encodes one input qubit at each vertex by an isometry
V:C^2 -> (C^2)^{tensor b}, then applies independent identical single-qubit
noise N on the outgoing edges. The depth-T channel is the recursively
concatenated noisy encoder E_T. The paper's principal results are for
Clifford/stabilizer encoders and Pauli noise. Proposition 1 gives, for a
logical Z operator of weight b_z,

    ||E_T - E_T o D_z||_diamond <= sqrt(2) [sqrt(b_z)|1-2p_z|]^T,

so sufficiently strong phase noise removes input coherence exponentially
with depth. Sections IV–VI analyze local recursive recovery for code families;
Eq. (78) gives the effective-channel recursion
N_{t+1}=R o N_t^{tensor b} o V o N. The Bell-tree analysis uses a specific
two-reliability-bit decoder. These are real quantum-tree results, including
preservation of classical information and, in some regimes, entanglement.

This is a close structural analogy, not the target theorem. The input is a
qubit and the node map is a specified code encoder plus edge noise; the
conclusions are tree-depth distinguishability bounds or existence/performance
of code-specific recoveries. They do not start from every supplied
self-compatible bistochastic HS-self-adjoint Phi and construct one canonical
pure EB Psi on the original system. Nor do they give Psi >= (2 Phi^2-I)_+ or
the claimed universal deficit constant. Their thresholds depend on code
weight/noise or decoder recursions, not a dimension-free all-Hermitian frame
comparison.

3. Direct joint-measurement-through-broadcasting precedent: Haapasalo
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Erkka Theodor Haapasalo, “Joint Measurements through Quantum Broadcasting,”
arXiv:2207.03226v1 (2022).

Definition 2 models a joint POVM as G = Phi^* o (M tensor N), where a channel
Phi sends the input to two outputs and M,N are measured locally. Theorem 1
states that for separable Hilbert spaces and local PVMs P,Q, any target POVM
G absolutely continuous with respect to P tensor Q can be generated by some
broadcasting channel. Theorem 2 gives necessary and sufficient conditions in
finite dimension D for a finite-outcome G to be generated from local POVMs
M,N: a linear-relation condition and complete positivity of the induced map
on span{M_x tensor N_y}; complete positivity is tested at ancilla size D^2.
Corollary 1 makes this explicit for noisy PVMs, with the componentwise
positive-operator condition (3) in the paper.

This is the most direct precedent for a broadcast channel being converted
into a joint measurement. Its gate is nevertheless different: it treats a
chosen pair of output observables and a chosen target joint POVM, with
existence/compatibility conditions for a broadcasting channel. It neither
fixes and recursively reuses the supplied channel Phi nor provides one POVM
simultaneously covering a full noncommuting eigenbasis. It also does not
prepare a canonical pure posterior state or compare its EB channel with
(2 Phi^2-I)_+. Pairwise broadcast-generated joint measurements do not alone
produce the one common measurement required here.

Decisive mathematical gate
--------------------------
The sources supply two halves of the intuition: scalar tree estimators have
the exact 2|lambda|^2>1 threshold, and quantum broadcasting can generate
joint observables under explicit compatibility hypotheses. They do not
provide the bridge from mode-by-mode bounded estimators for a supplied
quantum broadcaster to one POVM whose pure posterior preparation dominates
the positive part of 2 Phi^2-I on every Hermitian direction, with constants
independent of dimension. That posterior-covariance / operator-order
conversion is the load-bearing theorem still needing proof or a counterexample.

This bounded check found no exact prior theorem matching that interface. This
is not an exhaustive literature survey and is not evidence of novelty or
priority; the candidate remains a proof-audit problem.
