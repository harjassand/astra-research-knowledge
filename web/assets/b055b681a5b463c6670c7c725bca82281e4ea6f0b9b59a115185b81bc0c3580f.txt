THEOREM (localized robust code-cost transfer)
Let M,g be a smooth closed Riemannian 3-manifold, X a smooth nowhere-zero divergence-free field, φ_t its flow, μ=dVol/V, S=Def X, and ν>0. Let A be a measurable set invariant under every φ_t, with α=μ(A)>0, and let μ_A=μ|A/α. Let c,ĉ:A→{1,...,K} be measurable K-symbol observables. Their stationary processes are c_t(x)=c(φ_t x), ĉ_t(x)=ĉ(φ_t x), under μ_A. Suppose μ_A{c!=ĉ}<=δ and a certified lower bound R is known for the entropy rate h(ĉ) in nats per sampled step. Define
  F_K(δ)=h_2(min{δ,1-1/K})+min{δ,1-1/K} log(K-1),
where h_2(p)=-p log p-(1-p)log(1-p), with 0 log 0=0. If X sustains the stationary strain-viscosity equation
  ∇_X X - 2ν div S + ∇p = F,
then, for samples once per unit of the chosen clock,
  ∫_A |S|^2 dμ >= 2α [R-F_K(δ)]_+^2,
  W >= 4ν V α [R-F_K(δ)]_+^2.
If the code is sampled every τ physical time units, replace the last rate by [R-F_K(δ)]_+/τ. Logs are natural; a rate stated in bits is multiplied by log 2.

FULL DERIVATION
1. Put r=|X|, e=X/r, P=I-e⊗e, q=<Se,e>=X(log r), b=PSe. The trace of B=PSP on the normal plane is -q, so its eigenvalues are -q/2±s for a measurable s>=0, and
     |S|^2 = 2s^2 + (3/2)q^2 + 2|b|^2 >= 2s^2.
2. The normal projective cocycle Q_t=P_{φ_t x}Dφ_t|_{e(x)^⊥} is invertible. For v(t)=Dφ_t v(0), w=Pv, write v=w+ae. Torsion-freeness gives ∇_Xv=∇_vX; projection and ∇_Xe=P∇_eX cancel the a terms, yielding P∇_Xw=P∇_wX. Thus
     d/dt log|w| = <Sw,w>/|w|^2 <= s(φ_t x)-q(φ_t x)/2.
   Integrating, log||Q_t(x)|| <= ∫_0^t s(φ_u x)du - (1/2)log[r(φ_t x)/r(x)]. Since r is bounded above and below, the endpoint term divided by t vanishes.
3. Volume preservation gives derivative determinant one. The flow direction has exponent zero because Dφ_t X=X∘φ_t and r is bounded; the normal determinant is r(x)/r(φ_t x), so the two normal exponents are χ(x),-χ(x), χ>=0. Oseledets and Birkhoff under μ_A therefore give ∫χ dμ_A <=∫s dμ_A. Ruelle's C1 entropy inequality applied to the invariant Borel probability μ_A gives
     h_{μ_A}(φ_1) <= ∫χ dμ_A <= ∫s dμ_A.
   No smoothness of the boundary of A is needed: μ_A is invariant, and Ruelle's theorem permits arbitrary invariant Borel probabilities. Multiplying by α yields α h_{μ_A} <= ∫_A s dμ.
4. For the two stationary symbolic processes, the per-step disagreement probability is at most δ by φ_t-invariance. Fano's inequality gives H(c_0|ĉ_0)<=F_K(δ); the same bound holds in the reverse direction. For a length-n block, conditional entropy is at most the sum of its coordinate conditional entropies, hence H(c_0^{n-1}|ĉ_0^{n-1}) and H(ĉ_0^{n-1}|c_0^{n-1}) are each at most nF_K(δ). Dividing the resulting two block-entropy comparisons by n and taking limits gives |h(c)-h(ĉ)|<=F_K(δ). Since c is a finite measurable partition, h(c)<=h_{μ_A}(φ_1). Therefore h_{μ_A}(φ_1)>=[R-F_K(δ)]_+.
5. Combining the last bounds and Cauchy-Schwarz,
     α [R-F_K(δ)]_+ <= ∫_A s dμ
       <= sqrt(α ∫_A s^2 dμ)
       <= sqrt((α/2) ∫_A |S|^2 dμ).
   Squaring proves the local strain-energy bound. On a closed manifold, pairing the stationary equation with X and integrating gives W=2ν∫|S|^2dVol=2νV∫|S|^2dμ. Since total strain energy dominates its integral over A, the power bound follows.

INTERFACE, COSTS, AND LIMITS
The theorem requires a flow-invariant region of certified volume fraction α; a certified stationary code model with entropy lower bound R; and a certified one-time disagreement upper bound δ under μ_A. These are supplied/acquired inputs, not outputs of the inequality. If R<=F_K(δ), it gives no positive floor. If a finite-state Markov presentation with m states is actually certified for ĉ, its entropy formula has m^2 transition terms; exact input bit lengths, stationary-vector acquisition, logarithm precision, and proof that this is the induced process must be charged separately. This statement makes no cost claim for finding A, the Markov presentation, α, δ, or the physical field. Empirical error estimates need mixing/confidence assumptions that are absent here.

The factor α is operational: a code active on a smaller invariant volume needs more conditional rate to sustain the same volume-averaged rate. In particular, fixing global rate G=αR while δ=0 gives W>=4νV G^2/α. No volume-independent power per computation step follows when α can shrink. The result is for smooth, steady, divergence-free, nonvanishing 3D flows on closed manifolds with strain viscosity. It implies no fluid regularity, actuator realizability, universal-computation rate, or topological-entropy bound.

EXACT FINITE-WINDOW OBSTRUCTION
A finite code trace cannot replace the asymptotic entropy-rate certificate. For any order N, take a binary de Bruijn cycle of period q=2^N, assign its q bits to q equal angular intervals on T^3, and let X=(1/q)∂_θ in the flat metric. The time-one map is rotation by 1/q, so it has finite order q, KS entropy zero, and Def X=0. A uniformly random starting angle selects each interval equally; every cyclic n-block for 1<=n<=N appears exactly 2^(N-n) times. Thus every observed block entropy through length N is exactly n log 2, despite zero asymptotic code entropy, zero strain, and zero sustaining power. The script debruijn_finite_window.py constructs the cycle by Hierholzer's algorithm and checks the exact word counts at N=6 (q=64); it reports PASS. This is an exact diagnostic counterexample to a finite-window shortcut, not to the theorem, whose premise is an asymptotic rate R.
