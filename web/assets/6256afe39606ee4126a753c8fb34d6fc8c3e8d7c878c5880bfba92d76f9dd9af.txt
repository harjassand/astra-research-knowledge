CYCLE 4 LEAF — REVERSIBLE SUBSTRATE CONVERSION EXTENSION
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the stated CTMC/Foster/tightness claims. The algebraic proof below is complete under standard falling-factorial mass action, but it has not received an independent external proof review. No novelty claim is made.

RESULT

Adding the reversible catalyst-mediated conversion A+B <-> A+C to the sparse paired-edge network yields a three-species, strongly connected weakly reversible network with rank-three stoichiometry. For every fixed rate vector in one explicit compact positive box, its standard stochastic mass-action CTMC has the entire lattice N_0^3 as one closed irreducible positive-recurrent class. A physical-time Foster function proves nonexplosion and recurrence. A second face-weighted Foster function gives the uniform stationary first-moment bound

  sup_theta E_pi_theta[A+B+C] <= 49.

Thus the stationary laws are uniformly tight over the rate box. An explicit finite-core path/minorization argument also proves strictly positive uniform stationary occupancy floors for both substrate isomers B and C. The floors are exact but astronomically small; they are formal positivity guarantees, not biologically useful quantitative estimates. The deterministic ODE has a unique positive equilibrium for every positive rate vector; global attraction is not claimed here.

NETWORK, STOCHASTIC CONVENTION, AND RATE BOX

Species A,B,C are interpreted as an externally maintained catalyst/template and two interconvertible substrate/product forms. Reactions are

  (R1) 0 <-> A                 (alpha, delta)
  (R2) A <-> A+B               (p, q)
  (R3) A+B <-> 2A+B             (u, v)
  (R4) A+B <-> A+C              (s, t)

Each pair lists forward then reverse rate constants. The CTMC state is x=(a,b,c) in N_0^3. Propensities use unit-volume standard stochastic mass action and falling factorials:

  R1: alpha, delta*a
  R2: p*a, q*a*b
  R3: u*a*b, v*(a)_2*b,     (a)_2=a(a-1)
  R4: s*a*b, t*a*c.

The compact independent rate box is

  alpha in [1,2],   delta in [5,6],
  p     in [1,2],   q     in [4,5],
  u     in [1/4,1/2], v   in [1,2],
  s     in [1/2,1], t     in [4,5].

The constants are exact rational interval endpoints. Every lower endpoint is strictly positive. “Uniform” below means uniform over this one fixed box, not over all positive rates or over predictable/time-varying rate controls.

SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION AND ITS LIMITS

R4 is the standard coarse reaction-graph motif “catalyst A converts substrate B reversibly to product C, with A regenerated.” Enzyme-catalyzed reversible isomerizations are real biochemical reactions: for example, Yoshida et al. report that L-rhamnose isomerase catalyzes reversible L-rhamnose/L-rhamnulose isomerization (FEBS Open Bio 3 (2013), DOI 10.1016/j.fob.2012.11.008). This source supports the generic reversible-isomerization analogy only; it does not validate this three-species network or its parameters.

R2 represents an A-supported B supply/clearance channel, and R3 represents B-catalyzed A amplification/deamplification. The open reaction list does not balance atoms: A immigration and the A-to-A+B channel suppress feedstock/reservoir species. The network is therefore a coarse-grained, open chemostat-style motif, not a fully specified enzyme mechanism, not a Michaelis–Menten scheme with an explicit enzyme-substrate intermediate, and not a claim about any identified pathway. An atom-balanced finite-resource realization would add feedstock, waste, and potentially enzyme-bound complexes; its state space and drift proof would have to be redone.

GRAPH, RANK, CLOSED CLASSES

The complexes are {0,A,A+B,2A+B,A+C}. The undirected reaction graph is the tree 0—A—(A+B), with leaves 2A+B and A+C attached to A+B. Every edge is present in both directions, so the directed graph is strongly connected and the network is weakly reversible with one linkage class. The reaction vectors include e_A from 0->A, e_B from A->A+B, and e_C-e_B from A+B->A+C. These span R^3; the stoichiometric rank is three. The complex count is five, hence the deficiency is 5-1-3=1. There is no linear conservation law.

The full lattice N_0^3 is one closed irreducible CTMC class. To reach zero from any state, immigrate A if a=0; while a>=1, use A+C->A+B followed by A+B->A to remove each C, use A+B->A to remove every B, then use A->0 to remove A. Each selected propensity is positive when selected. Conversely, from zero immigrate A, create the required total b+c B molecules with A->A+B, convert c of them to C with A+B->A+C, adjust a by immigration/death, and if the target has a=0 remove the final A. Thus every lattice state communicates with zero. All reactions preserve nonnegativity by the stated source-factorial propensities, and an irreducible whole state space is closed.

DETERMINISTIC EQUILIBRIUM (NO GLOBAL-STABILITY CLAIM)

The mass-action ODE is

  a' = alpha-delta*a + u*a*b - v*a^2*b,
  b' = p*a - q*a*b - s*a*b + t*a*c,
  c' = s*a*b - t*a*c.

At any equilibrium, a cannot be zero because a'=alpha at a=0. Since b'+c'=p*a-q*a*b, every equilibrium has b*=p/q. The c equation then gives c*=s*p/(t*q). The remaining equation is

  alpha + (u*p/q-delta)*a - (v*p/q)*a^2 = 0.

Its constant is positive and its leading coefficient is negative, so it has exactly one positive root,

  a* = [-(delta-u*p/q) + sqrt((delta-u*p/q)^2+4(v*p/q)alpha)]/[2(v*p/q)].

There is no boundary equilibrium, and the displayed point is the unique positive equilibrium. This calculation does not establish global attraction, local stability, or an endemic threshold.

PHYSICAL-TIME FOSTER PROOF

Let W0=a+b+(3/2)c. Directly summing propensity times W0-increment gives

  L W0 = alpha + (p-delta)a + (u-q+s/2)ab - (t/2)ac - v(a)_2 b.

Set mu=delta-p, nu=q-u-s/2, and rho=t/2. On the rate box,

  mu >= 3,   nu >= 4-1/2-1/2 = 3,   rho >= 2.

Consequently,

  L W0 <= 2 - 3a - 3ab - 2ac - v(a)_2b.                 (1)

W0 has positive drift alpha on the face a=0, so add a face correction:

  W = 1+a+b+(3/2)c + 2*1_{a=0}.

At a=0 the only enabled reaction is immigration, whose W-jump is -1; hence LW=-alpha<=-1. At a>=1, except for death from a=1 to a=0, the correction is unchanged. That boundary death adds 2delta to the base drift. Therefore, exactly,

  LW = alpha-mu*a-nu*a*b-rho*a*c-v(a)_2*b
       +2delta*1_{a=1}-2alpha*1_{a=0}.                  (2)

For a>=1, (2) and the rate box imply

  LW <= 14-3a-3ab-2ac-v(a)_2*b
     <= 14-3a-2(b+c).

Thus when a>=1 and N=a+b+c>=8, LW<=14-2N<=-2. When a=0, LW=-alpha<=-1 regardless of b,c. With the finite set F={N<=7},

  LW <= -1 off F,      LW <= 14 globally.                (3)

W is nonnegative and proper. For sigma_m=inf{t:W(X_t)>=m}, stopped Dynkin and (3) give, for m>W(x),

  m P_x(sigma_m<=T) <= E_x W(X_(T wedge sigma_m)) <= W(x)+14T.

The sublevel sets of W are finite and their one-step successor sets are finite. Letting m increase shows no path exits all such finite sets in a finite time. Explosion while remaining in any fixed finite set is impossible because its total jump rate is bounded. This proves nonexplosion in physical time. The strict finite-set drift (3), the finite rates on finite sets, and irreducibility give positive recurrence by the standard countable-state CTMC Foster criterion. There is a unique invariant probability pi_theta for each fixed theta in the box.

UNIFORM STATIONARY TIGHTNESS AND MOMENT BOUND

For a stronger drift with substrate coercivity, set r_B=3/7 and r_C=2/7 and define

  W1=1+a+b+(3/2)c + (2+r_B*b+r_C*c)*1_{a=0}.

At a=0 the only enabled event is immigration; it changes W1 by -(1+r_B*b+r_C*c), so

  LW1 = -alpha*(1+r_B*b+r_C*c).

For a>=1 the correction can change only on death from a=1 to a=0. The extra drift from that death is delta*(2+r_B*b+r_C*c). In the substrate coefficients this leaves, at a=1,

  coefficient of -b: nu-delta*r_B >= 3-6*(3/7)=3/7,
  coefficient of -c: rho-delta*r_C >= 2-6*(2/7)=2/7.

At a>=2 the correction is unchanged and (1) supplies at least these same margins. The additive terms are bounded above by 14 uniformly. Therefore throughout the lattice,

  LW1 <= 14 - 3a - (3/7)b - (2/7)c.                    (4)

Here W1 is proper. Applying stopped Dynkin to (4), averaging over [0,T], using nonexplosion from W, and then passing to the invariant Cesaro limit via bounded truncations of the nonnegative function 3a+(3/7)b+(2/7)c gives

  3 E_pi[A] + (3/7) E_pi[B] + (2/7) E_pi[C] <= 14.     (5)

This standard truncation/Fatou argument also establishes finiteness of the moments in (5); it does not assume integrability of W1 under pi in advance. Since the smallest coefficient is 2/7,

  E_pi[A+B+C] <= 49,
  sup_theta pi_theta(A+B+C >= M) <= 49/M,   M>0.        (6)

The second inequality is Markov's bound. It proves uniform tightness of the stationary-law family over the stated compact positive box. It does not give uniform mixing, a useful mean return-time constant, or any claim as rate lower endpoints approach zero.

UNIFORM ENDEMIC OCCUPANCY FLOOR

The first-moment bound implies

  pi_theta(N<=98) >= 1 - 49/99 = 50/99 > 1/2,
  N=A+B+C.

For any state with N<=98 construct a path to x_B=(1,1,0):
1. If a=0, immigrate A; if a>=2, apply A->0 until a=1.
2. Remove each B by A+B->A.
3. For each C, apply A+C->A+B, then remove that B by A+B->A.
4. Create one B by A->A+B.

The path has at most H_B=198 labelled reaction firings, remains in N<=99, and each chosen channel has rate at least m_B=1 throughout the rate box. To reach x_C=(1,0,1), use the same initial adjustment and drain all B and C, create one B, then apply A+B->A+C. This path has at most H_C=199 firings, remains in N<=99, and each selected channel has rate at least m_C=1/2.

On N<=99 the total exit rate is bounded uniformly by

  Q = 2 + (6+2)*99 + (5+1/2+1+5)*99^2 + 2*99^3
    = 2,054,104.

The terms bound, respectively, immigration, A-death and A-supported B birth, the q/u/s/t bilinear channels, and v(a)_2b. If a prescribed labelled path has h jumps, each selected hazard is at least m and every total hazard along it is at most Q. The probability it makes exactly these h jumps and then no further jump by T=1/Q is at least

  exp(-1)*(m/Q)^h/h!.

This follows by integrating the product of selected hazards over the h-jump time simplex; the survival factor on the entire interval is at least exp(-QT)=exp(-1). Since m/Q<1, the bound decreases with h. Thus uniformly for every x with N<=98 and every rate vector in the box,

  P_x(X_(1/Q)=x_B) >= eta_B := exp(-1)*Q^(-198)/198!,
  P_x(X_(1/Q)=x_C) >= eta_C := exp(-1)*(2Q)^(-199)/199!.

For an invariant law, pi(y)=sum_x pi(x)P_(1/Q)(x,y). Restricting the sum to N<=98 gives

  inf_theta pi_theta(B>0) >= eta_B/2 > 0,
  inf_theta pi_theta(C>0) >= eta_C/2 > 0.                (7)

The literal target-state mass is bounded below, so (7) is rigorous. These constants are far too small to be practical biological estimates. The stationary chain is irreducible, so fixed-parameter positivity also follows from full support; the finite-core minorization is what upgrades this to uniform positivity over the compact rate box.

There is also a sharper exact bound for catalyst occupancy. The only transitions crossing the cut a=0 are immigration at rate alpha and death at rate delta from a=1, so stationarity gives alpha*pi(a=0)=delta*pi(a=1). Since pi(a=1)<=pi(a>0),

  pi_theta(A>0) >= alpha/(alpha+delta) >= 1/7.

This cut identity concerns catalyst presence; it does not replace the B/C minorization bounds because the substrates can be absent while A is present.

RELATION TO PRIOR RESULTS AND PRECISE NON-CONCLUSIONS

1. N33 is a deterministic all-endotactic permanence candidate with positive bounded rate paths and positive-class quantifiers. The present network is weakly reversible, hence endotactic, and its ODE has the unique positive equilibrium computed above. I do not use N33 in any CTMC proof. No deterministic global-attraction conclusion is inferred from CTMC recurrence.
2. N35 is a finite-horizon controlled-safety certificate, not a stationary theorem; no such certificate is used here.
3. N77 gives an exact source-reported family of distinct positive-recurrent stochastic classes inside one deterministic linear class, with no cross-class stationary tightness. Its quantifier differs: this extension has one full-lattice irreducible class for each rate vector, and (6)-(7) are uniform over a compact rate box for this fixed network. It neither contradicts nor repairs N77.
4. The inherited C10_S03 coordinate-hinge result covers a different class: every species has primitive immigration/degradation; its actual complexes have molecularity at most two; and its reciprocal catalytic-removal pattern is checked as a finite exact input grammar. Here only A has primitive immigration/death, the product 2A+B has molecularity three, and the added A+B<->A+C conversion is substrate isomerization. C10_S03 therefore does not state the present theorem. The cycle-4 larger-ODE/CTMC report extends the core through high-order reverse channels and unary conversion among catalysts; it does not include the present B<->C isomerization edge. No novelty claim follows from these scope distinctions.
5. Anderson-Cappelletti-Kim-Kurtz product form applies to complex-balanced systems. This rate box is not asserted complex balanced, and the proof above is a direct Foster argument. Anderson-Kim's binary-network positive-recurrence criteria provide an important prior baseline, but the present actual reaction list includes a three-molecule product and lacks primitive B/C inflows/outflows; it is not simply the all-species immigration/degradation coordinate-hinge class.

The exact model establishes stationary substrate occupancy, not extinction thresholds, a quasi-stationary epidemic law, robustness under deleting A, finite-resource persistence, parameter inference, or empirical relevance. If a lower rate endpoint is allowed to tend to zero, the compact-box constants and minorization collapse; no all-positive-rate uniform floor is claimed.

INPUTS, COSTS, BASELINE, FALSIFIERS

The proof uses rational arithmetic for every drift margin, count threshold, and Q. The path lengths and core are explicit. The occupancy lower bounds involve exp(-1), factorials up to 199, and Q^199; storing them symbolically is compact, while decimal expansion is unnecessary and the resulting floor is not quantitatively useful. No timing or energy claim is made.

Strongest baseline: complex-balanced product-form stationary laws and established CTMC Foster-Lyapunov criteria; plus the inherited weakly-reversible higher-order feedback family. The exact addition here is a reversible substrate-isomerization channel, with rate-box-uniform moment/tightness/occupancy calculations. Whether this extension or its proof has prior exact overlap remains unverified.

Direct falsifiers under the stated interface include any violation of (1) or (4) at a rate vector in the box, a reachable closed class split contradicting full-lattice irreducibility, or an invariant law whose first moment violates (5). An external chemical interpretation claim would require evidence beyond the generic reversible-isomerization analogy cited here.
