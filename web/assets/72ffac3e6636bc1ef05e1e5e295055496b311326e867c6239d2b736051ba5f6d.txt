1. CONVENTION AND FINITE CHAIN

Use D[L](rho)=LrhoL^dagger-(L^dagger Lrho+rho L^dagger L)/2,
Gamma>=0, nu>=0 finite, and J_-=sum_i sigma_-, J_+=J_-^dagger.
Let r be EXCITATION number, so J_-|D_K,r>=sqrt[r(K-r+1)]|D_K,r-1>.
The diagonal symmetric block's birth/death rates are
 lambda_r=Gamma nu(r+1)(K-r),
 mu_r=Gamma(nu+1)r(K-r+1), r=0,...,K.
No Dicke off-diagonal coherence is generated from a diagonal input. K=0
is an empty, invariant block and is handled separately. Gamma=0 is static.

2. INDEPENDENT POLYNOMIAL IDENTITIES

For K>=1 define B_r(x)=binom(K,r)x^r(1-x)^(K-r) and
 a(x)=nu-(2nu+1)x-(K-1)x(1-x)
     =nu-(K+2nu)x+(K-1)x^2,
 D(x)=x(1-x)(nu+x)=nu x+(1-nu)x^2-x^3,
 Lf=a f'+D f''.
Straight differentiation gives, for ell>=1,
 L x^ell=nu ell^2 x^(ell-1)
          -ell[K-ell+1+nu(ell+1)]x^ell
          +ell(K-ell)x^(ell+1),
 L1=0.
Thus degree<=K polynomials are invariant; the possible degree K+1 term
vanishes at ell=K. Direct Bernstein differentiation gives
 L B_r=(nu+1)(r+1)(K-r) B_(r+1)
       +nu r(K-r+1) B_(r-1)
       -[(nu+1)r(K-r+1)+nu(r+1)(K-r)]B_r,
with nonexistent terms zero. Integrating B_r against a positive measure is
therefore precisely the FORWARD quantum population chain above.

The factorial observable F_ell(r)=binom(r,ell)/binom(K,ell) satisfies the
same backward chain identity, with F_0=1 and top coefficient zero at ell=K.
This independently checks ordinary moment normalization, not merely one
coordinate convention. In particular the K1 mean satisfies
 d pi1/dt=Gamma[nu-(2nu+1)pi1].

The diffusion convention is
 dX=Gamma a(X)dt+sqrt[2Gamma D(X)]dW.
Using sqrt[Gamma D] would halve the second-derivative term and give the
wrong chain. The alternative doubled Lindblad dissipator doubles all physical
rates; its Gamma must be converted explicitly. No microscopic approximation
to a different thermal model is inferred.

3. BOUNDARIES AND A POSITIVE FINITE-GRID PROOF

D>=0 on[0,1], D(0)=D(1)=0, a(0)=nu>=0, a(1)=-(nu+1)<0.
At nu0 the x0 boundary is absorbing. The x1 boundary is inward even at nu0.
An existence theorem for a degenerate SDE is unnecessary for cone preservation:
one can prove it directly by positive finite chains and finite-dimensional
moment convergence.

On mesh h=1/M let x_i=i h, i0..M. Put a_+=max(a,0), a_-=max(-a,0) and
 q_i^+=Gamma[D(x_i)/h^2+a_+(x_i)/h],
 q_i^-=Gamma[D(x_i)/h^2+a_-(x_i)/h].
At i0 the absent downward rate is zero by the boundary identities, and at iM
the absent upward rate is zero. All valid rates are nonnegative; set the
diagonal to minus their sum. This is an exact conservative Markov generator.

For every polynomial f of degree<=K the grid backward generator converges
uniformly to Gamma Lf. Interior central differences have error at most
 Gamma Dmax ||f''''||_infty h^2/12;
upwind drift contributes at most Gamma Amax ||f''||_infty h/2, including
both boundaries. For degree<4 take the nonexistent derivative as zero.
Sufficient bounds are Dmax<=(nu+1)/4 and
Amax<=nu+1+(K-1)/4. The proof is for each fixed finite K,nu,Gamma,T.

Quantize any initial representing measure mu onto this grid. This is an
existence construction; neither mu nor its grid weights need be acquired.
The grid law is positive at all times, hence its Bernstein population vector
belongs to the compact convex cone of separable diagonal symmetric states.
For the moments u_h=(E1,EX,...,EX^K), the polynomial identity above gives
 du_h/dt=Gamma C u_h+r_h(t), ||r_h(t)||<=C0 h.
Quantization error is O(Kh). Variation of constants gives uniform convergence
on [0,T] to the exact finite ODE du/dt=Gamma C u. The Bernstein identities
identify this with the quantum birth/death solution. Closedness of the
finite-dimensional separable cone therefore proves that every initially
separable Dicke-diagonal symmetric block stays separable for every t>=0.
Each finite T is arbitrary; no uniform-in-infinite-time error bound or
computationally efficient grid simulation is claimed.

4. LIMITS AND AN INDEPENDENT STATIONARY CHECK

At nu0 all symmetric blocks ultimately reach r0, a pure product state.
For nu>0 the chain stationary law is pi_r proportional to z^r,
z=nu/(nu+1). The diffusion has stationary density proportional to
 (nu+x)^(-K-2).
Indeed D'-(K+2)D/(nu+x)=a; its zero current vanishes at both endpoints.
Its Bernstein populations are the geometric law: this also follows by
filtering the uniform coherent measure by diag(1,sqrt z) on every site.
As nu becomes large, after rescaling time by Gamma nu, L approaches
(1-2x)f'+x(1-x)f'', with uniform stationary coherent measure. These checks
agree with the finite chain and do not replace the all-time proof.

5. WHOLE-SPACE TWO-PRODUCIBLE CONCLUSION

Assume the admitted initial state is permutation invariant and Dicke diagonal
within each spin block, with scalar multiplicity identity. Its conserved
spin weights q_b and K=N-2b symmetric blocks admit the Schur representation
as uniform permutation averages of b singlet pairs and one symmetric K-site
block. Singlets have total J_+=J_-=0; the collective generator acts only on
the remaining symmetric sites. Permutation averaging commutes with it.

Every positive block representing measure at time t decomposes that block
into pure iid products using the exact K+1 phase grid. Each whole-space term
therefore has only singlet pair factors and one-site factors. It is
2-producible, and convex mixtures/permutation averages remain 2-producible.
If a valid full-separability witness is strictly violated, the state is not
1-producible, so its exact entanglement depth is2. Without a witness violation
only the depth<=2 upper bound is certified.

This claim does not cover arbitrary symmetric coherent initial states,
arbitrary multiplicity correlations or arbitrary non-diagonal spin blocks.
It covers the admitted qubit thermal trajectories because their exact source
theorem gives separability of every initial symmetric Dicke block (effective
deltaK/N), with scalar permutation multiplicity. For signed delta<=2log2,
alpha>=0 and the admitted field, those hypotheses are the exact imports.
No finite-bit all-time sampler, polynomial-in-time mixing guarantee, new
separability domain or experimental implementation follows from this lemma.
