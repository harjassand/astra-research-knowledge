THEOREM: FINITE-NET BROADCASTING TO A BOUNDED CLASSICAL ALPHABET

Let E be any set of density matrices on a finite-dimensional H, and let
rho_1,...,rho_N in E be an internal trace-distance a-net of E, with N>=2.
Suppose a supplied CPTP map B:H->H tensor H has both marginal errors at most b
on every member of E, using T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. For every integer
L>=0 and mesh delta>0, there exists a full-domain EB channel Lambda_c:H->H
such that

  sup_(rho in E) T(Lambda_c(rho),rho)
    <= 2a + L b + sqrt(N ln(N)/(2*2^L)) + delta/2,                 (1)

and Lambda_c has at most

  K^N outcomes,  K = ceil(N/delta)+1.                              (2)

Thus a fixed-length classical record needs at most ceil(N log_2 K) bits.
No common sandwich, Holevo-capacity bound, or faithful reference state is
needed for this finite-cover statement. If the N net centers instead obey a
supplied faithful sandwich (1-theta)sigma <= rho_i <= (1+theta)sigma with
0<theta<1, the same proof improves K to ceil(2 theta/delta)+1, recovering
the stronger count in immutable INITIAL.txt.

PROOF AND EXACT INTERFACES

1. Finite-label extraction. Put K_tree=2^L and recursively apply B to every
leaf, giving C_L:H->H^(tensor K_tree). Every leaf marginal is a composition
of L marginal channels of B; contractivity and telescoping give error at most
L b on each rho_i. For a design prior pi on the N labels, adaptively measure
the leaves. At a history h before leaf j, write p_i(h) for the posterior,
tau_h=sum_i p_i(h) rho_i^h for the conditional state of that leaf, and
d_i(h)=T(rho_i^h,tau_h). Measure the two-outcome spectral sign projector of
rho_i(h)-tau_h for an index i maximizing p_i(h)d_i(h)^2. Classical Pinsker
and Jensen give

  I(X:Y_j | h) >= 2 max_i p_i(h)d_i(h)^2
                 >= (2/N) (sum_i p_i(h)d_i(h))^2.

The chain rule gives sum_(j=1)^K_tree I(X:Y_j|Y_<j)<=H(pi)<=ln N.
Therefore some leaf j has average conditional trace distance at most
sqrt(N ln(N)/(2 K_tree)). Measure only the prefix before that leaf and
prepare the conditional average state at the selected leaf. The preparation
depends on the known ensemble, B and measurement history, not the unknown
input label. Composing this decoder with C_L makes an EB channel on H. The
prior-average reconstruction bound holds for every prior; finite-dimensional
minimax over the compact convex EB-channel set gives one channel with the
same worst-label bound. Consequently there is an EB map Lambda on H with

  max_i T(Lambda(rho_i),rho_i)
    <= eta_(N,L)(b) := L b + sqrt(N ln(N)/(2*2^L)).                (3)

The factor N, the conditional estimate, the finite-label tree construction
and the minimax step are c05_l09's source claim. I reconstructed the steps
above to audit the constants and interfaces. Only the finite promised states
are controlled; this is not diamond-norm or full-channel approximation.

2. Remove dependence of the message alphabet on the tree decoder. Since
Lambda is finite-dimensional EB, choose any finite measure-and-prepare form

  Lambda(rho)=sum_y Tr(M_y rho) omega_y,
  M_y>=0, sum_y M_y=I, omega_y density matrices.

Use the net-average input state sigma_N=(1/N)sum_i rho_i, which need not be
faithful. Let p_0(y)=Tr(M_y sigma_N) and p_i(y)=Tr(M_y rho_i). Positivity and
rho_i<=N sigma_N imply p_i(y)<=N p_0(y). If p_0(y)=0, every p_i(y)=0; assign
q_i(y)=0. Otherwise set q_i(y)=p_i(y)/p_0(y), so every coordinate lies in
[0,N]. Partition each coordinate into half-open intervals of width delta
(with a closed final interval), and group outcomes with the same joint
quantization key. There are at most K^N cells for K=ceil(N/delta)+1.

For a cell J with p_0(J)>0, put

  M_J=sum_(y in J) M_y,
  omega_J = sum_(y in J) p_0(y) omega_y / p_0(J).

If p_0(J)=0, then p_i(J)=0 for every center; assign any density operator
omega_J. The grouped effects still sum to I, so Lambda_c is a full-domain EB
channel, including on inputs outside supp(sigma_N). For each center i with
p_0(J)>0, define qbar_i(J)=p_i(J)/p_0(J). It is the p_0-weighted average of
the q_i(y) values in J. All these values lie in a single interval of width
delta, hence |q_i(y)-qbar_i(J)|<=delta. Direct subtraction gives

  Lambda(rho_i)-Lambda_c(rho_i)
    = sum_J sum_(y in J) p_0(y)(q_i(y)-qbar_i(J)) omega_y.

Trace norm and positivity of omega_y imply ||Lambda(rho_i)-Lambda_c(rho_i)||_1
<=delta sum_y p_0(y)=delta. A zero-mass cell contributes zero on all net
states. Thus T(Lambda(rho_i),Lambda_c(rho_i))<=delta/2.

3. Extend to the covered family. If T(rho,rho_i)<=a, channel contractivity
and the triangle inequality give

  T(Lambda_c(rho),rho)
    <= T(Lambda_c(rho),Lambda_c(rho_i))
       +T(Lambda_c(rho_i),Lambda(rho_i))
       +T(Lambda(rho_i),rho_i)+T(rho_i,rho)
    <= a+delta/2+eta_(N,L)(b)+a.

This proves (1). No state outside E is used as a net center. The channel is
still defined on all of H because the grouped effects form a POVM.

QUANTITATIVE CONSEQUENCES AND RESOURCE BOUNDARIES

For b>0 choose L=ceil(log_2(N ln(N)/b^2)). Then the last term in (3) is at
most b/sqrt(2), and

  eta_(N,L)(b) <= b[log_2(N ln(N)/b^2)+1+1/sqrt(2)].             (4)

Up to the harmless integer ceiling, this is O(b(log N+log(1/b))) when
N>=2. Therefore for a sequence E_n, if there are nets with a_n->0 and
b_n log N_n(a_n)->0, then choosing L_n by (4) and delta_n->0 yields one EB
reconstruction channel with worst-state error tending to zero. This conclusion
needs neither the Bansil--Kachkovskiy whole-range lift nor a fixed-Holevo
assumption. It does require a trace-distance cover whose entropy is small
relative to 1/b. In the N61 copy ladder, c05_l09/c05_l10's sourced internal
calculation has b log N_E(a) bounded below asymptotically by log 2, so this
sufficient condition correctly fails; that ladder also violates the fixed
sandwich premise of the more economical INITIAL version.

The number of outcomes after coarsening is at most (ceil(N/delta)+1)^N;
with a fixed sandwich it is at most (ceil(2 theta/delta)+1)^N. The outcome
alphabet cap is independent of d and L. This is only a bound on message
cardinality. The tree uses 2^L-1 calls to B and 2^L output systems. The
minimax EB map and its finite decomposition may be very costly to discover;
grouping requires its effects/preparations and exact probabilities against
the net and sigma_N. Net discovery, channel-description length, numerical
precision, storage of POVM effects/preparations, runtime, sample complexity
and energy are not bounded. No efficient codec or hardware procedure follows.
