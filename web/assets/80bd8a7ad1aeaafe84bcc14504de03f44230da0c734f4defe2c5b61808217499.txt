CYCLE 4 LEAF — LARGER ALL-RATE WR STOCHASTIC FAMILY
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE. A physical-time Foster proof gives nonexplosion and positive recurrence for a larger weakly reversible family with arbitrary fixed positive rates. The proof is stochastic-generator based; deterministic permanence is not used. External proof review and novelty remain open.

RESULT

Add arbitrary reversible first-order conversion among the substrate species to the paired substrate/autocatalytic family. The resulting CTMC is still classwise positive recurrent for every fixed positive rate vector, without Cycle 3's strict linear LP. The useful Lyapunov function is a weighted linear B-count plus a small factorial-log potential on the A-counts and a constant correction on the all-A-zero face. The factorial-log term makes the high-order reverse channel dominate arbitrary forward autocatalysis at large A, while a small scale keeps the low-count forward channel below B-substrate removal. Reversible A-to-A conversion can increase the log potential on individual transitions, but its total positive drift is only O(A), dominated by A-death drift of order -A log A.

NETWORK AND EXACT STOCHASTIC INTERFACE

Take finite nonempty index sets J and I, species A_j (j in J) and B_i (i in I), positive integers k_ji>=1, and fixed positive rates alpha_j, delta_j, p_ji, q_ji, u_ji, v_ji. Include

  0 <-> A_j,                       rates alpha_j, delta_j,
  A_j <-> A_j+B_i,                 rates p_ji, q_ji,
  A_j+B_i <-> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i,     rates u_ji, v_ji.

In addition, choose any finite directed conversion graph E on J, with j!=l and a positive rate r_jl on each edge j->l; require every edge to have its reverse with positive rate. Add A_j <-> A_l. (An empty conversion graph recovers the earlier family.) Every reaction is in a reverse pair, so the graph is weakly reversible. The 0<->A_j and A_j<->A_j+B_i reaction vectors span every coordinate, so the stoichiometric lattice is full rank.

The CTMC is on N_0^(|J|+|I|) with standard source falling-factorial propensities. In particular, the high-order reverse channel has propensity v_ji(a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i and is disabled at a_j<=k_ji. All rates here are fixed constants; “all-rate” means arbitrary fixed positive values, not one Foster function uniform over an unbounded rate set.

THEOREM CANDIDATE

For every such finite network and every positive rate vector, the CTMC on its full lattice is nonexplosive and irreducible positive recurrent. No complex-balance or rate-feasibility condition is assumed. The proof constructs a proper V with LV<=C globally and LV<=-1 off a finite set.

Let A=sum_j a_j, B=sum_i b_i, and choose arbitrary weights w_i>0 (w_i=1 suffices). For n>=1 define

  Psi_ji(n) = u_ji log((n+k_ji)!/n!)
              - v_ji (n)_(k_ji+1)/n * log((n)_(k_ji))     if n>=k_ji+1,
            = u_ji log((n+k_ji)!/n!)                       if 1<=n<=k_ji.

Because the first term is O(log n), while the second is asymptotic to
v_ji*n^k_ji*k_ji*log n, Psi_ji(n)->-infinity. Thus
M_ji=max(0,sup_{n>=1} Psi_ji(n)) is finite and attained. Choose epsilon>0 so

epsilon*M_ji <= q_ji*w_i/2  for every (j,i).

Now set

  V(a,b)=epsilon*sum_j log(a_j!) + sum_i w_i*b_i + H*1_{A=0},

where H>0 is arbitrary. This V is nonnegative and norm-like: bounded V bounds every B_i and every log(a_j!), hence every population coordinate.

For n=a_j>=1, the coefficient of b_i in LV from the three A_j/B_i reaction pairs is exactly

  G_ji(n) = -q_ji*w_i*n
            + epsilon*u_ji*n*log((n+k_ji)!/n!)
            - epsilon*v_ji*(n)_(k_ji+1)*log((n)_(k_ji))
          = n*(-q_ji*w_i + epsilon*Psi_ji(n)),

with the last term zero when n<=k_ji. By construction,

  G_ji(n) <= -q_ji*w_i*n/2 < 0  for every n>=1.

This exact inequality closes the low-population stratum: if n<=k_ji the factorial reverse is zero, but epsilon was chosen so the forward factorial-log increment remains strictly dominated by q_ji substrate removal. For large n the high-order reverse supplies the negative term that makes Psi tend to minus infinity.

The only correction H*1_{A=0} changes on immigration from A=0 to A=e_j and on death from A=e_j to A=0. The generator has three exhaustive strata.

1. A=0. Every catalytic and conversion channel is disabled. Since log(1!)=log(0!)=0, each 0->A_j jump changes V by -H. Hence

     LV = -H*sum_j alpha_j < 0,

independently of how large B is. The frozen face is not absorbing.

2. A=1, with sole A_j molecule. The death A_j->0 adds at most H*delta_j; conversion transitions and immigration contribute only finite constants. The reverse high-order channel is disabled, but the G inequality gives

     LV <= C_j - (1/2)*sum_i q_ji*w_i*b_i.

Since I is nonempty, this is negative outside a finite B-set. This also covers ties among all enabled channels: the expression is the full generator sum.

3. A>=2. The indicator is unchanged by every one-step reaction: a unit A-death from A=2 leaves A=1, and a high-order reverse reaction leaves one A_j in its product. Thus the correction contributes zero. Write

     D(a)=sum_j a_j*log(a_j),   with 0*log(0)=0,
     delta_* = min_j delta_j > 0,
     q_* = min_(j,i) q_ji*w_i > 0,
     P_* = max_j sum_i p_ji*w_i,
     R_* = max_j sum_(l:j->l in E) r_jl.

Immigration contributes at most epsilon*(sum_j alpha_j)*log(A+1). A->A+B births contribute at most P_* A. By convexity, D(a)>=A*log(A/|J|). The total positive contribution from substrate conversions is bounded by

  epsilon*R_* * sum_(j:a_j>0) a_j*log((A+1)/a_j)
    <= epsilon*R_* A*log(2|J|),        A>=1.

Indeed, for each edge j->l the potential increment is
epsilon*(log(a_l+1)-log(a_j)), which is at most
epsilon*log((A+1)/a_j); sum over edges and use
sum_j a_j log(a_j) >= A log(A/|J|). All A-death terms sum to
-epsilon*sum_j delta_j*a_j log(a_j) <= -epsilon*delta_* A log(A/|J|).
The B-dependent pair terms satisfy

  sum_(j,i) b_i*G_ji(a_j) <= -(q_*/2)*A*B.

Consequently, for A>=2,

  LV <= epsilon*(sum_j alpha_j)*log(A+1)
        + (P_*+epsilon*R_*log(2|J|))*A
        - epsilon*delta_* A*log(A/|J|)
        - (q_*/2)*A*B.

As A grows, the negative A log A term dominates all positive O(A) terms. For bounded A>=2, the negative A B term dominates as B grows. This gives both a finite global upper bound on LV and strict negative drift outside a finite set. Together with strata 1 and 2 this proves the claimed Foster inequalities.

PHYSICAL-TIME NONEXPLOSION, IRREDUCIBILITY, RECURRENCE

Nonexplosion is not inferred from an event-chain estimate. The same coercive V has LV<=C. For sigma_n=inf{t:V(X_t)>=n}, the pre-exit set {V<n} is finite, and its one-step successor set is finite because the network has finitely many fixed jumps. Thus the stopped chain has bounded rates and stopped Dynkin is valid. For n>V(x),

  n*P_x(sigma_n<=T) <= E_x[V(X_(T wedge sigma_n))]
                         <= V(x)+C*T.

Hence no path can exit every V-sublevel set by a fixed finite time. An explosion while remaining in one finite set is impossible because its total exit rate is bounded. This localization works even though the log-factorial potential can have state-dependent jump sizes under A_j<->A_l; bounded global V-jumps are not assumed.

All enabled transitions remain in the nonnegative lattice by source-factorial semantics. To reach zero from any state, if A=0 first immigrate one A_j; then use A_j+B_i->A_j to drain every B_i and A_j->0 to drain all A. From zero, immigrate the desired A counts, create any desired B_i using A_j->A_j+B_i (temporarily create a catalyst if needed), then remove temporary A molecules. These are finite paths of positive transition rates. The full lattice is therefore one closed irreducible class. Nonexplosion plus the strict finite-set Foster drift gives positive recurrence and an invariant probability in physical time.

ADVERSARIAL TESTS AND LIMITS

The main potential failure was a substrate-conversion stratum with a rare source species and a populous destination: at a_1=1,a_2=N, the transition A_1->A_2 adds epsilon*log(N+1) to V. This is unbounded on that ray, so a naive “conversion has bounded drift” assertion would be false. The correct sum bound above charges it to O(A); the A_2 death term is -epsilon*delta_2*N*log N and dominates. If B is also large, every active A_j contributes a strictly negative G_ji(a_j)b_i term. If A=0 and B is large, the constant boundary correction gives negative drift. Thus these apparent gaps are closed by the actual inequalities, not by ignoring the boundary.

A second tempting gap is to cite deterministic ODE permanence. That would not establish a CTMC result, because falling-factorial reverse propensities vanish on integer boundary strata. Here those strata are explicitly handled in G_ji(n), and the localized generator proof is for the physical-time CTMC. No deterministic theorem is used.

Exact scope: J,I and E are finite; all six rate arrays and conversion rates are fixed positive constants; k_ji are finite positive integers; every A_j/B_i pair has both substrate-removal and high-order reverse channels. The Lyapunov scale epsilon may be very small and depends on rates and k. No uniform return-time, stationary-tail, mixing, rate-family-uniformity, or effective bit-complexity bound is proved. The finite supremum defining epsilon is mathematically attained since Psi tends to minus infinity, but an efficient exact synthesis procedure for it is not claimed. Dropping q_ji>0, v_ji>0, or the corresponding channels invalidates the proof; no nonrecurrence claim follows from that failure.

DECISIVE CHECK: LOW-COUNT AND CONVERSION RAYS

For J=2, I=1, k_11=1, k_21=2 and any positive rates, the B coefficient is

  G_11(1) = -q_11*w_1 + epsilon*u_11*log 2,
  G_21(1) = -q_21*w_1 + epsilon*u_21*log 6,

because both high-order reverse propensities vanish at one substrate molecule. Choosing epsilon below each q*w/(2u*log-factor) makes both strictly negative. At n>=k+1 the negative term is respectively
v_11*n(n-1)*log n and v_21*n(n-1)(n-2)*log(n(n-1)); each dominates its forward log increment. For the conversion stress ray (a_1,a_2,b_1)=(1,N,M), A_1->A_2 contributes r_12*epsilon*log(N+1), while the general conversion bound is at most epsilon*R_* A log(4); A death contributes at most -epsilon*delta_* A log(A/2), and the B drift at most -(q_*/2)A M. The bound has the correct signs simultaneously as N or M grows. This is a hand-checked exact asymptotic inequality, not a finite simulation or a proof by sampling.

PRIOR ART AND STATUS

Established baselines include product-form stationary laws and nonexplosion for complex-balanced stochastic mass action. The new candidate covers arbitrary positive rate vectors in this explicit higher-order family, not arbitrary weakly reversible networks. It is an internal proof candidate; external correctness, overlap with unpublished tier/Foster arguments, and novelty/priority are unresolved. No field-breaking or general-WR conclusion is claimed.
