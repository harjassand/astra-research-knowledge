# Cycle 10 Grothendieck transfer audit

Date: 2026-10-08. Focus: whether noncommutative Grothendieck, little-GT, or ordinary 2-summing factorization improves the quadratic estimator penalty while retaining a legal measurement on the supplied two-output system and the canonical barycenter.

## Verdict

No theorem-level transfer was found. The standard results control bilinear forms or Hilbert-valued maps by states/positive functionals and operator-algebra row/column norms. The needed object is more constrained: one finite POVM on the actual \(AB\) output, with estimators unbiased for every active frame coordinate and total quadratic outcome cost at most \(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q\). The noncommutative-GT factor \(2\) is a bilinear/factorization constant; it is not the order coefficient \(2\) together with the required negative \(-\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q\) term.

An exact sub-POVM completion lemma shows how a successful rounded construction would be made legal. Conversely, a rank-one example gives a factor \(d-1\) gap between ordinary 2-summing size and canonical support. This is an obstruction to the direct relaxation/rounding bridge, not a refutation of the target. The arbitrary-rank factor-two claim and existence of a legal counterexample remain **UNKNOWN**.

## Fixed legal interface and sufficient estimator gate

Let \(\Phi:M_d\to M_d\) be unital, trace-preserving, CP and Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint, and let a symmetric CP broadcaster \(\mathcal B:M_d\to M_d\otimes M_d\) be supplied with both marginals equal to \(\Phi\). Write \(\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/d\), \(\sigma_{AB}=\mathcal B(I/d)\), and hence \(\sigma_A=\sigma_B=I/d\). For an active positive dual choose an orthonormal Hermitian frame

\[
Q=\sum_{i=1}^r w_i|A_i\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A_i|,
\quad w_i>0,\quad \tau(A_i)=0,\quad \tau(A_iA_j)=\delta_{ij}.
\]

For an \(AB\)-POVM \(E=(E_y)\), let \(M_y=\mathcal B^*(E_y)\), \(p_y=\tau(M_y)\), and \(t_{iy}=\tau(M_yA_i)\). These effects already have the input POVM normalization \(\sum_yM_y=I\). A sufficient, exact estimator interface is

\[
\sum_y f_i(y)M_y=\Phi(A_i)\quad(1\le i\le r),\qquad
\sum_iw_i\sum_y p_y f_i(y)^2\le\sum_iw_i.
\tag{G*}
\]

For each \(p_y>0\), \(t_{iy}^2/p_y\ge2t_{iy}f_i(y)-p_yf_i(y)^2\). Summing and using Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjointness gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
S_d(Q)&\ge\sum_iw_i\sum_y\frac{t_{iy}^2}{p_y}\\
&\ge2\sum_iw_i\tau(A_i\Phi(A_i))-\sum_iw_i\\
&=2\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Phi)-\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus the quantitative gap that a Grothendieck-style rounding must close is exactly the **classical quadratic estimator cost** in (G*). A norm factorization alone does not establish (G*).

## Exact legal finite completion for rounded data

There are two useful completion interfaces.

**An \(AB\) sub-POVM.** If rounding returns finite \(E_y\succeq0\) with \(\sum_yE_y\preceq I_{AB}\), set \(E_0=I_{AB}-\sum_yE_y\). Then \(M_y=\mathcal B^*(E_y)\), including \(M_0=\mathcal B^*(E_0)\), are positive and sum exactly to \(I\). Assign \(f_i(0)=0\); this leaves every first-moment identity and the estimator cost unchanged. For \(p_y=\tau(M_y)>0\), define \(\rho_y=M_y/(d p_y)\). Then

\[
\sum_y p_y\rho_y=\frac1d\sum_yM_y=I/d.
\]

So every *actual positive sub-POVM* on \(AB\), plus its estimator values, has an exact finite canonical input realization and retains the physical source \(\sigma_{AB}\) and its marginals. If a mixed input effect \(M_y\) is spectrally refined and the same \(f_i(y)\) is copied to its refined outcomes, both its first moments and its estimator penalty are preserved exactly.

**A state-valued moment/Gram law.** If rounded data already give finite density matrices \(\rho_y\succeq0\), probabilities \(p_y\ge0\), \(\sum p_y=1\), and exact barycenter \(\sum p_y\rho_y=I/d\), then \(M_y=dp_y\rho_y\) is the required finite POVM. More generally, a measure with that barycenter can be compressed by Carathéodory in the finite vector of barycenter plus second-moment coordinates; this retains the induced canonical map. Positive Gram coordinates without state atoms and the barycenter equation do not meet this interface.

This state-valued completion yields a canonical input POVM only. It does not assert that its effects have preimages \(E_y\succeq0\) under \(\mathcal B^*\), so it does not preserve the supplied \(AB\) realization or source \(\sigma_{AB}\). For the fixed broadcaster, the exact interface that automatically preserves those constraints is an actual \(AB\) sub-POVM (or POVM), as in the preceding paragraph.

There is also an exact completion when an unconstrained state-valued rounding has barycenter \(\sigma\ne I/d\). Let it have finite atoms \(q_j,\rho_j\), with \(\sum_jq_j\rho_j=\sigma\). Choose any \(0\le s\le1/(d\|\sigma\|_\infty)\). Then \(s\sigma\preceq I/d\); diagonalize \(R=I/d-s\sigma=\sum_k r_kP_k\succeq0\). The weights \(sq_j\) and \(r_k\) form a probability measure on density matrices with barycenter \(I/d\). Its finite canonical POVM is

\[
M_j=d s q_j\rho_j,\qquad M_k'=d r_kP_k,
\]

and these sum to \(I\). The original quadratic contribution is retained only with multiplier \(s\); the residual adds a nonnegative support contribution. In the worst case \(\sigma\) is pure, so \(s\le1/d\). This completion makes the barycenter legal but supplies no dimension-free preservation of the Gram score.

## Exact gap for ordinary 2-summing relaxation

For a rank-one dual \(Q=|A\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A|\) with \(\tau(A)=0\) and \(\tau(A^2)=1\), define the scalar map \(T_A:S_1^d\to\mathbb C\) by \(T_A(X)=\operatorname{Tr}(AX)\). Its ordinary 2-summing norm equals its operator norm, because a scalar-valued functional is 2-summing with \(\pi_2(T_A)=\|T_A\|\). Trace duality gives

\[
\pi_2(T_A)^2=\|A\|_\infty^2.
\]

For every \(d\ge2\), take

\[
A_d=\operatorname{diag}\left(\sqrt{d-1},-\frac1{\sqrt{d-1}},\ldots,-\frac1{\sqrt{d-1}}\right).
\]

Then \(\operatorname{Tr}A_d=0\), \(\tau(A_d^2)=1\), and \(\pi_2(T_{A_d})^2=d-1\). By contrast, the canonical support is exactly \(S_d(|A_d\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A_d|)=1\): the spectral PVM attains \(\tau(A_d^2)=1\), while any POVM score is at most its averaged second moment \(\tau(A_d^2)\). Equivalently, the unconstrained pure seed at the top eigenvector has score \(d-1\), but its mass in a barycenter-\(I/d\) ensemble is at most \(1/d\), since \(pP\preceq I/d\). The full pure-seed quadratic moment is therefore not canonical support.

This exact \(d-1\) separation means ordinary 2-summing/Pietsch data cannot simply be relabeled as a canonical moment law. A new theorem would need a positivity-and-barycenter-preserving rounding, or a direct way to output the \(AB\) sub-POVM and estimators in (G*). Carathéodory only compresses data *after* the barycenter/positivity constraints are satisfied; it does not impose them on a generic Gram factorization.

## What the Grothendieck theorems actually return

The ordinary noncommutative Grothendieck theorem for a bounded bilinear form \(u:\mathcal A\times\mathcal C\to\mathbb C\) on \(C^*\)-algebras gives states \(f_1,f_2,g_1,g_2\) with

\[
|u(x,y)|\le\|u\|\bigl(f_1(x^*x)+f_2(xx^*)\bigr)^{1/2}
                  \bigl(g_1(y^*y)+g_2(yy^*)\bigr)^{1/2}.
\]

Its equivalent sequence form controls \(\sum_j u(x_j,y_j)\) by the product of the row/column \(C^*\)-norms. For a Hermitian common frame \(x_j=y_j=\sqrt{w_j}A_j\), that estimate has right-hand side \(2\|u\|\|\sum_jw_jA_j^2\|_\infty\). It is an upper bound on the bilinear correlation in an operator norm geometry. It neither lower-bounds the canonical support nor yields a legal POVM. The factor \(2\) here is a coefficient of that sequence estimate; the associated general map-to-dual Hilbert factorization has \(\|R\|\|S\|\le2\|T\|\). Neither is the order formula in (G0).

The noncommutative little-GT theorem for \(T:\mathcal A\to H\) gives states \(f_1,f_2\) such that

\[
\|T(x)\|^2\le\|T\|^2\bigl(f_1(x^*x)+f_2(xx^*)\bigr).
\]

This is an upper state domination of the Hilbert-valued map. The variance gate instead asks for a common tracial-barycenter ensemble whose *averaged variances are bounded above by the channel's Dirichlet energy*, or equivalently for estimators satisfying (G*). Reversing that inequality is not licensed by little GT.

The Effros--Ruan jointly-completely-bounded Grothendieck theorem has constant one relative to the **jcb norm**, but still returns four states, not effects \(E_y\) with a first-moment identity and a cost bound. No dimension-free bound on the relevant jcb norm together with a marginal-preserving POVM realization was derived here. Invoking its constant one without those two bridges would omit both a norm hypothesis and the outcome/barycenter construction.

For the natural form \(u_\Phi(A,B)=\tau(A\Phi(B))\), the ordinary bilinear norm is at most one by trace duality and channel contraction. The sequence Grothendieck estimate then sees \(2\|\sum_iw_iA_i^2\|_\infty\), not the canonical support \(S_d(Q)\). For \(A_d\) above, this \(C^*\)-norm is \(d-1\) while \(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q=1\) and \(S_d(Q)=1\). The estimate is therefore not on the required scale even in rank one. This is a failure of this transfer, not of the target, whose rank-one case has a direct spectral-PVM proof.

## Exact random-spectral check against the penalty gate

The parent cycle note's one-scalar sphere sketch is a useful calibration: for \(u\) uniform on \(S^{r-1}\), measure the spectral PVM of \(G_u=\sum_i\sqrt{w_i}u_i(A_i\otimes I+I\otimes A_i)\), and use \(f_i(u,k)=r u_i\lambda_{u,k}/(2\sqrt{w_i})\). Its first moments are unbiased under \(\mathbb E u_iu_j=\delta_{ij}/r\), and every effect is an actual pullback through \(\mathcal B^*\). The exact penalty is

\[
P=\frac r4\sum_iw_i\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\sigma_{AB}
 (A_i\otimes I+I\otimes A_i)^2\right]\le r\sum_iw_i,
\]

using the two fixed tracial marginals and state Cauchy--Schwarz. Thus this valid estimator gives \(S_d(Q)\ge2\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Phi)-r\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q\). It does not prove (G0) except at rank one. The rank factor is in the quadratic estimator covariance, not in POVM legality. No lower bound showing this rank factor is unavoidable for all collective estimators was established.
