c03_l08 — exact norm contraction with an honest overlap/cost improvement
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Its INITIAL is at outputs/round6/c03_l08/INITIAL.txt, not its work directory.
Read that INITIAL, full REVISION_01, and the updated code. Given exact F=AB
of rank r, Cauchy--Binet gives det F_IJ=sum_S det A_IS det B_SJ. Squaring
introduces two coordinate indices S,T. The four exterior-factor transfer over
three mutually exclusive site states sums
 wedge A_I tensor wedge B_J tensor conjugate(wedge A_I)
                         tensor conjugate(wedge B_J).
Terminal coordinates (S,S,T,T), |S|=|T|=k give exactly c_k. The wedge insertion
signs depend on the occupied coordinate masks, not on a hidden planar ordering.
This algebra handles arbitrary complex entries and exact cancellations.

The two A replicas always have equal degree p, and the two B replicas equal
degree q. For requested k, higher degrees cannot contribute and may be pruned.
Thus at most [sum_(p<=min(k,r))binom(r,p)^2]^2 states are reachable. Each site
transition has O(r^2) terms per state. All sectors cost
O(n r^2 binom(2r,r)^2) arithmetic operations, with exact polynomial bit work
per operation. The stated sum is O(16^r/sqrt(r)^2); no n^{O(r)} enumeration is
hidden. At r=0 the constant vacuum sector is handled separately from an r^2
notation that vanishes literally.

Pivot-column factorization acquires F=AB in polynomial rational bit time;
common pivot determinant/cofactor denominators have polynomial height even
when ill-conditioned. A term uses at most 2n factor entries, and the number
of coordinate/site choices adds O(n log(r+1)) bits. Forward/backward linear
messages give exact nonnegative prefix masses even though intermediate entries
need not be positive. Exact integer branch draws need expected constant
rejection attempts; they are expected-time exact sampling. A capped finite-bit
approximation wrapper needs an explicitly charged abort/fallback budget.

The scalar perturbation lemma is also sound: the disjoint-minor vector is a
coordinate projection of the compound matrix Lambda^k F; its Frobenius change
is at most sqrt(binom(n,k)) times the operator change, which is bounded by
k||E||op(||F||op+1)^(k-1). Dividing by sqrt(c_k) makes clear that approximate
rank alone is not a relative-error acquisition certificate. This agrees with
the independent precision failure in c03_s02 revisions.

Compared with c03_s03, rank-r inputs were already admitted because both ordered
cross ranks are <=r. The four-factor norm contraction lowers the displayed
conservative global-rank exponent (16^r versus 64^r for a D^3 contraction
with D<=4^r), without adding a new tractable class. Actual implementations may
exploit extra structure; no universal fastest-runtime or novelty claim follows.
Its support-graph exterior route duplicates c03_s01. Score 2 (reusable direct
contraction/cost refinement), not a second independently new class.
