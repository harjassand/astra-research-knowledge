THEOREM: COMPLETE INTERFACE

For every integer d>=2, let tau=Tr/d on M_d. Let Phi:M_d -> M_d be unital,
completely positive and tau-preserving. Suppose

  Phi(U X U^T)=U Phi(X) U^T for every real orthogonal U in O(d),

and suppose Phi is self-compatible: an actual UCP map
J:M_d tensor M_d -> M_d has J(X tensor I)=J(I tensor X)=Phi(X).

Then there is an orthogonal-orbit measure-and-prepare map Psi, unital,
tau-preserving, positive selfadjoint on L2(tau), such that

  I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi)                                      (T)

in the order of selfadjoint operators on L2(tau). This is a whole-matrix-
algebra comparison. The constant 2 is sharp uniformly in every d>=2.

This theorem covers all O(d)-covariant self-compatible channels. Covariance
forces L2-selfadjointness; positivity as a SUPEROPERATOR is unnecessary
for (T) or the reconstruction estimate, but is retained for the power
corollary below. In the Schrodinger interface a covariant CPTP channel is
automatically bistochastic because the natural representation is
irreducible. In d=4, full O(4), rather than SO(4), is required: the latter has
additional invariant antisymmetric subspaces. No claim is made that an
arbitrary channel can be reduced to this symmetry at negligible cost.

1. THE TWO NONTRIVIAL ORTHOGONAL SECTORS

The complex-linear representation X -> U X U^T decomposes as scalars,
symmetric traceless matrices, and antisymmetric matrices. Each nontrivial
summand is irreducible under the full O(d), and they are inequivalent. This
holds also for d=2 and d=4; including reflections matters. Their Hermitian
real forms are real symmetric traceless matrices S and imaginary
antisymmetric matrices A, of dimensions

  n_s=(d-1)(d+2)/2,             n_a=d(d-1)/2.

One elementary verification of irreducibility uses diagonal sign matrices:
their characters isolate each off-diagonal coordinate pair. Permutations
then move each pair to all others, and a rotation of a coordinate plane
mixes a symmetric off-diagonal matrix with its diagonal traceless partner.
If an invariant symmetric subspace contains only diagonal matrices, a
coordinate-plane rotation first produces a nonzero off-diagonal pair.
The antisymmetric coordinates are moved transitively by permutations.
The sign/rotation argument also shows a commuting endomorphism must be
scalar on each sector; its off-diagonal and diagonal symmetric scalars
coincide. The scalar sector is fixed by unitality.

Thus Phi is scalar lambda_s on S and lambda_a on A. Hermiticity preservation
makes both scalars real, hence Phi is automatically L2-selfadjoint. The UCP
Schwarz contraction gives -1<=lambda_s,lambda_a<=1. Equivalently
Phi(X)=aX+bX^T+c Tr(X)I, with a=(lambda_s+lambda_a)/2,
b=(lambda_s-lambda_a)/2 and c=(1-lambda_s)/d. No separate Choi-domain
classification is needed for the proof.

2. EXACT EB ORBIT GEOMETRY

Fix a unit vector psi in C^d and set z=|psi^T psi|^2 in [0,1]. For Haar
probability on O(d), put P_U=U|psi><psi|U^T and define

  Psi_z(X)=d int_O(d) Tr(P_U X) P_U dU.                   (1)

This is the canonical rank-one POVM dP_U dU followed by preparation P_U.
The natural O(d) representation is irreducible over C, so int P_U dU=I/d.
Consequently (1) is UCP, tau-preserving, and entanglement breaking. Moreover

  <X,Psi_z X>_tau = int |Tr(P_U X)|^2 dU >=0,

so it is positive selfadjoint on L2(tau). Its sector eigenvalues are obtained
from the squared pure-state coordinates in a Hermitian tau-orthonormal
basis. If P=|psi><psi|, its symmetric and antisymmetric Hermitian components
are (P+P^T)/2 and (P-P^T)/2. Since Tr(PP^T)=z, their coordinate captures are

  S_a=d(1-z)/2,             S_s=d(1+z)/2-1.

Schur orthogonality therefore gives

  mu_a=(1-z)/(d-1),
  mu_s=[d(1+z)-2]/[(d-1)(d+2)].                           (2)

Every z in [0,1] is realized explicitly by

  psi_z=sqrt[(1+sqrt(z))/2] e_1
          + i sqrt[(1-sqrt(z))/2] e_2.                   (3)

Thus the canonical EB sector pairs form precisely the displayed segment.
Only this sufficient segment is used; no classification of all EB maps is
asserted as a new result.

3. SELF-COMPATIBILITY GIVES THREE NECESSARY CORRELATION BOUNDS

Let omega_RAB be the normalized Choi state of the supplied two-output
channel, the Schrodinger adjoint of J. For every Hermitian matrix B,

  Tr[omega (B_R^T tensor B_A tensor I)] = tau(B Phi(B)),

and the same identity holds with A replaced by B. For a tau-orthonormal
Hermitian basis of a sector F, define its star operator

  W_F=sum_(H in F) H_R^T tensor (H_A tensor I+I tensor H_B).

Hence

  Tr(omega W_s)=2n_s lambda_s,
  Tr(omega W_a)=2n_a lambda_a.                            (4)

No covariance, symmetry, or independence is assumed of the joint extension.
These identities and elementary spectral bounds apply to the actual state.

Let P denote the normalized maximally entangled projector on a pair of
d-dimensional registers, and F the pairwise swap. A complete Hermitian
tau-orthonormal basis obeys sum H^T tensor H=d^2P. Transposition has signs
+1 on S and -1 on A. Including the scalar I in the symmetric sum gives

  sum_S H^T tensor H = (d/2)(dP+F)-I,
  sum_A H^T tensor H = (d/2)(dP-F).

Write P_sum=P_RA+P_RB and F_sum=F_RA+F_RB. Therefore

  W_a=(d^2/2)P_sum-(d/2)F_sum,
  W_s=(d^2/2)P_sum+(d/2)F_sum-2I,
  W_all=d^2 P_sum-2I.                                    (5)

The two entangled-projector ranges have cross overlap 1/d, so
||P_sum||=1+1/d. Applying (4) to W_all yields

  (d+2)lambda_s+d lambda_a <= d+2.                        (6)

4. EXACT ALL-d STAR SPECTRA: THE THREE CONTRACTION COPIES

For v in C^d introduce

  x(v)=|Omega>_RA tensor v_B,
  y(v)=|Omega>_RB tensor v_A,
  w(v)=v_R tensor |Omega>_AB,

where each |Omega> is normalized. Let C be their span. Their Gram matrix
on each fixed coordinate of v has diagonal entries 1 and off-diagonal
entries 1/d. It is positive for d>=2. Thus dim C=3d, and C is a direct sum
of d copies of this three-dimensional coefficient space. It is invariant
under all pairwise swaps and entangled projections. For example the exact
actions are

  P_sum x=x+y/d,       P_sum y=x/d+y,    P_sum w=(x+y)/d,
  F_sum x=x+w,         F_sum y=y+w,      F_sum w=x+y.

Because the operators are selfadjoint, C-perp is invariant. On C-perp all
three entangled projections vanish, and ||F_sum||<=2. Consequently

  W_a|_(C-perp) <= d I,
  W_s|_(C-perp) <= (d-2)I.                                (7)

On the output-antisymmetric vector x-y, P_sum has eigenvalue 1-1/d and
F_sum has eigenvalue 1. Thus W_a has eigenvalue d(d-2)/2 there, and W_s has
eigenvalue d^2/2-2.

On the output-symmetric span u=x+y,w, the coefficient matrices (columns
are images of u,w) are

  P_sum = [[1+1/d, 1/d], [0, 0]],
  F_sum = [[1, 1], [2, 0]].

They act in the inherited nonorthogonal Gram metric. Substitution into (5)
gives

  W_a = [[d^2/2, 0], [-d, 0]],
  W_s = [[d^2/2+d-2, d], [d, -2]].                        (8)

Their eigenvalues are respectively {d^2/2,0} and

  L_plus,minus(d)=[d^2+2d-8 +/- d sqrt(d^2+4d+20)]/4.

For d>=2, d^2/2 dominates both d and d(d-2)/2; L_plus dominates both d-2
and d^2/2-2. Thus these are the exact maxima of W_a and W_s. Equations
(4) imply

  lambda_a <= d/[2(d-1)],
  lambda_s <= L_plus(d)/[(d-1)(d+2)]
           <= (d+4)/[2(d+2)].                            (9)

The last inequality reduces to sqrt(d^2+4d+20)<=d+4. Its squared difference
is 4(d-1)>=0. The unused spectral parts need not be classified for (9).
ORTHOGONAL_CERTIFICATE.py checks (8), its characteristic polynomials, the
Gram selfadjointness, and full-matrix spectra in small dimensions; those
checks audit transcription, while the preceding analytic argument covers
all dimensions.

5. EXPLICIT ROUNDING AND THE FACTOR-TWO COMPARISON

Put u_a=max(0,2lambda_a-1) and u_s=max(0,2lambda_s-1). From (9),

  0<=u_a<=1/(d-1),           0<=u_s<=2/(d+2).

Choose z=1-(d-1)u_a in (3). Formula (2) gives

  mu_a=u_a,
  mu_s=(2-d u_a)/(d+2).                                  (10)

Both mu values are nonnegative. Indeed d u_a<=d/(d-1)<=2. If u_a=0,
(9) says mu_s=2/(d+2)>=u_s. If u_s=0, nonnegativity suffices. If both
are positive, (6) becomes

  (d+2)u_s+d u_a<=2,

which is exactly mu_s>=u_s. Thus mu_a>=2lambda_a-1 and
mu_s>=2lambda_s-1 on both nontrivial sectors, and identity is fixed. This
proves (T) over the entire complex Hilbert space L2(tau).

6. SHARPNESS AND THE EQUALLY-INFORMED CONTROL

The universal symmetric 1-to-2 d-dimensional cloner is an actual channel
with both marginals the depolarizing map

  Phi_cl(X)=lambda X+(1-lambda)tau(X)I,
  lambda=(d+2)/[2(d+1)].

For completeness its Schrodinger joint map is
  rho -> [2/(d+1)] P_sym (rho tensor I) P_sym,
where P_sym=(I+F)/2. Its trace is Tr rho; direct partial tracing gives the
stated marginals. It meets every hypothesis, including full unitary hence
orthogonal covariance and L2 positivity.

If any EB comparator obeys I-Psi<=c(I-Phi_cl), average it over all unitary
conjugations. EB and the operator inequality are preserved, and the
averaged map is depolarizing with parameter mu. The normalized Choi state
of an EB map is separable, whose overlap with the maximally-entangled
projector is at most 1/d. For a depolarizing map that overlap is
[1+(d^2-1)mu]/d^2; hence mu<=1/(d+1). The inequality then forces

  c >= [1-1/(d+1)]/[1-(d+2)/(2(d+1))] = 2.

At this cloner our construction has z=2/(d+1) and
mu_s=mu_a=1/(d+1), so it attains the control. The earlier N92 depolarizing
factor-two theorem is therefore an equally-informed baseline and the
sharpness example, not a new contribution here.

7. EXPERIMENT RECONSTRUCTION AND A SCOPED POWER LIFT

For Hermitian H with tau(H)=0 and ||H||_infinity<=theta, let
rho_H=(I+H)/d. Let r=sup_H ||Phi(H)-H||_(1,tau) for any chosen family.
Since Psi is positive selfadjoint with spectrum in [0,1],

  ||(I-Psi)H||_2^2 <= <H,(I-Psi)H>
       <=2<H,(I-Phi)H>
       <=2 theta ||(I-Phi)H||_1.

Normalized-trace Cauchy-Schwarz now proves, uniformly using one POVM,

  sup_H T(Psi(rho_H),rho_H) <= (1/2)sqrt(2 theta r).        (11)

The central-reference weighted direct-sum version follows by applying the
same argument blockwise with the faithful normalized weighted trace; it
requires the supplied block-preserving interface. No capacity or number of
states enters (11).

For the N93 POSITIVE-SUPEROPERATOR subclass, every integer m>=0 has the
following power lift. E_m=Psi Phi^m is EB. For Hermitian ||X||<=1,

  ||E_m(X)-Phi^m(X)||_1^2
    <=2 <Phi^m X,(I-Phi)Phi^m X>
    <=2 max_(0<=t<=1) t^(2m)(1-t)
    =2 (2m)^(2m)/(2m+1)^(2m+1) <=2/(2m+1),              (12)

with 0^0=1 at m=0. The maximization over [0,1] requires Phi's nonnegative
L2 spectrum. Signed eigenvalues of arbitrary covariant channels are not
covered by this power estimate. This is a dimension-free infinity-to-L1
lift on the positive subclass; it does not establish the unrestricted gate.

8. STABILITY UNDER APPROXIMATE COVARIANCE: ACTUAL RESIDUAL IS CHARGED

Let Phi now meet the tracial, selfadjoint, self-compatible
interface without exact covariance, and let T_U(X)=U X U^T. Define

  barPhi=int T_U^-1 Phi T_U dU.

The average is O(d)-covariant and preserves all the other hypotheses:
twirl the actual joint channel with the same U on both outputs to retain
compatibility. Define the ACTUAL directional symmetry residual

  delta_E=sup_(H in E) ||(barPhi-Phi)H||_(1,tau).

Applying the theorem to barPhi yields a common EB Psi with

  sup_H T(Psi(rho_H),rho_H) <= (1/2)sqrt[2theta(r+delta_E)]. (13)

No smallness of delta_E follows from broadcasting or capacity alone. A
supplied covariance certificate eta= int ||Phi T_U-T_U Phi||_(infty->1) dU
implies delta_E<=theta eta; proving or acquiring eta is a separate charged
task. Alternatively a supplied L2-operator certificate
||barPhi-Phi||_(2->2)<=epsilon gives the whole-space relaxed comparison

  I-Psi<=2(I-Phi)+2epsilon I,

and error at most (1/2)sqrt[2theta r+2epsilon theta^2]. These formulas make
approximate covariance quantitative, but do not assume it for free.

The orbit integral is an exact explicit EB construction. A finite exact
realization exists with at most d^4 outcomes: the unnormalized Choi moment
d int P_U^T tensor P_U dU lies in the convex hull of compact trace-d
rank-one separable matrices in a real affine space of dimension at most
d^4-1. Caratheodory gives weights w_i>=0, sum w_i=1 and points U_i, with
effects d w_i P_Ui and preparation P_Ui. Equality of the first partial
trace proves POVM completeness. This is a size bound and existence proof,
not an efficient exact-cubature acquisition algorithm. In the continuum
description apparatus size is the orbit; in a finite realization the
output size can grow as d^4, and exact real-number precision is not free.

the sector operator error is at most 2|error(lambda_a)| times

the finite POVM/preparations introduces an additional uniform CPTP
