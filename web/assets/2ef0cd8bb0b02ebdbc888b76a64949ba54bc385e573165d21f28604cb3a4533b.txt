2. EXACT LOCAL HESSIAN FORMULA AND NECESSARY COMMUTANT INVARIANCE

This section applies to any finite faithful sigma and any channel meeting
the assigned KMS hypothesis. T is HS-self-adjoint, fixes R=sqrt(sigma),
and has spectrum in [-1,1]; the contraction follows either from the
stationary UCP KMS-adjoint argument or its finite-dimensional spectral

For any Hermitian trace-zero A, put C=S^(-1)AS^(-1); the trace constraint
is <R,C>_HS=0. Along rho_t=sigma+tA the first nonzero coefficients are

The logarithm derivative in a sigma eigenbasis multiplies entry A_ij by
(log s_i-log s_j)/(s_i-s_j), with 1/s_i on the diagonal. The square-root
derivative multiplies A_ij by 1/(sqrt(s_i)+sqrt(s_j)). Consequently
 J_2=<C,(DF+FD)C/2>_HS,  E_2=<LC,DLC>_HS,                 (2)
where the real diagonal superoperators F,L have matrix-unit coefficients
 F_ij=sqrt(s_i s_j)(log s_i-log s_j)/(s_i-s_j),
 L_ij=(s_i s_j)^(1/4)/(sqrt(s_i)+sqrt(s_j)).
At equal eigenvalues F_ij=1 and L_ij=1/2. For unequal eigenvalues set
u=|log(s_i/s_j)|/2>0, obtaining F_ij=u/sinh u and
L_ij=1/(2cosh(u/2)). Formula (2) is derived from the actual state Hessian,
not a claimed operator Jensen inequality.

If J>=4E for EVERY faithful rho for this fixed channel, necessarily
 Q_4=(DF+FD)/2-4LDL >=0 on R^perp.                         (3)
Let U be the Hermitian commutant of sigma. On U, F=I,L=I/2, so Q_4 has
EXACTLY ZERO quadratic form on U intersect R^perp. For an unequal-
eigenvalue coherence v and u_0 in U intersect R^perp,
 <u_0,Q_4 v>=[(1+u/sinh u)/2-sech(u/2)]<u_0,Dv>.         (4)
The bracket is strictly positive. Indeed with w=u/2>0, multiplying it by
2cosh(w)sinh(w) gives
 g(w)=(1/2)sinh(2w)-2sinh(w)+w,
and g(0)=0, g'(w)=2cosh(w)(cosh(w)-1)>0.
For a PSD quadratic form, a vector with zero quadratic value has all cross
terms zero. Therefore (3),(4) force every <u_0,Dv>=0. D(R)=0 deals with
the remaining reference direction, so D, equivalently T, must preserve
the ENTIRE commutant of sigma. Multiplication by S preserves that same
commutant, making this also commutant invariance of H/Phi in their natural

Thus universal FOUR for a fixed KMS-reversible channel has the exact LOCAL
necessary condition of sigma-commutant invariance. It is not asserted

The channel in (1) violates it: its nonzero U_X/U_Z coupling makes Q_4
