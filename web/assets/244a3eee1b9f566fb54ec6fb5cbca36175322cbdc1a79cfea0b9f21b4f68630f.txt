1. EXACT STATIONARY SECTOR LAW
In spin j_b with K=2j_b, write r=m+j_b=0,...,K. Birth and death rates are
 a_r=Gamma nu (r+1)(K-r),
 d_r=Gamma(nu+1)r(K-r+1).
For K>=1 they form an irreducible birth/death chain. Put z=nu/(nu+1) in(0,1).
Detailed balance gives the UNIQUE stationary population law
 pi_K(r)=z^r/S_K(z), S_K(z)=sum_(r=0)^K z^r.
For K0 use pi_0(0)=1. Every sector retains its original weight q_b.
Let mu_K=sum_r r z^r/S_K. Then
 E_infty=beta+sum_b q_b mu_(2j_b)>=beta,
 R_infty=sum_b q_b sum_r pi_K(r)r(K-r+1).                  (1)
The mean equation, derived directly from the two Lindblad terms, is
 d<E>/dt=Gamma[nu N-2nu<E>-<R>].
At stationarity this gives the exact identity
 R_infty=nu(N-2E_infty)<=2nu jbar.                         (2)
The signs follow from J_-J_+=J_+J_--2Jz. The stationary mean is <=N/2,
as is also clear from the decreasing geometric population law.

3. EXACT GAP COMPARISON
For K>=1 every nonzero ladder factor r(K-r+1) and(r+1)(K-r) is>=K.
Compare the chain to a reflecting constant-rate walk on0,...,K with births
Gamma K nu and deaths Gamma K(nu+1). It has the SAME pi_K. The Dirichlet
form on every edge of the full chain dominates that of this walk, so its
spectral gap is no smaller.

For constant a,b>0 the reversible tridiagonal matrix has eigenvalues0 and
 a+b-2sqrt(ab)cos[l pi/(K+1)], l=1,...,K.
For completeness: symmetrize by sqrt(pi); the interior diagonal is a+b,
the end diagonals are a and b, and off-diagonals are-sqrt(ab). For
theta=l pi/(K+1), the nonzero eigenvector components
 g_r=sin[(r+1)theta]-sqrt(b/a)sin(r theta)
satisfy the interior equation and both reflecting boundary equations. These
K distinct vectors plus the zero vector sqrt(pi) account for all K+1 eigenvalues.
Consequently the full sector gap gamma_K obeys
 gamma_K>=Gamma K(sqrt(nu+1)-sqrt(nu))^2
          >=Gamma K/(4nu+2).                              (4)
The final reciprocal bound uses2sqrt[nu(nu+1)]<=2nu+1. No numerical spectral
scan, large matrix calculation or infinite-N extrapolation enters (4).

Here pi_min=z^K/S_K, so

This controls R and E even for initially coherent inputs; only populations
are used, not a claimed trace-distance bound on all quantum coherences.

Always E(t)>=beta. Since0<=R<=M_N=floor((N+1)^2/4), (5) implies
