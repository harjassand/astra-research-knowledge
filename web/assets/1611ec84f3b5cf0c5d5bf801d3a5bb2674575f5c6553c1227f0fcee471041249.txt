ANTISYMMETRIC LOCAL BROADCASTING LADDER — ROOT CANDIDATE v1
Internal direct derivation. No formal or external certification or novelty claim.
Created independently of all Cycle05 local-reference worker proofs.

CONTRACT AND RESULT
T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2; all logarithms natural.
For a bipartite state rho_AB let b_loc be the infimum over full-domain CPTP
B:B->B tensor B of max_j T((id_A tensor B_j)rho,rho), and let e_loc be the
infimum over EB CPTP Lambda:B->B of T((id_A tensor Lambda)rho,rho).
For every integer L>=1, the construction below has
 b_loc <= 1/L, e_loc >= 1/2,
 I(A:B) = (1/L) sum_(j=0)^(L-1) ln[d(2^j+1)/(d-2^j)], d=2^L,
 (L-1)ln2/2 <= I(A:B) <= (L-1)ln2/2 + 4/L.
It therefore rules out a universal bound
 e_loc^2 <= K(I(A:B)+1)b_loc^alpha
for ANY fixed finite K and alpha>1. It does NOT rule out a modulus at fixed
mutual information, any alpha<=1, or the programme's state-family theorem.

CONSTRUCTION
Let V=C^d, H_B=direct_sum_(j=0)^(L-1) wedge^(n_j)V, n_j=2^j.
Write P_r for the orthogonal projection from V^tensor r onto wedge^r V and
a_r=P_r/binom(d,r). Regard a_(n+1) as a state on V tensor wedge^n V.
Set rho_AB=(1/L) direct_sum_j a_(n_j+1). The direct sum is on B only.
The k-particle marginal of a_r is a_k for every k<=r: it is supported in
wedge^k V and invariant under U(d), whose wedge^k representation is
irreducible. Normalization fixes the scalar. Alternatively this follows by
counting how many r-subsets contain each k-subset in the wedge basis.
In particular rho_A=I/d and rho_B=(1/L) direct_sum_j I_j/binom(d,n_j).

ACTUAL LOCAL CPTP BROADCASTER
First pinch the B sector flag. For sector j>=1, use the natural isometry
 wedge^(2n)V -> wedge^n V tensor wedge^n V,
obtained by grouping the first and last n tensor factors in the standard
antisymmetric tensor embedding. Both output flags are j-1. The input is
already in the two antisymmetric subspaces, so grouping preserves its norm.
For sector j=0, discard the input and prepare omega tensor omega, where
omega is any fixed state in sector0=V. Pinching plus these maps defines a
CPTP channel on ALL operators on H_B, including arbitrary off-diagonal
sector coherences; no restriction to the displayed states is imposed.
On rho_AB the two marginal outputs agree. Every j>=1 block maps to the
original j-1 block a_(n_(j-1)+1)/L. The bottom block maps to tau_A tensor
omega/L in output sector0. Thus the output minus rho is exactly
 (tau_A tensor omega in sector0 - a_(n_(L-1)+1) in sector L-1)/L.
For L>=2 the two terms have orthogonal B supports and each trace1, so the
displayed channel has marginal error EXACTLY1/L. For L=1 this support
argument does not apply, but contractivity still gives error<=1=1/L.

EB LOWER BOUND, ARBITRARY EB CHANNELS
Define the full-domain CPTP decoder Q:B->V by pinching the flag and retaining
one fermion in that block, via its tensor embedding and partial trace.
Then (id_A tensor Q)rho_AB=a_2. For any EB Lambda on B,
(id_A tensor Q Lambda)rho_AB is separable across A:V. For a product density
u tensor v, Tr[P_2(u tensor v)]=(1-Tr uv)/2<=1/2. Convex combinations and
limits preserve this inequality. Since Tr P_2 a_2=1, the binary effect P_2
and trace contraction give
 T(rho_AB,(id_A tensor Lambda)rho_AB)
 >=T(a_2,(id_A tensor Q Lambda)rho_AB)>=1/2.
This proves the lower bound against every EB channel, not just covariant
ones, and does not assume the decoder is EB.

MUTUAL INFORMATION, EXACT
S(rho_A)=ln d,
S(rho_B)=ln L+(1/L)sum_j ln binom(d,n_j),
S(rho_AB)=ln L+(1/L)sum_j ln binom(d,n_j+1).
Their difference is the displayed formula. Substitute d=2^L to write
 I=(L-1)ln2/2+(1/L)sum_j[ln(1+2^-j)-ln(1-2^(j-L))].
Every summand in the correction is positive. The first sum is <=2 by
ln(1+x)<=x; the second is <2 since -ln(1-x)<=2x for0<=x<=1/2 and
sum_j2^(j-L)<1. This proves the claimed bounds. Consequently for alpha>1,
K(I+1)b_loc^alpha <=K[((L-1)ln2/2)+4/L+1]L^-alpha ->0,
contradicting e_loc^2>=1/4 for largeL.

CONDITIONAL FAMILY AND THE QUANTUM REFERENCE GAP
For a rank-one outcome |psi><psi| on A, the normalized conditional B state
is eta_psi=(1/L)direct_sum_j P_(wedge^n_j psi^perp)/binom(d-1,n_j).
One can see this by contracting the first leg of each wedge basis tensor
in a basis with psi as its first vector: subsets not containing psi vanish,
and the surviving n-subsets lie in psi^perp with equal weights.
Let sigma=rho_B. Each eta_psi has relative entropy
 D(eta_psi||sigma)=(1/L)sum_j ln[d/(d-n_j)]=:c_L<=2/L.
The continuous Haar average of eta_psi is sigma. All eta_psi have the same
entropy. The information-radius bound gives Holevo capacity <=c_L, and
the Haar prior gives equality. The finite-dimensional convex hull contains
sigma, so a finite prior also attains it (Caratheodory suffices).
The replacement channel X->Tr(X)sigma is EB and has uniform error
 T(eta_psi,sigma)=(1/L)sum_j n_j/d=(1-1/d)/L.
For each block, uniform density on a rank-r subspace versus uniform density
on rank-N space has half-trace distance1-r/N, and r/N=(d-n_j)/d.
Thus even a rank-one informationally complete conditional family may have
vanishing capacity and vanishing reconstruction error, while the joint
quantum-reference error of EVERY local EB channel is at least1/2.
This calculation is not a counterexample to a theorem whose norm is only
statewise output trace distance.
