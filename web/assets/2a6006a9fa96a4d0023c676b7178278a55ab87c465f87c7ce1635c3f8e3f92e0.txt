A POSITIVE MARGINAL AND A GLOBAL NO-GO COEXIST
The six-site completion in REVISION_01 has two distinct four-site pinnings:

1. Pin the new hole variables 4,5 to zero, so both new coordinates are retained. Seven cross-coordinate auxiliaries make this four-site slice even-windable, with the exact witness in REVISION_01/02.
2. Pin the new coordinates as holes, i.e. restrict to hole sets S union {4,5}. The remaining retained coordinates lie entirely in the old four-site block. The resulting signature is exactly the original F4 hole signature, including f(empty)=4, f(all)=1, and pairing capacities (0,1,1); its defect remains -2.

The current executable check confirms the second slice coefficient by coefficient for the seven-edge fixture: `pin_both_new_coordinates_as_holes_signature` equals the original F4 signature and has Delta=-2. Thus the full extended signature is still not even-windable. A witness for one four-site pinning cannot be promoted to a witness for the six-site object.

GENERAL PINNING OBSTRUCTION
This conclusion holds for any number of appended coordinates under the following exact interface:
- the old 4x4 principal block of F' is the F4 witness matrix;
- auxiliary additions are coordinate-pair operators with nonnegative activities;
- the target is an even-windable boundary signature on all old and new coordinates.

Pin every appended coordinate as a hole. In the determinant-hole definition, the retained rows and columns are then only the old four, so the unextended pinned signature is exactly F4, regardless of cross-block entries of F'. An auxiliary edge touching an appended coordinate contributes zero on this pin because that endpoint is already a hole. The remaining old-only auxiliary edges act on F4 by the four-site matching transform. REVISION_01 proves its Delta is invariant under every such transform, hence it stays -2 and is not even-windable. McQuillan's pinnings/2-decomposition criterion requires every pinning of an even-windable signature to be even-windable. Also, McQuillan's matching-circuit lemma together with the windable/even-windable equivalence for even-supported functions makes even-windability necessary for a matching-circuit realization here. Therefore no full positive matching-circuit lift in this class can repair the embedded F4 obstruction, even if another pinning has a positive witness.

This is a scoped obstruction, not an arbitrary sampler impossibility. It applies only when F4 remains as an exact old principal block, the extension is represented by determinant-hole coefficients, and auxiliary additions are coordinate-pair operators. It does not rule out a representation that changes the old block, a hidden-state construction that does not expose this pinning, or a non-matching local energy flow.

STATUS / NEXT GATE
The local positive auxiliary transform has a conserved obstruction; cross-coordinate coupling can change a different pinned marginal and make that marginal windable; but the bad F4 pin survives every such extension preserving the old block. To escape this obstruction, a proposed algorithmic representation must explain how it removes or never exposes that pinning while preserving the desired scalar partition function, and must give acquired transition/weight data and an integrated two-replica energy bound. No such construction is supplied here. Coefficient acquisition, sampler, FPRAS, energy/congestion, and priority remain open.
