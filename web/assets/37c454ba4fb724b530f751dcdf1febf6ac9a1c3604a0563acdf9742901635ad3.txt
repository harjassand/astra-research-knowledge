PRINCIPAL-CARTAN CAP FILTER FOR REGULAR SU(3) HIGHEST WEIGHTS
Date: 2026-10-08

Purpose and status
------------------

This is an independent cap/taper along the principal Cartan element
H=diag(1,0,-1), distinct from the E_11 Gelfand--Tsetlin-axis filter in
filter_construction.txt. For N>=16 and N<=a,b<=2N, it gives a positive
semidefinite witness of rank at most D-Q, with a checked energy upper bound
of order (Q/D)^(2/3). Here

  D=dim V_(a,b)=(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)/2.

The rank is D-V_R, where V_R is the first whole principal-Cartan cap with
at least Q weights; it can be less than D-Q because the cap-volume rounding
overshoots Q. Thus this proves the rank-at-most constraint. It does not give
an exact-rank D-Q witness when Q is not a cap volume.

The uniform estimates below are derived analytically. The accompanying
principal_cartan_filter_check.py uses GT-pattern enumeration and Fraction
arithmetic for finite checks; those checks do not replace the derivation.

1. H-weight cap and exact dimensions
------------------------------------

Realize the SU(3) highest weight (a,b) as the polynomial U(3) highest row
(a+b,b,0). A GT pattern has rows (a+b,b,0), (u,v), (n), with

  b<=u<=a+b,  0<=v<=b,  v<=n<=u.

Use integer coordinates

  x=a+b-u,  y=b-v,  z=u-n.

Then 0<=x<=a, 0<=y<=b, 0<=z<=a-x+y. The three diagonal weight entries are

  m_1=a+b-x-z,  m_2=b-y+z,  m_3=x+y.

Consequently the H-eigenvalue is a+b-q, where

  q=2x+y+z.

For 0<=q<=m:=min(a,b), every nonnegative solution of 2x+y+z=q is a valid
GT pattern: the coordinate bounds follow from q<=a,b, and
z<=a-x+y follows from q<=a+x+2y. Thus the exact shell dimension is

  g_q=dim W_q = sum_(x=0)^floor(q/2) (q-2x+1)
      = floor((q+2)^2/4),

so g_(2r)=(r+1)^2 and g_(2r+1)=(r+1)(r+2). The cap nullity through shell R
is

  V_R=sum_(q=0)^R g_q
      = (r+1)(r+2)(4r+3)/6,  R=2r,
        (r+1)(r+2)(4r+9)/6,  R=2r+1.

Use V_(-1)=0. For an integer Q>=1, choose the least R>=0 with V_R>=Q;
this is the explicit integer cutoff/rounding rule.

2. Exact commutator crossing conductances
-----------------------------------------

Let W_q be the H-weight shell. In the GT basis the simple-coroot values
within that shell are

  h_12=a-x+y-2z,  h_23=b-x-2y+z,  h_13=a+b-q.

Symmetry between y and z in the solutions to 2x+y+z=q gives
  sum_(W_q) x + sum_(W_q) y = q g_q/2.
Therefore the exact shell traces, valid for 0<=q<=m, are

  s_12(q)=Tr_(W_q)(E_11-E_22)=(a-q/2)g_q,
  s_23(q)=Tr_(W_q)(E_22-E_33)=(b-q/2)g_q,
  s_13(q)=Tr_(W_q)(E_11-E_33)=(a+b-q)g_q.

For ij in {12,23,13}, define the source-shell conductance

  c_ij(q)=Tr_(W_q)(E_ji E_ij),

where E_ij raises the H-weight, hence lowers q by r_ij=1 for 12,23 and by
2 for 13. Using E_ij^*=E_ji, the commutator
[E_ij,E_ji]=E_ii-E_jj, and cyclicity of trace between adjacent shells,

  c_ij(q+r_ij)-c_ij(q)=s_ij(q).

At the top c_12(0)=c_23(0)=c_13(0)=c_13(1)=0. Iteration gives the exact
crossing formulas (all indices used below lie in the cap-valid range):

  c_12(q)=sum_(p=0)^(q-1) (a-p/2) floor((p+2)^2/4),
  c_23(q)=sum_(p=0)^(q-1) (b-p/2) floor((p+2)^2/4),

  c_13(q)=sum_{0<=p<=q-2, p congruent q (mod 2)}
                    (a+b-p) floor((p+2)^2/4).

The first two formulas apply for 1<=q<=m; the third applies for
2<=q<=m+1. Define the combined one-step conductance

  c(q)=c_12(q)+c_23(q)
      =sum_(p=0)^(q-1) (a+b-p)g_p.

For m=min(a,b), 1<=q<=m, direct termwise bounds give

  m q(q+1)(2q+1)/48 <= c_12(q), c_23(q)
      <= m(q+1)(q+2)(2q+3)/12.

Indeed m<=a,b<=2m, and for p<q one has
m/2<=a-p/2,b-p/2<=2m, while
(p+1)^2/4<=g_p<=(p+2)^2/4. In particular each one-step conductance is
Theta(m q^3). For 2<=q<=m+1, the two-step crossing satisfies the uniform
two-sided estimate

  m q^3/128 <= c_13(q) <= (2/3)m q^3.

For the upper bound, S=a+b<=4m and g_p<=(p+2)^2/4; sum over all p<q.
For the lower bound, S-p>=m on p<=q-2. For q>=8 retain terms p of the
required parity in [ceil(q/2),q-2]; there are at least q/8 such terms and
each g_p>=q^2/16. The cases 2<=q<=7 follow by direct inspection of the
parity sums. Another useful exact comparison is

  c_13(q) <= c(q-1),  2<=q<=m+1,

because c_13(q) is a parity sub-sum of c(q-1), and all cap terms are
positive.

3. Taper, positivity, and exact rank accounting
------------------------------------------------

Assume N>=16 and N<=a,b<=2N. Set m=min(a,b), and suppose

  1<=Q<=floor(D/512).

Choose R as above and set B=R+1 and M=2B. Then M<=m. To see this, for
m>=16 the dimension formula gives D<=(7/2)m^3, while
V_(R-1)>=R^3/12. Minimality of R and Q<=D/512 imply
R^3 < (42/512)m^3, hence R<7m/16 and M=2(R+1)<=m.

Define the resistance and profile

  rho=sum_(q=R+1)^M 1/c(q),

  alpha_q=1                                      for q<=R,
          rho^(-1) sum_(j=q+1)^M 1/c(j)         for R<q<M,
          0                                      for q>=M.

Let A act by alpha_q on W_q and put X=I-A. Since all c(q)>0, alpha is
nonincreasing from 1 to 0, with strict decrease over every taper edge.
Thus X is positive semidefinite, its nullspace is exactly the cap q<=R,
and

  nullity(X)=V_R>=Q,   rank(X)=D-V_R<=D-Q.

The integer cutoff is explicit; the possible excess nullity is exactly
V_R-Q. This whole-shell choice is the point where the rank inequality, not
exact rank equality, is used.

4. Energy in the Hermitian-generator normalization
---------------------------------------------------

Use Tr_fund(T_A T_B)=delta_AB/2 and
E(X)=sum_A ||[T_A,X]||_HS^2. For diagonal X, the Cartan generators
commute. The three positive-root pairs have H-steps 1,1,2. The two
Hermitian generators built from E_ij,E_ji contribute exactly the
conductance c_ij times the squared difference across that root edge (there
is no additional factor of two under this normalization).

The 12 and 23 root pairs combine to the one-step path conductance c(q).
Since alpha_(q-1)-alpha_q=1/(rho c(q)) for R+1<=q<=M, their total energy
is exactly

  E_1=sum_(q=R+1)^M c(q)(alpha_(q-1)-alpha_q)^2=1/rho.

For the 13 root, alpha_(q-2)-alpha_q is the sum of the two consecutive
one-step increments. By (u+v)^2<=2u^2+2v^2 and c_13(q)<=c(q-1), its
energy obeys E_13<=4 E_1. Consequently

  E(X)=E_1+E_13 <= 5/rho.

The one-step profile is the exact energy minimizer for its fixed endpoints
on this weighted path; the 13-root term is bounded separately above.

5. Uniform rate and comparison with E_11
----------------------------------------

For B=R+1 and B<=q<=M=2B,

  c(q)<= (a+b)V_(q-1) <=4m V_(2B-1) <=12mB^3.

The last inequality follows from
V_(2B-1)=B(B+1)(4B+5)/6<=3B^3. There are B+1 terms in rho, hence
rho>=1/(12mB^2), and E(X)<=60mB^2. Also

  ||X||_HS^2 >= D-V_(M-1) >= D-V_(m-1) >= 7D/8.

Here D>=m^3 and, for m>=16,
V_(m-1)<= (m+1)(m+2)(2m+3)/24 <=m^3/8. Therefore

  E(X)/||X||_HS^2 <= (480/7) m(R+1)^2/D
                    < 743 (Q/D)^(2/3).

For the last step, V_(R-1)>=R^3/12 and V_(R-1)<Q give
R+1<=(12^(1/3)+1)Q^(1/3); also D>=m^3. The exact formulas and this
uniform bound show a principal-Cartan rate O((Q/D)^(2/3)). In particular,
at Q=1 and D=Theta(N^3), this gives O(N^-2).

For scale comparison, filter_construction.txt gives for the E_11 filter
the coarser bound

  24/N + 24 sqrt(2) sqrt(Q/D).

Thus the principal cap has the better small-Q/D exponent; at Q=1 its
asymptotic upper rate is N^-2 instead of the E_11 bound's N^-1 term. The
constant 743 is deliberately coarse and does not by itself prove uniform
numerical dominance over the exact E_11 taper throughout the full range.
The finite exact comparisons below are additional examples, not a
uniform-domination theorem.

6. Reproducible finite checker
------------------------------

Run from the repository root:

  python3 outputs/research/spectral_geometry/su3_regular/principal_cartan_filter_check.py

The checker enumerates all (x,y,z) GT patterns for selected (a,b), verifies
the dimension, cap shell counts and Cartan traces, builds the conductances
from the trace recurrences with exact rational arithmetic, checks the
two-sided conductance bounds, applies the rounded taper, and verifies
positivity, nullity/rank, norm, E_1=1/rho, E_13<=4E_1, and the stated coarse
energy/norm bound. It also computes the exact finite normalized ratio of
the existing E_11 harmonic taper from its GT E_11 shell counts.

Representative output (balanced a=b=N):

  N=64,  Q=1:   principal 0.00087346711; E_11 exact 0.023031773
  N=64,  Q=536: principal 0.095244497;   E_11 exact 0.096847157
  N=128, Q=1:   principal 0.00022354195; E_11 exact 0.011579109
  N=128, Q=4192: principal 0.093246692;   E_11 exact 0.097794363

All listed values are finite exact-rational computations converted to
decimal for display; they validate only those instances. No Sym^N occupation
formula is used anywhere in the derivation or checker.
