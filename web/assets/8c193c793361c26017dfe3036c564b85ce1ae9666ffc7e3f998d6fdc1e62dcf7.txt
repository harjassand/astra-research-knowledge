on the FULL real Hermitian HS space, with ONE pure canonical EB channel.
No extra spectral gap and no separate comparator per direction is used.
The algebraic finite theorem allows arbitrary exact complex B. The effective
compiler theorem assumes its dense Choi matrix is supplied as exact rational
complex entries, and delta is supplied as a rational bit input.

The compiler returns at most T+d outcomes, T=ceil(64d^2/delta^2), each an

THE NEW MECHANISMS

1. Fixed rational rank-one informationally complete leaf, with covariance
inverse <=rI, r=2d-1. The operational leaf needs no Phi eigenbasis. Kadison-
Schwarz and trace preservation imply Hermitian HS contraction of Phi, so
its self-adjoint spectrum lies in [-1,1]; positive HS is not an extra premise.

2. Only the modes needed for the C4 deficit are analyzed, lambda>3/4. The
correlated sibling covariance recursion gives a FULL Schur lower bound.
Its finite loss is <=r(8/9)^m. Negative/critical modes are harmless because
4lambda-3<=0 there. This removes the near-1/sqrt(2) nonuniformity without
adding a supplied gap. The +1 endpoint is included, with 2^-m decay.

3. Rational Choi LDL produces a single-Kraus instrument. Retaining all
internal instrument labels and rank-one leaf labels makes every input effect
rank one. Its scalar square roots factor out and cancel in the pure posterior.
Thus the raw finite comparator is pure canonical without a limit argument.

4. Any partial fine record has its probability computed by d-by-d CP-adjoint
messages up the tree, with unassigned labels summed locally. Correlations
are retained; no sibling independence is assumed. All event probabilities
share a polynomial-height rational denominator. Exact conditional sampling
does not enumerate the exponentially large raw alphabet or use postselection.

5. Independently sampled posteriors give an empirical covariance. Scale its
effects by 1/(1+epsilon) and LDL-refine the exact PSD residual to complete a
rank-one POVM. Accept only after its complete common-channel quadratic test.
Frobenius second moments give a >=1/2 batch-success guarantee. The scale
and residual repair the empirical mean obstruction rather than pretending
the sample mean equals I.

6. Canonical POVM channels satisfy an explicit probability-free Lipschitz
bound, <=3 sum_z||F_z-G_z||_1, and <=3d times measurement diamond error.
This handles rare outcomes in a fixed matched instrument without a free
minimum-probability premise. Finite gate implementation explicitly measures
all, discards the original registers, and prepares a fresh output, so it is
exactly EB and diamond-close to the exact rational canonical comparator.
Exact canonicality/unitality/HS-self-adjointness belong to the ideal map.

BOUNDED CONFIDENCE VARIANT

The Las Vegas construction can also be capped without ever returning an
uncertified channel. For requested failure probability kappa, take
q=ceil(log2(2/kappa)) batches and cap every integer sampler at
j=ceil(log2(2N/kappa)) proposals, N=qT(2K-1). A coupling to the uncapped
algorithm gives batch failure <=2^-q and any capped draw failure <=N2^-j;
their sum is <=kappa. The algorithm returns either a certified comparator
or an explicit failure, with deterministic polynomial cost in the input
lengths,d,1/delta,log(1/kappa). The input bit length of kappa must also be
read and charged. No elapsed time is treated as a successful certificate.
