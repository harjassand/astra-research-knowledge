CYCLE 3 LEAF — MULTI-CATALYST CERTIFICATE THRESHOLD
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact face-drift obstruction and the
displayed complex-balanced example; CTMC behavior outside the certificate in
general remains UNKNOWN. This report is not a weakly-reversible recurrence
counterexample.

RESULT

For the specified reversible family, the proposed positive linear-weight
certificate is exactly the strict LP below. Its face inequalities are forced
by two rays. In the one-catalyst/one-B subcase, the strict threshold is

                 k sigma rho < mu tau.                       (SG-1)

If this inequality fails, no positive linear weights can give negative
linear-order generator drift on both rays. This proves failure of that
certificate only. It does not imply transience, null recurrence, or explosion.

There is an exact three-species counterexample to necessity of the LP: take two
catalysts A_1,A_2, one species B, k_11=k_21=2, and the same rates for each j:

  0 <-> A_j:             (birth, death) = (2, 1)
  A_j <-> A_j+B:         (birth, removal) = (1, 1)
  A_j+B <-> 3A_j+B:      (growth, return) = (4, 1).

Under standard stochastic mass-action propensities with falling factorials,
this network is irreducible and complex balanced at c=(2,2,1). The product
Poisson law Pois(2) x Pois(2) x Pois(1) is detailed-balanced; the chain is
non-explosive and positive recurrent. Nevertheless, no positive linear
weights satisfy the strict face LP: each j would require simultaneously
u_j>v and v>8u_j. Thus the certificate is sufficient, not necessary; this
parameter point is not an actual recurrence boundary or counterexample.

1. MODEL AND GENERATOR

Let j=1,...,J index A species and i=1,...,I index B species. All constants
below are fixed and strictly positive; k_ji is a positive integer. For state
(x,z) in N_0^J x N_0^I, the directed channels and stochastic propensities are

  0 -> A_j                         alpha_j,
  A_j -> 0                         delta_j x_j,
  A_j -> A_j+B_i                   sigma_ji x_j,
  A_j+B_i -> A_j                   tau_ji x_j z_i,
  A_j+B_i -> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i       rho_ji x_j z_i,
  (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i -> A_j+B_i       eta_ji (x_j)_(k_ji+1) z_i.

Here (x)_m=x(x-1)...(x-m+1), and a reaction y->y' with constant c has
propensity c (x)_y, with no factorial normalization. The first two reactions
have unit stoichiometric change in A_j; the last pair changes A_j by +/-k_ji.
Every arrow is paired with its reverse, so the finite reaction diagram is
reversible (hence weakly reversible). The top reverse channel has molecularity
k_ji+2.

For positive weights u_j,v_i, set V(x,z)=sum_j u_j x_j+sum_i v_i z_i. Direct
application of the physical-time CTMC generator gives

 LV = sum_j u_j(alpha_j-delta_j x_j)
     +sum_{j,i} v_i(sigma_ji x_j-tau_ji x_j z_i)
     +sum_{j,i} k_ji u_j(rho_ji x_j z_i
                         -eta_ji (x_j)_(k_ji+1) z_i).       (1)

This formula includes the actual jump rates; it is not an embedded-chain drift.

2. EXACT BOUNDARY-RAY DRIFTS AND THE CERTIFICATE THRESHOLD

Write e_j and f_i for coordinate unit vectors. On the catalyst axis
(x,z)=(n e_j,0), all B-dependent and autocatalytic channels are disabled, so

 LV(n e_j,0)=sum_l u_l alpha_l
              +n(-u_j delta_j+sum_i v_i sigma_ji).           (2)

Thus a strict negative linear-order drift on every catalyst ray requires

                 u_j delta_j > sum_i v_i sigma_ji  for each j.  (3)

On a mixed ray (x,z)=(e_j,n f_i), the reverse autocatalytic channel is disabled
because (1)_(k_ji+1)=0. All other terms are constant in n, and

 LV(e_j,n f_i)=C_j(u,v)+n(-v_i tau_ji+k_ji u_j rho_ji),    (4)

where C_j(u,v)=sum_l u_l alpha_l-u_j delta_j+sum_l v_l sigma_jl.
Consequently strict negative linear-order drift on every mixed ray requires

                 v_i tau_ji > k_ji u_j rho_ji  for each (j,i). (5)

The exact positive linear-weight certificate tested here is therefore

  find u_j>0,v_i>0 such that
      delta_j u_j - sum_i sigma_ji v_i > 0                 (all j),
      tau_ji v_i - k_ji rho_ji u_j > 0                    (all j,i). (LP)

Conditions (3) and (5) are necessary for this V to have a negative coefficient
of n on the specified rays. For every pair (j,i), they imply the strict
pairwise threshold

                 delta_j tau_ji > k_ji sigma_ji rho_ji.    (6)

Indeed, (3) implies delta_j u_j>sigma_ji v_i, while (5) implies
tau_ji v_i>k_ji rho_ji u_j; multiplying and cancelling u_j v_i proves (6).
If (6) fails for even one pair, LP is infeasible. The converse of these
pairwise inequalities is not asserted when several B species share a row:
the row sum in (LP) can create an additional multivariate small-gain
obstruction. LP itself, rather than pairwise checks alone, is the exact
certificate condition.

For J=I=1 this is sharp for LP feasibility: the open interval
  k rho u/tau < v < delta u/sigma
is nonempty exactly when k sigma rho<delta tau. At equality the strict LP is
still infeasible, and at equality at least one face slope can only be zero
under the corresponding non-strict inequalities. The threshold is a boundary
of this linear-weight drift certificate, not a proven phase boundary of the
CTMC.

On the pure B ray (x,z)=(0,n f_i), every reaction except the inflows 0->A_j
is disabled, and

                 LV(0,n f_i)=sum_j u_j alpha_j > 0,         (7)

independently of n. Hence V cannot satisfy the usual Foster condition
LV<=-epsilon outside a finite set on the entire state space, even when LP
holds. A separate tier/holding-time or nonlinear argument would be needed to
turn these face inequalities into a global recurrence theorem. This does not
make the process transient: it only records the limitation of this V.

3. EXACT THREE-SPECIES COMPLEX-BALANCED POINT OUTSIDE LP

Use J=2,I=1 and, for j=1,2,

  k_j=2; alpha_j=2; delta_j=1; sigma_j=1; tau_j=1;
  rho_j=4; eta_j=1.

At c_A1=c_A2=2,c_B=1 each reversible pair balances separately in the
deterministic mass-action system:

  alpha_j = delta_j c_Aj = 2,
  sigma_j c_Aj = tau_j c_Aj c_B = 2,
  rho_j c_Aj c_B = eta_j c_Aj^3 c_B = 8.

So c is a positive detailed-balanced, hence complex-balanced, equilibrium.
The stochastic rates use the falling factorial convention from Section 1;
the displayed deterministic balance equations use ordinary monomials, as
required for complex balance.

For arbitrary u_1,u_2,v>0, equations (2), (4), and (7) become

  LV(n e_j,0) = 2(u_1+u_2)+(v-u_j)n,
  LV(e_j,n)   = 2(u_1+u_2)+(v-u_j)+(8u_j-v)n,
  LV(0,n)     = 2(u_1+u_2).                                (8)

The first ray forces u_j>v; the second forces v>8u_j. No positive weights
can satisfy both. Equivalently, delta_j tau_j=1 while k_j sigma_j rho_j=8,
so (6) fails by a factor of eight.

The full state space N_0^3 is one irreducible class. To reach the origin,
reduce A counts using A_j->0; when both A counts are zero and B>0, first create
one A_j and then repeatedly use A_j+B->A_j. To reach an arbitrary target,
create a catalyst, adjust B using A_j<->A_j+B, then adjust the A counts with
0<->A_j (choosing the desired enabled transitions). Every finite path has
positive probability.

The stationary product-Poisson law is

  pi(a_1,a_2,b)=exp(-5) (2^a1/a1!) (2^a2/a2!) (1/b!).       (9)

For a reversible pair y<->y' with constants c_+,c_-, the product-Poisson
edge fluxes agree because

  pi(x)c_+(x)_y = C c_+ c^y c^(x-y)/(x-y)!,
  pi(x+y'-y)c_-(x+y'-y)_(y')
                 = C c_- c^(y') c^(x-y)/(x-y)!,

and pairwise complex balance is c_+c^y=c_-c^(y'). This verifies detailed
balance directly. The finite expected total jump rate under (9) is 48:
for each j the six directed channel fluxes are 2,2,2,2,8,8. In particular,
the fourth-order reverse propensity has finite Poisson expectation
E[(A_j)_3 B]=2^3*1=8. Anderson–Cappelletti–Koyama–Kurtz prove that
complex-balanced stochastic mass-action networks are non-explosive; applying
that theorem (or its finite-stationary-flux criterion) and irreducibility gives
positive recurrence of every state. The exact example therefore proves
certificate non-necessity, and nothing about an actual recurrence boundary.

4. CHECK FOR ACTUAL TRANSIENCE, NULL RECURRENCE, OR EXPLOSION

No exact transient, null recurrent, or explosive weakly reversible example
outside LP was established in this leaf. The parameter point in Section 3 is
outside LP but positive recurrent. For a non-complex-balanced choice of rates
outside LP, the recurrence class is UNKNOWN here.

The elementary runaway tests do not settle this. Along B=0 the high-order
channels are off and A_j has only constant immigration, linear death, and
linear B production. Along A_j=1,B_i=n the forward autocatalytic channel can
produce a positive O(n) coefficient when (5) fails, but the return channel
switches on at larger A_j with degree k_ji+1 in A_j; this blocks the inference
of a persistent monotone A_j runaway from that one ray. Along A=0,B_i=n the
only enabled channels are the constant-rate catalyst inflows (7); once a
catalyst appears, both B-removal and autocatalytic rates are O(n). This
boundary holding-time observation also does not establish a recurrence class
or its return-time tail. We do not have a physical-time or embedded-chain
argument proving transience, null recurrence, or explosion for any non-CB
member of this family.

This is consistent with the literature gate, not proof that no such example
exists. The general positive-recurrence conjecture for weakly reversible
stochastic mass-action networks is still reported open in the checked primary
network papers; the ConStRAINeD publications page (accessed 2026-10-08) lists
the announced two-species proof as “in preparation” and says the arbitrary-
dimension conjecture remains open. The present network has three species and
stoichiometric rank three, so that reported two-dimensional result would not
apply even if its manuscript were available. A one-dimensional theorem does
not apply either. Strongly endotactic transient/explosive examples from the
literature are not substituted for a weakly reversible example.

The physical-time generator in (1) is the object used for every drift above.
Embedded-chain recurrence alone would not settle continuous-time positive
recurrence without controlling holding times. No simulation was used as
evidence for recurrence or nonrecurrence.

5. COST, MODEL BOUNDARY, AND FALSIFIERS

For input dimensions (J,I), LP has J+I positive variables and J+JI strict
linear inequalities; it uses O(JI) rational arithmetic operations per
constraint evaluation, with exact LP bit complexity depending on rate and
k_ji encodings. A rational feasible open polyhedron has a rational feasible
point. The CTMC model has J+I species and J+JI reversible pairs; generator
evaluation for a supplied state costs O(JI) reaction-channel evaluations,
plus the arithmetic cost of falling factorials through orders k_ji+1. The
three-species instance uses 12 directed channels, integer rates at most 4,
and maximum source molecularity 4. No oracle, simulation, optimization
solver, or floating-point tolerance was needed for the exact example.

Falsifiers and proof boundaries:

* A sign or stoichiometric error in (1) changes the LP; all increments are
  explicitly included above, including the +k A_j increment of the forward
  growth pair and the -k A_j return increment.
* At A_j=1 the reverse autocatalytic propensity is exactly zero for every
  k_ji>=1; replacing the falling factorial by a concentration monomial at a
  lattice state would invalidate (4).
* The failure of LP is not a Foster converse and does not imply any CTMC
  classification. Equation (9) explicitly refutes that inference.
* The pure-B ray (7) shows the linear V lacks global negative drift even
  inside LP; an additional argument is required for any theorem about
  positive recurrence.
* The complex-balanced choice establishes non-necessity only for this LP.
  It does not show the threshold (6) is unnecessary for another Lyapunov
  method, nor locate the chain's actual rate-dependent recurrence boundary.
