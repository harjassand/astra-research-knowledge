P16 cost audit (conditional on the stated TMSV/noise convention)

For `R=4^k`, `λ=2^{-R²}`, `ν=R^{-a}`, `η=1/R`, the ideal `N=R` herald mass is

`p_id^LB=(1-2^{-2R²}) 2^{-2R³} R!/R^{2R}`.

Therefore

`log₂(1/p_id^LB)=2R³+2R log₂R-log₂(R!)-log₂(1-2^{-2R²})`

`=2R³+R log₂R+R log₂e-½log₂(2πR)+O(1/R)+O(2^{-2R²}).`

The `N>R` tail changes this by only a vanishing relative amount, so ideal physical rejection costs asymptotically `1/p_id^LB` attempts. For the noisy vacuum reference, `t=ην=R^{-(a+1)}` and

`p₀=[t/(1+t)²]^R`,

`log₂(1/p₀)=(a+1)R log₂R+2R log₂(1+R^{-(a+1)})`.

The noisy TMSV herald probability differs from `p₀` by at most `λ`, while `λ/p₀=2^{-R²+(a+1)Rlog₂R+o(1)}→0`; noisy physical rejection therefore costs `(1+o(1))/p₀`, far less than the ideal rejection cost, though still superpolynomial in `R` and in the natural explicit input encodings of polynomial size in `R`.

Encoded versus numerical costs: exact `λ` needs `R²+O(1)` bits, so `L≥Ω(R²)`. `η` uses `O(log R)` bits but `1/η=R`; `h=R`, `m=R+1`, and an explicit all-ones event vector has length `R`. Since `R=4^k`, Walsh entries are rational `±2^{-k}`; a separately encoded dense matrix costs `Θ(R²logR)` bits, with smaller shared-denominator or circuit encodings possible. A flat expanded kernel may cost more, so a unique total `L` depends on the supplied representation. Under the stated displacement convention, `E_in=(R+1)R^{-a}+2λ²/(1-λ²)=O(1)` for every fixed `a≥1`; after loss, `E_out=(R+1)R^{-(a+1)}+2ηλ²/(1-λ²)`.

Description-based conditioning is distinct from rejection. If the instance satisfies N42's supplied entrywise-nonnegative rational-kernel promise and OA113, its claimed cost is `poly(L,m,h,1+E,ε⁻¹)` without `1/p_H`; this instance's exact kernel/certificate and acquisition cost are not verified, so N42 is not asserted here. A family-specific noisy sampler can output vacuum: its TV error is at most `λ/(p₀-λ)+t/(1+t)=o(1)`. The ideal conditional signal is `Bin(R,1/R)` up to vanishing TV error. Yet the ideal and noisy conditional laws have TV gap tending `1-e^{-1}`. This refutes a uniform conditional-TV modulus independent of herald mass; it establishes no computational hardness or runtime lower bound.
