Cycle 3 package: unknown-mixing stationary-history KL certification

Primary result: RESULT.txt, Sections 2-5. The exact impossibility is for
post-hoc certification of a supplied predictor. The reducible construction
is statistically indistinguishable even from the infinite path. The
irreducible extension rules out uniform almost-sure finite stopping when no
mixing-rate lower bound is known.

Claims are in CLAIMS.json. The source ledger is SOURCES.txt. The checker in
work/programmes/acquisition_learning/cycle3/unknown_mixing_certificate/
verify_example.py produces check_output.json. The checker is a finite
fixture, not a theorem prover.

Important boundary: do not upgrade this to “no learner can predict.” Han,
Jana, and Wu prove useful expected prediction without a gap for known-state
finite Markov chains. No result here gives a matching impossibility for all
data-dependent predictors, nor a sufficient adaptive certificate under
known mixing and context-floor assumptions.

Next decisive attacks:
1. Try to turn the fixed-q testing witness into a lower bound against any
   data-dependent output predictor under full-history loss; a successful
   route must prevent the predictor from hard-coding a robust policy for
   unseen histories.
2. Under an explicit lower bound on stationary context mass and a known
   minorization/mixing rate, derive an anytime confidence sequence for the
   stationary-history KL risk and charge independent validation versus
   retained training data.
3. Check whether an expected-risk theorem can be converted into a
   high-confidence post-hoc certificate without independent restarts; do not
   conflate the two targets.
