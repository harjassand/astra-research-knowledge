CANDIDATE THEOREM — arbitrary relation biases with sharp exponent
2026-10-07. Internal derivation; external correctness/priority unverified.

INTERFACE
Fix r>=2 and q>=1. Relation symbol t has positive arity a_t<=r, coordinates (t,tuple in [n]^{a_t}); repeated vertices, loops, ordering allowed. Coordinates independently Bernoulli(p_t), arbitrary 0<p_t<1, possibly depending on n. f is Boolean and invariant under every simultaneous vertex permutation. I_t is sum of section-disagreement probabilities over type t, I=sum I_t, alpha=r/(r-1).
Claim Var(f)<= C_{r,q} I/(log n)^alpha, uniformly all p_t, with C_{r,q}=O_r(q^{1/(r-1)}) plus a finite small-n term.
This closes the heterogeneous-bias service left open in prior relational deduction; source family186 itself states only common p on simple uniform hyperedges.

PROOF
Let sigma_t=sqrt(p_t(1-p_t)), a_t'=log(4/sigma_t). Product Fourier basis chi_i=(x_i-p_t)/sigma_t. For support S, define analytic cost A(S)=sum_t a_t' |S_t| and capture degree W(S)=sum_t |S_t|/sigma_t^alpha.

1. Variable-bias low-cost lemma. On any finite product cube, if every pivotal I_i(h)<=I(h)/m, then with K=log(m)/32,
 sum_{nonempty S:A(S)<=K} hat h(S)^2 <=m^(-1/4) I(h).
Proof: rho_i=sigma_i/4. One-coordinate expansion gives E(a+rho_i b chi_i)^4 <=(a^2+b^2)^2; tensorize with L2 triangle inequality to get ||Tg||4<=||g||2, T chi_S=exp(-A(S))chi_S. For cost projection P_{A<=K}, duality gives ||Pg||2<=exp(K)||g||_{4/3}. Charge each nonempty support to its coordinates and use hat h(S)=sigma_i hat Delta_i h(S\i): mass<=exp(2K) sum_i sigma_i^2 I_i^(3/2). If I<=m^1/4, sqrt(I_i)<=m^-3/8, and exp(2K)=m^1/16, giving <=m^-5/16 I/4<=m^-1/4 I. If I>m^1/4, RHS>1 and mass<=Var<=1/4.

2. Random block restriction. Uniform m-subset B of vertices, m>=r+1. Free every coordinate touching B, fix all other coordinates. Permutations supported in B fix exterior assignment individually and preserve every type bias. A free ordered tuple with j distinct vertices in B has orbit (m)_j>=m, so each restricted I_i<=I_restricted/m. Averaging the lemma over fixed exterior and B gives
 sum_S P_B(0<A(S intersect F_B)<=K)hat f(S)^2 <=m^-1/4 (rm/n) I.
(Nonempty support exactly A>0.) This follows from exterior orthogonality, and P_B(i free)<=rm/n.

3. New weighted capture. Put L=(log(m)/(64 r q^(1/r)))^alpha. If 0<W(S)<=L, let s_t=|S_t|, s=sum s_t and v=number support vertices. Because at most q v^r relation coordinates exist on v vertices, v>=(s/q)^(1/r). The sum over vertices of their analytic incidence costs is <=r sum_t a_t' s_t. Since sigma log(4/sigma)<=2, this is <=2r sum_t s_t/sigma_t. Holder with conjugate exponents alpha and r yields
 sum_t s_t/sigma_t = sum_t (s_t/sigma_t^alpha)^(1/alpha)s_t^(1/r) <=W(S)^(1/alpha) s^(1/r).
Thus some support vertex u has strictly positive analytic incidence cost <=2r q^(1/r) W(S)^(1/alpha)<=K.
Also s<=W(S) because sigma_t<=1, so v<=rL. If rL(m-1)/(n-1)<=1/2, P(B intersect support vertices={u})>=m/(2n). On this event, nonempty restricted support has analytic cost in (0,K]. Therefore
 sum_{0<W(S)<=L}hat f(S)^2 <=2r m^-1/4 I.

4. Weighted tail. Exact derivative Parseval gives
 sum_S W(S)hat f(S)^2 =sum_t sigma_t^(2-alpha) I_t <= I,
since alpha<=2 and sigma_t<=1. Consequently mass of W>L is <=I/L. Combining proves
 Var(f)<=[2r m^-1/4 +(64r q^(1/r)/log m)^alpha]I
whenever block/capture conditions hold.
Set m=floor(sqrt n). Choose N_r independent of q,p with m>=r+1, log m>=log n/3, r(log n)^alpha n^-1/2<=1/2, m^-1/4(log n)^alpha<=1. Since L<=(log n)^alpha, conditions hold for n>=N_r. Then C=2r+(192r q^(1/r))^alpha. For 2<=n<N_r ordinary product Poincare gives Var<=sum sigma_t²I_t<=I/4; enlarge C to (log N_r)^alpha/4.

SECOND-ORDER CONSEQUENCES
A. Finite categorical/one-hot relations: relation t independently takes one of k_t colours according to arbitrary pi_t, same distribution across tuples of that symbol. Define J_t= sum_i P_over_other_categories(local section is nonconstant on SUPPORT(pi_t)); J=sum J_t. Remove zero-probability colours. Choose a full binary tree with these k_t support colours as leaves, depth at most d_t=ceil(log2 k_t). For each internal node v independently toss Bernoulli(p_v), p_v=mass(left child)/mass(v), and follow the root-to-leaf choices to sample the categorical coordinate. This uses exactly k_t-1 independent Bernoulli relation symbols of same arity, all biases strictly in (0,1). Lift f invariant to this product.
For a fixed local category section g, flipping the coin at node v can change g only when ancestor coins route to v, which has probability mass(v). If g is constant on category support, no node coin has influence. Hence the sum of bit pivotal influences at this category coordinate is <=sum_v mass(v) *1_{g nonconstant}=E_pi[leaf depth] *1_{g nonconstant} <=d_t*1_{g nonconstant}. Averaging other category coordinates gives I_bits<=sum_t ell_t J_t<=d_max J, where ell_t=expected depth and d_max=max ceil(log2 k_t). With Q=sum_t(k_t-1),
 Var(f)<=C_{r,Q} d_max J/(log n)^alpha.
More precisely replace d_max J by sum_t ell_t J_t. This includes actual one-hot mutually exclusive colours. The categorical pivot J is NOT resampling disagreement; rare-colour probabilities are not charged in J. A Huffman-type tree would reduce ell_t to H2(pi_t)+1, but balanced-tree bound above is self-contained.

B. Heterogeneous monotone schedules: if p_t(u) are differentiable increasing and min_t p_t'(u)>=a>0 across an interval, Russo differentiation gives dE f/du=sum_t p_t' I_t >=a I. Between epsilon and 1-epsilon quantiles, width <=2 C_{r,q} log((1-epsilon)/epsilon)/(a (log n)^alpha).
C. Categorical ordered exponential tilts: pi_t,c(u) proportional nu_t,c exp[u b_t,c], with strictly ordered scores and f coordinatewise increasing in colours. Let d(u)=min_t pi_t,min(u) pi_t,max(u)(b_t,max-b_t,min)>0. Conditional covariance identity gives q'(u)>=d(u) J. Thus integrated metric width int_{u_eps}^{u_1eps}d(u)du<=2 C_{r,Q} d_max log((1-epsilon)/epsilon)/(log n)^alpha. If d>=d0, divide by d0. Zero atoms/empty nontrivial intervals require support-aware endpoint definitions.
D. Anisotropic noise: if each type t resamples with probability theta_t, define tau=min_t theta_t sigma_t^alpha. Fourier covariance <=2r m^-1/4 I+exp(-tau L)Var. Same block/capture conditions. If I/Var=o(m^1/4) and tau (log n)^alpha->infty, normalized covariance->0. Rare types can make tau small; do not claim general sparse critical-window improvement.

BOUNDARIES/CONTROLS
No nullary symbol: a single invariant global bit contradicts log decay. No distinguished coordinates: invariance essential. Boolean only: real-valued sums violate gradient-gap version. Within-type identical distributions and full independence remain essential; arbitrary coordinate-varying p destroys permutation-invariant measure. One-hot transfer pays categorical support-pivotal influence; uniform resampling influence version is false at vanishing rare probabilities. Growing q is charged by q^{1/(r-1)} and growing k by encoding factor. No evaluation, influence-estimation, threshold-finding, or sampling algorithm obtained.

EXACT FAILURE WITNESSES FOR STRONGER INTERFACES
1. Uniform bound with RESAMPLING influence is false at rare biases even for unary invariant events. Let f=OR of n unary marks with p=c/n, c>0. Var tends e^-c(1-e^-c)>0. Pivotal I=n(1-p)^(n-1)~n e^-c, whereas resampling disagreement J=2p(1-p)I~2c e^-c stays bounded. Thus Var<=C J/(log n)^alpha fails. The proved categorical support-pivotal normalization is essential.
2. Arbitrary biases varying WITHIN one relation's tuples can fail. Use a simple graph relation and unary bit relation. Set graph-edge biases 1-delta on a labelled n-vertex star centered at vertex1, delta on other edges, delta=n^-4. Set unary-bit bias1/2 at vertex1, delta elsewhere. Define vertex-invariant f to be the unary bit at the unique maximum-degree vertex, and zero if there is a tie. On the exact star, for n>=5 any one edge flip preserves unique maximum-degree vertex1. Consequently each graph-edge pivotal event requires at least one error among OTHER edges; union bound gives edge total influence <=M(M-1)delta=O(n^4 delta)=O(1), not yet vanishing with n^-4. Choosing delta=n^-6 instead gives o(1). Leaf unary pivotal probability is at most P(graph not exact star)<=Mdelta, so their total is O(n^3delta)=o(1); central unary pivotal<=1. Therefore I<=1+o(1), while Var->1/4. This disproves all-biases-variable-within-symbol version. Strict positive biases maintained. Exact star max degree n-1 versus1 leaves; one edge flip makes center>=n-2 and others<=2, unique for n>=5 (at n=4 center2 may tie leaf2, so require n>=5).
