1. THEOREM-LEVEL OUTCOME: CRITICAL FULL-ORBIT CLASSICAL RECONSTRUCTION

For n identical qubits rho_(r,v)=(I+r v.sigma)/2, known radius
0<r<=1/2 with c0<=r sqrt(n)<=c1 for fixed 0<c0<=c1<infinity, and unknown
full direction v in S^2, the optimal worst-case half-trace reconstruction
error over all entanglement-breaking channels is

    e_EB^*(n,r)=Theta_(c0,c1)(r)=Theta_(c0,c1)(n^(-1/2)).

This is a whole-density reconstruction theorem. The archive can use any
classical outcome space, and the decoder can change Schur sectors arbitrarily;
there is no free side register or entangled receiver. The lower proof is a
bounded posterior-vector score and a constant-mass critical spin window. It
improves the old c/sqrt(n log n) floor and does not rely on sector transport
being small. The upper is the spin-coherent measure-and-prepare channel with
error at most 2r/(1+r). Full derivation and exact interface: [critical qubit
score audit](critical_qubit_EB_score.txt). Finite rational replay:
`work/programmes/combinatorics_optimization/cycle3/qubit_compression/q02_log_removal_witness/check_witness.py`.

The proof's key finite inequality is

    e_EB >= sum_(L>0) P_L a_L^2/L,

where L is the doubled spin, j=L/2, and a_L=Tr(tau_(L,z)J_z)/(j+1).
The completed square a_L a_K-a_K^2/2<=a_L^2/2 holds for every input/output
pair L,K, so the converse explicitly covers sector-changing EB maps. A
uniform binomial Berry--Esseen lower tail plus the exact Casimir second moment
places constant mass in [2/r,B/r]. On this interval a_L is bounded below,
giving e_EB>=c r. The finite checks covered 270 exact spin sectors and 43,172
sector-transition pairs; they support normalization and implementation, while
the asymptotic proof is analytic.

The result is an internally proved candidate, not externally reviewed. Its
nearest cited components are standard spin posterior-radius formulas in
Bagan et al. (2005) and Schur compression in Yang et al. (2016, 2017-2019).
The source audits do not settle priority for this exact full-density,
worst-case, critical-radius EB objective; priority remains UNKNOWN.

2. ACCURACY AND MEMORY CONSEQUENCES

The matching EB upper can be implemented with a finite outcome alphabet of
O(n^5) symbols by a Choi-space Caratheodory argument, hence O(log n) retained
bits at error O(n^(-1/2)); the finite decomposition's synthesis and exact
finite-bit description are separate costs. For any epsilon<1/4 an exact
Schur-tail quantum codec charges every retained spin branch and its label,
uses D=O(min{n^2,n[1+log(1/epsilon)]}), and has whole-density error at most
epsilon. A direct independent proof is in the q03/q08 leaf records and
Sol's Sections 3 and 7. At epsilon=n^(-1), D=O(n log n), so the inherited
epsilon^(-3/2) law cannot hold uniformly at all finite-n accuracies. Its
valid stated regime requires epsilon sqrt(n)->infinity and a bridge remainder
smaller than epsilon. The sharp EB floor does not determine the optimal
hybrid quantum/classical memory below that crossover.

3. SECOND MECHANISM: CONDITIONAL INSTABILITY UNDER RARE HERALDING

The cycle's second branch audits the root construction in
[ROOT_RARE_HERALD_NOISE.txt](../../ROOT_RARE_HERALD_NOISE.txt). For R=4^k, a
two-mode squeezed vacuum with amplitude lambda=2^(-R^2), rational Walsh
fanout, uniform pure loss eta=1/R, exact-one PNR heralding on all R idler
ports, and independent isotropic additive noise of mean photons
nu=R^(-a) on every mode before loss (fixed integer a>=1),

    ideal heralded signal count law -> Poisson(1),
    noisy heralded signal count law -> vacuum,
    conditional TV gap -> 1-exp(-1).

The ideal posterior excess pair count J=N-R is exactly negative binomial.
The retained signal must also undergo loss eta; without that loss, the signal
count instead remains near R and the Poisson limit fails. Under the vacuum
reference, the noisy output is a product thermal state with per-mode mean
t=eta nu=R^(-(a+1)); its all-one probability is
[t/(1+t)^2]^R, while the conditional signal is thermal and tends to vacuum.
The initial TMSV is within trace distance lambda of vacuum, and postselection
continuity transfers this reference result because lambda/p0->0. See p11,
p12, p13, p14, and p17 cycle3 records for independent normalization, exact
posterior, thermal, continuity, and PGF audits.

The construction is a conditioning-instability witness, not a hardness or
runtime lower bound. The ideal herald success is extraordinarily small:
log2(1/p_H)=2R^3+R log2 R+R log2(e)-0.5log2(2pi R)+o(1). The noisy reference
herald mass also vanishes rapidly. Input description length is polynomial in
R (lambda uses O(R^2) bits; fanout has R^2 dyadic entries); added mean energy
is O(R nu). Physical rejection sampling and efficient sampling from this
explicit family are distinct. Rational optical matrices/covariances do not
make the TMSV ket or loss dilation amplitudes rational.

An extension with noise injected into one source-idler mode before fanout has
reference all-one probability

    q=(R!/R^R) (eta nu)^R/(1+eta nu)^(R+1),

and q/lambda->infinity for the stated polynomial nu and eta. At R=16 the
exact-rational check gives q/lambda>2^107. This is a model-specific correlated
noise extension. It does not provide a universal noise threshold: the
conditional-continuity certificate for lambda=2^(-R^b) vanishes for b>1 and
is inconclusive for b<=1; these are proof thresholds, not physical stability
thresholds. Partial-noise and correlated-noise boundaries are scoped in p15
and p18. Exact all-one PNR semantics matter; threshold-click heralding needs
new likelihood formulas.
