## Result

The exact minimax formulation for a finite-dimensional faithful tracial algebra does encode one common POVM across the entire finite label family. The `{X,Z}` qubit instance has collective minimax loss `1/2`, while each label separately has zero loss under its own projective measurement. Thus per-label optimized measurements cannot replace the common measurement in a broadcasting-to-EB argument. With a faithful central block of weight `w` and likelihood amplitude `theta`, this same example has physical-trace loss `w theta^2/2`; no inverse minimum-block-weight factor appears.

Finite-dimensional duality does not erase growing-family complexity. An all-frame separating witness uses at most `D` arbitrary Hermitian test directions in a real test space of dimension `D`. An experiment-specific optimal prior has a Carathéodory bound of at most `D(D+1)/2+1` labels because it enters through a point in `Sym(R^D)`. These are distinct statements, and both bounds grow with dimension. Therefore fixed-support estimates alone do not control `q(E_n)` for increasing algebras. A family-independent full operator comparison would cover all label counts; a weaker route needs a modulus over the actual growing family or an explicit entropy/covering assumption.

The three-Pauli qubit frame against the universal-cloner marginal gives the exact ratio `2`, so no universal constant below `2` can hold. It is a sharp calibration, not a counterexample to the `c=2` candidate. The weighted-block calculation, common-map witness, and dimension-growth distinction are established in the self-contained finite-dimensional derivation in `INITIAL.txt` and `revisions/REVISION_01.txt`, with support clarification in `REVISION_02.txt`.
