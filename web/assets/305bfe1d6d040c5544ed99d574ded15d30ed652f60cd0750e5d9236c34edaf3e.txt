PRIMARY-SOURCE SCOPE CHECK — 7 OCTOBER 2026

A narrow search was aimed at algorithmic prior art for the measured interface.

- Iwata and Kobayashi, “A Weighted Linear Matroid Parity Algorithm,” arXiv:1905.13371, https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.13371. The primary text describes a deterministic polynomial-time weighted linear matroid parity optimization algorithm using a Pfaffian formulation and augmenting paths. It does not describe the determinant-squared partition sampler considered here.
- Matoya and Oki, “Pfaffian Pairs and Parities: Counting on Linear Matroid Intersection and Parity Problems,” arXiv:1912.00620, https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.00620. The abstract describes polynomial-time counts of minimum-weight solutions on weighted Pfaffian pairs and parities. That is not the full weighted sum over the squared complementary-minor measure used here.
- Anari, Liu, Oveis Gharan, Vinzant, “Log-Concave Polynomials II: High-Dimensional Walks and an FPRAS for Counting Bases of a Matroid,” arXiv:1811.01816, https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.01816. Its stated FPRAS is for matroid bases. The positive support of this example is not a matroid base family: with zero-based indices X={2,4,5}, Y={0,3,4}, and x=2 in X\\Y, both exchanges X-x+0={0,4,5} and X-x+3={3,4,5} have zero determinant weight. Thus its base-exchange theorem is not a direct sampler for this measure.

The adjacent literature does not certify novelty or rule out a more specialized exact algorithm. No priority claim is made. The exact bottleneck proof is independent of these sources.
