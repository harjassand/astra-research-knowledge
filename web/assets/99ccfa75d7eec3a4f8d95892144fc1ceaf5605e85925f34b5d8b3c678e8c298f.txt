## Attributed cross-audit and deeper result

The post-freeze review is recorded in `revisions/CROSS_AUDIT_AND_GROWTH.txt`.

- **c05_s01 (attributed):** the Walsh sign-frame identity and bounded-preimage / low-leakage-MASA lifts are valid at their stated conditional interfaces. The missing pieces are acquisition of the sign-frame preimages or low-leakage MASA and uniform control of their errors for the channel powers.
- **c05_s02 (attributed):** the supertrace obstruction is limited to the corrected canonical-instrument closure. The universal-cloner powers have EB error tending to zero, so they do not refute the full target.
- **c05_s03 (attributed and independently algebra-checked):** finite-generator, finite-range-net, and range-rank pinching give conditional EB lifts whose constants grow with complexity. I derived an explicit envelope from its recurrence: if `b_k=C_k^2` and `b_k=(k-1)^(2/3)+g_k b_(k-1)`, with `g_k=[1+4(k-1)^(1/3)]^(2/3^(k-2))`, then `G=prod_{k>=3}g_k<infinity`, `b_k<=G k^(5/3)`, hence `C_k<=sqrt(G)k^(5/6)`. The finite-rank lift tends to zero if, for a fixed q>0, `r_m <= (1-q) log_3(log(1/delta_m))` eventually, with `delta_m=2/sqrt(m+1)+4/(m+1)`. This is a strictly growing but highly restrictive conditional subclass; the hypotheses do not bound `r_m` that way.
- **c05_l06 (attributed):** its independent audit agrees with the supplied N46 bridge and leaves the uniform EB-lift gate open. Other c05 Luna initial artifacts were not present in the shared output directory at the time of review.
- **Brandao--Harrow route (primary-source audit):** arXiv:1210.6367v5, Theorem 1(1), gives a dimension-free bound for one fixed binary A-test and a worst-case B measurement, or for an average over a chosen distribution of A-tests. Its separable witness can depend on the A-test / distribution. The target is `min_EB sup_A sup_B`; the theorem gives `min_sep max_B E_A`. The abstract diagonal payoff matrix shows that this quantifier change can cost a factor n. The witness is not a quantum counterexample; it only blocks the inference without additional structure. The theorem also does not state an exact Choi reference marginal for its separable approximant.
- **Ye--Bergh--Datta route (primary-source audit):** arXiv:2609.40343v1, Theorems 2--3, gives CP-order de Finetti bounds for permutation-covariant many-input/many-output channels, with dimension and defect parameters, and iid or Choi-almost-iid channel approximants. Its interface is not a one-input K-output broadcaster, and its approximants need not be EB. I derived an elementary useful transfer: if unital CP `T <=_CP lambda E` with E also unital, then `||T-E||_(infinity->1,tau,Herm) <= 2(lambda-1)`. Therefore a future CP-order approximation by an EB unital E with `lambda->1` would settle the desired map norm, but this source does not provide that E.

## Final status and decisive gate

The strongest supported advance is the conditional slow-range-growth corollary plus a sharper transfer diagnosis: dimension-free averaged local-measurement shareability and generic CP-order channel de Finetti do not automatically become one exact-marginal EB map in the uniform normalized `L-infinity -> L-one` norm. The required `beta_m -> 0` statement remains unproved and no decisive map-level counterexample was found.

The next decisive result is either a dimension-free separable approximation of K-extendible Choi states in the product-observable norm with the exact reference marginal, or a direct entropy/anchor/metric-entropy bound on the actual CP-power range that supplies the simultaneous EB lift without range-rank growth restrictions. A CP-order-to-EB bound would also suffice by the lemma above.
