1. CONTRACT AND ALL-Q RESULT

Let H_m=Sym^m(C^3), d=(m+1)(m+2)/2, m>=1. The conjugate representation
has the identical statements. For a unit direction v, P_v=|v^tensor m><v^tensor m|.
The unknown coherent pure orbit has full U(3) direction, with Haar prior for
the converse and supremum half-trace error for the code.

The thermal extension has occupation basis n1+n2+n3=m, seed

  rho=Z^(-1) q1^n1 q2^n2,
  0<=q1,q2<=qbar<1, known q_i; w0=1/Z,
  rho_U=pi(U)rho pi(U)^dag, U in U(3) entirely unknown.     (1.1)

The unique maximal eigenvector is e3^tensor m. Zero q_i are interpreted by
continuity and 0^0=1. The whole supplied-carrier density must be reconstructed;
no purifying reference or assistance is supplied. Every quantum branch has
dimension <=Q. Classical labels may be unlimited in the converse and must be
counted in an upper. Decoder output is H_m; adding ambient output sectors
does not help, since projection back with a fallback is CPTP and contracts
distance to the target without increasing the maximum branch dimension.

Decompose End(H_m)=direct_sum_(l=0..m) H_(l,l), and write B_l for the
Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal components. Define

  b_l=m!(m+2)!/[(m-l)!(m+l+2)!],
  Lambda_m(Q)=min_(rank B<=Q, ||B||_HS=1)
                    sum_l(1-b_l)||B_l||_HS^2.             (1.2)

The exact pure-orbit capacity and thermal comparison are

  e_Q^pure=Lambda_m(Q),
  (1-qbar)^4 Lambda_m(Q)<=e_Q^thermal<=Lambda_m(Q),        (1.3)

for every 1<=Q<=d, including zero at Q=d. Error is optimal worst-case half
trace distance over the declared orbit. Equality for the pure orbit uses an
existential positive minimizing filter; its acquisition is not proved efficient.
A finite alphabet of at most d^4+1 labels is enough to realize its exact
covariant channel, with total dimension <=(d^4+1)Q. This is not an optimal
classical/total-memory law or an efficient-circuit result.

An EXPLICIT radial taper proves the fixed-fraction consequence

  e_Q^pure=Theta(Q^(-1/2)),
  e_Q^thermal=Theta_qbar(Q^(-1/2)), 1<=Q<=d/32.           (1.4)

Consequently quantum branch Q=Theta(epsilon^(-2)) is necessary and sufficient
before physical saturation in this supplied cold carrier. This supplies the
external two-mode precision order, not a global iid collision converse.

2. EXACT BEREZIN SPECTRUM BY A FINITE MONOMIAL CALCULATION

Let B_m(X)=d int_CP2 P_v Tr(P_v X)dv. Coherent resolution intP_v=I/d
makes this an unital TP self-adjoint positive operator on Hilbert-Schmidt
matrix space. It commutes with SU(3). In bosonic creation notation the highest
operator X_l=(a1^dag)^l a3^l has adjoint highest weight (l,l), l=0,...,m.
The corresponding SU(3) irreps have dimensions (l+1)^3. They are inequivalent,
and sum_l(l+1)^3=[(m+1)(m+2)/2]^2=d^2. Complete reducibility therefore
gives the claimed multiplicity-one decomposition of End(H_m).

The coherent symbol is
<v^m|X_l|v^m>=[m!/(m-l)!](conj(v1)v3)^l.
Dirichlet(1,1,1) monomial integration yields

  int |v1|^(2l)|v3|^(2l)dv=2(l!)^2/(2l+2)!.

In the occupation basis, set n3'=n3-l. The squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm is

  ||X_l||^2=(l!)^2 sum_(n1+n2+n3'=m-l)
                            binom(n1+l,l)binom(n3'+l,l)
           =(l!)^2 binom(m+l+2,2l+2).                     (2.1)

The generating function for the sum is (1-z)^(-2l-3). Since B_m is scalar
on this irrep, b_l=d int|<v^m|X_l|v^m>|^2/||X_l||^2. Substitution gives
exactly (1.2), including b0=1 and b1=m/(m+3). Thus for every matrix B,

  int||[P_v,B]||_HS^2
       =(2/d)sum_l(1-b_l)||B_l||_HS^2.                    (2.2)

The product formula and its elementary estimate are

  b_l=prod_(a=0..l-1) (m-a)/(m+3+a),
  b_l<=exp[-l(l+2)/(m+l+2)].                              (2.3)

Also 1-b_l<=sum_a (3+2a)/(m+3+a)<=l(l+2)/m, and it is <=1. Hence
1-b_l is comparable, with absolute constants, to min{l(l+2)/m,1}
uniformly for 1<=l<=m. No asymptotic Gaussian replacement is used.

3. RANK-Q LOW-MODE PROJECTOR BOUND

Let P_<=L project onto direct_sum_(l=0..L) H_(l,l) in vectorized matrix
space H_m tensor conjugate(H_m). Its rank is

  D_L=sum_(l=0..L)(l+1)^3=[(L+1)(L+2)/2]^2.              (3.1)

Both partial traces of P_<=L are (D_L/d)I: covariance and irreducibility
make them scalar, and their trace fixes the constant. A unit vectorized
rank-Q matrix has Schmidt rank <=Q. Let Pi_S project onto its first-factor
Schmidt support. Its low-mode mass is bounded by

  <B|P_<=L|B><=||(Pi_S tensor I)P_<=L(Pi_S tensor I)||_op
             <=Tr((Pi_S tensor I)P_<=L)<=Q D_L/d.          (3.2)

Since b_l decreases,

  Lambda_m(Q)>=(1-b_(L+1))[1-QD_L/d]_+                   (3.3)

for L<m. This holds for every complex rank-Q matrix, with no diagonal,
normality, positive-Kraus or source-sector assumption.

For 1<=Q<=d/32, set t=[d/(2Q)]^(1/4)>=2 and
L=floor(t/2)-1>=0. Then D_L<=(L+2)^4/4<=t^4/4,
QD_L/d<=1/8, and l=L+1=floor(t/2)>=t/4. The cutoff lies below m.
As d>=m^2/2, l^2/m>=1/(32sqrt Q); m+l+2<=4m gives
l(l+2)/(m+l+2)>=1/(128sqrt Q). Equation (2.3) therefore implies

  Lambda_m(Q)>=(1-e^(-1))/(256sqrt Q).                    (3.4)

The constants are deliberately loose. The fixed-fraction domain cannot
be dropped; Section 7 gives a near-full counterexample to (1.4).

4. PURE-ORBIT CONVERSE FOR ARBITRARY CHANNELS

Any legal hybrid code with maximum branch Q has composite Kraus operators
A_i of rank <=Q. Put B_i=A_i/sqrt d, so sum_i||B_i||_HS^2=1. For a pure
target P_v, half-trace error is at least its infidelity
1-Tr P_v Phi(P_v). Haar averaging and the TP condition give

  int[1-Tr P_v Phi(P_v)]dv
    =sum_i sum_l(1-b_l)||(B_i)_l||_HS^2
    >=Lambda_m(Q).                                        (4.1)

Worst-case error dominates this average. The identical conclusion follows
from the reference-weighted self-score with sigma=I/d, L_v=dP_v and scalar
C=intL_v^2=dI, so no unitality or covariance is assumed of the competitor.
Unlimited classical outcomes and all legal intermediate branches are included.

5. POSITIVE MINIMIZER AND EXACT PURE UPPER

The normalized rank-Q matrix set is compact. A positive minimizer exists:
for A=sum_i sigma_i |u_i><v_i| and every Hermitian T, direct expansion gives

  ||[T,A]||^2-[||[T,|A|]||^2+||[T,|A^dag|]||^2]/2
    =sum_ij sigma_i sigma_j
       |<u_i|T|u_j>-<v_i|T|v_j>|^2>=0.                    (5.1)

Integrate T=P_v. Both moduli have the same Hilbert-Schmidt norm and rank
as A; at least one has no greater energy. By (2.2), Lambda_m(Q) is achieved
by a positive F with ||F||_HS=1, rank F<=Q.

Use Phi_F(X)=d int F_U X F_U dU. Schur averaging makes it unital TP.
Its coherent-input infidelity is exactly Lambda_m(Q). By covariance the
output on P_v is invariant under its stabilizer U(2)xU(1). P_v is the
unique rank-one N=0 component; central U(1) invariance eliminates cross
blocks between it and its orthogonal complement. Consequently half-trace
error EQUALS the infidelity. It is constant in v, so this filter achieves
e_Q^pure=Lambda_m(Q), proving equality in (1.3).

The Haar average of the rank-one Kraus Choi matrices is a point in a compact
convex hull in real dimension <=d^4. Caratheodory gives <=d^4+1 positive
nodes preserving the exact composed channel and hence its TP condition.
For each public node U and weight, encode into the fixed support of F,
store its charged index, and decode by embedding/rotation. This proves a
finite legal hybrid implementation with the stated alphabet bound. Finding
the positive optimizer or this exact quadrature efficiently is not proved.

6. FINITE-TEMPERATURE COERCIVITY AND CAPACITY

For (1.1), every nonvacuum eigenvalue is <=qbar w0, while the vacuum eigenvalue
is w0. In the occupation eigenbasis, the squared commutator is a positive
sum over matrix entries. On entries connecting the vacuum to its complement,
|rho0-rho_N|>=w0(1-qbar). Therefore, POINTWISE for every U and B,

  ||[rho_U,B]||_HS^2
       >=w0^2(1-qbar)^2||[P_(Ue3),B]||_HS^2.              (6.1)

This is the lower transfer; no coherent-mixture lower inference is made.
The Haar mean is I/d and C=int(d rho_U)^2=d Tr(rho^2)I. The scalar-reference
self-score identity, with M=d w0, yields for arbitrary rank-Q code

  e>=[d/(2w0)]sum_i int||[rho_U,B_i]||^2 dU
    >=w0(1-qbar)^2 Lambda_m(Q).                            (6.2)

Here B_i=A_i/sqrt d. Since Z<=1/[(1-q1)(1-q2)], w0>=(1-qbar)^2.
This proves the thermal lower in (1.3), uniformly over anisotropic q_i.

For the upper let A=diag(q1,q2,1). Coherent resolution of identity gives

  pi(A)=d int pi(A^(1/2))P_v pi(A^(1/2))dv.

The right integrand is a positive multiple of the coherent projector in
direction A^(1/2)v. Normalize its trace to obtain a positive coherent
mixture for rho, also for singular A by continuity. Covariant Phi_F has
uniform pure error Lambda_m(Q), so trace-norm convexity gives error <=that
value on every thermal rho_U. This is the valid UPPER mixture direction.

The normalized actual thermal sandwich kernel is
S_rho(X)=[d/Tr(rho^2)]int rho_U X rho_U dU, with eigenvalues c_l on H_(l,l).
It is unital TP, positive as a Hilbert-Schmidt operator and covariant. Equations
(2.2),(6.1) give
1-c_l>=w0^2(1-qbar)^2[1-b_l]/Tr(rho^2)>=(1-qbar)^4(1-b_l),
since Tr(rho^2)<=w0. Jensen applied to the positive coherent mixture gives
int||[rho_U,B]||^2<=int||[P_v,B]||^2, and Tr(rho^2)>=w0^2.
Thus

  (1-qbar)^4(1-b_l)<=1-c_l<=(1-qbar)^(-4)(1-b_l).          (6.3)

Combined with (2.3), this proves actual finite-temperature low-mode
coercivity comparable to min{l(l+2)/m,1}, uniformly over all harmonics.
Exact thermal eigenvalues need not be acquired to establish this comparison.

7. EXPLICIT TAPER UPPER AND THE NEAR-FULL BOUNDARY

For 0<=K<=m use F=f(N), f_N=(K+1-N)/(K+1) on N<=K and zero otherwise.
Its rank is (K+1)(K+2)/2. The exact radial energy and annihilation gap of
GLOBAL_CAPTURE with k=0 give coherent error

  e(Phi_F)<=2E(F)/(m Tr F^2)
           =2(4m-3K)/[m(K+2)]<=8/(K+2).                 (7.1)

The coherent input is rank one, so leakage is the whole-trace error. The
positive mixture transports this upper to all thermal states in (1.1).
Take K maximal with (K+1)(K+2)/2<=Q. This yields C/sqrt Q, establishing
(1.4) together with (3.4). The exact CP^2 phase/Gauss cubature of
GLOBAL_CAPTURE realizes this explicit radial channel with
C_frame=[(2m+1)(m+1)]^2=O(m^4) finite positive labels and total D<=C_frame Q.
All stored frame indices count; circuit runtime/gates remain unproved.

The power law fails near Q=d. A positive filter F=I-P_v has rank d-1 and

  sum_l(1-b_l)||F_l||^2/Tr F^2
      =[1-d/d_(2m)]/(d-1)=O(1/d)=O(m^(-2)),              (7.2)

because int|<v,w>|^(4m)dw=1/d_(2m). It gives pure/thermal upper O(m^(-2))
at Q=d-1, smaller than Q^(-1/2)=Theta(m^(-1)). The all-Q variational result
(1.3) remains valid; only the fixed-fraction simplification (1.4) is restricted.
