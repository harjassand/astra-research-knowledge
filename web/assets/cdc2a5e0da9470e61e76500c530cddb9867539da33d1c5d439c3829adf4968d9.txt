1. FINITE HEAT BATHS: UNIFORM MARGINALS WITHOUT A HIDDEN ERGODICITY ASSUMPTION
For a positive-volume compact square hard-constraint polytope K on n
free coordinates, a single-site conditional law is uniform on
  [a_x,b_x]=[max_{y~x} h_y-1, min_{y~x} h_y+1].
Degenerate intervals are updated to their single point. Random-scan
heat baths preserve uniform Lebesgue measure by conditional integration.
Both endpoints and the quantile (1-U)a_x+U b_x are increasing in the
neighbor heights. Every interval has length <=2.

For completeness the chain has a unique invariant law even when started
on a singular face. This needs no volume-uniform gap or mixing estimate.
Choose any interior g of K and delta in (0,1] smaller than the slack in
EVERY edge constraint involving a free coordinate. Positive full volume
ensures such strict slack; fixed-fixed constraints are excluded. Let
d_x=h_x-g_x, with d=0 on pins. If max_free d_x>delta/4, choose a maximizing
x. For every neighbor y,
  h_x-(h_y-1)=(g_x-g_y+1)+(d_x-d_y)>=delta,
so the conditional interval permits lowering h_x by any amount between
delta/16 and delta/8. This lowers sum|d| by at least delta/16 without
changing sign. If min_free d_x<-delta/4, the corresponding increasing
move is legal by the same argument. On compact K, sum|d| is uniformly
bounded. Thus at most T fixed such successful moves are needed to enter
C={||h-g||_infinity<=delta/4}. Each prescribed adaptive move has random-
scan probability at least delta/(32n), uniformly over its starting point.
After reaching C, pad the remaining steps by updates into
[g_x-delta/8,g_x+delta/8], which stay in C and have at least that probability.
Consequently P^T(h,C)>=p0>0 for EVERY h in K, with T,p0 depending on K.

From C, update coordinates 1,...,n once each, using the entire smaller
cube D={||h-g||_infinity<=delta/8}. Every such choice is legal since all
intermediate coordinates stay in C and edge slack remains at least
delta/2. Conditional densities are >=1/2, and the probability of the
chosen site order is n^(-n). Hence
  P^(T+n)(h,A)>=p0(2n)^(-n) Leb(A intersect D).
This is a fixed positive minorization by the uniform law on D for every
h in K. Coupling that minorized draw in successive blocks proves unique
invariance and convergence in total variation from every start. The
invariant law is the uniform law already known to be stationary. If n=0
the assertion is immediate. These constants may be extraordinarily bad
and depend on the whole polytope; no resource or uniform-mixing claim is
being made or used in the moment bound.

2. ONE ZERO-DATA SAMPLE COUPLED TO BOUNDED DATA
Take any feasible initial h_b with pin values |b|<=B. Define
h_0=T_B(h_b), the soft truncation sign(t)(|t|-B)_+. This is a feasible
zero-pinned field: T_B is 1-Lipschitz, and sends all pin values to zero.
Moreover |h_b-h_0|<=B coordinatewise. Define h_-=h_0-B and h_+=h_0+B,
which are feasible fields with all pins respectively -B and +B and
bracket h_b. Run these three finite heat baths with common sites and U.
Monotone conditional endpoints preserve h_-<=h_b<=h_+, and translation
equivariance preserves h_+=h_0+B, h_-=h_0-B exactly at every step.

By step 1 the middle and zero-data marginals converge to mu_b and mu_0.
Their joint laws live in a compact set, so any subsequential weak limit
is a coupling of these exact uniform marginals retaining the closed
inequality |h_b-h_0|<=B. This construction works simultaneously across
all free components; it does not choose a separate comparison sample for
each statistic.

For every deterministic q,
  |sum q_x h_b(x)-sum q_x h_0(x)|<=B sum|q_x|.
Thus if (a,M0) is the zero-data moment pair, the SAME exponent works and
  E_b exp(a|sum qh_b|)<=exp(a B C0) M0=:M_B.         (21)
Constants in (21) depend only on the permitted target parameters, despite
the auxiliary mixing argument's domain-dependent constants. Conditioning
first on bounded pin values gives the identical bound for all admissible
mixtures. This proves the full f:averages target in TARGET_AND_PREMISES.txt.

3. THE ACTUAL SMALL-MACROSCOPIC-TILT INTERFACE
Fix a support-box constant Cbox and, in the anchored alternative, an
anchor constant c0. Let R>=1 and deterministic coefficients t_x obey
  |t_x|<=alpha R^(-2),
with support in a box of diameter <=Cbox R. Assume either EXACT sum t_x=0
or the connected macroscopic pin anchor of the target with these geometry
constants. Put Y=sum t_x h_x. If alpha=0 the tilt is trivial. Otherwise
X=Y/alpha has weights q_x=t_x/alpha with max|q|<=R^(-2) and
sum|q|<=Cgeom, since the support has at most (Cbox+1)^2 R^2 vertices.
Choose the target constant C0 large enough for these fixed geometry
bounds. Let (a,M), M>=1, be the resulting zero- or bounded-data moment
constants already established above.

For 0<alpha<=a/2, the probability density
  W_Y=exp(Y)/(E exp(Y))
relative to that exact base law has a UNIFORM L2 bound. Indeed
  E exp(2Y)<=E exp(2alpha|X|)<=M,
and Jensen with the exponential-moment bound gives
  E|X|<=log(M)/a,
  E exp(Y)>=exp(EY)>=M^(-alpha/a).
Consequently
  ||W_Y||_2^2 <= M^(1+2alpha/a)<=M^2,
  ||W_Y||_2 <= M^(1/2+alpha/a)<=M=:K2.              (22)
At zero pins height reflection gives EY=0, so the stronger squared-norm
bound <=M holds. Neither conclusion requires independence, a regular
domain, a spectral gap, or a microscopic tilted-gradient bound.

For every event E and every admissible tested statistic T,
  P_Y(E)<=K2 P(E)^(1/2),
  E_Y |T|^m <=K2 (E|T|^(2m))^(1/2).
Thus all fixed moments stay bounded. If T has base exponential exponent
a_T, Cauchy-Schwarz also gives a positive tilted exponent a_T/2.
Combining finitely many tests is allowed only after verifying the summed
coefficient support, mass/maximum and zero-mass or anchor conditions,
or by Holder with a further amplitude reduction.

4. TILTED ZERO-DATA ANIMAL TRANSFER AND NO PARAMETER CIRCULARITY
Fix the moment proof's initial small animal parameter ONCE to obtain a,M
and K2. For any new desired tilted animal parameter 0<rho<1, invoke the
zero-data sparse theorem with parameter (rho/K2)^2, possibly changing its
local delta,delta0,k0,L tests. For any nonempty deterministic A,
  P_Y(A subset Bad_new)
     <=K2 [(rho/K2)^(2|A|)]^(1/2)
     =rho^|A| K2^(1-|A|)<=rho^|A|.
The empty A is automatic. The macroscopic L2 constants are NOT recomputed
from this new block choice, so there is no circular selection of small
tilt amplitude versus desired animal parameter.

This animal transfer is asserted for the ZERO-DATA base sparse theorem
and its small macroscopic tilts. Bounded-data moments/L2 do not by
themselves imply the same microscopic CAP rarity for arbitrary bounded
pins. Long-distance slack can transfer at additive cost 2B, but local
edge slack requires a separate argument if it is needed downstream.

This closes the requested small-tilt L2 gate from the exact square
macroscopic moment interface. It does not close reflected comparison,
uniqueness, force-transfer/recurrence, scalar positive response, covariance
identification, or the square GFF gate.
