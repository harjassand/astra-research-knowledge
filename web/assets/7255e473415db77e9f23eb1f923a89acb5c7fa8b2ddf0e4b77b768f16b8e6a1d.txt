1. c06_l03 / c06_l08: rational second-order gates and product error
-------------------------------------------------------------------
I independently reconstructed the local formulas and the L08 constant
bound. I found no algebraic gap in the displayed derivation, subject to the
stated positive-semidefinite local shift and imported counting theorem.

For an edge A_e, its eigenvalues are alpha-gamma, 3alpha+gamma (twice), and
5alpha-gamma. On |gamma|<=alpha they are ordered and nonnegative. Applying
p2(x)=1+x+x^2/2 gives the six-vertex weights

  low=p2(h(alpha-gamma)), mid=p2(h(3alpha+gamma)),
  high=p2(h(5alpha-gamma)),
  a=mid, beta=(high+low)/2, c=(high-low)/2.

Since p2 is increasing on [0,infinity), low<=a<=high, which implies
|a-beta|<=c. Also beta-c=low>=0 and a>0, so c<=a+beta. These are exactly
the triangle conditions for the complemented homogeneous quadratic. This
derives the local edge signature without relying on finite fixtures.

For a field A_v=rI+bX+cZ, r=b+|c|, its eigenvalues are nonnegative. The
matrix p2(hA_v) has rational entries

  U=1+hr+h^2(r^2+b^2+c^2)/2,
  w=h+h^2r,
  [[U+wc,wb],[wb,U-wc]].

With u=U-wc, v=U+wc, d=wb, its determinant is
uv-d^2=U^2-w^2(b^2+c^2)>0 because p2 is strictly positive on every
nonnegative eigenvalue of A_v. The existing two-dummy lift is rational and
log-concave when d^2<=uv. Thus the local gates stay within the original
physical XXZ cone.

For the global error, put h=beta/(2m), F_i=p2(hA_i), G_i=log(F_i),
S=sum_i G_i, g=sum_i ||G_i||. Since 1<=p2(x)<=exp(x) for x>=0,
0<=G_i<=hA_i and g<=hD. The symmetric product
B=F_1...F_p F_p...F_1 is a palindrome of exponentials. Its homogeneous
terms through degree two in the G_i match exp(2S): the degree-two difference
is one half the sum of pairwise commutators, which cancels under reversal.
For the remaining series, the sum of norms of all degree-k terms of either
expression is bounded by (2g)^k/k!. Hence

  ||B-exp(2S)|| <= 2 sum_{k>=3}(2g)^k/k!
                 <= (8/3)g^3 exp(2g).

When m>=2 beta D, g<=1/4, so exp(2g)<2 and this is at most
(16/3)h^3D^3.

Scalar calculus gives, for x in [0,1],
0<=x-log(p2(x))<=-log(1-x^3/6)<=x^3/5.
By spectral calculus and sum_i ||A_i||^3<=D^3,

  ||2S-2h sum_i A_i|| <= (2/5)h^3D^3.

Both B and exp(2S) are >=I. The integral resolvent formula for log shows
||log X-log Y||<=||X-Y|| for X,Y>=I, since the resolvent product norm is
bounded by (1+t)^-2 and its integral is one. Combining the two bounds gives

  ||m log B-beta(H+CI)|| <= (43/60)(beta D)^3/m^2.

Indeed (86/15)h^3D^3 is the one-step bound and h=beta/(2m). The L08 choice
m>=2 beta D and m^2>=43(beta D)^3/(15 epsilon) makes this <=epsilon/4.
No operator-monotonicity claim for exp is needed. This improves the Euler
layer count asymptotically, but the imported N^10 coefficient-count bound
still gives a very large polynomial; neither worker executed a complete
FPRAS. Priority of this exact gate/count composition remains unresolved.

Independent executable check: peer_audit.py verifies 80 rational edge and
80 rational field P2 gates exactly. All triangle margins pass and the
smallest field determinant margin in this sample is 1. These are checks of
the displayed formulas only; the universal result is the derivation above.

2. c06_s03: c_v=0 complement symmetry
-------------------------------------
For a pair gate, the six monomials occur in complementary pairs with equal
coefficients. For a field gate, complementing all four local variables pairs
d z0z1 with dxy, (u/2)xz0 with (v/2)yz1, and (u/2)xz1 with
(v/2)yz0. Therefore the lifted local coefficient measure is complement-
invariant exactly when u=v, which holds for c=0. Nonzero c generally breaks
this symmetry. A wire factor's one-of-two support is complement-invariant;
the rank-f uniform dummy support among 2f coordinates is also invariant.
Products retain the symmetry.

For a common-ground-set pair (S,T) of half-size supports, the involution
(S,T)->(U\S,U\T) preserves |S intersect T|, since its new value is
|U|-|S|-|T|+|S intersect T|=|S intersect T|. Thus the soft law with weight
mu(S)nu(T)t^|S intersect T| has equal zero-copy and two-copy marginals at
every coordinate. This part of c06_s03's route is correct.

I enumerated two small exact rational soft laws independently. For a pair plus
a c=0 field (N=8, t=1/16384), all coordinates have p0=p2, both measures are
complement-invariant, and the hard complementary event has probability
11024811887802974208/11031462472350040217 (>3/4). A single field with
c=1/3 gives an exact control with p0!=p2; nu remains symmetric while mu does
not. Thus the zero-Z-field restriction is load-bearing for this symmetry
argument, not a cosmetic simplification.

I also tested whether opposite pure-Z fields might cancel the complement
defect globally. With two b=0 field gates c=+/-1/3 and the same s=1/5, take
field. Its exact coefficient ratio mu(S)/mu(U-S)=289/225. The local ratios
cancel for one orientation pattern but square for another, because the
complement ratio depends on which of the two cross terms is selected at each
field. Thus balancing the sum or pairing
the signed field values does not by itself restore the symmetry needed by
this particular hard-complement sampler.

The all-size lower bound 3/4 still depends on the imported uniform-field
Poincare statement (Chen-Liu Lemma 5.5) plus the peer's derivation and sample
wrapper. My finite enumeration does not validate that gap, the local-chain
mixing guarantee, bounded-tape probability over all calls, or a full FPRAS.
The peer explicitly reports that the wrapper is not yet executed. Treat the
result as CONDITIONAL_ON_CHEN_LIU_LEMMA_5_5, with the exact finite symmetry
checks separately VERIFIED_INTERNAL.

3. c06_s01: higher-spin projector
----------------------------------
The site projector formula passed an independent small-q audit. For q
constituent qubits, the symmetric projector has matrix entry
1/binom(q,|r|) when row and column have equal Hamming weight and zero otherwise.
With complemented columns, its polynomial is

  p_q(x,y)=sum_{r=0}^q e_r(x)e_{q-r}(y)/binom(q,r).

Every q-subset of its 2q variables appears, so the support is the uniform
rank-q matroid and is M-convex. For a derivative with a row and b column
variables differentiated, a+b=q-2, the remaining Hessian has row-sum-zero
eigenvalue -A and column-sum-zero eigenvalue -D; its two-dimensional sum block
is [[A(m-1), B sqrt(m ell)],[B sqrt(m ell),D(ell-1)]], where
m=q-a, ell=q-b, A=1/binom(q,a+2), B=1/binom(q,a+1), D=1/binom(q,a).
The exact identity

  A D (m-1)(ell-1)=B^2 m ell

makes that block positive semidefinite with one positive eigenvalue and one
zero eigenvalue. Branden-Huh's Lorentzian characterization then gives the
all-q result. I verified the projector matrix idempotence, trace q+1, support
size binom(2q,q), and these derivative identities for q=1..5 (10 nontrivial
derivatives), and checked [Pi,Hhat]=0 exactly for one spin-1 plus spin-1/2
rational XXZ/field instance.

This supports, but does not independently certify, the full all-q theorem or
the full compressed Euler/counting construction. The source theorem supplies
the crucial Lorentzian-to-log-concave implication. The peer correctly
distinguishes the failed one-boundary norm comparison and the q=4 failure of
I+s Pi as a local infinitesimal LC gate; the every-layer compression is the
substantive repair. The source's FPRAS remains imported, and higher-spin
priority is not resolved here.

4. c06_l05: easy-axis local boundary
------------------------------------
The existing pair-gate Hessian eigenvalues outside the admitted cone include
2s(gamma-alpha)>0 when gamma>alpha, and -2s(gamma+alpha)>0 when gamma<-alpha.
Together with the always-positive all-ones eigenvalue, either outside side
has at least two positive eigenvalues. At alpha=1,s=1/10,gamma=+/-11/10,
the positive log-Hessian eigenvalue at all variables one is exactly 1/280 on
both sides. I independently reproduced this exact witness. Restricting a
log-concave local lift to fixed positive auxiliary coordinates preserves
log-concavity; taking a fixed multiaffine auxiliary coefficient by derivative
and zero specialization preserves it as well. Hence these direct local-lift
routes cannot simply extend the gate to |gamma|>alpha. This is not a hardness
result and does not exclude a global correlated encoding.

5. c06_l01: prior-art scope and comparator boundaries
------------------------------------------------------
The literature conclusions are attributed to c06_l01, not independently
validated by these small checks. I independently recomputed its exact
nonzero-gamma gate fixture. At alpha=1, s=1/10, gamma=+/-1/2, the linear
triangle inequalities hold, while exactly one of the old squared-triangle
inequalities fails by 11/50. This supports the narrow claim that the
Cai-Liu-Lu squared-triangle theorem does not directly cover these gates; it
does not certify the theorem statement or show hardness.

The EPR endpoint identity is exact:

  P_PsiPlus=(I+XX+YY-ZZ)/4,
  XX+YY-ZZ=4 P_PsiPlus-I.

The common Rx(pi/2) rotation maps P_PhiPlus to P_PsiPlus. Therefore the
Chen-Liu gamma=-alpha, zero-field endpoint is a genuine overlap after a scalar
shift and basis rotation, not a full arbitrary-field result. c06_l01's other
scope observations (the Bravyi-Gosset gamma=0,b=0 slice, HMS zero-location
versus approximation distinction, and the high-temperature comparator) are
useful guards against overclaiming novelty. Their source-theorem coverage and
literature completeness remain unverified here.

6. c06_l04: local boundary and mixed-field trace cube
-----------------------------------------------------
The c06_l04 boundary calculation gives the same exact anisotropy obstruction
as c06_l05, so these are corroborating reports of one local route failure,
not separate discoveries. For a shifted edge gate
F=I+s(kappa I+alpha(XX+YY)+gamma ZZ), the row/column-complemented quadratic
has coefficients a=1+s(kappa+gamma), b=1+s(kappa-gamma), c=2s alpha.
Its anisotropy-sensitive Hessian eigenvalues are

  a-b-c=2s(gamma-alpha),  -a+b-c=-2s(gamma+alpha).

They do not depend on the scalar shift kappa. Outside |gamma|<=alpha one is
positive, so at the all-ones point the corresponding log-Hessian curvature
is positive as well. Increasing kappa changes the positive normalization,
but cannot change this sign. This certifies failure of that direct local
quadratic route only, not a global hardness result.

For the separate two-site identity, direct Pauli multiplication gives

  Tr(H^3)=24 alpha (b1*b2-alpha*gamma),
  H=alpha(XX+YY)+gamma ZZ+b1 XI+b2 IX.

The only identity-producing triples are {XX,YY,ZZ}, whose product is -I,
and {XX,XI,IX}, whose product is +I; each contributes six orderings and
Tr(I)=4. My Fraction matrix check independently verified the formula on 32
fixed-seed rational instances. In particular the moment may change sign with
the field signs. This does not threaten positivity of Tr(exp(beta H)) or the
shifted-gate construction; it blocks only an inference that every odd
moment is positive. During harness development the first matrix check caught
the real-representation sign convention for YY (iY tensor iY equals -YY); I
corrected the representation and reran the full suite successfully.
