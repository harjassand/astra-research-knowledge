# C13 immune-dynamics continuation: frozen partial state

The C12 audit is unchanged. C13 identified a more promising dynamical target in two 2026 Cell Systems primary studies of trained human macrophages. One reports stimulus-specific training memory; the companion reports >90,000 single-cell RNA-FISH counts across 26 donors and an early trained-response advantage followed by later untrained catch-up. The source data README advertises donor-, condition-, stimulus-, time-, field-, morphology-, and per-gene spot-count columns, with a 0–8 h kinetic subset that appears to cover two donors.

I acquired article abstracts/indexed text, the Dryad landing page, its anonymous API manifest, and the data-schema description. I did not acquire or analyze numeric cell data. The Dryad byte endpoints responded 401/403. The authors' Dropbox shared folder resolves to a 4.214 GB whole-folder ZIP; I inspected only response metadata and its initial ZIP bytes and did not download it. The publisher full texts also returned 403. Exact access boundary and hashes are recorded in [RESTART.md](RESTART.md) and [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

The prospective capability test is whether trained single-cell response distributions can be predicted at held-out times by one donor-specific response-time map shared across genes, after competing against gene-specific kinetics, changes in activation probability/per-cell transcript counts, and donor/date/density/morphology mixture models. That is a model contrast, not a C13 result; cross-sectional destructive FISH cannot establish individual-cell trajectories. Donor-blocked holdout, exact independent sample reconstruction, and a cell-level distribution score are required. See [partial_dynamical_interface.md](proof/partial_dynamical_interface.md).

No theorem, new biological law, quantitative held-out prediction, or control capability has been established. The parent requested a quick freeze; the authorized next state is preserved in this package. Do not elevate the source's “faster, not stronger” interpretation into an independent conclusion.
