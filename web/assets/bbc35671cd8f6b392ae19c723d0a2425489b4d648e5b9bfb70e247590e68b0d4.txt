OPTIMAL ONE-MATRIX-QUERY ACQUISITION OF SPARSE NC POLYNOMIALS
2026-10-07. Derived in this run from the complete July2026 primary sparse hitting proof.
Status: unconditional mathematical consequence of the explicit affine-action identity; exact proof below. Historical novelty unverified. Standard Prony/Hankel recovery is the decoder, so no novelty claimed for it.

THEOREM.
Given n,s>=1 and an exact full-matrix evaluation oracle for an unknown polynomial f in Q<x_1,...,x_n> with at most s nonzero words, ONE explicitly constructed rational/integer matrix tuple of dimension 2s determines and reconstructs the entire sparse polynomial, including every ordered word and rational coefficient. There is no supplied degree bound. Matrix entries have O(s log(n+1)) bits. Deterministic decoding takes polynomial time in n,s and the actual bit length of the returned rational matrix. Dimension2s is optimal for any fixed single-query universal learner whose query and unknown coefficients are over the same field (in particular rational inputs/coefficients).

The bit-time bound is output-sensitive. A succinct circuit of exponential degree can return exponentially many bits and have exponentially long explicit words; neither evaluation nor reconstruction is polynomial in that circuit's succinct size. Exact active noncommuting matrix queries are supplied. Passive samples, numerical stability and succinct compressed-word reconstruction are not obtained.

EXPLICIT QUERY AND PROOF.
Set B=n+1 and dimension D=2s. Index rows/columns by0,...,D-1. For each letter i=1,...,n define upper-triangular integer matrix
 (A_i)_(a,b) = binom(b,a) B^a i^(b-a) for a<=b; zero otherwise.
Let v_D(z)=(1,z,...,z^(D-1)). The binomial theorem gives
 v_D(z) A_i = v_D(Bz+i).
For word w=i_1...i_l, put
 alpha(w)=B^l + sum_(t=1)^l i_t B^(l-t), alpha(empty)=1.
Its base-B representation has a leading1 followed by digits i_t in{1,...,n}; hence alpha is injective on all finite ordered words and exactly decodable. Therefore
 v_D(1) A_w = v_D(alpha(w)).
These are precisely the explicit matrices and identity proved in Lakhani-Mukhopadhyay, ECCC TR26-120 rev2, Lemmas2.3/2.4. We use their same construction with twice the sparsity bound.

Write actual f=sum_(j=1)^r c_j w_j, r<=s, c_j nonzero, alpha_j=alpha(w_j) distinct positive integers. From the single full response F=f(A), column sums give the2s exact moments
 t_k=(v_D(1)F)_k=sum_(j=1)^r c_j alpha_j^k, 0<=k<2s.
Build s-by-s Hankel H=(t_(a+b))_(0<=a,b<s). If V=(alpha_j^a)_(0<=a<s,1<=j<=r), then H=V diag(c_j) V^T. More importantly its leading r-by-r block H_r is invertible:
 det H_r = (product_j c_j) (product_(i<j)(alpha_j-alpha_i))^2 !=0.
All (r+1)-minors vanish, so rank H=r. This remains true with signed/rational coefficients, even if early moments or smaller leading principal minors vanish. No positivity assumption is used. Compute r exactly; ifr=0, f=0.

Let p(z)=z^r+sum_(j=0)^(r-1) p_j z^j. Solve H_r p=-[t_r,...,t_(2r-1)]^T. The true annihilator product_j(z-alpha_j) satisfies these equations, because for k=0,...,r-1,
 sum_(j=0)^r p_j t_(k+j)=sum_i c_i alpha_i^k p(alpha_i)=0.
Invertibility of H_r gives uniqueness, so the computed p is exactly product_i(z-alpha_i). Its distinct roots are positive integers. Recover them deterministically by exact polynomial integer-root isolation/factorization; then solve the r-by-r Vandermonde system t_k=sum_i c_i alpha_i^k, 0<=k<r, to recover coefficients. Base-B decoding of the roots recovers ordered words. This proves exact reconstruction.

BIT COST, WITHOUT AN UNCHARGED ROOT-FINDING ORACLE.
Let L be the total bit length of supplied rational moments; it is polynomially bounded by the full response size. Rational Gaussian elimination and determinant bounds show that p's coefficient bit lengths are polynomial in(s,L). An elementary exact deterministic root-isolation procedure suffices: compute a Sturm sequence overQ; a Cauchy root bound has polynomial bit length in p; recursively bisect rational intervals, discard intervals withzero roots, and isolate the r promised simple INTEGER roots to width<1/2. At each depth there are O(r) nonempty intervals; number of depths is polynomial in coefficient bits. Root counts and signs use rational polynomial arithmetic of polynomial degree/height. Handle a rational midpoint root explicitly. Rounding the unique isolated integer roots and verifying p(alpha)=0 gives them in polynomial bit time. Alternatively standard exactQ polynomial factoring may be used.

Root magnitude logarithms are polynomial in(s,L) by the same Cauchy bound; thus explicit base-B word decoding itself is polynomial in(s,L), and no hidden exponentially large output is claimed. Vandermonde solve uses integers/rationals of polynomial height. The query matrices' entries are at most2^(D)(n+1)^D, so their bit length is O(s log(n+1)). All relevant bits are charged.

OPTIMAL MATRIX DIMENSION.
Suppose one fixed d-by-d matrix tuple T over a field K universally identifies every at-most-s-sparse nc polynomial overK, and d<2s. The minimal polynomial mu(T_1) is a nonzero univariate polynomial of degree<=d and at most d+1<=2s nonzero terms. Partition its support into two sets of size<=s, write mu=g-h with each g,h at mosts sparse and g!=h. Then g(T)=h(T), contradicting universal acquisition. Thus d>=2s. Our rational construction realizes the bound exactly. This is an identifiability bound for acquisition, extending the primary paper's d>=s single-query PIT bound.

OPTIONAL FIXED-DIAGONAL READOUT FORM.
The2s moments can also be acquired with exactly2s scalar diagonal queries at the same dimension2s. For each column j, choose a unimodular integer matrix P_j whose first row is the all-ones row, and whose remaining rows are standard rows e_k^T for k!=j. Then P_j^-1 e_1=e_j, and
 (P_j f(A) P_j^-1)_(1,1) = ones f(A)e_j=t_j.
Query the simultaneously conjugated tuple P_j A_i P_j^-1. Thus full response is convenient batching; exactly2s scalar calls suffice, with polynomial integer input bits. For unrestricted coefficients, at leasts scalar responses are needed on a fixed support of sizes: each response is one linear functional of those s coefficients, and adaptive zero-transcript linear algebra gives the lower bound. This does not contradict finite-height scalar radix packing, which charges total returnedbits.

BOUNDARIES.
Over a fixed finite field, a fixed tuple generates a finite matrix semigroup, so two words have identical evaluations. Their difference is2 sparse; therefore a universal one-query learner for unbounded-length words is impossible there. The primary paper's large-extension positive-characteristic result needs the degree-related field representation contract; it does not supply free polynomial explicit field bits. Our theorem is overQ/characteristiczero.

The moment/Vandermonde decoder may be badly conditioned numerically; no noisy approximate analogue is proved. This is active exact algebraic acquisition, not a practical hardware oracle. The single response can have huge height when words are long.
