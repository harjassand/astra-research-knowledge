# Sources and prior-art boundary

1. **McDonald & Yuksel (2020), “Exponential Filter Stability via Dobrushin's Coefficient.”** [arXiv primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08463). Theorem 12 treats controlled partially observed processes and bounds filter stability using transition and measurement Dobrushin coefficients. It establishes the relevant contraction/filter-stability framework, not the weak-reporter \(\kappa^4\) transcript-KL or adaptive testing lower bound proved here.
2. **Le Gland & Mevel (2000), “Exponential Forgetting and Geometric Ergodicity in Hidden Markov Models.”** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00009861). Primary prior art for exponential forgetting of predictive filters under HMM assumptions. It is not an information-theoretic minimax lower bound for the present action class.
3. **Gopich & Szabo (2009), “Decoding the Pattern of Photon Colors in Single-Molecule FRET.”** [Primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2802060/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp903671p). Likelihood prior art for photon-color/interarrival-time HMM analysis; it is relevant to the equally informed full-likelihood control, not evidence that such a likelihood is physically calibrated for the acquisition theorem.
4. **Zhang et al. (2025), “Observation of metastability in open quantum dynamics of a solid-state system.”** [Nature Communications article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64772-6), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64772-6). Primary NV-\(^{14}\)N repeated-read source; full operating-point audit is in ../robust_nv/REPORT.md and ../robust_nv/SOURCES.md.
5. **Hohng et al. (2007), “Fluorescence-force spectroscopy maps two-dimensional reaction landscape of the Holliday junction.”** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3558530/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1146113). Primary reversible force-smFRET protocol; experimental limitations are detailed in ../application_smfret/REPORT.md.
