EXACT STATEMENT

Let F_1,...,F_8 be an HS-orthonormal traceless Hermitian basis of M_3,
T_a=sum_i F_a^(i), Q_N=sum_a T_a^2, and

 rho_N=e^[(3/(2N)) Q_N]/Tr e^[(3/(2N)) Q_N].

If rho_N has ANY positive identical-product representation
 rho_N=int eta^tensor N dmu_N(eta)
over normalized one-qutrit states eta, then, for every N>=4254,

 mu_N{||eta-I/3||_HS>1/32}>=13/12270>0.              (1)

Thus no such exact representation can supply an exp(-cN) near-central seed
tail on this fixed small ball. The statement neither disproves separability
nor excludes positive representations centered at ordered local states.
It does not concern higher-spin SU(2), or the decoder problem.

1. CASIMIR NORMALIZATION AND A PRODUCT FREE-ENERGY WITNESS

HS completeness gives
 sum_a F_a tensor F_a=Swap-I tensor I/3,
 sum_a F_a^2=(3-1/3) I.
Consequently for any one-qutrit state eta,

 Tr[eta^tensor N Q_N]
   =N^2(Tr eta^2-1/3)+N(3-Tr eta^2).                 (2)

The analogous general-d coefficient whose quadratic entropy Hessian at I/d
vanishes is beta=d/2 in e^(beta Q_N/N): for eta=I/d+X, the free-entropy
functional is S(eta)-log d+beta Tr X^2, whose quadratic term is
(-d/2+beta)Tr X^2. For d=2, Q_N=2 J^2 and beta=1 exactly matches
c07_s02's 2J^2/N. For d=3, beta=3/2 is a local spinodal, not the actual
first-order transition point. Equating those points would be another error.

Take eta_star=diag(1/2,1/4,1/4). Its entropy is (3/2)log 2 and its purity
is 3/8. At beta=3/2 the per-site free-energy excess over I/3 is

 f= (3/2)log2-log3+(3/2)(3/8-1/3)
  = (1/2)[1/8-log(9/8)]>0.                            (3)

More quantitatively, log(1+x)<=x-x^2/2+x^3/3 for x>=0 follows by integrating
1/(1+t)<=1-t+t^2. At x=1/8 this yields

 f>=11/3072.                                         (4)

No thermodynamic theorem or numerical estimate is needed for positivity.

2. A QUANTUM FINITE-N LOWER BOUND ON COLLECTIVE PURITY

Put p_N(beta)=log[3^-N Tr e^(beta Q_N/N)]. Gibbs variational principle with
the honest product trial eta_star^tensor N and (2) yields

 p_N(3/2)>=N f+(3/2)(3-Tr eta_star^2)>=N f.

The finite-dimensional function p_N is convex, p_N(0)=0, and
p_N'(beta)=Tr(rho_N(beta) Q_N/N). The tangent/secant inequality gives
p_N'(beta)>=p_N(beta)/beta. Hence at beta=3/2,

 Tr(rho_N Q_N)/N^2>=f/(3/2)>=11/4608.                (5)

Gibbs variational itself follows directly from positivity of relative entropy
to e^(beta Q_N/N)/Z; it does not assume commuting input matrices.

3. EVERY POSITIVE IID LAW MUST PLACE CONSTANT MASS OUTSIDE

Let r=1/32 and p be the mu_N mass with ||eta-I/3||_HS>r. Purity satisfies
Tr eta^2-1/3=||eta-I/3||_HS^2, which is <=r^2 inside and <=2/3 everywhere.
Combining (2), (5), and the loose bound 3-Tr eta^2<=3 gives

 11/4608 <= r^2+p(2/3-r^2)+3/N.

The fixed gap is
 11/4608-r^2=13/9216.
For N>=ceil[6/(13/9216)]=4254, 3/N<=13/18432, so

 p >= [13/18432]/[2/3-1/1024]=13/12270,

proving (1). This is a bound for EVERY positive normalized iid-product law,
not just a guessed Hubbard-Stratonovich or heat-kernel formula.

For an approximating positive mixture with half-trace error e_N, the same
argument replaces the numerator by
 13/9216-3/N-16e_N,
because ||Q_N||<=8N^2 and trace duality gives an observable error <=16e_N.
In particular exponentially accurate approximants also cannot put vanishing
outer mass on this ball. The loose operator constant does not affect the
qualitative obstruction.

4. BASELINE AND LITERATURE RELATION

In general d,
 Q_N=(Nd-N^2/d)I+2 sum_(i<j) Swap_ij.
Thus beta Q_N/N differs by a scalar from the transposition-model Hamiltonian
with inverse-temperature parameter 2beta. Bjornberg arXiv:1512.06986,
Theorem1.2, gives its discontinuous transition for d>=3 and parameter
beta'_c=2(d-1)/(d-2) log(d-1). At d=3 this is 4log2<3, consistent with
the explicit spinodal witness here. That literature establishes the phase
behavior; it is NOT a premise of the finite-N inequalities (1)-(5).

The elementary entropy/purity witness is therefore not claimed as novel
phase-transition physics. Its contribution here is the exact interface
obstruction: a critical SU(d) seed cannot be assumed to inherit the source's
near-mixed qubit tail needed for the stopped comparison. A different seed
acquisition/positivity region must be supplied for a broader theorem.
