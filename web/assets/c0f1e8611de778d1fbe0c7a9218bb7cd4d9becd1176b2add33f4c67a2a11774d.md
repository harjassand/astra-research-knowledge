# Primary-source notes for the constructive compiler audit

Search date: 2026-10-08. Scope: channel compatibility, finite-output broadcasting and de Finetti, random-matrix concentration, and matrix multiplicative weights. This is a bounded primary-source pass, not exhaustive novelty clearance.

| Source | Primary statement checked | Collision boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Heinosaari & Miyadera, [*Incompatibility of quantum channels*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.01794), arXiv:1608.01794 (2017), abstract and compatibility characterization | Compatible channels are marginals of one broadcasting channel; the paper characterizes compatibility using concatenation/conjugation and identifies the self-compatibility/antidegradability structure. | Establishes that the compiler's input is a standard 2-self-compatible channel interface. It does not construct a common canonical EB comparator or a Loewner comparison with (I-\Phi). |
| Heinosaari, Kiukas, Reitzner & Schultz, [*Incompatibility breaking quantum channels*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.05768), arXiv:1504.05768 (2015), §§3–5 | Studies channels that turn relevant classes of measurements jointly measurable; EB channels are incompatibility breaking, and the converse does not hold in general. | Joint measurability of selected output PVMs is weaker than production of one EB channel that approximates every test direction in operator order. |
| Xie, Fang, Wang & Duan, [*Approximate broadcasting of quantum correlations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.06071), arXiv:1705.06071 (2017), Lemma 3 / Eq. (3) | Gives an absolute diamond-distance approximation by EB channels for most marginals of an (n)-output broadcasting channel; the error contains explicit dimension and output-count dependence. | This is a close many-output result, but it assumes many outputs of the same broadcast map and uses diamond distance. The recursive binary tree has single-leaf marginal (\Phi^m), not (\Phi), so it is not a many-output extension of the target marginal. |
| Chiribella, [*On quantum estimation, quantum cloning and finite quantum de Finetti theorems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.1875), arXiv:1010.1875 (2010), Theorem 2.2 | A symmetric (M)-receiver broadcast channel has a measure-and-prepare approximation to its (k)-receiver restriction with diamond error at most (2kd^2/M) (using the paper's full-space bound). | Stronger for a premise of many symmetric extensions of the same channel. Two-output self-compatibility gives no such (M\gg1) extension of (\Phi); iterating the given broadcaster instead changes the leaf channel to (\Phi^m). |
| Ye, Bergh & Datta, [*Quantum de Finetti theorems for states and channels in any distance measure*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40343), arXiv:2609.40343 (submitted 2026-09-30; preprint) | States and channels receive operator-order de Finetti statements; permutation-covariant maps (N^{(n)}:A^{\otimes n}\to B^{\otimes n}) have reduced channels CP-dominated by mixtures of tensor-power channels with stated polynomial dimension dependence and (O(k/\sqrt n)) error. | Recent and relevant to CP-order channel approximation, but its (n)-input/no-signalling or permutation-covariant interface is different from one input with a two-output self-broadcaster. No direct theorem collision identified. |
| Tropp, [*User-Friendly Tail Bounds for Sums of Random Matrices*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.4389), arXiv:1004.4389 / Found. Comput. Math. 12 (2012) | Proves matrix Bernstein, Chernoff, and related concentration inequalities for independent self-adjoint random matrices. | Direct standard support for the covariance and POVM-completion bounds. The application-specific facts are (\|d^2|\rho-I/d\rangle\langle\rho-I/d|\|\le d-1), (Y^2\preceq(d-1)Y), and (\mathbb E\rho=I/d). |
| Arora, Hazan & Kale, [*The Multiplicative Weights Update Method: a Meta-Algorithm and Applications*](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v008a006/v008a006.pdf), Theory of Computing 8 (2012) | Gives the standard multiplicative-weights meta-algorithm and its matrix/approximate-SDP applications. | Supports the regret layer once the support response is supplied. The plus-sign maximizing update and exact loss convention must be matched to largest-eigenvalue control; the report's Eq. (17) has a missing (+) in the typeset display. |

The result collision tested here is specifically: one explicit common canonical EB channel Psi-hat with

\[
I-\widehat\Psi\preceq2\lceil\log_2(d^2-1)\rceil(I-\Phi)+\delta I,
\]

for a rational dense 2-self-compatible, unital, TP, HS-selfadjoint marginal. No inspected source states this same interface. The compiler layer is an internal construction assembled from the audited tree response and standard concentration/MW tools; this source pass does not claim publication priority or independent external proof validation.
