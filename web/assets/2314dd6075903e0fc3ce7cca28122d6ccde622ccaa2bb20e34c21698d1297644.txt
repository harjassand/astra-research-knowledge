STRONGEST CLAIM

For a finite bistochastic qubit instrument with outcome effects M_y, total
channel L, and any reference-preserving measure-and-prepare branch Q using
those same effects, put C=(L+Q)/2 and Phi=C^*C with tau=Tr/2. Then

  Phi=Delta_lambda iff 0<=lambda<=(3+sqrt(5))/18 ~= 0.2909.

The upper bound holds for any finite number of outcomes and multiple Kraus
operators per outcome. A four-outcome tetrahedral POVM with its Lüders
instrument and tetrahedral pure preparations attains the endpoint. REV1's
canonical same-effect subclass reaches exactly [0,1/4]; the broader branch
preparation class strictly enlarges this qubit depolarizing range. The
universal qubit cloner marginal Delta_(2/3) is excluded from both instrument-
Petz representation classes.

PROOF AND REPAIR

The universal upper-bound proof, including the multi-Kraus per-effect trace
inequality, is in `revisions/REV2.txt`. It reduces C's Bloch matrix to sO,
uses the CP bound s<=1/3 for det(O)=-1, and for det(O)=+1 combines the
entanglement-fidelity bound on L with the same-effect preparation bound on Q:

  6s <= 1 + sum_y p_y(2 sqrt(1-u_y)+sqrt(u_y)) <= 1+sqrt(5),

where u_y=|a_y|^2 and M_y=p_y(I+a_y.sigma). Therefore
s<= (1+sqrt(5))/6.

REV2's original final sentence had an attainability gap: its canonical
construction alone covers only [0,1/4]. `revisions/REV2_ADDENDUM.txt` repairs
this without modifying INITIAL or REV2. The tetrahedral construction has

  s(a)=(1+2 sqrt(1-a^2)+a)/6.

It is continuous and strictly increasing on [0,1/sqrt(5)], with s(0)=1/2
and s(1/sqrt(5))=(1+sqrt(5))/6. Hence s(a)^2 fills
[1/4,(3+sqrt(5))/18] by the intermediate value theorem. REV1's canonical
Pauli instrument fills [0,1/4], completing the iff claim. The revised exact
script passed:

  python3 work/cycle6/c04_l09/qubit_general_branch_cap.py

This finite symbolic check verifies tetrahedral moments, endpoint radicals,
the derivative identity, and exact comparisons. The universal upper bound and
