ALGEBRAIC COMPLEXITY — CYCLE 2 LEAD SYNTHESIS
Fixed Gutzwiller exact filter, weighted matching transfer, and representation barriers
8 October 2026

STATUS
======
No general polynomial-time approximation scheme or reverse approximation-
preserving reduction for the fixed no-double-occupancy normalizer was found.
The strongest exact positive result is a one-query reduction from nonnegative
rational weighted perfect matchings to a half-filled rational-matrix filter
Z_{N/2}(A). Two complete constructions now preserve that reduction: a compact
dyadic square-root chart and a larger exact rational-square-variant chart.
Both have polynomial bit cost, explicit zero handling, and exact finite
replays. This reduction no longer yields a new open-problem consequence:
the pinned September 2026 OA113 manuscript source-reports an FPRAS for simple
unweighted general-graph perfect matchings, and the separate PC-05 binary
edge gadget conditionally extends it to nonnegative rational weights. Neither
the OA113 proof nor PC-05 has independent external validation in this packet.

The representation audit gives exact small counterexamples to stability for
the natural fixed-sector row-activity polynomial, a stable exactly solvable
block-pair class, a promised importance-sampling FPRAS, and two route-specific
sampling/encoding obstructions. These do not prove general hardness or rule
out alternative determinant lifts.

TARGET AND EXACT MATCHING TRANSFER
=================================
For rational A and 0<=k<=floor(n/2),

  Z_k(A) = sum_{|I|=|J|=k, I intersect J = empty} det(A[I,J])^2.

For a weighted even-order simple graph H with nonnegative rational edge
weights w_e, let PM_w(H)=sum_M product_{e in M} w_e. The half-filled target
has n=N and k=N/2, supplied with the instance; k grows with N. Every legal
pair is then (S,S^c).

The compact chart is proved in
`determinant_beyond_degree/WEIGHTED_PM_TO_Z.md`. After removing zero-weight
edges and testing support feasibility, pad a sparse support graph with a
forced unit edge if needed. Normalize each positive edge by M=max w_e and
approximate sqrt(w_e/M) from below by a dyadic rational a_e. Put one paired
site on each graph edge, with columns e_u and a_e e_v, and add private dummy
pairs. If B is the first-column matrix, C the second-column matrix, and

  R(t) = sum_{|S|=k} det([b_j+t e_j,c_j]_{j in S})^2,

then the zero-perturbation sum is the normalized weighted matching count up
to the charged square-root approximation. For dyadic t=2^-q, T=B+tI is
invertible and A^T=T^-1 C satisfies

  R(t) = det(T)^2 Z_k(A).

Explicit choices give |R(t)-P| <= rho P/16 for normalized source value P.
The source zero case is decided exactly first, since a zero-source star can
have R(t)>0 after perturbation. On a positive source, one target FPRAS query
at error rho/4 followed by exact rational rescaling gives relative error at
most 21rho/64. The corrected proof distinguishes the source matching size
r=m/2 from target sector size k=N/2. It charges dense chart size, dyadic
precision, exact arithmetic, and conditioning, with log kappa_2(T)=
O(N(q+log N)); no floating-point inverse is assumed.

The independent exact-amplitude construction is proved in
`border_rank_bits/RESULT.txt`. For w=p/q, write pq as a binary sum of squares;
each term yields one or two rational amplitudes alpha with sum alpha^2=w.
Use one paired site per edge variant and the same dummy-coordinate device.
Two variants of one source edge cannot occur together in a nonzero determinant
because they repeat an endpoint row. Summing the squared determinants over
variant choices therefore gives exactly

  R(0)=PM_w(H),     R(t)=det(B+tI)^2 Z_k(A_t).

For W=[z^k] product_s(1+alpha_s^2 z), the proof establishes
|R(t)-PM_w(H)| <= 2NWt when Nt<=1/2. A support matching supplies a positive
lower bound L, and a denominator product Delta clears the source grid. The
leaf gives explicit dyadic parameters for exact nearest-grid recovery and
for a one-query relative-error transfer. It uses O(log(pq)) variants per
edge, removing square-root approximation bias and normalization at the cost
of a larger target matrix. Both reductions are polynomial in input bit size;
neither is a fine-grained exponent-preserving reduction or an approximation-
hardness theorem.

DETERMINANT, STABILITY, AND SAMPLING FINDINGS
============================================
For real rational A define the row-activity polynomial

  F_{A,k}(x)=sum_{|S|=k} (sum_{J subset S^c, |J|=k} det A[S,J]^2) x^S.

Its coefficients are nonnegative and F(1)=Z_k(A), but it need not be real
stable or Rayleigh. For the directed C4 permutation at k=2,
F=x1*x3+x2*x4; it has an exact zero with x1=x3=1+i and x2=x4=-1+i,
and Rayleigh difference Delta_13=-1 at the all-ones point. This is dimension-
minimal for this row-activity polynomial. For the 0/1 path P5 adjacency at
k=2, the exact polynomial has coefficients (in lexicographic 2-subsets)
(0,1,2,1,1,3,2,1,1,0), has the rational upper-half-plane zero recorded in
`determinant_finiteness/RESULT.txt`, and has Delta_14=-99/100 at a positive
real point. Thus the non-stability is not confined to the full sector.

Every nonzero same-variable pencil c det(sum_i x_i M_i) with Hermitian PSD
M_i is real stable: in the upper half-plane the pencil has positive-definite
imaginary part and is nonsingular. The C4/P5 examples therefore rule out
that direct PSD-pencil or standard positive mixed-discriminant representation
of these natural polynomials. They do not rule out signed pencils, auxiliary
variables, projections, or other algorithms. The paired-column Gram support
is linear-matroid parity, not a #SAT support family.

Positive structured cases and algorithmic limits:

* For A=direct-sum_b [[0,a_b],[b_b,0]] with nonnegative rational entries,
  F_{A,k}=e_k(a_b^2 x_{b,L}+b_b^2 x_{b,R}) and
  Z_k=e_k(a_b^2+b_b^2). This row polynomial is stable and an exact O(sk)
  rational-operation DP computes Z_k. Exact Born sampling is available on
  this promised block class, even if physical postselection is rare.
* For general rational A, exact sampling of the unfiltered fixed-k minor-
  square law followed by no-double rejection gives an FPRAS and exact target
  sampler under the supplied promise p_G=Z_k/W_k >= p_0 >= 1/poly(n,L).
  The rejection cost is O(p_0^-1); bit caps and TV error are charged. This
  is a promised-class algorithm, not a general result.
* Uniformly sampling all legal pairs gives an FPRAS under supplied support
  density s_w/|Omega|>=rho_0 and positive-weight ratio max(w)/min(w)<=R_0.
  The exact-moment bound yields O((R_0/rho_0) epsilon^-2 log(1/delta))
  samples; exact determinant evaluation is polynomial in the rational input
  bit length. The report gives the full bit bound. A directed even cycle has
  ratio one but density 2/binom(n,n/2), so this proposal is exponential there.
* The full-sector positive Pfaffian identity is unbiased, but on d independent
  directed C4 blocks its direct estimator has relative variance 2^d-1 and
  needs Omega(2^d) samples for constant-success relative estimation. This is
  only a lower bound for that estimator; the same structured family is easy
  by exact block dynamic programming.
* On directed even cycles, the support is exactly the two alternating
  complementary minor pairs. Any input-independent individual-minor probe
  list that hits every such instance needs at least binom(n,n/2)/2 atoms;
  a black-box atom-probe algorithm needs Omega(binomial(n,n/2)) probes for
  constant success. This does not apply to algorithms that inspect A or query
  polynomial evaluations aggregating many terms.
* For the skew-adjacency matrix of K4 at k=2, Z_2=8, while keeping only
  diagonal permutation-pair squares gives 12: signed determinant cross terms
  aggregate to -4. This refutes the naive positive-term expansion only; it
  is not a no-go theorem for every reverse AP reduction.

RESTART GATE
============
The next mathematical attack is the reverse interface: either construct a
polynomial-bit AP reduction from arbitrary rational-complex Z_k to
nonnegative weighted perfect matching, with explicit error/zero transfer, or
find a direct positive estimator/sampler with a worst-case polynomial
second-moment, conditional-mass, or acceptance bound. A successful route
must preserve supplied k, keep all target weights nonnegative rational, and
charge matrix bit height, conditioning, normalization, and every random-bit
or retry cost. Do not infer hardness from the PM-to-Z direction, the K4
interference example, instability of the natural polynomial, or exponential
variance of one estimator. OA113 and PC-05 remain conditional/source-reported
until independently validated.
