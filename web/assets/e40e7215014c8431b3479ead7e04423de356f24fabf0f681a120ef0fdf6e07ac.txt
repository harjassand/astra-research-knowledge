OUTCOME

Two concrete interfaces are closed at the written-proof level:

1. One common EB reconstruction produces one classical experiment that
simulates every bounded-loss ordinary quantum decision within epsilon,
while preserving exact matrix preparation equivalences. A contextuality
score gap Delta then implies a broadcasting lower bound
 exp[-160000(C+1)/Delta^2]. This is a quantitative consequence of the
SUPPLIED candidate modulus, not a new quantum Blackwell theorem or a
new exact contextuality characterization.

2. For a supplied quantum instrument and all its original reachable
conditional memory states, the same common map yields one fixed classical
HMM with n-output TV error at most n epsilon. The hybrid proof only uses
the promise on original conditional states, so a reset trajectory leaving
the original family does not invalidate the theorem. Composing the
modulus gives n*400*sqrt((C+1)/ln(1/b)), capped at 1. The construction
uses a supplied instrument and EB decomposition; acquisition is unresolved.

The most decisive result is the negative memory gate, which does not
depend on the supplied modulus. A stationary commuting quantum HMM can
have ALL-PRIORS reachable-state capacity exactly ln 4 and b=0 while its
minimum exact classical hidden-memory entropy is
 ln 3+(ln N)/3 -> infinity.
The bound is attained by the basis HMM, and holds against arbitrary
nonunifilar HMMs, not only against causal-state constructions. Its two-step
law is already enough: TV<=1/192 forces K=Omega(N); TV<=1/384 forces
Shannon memory entropy Omega(ln N). Uniform balanced subset preparations
also separately have capacity ln 2, exact broadcasting, and reconstruction
alphabet K=Omega(N) at half trace error 1/16.

Exact lower-bound mechanism: balanced incidence (uniform S of size N/2,
uniform i in S) has mutual information ln 2 but Wyner common information
ln N. Every conditional-product component must live on a rectangle whose
index support T is contained in every possible S. The support entropy
bound forces KL>=ln N for every component. The stationary process adds
a three-phase flag, yielding ln 3+(ln N)/3. Robustness comes from a
balanced-subset code and l_1 simplex packing; entropy robustness comes
from truncating a high-entropy hidden distribution before applying that
packing bound.

CHARGED LIMITS

The stationary example has dimension N+2, an output alphabet of
binom(N,N/2)+N+1, and a periodic three-phase process. The alphabet is
exponential and no fixed-alphabet or mixing result is claimed. The
all-priors capacity is bounded, rather than just the chosen stationary
prior's information. All states commute: this is a counterexample to
capacity-only resource compression, not a quantum memory advantage.
The original physical quantum memory also has stationary entropy
ln 3+(ln N)/3. The classical lower bound does not exploit a quantum
purification or independent copies, and it even survives fixed-error
observational approximation.

For the positive transfer, no reference/diamond-norm conclusion follows.
No small latent alphabet, entropy bound, efficiently sampled instrument,
teacher-free acquisition, infinite-path TV, or structural commutative
algebra approximation is asserted. The finite EB decomposition bound
K<=d^4 depends on the original dimension and is existential. Restricted
subtheory operational equivalences need additional checks: state/effect
equivalence relative to a restricted test family need not be an operator
identity, and decoder states may leave that family.

common-map mechanism removes a real quantifier obstruction for decisions
