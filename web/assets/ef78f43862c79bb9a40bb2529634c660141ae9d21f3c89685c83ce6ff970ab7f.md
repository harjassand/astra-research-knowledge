# Primary mathematical source notes

## Jump-process path likelihood and entropy

Kaiser et al., “Canonical Structure and Orthogonality of Forces and Currents in Irreversible Markov Chains,” *Journal of Statistical Physics*, DOI [10.1007/s10955-018-1986-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-018-1986-0). The browser-exposed Appendix A gives the path density as initial mass times products of jump rates times the integrated escape-rate exponential; its log Radon–Nikodym derivative is a sum over observed jumps and a time integral of rate differences. It also states the path-space relative entropy as the time integral of the state-occupancy-weighted local rate divergences. This is precisely the identity specialized in PROOF.md. Read depth: abstract and the search/open excerpt of Appendix A; no claim of complete-paper review.

## Complete paths versus endpoint-only data; rate estimation

Marius Pfeuffer, “ctmcd: An R Package for Estimating the Parameters of a Continuous-Time Markov Chain from Discrete-Time Data,” *The R Journal* (2017), [article](https://journal.r-project.org/articles/RJ-2017-038/). Sections 1 and 3 distinguish complete paths from observations only at 0 and T; Section 3 gives the complete-path likelihood in terms of transition counts and state sojourns, and (q_{ij,ML}=N_{ij}(T)/R_i(T)). The article also notes the embedding ambiguity of recovering continuous-time generators from endpoint transition matrices. Browser lines used: 13–19 and 56–62. Read depth: those exact sections.
