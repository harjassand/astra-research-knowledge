This falsifies a proposed missing lemma, NOT the main power-law target.

THE FALSIFIED LEMMA

The tracial proof charges E_Phi(sqrt(rho)) by a bounded log gradient through
a genuine scalar doubly stochastic kernel. A tempting nontracial analogue
uses L=sigma^(-1/2)rho sigma^(-1/2), the KMS Dirichlet form

  E_Theta(A,C)=<A,(I-Theta)C>_sigma,
  <A,C>_sigma=Tr[sqrt(sigma) A sqrt(sigma) C],

and hopes for a dimension-free root/log comparison, for instance

  E_Theta(sqrt(L)) <= c E_Theta(L,ln L).                 (F1)

Even with a fixed likelihood bound 1/5 I<=L<=9/5 I and fixed dimension 3,
no finite universal c exists. More generally, E_Theta(sqrt(L)) cannot be
bounded by any modulus of E_Theta(L) that tends to zero at zero. This is
an exact legal sigma-preserving compatible CP counterexample to (F1).

SUPPLIED MATRICES AND LEGAL BROADCASTER

For rational 0<=t<1 put

  H_t = [[1,t,0],[t,1,0],[0,0,1+t]],
  N_t = Tr(H_t^2)=3+2t+3t^2,
  sigma_t=H_t^2/N_t, S_t=H_t/sqrt(N_t),
  L=diag(1/5,9/5,1), rho_t=H_t L H_t/N_t.

H_t is positive definite: its eigenvalues are 1-t,1+t,1+t. Thus sigma_t
and rho_t are faithful density matrices. In detail, Tr(sigma_t L)=1
because the first two diagonal entries of sigma_t coincide and the first
two entries of L sum to 2. Also

  (1/5)sigma_t <=rho_t <=(9/5)sigma_t.

All supplied state and channel matrices are rational if t is rational.
Define the full-domain broadcaster and its identical marginals by

  B_t(X)=Tr(X) sigma_t tensor sigma_t,
  Theta_t^*(X)=Tr(X) sigma_t,
  Theta_t(A)=Tr(sigma_t A) I.

They are CPTP, the marginal fixes sigma_t, and Theta_t is KMS self-adjoint,
KMS positive, unital and self-compatible. It is already an EB replacer.
There is no illegal state-dependent choice of map or restriction of its
input domain. The supplied broadcaster discards its input on the entire
matrix algebra.

EXACT COMPUTATIONS

For this replacer,

  E_t(A,C)=Tr(S_t A S_t C)-Tr(sigma_t A)Tr(sigma_t C).

For diagonal A,C the first term is a weighted sum with weights
|H_t,ij|^2/N_t. Substituting the three diagonal entries gives

  E_t(L)=32(1-t^2)/(25 N_t),                            (F2)
  E_t(L,ln L)=(4/5)(1-t^2)ln9/N_t.                     (F3)

Both tend to zero as t tends to 1 from below. The second expression is
nonnegative, so this is not merely a wrong sign in an entropy production.
For A=sqrt(L)=diag(1/sqrt5,3/sqrt5,1), the exact expression is

  E_t(A)=[2+(6/5)t^2+(1+t)^2]/N_t
          -[(4/sqrt5)(1+t^2)+(1+t)^2]^2/N_t^2.          (F4)

Its strictly positive limit is

  lim_(t->1-) E_t(sqrt(L))=(9-4sqrt5)/20>0.             (F5)

For example t_n=1-2^(-n) is a fully explicit rational faithful sequence.
Equations (F2)--(F5) prove the assertions, without optimization, SDP,
random sampling or a limit member outside the legal faithful class.
The limit is used only to establish a lower bound along legal members.

WHAT FAILS AND WHAT DOES NOT

The KMS bilinear form on spectral functions of L need not correspond to
a Markov kernel on the eigenvalues of L. In this example, the limiting
support of sigma_t sees L as a scalar after compression, so its KMS energy
vanishes; it does not see sqrt(L) as the same scalar. Functional calculus
does not commute with this compression. Thus spectral clipping of L cannot
be transported through the tracial proof's scalar Dirichlet argument.

This does not refute a power bound for the actual states. Directly,

  rho_t-sigma_t=(4/5)(t^2-1)diag(1,-1,0)/N_t,
  T(rho_t,sigma_t)=(4/5)(1-t^2)/N_t.                   (F6)

The supplied EB replacer therefore reconstructs rho_t with an error that
tends to zero. In particular the two-state family's optimal e and b are
at most (F6), since this same EB replacer can also prepare two copies.
The optimized-radius target is intact. This also does not falsify an
entropy-to-ACTUAL-EB-TRACE-error comparison: the map here is itself EB and
the actual trace error (F6) vanishes. Only an intermediate KMS spectral
root-energy bound is disproved.

The residual effect of the failure is precise. A power route based on
ordinary spectral functions of L needs a new replacement for the root/log
comparison. Possibilities such as a natural-cone truncation or a minimax
reconstruction argument must control the actual channel error without
asserting (F1) or a root-energy modulus contradicted by this example.

COSTS AND STATUS

No acquired broadcaster, state tomography, postselection or oracle is free.
This is an analytic counterexample to a component lemma, not an effective
