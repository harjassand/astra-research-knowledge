SPIN-COVARIANT BOTH-SMALL-CMI FAMILY -- A DECISIVE FAILED COUNTERMECHANISM
2026-10-09. foundational_transfer_sol. Independent of Cycle07 sibling proofs.

This construction is different from the balanced exterior-power family:
A is one qubit, B and C are large irreducible SU(2) spins. It has BOTH
conditional mutual informations tending to zero and a nonclassical
record marginal. Its entanglement gap nevertheless vanishes at a known
rate, and tensor amplification cannot restore a persistent gap while
preserving the two-small-CMI contract. No counterexample to the general
gate is claimed.

EXACT CONSTRUCTION AND NORMALIZATION

Let j>=1/2 be a half-integer. B,C carry spin j, each dimension 2j+1.
Inside B tensor C select the unique maximal-spin J=2j irreducible
subspace; let Pi_J be its projector and S=J_B+J_C restricted there.
A carries spin1/2 with spin operator s. In A tensor Pi_J, the two
total spins are L_-=J-1/2 and L_+=J+1/2. Their projectors P_-,P_+
have ranks 2J and 2J+2. Define
 rho_ABC=P_-/(2J), rho_+=P_+/(2J+2).
On A tensor Pi_J,
 P_-=(J I-2 s dot S)/(2J+1).                         (1)
The eigenvalues of s dot S are -(J+1)/2 and J/2, so (1) is an actual
orthogonal projector. Outside Pi_J it is zero. Thus rho is positive
and normalized; no formal dimensional-limit density is being used.

Rotational invariance and irreducibility give
 rho_A=I_2/2, rho_B=rho_C=I_(2j+1)/(2j+1),
 rho_BC=Pi_J/(2J+1), S(ABC)=ln(2J).
B,C exchange symmetry gives delta_B=delta_C.

THE TWO CMIs -- EXACT AND ASYMPTOTIC

The invariant AB marginal is a mixture of total spins j-1/2 and j+1/2:
 rho_AB=w_- Pi_(j-1/2)/(2j)
            +w_+ Pi_(j+1/2)/(2j+2),
 w_+=1/(4j+2), w_-=1-w_+.
Indeed the total rho has
 <s dot(J_B+J_C)>=-(2j+1)/2.
Symmetry makes <s dot J_B>=-(2j+1)/4. Its two AB eigenvalues
j/2 and -(j+1)/2 determine exactly the weights above. This determines
the whole marginal because each irrep has multiplicity one.

Let h(q)=-q ln q-(1-q)ln(1-q), q=1/(4j+2). Then
 H_AB=h(q)+(1-q)ln(2j)+q ln(2j+2),
 delta_B=delta_C
  =H_AB+ln(4j+1)-ln(2j+1)-ln(4j).                   (2)
Equivalently
 delta_j=h(q)+q ln(1+1/j)+ln[(4j+1)/(4j+2)].
The cancellation of constant first-order terms gives
 delta_j=(ln(4j))/(4j)+O((ln j)/j^2)->0.             (3)
The exact formula (2), not the asymptotic, defines every finite state.
I(A:B)=ln2+ln(2j+1)-H_AB tends to ln2 and is bounded
by 2ln2 from the qubit dimension. Thus it also falls in the older
bounded-I theorem; there is no hidden high-I counterexample here.

EXACT SEP(A:BC) DISTANCE AND EXACT MARGINALS

For any unit qubit a, rotate it to spin up. Contraction of (1) gives
 <a|P_-|a>=(J I-S_z)/(2J+1) on Pi_J, zero elsewhere.
Its largest eigenvalue is p=2J/(2J+1), attained at m=-J.
Thus every state separable across A:(BC) has P_- expectation <=p,
while rho has expectation1. The half-trace witness lower bound is
 dist_Sep(A:BC)(rho)>=1/(2J+1).                     (4)

Twirl the PRODUCT state |up><up|_A tensor |J,-J><J,-J|_BC by the
same SU(2) rotation on A,B,C. Twirling is an average of local unitaries
for the A:(BC) cut, so the output sigma is separable. Multiplicity one
and the preceding overlap identify it exactly as
 sigma=p rho+(1-p)rho_+.
The two supports are orthogonal, hence
 T(rho,sigma)=1-p=1/(4j+1).                          (5)
The twirled BC marginal is Pi_J/(2J+1), and the A marginal is I_2/2.
Both ORIGINAL marginals are exact. Combining (4)-(5),
 dist_Sep(A:BC)(rho)=dist_Sep_with_exact_A_BC(rho)=1/(4j+1).
Here |J,-J>_BC is the product |j,-j>_B |j,-j>_C, so sigma even has
an explicit convex-integral fully product representation.

TENSOR AMPLIFICATION FAILURE -- NO MULTIPLICATIVITY ASSUMPTION

For N copies, both CMIs add exactly, giving N delta_j each. The explicit
separable approximation sigma^(tensor N) gives
 T(rho^(tensor N),sigma^(tensor N))=1-p^N<=N/(4j+1).  (6)
The equality follows by its expansion into mutually orthogonal +/- irrep
sectors; the target sits entirely in the all-minus sector. No claim that
the maximal product overlap of P_-^(tensor N) is multiplicative is used.

If N delta_j->0 along j->infinity, (3) implies
 N/(4j+1)=O(N delta_j/ln j)->0.
Then (6) forces the separable gap to vanish. Choosing enough copies to
keep the upper bound of constant order instead spends order ln j in
each conditional mutual information. Increasing the number of copies
does not turn this family into the requested counterexample.

DECISIVE CONCLUSION

The nonclassicality of the BC marginal by itself does not force a gap:
rho_BC is a symmetric coherent-spin mixture, not diagonal in a product
basis, yet the total state admits a simple exact-marginal separable
approximation. In this family the increasing record size makes quantum
correlation classically reproducible, and the small entropy losses track
that decrease. It is a real both-small-CMI construction and a precise
failed amplification route, not a catalogue analogy or a successful
solution of the arbitrary-information target.
