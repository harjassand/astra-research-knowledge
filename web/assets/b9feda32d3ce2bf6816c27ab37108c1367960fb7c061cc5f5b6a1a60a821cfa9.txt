2. CONDITIONAL-DPP FIBER KERNEL

Given I, form A_I=F[I,I^c]. By Cauchy–Binet,
  Z_I=sum_{J subset I^c, |J|=k}|det A_I[:,J]|^2=det(A_I A_I^*).
When Z_I>0,
  P_I=A_I^*(A_I A_I^*)^{-1}A_I
is a rank-k Hermitian projection, and its projection-DPP law satisfies
  det(P_I[J,J])=|det A_I[:,J]|^2/Z_I.
An exact rational sequential sampler chooses coordinate j with probability
P[j,j]/r for current rank r, then replaces P by the Schur complement
P[-j,-j]-P[-j,j]P[j,-j]/P[j,j]. The chosen-set probability is det(P[J,J]):
each of the r possible first choices contributes det(P[J,J])/r. Thus J|I
is sampled without enumerating its subsets or computing c_k.

Define B=binom(n,k). From current x=(I,J), propose I' uniformly among all
k-subsets, sample J' from this exact conditional law if Z_I'>0, and accept
(I',J') with probability min(1,Z_I'/Z_I). A zero fiber is an immediate
rejection. The off-diagonal stationary flow is exactly
  pi(x)K(x,y)=w(x)w(y)/[c_k B max(Z_I,Z_I')]
             >= pi(x)pi(y)/B.
It follows that K is reversible and E_K(g)>=Var_pi(g)/B for every real g.
For independent synchronous updates K tensor K, product independence gives
  E_(K tensor K)(g(x)+h(y))=E_K(g)+E_K(h),
so Var(g(x)+h(y)) <= B E_(K tensor K)(g(x)+h(y)). Attaching and updating
the mismatch register is an injective relabeling and leaves this comparison
unchanged. If a full Poincare bound for arbitrary joint functions is needed,
lazify each K; standard tensorization then gives coefficient 2B for the
full product chain. The additive class has the sharper B bound above.
