# CYCLE2 primary source ledger

## Algorithmic signing primitive

Xiaoyu Li, “Fast Spectral Signing for Vector Balancing,” arXiv:2609.30044v1, Sept. 24, 2026. [Pinned primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30044v1), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30044v1). Theorem 1.1 states deterministic signs with \(\|V\epsilon\|_\infty<99\) for rational columns of Euclidean norm at most one; it reports \(O(c^2\log c)\) updates, \(O(dc+c^{\omega+2}\log^3c)\) real arithmetic/comparison operations for fixed attainable \(\omega>2\), and polynomial bit complexity for rational input. We use this only on the cycle columns \(w_C/U_t\). The source does not bound \(\|A\delta_C\|_2\).

## Bipartite dependent-rounding prior art

Rajiv Gandhi, Samir Khuller, Srinivasan Parthasarathy, Aravind Srinivasan, “Dependent Rounding and its Applications to Approximation Algorithms,” JACM 53(3), 2006. [Primary author PDF](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~srin/PDF/2006/depround-jou.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/1147954.1147956). Local source PDF `sources/gandhi-dependent-rounding.pdf`, SHA-256 `7541da1051f847b72d0b777934633f536f2ac6f2af652fa02db772492f236582`; pypdf extraction `sources/gandhi-dependent-rounding.txt`, SHA-256 `649317d2d846578f62d53bac39ba03b0ef5b1544a0182e3cef9991cae088d46f`.

Relevant primary ranges: Section 2.1, printed pp. 2–4; it finds a simple cycle or maximal path in floating bipartite edges, chooses one of two feasible endpoint moves with probabilities making the step mean zero, and fixes at least one floating edge. Lemma 2.1 proves exact edge marginals, Lemma 2.2 handles negative correlation, and Theorem 2.3 gives (O((|A|+|B|)|E|)) time and degree preservation up to floor/ceiling. Our initial residual b-matching starts with integer residual degree at every vertex, so the fractional support cannot have degree-one vertices and only the cycle case is needed; this exact specialization is proved in `CYCLE2_REPORT.md`. We do not infer a hard-feature bound from the cited dependent-rounding theorem.
