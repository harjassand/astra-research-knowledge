1. POSITIVE PART DOES NOT FOLLOW FROM WEAKER DOMINATION

Even for positive contractions Y, knowing Y>=A and Y>=0 does not imply
Y>=A_+ when Y and A do not commute. The exact real2x2 example is
  A=diag(1/2,-1/2),
  Y=[[3/5,1/4],[1/4,1/5]].
The principal diagonal entries of Y and I-Y are positive, with
  det Y=23/400, det(I-Y)=103/400.
Also Y-A has positive diagonal and determinant3/400, so Y>=A.
But det(Y-A_+)=-17/400<0. Thus Y does not dominate A_+.
This is an algebraic logical-transfer counterexample, not a legal-channel
counterexample. The universal proof obtains the full fast-space target
from the ENTIRE input Gram matrix and its inclusion J. It never derives
positive-part order from a bare weaker lower bound or a free pinching.

sum_j p_j pi_j/tau(pi_j)=I, as required. Using naive uniform p_j=1/3

canonical map is not unital. The ordinary matrix barycenter of the valid
weighted pure ensemble is diag(3/10,3/10,2/5), not global I3/3.
The correct reference is the functional tau, not a uniform matrix trace.
