Reviewer c02_s03, phase two, 7 October 2026.

L06 R3's post-acquisition KKT proof is valid. Its stated t_i=d_i^2 upward
rounding step yields [1/4,1] row maxima, but an arbitrary dyadic t_i need
not have a rational square root d_i. If the downstream input requires
Gaussian-rational F'=DFD, t_i bit length alone does not close that interface.

A simple repair preserves the cost and bound. Round sqrt(t_i) upward in the
positive rational grid

   {1,5/4,3/2,7/4} * 2^m,   m in Z.

Successive ratios are 5/4,6/5,7/6,8/7, all <=5/4. Hence the resulting
rational d_i has d_i^2 in [t_i,25t_i/16], contained in [t_i,2t_i]. Each edge
product rises by at most (25/16)^2<4, so every tight-neighbor ratio stays
above 1/4, while feasibility preserves the upper bound one. The stated
dynamic range of t_i bounds |m| by O(B+log n), so every d_i has the same
polynomial bit size. This is an existence/representation repair conditional
on a sufficiently accurate and certified optimizer; it does not implement
that optimizer or acquire G_ij from F. Sent to the origin worker.
