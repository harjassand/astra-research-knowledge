Date: 2026-10-08

Scientific outcome
------------------

The dimension-free bounded-Holevo implication remains UNKNOWN. For finite-
dimensional state families E_n, uniformly bounded Holevo capacity and
vanishing worst-case pair-broadcast error have not been shown here to imply
vanishing worst-case error for one full-domain entanglement-breaking (EB)
channel, and no sequence satisfying all three target conditions was found.
Cycle 4 produced an exact finite refutation of the stronger proposed
constant-two EB Dirichlet/reconstruction estimate, plus a logarithmic
common-design theorem and several exact obstructions to tempting proof
shortcuts. None is a counterexample to qualitative broadcasting-to-EB.

The legal target interface is one CPTP broadcaster B_n:B(H_n)->B(H_n)⊗
B(H_n), fixed for all rho in E_n, with both marginals close in half trace
distance. The EB decoder is also one channel on the entire input algebra,
independent of the unknown state, and may use any POVM and preparation map.
There is no covariance, locality, rank, reference, runtime, or independent-
output promise. Capacity is C(E)=sup_pi chi(pi,E), including all finite
priors; no rank or trace-cover bound is inferred from C(E).

1. Exact d=2 factor-two obstruction (fixed finite family)
----------------------------------------------------------

For rho_0=|0><0| and rho_1=|v><v| with v=(10,1)/sqrt(101), the exact
rational projectors are

  rho_0 = [[1,0],[0,0]],
  rho_1 = (1/101) [[100,10],[10,1]].

After one common unitary they are rho_±=(I±SX+CZ)/2 with S=1/sqrt(101)
and C=10/sqrt(101). Twirling any EB channel over the pair swap and complex
conjugation preserves EB and cannot increase its worst error. Its resulting
Bloch map has parameters (a,b,c,u), and PPT of its qubit Choi state yields

  a^2+u^2 <= (1-|c|)^2.

The output error for either state obeys

  2 T >= (3S+C-sqrt(9S^2+1))/sqrt(10),

by the Euclidean supporting-vector inequality and Cauchy-Schwarz. Minimizing
over c gives

  e_EB >= (13-sqrt(110))/(2 sqrt(1010)) > 39/1000.

For an actual symmetric CPTP broadcaster, use the isometry

  |0> -> |00>,   |1> -> (|01>+|10>)/sqrt(2).

Each marginal has tangent Bloch shrinkage 1/sqrt(2) and axial map
z -> (z+1)/2. Direct reduction of the isometry gives

  b_2 <= sqrt((1-1/sqrt(2))^2/(4*101)
              +(1-10/sqrt(101))^2/16) < 0.0155.

Therefore e_EB>2 b_2 for this exact two-state family. This REFUTES the
universal finite-family estimate e_EB<=2b_2, not the qualitative implication:
the states coalesce in the small-angle limit, and both the explicit
broadcaster and bisector-basis EB errors tend to zero. The claim and proof
are independent of the floating-point SDP. The code's d=2 PPT formulation
is exact because PPT is equivalent to separability in 2x2; its solver output
is not used as the certificate.

As exact controls, complete projective MUB designs in d=2 and d=3 satisfy

  (b_2,e_EB)=(1/6,1/3) and (1/4,1/2), respectively.

The proof uses the projective 2-design identity, a symmetric two-output
Choi overlap bound for b_2, a separable Choi overlap bound for e_EB, and the
universal cloner/Haar EB channel attaining both bounds. The random Choi SDP
screens are exploratory only: their floating solver outputs lack rational
primal/dual certificates, two random qubit solves returned
`optimal_inaccurate`, and qutrit PPT is only a lower relaxation.

2. Actual-broadcaster common-design bound with logarithmic score rank
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Let Phi be a bistochastic, selfadjoint L2 channel with spectrum in [-1,1]
and let B be a supplied physical CPTP two-output extension whose marginal
is Phi. If S is a supplied r-dimensional subspace of centered Hermitian
scores, r>=2, one common canonical pure-state 1-design EB channel Psi_S
exists with

  P_S (I-Psi_S) P_S <= 2 ceil(log_2 r) P_S (I-Phi) P_S.

Proof mechanism: recurse the actual physical broadcaster down a balanced
binary tree to k=2^ceil(log2 r) correlated outputs, measure each output in
one spectral group, pull the joint measurement back to an input POVM and
rank-one refine it to a pure 1-design. The exact meter identity gives the
one-design score variance at most twice the Dirichlet energy for Phi^m;
the scalar inequality 1-lambda^m<=m(1-lambda), valid on [-1,1], gives the
factor 2m. Compact-convex separation selects one design for all PSD score
covariances supported on S. No output independence is assumed.

For S=H_0, this yields a channel-dependent O(log d) full-algebra comparison.
The tree uses k-1 broadcaster calls and k<=2r outputs; the explicit product
measurement can have d^k outcomes. The separation argument is existential,
not an efficient decoder compiler. It does not replace log r by a dimension-
free constant and does not establish a score subspace of small rank from a
Holevo bound. This theorem is separate from the N93 common-POVM gate.

For a finite family E={rho_x}, prior pi, and one broadcaster, sequential
Helstrom measurements on K correlated receivers give the complementary
prior-dependent estimate

  inf_EB sum_x pi_x T(Lambda(rho_x),rho_x)
    <= phi(K) b_2 + sqrt(N_(1/2)(pi) chi(pi,E)/(2K)),

  phi(K)=s+2-2^(s+1)/K, 2^s<=K<=2^(s+1),
  N_(1/2)(pi)=(sum_x sqrt(pi_x))^2.

Finite-family minimax turns this into ONE worst-case EB decoder with
Gamma(E)=max_pi N_(1/2)(pi) chi(pi,E) in the second term. This is dimension-
free when Gamma is controlled, but C(E) alone does not control N_(1/2) or
Gamma. Bounded-capacity commuting balanced-sign families have exponential
trace covers yet are fixed exactly by one dephasing EB channel; hence capacity
alone cannot substitute for N46's cover hypothesis. No rank-to-capacity
inference was used.

3. Exact compact-group multiplicity test
----------------------------------------

On the d=8 SU(3) adjoint carrier, the legal symmetric extension

  Gamma_P(X)=(4/r) P[(X tensor I)+(I tensor X)]P

with P=P_27 and r=27 gives a bistochastic compatible marginal. Its transfer
block on the two adjoint copies 8_s and 8_a is exactly diag(14/25,2/3),
with trace 92/75. Every normalized covariant orbit measure-and-prepare
channel, including convex mixtures of such orbits, has adjoint transfer
block trace at most 7/8 by a pure-seed Gram/Cauchy-Schwarz bound. Thus unit-
coefficient Loewner domination by this restricted orbit family is impossible;
a scalar factor would need at least 736/525 by trace. A complex seed does
give a finite factor 14/5 on this block, so the result does not refute a
finite scaled, squared, or arbitrary-EB comparison. Exact SymPy checks verify
projectors, ranks, partial traces, transfer blocks, and seed moments.

This is a scoped nontrivial multiplicity witness, not a counterexample to the
bounded-Holevo target. The complete SU(3)-covariant extension cone is given
as an exact spectrahedral image, but its explicit Racah recoupling matrices
have not been expanded. No arbitrary compact-group conclusion follows.

4. Proof routes that failed, with exact boundaries
--------------------------------------------------

* Environment or purification decoupling: balanced diagonal sign states
  have C(E)=c(a), exact full-domain broadcaster, and exact EB decoder. Yet
  the canonical purified receiver-reference trace distance is at least
  1-(1+a^2)/d -> 1, and I(A_1:A_2|X)=ln d-c(a) diverges. Thus the unreferenced
  state-family premise does not imply channel/diamond closeness, purification
  preservation, or small CMI. This REFUTES that intermediate inference only.
* Accessible-information or pairwise-commutator shortcut: the direct
  sequential-Helstrom derivation exhibits the effective-support factor;
  small Hilbert-Schmidt commutators from actual compatibility do not alone
  give one common POVM. The Clifford/Pauli controls have a positive
  broadcasting lower bound, so they are not qualifying counterexamples.
* One fixed covariant/Haar comparator: noncovariant dephasing has an exact
  broadcaster and exact channel-dependent EB design, while the fixed Haar
  comparator fails on its fixed diagonal algebra. The mixture of dephasing
  with the universal cloner is non-EB and still has a channel-dependent
  factor-two design. Therefore this refutes using a universal fixed orbit,
  not channel-dependent EB selection.
* Bounded-Holevo counterexamples: the candidate product of weak incompatible
  qubit signals has C(E_q)<=q c(t_q/sqrt(q)); an explicit tensor cloner gives
  b_2=O(t_q). If t_q->0 the full-algebra replacer also has EB error O(t_q);
  for fixed t the displayed broadcaster bound does not vanish. The copy
  ladders fail bounded capacity or already admit a good global decoder. No
  qualifying sequence was certified.
* Approximate Markov recovery: a recovery theorem starts with a specified
  tripartite state's small CMI and does not itself produce one input-
  independent EB map. The exact environment example above blocks deriving
  those premises from family-relative marginal error.

5. Prior-art boundary
---------------------

The comparison inspected exact no-broadcasting (Barnum, Caves, Fuchs,
Jozsa, Schumacher, 1996), state de Finetti (Christandl, Konig, Mitchison,
Renner, 2007), and measured/channel de Finetti for constrained SDP
hierarchies (Berta, Borderi, Fawzi, Scholz, 2022). Replacing label entropy
by Holevo capacity in the measured-conditioning chain-rule step is valid;
the information-complete trace lift still costs d^(3/2) in the displayed
bound, and their channel corollary has a different multi-input/no-signalling
interface and dimension factors. The source results do not establish the
dimension-free target. Glebsky's fixed-k normalized-HS almost-commuting
result and Bansil-Kachkovskiy's two-observable estimate do not give a
growing-family common measurement. Exact 2x2 PPT/separability and Werner's
universal cloner provide ingredients for the finite d=2/3 controls, not a
general asymptotic theorem. Source URLs and exact scopes are in the individual
notes listed below. No priority or field-breaking claim is made.
