REVISION R1 — A CONNECTED-SUPPORT, BIT-PRECISION ENERGY OBSTRUCTION
Worker c01_l07. 7 October 2026. Exact internal result; no peer exposure.

RESULT

The one-pair Metropolis chain has an exponentially bad certified energy
constant on a fixed six-coordinate family, even after the directed-cycle
support obstruction is removed. This applies to the precisely defined
fixed-radius pair-exchange kernels below, not to arbitrary kernels.

Let n=6, indices be cyclic modulo 6, and let

  F_delta[i,i+1]=1,  F_delta[i,i-1]=delta,  all other entries 0,
  delta=2^(-b), b>=1.

For I of size 3, write w(I)=|det F_delta[I,I^c]|^2 and Z=sum_I w(I). The
microscopic law is pi(I)=w(I)/Z. The 20 exact complementary minors fall into
these groups:

  determinant                         states
  1+delta^3                           024, 135
  delta                               013, 025, 034, 124, 145, 235
  delta^2                             014, 023, 035, 125, 134, 245
  0                                   012, 015, 045, 123, 234, 345

Here, for example, 024 means {0,2,4}. Thus 14 states have positive weight;
the support graph under one selected/unselected exchange is connected for
every delta>0. Its exact partition function is

  Z=2(1+delta^3)^2+6delta^2+6delta^4.

Define s(I)=+1 when I contains at least two even indices, and -1 otherwise.
Every state has one of these signs. Cyclic shift by one preserves F_delta,
interchanges the signs, and preserves weights. Hence E_pi[s]=0 and
Var_pi(s)=1.

KERNELS AND EXACT ENERGY

For t=1 or t=2, the one-replica kernel Q_t chooses uniformly among the
binom(3,t)^2 states obtained by removing t selected labels and adding t
unselected labels; it then uses Metropolis acceptance min(1,w(J)/w(I)). A
zero-weight proposal is rejected. Each kernel is reversible with respect to
pi and each proposal normalizer is exactly 9.

The two-replica kernel is

  P_t=(Q_t tensor Id + Id tensor Q_t + Swap)/3,

where Swap sends (A,B) to (B,A). This is reversible under pi tensor pi. For
F(A,B)=s(A)+s(B), the layer swap contributes zero energy. Direct exact
enumeration of the one-swap and two-swap cut edges gives:

  t     crossing edges     sum_cross min(w(I),w(J))
  1          18             3delta^2 + 15delta^4
  2          24             12delta^2 + 12delta^4

Since s changes by 2 across each crossing edge, uniform proposal probability
is 1/9, and pi=w/Z, the single-replica Rayleigh quotient is

  E_Qt(s)/Var_pi(s)=4 S_t/(9Z),

where S_t is the corresponding crossing sum. The replica swap is invariant on
F, while the two independent coordinate updates each contribute one third of
the single-replica Dirichlet form. Therefore

  E_Pt(F)=8 S_t/(27Z),   Var_pi(s)=1.

Any constant L in Var_pi(f1)<=L E_Pt(f1(A)+f2(B)) must consequently obey

  L >= 27Z/[8(3delta^2+15delta^4)]       for t=1,
  L >= 27Z/[8(12delta^2+12delta^4)]      for t=2.

As delta tends to zero, these lower bounds are respectively
  (9/4+o(1))delta^(-2) and (9/16+o(1))delta^(-2).
For every delta<=1/2, the simpler certified bounds are
  L>=delta^(-2)=4^b for t=1, and
  L>=(9/20)delta^(-2) for t=2.
Thus even in fixed dimension six, these kernels have an energy constant
exponential in the input precision b. The t=1 support graph remains connected;
the obstruction is a low-weight cut, not reducibility. This also upper-bounds
the one-replica spectral gap by 4S_t/(9Z).

PROOF DETAILS

The determinant table above is obtained by expanding each 3-by-3 minor. For
the only two terms with two permutation contributions, the signs agree and
give 1+delta^3. All other nonzero minors have one term, delta or delta^2.
The listed zero minors have no perfect matching in their cycle-neighbor
support. The checker independently computes every minor with exact rational
Gaussian elimination.

For a crossing edge {I,J}, detailed balance gives its conductance
  pi(I) Q_t(I,J)=min(w(I),w(J))/(9Z).
The unordered-edge Dirichlet sum is this conductance times (s(I)-s(J))^2=4.
This proves the displayed single-replica formula. For the pair kernel, the
layer swap leaves F fixed. The two Q_t updates affect separate summands, each
with mixture weight 1/3; this gives E_Pt(F)=2E_Qt(s)/3. The lower bounds then
follow from the required inequality applied to f1=f2=s.

BOUNDARY AND NEXT GATE

This is a lower bound for uniform exactly-t pair exchange (t=1,2) with
Metropolis acceptance, plus a whole-layer replica swap. It does not rule out
state-dependent long exchanges, auxiliary mismatch registers, or any general
reversible kernel. A uniform proposal over all 20 size-three subsets can
cross the low-weight cut in this fixed instance; the earlier directed-cycle
benchmark shows why extending that proposal to n dimensions charges
binom(n,n/2) candidates and gives an exponential-in-n energy constant.

The next decisive gate is a proposal that avoids both penalties: its transition
normalizer must be computable from polynomial-size rational data, it must
permit macroscopic reorientation when needed, and its additive two-replica
energy must remain polynomial in both n and input bit length. Any candidate
should be checked on this F_delta family and the prior n=8 common-core sewing
counter before being generalized. This result does not modify INITIAL.txt.
