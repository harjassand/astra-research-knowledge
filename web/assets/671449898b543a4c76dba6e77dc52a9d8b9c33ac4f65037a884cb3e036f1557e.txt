THEOREM CANDIDATE

For each FIXED finite M,B>=0 there are constants c,C>0 and N0<infinity such that
for all N>=N0, real symmetric ||A||<=M and |b|<=B, the WHOLE critical density
 rho_N(A,b) proportional to e^[2J^2/N+Q_A/N^1.5+b.J/N^.75]
has an explicitly defined positive mixture sigma_N of IDENTICAL one-qubit
products satisfying
 T(rho_N,sigma_N)<=C e^-cN.                              (S1)
The mixing law uses the KNOWN A,b and a stopped diffusion; it is not the
parameter-independent decoder of INITIAL. The baseline O(N^-3/2) upper is
therefore non-sharp if this proof passes independent review.
No positive exponential LOWER bound is asserted: scalar A has distance zero.
The fixed universal decoder still has the sharp N^-1/2 error proved in INITIAL.

1. AN EXACT DIFFERENTIAL IDENTITY FOR THE GIBBS SANDWICH GENERATOR

Work in ordinary Bloch coordinates m, |m|<1, not the scaled variable of INITIAL.
Set k_N(m)=((I+m.sigma)/2)^tensor N. For a unit vector n define
 u_n=n.m, v_n=n-u_n m,
 B_n=N u_n+v_n.grad_m, R_n=(n cross m).grad_m.
Direct normalized local filtering and local rotation give EXACT identities
 {n.J,k_N}=B_n k_N, [n.J,k_N]=i R_n k_N.
The differential operators commute with constant matrix left/right action.
Thus
 { (n.J)^2,k_N}/2=(B_n^2-R_n^2)k_N/4.                   (S2)
There is no asymptotic or commuting-anisotropy assumption.
The minus sign reflects backward angular diffusion and is essential.

Choose Lambda=M+1 and C=A+Lambda I. Then
 I<=C<=(2M+1)I. Let Lc=2M+1 and Tc=Tr C<=3Lc.
Set s=N^3/4, t0=2/N-Lambda/s^2>0, and
 H=Q_C/s^2+b.J/s.
Then K=t0J^2+H. Diagonalize C=sum lambda_i n_i n_i^T.
The quantum sandwich generator M_H(X)={H,X}/2, on product kernels, equals
 G k_N, where
 G=sum_i lambda_i/(4s^2)(B_ni^2-R_ni^2)+B_b/(2s).        (S3)
Here B_b=N(b.m)+(b-(b.m)m).grad, by linearity.

Expanding (S3) writes it as
 Gf=V_N(m)f+a_N(m).grad f
                    +D_C(m):Hess f/(4s^2),             (S4)
where
 D_C(m)=sum_i lambda_i[v_i v_i^T-r_i r_i^T],
 r_i=n_i cross m,
 a_N=sum_i lambda_i/(4s^2)
       [2N u_i v_i+(v_i.grad)v_i-(r_i.grad)r_i]
       +(b-(b.m)m)/(2s),
 V_N= [N^2 m^TCm+N(Tc-m^TCm)]/(4s^2)
                      +N b.m/(2s).
V_N=Tr H k_N exactly. All coefficients are explicit polynomials except the
positive square root used for diffusion. The resulting representation is
basis independent; eigenaxes merely display the derivation.

2. POSITIVE DIFFUSION ON A FIXED INNER BLOCH BALL

With cross-product matrix [m]_cross,
 D_C=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T.
The sign of [m]_cross is irrelevant to its outer product.
For every real vector z,
 z^TD_C z >=[(1-|m|^2)^2-Lc|m|^2]|z|^2.                (S5)
Indeed C>=I bounds the first term below and C<=Lc I bounds the second above.

Choose r0^2=1/[16(Lc+1)], so r0<=1/sqrt32<1.
For |m|<=r0, (S5) is >3|z|^2/4, uniformly in the unknown admitted A.
Also D_C<=sum lambda_i v_i v_i^T<=Tc I because
 |v_i|^2=1-(2-|m|^2)u_i^2<=1.
Thus D_C is uniformly positive and smoothly square-rootable on this ball.

Let m_t solve until its first hit tau of |m|=r0:
 dm_t=a_N(m_t)dt+sqrt(D_C(m_t)/(2s^2)) dW_t,
with ordinary three-dimensional Brownian motion.
All coefficients are smooth and Lipschitz on a neighborhood of the closed
ball; standard finite-dimensional SDE existence/uniqueness until tau applies.
No PSD test with an unknown spectral gap is used: (S5) is an explicit margin.
After tau freeze both m and its positive Feynman--Kac weight
 w_t=exp[int_0^(t wedge tau) V_N(m_u)du].
Every path output w_t k_N(m_(t wedge tau)) is a positive identical-product
operator. Its expectation W_t is therefore in that cone.

Global operator bound on H:
 hN= Lc (N/2)(N/2+1)/s^2+B N/(2s)
   = (Lc/4)sqrt(N)+Lc/(2sqrt(N))+(B/2)N^1/4.             (S6)
Hence |V_N|<=hN, w_t<=e^hN and
 ||e^(u M_H)||_(Hermitian trace -> trace)<=e^(u hN).
These costs are kept explicit rather than treated as free normalization.

3. EXPONENTIALLY SMALL INITIAL OUTER MASS

The exact positive heat seed rho0=e^(t0J^2)/Tr e^(t0J^2) has the Bloch mixture
obtained from the radial field density
 q_t(r) proportional to
 r^2 sinch(r/2) exp[-r^2/(4t0)+N logcosh(r/2)],

Let a0=2atanh(r0/2)>0. The event |m|>r0/2 is precisely r>a0.

Put f(u)=u^2/2-logcosh u, delta=1/2-logcosh1>0.
Because t0<=2/N, the radial unnormalized density is bounded above by
 r^2 sinch(r/2) e^-N f(r/2).
For 0<=u<=1, f(u)>=u^4/48; for u>=1, f(u)>=delta u^2.
On a0<=r<=2 the integral is <=C exp[-N a0^4/768].
For r>=2, sinch(r/2)<=e^(r/2), and for Ndelta>=2,
 -Ndelta r^2/4+r/2<=-Ndelta r^2/8.
Gaussian tail integration gives <=C_delta exp[-delta N/4].

For N>=Lambda^2, changing r=N^-1/4 R and integrating R in [1,2] gives the
explicit lower normalizer
 int q_t_unnormalized(r)dr
 >=N^-3/4 Lbase,
 Lbase=(7/3)exp[-Lambda/2-1/12].
This is the same elementary heat-seed lower bound as the baseline, with
M replaced by Lambda. It uses f(u)<=u^4/12 and d0>=1/2.
Consequently
 pbad=P_rho0(|m|>r0/2)

Replace each bad initial branch by m=0 and retain each good branch. Let

This is a known-parameter mathematical mixing construction; no exact-real
sampler is asserted as an executed algorithm.

4. STOPPING PROBABILITY, INCLUDING DRIFT AND WEIGHTS

Inside the stopped ball,
 |(v_i.grad)v_i|<=2r0, |(r_i.grad)r_i|<=r0, |u_i v_i|<=r0.
Also ||I-mm^T||<=1. Therefore
 sup_|m|<=r0 |a_N(m)|
 <= betaN:= Tc r0/(2sqrt(N))+3Tc r0/(4s^2)+B/(2s).       (S8)
Choose N0 large enough that betaN<=r0/8, as well as the previous heat/tail
thresholds. All choices depend only on fixed M,B (Tc may be replaced by 3Lc).

If a path from |m_0|<=r0/2 hits radius r0 by time one, its martingale part
has supremum magnitude >=3r0/8, because total drift movement is <=r0/8.
Each coordinate martingale has quadratic variation at most Tc/(2s^2).
The exponential-martingale maximal inequality plus a union bound over signs
and three coordinates therefore gives, conservatively,
 P(tau<=1)<=6 exp[-3r0^2 s^2/(256 Tc)]
          <=6 exp[-kappa1 N^1.5],
 kappa1=r0^2/(256 Lc)>0.                               (S9)
The constant used is deliberately weaker than the direct variance calculation.
This bound is uniform over every retained starting branch and admitted A,b.
The positive trace weight cannot eliminate the saving:
 E[w_tau 1_(tau<=1)]<=e^hN P(tau<=1).

5. EXACT DUHAMEL COMPARISON, WHOLE DENSITY AND NORMALIZATION

Ito plus (S2)-(S4) gives
 W_t'=E[1_(t<tau) w_t M_H k_N(m_t)].
Meanwhile linearity gives M_H W_t as the expectation over stopped and
unstopped branches. Hence
 W_t'-M_H W_t
   =-E[1_(tau<=t)w_tau M_H k_N(m_tau)].
Duhamel now gives an EXACT identity against the finite-dimensional quantum
semigroup, not a claim that the nonpositive global differential operator
generates a positive process:
 e^(M_H)rho0'-W_1
   =int_0^1 e^((1-t)M_H)
       E[1_(tau<=t)w_tau M_H k_N(m_tau)] dt.
Since ||M_H k_N(m)||_1<=hN, its raw trace-norm bound is
 ||e^(M_H)rho0'-W_1||_1
 <=hN e^(2hN) P(tau<=1).                               (S10)
The initial replacement costs at most 2e^hN pbad. Thus
 ||e^(M_H)rho0-W_1||_1
 <=2e^hN pbad+6hN exp[2hN-kappa1 N^1.5].               (S11)

The unnormalized target e^(M_H)rho0 is exactly
 e^K/Zbase because t0J^2 is central.
Its trace is >=1: in every spin sector Tr e^(H_j)>=d_j by scalar trace
Jensen, since C>=I and Tr H_j/d_j=Tc j(j+1)/(3s^2)>=0.
For positive operators G,W,
 T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG.
Normalize W_1, giving the positive iid-product mixture sigma_N.
Equations (S7),(S11) then yield the public nonasymptotic certificate

 <=2 C_Lambda,delta N^3/4 e^(hN-kappa0 N)
      +6hN e^(2hN-kappa1 N^1.5)                        (S12)
whenever the explicitly listed thresholds hold. hN=O_(M,B)(sqrt N);
absorbing polynomial and sublinear exponents proves (S1), e.g. with any
c<kappa0. All trace-weight, seed-replacement, stopping and normalization

6. WHY A NAIVE GLOBAL CLAIM WOULD FAIL

D_C need not be positive on the entire Bloch ball. At |m|=1 its transverse
restriction is C_transverse minus its 90-degree-rotated version. For a diagonal
C with two different transverse eigenvalues it has both signs. Thus (S3) cannot
be declared a global positive diffusion for arbitrary anisotropy. Stopping in
the explicitly certified inner ball, and bounding the exact stopped residual
(S10), are load-bearing. Dropping the angular term changes the target and gives
only the baseline CP local-filter approximation.

Nothing here proves exact separability of every admitted finite-N target,
or a sharp exponential entanglement tail. The scalar A=aI exact case in INITIAL
shows that any positive family-wide matching lower rate is false.
The stopped construction depends on A,b and cannot improve the unchanged
universal reverse decoder's sharp N^-1/2 rate by substitution.
