## Internal proof status

`PROOFS.md` gives an independent finite-word proof that
\(C=\{(a,b):a\ge3,b\ge0\}\) is a single closed irreducible class, and a
full pathwise derivation of positive-probability finite-time explosion for
every fixed positive rate vector. The source already states the network is
strongly endotactic and explosive; the added class connectivity is internally
proved. No external verifier was used and no historical priority claim is
made.

No new broad positive recurrence theorem was proved. The exact next proof
obligation, if pursuing a new theorem, is a structural condition that controls
physical-time source-tier excursions strongly enough to preclude both a
positive infinite product of channel-selection probabilities and summable
holding times. The known CTMC Foster and embedded-chain theorems above are
used only at their stated interfaces.
