CLAIM
For the F^(q)=diag(J4-I4,...,J4-I4) family and the exact positive constant-parity Hermitian determinant-mixture interface in INITIAL, even coefficientwise relative accuracy 1/1000 on all nonzero hole coefficients, with exact zeros, requires

components. The stronger exact rank bound s>=ceil((4/3)^q) remains valid. This is a constant-accuracy positive-mixture-size obstruction, not just instability of exact matrix rank.

PROOF
Let g_R=sum_{ell=1}^s c_ell a_ell(R), c_ell>0, be the proposed signature and assume
 (1-rho) Z_R <=g_R<=(1+rho) Z_R, rho=1/1000,
for every R (so g_R=0 when Z_R=0). Each component has nonnegative even retained coefficients, all odd principal coefficients zero, and a Hermitian constant kernel as in INITIAL. The same zero arguments force every component kernel block diagonal and its four-by-four blocks rank at most two. No approximate-zero inference is used: multiplicative coefficient accuracy preserves zeros exactly.

For any nonzero component block put W_ij=|Aij|^2 and S=sum_{i<j}W_ij>0. Then the six-pair probability vector p_ij=W_ij/S sums to one. INITIAL proves rank W<=3. Let M have off-diagonal entries p_ij and diagonal zero, and let U=(J4-I4)/6. Because rank M<=3 there is a unit vector z with Mz=0. U has singular values1/2,1/6,1/6,1/6, so
 ||M-U||_F >=||(M-U)z||=||Uz||>=1/6.
Both matrices are symmetric with diagonal zero. Thus
 sum_{i<j}(p_ij-1/6)^2 =||M-U||_F^2/2>=1/72.

A direct entropy bound needs no theorem import. Along the segment from the uniform vector u=(1/6,...,1/6) to p, the Hessian of sum_j p_j log p_j is diagonal1/p_j>=1 (each probability<=1). Its first-order term at u is zero because sum(p_j-u_j)=0. Integrating the second derivative, and taking limits for zero probabilities, gives
 D(p||u)=log6-H(p)>= (1/2) sum_j(p_j-1/6)^2>=1/144.  (1)
Hence every component's nonzero four-site pair law has entropy at most log6-1/144.

Restrict the signature to the sector selecting exactly two retained coordinates in each block and normalize. There are N=6^q labels. The target is the uniform law P on those labels. The mixture law Q has the form
 Q=sum_{ell} pi_ell product_{b=1}^q p_(ell,b),
where pi_ell are the positive c_ell times the products of local pair masses, renormalized; zero-sector components are discarded. Introducing the component label L gives
 H(Q)<=H(L)+H(Q|L)<=log s+q(log6-1/144).              (2)
This elementary mixture-entropy inequality is not an assumption about independent draws: conditioned on L, block factorization is exact from the block-diagonal kernel.

The coefficientwise bound on this sector implies TV(P,Q)<=rho/(1-rho)=1/999. Indeed write each sector weight as 2^q(1+e_x), |e_x|<=rho; normalization divides by1+average(e), and half the mean absolute difference is at most rho/(1-rho).
For epsilon=TV(P,Q), a maximal coupling has mismatch probability epsilon. If E records mismatch, conditioning on Q and E shows
 H(P)<=H(Q)+H(E)+epsilon log N<=H(Q)+log2+epsilon q log6.
Since H(P)=qlog6, combine with(2):
 log s>=q(1/144-epsilon log6)-log2.
Use epsilon<=1/999 and log6<2. Then
 1/144-2/999=79/15984>0,
yielding the claim.

All arguments include zero component block masses (discard those components on the selected sector). Scalars/normalization of the whole approximating signature are immaterial to the sector law. Fixed Hermitian auxiliary modes with rank-one boundary variables are already covered by Schur reduction in INITIAL. Complex phases are allowed inside each Hermitian kernel.

LIMITATIONS
Zeros must be preserved and each component itself must be a positive constant-parity Hermitian determinant signature. Signed cancellation of odd coefficients, nonlinear label maps, interacting boundary sources, variable-dependent rational kernels, or scalar-only approximations are outside the theorem. The original block family is easy by independent-block arithmetic. The theorem excludes a proposed compressed positive representation, not polynomial counting itself.
