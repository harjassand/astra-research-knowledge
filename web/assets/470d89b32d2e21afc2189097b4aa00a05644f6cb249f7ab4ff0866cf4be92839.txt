PRIMARY STATEMENTS INSPECTED IN CYCLE04, 2026-10-09

1. M. Horodecki, P. Shor, M. Ruskai, Entanglement Breaking Channels,
arXiv:quant-ph/0302031v2 (04 June 2003).
https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0302031v2
Actual Theorems 2 and 4 inspected. EB is equivalent to measure-and-prepare
form, and trace preservation gives a POVM. This is the representation
used here. The Choi separability equivalence plus Caratheodory supplies
finite support. This prior result gives no capacity-only alphabet bound.

2. A. Jenčova, Comparison of quantum channels and statistical experiments,
arXiv:1601.06370v1 (2016).
https://arxiv.org/html/1601.06370v1
Actual Section V and Theorem 4 inspected. The statewise reconstruction
criterion is sup_theta ||sigma_theta-alpha(rho_theta)||_1<=2epsilon,
with quantum comparison characterized using the enlarged guessing tests.
The discussion preceding it explicitly says the ordinary classical
randomization criterion does not extend unchanged to quantum experiments,
even with one classical experiment. Our A1 uses explicit M,R and only
the safe forward risk implication. We claim no converse or closeness
of Gamma to the identity in diamond norm.

3. A. Monras, A. Beige, K. Wiesner, Hidden Quantum Markov Models and
non-adaptive read-out of many-body states, arXiv:1002.2337v2 (2010).
https://arxiv.org/html/1002.2337v2
Actual Sections 4.2–4.3 inspected. The quantum generator is an initial
d-level state with CP operations whose sum is TP; normalized postoutput
states update the memory. The classical generator is represented by
substochastic matrices whose sum is stochastic, with output on transitions.
Our supplied-instrument and edge-HMM definitions match this interface.
The fixed kernel construction uses the standard measure/prepare transfer;
our contribution here is the uniform statewise-error and all-history
hybrid audit, not a new definition of a quantum HMM.

4. R. Spekkens, D. Buzacott, A. Keehn, B. Toner, G. Pryde, Preparation
contextuality powers parity-oblivious multiplexing, arXiv:0805.1463v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/0805.1463v2
Actual Theorem 2 and Appendix A inspected. Its preparation-noncontextual
n-bit success bound is (n+1)/(2n). For n=2 this is 3/4. Our four qubit
preparations and the two-bit derivation instantiate the known task.
The supplied broadcasting modulus gives a quantitative witness-error
composition, whose sharpness and historical priority are not claimed.

5. P. Jokinen et al., No-broadcasting characterizes operational
contextuality, arXiv:2406.07305v1 (11 June 2024).
https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07305v1
Actual Section IV Theorem 1 inspected: existence of one EB map fixing
a state set is equivalent to broadcasting that set for all observables.
The paper treats restricted subtheories separately through pseudo-
broadcasting. The exact contextuality/broadcasting equivalence is thus
prior. Our matrix-identity witness condition intentionally does not
silently identify restricted operational equivalences with full operator
identities. No robust full-subtheory characterization is proved here.

6. R. Jain, Y. Shi, Z. Wei, S. Zhang, Efficient protocols for generating
bipartite classical distributions and quantum states. Inspected author
PDF, cached with hash; unversioned host URL, not an arXiv version pin.
https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~syzhang/papers/QStateGen.pdf
Actual Definition 1.1, Theorem 1.3, Corollary 1.1, and Section 4 inspected.
The latent cost is controlled using Wyner common information
inf_{X independent Y | W} I(X,Y;W), rather than ordinary I(X;Y).
It bounds approximate nonnegative rank/correlation generation; the
author PDF's theorem uses 7delta in its particular normalization, unlike
another author's cached 8delta formulation found in search. We use no
numerical constant from that theorem. Our B3a directly computes its
invariant and our own packing/truncation establishes the robust claims.

7. G. Braun, S. Pokutta, Common information and unique disjointness,
ECCC report 2013/056 revision 5; PDF dated 23 June 2016.
https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/056/revision/5/download/
Actual Section 3, Definition 3.1 and Lemma 3.4 inspected. Nonnegative
factorizations yield conditionally independent latent mixtures; common
information lower bounds their latent entropy and logarithmic support.
The paper studies other support patterns, especially unique disjointness.
This is prior framework for our entropy obstruction; we prove the balanced
incidence bound directly and do not import the UDISJ theorem as a premise.

8. G. Braun, R. Jain, T. Lee, S. Pokutta, Information-theoretic
approximations of the nonnegative rank, ECCC report 2013/158 revision 2;
PDF dated 18 August 2015.
https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/158/revision/2/download/
Actual introduction's theorem mapping inspected (web PDF): common
information characterizes amortized logarithmic nonnegative rank, with
quantitative compression and robust witnesses. This reinforces the prior
distinction between channel information and latent realization resources.
Theorem 4.1's full proof was not inspected or used. This report is NOT
the differently titled 2013/056 revision 5; the cache names distinguish
them. No claim that our example is the first such separation is made.
