C05_L09 DEEPER ATTACK / ATTRIBUTED CROSS-EXPOSURE
Recorded 2026-10-07 UTC. INITIAL remains immutable.

QUESTION TESTED

Can the finite-cover bridge be strengthened enough to remove the remaining
complexity gate, and do the exposed c05 proposals supply that strengthening?
I found no route that closes the fixed-sandwich, bounded-Holevo target. I did
derive the exact continuous-depth optimizer for the existing bridge and
construct a finite witness that falsifies a plausible much-weaker cover
penalty in the unrestricted experiment class. The witness remains outside
the target because its Holevo capacity diverges and its states do not obey a
fixed common sandwich.

1. EXACT OPTIMIZATION OF THE N46 FINITE-ENSEMBLE BOUND

For m>=2 supplied states, the reconstructed bound at integer tree depth L is

  F(L)=L b + A exp(-alpha L),
  A=sqrt(m ln(m)/2),  alpha=(ln 2)/2.

Treating L as real, the unique interior minimum satisfies

  exp(-alpha L*) = b/(alpha A),
  L*=(2/ln 2) ln((ln 2) A/(2b)),

when L*>0. Its value is

  F(L*)=(2b/ln 2)[ln((ln 2) A/(2b))+1].

Rounding L* to a neighboring integer adds O(b) (the exact integer minimum
is the minimum of the two adjacent evaluations). Thus the displayed
finite-cover theorem yields the scale

  b[ln m + ln(1/b) + ln ln m + O(1)].

Since b ln(1/b)->0, the condition a->0 and b ln N(a)->0 suffices. This
optimization changes no qualitative condition: the factor ln m is still
there, and this is a sufficient bound, not a necessary one.

2. FINITE LADDER TEST OF A STRONGER COVER CONDITION

I independently checked the exact N61 construction in my pre-release
source file N61_PROOF.md, Sections 2--5. Peer cross-exposure later confirmed
the same boundary in c05_l04/INITIAL.txt and c05_l10/INITIAL.txt. For
ell>=2, put m=2^(ell-1), d=ell*m, k_j=2^j, and

  H=direct_sum_(j=0)^(ell-1) Sym^(k_j)(C^d),
  rho_psi=(1/ell) direct_sum_j (|psi><psi|)^(tensor k_j).

The supplied all-input level-shift broadcaster has both marginal errors at
most 1/ell. The one-copy decoder from sector j has k_j copies of psi; the
Haar estimation inequality gives, for every EB reconstruction,

  e_EB >= 1 - (1/ell) sum_j (2^j+1)/(2^j+d)
        >= 1 - (m+1)/(m+d) = 1-O(1/ell).

This is an operational worst-case lower bound, witnessed by a prior; no
prior is supplied to the reconstruction channel.

Here is a finite restriction that also has large fixed-radius covering
number. Let t=m+1 and D_t=binom(d+t-1,t). By the exact Haar symmetric-t
moment and Caratheodory, at most D_t^2 pure vectors suffice for a finite
weighted design reproducing every moment through order t. It preserves the
EB lower bound above. Independently select a projective packing whose
distinct vectors satisfy |<psi,phi>|^2<=1/4. A cap of squared-overlap
threshold 1/4 has Haar measure (3/4)^(d-1); a maximal packing therefore has
at least (4/3)^(d-1) points. For each such pair,

  T(rho_psi,rho_phi)
    =(1/ell) sum_j sqrt(1-|<psi,phi>|^(2*2^j)) >= sqrt(3)/2.

Consequently, for any fixed cover radius a<sqrt(3)/4, the packing forces
N(a)>=(4/3)^(d-1). Take exactly that many packing points, up to rounding,
and adjoin the finite moment design. Since t=m+1 and d=ell*m,

  ln(D_t^2) <= 2t ln(e(d+t-1)/t)=O(m ln ell)=o(d).

The resulting finite experiment has log-cardinality and log-cover size
Theta(d), hence

  ln ln N(a)=ln d+O(1)=ell ln 2+O(ln ell).

It falsifies the proposed stronger sufficiency condition
  b*sqrt(ln ln N(a))->0
on unrestricted experiments: its left side is O(ell^(-1/2))->0, while its
EB error tends to one. This tests one plausible sharper penalty, not every
possible entropy expression. It also rules out any universal modulus
depending only on b, since b<=1/ell->0 while e_EB->1.

For a faithful version, replace every state by
  rho_tilde=(1-eta)rho+eta I_H/dim(H), eta=ell^(-3).
The displayed broadcaster then has error at most 1/ell+eta; every EB error
is at least the unperturbed lower bound minus 2eta; and pair distances are
multiplied by 1-eta. Thus faithfulness does not alter the counterexample to
the unrestricted b*sqrt(ln ln N) criterion.

This does NOT close or refute the stated bounded-Holevo, fixed-sandwich
target. The likelihood is rank-deficient before perturbation and has
vanishing relative eigenvalues after perturbation, so no fixed lower
sandwich holds. Its capacity is unbounded: the last symmetric sector alone
has log dimension log binom(d+m-1,m) of order m log ell, and the mean
Holevo information scales at least m log ell/ell. The entropy gate is
therefore still open in the target class.

3. LOCKING ATTACK REVISITED

The locking ensemble in INITIAL remains a valid falsifier of the proposed
single-leaf accessible-information inequality: its accessible information
is exponentially small while its average trace distance to the barycenter
is 1/2. But its anti-commuting pairs have a constant N46 affinity gap.
Applying the N46 two-state necessary condition to any broadcaster that
works on all these states prevents its marginal error from tending to zero.
Thus locking blocks that local proof upgrade; it is not a growing-
dimension broadcaster counterexample and cannot itself replace the finite
ladder attack.

4. ATTRIBUTED PEER EXPOSURE AND AUDIT

- c05_l10/INITIAL.txt: the finite-alphabet coarsening lemma reduces the
  message alphabet of an already supplied finite-outcome EB approximation;
  it adds at most 2a+delta/2 to an existing reconstruction error. Its
  output-count bound K^N does not furnish the missing EB map or improve the
  finite-cover error. However, its *separate supplied-instrument subclass*
  is a real stronger estimate: the exact SOS
  2(I-Phi)-(I-Psi)=(I-L*L)+(Psi-Psi^2)+(L-Psi)*(L-Psi)/2 >=0
  gives one EB map with error at most (1/2)sqrt(2 theta b), and coarsening
  extends this to a finite net with error 2a+delta/2+(1/2)sqrt(2 theta b).
  I checked the expansion and the norm conversion: for rho=sigma(I+A),
  <A,(I-Phi)A>_tau<=theta b, the SOS gives <A,(I-Psi)A>_tau<=2theta b,
  and 0<=Psi<=I gives ||A-Psi A||_2^2<=2theta b. This removes the cover-
  entropy factor inside the stated bistochastic-instrument interface.
  The target does not supply such a Kraus representation for every
  selfcompatible channel, so this route does not prove the global lift.
  The separate finite-tuple route has an exponent that collapses with N.
  Its N61 boundary matches the finite-ladder calculation above, with the
  same caveat that the target's fixed sandwich and bounded capacity fail.
- c05_s03/INITIAL.txt and c05_l02/INITIAL.txt: their finite-generator or
  fixed-N almost-commuting lifts do give one EB map when the generator count
  and coefficient costs are controlled. Their supplied assumptions do not
  follow from a general growing cover; constants/exponents deteriorate as
  that count grows. Neither improves the N46 estimate uniformly in N(a).
- c05_s01/INITIAL.txt: its sign-frame lift is a dimension-free map-level
  result conditional on supplied bounded approximate preimages of a full
  diagonal sign frame. The anchor norm and average error are extra inputs;
  the baseline power-range commutator estimate supplies neither.
- c05_l08/INITIAL.txt: the conditional global modulus would close the
  target if its uniform CP-range lift omega existed. The worker explicitly
  leaves that map-level lift open, so it is a useful statement of the exact
  remaining gate rather than a finite-cover improvement.
- c05_l01/INITIAL.txt: diagonal pinching has exponentially many separated
  states in its full operator-ball image despite already being EB. This
  blocks applying the N46 compact-experiment extraction to a whole CP
  image; it is a route obstruction, not a negative EB result.
- c05_l04/INITIAL.txt and c05_l10/INITIAL.txt: the N61 ladder is a genuine
  unrestricted no-modulus boundary; c05_l04 records its unbounded capacity
  and failure of a fixed lower sandwich. These limitations match the
  assumptions that prevent it from settling the target.

No peer idea supplied the missing step from bounded Holevo plus small
broadcast error to either (i) b ln N(a)->0 for a shrinking net, (ii) the
bounded anchor/generator descriptions above, or (iii) a dimension-free
whole-CP-range EB lift. I found no sound stronger finite-cover estimate
that removes this step. A sharper estimate does remove it for the exact
supplied-instrument subclass described above; the missing global step is
showing that arbitrary compatible channels admit that structure or another
uniform EB-lifting mechanism.
