BOUNDED PRIMARY-SOURCE APPLICABILITY AUDIT
Mixed-cofactor toggle recurrence candidate
Checked: 2026-10-08

Question
--------
Does a published higher-dimensional stochastic recurrence theorem already
cover the four-species non-complex-balanced fixture in RESULT.txt, especially
through tier, projected, or endotactic criteria?

Finding
-------
No theorem located in this bounded primary-source check covers the fixture.
The exact source statement and the failed hypotheses are below. This is a
noncoverage finding for the named results, not a literature-completeness or
novelty claim. The internally proved Foster result remains under the explicit
rate condition kappa>0; no all-weakly-reversible claim follows.

1. Anderson--Kim stochastic tier criteria
------------------------------------------
D. F. Anderson and J. Kim, “Some Network Conditions for Positive Recurrence
of Stochastically Modeled Reaction Networks,” arXiv:1710.11263v3,
Theorem 4.2 and Corollary 4.3:
  https://arxiv.org/html/1710.11263v3

Theorem 4.2 is a general sufficient condition, not restricted to binary
networks: for every tier sequence, the highest stochastic source tier
T^{S,1} must intersect D, the sources of descending reactions. Corollary
4.3 gives the stronger sufficient gate T^{D,1}=T^{S,1} and D nonempty for
every tier sequence. The conclusions concern positive recurrence of each
state in every closed irreducible component and finite expected entrance
time into the union of closed classes.

The exact fixture in RESULT.txt fails Theorem 4.2's gate. Order coordinates
(A,B,C,D) and take x_n=(0,n,n,2). With x_n vee 1, the leading deterministic
monomial tier is exactly {2C+D, A+2C+D}; both monomials equal 2n^2. The
highest enabled stochastic source is 2C+D, with rate (n)_2*2. The other
top deterministic source A+2C+D is disabled because A=0. The two reactions
between 2C+D and A+2C+D preserve the x vee 1 monomial along this sequence,
since the A coordinate is clipped to 1, so neither is descending. The
reaction A+B -> A is descending but its source monomial is only n, below
the n^2 stochastic top. Hence T^{S,1} cap D is empty on this tier sequence.
This only rules out that sufficient theorem; it does not contradict the
positive recurrence proved by our different Lyapunov function.

2. Strongly endotactic stochastic recurrence subclass
-----------------------------------------------------
D. F. Anderson, D. Cappelletti, J. Kim, and T. Nguyen, “Tier Structure of
Strongly Endotactic Reaction Networks,” arXiv:1808.05328v2, Theorem 7.2
and Corollary 7.1:
  https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05328v2

Strong endotacticity by itself is not a stochastic recurrence theorem; the
paper gives transient and explosive strongly endotactic examples. Theorem
7.2 adds, for each species S_i, a nonempty family of pure-species drains
a S_i -> sum_j r'_j S_j with a>=m-1 and total product order <=a-1, where m
is the maximum source-complex molecularity. It also requires the maximum
product order among those drains to be below their minimum source order.
The fixture has m=4. Its only pure-species drains are the first-order
A->0, C->0, and D->0; B has no pure drain. None meet the required a>=3,
and its displayed higher-order deactivation channels have mixed
A-plus-cofactor sources. Thus it does not satisfy Theorem 7.2. Corollary
7.1 is the binary case and also fails here.

The base fixture is not strongly endotactic either. For w=(0,-100,1,1),
the four source complexes 2C+D, A+2C+D, C+2D, and A+C+2D are all w-maximal
with value 3. Every reaction out of these maximal complexes changes only
A, so its w-increment is zero. There is no strictly inward reaction from
the maximal source set, violating Definition 3.2(ii) in the source. The
positive recurrence theorem therefore cannot be reached by labeling this
fixture strongly endotactic.

3. Other established controls
-----------------------------
* Anderson--Cappelletti--Kim, arXiv:1904.08967v2, Theorem 4.1, proves
  positive recurrence for a weakly reversible single-linkage binary network
  after adding in-flows and out-flows, with a pure S_i or 2S_i complex for
  every species. The fixture has three linkage classes and molecularity-3/4
  toggle complexes, so it fails the single-linkage and binary gates; B also
  lacks a pure multiple. C and D do have pure unit complexes from 0<->C,D.
  https://arxiv.org/html/1904.08967v2
* Wiuf--Xu, arXiv:2012.07954v3, proves the all-weakly-reversible result for
  one-dimensional stoichiometric subspaces. The fixture has rank four:
  its 0<->A, 0<->C, 0<->D, and A<->A+B differences already span all four
  coordinates. The naive projection onto A,B is not itself a CTMC because
  the A toggle propensities depend on C,D.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07954v3
* The ConStRAINeD page reports a complete proof of the two-species,
  all-weakly-reversible recurrence conjecture, but lists the manuscript as
  “In preparation.” The July 2026 conference abstract is also a report, not
  the full proof. Four species/rank four puts this fixture outside that
  result in any case. Status checked on 2026-10-08:
  https://constrained.polito.it/publications/
  https://contributions.ecmtb2026.org/event/1/sessions/64/
* Product-Poisson/complex-balanced results do not cover the chosen fixture.
  The 0<->A edge forces activity a_A=alpha/delta=2, while the two toggle
  edges force a_A=zeta_1/eta_1=1 and a_A=zeta_2/eta_2=1/2. Thus there is no
  positive complex-balanced activity vector for the fixture.
  https://arxiv.org/html/0803.3042v4
