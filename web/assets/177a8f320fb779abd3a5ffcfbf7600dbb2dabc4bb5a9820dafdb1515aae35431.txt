A. c10_l02: infinite supplied rank-one weakly reversible mass-action class.
Origin: work/cycle6/c10_l02/INITIAL.txt; exact hash in CROSS_REVIEW.json.
The claimed factorial-potential asymptotic survives. Infinite nonnegative rank-one rays require a generator v>=0 after orientation; mixed signs give a bounded segment. Coordinates outside support(v) are fixed, so within each linkage their requirements agree and a linkage is either eventually enabled or permanently disabled. On an enabled ray x=b+t v, reactant falling-factorial rates are positive-leading polynomials of degree d(y)=sum_{v_i>0} y_i. The degree decreases by |alpha| G under an inward jump alpha v, where G=sum v_i>0. At the largest degree p, all nonzero jumps are inward and at least one exists, by weak reversibility of the active linkage. Thus scalar drift D(t)=-d_p t^p+O(t^{p-1}), d_p>0. Since every source and product has size<=2, 1<=p<=2.
Required clarification: F(t)=G t log t+O(t) ALONE does not justify differencing the error at adjacent states. The correct proof uses the exact factorial quotient: for each fixed integer alpha, sum_i log((x_i+alpha v_i)!/x_i!)=alpha G log t+O(1), by finitely many factors x_i+j=v_i t+O(1). The maximal available falling-factorial polynomial M(t) is eventually a single positive-leading polynomial (finite polynomial crossings); log(M(t+alpha)/M(t))=O(1/t). Hence Delta F=alpha G log t+O(1), and LF=-G d_p t^p log t+O(t^p). Properness and bounded jumps follow. The supplied nonexplosion premise or independent rank-one linear drift suffices for the ordinary Foster conclusion. No acquisition of the exact irreducible class is supplied.
The same leading-rate argument gives an executable linear potential t and a coefficient-derived threshold, with rational arithmetic of bounded polynomial degree. If D=-a t^p+sum_{j<p} d_j t^j, t>=max(1,ceil(2 sum |d_j|/a)) ensures negative leading domination; increase the threshold if a unit drift margin is requested. The potential may be translated to remain nonnegative on the finite beginning of the supplied class.

None subsumes this rank-one factorial identity, but rank-one recurrence itself is established prior literature. Wiuf--Xu, arXiv:2012.07954v2, Corollary 4.12, assumes rank one and a nonnegative oriented stoichiometric gcd; weak reversibility supplies the bidirectionality hypothesis. Those hypotheses match the admitted infinite class here after removing irrelevant fixed-coordinate disabled linkages. Its conclusion is stronger than bare positive recurrence and is not restricted to complexes of size<=2. Thus do not count the recurrence conclusion as a new gate. The potential calculation and honest event-clock diagnostic remain useful.

B. c10_l05: 0->B, B->2A, A+B->B, fixed positive rational rates alpha,beta,gamma.
The generator calculation is correct. With q=max(1,ceil(2 beta/gamma)), V=a/(b+q)+b, the a coefficient is -alpha/[q(q+1)] at b=0, and for b>=1 is
-alpha/[(b+q)(b+q+1)] + b/(b+q)[beta/(b+q-1)-gamma] <= -gamma/[2(q+1)].
The additive coefficient is alpha at b=0 and alpha+2 beta b/(b+q-1)-beta b<=alpha+2 beta-beta b at b>=1. Consequently LV<=C-eta V, C=alpha+2 beta, eta=min(q delta,beta), delta=min(alpha/[q(q+1)],gamma/[2(q+1)]). This is an exact all-state inequality; V is proper because V<=M implies b<=M and a<=M(M+q). Nonexplosion follows from autonomous immigration-death B and pathwise finite A birth counts.
The closed class C={b>=1} union {b=0,a>=2} is correct. Every state can reach (0,1) by introducing B, deleting A one at a time, and deleting excess B while compensating the A generated. From (0,1), keep b>=1 and repeat an immigration followed by B conversion to increase A by two; delete excess A to set any target, then immigrate B as needed. To finish at b=0,a>=2, first set a-2 at b=1 and use one final B conversion. The two excluded states (0,0),(1,0) enter C via immigration. Every enabled jump within C stays within C.
Endotactic proof clarification: maxima over all sources do not in general equal maxima over w-essential sources. For this network the missing tie check is short. For w=(u,v), source scores are 0,v,u+v and reaction displacements project to v,2u-v,-u. If source 0 is maximal and neutral then v=0, u<0 makes B tied and essential. If A+B is maximal and neutral then u=0,v>0 makes B tied and essential. A neutral B source has v=2u and can be maximal only when u=v=0. Thus, for w!=0, every maximal neutral source has an essential source tied at that score. The author's inequalities at all-source maxima then prove endotacticity in the full essential-source definition.

C. c10_l08: closed mA<->(m-1)A+B and mB<->A+(m-1)B, conserved count V, predictable rates in [kappa,K].
The finite class I={m-1,...,V-m+1} and the unit-jump birth/death intensities are correct. For m<=L<V/2 and mu=V^(1-m)[kappa(V-L)_m-K(L)_m]>0, monotonicity gives drift>=mu at every j<L. Stopped Dynkin with the finite-state process gives E tau_L <=(L-j)/mu; it holds for history-dependent predictable rates, without a stationary controller assumption.
The tail proof also survives. The decreasing test exp[-theta(j-j0)] gives its worst generator at minimum birth and maximum death. For theta=mu/(2 V S)<=1/2, S=kappa+K(L/V)^m, quadratic exponential bounds give killed-generator drift<=-alpha times the live test, alpha=mu^2/(4 V S). Killing at L only improves the inequality. The live test is at least exp[-theta(L-j0)], so P(tau>t)<=min(1,exp[theta(L-j0)-alpha t]). At t=4 G/mu, G=L-(m-1), the uniform exponent is -mu G/(2 V S).
With rational q<p=(1+(K/kappa)^(1/m))^-1, f(q)=kappa(1-q)^m-K q^m>0; eta=f(q)/[2m(kappa+K)], u=q+eta gives f(u)>=f(q)/2 by the Lipschitz bound. For V large enough that (m-1)/V<=eta and L=floor(qV)>=m, falling-factorial inequalities yield mu/V>=f(u)>0. The certificate has explicit volume threshold, mean bound q/f(u), and exponentially small tail at an explicit O(1) time for fixed positive margin. This is a finite conservative subclass return theorem, not infinite-class positive recurrence or arbitrary network acquisition.
The exact birth--death mean formula is standard and the supplied weights have the correct consecutive ratio. Copied scripts verify the backward Poisson equation exactly at V=64,128,256. That verification is separate from the predictable-control proof. The short-input m=2 example K=2^(2b), p=1/(2^b+1), q=p-p^2 yields exponent 2D(q||p) asymptotic to (2^b+1)^-3. It accurately rules out a margin-independent positive constant; its conservative volume floor is not a necessary lower bound.
