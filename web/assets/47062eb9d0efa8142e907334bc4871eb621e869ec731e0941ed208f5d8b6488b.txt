1. EXACT TOP-FIBER EXPERIMENT AND PURE CARRIER LIFT

Let k>=0, m>=1 and M=m+k. Write

  G_(k,m)=ker Delta in Sym^k(C^3) tensor Sym^m((C^3)^*),
  Delta=sum_(i=1..3)partial_(x_i)partial_(y_i).

This is the irreducible SU(3) carrier of highest weight (k,m), with
dimension (k+1)(m+1)(k+m+2)/2. For a unit normal v, let

  P_(k,m),v be the projector onto
  Sym^k(v^perp) tensor span{bar(v)^tensor m} in G_(k,m),
  omega_v=P_(k,m),v/(k+1).                               (1.1)

The factor bar(v) denotes the dual carrier direction in a fixed standard
basis. Its tensor with every polynomial supported on v^perp is harmonic,
so this subspace has exactly k+1 dimensions and is the top U(2) fiber.
The entire normal v is unknown. No plane or inner frame is supplied.

There are explicit covariant CPTP channels

  C_(m,k): Sym^M((C^3)^*) -> G_(k,m),
  R_(m,k): G_(k,m) -> Sym^m((C^3)^*),

such that, EXACTLY for every v,

  C_(m,k)(P_bar(v)^M)=omega_v,
  R_(m,k)(omega_v)=P_bar(v)^m.                            (1.2)

Consequently a Q-branch reconstruction code for omega_v at error epsilon
induces, by these parameter-independent pre/post channels, a Q-branch code
from the pure coherent M carrier to the pure coherent m carrier at no
larger error. This is an ASYMMETRIC pure coherent conversion problem.
It is not the same-state thermal capacity in COLD_CARRIER.txt.

2. EXPLICIT ANTISYMMETRIC ISOMETRY AND ITS NORMALIZATION

Define the fundamental SU(3) isometry

  W1 bar(e_i)=2^(-1/2)sum_(j,l)epsilon_(ijl)e_j tensor e_l.

The two nonzero terms per basis vector make W1^dag W1=I. Epsilon invariance
gives covariance from the dual fundamental to two fundamental factors.
For k copies, regroup its outputs into two k-factor registers and project
each to Sym^k(C^3). On the input Sym^k((C^3)^*) the resulting map Vk is
an intertwiner. At bar(e3)^tensor k its vector, up to an overall phase, is

  Vk bar(e3)^tensor k
    =2^(-k/2)sum_(r=0..k)(-1)^r
       |r,k-r> tensor |k-r,r>,                           (2.1)

where the displayed two-mode occupation vectors are normalized. Indeed,
each group of binom(k,r) equal tensor configurations contributes that many
terms, while the two symmetric occupation overlaps contribute their inverse
binomial factor. Thus its squared norm is (k+1)/2^k, independent of the
unknown direction. Schur's lemma gives

  Vk^dag Vk=[(k+1)/2^k] I,
  Wk=sqrt(2^k/(k+1))Vk, Wk^dag Wk=I.                    (2.2)

For k=0 both maps are the scalar identity. Equation (2.1) becomes a
maximally entangled pair of the two Sym^k(v^perp) spaces after covariance.
Tracing either factor gives the normalized identity on that plane carrier.

Let S_(m,k) be the canonical split isometry from Sym^M dual into
Sym^m dual tensor Sym^k dual, obtained by regrouping its tensor factors.
It maps every coherent vector to bar(v)^m tensor bar(v)^k. Apply Wk to
the second factor and reorder the first two outputs. For every coherent
input, the resulting first/second pair belongs to G_(k,m): its fundamental
factor lies in v^perp, so contraction against bar(v) vanishes. Coherent
vectors span Sym^M dual; therefore the full isometry has image in
G_(k,m) tensor Sym^k(C^3). Denote it I_(m,k). Then

  C_(m,k)(X)=Tr_environment I_(m,k) X I_(m,k)^dag.

This is a deterministic CPTP channel, not postselection with uncharged
success probability. Its discarded environment has dimension
(k+1)(k+2)/2. The normalization (2.2) is part of the isometry.

For completeness the harmonic carrier identification is finite algebra.
Multiplication by x dot y and Delta form the usual contraction recursion;
on bidegree (k-1,m-1), Delta[(x dot y)f]=(k+m+1)f+(x dot y)Delta f.
This positive recursion gives surjectivity of Delta, so its kernel has
dimension dim Sym^k dim Sym^m-dim Sym^(k-1)dim Sym^(m-1), equal to the
dimension in Section 1. The highest vector x1^k y3^m has weight (k,m).
Its irrep has that same dimension, identifying the whole kernel without
an unproved phase convention.

Finally include G_(k,m) into Sym^k fundamental tensor Sym^m dual and trace
the fundamental register. On omega_v it returns P_bar(v)^m exactly, giving
R_(m,k) in (1.2). All maps are covariant; U(3) determinant phases cancel
on density operators. A code factoring through branches of dimension Q
continues to factor through the same branches after composition. No hidden
quantum memory is introduced by the converter environments, which are
discarded before/after the retained code, respectively.

3. EXACT PREPARATION OF A DEGENERATE PLANE-ONLY IID CONTROL

The d=3 Werner-Holevo channel is

  W(X)=[Tr(X)I-X^T]/2.

Its unnormalized Choi matrix is (I-SWAP)/2>=0, and its output partial trace
is I. Thus W is CPTP, with the conjugate-to-fundamental covariance used
above. For a coherent fundamental direction,

  W(P_bar(v))=(I-P_v)/2.

For supplied 1/3<a<1/2, b=1-2a, put t=6a-2 in (0,1), and define

  W_a=t W+(1-t)[Tr(X)I/3].

Then W_a(P_bar(v))=aI-(a-b)P_v, with spectrum (a,a,b). Embed a coherent
source Sym^n dual into n dual qutrit factors and apply W_a^tensor n:

  P_bar(v)^n -> [aI-(a-b)P_v]^tensor n.                  (3.1)

This is exact and globally covariant. However, the degenerate spectrum
has c=0; it is NOT contained in JOINT_CAPTURE.txt's positive critical
c in [c0,c1]. Averaging the actual critical orbit over one common U(2)
rotation gives int_g rho_(Ug)^tensor n dg, which is not
[int_g rho_(Ug)dg]^tensor n. Substituting the latter would silently change
the experiment. This control therefore supplies no automatic critical iid
converse, even before the thermal issue in Section 4.

4. DECISIVE PURE-INPUT/WARM-TARGET RANK-ONE COUNTEREXAMPLE

Consider the coherent covariant measure-and-prepare channel on Sym^m C3,
with d_m=(m+1)(m+2)/2:

  E_m(X)=d_m int_CP2 P_w^m Tr(P_w^m X)dw.                (4.1)

It is EB and has quantum branch Q=1. Its POVM is normalized by coherent
resolution. The output on input P_v^m is covariant and diagonal in the
occupation layers N=m-n_v, with N+1 identical eigenvalues per layer.
Set v=e3. Dirichlet monomial integration gives those eigenvalues exactly:

  c_N=(m+1)(m+2)m!(2m-N)!/[(m-N)!(2m+2)!],
         0<=N<=m,
  c_0=(m+2)/[2(2m+1)]=1/4+3/[4(2m+1)],
  c_(N+1)/c_N=(m-N)/(2m-N).                             (4.2)

Proof: a normalized occupation vector (n1,n2,m-N) has coherent probability
m!|w1|^(2n1)|w2|^(2n2)|w3|^(2m-2N)/(n1!n2!(m-N)!). Multiplying by
the outcome likelihood |w3|^(2m) and using the Haar Dirichlet moment gives
2d_m m!(2m-N)!/[(m-N)!(2m+2)!], exactly (4.2). Phase integration eliminates
off-diagonal entries, and covariance gives the formula for every unknown v.

Let the TARGET, distinct from the pure input, be the genuine warm cold-
carrier density at q1=q2=1/2:

  sigma_(m,v)=Z_m^(-1) 2^(-N_v),
  Z_m=sum_(N=0..m)(N+1)2^(-N).

This seed satisfies all temperature/gap hypotheses of COLD_CARRIER.txt.
Nevertheless the rank-one channel (4.1) gives

  sup_v T(E_m(P_v^m),sigma_(m,v))<=5/m.                 (4.3)

This whole-trace estimate is uniform, not just a covariance or first-moment
calculation. To prove it, set q=1/2 and w_infty=1/4. For N<=m,

  c_N/c_0=q^N prod_(j=0..N-1)(1-j/m)/(1-j/(2m)).

Each product factor lies in [0,1]; its deficit is j/(2m-j)<=j/m. The union
bound therefore gives

  0<=q^N-c_N/c_0<=q^N N(N-1)/(2m).

Since c0<=1/2 and |c0-1/4|<=3/(8m),

  sum_(N=0..m)(N+1)|c_N-w_infty q^N|
    <=[3/(8m)]sum_(N>=0)(N+1)q^N
       +[c0/(2m)]sum_(N>=0)(N+1)N(N-1)q^N
    <=3/(2m)+6/m=15/(2m).                               (4.4)

The two sums are 4 and 24. Normalizing the finite thermal target adds
L1 discrepancy delta=1-w_infty Z_m=(m+3)2^(-m)/4. Half the total L1 bound
is at most 15/(4m)+(m+3)2^(-m)/8<=5/m, proving (4.3).

Its error actually has the exact positive leading limit

  m sup_v T(E_m(P_v^m),sigma_(m,v)) -> 15/16.             (4.5)

For every fixed N, m(c_N-2^(-N)/4) tends to
2^(-N)[3/8-N(N-1)/16]. The bound used in (4.4) supplies a summable
geometric times quadratic majorant, and the finite thermal normalization
has exponentially small correction. Dominated convergence therefore gives
the half-L1 limit. The limiting signed coefficients are positive for
N=0,1,2, zero for N=3 and negative thereafter. Their multiplicity-weighted
positive sum is 3/8+3/8+3/16=15/16, proving (4.5).

By contrast, reconstructing sigma_(m,v) FROM THE SAME WARM INPUT at Q=1
has error bounded below by a positive constant from COLD_CARRIER.txt.
Indeed Lambda_m(1) is bounded below uniformly and the thermal multiplier
is (1-1/2)^4=1/16. The counterexample changes the input experiment, not
that theorem. It shows why a pure-coherent preparation channel followed
by an unspecified warm retrieval cannot transfer the warm reconstruction
lower. Its apparent residual can already be generated classically at
o(m^(-1/2)), even with equal input/output carrier size m.
