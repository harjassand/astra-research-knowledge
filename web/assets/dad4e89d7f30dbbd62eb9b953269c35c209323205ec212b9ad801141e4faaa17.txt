## New conditional consequence: very slowly growing range complexity

The c05_s03 recurrence is
\[
F_1=0,\quad F_k(\delta)=\min\{1,[(k-1)^{2/3}\delta^{2/3}+F_{k-1}(\min(2,\delta+4((k-1)\delta)^{1/3}))^2]^{1/2}\}.
\]
For each fixed k, induction gives `F_k(delta) <= C_k delta^(3^(-(k-1)))`. Its stated constants satisfy, with `b_k=C_k^2`,
\[
b_1=0,\qquad b_k=(k-1)^{2/3}+g_k b_{k-1},\quad
 g_k=[1+4(k-1)^{1/3}]^{2/3^{k-2}}.
\]
Now `sum_{k>=3} log(g_k) < infinity`, because its terms are `O(3^{-k} log k)`. Let `G=prod_{k>=3} g_k < infinity`. Unrolling the positive recurrence gives
\[
b_k=\sum_{j=2}^k(j-1)^{2/3}\prod_{\ell=j+1}^k g_\ell
 \leq G\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}j^{2/3}\leq G k^{5/3},
\]
so `C_k <= sqrt(G) k^(5/6)`. Consequently the finite-rank c05_s03 lift obeys
\[
\operatorname{dist}_{\infty\to1}(\Phi^m,EB)
 \leq r_m F_{r_m}(\delta_m)
 \leq \sqrt G\, r_m^{11/6}
       \exp[-L_m/3^{r_m-1}],
\quad L_m=\log(1/\delta_m),\quad
\delta_m=2/\sqrt{m+1}+4/(m+1).
\]
For any fixed `q>0`, if eventually
\[
r_m\leq (1-q)\log_3 L_m,
\]
then `L_m/3^(r_m-1) >= 3 L_m^q`, which dominates the logarithm of the polynomial prefactor. The right side tends to zero. Since `L_m ~ (1/2) log m`, a simpler sufficient condition is `r_m <= (1-q) log_3(log m)` for all sufficiently large m. Likewise, a **certified** L2-net of the entire range with size `N_m <= (1-q) log_3 L_m` and radius `epsilon_m ->0` gives error `epsilon_m+F_{N_m}(delta_m) ->0`.

This is a real growing-complexity corollary, beyond fixed rank / fixed net size, but it is very restrictive and conditional on the channel sequence having that range-rank or certified-cover profile. No such profile follows from the problem hypotheses. Range rank is not automatically small: for a full-rank positive selfadjoint channel, `rank(Phi^m)` can stay at its original value as m increases. The theorem therefore does not give arbitrary-dimension uniformity.

Numerical recurrence audit: `range_growth_audit.py` computes the recurrence through k=80 and logs the predicted finite-rank bound for sample m. Its output is `range_growth_audit.json`; the partial product through 80 is 6.3523 and the computed ratio `b_80/80^(5/3)` is 0.5988. The script explicitly labels this as recurrence arithmetic only. The asymptotic proof above, not the finite computation, establishes convergence of the infinite product.

## New norm-level transfer audit: what shareability gives and misses

Let `T=Phi^m` on `M_d` with normalized trace. The iterated broadcaster makes the Schrödinger predual `T_*` `K=2^m`-extendible on its output: recursively split each receiver output, then symmetrize the `K` outputs. Its normalized Choi state
\[
\Omega_{T_*}=(id\otimes T_*)(|\Omega_d\rangle\langle\Omega_d|),
\quad |\Omega_d\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_i|ii\rangle,
\]
has a symmetric K-extension. For a Hermiticity-preserving Heisenberg-map difference `Delta=T-E`, let `Delta_*` be its Schrödinger predual. The exact pairing is
\[
\operatorname{Tr}[\Omega_{\Delta_*}(B^T\otimes A)]
 =\tau(B\Delta(A)),\qquad
\|\Delta\|_{\infty\to1,\tau,Herm}
 =\sup_{A=A^*,B=B^*,\|A\|,\|B\|\leq1}
   |\operatorname{Tr}[\Omega_{\Delta_*}(B^T\otimes A)]|.
\]
Thus the requested map metric is the Choi **product-observable / injective correlation norm**, weaker than trace distance and one-way-LOCC distinguishability. A separable Choi approximant with exact reference marginal `I/d` is equivalent to the required EB trace-preserving channel.

The primary Brandao--Christandl--Yard theorem does not yet provide a dimension-free conclusion. Their Corollary 2 gives, for a K-extendible bipartite state, a separable approximant in one-way LOCC distance at most `sqrt(16 ln(2) log(d_R)/K)` (arXiv:1010.1750, Cor. 2, https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.1750). Since product-observable tests are among one-way-LOCC tests, this is stronger in norm, but its error with `K=2^m` is `O(sqrt(log d/2^m))`. The target allows `d` to grow arbitrarily; choosing `d >= exp(c 2^m)` leaves this estimate constant. In addition, the theorem's separable approximant is not stated with the exact Choi reference marginal needed for trace preservation. I have not proved a dimension-free marginal repair. The usual trace-norm de Finetti estimate has a dimension-squared factor and is still less suitable. These are failed direct applications, not counterexamples to a possible product-norm theorem.

This pinpoints a stronger, potentially decisive alternative gate: prove a K-extendible Choi-to-separable approximation in the product-observable norm with a modulus independent of both local dimensions and with exact fixed reference marginal, or exploit the extra positive-selfadjoint semigroup structure of `Phi^m` to obtain such a bound. Existing LOCC/trace de Finetti estimates do not establish it.

The recent Alekseev--Thom Ulam-stability result for the hyperfinite II1 factor (arXiv:2606.07369, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07369) was checked at the abstract/interface level. It concerns approximately additive/multiplicative/unital/*-preserving maps from a fixed hyperfinite factor and permits a small target amplification. Our input is a finite-dimensional UCP channel whose whole image is almost commuting; no approximately multiplicative map from the hyperfinite factor is supplied. Thus this theorem does not directly yield the required EB map. A substantive bridge would be needed; injectivity/Ulam-stability cannot be cited as the missing lift itself.

## Deeper transfer attack: local-measurement de Finetti and channel CP order

I checked two additional primary-source routes against the exact target norm. These audits do not alter the frozen INITIAL.

### Brandao--Harrow local-measurement theorem: exact quantifiers

Theorem 1(1) of Brandao--Harrow, *Quantum de Finetti Theorems under Local Measurements with Applications*, arXiv:1210.6367v5, states for a K-extendible state `rho_AB` and a distribution `mu` over operations `E_m:A->Atilde` that
\[
\min_{\sigma\in Sep(A:B)}\max_{\Lambda^B\in M}
  \mathbb E_{m\sim\mu}\| (E_m\otimes\Lambda^B)(\rho-\sigma)\|_1
  \le \sqrt{2\ln|\widetilde A|/K}.
\]
For a **single fixed** Hermitian contraction A, use its binary measurement as `E_A` and `mu=delta_A`; this gives a separable `sigma_A` with dimension-free error `sqrt(2 ln 2/K)` against every B measurement. But `sigma_A` may depend on A. For one fixed `mu` over many A-tests, the theorem only controls the average over A and a common worst-case B test. The desired `infinity-to-one` map norm instead requires one EB map that works for `sup_A sup_B`. The missing operation is not a harmless norm conversion: even the scalar payoff matrix `M_ij=1{i=j}` has `max_ij M_ij=1`, while `min_{mu on [n]} max_j E_{i~mu} M_ij=1/n`. This exact witness only proves that the theorem's average-A quantifier cannot logically be changed to a uniform-A quantifier without additional structure; it is not a quantum counterexample.

There is also a marginal issue. A separable Choi state is trace-preserving exactly when its reference marginal is `I/d`. The cited theorem states closeness to the unrestricted separable set; it does not state that the selected `sigma` has this exact marginal. The single-A conclusion therefore is not yet an EB-TP channel conclusion. A usable extension would have to provide simultaneously (i) a common `sigma` for all A-tests and (ii) the exact reference marginal, or prove a dimension-free repair for both.

### New channel de Finetti theorem: CP-order lemma and interface rejection

Ye--Bergh--Datta, *Quantum de Finetti theorems for states and channels in any distance measure*, arXiv:2609.40343v1 (submitted 30 September 2026), Theorem 2 gives for a permutation-covariant `N^(n):A^n->B^n` a CP-order domination of its k-site reduced channel by a mixture of iid channels:
\[
 (1-\varepsilon_{nk})N^{(k)}\le_{CP}\int \Psi^{\otimes k}\,d\nu(\Psi),
\]
with the displayed error bound
\[
\varepsilon_{nk}\le {d_A^2d_B^2 k\over n}
 +2d_A k\sqrt{{d_A^2d_B^2-1\over n+d_A^2d_B^2-1}}
 +{d_A^2k^2\over n+d_A^2d_B^2-1}.
\]
This suggests a useful elementary conversion. If same-domain unital CP maps T and E obey `T <=_CP lambda E`, with `lambda>=1`, then D=`lambda E-T` is CP and D(I)=`(lambda-1)I`. For Hermitian `||A||_infinity<=1`, positivity gives `-(lambda-1)I<=D(A)<=(lambda-1)I`; since `T-E=(lambda-1)E-D`,
\[
 \sup_{A=A^*,\|A\|_\infty\le1}\|T(A)-E(A)\|_{1,\tau}
 \le 2(\lambda-1).
\]
Thus if one ever obtains CP domination by an **EB unital** E with `lambda->1`, it immediately proves the needed map-norm lift, dimension-free. The cited theorem does not provide that: its components `Psi` are arbitrary channels, so the mixture need not be EB, and its hypotheses concern `n` input and `n` output slots, not a single-input multi-output broadcaster / symmetric Choi extension. Even in its native class, taking `k=1`, `n=K`, `d_A=d_B=d` leaves `epsilon` of order `d^3/sqrt(K)`; it does not give uniformity when d is unrestricted. The stronger no-signalling exponential theorem (Theorem 3) approximates by Choi-almost-iid channels, also not by EB channels, and has its own dimension/defect parameters.

The two-source transfer result is therefore a sharpened diagnosis, not a theorem for `beta_m`: there are dimension-free **test-averaged** shareability bounds and channel **CP-order** bounds, but the target needs a single exact-marginal EB channel under the uniform product-observable norm. The first route misses simultaneous quantifiers; the second misses both the broadcast interface and separability of its approximants.
