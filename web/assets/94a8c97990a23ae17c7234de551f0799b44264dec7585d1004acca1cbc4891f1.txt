Current lead: Zhen–Chen–Wang, arXiv:2610.06533v1, Corollary 2.2.

Channel interface

- One finite-dimensional CPTP map Phi_*: M_n(C) -> M_D(C).
- D=256=2^8 output dimension (8 output qubits per channel use).
- n=2^N with N=37,608,898 input qubits per channel use.
- The witness is Omega_n on two channel inputs, requiring 2N=75,217,796
  input qubits across the two registers; the two channel outputs together
  have dimension D^2=65,536 (16 qubits).
- The claim is 2*S_min(Phi_*) - S(Phi_*^tensor2(Omega_n)) > 1/1024 nats.
  The one-copy S_min lower bound is uniform over every input state.

Exact construction parameters in the p=1 specialization

- gamma=2, k=64, K=D=2 gamma k=256.
- One permutation leg has q_m=2^(24m-7), and n=q_m^2.
- d=D(D-1)=65,280; m=3(4d+53)=783,519.
- Hence N=48m-14=37,608,898.
- C^2=103/2, delta=2^-40, and the filter is F=(I+R)^(-1/2), with R
  specified by a finite spherical average of a degree-2m Chebyshev polynomial.
- The channel and filter entries are prescribed real algebraic numbers.

Materialization and physical interpretation

- No n-by-n permutation matrices, filter, channel Choi matrix, or Bell output
  was materialized. The closed formulas are an explicit mathematical
  specification, not a feasible implementation at this size.
- This is a theorem about finite-dimensional channels; the stated theorem
  does not provide a laboratory preparation/circuit cost or a physical
  sensing/detection protocol.
- The two-use Bell witness is a mathematical state over two huge input
  registers. Its exact preparation cost is not bounded by the entropy proof.
- The exact-arithmetic replay validates constants only. Full proof review,
  external validation, and code-level matrix construction remain UNKNOWN.

Structural uniform-net gate

- The fixed-vector obstruction uses only exact scalar arithmetic and the
  source's stated D=256 branch formula. The replay script runs in negligible
  local time and memory; no large matrices are generated.
- A filter preserving the common line must have f^2<=C/sqrt(D(D-1))
  (about 0.02809). Suppressing that one dimension costs at most 1/n in
  normalized trace, so this is a necessary condition, not a resource lower
  bound on the full filter.
- No computation, channel, or smaller construction is claimed. The remaining
  proof target is an operator-valued/product-vector certificate controlling
  all coefficient matrices with small normalized filter loss.
