# Constructive comparator compiler: cycle 6

## Verdict

The logarithmic tree support proof can be compiled into one explicit finite EB channel with additive slack, in randomized polynomial time from an exact dense finite-bit broadcaster descriptor. The exponentially many raw tree outcomes are not a compiler obstruction. A recursive sampler draws each full correlated outcome and contracts its pullback effect with polynomially many (d\times d) messages; a rational residual POVM effect turns the sampled covariance into a genuine canonical EB channel; matrix multiplicative weights combines polynomially many approximate support responses.

For \(n=d^2-1\), \(m=\lceil\log_2n\rceil\), the output satisfies

\[
I-\widehat\Psi\preceq 2m(I-\Phi)+\delta I
\]

with probability at least (1-\eta). It has (T(N+1)) explicitly listed effects, where (T=O(m^2\log(n)/\delta^2)) and (N=O(d\delta^{-2}\log(dTn/\eta))). The full argument, constants, concentration events, bit model, deployment costs, and literature boundary are in [CONSTRUCTIVE_COMPILER.md](CONSTRUCTIVE_COMPILER.md); the claim ledger is [CLAIMS.json](CLAIMS.json).

This removes the compiler gate for the already known logarithmic comparison under exact dense access. It does not prove the sharp dimension-free factor 2 or settle the bounded-Holevo approximate-broadcasting lift. The claim is conditional on finite, efficiently evaluable input data, randomized output with additive slack, and standard approximate matrix/eigenbasis computations; a physical black box or arbitrary exact-real descriptor is outside the bit model.
