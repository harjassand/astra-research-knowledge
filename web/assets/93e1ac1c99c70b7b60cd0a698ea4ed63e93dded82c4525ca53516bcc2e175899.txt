1. Exact interface and conclusion

Fix one compact connected simple group G, its root data with long roots of
squared length 2, rank r, g=dim(G), p=|Phi+|, fundamental weights
omega_1,...,omega_r, and Weyl vector rho. For each carrier let

  Lambda=sum_i n_i omega_i != 0,   N=sum_i n_i >=1,
  d=dim(V_Lambda),   tau=Tr/d.

No active-face lower bound is assumed: any n_i may be zero or small. Choose
the physics-scaled Hermitian generators T_1,...,T_g in the normalization
used in cycle6/RESULT.txt:

  sum_a T_a^2=C_Lambda I,
  tau(T_a T_b)=(C_Lambda/g) delta_ab,
  C_Lambda=(||Lambda||^2+2(Lambda,rho))/2.

Set Xi_a=T_a/N. Constants below may depend on this fixed G, but not on
Lambda, N, its active face, or d.

Assume one actual equal-marginal two-output CPTP broadcaster exists. Its
Heisenberg marginal Phi is required to be G-covariant and bistochastic;
the joint extension itself need not be covariant. Equivalently, one may
start with a broadcaster and twirl it only when the promised experiment is
G-conjugation-closed, after first symmetrizing its output order. Write
Phi^sharp for the Schrödinger marginal, S=(Phi+Phi^sharp)/2, and

  b=sup_x (1/2)||Phi^sharp(rho_x)-rho_x||_1.

The family consists of Gibbs states

  rho_x=exp(X_x)/Tr exp(X_x),
  X_x=X_x^*,  ||X_x||_infinity<=h,

where every X_x has a supplied noncommutative polynomial expression in the
Xi_a of degree at most a fixed D>=1 and coefficient l1 norm at most a fixed
A. These bounds are uniform in x and along the carrier sequence. Let

  epsilon_K=exp(2h) h^(K+1)/(K+1)!.

There is one canonical coherent measure-and-prepare channel Psi_Lambda,
independent of x, K, A, D, h, and the actual residual, such that for every
K>=1

  e := sup_x (1/2)||Psi_Lambda(rho_x)-rho_x||_1
       <= min { C_src N^(-1/2),
                C_G N (D K)^(p/2) sqrt(theta b+epsilon_K^2)
                   +epsilon_K/2 },                              (1)

where theta=exp(2h)-1,

  C_src=(1/2)exp(2h) A D R_G^(D-1) sqrt(c_G),
  R_G=max(1,sqrt(M_G)),
  c_G=max_i (omega_i,rho),
  M_G=Omega_G^2/2+Omega_G||rho||,
  Omega_G=max_i||omega_i||,

and one valid root-data constant is

  C_G=sqrt(144 M_G(1+a_G)^p/(g c_0)),
  c_0=min_i(omega_i,rho)>0,
  a_G=max_(alpha>0) sqrt(2)||alpha^vee||/(rho,alpha^vee).

Thus no face-regularity parameter appears. Also D(rho_x||I/d)<=2h, so
chi(E)<=2h. For fixed G,A,D,h, optimizing N and K yields, as b down to 0,

  e=O_(G,A,D,h)( b^(1/6)
       [log(1/b)/loglog(1/b)]^(p/6) ).                         (2)

The claim is for a fixed root system and a covariant actual compatible
marginal, or for a conjugation-closed family where twirling preserves the
uniform residual. It is not uniform over root systems and does not follow
from bounded Holevo capacity alone.

2. Coherent channel and source estimate on every wall

Let v_Lambda be a unit highest vector, P_g=|U(g)v_Lambda><U(g)v_Lambda|,
and define

  Psi(A)=d integral_G Tr(P_g A) P_g dg.

Schur orthogonality gives d integral P_g dg=I. Define the coherent symbol
L(A)(g)=Tr(P_g A). Then Psi=L^dagger L on the normalized-trace Hilbert
space: it is selfadjoint, positive as an L2 operator, and a contraction
with spectrum in [0,1]. It is also a full-domain EB channel.

For every A, including non-Hermitian A, the resolution identity gives

  E_Psi(A):=<A,(I-Psi)A>_tau
     = integral_G [<v_g,A^*A v_g>-|<v_g,A v_g>|^2] dg
     = integral_G ||(A-<v_g,A v_g>)v_g||^2 dg.                 (3)

Thus the Dirichlet energy is exactly mean pure-state variance. This exact
identity is the source approximation mechanism; it does not use the
broadcaster.

For the generator coordinates, covariance rotates the vector
(<v_g,T_a v_g>)_a orthogonally, so its squared norm is constant in g. Under
the displayed physics/half-Casimir normalization, the highest-vector
expectation norm squared is ||Lambda||^2/2. Therefore

  sum_a E_Psi(Xi_a)
    = [sum_a tau(T_a^2)-sum_a <v_Lambda,T_a v_Lambda>^2]/N^2
    = [C_Lambda-||Lambda||^2/2]/N^2
    = (Lambda,rho)/N^2
    <= c_G/N.                                                  (4)

The factor 1/2 in the coherent first-moment norm is essential: with
SU(2), Lambda=n omega and T_a=J_a, the two terms are j(j+1) and j^2,
whose difference is j=(Lambda,rho). Using ||Lambda||^2 instead of
||Lambda||^2/2 would be inconsistent with this Casimir normalization.

The generator scale is uniformly bounded on all carriers. Since each
T_a^2<=sum_b T_b^2=C_Lambda I,

  ||Xi_a||_infinity <= sqrt(C_Lambda)/N <= sqrt(M_G).           (5)

Indeed ||Lambda||<=Omega_G N and
C_Lambda=||Lambda||^2/2+(Lambda,rho)<=M_G N^2 for N>=1. Also
(Lambda,rho)=sum_i n_i(omega_i,rho)<=c_G N. These upper bounds require
no lower bound on any active n_i.

3. Noncommutative polynomial variance bound (Kraus/word Leibniz)

Fix a coherent unit vector psi and write v_a=Var_psi(Xi_a). For a word
W=Xi_(i1)...Xi_(im), let a_t=<psi,Xi_(it)psi>. The exact telescoping
identity is

  W-(product_t a_t)I
    =sum_(t=1)^m Xi_(i1)...Xi_(i,t-1)
       (Xi_(it)-a_t I) (product_(u>t) a_u).

With R_G from (5), |a_t|<=R_G and
||Xi_(i1)...Xi_(i,t-1)||<=R_G^(t-1). Since
sqrt(Var_psi(W))=||(W-<W>)psi|| is the norm after projecting W psi
orthogonally to psi, it is no larger than ||(W-cI)psi|| for any scalar c.
Choosing c=product a_t and applying Cauchy-Schwarz gives

  sqrt(Var_psi(W))
      <= R_G^(m-1) sum_t sqrt(v_(it))
      <= m R_G^(m-1) sqrt(sum_a v_a).                         (6)

For X=sum_w c_w W_w, sum_w|c_w|<=A and word lengths m_w<=D, Minkowski
and (6) yield

  Var_psi(X) <= A^2 D^2 R_G^(2D-2) sum_a Var_psi(Xi_a).

Integrating (3) and using (4),

  E_Psi(X) <= A^2 D^2 R_G^(2D-2) c_G/N.                       (7)

This is an all-wall statement. It uses coefficient l1 control; a PBW degree
bound and operator-norm bound alone do not imply (7) with this argument.
The coefficient promise is on an actual scaled-generator expression and
is not inferred from a Holevo bound.

4. One-channel energy-to-Gibbs trace error

Let X=X^*, ||X||<=h, z=tau(e^X), F=e^X/z=d rho. Then
e^(-h)<=z<=e^h. For each pure coherent vector psi, the spectral measure of
X in psi is an ordinary probability distribution. For f(t)=e^t on [-h,h],
the divided-difference bound

  |(e^s-e^t)/(s-t)|<=e^h

and the pairwise variance identity

  Var_mu(f)=1/2 sum_(u,v) mu_u mu_v |f(u)-f(v)|^2

give Var_psi(e^X)<=e^(2h)Var_psi(X). By (3),

  E_Psi(e^X)<=e^(2h)E_Psi(X),
  E_Psi(F)<=e^(4h)E_Psi(X).                                 (8)

Because Psi is a positive selfadjoint L2 contraction,
(I-Psi)^2<=I-Psi, hence

  ||(I-Psi)F||_(2,tau)^2<=E_Psi(F).

Normalized trace satisfies ||Y||_(1,tau)<=||Y||_(2,tau), and physical
half-trace for rho=F/d is one half of normalized trace norm. Thus, for this
same single coherent EB map,

  T(Psi(rho),rho)
    =1/2 ||(I-Psi)F||_(1,tau)
    <=(1/2)exp(2h)sqrt(E_Psi(X))
    <=C_src N^(-1/2).                                        (9)

This estimate applies simultaneously to all x because A,D,h are uniform.
It does not require covariance or compatibility once the carrier's coherent
channel is defined. Its limits are the supplied coefficient representation
and fixed degree; it is not a generic bound for all bounded-Holevo states.

The Holevo claim uses the supplied tracial reference. Since
log(d rho)=X-log(z)I, D(rho||I/d)=tau(FX)-log z<=h+h=2h. For any prior,
chi=sum_x p_x D(rho_x||I/d)-D(rho_bar||I/d)<=2h.

5. Rebuild of the all-carrier actual-compatible floor

This section uses only actual compatibility and group covariance, not the
coherent kernel's face overlap. Let W be a nontrivial real irreducible
Hermitian conjugation type in End(V_Lambda), of real dimension s=r_W and
Casimir c_W. Choose a tau-orthonormal Hermitian basis B_1,...,B_s of a
copy with sum_i B_i^2=sI. This scalar identity follows because the sum is
G-invariant and the carrier is irreducible; its normalized trace is s.

For the actual equal-marginal Heisenberg joint UCP map J, take a Stinespring
representation J(Z)=V^*pi(Z)V and P=VV^*. Put

  F_i=pi(B_i tensor I),  G_i=pi(I tensor B_i),
  M_i=V^*F_iV=V^*G_iV=Phi(B_i),
  L_i=(I-P)F_iV,  R_i=(I-P)G_iV,
  delta=s-sum_i tau(M_i^2),
  h_B=sum_i||B_i-M_i||_(2,tau)^2,
  eps=1-(1/s)sum_i tau(B_i Phi(B_i)).

Kadison-Schwarz plus bistochasticity gives delta>=0; direct expansion gives
h_B=2s eps-delta, so 0<=delta,h_B<=2s eps. Since the two output
algebras commute,

  [M_i,M_j]=-L_i^*R_j+R_j^*L_i.

The Stinespring leakages obey
sum_i L_i L_i^*<=s(I-P), sum_i R_i R_i^*<=s(I-P), and
sum_i||L_i||_2^2=sum_i||R_i||_2^2=delta. Cauchy-Schwarz in the direct
sum over i,j yields

  (sum_ij||[M_i,M_j]||_2^2)^(1/2)<=2 sqrt(s delta).

Writing C_i=B_i-M_i, the same frame identity and
sum_i M_i^2<=sI give

  (sum_ij||[C_i,B_j]||_2^2)^(1/2)<=2 sqrt(s h_B),
  (sum_ij||[M_i,C_j]||_2^2)^(1/2)<=2 sqrt(s h_B).

Minkowski and delta,h_B<=2s eps imply

  (sum_ij||[B_i,B_j]||_2^2)^(1/2)
    <=(sqrt(8)+sqrt(32))s sqrt(eps),
  q(B):=(1/(2s^2))sum_ij||[B_i,B_j]||_2^2<=36 eps.           (10)

For physics generators, put kappa=C_Lambda/g and Q_a=T_a/sqrt(kappa), an
orthonormal Hermitian frame. On a real irreducible W-copy, write

  [T_a,B_i]=i sum_j (L_a)_(ji) B_j,
  -sum_a L_a^2=c_W I_s.

Cyclicity and Bessel projection of each [B_i,B_j] onto span{Q_a} give

  q(B)>= g c_W/(2s C_Lambda).                                (11)

The constants can be checked directly: the sum of squared projected
coefficients is kappa^(-1)sum_a||L_a||_F^2. Since the L_a are real skew
and -sum_a L_a^2=c_W I_s, this is
kappa^(-1)s c_W=g s c_W/C_Lambda; dividing by 2s^2 gives
g c_W/(2s C_Lambda).

Now S=(Phi+Phi^sharp)/2 is selfadjoint and G-covariant. Diagonalize S on
the multiplicity space and choose an irreducible real copy on which it is
scalar lambda. The anti-selfadjoint part Phi-S has zero quadratic form on
Hermitian B_i, so eps=1-lambda in (10). Combining (10)-(11) proves, on
every full W-isotypic block, including all multiplicities,

  I-S >= [g c_W/(72 r_W C_Lambda)] I.                       (12)

This floor is carrier-uniform in its explicit C_Lambda dependence and does
not use n_*, gamma_(G,I), coherent-symbol injectivity, or face regularity.
It does use a genuine common-marginal UCP extension. Compatibility is not
inferred for S; the compatible map used in (10) is Phi itself.

PBW support for degree <=k lies in adjoint tensor powers <=k. Thus every
nontrivial highest weight nu in the support is dominant and satisfies
||nu||<=sqrt(2)k. Weyl's dimension formula gives

  dim_C(V_nu)<=product_(alpha>0)
       (1+k sqrt(2)||alpha^vee||/(rho,alpha^vee))
       <=(1+a_G k)^p.

The underlying real Hermitian type obeys r_W<=2 dim_C(V_nu). Also
c_W=(||nu||^2+2(nu,rho))/2 >=(nu,rho)>=c_0, since nu is nonzero dominant.
Using C_Lambda<=M_G N^2 in (12), for every nontrivial type in PBW degree
<=k,

  1-S >= gamma_G/(N^2 k^p),
  gamma_G=g c_0/[144 M_G(1+a_G)^p].                           (13)

The trivial type is scalar and fixed by S. Therefore for every Hermitian
p in the PBW image of degree <=k,

  E_S(p)>=gamma_G/(N^2 k^p) ||p-tau(p)I||_(2,tau)^2.          (14)

This is the all-carrier PBW floor needed below. The division-algebra
multiplicity issue does not change (12): a selfadjoint equivariant map
diagonalizes into invariant real irreducible copies (with the usual real,
complex, or quaternionic multiplicity structure), and the floor is scalar
on each such copy.

6. Compatibility transfer for a Gibbs Taylor polynomial

Let F_x=d rho_x=e^(X_x)/z_x and

  p_(x,K)=z_x^(-1) sum_(j=0)^K X_x^j/j!,
  T_(x,K)=F_x-p_(x,K).

The operator Taylor remainder and z_x>=e^-h imply

  ||T_(x,K)||_(2,tau)<=||T_(x,K)||_infinity
       <=epsilon_K=exp(2h)h^(K+1)/(K+1)! .                    (15)

The scalar component of p is allowed; its PBW degree is at most k=DK.
The likelihood obeys ||F_x-I||_infinity<=theta=exp(2h)-1. By S(I)=I,
the physical residual and trace duality give

  E_S(F_x)=E_S(F_x-I)
    =tau[(F_x-I)(F_x-Phi^sharp(F_x))]
    <=2 theta b.                                               (16)

Since 0<=I-S<=2I,

  E_S(p_(x,K))
    <=2E_S(F_x)+2E_S(T_(x,K))
    <=4(theta b+epsilon_K^2).                                  (17)

Apply (14) with k=DK. Psi is a positive L2 contraction, so
||I-Psi||_(2->2)<=1 and Psi fixes scalars. Equations (14),(17) yield

  ||(I-Psi)p_(x,K)||_(2,tau)
      <=2 gamma_G^(-1/2) N(DK)^(p/2)
          sqrt(theta b+epsilon_K^2).

Finally, (15), trace-norm/L2 monotonicity, and the physical half-trace
factor give

  (1/2)||Psi(rho_x)-rho_x||_1
      = (1/2)||(I-Psi)F_x||_(1,tau)
      <=gamma_G^(-1/2)N(DK)^(p/2)
          sqrt(theta b+epsilon_K^2)+epsilon_K/2.               (18)

This is the second branch of (1), with C_G=gamma_G^(-1/2). It is the same
Psi_Lambda as in (9). The compatibility floor uses actual Phi, while the
energy E_S uses its selfadjoint part; no compatible extension of S is
claimed. No active-face overlap bound enters (18).

7. Optimization and quantifiers

For fixed h>0 and b sufficiently small, let K_b be the least K>=1 with
epsilon_K<=sqrt(b). Stirling gives

  K_b=O_h(log(1/b)/loglog(1/b)).

Set L_b=D K_b. The compatibility branch (18) is at most
c_1 N L_b^(p/2)sqrt(b), where c_1 depends only on the fixed G,D,h; the
epsilon term is absorbed because L_b>=1 and sqrt(b)<=L_b^(p/2)sqrt(b).
The source branch is c_0' N^(-1/2), with c_0'=C_src. For all N>=1,

  min{c_0' N^(-1/2), c_1 N L_b^(p/2)sqrt(b)}
    <=(c_0')^(2/3) c_1^(1/3)
       b^(1/6)L_b^(p/6).                                     (19)

Indeed split at N_*=(c_0'/(c_1 L_b^(p/2)sqrt(b)))^(2/3); below it use
the second term and above it the first. Substitution of the K_b estimate
proves (2), uniformly over all dominant carriers, including walls. If
h=0 all states are maximally mixed and e=0. If b=0, take K->infinity in
(18) at each fixed carrier; the asymptotic statement is then interpreted
by this direct zero-residual limit, not by the finite K_b formula.

The optimization is an upper-bound envelope over carrier size N; it does
not change a given carrier. For each actual N, both branches hold and one
uses their minimum. Taking the supremum over N of that minimum gives (19).

8. Audit verdict and limits

The proposed chain of implications is valid under the explicit interface
above, with these corrections made visible:

* the coherent highest-vector first-moment square is ||Lambda||^2/2,
  not ||Lambda||^2, in the half-Casimir physics convention;
* the polynomial source energy carries the factor
  R_G^(2D-2), and the coefficient l1 bound A is an independent promise;
* physical half trace introduces the factors 1/2 in (9),(18); the
  compatibility branch has +epsilon_K/2 (the looser +epsilon_K is safe);
* the block floor is an all-carrier result from the actual Stinespring
  extension, whereas the face-regular theorem's coherent-defect estimate
  is not used here;
* covariance of the marginal, or conjugation closure before twirling, is
  essential. An arbitrary noncovariant broadcaster on a non-closed family
  does not inherit (12).

The resulting route is a real positive structured theorem with a weaker
rate and added coefficient control. It does not show that bounded Holevo
capacity supplies a polynomial expression, bounded degree, bounded
coefficients, covariance, or an actual broadcaster. Without these promises
the unrestricted bounded-Holevo classicalization question remains
UNKNOWN. No external correctness, novelty, or priority claim is made.
