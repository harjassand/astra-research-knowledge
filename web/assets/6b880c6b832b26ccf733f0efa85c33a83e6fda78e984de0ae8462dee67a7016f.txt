1. EXACT ORDERED CUT-RANK MPS, ALL RATIONAL ONSITE FILTERS

For explicit F over Q(i) and a supplied site order, let
 c_p=rank F[prefix,suffix]+rank F[suffix,prefix], D=2^(max c_p).
The program exactly acquires an MPS with those cut dimensions, using
rational column bases and invertible pivot-amplitude evaluations. It needs
no SVD threshold, supplied count, state-vector enumeration, or approximation.
It computes all canonical/prefix norm coefficients with arbitrary rational
onsite squared t_i>=0 and arbitrary local masks among {0,u,d,ud}. Sequential
exact rational Born sampling has expected polynomial-bit work for D polynomial.
Thus c=O(log n) gives an exact polynomial-bit theorem, including dense,
nonbipartite, full-rank quasiseparable and low-rank matrices.

Conservative arithmetic: acquisition O(n^4+nD^3(c+2)^2), all-sector suffix
Gram tables O(n^2D^3), storage O(n^2D^2), and sample after cached tables
O(nD^2). The reference sample() recomputes its suffix tables per call, so
that O(n^2D^3) cost must be charged to the executable. A favorable different
order is not acquired for free. Exact operand heights are polynomial in
n,D,L; singular or tiny positive norms need no inverse-magnitude operation
count. Hard zeros are handled exactly.

The dense full-rank family F_ij=1 for i<j, 2 for i>j, 0 diagonal has c=2,
det F=(-1)^(n-1)(2^n-2), and c_k=5 binomial(n,2k) for k>=1, c_0=1.
The two surviving alternating color orientations have squared determinants
1 and 4 on each occupied 2k-set. This is a complete all-size derivation,
not a new general parity theorem.

4. POST-EXPOSURE LOW-k GENERAL-F PIPELINE AND STRONGER COST

The origins are c03_l10 (random-sign Pfaffian estimator), c03_l06 (positive
forced-row projection and exact conditional column volumes), c03_s02
(central-binomial moment bound and a distinct physical-prefix implementation),
and c03_s03 (independent implementation and Hellinger cost refinement).

For every explicit rational-complex F, hard k<=n/2, C_k=binomial(2k,k), the
unbiased sample obeys 0<=X<=C_k c_k. A formal skew-block Pfaffian identity
proves that X is the coefficient of sqrt(det(I+tA* A)); exact Newton traces
compute it in O((k+1)n^3) arithmetic, without principal-minor enumeration,
numerical normal forms, or hypercontractivity. Forced-row acquisition uses
an exact Gram projection. The sharp moment proof wedges it back into original
coordinates: projecting identity columns does not license replacing k by the
smaller residual r in binomial(2r,r).

The resulting relative count needs O(C_k eta^-2 log(1/delta)) sign evaluations.
The full joint sampler first chooses rows by estimated prefix masses, then
columns by exact volume completion coefficients. Dyadic branch thresholds
give bounded random-bit decisions. A sequential Hellinger-affinity argument
permits eta=epsilon/(16ceil(sqrt n)) instead of epsilon/n. With per-call
failure epsilon/(8n) and grid >=8n/epsilon, total joint TV<=3epsilon/4.
Its uniform count is

 O(C_k n^2 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon)) sign evaluations,
 O(C_k (k+1)n^5 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon)) arithmetic operations,

plus polynomial acquisition/column work and polynomial bit heights. This
saves one polynomial n factor over the l06 sequential-TV sign count.
For k=O(log n) it is polynomial-bit, with no inverse sector/projection norm.
The sampler has the positive-sector promise c_k>0. A randomized zero count
is not an exact zero certificate; no external parity-support solver is hidden
in the executable. Soft t filters and unrestricted high k are outside scope.
