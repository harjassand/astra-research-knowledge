# Mathematics and algorithms lane: final report

## Verdict

This lane reconstructed several substantial OpenAI/math release mechanisms and derived scoped algorithmic consequences, but it did not clear a genuinely new theorem or independently validate the pinned release proofs. The most actionable new work product is an exact, polynomial-bit finite-data implementation of the family-332 expected-median witness for rational (L_1^m) inputs. The underlying median formula already appears in that source, so the implementation is a reusable capability candidate rather than a novelty claim.

The strongest mathematical line was the family-332 metric Markov cotype proof for real sequence ℓ₁ with (N_2\le12\sqrt{21}). The cut representation, quartic smoothing, stopped-walk energy identity, and 108-by-28 endpoint bound were reconstructed and independently source-audited. However, Assaf Naor's arXiv v2 *De-Höldering factorization* added-in-proof explicitly reports forthcoming Mendel–Naor work proving metric Markov cotype 2 for Lebesgue (L_1[0,1]) and (e(L_q;L_1)\lesssim\sqrt q). Thus the earlier open-question sentence in that paper is superseded. The cited [MN26] full manuscript was not located; this is author-source-reported prior art, not proof-level external validation. The ambient-(L_1) transfer and Markov-type-source extension are therefore preserved as conditional derivations, with no novelty claim.

## Strongest concrete consequence

For a supplied rational stochastic matrix (A), integer (t\ge1), and rational vectors (x_1,\dots,x_n\in\mathbb Q^m), the exact endpoint law is (G=((t+1)I-tA)^{-1}). The rounded vector is
\[
y_i=\mathbb E\,\mathrm{med}(x_{J_1},x_{J_2},x_{J_3}),\qquad J_a\overset{ind}{\sim}G_{i,\cdot}.
\]
Sorted feature thresholds evaluate this in (O(n^3+mn^2+mn\log n)) rational operations and polynomial bit complexity, avoiding the exponential all-cut enumeration or an (n^3)-tuple average. Exact differential fixtures and the finite cotype inequality pass. See [FINITE_MEDIAN_CONSTRUCTOR.md](FINITE_MEDIAN_CONSTRUCTOR.md) and [finite_l1_median_rounding.py](finite_l1_median_rounding.py). The universal cotype inequality remains conditional on the release proof.
