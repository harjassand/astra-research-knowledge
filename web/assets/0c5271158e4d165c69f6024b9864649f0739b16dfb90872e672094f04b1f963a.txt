1. Correction to v1 §3

For independent Bernoulli mode-coupling probabilities q_1,…,q_K, the count probability is not e_k(q). The exact probability generating function and outcome law are

    G(z) = E[z^Y] = Π_j (1−q_j+q_j z)
    P(Y=r) = [z^r]G(z)
             = Σ_{S⊆{1,…,K}, |S|=r} Π_{j∈S}q_j Π_{j∉S}(1−q_j).

The elementary symmetric polynomial e_k(q) is instead the coefficient of u^k in G(1+u):

    G(1+u)=Π_j(1+q_j u)=Σ_{k=0}^K e_k(q)u^k,
    e_k(q)=E[binom(Y,k)]=Σ_{r=k}^K binom(r,k)P(Y=r).

Thus the complete PNR law yields e_1,…,e_K via factorial moments. Newton identities then yield the power sums p_ℓ=Σ_j q_j^ℓ: for 1≤ℓ≤K,

    p_ℓ−e_1p_{ℓ−1}+e_2p_{ℓ−2}−⋯+(-1)^{ℓ−1}e_{ℓ−1}p_1+(-1)^ℓ ℓe_ℓ=0,

and for ℓ>K,

    p_ℓ−e_1p_{ℓ−1}+e_2p_{ℓ−2}−⋯+(-1)^K e_Kp_{ℓ−K}=0.

The population factorial-cumulant identity in v1 remains valid:

    [d^ℓ/du^ℓ log G(1+u)]_{u=0}=(-1)^{ℓ−1}(ℓ−1)!p_ℓ.

The earlier inversion statement was false as written; any empirical use must also handle the variance/conditioning of factorial-moment and Newton-identity estimates. If K is unknown and some source has q_j(t)=0 (or an emitter has zero rate), the count law at that setting cannot reveal its presence.

2. Candidate-gap update from primary prior art

The core finite-moment/no-separation direction is already substantially addressed outside optics. Fan and Li, “Efficient Algorithms for Sparse Moment Problems without Separation,” arXiv:2207.13008v2 (2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.13008, Theorem 2.1, recover an arbitrary k-spike probability measure on [0,1] to transportation error O(ε) from noisy moments 0,…,2k−1 of accuracy (ε/k)^{O(k)}, in O(k²) arithmetic operations, without a separation or minimum-weight assumption. This sharply weakens the candidate novelty described in v1 §7.

There is an exact measurement-to-moment reduction worth recording, but it is not a new result. With v1’s Gaussian HG0 setting t_r=rΔ, let s_r=E[Y_{t_r}]. Then

    η^{-1} exp(r²Δ²/(2σ²)) s_r
      = Σ_j μ_j exp(−x_j²/(2σ²)) (exp(Δx_j/σ²))^r.

These are ordinary moments of a positive atomic measure in z_j=exp(Δx_j/σ²), with weights b_j=μ_j exp(−x_j²/(2σ²)). This gives 2K_max moments from 2K_max scan settings in the ideal calibrated model. Turning finite-frame counts into these noisy moments incurs setting-dependent concentration and calibration costs; an operational bridge to Fan–Li is a statistical corollary candidate, not presently an optical superresolution advance. No resource-matched comparison or new accuracy theorem is established here.

3. Updated status

The corrected PNR identity is source-compatible algebra, not a breakthrough. BLESS and QSIPS already close the initially tempting two-source/high-order-statistics gaps, and Fan–Li closes much of the proposed generic positive-mixture/no-separation inversion gap. The remaining optical measurement/statistics bridge may be useful, but it does not yet cross a matched operational limit. No claim of discovery, novelty, or validated capability is made.
