   Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 369(4) (2017), 2285–2310,
   DOI 10.1090/tran/6706.

   * Proposition 2.8, lines 146–153: rank ultraproducts are stably finite,
     by rank(I-AB)=rank(I-BA), also at fixed matrix amplifications.
   * Theorem 7.4, lines 454–456 and proof beginning lines 457–480: an injective
     rank-ultraproduct group morphism over one fixed field gives an injective

     over a sequence of finite fields, with no prescribed characteristic.

     coordinate-dependent prime p_k.

     field and notes a positive answer would settle Kaplansky direct
     finiteness for linear-sofic groups. This is the paper's 2012 question;
     this cycle makes no claim about its present global status.
