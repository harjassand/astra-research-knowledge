MAIN RESULT
Let n>=1, D=2^n, and let Phi_j:M_2->M_2 be unital, trace-preserving,
completely positive, Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint channels. Assume each
comes with a legal two-output broadcaster B_j with both marginals Phi_j.
For Phi=Phi_1 tensor ... tensor Phi_n there is ONE canonical pure-ensemble
EB channel Psi, independent of any requested observable, satisfying

  I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi)                                            (T1)

as quadratic forms on the ENTIRE Hermitian Hilbert-Schmidt space of M_D.
Local Phi_j may have negative Hilbert-Schmidt eigenvalues. Thus for every
finite traceless Hermitian frame A_i, with tau_D=Tr/D,

  V-k <= V-s_Psi <= 2(V-s_Phi).                                (T2)

Here k is the maximum over ALL pure ensembles with barycenter I/D,
including entangled ensembles. No product-state upper bound for k is
used. The constant two is sharp within this channel family.

The class can additionally be closed under finite convex mixtures,
global unitary encodings, and tracial bistochastic sandwiches
F^dagger Phi F. In particular it includes trace-preserving conditional
compressions onto any unital finite-dimensional subalgebra of M_D.
These representation assumptions are part of the theorem; no claim is
made that every legal self-compatible channel has such a representation.

1. EXACT HILBERT-SPACE CONVENTIONS

Write H_d for the REAL Hilbert space of all d-by-d Hermitian matrices,
with inner product <X,Y>_tau=Tr(XY)/d. In this note I denotes either the
identity matrix or the identity operator on H_d, as indicated by context.
A superoperator inequality is a quadratic-form/Loewner inequality on H_d;
it is not an inequality between positive matrices on the physical system.

Every bistochastic CP channel is a contraction on H_d. Indeed, the
operator Schwarz inequality gives Phi(X)^2<=Phi(X^2), and taking the
preserved trace proves ||Phi(X)||_tau<=||X||_tau. If Phi is self-adjoint,
its Hilbert-Schmidt spectrum lies in [-1,1]. Hilbert-Schmidt positivity is
an additional premise and is distinct from positivity as a map.

A pure ensemble {p_y,pi_y}, sum_y p_y pi_y=I/d, defines

  Psi(X)=d sum_y p_y Tr(pi_y X) pi_y.                            (C1)

It is CP, TP, unital, EB, self-adjoint, and positive semidefinite as a
Hilbert-Schmidt operator. Its quadratic form is

  <X,Psi(X)>_tau=sum_y p_y [Tr(pi_y X)]^2.                       (C2)

It is therefore also a positive contraction on H_d. Tensor products of
such maps are again canonical: take the product probabilities and pure
product states. This is an admissible ensemble, irrespective of whether
it maximizes the unrestricted support k.

2. OPERATOR TENSOR BERNOULLI LEMMA

For every finite n, every C>=1, and positive contractions X_j,Y_j on
arbitrary finite-dimensional real or complex Hilbert spaces, assume

  I-Y_j <= C(I-X_j)       for every j.                          (B1)

Then

  I-(tensor_j Y_j) <= C[I-(tensor_j X_j)].                     (B2)

No commutation between X_j and Y_j is assumed.

Proof. Put p=1/C and M_j=p Y_j+(1-p)I. Equation (B1) says X_j<=M_j.
All X_j and M_j are positive, so tensor order gives tensor X_j<=tensor M_j.
For two positive contractions A,B the exact identity

  p(A tensor B)+(1-p)I
  -[pA+(1-p)I] tensor [pB+(1-p)I]
    =p(1-p)(I-A) tensor (I-B) >=0                              (B3)

proves the required Bernoulli inequality. Induct using that a tensor
product of positive contractions is a positive contraction. This gives

  tensor M_j <= p(tensor Y_j)+(1-p)I.

Together with tensor X_j<=tensor M_j, this rearranges to (B2). QED.

If factors have constants C_j>=1, use C=max_j C_j, because I-X_j>=0.
The maximum constant tensorizes without dependence on n, local dimension,
frame rank, frame entanglement, or the number of scales in the frame.

C>=1 is necessary for this general lemma: scalar X=1/2,Y=3/4 satisfy
I-Y=(1/2)(I-X), but their twofold tensor ratio is
(1-9/16)/(1-1/4)=7/12>1/2.

For channels, identify tensor_j H_{d_j} with H_{product d_j}. This is the
FULL Hermitian space, including sums of product matrices. Hence the
lemma proves an all-observable comparison; it does not restrict X to a
product observable.

3. WHY SIGNED LOCAL COMPARATORS CANNOT BE TENSORED DIRECTLY

Let D_Z be dephasing in the computational basis and let

  Phi(X)=sigma_x D_Z(X) sigma_x.

Its Pauli eigenvalues are (lambda_x,lambda_y,lambda_z)=(0,0,-1).
An explicit broadcaster measures z and prepares |1-z> on BOTH outputs.
Thus Phi is CP, TP, unital, EB, self-adjoint, and self-compatible.

The canonical channel Psi=D_X (dephasing in the x basis) satisfies
I-Psi<=I-Phi on the full local space, with C=1. However Phi tensor Phi
fixes Z tensor Z, while Psi tensor Psi sends it to zero. On this observable
the tensor comparator has loss one and the broadcast marginal has loss
zero. Thus a direct signed version of (B2) is FALSE, even for legal EB
channels and canonical local comparators. This does not falsify (T2):
the global computational-basis dephasing is an exact C=1 comparator.

4. QUBIT ABSOLUTE-VALUE CHANNEL IS LEGAL

For a unital, TP, self-adjoint qubit channel write its Bloch matrix T.
Self-adjointness says T=T^T. Diagonalize it by a proper orthogonal rotation
(change an eigenvector sign if necessary), which is implemented by the
same qubit unitary before and after the channel. Thus in a suitable Pauli
basis Phi has eigenvalues 1,lambda_1,lambda_2,lambda_3.

Define |Phi| as the Hilbert-Schmidt absolute value, with eigenvalues
1,|lambda_1|,|lambda_2|,|lambda_3|. In dimension two this is again a legal
unital TP self-adjoint self-compatible channel:

(a) If lambda_1 lambda_2 lambda_3>0, there are zero or two negative
eigenvalues. A Pauli output unitary flips precisely the two required
signs, giving |Phi|. Apply that unitary to BOTH outputs of the supplied
broadcaster. If the determinant is zero, a sign on a zero eigendirection
can be chosen to make the sign rotation proper; the same construction
works. The resulting marginal remains self-adjoint because it is diagonal
with the nonnegative eigenvalues in this basis.

(b) If the determinant is negative, even output sign flips reduce the
Pauli parameters to (a,b,-c), with a,b,c>0. Complete positivity implies
1-c>=a+b. Therefore a+b+c<=1. Independently reconstructing this condition,
the normalized Choi matrix is

  J(Phi)=[I tensor I + sum_i lambda_i sigma_i^T tensor sigma_i]/4,

whose Bell eigenvalues are the four Pauli probabilities
(1+lambda_1+lambda_2+lambda_3)/4,
(1+lambda_1-lambda_2-lambda_3)/4,
(1-lambda_1+lambda_2-lambda_3)/4,
(1-lambda_1-lambda_2+lambda_3)/4.
For (a,b,-c), nonnegativity of the last probability gives a+b+c<=1.

Consequently |Phi|=aD_X+bD_Y+cD_Z+(1-a-b-c)D_0, where D_0(X)=Tr(X)I/2.
Each term is EB and has an explicit measure-and-prepare broadcaster.
The depolarizing term can prepare I/2 tensor I/2 independently of input.
Thus the mixture is a legal broadcaster for |Phi|. Undo the diagonalizing
unitary to finish the construction.

This is the familiar qubit CP tetrahedron/EB octahedron mechanism,
reconstructed here rather than assumed from a numerical separability
test. Primary sources: Ruskai-Szarek-Werner quant-ph/0101003v2, Eq. (12)
and the unital tetrahedron; Ruskai quant-ph/0302032v3, Eq. (5), Theorem 4.
No sign-removal claim is made for local dimension greater than two.

5. LOCAL CANONICAL C2 COMPARATOR, WITH PURE ENSEMBLE ACCOUNTING

Let a_i=|lambda_i|, and use the legal broadcaster for Delta=|Phi|.
For S subset {1,2,3}, let

  W_S=sum_{i in S} sigma_i^T tensor(sigma_i^B+sigma_i^C).

Its expectation in R=(Id tensor B_Delta)(|Omega><Omega|) is
2 sum_{i in S} a_i. This is a state expectation of ONE supplied joint CP
map. It is not a marginal POVM-compatibility assertion.

The Pauli stars have exact maximum eigenvalues

  |S|=1: 2;       |S|=2: 2 sqrt(2);       |S|=3: 4.             (P1)

For completeness: conjugating the reference by sigma_y turns
sigma_i^T into -sigma_i. For one axis the spectrum is immediate. For two
axes rotate to x,y; the operator is minus twice the excitation-conserving
three-vertex star adjacency matrix in each of the one- and two-excitation
sectors, giving eigenvalues +-2sqrt(2),0. For all three axes it is
-4 S_A dot(S_B+S_C). The BC singlet gives zero. In the BC spin-one sector,
total spin 1/2 gives four and total spin 3/2 gives minus two. Thus (P1)
follows without constraints on the state beyond positivity and trace one.

Let b_i=max(0,2a_i-1). Choose S to be the active indices. Equation (P1)
gives sum_i b_i<=1: for one active index use a_i<=1; for two use
sum a_i<=sqrt(2), hence sum b_i<=2sqrt(2)-2<1; for three use sum a_i<=2.
The empty active set gives zero.

IMPORTANT: the positive part b need not itself be a PURE canonical
covariance. For a full qubit pure-ensemble covariance its trace must equal
one. Fill the unused trace explicitly:

  r=1-sum_i b_i,     mu_i=b_i+r/3.                             (P2)

Now mu_i>=0, sum_i mu_i=1, and mu_i>=2a_i-1. Let pi_{i,+/-} be the two
pure Pauli-axis eigenstates and p_{i,+/-}=mu_i/2. They have barycenter I/2.
Their canonical EB map is Psi=sum_i mu_i D_i, and it satisfies

  I-Psi <= 2(I-|Phi|).                                        (P3)

All Pauli eigendirections and the identity direction have been checked.
This yields one full-domain comparator. It is not a separate measurement
chosen for each frame direction.

For an EB qubit channel, sum_i a_i<=1. An even stronger construction
mu_i=a_i+(1-sum a_i)/3 gives Psi>=|Phi|, hence local C=1 against |Phi|.

6. PROOF OF THE MAIN THEOREM, INCLUDING SIGN CANCELLATION

Apply the tensor Bernoulli lemma with X_j=|Phi_j| and Y_j=Psi_j from (P3):

  I-Psi_product <= 2[I-(tensor_j |Phi_j|)].                    (T3)

The tensor absolute value equals |Phi_product|, because the factors are
self-adjoint and have product eigenbases. Therefore

  tensor_j |Phi_j|=|Phi_product| >= Phi_product,

and (T3) implies (T1). This handles all sign cancellations, including the
exact ZZ obstruction from Section 3.

The product broadcaster is the tensor product of B_j, followed by the
register permutation collecting all first outputs and all second outputs.
Both marginals are exactly Phi_product. Every channel premise is retained.

For every frame, the explicit canonical product ensemble supplies
s_Psi<=k. Taking the trace of Q against (T1) proves (T2). This concludes
the theorem on the entire global Hermitian space, including arbitrary
cross terms between Pauli strings. No support computation is needed.

Sharpness. For the single-qubit channel Delta(X)=(2/3)X+Tr(X)I/6, the
legal broadcaster is

  B(X)=(2/3)P_sym(X tensor I)P_sym,
  P_sym=(I+Swap)/2.                                           (T4)

It is CP; Tr[P_sym(X tensor I)]=(3/2)Tr X proves TP; symmetry gives equal
marginals. Expanding P_sym gives each marginal Delta. On the frame X,Y,Z,
V=3, s_Delta=2, and k=1, because every pure qubit has Bloch norm one.
The ratio (V-k)/(V-s)=2. The same witness embeds into any n by tensoring
the observables with identities and choosing arbitrary legal remaining
local channels. Thus two cannot be reduced for this family.
The cloner (T4) is Werner's 1->2 construction, quant-ph/9804001v1,
Eq. (3.3); its marginal factor is Eq. (3.8).

7. BISTOCHASTIC SANDWICH CLOSURE

Suppose Phi and a canonical comparator Psi already satisfy
I-Psi<=C(I-Phi), C>=1, on M_D. Let F:M_D->M_D be ANY supplied bistochastic
CP channel, with Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint F^dagger. Its adjoint is also CP,
TP, and unital. Put

  Phi_tilde=F^dagger Phi F,       Psi_tilde=F^dagger Psi F.      (S1)

Phi_tilde is CP, TP, unital and self-adjoint. A legal broadcaster is

  B_tilde=(F^dagger tensor F^dagger) B F.                       (S2)

Psi_tilde is EB. Schwarz gives F^dagger F<=I on Hilbert-Schmidt space.
The exact identities

  I-Psi_tilde=(I-F^dagger F)+F^dagger(I-Psi)F,
  I-Phi_tilde=(I-F^dagger F)+F^dagger(I-Phi)F                  (S3)

therefore prove I-Psi_tilde<=C(I-Phi_tilde). No multiplicative cost in C
is incurred, even after finitely many such sandwiches.

Canonical accounting is exact. If Psi has rank-one POVM effects
M_y=D p_y pi_y, write N_y=F^dagger(M_y). Since F and F^dagger preserve
identity and trace, sum_y N_y=I and tau(N_y)=tau(M_y). Then

  Psi_tilde(X)=sum_y tau(N_y X) N_y/tau(N_y).                   (S4)

This is one EB channel from one POVM, although N_y may have rank>1.
Refine spectrally N_y=sum_l n_{y,l} pi_{y,l}; the probabilities
p_{y,l}=n_{y,l}/D have barycenter I/D. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives

  sum_y tau(N_y X)^2/tau(N_y)
     <= (1/D)sum_{y,l} n_{y,l}[Tr(pi_{y,l}X)]^2.               (S5)

Thus the pure canonical channel of the refined ensemble dominates
Psi_tilde as a Hilbert-Schmidt quadratic form. Replacing Psi_tilde by this
pure canonical map preserves the comparison and proves the unrestricted
k inequality. Refinement is an existence/construction step with charged
spectral costs, not a claim that mixed effects were already pure.

8. CONDITIONAL EXPECTATIONS AND THE TRACIAL REFERENCE

Let A be any unital C*-subalgebra of M_D, and E_A the tau_D-preserving CP
conditional expectation. It is an orthogonal Hilbert-Schmidt projection
and is bistochastic, so Section 7 applies directly to E_A Phi E_A.
For X in A, the comparator and channel quadratic forms are exactly the
original forms on X; on the perpendicular space both compressed maps
vanish. Equivalently,

  loss(E_A Psi E_A;X)=||(I-E_A)X||_tau^2+loss(Psi;E_A X).

The same identity holds for Phi and proves the same C>=1 on the FULL
ambient domain, not only on A. The broadcaster is
(E_A tensor E_A) B E_A and its marginals are E_A Phi E_A.

If A=direct_sum_r(M_{d_r} tensor I_{m_r}), D=sum_r d_r m_r, then the
conditional expectation is

  E_A(X)=direct_sum_r [Tr_mult(P_r X P_r)/m_r] tensor I_{m_r}.

The restricted normalized trace has central weights d_r m_r/D. Those
weights are retained; replacing them by an unweighted abstract-block
trace silently changes the reference and is not justified by this proof.

Rectangular isometries do not automatically give this transfer. For the
code V:C^2->C^4 with images |00>,|11>, compressing two 2/3 clone marginals
on input |00><00| retains trace (5/6)^2+(1/6)^2=13/18. Hence
V^dagger Phi_product(V X V^dagger)V is not TP (and is not unital on the
code). Postselection or renormalization changes the channel and the joint
broadcast constraints; no theorem here treats that repair as free.

9. MIXTURES, PAULI GRAPHS, AND A CONCRETE MANY-BODY FAMILY

If each Phi_r has a common-C comparator Psi_r, then
Phi=sum_r q_r Phi_r and Psi=sum_r q_r Psi_r satisfy the same comparison.
Broadcast the random branch with the supplied B_r. A convex combination
of canonical ensembles still has barycenter I/D. Global unitary
conjugation Ad_U Phi Ad_{U^dagger} likewise preserves the comparison and
canonical ensemble, transforming each pure state to U pi U^dagger.

For any simple graph G on n qubits let U_G be the product of CZ gates on
its edges. With Delta the 2/3 clone marginal define

  Phi_G=(Delta^tensor_n + Ad_U_G Delta^tensor_n Ad_U_G^dagger)/2. (G1)

This is a legal unital TP self-adjoint self-compatible channel. Its
supplied broadcaster randomly chooses the unencoded or graph-encoded
product broadcaster. It generally couples the Pauli weights through the
graph and is not a plain product channel in the displayed basis.

For a Pauli string P, let w(P) count its nonidentity factors. Because U_G
is Clifford, the exact marginal eigenvalue is

  lambda_G(P)=[(2/3)^w(P)+(2/3)^w(U_G^dagger P U_G)]/2.         (G2)

The explicit canonical comparator substitutes 1/3 for 2/3 in (G2), since
the local cloner comparator has mu=(1/3,1/3,1/3). Its ensemble is the
mixture of product six-axis pure states and their graph-unitary images;
those images can be entangled. The all-frame C2 comparison follows by
tensorization and convexity, including arbitrarily weighted multispace
Pauli frames. Conditional expectations or arbitrary bistochastic
sandwiches can be added while preserving the bound.

For a DIAGONAL weighted Pauli frame, k=max_pure sum_P w_P<psi|P|psi>^2
exactly. To prove this, twirl any pure optimizer by the full Pauli group.
The average state is I/D and every squared Pauli expectation is invariant
under the twirl. Thus the barycenter constraint is free in this specific
Pauli-diagonal support problem, and entangled pure states remain allowed.
This equivalence is not asserted for arbitrary offdiagonal Q.

Entangled-support falsifier for a bad product-state reduction: on two
qubits take the nine observables sigma_a tensor sigma_b, a,b in {x,y,z}.
Every pure product state scores one. The unrestricted support is k=3:
the Pauli purity identity gives total nonidentity score three for every
pure two-qubit state, so the nine double-axis terms score at most three;
Bell states attain three and their Pauli twirl has barycenter I/4.
For Delta tensor Delta, V=9,s=4,k=3, so the exact ratio is 6/5, while the
incorrect product-support substitution would produce 8/5.

For the full nonidentity Pauli frame on n qubits, V=4^n-1,k=2^n-1, and
s_(Delta^tensor_n)=3^n-1. Its ratio is
(4^n-2^n)/(4^n-3^n), equal to two at n=1 and tending to one. The general
tensor theorem, not this one frame calculation, rules out logarithmic
growth from independent positive local channels and their listed closures.

10. EXACT HIGHER-DIMENSION ABSOLUTE-VALUE OBSTRUCTION

Let S be the symmetric nearest-neighbor transition matrix on C5:
S_ij=1/2 if i-j=+-1 mod 5, and zero otherwise. Define

  Phi(X)=sum_{i,j} S_ij X_jj |i><i|,
  B(X)=sum_{i,j} S_ij X_jj |ii><ii|.                           (F1)

These are legal CP TP maps; Phi is unital, EB, self-adjoint, and has both
marginals supplied by B. On diagonal matrices, Phi acts as S; all
offdiagonal Hilbert-Schmidt eigendirections have eigenvalue zero.

The eigenvalues of S are 1, a,a,-b,-b, with
a=(sqrt(5)-1)/4 and b=(sqrt(5)+1)/4. Its Hilbert-Schmidt absolute value
restricts to |S| on diagonal matrices. Fourier inversion gives

  (|S|)_(0,1)=[1+2a^2-2b^2]/5=(2-sqrt(5))/10<0.              (F2)

Therefore |Phi|(|1><1|) has a negative diagonal entry. It is not a positive
map, although Phi is a legal classical EB channel. This is an exact
failed-transfer witness for the qubit absolute-value construction, not
a counterexample to any finite-C target; Phi itself is EB.

11. INTERFACE, COSTS, TESTS, AND REMAINING GATES

Supplied information: the local channel matrices and legal broadcaster
promises/certificates; a product, mixture, unitary, conditional-expectation,
or bistochastic-sandwich representation when that scope is claimed. The
representation is NOT acquired for free from a dense global Choi matrix.

Exact product comparator: O(n) constant-size Bloch diagonalizations and
weights, then a compact local measurement/preparation prescription. It
uses at most six outcomes per local POVM, hence at most 6^n listed global
outcomes. Operationally choose one of three axes at each site, measure it,
and prepare the measured eigenstate, using O(n) local operations with
supplied exact rotations. This describes a compact product implementation;
it does not make dense D-by-D input reading polynomial in n.

Graph encoding adds the supplied U_G circuit before and after the EB map,
at most 2|E(G)| CZ gates per branch. Mixture choices and arbitrary supplied
F,F^dagger implementations incur their actual costs. Spectral rank-one
refinement of M POVM effects can require up to MD outcomes and M dense
D-dimensional diagonalizations. The pure canonical existence proof is
not an efficient output-list guarantee.

Exact real weights/rotations are part of the mathematical construction.
If approximate canonical factors have Hilbert-Schmidt operator errors
epsilon_j and remain contractions, tensor telescoping gives total error
at most sum_j epsilon_j, hence an additive frame-score allowance
(sum_j epsilon_j)V. A uniform finite-precision multiplicative guarantee
without a gap is not provided.

Proving representability, or finding an exact separating family with

is assumed here.

This theorem removes independent tensor products and their listed
tracial closures as sources of an accumulating logarithmic loss. It is a
scoped constant-factor advance, not a solution of the general target.
