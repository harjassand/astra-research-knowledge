## PGF and decision reversal

For \(H(x,t)=E[x^{R_t}1\{U_t=0\}\mid S_0=1]\), first-event conditioning gives

\[
\partial_tH=b(H^2-H)+d(1-H)+a(x-H)-qH
=bH^2-(b+d+a+q)H+d+ax.
\]

Therefore \(P(R_T>0,U_T=0)=H(1,T)-H(0,T)\) and \(P(U_T>0)=1-H(1,T)\). The auditor independently integrated the Riccati equation with RK4 and matched all four closed-form values:

## Endpoint-moment inverse

Set \(r=b-d-a-q\) and \(h=d+a+q\). The safe count is a linear birth-death process with birth rate b and removal rate h:

\[
E[S_T]=e^{rT},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(S_T)=\begin{cases}
\frac{b+h}{r}m_S(m_S-1),&r\ne0,\\
(b+h)T,&r=0.
\end{cases}
\]

The terminal R/U means identify a,q after r is recovered from \(m_S\). The safe-count variance then identifies \(b+h\), and r identifies \(b-h\). The stated inverse recovers all four rates in the supplied model rows. The auditor noted that near \(r=0\), finite-sample inversion may be ill-conditioned; the report correctly makes no finite-sample rate-estimation guarantee from the exact-moment algebra.

## Finite-sample rule

For the two-action difference of independent Bernoulli sample means, Hoeffding yields \(P(\text{wrong sign})\le e^{-n\Delta^2}\). At \(\Delta=0.033554343983\), n=2,661 founders per arm gives 0.0499864. For the robust selector, \(h=\sqrt{\log(4m/\alpha)/(2n)}\); with m=2, alpha=0.05, n=1,016 gives h≤0.05. On simultaneous coverage, the risk constraint and 2h target-regret statement follow as written.
