  M = [[0_(a x b), P], [-Q^T, 0_(a x b)]],

Operator proof: the original exponent is
sum P_ij up_Ai^dagger down_Bj^dagger +
sum Q_ji up_Bj^dagger down_Ai^dagger.
The all-spin B transformation sends the first term to
P_ij up_Ai^dagger hole-down_Bj and the second to
-Q_ji down_Ai^dagger hole-up_Bj. These are number-conserving hopping terms
from the initially filled B-hole orbitals to the A orbitals. Exponentiating
them sends each occupied B-hole creation operator to the corresponding column

of the rank of F.

6. DECISIVE CONSTANT FRACTIONAL-LC / SECTOR / LOCAL-CHAIN OBSTRUCTION

Let n=2q and F be the cyclic permutation matrix, F_(i,i+1 mod n)=1. For a
down set J, the only nonzero same-size row minor uses I=predecessor(J).
Disjointness means J contains no adjacent sites of the site cycle. At k=q
there are exactly TWO feasible sets: all even sites, or all odd sites. The
same fact holds for the pair-index I measure in (5). Each determinant weight
is one. Its homogeneous parity polynomial is

  p_q(z)=product_(i even) z_i + product_(i odd) z_i.         (6)

Fractional logconcavity obstruction, in the standard convention that
log p_q(z_1^alpha,...,z_n^alpha) must be concave: at z=ones, the two indicator
vectors have marginals 1/2 and covariance vv^T/4, where v_i alternates +/-1.
The log Hessian is

  H=alpha^2 vv^T/4 - alpha I/2.

Its eigenvalue along v is (alpha^2 q-alpha)/2. Hence alpha<=1/q is necessary.
No constant positive fractional-LC parameter works uniformly in n. This is
not just ordinary-LC failure at a fixed small n.

Sector obstruction: assign all even variables exp(+i pi/(2q)) and all odd
variables exp(-i pi/(2q)). They lie in every open positive-real centered
sector of aperture >pi/q, while the two monomials in (6) are +i and -i.
Thus p_q vanishes there. No uniform constant-aperture sector stability holds.

Chain obstruction: the two supports differ in q elements. A k<->(k-d)
down-up chain with d<q retains at least one old element, which excludes the
other alternating support. It stays in its starting configuration forever.
In particular the constant-block walk needed by the constant-fractional-LC
sampling theorem cannot be used on this parity measure.
