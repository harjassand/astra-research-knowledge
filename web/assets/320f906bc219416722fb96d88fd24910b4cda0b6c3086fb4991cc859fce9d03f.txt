THEOREM CANDIDATE (FULL SU(d) INTERFACE)

Fix d>=2, alpha>0, gamma>0, M,B>=0. Choose any supplied traceless Hermitian
Hilbert--Schmidt orthonormal basis T_1,...,T_p of M_d, p=d^2-1,
Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab. For each N let A=A^T be real, ||A||<=M, and b real,
||b||<=B. They may vary with N. Put F_a=sum_(i=1)^N T_a^(i),
Q_C=sum_ab C_ab {F_a,F_b}/2, C_N=alpha I+A N^-gamma, and

             K_N=Q_(C_N)/N + sum_a b_a F_a.

Then for all sufficiently large N, uniformly over the displayed A,b, there
is a defined positive mixture sigma_N of IDENTICAL one-qudit products with

 T(e^K_N/Tr e^K_N,sigma_N) <= C exp[-c N (log N)^2].       (T1)

Constants and thresholds depend on d,alpha,gamma,M,B. No dimension-uniform
or parameter-independent decoder claim is made. A smaller field, including
b.F/N^.75, is included by replacing the supplied b with that smaller value.
For d=2, T_a=sigma_a/sqrt2 and alpha=1 give the critical baseline 2J^2/N;
the baseline A,b are rescaled by 1/2 and 1/sqrt2, respectively. Thus (T1), if
the audit passes, improves its known-parameter distance upper, including the
exp(-cN) c07_s02 candidate. It does not change the universal decoder lower.

This is a probabilistic representation/trace-distance theorem, not a claimed

1. EXACT DIFFERENTIAL IDENTITIES

Use the full local density variable rho>0, Tr rho=1, and k(rho)=rho^tensorN.
The tangent inner product is the ordinary Hermitian Hilbert--Schmidt product.
For traceless Hermitian T define
 t_T=Tr rho T,
 V_T(rho)={T,rho}/2-t_T rho,
 R_T(rho)=-i[T,rho],
 B_T=N t_T+V_T.grad.
Normalized one-site filtering and rotation give exactly
 {F_T,k}/2=B_T k,               -i[F_T,k]=R_T.grad k.
Constant matrix actions commute with differentiation. Since left and right
F actions commute, their sum-of-squares identity gives

 {F_T^2,k}/2=(B_T^2-(R_T.grad)^2/4) k.                   (T2)

Polarize (T2) for mixed coefficients. The sandwich map M_K(X)={K,X}/2
therefore acts on k by a second-order operator G=U+a.grad+D:Hess/N, where

 D_C=sum_ab C_ab [V_a tensor V_b-R_a tensor R_b/4],
 a=2 sum_ab C_ab t_a V_b
       +(1/N)sum_ab C_ab [(DV_b)V_a-(DR_b)R_a/4]+V_(T_b),
 U= N t^T C t + s_C-t^T C t + N b.t,
 s_C=Tr rho Z_C,         Z_C=sum_ab C_ab {T_a,T_b}/2.     (T3)

Here a has its last term from T_b=sum b_aT_a, not an outcome label. U is
exactly Tr K_N k. All a,D,U are explicit polynomials in the matrix entries
of rho and the supplied local matrices. The coordinate derivatives are
 DV_T[Z]={T,Z}/2-Tr(ZT)rho-t_T Z,
 DR_T[Z]=-i[T,Z].
These specify the coefficients without a field oracle or spectral ansatz.

2. ISOTROPIC FORM AND UNIFORM POSITIVITY ON A FULL-RANK REGION

For a traceless Hermitian test Z put z=Tr rho Z and Y=Z-zI. Basis completeness
gives the exact identity

 <Z,D_I Z>=Tr[rho Y rho Y]=||sqrt(rho)Y sqrt(rho)||_2^2.   (T4)

To verify it, V_a*Z=Tr T_a({rho,Z}/2-z rho), whose matrix in parentheses is
traceless, while R_a*Z=Tr T_a[-i(rho Z-Zrho)]. Parseval gives the squared
HS norms. Subtracting one quarter of the second from the first expands to
Tr rho Z rho Z-2z Tr rho^2 Z+z^2 Tr rho^2, exactly (T4).
In particular, rho>=epsilon I implies D_I>=epsilon^2 I on the traceless
tangent space: ||Y||_2^2=||Z||_2^2+d z^2>=||Z||_2^2.

Globally, as operators from the traceless generator space to the tangent,
||V||<=2 and ||R||<=2. Indeed ||{T,rho}/2||_2<=||T||_2 and
|Tr rho T| ||rho||_2<=||T||_2; the commutator bound is twice this first bound.
Consequently for any symmetric ||A||<=M,

             |<Z,D_A Z>| <=5M ||Z||_2^2.                (T5)

For M>0 set k0=sqrt(32M/alpha) and epsilon_N=k0 N^-gamma/2. Require
 N^gamma>=128Md^2/alpha. Then epsilon_N<=1/(2d), C_N lies between
alpha I/2 and 2alpha I, and on rho>=epsilon_N I,

 D_(C_N)>=alpha epsilon_N^2 I-5M N^-gamma I
          =27M N^-gamma I>0.                            (T6)

There is even a positive margin on rho>=epsilon_N I/2 (3M N^-gamma), so
the positive square root is smooth on a neighborhood of the stopping region.
This is an acquired numerical margin, not a generic PPT or unknown-gap test.

3. LOG-DETERMINANT CONTROL; NO CENTRAL HEAT SEED IS NEEDED

Start rho_0=I/d. Until tau=inf{t:lambda_min(rho_t)=epsilon_N}, solve the
finite-dimensional Ito SDE

 d rho_t=a(rho_t)dt+sqrt(2D_(C_N)(rho_t)/N)dW_t,          (T7)

in the real traceless Hermitian coordinates. Positivity of rho is maintained
until tau by this definition, and trace is exactly one. On this compact region
the coefficients are smooth and have a known strictly positive margin, hence
the ordinary finite-dimensional existence/uniqueness theorem applies. Freeze
rho and its positive weight w_t=exp integral_0^(t wedge tau) U(rho_s)ds after
tau. Every w_t k(rho_(t wedge tau)) is a positive iid-product operator.

Let ell(rho)=logdet rho. For every traceless T,
 V_T ell=-d t_T,             R_T ell=0.
The exact diffusion generator L=G-U thus satisfies

 L ell= -d(2-1/N)t^T C_N t -(d/N)s_(C_N)-d b.t,          (T8)
 d<ell-martingale>_t=(2d^2/N)t^T C_N t dt.              (T9)

All inverse-density terms cancel in these formulas. They do not deteriorate
as lambda_min rho approaches the stopping cutoff. The identities follow by
V_a t_b=Tr rho{T_a,T_b}/2-t_at_b and R_b ell=0 in (T3).

Use ||t||^2=Tr rho^2-1/d<=(d-1)/d, Z_(C_N)<=2alpha sum T_a^2,
sum T_a^2=(d^2-1)I/d, and ||T_b||<=B. A conservative uniform bound is

 |L ell|<=beta:=2alpha(d-1)(d+3)+dB,
 <ell-martingale>_1<=v_N:=4alpha d(d-1)/N.              (T10)

At a hit, det rho_tau<=epsilon_N (each other eigenvalue is <=1). Since
ell(I/d)=-d log d, define

 L_N=(gamma/2)log N-log k0-d log d-beta.

Whenever L_N>0, a hit by time one forces the scalar martingale to cross
-L_N. The one-sided exponential-martingale maximal inequality therefore gives

 P(tau<=1)<=exp[-N L_N^2/(8alpha d(d-1))].              (T11)

The probability here is the unweighted SDE law. Its Feynman--Kac weight is
kept in the next comparison, so no importance-sampling normalization is free.

The exponent is h_N, since tau<=t; the looser exp(2h_N) bound also works.

6. COUNTEREXAMPLE TO TRANSFERRING THE CENTRAL CRITICAL SEED

The stronger direct proof does not rely on qubit seed concentration. For
d>=3, the susceptibility-critical fundamental SU(d) seed exp[d sum F_a^2/(2N)]
cannot concentrate in a shrinking neighborhood of I/d in any positive iid
mixture. Choose local eta with eigenvalues (d-1)/d once and
1/[d(d-1)] the other d-1 times. Then
 S(eta)=logd-(d-2)log(d-1)/d,
 Tr(eta-I/d)^2=(d-2)^2/[d(d-1)].
At alpha=d/2 its product variational free-energy gain is
 Delta_d=(d-2)^2/[2(d-1)]-(d-2)log(d-1)/d>0.
Strict positivity is x-1/x>2logx for x=d-1>1; its derivative is
(x-1)^2/x^2 and it vanishes at x=1. The Gibbs variational principle gives
logZ(alpha)-Nlogd>=N Delta_d (the finite-N correction is positive).
Convexity in alpha gives <sumF^2>>=N^2 Delta_d/alpha. In every exact positive
iid mixture, <sumF^2>=N(d^2-1)/d+N(N-1)E Tr(eta-I/d)^2. Thus the mean
squared displacement is bounded below by Delta_d/alpha-o(1). A fixed
positive fraction lies outside a fixed sufficiently small ball. This is a
route obstruction, not an obstruction to (T1) or separability.
