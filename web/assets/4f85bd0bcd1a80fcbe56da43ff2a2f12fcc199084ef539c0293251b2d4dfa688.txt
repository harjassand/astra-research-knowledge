CYCLE 5 — INDEPENDENT SIX-HOLE FALSIFICATION / STABILITY AUDIT
7 October 2026. Algebraic proof audit; no new random search.

VERDICT
The proposed leading-constant-one square-root exchange inequality survives the independent audit. In fact it holds for every symmetric-difference size, not only six, for the actual signed-stable ambient-hole coefficients and their coordinate-pair auxiliary extensions. Zero coefficients, boundary roots, common polynomial factors, and possible specialization degree drops do not create a gap. A direct rational Pick-function proof avoids relying on a strict Hurwitz-matrix theorem or on continuity of a particular minor formula.

This closes the stated six-hole algebraic gate. It does not provide the missing microscopic determinant-energy encoding, a spectral-gap proof, a partition oracle, or an FPRAS. No numerical search is used as theorem evidence here. The parent's reported million exact tests were not rerun.

INPUT AND PRECISE CLAIM
Let n be even and F an arbitrary complex n-by-n matrix. For even retained R,
 Z_R(F) = sum_{I subset R, |I|=|R|/2} |det F[I,R\I]|^2,
 Z_empty = 1,
and set f(U)=Z_[n]\U for even U and f(U)=0 for odd U.
The source work/cycle4/parity_matching_transfer_attack.txt supplies the real-stable multiaffine polynomial
 Q(x) = sum_U (-1)^((n-|U|)/2) f(U) x^U.              (1)
The audit below verifies its construction and then proves: for any even S,T and any a in D=S symmetric-difference T,
 sqrt(f(S)f(T)) <= sum_{j in D\{a}}
   sqrt(f(S symmetric-difference {a,j})
        f(T symmetric-difference {a,j})).             (2)
There is no assumption that S and T are disjoint or that their complements have no common occupied core. Thus |S|=4, |T|=2, |D|=6, n>=8 is covered directly.

GENERAL COEFFICIENT THEOREM
The proof only needs a nonnegative even-support multiaffine polynomial
 h(z)=sum_{U even} f(U)z^U
which is nonvanishing whenever all Re(z_i)>0. For (1) this follows from
 h(z)=(-1)^(n/2) Q(i z).                               (3)
Indeed i maps the open right half-plane to the open upper half-plane, and the phases of every even-support coefficient in (1) cancel.

If f(S)f(T)=0, (2) is immediate. In the remaining case both endpoints are strictly positive. Make the simultaneous full reciprocal twist on T:
 h_T(z) = z^T h(z_i^{-1} for i in T; z_i otherwise).
Because h is multiaffine, this is a polynomial with exact coefficient formula
 h_T(z)=sum_U f(T symmetric-difference U)z^U.           (4)
The map z->1/z preserves the open right half-plane, and the prefactor z^T is nonzero there. Hence h_T is right-half-plane stable. As |T| is even, its support is still even.

Set every variable outside D to zero. This is a boundary specialization, implemented by setting them to a common positive epsilon and taking epsilon down to zero. Multivariate Hurwitz continuity gives either a stable polynomial or the zero polynomial. The zero option is excluded by the surviving constant f(T)>0; the top coefficient on D is f(S)>0. Let this nonzero restriction be r(z_D).

Put q(x_D)=r(-i x_D). This is real stable, with alternating real signs by even degree:
 q(x)=sum_{U subset D, |U| even} (-1)^(|U|/2)
       f(T symmetric-difference U)x^U.                 (5)
Write |D|=2k, d=2k-1. For arbitrary strictly positive w_j, j in D\{a}, specialize x_a=u and x_j=w_j t. Stability is preserved since positive multiples of an upper-half-plane t are in that half-plane. We obtain
 q(u,(w_j t))=A(t)+u B(t),                             (6)
where
 A(t)=a_0-a_2 t^2+a_4 t^4-...+(-1)^(k-1)a_(d-1)t^(d-1),
 B(t)=-a_1 t+a_3 t^3-...+(-1)^k a_d t^d,
with every a_l nonnegative. The endpoints give a_0>0 and a_d>0. Thus deg(B)=d exactly, deg(A)<=d-1, and A(0)=a_0>0.

ELEMENTARY PICK RESIDUE LEMMA — THE LOAD-BEARING INEQUALITY
For any real stable bivariate polynomial A(t)+u B(t) of the form (6), with a_0 a_d>0,
 a_1 a_(d-1) >= a_0 a_d.                              (7)

Proof. First B has no zero in the open upper half-plane. One way to see this without invoking a derivative theorem is to let R->infinity in
 q(iR,t)/(iR) = B(t)+A(t)/(iR).
Every polynomial on the left is nonvanishing for Im(t)>0. The limit B is not identically zero, so Hurwitz applies. As B has real coefficients, every zero of B is real.

For Im(t)>0, the unique root of (6) in u is -A(t)/B(t). Stability forbids this root from the open upper half-plane. Consequently
 Im(A(t)/B(t)) >= 0.                                  (8)
Thus L(t)=A(t)/B(t) is a real rational Pick function. It is proper because deg(A)<deg(B), including after cancellation of common factors.

After cancellation, every pole of L lies on the real axis and is simple. To justify simplicity directly, a real pole r of order m>=2 would have a leading Laurent term c(t-r)^(-m), c real and nonzero. Along t=r+rho exp(i theta), 0<theta<pi, its imaginary part has both signs for suitable theta, as rho->0. This contradicts (8). At any simple pole r the real residue c_r is nonpositive, since evaluation at r+i rho has leading imaginary part -c_r/rho.

At zero, A(0)=a_0>0, so no cancellation is possible. Since B is odd, a_1=0 would make its zero at 0 have odd order at least three, contradicting the simple-pole conclusion. Therefore a_1>0 and
 Res_0 L = -a_0/a_1.

There is no polynomial part in the partial-fraction expansion. Hence
 lim_{t->infinity} tL(t) = sum_r c_r = -a_(d-1)/a_d.
All residues are nonpositive, and the residue at zero is one of them. Therefore
 -a_(d-1)/a_d <= -a_0/a_1,
which proves (7). This also forces a_(d-1)>0; no extra penultimate-degree assumption is needed. Common factors elsewhere and repeated zeros of B cause no problem after cancellation. Boundary roots are included rather than excluded. QED.

For |D|=2, d=1 and (7) is equality. This case is also the immediate one-term swap of S and T. If D is empty there is no a to quantify over.

FIELD OPTIMIZATION AND ALL ZERO CASES
Let P=product_{j in D\{a}} w_j and put
 alpha_j=f(T symmetric-difference {a,j}),
 beta_j =f(S symmetric-difference {a,j}).
Reading the four coefficients of (5)-(6) gives exactly
 a_0=f(T),
 a_1=sum_j alpha_j w_j,
 a_(d-1)=P sum_j beta_j/w_j,
 a_d=P f(S).
In the penultimate coefficient, the contributing subsets are D\{a,j}; the twist changes these to S symmetric-difference {a,j}. This identity remains true even when S and T overlap outside D.

After cancelling P>0 in (7), for every positive field vector w,
 f(S)f(T) <= (sum_j alpha_j w_j)(sum_j beta_j/w_j).      (9)

For nonnegative alpha,beta,
 inf_{w_j>0}(sum alpha_j w_j)(sum beta_j/w_j)
   = (sum sqrt(alpha_j beta_j))^2.                     (10)
Cauchy-Schwarz gives the lower bound. For indices with both coefficients positive, set w_j=sqrt(beta_j/alpha_j). If alpha_j>0,beta_j=0, let w_j->0. If alpha_j=0,beta_j>0, let w_j->infinity. Indices with both zero may have any fixed positive field. These limits give equality in the infimum. Only strictly positive fields are evaluated in (9); zero and infinite fields are limits, not illegal substitutions.

Taking the infimum in (9) and then nonnegative square roots proves (2). If all alpha_j beta_j vanish, (10) gives zero; this cannot occur with f(S)f(T)>0. The proof itself establishes the existence of at least one feasible exchange in that case.

ACTUAL SIGNED-HOLE STABILITY AUDIT
The supplied construction is sound. Set
 H=[[0,2F],[2F*,0]],  P(u,v)=det(diag(u,v)-H).
For all Im(u_i),Im(v_i)>0, the imaginary part of the diagonal-minus-H matrix is positive definite. If it killed a nonzero vector, the imaginary part of its quadratic form would be positive, a contradiction. Therefore P is stable; it has real coefficients since H is Hermitian.

Apply 2^(-n) product_i(partial_u_i+partial_v_i), then identify u_i=v_i=x_i. A positive directional derivative preserves stability or produces zero: introduce a variable t translating the two coordinates by t, differentiate t, and use the usual derivative and boundary-specialization closures. Identification preserves stability directly. The final polynomial is not zero because its leading coefficient is one.

For the coefficient identity, interpret the derivative as an average over principal characteristic polynomials retaining exactly one of u_i,v_i at each site. A chiral principal minor is zero unless the chosen up and down index sets I,J have equal size k. The sites are distinct, so I intersect J is empty. Its determinant is (-1)^k 2^(2k)|det F[I,J]|^2. It appears with probability 2^(-2k); their product is exactly the coefficient in (1). The factor 2 in H is essential and was checked. No assumption that the pair-label generating polynomial itself is stable enters this proof.

Positive pair activities can be included by scaling row_i(F) by the square root of activity_i, purely as a proof operation. This covers arbitrary positive real activities; it is not a claim that such square roots are acquired exactly in a rational bit interface. Limits cover zero activities.

COORDINATE-PAIR AUXILIARY CLOSURE
Adding a distinct pair (e_i,e_j), i!=j, of activity w>=0 gives, by determinant expansion,
 f_new(U)=f(U)+w f(U union {i,j}) if U avoids i,j,
 f_new(U)=f(U) otherwise.
The signed polynomial operation is Q_new=(1-w partial_i partial_j)Q.

The operator's multiaffine algebraic symbol is
 [(z_i+y_i)(z_j+y_j)-w] product_{l!=i,j}(z_l+y_l).
It is stable: each sum is in the upper half-plane, and the product of two such numbers cannot be nonnegative real w. For w=0, their product is nonzero. Theorem 1.1(b), or its real version Theorem 1.2(b), of Borcea-Branden therefore supplies stability preservation. The operator has real coefficients, and its top-degree coefficient is unchanged, so the zero-output alternative cannot occur for this Q. The coefficient recurrence retains nonnegativity and even support. Iteration, including overlapping auxiliary pairs, is legal; multiaffinity makes repeated-coordinate derivative terms vanish exactly as repeated coordinate columns do in determinants.

NECESSARY-HYPOTHESIS FALSIFICATION
Nonnegative even coefficients alone do not imply (2). On six variables, h(z)=1+z_1...z_6 has positive constant and top coefficient but all required two- and four-variable coefficients vanish; (2) would read 1<=0. It is not right-half-plane stable: choose every z_j=exp(i*pi/6), all with positive real part, and their product is -1. Thus the stability hypothesis is doing essential work; finite matrix tests or nonnegative parity support are not substitutes.

SCALING AND ALGORITHMIC LIMITS
The source's statement about restoring e_i after a diagonal ambient transform needs its activity convention made explicit. A common invertible ambient transform alone multiplies every full basis determinant by the same factor and preserves its normalized law. Subsequently rescaling both vectors of pair i by 1/lambda_i multiplies the squared weight of any basis selecting that pair by lambda_i^(-4). To preserve a fixed weighted law, compensate by replacing its pair activity t_i with t_i lambda_i^4. This is a bookkeeping clarification, not a counterexample to (2): the proof covers every positive pair activity. Vertex multipliers of hole coefficients also preserve (2), because each product has the same coordinate multiplicities before and after a simultaneous two-index swap.

The exchange theorem gives a leading coefficient one in the proposed relocation recurrence and removes the exponential path-loss objection to a constant-factor replacement. Actual relocation still needs its separate normalization and error bookkeeping checked. More decisively, no determinant analogue of the two-replica positive weight-preserving discrepancy-cycle encoding has been constructed. A true coefficient inequality does not manufacture that microscopic flow, bound its congestion, provide an exact-zero oracle, or pay numerical/bit costs for counting. The next gate remains that independent determinant-energy construction.
