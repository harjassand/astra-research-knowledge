1. POSITIVE RANK-ENERGY MINIMIZER AND FILTER

For A=U Sigma V^dagger, put X_A=U^dagger T_A U and Y_A=V^dagger T_A V.
Expansion using sum_A T_A^2=C2 I gives

  E(A)=2 C2 ||A||_HS^2 - 2 Re sum_(A,i,j) sigma_i sigma_j
                                      (X_A)_ij conjugate((Y_A)_ij).

For F_L=U Sigma U^dagger and F_R=V Sigma V^dagger, the same expansion
gives the individual square sums sum sigma_i sigma_j |X_ij|^2 and
sum sigma_i sigma_j |Y_ij|^2. Subtraction yields exactly

  E(A)-[E(F_L)+E(F_R)]/2
    =sum_(A,i,j) sigma_i sigma_j |(X_A)_ij-(Y_A)_ij|^2 >=0.

No normality or positivity of A is used. Each modulus is positive and
preserves rank and Hilbert-Schmidt norm. Since their average energy is
at most E(A), one has energy at most E(A), which suffices to replace a
global minimizer by a positive minimizer. Existence of the original
minimizer follows from compactness of the closed rank-at-most-Q unit
Hilbert-Schmidt sphere.

For positive F, S_F=Tr(F^2)>0 and Haar twirling gives
int F_g^2 dg=(S_F/d)I. Thus Phi_F(X)=(d/S_F)int F_g X F_g dg is CP,
TP, unital, and

  Phi_F-I=-(d/(2S_F)) D_F,
  D_F(X)=int [F_g,[F_g,X]]dg.

The sign and factor follow by expanding the double commutator and using
the twirled F_g^2 identity. Every Kraus operator has rank q=rank(F)<=Q.
For finite labels, §14 applies Caratheodory to the compact Choi orbit in
the real d^4-dimensional Hermitian Choi space, retaining the full channel
and TP exactly with at most d^4+1 nodes. The encoder and conditional
decoder factor each node through supp(F), so this establishes the stated
branch dimension once F is supplied.

2. TWO ADJOINT COPIES AND QUARTIC/GRAM NORMALIZATION

With D_A=d_ABC T_B T_C, the family D_A is Hermitian and transforms as an
adjoint. The identity

  sum_A D_A^2=C2(C2/3+1/4)I

has a valid central-element proof in the target: its degree-four symbol
is (1/3)(sum x_A^2)^2; outer conjugation removes the cubic central term;
the trivial representation removes the constant; the defining
representation fixes the remaining C2 coefficient to 1/4. The
normalization check D_A=(5/6)t_A in the fundamental gives
sum D_A^2=25/27 I, consistent with the formula.

The cubic contraction sum_A T_A D_A=C3 I and Schur invariance yield the
ordinary-trace Gram entries d C2/8 and d C3/8. Therefore for
S_A=(D_A-(C3/C2)T_A)/N,

  Tr(T_A S_B)=0,
  Tr(S_A S_B)=d (Delta/N^2) delta_AB/8,
  sum_A S_A^2=(Delta/N^2)I.

These factors agree: using normalized trace removes d, while ordinary
trace retains it. The factorization

  C2 Delta=ab(a+2)(b+2)(a+b+1)(a+b+3)/12

is positive exactly on the regular cone and supports both embeddings.
The tensor rule 3 tensor (a,b) has three present branches when a,b>0;
3bar applied to each returns one (a,b), and 3 tensor 3bar=1+8 removes
one copy. Hence the adjoint multiplicity is exactly two; no third
component is omitted in the D_F(T_A) ansatz.

The regular-cone constants also check algebraically. From a,b in
[cN,CN], C2 is between c^2 N^2 and K N^2, with K=C^2+2C. The
factorization gives Delta>=c^6 N^4/(3K)=L N^4, hence C_S>=L N^2.
Delta>=0 gives alpha^2<=C2/3+1/4. These imply ||T_H||_op<=B0 N
for B0=r sqrt(K), as used in the preceding bounded-Gibbs step.

3. COMMUTATOR CONTROL FOR THE SECOND COPY

The product rule

  [D_A,F]=sum_BC d_ABC( [T_B,F]T_C + T_B[T_C,F] )

is exact. As a map from 64 pair coordinates to 8 coordinates, d has
squared operator norm 5/3 by sum_BC d_ABC d_EBC=(5/3)delta_AE.
Each of the two summands before addition has total squared HS norm
C2 E(F), using sum T_A^2=C2 I and cyclicity; squaring their sum costs
at most factor 2. Therefore

  sum_A ||[D_A,F]||_HS^2 <= (5/3)(4 C2 E(F))=(20/3)C2 E(F).

For S=(D-alpha T)/N, (x+y)^2<=2x^2+2y^2 then gives

  E_S(F)<=((40/3)C2+2alpha^2)E(F)/N^2
         <=(14K+1/2)E(F).

The last constant uses N>=1: (40/3)K+(2/3)K=14K and
(1/2)/N^2<=1/2. This holds for every matrix F; the filter later uses a
Hermitian positive F.

4. DOUBLE-COMMUTATOR COEFFICIENTS AND TRACE-NORM CONTROL

D_F is a covariant self-adjoint map and its action on the adjoint family
has the complete form D_F(T_A)=uT_A+vS_A. Pairing and summing against
T_A and S_A, respectively, gives

  u=E(F)/(d C2),
  v=E_TS(F)/(d C_S),
  E_TS(F)=sum_A Tr([S_A,F]^dagger[T_A,F]).

The sign is correct for the convention D_F(X)=[F,[F,X]]:
Tr(X[F,[F,Y]])=Tr([F,X]^dagger[F,Y]) for Hermitian X,Y.
Simultaneous adjoint rotation of T and S leaves the summed pairings
unchanged, so Haar averaging introduces no additional 8, d, or N factor.
For Hermitian F the cross pairing is real. Cauchy-Schwarz and the
commutator estimate give |E_TS(F)|<=sqrt(C_E) E(F).

For real x with ||x||_2=r, sum T_A^2=C2 I and sum S_A^2=C_S I imply
||T_H||_op<=r sqrt(C2), ||S_H||_op<=r sqrt(C_S). Since
B=T_H/N, trace norm is bounded by d times operator norm, and the
coefficients above yield

  ||D_F(B)||_1
    <= r E(F)/N [1/sqrt(C2)+sqrt(C_E)/sqrt(C_S)]
    <= rho E(F)/N^2,

where rho=r(1/c+sqrt(C_E)/sqrt(L)). This is linear in E(F), as needed.

5. ORDERED GIBBS SERIES AND FULL TRACE NORM

C_B=(B+B0 I)/(2B0) obeys 0<=C_B<=I, D_F(C_B)=D_F(B)/(2B0), and

  ||D_F(C_B)||_1<=c1 E(F)/N^2,
  int ||[F_g,C_B]||_2^2 dg=c2 E(F)/N^2,
  c1=rho/(2B0), c2=gamma/(4B0^2).

The second equality is the exact adjoint second moment
int (R(g)x)_A(R(g)x)_B dg=(r^2/8)delta_AB, followed by the
2B0 rescaling. For each k, applying delta_g=[F_g,.] twice to C_B^k
produces k same-factor terms and 2*binom(k,2)=k(k-1) cross terms.
After averaging, the first class is bounded by k||D_F(C_B)||_1.
Schatten Holder bounds each cross product by ||delta_g(C_B)||_2^2,
because all powers of C_B are contractions. Thus

  ||D_F(C_B^k)||_1 <= [c1 k+c2 k(k-1)] E(F)/N^2.

This does not commute D_F(C_B) past C_B. Absolute norm convergence of the
finite-dimensional exponential series gives

  ||D_F(exp(ell C_B))||_1
    <=exp(ell)[c1 ell+c2 ell^2]E(F)/N^2.

Finally tau=exp(ell C_B)/Z_C with Z_C>=d and ell=2hB0. From the exact
filter identity and the half-trace factor,

  (1/2)||Phi_F(tau)-tau||_1
    <= exp(2hB0)[h rho+gamma h^2] E(F)/(4 S_F N^2).

Taking h<=h1 and a positive minimizer with E(F)/S_F=Lambda(Q) gives
exactly the stated C_* upper. The argument controls the full output
density in trace norm; it is not a first-moment-only inference.
