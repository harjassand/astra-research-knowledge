## Consequence for the novelty/comparator claim

The admitted qubit Gibbs state
\[
\rho_N\propto \exp[(\alpha J^2-\delta J_z^2)/N+hJ_z]
\]
is invariant under every permutation of its N sites. Given an acquired exact MPO/symmetrized-Pauli description (or a certified trace-norm approximation) of any such state, a polynomial-arithmetic centralized classical simulator for arbitrary *product* measurements follows directly by representation and contraction; it does not require the state to be separable. Thus the positive separable-mixture compiler must not be presented as the first classical simulation of collective-spin Gibbs states or as necessary for efficient product-measurement statistics.

A primary construction gives every permutation-invariant qubit operator an MPO with bond dimension O(N^3): [Thurtell and Miyake, *Optimizing one-axis twists for variational Bayesian quantum metrology*, Phys. Rev. Research 6, 023179 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.023179), Appendix D, especially Eqs. (D4)–(D6) and the concluding bond-dimension bound. Their representation identifies the PI operator by O(N^3) coefficients. [Tóth et al., *Permutationally Invariant Quantum Tomography*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 105, 250403 (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.250403) give the equivalent symmetrized-Pauli expansion and polynomial-size PI state description. Separately, [Anschuetz et al., *Efficient classical algorithms for simulating symmetric quantum systems*, Quantum 7, 1189 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2023-11-28-1189) give polynomial classical algorithms for PI Hamiltonians supplied in the symmetrized-Pauli basis, including Schur-basis computation of ground states and dynamics. These are primary precedents for polynomial classical processing of permutation symmetry; they do not themselves state the exact arbitrary-Gibbs-product-POVM sampler below.

For N local POVM effects \(E_{i,a_i}\), the joint outcome probability is
\[
 p(a_1,\ldots,a_N)=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\rho_N\bigotimes_i E_{i,a_i}\right].
\]
Contracting an MPO for \(\rho_N\) against these one-site effects takes polynomial arithmetic in N and its bond dimension. Sequential exact sampling uses
\(p(a_i\mid a_{<i})=p(a_{\le i})/p(a_{<i})\); the prefix sites use the observed effects and the unmeasured suffix uses identity effects. Prefix and suffix transfer environments can be updated/precomputed, so the number of arithmetic operations is polynomial (a direct dense-bond implementation is already polynomial with bond O(N^3)). This is a centralized simulator that receives the full measurement specification. It is not a no-communication local-hidden-variable model, and no positive product-state ensemble is used. In a bit-complexity claim, coefficient/effect encoding and numerical precision/conditioning must be stated; the representation theorem alone does not acquire unknown coefficients.

## Keep the interfaces separate

- **Acquired coefficients:** An O(N^3)-size PI/MPO description is the simulator's input. Supplying it is an assumption. This audit does not certify a bit-efficient procedure that computes every target Gibbs coefficient from \((\alpha,\delta,h)\) to arbitrary requested precision.
- **Product-measurement simulation:** Given that input plus the local POVM settings, a central classical algorithm samples the full product-measurement output distribution in polynomial arithmetic, regardless of whether \(\rho_N\) is separable or entangled. It can depend on the settings and can use global classical computation.
- **One positive hidden-variable mixture:** A law \(p(d\lambda)\) and product states \(\bigotimes_i\tau_i(\lambda)\) with \(\rho_N=\int p(d\lambda)\bigotimes_i\tau_i(\lambda)\) give a single setting-independent positive decomposition valid for all local POVMs. Such a representation is full separability and cannot cover entangled members of the PI family. It is a stronger structural certificate than merely sampling the product-measurement distribution.
- **Local product-state preparation:** Sampling \(\lambda\) and physically preparing the corresponding one-site states realizes the mixture on hardware. This costs classical randomness and N local preparations per copy. A classical probability sampler for measurement outputs does not itself prepare \(\rho_N\).

**Priority disposition:** polynomial PI representations and classical symmetry-based simulation are established prior art. The exact product-POVM sampler is a direct consequence of the standard MPO contraction interface, not claimed here as a separately published theorem. A new positive-mixture compiler can claim its own constructive, setting-independent decomposition/preparation interface and error/cost guarantee, but not first classical simulability of the PI Gibbs family. This is a targeted comparator audit, not an exhaustive priority search.
