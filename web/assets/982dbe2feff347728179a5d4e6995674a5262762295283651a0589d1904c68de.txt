PRIMARY SOURCES CHECKED (BOUNDED SEARCH; EXACT URLS)
- S. Torquato, A. Scardicchio, C. E. Zachary, “Point processes in arbitrary
  dimension from fermionic gases, random matrix theory, and number theory,”
  arXiv:0809.0449 (submitted 2008-09-02; v5 2008-11-25),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.0449. Supports arbitrary-dimensional fermionic
  DPP correlations, not the target Gibbs stability theorem.
- C. Boccato, F. Pieroni, D. Trevisan, “On Distances Among Slater Determinant
  States and Determinantal Point Processes,” arXiv:2504.08971,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08971, DOI 10.1007/s00023-026-01658-3 (published
  2026-02-21). Quantum/classical Slater-DPP distance results; no target
  interacting whole-Fock Gibbs claim identified from inspected theorems.
- Capel, Moscolari, Teufel, Wessel et al., “From Decay of Correlations to
  Locality and Stability of the Gibbs State,” arXiv:2310.09182,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09182, DOI 10.1007/s00220-024-05198-x (2025).
  Proposition 15 is whole-state norm-small perturbation stability; Theorem 34
  is local-observable stability under fixed-beta correlation assumptions.
- E. Lytvynov, “Fermion and boson random point processes as particle
  distributions of infinite free Fermi and Bose gases of finite density,”
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0112006 (2002), continuum free-gas DPP context.
- Z. Han, Y. Zhao, Z.-W. Liu, “Weakly interacting fermionic Gibbs states are
  Gaussian mixtures and classically simulable,” arXiv:2609.35159v1
  (2026-09-28), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35159. Weak-local-interaction,
  beta-dependent threshold; does not cover arbitrary W P=0.
