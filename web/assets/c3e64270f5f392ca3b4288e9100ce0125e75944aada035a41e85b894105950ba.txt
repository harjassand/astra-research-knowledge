§7: fixed sectors and full-Fock convergence
-------------------------------------------
For fixed m and <A_L> bounded, (16) gives

 <W> <= 4m(m-1)/(L-1)
       +4(m-1)sqrt(m/(2J))*sqrt(<A_L>)/L = O_m(L^-1).

This is the only step that previously required a strictly positive scaled
contact. The contraction K has norm loss at most <W>; the polar isometry U
and K therefore differ in norm by O_m(L^-1/2). The labeled-cube form
inequality (18), with Lh=L/(L-m+1)->1 for fixed m, bounds the multilinear
interpolants in H^1_0. The step/interpolant discrepancy is O_m(h) in norm
on bounded cube-energy sets. Thus any bounded-energy source sequence has a
strongly convergent subsequence under the exact isometry I_{L,m}, and
lower semicontinuity of the free Dirichlet form gives the kinetic liminf.
For nonnegative g_L, its contact term is nonnegative and may be discarded
in this liminf.

For a smooth symmetric compactly supported F, the proposed source recovery
amplitudes psi_L(x)=sqrt(m!) h^(m/2)F(hy) have norm tending to ||F||. Kpsi
is exactly the sampled cube function. On transitions with distinct gap
coordinates at both endpoints, each cube/source edge coefficient is one.
The remaining transitions touch a coincidence stratum; for fixed m there
are O_m(L^(m-1)) of them, and each smooth sampled difference is O_F(h).
After the L^2 scaling their total kinetic discrepancy is O_{m,F}(L^-1).
For the contact term, a single double tie gives the coefficient 2g and its
collapsed diagonal Riemann sum. Triple ties or two simultaneous ties have
O_m(L^(m-2)) nodes and contribute O_{m,F}(L^-1) after the g_L L scaling.
Hence smooth recovery energies tend to the free form when g_L->0; density
of smooth symmetric Dirichlet functions supplies the general recovery
sequence. This is the exact point at which the isometric embedding matters:
K itself is only a contraction, but its discrepancy from U vanishes on
bounded-energy sequences.

The compact-form/resolvent route is sound provided the interpolation
estimates (2.3),(3.2) from continuum_limit.txt are used as stated. They are
finite-grid estimates: a fixed edge belongs to at most 3^m local stencil
stars, and the contact comparison is on the full labeled grid, not the
ordered chamber. The norm-resolvent argument uses collective compactness
of bounded-energy resolvent images, the liminf/recovery pair, and
self-adjointness; compactness of the continuum resolvent upgrades strong
resolvent convergence to operator norm. For g_L->0 the common domain and
continuous diagonal trace form also give continuity of the continuum
resolvent. This handles parameter subsequences and yields compact-parameter
uniformity.

Operator-norm convergence of the resolvents implies operator-norm
convergence of heat operators by continuous functional calculus applied to
f_tau(s)=exp[-tau(s^-1-1)] with f_tau(0)=0. It alone would not imply trace
norm. The half-temperature bound is the necessary second ingredient: the
all-sector estimate from proof.txt, applied in a fixed sector and with
g_L>=0, gives a uniform bound B_m on Tr exp[-(tau/2)A_L]. Then

 sum_{j>K}e^{-tau a_{L,j}}
    <= B_m exp[-(tau/2)a_{L,K+1}].

Eigenvalue convergence and diverging continuum eigenvalues make these tails
uniformly small. A finite-rank block plus convergence of total traces gives
trace-norm convergence for positive heat operators; this is a valid
positive-operator argument and does not conflate operator norm with trace
norm. Finally, Theorem A supplies a uniform grand-canonical number tail on
each compact lambda set below lambda_c. Summing finitely many fixed-m
trace-norm limits and then sending the tail cutoff to infinity gives the
full Fock trace-norm limit. For lambda>=lambda_c, the fixed-sector limits
and the first-mode m-boson state give arbitrarily large finite partial
partition sums; the low-mass numerator stays bounded, so the claimed
failure of number tightness follows.

The prior continuum_limit.txt is stated for g bounded away from zero. Its
contact coercivity cannot be imported here. RESULT.txt's new contact
estimate (16) is sufficient to replace that one role; the rest of the
grid/interpolation argument is at fixed m and its constants may depend on
m. The subsequent infinite-m sum is separately handled by the new
all-sector tail theorem. I found no need to make any fixed-m constant
uniform in m.

§8: negative marginal sufficient region
---------------------------------------
For g_L=-kappa/L, scaled energy is A_L-kappa W-lambda N. Since W<=N,
sector min-max gives the exact comparator
Z_{L,m}(-kappa/L,lambda)<=Z_{L,m}(0,lambda+kappa). The positive-side
tail theorem therefore gives tightness when lambda+kappa<lambda_c. In a
fixed m sector, W<=m-1, so bounded energy of A_L-kappa W implies bounded
critical A_L energy. The contact estimate again yields <W>=O_m(L^-1); the
negative perturbation vanishes on bounded-energy sequences and on smooth
recovery vectors. Thus the free fixed-sector limit remains at original
lambda. Combining it with the comparator tail proves the stated sufficient
full-Fock limit. The argument does not identify a boundary outside this
region.

§9: entropy at speed L
----------------------
With kappa,lambda bounded, W,N<=L-1 implies E_min in [-C/L,0]. Also
H_L=Hcrit-R_L with R_L<=C/L, so

 H_L >= alpha Hcrit+(1-alpha)E_min I-alpha C/L I.

Min-max gives log Z_L<=-tau(1-alpha)L^2E_min+tau alpha C L+
log T_L(alpha tau), while the ground state gives log Z_L>=-tau L²E_min.
Since E_min<=0, these imply

 0 <= [log Z_L+tau L²E_min]/L <= tau alpha C+o(1).

Taking L first and then alpha down to zero proves (31), without a ground
energy limit assumption. Signs and scaling check.

Remaining mathematical gate
---------------------------
The candidate full-Fock zero-coupling theorem is supported by a coherent
compactness/recovery/tail proof. The unresolved high-ceiling question is
different: at negative marginal coupling g_L=-kappa/L, obtain a matching
lower bound for the speed-L ground energy against the coherent
Landau--Lifshitz functional, or a counterexample to that functional. The
coherent product profile supplies only a variational upper bound on the
ground energy (and therefore a partition lower bound). No quantum phase
boundary outside lambda+kappa<lambda_c follows without that lower bound.
