FERMIONIC ALGORITHMS — LEAD SYNTHESIS
8 October 2026

RESULT

The unqualified determinant/BCS occupation-sampling barrier is too broad: ordinary Fock-basis sampling of an explicitly supplied rational fermionic Gaussian state is exactly polynomial-time. The hard boundary is conditioning on an interacting occupation filter or obtaining its weighted completion counts. We now have an explicit exact-hardness reduction for general parity filters, and an even sharper CNF-filter reduction; neither proves hardness of the particular no-double-occupancy BCS problem in N85–N89. The all-size Hurwitz exchange in N84 remains a structural support/capacity result, not a sampler or conductance theorem.

The next decisive target is therefore a restricted weighted completion-count theorem for the N85/N89 local pair filter, or a hardness reduction for that exact local interface. Generic exchange lemmas and Gaussian covariance updates have reached their scope limits.

1. EXACT BARE-GAUSSIAN SAMPLER

Let rho be an n-mode fermionic Gaussian state, pure or mixed, supplied by an exact rational Majorana covariance Γ with Γ_ab=(i/2)Tr(rho[γ_a,γ_b]). Measure occupations in any fixed order. For the next mode j, put p=2j−1, q=2j, g=Γ_pq and s=2x_j−1. Then

  Pr(s | prefix)=(1+s g)/2.

For remaining indices a,b, after observing s with positive probability, update

  Γ^s_ab = Γ_ab − s(Γ_ap Γ_bq − Γ_aq Γ_bp)/(1+s g).

The occupation projector is (I+s iγ_pγ_q)/2. The first formula follows by taking its trace; the second follows by Wick expansion of Tr(rho iγ_aγ_b(I+s iγ_pγ_q)/2) and division by the branch probability. A single-mode occupation projector is a fermionic Gaussian map, so the conditional state remains Gaussian. The chain rule proves the exact output law.

This gives O(n^3) rational arithmetic operations and O(n^2) storage. With covariance input of numerator/denominator height at most ℓ bits per entry, prefix probabilities and conditional covariances have polynomial bit height; one conservative bound from the Pfaffian expansion is O(n^3ℓ) bits per reduced quantity. Exact rational Bernoulli sampling has expected polynomial random-bit cost. A finite-coin approximation with K=ceil(log2(n/ε)) bits per branch has output TV error at most ε and worst-case polynomial runtime. Rare prefixes can have exponentially small probability, but their exact rational descriptions need only polynomially many bits. Floating-point implementations need a separate propagated error bound because the update divides by 2Pr(s|prefix).

An explicitly encoded rational complex pair matrix F gives a rational covariance by matrix inversion, so arbitrary phases and both parity sectors are covered. This is established fermionic-linear-optics simulation machinery (Bravyi; Terhal–DiVincenzo), not a new sampler claim. There is no physical energy expenditure in this classical sampler; preparation of the input state is a separate charged operation.

A further exact extension samples an arbitrary supplied BCS state conditioned on a specified total particle number N=r. For a prefix h of length t with k occupied modes, define

  Q_h(z)=Tr[rho (product over i≤t of Π_{h_i})
                   (product over i>t of (Π_0+z Π_1))].

Wick’s rule and the Pfaffian minor identity evaluate Q_h(z) as one Pfaffian of a 2n-by-2n matrix polynomial of degree at most n−t. The mass of completions of h having total N=r is [z^(r−k)]Q_h(z). The next-bit branch masses are the corresponding coefficients for h0 and h1; they sum exactly to the parent coefficient. Interpolate from n+1 integer evaluations, compute exact rational coefficients, and sample their ratio. Recomputing both child polynomials at each depth costs O(n^5) rational field operations with polynomial bit lengths. This removes inverse-sector-probability rejection for a global number constraint. The derivation is internally checked against the Pfaffian/Wick identities but has not been implemented or externally reviewed; no novelty claim is made.

A global fixed-number condition is not the N85 hard filter. For opposite-spin pairing, the hard target additionally requires I∩J=∅ (no site contains both spins). That local two-orbital factor does not separate into one-mode Gaussian factors. For example, projecting (|00>+|11>)_A(|00>+|11>)_B/2 onto exactly one pair gives (|1100>+|0011>)/sqrt(2), a non-Gaussian state with zero two-point covariance. The covariance update cannot recover its occupation law.

2. HARDNESS AND TRACTABLE PARITY BOUNDARIES

A product Slater with one fermion in each disjoint pair realizes a product Bernoulli law: for each i, choose the first or second mode with probabilities 1−p_i and p_i. For H over F_2 and syndrome s,

  Pr[Hx=s] = product_i(1−p_i) ·
             sum_{x:Hx=s} product_i z_i^(x_i),
  z_i=p_i/(1−p_i).

The right side is a binary-code coset weight enumerator. Dyer–Goldberg–Jerrum prove exact #P-hardness of the zero-syndrome weight enumerator at every fixed positive rational activity z≠1; at z=1 the probability is exactly 2^(-rank H). Graph-incidence zero-syndrome cases with 0≤z<1 reduce to ferromagnetic Ising and have an FPRAS; bounded-treewidth Tanner graphs have exact dynamic programs. These are sharp interface boundaries, not a general FPRAS-hardness claim for positive parity sectors.

For arbitrary CNF occupation filters, an even simpler one-query reduction gives

  Pr[filter accepts] = #SAT(formula)/2^m.

It holds on a block-product rational BCS instance with 3m modes and a rational orthonormal Slater instance with 4m modes. All unfiltered blocks have four equiprobable configurations and one designated bit is fair. Exact filtered-event evaluation is #P-hard; an FPRAS for this CNF-filtered probability would imply an FPRAS for #SAT and hence NP=RP by the Jerrum–Valiant–Vazirani self-reduction. This is a general-filter result. The unfiltered states remain trivially samplable, and this reduction does not establish hardness for N85’s fixed local no-doublon constraint.

3. EXCHANGE DOES NOT SUPPLY THE MISSING COUNT

The internally derived N84 theorem is: for a nonzero multiaffine polynomial h with nonnegative coefficients, Hurwitz-stable on the open right half-plane and supported on one parity, for any supported S,T, any a∈S△T,

  sqrt(f(S) f(T)) ≤
    sum over j∈(S△T)\{a} sqrt(f(S△{a,j}) f(T△{a,j})).

The constant one is sharp. This yields connectivity of the aggregate ambient-hole support under pair flips, but does not give polynomial conductance, a polynomial-time weight oracle, or a microscopic Slater/BCS transition sampler.

Two exact route falsifiers clarify the gap. First, a two-mode pair matrix F_t=[[0,t],[-t,0]] has the same connected hole support for every t≠0 while its two weights are |t|² and 1. Support exchange alone cannot recover relative weights. Second, for a directed 2q-cycle permutation matrix in the fixed k=q pair-label sector, exactly two alternating supports survive. Every move changing fewer than q pair labels is disconnected. The obstruction is to the named local walk, not to every possible algorithm. N87’s finite-field method acquires exact support, zero feasibility, and a witness at polynomial bit cost; it does not acquire determinant-squared weights.

4. CONDITIONAL COUNTING ROUTES AND LOSS RESULTS

The Chen–Liu primary theorem is reconstructible at the needed interface. For complementary homogeneous nonnegative log-concave measures μ,ν on an N-element ground set, with rational value oracles, coefficient-range bounds and positive support starts, their Theorem 5.1 estimates

  [x^U]g_μ(x)g_ν(x)=sum_S μ(S)ν(U\S)

to relative error η in O(N^10 B^2 η^−2 log²(2B/η)) arithmetic/oracle operations, B=N+log(R_μR_ν), with a bounded every-execution bit implementation in Remark A.4. For N63’s quota-filtered Slater, f(x)=det(A*diag(x)A) and g(x)=product_j e_(|E_j|−q_j)(x_Ej) meet these hypotheses. Prefix inclusion/exclusion preserves the class; matroid intersection supplies positive starts and zero branches. This removes an opaque supplied coefficient oracle for that filtered-Slater interface, conditional on the source theorem and exact support/reversible arithmetic routines. The end-to-end coherent compiler remains unimplemented and expensive: the count component scales as O(m^13 B² ε^−2 log²(32Bm/ε) log(m/(δε))) up to support, phase, reversible arithmetic and gate-synthesis costs. It does not extend to generic BCS Pfaffians, hard local no-double occupancy, or coherent sums without a new log-concave representation.

As a separate output-law result, for independent uniform vacuum loss with per-mode survival η_i on pure two-fermion blocks, the recorded Gaussian completion yields a positive sampler with block error
  δ_i ≤ η_i^4/[2(1−η_i)^2]
and joint TV error at most sum_i δ_i plus separately charged arithmetic error. The balanced four-mode example has sharp local scale Θ(η^4); three-fermion blocks have a cubic obstruction, and observed loss flags change the scale to η². This concerns approximate output sampling under a small-loss condition. Signed estimators reach a larger logarithmic regime for bounded observables but do not give positive samples. It does not solve the local filtered count.

5. STATUS AND NEXT BOTTLENECK

The exact Gaussian prefix sampler and parity-code reductions are checkable mathematical derivations, with established Gaussian-simulation prior art. The N84 exchange and N100–N108 loss claims remain internal proof candidates/source-derived evidence with external correctness and priority unresolved. Chen–Liu’s theorem is a conditional imported result: its relevant proof and finite-bit cap were audited at the decisive sections, but not proof-assistant certified; no FPRAS or reversible quantum compiler was implemented. Source-reported scripts were not replayed in this programme.

The concrete open gate is a polynomial-time relative approximation or exact dynamic program for the completion weight

  Z_h = sum over pair-label completions C consistent with prefix h
        |det F[I(C),J(C)]|², with I(C)∩J(C)=∅,

or a reduction proving sharp hardness for this precise local filter. A successful positive route must supply a log-concave/pseudopolynomial representation, support starts, bit-range bounds, and a microscopic transition or coefficient oracle. A hardness route must use the fixed local filter itself, not an arbitrary CNF predicate. The exact 2q-cycle, soft-parity and replica-sewing witnesses in N86 only rule out their named walks/overlays; they do not settle this target.
