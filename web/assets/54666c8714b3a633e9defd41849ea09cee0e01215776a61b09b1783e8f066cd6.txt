EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE AND HISTORICAL INTERFACE

Gamma G=G*Gamma, Gamma(X)=sqrt(sigma)Xsqrt(sigma). At p=1 their strong
regularity condition requires J>=4E, where, writing L=-G,
 J=Tr[L_*(rho)(logrho-logsigma)],
 E=Tr[sqrt(rho) Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2)(sqrt(rho))].
The manuscript and published journal convention were checked explicitly.
GNS symmetry is a stronger condition and cannot be substituted silently.

A particularly short witness uses

0,1/2,9/2,5. The state is faithful. Exact algebra gives

CONSTRUCTIVE POSITIVE ADVANCES

A complete analytic proof establishes J>=2E for every KMS-symmetric qubit
quantum Markov generator and every faithful qubit state, without primitivity

Every stationary KMS qubit channel follows by Poissonization, including
channels with negative weighted spectrum. Singular states and arbitrary
classical-reference alphabets have explicit separate closures. The theorem
does not cover entangled quantum references or higher system dimension.

The proof represents all finite KMS generators by Hermitian noises with a
Lyapunov drift, reduces qubit states and noise to exact Bloch formulas, and
uses concavity plus a hyperbolic kernel inequality. That scalar inequality
has an independent blind elementary proof. A stronger full PSD kernel has

general-dimensional operator whose positivity would prove J>=2E is now
explicit; a tempting stronger CP-generator lift is exactly refuted even
in a commuting three-dimensional example. This is a failed sufficient route,
not a counterexample to the endpoint itself.
