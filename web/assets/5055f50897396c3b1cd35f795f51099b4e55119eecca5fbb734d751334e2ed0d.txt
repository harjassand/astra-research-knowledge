STRONGEST CLAIM AT THIS CHECKPOINT
I independently reconstruct the supplied finite-ensemble implication: if E={rho_x}_{x=1}^m is a finite ensemble and one CPTP B:H->H tensor H has both marginal trace errors at most epsilon on every supplied state, then for every integer L>=0 there exists one EB map Lambda:H->H with

  max_x T(Lambda(rho_x),rho_x) <= L epsilon + sqrt(m ln(m)/(2*2^L)).

For an a-net of a general family E_n with m=N_n(a) centers and optimal two-output error b_n, channel contraction gives

  e_n <= 2a + L b_n + sqrt(m ln(m)/(2*2^L)).

Thus a_n->0 and b_n ln N_n(a_n)->0 suffice for e_n->0. The construction is an existence theorem with very large costs. It does not make the root-fidelity/affinity implication constructive: N46's removal of a common reference for the intrinsic-gap route still uses a qualitative fixed-m ultraproduct mixture lemma.

DERIVATION RECONSTRUCTED
1. Iterate B on every leaf of a binary tree of depth L. This gives C_L:H->H^(tensor K), K=2^L, with K-1 calls to B. Each leaf marginal is a composition of L marginal channels of B. By trace-distance contractivity and induction, each leaf is within L epsilon of rho_x for each supplied x; no product approximation or independent-clone property is assumed.

2. Fix any design prior pi on the m labels. Imagine adaptively measuring the leaves. At stage j and history h, write p_x=P(X=x|h), sigma_x^h for the conditional state of leaf j, tau_h=sum_x p_x sigma_x^h, and d_x=T(sigma_x^h,tau_h). Choose x_h maximizing p_x d_x^2, and measure the two-outcome positive spectral projector of sigma_(x_h)^h-tau_h. Its output laws under sigma_(x_h)^h and tau_h have total variation exactly d_(x_h). Classical Pinsker gives the conditional mutual-information gain at least 2 p_(x_h)d_(x_h)^2. Since max_x p_x d_x^2 >= m^(-1) sum_x p_x d_x^2 and sum_x p_x d_x^2 >= (sum_x p_x d_x)^2, the gain is at least (2/m)(sum_x p_x d_x)^2.

3. The chain rule sums the K gains to at most H(pi)<=ln(m). Hence some leaf j has prior-average conditional distance at most sqrt(m ln(m)/(2K)). The reconstruction channel measures only the prefix leaves 1,...,j-1 and prepares tau_h on leaf j. Nonselective operations on the prefix leave leaf j's original marginal unchanged. The prior-average trace error is therefore at most L epsilon plus the displayed conditional-distance term.

4. The selected leaf may depend on pi. The EB channel set is compact and convex in fixed finite dimensions, and the risk is continuous/convex in the channel and linear in pi. Sion minimax gives one channel with the same bound simultaneously for all labels. The cited package also derives a finite alphabet bound by compactness and Caratheodory; that does not make its acquisition efficient.

5. For the cover extension, reconstruct the m net states and extend to any rho within distance a of a center: T(Lambda rho,rho)<=T(Lambda rho,Lambda rho_i)+T(Lambda rho_i,rho_i)+T(rho_i,rho)<=2a+net_error. Choosing L approximately log_2(m ln(m)/(2 b_n^2)) yields the explicit O(b_n[log(1/b_n)+log m+log ln(m)]) term in the supplied theorem. This proves the stated sufficient condition, not its necessity.

ATTACK ON A NATURAL STRONGER LOCAL ESTIMATE
The factor m in the posterior-information step cannot be replaced by ln(m) using only a universal accessible-information-versus-average-trace-distance inequality. A primary-source quantum-locking ensemble gives an exact obstruction.

For q qubits, D=2^q and y in {1,2,3}^q, let Gamma_y be the corresponding tensor Pauli string. For x in {0,1}, set

  sigma_(x,y)=D^(-1)(I+(-1)^x Gamma_y).

These are density matrices with eigenvalues 0 and 2/D; their uniform average is I/D and every member has T(sigma_(x,y),I/D)=1/2. For q>=2 their pairwise trace distance is at least 1/2: distinct commuting Pauli strings give 1/2, anticommuting strings give 1/sqrt(2), and opposite signs of the same string give 1. For an anticommuting pair, direct projector algebra gives root fidelity 1/sqrt(2), affinity 1/2, and Delta=1/sqrt(2)-1/2, so the ensemble itself has a dimension-independent EB lower bound Delta^2/8 by N46's necessity inequality.

Koenig, Renner, Bariska and Maurer prove for the uniform label Z=(X,Y) that I_acc(Z;E) <= (2/3)^(q/2). Since |Z|=2*3^q and the average trace distance to the mean is 1/2, this contradicts any universal one-stage inequality of the form

  I_acc(Z;E) >= c [E_Z T(sigma_Z,bar_sigma)]^2 / ln |Z|

for fixed c>0: its right side is order 1/q, while the established upper bound is exponentially small. A shared depolarizing perturbation preserves the accessible-information upper bound by data processing and makes every state faithful if that interface is desired.

This refutes the obvious route that would replace the current posterior estimate (gain >= (2/m) average_distance^2) with a gain >= c average_distance^2/ln(m). Such a replacement would lead, through the same leaf argument, to a depth O(log ln m) and a condition like b log log m->0. The locking example blocks that local lemma. It does NOT refute every stronger global broadcasting-to-EB theorem; a different proof might use constraints across the whole tree or additional map structure. This is a precise obstruction to one tempting strengthening, not a counterexample to the current finite-cover theorem.

COSTS, INTERFACES, AND LIMITS
The finite-ensemble protocol uses K=2^L output systems and K-1 broadcaster calls, with the explicit sharper choice K on the order of m ln(m)/epsilon^2. Conditional joint matrices, spectral sign measurements, adaptive posterior calculations and conditional-state preparation may be exponentially large. Minimax supplies one worst-case channel but no efficient acquisition. Cover-net discovery is also charged. The result concerns full marginal states on supplied inputs; it does not preserve purifications, guarantee diamond-norm channel approximation, provide independent clones, or act on unpromised states. Root-gap -> EB remains qualitative at growing covers. The locking posterior obstruction stops a simple log(m) improvement but does not prove the current entropy condition optimal.
