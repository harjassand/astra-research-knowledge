1. COMMON BOUNDED PROPER-LOSS TRANSFER

This checks the load-bearing lower step in c09_l04, c09_l07 and c09_l10.
Let mu be a reference and the two alternatives have likelihoods 1 +/- s U,
where E_mu U=0, |U|<=1 and 0<s<=1. The posterior sign probability is
p=(1+s U)/2. Put F(1/2)=0 and F'(p)=clip((2p-1)/s,-1,1). On the posterior
interval, F(p)=(p-1/2)^2/s. Its canonical proper losses are
 l_0(a)=a F'(a)-F(a)+1/2-s/4,
 l_1(a)=(a-1)F'(a)-F(a)+1/2-s/4.
For a in [0,1] both losses are in [0,1]. On the relevant interval their
extremal values are 0 and 1; outside it the linear continuation of F makes
the corresponding loss constant. The posterior regret is the Bregman
divergence of F, hence exactly s(U(X)-U(Y))^2/4.

For ONE common blind encoder/decoder with TV error <=epsilon under BOTH
alternatives, use its actual coupling (X,Y) and the action a=p(Y). Risk for
the reconstructed observation differs from the optimal original-observation
risk by <=epsilon, because l_theta(p(y)) is bounded in [0,1]. Conditional
regret under X is nonnegative and equal to the displayed quadratic. Thus
 E_mu (U(X)-U(Y))^2 <=4epsilon/s.
This uses output-law accuracy and the common channel. It does not suppose
the decoder knows the sign, parameter or input posterior.

If U has finite differential entropy h(U), conditional maximum entropy and
data processing give I(U;Y,seed)>=h(U)-.5 log(2 pi e*4epsilon/s).
For independent reference features U_1,...,U_d and any single D-dimensional
hybrid memory Q with independent seed S, cq strong subadditivity gives
 sum_i I(U_i;Q|S)<=I(U_1,...,U_d;Q|S)<=log D.
Coordinate-specific measurements after storage only reduce the left terms.
One does not assume compatible or repeated access. These steps validate the
scoped lower mechanisms; their application requires the asserted independence
under the SAME reference law, not orthogonality of Fisher features.

multi-index alpha is (-1)^|alpha| (|alpha|!/alpha!) tau^(|alpha|+2)

 partial^alpha F_ij(0)=(-1)^|alpha| |alpha|! tau^(|alpha|+2)
