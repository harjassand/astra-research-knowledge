THEOREM AND LEGAL INTERFACE

Fix an integer d>=2. Let H_N=(C^d)^{tensor N}, n=d^N, and let A_N be the
algebra commuting with all tensor-factor permutations. Define

 D=binom(N+d^2-1,d^2-1),
 C_iid=conv{sigma^{tensor N}: sigma>=0, Tr sigma=1},
 distance_iid(R)=min_{S in C_iid} (1/2)||R-S||_1.                     (1)

(a) Given a rational Hermitian Rtilde in A_N, in the explicit coordinates
below, with Tr Rtilde=1 and ||Rtilde||_op<=2, and rational 0<eps<1, one can
compute rational one-qudit states sigma_j and dyadic weights w_j>=0,
sum_j w_j=1, with at most D nonzero weights, in time polynomial in

 N, eps^{-1}, and the input bit length L, for each fixed d.              (2)

The output S_hat=sum_j w_j sigma_j^{tensor N} satisfies

 (1/2)||Rtilde-S_hat||_1
       <= min_{S in C_iid}(1/2)||Rtilde-S||_1 + eps/2.                 (3)

The target need not itself be PSD in (a). This allows a rational approximation
to a Gibbs state, with its independently charged approximation error.

(b) Suppose a known Hermitian permutation-invariant K has rational orbit
coordinates and a supplied certified numerical bound ||K||_op<=h. If h is
polynomial in N and L, a rational trace-one Rtilde with

 (1/2)||Rtilde-exp(K)/Tr exp(K)||_1 <= eps/32                            (4)

is computable in polynomial time in N,L,h,log(1/eps). In particular, fixed-
coefficient quadratic collective Hamiltonians below have h=O(N), and the
compiler produces

 T(exp(K)/Tr exp(K),S_hat) <= distance_iid(exp(K)/Tr exp(K))+eps.        (5)

Fixed algebraic or polynomial-time computable local coefficients are also
allowed with supplied evaluators and norm bounds, by the perturbation step
in Section 5. Arbitrary uncomputable public reals are not a finite-bit input.
Unbounded binary-encoded couplings do not acquire a runtime polynomial only
in their bit length from this proof: h is charged. N is a numerical size;
the claim is not polynomial in log N.

All target parameters are supplied. No unknown-field/reference acquisition
or universal decoder is implied. A rational encoding of one-qudit states
and mixture probabilities is the output. An optional physical implementation
must supply a one-qudit preparation interface, charged in Section 9.

1. ACQUIRE THE POLYNOMIAL ALGEBRA WITHOUT A SCHUR ORACLE

Write E_ab=|a><b|, 1<=a,b<=d. For a d-by-d table m=(m_ab)>=0 with sum m_ab=N,
let B_m be the sum of all DISTINCT ordered tensor products of the E_ab
having exactly m_ab factors of each kind. The tensor products are matrix
units for the whole H_N. A permutation permutes these matrix units, and two
are in the same orbit exactly when their tables m agree. Therefore B_m are
a basis of A_N and their number is exactly D. No enumeration of N! site
permutations or d^N physical vectors is required to describe this basis.

The adjoint, Gram matrix, trace and identity are explicit:

 B_m^*=B_{m^T},
 Tr(B_m^* B_k)=1_{m=k} s_m,      s_m=N!/prod_ab m_ab!,
 Tr B_m=s_m if every off-diagonal m_ab is zero, and zero otherwise,
 I=sum_{m diagonal} B_m.                                               (6)

Let G=diag(s_m). We have 1<=s_m<=d^{2N}. All these integers have O_d(N)
bits; factorial intermediates can be computed with O(N log N) bits. An
explicit rational real basis of Hermitian elements uses B_m for m=m^T,
and B_m+B_{m^T}, i(B_m-B_{m^T}) for one representative of each other pair.
It has real dimension D. In this basis an element X of op norm <=c has each
coordinate bounded by c sqrt(n), since orthogonal matrix-unit orbit sums
have Hilbert-Schmidt norm at least one and ||X||_F<=sqrt(n)||X||_op.

For multiplication enumerate nonnegative three-index tables t_abc of sum N.
Their three margins are

 m_ab=sum_c t_abc, k_bc=sum_a t_abc, p_ac=sum_b t_abc.

Then

 B_m B_k=sum_p c(m,k,p) B_p,
 c(m,k,p)=sum_{t with these margins}
                    [prod_ac p_ac!]/[prod_abc t_abc!].                (7)

Proof: fix an output/input word pair of table p. An intermediate word b
contributes precisely when the two pair tables are m,k. For each output/
input letter pair (a,c), the number of assignments of the intermediate
letters with counts t_abc is its multinomial coefficient. Their product
is (7), independently of which pair of words has table p.

There are E=binom(N+d^3-1,d^3-1) triple tables. Thus the entire structural
tensor is acquired with E*poly(N,d) integer arithmetic operations and at
most D^3 stored entries, both polynomial for fixed d. Each c is at most
d^N, because it counts intermediate words for a fixed word pair. This
also bounds their bit length by O_d(N). A sparse storage is optional.

Let L_X be the D-by-D matrix of left multiplication by X, acquired from
(7). It is rational when X is rational; L_X L_Y=L_{XY}, L_I=I_D.

2. RATIONAL PSD TEST AND THE CORRECT PHYSICAL TRACE

For Hermitian X, G L_X is Hermitian, and

                 X>=0 on H_N iff G L_X>=0 on C^D.                    (8)

Indeed, if y are the coefficients of Y in A_N, then

 y^* G L_X y = Tr(Y^* X Y).

This is nonnegative for X>=0. Conversely, if X has a negative eigenvalue,
its negative spectral projector P_- is a polynomial in X (interpolate on
its finitely many distinct eigenvalues), so P_- belongs to A_N. Taking
Y=P_- makes the displayed trace strictly negative. This proves (8).
Moreover X>=c I implies G L_X>=c G>=c I_D. This supplies an explicit
interior margin. For a complex rational Hermitian matrix H, use its usual
realification [[Re H,-Im H],[Im H,Re H]] as a rational real symmetric LMI.

Do NOT replace the physical trace in (6) by Tr L_X. In Schur-Weyl notation

 H_N = direct-sum_{lambda partition N, length<=d} U_lambda tensor W_lambda,
 X = direct-sum X_lambda tensor I_{W_lambda},
 Tr_H X=sum_lambda f_lambda Tr X_lambda,
 ||X||_1=sum_lambda f_lambda ||X_lambda||_1.                           (9)

Here r_lambda=dim U_lambda is polynomial in N for fixed d, with Weyl formula

 r_lambda=prod_{i<j}(lambda_i-lambda_j+j-i)/(j-i),

and f_lambda=dim W_lambda=N!/prod_{cells} hook_length has O_d(N) bits.
Also sum r_lambda^2=D. Left multiplication instead repeats eigenvalues of
X_lambda r_lambda times, whereas their physical multiplicity is f_lambda.
The explicit trace functional (6) retains the latter correctly, with no
Schur basis, Clebsch-Gordan coefficients, or hidden basis-acquisition oracle.
Bacon–Chuang–Harrow supplies the standard Schur context and efficient
circuits, but (6)–(8) are the self-contained data construction used here.

3. POSITIVE RATIONAL ONE-QUDIT NET

Put delta=eps/(80dN), k=ceil(4d/delta), and p=d^2-1. Enumerate the p real
coordinates consisting of the first d-1 diagonal entries and the real/
imaginary parts of the upper off-diagonal entries, each on the lattice
{-k,...,k}/k. Make Q Hermitian and set its final diagonal entry so Tr Q=1.
Retain exactly the Q satisfying Q+delta I>=0, using all fixed-dimensional
principal minors. For every retained Q output

               sigma_Q=(Q+2delta I)/(1+2d delta).                     (10)

These are rational trace-one positive states, and their least eigenvalue
is at least delta/(1+2d delta). The enumeration contains at most

 M <= (2k+1)^p <= (c_d N/eps)^{d^2-1}                                 (11)

states, each with O_d(log(N/eps)+bit_length(eps)) bits per entry.

For any state rho, round the p independent coordinates to the nearest grid
point. Each independent real rounding error is at most 1/(2k); the final
diagonal error is at most (d-1)/(2k), and the off-diagonal complex error is
at most 1/k. The maximum absolute row sum is less than 2d/k<=delta/2, so
||Q-rho||_op<=delta/2. In particular this Q is retained. More conservatively,
||Q-rho||_1<=d delta, and

 ||sigma_Q-rho||_1
 <=[||Q-rho||_1+2delta||I-d rho||_1]/(1+2d delta)
 <=5d delta.                                                        (12)

Tensor telescoping, using norm-one positive tensor factors, gives

 T(sigma_Q^{tensor N},rho^{tensor N})<=5dNdelta/2=eps/32.                (13)

Consequently every iid-product mixture can be approximated by a mixture
of the enumerated nodes within eps/32. Mapping a continuous mixture to
the finite net is used only to prove existence of good weights; the weights
are actually acquired by the SDP. Compactness and finite-dimensional
Caratheodory also make the minimum in (1) attained.

For each node its exact orbit coordinates are particularly simple:

 sigma^{tensor N}=sum_m [prod_ab sigma_ab^{m_ab}] B_m.                 (14)

There is no multinomial factor in (14). Rational powering takes polynomial
time and O_d(N log(N/eps)+N*bit_length(eps)) bits. Combining (14),(7),(8)
acquires the complete rational SDP matrices, rather than assuming a product-
state block oracle.

4. THE FINITE PHYSICAL TRACE-NORM SDP

Let S_j=sigma_j^{tensor N}. For any Hermitian Delta in A_N,

 ||Delta||_1=min{Tr(P+Q): P,Q in A_N, P,Q>=0, P-Q=Delta}.              (15)

The usual inequality gives >=||Delta||_1; equality follows by taking its
positive and negative spectral parts, which are polynomials in Delta and
belong to A_N. Therefore solve the exact rational SDP

 minimize (1/2)Tr(P+Q),
 P-Q=Rtilde-sum_j w_j S_j,
 sum_j w_j=1, w_j>=0, G L_P>=0, G L_Q>=0.                            (16)

Its objective is the FULL physical half trace norm with the multiplicities
of (9), not a compressed-state norm. The rational PSD matrices have size
2D after realification, and all variables/data are polynomial in D,M,L.
Because Tr Delta=0, eliminating P=Q+Delta leaves objective Tr Q.
Existence of the net gives

 opt(16)<=min_{S in C_iid}T(Rtilde,S)+eps/32.                          (17)

5. ACQUIRING THE GIBBS TARGET IN POLYNOMIAL BITS

First suppose K is rational. Replace its norm bound by an integer h>=1.
Set A=K-hI, so its physical spectrum lies in [-2h,0]. Compute exactly in
the acquired algebra the rational Taylor polynomial

 W_r=sum_{j=0}^r A^j/j!.

One can update a coefficient vector by L_A and divide by j; no physical
matrix of size d^N is formed. The physical norm bound gives directly

 ||W_r-exp(A)||_1 <= n e^{2h}(2h)^{r+1}/(r+1)!.                      (18)

This estimate is made on H_N; a possibly poorly conditioned unweighted
regular matrix is not used to choose the Taylor degree. The exponential
itself has Z=Tr exp(A)>=n e^{-2h}>=z_min=2^{-(3h+1)}. Choose r so the
right side of (18) is <=eps z_min/64. For example a constant multiple of

 h + N ceil(log_2 d) + ceil(log_2(1/eps)) + 1

suffices, by r!>=(r/e)^r. A concrete safe choice is
r=ceil[6(5h+N ceil(log_2 d)+ceil(log_2(1/eps))+8)]. The factorial-tail
ratio is <1/2; the displayed choice bounds even twice the first term.
All powers and sums are rational. With a common denominator for the
input coefficients, their bit length is polynomial in r,D,L,N; the r!
and denominator powers add only O(r log r+r*input_bits). Thus exact
arithmetic is permitted in the claimed Turing bound, despite cancellation.

Let Z_r=Tr W_r, acquired by (6). It is positive since
|Z_r-Z|<=eps z_min/64 and Z>=z_min. Rtilde=W_r/Z_r is rational Hermitian
with exact trace one. If a=eps/64, normalization bounds

 ||W_r/Z_r-exp(A)/Z||_1 <= 4a = eps/16,

so T<=eps/32, as promised. Its op norm is <=1+eps/16<=2.
This needs O(N+h+log(1/eps)) precision/degree, not an inverse exponentially
small normalization probability in the running time. Exponentially large
physical multiplicities and exponentially small eigenvalues cost bits.

For fixed-coefficient quadratic collective inputs write

 F_ab=sum_i E_ab^{(i)}, F(T)=sum_ab T_ab F_ab,
 K=(1/N)sum_uv C_uv {F(T_u),F(T_v)}/2+F(B).                          (19)

Local matrices and coefficients determine K in the acquired algebra.
For completeness, the sparse left action is

 L_{F_aa} B_m=(sum_c m_ac) B_m,
 L_{F_ab} B_m=sum_{c:m_bc>0}(m_ac+1)B_{m-e_bc+e_ac}, a!=b.            (20)

It follows by replacing the output letter b by a. These matrices have at
most d nonzeros per column, all <=N. Fixed bounded local coefficients and
C,B give ||K||<=c_d N, and their assembly is polynomial. In the source's
normalization, its stated h_N<=H0 N can be used with a certified upper
bound for H0. We do not need to diagonalize or implement a Schur transform.

Fixed algebraic local matrices, such as the usual normalized SU(d) basis,
can be approximated by dyadic matrices. An O(log N+log(1/eps)) number of
accurate local bits makes ||K'-K||_op<=eps/32 for bounded coefficients in
(19). This approximation preserves Hermiticity. The Gibbs perturbation
bound T(rho_K,rho_{K'})<=||K-K'||_op follows by differentiating along
K_t=K+t(K'-K): Duhamel and Schatten Holder give

 ||d exp(K_t)/dt||_1 <= Tr(exp(K_t))||K'-K||_op,
 ||d rho_{K_t}/dt||_1 <=2||K'-K||_op.

This bound does not use the false assertion that the noncommuting matrix
exponential is operator monotone. The two target approximation steps then
cost at most eps/16 together. The norm bound for the rounded K is h+1.

6. EXPLICIT SLATER POINT AND BOUNDED DOMAIN

Eliminate w_M=1-sum_{j<M}w_j and P=Q+Delta(w). Let q be the D real Hermitian
coordinates of Q and take the uniform weights w_j=1/M. Define

 Delta_0=Rtilde-(1/M)sum_j S_j,
 Q_0=4I-Delta_0/2, P_0=4I+Delta_0/2.                                (21)

Since ||Rtilde||_op<=2 and the average S_j is a state, ||Delta_0||_op<=3.
Thus P_0,Q_0>=2I, and G L_{P_0},G L_{Q_0}>=2I_D. This is a rational
strictly feasible point with weights 1/M. Its entries have polynomial bits.

Add box constraints |q_l|<=R0, R0=32d^N. These do not change the optimum:
for any weights, taking Q=Delta_- has op norm <=3 and coordinates bounded
by 3 sqrt(n)<R0. The center (21) also has coordinates <6n, hence box margin
at least one. The weights already lie in [0,1]. This bounded feasible set
has outer parameter-space radius at most R=4R0(D+M+1).

Let Amax>=1 be a rational upper bound on the absolute value of every entry
of each Hermitian basis LMI and each node LMI, all explicitly computed.
The bound log Amax is polynomial in N,L,log(N/eps), by (6),(7),(14).
Matrix perturbations from a parameter-space Euclidean ball of radius r
are bounded by 3(D+M+1)^2 Amax r; the simplex's last coordinate changes
by at most M r. Thus, for example,

 r=[100(D+M+1)^3(Amax+1)]^{-1}                                      (22)

is a known interior ball around (21), also inside the boxes and simplex.
Consequently log(R/r) is polynomial in the charged input bits. An
exponentially thin physical cone is allowed; it is not an uncharged
condition number because only its logarithmic radius enters the theorem.

To convert to standard rational primal SDP form, take block diagonal
matrices consisting of realified G L_Q, G L_P, the M scalar weights, and
the 2D box slacks R0+q_l,R0-q_l. Encode the affine relations with rational
equalities. The cone-matrix size is 6D+M. The box/weight scalar coordinates
make the affine map injective, with explicitly bounded inverse; parameter
and Frobenius radii change by factors with polynomial logarithmic bits.
The objective Tr Q can be read linearly from the box slacks. Redundant
equalities can be removed by rational Gaussian elimination. Thus the
standard SDP theorem's relative-interior and boundedness hypotheses are
actually supplied, rather than inferred from an arbitrary solver status.

7. CHARGED IMPORTED SDP THEOREM

De Klerk–Vallentin (2016; arXiv:1507.03549v2), Theorem 1.1, states the
Turing weak-optimization theorem for rational primal SDP data. Given a
rational feasible center and rational inner/outer radii in its affine
subspace, it returns an EXACTLY feasible rational point with additive
objective error zeta in time polynomial in the matrix/equality sizes,
data/center bits, log(R/r), and log(1/zeta). It attributes the ellipsoid
version to Grotschel–Lovasz–Schrijver and supplies an interior-point proof
with bounded intermediate bit sizes. This precise imported theorem,
not the unsupported slogan 'SDP is polynomial', is used here.

Apply it to Section 6 with zeta=eps/8. The output weights are rational
and genuinely feasible, so (15) bounds their physical trace distance.
All inputs to this theorem have polynomial bits and polynomial size in
D,M,E,N,L. No strict feasibility of an optimal boundary point is required.
The exact rational feasibility guarantee is an algorithmic theorem; the
floating NumPy fixture does not implement that guarantee.

8. CONSTRUCTIVELY REDUCE TO D NODES; MAKE SAMPLING FINITE-BIT

The states S_j live in a trace-one real affine space of dimension D-1.
Starting from the acquired rational weights, while support exceeds D,
find a nonzero integer vector v supported on it satisfying

 sum_j v_j S_j=0, sum_j v_j=0.

Rational Gaussian elimination on the explicit columns acquires such v.
It has both signs. Set theta=min_{v_j>0} w_j/v_j and replace w by w-theta v.
Weights stay nonnegative, total mass and the represented state stay fixed,
and one weight becomes zero. Integer v can be chosen with determinant
bit bounds polynomial in D and column bits. Using a common denominator
for w, each step multiplies it only by one integer v_j. At most M steps
therefore retain polynomial total bit length. This is a charged finite
support algorithm, not ambient d^{2N}-dimensional Caratheodory.

Let K_out<=D be the resulting support. Round its first K_out-1 weights
down to multiples of 2^{-b}, assigning the remaining mass to the last
node, where b=ceil(log_2(16D/eps)). Their l1 error is <=2D*2^{-b}, so
the output mixture's half trace distance changes by <=eps/16. The
weights now have a common dyadic denominator. Sampling uses exactly b
fair bits and a cumulative-integer interval lookup, without rejection.
All one-qudit nodes remain exactly rational PSD by (10).

The net, optimizer and weight-rounding errors are eps/32, eps/8, eps/16.
This is <eps/2 in (a). In (b), the Gibbs/local-coefficient approximations
add <=eps/16; triangle inequalities compare the optimum at Rtilde to
the optimum at the true target once more. Even charging that comparison
twice, the complete budget remains <eps. Thus (3) and (5) follow.

9. SPIN OPEN-BALL CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITS

Use c04_s02's exact normalization Tr T_a T_b=delta_ab/2, not c04_s01's
different generator normalization. For fixed public d,beta>0,B0>=0 set

 D0=3 beta(d-1)+d B0,
 H0=2 beta(d-1)/d+B0,
 L0=sqrt(4 beta d(d-1)(H0+2)),
 eta=exp[-d log d-D0-L0], a_star=beta eta^2/10.

The peer theorem states that, for N>=d+1, known real symmetric A with
||A||<=a_star and known traceless Hermitian B with ||B||<=B0,

 K_N=(beta Q_I+Q_A)/N+J_B

has distance_iid(rho_N)<=delta_N=min(1,H0 N exp(-2N)). Conditional on
that existence proof, the above fully acquired compiler gives

 T(rho_N,S_hat)<=min(1,delta_N+eps),
 runtime poly_d(N,eps^{-1},L), support <=binom(N+d^2-1,d^2-1).          (23)

The public reals must have evaluators/certified upper bounds. One can use
any smaller certified rational anisotropy radius; the theorem's boundary
membership is a promise, not an unprovided exact transcendental comparison
algorithm. The compiler itself also runs outside the admitted ball, but
then (23)'s exponentially small delta_N is not justified.

Output length, not d^{2N}, is polynomial. The mesh still costs a power of
1/eps: asking for eps comparable to exp(-2N) costs exp(O_d(N)). This
proof by itself does NOT give polynomial-in-N synthesis at the source's
exponential accuracy, or remove its error floor. The proposed small-N
full-separability fallback is a different route, being audited by c08_s03;
its parity-ball correction need not preserve the iid-product class.

Optional physical use: after sampling one dyadic outcome, prepare N copies
of its supplied one-qudit density, sharing only the classical label. If
a fixed-d one-qudit circuit preparer is supplied at trace error <=eps/(8N),
its N calls add <=eps/8 by tensor telescoping. Standard purification from
sqrt(sigma_j), fixed-dimensional rotations, and a universal gate compiler
can provide this interface; their finite-precision work and N output
systems must be charged. No apparatus, preparation code or hardware test
has been acquired/executed in this fixture. The classical compiler result
is independent of a physical gate library and does not require weighted
SDE simulation, stopping-time detection, or exp(N) path rejection.

10. RUNNABLE BOUNDED EVIDENCE

Own script: commutant_compiler_fixture.py; owned JSON:
commutant_compiler_checks.json. Python/NumPy only, BLAS thread count one.
The small verifier enumerates physical matrices ONLY to check the scalable
algebra. It checked 2,525 integer basis-product identities at (d,N)=(2,2),
(2,3),(3,2), the Gram and physical trace exactly, eight random Hermitian
PSD spectra per case, and every collective matrix-unit action in (20).
The general compiler construction does not use that enumeration.

At d=2,N=3 it actually optimized (16) with a floating log-det Newton
solver on the orbit algebra, using seven rational nodes: I/2 and the six
pure Pauli eigenstates. For K=J^2/3 it returned T~5.94e-10. Independently,
the exact seven-node mixture has total pure-node weight
(e-1)/(e+1), equally divided among the six directions; the octahedron is
a degree-three design, so this is an exact analytical solution.

The nonadmitted antiferromagnetic control K=-J^2/3 returned
T~0.23106458, versus the analytical optimum
(e-1)/[2(e+1)]~0.23105858. Proof: every iid qubit product has
<J^2>=3N/4+N(N-1)|r|^2/4>=9/4, while the control has
<J^2>=3/4+3/(e+1). The J^2 spectral range is 3, hence
T>=1/2-1/(e+1), attained by the I/2 product. This is a negative control
for claiming all symmetric Gibbs states lie in the iid cone.

Floating SDP objectives upper-bounded the physical errors and had positive
P,Q eigenvalues; barrier gap bounds are nominal floating diagnostics,
not certified dual/Turing witnesses. Total runtime was about 0.21 seconds.
The implementation does not establish the peer spin generator theorem,
priority, or the whole compiler's practical runtime exponent.
