1. PRECISE INTERFACE AND THEOREM

weight, varrho for the half-sum of positive roots, and

  a=||Lambda||,
  C_Lambda=(Lambda,Lambda+2varrho),
  sum_a T_a^2=C_Lambda I,
  kappa=tau(T_a^2)=C_Lambda/r,  tau=Tr/d,
  B_a=T_a/sqrt(kappa),  W_Lie=span_R{B_a}.

Ad-invariance of tau(T_a T_b) and simplicity give tau(B_a B_b)=delta_ab.
The representation has no nonzero Lie algebra kernel since that kernel is
an ideal and U is nontrivial. Thus W_Lie is the real irreducible adjoint
copy of dimension r, even when End(H) contains other adjoint copies.

2. THE SHARP DIRECT LIE STAR

On the Choi reference bar(H)_R and outputs A,B, set

  W=sum_a B_a,R^T tensor(B_a,A tensor I_B+I_A tensor B_a,B).

Let

  mu_Lie=||Lambda||^2/C_Lambda.

Then the EXACT operator bound is

  W <= r(1+mu_Lie) I,                                      (4)

and its largest eigenvalue is exactly r(1+mu_Lie). This is a Casimir
identity plus two elementary weight-polytope inequalities, proved next.

The reference conjugate representation has generators bar(T)_a=-T_a^T;
this sign is essential. Decompose output H tensor H into irreducible
constituents V_nu. Then decompose bar(H) tensor V_nu into constituents
V_zeta. The output Casimir and total three-body Casimir commute, so W
acts on every such joint constituent (including multiplicities) by

  [C_Lambda+C_nu-C_zeta]/(2kappa).                          (5)

Indeed sum_a(bar(T)_a+T_a,A+T_a,B)^2 subtracts the reference and output
Casimirs to give twice the cross term. The minus sign in W yields (5).
The conjugate carrier has the same Casimir C_Lambda.

Every weight beta of V_Lambda lies in conv(WeylOrbit(Lambda)). One quick
proof is to test every linear functional h: Weyl-reflect h to the dominant
chamber, where Lambda-beta is a nonnegative sum of simple roots. Thus
(beta,h)<=max_w(w Lambda,h). In particular ||beta||<=a. Since nu is a
weight of H tensor H,

  ||nu||<=2a.                                              (6)

Frobenius reciprocity for the constituent V_zeta in bar(H) tensor V_nu
implies V_nu is a constituent of V_zeta tensor H. Therefore nu is a sum
of one weight of V_zeta and one of V_Lambda, giving

  ||nu||<=||zeta||+a.                                       (7)

Moreover nu<=zeta+Lambda in highest-weight dominance: their difference is
a nonnegative sum of simple roots. Since (alpha_i,varrho)>0 for every
simple root,

  (nu-zeta,varrho)<=(Lambda,varrho).                        (8)

Let x=||nu||, y=||zeta||. Equations(6)--(7) give x<=2a and
 y>=max(0,x-a). Hence

  x^2-y^2<=3a^2.                                           (9)

For x<=a the bound is x^2<=a^2; for a<=x<=2a it is
x^2-(x-a)^2=2ax-a^2<=3a^2. Combining (8)--(9),

  C_nu-C_zeta
   <=3a^2+2(Lambda,varrho)
    =C_(2Lambda)-C_Lambda.                                 (10)

Thus (5)<=C_(2Lambda)/(2kappa). Finally
C_(2Lambda)=2C_Lambda+2||Lambda||^2, so this is exactly r(1+mu_Lie),
proving(4). The proof is uniform in the root system and highest weight.
