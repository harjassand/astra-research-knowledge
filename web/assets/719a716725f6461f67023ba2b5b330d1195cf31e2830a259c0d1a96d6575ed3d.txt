## Exact claim

Fix rational rates `0 < ell <= K`. For every integer total `N >= 4`, consider
the four-channel network

    A+B -> 2A,   2A -> A+B,   A+B -> 2B,   2B -> A+B,

with falling-factorial propensities and four independent, possibly
history-dependent predictable rate controls, each taking values in `[ell,K]`.
The conserved class is `A+B=N`. Let

    C_N = {(a,b): a+b=N, a>=2, b>=2}.

For the first hitting time `tau_C_N`, an exact rational potential gives

    E_(a,b)[tau_C_N] <= h_N(a),

where

    delta_N = 1 / (ell N(N-1)),
    b_N     = 1 / (ell (N-1)^2) + K / (ell^2 N(N-1)^2),
    a_N     = b_N + delta_N,

and `h_N(0,N)=h_N(N,0)=a_N`, `h_N(1,N-1)=h_N(N-1,1)=b_N`, and `h_N=0`
on `C_N`. The bound is uniform over all admissible predictable rate
controllers. Since each positive term in `a_N` decreases with `N`, it also
gives the uniform-over-totals bound

    sup_(N>=4) sup_(a+b=N) E_(a,b)[tau_C_N]
      <= a_4 = 7/(36 ell) + K/(36 ell^2).

This is a constructive fixed-conservation-class result for this one network
family. Each class is finite and therefore nonexplosive. It does not prove a
general weak-reversibility, endotactic, or unbounded-state theorem and makes no
stationary-distribution assertion for non-Markov controllers.

## Pointwise generator proof

Write the state as `n=A`, so `B=N-n`. The potential is nonnegative and zero
on the target. For any state outside the target:

* At `n=0`, only `2B -> A+B` is enabled. Its propensity is `N(N-1)`, the
  potential increment is `b_N-a_N=-delta_N`, and its rate is at least `ell`.
  Thus `L_t h_N <= -ell N(N-1) delta_N = -1`. The state `n=N` is symmetric.
* At `n=1`, `A+B -> 2A` has propensity `N-1` and increment `-b_N`;
  `A+B -> 2B` has propensity `N-1` and increment `delta_N`; and
  `2B -> A+B` has propensity `(N-1)(N-2)` and increment `-b_N`.
  The other channel is disabled. Maximizing over the independent rate box
  therefore gives

      L_t h_N <= -ell (N-1)^2 b_N + K (N-1) delta_N = -1.

  The formula at `n=N-1` is obtained by swapping the species. These are the
  only states outside `C_N`, including when `N=4`.

The generator is affine in each rate, so the bounds hold at every point of the
box, not merely for constant rates. For each fixed `N` the state space is
finite and the potential bounded. Stopped Dynkin's formula gives
`E[t ^ tau_C_N] <= h_N(a)`; monotone convergence yields the claimed result.

## Scope and open gaps

The integer-lattice counterexample in INITIAL remains relevant: deterministic
boundary inward-pointing does not ensure that a target is in the stochastic
reaction class. Here the class is specified by fixed total `N`, and the
four-channel jumps connect the boundary states to `C_N`; the potential proof
verifies that explicitly. No claim is made for other stoichiometries,
non-rectangular or zero-lower-bound rate sets, higher molecularity, or general
endotactic networks. It remains an elementary one-network finite-class
certificate. Any priority or novelty claim is UNKNOWN.
