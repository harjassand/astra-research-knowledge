I reconstructed a new rational parameter schedule for family 029 which, **conditional on its exact normalized cubic-theta/reflection identities**, gives zero-freeness in `Re s>49/50`, instead of the released `Re s>1-10^-6`. This is a concrete analytic deduction with every contour exponent and Euler-convergence margin specified, not merely a proposed optimization. The exact-fraction certificate passes. The certificate does not verify the source's global automorphic realization or reflection identity; those remain the load-bearing correctness gate.

The strongest consequence requiring only the **released cyclotomic field scope** is the sharper auxiliary-prime theorem
\[
\ell_q\equiv1\pmod{12q},\quad
p^{(\ell_q-1)/q}\not\equiv1\pmod{\ell_q},\quad
\ell_q\ll[q(\log p+\log q+2)]^{50}.
\]
For family 142 this yields `E=O(B^50(1+log B)^50)`, with an absolute implied constant, instead of its `O(B^20000000)` auxiliary bound. The transfer is proved from a precisely stated fixed-width premise; it does not need my additional general-field extension. Its new accounting retains the `1/q` in the Kummer zeta difference rather than discarding that cancellation.

A separate, more consequential candidate extends the analytic architecture from cyclotomic fields to every number field containing mu12. I found no cyclotomic obstruction in the inspected arithmetic, quadratic-sieve, norm-sheet, or local Gauss-correction interfaces. If that extension survives the normalized theta/reflection review, it gives the same strip for all finite-order Hecke characters over **every number field**, and hence
\[
\text{least prime in any Frobenius class of }L/\mathbb Q
\ll(\log D_L+[L:\mathbb Q]+2)^{50}.
\]
This would provide deterministic polynomial-time Frobenius witness search for a Galois field when its defining polynomial, all automorphisms, and the requested conjugacy class are supplied. It is not a theorem for an arbitrary short polynomial with its possibly factorial-size Galois closure concealed.

No external proof validation or priority finding has been established. ## The sharper Kummer transfer

The complete proof, independent of the general-field extension, is in `SHARPER_AUXILIARY_PRIMES.md`.

For primes p,q with `p not dividing 12q`, let `K=Q(mu12q)` and `M=K(p^(1/q))`. The cyclic extension has relative degree q. Abelian factorization expresses zeta_M through finite-order Hecke L-functions of the cyclotomic base K. Thus a fixed strip of width delta in the released field scope suffices for both fields.

The uniform smoothed explicit formula gives
\[
\Psi_K(x)-q^{-1}\Psi_M(x)
=(1-q^{-1})x\Phi(1)+O(x^{1-\delta}A_q),
\]
where the correct parameter is
\[
A_q=\log D_K+d_K+q^{-1}(\log D_M+d_M)
\ll q(\log p+\log q+2).
\]
The proof uses `d_M=q d_K` and the relative power-basis discriminant bound before simplifying. The coefficient `1/q` cancels the extra Kummer factor. With `x=T[q(log p+log q+2)]^(1/delta)`, the main term dominates uniformly for sufficiently large absolute T. Higher powers, higher residue degrees and the four excluded rational primes are smaller uniformly because delta is below 1/2. Complete splitting primes cancel exactly; a positive difference therefore forces the required nonresidue prime in `(x,2x)`.

The source's field-dependent onset and theta constants disappear from this transfer. A globally established strip plus standard uniform zero counting produces an absolute witness bound. Upward prime search does not need the value of that absolute constant.

Three premise levels are available:

| Analytic premise | Auxiliary-prime exponent on `q(log p+log q+2)` |
|---|---:|
| Released `delta=10^-6` | 1,000,000 |
| Printed comparison estimates, no new contours | 6,000 |
| Proposed retuned schedule | 50 |

This is the strongest consequence ready for review without resolving the general-field scope question.

## General-field scope and Chebotarev consequence

The source declares F cyclotomic, but the inspected supporting arithmetic requires only `mu12 subset F`: this ensures complex archimedean places and an alternating sextic Hilbert pairing. S-principalization, S-unit lattices, Kummer reciprocity and balanced generators work over general F. The local Gauss correction is pulled back from `Q(omega)` by the norm map, which exists for any such F. The quadratic sieve and the Kazhdan–Patterson global theta construction are stated over general number/global fields in their primary sources. The norm-sheet estimates' power exponents do not depend on the number of complex places; their constants can depend on the field.

The remaining scope question is whether the **exact** normalized global theta coefficients and reflected expansion used by this source have this generality. A keyword audit finding no other use of cyclotomicity is evidence, not a proof of that global interface. This extra extension must remain a candidate until that question is resolved.

If it does hold, take arbitrary K and `F=K(mu12)`. This is abelian of degree at most four. Norm pullback gives
\[
L_F(s,\chi\circ N_{F/K})
=\prod_{\psi\in\widehat{\mathrm{Gal}(F/K)}}L_K(s,\chi\psi).
\]
Away from 1 no factor has a pole, so a zero of the target factor cannot cancel. At 1 ordinary Hecke nonvanishing handles nonprincipal characters, and the principal pole is permitted. The same `49/50` strip follows over all K.

`CHEBOTAREV_CONSEQUENCES.md` proves the classical transfer from a fixed zeta strip to a uniformly small prime in any Frobenius class. Given sigma in the class, reduce to the cyclic subgroup generated by sigma and its fixed field. Orthogonality contributes a factor `1/m`; summing all cyclic Hecke zero contributions charges the zero count of zeta_L and hence `log D_L+d`. For any fixed `0<delta<1/2`, smoothing and removal of higher-degree primes/powers yield a witness
\[
\ell\le C_\delta(\log D_L+d+2)^{1/\delta}.
\]
An additionally excluded finite prime product R can be charged through `log R`. Under the candidate width, exponent 50 follows. No claim of Artin holomorphy or GRH is made.

The same character-separation proof gives degree-one prime generators for a ray class group, with norm
`O_delta((log D_K+log N modulus+n_K+2)^(1/delta))`. It avoids passing to a potentially huge ray class field. Generating ideals are not a polynomial-time class-group presentation: relation acquisition, principal-ideal testing, units, regulators and discrete logarithms remain separate costs.

This remains a **separate unverified implementation change**; I have not replayed all algebraic routines. It is not part of the proved analytic transfer.

The discovery-foundations teammate supplied a concrete practical caveat: in the legal small-prime branch, take `h=(x+1)(x+2)` and the nonscalar Berlekamp element `b=x`. The loop `gcd(h,b-a)` for `a=0,...,p-1` first succeeds at `a=p-2`, requiring Theta(p) trials. The conventional basis `{1,x}` gives this b when choosing the first nonscalar basis element. A smarter permitted b, e.g. `x+2`, avoids that fixture, so it is a flaw in a legal implementation choice rather than a lower bound for every splitter. It shows why large asymptotic powers should not be reported as a practical factorization result.

For the Frobenius witness search, supply a monic irreducible polynomial defining a promised Galois field, all d rational-polynomial automorphism images and the requested conjugacy class. Bound the field discriminant by the polynomial discriminant and exclude denominator primes by divisibility, without an integer-factorization oracle. Deterministic Berlekamp factoring at each searched prime and modular comparisons with `X^ell` identify Frobenius. The numerical prime bound makes the complete search polynomial in the displayed input length. Acquisition of a Galois closure or automorphism/class tables is excluded and must be charged if requested. Retained data are the supplied tables plus one polynomial factorization and residue algebra, all polynomial in that explicit input length. The exponent 50 is a witness-size exponent, not the total bit exponent.

## Prior art and novelty boundary

* [Goldmakher–Louvel, 2013](https://arxiv.org/html/1112.1642), Definition 1 and Theorem 1.1: quadratic Hecke families and the quadratic large sieve over arbitrary number fields. This is an imported tool; the general field scope is already theirs.
* [Kazhdan–Patterson, 1984](https://www.numdam.org/article/PMIHES_1984__59__35_0.pdf): general global metaplectic constructions with root-of-unity conditions. Their existence theorem does not by itself establish the source's normalized coefficient and Weyl-action formulas; its erratum matters.
* [Thorner–Zaman, A unified and improved Chebotarev density theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.02823): cyclic/abelian subgroup reduction and Hecke conductor-discriminant bookkeeping are standard. Their established unconditional hypotheses and field dependencies differ from a globally fixed strip.
* [Bach–Sorenson, 1996](https://digitalcommons.butler.edu/facsch_papers/1121/): essentially quadratic small-prime Artin-symbol bounds under extended RH. The proposed exponent 50 would be weaker numerically but would use a fixed-strip premise supplied without RH if the source proof were valid.
* [Grenié–Molteni, 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.02430): explicit class-group generator bounds under GRH. The character-separation mechanism and distinction between small generators and a full class-group algorithm are established prior art.
* [Altman, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12705): deterministic factorization of many reductions of a fixed integer polynomial with amortized cost charged to the splitting-field degree. This does not provide the same worst-case single-prime/dense-prime-field interface, and illustrates why Galois closure data cannot be hidden.

The limited primary-source search found no established unconditional all-field finite-order Hecke strip `Re s>49/50` or the corresponding unconditional polynomial-logarithmic all-class Chebotarev witness bound. That is not proof of priority. The new candidate is the explicit retuning and proposed exact field-scope transfer; the Chebotarev principle, Prony reconstruction, Berlekamp method and class-group character argument are not newly invented here. The sharper weighted discriminant accounting is a concrete refinement of the released reduction, not an independent solution of finite-field factorization.

## Checks, failures and decisive falsification

`check_schedule.py` uses exact fractions, verifies every displayed saving and Euler-domain margin, and writes `schedule_certificate.json`. It checks feasibility only. `frobenius_fixture.py` is a pure-standard-library modular-polynomial fixture. For `f=x^4-x-1` (discriminant -283) it finds all five S4 cycle types at primes 2,7,17,71,83; `frobenius_fixture.json` records the modular gcd degrees and cycle counts. The 4-cycle and 3-cycle establish the S4 group through the transitive degree-four classification. This finite fixture checks modular Frobenius identification, not analytic zero-freeness or field uniformity.

Rejected shortcuts and retained limitations:

* Extending only the headline Euler rectangle would be unjustified; I derived the new domains from the exact closed factor.
* Treating source constants as field-uniform would be wrong. The strip exponent is uniform; fixed-field onsets/constants are permitted and later eliminated from the witness transfer by the uniform explicit formula.
* The first `E=O(B^150)` transfer was correct but coarse. It is superseded by the `1/q`-weighted bound above; it is retained as an explanatory intermediate step in the consequence note.
* A fixed strip is not GRH, does not give full Artin holomorphy, and does not hide class-group or Galois-data acquisition.
* No canonical factorization implementation or comparative runtime experiment was completed. The scalar-loop fixture supplies a concrete legal slow branch, with its free-choice caveat.

The decisive next falsification is an exact source-level failure of the normalized cubic theta/Weyl reflection interface, or a missing field-scope dependence in that identity. A secondary focused attack should verify the retuned contour paths, the `0.001` Euler margin, and the source moment's arbitrary-M applicability. A single such failure defeats the strip claim; more rational scans or finite Frobenius tests cannot rescue it. The separate Kummer transfer can still be accepted as a theorem **conditional on any valid fixed-width strip**, even if the proposed analytic construction fails.
