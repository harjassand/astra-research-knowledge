C08_L10 CROSS-PHASE REVISION 01
Robust simulator region: exact volume/work tradeoff in the Beltrami construction
Created 2026-10-07T13:39:49Z. INITIAL remains immutable.

STATUS

A source-specific quantitative consequence has been derived from the flat-torus
localization construction of Cardona, Miranda and Peralta-Salas (arXiv:2111.03559v3,
Theorem 25, Theorem 26, Remark 28, Proposition 31). If v is their tape-bounded
simulator with positive Beltrami eigenvalue lambda, normalized ||v||_2=1, and
its controlled simulation patch is K=Psi^{-1}(B_N/lambda), then, while this is an
embedded Euclidean ball,

  mu(K) = N^3/(6 pi^2 lambda^3),
  W_strain(v) = nu lambda^2,
  W_strain(v) mu(K)^(2/3) = nu N^2/(6 pi^2)^(2/3).                 (1)

Thus large robust computational domains and high sustaining power cannot be
varied independently in this construction. The source's robustness/localization
bounds further force lambda to grow at least triple-exponentially in tape size,
so K's normalized volume tends to zero and sustaining power diverges. This is a
conditional derivation about that published construction and the specified
strain-viscosity forcing interface, not a universal lower bound for computation.
No code, invariant positive-volume factor, or volume-entropy rate is produced.

EXACT DERIVATION

The source works on the standard flat torus T^3=(R/2 pi Z)^3. Its localized
simulator has curl v=lambda v, lambda>0, and the simulation patch is the image
of the Euclidean ball B_N/lambda in normal coordinates. For sufficiently large
tape size the radius N/lambda is below the injectivity radius; on the flat torus
the chart is an isometry there. Therefore

  Vol(K)=4 pi (N/lambda)^3/3,
  Vol(T^3)=8 pi^3,
  mu(K)=Vol(K)/Vol(T^3)=N^3/(6 pi^2 lambda^3).

For this Beltrami field, div v=0 because div(curl v)=0 and lambda is nonzero.
The flat-torus identities curl curl v=grad div v-Delta v and
2 div(Def v)=Delta v+grad div v yield Delta v=-lambda^2 v and
2 div(Def v)=Delta v. The vector identity
  nabla_v v = grad(|v|^2/2)-v x curl v
then gives nabla_v v=grad(|v|^2/2). Set p=-|v|^2/2 and
F=nu lambda^2 v. The stationary strain-viscosity equation is exactly

  nabla_v v - 2 nu div(Def v) + grad p = F,

and its work is W=integral F dot v = nu lambda^2 ||v||_2^2 = nu lambda^2.
Multiplying this by mu(K)^(2/3) gives (1). If the field is rescaled to a v,
the work is multiplied by a^2 and the patch volume is unchanged; the claimed
formula uses the source's stated L2 normalization.

The same calculation gives the H1 conversion without relying on normalization:
periodic integration by parts gives ||grad v||_2^2=||curl v||_2^2 for
divergence-free v, hence
  ||v||_{H1}^2=(1+lambda^2)||v||_2^2,
  W=nu lambda^2/(1+lambda^2) ||v||_{H1}^2.
For lambda>=1 this is at least (nu/2)||v||_{H1}^2. This independently
checks the power conversion in immutable INITIAL.

TAPE-SIZE CONSEQUENCE

Write G_s=exp(exp(exp(c s))) with positive source-dependent constants allowed
to change between inequalities. Proposition 31 gives a robustness tolerance
  epsilon <= C exp(-exp(exp(c s_b))).
Its proof uses inverse localization with delta<epsilon/2 and lambda>=c'/delta.
Consequently, for sufficiently large s_b,
  lambda >= c'' G_{s_b},
  W_strain >= c''^2 nu G_{s_b}^2.
This is consistent with the source-reported ||v||_{H1}>=c G_{s_b} and the
identity above. The source also uses N(s_b) comparable to exp(c s_b); thus

  mu(K) <= C exp(C_1 s_b) / G_{s_b}^3 -> 0.                     (2)

More explicitly, log mu(K) <= O(s_b)-3 exp(exp(c s_b)). The constants and
thresholds are those of the imported localization construction; (2) is not a
uniform statement about alternate simulators.

INTERFACE AND CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ENTROPY-POWER CLAIM

Theorem 26 controls computation only inside K and only up to departure from K;
the source explicitly warns that the whole torus cannot simply be substituted,
since an orbit could later return to K. K is not asserted to be flow invariant.
Its volume therefore cannot be inserted as the alpha of a stationary positive-
volume information factor. As tape size grows, even the complete available
simulation patch has vanishing normalized volume in this construction. Robust
finite-horizon point-to-set reachability remains an exact and valuable result,
but does not satisfy the positive-volume, asymptotic symbolic-rate premise of
the throughput inequality in INITIAL.

The cross-phase Sol initial c08_s02 independently derived a general bound
mu(A_N)<=1/(N+1) for an invertible measure-preserving system when a prescribed
run follows N+1 distinct full-configuration labels in disjoint cells. That
result is attributed to c08_s02, not claimed here. It is complementary: even
without geometric localization, an unbounded nonrepeating configuration trace
cannot occupy a positive-measure set of initial states under that decoder
interface. It does not cover overlapping or partial readouts, changing
volumes, or an omitted environment register.

The cross-phase Sol initial c08_s03 gives a finite-acquisition cone certificate
for positive volume entropy only under an everywhere-positive strain/relative-
rotation margin and explicit supplied derivative bounds. It does not construct
those inputs for the Cardona simulator. Likewise c08_l02's localized Fano
bridge is conditional on a certified invariant code region, entropy rate, and
decoder-error measure; none is supplied by this tape-bounded reachability
construction. These attribution boundaries are preserved.

The available c08_l08 initial independently gives a trajectory-only minimax
obstruction: without a uniform derivative/energy envelope, finitely many
chosen trajectory starts cannot uniformly distinguish zero volume entropy
from a fixed positive rate because the active positive-entropy pocket can be
hidden among many disjoint regions. This addresses entropy acquisition rather
than the simulation patch of (1), but supports the same practical boundary:
robust local computation does not give an affordable global entropy estimate.
The c08_l09 initial states a conditional route from an invariant positive-
volume region and a uniform asymptotic word-law TV certificate to a work floor.
Again, the Cardona K is not invariant and has no such block-law certificate.
The c08_l05 mapping-torus path theorem is a scoped positive geometry-stability
result; it does not enlarge the computation interface. All of these peer
claims remain attributed candidate results, not premises of this derivation.

OUTWARD LITERATURE AUDIT

Bruera, Cardona, Miranda and Peralta-Salas, arXiv:2404.07288v3, Theorem 1 and
Corollary 4, show that branching universal Turing machines can yield positive
topological entropy in the associated area-preserving disk/Euler construction.
The invariant code set used there is a square ternary Cantor set; its dimension
is 2 log(2)/log(3)<2, so it has zero planar area. Suspending this section set
adds one flow direction but still gives a zero-volume subset of the 3D ambient
manifold. This is topological entropy carried on a null code set, not positive
normalized-volume entropy. Their Theorem 2 also constructs a universal machine
with zero topological entropy, so universality alone has no positive-entropy
implication.

The c08_l01 source audit also identifies Biswas, Manoharan and Joy,
arXiv:2506.20973v3, as adjacent terminology: its reported “topological
entropy” is material-line growth estimated from strain statistics, not the
normalized-volume Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy used here. That work does not supply
the missing invariant positive-volume computation rate or its acquisition
cost.

Lin, Thiffeault and Doering, arXiv:1009.0834, study a different nonequilibrium
objective: decay of the passive-scalar H^{-1} mix norm under time-dependent
incompressible stirring with prescribed instantaneous velocity or strain/power
budgets. Their paper identifies H^{-a}->0 with weak self-averaging for a
uniformly L2-bounded scalar and derives separate lower bounds on mixing time.
This is not an entropy rate of the particle time-one map, and the stationary
entropy-power inequality does not supply an H^{-1} decay law or mixing time.
The exact Sol time-one example in the supplied baseline is itself nonmixing,
so no inference from positive metric entropy to mixing is licensed.
