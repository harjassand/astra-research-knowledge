1. UNIFORM EQUIVALENCE OF TWO OPERATIONAL DISTURBANCES

For a finite-dimensional rho_AB define
 beta(rho)=min_{actual CPTP C:B->B_1B_2} max_j
             T((id_A tensor C_j)rho,rho),
 epsilon(rho)=min_{EB CPTP Lambda:B->B} T((id_A tensor Lambda)rho,rho).
Both minima exist by finite-dimensional compactness of the relevant
Choi-channel sets, including the closed separable/EB set. HALF trace
norm is used throughout, and I=I(A:B)_rho is in nats. Then
 beta(rho)<=epsilon(rho),
 epsilon(rho)<=min{1,6sqrt[(I+1)/ln(1/beta(rho))]}        (1)
for 0<beta<1, with epsilon=0 at beta=0.

The first inequality has an explicit actual broadcaster. If
Lambda(X)=sum_y Tr(M_y X)pi_y is EB, set
C_Lambda(X)=sum_y Tr(M_y X)pi_y tensor pi_y.
It is CP/TP and both marginals equal Lambda on EVERY input. This
construction does not assert that the originally supplied broadcaster
produces independent copies; the new channel uses one classical label
and globally correlated outputs. The second inequality is the full
Q-orbit theorem applied to a beta-minimizing actual channel.

Thus at any fixed mutual-information budget, near-perfect actual TWO-
output local broadcasting and near-perfect local classical relocation
are equivalent uniformly in both dimensions. A real obstruction has
been removed: bounded I does not imply bounded all-priors conditional
capacity, and a quantum reference cannot be replaced by a conditional
ensemble or weak measurement norm. The proof establishes actual joint
trace reconstruction using the product-reference Q metric.

For epsilon(rho)>0, (1) also yields the dimension-free inverse bound
 beta(rho)>=exp[-36(I+1)/epsilon(rho)^2].               (2)
This is a very weak quantitative gap, not an optimality claim. Fixed
entanglement/reconstruction witnesses at bounded I cannot coexist with
arbitrarily small actual local broadcasting errors in growing dimensions.
