CLAIM AND ACQUISITION MODEL

The conditional field mean need not be acquired exactly. Assume a supplied
finite range certificate 0<=R(X)<=r I, where R is a measurable PSD m-by-m
field and A=R^2. Admit a reference sampler/evaluator for R, and a fixed family
dP_v/dmu<=C, with this DOMINATION certificate supplied independently. A
global D-cell classifier J can be trained on n reference draws, then frozen.
Each cell's training mean Rhat_j is PSD and at most r I. Empty cells use zero.
Choose a public ridge 0<gamma<=r, set
 Q_j=Rhat_j+gamma I, B_j=Q_j^2,
and freeze the codebook before validation and before the archival observation.

The encoder observes ONE X~P_v, computes J(X), and archives ONLY that label.
It does not keep R(X), X, a latent parameter, or any calibration transcript
as another input-dependent register. Public codebook/model storage is
independent of that observation, but its D*m^2 numbers and n acquisition
draws remain explicit costs rather than free oracles.

For the DECLARED downstream experiment choose k independent observations
conditional on the same X, with public scales distributed as w(T)dT,
w(T)<=c_w/T^2. Targets have covariance I+f_L(T R(X)^2), f_L(u)=Lu/(L+u).
The decoder draws the scales and centered Gaussians with covariances
I+f_L(T B_{J(X)}). It is blind to v and X. Gaussian/scale output generation
is an ideal observation primitive here; this is not original-X reconstruction
or a finite-bit continuous-output TV claim.

Let
 b(v)=(2v^2log v-v^2+1)/(v-1)^2, b(1)=2,
 W=m(r+gamma)^2,
 ell(X)=||R(X)-Q_{J(X)}||_F^2 in [0,W].
Acquire N fresh iid mu-draws independent of training. For an upper interval
estimate u_i>=ell(X_i), define
 U=mean_i u_i+W sqrt(log(1/delta)/(2N)).
Then one event, with probability >=1-delta conditional on frozen training,
gives BOTH

 E_mu D_matrix(R^2||B_J)<=b(r/gamma) U,
 sup_v TV(k target outputs,k decoded outputs)^2
                <=k C c_w b(r/gamma) U/4.              (CAL)

This simultaneously covers every dominated P_v; no parameter-wise union
bound is needed. Accept only if the computed rational upper in (CAL) is
<=epsilon^2. Otherwise report UNKNOWN. No finite validation sample supplies
the range r, dominance C, evaluator, or independence contract.

PROOF

1. My revision03 proves for ANY two PSD/positive root matrices R,Q, in their
two eigenbases, D(R^2||Q^2)=sum_ij w_ij d(r_i^2||q_j^2). The scalar ratio is
 d(u^2||v^2)/(u-v)^2=b(u/v), and b is increasing. Since u<=r and v>=gamma,

 D(R(X)^2||Q_j^2)<=b(r/gamma)||R(X)-Q_j||_F^2.         (SQ)

Neither commuting matrices nor the true mean R is used. Also
 -(r+gamma)I<=R-Q_j<=(r-gamma)I, so ||R-Q_j||_F^2<=W.
Hoeffding for the bounded squared error, conditional on the frozen training,
gives E ell<=U. Applying (SQ) proves the risk inequality in (CAL).
For a complete concentration derivation, log E exp(lambda(ell-Eell)) has
second derivative equal to the exponentially tilted variance, at most W^2/4
because ell is in [0,W]. Its value and first derivative are zero at zero,
so it is <=lambda^2W^2/8 for either sign. Conditional independence multiplies
the N moment generating functions. Markov's inequality and optimization give
Pr(Eell-mean ell_i>=t)<=exp(-2Nt^2/W^2), exactly the radius used here.
This is established bounded-variable concentration, not a new inequality.

2. The actual reference decoder uses c09_s01's subsequent general-reference
theorem, independently audited from revisions/phase2_acquired_decoder.txt:
for fixed PSD A and positive B,

 integral KL(N(0,I+f_L(TA))||N(0,I+f_L(TB)))dT/T^2
                         <=D_matrix(A||B)/2.          (REF)

Here is the full load-bearing derivation. With R_A=(LI+TA)^-1,
R_B=(LI+TB)^-1 and Delta=A-B, the exact resolvent remainder
 R_A-R_B+T R_B Delta R_B=T^2 R_B Delta R_A Delta R_B>=0
gives the cap's operator-concave tangent upper. In the Gaussian KL formula,
trace cyclicity turns that tangent into the tangent of
g_T(B)=log(I+f_L(TB)); its scalar trace derivative is
T[(I+TB/L)(I+(1+1/L)TB)]^-1. Thus KL is at most half the trace tangent gap
 tr g_T(B)+tr g_T'(B)(A-B)-tr g_T(A).
For c>0, the integrated log tangent gap is exactly c D_matrix(A||B).
The scalar primitive, applied spectrally and differentiated locally at
positive B, is
 integral_epsilon^infinity tr log(I+TX)dT/T^2
 =(1-log epsilon)tr X-tr(Xlog X)+o(1).
Linear terms cancel with the tangent. At positive B the derivative remainder
is locally uniform; at PSD A use 0log0=0. The resulting tangent integrals
are nonnegative and finite. Split g_T into logs at coefficients 1+1/L and
1/L; subtract their finite integrals. The coefficient difference is one,
proving (REF). The uncapped Gaussian channel gives equality. No component
of B depends on the archival observation beyond the retained label.

Condition on J, average nonnegative KL, transfer mu to P_v using C, and
bound the scale density by c_w/T^2. k conditional independent outputs add
KL. Pinsker gives TV^2<=KL/2 and marginalization only contracts it. This
proves the second inequality of (CAL). It constructs an actual idealized
downstream reference kernel, not a codec inferred from small information.

CALIBRATION AND PRECISION COSTS

This root-loss validator evaluates no matrix logarithm on each calibration
sample. It requires R, not merely A. If only A is supplied, computing a
certified square root remains a charged acquisition operation. Known r is
substantive: the rare-field examples make it exponential. General dense
matrix R and Q need O(m^2) arithmetic per loss evaluation, O((n+N)m^2)
source scalars, and O(Dm^2) model storage. Training/validation source draws,
classifier evaluation and model construction all count. Public b(r/gamma)
requires just one certified scalar logarithm; log(1/delta) is another.

For certified evaluated roots Rtilde with ||Rtilde-R||_F<=eta, the numerical
loss can be upper-corrected by
 2 sqrt(m)(r+gamma) eta+eta^2.
Indeed squared norms differ by at most twice the true norm times eta plus
eta^2, and true norm is <=sqrt(m)(r+gamma). The actual frozen Q remains the
decoder model: its training error is captured in validation loss rather than
declared absent. Its PSD/range/positive-ridge certificate must be maintained.
The implementation below uses exact rational diagonal R and Q, eta=0.

If observed numerical mean loss<=theta/2, N>=2 W^2 log(1/delta)/theta^2
suffices for E ell<=theta. With theta=4epsilon^2/[k C c_w b(r/gamma)],
this is N>=k^2 C^2 c_w^2 b(r/gamma)^2 W^2 log(1/delta)/(8epsilon^4).
The dependence on m, field amplitude, ridge, k, dominance, scale density
and precision is explicit. This is not a claimed sharp sample lower/upper
or an advantage over every equally informed covariance calibrator.

The ridge bias is real. If R is constant within each correctly learned cell,
ell=m gamma^2, so b(r/gamma)m gamma^2 enters the target tolerance. Small
gamma lowers this bias but increases the logarithmic certificate coefficient
and numerical conditioning. Fixed held-out records cannot be reused to choose
a new J/gamma; refit before fresh validation or supply a uniform-selection
bound. The deterministic demo tries different epsilon acceptance thresholds
against one already fixed certificate, which requires no refitting.
