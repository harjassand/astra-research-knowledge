1. EXACT CONTROLLED INTERFACE

Root species R_i have 0 <-> R_i. Middle species Y_j have a nonempty root
parent set P_j and paired reactions R_i <-> R_i+Y_j for i in P_j. Leaf Z_k
has exactly one middle parent p(k), with Y_p <-> Y_p+Z_k. There are no other
reactions; all species sets are disjoint; roots are nonempty. The associated
fixed reaction diagram is binary and weakly reversible/endotactic.

Every root immigration coefficient b_i(t), root death coefficient delta_i(t),
middle catalytic birth alpha_ij(t) and death beta_ij(t), and leaf catalytic
birth a_k(t) and death zeta_k(t), may be an arbitrary predictable function of
the ENTIRE past, including leaf feedback and controller randomness. Each lies
in its supplied compact rational interval [rate^-,rate^+], with positive
lower endpoint. Rates multiply the actual integer intensities 1, r_i,
r_i y_j, y_p and y_p z_k as appropriate. Children never alter their catalyst
count in a reaction. Predictability, not a Markov or autonomy premise, is used.

Acquisition outputs one rational V, C>0, lambda>0 and count threshold R,
before initial states or policies are selected. At every integer state and
every admissible rate vector, the controlled extended generator obeys
  L_t V <= C-lambda V.
For EVERY admissible policy, every finite initial count vector and every t,
the process is nonexplosive and
  E V(X_t) <= exp(-lambda t)V(X_0)
               +(C/lambda)(1-exp(-lambda t)).
There is a supplied-data finite count core K with E tau_K<=V(X_0), and a
larger core K_e with E exp[(lambda/2)tau_Ke]<=1+V(X_0). The same return
bounds apply conditionally after any starting time, with its observed state.
Thus this is a global physical-time recovery certificate, including boundary
states. It is more than a finite-horizon collar safety bound.

For fixed time-homogeneous state feedback coefficients within the same box,
the resulting count CTMC is irreducible on the entire lattice and positive
recurrent, with a unique stationary law and E_pi V<=C/lambda. Arbitrary
history or time-dependent controls need not define a stationary Markov law;
no invariant distribution is attributed to such a policy.

2. MIDDLE CORRECTION

For each middle j set
  S_j=sum_{i in P_j}r_i,
  B_j^-=sum_{i in P_j}b_i^-,
  D_j^+=max_{i in P_j}delta_i^+,
  alpha_j^-=min_{i in P_j}alpha_ij^-,
  beta_j^-=min_{i in P_j}beta_ij^-,
  beta_j^+=max_{i in P_j}beta_ij^+,
  c_j=min(1,beta_j^-/D_j^+),
  gamma_j=min(c_j B_j^-/2,beta_j^-/2),
  g_j(S)=1+c_j/(S+1).
Exactly as in INITIAL, the scalar generator contribution per y_j is
  L_t g_j - [sum_i beta_ij(t)r_i]g_j <= -gamma_j.
This still holds with feedback because it is a pointwise intensity inequality.
For the negative immigration contribution use b_i^-; for the positive death
contribution use delta_i^+. The root count S_j is changed only by root
immigration/death, even when their rates depend on leaves.
The middle birth contribution is at most
  (1+c_j)sum_i alpha_ij^+ r_i.

3. THIRD-LAYER CORRECTION AND COMPLETE BOUNDARY DERIVATION

For each leaf k, abbreviate j=p(k), S=S_j, Y=y_j, B=B_j^-, D=D_j^+,
alpha=alpha_j^-, beta=beta_j^+, zeta=zeta_k^-.
Acquire rational parameters
  s_k=1+4D/alpha,
  c_k=min(1,zeta/[D/s_k+beta]),
  Gamma_k=min(c_k B/[s_k(s_k+1)],
              c_k alpha/[4(s_k+1)], zeta/2).
All are strictly positive. Define
  q_k(S,Y)=1+c_k/[(S+s_k)(Y+1)].
It satisfies 1<=q_k<=1+c_k/s_k. We prove globally that
  L_t q_k - zeta_k(t)Y q_k <= -Gamma_k.              (G)

Write f(S)=1/(S+s) and h(Y)=1/(Y+1). For root events,
  L_t f
   = -[sum_i b_i(t)]/[(S+s)(S+s+1)]
       +1_{S>=1}[sum_i delta_i(t)r_i]/[(S+s-1)(S+s)].
For middle events (which leave S unchanged),
  L_t h
   = -H(r)/[(Y+1)(Y+2)]
       +1_{Y>=1}A(r)/(Y+1),
where H=sum_i alpha_ij(t)r_i>=alpha S and
A=sum_i beta_ij(t)r_i<=beta S. There are no simultaneous jumps of S and Y;
therefore L_t(fh)=h L_t f+f L_t h. No independence of their rates is assumed.

Case Y=0,S=0: middle birth/death and root deaths are disabled. Then
  L_t q_k-zeta_k(t)Yq_k
    = -c_k [sum_i b_i(t)]/[s_k(s_k+1)]
    <= -c_k B/[s_k(s_k+1)].
This is the doubly empty catalyst gate: root immigration is the sole recovery
event, and changes q_k by a definite negative rational amount per leaf count.

Case Y=0,S>=1: discard negative root immigration, and use the bounds above:
  L_t q_k
    <= c_k/(S+s_k)[D S/(S+s_k-1)-alpha S/2].
Since s_k>=4D/alpha and S+s_k-1>=s_k,
  D/(S+s_k-1)<=alpha/4.
Hence
  L_t q_k<=-c_k alpha S/[4(S+s_k)]
           <=-c_k alpha/[4(s_k+1)].
The latter uses S>=1. This proves a uniform negative correction even when
root populations are arbitrarily large and the middle catalyst is absent.

Case Y>=1: discard both negative birth terms. The positive root-death term
obeys
  c_k h [D S/((S+s_k-1)(S+s_k))]
     <= c_k (D/s_k)/(Y+1),
because S/(S+s_k)<=1 and S+s_k-1>=s_k when S>=1; when S=0 it is zero.
The positive middle-death term obeys
  c_k f A/(Y+1)<=c_k beta/(Y+1).
Therefore
  L_t q_k-zeta_k(t)Yq_k
     <=c_k(D/s_k+beta)/(Y+1)-zeta Y
     <=zeta/(Y+1)-zeta Y<=-zeta/2.
The three cases prove (G), including every zero and one count.

4. GLOBAL WEIGHTS, FOSTER DRIFT AND EFFECTIVE SYNTHESIS

For each middle j choose
  v_j=[1+sum_{k:p(k)=j}(1+c_k/s_k)a_k^+]/gamma_j.
For each root i choose
  w_i=[1+sum_{j:i in P_j}v_j(1+c_j)alpha_ij^+]/delta_i^-.
Put
  V=sum_i w_i r_i +sum_j v_j g_j(S_j)y_j
      +sum_k q_k(S_p,y_p)z_k,
  C=sum_i w_i b_i^+,
  lambda=min({1/w_i}_i,
             {1/[v_j(1+c_j)]}_j,
             {Gamma_k/[1+c_k/s_k]}_k).
Root immigration contributes at most C and root loss contributes at most
-sum_i w_i delta_i^- r_i. Middle production contributes at most
sum_j v_j(1+c_j)sum_i alpha_ij^+ r_i, while middle count drift contributes
at most -sum_j v_j gamma_j y_j. Leaf production contributes at most
sum_k(1+c_k/s_k)a_k^+ y_p, and its count drift is at most -sum_k Gamma_k z_k.
The displayed weights make the root and middle coefficients exactly -1:
  L_t V <= C-sum_i r_i-sum_j y_j-sum_k Gamma_k z_k
         <= C-lambda V.                                (R)
V is norm-like because every coefficient is bounded above and bounded
strictly below by positive acquired rational numbers. For example
  a_0=min({w_i}_i union {v_j}_j union {1})
gives V>=a_0 N, where N is the total count.

This is an explicit algorithm, not an existential Lyapunov claim. It uses
O(d+m) rational arithmetic operations once the graph/species partition is
known. The raw-table entry point acquires that partition too: it consumes
every integer source/target record, recognizes a unit count change with zero
or one unchanged catalyst, aggregates parallel channels, checks both positive
directions, identifies roots from the zero-complex channels, then identifies
middle and leaf parent sets. Dense source/target reading uses O(md) structural
work; subsequent graph checks and rational acquisition are polynomial in
the explicit input length. An unused species, self-catalysis, burst,
conversion, unpaired edge, unknown field, root receiving catalytic changes,
or any deeper/feedback reaction edge is rejected. Feedback in RATE CHOICE,
which changes no reaction vectors, is allowed. The syntactic recognizer
therefore checks that the input consists exactly of
the displayed paired channels, all bounds are positive ordered rationals,
middle parents are roots, and every leaf has exactly one middle parent.
Finding an equivalent realization of an arbitrary CRN is not included.
No state enumeration or real quantifier elimination enters the acquisition.
Encoded coefficient bit heights, including s_k and the core radius, are
polynomial in the explicit input length. Their NUMERICAL magnitudes can be
exponential or worse for a growing family, so the theorem does not promise
polynomial physical return time, molecule count or CTMC simulation time.

5. PHYSICAL-TIME CONSEQUENCES UNDER ALL ADMISSIBLE POLICIES

Positive molecule-count changes have intensity at most
  b^+ + A^+ N,
where b^+=sum_i b_i^+ and A^+ is the maximum over all catalyst species of
the sum of their outgoing catalytic birth upper coefficients. Thus the
stopped linear count argument in INITIAL proves nonexplosion for predictable
rate paths too. Explosion cannot be hidden in infinitely many bounded-count
events because every finite count set has uniformly bounded total intensity.

Let gamma_0=min(1,min_k Gamma_k) and
  R=max(1,ceil[(C+1)/gamma_0]), K={N<R}.
By (R), L_t V<=-1 outside K. Stopped compensator/Dynkin arguments give
E_x tau_K<=V(x), uniformly over policies. The integrating-factor argument
gives the stated all-time first-moment bound. Replacing V by W=1+V and
enlarging the core until W>=2(C+lambda)/lambda gives the exponential
return-time bound. These arguments condition on the filtration and require
predictability and bounded stopped intensities; neither root autonomy nor
future independence of a controller is used.

Fixed state feedback defines a nonexplosive count CTMC with every specified
edge still positive when enabled. A finite reachability construction proves
full-lattice irreducibility: activate roots, activate every middle catalyst,
adjust leaves, adjust middle species, then adjust roots. Hence positive
recurrence follows from (R). The resulting stationary first-moment bound is
uniform over all these feedback policies using the SAME supplied rate box.
This uniformity concerns a proved full-lattice component. It does not compare
unidentified components inside one stoichiometric plane, as refuted by N77.

For arbitrary predictable controls, the result is uniform recovery and
expected occupation tightness, without attributing a stationary distribution.
E[N(X_t)]<=(V(x)+C/lambda)/a_0, and
  E[(1/T)integral_0^T 1{N(X_t)>=M}dt]
     <= [V(x)+C/lambda]/(a_0 M).
Sharper exponential count tails are not obtained by exponentiating V:
the exact INITIAL counterexample to that shortcut remains applicable.
