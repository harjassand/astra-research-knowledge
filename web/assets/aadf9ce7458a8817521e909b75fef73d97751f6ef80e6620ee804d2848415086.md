# Stochastic recurrence for endotactic mass-action networks: target falsified

The proposed universal claim—every closed communicating class of every
time-homogeneous integer mass-action endotactic network is nonexplosive and
positive recurrent—is false. A published strongly endotactic four-reaction
network has a closed irreducible class on which the exact falling-factorial
CTMC explodes with positive probability for every fixed positive rate vector.
This is a physical-time counterexample, not a statement about embedded jump
counts or nonuniform stationary tightness.

For
\[
0\xrightarrow{r_1}2A\xrightarrow{r_2}4A+B
\xrightarrow{r_3}6A+4B\xrightarrow{r_4}3A,
\]
take propensities \(\lambda_r(x)=\kappa_r x_{\underline{y_r}}\) with fixed
\(\kappa_r>0\). The set \(C=\{(a,b):a\ge3,b\ge0\}\) is closed and
irreducible. Inside C, the cycle
\[
(n,0)\to(n+2,1)\to(n+4,4)\to(n+1,0),\qquad n\ge3,
\]
has desired channel probabilities \(1-O_\kappa(n^{-2})\) at each of its
three stages. Their infinite product is positive. The corresponding mean
holding times are bounded by constants times \(n^{-2},n^{-4},n^{-6}\), so
their sum is finite. The CTMC therefore explodes with positive probability
from \((3,0)\) within a closed irreducible class. Full exact propensities and
the reachability proof are in [PROOFS.md](PROOFS.md).

This network and its mechanism are already in the primary literature:
Anderson, Cappelletti, Kim, and Nguyen, *Tier structure of strongly
endotactic reaction networks*, Example 3.2, arXiv:1808.05328v2. Their source
proves strong endotacticity and explosive behavior for any positive rates,
and bounds the expected time for the prescribed sequence by a summable
series. The additional result here is a direct proof that one such explosive
trajectory lies in a closed irreducible class, with every channel race and
class-connectivity move made explicit. No historical novelty is claimed.

The implication for a general all-endotactic theorem is decisive: deterministic
permanence (including the N33 candidate), finite-horizon stochastic safety
(N35), or an event-chain upward bound (N76) cannot yield classwise physical-time
recurrence under endotacticity alone. N77 is a different obstruction: it
blocks tightness uniform across classes while allowing each displayed class
to be positive recurrent; it is not needed for this classwise falsification.

The closest defensible positive recurrence statements have genuinely narrower
hypotheses and already exist in primary sources:

* Anderson, Cappelletti, and Kim, *Stochastically modeled weakly reversible
  reaction networks with a single linkage class*, Theorem 4.1: a weakly
  reversible single-linkage-class network, binary (all complexes have total
  molecularity at most two), with a complex S or 2S for every species, is
  positive recurrent for all positive rate constants. The proof uses a
  suitably fixed-step embedded-chain entropy drift. The paper then bridges
  jump-chain recurrence to CTMC recurrence using a positive lower bound on
  total activity over the reachable class, which weak reversibility supplies
  away from absorbing states (Theorem 3.1 and Lemma 3.2).
* Wiuf and Xu, *Classification and threshold dynamics of stochastic reaction
  networks*, Theorem 4.8: all one-dimensional endotactic mass-action systems
  are nonexplosive, and positive recurrent/ergodic on positive closed
  communicating classes under their H2–H5 assumptions: stoichiometric
  dimension one; both positive and negative reaction directions; an integer
  primitive stoichiometric direction; and no frozen species (frozen counts
  can instead be absorbed into effective rates). This permits arbitrary
  molecularity in the one-dimensional direction.
* Xu, *Exponential ergodicity of first order endotactic stochastic reaction
  systems*, Theorems 3.4 and 3.10 (August 2026 arXiv preprint): first-order
  endotactic mass-action systems are essential and exponentially ergodic.
  This is the broadest all-dimensional endotactic family identified in this
  source scan; the source is a preprint, and its convention for “first order”
  should be kept as defined in that paper.
* Anderson, Cappelletti, Kim, and Nguyen, Theorem 7.2: arbitrary strongly
  endotactic base networks become positive recurrent on every closed
  irreducible component after adding, for every species, nonempty finite
  pure-species reactions \(aS_i\to y'\) with \(a\ge m-1\) and
  \(|y'|\le a-1\), where m is maximum source molecularity of the base;
  the added drain products must satisfy
  \(\max_{R'}|y'|<\min_{R'}|y|\). Additional arbitrary-product reactions
  with source molecularity at most \(m-2\) are allowed. All rates are fixed
  positive. Their proof is a direct CTMC Foster–Lyapunov argument with an
  entropy-like coercive function and tier-sequence control, and it also gives
  finite expected entrance time to the union of closed classes.

Xu's 2024 preprint, Theorem 4.6, separately proves nonexplosion for every
second-order endotactic mass-action system. That is a safety theorem, not a
positive recurrence theorem. For bimolecular weakly reversible systems,
Theorem 4.13 states positive recurrence exactly when a stationary distribution
exists in every communicating class; it does not supply that existence in
general. These are meaningful gaps, not evidence for the unrestricted claim.

The exact proof requires only a two-species/four-reaction diagram, the
factorial propensity convention, and fixed positive \(\kappa_1,\ldots,\kappa_4\).
The proof's \(O_\kappa\)-constants and the point at which the asymptotic
estimates become small depend on the rates; no uniformity over rate vectors
is claimed. There is no experiment or data acquisition, and no algorithm for
finding such staircases across all networks was developed. The diagram's
maximum reactant molecularity is ten, and the high-order reaction makes it a
mathematical counterexample rather than a directly asserted biochemical
model. Experimental use would require constructing/calibrating these
channels, count-resolved state observations, and bandwidth sufficient for
the increasing rate \(\Theta(n^6)\); none of those resources was acquired.

The remaining research gate is not “prove all-endotactic recurrence.” That
claim is already refuted. A sharper target would be a checkable structural
criterion for the top factorial source tiers that rules out summable-failure,
summable-holding-time staircases while still allowing multidimensional,
higher-order endotactic networks. The present work proves no such general
criterion. A near-term literature-aligned target is to determine the minimum
reactant order of an endotactic explosive class or to identify an exact
recurrence criterion beyond the first-order and one-dimensional cases.
