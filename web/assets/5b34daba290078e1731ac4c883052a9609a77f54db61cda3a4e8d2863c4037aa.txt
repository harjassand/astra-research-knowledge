CYCLE 3 LEAF — GRAPH INVARIANTS, COMPLEX BALANCE, WEIGHTED FOSTER GATE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact graph and algebra below.
Scope: the supplied generalized catalyst/substrate family and its fixed-rate
stochastic mass-action CTMC. No general WR recurrence or novelty claim.

NETWORK AND RATE CONVENTION

There are r catalyst species A_1,...,A_r and s substrate species B_1,...,B_s,
with r,s>=1. For each j,i, k_ji is an integer >=1. Every displayed pair has
both directions, with positive rate constants in this order:

  0 <-> A_j                    (alpha_j, delta_j),
  A_j <-> A_j+B_i              (p_ji, q_ji),
  A_j+B_i <-> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i  (u_ji, v_ji).

The state is (a,b) in N_0^r x N_0^s. Standard stochastic mass action is used:
the respective forward/reverse propensities are alpha_j, delta_j a_j;
p_ji a_j, q_ji a_j b_i; u_ji a_j b_i,
v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1) b_i. Here (n)_m=n(n-1)...(n-m+1), zero if n<m.

GRAPH INVARIANTS

The distinct complexes are

  0;  A_j (j=1..r);
  U_ji=A_j+B_i and V_ji=(k_ji+1)A_j+B_i (j=1..r,i=1..s).

They are pairwise distinct: k_ji+1>=2 separates V_ji from U_ji and A_j;
the A and B coordinates identify j and i across pairs. Therefore
  n_c=1+r+2rs.
The undirected complex graph is a tree rooted at 0: each A_j is attached to 0,
each U_ji to A_j, and each V_ji to U_ji. It has r+2rs=n_c-1 undirected edges.
Every edge is bidirected, so the directed graph is strongly connected and has
one linkage class (l=1); in particular it is weakly reversible.

Reaction vectors include +e_Aj from 0->A_j and +e_Bi from A_j->A_j+B_i.
These r+s vectors span the whole species space; every other vector is a
multiple of an e_Aj or e_Bi. Thus rank(S)=r+s, with no linear conservation
law. The deficiency is

  delta_def=n_c-l-rank(S)
            =(1+r+2rs)-1-(r+s)=s(2r-1).

This formula holds for every r,s>=1 and every integer matrix k_ji>=1. For the
smallest member r=s=k_11=1, there are 4 complexes, 3 linkage edges, rank 2,
and deficiency 1. For the smallest member with at least 3 species, r=1,s=2,
k_11=k_12=1, there are 6 complexes, 5 edges, rank 3, and deficiency 2.

ALL COMPLEX-BALANCE RATE CONSTRAINTS

Because the graph is a tree, its oriented incidence matrix has trivial kernel
on edge flows. Complex balance is therefore equivalent to zero net flux on
each reversible edge (detailed balance); this is not true for general cyclic
weakly reversible graphs. At a positive concentration c=(c_A,c_B), the exact
edge equations are

  alpha_j = delta_j c_Aj,
  p_ji c_Aj = q_ji c_Aj c_Bi,
  u_ji c_Aj c_Bi = v_ji c_Aj^(k_ji+1) c_Bi.

After dividing only by positive coordinates, the network is complex balanced
iff all of the following finite rate constraints hold:

  c_Aj=alpha_j/delta_j for every j;
  p_ji/q_ji is independent of j for each fixed i;
  u_ji/v_ji=(alpha_j/delta_j)^(k_ji) for every j,i.

When they hold, choose c_Bi to be the common p_ji/q_ji. Conversely, any
positive complex-balanced c forces the displayed constraints by tree-edge
flow balance. These are necessary and sufficient, not merely a sufficient
cycle test.

SMALLEST EXACT NON-COMPLEX-BALANCED CERTIFICATE WITNESS

Absolute smallest parameters are r=s=1, k_11=1. Set

  (alpha_1,delta_1,p_11,q_11,u_11,v_11)=(1,2,1,2,1,1).

The strict weighted-drift inequalities supplied for this family are
  delta_j ell_j > sum_i p_ji w_i,
  q_ji w_i > k_ji u_ji ell_j,
  ell_j,w_i>0.
They hold exactly with ell_1=w_1=1: 2>1 and 2>1. Yet complex balance
would require c_A=1/2 from 0<->A and c_A=u/v=1 from A+B<->2A+B. (The middle
edge also forces c_B=p/q=1/2.) Hence no positive complex-balanced equilibrium
exists. Minimality is literal: the grammar requires r,s>=1 and k_ji>=1, so no
smaller index or amplification parameter is allowed.

For a smallest >=3-species member (to sit outside the reported 2D boundary),
take r=1,s=2,k_11=k_12=1 and

  alpha_1=1, delta_1=3;
  p_11=p_12=1, q_11=q_12=2;
  u_11=u_12=v_11=v_12=1.

With ell_1=w_1=w_2=1, the inequalities are 3>1+1 and 2>1 for each i.
Complex balance forces c_A=1/3 from 0<->A but c_A=1 from either amplifier
edge, so this dimension-three rational rate vector is not complex balanced.
It minimizes species count r+s>=3; among these, r=1,s=2 minimizes n_c and
deficiency compared with r=2,s=1. (Both have all k at their minimum 1.)

The pattern extends to any r,s: take k_ji=1, alpha_j=1, delta_j=s+1,
p_ji=1, q_ji=2, u_ji=v_ji=1, and ell_j=w_i=1. Then s+1>s and 2>1,
while complex balance would require c_Aj=1/(s+1)=1 simultaneously with the
amplifier constraint. This is an explicit non-CB rate family, not an
all-positive-rates recurrence statement.

WHAT THE STRICT LP CERTIFICATE DOES AND DOES NOT DECIDE

For a fixed input network and fixed positive rational rates, the inequalities
are a homogeneous finite strict rational linear system in ell,w. Exact
feasibility is decidable by rational linear programming. An equivalent
bounded-margin LP normalizes sum_j ell_j+sum_i w_i=1 and maximizes eta subject
to ell_j,w_i>=eta, delta_j ell_j-sum_i p_ji w_i>=eta,
q_ji w_i-k_ji u_ji ell_j>=eta, and eta>=0. Its optimum is positive iff the
strict system is feasible. If feasible over R, it has a positive rational
solution because the constraints define an open rational polyhedral cone.
This fixes the quantifiers: the graph, integer k_ji, and one rate vector are
input; the claim is existence of weights for that particular input. It does
not say all rate vectors pass, that some favorable rate vector can be chosen,
or that LP failure means nonrecurrence. Standard exact LP gives a terminating
certificate recognizer (polynomial bit complexity in the rational LP input);
this is recognition of a sufficient Foster condition, not a decision
procedure for positive recurrence.

For completeness, the weighted linear part V_0=ell.a+w.b has exact drift

  L V_0 = sum_j alpha_j ell_j
       - sum_j (delta_j ell_j-sum_i p_ji w_i) a_j
       - sum_ji (q_ji w_i-k_ji u_ji ell_j) a_j b_i
       - sum_ji k_ji v_ji ell_j (a_j)_(k_ji+1) b_i.

The strict LP makes every nonconstant term nonpositive with a strict
coefficient. V_0 alone still has positive drift sum alpha_j ell_j on the
entire catalyst-free face a=0, whatever the substrate counts. A boundary
correction K 1_{a=0}, K>max_j ell_j, makes all 0->A_j jumps have negative
potential change on that face. The only reverse indicator jumps are from
a=e_Aj to a=0; their total contribution is bounded and occurs at finitely
many catalyst vectors. When a!=0, the negative a_j b_i terms control any
unbounded substrate coordinate, since every catalyst-substrate pair is
present; the negative linear catalyst terms control unbounded a. Thus the
corrected potential is proper, has negative drift outside a finite set, and
has an upper generator bound (positive jumps are bounded and high-order
reverse jumps are negative). Combined with the one-class reachability proof
below, this is a direct fixed-rate Foster route for inputs passing the LP.
The potential/certificate is rate-specific and is not a new general WR
criterion.

EXACT REACHABLE BOUNDARY / ACK TIER COMPARISON

For any r,s>=1 choose i0 and define x_n=(a=0,b=n e_i0), n>=1. It is reachable
from the origin by the enabled word 0->A_1, then n repetitions of
A_1->A_1+B_i0, then A_1->0. More generally every state reaches zero by
creating helper A_1 if needed, removing each substrate by A_1+B_i->A_1, then
degrading all catalysts; zero reaches any target by creating its catalysts,
using a temporary A_1 if the target has none, making the substrates, then
draining the helper. Every prescribed finite word uses enabled positive-rate
reactions. Thus the CTMC has one communicating class, all of N_0^(r+s), and
x_n is a reachable unbounded boundary sequence.

At x_n, every source involving any A_j has stochastic propensity zero. The
source 0 has propensity 1, so T^{S,1}_{x_n}={0}. In deterministic tier order,
(x_n vee 1)^0=1, (x_n vee 1)^Aj=1, but
(x_n vee 1)^(A_j+B_i0)=(x_n vee 1)^((k_ji0+1)A_j+B_i0)=n.
Hence 0 is strictly below the deterministic top tier, which contains the
substrate-bearing complexes; T^{S,1} is not contained in T^{D,1}. This is a
concrete reachable failure of the premise of ACK Theorem 6.1 for every family
member, not a counterexample to recurrence or to the theorem. It also explains
why V_0 needs the catalyst-free boundary bonus. The ACK 2020 binary structural
Theorem 4.1 is inapplicable: the amplifier product has molecularity k_ji+2>=3
and no pure B_i or 2B_i complex occurs. The general ACK tier theorem therefore
does not supply a recurrence conclusion here.

The smallest r=s=1 example is two-dimensional and lies within the scope of a
later reported all-rate 2D WR recurrence result. The ECMTB 2026 abstract says
every 2-species WR stochastic mass-action CTMC is positive recurrent on each
closed irreducible class; the project publication page calls this a complete
proof but lists the paper as “In preparation.” I therefore record this as
SOURCE-REPORTED, not as an independently audited manuscript. The absolute
minimal witness is not evidence of a new 2D recurrence result. The r=1,s=2
example has three species and is outside that dimensional result; it is
covered by the explicit Foster calculation only because its strict LP passes.
The reported 2D theorem does not extend to arbitrary d>=3.

RELATION TO PRIOR C10_S03 CERTIFICATES

This family is not inside the admitted C10_S03 coordinate-hinge grammar:
that class has all species' primitive immigrations and degradations, only
bimolecular source complexes, and reciprocal quadratic removal of a created
species' catalyst. Here B_i has no 0<->B_i primitive flow; the reverse
amplifier has source order k_ji+2>=3; q_ji removes substrate B_i while leaving
A_j; and v_ji removes k_ji catalyst molecules through the higher-order source
(k_ji+1)A_j+B_i. The current LP uses a different, rate-sensitive small-gain
balance between catalyst degradation, substrate birth/removal and catalyst
amplification, plus a bounded indicator correction on a=0. C10_S03's rational
hinge compiler handles broad positive rate boxes under its own gates, while
this result is for one fixed rate vector satisfying strict inequalities.
Neither class contains the other from the stated hypotheses. Both use exact
Foster certificates; the fact that an LP recognizes this one is not counted
as a scientific advance here.

STATUS AND LIMITS

The graph counts/rank/deficiency, edgewise detailed-balance characterization,
small rational witness, LP normalization, reachable boundary path, and ACK
tier failure are exact algebraic derivations. The strict weighted condition
is sufficient, not necessary. Failure of its LP leaves recurrence UNKNOWN;
the general higher-dimensional all-rate WR problem remains outside this leaf.
No complexity advantage over standard exact LP is claimed. No stationary law,
tail estimate, mixing time or theorem beyond the direct Foster implication is
derived. Exact local calculation is not a formal proof assistant check and no
external proof review or priority search was performed for this family.
