1. EXACT NONNEGATIVE SCALAR EVALUATION

Write the paired columns as (u_i,v_i). For arbitrary real or complex rational
x_i define the complex skew matrix
  A_x=sum_i x_i(u_i v_i^T-v_i u_i^T).
Transpose here is ordinary transpose. Put
  p_R(x)=Pf(A_x[R])
        =sum_{|S|=k} det(V[R,P(S)]) product_{i in S}x_i,
  Y_k(x)=sum_{|R|=2k}|p_R(x)|^2 >=0.
The expansion follows directly by expanding the kth exterior power of the
two-form: each decomposable pair wedge squares to zero; distinct degree-two
wedges commute; division by k! removes order multiplicity. Thus every p_R
is a homogeneous multilinear polynomial of total degree k. Cauchy--Binet
gives g(S)=sum_R|det(V[R,P(S)])|^2.

Y_k can be computed in polynomial bit time without enumerating R or S:
  Y_k(x)=[t^k] sqrt(det(I+t A_x^* A_x)).                  (1)
Here the square root is the unique formal series with constant term1.

A direct block-Pfaffian proof avoids importing a numerical congruence form.
For ANY complex skew A of size m set
  K(t)=[[t A,I_m],[-I_m,A^*]],  c_m=(-1)^(m(m-1)/2).
It is skew. Expansion of its Pfaffian chooses the same even index set R in
the two diagonal blocks, since all remaining matches use the identity block.
The identity-block sign is c_m(-1)^k for |R|=2k, while
Pf(A^*[R])=(-1)^k conjugate(Pf(A[R])). Hence
  Pf K(t)=c_m sum_k t^k sum_{|R|=2k}|Pf(A[R])|^2.
After swapping its two block columns, Schur elimination of the identity block
gives det K(t)=det(I+t A^* A): the two (-1)^m signs cancel. The elementary
Pfaffian determinant identity det K=(Pf K)^2 proves (1), including odd m.
The block expansion is also a specialization of Okada's primary Pfaffian
minor-summation formulas; it is not a claimed new identity.

Compute d_j=[t^j]det(I+t H), H=A_x^*A_x, by Newton traces:
  d_0=1, d_j=(1/j)sum_{a=1}^j(-1)^(a-1)d_{j-a}tr(H^a).
Then q_0=1 and
  q_j=(d_j-sum_{a=1}^{j-1}q_a q_{j-a})/2.
Return q_k. This takes O(Nm^2+k m^3) Gaussian-rational arithmetic operations
using streamed powers and O(m^2+k) rational storage, apart from the input.
There is no eigenvalue tolerance, nonrational square root, or norm oracle.

Clear all V denominators with one D of polynomial bit length. For grid x of
O(log G) bits, A entries have denominator D^2 and polynomial numerator height;
Y_k has denominator dividing D^(4k). Its Pfaffian expansion bounds heights by
O(poly(m,N,k,L,log G)); exponentially many formal terms contribute only their
log term count. The same bounds apply to traces, determinant coefficients,
formal-root coefficients and finite differences. Exact integer/rational
arithmetic pays these bits. A tiny positive output is not charged as an
inverse-probability loop.

2. ONE-SIDED SUPPORT PIT, INCLUDING CONSTRAINTS

If Z_k=0, every determinant coefficient and every p_R is zero. Conversely,
if Z_k>0, one coefficient det(V[R,P(S)]) is nonzero, so one p_R is a NONZERO
polynomial of degree k. Draw independent x_i uniformly from {1,...,G}, with
G a power of2. Then
  Pr[Y_k(x)=0 | Z_k>0] <= k/G.                           (2)
Indeed Y=0 forces that chosen p_R=0. The polynomial zero bound follows by
induction on variable count: write degree a in the last variable, charge
failure of the leading coefficient by (k-a)/G, then at most a/G roots in the
last variable. It holds over Q(i) for real integer sampling sets. There is no
false-positive test: Y>0 always implies positive support. G may be numerically
large; drawing a grid entry uses log2 G fair bits, not G operations.

For disjoint forced A and excluded B, define the positive prefix mass
  W_k(A,B)=sum_{|S|=k,A subset S,S disjoint B}g(S).
If |A|>k or C=V_A is dependent, it is exactly0. Otherwise, with
  G_A=C^*C, d_A=det G_A>0,
  P_A=I-C G_A^(-1)C^*, E=[N] minus(A union B), r=k-|A|,
Schur complements give the positive identity (c03_l06 origin)
  W_k(A,B)=d_A Z_r(P_A V_E).                            (3)
The remaining columns retain their original pair grouping. This is pair-label
deletion/projection, NOT ambient-coordinate deletion of F. Apply (2) at degree
r<=k to this projected frame. Inverting its Gram matrix is explicitly paid;
even if d_A is tiny, adjugate/minor bit bounds are polynomial in the ORIGINAL
input length. Recompute each projection from original V rather than nesting
uncontrolled numerical projections. If r=0, support is recognized exactly
from d_A>0 without random evaluation.

3. ACQUIRE AN ACTUAL S0 AND ITS RADIUS

Use one root support query. If it returns zero, report ZERO_WITH_CONFIDENCE.
On positive instances this can be wrong only in the budget (2). On true-zero
instances it is always correct.

Scan i=1,...,N. At forced/excluded prefix (A,B), test W_k(A union{i},B)>0.
If positive include i; otherwise exclude it. Once |A|=k, exclude the rest.
On the event that every support query is correct, one positive child always
survives, so this produces a supported k-set S0. Independently verify
|S0|=k and g(S0)>0 by an exact Gram determinant; failed verification returns
UNKNOWN. There are at most N+1 support queries. The successful S0 is the
deterministic greedy witness; optimal-center search is not assumed.

Now use fresh independent integer x_i from the same grid. Put
  A_x(z)=sum_{i in S0}x_i pair_skew_i
         +z sum_{i notin S0}x_i pair_skew_i,
  Q_x(z)=Y_k(A_x(z)).
For real z this is a rational polynomial of degree at most2k. Let
d=max_{g(S)>0}|S minus S0|. Write each Pfaffian as
  p_R(x,z)=sum_{j=0}^d b_{R,j}(x)z^j,
  b_{R,d}(x)=sum_{S:|S minus S0|=d}det(V[R,P(S)])x_S.
One b_{R,d} is a nonzero degree-k polynomial, since at least one positive S
attains d and distinct S give distinct x monomials. The leading coefficient
of Q_x is sum_R |b_{R,d}(x)|^2. Thus
  Pr[deg Q_x < 2d] <= k/G; deg Q_x can NEVER exceed2d.   (4)
Also Q_x(0)=g(S0)x_(S0)^2>0 because grid entries are positive.

Evaluate (1) at z=0,1,...,2k with the SAME sampled x. A degree-at-most2k
polynomial's highest nonzero forward difference at0 is its degree: the dth
one is d! times its leading coefficient. Compute its differences in O(k^2)
rational operations. The highest degree is even, so half it gives d_hat.
This acquires d with the one-sided underestimation failure (4). A claimed
EXACT radius certificate from one random evaluation would be false.

Choose power-of2 G>=k(N+2)/delta. Conditional on every prior adaptive history,
a fresh support test still satisfies (2). A union bound over N+1 support
tests and the radius test gives jointly correct classification, S0 and d
with probability >=1-delta. Fair-bit use is at most
O(N(N+2) log(k(N+2)/delta)); all loops are deterministic polynomial length.
The N+1 projected queries and 2k+1 scalar evaluations, projections, and
differences have polynomial bit work in m,N,k,L,log(1/delta).

4. FOUR-PHASE MOMENT AND ITS PREFIX INHERITANCE

This is c01_s01's peer-origin tensor lemma, independently reconstructed here.
Let a_S be the exterior coefficient vector with norm squared g(S). For
independent uniform z_i in {1,-1,i,-i},
  p(z)=sum_S a_S z_S, X=||p(z)||^2, E X=Z_k.
In p tensor p, a monomial exponent vector alpha has entries0,1,2. These
monomials are mutually orthogonal on the fourth-root product grid. If a pair
S,T has Johnson distance ell=|S minus T|, its collision class has exactly
binom(2ell,ell) ordered decompositions. Hilbert-space Cauchy--Schwarz gives
  E X^2 <= binom(2D,D) Z_k^2
whenever every contributing positive pair has distance <=D. Zero-weight S
has coefficient vector zero, so it contributes nothing. This is a SECOND
MOMENT bound, not a pointwise envelope or deterministic zero test.

Our acquired radius implies |S minus T|<=2d by the triangle inequality.
For a projected prefix in (3), a nonzero residual label set T corresponds
exactly to the original supported S=A union T respecting the exclusions.
For two completions,
  |T minus T'|=|(A union T) minus(A union T')|<=2d.
Therefore the SAME B_d bounds the relative second moment of every projected
prefix estimator, even when residual r=k-|A| is large. Projection need not
preserve canonical identity columns: the general exterior tensor proof covers
arbitrary rational paired vectors. This is the load-bearing sampler bridge.

6. POSITIVE RATIONAL ACTIVITIES, WITHOUT ALGEBRAIC-ROOT ORACLES

Strictly positive activities lambda_i leave support, witness and radius
unchanged. For counting, approximate each remaining activity by a rational
square b_i^2 with
  lambda_i <=b_i^2 <=(1+gamma)lambda_i.
Scale ONE vector in each pair by b_i. This scales every positive g(S) by
product_(i in S)b_i^2/lambda_i in [1,(1+gamma)^k]; it never rounds a matrix
entry inside an interfering determinant.

Acquire b_i explicitly: normalize lambda_i=2^(2a)x with1<=x<4, choose
p=ceil(log2(3/gamma)), and use integer square root to obtain
q=ceil(2^p sqrt(x)). Set b_i=2^a q/2^p. Since the upward error is <=2^-p,
b_i^2/lambda_i<=1+3*2^-p<=1+gamma. All bit lengths are
O(L+log(1/gamma)); constructing powers of2 or an integer square root is
polynomial in ENCODING length, including tiny lambda_i.

For a relative epsilon count choose gamma=epsilon/(8 max(1,k)), estimate the
squared-activity count to relative epsilon/4. Its multiplicative bias is at
most exp(epsilon/8)<=1+epsilon/4 for0<epsilon<1, so the final relative error
is <epsilon. For TV sampling allocate epsilon/4 to this activity perturbation;
the normalized density ratio is bounded by the same multiplicative interval.
Confidence and branch budgets can use the remaining epsilon budget above.
All scalar/count/prefix calls retain the acquired radius and polynomial bits.
This avoids uncharged independent algebraic square roots or numerical activity
floors. The implemented mandatory-core counter includes this rounding routine;
the generic-radius wrapper is unweighted, with the weighted version specified
by this explicit scaling.

7. MANDATORY-CORE SUBCASE AND A NONEMPTY HIGH-k EXAMPLE

An earlier owned implementation acquires C=intersection of all positive
k-pair supports by the same root support test and N pair-DELETION queries.
If deletion of i destroys support, i is mandatory. On jointly correct tests,
  Z_k=det(V_C^*V_C) Z_(k-|C|)(P_C V_remaining).
This gives a factor binom(2r,r), r=k-|C|, with positive-rational forced-core
activities multiplied exactly. The acquired-radius route is stronger in that
it does not require a large mandatory intersection. Both are admitted classes,
not new generic hardness or mixing results.

Example: n=2k, H={1,...,k+d}, with1<=d<=k. Let
  F_ij=1/(i+j+1) for i in H (using zero-based indices), and0 otherwise.
Every square Cauchy subminor is nonzero: multiply a purported column relation
sum_j a_j/(x+y_j) by product_j(x+y_j); its numerator has degree at mostq-1,
yet q distinct roots x_i, so is identically zero; substitution x=-y_j then
forces all a_j=0. Thus the positive top-row support is exactly all k-subsets
of H. Its intersection is empty, radius of every witness is d, and diameter
is d. Our method admits d=O(log n) with central k.

All k+d active rows are dense, so the frozen near-monomial c01_s01 initial
needs t>=k+d exceptional rows. The support graph contains a clique of that

NOT approximation hardness or superiority to every alternative exact method.
