I. INDEPENDENT GENERATOR CHECK AND A FIXED POSITIVE BALL

For a unit n, let u=n.m, v=n-u m, r=n cross m and
 B_n=N u+v.grad, R_n=r.grad, k(m)=tau_m^tensor N.
Pauli multiplication at one site gives {n.J,k}=B_n k and [n.J,k]=iR_n k.
Since differentiation commutes with constant matrix left/right multiplication,
 B_n^2 k={n.J,{n.J,k}}, R_n^2 k=-[n.J,[n.J,k]].
Adding the nested anticommutator and commutator expressions gives
        (B_n^2-R_n^2)k/4 = {(n.J)^2,k}/2.             (P2)
The minus angular diffusion sign is necessary; no commutativity of Q_A and
b.J is assumed.

Instead of the source's Lambda=M+1, take
 Lambda=2M+1, C=A+Lambda I, mu=M+1, L=3M+1.
Then mu I<=C<=L I and L/mu<=3 for EVERY M>=0. Put
 t0=2/N-Lambda/N^(3/2), H=Q_C/N^(3/2)+b.J/N^(3/4).
K=t0 J^2+H, with J^2 central. Require N>=Lambda^2, so t0>=1/N>0.
On kernels, M_H(X)={H,X}/2 equals the differential operator
 G=V+a.grad+D:Hess/(4N^(3/2)), where
 D=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T,
 a=(N-1)/(2N^(3/2)) [C m-(m^T C m)m]
                              +[b-(b.m)m]/(2N^(3/4)),
 V=N(N-1)m^T C m/(4N^(3/2))
                       +N Tr C/(4N^(3/2))+N b.m/(2N^(3/4)).
For the drift, (v.grad)v=-m-u n+2u^2m and (r.grad)r=u n-m, so their
difference is -2u v. This proves the simplified coefficients for arbitrary C.
The potential also follows directly from the independent-product second
moment E Q_C=N Tr C/4+N(N-1)m^T C m/4.

Choose the CONSTANT r_*=1/8. For |m|<=r_* and every z,
 z^T D z >=[mu(1-|m|^2)^2-L|m|^2]|z|^2
           >=mu(3777/4096)|z|^2.                       (P3)
The first bound uses the minimum eigenvalue 1-|m|^2 of I-mm^T and
||[m]_cross||<=|m|. Also D<=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)<=L I. Thus D has a known
uniform positive margin on a neighborhood of the closed stopping ball.
The eigenvalue scale may grow with M, but the condition ratio and radius do not.

Define the unique stopped SDE up to tau=first exit from |m|<r_*:
 dm_t=a(m_t)dt+sqrt(D(m_t)/(2N^(3/2)))dW_t.
Freeze m and w after tau, where w=exp[integral_0^(t wedge tau)V(m_u)du].
The square root is smooth on the certified ball and coefficients are bounded
and Lipschitz for each N. Every output w k(m) is a positive iid-product operator.
This defines an exact probabilistic mixing law; Brownian/stopping/weight draws
have not been compiled to certified finite bits.

II. EXPLICIT SEED TAIL, DRIFT, AND NORMALIZED CERTIFICATE

The positive heat identity
 e^(tJ^2)=integral e^(v.J)(4pi t)^(-3/2)e^(-t/4)
                      e^(-|v|^2/(4t))sinch(|v|/2)d^3v
follows by rotational invariance and the spin-j character
sinh((j+1/2)r)/sinh(r/2): the radial Gaussian derivative gives exactly
exp[tj(j+1)]. The j=0 case normalizes the measure. Each e^(v.J) is a tensor
power of a positive one-qubit operator. The normalized seed rho0=e^(t0J^2)/Z0
has radial field density proportional to
 r^2 sinch(r/2) exp[-r^2/(4t0)+N logcosh(r/2)], m=tanh(r/2)u.

Let a_*=2atanh(1/16), d=1/2-logcosh1>0, and
 k_*=min(a_*^4/768,d/4)>0,
 C_seed=(3/7)e^(1/12)[8e/3+16sqrt(pi)d^(-3/2)].
If N>=Lambda^2 and Nd>=2, its bad mass p=P(|m|>r_*/2) obeys
        p<=C_seed N^(3/4) exp[Lambda/2-k_* N].         (P4)
Derivation: f(u)=u^2/2-logcosh u has f''=tanh^2u. On [0,1], u/2<=tanh u<=u
gives u^4/48<=f(u)<=u^4/12. For u>=1, f(u)>=d u^2 because logcosh(u)/u^2
is decreasing (differentiate twice, or use tanh u-u sech^2u>=0).
For a_*<=r<=2, the numerator is <=(8e/3)exp[-N a_*^4/768]. For r>=2,
sinch(r/2)<=e^(r/2), and Nd>=2 allows absorption into exp[-Nd r^2/8].
Splitting that Gaussian in two bounds its integral by
16sqrt(pi)d^(-3/2) exp[-dN/4]. For the normalizer use r=N^(-1/4)R on R in
[1,2]; t0=(2/N)d0, d0=1-Lambda/(2sqrt N)>=1/2. The extra quadratic penalty
is <=Lambda/2 and the quartic penalty <=1/12, giving lower normalizer
N^(-3/4)(7/3)exp[-Lambda/2-1/12]. These yield (P4) without a supplied
large-deviation rate function. The original shrinking-ball loss is removed.

Replace bad initial branches by m=0, leaving a normalized positive mixture
rho0' with ||rho0'-rho0||_1<=2p. For retained starts |m0|<=r_*/2,
 |a(m)|<= beta=L r_*/(2sqrt N)+B/(2N^(3/4)).
Require beta<=r_*/8, equivalently
          L/sqrt N+8B/N^(3/4)<=1/4.                  (P5)
On a hit before time one, the martingale part has radial maximum >=3r_*/8.
One of its three coordinates therefore has absolute maximum >=sqrt(3)r_*/8.
Each has quadratic variation <=L/(2N^(3/2)). The two-sided exponential
martingale inequality and a union bound give
       q=P(tau<=1)<=6exp[-3r_*^2 N^(3/2)/(64L)].       (P6)

Let
 h=L sqrt N/4+L/(2sqrt N)+B N^(1/4)/2.
Then ||H||<=h, |V|<=h, w_t<=e^(t h), and ||e^(u M_H)||_1->1<=e^(u h).
Ito gives W_t=E[w_(t wedge tau)k(m_(t wedge tau))] and the EXACT residual
 W_t'-M_H W_t=-E[1_(tau<=t)w_tau M_H k(m_tau)].
Duhamel compares W_1 with e^(M_H)rho0'. Here an improvement over the source's
e^(2h) bound is available: on tau<=t, e^((1-t)h)w_tau<=e^h. Hence
 ||e^(M_H)rho0'-W_1||_1<=h e^h q,
 ||e^(M_H)rho0-W_1||_1<=e^h(2p+hq).                  (P7)
All paths, including stopped ones, remain positive. There is no assumption
that the indefinite global differential generator is Markov.

Since J^2 is central, G0=e^(M_H)rho0=e^K/Z0. In each spin sector
Tr H_j/d_j=Tr(C)j(j+1)/(3N^(3/2))>=0, so scalar trace Jensen gives
Tr e^(H_j)>=d_j and Tr G0>=1. For positive G,W,
 T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG.
Normalizing W_1 therefore gives the public certificate
 T(rho,sigma)<=
  2 C_seed N^(3/4) exp[h+Lambda/2-k_*N]
       +6h exp[h-3r_*^2 N^(3/2)/(64L)].               (P8)
It holds under N>=Lambda^2, Nd>=2, and (P5).

Under (P1), h=o(N), Lambda=o(sqrt N), N^(3/2)/L=omega(N), and all thresholds
eventually hold. Polynomial prefactors are absorbed, proving exp(-cN) for any

III. AN EXACT IID-PRODUCT OPTIMIZER FOR ANTIFERROMAGNETIC GIBBS STATES

Let the allowed spins be j=N/2,N/2-1,..., with d_j=2j+1 and
 m_N(j)=binom(N,N/2-j)-binom(N,N/2-j-1).
For the maximally mixed state q_j=d_j m_N(j)/2^N. If tau has Bloch length
r=tanh h, 0<=h<infinity, its spin-sector probability is exactly
 p_j(h)=m_N(j)2^(-N)cosh(h)^(-N) sinh(d_j h)/sinh h.
The direction of the Bloch vector does not matter because P_j is invariant.
Consequently p_j(h)/q_j=cosh(h)^(-N)sinh(d_j h)/(d_j sinh h), an INCREASING
function of j: d -> sinh(dh)/d has positive derivative for h>0.
Normalization and the increasing likelihood ratio imply
 sum_{j<=J}p_j(h)<=sum_{j<=J}q_j for every cutoff J.     (P9)
For example, apply negative covariance to the decreasing indicator j<=J and
the increasing likelihood ratio under q. The r=0 case is equality, r=1 follows
by continuity, and convex averaging proves (P9) for EVERY iid-product mixture.

For rho_a=e^(-aJ^2)/Z_a with a>0, its sector law is
 r_j=q_j e^(-a j(j+1))/sum_k q_k e^(-a k(k+1)).
r_j/q_j is decreasing. Thus the positive part of r-q is precisely a low-spin
cutoff, and its probability gap equals TV(r,q). By (P9), every iid-product
mixture sigma has smaller or equal probability in that cutoff than I/2^N.
Sector measurement therefore gives T(rho_a,sigma)>=TV(r,q). Choosing
sigma=I/2^N gives equality because both central density matrices are scalar
in the same full spin/multiplicity blocks. Hence the EXACT finite-N identity
 inf_sigma_in_iid_cone T(rho_a,sigma)=T(rho_a,I/2^N)=TV(r,q).    (P10)
This proves an optimizer, not merely a mean-spin witness. It requires no
numerical optimization, entropy estimate, or inaccessible quantum oracle.

IV. COUNTEREXAMPLE AT THE FIRST MACROSCOPIC ANISOTROPY SCALE

For fixed kappa>0 choose A_N=-(2+kappa)sqrt N I, b=0. Then
 K=-kappa J^2/N. This is an ordinary full-rank antiferromagnetic Gibbs state
of a fully connected Hamiltonian with extensive 1/N coupling, within the
same N-qubit physical Hilbert space. It lies outside the fixed critical
window, at M_N=Theta(sqrt N).

Stirling on the exact q_j shows j/sqrt N has limiting density
 g_alpha(x)=4alpha^(3/2)x^2e^(-alpha x^2)/sqrt pi, x>=0,
with alpha=2 for q. Tilting by e^(-kappa j(j+1)/N) gives alpha=2+kappa.
For completeness, on bounded x intervals the multiplicity formula gives
sqrt N q_j ->8sqrt(2/pi)x^2e^(-2x^2); interior binomial entropy bounds and
the exponentially small exterior provide a uniform Gaussian-polynomial
envelope. Riemann sums therefore converge in L1 and for the x^2 moment.
No qualitative central limit alone is being used to infer trace distance.

Let F_alpha(x)=erf(sqrt(alpha)x)-2sqrt(alpha)x e^(-alpha x^2)/sqrt pi and
 x_kappa^2=3log((2+kappa)/2)/(2kappa).
The two limiting densities cross once, so (P10) gives
 lim_N inf_iid_sigma T(rho,sigma)
       =F_(2+kappa)(x_kappa)-F_2(x_kappa)=c_kappa>0.    (P11)
At kappa=2, x=sqrt(3log2/4), c_kappa approximately 0.31127221956144.
Therefore no vanishing, hence no exp(-cN), iid-product approximation can
hold over unrestricted O(sqrt N) anisotropy envelopes. The little-o scale
in (P1) is optimal in that order-of-growth sense, not a sharp constant threshold.
At A_N=-2sqrt N I, K=0 is exactly a product; the sign boundary is substantive.

This also supplies a FULL-SEPARABLE obstruction, with a weaker rate. Every
product of possibly different local states has
 E J^2=3N/4+(|sum m_i|^2-sum|m_i|^2)/4>=N/2,
and mixtures preserve that lower bound. In our target,
 E J^2/N ->3/[2(2+kappa)]. For kappa>1, this violates the classical collective
spin separability floor. The effect J^2/[N(N+2)/4] then gives
 inf_fully_separable_sigma T(rho,sigma)
 >=[2(kappa-1)/(kappa+2)+o(1)]/N.                    (P12)
Thus even allowing nonidentical products cannot recover exponential accuracy
for this family. The general collective-spin floor and antiferromagnetic
thermal entanglement are prior art; their role here is a scoped rate counterexample.

At the more extreme A_N=-(2sqrt N+gamma N^(3/2))I, gamma>0 fixed and even N,
K=-gamma J^2. Since m_N(j)/m_N(0)<=2j+1, the target mass above a fixed J is
bounded by sum_{j>J}(2j+1)^2 e^(-gamma j(j+1)). Meanwhile q{j<=J}->0. First
N->infinity and then J->infinity proves the iid-cone distance tends to ONE.
Again (P10) supplies its exact finite-N value.

V. A MACROSCOPIC FIELD COUNTEREXAMPLE TO FIXED-BALL TRUNCATION

Take A=0, b_N=4N^(3/4)e_z, so K=2J^2/N+4J_z. This target remains exactly
an iid-product mixture: sandwich the positive isotropic heat mixture by
e^(2J_z), which acts identically on every local qubit. No nonseparability
claim is made here.

Set E={J_z/(N/2)>1/2}. Since J^2 and J_z commute, on E^c the exponent obeys
 K<=N/2+1+N=3N/2+1. The single fully polarized state contributes
exp(5N/2+1) to the partition function. Bounding dim(E^c)<=2^N yields
             rho(E^c)<=exp[-(1-log2)N].               (P13)
For any iid tau_m^N with |m|<=r_*=1/8, measuring sigma_z at all sites gives
N independent +/-1 outcomes of mean m_z<=1/8. Hoeffding gives
             tau_m^N(E)<=exp[-9N/128].               (P14)
Every positive mixture confined to that closed ball satisfies the same bound.
This includes the stopped/rescaled construction above whenever its output is
confined there, irrespective of how its weights are assigned. Thus
 T(rho,sigma_inner)>=1-exp[-(1-log2)N]-exp[-9N/128].    (P15)
At the B_N=Theta(N^(3/4)) scale the fixed-ball construction can fail maximally.
The result distinguishes failure of a support/tail truncation from failure
of the larger product-mixture cone. It does not exclude different samplers.
For the original c07 ball, r0<=1/sqrt32, the same proof replaces 9/128 by
(1/2-1/sqrt32)^2/2>0. Thus the example directly blocks extending that original
fixed-ball truncation to this field scale as well.

VI. THE OUTER-BALL SIGN AND WHAT UNWEIGHTED TAILS CANNOT PROVE

For C=diag(1,1,3) and the PHYSICAL local state m=(r,0,0), |r|<1,
 D=diag((1-r^2)^2,1-3r^2,3-r^2).                      (P16)
At r=3/4 its second eigenvalue is -11/16. This is an interior Bloch point,
not a formal out-of-domain state. The exact generator has no positive real
diffusion representation with that covariance on the whole Bloch ball.
Another product mixing representation may exist; (P16) does not disprove it.

Equation (P7) gives the relevant tail stability: initial discarded trace mass
p costs <=2e^h p; stopping probability q costs <=h e^h q. Dropping weights or
normalization is not justified. As a separate physical filter illustration,
take rho_seed=(1-p)|->^N< -|^N+p|+>^N<+|^N with p=e^(-aN), and sandwich by
H=aJ_z+(aN/2)I. The discarded plus branch has initial mass p but final weight
one against a remaining weight near one, so it contributes nearly 1/2 after
normalization. Its target trace is near two, not small. A tiny unweighted tail
therefore does not by itself imply tiny normalized output error. This example
is outside the critical heat-seed premises and is used only to demonstrate
the weight accounting requirement, which the certified critical proof respects.
