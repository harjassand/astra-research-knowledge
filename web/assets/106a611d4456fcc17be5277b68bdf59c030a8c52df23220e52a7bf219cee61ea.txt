QUANTUM CAPABILITY PROGRAMME — RESULT
2026-10-08

Main result: a shared compact nuisance that commutes with the input energy
has an exact minimax reduction to the Haar-averaged tensor effect. In the
N32/N62 qutrit example, an unobserved phase shared across two uses removes the
known-model entanglement advantage exactly: at hard ceiling Q=3, the robust
entangled and fully separable misses are both 1/4. The calibrated comparison
is  (3-sqrt(5))/8 < 1/8. The nuisance calculation is an internal proof
candidate; it is a concrete falsifier of any blanket robustness claim.

A second exact boundary result shows that positive-false-alarm performance
cannot be recovered from the N62 pair (E,H) alone. Two CPTP alternatives with
identical H and pure-null overlap effect E agree on every zero-false-alarm
hard-support miss, but at Q=1 and alpha=.01 their optimal powers differ: the
orthogonal-flag channel has global optimum 101/200=.505, while another channel
attains 1/2+sqrt(11)/100 about .533166. Full output geometry becomes a needed
input as soon as alpha is positive.

The programme also proves a scoped adaptive flattening theorem: for the
direct sequential extension of N62's *whole-sequence spectral-support*
contract, arbitrary retained memory and stop-on-click feedback have exactly
the parallel optimum. The all-no-click schedule unrolls to one legal virtual
parallel probe, and its miss is Tr(sigma E^tensor n). This is not the same as
a per-call hidden energy-history budget; for that altered contract, exact
adaptive equality is established only when E commutes with H. The
noncommuting qutrit under that per-call history contract remains unresolved.
Early stopping lowers alternative-side expected query energy in the qutrit
example, but leaves its worst-case support at 3.

The programme also proves an exact mixed-null extension under a common
orthogonal outcome flag: for every fixed probe, optimal type-II error at
type-I budget delta is exactly (1-delta) times its all-no-click probability.
Thus N62's hard-support rates transfer conditionally to that flagged class
with only a constant shift in log miss. A globally full-rank overlapping
null has no such universal reduction.

Inherited N62 proof status remains INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE: its supplied
finite-dimensional fixed-use theorem and hard/separable exponents were
independently reconstructed in two leaves, with no within-contract flaw
found. The source and audits are not external proof validation. The qutrit
finite calculations are exact. One exact script checks 17 finite
compressed-Gram and degeneracy fixtures; finite checks do not certify the
uniform theorem.

Other scoped findings: the structural entangled/separable exponent ratio is
bounded by the number k of distinct positive energy levels and this bound is
sharp as a supremum; an exact k=3 example has ratio greater than 2. A
phase-covariant input-preparation restriction removes the N32 qutrit's
two-use coherent gain, while one logical-qubit code can attain that gain if
supplied with a coherent battery. A deterministic bounded-control symmetric
state family realizes the qutrit's reported asymptotic hard exponent in the
ideal circuit model with exact support and O(Q) nearest-neighbor depth; its
drive/reference work remains outside the detector theorem. Chosen drive-work
costs can reverse the signal-only gain, but no minimum physical preparation
cost was established. Communication coding, many-body onsite scaling, and
other finite-model consequences are scoped in the leaf files listed below.

The key open bottlenecks are operational adaptive costs under a per-call
pathwise energy-history budget for noncommuting effects, and useful
finite-false-alarm rates for structured overlapping or globally full-rank
nulls. The direct sequential N62 whole-sequence support extension is proved
internally; the commutative channel-pair result covers a different history
budget. Neither makes preparation, reference, or control work part of N62.
