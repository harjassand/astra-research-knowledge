Audited artifacts
=================

`conditional_gaussian_modewise_preconditioner.txt` and its exact-rational
fixture `verify_conditional_gaussian_modewise.py` were checked for proposal
normalization, Gamma scales, whitened precision domination, Jensen constants,

Findings
========

For each positive-count 2D mode, the Gaussian radius-square divided by two is
exponential with scale s_+ b_j. Multiplication by its k_j-th power gives the
claimed Gamma(shape k_j+1, scale s_+ b_j) law and proposal normalizer
Z_q=product_j(s_+b_j)^k_j. In whitened coordinates the target precision gap
is S'^(-1)-s_+^(-1)I, with norm at most s_-^(-1)-s_+^(-1). Since the proposal
has E||Y||^2=2s_+K0, Jensen yields alpha>=exp[-K0(s_+/s_- -1)]; the dyadic
Lambda envelope is valid. Taking D0=bI recovers the PC-17 Jensen exponent.

The modewise squared-radius error sums to at most
2s_+ b_max K0 eta_e, so its Euclidean radial contribution is at most tau/8.
The separate absolute square-root tolerance covers the sqrt(m) accumulation;
the angular error is controlled by the total core norm B_X. The theorem now
specifies that these square-root approximations apply to original-coordinate
radii R_j=||X_j||. The accepted-law normalization bound carries over from
PC-17: epsilon_try<=delta/(64Lambda), with accepted-core coupling error at
most delta/16. The output-rate cap uses ||X||<=ceil(sqrt(2m))B_X, and the
conditional energy bound follows from E_pi||D0^(-1/2)X||^2<=2s_+K0Lambda
and A<=tr(A)I for A positive semidefinite. Numeric kappa, Lambda, M_out,
delta^(-1), log(xi^(-1)), covariance certificate/factorization costs, and
physical 1/p_H remain explicit.

The finite diagnostic passes on D0=diag(1/9,1/9,4/9,4/9), with relative
certificate s_-=7/8, s_+=9/8, positive counts (1,2), exact acceptance
1232/2187, and output covariance/energy checks. This is a fixture only and
does not validate the theorem.

Scope and limits
================

The review found no counterexample in the audited derivation. It was an
internal sibling-leaf review, not an external mathematical validation,
priority search, or proof of novelty. The sampler requires a centered,
strictly positive-P covariance and a supplied exact rational modewise
relative covariance certificate. It does not cover arbitrary anisotropic
or displaced inputs. Physical repeat-until-herald cost remains 1/p_H.
