The audited theorem and interface
--------------------------------
For every input n>=1 and binary trajectory x_1,...,x_n, the horizon-N code
below, N=n+1, computes a rational Bernoulli prediction in polynomial bit time.
Uniformly over every stationary binary renewal process whose positive integer
interarrival distribution has finite mean, without any uniform bound on that
mean, its expected conditional KL risk is O(n^{-1/2}). The established HJW
lower bound has the same order. The new candidate concerns efficient exact
marginalization, not a new statistical rate or compression-to-prediction
principle. Input is the one observed binary trajectory; no reset trajectories,
known hazard, gap law, support bound, mixing bound, or model oracle is supplied.

What the guarantee does not say: it is not high-probability model certification,
worst-context accuracy, a multi-step prediction guarantee, or a frozen reusable
finite-state model. Stationarity is essential to the conversion; finite mean is
needed to define the stationary renewal law, but does not appear as a constant
in the risk or runtime. Long gaps are not truncated in the true model.

1. Exact type counting and a proper dominating code
--------------------------------------------------
Use 1-based positions. A nonzero binary length-N string has first one at a,
R further ones, R ordered positive distances between consecutive ones, count
C_j of distance j for 1<=j<=N-1, and span S=sum_j j C_j. Thus R=sum_j C_j,
0<=R<=S<=N-1, and 1<=a<=N-S. Conversely any such gap-count vector, any ordering
of its gaps, and any anchor in [N-S] give a unique binary string. Hence the
type has exactly

  L(C)=(N-S) R!/product_j C_j!

strings. C=0 has N single-one strings; it is distinct from the allzero string.
The latter is a separate singleton type. With p(s) the integer partition count,
p(0)=1, there are K_N=1+sum_{s=0}^{N-1}p(s) types. Choose a type uniformly and
then a string uniformly within it. This is a proper full-support distribution
Q_N, with Q_N(x)=1/(K_N L(C)) for nonzero x and Q_N(0^N)=1/K_N.

Let the true gap law be mu_j and M=sum j mu_j<infinity. For a nonzero x of
span S and anchor a, its final zero age is b=N-a-S. Stationary regeneration
gives the exact probability

  P_N(x)=[P_mu(G>=a)/M] product_j mu_j^{C_j} P_mu(G>b).

The bracket and final survival are each at most 1, uniformly in M and all tails.
For R>0 the multinomial probability
(R!/product C_j!) product mu_j^{C_j} is at most 1; for R=0 the empty product
is 1. Therefore P_N(x)/Q_N(x)<=K_N(N-S)<=K_N N. For the allzero string the
ratio is at most K_N. This proves D(P_N||Q_N)<=log K_N+log N for all the stated
processes. It does not need to estimate the length-biased initial wait law.

A self-contained partition bound uses beta>0 and the generating product:

  sum_{s=0}^{N-1}p(s)<=exp(beta N) product_{j>=1}(1-exp(-beta j))^{-1},
  log product=sum_{k>=1}1/[k(exp(beta k)-1)]<=2/beta.

For beta=sqrt(2/N), log K_N<=log2+2sqrt(2N). This needs neither a partition
asymptotic formula nor an externally supplied complexity bound. K_N itself is
computed by the ordinary integer partition DP in polynomial time.

2. The ordered-prefix formula, including boundaries
--------------------------------------------------
Throughout this audit q_N(y) means the prefix marginal of ONE FIXED joint Q_N.
It is not the independently normalized shorter-horizon code Q_|y|.

For a prefix y of length t with at least one one, let a be its first-one
position, last its last-one position, r its number of completed inter-one gaps,
c_j their counts, and b=t-last the terminal zero age. For a full type C define
R=sum C_j, S=sum j C_j. Feasibility is C>=c and S<=N-a. Under the uniform type
law, the anchor is uniform on [N-S], and an ordered gap list is uniformly
permuted. The probability that its first r gaps equal the particular ordered
prefix gaps is

  W(C)/(R)_r, where W(C)=product_j (C_j)_{c_j},

and (u)_v=u!/(u-v)! denotes falling factorial. This probability is independent
of the order after the observed gaps, but observes the order already specified
by y. If R=r there can be no more ones and the terminal zero constraint is
automatic. If R>r the next gap must be STRICTLY greater than b; the remaining
gap multiset chooses its next gap with probability

  L_b(C)/(R-r), where L_b(C)=sum_{j>b}(C_j-c_j).

Thus the per-type prefix mass is exactly the proposed formula. The strict
j>b inequality and the S<=N-a constraint are both necessary. R=r does not
divide by R-r. Adjacent ones use gap j=1 and need no special convention.
With b=0 every positive residual gap passes the terminal test, as it should.
At t=N, any R>r term passing j>b is impossible because its remaining total
span is at most b, so the formula reduces to the unique full-string type.

For y=0^t, there are (N-S-t)_+ allowed anchors out of N-S; every gap ordering
has the same admissibility. Therefore

  q_N(0^t)=(1/K_N)[1+sum_{S=0}^{N-1}p(S)(N-S-t)_+/(N-S)].

The leading 1 is the allzero type. This gives q_N(empty)=1 and q_N(0^N)=1/K_N.
It covers the fully censored initial interval without a gap-law oracle.

3. Exact product-weight DP and the N! denominator lemma
------------------------------------------------------
For fixed prefix statistics, aggregate all feasible type vectors by (S,R):

  A(S,R)=sum_C W(C), B(S,R)=sum_C W(C)L_b(C).

Initialize A_0(0,0)=1 and B_0=0. At length j, choose multiplicity u>=c_j,
w=(u)_{c_j}, and ell=(u-c_j) if j>b else 0. Update

  A_new(S+ju,R+u)+=w A_old(S,R),
  B_new(S+ju,R+u)+=w[B_old(S,R)+ell A_old(S,R)].

Every type is reached exactly once by its vector of local multiplicities.
The product weights factor and the one marked additive statistic obeys the
usual product rule; there is no hidden ordering enumeration. Cap S at N-a,
or cap it at N-1 and restrict the final sum as root does. Both are exact.
The marginal is the sum of

  A(S,r)/[(N-S) r!]                         for R=r,
  B(S,R)/[(N-S)(R)_r(R-r)]                  for R>r,

divided by K_N. In the second line (R)_r(R-r)=(R)_{r+1}.

The integer denominator lemma is sharper than a generic factorial-power bound:
every displayed denominator DIVIDES N!. Indeed S>=R, so N-S<=N-R. Since

  N!/R!=binom(N,R)(N-R)!,

the factor N-S divides N!/R!, and hence (N-S)R! divides N!. Also (R)_{r+1}
divides R!, and the R=r denominator is (N-S)R! itself. The no-one prefix
denominator N-S divides N! directly. Consequently

  H_N(y):=K_N N! q_N(y)

is a nonnegative integer, computed by multiplying each grouped DP term by
its EXACT integer quotient N!/denominator. H_N(empty)=K_N N!. All prefix
consistency identities become H_N(y)=H_N(y0)+H_N(y1). A sequential conditional
is H_N(y1)/H_N(y), with positive denominator for every legal y.

The table uses O(N^2) states, O(N) length layers, and at most N multiplicity
choices, giving the conservative O(N^4) integer operations per marginal.
Partial products are bounded by N^r, r<=N-1, there are at most 2^N partial
type vectors, and the marked sum is at most N. Thus table entries have
O(N log(N+1)) bits. H_N(y)<=K_N N! has the same order of bit length.
Schoolbook arithmetic gives a conservative O(N^6 log^2(N+1)) bit cost per
marginal and O(N^3 log(N+1)) bits of working table memory. These polynomial
bounds are not claims of practical efficiency or optimized exponents.

4. Fixed-horizon terminal prediction and a full memory proof
-----------------------------------------------------------
On an input path of length n set N=n+1. For t=1,...,n let z_t be the LAST t
symbols of the observed path, interpreted as a prefix in the same horizon N.
Output the average of Bernoulli conditionals

  qhat_n(1|x^n)=(1/n)sum_{t=1}^n H_N(z_t 1)/H_N(z_t).

At most O(n) marginal calls are required. Each ratio has O(N log N) numerator
and denominator bits. Exact rational addition of n such ratios can produce
O(N^2 log N) bits, not necessarily O(N log N). This remains polynomial and
must be charged in the output/working cost. The conservative end-to-end bit
cost O(n^7 log^2(n+2)) covers the DP calls and exact averaging. No random
algorithm, approximate denominator, or precision oracle is needed.

Here is a first-principles risk derivation. Let Y=X_N. Convexity of negative
log bounds the terminal conditional KL to the average by the mean of the KLs
to each suffix conditional. For each t, add and subtract the true conditional
given the last t observations. The added term is
I(Y;X^{n-t}|X_{n-t+1}^n). Stationarity identifies the other term with the t-th
sequential KL contribution for the fixed block Q_N. Summing those contributions
is at most D(P_N||Q_N), because the omitted first-symbol KL is nonnegative.
Thus the risk is at most

  [D(P_N||Q_N)+sum_{t=1}^n I(Y;X^{n-t}|X_{n-t+1}^n)]/n.

To bound the memory sum without assuming entropy of an infinite hidden state,
represent the process by the stationary countdown chain Z_t: Z_t=1 emits a
one and redraws the next state from mu; Z_t>=2 emits zero and decrements.
Conditional data processing gives

  I(X^{n-t};Y|X_{n-t+1}^n)
     <=I(Z_{n-t+1};Y|X_{n-t+1}^n)
     =I(Z_1;X_{t+1}|X^t),

where the equality uses stationarity of the joint hidden/observed process.
The chain rule therefore bounds the sum by I(Z_1;X^N). Truncate only the
INITIAL hidden state to Zbar=min(Z_1,N+1). If Z_1>N, the whole length-N
observation is zero; if Z_1<=N its exact value is retained and the independent
post-renewal gaps generate the remaining observations. Hence X^N is independent
of Z_1 given Zbar, and

  I(Z_1;X^N)=I(Zbar;X^N)<=H(Zbar)<=log(N+1).

All these informations are finite because the observed block is finite-valued;
no subtraction involving H(Z_1)=infinity is used. Combining the bounds proves

  sup_P E KL(P(X_{n+1}|X^n)||qhat_n(.|X^n))
    <=[log2+2sqrt(2(n+1))+log(n+1)+log(n+2)]/n.

This matches root's candidate guarantee for the entire stationary finite-mean
class. Root's alternate entropy-rate proof of the memory bound is also valid,
but the countdown DPI/chain argument makes the infinite-entropy boundary
explicit. No reset or mean bound is inserted into the proof.

5. Explicit implementation traps and adversarial guards
------------------------------------------------------
The horizon family is NOT projective: direct computation gives
Q_2(X_1=0)=1/2 but Q_3(X_1=0)=13/30. Mixing Q_{t+1} conditionals instead of
the fixed Q_N would break the KL telescoping argument. Root's actual definition
correctly fixes N, so this is a falsifier for alternate implementations, not
a failure of the written candidate.

The fully censored no-one prefix must not use the ordinary after-renewal
gap hazard at the artificial origin. For a stationary law and positive
P(0^n), its actual one-step hazard is

  P_mu(G>=n+1) / E_mu[(G-n)_+].

For deterministic G=M>n this is 1/(M-n), whereas the ordinary gap hazard
mu(n+1)/P(G>n) is zero unless M=n+1. The candidate avoids this trap by
marginalizing the no-one branch directly. This observation is not used to
challenge HJW's minimax theorem or its compression proof.

Other decisive falsifiers are failure of ordered-prefix counts, replacement
of j>b by j>=b, loss of the anchor restriction S<=N-a, treating C=0 as the
allzero type, division by R-r at R=r, integer division without the divisibility
lemma, or a hidden exponential iteration over types/suffixes. None is present
in the audited construction or the independent checker.

-------------------------------------------------------

brute finite code by grouping every binary string into its actual gap-count
type, assigns integer full-string weights, and sums them into prefixes. It

The nonprojectivity example is checked explicitly. CHECKS.json records the
