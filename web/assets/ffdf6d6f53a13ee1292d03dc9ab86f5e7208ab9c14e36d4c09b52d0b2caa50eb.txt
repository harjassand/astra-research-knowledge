swapping the two heights at x alone violates an original edge bound in

dependency edge. Include one extra lattice collar around U to handle

II. A GRAPH-GENERIC FEASIBLE-SHIFT THEOREM
Let P be any finite full-dimensional uniform difference-constraint
polytope in the admitted sign/pin conditioning class. On a patch of at
most A R^2 vertices assume near-contact components at some fixed slack

sparsity gives these moments by the usual separated-box animal count.
This is under the exact ORIGINAL sign kernel, not after an unspecified
height-value exploration. Restricted local graphs only decrease components.

Given a deterministic prescription f supported on a buffered free patch,
zero at all original fixed, sign/wall and outer-neighbor sites, suppose
|f|<=M and |f_x-f_y|<=L/R. Grounding means zero SHIFT; a grounded height
may still be random in P. Ground ALL sites outside the patch as well,
and keep only edges with a nongrounded endpoint. Thus every potentially
nonzero row and every incident grounded derivative column is in the
patch plus its fixed-width collar, at most C A R^2 sites. Ground-ground
edges are preserved identically and are omitted from the cluster graph.
Choose fixed0<s0<s1<s2<s_* and Delta=s1-s0. Write t_e=1-|h_x-h_y|.
For s in[s0,s1], average f on each component formed by t_e<=s; if a
component touches any grounded site, use0 throughout that component.
Call this v_s(h), and put u(h)=Delta^-1 integral v_s(h) ds.

If H_x is its s2-component size, then
                         |u_x-f_x|<=L H_x/R.             (FS1)
For an ungrounded component this is the oscillation of f; for a grounded
one use a path to its zero-shift site. The field u is continuous piecewise
affine in h, by subdividing according to signs/order of the finitely many
edge slacks and the thresholds. Every facet is tangent: at an original
edge contact its endpoints share a component for every s, and at an
individual wall or fixed site the shift is0. All true-pin edge facets
are retained; neither fixed endpoints nor observed signs are shifted.

Derivative calculation: when a threshold crosses t_e, it merges two
components, changing their prescription by J_e. At each affected row
|J_e(x)|<=L H_x/R, even if a merged component is grounded. Almost everywhere
         Du=-Delta^-1 sum_(s0<t_e<s1) J_e tensor grad t_e.
Each column receives at most the degree many edge terms. Entries connect
only sites in the same s1 component. Row/column sums and square sums give
 ||Du||op<=C L Hmax^2/R,
 ||Du||HS^2<=C L^2 R^-2 sum_x H_x^3.                     (FS2)
Ties telescope under a fixed edge order. Outside the patch, rows vanish;
adjoined grounded heights can be derivative columns without increasing

Here is the independent tangential determinant identity used for volume.
For ANY Lipschitz field u tangent to every facet of a compact full-dimensional
polytope, I+t u is a bijection P->P for sufficiently small signed t. If
ell_i is a facet slack, polytope vertex interpolation bounds distance to
its face by C_i ell_i. Tangency implies |grad ell_i dot u|<=C'_i ell_i,
so small signed t preserves P and its faces. Injectivity follows from
|t|Lip(u)<1. For surjectivity solve x=projection_P(y-t u(x)) by contraction.
The residual n=y-t u(x)-x is an outward normal, and n dot u(x)=0 by
facet tangency. Then0>=n dot(y-x)=|n|^2, so n=0. The area formula gives
 integral_P det(I+t Du)=volume(P).
Compare its first two polynomial coefficients:
 E div u=0,
 E(div u)^2=E tr((Du)^2)<=E||Du||HS^2.                  (FS3)
No symmetry of Du is assumed. Polytope-dependent small-t restrictions
disappear from this exact coefficient identity. It applies to the full

III. THE PRE-HOOKUP WALK FACTOR
Use the standard SRW potential kernel a with a(0)=0, a(x)=kappa log|x|+O(1),
kappa>0. For q' nonzero, bounded optional stopping of
 [a(x)+a(q')-a(x-q')]/[2a(q')]
at{0,q'} gives P_q(T_q'<T_0). For q,q' on a radius3R entrance rim,
the numerator is>=kappa log R-O(1), the denominator<=2kappa log R+O(1).
Hence this visiting probability is>=c>0 uniformly in both entrance points,
for sufficiently large R. The case q=q' is immediate.

Under the strong Markov property at q' before0, the suffix q'->0 has
the same law as a fresh walk from q'. Since q' is outside2R, the reversed
inner portion from0 to its first exit2R belongs entirely to this suffix.
Thus the law of each initially reversed inner-walk event from q dominates
c times its law from q'; reverse the entrance points for the other bound.
This is an event-level estimate with no path-length probability product.
It is BEFORE hookup, on the full-plane walk. Killing at the actual
strand/domain is exactly the additional Z event and cannot be discarded.
