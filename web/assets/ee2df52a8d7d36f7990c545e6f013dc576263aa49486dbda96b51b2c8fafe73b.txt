Strongest claim. For any complex r-by-m matrix V, any two ordered r-element column sets A,B, and any a in A\B, the Grassmann–Plücker relation expresses the product of their basis determinants as a signed sum of at most d=|B\A| products obtained by transferring one column b from B to A and a from A to B. Consequently

  |det(V_A) det(V_B)|^2
    <= d sum_{b in B\A} |det(V_{A-a+b}) det(V_{B-b+a})|^2.       (P)

The statement is valid with zero minors and arbitrary common core. In a paired-basis instance where A and B are each unions of whole two-column labels and differ on q labels, d=2q. Thus one can compare independent-replica mass to O(r) computable split-orientation terms, without projecting both replicas onto one common cut and without an exponential number of Plücker terms.

Derivation. Let X be the ordered list A with a deleted, y_0=a, and y_1,...,y_r be the columns of B in a fixed order. The r+1 vectors y_t in an r-dimensional space have the alternating cofactor dependence

  sum_{t=0}^r (-1)^t det(V_[y_0,...,hat(y_t),...,y_r]) y_t = 0.

Wedge with the (r-1)-vector made from X. The t=0 term is, up to the fixed ordering sign, det(V_A)det(V_B). Terms with y_t in A intersect B vanish because the first determinant repeats a column from X. The remaining terms are exactly the exchanges b in B\A, with signs determined by column order. Isolating the endpoint and applying Cauchy–Schwarz over d terms proves (P). No sign or phase assumption is used. For individual-column activities z_c, the product of activities across the two replicas is invariant under each simultaneous transfer, so (P) also holds for the corresponding weighted masses.

Why this is relevant. The earlier common-cut projector can annihilate independent replica norm, and the natural pair-label local walk can be disconnected on directed even cycles. Formula (P) keeps the two replicas' column orientations distinct. It gives a linear-size exchange certificate and proves that, for every nonzero endpoint product and every chosen a in A\B, at least one computable exchanged product is nonzero. For a proposed symmetric cross-replica Metropolis move that chooses a uniformly and then b uniformly, (P) implies an aggregate one-step acceptance probability at least d^(-2): if r_b is the destination/source product-weight ratio, sum_b r_b >=1/d, so sum_b min(1,r_b)>=1/d. This is only a local acceptance bound, not a global gap theorem.

Exact executable scope. `plucker_replica_sos.py` uses Bareiss integer determinants and checks both the ordered Plücker identity and (P) on 27 seeded small integer matrices (17,940 ordered instances, 11,586 with nonzero endpoint), and on all 30 ordered pairs of distinct pair-closed bases for the four-site BCS representation V=[I,F^T]. `plucker_replica_sos_result.txt` records the run. On the directed 4-cycle permutation example, the two supported pair-closed bases have amplitudes -1 and +1; every one-pair local pair-basis update leaves support, while the Plücker expansion has one nonzero split-orientation product. The diagnostic was an exact bounded check only; the proof above is the universal argument. No Markov chain, sampler, gap estimate, or FPRAS was implemented or run.

Boundaries and unresolved gate. The exchanged sets A-a+b and B-b+a are valid full column bases when their determinants are nonzero, but when A,B are paired bases they generally split one or two original two-column pairs. Therefore (P) does not itself define transitions in the target paired-basis state space. Enlarging the stationary state space to all ordinary bases changes the law and can make the paired event rare. The local acceptance bound also leaves invariant the union of the two replicas and does not control variation between different union/intersection fibers. I have not constructed an extended tagged-orientation kernel with exact target marginal, reversible polynomial conductance, and controlled preimages, nor proved the requested two-coordinate additive-function energy inequality. Repeated local use could still incur exponential path load; (P) does not rule that out. There is no counting oracle or source of unknown aggregate coefficients hidden in the identity, but all actual transition probabilities, refreshes, and annealing costs remain to be constructed.
