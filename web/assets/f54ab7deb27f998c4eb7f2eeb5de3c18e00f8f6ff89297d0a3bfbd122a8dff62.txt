CYCLE 2 — FIXED-CANDIDATE RATE-WEIGHTED CTMC CERTIFICATE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact supplied 3-species network and rates; UNKNOWN for a complete higher-dimensional recognition rule. No novelty claim.

FINDING

An exact rational checker verifies a rate-weighted physical-time Foster certificate for one irreducible, weakly reversible, third-order stochastic mass-action system on N_0^3. The exact generator drift of V=1+A+B+C is

  L V = 3 - A - B - C - A(A-2)(A-3).

The correction A(A-2)(A-3) is nonnegative at every integer A>=0, despite being negative on the real interval (2,3). Thus L V<=3-(A+B+C), giving strict negative drift outside a finite set; the same identity gives nonexplosion. The proof uses physical-time generator drift, not an embedded event-chain estimate. The checker verifies this supplied candidate only; it neither finds certificates nor decides whether an arbitrary weakly reversible network admits one.

1. INPUT AND NETWORK

The input is a finite time-homogeneous CTMC on the full state space N_0^3 with standard stochastic mass-action propensities

  lambda_(y->y')(x)=k_(y->y') product_i x_i!/(x_i-y_i)!,

zero when any source count is unavailable. All rate constants are fixed positive rationals. Species order is A,B,C. Reactions and rates are

  0 -> A (1),       A -> 0 (3),
  0 -> B (1),       B -> 0 (1),
  0 -> C (1),       C -> 0 (1),
  2A -> 3A (2),    3A -> 2A (1),
  A+B+C -> 2A+B (1),   2A+B -> A+B+C (1).

Every edge has its reverse, so each linkage class is strongly connected and the finite diagram is weakly reversible. The source molecularity is at most three, and the final reversible pair is a genuine termolecular interaction involving all three species. The three monomolecular birth/death pairs make the chain irreducible on N_0^3: from any state, repeatedly fire each enabled first-order death to reach 0; from 0, fire births to reach any prescribed state. Each path has positive probability. The stoichiometric rank is three because the birth vectors span R^3.

This rate vector is not complex-balanced. Complex balance at A, whose only incident reactions are 0<->A, requires 1=3c_A and hence c_A=1/3. Complex balance at 2A, whose only incident reactions are 2A<->3A, requires 2c_A^2=c_A^3 and hence c_A=2. No positive c can satisfy both.

2. EXACT RATE-WEIGHTED TIER DRIFT

Use the supplied candidate V(x)=1+A+B+C. Its increment is the change in total population. The rate-weighted generator contributions are:

  0 -> A,B,C:       +3,
  A -> 0:           -3A,
  B -> 0, C -> 0:   -B-C,
  2A -> 3A:         +2(A)_2,
  3A -> 2A:         -(A)_3,
  A+B+C <-> 2A+B:    0 from each direction.

Here (A)_2=A(A-1) and (A)_3=A(A-1)(A-2). The last pair has order-three propensities but conserves total population in each jump, so its contribution to this candidate is exactly zero at every state, regardless of its activity or rate. Combining the remaining tiers gives

  L V = 3-3A-B-C+2A(A-1)-A(A-1)(A-2)
      = 3-A-B-C-A(A-2)(A-3).                         (1)

The coefficient choices matter. The quadratic upward reaction has rate 2, its cubic reverse has rate 1, and the A death has rate 3. Their combined exact falling-factorial drift gives the correction in (1); this is not obtained by replacing propensities with their leading monomials.

Integer-lattice sign certificate. Put q(n)=n(n-2)(n-3). For n=0,1,2, direct exact evaluation gives q(n)=0,2,0. For every integer n>=3, each of n, n-2, n-3 is nonnegative, so q(n)>=0. These finite exceptions plus one factored tail prove q>=0 on N_0. The domain matters: q(5/2)=-5/8, so the same inequality is false as a real-variable polynomial inequality. This certificate is specific to integer molecular counts.

It follows that

  L V(x) <= 3-(A+B+C).                                (2)

For K={x:A+B+C<=3}, (2) yields LV<=-1 on K^c. The set K has 20 states. Also V=1+A+B+C is norm-like with finite sublevel sets, and

  LV <= 3-(A+B+C) <= 3V.                              (3)

The standard CTMC Lyapunov nonexplosion criterion applied to (3) makes the minimal chain conservative. One direct localization is to stop at the first exit from {V<R}; Dynkin's inequality gives E[V(X_(t wedge tau_R))]<=V(X_0)e^(3t), hence P(tau_R<=t)<=V(X_0)e^(3t)/R. Thus the chain cannot escape to infinity in finite time. Any path confined to a finite state set cannot make infinitely many jumps in finite time, since the total rates are bounded on that finite set.

After nonexplosion, the finite-set Foster criterion applied to LV<=-1 outside K gives positive recurrence of the irreducible chain and finite expected physical-time entrance to K. The stopped-drift calculation also gives E_x[tau_K]<=V(x) for x outside K. These conclusions concern elapsed CTMC time and do not rely on jump counts.

3. THE CHECKER AND ITS DECISION BOUNDARY

`checker.py` is a standard-library-only exact checker for this fixed data. It uses Fraction arithmetic and sparse multivariate polynomials. It verifies positive rates; reverse edges; the three birth/death paths supporting irreducibility; third-order molecularity and the cross-species source; and exact polynomial equality between the reaction-generator expansion and (1). It checks the three finite sign cases, the tail factor thresholds, and the rational real countercheck q(5/2)=-5/8. It also checks the displayed complex-balance contradiction. Run from the workspace root:

  python3 -B work/programmes/reaction_dynamics/leaf15_certificate_decidability/cycle2/checker.py

The checker returned `VERIFIED` for each arithmetic and graph obligation and concluded “fixed-candidate Foster certificate proves physical-time positive recurrence”; its output explicitly says “no completeness or recognition claim.” The exact computation involves ten reactions with degree at most three and small rational coefficients; there is no oracle, simulation, numerical tolerance, or coefficient acquisition. For a generic supplied network, polynomial expansion and coefficient bit growth would need to be charged; this fixture gives no runtime guarantee for a general compiler.

A finite sound certificate interface suggested by this instance is: supply a finite rational CRN, positive rational rate vector, positive rational weights w, V=1+w·x, constants C>=0 and epsilon>0, a rational polynomial P, and a finite sign proof that P>=0 on N_0^d; check exactly that

  L V(x) = C - epsilon(w·x) - P(x).

Then V is proper, LV<=C<=C V, and LV<=-1 whenever w·x>=(C+1)/epsilon. This is a finite Foster/nonexplosion proof, provided the stated graph/class conditions are separately established. The present checker implements only a particular input and sign-proof form (finite exceptional integer cases plus a product of affine tail factors); it is not yet a generic parser or proof-object kernel.

Verification is decidable for a fixed certificate in this finite language. The following are separate questions and remain UNKNOWN here:

* Whether a suitable w, P, or alternative Lyapunov candidate can be found for an arbitrary supplied higher-order weakly reversible CRN.
* Whether any finite candidate language is complete for positive recurrence, or a general exact algorithm recognizes recurrence for all rational higher-order WR inputs.
* Whether the unrestricted classwise positive-recurrence conjecture holds in dimension at least three. No undecidability reduction was found or is claimed.

The distinction from asymptotic tier criteria is important. Anderson–Kim's published tier theorem is a sound sufficient condition: it quantifies over every tier-sequence escaping to infinity and asks for an active descending source in the top propensity tier. Their Corollary 4.3 implies positive recurrence when its top-tier hypotheses hold. The paper's explicit network-structure theorems are restricted to binary classes. This report has not completed a Corollary 4.3 tier-sequence audit for this exact graph, so it does not claim the example is outside that theorem. Here, instead, one supplied rate vector and one supplied V admit a direct exact polynomial check. Neither the candidate checker nor a finite-box drift check decides the universal tier-sequence condition for arbitrary input.

4. THE 2D RESULT AND HIGHER-DIMENSIONAL SCOPE

The ConStRAINeD project's current publications page reports a proof that every weakly reversible two-species stochastic reaction network is positive recurrent for every positive rate vector. It describes a Wentzell–Freidlin-style coarse graining near the axes and patching of local weakly-endotactic Lyapunov functions in the bulk. The same page lists the five-author manuscript, “A proof of the chemical recurrence conjecture in two dimensions,” under “In preparation.” I found no full proof manuscript there. This is therefore SOURCE-REPORTED, not independently verified theorem evidence. The page itself says the arbitrary-dimensional conjecture remains open.

No extrapolation to three or more species follows from the author-reported 2D result. The geometric proof summary is dimension-specific; higher-dimensional faces and their intersections permit several simultaneous abundance scales. The candidate above supplies one exact 3D higher-order recurrence proof but does not address those general boundary/tier interactions. It also gives no evidence against the 2D claim. Xu's 2026 preprint proves nonexplosion for second-order weakly reversible systems; for the bimolecular WR class it reduces positive recurrence to existence of a stationary distribution in each class, rather than proving that distribution always exists. This third-order example lies beyond that molecularity bound.
