ROOT CONSEQUENCE OF THE GLOBAL QUBIT PI THEOREM

This short derivation is conditional on the separately audited global qubit
theorem being accepted in the final Cycle07 dependency closure. It preserves
the exact stationary compatible-channel interface from checkpoint06 and does
not assert an arbitrary raw-broadcaster or optimized-radius theorem.

Premises (immutable earlier proofs):
 1. A finite faithful stationary KMS self-compatible channel Phi has ONE pure
    canonical sigma-Petz EB comparator Psi with weighted root energies
    E_Psi(rho)<=4 E_Phi(rho) for every input rho.
 2. For the same Psi, e(rho)^2<=A E_Psi(rho),
    A=1+1/sqrt(2), e=T(rho,Psi rho) with HALF trace distance.
 3. For a fixed comparator, the Poisson-orbit Lipschitz-cone entropy argument
    gives e(rho)^3<=6 C D(rho||sigma)b whenever e(tau)^2<=C J_Phi(tau)
    along the orbit, b=T(rho,Phi rho).
The exact proofs and scope are in outputs/canonical-energy/ and its stated
checkpoint06 dependencies; no energy or channel is selected separately for
each state.

The new global scalar-qubit theorem applies to the positive Poisson generator
L_*=I-Phi, which is KMS symmetric and sigma stationary even when the transformed
channel has negative spectrum. It gives J_Phi>=pi E_Phi. Therefore
 e^2<=A E_Psi<=4A E_Phi<=(4A/pi)J_Phi,
and the same single Psi satisfies, for every qubit input,
 e^3<=[24(1+1/sqrt(2))/pi]D(rho||sigma)b.
This improves the earlier weak2-based constant12(1+1/sqrt(2)). Faithful input
regularization and continuity give the finite-dimensional singular-input
extension exactly as in the older fixed-orbit argument.

For an untouched finite classical reference, rho_RB=sum_j p_j |j><j| tensor
rho_j, trace distance decomposes e_R=sum_j p_j e_j and energy decomposes
E_R=sum_j p_j E_j. Jensen gives e_R^2<=sum_j p_j e_j^2<=A E_Psi,R, so the
SAME scalar constant applies. The global qubit pi theorem also sums blockwise;
the fixed-orbit proof yields
 e_R^3<=[24(1+1/sqrt(2))/pi]D(rho_RB||tau_R tensor sigma)b_R.
When rho_B=sigma and tau_R=rho_R, the entropy is I(R:B). Zero classical
weights are discarded on support; no alphabet-size constant is introduced.

No nonlinear arbitrary-quantum-reference pi theorem is available. The new
stationary complete pi theorem does not justify substituting it along a
nonlinear orbit. Thus this addendum does NOT improve the older GNS normal
quantum-reference constant or solve the unrestricted KMS reference power gate.
Novelty, external validation and formal proof remain unclaimed.
