1. Fixed-sigma resource classes and an entire-family theorem

CPTP Phi fixing sigma, with Phi* KMS-self-adjoint at sigma, and with one
actual full-domain CPTP broadcaster whose two marginals BOTH equal Phi.
Let P_sigma be all pure sigma-Petz canonical maps
  sigma=sum_z p_z pi_z,
  Psi(X)=sum_z Tr[M_z X]pi_z,
  M_z=p_z sigma^(-1/2)pi_z sigma^(-1/2).
These are full-domain physical maps; no reference or state labels are inputs.

Let F be ANY nonempty, possibly infinite, family of joint states rho_i on
R_i tensor B, where each R_i is finite but dimensions may vary without bound.
Define the genuine fixed-sigma information budget
  C_sigma(F)=sup_i [I(R_i:B)_(rho_i)+D((rho_i)_B||sigma)]<infinity.
For fixed local maps use worst HALF trace errors over F, and put
  beta_sigma(F)=inf_(Phi in C_sigma) sup_i T(rho_i,(id_Ri tensor Phi)rho_i),
  epsilon_sigma(F)=inf_(Psi in P_sigma) sup_i T(rho_i,(id_Ri tensor Psi)rho_i).
Then

  beta_sigma(F) <= epsilon_sigma(F)
    <= min{1,[K_Q C_sigma(F) beta_sigma(F)]^(1/3)},
  K_Q=24(2+sqrt(2))/pi.                               (R)

If every B marginal is sigma, C_sigma(F)=sup_i I(R_i:B)_(rho_i). For a
family of ordinary states without references, use C_sigma(F)=sup D(rho_i||sigma)
and the smaller K_S=24(1+1/sqrt(2))/pi. The smaller coefficient also applies
to fixed classical-reference families by the separate composition theorem.
Consequently the general quantum-reference family has the scoped squared-
error power law
  epsilon_sigma(F)^2 <= K_Q^(2/3)[C_sigma(F)+1] beta_sigma(F)^(2/3),
because C^(2/3)<=C+1 for C>=0. This is a real exponent-two-thirds theorem in
this exact restricted copy class, not a bound in unrestricted raw b2.

2. Every canonical comparator is itself in the restricted copy class

3. Optional exact attainment and uniform topology for arbitrary references

In fixed local dimension, C_sigma is compact. The Choi matrices of CPTP
broadcasters form a compact finite-dimensional set. Equal marginals, sigma
stationarity and fixed-sigma KMS symmetry are closed linear conditions.
Their continuous marginal image is therefore compact. It is nonempty,
since sigma-spectral dephasing is a pure canonical map in P_sigma.

P_sigma is also compact; finite ensembles of unbounded length do not create
a missing closure. In a real Hermitian orthonormal basis with n=d^2, encode
each pure pi by its trace-one first moments (n-1 coordinates) and the symmetric
weighted Gram second moment v_pi v_pi^T (n(n+1)/2 coordinates), where
  (v_pi)_a=Tr[F_a sigma^(-1/4)pi sigma^(-1/4)].
The image of the compact pure-state space is compact; its finite convex hull
is compact and Caratheodory gives at most n(n+3)/2 atoms. Requiring first
moment sigma is a closed slice. The physical canonical map depends linearly
and continuously on the second moment through the fixed weighted congruence.
Its image is precisely P_sigma, so this channel set is compact. A limiting
measure can thus be represented by one finite pure ensemble with the same
mean sigma and SAME channel, not just approximately represented.

The family error is continuous even when F contains arbitrary finite R_i
dimensions. For a local difference Delta, define the UNNORMALIZED input-first
Choi matrix J_Delta=sum_ab |a><b| tensor Delta(|a><b|). For any pure joint
input |psi>=sum_a |r_a> tensor |a>, let F_psi|a>=|r_a>. Then
  (id_R tensor Delta)(|psi><psi|)
       =(F_psi tensor I)J_Delta(F_psi* tensor I),
  ||F_psi||_infinity^2<=Tr(F_psi*F_psi)=1.
Thus its output trace norm is <=||J_Delta||_1. Convex decomposition extends
this bound to every joint density, uniformly in R. Consequently the worst
HALF error on ANY F is Lipschitz by (1/2)||J_Delta||_1. Both infima in (R)
are therefore attained. Choosing a beta-optimal Phi and then its C4 comparator
produces ONE Psi obeying (R) on the entire family. This comparator need not
be an epsilon-optimal map; no optimality of its Dirichlet construction is claimed.
The compactness reasoning fixes local d and sigma; it supplies no uniform

4. Actual quantitative copy-deficit / classical-relocation floor

For one rho_RB with rho_B=sigma and an actual Phi in C_sigma, write
  b=T(rho,(id_R tensor Phi)rho), I=I(R:B)_rho.
Let delta_EB(rho) be the infimum of the same half joint error over ALL local
full-domain EB channels, and delta_SEP(rho) its half distance to separable
joint states. The produced Psi gives

  delta_SEP(rho)^3 <= delta_EB(rho)^3 <= K_Q I b.       (D)

Thus any genuine local classical-relocation deficit eta>0 forces
  b>=eta^3/(K_Q I)
for every stationary KMS common marginal in this compatible class. The
division is used only when I>0; I=0 implies product rho_R tensor sigma and
zero deficit. No two-extendible-state substitution is made.

For example, if one bounded joint effect 0<=F<=I has an expectation gap eta
above its supremum over separable states, half trace-distance duality gives
delta_SEP>=eta. Such a witness therefore forces the same explicit copy-error
floor eta^3/(K_Q I). This is a dimension-free operational obstruction under
the supplied channel interface, not a general entanglement-measure monogamy
theorem or a claim about arbitrary raw broadcasters.
