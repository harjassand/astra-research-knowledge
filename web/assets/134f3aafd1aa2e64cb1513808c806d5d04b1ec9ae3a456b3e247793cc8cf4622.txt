DERIVATION AND INTERFACE

work/cycle6/c07_l07/spin_certificates_notes.txt: Schur multiplicities and
binomials are exact, fourth roots are enclosed by integer-root arithmetic,
positive exponentials use a range-reduced Taylor interval with an explicit
geometric tail, and all sums/products/divisions round outwards. It checks
sum_j (2j+1)m_N(j)=2^N and requires the exact rational inequality 4N>M^2,
equivalent to t0>0. The result is min(1,3M U_N/(2N^(3/2)D_N)); D_N is accepted
only with a strictly positive lower endpoint. The formula is inherited from
the prior internal separability proof, not reproved by this finite script.

For the square root, alpha=(q/4)^2 and D=C+alpha I place every eigenvalue in
[alpha,2M+alpha]. A dyadic gamma upper-encloses sqrt(2M+alpha) with overshoot
<=q/4, so gamma<=2sqrt(2M+alpha). Exact commuting Newton iterates reduce
z=x/sqrt(lambda) by at least the 5/8 factor while z>=2 and then satisfy
e_next<=e^2/2. The iteration counts are selected by exact rational
inequalities. For rounded updates, the inverse identity bounds the map
Lipschitz constant by Lnewt=1+4(2M+alpha)/alpha as long as the current iterate
stays within sqrt(alpha)/4 of the exact iterate. With k updates, entrywise
nearest dyadic rounding is set so its 3x3 Frobenius/operator contribution is at
most r=min(q,sqrt(alpha))/(16 k Lnewt^k). Telescoping gives total rounding
error <=min(q,sqrt(alpha))/16 and closes the invertibility induction. The exact
Newton error and regularization bias are each <=q/4, so the full error is
<=33q/64<q. The complete argument and constants are written in
spin_certificates_notes.txt; the code checks every rounded iterate is exactly
positive definite.
