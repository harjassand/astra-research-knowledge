REVISION R2 — WHOLE-SYSTEM COMPLEMENT DOES NOT REMOVE THE TENSOR BOTTLENECK
Worker c01_l07. 7 October 2026. Exact internal diagnostic; no peer exposure.

RESULT

The one-replica whole-complement Metropolis proposal repairs the two-phase
obstruction on a single directed cycle, but a single global complement is
still insufficient after tensoring two nearly disconnected blocks. A product
observable is invariant under the complement of both blocks at once, and its
local one-swap energy remains exponentially small in the input precision.

CONSTRUCTION AND INTERFACE

Let F_delta be the six-by-six cyclic matrix from R1:
F[i,i+1]=1, F[i,i-1]=delta=2^(-b), other entries zero. Set
F=diag(F_delta,F_delta), of dimension 12. A microscopic state is a size-six
set I with weight |det F[I,I^c]|^2. Block diagonality forces exactly three
selected labels in each block, so the law is pi_delta tensor pi_delta with
196 positive states among the 924 size-six candidates. Each one-block law has
Z6=2(1+delta^3)^2+6delta^2+6delta^4.

Let s(I_b)=+1 if block b has at least two even local coordinates and -1
otherwise. Define h(I)=s(I_1)s(I_2). The product law is invariant under
cyclic shift in either block, each shift flips one sign, and therefore
E_pi[h]=0 and Var_pi(h)=1.

The one-replica kernel Q chooses with probability 1/2 between:

  (a) a uniform one-selected/one-unselected swap among the 6*6=36 possible
      swaps in the 12-coordinate configuration, accepted by Metropolis; or
  (b) the whole-system complement I -> I^c, accepted by Metropolis.

Both proposals are symmetric and use only raw determinant ratios. A
cross-block one-swap produces block row/column counts 2 versus 4 and hence a
zero determinant, so those proposals hold. Of the 36 swaps, 9 are within each
block. Consequently the first branch is exactly

  Q_loc = (Q6 tensor Id + Id tensor Q6)/4 + Id/2,

where Q6 is the one-block radius-one Metropolis chain. The second branch
complements both blocks. It may reject, but every accepted complement flips
both signs and leaves h unchanged. Thus its Dirichlet contribution on h is
zero.

EXACT ENERGY CALCULATION

R1 established by exact minors and cut-edge enumeration that

  E_Q6(s)=4(3delta^2+15delta^4)/(9Z6).

Under Q_loc, an update of either block changes h by the corresponding change
in s multiplied by the other block's sign, whose square is one. The two
updates have coefficient 1/4 each; hence E_Qloc(h)=E_Q6(s)/2. Mixing Q_loc
and the whole-complement proposal equally gives

  E_Q(h)=E_Q6(s)/4.

On two independent replicas use

  P=(Q tensor Id + Id tensor Q + Swap)/3,
  G(A,B)=h(A)+h(B).

The layer swap preserves G; the other terms contribute twice one third of
E_Q(h). Hence

  E_P(G)=2E_Q(h)/3=E_Q6(s)/6,
  Var_pi(h)=1,
  L_required >= 6/E_Q6(s)
      = 27 Z6/[2(3delta^2+15delta^4)].

As delta tends to zero this is (9+o(1))delta^(-2). For delta<=1/2,
Z6>=2 and 3delta^2+15delta^4<=27delta^2/4, so

  L_required >= 4/delta^2 = 4^(b+1).

This is a lower bound for the specified kernel. It is not a lower bound for
all reversible or auxiliary-state kernels.

BOUNDARY AND NEXT GATE

The combined one-replica proposal is a sum of elementary one-swap changes and
one complement of the entire system. Its failure comes from preserving the
product of two block signs. A kernel that detects the blocks and complements
one block at a time would evade this witness; the present result does not
show that such a construction is impossible. The useful design constraint is
more precise: macroscopic reorientation must be selectively addressable, and
the selector/proposal normalizer must be computable without a partition
oracle. A general mismatch-register flow should be tested against both this
tensor witness and the single-block bit-precision witness from R1.
