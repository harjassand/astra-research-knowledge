1. EXACT CHARACTER ACROSS THE FIBER-SATURATION TRANSITION

Use J=pi(diag(1,0,-1)), maximum eigenvalue N, and its whole shell K_t
at eigenvalue N-t. Write g_t=dim K_t. The exact Weyl specialization is

 sum g_t z^t=(1-z^(a+1))(1-z^B)(1-z^(N+2))
                         /[(1-z)^2(1-z^2)].             (1.1)

Define G_t=floor((t+2)^2/4) for t>=0, and G_t=0 for t<0. Since the
first a-dependent subtraction does not start before a+1,

                    g_t=G_t-G_(t-B), 0<=t<=a.           (1.2)

Consequently, throughout the ENTIRE interval needed here,

 (t+1)min(t+1,B)/4 <=g_t<=(t+1)min(t+1,B).               (1.3)

For t<B this follows from the original quadratic G_t bounds. For
t>=B use G_s-G_(s-1)=floor((s+2)/2) and sum it from s=t-B+1 to t.
Each summand is between(s+1)/2 and s+1. The lower sum is
B(t+1)/2-B(B-1)/4>=B(t+1)/4; the upper is<=B(t+1).

Set V_R=sum_(t=0)^(R-1)g_t for1<=R<=a. It satisfies

 R^2 min(R,B)/32 <=V_R<=R^2 min(R,B).                    (1.4)

For the upper, sum(t+1)<=R(R+1)/2<=R^2. For R<=B, the lower follows

each has t+1>=R/2 and min(t+1,B)>=min(R,B)/2=B/2. Equation(1.3)

The exact character is symmetric under J->-J, a represented Weyl
