CYCLE08 COMPLETE NONLINEAR QUBIT PRIOR CHECK
Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: bounded primary-source comparison for a proposed complete nonlinear estimate J >= pi E on every finite KMS-symmetric qubit QMS, every faithful joint system-reference state, and any finite untouched quantum reference. J is physical entropy production; E is the KMS-symmetric-root Dirichlet form. This memo does not validate the internally derived candidate or make a priority claim.

Finding
No checked primary source states a positive universal constant for this exact complete KMS-qubit J/E inequality. There is a precise complete C=4 theorem on the GNS-symmetric subclass, but it does not extend to arbitrary KMS symmetry. Kastoryano--Temme's p=1 regularity conjectures give exact candidate constants 2 (weak) and 4 (strong) at the system-only level; they are not theorems and contain no ancillary quantifier. Liu--Wan--Wu give a primitive KMS qubit with complete MLSI constant zero, but their inequality compares entropy production with relative entropy, not with E; that example is not a counterexample to J >= pi E.

1. Exact p=1 interface in Kastoryano--Temme
Kastoryano--Temme, arXiv:1207.3261v2 (12 Jun 2013), define reversibility by Gamma_sigma L = L* Gamma_sigma (Definition 6). Their p-Dirichlet form has E_1(f)=J(f)/2 after f=Gamma_sigma^{-1}(rho), and E_2(I_{2,1}(f)) equals the KMS-root Dirichlet form at sqrt(rho). Therefore their weak p=1 endpoint E_1 >= E_2 would give J >= 2E; their strong endpoint E_1 >= 2E_2 would give J >= 4E. The map conversion is essential: I_{2,1}(f)=Gamma_sigma^{-1/2}(sqrt(rho)), so E_2 is evaluated on the relative square root and is not an unweighted observable Dirichlet form.

Neither endpoint is asserted as a general theorem. Definition 9 defines weak and strong regularity; Conjecture 19 says every primitive Liouvillian is weakly regular and that reversible ones are strongly regular. The paper explicitly leaves the conjecture unresolved in its conclusion. Its claim is system-only: it quantifies over f in M_d and does not assert the inequality for L tensor id_n. In particular, the strong conjecture cannot be treated as a prior C=4 proof for KMS qubits, and the weak conjecture cannot be treated as a prior C=2 proof.

2. Proven complete C=4 comparator, restricted to GNS symmetry
Bardet, arXiv:1710.01039v1 (3 Oct 2017), Theorem 4.1 proves strong L_p regularity for all p under sigma_Tr-DBC; Corollary 4.1 specializes to EP_L(rho) >= 4 E_L(sigma_Tr^{-1/4} sqrt(rho) sigma_Tr^{-1/4}). Definition 4.2 makes sigma_Tr-DBC self-adjointness for the GNS-type inner product Tr[sigma_Tr X*Y], and the paper explicitly says this is stronger than its KMS reversibility.

For a primitive GNS-symmetric system and finite untouched reference R, apply that theorem to the amplified QMS P tensor id_R. Its decoherence-free algebra is the reference factor and its sigma_Tr is sigma tensor I_R/d_R; this is the product case of the paper's sigma_Tr construction. The theorem then covers arbitrary faithful joint rho, without a fixed-marginal condition, and gives complete C=4 for this GNS subclass in the target root-energy convention. The target J and E are unchanged if the reference component of the product stationary state is replaced by any faithful eta_R: the generator acts trivially on R, and the eta_R factor cancels in Gamma^(1/2) L Gamma^(-1/2). This is a useful exact comparator, but the non-GNS KMS qubit class is outside its hypothesis.

3. A complete entropy-to-entropy route fails, but this is not an E counterexample
Liu--Wan--Wu, arXiv:2609.13726v1 (12 Sep 2026), construct a primitive KMS-symmetric QMS on M_2 that fails CMLSI. The example uses V=[[0,1],[2,0]] and invariant state diag(1/5,4/5); Proposition 5.1 verifies KMS symmetry and non-GNS symmetry. Theorem 5.2 and Corollary 5.3 show complete MLSI constant zero, witnessed with an M_2 ancilla. CMLSI is a lower bound of J by a positive multiple of relative entropy to the reference. It is not a lower bound of J by the root Dirichlet energy E, so it does not refute J >= pi E.

Gao--Wang, arXiv:2609.20753v2 (5 Oct 2026), Theorem B gives a positive ordinary MLSI bound for primitive finite-dimensional KMS-symmetric QMS from a spectral gap and hyperboundedness, and a bound in terms of KMS coercivity and sigma. It removes the need for L_p regularity in that entropy-to-entropy estimate. It neither proves weak or strong p=1 regularity nor states a complete J/E bound.

Status boundary
For all KMS-symmetric qubit QMS with arbitrary untouched finite reference, the exact best prior constant in J >= C E is UNKNOWN in this bounded primary-source check. The verified C=4 result covers the GNS subclass only. KT's candidate weak C=2 and strong C=4 endpoints are explicitly conjectural and system-only. The LWW CMLSI obstruction is not an E obstruction. No priority claim follows from these source checks.
