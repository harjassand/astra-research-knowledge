## Cycle 2 lead: directed finite-volume safety with guarded switches

The load-bearing transport inequality is an endpoint minimization. With concentration (x_i=Z_i/(V\alpha_i)) and one-molecule hop intensity (V c_{ik}x_i),

\[
m_i(x)=\frac{\sum_k c_{ki}x_k-x_i\sum_kc_{ik}}{\alpha_i}.
\]

For (a_i\le x_i\le b_i), the migration contribution to lower- and upper-face inward drift on collars of width \(\ell\) is respectively

\[
\frac{\sum_kc_{ki}a_k-(a_i+\ell)\sum_kc_{ik}}{\alpha_i},\qquad
\frac{(b_i-\ell)\sum_kc_{ik}-\sum_kc_{ki}b_k}{\alpha_i}.
\]

Adding these directed-flux margins to a uniformly positive local reaction margin gives an effective face drift. The proof inserts it into the finite-volume factorial-corrected exponential-potential argument. On a switch, a mode-specific potential restarts only if the state is still in the source box and in the target guard core. For (N_T) segments and the explicit constants in the proof,

\[
\Pr\{\text{exit an active mode box by }T\}
\le A_V\,\mathbb E[N_T]+C_*VT e^{-c_*V}.
\]

The mode at a jump-tied switch is the old predictable mode (J_{t-}) for that jump; the selected new mode governs subsequent intensities. This is a single-mark CTMC theorem; common shocks or batch jumps need aggregate mark bounds. The fixture's listed reaction rates are fixed singleton values. Any adaptive rate set must separately pass the same uniform collar checks.

In the exact three-site fixture, site volume multipliers are \((1,2,3)\), the transport graph is the one-way cycle (1\to2\to3\to1), two cubic reaction modes have local boxes \([0.2,0.6]^3\) and \([0.4,0.9]^3\), and the shared guard is \([0.45,0.55]^3\). Exact rational Bernstein checks give (V_0=1005), (c_*=131/26174250), and maximum activity \(7572/625\). For (V=20{,}000{,}000), at most two segments, and (T=e^{c_*V/2}\), the risk upper bound evaluates to (5.33894\times10^{-13}); this decimal uses ordinary floating point, not directed rounding. A rate-(V\Lambda) thinning implementation can still require up to (1.31962\times10^{30}) candidate events over that horizon, so the certificate is not a feasible simulation claim.

The fixture also excludes a common convex invariant polytope inside the stated safe cube that contains the whole diagonal guard: the balanced directed ring leaves the equal-concentration diagonal invariant, and the two scalar mode drifts force incompatible lower- and upper-endpoint signs. This rules out the N35 common-polytope certificate route for this fixture, not all possible barriers or controllers.

Two independent mathematical audits found no fatal gap under the stated single-mark CTMC, stopping-time, no-reset, mode-at-jump, and finite-expected-segment assumptions. Both reproduced the exact constants. A systems audit added the singleton-rate qualification. The detailed independent review is [here](robust_acquisition/directed_guarded_switching_audit.md). No physical plant, experimental rate acquisition, external proof review, or finite-volume simulator was supplied.

The controlled switching example is implemented separately as a two-mode finite-volume birth/death relay. With (V=100), it runs mode H from molecule count 45 until 55, then mode L until 45, with safety aborts at 10 and 90. A one-second leg deadline has exact subgenerator/uniformization probability (p_H(1)\approx0.9999720896); twenty alternating legs succeed with probability (p_H(1)^{20}\approx0.99944194). It cannot promise probability-one guard arrival at a fixed deadline: the no-event trajectory at the leg start has positive mass (e^{-155t}). Removing the deadline gives eventual hits for the full irreducible chain, but operation in a proper finite safety band exits almost surely under infinite repetition. The finite-state algorithm also computes event and occupancy costs; numerical joule parameters are declared examples only. Because birth and death work charges differ, the expected cost recursion alternates H- and L-leg costs even though their hit probabilities match. The exact interface allows no mode, rate, or timing choices, so its legal policy class is a singleton: an equally restricted CTMDP can only tie this fixed-policy CTMC evaluation. This does not establish optimality among controllers allowed to modulate rates, delay guard switches, or choose modes. The resettable per-leg deadline is a continuous clock; exact finite-leg kernels and strong-Markov recursion are used rather than claiming the complete relay is a homogeneous finite-state CTMDP. Details are in the [relay addendum](energy_feedback/CYCLE2_ADDENDUM.md), [comparator audit](energy_feedback/CYCLE2_COMPARATOR.md), and [script](energy_feedback/cycle2_relay.py).

Prior art sharply limits the claim. Anand–Jagtap–Zamani's Theorem III.4 already treats bounded Lipschitz state-dependent mode-switch intensities with coupled mode-specific barriers for diffusion states ([primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.12420v1)). Wisniewski–Bujorianu–Sloth's 2020 p-safety paper explicitly includes spontaneous and guard-forced jumps in PDMPs; a fixed finite-volume CTMC lies in that broad process class. Their safety formulation uses occupation measures for a specified process. Zaker et al. (2026) give compositional safety-controller synthesis for stochastic hybrid interconnections. The retained distinction is the explicit finite-volume, directed heterogeneous-site face calculation and its guarded restart bound; whether those general frameworks or exact augmented-state reachability subsume or improve it remains unresolved. See [the proof](directed_guarded_switching/PROOF.md), [source record](directed_guarded_switching/SOURCES.md), [exact verifier](directed_guarded_switching/verify_certificate.py), and [Cycle 2 report](CYCLE2_REPORT.md).

## Decision and next decisive gate

Do not present the directed-switching result as a new barrier method. Keep it as a precise conditional theorem and exact fixture. The next high-ceiling engineering question is whether the safe, dithered initialization and covariance updates in the 2026 rate-optimal chance-constrained LQR can be proved for singular process noise using a safely maintained finite-horizon reachable-covariance lower bound. The current file only proves static covariance identities and block contraction; before claiming anything, a full adaptive regression-confidence, covariance-tracking, every-time chance-feasibility, and regret argument must be completed against the exact source assumptions. If that cannot be closed, retain `UNKNOWN` and report the block Gramian as an acquisition diagnostic.

For the directed CTMC theorem, the shortest remaining decisive gate is an exact source-level embedding against 2021 state-dependent stochastic switching, 2020 PDMP p-safety, and finite-state CTMDP reachability, followed by external proof review only if a strict interface or complexity advantage survives. For the PDE filter, it is an adversarial exact-characteristic simulator with within-cell extremal profiles and matched delay-CBF controls. For any real application, acquire direction-specific rates, site volumes, model discrepancy, sensors, latency and energy; finite coupon/tracer averages do not identify the transport geometry or validate these parameters.
