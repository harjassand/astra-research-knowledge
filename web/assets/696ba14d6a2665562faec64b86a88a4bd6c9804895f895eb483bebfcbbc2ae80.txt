EXACT INTERFACE AND STRONGEST CANDIDATE

Fix a local irreducible SU(2) spin j=p/2>0, p an integer, local dimension
D=p+1; beta>0; delta>0; finite M,B>=0. These are fixed as N grows. On
(C^D)^tensor N let S_a be the local spin matrices, J_a=sum_i S_a^(i),
J^2=sum_a J_a^2, and Q_A=sum_ab A_ab {J_a,J_b}/2. The known parameters
A=A^T real, ||A||<=M, and b real, |b|<=B may vary with N. Put

 K_N = beta J^2/N + Q_A/N^(1+delta) + b.J,
 rho_N = exp(K_N)/Tr exp(K_N).

There is an explicitly specified positive probability mixture sigma_N of
IDENTICAL local spin-j thermal products R_j(m)^tensor N such that

  T(rho_N,sigma_N) <= C exp[-c N (log N)^2],                  (H1)

for all N>=N0, uniformly over admitted known A,b. Here c,C,N0 depend only
on fixed j,beta,delta,M,B. One safe asymptotic c is
delta^2/(8192 beta). A more useful public statement is the exact
nonasymptotic certificate (H15) below, including very poor constants.

Later companion addenda FIXED_ANISOTROPY_NEIGHBORHOOD.txt and
EXACT_FULL_SEPARABILITY.txt strengthen the result to a fixed small
anisotropy neighborhood and exact FULL separability, respectively.
They are subsequent attributed work, not part of the frozen INITIAL.

This covers every fixed higher spin and qudits carrying the irreducible
spin-j representation. It does NOT cover all generators of fundamental
SU(D), unknown-parameter decoding, growing local j, arbitrary anisotropy
of order 1/N, negative beta, or an external-reference-preserving channel.
Taking b=b0/N^eta with any eta>=0 is included. Taking j=1/2, beta=2,
delta=1/2 and b=b0/N^(3/4) includes the peer's critical model.

The fixed-cutoff argument already gives exp(-cN) for every prescribed c
after increasing N0. The growing cutoff supplies a single explicit
exp[-cN(logN)^2] construction. No preparation/runtime advantage follows
without a finite-bit SDE, stopping and weighted-sampling compiler.

1. THE EXACT NORMALIZED HIGHER-SPIN PRODUCT KERNEL

Use Bloch coordinates m in the OPEN unit ball, x=|m|^2. Let
rho_1/2(m)=(I+m.sigma)/2. On the spin-j subspace Sym^p(C^2), define
q_p(m)=rho_1/2(m)^tensor p restricted to Sym^p, and R_j=q_p/Tr q_p.
This is a genuine positive local density. Define the positive polynomial

 F_p(x)=sum_(h=0)^floor(p/2) binom(p+1,2h+1) x^h,
 z_p(m)=Tr q_p(m)=2^(-p) F_p(x),
 eta_p(x)=p+2(1-x) F_p'(x)/F_p(x).                         (H2)

The trace formula follows by summing the p+1 eigenvalues
2^(-p)(1+|m|)^(p-k)(1-|m|)^k. F_p>=p+1, so all rational
coefficients and their derivatives are smooth through m=0.
Writing m=tanh(r/2)u, |u|=1, gives exactly

 R_j(m)=exp(r u.S)/Z_j(r),
 Z_j(r)=sum_(h=-j)^j exp(rh)
       =sinh((p+1)r/2)/sinh(r/2), Z_j(0)=p+1.              (H3)

Set k_N(m)=R_j(m)^tensor N. For a unit real n, u_n=n.m,
v_n=n-u_n m, r_n=n cross m, and define differential operators

 B_n=N eta_p(x)u_n + v_n.grad,
 R_n=r_n.grad.

Then, as FULL matrix identities,

 {n.J,k_N}=B_n k_N,       [n.J,k_N]=i R_n k_N,
 { (n.J)^2,k_N}/2=(B_n^2-R_n^2)k_N/4.                    (H4)

Proof without a semiclassical approximation: project pN virtual qubits
onto Sym^p in each of the N local blocks. These fixed projections commute
with collective virtual spin, and the projected unnormalized kernel is
q_p(m)^tensor N. The exact virtual-qubit identity has B_virtual=pN u_n+
v_n.grad. Since q_p^tensor N=z_p^N k_N, conjugating this differential
operator by z_p^N adds N v_n.grad log z_p. This equals
2N(1-x)F_p'/F_p u_n, giving (H2),(H4). Rotation leaves z_p invariant,
so the commutator identity has no normalization correction. Constant
matrix left/right actions commute with scalar differential operators.
Applying them twice gives B_n^2=the anticommutator squared and
R_n^2=minus the commutator squared; their difference is 2{(n.J)^2,k_N}.
In particular the angular MINUS sign cannot be discarded.

2. AN EXACT POSITIVE HEAT SEED FOR EVERY LOCAL SPIN

For any t>0 and any finite direct sum of spin representations,

 e^(tJ^2) = c_t int_0^infinity r^2 sinch(r/2) exp[-r^2/(4t)]
                       int_(S^2) exp(r u.J) du dr,
 c_t=[2 sqrt(pi) t^(3/2) exp(t/4)]^(-1),                  (H5)

with normalized spherical measure. This is verified directly on every
spin-l irrep: the spherical average is Z_l(r)/(2l+1) times identity.
Cancel sinh(r/2) with sinch(r/2), and the scalar integral becomes

 (2/(2l+1)) int_0^infinity r exp[-r^2/(4t)]
                              sinh((2l+1)r/2) dr
 =2 sqrt(pi) t^(3/2) exp[t(l+1/2)^2].

The last equality is the derivative of the elementary Gaussian cosh
integral. This proves (H5), including multiplicity spaces, without a
claimed general SU(D) heat formula.

Consequently e^(tJ^2)/Tr e^(tJ^2) is EXACTLY a positive identical-spin-
thermal-product mixture with radial field density proportional to

 r^2 sinch(r/2) exp[-r^2/(4t)] [Z_j(r)/(p+1)]^N,
 m=tanh(r/2)u.                                           (H6)

Useful elementary bounds valid for r>=0 are
1 <= Z_j(r)/(p+1) <= exp(jr), and sinch(r/2)<=exp(r/2).
The lower bound uses symmetry of the magnetic levels. These weak bounds
suffice for every beta>0, including the ordered regime; no de Finetti,
critical CLT or unverified large-deviation theorem is imported.

3. CHOOSING A GROWING CUTOFF AND ISOTROPIC SHIFT

Let gamma=1+delta, k=delta/8, and choose the public constant

 a0=max{2,8 beta j,8 beta},
 a_N=a0+k log N,
 m_in=tanh(a_N/2),
 r_star=tanh((a_N+B)/2),
 g_N=(1-r_star)/2=1/[exp(a_N+B)+1],
 r0_N=(1+r_star)/2=1-g_N,
 Lambda_N=(2M+1)/(1-r0_N^2)^2,
 C_N=A+Lambda_N I, L_N=Lambda_N+M,
 t0_N=beta/N-Lambda_N/N^gamma.                           (H7)

For all sufficiently large N, t0_N>=beta/(2N)>0. Quantitative public
upper/lower bounds are obtained from

 G0=exp(a0+B)+1,
 Lambda0=(2M+1)G0^2,
 L0=Lambda0+M,
 g_N>=G0^(-1)N^(-k),
 Lambda_N<=Lambda0 N^(2k), L_N<=L0 N^(2k).                (H8)

Indeed 1-r0^2=g_N(2-g_N)>=g_N. The heat condition follows from
N^(delta-2k)>=2 Lambda0/beta. Lambda_N>=2M+1, so C_N>=I.
Write

 H_N=Q_(C_N)/N^gamma+b.J,
 K_N=t0_N J^2+H_N.                                      (H9)

Casimir J^2 commutes with every collective spin polynomial. This shift
changes only the mathematical seed, not the target K_N.

4. EXACT GENERATOR AND CERTIFIED DIFFUSION MARGIN

Diagonalize C=sum lambda_i n_i n_i^T solely to display the derivation.
By (H4), the sandwich generator M_H(X)={H,X}/2 satisfies

 M_H k_N=G_N k_N,
 G_N f=V_N f+a_N.grad f+D_C:Hess f/(4N^gamma),
 D_C=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T,
 a_N=(N eta_p(x)-1)/(2N^gamma)
                   [C m-(m^TCm)m]+[b-(b.m)m]/2,
 V_N={N^2 eta_p(x)^2 m^TCm+N eta_p(x)Tr C
       +N[2(1-x)eta_p'(x)-eta_p(x)]m^TCm}/(4N^gamma)
                   +N eta_p(x)b.m/2.                    (H10)

These formulas are basis independent, exact and smooth. They follow from
(v_n.grad)v_n-(r_n.grad)r_n=-2u_n v_n and
v_n.grad[eta_p(x)u_n]=eta_p(1-u_n^2)
                         +2(1-x)eta_p' u_n^2.
Taking the trace in (H10) proves V_N=Tr(H_N k_N) exactly.

For the identity coefficient,
D_I=(1-x)(I-mm^T), whose smallest eigenvalue is (1-x)^2.
For arbitrary ||A||<=M, ||D_A||<=M(1+x)<=2M on the unit ball.
Thus throughout |m|<=r0_N,

 D_C=Lambda_N D_I+D_A >= I,
 D_C <= (I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T) <= L_N I.                      (H11)

The upper inequality uses C>=0 and ||I-mm^T||<=1. In particular the
square root has an explicit positive margin even though the radius
approaches the boundary. The global anisotropic diffusion is NOT
declared positive; the entire proof uses (H11) and stopping.

5. SEED TAIL, WITH THE WHOLE NORMALIZER CHARGED

Let rho0=e^(t0_N J^2)/Tr e^(t0_N J^2), represented by (H6).
The event |m|>m_in is exactly r>a_N. Since t0_N<=beta/N, for r>=a_N
the unnormalized density is at most

 r^2 exp[-N(r^2/(4beta)-log(Z_j(r)/(p+1)))+r/2]
 <= r^2 exp[-N r^2/(16beta)].

Here a_N>=8beta j gives r^2/(4beta)-jr>=r^2/(8beta), and
a_N>=8beta pays r/2. Split the Gaussian exponent in half and integrate
the other half over [0,infinity). The tail integral is at most

 [sqrt(pi)/4](32beta/N)^(3/2)
                         exp[-N a_N^2/(32beta)].

For the lower normalizer, integrate r=N^(-1/2)R, R in [1,2], use
t0_N>=beta/(2N), sinch>=1 and Z_j/(p+1)>=1. This gives

 lower_normalizer >= Lbeta N^(-3/2),
 Lbeta=(7/3)exp(-2/beta).

Consequently, with the entirely explicit constant

 Cbeta=sqrt(pi)(32beta)^(3/2)/(4 Lbeta),
 p_bad <= Cbeta exp[-N a_N^2/(32beta)].                   (H12)

Replace bad seed branches by m=0. The resulting normalized product
mixture rho0' has ||rho0'-rho0||_1<=2p_bad and every starting point has
|m0|<=m_in. It is specified by public scalar density, not a rho_N oracle.

6. STOPPING PROBABILITY WITH BOUNDED UNSCALED FIELD

Run until first hitting |m|=r0_N the ordinary 3-dimensional SDE

 dm=a_N(m)dt+sqrt[D_C(m)/(2N^gamma)]dW,
 w_t=exp[int_0^(t wedge tau) V_N(m_u)du],

and freeze both m and w after tau. Smooth coefficients on a neighborhood
of the closed ball plus (H11) give standard existence until stopping.
Every path output w_t k_N(m_(t wedge tau)) is positive and iid.

The exact field drift is F_b(m)=[b-(b.m)m]/2. Its Lipschitz constant on
the unit ball is at most B. A field-only deterministic trajectory q_t
from m0 satisfies |q_t|<=tanh(atanh|m0|+Bt/2)<=r_star for t<=1;
this follows directly from d|q|/dt<=(B/2)(1-|q|^2).

Let U_p=p+2F_p'(1)/(p+1). Since coefficients of F_p are nonnegative,
0<eta_p(x)<=U_p for 0<=x<=1. The anisotropic drift obeys

 beta_aniso<=L_N r0_N (U_p+1)N^(-delta)/2
            <=L0(U_p+1)N^(-delta+2k)/2.

For N^(delta-3k)>=2 exp(B)G0 L0(U_p+1), this is <=g_N/[4exp(B)].
Gronwall compares the stopped SDE with its field-only trajectory:
sup|m-q|<=exp(B)[beta_aniso+sup|M|], where M is its martingale part.
At an exit the discrepancy is at least g_N, hence
sup|M|>=3g_N/[4exp(B)]. Each coordinate has quadratic variation
<=L_N/(2N^gamma). Exponential-martingale maximal bounds and a union
bound over three coordinates and both signs give

 P(tau<=1)<=6 exp[-3g_N^2 N^gamma/(16 exp(2B)L_N)]
            <=6 exp[-K1 N^(1+delta-4k)],
 K1=3/[16 exp(2B)G0^2 L0]>0.                            (H13)

Here delta-3k=5delta/8>0 and delta-4k=delta/2>0. Unlike a crude
absolute drift bound, the exact field-flow comparison permits bounded
unscaled b.J. No unscaled-field weight or success cost is omitted.

7. EXACT STOPPED DUHAMEL ERROR AND TRACE NORMALIZATION

The global operator bound is

 ||H_N||<=h_N=L_N jN(jN+1)/N^gamma+BjN
             <=BjN+H0 N^(1-delta+2k),
 H0=L0 j(j+1).                                         (H14)

In particular w_t<=exp(h_N); ||M_H k_N||_1<=h_N; and the Hermitian
trace-norm semigroup norm of exp(t M_H) is at most exp(t h_N).
Set W_t=E[w_t k_N(m_(t wedge tau))]. Ito and (H10) give EXACTLY

 W_t'-M_H W_t=-E[1_(tau<=t) w_tau M_H k_N(m_tau)],
 exp(M_H)rho0'-W_1=int_0^1 exp[(1-t)M_H]
                        E[1_(tau<=t)w_tau M_H k_N(m_tau)]dt.

Hence ||exp(M_H)rho0'-W_1||_1<=h_N exp(2h_N)P(tau<=1), and seed
replacement contributes at most 2exp(h_N)p_bad. Because J^2 is central,
G=exp(M_H)rho0=exp(K_N)/Tr exp(t0_NJ^2).
Its trace is >=1: in each spin-l irrep,
Tr H_l/(2l+1)=Tr C l(l+1)/(3N^gamma)>=0, and scalar trace Jensen
gives Tr exp(H_l)>=2l+1. Multiplicity weights and exp(t0_lJ^2)
are positive, so the inequality survives summation.
For positive G,W, T(G/TrG,W/TrW)<=||G-W||_1/TrG.

Normalize W_1 to obtain sigma_N. The public whole-density certificate is

 T(rho_N,sigma_N)
 <=2 Cbeta exp[h_N-N a_N^2/(32beta)]
     +6 h_N exp[2h_N-K1 N^(1+delta/2)].                  (H15)

There is one positive mixture for the full N-spin density. No fixed-k
marginal is substituted. Each local output is the same R_j(m) for every
site in a given branch. Its normalized mixing law weights the path by
w_1/Ew_1; neither the sign nor normalization is free.

Since k=delta/8, a_N>=k logN and h_N/N<=Bj+H0 N^(-3delta/4), the first
term is eventually <=2Cbeta exp[-k^2 N(logN)^2/(64beta)]. The second
term is smaller eventually: N^(delta/2) dominates (logN)^2 and h_N=O(N).
Absorbing its prefactor gives (H1) with c=k^2/(128beta).
All sufficient N thresholds are public scalar inequalities in (H8),
(H12),(H13) and the final elementary growth comparisons. They may be
enormous. This is not an asymptotic claim uniform as delta approaches 0.

8. CRITICAL NORMALIZATION LEMMA AND BOUNDARIES

For the special critical coupling beta_c=3/[2j(j+1)], put v=j(j+1)/3.
The local character has the exact scalar defect

 f_j(r)=v r^2/2-log[Z_j(r)/(p+1)],
 gamma_j=((p+1)^4-1)/2880,
 gamma_j r^4/[1+(p+1)^2r^2/(4pi^2)] <= f_j(r) <=gamma_j r^4.

Proof: the primary NIST sinh product gives
log[Z_j/D]=sum_n log[(1+D^2 x_n)/(1+x_n)], x_n=r^2/(4pi^2n^2).
Subtracting the quadratic term leaves
sum_n int_1^(D^2) t x_n^2/(1+t x_n)dt.
Use 1<=1+t x_n<=1+D^2r^2/(4pi^2) and sum n^-4=pi^4/90.
This recovers the N^(-3/4) critical radial normalization and confirms
the higher-spin critical coupling, but is NOT needed for (H1).

Known scalar/isotropic cases can have distance exactly zero, so no
matching positive exponential lower bound is asserted. Conversely this
moving-cutoff proof alone supplies no extension to gamma=1: the isotropic shift and
anisotropic drift inequalities require strictly positive delta. No
macro-anisotropy conclusion is smuggled into that limit. The separate
fixed-neighborhood addendum instead imposes an explicit small amplitude.
