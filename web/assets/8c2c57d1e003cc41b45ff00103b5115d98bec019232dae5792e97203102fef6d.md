## Exact frame and support

On \(M_3\), let

\[
B_1=|0\rangle\langle1|+|1\rangle\langle0|,
\quad B_2=|1\rangle\langle2|+|2\rangle\langle1|,
\quad B_3=-i|1\rangle\langle2|+i|2\rangle\langle1|,
\]

and use the positive HS operator

\[
 Q=\frac32\sum_{j=1}^3|B_j\rangle\langle B_j|.
\]

The matrices have rational real/imaginary entries, are traceless and pairwise \(\tau\)-orthogonal, and \(\tau(B_j^2)=2/3\). Hence \(Q\) is a rank-three orthogonal projector and \(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q=3\).

For a pure state \(P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\), write \(p_k=|\psi_k|^2\). Directly,

\[
 V_Q(P)=\frac32\sum_j\langle\psi|B_j|\psi\rangle^2
 \le 6p_1(p_0+p_2)=6p_1(1-p_1).
\]

Rank-one refinement can only increase the POVM support objective, by weighted Cauchy--Schwarz. Every rank-one POVM is an ensemble with barycenter \(I/3\), so its mean \(p_1\) is \(1/3\). Jensen's inequality gives the global upper bound

\[
S_3(Q)\le 6\left(\frac13-\mathbb E p_1^2\right)\le\frac43.
\]

This value is attained by the four states

\[
|\psi_{s,t}\rangle=(|0\rangle+s|1\rangle+t|2\rangle)/\sqrt3,
\qquad s,t\in\{-1,1\},
\]

with effects \(M_{s,t}=\frac34|\psi_{s,t}\rangle\langle\psi_{s,t}|\). Their projectors average to \(I/3\), so the effects sum to \(I\), and each has score \(4/3\). Thus the canonical support is exactly \(4/3\), globally over all POVMs.

## Global extension-side dual certificate

Let \(J_{RAB}\) be the normalized Choi state of any symmetric broadcaster, with tensor order \(R,A,B\). Its one-body marginals are \(I/3\): trace preservation fixes \(R\), unitality fixes \(A\), and output symmetry fixes \(B\). Define the exact integer-entry Hermitian matrix

\[
 K=\sum_{j=1}^3\left(B_j^T\otimes B_j\otimes I
       +B_j^T\otimes I\otimes B_j
       -(B_j^2)^T\otimes I\otimes I\right).
\]

Using the Choi correlation identity and symmetry of the two output marginals,

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q(2\Phi-I))=\frac32\operatorname{Tr}(KJ_{RAB}).
\]

The following rational diagonal potentials give a strict operator certificate:

\[
Y=\operatorname{diag}(237,439,158)/1000,
\qquad Z=\operatorname{diag}(123,554,157)/1000,
\]

\[
Y\otimes I\otimes I+I\otimes Z\otimes I+I\otimes I\otimes Z-K\succ0.
\]

The exact LDL factorization has 27 positive rational pivots; `exact_certificate.py` reconstructs the matrices, checks `L*D*L.T` entrywise, and asserts every pivot is positive. Since each marginal of \(J\) is \(I/3\),

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(KJ)\le\frac{\operatorname{Tr}Y+2\operatorname{Tr}Z}{3}
 =\frac{417}{500},
\quad\text{and therefore}\quad
 \operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q(2\Phi-I))\le\frac{1251}{1000}.
\]

The certificate needs only Choi positivity and the three fixed one-body marginals. It covers all channels/extensions in the assigned interface, including those without the extra HS-self-adjoint or HS-positive restrictions.

The attempted legal channel is the qutrit universal cloner marginal, used as a control rather than as a counterexample. With \(P_{sym}=(I+F)/2\) on \(A\otimes B\), define

\[
 \mathcal B(\rho)=\frac12P_{sym}(\rho\otimes I)P_{sym}.
\]

Its normalized \(27\times27\) Choi matrix is the rational matrix

\[
 J_{RAB}=\frac16\sum_{i,j=0}^2 |i\rangle\langle j|_R\otimes
 P_{sym}(|i\rangle\langle j|\otimes I)P_{sym}.
\]

The exact script checks Choi positivity, trace one, output-swap invariance, and all three marginals equal to \(I/3\). The marginal is

\[
 \Phi(A)=\frac58 A+\frac18\operatorname{Tr}(A)I,
\]

so it is unital, trace-preserving, CP, HS-self-adjoint, and HS-positive (eigenvalues \(1\) on identity and \(5/8\) on traceless matrices). For the rank-three \(Q\) above its channel-side value is exactly \(3/4\), far below support \(4/3\).
