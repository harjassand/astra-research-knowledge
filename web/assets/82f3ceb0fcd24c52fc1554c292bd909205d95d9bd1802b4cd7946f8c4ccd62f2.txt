1. Candidate exact suspension transfer, sharpening arXiv:2610.05474v1 Prop 7.1
Source read live: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05474v1, sections 6 and 7.
The source states a factor 2 in the squared tilt-Taylor norm for general f,

Hypothesis: for every isotropic log-concave nu in every dimension,
||kappa_(d+1)^nu(u,.,...,.)||_HS^2 <= b ||u||^2.
Let mu be isotropic regular strongly log-concave, f smooth bounded Hessian,
E f=0, E f^2=1; do NOT assume E[X f]=0. With N independent Xi and Laplace
noise eta_beta, put S=(N^-1/2 sum f(Xi)+eta_beta)/sigma_beta,
sigma_beta^2=1+2/beta^2. The source's convexity argument still applies when
N >= (beta ||Hess f||_infty / a)^2. Let Y=(S,X1,...,XN), C=Cov(Y).
The X-block of C is exactly identity and C_SS=1. C is positive definite
because the added independent noise has positive variance. Thus Z=C^-1/2 Y
is isotropic and log-concave.

For any cumulant order d+1,
kappa_Y(v0,...,vd)=kappa_Z(C^1/2 v0,...,C^1/2 vd).
The vectors C^1/2 e_(i,j) over all X-coordinates are orthonormal, since their
Gram matrix is the X-block of C. The first vector C^1/2 e_S has norm one.
Restricting the remaining tensor indices to this orthonormal system can only
decrease its Hilbert-Schmidt norm. Consequently the squared sum of all
kappa_Y(e_S,e_X,...,e_X) entries is <= b. Independence across copies gives
exactly (d!)^2 ||T_d f||_HS^2 / sigma_beta^2 for that squared sum. Hence
||T_d f||_HS^2 <= b sigma_beta^2/(d!)^2. Let beta -> infinity, choosing N
each time, and extend by L2-density to arbitrary centered f:
        ||T_d f||_HS^2 <= b Var_mu(f)/(d!)^2.
The reverse comparison for universal constants follows by taking f linear:
T_d <v,x> = kappa_(d+1)(v,...)/(d!). Thus the best universal constants
for these two interfaces should be exactly equal (after the factorial).

This is a sharpened imported mechanism, not a claim to solve KLS or a major
open problem. It is not yet independently checked. It does NOT make numerical
