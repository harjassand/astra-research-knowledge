PRIMARY SOURCES AND ROLE IN THIS RESULT

1. Carlyle, J. W. and Paz, A. (1971). “Realizations by Stochastic Finite Automata.” Journal of Computer and System Sciences 5(1), 26–40. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-0000(71)80005-3
   Supports the finite-rank Hankel / finite linear realization relationship and the predictive-rank interpretation.

2. Hsu, D., Kakade, S. M., and Zhang, T. (2012). “A Spectral Algorithm for Learning Hidden Markov Models.” Journal of Computer and System Sciences 78(5), 1460–1480. arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.4413
   Primary raw-observation spectral-learning context under singular separation. The result here is only a finite Hankel/CUR statement and does not claim HMM parameter recovery.

3. Tropp, J. A. (2012). “User-Friendly Tail Bounds for Sums of Random Matrices.” Foundations of Computational Mathematics 12, 389–434. arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.4389
   Supplies the matrix Chernoff lower-tail inequality applied to uniformly sampled rows/columns of orthonormal singular bases.

4. Wedin, P.-A. (1973). “Perturbation Theory for Pseudo-Inverses.” BIT 13, 217–232. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01933494
   Supports same-rank pseudoinverse and singular-subspace perturbation bounds.

5. Drineas, P., Mahoney, M. W., and Muthukrishnan, S. (2008). “Relative-Error CUR Matrix Decompositions.” SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications 30(2), 844–881. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/07070471X ; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3696
   Places the factorized column/core/row approximation in standard CUR literature; cited algorithms assume a rank parameter.

These are primary research sources. The report's finite-sample constants are derived directly in RESULT.txt from Hoeffding, matrix Chernoff, Weyl, the CUR identity, and Wedin perturbation, rather than imported as a black-box theorem.
