THEOREM AND INTERFACE

Let U_2 be the irreducible spin-2 representation on C^5, tau=Tr/5, and
Phi:M_5 -> M_5 a UCP, tau-preserving, SU(2)-covariant map. Suppose an actual
UCP J:M_5 tensor M_5 -> M_5 has J(X tensor I)=J(I tensor X)=Phi(X).
Then there is a canonical, positive selfadjoint, bistochastic EB map Psi,
explicitly selected from mixtures of THREE specified pure-state orbits, with

  I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi)                                      (T)

on the entire L2(tau) operator space. The constant 2 is sharp. Positivity
as a superoperator is not required for this comparison: covariance and
Hermiticity already force real sector eigenvalues. For the N93 positive
subclass, tensor closure and the dimension-free power lift below apply.

For any family of bounded Hermitian likelihoods H, tau(H)=0, ||H||<=theta,
physical rho_H=(I+H)/5 and r=sup_H||Phi H-H||_(1,tau), this SAME channel has

  sup_H T(Psi(rho_H),rho_H) <= sqrt(2theta r)/2.           (E)

The family size, Holevo capacity and unknown state label do not enter the
construction. Spin-2 covariance and an actual compatible channel are
substantive supplied restrictions. This is not an unrestricted N93 theorem.

1. FULL MULTIPOLE DECOMPOSITION AND THE THREE PHYSICAL SEEDS

The conjugation representation U_2 X U_2* decomposes without multiplicity
into V_l, l=0,...,4, of dimensions n_l=2l+1. V_0 consists of constants.
Schur's lemma makes Phi scalar lambda_l on each V_l. Hermiticity makes
lambda_l real, and UCP Schwarz plus trace preservation gives |lambda_l|<=1.
Thus Phi is automatically L2-selfadjoint. We do not conflate CP positivity
with nonnegative L2 eigenvalues.

Fix the standard spin basis |m>, m=2,1,0,-1,-2. For a pure psi, its orbit
P_g=U_2(g)|psi><psi|U_2(g)* has mean I/5, and

  Psi_psi(X)=5 int_SU(2) Tr(P_g X) P_g dg                 (1)

is an actual UCP, trace-preserving, positive selfadjoint EB channel. For
Hermitian tau-orthonormal multipoles B_l,a, let

  S_l(psi)=sum_a <psi|B_l,a|psi>^2.

Schur orthogonality gives the sector eigenvalue S_l/n_l of (1). Use seeds

  F=|2>,      N=|0>,      C=(|2>+sqrt(2)|-1>)/sqrt(3).

Their EXACT captures and eigenvalues are

  seed     (S_1,S_2,S_3,S_4)
  F        (2,10/7,1/2,1/14)
  N        (0,10/7,0,18/7)
  C        (0,0,5/2,3/2)

  seed     (mu_1,mu_2,mu_3,mu_4)
  F        (2/3,2/7,1/14,1/126)

These numbers are calculated by the displayed finite exact multipoles in

2. COMPATIBILITY, STAR OPERATORS AND THE TEN SUFFICIENT TESTS

Let omega_RAB be the normalized Choi state of the actual two-output channel.
For tau-orthonormal Hermitian B_l,a define

  W_l=sum_a B_l,a^T tensor (B_l,a tensor I+I tensor B_l,a).

The exact Choi identity is

  Tr(omega W_l)=2n_l lambda_l.                            (3)

The joint state need not respect SU(2) or have independent outputs. For
nonnegative weights w=(w_1,...,w_4), put W_w=sum_l w_l W_l,
V(w)=sum_l n_l w_l and C_seed(w)=max_(p in {F,N,C}) sum_l w_l S_l(p).
We prove the operator bound

  W_w <= [V(w)+C_seed(w)] I                               (4)

for ALL nonnegative real weights by the following ten exact ray tests:

  w                 V       C_seed      V+C_seed
  (0,0,0,1)          9       18/7        81/7
  (0,0,1,0)          7       5/2         19/2
  (0,0,3,7)         84       18          102
  (0,1,0,0)          5       10/7        45/7
  (0,7,5,0)         70       25/2        165/2
  (0,8,5,1)         84       14          98
  (1,0,0,0)          3       2           5
  (1,0,1,0)         10       5/2         25/2
  (5,0,0,4)         51       72/7        429/7
  (8,0,3,7)        108       18          126.             (5)

Here is why those finitely many tests cover the entire positive orthant.
For each seed p, take the pointed polyhedral cone

  K_p={w>=0: (S(p)-S(q)).w>=0 for the other two seeds q}.

There are six homogeneous linear inequalities in four variables. A ray of
a full-dimensional pointed cone is an intersection of three independent
active boundary hyperplanes. Enumerating the 20 choices for each K_p,
retaining its nonnegative feasible one-dimensional nullspaces, gives exactly
the ten rational rays in (5). The enumeration uses rational linear algebra
and is asserted against the displayed list in the certificate. Each K_p
is full dimensional (p wins with positive weights near, respectively, the
first, fourth and third coordinate rays). Every pointed polyhedral cone is
generated by its extreme rays. On K_p, C_seed=S(p).w is linear. Summing the
ray operator inequalities therefore proves (4) on K_p, and the three cones
cover the orthant. Merely checking the four separate multipoles would not
prove (4); the six mixed tests are load-bearing.

3. COMPLETE EXACT FINITE PROOF OF THE RAY OPERATOR BOUNDS

All matrices in (5) are fixed 125-by-125 matrices; no limiting or numerical
semidefinite claim enters. A reproducible exact construction is as follows.
For each l and q=-l,...,l, use the normalized spherical tensor

  (T_lq)_(m1,m2)=sqrt(5)(-1)^(2-m2)
                 CG(2,m1;2,-m2 | l,q).

The tensors obey Tr(T_lq* T_lq')/5=delta_qq' and
T_lq*=(-1)^q T_l,-q. A Hermitian orthonormal basis is T_l0 and, for q>0,
(T_lq+T_lq*)/sqrt(2), (T_lq-T_lq*)/(i sqrt(2)). Consequently the star may
be constructed equivalently as

  W_l=sum_q T_lq^T tensor (T_lq* tensor I+I tensor T_lq*). (6)

All coefficients here are exact algebraic numbers. The standard real CG
convention is the one implemented in SymPy.physics.wigner.clebsch_gordan.
Hermiticity and equality with the Hermitian-basis expression follow from
the tensor-adjoint relation; the code also checks exact Hermiticity.

Conjugate the input R with the real spin-flip unitary
U|m>=(-1)^(2-m)|-m>. It sends J_a^T to -J_a and converts the Choi input
representation to the ordinary spin-2 representation. The resulting W_l'
commute with simultaneous rotations of R,A,B. Couple A,B to output spin
L=0,...,4 and then R with L to total spin f=0,...,6. At magnetic number M=f,
the exact orthonormal coefficient columns are

  (C_f)_(r,a,b;L)=CG(2,a;2,b | L,a+b)
                  CG(2,r;L,a+b | f,f),                  (7)

with |L-2|<=f<=L+2. Their multiplicity sizes are
1,3,5,4,3,2,1 for f=0,...,6. Their magnetic multiplicities 2f+1 give
sum_f (2f+1)dim C_f=125. Rotational invariance identifies all magnetic
copies, so it suffices to bound the matrices K_lf=C_f* W_l' C_f, of size
at most five. The certificate checks both C_f* C_f=I and
W_l' C_f=C_f K_lf EXACTLY, in addition to Hermiticity.

For every ray w and every f it constructs

  Q_wf=[V(w)+C_seed(w)]I-sum_l w_l K_lf

and proves Q_wf>=0 by an exact Hermitian LDL decomposition
Q=L diag(d_k)L*. Every pivot d_k is a nonnegative exact rational/algebraic
number. A zero pivot is explicitly required to have a zero residual row;
thus singular sharp inequalities are checked rather than divided by zero.
The equality Q-L diag(d_k)L*=0 is also checked exactly. All 70 block
certificates passed. SPIN2_CERTIFICATE.json contains every pivot with its
ray, total spin and output spins. The accompanying script is the full
finite coefficient specification, construction and equality proof; it
requires only Python and SymPy 1.14.0 and uses no floating eigenvalues,
optimization solver or PPT relaxation. These finite exact identities are
the computational portion of this fixed-dimension proof. They do not prove
an arbitrary-spin extrapolation or establish external validation.

4. FROM EVERY POSITIVE TEST TO ONE COMMON EB CHANNEL

Equations (3),(4) imply

  sum_l n_l (2lambda_l-1) w_l <= C_seed(w) for all w>=0.

Put u_l=max(0,2lambda_l-1). For a given nonnegative w, restrict it to
w_l'=w_l on positive deficits and zero elsewhere. Since all seed captures
are nonnegative, C_seed(w')<=C_seed(w), and hence

  sum_l n_l u_l w_l <= C_seed(w) for all w>=0.            (8)

Let M be the triangle of the three eigenvalue rows in (2), and let D be
its downward closure in the nonnegative orthant:
D={v>=0: there is mu in M with v_l<=mu_l for every l}. It is compact and
convex. Its support function at n_l w_l, w>=0, is C_seed(w). If u>=0 were
outside D, compact convex separation would give a separating functional.
Its negative coordinates may be replaced by zero because D is downward
closed and u>=0, preserving or improving separation. This contradicts (8).
Therefore ONE mu in M dominates u coordinatewise. The same mixture of
the three actual channels (1) is EB, canonical, positive selfadjoint and
bistochastic, and mu_l>=2lambda_l-1. Identity is fixed, so this proves (T).

This is a common-channel argument over the entire algebra. It does not
choose a different POVM for each state or each multipole.
