REVISION R2 — A ONE-COPY GAP IS A SUFFICIENT TWO-REPLICA ENERGY COMPILER
7 October 2026. Independent interface reduction.

Let Omega be the finite set of augmented microscopic pair-basis configurations and pi their determinant-squared target law. Suppose there exists a reversible one-copy kernel Q on Omega with stationary pi and Poincare constant gamma>0:
  Var_pi f <= gamma^(-1) E_{pi,Q}(f) for every real f.
Define a random-scan product kernel on Omega^2 by updating A with Q with probability 1/2 and updating B with Q with probability 1/2:
  P((A,B),(A',B')) = 1/2 Q(A,A') 1_{B'=B} + 1/2 1_{A'=A} Q(B,B').
This is reversible for pi tensor pi, and for F(A,B)=f1(A)+f2(B), its Dirichlet form is exactly
  E_{pi tensor pi,P}(F)=1/2 E_{pi,Q}(f1)+1/2 E_{pi,Q}(f2).
Therefore
  Var_pi f1 <= (2/gamma) E_{pi tensor pi,P}(F)
for every f1,f2. So the assigned all-functions pair-energy inequality is implied by a polynomial one-copy spectral gap; no correlated mismatch-register coupling is required if such a Q can be found.

This is a sufficiency reduction only. It does not construct Q or prove a gap for linear matroid-parity bases. The directed even-cycle hard fiber has two isolated supports under sub-q pair-label moves, so the naive hard-fiber local chain fails. Positive coordinate auxiliaries may connect the enlarged state space, but the needed conductance and determinant-ratio bounds remain unknown. The reduction narrows the search: either construct a finite-bit one-copy gap kernel on the augmented pair system, or use the extra freedom of a genuinely two-replica kernel to evade that requirement.

Costs and interface: Q's move probabilities and Metropolis ratios must be computed from polynomial-bit determinant data without f(U) coefficient queries. A uniform gamma lower bound must hold over the full annealing path. Once Q is available, P adds only one fair random bit and one Q transition; the pair-energy constant is exactly doubled.
