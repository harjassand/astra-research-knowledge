REFERENCE-WEIGHTED SELF-SCORE UNDER ARBITRARY SECTOR TRANSPORT
Root derivation, 2026-10-08. INTERNALLY-PROVED CANDIDATE; independent Sol and
combinatorics algebra audits passed. A finite-dimensional sufficient-condition
lemma, not an iid converse. External validation and priority remain unknown.

Let {rho_x} be an ensemble with mean sigma>0, Tr sigma=1. Assume sigma commutes
with every rho_x and L_x=sigma^(-1/2)rho_x sigma^(-1/2) obeys 0<=L_x<=M I,
uniformly, with a supplied finite M>=1. Thus int L_x=I and rho_x=sigma L_x.
For a TP channel Phi with Kraus A_i define B_i=A_i sigma^(1/2), C=int L_x².
The B_i have the same ranks as A_i and sum_i||B_i||HS²=1.

EXACT IDENTITY
Let e=int (1/2)||Phi(rho_x)-rho_x||1 and
 D=sum_i int ||[L_x,B_i]||HS².
Expansion of the commutators gives
 D/2=int Tr[L_x(rho_x-Phi(rho_x))] + (1/2)Tr[C(Phi(sigma)-sigma)].
Indeed the first positive squared term is Tr C Phi(sigma), the second is
Tr C sigma (using sum A_i*A_i=I), the cross term is twice
int Tr L_x Phi(rho_x), and target Tr L_x rho_x=Tr sigma L_x².
This is where commutation with sigma matters. No unitality, covariance, or
preservation of the central reference is imposed on the legal code.

LOWER BOUND
The witness L_x/M is an effect, so the first discrepancy is <=M e.
Operator Jensen gives C>=I; L_x²<=M L_x gives C<=M I. Since Phi(sigma)-sigma
has trace zero, the effect (C-I)/(M-1) gives
 |Tr C(Phi(sigma)-sigma)| <=(M-1) T(Phi(sigma),sigma) <=(M-1)e.
For M=1, L_x=I a.e. and everything is zero. Therefore for M>1,
 e >= D/(3M-1).
If Phi(sigma)=sigma exactly, or C is scalar, the stronger e>=D/(2M) holds.

Let Lambda_L(Q) be the minimum of int||[L_x,B]||HS² over all complex B of
rank<=Q and ||B||HS=1. Any code whose maximum quantum branch is Q has
rank-Q composite Kraus operators. The weighted identity and sum||B_i||²=1
therefore imply e>=Lambda_L(Q)/(3M-1), including arbitrary sector changes.
Maximum risk dominates this ensemble-average risk. Classical labels are free
in this converse, not implicitly omitted from an upper construction.

REDUCIBLE COVARIANT ENSEMBLES
For a block-diagonal ensemble with each block an irreducible group orbit,
the mean sigma is block-scalar and commutes with every state. The lemma applies
without requiring sigma to be scalar on the whole Hilbert space. The rank
minimum is over the entire matrix space, including rectangular inter-sector
blocks. This keeps possible decoder transport inside the variational problem.
It does not justify minimizing each source sector separately.

LIMITATIONS AND NEXT GATE
A uniform likelihood envelope is an actual premise/cost. For a fixed-spectrum
full U(d) iid orbit, the Schur-Haar mean gives L_lambda=d_lambda rho_lambda
(identity on multiplicity spaces), so M<=max d_lambda is polynomial for fixed d.
The earlier claim of exponential growth from rare sectors was incorrect for
this interface: their probabilities and multiplicities cancel on whitening.
At critical qubit radius the full envelope is O(sqrt n); for the fixed-gap
qutrit collision it is O(n^(5/2)), with typical bound O(n² sqrt(log n)).
Varying-spectrum priors can have additional sector likelihood ratios. One cannot
delete sectors at constant mass and claim an n^-alpha converse, nor replace
sigma by an uncharged reference whose reconstruction is not guaranteed.
The lemma is useful only when M is bounded at the needed scale or a separate
valid weighted/trimmed witness removes that issue. It is not yet a general
critical-qutrit arbitrary-sector lower. Positivity/SVD replacement and efficient
minimizer acquisition for Lambda_L are not asserted.
