## Independent result retained from INITIAL

For a finite weakly reversible reaction graph, if `x >= y` and `y=c_0 -> ...
-> c_l=z` is a complex path, the state path is exactly `x-y+c_k`. The fixed
spectator `x-y` makes every step enabled, and the reverse graph path returns
the endpoint. If `y` maximizes the available source falling factorial, the
endpoint satisfies `F(x-y+z) <= F(x)` for
`F(x)=log(x!)-log(max_{c<=x} x_under_c)`. This is a component-preserving
communication lemma, not negative drift: only the selected endpoint is
controlled, and intermediate `F` can rise.

For bimolecular paths, each state `x-y+c_k` has total count at most
`N+M_*`, where `N=|x|_1` and `M_*` is the largest complex molecularity; the
bound does not grow as `N+l M_*` because the path telescopes. Thus, for fixed
positive rates, a specified length-`l` path has next-jump probability at
least `[k_min/(|R| k_max (1+N+M_*)^2)]^l`. This may decay polynomially in `N`
for fixed `l`, so it cannot by itself yield uniform repeated recovery or
Foster drift. In predictable-rate variants, the same bounded-window hazard
argument gives a success-probability bound, but a holding-time estimate
conditioned on following the selected word must not be inferred from an
unconditional holding-time bound.

The deterministic support-face criterion is also exact: a coordinate face
is invariant iff every reaction sourced within it has product support inside
it. A face-crossing edge is enabled at a lattice state only once all its
present species meet source thresholds. For `A+B <-> 2A`, the deterministic
face `B=0` is non-invariant, yet `(1,0)` is absorbing; `(2,0)` has the enabled
recovery jump to `(1,1)`. This is the precise limit on inferring stochastic
boundary recovery from deterministic geometry alone.

`boundary_recovery.py` is my original exact diagnostic. It checked the
source-swap updates, support criterion, rational path lower bound, and small
fixtures; its finite output is `certificate_checks.json`. The all-state claims
above rest on the derivations, not those finite checks.

The fixed-rate recurrence class has a direct prior-art representation. In
Cappelletti, Pal Majumder & Wiuf, *Long-time asymptotics of stochastic reaction
systems* (arXiv:1912.00401), Assumption 1 permits environment-dependent

finite Poisson means. Conditional child production and removal rates are
`h_j(r)=sum_i alpha_ij r_i` and `a_j(r)=sum_i beta_ij r_i`; these are exactly
the paper's `0 -> Y_j` and `Y_j -> 0` linear rates in the environment. Every
child is properly produced/degraded, and `E_pi h_j(X)<infinity`. Theorem 6.1
therefore covers positive recurrence of this fixed-rate class. It does not
provide c10_s01's explicit acquired rational Foster coefficients, core, or
return constants; their exact-formulation priority remains unknown.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.00401

For the later HINGE_RECOVERY class, I reconstructed the threshold-cell
cancellation. A catalytic birth of `i` by large catalyst `j` is offset by
the reciprocal removal `X_i+X_j -> X_i` only when `x_i>=h_i`; if `j` is not
large its birth contribution is bounded by `beta_ij^+ h_j`. For self birth,
`x_i>h_i` gives `x_i-1>=h_i`, so `2X_i -> X_i` cancels the unbounded part.
Unary death contributes `-delta z_i`; conversions are nonpositive when their
source is above threshold and bounded by `k^+ h_source` otherwise. Together
these yield `LV<=C-delta Z` exactly on the integer lattice. The compact-core
drain/immigration word and per-step bounded-window hazard lower bound are
consistent with that drift. I found no concrete proof gap in this narrow
class. My copy of `hinge_compiler.py` plus its dependency exited 0 on six exact
fixtures: 514 complete-generator states, 514 exponential-ratio states, 60
boundary words and 11 reciprocal inequalities in the saved fixture set.

For fixed rates this class is also covered by established broader prior art.
Every admitted reaction is binary and touches a unary complex; all species
have `0 <-> X_i`, so all complexes lie in the one linkage class through zero.
The whole network is weakly reversible. Anderson & Kim, *Some network

Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11263 and

**c10_l02 cross-exposure note.** I read its frozen rank-one transfer after
release. Its recurrence conclusion is within Wiuf--Xu's one-dimensional
weakly-reversible class results, not a multidimensional advance. The displayed
coarse estimate `F(t)=G t log t+O(t)` alone does not justify the claimed
finite-difference asymptotic. The repair is valid: on the eventual tail,
`M(t)` is the maximum of finitely many positive-leading-coefficient
polynomials, hence equals one eventual maximizing polynomial (ties may be
identical); therefore `log M(t+alpha)-log M(t)=O(1/t)`. The exact finite
factorial ratio separately gives `alpha G log t+O(1)`. This fixes that local
derivation gap; it does not change the prior-art boundary.

## Primary-source boundary and decisive gap

Xu, *On the Regularity of Reaction Systems*, arXiv:2409.05340v2, Theorem 4.6
establishes second-order endotactic regularity/non-explosion. Its recurrence
results require additional hypotheses or an invariant probability; it does
not turn non-explosion or deterministic permanence into general recurrence.
The broader multidimensional weakly reversible/endotactic boundary-recovery
gate remains open in this work. The finite path lemma is a local bridge;
the exact missing step is a uniform physical-time drift or summable recovery
mechanism from repeated thin-boundary states on each actual closed component.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2
