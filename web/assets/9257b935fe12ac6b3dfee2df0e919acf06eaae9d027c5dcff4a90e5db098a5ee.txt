LOAD-BEARING OBLIGATIONS

1. Hypotheses are contracts: homogeneous multiaffine LC coefficients,
   polynomial exact rational oracles/output length, correct positive seeds,
   and range upper bounds. validate() checks seeds/degrees/basic ranges; it
   does not certify LC or arbitrary oracle cost. self_complementary=True is
   also a proved input contract; no diagnostic inference licenses it.
2. Choosing among n present copies and accepting deletion with probability
   t^(1-indicator other)/2 gives exactly t/(2n) times source Eq.(19).
   Reinsertion has z_v*coefficient and no extra t_v. Self-reinsertion ensures
   a positive denominator. Lazy diagonals are >=1/2. Imported Lemma5.5 gives
   gap >=t/(2n); the code does not prove this all-size source theorem.
3. restricted() retains original degree using forced membership/exclusion.
   Multiplication of coordinate derivatives by the forced coordinate product
   and zero specialization preserve LC. The source all-field gap therefore
   applies to the conditional product law on the original ground set.
4. Each field update is rational (1+1/(32n))^integer, with aggregate absolute
   exponent <=K. The field spread and h correctly dominate negative log2
   stationary masses, including every conditional witness on bad histories.
5. Uniform mixing from any supported witness permits adaptive coupling to
   independent exact conditional draws. Summed TV is <=1/256. Consecutive
   production chain calls are not claimed to be independent without this
   coupling argument. A final fresh original seed remains supported.
6. The coordinate-descent cap suffices because good nonstopping steps lower
   Phi by eta^2/128 and Phi(0)-inf Phi<=B when hard mass is positive.
   On zero hard mass the cap can terminate unsuccessfully; either branch
   returns zero. It need not solve feasibility deterministically.
7. Hoeffding constants use binary-log ceilings as upper bounds on natural
   logs: field, 2n normalizer frequencies and complementary frequency each
   consume <=1/64 failure. The local tilt uses the source UNEQUAL penalty
   and arbitrary field theorem. Common-penalty-only mixing would not suffice.
8. Empirical majority phat>=1/2 holds on every tape, and retaining an actual
   witness makes the next restriction supported. The exact terminal weight
   telescopes to the soft normalizer; no rare prescribed branch is imposed.
9. Coordinate flips (including originally forced/absent deterministic bits)
   still give a supported state and phat=1 for deterministic frequencies.
   The 2n-stage cap is safe. Zero complement mass yields zero on every tape.
   Zero returned on a positive bad tape is NOT a support certificate.
10. At most 2n+1 binary choices per step is safe (n-1 present-copy choices,
    one acceptance, <=n-1 reinsert choices). Sequential categorical handles
    trailing zero weights because a preceding positive p=1 choice decides.
11. Prefix interval decisions implement exact rational coins until ambiguity
    at the cap. That event aborts the WHOLE count. Conditional abort chance
    <=2^-R and union bound over the predetermined global choice cap <=1/64.
    The diagnostic and production proof assume fair independent source bits;
    random.Random used in finite tests is merely a reproducibility device.
12. All loop caps are polynomial integers, so Fraction fields/products have
    polynomial bit length under the oracle contracts. Every fixed count
    repetition and fixed odd median amplification bounds every execution.
13. Exact enumeration is restricted to the fixed cutoff n<=12; the thermal
    local-factor fallback has a separately fixed branch cap. Neither gives a
    large-instance FPRAS execution. User-supplied larger exact_cutoff can
    intentionally change that guarantee and is outside the fixed-default
    polynomial-time contract.
