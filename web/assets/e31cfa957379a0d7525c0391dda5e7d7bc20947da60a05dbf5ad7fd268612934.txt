Exact-zero behavior and prefix identity
---------------------------------------
For F in Q(i)^(n by n), let

  c_k(F) = sum_{|I|=|J|=k, I cap J=empty} |det F[I,J]|^2.

For a partial physical prefix (U forced up, D forced down, H forced empty), let
c_P be the same sum restricted to U subset I, D subset J, and (I union J)
disjoint from H.  Put a=|U|, b=|D|, and N=[n]\(U union D union H).  For each
sign word s on N, set tau_i(z)=z on U, s_i on N, and 0 on D union H, and form
A(z,s)=diag(tau)F-F^T diag(tau).  Define

  h_R(s) = [z^a] Pf(A(z,s)[R]),
  Y_P(s) = sum_{|R|=2k, D subset R, R cap H=empty} |h_R(s)|^2.

Expanding the Pfaffian by pairings and collecting the z^a coefficient gives
terms indexed by S subset R with |S|=k, U subset S, and S cap (D union H)
empty.  Each term is a coefficient d_(R,S) times the sign character
prod_(i in S\U) s_i.  Distinct S give distinct characters; independent
uniform signs make distinct characters orthogonal.  The pair (R,S) identifies
the disjoint minor (I,J)=(S,R\S), and D subset R plus R cap H empty imposes
exactly the remaining prefix constraints.  Consequently E_s Y_P(s)=c_P.

For each fixed R there are at most
  C_(a,b)=binom(2k-a-b,k-a)
eligible S (zero if infeasible).  Cauchy-Schwarz on that many unit-modulus
characters, followed by summing R, gives the pointwise and second-moment
bounds
  0 <= Y_P(s) <= C_(a,b)c_P,
  E Y_P^2 <= C_(a,b)c_P^2.
Thus if c_P=0, every sign word has Y_P(s)=0: this is an exact zero guarantee,
not merely a high-probability statement.  On a positive-mass prefix a finite
sample estimate can still be zero on its failure event; zero output is not a
deterministic certificate of a zero sector.

Exact coefficient computation and bit cost
-------------------------------------------
For skew A, the identity
  X_k(A)=[t^k] sqrt(det(I+t A* A))
        = sum_{|R|=2k} |Pf(A[R])|^2
allows the needed principal-Pfaffian norm to be evaluated without listing
minors.  With B=A* A, compute Tr(B^j), j=1..k, recover the determinant
coefficients by Newton identities, and recover the formal square-root
coefficients by 2q_m=d_m-sum_(j=1..m-1)q_j q_(m-j).  This uses O(k n^3+k^2)
exact ring operations for one matrix and coefficient.  To impose D subset R,
use inclusion-exclusion over T subset D on the restricted matrices with H union
T deleted.  To select the top z-degree 2a of X_k(A(z)), evaluate at z=0,...,2a
and take the exact (2a)-th finite difference divided by (2a)!.  Thus one Y
evaluation uses 2^b(2a+1) coefficient evaluations.  Since
  2^b C_(a,b) <= binom(2k,k)=C_k,
the sign-sampling cap times the inclusion-exclusion factor is bounded by C_k
times a polynomial in k.

For bit complexity, clear all input denominators by a common integer D whose
bit length is at most the total input encoding length L, and write F=G/D with
integer Gaussian G.  Every target sector is a sum of products of 2k entries,
with denominator D^(2k).  Numerator heights are bounded by the product height
O(kL) plus the logarithm of the number of determinant/pairing terms, which is
O(k log n+k log k).  The trace/Newton/square-root computation has polynomial
degree at most 2k in G and polynomially many additions and multiplications;
the intermediate heights therefore remain polynomial in n,k,L.  Finite
differences add O(a log a) bits, and inclusion-exclusion adds at most b bits
to summation heights.  All arithmetic is rational/integer; cancellation is
exact, with no floating-point zero test or relative-precision assumption.

For the capped estimate, if mu=c_P>0 then 0<=Y<=C_(a,b)mu.  The bounded
multiplicative Chernoff bound gives relative error at most eta with failure
at most delta using O(C_(a,b) eta^-2 log(1/delta)) independent signs; for
mu=0 every sample is exactly zero.  Coupled with the 2^b inclusion-exclusion
factor and the inequality above, this is
C_k poly(n,k,L,eta^-1,log(delta^-1)) bit work per estimate.  A full prefix
sampler makes at most 3n child estimates.  With eta=epsilon/(8n) and
delta=epsilon/(12n), its total is
C_k poly(n,k,L,epsilon^-1,log(n/epsilon)).
This is fixed-parameter tractable in k and polynomial in n,L for fixed k; it
does not claim polynomial time uniformly for unrestricted k, since C_k is
exponential in k.  For completeness, fix a and compare
f(b)=2^b binom(2k-a-b,k-a): the ratio f(b+1)/f(b) is
2(k-b)/(2k-a-b), so its maximum is attained at b=a or a+1.  There the value
is 2^a binom(2k-2a,k-a).  As a increases by one, this last expression has
successive ratio (k-a)/(2(k-a)-1)<=1, and at a=0 it equals binom(2k,k).
Hence 2^b C_(a,b)<=C_k.

Capped physical-prefix TV guarantee
-----------------------------------
At each site estimate the up/down/empty child masses.  On the event that all
three estimates are eta-relative accurate, a true zero child is still exactly
zero, and normalization changes the three-way distribution by at most
eta/(1-eta) <= 2 eta (eta<=1/2).  Round the two cumulative boundaries down to
a grid of q=2^B points, with B=ceil(log2(8n/epsilon)); the induced one-site TV
distance is at most 2/q.  There are at most 3n estimator calls, so their bad
event probability is at most 3n delta.  Sequential maximal coupling over at
most n sites yields total variation at most

  n(2 eta + 2/q) + 3n delta
    <= epsilon/4 + epsilon/4 + epsilon/4
    = 3 epsilon/4 < epsilon.

The code copy’s all-zero-estimate branch returned `{'status':'FAIL',...}`.
That is a contract mismatch if the requested output space is physical
k-pair occupations.  It may be treated as a failure sink of probability
covered by the estimator bad-event bound, but it does not produce a pattern.
A legal repair is to complete the current feasible prefix deterministically:
with suffix [site,n), take k-|U| remaining up sites, then k-|D| remaining down
sites, and mark the rest empty.  Feasibility holds on the good path.  More
generally every selected child with a positive estimated weight has positive
true mass, because true-zero children always estimate to zero; hence a
positive initial sector stays combinatorially completable even before the
first bad estimate.  If all estimated weights vanish at a feasible positive
prefix, at least one positive child estimate failed, so fallback probability
is charged to the same bad-call event.  The local repaired copy and fixture
are retained for provenance, but the independent checker below validates the
completion rule directly.

Fraction-complex determinant and Pfaffian expansions, it checked the exact
prefix mean identity and C_(a,b) pointwise cap for 1,024 cases: every one of

also propagated the exact 64-point dyadic self-reduction law, computed exact
TV 6077931/576266240, and verified the proved ceiling 1/8 and support
containment.  The deterministic legal-completion rule returned I={0}, J={1},

Separate phase-matching extension
---------------------------------
The inspected c03_s01 sidecar uses alternating phases 1 and i on a fixed
matching, with baseline pair weights beta_j=a_j^2+b_j^2 and residual squared
Frobenius norm delta^2.  Its acceptance condition is beta_min>0 and
delta^2<=beta_min/(256 k^2).  For each sign word, the baseline skew blocks
have singular values sqrt(beta_j), each repeated twice.  The perturbation
skew has Frobenius norm at most 2 delta; Weyl therefore moves each paired
singular value by at most sqrt(beta_min)/(8k).  Since
X_k=e_k(sigma_1^2,...,sigma_(n/2)^2), termwise comparison gives
  (1-1/(8k))^(2k) <= X_k/X_k(G) <= (1+1/(8k))^(2k),
which lies inside [3/4,4/3].  Sign-character orthogonality still gives the
unbiased mean under unit phases.  This explains the certificate’s intended
pointwise control; it remains a separate count estimator and supplies no
physical-prefix sampler or TV theorem.  The provided JSON is finite fixture
evidence only, not an independent code execution or external validation.

Remaining gaps and claim boundary
---------------------------------
The general identities and capped TV argument are mathematical statements
conditional on implementing the exact coefficient and finite-difference
arithmetic as specified.  The independent checker is finite corroboration,
not a proof of the implementation for arbitrary n,k.  This lane did not run a
generic large-instance benchmark, a generic confidence-coverage study, or an
independent source-level verification of every line in the peer sampler.  The
original FAIL output contract must be changed before claiming a sampler that
always returns a legal physical occupation; the prefix-consistent repair
described here closes that interface gap.  Complexity is C_k-FPT in k, not
uniformly polynomial for variable k.  The phase-matching sidecar is not
substitutable for the physical-prefix route.
