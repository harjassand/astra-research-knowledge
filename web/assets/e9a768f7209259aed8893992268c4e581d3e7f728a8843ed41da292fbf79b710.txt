Core proved conditional result
If a finitely generated group G has a nonidentity element w killed by every homomorphism to every sofic group, then every exact finite-dimensional linear representation G->GL_d(K), for every field K, kills w. Mal'cev residual finiteness of the f.g. linear image gives the proof. For a finite presentation and expanded word length L, rank or normalized-HS distance from an approximate tuple A to every exact same-dimension representation is at least d(w(A),I)/L. Hence any linear-sofic or hyperlinear model for such a group makes that presentation unstable in the corresponding metric. This does not rule out either approximation class.
