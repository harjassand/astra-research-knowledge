1. EXACT CLAIM AND QUANTIFIERS

Take the six-reaction BIMolecular reversible network from INITIAL:
    A+C -> A+B rate k,
    A+B -> A+C rate 1,
    A+B -> B rate 1, B -> A+B rate 1,
    C -> 0 rate 1, 0 -> C rate 1.
Allow ANY predictable/measurable k(t,past) in [1,2]. The deterministic ODE has
ONE explicit rational compact invariant absorber for all such rate choices and
all positive initial states. Both STATIC endpoint stochastic models k=1 and k=2
are nonexplosive and positive recurrent on the SAME infinite component
Gamma={a+b>=1}, and each has a light-tailed product-form invariant law.

Nevertheless, NO single coercive real quadratic polynomial W on Gamma can
obey a strict Foster condition off a finite set for BOTH static endpoint
generators. This remains impossible even if the endpoints may have different
finite exception sets and different positive drift margins. Therefore such W
cannot certify every predictable [1,2] control. Arbitrarily many bits, a
non-diagonal quadratic matrix, negative cross terms, or a larger finite box do
not fix this obstruction. Fixed-rate quadratic certificates EXIST by the
previous revision; common quadratic existence is a strictly different claim.

No recurrence counterexample, generic no-Lyapunov theorem, no-go for higher
degree/state-dependent/phase-aware functions, or general field-breaking result
is claimed.

2. DIRECT ROBUST DETERMINISTIC ABSORBER

The ODE is
    a'=b(1-a), b'=a(k c-b), c'=a(b-k c)+1-c.              (1)
For initial positive a, l=min(a(0),1)>0 and U=max(a(0),1) still bound a exactly.
Choosing 2U/(2U+1)<q<1 gives, for H=b+q c,
    H'=-(1-q)ab+[(1-q)k a-q]c+q
       <=-(1-q)l b-[q-2(1-q)U]c+q.
Both coefficients are strictly positive. This proves global boundedness.
The comparison c'>=1-(2U+1)c gives an eventual positive lower bound for c.
Because b'=a(kc-b)>=a(c-b), that same eventual c lower bound gives an
eventual b lower bound by the integrating factor with a>=l. Thus integral b
diverges and a->1. Eventually a is in [1/2,2], uniformly over controls for
each supplied initial state (the entry time may depend on the initial state).

Inside that A strip choose q=5/6 and H=b+(5/6)c. Uniformly over k in [1,2],
    H'<=-b/12-c/6+5/6 <=-H/12+5/6.
Thus limsup H<=10. Also c'>=1-5c gives liminf c>=1/5. For every
ell<1/5, eventually c>=ell; then b'>=a(ell-b) and the integrating-factor
comparison gives liminf b>=ell. Letting ell increase proves liminf b>=1/5.

Consequently every positive trajectory eventually enters the SAME rational
polytope
    P={ 1/2<=a<=2, b>=1/16, c>=1/16, b+5c/6<=12 }.     (2)
It is compact and strictly positive. It is invariant for every allowed rate:
A facets have the signs of 1-a; on b=1/16, kc-b>=0; on c=1/16,
c'>=1-3/16>0 because a<=2,b>=1/16,k<=2; on H=12,
H'<=-1+5/6=-1/6. This proof accommodates bounded measurable k and is
independent of general N33/N50 candidate correctness or recurrence claims.

3. COMPONENTWISE STOCHASTIC STATUS

All endpoint rates are positive; the INITIAL reversible path argument yields
the same closed irreducible Gamma. The total-population drift remains
L(a+b+c)=b-ab+1-c independently of k, so the nonexplosion proof even holds
uniformly for predictable switching. For STATIC k, detailed balance gives
Poisson means (1,k,1), conditioned on a+b>=1; the normalizer is finite.
Thus every fixed member is positive recurrent on Gamma. The common-certificate
failure does NOT establish failure of controlled recurrence or stability.
It also does NOT establish an escaping stationary family: k lies in a compact
interval and these fixed endpoint Poisson laws are tight.

4. COMPLETE QUADRATIC NO-GO PROOF

Write the most general quadratic polynomial as
    W(x)=x^T Q x+l^T x+d,
where Q is real symmetric. Coercivity means W(x)->infinity along every
Gamma integer sequence whose norm goes to infinity. We do not assume Q is
positive definite, Q has nonnegative entries, W is separable, or W is convex.
Assume for contradiction that, for each k=1,2, some finite K_k and eps_k>0
have L_k W<=-eps_k on Gamma\K_k. Each unbounded sequence below eventually
leaves both finite sets, so a positive leading drift coefficient contradicts
the assumption. All reactions preserve Gamma exactly.

STEP A. Every A-to-B/C quadratic cross coefficient vanishes.

At z_n=(0,n,0), the only enabled jumps are B->A+B at rate n and 0->C at
rate one. The leading n^2 coefficient of L W is 2Q_AB. Therefore Q_AB<=0.
At (a,0,0), only 0->C is enabled; its increment is
    2Q_AC a+Q_CC+l_C.
Let a->infinity to obtain Q_AC<=0.
At (a,0,1), C->B conversion has rate k a, and its W increment is
    2(Q_AB-Q_AC)a+(Q_BB-Q_CC)+(l_B-l_C).
Other enabled jumps contribute only O(a). Hence the leading a^2 term is
2k(Q_AB-Q_AC), and Q_AB<=Q_AC<=0.

Now fix ANY positive integer a and use b=n,c=n for endpoint k=1. Conversion
rates are both a n, so their quadratic gradient terms cancel exactly; their
jump-noise contribution is only O(n). Birth/death of A has leading n^2 term
    2(1-a)(Q_AB+Q_AC)n^2.
C death contributes -2(Q_BC+Q_CC)n^2; immigration contributes O(n).
Thus the n^2 coefficient is
    2(1-a)(Q_AB+Q_AC)-2(Q_BC+Q_CC).                       (3)
If Q_AB+Q_AC<0, choosing a sufficiently large FIXED integer makes (3)
positive. Then n->infinity violates Foster drift. Therefore Q_AB+Q_AC>=0.
Since both entries are nonpositive, Q_AB=Q_AC=0.

STEP B. A common B,C quadratic block is forced to depend only on b+c.

Put p=Q_BB, q=Q_BC, s=Q_CC. Hold a as an arbitrarily large but FIXED
integer, and take b=t n,c=n with any positive rational t (choose n multiples
of its denominator). The leading n^2 contribution of conversion is
    2a(k-t)[(p-q)t+(q-s)].                              (4)
Other n^2 terms, coming from C death, are independent of a. A birth/death
has only O(n) drift now that its cross coefficients vanish. Hence if
    (k-t)[(p-q)t+(q-s)]>0,
we can choose large fixed a to make the total leading coefficient positive.
The Foster assumption forces this product to be <=0 for ALL rational t>0,
and by continuity for all real t>0, at each endpoint k.

The factor k-t changes sign at t=k. Therefore the linear second factor must
vanish there; otherwise one nearby side gives positive product. Thus
    (p-q)k+q-s=0.
For k=1 and k=2 these two equations imply p-q=0 and q-s=0, so p=q=s.
The whole polynomial is forced to have the form
    W=a_2 a^2+q(b+c)^2+l_A a+l_B b+l_C c+d.              (5)

STEP C. The remaining form cannot have strict Foster drift.

Coercivity on B or C rays forces q>=0. If q=0, coercivity along (1,0,c)
forces l_C>0; but at (a,0,0) the only jump gives L W=l_C>0 for every a,
already impossible. Thus q>0.
Let d_0=l_C-l_B. At (a,0,1), all B,C square terms are preserved by
conversion, and the drift leading in a is -k d_0 a. The finite-set Foster
condition forces d_0>=0.

For fixed nonnegative integer a, take (a,n,0), n->infinity, at endpoint k=1.
Let g(a)=a_2 a^2+l_A a. The coefficient of n in L W is
    R(a)=[g(a+1)-g(a)]+a[g(a-1)-g(a)]+a d_0+2q,         (6)
with the a=0 death term zero. The first two terms are the immigration-death
generator Q_A g at birth rate one and death rate a. The common drift
assumption requires R(a)<=0 for EVERY nonnegative integer a, since these
states lie in Gamma even when a=0.

Average (6) over a Poisson(1) integer. Because g is quadratic, this average
is finite and E Q_A g=0, directly using E A=1 and E A^2=2, or the elementary
Poisson detailed-balance sum. Therefore
    E R(A)=d_0+2q>0,
contradicting R(a)<=0 for all a. This completes the proof.

This proof also excludes geometric quadratic Foster drift, which implies
strict negative drift outside a finite set for coercive W. It excludes real
as well as rational quadratics, even with endpoint-dependent exception sets.

6. DECISIVE CONSEQUENCE

A proposed robustness compiler cannot promise a common coercive quadratic
Foster certificate for ALL rational bimolecular endotactic inputs with bounded
predictable rate boxes. It already fails on this six-reaction, two-endpoint
fixture with an explicit robust deterministic absorber and individually
recurrent endpoint chains. A valid compiler must restrict the admitted rate
structure, allow a richer nonquadratic/state/phase certificate, or return
UNKNOWN/refutation of the candidate architecture without refuting recurrence.

SHARPER ELEMENTARY STEP C (FINAL PROOF SIMPLIFICATION)
The Poisson averaging argument above is valid but unnecessary. Coercivity
along (a,0,0) forces a_2=Q_AA>=0. After Steps A/B and the necessary d_0>=0,
the EXACT drift at (1,n,0) under endpoint k=1 is
    L W(1,n,0)=n(2a_2+d_0+2q)+(q+l_C).
A birth and death are equally likely at a=1, so their quadratic increments
sum to 2a_2; conversion contributes d_0 n; C influx contributes 2q n+q+l_C.
Since q>0, the coefficient of n is strictly positive. Letting n grow is an
immediate contradiction using only discrete polynomial algebra. This removes
Poisson averaging from the no-go's load-bearing premises. Stationary Poisson
laws remain independently useful for confirming each endpoint's recurrence.
