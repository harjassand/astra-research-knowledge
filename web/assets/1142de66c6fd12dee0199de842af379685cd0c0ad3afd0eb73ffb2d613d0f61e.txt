SU3 WALL WEIGHTED SPECTRAL PROFILE: LOW-WINDOW COUNT AND RANK TRANSFER
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: the low-window weighted count H and its rank/codimension transfer
are internally proved, conditional on the exact CPV-to-physical source map.
The stronger all-output interpolation (2.1) remains open and is not needed.

1. INPUT AND TARGET

Fix integers a>=b>=1, N=a+b, B=b+1, and V=V_(a,b) for SU(3), with
d=dim(V)=(a+1)(b+1)(N+2)/2. Use the exact T,S,C_S normalization in
../sol_semiclassical_memory/wall_su3/WEIGHTED_POSTERIOR_CONVERSE.txt.
The positive G-invariant operator on End(V) is

  L_w=L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)),
  <X,L_T X>=E_T(X),  <X,L_S X>=E_S(X).

For interior carriers, ker L_w=C I. Let

  D_w(t)=Tr 1_(0,t](L_w),

so the trivial scalar mode is excluded. The low-window all-matrix count H is

  D_w(t) <= C d[(N^2/B)t^2+N^3 t^3], 0<t<=c_0/N,           (1)

with constants independent of a,b,N. It is proved in
NARROW_WALL_WEIGHTED_COUNT.txt by an exact low-window Jacobi comparison;
the proof and its algebraic identities are independently audited in
audit_narrow_wall_weighted_count_explicit_polytope_models.txt, conditional
on the source-to-physical quartic map. Equivalently, writing T=N^2 t,

  D_w(T/N^2) <= C[T^2+(B/N)T^3], 0<T<=c_0 N.              (2)

This has the geometric form “two-dimensional base modes times a fiber
factor 1+NBt”; that interpretation remains a heuristic. Equation (1) is
the proved low-window count, subject to the source-map evidence boundary
above.

2. LOW-RANK PROFILE FROM (1)

Every spectral projector of L_w is SU(3)-invariant. Schur's lemma gives
Tr_2(P_t)=(D_w(t)/d)I. If ||A||_HS=1 and rank A<=Q, vectorization has
Schmidt rank at most Q, hence

  ||P_t A||_HS^2 <= Q D_w(t)/d.

Layer cake therefore gives

  <A,L_w A> >= integral_0^infinity [1-Q D_w(t)/d]_+ dt.    (3)

Set

  t_Q=(c/N) min{Q^(-1/3), sqrt(B/Q)}.

For Q<=B^3, Q^(-1/3)<=sqrt(B/Q); at t_Q the cubic term in (1),
multiplied by Q, is O(c^3), while the quadratic term is at most that
size because Q^(1/3)<=B. For Q>=B^3 the quadratic term is O(c^2),
and the cubic term is no larger. Choose c small enough that the bracket
in (3) is at least 1/2 on [0,t_Q]. Also t_Q<=c_0/N after reducing c.
Thus (1) implies

  Lambda_w(Q) >= c'/N min{Q^(-1/3), sqrt(B/Q)},
                   1<=Q<=d/2.                             (4)

The two branches cross at Q=B^3, exactly where Sol's joint-height and
whole-fiber constructive upper comparators meet.

3. NEAR-FULL PROFILE FOR ARBITRARY MATRICES

Let rank A<=d-k and choose a rank-k projection P onto a subspace of
ker A. Write A=zI+Y with Tr Y=0 and P_0=P-(k/d)I. From AP=0,

  kz=-Tr(P_0Y).

Set G(P)=<P_0,L_w^(-1)P_0>. Since the first nonzero SU(3) Casimir is
3, L_w>=L_T/N^2 gives gap gamma_w>=3/N^2. Cauchy-Schwarz in the
L_w^{-1}/L_w pairing yields the exact bridge

  ||A||_HS^2 <= <A,L_w A>[d G(P)/k^2+1/gamma_w].          (5)

For F_P(t)=||1_(0,t](L_w)P_0||_HS^2, the same invariant-projector
Schmidt argument and the exact norm of P_0 give

  F_P(t)<=min{k-k^2/d, k^2 D_w(t)/d},
  G(P)=integral_0^infinity F_P(t)t^(-2)dt.                  (6)

Assume (1), and put

  S_k=1/[N max{k^(1/3), sqrt(k/B)}]
      = (1/N) min{k^(-1/3), sqrt(B/k)}.

For k<=B^3, S_k=1/(N k^(1/3)); for k>=B^3,
S_k=sqrt(B)/(N sqrt(k)). In both cases S_k<=1/N, so the low-count
bound (1) applies on [0,S_k]. Split (6) at S_k. The low part gives

  (d/k^2) integral_0^S F_P(t)t^(-2)dt
       <= C d[(N^2/B)S+N^3 S^2],

and the high part gives

  (d/k^2) integral_S^infinity F_P(t)t^(-2)dt <= d/(kS).

Substituting S=S_k, d~N^2 B, and separating k<=B^3 from k>=B^3
shows that each term is bounded by

  C N d / min{k^(2/3), sqrt(Bk)}.                           (7)

The gap term N^2 is absorbed by (7) for 1<=k<=d/2. Hence (5) implies

  Lambda_w(d-k) >= c/[N d] min{k^(2/3),sqrt(Bk)},
                   1<=k<=d/2.                             (8)

The branch transition is again k=B^3. This proof uses a right kernel
and is valid for arbitrary complex, nonnormal A; no positive-filter
assumption is used in the lower bound.

4. FAILURE OF THE ORDINARY-CASIMIR SHORTCUT

The easy comparison L_w>=L_T/N^2 would reduce (1) to the carrier-specific
ordinary Casimir estimate

  dim 1_(0,T](L_T) <= C[T^2+(B/N)T^3], 0<T<=c_0 N.           (9)

However, (9) is false in a growing near-wall regime. Take B=b+1 with
B~N^alpha, 0<alpha<1, and Casimir cutoff T=B. Then sqrt(T)~sqrt(B)
is much smaller than b. The Racah-Speiser formula for the multiplicity
of V_lambda in V_lambda tensor V_nu has the identity term
dim V_nu[0]; every nonidentity Weyl shift has root height at least
b+1, so it lies outside the V_nu weight diagram when ||nu||<=c sqrt(B).
Therefore, uniformly on a fixed interior cone of such small labels,

  mult(V_nu,End(V_lambda))=dim V_nu[0].

For SU(3), a root-lattice highest weight nu=(p,q) has zero-weight
multiplicity min(p,q)+1. Restrict p,q to a fixed interior subcone with
p-q divisible by 3 and p,q<=c sqrt(B). There are Theta(B) such labels;
each has dim V_(p,q)~B^(3/2) and zero-weight multiplicity~sqrt(B).
Consequently

  dim 1_(0,B](L_T) >= c B^3.                            (10)

But the proposed right side in (9) is

  B^2+(B/N)B^3=o(B^3)

because B->infinity and B/N->0. Thus ordinary Casimir projectors cannot
prove (1); the positive L_S term must lift a substantial part of the
stable low-Casimir multiplicity space. This does not refute the weighted
count (1).

Exact End(V_(a,b)) Littlewood-Richardson/BZ multiplicities and a
zero-weight/branching estimate remain useful for measuring the stable
subspace that L_S must split. The global all-carrier
Fronsdal-Hirai/Kostant count O((1+T)^3) is also too weak near the wall.

A different route is to decompose End(V) into SU(3) isotypic blocks.
L_T is c_2(nu) times identity on each block; L_S is a positive matrix
on the outer-multiplicity space. The invariant mixed operator

  M=sum_A ad(T_A) ad(S_A)

preserves those blocks, and for a vector psi in a c_2(nu)=c block,
row-operator Cauchy-Schwarz gives ||M psi||^2<=c <psi,L_S psi>.
Thus L_S>=M^2/c there. On V_(a,b) tensor V_(b,a), M is expressible
as an explicit cubic/missing-label operator, proportional to

  [2 C_3+(A-B)-alpha L_T]/N,

where A-B is the cross cubic. A useful proof would diagonalize or bound
this operator on each outer-multiplicity fiber and sum its sublevel
counts. This statement is a tool, not a completed count proof.

5. EVIDENCE BOUNDARY AND NEXT CHECKS

The low-rank and near-full transfer estimates (3)-(8) follow from the
proved count (1). Sol's separately banked joint-height and whole-fiber
upper filters and weighted posterior converse supply the remaining
dependencies for the operational order profile. The all-output sorted
eigenvalue interpolation (2.1) is still unknown and stronger than needed.
The adjoint multiplicity-two block shows a uniform secondary-ellipticity
inequality L_S>=c L_T fails as b/N->0; it does not refute H. A finite
matrix search can falsify small cases, not prove the uniform count. Keep
pure walls C_S=0 separate; the weighted normalization is undefined there.
