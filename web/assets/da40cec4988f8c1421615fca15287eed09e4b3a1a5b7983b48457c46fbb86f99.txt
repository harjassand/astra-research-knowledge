RESULT A — SPLITTING-FIELD K3 CARRIER IS IMPOSSIBLE IN THE TEST CASE

Let E=Q(sqrt(5)), with real embeddings sigma_0(sqrt(5))=+sqrt(5) and
sigma_1(sqrt(5))=-sqrt(5). Let p_2=(2) and p_3=(3) in O_E. Since
theta=(1+sqrt(5))/2 has polynomial x^2-x-1 irreducible modulo both 2 and 3,
these are prime ideals. Choose quaternion classes D_A,D_B by the ABHN
ramification data

  Ram(D_A)={sigma_1,p_2},   Ram(D_B)={sigma_1,p_3}.

Then beta=[D_A]+[D_B] is ramified exactly at p_2,p_3 and has index two.
At sigma_0 both D_A,D_B split, so the active real spin group is
SL_2(R) x SL_2(R). Its four-dimensional tensor-spin representation W has
cocharacter weights (-1,0,0,1), with one top line. The invariant symmetric
form epsilon tensor epsilon has signature (2,2).

Theorem (no splitting-extension K3 carrier, exact scope). Suppose a proposed
auxiliary K3-type Hodge structure is obtained by taking the four-dimensional
tensor-spin representation after a field extension L/E that splits beta,
then restricting scalars to Q, with the target Hodge cocharacter acting on
each conjugate copy through the same spin-tensor representation and the full
G-action preserving the carrier polarization. No such K3-type carrier exists.

Proof. At p_2, inv_{p_2}(beta)=1/2. For any place w|p_2 in a splitting
extension L, the local invariant identity gives
  0=inv_w(beta_L)=[L_w:E_{p_2}]/2 mod 1,
so [L_w:E_{p_2}] is even and [L:E]>=2.

If L is totally real, each real embedding over sigma_0 carries a copy of W.
The unique invariant form on W has signature (2,2), unchanged by multiplying
it by -1. At least two copies therefore contribute at least four positive
directions to the trace polarization. A K3-type polarization has exactly two
positive directions, contradiction.

If L is CM, its maximal real subfield F contains E. At a real place of F over
sigma_0 the complex W has an invariant Hermitian form obtained from its real
structure and q=epsilon tensor epsilon. Irreducibility makes this Hermitian
form unique up to real scalar, and its signature is (2,2). The underlying
real trace form has signature (4,4), already contributing four positive
directions. This again contradicts the K3-type signature. Thus simply
splitting beta in a larger totally real or CM coefficient field does not
repair the rank-four K3 carrier.

For a simple K3-type carrier the coefficient field is totally real or CM by
Zarhin's theorem, so these cases cover the standard scalar-restriction
repair. A construction using a different representation or a different
algebraic group is outside this result.

The obstruction does not apply to a different irreducible representation,
to an abelian or higher-dimensional carrier, or to a construction that does
not restrict scalars from W_L. Those remain separate routes.

EXACT TEST FIXTURE. Leaf09's classes realize the obstruction with actual
rank-three RM K3 Hodge structures: their full product group is
Res_{E/Q}SO(V_A) x Res_{E/Q}SO(V_B), active at sigma_0, and their Clifford
classes are D_A,D_B. In H^4(S_A x S_B), the rational cross orbit

  H_C=(V_{A,sigma_0} tensor V_{B,sigma_1})
       direct-sum (V_{A,sigma_1} tensor V_{B,sigma_0})

has Hodge types (3,1),(2,2),(1,3), so it is a coniveau-one test target with
d=2,p=1. The target needs one K3-return saving. The product carrier S_A x S_B
uses two ordinary k=1 returns and gives support only d-2=0; the common-class
one-K3 carrier would save one return, but the index-two beta obstruction
prevents its rank-four spin tensor over E. This is the precise boundary, not
a refutation of GHC.

RESULT B — NONCRITICAL CODIMENSION FALLBACK

Let X be a finite product of K3 surfaces S_i, and assume its connected Hodge
group on the transcendental parts is the full product of the relevant
Res_{E_i/Q}SO(V_i,q_i), where each V_i has rank three over the totally real
field E_i. Let H be a rationally simple Hodge substructure of H^{2d}(X,Q).
After Tate normalization, choose an absolutely irreducible constituent of
H_C, with SO_3 highest weight j_{i,sigma} at each surface/embedding factor.
Its total tensor height is

  M(H)=sum_{i,sigma} j_{i,sigma}.

The value is constant on the Galois orbit defining H. Conditional on the
released ordinary HC for all finite K3 products and the exact k=1 KS return
R_{S,1}, if

  M(H) <= d-p,

then H is contained in geometric coniveau p: H subset N^pH^{2d}(X,Q).
This result allows arbitrary independent fields and arbitrary individual
quaternion Clifford classes. It does not cover the critical cross orbit above,
where M=2>d-p=1.

Proof. For each i set J_i=sum_sigma j_{i,sigma}; then sum_i J_i=M. The
rational carrier

  C= tensor_i V_i^{tensor J_i}

contains the chosen constituent: for each SO_3 factor, the J_i tensor power
contains the Cartan component of highest weight j_{i,sigma}, and V_i over Q
contains its standard module at every embedding. The carrier is defined over
Q, so a nonzero complex Hom from C_C to H_C descends to a nonzero rational
Hodge map C -> H(d); simplicity makes it surjective.

Take Y=product_i S_i^{J_i}; C occurs in H^{2M}(Y,Q)(M). Poincare duality
turns the map into a rational Hodge class of codimension M+d on Y x X.
The ordinary-HC premise makes this class algebraic. For each of the M copies
of S_i in Y, compose the k=1 KS return from its prescribed abelian
Kuga–Satake square. Their product is an algebraic correspondence into Y
whose action is a right inverse on C; k=1 has support codimension zero.
Composing with the Hodge-class cycle gives a cycle on Z x X of codimension
dim(Z)+d-M. Its projection to X has codimension at least d-M>=p. The image
is H, proving the conditional claim.

The inequality M<=d-p is the load-bearing gate. The theorem does not infer it
from Hodge coniveau; it is a representation-height hypothesis to be checked
on the target. For the same-field, different-quaternion cross target,
M=2,d-p=1, so this fallback stops exactly one return short.

FOUR MECHANISMS AND THEIR OPPOSING ROUTES

1. Rank-four E-descent. Proof route: if D_A and D_B have the same class, the
   spin tensor descends to the required four-dimensional E-module and the
   N56 auxiliary-K3 construction applies. Obstruction route: for the
   explicit D_A,D_B above, beta is nonzero of index two; the tensor has no
   four-dimensional E-form, only an eight-dimensional E-simple module. This
   is established for the named carrier.

2. Scalar extension to split beta. Proof route: choose L/E splitting beta,
   construct the spin tensor over L, and try to descend an auxiliary K3.
   Obstruction route: splitting degree is at least two; restriction of
   scalars repeats a (2,2) real spin module, or in the CM case its (2,2)
   Hermitian module, exceeding the two-positive-direction K3 signature.
   This closes this natural repair, not all coefficient-field techniques.

3. Product-of-original-surfaces carrier. Proof route: use ordinary HC on
   Y x X and individual k=1 returns; the theorem above succeeds whenever
   the total tensor height M fits inside d-p. Obstruction route: for the
   critical cross orbit M=2,d-p=1, so the composed support bound is zero.
   The estimate is exact and identifies a one-return deficit.

4. Different abelian/higher-dimensional carrier. Proof route: construct a
   rational algebraic correspondence whose image carries the cross orbit
   with one fewer support return than the product baseline. Obstruction
   route: prove every such carrier must retain multiplicity two or has
   insufficient support codimension. No such theorem or counterexample is
   established here; status UNKNOWN. The existing beta class alone does not
   decide this route.

out the first possibility; the exact local signature calculation rules out
the second. Falsifier for Result B: a
finite highest-weight fixture with M<=d-p but no rational Hom from the tensor
carrier, or an exact support calculation failing to give codimension d-M.
The first is ruled out by the Cartan component and scalar extension of Hom;
the second is contradicted by the correspondence codimension formula. These
are internal proofs, not formal verification.
