1. NO HS-SELF-ADJOINTNESS: ONE C8 CANONICAL EB COMPARATOR
Let Phi:M_d->M_d be any CPTP unital channel with a supplied CPTP broadcaster
B whose two marginals are Phi. Phi need not be HS-self-adjoint or normal.
Because Phi is bistochastic, Phi* is also CPTP and unital. Define
  Theta=Phi* composed with Phi,
  B_Theta=(Phi* tensor Phi*) composed with B.
Then Theta is CPTP, unital, positive and self-adjoint on real Hermitian
HS space, and B_Theta has both marginals Theta. The main theorem supplies
ONE pure canonical EB Psi with I-Psi<=4(I-Theta).

The exact HS identity
  (I-Phi)*(I-Phi)=I-Phi-Phi*+Phi*Phi>=0
implies
  I-Phi*Phi<=2[I-(Phi+Phi*)/2].
Consequently
  I-Psi<=8[I-(Phi+Phi*)/2].
The quadratic form of Phi is that of its symmetric part, so the same
Psi satisfies
  tau[A(A-Psi(A))]<=8 tau[A(A-Phi(A))]
for EVERY Hermitian A. No compatibility of Phi* is assumed; only the
explicit postprocessing of the supplied B is used. No independent
choice of comparator for each direction is introduced.

2. ARBITRARY BOUNDED TRACIAL LIKELIHOODS: DIMENSION-FREE TRACE BOUND
Let H be any family of traceless Hermitian matrices with
  ||H||_infinity<=theta<1,
and rho_H=(I+H)/d. Suppose ONE legal Phi obeys
  sup_H T(rho_H,Phi(rho_H))<=epsilon,
T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. Unitality gives
  tau|H-Phi(H)|<=2epsilon.
Holder and HS contraction imply
  0<=delta_Phi(H)=tau[H(H-Phi(H))]<=2theta epsilon.

The canonical comparator is a positive HS contraction, hence
  (I-Psi)^2<=I-Psi.
If the Dirichlet constant is C, normalized Schatten Cauchy-Schwarz gives
  T(rho_H,Psi(rho_H))
   =tau|(I-Psi)H|/2
   <=sqrt(tau[H(I-Psi)^2H])/2
   <=sqrt(C delta_Phi(H))/2
   <=sqrt(C theta epsilon/2).

Thus the SAME channel gives the full tracial-family error
  sqrt(2theta epsilon) for HS-self-adjoint Phi (C4),
  2sqrt(theta epsilon) for general bistochastic Phi (C8).
The bounds also hold at theta=1, but theta<1 provides faithful states.
This covers arbitrary full-domain tracial likelihood families, not just
Clifford/spin spaces. It does not itself reconstruct the separately
imported N93 nontracial/flagged transport or acquisition reductions.
