INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE p2 GATES AND ERROR

Write p2(x)=1+x+x^2/2. For each shifted local positive term A_i use
F_i=p2(h A_i), h=beta/(2m), and form
B=F_1 ... F_p F_p ... F_1, then approximate exp(beta K) for
K=sum_i A_i=H+C I by B^m. All statements here concern the same rational
in-cone interface as INITIAL: alpha>=|gamma|, b>=0, arbitrary rational Z
fields, rational beta>=0. Every A_i is PSD and entrywise nonnegative in the
computational basis; hence F_i is rational, entrywise nonnegative, and
F_i>=I.

Edge gate. The eigenvalues of A_e are x0=alpha-gamma,
x1=3alpha+gamma, and x2=5alpha-gamma, with multiplicity two for x1.
The cone gives 0<=x0<=x1<=x2. If l=p2(h x0), a=p2(h x1),
u=p2(h x2), then the six-vertex weights are b=(nu+l)/2 and
c=(nu-l)/2. Since p2 is increasing on [0,infinity), l<=a<=nu and
c>=0. Thus |a-b|<=c and c<=a+b; equivalently the three non-leading
Hessian eigenvalues a-b-c, -a+b-c, -a-b+c are nonpositive. The
homogeneous quadratic has exactly one positive Hessian eigenvalue, which
suffices for log-concavity on the positive orthant. This is an exact
all-parameter derivation, not a finite-grid inference.

Field gate. Put r=b+|c| and q=sqrt(b^2+c^2)<=r. The shifted field
A_v has eigenvalues r+-q>=0. Write
F_v=[[U+w c,w b],[w b,U-w c]], where
U=1+h r+h^2(r^2+q^2)/2 and w=h+h^2 r. Its off-diagonal d=wb is
nonnegative. For the two-dummy lift the required determinant inequality is
U^2-w^2 q^2>=0. Directly,
U-wq=1+h(r-q)+(h^2/2)(r-q)^2>=1 and U+wq>0, so it holds strictly.
The lift's Hessian, for d>0, has an (x,y) block of inertia (1,1); its
Schur complement is
[[-uv/(2d), d-uv/(2d)],[d-uv/(2d),-uv/(2d)]],
with eigenvalues -d and (d^2-uv)/d<=0. If d=0 it factors as a product
of nonnegative linear forms. Thus the local homogeneous multiaffine lift is
log-concave, including b=0 and cone boundary cases.

Layer error. Let G_i=log p2(h A_i), S=sum_i G_i, and
g=sum_i ||G_i||<=hD, where D=2C and sum_i||A_i||<=D. For
0<=x<=1, the exponential series gives
0<=x-log p2(x)<=-log(1-x^3/6)<=x^3/5.
Functional calculus and sum_i||A_i||^3<=D^3 imply
||2S-2hK||<=2 h^3 D^3/5.

In the product defining B, the degree-zero and degree-one terms agree with
exp(2S). Its degree-two term is 2S^2: reversal pairs each ordered product
G_iG_j with G_jG_i, canceling the commutator sum. The norm of each
remaining tail is at most (2g)^3 exp(2g)/6. If m>=2 beta D, then
g<=1/4, so
||B-exp(2S)|| <= (8/3)g^3 exp(2g) <= (16/3)h^3D^3.
Both matrices are >=I. For positive X,Y>=I, the integral formula for log
and the resolvent identity yield ||log X-log Y||<=||X-Y||. Therefore
||log B-2hK|| <= (86/15)h^3D^3 and, since h=beta/(2m),
||m log B-beta K|| <= (43/60)(beta D)^3/m^2.
Thus the peer schedule
m=ceil max{1, 2 tau, sqrt(43 tau^3/(15 eta))}, tau=beta D,
ensures generator error <=eta/4. The factor 43/60 and the square-root
schedule are internally consistent. The separate c06_l03 estimate uses a
coarser product-tail/Loewner route and a slightly larger sufficient m; it
is compatible, not contradictory.

RATIONALITY/COUNT INTERFACE. p2(h A_i) has rational entries. Its local
polynomial is degree two on four fresh variables; the edge signature is
homogeneous LC by the triangle calculation, and the field signature is LC
by the two-dummy Hessian. In B^m there are 2mp local gates and N=8mp
variables. For an edge count e and field count f, the physical wire count is
W=2e+f. The product P of local signatures has degree 2(e+f), and Q is the
product of W pair factors with e_f on 2f dummy variables; both degrees are
2e+2f. Because local blocks are disjoint, P's coefficient at any subset is
a product of one local rational coefficient per gate, evaluable directly in
O(mp) rational multiplications. Q's coefficient is 0 or 1. Positive support
seeds are constructed locally. A crude max/min coefficient ratio contributes
log R=O(mp ell), ell=1+L+log m, since a coefficient is a sum of at most
2^N products whose factors have O(ell)-bit coefficients. This charge is not
free.

The imported Chen--Liu homogeneous theorem gives arithmetic/oracle work
O(N^10 B0^2 rho^-2 log^2(2B0/rho)), B0=N+ln(R_P R_Q).
Substituting N=8mp and B0=O(mp ell) gives an abstract-oracle core
O((mp)^12 ell^2 rho^-2 log^2(2mp ell/rho)). Evaluating each P oracle
answer directly costs O(mp) rational multiplications, so the explicit
unit-cost rational composition is O((mp)^13 ell^2 rho^-2 log^2(...));
integer bit costs remain polynomial in the operand lengths, but the source
does not supply a sharper bit-operation exponent for this composition.
This remains conditional on Theorem 5.1 and Remark A.4 of the imported
arXiv preprint. I did not run its coefficient FPRAS.

COST RECONCILIATION AND REAL LIMIT

For one partition estimate with generator budget eta=Theta(epsilon), count
relative error rho=Theta(epsilon), and large tau, the new m is
Theta(tau^(3/2) epsilon^(-1/2)). Therefore the abstract core is
O(p^12 tau^18 epsilon^-8) up to logarithms; charging direct local-oracle
construction gives O(p^13 tau^(39/2) epsilon^(-17/2)). The first-order
Euler counterpart is O(p^12 tau^24 epsilon^-14), or
O(p^13 tau^26 epsilon^-15) with explicit oracle evaluation. At fixed tau,
the new single partition count is epsilon^-8 up to logs (versus epsilon^-14).
These are asymptotic upper bounds, not measured speedups.

For largest-eigenvalue extraction, the independent baseline inequality is
lambda_max(H)>=max(A+B,T-A)>=(3A+4B+T)/5>=C/5,
where A=sum alpha, B=sum b, T=sum |c|. Also ||H||<=C. Choose
beta=20(n+1)/(epsilon C), making tau=40(n+1)/epsilon. A constant
relative count and constant generator error suffice because
log Tr(exp(beta(H+C I)))/beta-C lies between lambda_max(H) and
lambda_max(H)+n log(2)/beta; the count's constant log error is also
O(1/beta). Both errors are below epsilon C/5 with slack. This avoids
forming exp(-beta C) and gives a binary-input relative FPRAS conditional on
the imported theorem. New m=O((n/epsilon)^(3/2)); the abstract dense-graph
count is O(n^42 epsilon^-18) up to logs, and explicit local-oracle
composition is O(n^(45.5) epsilon^(-19.5)) up to logs. The latter is the
more honest arithmetic operation accounting, not a practical guarantee.

For the thermal partition function itself, the shift recovery multiplies by
exp(-beta C). Relative rational output can require Theta(beta C+
log(1/epsilon)) scale bits and computation polynomial in numerical beta C;
this is not uniformly polynomial in binary log(beta C). Rational gate
entries have polynomial bit length O(L+log m) per local input coefficient;
the shift scalar, not the gate schedule, is the numerical-temperature
bottleneck.

For a k-site reduced matrix, let Delta=|a|-|b| for row string a and
column string b. The open-wire pins select k+Delta P variables. With
N=4(e+f), P's residual degree is N/2-k-Delta. There are W-k closed
physical wire factors, so Q must use e_(f+Delta) on the 2f dummy variables;
its degree is N/2-k+Delta. These degrees sum to N-2k. The rank correction
is necessary for nonzero X-field charge changes and is the same rule found
in peer c06_s02. Estimating all d^2 entries and the denominator to
rho=epsilon/(100d^2), d=2^k, yields total entrywise error at most
3epsilon/100 before projection; symmetrizing, PSD clipping and trace
normalization cost at most four times that error. A direct perturbation
check also bounds normalized Gibbs-state trace distance: along
K_t=K_0+tE, ||E||<=eta, the Duhamel derivative of rho_t is its
Kubo--Mori covariance with E. Cauchy--Schwarz for that covariance and
Hölder's Schatten bound give ||d rho_t/dt||_1<=eta; integrating gives
||rho_1-rho_0||_1<=eta. Thus eta=epsilon/4 leaves ample budget for the
thermal-transfer and entry-estimation errors. Overall counting multiplicity
is O(d^6 epsilon^-2 log(d/delta)); for k=c log_2 n, fixed c, d=n^c.
This is reconstruction from entry counts, not global state preparation, and
gives additive observable guarantees through trace norm, not relative ones
for signed observables.
