2. The FULL N^4(A+I) heat moment

field margin. The positive trace weight N^4 exp(-uB_v) is legitimate
because N and B_v commute. Cyclicity lets the operator FORM
F<=B_v/a+I be spent under that positive weight even though F and B_v
do not commute:

 Tr[N^4 F exp(-uB)]
 <=Tr[N^4(B/a+I) exp(-uB)].

This trace is nonnegative. In a joint eigenbasis of N,B the remaining
bound is entirely scalar. For energy b>=0,

 (b/a+1) exp(-u b/2)<=1+2/(e a u).

For integer number m>=0, relaxed to all real m>=0,

 m^4 exp(-u s m/2)<=[8/(e u s)]^4.

Multiplying these two inequalities leaves exp[-(u/2)(b-sm)]. Thus

 Tr[N^4 F exp(-uB_v(lambda))]
 <=[8/(e u s)]^4[1+2/(e a u)]
     Tr exp[-(u/2)(B_v(lambda)-sN)]
 <=R_*=[8/(e u s)]^4[1+2/(e a u)]C_q.

Here C_q is the uniform full partition supremum at shifted field
lambda+s and time u/2=tau/4. Both quarter time and extra chemical margin

sector multiplicity. Vacuum gives Z_v>=1 at all L, while positive B and

3. Homogeneous contact and the short heat sandwich

lose the integrable exponent. For m>=1,L>=2,
a_m<=8m^2/L and b_m<=b_Jm^2/L, b_J=4/sqrt(2J).

Take any unit phi in an m-sector and z=exp(-tB/2)phi, t>0. Since B>=0,
||z||<=1. Since B>=aA,

 <z,Az><=a^-1<phi,B exp(-tB)phi><=1/(e a t).

The last inequality is scalar spectral maximization, not a comparison of
exponentials of different operators. Consequently

 ||exp(-tB/2)W exp(-tB/2)||
 <=(m^2/L)[8+b_J/sqrt(e a t)].

With X=W^(1/2)exp(-tB/2), XX*=W^(1/2)exp(-tB)W^(1/2)
and X*X=exp(-tB/2)W exp(-tB/2). Equality of their norms gives exactly

For long time only, sqrt(x)<=(x+1)/2 gives the FORM

 Tr_m[W exp(-tB)]<=c_J(m^2/L)Tr_m[F exp(-uB)].

4. Exact noncommutative KMS factors and Hessian constant

Let f(v)=log Z_v. Finite-matrix Duhamel gives

 d exp(-tau B_v)/dv
   =integral_0^tau exp(-tB_v)W exp[-(tau-t)B_v]dt.

Taking cyclic traces gives Z'=tau Tr[W exp(-tau B)]. Differentiating once
more therefore yields

 f''(v)=tau/Z_v integral_0^tau I(t)dt-tau^2<W>_v^2,
 I(t)=Tr[exp(-tB)W exp[-(tau-t)B]W].

The outer tau in this expression is correct. In a B eigenbasis the
diagonal portion after subtracting the squared mean is tau^2 times a
classical variance; each off-diagonal contribution is a positive scalar
weight times |W_ij|^2. Thus f''>=0 without requiring commuting W,B.

Inside one m-sector put P_t=W^(1/2)exp(-tB)W^(1/2)>=0. Exact cyclic
factorization gives

 I_m(t)=Tr(P_t P_(tau-t)).

Indeed the adjacent two W^(1/2) factors multiply to W; cyclically moving
the outside roots produces the second W. Thus I_m is real nonnegative
and <=||P_t|| Tr(P_(tau-t)). For 0<t<=u the second time is >=u. The
short norm and long trace from Section3 give

 I_m(t)<=c_J(m^4/L^2)[8+b_J/sqrt(e a t)]
             Tr_m[F exp(-uB)].

Trace cyclicity also gives I_m(t)=I_m(tau-t). The complete sum of the
m^4-weighted long traces is exactly R_4 from Section2. All sums are
finite for each L; no uncontrolled infinite spectral interchange occurs.
The t=0, tau endpoints have measure zero and the envelope is integrable.
Use Z_v>=1 and discard the nonnegative mean square to obtain
