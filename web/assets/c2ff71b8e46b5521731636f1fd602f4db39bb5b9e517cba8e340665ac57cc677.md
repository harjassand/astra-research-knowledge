# Independent hostile audit: Spin(9) pure-spinor moment body

**Scope.** This report audits the Spin(9) pure-seed moment formulas, their attainability, the canonical-EB support used by the comparison, and the finite support-cone reduction. It does not independently verify the three-copy star matrices or their PSD certificates. The overall broadcaster-to-EB statement therefore remains conditional on that separate star-block proof and on its stated covariant/legal-Choi interface.

## Finding

The claimed pure-seed moment body is exactly the triangle with vertices

\[
v_0=(1,0,0,14),\qquad v_H=(0,1,7,7),\qquad v_V=(1,4,4,6).
\]

The representation-theoretic orbit reduction and the Clifford moment calculation both check out. For every nonnegative invariant weight \(\alpha=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_4)\), the support of the full-domain canonical EB class is

\[
S_{\rm can}(\alpha)=\max\{\alpha_1+14\alpha_4,\;\alpha_2+7\alpha_3+7\alpha_4,\;\alpha_1+4\alpha_2+4\alpha_3+6\alpha_4\}.
\]

There is one wording trap: the vectors of **all** canonical EB maps are not the pure-seed triangle. For example, the canonical POVM with its sole effect \(I\) is the replacer \(X\mapsto \operatorname{Tr}(X)I/16\), whose four traceless-sector scores are zero; each pure-seed vector has coordinate sum 15. What is true, and sufficient for the support formula, is that the score functional is convex in each normalized effect state when \(\alpha_k\ge0\), so higher-rank effects cannot exceed the pure-seed maximum. The three vertices are attained by full-domain Spin(9)-orbit POVMs.

The ray audit independently confirms the ten distinct generators of the three winner cones, including all tie faces. Because the matrix gap is linear in \(\alpha\) on each cone, PSD checks at those ten rays suffice for all nonnegative weights. The parent star certificate is a separate input to this implication.

## Orbit reconstruction and endpoint audit

Use the real spin module \(S_{\mathbb R}\cong\mathbb O\oplus\mathbb O\), and real symmetric Clifford generators. The classical action facts needed are: Spin(9) is transitive on the real unit spinor sphere with point stabilizer Spin(7); the Spin(7) isotropy module at a unit spinor splits as the standard real 7 and real spin 8; Spin(7) acts transitively on the unit sphere of the spin 8 with stabilizer G\(_2\); and this G\(_2\) acts transitively on the unit sphere of the standard 7. This is the *valid* chain: G\(_2\) is the stabilizer of a unit vector in the spin-8 action, not the stabilizer of a vector in the 7. The published octonionic derivation gives these actions and isotropy representations explicitly ([Daura Serrano, Kohn, Lawn, Appendix, especially the Spin(7) and Spin(9) actions](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10455-022-09855-z)).

For a complex pure ray, choose phase so its real and imaginary parts are orthogonal and write

\[
\psi=u x+i v y,\quad x\perp y,\quad \|x\|=\|y\|=1,\quad u^2+v^2=1,\quad u\ge v\ge0.
\]

Set \(p=(u^2-v^2)^2=|\psi^T\psi|^2\). Decompose \(y=y_7+y_8\) relative to \(x^\perp=V_7\oplus H_8\) and set \(t=\|y_7\|^2\). Spin(9) first moves \(x\) to a fixed basepoint. The residual Spin(7) moves the direction of \(y_8\) transitively; its G\(_2\) stabilizer moves the direction of \(y_7\) transitively. Thus at fixed component norms there is one ordered-frame orbit, including the endpoints where one component vanishes. Equivalently, one may first fix the 7-vector direction using Spin(7)/Spin(6), then use Spin(6)\(\cong\)SU(4) on the realification of its defining \(\mathbb C^4\) in \(H_8\); it is transitive on \(S^7\). The Spin(7)-then-G\(_2\) chain above avoids confusing which subgroup stabilizes which representation.

When \(p>0\), the phase choice makes the decomposition unique up to harmless signs. When \(p=0\), the phase is not fixed, but \(t\) remains determined by the phase-invariant pure-projector score \(s_1=t\) from the formula below. An independent invariant gives the same conclusion: with \(q_i=\psi^T\Gamma_i\psi\), \(R=\sum_i|q_i|^2\) is invariant under Spin(9) and global phase, and the Clifford relations give \(R=1+(1-p)(1-2t)\). At \(p=0\), this recovers \(t=1-R/2\). At \(p=1\), \(\psi\) is real up to phase, so \(t\) is immaterial. Every \((p,t)\in[0,1]^2\) is realized by the representative

\[
u=\sqrt{(1+\sqrt p)/2},\quad v=\sqrt{(1-\sqrt p)/2},\quad
y=\sqrt t\,e_1+\sqrt{1-t}\,e_8,
\quad x=e_0,\quad \psi=ux+ivy.
\]

No hidden orbit or endpoint assumption was found.

## Moment derivation, normalizations, and attainability

Let \(T_A=i^{k(k-1)/2}\Gamma_A\) for \(|A|=k\le4\), and \(\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/16\). The 256 operators at grades 0 through 4 are a Hermitian orthonormal basis for the \(\tau\)-inner product: each squares to \(I\), and the trace of each nonempty Clifford monomial of grade at most 8 vanishes by conjugating with an included generator when its grade is even and an excluded generator when its grade is odd. Thus

\[
s_k=16^2\|\Pi_k(P_\psi-I/16)\|_{2,\tau}^2
=\sum_{|A|=k}(\psi^*T_A\psi)^2,
\qquad \sum_{k=1}^4s_k=16\operatorname{Tr}(P_\psi^2)-1=15.
\]

At a real unit \(x\), let \(h_i=x^T\Gamma_i x\) and \(b_i(z)=z^T\Gamma_i z\). The octonionic Hopf/Clifford identity gives \(\|b(z)\|=1\), \(\Gamma(h)x=x\), and

\[
x^\perp=V_7\oplus H_8=(E_+(\Gamma(h))\ominus\mathbb Rx)\oplus E_-(\Gamma(h)).
\]

For unit \(y=y_7+y_8\), this yields \(h\cdot b(y)=2t-1\). Symmetry of the grade-one generators removes the cross terms in \(\psi^*\Gamma_i\psi\), so

\[
s_1=\|u^2b(x)+v^2b(y)\|^2=p+(1-p)t.
\]

For grades 2 and 3 the real Clifford monomials \(\Gamma_A\) are skew-symmetric. Their Hermitian versions have expectation, up to an irrelevant sign, \(2uv\,x^T\Gamma_Ay\). Therefore \(s_k=(1-p)B_k\), where \(B_k=\sum_{|A|=k}(x^T\Gamma_Ay)^2\). The Spin(7)-invariant quadratic form \(B_k\) has one scalar on each of the inequivalent summands \(V_7,H_8\).

For grade 2, set \(L_{ij}=\Gamma_i\Gamma_j/2\). Then \(B_2=4\sum_{i<j}\langle y,L_{ij}x\rangle^2\). With the ninth axis equal to \(h\), the eight \(h\wedge h^\perp\) generators give a \(1/2\)-scaled orthonormal frame of \(H_8\), hence frame operator \(I/4\) there. The other 28 generators act within \(E_+(\Gamma(h))\); their sum of squares is \(-28I/4=-7I\). The associated invariant frame operator is scalar on irreducible \(V_7\), with trace \(7\), hence it is \(I\) there. Thus \(B_2=4(t+(1-t)/4)=1+3t\).

For grade 3, an exact octonion count at \(x=(1,0)\) gives \(B_3=4\) on a unit vertical basis vector and \(B_3=7\) on a unit horizontal basis vector. In the explicit real Clifford model \(\Gamma_a=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&L_{e_a}\\L_{e_a}^T&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\) for \(a=0,\ldots,7\), with \(\Gamma_8=\operatorname{diag}(I,-I)\), the vertical count consists of the four triples \((0,1,8),(2,3,8),(4,5,8),(6,7,8)\); the horizontal count consists of the seven Fano triples \((1,2,3),(1,4,5),(1,6,7),(2,4,6),(2,5,7),(3,4,7),(3,5,6)\). Every other triple gives zero in the respective bilinear. Hence \(B_3=4t+7(1-t)=7-3t\). Parseval then gives

\[
(s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4)=
(p+(1-p)t,\ (1-p)(1+3t),\ (1-p)(7-3t),\ 7(1+p)-(1-p)t).
\]

The barycentric identity is exact:

\[
(s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4)=p v_0+(1-p)(1-t)v_H+(1-p)t v_V.
\]

The weights are nonnegative and sum to one. Conversely, for barycentric weights \((\lambda_0,\lambda_H,\lambda_V)\), set \(p=\lambda_0\), and for \(p<1\), \(t=\lambda_V/(1-p)\). The explicit representative above realizes the point; at \(p=1\), the only point is \(v_0\). This proves the pure moment image is the whole triangle, not just contained in it.

The exact SymPy check in [`spin9_octonion_moments.py`](proof/exactchecks/spin9_octonion_moments.py) constructs all nine 16-by-16 real symmetric Clifford matrices from a Fano multiplication table, verifies the Clifford relations, sums every Clifford form at grades 1 through 4, and reduces the identities exactly under \(u^2+v^2=1\) and \(x^2+y^2=1\) (script variables \(a,b,u,v\)). It also checks the phase-invariant quartic and all three endpoints. This is a reproducible algebra check supplementing, not replacing, the derivation above.

## Canonical EB support and legal interface

For an effect \(E_z\), a canonical measure-and-prepare channel prepares \(\rho_z=E_z/\operatorname{Tr}E_z\). Its score is

\[
\operatorname{Score}_\alpha(\Psi)=
\sum_z \frac{\operatorname{Tr}E_z}{16}
F_\alpha(\rho_z),\quad
F_\alpha(\rho)=\sum_{k=1}^4\alpha_k\sum_{|A|=k}(\operatorname{Tr}(\rho T_A))^2.
\]

The weights sum to one since \(\sum_zE_z=I\). For \(\alpha_k\ge0\), \(F_\alpha\) is a sum of nonnegative multiples of squares of linear functionals, hence convex. If \(\rho=\sum_jq_jP_j\), Jensen gives \(F_\alpha(\rho)\le\sum_jq_jF_\alpha(P_j)\). Thus the entire canonical class has score at most the maximum pure-seed score, which by the triangle formula is \(\max\{\alpha\cdot v_0,\alpha\cdot v_H,\alpha\cdot v_V\}\).

Each vertex score is achieved by the Spin(9) orbit POVM

\[
M(dg)=16P_g\,dg,\qquad \Psi(X)=16\int\operatorname{Tr}(P_gX)P_g\,dg,
\]

where \(dg\) is normalized Haar measure and \(P_g=U_gP U_g^*\) for a seed at that vertex. Complex irreducibility gives \(\int P_g dg=I/16\). This is a full-domain rank-one canonical POVM; it is trace preserving, unital, completely positive, entanglement breaking, and Hilbert-Schmidt self-adjoint. Invariance of \(\alpha\) makes its score equal to the seed moment score. If the construction requires a finite POVM, finite-dimensional moment/Carathéodory reduction preserves the same averaged channel and score.

The support claim is for **canonical** EB channels. It does not cover an unrestricted EB channel with independent measurement effects and preparation states. Also, the exact support formula is a maximum over the three pure-seed vertices, not a statement that every canonical EB transfer vector belongs to their triangle.

For a general positive dual \(Q\), averaging over Spin(9) is valid for a covariant marginal: its pairing with that marginal stays fixed. The support function of a Spin(9)-invariant canonical-EB set is convex and invariant, so the averaged dual's support is no larger than the original support. This reduction is therefore valid only after checking the channel/objective is covariant as claimed. The three-party star to broadcaster Choi conversion and the subsequent separation are outside this audit; the pure-state result supplies only the EB support side.

## Exact cone/tie audit and sufficiency of ten PSD rays

Let \(L_0=\alpha\cdot v_0\), \(L_H=\alpha\cdot v_H\), and \(L_V=\alpha\cdot v_V\). The regions

\[
C_i=\{\alpha\in\mathbb R_+^4:L_i\ge L_j\text{ for each }j\}
\]

are full-dimensional (strict positive witnesses are \((1,1,1,2)\), \((1,1,2,1)\), and \((1,2,1,1)\)) and pointed (they lie in the nonnegative orthant). Exact active-facet/nullspace enumeration gives:

- \(C_0\): \(e_4,(0,0,1,1),(0,2,0,1),(0,7,5,6),e_1,(7,0,2,1)\).
- \(C_H\): \(e_3,(0,0,1,1),(0,1,1,0),(0,7,5,6),(3,0,1,0),(7,0,2,1)\).
- \(C_V\): \(e_2,(0,1,1,0),(0,2,0,1),(0,7,5,6),e_1,(3,0,1,0),(7,0,2,1)\).

Their union has ten distinct rays. The tie sets, in the order \((L_0,L_H,L_V)\), are: \(e_4\) only 0; \(e_3\) only H; \(e_2\) only V; \((0,0,1,1)\) tie 0/H; \((0,1,1,0)\) tie H/V; \((0,2,0,1)\) and \(e_1\) tie 0/V; \((0,7,5,6)\) and \((7,0,2,1)\) tie all three; \((3,0,1,0)\) tie H/V. Ties do not invalidate the reduction: on a shared cone face the winning linear forms agree.

For the star inequality, on each \(C_i\) the gap

\[
G_i(\alpha)=\big(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q(\alpha)+L_i(\alpha)\big)I-H(\alpha)
\]

is linear in \(\alpha\). A pointed polyhedral cone is the nonnegative span of its extreme rays, so if the full star gap is PSD at the union of ten rays (on every required block), then for any \(\alpha\in C_i\), \(G_i(\alpha)\) is a nonnegative sum of PSD ray gaps and hence PSD. Every nonnegative \(\alpha\) belongs to at least one winner cone. This verifies the finite-ray sufficiency argument exactly; it does **not** verify that the separate parent matrices are the correct blocks or that their ten PSD calculations pass.

Independent exact enumeration and all tie scores are recorded in [`support_cones_independent.py`](proof/exactchecks/support_cones_independent.py). It exhausts all triples of active normals over rational arithmetic, checks the rank-3 active condition, verifies strict interior witnesses, and compares with the listed ray sets.
