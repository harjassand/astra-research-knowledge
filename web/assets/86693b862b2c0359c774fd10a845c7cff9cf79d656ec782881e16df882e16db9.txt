MAIN LITERATURE FINDING

The exact physical interface is closer to charge-measurement universality than
the mode-resolved FLO simulation result alone suggests. Knill's theorem covers
FLO with individual particle measurements, which preserve the Gaussian/Slater
simulation. DiVincenzo–Terhal identify the two-mode charge-one projector
P1=P10+P01 as a sum of two Slater terms; their Corollary 1 includes the grouped
P_{0,1}/P2 measurement (no-double versus double), and says charge measurement
plus FLO admits nondeterministic universal computation. Beenakker et al. write
P1 explicitly and note that asking M sites to be singly occupied yields a sum
of 2^M determinant terms. This is not a proof of #P-hardness or absence of an
FPRAS. At k=n/2, c_k(F) is exactly the BCS squared norm after requiring one
particle at every site; thus the physical event matches the charge-measurement
literature. However, those universality results use interleaved/adaptive
measurements. No cited source compiles that model to one static rational
opposite-spin pair matrix with only a final onsite projector.

OTHER EXACT INTERFACES

Hamada–Iwata's linear matroid parity theorem optimizes support and returns a
maximum-cardinality witness; it does not count weighted bases. Matoya–Oki's
Pfaffian parity count requires det A[B]=c for every parity base, so it does
not sum arbitrary squared-Gram weights. Chen–Liu's 2026 common-base FPRAS
requires log-concave generating polynomials and bounded coefficient ranges;
the direct pair factor 1+xy fails log-concavity because det Hess log(1+xy)
is ((xy)^2-1)/(1+xy)^4<0 for xy<1. This blocks that direct encoding only.

Gaussian-to-MPS and Gutzwiller projection are established prior art. Liu et
al. explicitly state that exact local Fock tensors are possible in principle,
but their projected workflow truncates after Gutzwiller projection. This is
not an exact rational-bit complexity theorem. The c03_s03 peer snapshot claims
an exact rational MPS algorithm for ordered cut rank c=O(log n), with bond
dimension 2^c; it includes dense full-rank nonbipartite F. I reconstructed the
cross-factor Schmidt-rank argument and independently checked exact cut ranks
on four small rational matrices and the dense staircase formula through n=8.
This supports the subclass claim but does not verify its universal pivot
tensor bit-height proof or novelty. A different c03_s01 peer construction uses
support graph treewidth; dense staircase F shows that cut rank and graph
treewidth give complementary tractable subclasses.

COUNTEREXAMPLE BOUNDARY

The c03_s02 peer family is dense, has strictly positive off-diagonal entries,
and is well-conditioned, yet every stationary kernel that cannot directly
jump between two endpoint configurations has an exponentially small escape
flow. I reconstructed the key stationary-flow inequality. The same family
admits a two-endpoint approximation whenever its explicit omitted-mass bound
r=2^n 4^{-L} is small; therefore it is a local-mixing obstruction, not
approximation hardness. The c03_l02 peer's repeated-row fermionic bunching
obstruction correctly kills the direct bosonic KLM buncher transfer, but not
the distinct charge-measurement route above.
