C04_L03 REVISION — EXACT NO-GO FOR THE CANONICAL BROADCASTER-SLICE ANSATZ
Date: 2026-10-07. INITIAL is unchanged.

STATUS

The broadcaster-slice construction from INITIAL is strictly too narrow for
the universal c=2 target. For every d-dimensional optimal universal cloner,
the exact best constant within the canonical same-effect slice ansatz is

  c_slice(d) = 2 + (3d+4)/(2(d+1)^2) > 2.

At d=2 this is 23/9, and no nonzero POVM effect can satisfy the proposed
local condition 2 Lambda_y - R_y >=CP 0. This does NOT refute c=2 for the
channel: in dimension two the six-Pauli measure-and-prepare channel is an
unrelated EB comparator and attains c=2 exactly. In d=3, peer c04_l07
reports an exact 12-outcome EB comparator attaining c=2 for the cloner.
The revision identifies an exact route obstruction and the replacement
structure a general proof must permit.

EXACT SETUP AND ALL-D TRACE OBSTRUCTION

Let tau=Tr/d. The optimal symmetric universal cloner has marginal

  Phi_d(X)=lambda_d X +(1-lambda_d)tau(X)I,
  lambda_d=(d+2)/(2(d+1)).

The broadcaster is the receiver partial trace of
  (2/(d+1)) P_+ (rho tensor I)P_+, P_+=(I+F)/2.
Its Heisenberg map is

  Gamma_d(X tensor Y)=
    [Tr(Y)X+XY+YX+Tr(X)Y]/[2(d+1)],

so Gamma_d(X tensor I)=Phi_d(X), and it is symmetric. This is the standard
optimal 1-to-2 universal cloner (Werner, quant-ph/9804001); the displayed
identities specify the exact instance.

For a receiver POVM with rank-one effects Q_y=t_y P_y,
sum_y t_y P_y=I and sum_y t_y=d. Its broadcaster slices and effects are

  Lambda_y(X)=Gamma_d(X tensor Q_y),   E_y=Lambda_y(I)=Phi_d(Q_y).

Use the INITIAL canonical same-effect comparator
R_y(X)=tau(E_y X) E_y/tau(E_y), Psi=sum_y R_y. Since tau(E_y)=t_y/d and
Phi_d is selfadjoint, the exact aggregate identity is

  Psi = Phi_d P_Q Phi_d,
  P_Q(X)=d sum_y t_y tau(P_y X) P_y.

On the real Hilbert space of traceless Hermitian matrices with inner product
tau(AB), write P_y^0=P_y-I/d. The restriction of P_Q is the positive
operator

  S_Q=d sum_y t_y |P_y^0><P_y^0|.

Every pure projector has ||P_y^0||_2^2=tau((P_y^0)^2)=(d-1)/d^2.
Therefore, independent of the POVM,

  Tr_0(S_Q)=d sum_y t_y (d-1)/d^2=d-1.

On traceless operators Phi_d=lambda_d I, so Psi has positive transfer
operator lambda_d^2 S_Q and trace lambda_d^2(d-1). Its smallest eigenvalue
is at most the mean lambda_d^2/(d+1). A Loewner comparison

  I-Psi <= c (I-Phi_d)

forces, by testing a minimum-eigenvalue direction,

  c >= [1-lambda_d^2/(d+1)]/[1-lambda_d]
    = 2 + (3d+4)/(2(d+1)^2) =: c_slice(d).

This is attained by the Haar rank-one POVM (or a finite exact
Caratheodory compression of its first and second projector moments), for
which S_Q=I_0/(d+1). Then Psi has scalar traceless eigenvalue
lambda_d^2/(d+1), and c_slice(d) is the exact optimum among rank-one
POVMs. Rank-one refinement weakly increases the canonical comparator's
quadratic form, so no mixed-effect POVM can improve this optimum. The excess
above 2 is positive for every finite d and tends to zero with d. It is
largest at d=2, where c_slice(2)=23/9.

In d=2, P_y=(I+n_y dot sigma)/2 and S_Q=(1/2)sum_y t_y n_y n_y^T, a
positive 3x3 matrix of trace one. The six Pauli effects Q_{+i}=P_{+i}/3,
Q_{-i}=P_{-i}/3 give S_Q=I_3/3, so Psi=Delta_{4/27} and the worst ratio
is exactly 23/9.

The conclusion extends to POVMs with arbitrary positive effects. Spectrally
refine each effect Q into rank-one Q_a. Then E=Phi(Q)=sum_a Phi(Q_a) and
tau(E)=sum_a tau(Phi(Q_a)). For every Hermitian X, scalar weighted
Cauchy-Schwarz gives

  sum_a tau(Phi(Q_a)X)^2/tau(Phi(Q_a))
       >= tau(Phi(Q)X)^2/tau(Phi(Q)).

Thus refinement weakly increases the canonical comparator in PSD quadratic
order. If a coarse POVM comparator beat c_slice(d), its rank-one refinement
would also beat it, contradicting the trace/eigenvalue bound. No coarse
POVM improves the exact optimum.

LOCAL CP GATE: EXACT FAILURE FOR EVERY EFFECT

The stronger branchwise proposal also fails for every nonzero qubit effect,
not merely for the Pauli or projective examples. By unitary covariance take
Q=diag(q_0,q_1), q_i>=0, t=q_0+q_1>0, p_i=q_i/t. Put alpha=1/6. Then

  Lambda_Q(X)=alpha[tX+XQ+QX+Tr(X)Q],
  E_Q=alpha[tI+4Q],
  R_Q(X)=Tr(E_Q X) E_Q/t.

In the unnormalized Choi convention J(T)=sum_ij |i><j| tensor
T(|i><j|), the diagonal entries on |0,1> and |1,0> are

  J(Lambda_Q)_{01,01}=alpha q_1,
  J(Lambda_Q)_{10,10}=alpha q_0,
  J(R_Q)_{ij,ij}= e_i e_j/t,
  e_i=alpha(t+4q_i).

Therefore CP of 2 Lambda_Q-R_Q would require both

  12 p_1 >= (1+4p_0)(1+4p_1),
  12 p_0 >= (1+4p_0)(1+4p_1).

Using p_0=1-p_1, the first inequality is
16 p_1^2-4 p_1-5>=0, hence p_1 >= (1+sqrt(21))/8 > 1/2.
The second similarly forces p_0>(1/2), impossible since p_0+p_1=1.
Thus no POVM can make every residual branch CP. This is a proof from two
principal Choi diagonal entries; it does not infer CP from positivity or a
PPT test.

WHY THIS DOES NOT DISPROVE THE TARGET

For every d, the independent Haar measure-and-prepare channel

  Psi_hat(X)=d integral Tr(P_psi X) P_psi d mu(psi)
            =(X+Tr(X)I)/(d+1)

is EB and has traceless eigenvalue 1/(d+1). Thus for the cloner marginal

  I-Psi_hat = 2(I-Phi_d)

exactly. It is not the slice-derived Phi_d P_Q Phi_d. In d=2 this EB map is
the six-Pauli channel; for d=3, peer c04_l07/INITIAL.txt reports its exact
12-outcome MUB realization and an independent separability lower bound.
The cloner therefore separates “there exists an EB rounder” from the much
stronger demand that the rounder be reconstructed canonically from measuring
the receiver of the supplied broadcaster. The full c=2 statement survives
this test in every cloner dimension.

  separability lower bound attain c=2; this reinforces that successful EB
  comparators need not be the simple receiver-slice comparator.
* c04_l05/INITIAL.txt: the symmetric-separable two-copy frame formulation
  and the all-PSD-frame inequality are a natural route for a non-slice proof.
* c04_l02/INITIAL.txt: broadcaster Stinespring calculus controls average
  pairwise commutators by Dirichlet energies, but the size-free common-EB

The sharp next interface is a comparator/instrument chosen from the whole
channel (or the full extension Choi operator), not one forced to be the
canonical prepare-the-effect map associated with a measured receiver
outcome. A proof of the unrestricted target still needs an all-frame
separable-support inequality or an equivalent dimension-free lift. This
revision establishes no such lift.
