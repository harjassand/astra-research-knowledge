1. Weighted direction first exits
---------------------------------
The receiver momentum dyad w is fixed over the whole J and q_X stays in its
enlarged range. Thus the normalization inequality and the signed bootstrap
apply to each complete first-exit interval, including both endpoint ramps.
The removed ramp integral is controlled by endpoint-relative signed direction
increments after integration by parts; it does not assume small absolute
direction variation. Lower exit lengths follow from the same bootstrap.

Each complete interval contributes >=w delta/2 to the norm of its weighted
direction increment. Taking a norm interval by interval is legitimate: the
resulting positive integrals of bulk error bounds sum over disjoint time
interiors. The selection of representative indices is identical on every
interval, and sum psi_i<=1. Consequently occupation/direct estimates may be
used once on the union with upper interval length I=(w/P)^2. This relies on
the explicitly stated measurable-subset version of those estimates, rather
than applying a separate estimate with a new constant per first-exit cell.

The exception is sum |psi_i'|, whose total integral is 2N_c. Its fixed-time
primitive coefficient is CL^2, so the error CL^2 N_c is exactly accounted for.
Initial-cone terms have receiver-time weight sum psi_i<=1 and cost CI, not
N_c CI. The moving projector Pi=wP_X/|V_X| is based on this same characteristic
and fixed dyad; its derivative is controlled by K_X with the stated projected
coefficient CL^2/w, without differentiating an energy indicator.

The resulting absorption is numerically sound:
  w delta >= w^(2/5) >= P^(6/25),
so CL^2 N_c can be absorbed into N_c w delta/2 at sufficiently large P.
All fixed-species constants can enter the C and the lower threshold for P;
none requires a positive sign for a pair charge factor c_alpha e_beta.

2. Receiver-energy stability on the extended source cell
-------------------------------------------------------
When h<=lambda_0=I_m^0/[C_2(M^2+AL)], every retarded hit of source cell F has
s in F and t=s+r within the right extension of F by C_T h. A single hit
therefore anchors receiver energy in the w range on the entire extension,
even at receiver times not eligible in J. First-exit stopping applies the
bootstrap on an enlarged nearest energy dyad, initially justified before
exit, both forwards and backwards. For elapsed length O(lambda_0), its
terms are bounded by Cm/sqrt(C_2) and Cm/C_2; choosing C_2 large excludes exit.
The same argument anchors each hit source's energy near p throughout F.
Here p is 64 times the source dyad but only fixed comparison constants change.

The extended interval length is O(lambda_0), so enlarging C_2 makes it <=
(j'/P)^2 for the nearest receiver/source dyad j'. Since j' is a fixed-factor
multiple of p or w, p,w>=P^(7/10) ensures j'>=P^(3/5) for large P. Choosing
delta=c_2 phi fits the direction lemma's window with polynomial margin:
  delta / j'^(-3/5) >= c j^(11/100),
  delta / j'^(-1/10) <= C j^(-3/50).
Thus the energy extension does not invoke the direction lemma outside its
time/energy hypotheses or beyond the bootstrap endpoint tau.

3. Common refinement and retarded stretching
--------------------------------------------
Receiver partition boundaries b split the exceptional hits with b in [s,t].
For each such b, s lies in [b-C_T h,b], hence these hits have total source-time
duration O(h times receiver-cell-count). This is valid even if dt/ds=d/D is
large or small; no transfer of receiver-time lengths by dt/ds is used.

At all other hits, s and t lie within one receiver direction cell. The
receiver velocity throughout [s,t] then differs from a(t) by O(c_2 phi+w^-2).
Using |a(t)-n|<=C phi gives |Y(s)-X(s)|<=C h phi. The receiver/source energy
stability established above controls these speed deficits even when s or
intermediate receiver times lie outside the eligible set J.

Source and receiver direction partitions are refined in the same source-time
coordinate s on F. The refinement has at most the SUM of the numbers of
intervals plus one, not their product. In an interval with a good hit, the
actual small-sector relative velocity has size >=c phi; replacing retarded
a(t) by same-time a(s), and then moving throughout the refined interval,
costs only O(c_2 phi+m^-2). The margin m^-2/phi<=m^(-151/100) makes this error
small. A fixed scalar projection of (Y-X)'=u-a consequently remains >=c phi.
At good hits the projected position stays within an interval of width C h phi,
so the TOTAL source-time duration in that refined interval is <=C h, including
disconnected good-hit sets. This yields the claimed labelwise duration.

The mass of all source labels hitting F is bounded at one common source time
using q>=cp, phase-volume transport and positive total particle energy. In
the finite-species lift, use sum_beta m_beta q g_beta; charge-weighted label
mass is dominated by max |e_beta|/m_beta times this energy. Label-dependent
reference choices prove numerical duration bounds only; their measurability
is not needed for integration of the already measurable hit set.

4. Dependence and status
------------------------
For fixed finite species, all source estimates and receiver coefficient
estimates acquire only fixed |c_alpha e_beta| and |c_beta| factors. A common
bootstrap over all normalized species characteristics supplies the same
stability and direction estimates. The final power absorption of
C(M,A)L^7 by phi^(-4/25)>=P^(56/3125) can absorb these fixed factors too.

No extra charge-sign hypothesis emerged in these two proofs. Uniformity in
massless limits, infinite species, or unbounded charge/mass ratios was not
checked and does not follow from this calculation. Other central estimates,
cutoff-cancellation and continuation must retain their own independent audits.
