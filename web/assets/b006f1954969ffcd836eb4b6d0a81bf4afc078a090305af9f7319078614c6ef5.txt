Lead result
-----------

carrier with all Dynkin labels comparable to N, and the conjugation Casimir
normalized by c2(nu)=(nu,nu+2rho)/2, the internally reconstructed rank profile
is recorded in RESULT.txt. With p=|Phi+| and d=dim(V_lambda), the low-rank
branch is Lambda(Q) ~ (d/Q)^(1/p). For near-full rank d-k, it is
Lambda(d-k) ~ (k/d)^((p-1)/p) for p>=2 and ~1/log(e*d/k) for A1. The lower
bounds apply to arbitrary complex matrices, while the upper filters use
principal height and a Weyl-symmetric Cartan patch.
