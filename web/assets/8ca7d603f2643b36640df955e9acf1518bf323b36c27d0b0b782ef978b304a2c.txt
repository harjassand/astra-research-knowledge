Section4's sentence "This is a genuine GNS/KMS-symmetric Markov generator"
describes the ADDED replacement generator delta(R_Sigma,H−I). It must not
be read as asserting that the TOTAL regularized generator is GNS when the
original generator is nonmodular. In that section the total is a genuine
KMS-symmetric primitive Markov generator with faithful invariant Sigma and
generally remains non-GNS. Its strict constant4 violation is preserved by
linearity/continuity. Section5's total regularized generator IS GNS because
the original generator in that sufficiency argument is assumed GNS.

This distinction was identified by root's independent reading and accepted
by the originator. All equivalences and constructive counterexample statements
in the frozen theorem retain the same scope.
