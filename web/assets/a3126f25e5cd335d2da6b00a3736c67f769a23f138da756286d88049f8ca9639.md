# Cancer evolution control: A3A event-kernel audit

Date: 2026-10-08  
Scope: primary literature checked; conditional mathematical result and executable counterexample derived; no biological experiment run.

## Verdict

The strongest route found is a **conditional, preclinical test of whether therapy-induced APOBEC3A (A3A) resistance is carried by clustered/structural mutation events whose joint genotype and persister-offspring effects change escape risk**. This is narrower than “APOBEC causes resistance.” It makes explicit the object that current per-variant projections omit: the treatment-specific joint law of persister descendants, damage/state changes, mutation/SV bundles, and establishment under rechallenge.

This does **not** pass a novelty or intervention-readiness gate. A3A-dependent persister survival, DNA breaks, clustered mutations/SVs, and reduced long-term resistant-colony emergence after A3A loss are already reported. A 2025 study also reports APOBEC3-associated DTP survival and a change in resistance trajectory/transdifferentiation; another 2025 paper projects patient-specific delays from APOBEC suppression and explicitly recognizes an indirect persister-reservoir route to APOBEC-independent resistance. The new mathematics below is a standard branching-process comparison applied to a more faithful event interface, not a novelty claim. The actionable advance is a **discriminating assay and a hard decision gate**: do not rank A3A suppression from signature burden or per-site mutation rates alone; measure the full marked event kernel or return `UNKNOWN`.

## What the literature establishes—and leaves open

Isozaki et al. report that targeted therapy induces A3A in drug-tolerant persisters; A3A promotes double-strand breaks; A3A loss reduces APOBEC mutations, clusters and structural variants; and A3A loss delays resistance in cell and xenograft models. Their experiments also show A3A manipulation changes DTP colony formation/survival. Crucially, they note that common acquired EGFR/ALK variants such as EGFR T790M/C797S and ALK G1202R occur outside APOBEC-preferred motifs and may not be direct A3A products; induction/signature accumulation is not universal. See [Isozaki et al., Nature 620 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06303-1), especially Figs. 3–4 and Discussion.

Garcia et al. report that APOBEC3 expression promotes DTP survival and that constitutive APOBEC3B changes the gefitinib-resistance trajectory toward de novo T790M and squamous transdifferentiation in the DTP state. This preempts a broad claim that the missing mechanism is merely “APOBEC couples persisters to resistance.” See [Garcia et al., Cancer Research Communications 5 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1158/2767-9764.CRC-24-0442).

Nousias et al. infer gene- and patient-specific trinucleotide mutation rates from 21 NSCLC cases and project APOBEC-suppression delay across 42 resistance variants. Their first-hit calculation treats variant rates as the sufficient input. They explicitly assume a first resistance mutation is the fundamentally important one, discuss other single-point routes, and state that APOBEC can enlarge the persister pool and thereby indirectly increase non-APOBEC resistance. They do not model persister branching, joint multi-locus/structural events, or genotype-specific establishment. See [Nousias et al., Lung Cancer 202 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lungcan.2025.108487), Methods and Discussion.

The gap that survives these papers is narrow: the cited A3A work measures total or clustered mutation/SV burden and resistant outgrowth, but does not estimate the **joint event-level distribution** linking (i) persister survival/division/state transition, (ii) a linked multi-mutation or structural bundle, and (iii) genotype-specific establishment. In the 2023 study the observed association of A3A with clusters/SVs is not evidence that a particular cluster or rearrangement seeded an established resistant lineage. That link remains `UNKNOWN`.

## Interface: what must be supplied versus acquired

The decision endpoint is a fixed, prespecified TKI exposure followed by a fixed rechallenge; “escape” means at least one descendant lineage forms a prespecified expanding resistant clone under that rechallenge. The model is only a local preclinical branching approximation; it is not a tumor model and cannot support clinical advice.

For each treatment policy (z), persister state (i), and persister division, the assay must estimate a marked outcome

\[
  (K, J, D, S, R),
\]

where (K) is the vector of persister daughter/state-transition counts, (J) is the *joint* mutation/SV bundle, (D) is a damage readout, (S) is cell survival or death, and (R) is the rechallenge establishment outcome of any candidate resistant lineage. The daughter and event marks may be dependent. A3A-independent resistance marks and pre-existing resistant lineages must be included as competing routes, not set to zero.

**Supplied by design:** starting lineage/barcode distribution; treatment assignment; TKI exposure/AUC; the state labels and escape endpoint; the event categories; sequence and damage assay detection limits; and a prespecified held-out evaluation set.

**Must be measured, not fitted as free route constants:** persister division/death and state-exit distributions under each arm; A3A/A3B activity; DNA damage; joint mutation/SV event bundles with lineage linkage; pre-existing clone frequencies; and clone-specific rechallenge establishment. Any missing route-specific event probability or establishment chance stays `UNKNOWN`. A signature fraction is not a substitute for any of these quantities.

## Conditional theorem: marked-offspring dominance orders escape risk

Assume a finite set of persister states; independently reproducing persister lineages conditional on state; a time-homogeneous policy; and a complete marked offspring kernel for each state. For a mark (J), let (w(J)\in[0,1]) be the measured probability that none of the resistant descendants produced by this event establishes. Integrate over the joint event law, retaining dependence between event marks and persister offspring. The no-escape map for policy (z) is

\[
 F_i^z(s)=\mathbb{E}_z\!\left[w(J)\prod_j s_j^{K_j}\mid i\right],\qquad s\in[0,1]^m.
\]

Let (p^z=\lim_{n\to\infty}(F^z)^{\circ n}(\mathbf 1)). This is the vector of no-escape probabilities: each finite iterate is the probability of no establishing lineage through that many branching generations. If

\[
 F_i^{A}(s)\ge F_i^{B}(s)\quad\text{for every state }i\text{ and every }s\in[0,1]^m,
\]

then (p^{A}\ge p^{B}) coordinatewise. Therefore policy A has no greater escape probability for any initial persister composition. The proof is monotonicity: (F^A\ge F^B), both maps are coordinatewise nondecreasing, and iteration from the all-ones vector preserves the order at every finite generation; take limits.

This is a sufficient, not necessary, ranking certificate. It requires the *full* event law; ordering only mean mutation counts, APOBEC signature burdens, or average growth rates does not imply the pointwise PGF order. For time-varying policies the same argument applies to finite backward composition of the measured generation-specific maps. Density dependence, shared environment, spatial interaction, and treatment-induced correlations between lineages violate the independent branching interface and require a richer model.

The result is mathematically elementary and standard in spirit. The source-specific value is the bookkeeping constraint: A3A inhibition must be ranked on a kernel that retains the same DSB/cluster/SV event’s effects on persister survival and the resistant genotype it produces. No new biology or clinical ranking follows until the kernel is measured.

### Finite-data ranking procedure

For a prespecified finite state and offspring support, an assay can turn the theorem into a conservative decision procedure without supplying route-specific mutation or establishment constants:

1. From independent barcoded founder lineages in each policy/state, record the complete offspring vector (K) and whether the event generated any clone that establishes in the standardized rechallenge. Construct a simultaneous confidence region for the joint categorical outcome probabilities. Sequencing or challenge failures remain unresolved categories; they are not coded as no escape.
2. For each offspring vector (k), use linear programs over the confidence region to bound the coefficient (c^z_{i,k}=P_z(K=k,\text{no escape caused by this individual's event}\mid i)). For a proposed better policy A, take lower coefficient bounds (c^{A,-}_{i,k}) and upper bounds (c^{B,+}_{i,k}), producing the conservative polynomial
   \[
   L_i(s)=\sum_k(c^{A,-}_{i,k}-c^{B,+}_{i,k})\prod_j s_j^{k_j}.
   \]
   On the simultaneous coverage event, (L_i(s)\le F^A_i(s)-F^B_i(s)) for every (s\in[0,1]^m).
3. Convert (L_i) to a tensor Bernstein basis on the unit cube. Nonnegative Bernstein coefficients certify (L_i\ge0) everywhere. If coefficients have mixed signs, subdivide the cube and repeat; a directly evaluated negative witness rejects this dominance direction. If neither certificate appears before the prespecified computation/precision limit, return `UNKNOWN`.
4. Certify all persister states before invoking the theorem. Confidence coverage, assay attrition and detection floors remain part of the reported bound. An empirical point estimate or a grid of sampled (s)-values is not a robust certificate.

This is a sufficient algorithmic gate, not a new estimate of biology. Its value over a first-hit clock is that it has no unmeasured route-specific \(\mu\) or (q); the observed joint lineage/challenge categories supply the coefficients. Its cost can be high, and failure to see a rare category correctly results in wide bounds or `UNKNOWN`.

## Exact counterexample: marginal mutation rates do not identify compound-event control

Consider a hypothetical resistant genotype that is produced only when two linked alterations A and B arise in the **same burst**. Single-hit intermediates are neutral and do not alter persister reproduction in this restricted toy model; sequential accumulation is excluded. This is a toy model for a compound/structural event, not an assertion that the named alterations or probabilities occur in lung cancer. A burst occurs on 20% of persister divisions. Conditional on a burst, compare:

* clustered law: both A and B occur with probability 0.01, otherwise neither;
* independent law: A and B occur independently with probability 0.01 each.

Both laws have exactly the same 1% marginal rate at each locus and the same expected 0.02 mutations per burst. But the compound-event probability is 0.01 versus 0.0001. Let the resistant descendant establish with the same hypothetical (q=0.8) under either law. Let persisters divide at (b=0.1\,\mathrm{day}^{-1}) and die at (d=0.2\,\mathrm{day}^{-1}), with no other differences. The event probability per persister division is (u=0.002) versus (u=0.00002).

For one persister founder, no-escape probability (p) obeys

\[
 p=\frac{d+b\big((1-u)p^2+u(1-q)p\big)}{b+d}.
\]

Writing (y=1-p), the exact positive root is

\[
y=\frac{2buq}{A+\sqrt{A^2+4b(1-u)buq}},\qquad A=d-b+bu(1+q).
\]

For (M) independent persister founders, risk is (1-(1-y)^M). With (M=1000), the executable calculation gives:

| Event law | Per-division compound event (u) | One-founder escape risk | Escape risk, 1000 founders |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Clustered | 0.002 | 0.00159174 | 0.796687 |
| Independent | 0.00002 | 0.0000159992 | 0.0158720 |

The ratio is 50.19 despite identical per-locus marginals and expected total mutation count. This is a constructive counterexample to controlling a multi-hit/structural escape route from marginal variant rates alone. It does not establish that the real A3A event law has this direction or magnitude. The 2023 A3A study makes event clustering and SVs biologically plausible, but the pair-to-established-resistance link is still unmeasured.

The hidden data cost is large. Under the toy probabilities and fixed independent division exposure, seeing at least one **established** event with 95% probability requires 1,871 divisions under the clustered law and 187,232 under the independent law. These are merely detection floors, not adequately powered comparisons; the branching model’s random division count and sequencing losses raise the needed number. Under the toy (b,d), expected persister divisions per initial founder are one, so the independent-law floor corresponds to roughly 187,232 starting persister founders before attrition, plus lineage-resolved sequencing and functional rechallenge. Actual costs cannot be priced without the platform and event yield.

## Strongest equally informed control and comparative claim

The strongest baseline is not a per-site mutation-rate model. It is the full treatment-specific multitype, possibly age-structured branching model fit to the same lineage, division, damage, mutation/SV and rechallenge observations, including APOBEC-independent and pre-existing resistance. The proposed PGF certificate is a convenient exact dominance check on that model, not an improvement over it. There is no demonstrated performance or data-efficiency advantage over this baseline.

For intuition only, under a fixed exposure (D_z) and a rare-event Poisson approximation, let (h_A) be the APOBEC-linked *establishment-weighted* hazard per persister division and (h_0) the background hazard. If inhibition reduces (h_A) by fraction \(\eta\), leaves (h_0) unchanged, and changes cumulative division exposure by factor \(\kappa=D_{inh}/D_{ctl}\), the hazard ratio is \(\kappa(1-\eta\alpha)\) with \(\alpha=h_A/(h_A+h_0)\). Benefit in this approximation requires \(\kappa_U(1-\eta_L\alpha_L)<1\). For example, if exposure rose 1.5-fold, more than one third of the total establishment-weighted hazard must be removed even with perfect suppression. This is a standard sanity check, not the result and not a clinical threshold. The primary model uses the exact marked branching kernel and directly measured (q), not a free per-route mutation or establishment parameter.

## Discriminating preclinical test

Use at least one EGFR model (PC9) and one ALK model (H3122) to avoid treating a single driver context as universal. Start with matched barcoded parental lineages and a prespecified TKI dose. Randomize both before TKI and at the measured DTP stage (the cited source used a 14-day TKI DTP induction in its standard model) to distinguish DTP formation from post-DTP evolution. Compare A3A suppression, scrambled control, A3A wild-type rescue, and catalytic-dead E72A rescue. Keep TKI exposure matched and include multiple independent knockout/rescue clones. Track individual barcoded lineages by live imaging so division, death and state exit are observed rather than inferred from endpoint cell counts. Measure A3A/A3B expression/activity, damage (e.g. γH2AX plus a DSB assay), and linked SNV clusters/SVs using sufficiently deep clone- or single-cell sequencing. Challenge every recoverable candidate genotype with the same TKI to estimate clone-specific establishment. Pre-existing resistant barcodes and A3A-independent resistance classes (copy-number/amplification and epigenetic state change) are explicit competing outcomes.

The preregistered prediction is conditional: **if** A3A suppression changes the linked event kernel so the measured no-escape PGF dominates the control kernel, the model predicts lower escape for any starting persister mixture. The distinctive observation is a change in joint resistant-bundle/establishment probability after recording the same lineages’ DTP divisions, deaths, state exits and damage—not merely fewer endpoint APOBEC calls. If the effect vanishes after complete lineage/exposure accounting, the direct mutation-burst control hypothesis fails; if only the persister reservoir shrinks, the effect is reservoir sensitization already covered by prior work; if APOBEC-independent clones escape, the whole-population ranking may remain unresolved.

This experiment is expensive and rare-event limited. Required outputs include TKI AUC; live-cell lineage movies; at least two damage measurements; barcode-aware WGS or single-cell/long-read sequencing; copy-number/SV calling; per-clone rechallenge wells; matched rescue and pre-existing-clone controls; and enough independent lineages to bound the rarest compound event. The 187,232-division detection floor above is an optimistic lower bound for the toy independent route. An assay that cannot reach informative bounds must report `UNKNOWN`, not impute route-specific \(\mu\) or establishment (q).

## Failure modes and scope limits

1. A3A deletion changes DTP formation/survival; end-point counts conflate founder number, division exposure, death, state exit and mutation. Division-normalization alone can also select a different survivor population; the lineage-level joint law is required.
2. A3A-associated DSBs, clusters and SVs are documented, but source papers do not show that a particular marked event generated an established resistant clone. Sequence signature is source attribution, not causal lineage linkage.
3. APOBEC3B and other mutational processes can contribute; common EGFR/ALK resistance mutations are often outside APOBEC-favored sequence contexts. The 2025 APOBEC3B study demonstrates a DTP/transdifferentiation path; non-genetic resistance, amplification and pre-existing clones remain competing routes.
4. Pointwise PGF ordering is sufficient but conservative. Failure to certify order does not mean equal risk. The independent branching assumption excludes ecological competition, spatial sanctuary and cross-lineage interactions.
5. This work is not a clinical recommendation, inhibitor proposal, validated patient model or evidence of benefit in humans. The cited A3A paper noted that potent selective catalytic inhibitors were not then established; intervention feasibility and status need fresh primary-source verification before any future translational claim.

## Negative routes retained

* **Generic mutation supply × establishment or division-normalized flux:** standard branching/thinning bookkeeping; reject as novelty.
* **APOBEC suppression delay from per-variant rates / a molecular-clock first-hit projection:** directly preempted by Nousias et al. 2025; do not repeat as a contribution.
* **A3A promotes DTP survival, DSBs, mutation clusters/SVs, and resistance:** directly reported by Isozaki et al. 2023. Coupling “mutation plus persister survival” alone is not new.
* **APOBEC3 broadly drives persisters and a resistance/transdifferentiation path:** directly advanced by Garcia et al. 2025 (APOBEC3B/p63 in a gefitinib model).
* **Generic adaptive therapy, schedule control, clone competition or ecological extinction:** preempted by the existing `work/agents/cancer_evolution/` and `work/agents/c4_biological_information/` reports and the parent’s prior art warning; no extension claimed.
* **Scalar evolvability as a cancer control policy:** current 2026 preprint uses scalar trait/evolvability ODEs; not a mechanistic advance over source-specific event data.

## Restart gates

Restart the mechanism/control search only if all of these are feasible:

1. An experiment can link A3A-associated cluster/SV events, persister state/offspring, and rechallenge establishment in the *same lineage*; otherwise the core kernel is not identified.
2. Per-route mutation/event and establishment probabilities are measured or bounded from that experiment; no free \(\mu\), (q), or background route rates are inserted.
3. Survival, division, DNA damage, A3B/other APOBEC activity, pre-existing resistance and APOBEC-independent routes are randomized/recorded controls.
4. The event-kernel policy ranking is compared with the strongest equally informed full branching baseline on held-out replicate pools. An actual advantage must appear in a decision, prediction or required-data reduction; otherwise retain only as a model specification.
5. Primary novelty checking finds no existing paper with the same lineage-resolved A3A joint-kernel/establishment assay or equivalent robust policy certificate. Until then, the priority status is `UNKNOWN`.
