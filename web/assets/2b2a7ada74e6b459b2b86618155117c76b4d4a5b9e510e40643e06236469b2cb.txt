STRONGEST RESULT: CONNECTED, FIXED-METRIC TOPOLOGICAL-POWER COUNTEREXAMPLE

The replacement h_mu(phi_1) -> h_top(phi_1) in W >= 4 nu V h_mu(phi_1)^2 fails even on one connected closed 3-manifold with a fixed flat metric and a nowhere-zero divergence-free field. Starting from a smooth area-preserving disk map f that is identity near the boundary and has positive metric entropy, take a compactly supported area-preserving isotopy f_z and its Eulerian velocity V_z. Compress only the transverse coordinates by epsilon and set

  X_epsilon(x,y,z)=partial_z + epsilon V_z(x/epsilon,y/epsilon).

For every epsilon>0 this is smooth, nowhere zero, and divergence free on the same unit flat T^3. On the invariant z=0 section its time-one map is spatially conjugate to f, so h_top(phi_1^epsilon) >= h_0>0 independent of epsilon. Direct differentiation and the transverse change of variables give

  integral |Def X_epsilon|^2 dVol = epsilon^2 A + epsilon^4 B,
  W_epsilon = 2 nu (epsilon^2 A + epsilon^4 B) -> 0,

for fixed finite A,B>=0 with A>0. Define F_epsilon as the smooth residual in the stated strain-viscosity equation with p=0. Thus W_epsilon/(4 nu V h_top^2) ->0. This refutes not only the coefficient four but any uniform positive total-volume power floor based on topological entropy alone in this interface. The prescribed-volume entropy is compatible with the valid theorem: the entropy-bearing transverse disk has area O(epsilon^2), and direct section disintegration gives h_mu(phi_1^epsilon)=epsilon^2 (Area(D)/Area(T^2)) h_D.

The imported disk map is from Berger–Turaev, Theorem A and §3.4, which establish a positive-area stochastic island and an extension equal to identity near the disk boundary: https://arxiv.org/html/1704.02473 . The compactly supported area-preserving isotopy is standard for disk maps; Bechara Senior §2 states contractibility/Hamiltonian isotopy: https://d-nb.info/124761025X/34 . The construction does not claim a finite-bit map or actuation implementation. In fact the p=0 force residual has peak scale Theta(nu/epsilon), since its viscous term differentiates strain once more; integrated signed work omits this actuator/force-envelope cost.
