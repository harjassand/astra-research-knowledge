RESULT IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The fixed-bank conditioning obstruction extends to randomized adaptive bounded
observable acquisition, including queries that jointly inspect several reset
trajectories. For a noisy-parity family with at most 4L+2 ordinary homogeneous
HMM states and fixed-time predictive rank at most two, any robust statistical
query learner obtaining TV error less than lambda/4 with probability 1-delta
must satisfy an exact query/tolerance/batch tradeoff. Polynomial query count
and inverse tolerance require batch size linear in L; its exponential
weighted sum is not itself a physical sample or computation cost. This is a direct scoped
application of established SQ orthogonality/tensorization, rather than a new
hardness mechanism. Raw noiseless trajectories escape the obstruction: Gaussian
elimination acquires the hidden parity and then constructs a bounded feature
Hankel matrix diag(1,1) from O(L+log(1/delta)) samples. Thus neither adaptive
small-batch moment selection alone nor importing a conditional-sampling oracle solves the
general acquisition gap. There is a concrete, certified escape in a supplied
noiseless linear-parity subclass; the general noisy/unknown-feature case remains
unresolved.

1. INTERFACE AND TWO OPPORTUNITIES

Target: acquire a finite predictive representation from observed reset
trajectories, accounting for trajectory count/length, finite precision,
conditioning, arithmetic, stored state, and feature description. A useful
mathematical outcome can also be a sharp obstruction to a proposed interface.

Opportunity A, selected for attack: Can adaptive bounded moment/feature queries
repair the lack of a uniform singular-value margin in the inherited universal
stochastic-observable bank? Answer: not uniformly with polynomial robust SQ
work and small batches, even for a family whose finite-horizon cut rank is two. The batch tradeoff
theorem below closes the simple adaptive escape and precisely limits its scope.

Opportunity B: Can the COLT 2023 conditional-sample learner be implemented from
ordinary reset trajectories without charging rare prefix events? Answer: no
uniform black-box bridge exists. A length-L rare-prefix family forces order
2^L trajectories for even one constant-accuracy conditional label. This does
not preclude a model-specific inference algorithm that exploits other data.

L01 and N68 routing: L01's exact full matrix-output learner and N68's degree-free
exact 2s-dimensional sparse decoder are not trajectory-sampling algorithms.
Their cards explicitly retain output-entry, coefficient-height, response-bit,
and numerical-conditioning debt. Their exact algebra is not a premise of the
lower bounds below. No source theorem from the release is promoted into a
passive-sample oracle. The inherited universal bank provides population rank
completeness and a conditional guarantee under a supplied singular-value margin;
that earlier mathematical claim was not revalidated in this phase.

2. THE SMALL-STATE LOW-RANK FAMILY

Let L>=1, s in {0,1}^L, and 0<lambda<=1. Let U be uniform on
Z={0,1}^L x {0,1}. With chi_s(x)=(-1)^(s dot x mod 2), define

 P_s(x,y) = 2^(-L-1) [1 + lambda (-1)^y chi_s(x)].                 (1)

Equivalently X is uniform and Y equals s dot X with probability
(1+lambda)/2. Lambda=1 is noiseless parity. For distinct s,t,

 TV(P_s,P_t)=lambda/2,     TV(P_s,U)=lambda/2.                    (2)

Proof of (2): chi_s and chi_t disagree on half the inputs. For each such x
the two conditional Bernoulli laws have TV lambda. Averaging gives lambda/2.

Ordinary homogeneous HMM realization, not a supplied matrix-query oracle:
Use states (i,a,z), i=1,...,L and a,z in {0,1}, together with label states
q_0,q_1. Emission in (i,a,z) is z and emission in q_y is y. Initial mass is
1/2 on (1,0,0) and (1,0,1). On leaving (i,a,z), set a'=a XOR s_i z.
For i<L, choose (i+1,a',z') with probability 1/2 for each z'. For i=L,
choose q_(a') with probability (1+lambda)/2 and q_(1-a') with the remaining
probability. Both label states restart at (1,0,z') fairly. Each block of L+1
emissions has exactly law (1), and blocks are independent. The state count is
4L+2; the alphabet is binary. For rational lambda the description is finite
and uses O(L log L + L * bitlength(lambda)) bits in an explicit sparse table.

At each fixed time cut t<=L, the conditional future depends on its prefix only
through the partial parity a=s_1:t dot x_1:t. Therefore each conditional matrix
has at most two distinct columns and rank at most two. This is the finite-
horizon cut rank of Kakade et al. Definition 2. It must not be confused with
the minimum dimension of a single time-homogeneous realization of the entire
infinite clocked process. The latter includes the clock and can scale with L.

3. ADAPTIVE AND BATCHED ROBUST-OBSERVABLE THEOREM

Oracle contract: A query consists of an integer b>=1, an arbitrary function
phi:Z^b -> [-1,1], and a tolerance tau>0. The answer may be any v satisfying
|v-E_(P_s^b) phi|<=tau. The learner must succeed for every allowed answer
strategy. It may be randomized, adaptive, use nonlinear query functions, and
choose b and tau adaptively. It outputs any probability law Q on Z.

Let the NULL RUN answer every query by E_(U^b)phi. Assume this run terminates
(otherwise the following sum can be infinite). Put

 B_null = sum over null-run queries j of
          ((1+lambda^2)^(b_j)-1) / tau_j^2.                    (3)

THEOREM. If for every s and every valid oracle strategy the learner outputs
TV(Q,P_s)<lambda/4 with probability at least 1-delta, then

 E_coins B_null >= (1-delta) 2^L - 1.                          (4)

No computational complexity assumption is used. The condition that the
null run terminates is automatic for an algorithm with a global finite
query budget; it is also natural for an alleged uniform rank<=2 learner,
since U itself has rank one.

PROOF. Write l_s=dP_s/dU=1+lambda(-1)^y chi_s(x). Orthogonality gives
E_U l_s l_t=1 when s!=t and 1+lambda^2 when s=t. For a b-trajectory query,
let g_s=dP_s^b/dU^b -1. Independence then gives

 <g_s,g_t>_(U^b) = 0 for s!=t,
 ||g_s||_2^2 = v_b=(1+lambda^2)^b-1.                           (5)

This is the exact tensorization identity in Steinhardt-Valiant-Wager,
Lemma 15, specialized to the orthogonal parity family. Since g_s/sqrt(v_b)
is an orthonormal system, Bessel's inequality yields

 sum_s |E_(P_s^b)phi-E_(U^b)phi|^2
       <= v_b E_(U^b)phi^2 <= v_b.                            (6)

Consequently at most v_b/tau^2 secrets make a reference answer invalid.
Freeze the learner's coins and follow the null transcript. The union of
secrets excluded by its queries has cardinality at most B_null. Define a
VALID oracle O_s that returns the reference answer whenever it is within
tau of the true mean, and otherwise returns the true mean. For any secret
outside that union, the entire run with O_s equals the null run, including
its output Q. By (2) and the triangle inequality, one Q can lie at distance
strictly less than lambda/4 from at most one P_s. Hence for uniform s,
success against these fixed valid O_s is at most (B_null+1)/2^L. Average
over coins. Uniform success at least 1-delta implies (4). QED.

Corollary: with unary queries b=1, common tolerance tau, and at most q queries,
q >= [(1-delta)2^L-1] tau^2/lambda^2. With b_j<=b and inverse tolerances,
query count bounded polynomially in L, condition (4) forces
b >= L ln 2 / ln(1+lambda^2) - O(log L) for fixed lambda. This is a necessary
threshold only; no noisy-parity algorithm is obtained at that threshold.

This includes controller-generated bounded statistics, since averaging any
independent controller randomness yields a function in [-1,1]. Multiple
returned moment coordinates count as multiple scalar queries. It also
includes arbitrary adaptive postprocessing of the returned means.

WHAT THE SUM MEANS. Inverse-square tolerance is the standard worst-case
Hoeffding estimation charge. Simulating q fixed queries independently uses
O(sum_j b_j tau_j^(-2) log(q/delta')) raw trajectories. The theorem obstructs
a polynomial robust SQ contract for unary/small-batch queries and its generic
simulation. The factor (1+lambda^2)^b is an orthogonality amplification factor,
not a charged running time or trajectory count: linear b can make the bound
compatible with polynomial actual resources, as the upper bound below shows.
It is NOT an
unconditional exponential raw-sample lower bound. Empirical answers need not
be adversarial, sample reuse can preserve information absent from the SQ
contract, and variance-aware services are distinct interfaces. Exact response
oracles have tau=0 and are outside the finite-work claim.

4. A DIRECT FEATURE-DISCOVERY VERSION

Suppose a robust SQ feature constructor instead outputs M bounded unary
tests psi_i, promising with probability 1-delta that at least one has
|E_(P_s)psi_i-E_U psi_i|>=alpha. Along a frozen null run, (6) with b=1 shows
that its output tests can cover at most M lambda^2/alpha^2 secrets. Therefore

 E B_null + M lambda^2/alpha^2 >= (1-delta)2^L.                 (7)

The proof repeats the preceding null-transcript argument; no identification
or TV sampler is required. The number and conditioning of output features
must therefore also be charged. A feature bank selected adaptively through
only polynomial robust unary/small-batch means cannot uniformly produce a polynomial-size
bank with inverse-polynomial observable advantage on this family. If a
proposed Hankel certificate implies such an advantage for one of its bounded
entries, (7) applies after charging the number of entries; it does not apply
to an unspecified normalization or unbounded feature without a bridge.

5. RAW NOISELESS ACQUISITION AND ITS CONDITIONING CERTIFICATE

Under the supplied subclass promise lambda=1, take
N=L+ceil(log_2(1/delta)) independent trajectories. Their X rows are uniform
and their labels satisfy Xs=Y over GF(2). For each nonzero v, probability
that all rows satisfy Xv=0 is 2^(-N). A union bound gives

 Pr(rank(X)<L) <= (2^L-1)2^(-N) <= delta.                      (8)

Gaussian elimination either produces the unique secret s (with a full-rank
certificate) or returns UNKNOWN. A straightforward implementation uses
O(NL^2) bit operations and O(NL) stored bits; bit packing can improve it.
All observed bits, elimination, and the supplied parity subclass are charged.
The promise is essential: consistent finite equations do not certify that
an arbitrary unknown source is a parity HMM.

For any cut t where s_1:t is nonzero, acquire the two bounded feature pairs

 f=(1, chi_(s_1:t)(X_1:t)),
 g=(1, (-1)^Y chi_(s_(t+1:L))(X_(t+1:L))).

Then H=E[f g^T]=diag(1,lambda). Its nonzero singular values are exactly
1 and lambda. At lambda=1 this gives a constant population margin after
actual raw-data feature acquisition. At cuts before the first selected
coordinate the predictive rank is one and no artificial rank-two claim is
needed. The recovered bit mask plus a clock and one parity bit is an exact
predictive recurrence on the supplied subclass. The representation uses
O(L) mask bits and O(log L) clock bits; prediction after acquisition uses
O(1) XOR work per symbol.

Independent new trajectories can estimate the entries/correlation to a
desired finite tolerance at the usual charged concentration cost. The full-
rank design certificate plus the noiseless-subclass premise already proves
exact recovery; a held-out pass alone would not prove the premise. This
construction is ordinary GF(2) learning, and no advantage over the equally
informed direct algebraic baseline is claimed. For lambda<1, the equations
are noisy, so this escape does not extend unchanged.

MATCHING LARGE-BATCH ROBUST-MOMENT ESCAPE, lambda=1. This also identifies an
important boundary of the negative theorem. Set b=L+2. For each coordinate
i, define one b-trajectory statistic phi_i by solving Xs=Y: if the design
has full rank and the equations are consistent, return (-1)^(s_i); otherwise
return zero. The statistic is a legal bounded function even off the target
family. Under P_s, the equations are always consistent, and (8) bounds the
failure probability by 1/4. Thus

 E_(P_s^b) phi_i = c (-1)^(s_i),  c>=3/4.

Each answer with tolerance tau=1/4 has the correct sign. L such queries
recover s exactly, then the feature matrix above has singular margin one.
The batch statistic takes O(bL^2) bit operations and O(bL) memory to compute.
Its generic independent-batch Monte Carlo simulation needs only
O(L*b*log(L/delta)) raw trajectories, because the tolerance is constant.
A raw one-batch implementation is the cheaper baseline (8). If queries are
implemented as observables of the homogeneous HMM, one statistic examines
b consecutive parity blocks: its observation-window length is b(L+1)=O(L^2).

For constant tolerance and polynomial query count, theorem (4) at lambda=1
requires b>=L-O(log L), so this upper bound matches that batch threshold to
lower-order terms. Large jointly processed batches can therefore acquire
a well-conditioned feature here with polynomial actual resources. The
information is extracted by Gaussian elimination inside the query statistic;
it is not supplied by a generic moment dictionary or free exact matrix
oracle. This established k-wise/GF(2) escape is not a new general HMM result.

6. RARE-PREFIX DEBT FOR A CONDITIONAL-SAMPLE BRIDGE

Let h be one specified length-L binary prefix, p=2^(-L). Define two reset
sources R_0,R_1 with X uniform; if X=h, their final label is respectively
0 or 1; otherwise the label is fair in both. A clock, a one-bit match flag,
and the emitted bit give ordinary O(L)-state homogeneous HMMs by the same
construction as above. Their joint TV is p. Couple each pair of trajectories
identically until X=h. Then

 TV(R_0^N,R_1^N) <= 1-(1-p)^N <= Np.                         (9)

Any data-only conditional-label sampler is a randomized map A of those N
trajectories. Data processing bounds its output TV by Np. The true laws at
h are the point masses delta_0 and delta_1. If A is within TV epsilon of
both corresponding conditional laws, triangle inequality forces

 N >= (1-2epsilon)/p.                                        (10)

Thus a uniform emulator of even one such conditional sample incurs
Omega(1/P(h)) raw trajectories; rejection achieves that order. Exact
conditional sampling at arbitrary prefixes cannot be silently supplied by
reset access. A structured learner can avoid querying h by inferring the
model from other evidence in a special subclass; theorem (10) rules out
only the universal passive-to-conditional oracle bridge, not those learners.

8. CHECKS, FAILURES, AND NEXT DECISIVE GATE

Executed: python3 scripts/check_acquisition.py. Results/exact_checks.json:
PASS; 1,360 exact HMM word-probability identities, 310 exact TV identities,
376 direct batch-Gram entries, 144 acquired-feature matrices, and 10 exact

returned UNKNOWN on a rank-deficient design. No wrong secret was returned.
These fixtures check the implementation and algebra; they are not the
universal proof or external validation.

Failed routes preserved:
* Replacing exact L01/N68 responses by estimated trajectory means did not
  yield a polynomial general learner; the interface remains unmatched.
* Adaptive robust bounded unary/small-batch moments do not repair the parity family.
* Small batches with inverse-polynomial precision do not evade that gate;
  large batches do, in the noiseless subclass, at polynomial physical cost.
* Conditional-sample theorem imported without prefix-probability cost fails.
* Noiseless Gaussian elimination does not extend to noisy arbitrary HMMs.

Unresolved: acquire a predictive basis from raw trajectories for an explicitly
specified beyond-parity class, certify its observable conditioning without
silently assuming the basis, and compare its end-to-end cost with a direct
same-data learner. The next productive gate is an acquisition operation that
uses joint retained sample structure, or an experimentally legal intervention
with a charged conditional-access cost. A new universal moment dictionary
or an exact algebraic response oracle alone does not address this gate.
