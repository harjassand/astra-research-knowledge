GROWING-DIMENSION OPERATIONAL MEMORY: EXACT LAWS AND NONUNIFORM FISHER/LAPLACE SCALES
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally proved for the stated exact interfaces; primary-source reconstruction completed for pure-state compression. No general finite-error law for growing-dimensional mixed-state orbits is claimed.

MAIN RESULT

Two exact archive laws expose different failures of a Fisher-only or fixed-dimension approximation.

1. For the blind pure-state orbit input |psi><psi|^tensor n in dimension d, with a fixed decoder required to return the entire n-qudit state exactly for every unknown psi, the minimum total hybrid dimension is

       D_0(n,d) = binom(n+d-1,d-1),
       bits = log2 binom(n+d-1,d-1).

   Here a hybrid memory is A=direct_sum_j M_(d_j), every input-dependent branch is counted, and D=sum_j d_j. The lower bound allows arbitrarily many dephased classical labels. It is stronger than the coherent-only optimality statement in the cited compression paper; the hybrid converse below is reconstructed in this note.

2. For one observation from a d-coordinate product model, the minimum exact archive dimension is 2^d for a product Bernoulli family whose Fisher matrix is I_d at every parameter. For the product Gaussian location family with the same Fisher matrix I_d, no finite-dimensional hybrid archive exactly reproduces the one-sample law for every parameter. These statements use the same blind interface and charge the same total hybrid dimension. Thus even the full Fisher matrix does not determine exact operational archive memory as d grows.

THEOREM A: EXACT PURE-ORBIT ARCHIVE, ALL n,d

Interface. The encoder receives n identical copies of an unknown pure d-level state, the pair of channels E,R is fixed and independent of psi, and the decoded state is required to equal the input density operator for every psi. The memory is the finite algebra A=direct_sum_j M_(d_j), with D=sum_j d_j. No side information, retained reference, or free input-dependent register is available. The output is a fresh n-qudit density operator. An arbitrary finite-dimensional quantum block is permitted, and all direct-sum labels are charged through D.

Proof. The family of vectors psi^tensor n spans Sym^n(C^d). More strongly, its rank-one projectors span B(Sym^n(C^d)): in the normalized occupation basis, an operator A has expectation on psi^tensor n equal to a homogeneous polynomial of bidegree (n,n) in the amplitudes and their conjugates. If this polynomial vanishes for every psi, all coefficients vanish. Hence exact reconstruction makes Phi=R E the identity channel on B(Sym^n(C^d)). Put L=dim Sym^n(C^d)=binom(n+d-1,d-1).

For memory block j, write Phi_j=R_j E_j, so Phi=sum_j Phi_j and each Phi_j is completely positive and factors through a Hilbert space of dimension d_j. The Choi operator of Phi is rank one. Since each Choi operator J(Phi_j) is positive and their sum is J(id), every nonzero J(Phi_j) is a positive multiple of J(id). Thus some Kraus operator of Phi_j is a nonzero scalar multiple of I_L. It factors through dimension d_j, so its rank is at most d_j; therefore d_j>=L. At least one block is nonzero, and D>=L. For the upper bound, identify Sym^n(C^d) isometrically with an L-dimensional memory and invert that isometry at the decoder. Extend the encoder to a channel on the full tensor-product space. This proves D_0=L.

The symmetric-subspace isometry and inverse are exact for the source family. For mixed states near a pure face, choose any channel extension outside Sym^n; the extension remains exact on the pure subfamily, which is all the continuity argument below needs.

GROWING-d ASYMPTOTICS: WHERE THE FIXED-d EXPANSION FAILS

Write m=d-1. The exact identity is

       ln D_0 = m ln n - ln(m!) + sum_(j=1)^m ln(1+j/n).

The usual fixed-d polynomial/Laplace term is n^m/m!. The exact ratio to that term is

       D_0 / (n^m/m!) = product_(j=1)^m (1+j/n).

When m=o(n), the logarithm of this ratio is m(m+1)/(2n)+O(m^3/n^2). Consequently the fixed-d approximation is a relative approximation only when m^2/n -> 0. At m/sqrt(n)->c>0, its ratio tends to exp(c^2/2), already a nonvanishing error. For sqrt(n)<<m<<n the ratio diverges. If m/n->alpha>0, then

       (1/n) ln[D_0/(n^m/m!)] -> (1+alpha)ln(1+alpha)-alpha > 0.

For m=n (so d=n+1),

       ln D_0 = 2n ln 2 - (1/2)ln(pi n) + o(1),
       ln(n^m/m!) = n + o(n),

and the fixed-d approximation undercounts D_0 by a factor exp((2ln2-1)n+o(n)). In bits the discrepancy is (2-1/log_e 2)n+o(n), approximately 0.5573 n bits. With d=n instead, the same leading exponents hold because m=n-1.

This is a precise nonuniformity result for the fixed-m Fisher/Laplace polynomial expansion, not a claim that every Fisher method fails. The full dimension-dependent Stirling form is uniform when n,m both diverge:

       ln D_0 = n[(1+a)ln(1+a)-a ln a]
                - (1/2)ln(2 pi n a/(1+a)) + O(1/n+1/m),
       a=m/n.

Thus the correct uniform entropy keeps the full ratio m/n (or simply the exact binomial coefficient); the fixed-d power law does not. The arithmetic sweep is in evidence/growth_table.py and growth_table.json.

THEOREM B: FULL-RANK FINITE-ERROR COUNTEREXAMPLE TO A DIMENSION-UNIFORM COLLISION LAW

For n>=2,d>=2, supply the spectrum

       p=(1-eta, eta/(d-1),...,eta/(d-1)),   eta=epsilon/(16n), 0<epsilon<=1,

and let the unknown eigenbasis be U. The decoder must reconstruct rho_U^tensor n, rho_U=U diag(p) U*, with worst-case half-trace error at most epsilon. E,R are fixed and blind to U. The same total-D accounting as Theorem A applies. No spectrum acquisition is required by this supplied-spectrum experiment; the symmetric-subspace construction in fact does not use p.

Let sigma_U=|Ue_1><Ue_1|^tensor n. Orthogonality of the product eigenbasis gives T(rho_U^tensor n,sigma_U)=1-(1-eta)^n<=n eta. Extend the exact pure-subspace compressor to a CPTP channel on the whole input space. Since it fixes sigma_U, contractivity and the triangle inequality give

       T(Phi(rho_U^tensor n),rho_U^tensor n) <= 2 n eta <= epsilon/8.

Thus D_epsilon(n,d,p)<=binom(n+d-1,d-1), uniformly in U, including when p is full rank.

For B_n(p)=product_(i<j)(1+n(p_i-p_j)^2), only the d-1 gaps between the large eigenvalue and the equal tail are nonzero. If d=n, then

       B_n(p)=(1+n Delta_n^2)^(n-1),  Delta_n=1-epsilon/[16(n-1)],
       log2 B_n(p)=(n-1)log2(n)+O(1),
       log2 D_epsilon <= log2 binom(2n-1,n)=2n-O(log n).

Therefore D_epsilon/B_n -> 0. No positive lower comparison c B_n<=D_epsilon can hold with c independent of d,n,p,epsilon. This does not refute the fixed-d candidate whose constants depend on d. The counterexample identifies the missing uniform parameter: the n-copy state is within O(epsilon) of a rank-one orbit, while the collision product counts all d-1 top-to-tail directions at full strength.

THEOREM C: SAME FISHER MATRIX, DIFFERENT EXACT MEMORY LAW, FOR GROWING d

Interface. A single classical observation X is drawn from P_theta, theta lies in the open box (pi/3,2pi/3)^d, the encoder sees X but not theta, and the fixed decoder outputs a fresh Y. It must satisfy R E(P_theta)=P_theta for every theta. The memory is A=direct_sum_j M_(d_j), D=sum_j d_j; the classical label count and every quantum block are charged. The output loss is total variation, and no theta, query, or side information is supplied at encoding or decoding.

Bernoulli model: P_theta is the law on {0,1}^d with independent coordinates and success probabilities p_i=sin^2(theta_i/2). Each scalar Fisher information is (p_i')^2/[p_i(1-p_i)]=1, so F(theta)=I_d. The exact archive dimension is D_0=2^d, or d bits.

For the upper bound, store the observed vector x in one of 2^d one-dimensional blocks and decode the same vector. For the lower bound, the product Bernoulli laws span the full 2^d-dimensional vector space of signed measures on {0,1}^d: their coordinates are tensor products of the linearly independent degree-one functions p_i and 1-p_i, and the parameter box contains an open set. Exactness and linearity therefore imply that the composite archive channel is the identity on every point mass delta_x. The encoded states E(delta_x) must be perfectly distinguishable because the decoder recovers the orthogonal point masses exactly; hence their supports are mutually orthogonal. A total memory Hilbert dimension D must be at least 2^d.

Gaussian model: P_theta=N(theta,I_d), which also has Fisher matrix I_d everywhere. No finite D gives exact archive. For any finite hybrid memory, the image of the composite linear channel has dimension at most sum_j d_j^2<=D^2 in the vector space of output measures. But the Gaussian translates over the parameter box have infinite linear span: take any finite number of distinct theta values varying only in the first coordinate; after dividing their densities by the common positive Gaussian factor, a linear dependence would give a linear dependence among exponentials exp(a_j x_1) with distinct a_j, which is impossible. Thus the exact output family cannot lie in the image of a finite-dimensional archive channel.

The Bernoulli and Gaussian results establish an exact-precision Fisher counterexample as d grows: the same full Fisher matrix yields finite optimal archive bits d in one model and no finite exact archive in the other. They do not assert a uniform nonzero-error Gaussian rate in growing d. The fixed-d Gaussian rate card N07 has constants depending on the mean geometry and does not close that gap.

STATUS AND LIMITS

- PROVED HERE: exact pure-orbit total-D law; explicit failure scale for its fixed-d n^m/m! approximation; full-rank finite-error upper against a dimension-independent collision-product lower; exact Bernoulli product law; no-finite-D exact Gaussian archive.
- SOURCE-RECONSTRUCTED: the symmetric-subspace compression construction, its occupation basis dimension, and coherent reversible optimality appear in Chen et al., arXiv:2307.12024v2, §§III–V. The paper's lower bound is explicitly for reversible coherent compression; it does not state the direct-sum hybrid converse proved above.
- NOT ESTABLISHED: a matching finite-epsilon optimum for general full-rank growing-d qudit orbits; a dimension-uniform Fisher/LAN memory law; gate or finite-precision costs for growing d; spectrum acquisition; or any optimal approximate archive rate for the d-dimensional Gaussian example with d varying.
- Fixed-d Yang–Chiribella–Ebler mixed-state compression is an important boundary source: it reports a discontinuous memory drop for nonzero error under its known-spectrum qubit interface. Its theorem is not a general growing-d law and is not used in the proofs above.
