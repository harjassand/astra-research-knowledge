# Cycle 2: exact identifiability result and the missing measurement gate

## Operational model

Let `r_t` denote a reversible assay-linked mode and `d_t` a candidate persistent mode. These are operational coordinates, not biological conclusions. After intervention washout, take

\[
z_{t+1}=Az_t,\quad A=\mathrm{diag}(\lambda,\mu),\quad
y_t=c^Tz_t+\epsilon_t,
\]

where `0 ≤ lambda < 1`, `0 ≤ mu ≤ 1`, and `c=(a,b)`. The meanings “reversible” and “persistent” are assigned by the decay parameters, not by the names of clocks.

## Proposition 1: two samples of one readout

With noiseless measurements at `t=0,1`, the state `z_0=(r_0,d_0)` is identifiable from the scalar output iff

\[
\mathcal O_2=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\a\lambda&b\mu\end{pmatrix}
\quad\text{has rank 2},\qquad
\det(\mathcal O_2)=ab(\mu-\lambda)\ne0.
\]

Proof: the two observations satisfy `y_0 = a r_0 + b d_0` and `y_1 = a lambda r_0 + b mu d_0`; invert this 2×2 system iff its determinant is nonzero. Then

\[
r_0=\frac{\mu y_0-y_1}{a(\mu-\lambda)},\qquad
d_0=\frac{y_1-\lambda y_0}{b(\mu-\lambda)}.
\]

Under bounded error `|epsilon_t| ≤ eta`, the worst-case reconstruction errors are bounded by `(|mu|+1) eta/(|a| |mu-lambda|)` for `r_0` and `(1+|lambda|) eta/(|b| |mu-lambda|)` for `d_0`. Thus the estimate becomes ill-conditioned when one mode is weakly read (`a` or `b` small) or the decay rates nearly coincide. With two simultaneous readouts `y=Cz`, the one-time criterion is `rank(C)=2`.

## Proposition 2: no finite test for exact irreversibility

Compare `mu=1` with `mu=1-delta`, for `0 < delta < 1`, holding `d_0` and the other mode fixed. If `|d_0| ≤ D`, the difference in the `d` contribution to the scalar readout at any `t ≤ T` is

\[
|b d_0[1-(1-\delta)^t]|\le |b|D\delta T,
\]

using Bernoulli's inequality `1-(1-delta)^t ≤ delta*t`. If each reading has bounded noise of radius `eta` and `|b|D delta T ≤ 2 eta`, the two models have overlapping admissible observations at every sampled time. Hence a finite experiment can establish “no detected recovery faster than the resolution over horizon T,” but cannot establish exact `mu=1` unless the experiment supplies a lower bound on any reversible rate (or a finite maximum slow-recovery time).

For independent Gaussian errors with variance `sigma²` and `m` replicates per time, the KL divergence between these fixed-parameter hypotheses is exactly

\[
D_{KL}=\frac{m b^2 d_0^2}{2\sigma^2}\sum_{t=0}^{T}[1-(1-\delta)^t]^2
\le \frac{m b^2 D^2\delta^2}{2\sigma^2}\sum_{t=0}^{T}t^2.
\]

This gives an assay- and horizon-dependent sample-size gate. It is a model result, not a fitted biological recovery rate.

## Proposition 3: latent labels are not fixed by clock outputs

For a linear state-space output `y_t = C A^t z_0`, any nonsingular coordinate change `S` gives

\[
z'_0=S z_0,\quad A'=SAS^{-1},\quad C'=CS^{-1},
\]

and exactly the same observed trajectory. Without calibrated loadings `C`, known/anchored perturbation directions, or restrictions that select a canonical state basis, the data identify only an input-output realization, not which coordinate is “epigenetic tag” versus “irreversible damage.” This is the same core issue underlying non-unique phase-type generator representations. In a clock panel, two scores are not automatically two sensors of two biological modes: both are projections of the same methylome, and their mode loadings are not known from the score differences alone. The determinant/inverse and a concrete similarity-transform invariance example are symbolically checked by `verify_lemma.py` (SymPy).

## Application to the v2 preprint

The v2 reanalysis is a donor-level comparison of two clock scores, with confidence intervals, not a fit of `(A,C)` or a washout-state model. It does not estimate clock-to-mode loadings, distinct recovery constants, or a resolution horizon for “irreversibility.” Thus the papers' Horvath/DamAge dissociation does not satisfy the observability criterion above. Under its own low donor counts, the wide MPTR DamAge interval (−18.0 to +44.2 arbitrary units) additionally permits substantial reversal.

The v2 mortality decomposition has a different conditional-identification claim: the authors report model-generated parameter recovery under their joint cross-sectional biomarker/survival model. That tests estimator recovery when the simulated data obey the assumed shared-slope model. It does not prove that the age gradient is a within-person drift, that `gamma_base` is a biological damage state, or that this decomposition is uniquely implied under alternative frailty/cohort/measurement-error models. The paper's own limitations identify those alternatives.

## Falsifiable experiment and design algorithm

For one preselected primary human cell type, donor-block and randomize a sham, a short identity-retaining reprogramming pulse, and a separately calibrated perturbation expected to load the candidate persistent mode. Use lineage barcodes or equivalent clonal tracking so population replacement cannot masquerade as recovery. Sample baseline, end-pulse, then after washout at predeclared days 1, 3, 7, 14 and 28; measure two independently generated clock modalities (for example DNAm and accessibility/mitotic-age), identity/pluripotency markers, a direct persistent-lesion measure, and a functional reserve/regeneration assay after washout. A stress-response marker alone does not certify lesion removal.

Fit a donor-hierarchical model with both state and direct assay effects:

\[
z_{i,t+1}=A z_{it}+B u_{it}+\xi_{it},\qquad
y_{it}=Cz_{it}+D u_{it}+Gq_{it}+\epsilon_{it},
\]

where `u` records pulses, `D` allows the intervention to change an assay directly (important for global demethylation), and `q` records measured cell composition/identity. Estimate `A,B,C,D` on discovery donors; report the smallest singular value of the noise-whitened observability matrix; validate clock/function predictions in held-out donors. Choose sample times and perturbations by maximizing `log det(sum_t (C A^t)^T R^{-1}(C A^t))`, subject to viability and identity constraints. Preregister a smallest meaningful recovery rate `delta_min` and equivalence margins in each assay/function unit. Report only `PERSISTS_THROUGH_T_AT_RESOLUTION`, `DECAYS_WITHIN_T`, `IDENTITY_CONFOUNDED`, or `UNIDENTIFIABLE`; do not label a finite result biologically irreversible.

Falsifiers of the proposed two-mode model include rank-deficient or ill-conditioned observability, no held-out predictive gain over a one-mode model, elimination of apparent clock change after lineage/composition adjustment, or no concordant functional/lesion response despite a clock score shift. This protocol is a design proposal; no sample-size or dollar estimate is possible until donor-level covariance and assay noise are measured.
