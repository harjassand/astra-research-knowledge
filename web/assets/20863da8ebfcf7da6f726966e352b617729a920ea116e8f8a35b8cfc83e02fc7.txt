SPIN CRITICAL GIBBS EXPERIMENT: UNIFORM POSITIVE-PENALTY TRACE BOUND
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the trace theorem and channel bound;
FINITE-EVIDENCE for the listed finite checks. External proof validation,
priority, and practical state preparation remain UNKNOWN.

Finding
-------
At the open XX saturation point, the full finite-chain Gibbs state is uniformly
close to its free-fermion Gibbs state at critical inverse temperature, even
after adding an arbitrarily strong positive operator that annihilates all
configurations without a forbidden close pair. The operator need not conserve
particle number or be local within the forbidden subspace. The error is
O(L^(-1/2)) in full trace norm, uniformly over the unknown bounded field and
over all such penalties. Consequently, the unknown-field Gibbs experiment has
an approximate entanglement-breaking reconstruction from measuring total
occupation M and preparing a free canonical sector state. This is an
asymptotic classical-sufficiency result for the unextended state experiment,
not an exact sufficiency theorem for non-number-conserving interactions.

1. Exact interface and theorem
------------------------------
There are N=L-1 spin-half sites on an open path. Put n_i=(I-Z_i)/2,
M=sum_i n_i, and

  K = -J sum_(i=1)^(L-2) (X_i X_(i+1)+Y_i Y_(i+1)) + 4J M,
  H_0(lambda) = K - lambda M/L^2,
  beta = tau L^2,

where J,tau>0, |lambda|<=B, and J,tau,B are fixed supplied constants. The
unknown parameter is lambda; no lambda estimate is acquired. Let R>=1 be a
fixed integer and let P be the projector onto occupation strings with no
occupied pair at any separation r=1,...,R. Q=I-P. For each L let W_L be any
finite-dimensional Hermitian positive-semidefinite operator with W_L P=0.
It may depend on L and have arbitrarily large norm. Equivalently one may state
P W_L P=0, since W_L>=0 implies
P W_L P=(W_L^(1/2)P)^*(W_L^(1/2)P)=0 and hence W_LP=0.
Define normalized states

  rho_0 = exp[-beta H_0]/Z_0,
  rho_W = exp[-beta(H_0+W_L)]/Z_W.

For k>=1 define

  c_0 = exp(tau B) sum_(k>=1) exp(-8 tau J k^2),
  c_2 = exp(tau B) sum_(k>=1) k^2 exp(-8 tau J k^2),
  A_R = 8 pi^2 c_0 c_2 sum_(r=1)^R r^2,
  a_L = A_R/L^3.

For a_L<=1/2, set

  G_L = { h_2(a_L) + a_L (L-1) ln 2
          + tau a_L [2 J pi^2 c_2 + 8 J L^2 (2+c_0)
                         + B c_0 + B(2+c_0)] }/(1-a_L),

where h_2(x)=-x ln x-(1-x)ln(1-x), with h_2(0)=h_2(1)=0. Then

  ||rho_W-rho_0||_1 <= 2 sqrt(a_L) + sqrt(2 G_L)
                    = O_(J,tau,B,R)(L^(-1/2)).                 (1)

The bound is independent of W_L and uniform over |lambda|<=B. When a_L>1/2,
the trivial ||rho_W-rho_0||_1<=2 is available; for fixed parameters and R,
only finitely many L are omitted. The theorem is about full finite-chain
density operators, not energies, correlators, or fixed-particle sectors.

2. Proof sufficient to check
----------------------------
The open Jordan-Wigner and sine transforms give one-particle energies and
wavefunctions

  epsilon_(L,k)=4J(1-cos(pi k/L)),
  phi_(L,k)(i)=sqrt(2/L) sin(pi k i/L), 1<=i,k<L.

They obey 8J k^2 <= L^2 epsilon_(L,k) <= 2J pi^2 k^2. Under rho_0 the mode
occupations are independent Bernoulli variables with
f_k=(1+exp(tau(L^2 epsilon_(L,k)-lambda)))^(-1), so
f_k<=exp(tau B-8 tau J k^2), E_0 M<=c_0, and
L^2 E_0 K<=2J pi^2 c_2. The site occupation process is determinantal with
positive semidefinite correlation matrix C_ij=sum_k f_k phi_k(i)phi_k(j).

For j=i+r, Cauchy-Binet gives

  E_0(n_i n_j)=sum_(k<ell) f_k f_ell
       |phi_k(i)phi_ell(j)-phi_ell(i)phi_k(j)|^2.

Subtracting the sine functions at separation r and using |sin x-sin y|<=|x-y|
shows that the squared wedge is at most
4 pi^2 r^2(k+ell)^2/L^4. Also
sum_(k<ell) f_k f_ell(k+ell)^2<=2 c_0 c_2. Summing over at most L valid
positions for each r gives

  delta := Tr(rho_0 Q) <= a_L.                                 (2)

The weighted version is needed for energy control. For each pair,

  E_0(M n_i n_j)=2 E_0(n_i n_j)+sum_(z notin{i,j}) det C_{i,j,z}.

For any positive semidefinite block C, Schur complementation gives
det C_(i,j,z)<=det C_(i,j) C_zz; singular blocks follow by continuity.
Since sum_z C_zz=Tr C=E_0 M<=c_0,

  q_M := Tr(rho_0 M Q) <= (2+c_0)a_L.                          (3)

Let p=1-delta and sigma=P rho_0 P/p. This is a coherent projection onto the
allowed space, not a measurement of each occupation. The gentle-measurement
bound (equivalently, purify rho_0 and project the purification) gives
||sigma-rho_0||_1<=2 sqrt(delta). Also W_L sigma=0.

The one-particle spectrum of K lies in [0,8J], hence 0<=K<=8JM and
[rho_0,K]=0. No commutation of P or Q with K is needed. Indeed

  P K P = K-QK-KQ+QKQ,
  Tr(rho_0 QK)=Tr(Q K rho_0)>=0,
  QKQ<=8J Q M Q=8J M Q,

so Tr(rho_0 P K P)<=Tr(rho_0 K)+8J q_M. Since P commutes with M,

  Tr(sigma K)-Tr(rho_0 K)
      <= [delta Tr(rho_0 K)+8J q_M]/p,
  |Tr(sigma M)-Tr(rho_0 M)| <= [delta c_0+q_M]/p.            (4)

Pinching rho_0 into P/Q blocks cannot decrease entropy. With omega=Q rho_0 Q/delta,
S(rho_0)<=h_2(delta)+pS(sigma)+delta S(omega), and log dim(H)=(L-1)ln2.
Therefore

  S(rho_0)-S(sigma) <= [h_2(delta)+delta(L-1)ln2]/p.        (5)

The factor 1/p is a convenient loose bound. For finite-dimensional W_L>=0,
min-max orders each eigenvalue of H_0+W_L above the corresponding eigenvalue
of H_0, so Z_W<=Z_0. This is a scalar partition-function comparison; it does
not assert e^(-beta(H_0+W_L))<=e^(-beta H_0) as an operator. The exact
relative-entropy identity is

  D(sigma||rho_W)= -S(sigma)+beta Tr(sigma H_0)
                    + beta Tr(sigma W_L)+ln Z_W.

Here Tr(sigma W_L)=0. Subtract the identity D(rho_0||rho_0)=0 and use
(2)-(5), |lambda|<=B, and Z_W<=Z_0. For a_L<=1/2, h_2(delta)<=h_2(a_L)
and p>=1-a_L; this yields D(sigma||rho_W)<=G_L as displayed. Quantum Pinsker
gives ||sigma-rho_W||_1<=sqrt(2G_L). The triangle inequality with
||sigma-rho_0||_1<=2sqrt(delta) proves (1).

3. General W extension, operational meaning, and costs
------------------------------------------------------
The W-dependent part of the proof uses only W>=0 and WP=0. It does not use
[W,M]=0, locality, or a density-polynomial form. For the general W, the
additional scalar term in the comparison is

  Gamma(W)= beta Tr(sigma W)+ln(Z_W/Z_0),

and the stated hypotheses imply Gamma(W)<=0. More generally, the comparison
would work if a certified Gamma(W)<=c were supplied, with G_L replaced by
G_L+c. Positivity and PWP=0 are a convenient verifiable sufficient condition,
not asserted necessary.

There is a parameter-independent entanglement-breaking decoder for the
unknown-field experiment. Let P_m project onto M=m and let
q_m(lambda)=Tr(P_m rho_0). The free state commutes with M and
omega^0_m=P_m rho_0 P_m/q_m(lambda) is independent of lambda because the
detuning contributes only the scalar exp(tau lambda m) inside sector m.
Define

  Phi_0(X)=sum_m Tr(P_m X) omega^0_m.

This is a CPTP measure-and-prepare channel, and Phi_0(rho_0(lambda))=rho_0(lambda)
for every |lambda|<=B. Contractivity and (1) imply

  ||Phi_0(rho_W(lambda))-rho_W(lambda)||_1
      <= 2 ||rho_W(lambda)-rho_0(lambda)||_1
      <= 4 sqrt(a_L)+2 sqrt(2G_L).                            (6)

Thus M is an approximate classical sufficient statistic in trace norm even
when the interacting state has cross-sector coherences. Exact M-sector
sufficiency for rho_W holds only if [W,M]=0. Equation (6) is only for the
unextended state; it is not a diamond-norm or reference-preserving guarantee.
For n copies with a common unknown lambda, tensor-product reconstruction error
is at most n times the one-copy bound, so n=o(sqrt(L)) suffices for vanishing
error at fixed parameters. Storing M costs ceil(log2 L) classical bits (or
ceil(log2(d+1)) bits after a d-sector overflow code); measuring all sites to
obtain it costs L single-site occupation readouts. The free conditional states
are mathematically specified by public N,J,tau, but their preparation cost is
not charged by this theorem. A finite-bit shifted-symmetric-polynomial/Givens
decoder was derived separately as an implementation candidate; it was not
executed as a certified compiler, and no physical state-preparation or
quantum-advantage claim follows from the archive size.

A finite alphabet also follows. Store m exactly for m<d and map all m>=d to
one overflow label; decode that label to any fixed state, for example vacuum.
Let delta_L=2sqrt(a_L)+sqrt(2G_L), so ||rho_W-rho_0||_1<=delta_L. Measurement
of the tail event gives

  P_W(M>=d) <= P_0(M>=d)+delta_L/2.

Comparing the finite-label decoder to Phi_0 on the overflow event adds at most
2P_W(M>=d) in trace norm. Therefore its total reconstruction error is at most

  3 delta_L + 2 P_0(M>=d).                                    (7)

For the free state, Chernoff and the sine-energy lower bound give, for s>0,

  P_0(M>=d) <= exp(-sd) product_(k>=1)
                         [1+exp(s+tau B-8 tau J k^2)].

The product is finite. Choosing s=2 tau J d^2 yields the explicit uniform
cubic tail in leaf01, log P_0(M>=d)<=-(4/3)tau J d^3+O_(J,tau,B)(d^2).
Thus at fixed J,tau,B and target epsilon, d=O((log(1/epsilon))^(1/3)) gives
O(log log(1/epsilon)) classical archive bits, provided L is large enough that
delta_L is within the remaining error budget. This is an asymptotic
single-copy archive guarantee; it is not an efficient physical preparation
claim. The detailed finite-chain tail constants are in
outputs/research/spin_statistical/cycle2/leaves/leaf01_local_critical_limit/RESULT.txt.

For a fixed measured sector m, the free conditional state also has a direct
finite-mode approximation. Let a_k=L^2 epsilon_(L,k), w_k=exp(-tau a_k),
c=8 tau J, and C=sum_(k>=1)exp(-c k^2). Its sine-mode subset distribution is
proportional to products of w_k over m-element subsets. If K>=m, then

  P_{omega_m^0}(some occupied mode k>K)
    <= exp(2 tau J pi^2 sum_(i=1)^m i^2) C^(m-1)/(m-1)!
       * sum_(k>K) exp(-c k^2).

Indeed, P(k occupied)=w_k e_(m-1)(w_except k)/e_m(w),
e_(m-1)<= (sum w)^(m-1)/(m-1)!, and e_m(w)>=product_(i=1)^m w_i;
then sum over k>K. The explicit bounds

  C <= exp(-c)(1+1/(2c)),
  sum_(k>K) exp(-c k^2)
    <= exp(-c(K+1)^2)(1+1/(2c(K+1)))

follow from a first term plus a Gaussian integral. Removing all modes above K
changes the normalized diagonal canonical state by trace distance exactly equal
to this omitted probability (full trace norm is twice that). A suffix
log-elementary-symmetric-polynomial table samples the truncated m-subset in
O(Km) arithmetic and O(Km) memory, with O(K) work per sample. This gives a
finite mode cutoff whose size is independent of L for fixed m,J,tau,error.

Actual local benchmark, 2026-10-08: Python 3.14.3 on macOS 26.4 arm64,
L=100000 (N=99999), J=tau=1, m=8, requested omitted-mode probability 1e-8.
The analytic first-term-plus-integral bound selected K=22 and was
1.1085e-117. Across nine runs, the median double-precision log-DP build took
37.6 microseconds; sampling 2000 subsets took 0.00774 seconds (about 258,346
subsets/s). An independent exhaustive K=9,m=3 fixture checked all 84 subsets;
the largest absolute probability discrepancy from direct enumeration was
2.72e-22.
Reproducible script and JSON are
outputs/research/spin_statistical/cycle2/evidence/free_canonical_dp_benchmark.py
and .json. These timings cover only classical subset generation. The DP uses
double-precision trigonometric/logarithmic operations and 53-bit random draws;
the cutoff inequality is mathematically derived but no interval-certified
implementation was used. No quantum circuit synthesis, sine/Givens execution,
gate fidelity, physical thermalization, or wall-clock quantum state preparation
was measured. The same sampler is available to a classical baseline, so this
benchmark establishes neither a speedup nor a quantum advantage.

4. Why positivity is essential; exact counterexample
----------------------------------------------------
For indefinite W, PWP=0 alone need not imply WP=0 or Z_W<=Z_0. The runnable
counterexample fixes L=3 (two sites), J=tau=1, lambda=0, R=1, beta=9 and

  W=-100(|00><11|+|11><00|),

in basis |00>,|01>,|10>,|11>. P=diag(1,1,1,0), so PWP=0, but W is indefinite.
H_0 has eigenvalues 0,2,6,8; H_0+W has eigenvalues 4+/-sqrt(10016),2,6.
The verified values are ln(Z_W/Z_0)=864.7197 and
Tr(rho_W Q)=0.480016, while Tr(rho_0 Q)=5.38e-32. The trace norm is at least
the difference in this binary measurement probability, 0.480016. At these
parameters the proposed positive-W RHS would be 0.011453. This refutes the
weakened indefinite assumption in a finite instance; it says nothing about
all attractive models.

The local-lattice theorem is separate from the Astra collective-spin claims.
N20 concerns a permutation-symmetric all-to-all quartic experiment and its
card explicitly says it supplies no local-lattice universality. N09/N10 and
N91 are collective-spin/unknown-field results; they do not prove a chain Gibbs
limit. The N117 axial Gibbs separability claim is a different all-qubit model.
The low-dimensional audit independently derives the exact N=2 PPT boundary

  (1-exp(-alpha))^2 <= 4 exp(-delta),

showing 2 ln2 is sharp uniformly over alpha,N but is not the boundary at each
finite alpha. N=3,4 checks have strict moment-cone margin at the endpoint.
N117's N=72 higher-order NPT witness uses delta=81/50>2 ln2 and is outside its
claimed interval; it is not a contradiction. The 197-case finite-prefix
certificates and N>=200 analytic moment-tail join are still source-reported
here, not replayed. N118's universal compiler remains unexecuted; the reported
N=64 end-to-end run missed its 60-second cap, with a 68.46-second
export-inclusive lower bound. There is no physical Gibbs preparation claim.

7. Falsifiers and restart gate
------------------------------
A failure in the open-chain dispersion, determinant wedge estimate, Schur
determinant inequality, or coherent-projection energy step would refute (1).
The sharp structural test is to find W>=0 with WP=0 violating min-max or the
relative-entropy comparison. For an attempted broader class, calculate
Gamma(W)=beta Tr(sigma W)+ln(Z_W/Z_0); a positive Gamma is the exact term the
current proof cannot absorb. Other open boundaries are R growing with L,
attractive interactions, local operators that charge P, higher dimensions,
and reference-preserving/diamond-norm reconstruction. Continue with an
independent specialist proof review of the constants and a source-level search
for existing trace-norm local Gibbs comparisons before making any priority
claim. For runtime, implement and benchmark the public free-sector sampler
and Givens decoder against an equally informed classical matrix/observable
baseline; do not infer runtime from a finite sector archive.
