The deeper revision proves a site-response theorem. For any such localized
fiber, arbitrary nonnegative activities, and global fugacity tau>0, let I_i
record whether one free coordinate is a hole and let K be the total half-hole
count. On every nonempty weighted fiber,

  0 <= Cov_tau(I_i,K) <= Var_tau(I_i).

The proof localizes the stable polynomial, separates the chosen coordinate,
and forms the odd real-rational quotient Phi(t) of the two radial pieces. The
zero-free two-variable pencil forces Phi to have nonnegative real part in the
right half-plane. A direct pole/residue argument yields

  Phi(t)/t = a + b/t^2 + sum_j c_j/(t^2+lambda_j),
  a,b,c_j >= 0, lambda_j>0.

This Stieltjes form makes the site-inclusion odds increase with fugacity and
bounds their log-fugacity response by the Bernoulli variance. Positive-activity
proof extends to zero activities by finite-polynomial limits; identically zero
fibers are excluded. Exact zero coefficients need no perturbation. The upper
bound is sharp for h(x,y)=1+xy, where I_x=K; the lower bound is attained when
localization fixes the site's state.

The theorem is not pairwise positive association: an exact n=4 signed
ambient-hole fixture has Cov(I_0,I_3)=-1/50 at unit activities. It supplies no
microscopic sampler, FPRAS, affordable partition-evaluation oracle, or energy
comparison. The proof and detailed boundary audit are in
revisions/total_covariance_extension.txt. The exact script checked 27,279
localized coordinate/fugacity marginals over dimension-6 determinant fixtures,

These checks are diagnostic, not a proof.
