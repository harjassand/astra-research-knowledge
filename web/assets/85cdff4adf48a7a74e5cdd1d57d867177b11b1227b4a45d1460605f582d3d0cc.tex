\begin{abstract}
This record reports a broad, iterative investigation using the Astra Research Knowledge snapshot
\texttt{1c5743f427b2200d7a1ba188063e5c07053a5744} as prior capital. Its principal completed derivation is an exact regularized exponent for parallel quantum detection at zero false-alarm probability, with a sharp order-of-growth comparison against all separable probes. The gain is at most the condition number of the positive-cost operator, and three-level examples attain that order. A separate proof reconstructs the whole critical mean-field spin experiment from a three-dimensional quartic observation, including unknown noncommuting anisotropy. These are scoped internal mathematical results, not externally certified solutions or priority claims. The accompanying evidence package retains proof attempts, counterexamples, prior-art downgrades, and unresolved interfaces across the broader search.
\end{abstract}

\section{Parallel detection per unit input cost}\label{sec:energy}
All logarithms in this section are natural. Let $\mathcal H$ be finite dimensional, let $H\geq0$ have the unique zero-cost ray $\C\ket0$, and let a known memoryless channel satisfy
\[
 \Phi(\ket0\bra0)=\ket v\bra v.
\]
Define its no-click effect and logarithm by
\[
 E=\Phi^*(\ket v\bra v),\qquad 0<E\leq I,\qquad L=-\log E.
\]
Then $E\ket0=\ket0$, $L\ket0=0$, and both preserve the orthogonal excited subspace. Write $H_+$ for the positive restriction of $H$. A probe $\rho$ on $r$ parallel inputs has charged expected input cost
\[
 Q=\Tr\rho H^{(r)},\qquad H^{(r)}=\sum_{i=1}^r H_i,
\]
and no-click probability $p_0=\Tr\rho E^{\otimes r}$. The null channel always outputs $\ket v$. Thus $p_0$ is precisely the minimum miss probability of a test with \emph{zero} false-alarm probability: reject the null on the orthogonal complement of $\ket v^{\otimes r}$. The model permits arbitrary parallel entanglement, unrestricted block length, and expected input cost. It does not charge the apparatus, driving reference, or state-preparation work as input cost. A hard constraint on the spectral support of $H^{(r)}$ is a different, unresolved extension.

Define
\begin{align*}
 \alpha&=\lambda_{\max}(H_+^{-1/2}L_+H_+^{-1/2}),\\
 \beta&=\sup_{\ket w:\,\bra wH\ket w>0}
 \frac{-\log\bra wE\ket w}{\bra wH\ket w}.
\end{align*}
\begin{theorem}[Exact parallel and separable exponents]\label{thm:exponent}
Every probe obeys $p_0\geq e^{-\alpha Q}$. The supremum of $-\log p_0/Q$ over all finite parallel blocks is $\alpha$. The same supremum over all separable probes, including arbitrary classical mixtures of unequal product inputs, is $\beta$. Consequently the least charged cost needed to achieve miss probability at most $\varepsilon$ has leading coefficient $1/\alpha$ for unrestricted probes and $1/\beta$ for separable probes, as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$, provided $L\ne0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The generalized eigenvalue definition gives $L\leq\alpha H$. Scalar Jensen applied to the spectral measure of $L^{(r)}=\sum_i L_i$ in $\rho$ gives
\[
 p_0=\Tr\rho e^{-L^{(r)}}\geq
 e^{-\Tr\rho L^{(r)}}\geq e^{-\alpha Q}.
\]
No operator monotonicity of the exponential is invoked.

Choose a unit vector $w$ with $\ell=\bra wL\ket w=\alpha h$, where $h=\bra wH\ket w>0$. For $\delta_r\downarrow0$ with $r\delta_r^2\to\infty$, let $P_r$ be the spectral projector of $L^{(r)}$ onto $[r(\ell-\delta_r),r(\ell+\delta_r)]$. The product-state spectral measure is iid and bounded, so
\[
 \eta_r=\bra{w^{\otimes r}}P_r\ket{w^{\otimes r}}\longrightarrow1.
\]
Set $\ket{\chi_r}=P_r\ket{w^{\otimes r}}/\sqrt{\eta_r}$. Its cost is $rh+o(r)$: its trace distance from the product state tends to zero, and $\|H^{(r)}\|=r\|H\|$. Its no-click probability is at most $e^{-r(\ell-\delta_r)}$. This proves attainment of the limiting ratio. Repeating any fixed nearly optimal block gives the asserted leading cost law for arbitrary small $\varepsilon$.

For each product pulse, the definition of $\beta$ gives $\bra wE\ket w\geq e^{-\beta\bra wH\ket w}$. The same inequality holds for mixed single-cell states by convexity, then for products by multiplication, and for all separable mixtures by Jensen. Conversely repeated copies of a pulse approaching the supremum attain $\beta$.
\end{proof}

The scalar Jensen step and spectral-typical construction are elementary forms of a familiar smoothing phenomenon: $-\log\bra wE\ket w$ becomes $\bra w(-\log E)\ket w$ after smoothing product vectors. Datta's min-relative entropy and Nuradha--Wilde's smooth fidelity-relative entropy provide close prior art; see Section~\ref{sec:refs}.

\subsection{The largest gain is of order the cost condition number}
Let $\kappa=\lambda_{\max}(H_+)/\lambda_{\min}(H_+)$. Overall rescaling of $H$ changes both exponents equally.
\begin{theorem}[Sharp order of the entanglement gain]\label{thm:kappa}
For every admissible pair with $L\ne0$,
\[
 1\leq\frac\alpha\beta\leq\kappa.
\]
Conversely, for every $\kappa\geq1$ there is a three-level example of cost condition number exactly $\kappa$ satisfying
\[
 \frac\alpha\beta\geq \frac{2-\sqrt3}{8}(\kappa+1).
\]
Thus the supremum gain over this class grows as $\Theta(\kappa)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Jensen gives $\beta\leq\alpha$. Put $\ell_{\max}=\|L\|>0$. Then $\alpha\leq\ell_{\max}/h_{\min}$. Using an $L$-maximizing eigenvector as a product pulse gives $\beta\geq\ell_{\max}/h_{\max}$, proving the upper bound.

For the construction put $c_0=(2-\sqrt3)/4$, $\eta=1/\kappa$, $M=c_0(\log4)\kappa$, and
\[
 E=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,e^{-M}),\quad
 H=0\oplus\begin{pmatrix}(1+\eta)/2&(1-\eta)/2\\
 (1-\eta)/2&(1+\eta)/2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The positive eigenvalues of $H$ are $\eta,1$. Since $L_+=M\ket2\bra2$,
\[
 \alpha=\frac{M(1+\eta)}{2\eta}=\frac{M(\kappa+1)}2.
\]
For an excited pure state of population $s$ in $\ket2$, the smallest cost over its relative phase is
\[
 h(s)=\frac{1+\eta}2-(1-\eta)\sqrt{s(1-s)},
\]
whereas $-\log\bra wE\ket w=-\log(1-s+se^{-M})$.
If $s\leq3/4$, this numerator is at most $\log4$ and $h(s)\geq\eta$. If $s>3/4$, then $h(s)\geq c_0$ and the numerator is at most $M$. Vacuum admixture cannot improve the ratio, by convexity of $-\log$. Therefore
\[
 \beta\leq\max\{\log4/\eta,M/c_0\}=(\log4)\kappa.
\]
The claimed ratio follows. The channel can be realized explicitly as the binary measure-and-prepare map
\[
 \Phi_E(\rho)=\Tr(E\rho)\ket v\bra v+
 \Tr((I-E)\rho)\ket c\bra c,\qquad \langle v,c\rangle=0.
\]
\end{proof}
The construction also has $\|{-\log E}\|=c_0(\log4)\kappa$. Its increasingly large effect dynamic range and small positive cost eigenvalue are part of the resource ledger. It does not give unbounded gain at fixed cost condition number.

\subsection{An exact two-use witness}
A strict advantage needs neither an asymptotic limit nor a large Hilbert space. Let $b=\log(8/3)$ and
\[
 E=1\oplus\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\\0&1/4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 H=0\oplus\begin{pmatrix}b-1/12&-1/4\\-1/4&b+7/12\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The eigenvalues of $H_+$ are $b-1/6,b+2/3>0$. For an excited state with first population $s$, the minimum cost is
\[
 h(s)=b+7/12-(2/3)s-\tfrac12\sqrt{s(1-s)}.
\]
The function $h(s)-\log(4/(1+s))$ is nonnegative: it and its derivative vanish at $s=1/2$, and its second derivative is
\[
 \frac1{8[s(1-s)]^{3/2}}-\frac1{(1+s)^2}\geq0.
\]
The endpoints follow by continuity. Thus $\beta=1$, with equality at $(\ket1+\ket2)/\sqrt2$.

For the entangled state
\[
 \ket\chi=\frac{4\ket{11}+5\ket{12}+5\ket{21}+4\ket{22}}{\sqrt{82}},
\]
direct algebra gives
\[
 p_0=45/328,\qquad Q=2b+1/82,\qquad
 -\log p_0-Q=\log(41/40)-1/82>0.
\]
This strictly exceeds every separable block's exponent per cost. The exact generalized eigenvalue is about $1.06013$, so the two-use example is not claimed optimal.

\subsection{Operational consequences and failure boundaries}
For the orthogonal-output realization $\Phi_E$, the exponent is also the optimal fixed-message communication reliability exponent under a \emph{maximum per-message expected input cost}. For $m$ equally likely messages, every word gives the all-vacuum output probability at least $e^{-\alpha Q}$. Any decoder has average error at least $(1-1/m)e^{-\alpha Q}$. Pulse-position coding with a nearly optimal pulse attains exponent $\alpha$, using $m$ pulse positions and charging their physical cell count separately. The same assertion with $\beta$ holds for separable words.

For general channels, this communication interpretation is false. With input cost $H=\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$, let a channel prepare $\ket v$ for input zero and $\ket u=\sqrt p\ket v+\sqrt{1-p}\ket{v^\perp}$ for input one. Then $E=\operatorname{diag}(1,p)$ and $\alpha=-\log p$. Binary pulse-position outputs have overlap amplitude $p^r$, so their optimal discrimination error is asymptotic to $p^{2r}/4$, with exponent $2\alpha$. This counterexample is retained in the evidence.

If $\Phi$ is time-translation covariant with respect to input cost Hamiltonian $H$, and the reference projector is invariant under output time translations, covariance implies $[E,H]=0$. A common eigenvector attains $\alpha$ already as a product pulse, so $\alpha=\beta$. The noncommuting advantage therefore requires a resource outside this autonomous covariant model.

A full-rank null output destroys the zero-false-alarm task: a positive rejection effect with zero null expectation must vanish. Allowing a small false-alarm tolerance is a different, potentially robust hypothesis-testing problem. Adaptive stopping under alternative-only expected cost is also different: repeat a pulse of cost $h$ and miss probability $p$ until a click or $k$ trials. Miss probability is $p^k$ but expected cost is at most $h/(1-p)$. The logarithmic parallel-cost law cannot be transferred to that convention.

\subsection{Constructive rather than purely existential attainment}
For a fixed three-level example with $L_+=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$, the typical projector is a window in the number of weight-four excitations. Prepare $w^{\otimes r}$, coherently count that number, compute a membership flag, measure only the flag, and uncompute the counter. Measuring the complete count would destroy the adjacent-type coherences responsible for the cost improvement. A window of width $\sqrt{2r\log r}$ has failure probability at most $2r^{-4}$. The resulting exponent approaches $\alpha$ within $O(\sqrt{\log r/r})$, with polynomial gate count and logarithmic counter workspace for this fixed spectral basis. State-synthesis error must preserve the chosen projector support; generic additive trace error is insufficient to protect an exponentially small miss probability. Exact formulas, finite checks, and this limitation are recorded in the constructive companion.

\section{A whole critical quantum experiment}\label{sec:gibbs}
Let $J=\tfrac12\sum_{i=1}^N\sigma^{(i)}$ be collective spin, and let
\[
 Q_A(J)=\sum_{a,b=1}^3 A_{ab}(J_aJ_b+J_bJ_a)/2
\]
for a real symmetric matrix $A$. For fixed compact parameter bounds, consider
\[
 \rho_{N,A,b}=Z_{N,A,b}^{-1}
 \exp\left\{\frac{2J^2}{N}+
 \frac{Q_A(J)}{N^{3/2}}+\frac{b\cdot J}{N^{3/4}}\right\}.
\]
Unknown $A$ includes both a critical temperature displacement and noncommuting anisotropy. The model, scaling and parameter bounds are public; no unknown microscopic Hamiltonian is acquired.

\begin{theorem}[Critical quartic experiment, internal reconstruction]
There are parameter-independent channels $T_N$ from the $N$ spins to one classical vector and $S_N$ in the reverse direction such that, uniformly over the compact parameter set,
\[
 \TV(T_N\rho_{N,A,b},p_{A,b})\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad \tfrac12\|S_Np_{A,b}-\rho_{N,A,b}\|_1\longrightarrow0,
\]
where
\[
 p_{A,b}(x)=Z_{A,b}^{-1}
 e^{-4|x|^4/3+x^TAx+b\cdot x},\qquad x\in\R^3.
\]
The reverse channel reconstructs the full density, including spin multiplicity registers. It does not preserve an arbitrary external purifying reference.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We record the load-bearing steps; the companion audit gives the exact symbol identities and tail details.

For any unital, trace-preserving completely positive map $B$ and $\tau=e^K/\Tr e^K$, operator Jensen for the logarithm yields
\[
 D(\tau\|B\tau)\leq\Tr\tau(K-BK)\leq\|K-BK\|.
\]
Quantum Pinsker then bounds trace distance by $\sqrt{\|K-BK\|/2}$. This step needs no commutation between $K$ and $B\tau$.

In spin sector $j$, use the coherent measure-and-prepare channel
\[
 B_j(X)=(2j+1)\int_{S^2}\bra uX\ket u\ket u\bra u\,d\mu(u).
\]
It fixes scalars, has rank-one multiplier $j/(j+1)$, and rank-two multiplier $j(2j-1)/[(j+1)(2j+3)]$. Writing $A_0=A-(\Tr A)I/3$, this gives a generator defect bounded by
\[
 3\|A_0\|\,j/N^{3/2}+|b|/N^{3/4}=O(N^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly over $j\leq N/2$. Hence the blockwise channel $B$ reconstructs $\rho$ with trace error $O(N^{-1/4})$.

Measure sector $j$ and coherent direction $u$. The reference sector weight is
\[
 q_N^0(j)\ \propto\ (2j+1)m_N(j)e^{2j(j+1)/N},\qquad
 m_N(j)=\frac{2j+1}{N+1}\binom{N+1}{N/2-j}.
\]
Put $k=j+1/2$ and $L=N+1$. Its unnormalized weight is
\[
 \frac{4k^2}{L}\binom{L}{L/2-k}\exp\{2(k^2-1/4)/N\}.
\]
The quadratic entropy term cancels the critical Gibbs term. Uniform Stirling expansion for bounded $k/N^{3/4}$ leaves $-4(k/N^{3/4})^4/3$, and the factor $k^2$ becomes the radial Jacobian. The entropy function $I(z)=\tfrac12[(1+z)\log(1+z)+(1-z)\log(1-z)]$ satisfies $I(z)\geq z^2/2+z^4/12$, supplying an integrable quartic tail. Bounded $A,b$ introduce at most an $e^{C(r^2+r)}$ tilt, preserving uniform tail control.

For completeness, define the sector perturbation $F_j=Q_A(J)/N^{3/2}+b\cdot J/N^{3/4}$, including the scalar part of $A$. Its lower symbol is
\[
 h_j^-(u)=\frac{j^2u^TAu+(j/2)(\Tr A-u^TAu)}{N^{3/2}}
 +\frac{j b\cdot u}{N^{3/4}}.
\]
Its exact upper symbol differs by at most
\[
 3(j+1/2)\|A_0\|/N^{3/2}+|b|/N^{3/4}=O(N^{-1/2}).
\]
Scalar Jensen gives a pointwise lower bound $\bra u e^{F_j}\ket u\geq e^{h_j^-(u)}$. Applying scalar Jensen to each eigenvector of $F_j$ and its coherent upper-symbol representation gives the corresponding integrated upper bound. The positive excess and this uniform gap show that normalized coherent output differs by $o(1)$ in total variation from the classical exponential tilt of $q_N^0$. Radial cell jitter then yields the displayed density $p_{A,b}$.

The decoder rounds radius back to sector $j$, prepares its coherent state in direction $u$, attaches the known maximally mixed multiplicity state and reverses the Schur transform. With matched radial cells, $S_NT_N=B$ exactly. The forward total-variation limit, contraction under decoding, and the reconstruction bound for $B$ prove the reverse assertion. All channels depend on $N$ and public geometry, not on $A,b$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Sharp precision exponent for the limiting experiment}
Let $e_D$ be the best uniform total-variation reconstruction error after encoding one $X\sim p_{A,b}$ into a classical, quantum or hybrid memory of total dimension $D$. All retained input-dependent labels count; independent public randomness is permitted. No uncharged preshared entangled decoder system is allowed, here or in the memory results below. Assume the parameter set contains $A=0$ and a nondegenerate field cube.
\begin{proposition}
There are constants $0<c<C<\infty$ such that $cD^{-2/3}\leq e_D\leq CD^{-2/3}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For the upper bound transform $y_i=\tanh x_i$. The transformed densities extend smoothly by zero at the cube boundary, uniformly in the bounded parameters, because quartic tails dominate every inverse boundary power. A grid of tensor linear hats of mesh $h$ gives the positive encoder $\Pr(k|y)=\phi_k(y)$ and decoder density $\phi_k/\int\phi_k$. The resulting positive operator has uniform $L^1$ error $O(h^2)$, using zero first moments at interior nodes and flat boundary tails; it has $D=O(h^{-3})$ labels.

For the lower bound restrict to $A=0$, $b\in[-B,B]^3$ and take prior density proportional to $Z_{0,b}$. The posterior mean has coordinates
\[
 m_i(x)=B\coth(Bx_i)-1/x_i,\quad m_i(0)=0.
\]
It is strictly increasing, with derivative comparable to $(1+x_i^2)^{-1}$. The distribution of $m(X)$ has bounded density, since its inverse Jacobian grows polynomially while the predictive density has quartic tails. If reconstructed $X'$ has uniform experiment error $e$, bounded quadratic Bayes-loss comparison gives
\[
 \mathbb E\|m(X)-m(X')\|^2\leq C_Be.
\]
For an encoder state $\omega_x\leq I$ and decoder POVM $M(dx')$, the trace identity $\int\Tr M=D$ gives
\[
 \Pr(\|m(X)-m(X')\|\leq r)\leq D C_0r^3.
\]
Choose $r$ so the right side is $1/2$, and lower-bound squared distortion by $r^2/2$. Conditioning on an independent seed preserves the trace bound. This proves the quantum as well as classical converse.
\end{proof}
For the finite spin experiment, channel composition gives only
\[
 cD^{-2/3}-a_N'\leq e_{N,D}\leq CD^{-2/3}+a_N,
 \qquad a_N,a_N'\to0.
\]
The thermodynamic remainders cannot be discarded when precision varies with $N$. No efficient Schur/coherent preparation compiler or local-lattice critical universality theorem is supplied by this argument.

\section{Further results and unresolved interfaces}
The evidence package contains independently developed results beyond the two main lines. Their proofs and status labels should be retrieved before reuse.
\begin{itemize}
\item For the one-observation experiment $\operatorname{Unif}[0,\theta]$, $\theta\in[1,2]$, arbitrary total quantum/hybrid dimension $D$ has reconstruction error $\Theta(D^{-1})$. One audited explicit bound is
\[
 \frac{(1-\log2)^2}{8\log2\,D}\leq e_D\leq\frac{\log2}{2D}.
\]
The continuum of endpoints is essential; finitely many endpoints have a finite exact sufficient alphabet. Moving support prevents a universal smooth $D^{-2/r}$ rule. A further independently reconstructed extension has density $(\gamma+1)(\theta-x)_+^\gamma/\theta^{\gamma+1}$ on $x\geq0$, for fixed $\gamma\geq0$: its error is $\Theta(D^{-\min\{\gamma+1,2\}})$. Scale-dependent bounded hinge scores supply the fractional-exponent converse, where a fixed quadratic posterior score is not sharp; a reflected positive reconstruction supplies the upper. Full proofs are in the statistical-geometry and general-principle companions.
\item The exact local pure-qudit family $(\ket0+\sum_{a=1}^{d-1}z_a\ket a/\sqrt n)^{\otimes n}$, properly normalized with bounded $z$, has precision dimension of order $(1+\min\{n,\log(1/\varepsilon)/\log\log(1/\varepsilon)\})^{d-1}$. The total-dimension result follows from established source-coding and photon-cutoff mechanisms. A separate internal proof lower-bounds the quantum branch dimension even with uncharged classical side labels, under blind encoding and no shared entanglement. The interface must not be conflated with visible parameter access.
\item A sharp positive-local-tester barrier allows arbitrary noncommuting auxiliary systems: if positive terms of average syndrome support at most $r$ distinguish a zero syndrome of energy at most $\eta$ from every nonzero syndrome with energy at least $c$, then $c\leq\eta+(r/t)(1-\eta)$ for $t$ syndrome bits. This does not exclude encodings that mix sectors.
\item Exact executable cube and partition-base rounding was developed with a weaker logarithmic discrepancy factor than the archive's strongest supplied-oracle claim. Its real advance in this campaign is explicit acquisition and sparse exact-mean support, not a claim to improve the best classical discrepancy theorem.
\item Several source-level failures were retained: temporal aliasing can conceal unbounded dissipation from finite predetermined samples; weak local spectral limits need not preserve a whole experiment; low predictive teacher risk does not control artificial spliced queries; positive output support and covariance promises remain substantive resources.
\end{itemize}
The nonsofic hyperbolic-group line is inherited candidate capital. Fresh workers checked its algebraic and geometric steps and investigated a smaller incidence construction. Agreement among these workers does not certify the group construction or establish priority. Its exact proofs and outstanding acquisition costs remain separate from the unconditional detection results in this report.
