Cycle 3 leaf: exponential substrate Foster function removes the small-gain restriction
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact finite family below; no novelty or priority claim.

RESULT
------
For the all-positive multi-catalyst/multi-substrate family in
outputs/research/reaction_dynamics/cycle3/leaves/foster_multicatalyst.txt,
the strict linear small-gain inequalities are unnecessary for positive
recurrence. A different, physical-time Foster function proves that the full
state-space-irreducible CTMC is nonexplosive and positive recurrent for every
fixed choice of strictly positive finite rates, every finite r,s>=1, and every
finite integer k_ji>=1.

The new function is linear in catalyst counts, exponential in each substrate
count, and has a bounded correction on the all-catalysts-zero face. It uses
only q_ji>0 to make large substrate counts costly, v_ji>0 to control large
catalyst counts when some substrate is present, and delta_j>0 to control
catalysts when all substrate counts vanish. The small-gain LP remains a
valid sufficient linear-certificate test, but failure of that LP is not a
signal of transient, null-recurrent, or explosive behavior. An exact
three-species, full-rank, non-complex-balanced, LP-infeasible instance is
given in Section 4.

1. Exact family and generator
-----------------------------
There are finite nonempty index sets j=1,...,r and i=1,...,s. All constants
alpha_j, delta_j, p_ji, q_ji, u_ji, v_ji are fixed and strictly positive;
k_ji is a fixed positive integer. Standard stochastic mass-action
propensities use falling factorials (n)_m=n(n-1)...(n-m+1), set to zero
when n<m:

    0 -> A_j:                              alpha_j
    A_j -> 0:                              delta_j a_j
    A_j -> A_j+B_i:                        p_ji a_j
    A_j+B_i -> A_j:                        q_ji a_j b_i
    A_j+B_i -> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i:            u_ji a_j b_i
    (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i -> A_j+B_i:            v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1) b_i.

Every edge has its reverse in the complex graph, so the network is weakly
reversible. The source/product in the last pair differ by k_ji A_j; its
reverse leaves one A_j. As in the supplied family report, the full lattice is
irreducible: clear each B_i with an active A_j+B_i->A_j reaction, then drain
the catalysts; to build a target, immigrate its catalysts, add substrates
with A_j->A_j+B_i, and use a temporary catalyst if the target has none.
Every finite prescribed path has positive probability because the relevant
rates are positive and finite. Hence the unique communicating class is
N_0^(r+s), and it is closed.

Let A=sum_j a_j, I_0=1_{A=0}, I_j=1_{a=e_j},
alpha_sum=sum_j alpha_j, and choose arbitrary catalyst weights lambda_j>0.
Write H=sum_j alpha_j lambda_j. For numbers R_i>1 to be selected below,
define

    V(a,b) = sum_j lambda_j a_j + sum_i R_i^(b_i) + K I_0.

Choose K>(H+1)/alpha_sum. This is strictly positive and norm-like: bounded
sublevel sets have bounded catalyst coordinates and bounded substrate
coordinates because each R_i>1.

Set c_i=R_i-1 and d_i=1-R_i^(-1). The indicator has exact generator

    L I_0 = -alpha_sum I_0 + sum_j delta_j I_j.

Only immigration leaves A=0, and only death of the sole catalyst enters
A=0. No other reaction can change I_0; in particular reverse autocatalysis
leaves one A_j. For substrate i define

    g_ji(m) = p_ji c_i R_i^m - q_ji d_i m R_i^m
                 + lambda_j k_ji u_ji m,       m in N_0.

Directly summing the rate times the exact jump in V gives

    L V = H - K alpha_sum I_0 + K sum_j delta_j I_j
          + sum_j a_j(-lambda_j delta_j + sum_i g_ji(b_i))
          - sum_(j,i) lambda_j k_ji v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1) b_i.        (1)

No continuum approximation is used. In particular the last term is exactly
zero when a_j<k_ji+1.

2. Choice of exponential weights and finite-set drift
-----------------------------------------------------
Fix any lambda_j>0, for example lambda_j=1. Since there are finitely many
positive p_ji and delta_j, choose rational R_i>1 sufficiently close to 1
that for every j

    chi_j := lambda_j delta_j - sum_i p_ji(R_i-1) > 0.             (2)

This choice is always possible and does not impose any relationship between
p,q,u,delta. Because q_ji>0 and R_i>1,

    g_ji(m) -> -infinity as m -> infinity,                         (3)

and consequently M_ji:=sup_(m in N_0) g_ji(m)<infinity. At b_i=0 for every
i, the coefficient of a_j in (1) is exactly -chi_j<0. This is the only
condition imposed on R_i; it is a small exponential-weight choice, not the
linear small-gain LP.

Choose K as above. At A=0, all a_j terms and I_j vanish, so

    L V(0,b)=H-K alpha_sum < -1                                 (4)

for every substrate vector b, including the unbounded frozen face.

Now consider any escaping sequence of states with A>=1. If the substrate
vector is unbounded, pass to a subsequence and choose i_0 with
b_i0->infinity. By (3), for each j,

    -lambda_j delta_j + sum_i g_ji(b_i) -> -infinity,

because all other g_ji are bounded above. The reverse-factorial terms in
(1) are nonpositive. The only positive boundary term is at A=1 and is at
most K delta_max, a fixed constant. Therefore L V->-infinity on this
subsequence.

If the substrate vector is bounded, pass to a subsequence on which b is
constant. If b=0, (2) gives

    L V <= H - sum_j chi_j a_j,

for A>=2, which tends to -infinity as A->infinity. If b is fixed and
nonzero, choose i_0 with b_i0>=1. For each j the j-th unbounded catalyst
coordinate has a negative term

    -lambda_j k_ji0 v_ji0 b_i0 (a_j)_(k_ji0+1),

of degree k_ji0+1>=2, whereas every other term in (1) involving that a_j
is at most linear in a_j for this fixed b. It follows that L V->-infinity
whenever any a_j->infinity. Since there are finitely many j and b is fixed,
every escaping sequence in this case has drift tending to -infinity as well.

Thus the set F={x: L V(x)>-1} is finite: an infinite such set would contain
an escaping sequence, contradicted by the preceding cases or by (4). This
also shows L V is globally bounded above: it is at most -1 off F and has a
finite maximum on F. The boundary correction is crucial for (4), while the
high-order reverse reaction is crucial only on fixed nonzero substrate
strata. No LP feasibility is used.

3. Physical-time nonexplosion and recurrence
--------------------------------------------
Let C=max(0,max_(x in F) LV(x)); then LV<=C on the full lattice. For
tau_R=inf{t>=0:V(X_t)>=R}, stopped Dynkin, localized on the finite V-sublevel
set and its finite one-jump boundary, gives

    E_x V(X_(t wedge tau_R)) <= V(x)+Ct,
    P_x(tau_R<=t) <= (V(x)+Ct)/R.                                (5)

All rates are finite at every state. A V-sublevel is finite, so its total
exit rates are bounded; infinitely many jumps cannot occur while the chain
stays in that finite set. Since V is norm-like, explosion would require
exiting every V-sublevel in finite time. Letting R tend to infinity in (5)
rules out explosion. This is a physical-time argument using the CTMC
generator and the actual holding rates; it is not an embedded-jump-chain
claim.

The chain is irreducible and nonexplosive, and V is positive with LV<=-1
outside the finite set F. The standard continuous-time Foster criterion
therefore gives positive recurrence of the whole lattice. Equivalently,
stopped Dynkin before tau_F=inf{t:X_t in F} gives E_x tau_F<=V(x) for
x outside F; the usual finite-set irreducible CTMC criterion then gives
positive recurrence. The invariant probability exists and is unique, but no
closed form or stationary-tail estimate is claimed for general rates.

The argument is pointwise for each fixed finite rate vector. It gives no
uniform bound on the size of F, on expected return times over a rate box, or
on mixing. Positive recurrence for every fixed parameter value does not by
itself imply uniform tightness of the corresponding stationary laws over a
varying parameter set; this report proves neither uniform tightness nor its
failure. No uniform-in-parameter extension is claimed here.

4. Exact three-species witness outside the LP and outside complex balance
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Take r=1,s=2,k_11=k_12=1 and

    alpha=delta=1,
    p_1=p_2=1, q_1=q_2=2,
    u_1=u_2=1, v_1=v_2=2.

The network has three species, full stoichiometric rank, and weakly
reversible reaction graph. The supplied linear small-gain inequalities
would require

    lambda > w_1+w_2,
    2w_1>lambda and 2w_2>lambda,

which is impossible because the last two inequalities imply
w_1+w_2>lambda. This is exact LP infeasibility; it is not a stochastic
nonrecurrence result.

This selected rate vector is also not complex balanced. The zero complex
and A complex pair forces a=alpha/delta=1. The leaf complex 2A+B_i in each
autocatalytic reversible pair forces u a=v a^2, hence a=u/v=1/2. These
conditions conflict. Thus product-form complex-balance recurrence is not
the explanation for the example.

Use lambda=1, R_1=R_2=6/5, K=3. The weight choice satisfies
sum_i p_i(R_i-1)=2/5<delta. Define

    g(m)=(6/5)^m(1/5-m/3)+m.

Equation (1) becomes the exact identity

    L V(a,b_1,b_2) = 1-a+a[g(b_1)+g(b_2)]
                      -2a(a-1)(b_1+b_2)
                      -3 1_{a=0}+3 1_{a=1}.                     (6)

For 0<=m<=9, direct rational evaluation gives max g(m)=5156/3125<2.
Also g(10)=-459022174/48828125<-9 and, for m>=10,

    g(m+1)-g(m) <= 1-(77/75)(6/5)^m < 0,

so g(m)<-9 for every m>=10. These exact bounds imply LV<=-1 outside

    F={ (a,b_1,b_2): 1<=a<=3, 0<=b_1,b_2<=9 },

a set of 300 states. Indeed, at a=0 the drift is -2. If some b_i>=10,
then g(b_1)+g(b_2)<=-7, so the drift is at most -4 for a=1 and at most
-15 for a>=2, before the additional nonpositive reverse term. If both
b_i<=9 and b_1+b_2=0, (6) reduces for a>=2 to 1-3a/5, which is <=-7/5
for a>=4. If both b_i<=9 and b_1+b_2>=1, then for a>=4,

    LV <= 1+3a-2a(a-1) = 1+5a-2a^2 <= -11.

This is an exact finite outside-LP, non-complex-balanced positive-recurrence
instance by Sections 2--3. No stationary law was computed explicitly; the
Foster theorem gives existence and uniqueness. The script
validate_exact.py uses Fraction arithmetic to verify all displayed
finite-value identities, the b>=10 monotonicity bound at its initial step,
the LP contradiction, and LV<=-1 on a large exact grid outside F. The
analytic inequalities above, rather than grid coverage, prove the all-state
claim.

5. Scope, status, and next gate
------------------------------
This result upgrades the exact named family beyond the earlier conditional
small-gain certificate: all fixed strictly positive finite rate vectors in
the family are now covered by a different Lyapunov function. It does not
prove recurrence of every weakly reversible stochastic mass-action network
or classify networks outside this family. It is not a transient/null/
explosive counterexample. Failure of the small-gain LP is shown to be only
failure of that linear certificate, not a rate threshold for recurrence.

Supplied inputs: finite r,s,k_ji and fixed exact positive rates. The proof
uses weights lambda_j>0, rational R_i>1 satisfying (2), and K. Since the
number of rates is finite and all p,delta are finite/positive, R_i can be
chosen arbitrarily close to one. For rational input rates, a rational choice
can be found and checked by exact arithmetic; for general real rates, the
argument is pointwise existential and no input-access or precision-cost
model for locating a certificate is claimed. No simulation, volume scaling,
or event-index approximation is used. No explicit complexity bound is
claimed for the thresholds defining F; they may be very large for small
q_ji or v_ji. The small exact checker has constant-sized rational arithmetic
per fixture and is finite evidence only.

Falsifier: a statewise generator discrepancy in (1); failure of g_ji(m) to
tend to -infinity under q_ji>0,R_i>1; a high-catalyst sequence with some
substrate present on which the reverse factorial term fails to dominate;
or an explosion path contradicting the localized proof (5). A numerical LP
failure, a finite simulation, or an embedded-chain observation alone does
not falsify the stochastic theorem.

A separate support-dependent argument for a sparse paired-edge graph is
recorded in SPARSE_EXTENSION.txt. It uses a different indicator-corrected
substrate weight and treats isolated substrate counts as class labels. It is
not used in the complete-pair theorem above and remains a separate
INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE pending independent review; no sparse conclusion
should be inferred by simply deleting terms from (1). Its exact switching
generator is checked on 4096 states by validate_sparse.py; that finite check
does not validate its all-state escaping-sequence proof.

Primary source used for the final CTMC step:

* D. F. Anderson and J. Kim, “Some network conditions for positive
  recurrence of stochastically modeled reaction networks,” arXiv:1710.11263v3
  (22 Aug 2018), https://arxiv.org/html/1710.11263v3 . Theorem 3.1 states
  the finite-set continuous-time Foster criterion: a positive function with
  generator drift <=-1 outside a finite set gives positive recurrence in
  each closed irreducible component. Our proof separately supplies
  nonexplosion before applying it.
