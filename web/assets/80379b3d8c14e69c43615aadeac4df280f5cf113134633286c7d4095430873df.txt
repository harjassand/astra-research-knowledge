Definitions: C(E) is MAX-PRIOR Holevo capacity in NATS; b2(E) is the infimum
worst HALF marginal trace distance of ONE full-domain CPTP H->H tensor H;
e(E) is the infimum worst HALF trace reconstruction error of ONE
full-domain EB H->H. No label is supplied. Correlated outputs are legal.

There are exact finite faithful families with b2->0 and e->1. Thus no
unrestricted dimension-independent implication e<=g(b2), g(b)->0, can
hold. Their capacity is quantified here. For the standard d=L*2^(L-1)
choice, C=Theta(2^L ln L/L) whereas b2=Theta(1/L). In particular this
family is FAR from saturating C+1>=e^2 ln(1/b2)/160000. It supplies no
matching exponential-capacity lower family, and does not refute any
universal e^2<=K C b^alpha or a logarithmic-C rate. Such rates are UNKNOWN.

2. EXACT FAMILY AND FULL-DOMAIN BROADCASTER

Let L>=2,d>=2 be integers, N_j=2^j (j=0,...,L-1), m=2^(L-1),
 H=directsum_(j=0)^(L-1) Sym^(N_j)(C^d),
 D_N=binom(N+d-1,N), D_tot=sum_j D_(N_j).
For a unit direction psi in C^d define
 rho_psi=(1/L) directsum_j (|psi><psi|)^(tensor N_j).    (1)
All promised states have entropy ln L. Initially let E consist of the
full projective orbit; Section 6 gives a finite exact algebraic family
with the same bounds and EXACT same capacity.

Let P_j be the sector projector, and fix any bottom-sector density omega.
For j>=1, grouping tensor coordinates into two equal groups restricts to
an isometry
 V_j:Sym^(N_j)(C^d)->Sym^(N_j/2)(C^d) tensor Sym^(N_j/2)(C^d)
with V_j psi^(tensor N_j)=psi^(tensor N_j/2) tensor psi^(tensor N_j/2).
The output sectors are both embedded in H. Define on ALL X in B(H)

 B(X)=Tr(P_0 X) omega tensor omega
                   +sum_(j=1)^(L-1) V_j P_j X P_j V_j*. (2)

This is CP. Pinching kills all off-diagonal flag coherences and preserves
trace; each V_j is an isometry and the replacement term has the input
trace. Hence B is TP on the FULL domain, including arbitrary unpromised
inputs. Its two equal marginals applied to (1) have discrepancy

 B_j(rho_psi)-rho_psi
      =(omega in bottom sector - psi^(tensor m) in top sector)/L.
The two terms have orthogonal support and each has trace one. Therefore

 sup_(psi,j) T(B_j(rho_psi),rho_psi)=1/L EXACTLY,
 and b2(E)<=1/L.                                     (3)

This is a statement about marginals; the joint output is not required
to approximate rho_psi tensor rho_psi. Exact sector flags and the fixed
bottom replacement are part of the supplied channel data.

3. EB LOWER BOUND, INCLUDING MIXED PREPARATIONS AND CROSS-SECTOR INPUTS

Let D:B(H)->B(C^d) pinch the flag and retain one tensor coordinate in
each symmetric sector. D is CPTP and D(rho_psi)=|psi><psi|. For any
full-domain EB Lambda, D Lambda restricted to sector Sym^N is an EB
N-copy-to-one channel, with arbitrary POVM effects M_y on Sym^N and
arbitrary prepared normalized densities sigma_y on C^d.

The projective Haar moment is
 integral psi^(tensor (N+1)) dpsi=P_sym^(N+1)/D_(N+1).
It follows that the averaged pure-state score of this channel is

 (1/D_(N+1)) sum_y Tr[(M_y tensor sigma_y)P_sym^(N+1)]
 <= (1/D_(N+1)) sum_y Tr M_y
 =D_N/D_(N+1)=(N+1)/(N+d).                            (4)

The inequality uses 0<=P_sym<=I on Sym^N tensor C^d and positivity of
M_y tensor sigma_y. Thus it covers arbitrary MIXED prepared states and
does not assume the EB output stays in a symmetric representation.
Restriction of a global cross-sector POVM simply compresses its effects
to the sector, and the compressed effects still sum to its identity.
Therefore no flag-by-flag restriction on the original EB channel is imposed.

If Lambda has worst HALF error e0 on E, trace contractivity and the
pure-state test give, for every psi,
 <psi|D Lambda(rho_psi)|psi> >=1-e0.
Averaging over psi and using (4) in every sector yields

 e(E)>=1-(1/L)sum_j (N_j+1)/(N_j+d)
      >=1-(m+1)/(m+d).                               (5)

The well-known pure-state estimation bound (4) is attributed to primary
estimation literature, e.g. Hayashi--Hashimoto--Horibe quant-ph/0410207v3;
the short projector proof above is sufficient logically. No optimality
or historical novelty is claimed for that bound.

4. A SELF-CONTAINED LOWER BOUND ON THE OPTIMAL b2

The source displayed B gives an exact error, not an exact optimum. We
reconstruct a sufficient matching-order lower bound without importing
a universal cloning theorem. Let an arbitrary candidate broadcaster
have worst HALF error b0 on E. Apply D to both outputs. On the bottom
input sector this gives an arbitrary CPTP channel Q:C^d->C^d tensor C^d.

Let J_Q>=0 be its UNNORMALIZED Choi matrix on A,B,C:
 Tr_(BC)J_Q=I_A, Tr J_Q=d.
Let P_AB and P_AC be normalized maximally entangled projectors, tensored
with the identity on the unused third system when used below. The average
of its two one-copy pure-state scores over projective Haar directions is
Tr(J_Q Omega), where

 Omega=[I_ABC+(d/2)(P_AB tensor I_C+P_AC tensor I_B)]/[d(d+1)]. (6)

Indeed integral (psi^T tensor psi)=[I+dP]/[d(d+1)] follows by partial
transpose from the two-copy symmetric moment. The unused output is
traced, which contributes its identity. This fixes every normalization
and transpose in (6).

The two projection ranges in (6) have orthonormal bases
 u_c=|EPR>_(AB) tensor |c>_C,
 v_b=|EPR>_(AC) tensor |b>_B.
Their overlap matrix is <u_c,v_b>=delta_(cb)/d. Therefore the sum of
the two projections has eigenvalues 1+1/d and 1-1/d on their span
(and zero on its orthogonal complement). Its norm is 1+1/d. Thus

 ||Omega||=(d+3)/[2d(d+1)],
 Tr(J_Q Omega)<=(d+3)/[2(d+1)].                        (7)

The other L-1 input sectors have average decoded score at most one.
On the full promised mixture, each decoded marginal score is at least
1-b0. Hence
 1-b0<= [(L-1)+(d+3)/(2(d+1))]/L,
and therefore

 (d-1)/[2L(d+1)] <= b2(E) <=1/L.                      (8)

This argument allows completely general outputs and any full-domain
joint channel. It does not impose a symmetric-output restriction. The
same one-copy optimum is established prior art in Keyl--Werner
quant-ph/9807010v1; (6)--(7) is the self-contained bound used here.
We do NOT infer the sharper source-reported b2~1/L from (8).

5. EXACT MAX-PRIOR HOLEVO CAPACITY AND ASYMPTOTICS

For any prior on directions, the mean retains every flag weight 1/L.
Writing its normalized block means as mu_(N_j),
 S(mean rho)=ln L+(1/L)sum_j S(mu_(N_j)).
Since each input state has entropy ln L, Holevo information is at most
(1/L)sum_j ln D_(N_j). Haar mean gives mu_N=I_(Sym^N)/D_N for every
sector simultaneously, so this bound is attained. The finite moment
design in Section 6 attains it with an actual finite prior. Thus

 C(E_(L,d))=(1/L)sum_(j=0)^(L-1) ln binom(N_j+d-1,N_j) EXACTLY. (9)

This is MAX-PRIOR capacity, not merely the Holevo value of a chosen
possibly nonmaximal prior. The maximizing finite prior and all entropy
arguments are supplied explicitly by the construction.

Choose d=Lm=L*2^(L-1). By (3),(5),(8), b2=Theta(1/L) and e->1. The
elementary binomial bounds
 (d/N)^N<=D_N<=[e(d+N)/N]^N
give the explicit capacity interval

 (m ln L)/L <= C(E_(L,Lm))
       <=(2m/L)[1+ln(L+1)+ln 2].                      (10)

For the lower bound use only the top block N=m. For the upper bound
write N=m*2^(-r), r=0,...,L-1, note d+N<=(L+1)m, and use
sum_r N<2m, sum_r rN<2m. This proves

 C=Theta(2^L ln L/L),
 ln(C+1)=(L-1)ln 2-ln L+ln ln L+O(1)=Theta(L),
 b2=Theta(1/ln(C+1)),
 ln(1/b2)=Theta(ln L)=Theta(ln ln(C+1)).               (11)

The family therefore requires enormously MORE capacity than the necessary
ln(1/b2) scale in the new general theorem. An exp[-Theta(C/e^2)] lower
family would have a qualitatively different capacity/error relation.
Neither (10) nor the source obstruction establishes such sharpness.
Every fixed-alpha expression C*b2^alpha diverges on this family, so it
does not falsify a prospective polynomial-in-b bound weighted by C.

6. FINITE EXACT ALGEBRAIC DIRECTIONS AND CHANNEL MATRICES

Set t=m+1, q=ceil((t+1)/2), r=t+1. The following finite product
quadrature realizes the degree-t complex projective moment exactly.
This is an explicit witness for existence, not a claim of efficient
design construction or a newly discovered quadrature theorem.

The squared coordinate magnitudes of a Haar vector have the uniform
Dirichlet(1,...,1) law. In stick-breaking coordinates
 p_1=y_1,
 p_a=y_a product_(h<a)(1-y_h), 2<=a<=d-1,
 p_d=product_(h<d)(1-y_h),
the y_a are independent Beta(1,d-a). Their density is
 (d-a)(1-y_a)^(d-a-1), 0<y_a<1.
For each a use q-node Gauss--Jacobi quadrature for this polynomial weight.
It has positive algebraic nodes and positive algebraic weights and is
exact for one-variable polynomials of degree <=2q-1>=t. These finite
nodes are the roots of an orthogonal polynomial with rational coefficients;
the quadrature weights are specified exactly by its usual moment equations.

For completeness, Gaussian quadrature existence here needs no black-box
acquisition claim. Gram--Schmidt for the positive rational-moment inner
product gives a monic degree-q orthogonal polynomial with rational
coefficients. It has q distinct roots inside (0,1): otherwise the product
of its interior sign-change factors has degree below q and its product
with the orthogonal polynomial has constant sign and nonzero integral,
contradicting orthogonality. Use these roots and weights integral(l_i w),
where l_i is the degree-(q-1) Lagrange cardinal polynomial. Division of
any degree-(2q-1) polynomial by the orthogonal polynomial proves exactness.
In particular each weight equals integral(l_i^2 w)>0. The nodes and
weights are algebraic because all moments of this polynomial weight are
rational. This proves the required exact finite quadrature property.

Independently choose phases k_a in {0,...,r-1}, a=1,...,d-1, uniformly,
and put
 psi_a=sqrt(p_a)*exp(2pi i k_a/r), a<d,
 psi_d=sqrt(p_d).
The design has at most (q r)^(d-1) weighted labels, all with algebraic
coordinates, and exact normalized product weights. For monomials with
multiindices alpha,beta of total degree t, phase averaging forces
alpha_a=beta_a for all a<d, because each difference lies in [-t,t] and
r>t. Equal total degrees then force alpha_d=beta_d. For surviving terms
the amplitude is product_a p_a^(alpha_a). Its degree in each y_h is
alpha_h+sum_(a>h)alpha_a<=t, so each Jacobi quadrature is exact.
Consequently

 sum_x w_x (|psi_x><psi_x|)^(tensor t)=P_sym^t/D_t.

Partial trace supplies every lower moment. In particular moments N+1
for all sector sizes prove the EB lower bound (4), moment 2 proves the
Choi bound (6), and moments N prove the exact capacity (9). The SAME
finite family and SAME prior meet all three obligations simultaneously.

In normalized occupation basis |alpha>, |alpha|=2n, the splitter matrix
has exact algebraic entries

 V|alpha>=sum_(beta+gamma=alpha,|beta|=|gamma|=n)
   sqrt[ multinomial(n;beta) multinomial(n;gamma)
                         /multinomial(2n;alpha)] |beta>|gamma>. (12)

The multivariate Vandermonde identity proves V*V=I. Different alpha
have disjoint occupation-sum support. This gives executable exact matrices
of (2), without hand-waving about a splitter defined only on promised
pure vectors. Bottom replacement has ordinary rank-one Kraus operators;
their adjoint products together with the splitter sectors sum to I_H.

The accompanying replay script uses the exact small instance d=2,L=2:
H dimension 5, output dimension 25, t=3, eight directions from
y=(1+/-1/sqrt(3))/2 and fourth-root phases. It checks Kraus TP, the
third moment and exact broadcaster discrepancy. This finite diagnostic
does not materialize the asymptotic sequence or certify the general proof.

7. FAITHFUL VERSION AND CAPACITY STABILITY

Let nu=I_H/D_tot, choose eta=L^(-3), and set
 rho'_psi=(1-eta)rho_psi+eta nu. Every rho'_psi is FULLY faithful on H.
The same all-input broadcaster satisfies

 b2(E')<=(1-eta)/L+eta.

For any single channel T, contractivity gives
 T(T rho,rho)<=T(T rho',rho')+2eta.
Applying this separately to any EB channel and both marginals of any B
transfers the earlier LOWER bounds:

 e(E')>=1-(m+1)/(m+d)-2eta,
 b2(E')>=(d-1)/[2L(d+1)]-2eta.                       (13)

For capacities, adjoin the Bernoulli mixture flag Z temporarily. It is
independent of label X; conditional on Z=0 the original experiment is
present and on Z=1 the fixed nu is present. Thus
 I(X:ZQ)=(1-eta)chi(E,prior).
Discarding Z loses I(X:Z|Q), which lies between zero and h2(eta), since
Z is classical. Taking the original maximizing finite prior proves

 (1-eta)C(E)-h2(eta)<=C(E')<=(1-eta)C(E).              (14)

The upper bound holds for every prior by the same flag argument. There
is also an exact entropy formula. Put a=(1-eta)/L,s=eta/D_tot. Each
promised state has eigenvalue a+s with multiplicity L and s with
multiplicity D_tot-L. Its maximizing mean has, in sector N, the eigenvalue
a/D_N+s with multiplicity D_N. Therefore C(E') is the entropy of that
mean minus the entropy of the displayed common spectrum. The same finite
design prior attains all the block means, so this is exact max-prior
capacity, not only an estimate. In particular (10)--(11) retain their
asymptotic order for the faithful families. No large entropy arrays need
to be instantiated to specify these exact formulas.

8. OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS DOES NOT HIDE THE KNOWN OBSTRUCTION

For the finite design family, let sigma be ANY element of closed conv(E).
Its top-sector trace is 1/L. For each design label psi, use the effect
F_psi projecting onto psi^(tensor m) in the top flag. On that state
p=1/L. Write q_psi=Tr(F_psi sigma). The design mean gives
 sum_psi w_psi q_psi=Tr(sigma_top)/D_m=1/(L D_m).
Jensen for the convex function -ln q, including q=0 as infinity, implies

 sup_(rho in E) D_obs(rho||sigma)
  >= sum_psi w_psi (1/L)ln[(1/L)/q_psi]
  >= (ln D_m)/L.

Taking the infimum over convex-hull centers and using the Holevo upper
comparison from Jain--Nayak gives

 (ln D_m)/L <= R(E) <= C(E)+ln 2.                    (15)

For d=Lm, (10) shows R(E)=Theta(C(E)). Thus this counterexample does
not defeat the observational-radius extension. The bounded-radius /
unbounded-capacity family in OBSERVATIONAL_RADIUS_PROOF.txt is a different,
already commuting family and must not be conflated with this obstruction.
