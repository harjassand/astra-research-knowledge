EXACT DOBRUSHIN CONTROL: EXTRA HEIGHT VALUES BREAK QUENCHED NEAR SLACK
An analytic counterexample to an extra-value extension, NOT to the
sign-only conditional-sparse theorem. No sampling or numerical solver.

1. DOMAIN, TRUE PINS AND A LEGAL FIXED-NE SIGN PREFIX

Use free square vertices F={1,2,3,4}^2 and the surrounding square
boundary at x or y in{0,5}. Put+lambda on the entire left side and
the portions of the top/bottom with x<=2; put-lambda on the entire
right side and the top/bottom portions with x>=3. Corners are assigned
consistently by the adjacent side rule. Take ANY0<lambda<=1/2.
These are two Dobrushin boundary arcs, and each boundary junction
edge has magnitude2lambda<=1. Original pins are all<=1/2.

Fix the NE diagonal in each cell. Start the sign strand at the
bottom boundary crossing(2,0)+,(3,0)-, and prescribe the following
successive new queries:
  lower triangle in row0: (3,1) negative;
  upper triangle in row0: (2,1) positive;
  lower triangle in row1: v=(3,2) negative;
  upper triangle in row1: p=(2,2) positive.
The successive exits are the diagonal(2,0),(3,1), then the NN edge
(2,1),(3,1), then the diagonal(2,1),v, then the NN edge p,v.
Stop after that exit. This is a legal finite sign-prefix stopping rule,
with no numerical height record and no affine crossing ratio.

2. THIS SIGN FIBER ALLOWS A POSITIVE-VOLUME BAND OF HIGH p VALUES

Fix eta in(0,1/2), and take any q in(1-eta,1). Assign left free
columns x<=2 the height+lambda, except h_p=q. Assign all right
free columns x>=3 the height-(1-q)/2. This has the recorded signs.
Every original NN edge involving a free vertex is STRICTLY legal:
  p-v difference = (1+q)/2 <1;
  p-other-left-neighbor difference = |q-lambda| <1;
  ordinary left/right difference = lambda+(1-q)/2 <1;
  right-free/right-boundary difference = |lambda-(1-q)/2| <1.
All other free-edge differences are0 or are already listed. Fixed
boundary junctions obey2lambda<=1. Thus this is an interior point
of the free-coordinate Lipschitz/sign body, and every q in the open
band has a positive-volume neighborhood with the same sign prefix.
The conditional density of h_p is positive on this band: fixing q
leaves an open neighborhood of the other15 free coordinates, and
Fubini gives its positive section volume.

3. AFTER AN EXTRA EXACT-VALUE REVEAL THE NEXT EDGE IS NEAR CONTACT

Now, AFTER the legal sign prefix, reveal the extra exact height h_p=q.
This is not an observation in the sign-prefix filtration. The remaining
law is the uniform pinned section with q fixed and the previous signs.
The recorded negative sign of v and the NN constraint p-v force
   q-1 <= h_v <=0,
and therefore
   |h_p-h_v| = q-h_v >=q >1-eta.                   (3.1)
This holds for every configuration in that conditional section, whose
remaining free volume is positive by Section2. Consequently, for ANY
proposed uniform microscopic deficit s>0, choosing eta<s gives
   P(|grad_{p,v} h|>1-s | sign prefix, h_p=q)=1
on a positive-probability band of values q. The conditioning on each
exact q is understood by the regular conditional density, not an
isolated probability-zero-event assertion.

The new fixed value q is even bounded by1. That bound suffices for
long-pair bounded coupling but does not make q an original small pin.
The tested edge has one still free endpoint v and one newly fixed
high endpoint p. Its strict-filling centre at free value0 has tested
gradient q, not<=1/2. This pin lies immediately adjacent to the tested
edge and violates the declared protection/local pin-distance criterion.

4. ORIGINAL-BOUNDARY DISTANCE DOES NOT REPAIR THE ERROR

The construction embeds in a square of arbitrary size by placing the
vertical boundary-sign split near the centre, using the same left/right
free heights, and placing p,v along that split arbitrarily far from the
original boundary. A straight finite sign prefix reaches p,v: at each
visited cell it queries one negative right vertex and one positive left
vertex. Raising the chosen p to q keeps the same strict NN inequalities
and the same legal recorded signs. Thus distance from the ORIGINAL
boundary alone does not eliminate the adjacent NEWLY revealed pin.

This does not contradict the conditional-sparse theorem for the legal
SIGN prefix, where p remains movable. It also does not contradict a
version restricted to patches protected from additional high pins.
It decisively rules out promoting one-time annealed value freezing to
a uniform quenched statement for all exact-value transcripts or to
arbitrary box selections made from those transcripts.

Two further scope controls are immediate. Original fixed-fixed boundary
junction edges at lambda=1/2 have unit gradient deterministically and
must be excluded from eligible tests. NE diagonals are drawing edges;
they are not original NN unit constraints and cannot be tested as if
their allowable gradient were1.
