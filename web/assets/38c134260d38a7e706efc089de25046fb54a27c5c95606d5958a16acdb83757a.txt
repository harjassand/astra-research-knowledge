LUNA AUDIT — FINITE BELL WORDS, HAAR CONCENTRATION, ENSEMBLE
2026-10-08

Scope and model

I audited the finite two-use Bell transfer in RESULT.txt and
CONSTANT_AUDIT.txt for Gamma=F_64 x F_64, K=256, d=64, k=2. The stated
finite law is 128 independent Haar U(N) matrices (64 generators per leg),
with branches (g_i^{+/-1},1) and (1,h_j^{+/-1}); pi_N acts on
C^N tensor C^N. This is an actual U(N) model, not an O(N)/SO(N) model.
The distinction matters below. Bordenave–Collins' expectation machinery
covers unitary matrices (their Theorem 1.1; their source also treats the
orthogonal case), while the concentration step used here is the unitary
product-Haar concentration theorem of Meckes, Theorem 5.17. The free
radius/quotient premises are properties of the abstract free group and do
not require replacing this finite U(N) law by an orthogonal one.

Word length and mean

A two-use Bell Gram entry is K^-2 tau_(N^2)(pi_N(w)), with w a product of
four branch letters of the form s^-1 t u^-1 v. Thus every required word has
length at most four; there are at most K^4=2^32 labels. Reduce separately
in the two free factors. For a reduced nonidentity word of length <=4 in
independent Haar U(N) generators, invariance under independent scalar
phases makes its normalized-trace mean zero unless each generator occurs
equally often with and without adjoint. No reduced balanced word of length
<4 exists; at length 4 the only nontrivial possibility is a commutator of
two distinct generators, up to inverse/cyclic variants. For that case,
Schur averaging gives E_U[U V U*]=tau_N(V) I and hence
E tau_N(U V U* V*)=E|tau_N(V)|^2=1/N^2. Therefore
|E tau_N(w)|<=N^-2. The tensor-leg trace factors, so a nonidentity word in
the product has mean at most N^-2 as well; identity words have trace 1
exactly, matching the free trace. These arguments include words supported
on one leg and words whose other leg reduces to identity.

The N^-2 statement must not be transferred to Haar O(N). For example,
for Haar O in O(N), the reduced word g^2 has E tau_N(O^2)=1/N, from the
second orthogonal entry moment. This word is among the Bell labels: choose
s=g^-1, t=g, and u=v. This falsifies the claimed N^-2 mean bound
for an orthogonal finite ensemble. It does not falsify the stated U(N)
model. An orthogonal variant would need a separate uniform mean bound and
recomputed constants; at the enormous stated N, an O(1/N) bias could be
numerically small enough, but that is not the proof audited here.

Lipschitz, tail, and union constants

For f_w(U,V)=tau_(N^2)(pi_N(w)), telescope the at-most-four letter
positions. For a perturbation of one generator, normalized trace duality
bounds each term by ||Delta U_i||_HS/sqrt(N) (and likewise on the other
leg). Cauchy–Schwarz in the product HS metric therefore gives
L(f_w)<=4/sqrt(N); no factor sqrt(128) is needed for a fixed word with at
most four positions. Apply Meckes Theorem 5.17 to plus/minus Re f_w and
plus/minus Im f_w. If |f_w-E f_w|>delta/2, one of those four one-sided
events has deviation >delta/(2 sqrt(2)). With Meckes' exponent
(N-2)t^2/(24 L^2), this yields

  P(|f_w-E f_w|>delta/2)
       <= 4 exp(-(N-2) N delta^2 / 3072).

Combining the N^-2 mean bias with this concentration event gives
|f_w-tau_F(w)|<=delta whenever N^-2<=delta/2. Union over at most K^4
labels has failure at most 1/6 if

  N(N-2) >= 3072 ln(24 K^4)/delta^2.

Using the package's delta=5.39570936800456e-12, the script's sufficient
integer threshold is N>=51,728,048,483,335 (the concentration condition
dominates the mean-bias condition). At the stated N=p^1104, p=4,228,297,946,534,
this gate is overwhelmingly satisfied. I reran rough_gate.py; it confirms
the displayed Bell threshold and log2(N)=46,305.30845. The score-net
concentration formula also matches the same Meckes constant: for 128
variables and score Lipschitz L<=2 sqrt(128)||Q_F||, the exponent has
denominator 24 L^2=96*128||Q_F||^2, matching the package's 96dk with
dk=128. Its stated one-sided tail is centered at E||Q_N||; the separate
Bordenave–Collins expectation estimate is not itself treated as a tail.

Spectral/entropy edge check

The ideal Bell Gram spectrum has zero eigenvalues and nontrivial
multiplicities (rank 48,897 in dimension 65,536). The transfer uses an
entrywise-to-trace-distance estimate followed by Audenaert continuity on
the full 65,536-dimensional density matrix, not a pointwise log-eigenvalue
perturbation or inverse on the support. Thus zero eigenvalues and degeneracy
do not create a hidden logarithmic singularity in this step. The stated
entry tolerance and trace-distance conversion are numerically reproduced
by rough_gate.py.

Status, assumptions, and strongest falsifier

Within the explicit independent-Haar-U(N) model, I found no gap in the
word-length, mean, Lipschitz, concentration, or finite-union steps. The
constants 3072 and 96dk follow directly from Meckes' 24 L^2 denominator
and the displayed Lipschitz bounds. Script verification is numerical
corroboration, not the proof. This receipt does not independently validate
the imported free-radius or Bell-quotient theorems, nor the separate
Bordenave–Collins expectation-constant extraction (which is recorded in
CONSTANT_AUDIT.txt). The strongest direct falsifier is the orthogonal
counterexample E tau(O^2)=1/N: any claim that the N^-2 word-mean argument
also covers Haar O(N) is false. If a real/orthogonal realization is
required by the intended interface, the finite U(N) argument does not
establish it.

Cost/status: read-only source audit plus one local Decimal script execution;
no Haar matrices sampled or materialized. Conditional finite probabilistic
existence under the package's free spectral premises remains the scope;
there is no explicit seed or practical channel construction here.
