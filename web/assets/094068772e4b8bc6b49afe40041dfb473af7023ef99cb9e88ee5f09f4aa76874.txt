CYCLE 10 PAIRWISE HEIGHT-POTENTIAL AUDIT
Worker: cycle10_recovery_duality_luna, Luna/max
Scope: test whether center-dependent recentered fits admit a stationarity-based two-point potential estimate at the critical center dimension s=m-1. This is a local obstruction search, not an acquired lemma or an ambient regularity proof.

Result. The proposed unweighted estimate

  integral_{x in C} integral |z_x(y)| |x-y|^{-m} dmu(y) dsigma(x)
      <= C * normalized_excess

is false even for an actual stationary integral varifold satisfying the near-flat, Q=2 density-center, and local-fit interface. A small-angle crossing of two planes gives a left side of order theta while the normalized tilt, projected-mass, and squared-height excess is order theta^2. The model has one common exact minimal reference through every center, so it does not refute a tilt-weighted estimate for genuinely center-dependent references.

The radial error that generated the critical endpoint contains the squared tilt e_x. Retaining the height gives the correct first candidate

  P_i(l) = integral_{x in C_i} integral_{B_{Cl}(x)}
             e_{i,x}(y) |z_{i,x}(y)| |x-y|^{-m}
             dmu_i(y) dsigma_i(x),

not the unweighted potential. Logarithmic scale integration also produces a second kernel

  K_m(r,l) = integral_{r/C}^l t^{1-m} dt

against e_{i,x}; it is O(l) for m=1, log(Cl/r) for m=2, and O(r^{2-m}) for m>2. Under the critical Frostman bound sigma_i(B_t)<=C t^{m-1} and the recentering estimate e_{i,x}<=C(e_i+a_i^2), this second term is bounded by C l a_i^2 using the local mass/tilt budget. The first term is the only logarithmic obstruction.

If all centers lie on one common minimal S and the same S is used for every center, then z_x(y)=z(y), |z(y)|<=dist(y,C_i), and the critical Riesz potential of sigma_i is at most C/dist(y,C_i). This gives P_i(l)<=C integral e_i dmu_i<=C a_i^2. That is the exact common-base cancellation from the earlier frozen packet. It is not available for recentered fits that vary with x. The fitting inputs currently give only a local |z_{i,x}(y)|<=C|x-y| bound and the global energy budget; those two estimates leave a logarithm in the pairwise potential. No stationarity estimate controlling that logarithm was acquired here.

The local fitting overlap result compares two centered fits with the moment, tilt, and mass conclusions. It explicitly does not apply automatically to center-passing translated fits: translation can change the first moment by O(a_i). Thus the desired pairwise estimate cannot be obtained by quoting the O(a_i^2) centered-fit overlap bound.

Falsifier status. The two-plane crossing is a valid falsifier of the unweighted candidate, but not of the corrected e_x-weighted estimate. Its center set C is an (m-1)-plane, its center measure is critical Frostman, and its common plane S0 is an admissible exact fit. Consequently it is not evidence for or against the genuinely variable-reference gate. No actual stationary counterexample to P_i(l)<=C a_i^2 was found.

The critical remaining lemma is precisely a uniform estimate P_i(l)<=C a_i^2, or the weaker eta_i P_i(l)=o(a_i^2), derived from stationarity and the center-dependent local fitting construction. A plausible sufficient replacement is a center-set coherence bound |z_{i,x}(y)|<=C dist(y,C_i) for e_i-a.e. y uniformly in x, or a direct Carleson/potential estimate for e_{i,x}|z_{i,x}|. Neither follows from the frozen hypotheses as currently stated. The new packet records the exact model calculation, kernel, source hashes, claim status, and freeze digest.
