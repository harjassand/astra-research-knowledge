# Hostile audit: N33 affine compact-cover exactification

**Scope.** Reconstruct the affine approximation lemma's local-slice / neighborhood / cut proof, and test what does and does not follow effectively for rational diagrams, rational rates, and the diagram-derived sign tolerance. This is an internal mathematical audit. The rational local-stage construction below is a proposed exact algorithmic route, not an implemented or formally verified compiler.

## Verdict

There is a decisive non-effectivity boundary for the lemma in its *universal interface*: it accepts an arbitrary pointwise-positive function (E:[a,b]^m\to(0,\infty)), with no representation, modulus, or computable evaluation rule. A uniform finite-size bound independent of (E) is also impossible. These facts do **not** obstruct exactification for the concrete rational (E_D) used by N33: at a rational slope its value is exactly rational and computable, even though (\inf_{r\in[a,b]^d}E_D(r)=0).

For a fixed rational slice (t), rational finite label family, and offsets that are finite rational sums of rational powers of (h), the compact bad-limit-set step can be replaced by finite exact rational polyhedral computations. I give a vertex / tropical-convex-hull construction below. I have not closed the remaining uniform-in-(t) finite-cover step. For diagram-derived (E_D), a finite piecewise-rational parameter stratification looks viable, but that bridge needs a proof and implementation before calling the whole compiler effective.

## Reconstructed proof spine

The source manuscript's affine lemma (local copy: [endotactic_permanence.tex](../../../permanence_hostile/endotactic_permanence.tex), §2, especially lines 142–367) fixes a slice (\mu(p)=\min_i p_i=t\).

1. **Recursive local family.** Include the type-zero baseline ((t\mathbf 1,0)). For (k=1,\ldots,m-1), choose (t<\beta_k<b), decreasing in (k), with (\beta_k-t<E(r)) for every already-built type-((k-1)) slope. For each (k)-coordinate set (U), apply the dimension-(k) lemma on ([\beta_k,b]^U), then lift its slopes by setting the other coordinates to (t) and its offset to (-h^{\beta_k}+d(h)), where (d=o(h^{\beta_k})).

2. **Activity pins exponents to the right face.** If a type-(k) label is active along (h_n\to0), comparison to the baseline forces every raised limiting exponent (p_i\), (i\in U), to be at least (\beta_k\). Within (U), shared terms cancel and the lower-dimensional induction controls the raised coordinates. A type-((k+1)) competitor on (U\cup\{j\}), with leading offset (-h^{\beta_{k+1}}), rules out any unraised (p_j>\beta_{k+1}). The remaining coordinates are therefore within (\beta_{k+1}-t<E(r)\); at the last type, the sole unraised coordinate equals the minimum (t\). This proves the guarantee on the exact slice, including ties and approaches to faces from outside the cube.

3. **Neighborhood from compact bad limits.** For each label, take (A_\lambda\subset[a,b]^m), the limits of exponent vectors along sequences where it is active and (h\to0\). A diagonal sequence proves (A_\lambda) closed. The finite union
   \[
   B_t=\bigcup_\lambda\{p\in A_\lambda:\|p-r_\lambda\|_\infty\ge E(r_\lambda)\}
   \]
   is compact and misses (\{\mu=t\}\) by the slice guarantee. Thus its minimum distance in the scalar statistic (\mu(p)\) from (t\) is positive. Choose (\rho_t>0\) below that distance and also small enough that every local offset is (o(h^{t+\rho_t})\). This extends the guarantee to limits with (\mu(p)\in(t-\rho_t,t+\rho_t)\). The use of (E) is only at finitely many fixed slopes; continuity is unnecessary.

4. **Finite cover and ordered cuts.** Compactness selects finitely many windows (I_{t_j}=(t_j-\rho_j,t_j+\rho_j)\) covering ([a,b]\). Delete contained windows and order the remaining centers. The retained left and right endpoints increase, consecutive windows overlap, and one can choose rational or real cuts
   \[
   \max(t_j,L_{j+1})<q_j<\min(t_{j+1},R_j).
   \]
   Then each slab ([q_{j-1},q_j]\) lies in its selected window. Unite the local families and shift family (j)'s offsets by (-\sum_{\ell<j}h^{q_\ell}\). Comparing a globally active label to the next baseline rules out \(\mu>q_j\); comparison to the previous baseline rules out \(\mu<q_{j-1}\). Thus its limiting minimum lies in its local window, where the local guarantee applies. All shift exponents exceed (a), so offsets remain (o(h^a)).

The formal reconstruction mirrors this structure and uses `noncomputable section`, `Classical.choice`, and compact finite-cover selection in [GlobalAffineComposition.lean](../../../c4_rational_hostile/formalization/project/N33Affine/GlobalAffineComposition.lean). Kernel-checked existence does not itself yield executable choices.

## Precise effectivity boundary and counterexamples

**Universal (E): no compiler interface is specified.** A Turing procedure cannot consume “an arbitrary positive function” as a finite input. If (E) is instead treated as an oracle, the theorem gives neither a computable oracle representation nor a modulus / effective lower bound. This blocks claiming that the universal Lean theorem is an executable construction. It does not prove that every computable (E), much less the specific rational (E_D), is hard.

**No family-size bound uniform over all positive tolerances.** Already for (m=1), (E(r)\equiv\epsilon>0), any valid (N)-label family must have its (N) open intervals ((r_\lambda-\epsilon,r_\lambda+\epsilon)) cover ([a,b]): fix any (p), choose active labels along (h_n\to0), and pass to a constant-label subsequence. Hence (2N\epsilon>b-a). So (N>(b-a)/(2\epsilon)); there is no bound depending only on (m,a,b) while (\epsilon\) ranges over all positive inputs. This is a lower bound on the universal interface, not on the diagram-specific construction when its full bit-level data are charged.

**No positive uniform floor for N33's concrete (E_D).** For the rational weakly reversible diagram (A\rightleftarrows B), take (D\supset\{e_1,e_2,(1,-1),(-1,1)\}) and the manuscript's
\[
E_D(r)=\min\left(\{1/2\}\cup\{|r\cdot v|/(2\|v\|_1):v\in D,\ r\cdot v\ne0\}\right).
\]
For (r_n=(1/2,1/2+1/n)\), (n\ge3), all components stay in the cube and (E_D(r_n)=1/(4n)\). Each value is positive and exactly rational, but the infimum over the cube is zero. Thus “take the minimum of (E_D) on the cube” and any floating-point fixed-sign margin are invalid. Exact zeros are omitted by definition, so rational ties must be retained exactly.

With rational diagram data, rational (D), and **rational generated slopes**, every queried (E_D(r)) is still a finite minimum of rational numbers. The recursive threshold choices can be rational, offsets remain finite sums of rational powers, and the finite-label value
\[
H=\min\bigl(\{1\}\cup\{|r_\lambda\cdot v|/2:\ r_\lambda\cdot v\ne0\}\bigr)
\]
is a computable positive rational once the family is fixed. For rational (\kappa,K,L\), a rational (h\) satisfying (KLh^H\le\kappa H\) can be found by exact integer-power comparisons. This resolves only the later sign/gap cutoff after the finite family exists; it does not compute the family, neighborhood windows, or class plateau. The class plateau also depends on the positive class representative (c\), and the source gives no bit-size or useful numeric bound on (h_P).

## Fixed-family checker and the remaining compact-cover gate

Once an explicit rational family exists, one can bypass the source proof's local $\\rho_t$ choices and check its global activity guarantee directly. I independently checked the exact parametric-polytope argument recorded by the sibling audit [RESULT.md](../constructive_margin_compiler/RESULT.md); I found no defect in its stated fixed-family interface.

For each label $\\lambda$, coordinate $i$, and side of the error condition, encode activity as
\\[
(r_\\lambda-r_\\mu)\\cdot x\\le b_\\mu(h)-b_\\lambda(h)\\quad(\\forall\\mu),
\\]
and the bad side as $x_i\\le h^{r_{\\lambda i}+E(r_\\lambda)}$ or $x_i\\ge h^{r_{\\lambda i}-E(r_\\lambda)}$. Add the cube box $h^b\\le x_i\\le h^a$, equivalent to $p\\in[a,b]^m$. For rational slopes, tolerance values, and finite rational-power offsets, use a common denominator and rescale to a bounded polytope with constant rational matrix and sparse-polynomial right sides. Enumerate every full-rank active-row basis. Each candidate vertex and each constraint residual is a rational polynomial in the small parameter; the first nonzero coefficient gives its eventual sign. A feasible basis for any bad polytope means bad activity persists for all sufficiently small scales. If every basis has an eventually negative residual, exact leading-term dominance bounds give a positive rational cutoff below which all bad polytopes are empty. This handles weak ties and avoids QE / an $\\mathbb R_{\\exp}$ oracle.

This fixed-family checker does **not** synthesize the family. It also shows the local compactness argument is not itself a fatal barrier once a full rational candidate is supplied: direct candidate validation can replace the radii and neighborhoods. The unsolved step is still producing a rational finite set of slice families and cuts that covers every real $t$.

For the concrete $E_D$, a possible route is to parameterize the recursive thresholds, slopes, offsets, and active-basis calculations by $t$. The tolerance is piecewise rational-affine with finitely many rational zero/sign breakpoints (including singleton breakpoint cases); active bases add finitely many determinant/sign breakpoints. On each parameter cell, a joint parametric rational-polyhedral bad set could test whether its minimum distance from $\\mu=t$ stays uniformly positive. If so, choose a rational grid fine enough to cover the cell, use the corresponding rational-center families, and run exact interval coverage/cut placement. I have not proved the needed joint closedness/uniform-positive-gap statement at cell boundaries, nor bounded the parametric basis expansion. A sequence of bad points with $t_n\\to t_*$ and $\\mu(p_n)-t_n\\to0$ at a parameter breakpoint is the concrete falsifier to search for. Do not label the full finite rational compiler complete until this gate is discharged.

## Primary-prior check and source state

- The affine lemma is attributed in the manuscript to OpenAI/math release 149; the pinned primary source is [affine.tex](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Uniform-Permanence-in-Weakly-Reversible-Mass-Action-Systems-October-5-2026/build/sections/affine.tex). The source itself states that it provides no useful label-count/activity-cutoff bound and no implemented compact-cover synthesis.
- The source affine construction itself says it establishes no useful label-count or activity-cutoff bound and that compactness selection is not an implemented synthesis: local manuscript §2 remark and effective-scope discussion near §10 ([endotactic_permanence.tex](../../../permanence_hostile/endotactic_permanence.tex)).
- Johnston–Pantea–Donnell's primary paper gives a MILP framework for recognizing endotactic / strongly endotactic networks, while describing general endotactic permanence as conjectural; that is a finite recognition algorithm, not an affine-family or absorbing-set compiler ([arXiv:1412.4662](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.4662); full text [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1412.4662)).
- Craciun–Nazarov–Pantea prove the time-varying endotactic permanence result in two species ([arXiv:1010.3050v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1010.3050v2)); Gopalkrishnan–Miller–Shiu prove all-dimensional permanence for the strongly endotactic subclass ([arXiv:1305.5303v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1305.5303v2)). The inspected primary results do not supply the desired all-endotactic rational affine compiler or its quantitative bit-growth bound.

This is a bounded prior-art check, not an exhaustive search. The pre-existing theorem-scope comparison and cutoff are in [endotactic_permanence_priority_audit_2026-10-08.md](../../../c9_endotactic_priority_audit/endotactic_permanence_priority_audit_2026-10-08.md).
