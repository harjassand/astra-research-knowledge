CYCLE04 — EXPOSED COUNTERS, SCOPE FAILURES, AND NON-CLAIMS
These are preserved separately from both frozen constructions.

1. Rank by itself cannot prove Shannon memory growth.

For N>=3 set epsilon_N=1/ln N,
p_1=1-epsilon_N, p_i=epsilon_N/(N-1) for i>=2. Let the stationary
process choose I~p once and output I forever. Its adjacent pair matrix
is diagonal with all N positive entries, hence rank N and at least N
exact classical states. The N-state realization stores I and has
H(I)=h(epsilon_N)+epsilon_N ln(N-1)<=ln2+1.
Thus a state-count/rank proof alone does not imply Omega(ln N) entropy.
This example does not have bounded all-prior capacity of its pure
reachable quantum states, and is NOT a counterexample to the separate
support-entropy proofs in either audited construction.

2. Finite-horizon n*epsilon does not give infinite-path TV, and need not
preserve stationarity of the chosen simulator.

Consider the diagonal qubit instrument
 L_a(rho)=rho_aa |a><a|, a in {0,1},
starting from |0><0|. The original stationary output process is all 0,
and its observed-history reachable family is the singleton {|0><0|}.
Choose the full-domain EB channel that measures the basis and prepares
 sigma_0=(1-epsilon)|0><0|+epsilon|1><1|,
 sigma_1=epsilon|0><0|+(1-epsilon)|1><1|.
Its error on the original reachable family is exactly epsilon. The B1
kernel starts in hidden state 0 and flips at each inserted reset with
probability epsilon. Its probability of the all-zero n-word is
(1-epsilon)^n, so
 TV(P_n,Q_n)=1-(1-epsilon)^n<=n*epsilon,
which is asymptotic to n*epsilon when n*epsilon is small. The simulator's
initial hidden distribution is not stationary. Infinite-path TV is 1:
the original all-zero path has probability one, and the simulator gives
it probability zero. This is a counterexample to stronger conclusions
for a given approximate EB channel, not to existence of an exact
channel fixing this singleton (one plainly exists).

3. The A2 benchmark must admit the reconstructed decoder responses.

A singleton pure preparation |0><0| with a deterministic quantum output
1 has exact EB reconstruction error e(E)=0. If an artificial classical
benchmark permits only output 0, its [0,1] success score is <=beta=0
while the quantum gap is Delta=1. No inference e(E)>=1 is possible.
The necessary repair is explicit admission of all Born-rule responses
d(a|z)=Tr(A_a sigma_z), or unrestricted stochastic responses. A genuine
restricted-subtheory constraint can have the same logical defect when
effects equivalent on E disagree on decoder states outside E. The
source mentions this compatibility boundary after its corollary; make
it a hypothesis if the corollary permits constrained response rules.

4. Ordinary preparation relations must be matrix identities for A1/A2.

For qubit |0><0| and |1><1|, a measurement subtheory consisting only of
the x-basis measurement sees identical outcome distributions. A z-basis
POVM distinguishes these states and hence does not preserve that weaker
operational equivalence. Linearity preserves exact equality of density
matrices, not every equivalence induced by a restricted measurement set.

5. Edge-emitting and strict Moore state-emitting counts differ.

The balanced process has edge-emitting minimum N+2 states and entropy
ln3+(ln N)/3. The strict Moore minimum is 2N+1 states and entropy
ln3+(2/3)ln N; EXPOSED_AUDIT.txt contains both lower and matching upper
proofs. A cardinality-preserving conversion is unavailable. This is an
interface warning, not a failure of the source's edge-emitting theorem.

6. The following stronger claims were NOT proved or used.

- Capacity bounds H(Z), classical support size, or Shannon memory.
  Both constructions explicitly refute this, even for one exact EB map.
- Exact rank lower bounds survive fixed positive TV with the same rate.
  The robust results use independent packing or parity-entropy proofs.
- A Wyner pair decomposition is a generator of every higher-order law.
  Consistency with one transition kernel is an additional requirement.
- The independent orthogonality robust state lower bound is Omega(N).
  The frozen bound is sqrt(N+1)/16 at TV<=1/100. The balanced-subset
  construction obtains Omega(N) with its much larger output alphabet.
- Either construction has constant quantum dimension, constant quantum
  memory entropy, or a quantum memory/computational advantage. It does not.
- The supplied broadcasting modulus is externally or formally validated
  by this transfer audit. It remains an upstream internal candidate.
- Agreement of worker proofs or finite arithmetic constitutes theorem
  certification or priority. Priority remains UNKNOWN; no external
  validation or formal theorem checking was obtained.

No counterexample to the stated B1/B2/B3 edge-emitting claims was found.
