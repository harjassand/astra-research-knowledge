Before the second exceptional event, there cannot be an arbitrarily long hidden b=1/b=2 excursion: if all remaining high-b events annihilate, the path terminates. From (n,1), the longest relevant prefix is

3. CALENDAR-TIME TRANSFER: PASS.
The return-chain event corresponds exactly to visits of the CTMC to (0,0), since b=0 holds are followed by the sole immigration reaction. All excursions terminate almost surely by the killed-kernel bound. In the recurrent regime, nullity of the return chain means infinite expected number M of return cycles. The event {M>j} is determined at the start of cycle j, before its Exp(lambda) hold, and that hold is independent of the past and the subsequent event skeleton. Thus Tonelli gives expected total b=0 hold time lambda^-1 E M=infinity. This is a valid proof of continuous-time nullity and does not rely on a general claim that all time changes preserve positive recurrence. Nonexplosion and the constant-rate holds separately rule out accumulation of cycles.

For explicit comparison with the full reaction-event embedded chain, each complete cycle contains at least two jumps.
