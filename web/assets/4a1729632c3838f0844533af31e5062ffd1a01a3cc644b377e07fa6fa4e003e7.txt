Local result proved
-------------------
I checked the finite constant-budget/extraction step on pp. 2–4. Under the PDF's displayed assumptions and its preceding interval-count/separator inputs, the extracted components necessarily retain at least 55H/64 of total length while each remaining component satisfies all the stated bounds of the closed bounded-system exclusion. Thus the constants in this specific budget step do yield the contradiction claimed on p. 4. The proof below is exact over the reals; it does not rely on a characteristic-zero argument or on a computation.

Let 0 < epsilon <= 1/4, H >= L >= 3, and D0 >= 0. Take

  U >= max(3, ceil(96 D0/epsilon)),
  eta = min(1/(32U), epsilon/(64U)),
  K0 = ceil((Csep/eta)^2), C = K0(U+D0), I = ceil(192 C),

as on p. 2. The p. 3 splitting/counting step supplies N L <= H, closure cost A <= 3 D0 H/U, and at most 6(N+1) comparisons before deletion of the exterior vertex. Since H>=L, N+1 <= 2H/L, so the comparison total is at most 12H/L. Assume the contradiction hypothesis n <= epsilon H/64 from p. 3.

First, U >= 96D0/epsilon gives

  A/H <= 3D0/U <= epsilon/32.

The planar separator bound on p. 4 removes at most Csep N/sqrt(K0) <= eta N piece vertices. Each piece has original length at most U L, so the removed original length M satisfies

  M <= eta N U L <= eta U H <= epsilon H/64.

Here the first inequality follows from K0 >= (Csep/eta)^2; the last follows from eta <= epsilon/(64U). Since epsilon <= 1/4, H-M >= (1-epsilon/64)H >= 255H/256 >= 63H/64. The surviving component total H_Z includes its closures and therefore is at least the surviving original length, so sum_Z H_Z >= 63H/64. A component has at most K0 piece vertices; each contributes at most U L original edges and at most D0 L closure edges. Hence H_Z <= K0(U+D0)L = C L.

The p. 4 bookkeeping gives sum_Z b_Z <= n+M+A: exterior deletion loses at most n partners, separator deletion at most M additional partners, and all closure positions are unpaired. Therefore

  sum_Z b_Z <= epsilon H/64 + epsilon H/64 + epsilon H/32 = epsilon H/16.

The comparison count gives sum_Z I_Z <= 12H/L. For components with b_Z > epsilon H_Z, summing the strict inequalities yields total H_Z < (sum_Z b_Z)/epsilon <= H/16. For components with I_Z > 192 H_Z/L, their total H_Z is less than (L/192) sum_Z I_Z <= H/16. Removing both bad classes leaves total component length at least

  63H/64 - H/16 - H/16 = 55H/64 > 0.

Thus at least one positive component remains. Each of its paths is a positive-length cyclically immersed closed path; the girth condition gives total length H_Z >= L. It has at most K0 paths, H_Z <= C L, b_Z <= epsilon H_Z, and I_Z <= 192 H_Z/L <= 192C <= ceil(192C)=I. It is exactly a forbidden closed bounded system as defined on p. 3. This contradicts that exclusion. The contradiction is a valid local consequence of the stated assumptions and preceding count/separator bounds.

The result is deliberately scoped: it verifies the arithmetic/rounding and mass extraction from the already-stated pairing-count and separator inputs. It does not independently prove the disk's pairing-interval count, the random matching estimates, nonemptiness of the girth-conditioned law, or any of N129's final group-algebra/topology conclusions.
