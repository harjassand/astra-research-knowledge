# d=5 Weyl-compatible lift audit

## Verdict

The stored d=5 line-cover SDP candidate is not solver noise: it has an exact rank-one compatibility-kernel repair over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\). Its non-line-constant Weyl spectrum violates the tempting extension
\[
  \sum_{L}\max_{x\in L\setminus\{0\}}(2\lambda_x-1)_+\le 1,

## Exact channel and compatibility witness

Use \(d=5\), labels \(m=(a,b)\in\mathbb Z_5^2\), and symplectic form
\[
S((a,b),(u,v))=av-bu.
\]
The normalized Fourier transform is \((\mathcal Fv)_{u,v}=\frac15\sum_{a,b}\exp(2\pi iS((a,b),(u,v))/5)v_{a,b}\). Put
\[
\alpha=\frac3{20},\qquad \beta=\frac9{100},\qquad \gamma=\frac14,
\]
and define the real vector \(v\) by the five classes
\[
v_{0,0}=x_0=\frac{23-2\sqrt5}{25},\quad
v_{a,\pm1}=x_1=\frac{75-16\sqrt5}{500},\quad
v_{a,\pm2}=x_2=\frac3{20}\quad(a\in\mathbb Z_5),
\]
\[
v_{\pm1,0}=x_3=\frac9{100},\qquad v_{\pm2,0}=x_4=\frac14.
\]
Every entry is strictly positive and \(v_{-m}=v_m\). Let
\[
N=\lVert v\rVert_2^2=x_0^2+10x_1^2+10x_2^2+2x_3^2+2x_4^2
 =\frac{1901}{1250}-\frac{152\sqrt5}{625},
\quad q_m=\frac{v_m^2}{N},\quad X=\frac{vv^T}{N}.
\]
The five Fourier-class equations reduce to
\[
\begin{aligned}
x_1&=(x_0+2x_3c_1+2x_4c_2)/5,\\
x_2&=(x_0+2x_3c_2+2x_4c_1)/5,\\
x_0&=(x_0+2x_3+2x_4)/5+2x_1+2x_2,\\
x_3&=(x_0+2x_3+2x_4)/5+2x_1c_1+2x_2c_2,\\
x_4&=(x_0+2x_3+2x_4)/5+2x_1c_2+2x_2c_1,
\end{aligned}
\qquad c_1=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{4},\quad c_2=-\frac{\sqrt5+1}{4}.
\]
They imply \(\mathcal Fv=v\) exactly. Therefore \(X\succeq0\), \(\operatorname{diag}X=q\), \(\sum_mq_m=1\), and
\[
\frac1{25}\sum_{m,n}e^{2\pi iS(m-n,r)/5}X_{mn}
 = |(\mathcal F(v/\sqrt N))_r|^2=q_r.
\]
This is precisely the finite Weyl compatibility-kernel criterion of Haapasalo, Corollary 2 / Eq. (22): \(X_{mn}=\beta_{mn}\sqrt{q_mq_n}\) with the all-ones positive kernel \(\beta_{mn}=1\). Thus the Weyl-covariant random-unitary channel \(\Phi(\rho)=\sum_mq_mW_m^*\rho W_m\) is exactly self-compatible. The evenness of \(q\) makes it Hilbert–Schmidt self-adjoint. This is an exact algebraic certificate; \(q\) and \(X\) are in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), not rational.

I did not obtain a rational \(q,X\) repair. Within this five-class fixed-vector ansatz, if the three free parameters \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma\) are rational and the amplitude entries are all rational, the Fourier equations force \(\beta=\gamma\); then the amplitudes are a linear combination of the six line indicators and the resulting random-Weyl spectrum is line-constant. This is only a restriction of the chosen rank-one ansatz. It does not rule out a rational higher-rank compatibility kernel or a different rational channel.

The primary-source criterion is [Haapasalo, *Compatibility of covariant quantum channels with emphasis on Weyl symmetry*, arXiv:1901.06113](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.06113), finite-phase-space Corollary 2, Eq. (22). The formula and map orientation were checked against the source TeX and the implementation at `work/agents/weyl_compatible_lift/scripts/weyl_sdp.py`.

## Exact spectrum and line-cover failure

For modes \((u,v)\), \(\lambda_{u,v}=\sum_{a,b}q_{a,b}e^{2\pi i(ub-va)/5}\). The complete nonidentity spectrum has four classes:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{mode condition}&\lambda_{u,v}&\text{decimal}\\\hline
v=\pm1&\dfrac{13,376,451-752,320\sqrt5}{25,213,768}&0.4638026469969644\\
v=\pm2&\dfrac{14,203,331+281,824\sqrt5}{25,213,768}&0.5883098718799547\\
v=0,\ u=\pm1&\dfrac{13,041,363+255,168\sqrt5}{25,213,768}&0.5398612374668739\\
v=0,\ u=\pm2&\dfrac{17,214,643+498,112\sqrt5}{25,213,768}&0.7269225009291898
\end{array}
\]
and \(\lambda_{0,0}=1\). The six projective lines are ordered as in `line_system(5)`: slopes \(s=0,1,2,3,4\), followed by vertical. The horizontal line peaks at \((2,0)\) or \((3,0)\), with eigenvalue \(\lambda_{u=\pm2,v=0}\). Each of the other five lines contains two modes with \(v=\pm2\), so its peak is \(\lambda_{v=\pm2}\). Hence
\[
\sum_L\max_{x\in L\setminus\{0\}}(2\lambda_x-1)_+
=2\lambda_{2,0}-1+5(2\lambda_{0,2}-1)
=\frac{6,294,997+953,616\sqrt5}{6,303,442}
=1.336943720657926\ldots>1.
\]
The strict inequality follows already from \(953,616\sqrt5>8,445\). This is not line-constant, so it does not conflict with the prime-d line-constant theorem. It falsifies only the unweighted “take the peak on each line” extension.

## EB comparator for the same exact channel

The six-state fixture at `work/agents/weyl_compatible_lift/d5_eb_comparator.json` supplies six vectors and positive mixture weights. For this audit, each stored decimal coordinate is interpreted as an exact rational complex number, each ket is normalized by its exact squared norm, and the positive decimal weights are divided by their exact rational sum. For each ket \(\psi_j\), take the Weyl twirl of the product Choi state
\[
|\psi_j\rangle\!\langle\psi_j|\otimes|\overline{\psi_j}\rangle\!\langle\overline{\psi_j}|.
\]
Each summand is separable; its Bell-diagonal Weyl twirl has maximally mixed marginals, so it is the Choi state of a Weyl-covariant EB channel. The exact rational-weighted mixture is therefore EB and trace preserving. Under the script's Weyl convention, the multiplier of a single twirled product state at mode \((u,v)\) is
\[
\mu_j(u,v)=\left|\sum_{k=0}^4\overline{\psi_{j,k+u}}\,e^{-2\pi ivk/5}\psi_{j,k}\right|^2,
\]
by the finite Weyl orthogonality relation. Thus the mixture is a single common EB channel \(\Psi\), not a modewise choice.

`work/agents/quantum_physics/broadcast_lift/scripts/d5_rational_interval_comparator.py` recomputes all 24 multipliers using exact-rational ket coordinates, weights, and interval endpoints. Cosines and sines are enclosed through \(\sqrt5\) and \(\sqrt{10\pm2\sqrt5}\); every radical enclosure is checked by integer squaring at scale \(10^{50}\). Every subsequent interval operation uses `Fraction`, with no floating-point operation in any inequality check.

- every direct order gap \(\mu_{u,v}-(2\lambda_{u,v}-1)\) exceeds \(59/2500=0.0236\); the smallest interval lower endpoint is at \((u,v)=(1,0)\);
- every clipped-threshold gap \(\mu_{u,v}-(2\lambda_{u,v}-1)_+\) exceeds \(139/10000=0.0139\); the smallest interval lower endpoint is at \((u,v)=(3,4)\);
- all 24 direct inequalities are strictly positive, so \(I-\Psi\preceq2(I-\Phi)\) for this channel. On the identity mode both maps have eigenvalue one.

`d5_exact_repair_audit.py` separately verifies the exact \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) fixed-vector identities and eigenvalue formulas. The rational-interval checker closes only this exact d=5 instance. It is not a proof of the arbitrary-channel operator lemma, nor a counterexample to it. The certificate is internally machine-checkable but has not been formally verified or externally reviewed.

## Boundary and next attack

The SDP search's original floating candidate is repaired by an exact \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) vector construction, and its apparent line-cover violation survives exactly. But this map itself admits the factor-two EB comparator above. Thus “line-cover peaks exceed one” cannot by itself refute an EB lift; any broad proof or counterexample must test a general common EB channel rather than the restricted MUB-dephasing mixture. This isolated d=5 example does not address varying-dimensional bounded-Holevo state families with vanishing two-marginal broadcast error. The QBL/N93 bounded-Holevo implication remains UNKNOWN.
