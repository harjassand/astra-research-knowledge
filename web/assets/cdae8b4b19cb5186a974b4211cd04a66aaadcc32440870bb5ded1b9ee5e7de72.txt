EXACT SETUP

Let W=Lambda^2 C^d, q=dim W=binom(d,2), and realize the irreducible highest-
weight representation V=V_(2 omega_2) as

  V = ker( wedge : Sym^2(W) -> Lambda^4 C^d ).

For d=4,5 the ambient dimensions are 21 and 55, and dim(V)=20 and 50.
Let P be the orthogonal projector onto V, viewed inside W tensor W. The
physical partial trace T: End(V) -> End(W) is restriction of Tr_2 after
embedding V in W tensor W. Its Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint is

  T*(Y) = P (Y tensor I_W) P,

and the actual Petz composite checked here is

  Phi = (dim(W)/dim(V)) T* T.

The symmetric inclusion supplies a two-output extension; Phi is the common
marginal. The script builds P over Q, constructs T and T* from the displayed
operators, and tests Phi directly. No SDP, numerical eigensolver, or PPT
surrogate is involved.
