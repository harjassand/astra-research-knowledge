RECONSTRUCTION 1: THE NO-DOUBLE PROJECTOR CANNOT BE MOVED THROUGH A MODE SWAP
Let sites i,j each have up/down orbitals and let P be the hard onsite projector, deleting any basis state with both spins at one site. Start with |psi>=u_i^dagger d_j^dagger|0>, which P accepts. Let G be the Gaussian one-particle mode swap u_i <-> u_j, leaving the other two orbitals fixed. Then

  G|psi> = u_j^dagger d_j^dagger|0>,
  P G|psi> = 0,
  G P|psi> = u_j^dagger d_j^dagger|0> != 0.

Thus P G != G P, even for a rational permutation matrix and a two-particle Slater input. The script `revisions/r01_static_gate_audit.py` represents exterior basis states as exact bitmasks and applies the mode permutation with the fermionic inversion sign; `r01_static_gate_audit.json` records PASS. This refutes the simple proposal to move every intermediate hard projector to the terminal projector in the parity norm. It does not refute a larger teleportation, tensor-network, or space-time encoding that uses additional modes and proves exact branch-weight preservation.

RECONSTRUCTION 2: THE OBVIOUS HERALD/SIGNAL PAIR SOURCE STAYS IN A SOLVED BIPARTITE SUBCLASS
For a rectangular pair matrix M between disjoint idler sites A and signal sites B, the k-pair state has amplitudes det M[I,J] on |I>_A|J>_B. Its full k-sector norm is

  C_k(M) = sum_{|I|=|J|=k} |det M[I,J]|^2
         = sum_{|I|=k} det((M M^*)[I,I])
         = e_k(M M^*),

by Cauchy--Binet, then the principal-minor identity for elementary symmetric polynomials. The total norm at fugacity one is det(I+M M^*). If the one-particle signal network is U, M is replaced by M U (or the dimensionally appropriate conjugate), preserving the disjoint A/B role partition and the same efficient determinant formulas. Since I subset A and J subset B, onsite overlap is identically absent: hard no-double projection has no effect. If a particular idler pattern is measured, the conditional signal state is a Slater state; mode-resolved fermionic linear optics and particle measurements/postselection remain in the classically simulable FLO/matchgate interface.

A separate exact 2x3 integer example M=[[1,1,0],[0,1,1]] has C_0=1,C_1=4,C_2=3, agreeing with e_k(MM^T) for MM^T=[[2,1],[1,2]]. The revision script checks the three coefficients by exhaustive minors and exact Fractions. This checks the algebra on one fixture; the general equality is the Cauchy--Binet derivation above. The identity is already implicit in the c03_l02 INITIAL's one-way source boundary; this revision sharpens its explicit source-side normalizer and labels peer corroboration.

The route to a genuinely hard parity instance must make up/down role support overlap so P acts nontrivially, or introduce an explicit non-Gaussian operation/resource. But merely declaring P available at the end does not implement the sequence of P, Gaussian gates, classical feedforward and further measurements from an adaptive computation. The exact mode-swap witness shows why the naive temporal-to-final reordering fails. The non-Gaussian nature of P also means Knill's FLO simulator is not a universal no-go for this richer target; the hard part is the legal static compilation with a positive and computably bounded herald.

WHAT SURVIVES FROM V AND WHAT DOES NOT
- Fermionic number attenuation F_g=g^{N_H}, followed by binary loss-heralding, has the derived false-herald bound g^2/p_0 (INITIAL §3), with a lower bound eta^h g^(2h)p_0/Z_g on the desired branch. It is a filter, not a source of the hard computation.
- Retaining R distinct output rails rather than bunching them gives the same constant PostBQP readout gap and expected one survivor at eta=1/R (INITIAL §2). It repairs the readout but supplies no fermionic controlled-sign/nonlinear-sign gadget.
- Directly importing bosonic KLM's nonlinear-sign gate is invalid: its theorem is about bosonic linear optics with particle detection. Fermionic FLO+particle measurement is classically simulable (Knill, quant-ph/0108033). The hard projector can be non-Gaussian/magic (Hebenstreit et al., arXiv:1905.08584), so this source result must not be overstated as an all-model no-go.
- The implication “if a uniform polynomial-bit sampler for this exact constructed parity interface exists, then PP=BPP” remains conditional and structurally valid only after a legal mapping into that interface. It is not itself a hardness result for the hard-BCS norm.
