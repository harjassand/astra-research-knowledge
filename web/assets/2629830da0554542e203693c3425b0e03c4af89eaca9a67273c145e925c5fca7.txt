1. THE MAIN NEW CONSTRUCTION: OBSERVABLE UNIVERSAL REALIZATION

Interface. An unknown probability process has alphabet size n and a real
linear representation f(w)=alpha B_w beta of minimal dimension r<=m.
Data are independent resets from the same initial law. Hidden parameters,
state interventions, a spanning word basis and a matrix-response oracle are
not supplied. Stationarity and full-rank one-symbol emissions are unnecessary.

Set d=2nm and K=1+m*d*(d-1)/2. Explicit nonnegative strictly descending
controllers have entries

  C_i^(ell)[a,b] = ell/(m*a*i^(a-b)), 0<=b<a<d, ell=1,...,m.

They are row-substochastic. Their word-entry functions, plus one empty-word
feature, define a fixed K-dimensional dictionary psi. A descending controller
visits a target state at most once, so the sum of its occupation probabilities
along a trajectory is at most one. This property converts the algebra into
bounded statistical observations without a horizon normalization penalty.

Define

  h_A    = sum_p psi_A(p) f(p),
  H_AB   = sum_(p,s) psi_A(p) psi_B(s) f(ps),
  Hi_AB  = sum_(p,s) psi_A(p) psi_B(s) f(p i s).

NEW THEOREM: rank H=r for every process in the interface. Exact moments give
a minimal equivalent linear realization. Every one of the M=K+(n+1)K^2
moments is the expectation of a [0,1] trajectory test of length at most
2d-1=4nm-1. The dictionary is constructed from n,m alone.

Proof mechanism. Write H=UW using weighted reachable rows and observable
columns. A null direction of U or W would produce a nonzero recognizable
series invisible to the universal detector, a contradiction. Diagonal sign
conjugation and bounded scalar rescaling turn the inherited Volterra
detector into the stochastic controllers above. Sequential controller
acceptance events make the population matrix statistically observable.
Neither independent observed blocks nor a hidden conditional oracle is used.

Conditional statistical consequence. With a supplied positive lower bound
sigma<=sigma_r(H), horizon T, total-variation target epsilon<=1, and failure
probability rho, entry accuracy

  eta = epsilon*sigma^3/(300*n*K^4*T)

and N>=log(2M/rho)/(2 eta^2) reset trajectories suffice, in the exact-arithmetic
analysis, for an efficiently evaluable signed recurrence and an efficient
length-T positive sampler of error <=epsilon. A same-empirical-basis argument
avoids singular-vector alignment assumptions. Nonnegative inserted-word
moment arrays give word-summed operator bounds uniform in length, avoiding
an n^T factor. General fixed noise has an exponential horizon bound; the
linear error estimate requires the displayed horizon-dependent accuracy.

The signed sampler uses prefix masses a A_p(sum_i A_i)^(T-|p|)b. At each prefix
it normalizes the positive child masses. Its output is dominated by the
positive part of the normalized signed leaf law, giving exact L1 correction
cost twice the negative mass. No exponential word enumeration is needed.
This correction is established prior art, not a new invention here.

What specifically enabled this: release family116's ordered hyperlogarithm
representation; inherited full-matrix bivariate-kernel detection and bounded
scalar-grid repair; the new positive stochastic conjugation, two-sided
universal rank argument, and occupation-event compilation. Acyclic word
independence alone would be insufficient for arbitrary cyclic processes.

Nearest prior. Feature-based predictive-state learning, spectral HMM learning,
and two-sided random compression are old (Boots–Gordon; Hsu–Kakade–Zhang;
Hamilton–Milani Fard–Pineau). Signed-law repair is Habrard–Denis–Esposito 2006.
The potentially new increment is the explicit deterministic universal bank
with its all-process rank guarantee and observation interface. The bounded
priority search did not locate that exact earlier construction; it does not
establish historical novelty.

Verification. Separate reconstructions checked chronological word order,
empty-word handling, rank, realization, perturbation bounds and signed repair.
Four exact rational fixtures have similarity certificates implying all-word
equality; direct passive expectations were checked on three. Three noisy
2,500-episode sanity fixtures gave length-six repaired-TV errors about
0.01962, 0.00640, and 0.00627. These are not an advantage benchmark or a test
of the worst-case sample bound. An empirical drift counterexample explicitly
rejects an unrestricted fixed-noise linear-horizon claim.

Limits. The learned minimal representation can be signed; no positive minimal
HMM is acquired. Rare-history conditional accuracy does not follow from
global finite-horizon TV. A fully implemented rounded SVD/recurrence precision
contract remains unfinished, although the audit provides rounding budgets.
The rational-input height theorem below is a distinct exact-moment route.

2. A SHARP BOUNDARY: UNIVERSAL FEATURES ARE NOT UNIFORMLY WELL CONDITIONED

For any M fixed [0,1] single-trajectory observables, there is an O(L)-state
positive parity-clock process P and fair iid Q with

  TV(P[1:L],Q[1:L])=1/2,
  ||E_P phi-E_Q phi||_2 <= sqrt(M/(4*(2^L-1))).

The observables can read arbitrarily long trajectories with the same fair
tail. Boolean Parseval over all nonzero parity masks proves the result.
A clock and one parity bit give <=2L+1 symbol-transition states, or <=4L+2
ordinary state-emitting HMM states. This is a new application of the old
statistical-query parity obstruction, not a new general lower-bound method.
The explicit full-parity example is even less visible to the Volterra bank:
its difference is exp[-Omega(m log(m/log m))] when L is proportional to m.

The distinction matters: raw paths reveal a known parity with constantly
many samples and permit exact linear-algebra discovery of unknown noiseless
constraints. The theorem blocks a uniformly stable fixed bounded dictionary;
it does not block arbitrary computations on batches or prove general passive
learning impossible. Thus the next substantial opportunity is adaptive
feature acquisition, not merely a larger fixed bank.

A separate public-height consequence gives exact recovery at polynomial
bit precision when a rational representation bound B is supplied. With
D0=m*d!*(n!)^d and L0=(2d+1)B+(2d-2)ceil(log2 D0), every moment denominator
is <=2^L0 and sigma_r(H)>=2^(-rL0)/K^(r-1). This yields a finite exact
statistical procedure, but its sample requirement is exponential in L0.

3. A NEW CORRELATED-QUANTUM STORAGE SCALE

Model. One correlated Gibbs density matrix
rho_(V,t,v) proportional exp[-beta H_V+t v.J] is supplied, with known SU(2)
invariant Hamiltonian H_V, known beta and field magnitude t->0, unknown
direction v, fixed spin S per site, and V sites. All retained quantum and
classical registers count. Density reconstruction is intended, not
purification preservation. The exact macroscopic-spin premise is

  j/V -> m_star>0 in probability under the zero-field total-spin law.

Family271's spherical-magnetization manuscript claims this premise for the
low-temperature nearest-neighbor three-dimensional Heisenberg ferromagnet.
That physical premise remains an imported source claim. The abstract archive
argument is separately reconstructed, and an established complete-graph
spherical law provides a distinct earlier setting for the same premise.

NEW CONDITIONAL RATE: the information scale is Vt. If Vt->infinity, optimal
leading memory is (1+o(1))*log2(Vt), achievable entirely classically. A finite
code has error <=1-exp(-t)+epsilon and dimension

  D <= C_S epsilon^(-3)(1+Vt)[1+ln(1+Vt)],

while every sufficiently accurate quantum/hybrid/classical code satisfies
D>=c_(S,m_star)Vt. If Vt->0, no retained bits are needed for vanishing error.
If Vt->c>0, any diverging classical capacity suffices. More precisely, put
alpha=m_star*c and lambda_alpha=coth(alpha)-1/alpha. The optimal worst-case
trace-distance reconstruction error with memory dimension D satisfies

  lambda_alpha/(2D)-a_V <= e*(V,D) <= C_alpha/D+a_V,
  a_V -> 0 independently of D.

The lower bound permits arbitrary quantum/hybrid memory and input-independent
common randomness; a classical archive achieves the upper bound. Thus the
critical accuracy/capacity law is Theta(1/D) in the iterated thermodynamic
limit. The additive a_V is essential for joint D,V limits: the macroscopic
spin premise supplies no rate. No shared entanglement is permitted.

Mechanism. SU(2) decomposition separates the unknown direction from a known
multiplicity Gibbs state. The finite-spin coherent-channel minorant controls
disturbance by O(t), uniformly in spin. Conditional reference resampling
coarsens the angular likelihood and the sector label without storing hidden
continuous data. Increasing field tilt preserves a macroscopic lower-spin
bound, even though it need not preserve the exact zero-field concentration
point in the supercritical regime. Coherent measurement plus sphere packing
gives the quantum-memory lower bound. The critical experiment is a von
Mises–Fisher sphere, not a Gaussian. Its exact positive coherent preparation
law reduces every memory code to a spherical Markov kernel. First spherical
moments turn reconstruction error into angular mean-square distortion;
the trace bound for a D-dimensional memory and exact cap area imply
distortion >=2/D, even conditional on a shared seed. Positive tensor-hat
kernels in smooth sphere charts attain O(1/D) approximation: integrated
centroid errors are O(h^2) without dividing by a minimum cell weight.
The earlier rank-only critical converse did not cover shared randomness;
the final proof repairs that gap explicitly.

This differs from the inherited iid weak-polarization scale nt^2. An ordered
phase has extensive spin already at zero field, so a field of order 1/V is
informative. For t=V^(-alpha), 0<alpha<1, the leading memory is
(1-alpha)log2 V. Preparation of known multiplicity Gibbs states and partition
functions can be costly; the result does not supply an efficient compiler.
The physical source proof, novelty, and external validation are unresolved.

4. FINITE-BIT PEPS FROM THE RELEASED GAUSSIAN COMPRESSION

Family265's analytic/protocol construction is retained as a premise for a
unique ground state of a constant-gap, bounded-interaction L-by-L grid.
The new downstream precision lemma bounds each already contracted local
tensor coefficient. For a contraction O and probability weights p,q,

  E|a_P|^2 = |s_P|^2 sum_(i,l) sqrt(p_i q_l)|O_li|^2 <= |s_P|^2.

This bound is independent of the enormous private Hilbert dimensions.
One can prove the inequality either by Schatten Holder or from the row and
column sum bounds on |O_li|^2. Fresh independent source slots, fixed local
maps, and bounded incidence are essential; sample-dependent contractions or
reused Gaussians can invalidate it.

A union bound and the source's global error event give one jointly good
realization with polynomially bounded local entries. Rounding the whole
density network before selecting a column avoids an uncharged tiny-amplitude
division. A good column has norm at least sqrt(3)/(4*q^(L^2/2)).

NEW CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCE: polynomial-bond PEPS with polynomial total
description and O(L^2 log L) coefficient bits, at any fixed inverse-polynomial
vector accuracy. Exact energy verification costs 2^O(L log L) times a
polynomial in Hamiltonian input bits. A supplied-description preparation
compiler, using family283's phase-sensitive lookup and an established
effective fixed-dimensional synthesis routine, has the same subexponential
scale after every oracle cost is included.

This closes a precision gap, not the tensor acquisition gap. A polynomial

7. REUSABLE MECHANISMS LEARNED FROM SUPPORTING PROOFS

The broad source pass yielded concrete proof components in
work/mechanism_kernels.md, not merely a list of headline results:

* Polynomial Poincare plus Appell recursion bounds EVERY matrix unfolding of
  an order-k cumulant by k! k^k beta^(k/2), without a dimension factor at each
  tensor contraction. Order, the actual Poincare constant and MGF existence
  remain charged. This comes from family139's supporting jet argument.
* Triangular precision recurrences stay polynomial when each layer's added
  cost depends only on strictly higher layers and the number of layers is
  fixed. Same-layer feedback destroys that deduction. Family159's discovery
  history shows why proof-internal precision and returned output width must
  have separate budgets.
* Noncommuting quadratic covariance correction can be solved by completing
  a square: T=(sqrt(C^2/4-lambda K)-C/2)/lambda. Positivity and the spectral
  denominator are part of the interface; matrix commutativity is unnecessary.
* Sparse exposure at rate k^(-a), 1/2<a<1, keeps k^(1-a) witnesses while
  quadratic disturbance k^(1-2a) vanishes. Independence after the actual
  conditioning/advice selection must be proved, as family102 illustrates.
* A supplied group-algebra defect ab=1!=ba becomes an explicit injective
  nonsurjective local cellular rule. Finite support and exact group equalities
  are prerequisites, not a consequence of a formal infinite correction.
* The flat cubic 3u^2-2u^3 converts nonlinear weighted-l1 motion into a quartic
  Hilbert Bregman remainder. Martingale cancellation requires an initially
  measurable anchor; a terminal outcome does not provide it.

The reasoning histories repeatedly supplied useful failure tests: extra
matrix dimensions do not necessarily add independent signal directions;
local Padé/kernel feasibility does not certify global winding; normalization
can merely move height costs; finite local recovery can lose information
at infinity; shared latent noises can create conditional synergy; and
formal correction may destroy finite support. These are recorded as
specific proof obligations, not universal slogans.

8. HIGHEST-VALUE UNRESOLVED OPPORTUNITY

The most valuable next target is a genuinely acquired, adaptive predictive
representation. The universal observable bank gives a deterministic starting
point and a complete population rank witness. The parity theorem proves
why uniformly stable learning cannot come from another fixed polynomial
bounded bank alone. Seek a declared useful process class in which new tests
can be acquired from the same data and one can certify a predictive error
or singular margin without a hidden teacher. Compare against ordinary
spectral, retained-data and direct algebraic controls at identical information
and cost. Keep sampling, numerical precision, online recurrence, positive
sampling and history-conditioned prediction as separate obligations.

For physics, the strongest new opportunity is to acquire the ordered-phase
archive's sector/multiplicity operations efficiently, or extend its theorem
to unknown field magnitude. The storage law alone does not pay for interacting
Gibbs preparation. For tensor networks, finite-bit existence now has a clear
certificate interface, but discovering useful tensors remains open. The
original sparse-PCP, deterministic selected-output multiplication, arbitrary
commutative PIT, and unrestricted teacher-free HMM targets are not resolved.

This package is intended to make the new proof machinery reusable with its
interfaces, costs, failure cases and evidence attached. It is not a claim of
permanent model-weight learning or full certification of the released corpus.
