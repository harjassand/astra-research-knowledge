Exact scope
-----------
The exact fixed no-double normalizer Z_k(A), restricted to half filling

Comparison
----------
The earlier square-root approximation chart uses one site per positive edge
and approximates normalized sqrt weights with dyadic rationals. It is more
compact and already suffices for the AP transfer. The new rational-square
variant chart eliminates that weight-approximation bias and gives exact
R(0)=weighted-PM, at the cost of O(log(pq)) variants per rational edge and a
larger matrix. It is a separate alternative, not a universal improvement.
