# Weyl-line conditional-expectation gate

## Exact restricted theorem

Let \(d\) be an odd prime and let \(\Phi:M_d\to M_d\) be Weyl-covariant, unital, trace-preserving, Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint and positive, and self-compatible. Write

\[
 \Phi(W_g)=\lambda_gW_g,\qquad \lambda_0=1,\qquad 0\le\lambda_g\le1,
\]

for the symmetrized Weyl operators. The last interval uses the extra HS-positivity gate. The nonzero phase-space labels partition into the \(d+1\) projective lines \(L\subset\mathbb Z_d^2\).

For each line, let \(\mathcal E_L\) be the trace-preserving conditional expectation onto the abelian Weyl MASA generated by \(\{W_g:g\in L\}\). Its spectrum on the Weyl basis is 1 on \(L\cup\{0\}\) and 0 outside. Given probabilities \(p_L\ge0\), \(\sum_Lp_L=1\), define

\[
 \Psi_p=\sum_Lp_L\mathcal E_L.
 \tag{1}
\]

This is one canonical EB channel generated by effects \(p_LP_{L,s}\): retain \(L\) as part of the outcome and measure its joint eigenbasis \(\{P_{L,s}\}_s\). On every nonidentity mode \(g\in L(g)\), its multiplier is \(p_{L(g)}\). Thus the full-domain comparison is equivalent, within this restricted family, to

\[
 p_L\ge r_L:=\max_{g\in L\setminus\{0\}}(2\lambda_g-1)_+
 \quad\text{for every line }L.
 \tag{2}
\]

In particular, if

\[
 \boxed{\sum_Lr_L\le1,}
 \tag{3}
\]

then choose \(p_L\ge r_L\) summing to one; the single \(\Psi_p\) satisfies \(\Psi_p\succeq2\Phi-I\) on every Hermitian input. In Dirichlet form, its gap on mode \(g\in L\) is \(1-p_L\), while the target gap is \(1-\lambda_g\); (2) is exactly \(1-p_L\le2(1-\lambda_g)\). This construction retains arbitrarily slow classical modes by allocating their line a correspondingly large probability. It assumes no positive spectral gap.

This is an all-input theorem for the subfamily satisfying (3), with the requested common-map quantifier. It is not necessary for arbitrary EB comparators.

## Exact boundary: \(d=5\)

The exact positive, self-adjoint, self-compatible Weyl channel in work/agents/quantum_physics/broadcast_lift/D5_AUDIT.md has a horizontal-line mode with

\[
 \lambda_h=\frac{17,214,643+498,112\sqrt5}{25,213,768}=0.7269\ldots,
\]

and each of the other five projective lines has a mode with

\[
 \lambda_s=\frac{14,203,331+281,824\sqrt5}{25,213,768}=0.5883\ldots.
\]

The audit gives the exact line budget

\[
 \sum_L\max_{g\in L\setminus\{0\}}(2\lambda_g-1)_+
 =\frac{6,294,997+953,616\sqrt5}{6,303,442}
 =1.3369\ldots>1.
 \tag{4}
\]

Therefore no convex mixture of these \(d+1\) line conditional expectations can be the desired comparator for this channel. This is a real obstruction to this particular common-measurement construction, not an artifact of dropping HS positivity: the exact channel has all \(\lambda_g\ge0\) and an exact compatibility kernel. It is not a counterexample to EB domination. D5_AUDIT.md supplies a single six-state Weyl-covariant EB channel whose 24 nonidentity multipliers all strictly dominate \(2\lambda_g-1\). The missing support lies outside the convex hull of line-dephasing moment vectors, not outside the full pure-state moment set.

## Receiver-pulled conditional expectations

There is a related, more restrictive implementation using the supplied marginal. Measure the output of \(\Phi\) in the line \(L\) eigenbasis and pull that POVM back to the input. Its effects are \(\Phi(P_{L,s})\), and its canonical channel is exactly

\[
 \widetilde\Psi_L=\Phi\circ\mathcal E_L\circ\Phi.
 \tag{5}
\]

Indeed, \(\Phi\) is trace-preserving and self-adjoint, so for rank-one \(P_{L,s}\), the canonical same-effect/same-preparation formula reduces to \(\sum_s d\tau(P_{L,s}\Phi(A))\Phi(P_{L,s})\). On \(g\in L\setminus\{0\}\), (5) has multiplier \(\lambda_g^2\); off \(L\) it is zero. A mixture with weights \(p_L\) works if

\[
 p_L\ge \widetilde r_L:=
 \max_{g\in L:\,\lambda_g>1/2}\frac{2\lambda_g-1}{\lambda_g^2},
 \qquad \sum_L\widetilde r_L\le1.
 \tag{6}
\]

For the same \(d=5\) channel, both displayed line peaks exceed \(2-\sqrt2\), so each of the six rates in (6) is greater than \(1/2\): for \(2-\sqrt2<\lambda<1\), \((2\lambda-1)/\lambda^2>1/2\). The strict threshold can be checked with rational bounds: \(\sqrt5>2.23\) gives \(\lambda_s>0.588\) and \(\lambda_h>0.72\), while \(\sqrt2>1.414\) gives \(2-\sqrt2<0.586\). Thus this receiver-pulled scheme needs total rate \(>3\), and fails decisively. Since \(0\le\lambda^2\le1\), for a fixed \(p_L\) its mode multiplier \(p_L\lambda_g^2\) is no larger than the direct conditional-expectation multiplier \(p_L\); hence it cannot improve the budget (3). It makes explicit that the obvious broadcaster-measurement implementation also does not close the gate.

## Relation to the Weyl/parity star twirl and status

For Weyl-invariant \(Q\), the displacement, parity, and output-swap twirl makes a top eigenstate of the star operator into a legal symmetric extension with maximally mixed one-body marginals and a Weyl-covariant, HS-self-adjoint channel. Its Weyl-diagonal Choi state has Bell probabilities \(p_h\ge0\) from complete positivity, and mode multipliers \(\lambda_g=\sum_h p_h\chi_g(h)\), the finite Fourier transform of those probabilities. Parity forces these multipliers to be real; it does not force every Fourier coefficient nonnegative. HS positivity is the additional condition \(\lambda_g\ge0\) for every \(g\). Hence the unconstrained star eigenvalue is always an upper bound, but a top-twirled state is a target-class witness only after its full HS spectrum is checked. For the six-mode \(d=5\) \(Q\) tested here, the exact rational-interval residual/gap certificate in `legal_sdp/d5_twirl_top_positivity_certificate.py` proves every top-twirled transfer coefficient exceeds \(0.472038\). This is a certified one-\(Q\) result; general HS positivity of top-twirled maximizers remains open. No material positivity mismatch was obtained here.

The exact lemma (1)--(3) is a useful Markov/conditional-expectation subcase that retains slow modes. Its exact \(d=5\) boundary (4) shows that line measurements alone do not supply a universal common EB moment map; general Weyl-orbit pure-state ensembles can be strictly stronger. The unrestricted all-\(Q\) comparison remains open. This note does not use the prior qubit CP-square obstruction or a \(d=3\) line-constant case.
