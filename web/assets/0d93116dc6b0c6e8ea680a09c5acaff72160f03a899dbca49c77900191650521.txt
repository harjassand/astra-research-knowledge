## Result: undecidable stochastic lifetimes under one fixed, globally stable deterministic flow

The strongest result obtained is a **complete undecidability construction**: the deterministic equations, their global polynomial Lyapunov function, and even the stochastic infinitesimal drift can all remain unchanged, while stochastic explosion encodes the halting problem.

The mechanism is an exact identity that makes **symmetric noise—normally invisible to the drift—produce a controllable signed drift for a different observable**.

Historical novelty remains unverified. I do not claim that this establishes the requested historic 10/10 breakthrough.

### 1. Exact theorem

Let \(K\subseteq\mathbb N\) be a fixed recursively enumerable complete set, such as the halting set.

**Theorem.** There exist a single finite mass-action reaction graph and integer-coefficient polynomial rate functions \(k_r(\theta)\), \(0\le\theta\le1\), such that:

- All rate constants lie in one fixed interval \([1,C]\). Every reaction changes one species count by one; source and product molecularities are at most eight.
- The **entire deterministic vector field is independent of \(\theta\)**. Its unique equilibrium \(\mathbf1\) is globally asymptotically stable, with one explicit coercive polynomial \(V\), of degree at most twelve, satisfying
  \[
  V(w)\ge\|w-\mathbf1\|^2,\qquad
  V(\mathbf1)=0,\qquad
  \dot V(w)\le-\|w-\mathbf1\|^2.
  \]
- At the computably specified rational parameters
  \[
  \theta_a=\frac1{a+1},
  \]
  the minimal stochastic chain, from the same initial state \(\mathbf1\), satisfies
  \[
  \boxed{\mathbb P^{\theta_a}_{\mathbf1}(\tau<\infty)=1_K(a).}
  \]
  Moreover, \(a\in K\) gives **finite mean lifetime**, whereas \(a\notin K\) gives **nonexplosion and positive recurrence**.

Consequently, deciding stochastic explosion or positive recurrence is undecidable even when the deterministic dynamics have already been completely resolved and their common polynomial Lyapunov certificate is supplied.

Here “explosion” means infinitely many jumps in finite time. Only the finitely many **rate constants** are bounded; the state-dependent jump intensities are unbounded. The mass-action convention is deterministic rate \(kx^u\) and stochastic propensity \(k(n)_u\), with falling factorials. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.09356)

## 2. The mathematical mechanism

### An exact harmonic response to symmetric cubic noise

Let \(N_t\in\mathbb N^m\) consist of independent immigration–death coordinates:
\[
n\longrightarrow n+e_i\quad\text{at rate }1,\qquad
n\longrightarrow n-e_i\quad\text{at rate }n_i.
\]
This autonomous environment is nonexplosive and positive recurrent, with product \(\operatorname{Pois}(1)\) stationary distribution.

Conditional on \(N_t=n\), give \(Z_t\ge1\) birth and death rates
\[
\begin{aligned}
b_n(z)&=\eta+\lambda(n)z(z-1)+H(n)z(z-1)^2,\\
d_n(z)&=\mu(n)z(z-1)+H(n)z(z-1)^2,
\end{aligned}
\]
where \(\eta>0\), \(\lambda,\mu\ge1\), and \(H>0\). Write \(D=\lambda-\mu\).

The symmetric \(H\)-term contributes **nothing** to the ordinary drift:
\[
\mathcal LZ=\eta+D(n)z(z-1).
\]

But consider the harmonic-number observable
\[
\ell(1)=0,\qquad
\ell(z)=\sum_{j=1}^{z-1}\frac1j.
\]
Since its forward and backward increments are \(1/z\) and \(-1/(z-1)\), direct substitution gives
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L\ell(z)
=(D(n)-H(n))(z-1)-\mu(n)+\frac{\eta}{z},
\qquad z\ge2.
}
\tag{1}
\]

Thus symmetric noise can change the sign controlling escape without changing the ordinary drift.

### The threshold is proved, including equality

For this process,
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\exists n:\ D(n)>H(n)
&\Longrightarrow \mathbb E_{n,z}\tau<\infty
\quad\text{from every state},\\
D(n)\le H(n)\ \forall n
&\Longrightarrow\text{nonexplosion and positive recurrence}.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{2}
\]

**Nonpositive case.** Equation (1) gives \(\mathcal L\ell\le\eta\) everywhere and \(\mathcal L\ell\le-1/2\) for sufficiently large \(z\). Therefore
\[
F(n,z)=1+\sum_i n_i+2(m+1)\ell(z)
\]
has finite sublevel sets, globally bounded-above generator, and strictly negative generator outside a finite set. Stopped Dynkin estimates exclude explosion; the finite-set return-time argument gives positive recurrence. This includes \(D=H\).

**Positive case.** Freeze a favorable environment state and put
\[
\alpha=\frac{\lambda-\mu}{H}>1.
\]
For the resulting birth–death chain define auxiliary weights
\[
\pi_1=1,\qquad
\pi_j=\frac{b_1\cdots b_{j-1}}{d_2\cdots d_j}.
\]
First-step recurrence gives the expected time to pass from \(j\) to \(j+1\):
\[
u_j=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{j}\pi_i}{b_j\pi_j}.
\]
Now
\[
\frac{b_{j-1}}{d_j}
=1+\frac{\alpha-3}{j}+O(j^{-2}),
\qquad
\pi_j\asymp j^{\alpha-3}.
\]
Hence
\[
u_j=
\begin{cases}
O(j^{-\alpha}),&1<\alpha<2,\\
O(j^{-2}\log j),&\alpha=2,\\
O(j^{-2}),&\alpha>2.
\end{cases}
\]
All three cases are summable, so
\[
M:=\mathbb E_1\tau=\sum_{j\ge1}u_j<\infty.
\]
Because the chain starting at one must pass through every finite higher level,
\[
\sup_{z\ge1}\mathbb E_z\tau\le M.
\]

This uniform bound closes the recurrent-exposure argument. At each visit of \(N_t\) to the favorable state \(v\), the probability of remaining there for \(2M\) and exploding during that interval is at least
\[
\delta=\tfrac12 e^{-2Mq(v)}>0.
\]
Survival through \(k\) visits is at most \((1-\delta)^k\). The environment has finite mean return cycles, so the total expected lifetime is finite.

No assertion that a rare favorable state is “eventually enough” is left unsupported.

## 3. Encoding arithmetic without changing either drift

The established DPRM theorem supplies a fixed system of integer quadratic equations
\[
f_1(a,n)=\cdots=f_s(a,n)=0,\qquad n\in\mathbb N^d,
\tag{3}
\]
solvable exactly when \(a\in K\). The quadratic form is obtained by replacing an arithmetic circuit with equations for its addition and multiplication gates. [Scholarpedia](https://www.scholarpedia.org/article/Matiyasevich_theorem)

Define
\[
\widetilde f_j(\theta,n)
=\theta^2f_j((1-\theta)/\theta,n).
\]
Because the \(f_j\) have total degree at most two, these extend polynomially through \(\theta=0\). Put
\[
\widetilde S_\theta(n)=\sum_j\widetilde f_j(\theta,n)^2.
\]
At \(\theta_a=1/(a+1)\),
\[
\widetilde S_{\theta_a}(n)
=\theta_a^4\sum_j f_j(a,n)^2.
\tag{4}
\]

Expand in falling factorials of the count variables:
\[
2\widetilde S_\theta(n)-\theta^4
=\sum_{|\beta|\le4}q_\beta(\theta)(n)_\beta.
\]
For a polynomial \(q\), let \(\|q\|_1\) denote the sum of absolute coefficients. Set
\[
b_\beta=2+\|q_\beta\|_1,\qquad
h_\beta(\theta)=b_\beta+q_\beta(\theta).
\]
Then, throughout \(0\le\theta\le1\),
\[
2\le h_\beta(\theta)\le2+2\|q_\beta\|_1.
\]
Thus all coefficients can be implemented as uniformly positive, bounded rate constants.

Write
\[
B(n)=\sum b_\beta(n)_\beta,\qquad
H_\theta(n)=\sum h_\beta(\theta)(n)_\beta.
\]
Their difference is exactly
\[
H_\theta(n)=B(n)-\theta^4+2\widetilde S_\theta(n).
\tag{5}
\]

Introduce one additional immigration–death controller \(Y\), and choose
\[
\lambda(n,y)=1+2B(n)y,\qquad
\mu(n,y)=1+B(n)+2B(n)(y)_2.
\]
Therefore
\[
D(n,y)=B(n)\bigl[1-2(y-1)^2\bigr].
\]
For integer \(y\neq1\), this is negative. At \(y=1\),
\[
D(n,1)-H_{\theta_a}(n)
=\theta_a^4\left(1-2\sum_jf_j(a,n)^2\right).
\tag{6}
\]

An integer sum of squares is either zero or at least one. Equations (2) and (6) therefore prove the exact halting dichotomy.

### The reaction realization is explicit

Let \(X_\beta=\sum_i\beta_iX_i\). For each \(\beta\), the fixed drift is implemented by
\[
\begin{aligned}
X_\beta+2Z&\xrightarrow{b_\beta}X_\beta+Z,\\
X_\beta+Y+2Z&\xrightarrow{2b_\beta}X_\beta+Y+3Z,\\
X_\beta+2Y+2Z&\xrightarrow{2b_\beta}X_\beta+2Y+Z.
\end{aligned}
\]
The symmetric noise is implemented by
\[
X_\beta+jZ
\xrightarrow{h_\beta(\theta)}
X_\beta+(j\pm1)Z,
\qquad j=2,3,
\]
meaning both signs are included.

Add rate-one immigration and death of the controllers, rate-one reactions \(2Z\to3Z\) and \(2Z\to Z\), and immigration of \(Z\) at the fixed rate
\[
\eta=\sum_\beta b_\beta.
\]
The symmetric propensities add to
\[
H_\theta(n)\bigl((z)_2+(z)_3\bigr)
=H_\theta(n)z(z-1)^2.
\]

Every reaction is legal. Parallel channels can be combined. The largest source and product complexes contain eight molecules.

## 4. Why the deterministic dynamics remain completely solved

Define
\[
\overline B(x)=\sum_\beta b_\beta x^\beta.
\]
The symmetric reactions cancel identically in the deterministic vector field. The complete system is
\[
\dot x_i=1-x_i,\qquad
\dot y=1-y,\qquad
\dot z=\eta-\Gamma(x,y)z^2,
\]
where
\[
\Gamma(x,y)
=\overline B(x)\left[2(y-\tfrac12)^2+\tfrac12\right]\ge1,
\qquad
\Gamma(\mathbf1,1)=\eta.
\tag{7}
\]
There is **no parameter dependence anywhere in these equations**, not merely along a special invariant subspace.

The underlying factorial-moment separation is
\[
g(1)=1,\qquad
g(y)=1-2(y-1)^2,
\]
but, for \(P\sim\operatorname{Pois}(y)\),
\[
\mathbb E g(P)
=-\left[2(y-\tfrac12)^2+\tfrac12\right]<0.
\]
Thus the count-level positive state is compatible with uniformly negative deterministic damping.

For an explicit common Lyapunov polynomial, write \(u=(x,y)\), and set
\[
Q(u)=
\int_0^1
\frac{[\Gamma(\mathbf1+s(u-\mathbf1))-\eta]^2}{s}\,ds.
\]
The numerator vanishes quadratically at \(s=0\), so \(Q\) is a nonnegative polynomial with rational coefficients. Along \(\dot u=\mathbf1-u\),
\[
\dot Q=-[\Gamma(u)-\eta]^2.
\]

Consequently
\[
V(u,z)=\|u-\mathbf1\|^2+(z-1)^2+Q(u)
\]
is coercive. Writing \(e=z-1\), \(\delta=\eta-\Gamma(u)\), direct differentiation gives
\[
\dot V
=-(\delta-e)^2
-[2\Gamma(u)(z+1)-1]e^2
-2\|u-\mathbf1\|^2
\le-e^2-2\|u-\mathbf1\|^2.
\]
This proves global existence and global asymptotic stability on the nonnegative orthant, uniformly across the entire parameter family.

## 5. Strongest consequences and an explicit small example

The theorem establishes more than disagreement between deterministic and stochastic predictions:

**Even complete knowledge of the deterministic flow, a supplied strict polynomial Lyapunov certificate, and the stochastic infinitesimal drift leaves stochastic conservativity undecidable.**

The explosive parameter set is relatively open:
\[
\mathcal E=
\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb N^d}
\left\{\theta:2\widetilde S_\theta(n)<\theta^4\right\}.
\]
It is not semialgebraic. Otherwise its membership along \(1/(a+1)\to0\) would eventually be constant, contradicting the noncomputability of \(K\).

The record also contains a fully listed **two-species, seven-reaction example**, with source and product order at most four, for which
\[
\boxed{
\text{finite-mean almost-sure explosion}
\iff
\operatorname{dist}(a,\mathbb N)<\frac1{\sqrt8},
\qquad a\ge1.
}
\]
Outside those intervals—including their endpoints—the chain is nonexplosive and positive recurrent. Its deterministic equilibrium is always \((1,1)\), globally attracting. This supplies an explicit nonsemialgebraic phase diagram without invoking a universal Diophantine polynomial.
