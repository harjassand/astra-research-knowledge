## Primary literature checked (8 October 2026)

1. Craciun, Nazarov, Pantea, “Persistence and permanence of mass-action and power-law dynamical systems,” arXiv:1010.3050 v2. Primary HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/1010.3050v2 . Exact pointers: system/rate definition 6, lines 108–121; two-species endotactic permanence Theorem 19, lines 442–448; introduction says two-species result and conjectures any number of species, lines 56–59. The abstract is broader-sounding, so cite Theorem 19 for the actual dimension scope.
2. Johnston, Pantea, Donnell, “A computational approach to persistence, permanence, and endotacticity of biochemical reaction systems,” arXiv:1412.4662. https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.4662 . Abstract lines 16–17 distinguishes known strong-endotactic permanence from the open general-endotactic conjecture; full HTML identifies strong-endotactic theorem and biochemical circadian example.
3. Gopalkrishnan, Miller, Shiu, “A geometric approach to the global attractor conjecture,” SIAM J. Applied Dynamical Systems 13 (2014), DOI 10.1137/130928170. Primary PDF: https://people.tamu.edu/~annejls/geometricGAC.pdf . Web-read PDF lines 78–86 @ pages 2–3 state Theorem 1.1 (strongly endotactic permanence) and its weakly reversible single-linkage corollary.
4. Brunner, Craciun, “Robust persistence and permanence of polynomial and power law dynamical systems,” SIAM J. Applied Mathematics (2018), DOI 10.1137/17M1133762; arXiv:1705.06785. https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.06785 . Abstract says two-dimensional tropically endotactic polynomial systems remain permanent for possibly time-dependent parameters.
5. Craciun, Deshpande, “Endotactic Networks and Toric Differential Inclusions,” arXiv:1906.08384. https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.08384 . Abstract supports embedding of endotactic systems into toric differential inclusions; it is not an all-endotactic permanence theorem.
6. Xu, “First order endotactic reaction networks,” arXiv:2409.01598 v2 (2025 version; current page notes 2026 publication). https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01598 . Abstract gives a detailed first-order characterization and global asymptotic stability for first-order endotactic linear systems; higher-order examples are handled via asymptotically autonomous methods, not arbitrary measurable all-dimensional kinetics.
7. Xu, “Exponential ergodicity of first order endotactic stochastic reaction systems,” arXiv:2601.00176. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00176 . Stochastic CTMC result; different from deterministic Carathéodory trajectories.

The literature scan did not produce an external proof of N33’s stated broad theorem. That is a scoped search result, not a priority guarantee.
