1. SHARP CRITICAL QUBIT QUANTUM-MEMORY PRECISION LAW

Contract. A single supplied block consists of n identically prepared qubits
rho_b=(I+b.sigma)/2, with unknown b in the whole ball ||b||<=c/sqrt(n).
The encoder and decoder must reconstruct the entire n-qubit density operator in
half trace distance. They are not given b, extra copies, a purification, or a
free input-dependent classical register. A wholly classical archive implements
an entanglement-breaking (measure-and-prepare) channel.

Candidate theorem. Let Q be the maximum retained quantum branch dimension,
allowing arbitrary classical labels. In the critical regime r~n^(-1/2), the
optimum worst-case full-state reconstruction error satisfies

  e_Q = Theta(r/Q), uniformly for 1<=Q<=c/r.

At requested error Theta(n^(-1)), Q=Theta(sqrt(n)) is necessary and achievable.
Thus the required quantum register grows even though the leading limiting
experiment becomes classical. The lower bound allows unlimited classical memory;
the upper uses O(n^3) finite labels, charged separately, and is radius blind.
This does not optimize total hybrid dimension or prove a runtime speedup.

The case Q=1 is the sharp classical reconstruction floor Theta_c(n^(-1/2)).
A public,
radius-blind quantum Schur cutoff achieves arbitrary error epsilon using total
hybrid dimension D<=C_c n[1+log(1/epsilon)], capped by the exact Schur archive.
The latter construction is inherited. The new branch-dimension converse and
matching tapered-filter construction go beyond that baseline and the EB floor.

Mechanism. In spin sector L=2j, let R_L=Tr(tau_L,z J_z), a_L=R_L/(j+1), and
P_L be the Schur weight. The isotropic direction posterior after any measurement
has mean norm at most a_L. The bounded output score

  F_K(v)=a_K J_v/j_K - a_K^2 I/2

absorbs arbitrary changes of output sector: its posterior expectation from input
sector L is at most a_L a_K-a_K^2/2<=a_L^2/2. The target exceeds this by
a_L^2/j_L. A common bounded normalization gives the exact finite converse

  e_EB >= [sum_(L>0) P_L a_L^2/L] / max_K a_K.

A positive-mass critical band L~1/r proves Omega(r) for c0<=r sqrt(n)<=c1.
The coherent classical channel gives O(r). This removes the square-root log
loss of the older tail-cutoff witness. Monotone likelihood ratios of Schur
weights make the quantum cutoff uniform over the unknown-radius ball.

The same bounded posterior-score lemma applies to direct-sum experiments with
a proved posterior-radius bound and bounded vector observables. It also gives
Theta(1/j) classical error for the single-spin family exp[(h/j)J_v]/Z, with
h in a fixed positive compact interval. These are actual mathematical transfer
statements; covariance by itself does not supply the lemma's premises.

1A. A GENERAL MEMORY/ENERGY EQUIVALENCE AND THE NEAR-FULL CAPACITY REGIME

Contract. One supplied state is tau_(h,v)=exp[(h/N)dGamma(|v><v|)]/Z on

  e_Q = Theta(Lambda(Q)/N^2), for every 1<=Q<=d.

have [(N+1)(2N+1)]^s exact positive phase/Jacobi labels. Ideal channel existence,

3. THE ACTUAL FIXED FERMIONIC FILTER CONTAINS PERFECT-MATCHING COUNTING

The legal target is

  Z_k(A)=sum_(|I|=|J|=k, I intersect J=empty) |det A[I,J]|^2,

with the explicit rational matrix A supplied. The filter has not been replaced
by an arbitrary Boolean predicate. At n=2k, paired columns of [I,A^T] express
Z as a sum of full determinant squares.

Candidate theorem. Exact Z is #P-hard, even for rational contractions, under a
polynomial-time one-query metric reduction; integer-input Z is in #P. General
graph perfect-matching counting and approximate uniform sampling reduce to
approximation/counting for this fixed filter with polynomial input bit length.
This transfers an approximation frontier. It does not show that an FPRAS is
impossible, nor imply NP=RP.

Construction. Graph endpoint column pairs, with forced dummy pairs, encode
perfect matchings as nonzero unit-column determinants. The first block B can
be singular, so use B_t=B+tI and A_t^T=B_t^-1 C. Each B column is a coordinate
vector: all nonzero eigenvalues are roots of unity, so B_t is nonsingular for
every rational 0<t<1. The identity

  det(B_t)^2 Z_(n/2)(A_t)=R(t),  R(0)=#PM(H)

and bound sum_S |d_S(t)^2-d_S(0)^2|<=8k 2^n t make polynomial-bit tiny t sufficient
for exact rounding, relative approximation and retained-law TV transfer.
Matrix condition numbers and physical success can be exponentially poor.

A distinct positive representation is

  Z_(n/2)(A)=E_sigma |Pf(D_sigma A-A^T D_sigma)|^2.

It is a legal unbiased estimator. Directed 4-cycle blocks give relative variance
2^d-1, even though that particular block family has an easy exact sampler.
Thus neither positivity nor a positive estimator removes the sampling barrier.

Graph parity encodings and Pfaffian minor summation are prior frameworks
(Matoya–Oki, 1912.00620). Priority of the common-chart reduction remains unknown.
No stronger PostBQP flattening or arbitrary-rank approximation algorithm was
obtained. OA113 now source-reports a general-graph unweighted matching FPRAS; a separate
binary-weight gadget extends it conditionally to rational nonnegative weights.
These claims remain unvalidated here. Thus PM-to-Z is not an approximation
hardness obstruction under that source, and supplies no reverse Z algorithm.
Historical claims that the matching FPRAS is still open have been corrected.

5. A RARE-HERALD NOISE COUNTEREXAMPLE WITH ALL COSTS VISIBLE

For R square, use TMSV amplitude lambda=2^-R^2, rational uniform fanout to R
herald modes, uniform transmission eta=1/R and the event exactly one photon in
every herald mode. The ideal conditional signal tends to Poisson(1). Add
independent phase-insensitive displacement noise nu=R^-a to every input mode
before loss. The noisy input is within trace distance lambda of a product
thermal reference whose herald probability is

  p0=[eta nu/(1+eta nu)^2]^R.

Since lambda/p0 tends to zero, the noisy conditional signal tends to vacuum.
The conditional trace-distance gap tends to 1-exp(-1). The signal energy stays
bounded. This refutes universal conditional-law stability based solely on weak
input noise, strong loss or bounded conditional energy.

Ideal log inverse success is Theta(R^3); noisy reference log inverse success
is Theta(R log R). Polynomial description size is not polynomial heralding cost.
Rational covariance/fanout data do not make every optical dilation amplitude
rational. This example is not a hardness reduction and does not itself refute
N81 noisy hardness by itself. The separate actual-N81 calculation is now banked:
under the specified all-mode noise, its positive-P covariance is relatively
exponentially close to thermal. Gaussian Radon-Nikodym/Gamma-posterior estimates
control the actual conditional law without dividing a global error by p_H.
Physical herald probability remains exponentially small.

An actual classical conditional sampler has also been derived. For supplied
positive-P covariance tA with (1-eps)I<=A<=(1+eps)I, use an inflated isotropic
Gaussian conditioned on the same PNR counts. Its independent gamma radii and

conditional law follows, with expected trials at most exp[C(d+kappa)eps], where

proof charges input bit size, NUMERIC photon count, output-energy/count cutoff,
accuracy, and the rejection bound. Consequently it is polynomial only under
those explicit polynomial budgets; a short binary encoding of a huge number
