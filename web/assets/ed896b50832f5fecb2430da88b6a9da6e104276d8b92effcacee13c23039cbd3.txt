STATEMENT

Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2, J_a=sum_i T_a^(i), C_N=sum_a J_a^2.

 R=8beta+4B+4,   a=exp(-2R)/q,
 Hstar=beta+B/sqrt(2),
 Kstar=q beta(1+cF)+qB/sqrt(2),
 Lstar=2q sqrt(beta(Hstar+1)),

For ANY fixed real symmetric A with ||A||<=Mstar and real |b|<=B,
and any field amplitude 0<=u<=1 (allowed to depend on N), the WHOLE Gibbs
density

 rho_N proportional to exp[((beta I+A) quadratic in J)/N +u J_b]

has an explicitly specified known-parameter positive identical-q-product
mixture sigma_N with

 T(rho_N,sigma_N)<=(2Cseed+Hstar N)exp(-2N),             (F1)
 Cseed=exp(1/beta)(8beta)^(D/2)Gamma(D/2+1),

for every integer N>=max{q, q(q-1)/(2sqrt(2)),1}. The bound can be capped
at one. The perturbation radius Mstar is extremely small and depends on q,

fixed anisotropy. No efficient preparation or finite-bit diffusion compiler

The scientific extension to a fixed small A/N rather than A/N^kappa with
