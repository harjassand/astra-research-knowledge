  F(c)=[-alpha-sum_k zeta_k c_k-Gamma/(S+2)+mu_bar(c)]/(S+1)=-theta(c),

where Gamma=sum gamma_k and mu_bar=sum mu_k c_k/S. At S=0,
F=-alpha-Gamma/2. For rational rates, theta_*>0 reduces to a finite exact

binomial(S0+r-1,r) count vectors; no polynomial-time guarantee is claimed.
