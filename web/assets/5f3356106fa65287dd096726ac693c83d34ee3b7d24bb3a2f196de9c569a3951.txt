RESULT
No PP-collapse transfer is proved. The direct bosonic KLM transfer has an exact physical obstruction: its R-to-one-mode buncher maps to a repeated-row exterior-power minor, hence amplitude zero for R>1. A constant-gap readout survives if R distinct fermion rails are kept, and the number-attenuation/loss filter has a fermionic false-herald bound g^2/p0. These repairs do not supply the computation.

The static-gate audit sharpened the remaining boundary. Moving interleaved no-double projectors to the terminal layer by commutation is false: for a rational up-mode swap G and |psi>=u_i†d_j†|0>, P G|psi>=0 while G P|psi>=u_j†d_j†|0> !=0. Exact standard-library verification is in revisions/r01_static_gate_audit.py/.json. This excludes naive reordering only.

The earlier easy-subclass statement is explicitly narrowed: a source whose idler-up and signal-down site-label supports stay disjoint has hard projection redundant and sector norm e_k(MM*), so that direct role-separated route is in the Gaussian/FLO boundary. A signal unitary that overlaps those labels can make the hard projector active; the bipartite easy argument does not cover it. A general active-projector route remains open.

The relevant local hard projector is P_hard=P_0+P_1=I-n_up n_down, exactly the grouped “charge 0 or 1” outcome in DiVincenzo–Terhal, not the two-mode parity measurement P_0+P_2 versus P_1. Primary source literature says grouped charge measurement with FLO participates in nondeterministic computation, so FLO simulability is not an all-model no-go. What is missing is a compiler from such interleaved operations to one explicit rational opposite-spin pair matrix and a single terminal product of hard projectors, with a legal output code and explicit positive branch bound. Revision r03 proves a simple sufficient deferral criterion for retired detector registers, but no universal scheme satisfying it was constructed or sourced.
