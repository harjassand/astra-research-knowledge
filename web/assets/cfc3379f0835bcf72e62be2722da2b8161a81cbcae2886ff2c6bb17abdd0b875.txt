CYCLE 3 LEAF — ADVERSARIAL FOSTER / ODE PERMANENCE AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the stated stochastic LP subclass; direct deterministic permanence proof for this family under arbitrary fixed positive rates (and under a common compact positive rate box). No counterexample found under the exact strict LP premises. The LP is sufficient, not necessary.

SCOPE AND INTERFACE

Let j range over a finite nonempty set J and i over a finite nonempty set I. For each pair (j,i), let k_ji be a positive integer. The reactions and fixed positive rates are

  0 -> A_j                 alpha_j       A_j -> 0                  delta_j
  A_j -> A_j+B_i           p_ji          A_j+B_i -> A_j            q_ji
  A_j+B_i -> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i  u_ji        (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i -> A_j+B_i  v_ji.

The stochastic semantics are standard falling-factorial mass action:
alpha_j, delta_j a_j, p_ji a_j, q_ji a_j b_i, u_ji a_j b_i, and
v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i, respectively, where (n)_r=0 for n<r.
The ODE uses ordinary monomials. In particular the reverse high-order
stochastic propensity is zero at a_j<=k_ji, whereas its ODE flux is
v_ji a_j^(k_ji+1)b_i for every positive a_j.

The proposed strict LP premises are positive ell_j,w_i satisfying

  c_j := delta_j ell_j - sum_i p_ji w_i > 0       for every j,
  d_ji := q_ji w_i - k_ji u_ji ell_j > 0          for every (j,i).

These are the exact margins used below. The reverse rates v_ji and immigration
rates alpha_j need only be positive; they do not enter feasibility of these
inequalities.

STOCHASTIC GENERATOR AUDIT

For U(a,b)=sum_j ell_j a_j + sum_i w_i b_i, direct summation over all six
reaction families gives the exact identity

  LU = C0 - sum_j c_j a_j - sum_(j,i) d_ji a_j b_i
           - sum_(j,i) k_ji v_ji ell_j (a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i,
  C0 = sum_j alpha_j ell_j.

This is not an asymptotic estimate: every term after C0 is nonpositive, and
the two strict LP margins are exactly the coefficients of the linear-A and
bilinear-A/B strata. Thus LU<=C0 everywhere. The term -d_ji a_jb_i is crucial
on low-A states: the factorial reverse term vanishes when a_j<=k_ji and
cannot control the positive forward autocatalysis there.

Now let A=sum_j a_j and set

  V = U + H 1_{A=0},       H > max_j ell_j.

The indicator is a bounded correction, so V remains nonnegative and
norm-like. Its generator has three exhaustive strata:

(1) A=0. Every A/B-catalytic channel is disabled. Each immigration jump leaves
the boundary and changes V by ell_j-H. Hence

  LV = sum_j alpha_j(ell_j-H) < 0,

uniformly over every b in N_0^I. This handles the infinite frozen face
{A=0, b arbitrary}; it is not an absorbing face because all alpha_j>0.

(2) A=1, so a=e_j for one j. Only the death A_j->0 activates the correction,
adding H delta_j. The high-order reverse channels are disabled since
(1)_(k_ji+1)=0 for k_ji>=1. Therefore

  LV = C0 - c_j + H delta_j - sum_i d_ji b_i.

For each j this tends to -infinity as |b|->infinity. All ties among active
reaction channels are already included in this exact generator sum; no
max-source or dominant-channel substitution is used.

(3) A>=2. No one-step reaction enters A=0: A_j->0 removes only one A, and
the reverse high-order reaction leaves one A_j in its product. Thus the
correction has zero generator contribution and LV=LU. Let
c_* = min_j c_j>0 and d_* = min_(j,i) d_ji>0. Then

  LV <= C0 - c_* A - d_* A sum_i b_i

(the omitted falling-factorial terms are nonpositive). This tends to -infinity
outside a finite subset of the A>=2 stratum: large A is controlled by c_*;
for bounded positive A, large total B is controlled by d_* A B. Together
with strata (1)-(2), LV<=-1 outside a finite set. The constant boundary
correction is sufficient; a correction proportional to b is unnecessary and
would create avoidable large jumps when the last A dies.

Nonexplosion is separate and valid. U is nonnegative and norm-like, every
reaction has a fixed finite jump, and its sublevel sets are finite. For
sigma_n=inf{t:U(X_t)>=n}, stopped Dynkin is valid on the finite set reached
before sigma_n and gives

  n P_x(sigma_n<=T) <= E_x U(X_(T wedge sigma_n)) <= U(x)+C0 T.

An exploding path must leave every finite U-sublevel set; infinitely many
jumps while remaining in a finite set are impossible because the total rates
there are bounded. Thus the minimal CTMC is nonexplosive, without using a
stationary law or an unrestricted Dynkin formula. The strict Foster drift,
finite sublevel sets, nonexplosion, and irreducibility imply positive
recurrence for this full class.

There is no hidden communicating-class obstruction under the all-positive
rates. Every enabled jump preserves N_0^(|J|+|I|) by the source factorial
convention. From any state with some A present, use A_j+B_i->A_j to drain
all B_i, then A_j->0 to drain A. From a state with A=0, first fire an
immigration. Conversely, from zero create any needed A_j by immigration,
create any needed B_i using A_j->A_j+B_i (temporarily create a catalyst if
its target A count is zero), then remove temporary A molecules. Every
specified finite path has positive probability. Also, the vectors 0->A_j
and A_j->A_j+B_i span the full coordinate lattice. Hence the entire lattice
is one closed irreducible class.

The correction does not invalidate the upper-generator bound: its only
positive contribution occurs on A=1 death-to-zero jumps and is at most
H max_j delta_j. Alternatively, and more cleanly, the coercive U alone has
LU<=C0 and proves nonexplosion by localization. The candidate therefore has
no explosion gap under these exact quantifiers.

DECISIVE EXACT LOW-COUNT CHECK

Smallest nontrivial case J=I={1}, k=1. Take alpha=1, delta=2, p=1,
q=2, u=1, v=1. The unit weights ell=w=1 satisfy delta ell>p w and
q w>k u ell. With H=2, at A=0 the exact drift is -1 for every B; at
(A,B)=(1,b), LV=4-b; and for a>=2,

  LU = 1 - a - ab - a(a-1)b <= 1-a-ab.

This explicitly checks the boundary-correction sign and the disabled
factorial-reverse stratum. No positive-drift stratum remains.

The second LP inequality cannot be omitted from the simple linear proof. In
the same one-A/one-B, k=1 network choose delta=2, p=1, q=1, u=2 (and any
alpha,v>0). The first inequality holds for ell=w=1, but at (a,b)=(1,n) the
factorial reverse propensity is zero and

  LU = alpha - 1 + n,

which is unbounded above. This is a counterexample to the weakened drift
claim only, not a counterexample to recurrence: the exact strict LP hypothesis
fails, and no nonrecurrence conclusion is inferred.

The LP is conservative, not necessary. In the smallest case k=1 with all six
rates alpha=delta=p=q=u=v=1, its two strict inequalities would require
ell>w and w>ell simultaneously. Nevertheless this network is complex balanced
at (a,b)=(1,1): each of its three reversible-pair fluxes balances. The
standard product-Poisson result gives a stationary law for the stochastic
mass-action chain, and the direct ODE argument below gives permanence. This
falsifies any claim that the LP is necessary; it does not falsify its use as
a sufficient certificate.

DETERMINISTIC ODE PERMANENCE, PROVED INDEPENDENTLY

For positive concentrations a_j,b_i the mass-action ODE is

  adot_j = alpha_j - delta_j a_j
             + sum_i k_ji a_j b_i (u_ji-v_ji a_j^k_ji),
  bdot_i = sum_j a_j(p_ji-q_ji b_i).

The stoichiometric span is all of R^(|J|+|I|): 0->A_j supplies each A
coordinate and A_j->A_j+B_i supplies each B coordinate. Thus the positive
stoichiometric class is the whole positive orthant. The argument below does
not use the LP; it uses only fixed positive rates and the special separated
coordinate structure of this family.

First, for each i put r_i^+=max_j p_ji/q_ji. At b_i>=r_i^+ every summand
in bdot_i is nonpositive, so

  b_i(t) <= B_i^0 := max{b_i(0),r_i^+}.

For each j put h_j=max_i (u_ji/v_ji)^(1/k_ji). When a_j>=h_j, every
catalytic term a_j b_i(u_ji-v_ji a_j^k) is nonpositive. Hence adot_j<=
alpha_j-delta_j a_j there. Any M_j>max{h_j,alpha_j/delta_j} is an inward upper
barrier, and a_j(t) eventually enters and remains below M_j, with finite
entry time depending on the initial value.

These bounds rule out finite-time blow-up. They also imply eventual lower
bounds. While a_j is sufficiently small, the B upper bounds give

  adot_j >= alpha_j-delta_j a_j
                 -sum_i k_ji v_ji B_i^0 a_j^(k_ji+1) >= alpha_j/2.

Choose eta_j^0>0 to make the last inequality hold for 0<=a_j<=eta_j^0.
Thus a_j reaches eta_j^0 in finite time if below it and cannot cross back
below it. The thresholds eta_j^0 may depend on the initial B^0; this is used
only to obtain a first positive lower bound.

For each i, write

  Q_i(t)=sum_j q_ji a_j(t)>0,
  R_i(t)=(sum_j p_ji a_j(t))/Q_i(t),
  bdot_i=Q_i(t)(R_i(t)-b_i(t)).

The value R_i(t) is a convex combination of p_ji/q_ji, so it lies in
[r_i^-,r_i^+], where r_i^-=min_j p_ji/q_ji>0. Once all a_j>=eta_j^0,
Q_i>=sum_j q_ji eta_j^0>0. The scalar tracking equation then puts each b_i,
after a finite initial-dependent time, inside the parameter-only interval
[r_i^-/2, r_i^++1]; that interval is forward invariant because the vector
field points inward at both endpoints.

Now use B_i<=r_i^++1 in the adot_j lower estimate and choose a smaller
parameter-only eta_j>0 such that, at a_j<=eta_j,

  alpha_j-delta_j eta_j
      -sum_i k_ji v_ji(r_i^++1)eta_j^(k_ji+1) > 0.

This gives the uniform lower barrier a_j>=eta_j after finite entry. At the
upper face a_j=M_j the previous upper-barrier estimate is strictly inward.
At b_i=r_i^-/2, every p_ji-q_ji b_i is positive; at b_i=r_i^++1 every
p_ji-q_ji b_i is negative. Consequently

  K = product_j[eta_j,M_j] x product_i[r_i^-/2,r_i^++1]

is compact, convex, lies in the positive orthant, is forward invariant, and
absorbs every positive initial state in finite time. Its coordinates depend
only on the fixed rates and k_ji; the entry time may depend on the initial
state. This proves deterministic permanence directly and independently of
the stochastic Foster theorem.

The ODE argument extends to arbitrary measurable rates in a supplied common
box [kappa,K] with kappa>0 by replacing p/q, u/v, alpha,delta,v bounds by
their worst-case interval endpoints. The resulting box remains common to
all paths, and each entry bound remains path-independent for fixed initial
state. This special-family proof is not a proof of the general N33 claim.

DETERMINISTIC/STOCHASTIC SEPARATION AND STATUS

The ODE and CTMC have different interfaces. The ODE reverse flux is
v a^(k+1)b and is nonzero at every positive a; the CTMC reverse propensity
v(a)_(k+1)b is zero on low integer strata. The LP's bilinear inequality
handles precisely the stochastic low-count case, while ODE permanence here
follows from positive immigration and scalar upper/lower barriers without
that LP. No deterministic permanence argument by itself establishes a CTMC
stationary law or nonexplosion. Here the stochastic result is separately
proved by exact generator drift, localization, class reachability, and the
Foster criterion. Conversely, failure of the LP only blocks this particular
sufficient Foster certificate; it does not imply stochastic nonrecurrence.

N33 is a broader supplied all-endotactic actual-power-law measurable-rate
proof candidate with a common compact convex absorber per positive class;
its relevant dependencies are affine activity/ties, weighted essential-source
drift, compact plateaux, and scale absorption. It treats a deterministic ODE,
not a stochastic chain. N34 keeps local certificates, finite initial-set
chains, and full-class absorbers distinct and leaves unbounded global
recognition open; it is not a recurrence theorem. Neither is used as a premise
in the stochastic proof above or in the specialized direct ODE proof.

CLAIM CONTRACT, COST, AND EXTERNAL BASELINE

Supplied inputs for the stochastic claim are the finite integer k_ji, all six
positive rate arrays, and one positive solution (ell,w) of the strict LP.
The calculation is O(|J||I|) terms and O(|J|+|J||I|) inequalities; an exact
rational LP solver can decide feasibility for rational data, but no complexity
or bit-height bound is established here. Nonexplosion and recurrence are
proved for each fixed feasible rate vector, not uniformly over unbounded rate
families. The ODE proof takes only positive rates (or their supplied compact
positive envelope) and does not acquire the LP witness.

Strongest relevant established baseline: the product-form theorem of
Anderson, Craciun, and Kurtz gives a product-Poisson stationary law on each
closed irreducible class whenever the deterministic mass-action network has
a complex-balanced equilibrium; Anderson, Cappelletti, Koyama, and Kurtz give
non-explosivity for complex-balanced stochastic mass action. These cover the
all-rates-one one-pair non-necessity fixture, but they do not prove the LP
subclass result for arbitrary positive rates, nor any all-WR recurrence claim.
The current result is a self-contained internal Foster proof for the named
family and LP interface. External review and novelty/priority remain open.

A decisive falsifier for the stochastic certificate would be (i) a state in
one of the three A-count strata where the displayed exact generator differs
from the channel sum, (ii) a reachable closed class other than the full
lattice under all-positive rates, or (iii) an explosion path contradicting
the localized coercive-U bound. None occurs. For the deterministic statement,
a counterexample would need violate one of the componentwise barrier/tracking
inequalities while keeping all rates positive; the displayed equation
structure rules this out. Failure of the LP is only failure of this
certificate and is not evidence of nonrecurrence.
