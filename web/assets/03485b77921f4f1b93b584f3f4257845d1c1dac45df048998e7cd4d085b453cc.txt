RANK CODIMENSION FROM AN INVARIANT GREEN OPERATOR
8 October 2026. Internally derived candidate; external validation/priority unknown.

This is the root reconstruction of the lower-bound mechanism developed with
sol_hyperbolic_audit and spectral_geometry. It applies to arbitrary matrices,
not just diagonal filters, and does not require a coherent optimizer theorem.

Let H be a d-dimensional irreducible unitary G-carrier. Let L be a positive,
self-adjoint conjugation-invariant operator on End(H), with kernel span(I).
Write its distinct positive eigenvalues as lambda_1<...<lambda_J, invariant
spectral projectors Pi_j of dimension b_j, and E(A)=<A,L A>_HS. Irreducibility
implies Tr_2 Pi_j=(b_j/d)I under vectorization. Define lambda_(J+1)=infinity.

For 1<=k<d and any A of rank at most d-k, choose a rank-k orthogonal projection
P in its right kernel. Then AP=0 and Tr(PA)=0. Write A=aI+X with Tr X=0.
The kernel condition gives ka=-Tr(PX). For P0=P-(k/d)I, put

  G(P)=<P0,L^(-1)P0>_HS.

Here L^(-1) means the inverse on the traceless subspace. Hilbert-space
Cauchy-Schwarz, including complex X, gives

  k^2 |a|^2 <= G(P) E(A),
  ||X||_HS^2 <= E(A)/lambda_1,
  ||A||_HS^2 <= E(A)[d G(P)/k^2 + 1/lambda_1].           (1)

No positivity of A is needed. Positivity replacement is needed for a legal
filter upper construction, but not for this converse.

The normalized vectorization of P has Schmidt rank k. Its projection onto any
invariant subspace of dimension b has squared norm at most k b/d. Restoring
||P||_HS^2=k therefore gives

  ||Pi_j P||_HS^2 <= k^2 b_j/d,
  sum_(j>=1) ||Pi_j P||_HS^2 = k-k^2/d <= k.

Thus every cutoff 0<=K<=J gives

  G(P) <= B_K(k)
       := (k^2/d) sum_(j=1)^K b_j/lambda_j + k/lambda_(K+1).

Let B(k)=min_K B_K(k). Combining with (1) proves the general converse

  inf_(rank A<=d-k) E(A)/||A||_HS^2
       >= [d B(k)/k^2 + 1/lambda_1]^(-1).               (2)

This is an exact finite spectral statement, with no geometry or asymptotic
optimizer premise. The cutoff may be chosen from supplied spectral data.
It complements the low-rank uncertainty bound; it is useful precisely when
the low-rank bound degenerates as the allowed rank approaches d.

SPECIALIZATION TO Sym^N(C^m)

Set s=m-1, d=binom(N+s,s). The Casimir eigenvalues and dimensions are
lambda_j=j(j+s), b_j=binom(j+s,s)^2-binom(j+s-1,s)^2, 1<=j<=N.
For s>=2, b_j<=A_s j^(2s-1) with a constant depending only on s. With
R=(d/k)^(1/(2s)), K=min(N,floor R), the low-frequency sum and high tail give

  G(P) <= C_s k^(1+1/s) d^(-1/s),
  Lambda_N,m(d-k) >= c_s (k/d)^(1-1/s)
                    = Theta_m((N/d) k^(1-1/s)).        (3)

The last equality compares the displayed scale, not an unproved matching
upper. Constants use d^(1/s) comparable to N for fixed m,N>=1. If K=N,
the tail is zero; the same bound follows without requiring K<N.

For s=1, b_j=2j+1 and (2j+1)/(j(j+1))=1/j+1/(j+1). The same cutoff gives

  G(P) <= (k^2/d)[3+log(d/k)],
  Lambda_N,2(d-k) >= 1/[3.5+log(d/k)].                  (4)

For example, k=1 yields a dimension-dependent capacity transition:
Lambda_N,2(d-1) >= c/log(N+1), while for m>=3,
Lambda_N,m(d-1) >= c_m N/d. The radial occupation-chain harmonic taper supplies
matching orders; its complete arbitrary-k upper is owned by spectral_geometry
and sol_hyperbolic_audit. Exact corner orientation optimality is not asserted.

OPERATIONAL CONSEQUENCE AND BOUNDARY

The separate supplied-carrier Gibbs theorem proves e_Q comparable to
Lambda_N,m(Q)/N^2 for fixed positive temperature-parameter interval. Therefore
(3)-(4) are whole-density trace reconstruction converses with unrestricted
classical labels for that experiment. They do not establish iid sector transport,
efficient construction of general spectral projectors, or a physical gate cost.
The mathematical argument is independent of finite diagnostics; agreement among
workers is not external validation. Prior-art comparison remains open.
