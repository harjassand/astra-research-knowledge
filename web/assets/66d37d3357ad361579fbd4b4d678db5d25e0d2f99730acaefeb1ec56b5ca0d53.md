# Primary-source notes: canonical EB majorization seam

## Directly relevant prior

**M. Horodecki, P. W. Shor, and M. B. Ruskai, “General Entanglement Breaking Channels,” Rev. Math. Phys. 15 (2003), 629–641, arXiv:quant-ph/0302031.**

- [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0302031); [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0302031).
- The paper states the Holevo form \(\Phi(\rho)=\sum_k R_k\operatorname{Tr}(F_k\rho)\), with states \(R_k\); for TP maps the \(F_k\) form a POVM (full text lines 29–50).
- Its Theorem 4 gives equivalence among EB, Holevo form, separable Choi state, and rank-one Kraus form; the TP qualification adds POVM normalization (lines 84–111).
- It explains that a TP adjoint/unital relation preserves POVM structure and that precomposition of a Holevo channel by a positivity/trace-preserving map replaces effects by their adjoint images (lines 130–138). This supports standard representation facts but is not the c7 domination argument.
- The paper does **not** state the full-domain result that a TP EB map quadratically dominating a unital self-adjoint target is necessarily unital, nor the PSD majorant
  \[
  (\Gamma_M+\Gamma_N)/2-(\Lambda+\Lambda^*)/2
  =\tfrac12\sum_y|u_y-v_y\rangle\langle u_y-v_y|.
  \]

## Adjacent structural sources checked

**K. Chen and L.-A. Wu, “A matrix realignment method for recognizing entanglement,” Phys. Rev. A 69, 022312 (2004), arXiv:quant-ph/0205017.** [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0205017); [published full text](https://harvest.aps.org/v2/journals/articles/10.1103/PhysRevA.69.022312/fulltext).

The realignment/cross-norm criterion gives a necessary separability test via a trace-norm bound on a realigned bipartite state. It is relevant to separable Choi-state detection and was checked as a possible “realignment” collision. It does not imply or state a Loewner majorant between a symmetric EB superoperator and a same-POVM canonical channel.

**J. K. Korbicz, P. Horodecki, and R. Horodecki, “Quantum-correlation breaking channels, broadcasting scenarios, and finite Markov chains,” Phys. Rev. A 86, 042319 (2012).** [APS record](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.86.042319).

This is adjacent to measurement maps, state broadcasting, and EB channels. Its question concerns channels that break broader quantum correlations and their broadcasting/Markov structure; no statement matching the c7 same-effect/second-POVM Gram domination was located.
