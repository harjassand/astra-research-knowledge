AUDITED INTERFACE
Fixed finite dimension D, H=diag(0,h1,...,h_{D-1}) with hi>0, known
0<E<=I and E|0>=|0>. Hard support in sum_i H_i<=nc for n parallel uses.
Energy-ordered upper Cholesky E=T* T, B=T^-1, A=B*B. Ground is a direct
scalar1 block because E is Hermitian and fixes |0>. Define

 phi(s)=log||exp(-sH/2) A exp(-sH/2)||,
 s*=inf{s>=0:A<=exp(sH)},
 g(c)=inf_{s>=0}[phi(s)+sc], c>=0.

Audit conclusion: the exact compressed-Gram identity, Cholesky degeneracy

STRONGER FINITE-n STATEMENT, UNIFORM IN ALL c>=0
Put hmax=max_i hi, K=(D-1)hmax, C_D=(D-1)+D(D-1)^2. Then

 n g(c)-2s* K-D log(n+1)-C_D
    <= log||B^tensor n P_{n,c}||^2 <= n g(c),                         (A)

where P_{n,c}=1[sum H_i<=nc]. The lower bound is uniform simultaneously over
all energy densities c and all n>=1 for this fixed (E,H). Consequently the
hard-support miss is between exp(-ng(c)) and
exp(2s*K+C_D)(n+1)^D exp(-ng(c)).

The old lowest-energy rounding proves a pointwise O(log n) remainder but
can have constants involving tiny p_j. The adjusted budget and max-probability
rounding below remove that dependence.

PRELIMINARY AUDITS
1. Upper triangular T and B lower individual energy. Tensor powers preserve
every total-energy cutoff, and restricted T_P has inverse B_P. Thus
P E^tensor n P|ran(P)=T_P* T_P and its smallest eigenvalue is
||B^tensor n P||^-2 exactly. No tensor leakage occurs.
2. For energy-block-upper factors T1,T2 of the same E, U=T2 T1^-1 is unitary
and block-upper. A finite block-upper unitary must be block diagonal (first
block-column norm orthogonality, then induction). It commutes with H. Hence
B2=B1 U*, A2=U A1 U*, preserving every threshold and tilted norm. Changes
of basis inside equal-energy blocks are covered by the same argument. Strict
positive definiteness of E is essential for inverses; singular effects are
outside this theorem. Nonunique ground is also outside the declared contract.
3. F(z)=B exp(-zH/2) is analytic on any finite strip, with
||F(a+it)||=||F(a)|| because the imaginary factor is unitary on the right.
Three-lines norm interpolation proves phi convex. Ground contributes exactly
1, so phi>=0, and phi=0 for every s>=s*. For E!=I, s*>0 and finite; for E=I,
phi=g=s*=0 and all formulas are exact trivially. The minimizing s for c>0
can be restricted to [0,s*]. Thus g is nondecreasing, concave, and globally
s*-Lipschitz on c>=0.
4. On the top eigenspace W of C_s=e^-sH/2 A e^-sH/2 with eigenvalue lambda,
P_W C'_s P_W=-lambda P_W H P_W. Therefore
partial phi(s)=[-hmax(W),-hmin(W)]. At an interior minimizer s>0 of phi+sc,
one can choose a unit w in W with <w,Hw>=c. At the endpoint s=0, optimality
gives hmin(W)<=c, so choose w in W with mean energy <=c. At c=0 choose ground.

UNIFORM TYPE PROBABILITY LEMMA
For any probability vector p on at most D coordinates and any n, choose
j* attaining max p_j, so p_j*>=1/D. Let k_j=floor(np_j) for j!=j*, and
k_j*=n-sum_{j!=j*}k_j. Zero-probability coordinates receive zero counts.
Write a=k_j*-np_j*, with 0<=a<=D-1, and q_j=k_j/n. Then

 n D(q||p)=sum_j k_j log(k_j/(np_j))
    <= k_j* log(1+a/(np_j*))
    <= a+a^2/(np_j*) <= (D-1)+D(D-1)^2=C_D.                          (B)

Terms for j!=j* are nonpositive. For the exact multinomial type probability,

 p_type=(n!/product k_j!) product p_j^k_j
        >=(n+1)^-D exp[-n D(q||p)]
        >=exp(-C_D)(n+1)^-D.                                       (C)

Here is an elementary proof of the first inequality, avoiding imported
method-of-types estimates. For the probability vector q=k/n the count vector
k is a mode: for any other count vector ell, the ratio P_q(ell)/P_q(k) is a
product of factors k_i! k_i^(ell_i-k_i)/ell_i!. Each factor is <=1 whether
ell_i>=k_i or ell_i<k_i; if k_i=0 and ell_i>0 the probability is zero.
There are at most (n+1)^D possible count vectors, so P_q(k)>=(n+1)^-D.
Changing q to p multiplies this probability by exp[-nD(q||p)].

Energy rounding has fixed absolute error. Since only the j* count increases
and the sum of all other decrements is a,

  |sum_j k_j h_j - n sum_j p_j h_j| <= a hmax <= K.                  (D)

PROOF OF UNIFORM LOWER BOUND (A)
If c<=K/n, the all-ground vector gives log norm >=0, while
ng(c)<=s*nc<=s*K, already sufficient for (A).

Otherwise set c'=c-K/n>0 and choose a dual minimizer s in [0,s*] and top unit
w as above: mean h=c' when s>0, and h<=c' when s=0. Expand w in the H basis,
p_j=|w_j|^2, and construct the uniform-probability type k. Let eta be the
normalized projection of w^tensor n onto that type. Its total energy
q_n=sum k_j h_j satisfies q_n<=nh+K<=nc, so eta is legal.

The normalized tilted matrix C_s/lambda is positive and has eigenvector w
with eigenvalue1, hence C_s/lambda>=|w><w|. Tensor order gives

 <eta,C_s^tensor n eta> >= lambda^n |<eta,w^tensor n>|^2
                         =lambda^n p_type.

Because eta has exact energy q_n,

 ||B^tensor n eta||^2
   =exp(s q_n)<eta,C_s^tensor n eta>
   >=exp[n phi(s)+s q_n] p_type.

If s>0, use q_n>=nc'-K. If s=0, no energy loss is needed. In both cases,

 log||B^tensor n P_{n,c}||^2
   >=ng(c')-s*K-Dlog(n+1)-C_D
   >=ng(c)-2s*K-Dlog(n+1)-C_D,

using s<=s* and the s*-Lipschitz property of g. This proves (A).
The upper bound is the original exact tensor-order tilted converse.

PHYSICAL CONSTRUCTION AND SUCCESS PROBABILITY
chi=B^tensor n eta/||B^tensor n eta|| stays under the hard support because
B lowers energy. Its miss is exactly ||B^tensor n eta||^-2. To prepare it by
a local filter, use M=B exp(-sH/2)/sqrt(lambda). M*M=C_s/lambda<=I, so
M is a contraction. On eta, its n-fold success probability is
<eta,(C_s/lambda)^tensor n eta>>=p_type>=e^-C_D(n+1)^-D. The conditional
state is chi. This is polynomially small success, not an uncharged always-
successful preparation protocol. The theorem constrains the accepted channel
input's hard support. Repeated attempts, input acquisition, arbitrary coherent
reference/driving work and engineering precision are distinct charged resources.
If eta itself is obtained by projecting w^tensor n onto the chosen type, that
first projection succeeds with probability p_type. The complete two-stage
attempt then succeeds with probability at least p_type^2, still polynomial;
direct coherent synthesis of eta has a different implementation/cost contract.

EXACT FINITE-USE ALL-SEPARABLE COMPARATOR — VERIFIED
Let 0=h0<h1<...<hk be the distinct positive spectral cutoff energies of H
and put
  ell0=0,
  ellj=-log lambda_min((P_{H<=hj} E P_{H<=hj})|ran(P_{H<=hj})).
Write K_n(c) for the integer allocation optimum

 max sum_{j=1}^k n_j ellj
   subject to n_j nonnegative integers,
              sum_j n_j<=n, sum_j n_j hj<=nc.                        (S1)

The exact n-use separable minimum miss is exp[-K_n(c)], including arbitrary
unequal product factors and arbitrary correlated separable mixtures.

Proof of the hard-contract point: if a positive mixture has zero weight outside
P_{n,c}, each positive product component has zero weight outside it. A local
factor's highest occupied energy h_j is detected by its positive H-basis
diagonal entry at that level. The product of all highest occupied diagonal
entries is positive, so the sum of these local maxima must be <=nc. For each
factor, the miss is at least exp(-ellj); the product miss is thus bounded by
the corresponding feasible (S1) allocation. Every mixture is an average of
such legal product misses, hence cannot improve the smallest product value.
Conversely, use a minimum-eigenvalue vector of each E_{<=hj} on the n_j assigned
sites and use ground on the remaining sites. This product is legal and has
miss exactly exp[-sum n_j ellj]. If an eigenvector actually has smaller
maximal support energy than its assigned cutoff, it remains legal; no extra
claim of exact occupation of the highest level is needed. This proves (S1).

Define the continuous allocation hull

 g_sep(c)=max {sum_{j=0}^k pi_j ellj:
                   pi_j>=0, sum pi_j=1, sum pi_j hj<=c}.

Ordinary finite-dimensional linear-program duality gives

 g_sep(c)=inf_{s>=0}{sc+max_{0<=j<=k}(ellj-s hj)}.                     (S2)

At c=0 the ground gives value0 and s>=max_j ellj/hj attains the dual value0;
for c>0 the feasible ground law has strict budget slack, or one can use direct
LP strong duality. Thus the curve is the nondecreasing upper concave envelope
of the cutoff points, with allowed budget <=c and a high-energy plateau.

For every n and c, floor n pi_j for all positive levels of an optimal
continuous allocation and fill unused sites with ground. Energy can only fall,
and the exponent loss is at most sum_{j=1}^k ellj. Hence, uniformly over c>=0,

 n g_sep(c)-sum_{j=1}^k ellj <= K_n(c)<=n g_sep(c).                    (S3)

In particular the finite remainder is O(k ellmax), independent of n and c.
This comparator is fully informed about E,H and allowed unequal products.
The entangled exponent g(c)>=g_sep(c) because every product probe is legal
in the entangled optimization; this inequality is also immediate from the
operational variational definitions rather than matrix comparison guesses.

N32 FULL-CURVE CONSEQUENCE — ANALYTICAL, NOT A NUMERIC PROOF
For H+=diag(1,2) and E+=[[1/2,sqrt2/4],[sqrt2/4,3/4]],
the cutoff points are (0,0),(1,log2),(2,log4), so
  g_sep(c)=min(c log2,log4).
The entangled tilted matrix uses A+=[[2,-sqrt2],[-sqrt2,3]] and has
  s*=log((1+sqrt17)/2)>log2.
At s=s*, the top full eigenspace contains ground and the excited threshold
vector w*, with mean energy h*=<w*,H+w*> (approximately 1.2876894374).
Therefore g(c)=s*c throughout 0<=c<=h*. At s=0, A+'s top eigenvalue is4
with unit vector (-1/sqrt3,sqrt(2/3)), whose mean energy is5/3. Thus
  g(c)=log4 for every c>=5/3.
Concavity, g(0)=0 and g(2)=log4 imply

  g(c)>g_sep(c) for EVERY 0<c<2,
  g(c)=g_sep(c) at c=0 and every c>=2.

Indeed one interior point h* has g(h*)>h*log2. Its chords to the two endpoint
points lie strictly above the separable line at all other interior c. Combining
(A) and (S3), for each fixed 0<c<2 the entangled/separable miss ratio has
logarithm n[g(c)-g_sep(c)]+O(log n), a strict exponential advantage in the
fixed-use, exact hard-support contract. The initial and terminal curve portions
are exact; the intermediate part is the one-dimensional tilted variational
formula, with no numerical optimizer claim needed.
