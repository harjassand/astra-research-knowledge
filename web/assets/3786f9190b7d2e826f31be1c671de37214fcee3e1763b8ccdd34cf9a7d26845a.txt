1. Guocheng Zhen, Chengkai Zhu, Ranyiliu Chen, Xin Wang, “Almost One Bit Violation of Minimum-Output Rényi Entropy Additivity Simultaneously at All Orders,” arXiv:2609.18747v1, submitted 2026-09-16.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18747 ; full text https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18747v1

Load-bearing source construction inspected: Sections 2.1–2.3 define the inverse-closed branches of a direct product F_d^r, K=2rd, and a real orthogonal representation; complementary channels are the random-unitary channel and its branch Gram complement. The Bell-output quotient groups branch pairs by their relative group operation; the exact fibre profile gives one fibre of size K, (r-1)K^2/(2r) fibres of size 2, and the rest size 1. Lemma 4.1 is the exact Gram-radius identity: the coefficient-matrix supremum equals max_x ||G_x-I||_HS, which exactly captures the global pure-input purity, not just a coherent-input subset. Sections 4.1–4.4 combine this quadratic-radius estimate with the exact rank/fibre spectrum to certify a gap for p<=2 and interpolate moments for p>2. The p=1 effect is not a coherent-input-only claim: the radius controls every pure input. Sections 6–7 transfer the free estimate to finite Haar-orthogonal tensor representations by strong convergence and retain the quotient rank via uniform linear independence of the finite set of group elements.

Source status: the paper states an asymptotic high-probability finite Haar-orthogonal construction for fixed parameters as matrix dimension tends to infinity, and a non-random existence sequence from strong convergence of finite-dimensional RAAG representations. That existence corollary gives neither explicit matrices nor an algorithm. Its finite-output K=320 specialization reports a gap >1.4e-3 nats simultaneously for all Renyi orders. All of these are SOURCE-REPORTED in this packet; I did not validate the full source proof or its imported strong-convergence theorems. The Gram-radius identity itself is source-reported in Lemma 4.1 and is re-derived independently in DERIVATION.txt §1; it is not a novelty claim.

2. Guocheng Zhen, Ranyiliu Chen, Xin Wang, “Closed-Form Arbitrary-Gap Rényi Nonadditivity Above Any Positive Order via Direct Products of Free Groups and High-Girth Graphs,” arXiv:2610.06533v1, submitted 2026-10-05.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06533 ; full text https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06533v1

The abstract reports closed-form real-algebraic finite-dimensional channels with arbitrarily large gaps uniformly above positive Renyi cutoffs. Its p=1 specialization reports output dimension 256, input dimension 2^37,608,898, and gap >1/1024 nats. Cycle2 locally replays the source's scalar filter, dimension and entropy substitutions, not the full graph, operator, and entropy proof. Source claim remains SOURCE-REPORTED.

3. Laura Shou and Alexey V. Gorshkov, “A constructive violation of additivity of minimum output von Neumann entropy,” arXiv:2609.23946v2, submitted 2026-09-20, revised 2026-09-24.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23946

This source reports an explicit non-random finite-dimensional von Neumann MOE additivity violation using a finite-dimensional imitation of free-Haar behavior, and an extension to arbitrary target gap uniformly over p>=p0>0. Cycle2 inspected the displayed theorem and parameter inequalities, but not a full independent proof. It is an earlier same-task baseline than the two papers above in this local scan.

Prior local quantum capability cards N18, N19, N32, N40, N62 concern expected-cost or hard-support detection, not MOE additivity; none is used as a premise in the present derivation. They remain relevant to the programme's distinct detection branch and their contracts must not be conflated with this channel problem.

Novelty/priority: no claim. The Pauli/Sidon no-go is a scoped internal derivation and has not been compared against all prior Pauli-channel entropy inequalities or externally reviewed.
