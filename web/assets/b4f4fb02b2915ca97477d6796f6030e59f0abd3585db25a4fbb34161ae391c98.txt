After the Sol baseline freeze and the explicit CROSS_PHASE release, the
stronger attack in revisions/UNIVERSAL_CLONER_SLICE_NO_GO.txt closes the
following route question. For the d-dimensional optimal universal cloner,
the canonical comparator built by measuring one receiver and preparing the
same slice effects has exact best Dirichlet constant

  c_slice(d)=2+(3d+4)/(2(d+1)^2)>2.

The proof is an all-d Liouville-trace argument: the POVM measurement-prepare
middle map has trace d-1 on the traceless Hermitian space, so after the two
cloner marginals the comparator's trace is only lambda_d^2(d-1). Its least
eigenvalue is at most lambda_d^2/(d+1), yielding the constant above. A Haar
rank-one POVM attains this bound, and rank-one refinement shows coarse
effects cannot improve it. In the qubit case, the strongest proposed
branchwise CP gate fails for every nonzero POVM effect by two incompatible
principal Choi diagonal constraints. The exact checker passes.

This is a construction-class obstruction, not a counterexample to the
target: the independent Haar EB channel has shrink 1/(d+1) and satisfies
I-Psi=2(I-Phi_d) exactly for every universal cloner. It does not arise from
the canonical same-effect broadcaster slices. Thus the universal problem
needs a rounder built from broader channel/extension structure. The present
work neither proves nor refutes universal c=2 or the weaker collective
variance implication.
