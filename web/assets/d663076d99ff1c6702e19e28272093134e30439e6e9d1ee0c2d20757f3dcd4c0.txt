1. ROBUST FINITE RANK WITNESS: NONSOFIC HYPERBOLIC GROUP
Source credit: family252, A torsion-free hyperbolic group that is not residually
finite, construction.tex. Its marked lines, Cartesian wiring, integral rank
reduction and interpolation identity are inherited from the released source.
The new argument preserves the rank contradiction under approximate
permutation relations; it does not assume permutation stability.

Fix h=20, c=100^(-20), r=100/c=10^42 and prime q=2^521-1. The 519-step exact
Lucas-Lehmer certificate was recomputed. Sample one projective direction at
each point of S^20, |S|=floor(q/100). A finite alteration removes short-cycle
labels, leaving a punctured point-line graph of girth>=14, N>=cm/4 lines,
m=q^20 points, degree>=4r^2 outside E, |E|<=m/(2r).
Delta=Nr/4-2m-r|E|>=15m/4. A rejection algorithm accepts with probability>1/2.
This is a finite effective acquisition procedure, but its tables are
astronomical and have not been emitted. m has 3137 decimal digits as a count.

For arbitrary D-point edge permutations with triangle Hamming defect<=delta
and fixed-point fraction<=lambda for every nondegenerate rectangle, the proof
gives the dimension-independent certificate
  Delta <= r^2 [ S_inc delta + N lambda/8 ],
where S_inc=sum_(z outside E)|I(z)|<=N(q-1).
Both defects <=1/(100rq) are impossible. Key steps: trace moments force high
real rank; bounded integral row norm keeps at least half that rank modulo q;
triangle errors cost at most delta D rank per matrix block; rank-one Lagrange
interpolation combines all pointwise low-rank bounds into one contradiction.
It never divides by D in the field; q dividing D is harmless.

Use hyperbolic triangle angles (27pi/50,9pi/50,9pi/50). The O-link has cycles
of length>=4 and the V,W links immerse in the girth>=14 incidence graph.
All link cycles have angular length>2pi; rectangle turns have distance>pi.
Standard CAT link and Cartan-Hadamard theorems give a proper CAT(-1) universal
cover, a torsion-free word-hyperbolic group and a finite 2D classifying complex.
Rectangles are nontrivial closed local geodesics. Soficity would supply exactly
the arbitrarily accurate finite permutation interface rejected above.

At least one rectangle is killed by every map into every sofic group: otherwise
a finite product of suitable sofic targets preserves all rectangles. Splitting
vertices also removes the presentation's gratuitous free factor and gives a
nonsofic CAT(-1) core H, with G=H*F_(B+2r-3), B the number of Cartesian blocks.
This gives an explicit common-kernel word after finite acquisition. Enumerate
based core rectangles h_1,...,h_M and choose an identification free generator
t. Put u_j=t^j h_j t^(-j), W_1=u_1, W_j=[W_(j-1),u_j]. Distinct conjugate
copies of H are free factors in the kernel of H*<t> -> <t>, so W_M is nontrivial.
It lies in every normal closure of h_j, hence every map to a sofic group kills
it. Expanded length is at most 16*2^M; a straight-line expression has O(M)
nodes. Neither huge expression nor incidence instance was materialized.

Consequences: a candidate nonsofic exact/property-A hyperbolic group; ordinary
hyperbolic word-problem algorithms remain available after fixed presentation
compilation. These do not make acquiring this presentation practical.
Nonsofic existence alone is not new: released family197 and Ten Advances
already cover it. Nonsofic hyperbolic actions are not nonsofic groups.

2. SHARP BOUNDED-GAUSSIAN EXPERIMENT MEMORY
Let Theta be a fixed bounded nonempty Gaussian mean set, fixed positive
covariance whitened, and s=dim aff(Theta)>=1. Encoder sees X~N(theta,I), never
theta. It retains total hybrid dimension D=sum_j d_j. Decoder outputs a fresh
classical observation. Independent shared classical randomness is allowed;
entanglement assistance, retained real-valued data and input-dependent side
registers outside D are not. Then the standalone derivation proves
  e_D(Theta) = Theta_Theta(D^(-2/s))
in worst-parameter total variation, even for s+1 affinely independent means.
Upper is classical; lower permits arbitrary quantum memory. Equal order and
exponent do not assert equal optimal constants.

Lower: a finite spanning prior gives injective posterior mean m(x) with
positive-definite Hessian. Small TV reconstruction bounds posterior squared
distortion. The finite-dimensional quantum trace bound controls small balls,
forcing distortion>=cD^(-2/s). An alternative derivation combines standard
Shannon/Holevo inequalities with finite posterior differential entropy.
Upper: transform by broad reference g=N(0,4), u=Phi(x/2). Target/reference
density ratios have periodic W^(2,1) extensions. Positive symmetric hat
encode/decode kernels cancel first moments; J^s labels give O(J^-2) TV
without a tail logarithm. Channels are measurable information-theoretic maps,
not a finite-bit implementation claim with continuous TV output.

Posterior experiment quantization, high-resolution quantization and the
information-distortion exponent have established prior art. The exact scoped
uniform-TV matching theorem was not located in a bounded primary-source
search; historical novelty is unresolved. Constants are not uniform as the
affine spanning geometry collapses or Theta changes with D.

Conditional consequence via the inherited internally audited tracial Gaussian
bridge: at critical delta~n^-1/2, precision eps with eps sqrt(n)->infinity
requires D=Theta(eps^(-s/2)). A fixed nonzero known-spectrum SU(d) orbit spans
all traceless Hermitians, so s=d^2-1 despite its smaller manifold dimension.
Critical qubits therefore have D=Theta(eps^-3/2). Precision below the bridge
remainder remains unresolved. The pure Gaussian theorem has no such bridge
dependency.

Known nontracial block algebra rho0=directsum_b a_b I_(m_b) admits explicit
block-count jitter and tracial-block channels: comparison error
O(delta+n^-1/2), s=sum_b m_b^2-1. This extension depends on the tracial bridge.
Unrestricted partial-collision QLAN still needs a compatible Fock/ground-block
factorization; moment diagnostics do not prove that missing theorem.

3. UNKNOWN-FIELD CORRELATED GIBBS ARCHIVES
Known SU(2)-invariant H_V on V fixed-spin-S sites, known beta, one full input
rho_(t,v)=exp(-beta H_V+t v.J)/Z, unknown t in [0,R], unknown v in S^2,
R<=1 tends to zero. All retained registers count. Uniform half-trace error.
No efficient multiplicity-state preparation is supplied.

For every such H_V a fixed classical decoder achieves
  error <=1-exp(-R)+eps,
  D <= C_S eps^-3 (1+VR)^(3/2).
Sector laws q_t have monotone likelihood ratio. Their fixed envelope
h_j=sup_t q_t(j), H_env=sum h_j satisfies H_env<=1+ell/2, where
ell=int_0^R sqrt(I_sector(t))dt. The exact score is increasing and
1-Lipschitz in j, giving
  ell^2 <= R [E_(q_R)j-E_(q_0)j] <= SVR.
The endpoint quantity measures sector response, not full angular susceptibility.
Raw-envelope-mass bins and geometric spin bins refine additively. A fixed
envelope-conditioned decoder prevents the old true-field side-information bug.

An explicit generally nonlocal spin-half Hamiltonian has q0(j) proportional to
(2j+1) exp[-R(j-mV)^2/(2cV)], hence zero-field macroscopic spin m. Under tilted
fields its Gaussian center sweeps cV while width is sqrt(cV/R). There are
Theta(sqrt(VR)) distinguishable magnitudes and Theta(VR) directions, forcing
D>=c'(VR)^(3/2) at constant error even for quantum memory. Thus the leading
worst-case vanishing-error cost is (3/2+o(1))log2(VR).
A shrinking c=1/log(e+VR) gives the same leading obstruction even with
uniform unquantified macroscopic concentration under every allowed tilt.

If VR->c>0 and q0 has macroscopic spin m>0, the family instead converges
uniformly to the vMF magnitude ball on S^2. One fixed q0 sector decoder and
positive hats give e_D=Theta(1/D), with explicit additive thermodynamic
remainders independent of D. Unknown magnitude adds no critical precision
dimension. Any diverging D permits vanishing error; bounded D cannot. Zero
retained bits suffice exactly in the asymptotic subcritical regime VR->0.

A public interval of width Delta capturing all tilted laws up to eta gives
D<=1+C eps^-3(1+j_max R)sqrt(1+R Delta), error<=1-exp(-R)+eta+eps.
For a specified local Heisenberg Hamiltonian the missing ingredient is a
quantitative endpoint-response, Fisher-length or tilted-width estimate. The
inspected family271 statements do not provide it. The nonlocal lower example
does not refute a better rate for that local physical model.
