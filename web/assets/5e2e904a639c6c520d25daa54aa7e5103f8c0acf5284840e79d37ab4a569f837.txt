STATUS AND STRONGEST CLAIM
For every a>0, v>0 and beta!=0, take a global orthonormal left-invariant
frame E0,E+,E- on G=PSL(2,R) with
  [E0,E+]=-a E+,  [E0,E-]=a E-,  [E+,E-]=beta E0,
and X=vE0. The brackets give sl2(R): H=-2E0/a, e=E+,
f=-2E-/(a beta) satisfy [H,e]=2e, [H,f]=-2f, [e,f]=H. For any
cocompact lattice Gamma<PSL(2,R), this metric and X descend to the closed
quotient Gamma\G. Haar volume is invariant. The flow has exact exponents
av, 0, -av; the smooth-volume Pesin formula gives H_mu(phi_1)=av.

An exact Koszul calculation gives, with rows as output and columns as input,
  nabla X = [[0,0,0],[0,av,beta*v/2],[0,-beta*v/2,-av]],
  S=Def X = diag(0,av,-av),  |S|^2=2a^2v^2,
  nabla_X X=0,  div S=-2a^2v E0=-2a^2 X.
Thus p=0 and F=-2nu div S=4nu a^2 X sustain the field in
  nabla_X X - 2nu div S + grad p = F.
The work is W=4nu a^2v^2 Vol(M)=4nu Vol(M) H_mu(phi_1)^2.
This is a compact, non-Sol equality family. beta changes the antisymmetric
part of nabla X and the geometry but not entropy, strain norm, or work at
fixed a,v,nu. The scalar curvature is -2a^2-beta^2/2, so beta=1 and beta=2
at a=3/2 give non-isometric metrics (scalar curvatures -5 and -13/2).

The only imported analytic step in the entropy value is the smooth-measure
Pesin entropy formula. The Lie brackets and Lyapunov exponents are explicit;
this report does not claim that a symbolic script proves Pesin's theorem.
This is an extension of the earlier single sl2 geodesic example, not a
novelty or field-breaking claim.

EXACT EXAMPLES
The executable artifact records these rational cases:

1. a=3/2, beta=1, v=2, nu=5/7. Then H=3, |S|^2=18,
   F=(45/7)X, and W/Vol=4nu H^2=180/7 exactly.
2. a=2/3, beta=-3/2, v=5/4, nu=7/5. Then H=5/6,
   |S|^2=25/18, F=(112/45)X, and W/Vol=4nu H^2=35/9 exactly.

There is also an explicit constant-curvature geodesic subfamily. On a closed
orientable surface of genus g>=2 and curvature -kappa^2, let X be its unit
geodesic field and use the Sasaki metric on T^1 Sigma. In the standard
orthonormal frame (X,H0,V0),
  [X,H0]=-kappa^2 V0, [X,V0]=-H0, [H0,V0]=X.
Then E+=(H0+kappa V0)/sqrt(1+kappa^2) and
E-=(H0-kappa V0)/sqrt(1+kappa^2) put it in the above form with
a=kappa, v=1, beta=-2kappa/(1+kappa^2). Gauss-Bonnet and the circle-fiber
volume give Vol(T^1 Sigma)=8pi^2(g-1)/kappa^2. Consequently
  H=kappa,  |S|^2=2kappa^2,  W/Vol=4nu kappa^2,
  W=32pi^2 nu (g-1).
For genus 2 and nu=1/3, both kappa=1/2 (curvature -1/4, volume
32pi^2, H=1/2) and kappa=2 (curvature -4, volume 2pi^2, H=2) have
the same exact total W=32pi^2/3. Their work densities are 1/3 and 16/3.
For genus 3, kappa=3/2, nu=7/5, the exact volume is 64pi^2/9,
H=3/2, and W=448pi^2/5.

DERIVATION AND BOUNDARIES
For a left-invariant frame, Koszul gives the displayed connection. Its
symmetric part has eigenvalues 0,+av,-av. Right translation by exp(tX)
acts on left-invariant vectors by Ad(exp(-tX)); since ad_X E+=-av E+ and
ad_X E-=av E-, the normal stretch factors are exp(avt) and exp(-avt).
The line X is neutral. The quotient is compact and Haar is smooth and
invariant, so Pesin gives H=av. The covariant divergence above is the
stress divergence in the stated constitutive equation. It is not the
Bochner or Hodge Laplacian; curvature terms would differ.

For the geodesic subfamily, the three standard brackets are taken as the
canonical constant-curvature frame identities. The frame transform is
verified symbolically. The Sasaki volume is fiber length 2pi times surface
area 4pi(g-1)/kappa^2. No force actuator or time-dependent Navier-Stokes
trajectory is supplied; F is the smooth steady sustaining residual.

ERROR CONTROLS AND COMPUTATIONAL COST
`homogeneous_power_examples.py` uses exact SymPy arithmetic for the
symbolic frame identities, and Python Fraction arithmetic for all rational
examples. Its 15 checks cover Jacobi, unimodularity, Koszul torsion and
metric compatibility, the connection/strain/divergence, scalar curvature,
the sl2 brackets, and the Sasaki-frame transform. The generated JSON records
the outputs. It uses no numerical ODE or entropy estimator.

The displayed total-work and volume decimals are outward intervals. pi is

An explicit input-error bound is included. At case 1, if independently
rounded P=W/Vol, nu and H have absolute errors deltaP=10^-7,
deltanu=10^-8, deltaH=10^-6, then the residual uncertainty is at most
440071506000001/25000000000000000000 (about 1.7603e-5). Thus decimal

exact shared-parameter identity is what certifies this example.

For fixed dimension three, the geometric identities require a constant
number of rational operations. With rational parameters encoded using B

pi/exp intervals are also tiny exact-rational calculations. These costs
assume a,beta,v,nu and the surface family are supplied. No protocol is given

volume is computed for the geodesic family using the supplied genus and
Gauss-Bonnet; a generic lattice-volume algorithm and its cost are outside
this result. The abstract sl2 examples report work per volume, so they do
