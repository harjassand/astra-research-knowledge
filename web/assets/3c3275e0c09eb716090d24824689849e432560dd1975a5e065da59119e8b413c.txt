PRIMARY SOURCE COMPARISON
Date: 2026-10-08

1. Multiplicity cap

C. Fronsdal and T. Hirai, “A remark on the tensor product of irreducible
representations of a compact Lie group,” Japanese Journal of Mathematics,
New Series 7(1) (1981), 201–211, DOI 10.4099/math1924.7.201.
Publisher page: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/math1924/7/1/7_1_201/_article
Publisher PDF: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/math1924/7/1/7_1_201/_pdf

Theorem 1 gives the torus-induced embedding for a conjugate tensor pair;
Theorems 2–3 give the stronger stabilizer form and its cyclic-vector
realization. The report's bound mult(V_nu, End(V_lambda))<=dim V_nu[0] is
therefore known prior art, not a novelty claim. The direct highest-vector
coefficient injection is reconstructed in multiplicity.txt and lower_profile.txt.

2. Zero-weight multiplicities

B. Kostant, “A formula for the multiplicity of a weight,” Proceedings of the
National Academy of Sciences 44(6) (1958), 588–589, DOI 10.1073/pnas.44.6.588.
Publisher/archive page: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC528626/

Kostant's formula is the primary source for the zero-weight multiplicity
formula used in the all-T count. The proof bounds the Kostant partition
function by choosing occupations of nonsimple positive roots and does not
replace the alternating sum by one unsigned term.

3. Standard structural inputs

PBW shell counting, the Weyl dimension formula, root-string sl2 identities,
and Schur partial-trace isotropy are used in their standard finite-dimensional
forms. Their exact normalizations, nonsimply-laced coroot/height distinctions,
and role in this argument are written explicitly in spectral_count.txt,
root_length_audit.txt, and upper_profile_working.txt. This list is not a claim
that every classical identity has been traced to its earliest publication.

Comparison boundary
-------------------
Fronsdal-Hirai establishes the multiplicity comparison. Kostant supplies
zero-weight multiplicities. The all-T cumulative estimate and the rank
constrained Casimir profile are internally derived by combining these with
PBW/Weyl counting, spectral projectors, Green estimates, and explicit filters.
Their novelty and priority have not been assessed, and no external source is
claimed to establish exactly those profile statements in the stated interface.
