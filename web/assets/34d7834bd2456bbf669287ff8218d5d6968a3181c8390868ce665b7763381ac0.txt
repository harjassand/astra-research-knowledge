## 1. Primary-source match

Live-checked the primary paper Aharonov, Kitaev, Preskill, “Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computation with Long-Range Correlated Noise,” *Physical Review Letters* 96, 050504 (2006), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.050504, publisher PDF https://preskill.caltech.edu/pubs/preskill-2006-long-range.pdf. In its fine-grained-to-coarse-grained discussion, the paper groups terms by the exact set of faulty macrolocations and defines `E(I_r)` as the sum of paths faulty on each member of a specified set `I_r`, with “no restriction” at other macrolocations (PDF p. 2, equations (7)–(9)). It states the local condition `||E(I_r)||<=epsilon^r` in Eq. (9). This is the same aggregate-cylinder form used in v1, after identifying its `I_r` with a mask `S` and its `E(I_r)` with `E(S)`.

The A22 inversion and multiround threshold-mask expansion are not claimed as a new fault-tolerance theorem or as a new Möbius-inversion result. They are a direct finite combinatorial consequence of the cylinder definition. AKP's Eq. (9) is a bound on quantum fault paths in its stated location model; it does not by itself establish the bound for all noisy classical decoder, storage, and readout locations in the QTC implementation. The physical-model gap recorded in A04 v1/v2 remains.

## 2. Why cross-round cylinders are necessary

Here is a scalar two-round counterexample to replacing the global bound by only per-round single-location cylinder bounds. Take one location `a` in round 1 and one `b` in round 2, and `k=N=1`. Let all operators act on the one-dimensional Hilbert space and set

`W_empty=2`, `W_{a}=-1`, `W_{b}=-1`, `W_{ab}=1`.

Then `V=sum_F W_F=1`, so `V` is an isometry, and the per-round single-location aggregates vanish:

`E({a})=W_a+W_ab=0`, `E({b})=W_b+W_ab=0`.

Thus each separate one-round cylinder bound holds for every `eta>=0`. Yet every nonempty exact path is globally bad, so `B=W_a+W_b+W_ab=-1` and `||B||=1`. The missing joint cylinder is `E({a,b})=W_ab=1`, which violates the global estimate `||E({a,b})||<=eta^2` for small `eta`. This demonstrates that per-round controls without cross-round joint masks cannot justify an exponentially small union bound when a common bath permits coherent cancellation among aggregate cylinders. The example does not satisfy the A22 global hypothesis and therefore is not a counterexample to v1.
