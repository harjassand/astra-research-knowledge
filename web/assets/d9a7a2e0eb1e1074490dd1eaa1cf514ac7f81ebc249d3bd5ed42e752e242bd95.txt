EXACT CONDITIONAL GEOMETRIC CONSEQUENCE
For every 4/9<v<1 and 1<c<9v/4 there is ONE complete smooth metric g on R^3,
Euclidean near the origin, with Ric>=0 and AVR(g)=v, such that
  limsup_{k integer -> infinity} h_k(R^3,g)/(k+1)^2 > c.
The metric has nonunique cone limits. No endpoint or full asymptotic-limit claim
is made. Unlike source Theorem 1.1, the same g serves an unbounded degree sequence.

   All full loops follow a common all-simple stem; only added high detours
   converge smoothly to round. Every selected full-degree block boundary has
   its own positive gap on the skeleton closure. The exact-double slice uses
   a C^2 Banach implicit-function chart restricted to smooth directions, rather
   than an unproved Frechet implicit-function theorem. Crossing curve spaces
   fix only value/first jet, with free or already all-simple endpoints.
2. Disjoint wide bands I_n=(B_{M_{n-1}},B_{M_n}], M_{n-1}/M_n->0, cycle together.
   In every later finite program a band-n label samples every rank of its own
   band equally at each phase. Its mean is bounded by beta_n~2M_n/(3sqrt(v)).
   This exact identity repairs the failure of naive sequential g_k patching,
   which can leave old modes at a rank too high for their intended degree.
3. All-order spatial/time envelopes and support comparability are fixed before
   radial stage starts. For each fixed cutoff p_n, a compact-family whole-ball
   freezing modulus Omega_n(t)->0 and graphs V_n=O_n(t^-9/16) hold uniformly
   over all derivative-bounded future schedules. Actual gaps are isolated only
   after their own finite thresholds, at least half their reference gap.
4. The ENTIRE local matrix at a band-n crossing is O_n(eta_m(t_nu)^3/4), not just
   its selected entry. Local matrix Taylor expansion and stage suppression of
   Omega_n(t)D_m^3/4 give this estimate with constants independent of m. It
   permits fixed birth-band column boxes and one common Ricci buffer.
5. Only NEW-band columns are matched in each fixed p_n graph. Each geometric
   crossing creates exactly one mismatch, so there are no duplicated control
   equations across cutoffs. Off-plateau |F|<=epsilon_m|r-1| telescopes exactly
   independently of interval length; local Gaussian crossing weights give
   opposite endpoint signs. A countable product fixed point selects one metric.
6. Distinct birth-graph columns tend to distinct common reference eigenvectors,
   proving every finite prefix independent without graph nesting. Complete and
   partial stage frequency remainders are o(log r); whole-ball contraction and
   uniform ellipticity give the pointwise growth bound at every radius. With
   k_n=ceil(qM_n), 2sqrt(C*)/(3sqrt(v))<q<1/sqrt(c), the count is
      h_{k_n} >=(M_n+1)^2-(M_0+1)^2,
   and its limiting subsequence ratio is at least 1/q^2>c.

AUDIT CORRECTION AND RETAINED FAILURE
The first v2 wording did not explicitly require the entire local matrix estimate.
The uniformity red-team supplied an exact counterexample to the weaker inference:
  E_21=delta D^-3/4, E_23=delta, v_1=e_1-rho e_3
gives normalized F_21=delta D^-3/4-rho delta<0 for large D, although the chosen
entry is positive and ||E||=O(delta). PROOF_v3 explicitly repairs this through
  E=a_nu z B_n+O_n(a_nu^2+local residual+Omega_n(t)t^-9/16),
  ||E||<=C'_n a_nu, a_nu=eta_m(t_nu)^3/4.
The comparison cylinder uses a slightly larger collar around chi=1, so edge
points need not have their entire slab inside the plateau. D_m explicitly
dominates first derivatives, square roots of second derivatives, and all bump/
gauge derivative constants. The fixed Ricci buffer and radial factor precede
the transfer modulus and all strong stage thresholds.
