## Result

The universal d-dimensional 1-to-2 cloning marginal is outside a broader construction class than the canonical instrument-Petz-square subclass excluded by peer c04_l09. Let τ=Tr/d on M_d, and suppose

    C = (L + Q)/2,       Φ = C^† C,

where L is any bistochastic UCP map, and Q is any reference-preserving EB branch of the peer c04_s03 form (Q=P T): T measures a POVM and P prepares arbitrary positive states whose weighted mean is the reference. No canonical preparation rule and no block-preserving instrument restriction is imposed. If Φ is depolarizing with contraction λ on traceless observables, then necessarily

    λ <= (1 + 1/sqrt(d+1))^2 / 4.                         (1)

The universal cloner has λ_d=(d+2)/(2(d+1)), and

    λ_d - (1 + 1/sqrt(d+1))^2/4
      = (sqrt(d+1)-1)^2 / (4(d+1)) > 0                       (2)

for every d>=2. Therefore its marginal cannot equal C^†C in this broader measurement-append / common Petz correction construction. At d=3, (1) gives λ<=9/16, while the explicit qutrit cloner has λ=5/8; the exact gap is 1/16.

This is a counterexample to a proposed representation route, not to the universal EB Dirichlet comparison. The qutrit clone still has the exact canonical rounder from INITIAL, satisfying I-Ψ=2(I-Φ). It also has nonvanishing residual on the full traceless frame, so it does not refute a vanishing-modulus theorem.

## Derivation

Work on the real Hilbert space of traceless Hermitian matrices with inner product τ(AB); its dimension is m=d^2-1. For an operator S on this space write ‖S‖_F for its Hilbert-Schmidt/Frobenius norm as a superoperator. Because L is UCP and τ-preserving, Schwarz and trace preservation imply ‖L‖_{2->2}<=1, hence

    ||L||_F^2 = Tr(L^†L) <= m.                              (3)

Write the measurement effects as M_y>=0, sum_y M_y=I, p_y=τ(M_y)>0. In the c04_s03 factorization, (T(X)_y=τ(M_yX)/p_y), (P(f)=∑_y p_y f_y A_y), where (A_y) are prepared likelihood densities. Kadison Schwarz and the trace show P is an L2 contraction. Thus

    Q^† Q = T^† P^† P T <= T^† T = Ψ_M.      (4)

This use of Ψ_M is only a domination device; Q itself need not be selfadjoint. The superoperator trace of Ψ_M on the traceless space is

    Tr_0 Ψ_M
      = sum_y τ(M_y^2)/p_y - 1
      = sum_y p_y τ((M_y/p_y)^2) - 1
      <= d - 1.                                                 (5)

The subtraction removes the scalar unit eigenvector. Each (M_y/p_y) is positive and τ-normalized, so τ((M_y/p_y)^2)<=d; the p_y sum to one. Taking the trace of (4) on this invariant traceless space gives

    ||Q||_F^2 <= d - 1.                                         (6)

Suppose Φ=C^†C is the depolarizing map λ I on this space. Then ‖C‖_F^2=λ m. The triangle inequality and (3),(6) give

    2 sqrt(λ m) = ||L+Q||_F
                    <= ||L||_F + ||Q||_F
                    <= sqrt(m) + sqrt(d-1).

Since (m=(d-1)(d+1)), squaring yields (1). Subtracting its right side from λ_d gives (2), so the exclusion is strict in every finite dimension d>=2. This proof does not assume L is selfadjoint or positive as an L2 operator, Q is selfadjoint, or Ψ_M<=Φ.

For completeness, the depolarizing witness is a genuine compatible channel, not merely an abstract superoperator: with F the output swap and P_+=(I+F)/2,

    B_d(X)=2/(d+1) P_+(X tensor I)P_+

is CPTP with symmetric outputs, and its two marginals are Φ_d(X)=((d+2)X+Tr(X)I)/(2(d+1)). This is the Werner 1-to-2 universal cloner specialization. The exact qutrit version and finite MUB EB comparator were separately checked in qutrit_cloner_exact.py.

## Evidence, costs, and boundaries

The all-d proof (3)--(6) is symbolic and dimension-independent. The accompanying exact arithmetic script broad_branch_square_cap.py checks the qutrit constants and the identity (2) at selected dimensions; its finite fixtures are transcription checks only. It uses no solver. The separate qutrit_cloner_exact.py exact matrix check uses matrices at most 9 by 9. The representation gap itself requires no additional oracle access, source acquisition, precision parameter, or asymptotic computation. The clone is supplied as a known finite channel; this is not an acquisition result for unknown apparatuses.

The obstruction only says that the c04_s03 midpoint (C=(L+Q)/2) followed by (C^†) on both outputs does not generate the universal-cloner marginal. That cloner is still c=2 roundable by a different EB map. A failure of this construction is not a failure of every dimension-free modulus and not a failure of all constructive procedures. The unrestricted channel-level question, including existence of a general c=2 comparator or a weaker ω(r)->0, remains open.
