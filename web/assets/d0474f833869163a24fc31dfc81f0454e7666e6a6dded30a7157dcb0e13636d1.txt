AN EB DECODER WITH EXACT ALLOWED SUPPORT, INCLUDING PHYSICAL HARD CORE
8 October 2026. Internally proved corollary of PROOF.txt.

The free canonical decoder in PROOF Section 8 produces states on the full
boson Fock space. For finite positive penalties that is a legitimate trace
comparison, but it need not have finite penalty energy for an arbitrary
unbounded W. For a physical hard constraint one can want an output inside
the physical allowed Hilbert space. The following fixed decoder closes
that support interface, with an explicitly charged conditioning cost.

Use all the hypotheses/constants of PROOF.txt. Let n copies share lambda,
their penalties w_j possibly different. Put P_n=P_R^{tensor n}. Every
output in P_n has exactly zero form energy for sum_j w_j. Any physical
hard allowed subspace containing P_R on each copy contains P_n.

For the public free canonical total-T sector state sigma^0_{n,t}, define
 p_t=Tr(P_n sigma^0_{n,t}),
 theta_{n,t}=P_n sigma^0_{n,t} P_n/p_t when p_t>0.
If p_t=0, set theta_{n,t} to the n-system vacuum. Each theta_{n,t} has
range in P_n. These definitions are independent of lambda and all w_j:
conditioning the free family on total T already removes lambda exactly.
The fixed channel

 Psi_n(X)=sum_{t>=0}Tr(P_{T=t}X) theta_{n,t}               (S.1)

is entanglement breaking and always prepares an admitted zero-penalty
output. It can be regarded as a channel to the physical hard Hilbert space,
transported by its known inclusion isometry into full Fock space.

Define the free hard-projection failure
 delta_R(lambda)=Tr rho0(lambda)(I-P_R).
The pair union bound and PROOF (2.4), this time at fixed radius R instead
of ell, give the explicit public estimate

 delta_R(lambda)<=16N0^2 S(2R+1)^2/L^4
                 <=c_R/L^2,
 c_R=16N0^2(2R+1)^2.                                    (S.2)

For the free n-system state, let delta_n=Tr rho0(lambda)^{tensor n}(I-P_n).
The projection product union bound gives delta_n<=n delta_R<=n c_R/L^2.
Decompose that free state as sum_t w_t(lambda) sigma^0_{n,t}. Its projection
failure decomposes exactly as delta_n=sum_t w_t(lambda)(1-p_t).

For p_t>0, purify sigma^0_{n,t}. Projecting the purification by P_n and
normalizing gives a purification of theta_{n,t} with overlap sqrt(p_t)
with the original one. Pure-state trace distance and partial-trace
contractivity prove

 ||theta_{n,t}-sigma^0_{n,t}||_1<=2sqrt(1-p_t).

For p_t=0 the same upper bound 2 holds with vacuum. Convexity of trace norm
and concavity of square root therefore give

 ||Psi_n(rho0(lambda)^{tensor n})-rho0(lambda)^{tensor n}||_1
 <=2sum_t w_t(lambda)sqrt(1-p_t)<=2sqrt(delta_n).         (S.3)

The interacting product input rho_prod is within sqrt(2nG_L) of that free
state by PROOF (8.1). Compare both inputs through Psi_n by contractivity,
insert (S.3), and obtain

 ||Psi_n(rho_prod)-rho_prod||_1
       <=min(2,2sqrt(2nG_L)+2sqrt(n c_R/L^2)).            (S.4)

Unlike the unrestricted canonical decoder, Psi_n need not fix the free
family exactly. Its explicit free error is O(sqrt n/L). The dominant
positive-penalty error and the sufficient copy regime n=o(log L) are
unchanged. The same proof applies to product physical hard Gibbs states
in larger allowed spaces containing P_R, because PROOF Section 7 gives
their identical direct KL estimate. The decoder outputs lie in the known
smaller P_R^tensor n, so it need not acquire the unknown stronger form or
the detailed physical interaction.

Finite classical alphabet

Retain t=0,...,t_* and prepare vacuum on overflow. The fixed finite-outcome
Psi_{n,t_*} still outputs exactly in P_n and satisfies

 ||Psi_{n,t_*}(rho_prod)-rho_prod||_1
 <=min(2,2sqrt(2nG_L)+2sqrt(n c_R/L^2)
             +2exp(nNtheta-theta(t_*+1))).              (S.5)

Indeed deleting the high-t terms in (S.3) adds at most twice their FREE
probability. Contractivity again needs no interacting tail estimate.
Thus the alphabet and classical bits are exactly those in PROOF Section 8.
There is no persistent quantum register or heralded encoding condition.
All conditional preparation is a public decoder operation independent of
the actual interacting input beyond its stored integer t.

No hidden preparation-conditioning assumption

One possible ideal decoder preparation is to generate sigma^0_{n,t},
measure the public geometric projection P_n, and repeat independently
until success. Its successful output is exactly theta_{n,t}, with expected
number of trials 1/p_t. If p_t=0 use vacuum as defined above. These trial
and active-workspace costs are real; (S.1) alone is not an efficiency theorem.
Alternatively compute a matrix representation of the public compressed
state and prepare it directly, charging its full size and diagonalization.

There is, however, a certified favorable conditioning bound in the admitted
growing-copy regime. At the reference lambda=0, all nS free modes are
public. Occupying only the first sine mode of one copy with t bosons gives
one valid free Fock basis vector of weight exp(-t q_g), where
 q_g=beta epsilon_(1,1)<=c_g:=4 pi^2 tau J.
Since Z0(0)<=exp(N0), its total-T probability satisfies

 w_t(0)>=exp(-nN0-c_g t).

The nonnegative decomposition of the lambda=0 projection failure gives
 w_t(0)(1-p_t)<=delta_n(0)<=n c_R/L^2. Hence

 1-p_t<=min(1,[n c_R/L^2]exp(nN0+c_g t)).                (S.6)

This is a public conditioning certificate, not a supplied success oracle.
For all retained counts, replace t by t_* in (S.6). If n+t_*=o(log L), its
right tends to zero; thus p_t tends uniformly to 1 and rejection work is
uniformly bounded for every retained t. In particular n=o(log L) and
log(1/epsilon_tail)=o(log L), with the cutoff from PROOF Section 8, satisfy
that condition. This includes natural slowly vanishing tail tolerances.
For a huge cutoff or a parameter-dependent small lattice this certificate
can fail, and success work must then be charged without an assumed bound.

Even favorable rejection conditioning does NOT remove canonical-generation
costs: sector dimension binomial(nS+t-1,t), weight precision, sine-mode
preparation, particle-register representation, projection verification,
physical output, and active decoder workspace remain. Fresh decoder
quantum workspace is not persistent quantum archive memory. If the physical
hardware cannot represent forbidden configurations even temporarily, the
state must be prepared via an allowed-space representation/ancilla and a
known isometry; this is an implementation cost, not a free primitive.

At the ideal mathematical level the support is exact. Approximate amplitudes
confined to an allowed basis also preserve zero w form energy, but a faulty
support measurement or leakage error cannot be assigned a uniform energy
bound when arbitrary penalty strengths are admitted. Trace reconstruction
error and finite-strength energy accuracy remain different interfaces.

Status: internally proved corollary. Exact support fixes the physical hard-
constraint output issue; it does not prove efficient preparation uniformly
in n/error, optimal copy scaling, or external validity/priority.
