CYCLE 5 EXTENSION — CATALYST-WEIGHTED INACTIVE-FACE CORRECTION
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the explicit coupled-toggle family
and rate conditions below; exact checker is FINITE-EVIDENCE. This is a
non-detailed-balanced weakly reversible positive-recurrence theorem, not a
blanket weak-reversibility result. External review and novelty are UNKNOWN.

1. NETWORK AND STRUCTURAL CONDITIONS
------------------------------------
Species: one substrate B, its base catalyst A, and r>=1 co-catalysts
C_1,...,C_r. For positive finite rates include

  0 <-> A             alpha, delta a
  0 <-> C_k            gamma_k, mu_k c_k
  A <-> A+B            p a, q a b
  A+B <-> 2A+B         u a b, v (a)_2 b
  A+C_k <-> C_k        eta_k a c_k, zeta_k c_k   (k=1,...,r).

These are stochastic mass-action propensities. Each pair is reversible, so
the network is weakly reversible. The A+C_k toggles preserve B but have
catalyst source order two. Let S=sum_k c_k, Gamma=sum_k gamma_k,
mu_max=max_k mu_k, and zeta_min=min_k zeta_k. For c in N^r define

  theta(c)=alpha+Gamma/2,                                      S=0,
  theta(c)=[alpha+sum_k zeta_k c_k+Gamma/(S+2)-mu_bar(c)]/(S+1), S>=1,
  mu_bar(c)=sum_k mu_k c_k/S.

Assume theta_*:=inf_{c in N^r} theta(c)>0, w_k mu_k>zeta_k for chosen
weights w_k>0, and chi:=delta-p(R-1)>0 for some R>1. A simple sufficient
condition for theta_*>0 is alpha>mu_max, in which case
theta_*>=min(alpha-mu_max,zeta_min)>0. For rational rates, theta_*>0 is
finitely checkable: theta(c)>=[alpha-mu_max+zeta_min S]/(S+1) for S>=1.
Writing M=max(mu_max-alpha,0), every S>=ceil(1+2M/zeta_min) has this
lower bound at least zeta_min/2; only the finitely many smaller count
vectors remain to check. If S0=ceil(1+2M/zeta_min), the number checked is
binomial(S0+r-1,r). This is an effective exact gate on rational inputs,
not a polynomial-time bound in the encoded rates or variable r.

The last condition is achieved by taking R sufficiently close to one.
Define h(a)=1_{a=0}; for any epsilon>0 and K>=1 set

  V(a,c,b)=1+a+sum_k w_k c_k
      + R^b [1 + epsilon h(a)/(1+S)]
      + K 1_{a+S=0}.                                      (1)

The co-catalyst denominator caps the rate eta_k a c_k of turning off the
last A without importing the global-substrate denominator failure. The
condition theta_*>0 makes the inactive correction's immigration/toggle
loss dominate its positive C-death denominator increments uniformly.

2. THEOREM
----------
For every finite r and every positive finite parameter vector satisfying
the displayed inequalities, V is positive and norm-like, and there are
finite C,c>0 such that

  LV <= C-c V.                                               (2)

Consequently the minimal CTMC is nonexplosive in physical time and is
positive recurrent on its irreducible class. The base immigration/death and
A<->A+B channels give irreducibility; the new reversible channels do not
remove those paths. The claim is scoped to the stated coupled-toggle family.

3. GENERATOR AND DRIFT PROOF
----------------------------
Write W=R^b, d=1-R^{-1}, and

  G(n)=p(R-1)R^n-q d nR^n+u n.

The non-correction base contribution is

  alpha-delta a + sum_k w_k(gamma_k-mu_k c_k)
  + sum_k c_k(zeta_k-eta_k a)
  + a G(b) - v(a)_2 b,

where (a)_2=a(a-1). The K-indicator part of the generator is bounded above
by the finite constant K max(delta,mu_max); immigration at the all-zero
catalyst state contributes -K times its rate in this component. The full
V jump can nevertheless be positive there because of a C_k weight; below
we explicitly bound its positive part.

Let u_0(S)=1/(1+S). On the inactive face a=0, the correction is

epsilon W u_0(S). Its exact generator coefficient, divided by epsilon W,
is

  F(c) = -alpha u_0(S) - sum_k zeta_k c_k u_0(S)
          +sum_k gamma_k[u_0(S+1)-u_0(S)]
          +sum_{k:c_k>0} mu_k c_k[u_0(S-1)-u_0(S)].                (3)

For S>=1 this simplifies to

  F(c)=[-alpha-sum_k zeta_k c_k-Gamma/(S+2)+mu_bar(c)]/(S+1),
  Gamma=sum_k gamma_k,
  mu_bar(c)=sum_k mu_k c_k/S <= mu_max.

By definition F(c)=-theta(c). Therefore the inactive correction
contributes at most -epsilon theta_* W uniformly over all co-catalyst
counts. Under the simple condition alpha>mu_max, the explicit lower bound
theta_*>=min(alpha-mu_max,zeta_min) follows from
sum_k zeta_k c_k>=zeta_min S; at S=0, theta(0)=alpha+Gamma/2.

On a>=1 the correction is zero. It can increase only when a=1 and either
A dies or A+C_k->C_k fires. The total positive correction drift is bounded
by

  epsilon W [delta+sum_k eta_k c_k]/(1+S)
       <= epsilon(delta+eta_max) W,                               (4)

where eta_max=max eta_k. All reverse activations at a=0 decrease the
correction. At a=0,S=0 a C_k-immigration jump has full increment

  Delta_k V=w_k-K-epsilon W/2,

so its positive part is at most (w_k-K)^+, uniformly in b; add the finite
constant sum_k gamma_k(w_k-K)^+ to C. A-immigration at that boundary has
increment 1-K-epsilon W<=0 for K>=1. K-indicator increases occur only on
the death of a sole catalyst, already bounded by K max(delta,mu_max).

The weighted co-catalyst and toggle linear terms obey

  sum_k c_k(zeta_k-eta_k a-w_k mu_k) <= -kappa S,
  kappa=min_k(w_k mu_k-zeta_k)>0.                                (5)

For b large, G(b)/(bR^b)->-q d. Choose N so that G(b)<=-(q d/2)bR^b
for b>=N, and increase N until the q sink absorbs both (4) and fixed
positive multiples of a and W. Then for a>=1,b>=N, the B-edge block
provides -c_a a-c_WW. For a=0,b>=N, (3) provides
-epsilon theta W. The C-weight drift (5) supplies -kappa S in both cases.

For b<N, W is bounded. At b=0, the A coefficient is exactly -chi a.
For each of the finitely many b=1,...,N-1, the term
-v(a)_2 b is quadratic negative and dominates all linear-in-a terms for
large a. Hence there are finite C_A,c_A>0 with

  -delta a+aG(b)-v(a)_2b <= C_A-c_A a  for all b<N.

The correction gain (4), K-boundary terms, and W itself are bounded on
this low-b region and enter C. Combining these cases with (5) gives
LV<=C_0-c_a a-c_S S-c_WW. Since V<=C_1+a+w_max S+(1+epsilon)W+K,
this implies (2). This proof charges the physical-time generator directly.

4. NON-DETAILED-BALANCED EXPLICIT INSTANCE
------------------------------------------
Take r=2 and

  alpha=2, delta=1, p=q=u=v=1;
  gamma_1=gamma_2=mu_1=mu_2=1;
  eta_1=eta_2=1, zeta_1=1, zeta_2=2;
  w_1=2, w_2=3, R=3/2, epsilon=K=1.

Then chi=1/2, theta_*>=1, and w_k mu_k-zeta_k=1 for k=1,2, so the
theorem applies. The network is not complex balanced, hence not detailed
balanced: the complex A+C_1 has only its reversible pair and complex balance
would force a*=zeta_1/eta_1=1; A+C_2 similarly forces a*=zeta_2/eta_2=2.
No positive equilibrium can satisfy both. This is an explicitly coupled,
weakly reversible, non-complex-balanced rate choice with a provable Foster
drift. The exact check at
`verify_catalyst_weighted_generator.py` validates the statewise generator
identity and the inactive-face bound on its finite fixture grid; the analytic
proof above, not the finite grid, proves (2).

Detailed-balance control: if all reversible-pair rate ratios are instead
compatible with a positive activity vector, the product-Poisson stationary
law gives an independent recurrence/nonexplosion control. In particular,
the original A+C<->C obstruction fixture with all paired unit rates is
detailed balanced; the failure of its unweighted local V is not stochastic
instability. The present non-DB theorem does not use a product-form law.

5. LIMITS AND NEXT GATE
-----------------------
This result treats co-catalysts that are independent immigration-death
coordinates and toggles that preserve all C_k and B counts. It does not
cover co-catalyst conversions, cofactor-dependent toggles, multiple
catalysts competing for one substrate support, substrate-changing channels,
or arbitrary weakly reversible networks. The rate condition theta_*>0 is
sufficient for this V, not asserted necessary for recurrence; alpha>mu_max
is a simpler sufficient condition for it. The denominator variation in (3)
is load-bearing: if theta(c)<0 at some fixed c, this V has positive
order-R^b drift along a=0, that fixed c, and b->infinity. Whether a
different correction covers that parameter region is open.
