The Python file uses exact fractions to instantiate four small constructions:
weighted parity cosets, a rational nonunit fugacity, same support with different
weights, and a one-block CNF filter. It is a reproducible finite illustration,
not proof of the general claims, not a sampler implementation, and not yet run
in this programme. The derivations and broader scope are in RESULT.txt and the
leaf notes cited in CLAIMS.json.
