C01_S01 — OPTIONAL CENTRAL SCALAR GIBBS WEIGHT ACQUISITION
8 October 2026. This is a small closed scalar interface, not acquisition
of an exponential matrix filter. Supplied-weight theorems do not need it.

Fix d. Suppose a central Hamiltonian's rational block energies b_lambda
are supplied, or acquired by a specified polynomial-time rational scalar
Casimir formula. Absorb the rational inverse temperature and sign into
rational exponents x_lambda. The desired central Gibbs weights are
 q_lambda=n_lambda exp(x_lambda)/sum_mu n_mu exp(x_mu),
 n_lambda=r_lambda f_lambda.
Weyl's formula and the hook-length formula compute n_lambda exactly with
O_d(N log(N+1)) integer bits, and sum_lambda n_lambda=d^N. Energy acquisition
cost is explicit input/computation; this file promises no unspecified
central-Hamiltonian spectral oracle.

Shift x_max=max_lambda x_lambda and put y_lambda=x_max-x_lambda>=0. No
large exponential is formed. The shifted normalizer is at least one.
For requested total-variation error tau>0, take an integer
 B>=N ceil(log2 d)+ceil(log2(128/tau))+2.
Drop terms y_lambda>B. Since e^-B<=2^-B, their total unnormalized weight
is at most d^N 2^-B<=tau/128. Comparing a binary huge y with B is exact and
polynomial in its rational encoding, so binary huge couplings do not
force a huge Taylor degree.

For a retained 0<=y<=B, choose an integer k with
 2^-k<=tau/(128d^N)
and n+1>=max(6B,k+2). The positive rational Taylor sum
 T_n(y)=sum_(j=0)^n y^j/j!
gives the positive rational approximation a(y)=1/T_n(y) to e^-y.
The tail ratio past n is at most 1/2, and j!>=(j/e)^j with e<3 yields
 0<=e^y-T_n(y)<=2[3B/(n+1)]^(n+1)<=2^-k.
Hence 0<=a(y)-e^-y<=2^-k. Its weighted error summed over retained labels
is at most d^N 2^-k<=tau/128. Numerator/denominator growth is polynomial
in N,L,log(1/tau), since n=O_d(N+log(1/tau)) and y has supplied rational
bits. The tau encoding is included in L.

Normalize the acquired nonnegative rational n_lambda a(y_lambda). Their
sum is positive (a maximizing label has y=0). For positive unnormalized
vectors with true mass at least one, total variation after normalization
is bounded by their l1 difference divided by that true mass. Thus the
rounded rational label law has TV<tau/32, with spare rounding budget.
It may be supplied to the core with a separate error allocation, yielding
the same polynomial finite-bit cluster sampler for central Casimir Gibbs
states whose scalar block energies are acquired as stated.

This derivation acquires only rational scalar labels. It does not acquire
g=exp(B_matrix/2), exterior-power contractions or a physical Gibbs state;
the filtered theorem still requires SUPPLIED rational g. No general Gibbs
backend run or novelty claim is represented here.
