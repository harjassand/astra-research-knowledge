CYCLE 6 LEAF — DATA HIDING / LOCKING OBSTRUCTION SEARCH
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: NO QUALIFYING COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND. N93 unrestricted gate remains UNKNOWN.

Question
========
Can one find finite-dimensional experiments E_n with all three properties:

  (i)  C(E_n) = sup_prior chi(E_n) <= C_0, with C_0 independent of n;
  (ii) one full-domain CPTP map B_n : B(H_n) -> B(H_n tensor H_n), with equal
       marginals, has worst-case trace residual b(B_n;E_n) -> 0;
  (iii) e_EB(E_n), minimized over one common full-algebra measure-and-prepare
        channel, is bounded below by a positive constant?

No family satisfying (i)-(iii) was constructed. The data-hiding and locking
routes checked here either constrain the wrong measurement class or fail one
of (ii)-(iii). This is a candidate-mechanism no-go, not a proof that the
unrestricted implication is true. The global N93 implication remains UNKNOWN.

Definitions and norm interface
==============================
For E={rho_x} on H, write

  T(rho,sigma) = (1/2)||rho-sigma||_1
               = sup_{0<=Q<=I} |Tr Q(rho-sigma)|,

where the supremum is over every effect on the entire input Hilbert space.
For a measurement restriction M, define

  d_M(rho,sigma) = sup_{POVM in M} TV(POVM(rho),POVM(sigma)).

Always d_M <= T. A data-hiding result typically says d_M is small for a
restricted class (LOCC, separable, or another locality restriction) while T
is large. The EB reconstruction error in this task is

  e_EB(E) = inf_{POVM {Q_y}, states {omega_y}}
              sup_x T(rho_x, sum_y Tr(Q_y rho_x) omega_y),

and its POVM is unrestricted and global. Thus a restricted-measurement
hiding bound is not an EB lower bound. In particular, hiding two orthogonal
states from LOCC does not obstruct global EB reconstruction: their global
support projections form a POVM, and the measure-and-prepare map using those
projections fixes both states exactly. More generally, if a global POVM
uniformly identifies a finite label x with error at most delta, preparing
rho_y after outcome y gives reconstruction error at most delta, since the
output is a mixture with weight at least 1-delta on rho_x.

The broadcaster quantity is

  b_2(E) = inf_B sup_x max_{k=1,2} T(B_k(rho_x),rho_x),

with B any CPTP map on the full input algebra. Swapping and averaging B with
its receiver-swapped version makes the two marginals equal without increasing
the worst marginal error. Correlations between outputs and output coherences
are unrestricted.

Locking/data-hiding checks
=========================
1. Accessible information is not the target decoder norm. A low accessible
information value for one prior, or for a restricted measurement class, does
not imply that every global measure-and-prepare channel has large worst-case
trace error. The capacity premise is stronger in a different direction:
C(E) takes a supremum over all priors. A locking construction must separately
prove that this supremum stays bounded, and then lower-bound reconstruction
against all global POVMs. If a key or flag is present in the quantum input,
the global decoder can measure it; if it is averaged out, a theorem about the
keyed ensemble does not automatically apply to the averaged states.

2. Strong equal-information control: the N93 flagged Clifford/Pauli family
has C(E)=c(a)<ln 2, uniform-prior accessible information at most c(a)/q,
and e_EB(E)=a(1-1/q)/2 -> a/2 for its affine-plane sequence. But its
collective broadcaster witness proves

  b_2(E) >= (a/2)(1-1/sqrt(2)) > 0

for fixed a>0. Thus bounded Holevo capacity, vanishing accessible information,
vanishing pairwise fidelity-affinity gap, and a constant EB lower bound can
coexist, but the actual pair-broadcast error does not vanish. The matching
subfamily has the exact value b_2=a(1-1/sqrt(2))/2. This is an equally
informed negative control, not a counterexample to (i)-(iii). The derivation
and full-domain witness are in
`work/programmes/classicality/leaves/clifford_sandwich.txt`.

3. Fixed finite data-hiding seed plus copy ladder. Let Sigma={sigma_i}_{i=1}^M
be any fixed finite alphabet of distinct states on K, n_j=2^j, and

  H_L = direct_sum_{j=0}^{L-1} K^{tensor n_j},
  rho_i = (1/L) direct_sum_j sigma_i^{tensor n_j}.

The sector flag is independent of i, so every prior has
C(E_L)=(1/L)sum_j chi({sigma_i^{tensor n_j}}) <= log M. The full-domain
channel that measures j and splits the tensor power evenly between the two
receivers (using a fixed replacement at j=0) has each marginal error at most
1/L. But this is not a counterexample: on sectors j>=ceil(sqrt(L)), the
input contains at least 2^sqrt(L) copies of one of finitely many distinct
states. For each pair i!=k, a binary Helstrom test has a fixed nonzero bias;
allocate disjoint tensor factors to the finitely many pair tests and majority
vote. For fixed M, the worst label error on these sectors is at most
M^2 exp(-c_M 2^sqrt(L)) for some c_M>0. Measure the flag, discard lower
sectors, and prepare the estimated full rho_i. This is a global full-algebra
EB channel and has error at most

  ceil(sqrt(L))/L + M^2 exp(-c_M 2^sqrt(L)) -> 0.

The mechanism that hides the seed from restricted measurements leaves enough
copies for an unrestricted collective decoder.

4. Growing Pauli alphabet with weak signal. Let d=2^L, let P range over
signed nonidentity Hermitian Pauli operators on C^d, and set

  sigma_{P,a}=(I+aP)/d,  a_L^2=alpha^2 L/2^(L-1),
  rho_P=(1/L) direct_sum_{j=0}^{L-1} sigma_{P,a_L}^{tensor 2^j}.

For c(a)=((1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a))/2, we have
D(sigma_{P,a}||I/d)=c(a)<=a^2. For every prior, relative-entropy centering
at I/d^{tensor 2^j} gives

  C(E_L) <= (c(a_L)/L) sum_j 2^j <= 2 alpha^2.

The same sector-splitting channel is a full-domain CPTP broadcaster with
b_2(E_L)<=1/L. Yet the full-domain replacer to

tau_L=(1/L) direct_sum_j I/d^{tensor 2^j}

is EB, and quantum Pinsker gives

  e_EB(E_L) <= (1/L)sum_j min{1, sqrt(2^j c(a_L)/2)}
             = O(log L/L) -> 0.

Shrinking the hidden signal enough to certify a dimension-free capacity
bound makes the constant-reference reconstruction effective on all but a
vanishing fraction of the diffuse flags. Exact formulas and the weighted-flag
conditional no-go are in
`work/programmes/classicality/leaves/followup_bounded_capacity_counterexample.txt`.

5. Rare outliers and convex dilution. A rare prior weight does not remove a
state from the worst-case supremum in e_EB or from the all-prior supremum in
C(E). If instead every state is diluted as
rho_x=(1-delta)tau+delta sigma_x, the full-domain EB replacer X->Tr(X)tau
has

  sup_x T(rho_x,tau) <= delta.

So a component whose weight tends to zero cannot itself support a constant
EB lower bound. A construction with a constant-weight hard component still
needs a uniform all-prior Holevo bound, a compatible broadcaster, and a
lower bound against the global decoder.

Scoped mixed-state obstruction: supplied bistochastic instruments
===============================================================
There is an exact no-go for a substantial but restricted broadcaster class.
It requires no Holevo premise. Work on B(C^d), with normalized trace
 tau(A)=Tr(A)/d and inner product <A,B>=tau(A*B). Suppose a finite Kraus
family {K_y} is supplied and is bistochastic:

  sum_y K_y* K_y = sum_y K_y K_y* = I.

Define the bistochastic channel and effects

  L(A)=sum_y K_y A K_y*,  M_y=K_y* K_y, p_y=tau(M_y),
  Psi(A)=sum_{y:p_y>0} tau(M_y A) M_y/p_y.

The full-domain Schrodinger EB channel corresponding to Psi is

  P(rho)=sum_y Tr(M_y rho) M_y/Tr(M_y).

Let C=(L+Psi)/2, and let * denote the Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint. The
instrument-append channel

  B_0(rho)=sum_y K_y rho K_y* tensor [M_y/Tr(M_y)]

is CPTP on every input, not just the promised family. Symmetrize its outputs.
Its two marginals are C. Since C is bistochastic, C* is also CPTP. Apply
C* to both output registers. The resulting full-domain channel

  B=(C* tensor C*) o (symmetrized B_0)

has equal marginals Phi=C*C. This is an actual compatible-channel
construction, with no presumed product form for its outputs.

The likelihood maps Psi and L are Hilbert-Schmidt contractions; Psi is also
positive and selfadjoint. Direct expansion gives the exact sum-of-squares
identity

  2(I-Phi)-(I-Psi)
    = (I-L*L) + (Psi-Psi^2) + (L-Psi)*(L-Psi)/2 >= 0.       (A)

Indeed, each term on the right is positive: L*L<=I by bistochastic
Kadison-Schwarz and trace preservation, while 0<=Psi<=I. In particular
0<=I-Psi<=2(I-Phi).

Now take any finite full-rank mixed-state family

  rho_H=(I+H)/d,  H=H*, Tr(H)=0, ||H||_infinity<=theta<1,

and suppose the residual of THIS constructed marginal is

  r = sup_H ||Phi(H)-H||_(1,tau),
  ||X||_(p,tau)=(tau(|X|^p))^(1/p).

Because 0<=I-Psi<=I, operator monotonicity on the positive contraction
I-Psi and (A) imply, state by state,

  ||(I-Psi)H||_(1,tau)^2
    <= ||(I-Psi)H||_(2,tau)^2
    = <H,(I-Psi)^2 H>
    <= <H,(I-Psi)H>
    <= 2 <H,(I-Phi)H>
    <= 2 theta r.

The last step is Holder:
<H,(I-Phi)H>=tau(H(H-Phi(H)))<=||H||_infinity
||H-Phi(H)||_(1,tau). Since P is a common full-domain EB channel, this
proves

  e_EB(E) <= (1/2)sqrt(2 theta r).

If beta is the worst half-trace residual of this particular B, then r=2 beta
and e_EB(E)<=sqrt(theta beta). This is a dimension-free mixed-state
rounding theorem for the supplied bistochastic-instrument/Petz-adjoint
construction. It includes all dimensions and finite POVM/Kraus outcome
counts, and imposes no spectral-gap assumption.

Boundary of this theorem: it does NOT say that an arbitrary broadcaster
with small b_2 has a marginal representable as Phi=C*C for a supplied
bistochastic instrument. It does not prove a dimension-free EB Dirichlet
comparison for every selfcompatible channel. Therefore it cannot close the
unrestricted N93 implication. The simpler classical-input case is immediate:
if the given joint broadcaster itself has measure-and-prepare form
B(rho)=sum_y Tr(Q_y rho) omega_y^AB, then B_1 is EB, so
 e_EB(E)<=sup_x T(B_1(rho_x),rho_x). Such a channel cannot witness a gap.

Falsifier and restart gate
==========================
A successful counterexample must place all three independently certified
items in one sequence: (a) C(E_n)<=C_0 for every prior, not merely small
accessible information under one prior; (b) one explicit full-domain CPTP
B_n with equal marginals and b_2(E_n)->0; (c) e_EB(E_n)>=c>0 for every
common global POVM-and-prepare channel. A restricted-POVM hiding inequality,
a large trace-cover number, Choi nonseparability, small pairwise affinity
gap, or a lower bound for one chosen decoder is not item (c). One global EB
decoder with error tending to zero falsifies a candidate immediately.

Next decisive search should target a compatible marginal outside the
supplied-instrument/C*C class, while deriving a lower bound in the global
common-POVM minimax norm. If the candidate uses flags or locking, expose the
flag/key to the unrestricted decoder in the control and compute the statewise
trace reconstruction error. Keep C as a supremum over priors. If no exact
construction passes those gates, retain UNKNOWN rather than upgrade the
scoped theorem to a global claim.

Costs, source boundary, and status
==================================
No numerical experiment was needed: the leaf consists of finite-dimensional
channel formulas, entropy bounds, Pinsker, and an exact Hilbert-Schmidt SOS.
The fixed-seed ladder can have exponentially many tensor factors; the
weak-Pauli ladder has d=2^L and a top block with 2^(L-1) copies, so these are
existence constructions and not efficient implementations. In the instrument
theorem the dimension d and finite outcome/Kraus count are arbitrary; no
cost for discovering the instrument or representing its Kraus operators is
claimed.

external references include Barnum et al. on exact no-broadcasting and Gao,

themselves settle the approximate bounded-Holevo implication. Identity (A)
and its channel realization are internal finite-dimensional algebra, not an
