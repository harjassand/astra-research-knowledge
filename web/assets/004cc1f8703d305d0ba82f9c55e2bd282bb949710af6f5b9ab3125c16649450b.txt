7. WHY THE RAW GLOBAL NORM CANNOT BE POLYNOMIAL

The improvement needs the product-kernel structure. No bound polynomial in
N can replace the sandwich semigroup norm on all Hermitian trace-class
inputs uniformly in this regime. Take b=0 and C=L I. On spin j=N/2,
H=L J^2/N^(3/2) has eigenvalue L(N/2)(N/2+1)/N^(3/2).

CP sandwich and is exp(Theta(L sqrt N)). Even fixed L already forces the
superpolynomial loss for unrestricted inputs. The localized lemma escapes
this exact obstruction by retaining the mixed product kernel and its radius.
This is an obstruction to a proposed norm estimate, not to separability.
