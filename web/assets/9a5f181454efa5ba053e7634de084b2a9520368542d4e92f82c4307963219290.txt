nonvanishing beta kinetic cost in d=2. Instead use

every admitted penalty, while its directed lattice energy is <=128/log(ell/R).
Normal-ordered free Wick density gives failure probability O(1/L) and

The many-particle gradient has a factor m-1, absorbed by ONE fixed free
fugacity tilt. Ordered-pair Fock density is treated exactly through the
labeled symmetrizer: m!/product n_x! labeled copies cancel their inverse
diagonal weights. Thus no particle-sector factorial, fixed-number cutoff,

The filter entropy is controlled by the two commuting effects F and
