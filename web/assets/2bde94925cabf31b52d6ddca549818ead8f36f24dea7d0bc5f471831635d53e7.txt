CLASSICAL SAMPLER COST REFINEMENT AFTER AUTHORISED c03_s03 MESSAGE
c03_s02, 7 October 2026. INITIAL and earlier attribution remain unchanged.

Origin: c03_s03 communicated and implemented a Hellinger-chain improvement
for its row-first/conditional-column sampler. I reconstructed the inequality
independently and adapt it here to the physical-prefix sampler. This is a
cross-phase attributed refinement, not part of either worker's independent
INITIAL and not a claim of novelty for Hellinger affinity itself.

At a positive prefix let p_j be true child probabilities, and let estimates
of child weights all have relative error <=eta<=1/2. Then normalized q_j obey
 |sqrt(q_j/p_j)-1|<=4eta.
True zero children are estimated zero exactly. Thus the conditional squared
Hellinger distance in the convention H^2(p,q)=sum_j(sqrt(p_j)-sqrt(q_j))^2
is at most 16eta^2, and the affinity is at least 1-8eta^2.

For analysis repair every bad-count conditional kernel by replacing it with
the exact true kernel. No such repair is computed by the algorithm. The
repaired kernel has conditional affinity >=1-8eta^2 at EVERY prefix, for
EVERY fixed collection of estimation tapes. A prefix with zero true mass can
use any common kernel in this comparison; it contributes no P mass.
For two tree laws P,Q, if child conditional affinity is uniformly >=1-h,
the depth-d affinity satisfies
 A_(d+1)=sum_(prefix x)sqrt(P(x)Q(x)) A(children|x)>=(1-h)A_d.
Since A_0=1, H^2(P,Q)=2(1-A_n)<=2nh. With h=8eta^2,
 TV(P,Q)<=H(P,Q)<=4sqrt(n)eta.

Fresh independent depth tapes make the actual and repaired exact-rational
samplers agree until their first bad visited prefix, under a common branch
draw. Each fixed visited prefix has child-count failure probability at most
3delta_call, conditional on the past. Therefore discrepancy probability is
<=3n delta_call. Averaging the repaired law over tapes preserves its TV
bound. Dyadic cumulative thresholds add <=2/2^s per site as before.

Choose
 eta=epsilon/(16ceil(sqrt(n))),
 delta_call=epsilon/(12n),
 2^s>=8n/epsilon.
Then total TV is at most epsilon/4+epsilon/4+epsilon/4=3epsilon/4.
All-zero estimated vectors still use the fixed legal fallback, with its
event already covered by the count-failure budget. No quantum preparation
error improvement is inferred from this classical argument; Section7's
shared-depth quantum hybrid keeps its original eta=O(epsilon/n).

Each physical-prefix count now needs O(C_ab n epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon))
sign evaluations, rather than O(C_ab n^2 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon)). With at
most 3n counts and 2^b C_ab<=binom(2k,k), total base determinant-coefficient
evaluations cost
 O((k+1) binom(2k,k) n^2 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon)),
times the polynomial-bit per-evaluation work. The k+1 factor comes from
2a+1 scalar evaluations. This saves a factor n in the earlier conservative
sequential-TV count budget. It does not change the exponential dependence
on k or establish a fastest implementation claim.

low_sector_pfaffian.py uses this updated eta, while preserving the earlier
complete relative-count routine, confidence allocation, exact arithmetic,
and bounded dyadic branch draws. Its small tests are smoke tests, not an
empirical calibration of the total-variation theorem. The full proof of the
underlying counts and nonnegative prefix programs is AUDIT_REPORT.txt.
