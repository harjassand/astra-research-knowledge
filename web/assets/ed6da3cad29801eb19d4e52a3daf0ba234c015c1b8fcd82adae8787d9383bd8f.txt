ACQUIRED FINITE-FRONTIER TRANSFER WITH EXTENSIVE PAIR NUMBER
c02_s01. Independent continuation after immutable INITIAL, 7 October 2026.

RESULT / EXACT INTERFACE
Given an explicit rational-complex n-by-n F in the supplied site order, acquire
 b=max{|i-j| : i!=j, Fij!=0}, with b=0 if that set is empty.
Let w=min(n,b+1). All coefficients
 c_k=sum_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k}|det F[I,J]|^2
are computed exactly, including arbitrary sitewise allowed subsets of
{empty,row,column} and nonnegative rational row/column activities, using
 O(n^2 (b+1)16^w) rational-complex field operations and
 O(n16^w+n^2) field/input storage. The n^2 scan to acquire bandwidth is included.
For a supplied canonical sector k, retaining only sectors<=k reduces transfer
work to O(n(b+1)(k+1)16^w), in addition to the input scan.
An exact self-reduction sampler is implemented, recomputing at most3 branch
counts per site. Its all-sector or canonical worst-case operation bound is
 O(n^3(b+1)16^w), with expected polynomial random-bit overhead per call.
No coefficient, ratio, mixing or phase oracle is supplied. No inverse sector
probability appears. Each arithmetic operation has polynomial input bit cost.
Thus b<=C log n for fixed public C admits exact polynomial-bit counting and
expected-polynomial-bit sampling. The bound does not limit active pair number:
k can equal floor(n/2) even when b is constant and rank F=n.

The parameter is acquired in the supplied order; finding an optimal ordering
is not part of the result. Diagonal entries are ignored exactly, because no
disjoint minor uses Fii. Full-rank, non-monomial complex band matrices are
admitted. Arbitrary dense matrices have b=Theta(n), making the bound
exponential. This does not solve the general parity FPRAS or energy gate.

AMPLITUDE IDENTITY
Use fermionic creation modes a_i^dagger (row/up) and d_i^dagger (column/down),
ordered by site: a_1,d_1,a_2,d_2,...,a_n,d_n. Put
 Delta=sum_{i!=j} Fij a_i^dagger d_j^dagger,
 |Psi>=exp(Delta)|vac>.
Every summand is an even product of two creation operators. Two such summands
commute: when they share a mode both products are zero; otherwise moving two
creators past two others introduces sign(+1). Each summand squares to zero.
Consequently
 exp(Delta)=product_{i!=j}(1+Fij a_i^dagger d_j^dagger).       (1)
The order in this finite product is arbitrary.

For row set I and column set J of size k, expand Delta^k/k! and reorder into
all row creators followed by all column creators, both in ascending indices.
The k! orderings of a matching cancel the factorial. Moving the interleaved
column creators past the later rows contributes(-1)^(k(k-1)/2). Reordering
the matched column indices contributes the matching permutation sign, so
the coefficient in that ordering is
 (-1)^(k(k-1)/2) det F[I,J].                              (2)
Changing to the site-ordered occupation basis adds another sign depending
only on I,J. Thus its squared modulus is |det F[I,J]|^2.
The hard local projection removes occupation of both modes at any site,
leaving exactly disjoint I,J. It cannot produce an unbalanced row/column
word: every creation term creates one of each. Including diagonal terms in
Delta would not change projected amplitudes, since every such term creates
both modes on one site and no subsequent creation can remove them.

For local table S_i and activities r_i,c_i let a local diagonal weight be
 d_i(empty)=1, d_i(row)=r_i, d_i(column)=c_i, d_i(both)=0,
with zero weight for states not in S_i. The projected, activity-weighted norm
then equals sum_k c_k, with the stated weighted version of c_k. This identity
is exact and retains determinant interference. It is not a sum of positive
matching weights or a postulated Gaussian approximation.

FRONTIER SCHEDULE / PROOF
Process sites a=0,...,n-1. Before tracing site a, apply all gates in(1) whose
leftmost physical endpoint is a. They are
 1+F[a,j] a_a^dagger d_j^dagger and
 1+F[j,a] a_j^dagger d_a^dagger,
for a<j<=min(n-1,a+b). There are at most2b such gates.
Retain only the frontier sites a,...,min(n-1,a+b). The newly admitted rightmost
site is vacuum: every earlier processed gate had both endpoints<=a+b-1.

This procedure also handles fermionic signs exactly. In the site-ordered
occupation tensor product, a creation operator carries the usual parity
string on earlier modes. In a product of two creators on sites i,j, strings
on sites strictly before min(i,j) appear twice and cancel. Hence its physical
support is contained in the interval[min(i,j),max(i,j)]. Every future gate
has min(i,j)>a and acts as identity on the traced site a. Thus applying the
local weighted trace at a before those gates is legitimate, even though
intermediate local gates are nonunitary. Pair gates can be reordered as in(1).
An induction therefore identifies the frontier density tensor with the
weighted partial trace of the processed part of |Psi><Psi|. At the final
site, the remaining scalar is exactly the desired weighted projected norm.

Track the number h of row occupations already traced. Partial trace requires
the ket and bra occupation at a to agree, so h is the same on both layers.
When that occupation is row, increment h; otherwise leave it unchanged.
At the end h=k selects c_k. A cap h<=k can be imposed during the transfer
because there is no annihilation and h cannot decrease. This computes the
specified canonical sector directly, including a rare one.

EXPLICIT LOCAL UPDATE / IMPLEMENTATION
With w sites the frontier ket basis has4^w occupation bit strings; the density
has16^w ket/bra entries. Each entry also carries h. The code's mode2t is row
and mode2t+1 is column on relative frontier site t.
Creating mode m on a bit string s is zero if it is occupied; otherwise it is
 s -> s|(1<<m), sign=(-1)^(number of occupied modes below m).
For K=a_up^dagger d_down^dagger, apply the down creator first and then the row
creator. This supplies its exact matrix entry(+/-1), including direction.
For T=1+fK update density as
 D -> D+fKD+conj(f)DK^*+|f|^2 KDK^*.                       (3)
Every old entry has at most4 output entries. Conjugation is only on the bra
gate amplitude; (3) is an actual conjugate-transpose density update. This
differs from the algebraic-transpose suffix dual in the low-rank algorithm.
For the weighted trace at the first site, keep only entries with equal local
ket/bra bit pair, kill the doubly occupied pair, multiply by its rational
activity, remove the lowest two bits from both masks, and increment h for row.
These are the literal updates in bandwidth_parity.py. Exact cancellation is
retained in Gaussian-rational Fraction arithmetic; entries need not be
termwise nonnegative. Positive final coefficients follow from(2) and the
trace induction, and are asserted in the implementation.

For a fixed prefix, restrict its tables to singleton choices. The three legal
next-choice masses are computed by the same transfer. They are nonnegative
rationals summing exactly to the current prefix mass. Rational integer draws
select a branch with those probabilities. Telescoping gives precisely the
activity times |det F[I,J]|^2 divided by the selected sector's total.
Zero sectors are detected exactly. The sampler uses no rare-sector rejection.
The integer rejection draw has acceptance at least1/2 and expected fewer than
2 draws; its expected bit cost is polynomial. A fixed wall-time guarantee or
coherent state-preparation circuit is not delivered.

COST / BIT ACCOUNTING
With h<=floor(n/2), there are at most(n+1)16^w density entries. Each of n
stages applies at most2b gates and one weighted trace with constant arithmetic
per entry. This proves the field-operation/storage bounds above. Keeping only
h<=k replaces the n+1 factor in transfer work by k+1. The simple sampler makes
at most3n+1 transfers and retains their existing dense input scan cost, giving
the stated O(n^3(b+1)16^w) upper bound. It is not a cached frontier sampler.

There are at most n(n-1) rational-complex pair amplitudes and n local rational
activity factors. A common denominator obtained by multiplying the real and
imaginary entry denominators twice, and all activity denominators, has
polynomial input bit length. Every update term has denominator dividing this
common denominator: a ket and bra can each use a given pair gate at most once,
and each site weight is used at most once. Expanding all density updates gives
at most4^(n(n-1)) times finitely many trace choices, whose logarithm is
polynomial in n. Magnitudes are bounded by products of(1+|Fij|)^2 and local
activities, so numerator bit lengths are also polynomial in total input
encoding size. Intermediate cancellation does not require numerical tolerance.
Exact integer branch probabilities have polynomial-bit denominators.
The library uses Fraction gcd reduction. This is an arithmetic and bit-cost
proof, not a physical energy or high-width performance benchmark.

PRIORITY / LIMITS
Fermionic Fock encodings, Jordan-Wigner signs, Gaussian amplitudes, finite-width
tensor contraction and exact counting self-reduction are established methods.
Primary related representation reference: Petrica, Zheng, Chan, Clark,
Finite and Infinite Matrix Product States for Gutzwiller Projected Mean-Field
Wavefunctions, arXiv2009.00064 (2020), https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.00064.
Its abstract/introduction and setup were inspected; the whole paper is not
independently audited and no result from it is required in the derivation above.
This transfer is a complete explicit acquired-width realization for the current
disjoint-minor interface. A new general tensor principle, an optimal exponent,
and external novelty are not claimed. Novelty UNKNOWN; external audit NONE.

The baseline's monomial-cycle exact DP already covers important narrow sparse
classes with extensive occupation; this width transfer also admits overlapping
local pair choices and their determinant interference. It does not dominate
the existing bipartite or monomial subclasses. Nonlocal edges enlarge the
frontier in the supplied order, and a low-rank dense instance may be handled
better by the separate four-exterior-register algorithm. General full-rank
dense counting remains unresolved. Source/model/backend energy is UNKNOWN.
