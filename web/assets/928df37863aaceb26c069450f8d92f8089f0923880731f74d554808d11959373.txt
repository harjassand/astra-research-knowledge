THEOREM 1 (representation-independent star norm)

Let G_1,...,G_k be Hermitian involutions on C^d with
  G_i G_j + G_j G_i = 0  (i != j).
No irreducibility, chirality or tensor-factor representation is assumed.
On C^d tensor C^d tensor C^d, define
  H(w)=sum_i w_i G_i^T tensor (G_i tensor I + I tensor G_i),
where w_i>=0. Then
  ||H(w)|| <= sum_i w_i + max_i w_i.
For equal weights w_i=1, the stronger bound is
  ||H(1)|| <= max_{1<=p<=k} 2 sqrt[p(k+1-p)]
           = k+1                         (k odd),
           = sqrt[k(k+2)]                (k even).
These are upper bounds; no saturation for every representation is asserted.

PROOF OF THE EQUAL-WEIGHT BOUND

Put
  X_i=G_i^T tensor G_i tensor I,
  Y_i=G_i^T tensor I tensor G_i,
  Z_i=X_i Y_i=I tensor G_i tensor G_i.
Each is a Hermitian involution. The Z_i mutually commute: the two minus
signs from anticommutation cancel. Also X_i commutes with Z_i and
anticommutes with Z_j for j!=i, and
  X_i+Y_i=X_i(I+Z_i).

For z=(z_1,...,z_k) in {+1,-1}^k, let
  P_z=product_i (I+z_i Z_i)/2.
The P_z are mutually orthogonal joint spectral projections summing to I;
some may be zero. Let f_i(z) retain z_i and reverse every other coordinate.
The commutation relations give X_i P_z=P_{f_i(z)} X_i. Therefore
  (X_i+Y_i)P_z=0                         if z_i=-1,
  (X_i+Y_i)P_z=2 X_i P_z                 if z_i=+1.
The nonzero block has norm 2, because X_i is unitary. If z has p plus
signs and z_i=+1, f_i(z) has q=k+1-p plus signs. Thus H(1) connects
only the paired layers p and q. The layer with no plus signs is annihilated.

Write H_p for the sum of the joint spectral subspaces with p plus signs.
Fix 1<=p<=k and q=k+1-p. For psi in H_q, the output component in a
sector z with p plus signs is
  P_z H(1)psi = 2 sum_{i:z_i=+1} X_i P_{f_i(z)}psi.
Cauchy-Schwarz over its p terms gives
  ||P_z H(1)psi||^2 <= 4p sum_{i:z_i=+1} ||P_{f_i(z)}psi||^2.
After summing over sectors z in layer p, each sector z' in layer q
occurs exactly q times, one for each of its positive coordinates.
It follows that
  ||P_{H_p} H(1) P_{H_q} psi||^2 <= 4pq ||psi||^2.
Zero joint sectors cause no difficulty; their corresponding components
are simply zero, and the X_i relations preserve the possible sectors.

If p!=q, H(1) on H_p directsum H_q has block form
  [0 B; B^* 0],
so its norm is ||B||<=2sqrt(pq). If p=q (the odd-k self-layer), the
same Cauchy-Schwarz calculation applies to H(1) on H_p itself and gives
||H(1)|_{H_p}||<=2p, exactly 2sqrt(pq). This handles self-loops, including
k=1, without invoking a bipartite decomposition. Maximizing p(k+1-p)
over integral p proves the stated equal-weight bound.

PROOF OF THE WEIGHTED BOUND

Relabel so w_1>=...>=w_k>=0 and set w_{k+1}=0. For each prefix define
  H_j=sum_{i=1}^j G_i^T tensor (G_i tensor I+I tensor G_i).
The equal-weight argument applies to this subset in the very same
representation and gives ||H_j||<=j+1. The layer-cake identity and triangle
inequality give
  ||H(w)||
    <= sum_{j=1}^k (w_j-w_{j+1})(j+1)
     = sum_i w_i+w_1.

THEOREM 2 (one canonical comparator on a Clifford span)

Suppose additionally Tr G_i=0. Let Phi:M_d->M_d be CPTP, unital, TP and
HS-self-adjoint, with a CPTP broadcaster B:M_d->M_d tensor M_d whose two
marginals equal Phi. Then there exists one canonical POVM EB channel Psi,
unital, TP and HS-self-adjoint, such that
  Tr[A(I-Psi)(A)] <= 2 Tr[A(I-Phi)(A)]
for every A in V=span_R{G_1,...,G_k}, simultaneously. Negative eigenvalues
of Phi, or of its compression to V, are allowed.

PROOF AND EXPLICIT CONSTRUCTION

The G_i are HS orthogonal: Tr(G_i G_j)=0 for i!=j by trace cyclicity and
anticommutation, and Tr G_i^2=d. Define the compressed score matrix
  M_ij=Tr[G_i Phi(G_j)]/d.
It is real symmetric by Hermiticity preservation and HS-self-adjointness.
Its eigenvalues lie in [-1,1], since unital CPTP TP maps are HS contractions.
Choose a real orthogonal change of basis so that
  G'_a=sum_i O_ai G_i,  Tr[G'_a Phi(G'_b)]/d=lambda_a delta_ab.
The G'_a are again traceless Hermitian pairwise anticommuting involutions:
  {G'_a,G'_b}=2 delta_ab I.

Use normalized |Omega>=d^(-1/2) sum_j |j> tensor |j> and form
  W=(id tensor B)(|Omega><Omega|).
This is a density operator on A B C. Its AB and AC marginals are both
the normalized Choi state J(Phi), giving
  Tr[W ((G'_a)^T tensor G'_a tensor I)]
   =Tr[W ((G'_a)^T tensor I tensor G'_a)]
   =Tr[G'_a Phi(G'_a)]/d=lambda_a.

Let S={a:lambda_a>1/2} and r=|S|. If r>0, the star norm bound for this
subset gives
  2 sum_{a in S} lambda_a <= r+1,
hence, for mu_a=(2lambda_a-1)_+,
  sum_a mu_a <=1.
If r=0 the same conclusion is immediate. Notice that this is one joint
constraint on the whole compressed score matrix; there is no selection
of a different final comparator for different directions.

For each a with mu_a>0, define P_a^+=(I+G'_a)/2 and P_a^-=(I-G'_a)/2.
These are orthogonal spectral projections, each of trace d/2. The canonical
channel D_a for this two-outcome POVM is
  D_a(X)=sum_{s in {+,-}} Tr(P_a^s X) P_a^s/Tr(P_a^s)
        =(Tr X/d) I +(Tr[G'_a X]/d) G'_a.
Let D_0(X)=(Tr X/d) I, the canonical channel for the one-effect POVM {I}.
Set
  Psi=sum_a mu_a D_a+(1-sum_a mu_a)D_0.
This is canonical: retain the mixing coin as part of the outcome, with
effects mu_a P_a^+, mu_a P_a^-, and (1-sum_a mu_a)I, omitting zero effects.
They are positive and sum to I. Thus Psi is a finite-outcome canonical EB
channel on all of M_d, unital, TP and HS-self-adjoint.

For A=sum_a alpha_a G'_a in V,
  Psi(A)=sum_a mu_a alpha_a G'_a,
  Tr[A(I-Psi)(A)] =d sum_a (1-mu_a) alpha_a^2,
  Tr[A(I-Phi)(A)] =d sum_a (1-lambda_a) alpha_a^2.
Since mu_a>=(2lambda_a-1), the simultaneous factor-two inequality follows.

DUAL SUPPORT CHECK

For any weights w_a>=0 the canonical support on these rotated generators
is exactly d max_a w_a. Indeed every state rho has expectation vector
r_a=Tr(G'_a rho) with sum_a r_a^2<=1: if r!=0, the observable
sum_a (r_a/||r||)G'_a is an involution and its expectation equals ||r||.
Hence sum_a w_a r_a^2<=max_a w_a. A spectral PVM of a generator with
maximal weight attains d max_a w_a. The weighted star bound is therefore
exactly the required weighted-frame support inequality
  2 sum_a w_a Tr[G'_a Phi(G'_a)]
       <= d sum_a w_a+d max_a w_a.

BOUNDARY

This theorem is restricted to a Clifford span. In M_2 the three Pauli
generators span every traceless Hermitian direction and thus recover a
full-domain qubit theorem after adding the identity. In higher dimension,
an arbitrary positive dual frame need not be orthogonally equivalent to
anticommuting involutions. Its common canonical support is a different
convex-roof optimization, and the sign-sector argument supplies no general
rounding theorem. The arbitrary-rank universal T2 comparison remains UNKNOWN.

For this restricted theorem the input Phi and broadcaster are supplied.
The explicit construction needs the k-by-k score matrix and its exact real
eigendecomposition, not an acquired implementation of an unknown channel.
There are at most 2k+1 POVM outcomes. Arithmetic/measurement precision and
experimental channel acquisition are separate obligations, not supplied
by this existential theorem.
