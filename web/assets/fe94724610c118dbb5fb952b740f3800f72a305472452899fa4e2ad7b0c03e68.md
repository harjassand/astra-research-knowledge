# Gap-free rounding when a classical EB projection contains the slow modes

This is an attributed cross-phase continuation. The abstract lemma is
derived here; the direct-sum reversible-center instance is the construction
reported by c05_l05 (`work/cycle6/c05_l05/FINAL.txt` and its
`revisions/deeper_center_fiber_lift.txt`). c05_l07 separately proves a
general Dirichlet-to-power EB approximation from a supplied EB comparator;
the stronger exponential rate here uses a supplied commuting projection and
uniform contraction on its complement.

## Commuting-projection lemma

Let \(\Phi:M_d\to M_d\) be UCP, trace-preserving and Hilbert--Schmidt
self-adjoint. Let \(E\) be a trace-preserving EB orthogonal projection on
Hilbert--Schmidt space such that \(E\Phi=\Phi E\). Suppose
\[
 \|\Phi(I-E)\|_{2\to2}\le\kappa<1.
\]
For every \(m\ge1\), define
\[
 \Psi_m=E\Phi^m=\Phi^mE.
\]
Then \(\Psi_m\) is EB, trace-preserving, and self-adjoint, and
\[
 \|\Phi^m-\Psi_m\|_{\infty\to1,\tau}\le\kappa^m,
 \qquad
 I-\Psi_m\preceq\frac{1}{1-\kappa^m}(I-\Phi^m).       \tag{1}
\]
The first norm is on Hermitian inputs. In particular the EB comparison
constant tends to 1. No spectral gap assumption is placed on the action of
\(\Phi\) inside \(\operatorname{ran}E\); those slow modes are copied exactly
by \(\Psi_m\).

### Proof

Because \(E\) is an EB channel and \(\Phi^m\) is a channel,
\(E\Phi^m\) is EB. Commutation and self-adjointness make \(\Psi_m\)
self-adjoint. On \(\operatorname{ran}E\), \(\Psi_m=\Phi^m\); on
\(\ker E\), \(\Psi_m=0\). The latter subspace is invariant under \(\Phi\)
and the restriction has self-adjoint spectrum \(|\lambda|\le\kappa\).
Hence
\[
 \|\Phi^m-\Psi_m\|_{2\to2}
 =\|\Phi^m(I-E)\|_{2\to2}\le\kappa^m.
\]
For \(\|A\|_\infty\le1\), normalized trace gives
\(\|A\|_{2,\tau}\le1\) and \(\|X\|_{1,\tau}\le\|X\|_{2,\tau}\), so
the induced \(\infty\to1\) norm is also at most \(\kappa^m\).

For the quadratic order, diagonalize \(\Phi\) jointly with \(E\). On
\(\operatorname{ran}E\), the two deficits in (1) agree. On \(\ker E\),
\(I-\Psi_m\) has eigenvalue 1 while \(I-\Phi^m\) has eigenvalue
\(1-\lambda^m\ge1-|\lambda|^m\ge1-\kappa^m\). This proves (1).

## Direct-sum classical center / quantum fiber example

For \(\mathcal A=\bigoplus_{z=1}^r M_q\) with faithful stationary weights
\(\pi_z\), let \(P\) be a reversible stochastic kernel and define
\[
 L(A)_w=\sum_zP_{wz}A_z,\qquad
 E_Z(A)_z=\operatorname{tr}_q(A_z)I_q,
 \qquad C=(L+E_Z)/2,\quad \Phi=C^2.
\]
Here \(E_Z\) projects onto the commutative center and is EB. Detailed
balance makes \(L\) self-adjoint in the weighted trace, and \(L\) commutes
with \(E_Z\). On the center \(E_Z=I\); on blockwise traceless fibers,
\(E_Z=0\) and \(\|L\|_{2\to2}\le1\). Thus \(\Phi\) commutes with \(E_Z\)
and \(\|\Phi(I-E_Z)\|_{2\to2}\le1/4\). The lemma yields
\[
 \Psi_m=E_Z\Phi^m,\qquad
 \|\Phi^m-\Psi_m\|_{\infty\to1,\tau}\le4^{-m},\qquad
 I-\Psi_m\preceq(1-4^{-m})^{-1}(I-\Phi^m).
\]
The factor \((1-4^{-m})^{-1}\) is 4/3 at \(m=1\) and tends to 1,
regardless of how close the reversible Markov kernel's nontrivial center
eigenvalues are to 1. c05_l05 supplies an actual broadcaster for \(\Phi\)
from the instrument append construction and reports a q=4 fiber where
\(\Phi\) is not EB; I attribute those parts to that worker. The abstract
gap-free lemma itself does not require a broadcaster once \(E\) and the
transverse contraction are supplied.

## Boundary and decisive gate

This separates two spectral sectors that the fixed-point-gap argument
cannot separate: classical dynamics may relax arbitrarily slowly, while
the noncommutative fibers contract uniformly. It is a genuinely uniform
power-to-EB result for this supplied direct-sum structure, but it does not
show every selfcompatible reversible channel has such a commuting EB
projection with a dimension-free transverse contraction. Finding or
refuting that structure for arbitrary compatible channels remains a
different open gate. No uniform construction cost is implied when the
center, kernel, reference weights or broadcaster are not supplied.

`commuting_EB_projection_check.py` checks the eigenvalue calculation and
both bounds exactly for a two-center fixture. It verifies formula
transcription only; the proof is the invariant-subspace calculation above.
