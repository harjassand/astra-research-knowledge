OPEN XXZ INTEGRABILITY AUDIT FOR THE MARGINAL TARGET
2026-10-08 | narrow primary-source comparison

Status
------
Positive finite-L identification: the exact target Hamiltonian is a scalar
plus a standard open spin-1/2 XXZ Hamiltonian with diagonal integrable
boundaries, multiplied by -J, plus a uniform longitudinal field. This holds
for every L>=2, J>0, kappa>=0, and real lambda. The uniform field preserves
the diagonal-boundary transfer-matrix family because it is proportional to
the conserved total z-magnetization. This establishes integrability and a
Bethe-ansatz representation, not by itself a completeness proof or a closed
formula for the full-Fock ground state/partition function.

The fixed-anisotropy, fixed-magnon droplet theorem does not supply a finite-size
formula in the double scaling Delta_L-1=O(L^-2). Its hypotheses take the chain
length to infinity at fixed Delta>1 and fixed magnon number. The exact
one-magnon sector gives a decisive finite-interval check of the lost
confinement term.

1. Exact target-to-open-XXZ identity
------------------------------------
Use the conventions in sol_ground_xxz/RESULT.txt: S=L-1 sites,
n_i=(1-Z_i)/2, N=sum_i n_i, W=sum_{i=1}^{S-1}n_i n_{i+1},

 Hcrit = J sum_{i=1}^{S-1}(I-X_iX_{i+1}-Y_iY_{i+1}-Z_iZ_{i+1})
             + J(I-Z_1)+J(I-Z_S),
 H_L = Hcrit - (kappa/L^2)W - (lambda/L^2)N.

Define
 Delta_L=1+kappa/(4 J L^2),   b_L=(kappa+lambda)/(2L^2),

and the Kitanine et al. open-chain convention
 Hdiag(Delta,h_-,h_+) = sum_{i=1}^{S-1}
       [X_iX_{i+1}+Y_iY_{i+1}+Delta(Z_iZ_{i+1}-I)]
       +h_- Z_1+h_+ Z_S.

Direct expansion of W and N gives the exact identity

 H_L = C_L I - J Hdiag(Delta_L,Delta_L,Delta_L) + b_L sum_{i=1}^S Z_i,
 C_L = 2J - kappa(S-1)/(2L^2) - lambda S/(2L^2).       (A)

The endpoint terms are essential: after the uniform field b_L is extracted,
each endpoint coefficient is exactly -J Delta_L. For L=2, S=1 and the two
endpoint terms act on the same site and are both retained; (A) still holds.
The lambda contribution is entirely in the uniform field and scalar, so no
special boundary constraint on lambda is needed.

2. Reflection-equation parameter match and field
------------------------------------------------
Primary source: Kitanine, Kozlowski, Maillet, Niccoli, Slavnov & Terras,
“Correlation functions of the open XXZ chain I,” arXiv:0707.1995v1,
submitted 13 July 2007, https://arxiv.org/html/0707.1995 . Their Eq. (2.1)
is exactly Hdiag above; immediately below it they set Delta=cosh(eta) and
h_±=sinh(eta)coth(xi_±). Their diagonal K matrix is Eq. (2.8), and the
commuting double-row transfer family is Eq. (2.10); Eq. (2.16) obtains the
Hamiltonian from its logarithmic derivative.

For kappa>0, let eta_L=arcosh(Delta_L)>0. Necessary and sufficient matching
within this paper's diagonal parameterization is

 sinh(eta_L)coth(xi_±)=Delta_L,
 hence coth(xi_±)=coth(eta_L), and one real choice is xi_-=xi_+=eta_L
 (modulo the i*pi periodicity of coth). Thus both target endpoint fields are
on the diagonal integrable boundary family. At kappa=0, Delta=1 and eta=0;
the equation is interpreted in the rational XXX limit xi_±/eta_L -> 1,
which gives h_±=1. It is not to be evaluated as sinh(0)coth(finite xi).

The six-vertex R matrix and diagonal K matrices conserve total z-spin. Thus
[T(u),sum_i Z_i]=0 for the transfer matrices T(u) in Eq. (2.10). Adding
b_L sum_i Z_i changes eigenvalues but not the common eigenvectors or the
Bethe equations. This is the precise reason the chemical/contact-generated
uniform field in (A) preserves integrability; it is not being absorbed into
the boundary parameters.

3. Bethe energy and an exact one-magnon check
--------------------------------------------
Kitanine et al. give the open-chain Bethe equations (Eqs. (2.27), equivalently
their logarithmic form (3.27)) and the energy in Eq. (3.31): for N down spins,

 Ediag = sinh(eta)(coth(xi_+)+coth(xi_-))
             + 4 sum_{j=1}^N (cos p_j-Delta).

With xi_±=eta_L, the all-up reference energy is 2 Delta_L. Applying (A),
the target energy for the corresponding Bethe state, relative to the vacuum,
is exactly

 E_target = 4J sum_j(Delta_L-cos p_j)-2b_L N
           = sum_j [4J(1-cos p_j)-lambda/L^2].             (B)

The last equality uses 4J(Delta_L-1)=kappa/L^2 and 2b_L=(kappa+lambda)/L^2.
The p_j remain coupled through the open Bethe equations with boundary
parameters xi_±=eta_L. Equation (B) is an exact energy formula for Bethe
solutions, not a claim here that completeness or a useful all-sector
partition-function algorithm follows automatically.

The N=1 sector is elementary and exact without Bethe ansatz: W=0 and Hcrit
reduces to the open Dirichlet discrete Laplacian with diagonal 4J and
off-diagonal -2J. Its modes are sin(pi r i/L), i=1,...,L-1, and

 E_{1,r}=4J[1-cos(pi r/L)]-lambda/L^2,
 L^2 E_{1,1} -> 2J pi^2-lambda.                        (C)

This is independent of kappa, as also follows from (B): the Delta shift in
the one-magnon kinetic energy cancels the kappa part of the uniform field.

Exact finite diagnostic: L=3, J=1, kappa=2, lambda=3 gives Delta=19/18,
b=5/18, C=14/9. Direct sector matrices give energies 0 in N=0, {5/3,17/3}
in N=1, and 28/9 in N=2. The N=1 values agree with (C). This checks the
constants and endpoint counting, but is only a finite algebra check.

4. What the exact droplet theorem does and does not transfer
------------------------------------------------------------
Primary source: Nachtergaele, Spitzer & Starr, “Droplet Excitations for the
Spin-1/2 XXZ Chain with Kink Boundary Conditions,” arXiv:math-ph/0508049v1,
25 August 2005, https://arxiv.org/html/math-ph/0508049 . In their Eqs. (4)-(5)
the bond is 1/4 I-S^3S^3-(S^1S^1+S^2S^2)/Delta, with S^a=Pauli_a/2. Their
droplet boundary term, Eq. (15), is delta/2(I-S_1^3-S_L^3). Multiplying their
H^1 by 4J Delta_L gives the bulk and endpoint spin terms of -J Hdiag in (A)
up to a scalar, so the target uses precisely their droplet boundary parameter
delta=1. The additional b_L sum Z is a magnetization tilt and shifts an
N-magnon energy relative to the all-up state by -2b_L N.

For fixed 0<q<1, Delta=(q+q^-1)/2=cosh(eta), and fixed n, their Theorem 2.4
states lim_{M->infinity} E^1(M,n)=E_Z(n); Proposition 3.1 gives

 E_Z(n)=(1-q^2)(1-q^n)/[(1+q^2)(1+q^n)]
       =tanh(eta)tanh(n eta/2).                           (D)

Thus the infinite-volume droplet comparison object in target energy units,
with eta set to eta_L, would be
 4J Delta_L tanh(eta_L)tanh(n eta_L/2) - (kappa+lambda)n/L^2.
This substitution is not a finite-L target eigenvalue theorem: the cited
limit holds at fixed Delta>1 and fixed n, while here Delta_L->1 and the
interval length is simultaneously comparable to the localization length.

Scaling makes the gap visible. For kappa>0,
 eta_L = a/L+O(L^-3),  a=sqrt(kappa/(2J)).
For fixed n, (D)'s formal infinite-chain energy is
 -lambda n/L^2+O(L^-4), but the actual finite-chain one-magnon energy has
the additional +2J pi^2/L^2 term in (C). Therefore (D) cannot be used as
the target's finite-size formula even for n=1 in this double scaling.
For n/L->rho, formal substitution into (D) gives the speed-L expression
 4J a tanh(a rho/2) - (kappa+lambda)rho,
but no cited theorem justifies this substitution: fixed n and fixed Delta
are both violated. The macroscopic-magnon finite-interval asymptotics remain
UNKNOWN from these sources. When kappa=0 the droplet paper's q<1 formula is
also outside its stated regime; the XXX limit requires separate analysis.

5. Scope / conclusion
---------------------
The target is exactly an integrable open XXZ Hamiltonian with equal diagonal
boundary fields h_-=h_+=Delta_L, plus its conserved uniform magnetization,
for each finite L. This is a positive structural identification. It gives
the standard open Bethe equations and the exact Bethe-state energy map (B).
It does not turn the marginal L-dependent anisotropy into the fixed-anisotropy
thermodynamic droplet regime, establish BA completeness in the needed uniform
double scaling, or prove the proposed continuum ground-energy/free-energy
limit. The exact one-magnon result (C) is the boundary witness against a naive
droplet-formula transfer.
