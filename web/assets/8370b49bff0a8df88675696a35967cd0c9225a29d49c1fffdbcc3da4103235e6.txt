1. EXACT MIXED SYMBOL, INCLUDING EVERY FACTOR OF TWO

Let B_pa=2N_p f_pa+v_pa.grad_p and R_pa=w_pa.grad_p. Direct differentiation
of the tensor product gives B_pa k={J_pa,k}, R_pa k=-i[J_pa,k]. Because
the collective matrices are constant with respect to state coordinates,
composing these differential operators is the same as composing their
constant left/right matrix actions on k. For p!=q, J_pa and J_qb commute.

 B_pa B_qb k - R_pa R_qb k
 = {J_pa,{J_qb,k}} + [J_pa,[J_qb,k]]
 = 2 J_pa J_qb k+2 k J_pa J_qb.

Thus M_Hcross k=(delta/(4N)) sum B_pq,ab
                   (B_pa B_qb-R_pa R_qb)k.
Its scalar potential is delta N_p N_q f_p^T B_pq f_q/N.
Its p-drift is delta N_q v_(B_pq f_q)/(2N).
Its mixed second-derivative coefficient is
delta B_pq,ab(v_pa tensor v_qb-w_pa tensor w_qb)/(4N).

The full symmetric coefficient matrix MUST have
G_pp=alpha_p I/N_p, G_pq=delta B_pq/(2N).
Then D=V G V^T-W G W^T, SDE covariance=D/2, and the ordinary diffusion
generator has second-derivative term D:Hess/4. The two off-diagonal blocks
produce exactly the mixed coefficient just derived. Using delta B_pq/N
as the matrix off-diagonal block would be a factor-two error; the author
does NOT make that error.

There is no mixed derivative correction to the drift: v_qb and f_qb are
constant under differentiation of species p. The local mixed drift is
(1/2)v_(k_p), k_p=(delta/N) sum_(q!=p)N_q B_pq f_q. Correlated Brownian
increments are allowed throughout. No independence of species is needed.

2. INDEPENDENT ISOTROPIC DRIFT AND PSD-MARGIN DERIVATION

For a complete HS-orthonormal traceless local basis, with d=d_p,
 sum_a F_a X F_a=Tr(X)I-X/d,
 sum_a F_a^2=(d-1/d)I,
 sum_a f_a F_a=rho-I/d,
 sum_a f_a v_a=2(rho^2-purity rho).

Writing S=rho^2-purity rho, direct differentiation gives
 sum_a Dv_a[v_a]=2I-2d rho-8S,
 sum_a Dw_a[w_a]=2I-2d rho.
The coefficient alpha/N_p multiplying (B_a^2-R_a^2)/4 therefore has
drift alpha sum f_a v_a+(alpha/(4N_p))(-8S)
     =2alpha(N_p-1)S/N_p.
Together with the cross and field terms this is exactly M4.

For a traceless Hermitian tangent covector Y, set
Y0=Y-Tr(rho Y)I. Completeness gives
 <Y,D_I Y>=4Tr(rho Y0 rho Y0).
If rho>=eta I/2, the right side is at least
eta^2 ||Y0||_HS^2>=eta^2 ||Y||_HS^2. The last inequality uses TrY=0,
not an assumption that Y0 is traceless.

For F=sum z_a F_a, ||F||_HS=||z||_2, positivity/trace one give
 ||{F,rho}-2Tr(F rho)rho||_HS<=4||F||_HS,
 ||-i[F,rho]||_HS<=2||F||_HS.
Direct sums retain these bounds, irrespective of K or differing dimensions.
As N_p<=N, the isotope part is at least alpha_min eta^2 I/N. The cross
part has norm at most (|delta|M/(2N))(4^2+2^2)=10|delta|M/N.
Consequently |delta|M<=alpha_min eta^2/20 implies
D>=alpha_min eta^2 I/(2N) on every rho_p>=eta I/2.

This verifies M5. Trace-zero noise and drift preserve every local trace.
A smooth covariance root on a neighborhood of the compact stopping domain
can be extended using a cutoff. Starting at I/d_p, the process stays a
density until its first eigenvalue reaches eta. The root's margin is
alpha_min eta^2/(4N) for the covariance, and is not a constant numerical
gap independent of N.

3. LOCAL LOGDETERMINANT: MIXED DRIFT BUT NO NEW MARGINAL VARIANCE

Let ell_p=log det rho_p. For any traceless F,
 v_F ell_p=-2d_p Tr(F rho_p), w_F ell_p=0,
 Hess ell_p[X,Y]=-Tr(rho_p^-1 X rho_p^-1 Y).
The cross contribution to D has zero p,p diagonal block. Hence both the
Itô Hessian contraction and the quadratic variation of ell_p use only
(alpha_p/N_p)D_(I,p), even though different ell_p martingales may correlate.
The mixed drift is
(1/2)v_(k_p) ell_p=-d_p f_p^T k_p.

For completeness, locally
 sum_a {Tr(rho^-1 v_a rho^-1 v_a)
          -Tr(rho^-1 w_a rho^-1 w_a)}
       =4(d^2-2+d purity).
Combining this with the drift in Section 2 yields, exactly,
 L ell_p=(alpha_p/N_p)[2N_p-d_p^2-d_p(2N_p-1)purity_p]
                  -d_p Tr(rho_p h_p)-d_p f_p^T k_p,
 d<M_p>/dt=2(alpha_p/N_p)d_p^2(purity_p-1/d_p).
This independently establishes M6, including its 2N_p-1 coefficient.

Since ||f_p||^2=purity_p-1/d_p<=1, ||B_pq||<=M, and
sum_(q!=p)N_q/N<=1, ||k_p||<=|delta|M. For |delta|<=1,
beta=alpha_max(dmax^2+2dmax)+dmax(B+M)
is a valid uniform lower-drift bound. The balance promise gives
<M_p>_1<=v_N=2alpha_max dmax^2/(lambda0 N).
The bound involves N_p before applying that promise; replacing N_p by N
for an arbitrarily small species would be invalid.

4. JOINT EXIT EVENT AND ALL-N QUANTUM COMPARISON

Stop ALL local states and their single Feynman-Kac weight at the first
minimum-eigenvalue hit eta=e^-x. If p causes that hit, det rho_p<=eta,
whereas ell_p(0)=-d_p log d_p and its time-integrated drift is >=-beta.
Its martingale therefore hits -L or lower, for
L=x-dmax log dmax-beta>0.

For theta>0 the exponential martingale for -theta M_p, and <M_p>_1<=v_N,
give P(inf M_p<=-L)<=exp(-theta L+theta^2 v_N/2).
Optimizing theta=L/v_N and union bounding, without independence, proves
P(tau<=1)<=K exp(-N L^2/kappa), kappa=4alpha_max dmax^2/lambda0.

The cross operator bound does not assume commuting within-species axes.
For any PSD real coefficient matrix C, sum_ij C_ij(J_iJ_j+J_jJ_i)/2
is PSD, by diagonalization into squares of Hermitian linear combinations.
Apply this to M I +/- mathcal B. Thus
-M sum_p Q_p <= Q_mathcalB <= M sum_p Q_p.
With ||Q_p||<=m_p N_p^2 and the cross coefficient delta/(2N),
||H_N||<=Hbar N, Hbar=alpha_max mmax+M mmax/2+B.

The product kernel starts at I/D, D=product_p d_p^N_p. With M_H(X)={H,X}/2,
its stopped expectation W_t obeys the exact residual
 W'_t-M_H W_t=-E[1_(tau<=t) w_tau M_H k(rho_tau)].
All stopped weights are <=exp(Hbar N), and the trace-norm semigroup bound
is <=exp(Hbar N). Duhamel gives
||e^H/D-W_1||_1<=K Hbar N exp[-N(L^2/kappa-2Hbar)].
Tr(H)/D=sum_p alpha_p(d_p-1/d_p)>=0; cross and fields have zero trace.
Spectral Jensen gives Tr(e^H)/D>=1. Normalizing W_1 therefore introduces
at most the stated factor two. This checks M9-M11 on the WHOLE density.

5. HETEROGENEOUS BALL WITHOUT AN IMPORTED BALL THEOREM

At physical site i use HS basis I/sqrt(d_i),F_ia. Every F_ia has trace
zero and operator norm <=1. For a nonidentity tensor label v, let P_v
place F_ia on its active support and I elsewhere. The state
(I+epsilon P_v)/D, epsilon=+1 or -1, is fully separable: uniformly average
tensor_i (I+s_i F_ia)/d_i on the active sites over signs whose product is
epsilon, with I/d_i on inactive sites. Every proper sign character has
zero average; the full character equals epsilon. This also covers an
active support of size one. Different site dimensions cause no change.

Expand trace-zero Hermitian E=sum_(v!=0)e_v T_v in the HS tensor basis.
Here T_v=c_v P_v with c_v=product_(inactive sites)d_i^-1/2<=1.
The parity-state coefficients are a_v=D c_v e_v. Hence
sum|a_v|<=D sqrt(D^2-1)||E||_HS<=D^2||E||_1.
If ||E||_1<=D^-2, I/D+E is a convex combination of the appropriate
parity states with weights |a_v| and I/D with the remaining weight.
This is a full physical-site separable ball, not merely a species ball.

6. EXTRACTION AND EXACT ABSORPTION

Every stopped local density is >=eta I, so
rho_p=d_p eta(I/d_p)+(1-d_p eta)rho'_p, with rho'_p a density.
Each whole product has the SAME identity component w=D eta^N.
Positive weighted averaging and normalization preserve this coefficient:
sigma=w I/D+(1-w)tau_sep. The remaining state is fully separable, though
it can lose identical factors within a species.

For E=rho_Gibbs-sigma, TrE=0. If ||E||_1<=wD^-2=eta^N/D, then
rho_Gibbs=w[I/D+E/w]+(1-w)tau_sep is exactly fully separable by Section 5.
An arbitrarily small approximation error alone would not imply this;
the strictly positive white component is the load-bearing extra margin.
As D<=dmax^N, error<=(eta/dmax)^N suffices. This validates M12.

8. ACTUAL UNBALANCED COUNTEREXAMPLE ATTEMPT: ISOTROPIC STAR

Take a qubit species A with N_A=1 and qubit species B with N_B=n, N=n+1.
Its HS basis is F_a=sigma_a/sqrt(2). In B's spin-j sector, the self-Casimir
terms are scalars. Choose negative isotropic cross coupling, so
H_cross=-(2|delta|/N) S_A.J_B=-g S_A.J_B.
S_A.J_B has eigenvalues j/2 and -(j+1)/2 in total-spin j+1/2 and j-1/2,
with dimensions 2j+2 and 2j. The lower-spin thermal probability L obeys
 L/(1-L)=[j/(j+1)] exp[g(2j+1)/2].

Any product between the central qubit and the spin-j system has
<S_A.J_B>>=-j/2. The invariant state's mean is j/2-(2j+1)L/2, so this
entanglement witness triggers iff L>2j/(2j+1), equivalently
 |delta| > [N/(2j+1)] log(2j+2).
This is also the exact bipartite-separability threshold for this invariant
two-projector family: twirling parallel spin coherent products gives L=0;
twirling antiparallel products gives L=2j/(2j+1); their mixtures fill the
allowed interval. Twirling uses a shared classical rotation and preserves
bipartite separability. For j=0 the state is a product.

As 2j+1<=N, the displayed threshold is minimized at maximal j=n/2 and
equals log(N+1). Consequently fixed weak delta cannot make this
unbalanced family entangled across A:B, and for all fixed delta the family
is eventually bipartite separable. Projection onto a B spin sector is a
legal B-local test of A:B entanglement. It is not a sitewise-local test
for full separability within B. This attack therefore yields NO
counterexample to either the promised theorem or its unbalanced extension.

9. ACTUAL UNBALANCED COUNTEREXAMPLE ATTEMPT: TWO-AXIS XY STAR

Use the same populations, zero fields, B_AB=diag(1,1,0), ||mathcal B||=1.
Then H_cross=(delta/N)(sigma_x J_Bx+sigma_y J_By)
               =(delta/N)(S_A+ J_B-+S_A- J_B+).
In a spin-j sector it couples |up,m> with |down,m+1> by
c_m=(delta/N)sqrt((j-m)(j+m+1)), m=-j,...,j-1.
Exponentiation gives diagonals cosh(c_m) and off-diagonals sinh(c_m);
the two uncoupled edge diagonals equal 1.

Partial transpose at A moves this off-diagonal into the block
|down,m>,|up,m+1>, whose diagonal entries are cosh(c_(m-1)) and
cosh(c_(m+1)). Set c_(-j-1)=c_j=0 for the edge convention. It follows
EXACTLY that sector positivity under partial transpose is equivalent to
 sinh^2(c_m)<=cosh(c_(m-1))cosh(c_(m+1)) for every m.

But |c_m|<=|delta|(j+1/2)/N<=|delta|/2. Thus for
|delta|<=2 asinh(1), the left side is <=1 and the right side >=1,
uniformly in n and every allowed j. The positive scalar Casimir weights
and B multiplicity identities preserve partial-transpose positivity in
the whole state. This is a genuine analytic obstruction to this attempted
weak-coupling NPT counterexample. PPT in a 2-by-large system does NOT
prove full separability or exclude bound entanglement.

The own program check_unbalanced_xy.py checks the formula against direct
matrix exponentiation/PT eigenvalues in 3 small sectors (dimensions 4,6,12)
and records 49 stress rows, n up to 50 and delta up to 16. Large-delta NPT
rows stress the witness outside the claimed delta_star regime. They are
not counterexamples. No full N-site dense operator was constructed, no
peer script was executed, and numerical signs are transcription evidence
only. The weak-coupling PPT conclusion above is proved by inequalities.

10. COSTS, OPEN GATES, AND REQUIRED PRESENTATION BOUNDARIES

Inputs are supplied known coefficients, not learned Hamiltonian parameters.
For rational/computably encoded inputs the read costs are O((sum m_p)^2)
cross entries, O(sum d_p^2) field entries and O(K logN) population bits,
plus the coefficient bit lengths. Arbitrary exact real inputs instead
require their supplied exact-real interface; no finite-bit compiler follows.
The SDE has sum m_p coordinates; N-site output still requires N emissions.
The covariance gap is O(eta^2/N); first-hit certification, root precision,
Brownian discretization, Feynman-Kac weight sampling, and normalizer accuracy
are all unimplemented. Weights can be exp(Hbar N). The dense ball
correction reads D^2-1 coefficients and can need exponentially many terms.
Exact real coefficients in a finite-dimensional cone membership proof are
not an exact finite rational-output decomposition algorithm.

Own diagnostic elapsed science compute was approximately 0.005 seconds,

This does not charge or claim execution of the SDE or a quantum device.
