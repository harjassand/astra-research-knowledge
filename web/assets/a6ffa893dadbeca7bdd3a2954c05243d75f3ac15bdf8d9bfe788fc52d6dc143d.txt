CYCLE09 NONREVERSIBLE WEAK-REGULARITY PRIOR ADDENDUM
Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: exact scope of KT Conjecture 19 and a bounded check for previously
identified weak-regularity counterexamples or corrections. No other proof

The source gate is unambiguous. Kastoryano–Temme Definition 9 defines weak
Lp-regularity for every p>=1 and every Hermitian f. Its lower branch is

  E_p(f) >= E_2(I_{2,p}(f)),       1<=p<=2.

Conjecture 19 says: “Let L:M_d -> M_d be a primitive Liouvillian. Then L is
weakly Lp-regular. If, furthermore, L is reversible, then it is strongly
Lp-regular.” The reversibility condition is only attached to the additional
strong conclusion. Thus the weak conjecture covers primitive stationary
generators without detailed balance, not just KMS-symmetric or reversible
ones. The source explicitly says its authors' numerical counterexamples
exclude strong regularity for all non-reversible Liouvillians; it does not
identify a weak-regularity counterexample.

Against that exact scope, the permitted internal non-reversible gate packet
proposes a primitive finite-dimensional stationary example with a strict
physical inequality J_L(rho)<2 E_sym,L(rho). Its separately supplied reverse
orientation says that for LSharp=Gamma_sigma^{-1} L_* Gamma_sigma and the
fixed positive f=Gamma_sigma^{-1}(rho), the KT positive-state endpoint obeys

  E_{1,LSharp}(f)=J_L(rho)/2 < E_{2,LSharp}(I_{2,1}(f)).

The endpoint gap is strict at one fixed faithful f. The packet then invokes
continuity in p to obtain some delta>0 for which the same fixed primitive
generator LSharp and fixed positive f violate

  E_p(f) >= E_2(I_{2,p}(f))

for every 1<p<1+delta. If the supplied construction and orientation proof
withstand their independent audits, this directly contradicts the meaningful
low-p weak branch of Conjecture 19, rather than only the known strong
non-reversible clause. The finite-p statement is an existence interval; the
packet does not certify a particular p or numerical epsilon. It does not rule
out a smaller positive non-reversible comparison constant or settle the best
non-reversible profile.

This endpoint is separate from the printed high-p coefficient issue in KT
Eq. (31): it uses only positive inputs in the low-p branch, whose coefficient
is 1. It is also separate from the KMS sine profile and KMS strong-regularity
examples. The required physical-Hamiltonian erratum was read first: the
physical Hamiltonian in the C representation is (C-C*)/(4i), not /(2i).
The original obstruction packet's direct physical C-form and replays are
reported unchanged by that erratum; this memo does not rely on or repeat the
incorrect Hamiltonian prose.

Prior-literature check: the journal PDF and arXiv v2 both retain the same
Definition 9 and Conjecture 19, including the universal primitive weak claim.
The journal text says the weak conjecture remains an open question and
distinguishes the already-known numerical counterexamples to the strong

identified counterexample to the weak branch and no correction/erratum to

weak-regularity counterexample or repair. This is only a bounded check, not
evidence of novelty or priority.
