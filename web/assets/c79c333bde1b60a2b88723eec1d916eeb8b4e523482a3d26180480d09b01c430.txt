1. INTERFACE AND EXACT FINITE FAMILY

Distances are T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. A broadcaster is ONE CPTP
map B(H)->B(H tensor H), acting on the whole matrix algebra. Its objective
is the worst of its two MARGINAL distances over all promised states. An EB
reconstructor is ONE full-domain EB map B(H)->B(H). No unknown label or
design key is given to either map. Maximum-prior Holevo capacity uses nats.

Let L>=2, d>=2, N_j=2^j, m=2^(L-1),
H=directsum_j Sym^(N_j)(C^d), D_N=binom(N+d-1,N), D_tot=sum_j D_(N_j).
For a unit vector psi put rho_psi=L^(-1)directsum_j psi^(tensor N_j),
where psi in a state expression denotes |psi><psi|. Each state has L
nonzero eigenvalues 1/L and entropy ln L.

The explicit weighted finite design has t=m+1, q=ceil((t+1)/2), r=t+1.
In stick-breaking coordinates, p_a=y_a product_(h<a)(1-y_h) for a<d and
p_d=product_(h<d)(1-y_h). Independent y_a have density
(d-a)(1-y_a)^(d-a-1) on (0,1). This follows directly from the simplex
Jacobian product_a (1-y_a)^(d-a-1) and uniform simplex normalization
(d-1)!=product_a(d-a). Thus no unverified design-existence oracle is used.

For each polynomial weight the moment inner product is strictly positive
on every nonzero polynomial, with rational moments. Gram--Schmidt gives a
monic degree-q rational orthogonal polynomial. It has q distinct interior
roots: if it had fewer interior sign-change roots, multiply it by the
product of those linear factors. The product then has constant nonzero
sign except at isolated zeros on (0,1), while the multiplier has degree
<q, contradicting orthogonality. Since the polynomial has degree q, the
q sign-change roots are all simple and account for every root.

For its roots z_i, let l_i be the Lagrange cardinal polynomial and define
w_i=integral l_i(y)w(y)dy. Polynomial division by the degree-q orthogonal
polynomial proves quadrature exactness through degree 2q-1: the quotient
has degree <=q-1 and its integral against the orthogonal polynomial is
zero; the remainder is integrated by interpolation. Applying exactness to
l_i^2 gives w_i=integral l_i^2 w>0. All nodes and weights are algebraic;
the moments are rational and the interpolation coefficients algebraic.
The weights sum to one. This verifies positivity as well as exactness.

Choose phases exp(2pi i k_a/r), independently uniform for a<d, and set
psi_a=sqrt(p_a)exp(2pi i k_a/r), psi_d=sqrt(p_d). The squared magnitudes
sum to one and are strictly positive. Every coordinate is algebraic.
There are (q r)^(d-1) weighted labels before possible duplicate removal.

For multiindices alpha,beta with |alpha|=|beta|=t, phase averaging kills
all terms unless alpha_a=beta_a for a<d: the differences lie in [-t,t]
and r>t, so the only multiple of r is zero. Equal total degrees force
equality in coordinate d as well. A surviving monomial product_a p_a^alpha_a
has degree alpha_h+sum_(a>h)alpha_a<=t in each stick variable y_h. Hence
the product quadrature integrates it exactly. In normalized occupation
basis, the diagonal Haar coefficient is

 multinomial(t;alpha) * (d-1)! product_a(alpha_a)!/(t+d-1)!
 =1/D_t.

The off-diagonal coefficients vanish. Consequently the finite design has
sum_x w_x psi_x^(tensor t)=P_sym^t/D_t exactly. Taking partial traces
supplies all moments up to t. The SAME finite family and SAME positive
prior therefore provide every N_j+1 estimation moment, the two-copy Choi
moment, and every N_j capacity moment. This is a weighted design; equal
weights are neither asserted nor required.

Compressing a global POVM to a sector gives effects M_(y,N) summing to

The inequality only uses positive factors and 0<=P_sym<=I. If a channel

 ||Omega||=(d+3)/[2d(d+1)],

6. FAITHFUL BOUNDARY: ERRORS AND A SEPARATE EXACT CAPACITY

Let eta=L^(-3), nu=I_H/D_tot and rho'_psi=(1-eta)rho_psi+eta nu.

 b2(E') <=(1-eta)/L+eta,

e(E')->1 for d=Lm.

Put a=(1-eta)/L and s=eta/D_tot. Each rho' has eigenvalue a+s with
multiplicity L and s with multiplicity D_tot-L. Every prior mean has
sector trace a+sD_N. Entropy is maximized by a scalar matrix in each
sector; the finite design makes that occur simultaneously. The maximum
mean eigenvalue in sector N is a/D_N+s, with multiplicity D_N. Consequently

 C(E')=-sum_j D_(N_j)(a/D_(N_j)+s)ln(a/D_(N_j)+s)
        +L(a+s)ln(a+s)+(D_tot-L)s ln s

is the EXACT faithful max-prior capacity. This formula is distinct from
the unperturbed binomial average and has an actual finite maximizing
prior. Adding a temporary independent mixture flag and then discarding

 (1-eta)C(E)-h2(eta)<=C(E')<=(1-eta)C(E).

For completeness, the lower bound also follows directly from entropy
concavity for the mean and the classical-mixture entropy upper bound for
each individual state. The upper bound is mutual-information data
processing after discarding the mixture flag. The discarded conditional
information is between 0 and h2(eta). These standard entropy inequalities
are used with finite-dimensional cq states, not as unverified candidate
source gates. In particular C(E')/C(E)->1, and all stated asymptotic
orders survive. Faithfulness does not introduce an unnoticed dimension

7. OBSERVATIONAL RADIUS: UNPERTURBED AND FAITHFUL

Use D_obs(rho||sigma)=sup_(0<=F<=I) p ln(p/q), p=Tr(F rho), q=Tr(F sigma),
with the usual zero/infinite support conventions. The center is required
to lie in closed conv(E), not to depend on the label. For any such center,
its top flag has trace 1/L. The top coherent projector F_psi gives
p=1/L and the design average of q_psi is 1/(L D_m). Jensen for -ln q
therefore proves R(E)>=(ln D_m)/L, including singular centers.

The stated upper R<=C+ln 2 is correct. Here an even stronger direct bound
R<=C avoids importing a general radius theorem. Choose the design mean
sigma_*=L^(-1)directsum_j I_N/D_N in conv(E). For an arbitrary effect put
alpha_j=<psi^tensor N_j|F_j|psi^tensor N_j> in [0,1]. Then

 p=sum_j alpha_j/L,
 q=sum_j Tr(F_j)/(L D_(N_j))>=sum_j alpha_j/(L D_(N_j)).

The classical log-sum inequality gives
p ln(p/q)<=sum_j(alpha_j/L)ln D_(N_j)<=C. Thus

 (ln D_m)/L <=R(E)<=C(E),

and R(E)=Theta(C(E)) for d=Lm. No exact value of R is claimed.

The faithful boundary can be checked separately rather than transferred
by an unjustified continuity assertion. For sigma' in conv(E'), its top
trace is a+sD_m. For F_psi the numerator is p'=a+s and its average
denominator is a/D_m+s. Hence

 R(E') >=(a+s)ln[(a+s)/(a/D_m+s)].

Since sD_m<=eta and eta/a=O(L^(-2)), the lower bound has order
(ln D_m)/L. For the upper choose the faithful design mean sigma'_*. It
commutes with every rho'_psi, is faithful and lies in conv(E'). The
relative entropy D(rho'_psi||sigma'_*) equals the exact C(E') for every
label, because sector traces are fixed. Simultaneous diagonalization and
the classical log-sum inequality for a binary effect give
D_obs<=D+ln 2. Therefore R(E')<=C(E')+ln 2 and

 R(E')=Theta(C(E'))

as well. This establishes that the FINITE FAITHFUL hard family does not
evade the proposed complexity resource. The Jain--Nayak Definition 1

8. PAULI: PRIOR QUANTIFIERS, EB OPTIMUM, AND b2 OPTIMUM

Let d=2^q>=2, 0<a<1 and E_P={rho_(P,s)=(I+s aP)/d}, over nonidentity
Hermitian tensor Paulis P and signs s=+/-1. Every state is faithful and
has entropy ln d-h(a), where

 h(a)=[(1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a)]/2.

Any prior mean has entropy <=ln d, while balanced signs attain I/d.
Thus maximum-prior Holevo capacity is EXACTLY h(a).

The uniform-prior accessible bound a^2/(d+1) passes. For POVM outcome
M_y with t_y=Tr M_y, write omega_y=M_y/t_y and r_(P,y)=Tr(P omega_y).
The conditional-to-unconditional likelihood ratio is 1+s a r_(P,y).
The classical inequality D<=chi-square bounds mutual information by
a^2 sum_y(t_y/d)sum_P r_(P,y)^2/(d^2-1). Pauli orthogonality gives
sum_P r_P^2=d Tr(omega_y^2)-1<=d-1, and sum_y t_y/d=1.
The same proof covers outcome integrals. Nothing here bounds all priors.

Exposed calculation A: maximum-prior ACCESSIBLE capacity also equals
h(a), exactly. A balanced prior on just the commuting pair rho_(P,+),
rho_(P,-), followed by measurement of P, attains h(a). For a self-contained
upper bound, fix any POVM and q_y=Tr M_y/d. For each label, diagonalize
its rho relative to I/d and apply classical log-sum to that measurement:
D(p_y|label||q_y)<=h(a). For any prior pi,

 I(X:Y)=sum_x pi_x D(p_y|x||q_y)-D(mean p_y||q_y)<=h(a).

So maximizing prior and measurement cannot exceed h(a). The original
uniform-prior information bound remains useful as a fixed-prior locking
benchmark, but cannot be substituted for a max-prior resource.
