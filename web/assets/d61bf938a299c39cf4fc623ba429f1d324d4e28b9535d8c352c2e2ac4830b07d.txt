STRONGEST CONCRETE RESULT
Internally derived sharp theorem, awaiting an independent proof/prior-art gate:
every smooth nowhere-zero steady divergence-free field X on every closed
Riemannian three-manifold (M,g), with V=Vol(M), has

                 ||Def X||_L2^2 >= 2 V h_mu(phi_1)^2,

where mu is normalized Riemannian volume, entropy uses natural logs and
phi_t is the particle flow. Consequently any stationary Newtonian solution
with strain viscosity L=2 div Def and kinematic viscosity nu>0 obeys

         integral F.X = 2nu ||Def X||_L2^2 >= 4nu V h_mu(phi_1)^2.

The constant 4 is attained by a Sol mapping-torus suspension of the cat map
[[2,1],[1,1]]. It is a complete scoped analytic derivation with a sharp
construction, rather than an imported new-release headline. All 11 symbolic
geometry/algebra checks passed; those checks are not the general proof.

The deficit Delta=(integral F.X)/(4nuV)-h_mu(phi_1)^2 also satisfies

       Delta >= Var_mu(s)+(3/4)E_mu q^2+E_mu |b|^2,

where q=X(log|X|), b=P(Def X)e, e=X/|X|, P=I-e tensor e, and the
normal strain block has eigenvalues -q/2 plus/minus s. Near equality therefore
certifies almost constant transverse strain and small log-speed/cross-strain
defects. No global metric-rigidity theorem is asserted.

MECHANISM AND PROOF
Family 376's full-box compiler prescribes a divergence-free velocity U and
then pays the residual force U_t+(U.grad)U-nu Delta U. Its local curl
extension explicitly realizes determinant-one diagonal motions on complete
boxes, so incompressible information processing is carried by stretching
and compensated contraction. This local mechanism was reconstructed from
geometry.tex, not inferred from the manuscript's universal-computation label.

The consequential external comparator is the April 2026 cosymplectic
stationary Navier--Stokes universality paper. Its adapted metric makes the
Reeb field Hodge harmonic, hence Hodge viscosity vanishes. However, strain
viscosity and Hodge viscosity differ by a Ricci term. That constitutive
distinction is old (Chan--Czubak--Disconzi; Samavaki--Tuomela), so I did not
claim its discovery. I attacked its quantitative dynamical consequence.

For general steady nowhere-zero X, the derivative flow preserves its line.
On the two-dimensional normal quotient, the symmetric generator has
eigenvalues -q/2 plus/minus s. The integrated q term equals a bounded
log-speed difference, so the top normal Lyapunov exponent is at most the
time average of s. Volume preservation and the neutral flow direction give
the full spectrum chi,0,-chi. Ruelle's inequality then gives
h_mu<=E_mu chi<=E_mu s. The pointwise identity

                   |Def X|^2=2s^2+(3/2)q^2+2|b|^2

and Cauchy--Schwarz give the theorem and deficit certificate. Stationary
energy balance gives its work interpretation. The general normal-cocycle
calculation is written explicitly in the separate proof; that is the most
important next independent audit.

SHARP CONSTRUCTION / EXACT CONSTITUTIVE COST
Let lambda=(3+sqrt(5))/2 and a=log lambda. On the mapping torus of the cat
map, using its real eigenbasis, set

                   g=e^(2at)dx^2+e^(-2at)dy^2+dt^2,
                   X=partial_t.

The gluing (v,t)->(Av,t-1) preserves the metric. Its volume is constant,
X-flat=dt is harmonic, the return-map entropy is a, and

   Def X=diag(a,-a,0), Ric(X)=-2a^2 X, L X=-4a^2 X.

Thus Hodge viscosity admits the unforced steady field, whereas strain
viscosity requires F=4nu a^2 X and has exactly 4nuVa^2 work. This proves
sharpness and gives an exact numerical calibration case for intrinsic
curved-fluid solvers. It does not construct a new Turing machine.

For any Hodge-harmonic X on closed M, not necessarily unit speed, the
physical-strain replacement is F=-2nu Ric(X), pressure -|X|^2/2. Every
pressure choice costs

              integral F.X=2nu||nabla X||^2
                          =-2nu integral Ric(X,X),

which vanishes iff X is parallel. The minimum L2 force after arbitrary
pressure adjustment is -2nu P_Leray Ric(X). These conclusions are exact
interface repairs, not proof that the broader fluid-computation problem
has been resolved.

PRIOR ART / STATUS / FALSIFICATION
Established import: Ruelle's compact C1 entropy inequality. Its five-page
proof was retrieved and read. The sharp example's entropy is also checked
directly by small partition cylinders of area O(lambda^(-n)); no separate
unread toral-entropy theorem is needed for that lower bound.
The metric/strain identities are classical and were independently derived.
Sol mapping tori and suspension dynamics are also old, e.g. Butler--Paternain
2007. No exhaustive search has cleared the sharp combined work inequality;
it may already exist as a stretching/entropy estimate in hydrodynamics.
The candidate's novelty is UNKNOWN, and its present scope is modest.

Several tempting upgrades are false or unsupported:
- Normalized-volume metric entropy cannot be replaced by topological entropy.
  Null-volume coding sets can carry complex dynamics.
- Universal computation does not imply positive normalized-volume entropy.
  Therefore this does not give a universal minimum power per machine step.
- Positive entropy does not imply universal computation.
- This is a steady, nowhere-zero, closed-manifold theorem. Time-dependent
  forcing, zero-speed sets, boundaries and stochastic robustness remain outside.
- Time rescaling X->cX scales entropy by c and work by c^2. A power floor
  independent of execution speed is therefore not supplied.
- Unforced stationary strain-viscosity flows are Killing by the old energy
  argument, but zero entropy alone is not a general algorithmic-decidability
  theorem for arbitrary computable descriptors or exact observation sets.
