1. POSITIVE FUNCTIONAL-CALCULUS GATES PRESERVE THE XXZ COUNTING CONE

Use the baseline exact convention H_phys=-H and shifted PSD operators
 A_e=3alpha I+alpha(XX+YY)+gamma ZZ, alpha>=|gamma|,
 A_v=rI+bX+cZ, r=b+|c|, b>=0.
Both are real, entrywise nonnegative and PSD. Their norm sum is bounded by
 D=6sum alpha+2sum(b+|c|)=2C. For any polynomial f with nonnegative rational
coefficients and constant coefficient at least one, define F=f(sA), s>=0.
F is rational, entrywise nonnegative, Hermitian and F>=I.

For the pair operator A_e, the two outer basis vectors have eigenvalue
 lambda0=3alpha+gamma, and the inner 2x2 block has eigenvalues
 lambda-=alpha-gamma, lambda+=5alpha-gamma.
The cone assumption implies 0<=lambda-<=lambda0<=lambda+. In the table
of F, its six weights satisfy
 a=f(slambda0), b-c=f(slambda-), b+c=f(slambda+).
Since f is nonnegative and nondecreasing on [0,infinity),
 b-c<=a<=b+c and b>=c>=0.
Consequently all three triangle inequalities among a,b,c hold. The
homogeneous quadratic Hessian has eigenvalues a+b+c,a-b-c,-a+b-c,-a-b+c,
and at most one is positive. This proves LC of the same four-halfedge gate.

For the field operator A_v, F keeps the real 2x2 form [[v,d],[d,u]].
Its entries are nonnegative, u,v>=1, and F>=I implies det F=uv-d^2>=1.
The prior baseline lift
 d z0z1+(u/2)x(z0+z1)+(v/2)y(z0+z1)+dxy
therefore remains homogeneous LC, by its proved uv>=d^2 criterion. There
is no newly supplied square root, eigenbasis, real-number oracle or phase.

In particular take f(x)=1+x+x^2/2. Pair weights are explicitly
 a=1+s(3alpha+gamma)+(s^2/2)(3alpha+gamma)^2,
 b=1+s(3alpha-gamma)+(s^2/2)[(3alpha-gamma)^2+4alpha^2],
 c=2salpha+2s^2alpha(3alpha-gamma).
Field weights, with upper=r+c, lower=r-c, are
 v=1+s upper+(s^2/2)(upper^2+b^2),
 u=1+s lower+(s^2/2)(lower^2+b^2),
 d=sb+s^2rb.
Every formula is an exact rational operation. The earlier local exchange
implementation works unchanged on these new coefficient tables. Their
encoding length is O(L+log m) in the thermal application.

2. COMPLETE SECOND-ORDER LOG-GENERATOR ERROR WITHOUT DIMENSION LOSS

Let A_1,...,A_p be the shifted local terms and s=beta/(2m). Put
 F_i=I+sA_i+(s^2/2)A_i^2,
 W=F_1...F_p, B=WW*=F_1...F_p F_p...F_1.
Let the palindromic increments be X_1,...,X_(2p), each equal to the appropriate
sA_i, S=sum X_j=beta(H+CI)/m, and delta=sum||X_j||<=tau/m, tau=beta D.
Assume delta<=1/4. Since every F_i>=I, each has minimum singular value at
least one; products inherit this property. Thus B>=I. Also exp(S)>=I.

The product expansion's degree-zero and degree-one terms are I and S.
Its degree-two term is
 (1/2)sum_j X_j^2+sum_(i<j)X_iX_j.
The ordered-pair commutator sum vanishes: reversing (i,j) to
(2p+1-j,2p+1-i) replaces [X_i,X_j] by its negative; fixed pairs have equal
operators and zero commutator. Thus the degree-two term is S^2/2 exactly.

The norm of all product terms of degree >=3 is majorized by the corresponding
tail of product_j(1+x_j+x_j^2/2), x_j=||X_j||. Coefficientwise this scalar
product is no larger than exp(sum x_j). The exponential exp(S) has the same
norm majorant. Therefore
 ||B-exp(S)||<=2 sum_(k>=3)delta^k/k!
 <=delta^3/[3(1-delta)]<=4delta^3/9<=delta^3.

For any positive matrices M,N>=I, the resolvent integral for their logarithms
gives
 log M-log N=integral_0^infinity[(N+tI)^-1-(M+tI)^-1]dt,
and the inverse difference identity bounds its norm by
 ||M-N|| integral_0^infinity(1+t)^-2dt=||M-N||.
This argument is valid for noncommuting M,N. Apply it to B and exp(S):
 ||log B-S||<=delta^3.
Consequently
 ||m log B-beta(H+CI)||<=tau^3/m^2.

The directly acquired rational/integer choice
 m=max{1,ceil(4tau),ceil_sqrt(4tau^3/epsilon)}
makes this generator error <=epsilon/4. ceil_sqrt is implemented using
integer isqrt and exact fraction comparisons, not a supplied real root.
The circuit has 2mp local gates, and the same homogeneous lift has N=8mp.
The previous first-order Euler certificate used
 m=ceil max{1,4tau,20tau^2/epsilon}.
The new m is O(1+tau+tau^(3/2)/sqrt(epsilon)). This does NOT remove the
numerical-temperature cost or change the admitted Hamiltonian cone.

Given generator error kappa, the earlier baseline's min-max trace argument
gives multiplicative exp(+-kappa) partition error, and its Jeffreys-divergence
argument gives whole normalized-state TV <=tanh(kappa/2). The scalar
exp(-beta C) still has charged finite-precision cost when a partition value
is output. The direct measurement sampler below needs no scalar normalizer.

3. ACQUIRED THIRD-ORDER PAULI CERTIFICATE

The second-order proof deliberately threw away all cancellations above degree
two. They can be acquired, rather than supplied as a full-matrix norm oracle.
Write the physical local A_i as their at-most-four rational Pauli terms.
Define the formal product
 B(h)=product_(palindromic A_i)[I+hA_i+h^2 A_i^2/2].
Let S0=2sum_i A_i, and C3 be the degree-three coefficient of B(h). Form the
exact Pauli dictionary
 R3=C3-S0^3/6=sum_P r_P P.
Palindromicity makes R3 Hermitian, so all r_P are real. Each Pauli has norm
one; acquire L3=sum_P |r_P|, giving ||R3||<=L3 without diagonalization.

The implemented multiplication represents each Pauli as (x,z), with
 P(x,z)=i^popcount(x&z) X^x Z^z.
The product key is (x xor x',z xor z'), and its exact phase exponent is
 popcount(x&z)+popcount(x'&z')-popcount((x xor x')&(z xor z'))
                   +2popcount(z&x') modulo4.
Coefficients are pairs of Fractions throughout. Thus no numerical phase or
approximate cancellation is assumed. At fixed degree three there are at most
O(p^3) potentially generated Pauli words; the deliberately simple sequential
implementation costs at most O(p^4) rational operations, with polynomial
bit cost in n,L, and stores O(p^3) words of O(n) bits. The default execution
admits at most12 local terms and4096 dictionary keys before any expansion.
These are resource guards, not the theoretical p-polynomial limit.

Since the exact degree-three difference is h^3R3, the same scalar majorant
for the remaining degree>=4 tails gives
 ||B(h)-exp(hS0)||<=h^3 L3+2 sum_(k>=4)delta^k/k!
 <=h^3L3+delta^4/[12(1-delta)]<=h^3L3+delta^4/9,
where delta=2hD<=1/4. The logarithm bound then yields, at h=beta/(2m),
 kappa<=beta^3 L3/(8m^2)+tau^4/(9m^3).
An acquired sufficient integer choice is
 m=max{1,ceil(4tau),ceil_sqrt(beta^3 L3/epsilon),
                       ceil_cuberoot(8tau^4/(9epsilon))}.
Each summand in kappa is <=epsilon/8, so kappa<=epsilon/4. The planner uses
this only when it improves the previously proved quadratic certificate.
The integer cube root is found by bounded binary search; no root oracle.

For the public own example, n=3, edges (0,1,alpha1,gamma1/2) and
(1,2,alpha2/3,gamma-1/3), X fields b0=1/4,b2=1/3, all c=0,
beta=1/2, epsilon=1/10:

No N=199520 or N=736 full FPRAS was run. The calculation is an exact
generator-resource certificate and preserves the imported counting debt.

An exact 4x4 noncommuting check uses an edge alpha1,gamma1/2 and a field
b2,c-1. It matches the Pauli dictionary to the dense degree-three coefficient;

5. DIRECT TRACE / THERMAL MEASUREMENT SAMPLING BY CAPPED REJECTION

The preceding hard mass >=3/4 gives a more useful operational consequence
than first approximating every conditional count. At z=1, conditioning the
soft law on S intersect T empty makes T=U\S and cancels the entire penalty.
The accepted law has mass proportional to mu(S)nu(U\S), exactly the trace
tensor law. Thus direct soft sampling plus rejection bypasses all counting,
the field-acquisition stage, and the 2n child-count reconstruction.

For total desired TV eta<=1/4, let
 r=ceil(log2(4/eta)), theta=eta/(4r).
Draw up to r fresh soft samples, each within TV theta, and accept the first
complementary pair. If all fail, output a fixed bit string. With exact soft
samples, acceptance probability is >=3/4, so all-fail probability is at most
4^-r<=eta/4. Couple each approximate sample to its exact counterpart;
the total coupling error is <=rtheta=eta/4. Let Nc bound all binary choices
in r mixing runs plus uniform bits for inactive qubits. Cap every coin at
ceil(log2(4Nc/eta)) digits and use a fixed bit-string fallback on any abort.
The union abort probability is <=eta/4. Hence total output TV <=3eta/4,
and in particular <=eta. All loops/tapes have deterministic caps.

At a trace cut, read the row halfedge of the first gate touching each active
qubit: its membership in S is the original physical bit. In an accepted pair
nu(T)>0 and T=U\S force exactly one selected S endpoint per wire; since every
column was complemented, this is equality of consecutive original gate bits.
Summing the field dummy choices recovers the original rational gate entries.
The resulting physical-bit distribution is therefore diag(B^m)/Tr(B^m) for
the constructed thermal circuit. Independently fair inactive-qubit bits
supply the exact extra identity trace factor. This is a classical measurement
sampler, not quantum Gibbs-state preparation.

With H=2N+ceil(log2 R_mu)+(N/2)ceil(log2(1/t)), the acquired step count is
 J=ceil[(2N/t)(H+ceil(log2(1/theta))+1)].
Using the proved local O(log N) transition, its arithmetic work is
 O(N^3 Rflip [H+log(r/eta)] log N log(1/eta)).
At Rflip=1 this is dramatically smaller than counting, with no eta^-2
statistical mass-estimation factor. Charge finite rational-coins/bit cost
as in INITIAL; random bits have the explicit Nc*coin-cap bound. For the
actual Gibbs diagonal law, add the thermal generator TV tanh(kappa/2).
The sampler accepts an explicit gate circuit; acquiring beta, coefficients,
their signs, or a Hamiltonian from experiments is outside the interface.
