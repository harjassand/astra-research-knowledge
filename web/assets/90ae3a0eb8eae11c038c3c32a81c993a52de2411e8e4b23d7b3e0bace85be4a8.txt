   positive full-basis configurations are the two alternating q-subsets,
   q=n/2, with equal weight. They are q swaps apart. A kernel limited to
   fewer than q within-replica swaps plus whole-replica layer exchange has a
   nonconstant additive invariant, so its energy constant is infinite.
   Uniform full-subset Metropolis avoids that reducibility but has exact
   one-replica gap 2/binom(n,q) and two-replica additive constant
   3M(M+2)/(4(M+1)), M=binom(n,q). Exact enumerated heat-bath has constant 2
   after M determinant evaluations.

   one-swap graph. Exact cut calculations show that the two-replica energy
   constant is at least 4^b for the t=1 Metropolis chain and at least
   (9/20)4^b for t=2, when paired with independent replica updates and whole
   layer swap. Thus connectivity does not prevent an exponential-in-input-bit
   bottleneck for these kernels.
