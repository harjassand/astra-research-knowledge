RESULT AND ATTRIBUTION

The S02-origin stopped-generator theorem is no longer only an exact-real law:
a cutoff, an acquired heat seed, three-dimensional Euler increments, positive
weights, dyadic selection and independent Pauli/Clifford local outputs give a
finite-bit construction. For fixed public M,B, known rational A,b and admitted N,
its averaged whole-density trace error has the form
  C exp(-cN) + C1 [1/T + N^(-1/4)/sqrt(T)]
              + exp(B²/2) sqrt(K2/R) + epsilon.              (P1)
All constants are computable from the fixed bounds; K2 is a public proved
second moment, not estimated from the executed sample. Thus accuracy eta in the
last two scientific terms is obtainable with
  T=O_(M,B)(eta^-1+N^-1/2 eta^-2), R=O_(M,B)(eta^-2).
The real arithmetic work, before logarithmic seed and bit-precision factors, is
  O_(M,B)(eta^-3+N^-1/2 eta^-4)+N local emissions.             (P2)
Bit work is polynomial in N, rational input length, eta^-1 and epsilon^-1 for
fixed public bounds. Exponentially small requested eta still costs exponentially
many steps/proposals; cone proximity does not imply a polynomial compiler at
exponential output accuracy.

1. EXACT COEFFICIENTS AND THE LOAD-BEARING INNER BALL

Use Lambda=M+1,C=A+Lambda I,Lc=2M+1,Tc=Tr C,s=N^3/4.
For k_N(m)=((I+m.sigma)/2)^tensor N, the own stopped proof gives exactly
  M_H k_N = V k_N + a.grad k_N + D:Hess k_N/(4s²),
  H=Q_C/s²+b.J/s,
  D=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T,
  a=(N-1)/(2s²)(I-mm^T)C m+(I-mm^T)b/(2s),
  V=N(N-1)m^TCm/(4s²)+N Tc/(4s²)+N b.m/(2s).
That exact identity follows from B_n=N(n.m)+(n-(n.m)m).grad,
R_n=(n cross m).grad and (B_n²-R_n²)k=2(F_n²k+kF_n²).
No angular term is omitted. The global D may be indefinite.
On r0²=1/[16(Lc+1)], D>=3I/4 and D<=Lc I.
Hence sqrt(D) is smooth with an acquired positive margin.

Define the rational radial retraction
 P(m)=m when |m|²<=r0², and P(m)=r0² m/|m|² otherwise.
It is globally 1-Lipschitz: radial and tangential derivative norms are <=1 on
either smooth region, and the map is continuous at the boundary. Its output
is always in the ball. Let chi(m)=clip(3-4|m|²/r0²,0,1). Use
  a_cut=chi a, Sigma_cut=chi sqrt(D)/(sqrt(2)s), V_cut=V(P(m)).
Only points with |m|<sqrt(3/4)r0 have nonzero drift/noise; elsewhere both
coefficients are exactly zero, so D need never be square-rooted there.
These coefficients are globally bounded/Lipschitz. The cutoff SDE stays inside
that boundary by pathwise uniqueness; the Euler approximation may overshoot,
but its potential and final kernel use P.

For the heat seed use m=tanh(r/2)u with its exact positive heat radial law.
Apply T_seed(m)=clip(2-16|m|²/r0²,0,1)m. It is unchanged on |m|<=r0/4,
has maximum radius (4/3)r0/sqrt(24)<r0/3, and is 4-Lipschitz (the radial
derivative in its taper is 2-48|m|²/r0², between -1 and -4).
Replacing the original seed by this contracted seed costs at most twice the
heat mass outside r0/4. The own heat-tail proof applies with
  kappa0 >= r0^4/12288,
since 2atanh(r0/4)>=r0/2 and f(u)>=u^4/48 at small u.
The prefactor remains O(exp(Lambda/2) N^3/4).

Couple the cutoff law and the original radius-r0 stopped law from this seed.
They agree until reaching r0/2 (in fact chi=1 farther out). For drift movement
<=r0/8, this hit from radius<r0/3 needs martingale excursion at least r0/24.
Each coordinate qv<=Lc/(2N^3/2); the six-coordinate maximal bound gives
  P(hit)<=6 exp[-r0² N^3/2/(1728 Lc)].
The raw weighted difference is bounded by two maximal weights times this
event. Adding the exact stopped Duhamel residual and seed change preserves
the C exp(-cN) full-density conclusion after normalization. This is an actual
coupling/residual bound, not a declaration that the global generator is positive.

2. UNIFORM NORMALIZATION AND A SHARPER PUBLIC SECOND MOMENT

For y=N^1/4|m| and N>=2,
  V(m)>=(1-1/N)y²/4-B y/2>=y²/8-B y/2>=-B²/2.
This applies to V(P(X)) as well. Exact cutoff and Euler weights therefore
obey w>=exp(-B²/2); no exponentially rare normalization oracle is hidden.

Let Z=N^1/4 sup|martingale|. Continuous or discrete conditional exponential
martingale bounds give, conditional on every seed branch,
  P(Z>z | seed)<=6 exp[-N z²/(3Lc)].
Discrete Gronwall and the drift bound give
  Y:=N^1/4 sup|m| <=exp[Lc/(2sqrt N)](Y0+B/(2sqrt N)+Z).
For p>=1, theta,kappa>0, set
  A_p=p(Lc+kappa)exp[Lc/sqrt N]/4,
  d=A_p(1+theta)/4, K=2A_p(1+1/theta).
Use V<= (Lc+kappa)Y²/4+Tc/(4sqrt N)+B²/(4kappa), and
  (Y0+F+Z)²<=(1+theta)Y0²+2(1+1/theta)(F²+Z²).
Since Y0<=R_seed/2, conditional integration of the martingale tail proves
  E w^p <= exp[pTc/(4sqrt N)+pB²/(4kappa)
                       +A_p(1+1/theta)B²/(2N)]
            *[1+6K/(N/(3Lc)-K)] E_seed exp(d R_seed²),     (P3)
provided the displayed denominator is positive. No independence between seed
and martingale is assumed; its conditional tail is used.

The exact scaled heat density, apart from radial R², is
 Q_N(R)=sinch(y) exp[-Lambda R²/(16d0)-N f(y)],
 y=R/(2N^1/4), d0=1-Lambda/(2sqrt N), f=y²/2-logcosh y.
The acquired rational bound f(1)>1/16, together with monotonic tanh(z)/z and
f(y)/y², gives f(y)>=min(y^4,y²)/16. Also logsinch(y)<=y²/6.
Thus the low-radius tilted numerator is bounded by
 R² exp[-R^4/256+D R²], D=d-Lambda/(16d0)+1/(24sqrt N).
Retaining the NEGATIVE scalar-shift term materially improves the moment bound.
The exterior is bounded by a Gaussian with coefficient
 sqrt N/64+Lambda/(16d0)-1/(24sqrt N)-d, when positive.
The denominator is bounded below by integral_0^8 R² exp(-Lambda R²/8-R^4/192)dR.
Exact lower/upper rectangles, rational Taylor enclosures and simple tail bounds
acquire these integrals. public_budgets.py does this, without Gamma, quadrature
or empirical-variance oracles. The construction's conservative generic moments
remain fixed-bound constants and are not polynomial in growing M,B.

3. THE SHARPER EULER ERROR, DERIVED WITH THE N POWERS RETAINED

For global drift/noise magnitude bounds A,S and Lipschitz bounds La,Lsigma,
the coefficients above admit
 A,La=O_(M,B)(N^-1/2), S,Lsigma=O_(M,B)(N^-3/4).
Let h=1/T and X_t be the continuous Euler interpolation. Conditional Gaussian
increments on one cell of lag u give exactly
 E|X_t-X_floor(t)|²<=A²u²+S²u.
Integrate across all T cells to get
  Lag<=A²h²/3+S²h/2.                                      (P4)
Subtract the integral equations for the exact and Euler paths. Cauchy-Schwarz
for drift, Doob L2 plus Ito isometry for noise, and |a+b|²<=2|a|²+2|b|² give
  d(t):=E sup_(u<=t)|m_u-X_u|²
    <=Lstar integral_0^t [d(u)+E|X_u-X_floor(u)|²]du,
  Lstar=4La²+16Lsigma².
Gronwall gives
  d(1)<=Lstar exp(Lstar)*Lag
       =O_(M,B)(N^-2 h²+N^-5/2 h).                       (P5)
The potential is Lipschitz with LV<=((Lc r0+B)/2)sqrt N.
For ell=int V(P(m_t))dt and ell_E=h sum V(P(X_j)), Jensen gives
 E|ell-ell_E|²<=2LV²[d(1)+Lag]=O(h²+N^-1/2 h).
Use |e^x-e^y|<=(e^x+e^y)|x-y| and the common second moment K2 to bound
 E|w-w_E|<=2sqrt(K2) sqrt(E|ell-ell_E|²).

Single-qubit eigenvalues on the ball are >=(1-r0)/2. The Sylvester integral
for square roots gives a purification distance <=|m-n|/[2sqrt(1-r0)].
Tensor fidelity therefore yields
 T(k_N(m),k_N(n))<=sqrt N |m-n|/[2sqrt(1-r0)].
The retraction cannot enlarge the endpoint error. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz
and the mean-weight lower bound now give the explicit error used in the code:
 T(sigma_cut,sigma_E)
 <=exp(B²/2)sqrt(K2)
       [2sqrt(2LV²(d(1)+Lag))+sqrt N sqrt(d(1))/sqrt(1-r0)].
This proves the second term in (P1) and the scaling in (P2).
Using only a uniform-in-N Euler constant would hide this improvement.

4. IMPORTANCE SELECTION CONCERNS THE AVERAGED OUTPUT

For R iid proposals let mu=Ew,nu=Ewf, f in [0,1], and hat_mu,hat_nu be
sample means. The weighted categorical selection has conditional mean
hat_nu/hat_mu. The identity
 E[hat_nu/hat_mu]-nu/mu=E[(mu-hat_mu)(hat_nu/hat_mu)]/mu
and Cauchy-Schwarz give absolute bias <=sqrt(Ew²)/(mu sqrt R).
Apply this to f(m)=Tr[E k_N(m)] for every effect E to get the averaged-state
trace bound exp(B²/2)sqrt(K2/R). There is no claim that a finite atomic
empirical measure is close in TV to a continuous seed law, or that one sampled
branch is close to the Gibbs state. Each output is a positive product state.

5. FINITE BITS AND THE EXECUTED PIPELINE

The script samples a uniform dyadic cube in the scaled heat field R and uses
outward rational intervals for logcosh, sinch and exp to make its rejection
coins. Q_N<=1 because Lambda>=1 and sinch's positive quadratic correction
is dominated by the scalar-shift term. On [-1,1]^3, Q_N>=2^-z,
z=ceil(2(Lambda+1)), hence acceptance>=p0=2^-z/L^3 in cube [-L,L]^3.
K=ceil((error_bits+8)/p0) supplies a charged exhaustion event and a fallback.
The log-density derivative is bounded by
 Rmax(Lambda/4+1/12)+Rmax³/48, Rmax=2L.
Dyadic proposal and rejection-coin errors are therefore coupled by a finite
R*K union budget. The cube tail uses the quartic/Gaussian heat envelopes.

Clipped inverse-CDF normals are acquired by the copied S03 rational primitive.
They are close in a pointwise COUPLING to ideal normals, never in TV to a
continuous Gaussian. Tail events of all 3RT normals are charged. On retained
draws, the Euler perturbation amplifies through at most
 exp[La+2L_normal Lsigma sqrt T]. Its logarithm, coordinate/kernel factors,
logweight error and categorical sensitivity determine all guard bits.
The code uses a still looser rational upper bound with T replacing sqrt T.
Any final amplification must be included BEFORE selecting precision.

The 3x3 SPD root uses a dyadically rounded symmetric Newton candidate, then
checks rational principal minors of X-I/2 and ||X²-D||op via an exact row norm.
If that residual is q, the Sylvester identity
 X(X-sqrt D)+(X-sqrt D)sqrt D=X²-D
and both spectral floors imply ||X-sqrt D||op<=q. This residual certificate
does not depend on floating convergence or acquired eigenaxes. Each run must
pass it; unsupported guard budgets fail before emitting a result.

All proposal logweights are shifted by their exact maximum; at least one
scaled weight is exactly1. Taylor enclosures and dyadic rounding give positive
integer weights and an exact finite-integer selector. Selector exhaustion has
a declared arbitrary fallback and <=2^-128 error. Pointwise logweight error
delta<1/8 changes normalized categorical TV by <=4delta. Combining these
budgets with product-kernel fidelity and source/cap unions certifies numerical
closeness to the SAME-R,T continuous heat-seed exact-normal Euler importance law.
This numerical certificate does not include the scientific terms in (P1).

For every retained local Bloch vector, sum|m_i|<=sqrt3 r0<1. Hence
 tau_m=sum_i |m_i| tau_(sign(m_i)e_i)+(1-sum|m_i|)I/2.
An independent Pauli-axis selector at each of N sites, and an independent Z
coin in each I/2 branch, gives EXACTLY tau_m^tensor N on averaging. Each branch
uses only H,S,X from initialized |0> and at most three ideal Clifford gates.
Sharing one local coin across sites would be incorrect. The script returns
these independent gate descriptions; physical reset/gate noise remains supplied.
