REVISION 01 — COEFFICIENT-POWER AND AUXILIARY BOUNDARIES
7 October 2026. Deeper independent continuation; INITIAL remains unchanged.

The initial six-site witness gives more than failure at the square. Keep the same F, f(U), and real evaluation z_1=z_2=z_3=-1, z_5=1, with Rayleigh pair (0,4). Replace f(U)^2 by f(U)^p for any real p>=2 (and 0^p=0). The grouped bivariate coefficients are

  A(p)=-6^p+3-2*3^p-2^p,
  B(p)=-5-2^p,
  C(p)=-1-4*2^p,
  D(p)=-3.

Thus
  Delta(p)=B(p)C(p)-A(p)D(p)
         =14+18a+4a^2-6b-3ab,
  where a=2^p and b=3^p.
For p>=2, a>=4 and b=a(3/2)^p >=(9/4)a. Hence
  Delta(p) <= 14+(9/2)a-(11/4)a^2.
The right side is -12 at a=4 and strictly decreases for a>=4, so Delta(p)<=-12<0 for every p>=2. The same determinant-derived family therefore defeats every coefficientwise power p>=2, not only the square. No numerical exponent search is part of the proof.

A stability-preserving coordinate-pair auxiliary does not rescue the square. Add the legal pair (e_0,e_1) with activity w=1. The signed-hole coefficient update, proved in the baseline, is
  g(U)=f(U)+f(U union {0,1}) if U avoids {0,1}; otherwise g(U)=f(U).
Its signed polynomial Q_g=Q_f-partial_0 partial_1 Q_f remains real stable by the coordinate-pair auxiliary closure. At the same real assignment and Rayleigh pair (0,4), exact collection gives
  Q_g(z_0,z_4)      = -14 - 7z_0 -11z_4 -3z_0z_4,
  Q_{g^[2]}(z_0,z_4)= -80 - 9z_0 -25z_4 -3z_0z_4.
The stable auxiliary has Delta=77-42=35; its coefficientwise square has Delta=225-240=-15. `check_auxiliary_square.py` recomputes this and gives a direct rational upper-half-plane zero for the squared polynomial. This shows that applying one valid coordinate-pair auxiliary before coefficientwise squaring still does not yield a stability closure.

Scope: the direct counterexample only rules out stability transfer under coefficientwise powers (and stable lifts whose specialization is the failed polynomial). It does not rule out algorithms that use exact fixed-k homogenization, explicit auxiliary states, or a different non-stability invariant. In particular, the baseline's exact dummy-coordinate identity remains valid. The microscopic conditional sampler/energy comparison remains the load-bearing open gate.
