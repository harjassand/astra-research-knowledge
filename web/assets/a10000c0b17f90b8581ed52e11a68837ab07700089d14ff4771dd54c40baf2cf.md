## Primary prior art

- Noga Alon, Omri Ben-Eliezer, Eldar Fischer, “Testing hereditary properties of ordered graphs and matrices,” arXiv:1704.02367v1 (2017): [arXiv source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.02367v1). Read the primary arXiv abstract and theorem summary. It establishes strong testability/qualitative removal for hereditary ordered matrix properties, with a distance-dependent sample bound but no polynomial rate claim.
- Noga Alon, Omri Ben-Eliezer, “Efficient Removal Lemmas for Matrices,” arXiv:1609.04235v3 (2017): [arXiv source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.04235v3). Read the primary arXiv abstract and introduction, including Theorem 1.2, Theorem 1.5, and Problem 1.4. The relevant positive results concern pairwise entry-disjoint copies and permutation-closed pattern families; its abstract also states a polynomial hitting-set result for fixed binary patterns.
- Eldar Fischer, Eyal Rozenberg, “Lower bounds for testing forbidden induced substructures in bipartite-graph-like combinatorial objects,” RANDOM 2007, DOI [10.1007/978-3-540-74208-1_34](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-74208-1_34). The pinned release cites the ternary-host predecessor. I checked the abstract-level record; I did not reconstruct that paper’s proof.
- Lior Gishboliner, Asaf Shapira, “Polynomial Property Testing,” arXiv:2508.16878v1 (2025): [arXiv source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16878v1). Survey cited by the release for the ordered binary matrix-removal conjecture. Abstract-level check only; no independent theorem relies on this survey.
