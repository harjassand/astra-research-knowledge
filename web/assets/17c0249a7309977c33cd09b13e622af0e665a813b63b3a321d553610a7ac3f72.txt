VERDICT
-------
The classical-types application supplies the abstract transfer hypotheses on its large-N range, with the constants in (6.1) matching the transfer theorem exactly. The frame is complete once both the positive quantum Gram bound and the exact adjoint multiplicity threshold hold. The argument also has a valid finite-N patch, and the self-score lower bound does not require that frame. The upper bound is for the full Gibbs density-state loss, not a first-moment proxy. I found no error in the stated operational constants or the Q=d and finite-N cases.

One documentation point: the abstract transfer contract should explicitly repeat the no-entanglement-assistance/no-extra-quantum-side-memory restriction if it is intended to stand alone. The classical-types application does satisfy it by referring to regular_su_m/RESULT.txt §1, which explicitly includes that restriction. If that referenced code interface is kept, the rank-Q composite-Kraus step is valid.

1. HYPOTHESES AND CONSTANT MATCH
---------------------------------
The abstract transfer requires: a compact group with real irreducible adjoint of dimension q; an irreducible carrier of dimension d; a fixed nonzero adjoint vector x with uniform operator bound ||B_x||<=B0; the entire orbit exp(hB_x)/Tr exp(hB_x) on a fixed interval 0<h0<=h<=h1; and a finite Hermitian adjoint frame Y_(nu,A) spanning the WHOLE adjoint isotypic component, with normalized Gram G>=g0 I, Tr G<=g1, and total commutator-energy bound sum_nu C_nu<=C0.

The classical-types setup supplies a compact connected simply connected group of type B_l, C_l, or D_l (with D_l restricted to l>=3), an irreducible carrier V_(N lambda), fixed l and lambda in a compact regular set K, and the whole orbit exp[(h/N)U_g T_H U_g^dagger]/Z_h. Its metric makes t_A an orthonormal real basis, Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=2 delta_AB; q=dim g, x_A are the coordinates of H, and r=|x|>0. The adjoint representation is real and irreducible for each simple compact Lie algebra. Haar adjoint orthogonality therefore gives
  integral x_A(g)x_B(g) dg = (r^2/q) delta_AB,
so the transfer's gamma is exactly gamma=r^2/q, with no extra trace-metric factor.

The uniform carrier bound B0=|x|B is valid in the supplied metric. From the scalar Casimir sum_A T_A^2=C_N I and C_N/N^2=|lambda|^2+beta(lambda)/N<=K_cap=B^2, for every unit vector psi,
  ||(T_H/N)psi||^2=||sum_A x_A(T_A/N)psi||^2
       <= |x|^2 sum_A ||(T_A/N)psi||^2
       = |x|^2 C_N/N^2 <= |x|^2 B^2.
Thus ||B_x||<=B0 for every carrier in the family, and conjugation preserves the bound. Since H is supplied nonzero, B0>0.

For N>=N_cut=max(N_Gram,N_dom), the constructed p=l families have
  lambda_min(G_N)>=kappa/(2q),
  Tr G_N<=(A+l kappa/2)/q,
  sum_s E_(Y_s)(F)<= (sum_s C_s) E_T(F)/N^2 for every F.
Hence the substitution is exactly
  g0=kappa/(2q), g1=(A+l kappa/2)/q, C0=sum_s C_s,
  gamma=|x|^2/q, B0=|x|B,
  rho=r sqrt(g1 C0)/(q g0).
These are the constants in source (6.1); each is independent of N and Q for the fixed type, K, H and h interval.

2. COMPLETE ADJOINT FRAME
-------------------------
The application does not invoke covariance alone as proof of completeness. The classical Cartan gradient matrix is nonsingular on the relevant regular cone: for B_l/C_l it uses l odd-power gradients; for D_l it uses l-1 odd-power gradients plus the SO(2l)-invariant Pfaffian gradient, including regular points with a zero Cartan coordinate. Its explicit determinant bounds give H>=kappa I. PBW symmetrization gives l Hermitian adjoint tensor families; the normalized quantum Gram satisfies ||G_N-H/q||_op<=J_0/(q sqrt N), hence G_N>=kappa I/(2q) for N>=N_Gram.

The representation-side adjoint multiplicity is exactly l for N>=N_dom. The Weyl character identity used in the source is applicable because every Lambda+mu, for mu a weight of the adjoint, is dominant once N delta_K>=M_ad; its Lambda coefficient is the adjoint zero-weight multiplicity l. Thus the l positive-Gram families are linearly independent l copies of the adjoint, and since End(V_Lambda) has exactly l adjoint copies they span the entire isotypic component. This closes the transfer's complete-frame hypothesis, including the repeated-degree D_l case. For N<N_cut the transfer upper frame is not asserted; the separate finite-carrier patch is therefore necessary and is not an implicit use of the large-N frame.

3. RANK-Q CHANNELS AND LOWER CONSTANT
------------------------------------
The inherited interface in regular_su_m/RESULT.txt §1 permits a quantum instrument with arbitrary classical outcome y, branch Hilbert dimension q_y<=Q, and branch decoder, while explicitly excluding entanglement assistance and a quantum side channel. Composing encoder Kraus E_(y,alpha) and decoder Kraus D_(y,beta) yields A_(y,beta,alpha)=D_(y,beta)E_(y,alpha), so rank A<=q_y<=Q. The composite family is TP, hence sum_i ||A_i||_HS^2=Tr I=d. Conversely, the usual polar support factorization realizes any such bounded-rank Kraus family with that branch memory and free classical labels. No unitality restriction is introduced. Continuous labels are harmless in finite dimension because the commutator-energy inequality is nonnegative and can be integrated over the instrument outcomes.

For fixed h, W_g=d tau_(h,g) has eigenvalues at most exp(2hB0), so M_0=exp(2h1B0) makes W_g/M_0 an effect. The irreducible twirl of W_g^2 is scalar; TP alone (not unitality) makes both square terms in the self-score commutator expansion equal. The divided difference of f(s)=exp(hs)/(Z_h/d) on [-B0,B0] is at least h exp(-2hB0), and its square supplies h0^2 exp(-4h1B0). Dividing by the witness cap M_0 contributes exp(-2h1B0). The half-trace risk lower bound is therefore
  e(Phi;h)>= [h0^2 exp(-6h1B0)/(2d)]
                    sum_i integral ||[B_g,A_i]||_HS^2 dg.
The extra 1/d is required because W=d tau in the self-score identity; it is present in ROOT_COVARIANT_GIBBS_TRANSFER.txt (83). Haar adjoint orthogonality gives the exact energy factor gamma E_T(A_i)/N^2. Applying rank-Q Lambda to each Kraus and summing the Hilbert-Schmidt weights yields
  e*_Q >= c_* Lambda(Q)/N^2,
  c_*=(h0^2 gamma/2)exp(-6h1B0),
which is exactly (6.1). The result holds at every fixed h in the interval and hence for the interval minimax; h0>0 and H nonzero are essential for a positive c_*.

4. FULL-GIBBS UPPER CONSTANT AND INTERFACE
------------------------------------------
For each Q, compactness gives a minimizing matrix for Lambda(Q). The scalar-Casimir SVD identity in the abstract transfer shows at least one of its positive moduli has the same rank/HS norm and no larger energy, so a positive minimizing filter F exists. This is an existence statement, not efficient acquisition. With S=Tr F^2, the orbit channel
  Phi_F(X)=(d/S) integral F_g X F_g dg
is CPTP by Schur twirling of F_g^2, and each branch Kraus has rank rank F<=Q. Its finite exact Choi-orbit quadrature uses positive weights and at most d^4+1 nodes by Caratheodory; this is legal because the application explicitly makes arbitrary classical labels free. It does not prove efficient computation of F or the quadrature.

The crucial upper hypothesis is the complete frame, used to obtain the linear bound ||D_F(B_x)||_1<=rho E_T(F)/N^2. The actual class-specific constants satisfy it as shown in §1. The shifted operator C_B=(B_x+B0 I)/(2B0) obeys 0<=C_B<=I. The ordered double-derivation estimate for C_B^k and exponential summation in the abstract theorem give
  (1/2)||Phi_F(tau_(h,e))-tau_(h,e)||_1
       <= (1/4) exp(2hB0)[h rho+gamma h^2]
                          E_T(F)/(S N^2).
Conjugation covariance makes this uniform in g; h<=h1 gives exactly
  e*_Q <= C_large Lambda(Q)/N^2,
  C_large=(1/4)exp(2h1B0)[h1 rho+gamma h1^2].
The use of the ordered noncommutative Gibbs series is necessary: the result controls the whole output density in half trace norm, not only an adjoint score or the first harmonic. No extra Q, q, d or trace-metric factor is missing when substituting (6.1).

5. FINITE-N PATCH AND ALL-Q EDGES
---------------------------------
For N<N_cut, compact K and the finite integer range leave finitely many carriers, so a finite d_max exists. The positive Casimir gap on traceless End(V) is at least sigma_G: every nontrivial dominant highest weight mu has Casimir ||mu||^2+2<mu,rho> >= ||mu||^2 >= sigma_G. Rank at most Q<d gives |Tr F|^2<=Q||F||_HS^2=Q, so the normalized F has traceless squared norm at least 1-Q/d>=1/d; consequently Lambda(Q)>=sigma_G/d. A one-dimensional reset has half-trace error at most 1. For every N<N_cut and Q<d,
  C_small Lambda(Q)/N^2
       >= [N_cut^2 d_max/sigma_G][sigma_G/d]/N^2 >=1,
using d<=d_max and N<N_cut. Thus C_small=N_cut^2 d_max/sigma_G does patch the upper. At Q=d, Lambda(d)=0 and the identity channel gives e*_d=0, so the zero endpoint is exact. The lower converse is valid for small N as it needs no adjoint-frame hypothesis; the source separately gives the adjoint Casimir identity and the uniform B0 bound.

6. REMAINING CONDITIONS / FALSIFIERS
------------------------------------
- If classical labels are charged, the resource conclusion changes; the finite quadrature can use up to d^4+1 labels. The theorem only optimizes the maximum quantum branch dimension Q.
- If a minimizing filter is not available as a supplied/computable object, the upper remains existential. The source explicitly retains this acquisition gap.
- If N lies below N_cut, do not claim the l-family Gram or adjoint-multiplicity proof; the scalar-reset patch is what carries those cases.
- If H=0 or h0=0, the self-score coefficient can vanish; strict positivity is not cosmetic.
- If a code receives extra quantum side information/entanglement, the rank-Q composite Kraus argument requires reproof. The actual type application excludes these resources.
- The result is for a fixed simple Lie type, fixed regular cone, fixed H and compact positive h interval. It does not itself cover weight walls, exceptional types, varying rank, iid Schur sectors, or a rank profile for Lambda.
