ADMITTED DIGITAL INFORMATION TRANSPORT: POWER AND PREPARATION PRECISION
c08_s01 / 7 October 2026 / independent continuation
Internal conditional consequence, not an acquired computation compiler.
INITIAL immutable; no peer reading or exposure. Historical novelty UNKNOWN.

The missing interface cannot be supplied by saying universal computation
or topological chaos. This note gives a precise sufficient digital-output
interface, derives its power floor, and charges a preparation resource that
the interface necessarily consumes. The coding assumption remains unbuilt.

ADMISSION

Use the smooth steady closed 3D strain-viscosity setting of INITIAL. Let
C be a measurable set invariant under every phi_t, with mu(C)=alpha>0,
and normalized invariant measure mu_C=mu|C/alpha. Fix one finite measurable
output partition P with K>=2 symbols and one physical clock period T>0,
both independent of input length n. For each n and uniform input word
W=(W_0,...,W_(n-1)) in {1,...,K}^n, an initial-state encoder supplies
probability measures mu_w^(n) supported in C, satisfying
  K^(-n) sum_w mu_w^(n)=mu_C.                           (C1)
The input-dependent initial registers retained for decoding consist only
of the fluid state x. The decoder sees the fixed trajectory observations
  Y_i=P(phi_(iT)x), i=0,...,n-1.
It has no retained copy of W or input-dependent outside controller.
Assume for EVERY n that the average symbol error satisfies
  n^(-1) sum_i Pr[Y_i!=W_i]<=p<1-1/K.                  (C2)
Readout/preparation errors are included in p. This is an explicit admitted
information-transport interface; it does not prove spatial noise tolerance,
universal computation or finite-bit acquisition. Different n may use
different encoder measures; consistency of a hypothetical infinite input
source is unnecessary. The fixed observation partition and exact marginal
condition are necessary to transfer to invariant-volume entropy.

Let h2(p)=-p log p-(1-p)log(1-p) and
  c_p=log K-h2(p)-p log(K-1)>0,
using natural logarithms. Then the total physical sustaining power obeys

  W>=4nu alpha V (c_p/T)^2.                            (C3)

Over n clock periods its integrated work per unit density is at least
  nT W>=4nu alpha V n c_p^2/T.
This is a rate-dependent fluid-work bound for the admitted output stream.
It is not a speed-independent minimum per general machine instruction.
Slowing the clock lowers the bound. Preparation, apparatus construction,
readout, force synthesis and any outside controller work are additional
resources not included in W.

COMPLETE PROOF

For each i, define E_i=1_{Y_i!=W_i} and p_i=Pr(E_i=1). The error indicator
is determined by W_i,Y_i. Conditional chain rule gives
  H(W_i|Y_i)=H(E_i|Y_i)+H(W_i|E_i,Y_i)
            <=h2(p_i)+p_i log(K-1).
Therefore, using concavity and monotonicity of this function up to
1-1/K, and independence/uniformity of the input symbols,
  H(Y_0,...,Y_(n-1))
    >=I(W;Y)>=n log K-sum_i H(W_i|Y_i)>=n c_p.
By (C1), the law of the output block is the law of the fixed partition
itinerary under mu_C. Its block entropy divided by n has a limit by
stationarity/subadditivity. Hence
  h_mu_C(phi_T,P)>=c_p, and h_mu_C(phi_T)>=c_p.
Ruelle for phi_T and its normal exponents T chi,-T chi gives
  c_p/T<=E_mu_C chi<=E_mu_C s.
The second inequality is the audited moving-normal estimate integrated
under the invariant mu_C; no smoothness of the indicator of C is needed.
Cauchy and the exact strain block identity yield
  E_mu_C |S|^2>=2(c_p/T)^2.
Finally total W=2nu integral_M |S|^2 is at least its nonnegative
dissipation integral over C, proving (C3). The restricted dissipation
integral is not misidentified with integral_C <F,X>: a spatial restriction
can have stress/pressure boundary flux. Only the global physical power
identity is used.

If encoders are supplied for only one finite n, or only n<=N, the proof
gives finite BLOCK entropy, not an asymptotic metric-entropy or power floor.
If the average law is an n-dependent/noninvariant thin coding measure,
the normalized-volume entropy transfer is not licensed. A coding set of
zero volume gives no positive alpha in (C3). External source injection
at every clock is a different nonautonomous/controller interface.

NECESSARY PREPARATION-CONCENTRATION COST

Suppose each finite-word initial preparation is absolutely continuous
relative to mu_C with density f_w<=C_n. Then

  log C_n>=n c_p.                                      (C4)

Proof. Because their average is mu_C, the classical mutual information
between W and x is
  I(W;x)=K^(-n) sum_w integral f_w log f_w dmu_C
          <=log C_n.
The observed itinerary is a function of x, so data processing and the
same conditional-entropy calculation give I(W;x)>=I(W;Y)>=n c_p.
This proves (C4). It is ordinary classical information accounting; the
flow does not create additional information about an initially supplied
input. The concentration budget log C_n is not silently a free oracle.

For uniform preparations on cells of mu_C-volume >=v_n, C_n<=1/v_n,
so v_n<=exp(-n c_p). With a fixed minimal preparation volume v>0,
the admitted interface can only handle n<=log(1/v)/c_p. This obstruction
holds for every deterministic flow, regardless of positive entropy or
sustaining power, and even before considering error accumulation.

If uniform physical balls of radius rho_n contained in C are used and
an acquired lower ball-volume bound Vol(B_rho)>=b rho^3 is supplied,
then v_n>=b rho_n^3/(alpha V). Consequently
  rho_n <=(alpha V/b)^(1/3) exp(-n c_p/3).              (C5)
The constant b and admissible radius range are geometric acquisition
inputs; no uniform b across arbitrary degenerating metrics is inferred.
Thus fixed spatial input precision cannot support arbitrarily long
words on this fixed bounded manifold, even if the output dynamics is
chaotic. One may grow registers, volume or precision, but must charge it.

An even simpler sharp whole-word statement is available. For a uniform
word among K^n possibilities and any input-independent decoder with
whole-word success >=1-epsilon, densities bounded by C_n imply
  C_n>=(1-epsilon)K^n.                                 (C6)
Let g_w(x) be the probability that the decoder outputs w, given x; then
sum_w g_w(x)<=1. Average success is at most
  K^(-n) sum_w integral f_w g_w dmu_C<=C_n/K^n.
This proof also allows decoder randomness independent of the input.
It does not allow a hidden retained input copy. With exact disjoint-cell
encoding (epsilon=0, density C_n=K^n) equality is attained. No flow is
needed to attain this preparation bound; it is a capacity accounting law.

PRIOR ART / SCOPE / COSTS

The information argument is established Fano/rate-distortion machinery,
reconstructed above rather than claimed new. Shannon1959 original
abstract and converse Theorem1 inspected at the primary-paper reprint:
https://www.mast.queensu.ca/~math474/shannon59.pdf
and original collected-paper record:
https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5311476
It gives the classical finite-alphabet lossy information-rate context.
No efficient code, theorem implementation, robust physical Markov
partition or encoder mu_w was built here. Ruelle and the sharp steady
strain identity are the previously admitted mathematical dependencies.

No new CPU experiment is used for this consequence: its proofs are the
finite-alphabet entropy inequalities and the exact kinematic calculation.
No preparation energy is deduced from concentration alone; a thermodynamic
or actuation model would be required. Output precision, partition geometry,
input acquisition, all initial-state concentration and outside registers
must be charged in any actual device. The missing positive-volume coding
mechanism remains OPEN. This result makes the admission and its unavoidable
precision debt explicit, rather than turning universal-computation labels
into a claimed power lower bound.
