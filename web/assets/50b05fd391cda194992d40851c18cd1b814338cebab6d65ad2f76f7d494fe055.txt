CYCLE 4 PRIMARY-SOURCE AUDIT: FREE RADIUS, PURITY, AND BELL QUOTIENT
Checked 2026-10-08

Scope and verdict

I checked the currently posted v1 primary source, Guocheng Zhen, Chengkai Zhu,
Ranyiliu Chen, Xin Wang, “Almost One Bit Violation of Minimum-Output Renyi
Entropy Additivity Simultaneously at All Orders,” arXiv:2609.18747v1 (submitted
2026-09-16), directly at https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18747v1. The primary
source specifies a real-orthogonal channel representation and Haar O(N)
finite model. Its exact Bell-fibre/collision result is not a theorem for the
cycle4 Haar U(N) model. The cycle4 CONSTANT_AUDIT.txt assertion that replacing
O(N) by U(N) leaves the Bell quotient unchanged is unsupported and is false as
an exact collision statement. This breaks the source-based Bell-entropy
transfer and leaves the combined positive-gap conclusion for the stated U(N)
model unproved unless a new Bell analysis is supplied.

The one-copy purity identity is different: the source explicitly proves it
for every finite unitary tuple. Its free regular-radius upper bound is also
correctly evaluated at r=2, but C^2=50 is an upper bound on the regular radius,
not an assertion that the exact radius equals 50. Thus the source formulas
support the one-copy algebra (with finite-U transfer supplied separately by
the cycle4 random-matrix argument); they do not support the two-copy quotient
for complex U(N) matrices.

Primary-source facts and exact parameters

The source’s current v1 gives G_{r,d}=product_{j=1}^r F_d^(j), K=2rd, and
inverse-closed branch sets in Section 2.1, Eqs. (1)-(4). It requires a real
orthogonal representation pi:G->O(m) for this channel construction; its random
finite model uses independent Haar O(N) matrices acting on separate tensor
legs, Eqs. (5) and surrounding text. See
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18747v1, Section 2.1, especially lines 118-142.

At r=2,d=64, K=2rd=256. Lemma 4.1, Eqs. (20)-(21), is stated for every
finite unitary tuple W, so it does apply to complex unitary branches:

  Gamma_K(W) = max_{||x||=1} ||G_x-I||_HS
              = K max_X ||Phi(X)-I/K||_HS,
  max_X Tr(Phi(X)^2) = 1/K + Gamma_K(W)^2/K^2.

Consequently a bound Gamma_K(W)<=C yields the global all-input purity bound
1/K+C^2/K^2 and the von Neumann lower bound
-ln(1/K+C^2/K^2). This is not restricted to a selected coherent input. The
source’s Proposition 5.1, Eq. (60), proves for the regular representation
Gamma_{r,d}<=C_r=sqrt(16r^2-7r). At r=2, C_r^2=64-14=50. The number 50 is
a proved uniform upper bound, not a claim of equality for the exact regular
radius. References: same primary source, Section 4.1, Lemma 4.1, lines 389-412;
Section 5.1, Proposition 5.1, lines 728-745.

The source’s exact combinatorial fibre count is Lemma 4.4. For k=K/r=128,
it gives one identity fibre of size K; (r-1)K^2/(2r)=16,384 mixed fibres of
size 2; and K^2/r-K=32,512 singleton same-factor fibres. These account for
256 + 2(16,384) + 32,512 = 65,536 = K^2 ordered branch pairs. The number of
relative-operation labels is M=1+16,384+32,512=48,897. These counts and the
mixed collision (s,t)~(t^-1,s^-1) follow from cross-factor commutation; see
Lemma 4.4, Eqs. (41)-(42), and its proof, lines 525-599.

For real orthogonal representations, Corollary 4.5 defines

  q = (1/256 once,
       2/65,536 repeated 16,384 times,
       1/65,536 repeated 32,512 times).

The entries sum to one. Its entropy is

  H(q) = 2 ln(256) - ln(256)/256 - (1/2)ln(2)
       = 10.722120449286654... nats.

Important precision: q is not in general the exact eigenvalue list of the
finite Bell output. The source factors the Bell output through the quotient
Gram matrix and proves q is majorized by that matrix’s eigenvalue vector,
therefore H_p(Z)<=H_p(q). The actual spectrum can depend on the representation
and its Gram matrix. Corollary 4.5 explicitly begins “For every real
orthogonal representation”; see Section 4.2, Eqs. (35)-(40), lines 499-524,
and Section 4.3, Corollary 4.5, Eqs. (43)-(46), lines 602-619. Thus the cycle4
fibre arithmetic and H(q) computation are correct as source-model arithmetic,
but RESULT.txt’s description of these multiplicities as Bell “eigenvalues” is
too strong even in the real case.

Why the Bell quotient does not transfer to cycle4 U(N)

For arbitrary complex unitaries, the maximally-entangled branch-pair vector is

  psi_(s,t) = (W_s^* tensor W_t^*)|Omega_m>
            = vec(W_s^* overline(W_t))/sqrt(m),

using (A tensor B)|Omega>=vec(AB^T)/sqrt(m). In the real orthogonal case,
overline(W_t)=W_t, so this is the vector associated with the single group
relative operation s^-1 t. This is the step used in the primary source’s
Eqs. (36)-(37), where equal relative operations give identical Bell vectors.

For a mixed-fibre pair (s,t) and its source-identified partner
(t^-1,s^-1), the corresponding complex matrices are

  F_(s,t) = W_s^* overline(W_t),
  F_(t^-1,s^-1) = W_t overline(W_s^*) = W_t W_s^T.

When s and t act on separate tensor legs, cross-leg commutation does not make
these equal: the first has W_s^* and overline(W_t), while the second has
W_s^T and W_t. Equality is enforced in the real model, not for generic complex
unitaries.

An explicit representation-level counterexample uses the same
F_64 x F_64 branch model and K=256, with N=2. Set one first-factor generator
to U=diag(1,i) on the first C^2 leg, one second-factor generator to V=I on
the second leg, and (for this counterexample only) map other generators to I.
This is a valid unitary representation. For s=g_(1,1), t=g_(2,1), the source
mixed-fibre partner is (t^-1,s^-1). The two vectorization matrices are

  F_(s,t) = U^* tensor I = diag(1,-i) tensor I,
  F_(t^-1,s^-1) = U^T tensor I = diag(1,i) tensor I.

They are distinct; their normalized vectorizations are orthogonal since
Tr((U tensor I)(U tensor I))/4=0. Thus the exact two-branch collision required
to aggregate this size-two fibre fails for a complex-unitary representation.
For independent Haar U(N) generators, the equality is not an algebraic
identity and fails generically; the U(N) ensemble is not the real-orthogonal
model of the source.

The same issue appears in the actual Bell-output entries. With row index (s,u)
and column index (t,v), direct evaluation gives

  Z[(s,u),(t,v)] = (1/K^2) tau_m((W_t^* W_s)(W_v^* W_u)^T),

where tau_m=Tr/m. If pi is the group representation, this is a mixed trace of
a pi-word and a transposed/conjugate pi-word, not generally tau_m(pi(w)) for
a single word w in F_64 x F_64. The real case turns the transpose into the
inverse/adjoint word and recovers the source relative-operation Gram matrix.
The cycle4 CONSTANT_AUDIT argument bounding ordinary reduced U(N)-word traces
of length at most four does not establish that these mixed traces approximate
the real free-quotient Bell matrix. Its finite trace tolerance and Bell entropy
transfer therefore target the wrong matrix for the stated complex model.

Claim-status consequences

- The exact K=256 fibre-count arithmetic and H(q)=10.72212... are verified for
the real source quotient.
- The source’s unitary-general exact purity identity is valid. The free bound
C^2=50 is correctly evaluated as a regular-representation upper bound. A
finite-U score transfer must stand on cycle4’s separate Bordenave-Collins
argument ([arXiv:2304.05714v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.05714v2)); the
source’s Haar-orthogonal probability statement alone is not a U(N) theorem.
- The cycle4 arithmetic claim qc4-free-gap-arithmetic remains a conditional
calculation: if a Bell entropy bound H_B<=H(q) applied, its arithmetic is
unchanged. The source does not establish that premise for cycle4’s U(N) model.
- The cycle4 claim qc4-bc-haar-tail’s one-copy score event may be audited on its
own U(N) assumptions, but its joint “score and Bell events” conclusion and
positive additivity-gap conclusion do not follow from the cited quotient.
- The finite Bell-trace gate in CONSTANT_AUDIT.txt must be replaced by a bound
for the mixed pi/overline-pi traces and an entropy estimate for their actual
Bell matrix, or the model must be changed back to real orthogonal matrices
with the corresponding O(N) finite-dimensional concentration/expectation
constants re-established. Until then the U(N) positive-gap existence claim is
not proved; this audit does not establish that the actual U(N) channel has no
gap.

Lead repair update (2026-10-08): the verdict above correctly rejects direct
transfer of the real-orthogonal quotient to Phi_N tensor Phi_N for complex
U(N). The actual finite witness has since been changed to
Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N). Its branch vectors vec(W_s W_t*)/sqrt(m) are indexed
by the same relative-operation fibres, and the complex PSD Gram diagonal q
implies entropy <=H(q) by Schur-Horn. This is derived in
PRODUCT_CONJUGATE_REPAIR.txt, with a standard flagged direct-sum conversion
to a same-channel tensor-square witness. Thus the old transfer is refuted,
while the corrected pairwise existence statement is conditional on the
source-reported free-radius premise.
