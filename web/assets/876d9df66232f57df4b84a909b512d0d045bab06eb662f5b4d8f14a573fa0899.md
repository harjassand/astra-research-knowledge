TOPOLOGICAL ENTROPY IS CONTROLLED BY A MAXIMAL STRAIN AVERAGE
Derived corollary of the invariant-measure estimate; no novelty claim.

Under the nowhere-zero smooth closed three-manifold interface in `invariant_code_power_lemma.md`, set

  s_1(x)=∫_0^1 s(φ_t x)dt,
  B(s_1)=max{∫s_1dη : η is φ_1-invariant probability}.

The normal-cocycle/Ruelle estimate there holds for every such η. The variational principle therefore gives

  h_top(φ_1)=sup_η h_η(φ_1) ≤ B(s_1) ≤ max_x s_1(x) ≤ max_x s(x).

This is a topological entropy bound by a *worst invariant average* of transverse strain. It does not give the factor-4 power theorem: the work theorem controls the volume-average quadratic quantity E_μ|Def X|², while B(s_1) can be selected by a singular invariant measure. In the disconnected Sol ⊔ flat example, the maximizing measure can concentrate on the Sol component while the global normalized-volume weight of that component tends to zero.

A useful sufficient condition for the topological-power conclusion is that μ itself maximizes the potential s_1 among all φ_1-invariant probabilities. Then

  h_top(φ_1) ≤ ∫s_1dμ=∫s dμ ≤ (∫s²dμ)^{1/2} ≤ ( (1/2)∫|Def X|²dμ )^{1/2},

so W≥4νV h_top(φ_1)². Another sufficient condition is that μ is a measure of maximal entropy, in which case h_top=h_μ and the original theorem applies. Neither condition is part of the baseline theorem.

Connected 3D Anosov comparator: De Simoi–Leguil–Vinhage–Yang, arXiv:2003.09345v3, Theorems A and D, state (i) volume is the MME exactly in the algebraic rigidity case for C^k, k≥5 conservative Anosov flows, and (ii) connected conservative 3D suspension flows can realize arbitrary c_top>c_μ>0 subject to their stated normalizations. The primary theorem was inspected but not independently re-proved. It establishes that volume=MMe is special and entropy gaps can be large; it does not compute W or refute a stronger topological-power inequality on connected flows.

Status: internal corollary from the invariant-measure estimate and the variational principle; external correctness/priority and the stronger connected coefficient-4 question remain open.
