STRONGEST RESULT

Bounded single-site biases and reversibility do not imply affordable acquisition,
even when the learner knows a two-model hypothesis class and receives every
update attempt and its exact time. An explicit empty-graph/complete-graph pair
requires exponentially long observation from an allowed initial state. This
prevents a broad transfer from exact acquisition, bounded local rates, or the
recent dynamical learning results to a general passive acquisition theorem.

There is also a positive, genuinely acquired representation: observed flip-count
covariances recover unknown disjoint parity hyperedges in polynomial observation
time and computation, without observing failed attempts or waiting for the full
configuration chain to mix. The count law has a two-state quotient which is
proved below; the unknown groups are learned rather than supplied. This is a
restricted family and ordinary covariance analysis is the competent method.

1. EXPONENTIAL OBSERVATION LOWER BOUND, WITH A TWO-HYPOTHESIS COMPARATOR

Interface. Let n>=2 be even, theta>0 fixed, and X(0)=(+1,...,+1). The unknown
potential is one of the two explicitly supplied descriptions
  psi_0(x)=theta sum_i x_i,
  psi_1(x)=theta |sum_i x_i|.
Each site has an independent unit-rate clock and at its rings is heat-bath
resampled for pi_b(x) proportional to exp(psi_b(x)). There are no interventions
or resets. The learner may observe the entire configuration path, all clocks,
all failed attempts, and exact times up to a fixed horizon T, and use arbitrary
computation, retention and randomness. It must recover the dependency graph
with error <=delta<1/2 under each hypothesis.

The graph for psi_0 is empty. The graph for psi_1 is complete: its conditional
field at i is theta sign(sum_{j!=i}x_j), and that odd sum can be changed from -1
to +1 by any other site with the remaining n-2 spins balanced. Under BOTH
models every actual conditional field has magnitude theta, so every resampling
probability lies in [1/(1+exp(2theta)), exp(2theta)/(1+exp(2theta))]. This is not
a zero-rate/frozen-state construction.

THEOREM. If both errors are <=delta, necessarily
  T >= (1-2delta) cosh(theta)^n / [n exp(2theta)].

Proof. Couple the clocks and update uniforms. At every configuration with
magnetization M=sum_i x_i>0, all flips lead to M-2x_i>=0; hence the two
potential increments and update laws are identical. The paths couple until
H=inf{t:M(t)=0}. Under psi_0, spins are independent at deterministic time t,
with mean m_t=tanh(theta)+(1-tanh(theta))exp(-t). Consequently
  E_0 exp(-theta M_t)
    =[cosh(theta)-m_t sinh(theta)]^n <= cosh(theta)^(-n),
  Pr_0(M_t<=2) <= exp(2theta) cosh(theta)^(-n).
Entry to M=0 comes from M=2 and has total instantaneous rate at most n.
The compensator/union bound therefore gives
  Pr_0(H<=T) <= n T exp(2theta) cosh(theta)^(-n).
This bounds total variation between the FULL observed path laws by coupling.
Any test with both errors <=delta requires TV>=1-2delta, proving the claim.

Costs and limitations. One time unit contains n attempted updates in expectation;
thus the lower bound is also exponential in total expected updates for a fixed
horizon. Processing or caching the full trace cannot create the missing event.
The equally informed likelihood-ratio comparator knows both concise potentials;
its information also first differs at the rare boundary. No computational
hardness assumption is used. This is a simple metastability/coupling application,
not a new general complexity lower bound. The order and graph degree grow with n,
and the Fourier l1 width of the majority conditional field is NOT uniformly
bounded. It does not contradict fixed-(k,d,alpha,lambda) dynamical learning.
Interventions that place the system at M=0 would remove this particular barrier.

2. ALL CLOSED FLIP-CYCLE RATE PRODUCTS CAN BE GRAPH-BLIND

Write x^i for flipping i and q_i(x) for the heat-bath flip intensity. If a
potential satisfies |partial_i psi(x)|=theta for every i,x, then
  q_i(x)=exp([psi(x^i)-psi(x)]/2)/(2 cosh(theta)).
For any closed flip path x_0,...,x_m=x_0, its product of rates is exactly
  product_t q_{i_t}(x_{t-1})=(2 cosh(theta))^(-m).
The potential increments telescope. Thus psi=theta sum_i x_i (empty graph)
and psi=theta product_i x_i (complete graph) have identical leading small-window
coefficients for EVERY closed flip word, not only a chosen eighth-order cycle.
The recent Ising cycle statistic is therefore not a general higher-order test.
Timed path laws also contain survival/holding factors, which this obstruction
does not erase; it does not refute every learner or every finite-window statistic.

The blind class is richer than disjoint parities. On five spins (a,b,c,s,e), let
  F=(ab+ac+abs-acs+eb-ec-ebs-ecs)/2,
  psi=theta F.
All five partial derivatives take only values +/-1 before multiplication by
theta. For example partial_s F=[a(b-c)-e(b+c)]/2 is +/-1 according to b=+/-c.
The other four derivatives are selector functions with values +/-1. The
dependency graph is connected, with eight edges (K5 minus {a,e} and {b,c}),
and degree 3 potential. This refutes the hope that cycle blindness can only
leave disjoint parity cliques or isolated Ising pairs.

3. POSITIVE FLIPS-ONLY ACQUISITION FOR UNKNOWN DISJOINT PARITY BLOCKS

Input promise. The potential is
  psi(x)=sum_a theta_a product_{i in U_a} x_i,
where the nonempty U_a are pairwise disjoint, |U_a|<=K (known), and the unknown
non-singleton blocks obey 0<tau_0<=|tanh(theta_a)|<=tau_1<1 (known rational
bounds). Zero-field leftover sites are allowed. Singleton fields can also be
included and estimated after structure recovery. There are no extra fields
inside larger blocks, overlapping interactions, supplied partition, stationary
initial state, or supplied equilibrium samples. Observe only initial spins and
actual flip times/labels from one continuous-time heat-bath path.

Exact identity. For a true block of size k put P=product_{i in U}X_i and
tau=tanh(theta). Each member has flip rate q(P)=(1-tau P)/2. Hence P itself is
a two-state chain with transition rate k(1-tau P)/2, stationary mean tau, and
relaxation exponent k. The observed flip labels within the block are uniform
marks. Its stationary per-site mean flip rate is r=(1-tau^2)/2. For i!=j in
the SAME block, let N_i(h) count flips in a window of length h. Then
  Cov(N_i(h),N_j(h))
    =r tau^2 [h/k-(1-exp(-kh))/k^2].                         (A)
Different blocks and independent leftover sites have zero covariance.

Proof of (A). A stationary flip has Palm pre-parity distribution proportional
to pi(P)q(P), which is uniform; its post-parity mean is therefore zero. At lag
u after a flip, the mean parity is tau(1-exp(-ku)), so the mean intensity of
site j is r+(tau^2/2)exp(-ku). Integrating both event orders gives
  E N_i N_j=r^2 h^2+r tau^2 integral_0^h (h-u)exp(-ku)du.
Subtract the product of means. This derivation uses actual flips, not hidden
attempt labels or a supplied polynomial basis.

An explicit conservative algorithm and guarantee. Set h=1/K and
  eta=(1-tau_1^2)tau_0^2/(12K^2).
For every true edge, (A)>=eta since exp(-kh)>=exp(-1)>1/3. Choose integer B>=3
so 2(B+1)^2/(B+1)! <= (eta/40)^2. Let
  M=ceil[2048 B^4 eta^(-2) log(8n^2/delta)],
  g=log(8nM/delta).
Collect M count windows of length h, separated by gaps g. Clip each count at
B; compute all empirical covariances; output i~j iff covariance>eta/2, and
recover U_a as graph components (isolated vertices handled separately).

Justification of the finite-sample guarantee. Conditional on any history, the
joint block-parity vector after gap g is within n exp(-g) in TV of its stationary
product law. Fresh Poisson increments make the count windows jointly within
delta/8 of independent stationary windows. The full spin configurations need
not mix, since count laws depend only on these fast parities. A site's count is
dominated by Poisson(h)<=Poisson(1). For Z~Poisson(1),
  E Z^2 1{Z>B} <= 2(B+1)^2/(B+1)!.
Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the clipping change of each covariance by <eta/8.
Hoeffding, for the clipped products and means, makes all empirical covariances
accurate to a further eta/16 at the displayed M, except probability <=delta/2.
This separates 0 from >=eta; the total failure probability is <delta.

Once groups are learned, sample their parity at sufficiently spaced times,
average to estimate tau_a, then theta_a=atanh(tau_a). To obtain coefficient
error epsilon, a further O([(1-tau_1^2)epsilon]^-2 log(n/delta)) samples suffice,
with the same mixing-gap accounting. No hypothesis enumeration is needed.

Charged costs. Physical observation time T_obs=M(g+h); expected actual records
<=n T_obs, with a Poisson high-probability bound. Processing O(n^2 M+n T_obs),
streaming memory O(n^2 log(MB^2)) bits for counters plus current spins and clock
bits. B=O(log(1/eta)) suffices. Thus M is polynomial in K, tau_0^-1,
(1-tau_1^2)^-1 and log(n/delta); even K=n remains polynomial. Timestamp rounding
at resolution Delta<=delta/(32nM) adds at most delta/8 failure from events near
window boundaries, so finite clock precision costs O(log(T_obs/Delta)) bits
per timestamp. Gap events are observed and charged even when discarded.

Comparator and interpretation. Ordinary count covariance is the proposed
learner itself; there is no special-architecture or empirical advantage claim.
Independent equilibrium samples of ONE hidden parity block encode standard
noisy parity (add the response bit as another spin), so a polynomial-time iid
graph learner would solve search-LPN at fixed noise. Dynamics is an additional
information interface, not a fair same-data performance comparison to iid
learning. The gain comes from physically observed transition timing.

4. A GENERAL STATIONARY REPAIR LEMMA, WITH THE REMAINING GAP VISIBLE

Suppose all conditional field magnitudes are fixed: |partial_i psi|=theta_i.
Let c(x_-ij)=partial_i partial_j psi. Write the conditional two-spin potential
as a xi+b xj+c xi xj. Constant field magnitudes imply ac=bc=0 pointwise. If
c!=0 then a=b=0 and theta_i=theta_j=|c|. Conditional flip-rate means are constant
r_i=(1-tanh(theta_i)^2)/2. Therefore, for a true edge in an amplitude-theta
component, with tau=tanh(theta),
  Cov_pi(q_i,q_j)=tau^2(1-tau^2) Pr_pi(c!=0)/4 >0,
and the covariance is zero for nonedges. Reversible marked-generator identities
give Cov(N_i(h),N_j(h))/h^2 -> Cov_pi(q_i,q_j) as h->0.
This repairs closed-cycle blindness under stationary access, including the
five-variable gadget. It does not prove affordable acquisition from arbitrary
initial states: the probability of active contexts and time to equilibrium can
be exponentially small/large, as section 1 demonstrates. A mixing-free repair
for general bounded-order MRFs with failed attempts unobserved remains missing.

5. SOURCE/PREVIOUS-CAPITAL COMPARISON AND READING STATUS

- Gaitonde–Moitra–Mossel, Bypassing the Noisy Parity Barrier (STOC 2025),
  https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05284v2 . Read definitions, main interfaces,
  technical mechanism and selected local-statistic proof passages. It learns
  bounded-(k,d,alpha,lambda) MRFs in O(n log n) observed updates/O(n^2 log n)
  time with parameter-dependent constants, observing failed attempts. Its
  population-square statistic uses actual independent refreshes; the hidden-
  attempt substitution has a genuine correlation/conditioning obligation.
- Gaitonde–Moitra–Mossel, Better Models and Algorithms for Learning Ising Models
  from Dynamics, https://arxiv.org/html/2507.15173v1 . Read weak observation
  interface, cycle mechanism, small-window formula, and parameter route. It
  removes failed-attempt observation for Ising/reversible single-site models;
  it treats isolated matching edges separately. These inspected sources do not
  supply general higher-order flips-only learning. Priority search was not a
  proof that no other paper exists.
- Shen–Wu–Majid–Moitra (2026), Gaussian trajectory acquisition:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31230v1 ; Tirukkonda–Dasarathy (July 2026),
  https://arxiv.org/html/2607.18559v1 . Read main promises/algorithm comparisons,
  not all proofs. These make local innovation/normalization informative without
  full-chain mixing; they do not provide a general nonlinear representation.
- Liu–Moitra, https://arxiv.org/html/2411.07536v1 : conditional low-rank learning
  already removes a fidelity restriction, but arbitrary-prefix conditional
  queries are acquisition services. No passive HMM transfer is justified here.
- Golowich–Moitra–Rohatgi (June 2026),
  https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11437v1 : improved sampling query costs given a
  bounded candidate class; inspected main theorem/limitations. Its explicit
  class enumeration is computationally inefficient. This does not acquire the
  class or prove computationally efficient general test-time learning.
- Astra N68, full proof d-61907dfde3cd00dc read: one fixed 2s-dimensional exact
  rational matrix query plus Prony recovers sparse nc words, with response bits
  and huge degree charged. Lakhani–Mukhopadhyay affine source:
  https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/120/revision/2/download . This source
  was identified but not reread in full here; the supplied full N68 derivation
  was read. Neither exact hardware oracle nor robust numeric acquisition follows.
- Astra N25 d-5ad21eeb8590ce66 noisy total-excitation barrier and spliced-teacher
  boundary, N13 passive-LPN boundary: cards read, full old proofs not replayed.
  They are distinct interfaces and do not establish the new path lower bound.

Failed transfer retained: direct replacement of known refresh samples by
endpoint-difference estimators does not automatically remove correlation of the
earlier i value with a later j update. Cycle squaring repairs that issue for
Ising but loses all graph information on the constant-magnitude class.

The next substantial question is whether timed local path observables can give
a nonnegative, mixing-free influence statistic for general bounded-order MRFs
when update attempts are hidden, with an explicit active-context lower bound.
The task is not solved by finding a better closed cycle or by assuming a
stationary law. Our lower bound states exactly which missing exploration
promise must be charged, while the parity-block learner closes one useful case.
