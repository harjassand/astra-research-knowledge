RESULT
The deeper attack gives an exact phase-retaining transfer for the subclass

  F = G + X Y^T,

where G is block diagonal on a supplied partition, every block has size
O(log n), and the update rank r is fixed. For each k, it computes

  c_k(F) = sum_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k} |det F[I,J]|^2

by a signed tag-pair tensor with at most T_r^2 * 4(k+1)^2 states, where

  T_r = sum_{p=0}^r binom(n,p)^2 binom(r,p) = O(n^(2r) 2^r).

Thus fixed r gives a polynomial-time exact coefficient algorithm, with a
large n^(4r) factor and polynomial bit growth. The same exact completion-mass
transfer gives a Las Vegas exact sampler in expected polynomial bit time
when c_k(F)>0. The general fixed-r sampler is derived but not implemented.

PROOF CONTENT AND AUDIT
For each k-minor, expand det(G+XY^T) by selecting p rows and p columns for
the update, then apply Cauchy–Binet over p update coordinates. Each tag
tau=(p,R,C,S) leaves a residual G minor, which factors over blocks exactly
when the row and column counts balance in every block. Squaring creates
ordered tag pairs. Each pair is contracted by enumerating at most 3^b
disjoint local row/column choices, with a four-state parity register for
cofactor signs and row/column charge truncated at k. Simultaneous coordinate
relabeling makes any supplied partition contiguous without changing c_k.
Exact rational bit growth is polynomial: if D is the product of input
denominators for G,X,Y, a tag term with update order p has denominator
dividing D^(k+p), and each cross term divides D^(2k+p+p') with p,p'<=r.

Finite exact checks passed: the rank-two tagged contraction matches direct
enumeration at k=0,1,2 for two 2x2 blocks; the minor expansion matches all 19
legal minors of that fixture; rank-one integer and Gaussian-integer moment
fixtures pass. These are bookkeeping diagnostics, not a proof by computation;
the universal claim is the derivation in `revisions/fixed_rank_block_tensor.txt`.

POSITIVITY / PHASE BOUNDARY
The tensor is signed/complex internally. A minimal-rank rank-two update
fixture has c_2=0, while the diagonal-only tag contraction contributes 2:
the p=0 and p=2 tag amplitudes are +1 and -1 and cancel completely. This
rules out dephasing the tag sum as a multiplicative proxy, not every positive
representation. The exact fixture is `tag_dephasing_check.py/json`.

The initial seam obstruction remains scoped to its specified global cut:
the independent two-replica orientation tensor has Schmidt rank 4^q, and
common-orientation sewing retains squared norm 2^-q. The earlier F4
even-windability lower bound also remains limited to positive
matching-circuit lifts. Neither is a lower bound on every tensor ordering,
local flow, or nonnegative sampler.
