REVISION 03 — FIXED FLOW AND VOLUME, UNBOUNDED CURVATURE AT EXACT EQUALITY

Question. Does exact entropy/work equality, even with the same compact
manifold, abstract flow, invariant volume, viscosity, entropy, and total work,
control scalar curvature? The following explicit sequence says no.

Construction. Fix one cocompact lattice Gamma<PSL(2,R), and fix a basis
H,e,f of sl2(R) with [H,e]=2e, [H,f]=-2f, [e,f]=H. For each integer n>=1,
define a left-invariant frame and flow by

  E0(n)=-H/(2n),  E+(n)=e,  E-(n)=-n f/2,
  a_n=1/n, beta_n=n^2, v_n=n, X_n=v_n E0(n)=-H/2.

Direct brackets give
  [E0,E+]=-E+/n=-a_n E+,
  [E0,E-]=-f/2=a_n E-,
  [E+,E-]=-nH/2=beta_n E0.
The vectors are orthonormal by definition of the metric g_n. Each is a
global left-invariant frame on the same group quotient; therefore it descends
under the same left lattice action. The flow generator X_n is exactly the
same Lie algebra element X=-H/2 for every n, so the underlying right-action
flow on Gamma\G is identical, not just orbit-equivalent.

The frame change has determinant
  det(E0(n),E+(n),E-(n) relative to (H,e,f))=1/4.
If mu0 is the fixed left Haar volume form with mu0(H,e,f)=1, then the
Riemannian volume form of every orthonormal frame is dvol_gn=4 mu0. Thus
the invariant probability measure after normalization is also fixed, and
Vol_gn(Gamma\G)=4 mu0(Gamma\G), independent of n. This finite positive
number need not be evaluated to compare the sequence.

For each n, the exact calculations in INITIAL apply with a_n v_n=1:
  entropy h_mu(phi_1)=1 (imported smooth-volume Pesin formula),
  |Def X|^2=2,  W_n/Vol=4 nu,  W_n=4 nu Vol,
  F_n=4 nu a_n^2 X=(4 nu/n^2)X.
The flow and invariant volume are literally fixed, and the entropy, viscosity,
power density, and total work are all fixed. Yet
  Scal(g_n)=-2/n^2 - n^4/2 -> -infinity.
This is a zero-deficit, exact compact non-Sol counterexample to any proposed
curvature bound that uses only the entropy/work equality data listed above.
It does not contradict rigidity of the projective dynamics or bounds that
also control the metric condition number, injectivity geometry, or higher
derivatives. The metrics become increasingly anisotropic and do not converge
in a topology controlling curvature. No claim about a general near-equality
theorem is made.

Derivation boundary. For each n, the one-time Lyapunov exponents are +1,0,-1
from the adjoint action, so the imported Pesin formula yields entropy 1. The
volume comparison is a determinant identity, not a numerical co-volume
estimate. Work is the exact steady residual work with F_n as in INITIAL;
there is no actuator or PDE trajectory. This result is geometric and does
not show computational cost for learning an unknown entropy.

Runnable exact check. `fixed_flow_curvature_blowup.py` uses Fraction inputs
and verifies the bracket identities, fixed generator, fixed frame-volume
determinant, equality data, and curvature at n=1,...,12. The symbolic
expressions in this text establish the all-n formulas and the limit; the
finite script is a reproducibility check, not the proof of the limit or of
Pesin's theorem.

Relation to released Sol baselines. The c08_s01 and c08_s02 INITIAL reports
give stronger near-equality warnings in a different direction: a connected
Sol bump-metric sequence approaches its background metric in C1 while its
curvature grows and its entropy-power deficit tends to zero; c08_s02 also
derives a projective strain-defect identity. This PSL(2,R) construction is
attributed as my independent exact, zero-deficit supplement. It does not
supersede their more quantitative near-equality example, and none of these
internal reports establishes novelty or external validation.
