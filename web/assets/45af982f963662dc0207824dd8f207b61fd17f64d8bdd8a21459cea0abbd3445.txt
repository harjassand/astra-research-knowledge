FULL DERIVATION OF THE STRAIN-PERSISTENCE CERTIFICATE

Step 1. The baseline moving normal cocycle is
  Q_t=P_(phi_t x) Dphi_t|N_x.
For an advected vector, its normal projection w solves
  nabla_X^N w=P nabla_w X.
This follows by decomposing the advected vector into w plus its flow-line
component: the normal moving-frame terms cancel because
nabla_X e=P nabla_e X. Q is a genuine cocycle since Dphi transports the
X-line to itself. Its normal symmetric generator is B, with tr B=-q.

Step 2. On any local strain eigenframe U+,U- with B0 U+=sU+ and
B0 U-=-sU-, let k=<nabla_X^N U+,U->. Differentiating this simple-eigenvalue
equation gives <D U+,U->=2sk. Hence R has matrix
  [[0,-k],[k,0]]
in this frame: explicitly [D,B0]/(4s^2) is exactly the eigenframe's angular
velocity, rather than the raw vorticity. Subtracting that frame rotation
from K gives the correct normal generator. With z=sqrt(r) times the
coordinates of w, X(log r)=q cancels the scalar -q/2 term, yielding

  d/dt [z1,z2]^T = [[s,-omega],[omega,-s]] [z1,z2]^T.         (2)

This includes both rotation of the normal plane and rotation of its strain
eigenlines. Merely measuring raw vorticity would omit R and is unsound.

Step 3. For z1!=0 put p=z2/z1. Direct differentiation yields
  p'=omega(1+p^2)-2sp.                                    (3)
For any constant 0<alpha<1, the cone |p|<=alpha is forward invariant if
  |omega|(1+alpha^2)<=2s alpha                             (4)
everywhere: at p=alpha the derivative is nonpositive, and at p=-alpha it
is nonnegative. Ordinary uniqueness/comparison for this scalar smooth ODE
gives nonescape. The cone can be defined globally by
||Pi- z||<=alpha ||Pi+ z||, so local eigenvector signs do not obstruct it.
Within the cone,
  d/dt log|z1|=s-omega p>=s-alpha |omega|.                 (5)
Starting on the positive eigenline at x gives, for every t>=0,
  log||Q_t(x)|| >= integral_0^t (s-alpha|omega|)(phi_tau x)d tau
                         +(1/2)log(r(x)/r(phi_t x)).       (6)
An initial cone boundary vector only adds a bounded norm factor. Since X
is nowhere zero on compact M, the endpoint term is uniformly bounded.

Step 4. Smooth volume preservation, the neutral flow line and the normal
determinant give the full Lyapunov spectrum chi,0,-chi. Oseledets applies
with bounded one-step derivatives and inverses. Apply discrete Birkhoff
to the bounded observable integral_0^1(s-alpha|omega|)(phi_tau x)d tau;
(6) then implies chi>=the time average of s-alpha|omega| almost everywhere.
For smooth invariant volume, the classical Pesin entropy formula for the
smooth time-one diffeomorphism gives H=E_mu chi. Therefore
  H>=E_mu(s-alpha|omega|).
The baseline estimate gives chi<=the time average of s and H<=E_mu s.

Step 5. If s>=s0>Omega>=|omega|, take
  alpha=Omega/(s0+sqrt(s0^2-Omega^2)) in (0,1).
Then Omega(1+alpha^2)=2s0 alpha and
s0-Omega alpha=sqrt(s0^2-Omega^2). Equations (A) and (B) follow. For
Omega=0, the positive strain eigenline is invariant for the normal quotient
and H=E_mu s directly. The universal upper power bound follows from
|S|^2=2s^2+(3/2)q^2+2|PSe|^2 and the energy identity. A constant generator
in (2) has exponents +/-sqrt(s^2-omega^2), so this uniform local-cocycle
constant cannot be improved under the given s0,Omega information.

EXACT HOMOGENEOUS SOL CLASS AND POWER-ONLY OBSTRUCTION

Use the baseline cat mapping torus and its global orthonormal frame
  [E0,E+]=-aE+, [E0,E-]=aE-, [E+,E-]=0.
For any constant (c,u,v)!=(0,0,0), Y=cE0+uE++vE- is smooth, nowhere-zero,
constant-speed and divergence-free. Advected components in this frame
solve vdot=Gv with
  G=-ad_Y=[[0,0,0],[-au,ac,0],[av,0,-ac]].                 (11)
This follows directly from [Y,advected vector]=0. The orthonormal frame
makes component norms the true Riemannian norms, so the full Lyapunov
exponents are precisely the real parts of G's eigenvalues: ac,0,-ac.
When c=0, G^2=0 and its derivative growth is only polynomial. Pesin
therefore gives H=a|c|, including zero for c=0.

S=(G+G^T)/2 has
  |S|^2=2a^2c^2+(a^2/2)(u^2+v^2).                         (12)
Pairing the stationary residual force with Y gives (C). At the normalized
flow direction e=(c,u,v)/sqrt(c^2+u^2+v^2), q=0. With P=I-ee^T, the
projected generator is Q=PGP. Its symmetric/antisymmetric parts B,Keff
have
  s^2=a^2 c^2+a^2u^2v^2/(c^2+u^2+v^2),
  omega^2=a^2u^2v^2/(c^2+u^2+v^2).                        (13)
Thus (A) is exact for constant homogeneous data whenever c!=0. At c=0
with uv!=0 the threshold s=|omega| has zero entropy despite strictly
positive s. At c=0,u=2,v=0, s=0 but |S|^2=2a^2, exactly matching the
optimizer c=1,u=v=0. The complete pointwise dissipation density and total
power are identical although their entropy rates differ by a.

For v=0 and c!=0, omega=0, s=a|c| and H=E s, but the b cross-strain
term is a^2u^2/4. This shows that saturation of the intermediate entropy
estimate H<=E s alone does not force the full equality-frame rigidity.
