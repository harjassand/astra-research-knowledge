On a nontrivial diagonal, the original predicate is already equality by the original synchronous premise, so this precedence alters no tested original constraint.

This is the parser equivalence claimed around source Equation27 and follows from the exact local prefix conditions below. The p=1,t=2 source counterexample has no padding ambiguity. Local recognition: a question has an active original view iff its prefix is view(v)=(bin(v),neigh(v),0,0,neigh(v),e_v), together with an old payload in L_v's range. On actual sampled questions, both players have active views iff the seed is nontrivial. To prove the difficult direction, Alice active implies s1=s4=neigh(v) and s5_v=1; Bob active implies s3=s5=neigh(u) and s4_u=1. Since the type graph is undirected these cross bits force (u,v)∈E, and all nontrivial conditions hold. The old payloads then come from the same old seed. A trivial pair therefore has at least one inactive side.

Take any supplied perfect globally synchronous oracularizable original strategy. On an active view use its original measurement; on every inactive view output the same fixed existing answer a0 (POVM effects I or0). On nontrivial pairs it is the original perfect strategy. On trivial off-diagonal pairs the repaired verifier accepts. On trivial diagonal pairs neither side can be active alone (the full questions are identical), so both use the same default answer. Thus the repair preserves perfect global synchrony and perfect oracularizability without a new answer, a new sampling stage, or a value algorithm.

D. ACQUIRED RESAMPLING GAP FOR THE ACTUAL DETYPED WIRES
This certificate uses the same intended symmetric prefix completion and valid register serialization stated above. It is independent of the acceptance predicate, so it applies to the published sampler as well as the repaired one.

Split the prefix seed into two independent private selector registers U_A=(s0,s1), U_B=(s2,s3), and the remaining shared register R=(s4,s5,old_seed). Alice's entire wire function depends only on U_A,R; Bob's depends only on U_B,R. On Alice's INVALID first prefix (the condition s0 labels a vertex and s1=neigh(vertex) fails), her second output and all body outputs vanish. Her entire wire then depends only on U_A. The same holds for Bob. Let q be the probability that a private first prefix is valid. Given s0, exactly one of the2^t neighbour strings s1 is valid, if any; hence
  q<=2^(−t).
The two sides have identical q and independent validity indicators. The ordinary/ordinary conditional wire law may be completely arbitrary, including every dependence introduced by R and the CL body. Invalid wires have disjoint recognizable first prefixes and are independent private salts. Thus the actual wire law is an independently erased arbitrary ordinary law, with ordinary probability q; no raw question alphabet or minimum atom has been enumerated.

Let a(X),b(Y) be centered local real functions. Let m_a,m_b and Var_a,Var_b be their means and variances on valid-prefix ordinary wires. On invalid wires they may vary with their private salts. Expanding the four branches and using global centering cancels all mean products:
  E[a b]=q^2 Cov_ordinary(a,b).
The total local variances satisfy
  ||a||^2>=q Var_a+(q/(1−q))m_a^2>=q Var_a,
  ||b||^2>=q Var_b+(q/(1−q))m_b^2>=q Var_b.
(Extra invalid-salt variances are nonnegative.) Hence
  |E[a b]|<=q ||a|| ||b||.
Thus the maximal local correlation is at most q. For centered pair f, set a=P_X f,b=P_Y f and S=||a||^2+||b||^2. Then
  ||a+b||^2<=(1+q)S,
  S=<f,a+b><=||f|| sqrt((1+q)S),
so
  <f,(P_X+P_Y)f/2><=(1+q)||f||^2/2.
Consequently
  sigma_D>= (1−q)/2 >=(1−2^(−t))/2 >=1/4.
Positive invalid-prefix probabilities also connect every supported local question to a common opposite invalid wire; the support is connected. For the exact t=2,p=1 fixture, q=1/4 and the proved bound is sigma>=3/8.

This is an ACQUIRED algebraic certificate from inspecting the named sampler, unlike a supplied gap lower bound. It needs no enumeration of the exponentially large old question support. It does not enforce any positive Hamiltonian constraint, produce constant predicate locality, or acquire a strategy.
