Exact dual form of the still-open Dirichlet gate

See RESULT.txt §H and CLAIMS.json record
UNRESTRICTED_C2_DUAL_SEPARATION_REFORMULATION. The universal c=2 lift is
equivalent to the marginal-constrained inequality

  2 Σ_i τ[A_i Φ(A_i)] − Tr(Q) <= h_1(Q),

for every PSD Q on traceless Hermitian L2(τ), where Q=Σ_i|A_i><A_i| and
h_1(Q) is optimized over a common pure-state 1-design with Σ_y p_yP_y=I/d.
The exact broadcaster Choi star expectation supplies the left 2Σ term.
A proof gives an EB moment operator Ψ⪰2Φ−I; a counterexample must give an
actual compatible Φ and a genuinely multi-observable Q violating the bound
after optimizing over all 1-designs. Rank-one witnesses collapse to ordinary
Kadison L2 contraction. The collision/common-POVM audit is complete: it transfers the critical
EB floor to q_n>=Omega(1/n) by Haar least-favourability, but this lower
bound vanishes and supplies neither b_2 nor broadcast energy. Its bounded-
posterior score hypotheses are family-specific and should not be identified
with h_1.

Do not replace Tr(Q)-h_1(Q) by the minimum variance of one pure state. The
former is the infimum of the design-AVERAGED variance over pure 1-designs;
the latter only lower-bounds that quantity. To prove the lift, the required
direction is 2E_Φ(Q)>=Tr(Q)-h_1(Q), not merely 2E_Φ(Q)>=m(Q).

The h_1 support function also has the dual operator-majorant form (H.2) in RESULT.txt. A useful proof must improve on B=Σ_i A_i²; for Q=I on H_0 this crude choice gives d²−1 while the optimum is d−1. The pure-1-design form equals Haar-prior accessible squared posterior score for a rank-one POVM. The critical collision proper-score lemma constrains posterior means in the opposite direction and currently does not transfer to this upper-lift gate.

Exact deficit: TrQ−h_1(Q) is the infimum, over pure-state 1-designs, of the average sum of variances Σ_i Varψ(A_i). It is not the minimum over one pure state. The latter m(Q) only gives TrQ−h_1(Q)≥m(Q); proving broadcaster energy ≥m(Q)/2 would be weaker and is not sufficient for the lift. The actual required inequality is twice the broadcaster energy ≥TrQ−h_1(Q).

A direct transfer of the N92 own-effects instrument comparator to the original
channel is refuted even on the universal cloner. For any POVM, the
superoperator-trace bound on its own-effects canonical map forces a
hypothetical c=2 comparator to have all rank-one effects, which would make the
non-EB cloner EB. The independent Haar comparator does attain c=2 but is not
an own-effects map of a Kraus representation. See RESULT.txt §I and
work/programmes/classicality/leaves/followup_covariant_instrument.txt. This
blocks one proof route only.
