RESULT AND ADMITTED SCOPE

For complex-rational F and a supplied site order, set
  r=max_(site cuts L|R) [rank_Q(i) F[L,R]+rank_Q(i) F[R,L]].
The hard determinant-parity coefficients, exact zeros, every forced-prefix
mass, and classical orientation Born samples are acquired in bit complexity
  poly(n,k,L) 16^r.
The construction is polynomial when r=O(log n), including dense semiseparable
matrices with complete support and arbitrary rational phases. All cross ranks
are computed by exact elimination; no low-rank factor, singular-value gap,
Schmidt basis, relative-norm oracle, or stable-polynomial coefficient oracle
is supplied. A full-refresh kernel has acquired rational weights, acceptance
one, gap one and an additive two-coordinate energy constant one. Its entire
contraction cost is paid. General r=Theta(n) is exponential.

A block extension covers any canonical colored system of lines
  l=(e_i,v_(i,c)), activity lambda_(i,c)>=0 rational,
including original lines (e_i,row_i F) AND every added coordinate pair
(e_i,e_j), represented as source i<j and vector e_j. Parallel copies are
permitted. Let m be the total number of colored lines, N=n+m the number of
fermionic modes in the construction, and r the maximum exact rank of its
cross block at a SITE cut. Exact norm contraction costs
  O((N+n)(k+1)16^r)
field operations, after polynomial(N,L) 4^r acquisition. The implemented
prefix sampler repeats at most N+n contractions, so costs
  O((N+n)^2(k+1)16^r)

These are acquired exact algorithms for admitted representation widths and

1. EXTERIOR-ALGEBRA GENERATOR

First consider one original row mode a_i and column mode b_i per site, in
site order (a_0,b_0,a_1,b_1,...). Define the 2n-by-2n skew matrix K by
  K_(a_i,b_j)=F_ij, K_(b_j,a_i)=-F_ij,
with same-kind blocks zero. This uses transpose, not conjugate, in the skew
amplitude matrix. Let theta_0,...,theta_(2n-1) be anticommuting variables and
  G(theta)=exp(q(theta)), q=1/2 theta^T K theta.
The coefficient of any ordered squarefree monomial theta_S is Pf K[S]. This
follows directly by expanding the exponential: degree2k contributes products
of k pair terms, with k! orders canceled by1/k!, and the Grassmann reorder
sign is the Pfaffian pairing sign. Therefore words containing only empty,
row, or column site states have coefficient equal, up to an explicit sign,
to det F[I,J]. Words with unequal total row and column numbers have zero
coefficient because every term of q contains one row and one column mode.
The hard local exclusion is simply restriction to those three site states.

For colored lines, site i has one row mode a_(i,c) for each line sourced at i,
and one column mode b_i. Replace F by the m-by-n row matrix of all v_(i,c),
and form the analogous skew K on N=n+m modes, ordered site by site. Restrict
to at most one occupied mode per site. Selected row modes identify a colored
line subset A with distinct source sites; selected column modes identify J
outside those sources. The amplitude is det(v_A[J]), up to ordering sign.
Multiplying a selected row color's squared amplitude by its lambda gives
  C_k=sum_(|A|=|J|=k, sources(A) disjoint J)
          prod_(l in A) lambda_l |det(v_A[J])|^2.
Cauchy--Binet shows that this is exactly the sum of weighted Gram determinants
of the selected canonical vector pairs. Two selected lines with the same
source would repeat e_i and have Gram determinant zero, so local exclusion
does not remove any nonzero parity basis. When 2k=n, J is the complement of
the source set and the point weight is the squared determinant of the full
selected paired columns. Thus the algorithm covers the actual original-plus-
coordinate full-basis system, not merely a different artificial polynomial.

2. THE ACQUIRED RESIDUAL SPACE AT A CUT

At a mode or site boundary p, split theta into the processed modes x and
remaining modes y. Factor, using commutation of even Grassmann elements,
  G=exp(q_processed(x)) exp(q_remaining(y)) exp(q_cross(x,y)).
Let B_p be a full-row-rank basis of the row space of K[processed,remaining],
obtained by exact reduced row echelon form over Q(i). Let its rank be r_p.
The cross exponential is a polynomial in x and the r_p linear forms
  l_a(y)=sum_j B_p[a,j] y_j.
Consequently, after extracting ANY prescribed processed occupation word,
its remaining generating function has the exact form
  P(l_1,...,l_(r_p)) exp(q_remaining(y)),
where P is in the exterior algebra on r_p formal generators. Its vector space
has dimension 2^(r_p). This is an exact residual-state representation, with
no approximation, positive decomposition, or Schmidt decomposition required.
It is important that the prefix's coefficient is extracted before this claim;
no equality of orientations between two independent probability replicas is
imposed. A local projector restricting processed occupation words preserves
this residual span.

For the original two-mode sites, at a site cut L|R, permuting the cross rows
and columns by spin reveals blocks F[L,R] and -F[R,L]^T, so
  r_p=rank F[L,R]+rank F[R,L].
At an intermediate cut inside one two-mode site, rank is at most the previous
site-cut rank plus1. The first implementation uses modewise transfers and
therefore sees at most r+1 modes of exact virtual rank. The block implementation
below avoids within-site rank growth even for many colored row modes.

3. EXPLICIT SINGLE-MODE TRANSFER, INCLUDING ALL SIGNS

Suppose the next remaining physical variable is theta and the others are z.
Write the old row basis as B=[beta | C]. Obtain a new RREF row basis B' of
K[processed plus current,remaining after current]. Its row space includes
all rows of C and the current future edge form ell=K[current,future]. Solve
  C=T B', ell=t B'
exactly. With an RREF basis the coordinates are just the entries on B's
pivot columns. The code independently reconstructs C and ell from T,t,B'
and checks equality before using the coordinates.

Write e for old virtual generators and e' for new ones. The substitution is
  e_a -> beta_a theta + sum_b T_ab e'_b.
For a homogeneous monomial e_(i1)...e_(id), its theta-free part P0 is the
wedge of the d corresponding T rows. Its theta coefficient P1 is
  sum_(h=1)^d (-1)^(h-1) beta_(ih)
      wedge_(j != h in original order) T_(ij).
The sign moves theta left past h-1 old factors. Extend these two operations
linearly. Let epsilon(P0) change sign on every odd-degree monomial. Since
  exp(q_remaining(theta,z))=(1+theta ell(z)) exp(q_future(z)),
we get the two acquired occupation transfers
  E0(P)=P0,
  E1(P)=P1+epsilon(P0) wedge t.                         (T)
The second sign moves theta left in P0 theta ell. Equations(T) are all that
is needed. Initial residual P=1 at the empty prefix; final residual has rank0
and its scalar coefficient is the desired amplitude. Nothing is divided by
an amplitude or a conditional norm.

Exterior multiplication in the code uses
  e_A wedge e_B=0 if A intersects B,
  otherwise (-1)^(#{a in A,b in B:a>b}) e_(A union B).
This canonical sign convention agrees with the Pfaffian occupation order.
Finite tests compare the resulting COMPLEX amplitudes, including phase,
against an independent recursive Pfaffian, not merely their absolute squares.

4. SITE-BLOCK TRANSFER FOR COLORED SOURCES

A site may contain h physical modes, but allowed occupation is empty or one
of these h modes. At the two surrounding SITE cuts, let B=[Beta | C], where
Beta contains the site's h columns. Compute C=T B' and the h future edge
forms ell_s=t_s B' in the next RREF basis. Terms involving two current-site
physical variables cannot contribute to the admitted empty/single occupancy,
so the internal site's quadratic generator can be omitted for these labels.
For each selected mode s, extract only that theta_s coefficient while setting
all other site variables to0. The same derivation gives
  A_empty(P)=P0,
  A_s(P)=P1_s+epsilon(P0) wedge t_s,
where P1_s is the beta_s contraction above. This is the complete explicit
site tensor. It uses dimensions 2^(r_before) and 2^(r_after), regardless of
how many colored modes the site contains. The cost grows polynomially with h;
it does not silently pay2^h before exploiting local exclusion.

For original two-mode sites, the allowed tensors are empty, row, column.
For a colored site the row tensors carry lambda_(i,c) in the NORM transfer;
empty and column tensors carry weight1. The amplitude tensors themselves
are rational and independent of activities; no rational square-root field
extension is hidden. Forbidding a site means retain only the empty tensor.
Forcing a row color or column occupation means retain its tensor alone.
Canonical total selected-line count is tracked by incrementing q on every
row tensor. Since every nonzero amplitude has equal total row/column count,
a final q=k suffices to isolate the desired k,k sector.

5. EXACT NORM, CONDITIONAL MASSES, AND THE CONCRETE KERNEL

If A_i^s are the acquired site tensor matrices, propagate the doubled state
  H_(i+1,q')=sum_(s,q+1_row(s)=q')
                weight_i(s) A_i^s H_(i,q) (A_i^s)^*,
starting with the1-by-1 matrix1. End with the scalar at q=k. Expanding this
recursion multiplies an amplitude history with its conjugate for the SAME
physical word and sums all admitted words, so the output is exactly C_k.
Allowed-label restrictions acquire every prefix norm by the identical
program. The final result is a nonnegative rational. Internal components
may be complex; no component is treated as a probability. There is no common
occupied-core-cut projection between two DIFFERENT physical words.

At a site compute the exact norm for every allowed label. Their sum is the
parent norm, and all are nonnegative. Clear their finitely many denominators
and draw from the resulting nonnegative integer categorical weights. Repeat
through n sites. This yields a colored line subset and J from the exact target.
A positive initialization is acquired by choosing the first positive label
at each step. Zero sectors are decided by the exact norm. No external
support/witness import is needed on this admitted class.

The full-refresh kernel P(x,y)=W(y)/C_k is thus computable from polynomial-
height rational data with all exponential rank cost explicit. Detailed balance
is immediate, acceptance is1, and one ideal refresh has precisely the target
law. P is the constant projection in L2(mu); its gap is1. Full two-coordinate
refresh has E(f1(x)+f2(y))=Var(f1)+Var(f2), so additive comparison constant1.
The lazy versions pay a factor2. These assertions do NOT say that a cheap
local determinant reassignment has polynomial gap. They hold because the
entire exact norm/prefix contraction is actually acquired.

The implemented uncapped categorical sampler is expected-time exact under
ideal fair bits. The implemented cap R in rank_frontier_sampler.py and
color_rank_sampler.py takes at most R trials at each site and uses a positive
fallback if rejection does not terminate. Coupling gives TV<=n2^(-R) per
sample. Each random integer has polynomially many bits, so the every-run
cost is bounded by the stated arithmetic budget plus O(n R poly(N,L)) bits.
The capped kernel is not assigned an exact stationary distribution or exact
spectral gap. A caller making H refreshes can allocate per-refresh TV eta/H.
No hardware fair-randomness or physical quantum preparation is certified.

6. BIT COMPLEXITY AND ACQUISITION

Exact row bases at the n+1 cuts are obtained from explicit rational K; a
straight implementation uses O(n N^3) field operations. Standard minor/
Gaussian-elimination bounds give polynomial(N,L) height for their RREF
entries. T,Beta,t are chosen from these entries and K, so have the same
polynomial-height property. The code reconstructs all coordinate relations.

Choose a common Gaussian-rational denominator E_i for the polynomially many
primitive entries T,Beta,t at a site. Its bit length is polynomial(N,L).
Every exterior matrix coefficient is a sum of products of at most r+1 such
entries, so all site tensor coefficients share denominator E_i^(r+1), with
polynomial-height numerators. The number of summands only increases bit
length by O(r log(r+1)). This shared primitive denominator is essential:
one must not incorrectly take a product over exponentially many individually
simplified tensor entries and infer an exponential bit-height debt.

Across n sites, common tensor denominators multiply. Their logarithms add,
and activity denominators add likewise; conjugate norm propagation doubles
these heights. The common denominator remains polynomial in N,L. A norm
operand is a sum of at most (N+2)^n 2^(2nr) virtual histories, each a product
of n polynomial-height tensor entries and activity factors. Its numerator
bit length is O(n(log(N+2)+r+poly(N,L))), again polynomial. Fractions may
cancel, but exact arithmetic needs no singular-value promise or relative
floating tolerance. The final rational C_k can be tiny in numerical value
without making its binary encoding exponentially long in input length.

D<=2^r is the site virtual dimension. Exterior tensor acquisition costs at
most polynomial(N,r)4^r field operations. A doubled message has at most
(k+1)D^2 entries. The dictionary implementation sums at most D^2 next virtual
pairs for each old pair and each physical label, giving the conservative
O((N+n)(k+1)16^r) contraction bound. This is not a claimed optimized cubic
matrix-multiplication implementation. At most N+n contractions during a
complete self-reduced sample gives the earlier squared-linear prefactor.
Stored tensors cost O((N+n)4^r), plus O((k+1)4^r) message entries and acquired
row bases/input. Physical energy and backend computation energy remain UNKNOWN.

7. ANNEALING AND APPROXIMATE BASE REFRESH

Activities enter only as rational positive label weights. If each selected
line changes activity by at most c=1+1/n between adjacent laws, a k-line
configuration changes by [c^(-k),c^k] and normalized densities by
[c^(-2k),c^(2k)] subset[e^(-1),e] for k<=n/2. If final and initial positive
activities lie in [2^(-B),2^B], O(n(B+1)) rational tiers suffice. Every tier
has its own acquired exact contraction/refresh, costing that many copies of
the stated rank-dependent budget; exact norm ratios are outputs. Since
activities do not change the unweighted cross row spans, rank is not reduced
by merely suppressing a positive activity. Zero activity is direct deletion,
not a falsely common-support positive annealing transition.

For the general original-plus-COMPLETE-coordinate system, the baseline base
refresh can also be concretely represented in this colored alphabet. Set all
coordinate activities1 and original activities epsilon. The coordinate-only
law is uniform perfect matchings; sample a uniform permutation and pair
adjacent vertices, then orient each coordinate line from its smaller source.
Original vector entries bounded by R>=1 give squared determinant at most
n^n R^(2n). With m total lines, all full-basis configurations containing an
original line have total weight at most epsilon 2^m n^n R^(2n). Coordinate
partition (n-1)!!>=1, so epsilon<=eta/[2^m n^n R^(2n)] acquires a TV-eta
approximate base in polynomial bits. This is a BASELINE DUPLICATE, retained

9. EXACT AND APPROXIMATE COMPRESSION BOUNDARIES

An exact MPS cannot have dimension-free bond width for every hard projected
state in a fixed order. Let n=2q, order all q left sites before all q right
sites, and F=I+epsilon P, where P swaps each left/right paired site and has
no other entries. In the full k=q hard sector, each pair contributes exactly
one of row-left/column-right or column-left/row-right, of amplitude epsilon.
The normalized projected state has2^q equal-modulus coefficients on a
one-to-one matched set of left/right occupation words, so its Schmidt
spectrum is flat. Therefore C_q=(2 epsilon^2)^q and Schmidt rank is2^q
across that cut. An MPS of bond dimension D has squared overlap at mostD/2^q with
this flat Schmidt vector. A pure-state trace-distance epsilon_out approximation
therefore requires D>=(1-epsilon_out^2)2^q.

All cross singular values of the underlying F blocks are epsilon, which can
be2^(-L). Thresholding them away produces F=I, whose full hard norm is zero,
although the nonzero conditioned state has exponential Schmidt rank. A
numerically tiny Gaussian tail is insufficient for uniform relative retained-
norm approximation. This is the previously earned rare-conditioning mechanism
in the precise rank-compression interface, not new hardness.

Even avoiding exact zero does not fix that error argument: on four sites,
FA=I+epsilon(E_01+E_23), FB=I+epsilon(E_02+E_13). Both have full hard norm
epsilon^4 and approach I as epsilon->0. The first retains only the basis
word I={0,2},J={1,3}, the second only I={0,1},J={2,3}. Their normalized
retained laws and pure states are orthogonal (TV/trace distance1).

The singlet family is easy to count/sample classically by independent pairs;
interleaving each pair also makes its exact cut rank at most2. These facts
prevent a false general counting-hardness interpretation. The obstruction
is to dimension-free FIXED-ORDER coherent/exterior message compression and
to using additive Gaussian truncation without a retained-norm budget. It
does not refute other acquired classical kernels, a better supplied order,
non-MPS representations, or the open general parity FPRAS.
