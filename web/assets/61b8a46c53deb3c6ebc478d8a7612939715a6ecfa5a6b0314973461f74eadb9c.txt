STRONGEST PRECISE CLAIM

Fix local irreducible spin j=p/2>0, local D=p+1, beta>0 and field bound
B>=0. There is an explicitly given epsilon_exact(j,beta,B)>0 such that
for EVERY N>=1, every known real symmetric ||A||<=1, every |b|<=B and
every 0<=epsilon<=epsilon_exact,

 rho_N proportional to exp[beta J^2/N+epsilon Q_A/N+b.J]

is EXACTLY FULLY SEPARABLE across the N local spin-j sites.              (E1)

The radius is very small and may have exponential dependence on the
fixed parameters. It is nevertheless positive and independent of N.
Thus the family includes a fixed open neighborhood of isotropic
ferromagnetic coupling, including indefinite anisotropic tensors.

Exact full separability is weaker than exact iid-product-mixture
representability. The decomposition below uses arbitrary products.
No exact iid representation, efficient decomposition, unknown-parameter
decoder or quantum-reference-preserving map is asserted.

1. THE ELEMENTARY FULLY-SEPARABLE BALL, INDEPENDENTLY RECONSTRUCTED

Choose a Hermitian Hilbert-Schmidt orthonormal basis on C^D with
T0=I/sqrt(D) and traceless T_a, a>=1. HS norm one implies ||T_a||<=1.
For a tensor word s with r>=1 nonidentity positions, write P_s for the
tensor product of its nonidentity T_a and I at unused positions.
The trace-one matrices

 I/D^N +/- P_s/D^N

are fully separable: average the product of local (I+z_i T_ai)/D over
uniform sign vectors with product_i z_i=+1 or -1. All nonempty proper
sign moments vanish. Use I/D at unused positions. This remains valid
for r=1, where the single sign is fixed. Each local factor is positive.

Expand a trace-zero Hermitian X in the tensor HS basis with real
coefficients x_s (excluding the all-identity word). Since
tensor_i T_si=D^[-(N-r_s)/2]P_s, put
a_s=D^[(N+r_s)/2]x_s. Then

 X=D^(-N)sum_s a_s P_s,
 sum_s |a_s|<=D^N sum_s|x_s|
             <=D^(2N)||X||_2<=D^(2N)||X||_1.

The number of words is at most D^(2N), justifying the Cauchy-Schwarz
factor D^N. Therefore, whenever ||X||_1<=D^(-2N),

 I/D^N+X

is a convex combination of the fully separable signed-word matrices
and I/D^N. This proves the full-separability ball without an imported
ball constant. It is deliberately loose but suffices here.

2. AMPLIFICATION OF A POSITIVE MIXTURE WITH A LOCAL FLOOR

Suppose a normalized positive mixture sigma of iid products tau^tensorN
has tau>=eta I for every branch, where eta>0. Expanding

 tau^tensorN=[eta I+(tau-eta I)]^tensorN

leaves the common white component eta^N I and a positive sum of product
operators. Consequently

 sigma=w I/D^N+(1-w)omega_sep, w=(D eta)^N,

where omega_sep is fully separable. If another density rho satisfies

 ||rho-sigma||_1 <= (eta/D)^N,                             (E2)

then ||(rho-sigma)/w||_1<=D^(-2N), with trace zero. The ball theorem
therefore makes I/D^N+(rho-sigma)/w fully separable, and

 rho=(1-w)omega_sep+w[I/D^N+(rho-sigma)/w]

is exactly fully separable. The remainder is not necessarily an iid
mixture. This exactness amplification was proposed by root in
work/cycle6/control/ROOT_RESEARCH.txt section 7; this author independently
checked its tensor coefficients, norm factors and higher-spin transfer.

3. THE HIGHER-SPIN LOCAL EIGENVALUE FLOOR

Use the exact normalized thermal kernel from the companion proof,
R_j(m)=exp(r u.S)/Z_j(r), m=tanh(r/2)u. On |m|<=r0=1-g,

 r<=log[(2-g)/g],
 lambda_min R_j=e^(-jr)/Z_j(r)
              >=D^(-1)e^(-2jr)
              >=eta:=D^(-1)[g/(2-g)]^p.                  (E3)

The middle bound uses Z_j(r)<=D exp(jr). This is a genuine local
physical eigenvalue floor, not an eigenvalue of a virtual projection.
Every endpoint in the stopped mixture has this floor, including
frozen boundary paths and replaced bad branches m=0. Positive
Feynman-Kac reweighting and normalization do not change the common floor.

All constants are positive and known functions of fixed j,beta,B. The

The last constraint gives the stopping exponent

No N-dependent radius is used for this all-N theorem.

5. CHECKING THE EXACTNESS THRESHOLD, INCLUDING PREFACTORS

The already independently reviewed stopped Duhamel proof gives the
normalized half-trace error

 T(rho,sigma)<=2Cbeta exp[h_N-kappa0 N]
                  +6h_N exp[2h_N-kappa1 N].

For epsilon>0 and every N>=1, equations (E5),(E6) imply

 2T <=4Cbeta exp[-(W+S0)N]
           +12Hbar N exp[-(W+1+log(24Hbar))N]
      <=(1/2)exp(-WN)+(1/2)exp(-WN)
      =exp(-WN).                                        (E7)

For the first term, 4Cbeta exp(-S0 N)<=1/2 by the definition of S0 and
N>=1. For the second, N exp(-N)<=1 and
12Hbar exp[-N log(24Hbar)]<=1/2, because Hbar>=1.

Finally, exp[a+B]>=1 gives

 log[(2-g)/g]=log[2exp(a+B)+1]<=a+B+log3.

Thus W>=2logD+p log[(2-g)/g], and

 exp(-WN) <=(eta/D)^N.                                  (E8)

Combine (E7),(E8) with the full trace norm identity ||rho-sigma||_1=2T
and the ball amplification (E2). This proves exact full separability
for every N>=1. For epsilon=0 the isotropic heat mixture and uniform
local field filtering are already exactly iid and fully separable, so
no division by epsilon is taken.

6. SUBLEADING ANISOTROPY CONSEQUENCE

For the broader known-parameter family

 rho_N proportional to exp[beta J^2/N+Q_(A_N)/N^(1+delta)+b_N.J],

with fixed j,beta,delta>0, ||A_N||<=M, |b_N|<=B, the actual Gibbs state
is exactly fully separable whenever M N^(-delta)<=epsilon_exact.
For M>0, an explicit sufficient threshold is

 N>=max{1,(M/epsilon_exact)^(1/delta)}.                    (E9)

This implication can use the fixed neighborhood directly. Alternatively
the moving-cutoff exp[-cN(logN)^2] approximation has eta_N bounded below
by a fixed constant times N^(-p delta/8). Its error dominates the
white-component attenuation exp[-O(NlogN)], yielding eventual exact
full separability independently of optimizing an all-N neighborhood.
The approximate iid-mixture theorem remains useful because exactness
here is only for arbitrary fully separable products.
