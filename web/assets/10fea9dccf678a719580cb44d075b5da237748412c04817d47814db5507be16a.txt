ORIGIN AND EXACT SCOPE

This is cross-exposed work, not an independent INITIAL claim. c03_l10
originated the random-site-sign Pfaffian estimator and formal square-root
evaluation. c03_l06 derived the positive forced-row projection identity and
the row-marginal / conditional-column sampler. c03_s02 sharpened the BCS
pointwise moment bound from 9^k to binomial(2k,k), derived an alternative
physical-prefix counter, and implemented that alternative. The present
work independently implements the l06 route with the s02 bound, reconstructs
the necessary algebra, and supplies a Hellinger refinement of its classical
sampling cost. No retroactive independent-origin or causal model claim is
made. The shared workspace/model roster is not a proof certificate.

Input: explicit F in Q(i)^(n x n), total binary encoding length L, and
0<=k<=floor(n/2). The hard norm and occupation law are

 c_k=sum_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k}|det F[I,J]|^2,
 pi(I,J)=|det F[I,J]|^2/c_k.

The count admits zero inputs. The normalized sampler has the promise c_k>0;
no deterministic general support optimizer was implemented here. A zero
randomized estimate is not an exact zero certificate. Exact base cases and
bounded exhaustive sign cubes can certify zeros on the instances they cover.
All entries, cancellations, inversions, traces, probabilities, and branch
thresholds are computed exactly. No inverse sector/projection probability
appears in the operation count. Soft onsite filters are outside this result.

STRONGEST THEOREM

Put C_k=binomial(2k,k). There is an acquired relative estimator for c_k and
every forced/excluded ROW prefix using

 O(C_k eta^-2 log(1/delta)) sign evaluations,
 O((k+1)n^3) exact arithmetic operations per sign,

and polynomial binary operand heights. Under c_k>0 there is a capped,
finite-random-bit sampler for the full joint hard-BCS occupation law, with
TV error <=epsilon, using

 O(C_k n^2 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon)) sign evaluations,
 O(C_k (k+1)n^5 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon)) arithmetic operations,

plus polynomial exact prefix acquisition and conditional-column work.
The count is relative and the sampler is additive TV; they are not equated.
For any fixed public A, k<=A log_2(n+1) gives polynomial bit time. This is
still exponential at k=Theta(n). No general parity FPRAS or AP-hardness
result is proved. Novelty and external specialist audit are UNKNOWN.

1. ELEMENTARY ESTIMATOR AND TARGET IDENTITY

Let u_i=e_i, v_i=row_i(F)^T, and let V have interleaved columns (u_i,v_i).
For independent signs s_i in {-1,1}, form
 A_s=sum_i s_i(u_i v_i^T-v_i u_i^T)=diag(s)F-F^Tdiag(s).
Ordinary transpose is used in A_s. Let omega_s=sum_i s_i u_i wedge v_i.
Degree-two forms commute; each decomposable form squares to zero. Thus

 omega_s^k/k! = sum_{|I|=k} chi_I(s) wedge_{i in I}(u_i wedge v_i).

For a coordinate set R of size 2k, its coefficient is

 p_R(s)=Pf(A_s[R])=sum_{|I|=k} chi_I(s) det V[R,P(I)].

The identity columns force I subset R. Expanding them leaves the minor
F[I,R minus I], up to a harmless sign fixed by the paired column order.
Boolean character orthogonality therefore gives

 E X_k(A_s)=c_k,
 X_k(A)=sum_{|R|=2k}|Pf(A[R])|^2.

This is a sum of squared amplitudes, not a diagonal sum over graph matchings.
For every R at most C_k terms occur. Complex Cauchy--Schwarz gives
 |p_R(s)|^2<=C_k sum_I |det V[R,P(I)]|^2. Summing yields the pointwise bound

 0<=X_k(A_s)<=C_k c_k,
 Var(X_k)<= (C_k-1)c_k^2.                         (1)

This is the s02 sharpening, reconstructed here. It uses the identity-column
BCS interface; binomial(2r,r) must not be silently applied to arbitrary
projected pair vectors. Hypercontractivity is unnecessary for (1).

2. EXACT PER-SIGN EVALUATION WITHOUT MINOR ENUMERATION OR YOULA INPUT

For a complex skew A of order m, consider the skew block matrix
 K(t)=[[tA,I],[-I,-conjugate(A)]]. Its Pfaffian has the identity

 (-1)^binomial(m,2) Pf K(t) = sum_r X_r(A)t^r.     (2)

Here is explicit sign accounting. In exterior variables x_1,...,x_m,
y_1,...,y_m, expand the exponential of the corresponding two-form. Choosing
upper and lower skew terms on R, |R|=2r, contributes t^r Pf(A[R]) and
(-1)^r conjugate(Pf(A[R])). Cross identity terms must occupy the complement
of R in both halves; otherwise a variable is missing or repeated. Their
coefficient in block order is (-1)^binomial(m-2r,2). Moving the two equal-size
R blocks past the complementary blocks adds no sign: 2r(m-2r) is even and
the two subset-ordering signs multiply to one. The remaining relative sign
is positive because

 r+binomial(m-2r,2)-binomial(m,2)=2r(r-m+1)

is even. This proves (2) for odd/even m, singular A, and complex entries.

Pf K squared equals det K by the ordinary Pfaffian identity, while swapping
block columns and taking the identity Schur complement gives
 det K=det(I-t conjugate(A) A)=det(I+t A* A), since A*=-conjugate(A).
Thus Q(t)=sum_r X_r t^r is the unique formal square root of this determinant
with Q(0)=1. No numerical normal form, singular value, or square root is used.

For B=A* A, compute T_j=Tr(B^j), j<=r, and use Newton identities
 d_0=q_0=1,
 j d_j=sum_{h=1}^j(-1)^(h-1)d_(j-h)T_h,
 2 q_j=d_j-sum_{h=1}^{j-1}q_h q_(j-h).
Return q_r. Naive matrix products take O(r m^3) arithmetic. If A has Gaussian
integer entries, all displayed divisions are integral and q_j>=0. The
production routine uses this polynomial-time path. Principal Pfaffians and
minor enumeration occur only in small independent diagnostic comparators.
The block identity is classical Pfaffian machinery, not claimed new.

3. POSITIVE ROW-PREFIX ACQUISITION, AND WHY THE SHARP BOUND SURVIVES

Let A be forced row sites, B excluded row sites, a=|A|, E=[n] minus(A union B),
and r=k-a. Infeasible cardinality or dependent forced pair columns gives zero.
Otherwise C=V_A, G=C* C, d=det G>0, and
 P=I-C G^-1 C* is the exact orthogonal projector off span(C).
For every T subset E of size r, Schur complement gives

 det(V_(A union T)* V_(A union T))
   =d det((P V_T)* (P V_T)).

Hence the desired row-prefix mass is W_k(A,B)=d Z_r(P V_E), and a sample is
Y_s=d X_r(sum_(i in E)s_i(Pu_i (Pv_i)^T-Pv_i(Pu_i)^T)). This is nonnegative
and unbiased. Gram determinants and projectors are acquired by exact
Gaussian elimination; an orthogonal basis/SVD oracle is not supplied.

The stronger pointwise bound must be proved using the original exterior
coordinates, not by assuming the projected identity columns stay sparse.
Let w_A=wedge_(i in A)(u_i wedge v_i). Wedge with w_A kills every forced-span
component in every remaining vector. Since ||w_A||^2=d and the projected
vectors lie in its orthogonal complement,

 ||w_A wedge omega_E(s)^r/r!||^2=d X_r(P omega_E(s)).

In the ORIGINAL coordinate basis this vector has coefficients
 h_R(s)=sum_(I subset R, |I|=k, A subset I, I cap B empty)
          det V[R,P(I)] chi_(I minus A)(s).
There are at most binomial(2k-a,k-a) eligible I per R, and also at most
binomial(|E|,r). Their squared Fourier coefficients sum to W_k(A,B).
Therefore with

 C_A,B=min{binomial(2k-a,k-a),binomial(|E|,r)}<=C_k,

we have 0<=Y_s<=C_A,B W, E Y_s=W, and Var(Y_s)<=(C_A,B-1)W^2.
This also explains the implemented exact shortcut when C_A,B=1: every sign
sample then equals its mean. If r=0 the mass is simply d. k=0 is vacuum, and
k=1 is exactly the sum of the allowed off-diagonal row weights. These exact
base cases do not rely on estimates or support promises.

4. FIXED-WORK RELATIVE ESTIMATOR

For C=C_A,B>1, take M=ceil(4(C-1)/eta^2) independent sign samples per batch.
Chebyshev and the variance bound give relative failure <=1/4. Use the median
of g=8 ceil(log_2(1/delta))+1 independent batch means. The standard Bernoulli
Hoeffding bound for a majority of failed batches is exp(-g/8)<=delta.
Each sample consumes exactly |E| fair bits. Both M and g are computed by
integer/rational arithmetic and are predetermined; no rejection or stopping
rule depends on the observed norm. The whole call has a bounded runtime.

If W=0, every sample is zero. If W>0, a zero sample/median is possible only
on the confidence-failure event; it is not a deterministic zero certificate.
The reference implementation optionally exhausts a sign cube only when its
size is below an explicit cap (default 256) and below the randomized work.
That finite exact branch can improve small-instance cost but is not used to
claim polynomial full-cube enumeration. A configured sign-budget rejection
reports INCOMPLETE and claims no estimate/sample guarantee for that call.

5. EXACT COLUMN-VOLUME COUNTS AND FINITE-BIT DECISIONS

After sampling a supported row set I, form M=F[I,I^c]. Its conditional column
law has weight |det M[:,J]|^2 and total det(M M*)=det(V_I*V_I)>0.
For forced columns S, excluded columns B, remaining columns R, q=k-|S|,
C=M[:,S], d=det(C*C), and P off span(C), its completion mass is

 d [t^q] det(I_k+t(P M_R)(P M_R)*).                (3)

Dependent C or insufficient remaining columns gives exact zero. For each
completion T, Schur complement extracts d and the projected T-Gram volume;
Cauchy--Binet sums those q-volumes to the indicated characteristic
coefficient. This proves (3). The coefficient is acquired by exact Newton
traces on a k by k Gram matrix. Two such positive masses per next column
partition the current completion mass, so unrounded rational self-reduction
samples the conditional volume law exactly, without rare-sector rejection.

The implementation uses fixed-bit dyadic choices for BOTH stages. With
D=2^h, replace a Bernoulli probability p by floor(Dp)/D and draw exactly h
fair bits when neither threshold endpoint is deterministic. One decision
has TV error <1/D; exact-zero choices retain zero probability. At most 2n
row/column decisions therefore add <=2n/D TV. No unbounded randbelow loop is
needed. The joint output is original physical site labels (I,J), not merely
the paired-volume row selector.

6. HELLINGER COST REFINEMENT: ETA SCALES AS EPSILON/SQRT(n)

This is the new classical cost refinement supplied by this implementation.
At a positive row prefix with true two-child law p and relative-eta child
estimates, their normalized law q obeys

 (1-eta)/(1+eta)<=q_j/p_j<=(1+eta)/(1-eta).

For eta<=1/2, |q_j/p_j-1|<=4eta. True zero children stay zero. Consequently
the squared Hellinger distance in convention H^2=sum_j(sqrt(q_j)-sqrt(p_j))^2
is at most 16eta^2, since |sqrt(x)-1|<=|x-1| for x>=0.
The conditional affinity rho=sum sqrt(p_j q_j) is thus >=1-8eta^2.

For the idealized estimator-driven sampler, replace any bad child-estimate
call by the TRUE child probabilities for analysis only. This is not an
algorithmic oracle. At each prefix its fresh estimation randomness either
gives a good law satisfying the ratio bound or this true law. The mixture
still has the same ratio bound. Sequential affinity recursion then gives

 rho(P_rows,Q_repaired)>= (1-8eta^2)^n >=1-8neta^2.

Indeed sum over the last two children and use the common lower affinity
bound before recursing on the preceding prefix; induction proves the product
bound even when kernels depend on every earlier choice. Thus
H^2(P_rows,Q_repaired)<=16neta^2, and Cauchy--Schwarz gives TV<=H<=4sqrt(n)eta.
This avoids a linear sum of per-step TV errors for the relative estimates.

The actual unrounded sampler couples to the repaired sampler unless a visited
call fails. At most 2n adaptive calls use fresh random bits, each with
conditional failure <=delta_call, so their failure union has probability
<=2n delta_call. If both estimates are zero, return a fixed legal hard k-pair
configuration. That branch cannot occur at a positive prefix on the good
event. Its possible zero target weight is charged to the same failure union.
Every positive estimate necessarily has positive true mass because a true
zero produces zero for every sign word. Therefore other selected row branches
retain positive support, even on a bad relative-estimation event.

Take s=ceil(sqrt(n)),
 eta=epsilon/(16s), delta_call=epsilon/(8n), D>=8n/epsilon.
Then repaired-row TV<=epsilon/4, failure coupling<=epsilon/4, and dyadic
rounding for BOTH stages <=epsilon/4. The exact conditional-column kernel
preserves the row TV error before rounding. The final joint TV is <=3epsilon/4
<epsilon. These budgets are implemented exactly, not supplied by an oracle.

As s^2<=4n for n>=1, eta^-2=O(n epsilon^-2). Multiplying by at most 2n calls
gives O(C_k n^2 epsilon^-2 log(n/epsilon)) sign evaluations. The original
l06 sequential-TV derivation used eta=O(epsilon/n), hence n^3 evaluations;
the Hellinger refinement saves one polynomial n factor without changing the
exponential k boundary. It does not automatically improve a coherent compiler's
triangle/hybrid bound; no such claim is made here.

7. ACQUISITION, BIT HEIGHTS AND COST RECEIPT

Gram/projection acquisition is O(n^3) rational arithmetic per prefix, plus
polynomial scans/gcd work. Forced Gram dimension is <=2k. Singular forced
spans are rejected by exact rank/determinant, not by a numerical threshold.
Small d is explicitly inverted through G^-1 when forming P; this is a
polynomial-size rational operation, not an inverse-magnitude runtime promise.

Clear the denominators of the projected paired vectors by their positive
least common multiple D'. Multiply both vectors in each pair by D'. A
r-pair Pfaffian norm scales by (D')^(4r), so the integer sample is multiplied
by the fixed rational d/(D')^(4r). All samples of ONE prefix therefore share
one denominator. Averaging M samples adds only O(log M) numerator bits.
G, its adjugate/inverse, P, and projected entries have polynomial heights by
determinant/Hadamard bounds in the explicit n,L input. Clearing the finitely
many denominators also has polynomial height. Entries of powers of A* A
through r<=k have polynomial height O(r times entry-height+r log n), and
the Newton/formal-root coefficients have the same polynomial bound.
Ratios and integer dyadic thresholds of two polynomial-height masses require
polynomial bit work even when one mass is exponentially small. No float,
conditioning promise, measured-input rounding, or smallest-singular-value
parameter is used. This is compatible with s02's cancellation-scale barriers.

The reference per-sign acquisition of a skew matrix is O(|E|n^2), and its
trace engine is O(r n^3). Thus O((k+1)n^3) is a conservative arithmetic bound.
Streaming these matrices uses polynomial O(n^2) coefficient storage rather
than an exponential occupation table. Column-prefix work takes at most
O(k^4+k^2n) arithmetic per call; O(n^2(k+1)^4) safely bounds that whole stage.
All operation bounds acquire the actual matrices/counts rather than treating
them as value oracles. Bit cost is these bounds times a polynomial in n,L,
log(1/eta),log(1/delta). Random bits for estimates are at most n times the
bounded number of sign evaluations; branches add at most 2n ceil(log_2(8n/eps)).

8. EXACT METHODS AS EQUALLY INFORMED COMPARATORS

All methods receive the same explicit rational F. Structural certificates
and their acquisition must be charged. The delivered exact cut-rank MPS
tests a given site order and compiles it, with bond dimension D=2^c; its
all-sector counts cost O(n^2 D^3), cached sampling O(nD^2), and acquisition
O(n^4+nD^3(c+2)^2) arithmetic. A different favorable order is not acquired
for free. The tree-cut compiler costs O(n^4+nD^5(c+1)^2), counts O(n^2D^5),
and its reference prefix sampler O(n^3D^5). Acquired forest decompositions
have c<=2; fixed-size balanced-separator search is polynomial but incurs the
stated O(n^(b+3)) graph acquisition and potentially n^(2b) dimensions.

The acquired dense gauged-Green class is much faster when admitted: O(n^2)
recognition and all-sector counts, then O(kn) cached exact random-bit samples.
Functional-digraph c03_l09 gives another O(n^2) exact count and O(n^3)
self-reduction on its row-functional class. Support-treewidth c03_s01 and
the all-t c03_l09 extension give 2^O(w) poly(n,L) exact contraction after
their supplied/acquired order/decomposition cost. These exact methods can
beat the low-k estimator by an exponential k factor on their admitted inputs.
Their structural restrictions are load-bearing. Merely choosing the cheaper
valid method is a charged portfolio, not a new general-sector theorem.

The swap-block example makes the estimator boundary explicit. At n=2k and
F the direct sum of k [[0,1],[1,0]] blocks, c_k=2^k and X is 4^k with
probability 2^-k and zero otherwise. Its relative second moment is 2^k.
Thus raw sign averaging needs exponentially many samples at dense k even
though an exact structural counter immediately solves the same instance.
This is estimator variance, not counting hardness. No Jacobi near-top shortcut
or claim that particle-hole holes replace k is used.
