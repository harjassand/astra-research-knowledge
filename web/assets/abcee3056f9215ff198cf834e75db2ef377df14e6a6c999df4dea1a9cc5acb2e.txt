Claim and exact interface
-------------------------
For a finite chronological circuit of rational symmetric gates F_i=I+s_i A_i,
the supported easy-plane edge terms and nonnegative-X / arbitrary-Z field
terms have an explicit rational, homogeneous, multiaffine, log-concave lift.
The all-ones coefficient of the product of this lifted local polynomial and
one exact-one wire polynomial times a uniform-matroid polynomial is exactly
the rational trace of the gate circuit. Both coefficient measures have degree
half the size of their common ground set, explicit positive support seeds,
exact rational value oracles, and an exact coefficient-range bound.

An executable compiler constructs those objects and their support/LC
certificates. It exactly contracts small instances and compares them to an
independent dense rational matrix product. This is an exact finite-instance
construction; it does not implement the imported approximation algorithm.

Supported physical gates
------------------------
On any finite loopless spatial multigraph, a pair occurrence on qubits u,v is

  A_e=3 alpha I + alpha (X_u X_v+Y_u Y_v) + gamma Z_u Z_v,
  alpha >= |gamma|, alpha,gamma in Q,

and a field occurrence at v is

  A_v=(b+|c|)I+b X_v+c Z_v,
  b>=0, b,c in Q.

Each local step s is any rational s>=0, and F=I+sA. The global circuit is an
ordered product of these local gates; it can contain repeated edges and
spatially varying fields. Qubit chronology supplies the trace wires; there is
no graph bipartition, planarity, degree, width, or gap condition. An untouched
qubit contributes its explicit factor 2 to the trace.

Pair-gate certificate
---------------------
In row/column order 00,01,10,11, the gate is

  [[a,0,0,0], [0,beta,c,0], [0,c,beta,0], [0,0,0,a]],
  a=1+s(3 alpha+gamma), beta=1+s(3 alpha-gamma), c=2s alpha.

Keep row bits and complement column bits. With four distinct variables, the
local polynomial is

  q2=a(x1 x2+x3 x4)+beta(x1 x4+x2 x3)+c(x1 x3+x2 x4).

Its Hessian is constant and has eigenvalues
  a+beta+c,
  a-beta-c = 2s(gamma-alpha),
  -a+beta-c = -2s(gamma+alpha),
  -a-beta+c = -2-4s alpha.
The last three are nonpositive. The coefficients are nonnegative, so the
homogeneous quadratic has at most one positive Hessian eigenvalue and is
log-concave on the positive orthant. The positive monomial a x1 x2 is a
support seed; a>=1 even on the cone boundary.

Field-gate certificate and homogeneous lift
-------------------------------------------
Set r=b+|c|, u=1+s(r+c), v=1+s(r-c), d=sb. Then u,v>=1, d>=0, and

  uv-d^2 = 1+2sr+2s^2 b|c| > 0.

After column complementation the field polynomial is q1=d+u x+v y+dxy.
Introduce two fresh variables z0,z1 and use the rational degree-two lift

  q1_tilde = d z0 z1 +(u/2)x(z0+z1)+(v/2)y(z0+z1)+dxy.

Setting z0=z1=1 recovers q1. For d>0, positively rescale x,y by factors
sqrt(v/u),sqrt(u/v). The three complementary pair weights of the resulting
four-variable homogeneous quadratic are d,sqrt(uv)/2,sqrt(uv)/2. Its Hessian
eigenvalues are d+sqrt(uv), d-sqrt(uv), -d, -d, so d^2<=uv gives at most one
positive eigenvalue and hence log-concavity. The square roots are only a proof
rescaling: all compiler coefficients are rational. If d=0, the lift factors
as (u x+v y)(z0+z1)/2 and is directly log-concave. A positive seed is
(u/2) x z0.

Global coefficient identity
----------------------------
Let e and f be the numbers of pair and field occurrences, respectively.
There are W=2e+f physical wires and 2f dummy variables, giving

  N=2W+2f=4(e+f),       deg(P)=deg(Q)=2(e+f)=N/2.

For each physical wire joining the row output of one occurrence to the
complemented column input of its chronological successor, use one distinct
variable at each endpoint. Let P be the product of q2 over pair gates and
q1_tilde over field gates. Let

  Q = product_{wire {h,h'}} (x_h+x_h') * e_f(z_1,...,z_{2f}),

where e_f is the elementary symmetric polynomial of degree f (e_0=1). The
wire factor is a partition-matroid basis polynomial; e_f is a uniform-matroid
basis polynomial. Products on disjoint variable sets preserve log-concavity,
so P and Q meet the imported homogeneous theorem's LC hypotheses.

The coefficient [x^U](P Q) forces exactly one P-selected variable per wire,
which is exactly the original equality of row/column bits after complementation.
P has degree 2e+2f. The wires force it to select W physical variables, leaving
exactly f of the 2f dummies. The complementary dummy selection in e_f has
coefficient one. Summing all these local dummy choices substitutes z_i=1 and
recovers every original q1 factor, including cases where a local term uses
zero or two dummies. Thus [x^U](P Q)=Tr(product of gates). If there are
inactive physical qubits, multiply by 2 per qubit.

Oracle/support/range certificate
-------------------------------
The mu oracle factors a proposed subset into each gate's local four-variable
table and multiplies exact rational coefficients; a subset violating a local
table has value zero. The nu oracle returns one iff it has one endpoint per
wire and exactly f dummies, else zero. Its positive coefficient range is 1.
For each pair gate, choose the monomial a x_row0 x_row1; for each field gate
choose (u/2)x_row z0. Their union is a positive mu seed of size
N/2. A nu seed selects the first endpoint of every wire and any fixed f
dummies; it also has size N/2 and value one. The coefficient range of mu is
exactly the product over gates of (largest positive local coefficient)/(the
smallest positive local coefficient), since gate variable blocks are
disjoint. The program writes all of these exact rational values and seed names
to diagnostics.json and checks each seed through its own oracle.

The imported Chen-Liu Theorem 5.1 requires two homogeneous log-concave
coefficient measures on a common N-element ground set with degree sum N,
value oracles, range upper bounds, and one positive support seed per measure.
The construction supplies these data. The theorem's arithmetic bound is
O(N^10 (N+log(R_mu R_nu))^2 eta^-2 log^2(2(N+log(R_mu R_nu))/eta)); the
primary paper's Remark A.4 claims a polynomial-bit, every-execution
implementation for rational polynomial-bit oracles. Both are imported claims.
The compiler has not invoked or implemented that FPRAS.
