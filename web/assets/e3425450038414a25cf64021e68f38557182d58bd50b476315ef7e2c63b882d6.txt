POST-AUDIT REPAIR — 2026-10-08

The cold audit at audit/REPORT.txt found no algebraic or all-state drift gap,
but identified that g_+(R) used an ambiguous N. RESULT.txt now defines

  g_+(R)=sup_{b in Z_{>=0}} max(G_R(b),0),

including the b=0 endpoint. The exact rational counterexample in the audit
shows this endpoint is essential: if it is omitted, the theorem condition
can pass while the b=0 linear A drift is positive and geometric Foster drift
fails.

The audit also supplied a simpler uniform H bound. If M=max_i c_i>=4d,
then every c-e_i with c_i>0 retains a coordinate at least M-1, so both Q(c)
and Q(c-e_i) are at least zeta_min(M/2)^d. Together with
Delta_i<=zeta_max*d*c_i^(d-1), this gives

  H(c)<=r*2^(2d)*mu_max*zeta_max*d/(zeta_min^2*M^d).

RESULT.txt, claims, restart notes, and checker use this sharper constant.
The finite fixture still uses M0=64; the audit's 64x64 enumeration therefore
remains a superset of the box required by the revised tail bound. The exact
checker was rerun after both edits.
