NEW DEEPER CONSEQUENCE: RATIONAL COVARIANCE ERROR TO PNR-TV

Mele et al., arXiv:2405.01431v4, Theorem 10 gives an explicit trace-distance
continuity inequality for energy-bounded Gaussian states. Translating their
covariance convention (their vacuum covariance is I, ours is I/2), two
centered M-mode inputs with covariances V and Vhat, passed through the same
passive W and uniform vacuum loss eta, satisfy

  TV(PNR(V_eta),PNR(Vhat_eta))
     <= d_tr(rho_eta,rhohat_eta)
     <= 2 f(N) sqrt(eta ||V-Vhat||_1),
  f(N)=(sqrt(N)+sqrt(N+1))/sqrt(2),

provided both output mean photon numbers are <=N. Here trace-distance is
one-half trace norm, output energy scales by eta, and PNR is a measurement.
If Vhat is rational and physical, triangle inequality adds the N72 reset
term evaluated on Vhat. Thus a certified covariance compilation error has
an explicit additive measurement-TV budget. A general raw rational
approximation with operator error delta can be made strictly physical by
adding 2 delta I; this has ||Vhat-V||_1<=6 M delta and increases the input
mean photon number by at most 3 M delta. The N72 active-support condition
still needs its exact instantiation check. This does not construct the
covariance compiler from a Hamiltonian, nor implement N73.
