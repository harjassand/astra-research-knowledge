C10_S01 — CONTROLLED RECOVERY TO AN ACQUIRED POSITIVE COUNT TARGET
Derived from the new four-layer Foster/event certificate and the bounded
hazard/retry mechanism already present in frozen c10_s03 INITIAL. The retry
principle and finite-state robust recovery are prior baseline contributions,
not newly attributed to this worker. The new application is the phase-corrected
four-layer grammar, which lacks the frozen compiler's required unary drains.
Status: complete internal derivation; independent review/priority UNKNOWN.

Let V,C0,f,kappa,R,T0,d be the acquired forest data in the preceding addendum.
For any supplied integer q>=1, define G_q={all species counts>=q}. This is a
hitting target; return to G_q does not imply all-time pathwise positivity.

1. A PATH IS ACQUIRED DIRECTLY FROM THE CATALYTIC GRAMMAR
From any state with N<R, raise each root count to at least q using its direct
immigration, then raise middle counts, third counts and fourth counts in that
order using one acquired parent channel each. Every catalyst stays unchanged
along the selected birth word and is positive when needed. Suppress competing
firings only for this witness event, not as a new allowed control action.
The word uses at most H=dq unit births and visits N<=D=R-1+H. No drain/reset,
supplied basin or component oracle is required. All enabled desired aggregate
channels have intensity at least lmin, the minimum channel lower bound.
Set
  Q=T0(D+1)^2, alpha=lmin/(2Q), t0=H/Q, p=alpha^H.
At each witness state total intensity is at most Q, and the desired hazard
is at least lmin. Conditional on every past history, the probability the next
event is that desired channel and occurs within 1/Q is at least
  lmin integral_0^(1/Q) exp(-Qs)ds >= lmin/(2Q)=alpha.
If the word has fewer than H steps, alpha^word_length>=alpha^H, since alpha<1.
Thus uniformly over all predictable controls and all core states,
  P(tau_Gq<=t0 | current history)>=p.                       (PATH)
Rates need not be piecewise constant, Markov, or independent of downstream
counts; the uniform hazard bounds remain true on every no-jump history.

2. ACQUIRED CORE POTENTIAL MAXIMUM
Every coordinate in N<R is at most R. Hence the following explicitly acquired
bound needs no core enumeration:
  Vcore=R^3[sum_i w_Ri+sum_j w_Bj G_j+sum_k w_Ck G_k
            +sum_l(M_l+1+epsilon_l(R+1)^2/s_l)].
The unbounded phase coefficient is still bounded on this finite count core by
the displayed last summand. Globally LV<=C0 gives conditional
  E[V(X_t0)]<=Vcore+C0 t0
when an attempt starts in the core. This follows by finite-box stopping and
Fatou, without assuming stationary moments or a bounded trajectory.

3. RESTART AND EXPECTED PHYSICAL TIME
First wait to hit K={N<R}. From K attempt recovery for t0. On failure, wait
to return to K, then restart. At each attempt, conditional failure probability
is at most 1-p. The unconditional conditional mean return cost after failure
is at most E[V(X_t0)], because the acquired return bound holds conditionally
on the entire history at the attempt's end. Thus each reached cycle has mean
duration at most Vcore+(C0+1)t0, even if failure correlates with high counts.
Summing the geometric probability of reaching successive cycles gives
  E_x tau_Gq <= V(x)+[Vcore+(C0+1)t0]/p.                    (TARGET_TIME)
Hitting G_q during an initial/failed-return interval can only decrease this
bound. No invariant distribution is assigned to a history-dependent policy.

4. CHARGE ALL REACTION FIRINGS IN THE ATTEMPTS AND RETURNS
At every state, including N=0,
  q_total<=T0+(d T0/kappa)f.
Stopped (MAIN) gives E integral_0^t0 f<=Vcore+C0t0 for an attempted interval.
So its expected firing count is at most
  T0t0+(d T0/kappa)(Vcore+C0t0).
On failure, the separately proved return-event bound gives an unconditional
mean at most (2d T0/kappa)E[V(X_t0)], bounded by the same acquired moment term.
Consequently the complete expected count of all firings through first G_q
entrance is bounded by
  (2d T0/kappa)V(x)
    +[T0t0+(3d T0/kappa)(Vcore+C0t0)]/p.                    (TARGET_EVENTS)
This includes the entrance firing, fast catalytic cycles and failed attempts.
Neither probability conditioning nor the elapsed-time estimate hides those
turnovers. Energy per turnover and controller implementation cost are UNKNOWN.

5. ENCODING AND COMPUTATION
The executable function acquire_positive_target in four_layer_forest_certificate.py
returns all rational coefficients and the pair (alpha,H), retaining p=alpha^H
as a compact exact rational-power expression. The compact representation has
polynomial bit length/work in reaction input plus binary q. Expanding p or
the numerical time/event bounds may require O(d q poly(input_length+log q))
bits and corresponding arithmetic work, exponential in binary q. Production
word expansion itself can take O(dq) steps. These costs are not relabeled
polynomial merely because the compact expression is small.

The recovery constants and physical event bounds can be enormous. This is an
effective certificate and cost bound, not a claimed efficient simulation or
chemical-computation implementation. Both four-species and eight-species exact
diagnostic records include q=1 compact certificates. The symbolic derivation
above, rather than their finite metadata, proves the controlled hitting claim.
