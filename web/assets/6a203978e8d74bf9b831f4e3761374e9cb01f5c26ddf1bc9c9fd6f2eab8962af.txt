RESULT

The strongest result is a scoped norm-transfer obstruction, strengthened after
the independent checkpoint: the exact normalized-Choi-Hilbert-Schmidt versus
full-ball normalized infinity-to-one gap already occurs for EB,
selfcompatible channels. It does not refute EB rounding or the broadcasting
target because the channel in the example is itself an exact EB comparator.

For even d, traceless Hermitian unitaries U,V, normalized trace tau=Tr/d and
0<=t<=1, set
  Phi_t(X)=tau(X)I+t tau(UX)V,     D(X)=tau(X)I.
With P_±=(I±U)/2 and sigma_±=(I±tV)/d,
  Phi_t(X)=sum_± Tr(P_±X) sigma_±.
This is a measure-and-prepare channel. The full-domain broadcaster
  B_t(X)=sum_± Tr(P_±X) sigma_± tensor sigma_±
is CPTP, swap-symmetric, and has both marginals Phi_t. The exact normalized
Choi and induced-norm values are
  ||Jhat(Phi_t)-Jhat(D)||_2=t/d,
  ||Phi_t-D||_(infinity-to-one,tau)=t,
with equality in the latter witnessed by X=U. Thus Choi-HS closeness alone
cannot imply dimension-free closeness on the full Hermitian operator ball,
even under selfcompatibility. This is not a failure of the EB target.
