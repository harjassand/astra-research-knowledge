# Endotactic source drift as a robust differential-inclusion certificate

## Verdict

I derive a classwise robustness extension of N33 for finite switched differential inclusions with additive, class-tangent disturbances. The extension is conditional on the N33 affine-activity/plateau construction and weighted essential-source estimate. Its added hypothesis is an explicit one-sided disturbance budget measured against the monomial mass of each mode's lowest essential source tier. Under that budget, one compact convex absorber works for every switching signal, every admissible rate selection, every admissible disturbance selection, and every solution of the inclusion; no uniqueness or dwell-time assumption is needed.

This is a mathematical transfer, not an externally validated or priority-cleared result. The common nonsmooth-Lyapunov/differential-inclusion framework is established prior art. The specific source-tier disturbance envelope and its finite-entry permanence consequence are the scoped contribution here. I do not claim that they are historically new.

## Theorem: source-weighted disturbance robustness

Fix the finite family of modes and a positive class $P$. Use the N33 affine construction on the union of all mode complexes. It supplies a finite label family, slopes $r_\lambda\in[-1,1]^d$, a constant $H>0$, scales $0<h\le h_P$,
\[
F_h(x)=\min_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\{r_\lambda\cdot x+b_\lambda(h)\},
\quad B_h=[h,h^{-1}]^d,
\quad K_h=\{x:F_h(x)=F_h(c)\},
\]
and $K_h\subset\operatorname{int}B_{\sqrt h}$. Write $A_h(x)=\{\lambda:\lambda\text{ minimizes }F_h(x)\}$, including every tied minimizer, and set
\[
L=\max_j\sum_{e\in E_j}\|\nu_{je}\|_1,
\qquad M=\max_{j,e}\|y_{je}\|_1.
\]
Set $M=0$ if the union of the edge sets is empty.
The affine scales can be chosen so $KLh^H\le\kappa H$ for every $h\le h_P$.

For each $r\notin S^\perp$ and mode $j$, define its lowest essential source tier
\[
E^*_{j,r}=\left\{e:r\cdot\nu_{je}\ne0,\quad
r\cdot y_{je}=\min_{q:r\cdot\nu_{jq}\ne0}r\cdot y_{jq}\right\},
\qquad
\Phi_{j,r}(x)=\max_{e\in E^*_{j,r}}x^{y_{je}},
\qquad
\underline\Phi_r(x)=\min_{1\le j\le J}\Phi_{j,r}(x).
\]
Endotacticity in direction $-r$ makes $E^*_{j,r}$ nonempty and gives $r\cdot\nu_{je}>0$ for every $e\in E^*_{j,r}$.

Let $\mathcal U(t,x)\subset S$ be a nonempty compact convex disturbance set, jointly measurable, upper semicontinuous in $x$ for almost every $t$, and locally bounded uniformly in time on compact subsets of the positive orthant. Fix $0\le\theta<1$. Assume, for almost every $t$, every $0<h\le h_P$, every $x\in B_h$, every $\lambda\in A_h(x)$ with $r_\lambda\notin S^\perp$, and every $u\in\mathcal U(t,x)$,
\[
r_\lambda\cdot u\ \ge\ -\theta\kappa H\underline\Phi_{r_\lambda}(x).
\tag{D}
\]
Consider the differential inclusion
\[
\dot x(t)\in\operatorname{co}\!\left(\bigcup_{j=1}^J
\left\{\sum_{e\in E_j}a_{je}x^{y_{je}}\nu_{je}:a_{je}\in[\kappa,K]\right\}\right)
+\mathcal U(t,x(t)).
\tag{DI}
\]
Then for every positive class $P$ there is one nonempty compact convex $K_P\subset P$, independent of the switching, rate, and disturbance selections, such that every maximal absolutely continuous solution of (DI) starting at any $x^0\in P$ is global, remains positive, enters $K_P$ in finite time, and stays there. For each $x^0$, the entry-time bound is independent of all selections and all solutions. There is no claim of one entry-time bound uniform over the unbounded class $P$ or over different classes.

Since $K_P$ is compact and lies inside the positive orthant, this also gives one $\epsilon_P>0$ such that every solution is eventually in $[\epsilon_P,\epsilon_P^{-1}]^d$, with the same $\epsilon_P$ for the entire class and every admissible inclusion selection.

At a scale $h$, the waiting-time bound from $B_h$ is
\[
W_{h,\theta}=\frac{F_h(c)-\min_{x\in B_h}F_h(x)}
{(1-\theta)\kappa Hh^M}.
\]
If $h_0\le h_P$ is chosen so $x^0\in K_{h_0}$, iterate $h_{q+1}=\min\{\sqrt{h_q},h_P\}$ until $h_P$. Then $T(x^0)\le\sum_q W_{h_q,\theta}$. The finite number of scales depends on $x^0$ and $P$, but not on a selected trajectory.

For $J=1$, condition (D) is a slope-specific source-tier bound on additive forcing. With $\mathcal U=\{0\}$, this includes all convexified rate-box solutions, even when the rates are selected adversarially as functions of time and state and the inclusion is nonunique. For $J>1$, common stoichiometric span is essential for classwise absorption.

## Proof

Fix a scale $h\le h_P$, a mode $j$, and a slope $r$ active at $x\in B_h$. Write $x_i=h^{p_i}$, $p\in[-1,1]^d$. N33's affine-activity construction ensures that every relevant nonzero comparison preserves sign and has a gap of at least $H$: for every source difference $v$ and every nonzero reaction vector used in its finite comparison set, $r\cdot v\ne0$ implies $\operatorname{sgn}(p\cdot v)=\operatorname{sgn}(r\cdot v)$ and $|p\cdot v|\ge H$. Hence if $r\cdot y_{je}>r\cdot y_{jq}$, then $x^{y_{je}}\le h^H x^{y_{jq}}$.

Choose $e_0\in E^*_{j,r}$ attaining $\Phi_{j,r}(x)$. The nonzero reaction-vector comparison gives $r\cdot\nu_{je_0}\ge2H$. Every edge with $r\cdot\nu_{je}<0$ has source strictly above the minimum tier, since every essential edge at the minimum tier points to positive $r$-direction. Therefore each negative monomial is at most $h^H\Phi_{j,r}(x)$. Since $|r\cdot\nu|\le\|\nu\|_1$,
\[
r\cdot\sum_e a_{je}x^{y_{je}}\nu_{je}
\ge 2\kappa H\Phi_{j,r}(x)
-KLh^H\Phi_{j,r}(x)
\ge\kappa H\Phi_{j,r}(x).
\tag{1}
\]
For $x\in B_h$, every source monomial satisfies $x^{y_{je}}\ge h^{\|y_{je}\|_1}\ge h^M$, including real or negative exponents, so $\Phi_{j,r}(x)\ge h^M$. Thus every mode, and then every convex mixture of modes, has projected drift at least $\kappa H\underline\Phi_r(x)\ge\kappa Hh^M$.

Adding any $u\in\mathcal U(t,x)$ costs at most the fraction $\theta$ of that common lower bound by (D); hence each active nonneutral slope has projected velocity at least $(1-\theta)\kappa H\underline\Phi_r(x)\ge(1-\theta)\kappa Hh^M$. If $r\in S^\perp$, all reaction vectors and all $u\in S$ have zero projection. Convexification preserves these linear inequalities.

Every inclusion solution is absolutely continuous. The finite affine minimum obeys, almost everywhere,
\[
\frac{d}{dt}F_h(x(t))=\min_{\lambda\in A_h(x(t))}r_\lambda\cdot\dot x(t).
\]
Consequently $F_h$ is nondecreasing on every trajectory segment in $B_h$. On $B_h\cap(P\setminus K_h)$, no active slope can lie in $S^\perp$: such an affine form is constant on $c+S$, has value at $c$ at least $F_h(c)$, and cannot be active where $F_h<F_h(c)$. Thus outside $K_h$, $F_h$ increases at rate at least $(1-\theta)\kappa Hh^M$. The plateau $K_h$ is compact, convex, positive, and misses the boundary of $B_h$ by N33's affine geometry. Nondecrease plus the cap $F_h\le F_h(c)$ makes $K_h$ invariant; strict increase bounds entry from $B_h$ by $W_{h,\theta}$. Since all velocities lie in $S$, $P$ is invariant. Compact containment in the positive orthant and local boundedness of (DI) give continuation, hence every trapped maximal solution is global.

For arbitrary $x^0\in P$, the N33 scale-selection argument chooses $h_0$ with $x^0\in K_{h_0}$; repeated square-root enlargement carries the trajectory through finitely many invariant plateaux to $K_{h_P}\cap P$. All drift and waiting bounds are uniform over the disturbance, rates, switching, and solution selections. This gives $K_P=K_{h_P}\cap P$ and the asserted classwise quantifiers.

## Failure boundaries and falsifiers

1. **Unrestricted forcing fails.** For $0\rightleftarrows A$ with both rates equal to 1, the diagram is endotactic and $S=\mathbb R$. Adding the tangent forcing $u(t,x)=-1$ gives $\dot x=1-x-1=-x$, so every positive solution tends to 0. Thus the disturbance cannot be arbitrary; if a counterexample satisfying (D) were found with $0\le\theta<1$, it would falsify the theorem.
2. **Union span does not replace common span.** The modes $0\rightleftarrows A$ and $0\rightleftarrows B$, each with unit rates, are endotactic with spans $\mathbb Re_1$ and $\mathbb Re_2$. Their union span is $\mathbb R^2$, but permanently selecting the first mode leaves $x_B(t)=x_B(0)$. Since $x_B(0)$ is arbitrary in the positive class for the union span, no compact classwise absorber works for every switching schedule.
3. **Positive lower reaction-rate bound is load-bearing.** For $0\rightleftarrows A$, let inflow $k_{in}(t)=1/(1+t)$, outflow 1. The equation $\dot x=1/(1+t)-x$ has $x(t)\to0$. This refutes dropping the supplied lower rate bound even without disturbances.

## Prior art and novelty boundary

- Della Rossa, Tanwani, and Zaccarian study max-min nonsmooth Lyapunov functions for differential inclusions whose right-hand sides are convex hulls of finitely many vector fields and for state-dependent switched systems ([primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.07227)). This rules out any claim that a finite min/max Lyapunov function or convexified switching framework is itself new.
- Della Rossa, Tanwani, and Zaccarian develop nonsmooth ISS-Lyapunov conditions for upper-semicontinuous differential inclusions with inputs ([primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.09092)). Robust differential-inclusion Lyapunov analysis is therefore established prior art. My disturbance condition is a source-tier-specific support budget; I found no source establishing this exact all-class finite-entry permanence corollary, but this bounded search does not clear priority.
- Brunner and Craciun prove permanence for two-dimensional tropically endotactic variable-coefficient polynomial/power-law systems, including time-dependent coefficients ([primary v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.06785v3), Theorems 1.1–1.2, especially lines 63–88). Craciun, Nazarov, and Pantea prove the two-species all-endotactic variable-rate case ([primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.3050)). These results mean the low-dimensional variable-rate case is prior art; the candidate transfer concerns arbitrary finite common-span mode families and a weighted additive-disturbance inclusion.
- Gopalkrishnan, Miller, and Shiu's primary source states permanence for strongly endotactic networks and distinguishes that condition from ordinary endotacticity ([arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1305.5303v2)). The N33 essential-source definition follows its stated convention.
- The supplied N33 proof attributes its affine approximation lemma to OpenAI's fixed release149 manuscript ([pinned release149 package](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Uniform-Permanence-in-Weakly-Reversible-Mass-Action-Systems-October-5-2026)). The theorem here inherits that candidate proof and its validation/priority debt.

The transfer has no model-recognition, affine-family-synthesis, bit-complexity, or rate-acquisition claim. The finite diagram and valid endotacticity assertion are supplied inputs. N33 gives only nonconstructive bounds on the affine family, $H$, $h_P$, and entry times; the disturbance envelope is defined relative to that supplied/reconstructed witness. The result concerns deterministic continuous-time differential inclusions, not stochastic jump processes, delays, PDEs, or arbitrary external forcing.

## Next decisive review

The theorem would be falsified by a valid endotactic common-span mode family and an upper-semicontinuous disturbance multifunction satisfying (D), together with a positive class solution that exits the plateau, becomes nonpositive, fails global continuation, or misses the claimed entry bound. A cold review should focus on (i) the source-tier monomial $\Phi$ under same-tier ties, (ii) preservation of the affine activity gap for the union of mode complexes, (iii) the inclusion chain rule at ties, and (iv) local existence/continuation assumptions for set-valued disturbances. No numerical experiment is needed for the proof; none is reported.
