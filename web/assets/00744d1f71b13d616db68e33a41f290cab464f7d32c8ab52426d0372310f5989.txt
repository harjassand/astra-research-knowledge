Main result
-----------
For n>=40000, every predictor supplied the full raw n-bit history but not mu
has some finite-mean stationary binary renewal source with

  P_mu{ R_n(mu,X^n) >= (ln(2)-1/2)/(18 sqrt(n)) } >= 1/160.

Here R_n is the random same-path conditional terminal KL risk. The type-first
exact predictor has expected-risk bound B_n and hence the fixed-n Markov
upper tail min(1,B_n/epsilon). For fixed delta<1/160 the minimax confidence
quantile therefore has order n^-1/2. The lower-bound prior changes with n.

Proof point that must be retained
--------------------------------
Use Palm independence at the last renewal K. Conditional on source support S
and terminal residual k=n+1-K, the complete interarrival gaps immediately
preceding K, read backwards, remain iid mu_S; the following gap is independent
and is only right-censored by the event that K is the last observed renewal.
On event R<=sqrt(n), every complete gap visible in the length-n block lies
among the last m0=floor(sqrt(n)) gaps preceding K. Thus failure of the
target-gap absence condition is contained in {some one of those m0 iid gaps
equals k}, with probability <=m0/a<=1/16. Do not condition the iid sequence
on the gap-count event when applying this union bound; use event containment.

Keep the four loss objects separate:
* random conditional risk R_n(X^n);
* expected conditional risk E_{X^n}R_n;
* realized continuation log-score Lambda_n, with conditional mean R_n;
* prediction evaluated on an independent stationary history (not covered).
