1. Legal elementary noise and complete quantifiers
--------------------------------------------------
Write s=sqrt(sigma), d=s^(1/2), U=log s, and ell_s(V)=(sV+Vs)/2.
The inherited all-generator representation is a finite sum of

 H_B(X)={V,X}/2-BXB,
 B=B^*, V=ell_s^(-1)(BsB).

Its physical Schrödinger generator is

 L_{B,*}(rho)=(A rho+rho A^*)/2-Krho K^*,
 K=dBd^(-1), A=dVd^(-1).

The Lyapunov identity gives A+A^*=2K^*K. Thus A=K^*K+iQ with Q Hermitian,
and -L_{B,*} is a legal Lindblad generator with Hamiltonian Q/2. This
Hamiltonian is retained; only its entropy trace against log rho vanishes.
H_B(s)=0 yields stationarity. The Heisenberg form is

 L_B(X)=(A^*X+XA)/2-K^*XK.

Both J and E are linear in the generator, so one may prove the theorem
for one such noise and sum. The representation covers all finite KMS
generators, not merely a GNS/covariant subclass.

2. Intrinsic modular-frequency decomposition
--------------------------------------------
Let Omega be the finite real frequency set of ad(U). Decompose

 B=sum_{omega in Omega} B_omega,
 [U,B_omega]=omega B_omega, B_omega^*=B_{-omega}.

The components are spectral blocks of the actual stationary U; no covariance
assumption on the entire generator is made. Arbitrarily many coherent
cross-frequency terms are retained. For indices omega,nu write

 c=(omega+nu)/2, delta=(omega-nu)/2.

The exact drift coefficients are

 V=sum_{omega,nu} [e^c/cosh(delta)] B_omega^*B_nu,
 K=sum_omega e^(omega/2)B_omega,
 A=sum_{omega,nu} [e^nu/cosh(delta)] B_omega^*B_nu.       (1)

Indeed [U,B_omega^*B_nu]=(nu-omega)B_omega^*B_nu,
B_omega^* s=e^omega sB_omega^*, and

 ell_s(B_omega^*B_nu)
       =[1+e^(omega-nu)]sB_omega^*B_nu/2.

This proves V in (1); conjugation by d supplies the additional factor
e^((nu-omega)/2), proving A. Its adjoint coefficient is e^omega/cosh(delta),
so A+A^*=2K^*K follows also from e^omega+e^nu=2e^c cosh(delta).

3. Exact reference-potential coefficient
-----------------------------------------
Using the Heisenberg formula and log sigma=2U,

 L_B(2U)=A^*U+UA-2K^*UK.

In each B_omega^*B_nu term, the coefficient of the rightmost U cancels
because (e^omega+e^nu)/cosh(delta)=2e^c. The remaining coefficient is

 e^nu(nu-omega)/cosh(delta)-2nu e^c
                  =-2e^c[c-delta tanh(delta)].

Consequently the exact operator identity is

 -L_B(log sigma)
   =sum_{omega,nu} 2e^c[c-delta tanh(delta)]B_omega^*B_nu.  (2)

The coefficients are real and symmetric under omega<->nu. No commuting
rho is used. Equation (2) includes the physical Hamiltonian correction via
A and cannot be obtained by discarding it from the generator.

4. Diagonalize only the actual state
------------------------------------
Take a full eigenbasis of rho, rho=sum_a p_a |a><a| with p_a>0, and put

 t_ab=sqrt(p_a p_b), beta_ab=(log p_a-log p_b)/2,
 b_omega^{ab}=<a|B_omega|b>.

All pairs (a,b) below are ORDERED, including a=b, so beta_ab can be negative.
No common eigenbasis of rho and sigma is assumed. The full-state basis can
include arbitrary entanglement with an untouched reference.

For real symmetric coefficient functions, the sum over all pairs/frequencies
can be symmetrized under

 (a,b,omega,nu) -> (b,a,-nu,-omega),
 b_omega^{ba}=conj(b_{-omega}^{ab}).                      (3)

The conjugation and frequency-index swap preserve the quadratic products.
The coefficient symmetry makes this equivalent to simultaneously reversing
the pair and both frequencies. This remains valid for diagonal pairs.

5. Energy kernel
-----------------
From (1), exactly

 E_B=Tr(Vrho)-Tr(Bsqrt(rho)Bsqrt(rho))
   =sum_{a,b,omega,nu}
        [p_b e^c/cosh(delta)-t_ab]
                conj(b_omega^{ab})b_nu^{ab}.             (4)

Average (4) with its reindexed version (3), using
p_a=t_ab e^beta_ab and p_b=t_ab e^(-beta_ab). The coefficient becomes

 t_ab[cosh(beta_ab-c)/cosh(delta)-1].

Define x=beta_ab-omega, y=beta_ab-nu. Their midpoint is beta_ab-c and
half-difference is -delta. Thus

 E_B=sum_{a,b}t_ab sum_{omega,nu}
                 L(x,y)conj(b_omega^{ab})b_nu^{ab},
 L(x,y)=2sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)/cosh((x-y)/2).               (5)

The hyperbolic identity used is
cosh((x+y)/2)-cosh((x-y)/2)=2sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2).
Ordered diagonal pairs in (5) have weight p_a, not a doubled pair weight.

6. Entropy-production kernel
-----------------------------
The Hamiltonian entropy trace vanishes because rho commutes with log rho.
Therefore

 Tr[L_{B,*}(rho)log rho]
  =sum_{a,b}p_b(log p_b-log p_a)|K_ab|^2
  =sum_{a,b,omega,nu}[-2beta_ab p_b e^c]
                             conj(b_omega^{ab})b_nu^{ab}.

After the exact symmetrization (3), its coefficient is

 2t_ab beta_ab sinh(beta_ab-c).                         (6)

For the reference contribution, (2) gives the unsymmetrized coefficient
2p_b e^c[c-delta tanh(delta)]. Its symmetrized coefficient is

 -2t_ab[c sinh(beta_ab-c)
                      +delta tanh(delta)cosh(beta_ab-c)]. (7)

Adding (6),(7), write m=beta_ab-c to obtain

 J_B=sum_{a,b,omega,nu}
  2t_ab[m sinh(m)-delta tanh(delta)cosh(m)]
                             conj(b_omega^{ab})b_nu^{ab}.

But for x=m-delta,y=m+delta,

 K_0(x,y)=[x sinh(y)+y sinh(x)]/[2cosh((x-y)/2)]
         =m sinh(m)-delta tanh(delta)cosh(m).

Hence the exact formula is

 J_B=2sum_{a,b}t_ab sum_{omega,nu}
           K_0(beta_ab-omega,beta_ab-nu)
                            conj(b_omega^{ab})b_nu^{ab}.  (8)

7. Decisive universal factorization
------------------------------------
For lambda=kappa/2 define the full signed Hardy kernel

 K_lambda(x,y)=[x sinh(y)+y sinh(x)
                    -4lambda sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)]
                                        /[2cosh((x-y)/2)]
              =K_0(x,y)-lambda L(x,y).

Equations (5),(8) prove exactly

 J_B-kappa E_B
 =2sum_{a,b}sqrt(p_a p_b) sum_{omega,nu}
     conj(b_omega^{ab})
       K_(kappa/2)(beta_ab-omega,beta_ab-nu)

For each fixed ordered pair, the frequencies give a finite set of arbitrary

The Hardy premise is not merely two-point positivity. Its full-node property
