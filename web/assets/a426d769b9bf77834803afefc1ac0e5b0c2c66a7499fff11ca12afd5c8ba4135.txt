SUFFICIENT ABSTRACT NESTING LEMMA -- PROVED
Assume for n<m, at every j>=max(J_n,J_m), E_j^{(n)} subset E_j^{(m)}. Assume that for either n or m the finite inward graph iteration from terminal graph zero at N converges in operator norm to G_j^{(n)} or G_j^{(m)}, and the graph iteration is exactly the image under T_j of the next graph (as in equation5.12). Then G_j^{(n)} subset G_j^{(m)}.
Proof: At N the terminal spaces are E_N^{(n)} subset E_N^{(m)}. Their exact inward images under T_j ... T_{N-1} are nested for every finite N. Finite graph iterations identify these images with the corresponding graphs. Their graph maps converge in norm at fixed j. If x belongs to G_j^{(n)}, choose x_N in its finite iterate with x_N ->x. Since x_N belongs to the m-th finite graph and the m-th graph maps converge in norm, its low coordinates converge and x belongs to G_j^{(m)}. Thus the inclusion follows. No uniform contraction constant in n is used.
