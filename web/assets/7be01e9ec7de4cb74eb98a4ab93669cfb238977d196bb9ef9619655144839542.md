## Exact proof interface

Let \(\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/d\), \(\mathcal H_0=\{A=A^*:\tau(A)=0\}\), and let
\[
Q=\sum_{i=1}^r w_i|A_i\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A_i|,
\qquad w_i>0,\quad \tau(A_i)=0,\quad \tau(A_iA_j)=\delta_{ij}.
\]
The all-dual gate for this \(Q\) is
\[
S_d(Q)\ge 2\sum_i w_i\tau(A_i\Phi(A_i))-\sum_iw_i.
\tag{1}
\]

Write \(\mathcal B^*:M_d\otimes M_d\to M_d\) for the unital CP adjoint of the supplied broadcaster and set \(\sigma_{AB}=\mathcal B(I/d)\). The two output marginals of \(\sigma_{AB}\) are \(I/d\). A sufficient estimator construction for (1) is a finite POVM \((E_y)\) on \(AB\) and real estimators \(f_i(y)\) satisfying
\[
\mathcal B^*\!\left(\sum_y f_i(y)E_y\right)=\Phi(A_i)\quad\text{for each }i,
\qquad
\sum_iw_i\sum_y\tau(\mathcal B^*(E_y))f_i(y)^2\le\sum_iw_i.
\tag{G*}
\]
Indeed, \(M_y=\mathcal B^*(E_y)\) is an input POVM with the required barycenter. For \(p_y=\tau(M_y)\) and \(t_{iy}=\tau(M_yA_i)\), \(t^2/p\ge 2tf-pf^2\) gives (1). A positive \(AB\) sub-POVM can be completed by its residual effect and assigned zero estimators there; this preserves the first moments and cost. Thus the missing Grothendieck step must yield a legal measurement-estimator pair with the stated quadratic budget. A state or Gram factorization alone is insufficient.

## Random spectral rounding and its exact rank loss

For \(u\) uniform on \(S^{r-1}\), measure the spectral PVM of
\[
G_u=\sum_j\sqrt{w_j}u_j(A_j\otimes I+I\otimes A_j)
=\sum_k\lambda_{u,k}P_{u,k}
\]
on the two outputs, and pull its effects back as \(M_{u,k}=\mathcal B^*(P_{u,k})\,d\nu(u)\). These randomized effects are legal and sum to \(I\). Set
\[
f_i(u,k)=\frac{r u_i\lambda_{u,k}}{2\sqrt{w_i}}.
\]
Since \(\mathbb E[u_iu_j]=\delta_{ij}/r\),
\[
\int\sum_k f_i(u,k)P_{u,k}\,d\nu(u)
=\frac12(A_i\otimes I+I\otimes A_i),
\]
so the pulled-back first moment is \(\Phi(A_i)\), using self-adjointness and the two equal marginals. The continuous mixture can be compressed to finite support by finite-dimensional convex reduction while preserving its moments and cost.

Its quadratic cost is exactly
\[
P=\frac r4\sum_iw_i\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
\sigma_{AB}(A_i\otimes I+I\otimes A_i)^2\right]\le r\sum_iw_i.
\]
The two local second moments are one; state Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the cross term in absolute value by one. Therefore this legal common-POVM construction proves only
\[
S_d(Q)\ge2\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Phi)-r\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q.
\]
The factor \(r\) is the squared cost of recovering all coordinates from one scalar spectral sketch. This route meets (G*) only at rank one; it gives no impossibility result for other collective POVMs.

## Grothendieck and 2-summing bridge audit

Standard noncommutative Grothendieck results control bounded bilinear forms through states and row/column \(C^*\)-norms; Hilbert-factorization products have constant \(2\). Little-Grothendieck gives an upper state domination for a Hilbert-valued map. The Effros--Ruan jointly completely bounded theorem has constant one relative to the **jcb norm**, but still returns states. None of these conclusions directly supplies a positive \(AB\) POVM, common unbiased first moments, barycenter \(I/d\), or the \(-\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q\) correction in (1). The coefficient \(2\) in a bilinear factorization is not the order inequality (1). The theorem-interface audit used [Haagerup (1985)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(85)90026-X), [Haagerup--Musat](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1851), and [de Laat](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.3256); it was not a priority search.

An exact normalization example rejects direct substitution of ordinary 2-summing size for canonical support. Let
\[
A_d=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\sqrt{d-1},-\frac1{\sqrt{d-1}},\ldots,-\frac1{\sqrt{d-1}}\right).
\]
Then \(\tau(A_d)=0\), \(\tau(A_d^2)=1\), and
\[
S_d(|A_d\rangle\langle A_d|)=1,
\]
attained by its spectral PVM. But \(T(X)=\operatorname{Tr}(A_dX):S_1^d\to\mathbb C\) has
\[
\pi_2(T)^2=\|A_d\|_\infty^2=d-1.
\]
The top pure seed is not a legal barycenter-\(I/d\) ensemble at full mass. This is a failure of direct norm-to-support rounding, not a channel counterexample. Exact theorem interfaces, constants, and the barycenter-preserving completion lemma are in work/agents/c9_channel_variance/cycle10/grothendieck/REPORT.md, SOURCE_NOTES.md, and CLAIMS.json.

## Rational qutrit rank-three direction: exact nonviolation

Take \(B_1=X_{01}\), \(B_2=X_{12}\), \(B_3=Y_{12}\), and
\[
Q=\frac32\sum_{j=1}^3|B_j\rangle\langle B_j|.
\]
The \(B_j\) are traceless and pairwise \(\tau\)-orthogonal, with \(\tau(B_j^2)=2/3\); hence \(Q\) has rank three and \(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q=3\). For a pure \(\psi=(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\),
\[
\frac32\sum_j\langle\psi|B_j|\psi\rangle^2
\le6|\beta|^2(1-|\beta|^2).
\]
Every rank-one POVM with barycenter \(I/3\) has mean \(|\beta|^2=1/3\). Jensen therefore bounds unrestricted canonical support by \(4/3\). The four states \((1,s,t)/\sqrt3\), \(s,t\in\{\pm1\}\), with effects \(3P_{s,t}/4\), attain it. Thus \(S_3(Q)=4/3\) globally over all POVMs.

For every positive symmetric Choi extension with one-body marginals \(I/3\), rational diagonal potentials
\[
Y=\operatorname{diag}(237,439,158)/1000,\qquad
Z=\operatorname{diag}(123,554,157)/1000
\]
satisfy
\[
Y\otimes I\otimes I+I\otimes Z\otimes I+I\otimes I\otimes Z-K\succ0,
\]
where
\[
K=\sum_j\left(B_j^T\otimes B_j\otimes I+B_j^T\otimes I\otimes B_j
-(B_j^2)^T\otimes I\otimes I\right).
\]
The exact 27-pivot rational LDL certificate gives
\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q(2\Phi-I))\le\frac{1251}{1000}<\frac43.
\]
It only rules out this one \(Q\)-direction, and the dual needs neither marginal-channel self-adjointness nor Hilbert--Schmidt positivity. The exact support proof and extension-side certificate are in work/agents/c9_channel_variance/cycle10/legal_counterexample/REPORT.md and exact_certificate.py.

A rational qutrit universal-cloner fixture is legal and has \(\Phi(A)=5A/8+\operatorname{Tr}(A)I/8\), with channel-side value \(3/4\) on this \(Q\). The approximate SCS point had a slightly negative Choi eigenvalue and is screening only; Clarabel failed. The exact dual, not the approximate SDP, certifies safety for this frame.

The rank-one scalar square identity cannot be summed: canonical support is nonadditive, already for qubit \(X,Z\), whose separate supports are one while the support of their sum is one. The qutrit dual rejects one rank-three direction but says nothing about other \(Q\).

A future counterexample needs both the legal extension and a complete barycenter-constrained support certificate. Finite scans, sampled pure states, unconstrained Gram moments, or source-oracle-free assumptions do not substitute for these objects.
