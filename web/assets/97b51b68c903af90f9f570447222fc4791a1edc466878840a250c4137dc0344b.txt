1. EXACT FINITE INTERFACE

Let H be finite dimensional, x have a probability prior, and rho_x be
trace-one positive operators with mean sigma>0. Suppose [rho_x,sigma]=0.
Let Phi be a CPTP map from H to H, rho'_x=Phi(rho_x), xi=Phi(sigma), and

  e=int (1/2)||rho_x-rho'_x||_1 dx.

Write E=supp(xi). The ADDITIONAL output-reference contract is

  [xi,sigma]=0, [xi,L_x]=0, [xi,rho'_x]=0,
  L_x=sigma^(-1/2)rho_x sigma^(-1/2).                    (1.1)

In particular E commutes with L_x. These conditions are not asserted for
an arbitrary noncovariant channel. Section 5 proves that a group orbit on a
direct sum of statistical irreducible carriers can legally be covariantly
twirled and output-pinched to satisfy them without increasing maximum
quantum branch dimension or reconstruction error.

For any Kraus representation Phi(X)=sum_i A_i X A_i^dag put

  B_i=A_i sqrt(sigma),
  sum_i B_i^dag B_i=sigma, sum_i B_i B_i^dag=xi.          (1.2)

For every supplied scalar A>0 define the bounded effect

  G_x=L_x(A+L_x)^(-1), 0<=G_x<=I.

Then the exact conclusion is

  A sum_i int||[G_x,B_i]||_HS^2 dx <=(5/2)e,
  e>=(2A/5)sum_i int||[G_x,B_i]||_HS^2 dx.             (1.3)

There is no L_max, condition number or dimension factor in (1.3). The
field G is a public test field determined by the source ensemble, not an
input-dependent register supplied to the archive. Maximum branch Q only
enters through rank B_i<=Q. No reference-preservation or sector-preservation
assumption is made. The map may move mass between arbitrary irreducible
sectors, and xi may have zero sectors.

2. EXACT RESOLVENT JENSEN DEFICIT

All inverses involving xi are on E. Every B_i has output range in E,
because sum_i B_i B_i^dag=xi. Define

  K_i=xi^(-1/2)B_i:H->E, sum_i K_i K_i^dag=I_E,
  Psi(X)=sum_i K_i X K_i^dag,
  L'_x=xi^(-1/2)rho'_x xi^(-1/2)=Psi(L_x).

By (1.1), L'_x commutes with xi; also rho'_x=xi L'_x on E. Set

  Z=A+L_x, Z'=A+L'_x=Psi(Z),
  R=Z^(-1), R'=Z'^(-1),
  C_i=R'K_i-K_i R.

Direct expansion, using RZ=ZR=I and sum K_i K_i^dag=I_E, gives

  sum_i C_i Z C_i^dag=Psi(R)-R'.                       (2.1)

This proves the inverse Jensen inequality and supplies its exact positive
remainder. Define the operator-convex scalar function and proper value

  h_A(t)=t^2/(A+t)=t-A+A^2/(A+t)=t-A[t/(A+t)],
  V_in(x)=Tr sigma h_A(L_x)=Tr rho_x G_x,
  G'_x=L'_x(A+L'_x)^(-1),
  V_out(x)=Tr xi h_A(L'_x)=Tr rho'_x G'_x.

Trace preservation cancels the linear and constant terms in h_A, because
Tr sigma=Tr xi=Tr rho_x=Tr rho'_x=1. Cyclic trace and (1.2) give

  J_x:=V_in(x)-V_out(x)
       =A^2 Tr xi[Psi(R)-R']
       =A^2 sum_i Tr xi C_i Z C_i^dag >=0.              (2.2)

Since Z>=A I_H, and xi commutes with G'_x,

  G'_x B_i-B_i G_x=-A sqrt(xi) C_i,
  J_x>=A sum_i||G'_x B_i-B_i G_x||_HS^2.              (2.3)

All expressions involving xi are restricted to E, but V_in and sigma
remain on the WHOLE input H. Thus no target mass outside E was discarded.
One may extend G'_x by zero off E; rows of every B_i there already vanish.

3. PROPER QUADRATIC RISK AND THE ERROR BOUND

For Hermitian H on E let

  q_x(H)=2Tr H rho'_x-Tr H^2(rho'_x+A xi).

The unique optimizer on E is G'_x, since
(rho'_x+A xi)G'_x=rho'_x and these two operators commute. Completing the
square by cyclic trace, without assuming H commutes with rho'_x, gives

  V_out(x)-q_x(H)
    =Tr(rho'_x+A xi)(G'_x-H)^2 >=0.                   (3.1)

Use H=E G_x E. Since E and xi commute with G_x, it is harmless to write
G_x in every xi-weighted term. Let

  R_x:=V_out(x)-q_x(G_x)
       >=A Tr xi(G'_x-G_x)^2.                         (3.2)

Equivalently, R_x is the trace Bregman remainder
Tr xi[h_A(L'_x)-h_A(L_x)-h'_A(L_x)(L'_x-L_x)], with
h'_A(L_x)=2G_x-G_x^2; equation (3.1) verifies the claimed trace identity
even when L_x and L'_x do not commute. No general noncommutative Bregman
inequality is imported here.

Input optimality gives Tr rho_x G_x=q_in(G_x), so direct subtraction yields

  J_x+R_x
   =Tr(2G_x-G_x^2)(rho_x-rho'_x)
                          +A Tr G_x^2(xi-sigma).       (3.3)

The first test is an effect: 0<=2G_x-G_x^2<=I. For t>=0,

  A[t/(A+t)]^2<=t/4,

because At/(A+t)^2<=1/4. Since int L_x dx=I_H, functional calculus and
integration therefore give

  0<=A int G_x^2 dx<=I_H/4.                           (3.4)

Convexity of half trace gives T(xi,sigma)<=e. Both state differences in
(3.3) are trace zero; the range of each effect is what sets the half-trace
normalization. Consequently

  0<=int(J_x+R_x)dx<=e+e/4=(5/4)e.                    (3.5)

Finally,

  [G_x,B_i]=(G_x-G'_x)B_i+(G'_x B_i-B_i G_x),
  sum_i||(G_x-G'_x)B_i||_HS^2=Tr xi(G_x-G'_x)^2.

The Hilbert-space squared triangle bound and (2.3),(3.2) yield

  A sum_i||[G_x,B_i]||_HS^2<=2(J_x+R_x).

Integration proves (1.3), including all constants. A singular xi requires
no continuity limit or free target crop: the preceding E-restricted proof
has already included all input target mass in (2.2),(3.3).

4. RANK, POSITIVE MASS AND RECTANGULAR TRANSFER

Every archive through conditional quantum registers of dimension <=Q has
a composite rank-<=Q Kraus representation: an encoding Kraus H->C^Q and
a decoding Kraus C^Q->H compose to rank<=Q. Unlimited classical outcomes
produce convex mixtures of this same rank cone. In finite dimensions the
Choi Schmidt-number-<=Q cone is closed, so a finite rank-Q Kraus refinement
exists even for a continuous-outcome archive. This is a representation of
the total channel, not a claim that its original alphabet was finite.

Use the SAME two positive moduli for each B_i,

  F_(i,1)=|B_i|/sqrt(2), F_(i,2)=|B_i^dag|/sqrt(2).

Then rank F_z<=Q, sum_z||F_z||_HS^2=1, and exactly

  sum_z F_z^2=(sigma+xi)/2.                            (4.1)

For every Hermitian field H_x the elementary SVD commutator identity gives

  sum_z int||[H_x,F_z]||_HS^2
                           <=sum_i int||[H_x,B_i]||_HS^2. (4.2)

Thus (1.3) and (4.2) simultaneously retain the resolvent score, the original
thermal score and any convexly mixed cold score. This is not a choice of
an energy-minimizing modulus that loses TP mass. Keeping all right moduli
alone would preserve sigma exactly, but need not decrease the energies;
choosing favorable orientations separately need not preserve mass. The
paired construction satisfies both constraints.

If P is a direct-sum sector projection commuting with every G_x, then

  ||[G_x,P F_z P]||_HS^2<=||[G_x,F_z]||_HS^2.

Every full source/output rectangle remains in the latter energy. Projection
is only permitted after the desired variational mass has been established

conditioning of the experiment on a constant-probability carrier subset.

A reusable thermal-band step is as follows. Suppose a vacuum rectangle
has likelihood eigenvalues L/A in [u_-,u_+] with 0<u_-<=u_+<infinity.
Put nu=u_-/(1+u_+)^2>0. Since

  d[x/(1+x)]/d(log x)=x/(1+x)^2>=nu,

entrywise squared resolvent commutators dominate nu^2 times squared LOG

  int_u||G_lambda,u b-b G_mu,u||_HS^2
   >=(nu^2 beta^2/3)sum_a||J_lambda,a b-b J_mu,a||_HS^2. (4.3)

This holds for unequal spins and every rectangular b. No equality of scalar

Scalar shifts are dropped only AFTER the Haar average. The application

5. LEGAL COVARIANCE CONTRACT AND CHARGED RESOURCES

Suppose the source is a compact-group orbit and H is a direct sum of its
inequivalent statistical irreducible carriers, with parameter-independent
multiplicities already traced and regenerated exactly. The source mean
sigma is scalar on every carrier. Average a competing code over conjugate
input/output actions of one common group element, retaining that element
as a classical label for the decoder, and pinch output into the carrier
blocks. The target is already block diagonal. Convexity and trace-norm
contraction show that neither operation increases worst-case orbit error;
Haar average error is preserved or decreased as well. Every conjugated,
pinched or fallback composite Kraus map still has rank <=Q. This is legal
for the lower bound, which admits arbitrary separately charged classical
labels. It is not a finite-alphabet upper or a claim that a frame is free.

The resulting Phi is covariant and has block-diagonal outputs. Its xi is
scalar on each carrier by irreducibility, hence commutes with sigma, every
target likelihood and every rho'_x. This proves (1.1). The positive scalar
sector weights in sigma may be unequal; xi need not equal sigma and may
have zero sectors. All cross-sector Kraus rectangles are retained.

For a representation with nontrivial unremoved statistical multiplicities,
covariance alone need not make xi central. One must establish (1.1) or an
alternative version before applying this theorem. No theorem for a wholly
arbitrary noncommuting mean/reference ensemble is asserted.

6. WHY A NAIVE CLIPPED SELF-SCORE IS NOT A PROOF

Replacing L by a bounded G in the old quadratic self-score changes its
target from sigma L to sigma G. Reconstruction accuracy for rho_x gives
no accuracy promise for that artificial family. In particular the mixed
commutator sum Re<[G,B],[L,B]> expands into BOTH

  Tr G Phi(sigma L) and Tr L Phi(sigma G),

not just the actual reconstruction score. The second output is unavailable
from the specified experiment. Discarding it would be a false identity.

The resolvent construction repairs this obstruction using the exact inverse
Jensen deficit and the output proper quadratic risk together. Their sum

The reference correction has the bounded averaged coefficient (3.4).
This is why (1.3) is valid without a clipped-likelihood reconstruction
promise or constant-mass conditioning.
