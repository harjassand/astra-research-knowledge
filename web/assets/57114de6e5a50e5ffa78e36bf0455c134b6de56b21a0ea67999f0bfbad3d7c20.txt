1. Constant cap by taking m/g→242

Keep q=1024, α=34, β=40.53, ρ_−=39.05239901777024556… and ρ_+=40.05676868555972676… from v1. Choose prime m_i with m_i/g_i→242. This exceeds d_i/g_i→74.53, so degree-m_i evaluation remains injective on L(D_i). The field E_i=F_{q^{m_i}} still has exactly the three proper subfields not contained in F_q:

  K_1=F_{2^{m_i}},  K_2=F_{2^{2m_i}},  K_5=F_{2^{5m_i}}.

The v1 selected-place estimate gives a place v_i with

  Z(v_i) ≤ 12d_i n_i^3 q^{−m_i/2}
        ≤ q^{(3ρ_+−242/2+o(1))g_i}
        = q^{(−0.8296939433…+o(1))g_i} → 0.

Since Z(v_i) is a nonnegative integer, Z(v_i)=0 for large i. If a K_j-affine coset intersects B_i in t points and h=|K_j∩F_q|=2,4,32, then t>h would supply t(t−1)(t−h)>0 nonconstant-ratio incidences, contradicting Z(v_i)=0. Hence

  |B_i∩(a+ξK_j)| ≤ h_j,  h_1=2, h_2=4, h_5=32,

for every a∈E_i and ξ∈E_i^×. For K⊂F_q, a+ξK has at most |K|≤1024 points. Thus the full finite field has the uniform proper-affine-subfield bound

  max_{K⊊E_i} max_{a∈E_i, ξ∈E_i^×} |B_i∩(a+ξK)| ≤ 1024.       (5)

The cardinality rate becomes

  ρ_−/242=0.1613735… ≤ liminf (log_{Q_i}n_i),
  limsup (log_{Q_i}n_i) ≤ ρ_+/242=0.1655238…,

so Q_i^0.16≤n_i≤Q_i^0.17 eventually. The source growth estimates remain |B_i+B_i|≤n_i^1.89 and |B_iB_i|≤n_i^1.91. This is the price of obtaining the fixed cap (5).

2. An orbit transversal meeting the Li–Roche–Newton hypothesis

The construction has F_q^×B_i=B_i. It contains no zero: P_i and G_i consist of nonzero sections, their products are nonzero, and evaluation is injective on L(D_i). The F_q^× action on E_i^× is free, so choose a transversal R_i⊂B_i with exactly one element from each scalar orbit. Then

  |R_i|=n_i/(q−1)=n_i/1023.

For every subfield K⊂F_{q_i}=E_i and every c∈E_i^×:

• If K⊂F_q, then |R_i∩cK|≤1. Indeed, for x,y∈cK, x/y∈K^×⊂F_q^×, so x and y belong to the same F_q^× orbit.
• If K=K_j, then |R_i∩cK_j|≤|B_i∩(0+cK_j)|≤h_j. For all sufficiently large i, h_j≤|K_j|^{1/2}.
• If K=E_i, then |R_i∩cE_i|=|R_i|≤Q_i^{0.17}<Q_i^{1/2}.

Consequently R_i satisfies the exact multiplicative-coset hypothesis of Li and Roche-Newton, “An improved sum-product estimate for general finite fields,” arXiv:1106.1148v1 (31 May 2011), Theorem 1.4: for every subfield K⊂E_i and c∈E_i, |R_i∩cK|≤|K|^{1/2}. The source theorem states

  max(|R_i+R_i|,|R_iR_i|) ≫ |R_i|^{12/11}/(log_2|R_i|)^{5/11}.

Our upper estimates give |R_i+R_i|≤1023^{1.89}|R_i|^{1.89} and |R_iR_i|≤1023^{1.91}|R_i|^{1.91}; these are fully compatible with that lower bound. Thus the theorem supplies a rigorously nonconcentrated large subfamily of the low-growth construction, not a contradiction to finite-field sum-product estimates.
