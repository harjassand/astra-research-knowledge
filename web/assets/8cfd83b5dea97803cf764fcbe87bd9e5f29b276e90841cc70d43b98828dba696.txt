c07_l07 PHASE 3 -- STOPPED Feynman--Kac WEIGHT MOMENTS AND A FINITE-BIT GATE
8 October 2026. INITIAL is unchanged. This is an attributed post-freeze audit
of the c07_s02 stopped law, not an implementation or an external review.

RESULT

For the c07_s02 qubit law, the crude pathwise bound w<=exp(h_N), h_N=O(sqrt N),
can be improved for the *actual stopped initial-seed law*: every fixed positive
integer moment E[w^p] is bounded by an explicit constant depending only on
p,M,B, uniformly in all sufficiently large N and all admitted A,b. The proof
uses the N^(-1/4)-scale seed's quartic tail and the N^(-1/2)-scale martingale
displacement in scaled Bloch coordinates. It does not use the rare-exit
probability as a proxy for a weight moment.

Together with the stopped Duhamel residual, this gives a constant lower bound
on the exact stopped normalizer. A certified truncation at a computable cap T
then has exponentially small weighted bias, while log T can be chosen
O_{M,B}(sqrt(log(1/epsilon))+log(1/epsilon)/N). This closes the *weight and
normalizer* gate. A finite-bit strong/weak simulator for the stopped SDE,
including first-hit handling, remains an explicit residual gate; no total
polynomial-time compiler is claimed here.

1. LAW AND CONSTANTS

Use exactly the c07_s02 law in revisions/STOPPED_DIFFUSION.txt (S2)--(S12):
M,B are fixed nonnegative public bounds, ||A||<=M, |b|<=B,
Lambda=M+1, C=A+Lambda I, Lc=2M+1, Tc=Tr C<=3Lc,
s=N^(3/4), r0=1/(4 sqrt(Lc+1)), and t0=2/N-Lambda/s^2. The seed is the
heat-seed Bloch law with the bad event |m|>r0/2 replaced by m=0. Starting from
that modified seed, m_t follows (S3)--(S4)'s SDE up to its first hit tau of
|m|=r0; after tau both m and

                 w_t=exp(integral_0^(t wedge tau) V_N(m_u)du)

are frozen. Let W=w_1 and X_t=N^(1/4)m_(t wedge tau), 0<=t<=1.

The following explicit conservative thresholds suffice. Set

  aw=(Lc+B)/4,  cw=(Tc+B)/4,  dbar=5Tc/16+B/2,
  delta=1/2-log(cosh 1)>1/16,
  Lseed=(7/3) exp(-Lambda/2-1/12),
  I4=1536^(3/4) Gamma(3/4)/4,
  S(D)=1+exp(1/2+384 D^2) I4/Lseed.

For a fixed integer p>=1 put Dp=3p aw/2. It is enough to require

  N>=Lambda^2,
  N>=36 p aw Tc,
  N>=Ndrift:=ceil(max(1,(12Tc)^2,18Tc,(12B/rlo)^2)),
  rlo=1/[8(M+1)].                                                (W1)

All thresholds are rationally upper-enclosable from rational M,B and the
supplied A; for a threshold uniform over every admitted A, replace Tc by
3Lc. Also rlo<=r0, so N>=Ndrift implies the c07_s02 drift condition
beta_N<=r0/8 in (S8). The moment constant is uniform in A,b because
Tc<=3Lc; one may replace cw by (3Lc+B)/4 and dbar by 15Lc/16+B/2.

The auxiliary constants have finite rational certificates. For example,
cosh(1)<2768657/1794240<2435873/1572864<exp(7/16), using the positive
Taylor sum through degree four for the last exponential and a geometric
bound on the remaining cosh series. Hence delta>1/16. Also
Gamma(3/4)<4/3+exp(-1)<11/6<2 and 1536^(3/4)<256, so I4<128. Therefore

  S(D)<Sbar(D):=1+(384/7)exp(Lambda/2+7/12+384D^2),
  Cseed<Cbarseed:=(384/7)exp(Lambda/2+7/12).

Using these and the replacements Tc<=3Lc, cw<=(3Lc+B)/4,
dbar<=15Lc/16+B/2 gives a rational-input upper Vbar_p for Vp using only
certified scalar exp/square-root enclosures. The claimed bound does not
require evaluating Gamma, logcosh, or any exact real comparison.

For a single threshold uniform over all admitted A, it suffices to use
N>=max{(M+1)^2,108 p aw Lc,
       ceil(max(1,(36Lc)^2,54Lc,(96B(M+1))^2))}.
A convenient explicit uniform rational moment bound is

  Vbar_p=exp[p(3Lc+B)/4+3p aw(15Lc/16+B/2)^2]
          sqrt(7[1+(384/7)exp((M+1)/2+7/12+384Dp^2)]).

It is enough to use a rational upper enclosure of this expression. The formula
(W2), which retains Tc, is slightly sharper when the supplied A is known.

2. MOMENT LEMMA

Under (W1), uniformly in N,A,b and with no dependence on an SDE time mesh,

  E W^p <= Vp := exp(p cw+3p aw dbar^2) sqrt(7 S(Dp)).              (W2)

Proof. Write X for the stopped scaled path and Z=sup_(t<=1)|M_t| for its
scaled martingale part. By (S8),

  sup_t |integral_0^(t wedge tau) N^(1/4) a_N(m_u)du| <= dbar.

Indeed the left side is at most Tc r0/(2 N^(1/4))+
3Tc r0/(4 N^(5/4))+B/(2 sqrt(N)); use r0<=1/4 and N>=1.
Since D_C<=Tc I on the stopped ball, each coordinate of the scaled
martingale has quadratic variation at most Tc/(2N). A union bound over the
three coordinates and the two signs, followed by the exponential-martingale
maximal inequality, gives

  P(Z>x)<=6 exp[-N x^2/(3Tc)].                                    (W3)

Consequently, for a< N/(3Tc), integration of this tail yields

  E exp(a Z^2)<=1+6a/(N/(3Tc)-a).

At a=6p aw, (W1) makes this at most 7.

For the seed, let R=N^(1/4)r be the radial field variable in (S7)'s heat
identity, so |m|=tanh(r/2) and |X_0|<=R/2. On a retained seed,
|m_0|<=r0/2 implies r/2<=atanh(r0/2)<1. With
f(u)=u^2/2-logcosh(u), f(u)>=u^4/48 for u<=1 and sinch(u)<=exp(u^2/2),
the scaled, unnormalized radial density is at most

  exp(1/2) R^2 exp(-R^4/768).

Its normalizer is at least Lseed by integrating R in [1,2]. Split the
modified seed into its bad atom at zero and its retained good part. The atom
contributes at most 1; on the good part the quartic envelope above applies.
For any D>=0, completing the square gives

  E exp(4D |X_0|^2) <= 1+exp(1/2+384D^2) I4/Lseed = S(D).          (W4)

Indeed |X_0|<=R/2, and
-R^4/768+DR^2<=-R^4/1536+384D^2; integrate the final quartic term to get
I4. The exterior branch is the zero atom, not a retained seed with large R,
so no tail bound for its original radius is needed in (W4). In particular
(W4) with D=Dp is the required E exp(6p aw |X_0|^2) bound.

Finally, from (S4), |m|<=r0<1 and C>=0,

  |V_N(m)| <= aw |X|^2+cw.

Here the quadratic term is at most Lc |X|^2/4, the trace correction at most
Tc/4, and (B/2)|X|<=B(1+|X|^2)/4. Also
sup_t |X_t|^2<=3(|X_0|^2+dbar^2+Z^2). Thus

 W^p <= exp(p cw+3p aw dbar^2)
          exp(3p aw |X_0|^2) exp(3p aw Z^2).

Cauchy--Schwarz, (W3)--(W4), and the just-established exponential moments
give (W2). This proof controls the complete path weight, including stopped
paths. In particular it does not multiply a small exit probability by the
worst-case exp(h_N) envelope.

3. NORMALIZER AND IMPORTANCE-RESAMPLING CONSEQUENCES

Let mu=E W=Tr W_1. The c07_s02 stopped Duhamel certificate (S11) gives

  |Tr W_1-Tr(e^(M_H)rho_0)|<=Delta_N,
  Delta_N=2 exp(h_N) p_bad
             +6 h_N exp(2h_N-kappa_1 N^(3/2)).

The target trace is at least 1. Hence, whenever the displayed c07_s02
thresholds hold and a certified upper bound for Delta_N is <=1/2,

  1/2<=mu<=sqrt(V2).                                             (W5)

The lower bound is a consequence of the *weighted Duhamel residual*; it does
not follow from the exit probability by itself. It also supplies a directly
usable normalizer gate.

For R iid exact stopped paths, sample an index with probabilities
W_i/sum_j W_j and emit its product state k_N(m_i). Its averaged output state
has trace distance at most sqrt(V2)/(mu sqrt(R))<=2sqrt(V2/R) from
W_1/Tr W_1. To see this, for any effect F put f_i=Tr(F k_N(m_i)) in [0,1],
and compare the selected expectation E[mean(Wf)/mean(W)] with E[Wf]/mu;
the absolute difference is at most E|mean(W)-mu|/mu. This is an expectation
over the algorithm's randomness, not a high-probability guarantee for each
sampled branch. Thus R>=4V2/epsilon_samp^2 suffices for expected error
epsilon_samp before numerical errors.

The exact sample normalizer also has a polynomial-confidence estimator.
For W_T=min(W,T), Var(W_T)<=V2. A median of k independent block means, each
of size n>=8 V2/rho^2, estimates E W_T to additive rho with failure at most
exp(-k/4); k>=4 log(1/zeta) gives confidence 1-zeta. This is O(V2 rho^-2
log(1/zeta)) path samples. Its bias for mu is handled by the following cap.

4. CERTIFIED WEIGHT TRUNCATION

Set Cseed=exp(1/2)I4/Lseed. The retained-seed envelope above implies

  P(|X_0|>x)<=Cseed exp(-x^4/96).

Combining this with (W3) and the drift displacement gives, for x>=2 dbar,

  P(X_* > x)<=Cseed exp(-x^4/24576)
                    +6 exp(-N x^2/(48Tc)),
  X_*:=sup_(t<=1)|X_t|.                                         (W6)

Since log W<=cw+aw X_*^2, put L=log(T)-cw. If L>=4 aw dbar^2, then

 E[W 1_(W>T)] <= sqrt(V2) [sqrt(Cseed) exp(-L^2/(49152 aw^2))
                         +sqrt(6) exp(-N L/(96 Tc aw))].          (W7)

This is Cauchy--Schwarz applied to (W6), using (W2) at p=2. It is a
weighted-tail estimate, stronger than merely bounding P(W>T). Given
epsilon_tail>0, any certified L satisfying

 L>=max{4 aw dbar^2,
        aw sqrt(49152 [log(2 sqrt(V2 Cseed)/epsilon_tail)]_+),
        (96 Tc aw/N)[log(2 sqrt(6V2)/epsilon_tail)]_+}

makes the right side of (W7) <=epsilon_tail. Here [x]_+=max{x,0}.
Choose a dyadic T>=exp(cw+L) by certified rational upper bounds. For fixed
M,B, log T=O(1+sqrt(log(1/epsilon_tail))+log(1/epsilon_tail)/N). In
particular, polynomial target accuracy needs only polylogarithmic (indeed
O(sqrt(log N)) for epsilon_tail=N^-k) weight-register bits; target
epsilon_tail=exp(-cN) needs O(sqrt N) bits. Neither case requires the
exp(h_N) worst-case weight range in the *register width*.

Let sigma_T=E[W_T k_N(m_1)]/E W_T. By positivity,

 T(sigma_T,W_1/Tr W_1)
   <= E[(W-T)_+]/mu <= 2 epsilon_tail                       (W8)

whenever (W5) holds. Thus E W_T>=1/2-epsilon_tail>=1/4 for
epsilon_tail<=1/4. Also E W_T^2<=V2. Weighted resampling from R capped
proposals has averaged error at most 4 sqrt(V2/R) from sigma_T. Choosing
epsilon_tail<=epsilon/4 and R>=64 V2/epsilon^2 therefore gives expected
error <=epsilon to the stopped mixture, before SDE and arithmetic errors.

5. FINITE-PRECISION ACCEPTANCE GATE; WHAT REMAINS OPEN

The weight/normalizer part of a finite-bit compiler can be certified without
paying exp(h_N) precision if its stopped-path routine supplies the following
coupling for one proposal. Couple its exact capped pair (U,rho) and returned
pair (Utilde,rhotilde), where U=min(W,T) and Utilde is also clipped at T.
Certify a bad-coupling probability <=alpha; on the good event certify
|log Utilde-log U|<=eta_w and T(rho,rhotilde)<=eta_state. Because U,Utilde<=T
and E U>=1/4, the normalizer and normalized *population* mixture discrepancy
is at most

  (exp(eta_w)-1)+exp(eta_w) eta_state+8 T alpha.                  (W9)

Proof: on the good event the weight ratio lies in [exp(-eta_w),exp(eta_w)];
the unnormalized scalar and trace-norm errors are respectively at most
(exp(eta_w)-1)E U and
[(exp(eta_w)-1)+2 exp(eta_w)eta_state]E U. On the bad event each clipped
weight is at most T, so the corresponding errors are at most 2T alpha.
Normalize using E U>=1/4. In particular, the absolute scalar normalizer
error is at most (exp(eta_w)-1)E U+2T alpha, and (W9) bounds its relative
error and the normalized population-mixture error. This is a complete
sufficient gate, rather than an inference from escape probability.

There are two distinct finite-sample gates. For a reported estimate of the
normalizer, also require T^2 alpha<=V2. Then
E Utilde^2<=exp(2 eta_w)V2+T^2 alpha<=(e^2+1)V2 when eta_w<=1. A median of
means on the computed capped weights estimates their mean to absolute rho
with confidence 1-zeta using O(V2 rho^-2 log(1/zeta)) paths. Add the population
bias from (W9) and the cap bias epsilon_tail to compare it with mu.

For the actual R-proposal categorical resampler, couple the R exact and
computed proposal pairs independently. A union bound makes the probability
of any bad pair at most R alpha. If every pair is good, per-weight log errors
<=eta_w change the normalized categorical probabilities in TV by at most
(exp(2 eta_w)-1)/2; replacing the selected product by its computed endpoint
costs at most eta_state. Thus an additional finite-batch error gate is

  R alpha+(exp(2 eta_w)-1)/2+eta_state+epsilon_select,             (W10)

where epsilon_select is any charged dyadic/integer-selection error. This
batch bound is not inferred from (W9); it compares the two finite R-batches.

For endpoint error, if Xtilde=N^(1/4)mtilde satisfies
|Xtilde-X_1|<=2 eta_state/N^(3/4), tensor telescoping gives
T(k_N(mtilde),k_N(m_1))<=eta_state. Weight clipping and logweight evaluation
are stable to the eta_w tolerance. To keep (W9)<=epsilon_fp, it suffices,
for example, to require eta_w<=epsilon_fp/8, eta_state<=epsilon_fp/8,
alpha<=epsilon_fp/(32T), with epsilon_fp<=1/4. To keep (W10)<=epsilon_fp
one can instead require eta_w<=epsilon_fp/16, eta_state<=epsilon_fp/4,
alpha<=epsilon_fp/(4R), and epsilon_select<=epsilon_fp/4. For both gates,
take the smaller alpha requirement and also enforce T^2 alpha<=V2 when a
normalizer estimator is needed. Its bit precision has
log(1/alpha)=O(log T+log R+log(1/epsilon_fp)).

This makes the residual interface precise: a finite-bit stopped-SDE simulator
must prove a coupling with these eta_w, eta_state, alpha tolerances and give
its step count/bit cost, including Brownian truncation, first-hit/stop
resolution, and path-integral quadrature. The required log(1/alpha) is only
O(log T+log(1/epsilon_fp)); the endpoint precision has O(log N+log(1/eta_state))
bits. The current artifact does not prove that the path simulator attains
these bounds in polynomial time. Coefficient smoothness and a known PSD margin
on the stopped ball alone do not establish that claim.

An end-to-end gate for target error epsilon is therefore:
  (i) certify the c07_s02 Delta_N bound <=epsilon/8 and (W1) for p=2;
  (ii) compute V2, choose T with epsilon_tail<=epsilon/16 in (W7), and verify
       the clipped normalizer lower bound;
  (iii) choose R>=1024 V2/epsilon^2, making the exact capped resampling error
        <=epsilon/8;
  (iv) certify (W10)<=epsilon/8 and local one-qubit preparation errors
       summing to <=epsilon/8.
The first two gates are proved analytically here. The last two are conditional
on an unimplemented stopped-path sampler. Consequently there is not yet a
finite-bit polynomial-runtime theorem.

6. CROSS-PHASE COMPARISON AND STATUS BOUNDARY

The key scale is specific to c07_s02's critical qubit model and its heat seed:
in X=N^(1/4)m coordinates, the seed has a uniform quartic tail, the stopped
drift displacement is O(1) (in fact tends to zero), the martingale variance is
O(1/N), and V_N is bounded by cw+aw|X|^2. This yields (W2) and (W7).

This does not automatically transfer to c04_s01 FULL_RANK_STOPPING (T10--T14).
There h_N=2 alpha(d-1)(N+d)/d+BN=O(N) at fixed d,alpha,B. T11's
unweighted logdet exit probability is exp[-N L_N^2/O(alpha)], but T13/T14
still multiply by exp(h_N); no weight moment or normalized stopped-law
normalizer bound follows from the exit estimate alone. The direct stopped
construction c04_s02 QUDIT_POSITIVITY_LOCALIZATION (T14) likewise leaves
finite-bit path/weight/normalizer errors open and uses an O(N) potential
envelope for fixed fields. c05_s01 03_su_q_casimir_stopped_filter (G10--G12)
has h_N=Lc alpha N(q-1)(N+q)/(2q)+uBN/sqrt(2); when gamma=0, its field term
is O(N), and its author explicitly leaves weighted SDE simulation open.
Those variants need their own typical-moment or truncation proof. The present
lemma is not evidence for a generic stopped-diffusion compiler.
