1. EXACT IMPORTED INTERFACE

U has n>=1 explicitly enumerated coordinates. mu is nonzero on a-element
subsets and nu on b-element subsets, a+b=n. Their nonnegative multiaffine
generating polynomials are log-concave on the positive orthant. Inputs are:
  - exact value oracles mu(S), nu(T);
  - separately positive initial supports S0, T0;
  - supplied upper bounds R_mu,R_nu>=1 on max/min POSITIVE coefficients;
  - rational relative error 0<rho<=1.
The result is an estimate of
  Zcap = sum_S mu(S) nu(U\S),
with relative error rho and success at least 3/4. The homogeneous arithmetic
and oracle-call bound is, on EVERY execution,
  O(n^10 B^2 rho^-2 log^2(2B/rho)), B=n+ln(R_mu R_nu).
Rational parameters and polynomial-time oracles returning uniformly polynomial
length rationals give polynomial BIT time on every execution. It is enough to
provide coarse coefficient-range bounds; no optimal range acquisition occurs.

If Zcap=0, every execution outputs zero, including bad statistical histories
and coin-cap aborts. This is a support-preservation fact, not an estimate that
distinguishes an exponentially small positive number from zero. For the N63
coherent compiler, deterministic matroid-intersection zero detection is still
the proper separate primitive: a positive instance's bad FPRAS tape can also
output zero and therefore the FPRAS output alone cannot certify feasibility.

2. RECONSTRUCTION OF THE LOAD-BEARING MIXING ARGUMENT

The implemented soft distribution is on PAIRS of original bases:
  pi_(t,z)(S,T) proportional to mu(S) nu(T) t^(S intersect T) z^S z^T,
where the vector penalty satisfies 0<t_u<=1 and z_u>0. To prove its gap, extend
both base measures downward by
  muhat_(lambda z)(R) = (lambda z)^R sum_(A superset R) mu(A),
and similarly nuhat. On the doubled Boolean cube let
  w(R,Q)=muhat(R)nuhat(Q), psi(R,Q)=product_(u in R intersect Q)t_u.
The common support is a Cartesian product of downward-closed families, hence
connected through the empty pair. There is no hidden support connectivity
assumption at this step.

On a cube state X, use birth b_i(X)=w(X+i)/w(X), and death
d_i(X)=psi(X-i)/psi(X), omitting infeasible births. Detailed balance follows
directly: pi(X)b_i(X)=w(X+i)psi(X)/Z=pi(X+i)d_i(X+i).
Let L be the positive Laplacian. The finite-cube identity is

  <f,(L^2-L)f>_pi
   = E_pi[(grad+ f)^T A^(w,0) (grad+ f)]
    +E_pi[(grad- f)^T D^(psi,1)(grad- f)]
    +(1/Z) sum_(X,i!=j absent, X+i+j supported)
             w(X+i+j)psi(X) [Delta_ij f(X)]^2.                  (I)

Here upper A has diagonal b_i^2 and off-diagonal
b_i b_j-w(X+i+j)/w(X). Lower D has diagonal d_i^2-d_i and
off-diagonal d_i d_j-psi(X-i-j)/psi(X).

Independent algebra check of (I): expanding <Lf,Lf> gives a quadratic form
in the first differences. Its diagonal, minus <f,Lf>, equals the upper/lower
curvature diagonals by edge detailed balance. For an ordered pair i!=j,
the remaining four corners X,X+i,X+j,X+i+j have coefficients
  +c,-c,-c,+c, c=w(X+i+j)psi(X)/Z.
Pairing the unchanged first difference along its own coordinate gives
c*(Delta_ij f)^2. Unsupported corners have zero coefficient; when the bottom
and top corners are supported, the intervening corners are supported because
the support is downward closed. Thus the regrouping is valid at boundaries.
This supplies the proof algebra in the special case actually used here.

Both curvature matrices are PSD:
  - For w, coefficient ratios at X come from a coordinate derivative of the
    log-concave product polynomial f(x)g(y), evaluated at a positive point.
    Multiaffine derivative closure follows directly from
      partial_i p(x)=lim_(h->infinity) p(x+h e_i)/h.
    The upper matrix is minus the log Hessian of that derivative, with positive
    diagonal rescaling from lambda z. Thus A^(w,0)>=0.
  - For psi, two present copies of a coordinate contribute exactly
      (1-t_u)/t_u^2 * [[1,1],[1,1]]
    to D^(psi,1); a single present copy contributes zero. Other-coordinate
    off-diagonals vanish. These blocks are PSD even for unequal penalties.

Identity (I) therefore gives <Lf,Lf>>=<f,Lf>. L is finite, reversible and
irreducible, so on a nonconstant eigenvector its eigenvalue ell obeys
ell^2>=ell and ell>0, hence ell>=1. This is the uniform continuous-time gap.

To return to original bases, take the trace chain on pairs of total cardinality
n. Its stationary conditional distribution is exactly pi_(t,z), independently
of lambda. A harmonic extension of a function on this trace set gives
  E_full(h,h)=pi(trace) E_trace(f,f),
  Var_full(h)>=pi(trace) Var_trace(f),
so the gap-one Poincare bound passes to the trace chain.

When a basis element u is deleted, its deletion rate is
t_u^(-1[u in the OTHER set]). At the resulting one-element-deficient pair,
the total insertion rate is proportional to lambda and positive (reinserting
u is feasible), whereas all further deletion rates stay bounded. Thus the
first insertion returns to a pair of bases with probability 1-O(1/lambda).
Its insertion law is exactly proportional to z_v mu(S-u+v). Letting lambda
increase gives the exchange rate

  q((S,T),(S-u+v,T))
   = t_u^(-1[u in T]) z_v mu(S-u+v)
        / sum_(w notin S-u) z_w mu(S-u+w).                     (II)

All denominators are strictly positive because the reinsertion-u term is
z_u mu(S)>0. The nu moves are symmetric. The finite-dimensional Dirichlet
forms converge, so (II) inherits gap at least one.

CRUCIAL COST DISTINCTION: lambda->infinity and the translated supersets sums
are proof devices. The algorithm never evaluates those sums, never simulates
a huge-lambda chain, and never pays 1/min mu. It runs (II) using at most n exact
ORIGINAL coefficient queries per move.

For uniform t, total deletion rate is at most n/t. A lazy discrete chain chooses
a present copy uniformly, accepts its deletion with probability t/2 or 1/2
according to whether its other copy is absent/present, and reinserts using (II),
including self reinsertion. Its gap is >=t/(2n). The least stationary weight is
bounded below by
  t^min(a,b)/(4^n R_mu R_nu) * (z_min/z_max)^n.
The usual finite reversible-chain L2 bound therefore yields polynomial mixing
time in 1/t, n, log(R_mu R_nu), log(z_max/z_min), and log(1/tau).

3. WHY A RARE HARD INTERSECTION BECOMES FREQUENT

Set y=log z and
  Phi(y)=log Z_(t,exp y)-sum_u y_u,
  G=1_S+1_T-1.
Then grad Phi=E G, Hess Phi=Cov G>=0 and every coordinate second derivative
is <=1, since each G_u lies in [-1,1]. For one coordinate let p_j be its
probability of appearing j times, j=0,1,2. Then
  partial_u Phi=p2-p0.

Assume p0p2>0, temporarily remove that coordinate's penalty (t_u'=1) and
multiply z_u by s=sqrt(t p0/p2). The tilted probabilities satisfy
  (q0,q1,q2) proportional to (p0, s p1, p0), hence q0=q2<=1/2.
Use the indicator g=1[coordinate absent]. An exchange modifies only one set;
it cannot move directly from two copies to zero. From a one-copy state its
total rate of removing the unique copy is <=1 (this follows directly from
(II), because the deleted coordinate is not in the other set). Detailed balance
thus bounds the indicator Dirichlet form by q1. Gap one gives
  q0(1-q0)<=q1, so q1/q0>=1/2.
Consequently
  p0p2<=4t p1^2,
  p0+p2<=|p2-p0|+2sqrt(p0p2)<=|partial_u Phi|+4sqrt(t).
Union over coordinates gives
  P(not complementary)<=n*(||grad Phi||_infinity+4sqrt(t)).   (III)

This proof specifically needs the gap for *unequal* penalties and all positive
fields: the balancing tilt leaves the original equal-penalty family. Section
5.1 really proves that stronger uniform statement, so there is no omitted
uniformity premise. The endpoint p0p2=0 is immediate and needs no division.

Choose t=1/(256n^2) and find ||grad Phi||_infinity<=1/(4n). Equation (III)
then gives P(complementary)>=1/2. No inverse Zcap occurs.

4. BOUNDED COORDINATE DESCENT, WITHOUT A MINIMIZER PROMISE

Write M=max mu * max nu and B0=2n+ceil(log_2(R_mu R_nu)). If Zcap>0,
every complementary pair contributes its unchanged weight to exp Phi(y), so
  Phi(y)>=log Zcap>=log(M/(R_mu R_nu)),
  Phi(0)<=log(4^n M),
hence the total potential drop is <=B0. No minimizing y has to exist.

For target eta=1/(4n), batch fresh approximate gradient samples, stop when the
empirical infinity norm is <=eta/2, otherwise move against one large coordinate.
Take a fixed step eta/16<=s<=eta/8 and cap the number of updates at
  K=ceil(128 B0 eta^-2)+1.
With all batches accurate to eta/4, each update drops Phi by at least eta^2/128,
so K updates would contradict the lower bound. The cap applies on every
history, including Zcap=0 and arbitrarily bad statistical replies. Thus
  ||y||_1<=Ks=O(nB0)
holds deterministically. Hoeffding batches with O(n^2 log(nB0)) samples give
O(n^4 B0 log B0) total gradient samples. Sampling each within 1/(64Q) total
variation couples the approximate and exact adaptive runs with total additional
failure at most 1/64. The initial empirical-error allocation is another 1/64.
Combining with the bounded-field mixing cost gives
O(n^10 B0^2 log B0) arithmetic/oracle work.

RATIONAL IMPLEMENTATION: choose s=log(1+1/(32n)) and maintain
  z_u=(1+1/(32n))^k_u, sum_u |k_u|<=K=O(n^2 B0).
There is no exp/log elementary-function oracle in the implemented updates.
The field numerators/denominators have O(n^2 B0 log n) bits under direct rational
storage. Fixed deterministic loop caps, rather than a numerical stopping promise,
are what make shared fixed tapes and reversible compilation legitimate.

5. COUNTING SELF-REDUCTION, SUPPORT, AND RATIONAL COINS

For ANY field returned (good or bad), the weight W(S,T) is an exact rational
product of original coefficients, fields, and t powers. The identity
  Zcap= Z_(t,z)/z^U * P_(t,z)(complementary)
holds exactly. Estimate the soft normalizer by a standard majority-path
self-reduction on its 2n membership bits; estimate complementary probability
by a separate batch. On a good field, the latter probability is >=1/2, so
additive O(rho) estimation is relative O(rho).

Here is an explicit way to fill in the self-reduction initialization detail.
At each stage draw independent supported samples from the current conditional
law, estimate the next-bit frequency, choose its empirical majority, and choose
one sample with that bit as the next conditional starting witness. The chosen
frequency is >=1/2 on EVERY execution, hence denominators never vanish and a
witness always exists. After all bits are fixed, evaluate the final supported
weight once and divide by the product of the selected empirical frequencies.
If each frequency is within alpha=O(rho/n) of its true conditional frequency,
each chosen true probability stays >=1/2-alpha, and the relative product error
is O(n alpha). Hoeffding plus conditional sampling bias O(alpha) needs
O(n^3 rho^-2 log(n/delta)) samples in total. This gives the source's Lemma 3.1
cost without a supplied exponentially rare path, or a hard-intersection witness.

Every restricted soft conditional is still a pair of homogeneous measures on
the original ground set: inclusion is x_i partial_i, exclusion is x_i=0.
Multiaffinity gives derivative closure by the limit above; boundary limits and
the concavity of log x_i handle the remaining operations. Degrees a,b stay
fixed, coefficient values/ranges are restricted, and the witness supplies
nonzero initial support of both factors. The exchange sampler still applies.
An infeasible individual factor is excluded before invoking the theorem.

All Markov choices are ratios of sums of <=n rational weighted oracle values.
Each is nonnegative and its denominator is nonzero. Categorical choices can be
made by boundedly many exact Bernoulli choices. Compare fair-coin binary digits
of U~Uniform[0,1] with an exact rational threshold p: after r digits the decision
can remain unresolved only in the one length-2^-r interval containing p.
Abort the ENTIRE count and return zero if any choice exceeds
ceil(log_2(64N)) bits, where N is a known polynomial bound on all binary choices.
The union failure cost is <=1/64 and the tape length is deterministically
polynomial. A capped run either follows the ideal supported chain exactly or
returns zero; it never produces an invalid-support sample.

Oracle output lengths, field lengths, sums, empirical frequencies, and final
products all have polynomial bit lengths. Median amplification uses a fixed
O(log(1/beta)) number of independent padded repetitions. Therefore the amplified
count program is total and uniformly polynomial on every prefix and every tape.
The source's pre-coin-cap homogeneous success is >=15/16, leaving ample margin
for the extra 1/64 failure; its stated 3/4 import is conservative.

6. N63 INPUT AND CONDITIONING CHECK

For explicit full-column-rank A in Q(i)^(m x r), disjoint blocks E_j and quotas
sum q_j=r,
  f(x)=det(A*diag(x)A)=sum_(|S|=r)|det A_S|^2 x^S,
  g(x)=product_j e_(|E_j|-q_j)(x_Ej).
The matrix inside f is positive definite for positive x, so logdet concavity
establishes log-concavity. Each block elementary symmetric polynomial is the
uniform-matroid base polynomial and log-concave; disjoint products preserve it.
Complementary degrees r,m-r and the coefficient identity [x^U]fg=Z are exact.
Complex A causes no sign problem here: |det A_S|^2 is a nonnegative RATIONAL.

An acquired common denominator D clears every real/imaginary entry. Every
positive squared minor is >=D^-2r, while
  |det A_S|^2 <= (r!)^2 (max_entry_modulus)^(2r).
Both logarithms and exact determinant numerator/denominator lengths are
polynomial in the explicit input length L,m. This supplies a uniform original
range bound; conditioning never makes it larger. Absolute norm/projection
probability and matrix condition number are unnecessary promises.

For forced-in I / forced-out J, f uses those restrictions and g uses the
complementary restrictions (J in, I out). A common witness is found by ordinary
matroid intersection between the row linear matroid of A and the quota partition
matroid, with I contracted and J deleted. Exact rank arithmetic in Q(i) has
polynomial bit cost. Dependence in I, excess quotas, or insufficient remaining
rank are deterministic empty cases. An acquired common witness also initializes
both counting factors. Full column rank of A alone does not imply projected Z>0.

The full S proof's shared-depth-tape compiler consequently has the claimed
*counting* contract. Fixing its tapes produces a total deterministic finite-bit
program; abort outputs and fallback branches do not leave unknown runtime loops.
The source arithmetic count composed with relative tolerance epsilon/(16m) and
per-child failure delta*epsilon^2/(64m^2) is
  O(m^13 B^2 epsilon^-2 log^2(2Bm/epsilon) log(m/(delta epsilon))),
plus acquired oracle, exact support/rank, bit-arithmetic, reversible-history,
and gate-synthesis costs. This is not a practical quantum resource estimate.
The coefficient oracle must be an explicit coherent-computable program, as it
is here; opaque independence or classical value-oracle access alone would not
give the quantum conclusion. The separately audited weighted-prefix hybrid,
determinant phase and rotation synthesis are not replaced by this counting audit.

7. ATTACKS / ACTUAL CHECKS / LIMITS

Falsification attempts addressed:
  * hidden exponentially small overlap probability: defeated by (III), not by
    running the equal-field penalty longer;
  * dependence on unknown minimum positive coefficient: replaced by supplied
    log range and, for A, an acquired Gaussian-integer denominator bound;
  * nonattaining convex minimizer: not required by the capped descent proof;
  * unbounded exact rational random duration: source explicitly caps digits;
  * bad tapes causing infeasible samples / division by zero: support-preserving
    transitions, majority-path witnesses and whole-count aborts cover them;
  * conditioned degrees changing / zero polynomial: forced variables retained,
    empty factors rejected; exact support acquisition precedes coherent use;
  * free evaluation of translated supersets sums / huge lambda: neither is run;
  * full-rank input silently implying nonzero projection: explicit counterexample.

PRIOR ART / NEXT DECISIVE QUESTION

The determinant-plus-matroid norm count is already Chen–Liu Corollary C.13,
and recursive probabilities-to-amplitudes are already Grover–Rudolph. This audit
adds source-depth/internal verification, not a new counting theorem or novelty
claim. Root should retain the distinction between an internally reconstructed
fresh public proof and external specialist acceptance. The useful next step is
a bounded implementation of the homogeneous field/count routine with exact
rational oracles and logged deterministic caps, then comparison against exact
small-instance norms on adversarial rare-projection families. A 65-page source
survey or more finite identities will not supply that operational evidence.
