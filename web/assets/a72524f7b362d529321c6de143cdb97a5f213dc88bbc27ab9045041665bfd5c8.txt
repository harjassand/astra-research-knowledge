2. THE PHYSICAL SPIN COROLLARY

Let N>=1 physical sites carry arbitrary finite spins j_i=p_i/2>=0, with
integer p_i>=0 and local dimension d_i=p_i+1. Put

 M=sum_i p_i=2sum_i j_i, J=sum_i S^(i).

If M>0, finite alpha>=0 and finite h, then the physical WHOLE Gibbs state

 rho_phys proportional to exp[(alpha J^2-delta J_z^2)/N+hJ_z]

is FULLY SEPARABLE across the N physical sites whenever

 delta <= 2N log2/M = N log2/(sum_i j_i).                (V1)

Negative delta is included. If every j_i=0, the physical Hilbert space
is one-dimensional and the conclusion is trivial; no division by M is
taken. N=1 is a single site and is separable for all finite parameters,
so the displayed sufficient condition is unnecessary in that case.

For identical j>0, the sufficient range is

 delta <= log2/j.                                      (V2)

No balance condition among the spins is required. Uniform collective
coupling and a common axial field are required. This statement does not
assert optimality for j>1/2, nonuniform pair couplings, nonaxial quadratic
tensors, nonuniform fields inside the Gibbs exponent, or negative alpha.
For j=1/2 it recovers the input qubit universal floor. The higher-spin
best universal threshold remains UNKNOWN.

3. EXACT VIRTUAL REPRESENTATION, INCLUDING THE DENOMINATOR

Partition M virtual qubits into the specified blocks of sizes p_i. Let
P_i be the symmetric projector on block i, and P=tensor_i P_i. Choose
the standard isometry U_i:C^(p_i+1)->Sym^(p_i)(C^2), taking spin magnetic
weight j_i-k to the normalized p_i-qubit Dicke vector with k ones.
For p_i=0 this is the one-dimensional identity. Write U=tensor_i U_i.
Then U^dagger U=I_phys and UU^dagger=P. The exact SU(2) intertwining is

 J_q,a U=U J_a, a=x,y,z.

This follows directly from the spin ladder coefficients in normalized
Dicke states, or from the local Sym^p representation. Each P_i commutes
with its block spin components; P therefore commutes with every collective
J_q,a, its Casimir and the axial polynomial. The virtual Hamiltonian

 K_q=(alpha J_q^2-delta J_q,z^2)/N+hJ_q,z

reduces the range of P, with U^dagger K_q U=K_phys. Since P commutes,
no complementary-sector term enters the square. Functional calculus gives

 U^dagger exp(K_q)U=exp(K_phys),
 P exp(K_q)P=U exp(K_phys)U^dagger.                       (V3)

To apply the L=M qubit theorem, rewrite the SAME virtual operator as

 K_q=(alpha_q J_q^2-delta_q J_q,z^2)/M+hJ_q,z,
 alpha_q=(M/N)alpha, delta_q=(M/N)delta.                  (V4)

Alpha_q>=0, h is unchanged, and V1 is exactly delta_q<=2log2.
There is no replacement of physical N by M without changing couplings.

Let Z_q=Tr_q exp(K_q), Z_phys=Tr_phys exp(K_phys). A physical successful
block projection has its actual trace probability

 s=Tr(P rho_q)=Z_phys/Z_q>0.

Its normalized physical output is EXACTLY

 U^dagger P rho_q P U/s=exp(K_phys)/Z_phys=rho_phys.       (V5)

The dimensional or postselection normalization is not discarded.

4. WHY THE LOCAL PROJECTION PRESERVES SITE SEPARABILITY

Take any fully separable virtual representation
rho_q=sum_l w_l tensor_(i,r) sigma_(l,i,r), with w_l>=0. Blocking factors
first yields a product across the N physical blocks. For each block put

 A_(l,i)=U_i^dagger[tensor_(r=1)^p_i sigma_(l,i,r)]U_i>=0,
 t_(l,i)=Tr A_(l,i).

Then U^dagger P rho_q P U=sum_l w_l tensor_i A_(l,i). If a product trace
vanishes, omit that zero term. Divide by the actual positive s to obtain
weights w_l product_i t_(l,i)/s and normalized local factors A_(l,i)/t_(l,i).
This is an explicit fully separable physical representation. Entanglement
among virtual qubits within one block has no bearing on site separability.
The projection acts locally with respect to PHYSICAL sites, even though
it is not a product of individual-qubit projections.

This proves V1 using the imported qubit FULLSEP premise. It does not
require or infer a virtual iid representation. Physical local factors
and weights generally depend on the block content and may differ by site.
