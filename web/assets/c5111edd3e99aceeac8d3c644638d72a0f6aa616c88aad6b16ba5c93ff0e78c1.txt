1. FROM THE FULL-SEP PROMISE TO A POSITIVE BERNSTEIN MEASURE

rho is supported on the symmetric subspace. Refine a finite full-separable
decomposition into positive pure product projectors (existence only). Since
Tr(P_sym_perp rho)=0, every positive-weight product vector lies in Sym^n.
For n>=2, invariance of a simple tensor under each swap forces its local
vectors to be proportional. Hence every vector is phi^tensor n. n1 is
trivial. Thus the symmetric full-separable state is an iid PURE mixture.

Average this mixture under common axial phase rotations. rho itself is
Dicke diagonal, so this does not change it. A local pure state with up
probability p has phase-averaged populations
B_nk(p)=binom(n,k)p^k(1-p)^(n-k). Therefore a positive probability measure
mu on[0,1] exists with pi_k=integral B_nk(p)dmu. This witness is not
acquired; the following moment transform eliminates that requirement.

2. UNIVERSAL REGULARIZATION AND TRUE MOMENTS

Set eta=eps/8 and tau_sym=P_sym/(n+1). Its Dicke populations are1/(n+1),
and integral_0^1 B_nk(p)dp=1/(n+1). Hence it is the coherent Haar average
corresponding to the uniform p-measure dp. Define
 rho_reg=(1-eta)rho+eta tau_sym,
 mu_reg=(1-eta)mu+eta dp.
mu_reg is a probability measure and mu_reg>=eta dp, even if mu is atomic.
Also T(rho_reg,rho)=eta T(tau_sym,rho)<=eta. There is no dimension factor.

The ordinary moments are acquired from populations by
 u_l=integral p^l dmu=sum_(k>=l) [binom(k,l)/binom(n,l)] pi_k.
Indeed binom(n,k)binom(k,l)=binom(n,l)binom(n-l,k-l), and the remaining
binomial sum is one. All transform coefficients lie in[0,1]. Thus
 u_l^reg=(1-eta)sum_(k>=l)[binom(k,l)/binom(n,l)]pi_k+eta/(l+1),
 u_0^reg=1 EXACTLY.
These are ORDINARY moments, not the homogeneous q_k=pi_k/binom(n,k).

5. CONSTRUCTIVE GAUSSIAN/RADAU RULE AND FINITE ACQUISITION

proves e0^T J^l e0=u_l through2m; K supplies2m+1. Cyclicity proves distinct
interior eigenvalues and positive spectral weights at e0. This is the
positive Gaussian rule, derived without a root/witness oracle.

one unknown last entry with v^T A^-1 v. The completed K is EXACT PSD rankm.
The EXISTING true next moment would only increase that bottom entry, so

one node0, and positive interior nodes/weights. An unknown next moment is
not acquired. These arguments still work when the ORIGINAL measure was a
