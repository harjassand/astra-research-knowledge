# Critical SU(2) mean-field Gibbs states at fixed higher spin

Status: independently derived internal candidate, not externally reviewed. This is a cross-model transfer of the positive heat/filter mechanism. It makes no novelty, optimality, finite-bit compiler, or hardware claim.

## Model and claim

At each of `N` sites take the irreducible spin-`s` space, for fixed `s` in `{1/2, 1, 3/2, ...}`. Let `S_a` be its Hermitian generators, `J_a=sum_i S_a^(i)`, and `J^2=sum_a J_a^2`. Put

`v_s=s(s+1)/3`, `a_s=1/(2v_s)=3/(2s(s+1))`, and `q=N^(3/4)`.

For real symmetric `A` with operator norm at most `M`, and `b` with Euclidean norm at most `B`, consider

`K_N = a_s J^2/N + Q_A(J)/N^(3/2) + b·J/N^(3/4)`,

where `Q_A(J)=sum_ab A_ab (J_a J_b+J_b J_a)/2`. The coefficient `a_s` is the critical quadratic coefficient: the normalized local character has expansion `log(Tr exp(r S_z)/(2s+1))=v_s r^2/2+O(r^4)`, and `1/(4a_s)=v_s/2` cancels this quadratic term in the positive heat representation.

For every `N` with `t0=a_s/N-M/N^(3/2)>=0`, the construction below gives a fully separable `sigma_N` satisfying the explicit finite-sector certificate (definitions below)

`T(exp(K_N)/Tr exp(K_N), sigma_N) <= E_N`.

For each fixed spin `s` and fixed finite `M,B`, `E_N <= C(s,M,B) N^(-3/2)` for all sufficiently large `N`. The full finite-`N` expression for `E_N` is exact apart from its elementary exponentials; the asymptotic constant is explicit in the one-variable character bounds `c_s,C_s` defined below. The spin-1 specialization has elementary rational bounds `c_1=1/240`, `C_1=1/9`.

## Positive heat/filter construction

Set `C=A+M I`, so `0<=C<=2M I`, choose an orthonormal eigenbasis `n_i` with eigenvalues `lambda_i`, and set `F_i=n_i·J`, `c_i=lambda_i/N^(3/2)`. Then

`K_N=t0 J^2+H`, `H=Q_C(J)/N^(3/2)+b·J/N^(3/4)=sum_i c_i F_i^2+b·J/N^(3/4)`.

Use the positive SU(2) heat identity

`exp(t J^2)=int exp(y·J) nu_t(dy)`,

`nu_t(dy)=(4 pi t)^(-3/2) exp(-t/4-|y|^2/(4t)) [sinh(|y|/2)/(|y|/2)] d^3 y`.

On each spin-`j` irrep, angular averaging reduces the integral to the character `sinh((j+1/2)r)/sinh(r/2)` and the radial Gaussian integral gives the eigenvalue `exp(t j(j+1))`. Hence the identity holds on every finite SU(2) representation, including `(C^(2s+1))^tensor N`. For real `y`, `exp(y·J)=exp(y·S)^tensor N`; after normalization each integrand is an identical-site product state. This heat identity is the standard representation-theoretic input, not a novelty claim.

Define the positive linear generator

`L(X)={H,X}/2 - sum_i c_i [F_i,[F_i,X]]/4`.

For each `i`, `exp(delta L_i)(X)=E_Z exp(sqrt(c_i delta/2) Z F_i) X exp(sqrt(c_i delta/2) Z F_i)`, with `Z` standard normal; the field part is the deterministic sandwich by `exp(delta b·J/(2q))`. Each factor is an identical-site product filter. Trotter products applied to `exp(t0 J^2)` are positive mixtures of product states, and their finite-dimensional limit remains in the closed fully separable cone. Set `W_N=exp(L)(exp(t0 J^2))`, `sigma_N=W_N/Tr W_N`.

## Explicit finite-`N` certificate

Let `mu_(N,s)(m)` be the coefficient of `z^m` in `(sum_(r=-s)^s z^r)^N`, and let `m_(N,s)(j)=mu_(N,s)(j)-mu_(N,s)(j+1)`. This is the exact multiplicity of total spin `j`; allowed `j` values start at `0` or `1/2` according to parity and increase by one up to `Ns`. Define

`w_j=(2j+1)m_(N,s)(j) exp(a_s j(j+1)/N)`,
`x_j=j(j+1)/N^(3/2)`, `ell_j=j/N^(3/4)`,
`P_j=2M x_j+(B/2)ell_j+(2M x_j+B ell_j)^2/3`,
`Z_-=sum_j w_j exp(-M x_j)`,
`U=sum_j w_j exp(M x_j+B ell_j) P_j`.

Then

`E_N=(3M/(2N^(3/2))) U/Z_-`.

Derivation: on spin `j`, `||H_j||<=2M x_j+B ell_j`. Rotating `H_j` around `F_i` gives `D1_i=||[F_i,H_j]||<=2M x_j+B ell_j` and `D2_i=||[F_i,[F_i,H_j]]||<=4M x_j+B ell_j`; the scalar `M J^2` part of `C` has zero rotational derivatives. Duhamel and the exponential-simplex identity give

`||[F_i,[F_i,exp(uH_j)]]||_1 <= (2j+1) exp(u||H_j||) [u D2_i+u^2 D1_i^2]`.

Integrating `u` and using `sum_i c_i=Tr(C)/N^(3/2)<=6M/N^(3/2)` gives the block error coefficient `(3M/(2N^(3/2))) P_j`. Since `Tr_j H_j=Tr(C) j(j+1)(2j+1)/3N^(3/2)>=0`, trace Jensen yields `Tr_j exp(H_j)>=2j+1`; thus the target normalization is at least `Z_-`. Normalizing positive operators turns the raw trace-norm error divided by the target partition function into the displayed trace-distance bound. No inverse success probability is used.

The multiplicities are exactly computable by the recurrence `mu_0(0)=1`, `mu_(n+1)(m)=sum_(r=-s)^s mu_n(m-r)`. For fixed `s`, this is polynomial work in `N` using integer arithmetic. The reported bound is a certificate formula; it does not claim an implemented state sampler.

## Uniform critical rate

Let `phi_s(r)=(2s+1)^(-1) sum_(m=-s)^s exp(mr)` and `delta_s(r)=v_s r^2/2-log(phi_s(r))`. The exact character is `phi_s(r)=sinh((s+1/2)r)/[(2s+1)sinh(r/2)]`. The fourth cumulant of the uniform weights `m=-s,...,s` is

`kappa_4=-s(s+1)(2s^2+2s+1)/15`,

so `delta_s(r)/r^4 -> -kappa_4/24>0` at zero. Also `delta_s(r)/r^2 -> v_s/2` at infinity. It is positive for every `r>0`: with `f(x)=x coth x`, the derivative of `log phi_s` is `[f((s+1/2)r)-f(r/2)]/r`; `g(x)=f(x)-x^2/3` is strictly decreasing since

`g'(x)=-[x cosh(2x)+2x-(3/2)sinh(2x)]/[3 sinh^2 x] < 0`,

and the bracket has derivative `2 sinh(2x)(x-tanh x)>0` and value zero at zero. It follows that `delta_s'(r)>0`. Thus the explicit one-variable constants

`c_s=inf_(r>0) delta_s(r)/min(r^4,r^2)>0`,
`C_s=sup_(0<r<=1) delta_s(r)/r^4<infinity`

are well-defined and can be certified from this elementary character function.

Write `Z_(N,s)(t)=Tr exp(t J^2)`. The heat identity gives its radial integral with integrand

`r^2 sinh(r/2)/(r/2) exp[-N delta_s(r)+beta_(N,alpha) r^2]`,

when `t=a_s/N+alpha/N^(3/2)`, where

`beta_(N,alpha)=v_s^2 alpha sqrt(N)/(1+alpha/(a_s sqrt(N)))`.

For `alpha=M+2`, this is at most `v_s^2(M+2) sqrt(N)`. On `r<=1`, put `R=N^(1/4)r`; the quartic lower bound gives the finite integral

`J_s(M)=int_0^infinity R^2 exp(-c_s R^4+v_s^2(M+2)R^2) dR`.

On `r>=1`, for `sqrt(N)>=6v_s^2(M+2)/(5c_s)`, the exponent is at most `-c_s N r^2/6`; using `sinh(r/2)/(r/2)<=exp(r^2/24)` and `N>=1/(2c_s)`, the tail integral is at most `(sqrt(pi)/4)(12/c_s)^(3/2) N^(-3/2)`. Therefore the `alpha=M+2` radial integral is at most

`N^(-3/4) [exp(1/24) J_s(M) + (sqrt(pi)/4)(12/c_s)^(3/2)]`.

For `alpha=-M`, `sqrt(N)>=2M/a_s` gives `|beta|<=2v_s^2 M sqrt(N)`. Restricting the integral to `R in [1,2]`, where `N delta_s(r)<=16 C_s`, lower-bounds it by `(7/3) exp(-16 C_s-8v_s^2 M) N^(-3/4)`. The heat-kernel prefactor ratio is at most one, so

`Z_(N,s)(a_s/N+(M+2)/N^(3/2)) / Z_(N,s)(a_s/N-M/N^(3/2)) <= R_s(M)`,

`R_s(M)=(3/7) exp(16 C_s+8v_s^2 M) [exp(1/24)J_s(M)+(sqrt(pi)/4)(12/c_s)^(3/2)]`.

This ratio controls `U/Z_-`: its numerator is bounded by `2 C0 exp(B^2/4) Z_(N,s)(a_s/N+(M+2)/N^(3/2))`, and its denominator is `Z_(N,s)(a_s/N-M/N^(3/2))`. Here `ell_j<=sqrt(x_j)`, `B sqrt(x)<=x+B^2/4`, and `P_j<=C0(1+x^2)` for `C0=2M+B/2+2B^2/3+8M^2/3`, with `1+x^2<=2exp(x)`. Thus

`U/Z_- <= 2 C0 exp(B^2/4) R_s(M)`.

Consequently

`E_N <= 3 M C0 exp(B^2/4) R_s(M) N^(-3/2)`

whenever `N>=max(16, 1/(2c_s), (6v_s^2(M+2)/(5c_s))^2, (2M/a_s)^2)`. This constant is intentionally conservative but explicit through one-dimensional integrals and character bounds. A computable upper bound for `J_s(M)` follows by completing a square:

`J_s(M) <= exp(v_s^4(M+2)^2/(2c_s)) (1/4)(2/c_s)^(3/4) Gamma(3/4)`.

### Spin-1 explicit constants

For `s=1`, `phi_1(r)=(1+2 cosh r)/3`, `v_1=2/3`, `a_1=3/4`. Direct differentiation gives

`delta_1'(r)=2[r(1+2cosh r)/3-sinh r]/(1+2cosh r)`.

Its numerator has positive series coefficients `(4k-1)/(3(2k+1)!)` on `r^(2k+1)`, `k>=1`. For `r<=1`, this gives `r^4/60<=delta_1(r)<=r^4/9`; for `r>=1`, `delta_1(r)>=r^2/240` (use `sinh r/(1+2cosh r)<1/2` after `r=2`). Hence `c_1=1/240` and `C_1=1/9` are valid explicit choices. The rate above applies to a qutrit spin-1 model with critical term `3J^2/(4N)`.

## Runnable diagnostic and boundaries

`spin1_certificate.py` computes the exact integer spin-1 multiplicities and the finite-sum certificate for bounded `N`; it checks `sum_j(2j+1)m_N(j)=3^N` and reports `N^(3/2) E_N`. For `M=1,B=0.5`, the binary64 diagnostic printed `U/Z_- = 2410.33,3371.11,4456.60,5559.27,6584.94,7474.17` at `N=16,32,64,128,256,512`, respectively. The increase is consistent with a very large quartic-exponential constant and is not used as evidence for the asymptotic theorem. This is arithmetic/finite-sum evidence only. No full density matrix, interval sampler, native qutrit preparation, gate compilation, or hardware run was executed.

The proof is an SU(2) representation transfer. It does not transfer automatically to a general `SU(d)` Casimir, noncollective/local-lattice Hamiltonians, reducible local representations with different critical cumulant signs, or growing `s,M,B`. The exact heat integral is established prior machinery; the possible contribution is this explicit separability construction and error accounting for fixed higher-spin critical perturbations. The 1991 Fannes–Kossakowski–Verbeure article reports critical-fluctuation/Ginzburg–Landau results for spin-1/2 models; its full proof was not inspected here, and priority remains open.
