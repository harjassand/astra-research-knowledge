an actual positive, tracial, selfcompatible channel, but its Liouville trace
exceeds the upper bound for the specified canonical corrected-instrument
closure. The peer explicitly proves this is a representation obstruction,
not an EB-lifting counterexample, and shows cloner powers become EB-close.
This confirms that the random-unitary 4^-m contraction in my INITIAL is
properly scoped and cannot be promoted to the entire channel class.

qubit channel has no CPTP C*C factorization. These examples rule out using
fixed-point erasure or universal C*C factorization as a global attack. They
do not challenge the instrument SOS or produce an EB obstruction.

c05_l08, c05_l10). Their constructions require supplied sign anchors,
