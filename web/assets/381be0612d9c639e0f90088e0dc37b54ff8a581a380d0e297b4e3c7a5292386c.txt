# Cycle 4 counterexample search: bounded Holevo, small pair broadcasting, and global EB error

Date: 2026-10-08

## Status

**UNKNOWN; no qualifying counterexample found.** In particular, this cycle has not produced a sequence (E_q) with all three certified properties

\[
 C(E_q)\le C_0<\infty,\qquad b_2(E_q)\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad \inf_q e_{\rm EB}(E_q)>0.
\]

The two most useful new checks are (i) a dimension-growing noncommuting product family with an exact uniform Holevo bound and an explicit full-domain broadcaster, for which the known channel bounds do not yield vanishing error at fixed signal strength, and (ii) a trace-radius EB decoder showing that signal-strength dilution cannot maintain an EB error floor. The established flagged constructions and the N93 Pauli/Clifford witness still fail one of the required limits. These are candidate-specific obstructions; they do not prove the global implication.

## Interface

For finite-dimensional input algebra \(\mathcal A_q=B(\mathcal H_q)\), let \(E_q\) be a set of density operators and use

\[
T(\rho,\sigma)=\tfrac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1,
\quad C(E_q)=\sup_\pi\chi(\pi,E_q),
\]

\[
b_2(E_q)=\inf_{\mathcal B_q:\,\mathrm{CPTP}\,\mathcal A_q\to\mathcal A_q\otimes\mathcal A_q}
 \sup_{\rho\in E_q}\max_{i=1,2}T((\mathcal B_q)_i(\rho),\rho),
\]

\[
e_{\rm EB}(E_q)=\inf_{\mathcal M_q:\,\mathrm{EB}\,\mathcal A_q\to\mathcal A_q}
 \sup_{\rho\in E_q}T(\mathcal M_q(\rho),\rho).
\]

The broadcaster is one CPTP map on the entire input matrix algebra for each \(q\); its two outputs may be correlated. The EB infimum includes every full-algebra POVM followed by arbitrary preparation. There is no locality, runtime, or reference-state restriction in either infimum.

## Fresh noncommuting product test

This family tests whether a growing multiplicity of individually weak, incompatible signals can keep EB error visible while the pair-cloning cost vanishes. For \(q\) qubits, let each coordinate label be \(x_i\in\{+X,-X,+Z,-Z\}\), where \(P_{x_i}\) is the corresponding signed Pauli matrix, and define

\[
 \rho_x^{(q)}=\bigotimes_{i=1}^q\frac{I+a_qP_{x_i}}2,
 \qquad a_q=t_q/\sqrt q\in(0,1).
\]

The family contains noncommuting states and has exponentially many labels. Every member has entropy \(q h((1+a_q)/2)\). For any prior, subadditivity gives \(S(\bar\rho)\le\sum_i S(\bar\rho_i)\le q\ln2\), so the Holevo supremum obeys the exact bound

\[
 C(E_q)\le q c(a_q),\quad
 c(a)=\ln2-h((1+a)/2)
 =\tfrac12[(1+a)\ln(1+a)+(1-a)\ln(1-a)].
\]

Consequently, if \(t_q\le t\), then \(C(E_q)\le t^2/2+O(t^4/q)\), uniformly over every prior. This bound is from the actual ensemble entropy, not score rank or accessible information.

There is an explicit full-domain broadcaster: apply the optimal symmetric universal qubit cloner independently to each input qubit. This is a CPTP map \(B_q:B((\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes q})\to B((\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes 2q})\) on all inputs; its output registers are regrouped into two \(q\)-qubit receivers. Each marginal is \(\Phi^{\otimes q}\), where \(\Phi(\omega)=\tfrac23\omega+\tfrac13 I/2\). Hence on \(\rho_x^{(q)}\) the marginal is the product state with signal amplitude \(2a_q/3\). The relative entropy to the target is

\[
D_q:=D(\rho_x^{(q)}\|\Phi^{\otimes q}(\rho_x^{(q)}))
 =q\,d(a_q\|2a_q/3),
\]

where

\[
d(a\|\lambda a)=\tfrac12(1+a)\ln\frac{1+a}{1+\lambda a}
 +\tfrac12(1-a)\ln\frac{1-a}{1-\lambda a}.
\]

Quantum Pinsker therefore yields the certified upper bound

\[
b_2(E_q)\le\min\{1,\sqrt{q\,d(a_q\|2a_q/3)/2}\}
 = O(t_q)\quad(a_q\to0).
\]

This is a genuine common full-domain broadcaster, but for fixed nonzero \(t_q\to t>0\) the displayed estimate is only a positive constant, not \(o(1)\). It cannot be called a counterexample on the basis of a small numerical constant. If instead \(t_q\to0\), the full-algebra constant-reference EB channel \(\mathcal R_q(X)=\mathrm{Tr}(X)I/2^q\) satisfies

\[
 e_{\rm EB}(E_q)\le\sup_xT(\rho_x^{(q)},I/2^q)
 \le\sqrt{q c(a_q)/2}=O(t_q)\longrightarrow0,
\]

again by quantum Pinsker. Thus simply weakening all local signals cannot preserve an EB-error floor.

At fixed \(t>0\), the product family remains an unresolved lead rather than a construction: the universal cloner bound does not show \(b_2\to0\), and the reference EB bound does not show \(e_{\rm EB}\) bounded below. No theorem or exact calculation in this cycle supplies the required optimized-channel lower or upper bounds. The ambient dimension is \(2^q\), so even moderate \(q\) is costly for a generic Choi SDP; no finite numerical solver was run.

## Candidate mechanisms checked against full-domain quantifiers

### Copy/flag ladders

The N61 dyadic pure-copy ladder has a full-domain sector-measure/split broadcaster with \(b_2\le1/L\) and \(e_{\rm EB}\to1\), but its Haar Holevo capacity diverges at least as \((2^{L-1}/L)\ln L\). N67 quantifies the receiver-growth cost for that same ladder. It fails bounded capacity, so it is only an unbounded-capacity control.

The N69 result closes the scoped bounded-capacity *pure uniform-ladder* route and includes a finite-label extraction bound. This is not a theorem for arbitrary mixed families.

The flagged fixed-alphabet tensor-copy construction has \(C(E_L)\le\ln M\) and a single CPTP flag/split channel with \(b_2\le1/L\). It fails because the EB decoder measures the flag, identifies the finite seed label on the high-copy sectors by a global POVM, and prepares the entire flagged state; discarded low-copy flag mass tends to zero. The argument is compatible with the unrestricted EB interface.

The flagged Pauli tensor-power ladder with \(a_L^2=\alpha^2L/2^{L-1}\) has a uniform Holevo bound and the same full-domain split broadcaster, but the global constant-reference EB channel has error \(O(\ln L/L)\). The estimate uses the actual relative-entropy radius and quantum Pinsker on every sector. The source-recorded weighted-flag no-go applies only when that radius certificate is available; bounded Holevo capacity alone has not been shown to imply it.

These flag checks rule out those constructions, not all block or multiplicity-rich experiments. A data-hiding seed does not help the fixed-alphabet construction because the decoder can use a global POVM. A locking claim about accessible information does not lower the Holevo supremum, and averaging over a key changes both the state family and its channel inequalities.

### Noncommuting fixed-spectrum controls

For the faithful Pauli/Clifford family recorded in N93, \(\rho_{P,\pm}=(I\pm aP)/d\), the all-prior Holevo capacity is \(c(a)\le\ln2\), and its full-algebra EB error is \(a d/[2(d+1)]\to a/2\). The collective broadcasting witness gives \(b_2\ge a(1-1/\sqrt2)/2\) (with a stronger exact matching statement in its perfect-matching case). For fixed \(a>0\), this fails \(b_2\to0\); sending \(a\to0\) also removes the stated EB lower bound. Small accessible information does not change this.

For any family made of states \(\rho_x=(1-\delta_q)\tau_q+\delta_q\sigma_{x,q}\) with a common state \(\tau_q\), the full-domain replacer \(X\mapsto\mathrm{Tr}(X)\tau_q\) has \(e_{\rm EB}\le\delta_q\). Thus vanishing convex signal dilution cannot make the desired lower bound. Fixed-spectrum unitary-orbit variants have a bounded entropy deficit, but no common full-domain broadcaster approaching identity on the chosen noncommuting orbit was constructed; covariance of an experiment cannot be assumed for a noncovariant family.

## Why none of these is a qualifying counterexample

The target needs a *single* channel extension of the marginal for all states, not a statewise two-extension or a low score rank. Conversely, its EB error is against unrestricted joint measurements on the whole input; spatial hiding is irrelevant without a global EB lower bound. Existing near misses split as follows:

| Candidate | Bounded \(C\) | Full-domain \(b_2\to0\) | Full-algebra \(e_{\rm EB}\not\to0\) | Failure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| N61 dyadic pure-copy ladder | No | Yes | Yes | Holevo diverges |
| N69 scoped pure ladder | Yes (scoped) | Relevant regime | No | EB reconstruction bound |
| N93 Pauli/Clifford witness | Yes | No | Yes | Pair-broadcast lower bound |
| Fixed-alphabet flagged copies | Yes | Yes | No | High-copy global label decoder |
| Weak-signal flagged Pauli copies | Yes | Yes | No | Constant-reference EB decoder |
| Product weak Pauli family above, \(t_q\to0\) | Yes | Explicit upper tends to 0 | No | Reference EB error tends to 0 |
| Product weak Pauli family above, \(t_q\to t>0\) | Yes | Not established | Not established | Unresolved, not a counterexample |

The missing issue is not a lack of a candidate alphabet. It is the simultaneous certification of: (1) a dimension-independent Holevo bound for all priors, (2) a *common full-domain compatible channel* with vanishing worst-case trace error, and (3) a minimax lower bound against a single unrestricted POVM and arbitrary preparations. N93 formulates the corresponding common-measurement / joint-posterior-variance lift; the individual broadcaster-energy estimates do not supply that lift. No failure of a scalar or covariant inequality is itself a counterexample to this experiment-level implication.
