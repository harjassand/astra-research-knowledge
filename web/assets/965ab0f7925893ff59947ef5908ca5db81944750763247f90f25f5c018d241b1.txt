REVISION 02 — PRIOR-ART AND DOMAIN BOUNDARY
7 October 2026. Selective primary-source check after the initial artifact.

The formal operation is the Schur/Hadamard product of multiaffine generating polynomials: (p bullet q)(z)=sum_U p_U q_U z^U. General multivariate non-closure examples for some definitions/domains of stability predate this task, so the bare statement “Hadamard products need not be stable” is not a new result and is not claimed here.

The relevant primary distinction is domain and operation. Borcea–Brändén, “The Lee-Yang and Pólya-Schur Programs. II,” arXiv:0809.3087v1, Theorem 8.5 says the Schur-Hadamard product of two multi-affine polynomials stable on the open unit disk is disk-stable. Theorem 8.7 instead treats symmetric-difference convolution for weakly Hurwitz-stable inputs. Neither theorem is a preservation claim for coefficientwise multiplication of right-half-plane-stable signed-hole coefficient polynomials. The witness establishes failure in the exact determinant-weighted ambient-hole subfamily used here. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.3087

Borcea–Brändén–Liggett, “Negative Dependence and the Geometry of Polynomials,” arXiv:0707.2340v2, supplies the strongly-Rayleigh/stability framework; the necessary bivariate Rayleigh test used in the certificate is derived directly in INITIAL. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2340

Novelty remains UNKNOWN. The new content worth specialist checking is the explicit connected unimodular six-site realization, its persistence after a legal coordinate-pair auxiliary, and the p>=2 coefficient-power family, not general non-closure of Hadamard products.
