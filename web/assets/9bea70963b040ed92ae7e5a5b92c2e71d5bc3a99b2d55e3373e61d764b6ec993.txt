T01 algebraic PIT — frozen Phase-I derivation
Date: 2026-10-09

Target under investigation
Given A_1,...,A_m in Q^{n x n}, each rank <= 2, decide deterministically in poly(n,m,input-bit-size) whether det(sum_i x_i A_i) is the zero polynomial. This is the homogeneous core. An affine constant A_0 of unrestricted rank makes the yes case immediate whenever A_0 is invertible; the difficult affine cases need a separate reduction/handling and are not silently included here.

Exact rank-two expansion
Factor A_i=U_i V_i^T with U_i,V_i in Q^{n x 2} (pad a rank-one factorization with a zero column). Concatenate U=[U_1 ... U_m], V=[V_1 ... V_m], and put D(x)=diag(x_1 I_2,...,x_m I_2). Then L(x)=U D(x) V^T. Cauchy-Binet gives
 det L(x) = sum_{S subseteq [2m], |S|=n} det(U_S) det(V_S) prod_{i=1}^m x_i^{|S intersect {2i-1,2i}|}.
Thus each variable degree is at most 2. For alpha in {0,1,2}^m with sum alpha_i=n, the coefficient is
 c_alpha = sum_{S: |S|=n, |S intersect {2i-1,2i}|=alpha_i for all i} det(U_S)det(V_S).
The rank-one case has singleton groups, so each exponent vector identifies at most one S and nonvanishing reduces to finding a common base of the two represented matroids. At rank two, many S can have the same alpha and their signed products can cancel. This is the precise obstruction to the direct rank-one compression.

Small cancellation invisible to nc-rank
Over Q let
 A_1 = [[0,0,0],[0,0,1],[0,-1,0]],
 A_2 = [[0,0,-1],[0,0,0],[1,0,0]],
 A_3 = [[0,1,0],[-1,0,0],[0,0,0]].
Each A_i has rank 2. With variables (x,y,z),
 L(x,y,z)=[[0,z,-y],[-z,0,x],[y,-x,0]].
This is skew-symmetric of odd order, so det L=0 for every commuting x,y,z (also directly, expansion cancels). For any nonzero (x,y,z), L has rank 2, hence commutative rank is exactly 2.

The cancellation in the Cauchy-Binet formula can be seen term by term. Use
 (U_1,V_1)=([e_2,e_3],[e_3,-e_2]),
 (U_2,V_2)=([e_3,e_1],[e_1,-e_3]),
 (U_3,V_3)=([e_1,e_2],[e_2,-e_1]).
Among the 2^3 subsets selecting one column from each pair, only S={1,3,5} and S={2,4,6} give nonzero products of determinants. The first contributes +1 to [xyz], the second −1, so the common-base terms cancel exactly. This refutes the naive rank-two extension “a common base exists, therefore det L is nonzero.”

Yet the noncommutative rank is 3. It suffices to give a 2x2 blow-up of full rank. Substitute X=I_2, Y=P=diag(1,-1), Z=Q=[[0,1],[1,0]] into the coefficient variables. The 6x6 block matrix is
 M=[[0,Q,-P],[-Q,0,I],[P,-I,0]].
If M(a,b,c)^T=0 with a,b,c in Q^2, the last two block rows give c=Qa and b=Pa. The first then gives (QP-PQ)a=0. Here QP-PQ=[[0,-2],[2,0]] has determinant 4, so a=b=c=0. Thus M is invertible. This directly exhibits a cancellation pattern not seen by testing whether nc-rank is full.

No algorithmic claim yet
The expansion is exact but has up to binom(2m,n) summands; collecting equal exponent vectors is still potentially exponential. The explicit example refutes the proposed shortcut “compute nc-rank and declare nonzero when it is n.” It does not rule out a more refined deterministic algorithm for rank-two generators.

Second control: no bounded-support witness search
There are rank-two generators for which every combination involving fewer than n variables is singular, although the determinant polynomial is nonzero. For n=3 take
 A_1=E_11+E_23, A_2=E_22+E_13, A_3=E_33+E_12.
Then
 L= x_1 A_1+x_2 A_2+x_3 A_3
  = [[x_1,x_3,x_2],[0,x_2,x_1],[0,0,x_3]],
so det L=x_1 x_2 x_3. Each A_i has rank 2. If any variable is zero, the matrix is upper triangular with a zero diagonal entry and is singular; consequently no support of size below 3 witnesses nonvanishing. For every n>=3 this extends: choose for each i distinct r_i,c_i in [n]\{i}, set r_i<c_i, and take A_i=E_ii+E_{r_i c_i}. The two matrix units have distinct rows and columns, so rank(A_i)=2, while every combination is upper triangular and det(sum_i x_i A_i)=prod_i x_i. Any approach that searches only supports bounded independently of n therefore fails. This control does not obstruct general deterministic PIT: the single evaluation x_i=1 certifies nonzero for this family.

Bit and field accounting
The displayed examples use integer entries of bit size 1. In the general rational-input expansion, rational rank factorization can be obtained by exact Gaussian elimination with polynomial bit growth; after clearing denominators, Hadamard's bound gives polynomial bit length for each n-by-n minor, and summing at most binom(2m,n) terms adds O(m) bits beyond the largest term. The obstacle established here is the number of terms (exponential in m), not a hidden unit-cost arithmetic assumption. A single rational or finite-field evaluation of det L costs polynomially many field operations, but no deterministic polynomial-size evaluation set is derived. The 2-by-2 blow-up witness has determinant 4, so its displayed full-rank conclusion holds in characteristic different from 2; the commutative zero identity itself is an integer-polynomial identity and persists after reduction to every characteristic. Rank and noncommutative-rank statements must be rechecked in each characteristic.

Runnable evidence
The standard-library script `work/theory/t01_algebraic_pit/verify_rank2_skew.py` computes exact determinants and ranks for the two 3-by-3 pencils, enumerates the Cauchy-Binet contributions for the skew pencil, and checks the displayed 6-by-6 blow-up determinant. Command: `python3 work/theory/t01_algebraic_pit/verify_rank2_skew.py`. On the recorded run it returned zero polynomial coefficients for the skew pencil, the two nonzero xyz terms +1 and -1, blow-up determinant 4, and determinant x_1*x_2*x_3 for the bounded-support control; all three coefficient ranks in each control were 2. This is finite computational corroboration, not proof beyond the exact arguments above.

Attempted positivity / fermionic compression
Over Q, p(x)=sum_alpha c_alpha x^alpha is zero iff S=sum_alpha c_alpha^2 is zero. Equivalently, S is the constant term CT_x p(x)p(x^{-1}). This turns identity testing into a positive scalar computation, but it does not yet give a polynomial-time algorithm. In the exterior/fermionic representation, c_alpha is the overlap of the two decomposable exterior vectors after projection onto local pair-occupancy alpha_i. Squaring and summing over alpha introduces, at each two-column group, the local constraint f(s_1,s_2,t_1,t_2)=1 iff s_1+s_2=t_1+t_2, where s and t encode the selected columns in the two copies. In the bit order (s_1,s_2,t_1,t_2), its even signature has f_empty=f_1234=1, f_12=f_34=0 and f_13=f_14=f_23=f_24=1. The 4-bit Grassmann-Plucker/matchgate identity would require f_empty*f_1234=f_12*f_34-f_13*f_24+f_14*f_23, but here the left side is 1 and the right side is 0. Thus the local number-equality filter is not a fermionic Gaussian/matchgate signature; the straightforward Pfaffian contraction route fails. This is a failure of that compression attempt, not a lower bound against other algorithms.

Primary literature checked
- Derksen–Makam, “On non-commutative rank and tensor rank,” arXiv:1606.06701v1 (2016), §1.1 Example 1.1 (rank-two odd skew pencil, commutative rank 2 and noncommutative rank 3): https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06701 . Their basis differs by a change of variables from the displayed pencil. Thus the nc-rank gap is a known phenomenon; our 3-variable instance is only an explicit Cauchy-Binet cancellation calculation.
- Bläser–Jindal–Pandey, “Greedy Strikes Again: A Deterministic PTAS for Commutative Rank of Matrix Spaces,” ECCC TR16-145 rev. 2 (2017), §1.1 and §1.2: deterministic polynomial time for rank-one generators; known deterministic algorithms for the rank-two skew-symmetric/matroid-parity case; a PTAS for general commutative rank; the paper says this approximation does not seem to resolve full-rank PIT. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2016/145/revision/2/download . Published version: Theory of Computing 14 (2018), Article 3, https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v014a003/ .
- Ivanyos–Mittal–Qiao, “Symbolic determinant identity testing and non-commutative ranks of matrix Lie algebras,” arXiv:2109.06403v3 (2021), abstract: deterministic polynomial-time SDIT for complex matrix Lie algebras. The displayed skew-symmetric 3x3 coefficient space is a Lie algebra, so this example is already inside a known tractable structural class; this record claims no novelty for the family. https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06403 .
- Lakhani–Mukhopadhyay, “Approximating commutative rank of matrix spaces in NC,” ECCC TR26-185, posted 15 September 2026, abstract: deterministic NC^3 $(1-\varepsilon)$-approximation for rational matrix spaces. It improves the resource bound for approximation, not the exact full-rank decision required here. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/185/ .

Frontier and priority limit
These primary sources establish the particular known cases and approximation results just listed. They do not amount to a comprehensive novelty search for all rank-two coefficient spaces. No new deterministic polynomial-time rank-two PIT method, complete proof, or priority claim is established in this record.
