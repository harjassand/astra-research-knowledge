S01 REFINEMENT: REMOVE THE FULL DENSITY-PDE AUDIT DEPENDENCY
7 October 2026. INITIAL remains unchanged. This is an own-proof refinement,
not acquired from S02. S02 independently also found the N^-1/2 decoder rate;
both independent INITIAL origins must be retained in root's comparison.

The INITIAL N^-1/2 proof can be based entirely on finite positive Gaussian
products, so no assertion about a full mixing-density SDE generator domain
is necessary. Keep INITIAL Sections 1-3 and their exact T_n and P_Q,P_B.

For a unit n let G_u be the density push-forward induced by local boost
e^(u n.sigma/2), multiplied by the N-fold branch trace. It is an actual
positive group: G_u G_v=G_(u+v), with L1 norm <=e^(N|u|).
Its generator on smooth rapidly decaying densities is exactly T_n.
Such densities lie in its first and second generator domains by ordinary
differentiation of the smooth coordinate diffeomorphism and integration by
parts. Their tails make all derivative terms integrable.

A single quadratic factor is the Gaussian group average
A_t f=E G_(sqrt(lambda t/(2N^(3/2)))Z) f.
Gaussian convolution and G_u G_v give its semigroup property. Differentiating
the Gaussian density and integrating twice by parts gives its L1 generator
lambda T_n^2/(4N^(3/2)); Gaussian tails beat e^(N|u|), so boundary terms vanish.
The field factor is simply G_(t b/(2N^(3/4))) using the linear vector b.
This justifies each one-factor variation-of-constants formula directly, without
an ellipticity assumption or a full three-dimensional density semigroup.

Take the finite positive Lie product with m repeats, each factor duration 1/m.
Compare it with the corresponding multiplication factors e^(V_i/m),
where V_i=lambda_i(n_i.x)^2 and V_field=b.x. Their full scalar product is e^V.
Telescope actual versus scalar factors from right to left. Before each differing
factor the density is q_N,M times a partial scalar tilt whose quadratic matrix
lies between 0 and C and whose field is theta b, 0<=theta<=1. Thus all the
INITIAL log-gradient/Hessian and defect bounds remain valid there.

For the remaining actual Gaussian product, including the local Duhamel remainder,
adjoint action on I is centrally dominated by
exp[(6M+1)J^2/N^(3/2)+B^2/4].
Indeed one quadratic factor's adjoint on I is exactly e^(t lambda F^2/N^(3/2)),
bounded by e^(t lambda J^2/N^(3/2)); every field factor completes the square.
All filters commute with the Casimir, so multiply the central upper bounds.
The summed times for each direction and for the field are <=1. No unknown
trajectory trace or norm is used in this estimate.

The same heat/Hoeffding estimate as INITIAL therefore bounds density mass by
C_R exp[K'_R r^2], with C_R=2^(3/2)e^(B^2/4), K'_R=2(6M+1),
provided N>=4(6M+1)^2. Integrate the local defect bound for each factor and
sum its durations; each quadratic direction occurs for total time one, as
does the field. The resulting finite-mixture state sigma_m obeys
T(sigma_m, normalized mixture e^V q_N,M)
 <=C_R N^-1/2 I'_N(M,B),
I'_N=integral q_N,M exp[(2M+K'_R)r^2+Br]P_(M,B)(r)dx,
uniformly in EVERY m. Its mass is >=1 by the determinant-one invariant-seed
argument, and scalar-mixture mass is >=1 by x,-x pairing.

Finite-dimensional quantum Lie-Trotter convergence gives sigma_m->sigma
in trace norm. There is no need for convergence of the mixing densities.
Pass to that quantum limit, then use Esep_N and Rprior_N as in INITIAL:
T(rho,S_N p)<=Esep_N+C_R I'_N N^-1/2+Rprior_N.
The prior quartic envelopes bound I'_N uniformly. Thus the same new rate
follows with a slightly looser public constant and threshold.

The only semigroup fact used above is one-dimensional weighted transport plus
ordinary Gaussian convolution, each given by an explicit integral. Cutoff
removal is elementary dominated convergence with Gaussian/seed tails. This
replaces the most delicate full-PDE presentation in INITIAL by a constructive
finite-mixture proof. It still needs an independent reviewer, not model agreement.
