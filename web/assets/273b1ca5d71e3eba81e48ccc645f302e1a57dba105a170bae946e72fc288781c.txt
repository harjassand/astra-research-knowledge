(a) doped and soft repulsive Gutzwiller/Jastrow Slaters; (b) their finite-

INPUT AND OUTPUT

Basic input: A in Q(i)^(m x r), full column rank, and positive rational external
row weights z_i. Let mu(S)=|det A_S|^2 prod_(i in S) z_i for |S|=r, zero
otherwise. Supply physical mode ordering. A determinant/rank program, the
input's denominator/height bounds, and polynomial reversibility are acquired
from this explicit input, not from an opaque oracle.

For disjoint blocks E_j, let rational b_j(k)>=0 be positive on an integer
interval [l_j,u_j]. Ordinary logconcavity b_j(k)^2>=b_j(k-1)b_j(k+1) suffices
for the basic repulsive-filter result. Define w(S)=prod_j b_j(|S cap E_j|).
The normalized target has amplitudes
  det A_S sqrt((prod_(i in S)z_i) w(S)), |S|=r.
The squared norm is Z. Exact zero and a support witness are acquired. If
Z>0, the N63 fixed-depth-tape compiler gives a randomized classical compiler
whose quantum output is within trace distance epsilon with probability
at least 1-delta over compiler randomness. Costs are polynomial in input
bits, m, epsilon^-1, and log delta^-1; no inverse Z or normalized filter
probability appears. Seeds that succeed are not efficiently certified.

EXACT BLOCK FILTER REDUCTION AND ACQUISITION

f(x)=det(A* diag(z_i x_i) A)=sum_|S|r mu(S) x^S.
For x>0 its log determinant is concave. Let
  g(x)=sum_|T|m-r prod_j b_j(|E_j|-|T cap E_j|) x^T.
Then [x_1...x_m] f g = Z. Reversed local interval tables are logconcave.
Their fixed-total-load weight is log-M-concave: for X,Y of the same size
and a in X\Y in block e, if x_e<=y_e choose b in (Y\X) cap E_e; this
keeps every load fixed. Otherwise choose a deficient block f with x_f<y_f
and b in (Y\X) cap E_f. Exchanging a,b moves both affected pairs of loads
toward equality, increasing each table product. This is the sufficient
Lorentzian/logconcavity certificate used by the source.

Support consists of r-subsets with l_j<=|S cap E_j|<=u_j. If nonempty it is
a base matroid. An independent set I extends to such a base exactly when
  |I cap E_j|<=u_j for all j, and sum_j max(l_j,|I cap E_j|)<=r.
The ambient feasibility inequalities sum l_j<=r<=sum u_j and u_j<=|E_j|
are checked first. This oracle and A's exact linear-matroid oracle acquire
zero detection by matroid intersection. Pinned inclusions/exclusions shift
the load intervals and delete unavailable modes. Coefficient evaluation is
one exact minor and table product. Log ranges are bounded by the explicit
minor height bound plus sum_j log(max positive b_j/min positive b_j) and
the external-weight bit bounds. There is no tiny-determinant promise.

Phases are solely the rational-complex determinant phase; all extra factors
are nonnegative. Child counts generate their square-root amplitudes, so an
exact rational square root of every table entry is unnecessary. Source
derivatives/exclusions of f,g implement prefixes; zero branches are handled
by the exact support test before counting.

For the N63 hybrid choose eta=epsilon/(16m) and per-child failure
beta=delta epsilon^2/(64m^2). The arithmetic/oracle counting contribution is
O(m^13 B^2 epsilon^-2 log^2(2Bm/epsilon) log(m/(delta epsilon))). Multiply
by acquired bit-oracle and reversible simulation costs. This is a very
high polynomial, not a plausible near-term gate count.

For spin sites E_j={j up,j down}, b_j=(1,1,g_j^2), rational 0<=g_j<=1,
gives amplitudes det A_S prod_j g_j^(n_jup n_jdown). At g_j=0, intervals
are [0,1]. Fixed r<=number of sites allows holes, acquiring the doped
no-double-occupancy case that exact one-per-site N63 quotas did not state.

ROOT LAMINAR EXTENSION

Root and laminar_jastrow_audit derived the analogous result for a laminar
hierarchy with logconcave interval tables. Starting at a in X\Y, ascend to
the first ancestor with nonpositive load discrepancy, choose a negative
sibling, and descend negative children to b in Y\X. Each affected a-side
cluster has x>y; each affected b-side cluster has x<y; common ancestors do
not change. Thus all factors increase. Exact prefix feasibility uses a
tree interval DP: leaves are [1,1] forced, [0,0] deleted, [0,1] free; node
interval is its table interval intersected with the sum of child intervals;
root fixes r. Backtracking acquires a witness in polynomial time. The
thermal construction below accepts those hierarchical filters too. This
extension is root's result, not claimed as this worker's independent novelty.

THERMAL PURIFICATION: EXACT IDENTITY

For B in Q(i)^(n x n), let A_B=[B;I_n], a 2n-by-n full-rank Slater matrix.
Order physical rows first, ancilla rows second. For physical S with |S|=k
and ancillary H with |H|=n-k, write J=[n]\H. Laplace expansion gives
  det (A_B)_(S,n+H) = sign(J,H) det B_(S,J).
For equal-size S,S', this sign cancels in the ancillary partial trace.
Cauchy–Binet gives
  sum_|J|k det B_(S,J) conj(det B_(S',J))
    = det (BB*)_(S,S').
Consequently the physical reduced state is
  Gamma(BB*) / det(I+BB*),
where Gamma(W)=direct_sum_(k=0)^n wedge^k W.

If D is any physical diagonal filter from the admissible tables and P_r
fixes physical total r, applying the corresponding filter to the doubled
Slater, with ancillary quota n-r, gives an acquired purification of
  rho_(B,D,r) = D P_r Gamma(BB*) P_r D /
                  Tr(D P_r Gamma(BB*) P_r D).
No success-probability division occurs in the algorithm's complexity.
For B=exp(-beta h/2), Gamma(BB*)=exp(-beta c* h c). This is the finite-
temperature Gutzwiller/Jastrow variational density family. The ancillary
quota and physical total-r node remain compatible with physical laminar
filters. Removing the physical total constraint is possible using the
same doubled Slater and unconstrained ancillary counts.

THERMAL INPUT ACQUISITION WITHOUT 1/PROBABILITY PRECISION

An ordinary additive approximation to h or its thermal state followed by
rare filtering is insufficient. Instead use exact rational polynomial
evaluation in the supplied rational Hermitian h. Let X=-beta h/2 and let
R>=||X|| be a supplied/acquired rational bound, e.g. the maximum absolute
row-sum bound. Set nu=2^-p and
  B=q_N(X), q_N(x)=sum_(j=0)^N x^j/j!,
  N+1 >= 12 ceil(R)+p+2.
For |x|<=R, the Taylor remainder and factorial bound give
  |q_N(x)/exp(x)-1|<=nu; in particular q_N(x)>0.
Hence, because B is a polynomial in X,
  (1-nu)^2 exp(-beta h) <= B^2 <= (1+nu)^2 exp(-beta h).
Degree is O(R+p). Exact rational matrix evaluation and coefficient heights
have bit cost polynomial in n, input length, numeric R, and p. No eigenvector
acquisition, minimum projection-weight bound, or division by rare probability
is required. Numeric beta||h|| is charged; exponentially large beta encoded
in few bits does not make this a polynomial-input-bit thermal algorithm.

For completeness, aW<=V<=bW implies
  a^r wedge^r W <= wedge^r V <= b^r wedge^r W:
conjugate by W^-1/2 and use the products of its relative eigenvalues.
Any physical filter or compression preserves the sandwich. For positive
matrices M,N with aM<=N<=bM, normalized trace distance is at most
  (sqrt(b)-sqrt(a))/(sqrt(b)+sqrt(a)) = tanh(log(b/a)/4).
A Helstrom two-outcome measurement reduces the proof to a two-point
likelihood ratio in [a,b]; maximize at relative normalizer sqrt(ab).
Thus thermal filtered states differ by at most tanh(r artanh(nu)).
postselection_stability sharpened r to min(r,n-r) by complementary exterior
powers and also proved the same bound for the canonical filtered purification:
the relative polynomial acts as a positive operator on the ancilla, commutes
with the physical filter, and its condition ratio obeys the same exterior-
power estimate. Choosing p=O(log(n/epsilon)) leaves a fixed epsilon budget
for the randomized compiler, independent of rarity.
Detailed stability proof: work/cycle2/postselection_stability.txt.

PAIRED BCS TRANSFER AND EXACT SIGNS

Let F in Q(i)^(n x n). Define the unnormalized opposite-spin paired state
  |F> = exp(sum_ij F_ij c_up_i* c_down_j*) |vac>.
Its amplitude at up set S and down set J, |S|=|J|=k, is
  (-1)^(k(k-1)/2) det F_(S,J)
in the all-ups-then-all-downs fermion ordering. F=F^T gives the genuine
spin-singlet subclass; arbitrary F is opposite-spin pairing more broadly.

The graph Slater A_0=[F;I_n] has, at up set S and hole set H=[n]\J,
  det(A_0)_(S,n+H)=(-1)^(sum_(j in J)j-k(k-1)/2) det F_(S,J),
with j zero-based. Bit-flip all hole bits, then apply Z on odd zero-based
physical down modes. The resulting phase is exactly the BCS phase above.
These are polynomial circuits, including a fixed ordering conversion if
site-interleaved encoding is wanted.

An onsite physical factor g_j^(n_up n_down), g_j>0, has squared hole-basis
weights (1,t_j,1,1) for (u,h)=(00,10,01,11), t_j=g_j^2. Factor it as
  t_j^u b_j(u+h), b_j=(1,1,t_j^-1).
The t_j factor is an external weight on each up row in f, leaving a
symmetric block table. Rational t_j suffices: do NOT need irrational
sqrt(t_j) inside a rational matrix. f's acquired oracle is
|det(A_0)_S|^2 prod_(j up in S)t_j, and its phase is det(A_0)_S.

The stronger sufficient condition from repulsive_filter_audit is
  b_j(k)^2 >= [k/(k+1)] b_j(k-1)b_j(k+1)
for the COMPLEMENTARY polynomial g, not generally the direct w polynomial.
For two-mode tables it allows b_j(2)<=2, thus t_j>=1/2, nonuniformly.
A direct rank-two derivative certificate: after positive diagonal scaling,
within-free-block Hessian entries are rho_j, cross-block entries one. On
antisymmetric block subspaces eigenvalues are -rho_j; the symmetric quotient
is vv*+diag((N_j-1)rho_j-N_j), v_j=sqrt(N_j). The factorial condition makes
the diagonal nonpositive, so every such Hessian has at most one positive
eigenvalue. For two-mode BCS tables N_j=2 gives rho_j=t_j^-1<=2, and N_j=1
has negative diagonal. This supplies the Lorentzian/LC counting certificate.

When every t_j=t<1/2, the four-mode rank-two complementary polynomial is
  a(x1 x2+x3 x4)+sum_cross x_i x_j, a=1/t>2.
Its Hessian eigenvalues are a+2,a-2,-a,-a: two positive eigenvalues. This
refutes this route below the threshold, not polynomial preparation itself.
At t=0, hard projected BCS remains outside. Fixed original particle number
also creates a crossing flavor quota: even diagonal F with two sites and
one occupied pair leaves graph support polynomial x_u1 x_h2+x_h1 x_u2,
which has two positive Hessian eigenvalues. Do not assert that extension.

WHY THIS IS NOT A GENERAL INTERACTING GIBBS ALGORITHM

P exp(-beta H_0) P generally differs from exp(-beta P H_0 P) on ran(P).
An exact two-site spinful hopping example has P=one fermion per site and
P H_0 P=0. Put a=beta t. The projected free thermal operator has triplet
weights 1 and singlet weight cosh(2a). Hence it is not the uniform Gibbs
state of P H_0 P. For a=ln2, rational c=5/4,s=3/4 gives singlet weight17/8
and trace distance27/164 from I_4/4. Iterating projections or inserting
noncommuting interactions creates additional layers; this report does not
acquire their counting program, a ground-state overlap, or an optimization
algorithm.

PRIOR ART / PRIORITY BOUNDARY

Grover–Rudolph already explicitly allow probabilistic integration in coherent
recursive preparation: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0208112 .
Kang et al. prepare generic BCS plus Gutzwiller via amplitude amplification,
with inverse square-root projected weight: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06919 .
Finite-temperature Gutzwiller/Jastrow density matrices are established
variational objects: https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.4927 .
MPS constructions https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.00064 retain bond/truncation cost;
they do not supply an all-input polynomial bond bound. Direct autoregressive
Slater–Jastrow work https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05871 uses path-dependent
normalization that changes a generic supplied filter's globally normalized
target. gutzwiller_prior_art checked these primary interfaces. These facts
support a narrowly scoped algorithmic corollary, not a new physical ansatz
or a certified field-breaking/priority claim.
