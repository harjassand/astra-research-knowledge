VERDICT

The full-algebra, channel-dependent dimension-free lifting question remains
UNKNOWN for general bistochastic, selfadjoint, actually self-compatible
channels in d>=3. No finite-dimensional witness against every
channel-dependent EB selector was found. A correct general score-span
extension does hold with a logarithmic rank loss:

  P_S(I-Psi)P_S <= 2 ceil(log2 dim(S)) P_S(I-Phi)P_S,

for one pure-design EB channel Psi on each supplied score subspace S (rank
at least two). On the full traceless algebra this is an O(log d) factor.
It uses an actual physical broadcaster and a common design obtained by
separation; it is not a modewise or hypothetical star-eigenstate argument.

There is an exact noncovariant test showing why an orbit comparator cannot
be transferred unchanged. Computational-basis dephasing has a symmetric
CPTP broadcaster, but the fixed Haar coherent EB channel loses a diagonal
traceless observable that dephasing preserves exactly. Thus no finite C
works for that fixed comparator. The channel-dependent basis design
Psi=Delta solves the same channel with C=1. A non-EB, noncovariant mixture
of dephasing and the universal cloner is also an actual broadcaster; a
channel-dependent mixture of two EB designs gives C=2. These examples
refute a fixed-orbit transfer, not a channel-dependent dimension-free
selector.

The exact runnable d=3 checks are in
noncovariant_designs_check.py. They verify the CPTP maps, equal marginals,
output swap symmetry, the fixed-Haar failure, the non-EB mixture, and its
channel-dependent factor-two design by exact arithmetic.

1. INTERFACE AND COMMON-DESIGN EXTENSION TO ARBITRARY SCORE RANK

Let A be a d-dimensional system. The physical interface is a CPTP map

  B: M_d -> M_d tensor M_d,

with equal marginals Phi_* = Tr_2 B = Tr_1 B on the entire input algebra.
The Heisenberg adjoint J=B^* is UCP and obeys
J(X tensor I)=J(I tensor X)=Phi(X). Assume Phi is bistochastic and
selfadjoint on normalized-trace L2; its eigenvalues may be signed. A
covariance or positivity-as-an-L2-operator assumption is not used here.

Write H0 for the real Hilbert space of traceless Hermitian matrices with
inner product tau(AB), tau=Tr/d. Let S subset H0 be a supplied real score
subspace of dimension r>=2. For a PSD witness Q supported on S, write
Q=sum_{i=1}^r |A_i><A_i| with Hermitian A_i in S, allowing zero factors.
Define

  E_Phi(Q)=sum_i tau[A_i(I-Phi)A_i],
  h_1(Q)=max_D sum_y p_y sum_i (Tr(P_y A_i))^2,

where D ranges over finite pure-state 1-designs, p_y>=0,
sum_y p_y P_y=I/d. The desired single-design comparison on S is
equivalent to h_1(Q)>=Tr(Q)-2m E_Phi(Q) for every PSD Q supported on S;
compact-convex separation then gives one design for all directions in S.

Let m=ceil(log2 r), k=2^m. Iterate the supplied physical broadcaster in a
binary tree: B_1=B, and, given B_l:A->A^{tensor 2^l}, set

  B_{l+1}=(B_l tensor B_l) composed with B.

This maps one input to 2^{l+1} outputs. Equivalently, apply B
independently to each output of B_l. Every individual leaf marginal is
Phi_*^{l+1}; this follows by induction from equality of the two supplied
marginals. In Heisenberg form, with J_l=B_l^*, the recursion is

  J_{l+1}=J composed with (J_l tensor J_l).

Unused leaves can be discarded. No independence of cloned outputs is
assumed.

For one witness Q, measure the spectral PVM of A_i on leaf i, for
i=1,...,r. The joint product PVM pulled back through B_m^* is an input
POVM {M_y}. Its first and second scalar moments for factor A_i are
Phi^m(A_i) and Phi^m(A_i^2). If a_i(y) is the corresponding eigenvalue,
then trace expansion and tau-preservation give the exact meter identity

  sum_y tau[(A_i-a_i(y)I) M_y (A_i-a_i(y)I)]
      = 2 tau[A_i(I-Phi^m)A_i].                       (1)

Every term on the left is nonnegative; no commutation between M_y and A_i
is needed. Spectrally refine each M_y=sum_z m_yz P_yz into rank-one
projectors and set p_yz=m_yz/d. These outcomes form one pure 1-design.
For every i,y,z,

  Tr(P_yz(A_i-a_i(y)I)^2)
    = Var_{P_yz}(A_i)+(Tr(P_yz A_i)-a_i(y))^2
    >= Var_{P_yz}(A_i).

Summing and using (1) proves

  Tr(Q)-h_1(Q) <= 2 E_(Phi^m)(Q).                     (2)

Since Phi is selfadjoint with spectrum in [-1,1], for each scalar
lambda in that interval

  1-lambda^m=(1-lambda) sum_{q=0}^{m-1} lambda^q
            <=m(1-lambda),

because every summand lambda^q<=1. Thus
E_(Phi^m)(Q)<=m E_Phi(Q), including signed spectrum. Equations (2) and
finite-dimensional separation over the compact pure-design moment body
yield one design D_S and its EB channel

  Psi(X)=d sum_y p_y Tr(P_y X)P_y

such that

  P_S(I-Psi)P_S <= 2m P_S(I-Phi)P_S.                 (3)

For S=H0, r=d^2-1 and the factor is
2 ceil(log2(d^2-1))=O(log d). This is the full-algebra extension obtained
from direct common-design control. It does not give a dimension-free
constant; no dimension-free lower bound for this logarithm is claimed.

Cost boundary: B is supplied on its full domain; learning or constructing
it is not charged as free. The tree uses k-1<2r calls to B, has k<=2r
output registers, and the product PVM has at most d^k outcomes before
effect refinement. A direct dense realization is exponential in r. The
existence proof via compact-convex separation does not by itself supply a
polynomial-time selector, a finite precision guarantee, or a small score
span from capacity. These are distinct algorithmic/acquisition questions.

2. EXACT NONCOVARIANT CONTROLS

2.1 Computational dephasing: actual extension, fixed-orbit failure,
channel-dependent solution.

For any d>=2, let Delta be dephasing in the computational basis and define

  B_Delta(X)=sum_{i=1}^d <i|X|i> |ii><ii|.

This is CPTP on the full algebra. Both output marginals are Delta and the
joint output is invariant under swapping the two receivers. Delta is
bistochastic, selfadjoint, and EB. It is already its own non-orbit pure
1-design EB decoder, so I-Psi=I-Phi with Psi=Delta: channel-dependent
factor C=1 on all of L2.

The fixed Haar pure-state measure/prepare channel is

  Psi_H(X)=(X+Tr(X)I)/(d+1),

whose eigenvalue on traceless matrices is 1/(d+1). For
A=diag(1,-1,0,...,0),

  (I-Delta)A=0,      (I-Psi_H)A=d A/(d+1) != 0.

Therefore I-Psi_H<=C(I-Delta) fails for every finite C, already at this
one fixed finite d. The problem is the fixed orbit comparator: it attenuates
a perfectly broadcastable classical direction. It is not a counterexample
to channel-dependent EB lifting, because Delta itself is a valid selector.

In fact, there is no single selfadjoint EB comparator that works with any
finite constant for every basis-dephasing channel. If it worked for
Delta_U(X)=sum_i |u_i><u_i|X|u_i><u_i|, then positivity of both sides and
the kernel of I-Delta_U would force it to fix every traceless Hermitian
matrix diagonal in basis U. Every Hermitian matrix is diagonal in some
basis, so it would be the identity channel on the full algebra, which is
not EB for d>=2. This only rules out one fixed comparator shared by all
channels.

2.2 A non-EB noncovariant broadcaster with a common non-orbit design.

Let F be the swap on two outputs, P_sym=(I+F)/2, and set

  B_cl(X)=2/(d+1) P_sym (X tensor I) P_sym.

This is a full-domain CPTP symmetric broadcaster. Expanding P_sym and
tracing either output gives the same marginal

  Phi_cl(X)=(d+2)/(2(d+1)) X + Tr(X)I/[2(d+1)].       (4)

The Haar EB channel Psi_H obeys I-Psi_H=2(I-Phi_cl) on the traceless
space. The identity sector has zero defect on both sides. Although written
as a Haar integral, this channel has an exact finite pure-state 2-design
representation: its second moment lies in the finite-dimensional convex
hull of the compact set {P tensor P}, so Caratheodory gives finite support.
Now mix actual
broadcasters and their EB designs:

  B_mix=(B_cl+B_Delta)/2,
  Phi_mix=(Phi_cl+Delta)/2,
  Psi_mix=(Psi_H+Delta)/2.

B_mix is CPTP with equal marginals, and is noncovariant. Psi_mix is EB as a
convex mixture of EB maps and is implemented by combining the Haar design
with the computational-basis design. Convexity of the operator order gives

  I-Psi_mix <= 2(I-Phi_mix).

Phi_mix is not EB: its normalized marginal Choi state has maximally
entangled overlap

  f_mix=1/2 [1/d + lambda_cl+(1-lambda_cl)/d^2] > 1/d,
  lambda_cl=(d+2)/(2(d+1)),

where 1/d is the maximum such overlap for a separable Choi state. For d=3,
the exact values are f_mix=1/2, and the diagonal/off-diagonal traceless
multipliers are 13/16 and 5/16, respectively. Thus this is a real
noncovariant, non-EB, exactly compatible control, not a star eigenstate
without the channel marginals. It demonstrates that a suitable
channel-dependent common design can survive breaking covariance.

3. WHAT THESE CONTROLS ESTABLISH

The tree/common-design proof is a general full-algebra result with an
O(log d) factor, under bistochasticity, L2-selfadjointness, and an actual
two-output CPTP broadcaster. The exact dephasing family proves that a
fixed coherent/Haar orbit cannot replace channel-dependent design choice
for arbitrary noncovariant channels. The non-EB mixture shows this remains
true even when the channel is not itself entanglement breaking, while a
channel-dependent mixture design still satisfies C=2.

None of these facts establishes a finite witness for which every
channel-dependent EB selector has a dimension-growing or unbounded
constant. The dephasing witness fails only for the fixed Haar comparator;
its optimal channel-dependent decoder is exact. The cloner mixture has an
explicit factor-two selector. Hence the all-dimension, all-channel
dimension-free selector question remains UNKNOWN, as does the broader
bounded-Holevo N93 implication. Covariance, actual compatibility, score
rank, and implementation costs must remain separate premises.
