## Representation derivation

Let $V=\mathbb R^9$ be the vector representation and $S$ the complexification of the real 16-dimensional spinor. Clifford multiplication identifies

\[
S^*\otimes S\simeq\operatorname{End}(S)
\simeq \bigoplus_{j=0}^4\Lambda^j V,
\]

because the grades $0$ through $4$ have total dimension $\sum_{j=0}^4\binom9j=256=16^2$, are orthogonal under normalized trace, and the remaining Clifford grades are Hodge-dual to these in the irreducible odd-dimensional module. For $j\le4$, Clifford contraction (gamma trace) on $\Lambda^jV\otimes S$ has kernel the gamma-traceless spinor-form $T_j$ of highest weight

\[
\lambda_j=(\underbrace{3/2,\ldots,3/2}_{j},\underbrace{1/2,\ldots,1/2}_{4-j}).
\]

The gamma-trace exact sequence gives recursively

\[
\Lambda^jV\otimes S\simeq T_j\oplus T_{j-1}\oplus\cdots\oplus T_0.
\]

Summing over $j=0,\ldots,4$ yields the actual ordinary triplet representation

\[
S^*\otimes S\otimes S\simeq\bigoplus_{r=0}^4 T_r\otimes\mathbb C^{5-r}.
\tag{1}
\]

The B4 Weyl dimension formula gives $\dim T_r=16(\binom9r-\binom9{r-1})=(16,128,432,768,672)$. With $\rho=(7/2,5/2,3/2,1/2)$, the quadratic Casimir eigenvalue is

\[
c_r=\langle\lambda_r,\lambda_r+2\rho\rangle=(9,18,25,30,33).
\]

These imply $\sum_r(5-r)\dim T_r=4096$, which covers the full tensor product. Because the vector representation has odd dimension 9 (type B4), its 16-dimensional spinor is not split into two chiral half-spinors; grades 5--9 are identified with grades 4--0 by the volume element. Equation (1) is an ordinary tensor decomposition; no Bose projection is made.

## Independent exact projector construction

The checker uses the four-qubit Jordan--Wigner Clifford model and encodes each of the three 16-dimensional factors as four Pauli sites. Exact Pauli multiplication verifies all 36 Clifford anticommutators, the 255 nonidentity words across grades 1--4 are distinct, and the four star generators have the Hilbert--Schmidt normalization $\operatorname{Tr}(H_kH_\ell)=4096\cdot2\binom9k\,\delta_{k\ell}$. On the full 12-site Pauli-word algebra it constructs

\[
C_4=-\sum_{i<j}(2J_{ij})^2,
\qquad
J_{ij}=-(L_{ij})^T\otimes I\otimes I+I\otimes L_{ij}\otimes I+I\otimes I\otimes L_{ij},
\qquad L_{ij}=\tfrac12\Gamma_i\Gamma_j.
\]

The first term is the conjugate/dual spinor generator on the reference factor. Exact commutator checks show $C_4$ commutes with all 36 diagonal Spin(9) generators, and the Pauli expansion verifies the exact minimal-polynomial identity $\prod_{r=0}^4(C_4-\mu_r I)=0$. The computed eigenvalues of $C_4=4C_2$ are $\mu_r=4c_r=(36,72,100,120,132)$. Because these five eigenvalues are distinct, exact Lagrange polynomials

\[
P_r=\prod_{s\ne r}\frac{C_4-\mu_sI}{\mu_r-\mu_s}
\]

are the isotypic projectors. Thus the decomposition is checked directly on the full representation rather than assumed from the dimension count alone.

The grade-$k$ star is built as the ordinary tensor operator

\[
H_k=\sum_{|A|=k}T_A^T\otimes(T_A\otimes I+I\otimes T_A),\qquad k=1,2,3,4.
\]

For $T_A=i^{k(k-1)/2}\Gamma_A$, its reference transpose sign is the Pauli-word sign $(-1)^{\#Y}$; all Clifford phases are retained exactly. The code verifies every grade word is Hermitian, and every intermediate Hermitian Pauli expansion has real coefficients. The Choi convention is also fixed: for normalized maximally entangled input, $\operatorname{Tr}[J_\mathcal B(T_A^T\otimes Y)]=\operatorname{Tr}[Y\Phi(T_A)]/16$. Since $H_k$ contains both output terms, its expectation is exactly twice the score with $\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/16$. The trace side is $\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q=9\alpha_1+36\alpha_2+84\alpha_3+126\alpha_4$.

Output swap is not discarded. In Pauli coordinates,

\[
S_{12}=\frac1{16}\sum_{P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}^{\otimes4}}I\otimes P\otimes P,
\qquad P_\pm=\tfrac12(I\pm S_{12}).
\]

The exact full-space traces of $P_rP_\pm$ divided by $\dim T_r$ yield these multiplicities:

| $r$ | $\dim T_r$ | $m_{r,+}$ | $m_{r,-}$ | isotypic dimensions $(+, -)$ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 16 | 3 | 2 | (48, 32) |
| 1 | 128 | 2 | 2 | (256, 256) |
| 2 | 432 | 1 | 2 | (432, 864) |
| 3 | 768 | 1 | 1 | (768, 768) |
| 4 | 672 | 1 | 0 | (672, 0) |

The independently derived totals are 2176 swap-even and 1920 swap-odd dimensions, summing to 4096. In particular, the antisymmetric-output sectors occupy 1920 dimensions and are present in all relevant grades; they are not inferred to be absent from symmetry of the broadcaster.

## All mixed weights and PSD certificates

Condition on the orbit task's exact canonical-EB support vertices

\[
v_R=(1,0,0,14),\quad v_H=(0,1,7,7),\quad v_V=(1,4,4,6),
\qquad S_{\rm can}(\alpha)=\max_i v_i\cdot\alpha.
\]

Intersecting the nonnegative orthant with each exact active-support cone yields respectively six, six, and seven rays; their union is the following ten primitive integer rays:

\[
e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4, (0,0,1,1),\ (0,2,0,1),\ (0,7,5,6),\ (7,0,2,1),\ (0,1,1,0),\ (3,0,1,0).
\]

This ray enumeration is independently implemented by exact integer cofactor kernels. At each ray $a$, define

\[
G(a)=\left(9a_1+36a_2+84a_3+126a_4+S_{\rm can}(a)\right)I-H(a).
\]

Rather than recover an orthonormal multiplicity basis, the code computes the full-space moments

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(P_rP_\pm G(a)^j)
\]

using Pauli-word products and the Casimir polynomial. Dividing by $\dim T_r$ gives the $j$-th power sum of the eigenvalues of the corresponding multiplicity block, whose size is at most 3. Newton identities convert the first $m_{r,\pm}$ power sums into its elementary symmetric coefficients $e_1,\ldots,e_m$. All 150 coefficients are nonnegative exact rationals: 145 are positive and 5 are zero (the determinant coefficients of the even $r=0$ block at five rays). The values are recorded in [PAULI_TRACE_CERTIFICATE.json](PAULI_TRACE_CERTIFICATE.json).

This is a PSD certificate because the block is Hermitian and hence has real eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m$. If all $e_j\ge0$, then

\[
\prod_{i=1}^m(t+\lambda_i)=t^m+e_1t^{m-1}+\cdots+e_m>0\quad(t>0).
\]

A negative eigenvalue would create a positive root $t=-\lambda_i$, contradiction. Thus each block gap is PSD. On each support cone the support maximum is one fixed linear form, so $G(\alpha)$ is linear in $\alpha$; positivity on its extreme rays proves the full nonnegative mixed-weight inequality. This tests all nine nonempty representation/swap blocks; it does not assume a top eigenvector lies in the symmetric output sector.
