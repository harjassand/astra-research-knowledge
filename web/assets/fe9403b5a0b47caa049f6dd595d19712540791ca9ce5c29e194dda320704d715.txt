DECISIVE RESULT 1: SHARP COST OF CONTINUOUS WINDING ERASURE
For EVERY real 2pi-periodic L-Lipschitz h,

  ||exp(i h(C))-C||_2^2 >= 2/(L+1), and ||exp(i h(C))-C|| = 2,

when C has Haar spectral law. The bound is attained for every L>=1 by
an explicit centered, piecewise linear h with sup norm < pi. Therefore
epsilon L2 accuracy requires L > 2 epsilon^{-2}-1. For a finite real
Laurent phase, the elementary weighted coefficient cost
Lambda=sum_k |k a_k| must obey the same lower bound. Complete proof and
sharpness construction are in owned proofs.txt, Theorem 1. The central
step is the integral of V(phi) phi', where V=2-2cos and phi=h-theta;
the degree mismatch forces exactly 4pi of signed potential traversal.
This is an internally reconstructed theorem. A bounded prior-art search
did not resolve whether this exact sharp formulation already appears;
no novelty inference is made from that absence.

DECISIVE RESULT 2: THE C*-NORM ABSORPTION TRANSFER FAILS SHARPLY
Let D=C*_r(F_{n+1}) and A_1,...,A_n,C its canonical free unitaries.
For ANY unitaries U_j in D with ||U_j-A_j||<2, EVERY word w satisfies

  ||w(U)-C||=2.

If U is free Haar and B=C*(U), then dist(C,B)=1 and ||v-C||=2 for every
unitary v in B. The proof uses Pimsner-Voiculescu's K1 basis, with norm
distance <2 implying unitary homotopy. It refutes the natural stronger
transfer before any extensive computation. Full proof is in proofs.txt,
Theorems 2 and 3. This is a consequence of established K-theory, not a
claim of a new foundational obstruction.

WHY THIS DOES NOT REFUTE THE ORIGINAL SOURCE
The paper's S=arg(C) is bounded BOREL functional calculus. S is outside
D: if S belonged to D, exp(i t S) would kill [C] in K1(D). The paper's
flow therefore operates in a larger C*-algebra. Its final generation
step is L2. The Lean development instead uses continuous/Mobius log
approximations and centered Laurent polynomials and obtains ONLY L2
error for the absorbed word; active generators remain norm-close.
That distinction is mathematically necessary, not a cosmetic weakening.

BOUNDARIES AND NEXT DECISIVE GATE
1. Do not try to upgrade the continuous/Laurent proof's absorbed-word
   metric to operator norm within the same D: Theorem 2 forbids it.
2. A possible surviving positive transfer is a weakly dense proper copy
   C*_r(F_n) inside C*_r(F_{n+1}), arbitrarily close to the first n
   canonical generators, with exact norm distance 1 from C. The source's
   continuous flow may supply it, but one must verify the entire iteration
   stays in the SAME D and restrict the Baire/local approximation orbit.
   This existence statement is conditional and not claimed proved here.
3. The sharp h_L supplies an optimal replacement primitive for the source
   Mobius phase. The Mobius formula has squared L2 error 2(1-r) and phase
   derivative 2r/(1-r); the optimum can reduce maximal derivative by an
   asymptotic factor two. Cocycle support, selected word length, polynomial
   support and total computation costs remain unbounded in the source,
   so no efficient implementation or new classification theorem follows.
4. A genuine foundational endpoint would require either independently
   validating the family's factor theorem, or a different norm-level
   classification target compatible with K-theory. The present findings
   justify neither a new open-problem resolution nor high novelty.
