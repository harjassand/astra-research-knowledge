1. BOUNDED KILLING CERTIFICATES

For a middle species use q_j=g_j(S_j), with 1<=q_j<=G_j=1+c_j,
and per-particle death a_j(t)=sum_i beta_ij(t)r_i. The earlier proof gives
  L_t q_j-a_j(t)q_j<=-gamma_j.
For a leaf use q_k(S_p,y_p), with 1<=q_k<=G_k=1+c_k/s_k,
and per-particle death a_k(t)=zeta_k(t)y_p. The third-layer proof gives
  L_t q_k-a_k(t)q_k<=-Gamma_k.
In either case write q,G,gamma and acquire eta=gamma/G. Since q<=G,
  L_t q-a(t)q<=-eta q.                             (K)

Label particles in the usual mass-action construction; aggregate deaths
choose a uniformly random currently present particle. An individual tagged
particle of this species has conditional death intensity a(t). Its death
changes no argument of q. All other reactions retain the already proved
generator bound, even when coefficients depend on the complete past and on
whether the tag has survived. Thus the killed compensator of
  1{tag alive at t} q(X_t)
is 1{alive}[L_t q-a(t)q]. Multiplication by exp(eta t), localization and
the nonnegative supermartingale argument prove, for any time sigma at which
the tag is present,
  P(tag survives to sigma+u | F_sigma)
     <=q(X_sigma) exp(-eta u)<=G exp(-eta u).         (L)
No future-path conditioning, independence of the environment, replacement
of physical time by event time, or assumption of an invariant law occurs.
For deterministic cohort endpoints sigma this is all that is needed below.
The same argument also works at admissible stopping times.

This is a recurrence-preserving physical-time treatment of the disabled
catalytic boundary for the admitted class. A vanished instantaneous death
hazard is replaced by a bounded acquired killing corrector. It is the new
load-bearing gate beyond N76's embedded-event upward bound.

2. ROOT MOMENTS UNDER PREDICTABLE FEEDBACK

For root i, actual immigration<=b_i^+ and per-particle death>=delta_i^-.
Nearest-neighbor order coupling with the ordinary immigration/death process
U_i having coefficients b_i^+,delta_i^- gives r_i(t)<=U_i(t) pathwise.
At equality, thin the birth/death clocks using the rate inequalities; when
counts differ, unit jumps cannot cross without meeting. Predictable control
does not alter the upper chain's autonomous marginal. Start U_i at
r_i(0)+Poisson(c_i), c_i=b_i^+/delta_i^-. Its law at t is
Binomial(r_i(0),exp(-delta_i^- t))+Poisson(c_i). Hence for every integer p>=1,
  sup_t E r_i(t)^p <= M_i,p=E[r_i(0)+Poisson(c_i)]^p.
This is an acquired rational Touchard-polynomial expression when p is fixed.
Independent upper chains or independence between roots are not needed.

3. A RATIONAL BIRTH-COUNT MOMENT BOUND

For one nonroot species, let ell(t) be its birth intensity and suppose
sup_t E ell(t)^p<=Lambda_p for a fixed integer p>=2. Let K_u count its
births over an interval of length at most one, starting from K_0=0. Counting
process compensators give the a.e. differential inequality
  d/du E K_u^p=E ell(u)[(K_u+1)^p-K_u^p].
After localization, use
  (K+1)^p-K^p<=p(K+1)^(p-1)
       <=p 2^(p-2)(K^(p-1)+1),
  K^(p-1)ell<=((p-1)/p)K^p+(1/p)ell^p,
  ell<=1+ell^p.
Then f(u)=E K_u^p obeys
  f'(u)<=a_p f(u)+b_p Lambda_p+d_p,
where
  a_p=2^(p-2)(p-1), b_p=2^(p-2)(p+1), d_p=p 2^(p-2).
Grönwall and exp(a_p)<=2^(2a_p) give the fully rational bound
  E K_u^p<=B_p=2^(2a_p)(b_p Lambda_p+d_p)/a_p,
uniformly over interval endpoints and admissible feedback policies. Remove
localization using Fatou; all parent moments invoked below have already
been acquired. p=4 uses a_p=12,b_p=20,d_p=16, so this factor is 2^24/12.
This is a conservative bound, not a fitted or observed arrival variance.

4. ALL-TIME CHILD MOMENTS WITHOUT INDEPENDENCE

Partition births before a fixed t into backward intervals of length one.
A cohort with endpoint sigma=t-k has at most K particles, with
E K^p<=B_p; K is known at sigma. If S is the number of those particles
surviving to t, then S^p<=K^(p-1)S. Apply the per-tag conditional bound (L)
to the at-most-K tags alive at sigma to obtain
  E[S^p|F_sigma]<=K^p G exp(-eta k).
Thus the Lp norm of that surviving cohort is at most
(G B_p)^(1/p)exp(-eta k/p). The n initial particles contribute at most
n G^(1/p)exp(-eta t/p). Minkowski, applied to the finitely many cohorts at
this finite time, gives
  ||X_j(t)||_p<=G^(1/p)[n+B_p^(1/p)/(1-exp(-eta/p))].
Since exp(-u)<=1/(1+u),
  1/(1-exp(-eta/p))<=1+p/eta.
Finally (a+b)^p<=2^(p-1)(a^p+b^p) yields the acquired rational bound
  sup_t E X_j(t)^p <=
  M_j,p=G 2^(p-1)[n^p+B_p(1+p/eta)^p].              (M)
Particles and their survival events can be correlated; no independence was
used. Rate feedback can depend on deaths and births in the same cohort.

For middle j, ell_j<=sum_i alpha_ij^+ r_i. Weighted Jensen gives
  Lambda_j,p <=(sum_i alpha_ij^+)^(p-1)
                 sum_i alpha_ij^+ M_i,p.
For leaf k with middle parent j,
  Lambda_k,p <=(a_k^+)^p M_j,p.
Roots -> middle -> leaves therefore acquires (M) in finitely many rational
operations for every FIXED p. Although the coefficients depend on leaves
through feedback, the bounds use only the declared structural count factors;
the moment induction does not require causal independence of rate selection.
For fixed state feedback, applying these bounds from zero and taking tight
Cesàro limits also gives finite stationary p-th moments with the same rate-box
constants. Arbitrary history policies retain the all-time moment statements.
The construction does not imply a common stationary law for those policies.

5. COMPLETE EXPECTED FIRING COST FOR RECOVERY

For the initial state x, acquire all species fourth moments from (M). Let
  M_N4=d^3 sum_i M_i,4.
Then E N(t)^4<=M_N4 by Jensen. The total actual firing intensity is bounded
by
  q_t<=b+A N(t)+B N(t)^2,
where b=sum_i b_i^+, A is the sum of all root death and catalytic birth
upper coefficients, and B is the sum of all catalytic death upper coefficients.
Because N is an integer, N^2<=N^4. Thus
  sup_t E q_t^2 <= Q_2=3[b^2+(A^2+B^2)M_N4].         (Q)
This counts EVERY reaction event, including fast catalytic cycles.

Let W=1+V and eta_0=lambda/2. Set
  R_e=max(1,ceil[(2(C+lambda)/lambda-1)/a_0]),
  K_e={N<R_e}, tau=first entry in K_e.
Outside K_e, LW<=-eta_0 W, and the previous stopped argument gives
  E_x exp(eta_0 tau)<=W(x).
Consequently P(tau>s)<=W(x)exp(-eta_0 s). Cauchy-Schwarz and Tonelli yield
  E_x integral_0^tau q_s ds
     <=sqrt(Q_2 W(x)) integral_0^infinity exp(-eta_0 s/2)ds
     =(4/lambda)sqrt(Q_2 W(x)).                      (F)
The left side is the expected total number of reaction firings up to and
including first core entry, by the stopped counting-process compensator.
The bound is finite; localization followed by monotone convergence proves
the equality without presupposing integrability. A rational certificate
replaces the square root by its exact integer ceiling. Starting inside
K_e gives tau=0 and zero firings.
