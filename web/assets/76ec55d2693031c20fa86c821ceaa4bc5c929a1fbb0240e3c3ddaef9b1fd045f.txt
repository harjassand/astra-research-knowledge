STATUS. Internally derived theorem candidate, with full proof below in an explicit
support-respecting aggregate fault model. The QTC decoder support construction is
independently supplied by A04 v2 and is also reconstructed below. Independent
blind audits have been requested. The novelty and scientific magnitude are not
established. This is a quantum-memory/recovery result, not a universal computation
or fault-tolerant input/output theorem. An ideal final decoding map is part of the
logical memory metric; it is not a free physical output operation.

1. EXACT OBJECTIVE AND INPUTS

Given a CSS stabilizer-code family C_n with k_n >= R n, d_n >= d0 n, bounded check
weight w and bounded qubit-check degree q, construct repeated constant-depth
single-shot recovery with O(n) active quantum/control registers and O(n) counted
locations per round. All syndrome measurements, readout, resets, idles, decoder
logic/messages and controlled physical corrections can fail. The bath is never
refreshed and can have arbitrary temporal memory. Prove a logical diamond-error
bound exponential in n without an exact-path norm bound or stochastic support
assumption.

The family and its sparse check matrices/graph are supplied. The construction
does not learn a code or certify a Hamiltonian from calibration data. Its constants
depend on fixed component codes, graph degrees and decoder iteration count. The
hardware contains the QTC incidence links and local controller links, with every
link operation charged. Uniform link latency is an architecture hypothesis;
fixed-dimensional Euclidean locality is not claimed.

The support decoder hypothesis used is concrete and established for QTCs: for
fixed alpha < 1 and beta,A,B,h > 0, the deterministic fixed-iteration parallel
decoder f obeys, for each Pauli component,

  A |e|_red + B |D|_V <= h n
  implies |e + f(syndrome(e)+D)|_red <= alpha |e|_red + beta |D|_V.       (1)

The QTC constants h can be taken min(C_delta,d0/Delta). X and Z decoding are
performed separately. Below, r denotes TOTAL Pauli reduced weight; applying the
two component inequalities gives the same alpha and suitably enlarged beta,A,B
because the component weights are <= total reduced weight and total residual
weight is <= the sum of component residual weights. Constants are chosen to
cover both components. Do not apply a component theorem to total weight without
this enlargement.

2. EXACT PURIFIED NOISE MODEL

Fix a horizon T. Let the complete physical/controller circuit have disjoint round
location sets L_j, |L_j| <= N0 n, for j=1,...,T. Pad with noiseless dummy locations
to N=ceil(N0 n) if convenient. Each elementary ideal location acts on at most a
fixed number a0 of SYSTEM registers. Bath registers are not included in a0.
Fresh ancillas, preparations and measurements are purified: preparation supplies
fresh |0> registers; measurement copies the computational outcome to a retained
record, and a further copy to a discarded environment register implements its
dephasing. Reset garbage is retained in the bath in this description. Ancillas
and controller scratch can be reused physically after reset. The active system
uses O(n) registers even though the mathematical environment grows with T.

The actual complete evolution is an isometry V_T (or a unitary on an enlarged
space), with exact support-respecting coarse fault expansion

  V_T = sum_{F subset L} W_F,   E_T(S) = sum_{F superset S} W_F,
  ||E_T(S)|| <= eta^{|S|} for every nonempty S subset L.                 (2)

The norm is operator norm, uniformly on the input bath space. The fixed initial
fresh registers may be restricted to their |0> subspace after this bound. The
aggregate condition is imposed on the GLOBAL circuit; it includes specified
locations in different rounds. No tensor product of channels, classical random
fault set, independent rounds, bath reset, or bound ||W_F|| <= eta^{|F|} is used.

Support-respecting means that each W_F is a sum/integral of histories containing
ideal system operations away from F and arbitrary operators on the system wires
of locations in F tensored with bath operators at those locations. A fault that
touches two concurrently active locations marks both. Bath operators in a
history can be noncommuting and arbitrarily correlated. Such histories admit a
system Pauli expansion on their marked supports, with operator coefficients on
the bath. Norms of those coefficients are NOT summed in the proof.

The support clause is essential. Bounds (2) on arbitrary unrelated W_F would
not attach those labels to physical errors, and could not imply recovery.

This model is nonempty and physically interpretable. AKP's Hamiltonian theorem
provides (2) for an arbitrary common H_B(t), ideal disjoint local gates H_S(t), and
pairwise system/bath interactions sum_{i<j} H_ij(t), under a uniform row-norm bound
mu = max_{i,t} sum_{j != i} ||H_ij(t)|| t0. For two-qubit gate locations their
bound is eta = C_A sqrt(2 mu), C_A = exp(1-1/(2e)). Its fault labels are precisely
the macrolocations touched by fine-grained interactions. The entire controller,
ancilla/readout dilation and data hardware must be in that SYSTEM model. One
cannot import a bound covering only data gates while declaring classical logic
perfect. Fixed-size local truth tables and readout/reset dilations can be built
from a constant number of one/two-qubit operations, increasing N0 and support
constants but not their n dependence. This is a Hamiltonian hypothesis, not an
empirical promise for conventional electronic readout hardware.

3. COUNTED RECOVERY CIRCUIT AND DETERMINISTIC FAULT INVARIANT

Use one ancilla per constant-weight CSS check. Color the check-data incidence
edges in at most max(w,q) colors, measuring X and Z checks in separate blocks.
Preparation, CNOTs, readout and idle layers therefore use constant depth and O(n)
locations. An elementary extraction fault has bounded system causal cone. Pushing
each Pauli term through the ideal Clifford part gives a data Pauli on <= a_ext
wires and an observed-syndrome flip on <= b_ext vertices per fault, after allowing
an additional q-dependent factor from syndrome changes of final data errors.
The latter factor matters: a fault moved to just before readout can leave records
of a preceding error and a later final data error; the mismatch with the syndrome
of that final error is still supported on checks touching its bounded cone.

For the fixed QTC degree Delta, every decoder local view has Delta^2 bits.
Initial local minimization, the mismatch-improvement test, maximal candidate
choice and local row/column decomposition are fixed finite functions. Fix
deterministic tie breaking. They have finite truth-table implementations with
constant gate count, scratch, depth and fanout, albeit potentially large constants
depending on Delta. In a given one of the four QTC vertex classes, local face
views are disjoint; serialize the four classes and a fixed number ell of parallel
iterations. The decoder therefore has bounded depth D_dec and O(n) locations,
with bounded fan-in/fanout on the incidence/controller graph.

Purify the classical circuit reversibly, retaining any garbage. Ideal truth-table
locations map computational basis strings to computational basis strings. If
truth tables are implemented with two-qubit quantum gates, an arbitrary fault
within their fixed-size block can be pushed through the remaining ideal block
to its end, becoming an arbitrary operator on that constant-size block. Thus
the argument does not assume that faulty classical hardware remains classical.

At most c_dec m_dec correction output bits can be altered by m_dec marked
decoder locations: take the union of their bounded forward cones. On a basis
syndrome input, each basis term of the resulting output operator differs from
the ideal output on only those output bits. Coherent phases or superpositions
remain permitted. Count output-control faults, final one-qubit Pauli corrections
and data idles during decoding in c_dec (or an additional fixed term). Apply
physical corrections each round, rather than maintain an unprotected persistent
Pauli-frame register. All local message transfers and scratch resets are counted.

For every system Pauli term of a support-respecting history, extraction faults
produce e_ext and D with |e_ext| <= a_ext m_ext and |D|_V <= b_ext m_ext. The ideal
decoder acts on that observed syndrome; faults in the decoder/recovery add a
Pauli correction supported on <= c_dec m_dec data wires. The resultant reduced
Pauli weights satisfy

  r_j <= alpha r_{j-1} + K_ext m_ext,j + c_dec m_dec,j,
  K_ext = alpha a_ext + beta b_ext, K = K_ext+c_dec.                    (3)

For total faults f_j=m_ext,j+m_dec,j <= tau n, (3) gives

  r_j <= alpha r_{j-1}+K tau n,
  r_j <= rho n, rho=K tau/(1-alpha), when r_0=0.                       (4)

Choose a positive fixed tau satisfying

  tau [ A(K/(1-alpha)+a_ext)+B b_ext ] < h,
  2 K tau/(1-alpha) < d0.                                             (5)

Such tau exists because all constants are finite and h,d0>0. At every round,
input data reduced weight <= (rho+a_ext tau)n and syndrome corruption <=b_ext
tau n, so (1)'s premise is satisfied, and the output invariant is rho n<d_n/2.
For exact integer thresholds below, put k_fault= floor(tau n)+1: a good round
has |F intersect L_j|<k_fault, hence <=tau n. These constants allow all hardware
fault classes included in (2).

4. CORRECTABLE SYNDROME SUBSYSTEM WITH NO LOGICAL HOLONOMY

Let P_n be the stabilizer code projector and Enc:H_L -> H_D an encoding isometry.
Let m=floor(rho n), 2m<d_n. For every syndrome s reachable by a Pauli of reduced
weight <=m, choose one Pauli R_s with physical weight <=m. If two such Paulis
have the same syndrome, their product has weight <=2m<d_n and commutes with
all stabilizers, so it lies in the stabilizer group up to phase. They therefore
have the same action on the logical code space. Distinct syndrome subspaces are
orthogonal, so

  J: H_L tensor H_Sm -> H_D,
  J(|psi> tensor |s>) = R_s Enc|psi>                                  (6)

is an isometry. Every Pauli Q of stabilizer-reduced weight <=m obeys

  Q Enc = omega_Q R_s(Q) Enc,  |omega_Q|=1.                            (7)

The word reduced is stabilizer-reduced, not merely syndrome-equivalent. A logical
Pauli of zero syndrome is NOT given reduced weight zero. This excludes a hidden
logical action when choosing canonical representatives.

Consider any good global history, any Pauli term of its local system fault
operators, and any input bath vector. Start on Enc|psi>. Ideal stabilizer
extraction of a Pauli error gives a syndrome independent of |psi>. Pauli faults
in the Clifford extraction yield a definite syndrome/record string per term.
The ideal purified decoder computes deterministic functions of those strings.
Decoder faults can be expanded after their local ideal truth-table blocks into
operators on bounded forward cones, so their basis terms give possibly distinct
but definite correction strings, with independent bath coefficients and phases.
Each term's complete action on the DATA is a Pauli Q with reduced weight <=m by
(3)-(5). Every syndrome record, decoder garbage, phase and bath coefficient
depends on the fault term and syndrome, but not on the logical vector |psi>.
Equation (7) then yields a common form

  G_T (Enc tensor I_B) = (J tensor I_environment) (I_L tensor g_T),      (8)

where G_T=sum_{F:every round good} W_F and g_T is a linear operator from the
input bath to the final syndrome+record+garbage+bath space. All coherent sums
over F and Pauli terms are taken before evaluating norms. Cross-path interference
is entirely in g_T. No fault-history postselection or syndrome-conditioned bath
reset is performed. This proves exact logical independence of the good part.

Equivalently choose one isometric final decoder D_n extending J^dagger on the
range of J. Then

  (D_n tensor I_env) G_T (Enc tensor I_B)=I_L tensor g_T.               (9)

The existence of D_n is enough for the logical metric. It need not solve a
minimum-weight problem: the source QTC parallel decoder with perfect syndrome
and O(log n) iterations corrects these errors if tau was additionally chosen
below its exact-decoding input radius. Follow that correction by an encoding
inverse obtained by polynomial-time stabilizer Gaussian elimination. This is
an efficient IDEAL final decoder. Its actual implementation with noisy hardware
is not part of the constant-round-memory theorem.

5. EXACT GLOBAL BAD-HISTORY NORM UNDER AGGREGATE NOISE

For one round with N locations and threshold k=k_fault, define the Boolean mask
b(F)=1[|F|>=k]. Its exact multilinear representation is

  b(F) = sum_{S subset F, |S|>=k} c_|S|,
  c_s=(-1)^(s-k) binom(s-1,k-1).                                     (10)

Proof: for |F|=f<k the sum is zero. For f>=k, write
binom(s-1,k-1)=k/s binom(s,k), hence the sum equals
binom(f,k) k integral_0^1 t^(k-1)(1-t)^(f-k) dt = 1.
The beta integral is (k-1)!(f-k)!/f!. This derivation includes f=k.

Consequently, for any exact expansion and aggregate cylinders,

  sum_{|F|>=k} W_F = sum_{|S|>=k} c_|S| E(S).                         (11)

Let the absolute coefficient norm for a round be

  x_n = sum_{s=k}^N binom(N,s) binom(s-1,k-1) eta^s.
  x_n <= b_n := binom(N,k) eta^k(1+eta)^(N-k).                        (12)

Indeed binom(N,s)binom(s-1,k-1)=(k/s)binom(N,k)binom(N-k,s-k), and k/s<=1.
The final bound is an INEQUALITY; b_n is not the exact absolute coefficient sum.

For disjoint round sets L_j, the bad-global-history mask is

  b_any(F)=1-product_j [1-b_j(F intersect L_j)].                      (13)

Expand the product of (10). The coefficient on a nonempty S is zero unless
every touched round contains at least k of S; otherwise it is a sign times the
product over touched rounds of binom(|S_j|-1,k-1). Its absolute eta-weighted
coefficient sum is exactly product_j(1+x_j)-1. Using GLOBAL E_T(S) from (2),

  B_T:=V_T-G_T,
  ||B_T|| <= product_j(1+x_j)-1 <= (1+b_n)^T-1=:delta_n,T.              (14)

This directly bounds the union of bad rounds with one shared bath. There is no
factorization of physical round operators or channels. A naive coherent union
bound T b_n would not be justified from (2) alone; (14) supplies the exact
replacement. For T b_n<=1, delta_n,T <= exp(T b_n)-1 <= (e-1)T b_n.

6. NORMALIZATION OF THE GOOD PART AND LOGICAL DIAMOND ERROR

Apply final D_n and restrict the physical input to clean encoded data. Write

  V=(D_n tensor I) V_T(Enc tensor I_B),  V^dagger V=I,
  G=I_L tensor g_T,  ||V-G||<=delta=delta_n,T.                          (15)

For every unit input vector x,
  1-delta <= ||Gx|| <= 1+delta.
In particular singular values of g_T lie in [1-delta,1+delta]. If delta<1,
g_T^dagger g_T is bounded below, and its polar isometry

  h_T=g_T(g_T^dagger g_T)^(-1/2)

obeys h_T^dagger h_T=I and ||g_T-h_T||<=delta. This follows from the spectral
bound on sqrt(g_T^dagger g_T), and holds for an infinite-dimensional bath when
the stated uniform operator bound holds. Therefore

  ||V-I_L tensor h_T||<=2delta.                                      (16)

For two isometries A,B, the induced channels differ in diamond norm by at most
2||A-B||, by expanding A rho A^dagger-B rho B^dagger and retaining an arbitrary
reference. Partial trace cannot increase that norm. The normalized reference
isometry I_L tensor h_T exactly preserves the reduced logical state, even for
logical/bath/reference correlations. Thus the actual logical memory channel,
for any fixed initial bath state, satisfies

  || D_n circle Memory_T circle Enc - id_L ||_diamond
       <= 4[(1+b_n)^T-1],                                            (17)

whenever the bracket is <1; otherwise use the trivial channel bound 2. The polar
isometry is a proof device, not a physical operation, recovery conditioned on
good histories, or an acquired description of the bath.

7. EXPLICIT POSITIVE THRESHOLD AND EXPONENTIAL MEMORY LIFETIME

For exact N=N0 n and k>=tau n (and eta small enough that eN0 eta/tau<1),

  b_n <= (eN0 eta/tau)^k exp(N0 eta n) <= exp(-c n),
  c=tau log[tau/(eN0 eta)]-N0 eta.                                   (18)

Choose eta>0 so c>0. An explicit sufficient choice is

  eta <= tau/(e^3 N0),
  c >= (2-e^(-3))tau > 0.                                            (19)

If N is padded/rounded, increase N0 to a fixed integer bound so N<=N0 n; (18)
then holds unchanged. With k=floor(tau n)+1, good rounds obey the integer premise
and k>tau n, which strengthens the estimate when the base is <1.

For target 0<epsilon<2, any horizon

  T <= exp(c n) log(1+epsilon/4)                                      (20)

has delta<epsilon/4<1 and logical diamond error <=epsilon. For a fixed error
budget this is an exponential number of noisy recovery rounds, while storing
at least Rn logical qubits. Per round: O(n) active data/ancilla/controller bits,
O(n) local quantum/control/link/reset/readout locations, bounded graph depth,
and one noisy extraction of each CSS check. Total work O(nT), active space O(n),
and O(n) communication edges. Offline code construction/description generation
is supplied; local truth tables are fixed. No probability oracle, actual fault
realization, model learning or bath reset is supplied to the decoder.

Under the AKP pair-Hamiltonian model covering ALL hardware, a sufficient row-norm
threshold is

  mu <= eta0^2/(2 C_A^2), eta0=tau/(e^3 N0).                          (21)

This threshold is positive and independent of n,T. Its constants may be very
small: the proof does not claim a useful numerical hardware threshold. Actual
physical wall-clock constant time requires bounded latency on the incidence
links; Euclidean routing/long-wire cost is not hidden inside O(n) gate counts.
For code families with bounded ratios of consecutive admissible lengths, padding
permits O(M+log(T/epsilon)) physical/control space to preserve M logical qubits;
without that family-size property, (20) is asserted only at its supplied lengths.

8. LIMITS, COUNTEREXAMPLES AND FAILED REPLACEMENTS

(a) Individual error marginals or individual reduced-channel diamond distances
do not imply (2). A minimum-weight logical L applied with probability p has local
event marginals <=p and logical error p independent of block size. Its specified
set event probability does not decay as p^|S|. The analogous shared-bath controlled
logical rotation also invalidates marginal-only claims.

(b) ||E_T(S)||<=eta^|S| does not imply ||W_F||<=eta^|F|, and an arbitrary selected
subsum need not have a coherent union norm bounded by its number of bad rounds.
The exact coefficients in (10)-(14) are needed. Absolute Pauli coefficients and
exact-path amplitudes were never summed in this proof.

(c) Merely ending in a SMALL SYNDROME does not suffice for (8). A logical Pauli
has zero syndrome. The deterministic guarantee is stabilizer-reduced PAULI
weight, so (7) rules out this failure. This distinction is essential.

(d) Treating G_T as a trace-preserving channel would be invalid. It is a coherent
masked operator sum; its norm can exceed 1. Equations (15)-(17) normalize it
without a measurement/postselection operation or independence assumption.

(e) Fixed-dimensional Euclidean-local stabilizer constraints cannot give this
finite rate plus linear distance family under BPT hypotheses. The graph-hardware
model is explicit. Additional routing can change depth, spread, fault budget and
threshold; no transfer is asserted.

(f) The theorem ends with encoded protected information. Efficient ideal final
decoding defines its metric. Fully noisy quantum input/output, arbitrary logical
gates, and a universal constant-overhead computer require additional gadgets.
No such result follows from this memory proof by naming existing threshold
theorems. A04 v2's demand to include final logical readout is stronger than the
present memory target; that end-to-end physical-output interface remains open.
