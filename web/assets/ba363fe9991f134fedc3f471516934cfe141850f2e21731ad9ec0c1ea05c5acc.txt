OUTCOME

The assignment's exact reformulation was completed in immutable INITIAL.txt: for every finite likelihood frame and prior, q_p is the minimum of Tr(eta K_p) over eta in conv{P⊗P : average P=I/d}, equivalently a restricted symmetric-separable two-copy Choi optimization, with the exact dual

  q_p = sup_{Y=Y*} tau(Y),  subject to <psi|Y|psi> <= sum_x p_x Var_psi(H_x) for all pure psi.

This identifies a precise coherent-product/separability constraint. It is not the unrestricted separable state set and not the set of all symmetric Choi extensions. A Bella-state relaxation counterexample is given below.

Two post-INITIAL advances sharpen the use and limits of the representation:

1. `revisions/REVISION_01.txt` gives an exact family {X_i,Z_i}_{i=1}^n with q_p=1/2 while the normalized prior-pair mean squared commutator is 2/n. An exact dual witness is Y=I/2; the matching 4^n-outcome product Pauli eigenstate frame is a primal witness. This rules out any inference q_p->0 from the prior-pair average alone. It does not refute q_p<=2 E: the tensor-product qubit cloner has mean energy 1/3, ratio q_p/E=3/2, and the peer pairwise bound remains valid.

2. `revisions/REVISION_02.txt` proves q_p is exactly the weighted diagonal SLD-QFI loss minimized over one common POVM for the local tangent model rho_theta=(I+sum theta_x H_x)/d at I/d. It is the lower convex roof of the *sum* of pure-state variances at I/d. Yu's cited one-generator unitary QFI convex-roof theorem does not solve this common-ensemble problem; separately optimal eigenbases can differ. This reframing clarifies the metrological meaning but no general structural measurement construction follows.

SEPARABILITY RELAXATION FAILURE

For n=1 and equal X,Z prior, q_p=1/2. The Bell state |Phi+> has maximally mixed marginals, is swap-symmetric, and gives Tr(eta K_p)=0 because XX and ZZ correlations are both +1. It is entangled, not a mixture of P⊗P. Thus allowing arbitrary symmetric two-qubit states in the primal destroys the value; the coherent-product/separability constraint cannot be dropped. This does not show that any compatible-channel Choi extension gives a bad rounding, because the optimized primal and a channel's Choi extension play different roles.
