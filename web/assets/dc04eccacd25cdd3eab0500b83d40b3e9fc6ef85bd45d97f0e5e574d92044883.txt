RESULT

Let n=2j, d=n+1, N=n(n+2), L=l(l+1), z=L/sqrt(N), and let

  u_k=sqrt[(2k+1)(2l+1)] {j j k; j j l},   k=0,...,n,

be the normalized real recoupling column. Thus sum_k u_k^2=1 and

  eta_l(l)=(-1)^n d {j j l; j j l}
          =(-1)^n d u_l/(2l+1).                         (1)

For every sequence n->infinity with l/n->0 and z->infinity, the exact
Jacobi recurrence and normalization imply

  u_l^2 <= C/W,     W=ceil(max{l,n/sqrt(z)}),            (2)

for an absolute C and all sufficiently advanced terms of the sequence.
Consequently

  u_l^2=o(l^2/n^2),
  eta_l(l)^2=O(z^(-1/2)), and eta_l(l)->0.                (3)

The window [l,l+W] is explicit, has W>=l, and W=o(n): at least a
theta(l)-length propagation window is certified, but no fixed positive
fraction of the full column is needed or certified. It contains at least
order sqrt(n) local oscillation periods. The scale n/sqrt(z) is the
delocalization enlargement needed when l alone is too short.

Combining (3) with -log(mu_l)>=L/d and z->infinity gives mu_l->0 and hence

  q_(n,l)=(1-eta_l(l))/(1-mu_l)^2 -> 1.                 (4)

In particular no q->0 sequence survives the residual regime. Together
with the already recorded low/log-band and fixed-ratio/endpoint reductions
in RACAH_BESSEL_LOG_BAND.txt and RACAH_MIDDLE_COMPACTNESS.txt, this proves
that inf_(n>=1,1<=l<=n) q_(n,l)>0. The proof is existential: it gives no
effective global lower constant or finite-spin cutoff. This closes the
scalar Racah gate at the level of a dimension-free positive constant, not
at a numerically certified value. The separate compatible-broadcaster
inequality can then turn that positive scalar infimum into an all-mode
coherent comparison with some finite, non-explicit constant.

1. EXACT RECURRENCE IN DIVERGENCE FORM

The column from RACAH_MIDDLE_COMPACTNESS.txt satisfies, for 1<=k<=n-1,

  L u_k=A_k u_k+B_k u_(k+1)+B_(k-1)u_(k-1),
  A_k=[N-k(k+1)]/2,
  B_k=(k+1)[d^2-(k+1)^2]/[2 sqrt((2k+1)(2k+3))].        (5)

Set v_k=(-1)^k u_k and a_k=B_k. Then

  L v_k=A_k v_k-a_k v_(k+1)-a_(k-1)v_(k-1).

Define

  q_k=A_k-a_k-a_(k-1),  V_k=L-q_k,
  F_k=a_k(v_(k+1)-v_k).

An exact rearrangement, with no continuum approximation, is

  F_k-F_(k-1)=-V_k v_k.                                 (6)

For the coefficient estimates, put

  alpha_m=(1-1/(4m^2))^(-1/2),
  c_m=(d^2-m^2)/4,     h_m=c_m(alpha_m-1).

Then a_k=c_(k+1)alpha_(k+1), and direct subtraction gives

  q_k=-1/4-h_k-h_(k+1).                                  (7)

For 1<=m<d, both c_m and alpha_m-1 are positive and decreasing, so
a_k and V_k=L+1/4+h_k+h_(k+1) are positive and decreasing on the
interior range used below. Also alpha_m-1<=1/(4m^2), whence

  0< -q_k <= 1/4+d^2/(8k^2).                             (8)

For l/n->0, z->infinity, we have l->infinity and n->infinity. On every
integer k in [l,l+W], with W as in (2), k/n->0 uniformly because
l/n->0 and W/n=max(l/n,1/sqrt(z))+o(1)->0. Therefore

  a_k/n^2 -> 1/4 uniformly,                              (9)
  V_k/L = 1+O(n^2/(l^2 L))+O(1/L)
        = 1+O(z^(-2))+o(1) uniformly.                    (10)

In particular, the total relative variation of each of a_k and V_k
across this whole window tends to zero. The local frequency

  p_k=sqrt(V_k/a_k)

satisfies p_k=(2l/n)(1+o(1)) uniformly; hence p_k->0 and

  W p_l >= (n/sqrt(z))(2l/n)(1+o(1))
          =2l/sqrt(z)(1+o(1)) ~ 2 N^(1/4) ~ 2 sqrt(n) -> infinity.
                                                               (11)

The last relation uses sqrt(L)/l->1. Thus the window contains many local
periods even though its length is o(n).

2. DISCRETE ADIABATIC PROPAGATION LEMMA

Lemma. Suppose positive nonincreasing sequences a_k,V_k on an interval
I=[K,K+W) obey

  max_I p_k ->0,   sup_I |p_k/p_K-1| ->0,
  log(a_K/a_(K+W))+log(V_K/V_(K+W)) ->0,
  W p_K -> infinity,       p_k=sqrt(V_k/a_k).             (12)

Every real solution of (6), with F_k=a_k(v_(k+1)-v_k), then satisfies,
for an absolute c>0 eventually,

  sum_(k in I) v_k^2 >= c W v_K^2.                       (13)

Proof. Divide I into consecutive blocks of length
m=ceil(8 pi/p_K), discarding the final incomplete block. The hypotheses
give m p_K=8 pi+o(1), p_k/p_K=1+o(1) uniformly, and the number of full
blocks tends to infinity. On a block starting at r, freeze
a=a_r, V=V_r, s=sqrt(aV), p=sqrt(V/a), and use the state
X_k=(v_k,F_(k-1)/s). With frozen coefficients, (6) is exactly

  X_(k+1)=M(p)X_k,
  M(p)=[[1-p^2,p],[-p,1]].                               (14)

The matrix has determinant one, trace 2-p^2, eigenvalues exp(+-i theta)
where cos(theta)=1-p^2/2, and preserves

  Q_p=[[1,-p/2],[-p/2,1]].                              (15)

For p small, Q_p is uniformly comparable to the identity. The first
coordinate of a frozen solution is A cos(t theta)+B sin(t theta), where
the map from X_r to (A,B) and its inverse have uniformly bounded norms.
Since m theta=8 pi+o(1), the Gram matrix of these sine/cosine samples has
smallest eigenvalue at least c_0 m: indeed the nonconstant geometric sum
has modulus at most 1/|sin(theta)|=O(1/p), whereas m is asymptotic to
8 pi/p. Thus a frozen block has

  sum_block v_k^2 >= c_1 m ||X_r||^2,                    (16)

and its end-state Q_p-energy equals its start-state Q_p-energy.

For the actual varying coefficients, still in the block's fixed scale s,
the one-step matrix is

  M_k=[[1-V_k/a_k, s/a_k],[-V_k/s,1]].                   (17)

If delta_r is the sum of the relative variations of a and V over this
block, then |M_k-M(p)|<=C p delta_r. The frozen powers are bounded
uniformly (by (15)); since m p=O(1), the product perturbation estimate
shows that the actual block trajectory differs from the frozen one by
at most C delta_r ||X_r|| at every step. Taking delta_r small enough,
(16) remains true with c_1/2. The block-end energy changes by a factor
1+O(delta_r). At the next block, changing the flux scale s and the
quadratic form Q_p costs another factor 1+O(delta_r+delta_(r+1)); these
changes are controlled by the same parameter variations.

Because a and V are monotone, the sum of all block relative variations
is bounded by a constant times
log(a_K/a_(K+W))+log(V_K/V_(K+W))=o(1). Multiplication of the block
energy estimates therefore keeps every block-start energy comparable
to the initial one by a universal factor (in fact 1+o(1)). Summing (16)
over the full blocks gives a lower bound c W ||X_K||^2, since their
combined length is W(1-o(1)); this is at least cW v_K^2. The discarded
tail is not needed. This proves (13).  QED.

The perturbation estimate uses only the exact matrices (14),(17), the
small local frequency, and the total monotone variation. It does not
assume a uniform WKB expansion, a stationary-phase formula, or an
unverified asymptotic remainder.

3. APPLYING THE LEMMA AND THE TWO-SCALE WINDOW

Take K=l and W=ceil(max{l,n/sqrt(z)}). For large terms, l+W<n, so (5)-(6)
hold throughout the propagation interval. Equations (9)-(11), monotonicity
and positivity verify all hypotheses (12). Column normalization and
(13) now give

  1 >= sum_(k=l..l+W-1) v_k^2 >= c W v_l^2,
  u_l^2=v_l^2 <= C/W <= C sqrt(z)/n.                     (18)

The window choice yields W>=n/sqrt(z), so W/(n/z)>=sqrt(z)->infinity.
Since z~l^2/n, (18) gives

  u_l^2/(l^2/n^2) <= C n sqrt(z)/l^2 = O(z^(-1/2))->0.  (19)

By the exact normalization relation (1),

  eta_l(l)^2 <= C [n^2/l^2][sqrt(z)/n]
              = O(z^(-1/2)),                            (20)

and so eta_l(l)->0. This is stronger than merely bounding eta away from
1. The uniform asymptotic bound along every residual sequence is
eta_l(l)=O(z^(-1/4)); constants in the propagation lemma are absolute,
but its “eventually” threshold has not been converted into an explicit
function of n,l.

Finally

  -log(mu_l)=2 sum_(s=1..l) artanh(s/d) >= L/d ~ z,

so mu_l->0. Substituting eta->0 and mu->0 proves (4). If a global
sequence had q->0, the previous fixed-z, logarithmic-band, and
fixed-ratio Jacobi compactness results force it into exactly this
residual regime, where q->1, a contradiction. Every finite pair has
q>0 by the earlier commuting-algebra argument; therefore the global
infimum is positive. No exact value of that infimum is asserted.
