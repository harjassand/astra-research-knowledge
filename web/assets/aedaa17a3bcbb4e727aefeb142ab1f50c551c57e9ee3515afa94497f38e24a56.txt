S1. Laure Saint-Raymond, “Hydrodynamic limits: some improvements of the relative entropy method,” Annales de l’Institut Henri Poincaré C, Analyse non linéaire 26 (2009), 705–744. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anihpc.2008.01.001 ; official article page: https://ems.press/journals/aihpc/articles/4077166 ; source PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/16282 . Submitted 2007-09-04, accepted 2008-01-14, published June 2009. Primary theorem/baseline.
Read evidence: Section 2.1 (pp. 712–713) states that general-data energy-flux control is a third-moment issue and cannot follow from relative entropy alone because the entropy Young inequality has quadratic velocity growth; Theorem 2.1 condition (2.3) adds a large-velocity estimate; Remark 2.2(i) identifies classical Guo solutions as an example satisfying tail control. Section 3.3, Lemma 3.5, decomposes the flux and bounds its contribution using modulated entropy plus entropy dissipation. Abstract says this extension requires nonuniform large-tail control.
Role: closest primary baseline; validates that the proposed weakness is a known proof requirement, and fixes the status as method obstruction rather than theorem refutation.

S2. Laure Saint-Raymond, “Convergence of Solutions to the Boltzmann Equation in the Incompressible Euler Limit,” Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis 166 (2003), 47–80. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-002-0228-3 ; publisher page: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00205-002-0228-3 . Published January 2003 (online December 2002). Primary predecessor.
Read evidence: publisher abstract says the incompressible Euler derivation is for well-prepared initial data and “without any additional assumption.” S1’s retrospective distinguishes a prior Golse proof with an extra nonlinear weak-compactness condition from Saint-Raymond’s well-prepared result, where refined dissipation estimates removed that condition.
Role: baseline comparison only; no claim that this well-prepared theorem covers the general-data cubic-flux question here.

S3. Yan Guo, “Classical solutions to the Boltzmann equation for molecules with an angular cutoff,” Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis 169 (2003), 305–353. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-003-0262-9 ; publisher page: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00205-003-0262-9 . Published 28 May 2003. Primary stronger-solution baseline.
Read evidence: bibliographic record and title/abstract identify construction of classical solutions near Maxwellians under the Grad angular-cutoff framework; S1 Remark 2.2(i) explicitly points to Guo-built classical solutions as satisfying its large-tail estimate.
Role: example of stronger solution regularity/weighted control supplying information not encoded in entropy alone.

S4. Yan Guo, “The Vlasov-Poisson-Boltzmann system near Maxwellians,” Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics 55 (2002), 1104–1135. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.10040 . First published 2002-06-25. Primary contextual baseline (coupled VPB problem).
Read evidence: official Wiley abstract describes smooth periodic perturbations near a global Maxwellian and a global classical-solution result under conservation constraints. S1 also cites Guo's nearby-Maxwellian classical framework.
Role: contextual evidence that classical near-Maxwellian theories impose regularity and conservation structure substantially stronger than a bare relative-entropy bound; not used as the direct Boltzmann theorem.
