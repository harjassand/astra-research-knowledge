Central completed theorem
-------------------------
If a group has an injective rank-metric ultraproduct model over finite fields
of one fixed characteristic p (degrees may vary), its group algebra is stably
finite over every field of characteristic p. Proof: extend each coordinate
field to contain an arbitrary finite F_{p^d}, restrict scalars while
preserving normalized rank, apply AP Theorem 7.4 and Proposition 2.8, then
specialize any hypothetical matrix one-sided inverse over an arbitrary field
to a finite extension using a polynomial system plus Zariski's lemma.
