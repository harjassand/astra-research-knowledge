Primary sources checked
-----------------------
1. Pestov–Kwiatkowska, “An Introduction to Hyperlinear and Sofic Groups,” PDF pp. 12–14, Theorem 4.2 and its proof. The proof re-embeds an arbitrary faithful HS metric-ultraproduct embedding as an orthonormal system using the conjugation representation. The local reconstruction records the normalized trace transformation explicitly.
   https://www.math.cmu.edu/~eschimme/Appalachian/PestovNotes.pdf
2. Steinberg, “Stable finiteness of ample groupoid algebras, traces and applications,” introduction pp. 2–3 and Proposition 3.5 pp. 9–10. It states characteristic-zero group algebras are stably finite, positive-characteristic group-algebra stable finiteness remains open, and that stable finiteness over every finite field is the field-reduction gate for arbitrary commutative coefficient rings.
   https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/52394
3. Bartholdi–de Cornulier–Kochloukova, “Hopfian wreath products and the stable finiteness conjecture,” Corollary 1.8 and Corollary 3.16. The first reduces stable finiteness in characteristic p from one field to all fields of that characteristic; the latter reduces Kaplansky’s stable-finiteness conjecture to direct finiteness over prime fields F_p. The proof uses local embeddings of finite coefficient sets into matrix algebras over F_p.
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00209-024-03589-3
