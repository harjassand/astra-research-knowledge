# Initial routes rejected or demoted

Date: 2026-10-08. These are route-level findings, not evidence that the biological system is ineffective.

1. **Treating the slow FCS component as proven receptor-bound Fog.** The 2026 article interprets the slow pool as membrane-associated or interacting, but the molecular identity is not established. Consequence: it cannot yet be inserted as a receptor-bound concentration in a validated transport PDE.
2. **Inferring biological clearance directly from MyoII length.** The MyoII profile is an output of Fog/GPCR, integrin, adhesion, contact, and contractility feedback. Equating its e-fold length to the active ligand-pool length is an unverified identification. The computed `k` values are conditional inversions only.
3. **Equating FCS mobility to lateral mobility in a material-cell frame.** FCS measures a local apical component; it does not by itself show that this is the effective long-range mobility in a deforming, moving epithelial sheet. Same-condition cell-relative `D` remains missing.
4. **Explaining the signal profile by a free-Fog concentration gradient.** This route conflicts with the 2026 source's observed near-uniform extracellular Fog::YFP. Do not use the very large `D_fast/v` scale as evidence for a free-ligand tail.
5. **Using a spatial exponential as evidence for transport.** A cell-local wave-timed response can reproduce the same profile, and in fact any one-dimensional snapshot can be mapped to a local age waveform over its observed interval. The spatial profile alone is non-identifying.
6. **Maximizing Fog/MyoII amplitude as the intervention objective.** The source reports that greater range/amplitude can strengthen adhesion, delay de-adhesion, and slow the wave. Use a calibrated reach-speed frontier with an adhesion/contact constraint instead.
7. **Promoting the linear moving-frame equation as a new morphogen-transport mechanism.** Receptor binding, endocytosis, transport, and degradation are established components of morphogen models. No novelty or priority claim for this equation or reaction-transport class is supported.
8. **Claiming a new regenerative or wound-healing capability from the Hydra analogy.** The 2025 Hydra paper is a stronger prior-art baseline for mechanically biasing organizer position; no cross-species controller, wound closure intervention, repair outcome, or safety result was tested here.

## What changes the decision

The leading candidate survives only as a testable model comparison. A paired Lagrangian experiment measuring cell-relative front speed, effective slow-pool mobility and clearance, contact age, and MyoII trajectory can either make the moving-pool law predictive or collapse it in favor of a cell-local response model. If passive range feasibility fails, retain an integrin/contact-gated source term as a separate candidate; do not patch the passive law silently.
