STRONGEST CLAIM WITHOUT AN EXTRA GEOMETRY IMPORT

Fix any d>=2, alpha>0 and finite B. Let T_1,...,T_(d^2-1) be traceless
Hermitian with Tr T_aT_b=delta_ab, F_a=sum_i T_a^(i), and Q_C=sum C_ab
{F_a,F_b}/2. There is an EXPLICIT delta_*(d,alpha,B)>0 such that for EVERY
N>=1, real symmetric A with ||A||<=delta_*, and real b with ||b||<=B,

       exp[Q_(alphaI+A)/N+F_b]/Tr exp[Q_(alphaI+A)/N+F_b]

is exactly fully separable across its N qudits. The radius is extremely small,
and no arbitrary-anisotropy claim is made. Full separability permits different
local factors in each product term. Exact membership in the identical-product
mixture cone DOES NOT follow. Permutation symmetry only permits permutation-
averaging those product terms; it does not make them identical products.

For fixed alpha>0,gamma>0,M,B, the vanishing-anisotropy models
 Q_(alphaI+A N^-gamma)/N+F_b with ||A||<=M are also exactly fully separable
for all sufficiently large N, uniformly over A,b. This follows already from
the stopped exp[-cN(logN)^2] error and its polynomial full-rank cutoff.

1. AN ELEMENTARY MULTIPARTITE FULLY SEPARABLE BALL

Let D=d^N. Supply a local Hermitian HS-orthonormal basis
 T_0=I/sqrt d, T_a traceless, a=1,...,d^2-1. Since ||T_a||_op<=||T_a||_2=1,
each (I+s T_a)/d is a density for s=+-1. For a tensor-basis label
v=(v_1,...,v_N)!=0 with nonidentity support S of size r>=1, set
 P_v=(tensor_(i in S)T_(v_i)) tensor (tensor_(i outside S)I).

The two operators (I+-P_v)/D are fully separable. Indeed choose the r signs
uniformly subject to their product being the desired +-1, and average the
local product states (I+s_i T_(v_i))/d (with I/d outside S). Every proper
nonempty sign character averages to zero and the full character equals +-1,
leaving exactly (I+-P_v)/D. At r=1 the same formula has one sign only.

Any Hermitian trace-zero E expands as E=sum_(v!=0)e_v tensor_i T_(v_i),
with real e_v and sum e_v^2=||E||_2^2. Since
tensor_i T_(v_i)=d^(-(N-r)/2)P_v,
one has

 I/D+E=(1-sum |a_v|) I/D
          +sum |a_v| [I+sign(a_v)P_v]/D,
 a_v=d^((N+r)/2)e_v.

When sum|a_v|<=1 this is an explicit convex combination of fully separable
states. Cauchy--Schwarz gives
 sum|a_v|<=d^N sum|e_v|
 <=d^N sqrt(d^(2N)-1)||E||_2<=d^(2N)||E||_1.
Consequently

                ||E||_1<=d^(-2N)                       (E1)

is a sufficient ball radius. This proof gives positivity too; no PPT
sufficiency, bipartite-to-multipartite substitution or unverified source bound
is used. A weighted Cauchy--Schwarz improves the radius somewhat, but (E1) is
sufficient. Standard separable-ball literature is prior art; no novelty of a
separable ball is asserted.

2. WHITE-NOISE COMPONENT OF THE STOPPED POSITIVE MIXTURE

If every local rho in an identical-product mixture sigma obeys rho>=etaI,
0<eta<1/d, write rho=d eta(I/d)+(1-d eta)rho', where rho' is a density.
Expand rho^tensorN. Its all-white term has common weight w=(d eta)^N,
and every other term is a product of positive local densities. Averaging gives

        sigma=w I/D+(1-w)tau_sep,     w=(d eta)^N,       (E2)

with tau_sep fully separable. It is generally a mixture of nonidentical
products. Put E=rho_target-sigma; it is trace zero. If

              ||E||_1<=w d^(-2N)=(eta/d)^N,             (E3)

then (E1) applies to E/w and
 rho_target=w[I/D+E/w]+(1-w)tau_sep
is exactly fully separable. A stopped half-trace error T<=epsilon contributes
||E||_1<=2epsilon; this factor two is retained below.

5. COSTS, PRIMARY SOURCES, AND LIMITS

The elementary ball proof is finite and constructive when dense E is supplied.

have 2^(r-1) product terms. Forming the dense Gibbs matrix costs its exponential
output size; obtaining exact coefficient descriptions can be costly. The
theorem does not supply a compact exact separable decomposition, efficient
sampler, polynomial classical simulation, quantum state-preparation compiler,

hit/weight and product emission remain unimplemented. A worst weight exp(O(N))
