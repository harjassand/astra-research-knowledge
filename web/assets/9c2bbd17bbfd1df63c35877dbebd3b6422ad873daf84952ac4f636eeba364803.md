# Connected nowhere-zero counterexample to topological-entropy power substitution

## Claim

Even if the intended model requires a **connected** closed 3-manifold and a **nowhere-zero** smooth divergence-free field, the total-volume substitution `h_mu(phi_1) -> h_top(phi_1)` in

```text
W >= 4 nu V h_mu(phi_1)^2
```

is false. In fact, for a fixed connected manifold `T^3` and each fixed `nu>0`, there is a family of smooth metrics `g_c` and smooth nowhere-zero `g_c`-divergence-free fields `X` for which `h_top(phi_1)` is one fixed positive number while `W_c -> 0` and `V_c` stays bounded below by a positive constant. No claim about topological entropy on arbitrary connected 3D flows is needed.

## Imported seed and its scope

Berger–Turaev, *On Herman’s Positive Entropy Conjecture*, Theorem A and §3.4, construct a smooth standard-area-preserving disk diffeomorphism `f` with a positive-area stochastic island and with `f=id` near the disk boundary. The island has positive smooth-area metric entropy, so `h_top(f)>0`. Section 3.4 explicitly extends the map by the identity outside a disk. This is the only non-elementary dynamical input here; its construction/priority is imported, not reproved. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/1704.02473 .

## Connected construction

Embed the disk in a flat `T^2`. Since `f` is identity near its boundary and the disk is simply connected, choose a smooth area-preserving isotopy `f_z`, `0<=z<=1`, from `id` to `f`, supported inside the disk. Reparameterize it to be constant near `z=0` and `z=1`; then its Eulerian velocity

```text
V_z = (d f_z/dz) o f_z^(-1)
```

is a smooth 1-periodic family of standard-area-divergence-free vector fields, all supported in one fixed compact subdisk `K`. On `M=T^2 x S^1_z`, set

```text
X(x,z) = ∂_z + V_z(x).
```

The field is smooth and nowhere zero because its `z` component is one. With the flat product metric `g_0`, it is divergence-free. The invariant section `T^2 x {0}` of the time-one map restricts to `f`, so

```text
h_top(phi_1) >= h_top(f) =: h_0 > 0.
```

In fact every section `{z=z_0}` is invariant under `phi_1`; one section suffices.

Choose nested disks `K subset D_0 subset D_1 subset T^2`, with `D_1` still a proper disk. For any `0<c<1`, choose a smooth positive radial function `sigma_c(x)` equal to `c` on a neighborhood of `D_0`, equal to `1` outside `D_1`, and interpolating only in `D_1\\D_0`. Define the smooth connected metric

```text
g_c = sigma_c(x)^2 (dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2),
```

using the product flat coordinates locally on the torus. It is independent of `z`; therefore `∂_z` is Killing. The metric volume density is `sigma_c^3 dx dy dz`. On `supp(V_z)`, `sigma_c=c` is constant and `div_{dxdy} V_z=0`; in the interpolation annulus `V_z=0`; and everywhere `∂_z(sigma_c^3)=0`. The coordinate divergence formula consequently gives

```text
div_(g_c) X = sigma_c^(-3) [∂_x(sigma_c^3 V_z^x)
                              + ∂_y(sigma_c^3 V_z^y)
                              + ∂_z(sigma_c^3)] = 0.
```

The strain localizes exactly. Since `∂_z` is Killing, `Def_(g_c) ∂_z=0`. On a neighborhood of `supp(V_z)`, `g_c=c^2 g_0`, so its Levi-Civita connection and the adjoint of an endomorphism are unchanged by this constant scale; thus the strain endomorphism and its squared norm there equal those for `V_z` under `g_0`. Off `supp(V_z)`, `X=∂_z`, hence its strain is zero. If

```text
E_0 = ∫_(T^3) |Def_(g_0) X|_(g_0)^2 dVol_(g_0),
```

then `E_0>0` (otherwise `X` would be a flat-torus Killing field and its time-one map would have zero entropy), and exactly

```text
∫_(T^3) |Def_(g_c) X|_(g_c)^2 dVol_(g_c) = c^3 E_0.
V_c = ∫_(T^3) sigma_c^3 dx dy dz >= Vol_(g_0)(T^2\\D_1) > 0.
```

For the admitted strain-viscosity convention, choose any smooth pressure, e.g. `p=0`, and define the smooth residual force `F_c=∇^c_X X - 2 nu div_(g_c)(Def_(g_c) X)+grad_(g_c)p`. Pairing with `X` on the closed manifold gives

```text
W_c = ∫ <F_c,X> dVol_(g_c) = 2 nu c^3 E_0 -> 0.
```

Meanwhile `h_top(phi_1)>=h_0` is independent of `c` and `V_c` has a fixed positive lower bound. Therefore, for all sufficiently small `c`,

```text
W_c < 4 nu V_c h_top(phi_1)^2.
```

The same limit contradicts any proposed bound `W>=C nu V h_top^2` with a fixed `C>0` under this interface. Each individual metric remains smooth and positive definite; no degenerate metric is used. The normalized-volume entropy can tend to zero consistently with the valid metric-entropy inequality, while the topological entropy remains fixed.

## Scope, exposure, and status

This construction is connected, closed, 3-dimensional, smooth, autonomous, divergence-free for its own Riemannian volume, and nowhere-zero. It uses a fixed positive physical time-one clock and the supplied strain-viscosity force convention. It does not provide an actuator cost, a bound uniform over metrics, or a claim about fixed metric (here the metric varies with `c`). It is a counterexample to a universal total-volume topological-entropy substitution when metric/volume choice is part of the input. The earlier disconnected Sol-plus-flat witness was simpler but depended on manifold-component conventions; this is the connected repair.
