CYCLE 3 LEAD RESULT — FINITE POSITIVE NEWTON APPROXIMATION
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE. The theorem and estimates below are proved in this record. The upstream N125 package is SOURCE-REPORTED / source-derived and externally unreviewed; no novelty or application-level claim is made.

1. Precise statement

Let N>=1 be an integer, k in {0,...,N}, x=(k-N/2)^2, a=1/N, and A_r=(N/2+r)^2 for r>=1. Let P_{2N-1} be the degree-at-most-(2N-1) polynomial interpolating f(y)=exp(-y/N) at A_1,...,A_{2N}. Then:

(i) P_{2N-1}(x) is a positive Newton polynomial: every Newton coefficient and every evaluated Newton basis term is nonnegative;
(ii) uniformly over all N+1 values of k,

       0 <= 1 - P_{2N-1}(x)/exp(-x/N) <= 2 exp(-N/40).

Consequently the positive Newton/Beta-mixture truncation has degree 2N-1 and relative error exp(-Omega(N)) at this scaling. It does not give exact interpolation away from the nodes, does not cancel the infinite-series defect, and does not establish a larger-radius separability or effective hitting-set theorem.

2. Exact identity and finite proof

Put lambda_{r,k}=A_r-x=(r+k)(r+N-k)>0, and let independent E_r have exponential rates lambda_{r,k}; T_{m,k}=sum_{r=1}^m E_r. The Newton/Lagrange identity is

       P_{m-1}(x) = exp(-x/N) Pr[T_{m,k}>1/N].                 (1)

For verification, the Lagrange expression at x is
  sum_i exp(-A_i/N) product_{j!=i}(x-A_j)/(A_i-A_j)
=sum_i exp(-x/N) exp(-lambda_i/N)
      product_{j!=i} lambda_j/(lambda_j-lambda_i),
which is the hypoexponential survival formula. It also follows by partial fractions of (1/s)(1-product_r lambda_r/(s+lambda_r)). This derives the identity from finite interpolation; it is not an infinite-series assertion. For each Newton order ell, the coefficient is (-1)^ell f[A_1,...,A_{ell+1}]>0 by the divided-difference mean-value theorem and f^(ell)(y)=(-1/N)^ell exp(-y/N). The basis value at x is product_{r<=ell}(A_r-x)>0. The N125 source further gives the exact Beta-kernel identity converting each such basis polynomial into a nonnegative beta-shaped measure.

The relative deficit in (1) is Pr[T_{m,k}<=1/N]. Write U_r as independent mean-one exponentials and couple E_r=U_r/lambda_{r,k}. Since lambda_{r,k}=(r+N/2)^2-(k-N/2)^2 <= (r+N/2)^2, this coupling gives T_{m,k}>=T_{m,c}, where the center comparison uses rates lambda_{r,c}=(r+N/2)^2 (a real comparison parameter when N is odd). Thus it suffices to bound the center probability.

Use Chernoff with s=N^2/4. For m=2N,
 Pr[T_{m,c}<=1/N]
 <= exp(N/4 - sum_{r=1}^{2N} log(1+N^2/(4(r+N/2)^2))).       (2)

Set h(u)=log(1+1/(4(u+1/2)^2)). It is positive and decreasing. The left Riemann-sum estimate used in N125 gives
 sum_{r>=1} h(r/N) >= N integral_0^infinity h(u)du - h(0).
Here h(0)=log 2 and, by the elementary antiderivative with theta=pi/4,
 integral_0^infinity h(u)du = pi/4 - (log 2)/2.
The omitted finite-sum tail obeys
 sum_{r>m} log(1+N^2/(4(r+N/2)^2))
 <= (N^2/4) sum_{r>m}(r+N/2)^(-2)
 <= N^2/[4(m+N/2)],
using log(1+z)<=z and the integral comparison for the decreasing reciprocal-square tail. Therefore for every m>=1,

 Pr[T_{m,k}<=1/N] <= 2 exp(-cN + N^2/[4(m+N/2)]),
 c=pi/4-(log 2)/2-1/4.                                      (3)

For m=2N, the tail term is N/10. Also c>1/8: pi>3 implies pi/4>3/4, and log 2<3/4 follows from e^(3/4)>1+3/4+(3/4)^2/2+(3/4)^3/6>2. Hence c-1/10>1/40. Substitution in (3) proves (ii). No asymptotic approximation or numerical probability calculation is used.

3. Effective coefficient precision and charged cost

The analytic polynomial has finite-bit approximations with polynomial cost in N and requested coefficient precision. For ell=0,...,2N-1 its positive Newton coefficient c_ell=(-1)^ell f[A_1,...,A_{ell+1}] has the Hermite-Genocchi integral and lower bound

 c_ell = N^(-ell) integral_{Delta_ell} exp(-sum_i t_i A_i/N) dt
       >= N^(-ell) exp(-A_{ell+1}/N)/ell!,                 (4)

where Delta_ell is the standard simplex of volume 1/ell! (the ell=0 case is interpreted directly). The Lagrange formula has total absolute term magnitude at most

 S_ell := sum_i exp(-A_i/N)/|product_{j!=i}(A_i-A_j)|
       <= 2^ell/((N+2)^ell ell!),

because |A_i-A_j|=|i-j|(N+i+j), the second factor is at least N+2, and sum_i 1/((i-1)!(ell+1-i)!)=2^ell/ell!. Thus S_ell/c_ell <= (2N/(N+2))^ell exp(A_{ell+1}/N) <= exp(8N) for ell<2N and N>=1 (the last constant is deliberately loose; A_{2N}=(5N/2)^2). Computing each exponential exp(-A_i/N) with absolute error <= eta exp(-8N), 0<eta<=1/2, makes the Lagrange sum error at most eta c_ell. Standard range-reduced rational exponential evaluation and rational arithmetic therefore give all 2N coefficients to relative error eta in time polynomial in N+log(1/eta), with polynomial bit lengths. Direct summation may need 8N guard bits; the positive integral avoids a sign/positivity ambiguity but is not itself an algorithm here.

If the approximated coefficients are kept positive and within relative eta, positivity of every Newton basis term implies the resulting evaluated polynomial differs from P_{2N-1}(x) by at most eta P_{2N-1}(x), simultaneously for all k. Combined relative error to exp(-x/N) is at most 2 exp(-N/40)+eta. Coefficient computation counts as charged acquisition/evaluation; no external oracle, unknown matrix, or field extension is involved. Evaluating all basis products at a supplied k uses O(N) multiplications on integers of O(N log N) bits; the beta-kernel normalizers have O(N log N) bits as well. Total dense measure output has polynomial bit size. The construction is not claimed to improve a downstream procedure unless that procedure accepts these coefficients and its other costs are separately bounded.

4. Scope, controls, and remaining gate

This sharpens the finite-degree question left by the N125 explosive-birth failure: although the positive infinite series misses the target by a strictly positive residual, a linear-degree finite truncation achieves exponentially small relative error at a=1/N. The residual itself remains real and prevents exactness. The result is a finite interpolation/approximation theorem, not a proof that all positive representations are limited by the residual. The equally informed baseline is the source's exact finite interpolation identity plus the source's all-m bound on the infinite defect; those do not alone provide this explicit finite-m exponent bound. A possible falsifier is an exact counterexample to (1), to the center stochastic domination, or to the finite Chernoff/tail inequalities; the algebraic/numerical checker is supplemental only.

Immediate bottleneck: establish that the target application needs only relative error at a=1/N (or another fixed delta/N with positive Chernoff rate), and that its normalization/observable map preserves this error without an exponentially bad condition number. Also independently inspect the original N125 package if this estimate is to be treated as new relative to that internal stream.
