SELECTIVE-READOUT PROOF

Let R be a rational expression in n freely noncommuting variables, and T an
n-tuple of d-by-d matrices where EVERY original inverse occurrence of R is
defined. Rational evaluation is simultaneously similarity-equivariant:

  R(P^{-1} T_1 P,...,P^{-1} T_n P)=P^{-1} R(T) P.

Proof is structural induction over scalars, variables, addition, multiplication,
and inversion. At an inverse occurrence A^{-1}, the transformed A is P^{-1}AP,
which is invertible iff A is. Thus equality of rational functions alone is not
being used to alter expression domains.

Write M=R(T). The scalar oracle reports the first diagonal entry. Permuting
coordinates obtains M_ii for every i. For i!=j let P=I+E_{j,i}; since E_{j,i}^2=0,
P^{-1}=I-E_{j,i} over EVERY field, including characteristic two. Then

  (P^{-1} M P)_ii=M_ii+M_ij.

Indeed row i of P^{-1} is row i of I, while column i of P is e_i+e_j. A permutation
moves this i-th diagonal to the fixed first readout. Subtracting the previously
acquired M_ii gives M_ij. d diagonal queries plus d(d-1) shear queries=d^2.
No dimension increase, no scalar origin, coefficient access or height bound.
Conjugating integer/rational inputs by these 0,+/-1 matrices takes only bounded
sums/differences of their entries, adding O(1) bits per elementary shear; integer
tuple generation remains polynomial with the full dense output charged.

For a promised INVERTIBLE M, keep only i=1: P_1=I and P_j=I+E_{j,1}, j=2,...,d.
The d readouts are M_11 and M_11+M_1j. If every readout were zero, row one of M
would be zero; contradiction. This gives the N51 conditional selective list.

Do not infer a simultaneous scalar-nonzero conjugate for all members of a B-
family from N51's common matrix-invertibility hit. Over a small finite field
different nonzero first-row linear forms can have no common nonzero readout.
Per-expression identity testing only needs its own successful conjugate.

OFF-DIAGONAL ORACLE AND THE CONSTANT GATE

For row a and column b define (d+2)-by-(d+2) block matrices

  X_i = [[0, a T_i, a T_i b],
         [0,   T_i,   T_i b],
         [0,     0,       0]].

For each positive word w, the designated top-right entry is a T_w b, by direct
block multiplication. Hence for any recognizable series regular at zero,
with constant c=R(0), this off-diagonal response is

  a (R(T)-c I) b.

All-zero diagonal blocks preserve legal evaluation for regular-at-zero linear
realization and jointly nilpotent T. It does NOT automatically preserve domains
of arbitrary expressions that are undefined at zero, e.g. [x,y]^{-1}.

An off-diagonal oracle CANNOT identify c: R and R+c give exactly the same outputs
at every dimension and every tuple. The L01 source's word 'proper' includes
uv!=0, so do not silently impose zero constant. Add one diagonal scalar value at
zero, supply c, restrict to zero-constant functions, or use the diagonal lemma.

Parent packing audit: for rational height-bounded response digits, choosing
a_i=K^{di+1}, b_j=K^j gives unique positive base-K exponents. The elementary
shear P=I+E_{last,first} on the two endpoint blocks turns the first DIAGONAL
response into c+a(R(T)-cI)b. Thus c has exponent zero and is not lost. The parent
owns the digit bounds/continued-fraction decoder/cost proof; this report does not
claim those bounds independently proved.

COMMUTATIVE TRANSFER PROOFS

Abelianization pi:F<X> -> F[X] sends a word to its commutative monomial, summing
all coefficients of words with the same Parikh vector. Every tuple of commuting
matrices respects this quotient. Thus xy-yx is indistinguishable from zero.
Its homogeneous coefficient matrices have ranks 1,2,1 at splits 0,1,2, hence
Hankel rank four. A four-state acyclic realization also gives the upper bound.

Suppose a deterministic scalar-only learner, given n,m, identifies pi(f) for
every at-most-m-state recognizable f using q queries. Run it on the zero oracle;
record its adaptively generated scalar tuples. If a nonzero commutative ABP of
size m vanishes at all those tuples, choose its nc path-order lift f. That f has
m recognizable states and pi(f) is the nonzero ABP polynomial. Its transcript
equals zero throughout, so the learner incorrectly returns zero. Therefore the
recorded tuples form a q-point commutative ABP hitting set. If query production
and bit lengths are polynomial, this yields an explicit polynomial-bit hitting
set. For rational functions, domain-safe oracle handling is an additional gate;
the polynomial subclass already proves the reduction without it.

No efficient syntax-respecting algebra homomorphic section of pi exists, even
without a complexity bound: a section sends x,y to x+terms of degree>=2 and
y+terms of degree>=2 (constant zero and linear parts are forced by pi). Their
commutator has nonzero degree-two part xy-yx, although section(xy-yx)=0. This
rules out an algebra homomorphic lift, not arbitrary nonlinear canonicalization.

SYMMETRIZATION RANK PROOF

Let S_d=(1/d!) sum_{sigma in S_d} x_sigma(1)...x_sigma(d), characteristic zero.
At a split k, rows are k-letter words and columns are (d-k)-letter words. A
nonzero coefficient requires no repetitions and complementary letter sets.
For each k-subset A of {1,...,d}, rows permuting A and columns permuting A^c
form one constant nonzero rectangular block; all other blocks vanish. There
are binom(d,k) disjoint rank-one blocks, so rank M_k=binom(d,k).

The full Hankel matrix has only blocks with row length k and column length d-k;
blocks for different k have disjoint row/column spaces. Its rank is therefore
sum_k binom(d,k)=2^d. Any m-state recognizable realization factors the Hankel
matrix as H_{u,v}=lambda B_u B_v gamma, so rank H<=m even for cyclic B_i.
Conversely, the subset automaton with 2^d states, transitions A -> A union {i}
when i not in A, initial state empty and accepting state full, recognizes the
permutation sum; scale its final output by 1/d!. Thus minimal m=2^d exactly.

Symmetrization is the unique lift invariant under permutation of positions with
pi(S_d)=x_1...x_d: every d! distinct order has the same coefficient, whose sum
must be one. The ordinary commutative monomial has a chain ABP with d+1 states.
Sorted word lifting avoids this particular example's exponential blowup, but
the task of obtaining a small canonical zero-preserving lift for general
black-box commutative ABPs has not been solved here.

POSITIVE COMMUTING ACQUISITION: SHORT PROOF AND LOAD-BEARING IMPORT

Let A_i commute, R(x)=(I-sum x_i A_i)^-1, f(x)=lambda R(x)gamma. The reachable
space W is cyclic over a commutative algebra; B(p(A)gamma,q(A)gamma)=lambda
p(A)q(A)gamma is well-defined and symmetric. Quotient its radical to obtain
the r<=m-dimensional reachable/observable space V with nondegenerate B.
Regular vectors R(u)gamma span V: a linear functional killing all of them
kills every Taylor coefficient (multinomial coefficients are nonzero in
characteristic zero), hence every reachable vector.

For regular endpoints u,v, x(t)=(1-t)u+t v, direct differentiation gives
  d/dt[t R(u)R(x(t))]=R(x(t))^2.
Define g(x)=d/ds[s f(sx)] at s=1=lambda R(x)^2 gamma and
h_i(x)=partial_i f(x)=lambda R(x)^2 A_i gamma. Consequently
  H(u,v)=lambda R(u)R(v)gamma=integral_0^1 g(x(t))dt,
  K_i(u,v)=lambda R(u)A_i R(v)gamma=integral_0^1 h_i(x(t))dt.
These are EXACT rational-antiderivative identities, even with interior poles;
they use no analytic path integration or numerical quadrature. Scalar
univariate interpolation acquires derivatives, then the integrands. Each
pairing costs at most12m^2+8m+1 scalar calls, including pole tests. For reduced
integrand p/q (degrees<=2m), a promised rational primitive F=U/q, deg U<=3m,
F(0)=0 is found by solving U'q-Uq'=pq, U(0)=0. Characteristic zero ensures
the primitive denominator divides q; endpoints are regular. No factorization.

For regular points spanning V, H has rank r. Choose nonsingular r-by-r block
H0=H[I,J], set alpha=H0^-1(f(u_i))_{i in I}, beta=(f(u_j))_{j in J},
B_i=H0^-1 K_i[I,J]. These are gamma, lambda and the commuting transitions
in the basis indexed by J. Exact rank provides minimality after the spanning
guarantee. A random m-point grid sample spans and is regular with failure
probability<=2m^2/|S|, giving O(nm^4) scalar calls (Monte Carlo).

Deterministic spanning import: for every nonzero row ell on V,
  T_ell(x)=sum_{k=0}^{m-1} ell A(x)^k gamma
is nonzero by reachability stabilization. For partial order d, all derivatives
are ell A^beta times one common polynomial vector, so their span is <=m;
summing the m orders gives PD dimension<=m^2. This is <=m^2, not generally
<=m: the ordinary resolvent factorial weights matter. Use the new hitter with
rho=m^2, B=n m^4+m^2,
  G_i(t)=sum_{k=1}^B (it)^k/k.
At (m-1)B+1 distinct t values some direction hits each T_ell. For a hit
direction v, ell R(sv)gamma is a nonzero univariate rational function with
numerator degree<=m-1 and denominator degree<=m. Any2m distinct scales include
a legal nonzero value. The legal vectors over this explicit scaled list span
V. Building H on all list points then K only on a basis yields the quoted
O(n^2m^14+nm^4) call bound. All pole flags are counted.
