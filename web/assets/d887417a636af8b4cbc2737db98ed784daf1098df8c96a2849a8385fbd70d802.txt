Contract
Let A=(S,Q,pi,h) be a finite irreducible stationary CTMC with reciprocal support, deterministic observation map h into a fixed alphabet, and stationary entropy-production rate sigma_A>0 (natural-log units). Observe one stationary path on [0,T], optionally including all visible jump times. An estimator L_T may be randomized using independent randomness. It is uniformly (1-alpha) valid when, for every member M of the class, P_M(L_T <= sigma_M) >= 1-alpha. The class permits adjoining at least one state with an observation label already present. No uniform lower bound on mixing rate, bridge rates, or stationary state masses is supplied.

Construction
Fix i0 in S, p in (0,1), epsilon>0. Adjoin one state b, put h(b)=h(i0), leave every internal A rate unchanged, and add only
  q(i0,b)=epsilon*(1-p)/(p*pi(i0)),
  q(b,i0)=epsilon.
The stationary law is rho(i)=p*pi(i) for i in S and rho(b)=1-p. The bridge is detailed-balanced because both directional stationary fluxes equal epsilon*(1-p). Interior stationary balances are inherited from pi Q=0. This chain is finite, irreducible, and reciprocal-support.

Use sigma(Q,rho)=sum_{i!=j} rho(i)q(i,j) log[rho(i)q(i,j)/(rho(j)q(j,i)] (equivalently the half-sum of currents times affinities). Internal affinities equal A affinities, internal fluxes are multiplied by p, and the bridge contributes zero. Hence sigma_{p,epsilon}=p*sigma_A EXACTLY.

Finite-horizon domination
Let E be any measurable data event, and allow independent estimator randomness as part of E. With r=epsilon*(1-p)/(p*pi(i0)), O_T=int_0^T 1{X_t=i0} dt, the probability of E together with starting in S and never crossing the bridge equals
  p E_A[1_E exp(-r O_T)].
This follows from the CTMC killed-path density; all internal rates and the conditional initial law pi are unchanged. Thus
  P_{p,epsilon}(E) >= p exp(-r*T) P_A(E).

Theorem: for every c in (0,1),
  P_A(L_T >= c*sigma_A) <= min(1,alpha/c).
Proof: choose any p<c. On E={L_T>=c*sigma_A}, L_T>sigma_{p,epsilon}=p*sigma_A, so validity gives alpha>=P_{p,epsilon}(E)>=p exp(-r*T)P_A(E). Send epsilon down to zero with p fixed, then p up to c. No statistical approximation or large-T assumption is used.

Independent replicates
For n independent stationary trajectories of arbitrary finite lengths T_1,...,T_n from the same chain, condition all initial states to lie in S and no trajectory to cross the bridge. Conditional density relative to A^{otimes n} is p^n exp(-r sum_k O_{T_k}). Therefore
  P_A^{otimes n}(L>=c*sigma_A) <= min(1,alpha/c^n).
The complete observation record of A is allowed: the new state can duplicate i0's observation symbol, and no-bridge paths produce exactly the base record.

Consequences / quantifiers
1. For a fixed alpha<1, pointwise consistency of uniformly honest lower bounds is impossible on this model class: choose c>alpha; the displayed bound is strictly below 1 at every T.
2. For simultaneous time-uniform coverage, apply the same proof to E_H={exists t<=H: L_t>=c*sigma_A}; if E_H is measurable on [0,H], its base probability is <=alpha/c. Let H increase to infinity. This also controls ever crossing the threshold at any finite time.
3. The obstruction is not an assertion that every valid L is identically zero. Small positive thresholds or rare positive outputs remain compatible with it. The word 'useful' must be operationalized.
4. Merely assuming ergodicity, arbitrary finite state count, or stationarity does not block the construction. Known stationary support/masses, an independently supplied quantitative mixing bound, or an experimental reset contract may block or change it.
5. The argument concerns the hidden stationary entropy-production rate. If the target is a different visible finite-horizon KL or a nonstationary path quantity, define it separately.
