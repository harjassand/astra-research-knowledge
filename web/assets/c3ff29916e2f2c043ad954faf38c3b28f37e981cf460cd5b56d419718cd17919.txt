STRONGEST RESULT / ROOT INTAKE

Internally proved counterexample to a broad dimension-uniform Fisher-spectral
archive principle. Two strictly positive, continuous, analytic likelihood families
have exactly the same tangent Fisher singular values sigma_i=a exp(-bi)/sqrt(3),
and their Fisher metrics remain uniformly comparable throughout the parameter
ball. Yet their optimal classical AND arbitrary unassisted quantum archive bits
are respectively

  Product sensor: B(epsilon)=(ln(1/epsilon))^2/(4b ln 2)+O_b(ln(1/epsilon));
  Shared scalar sensor: B(epsilon)=.5 log_2(1/epsilon)+O_b(1).

The ratio diverges. This is stronger than N66's discrete-Rademacher obstruction:
no atom, changing support, vanishing density, unbounded signal, or bad local
Fisher approximation is involved. The independent reference coordinates in the
quantum random-access converse are substantive; orthogonal scores do not replace
them. Matching bounds below are reconstructed independently for explicit models,
without importing the missing N30/N31 full proof packets.

This is a useful exact obstruction, not evidence of a field-breaking discovery.
The positive approximation and entropy ingredients are established machinery.
Exact priority for the paired counterexample remains unverified.

2. THE TWO EXPERIMENTS AND THEIR EXACT FISHER COMPARISON

Let mu_d be the product of d Unif[-1,1] laws. Both examples can be placed on the
SAME observation space [-1,1]^d, with the SAME reference mu_d.

P (product):
  dP_v/dmu_d(x)=product_{i=1}^d (1+s_i v_i x_i).

C (shared scalar):
  dC_v/dmu_d(x)=1+sum_{i=1}^d s_i v_i psi_i(x_1),
  psi_i(x)=c cos(pi i(x+1)).
Only the first coordinate carries information in C; the others are regenerated
exactly as ancillary uniform observations. The scalar t=(x_1+1)/2 lives on the
unit circle. All C densities are analytic periodic functions of t.

Positivity/regularity are uniform: ||s||_2<=1/4, so |sum s_i v_i psi_i|<=c/4<1/4.
Thus 3/4<C_v density<5/4. For P, sum_i |s_i v_i|<=1/4 and each |s_i v_i|<=1/4;
  exp(-1/3)<=dP_v/dmu_d<=exp(1/4).
Finite-dimensional likelihoods are analytic, supports do not depend on v, and
the global density bounds are independent of d. The infinite product converges
uniformly since sum |s_i v_i| is finite. In C every observation derivative order
has a uniform finite bound, since sum i^{2k}s_i^2<infinity. Analytic continuation
is uniform on every strip |Im t|<b/(2pi).

At v=0 the two score maps into L2(mu_d) are
  T_P v=sum_i s_i v_i x_i,
  T_C v=sum_i s_i v_i psi_i(x_1).
Under mu_d, x_i are centered independent variables of variance 1/3. Fourier
orthogonality gives integral psi_i psi_j dmu_1=delta_ij/3. Consequently
  T_P^*T_P=T_C^*T_C=diag(s_i^2/3).
The tangent singular values are EXACTLY identical, not just comparable.

The equivalence is not confined to a small Taylor neighborhood. For every v,
the product Fisher matrix has diagonal entries
  s_i^2 integral_{-1}^1 x^2/(1+s_i v_i x) dx/2,
and zero off-diagonal entries. C's Fisher quadratic form is
  integral (sum_i s_i w_i psi_i)^2/(1+sum_i s_i v_i psi_i) dmu_1.
Both are between (4/5)diag(s_i^2/3) and (4/3)diag(s_i^2/3) in PSD order.
Thus globally uniformly equivalent Fisher metrics do not determine archive bits.

3. A BOUNDED PROPER SCORE: THE DECISIVE WEAK-SIGNAL MECHANISM

This lemma supplies all lowers, including the quantum ones. Suppose the two
alternatives have densities 1+/-s U under a reference mu, |U|<=c_0, sc_0<=1.
Their equal mixture is EXACTLY mu and their posterior is p(U)=(1+sU)/2.
Define convex F through
  F'(p)=clip((2p-1)/(s c_0),[-1,1]).
Its derivative is odd about 1/2, hence F(0)=F(1). The Savage loss
  ell(t,p)=F(t)-F(p)-(t-p)F'(p), t in {0,1},
lies in [0,1]: convexity makes it nonnegative, each wrong-endpoint loss is 1,
and the losses are monotone from the correct to wrong endpoint. In the posterior
interval [1/2-sc_0/2,1/2+sc_0/2], F''=2/(sc_0), so
  D_F(p(U),p(V))=s(U-V)^2/(4c_0).

An epsilon-TV reconstruction under BOTH alternatives changes the risk of this
fixed bounded rule by at most epsilon. Blindness supplies the Markov relation
T-X-(memory,seed)-Y, so the risk increase is exactly the nonnegative posterior
Bregman regret. Therefore, under the common reference coupling,
  E_mu (U(X)-U(Y))^2<=4c_0 epsilon/s.                         (L1)
There is no accuracy premise at the null, and no accuracy premise at each seed.
The rule used to convert TV into risk is seed-independent, which is essential.

For a continuous feature U of differential entropy h(U), and V=U(Y), Shannon's
entropy-distortion inequality and Holevo/data processing give
  ln D>=I(U;V)>=h(U)-.5 ln(2pi e E(U-V)^2).                 (L2)
Shared seeds are handled by I(X;Y,S)=I(X;Y|S)<=ln D. A direct-sum memory is a
block diagonal D-dimensional memory. Standard finite partitions and increasing
refinement justify continuous variables; no differential entropy is assigned
to a quantum register.

  I(X_i;Y_i,S)>=.5 ln[s_i/(2pi e epsilon)].

Under the independent reference the SAME cq memory Q satisfies
  sum_{i in I} I(X_i;Y_i,S)
    <=sum_{i in I} I(X_i;Q|S)

6. WHY THE BROAD EXTENSION FAILS / WHAT SURVIVES

For the exact same sigma_i=s_i/sqrt(3), .5 S_sigma(epsilon) grows as log-squared.
It describes P and is false for C by an unbounded multiplicative factor. Every
C score is orthogonal in L2, and its Gram matrix is diagonal, yet all scores are
deterministic functions of ONE scalar. Their joint reference distribution lies
on a curve. The product lower's additivity sum I(X_i;Q)<=ln D has no analogue for
these mutually dependent feature variables; one label estimates all of them at
once. The scalar positive stencil simultaneously preserves all Fourier modes.

This refutes replacement of independence by score orthogonality, bounded density
ratios, analyticity, globally equivalent Fisher metrics, or compact singular
spectrum. In Gaussian shifts, whitening succeeds because orthogonal Gaussian
scores are actually independent; that extra distributional fact matters.

A plausible broad theorem must use posterior-feature support/conditional entropy
geometry in addition to Fisher spectral values, plus a separately verified
positive reconstruction mechanism. M's memory_general_principle already explains
regret-volume lower bounds and finite-dimensional smooth positive uppers. The
paired example sharpens its obstruction into exact identical spectra and an
arbitrarily large asymptotic gap; it is not a claim to have invented that principle.

7. SCOPED POSITIVE TRANSFER TO NONLINEAR INVERSE MODELS

There is an elementary useful image-sandwich lemma. Let A>=alpha I be a known
positive self-adjoint operator with compact inverse on H, eigenvalues lambda_i.
Let g:H->H be known monotone, g(0)=0, and L-Lipschitz. Assume every ||v||<=1 has
a unique supplied/defined solution u(v) in D(A) of Au+g(u)=v. Put C=1+L/alpha.
Then, purely by coercivity and the triangle inequality,
  C^-1 A^-1(B_H) subset {u(v):||v||<=1} subset C A^-1(B_H).
Indeed ||u||<=1/alpha and ||Au||<=C for the upper. For ||Au||<=1/C,
||Au+g(u)||<=||Au||+L||u||<=C||Au||<=1 for the lower. The image also contains
each +/-e_i/(lambda_i+L) by directly bounding the corresponding forcing norm.

Thus Gaussian white-noise observations of a nonlinear monotone elliptic inverse
map have the same spectral POWER law, up to fixed threshold constants, as the
linear reference whenever the dimension-uniform Gaussian archive theorem is
available at the desired precision. To apply the WEAK N31 upper directly require
C/alpha<=1 (or supply a noise scale making every noise-normalized axis <=1).
Outside that regime the finitely many strong axes need a separately verified
fixed-dimensional codec; N31 alone does not supply it. Exponential lambda_i preserves the sharp
log-squared leading coefficient; polynomial lambda_i~i^a gives Theta(epsilon^-1/a).
