FROZEN ROOT DERIVATION V5 — GENERAL HIDDEN MARKOV PATH SPACE
9 October 2026. Internal mathematical extension of v2/v4; no priority or empirical claim.

The midpoint bounds do not require a finite number of hidden states. Here is an exact formulation that avoids assuming a particular jump-generator formula for entropy production.

Let X be a two-sided stationary Markov process on a standard Borel state space, with regular conditional past and future path laws given X_0. Physical time reversal fixes each hidden state (all variables even). Let M_s^x be its conditional forward path law on [0,s], and M_s^{dagger,x} its conditional reversed-past law, both starting at x. Write pi for the stationary law. Assume the two integrated conditional relative entropies obey

 integral D(M_s^x || M_s^{dagger,x}) pi(dx) = s sigma,
 integral D(M_s^{dagger,x} || M_s^x) pi(dx) = s sigma.             (1)

For stationary time-homogeneous finite CTMCs with finite entropy production this follows from the usual path likelihood and stationarity. In other models (1) is an explicit hypothesis to be established from their path measure; it must not be asserted from endpoint transition divergences alone. Infinite sigma gives a vacuous bound. The same argument works with the sum of the two quantities in (1) instead of 2s sigma when a constant rate is not supplied.

An observation is any common measurable function of the hidden state, or a common stochastic observation kernel using conditionally independent noise on the two half paths. Choose finitely many observation times in (0,s], a finite-valued word statistic on each half, and reverse only the time labels of the past word. Call the resulting words U and V. Given X_0=x their laws p_x and q_x are independent. Hence their observed pair table is
 P = integral p_x q_x^T pi(dx).
Data processing in both directions gives
 integral J(p_x,q_x) pi(dx) <= 2s sigma,                      (2)
where J is Jeffreys divergence. This is an ordinary classical mixture; no finite latent rank is required.

Let T=2s. For any symmetric copositive matrix W, put M=max_ab |W_ab|>0 and delta=max(0,-<W,P>). For probability vectors p,q, copositivity gives
 p^T W q = ((p+q)^T W(p+q) - (p-q)^T W(p-q))/4
          >= -M TV(p,q)^2.
Therefore E TV(p_x,q_x)^2 >= delta/M. The exact elementary inequality
 J(p,q)>=4 TV(p,q) atanh(TV(p,q))
and convexity of g(z)=4 sqrt(z) atanh(sqrt(z)) imply

                  sigma T >= g(delta/M).                   (3)

For a real bounded statistic f of a half word, center it by its stationary mean and let R be its range. If c=-E[f(U) f(V)]>0, put a_x=E[f(U)|X_0=x] and b_x=E[f(V)|X_0=x]. Then
 E a_x b_x = (E(a_x+b_x)^2-E(a_x-b_x)^2)/4
             >= -R^2 E TV(p_x,q_x)^2/4.
Consequently

                  sigma T >= g(4c/R^2).                    (4)

At equilibrium M_s^x=M_s^{dagger,x}, so P=integral p_x p_x^T pi(dx) is completely positive. The finite-dimensional CP cone is closed, so integrals and limits preserve this property. Equations (3)-(4) therefore exclude every equilibrium hidden model in this class, including arbitrary finite, countable, and continuous hidden state spaces, whenever the corresponding witness is negative. The exact one-lag CP closure theorem in v3, proved by finite CTMC constructions, also describes the finite-observation pair closure of this larger equilibrium model class.

The assumption of all physically even variables is indispensable: a mathematically reversible visible process may admit an equilibrium realization with physically odd variables and a nontrivial reversal involution. Relabeling the sheet-swap symmetry of the clock examples as physical parity would change the model class. No experiment here determines physical parity. The theorem is therefore an inference conditional on the stated microscopic model class, not a universal empirical assertion that a reversible signal dissipates.

Indispensable mechanism: condition on the hidden midpoint, use conditional past/future independence, compare the two half-path laws by data processing, and turn a copositive or scalar reflection violation into a lower bound on their discrepancy. No guessed hidden cardinality, parameter fit, or observed arrow of time is needed.
