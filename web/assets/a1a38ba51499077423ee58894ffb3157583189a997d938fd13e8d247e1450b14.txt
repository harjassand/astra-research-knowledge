multipartite quantum states, arXiv:quant-ph/0302102,
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0302102,

3. Barry--Drummond (2008), Qubit phase-space: SU(n) coherent state
P-representations, https://arxiv.org/html/0808.2226.
Read SectionsV-VI, especially diagonal Eq58 and off-diagonal Eqs60-61.

stochastic group-generator and weighted representation machinery is prior

4. Mathé--Usui--Gühne--Vitagliano (2026), Estimating the best separable

https://arxiv.org/html/2504.07814v2,
https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-04-21-2078.
Read Sections2.3-2.4 and3.3-3.4. The thermal Hamiltonian is
 H=[g(Jx^2+Jy^2)+gz Jz^2]/N+hJz, rho=exp(-H/T)/Z.

The AFM examples have negative coupling, and the FM XX re-entrant example
has a zero coupling eigenvalue; neither lies in our positive isotropic ball.
Their general XXZ investigation is relevant context, and its numerical

explicit radius. Full historical overlap remains UNKNOWN. Their Section2.3
