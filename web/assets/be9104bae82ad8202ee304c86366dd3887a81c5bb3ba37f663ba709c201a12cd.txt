Let C<1/2,S=1-C,0<c<2, a0=1-c/2, and suppose a0>C/S.
In the notation of CORRELATED_VERTICAL_LEMMA.txt, put
 kappa=a0-C/S>0.
Then for every0<=t<=1 and0<=z<=ct,

 F(t,z)=log[Q(t,z)/C]+tz-z^2/(2c) >= kappa*t^2.               (1)

PROOF
The hypothesis implies c<2S, so the original lemma applies and F decreases in
z. Its minimum is G(t)=F(t,ct). For0<t<1 let
w=tanh(atanh t-ct/2) and r be the smaller root of r=t(C+Sr^2).
Since C<S, this smaller root increases to C/S at t1 and is at most C/S.
One can avoid using monotonicity: the quadratic at r=C/S is C(t-1)/S<=0,
so r<=C/S; then its equation gives r/t=C+Sr^2<=C/S.
Thus r<=(C/S)t. Lipschitz tanh gives w>=a0t, while w<=t. Consequently
 w-r>=kappa*t,
 P:=w-t(C+Sw^2)=(w-r)[1-St(w+r)]>=kappa*t*(1-t^2),
where St(w+r)<=St[t+(C/S)t]=t^2. Also C+Sw^2<=C+St^2.
Writing A=1/(1-t^2)-c/2, the exact factorized derivative is

 G'(t)=2A P/(C+Sw^2)
 >=2kappa*t [a0+(c/2)t^2]/[C+St^2]>=2kappa*t.

The last inequality holds because numerator minus denominator is
(a0-C)(1-t^2)>=0. Integrating from G0=0 gives G>=kappa t^2. Continuity
extends it to t0/t1, and F(t,z)>=G(t) proves(1).

NORMALIZED EDGE COROLLARY
For the axial saddle c=delta lambda,lambda=(N+2)/N, let
 r_N=a^2/(4delta)-lambda logsec(a/2).
The combined two-edge normalized absolute error is at most

 lambda sqrt(delta N/pi)*t exp[-N r_N-Nlambda kappa*t^2/2]
 <=sqrt[delta lambda/(pi e kappa)] exp[-N r_N].               (2)

This uses sup_{t>=0} t e^(-A t^2)=1/sqrt(2eA). It removes the spurious
sqrtN prefactor while retaining the zero edge length at t0. The bound is
valid even if the unconstrained maximum t exceeds1, because that only enlarges
the supremum. It retains the same t in length and amplitude.
