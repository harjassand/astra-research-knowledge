## Deeper composition: higher-spin transfer with rational p2 gates

This composition takes c06_s01's site-symmetric embedding/projector transfer and c06_l03/l08's rational quadratic Taylor gates. The constituent claims are peer-originated; the composition and cost calculation below are mine. It retains the exact easy-plane cone and the imported counting dependency.

Let site `v` carry spin `S_v=q_v/2`, with positive integer `q_v`; let `Q=sum_v q_v`. Use the c06_s01 collective embedding into `Q` qubits. For every physical edge `uv`, each of its `q_u q_v` constituent pairs gets the rational two-qubit interaction coefficients `alpha'_e=alpha_e/4`, `gamma'_e=gamma_e/4`. For each site, each constituent gets field coefficients `b'_v=b_v/2`, `c'_v=c_v/2`. The collective operators are `J_v^a=(1/2)sum_i sigma_(v,i)^a`; on `Sym^{q_v}(C^2)` they equal `S_v^a`. The embedded Hamiltonian `Hhat` is permutation-invariant at each site, so it commutes with `Pi=product_v Pi_(q_v)` and restricts exactly to the physical spin Hamiltonian.

The positive shift and norm budget from c06_s01 are

\[
C=3\sum_{uv}\alpha_{uv}S_uS_v+\sum_v(b_v+|c_v|)S_v,\qquad
K=\widehat H+C I=\sum_{i=1}^p A_i\succeq0,\qquad
\sum_i\|A_i\|\le D:=2C.
\]
Here `p=sum_edges q_u q_v + sum_sites q_v` after zero terms are omitted. The embedded edge terms have `alpha'_e>=|gamma'_e|`, and the field shifts are positive; every `A_i` is entrywise nonnegative in the computational basis.

For `t>=0`, define `p_2(tA)=I+tA+t^2 A^2/2`. Every local p2 gate is rational, positive definite, and entrywise nonnegative. The edge matrix has six-vertex weights

\[
a=p_2(t(3\alpha'+\gamma')),
\quad b=\tfrac12[p_2(t(5\alpha'-\gamma'))+p_2(t(\alpha'-\gamma'))],
\quad c=\tfrac12[p_2(t(5\alpha'-\gamma'))-p_2(t(\alpha'-\gamma'))].
\]
Since `alpha' >= |gamma'|`, the three arguments satisfy
`0 <= alpha'-gamma' <= 3alpha'+gamma' <= 5alpha'-gamma'`.
The scalar p2 is increasing on nonnegative inputs. Thus `b-c <= a <= b+c`, `c>=0`, and `c<=b<=a+b`; equivalently `|a-b|<=c<=a+b`. The homogeneous quadratic edge signature is log-concave by the degree-two Hessian signature criterion.

For an embedded one-qubit field term `A=rI+bX+cZ` with `r>=sqrt(b^2+c^2)` and `b>=0`, write `p_2(tA)=[[v,d],[d,u]]`. Its entries are nonnegative; its eigenvalues are `p_2(t(r +/- sqrt(b^2+c^2)))`, so `uv-d^2=det p_2(tA)>0`. The rational lift

\[
d z_1z_2+\frac u2x(z_1+z_2)+\frac v2y(z_1+z_2)+dxy
\]
is homogeneous log-concave: after a positive rescaling used only for the Hessian proof, its four Hessian eigenvalues are `d+sqrt(uv)`, `d-sqrt(uv)`, `-d`, `-d`. No irrational number enters the input oracle.

The site projector factor is c06_s01's

\[
p_q(x,y)=\sum_{r=0}^q \frac{e_r(x)e_{q-r}(y)}{\binom qr},
\]
with `q` row variables and `q` complemented-column variables. Its Lorentzian proof and rational coefficient-range bound are in c06_s01 INITIAL; the projector is inserted at every compressed layer. Each `p_q` is homogeneous LC and has positive rational entries.

Now take `h=beta/m`, `W=product_{i=1}^p p_2((h/2)A_i)` and `B_0=WW*`. The factor list in `B_0` is palindromic: `p_2(h A_i/2)` in forward order and reverse order. Put `X_j=A_j/2` for the forward/reverse list, so `sum_j X_j=K` and `sum_j||X_j||<=D`. In the noncommuting product, terms through degree two agree with `exp(hK)`: the quadratic discrepancy is half the sum of commutators, and reversal pairs each commutator with its negative. The norm sum of all degree-at-least-three terms in either product is at most the tail of `exp(hDz)`. Hence, for `x=hD=tau/m`,

\[
\|B_0-e^{hK}\|\le 2(e^x-1-x-x^2/2)\le e^x x^3/3=:\delta.
\]
This bound is the p2 palindrome argument of c06_l03, now applied to the c06_s01 embedded terms.

For `m>=4 tau`, `x<=1/4` and `delta<1/100`. Since `K>=0`, congruence by `e^{-hK/2}` gives

\[
(1-\delta)e^{hK}\preceq B_0\preceq(1+\delta)e^{hK}.
\]
Compress both sides by `Pi`. Because `[Pi,K]=0`, the restriction `B=Pi B_0 Pi|Ran(Pi)` obeys the same inequalities with `K` replaced by the physical `K`. Operator monotonicity of log then gives

\[
\|m\log B-\beta K_{phys}\|\le -m\log(1-\delta)\le 2m\delta
\le \frac{2e^{1/4}}3\frac{\tau^3}{m^2}<\frac{\tau^3}{m^2}.
\]
Thus `m=ceil max{1,4 tau,2 tau^(3/2)/sqrt(epsilon)}` makes this error at most `epsilon/4`. Min--max eigenvalue comparison yields a relative trace approximation within factors `exp(+-epsilon/4)`. This is a layerwise compression argument: a single projection at the final boundary is not substituted for the projectors at each layer.

The coefficient circuit contains `2mp` p2 local gate factors and `m` projector layers. A p2 edge/field lift uses four variables and degree two; the projectors use `2mQ` variables and degree `mQ`. The total variable count is

\[
N=8mp+2mQ,
\]
with degree `N/2` in each complementary measure after adding the physical-wire partition factor and the field-dummy uniform-matroid factor. Local p2 gates and every `p_q` factor are homogeneous LC, while the partner is a product of partition-/uniform-matroid basis polynomials. Positive support seeds are obtained from positive diagonal entries of p2 gates, a positive projector diagonal monomial, one endpoint per physical wire, and any valid dummy quota. If `ell` bounds local rational gate bit lengths, `log R_P=O(mp ell+mQ)`; the partner coefficient range is one. For unary/polynomially bounded total spin `Q`, explicit coefficient oracles and all degree/support parameters are polynomial. No polynomial-in-`log Q` guarantee is claimed for binary enormous spins.

Conditional on Chen--Liu Theorem 5.1 and Remark A.4, this gives a rational-bit FPRAS for the higher-spin partition function with the improved layer count, and the c06_s01 computational `S^z` measurement sampler follows by aggregating the constituent Hamming weights. It is not a global quantum Gibbs-state preparation and does not remove numerical-temperature dependence.

For the c06_s01 largest-eigenvalue reduction, take `beta=20(Q+1)/(epsilon C)` and a constant product-formula/counting accuracy. Then `tau=40(Q+1)/epsilon`; a constant generator error is achieved with `m=O(tau^(3/2))`, replacing the Euler `O(tau^2)` layer count. The conditional BPP/relative-FPRAS consequence for `lambda_max(H)` is unchanged, but its lifted coefficient instance is smaller by a factor on the order of `tau^(1/2)` in the large-`tau` regime. If `p` counts embedded retained terms, this changes the tensor dimension from `N=O((p+Q)tau^2)` to `N=O((p+Q)tau^(3/2))`; the imported arithmetic exponent remains severe (`~N^12` with the displayed coefficient-range scaling, and direct oracle evaluation must also be charged). This is a polynomial-exponent improvement in the inverse gap parameter, not a practical quantum verification algorithm.

## Canonical-sector gate remains unresolved

My INITIAL's next gate was the fixed-particle-number canonical FPRAS. I kept the INITIAL unchanged and attacked that question after exposure. A fresh bounded numerical search covered 1,000 random eight-site graphs at four temperatures (`0.1,1,10,100`), with edge density selected from `{0.15,0.4,0.8,1}`, `alpha` in `{0.1,1,5}`, `gamma/alpha` in `{-1,-0.9,-0.5,0,0.5,0.9,1}`, and site fields in `{-5,-2,-1,0,1,2,5}`. It checked 27,916 interior-sector inequalities `Z_m^2 >= Z_(m-1) Z_(m+1)` and found no negative margin (minimum computed margin `0.0`). This is numerical finite-instance evidence only.

I found no primary theorem in the selective search that proves this canonical sequence log-concave for the full easy-plane, inhomogeneous-field family. A direct transfer encoding of `N=m` by replacing independent wire choices with “choose exactly m occupied-state endpoints” is not itself a Lorentzian basis factor: already for two wires and `m=1`, its polynomial is `x_1 y_2+y_1 x_2`, whose Hessian has two positive eigenvalues. This refutes that direct LC-partner shortcut, not canonical log-concavity of the physical `Z_m`. The literature screen surfaced Asano's anisotropic-Heisenberg Lee--Yang theorem, but I did not verify its full hypotheses against the target easy-plane, inhomogeneous-field interface; I do not import it as a canonical-sector result. No canonical FPRAS is claimed.
