# Directed finite-volume safety with guarded mode switching

## Status

This is an internally derived conditional theorem and exact rational fixture. It combines the N35 chemical-jump exponential potential with a directed-flux face estimate and restarts at guard-checked switches. It is not externally proof-reviewed; novelty is UNKNOWN. Multiple stochastic barrier certificates and compositional hybrid safety are established prior art. No physical plant or PDE claim is made.

## Model and directed margins

There are finitely many sites i, species s, and modes j. Site i has volume α_i V, with concentration x_{i,s}=Z_{i,s}/(α_i V). The process is a single-mark CTMC: distinct jump marks do not occur simultaneously (as for finitely many bounded-intensity counting processes with no common-shock events). If the physical model has common shocks or batch events, the aggregate jump size and activity must be substituted in J, B, and Λ. In mode j, local reaction r has complex y_{jir}, integer jump ν_{jir}, predictable coefficient 0≤k_{jir}(t)≤K_{jir}, and intensity

\[
α_i V k_{jir}(t) φ_{y_{jir}}^{α_iV}(x_i),\qquad
φ_y^W(x)=W^{-|y|}\prod_s(Wx_s)_{y_s}.
\]

A directed transfer i→k of species s moves one molecule from i to k at intensity V c_{j,ik,s} x_{i,s}. Thus Δx_{i,s}=−1/(Vα_i) and Δx_{k,s}=1/(Vα_k), and the migration drift at site i is m_{j,i,s}=α_i^{-1}(Σ_k c_{j,ki,s}x_{k,s}−C^out_{j,i,s}x_{i,s}). The coefficient c is a finite-volume conductance; the model does not use the alternative convention Vα_i c x_i. Directed coefficients need not be reciprocal.

Each mode has a compact product box Q_j=∏_{i,s}[a_{j,i,s},b_{j,i,s}] inside a prescribed safe set S. Supply collar widths ℓ_{j,i,s}± and reaction margins γ_{j,i,s}±>0, uniformly over x∈Q_j and the allowed local reaction-rate choices. The local deterministic reaction drift is f^rxn_{j,i,s}(x_i)=Σ_r k_{jir}x_i^{y_{jir}}ν_{jir,s}; it is at least γ− on a lower collar and at most −γ+ on an upper collar.

Write C^out_{j,i,s}=Σ_k c_{j,ik,s}. On the lower-face collar, the directed migration drift into site i is bounded below by

\[
M^-_{j,i,s}=
\frac{\sum_k c_{j,ki,s}a_{j,k,s}-(a_{j,i,s}+ℓ^-)C^out_{j,i,s}}{α_i}.
\]

On the upper-face collar, its inward component is at least

\[
M^+_{j,i,s}=
\frac{(b_{j,i,s}-ℓ^+)C^out_{j,i,s}-\sum_k c_{j,ki,s}b_{j,k,s}}{α_i}.
\]

These follow by taking each incoming source at its worst allowed endpoint and the local source concentration at its worst endpoint. Require the effective margins \barγ_q=γ_q+M_q to be strictly positive for every mode-face q. This reduces to the reciprocal-exchange loss C_iℓ/α_i when incoming/outgoing coefficients and endpoints match; directed flow also pays its imbalance.

For U_{j,i,s}=b_{j,i,s}, set

\[
E_y(U)=\sum_{t:y_t≥2}{y_t\choose2}U_t^{y_t-1}\prod_{u\ne t}U_u^{y_u},
\]
\[
D_q=\frac1{α_i}\sum_r K_{jir}|ν_{jir,s}|E_{y_{jir}}(U_{j,i}),\quad
J_q=\frac1{α_i}\max(\{|ν_{jir,s}|\}_r\cup\{1:\text{incident transfer exists}\}),
\]
\[
B_q=\frac1{α_i}\sum_rK_{jir}ν_{jir,s}^2U_{j,i}^{y_{jir}}
+\frac{\sum_k c_{j,ki,s}b_{j,k,s}+C^out_{j,i,s}b_{j,i,s}}{α_i^2},
\]
\[
Λ_j=\sum_iα_i\sum_rK_{jir}U_{j,i}^{y_{jir}}
+\sum_{i→k,s}c_{j,ik,s}b_{j,i,s}.
\]

Define V_0=max(1,max_q ceil(2D_q/\barγ_q)), μ_q=\barγ_q/2, θ_q=min(J_q^{-1},μ_q/(3B_q)), and c_*=min_q θ_qℓ_q>0 over all essential faces in all modes. Let M_j be the number of essential faces. A target guard core Q_j^{d_j} has every face gap at least d_{jq}>0. Put A_V=max_jΣ_q exp(−θ_qVd_{jq}) and C_*=2max_j M_jΛ_j.

## Guarded switching theorem

Start from a lattice-compatible point in the initial mode's guard core. The controller is piecewise constant in mode and has no state reset. It accepts j→k only when the current state lies in the source box Q_j and target guard Q_k^{d_k}. A post-jump state outside Q_j is a failure before any mode update. If a switch request is made at the same stopping time as a molecular jump, that jump is generated with the old predictable mode J_{t-}; the post-jump history may select the new mode, which governs only subsequent intensities. Let N_T count mode segments started by T before the first exit (including the initial segment), and assume E[N_T]<∞. Equivalently, an actual total segment count may be used as an upper bound. For V≥V_0, uniformly over predictable reaction controls obeying the envelopes,

\[
\Pr\{\text{an active }Q_j\text{ is exited by }T\}
\le A_V\,\mathbb E[N_T]+C_*VT e^{-c_*V}.
\]

If N_T≤N deterministically, replace E[N_T] by N. Measurement error δ_x is handled by shrinking every accepted target guard by δ_x per coordinate; a state-observation delay needs its own reachable-set margin.

### Proof

For each reaction, 0≤x^y−φ_y^{α_iV}(x)≤E_y(U)/(α_iV), including insufficient-reactant states. The finite-volume reaction drift error in coordinate i,s is at most D_q/V; transfers have no factorial correction. Therefore the finite-volume inward gap drift in collar q is at least \barγ_q−D_q/V≥\barγ_q/2=μ_q.

The face jump mark ξ=VΔg_q obeys |ξ|≤J_q, and V^{-1}Σ_jumps λξ²≤B_q. Total jump intensity in Q_j is at most VΛ_j. For Φ_q=exp(−θ_q Vg_q),

\[
\frac{\mathcal L_tΦ_q}{Φ_q}=\sum_{\rm jumps}λ(e^{-θ_qξ}-1).
\]

When g_q≤ℓ_q, use e^{-z}−1≤−z+z²e^{|z|}/2, θ_qJ_q≤1, and e<3 to obtain
\[
\mathcal L_tΦ_q/Φ_q
\le V\{-θ_qμ_q+(3/2)θ_q^2B_q\}\le0.
\]
Outside the collar, the positive generator contribution is at most 2VΛ_j exp(−θ_qVℓ_q). Thus for Φ_j=Σ_qΦ_q, \mathcal L_tΦ_j≤2M_jVΛ_j exp(−c_*V) before exit from Q_j.

At exit, Φ_j≥1; overshoot is at most J_q/V per face, so the stopped potential is bounded by 3M_j. At the start of a segment, the target guard gives Φ_j≤A_V. Stop each segment at its next accepted switch, first active-box exit, or T. Rates bounded on Q_j make the stopped process nonexplosive and Dynkin-integrable. Conditional Dynkin's formula bounds exit probability in that segment by its starting potential plus 2M_jVΛ_j exp(−c_*V) times its expected duration. Summing over safe-prefix segments, their durations total at most T and their start indicators total N_T. This proves the inequality. Finite expected segment count and guard compliance are essential.

## Exact three-site fixture

Use one species A, α=(1,2,3), and a one-way directed cycle 1→2→3→1 with coefficient c=1/1000 on each edge. At each site the two mode-specific reaction lists and deterministic drifts are:

| Mode | Reactions (rate coefficients) | Drift |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ∅→A: 741/4000; 2A→3A: 19/10; A→∅: 439/400; 3A→2A: 1 | −(x−3/10)(x−13/20)(x−19/20) |
| 2 | ∅→A: 7/500; 2A→3A: 6/5; A→∅: 27/80; 3A→2A: 1 | −(x−1/20)(x−7/20)(x−4/5) |

Take safe set S=[1/10,9/10]^3, Q_1=[1/5,3/5]^3, Q_2=[2/5,9/10]^3, guard G=[9/20,11/20]^3, and initial concentration (1/2,1/2,1/2). The initial counts are lattice-compatible for even V. The listed reaction coefficients are fixed singleton rate choices in each mode; the example does not certify arbitrary modulation over the full intervals [0,K]. A feedback-modulated rate set is admissible only after the reaction collar minima are verified uniformly over that entire set. Accept a requested mode switch only in Q_j∩G.

Exact rational Bernstein coefficients verify the full reaction collars of width ℓ=1/100. Reaction inward margins are:

| Mode | lower margin | upper margin |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3663/125000 | 21/4000 |
| 2 | 7/1000 | 5103/125000 |

The directed ring has one incoming and one outgoing edge of equal coefficient at each site, so it costs cℓ/α_i=1/(100000α_i) on either face. The exact finite-volume constants are V_0=1005, c_*=131/26174250, min_q θ_q=262/523485, and max_jΛ_j=7572/625. For at most two segments, V=20,000,000, and T=exp(c_*V/2)≈5.4461×10^21, the bound is approximately 5.34×10^−13. Constants are exact rationals; the displayed decimal uses ordinary floating point for exponentials, not directed-rounding interval arithmetic.

### Why no common polytope covers the guard

At uniform concentration x_1=x_2=x_3=x, the directed ring has zero migration drift. The diagonal is invariant under both deterministic vector fields, which there reduce to scalar f_1 and f_2. Any common convex invariant polytope inside S containing the diagonal guard intersects the diagonal in an interval [a,b]⊂[1/10,9/10] with a≤9/20 and b≥11/20. Invariance at the lower endpoint requires f_1(a),f_2(a)≥0. Since a≤9/20, mode 1 forces a≤3/10 while mode 2 forces a≥7/20, a contradiction. At the upper endpoint it requires f_1(b),f_2(b)≤0; with b≥11/20, mode 1 forces b≤13/20 while mode 2 forces b≥4/5. Thus the shared strict-inward-polytope premise used by N35 cannot cover this switching guard, while each local box passes.

This only rules out a common certificate for this fixture. Multiple-barrier and guarded-switching theory may subsume the restart mechanism; priority remains UNKNOWN.

## Acquisition, runtime, and costs

Inputs are the directed edge list in the stated units, site volumes, reaction complexes and rate envelopes, candidate safe boxes and collars, guard, lattice-compatible initial counts, measurement error, switch cap, and horizon. The theorem does not infer rates from finite observations. If transport parameters are acquired as intervals, use outgoing upper and incoming lower endpoints in the face margins; this interval form is algebraic but has not been experimentally validated.

For this three-site degree-three fixture, offline rational checking is O(|I|(|R|+|E|)) arithmetic operations. Online switch acceptance takes six interval comparisons and O(|I|) state reads. Generic bit complexity depends on coefficient encoding lengths. At sample frequency f_s, the supervisor uses H=ceil(f_sT) three-site measurements; resolution δ_x<1/100 leaves a nonempty shrunken guard. A count sensor stores O(log(VU)) bits per site per sample. With N−1 accepted switches, physical overhead is (N−1)E_sw+HE_s for measured per-switch and per-sample costs. Reaction and hopping event/thinning work is bounded by VΛ_maxT; at the displayed horizon this is ≤1.32×10^30 candidate events. It is a mathematical certificate, not a practical simulation plan. Turnover energy, sensor latency, rate calibration, controller electronics, and physical validation are unmeasured charged inputs.

## Falsifiers

The claim fails if the directed coefficient uses a different volume convention, a site volume is wrong, a collar margin is understated, any effective margin is nonpositive, a switch is outside the source box or target guard, the propensity or jump model differs, or switch count is unbounded without finite expectation. The bound gives no infinite-time containment, permanence, mesh-refinement limit, general multispecies synthesis routine, physical validation, or external proof review.
