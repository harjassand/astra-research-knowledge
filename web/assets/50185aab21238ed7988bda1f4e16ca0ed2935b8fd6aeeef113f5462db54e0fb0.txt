SU(3) WALL BRANCHING AUDIT: EXACT TRUNCATED ZERO-WEIGHT STRING,
STABLE ORDINARY-COUNT REFUTATION, AND THE UNRESOLVED WEIGHTED BLOCK GATE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: exact branching identities and an ordinary-count counterexample
are proved below. The target weighted count is neither proved nor refuted.

1. SETUP AND EXACT END MULTIPLICITY INPUT

Take integers a>=b>=1, N=a+b, and the SU(3) carrier V_(a,b). Use
Hermitian generators Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2, so

  C2(r,s)=(r^2+rs+s^2+3r+3s)/3,
  dim V_(r,s)=(r+1)(s+1)(r+s+2)/2.

Write q=(r+2s)/3 and r1=(2r+s)/3. The output label (r,s) has
triality zero exactly when r congruent s (mod 3), equivalently q is an
integer. In that case the exact GL(3) LR formula for its multiplicity in
End(V_(a,b)) is the one from the carrier partition (N,b,0) times its dual
(N,a,0):

  m_(r,s)=max(0,U-L+1),
  L=max(0,q-b,s-q),
  U=min(r1,a+s-q,s,a,q),                                  (1)

provided q<=N, r1<=N and a+s-q>=0; otherwise m=0. Labels of nonzero
Casimir at cutoff T satisfy r+s<=2 sqrt(T), since
C2(r,s)>=(r+s)^2/4.

2. EXACT U(2)/GELFAND-TSETLIN SPIN STRING

Lift V_(r,s) to the GL(3) highest partition (r+s,s,0). A zero SU(3)
weight is the GL(3) weight (q,q,q), requiring q=(r+2s)/3 to be an integer.
In a GT pattern

       (r+s, s, 0)
          (u, v)
             q

that weight condition is u+v=2q. Put j=u-q=q-v. The interlacing
conditions are equivalent to

  j_min=|r-s|/3 <= j <= j_max=min(q,r1)
                         =(2 min(r,s)+max(r,s))/3,             (2)

with integer steps of one. Thus dim V_(r,s)[0]=j_max-j_min+1
=min(r,s)+1 on the root lattice and is zero off it.

This also gives a direct bridge from the LR tableaux in (1) to the spin
index. Let u_LR be the number of 2s in the second row of the LR tableau.
The row choices are exactly the integers L<=u_LR<=U in (1), and

  j=q-u_LR.                                                     (3)

In the range r+s<=a, the carrier-dependent constraints in (1) are
inactive: q<=a, r1<=a, a+s-q>=0. Hence U=j_max. The remaining lower
endpoint is

  L=max(0,q-b,s-q).

By (3), its upper spin is q-L=min(j_max,b), while its lower spin is
q-U=j_min. Consequently the exact multiplicity identity is

  m_(r,s)(a,b)
   = 1_{r congruent s (mod 3)}
       [ min(j_max,b)-j_min+1 ]_+,   whenever r+s<=a.          (4)

Here [x]_+=max(x,0). This is an exact counting/bijection statement:
inside this output region, the LR multiplicity labels are in one-to-one
correspondence with the GT zero-weight spin labels

  j=j_min,j_min+1,...,min(j_max,b).                              (5)

It does NOT assert that these tableau-labelled vectors are eigenvectors
of the weighted operator below.

The stable subrange r+s<=b is a special case: j_max<=b, so the full
zero-weight string survives and m=min(r,s)+1. This identity can also be
read directly from (1): q<=b, so L=max(0,s-q), and U=j_max.

For any fixed c0 and all sufficiently large N, every output with
C2(r,s)<=T<=c0 N lies in r+s<=a: indeed r+s<=2 sqrt(c0 N), while
 a>=N/2. Thus (4) describes every SU(3) block relevant to the proposed
low-window weighted count, up to the finite small-N range. In particular,
when the output radius exceeds b, the multiplicity string is truncated
at j=b and labels exist only when j_min<=b, i.e. |r-s|<=3b.

3. EXACT STABLE-RANGE COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE ORDINARY COUNT SHORTCUT

This counterexample concerns only the ordinary conjugation-Casimir
projector count D_T(T); it does not refute the weighted count. For b>=64,
put r0=floor(sqrt(b)/2) and I={ceil(r0/2),...,r0}. Then |I|>=r0/2.
At least |I|^2/3>=r0^2/12 ordered pairs (r,s) in I^2 satisfy
r congruent s (mod 3), by summing the squares of the three residue-class
sizes. For each such pair,

  r+s<=2r0<=sqrt(b)<=b,
  C2(r,s)<=r0^2+2r0<=b,
  m_(r,s)=min(r,s)+1>=r0/2,
  dim V_(r,s)>=r0^3/8.

The multiplicity identity is exact here by the stable case of (4). Thus

  D_T(b):=sum_{C2(r,s)<=b} m_(r,s) dim V_(r,s)
           >= r0^6/192 >= c b^3                               (6)

for an absolute c>0 and all b>=64 (for instance r0>=sqrt(b)/3 gives
c=1/(192*3^6)).

Now choose any fixed 0<alpha<1, b_N=floor(N^alpha), a_N=N-b_N. Then
for large N, a_N>=b_N and b_N->infinity with b_N/N->0. At T=b_N,

  T^2+((b_N+1)/N)T^3 = b_N^2+((b_N+1)/N)b_N^3=o(b_N^3),

whereas (6) gives D_T(T)>=c b_N^3. Therefore no carrier-independent
constant can make

  D_T(T)<=C[T^2+((b+1)/N)T^3]

hold throughout 0<T<=c0 N for any fixed c0>0. This exact sequence
certifies that dropping the positive L_S term cannot prove the weighted
profile. It does not show that the true weighted profile fails.

4. WHAT BRANCHING WOULD GIVE IF THE WEIGHTED FIBER SPECTRUM WERE KNOWN

On End(V_(a,b)), write

  L_w=L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)),
  End(V_(a,b))= direct_sum_(r,s) V_(r,s) tensor M_(r,s),
  L_S|_(V_(r,s) tensor M_(r,s))=I tensor K_(r,s),

where K_(r,s)>=0 is an m_(r,s) by m_(r,s) Hermitian matrix. If its
eigenvalues are kappa_(r,s,1)<=...<=kappa_(r,s,m), then the exact block
eigenvalues of L_w are

  C2(r,s)/N^2 + kappa_(r,s,l)/(N sqrt(C_S)).                    (7)

The target is the positive-eigenvalue count

  D_w(T/N^2) <= C[T^2+((b+1)/N)T^3], 0<T<=c0 N.               (8a)

For a>=b>=1, the exact formula in the supplied normalization is

  C_S = a(a+2)b(b+2)(N+1)(N+3)/(12 C2 N^2),

which implies sqrt(C_S) is comparable to b+1 with absolute constants.
Thus a mode counted by D_w(T/N^2) must satisfy C2(r,s)<=T and
kappa_(r,s,l)<=C (b+1)T/N.

Here is a precise sufficient local estimate that would imply the desired
global count. Let delta=|r-s|/3. Uniformly for all a>=b>=1, low labels
r+s<=a, and all R>=0, prove

  #{l : kappa_(r,s,l)<=R}
       <= C_0 [1+C_1 sqrt(R)-delta]_+.                           (8b)

The assertion is vacuous in a block with m=0. A sufficient, stronger
condition is the sorted-eigenvalue lower bound
kappa_(r,s,l) >= c_* (delta+l-1)^2, l=1,...,m_(r,s); it implies
(8b) with constants depending only on c_*. Combining (8b) with the
exact branch interval (5) gives the proposed count as follows. Set
K=sqrt(T) and J=C sqrt((b+1)T/N). For each fixed delta, the Casimir ball
contains O(K) triality labels with |r-s|/3=delta; each has dimension
O(K^3). The number of low-fiber modes in that block is at most
C_0(1+C_1 J-delta)_+. Summing delta=0,...,floor(C_1 J) gives

  D_w(T/N^2) <= C K^4 (1+J^2)
               <= C[T^2+((b+1)/N)T^3],                        (9)

for 3<=T<=c0 N; below the first nonzero Casimir the count is zero.
All constants in this implication would be absolute if (8) were proved.
This identifies the missing statement more sharply than an unweighted
projector bound: it must count low K_(r,s) eigenvalues with a penalty
starting at the branching floor delta, not merely show a bound on the
ordinary multiplicity m_(r,s).

The GT/LR derivation proves (4)-(5), but does not prove (8). The
multiplicity-space matrix K_(r,s) need not be diagonal in the basis
suggested by the integer j labels. A scalar count of labels cannot be
substituted for its spectrum.

5. OPERATOR TOOL AND PRIMARY-LITERATURE OVERLAP

An existing exact operator inequality on each L_T-isotypic block is

  L_S >= M^2/C2(r,s),
  M=sum_A ad(T_A)ad(S_A),                                    (10)

where M is a self-adjoint SU(3)-invariant mixed cubic operator. It follows
by applying the row-operator Cauchy-Schwarz inequality to the family
ad(T_A) after projecting ad(S_A) into the block; it does not assume that
L_S preserves individual multiplicity copies. Formula (10) would help
with (8) if one proved a uniform lower profile for the absolute
multiplicity-space eigenvalues of M. No such profile is established in
this memo.

Closest primary theorem located: N. Crampé, L. Poulain d'Andecy, and
L. Vinet, “The missing label of su_3 and its symmetry,” arXiv:2110.03521v1,
submitted 2021-10-07, later Commun. Math. Phys. 400 (2023), 179–213;
https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03521 and
https://arxiv.org/html/2110.03521v1. The paper treats a general tensor
product [m1,m2] tensor [m1',m2'] and output [m1'',m2'']; it identifies
the diagonal centralizer action on the LR multiplicity space, gives its
multiplicity as an LR interval/minimum of 18 parameters (§1.1), and
constructs two missing-label operators X_m,Y_m as explicit tridiagonal
matrices on that multiplicity space (§1.2–1.3). Its representation labels
m_i are positive integers shifted by one from Dynkin labels. This is
exact prior art for an explicit multiplicity-space realization and
tridiagonal missing-label operators. It is not a theorem about K_(r,s),
its eigenvalue count, the weighted Dirichlet operator L_w, or the
specific inequality (8b). No exact normalization identification of our M
with the source's X_m is asserted here.

A second primary source, M. Mathur, A. Rathor, and T. P. Sreeraj,
“Resolution of SU(3) Outer Multiplicity Problem and the SU(3)⊗SU(3)
Invariant Group SO(4,2),” arXiv:1906.10410v1, submitted
2019-06-25, https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10410, gives an invariant-operator
construction resolving outer multiplicities. The source supplies no
weighted spectral-profile theorem for L_S. These sources motivate
computing K_(r,s) in a centralizer basis; they do not close the present
gate. This is a bounded source check, not a novelty or priority claim.

6. STATUS

Proved here: exact GT zero-weight spin range (2); exact LR-to-spin
cardinality map and truncated multiplicity formula (3)-(5) throughout
r+s<=a; the stable ordinary-count lower bound (6) and its near-wall
counterexample sequence; and the conditional implication (8b)->(9).

Not proved: any uniform lower bound on K_(r,s) that implies (8b), a
simultaneous eigenbasis for K_(r,s) and the GT spin labels, or a
counterexample to the desired weighted D_w count. The adjoint block in
the companion `../subelliptic/adjoint_block_and_count_obstruction.txt`
shows the predicted first vertical scale in one multiplicity-two block,
but cannot settle the growing blocks required for (8b).
