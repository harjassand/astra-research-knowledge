STABLE SU(3) WALL BLOCK: CPV TRIDIAGONAL SPECIALIZATION,
GT-INDEX MAP, AND A FAILED POSITIVE-FLOOR COMPARISON
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: exact stable-range CPV parameters and the full L_S block matrix
are derived below. The requested uniform +(1)^2 lower for the L_S block
is refuted by the stable adjoint. The sharper j^2 lower needed for the
weighted count remains open.

1. SCOPE AND NORMALIZATION

Let a>=b>=1, N=a+b, and use Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2. On the carrier
V_(a,b), put

  C2=(a^2+ab+b^2+3a+3b)/3,
  C3=(a-b)(2a+b+3)(a+2b+3)/18,
  alpha=C3/C2,
  C_S=Delta/N^2,
  L_S(X)=sum_A [S_A,[S_A,X]].

On End(V_(a,b))=V_(a,b) tensor V_(b,a), decompose under diagonal SU(3)
as direct_sum_(p,q) V_(p,q) tensor M_(p,q). The scalar operator L_S
acts on an isotypic block as I tensor K^S_(p,q), where K^S_(p,q) is
positive Hermitian in the Hilbert-space multiplicity metric. We specialize
to the stable labels

  p,q>=0, p<=q, q-p=3 delta, delta in Z_{>=0}, p+q<=b.          (1)

The stable GT spins are J=delta+s, s=0,...,p; thus
J=delta,...,R, where R=p+delta=(2p+q)/3. The stable multiplicity is
p+1.

Normalization warning. In MISSING_LABEL_MAPPING.txt §4, the CPV affine
matrix is not K^S_(p,q) itself. Define

  Khat_(p,q) := N^2 K^S_(p,q).

Then the exact source-normalized identity is

  Khat_(p,q)=-Y_CPV-2 alpha X_CPV+sigma_(p,q) I,             (2)

  sigma_(p,q)=-c^2/12+(C2/3+1/4+alpha^2)c-(4/3)alpha C3,
  c=C2(p,q).

Equivalently, the weighted block is

  L_w|_(p,q)=c/N^2 I + Khat_(p,q)/(N^3 sqrt(C_S)).            (3)

Thus an estimate on the affine bracket Khat must retain its factor N^2
when translated to K^S. The target vertical scale j^2/(N(b+1)) in (3)
requires K^S of order j^2, equivalently Khat of order N^2 j^2; a bound
Khat>=c(j+1)^2 alone is far too weak for that purpose.

2. CPV PARAMETER SUBSTITUTION AND INDEX MAP

Use the shifted CPV labels

  m1=a+1, m2=b+1, m1'=b+1, m2'=a+1,
  m1''=p+1, m2''=q+1.

Their parameters are

  ell=N+1-Q, n=N+1-R, Q=(p+2q)/3=p+2 delta,
  R=(2p+q)/3=p+delta,
  ell-n=-delta<=0.                                           (4)

The stable condition p+q<=b implies b-R>=0 and a>=R. In the CPV
case ell<=n, the six parameters (CPV §1.3) therefore reduce to

  (xi1,xi2,xi3)=(N+1-Q, b+1, b+1-delta),
  (xi4,xi5,xi6)=(b-Q, b-R, 0).                              (5)

The CPV endpoints are

  xi_b=max(xi4,xi5,xi6)=b-R,
  xi_a=min(xi1,xi2,xi3)=b+1-delta.

For the second identity, xi3<=xi2 and xi1-xi3=a-R>=0. Hence the CPV
basis {omega_m} has exactly the indices

  m=xi_b,...,xi_a-1 = b-R,...,b-delta,                       (6)

so its dimension is R-delta+1=p+1. Relabeling by

  J=b-m

sends (6) bijectively, in reverse order, to the GT spin list

  J=delta,...,R, equivalently J=delta+s, s=0,...,p.           (7)

This is an exact equality of index sets. The CPV vectors omega_m are
highest-weight vectors in the tensor product, while the GT vectors are
weight vectors in V_(p,q)[0]. The index map (7) does not by itself prove
the vectors coincide under a unitary identification of M_(p,q) with
V_(p,q)[0]. No such identification is used below.

The remaining scalar parameters in CPV §1.3 simplify to

  Lambda=N+Q+3,
  lambda_+=2(a+2b+3)/3,
  lambda_-=(-a+b-3Q-3)/3.                                    (8)

For any CPV matrix index k=1,...,p, the source's two X off-diagonal
coefficients and associated Y coefficients (CPV equation (18)) become

  A_(k,k+1)=(k-a-1+delta)(k-p-delta-1)(k-p-1),
  A_(k+1,k)=k(k+delta)(k+b-p-delta),                         (9)

  B_(k,k+1)=A_(k,k+1)(a+2b+3+3k+3delta)/3,
  B_(k+1,k)=A_(k+1,k)(3k-2a-b-3p-3delta-6)/3.              (10)

Here A are entries of X_CPV and B are entries of Y_CPV in the CPV
basis convention of §1.3. The formulas follow by substituting (5), (8)
into the primary tridiagonal expressions; k runs over adjacent pairs of
the p+1 dimensional basis.

3. FULL EXACT L_S BLOCK IN THIS STABLE CPV BASIS

For completeness, the diagonal entries are also explicit. For each
matrix index k=1,...,p+1, set

  x_i(k)=xi_i-k-xi_b+1/2,
  E_(k,r)=sum_(i=1)^6 x_i(k)^r,                              (11)

using (5), xi_b=b-R, and Lambda=N+Q+3. The CPV diagonal entries are

  X_(k,k)=-1/108[(7/2)E_(k,1)^3-18E_(k,1)E_(k,2)+18E_(k,3)]
          -1/24 E_(k,1)(Lambda^2+2),                         (12)

  Y_(k,k)=1/288[(5/2)E_(k,1)^4+32E_(k,1)E_(k,3)
      +6(E_(k,2)^2-3E_(k,1)^2E_(k,2)-4E_(k,4))
      +6E_(k,2)(Lambda^2+2)-3E_(k,1)^2(Lambda^2-2)
      -(3/2)Lambda^4+6Lambda^2-36].                           (13)

These are CPV §1.3 equations (18), with their stated E-variables. Let

  H_(k,k)=-Y_(k,k)-2 alpha X_(k,k)+sigma_(p,q),
  H_(k,k+1)=-A_(k,k+1)[(a+2b+3+3k+3delta)/3+2alpha],
  H_(k+1,k)=-A_(k+1,k)[(3k-2a-b-3p-3delta-6)/3+2alpha].      (14)

All other entries of H vanish. Then the full block is exactly

  Khat_(p,q)=H,             K^S_(p,q)=H/N^2.                  (15)

Equations (5), (8), (11)-(15) are a closed finite formula for every
stable block (1), without omitting the quartic CPV term Y. The matrix is
written in the CPV basis, which is not normalized to be orthonormal; its
spectrum is nevertheless the spectrum of the self-adjoint block in its
physical Gram metric.

There is an immediate non-diagonality result in this CPV basis. For
1<=k<=p, stability gives R=p+delta<=b<=a and hence

  k-a-1+delta<=R-a-1<0,
  k-p-delta-1<0,
  k-p-1<0.

Thus A_(k,k+1)<0. Also

  (a+2b+3+3k+3delta)/3+2alpha>0,

because a>=b implies alpha>=0. Therefore H_(k,k+1)>0 for every
adjacent pair. For p>=1, the exact full L_S block is not diagonal in the
CPV basis; merely relabeling that basis by the GT index J=b-m does not
make it diagonal. This does not prove non-diagonality in the actual
orthonormal GT branching basis, since (7) is only an index bijection.

4. THE REQUESTED +(1)^2 LOWER FOR L_S IS FALSE

Take the stable output adjoint (p,q)=(1,1), available whenever b>=2.
Its zero-weight GT spins are J=0,1. In the orthonormal adjoint-copy basis
T_A/sqrt(d C2/8), S_A/sqrt(d C_S/8), the exact L_S multiplicity matrix
from adjoint_block_and_count_obstruction.txt is

  K^S_adj = [ 3 C_S/C2, -6(alpha/N)sqrt(C_S/C2) ;
              -6(alpha/N)sqrt(C_S/C2), kappa ].               (16)

It is positive, so its least eigenvalue is at most the Rayleigh quotient
of the first unit vector:

  lambda_min(K^S_adj)<=3 C_S/C2.                              (17)

Choose b=floor(sqrt(N)) and a=N-b, with N tending to infinity through
integers. Then b>=2 and a>=b eventually; (1) holds for the adjoint, and
b/N->0. The exact carrier formula gives

  C2/N^2->1/3,      C_S/[b(b+2)]->1/4,
  3 C_S/C2=(9/4+o(1))(b/N)^2 ->0.                            (18)

Thus no c>0 independent of a,b can satisfy

  K^S_(p,q) >= c (J+1)^2

on all stable blocks, whether interpreted as a diagonal-form comparison
or as assigning every eigenvalue to one of the spins J>=0: the right
side is at least c I, contradicted by (17)-(18). This is an exact
counterexample family, not a finite numerical indication.

If instead the symbol K_nu denotes the unscaled CPV affine bracket
Khat=N^2 K^S, (17)-(18) only give

  lambda_min(Khat_adj) <= (9/4+o(1)) b^2,

which does NOT refute Khat>=c(J+1)^2. But that latter estimate is too
weak for the weighted target: (3) shows that a fiber cost J^2/(NB)
requires Khat of size N^2 J^2, or equivalently K^S of size J^2. The
normalization in the claim must therefore be fixed before it is used.

5. WHAT REMAINS FOR THE WEIGHTED COUNT

The stable index interval (7) and the explicit tridiagonal block (15)
do not prove an eigenvalue-by-spin correspondence. The CPV tridiagonal
matrix is generally non-diagonal, and its basis Gram matrix cannot be
dropped when using Loewner or Rayleigh arguments. Even after removing
the false positive floor in Section 4, the natural remaining sufficient
stable-block estimate is a sublevel count, not a pointwise diagonal
identity. With delta=(q-p)/3, one would need uniformly

  #{eigenvalues kappa of K^S_(p,q) with kappa<=R}
       <= C [1+C' sqrt(R)-delta]_+,                            (19)

or a form/spectral theorem strong enough to imply it, for all stable
labels (1). For the full near-wall result, analogous control is also
needed for nonstable low-Casimir labels p+q>b. Equation (19) allows a
soft spin-zero channel and starts the vertical penalty at the true
branch floor delta; it is strictly weaker than K^S>=c(J+1)^2.
No proof of (19), no GT-basis diagonalization, and no weighted-count
counterexample are obtained here.
