P11 RATIONAL FANOUT AUDIT
8 October 2026 | Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; finite exact checks pass; external review and novelty remain UNKNOWN.

The Walsh fanout works exactly for R=4^k. Let W_R be the Sylvester matrix with W_R^T W_R=R I and first column 1_R. Then H_R=W_R/2^k has rational entries +/-2^-k, H_R^T H_R=I, and H_R e_0=1_R/sqrt(R). The active idler plus R-1 vacuum ancillas are transformed by this R-mode passive unitary; the retained signal bypasses it, for R+1 total modes. Fock-space second quantization preserves photon number and the bosonic commutation relations.

For one |R> idler input, the coefficient of |1,...,1> is sqrt(R!)/R^(R/2), so the exact all-one probability is R!/R^R. Uniform pure loss commutes with H_R. Thus with source pair number N=n, exact-one PNR heralding has probability

  q_n = 1_{n>=R} binom(n,R) eta^R(1-eta)^(n-R) R!/R^R.

At eta=1/R, q_R=R!/R^(2R), and no N<R sector can herald in the ideal experiment. The ideal total herald mass is

  p_id=(1-lambda^2)lambda^(2R)eta^R(R!/R^R)(1-lambda^2(1-eta))^(-(R+1)),

where lambda=2^-R^2 is the TMSV amplitude parameter, so Pr[N=n]=(1-lambda^2)lambda^(2n). Conditional on the herald, N-R is negative binomial with parameter x=lambda^2(1-eta). The retained signal count is Bin(N,eta); its conditional mean tends to 1, and its law tends to Poisson(1).

Interpret nu=R^-a as mean added photon number per mode, equivalently E|alpha|^2=nu for circular complex Gaussian displacements. Noise all R+1 modes after fanout and before loss; then vacuum becomes a product thermal state of mean t=eta nu=R^(-a-1). Exact-one PNR herald probability is p0=[t/(1+t)^2]^R. Since the TMSV-vacuum trace distance is lambda and

  log2(lambda/p0)=-R^2+(a+1)R log2 R+2R log2(1+t) -> -infinity

for fixed integer a>=1, the noisy herald has mass at least p0-lambda>0 eventually. Postselection continuity gives T(noisy conditional, thermal(t))<=lambda/(p0-lambda), hence the noisy conditional state tends to vacuum. A direct energy bound also holds: the idler herald effect is number diagonal by phase covariance, and signal noise/loss are independent, so

  E[n_S | noisy herald] = t+eta E[N 1_herald]/p_noisy
      <= t+eta lambda^2/((1-lambda^2)(p0-lambda)) -> 0.

Therefore the conditional trace-distance gap tends to 1-e^-1. Ideal rejection has log(1/p_id)=Theta(R^3); noisy-reference rejection has log(1/p0)=Theta(a R log R). No efficient successful-herald preparation, sampler, hardness result, or N81-specific implication is claimed. Unconditional output energy with noise is eta[2lambda^2/(1-lambda^2)+(R+1)nu]=O(R^-a).

Encoding boundary: the Walsh matrix, TMSV covariance, additive-noise covariance, and loss-channel covariance data are rational with polynomial description size in explicit mode count R. Lambda needs R^2+1 denominator bits; a dense Walsh matrix uses O(R^2 log R) bits. This is not a claim that all optical dilation amplitudes are rational: the usual loss beam splitter also contains sqrt(1-eta)=sqrt(R-1)/sqrt(R), and the normalized TMSV ket contains sqrt(1-lambda^2). The Gaussian channels remain physical and rationally parametrized. “One photon per port” is exact PNR; threshold all-click detection has different finite-R formulas. Uniform loss and noise placement are essential to the stated commuting argument.
