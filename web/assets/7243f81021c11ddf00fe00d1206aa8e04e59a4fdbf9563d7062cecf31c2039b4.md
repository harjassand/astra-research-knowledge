# Stronger comparator for the symplectic PPT family

This continuation preserves `INITIAL.txt`; it sharpens the concrete example
without changing its scope.

For even \(d\ge4\), take the symplectic channel from Prudhoe's source:
\[
 \Phi_0(X)=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(X)I+X+V X^\top V^*}{d+2},
 \qquad V^\top=-V,\quad V^*V=I.
\]
The source establishes that this is a PPT, non-EB channel. In the real
standard symplectic basis, set \(\Theta(X)=VX^\top V^\top\) and
\(\Delta(X)=\tau(X)I\). Then \(\Theta\) is a Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjoint
involution, fixes \(I\), and preserves the traceless subspace. On that
subspace
\[
 \Phi_0=\frac{I+\Theta}{d+2},
\]
so the eigenvalues are \(2/(d+2)\) on \(\Theta=+1\) and 0 on
\(\Theta=-1\). On scalars, \(\Phi_0=I\). This independently explains
the exact spectra checked for \(d=4,6,8\) in
`verification.txt`.

The completely depolarizing channel \(\Delta\) is EB and is the orthogonal
projection onto the scalar eigenspace. Thus \(I-\Delta\) is 0 on scalars
and 1 on traceless operators, whereas \(I-\Phi_0\) is 0 on scalars,
\(d/(d+2)\) on the \(+1\) eigenspace of \(\Theta\), and 1 on the
\(-1\) eigenspace. Therefore the exact comparison is
\[
 I-\Delta\preceq \frac{d+2}{d}(I-\Phi_0).                \tag{1}
\]
For \(d=4,6,8\), this gives constants \(3/2,4/3,5/4\), respectively;
the constant tends to 1 as even \(d\to\infty\). This is strictly stronger
than the earlier use of the imported fact that \(\Phi_0^2\) is EB followed
by the generic power lemma with constant 2. It does not need
\(\Phi_0^2\) to be EB.

Scope is unchanged: the non-EB and PPT status of \(\Phi_0\) are imported
from Prudhoe; the Dirichlet comparison is derived from its exact
Hilbert--Schmidt eigenspaces. This family has not been shown to be
selfcompatible, so (1) is a nontrivial channel example and does not settle
the universal broadcasting question. It also is not the O(d)-covariant
selfcompatible class from peer c04_s01; the symplectic real structure is a
different covariance promise.
