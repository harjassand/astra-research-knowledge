SHARP L-INFINITY OBSTRUCTION

Use the common family M_B and identity (1) in INITIAL. Fix a smooth bump b
with support in (-1/2,1/2), 0<=b<=1, max b=1; put B_j=int b^j. For
epsilon small, let b_epsilon be the period-one bump centered at 1/2,
m_epsilon=epsilon B_1, and
  delta_epsilon=c(b_epsilon-m_epsilon),
  u_epsilon(t)=a t+int_0^t delta_epsilon(s) ds,
where 0<c<a/2. The primitive is period one because delta has zero integral.
For small epsilon, u'_epsilon=a+delta_epsilon remains positive. The exact
entropy-power gap and strain norm satisfy
  Delta_epsilon/H^2
    = c^2(epsilon B_2-epsilon^2 B_1^2)/a^2 -> 0,
  ||s-H||_infinity >= c(1-epsilon B_1) -> c.
The same M, volume form, X, measure and return map are used throughout.
Therefore there is no function rho(z)->0 for which
  ||s-H||_infinity/H <= rho(Delta/H^2)
holds uniformly over the stated smooth-flow class. For every small epsilon,
the strain differs by order c only on a set of measure O(epsilon): this is
exactly why the L2 deficit can tend to zero. It is consistent with the
baseline variance term and with c08_s01's averaged Lyapunov estimates.

The exact force remains bounded as well. Direct covariant divergence gives
  div(Def X)=-2(u'_epsilon)^2 X,
and nabla_X X=0. Thus p=0 and
  F_epsilon=4 nu (u'_epsilon)^2 X
sustain X, with sup |F_epsilon|<=4 nu(a+c)^2. The code now symbolically
checks the divergence identity as well as the sectional curvatures. In the
transition layer, u''=c epsilon^-1 b'(scaled coordinate); from (3) of
INITIAL, the sectional curvature is unbounded. The pointwise force is bounded
because this stationary force identity depends on u' but not u''.

FIXED-VOLUME EXACT COLLAPSE DERIVATION

The construction from INITIAL is restated here because it is the strongest
new exact geometric boundary. For n>=2 set d=sqrt(n^2-1), lambda=n+d,
a_n=log(lambda), and
  A_n=[[n,1],[n^2-1,n]],
  P_n=(1/n)[[1,1],[d,-d]],
  D_n=diag(lambda,lambda^-1).
Exact matrix arithmetic gives det A_n=1, A_n=P_n D_n P_n^-1, and
|det P_n|=2d/n^2. With r_n=sqrt(|det P_n|), the eigen-coordinate lattice
  Lambda_n=r_n P_n^-1 Z^2
is D_n-invariant and has covolume one. At t=0 the flat fiber metric is
dx^2+dy^2. The vector from (0,1) in Z^2 produces a noncontractible torus
loop whose squared length is
  r_n^2 |P_n^-1(0,1)|^2=1/d.
Hence the fiber systole is at most d^-1/2 in length units, and the global
injectivity radius is at most (2 sqrt(d))^-1 -> 0.

On this volume-one mapping torus, take u(t)=a_n t and X_n=partial_t/a_n.
The cross-section return map is A_n; constant-roof entropy rate is a_n, so
the rescaled time-one entropy is H_n=1. The local strain eigenvalues are
(1,-1,0), and the Sol sectional curvatures have magnitudes a_n^2. Therefore
  Delta_n=0, V_n=1, H_n=1,
  inj(M_n)->0,  sup|Rm(g_n)|->infinity.
This is exact equality, not an approximate perturbation, on connected closed
manifolds. It falsifies any uniform positive injectivity-radius or curvature
bound inferred from deficit even at delta=0, unless the theorem fixes a
geometry class or explicitly quotients by arbitrary equality models.

The time-scaling import is Abramov's flow-entropy formula: for a continuous
measure-preserving flow, h(phi_t)=|t| h(phi_1). A directly inspected primary
proof is Shunji Ito, “An Elementary Proof of Abramov's Result on the Entropy
of a Flow,” Nagoya Math. J. 41 (1971), pp. 1-5,
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/1B64ECAD23D2576B5FFEF3EB86EF3E52/S0027763000014008a.pdf . The base-map entropy
is the standard Haar entropy of the hyperbolic toral automorphism (Adler and
Weiss, PNAS 57 (1967), https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.57.6.1573); it
is also reconstructed for the baseline cat suspension in
outputs/research_state/work/cycle1/pde_transport_proof.txt.

For a different global obstruction, the m-fold cover of one fixed Sol model
has the same entropy rate a and zero deficit, but the 1-Lipschitz projection
to its base circle of length m yields diam(M_m)>=m/2. Here the volume grows
with m; the conclusion is a failure of global diameter compactness, not
fixed-volume collapse.

BOUNDARY TABLE IN WORDS

The exact inequality and its L2 decomposition survive. The covers and
varying-monodromy sequence show that exact local equality does not select a
uniform compact geometric scale. The intermittent family shows that the
integrated deficit cannot control the essential supremum of strain. The
high-frequency family (duplicate after exposure) shows that small or even
vanishing strain error does not control curvature derivatives. No example
here refutes stability in a normalized weak metric, the projective-line L2
identity, the nonhyperbolic-volume estimate, or a theorem with fixed lower
H, bounded geometry and explicit derivatives. The input families also supply
their exact smooth metric and return map: none is an acquired finite-bit
observation or a device implementation.

The exact-collapse claim's cost is algebraic: for each n the matrix entries
have O(log n) bits; eigenvalues are quadratic surds; a_n uses a real
logarithm; the lattice and metric normalization are explicit. The symbolic
checker does not approximate a_n, simulate a flow, estimate entropy, or
construct a finite-element mesh. To use these examples as computational
conditioning lower bounds, one would need to specify an encoding, precision,
observation channel, and cost model. No such complexity theorem is asserted.
