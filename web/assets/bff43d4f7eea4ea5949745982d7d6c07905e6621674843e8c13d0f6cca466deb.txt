Objective and interface
Target the near-linear-time robust spectral initializer for real Gaussian phase retrieval under heavy-tailed noise and strong adaptive sample corruption. Clean samples are independent pairs (a_i,y_i), with a_i ~ N(0,I_d), y_i=(a_i^T x*)^2+ζ_i. The clean noise satisfies E[ζ_i|a_i]=0, E[ζ_i^2|a_i]=σ², and E[ζ_i^4|a_i]≤K4^4 (the 2026 source assumes the conditional second and fourth moments are constants independent of a_i). After seeing all clean pairs, a strong adversary may replace up to εm whole pairs (a_i,y_i) arbitrarily. The learner only receives the corrupted sample. Put q=||x*||>0 and r=K4/q²; the source assumes a known upper bound r_up≥r. Its robust gradient stage additionally uses independent fresh batches; every such batch is subject to the stated strong-corruption model.

The source's spectral sample representation is the vector
  Xτ = ((a^T x*)²+ζ) a · 1{ ||((a^T x*)²+ζ)a||₂ ≤ τ }.
The robust PCA routine is applied to the vectors Xτ, so its target is their raw second moment Qτ=E[Xτ Xτ^T]. For untruncated X=ya, conditional mean-zero and homoscedastic conditional variance give
  Q0=E[XX^T]=(3q⁴+σ²)I_d+12q² x*x*^T.
Hence λ_min(Q0)=3q⁴+σ², λ_max(Q0)=15q⁴+σ² and the eigengap is 12q⁴. The source's Question 4.8 instead prints Σ=q²I+2x*x*^T for this vector sample. That formula is E[y aa^T] and is not E[(ya)(ya)^T]; the paper itself gives the Q0 eigenvalues above in §4.1 and uses their 12q⁴ gap in §E. I treat the Q4.8 expression as an apparent covariance typo and formulate stability relative to Qτ (as Proposition F.2 does), not the printed Σ.

Known method and the actual gap
The paper obtains statistical error dist(xhat,x*)=O(σ√ε/q) using a scalar robust-mean norm estimate, norm-truncated rank-one robust PCA, then robust gradient descent. Its zero-mean theorem uses m₂=Ω(d(r_up²+1) log(2d/(δε))/ε) samples for the PCA stage and charges filtering runtime O(m₂²d); gradient descent uses P fresh batches of size Õ(d r_up²) and robust-mean subroutine costs. The AISTATS paper states that if the empirical distribution of clean truncated vectors is (ε,γ)-stable, its robust PCA initializer can instead use the near-linear robust PCA algorithm of Diakonikolas–Kane–Pensia–Pittas (ICML 2023), yielding runtime Õ(md) and an Õ(d) sample algorithm; it proves this stability only at Õ(d⁵) samples. Thus the concrete gap is the empirical stability sample threshold for this structured, truncated vector distribution, including an adversarial deletion chosen after seeing the entire dataset.

Stability definition (verbatim mathematical content, notation normalized)
For a distribution G on R^d and PSD Q, G is (ε,γ)-stable w.r.t. Q if every measurable w:R^d→[0,1] with E_G w≥1−ε has
  (1−γ)Q ⪯ E_G[w(X)XX^T]/E_G[w(X)] ⪯ (1+γ)Q.
The definition includes fractional, data-dependent weights; proving ordinary empirical covariance concentration alone is insufficient.

Candidate mechanism from direct derivation (internal, not yet fully theorem-checked)
Assume clean pairs (a_i,Y_i) are i.i.d., a_i~N(0,I_d), Y_i may depend on a_i, and X_i=Y_i a_i 1{||Y_i a_i||≤τ}. Let Q=E[X_iX_i^T] have λ_min(Q)≥λ>0 and ||Q||op≤Λ. For a scalar threshold L>0, define T_L=E[X_iX_i^T 1{|Y_i|>L}] and η=||T_L||op. Write Qhat=m^{-1}ΣX_iX_i^T. For an arbitrary adaptive fractional deletion w_i∈[0,1], let α=m^{-1}Σ(1−w_i)≤ε, D=m^{-1}Σ(1−w_i)X_iX_i^T. Then the retained covariance is exactly Q_w=(Qhat−D)/(1−α). It suffices to prove uniformly that
  ||Qhat−Q||op + ||D||op + εΛ ≤ (1−ε)γλ.
This implies ||Q_w−Q||op≤γλ, hence (1−γ)Q⪯Q_w⪯(1+γ)Q for every adaptive fractional deletion.

Split D into |Y_i|≤L and |Y_i|>L. On the former, pointwise X_iX_i^T≤L²a_i a_i^T. A Gaussian row-subset bound, uniform over every subset I selected after observing the full dataset, gives with probability ≥1−δ
  sup_{|I|≤εm} ||m^{-1}Σ_{i∈I}a_i a_i^T||op
    ≤ C[d/m+ε log(e/ε)+log(1/δ)/m].
It follows by a union bound over subsets using ||A_I||op≤√|I|+√d+t for a fixed |I|×d standard Gaussian matrix. Convexity/extreme points (or ordering each directional quadratic form) extends the same bound to all fractional deletion weights with total mass ≤εm. Thus the low-amplitude part of ||D||op is at most L² times the displayed bound; no independence between Y_i and a_i is needed.

For the tail part Z_i=X_iX_i^T1{|Y_i|>L}, 0⪯Z_i⪯τ²I and E[Z_i²]⪯τ²E[Z_i]=τ²T_L. Matrix Bernstein therefore bounds the empirical tail covariance simultaneously for all deletion sets by
  ||m^{-1}ΣZ_i||op ≤ η + C[sqrt(τ²η log(2d/δ)/m)+τ²log(2d/δ)/m].
Similarly, with probability ≥1−δ,
  ||Qhat−Q||op ≤ C[sqrt(τ²Λ log(2d/δ)/m)+τ²log(2d/δ)/m].
Combining these proves stability if their sum, L² C[d/m+εlog(e/ε)+log(1/δ)/m], and εΛ are at most (1−ε)γλ. This is an exact, explicit sufficient inequality in (m,d,ε,γ,δ,τ,L,λ,Λ,η); no outlier labels or robust-mean oracle enter the deletion proof. For fixed model ratios, choose L so η is small, require εlog(e/ε)≲γλ/L², and take m≳τ²log(d/δ)·max{Λ/(γ²λ²), η/(γ²λ²), 1/(γλ)} plus dL²/(γλ) and logarithmic terms. With τ²=Θ(d) times fixed signal/noise constants, this is m=O(d log(d/δ)) for fixed ε,γ and ratios. The contamination range may shrink with L and is not asserted equal to the original AISTATS theorem's range.

Phase retrieval tail moment check (candidate)
Let s=(a^Tx*)²≥0. On {|s+ζ|>L}, either s>L/2 or |ζ|>L/2; also (s+ζ)²≤2s²+2ζ². Consequently, by PSD ordering and conditional moments,
  T_L ⪯ 2E[s²aa^T 1{s>L/2}]
       + 32 K4⁴ L^{-4} E[s²aa^T]
       + 2σ² E[aa^T1{s>L/2}]
       + 8 K4⁴ L^{-2} I_d.
Here P(|ζ|>L/2|a)≤16K4⁴/L⁴ and E[ζ²1{|ζ|>L/2}|a]≤4K4⁴/L². The two terms restricted to s>L/2 are explicit Gaussian tail moments because a^Tx*/q~N(0,1); both their operator norms vanish as L/q²→∞. This derivation allows arbitrary conditional dependence of ζ on a. Since K4⁴≤r_up⁴q⁸ and σ²≤K4²≤r_up²q⁴, taking L=C(r_up²+1)q²/√η makes η≤C'η q⁴ for any fixed desired small η after increasing C. In implementation q is unknown: use the source's separate robust scalar mean estimate of E[Y]=q², condition on its certified interval, and choose L from r_up and that estimate. An independent batch permits conditioning; a same-batch scale choice needs a separate uniformity proof.

Strongest supported consequences if the candidate stability lemma closes
By AISTATS 2026 Proposition F.2, replacing its O(m₂²d) robust-PCA primitive with ICML 2023's stability-based PCA primitive changes the spectral sample/run cost while preserving the existing robust-gradient proof. For d-dimensional input vectors, the latter runs Õ(m d) when clean empirical G is stable and the adversarial fraction is below its stated small-constant/gap requirements. Our sufficient stability inequality can therefore yield a polynomial-time near-linear-in-input initializer with m=O(d log d) for fixed q,K4,σ,γ,ε. Total algorithm costs must still include: independent norm batch m₁=Ω((1+r_up²)log(1/δ)) and its robust mean call; spectral batch m₂ meeting the derived stability inequality and Õ(m₂d) PCA time; P=O((1−√ε)^{-1}log(1/(r_up√ε))) independent gradient batches each with source size Õ(d r_up²) and their robust-mean calls/runtime. Total sample is m₁+m₂+P·Õ(d r_up²), plus any constants required by batchwise contamination. Near-linear spectral initialization alone does not make total optimization runtime near-linear if the imported robust mean routine's cost is higher.

Unresolved proof and risk register
1. Verify exact high-probability Gaussian subset bound including noninteger εm and fractional weights; carry the failure probability and constants.
2. Recheck matrix Bernstein constants and tail second-moment relation for Z_i.
3. Establish the stated operator tail bound in full, with constants, and ensure L can be chosen under only the source's known ratio input and independent scalar estimate.
4. Audit the robust PCA theorem's exact stability orientation, sample complexity, contamination fraction (it may need a (20ε,γ)-stable inlier set), runtime, and δ dependence before composition.
5. Audit AISTATS Proposition F.2 because its displayed Σ differs from the correct Qτ; verify that the proof only needs Qτ stability and eigen-gap, as it appears to do.
6. State batch oracle and adversary precisely; if samples are partitioned from one global corrupted sample, control corruption fractions hypergeometrically or charge additional samples.
7. Convert the sufficient inequalities to a final exact tradeoff and compare strongest equally informed baseline under identical access, error and confidence targets.
8. The mathematical mechanism may be a straightforward application of robust covariance concentration rather than new scientific insight. No priority or novelty claim is made.
