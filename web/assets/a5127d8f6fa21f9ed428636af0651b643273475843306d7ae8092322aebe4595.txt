If extending the audit, read the full Section 9 induction proving Proposition
5.3 and Section 7 error estimates in the Annals paper alongside Theorem 9.1
of arXiv:math-ph/0508010 v3. The most concrete quantitative target is a

proof takes N to infinity for fixed k and its threshold is N0(k,C0).
