# Cancer spatial eco-evolution lead: phase response with directed migration

**Verdict:** one compact model result survives attack, but it is conditional theory with no biological validation and no cleared novelty claim. The robust conclusion is that the sign of a spatial phase intervention cannot be inferred from field overlap or the diffusion-only case once directed migration is possible. The model gives a closed-form, testable finite-horizon response and an explicit reversal. This is a candidate mechanism/measurement gate, not a treatment recommendation.

## Main result

Use the model and exact conditions in [the proof note](proofs/phase_response.md). In brief, tolerant-state density `R` is created at a spatially varying transition source `j0 ± j1 cos(kx)` and grows at mapped rate `r0+g cos(kx)`, while moving by diffusion `D` and directional drift `v`. The two source arms have identical one-field distributions; only phase differs. The directional-drift transport has a resolvable effect on the final total state burden:

`d/dg mean(R_aligned(T)-R_opposed(T)) at g=0 = j1 F(T)`,

`F(T)=∫_0^T exp(r0 τ) ∫_0^τ exp(-D k² u) cos(v k u) du dτ`.

The cosine factor is the migration phase lag. With `k=v=r0=1`, `D=0.1`, `T=3π/2`, `F=-18.8899185474`; independent Simpson quadrature agrees to the shown digits. A periodic Fourier/RK4 calculation at finite `g=0.01` gives aligned-minus-opposed mean `-0.1888980661`. Grid sizes 32, 64, 128 agree to <4e-14 at the displayed precision. The linear-response crossover at this fixed `k,v,r0,T` is `Dk²≈0.1703914966`; this crossover belongs to the conditional model and may shift at finite `g`.

Pursuing the result beyond the sign corollary gives a design calculation: with the same dimensionless parameters, numerical minimization of `F(k)` over `0.05≤k≤10` selects `k≈1.1002978` (period `≈5.710`) and `F≈−23.3080664`. At finite `g=0.01`, the computed phase contrast is `−0.2330838231`, agreeing across `N=64,128`. This wavelength is only an illustrative model optimum on the declared interval. In an assay, `D,v,r0,T` and the feasible pattern band must be measured first; mechanical amplitude and active-drug growth amplitude may themselves vary with wavelength. The optimizer is a test-design candidate, not a therapy instruction, certified global optimum, or biological finding.

An independent result is useful as a strict baseline: if the same reduced model has `v=0`, the aligned source yields strictly greater total tolerant-state density for every horizon `T>0`. The maximum-principle proof is in the proof note. With drift, the same parameter fixture has a temporal sign reversal: early aligned-minus-opposed is positive (`+0.08047` at `T=4`), near zero at `T≈4.3009`, then negative (`−0.18890` at `T=3π/2≈4.7124`). Grid-64 and grid-128 root checks agree to about `5e−9`. This makes assay timing part of the prediction: a single endpoint can hide or reverse the early effect. The result demonstrates a conditional finite-horizon model effect, not a cancer outcome.

## What is established, and what is not

- **Internally derived conditional model statement:** the Fourier linear-response formula; the diffusion-only strict ordering; the directed-drift reversal at the stated parameters for sufficiently small positive `g`.
- **Finite numerical computation:** one finite-amplitude PDE fixture with periodic spectral derivatives and RK4, converged across three grids. It checks the model calculation, not the theorem or a cell system.
- **Source-reported biological priors:** cancer cells can durotax; stiffness can alter drug response; tumor-like drug gradients plus cell movement can affect resistance; confinement/mechanics can induce spatial resistant phenotypes. These facts motivate the joint experiment but do not validate our `j,r,D,v` fields.
- **Novelty/priority:** `UNKNOWN`. The PDE algebra is elementary linear response/maximum-principle analysis. No priority claim is made for the formula, phase-swap idea, or biology. The narrow candidate is prospective testing of whether the direction of a mechanically controlled state-entry field relative to independently mapped active-drug-conditioned fitness changes lineage-linked tolerant-state accumulation through measured migration lag.
- **No nature claim:** no inference from Astra's chemical PDE to cancer cells. No inference from a tumor spheroid to a patient. No clinical advice.

## Prior art and boundary

Primary literature already preempts broad claims:

- Wu et al. placed motile metastatic breast cancer cells in a mutagenic doxorubicin gradient and tracked movement/proliferation at high-drug locations over 72 h ([PNAS 2013, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1314385110](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314385110)). Thus drug gradients plus migration and resistance emergence are not new.
- DuChez et al. tracked human glioblastoma, metastatic breast-cancer, and fibrosarcoma cells on stiffness gradients and reported directional durotaxis ([Biophysical Journal 2019, DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2019.01.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2019.01.009)). Thus stiffness-dependent migration is not new.
- Joyce et al. reported 3D matrix-stiffness-dependent doxorubicin responses in MDA-MB-231 cells and dynamic stiffness modulation; MCF7 did not show the same stiffness-dependent response in that platform ([Frontiers in Oncology 2018, DOI 10.3389/fonc.2018.00337](https://doi.org/10.3389/fonc.2018.00337)). Thus mechanics plus chemotherapy response is not new or universal across lines.
- Soliman et al. used confined MCF7 spheroids, reported peripheral CD44+/CD133+ cells, fitted IC50 values about two-fold apart (16.31 vs 7.74 μM; reported `p=0.104`, `N=3`), YAP/myosin perturbations, and confocal doxorubicin fluorescence penetration. They reported no difference in diffusion distance through 24 h, and note that the 48 h difference could reflect uptake/efflux response ([Advanced Science 2025, DOI 10.1002/advs.202509361](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202509361)). This is especially close. It also shows why fluorescence alone cannot be used as active intracellular exposure and why cell-state mechanisms, uptake/efflux, and transport need separate measurements.
- Fu et al. already modelled sanctuary regions, limited migration, and spatial drug concentration ([PLOS Computational Biology 2015, DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004142](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004142)); adaptive-therapy clone-layout studies and fixed-total-drug spatial pattern studies also preempt generic refuge-layout or spacing novelty ([Strobl et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-022-00110-x), [Freire et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012098)).

The bounded search did not identify a study that randomizes the phase of a mechanical state-entry cue against an independently measured active-drug-conditioned local net-growth field while holding both marginal field distributions fixed and linking the same cell tracks/states to lineage-resolved washout/rechallenge fate. This is **not** evidence that no such study exists; scope and priority remain open. The absence of that combination in the checked papers is not novelty clearance.

## Falsification-first experiment gate

An informative assay would use matched periodic or repeated-pattern 3D hydrogel/microfluidic chambers. Hold the measured active-drug field fixed; randomize the mechanical pattern between aligned and half-period-shifted phases, preserving its histogram. Include uniform-mechanics and uniform-drug controls. Predefine one lineage-level endpoint, e.g. probability a barcode yields a colony after washout/rechallenge, and a time series of mean tolerant-state burden spanning the pilot-predicted early and late regimes; do not choose the endpoint after seeing the crossing. Blind the image/lineage analysis. Independently measure local active drug/target engagement, stiffness or solid stress, cell positions and tracks, state-entry/reversal, division/death, oxygen, pH, nutrients, local density, matrix remodeling and media flow.

Estimate `D` and drift from tracks, `r(x)` from state-conditioned local division/death under target engagement, and `j(x)` from lineage-linked state transitions from the susceptible-like state. The current formula requires common measured transport across arms; an exact constant `+v/-v` reflection-symmetric special case has the same mean response, but general arm-/space-dependent durotaxis is outside it. Then freeze predictions before held-out phase/wavelength conditions. Include three comparators: (1) marginal-only/well-mixed model, which should predict no phase contrast when marginal maps match; (2) matched-information single-cell path/exposure model using observed tracks and exposure histories; (3) full spatial PDE with the same acquired fields. The candidate formula must beat neither by assumption: if the path baseline matches equally well, the formula is a compact mechanistic summary, not an algorithmic advantage. Use chamber/organoid as the randomized unit; cells are subsamples. After a blinded variance pilot, a paired design needs approximately `n ≈ ((z_.975+z_.80) σ_pair / δ)^2` independent chamber pairs for a two-sided 5% test and 80% power, with multiplicity/attrition added for multiple wavelengths; no sample count is supplied because `σ_pair` and biological `δ` are not known.

**Stop/falsify this model transfer** if active drug or target engagement changes with mechanical phase; measured state-entry source is not phase-locked; estimated drift/diffusion do not predict held-out response; arm-specific migration changes outside the symmetric case; phenotype markers do not forecast same-lineage rechallenge; or the matched-information path baseline predicts equally well while this model misses. Nonlinear crowding, resource competition, immune killing, reverse switching, state-specific motion, uptake/efflux, matrix feedback, and boundaries are expected departures to measure—not residuals to hide.

## Charged costs and practical limits

The toy formula is cheap to evaluate, but acquiring its input is not. Charge hydrogel fabrication and array throughput, stiffness/solid-stress calibration, drug mass transport and binding characterization, active free/nuclear exposure or target-engagement calibration (not only bulk fluorescence), oxygen/pH/nutrient and flow maps, 3D time-lapse tracks for `D,v`, repeated lineage/state reporter validation and barcode capture, division/death and phenotype transition rates, washout/rechallenge, matrix remodeling, chamber-level replication/attrition, imaging phototoxicity, reporter/barcode perturbation, sequencing, and data-processing costs. Hidden environment can make `r` and `j` non-identifiable. Toxicity constraints here are assay viability and imaging/reporter/drug exposure effects; no therapeutic dosing control is proposed. No sample count or resource budget is claimed before a variance and feasibility pilot. Fit uncertainty in all of `D,v,r0,g,j1,k,T`; a point estimate near the crossover is not an actionable sign prediction.

This line is independent of the known preempted cancer branching/CTMDP/A3A bundle routes and the existing spatial refuge principal-eigenvalue/PDE source-flux gauges ([spatial ecology](../cancer_evolution/children/spatial_ecology.md), [sanctuary source-flux gate](../cancer_evolution/children/cycle2_spatial_gate.md), [A3A/cancer-control report](../c7_cancer_evolution_control/FINAL_REPORT.md)). It also does not use the N54 stoichiometric heat-mixing result. The pinned [N54 card](../../astra_prior/cards/N54-spatial-permanence.txt) and source extract ([relative-reaction bridge](../systems_metabolism/reaction_inference/sources/proofs/d-cfb13618e402da57.html.txt)) describe a source-system mechanism only; they transfer neither cell trajectories nor tumor permanence. In particular, a phenotype-creation term `S→R` yields a per-capita contribution proportional to `S/R`, unbounded as `R→0`, which is outside the source report's relative-reaction heat-kernel bound—the present assay model deliberately starts at `R=0` with an additive measured source. Its useful design lesson is interface discipline: the same diffusion coefficient, source, hidden environment, and positivity assumptions must actually be measured before any model-to-nature transfer.
