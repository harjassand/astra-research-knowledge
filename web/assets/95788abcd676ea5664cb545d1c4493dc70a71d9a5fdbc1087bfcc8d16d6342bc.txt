Tensor parametrization and stress conjugacy clarification
Status: append-only clarification of v2; enlarged theorem still pending internal audit.

The causal transparency lemma in v2 should identify the metric tensor probe
using a unimodular tensor parametrization,

  g_ij=a^2 [exp(h)]_ij,  tr h=0,  partial_i h_ij=0 at linear order.

Then det(exp h)=1, so the tensor variation does not perturb the spatial
volume and its action-conjugate matter operator is the mixed traceless
stress at linear order, with only a fixed positive polarization factor.
This removes the spurious background-pressure quadratic terms present
when one parametrizes g_ij=a^2(delta_ij+h_ij) through second order. Those
terms cancel against background gravity in the full action, but carrying
them in the matter susceptibility alone would obscure the PSD contact
claim. There is no change to any LINEAR tensor or scalar variable in v1-v2.

For a cross shear h_xz=h_zx=h, direct expansion gives

 delta B_1=-h S_z(b_x),
 delta B_2=1/2 b_i tensor b_j (h^2)_ij,
 P_2=P(b_x,b_x) h^2+1/2 H(S_z(b_x),S_z(b_x))h^2
                                        =-mu_T h^2/2.

Thus the bare tensor contact in the v2 quadratic Lagrangian is exactly
-M h^2/2, with M the positive-volume-scaled mu_T, and no cosmological
constant or isotropic pressure term enters M. Cross terms h E q and
h F qdot are unchanged because exp(h)=I+h+O(h^2). The canonical
feedthrough -(M+F K^{-1}F^T) and first regular coefficient
s E K^{-1}E^T are therefore action-conjugate stress response coefficients
with the intended signs.

When treating q or Fourier modes in a complex basis, transpose in the
quadratic PSD coefficients is Hermitian adjoint at the paired momentum
-p. A real sine/cosine basis, used in v2, avoids that convention issue.

Operational matching must also refer to the complete sourced tensor
Green function. Equality of a free on-shell dispersion or wavefront speed
cannot be substituted for equality of that Green function and its known
source normalization. The Ward scalar theorem for singlet/triplet media
requires only the tensor mass/contact zero because no field tensor modes
exist there; the arbitrary-multiplet extension needs the full retarded
kernel zero. Neither follows from GW170817's speed constraint alone.
