STRONGEST POST-INITIAL RESULTS

1. The symplectic PPT example has a sharper EB comparison than the generic
power lemma in INITIAL. For Prudhoe's imported channel Phi_0, its HS spectrum
is 1 on scalars, 2/(d+2) on the traceless +1 eigenspace of the symplectic
transpose involution, and 0 on the -1 eigenspace. The completely depolarizing
EB channel Delta is the scalar projection, so

    I-Delta <= ((d+2)/d)(I-Phi_0).

The constant tends to 1 through even dimensions. This is an exact spectral
corollary; PPT/non-EB status remains imported. The family has not been shown
selfcompatible and does not settle the broadcasting target. See
revisions/03_symplectic_spectral_comparator.md. The original c=2 power result
and its source attribution remain valid but are weaker for this example.

2. For any finite-dimensional bistochastic UCP self-adjoint channel Phi with
an actual two-output broadcaster, Fix(Phi) is abelian. Kadison--Schwarz plus
faithful trace puts fixed points in the multiplicative domain of Phi; applying
the same faithful-defect argument to each output leg of the broadcaster puts
X tensor I and I tensor Y in the broadcaster's multiplicative domain, forcing
XY=YX. The Cesaro spectral projection E onto Fix(Phi) is then a
trace-preserving EB conditional expectation and

    I-E <= gamma(Phi)^(-1)(I-Phi),

where gamma is the least nonzero deficit 1-lambda. This gives a one-map
half-trace reconstruction bound (1/2)sqrt(theta r/gamma) for bounded
likelihoods. The proof does not require Hilbert--Schmidt positivity of Phi;
that assumption makes the ordinary powers converge to E. The gap-dependent
mechanism overlaps c05_l03's independent fixed-point algebra / EB projection
result. I attribute that overlap explicitly; this extension is elementary
and carries no novelty claim. A classical two-state EB channel has arbitrarily
small gamma, showing only that the fixed-projection route is not a uniform
bound.

3. A gap-free power variant applies when a supplied EB orthogonal projection
E commutes with Phi and the complement contracts in L2 by kappa<1. Then
Psi_m=E Phi^m is EB and

    ||Phi^m-Psi_m||_(infinity-to-normalized-one) <= kappa^m,
    I-Psi_m <= (1-kappa^m)^(-1)(I-Phi^m).

This preserves all slow modes inside range(E); the comparison constant tends
to 1. The direct-sum reversible-center/quantum-fiber construction of c05_l05
is an exact instance with kappa=1/4, hence 4^(-m) map error and comparison
constant (1-4^(-m))^(-1). That construction and its actual broadcaster remain
attributed to c05_l05. The general lemma isolates its useful structural
condition; it does not show such an E exists for arbitrary compatible maps.
See revisions/04_invariant_EB_projection.md and the exact rational spectral
checker.

4. The incompatibility-breaking route yields only pairwise parents from a
two-output broadcaster. A UCP broadcaster makes Phi(A_i) and Phi(B_j)
jointly measurable for every chosen pair of output POVMs, via
G_ij=Gamma(A_i tensor B_j). An explicit qubit boundary is Delta_(3/5): mixing
the symmetric 1-to-2 cloner marginal Delta_(2/3) with a replacement channel
in weights 9/10 and 1/10 gives a selfcompatible channel. Every pair among the
three noisy Pauli binary POVMs has an explicit joint parent, but the triple is
incompatible since 3(3/5)^2>1. Therefore Delta_(3/5) is 2-selfcompatible but
not 3-selfcompatible. This blocks a direct proof that pairwise compatibility
provides one parent for a growing frame. It is not an EB-rounding
counterexample. The exact Sympy/Fraction checker verifies the cloner marginal,
pair parents, triple threshold and a small classical-gap fixture.
