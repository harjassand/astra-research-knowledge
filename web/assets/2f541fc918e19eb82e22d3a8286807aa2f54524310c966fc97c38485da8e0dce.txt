RESULT
The squared-complementary-minor support problem for rational-complex F has an exact representation as weighted intersection of two projected linear delta-matroids. One is D(A) for A=[[0,F],[-F^T,0]]; the other is a local projected gadget forbidding both orientations of a site. A common feasible set with site projection R exists exactly when f(V\R)>0. Assigning weight w_i to each orientation turns maximum support weight into a maximum-weight common feasible set.

The algebraic equivalence is proved in INITIAL.txt. The small exact diagnostic passes 80 Gaussian-integer matrices n=1..5, validates the 3-vertex projection gadget, and shows why the at-most-one constraint cannot be dropped. Koana–Wahlström's STACS 2025 Theorem 5/Corollary 6 provide a randomized weighted delta-matroid intersection search algorithm over sufficiently large finite fields, with O(WN^(ω+1)) field operations (N=2n). Revision 001 supplies a rational-complex transfer: clearing denominators, a polynomial bit bound on every witness minor's Gaussian norm, B+1 polynomial-size candidate primes, finite extensions of polynomial numeric size, one-sided lifting of modular witnesses, and repetition to control the algorithm's random error. This gives an end-to-end randomized polynomial-bit algorithm for unweighted maximum support and pseudo-polynomial time in integer maximum weight, conditional on the cited imported algorithm. It remains an optimization of zero/nonzero support only.

A separate obstruction targets the direct quantum/matchgate route. The local two-replica equality-plus-no-double-occupancy tensor has signature 1 on {0000,1010,0101} and 0 elsewhere. It violates the four-leg Matchgate Identity, so a naive doubled BCS Gaussian contraction cannot encode the norm sum with this natural local tensor. The violation also persists under wire permutations and invertible diagonal leg gauges. The executable `copy_gate_mgi_check.py` confirms the identity remains nonzero for all 24 wire permutations and 2^8 sign diagonal gauges. This is not a no-go for arbitrary non-diagonal basis changes or larger non-Gaussian encodings. The aggregate f itself also has a 4x4 natural-hole-basis MGI counterexample (INITIAL).

The result does not compute f(U), estimate positive values, sample determinant configurations, prove mixing/energy bounds, or imply an FPRAS. Weighted support is pseudo-polynomial in W; the field-intersection theorem is randomized and imported, not implemented here. No novelty/priority claim is made. No peer cycle6 outputs were read; INITIAL was frozen before further work.
