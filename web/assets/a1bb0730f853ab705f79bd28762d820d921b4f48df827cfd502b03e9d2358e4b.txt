STRONGEST CLAIM
A dimension-free Hilbert-Schmidt bound on a normalized Choi operator does not by itself imply dimension-free approximation of the entire Hermitian operator-norm unit ball in normalized trace norm. The failure already occurs for explicit bistochastic completely positive maps, with an exact ratio d. This blocks a tempting norm-conversion step from Hilbert-Schmidt de Finetti estimates to the normalized infinity-to-one map norm used by the current EB-rounding gate. It is only a norm-conversion counterexample: the maps below are not asserted to be selfcompatible, and this does not refute the main broadcasting-to-EB target.

EXACT INTERFACE AND DERIVATION
Let d be even, M_d carry normalized trace tau(X)=Tr(X)/d, and let U,V be traceless Hermitian unitaries. For 0<=t<=1 define the depolarizing map D(X)=tau(X)I and

    Phi_t(X) = tau(X) I + t tau(U X) V.

Use the normalized Choi convention Jhat(Phi)=(id tensor Phi)(|Omega><Omega|), where |Omega>=d^(-1/2) sum_i |ii>. Then

    Jhat(D)=I_(d^2)/d^2,
    Jhat(Phi_t)=(I_(d^2)+t U^T tensor V)/d^2.

Since U^T tensor V is a Hermitian unitary, Jhat(Phi_t)>=0 for t<=1. The formula for Phi_t also gives Phi_t(I)=I and tau(Phi_t(X))=tau(X), so Phi_t is a trace-preserving UCP map (and its Schrödinger adjoint is a bistochastic channel).

Define ||T||_(infinity-to-1,tau)=sup{tau(|T(X)|): X=X*, ||X||_infinity<=1}. For every such X, trace Holder gives |tau(UX)|<=1, hence

    ||Phi_t-D||_(infinity-to-1,tau) <= t tau(|V|)=t.

Taking X=U gives equality because (Phi_t-D)(U)=tV and tau(|V|)=1. Meanwhile, in ordinary Hilbert-Schmidt norm on the normalized Choi matrix,

    ||Jhat(Phi_t)-Jhat(D)||_2
       = t ||U^T tensor V||_2/d^2 = t/d.

Thus the full-ball normalized infinity-to-one error is t while normalized-Choi Hilbert-Schmidt error tends to zero as t/d. Equivalently, any estimate based only on that Choi-HS quantity needs a factor at least d. This is exact for every even d, not a numerical conjecture.

The diagnostic `norm_interface_check.py` constructed d=2,4,8 cases at t=1/2. It checked Choi positivity, unit trace, the Hilbert-Schmidt distance t/d, and the witness output error t. All three bounded fixtures passed. They are transcription checks; the preceding formulas prove the all-even-dimension claim. Matrix sizes were at most 64 by 64. No solver, large computation, download, or external service was used.

PRIMARY-SOURCE AND PRIOR-ART AUDIT

1. Exact no-broadcasting is established, but is qualitative at this interface. Barnum, Caves, Fuchs, Jozsa and Schumacher define broadcasting by a channel whose two marginals reproduce each member of a state family and prove that a pair is broadcastable exactly when its states commute. For a commuting family, measurement in a common eigenbasis followed by preparation broadcasts it. This is exact, finite-family structure; it supplies no approximate modulus for growing families, no bounded-Holevo uniform EB theorem, and no estimate in the channel norm above. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/9511010 (especially the definition and theorem at lines 39-47).

2. The dimension-free information-disturbance and no-broadcasting continuity bounds are in completely bounded norm, not merely the unamplified norm. Kretschmann, Schlingemann and Werner prove

    inf_U ||(1 tensor U)V_1 - V_2||_infinity^2 <= ||T_1-T_2||_cb
       <= 2 inf_U ||(1 tensor U)V_1 - V_2||_infinity,

and derive a dimension-free information-disturbance inequality comparing the complementary channel with a constant channel when the main channel has a decoder close to identity in cb norm. Their no-broadcasting corollary likewise assumes one output marginal is close to identity in cb norm. The target here assumes preservation only on a supplied, bounded-capacity family, not on every input and reference extension.

   The same paper gives an exact topology warning: with S the depolarizing channel, Theta the transpose, and T=S+(Theta-S)/(d+1), T is a channel and ||T-S||_infinity<=2/(d+1) while ||T-S||_cb >=(d-1)/(d+1). Therefore no dimension-free upgrade from unamplified channel norm to cb norm is valid in general, even near a constant channel. This is a direct obstruction to applying the cb theorem from only a state-level/unamplified estimate. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0605009, Theorem 1, Theorem 3/Corollary 4, and Proposition 5 / equations (43)-(48).

3. Joint measurability and nondisturbance concern different interfaces. Heinosaari and Wolf define joint measurability by a parent POVM with specified marginals. An instrument implementing A that leaves all B outcome probabilities unchanged yields joint effects G_(x,y)=I_x^*(B_y). But joint measurability need not yield an instrument on the same output system that preserves B's statistics; the paper gives examples, including compatible noisy versions of noncommuting sharp observables. Consequently, a pairwise POVM compatibility statement is not a uniform EB reconstruction theorem for a whole family or for a CP map's entire unit ball. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/1005.5659, Sections 2.2-2.4 and the two-outcome example in Section 3.1.

4. The local-measurement de Finetti theorem does not permit moving its maximization inside its averaging. Brandao and Harrow's Theorem 1 has the form

    min_(sigma separable) max_(Lambda^B) E_m ||(E_m tensor Lambda^B)(rho-sigma)||_1
       <= sqrt(2 ln |A-tilde|/k).

Their Conjecture 5 asks for the stronger order E_m max_(Lambda^B), equivalently a worst local operation and correlated measurement before averaging, and explicitly says a different proof approach is needed. This is precisely a collective-measurement placement issue; it cannot be resolved by algebraically exchanging max and expectation. Their theorem's dimension-free dependence is on the number of outcomes of a selected local operation, not a trace-distance approximation of a channel on its complete operator ball. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/1210.6367, Theorem 1 and Conjecture 5 (equations (1), (12)-(13)).

5. The August 2026 optimal de Finetti preprint gives a real dimension-free result, but in a different metric and under Bose support. Jeronimo, Wu and Xu prove for a two-site marginal of a state supported on Sym^N(C^d) that distance to a Hartree mixture in ordinary Hilbert-Schmidt norm is at most min{sqrt(d-1)/(N-1), 8/sqrt(N-1)}. The source explicitly warns that purification does not transfer this HS conclusion to arbitrary exchangeable states because partial trace need not contract Hilbert-Schmidt norm. Even if a corresponding HS bound were available for normalized Choi states, the exact channel family above shows that HS closeness alone cannot control the needed normalized infinity-to-one map norm independently of d. Bose-subspace support and the Choi/TP constraints also require separate verification for any proposed channel transfer. This is an arXiv preprint result, not an external validation of this application. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02590, Theorem 6.1 and the discussion at the end of Section 1.2.2 / start of Section 6.

6. The September 30, 2026 channel de Finetti preprint is an important update, but it still has a dimension-dependent, multi-input interface. Ye, Bergh and Datta, arXiv:2609.40343v1, Theorem 2, assume a permutation-covariant channel N^(n):A^n->B^n and form its reduced channel by feeding maximally mixed states to discarded inputs and tracing discarded outputs. They prove CP domination of the reduced k-site channel by a mixture of iid channels, with

    epsilon <= d_A^2 d_B^2 k/n
        + 2 d_A k sqrt((d_A^2 d_B^2-1)/(n+d_A^2 d_B^2-1))
        + d_A^2 k^2/(n+d_A^2 d_B^2-1).

This improves dimension dependence over the earlier channel de Finetti conversion and directly yields diamond-norm bounds, but the displayed error retains local dimensions. Its separate exponential theorem adds a no-signalling hypothesis. A one-input splitter A->B^K does not automatically define an A^n->B^n channel satisfying these hypotheses: if one active input is used to prepare several receivers, that input signals to outputs at other sites. A valid embedding with the exact covariance/no-signalling conditions, and error independent of local dimensions, is not supplied by the theorem. The phrase “operator inequality” here means Loewner/CP domination, not a dimension-free operator norm estimate. This is a recent arXiv preprint and is not externally validated here. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.40343, Theorem 2 and channel setup, equations (7)-(8), (58)-(61).

CURRENT TARGET AND BOUNDARIES
The exact inherited target is still one EB map approximating the whole bounded-likelihood experiment, or the stronger tracial gate I-Psi<=c(I-Phi) for arbitrary positive trace-selfadjoint selfcompatible Phi. The current report `work/cycle4/detailed_balance_broadcast_attack.txt` proves this comparison only for specified POVM/Luders and supplied bistochastic-instrument constructions, and some factor/tensor closures. It explicitly leaves the unrestricted case open. The minimax convex-roof form there is collective: one common measurement must control the prior-weighted variance simultaneously. The prior-source audit above does not prove that general comparison, produce a qualifying counterexample, or determine novelty.

The strongest precise obstruction found here is therefore a transfer obstruction: a metric-specific dimension-free theorem cannot be silently upgraded across the Hilbert-Schmidt, unamplified operator, cb/diamond, and full experiment operator-ball norms. The next decisive gate for this source-audit line is either (i) a source theorem whose exact hypotheses yield the required normalized infinity-to-one approximation without dimension, or (ii) a new argument converting the current broadcaster/capacity premises to that norm. The cited literature supplies neither as checked here. For the larger mathematical target, the load-bearing gate remains proving or refuting the universal EB Dirichlet/collective-variance bound.
