1. Load-bearing local estimates independently replayed

Fix 1<p<2, uniform shape-regular quasiuniform P1 mesh, and common buffer.
Let G=||grad u_h||_p. The P1 inverse estimate and Poincare imply, at ANY
point x (not just a vertex),

  |u_h(x)-M_h u_h(x)|^p
    <= C h^{p-2} integral_{B(x,C h)} |grad u_h|^p.        (1.1)

Only O(1) triangles meet this h-ball; each has area comparable to h^2.
For t>=h, smooth averaging gives

  |M_t u_h(x)-M_{2t}u_h(x)|^p
    <= C t^{p-2} integral_{B(x,Ct)} |grad u_h|^p.        (1.2)

An arbitrary final ratio of scales between1 and2 is handled by exactly the
same estimate, so the dyadic proof does not require s/h to be a power of2.

For a positive measure mu, integrating either inequality and applying
Tonelli replaces the center mass by mu(B(y,Ct)). Thus if mu has genuine
fine growth A(r+h)^alpha, and theta=(p+alpha-2)/p>0,

  ||u_h-M_su_h||_{L^p(mu)} <= C A^{1/p}s^theta G,
                  h<=s,                              (1.3)

by summing the pth-root errors with Minkowski. This proves DIRECT(1)
including its atom-scale charge and also verifies its use for the ORIGINAL
marginal in ANCHOR(12). Positivity is used in Tonelli; no atom count and no
probability normalization are used.

For mu=tau#nu, displacement <=R>=h supplies only

                  mu(B(y,r))<=A(r+R+h)^alpha.           (1.4)

The atom-scale pth-power cost is now C A h^{p-2}R^alpha G^p.
At h<=t<=R, each increment norm is at most
C A^{1/p} R^{alpha/p} t^{(p-2)/p}G. Since p<2, their sum is controlled
by the SMALLEST scale h. At t>=R the growth is C A t^alpha and the sum
to s is controlled by the LARGEST scale s because theta>0. This proves

  ||u_h-M_su_h||_{L^p(tau#nu)}
    <= C A^{1/p}G [(h^{p-2}R^alpha)^{1/p}+s^theta],
                    h<=R<=s,                          (1.5)

which is exactly ANCHOR(4). There is no assignment of atom mass O(h^alpha)
to the push. Its atoms can have mass O(R^alpha).
