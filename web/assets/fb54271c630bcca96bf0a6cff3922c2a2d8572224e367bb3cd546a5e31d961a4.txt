1. INTERFACE AND CERTIFICATE

Use the finite and continuum definitions in proof.txt. J,tau>0 and the
public coupling interval is 0<g_-<=g<=g_+. Put gamma=2g_+ and define

 C0=4 sqrt[(1+pi^2/6)/24],
 ell=sqrt(3) sqrt(1-gamma/(6J))-sqrt(C0 gamma/J),
 Emax=(64/9)J pi^2+4gamma,
 b0=ell^2/2,
 L0=max(4,ceil(Emax/gamma)).                             (1.1)

This module applies if gamma<6J and ell>0. All inequalities may instead
be certified using rational upper/lower enclosures. Then uniformly for
ALL finite L>=L0 and for the continuum two-body canonical heat trace,

 z_2(g_+)-z_2(2g_+) >= d0:=tau g_+ b0 exp(-tau Emax)>0.  (1.2)

Consequently F(g)=z_2(g)/z_1^2 satisfies, for g_-<=a<b<=g_+,

 F(a)-F(b)>=kappa(b-a),
 kappa=d0/[g_+(z_1^*)^2],
 z_1^*=sum_(k>=1)exp(-8tau J k^2).                     (1.3)

Thus the nondestructive count localizer has an entirely explicit uniform
inverse-slope certificate in this window. Neither an interacting spectrum
nor an interacting ground state is supplied or acquired by this lemma.
It is a bound, not a Gibbs-state preparation algorithm or a practical
certificate for every other constant in proof.txt.

2. EXACT TWO-PARTICLE K IDENTITIES, NOT ONLY AN INEQUALITY

For x1<x2 in {1,...,L-1}, set y1=x1, y2=x2-1 in {1,...,n}, n=L-2.
The map K sends psi(y1,y2) to psi(sort z)/sqrt(2) on the full labeled
cube. In the normalized symmetric occupation basis, its diagonal entry
is 1 on distinct y values and 1/sqrt(2) on ties. It is invertible between
the finite two-particle sector and the symmetric cube. Let P be the cube
diagonal projector, T the two-boson Dirichlet path kinetic operator with
one-particle eigenvalues 4JL^2[1-cos(pi k/(L-1))], and B=2L P. Then

 K^*T K=L^2 Hcrit,2,  K^*B K=L W,
 (K^-1)^*K^-1=I+P=I+B/(2L).                            (2.1)

Here the first identity is EXACT: every legal two-particle chamber edge
has precisely 2 labeled lifts. A distinct-to-distinct hop has the other
coordinate fixed, a tie-to-distinct hop has two choices of moving label,
and a distinct-to-tie hop has two incoming label arrangements. A ghost
edge at either endpoint also has precisely 2 lifts, including a tie at
that endpoint. The K amplitude factor 1/2 cancels this multiplicity.
The other identities are the diagonal normalization just stated. These
special equalities are not asserted at m>2.

Let E be the lowest eigenvalue of the positive physical A_L,2(gamma) and
psi a normalized ground vector. Put u=K psi. Rayleigh positivity for all
physical vectors, together with invertibility of K, implies

 T+(gamma-E/(2L))B-E I>=0,
 [T+(gamma-E/(2L))B]u=E u.                             (2.2)

This is a generalized mass problem turned into an ordinary ground-state
problem at alpha=gamma-E/(2L). No Perron-Frobenius argument is needed.
Also ||u||^2=1-<psi,W psi>/2, since W is 0 or 1 at m=2.

3. FREE GROUND, GAP AND UNIFORM UPPER ENERGY

For L>=4, q=pi/(L-1)<=pi/3. The normalized free two-boson ground is
phi=phi1 tensor phi1, phi1(v)=sqrt(2/(L-1))sin(qv). Its free energy e0
and gap Delta above it obey

 e0=8JL^2(1-cos q)<=4Jpi^2 L^2/(L-1)^2,
 Delta=4JL^2(cos q-cos 2q)>=24J L^2/(L-1)^2>=24J,
 e0/Delta<=pi^2/6,
 B[phi]=2L sum_v|phi1(v)|^4=3L/(L-1) in [3,4].         (3.1)

For the gap bound use cos q-cos 2q=2sin(3q/2)sin(q/2) and
sin s>=2s/pi on [0,pi/2]. The sine-fourth sum is 3(L-1)/8 for
L-1>=3; L=3 is deliberately excluded because of aliasing.

The physical trial K^-1 phi has squared norm >=1, so its Rayleigh quotient
gives E<=e0+gamma B[phi]<=Emax. If L>=L0, then E<=gamma L. Therefore
alpha=gamma-E/(2L) is in [gamma/2,gamma] and

 ||u||^2>=1-E/(2gamma L)>=1/2.                         (3.2)

In the continuum, the same free constants are safely bounded by (3.1):
e0=4Jpi^2, Delta=6Jpi^2>=24J, B[phi]=3. The continuum ground at gamma
directly has positive effective coupling gamma and norm one.

4. A DISCRETE AND CONTINUUM CONTACT TRACE INEQUALITY

For any finitely supported one-dimensional complex sequence a extended
by zero, adding telescoping identities from both ends and applying
Cauchy-Schwarz proves

 |a_s|^2 <= ||a|| (sum_v|a_(v+1)-a_v|^2)^(1/2).        (4.1)

Indeed 2|a_s|^2 is at most sum_v |a_(v+1)-a_v|(|a_(v+1)|+|a_v|),
whose second squared factor is at most 4||a||^2. For a cube vector w,
apply this to coordinate 1 at s=coordinate 2, sum the other coordinate,
then use Cauchy-Schwarz. If w is symmetric, its two directional energies
are equal and T[w]=4JL^2 D1[w]. Thus

 B[w]=2L sum_s|w(s,s)|^2 <=J^-1/2 ||w|| sqrt(T[w]).     (4.2)

For H^1_0 symmetric continuum vectors the same proof uses zero extension
and |a(s)|^2<=||a||||a'||. Density extends the inequality to the full
common form domain. This is a trace inequality, not an operator bound
on the unbounded contact form or a kinetic second-moment estimate.

5. QUANTITATIVE GROUND CONTACT WITHOUT SOLVING THE MODEL

Normalize v=u/||u|| in (2.2) and decompose v=a phi+w, w perpendicular
to phi. Since alpha>=0, T[v]<=E<=e0+4gamma. The free gap gives

 ||w||^2<=4gamma/Delta<=gamma/(6J),
 T[w]<=4gamma+e0||w||^2<=4gamma(1+pi^2/6).              (5.1)

By (4.2), B[w]<=C0 gamma/J. The triangle inequality in the contact trace
space and (3.1) yield

 sqrt(B[v])>=|a|sqrt(B[phi])-sqrt(B[w])>=ell.

For the finite physical ground, B[u]=<psi,LW psi>, and (3.2) implies
<psi,LW psi>>=ell^2/2=b0. The continuum ground has the stronger bound
B[v]>=ell^2, so the unified lower bound b0 is valid there as well.

6. FROM CONTACT TO HEAT SECANT AND INVERSE SLOPE

Use the ground at gamma=2g_+ as a trial comparison at g_+. The common
positive-form variational principle gives

 E_2(gamma)-E_2(g_+)>=g_+ b0.                         (6.1)

Every other ordered eigenvalue is nondecreasing in g. Consequently the
entire heat-trace difference is at least its ground-term difference:

 z_2(g_+)-z_2(gamma)
 >=exp(-tau E_2(gamma))[exp(tau(E_2(gamma)-E_2(g_+)))-1]
 >=tau g_+ b0 exp(-tau Emax)=d0.

Finite heat traces are convex and decreasing by the positive Duhamel
second derivative. Continuum convexity follows from the fixed-sector
heat-trace limit already banked, or bounded positive contact-form
regularization; no pointwise unbounded derivative assumption is made.
The secant inside [g_-,g_+] is bounded by the future secant on
[g_+,2g_+], giving (1.3) with z_1<=z_1^*. The lemma works uniformly
in bounded lambda because the ratio cancels lambda exactly.
