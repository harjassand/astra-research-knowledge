Let g be ANY finite-dimensional real subspace of traceless Hermitian d-by-d
matrices closed under -i[T,S]. Define
 f(H)=rho=e^H/Tr e^H, H in g.
This is an injective smooth map with inverse on its image
 H=logrho-(Tr logrho/d)I.
For local injectivity, differentiate in a traceless Hermitian direction Y.
In an eigenbasis H=diag(h_i), let p_i=e^h_i/Tr e^H. The derivative has

 (Df_H[Y])_ij=(p_i-p_j)/(h_i-h_j) Y_ij,       i!=j,
 (Df_H[Y])_ii=p_i[Y_ii-Tr(rhoY)].

At coincident eigenvalues use the continuous quotient p_i. This formula follows
from Dexp_H[Y]=integral_0^1 e^((1-s)H)Y e^(sH)ds and differentiating the
normalizer. If the derivative vanishes, every offdiagonal Y vanishes and all
Y_ii equal Tr rhoY; trace zero then implies Y=0. Thus f is an immersion, and
its continuous smooth log inverse makes the image an embedded manifold M.
For rho>=etaI, all h_i=logp_i-mean_j logp_j are bounded by log(1/eta) in
absolute value. The corresponding H set in the closed finite subspace g is
compact. No hidden noncompact chart boundary remains inside this cutoff.

Define the even real function
              g0(z)=(z/2)coth(z/2),      g0(0)=1.
Define ad_H(X)=[H,X], the ORDINARY commutator on the complexification g_C;
it is not the Hermitian Lie bracket -i[H,X]. The operator ad_H on complex
matrices is HS selfadjoint, with real spectrum
h_i-h_j. g0(ad_H) is therefore smooth spectral functional calculus. Since
the complexification of g is invariant under ad_H and the even real function
preserves the real Hermitian form, Y_T=g0(ad_H)T belongs to g for every T in g.
Equivalently one may interpolate the even real function on the finite
spectrum by an even real polynomial; its even commutator powers preserve g.
The diagonal entries of Y_T equal T_ii, so Tr rhoY_T=Tr rhoT=t_T.

Substitute Y_T in the derivative formula. Offdiagonal entries become
 (p_i-p_j)/(h_i-h_j) *[(h_i-h_j)/2 coth((h_i-h_j)/2)]T_ij
              =(p_i+p_j)T_ij/2.

             Df_H[g0(ad_H)T]=V_T(rho).                  (C1)
