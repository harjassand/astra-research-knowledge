# Failed or bounded routes

1. **Pooled H3K9me3/CENP-A D1:D2 cross-odds with “shared DNA weights cancel.”** Withdrawn. The copy weights remain inside domain sums. A concrete two-tag counterexample gives raw `Phi=9.90198` while the equal-tag per-copy value is exactly `1`. Original pooled values persist only in the corrected JSON under a field explicitly labeled invalid.
2. **Promote the ~2× pooled number as a per-copy or absolute occupancy discovery.** Rejected. The repaired ~1.99 tagwise interaction is a conditional compositional phase-shape estimate from one PC-sequencing library per marker-condition. It assumes same-code copy structure and tag bias stable across compared aliquots and does not identify absolute occupancy, a joint same-cell state, or HAC retention.
3. **Treat count-only Poisson confidence limits as biological confidence.** Rejected. Technical mapped-read uncertainty is small; biological replicate, clone-mixture, synchronization-purity, structure, and assay variance are unestimated.
4. **Fit an exponential reload/relaxation rate from COL to one CR3 3-h point.** Rejected as nonidentified. An exponential in `log(Phi)` and an exponential in `Phi−1` both fit the same endpoints and give different half-times; the samples are separate synchronization conditions, not a same-clone trajectory. No rate is carried into the final claims.
5. **Infer nonlinear neighbor invasion/front velocity or a universal 3-h recovery law from 2-kb barcodes alone.** Rejected. A source-constrained sign pattern is possible, but free local recruitment/replication forcing can exactly alias any chosen spatial coupling. Corrected HAC120 D1−D2 log2(Phi) falls from 0.991 in COL to 0.559 in CR3, while HAC465 D3−D4 rises from 0.227 to 0.929. The dynamic model is a candidate awaiting local replication timing and a same-clone time series; the two clone estimates oppose a universal relaxation story.
6. **Claim HJURP/KAT7 phase targeting as a novel result.** Prior-art bounded. Early-G1 CENP-A loading, HJURP, KAT7, targeted HAC chromatin engineering, and H3K9 antagonism are all established or source-reported. Core-specific barcoded recruitment plus functional single-kinetochore/structural outcomes is a distinct untested experiment, not an advance completed here.
7. **Assume one CENP-A dense domain equals one functional kinetochore, or assume D1 and D2 are either one or two kinetochores.** Rejected. The NAR source notes two sites may both matter and spacing may matter; 2026 NSMB demonstrates that ectopic CENP-A/CENP-C domains can form functional dicentrics in another context. Count CENP-C plus KNL1/NDC80 loci per intact HAC.
8. **Import a generic replication-origin map to this synthetic HAC.** Rejected. The 2014 alphoid-tetO HAC suppresses initiation within the alpha-satellite array and relies on transcriptionally active Bsr inserts, unlike the distinct 2026 barcoded construction. Local fork timing must be measured in the actual clone.
9. **Treat megabase DNA delivery as chromosome replication/segregation.** Rejected. SynNICE delivered 1.14-Mb hAZFa into early mouse embryos but reported that its average DNA depth was not halved with cell division, consistent with no tandem replication. Large DNA delivery and autonomous chromosome inheritance are separate capabilities.

The scientifically defensible outcome of this branch is a corrected descriptive statistic, a copy-cancellation proof with explicit assumptions, and a narrow experiment/model gate—not a demonstrated stability-control principle.
