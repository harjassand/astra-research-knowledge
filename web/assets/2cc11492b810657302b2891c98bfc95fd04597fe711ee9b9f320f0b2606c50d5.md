# Cycle 9 model attack: what can and cannot be inferred about Fog–integrin recruitment

**Question.** Can the 2026 Fog/Smog/Gα/endocytosis results support a nonlinear mechanochemical recruitment model with a sharp robust-propagation threshold, and is that threshold identifiable against contact-age and mechanical-relay alternatives?

**Outcome.** A source-constrained *model architecture* is possible, and one chosen positive-feedback module has an exact saddle-node threshold. But the papers do not identify its parameters or convert that local recruitment fold into a wave-propagation threshold. More strongly, a cell-local contact-age model and a Fog-gated mechanical relay reproduce the reported moving-frame exponential profiles and intervention directions without lateral transport of the slow Fog pool. Therefore no unique transport, ligand-dose, receptor-clearance, or range–speed threshold follows from the current evidence. This is an identifiability no-go, not a claim that the reported Fog mechanism is false.

## 1. Source constraints

The 2026 primary study establishes the following constraints on any candidate model:

1. The primordium can initiate invagination when Gα is rescued there, yet with Gα absent from the propagation zone the wave recruits MyoII for only about 2.5 rows versus 7.1 in controls. So initiation and sustained propagation are separable, and propagation-zone Fog–GPCR output is necessary in the tested setup.
2. Diffusible Fog expressed only in the primordium rescues about 5.3 rows after Fog depletion; similarly sourced membrane-tethered Fog gives about 1.7 rows despite a similar initial invagination. Thus a dispersed source is necessary in that assay. This does not imply a free-Fog concentration gradient: endogenous Fog::YFP is reported approximately uniform in the extracellular perivitelline space.
3. Apical FCS resolves an 84% fast pool (`D≈51 μm²/s`) and a 16% slow pool (`D≈1.6 μm²/s`) in the propagation zone. The slow pool is *presumed* membrane-interacting/receptor-bound, not chemically identified. Gprk2 RNAi raises the slow fraction to about 30% and flattens its spatial slope; Smog brightness (an oligomerization/activation proxy) also responds to Fog and Gprk2.
4. Integrin loss suppresses the expanded/high MyoII gradient under Gprk2 depletion, yet does not reduce Smog brightness; the authors therefore place integrins as signaling cofactors downstream of clustering, not as a demonstrated cause of clustering.
5. Higher Fog output or Gprk2 depletion expands the MyoII profile and slows invagination, consistent with stronger adhesion and slower de-adhesion. VM contact and βPS-integrin profiles expand under Gprk2 depletion as well.
6. The source itself describes a repeated contact → integrin adhesion → MyoII activation → detachment cycle, MyoII–adhesion positive feedback, and temporal maturation of signaling in each cell as it approaches the front. It explicitly says that the exact molecular mechanism is not fully resolved and leaves Fog modification/extracellular regulation open.

These constraints reject a *pure* mechanical relay without propagation-zone GPCR input and reject a simple free-ligand-gradient account. They do not reject a Fog-gated mechanical relay or a contact-age model with uniform free Fog.

## 2. Minimal nonlinear model consistent with those constraints

Use a discrete row index `i`, increasing anteriorly, with a moving furrow. The variables are:

- `F0`: free Fog concentration available in the propagation zone. The primordium is its source; for a reduced model it is approximately spatially uniform there, as observed.
- `r_i`: normalized surface-active Fog/Smog state. This is a latent state informed by slow-pool fraction and Smog brightness; it is not equated with either fluorescence readout.
- `c_i`: local vitelline-membrane contact/deformation state.
- `I_i`: integrin/focal-adhesion activity.
- `m_i`: MyoII activity.
- `p_i`: mechanical input arriving from the neighboring invaginating row.
- `a_i`: effective adhesion load.

A compact phenomenological system is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_r\dot r_i &= \mathcal R(F_0,k_e(G),\zeta_i)-r_i,\\
\tau_I\dot I_i &= k_I c_i(I_0+\chi m_i)(1-I_i)-\delta_I I_i,\\
\tau_m\dot m_i &= -m_i+m_0 + A\,G_i\,\frac{r_i}{K_r+r_i}\,\frac{I_i}{K_I+I_i}
                  +B\,\frac{m_i^n}{K_m^n+m_i^n}+C p_i,\\
\tau_a\dot a_i &= k_a I_i+\chi_a m_i-a_i,\\
 p_{i+1} &= \mathcal P(m_i,c_i,a_i),\qquad
 v \simeq \frac{\text{row width}}{\tau_{\rm contact}+\tau_{\rm recruit}+\tau_{\rm detach}(a,m)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Here `G_i` is propagation-zone Gα availability; `k_e(G)` is an effective Gprk2-dependent removal/endocytosis rate; `ζ_i` is cell-local exposure/contact history; and `R` is an unspecified receptor/surface-pool recruitment law. `I_i/(K_I+I_i)` gates output but is not put into the Smog-clustering equation, matching the finding that integrin loss does not remove Smog brightness. The Hill term in `m_i` is a coarse-grained positive feedback, not a claim of direct MyoII autocatalysis. The contact and detachment terms encode the established tissue cycle. Higher `a_i` can increase detachment time over the reported operating range, so more recruitment need not mean a faster wave.

This is the most that can be source-constrained without inventing molecular detail. The papers fix several *signs and dependencies*—`F0` and `G_i` are needed for PZ output, reduced `k_e` raises receptor-associated activity, integrin gates signaling, and adhesion can slow detachment—but they do not specify `R`, `P`, Hill coefficients, the feedback gain, or the mechanical force law. The term `ζ_i` is deliberately explicit: source authors describe cell-frame temporal maturation, but its molecular implementation remains open.

## 3. A sharp threshold exists inside one assumed recruitment module

Freeze the slowly varying cell/contact state and the neighbor input into a dimensionless drive `h≥0`; scale MyoII to `K_m`; and retain the simplest saturating positive feedback:

\[
\tau\dot y=h+\beta\frac{y^2}{1+y^2}-y,\qquad y\ge0.
\]

A saddle-node occurs where both the right-hand side and its derivative vanish. Eliminating `h` gives the exact fold curve

\[
\boxed{\quad \beta=\frac{(1+y^2)^2}{2y},\qquad h_{\rm fold}=\frac{y(1-y^2)}{2}.\quad}
\]

The feedback must exceed `min_y (1+y²)²/(2y)=8√3/9≈1.54` for this module to have a bistable region. For the positive-drive fold at which the low-recruitment state disappears, choose the smaller root `y_-(β)<1/√3`; its local ignition drive is `h_c(β)=y_-(1-y_-²)/2`. This is a sharp conditional threshold for *this ODE*, not for the embryo. A row must also receive a mechanical input sufficient to cross the intervening unstable state, and detachment must still occur. Neither `h`, `β`, the unstable-state crossing, nor detachment loads are calibrated by the papers, so the fold cannot be evaluated or equated with a measured Fog dose or a “minimum propagation range.”

This distinction matters: a local receptor/MyoII threshold and a traveling-wave invasion threshold are not the same object. Deriving the latter needs the coupling kernel `P`, the contact geometry dynamics, and the detachment law. None is quantitatively identified here.

## 4. Exact profile alias: transport versus contact-age kinetics

Let the reported moving-front MyoII profile in condition `j` be

\[
P_j(d)=b_j+(A_j-b_j)e^{-d/\lambda_j},\qquad d\ge0,
\]

where `d` is distance anterior to the front. Suppose each cell first contacts the VM a lead time `Δ` before the furrow reaches it, so its local contact age at a snapshot is `a=Δ-d/v_j`. Define a cell-local response waveform

\[
M_j(a)=b_j+(A_j-b_j)e^{-(\Delta-a)/\tau_j},\qquad \tau_j=\lambda_j/v_j.
\]

Then `M_j(Δ-d/v_j)=P_j(d)` exactly on the observed contact-age window. The waveform can be implemented by a local, self-amplifying kinetic segment `\dot M=(M-b_j)/\tau_j`, capped/reset at detachment; smoothing the cap leaves the observed exponential segment unchanged. There is no lateral transport term in this construction. Uniform source-derived `F0` can still be required as a permissive GPCR input, Gprk2 can change local surface residence/kinetics, and integrins can gate the conversion to MyoII. The slow Fog/Smog readouts can likewise be generated by age-dependent local binding/clustering at each cell surface; the published FCS data do not track the same molecules along tissue material trajectories.

A second compatible model is a *Fog-gated mechanical relay*: deformation/contact supplies `p_i`; uniform diffused Fog supplies the permissive GPCR drive; integrin–MyoII feedback recruits and anchors the next row; increased adhesion slows detachment. This honors the primordium-only diffusible-versus-tethered Fog and PZ-Gα controls, unlike a Fog-free relay. It does not require the measured `D_slow` to set the MyoII length.

Consequently, for any measured `λ_j` the contact-age model sets `τ_j=λ_j/v_j`. A passive moving-pool model instead predicts

\[
\lambda_j=\frac{2D_j}{v_j+\sqrt{v_j^2+4D_j k_j}}.
\]

Both can match the same profile. The latter predicts the bound `v_j λ_j≤D_j`, but the published FCS `D_slow` is not shown to be the material-frame mobility of the active signaling state, and the reported inputs do not provide same-cell, same-condition `D_j`, `v_j`, and clearance `k_j` tied to the MyoII fit. Even if those quantities were measured, satisfying the bound would be necessary for the passive model, not sufficient to distinguish it from the age/relay models.

## 5. No-go conclusion on a source-derived propagation threshold

The observations identify causal requirements and directional effects, but not a unique hidden-state model. A formal counterexample is the pair above: choose `R`, `P`, and the age waveform in the nonlinear model to reproduce, at every published condition, (i) the MyoII amplitudes and exponential lengths, (ii) the Gprk2-associated increase/flattening of the slow-pool readout and Smog clustering, (iii) the reduction of MyoII when integrins are absent despite preserved clustering, (iv) the primordium-only Gα/Fog rescue outcomes, and (v) the slower wave when recruitment/adhesion expands. Model T attributes `λ` partly to lateral movement and clearance of an active surface pool; model C attributes the same profile to contact-age kinetics and geometry, with uniform free Fog. No reported experiment independently varies slow-pool mobility while holding receptor occupancy, Fog dose, contact history, and adhesion fixed, so both have the same source-constrained fit and different untested predictions.

Likewise, the same finite set of tested genotypes is compatible with any chosen ignition threshold lying between unmeasured molecular states: the reported readouts are not calibrated values of `h`, and no continuous dose series locates the transition. Thus no sharp numerical threshold in `F0`, `D_slow`, endocytic rate, MyoII length, or contact range can be deduced. The only source-supported threshold-like statements are categorical necessities in the tested regimes: PZ Gα/Fog output is needed for sustained propagation; integrin function is needed for the Gprk2-dependent expanded gradient; and excess signal can slow the wave. These are causal constraints, not a bifurcation law.

This no-go does not undermine the 2026 paper’s main mechanism. It blocks a stronger extension: treating a one-dimensional transport formula or a fitted exponential decay as an identified threshold law for tissue propagation.

## 6. Discriminating experiment and falsifiers

A useful experiment must break the profile alias rather than collect more spatial averages.

1. Track the *same cells* from first VM contact through MyoII rise and detachment, while recording contact geometry/βPS, Smog brightness, Fog slow fraction, and front-relative position. Fit a local age-response model using a subset of cells, then predict held-out cells and a held-out front speed. If `M(d)` collapses to a common function of contact age and the measured slow-pool mobility/clearance adds no out-of-sample prediction, the transport contribution to the MyoII range is unnecessary.
2. Independently perturb surface-pool mobility or ligand/receptor residence while matching extracellular Fog, measured Smog activation, initial invagination, and contact geometry. A transport model predicts a quantitative profile-length change through the measured `D,k`; a contact-age model predicts no such change once the local state trajectory is matched. Gprk2 RNAi alone is not a clean test because it changes clustering, surface residence, contact range, MyoII, and wave speed together.
3. Measure per-cell detachment timing under graded Fog/Gprk2 input with contact geometry controlled or stratified. This is needed to distinguish the local fold above from the wave threshold and to locate any nonmonotone range–speed frontier.

Reject the reduced passive-pool explanation if matched measurements violate `v_rel λ≤D_eff` under its no-local-gain assumptions. Reject the contact-age explanation if a mobility-only perturbation changes held-out MyoII profiles at fixed cell contact-age trajectories and matched receptor occupancy. Reject a Fog-gated relay if a mechanically matched PZ cell fails or succeeds solely according to Fog–GPCR input contrary to the measured response. These falsifiers target different model components; no single existing endpoint resolves all three.

## Assumptions and scope

- `F0` is coarse-grained as uniform in the propagation zone; the primordium remains a localized Fog source. This matches the reported extracellular distribution and does not remove the need for Fog diffusion from its source.
- `r_i` is latent. FCS slow-pool fraction and Smog brightness are separate proxies with different caveats, not direct calibrated measurements of bound active receptor.
- Hill feedback and local saturating kinetics are candidate closures, not fitted biology.
- Mechanical stress, contact, adhesion, detachment, and front speed need a cell/material-frame measurement. They are not represented by a single diffusion coefficient.
- The exact response-alias construction is about observability: it demonstrates that moving-frame profiles alone do not identify the cause. The source papers also report cell time courses, which support temporal maturation but still do not provide the orthogonal mobility/contact controls above.
- No novelty claim is made for receptor/endocytosis reaction kinetics, bistable recruitment, contact-age waves, or mechanical relays. The identified scientific boundary is the absence of a source-validated threshold linking Fog-slow mobility/clearance, integrin/contact state, and propagation speed.
