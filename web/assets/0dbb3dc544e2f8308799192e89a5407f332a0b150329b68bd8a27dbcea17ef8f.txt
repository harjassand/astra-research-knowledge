DECISIVE RESULT

bistochastic quantum channel Psi satisfying the full Hermitian form order
I-Psi<=C(I-Phi), every state rho obeys

The proof includes singular states, negative Phi eigenvalues and D=0/b=0.
Psi need not be self-adjoint, HS-positive or EB for the analytic transfer.

Invoking the supplied universal C4 theorem for a legal bistochastic,

MECHANISM
The legal Poisson flow Q_t=exp[-t(I-Phi)] keeps rho within distance t b.
Reversibility and traciality give
  -d/dt D(Q_t rho||I/d)>=4 E_Phi(sqrt(d Q_t rho)).
The entropy budget guarantees one s in [0,T] with energy <=D/(4T).
CP Schwarz/Jensen followed by canonical purification gives
  T(rho_s,Psi rho_s)^2<=2 E_Psi(sqrt(d rho_s))<=CD/(2T).
Trace contraction of the SAME fixed Psi then gives
  e<=2Tb+sqrt(CD/(2T)).
The unconstrained choice T=(CD/(32b^2))^(1/3) yields the exact cubic
constant 27C/4. T and s are comparison parameters; Psi never changes.

CHECKS AND FALSIFICATION INTERFACE
The state-eigenbasis p_ij=Tr[P_i Phi(P_j)] are entrywise nonnegative,
symmetric and bistochastic even when Phi has negative HS spectrum.
Their use needs no invariant diagonal algebra. The square-root step uses
actual CP/unital Psi and preserves only SCALAR trace order after positive
multiplication; it does not assume that noncommuting products are PSD.
The trace-distance factor is derived directly from canonical vectorizations,
so neither squared/root fidelity notation nor a transpose convention is
left implicit. Regularization mixes with I/d and keeps the same Phi/Psi,
with exact scalings b_delta=(1-delta)b, e_delta=(1-delta)e.

An exact d=4 classical four-cycle channel has an explicit CPTP joint
broadcaster and a genuine -1 HS eigenvalue. A 256-vector pure canonical
phase ensemble supplies an actual full-domain EB comparator with C=1.
Its full matrix-unit action and form-order certificate pass rational replay.
This is a finite audit, not a proof of the general theorem.

An exact nonphysical C4 form comparator Gamma=4*dephasing-3*identity
violates the cubic conclusion on a strictly positive four-dimensional rho,
even though Gamma(rho) itself is positive. It is not globally positive
and hence NOT a legal counterexample. It isolates why arbitrary HS order
cannot replace the actual quantum-channel assumptions. Every legal
form-comparable bistochastic comparator is covered by the theorem; a
bad legal comparator cannot furnish an obstruction within this scope.
