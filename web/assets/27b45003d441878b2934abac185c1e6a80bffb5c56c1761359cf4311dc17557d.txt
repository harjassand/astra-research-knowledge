1. INTERFACE THAT THE PROOF ACTUALLY COVERS

The carrier result assumes known/supplied spectrum
p=(a+c/(2 sqrt(n)),a-c/(2 sqrt(n)),b), a in (1/3,1/2), b=1-2a, and a supplied reference
plane span(e1,e2). The unknown parameters are g in SU(2) and bounded
z in C^2. It assumes k=lambda1-lambda2 <= C sqrt(n log n) and
m=lambda2-lambda3 in [m0 n,m1 n], with m0>0. It gives two parameter-independent
channels, with respect to g,z, between each full carrier orbit and a finite
model. The actual spectrum is part of the supplied statistical family; no
spectrum acquisition is proved. The local-iid extension is also proved, but
only with the same reference plane and spectrum supplied. No global-plane
acquisition or arbitrary-U(3) theorem follows.

2. DYSON ORDERING AND POLAR GRAM

Write a_i=partial_yi, a_i^dag=y_i, N=sum_i y_i partial_yi, and
S_ij=lambda2 delta_ij+x_i partial_xj. With

  D_ij=S_ij-a_j^dag a_i,  D_33=lambda3+N,
  D_3i=a_i^dag,  D_i3=sum_j(S_ij-lambda3 delta_ij)a_j-N a_i,

the ordering of N to the left of a_i is material. Directly,

  [D_i3,y_j]=S_ij-y_j a_i-delta_ij(lambda3+N)
           =D_ij-delta_ij D_33.

Since N a_i=(N-1)a_i on a degree-N input, substitution of S_ij gives

  D_i3=(m-N+1) partial_yi+x_i Delta,
  Delta=sum_j partial_xj partial_yj.

The remaining cross-commutators follow from the canonical bosons and
[S_ij,S_lq]=delta_jl S_iq-delta_qi S_lj; the trace is
S_11+S_22-N+lambda3+N=n. The negative roots commute, so T defined by
T(y1^u y2^v chi)=pi(E31)^u pi(E32)^v chi intertwines them. The displayed
commutator and induction through the negative roots give the positive-root
intertwining. The N=0 U(2) module is the top branching module; the U(3)
highest vector generates V_lambda, so T is onto.

On the multiplicity-one component (s,N), t=N-s, the highest vector
f_(s,N)=x1^(k-s)y2^t(x1y1+x2y2)^s obeys

  Delta f_(s,N)=s(k+N-s+1)x1^(k-s)y2^t(x1y1+x2y2)^(s-1),
  partial_y1 f_(s,N)=s f_(s-1,N-1),
  D_13 f_(s,N)=s(m+k-s+2) f_(s-1,N-1).

For the s-edge, standard Bargmann adjointness gives
<y1 f_(s-1,N-1),f_(s,N)> = s ||f_(s-1,N-1)||^2. Physical adjointness
therefore gives gamma_(s,N)/gamma_(s-1,N-1)=m+k-s+2. For s=0,
||y2 f_(0,N-1)||^2=N||f_(0,N-1)||^2, while D_23 contributes
N(m-N+1); hence gamma_(0,N)/gamma_(0,N-1)=m-N+1. With gamma_(0,0)=1,
these recurrences give exactly

  gamma_(s,N)=(m+k+1)_falling(s) (m)_falling(N-s).

This is positive exactly for s<=k and N-s<=m. The multiplicity-free
U(2) decomposition makes T^dag T scalar on each component. Its support
therefore has dimension dim V_lambda, and the polar partial isometry is a
unitary V from P_m(H_k tensor F) onto V_lambda. This verifies the claimed
polar W=V^dag, including the exact t=m physical boundary.

3. EXACT ROOT COEFFICIENTS AND FINITE OPERATOR ERROR

From T a_i^dag=pi(E_3i)T and T=V G^(1/2),
W pi(E_3i)W^dag=G^(1/2)a_i^dag G^(-1/2). Coupling the dual fundamental
changes the spin label from s to s or s+1. The adjacent Gram ratios are

  gamma_(s,N+1)/gamma_(s,N)=m-t,
  gamma_(s+1,N+1)/gamma_(s,N)=m+k+1-s.

Thus (4.2) follows with no extra factorial or normalization. The first
channel vanishes when t=m; the second keeps t fixed. For each channel the
source and target components are orthogonal across s. Each compressed
creation block has norm at most sqrt(N+1), so the two-channel triangle bound
in (4.3) is valid. The claim that both channels are present is not needed at
edge components: missing projectors are zero.

For |x|<=1/2, the binomial tail satisfies
|sqrt(1+x)-T_R(x)|<=|x|^(R+1) for R>=1: the binomial coefficients decrease
after the linear term and their remaining geometric majorant is at most
|x|^(R+1). Also |T_R(x)|<=sqrt(3/2)+1/4<2. Under k+K+1<=m/2, both
arguments -t/m and (k+1-s)/m have magnitude at most
rho=(k+K+1)/m. On N<K, each of the two root-channel coefficient errors
is at most sqrt(m)rho^(R+1); the compressed creation norm is at most
sqrt(K+1). Summing two modes costs at most sqrt(2)z0, and adding the
adjoint/raising-lowering terms costs at most another factor 2. The resulting
constant 8 in (5.4) is conservative.

The first omitted order is therefore

  G_R=O(sqrt(m/n) sqrt(K+1) ((k+K+1)/m)^(R+1)).

For m=Theta(n), k=O(sqrt(n log n)), K=O(log n), this is
O(n^(-(R+1)/2) log^((R+2)/2)n). In particular R=1 gives
O(log^(3/2)n/n), which is o(n^(-1/2)). The retained linear term distinguishes
the two channels and preserves the order-k/m internal-spin correction.

Both exact and corrected generators are number-tridiagonal. Since
m+k=lambda1-lambda3<=n, (4.3) gives a uniform edge bound after division by
sqrt(n); |T_R|<2 gives the same for the corrected generator. A=8(1+z0)
dominates these edge norms, including all n>=1.

4. WHOLE-STATE MOMENT AND DUHAMEL CHECK

For a positive state with p_N=Tr(P_N rho), positivity of its two-number
block implies
||P_N rho P_(N+1)||_1<=sqrt(p_N p_(N+1)). If
||P_(N+1)H_*P_N||<=A sqrt(N+1), differentiating
M_gamma=Tr(e^(gamma N)rho_t) gives

  |dot M_gamma| <= 2A(e^gamma-1) sum_N sqrt(N+1)e^(gamma N)
                                   sqrt(p_N p_(N+1)).

Set a_N=e^(gamma N/2)sqrt(p_N). The coefficient becomes
2A(e^gamma-1)e^(-gamma/2)=4A sinh(gamma/2). Applying 2ab<=a^2+b^2
and (sqrt(N+1)+sqrt(N))/2<=sqrt(N+1) proves (7.1), including its
normalization constant.

For gamma(t)=gamma0-kappa t, gamma0=0.5 log(1/qbar), kappa=gamma0/2,
the derivative of the weight adds -kappa N. With
C_gamma=4A sinh(gamma0/2), for x=sqrt(N+1),

  C_gamma sqrt(N+1)-kappa N
    =kappa+C_gamma x-kappa x^2
    <=kappa+C_gamma^2/(4kappa)=L.

Gronwall gives (7.2). The two geometric modes have
Tr(e^(gamma0 N) Omega_g)<= (1-sqrt(qbar))^(-2); joint g conjugation
preserves this moment. If delta_K<=1/2, normalization after Q_K truncation
increases the initial moment by at most 2. Since gamma(t)>=gamma0/2 on
[0,1], the evolved corrected state has
Tr(e^(gamma0 N/2)rho_c(t))<=M_* and therefore
p_K(t)<=M_* exp(-gamma0 K/2). This remains true though the cut state has a
component at N=K; no unsupported buffer assumption is needed.

Duhamel on the canonical purification of Omega_(g,K) splits H-H_c into
the interior Q_K block, bounded by G_R, and the sole outgoing edge K to K+1.
The latter acts only on P_K and is bounded in Hilbert-Schmidt norm by
A sqrt(K+1) sqrt(p_K(t)). Integration over unit time gives exactly

  T(e^(-iH)Omega_(g,K)e^(iH), Sigma_(g,z,K))
    <= G_R + A sqrt(K+1) sqrt(M_*) exp(-gamma0 K/4).

The half trace distance of the purifications is no larger than their vector
distance, and partial trace contracts it. This verifies a whole-density
bound, not merely a low-number operator estimate. Adding the two commuting
thermal truncation defects by unitary invariance/triangle inequality yields
(7.5). With K=B_alpha log n and B_alpha>4(alpha+2)/gamma0, the thermal and
boundary terms are o(n^-alpha); choosing fixed R with (R+1)/2>alpha makes
G_R=o(n^-alpha).

5. CHANNELS, LABELS, AND LOCAL-IID TAIL

The compression map on the embedded carrier is
Y -> Q_K Y Q_K + Tr[(I-Q_K)Y] sigma0. It is CPTP, fixes every model state
supported on Q_K, and does not depend on g,z. Composing with W gives (8.1).
On Q_K, W^dag is an isometry into V_lambda, so (8.2) is TP. For a map on
the full product, use W^dag P_m(.)P_m W plus a fixed fallback on I-P_m.
Contractivity on the two sides of (7.5) gives forward and reverse errors
<=e_(lambda,K,R); composing them gives <=2e. The model state is fixed by
the intermediate product-space cutoff map. (The source's phrase “fixed by
the cutoff channel” can be made domain-clear by naming this intermediate map.)

For the separate local-iid statement, exact Schur-Weyl decomposition gives
P_lambda=f_lambda s_lambda(p), with a maximally mixed, parameter-independent
multiplicity register. For sorted p, every weight mu of V_lambda obeys
p^mu<=p^lambda, hence s_lambda(p)<=dim(V_lambda)p^lambda. Standard tableaux
inject into row tabloids, so f_lambda<=n!/prod_i lambda_i!<=exp(nH(lambda/n));
Weyl's formula gives dim(V_lambda)<=(n+1)^3. Thus

  P_lambda <= (n+1)^3 exp[-n D(lambda/n || p)].

If coordinate i differs by delta, relative entropy decreases under merging
the other coordinates to binary relative entropy. Its second derivative is
1/[x(1-x)]>=4, giving D>=2 delta^2. A union bound over at most (n+1)^2
partitions and delta_n=sqrt((alpha+7)log(n+1)/(2n)) gives bad mass at most
(n+1)^(-alpha-2). On the good window, k=O_alpha(sqrt(n log n)) and
m=Theta(n), so the carrier theorem applies. There are
O_alpha(n log n) good labels. Each branch is exactly
(k+1)(K+1)(K+2)/2=O_alpha(sqrt(n)log^(5/2)n), giving total direct-sum
dimension O_alpha(n^(3/2)log^(7/2)n). The encoder uses the Schur transform,
sector compression, and a bad-sector flag; the decoder regenerates the
parameter-independent multiplicity state and inverse Schur map. The bad
flag costs its probability in reverse trace distance. No g,z estimate is
used. These claims depend on the reference plane and spectrum being supplied.

6. LIMITS AND REMAINING COSTS

The proof is a concrete partial local law, not an acquired-geometry theorem.
It covers fixed qutrit dimension, a supplied two-plane, supplied spectral
envelope/spectrum, k up to O(sqrt(n log n)), m=Theta(n), bounded local
transverse z, and whole-state half trace distance. It does not prove unknown
plane or unknown spectrum acquisition, a growing-dimension entropy law,
optimal quantum or classical dimension, a lower bound, or a general inverse
problem theorem. It charges the finite quantum branch and all Schur labels;
numeric precision, conditioning, gate synthesis/runtime, error from finite
implementation, and fresh output-qutrit preparation remain outside the
theorem, as the source document itself states.

No finite fixture or agent agreement upgrades this audit to external
validation or novelty clearance. The Sol evidence script is scoped to exact
small carrier/root and remainder fixtures; it is consistent with, but not a
substitute for, the analytic checks above.
