log-concavity AND sup_{|u|=1} E exp(<u,X>^2/a^2)<=2. Conclusion is LSI with
entropy coefficient C a^2, hence Poincare with coefficient O(a^2).
101, build/manuscript/introduction.tex, intro:entropy, states
h(mu)>=n+(1/2)log det Cov(mu). Its equality laws are affine products of

Neither covariance normalization nor the entropy bound supplies a finite
linear psi2 parameter for arbitrary log-concave laws.

2. Exact compact counterexample to an entropy-to-psi2 bridge

Let X be uniform on an isotropic regular n-simplex. A standardized marginal
in a vertex direction is Z=sqrt((n+2)/n)*((n+1)B-1), where B~Beta(1,n).
E Z=0 and Var Z=1. The event B>=1/2 has probability 2^{-n}; on this event
Z>=sqrt((n+2)/n)*(n-1)/2. Therefore E exp(Z^2/a^2)<=2 requires

 a^2 >= (n+2)(n-1)^2 / [4n(n+1)log 2],       n>=2.

This is asymptotic to n/(4log2). On the other hand, the simplex formula is

 L_n=(n!)^(1/n)/[(n+1)^((n+1)/(2n))*sqrt(n+2)],

so its entropy excess above 101's minimum is

 h(X)-n = ((n+1)log(n+1)+n log(n+2))/2 - log(n!) - n
        -> 3/2-(1/2)log(2pi) = 0.5810614667... .

Thus bounded entropy excess, not merely bounded excess per dimension,
cannot supply dimension-free a in 093. The product of centered Exp(1)
laws is an even simpler noncompact example: entropy excess exactly zero,
Cov=I, but E exp(X_1^2/a^2)=infinity for every finite a. Gaussian
convolution preserves the exponential tail and does not repair this.
Quadratic tilting repairs psi2 only at a scale depending on the added
curvature; letting that curvature go to zero loses the uniform constant.

3. What the entropy inequality actually supplies under localization

For smooth compact initial laws, take covariance-adaptive Eldan
localization with d p_t(x)=p_t(x)<A_t^{-1/2}(x-a_t),dB_t>,
dA_t=sum_i H_{i,t}dB_{i,t}-A_t dt, where
H_i=kappa_3(mu_t)(.,.,A_t^{-1/2}e_i). Put
S_i=A_t^{-1/2}H_i A_t^{-1/2}, and whiten mu_t to nu_t.

Ito gives drift h(mu_t)=-n/2 and
drift log det A_t=-n-(1/2)sum_i tr S_i^2.
Consequently D_t=(n/2)log(2pi e)+(1/2)log det A_t-h(mu_t) satisfies

 drift D_t=-(1/4)sum_i tr S_i^2=-(1/4)tr R_3(nu_t),

where R_m(nu) is the Gram matrix of the cumulant slice
u -> kappa_m(nu)(u,.,...,.) in Hilbert--Schmidt norm.
Gaussian maximum entropy gives D_t>=0. For isotropic mu_0, 101 gives
D_0<=(n/2)log(2pi/e). Thus (with routine localization/integrability
justification, or stopped first)

 integral_0^T E tr R_3(nu_t) dt <=4D_0<=2n log(2pi/e).

This is an integrated trace bound of order n. The missing bridge to the
cumulant route is dimension-free, pointwise operator control
R_3(nu)<=B I for every isotropic log-concave nu. An entropy-based proof
must convert extensive trace budget to that stronger uniform statement;
the two released theorem statements do not make that conversion.

4. Current primary KLS claims and load-bearing route

Sources checked live on 7 October 2026:
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05474v1
  Bizeul--Klartag--Lehec, posted 4 October 2026, Theorem 1.1 claims full
  dimension-free Cp. The precise route is Sections 3--7, not a transfer
  from a finite psi2 bound.
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01447v2
  Song--Zhang current title is An O(1) Bound for the KLS Constant.
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24164v1
  Letwin, Theorem 1.2 states Var(X^T M X)<=8||M||HS^2 for centered
  isotropic log-concave X and symmetric M.

BKL Lemma 4.2 deduces R_3<=8I from Letwin using Cauchy--Schwarz against
quadratic forms. This algebra is valid conditional on Letwin's result.
Its Sections 4--6 then use inverse-covariance cumulant energies and the
identity that each partition contribution satisfies
||P_a||_t^2 <= b_{m-a+1} E_{a+1}(t).
After time integration and the triangle inequality, the binomial term
C(m-1,a-1)*sqrt(b_a b_{m-a+1}) equals sqrt(b_m) for
b_m=K^(m-1)((m-1)!)^2. The factorial bookkeeping checks directly.

Suspension Proposition 7.1 also checks algebraically. For strongly convex
regular mu with curvature a>0 and a smooth f orthogonal to affine tests,
use N independent copies and S=(N^{-1/2}sum f(X_i)+Laplace_beta)/sigma.
The joint potential is the max of sum(V +/- beta f/sqrtN) +/- beta sigma S.
It is convex when N>=(beta sup||Hess f||/a)^2. Its covariance is identity.
One S index and d indices from a common copy contribute
(d!)/(sigma sqrtN) times the tilt-average Taylor tensor T_d f.
Summing its squared norm over N copies cancels N exactly. This proves
||T_d f||<=sqrt(b)/d! on affine-orthogonal f, conditional on universal
R_{d+1}<=bI; projecting off affine functions costs at most sqrt2.
The Song--Zhang/BKL criterion is then ||T_d||<=R^d for all d implies
Cp<=C R^2. No sampling/oracle acquisition is supplied by these analytic
statements. Suspension increases dimension to Nn+1 and needs an already
dimension-uniform cumulant hypothesis.

One minor exposition issue: BKL Lemma 5.2 says time-integrability allows
E E_m(T)->0. It guarantees only a sequence T_j->infinity with that limit.
Choosing this sequence repairs the step; it is not a counterexample.
No explicit counterexample to the actual homogeneous quadratic base was
found here, and no conclusion about full proof correctness is claimed.
Further audit of Letwin's main argument: Theorem 2.5 takes the moment-map
Hessian H and uses EH=I. For B>=0, its differentiated Monge--Ampere
identity gives Q=E tr(BHBH)>=2 derivative energy; Brascamp--Lieb gives
Q-tr(B^2)<=derivative energy. Combining them proves Q<=2tr(B^2).
The pointwise normalization H=I and diagonalization of B compare the
two cubic-derivative contractions through sum (b_i-b_j)^2 phi_ijk^2/2,
so the claimed noncommuting cancellation checks algebraically. The
extension to indefinite B reduces to |B| by expanding in its eigenbasis.
The final transformation Z=|M|^(1/2)X aligns the transported Stein-kernel
Hilbert--Schmidt norm with this estimate; the H^{-1} inequality applies
because the homogeneous quadratic gradient has mean zero. Appendix A.4
uses compact cutoffs of the moment-map potential and A.5 justifies
integrating a bounded Lq+cq>=0 test. Their displayed cutoff calculations
were read and showed no immediate algebraic fault. These checks remain
conditional on the cited moment-measure regularity, Brascamp--Lieb and
Barthe--Klartag H^{-1} results; not all those originals were audited here.

5. Guard against misidentifying a false estimate

For X=Exp(1)-1, E X^2=1, E X^3=2, E X^4=9.
For f=X^2+bX, Var f=8+4b+b^2 and E(f')^2=4+b^2.
At b=sqrt5-1, the ratio is (3+sqrt5)/2>2.
This refutes an extension of the homogeneous quadratic coefficient 2 to
ALL degree-two polynomials. It does NOT refute Letwin Theorem 1.2,
BKL Lemma 4.2, or the current Song--Zhang initial bound (whose constants
are much larger). Any secondary-review objection must preserve this
distinction before being used to reject the new KLS claims.

with bounded absolute entropy excess and psi2^2=Omega(n).
