GROUPS/TOPLOGY: QUADRATIC BRUNNIAN WORD WITH AN EXPLICIT LINEAR GAP
7 October 2026. Internally proved transfer lemma; priority unclaimed.

Supplied interface.
A finite presentation/triangle complex for G supplies nonidentity rectangle
words h_0,...,h_(M-1), each of length <=ell in based edge generators, and
0<delta_0<=1, 0<lambda_0<1, such that NO permutation assignment of ANY
degree has all triangle defects <=delta_0 and all h_i fixed fractions
<=lambda_0. This is precisely the finite rank interface of N06/N27/N65,
when its incidence/geometry/rank premises have been proved.

Pass to G*<t>. This adds one free generator and no relator. If G is
torsion-free hyperbolic with a finite locally CAT(-1) 2-complex, wedging one
circle preserves these properties; vertex links just acquire disconnected
directions. No claim that the original group already has a particular
free factor is needed.

Construction.
Let N be the least power of two >=M, so N<2M except the harmless M=1 case.
Leaf j uses h_(j mod M). For a dyadic interval [a,a+n), define words
relative to its left endpoint:
  V_(a,1)=h_(a mod M),
  V_(a,2k)=[ V_(a,k), t^k V_(a+k,k) t^-k ],
with [A,B]=A B A^-1 B^-1. Put W=V_(0,N).
Every original h_i occurs at most twice among leaves, and each leaf is
repeated N times in the expanded commutator tree. Thus each h_i has at
most 2N occurrences as a whole input word, counting signs.

Nontriviality and normal closures.
The kernel of the retraction G*<t> -> <t> is the free product of conjugate
copies t^j G t^-j. The left and right halves at each node are supported
on disjoint sets of these copies. Nonzero children therefore have a
nonzero commutator by free-product normal form. Every leaf is nonzero, so
W!=1. Deleting any original h_i kills at least one leaf and then every
commutator on its path to the root; W becomes 1. Hence W is in the normal
closure of EVERY original h_i. All maps from G*<t> into sofic groups kill
W, because their restrictions cannot keep every original rectangle
nonidentity. This last assertion also follows from the quantitative law
below without choosing an unidentified universal-kernel rectangle.

Length and compiler cost.
There are N occurrences of each leaf word in the raw expansion. The shifts
contribute exactly 2N^2-2N letters t^{+/-1}. Therefore
  |W| <= N sum_j |h_(j mod M)| +2N^2-2N
       <=(ell+2)N^2-2N <4(ell+2)M^2.
A straight-line program needs O(N) commutator/conjugation nodes after the
input words are acquired; the powers t^(2^j) need only O(log N) shared
squaring nodes. Expanded emission still costs Theta(|W|), and reading the
M original words costs their total encoded size. This does not acquire
the astronomical incidence/presentation input.

Hamming estimate.
Evaluate W at arbitrary permutations P_i=h_i(sigma), T=t(sigma). Replacing
all occurrences of one P_i by identity makes the result identity. Each
replacement costs d_H(P_i,1), because the metric is bi-invariant. Thus
  a:=d_H(W(sigma),1) <= 2N d_H(P_i,1) for every i.
For k diagonal copies on D^k points, every h_i has fixed fraction
  (1-d_H(P_i,1))^k <= exp(-k a/(2N)),
and every triangle defect grows by at most a factor k. These statements
are exact tensor/Cartesian-action identities, with no independent-action
or exact-homomorphism premise on the assigned generators.

Explicit approximate law.
Choose an integer L>=log(1/lambda_0); for rational lambda_0, the least
integer L with 2^-L<=lambda_0 is a conservative exact choice. For any
0<tau<=1, let k=ceil(2N L/tau). Triangle defects <=delta_0/k force
  d_H(W(sigma),1)<tau
at EVERY original degree D: otherwise the diagonal assignment would meet
the forbidden source interface.

More strongly, for triangle defect delta<=delta_0/2 take
k=floor(delta_0/delta). If a>=2N L/k, the same contradiction follows.
Since k>=delta_0/(2delta),
  d_H(W(sigma),1) <= C_W delta,
  C_W=max{2/delta_0,4N L/delta_0}.
The first term handles delta>delta_0/2. Exact delta=0 gives W=1 by letting
k increase; no logarithmic or dimension-dependent error is hidden.

Source-specific conditional ledger.
For the stronger N65 parameters q=8796093022237, r=19000,
delta_0=lambda_0=1/(100 r q), L=64 suffices. Taking ALL ordered
nondegenerate rectangles gives
  M=binom((q-3)/2+5,5) r^2 (r-1)^2.
The conditional exact arithmetic ledger groups_word_budget.json reports:
M and N have 79 decimal digits; ell=4 gives a word-length upper bound

Only these count formulas were evaluated. No giant graph, permutation
assignment, presentation, or actual common-kernel expression was emitted.

Simple envelope transfer.
If an embedding into a finitely presented simple S is supplied, with each
source triangle derivable from the relators of S using <=T conjugate
relator factors, and each S generator expressible as <=A conjugates of
the embedded W plus <=B relator factors, then arbitrary S assignments obey
  max_generator movement <=(A C_W T+B) max_relator defect.
All three finite derivation ledgers must be acquired and charged. Their
existence follows from the embedding and simplicity; no small values or
bounded compiler costs follow from those facts.
