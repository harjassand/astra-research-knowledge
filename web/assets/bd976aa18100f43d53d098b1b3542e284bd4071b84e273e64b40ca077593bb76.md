## Exact fields and characteristic condition

Let F be ANY field of characteristic p, let Omega be ANY primitive M-th root in an extension of F, and assume p does not divide M. Take q,eta,u,v fresh and algebraically independent over F(Omega), and set

    K=F(Omega)(q,eta).

The coefficients of every pencil lie in the central scalar field K; q and eta are central coefficients of the reversal matrices. Only u and v are the fresh generators used in the division lift. These roles must not be interchanged with pre-existing elements of F carrying the same variable names.

On the basis e_0,...,e_(M-1), let D e_j=Omega^j e_j and S e_j=e_(j+1), with cyclic subscripts. Then DS=Omega SD, D^M=S^M=I, and D,S are invertible.

## 1. Fourier lift and its only raw denominator

For any R in Mat_M(K), the M^2 matrices D^a S^b, 0<=a,b<M, form a K-basis of Mat_M(K). Indeed, the (j,j-b) entry of D^a S^b is Omega^(aj), and

    sum_(j=0)^(M-1) Omega^((a-a')j)=M if a=a', and 0 otherwise.

The zero case follows from the geometric-series identity because Omega^(a-a')!=1; the nonzero case is M!=0 in K. Therefore

    R=sum_(a,b) c_(a,b) D^a S^b,
    c_(a,b)=M^(-1) sum_(j=0)^(M-1) Omega^(-aj) R_(j,j-b).

No distinctness of integer residues modulo p is used: distinctness comes from the primitive M-th root. The Fourier formula needs only p not dividing M. Define

    R^*=sum_(a,b) c_(a,b) u^a v^b D^a S^b.

It is polynomial in u,v and specializes exactly to R at u=v=1. If R has polynomial q,eta entries, R^* has polynomial q,eta,u,v entries. Its sole coefficient denominator introduced by this formula is the scalar M, a unit in F_p. Negative powers of Omega are units and can be replaced by nonnegative powers using Omega^M=1; no parameter denominator occurs.

## 2. Ordered-monomial proof is characteristic-free

Put X=uD and Y=vS, and let A be the unital K-algebra generated by X,Y inside Mat_M(K(u,v)). Their multiplication rule is

    XY=Omega YX,
    (X^i Y^j)(X^k Y^ell)=Omega^(-jk) X^(i+k) Y^(j+ell).

The ordered monomials X^iY^j, i,j>=0, span A. They are K-linearly independent: in a finite relation, the scalar monomial u^i v^j has matrix coefficient c_(i,j)D^iS^j. Algebraic independence of u,v forces this coefficient matrix to vanish; D^iS^j is invertible, so c_(i,j)=0. A finite-dimensional ambient matrix space over K(u,v) causes no contradiction, since this independence is over the smaller scalar field K.

Order exponent pairs lexicographically. This order is translation invariant. For two nonzero finite sums, the pair formed by adding their largest pairs is the unique largest pair in their product: any decrease in either summand pair makes the sum strictly smaller. Its coefficient is a product of two nonzero K-scalars and a nonzero power of Omega. It cannot vanish in any characteristic. Thus A is a domain.

The elements X^M=u^M I and Y^M=v^M I are central. Localize A at the nonzero scalar polynomials K[u^M,v^M]I. This localization embeds into Mat_M(K(u,v)); denominators are central nonzero rational scalar functions. Call the resulting algebra Dcal, and let K_0=K(u^M,v^M).

Reducing exponents modulo M shows that X^aY^b, 0<=a,b<M, span Dcal over K_0. They are independent over K_0: clear a common denominator, expand its coefficient polynomials in u^M,v^M, and recover a K-linear ordered-monomial relation. Distinct (a,b) have distinct exponent residues modulo M, so coefficients cannot overlap. Therefore dim_(K_0)Dcal=M^2.

Dcal remains a domain. Left and right multiplication by a nonzero element are injective K_0-linear maps on a finite-dimensional space and hence surjective. This gives left and right inverses; associativity identifies them. Thus Dcal is a division algebra. This step uses neither characteristic zero nor perfectness of F, and does not require K to omit any algebraic constants. Generic independent u,v prevent the scalar parameters from degenerating.

Every raw lift R^* belongs to A, since u^a v^b D^a S^b=X^aY^b.

## 3. Rank divisibility and every-root determinant nonvanishing

Any finite matrix with blocks in Dcal can be reduced by block row and column elimination to identity blocks and zero blocks. A nonzero block is invertible in Dcal; the Schur complement remains in Dcal. These operations are also invertible ordinary matrices over K(u,v). Each identity block has ordinary size M, so the ordinary rank is a multiple of M.

Fix an order-r<=B feasible affine pencil with coefficients A_i in Mat_r(F). The audited reversal construction gives

    rank_(F(q,eta))(sum_i A_i tensor R_i)>(r-1)M.

A corresponding nonzero minor persists after the field extension F(q,eta)->F(Omega)(q,eta)=K. The same minor of sum_i A_i tensor R_i^* specializes at u=v=1 to that nonzero minor, so the lifted rank over K(u,v) is also greater than (r-1)M.

Every M by M block of the lifted pencil is a K-linear combination of raw lifts, since the entries of A_i lie in F, a central subfield of K. Hence all blocks lie in Dcal. Rank divisibility and the total order rM force rank exactly rM. Consequently

    det(sum_i A_i tensor R_i^*) != 0.

This determinant is a polynomial, not merely a rational function, in q,eta,u,v. The proof works for EVERY primitive Omega: it invokes only its exact order M and invertibility of M, not a specially selected complex root or a coefficient-field embedding with extra properties. Applying it to the constant pencil L=1 gives det R_0^*!=0, provided B>=1 (the B=0 input can be handled trivially).

## 4. Explicit total-degree accounting

For the candidate Newton construction, each basis polynomial has q-degree at most C=M(w+M). Its monomial coordinate matrix is unit triangular; the inverse is the finite sum I-N+N^2-... of at most M triangular powers. Each inverse entry therefore has q-degree at most (M-1)C<=M^2(w+M). Multiplying by the output Newton coordinate matrix, weighting by eta^a, reversal, and zero padding preserves the safe candidate bounds

    deg_q R_i entry <=2M^2(w+M),
    deg_eta R_i entry <=N-1<M.

No q-dependent denominator appears in unit triangular inversion. Fourier coefficients only make constant linear combinations of entries. A lift term also has u-degree a<=M-1 and v-degree b<=M-1. Thus every lifted entry has TOTAL four-parameter degree at most

    T=2M^2(w+M)+3(M-1).

Linear combination with arbitrary pencil coefficients in F cannot increase this degree, because q,eta,u,v are fresh relative to F. An rM by rM determinant has total degree at most rM T, so

    deg_total P_(L,Omega)
      <=BM[2M^2(w+M)+3(M-1)]
      <10 B M^3(w+M+1)=D0.

For example 3(M-1)<=3M^2, so the left side is already bounded by 3BM^3(w+M+1). The proposed constant 10 is generous. The same D0 covers the r=1 constant-pencil determinant. Reduction modulo p can only reduce polynomial degree.

## 5. Normalization denominators are distinct from raw-lift denominators

The statement 'only denominator M' is correct for the RAW lifted matrix entries. Abstractly forming X_i=(R_0^*)^(-1)R_i^* additionally introduces det R_0^* through the adjugate formula. This denominator is not being ignored: it is the extra all-branch determinant constraint in the grid descent. At retained points, the ordinary F_p regular-representation matrix R_0^* is nonsingular and its inverse is computed by exact Gaussian elimination.

The affine factorization has the correct left order:

    A_0 tensor I+sum_(i>=1) A_i tensor X_i
      =(I_r tensor (R_0^*)^(-1))(sum_(i>=0) A_i tensor R_i^*).

Therefore nonzero raw-pencil determinants and det R_0^* suffice. The normalized rational entries require no new independent parameter constraint or degree budget; the generator outputs concrete finite-field matrices after pointwise inversion, rather than symbolic rational functions.
