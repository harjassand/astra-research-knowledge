## 1. Exact source interface

Coefficients and each A_h are arbitrary. Write B_X(M)=Σ_u y_u z_u for the length-M dot tensor on the two legs opposite X.

## 2. Scalar-descent lemma

Let E/F be an extension of degree k, choose an F basis e_1=1,e_2,...,e_k, and choose an F-linear map ℓ:E→F satisfying ℓ(1)=1. Suppose an E-valued trilinear restriction of a tensor A with coefficients in F has a target B with coefficients in F. Expand each E-valued leg substitution into the chosen basis:

    U_X=Σ_α e_α U_X,α,
    U_Y=Σ_β e_β U_Y,β,
    U_Z=Σ_γ e_γ U_Z,γ.

Apply ℓ coefficientwise. Because the coefficients of A lie in F,

    ℓ(A(U_X,U_Y,U_Z))
      =Σ_(α,β,γ) ℓ(e_α e_β e_γ)
          A(U_X,α,U_Y,β,U_Z,γ).

Each summand is an F-valued restriction of one whole copy of A. Its scalar can be absorbed on any leg. Thus B is a restriction of at most k³ copies of A over F.

The same assertion holds for Laurent-polynomial substitutions and leading-coefficient degenerations. Expand their coefficients in the same basis, keep the formal indeterminate intact, and project coefficientwise. Projection preserves every zero coefficient below the leading order and preserves the F-valued leading target because ℓ(1)=1. Negative monomial weights can be shifted to nonnegative weights without changing the leading tensor.

This construction descends the whole expression once. If a rank-r expression over E is tensor-powered j times while E stays fixed, the correct F-rank charge is at most k³r^j, not (k³r)^j. This distinction is essential when passing to tensor characters or asymptotic rank.

The crude k³ bound is sufficient here. It is not a novelty claim: scalar-extension invariance of asymptotic tensor rank and matrix-multiplication exponents is classical. The useful cycle19 deduction is applying this concrete version to the newly supplied separator, with its label-dependent field extension and exact characteristic-two weights.

## 3. Construction over every fixed finite field

Fix F_q. Choose k minimal with L=q^k−1>3(M−1), let E=F_(q^k), and choose a primitive L-th root ζ in E. L is coprime to the characteristic, so 1/L exists. For M≥2, L<q(3(M−1)+1), and k=O_q(log M).

Use L source copies of A, indexed by r=0,...,L−1. On copy r substitute

    x_a ↦ Σ_g ζ^(2rg) X_a,g,
    y_h,b ↦ Σ_u ζ^(r(u−h)) Y_h,b,u,
    z_h,c ↦ (1/L) Σ_v ζ^(r(−v−h)) Z_h,c,v,

where g,u,v,h∈{1,...,M}. The coefficient from the Fourier sum is1 exactly when

    u−v+2(g−h)=0,

and zero otherwise: the absolute mismatch is at most3(M−1)<L. This is an integer congruence in the exponent group, not an equality taken modulo the field characteristic.

Now multiply the variables by formal powers with integer weights

    w_X(g)=g²,
    w_Y(h,u)=hu−h²,
    w_Z(h,v)=−hv.

For a surviving term their sum is

    g²+h(u−v)−h²=(g−h)².

This is nonnegative as an integer and zero exactly for g=h and u=v. The leading tensor is therefore ⊕_h(A_h⊗B_X(M)), with the original arbitrary payload coefficients preserved. In characteristic2 the valuation weights do not reduce modulo2; the same proof applies.

Apply §2 to all L copies. This proves over F_q

    A^(⊕Lk³) ↝ ⊕_h(A_h⊗B_X(M)),

with source-copy budget O_q(M log³ M). The constant can depend on the fixed field. For an arbitrary field of characteristic p, the same F_p construction embeds into it.

    log(source-copy budget)−log M_n=O(log n)=o(n).

## 5. Two additional finite-field gates

First, a formal Laurent degeneration can be converted to exact restrictions without interpolation. Tensor-power it j times, expand each leg map into its O(j) coefficient maps, and extract the target leading coefficient. Only O(j²) triples of leg coefficients have the required total exponent. Thus B^⊗j is an F-restriction of O(j²) copies of A^⊗j. Taking j-th roots proves degeneration monotonicity for tensor characters over any field. This is also the mechanism behind the integral coefficient-extraction witness in family107's staggered-extraction manuscript. It avoids a finite-field sampling assumption.

Second, polynomial convolution C(a,b) has exact character upper bound a+b−1 over every field. For fixed a,b put m=a+b−1, choose one fixed finite extension E of F with at least m distinct evaluation points, and use evaluation/interpolation there to obtain rank_E(C^⊗j)≤m^j. Descend the whole powered expression once to get rank_F(C^⊗j)≤k³m^j. For a tensor character λ,

    λ(C)^j≤k³m^j,

so λ(C)≤m after taking j-th roots. The fixed basis dimension k does not grow with j. Charging k³ separately at every tensor factor would give a false loss.

The determinant exact sequence and shifted-tripling construction in the October2 source use integer coordinate maps, integer formal weights, and disjoint blocks. They do not require complex conjugation or division by the characteristic. The appendix detecting-character argument is a real convex-state argument on the tensor semiring; root is independently checking its field-independent hypotheses.

Together these are the concrete repair points for a proposed all-field extension of ω≤9/4. This worker's complete reusable proof is §§2–4; the full square consequence should be adopted only after the remaining source proof chain has survived the independent audit.
