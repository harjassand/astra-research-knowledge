1. KMS CONTRACTION BY AN ELEMENTARY UCP SPECTRAL ARGUMENT
Let sigma>0 be ANY density matrix on M_d, S=sigma^(1/2). Equip likelihood
operators with the Hilbert inner product
  <X,Y>_sigma=Tr[X* S Y S].
For a physical CPTP map L_phys preserving sigma define
  L(X)=S^-1 L_phys(S X S) S^-1.
It is CP unital. Its Hilbert adjoint is exactly
  L^dagger(X)=L_phys^*(X).                              (1)
Proof: <X,LY>=Tr X* L_phys(SYS)=Tr L_phys^*(X*) S Y S.
L^dagger is CP unital by physical trace preservation. Thus N=L^dagger L is
CP unital and a positive selfadjoint HILBERT operator. Every CP unital map is
an operator-norm contraction, so every eigenvalue of N has modulus <=1.
Hilbert positivity makes these eigenvalues real and nonnegative. Finite-
dimensional spectral calculus therefore gives
  0<=L^dagger L<=I.                                    (2)
This proof does not assume sigma commutes with inputs, effects or Kraus
operators. It does not invoke a comparison with an unweighted HS norm or a
minimum reference eigenvalue. The same argument works for maps between a
quantum KMS space and a weighted commutative Hilbert space whenever the map
AND its displayed Hilbert adjoint are CP unital.

2. MEASUREMENT / PREPARATION FACTORIZATION
Supply a POVM M_y, p_y=Tr(sigma M_y)>0, and density states rho_y with
  sum_y p_y rho_y=sigma.
Define maps between M_d and C^m with weighted trace inner product
<f,g>_p=sum p_y conjugate(f_y)g_y:
  T(X)_y=Tr(M_y S X S)/p_y,
  T^dagger(f)=sum_y f_y M_y,
  P(f)=sum_y p_y f_y S^-1 rho_y S^-1,
  P^dagger(X)_y=Tr(rho_y X).                            (3)
All four maps are CP unital. Their adjoint identities follow directly from
trace cyclicity; no traciality of Tr(sigma X) is assumed. Section1's spectral
argument proves T^dagger T<=I and P^dagger P<=I. Set
  Psi=T^dagger T, Q=P T.
Then
  0<=Psi<=I, Q^dagger Q<=Psi.                           (4)
Psi is CP unital, KMS-positive/selfadjoint and fixes the physical reference.
It is a genuine EB physical channel with canonical preparations
  Psi_phys(rho)=sum_y Tr(M_y rho) S M_y S/p_y.           (5)
The preparation states are positive and have trace one. Q_phys is the
arbitrary measure-and-prepare branch sum Tr(M_y rho)rho_y. It fixes sigma.

3. SAME EXACT FACTOR-TWO ROUNDING, NOW NONCENTRAL
For ANY sigma-preserving physical L_phys put C=(L+Q)/2 and Phi=C^dagger C.
Both C and its KMS adjoint are CP unital; Phi is a positive KMS-selfadjoint
channel. The EXACT Hilbert-operator identity is
  2(I-Phi)-(I-Psi)
   =(I-L^dagger L)+(Psi-Q^dagger Q)
                            +(L-Q)^dagger(L-Q)/2>=0.   (6)
Thus I-Psi<=2(I-Phi) on the KMS space. The algebra is identical to INITIAL;
what changed is the valid adjoint/contraction argument for noncentral sigma.

For Hermitian ||H||<=theta let Z=(I-Psi)H. Schatten Holder gives
 ||S Z S||_1
 =||sigma^(1/4)[sigma^(1/4)Z sigma^(1/4)]sigma^(1/4)||_1
 <=||sigma^(1/4)||_4^2 ||sigma^(1/4)Z sigma^(1/4)||_2
 =||Z||_(2,sigma),                                     (7)
because Tr sigma=1. Positivity 0<=Psi<=I and (6) then give
 ||S(I-Psi)H S||_1^2
 <=<H,(I-Psi)H>_sigma
 <=2<H,(I-Phi)H>_sigma
 =2 Tr[H S(I-Phi)H S]
 <=2 theta ||S(I-Phi)H S||_1.                          (8)
For any family rho_H=S(I+H)S, Tr sigma H=0, ||H||<=theta<1, one EB channel
therefore has uniform half-trace error
  e_EB <=(1/2)sqrt(2 theta r),
  r=sup_H ||Phi_phys(rho_H)-rho_H||_1.                 (9)
No reference eigenvalue ratio, dimension, labels or outcome factor appears.
No N46 bridge or log-bin centralization is used for this admitted subclass.

4. ACTUAL INSTRUMENT / BROADCASTER INTERFACE
Suppose L_phys=sum_y I_y is a supplied physical CP instrument and fixes sigma,
with M_y=I_y^*(I). Supply rho_y with the reference-mean condition above.
Then B0(rho)=sum_y I_y(rho) tensor rho_y is CPTP with marginals L_phys,Q_phys.
Average B0 and its receiver swap to give common marginal C_phys. The actual
Petz recovery
 R_phys(z)=S C_phys^*(S^-1 z S^-1) S
is CPTP: it is visibly CP and Tr R_phys(z)=Tr z follows from C_phys(sigma)=sigma.
It fixes sigma because C_phys^*(I)=I. Its likelihood map is C^dagger by (1).
Applying R_phys to BOTH outputs gives the common physical marginal Phi_phys.
Thus the corrected Phi in (6) really is selfcompatible. Arbitrary measurement
effects, preparations and instrument Kraus operators may fail to commute with
sigma. Without a supplied compatible instrument/branch decomposition an
arbitrary input joint Choi does not automatically satisfy this interface.
The comparison (6) itself needs no compatibility; compatibility is supplied
by this actual joint channel when interpreting the result as broadcasting.

5. NONCENTRAL PRECISION CONTINUITY
For a physical bounded-likelihood perturbation rho_H-sigma=S H S, the
canonical EB response is a sum of
  F_H(a_y)=Tr(a_y H) a_y/Tr(a_y), a_y=S M_y S.
The INITIAL positive-effect proof applies with the ordinary physical trace:
 ||F_H(a)-F_H(b)||_1<=3 theta ||a-b||_1.
Hence matching POVMs satisfy
 sup_(||H||<=theta) ||(Psi_M,phys-Psi_N,phys)(S H S)||_1
    <=3 theta sum_y ||S(M_y-N_y)S||_1.                 (10)
Round effects and add the same identity shifts as INITIAL. Since
0<=N_y-M_y<=2uI implies 0<=S(N_y-M_y)S<=2u sigma, the repaired POVM has total
physical effect discrepancy <=6e. Its physical full-trace reconstruction
error is therefore at most sqrt(2 theta r)+18 theta e, even for noncentral
sigma and arbitrarily small reference/outcome eigenvalues.

The POVM effects can be rational. Canonical preparations S M'_y S/p'_y are
algebraic when S is algebraic, and need not be rational merely because sigma
and M'_y are rational. Their root evaluation, exact normalization and final
state-precision costs must be charged. The INITIAL claim of rational canonical
preparations concerned a CENTRAL rational reference; do not silently transfer
that representation claim here. No full general algebraic-root/state compiler
