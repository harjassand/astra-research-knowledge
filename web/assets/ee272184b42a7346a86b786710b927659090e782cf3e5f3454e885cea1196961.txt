The drift in (S4) simplifies to the basis-independent polynomial
 a_N(m)=(N-1)/(2s^2)[Cm-(m^TCm)m]
                   +[b-(b.m)m]/(2s).
Indeed
 (v_n.grad)v_n=-m-(n.m)n+2(n.m)^2m,
 (r_n.grad)r_n=(n.m)n-m,
 so their difference is -2[(n.m)n-(n.m)^2m].
This supplies the exact acquired vector field without eigenaxis decisions.
The diffusion matrix and potential already have basis-independent forms
 D=(I-mm^T)C(I-mm^T)-[m]_cross C[m]_cross^T,
 V=N(N-1)m^TCm/(4s^2)+N Tr C/(4s^2)+N b.m/(2s).
C has supplied uniform spectrum [1,Lc], and D has margin >=3/4 on the
stopping ball. The 3-by-3 square root can use the gap-free rational/Newton
method of the baseline with this simpler known positive margin; that
observation does not by itself implement SDE/stopping/weight sampling.

An explicit sufficient N0 for all displayed initial-tail and drift thresholds is
 ceil max{Lambda^2,(36Lc)^2,(54Lc)^(2/3),
          (12B/r0)^(4/3),2/delta,1}.
Then each of the three terms in betaN of (S8) is <=r0/24.
All parameters Lambda=M+1, Lc=2M+1, r0^2=1/[16(Lc+1)],
delta=1/2-logcosh1 are public fixed data.

For all N>=N0, the nonasymptotic certificate (S12) remains the relevant
bound; replacing it by C exp(-cN) hides very poor constants.
The seed-tail prefactor C_Lambda,delta may be obtained directly from
the [a0,2] integral upper bound and the elementary Gaussian exterior
integral, divided by Lbase. Thus no unspecified large-deviation theorem
or unknown rate function is needed.
