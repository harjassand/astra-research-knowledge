# Retained failures and rejected directions

## CMA–senescence / clearance control

- A coupled production–clearance model for senescent-cell burden is standard and directly overlaps published age-dependent turnover and wound/fibrosis models. No new rates or output law were acquired.
- A simple macrophage-only “bypass SIRPα” proposal is preempted by Schloesser et al. (2022), who study CD47/SIRPα/QPCT/L suppression and blockade. CD47 loss alone also does not induce senescent-cell engulfment, so that intervention does not answer the full fate question.
- The 2026 CA77.1 paper’s in-vivo pharmacology is systemic and long-term; it cannot separate macrophage, senescent-cell, fibroblast, and other immune-cell contributions. Isolated aged BMDM bead uptake is a cell assay, not in-vivo senescent-cell removal.
- The source data show substantial within-assay macrophage-genotype dependence and partial rescue under CMA-deficient secretome, but Fig. 4h fields are nested within three experiments and direct senescent target-cell fate is not the measurement. Treating fields as independent biological replicates would be pseudoreplication; the script therefore gives descriptive means only.
- A more elaborate bistability or positive-feedback theorem would still use unacquired birth, recruitment, per-cell uptake, and death parameters. This is not advanced as a result.

## Muscle MuSC CMA/glutamine metabolism

- Generic activation-versus-reserve branching models are familiar and the 2025/2026 primary studies report acute proliferation/transplantation outcomes, not a measured repeated-injury reserve trajectory. Fitting a transition matrix from invented parameters would manufacture the target result.
- Stable-isotope enrichment and D2O-derived lipogenesis fractions establish pathway use under reported conditions, but do not alone identify in-vivo fluxes, self-renewal transition probabilities, or age-wide reserve dynamics.
- Marker change, EdU incorporation, transplantation engraftment, regenerated-fiber size, and functional readouts are different endpoints; they cannot be collapsed into a single “rejuvenation” state variable without an acquired mapping.

## Mitochondrial mtDNA turnover

- Neutral Moran threshold probability and event-clock rescaling are established population-genetic/stochastic-process facts; network time rescaling and exact threshold/takeover analyses are prior art. The source-parameter arithmetic is a conditional accounting corollary, not a new aging mechanism.
- Faster neutral turnover can increase finite-horizon threshold crossing by advancing the event clock; allele-specific selection can reverse its direction. Therefore “more turnover repairs mtDNA” and “more turnover causes damage” are both unsupported without genotype-conditioned rates.
- An mtDNA heteroplasmy reporter/sequence count is not itself a measured lesion, functional threshold, or organ-level aging outcome.

## Paneth niche repair

- The event-budget and pooled-crypt inequalities are conditional mass-balance/Markov bounds. They expose missing denominators but do not identify a biological probability because the needed division flux, Paneth residence-time distribution, conditional progeny output, and functional threshold are absent.
- A pooled EdU or Notum mean cannot identify per-crypt coverage, but the resulting counterexamples are mathematical identification witnesses, not empirical distributions.
- The broad Paneth–Notum–Wnt repair mechanism and αKG intervention are primary-literature prior art; no new tissue-repair capability is claimed.

## Search limits

Search was selective, oriented around recent primary sources, exact cited priors, and the named route. There was no exhaustive bibliography, full systematic-review protocol, external expert review, independently performed animal experiment, or clinical work.
