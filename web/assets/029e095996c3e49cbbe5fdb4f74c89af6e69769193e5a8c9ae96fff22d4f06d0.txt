1. Exact scope and normalization

Let S=diag(1,r_1,...,r_n), r_i>0, sigma=S^2/Z_sigma, Z_sigma=Tr S^2.
Let B be an arbitrary Hermitian STAR noise relative to this center:
 B=[[0,b^*],[b,0]], b_i=sqrt(r_i)v_i, v_i complex and arbitrary.
Let V=2Lyap_S^{-1}(BSB), H(Y)={V,Y}/2-BYB. Its sigma-preserving KMS
physical Heisenberg generator is L=d^{-1}H(d(.)d)d^{-1}, d=S^(1/2).
Let rho=R/Z_rho with
 R=c direct_sum P, c>0, P>0 arbitrary, Z_rho=c+Tr P.

P need NOT commute with diag(r_i), match stationary covariance, or have a
prescribed spectrum. There is no equality Z_rho=Z_sigma. The center is a
common eigenvector of rho and sigma, while B couples it to the rest. Zero
entries of v allow arbitrary inactive anchors. A general faithful reference
with such a center can be put in this form by diagonalizing its complementary
block and dividing its positive root by the center root; scaling S leaves
the same H and physical generator. This is a scope reduction by unitary
coordinates/scalar normalization, not simultaneous diagonalization of rho
and sigma on the complementary block.

THEOREM: for this SAME generator and every such state,
J=Tr[L^*(rho)(log rho-log sigma)] >= pi E,
E=Tr[sqrt(rho)H(sqrt(rho))].
Any positive sigma replacement rate preserves the inequality and makes the
generator primitive. This does not yet settle a general noise with a rest-

2. Exact physical transport with complex noise amplitudes

The relevant blocks are

All center/rest drift cross entries are zero. Thus
L^*(Y)=(CY+YC^*)/2-KYK^*, C+C^*=2K^*K.
The rest drift has Hermitian part (C_rest+C_rest^*)/2=vv^* exactly.
K,C retain the necessary Hamiltonian drift and fix S^2. H is HS selfadjoint
and H(S)=0. The same finite CP Lyapunov-noise legality proof as in the blind
baseline applies. The factor Z_sigma cancels under the physical sandwich.

Write U=diag(log r_i), Q=sqrt P. For raw R=c direct_sum P,

Here rv denotes the row-root diagonal multiplication diag(r_i)v.
Trace preservation gives Tr G=0. The scalar normalization in both physical
logarithms cancels, and the physical J numerator is

 Z_rho J=g_0 log c+Tr[G_rest(log P-2U)].

Since P commutes with log P and the drift's Hermitian part is vv^*,
Tr[G_rest log P]=v^*Plog(P)v-c(rv)^*log(P)(rv).
The reference-log drift coefficient, including its off-diagonal entries, is

 =2[r_i log r_i+r_j log r_j]/(r_i+r_j) v_i conjugate(v_j).
No off-diagonal reference contribution is dropped.

The actual positive-root energy numerator is

This uses sqrt R=sqrt c direct_sum sqrt P. Both quantities are exact full

Set a_i=sqrt(c)r_i and P=sum_l nu_l P_l, nu_l>0, P_l orthogonal spectral

6. Sums, a dimension reduction, and primitive extension

The gap is linear in H. Consequently finite sums of Hermitian star noises
with the same common center, and the same rho and sigma, also satisfy pi.
The complementary state P can remain fully noncommuting in every summand.

There is also an exact reduction for an ARBITRARY Hermitian noise B when
rho and sigma share a center eigenvector. Decompose B into its block diagonal
part B_even and center/rest star part B_odd. The cross terms in BsB and the
Lyapunov drift are off block diagonal, as are the corresponding cross terms
in BrB and BqB. Since rho,q,r,z are block diagonal, every such cross term
has zero trace in J and E. Thus
 (J-kappa E)(B)=(J-kappa E)(B_odd)+(J-kappa E)(B_even).
The scalar center entry of B_even contributes zero. The remaining even term
is the LOWER-DIMENSIONAL physical gap on the complementary block, multiplied
by its probability Tr(P)/Z_rho. Normalizing the complementary reference
and state changes logarithms only by scalars, annihilated by trace
preservation; it does not change the transported generator. This reduction
also applies to sums of noises. Therefore a minimal-dimensional counter-
example among faithful pairs to the pi endpoint cannot have a common
rho/sigma eigenvector.
Using the supplied internally audited scalar-qubit global pi theorem gives
in particular the full qutrit generator pi inequality whenever rho and sigma
share an eigenvector. No general qutrit or amplified-qubit assertion follows.

The same elementary spectral Jeffreys/Hellinger calculation in the blind

the entire complementary state P is arbitrary and may be noncommuting.
