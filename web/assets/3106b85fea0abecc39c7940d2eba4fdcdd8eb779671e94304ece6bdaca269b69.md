## Main result: exact scope of the N75 information obstruction

Family 093, Proposition 7.2, proves a scale-integrated Gaussian-channel mutual-information bound from an LSI premise and the displayed moment/Sylvester/commutator assumptions. Its formal replacement by near-extremal Poincaré control is false: there is a smooth, positive, log-concave, one-dimensional family with best Poincaré constant exactly 1 satisfying those displayed field assumptions, for which the scale-integrated mutual information has no bound uniform in the fixed cap (L).

This is a falsification of the **displayed abstract interface**, not of Family 093's original proposition or N75's full upstream route. Indeed, the counterexample's size-biased tail escape forces 
\(\operatorname{Ent}(R_j^2)\to\infty\), contradicting the bounded-LSI premise in the original critical-field construction. Thus the example cannot satisfy the source's full provenance. A replacement using those additional source-specific eigenfunction/tensor constraints remains open.

### Pin down the quantifiers

Write \(P(\eta)\) for the optimal Poincaré constant in \(\operatorname{Var}_\eta f\le P(\eta)\mathbb E_\eta|f'|^2\). Family 093, §7.1, Proposition 7.2 and equations (7.1)–(7.8), assumes stationary \(Y_{j,t}\sim\eta_j\), LSI constants \(\mathcal R(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1}\) with \(\lambda_j\to1\), and PSD fields \(R_j\) and symmetric fields \(\widehat H_{j,i}\) such that
\[
\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}R_j^2\to1,\quad \mathbb E|DR_j|^2\to1,\quad
D_iR_j=(\widehat H_{j,i}R_j+R_j\widehat H_{j,i})/2,\quad
\sum_i\mathbb E\|[\widehat H_{j,i},R_j]\|_{HS}^2\to0.
\]
For fixed \(t>0,L\ge1\), it sets \(f_L(q)=q/(1+q/L)\), \(C_{j,T}=I+f_L(TR_j^2)\), and \(Z_{j,T}=C_{j,T}(Y_{j,t})^{1/2}G\) with independent standard Gaussian \(G\). Its conclusion is
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}J_j(L)\le9/4,\qquad
J_j(L):=\int_0^\infty I(Y_{j,t};Z_{j,T})\,\frac{dT}{T^2},
\]
where \(j\to\infty\) is taken with each \(L\) fixed and the bound is independent of \(L\). Primary source: [Family 093 PDF at the pinned OpenAI/math commit](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-dimension-free-logarithmic-sobolev-inequality-for-subgaussian-log-concave-measures-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), local SHA-256 `767a5eb7cd819af7e33c8b867c41403aac36ec015c89c1d2beb186abca9ec89e`.

The proposed Poincaré-only replacement changes only the LSI premise to \(P(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1}\) and seeks a finite absolute upper bound independent of \(t,L\). The following smooth example refutes this replacement under the displayed field assumptions.

### Smooth exact-Poincaré counterexample

Start with the centered rate-2 Laplace law \(\mu(dx)=e^{-2|x|}dx\). Its sharp Poincaré constant is \(P(\mu)=1\): conjugating its reversible generator by the square root of the density gives the Schrödinger operator \(-\partial_x^2+1-2\delta_0\), whose zero ground state is followed by continuous spectrum beginning at 1. Its variance is \(1/2\); the Poincaré constant comes from the tail spectral gap, not the linear test.

For \(\delta_j=j^{-4}\), let \(W_j=X+\sqrt{\delta_j}G_0\), where \(X\sim\mu\), \(G_0\sim N(0,1)\) are independent. Define \(p_j=P(\mathcal L(W_j))\), \(b_j=\sqrt{p_j}\), and \(\eta_j=\mathcal L(W_j/b_j)\). Convolution tensorization gives \(p_j\le1+\delta_j\). For each fixed compactly supported smooth test function, its variance and Dirichlet integral under \(\mathcal L(W_j)\) converge to those under \(\mu\); taking the supremum of the limiting quotients gives \(\liminf p_j\ge P(\mu)=1\). Therefore \(p_j\to1\), \(b_j\to1\). Scaling yields \(P(\eta_j)=1\) exactly. Each \(\eta_j\) has a smooth positive log-concave density.

For \(u\ge2\), direct convolution gives
\[
p_{W_j}(u)=e^{2\delta_j}\left[e^{-2u}\Phi\!\left(\frac{u-2\delta_j}{\sqrt{\delta_j}}\right)+e^{2u}\Phi\!\left(\frac{-u-2\delta_j}{\sqrt{\delta_j}}\right)\right].
\]
For all large \(j\), this lies between \(\frac12e^{2\delta_j}e^{-2u}\) and \(e^{2\delta_j}e^{-2u}\). After rescaling, for \(y\ge4\) and large \(j\),
\[
\tfrac12 b_je^{2\delta_j}e^{-2b_jy}\le p_{\eta_j}(y)\le b_je^{2\delta_j}e^{-2b_jy}.
\]
The density tail exponent is thus \(2b_j\), with \(b_j\to1\).

Choose a fixed smooth nondecreasing \(\chi:\mathbb R\to[0,1]\), equal to zero on \(( -\infty,0]\) and one on \([2,\infty)\). Put \(\psi(y)=\int_{-\infty}^y\chi(s)ds\), so \(\psi=0\) for \(y\le0\) and \(\psi(y)=y-c_\chi\) for \(y\ge2\). With \(\varepsilon_j=j^{-1}\), set \(a_j=b_j-\varepsilon_j>0\) for all sufficiently large \(j\), and
\[
Z_j=\mathbb E_{\eta_j}e^{2a_j\psi(Y)},\quad q_j=Z_j^{-1/2},\quad
R_j(y)=[q_je^{a_j\psi(y)}],\quad \widehat H_j(y)=[a_j\chi(y)].
\]
The density bounds show \(Z_j\asymp\varepsilon_j^{-1}\), hence \(q_j^2\asymp\varepsilon_j\), and the normalized density proportional to \(e^{2a_j\psi}d\eta_j\) assigns mass \(1-o(1)\) to \(y\ge4\). Consequently
\[
\mathbb E_{\eta_j}R_j^2=1,\qquad
\mathbb E_{\eta_j}|DR_j|^2=a_j^2(1-o(1))\to1.
\]
The scalar Sylvester equation is exact, \(R'_j=\widehat H_jR_j\), and every commutator is zero. A reversible diffusion with invariant law \(\eta_j\) supplies stationary \(Y_{j,t}\).

Let \(X_j=R_j(Y)^2\). For every fixed \(K>0\), its tail threshold
\[
y_{K,j}=c_\chi+\frac{\log(K/q_j^2)}{2a_j}=O(\log j)
\]
satisfies \(\varepsilon_jy_{K,j}\to0\). The same density bounds imply
\[
\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge K}]\to1.
\]
For \(u\ge u_{0,j}=q_j^2e^{2a_j(4-c_\chi)}\to0\),
\[
\mathbb P(X_j>u)\le A_ju^{-\beta_j},\quad
\beta_j=b_j/a_j>1,\quad
A_j=\tfrac12e^{2\delta_j-2b_jc_\chi}q_j^{2\beta_j}\to0.
\]
Thus eventually \(\mathbb P(X_j>u)\le1/u\) for every \(u\ge1\).

### Binary-event scale lemma

This lower bound uses only \(\mathbb EX_j=1\), the fixed-threshold size-biased escape \(\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge K}]\to1\), and the envelope \(\mathbb P(X_j>u)\le1/u\) for \(u\ge1\). Since the scalar channel depends on \(Y\) only through \(X=R_j(Y)^2\), \(I(Y;Z_T)=I(X;Z_T)\).

Let \(p_0=\mathbb P(|G|\ge\sqrt2)>0\), and choose absolute \(h_0\) such that
\[
p_0(h/4-\log3)-\log2\ge(p_0/8)h\quad(h\ge h_0).
\]
Fix \(L\) and any \(H\) with \(h_0<H\le L\). For each latent value \(x\ge K_H:=He^{H/4}\), take \(T=h/x\) for \(h_0\le h\le H\) and use \(A_h=\{|Z|\ge\sqrt h\}\). Because \(f_L(h)\ge h/2\), \(\mathbb P(A_h\mid X=x)\ge p_0\). For an independent \(X'_j\), split on \(TX'_j\le1\): there the output variance is at most 2 and the Gaussian tail is at most \(2e^{-h/4}\). On the complement, \(x/h\ge e^{H/4}\ge e^{h/4}\ge1\), so the tail envelope gives \(\mathbb P(X'_j>x/h)\le e^{-h/4}\). Thus the marginal event probability \(q_h\le3e^{-h/4}\). Binary data processing yields, for each fixed input and its associated event,
\[
D(\mathcal L(Z_{j,h/x}\mid X_j=x)\|\mathcal L(Z_{j,h/x}))
\ge p_0\log(1/q_h)-\log2\ge(p_0/8)h.
\]
Averaging the conditional KL and integrating only over \(x\ge K_H\) and \(T\in[h_0/x,H/x]\), with \(h=Tx\) and \(dT/T^2=x\,dh/h^2\), gives
\[
J_j(L)\ge\frac{p_0}{8}\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge K_H}]\log(H/h_0).
\]
For every fixed \(L\ge H>h_0\), take \(j\to\infty\) first. Setting \(H=L/2\) gives
\[
\liminf_{j\to\infty}J_j(L)\ge\frac{p_0}{8}\log\!\left(\frac{L}{2h_0}\right),\qquad L>2h_0.
\]
The lower bound diverges over fixed caps \(L\); the argument does not exchange the \(j\)- and \(L\)-limits. Hence no Poincaré-only bound with a finite constant independent of \(L\) follows from the displayed Family 093 field interface.

### Full source provenance blocks this counterexample

Family 093's critical-field provenance also assumes near-unit LSI, \(\mathcal R(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1}\to1\) (Proposition 3.9, equations (3.28)–(3.30), and Proposition 7.2). For a normalized nonnegative scalar field, write \(X_j=R_j^2\). For every fixed \(K>1\),
\[
\operatorname{Ent}(X_j)=\mathbb E[X_j\log X_j]\ge(\log K)\mathbb E[X_j\mathbf1_{X_j\ge K}]-1/e.
\]
The size-biased escape proved above implies that the liminf of this entropy is at least \(\log K-1/e\) for every \(K\), hence \(\operatorname{Ent}(X_j)\to\infty\). But the source LSI \(\operatorname{Ent}(g^2)\le2\mathcal R(\eta_j)\mathbb E|Dg|^2\), applied to \(g=R_j\), bounds it by \(2+o(1)\). Contradiction. The counterexample cannot satisfy the original near-unit-LSI source class while retaining this tail-escape profile.

Smoothness is not the issue: \(\eta_j\) is smooth and log-concave, but its exponential tails preclude finite LSI. The source-native natural one-dimensional eigenfield attempt also fails Proposition 5.1's projection/outlier conditions after amplitude rescaling; the independent details are in the KLS lane report. That failed reconstruction is supplementary: entropy already rules out every source realization with the same normalized tail escape and energy limit. This does not decide whether a different covariance-only estimate can exploit the source's high-dimensional tensor hierarchy.

**Scope.** The Poincaré-only replacement under just the displayed Section 7 field, moment, Sylvester, and commutator assumptions is refuted by a smooth exact-\(P=1\) family and a cap-divergent lower bound. The original LSI proposition is not refuted. The source-specific N75 gap remains open; any valid replacement must exploit assumptions beyond those displayed in Section 7. ## N77 cycle 2: lattice saturation is not stochastic connectivity

The detailed-balanced reversible completion is
\[
2C\rightleftarrows A+B+C,\quad A+B+C\rightleftarrows3A+2B,
\quad3A+2B\rightleftarrows2C,\quad2C\rightleftarrows2C+D,
\]
with all directional constants one. Its reaction vectors have rank three; the only conservation row is \(K=(0,1,1,0)\), and the reaction lattice is already saturated:
\[
L=\ker_{\mathbb Z}K=S\cap\mathbb Z^4.
\]
The minor on rows \(A,B,D\) and columns \(\nu_1,\nu_2,\nu_4\) equals \(-1\). Yet \((0,0,2,0)\) and \((1,0,2,0)\) are in the same integer fiber and differ by \(e_A=\nu_2-\nu_1\), but lie in distinct closed classes \(\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1\). The enabled-reaction invariant \(H=A-B(B+1)/2\) separates them. Thus saturation alone does not connect feasible reaction states.

The exact fiber ideal makes the failure algebraic. For \(K=(0,1,1,0)\),
\[
I_K=\ker(a\mapsto1,b\mapsto t,c\mapsto t,d\mapsto1)
=\langle a-1,b-c,d-1\rangle.
\]
The primitive move binomials of the candidate reactions are \(c-ab,c-a^2b,a^3b^2-c^2,1-d\). They all vanish at \((a,b,c,d)=(0,1,0,1)\), while \(a-1\) and \(b-c\) do not; so the moves do not generate \(I_K\) and are not a Markov basis. Actual reaction enabledness is still more restrictive when complexes retain common catalysts.

The standard Diaconis–Sturmfels criterion gives an algebraically checkable sufficient condition: a computed Markov basis for the saturated conservation lattice, represented by positive-rate reversible catalyst-free pairs \(u^-\rightleftarrows u^+\), connects every nonnegative integer fiber. Each feasible basis move is then an enabled reaction; positive rates make every edge traversable. This yields one irreducible CTMC class per nonempty integer fiber. It removes hidden-component stationary escape within a fixed fiber, conditional on that class having a stationary law. It proves no nonexplosion, recurrence, or tail bound; the criterion itself is established algebraic-statistics machinery, not a new theorem. Primary source: [Diaconis–Sturmfels (1998)](https://statistics.stanford.edu/technical-reports/algebraic-algorithms-sampling-conditional-distributions).

For the displayed N77 fiber, add \(\varnothing\rightleftarrows A\), \(B\rightleftarrows C\), and \(\varnothing\rightleftarrows D\), with unit rates. These catalyst-free moves \(e_A,e_B-e_C,e_D\) generate \(\ker_{\mathbb Z}K\), and their binomials generate \(I_K\). They connect all states in the fiber \(B+C=2\). All pairs remain detailed balanced at activity vector \((1,1,1,1)\). The normalized product-Poisson(1) law restricted to that fiber has independent \(A,D\sim\mathrm{Pois}(1)\), \(B\sim\mathrm{Binom}(2,1/2)\), and \(C=2-B\).

The augmented chain is nonexplosive: \(B,C\le2\), immigrations have bounded rates, and the total rate of \(A\)-increasing jumps is at most \(c(1+A)\) with jumps bounded by three. Comparison with a linear-rate pure-birth process rules out infinitely many upward \(A\) jumps in finite time. Downward \(A\) jumps are bounded by the initial \(A\) plus total upward increment, and \(D\)-deaths cannot increase \(D\), so only finitely many jumps occur on bounded time intervals. Detailed balance gives a finite invariant probability on the sole fiber; irreducibility and nonexplosion yield positive recurrence and uniqueness there, with Poisson tails in \(A,D\). This completion removes the hidden-component escape mechanism in this fixed model.

Connectivity still gives no uniform tails over model parameters. The one-species network \(\varnothing\rightleftarrows X\), with birth rate \(\theta\) and death rate \(x\), has a saturated Markov basis \(\{+1,-1\}\) and unique stationary law \(\mathrm{Pois}(\theta)\). As \(\theta\to\infty\), this family escapes every fixed finite set. Kinetic/rate bounds are therefore a separate requirement for any uniform tail statement.

The full class reconstruction records four additional absorbing singleton profiles per \(d\) inside \(B+C=2\); the \(\Gamma_n\) classes cover the remaining states with \(H\ge0\) but do not exhaust this fiber. ## Verdict and next gate

The concrete consequential output is a rigorous cap-divergent lower bound for the formal Poincaré-only Family 093 channel interface, paired with a proof that this obstruction cannot satisfy the source route's LSI critical-field provenance. It closes one proposed shortcut while leaving the actual source-specific N75 gap open. For reaction kinetics, we identify the missing structural condition between lattice algebra and stochastic communicating classes: feasible catalyst-free Markov moves. That condition repairs N77's hidden-component issue but has no recurrence or uniform-tail content. Neither result is a claim about biological, chemical, or physical systems without model-specific validation.
