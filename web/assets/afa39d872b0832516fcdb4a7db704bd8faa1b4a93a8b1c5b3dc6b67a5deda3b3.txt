Gaussian block example
For one pair, use u+=(1,1)/sqrt(2), u-=(1,-1)/sqrt(2). The matrix C_sigma=[[1,sigma*rho],[sigma*rho,1]] has eigenvectors u+,u- and eigenvalues 1+sigma*rho,1-sigma*rho. Across m independent pair blocks, the eigenvalue multiset is m copies of (1+rho) and m copies of (1-rho), independent of the sign vector sigma.

If the mean is a*v_i*u+ on block i, whitening gives mean radius r_i=a/sqrt(1+sigma_i*rho) on u+ and zero mean on u-. With rho=1-delta and a=tau*sqrt(delta): sigma_i=-1 gives r_i=tau; sigma_i=+1 gives r_i=tau*sqrt(delta/(2-delta)). The condition r_i<=eps is equivalent to delta<=2 eps^2/(tau^2+eps^2). For r<=eps, TV(N(rw,I),N(0,I))=2 Phi(r/2)-1<=r/sqrt(2 pi)<eps. Therefore a one-symbol archive that regenerates the known covariance and zero-mean law is admissible on all-plus signs. In the all-minus case, the N31 source-reported lower bound, when 100 eps<tau<=1, is log2 D>=m/2 log2(tau/(100 eps)). This memory comparison is conditional on N31; equality of covariance spectra and the whitened signal radii are exact algebra.

Finite-support weak-field example
Choose k=ceil(log2(m+1)) and m distinct nonzero a_i in F_2^k spanning F_2^k. For U uniform on F_2^k set X_i=(-1)^(a_i dot U). Character orthogonality gives E X_i=0 and E X_iX_j=0 for i!=j. Since the a_i span, U->X is injective; hence |supp(P0)|=2^k. iid Rademachers also have mean zero and covariance I_m but support size 2^m.

For any finite support S subset {-1,+1}^m, define p_theta(x)=p_0(x) exp(theta dot x)/Z(theta), theta in a nonempty open box around zero. At zero the score is x and Fisher information is Cov_0(X). For the two laws above this is I_m. The distributions p_theta span R^S: suppose sum_x c_x p_theta(x)=0 throughout the open box. After removing the nonzero common normalizer, sum_x c_x p_0(x) exp(theta dot x)=0. Choose w so w dot x are pairwise distinct (avoid the finite union of hyperplanes w dot (x-x')=0) and restrict theta=t w to an interval in the box. The resulting linear combination of distinct exponentials exp(t w dot x) vanishes on an interval, so all coefficients are zero.

Let T be any classical stochastic channel induced by the encoder/decoder, or by a quantum/hybrid encoder followed by a final classical-output measurement. Exact recovery says T p_theta=p_theta for every theta. Since these probability vectors span R^S, T=I. Factoring identity through memory requires |S| perfectly distinguishable memory states, so the total hybrid Hilbert dimension D is at least |S|; storing x attains it. Free shared randomness does not help because zero off-diagonal transition probabilities in the average channel force zero off-diagonals for almost every seeded channel. Thus exact archive cost is log2|S|, giving m bits for iid Rademachers and k bits for the dependent character field, with the same local Fisher matrix I_m.

For fixed m and fixed compact parameter box, outputs can be projected into S without increasing TV, and the channel optimization at each D<|S| is compact with a continuous worst-case-TV objective. Its minimum cannot be zero by the exact argument, hence is strictly positive. The same holds for free shared randomness because the convex hull of the finite-dimensional channel set is compact. The minimum across the finite set of dimensions D<|S| is a positive epsilon_m. Therefore any epsilon<epsilon_m forces D>=|S|. This threshold is not quantified as a function of m, so this proves a fixed-dimension small-error obstruction, not a uniform two-parameter rate.
