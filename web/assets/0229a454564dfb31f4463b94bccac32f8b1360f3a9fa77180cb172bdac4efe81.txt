RESULT
The strongest result is a scoped no-go for positive matching-circuit repairs of the F4 determinant-hole witness by coordinate-pair auxiliary extensions that preserve F4 as an old principal block. Pin every appended coordinate as a hole. This recovers F4 exactly; auxiliaries touching pinned coordinates contribute zero, and old-only auxiliaries preserve its four-hole defect Delta=-2. Since even-windability is closed under pinnings, the full extension cannot be even-windable. This rules out the stated matching-circuit lift, not arbitrary representations or samplers.

The intermediate boundary is informative: cross-coordinate auxiliaries can repair a different pinning. A six-site rational completion and seven unit-activity edges on K_{4,2} change one four-site pinned marginal's Delta from -245931/32768 to 28101/32768. An explicit exact four-site even-windability witness is given and exhaustively checked on all ordered assignment pairs and applicable pair flips. The completion does not preserve F4's standalone signature: its cross-block entries already alter the marginal. Nor does this marginal witness certify the full six-site signature.

An exact four-site recognizer was derived. For A=f(empty)f(V), pairing capacities h_i=f(P_i)f(V\P_i), B=(sum h_i-A)/2, and L_i=max(0,h_i-B), an even-supported nonnegative four-site rational function is even-windable iff B>=0 and sum_i L_i<=A. Thus Delta>=0 alone is insufficient; the synthetic signature with A=1 and h=(3,0,0) passes Delta but fails the lower-bound test (sum L=2).

DERIVATION / SCOPE
For same-four-site auxiliaries, the polynomial action is product_e (I+w_e partial_i partial_j). Multiaffinity reduces the expansion to matchings. On four sites, each two-hole coefficient gets the activity on its complementary edge, the empty coefficient gains all single-edge terms and the three perfect-pair products, and the all-hole coefficient is fixed. Expanding the three complementary-pair products cancels every added term in Delta exactly. This is a polynomial identity for arbitrary even signature coefficients and all six edge activities, not an activity-grid inference.

For appended coordinates, pinning them all as holes leaves only the old principal block in the determinant-hole formula. Every auxiliary edge incident to an appended coordinate is disabled on this pin. The remaining old edges fall under the invariant above. McQuillan's pinnings criterion then obstructs full even-windability; the matching-circuit implication is limited to this even-supported signature interface. Changing the old block, hiding the pinning, or using a non-matching local energy flow lies outside this proof.
