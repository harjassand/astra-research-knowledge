RUNNABLE FINITE EVIDENCE
Date: 2026-10-08

These files are selected, copied runnable evidence from the matching work
programme. They reproduce finite calculations only; none certifies a general
theorem or establishes external validity.

- `python3 exact_fixtures.py`: requires SymPy; rebuilds `fixtures.json` and
  checks 17 exact compressed-Gram, degeneracy, threshold, and qutrit cases.
  The successful run log is `exact_fixtures_run.log`.
- `python3 leaf_03_check.py`: requires SymPy; checks the displayed qutrit
  cutoff spectrum, stop-on-click branch probabilities, and energy arithmetic.
- `python3 leaf_07_probe_calc.py`: standard library only; prints exact-score
  and finite numerical fixtures for the Dicke/symmetric-state family.

The scripts do not acquire a channel, implement a physical detector, model
drive or reference work, or externally validate N62. Source leaves are in
`work/programmes/quantum_capability/leaves/`.
