# Failure cases, controls, and unresolved gates

## Exact counterexamples to overbroad readings

- The terminal response depends on \(p_r\) and \(C_r\) only through \(p_rC_r\). Scaling one upward and the other downward preserves every delay curve. Thus delay density alone does not reveal occupancy or state-specific effect.
- A transient state with \(C_r=0\) is behaviorally invisible to this output. More delay samples cannot recover its decay rate.
- Two transient modes with identical \(\delta\) collapse to one exponential whose amplitude is the sum of the two products. Their separate labels cannot be inferred.
- A known-state-count result does not identify an unknown state count. Exact fit at order \(k\) does not prove that \(k\) is minimal unless the Hankel rank/order is separately resolved.
- Structural identifiability does not ensure practical estimation: close rates yield nearly collinear Vandermonde columns, and late delays suppress the signal exponentially.
- The theorem does not cover full output paths, multiple nonterminal emissions, transitions among transient modes, hidden-state-dependent test timing, or feedback from output to state.

## Matched controls and observation requirements

- Reset/no-initialization control, same test inputs: estimates the baseline discrimination \(D_0\).
- Zero-mode model \(k=0\): same input alphabet and delay set, no transient memory state.
- A direct categorical state/occupancy channel or independently supplied \(p_r\): required to separate initialization probability from per-mode discrimination contrast.

## Quantitative finite-data gate

For one mode, let \(z_j=D(\tau_j)-D_0=Ae^{-\delta\tau_j}\), and let \(\Delta=\tau_2-\tau_1>0\). If estimated excess signals satisfy \(|\widehat z_j/z_j-1|\leq\eta<1\), then
\[
|\widehat\delta-\delta|\leq \frac{\log((1+\eta)/(1-\eta))}{\Delta}.
\]
To obtain a relative error \(\eta\) on the smaller excess signal \(z_{\min}\), estimating the Bernoulli channels by Hoeffding bounds costs \(O(\eta^{-2}z_{\min}^{-2}\log(K/\alpha))\) trials per channel for simultaneous confidence \(1-\alpha\), where \(K\) counts the means being estimated. Since \(z_{\min}\) shrinks with delay, increased time separation improves the denominator but makes the later signal more expensive to estimate.

For \(k>1\), the exact Prony inversion is \(O(k^3)\) arithmetic, but stable finite-noise recovery needs explicit minimum amplitude and node-separation bounds. No such uniform bound is claimed here.

## Next decisive mathematical check

Extend the generator from independent pure-death modes to a general transient subgenerator \(T\). The delay response then has form \(a^\mathsf{T}e^{T\tau}b\). Determine the minimal observable realization and its similarity-class ambiguity under the same terminal readout, then compare the minimal number of time samples and extra state labels needed to identify scientifically interpretable CTMC rates.
