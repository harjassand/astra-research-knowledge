1. **Universal cloning.** M. Keyl and R. F. Werner, “Optimal Cloning of Pure States, Judging Single Clones,” [arXiv:quant-ph/9807010](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9807010). The paper establishes the optimal symmetric universal cloning device. The marginal parameter used here is η=(d+2)/(2(d+1)); the conversion from the standard single-clone fidelity to this depolarizing parameter is shown in the proof report. The channel theorem does not depend on novelty of this cloning result.
