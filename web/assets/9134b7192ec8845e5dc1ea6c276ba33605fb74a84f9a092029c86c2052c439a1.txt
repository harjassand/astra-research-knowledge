STATUS AND STRONGEST CLAIM
The target hard-BCS coefficient is a volume-sampling distribution on paired columns, equivalently a linear-matroid-parity-constrained DPP. Two natural outward transfers do not provide a polynomial sampler under the exact input interface: (i) rejection from the ordinary fixed-size DPP can succeed with probability 2^(1-n) on a unitary cyclic-shift family; and (ii) replacing the sitewise pair condition by two global partition quotas gives an exponentially rare target event even for a 2-by-2 rational matrix with O(L) input bits, and diagonal item fields that preserve both target outcomes within constant relative accuracy cannot improve this to a constant success probability. The sector/FLC localization route also has no uniform parameter: its admitted cyclic family has sector aperture at most pi/q and fractional-LC parameter at most 1/q.

These are precise failures of specified DPP rejection, global-quota, field-reweighting, and constant-sector down-up transfers. They do NOT show hardness of the target count, rule out a pair-aware kernel or localization, or settle the general sampler. The next load-bearing object is an efficiently computable conditional kernel that preserves the sitewise 0-or-2 constraint without introducing cross-site mismatches.

INTERFACE
For F in Q(i)^(n x n), put f_i=(row_i F)^T and V=[I_n,F^T], with columns e_1,...,e_n,f_1,...,f_n. Let L=V*V. For a site set S, P(S) denotes both columns e_i,f_i for i in S. For |S|=k,

  det L[P(S)] = sum_{J subset [n]\S, |J|=k} |det F[S,J]|^2,
  c_k(F) = sum_{|S|=k} det L[P(S)]
         = sum_{I,J disjoint, |I|=|J|=k} |det F[I,J]|^2.

This is the exact positive volume weight from Cauchy-Binet. The requested law is pi_k(S)=det L[P(S)]/c_k(F). An ordinary 2k-DPP on the 2n individual columns instead samples Q with |Q|=2k and weight det L[Q]. The target is that DPP conditioned on Q being a union of whole site-pairs.

ATTACK 1 — ORDINARY DPP REJECTION CAN BE EXPONENTIAL
Let n=2q and F be the cyclic shift F_(i,i+1 mod n)=1. Then F is a real unitary permutation and VV*=I+F^T F=2I. The normalizer of the n-DPP on the 2n columns is, by Cauchy-Binet,

  sum_{Q subset [2n], |Q|=n} det L[Q] = det(VV*) = 2^n.

A target event chooses q sites S and the n columns P(S). Each e_i is supported on coordinate i and each f_i on i+1. The n selected columns are a basis only if S and S+1 are disjoint and cover all n coordinates. Thus S+1=S^c, which on an even cycle gives exactly the two alternating site sets. Each corresponding determinant has absolute value one. Therefore c_q(F)=2 and

  Pr_{n-DPP}[Q=P(S) for some |S|=q] = 2/2^n = 2^(1-n).

Rejection takes expected 2^(n-1) proposals on an O(n)-bit, exactly rank-n input. This is an exact all-n argument; the executable enumeration checks n=4,6,8. The input itself is easy, so the result rejects this proposal only, not the existence of another polynomial sampler.

ATTACK 2 — TWO GLOBAL PARTITION QUOTAS DO NOT ENFORCE SITE PAIRING
Take rational eps>0 and

  F_eps = [[1,eps],[eps,1]],
  V=[I,F_eps^T],  e_1=(1,0), e_2=(0,1),
  f_1=(1,eps), f_2=(eps,1).

A Partition-DPP with two parts E={e_1,e_2}, G={f_1,f_2} and quota one from each has four possible column pairs. Their determinant-squared weights, indexed by (e_i,f_j), are

                 j=1       j=2
       i=1      eps^2       1
       i=2       1         eps^2.

The BCS target additionally requires i=j, so its mass in this proposal is 2eps^2; the two-global-quota normalizer is 2+2eps^2. The proposal accepts the target with probability eps^2/(1+eps^2). With eps=2^(-L), the matrix has O(L) bits and rejection takes expected Theta(2^(2L)) trials.

This remains true for a natural field-tuning repair. Scale the e_i and f_j columns with positive diagonal DPP weights a_i,b_j. Let d_i=a_i b_i be the relative target weights of the two valid site pairs. The target mass is eps^2(d_1+d_2), while the cross-site mass is

  a_1 b_2+a_2 b_1 >= 2 sqrt(d_1 d_2)

by AM-GM. If fields keep the two target outcomes within a factor rho, so 1/rho <= d_1/d_2 <= rho, then

  Pr[target] <= eps^2 / (eps^2 + 2/(sqrt(rho)+1/sqrt(rho))).

For every fixed rho this is O_rho(eps^2). In particular, fields preserving the two outcomes exactly (rho=1) cannot beat eps^2/(1+eps^2). A field can raise acceptance by concentrating almost all mass on one target outcome, but that changes the desired distribution by an unbounded factor. The script checks the exact epsilon=1/8 example and a nontrivial balanced field choice.

The primary Partition-DPP sampler of Kathuria–Deshpande handles fixed counts in p partitions in time O(m^(p+4) r k/p^p), and its interpolation cost is exponential in p; it is polynomial for fixed p. Sitewise “choose both or choose neither” uses n local constraints (and the allowed local counts vary with the chosen site set). Two global parts E/G only enforce equal total counts, as the counterexample demonstrates. Taking p=n in the stated method is not a polynomial transfer. This does not exclude a more compact pair-aware representation.

ATTACK 3 — CONSTANT-SECTOR LOCALIZATION DOES NOT TRANSFER
For the same cyclic-shift family at k=q, the target site polynomial is

  p_q(z)= product_(i even) z_i + product_(i odd) z_i.

At z_i=1, the Hessian of log p_q(z_1^alpha,...,z_n^alpha) has eigenvalue (alpha^2 q-alpha)/2 in the alternating direction. Log-concavity therefore forces alpha<=1/q. Also set z_i=exp(i pi/(2q)) on even sites and exp(-i pi/(2q)) on odd sites. The two monomials then equal i and -i, so p_q vanishes. Both phases lie in every positive-real-centered sector of aperture greater than pi/q. Hence the maximum root-free aperture is at most pi/q. The AASV multi-site down-up theorem requires a constant aperture for a polynomial relaxation-time bound, so it cannot give a uniform sampler here. This does not rule out localization with defect/mismatch variables or nonlocal updates.

INTERLACING / RANDOMIZED DETERMINANT BOUNDARY
The scalar pair-count polynomial is real-rooted by the cloned expected-characteristic-polynomial identity. The primary interlacing result of Leake–Ravichandran controls roots/extremal eigenvalues of expected characteristic polynomials; it is not a relative coefficient oracle. In the existing cycle-4 audit, direct uniform principal-deletion and random-sign Pfaffian estimators have relative variance exponential in independent two-site swap blocks. These source-derived facts and this checkpoint agree that root reality alone does not supply the microscopic partition sum. No new variance theorem is claimed in this checkpoint.
