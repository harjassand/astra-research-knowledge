NEW SCOPED RESULT: MANY AUTONOMOUS COFACTORS

Let k>=1 and consider species A,B_1,...,B_k with positive rational rates
alpha_i,beta_i,gamma_i and integers m_i in {1,2}:

  0 -> B_i       at alpha_i,
  B_i -> m_i A   at beta_i B_i,
  A+B_i -> B_i   at gamma_i A B_i,       i=1,...,k.

Every reactant has molecularity at most two. Define
  n=sum_i B_i, alpha*=sum_i alpha_i,
  beta_min=min_i beta_i, beta_max=max_i beta_i,
  gamma_min=min_i gamma_i,
  q=max(2, ceil(2 beta_max/gamma_min)),
  delta=min(alpha*/[q(q+1)], gamma_min/[2(q+1)]),
  eta=min(q delta, beta_min/2),
  V(A,B)=A/(n+q)+2n.
These are positive rational certificate data except q, which is a positive
integer. For the full generator at every lattice state, including n=0,

                         LV <= 2 alpha* - eta V.             (1)

Derivation. Put B_beta=sum_i beta_i B_i and B_gamma=sum_i gamma_i B_i.
For n>=1, direct reaction-increment calculation gives
  LV=A C(n,B)+D(n,B),
  C=-alpha*/[(n+q)(n+q+1)]
       +B_beta/[(n+q-1)(n+q)]-B_gamma/(n+q),
  D=2 alpha* + sum_i beta_i B_i[m_i/(n+q-1)-2].
At n=0, C=-alpha*/[q(q+1)] and D=2alpha*. For n>=1,
n+q-1>=q>=2 beta_max/gamma_min, so
  B_beta/(n+q-1) <= gamma_min n/2 <= B_gamma-gamma_min n/2,
and therefore
  C <= -gamma_min n/[2(n+q)] <= -gamma_min/[2(q+1)].
At n=0, C=-alpha*/[q(q+1)]. Thus C<=-delta at every state.
Also, n+q-1>=q>=2 and m_i<=2, whence
  m_i/(n+q-1)-2 <= 2/q-2 <= -1.
So D<=2alpha*-sum_i beta_i B_i<=2alpha*-beta_min n; at n=0 the same
bound holds with n=0. Combining gives
  LV<=-delta A+2alpha*-beta_min n
     <=2alpha*-min(q delta,beta_min/2)[A/(n+q)+2n],
which is (1). This proof is all-state; finite-state checks below are not used
to pass from a bounded box to the lattice.

The potential is nonnegative and norm-like: V>=2n, and with n bounded,
A/(n+q) tends to infinity with A. Thus the certificate supplies a finite
explicit return core, e.g. K={V<=(2alpha*+1)/eta}; outside K, LV<=-1.
The rates and certificate are acquired with O(k) exact rational operations
plus comparisons and one rational ceiling. Evaluation cost is ordinary exact
arithmetic in the encoded state and inputs. This is a certificate for this
specialized syntax, not a general reaction-network recognizer.

The coordinate vector B is an autonomous product immigration-death chain:
its only changes are 0->B_i and B_i->m_i A, and A+B_i->B_i leaves B_i fixed.
Each B_i has immigration rate alpha_i and death rate beta_i B_i. On a finite
time interval each B_i has finitely many births and deaths almost surely;
A can gain at most two molecules per B-death event. These bounds make A
bounded on that interval, after which the A-death rate A sum_i gamma_i B_i is
bounded. This proves nonexplosion independently of the recurrence conclusion.

For completeness, the unique closed irreducible class is
  C={n>=1} union {(A,0): A>=m_*},  m_*=min_i m_i.
It is closed because the last cofactor death from n=1 leaves at least m_*
A molecules. All states outside C have n=0 and A<m_* and enter C on the next
immigration. To see irreducibility, fix j with m_j=m_*. From any state with
n>=1, immigrate B_j if necessary, consume all A using A+B_j->B_j, remove
each unwanted B_i by its death reaction and consume the produced A while
retaining B_j, and reduce B_j to one in the same way. This reaches (0,e_j).
States (A,0), A>=m_*, first immigrate B_j and then drain A. Conversely, from
(0,e_j), arbitrary states with n>=1 are built by immigrating or removing
cofactors while a positive target cofactor is held as an A-drain anchor;
cofactor death/immigration cycles add m_j A molecules and A-drain jumps
adjust A to any desired count. A target (A,0) with A>=m_* is obtained by
making A-m_* molecules with B_j present and then firing its final death.
Together with nonexplosion, (1) and the norm-like property imply positive
recurrence of C by the standard countable-state Foster criterion.

Every member of this family is ordinary all-directions endotactic. For a
direction w=(u,v_1,...,v_k), the source scores are 0, v_i, and u+v_i.
If 0 is maximal, v_i<=0, so every 0->B_i edge has nonpositive projection.
If B_i is maximal, v_i>=0 and u<=0, so (m_i u-v_i)<=0 for B_i->m_i A.
If A+B_i is maximal, comparison to its B_i source gives u>=0, so the
projection -u of A+B_i->B_i is nonpositive. These cases cover ties, hence
there is no maximal-source edge with positive projection.

EVIDENCE AND LIMITS

`multico_factor_aggregate.py` independently expands every legal transition,
computes exact Fraction generator values, checks the closed formula above,
and checks (1) on bounded boxes. `multico_factor_aggregate_checks.json` records
20,580 states total in three fixtures: 3,721 unit-rate one-cofactor states,
6,859 two-cofactor states with heterogeneous rates and m=(1,2), and 10,000
three-cofactor states with heterogeneous rates and m=(1,2,1). All checks
passed with exact arithmetic; minimum slacks were zero. This is a finite
diagnostic only. No simulation, unbounded search, formal proof assistant,
external review, novelty search for this exact family, or total token/energy
measurement was performed.

This revision overlaps conceptually with c10_s01's acquired reciprocal
correction for a different two-layer catalytic class. The construction here
was derived from c10_l05's frozen one-cofactor INITIAL after cross-exposure;
this post-exposure derivation is not being described as statistically
independent of the Sol baseline. Its new structural feature is aggregation of
arbitrarily many independent autonomous cofactor coordinates, with
heterogeneous beta_i/gamma_i and m_i in {1,2}, into n=sum_i B_i. The stated
proof uses only the extrema beta_max, beta_min, gamma_min and alpha*.
No novelty or priority claim is made.

Literature and exact scope checked against primary sources:

* Xu, “On the Regularity of Reaction Systems,” arXiv:2409.05340v2
  (https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05340v2), Theorem 4.6: second-order endotactic
  mass-action systems are regular/non-explosive, not thereby recurrent.
  The same paper's Theorem 4.13 reduces bimolecular weakly reversible
  positive recurrence to existence of stationary distributions per
  communicating class; its discussion says the general conjecture remains
  open. This is the correct external boundary for the C10 recurrence claims.
* Wiuf-Xu, “Classification and threshold dynamics of stochastic reaction
  networks,” arXiv:2012.07954v2
  (https://arxiv.org/html/2012.07954v2), Theorem 4.11 and Corollary 4.12:
  one-dimensional endotactic / weakly reversible classwise recurrence is
  covered under stated H2-H4/PIC hypotheses. This is prior-art overlap for
  c10_l02, but our k-cofactor family has stoichiometric rank k+1>=2 and is
  not weakly reversible, so this theorem does not settle the new family.
* Gupta-Briat-Khammash, “A Scalable Computational Framework for Establishing
  Long-Term Behavior of Stochastic Reaction Networks,” PLOS Comput Biol
  10(6), e1003669 (2014)
  (https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003669):
  established Foster-Lyapunov and computational/LP conditions for stochastic
  network ergodicity and moment bounds, with prior quadratic Foster methods

  potential contribution is narrower exact acquisition and recovery packaging;

* Anderson-Craciun-Kurtz, “Product-form stationary distributions for

  art relevant to c10_s02 and c10_l04; their directly checked detailed
