EXTENDING THE PRODUCT-LEG MOMENT GATE FROM THE BORDENAVE-COLLINS HIGH TRACE THEOREM
2026-10-08

Parameter note: the d=64 example below records the first stronger-gate
replay and is retained as a derivation illustration. The selected point
after joint d/slack optimization is d=65,K=260,s=0.165 with
p=15,999,300,534 and N=16(130)^14 p^836; see

Result

For the cycle4 score polynomial, one can improve the finite exponent gate in
the stated Bordenave-Collins Lemma 9.3 by re-running its proof from their
Theorem 5.1 moment estimate at a larger even Schatten order. This is an
internal extension of the proof, not the published parameter range of
Lemma 9.3. It replaces the source exponent 1/(16d+80)=1/1104 at d=64 by the
Theorem 5.1 ell_0 exponent 1/(12d+56)=1/824, with an explicit leading factor
in N. Under the other audited gates this lowers the input log-dimension by
about 26% at the same score slack s=0.11.

The output remains a conditional probabilistic existence result. It still
uses the source's free-radius upper bound, the reconstructed expectation
constant, and a dense Haar description; it does not provide a matrix seed or
practical channel.

1. Stronger one-leg expectation bound from Theorem 5.1

Let A_N be a self-adjoint linear polynomial in d independent Haar U(N)
generators with coefficients in a tracial C*-algebra, let A_F be its free
counterpart, and rho=||A_F||. Bordenave-Collins Theorem 5.1 gives, for even
p<=ell_0 and its stated ell_0 condition,

  | E ||A_N||_p^p - ||A_F||_p^p |
      <= c d^4 p^(4d+16) rho^p/N
      <= (c/sqrt(N)) rho^p.

The first inequality is their explicit Theorem 5.1 moment estimate; the
second is its displayed consequence under ell<=ell_0. Therefore

  E ||A_N||_p^p <= rho^p(1+c/sqrt(N)),
  E ||A_N||_p <= rho(1+c/sqrt(N))^(1/p)
                 <= rho(1+c/sqrt(N)).

The second line follows by Jensen and p>=1. This reproduces the one-leg
expectation estimate used in Lemma 9.3, but permits every even p satisfying
the Theorem 5.1 ell_0 gate rather than only the source's selected
p=N^(1/(16d+80)). Its coefficient algebra may contain the other fixed or
already-free tensor legs, as in the source proof of Lemma 9.3; Theorem 5.1
is stated for general tracial coefficient algebras.

2. Re-run the product-leg interpolation

Use the interpolation A_(N,l), l=0,...,k, from the proof of Lemma 9.3, with
l finite tensor legs replaced successively by free generators. At each step,
condition on all remaining variables and apply the preceding one-leg bound:

  E_l ||A_(N,l-1)||_p
      <= ||A_(N,l)||(1+c/sqrt(N)).

As in source Lemma 9.4, tensorized Haagerup gives

  ||A_(N,l)||^p
      <= 2 n N^(k-l) p^(3l) ||A_(N,l)||_p^p.

The initial finite-dimensional conversion is
||A_(N,0)||<= (n N^k)^(1/p)||A_(N,0)||_p. Multiplying the k conditional
bounds and using (nN^k)^(1/p)<= (2n)^(1/p)N^(k/p) yields

 E||A_N|| <= ||A_(F^k)||
    (2n)^((k+1)/p) N^(k(k+1)/(2p)) p^(3k(k+1)/(2p))
    (1+c/sqrt(N))^k.                         (E1)

This is the same factor as Lemma 9.3, with its proof's parameter range
extended to even p satisfying the stronger one-leg moment gate. In cycle4,
(d,k,n)=(64,2,514), so (E1) is

 R(N,p)=1028^(3/p) N^(3/p) p^(9/p) (1+c_E/sqrt(N))^2.

This extension depends on using Theorem 5.1's moment estimate directly. It
does not follow by reading the published Lemma 9.3 range as larger than
stated.

3. Exact finite parameter gate

At d=64, Theorem 5.1 defines ell_0 by

  2(2d)^(7/2) ell^(3d+14) <= N^(1/4).

Here 3d+14=206. Set p even and

  N = N_0 p^824,
  N_0 = (2(2d)^(7/2))^4 = 16(2d)^14 = 2^102
      = 5,070,602,400,912,917,605,986,812,821,504.

Then N^(1/4)=2(2d)^(7/2)p^206, so ell=p satisfies the Theorem 5.1 ell_0
condition with equality. Its displayed second-moment simplification also
holds: d^4p^(4d+16)<=sqrt(N), since 64^4 p^272=2^24p^272<=2^51p^412=sqrt(N).
The remaining Bordenave-Collins gate N>=64^70 is satisfied by the parameter
below. Theorem 11's 2q^(7/2)<=N^2 gate is also satisfied for every path count
q<=p.

The explicit expectation constant is the reconstructed
c_E<=64 exp(5/2)<780 from CONSTANT_AUDIT.txt. For score slack s=0.11, set

  eta=s/(2K)=0.00021484375,
  e=s/(6*258*sqrt(50))=1.004932117965...e-5.

Choose p as the least even integer for which the known logarithm in (E1)
is at most one half of ln(1+e/2), and verify the remaining constant term
with 2ln(1+780/sqrt(N))<=1560/sqrt(N). The Decimal replay gives

  p = 23,682,886,248,
  N = 2^102 p^824,
  log2(N) = 28,499.6157108585...,
  log2(N^2) = 56,999.2314217170... per Phi input.

At this N, the product-Haar score concentration gate for failure <=1/3
requires only log2(N)>=67.99. Hence the score net has a good event with
probability >=2/3.

4. Entropy and resource consequence

The exact finite product-conjugate quotient-Gram argument gives Bell output
entropy <=H(q)=10.722120449286654... nats for every representation. With
Gamma_K<=sqrt(50)+0.11 on the score event, the purity identity gives
S_min(Phi_N)=S_min(bar(Phi_N)) >=5.361659549811700... nats. Therefore

  S_min(Phi_N)+S_min(bar(Phi_N))
    - S((Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N))(Omega))
    >=0.001198650336747106... nats.

The pairwise witness uses two N^2-dimensional inputs, or
113,998.462843... qubit-equivalent dimensions across both. The standard
flagged same-channel construction adds one input qubit-equivalent dimension
per use and preserves the gap.

For comparison, applying the same score slack s=0.11 through only the
published Lemma 9.3 range gives N=p^1104 with p=31,982,608,962 and
log2(N)=38,525.8117117..., or 77,051.6234 qubit-equivalent dimensions per
input. The extended moment gate reduces this register exponent by about
26%. It is a proof-range improvement from Theorem 5.1, not a new random
matrix ensemble. It remains impractical and does not materialize matrices.

Status and falsifiers

The one-leg extension is internally derived from the explicit Theorem 5.1
moment estimate; the product-leg bound (E1) reuses the source's Lemma 9.3
interpolation and Lemma 9.4 tensor Haagerup step. The finite integer gate is
replayed by rough_gate.py. This has not been externally or formally checked.
The main proof failure points are: (i) whether Theorem 5.1's moment bound
applies unchanged to the hybrid tracial coefficient algebras in each
conditional step; (ii) whether the coefficient symmetry and normalized
trace conventions remain exactly those of Lemma 9.3; and (iii) any omitted
constant in the c_E reconstruction. These are made explicit to invite
adversarial review.
