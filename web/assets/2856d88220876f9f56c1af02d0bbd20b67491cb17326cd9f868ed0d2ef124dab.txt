1. KRETSCHMANN-SCHLINGEMANN-WERNER NORM PRECISION
The KSW continuity and information-disturbance theorems use the completely bounded norm (equivalently the channel diamond norm after picture conventions). Their explicit counterexample separates this from the ordinary Heisenberg operator norm ||T||_(infinity-to-infinity): the paper constructs a channel T with ||T-S||_(infinity-to-infinity)<=2/(d+1) while ||T-S||_cb >=(d-1)/(d+1).

The present EB-rounding target is the tracial map norm ||E-Phi^m||_(infinity-to-one,tau), so it is a third norm. KSW's example is not a counterexample to a direct inequality between this target norm and cb norm. It does show that KSW's cb theorem cannot be invoked after replacing its hypothesis with the ordinary unamplified operator norm. To use KSW for the current target one must first prove the exact conversion from normalized infinity-to-one control (or the experiment-restricted guarantee) to cb/diamond control. No such dimension-free conversion was found in the cited theorem. The exact family in INITIAL independently shows that Choi-Hilbert-Schmidt control alone does not supply the required infinity-to-one control.

2. EXACT BOSONIC-SUPPORT MISMATCH
Permutation invariance of an extension is weaker than support in the symmetric subspace. For qubits let P^-=(I-F)/2 be the singlet projector. P^- is invariant under swap (F P^- F=P^-) but has support wholly in the antisymmetric subspace. The normalized Choi state

    Jhat = (I_R/2) tensor P^-_(B1B2)

is positive, has input-reference marginal I_R/2, and is invariant under swapping the two outputs. It is the Choi state of the valid channel that ignores its input and prepares a singlet on B1B2; its one-receiver marginals are both replacement by I/2. Yet its output extension is not supported on Sym^2(C^2). This is a finite exact example showing that output permutation symmetry alone does not satisfy the hypothesis of the dimension-free bosonic Hilbert-Schmidt theorem. It is not a counterexample to broadcasting-to-EB, since the marginal is itself EB.

3. EXACT CHANNEL DE FINETTI NO-SIGNALLING MISMATCH
Suppose an attempted embedding of a one-input splitter Gamma:A->B1 tensor ... tensor Bn into N^(n):A^n->B^n feeds input A1 to Gamma and ignores A2,...,An. For any retained output subset containing some B_j with j>1, its reduced output is Gamma's B_j marginal applied to rho_A1. If this marginal depends on rho_A1, then replacing A1 by the maximally mixed state changes the retained output. This violates the source's no-signalling condition for discarded input A1 (equation (61)). Permutation-averaging the active input does not establish the condition; it generally makes all input sites influence multiple output sites. Thus the most direct embedding fails exactly at no-signalling. This does not rule out a different, nontrivial reduction from a one-input broadcaster to the channel-de-Finetti interface.

These qualifications narrow, rather than change, the INITIAL conclusion: the primary-source audit yields metric and hypothesis mismatches, not a proof or counterexample for the unrestricted EB theorem.
