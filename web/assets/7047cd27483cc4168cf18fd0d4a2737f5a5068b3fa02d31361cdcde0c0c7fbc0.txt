THE EXACT CANDIDATE

Fix local irreducible spin j=p/2>0, beta>0 and a finite field bound B>=0.
There is an explicit epsilon_*(j,beta,B)>0 such that for every N>=1,
every known real symmetric A with ||A||<=1, every known |b|<=B, and
every 0<=epsilon<=epsilon_*, the WHOLE N-spin density

 rho_N proportional to exp[beta J^2/N+epsilon Q_A/N+b.J]

has an explicitly specified positive mixture sigma_N of identical local
spin-j thermal products satisfying

 T(rho_N,sigma_N)<=C(j,beta,B) exp(-N).                    (F1)

The displayed C may be very large, so (F1) is only informative for large
N. The radius epsilon_* may be exponentially tiny in beta,B,j. It is
strictly positive and independent of N. Equivalently the allowed
anisotropic tensor T=epsilon A contains a fixed open operator-norm
neighborhood of zero. Negative and indefinite A are admitted; no
isotropic-commutation or positive-anisotropy assumption is made.

This extends the subleading exponent gamma>1 in the accompanying
HIGHER_SPIN_STOPPED_DIFFUSION.txt to a fixed small macro-scale anisotropy
epsilon/N. It does not admit arbitrary anisotropy at that scale.

1. PUBLIC CONSTANTS

All kernel and spin conventions are (H2)-(H5) of the main proof. Set

 a=max{2,8beta j,8beta,sqrt[32beta(Bj+2)]},
 m_in=tanh(a/2), r_star=tanh((a+B)/2),
 g=(1-r_star)/2=1/[exp(a+B)+1], r0=1-g,
 Lambda=3/(1-r0^2)^2, L=Lambda+1,
 U_p=p+2F_p'(1)/(p+1),
 Lbeta=(7/3)exp(-2/beta),
 Cbeta=sqrt(pi)(32beta)^(3/2)/(4Lbeta),
 epsilon_* = min{
   beta/(2Lambda),
   g/[2exp(B)L r0(U_p+1)],
   1/[L j(j+1)],
   3g^2/[16exp(2B)L(2Bj+4)]
 },
 C=2Cbeta+6(Bj+1)/e.                                    (F2)

Every denominator is positive, so epsilon_*>0. All values depend only
on supplied fixed local representation, beta and B. As usual natural
logarithms and the scalar e are used. No constant depends on A,b or N.

2. THE ISOTROPIC SHIFT LEAVES A POSITIVE HEAT SEED

For epsilon>0, put C_A=A+Lambda I and

 t0=(beta-epsilon Lambda)/N,
 H=epsilon Q_(C_A)/N+b.J.

Then K=t0J^2+H exactly, C_A>=I, ||C_A||<=L, and
beta/(2N)<=t0<=beta/N by the first epsilon_* constraint. The exact
positive heat-seed identity (H5) supplies rho0 and its radial product
mixture. The elementary character bound and Gaussian tail proof (H12)
apply with fixed cutoff a and give

 p_bad=P(|m0|>m_in)<=Cbeta exp[-a^2 N/(32beta)]
                         <=Cbeta exp[-(Bj+2)N].          (F3)

Replace bad starting branches with m=0. This costs full trace norm
<=2p_bad. No Gibbs-state oracle or physical postselection is used.

3. A POSITIVE LOCAL DIFFUSION WITH A FAST ENOUGH EXIT EXPONENT

The same exact differential identity (H4) yields drift

 a_N(m)=epsilon(N eta_p(|m|^2)-1)/(2N)
                    [C_A m-(m^TC_A m)m]
                    +[b-(b.m)m]/2,

diffusion covariance epsilon D_(C_A)/(2N), and its exact potential
V_N=Tr(H k_N). On |m|<=r0,

 D_(C_A)=Lambda D_I+D_A>=I,
 D_(C_A)<=L I,

because D_I has smallest eigenvalue >=(1-r0^2)^2 and ||D_A||<=2.
Thus Lambda(1-r0^2)^2-2=1. All coefficients are smooth on a slightly
larger ball, and a stopped 3-dimensional SDE is well defined.

The exact field-only flow has radius <=r_star through time one and
Lipschitz constant <=B. The anisotropic drift norm is at most

 beta_aniso<=epsilon L r0(U_p+1)/2<=g/[4exp(B)],

using the second epsilon_* constraint. Gronwall therefore implies
that an exit from the ball r0 requires martingale displacement
>=3g/[4exp(B)]. Coordinate quadratic variations are <=epsilon L/(2N).
The exponential-martingale maximal inequality gives

 P(tau<=1)<=6 exp[-kappa1 N],
 kappa1=3g^2/[16exp(2B)epsilon L]>=2Bj+4,                 (F4)

by the fourth epsilon_* constraint. This is the load-bearing new
point: small fixed epsilon makes the N-order stopping exponent larger
than the N-order unscaled-field weight. The method does not require
epsilon to vanish with N. No global anisotropic positivity is asserted.

4. WHOLE-DENSITY DUHAMEL ERROR, INCLUDING ALL WEIGHTS

The global Hamiltonian bound is

 ||H||<=h_N=epsilon L jN(jN+1)/N+BjN
            <=[Bj+epsilon L j(j+1)]N<=(Bj+1)N,            (F5)

by the third epsilon_* constraint. Freeze both the local state and its
positive Feynman-Kac weight on hitting r0. The exact stopped Ito
residual and Duhamel comparison proved in section 7 of the main file
give, before normalizing,

 ||exp(M_H)rho0-W_1||_1
 <=2exp(h_N)p_bad+h_N exp(2h_N)P(tau<=1).

Casimir commutes with H, and C_A>=I gives Tr exp(M_H)rho0>=1 by
spin-sector trace Jensen. Normalize W_1; it is a positive identical-
product mixture. The normalized half-trace error is consequently

 T<=2Cbeta exp[h_N-(Bj+2)N]
       +6h_N exp[2h_N-(2Bj+4)N]
   <=2Cbeta exp(-N)+6(Bj+1)N exp(-2N)
   <=[2Cbeta+6(Bj+1)/e]exp(-N),                          (F6)

since N exp(-N)<=1/e for N>=1. This proves (F1) with the fully public
constants (F2). For epsilon=0 the positive Casimir heat mixture followed
by uniform local field filtering is exact, so the distance is zero;
no division by epsilon is taken.

5. BOUNDARIES, ORIGIN AND COST

The theorem uses known A,b, a fixed irreducible spin-j local space and
the positive isotropic beta J^2/N baseline. It is a whole-density trace
approximation, not a fixed-k marginal or an almost-iid component with
exceptional sites. The product law depends on A,b. It does not improve
a parameter-independent decoder or preserve an inaccessible reference.

Root requested this fixed-amplitude probe after the author completed
the subleading higher-spin extension. The counterexample work of
c05_s03 at unrestricted macro-anisotropy is compatible with a tiny
positive neighborhood. No claim uniform in beta approaching zero,
growing local j, unbounded fields or arbitrary anisotropy is made.

All trace weights are charged in (F3)-(F6). The probability law is
specified mathematically by explicit scalar heat density and smooth
SDE coefficients, but no certified finite-bit sampling/compiler is
implemented. Worst-case weight is exp(O(N)); practical runtime, query
cost, acceptance rate, memory advantage and energy remain UNKNOWN.
The virtual qubits in (H4) are only an exact derivation of the known
local D-dimensional density R_j. No exponential physical virtual-
qubit postselection or state oracle is supplied.

Exact small-matrix diagnostics and independent audit are stored as
separate evidence. They do not prove historical novelty. Spin-coherent
and quantum mean-field separability literature requires a dedicated
priority review before dissemination. This is an internal candidate,
not an externally established field-breaking result.
