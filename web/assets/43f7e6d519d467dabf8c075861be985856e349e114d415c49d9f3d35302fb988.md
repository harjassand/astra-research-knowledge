# Direct reconstruction of the N175 lower mechanism

This document records the mathematical work behind the verdict. The proof under audit is v3, not the earlier source-pending freeze. The ordinary elliptic and topological imports are identified in the report. Reading the older audit reports does not supply any implication below.

## 1. Finite angular inputs

Write `mu=dvol(g0)/(4pi)`, `rho_w=exp(2w)/integral exp(2w) dmu`, and `h0(w)=rho_w g0`. The dimension-two energy form is unchanged by this conformal factor. Its mass form has derivative

`dot m_w(u_i,u_j)=2 integral u_i u_j (xi-mean_w(xi)) dmu_w`.

At a positive scalar eigenspace the cluster variation is `-lambda dot m_w`. For a real orthonormal pair `u,v`, its two trace-free functionals are represented by `u^2-v^2` and `2uv`. If a nonzero linear combination vanished identically, the image `(u,v)` would locally lie in one line of the zero set of a nonzero trace-free quadratic form. A nonzero eigenfunction combination would vanish on an open set, contradicting scalar unique continuation. Thus the two functionals are independent. For a same-parity pair their representing functions are even, so the even family retains rank two.

For a finite cutoff and a smooth preliminary path, bounded eigenvalues, unit mass norms and uniform elliptic bounds give compactness of all relevant repeated-eigenvalue pairs. A finite pool of directions supplies rank two on every such pair, also in a small parameter neighborhood of the path. At an exact repeated block of multiplicity `m`, use a smooth Riesz frame and the two equations `M11-M22=0`, `2M12=0`. Their parameter derivative is onto. The locus where that *whole indexed block* stays scalar is contained in this codimension-two zero set. A partial repetition uses its own smaller indexed block and its own chart. Each indexed stratum has a countable subcover because the finite parameter space is second countable. Each chart's zero set has a projection to parameter space of measure zero: two parameter coordinates are smooth functions of time and the remaining coordinates. A generic arbitrarily small parameter therefore avoids every bad stratum along the path. End collars can be frozen only where they are already simple. This reconstructs S1; full high-multiplicity matrix surjectivity is unnecessary here.

For S2 let the retained exact double have basis `u,v` and let `K` be the joint kernel of its two trace-free variation functionals. Suppose every direction in `K` were scalar on an unwanted repeated eigenspace with basis pair `a,b`. The two functionals of `a,b` would span exactly those of `u,v`, by the same independence and the two-dimensional annihilator of `K`. Thus

`Q(a,b)=T Q(u,v)`, where `Q(x,y)=(x^2-y^2,2xy)` and `T` is invertible.

There is no additive constant: equality holds on the full space of smooth directions. The zero set of `a` is the inverse image of one closed ray under `Q(a,b)`. The inverse image of that ray under `T` is a ray; the squaring map `Q` pulls it back to one unoriented line. Therefore `a` has exactly the same nodal set as some nonzero `f=cu+dv`. On a connected component of their common nonzero set choose both signs positive. Both restrictions lie in `H^1_0` there: positive truncations at level epsilon have compact support inside the component and converge in `H^1`. Testing the two weak eigenvalue equations against one another forces their distinct eigenvalues to agree because `integral af > 0`. This contradiction produces a nonscalar retaining tangent for every unwanted cluster. Two auxiliary directions integrate that tangent by the finite-dimensional implicit-function theorem while retaining the chosen double exactly. Each unwanted split decreases the finite integer sum of pair multiplicities; existing strict gaps are preserved. Finite iteration proves arbitrary-small finite repair.

For the within-round-block part of S3, multiplication maps `Sym^2(Y_l)` to `direct_sum_{j=0}^l Y_{2j}`. Its rotation-invariant image contains `F_l(z)=(1-z^2)^l`, the sum of squares of the real and imaginary parts of `(x+iy)^l`. Direct differentiation gives

`(Delta0+2l(2l+1)) F_l=4l^2 F_{l-1}`.

The top Legendre coefficient is nonzero by degree. The displayed recurrence proves inductively that every lower even Legendre coefficient is nonzero. Spectral polynomial projections and rotations put every `Y_{2j}` in the image. Source and target dimensions both equal `(l+1)(2l+1)`, so multiplication is an isomorphism. A nonzero trace-free matrix `C` consequently gives a nonzero product function `sum C_ab u_a u_b` of mean zero. Testing the variation in that function proves surjectivity onto the entire trace-free symmetric block. A finite-dimensional submersion realizes a small trace-free matrix with exactly the desired adjacent repeated diagonal entries. Smallness retains the outer round-degree gaps. S2 then repairs all other finite clusters.

For the cross-block case choose the fixed smooth reflection-symmetric equatorial bump `psi` of maximum one and meridional average `bar psi<1/10`; set `zeta=1-(3/2) psi/l`. The parity Rayleigh quotient comparison confines the round degree `q` positions to

`[q(q+1), (1-(3/2)/l)^(-2) q(q+1)]`.

Same-parity groups through `l+1` stay separated for large `l`, because their round gaps are `4q+6` while the perturbation is at most `(3+o(1))(q+1)`. In azimuthal sector `m=l`, the lowest round energy is `l(l+1)`. The deformed sector minimizer satisfies `integral cot(theta)^2 u^2=O(1/l)`, by its azimuthal energy and a round-sector trial function. Its mass concentrates at the equator; smooth maximality of the bump gives `integral psi u^2=1+O(1/l)`. Thus its eigenvalue is `l^2+4l+O(1)` and it lies in the parity `(-1)^l` degree-l group.

The metric becomes `R^2(dx^2+sin(x)^2 F(x)^2 dphi^2)`, with positive smooth-even `F=1+O_r(1/l)` at both poles. Multiplication by `sqrt(F)` conjugates the axial quadratic form to the round form plus

To retain smooth smallness along the high-degree sequence, impose correction norm at most `l^-2` in `C^l`. This gives `O_r(1/l)` for every fixed `r`. No uniform spectral IFT radius or slope is used.

## 6. One countable fixed point

`u_hi(j+1)=r_hi(j)u_hi(j)+F_hi(j)`, `r_hi=alpha_h/alpha_i`.

`E_hi+sum_{l!=i} E_hl u_li-r_hi u_hi(E_ii+sum_{l!=i}E_il u_li)`.

The reference diagonal cancels. Choose a positive fixed `rho_n` so the terms with column coordinates cannot reverse the signed local leading entry, using the *entire* local norm. Denominators remain positive by the fixed-cutoff inverse range and the global small error.

Every lower-band component has a strict reference gap and is forward stable. Within the new band, zeros of `log r_hi` alternate: the positive-to-negative orientation gets prescribed value zero at the cut; the reverse negative-to-positive orientation matches the two incoming stable propagations. Thus one geometric crossing creates exactly one mismatch. It occurs in one birth system only. Later cutoff systems do not reproduce earlier columns or mismatches.

## 7. Growth, counting and fixed-metric quantifiers

Lift each birth column through its own invariant graph. Exact positive multipliers give compatible `L^2` boundary traces on nested whole balls. Each is smooth after inward restriction from the next ball; classical Dirichlet uniqueness glues the extensions to one entire smooth harmonic function. `beta_n=(sqrt(C*)/a)(F(M_n)-F(M_{n-1}))/(B(M_n)-B(M_{n-1}))`,

where `B(M)=(M+1)^2` and `F(M)=(M+1)(M+2)(4M+3)/6=sum_{l=0}^M(2l+1)(l+1)`.

Require its ratio to `t` to be at most `2^-m` at the stage start; it stays so afterward. The geometric start growth bounds the accumulated later remainders by a vanishing tail multiple of the current time; every finite earlier sum is negligible. Hence the chronological upper frequency average is at most `beta_n`, at all radii, including incomplete stages.

The candidate has nonunique tangent cones; Huang's unique-cone coefficient is not a conflicting theorem.

## Audit failures retained

* A selected off-diagonal sign together with a global norm bound does not suffice. With `A0=diag(1,1,2)`, `E21=delta D^-3/4`, `E23=delta`, and `v1=e1-rho e3`, the exact row-2 force is `delta D^-3/4-rho delta`, negative for large `D`. The entire local estimate excludes this failure.
* The preliminary axisymmetric crossing has the two real `m=l` modes plus the axial mode. Treating it as the final exact double would be wrong.
* Smooth compact link images alone do not bound continued eigenframe time derivatives near increasingly small internal gaps. The pre-fixed `D_m` includes those frame bounds separately.
* Existing all-other-simple double data do not justify freezing one entire crossing neighborhood through all later finite cutoff repairs. The relative value/first-jet path construction avoids this error.
