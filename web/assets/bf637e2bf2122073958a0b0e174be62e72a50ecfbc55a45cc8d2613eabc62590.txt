FIXED-QUANTUM-BRANCH OBSTRUCTION AT THE CRITICAL QUIBIT RADIUS
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally derived finite-spin converse; external mathematical and
priority review remain open. This note answers only whether bounded Q can
achieve o(r); it does not optimize total hybrid dimension.

EXACT INTERFACE

Input family: rho_(r,v)^{tensor n}, rho_(r,v)=(I+r v.sigma)/2,
v in S^2 unknown. Known n,r with 0<c0<=r sqrt(n)<=c1<infinity and n large.
The decoder reconstructs the full density with

  e=sup_v (1/2)||D E(rho_(r,v)^{tensor n})-rho_(r,v)^{tensor n}||_1.

No reference direction, purifying reference, entanglement assistance, or
feedback is supplied. The encoder/decoder may use any dephased classical
labels (including a continuous alphabet) and a quantum register of dimension
at most Q on every label. Every label is charged separately in
D=sum_y dim(H_y); this proof lower-bounds Q alone and makes no claim that D
is optimal. The radius is supplied; any radius-estimation cost is absent by
contract. Computation is unrestricted.

CLAIM

There are C,r0>0 depending only on c0,c1 such that, for 0<r<=r0,

  Q<=1/(32r)   =>   e>=C r/Q.                              (1)

Thus for every sequence Q_n=O(1), e_n/r_n is bounded below by a positive
constant: a constant-dimensional residual spin/oscillator register plus
arbitrarily many classical labels cannot achieve e=o(r). More generally,
e_n=o(r_n) forces Q_n->infinity. This is a branch-dimension result, not an
EB inference and not a total-D theorem.

FINITE-SPIN PROOF

1. Exact reduction and arbitrary sector changes. The Schur decomposition is

  rho_(r,v)^{tensor n}=⊕_j P_j tau_(j,v) tensor I_(m_j)/m_j,
  tau_(j,v)=exp(beta J_v)/Z_j, beta=2 atanh(r), j=L/2.

The weights P_j are independent of v. Discarding and regenerating the
maximally mixed multiplicities are exact parameter-independent channels.
After composing any proposed archive with these channels and pinching the
decoder output into Schur sectors, every composite Kraus map
A_(kjz):V_j->V_k still has rank at most Q. For

  w_(jkz)=||A_(kjz)||_HS^2/(2j+1),

trace preservation gives sum_(k,z) w_(jkz)=1 for every input j. No output
sector marginal or sector preservation is assumed.

2. Bounded score. Set R_j=Tr(tau_(j,z)J_z), a_j=R_j/(j+1), a_0=0, and use
the normalized uniform prior on v. For output sector k>0 define

  F_k(v)=a_k v.J^(k)/k - a_k^2 I/2;   F_0(v)=0.

Since 0<=a_k<=1, globally -3I/2<=F_k(v)<=I/2. Therefore full trace error
e implies the integrated target-minus-output score is at most 2e.
The target score in input sector j>0 is

  T_j=a_j^2/2+a_j^2/j.

For a nonzero A:V_j->V_k, put

  h_A= sum_i Tr(A J_i^(j) A^dagger J_i^(k))/||A||_HS^2.

The exact first-harmonic identity
int v_i tau_(j,v)dv=R_j J_i/[(2j+1)j(j+1)] gives output score
contribution w_{jkz}[a_j a_k h_A/(jk)-a_k^2/2]. Under Choi
vectorization and spin time reversal, h_A is the expectation of
H=-sum_i J_i^(j) tensor J_i^(k). Its largest eigenvalue is
jk+min(j,k). If A has rank<=Q, the rank-Q projector bound below gives

  h_A<=jk+min(j,k)-g_Q(j,k),

and hence the pair score deficit is at least

  Delta_jk >= (a_k-a_j)^2/2
       + a_j^2/j - a_j a_k/max(j,k)
       + a_j a_k g_Q(j,k)/(jk).                            (2)

All terms are nonnegative: a_j increases with j and a_j/j decreases with
j. For k=0 the deficit is T_j>=a_j^2/2; for input j=0 the output score is
nonpositive. Thus sector changes cannot cancel deficits from other sectors.

3. Rank-Q finite-spin projector estimate. Write M=max(j,k), m=min(j,k),
delta=M-m. The spin-coupling irreps are indexed by ell=0,...,2m, have
total spin delta+ell, and their H-energy deficit below jk+m is

  E_ell=ell(2delta+ell+1)/2.

The projector Pi_t onto ell<=t has dimension
D_t=(t+1)(2delta+t+1); its partial trace onto V_j is D_t/(2j+1) I_j,
and onto V_k is D_t/(2k+1) I_k. For every normalized
Schmidt-rank<=Q vector psi, write rho_j and rho_k for its two reduced
states. Then

  |psi><psi| <= Q rho_j tensor I_k,
  |psi><psi| <= Q I_j tensor rho_k,

The reduced states here need not be maximally mixed: writing
psi=sum_(u=1)^q sqrt(lambda_u)|u>|u'>, q<=Q, Cauchy–Schwarz gives both
operator inequalities. The partial-trace identities for Pi_t then imply

  <psi|Pi_t|psi> <= min{1,Q D_t/(2M+1)}.

For delta<=M/(16Q) and M>=16Q, take the spectral cutoff E=M/(16Q), and
let t be the largest ell with E_ell<=E. Since E_ell>=ell^2/2,
t<=sqrt(2E), while
D_t=2E_t+2delta+t+1<=2E+2delta+sqrt(2E)+1<=M/(2Q).
Thus QD_t/(2M+1)<1/4; at least 3/4 of psi lies above the cutoff.
Therefore

  g_Q(j,k)>=M/(32Q).                                      (3)

This applies to every rank-Q Kraus operator, with arbitrary matrix
orientation; it is not a highest-weight ansatz.

4. A positive-mass critical band. The exact spin occupation obeys

  E_(tau_(j,z))[j-J_z]
    =q/(1-q)-(2j+1)q^(2j+1)/(1-q^(2j+1))
    <=(1-r)/(2r),  q=(1-r)/(1+r).

Hence on 1/r<=j<=B/(2r), for fixed B>=2 and r<=1/2, a_j>=1/3. The
Schur law has uniformly positive mass on this band: if L=2j, measuring
J_v gives L>=|S_n| for S_n a sum of n independent signs with mean r.
The Berry–Esseen bound and r sqrt(n) in [c0,c1] give
P(L>=2/r)>=alpha(c0,c1)>0 for large n. Also the exact Casimir moment
E[L(L+2)]=3n+n(n-1)r^2 gives

  P(L>B/r)<=(3c1^2+c1^4)/B^2.

Choose fixed B large enough that this tail is <=alpha/2. Then
P(1/r<=j<=B/(2r))>=alpha/2.

5. Uniform deficit on that band, for every output k. If
delta=|j-k|<=M/(16Q), then Q<=1/(32r) and j>=1/r imply M>=16Q and
k>=15j/16. The occupation bound gives a_j>=1/3 and a_k>=1/5. Combining
(2) and (3), using min(j,k)<=j<=B/(2r),

  Delta_jk>=r/(240 B Q).                                 (4)

If delta>M/(16Q), let s=1/(16Q). The exact smooth spin extension of a_j
as a function of x=2rj is

  A(x,r)=[(x+r)coth((x+r)atanh(r)/r)
          -r coth(atanh(r))]/(x+2r).

On compact x intervals in [1,2B], A_x converges uniformly as r->0 to
L'(x)=1/x^2-csch(x)^2>0, where L(x)=coth(x)-1/x. Thus for small r there
is m_B>0 with A_x>=m_B/2 throughout that interval. If k<j(1-s),
monotonicity lets us compare k with j(1-s); if k>j/(1-s), compare with
j/(1-s). The corresponding x separation is at least 1/(8Q), so

  |a_j-a_k|>=m_B/(16Q),
  Delta_jk >=m_B^2/(512Q^2)>=m_B^2 r/(16Q),              (5)

the last step using Q<=1/(32r). Outputs arbitrarily far outside the band
are covered by the exact monotonicities, not by extrapolating the local
limit. Equations (4)-(5) imply Delta_jk>=c_B r/Q on the whole source band,
for a constant c_B>0 independent of n,Q,k.

6. Sum. Since sum_(k,z)w_(jkz)=1, the nonnegative pair deficits and
band mass imply

  2e >= sum_j P_j sum_(k,z)w_(jkz) Delta_jk
     >= (alpha/2)c_B r/Q.

This proves (1), with C=alpha c_B/4. If Q>1/(32r), Q already diverges;
otherwise the bound proves Q>=C r/e. In either case e=o(r) implies
Q->infinity.

COST CONTROL AND FALSIFIERS

An explicit covariant rank-Q tapered-spin instrument has worst-case
full-state error <=C_up(c1)r/Q, with an O(n^3) finite charged label
alphabet, so its total D is O(n^3 Q). This is a branch-coordinate upper,
not an optimal D claim; the exact Schur archive has Q=n+1 and D=O(n^2),
and accuracy-dependent Schur truncation can have better D at some targets.
The present converse makes labels arbitrary and never treats them as free
in D; they are merely unrestricted while testing Q. No input-dependent
radius, direction, or frame is supplied.

Falsifier: an explicit no-assistance instrument with fixed Q, all labels
charged, and e_n/r_n->0 for the stated critical interval. Proof-level
failure points are a sign/normalization error in (2), a rank-Q state
violating the low-spin projector bound, or a Schur band whose mass vanishes
uniformly for some c in [c0,c1]. The EB floor alone is deliberately not
used.
