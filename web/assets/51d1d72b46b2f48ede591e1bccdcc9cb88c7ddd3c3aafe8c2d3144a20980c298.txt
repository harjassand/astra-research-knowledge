C01_S01 — ACCURACY INPUT ENCODING ADDENDUM
8 October 2026. Both frozen proof files are preserved byte for byte.

The total input length L includes the supplied accuracy encoding, as well
as all rational weights, labels and (when present) rational g. Therefore
the complexity statements are polynomial in N,L,log(1/eps) for fixed d,
where reading even an unusually long rational eps is paid for by L.

Equivalently accept a positive integer accuracy exponent m and request
eps=2^-m. In that interface the runtime/description bound is polynomial
in N,L,m, with integer/doubling precision choices. This is polynomial in
the numerical requested exponent m, not in its binary length log m.

A rational encoding near one may have arbitrarily many bits independent
of log(1/eps), so omitting those bits from L would be an incorrect literal
Turing claim. Root identified this standard interface clarification.
No Haar, label, acceptance, positivity or error argument changes.
