# A sharper auxiliary-prime transfer, without a general-field extension

This is a proved implication from a fixed-width Hecke zero-free premise. Its mechanism is classical; the new work here is the exact discriminant accounting and, separately, the proposed width improvement in `RETUNING_PROOF.md`. No numerical fixture proves the analytic premise.

## Exact statement

Fix `0<delta<1/2`. Suppose that, for every cyclotomic number field K containing mu12 and every finite-order Hecke character chi of K, `L_K(s,chi)` has no nontrivial zero in `Re s>1-delta`.

For primes p,q with `p not dividing 12q`, there is a rational prime ell such that
\[
\ell\equiv1\pmod{12q},\qquad
p^{(\ell-1)/q}\not\equiv1\pmod\ell,
\qquad
\ell\le C_\delta\,[q(\log p+\log q+2)]^{1/\delta}.
\]
The constant depends only on delta and a fixed smooth test function. In particular, no field-dependent threshold from the theta argument enters this transfer.

## Proof and the cancellation missed by the coarse source bound

Let `K=Q(mu12q)`, `M=K(p^(1/q))`. The prime p is unramified in K; `X^q-p` is Eisenstein at a prime above p. Hence `M/K` is cyclic of degree q. Abelian zeta factorization and the assumed strip give that strip for both zeta_K and zeta_M. This uses Hecke characters over the **cyclotomic base K**, so M need not itself be cyclotomic.

Write `d_K=[K:Q]`, `d_M=[M:Q]` and absolute discriminants `D_K,D_M`. The elementary bounds used in family 142 are
\[
d_K\le12q,\quad d_M=qd_K,\quad
\log D_K\le d_K\log(12q),
\]
\[
\log D_M\le q\log D_K+
d_K\{q\log q+(q-1)\log p\}.
\]
For a fixed nonzero nonnegative smooth function phi supported in `(1,2)`, let Psi denote the smoothed weighted prime-ideal-power sum. The source's uniform explicit formula is
\[
\Psi_E(x)=x\Phi(1)+
O_\phi\bigl(x^{1-\delta}(\log D_E+d_E)\bigr).
\]
Keep the coefficient `1/q` before applying this bound. Then
\[
\Psi_K(x)-q^{-1}\Psi_M(x)
=(1-q^{-1})x\Phi(1)+O_\phi(x^{1-\delta}A_q),
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_q&=\log D_K+d_K+q^{-1}(\log D_M+d_M)\\
&\le 2\log D_K+2d_K+
d_K\{\log q+(1-1/q)\log p\}\\
&\le12q\{\log p+3\log q+2\log12+2\}\\
&\ll q(\log p+\log q+2).
\end{aligned}
\]
The source instead bounds each field's expression separately by `O(B^3)` before combining them. The `1/q` cancels the additional Kummer degree factor, and the logarithm of p stays a logarithm.

Put `A=q(log p+log q+2)` and `x=T A^(1/delta)`. The explicit-formula error divided by x is `O(T^(-delta))`; the main coefficient is at least `Phi(1)/2`. Choose the absolute T large enough to make the error less than a fixed fraction of that main term.

On the K side, higher powers and prime ideals of rational degree at least two cost
\[
O_\phi(d_K\sqrt x\log^2(2x)),
\]
and first-power degree-one ideals above `2,3,p,q` cost `O_phi(d_K log(2x))`. Dividing by x, using `d_K<=12q<=12A`, and substituting the chosen x gives negative powers of A, up to logarithms, since delta is below 1/2. Increasing the absolute T makes these errors uniformly smaller than the positive main term.

Every other first-power degree-one prime ideal of K lies over a prime `ell=1 mod 12q`. If `p^((ell-1)/q)=1 mod ell`, its residue-field polynomial `X^q-p` splits completely with distinct roots, and the corresponding contribution to Psi_K cancels exactly against its q primes in `q^-1 Psi_M`. Remaining negative terms may be discarded in an upper bound. Therefore absence of a desired auxiliary prime in `(x,2x)` contradicts the positive difference. Take `C_delta=2T`, enlarged to absorb the fixed constants in A_q.

The uniform explicit formula uses ordinary Hecke/Dedekind continuation and zero counts, independently of the theta constants. Once the fixed strip is established at every height, the latter constants and onsets are immaterial to this prime bound.

## Numerical consequences under the three premise levels

* The released theorem's width `delta=10^-6` gives exponent `1,000,000`, instead of the printed generous `20,000,000` power of B, with a sharper input expression.
* Accepting the printed comparison and probe estimates alone gives width `1/6000`, hence exponent `6000`, without any contour retuning.
* The proposed retuned width `1/50` gives
  \[
  \ell_q\ll[q(\log p+\log q+2)]^{50}.
  \]

For family 142, `B=20+(n+1)(ceil(log_2 p)+1)` and all requested primes `q<=n`. Consequently
\[
E=\max_q\ell_q\ll[n(\log p+\log n+2)]^{50}
\ll B^{50}(1+\log B)^{50}.
\]
For every fixed positive epsilon this is also `O_epsilon(B^(50+epsilon))`. The exponent 50 bounds the numerical prime, not the bit cost of the complete factorization algorithm.

The unchanged algebraic proposition still gives coarse sequential cost `O(B^500000+B^100 E^20)=O(B^500000)`; its small-characteristic branch is dominant. Improving the cutoff and reviewing the algebra are separate tasks. None of the above proves a practical factorization runtime.
