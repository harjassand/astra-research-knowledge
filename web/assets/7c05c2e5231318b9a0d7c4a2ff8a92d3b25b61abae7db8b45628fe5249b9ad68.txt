INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE CUMULANT-ENERGY RECURRENCE

For compact support, the paper defines E_m(t)=|kappa_m(t)(u)|_t^2, where the
time-dependent norm whitens every free tensor index by A_t^(-1/2). Its
covariance and cumulant SDEs are (69) and (73); the drift correction L_m is
(72). I checked the displayed Itô algebra in Section 5 against the quadratic
functional F(A,T)=<T,(A^-1)^(tensor(m-1))T>. Congruence with P=A^-1/2
reduces it to A=Id without introducing a condition-number or dimension factor.
The second variation has the five terms in (88). The cross term is bounded
by 2|<W,Sigma_H T>| <= |W|^2/2 + 2|Sigma_H T|^2. The third-cumulant matrix
bound sum_i H_i A^-1 H_i <= 8A gives, after whitening,
sum_i |Sigma_Htilde_i Ttilde|^2 <= 8(m-1)^2 E_m. Thus the source's (80)
coefficient Cm^2 follows with a universal C. This algebra checks; validity of
its SDE and integrability hypotheses remain conditional on the localization
regularity described below.

The induction combinatorics also check from the source formulas. For each
size a=2,...,m-2, there are choose(m-1,a-1) equal-permutation terms P_a.
The pointwise cumulant bound at order m-a+1 and the integrated energy bound at
order a+1 yield integral E|P_a|_t^2 dt <= 2 b_a b_(m-a+1). With
b_r=K^(r-1)((r-1)!)^2,
choose(m-1,a-1)*sqrt(b_a*b_(m-a+1))=sqrt(b_m).
There are m-3 sizes, so the L_m energy square-root is at most
sqrt(2)(m-3)sqrt(b_m). Combining with Lemma 5.2 and integral E_m<=2b_(m-1)
gives the ratio
(2C/K)*m^2/(m-1)^2 + (4/sqrt(K))*(m-3)/(m-1).
The paper's K>=max(5,9C,64) makes this at most one for every m>=3. The base
E_3(t)<=8<A_t u,u> and E<A_t>=e^-t Id imply integral E_3<=8; L_3=0.
This verifies the factorial cancellation and dimension-free recurrence
constants, conditional on the base R_3 theorem and the energy identity.

FINITE-HORIZON INVERSE-COVARIANCE REGULARITY (CONDITIONAL LEMMA)

Revision 02 gives the determinant estimate. I make its stopped-process
implications explicit here. Assume full-dimensional compact support in B(0,R),
A_0=Id, the localization equations (67)/(69), and imported isotropic
R_3<=8Id at every posterior. Before the first possible covariance singularity,
put C_i=A^-1/2 H_i A^-1/2. Equation (79) gives sum_i C_i^2<=8Id. For
Z_t=log det A_t, Itô gives
  dZ_t = sum_i tr(C_i)dB_i + [-n-(1/2)sum_i tr(C_i^2)]dt.
The drift lies in [-5n,-n] and the martingale quadratic variation rate is
sum_i tr(C_i)^2 <= n sum_i tr(C_i^2) <= 8n^2. Consequently, for each p>0
and finite T,
  sup_(t<=T) E(det A_t)^(-p) <= exp(5pnT+4p^2 n^2 T).
Since support remains in B(0,R), lambda_max(A_t)<=R^2 and therefore
  sup_(t<=T) E lambda_min(A_t)^(-p)
  <= R^(2p(n-1)) exp(5pnT+4p^2n^2T).
These constants depend strongly on n,R,T and are not used in the universal K.

For a stopping-time proof, stop when lambda_min(A)<=epsilon and localize any
other coefficient. The relative third-cumulant bound implies ||C_i||<=sqrt(8)
for each i, so ||H_i||<=sqrt(8)||A||<=sqrt(8)R^2. Up to the covariance
stopping time the logdet martingale has bounded quadratic variation on every
finite interval, hence a finite continuous limit; its drift is bounded below
by -5n. It cannot drive logdet to -infinity at finite time. Thus covariance
collapse cannot occur at finite time. The same inverse-minimum moments imply
finite moments of A^-1 on finite horizons. Since the posterior retains compact
support, every fixed raw cumulant is bounded by a finite constant depending on
R, its order, and n; each whitened cumulant, L_m, and each finite-order Itô
coefficient is consequently bounded by a polynomial in lambda_min(A)^(-1/2).
The all-p inverse moment estimate supplies finite time-integrated second
moments for the local martingale integrands and drift terms. Localization can
therefore be removed by uniform integrability for each fixed m,n,T, making the
finite-T expected Itô inequality legitimate. Q_t and c_t also have finite
finite-horizon moments from their A^-1 and A^-1/2 coefficients, so this closes
the finite-time continuation issue for compact support under the imported R_3
input.

For the infinite-time passage in Lemma 5.2, integrability of
f(t)=E E_m(t)>=0 gives a sequence T_j increasing to infinity, outside any
finite-time exceptional null set, with f(T_j)->0. Apply the now-justified
finite-T inequality on T_j. The terminal term vanishes; the drift cross term
is controlled by the two stated L2 time integrals; the E_(m+1) integral
converges monotonically. No claim that f(T)->0 for all T is needed. This
repairs the source's brief “integrability ... allows T to infinity” sentence.

This regularity argument is conditional on the source's R_3 estimate and
standard stochastic localization construction. It is not a standalone proof
of Letwin/Chen–Klartag, does not remove the need to justify the source's
compact-support approximation, and does not establish external acceptance or
priority. It closes a finite-horizon inverse-covariance/true-martingale gate
for the stated compact-support setting, with constants allowed to depend on
n,R,T only in this analytic justification.

NOVELTY / EXACT REMAINING TARGET

The primary preprint is a full KLS proof claim, not an external field consensus
or peer-reviewed resolution on the evidence collected here. Its own explicit
acknowledgement says most proofs and mathematical ideas were found by ChatGPT;
the authors note the suspension idea as theirs and say their role was mainly
to understand and improve exposition. Song–Zhang are credited with the earlier
high-order tilt-average criterion, but BKL Section 3 proves a version of it
within the preprint, so it should not be described as an unchecked imported
proof dependency. The main hard imported input in this chain is Letwin's
quadratic-form variance theorem, itself relying on the anisotropic generalized
thin-shell/moment-measure line.

For the separate target “joint-posterior acquisition beyond Fisher spectra,”
the exact unresolved theorem still needs a formal interface: supplied versus
learned observation channel; what posterior queries the learner receives;
classical or quantum memory; reconstruction/error guarantee; and charged
sample, bit, precision, and computation costs. A theorem must then bound
acquisition/reconstruction using the full joint posterior or prove an
impossibility. KLS, Fisher-eigenvalue matching, and a scale-integrated MI
inequality do not provide that codec. For the older N75/family093 route, the
specific gap remains an information estimate with the source's dT/T^2 weight
for the exact critical R^2 field under its admissible non-LSI assumptions (or
a counterexample satisfying those exact hypotheses). The S03 log-field result
uses dT/T and B positive definite, so does not close that interface.
