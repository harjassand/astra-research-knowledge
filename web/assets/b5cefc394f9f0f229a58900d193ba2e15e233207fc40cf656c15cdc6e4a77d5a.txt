LEMMA
On the maximal-spin symmetric subspace of K qubits, EVERY fully separable
Dicke-diagonal state remains fully separable under
 Gamma[(nu+1)D[J_-]+nu D[J_+]], for every nu>=0,Gamma>0 and every finite time.
This reconstructs the preservation ingredient by an explicit positive
ordinary-moment generator. It is confined to symmetric Dicke-diagonal inputs.
The thermal preservation conclusion already follows from Bassler's scoped
downward result, global X conjugation, and finite-dimensional Lie-Trotter
composition, as detailed in Section5. It is not a newly closed thermal gate.

1. FACTORIAL MOMENT INTERFACE
The positive coherent input law exists for every state in the lemma: refine
a fully separable decomposition to pure products. Because the total state
is supported in the symmetric subspace, each positive-weight pure product
must also lie in that subspace (its squared norm outside it is nonnegative
and their weighted sum is0). Exchange of two factors then forces all local
pure factors to be proportional, giving a coherent power |psi>^tensor K.
Common z-phase averaging leaves a Dicke-diagonal state unchanged and reduces
that decomposition to a positive probability law mu for x=|<e|psi>|^2.
Conversely every such phase-averaged positive law gives a fully SEP state.

A positive coherent population law mu on[0,1] has populations
 p_r=integral binom(K,r)x^r(1-x)^(K-r)dmu, r0..K.
The ordinary moments u_l=integral x^l dmu are exactly
 u_l=sum_r (r)_l/(K)_l p_r, 0<=l<=K,
where falling factorials are used and u0=1. The transform is triangular and
invertible. These are ordinary x-moments, unlike the homogeneous q_r in the
static Gaussian-Dicke Hankel interface.

2. EXACT POSITIVE DIFFERENTIAL GENERATOR
In dimensionless time Gamma t, downward jumps have lambda_r=r(K-r+1).
Since (r-1)_l-(r)_l=-l(r-1)_(l-1), direct factorial algebra gives
 dot(u_l)=-l(K-l+1)u_l+l(K-l)u_(l+1).
The final coefficient vanishes at lK; the moment system closes at degree K.
This is exactly the action on x^l of
 A_-f=[-x-(K-1)x(1-x)]f'+x^2(1-x)f''.
Upward emission is the reflected chain r to K-r, and x to1-x, giving
 A_+f=[(1-x)+(K-1)x(1-x)]f'+x(1-x)^2 f''.
Consequently the thermal combination has
 A_nu f=a(x)f'+D(x)f'',
 a(x)=nu-(2nu+1)x-(K-1)x(1-x),
 D(x)=x(1-x)(nu+x)>=0 on[0,1].                            (1)
Its action on monomials is explicitly
 A_nu x^l=nu l^2 x^(l-1)
       -l[K-l+1+nu(l+1)]x^l+l(K-l)x^(l+1).               (2)
The last coefficient is0 at lK, so (2) closes the same finite moment system
as the exact birth/death populations. At endpoints a0=nu>=0 and a1=-nu-1<0:
the differential operator has inward drift and nonnegative covariance.
If represented as an SDE in physical time, the convention is
 dX=Gamma a(X)dt+sqrt(2Gamma D(X))dW.
Thus D is the coefficient of f'', not the quadratic-variation coefficient.

3. POSITIVITY WITHOUT IMPORTING A DIFFUSION EXISTENCE THEOREM
For integer H use the grid x=i/H. At interior points define nonnegative rates
 r_+(x)=H^2 D(x)+H max(a(x),0),
 r_-(x)=H^2 D(x)+H max(-a(x),0).
At0 the downward rate is0; at1 the upward rate is0, so this is a genuine
finite Markov chain on the compact grid. Let mu_H(0) be the initial positive
law pushed to its nearest grid point. It differs in each degree<=K moment
by O(K/H). Uniform Taylor expansion for each fixed polynomial gives
 A_H f=A_nu f+O(1/H), uniformly on the grid including its endpoints.
The constants depend on fixed K,nu and the polynomial derivative bounds.

Let U_H(tau) be the vector of its first K moments in dimensionless time
tau=Gamma t. By (2),
 U_H'(tau)=C U_H(tau)+e_H(tau), ||e_H(tau)||<=c_(K,nu)/H.
Variation of constants/Gronwall gives U_H(tau) to e^(Ctau)U0 at each fixed
finite tau. Every U_H(tau) is a positive probability moment vector on[0,1]. Their
limit stays in the compact convex hull of(1,x,...,x^K), hence is also the
moment vector of a positive probability measure. The factorial transform
then identifies its Bernstein populations with the EXACT collective
birth/death solution, by uniqueness of the finite linear ODE. This proves
the lemma for all K,nu,t; no finite numerical test is extrapolated.

This argument is an existence/positivity proof. The grid resolution limit,
its error constants and exp(||C||t) have not been implemented or charged as
a sampler. It does not supply a polynomial-time numerical dynamics compiler.
K0 is scalar and needs no Markov construction.

4. CHECK OF THE STATIONARY COHERENT LAW
For nu>0, the zero-flux density satisfies (D P)'=aP. Since
 a-D'=-(K+2)x(1-x), a stationary positive density is proportional to
 (nu+x)^(-K-2).
This agrees exactly with the geometric Dicke-law density derived separately
in COLLECTIVE_STATIONARY_PAIR_REPRESENTATION.txt. Agreement is a structural
identity, not proof of an unimplemented sampler or a source-priority claim.
For nu0 the ground-point measure is stationary.

5. DIRECT IMPLICATION OF THE SCOPED PRIOR RESULT
Bassler arXiv:2504.13646v3, SectionsIV andVIII, states downward SEP preservation
for symmetric Dicke-diagonal SEP inputs at every finite K and time. Let
U=X^tensor K. Then U J_- Udagger=J_+, and conjugation by U maps this class
of SEP states into itself. Upward evolution e^(s D[J_+]) consequently preserves
the same class. For fixed finite K, both generators are bounded matrices and
 e^(tau[(nu+1)D[J_-]+nu D[J_+]])
 =lim_(n to infinity)
   [e^(tau(nu+1)D[J_-]/n) e^(tau nu D[J_+]/n)]^n.
Every finite composition preserves SEP, symmetric support and Dicke
diagonality. The fully SEP set is compact and therefore closed in finite
dimension, so the limit preserves them too. The warm preservation claim
therefore already follows from the prior scoped downward theorem. The
explicit generator/grid argument above gives a self-contained reconstruction
and supporting positive moment representation, with no dynamics acquisition
or priority claim. This implication was pointed out by root before freeze.
