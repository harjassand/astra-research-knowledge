IMPLEMENTED CANCELLATION LEMMA

Let S be supported by mu, u in S, and S'=S-u. Every local factor has fixed
degree two. If v lies in a different factor from u, S'+v has degree one in
u's factor and degree three in v's factor, and its mu coefficient is zero.
If v is in u's factor, all other factors cancel from

  z_v mu(S'+v) / sum_w z_w mu(S'+w).

Only the at-most-three local reinsertion choices remain. Their exact weights
are z_v times the corresponding entry of u's four-variable table. A pinning
restriction merely removes forbidden local choices. If u is forced in,
only self-reinsertion remains; skipping this self loop is exact.

The nu side has the analogous fixed-quota cancellation. A physical wire has
at most two choices. For a dummy deletion u, the candidates are u plus all
currently unselected, non-excluded dummy coordinates. Their weights are z_v.
A binary sum tree stores the latter set, excluding all currently selected
coordinates. First choose reinsertion u with probability
 z_u/(z_u+tree_total); otherwise descend the tree by exact rational left-mass
probabilities. A move u->v makes the two leaf updates eligible(u)=z_u and
eligible(v)=0. A field update changes just one eligible leaf. Cost O(log N).
No global mu coefficient product is evaluated in a production step.

Both base states have together N present copies. Choose one uniformly. For
the chosen copy u, do a deletion/resampling attempt with probability 1/2 if
u is in the other set, t/2 otherwise. Reinsert using the cancelled weights,
including u itself. This is EXACTLY the lazy chain

    P = I - [t/(2N)] L_q,

where q is source Eq.(19). The non-self q rate is
 t^(-1[u in other]) z_v mu(S-u+v)/sum_w z_w mu(S-u+w).
The factor t_v is NOT inserted into the reinsertion law. Detailed balance
holds because the deletion t_u^(-1[u in other]) cancels the departing overlap
penalty. In particular adding t_v by intuition would implement a different
chain; this exact detail was checked against a literal global-oracle version.

Support is preserved: every candidate has a positive coefficient; every
denominator is positive because self-reinsertion is supported. State mutation
occurs after all random choices succeed. A coin-cap abort raises TapeAbort;
the enclosing sample/count must abort wholly, rather than return a biased
partial state. Production code does not claim a whole-count wrapper yet.

COMPLEMENT-SYMMETRY LEMMA — COMPLETE DERIVATION

Assume mu(S)=mu(U\S), nu(T)=nu(U\T), both supported on N/2-subsets. At z=1,
the soft law is
  pi_t(S,T) proportional to mu(S)nu(T)t^|S intersect T|.
Under (S,T)->(U\S,U\T), the overlap becomes
  N-|S|-|T|+|S intersect T|=|S intersect T|.
Thus the involution preserves every soft weight, and swaps the zero-copy
and two-copy events at every coordinate. Their probabilities p0,p2 agree.

The imported Lemma5.5 supplies a gap-one continuous exchange chain uniformly
over unequal penalties and positive fields. Here is the required consequence,
reconstructed explicitly rather than treating Theorem5.1 as a black box.
For any coordinate u with p0p2>0, remove its overlap penalty and multiply its
field by s=sqrt(t p0/p2). The resulting copy-count probabilities satisfy
  (q0,q1,q2) proportional to (p0,s p1,p0), so q0=q2<=1/2.
The chain removes a unique present copy at total rate <=1 and cannot go
directly from two copies to zero. For the absent-coordinate indicator g,
its Dirichlet form is at most q1. The gap-one inequality therefore gives
 q0(1-q0)<=q1, whence q1/q0>=1/2 and p0p2<=4t p1^2.
Cases p0p2=0 are immediate. Since p0=p2,
 p0+p2=2sqrt(p0p2)<=4sqrt(t)p1<=4sqrt(t).
Union over N coordinates gives
 P(T!=U\S)<=4N sqrt(t)=1/4 when t=1/(256N^2).
Consequently the hard event has probability >=3/4 without any acquired field.
Square roots here are proof devices; the actual fields are exactly one.

Every pair gate is complement-invariant on its four variables. For a field
gate c_v=0 implies u=v, and every monomial has the same coefficient as its
four-variable complement. The product mu is consequently complement-invariant.
Each physical wire's quota is half its size, and the dummy quota f is half
2f; their nu factor is complement-invariant. This proves the XXZ subclass
claim for any circuit size and ordering. It covers gamma=+-alpha, zero X
fields, and inactive qubits. It does not cover arbitrary c_v!=0. Pinnings
used later in soft normalizer self-reduction need not preserve the involution;
their mixing still uses the imported uniform gap, with the unchanged z=1.

COUNTING REDUCTION AND EXPLICIT CAPS

Let K=2N, 0<rho<=1 and 0<delta<=1/4. Put alpha=rho/(128N). In each of the
K membership stages, draw M=ceil[2alpha^-2 ln(8K/delta)] independent
conditional soft samples, each within TV alpha/2. Estimate the next-bit
frequency and choose its empirical majority. Denote the selected empirical
frequency by phat_j>=1/2. Retain one sampled witness with that chosen bit;
it always exists and supplies the next conditioned supported state.

For each stage, Hoeffding bounds fluctuation by alpha/2 except with
probability <=delta/(4K). Sampling bias is at most alpha/2. Thus the true
selected conditional probability pj satisfies |pj-phat_j|<=alpha and
pj>=1/2-alpha>=1/4. Summing the logarithmic error gives
 |sum_j log(pj/phat_j)|<=K*4alpha=rho/16.
For the final supported state X, the exact telescoping identity is
 Z_soft=W(X)/product_j pj. Hence the empirical denominator estimates Z_soft
within exp(+-rho/16), with total statistical failure <=delta/4.

Separately estimate p_hard with
 Mc=ceil[2(64/rho)^2 ln(8/delta)] independent unconditional soft samples,
each within TV rho/128. Hoeffding plus bias gives absolute error <=rho/64
except with probability <=delta/4. Since p_hard>=3/4, its relative error
is <=rho/48. Multiply this estimate by the soft normalizer. Its total
multiplicative error is inside (1+-rho) for rho<=1. There is no inverse
thermal trace, prefix probability, or projection rarity in these sample caps.
All bitwise-conditioned measures retain fixed degrees and are LC by
derivative/zero-coordinate closure, with coefficient range no larger than R.

For every such conditional, source Lemma5.5 gives discrete gap >=t/(2N),
and pi_min>=t^(N/2)/(4^N R) at z=1. Define
 H=2N+ceil(log2 R)+(N/2)ceil(log2(1/t)).
Then ln(1/pi_min)<=H. The standard reversible L2 bound gives
 TV(P^J(X,.),pi)<=0.5*pi(X)^(-1/2)*exp[-Jt/(2N)].
A sufficient directly computable integer is
 J=ceil[(2N/t)(H+ceil(log2(1/theta))+1)],
with theta=rho/(256N), small enough for both sample stages. Binary logs
are computed by exact doubling comparisons and upper-bound natural logs.
J=O(N^3(H+log(N/rho))). The number Q of soft samples is
O(N^3 rho^-2 log(N/delta)). Every production transition uses at most
2ceil(log2 N)+2 nontrivial rational binary choices. Let Nc be the actual
deterministic bound Q*J*[2ceil(log2 N)+2]. Cap each binary choice at
r=ceil(log2(4Nc/delta)) fair bits. One unresolved coin has probability
<=2^-r. Aborting the whole count on any unresolved choice costs <=delta/4.
All loops, random tape length, and rational output sizes are bounded even
on bad histories. Pseudorandom seeded tests are reproducibility tools, not
a claim that a computer PRNG supplies mathematical independent fair bits.

The local component uses O(log N) rational operations per transition.

factor here: balance work becomes O(N^9 B^2 log N log B), rather than the
generic O(N^10 B^2 log B), before bit costs. This is an implementation
structure saving, not a new gap theorem. More substantially, symmetry removes
that entire balancing stage and gives the displayed O(N^6 H rho^-2 polylog)
count arithmetic bound, with the explicit logarithms above.

Let ell bound local positive rational table encoding lengths. At z=1 the
tree has integer masses <=2f, and local candidate ratios involve <=3
ell-bit entries. No global N*ell-bit coefficient occurs per transition.
With conservative schoolbook rational costs, one may upper-bound the full
count by the arithmetic count times
 O(r(ell+log N+r)^3), plus Q initializations at
 O(N*poly(N ell+NlogN)) bit work, and the final rational products at
 polynomial cost in N ell+Nlog M+Nlog(1/t).
This deliberately loose bit bound does not call rationals unit-cost objects.

tree sums; source common-base fields permit the sharper O(K_updates logN)
encoding because total exponent absolute value is capped. That improvement
does not silently extend to an opaque real-field oracle.
