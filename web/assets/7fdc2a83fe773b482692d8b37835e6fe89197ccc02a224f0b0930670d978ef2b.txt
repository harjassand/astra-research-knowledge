SU3 WALL-UNIFORM POSTERIOR MOMENTS AND A WEIGHTED ARBITRARY-CODE CONVERSE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; independent audit requested.
This memo proves the converse alone. The subsequently derived all-Q
profile and ordinary-energy separation are in WALL_PROFILE_COROLLARY.txt.

Main result. Let a,b>=1, N=a+b, and supply V_(a,b). For any fixed regular
traceless Hermitian fundamental H, there is a fixed explicit h_*(H)>0
such that, at every SINGLE supplied h in (0,h_*], arbitrary legal blind
full-Gibbs recovery through maximum quantum branch Q obeys a converse
for the weighted energy

  E_wall(A)=E_T(A)/N^2+E_S(A)/(N sqrt(C_S)).                 (0.1)

The constants are independent of a/b, N and Q. This is stronger than
the ordinary Casimir self-score when the second term is larger. It
does not establish that the rank minimum of (0.1) differs in order
from Lambda_T(Q)/N^2. That comparison now follows separately from the
internally audited count and harmonic ordinary-energy comparator; see
WALL_PROFILE_COROLLARY Section6. It is not inferred from this converse alone.
The proof also gives a finite exact divisibility theorem for every
second-adjoint Gibbs Taylor moment, with a uniform analytic remainder.
It uses two orbit directions at ONE temperature, not two temperatures.

1. INTERFACE, EXACT TENSOR ALGEBRA AND DEFINITIONS

Use the notation/normalization of ../regular_su3/RESULT.txt:
Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2, q=8, T_A=d pi(t_A),

  D_A=d_ABC T_BT_C, C2=(a^2+ab+b^2+3a+3b)/3,
  C3=(a-b)(2a+b+3)(a+2b+3)/18, alpha=C3/C2,
  S_A=(D_A-alpha T_A)/N,
  Delta=C2(C2/3+1/4)-C3^2/C2, C_S=Delta/N^2,
  P6=ab(a+2)(b+2)(a+b+1)(a+b+3)=12 C2 Delta.                (1.1)

All the following are exact operator identities:

  sum T_A^2=C2 I, sum D_A^2=Omega I,
  Omega=C2(C2/3+1/4), sum T_A D_A=C3 I,
  sum S_A^2=C_S I,
  Tr(T_AS_B)=0,
  Tr(T_AT_B)=d C2 delta_AB/8,
  Tr(S_AS_B)=d C_S delta_AB/8.                              (1.2)

For a,b>=1 the adjoint multiplicity in End(V) is exactly two and T,S
are its complete orthogonal Hermitian embeddings. On either pure wall
a=0 or b=0, Delta=0 and D_A=alpha T_A for every A. The last assertion
follows from sum(D_A-alpha T_A)^2=Delta I and Hermiticity; it is not
inferred from a zero cubic eigenvalue. The independent Gram/PBW proof
of (1.1)--(1.2) is preserved in the regular_su3 packet.

Write H=sum x_A t_A, r=||x||>0, T_H=x_A T_A, B=T_H/N and
tau_(h,g)=U_g exp(hB) U_g^dagger/Z_h. Define y by

  H^2-(Tr_fund H^2/3)I=sum y_A t_A,
  y_A=(1/2)d_ABC x_Bx_C, Y=||y||,
  sigma=||y-(x dot y/r^2)x||.                              (1.3)

Regular means H has three distinct eigenvalues. Then sigma>0: if y
were proportional to x, all three eigenvalues of H would satisfy the
same quadratic, impossible with three distinct values. In particular
Tr H^3 may be zero; nonzero cubic trace is NOT required.

The operational interface is one supplied carrier, same-carrier output,
full half-trace distance, arbitrary free classical labels, maximum
quantum branch Q, no entanglement or quantum side channel. The encoder
is blind to g. A single fixed h can be supplied to the code. A legal
code Phi has composite Kraus A_i of rank<=Q and sum A_i^dagger A_i=I,
so sum ||A_i||_HS^2=d. Rank-Kraus/free-classical memory equivalence is
established prior work (Bisio et al. 2012); it is not a new claim here.

Set E_T(A)=sum||[T_A,A]||^2 and E_S(A)=sum||[S_A,A]||^2.
For interior carriers C_S>0, define

  Lambda_wall(Q)=min_(rank A<=Q,||A||HS=1) E_wall(A).         (1.4)

This is an all-matrix minimum. A positive minimizer exists: the
two-modulus argument applies to the combined Hermitian generator family
{T_A/N,S_A/(N sqrt(C_S))^(1/2)}, whose sum of squares is scalar.
This observation gives no efficient optimizer or matching Gibbs upper.

2. A CENTRAL-CHARACTER DIVISIBILITY LEMMA

Fix ANY traceless fundamental Hermitian Z=sum z_A t_A. For k>=0 let

  u_k=Haar_Ad(T_Z T_H^k),
  v_k=Haar_Ad(D_Z T_H^k).                                  (2.1)

Haar averaging is over the finite PBW filtration subspace containing
the word, so (2.1) is a well-defined universal enveloping algebra
element. It is invariant under all adjoint generators, hence central.
Its filtrations are at most k+1 and k+2, respectively. Its scalar in
V_(a,b) equals the normalized trace of the corresponding word. Write
these central-character polynomials U_k(a,b),V_k(a,b).

For completeness, two facts about their polynomials can be proved
directly. In PBW order (lowering,Cartan,raising), evaluation of a
central word on a Verma highest vector retains only its Cartan term.
The result is a polynomial in a,b of degree at most the filtration.
It is shifted-Weyl invariant. For a simple root i and every integer
lambda(H_i)=n>=0, the vector f_i^(n+1)v_lambda is a nonzero highest
vector of weight s_i(lambda+rho)-rho. Indeed the sl2 identity kills
it under e_i, and [e_j,f_i]=0 for j!=i. A central word acts on this
submodule by both central characters, so the character polynomials
agree on infinitely many hyperplanes lambda(H_i)=n. Their difference
is the zero polynomial. This proves invariance for both simple
reflections and hence the Weyl group, without an assumed trace limit.

Consider the polynomial

  P_k(a,b)=C2(a,b)V_k(a,b)-C3(a,b)U_k(a,b).                 (2.2)

It has degree<=k+4 and is shifted-Weyl invariant. On a=0 or b=0 the
exact wall identity D_Z=alpha T_Z gives P_k=0 for infinitely many
integer points, hence identically along each whole affine line.
Put u=a+1,v=b+1. The shifted Weyl group acts by
s1(u,v)=(-u,u+v), s2(u,v)=(u+v,-v). The Weyl orbit of u=1 and v=1
is the six distinct lines u=+/-1, v=+/-1, u+v=+/-1. Their product
in a,b is P6. Each linear factor divides P_k; distinct factors are
coprime in the polynomial ring. Therefore

  P_k=P6 R_k,       deg R_k<=k-2.                         (2.3)

For k=0,1 this means P_k=0. For every interior carrier,

  Tr(S_Z B^k)/d=12 Delta R_k/(N^(k+1))
                =12 C_S R_k/N^(k-1).                    (2.4)

The quotient depends linearly on Z and homogeneously of degree k on H.
No numerator division at a wall is needed in the proof.

3. EXPLICIT UNIFORM ANALYTIC BOUND ON THE QUOTIENT

This step supplies the uniformity missing from an ordinary small-h
Taylor statement. Fix k>=3, ell=k-2, L=max(N,ell). On any integer
grid carrier with L<=a',b'<=2L,

  C2<=8L^2, sqrt(Omega)<=5L^2, sqrt(C2)<=3L,
  P6>=4L^6.                                               (3.1)

Scalar sums of squares in (1.2) imply
||T_Z||<=||z||sqrt(C2), ||D_Z||<=||z||sqrt(Omega), and
|C3|<=sqrt(C2 Omega). Normalized traces of words are bounded by their
operator norm. Consequently, at every such integer grid point,

  |R_k|<=20||z||(3r)^k L^ell.                             (3.2)

There is an elementary interpolation bound extending this to the
actual a,b<=N. Set t=floor(L/ell)>=L/(2ell), and choose ell+1 integer
nodes L+jt, j=0,...,ell in [L,2L]. At a point w in [0,N], every node
distance is <=2L. Each Lagrange cardinal polynomial has absolute value
at most (4ell)^ell/[j!(ell-j)!]. Summing gives a one-variable Lebesgue
bound (8ell)^ell/ell!<=(8e)^ell. Interpolate successively in a,b;
R_k has degree<=ell in each variable. Thus

  |R_k(a,b)|<=20||z||(3r)^k (64e^2)^ell L^ell.              (3.3)

Set A0=64e^2, U0=3er A0=192e^3 r. Since L/N<=ell and
ell^ell/k!<=e^k/k^2, (2.4)--(3.3) give the norm-convergent bound

 |Tr(S_Z B^k)/d| h^k/k!
   <=(240/A0^2)||z||(C_S/N)(U0 h)^k/k^2.                 (3.4)

For 0<=h<=1/(2U0), the sum of (3.4) over k>=3 is at most

  ||z||(C_S/N) D_S h^3,
  D_S=160 U0^3/(3A0^2)=92160 e^5 r^3.                    (3.5)

These constants are deliberately loose but fixed before a,b,N,Q.
The argument applies for k larger than N; factorial summability is
explicit, so it does not interchange an uncontrolled large-word limit.

The exact quadratic moment is obtained from
Tr(S_A T_B T_C)/d=3 Delta d_ABC/(40N). Its symmetric tensor proof is
given in ../regular_su3/RESULT.txt Section11. By (1.3), the raw vector
f_S,A(h)=Tr(S_A exp(hB))/d therefore satisfies

  f_S(h)=(C_S/N)[(3/40)h^2 y+R_S(h)],
  ||R_S(h)||<=D_S h^3, 0<=h<=1/(2U0).                   (3.6)

This is uniform all the way to either wall and includes b fixed or
b=N^beta. A naive ||S|| exponential remainder would only give an
O(sqrt(C_S)h^3) bound and would NOT establish (3.6).

4. ORDINARY MOMENT AND A SINGLE-TEMPERATURE SEPARATING DIRECTION

For N=a+b>=1, N^2/4<=C2<=4N^2/3. Hence ||B||<=2r and N/sqrt(C2)<=2.
The raw first vector f_T,A(h)=Tr(T_A exp(hB))/d obeys

  f_T(h)=(C2/N)[h x/8+R_T(h)],
  ||R_T(h)||<=8r^2 h^2,    0<=h<=log(2)/(2r).            (4.1)

Indeed, normalized trace/HS duality and ||T_Z||<=||z||sqrt(C2) bound
the remainder by sqrt(C2)[exp(2rh)-1-2rh]. The exact first term is
Tr(T_A T_H)/d=C2 x_A/8. The displayed constant follows after scaling
by N/C2. Write v(h)=8N f_T(h)/(h C2)=x+w(h),
||w(h)||<=64r^2h.

Set a_S=3/40 and fix

 h_* = min {1/(2U0), log(2)/(2r),
             sigma/(256rY), a_S sigma/(4D_S)} >0.          (4.2)

For 0<h<=h_*, define the real supplied testing direction

  z(h)=y-[y dot v(h)/||v(h)||^2]v(h).                     (4.3)

It is nonzero, f_T dot z=0, and
sigma/2<=||z||<=Y, z dot y=||z||^2. To verify the lower bound,
||v-x||<=r sigma/(4Y)<=r/4. The two rank-one directional projections
differ in operator norm by at most 2||v-x||/r, so projecting y changes
y_perp by at most sigma/2.

The Gibbs normalization zeta=Z_h/d is in [1,2]: its lower bound follows
from convexity and Tr B=0, and the upper follows from ||B||<=2r. Define
m_T=f_T/zeta, m_S=f_S/zeta. Combining (3.6),(4.2),(4.3) gives

  m_T dot z=0,
  m_S dot z >= (C_S/N)h^2||z|| a_S sigma/8 >0.             (4.4)

Thus two adjoint directions can separate the two posterior components
at a SINGLE fixed temperature. The direction is supplied to a converse
witness; no acquisition of the unknown g or physical measurement of
the witness by the code is asserted.

5. EXACT POSTERIOR AND NONUNITAL SCORE

Let z(g)=R(g)z. Adjoint Schur orthogonality and the complete T,S frame
give exactly

  M_z,A=int z_A(g)tau_(h,g)dg
       =(1/d)[(m_T dot z/C2)T_A+(m_S dot z/C_S)S_A]
       =(m_S dot z)/(d C_S) S_A.                         (5.1)

For any TP code define S_z,g=sum z_A(g)S_A and the legal effect

  E_g=(I+S_z,g/(||z||sqrt(C_S)))/2.                       (5.2)

It is an effect because sum S_A^2=C_S I gives the required norm bound.
Trace preservation cancels its identity term, and (5.1) implies

 int Tr E_g(tau_g-Phi(tau_g))dg
   =(m_S dot z)/(2d||z|| C_S^(3/2))
                          sum_A Tr S_A(S_A-Phi(S_A)).    (5.3)

For arbitrary TP Kraus A_i, expansion using sum S_A^2=C_S I gives

  sum_A Tr S_A(S_A-Phi(S_A))=(1/2)sum_i E_S(A_i).          (5.4)

The two positive commutator-square terms both become
C_S sum_i||A_i||^2=d C_S. This requires trace preservation, not
unitality. Half-trace error bounds (5.3) from above, so (4.4) yields

  e(Phi;h)>=(3 sigma h^2/1280)
                     sum_i E_S(A_i)/(d N sqrt(C_S)).      (5.5)

This applies to Haar-average risk as well as worst orbit error and to
arbitrary classical labels/branch decoders. It does not assume a
self-adjoint code or a covariant code in the converse.

6. COMBINED RANK CONVERSE AND THE UNRESOLVED UPPER

The independent Gibbs self-score, which needs no posterior/frame,
continues to give

 e(Phi;h)>=[h^2 r^2 exp(-12rh)/16]
                             sum_i E_T(A_i)/(d N^2).     (6.1)

Let c_T(h)=h^2r^2exp(-12rh)/16 and c_S(h)=3sigma h^2/1280.
Equations (5.5),(6.1), the rank<=Q Kraus constraint and
sum_i||A_i||^2=d prove

  e*_Q(h)>=(1/2)min{c_T(h),c_S(h)} Lambda_wall(Q).          (6.2)

Uniform constants for any compact [h0,h1] subset (0,h_*] follow by
replacing h^2 by h0^2 and exp(-12rh) by exp(-12rh1). This already
holds for each supplied h separately; unknown-temperature code is
not needed to obtain the bound.

For comparison, the exact two-adjoint filter block gives for EVERY
Hermitian positive filter F, S_F=Tr F^2 and E_TS(F)=sum Tr[S_A,F]^dagger
[T_A,F],

 ||D_F(B)||1 <= 2r E_T(F)/N^2
                          +r |E_TS(F)|/(N sqrt(C_S)).    (6.3)

The ordered exponential proof therefore gives a legal covariant full-
Gibbs upper of the same fixed-constant order in

 [E_T(F)/N^2+|E_TS(F)|/(N sqrt(C_S))]/S_F.                 (6.4)

Cauchy bounds |E_TS|<=sqrt(E_T E_S); this does not by itself make
(6.4) comparable to (0.1). Nor is the minimizer of (0.1) acquired.

The generic-filter witness in GENERIC_FILTER_OBSTRUCTION.txt is
consistent with (5.5): its second energy is not small and its actual
Gibbs error is order1/N. It is NOT an optimal rank-profile witness.
Closing the wall bridge now requires a weighted rank profile and a
matching actual code, or an order gap between Lambda_wall(Q) and
Lambda_T(Q)/N^2. Neither is established here. Pure-wall C_S=0 cases
retain their separate symmetric-carrier theorem and should not be
inserted into denominators in (0.1),(5.2) or (6.3).
