VERDICT
No constant, summation-index, or open-boundary error found in these three claims. Under the stated open chain and free Gibbs definitions, the displayed bounds follow. The proof is internally complete for this scope; this is not external validation or a novelty finding.

SETUP AND INPUTS
Sites are i=1,...,L-1 (N=L-1), J,tau>0, |lambda|<=B, beta=tau L^2, and R is a fixed positive integer. The supplied free one-particle data are
  epsilon_k=4J(1-cos(pi k/L)), phi_k(i)=sqrt(2/L) sin(pi k i/L),
  f_k=(1+exp(tau(L^2 epsilon_k-lambda)))^(-1), 1<=k<=L-1.
The unknown lambda is used only through the supplied bound B. No interaction entries or further parameter acquisition enter these three estimates. Write c0=exp(tau B) sum_{k>=1} exp(-8 tau J k^2), c2=exp(tau B) sum_{k>=1} k^2 exp(-8 tau J k^2), and A=8 pi^2 c0 c2 sum_{r=1}^R r^2.

1. FORBIDDEN-PAIR MASS: CONSTANTS AND BOUNDARIES
The elementary cosine inequalities on [0,pi] give
  8J k^2 <= L^2 epsilon_k <= 2J pi^2 k^2.
Thus 0<=f_k<=exp(tau B-8 tau J k^2), including every finite-chain mode k=1,...,L-1. In particular sum f_k<=c0 and sum k^2 f_k<=c2.

For a valid pair j=i+r, the DPP two-point inclusion probability is the 2x2 determinant of C. Cauchy-Binet expands it as
  P_ij=E_0[n_i n_j]=sum_{k<ell} f_k f_ell |phi_k(i)phi_ell(j)-phi_ell(i)phi_k(j)|^2.
Writing alpha=pi k i/L, beta=pi ell i/L, the unnormalized sine determinant can be rearranged as
  sin(alpha)[sin(beta+pi ell r/L)-sin(beta)]
  -sin(beta)[sin(alpha+pi k r/L)-sin(alpha)].
Since |sin x-sin y|<=|x-y| and |sin|<=1, its magnitude is at most pi r(k+ell)/L. The two factors sqrt(2/L) in the modes therefore give
  |phi_k(i)phi_ell(i+r)-phi_ell(i)phi_k(i+r)|^2
       <=4 pi^2 r^2(k+ell)^2/L^4.
Also
  sum_{k<ell} f_k f_ell(k+ell)^2
  <=2 sum_{k<ell}f_k f_ell(k^2+ell^2)
  <=2 (sum_k k^2 f_k)(sum_ell f_ell)<=2c2 c0.
Hence P_{i,i+r}<=8 pi^2 r^2 c0 c2/L^4.

Boundary count: for fixed r, valid sites are i=1,...,L-1-r, exactly L-1-r of them when r<=L-2, and none otherwise. Replacing this count by L is a safe overestimate. Therefore
  sum_{r=1}^{min(R,L-2)} sum_{i=1}^{L-1-r} P_{i,i+r}
       <=8 pi^2 c0 c2 L^(-3) sum_{r=1}^R r^2 = a_L.
Pointwise Q<=sum_{r,i}n_i n_{i+r}, so delta=Tr(rho0 Q)<=a_L. The proof bounds the union probability by the expected number of forbidden pairs; it does not identify those two quantities.

2. M-WEIGHTED FORBIDDEN-PAIR MASS
For each valid pair, number conservation gives
  E_0[M n_i n_j]=2P_ij+sum_{z notin{i,j}} E_0[n_i n_j n_z]
               =2P_ij+sum_z det C_{i,j,z}.
The covariance C is positive semidefinite. For its 2x2 principal block A=C_{i,j} and the appended coordinate z, PSD Schur complementation yields det C_{i,j,z}<=det(C_{i,j}) C_zz. If A is singular, the same inequality follows by continuity (or the generalized Schur complement). Since sum_z C_zz=Tr C=sum_k f_k<=c0,
  E_0[M n_i n_j]<=(2+c0)P_ij.
Pointwise M Q<=sum_{r,i} M n_i n_{i+r}; summing the last inequality over valid pairs and using the pair-sum bound above gives
  q_M=Tr(rho0 M Q)<=(2+c0) sum_{r,i}P_{i,i+r}<=(2+c0)a_L.
Thus the factor 2 is the pair's own two occupied sites, and c0 accounts for all possible third sites. The use of sum_z C_zz over all sites only enlarges the z!=i,j sum.

3. FREE KINETIC INCREMENT AFTER COHERENT PROJECTION
Under open-chain Jordan-Wigner, K=dGamma(h) with one-particle eigenvalues epsilon_k in [0,8J]. Consequently 0<=K<=8J M. The free rho0 commutes with K (both are diagonal in the sine-mode occupation basis), so K rho0 is positive semidefinite. For projection Q,
  Tr(rho0 QK)=Tr(Q K rho0)>=0,
  Tr(rho0 KQ)=Tr(Q rho0 K)=Tr(Q K rho0)>=0.
These signs do not require [Q,K]=0. Expanding P=I-Q,
  P K P=K-QK-KQ+QKQ.
Congruence of K<=8JM by Q gives QKQ<=8J QMQ=8J M Q, where [Q,M]=0 because both are diagonal in site occupations. Therefore
  Tr(rho0 P K P)<=Tr(rho0 K)+8J q_M.
With p=Tr(rho0 P)=1-delta>0 and sigma=P rho0 P/p, cyclicity gives Tr(sigma K)=Tr(rho0 P K P)/p. It follows that
  Tr(sigma K)-Tr(rho0 K)
       <=[delta Tr(rho0 K)+8J q_M]/p.
The mode upper bound L^2 epsilon_k<=2J pi^2 k^2 gives L^2 Tr(rho0 K)<=2J pi^2 c2. Multiplying by L^2 recovers exactly
  L^2[Tr(sigma K)-Tr(rho0 K)]
       <=[delta(2J pi^2 c2)+8J L^2 q_M]/p.
No commutation of P or Q with K is assumed. With fixed parameters and R, the displayed input bounds imply delta,q_M=O(L^-3), so the right side is O(L^-1) after the L^2 scaling.
