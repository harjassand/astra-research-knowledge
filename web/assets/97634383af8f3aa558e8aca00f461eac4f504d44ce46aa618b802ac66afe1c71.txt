Primary-source prior-art audit — 2026-10-07

VERDICT
The sharp complete constant 2 would improve existing dimension- and length-independent matrix functional-calculus composition bounds. It does not newly make those bounds independent of product length. That feature already follows from the complete Crouzeix–Palencia bound C=1+sqrt(2) and classical Paulsen similarity. Scalar GMRES/Arnoldi improvements to 2/4 are already explicitly in September 2026 primary literature. All deductions using the OpenAI endpoint remain conditional on its theorem being correct; this audit did not verify its proof.

EXACT INTERFACE AND DERIVATION
Let K=closure W(A). For auxiliary coefficient matrices F_j in M_d(R(K)), evaluation rho_A^(d) is an algebra homomorphism:
 F_j[A]=sum_k A^k tensor B_jk,
 F_m[A]...F_1[A]=(F_m...F_1)[A].
Thus a complete C-spectral-set estimate, applied once to the ordered product, gives
 ||F_m[A]...F_1[A]|| <= C sup_K ||F_m(z)...F_1(z)||
                         <= C product_j sup_K ||F_j(z)||.
The B_jk may fail to commute with each other: the order is preserved. Compatible rectangular factors follow by block embedding. This is an immediate derivation from old theory, even if no paper packages exactly this product corollary. C=2 instead of 1+sqrt(2) reduces the universal prefactor by about 17.2%; the exponent never needed to be m.

Paulsen's 1984 theorem gives one S with cond(S)=||rho_A||cb and conjugated rho_A completely contractive. Amplification uses S tensor I_d, independent of the functions, coefficient size, or number of factors. The corresponding common quadratic metric H=S* S satisfies cond(H)<=C^2, and every sup_K||F_j||<=q_j yields F_j[A]*(H tensor I)F_j[A]<=q_j^2(H tensor I). For all q_j<=q<1 this gives uniform switched-product decay <=C q^m in this restricted common-generator family. The endpoint improves metric condition 5.828... to 4, rather than enabling the common metric for the first time.

COMPOSABLE APPROXIMATION
Set a_i=sup_K||F_i||, b_i=sup_K||P_i||, epsilon_i=sup_K||F_i-P_i||. Ordered telescoping in the function algebra gives
 ||product_i F_i[A]-product_i P_i[A]||
 <= C sum_j epsilon_j (product_{i>j} a_i)(product_{i<j} b_i).
This is the same old one-constant mechanism. Endpoint2 sharpens C. It does not eliminate the ordinary accumulation of pointwise approximation error, and it does not cover arbitrary perturbations outside the evaluation algebra.

EXACT OBSTRUCTIONS TO BROADER CONTROL CLAIMS
(1) Arbitrary inserted contractions acting on the base space need not commute with A and are outside matrix/tensor-coefficient evaluation. Let A=2E12, B=E21 in M_2. Then W(A)=closed unit disk and ||B||=1, but (AB)^m=2^m E11. A claimed length-independent2 bound for such arbitrary insertions is false.
(2) Separate numerical-radius-small generators do not acquire a common similarity: T1=cE12, T2=cE21, 1<c<2, each has numerical radius c/2<1 and spectral radius0, yet (T1T2)^m=c^(2m)E11. A common similarity making both contractions would contradict this growth. The common-generator hypothesis is essential. These are direct exact algebraic counterexamples from this audit.
(3) A norm bound <=2 for a single function does not establish temporal small-gain hypotheses or causal feedback well-posedness. Robust-control applications need the uncertainty/interconnection to remain in the same evaluated function algebra and separately need their usual causal/stability interface. No general switched-system breakthrough follows.

STRUCTURAL MANUSCRIPT SCOPE
The supplied OpenAI paper's Theorem1.2 makes the conformal disk image f(A) similar to an operator-norm contraction with condition number<=2. It is not merely a numerical-radius contraction theorem. Its Proposition2.1 explicitly attributes the disk-resolvent positive representation to Delyon–Delyon and the common compression to dilation theory. Metric attainment for finite rho(f(A))<1 follows from finite SDP compactness independently of the endpoint. The new load-bearing assertion is the universal sharp condition-number bound. The structural statements assume a finite A and an analytic convex neighborhood; the paper does not assert limiting similarity/density on K in its direct outer-approximation passage.

RESEARCH DIRECTION ASSESSMENT
An application paper could still contribute a concrete robust-control model within this common-generator tensor interface, a useful certified conformal/metric algorithm with costs and numerical error, or a sharper application-specific bound. Rebranding one C versus C^m as newly unlocked composition is not supported by this prior art. Enlarging to arbitrary noncommuting base-space insertions or independent generators is directly falsified above. This was a targeted primary-source check, not an exhaustive novelty certification.
