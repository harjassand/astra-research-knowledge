3. HOMOGENEOUS CONTACT AND AN INTEGRABLE SHORT-TIME HEAT BOUND
------------------------------------------------------------
The exact imported contact inequality for normalized m-sector vectors is

 <W><=a_m+b_m sqrt(<A>),
 a_m=4m(m-1)/(L-1), b_m=4(m-1)sqrt(m/(2J))/L.            (9)

makes it

 <v,Wv><=a_m||v||^2+b_m||v||sqrt(<v,Av>).                (10)

Write b_J=4/sqrt(2J). For L>=2,m>=1,

Let B=B_v at any allowed coupling. For any normalized phi and t>0,
set z=exp(-tB/2)phi. Since B>=aA>=0,

Substitution into (10) gives the exact norm estimate

 ||exp(-tB/2) W exp(-tB/2)||_infinity
            <=a_m+b_m/sqrt(e a t)
            <=(m^2/L)[8+b_J/sqrt(e a t)].              (11)
