A. INDEPENDENT RECONSTRUCTION OF THE SHARPER POWER-RANGE BOUND

Assume the exact tracial interface: Phi is UCP, tau-preserving, positive as an L2(tau) operator, L2-selfadjoint and selfcompatible; J:A tensor A -> A is a UCP joint map with both marginals Phi. Positivity as an L2 operator is needed here to put the spectrum of Phi in [0,1]. For Hermitian contractions A,C and m>=1, put x=Phi^(m-1)(A), y=Phi^(m-1)(C). Let J(z)=V* pi(z)V be Stinespring, P=VV*, Q=I-P, pi_1(a)=pi(a tensor I), pi_2(a)=pi(I tensor a), and R_x=Q pi_1(x)V, R_y=Q pi_2(y)V. The represented tensor factors commute, and the marginal conditions imply

  R_x*R_x=Phi(x^2)-Phi(x)^2,
  R_y*R_y=Phi(y^2)-Phi(y)^2.

Inserting P+Q between pi_1(x) and pi_2(y), then using their commutation, gives exactly

  [Phi(x),Phi(y)] = R_y*R_x-R_x*R_y.

Thus Schatten multiplication in normalized L2 gives

  ||[Phi(x),Phi(y)]||_2
     <= 2 min{ ||x||_infinity sqrt(tau(R_y*R_y)),
               ||y||_infinity sqrt(tau(R_x*R_x)) }.

Both operator norms are at most one. Since Phi is L2-selfadjoint and tau-preserving, its spectral theorem gives

  tau(Phi(x^2)-Phi(x)^2)
     = ||Phi^(m-1)(A)||_2^2-||Phi^m(A)||_2^2
     = integral_[0,1] (1-lambda^2)lambda^(2m-2) d mu_A(lambda).

The scalar supremum is d_(m-1); for m=1 the k=0 value is 1. Hence for every pair in the entire Hermitian unit ball,

  ||[Phi^m(A),Phi^m(C)]||_2 <= deltaHat_m := 2 sqrt(d_(m-1)).       (1)

The maximum for k>=1 is attained at lambda^2=k/(k+1); d_k=k^k/(k+1)^(k+1). In particular

  deltaHat_m = (2/sqrt(e)) m^(-1/2)(1+O(1/m)).

This exact compatible-dilation argument needs no comparison of Phi^m(A) with Phi^(m+1)(A); that comparison was the source of the separate 4d_m term in the INITIAL bound. I checked the Stinespring identity, both Schatten estimates, and the spectral index directly. The source-reported finite-matrix diagnostic in c05_l03/power_range_check.json is not used as proof.

At the same interface, Phi^m is selfcompatible: apply Phi^(m-1) to each output of J. It remains UCP, tau-preserving, positive and L2-selfadjoint. Therefore any admissible lift can be applied to Phi^m.

C. SECOND CONDITIONAL RESULT: AN OPTIMIZED BOUNDED-RANGE-RANK LIFT

This is an independently checked refinement of c05_s03's finite-generator construction. Let T:A->A be any finite-dimensional UCP tau-preserving map (selfadjointness/compatibility are not needed for this sublemma). Let V=T(A_sa) be its real Hermitian range, R=dim_R V, and assume

  sup_{X,Y in T(B_sa), ||X||_infinity,||Y||_infinity<=1}
       ||[X,Y]||_2 <= delta,  0<=delta<=2.

If R=1, T has scalar range and is EB. If R>=2, set k=R-1. In the quotient real space W=V/R I, the image of T's Hermitian unit ball is a compact symmetric convex body spanning W. Choose B_1,...,B_k in T(B_sa) whose quotient classes maximize the absolute determinant in any fixed volume coordinates. For any X in T(B_sa), write

  X=a I + sum_(i=1)^k c_i B_i.

Replacing column i by the quotient class of X changes the determinant by factor c_i. Maximality therefore gives |c_i|<=1 for every i and sum_i |c_i|<=k. This fixes the coefficient budget while treating the scalar coefficient as free, because every unital conditional expectation fixes I. No assumption about a Euclidean unit ball in the image is hidden here: the maximum is taken over the actual compact image-body quotient, so it controls every X in that same body.

Here is the optimized pinching step. In an existing direct-sum algebra, diagonalize a Hermitian contraction X in each block, use shifted bins of width h, and let P_u be the block pinching. For another Hermitian contraction Y, entrywise averaging over u in [0,h) gives

  E_u ||Y-P_u(Y)||_2^2 <= ||[X,Y]||_2/h.

For a family Y_1,...,Y_(k-1) with pair defects at most delta, set D=(k-1)delta. Summing this inequality shows that some single shift satisfies

  sum_j ||Y_j-P_u(Y_j)||_2^2 <= D/h,

so every compressed Y_j moves by at most sqrt(D/h) in L2. Replace X by the interval midpoint, clipped to [-1,1]; its L2 change is at most h/2 and it is central in the refined block algebra. Minimize the worst of these two errors:

  q=max{h/2,sqrt(D/h)}.

They agree at h=(4D)^(1/3), yielding q=2^(-1/3)D^(1/3), an improvement by factor 2^(-1/3) over choosing h=D^(1/3). If D=0 all generators commute and are jointly diagonalizable. For D>0, h<=2 whenever q<=1; if q>1 use the trivial displacement bound one. Every remaining pairwise commutator increases by at most 4q, since both arguments move by at most q and are contractions. For an acquired quotient frame B_i=Phi^m(A_i), c05_l03's input-sensitive estimate permits pair budgets delta_ij=2 sqrt(min{Q_(m-1)(A_i),Q_(m-1)(A_j)}). At a pivot i one can set D_i=sum_(j!=i)delta_ij and use h=(4D_i)^(1/3), giving q_i=2^(-1/3)D_i^(1/3), potentially smaller than (k-1)delta. This requires the selected preimages and their spectral profiles; after that pivot, propagate the updated pair matrix delta_jl+4q_i. It is not a free uniform improvement, since the spectral data and frame acquisition are charged.

Define for inputs delta in [0,2] the explicit recurrence

  G_1(delta)=0,
  q_k(delta)=2^(-1/3)((k-1)delta)^(1/3),
  G_k(delta)=1                         if q_k(delta)>=1,
  G_k(delta)=min{1,sqrt(q_k(delta)^2
           +G_(k-1)(min{2,delta+4q_k(delta)})^2)} otherwise.       (3)

The orthogonality of errors at successive pinching stages gives one MASA expectation P_D with max_i ||B_i-P_D(B_i)||_2<=G_k(delta). The quotient-basis representation, scalar fixation, triangle inequality and ||.||_1<=||.||_2 then yield one EB map E=P_D T satisfying

  sup_(A=A*,||A||<=1)||E(A)-T(A)||_(1,tau) <= k G_k(delta).         (4)

Formula (4) is an actual finite-dimensional measure-and-prepare channel: if Q_z are the minimal projections of D and p_z=tau(Q_z), then
  E(A)=sum_z tau(T^dagger(Q_z) A) Q_z/p_z,
where T^dagger is the tracial adjoint, the effects T^dagger(Q_z) are positive and sum to I, and each preparation Q_z/p_z has trace one. Thus no positive-to-CP or separability repair is omitted.

For k=1, the whole range lies in span{I,B_1}, which is abelian, so E=T exactly. For k=2, (3) gives G_2(delta)=2^(-1/3)delta^(1/3) on [0,2], and (4) is the explicit bound 2^(2/3)delta^(1/3). More generally, for fixed k the recurrence tends to zero and has an effective power-law upper bound G_k(delta)<=C_k delta^(3^(-(k-1))) for sufficiently small delta; a valid C_k follows by induction from q_k=a_k delta^(1/3), a_k=2^(-1/3)(k-1)^(1/3), and

  C_k^2 = a_k^2 + C_(k-1)^2 (1+4a_k)^(2/3^(k-2)),
  C_1=0,

with a harmless enlargement to at least one if a bound is desired on all delta in [0,2]. The exponent already appears in the finite-family literature/c05_s03 analysis; the optimized constant and scalar quotient bookkeeping are not claimed novel.

Apply (4) to T=Phi^m, whose real range rank is R_m<=R_1. From (1),

  e(original experiment) <= eps_b*/2
     +(1/2) inf_m [ theta k G_k(deltaHat_m) + m r_b ]       (5)

whenever the post-centralization channel Phi has R_m<=R for all m. In fact range dimensions are nonincreasing under powers, so R_m<=R_1. If the post-centralization corrected channel has a fixed rank bound R independent of physical dimension, (5) proves an explicit EB modulus for that subclass. The encoder/decoder is dimension-expanding, so no transfer of a rank bound from the pre-centralized map is asserted. For R=3 (k=2), use G_2 exactly: the lifted term is at most

  theta 2^(2/3) deltaHat_m^(1/3),

because the explicit construction E=P_D Phi^m is UCP and fixes I, so its difference from Phi^m vanishes on the scalar part. Thus alpha=1/3 in the depth optimization, m scales as r_b^(-6/7), and the optimized power/lift contribution is O(r_b^(1/7)). A rank bound is a genuine premise. Since R can grow up to d^2, this does not prove the unrestricted theorem.

E. COST, PRECISION, AND REMAINING GATE

The universal theorem still requires a modulus omega not supplied by any read artifact. The bounded-rank construction avoids that unknown only when a verified range-rank bound/frame is available. Its generator discovery is not free: a supplied frame is cheap to state but must be certified against the whole image; c05_s03's generic exact-superoperator/rational-net maximum-volume search is exponential in input dimension, and finite-precision noisy oracle access does not meet its exact-frame premise without a certified envelope. The quotient maximum-volume basis similarly needs exact/sufficiently certified linear and determinant decisions.

For physical dimension d, the inherited exact random-log encoder has at most d+1 flags and total dimension at most d(d+1), using sigma's eigenbasis and its log-spectrum breakpoints. A rational shift grid with dyadic N>=2d/eta controls the stated shift error by eta; choosing the next power of two costs less than twice the minimal grid size. Log-residue accuracy xi<=eta t/(4d) controls its additional averaging loss by eta. Sampling a dyadic grid index costs exactly log2(N) random bits. Eigenbasis/filter/CPTP compilation stability is still unimplemented. Computing c05_l03's input-specific Q profile requires the channel's L2 spectral data and input coordinates; the uniform envelope (1) avoids that measurement but gives no acquisition savings for Phi itself. Evaluating Phi^m takes m channel uses; naive Kraus composition can grow exponentially. The EB lift's measurement/preparation description has outcome, storage, query, runtime and bit-precision costs; the conditional omega premise charges none unless augmented.

Finally, N46's v(b), the fidelity-affinity bridge, Petz correction, and original-channel acquisition remain imported from the campaign baseline. v(b)'s asymptotic big-O constant is not identified as an acquired experiment certificate here. If v(b)<=V sqrt(b log(1/b)) with known V, then x_b=O((b log(1/b))^(1/4)), eps_b*=O((b log(1/b))^(1/12)), b_c=O((b log(1/b))^(1/12)), and r_b=O((b log(1/b))^(1/48)). A Holder lift of exponent alpha would yield overall power contribution O((b log(1/b))^(alpha/[48(alpha+2)])); e.g. alpha=1 gives exponent 1/144. These are only conditional rates; constants V,K and computation/acquisition costs must be supplied. For the rank-three subclass, alpha=1/3 gives exponent 1/336.

The decisive missing gate is unchanged: establish a dimension/rank-independent whole-CP-range-to-EB lift in normalized infinity-to-one trace norm, with a rate and an acquisition/precision cost, or construct an admissible growing-range obstruction. No counterexample satisfying the fixed-sandwich, bounded-capacity, vanishing-broadcast interface was found. No novelty or priority claim is made. The unrestricted result remains unresolved.
