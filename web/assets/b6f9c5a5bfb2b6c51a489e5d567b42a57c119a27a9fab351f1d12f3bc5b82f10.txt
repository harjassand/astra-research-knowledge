1. SUPPLIED PROBE ORBIT AND ACTUAL BROADCASTER

Let U be a nontrivial irreducible carrier of a compact connected simple
group G, and choose a supplied Hermitian Lie-generator score X_0 with
||X_0||_infinity<=1. Put X_g=U_g X_0 U_g^*, and fix 0<epsilon<=1.
The calibration states are

  rho_g^cal=(I+epsilon X_g)/d.                              (1)

Lie generators are traceless, so these are positive normalized states.
Suppose one unknown-member-independent ACTUAL CPTP two-output broadcaster
has both marginal half-trace errors <=b on every calibration state.
It may be noncovariant, nonunital and have unequal marginals initially.
Swap averaging and group twirling on this SUPPLIED closed calibration
orbit yield a covariant bistochastic common marginal Phi_* of an actual
broadcaster, with the same calibration error <=b. This operation is only
an existence argument; the decoder does not need to execute the twirl.
If targets also belong to a larger experiment on which that original
broadcaster works, this uses its calibration subset only.

Let Psi be the ONE highest-weight coherent orbit EB decoder in
LIE_COHERENT_STAR_PROOF.txt. Its linear generator-energy theorem and (1)
give, for every g,

  E_Psi(X_g)<=2 E_Phi(X_g)<=4b/epsilon,                    (2)
  E_Theta(A)=tau(A^2)-tau(A Theta(A)).

The second step uses the physical trace normalization
||(I-Phi_*)X_g||_(1,tau)<=2b/epsilon, ||X_g||_infinity<=1,
and E_Phi(X_g)=tau(X_g(X_g-Phi_*(X_g))). It also follows that
E_Psi(X_g) has one constant value by covariance. There is no lower
bound assumed on ||X_0||_2 and no division by its carrier-dependent norm.
If X_0=0 the following algebra consists of constants and the result is
trivial. Nonzero X_0's orbit spans the Lie algebra by simplicity, but
acquiring coefficients for a chosen target has its own charge below.

2. WORD ALGEBRA AND ITS EXPLICIT WEIGHTED COST

Allow any finite set of group nodes in each target and use arbitrary
noncommuting words

  X_w=X_(g_1) X_(g_2)...X_(g_q),  |w|=q.

For a supplied expression H=sum_w c_w X_w, define

  A(H)=sum_w |c_w|,
  L(H)=sum_w |c_w| |w|.                                   (3)

Constant words have length0. These are expression-dependent UPPER costs;
we do not optimize them or identify them with the intrinsic norm of H.
Every word has operator norm<=1, so ||H||_infinity<=A(H).
If the expression has degree<=k, L(H)<=k A(H). Complex coefficients
and non-Hermitian words are permitted, but the final density score H
must be Hermitian and satisfy the specified normalization/positivity.

For every Kraus K of the bistochastic Psi, exact Leibniz telescoping gives

  [K,X_w]=sum_(i=1)^q X_(g_1)...X_(g_(i-1))
                  [K,X_(g_i)] X_(g_(i+1))...X_(g_q).

The outer products have operator norm<=1. Minkowski in the DIRECT SUM
of normalized Hilbert-Schmidt spaces over all Kraus labels therefore
gives

  (sum_j ||[K_j,H]||_2^2)^(1/2)
   <=sum_w |c_w| sum_(i=1)^|w|
             (sum_j ||[K_j,X_(g_i)]||_2^2)^(1/2)
   <=L(H) sqrt(8b/epsilon),                               (4)

where the last step is (2) and the exact bistochastic identity
sum_j||[K_j,X]||_2^2=2E_Psi(X). For Hermitian H, the same identity
on the left yields

  E_Psi(H)<=4 L(H)^2 b/epsilon.                            (5)

No compatibility is assigned to a new channel, and no sector eigenvalue,
scalar gap, moment optimizer or preservation of an adjoint copy is used.
Products are fully noncommutative; the proof does not replace them by
classical product symbols or assume a multiplicative channel.

4. GENERAL POLYNOMIAL HAMILTONIAN GIBBS WITHOUT A TRUNCATION TAIL

Let P=P^* be ANY supplied noncommutative polynomial in calibration

expressions, word concatenation and triangle inequalities give

  A(PQ)<=A(P)A(Q),
  L(PQ)<=L(P)A(Q)+A(P)L(Q),
  L(P^r)<=r A(P)^(r-1)L(P).                               (8)

upper norm and hence in operator norm. It defines a countable absolutely

  L(exp(hP))<=|h| L(P) exp(|h|A(P)).                       (9)

All estimates (4)--(5) extend to such an expression by its finite partial
sums and finite-dimensional norm continuity. Positivity/trace of the
partial sums are not used in that intermediate energy estimate.
Let z=tau(exp(hP)). Since ||P||_infinity<=A(P),
z>=exp(-|h|A(P)). Thus the Hermitian density score

  H=exp(hP)/z-I

  L(H)<=|h|L(P)exp(2|h|A(P)).                              (10)
