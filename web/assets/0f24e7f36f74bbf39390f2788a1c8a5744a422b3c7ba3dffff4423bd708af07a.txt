CYCLE 4 — RATIONAL POSITIVE OPERATOR CONSUMER FOR THE N125 MIXTURE
Date: 8 October 2026
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE, conditional only on the Cycle-3 rational kernel-mixture theorem whose proof is in `outputs/research/algebra_complexity/cycle3/newton_correction/RESULT.txt`. This result gives an exact separable finite rational operator approximant and a bounded-random-bit label sampler. It does not claim an exact decomposition of the target or an approximation to the full all-Schur Gibbs state.

1. Target and interface

For N qubits, let

    |D_k> = binom(N,k)^(-1/2) sum_{S subset [N], |S|=k} |S>,
    f_k = exp(-(k-N/2)^2/N),   F_N=sum_{k=0}^N f_k,
    rho_N^sym = F_N^(-1) sum_{k=0}^N f_k |D_k><D_k|.

Use D(rho,sigma)=1/2 ||rho-sigma||_1 for trace distance.

This is the normalized Gibbs operator exp(-J_z^2/N) restricted to the maximal-spin symmetric subspace, where J_z|D_k>=(k-N/2)|D_k>. It is not the full N-qubit exp(-J_z^2/N) operator across all Schur sectors.

Let 0<epsilon<=1/2 and assume N>=40 ln(16/epsilon). Invoke the Cycle-3 finite-support theorem with that epsilon. It supplies q=ceil((5N-1)/2)=Theta(N) rational nodes p_i in (0,1), positive rational weights mu_i, and

    g_k = sum_{i=1}^q mu_i binom(N,k) p_i^k(1-p_i)^(N-k),
    |g_k-f_k| <= (epsilon/4) f_k       for all k=0,...,N.

Every emitted node and weight has polynomial bit length and deterministic construction time in N plus the accuracy-input bit length; the upstream root-separation, coefficient cancellation, rational normalization and runtime bounds remain charged in the cited leaf. Put G_N=sum_k g_k=sum_i mu_i and lambda_i=mu_i/G_N. These are exact positive rationals; no small probability is used as an acceptance probability.

2. Exact finite algebraic product-state operator

For each node p_i and each (N+1)-st root of unity zeta_j=exp(2 pi i j/(N+1)), j=0,...,N, define

    |psi_{i,j}> = (sqrt(1-p_i)|0> + sqrt(p_i) zeta_j |1>)^(tensor N),
    Omega_N = sum_{i=1}^q sum_{j=0}^N lambda_i/(N+1) |psi_{i,j}><psi_{i,j}|.

Each term is a pure product state and every coefficient is positive. To compute the operator, for computational strings S,T the matrix element of a fixed i term contains zeta_j^(|S|-|T|). Averaging j kills it unless N+1 divides |S|-|T|. Since |S|-|T| is in [-N,N], this occurs exactly when |S|=|T|. Therefore the phase average is

    (1/(N+1)) sum_j |psi_{i,j}><psi_{i,j}|
       = sum_{k=0}^N binom(N,k) p_i^k(1-p_i)^(N-k) |D_k><D_k|.

Consequently, exactly,

    Omega_N = G_N^(-1) sum_{k=0}^N g_k |D_k><D_k|.

This proves Omega_N is trace one, positive, supported on the symmetric subspace, and fully separable. Its whole operator is represented by q(N+1)=Theta(N^2) positive product terms. The beta/Newton mixture is consumed at the operator level without an inverse moment matrix, a PSD feasibility oracle, or division by a rare event probability.

3. Trace-distance guarantee and normalization condition

Set delta=epsilon/4, p_k=f_k/F_N, q_k=g_k/G_N and r_k=g_k/f_k. Then 1-delta<=r_k<=1+delta, and G_N/F_N=sum_k p_k r_k lies in the same interval. Hence

    |q_k/p_k-1| <= 2 delta/(1-delta),
    (1/2) sum_k |q_k-p_k| <= delta/(1-delta)=epsilon/(4-epsilon).

The two operators are diagonal in the same orthonormal Dicke basis, so their trace distance equals this classical total variation distance. In particular no factor proportional to the dimension, inverse minimum eigenvalue, or F_N^-1 is introduced by normalization. For epsilon<=1/2 this bound is at most 2epsilon/7.

4. Rational amplitudes without square roots

For each rational atom p_i in (0,1), solve approximately for s_i in (0,1)

    phi(s)=4s^2/(1+s^2)^2 = p_i.

The map phi is strictly increasing on (0,1), maps [0,1] onto [0,1], and
has derivative phi'(s)=8s(1-s^2)/(1+s^2)^3 in [0,8]. Use exact rational
bisection on [0,1] for B_A=ceil(log_2(64N/epsilon)) steps, unless equality
is reached earlier. Let s_i be the midpoint of the final interval, and
set

    a_i=(1-s_i^2)/(1+s_i^2),  b_i=2s_i/(1+s_i^2),  p_i'=b_i^2.

Then a_i,b_i are positive rationals and a_i^2+b_i^2=1. The midpoint is
within 2^(-B_A-1) of the exact inverse image, so

    |p_i'-p_i| <= 8*2^(-B_A-1) <= epsilon/(16N).

All comparisons are exact rational comparisons involving the supplied
p_i. No irrational amplitude or ill-conditioned inverse square root is
used. The bit lengths of s_i,a_i,b_i are O(B_A+bits(p_i)).

Replace the exact binomial component Phi_N(p_i) by Phi_N(p_i'). Couple
the N Bernoulli bits using the same N independent uniforms. The probability
of any bit mismatch is at most N|p_i-p_i'|, and mapping the bit string to
its Hamming weight cannot increase total variation. Therefore

    D(Phi_N(p_i),Phi_N(p_i')) <= epsilon/16.

Convexity gives the same bound after mixing i with lambda_i. This is a
global trace-distance bound; it does not assert a relative-error bound in
each rare Dicke sector after amplitude rounding.

5. Rational phases and bounded-bit label sampling

For N=1 use the exact phases 1,-1. For N>=2 put

    x=pi/(N+1),  t_*=tan(x),
    z_*=(1-t_*^2+2 i t_*)/(1+t_*^2)=exp(2 pi i/(N+1)).

Choose a dyadic rational t with |t-t_*|<=delta_phi=epsilon/(16N^2),
and define

    z=(1-t^2+2 i t)/(1+t^2),  z_j=z^j,   j=0,...,N.

The rational parametrization has |z|=1 and derivative magnitude
|dz/dt|=2/(1+t^2)<=2, so |z-z_*|<=2 delta_phi. Since both phases have
unit modulus, telescoping their j-th powers gives

    |z_j-z_*^j| <= j |z-z_*| <= 2N delta_phi.

For the one-qubit vectors with the same real amplitudes a_i,b_i, pure-state
trace distance is a_i b_i |z_j-z_*^j| <= |z_j-z_*^j|/2. Telescoping the
N tensor factors adds at most a factor N. Since a_i b_i<=1/2,

    D((a_i|0>+b_i z_j|1>)^tensor N,
      (a_i|0>+b_i z_*^j|1>)^tensor N) <= N^2 delta_phi
                                                    <= epsilon/16.

To obtain t using rational arithmetic, approximate pi by Machin's identity
pi=16 atan(1/5)-4 atan(1/239), truncating the alternating rational atan
series. Divide by N+1, then approximate sine and cosine by rational Taylor
polynomials on x<=pi/3. Cosine is at least 1/2 there, so a certified
absolute precision O(delta_phi) in the two Taylor values gives a certified
O(delta_phi) approximation to their ratio tan(x); round that ratio to a
dyadic mesh fine enough to meet the displayed bound. The series lengths,
intermediate rational bit lengths, and all comparisons are polynomial in
log(N/epsilon) and the encoded precision of epsilon. Thus t,z,z^j are
explicit rationals in Q(i), exactly unit modulus, with bit lengths
polynomial in N and log(1/epsilon). The derivative bound and power estimate
above charge all phase conditioning.

Write |Psi_ij>=(a_i|0>+b_i z_j|1>)^tensor N and define the
rational-weight operator

    Omega_rat = sum_(i=1)^q sum_(j=0)^N lambda_i/(N+1)
       |Psi_ij><Psi_ij|.

It is an exactly normalized positive mixture of q(N+1) pure product
states. Every local amplitude is in Q, every local phase is in Q(i), and
every density-matrix entry is rational complex. Each vector is symmetric,
so Omega_rat has exactly zero support outside the symmetric subspace. The
phase perturbation need not cancel off-diagonal Dicke entries. Combining
the sector normalization, amplitude and phase bounds gives

    D(rho_N^sym, Omega_rat)
       <= epsilon/(4-epsilon) + epsilon/8.

To get a hard bit cap for the ensemble label, enumerate M=q(N+1) exact
rational weights w_ij=lambda_i/(N+1), and choose

    b=ceil(log_2(8M/epsilon)).

Round each interior cumulative probability upward to the grid of denominator
2^b (keep 0 and 1 fixed), and let c_ij be the resulting nonnegative
integer interval lengths. Draw exactly b fair bits and select the interval
containing the resulting grid point. The resulting label law is exactly
c_ij/2^b, and zero-count labels are omitted from the output. Its total
variation distance from (w_ij) is at most
2M/2^b<=epsilon/4. The dyadic-mixture output Omega_grid therefore obeys

    Omega_grid = sum_(i,j:c_ij>0) c_ij/2^b |Psi_ij><Psi_ij|,

with retained weights strictly positive and total weight exactly one.

    D(rho_N^sym, Omega_grid)
       <= epsilon/(4-epsilon) + epsilon/8 + epsilon/4
       <= 37 epsilon/56 < epsilon,      0<epsilon<=1/2.

The exact rational ensemble Omega_rat and the dyadic-weight Omega_grid are
both positive product-state descriptions. Omega_rat uses exact rational
weights; Omega_grid is the one with a fixed-bit exact label sampler.

5. Complete cost and conditioning ledger

Inputs: integer N; rational/dyadic epsilon; no oracle, state tomography, or unknown matrix. The approximation routine emits q=Theta(N) rational p_i,mu_i. Its finite Newton coefficient work, common denominators, exp(8N) cancellation/guard bits, rational quadrature weights, Sturm root isolation, and normalization are charged in Cycle 3. This result adds q amplitude bisections of O(log(N/epsilon)) exact rational steps, one rational Machin/Taylor phase computation at precision O(epsilon/N^2), O(N) rational multiplications to form the powers z^j, and O(N^2) label intervals. All operands have bit length polynomial in N, log(1/epsilon), and the Cycle-3 node bit length. The label sampler compares M=O(N^2) cumulative rational weights and uses b=O(log N+log(1/epsilon)) fair bits; there is no acceptance/rejection loop and no inverse-normalizer runtime.

Succinct output: store the q node/weight/amplitude records once, the rational phase base z once, and each label by indices (i,j,N), with its rational or dyadic weight. This has polynomial encoding length; z^j can be generated by O(N) rational multiplications. If every one-qubit factor and each expanded rational z^j is written in every record, the output remains polynomial (at most O(N^4) rational-factor entries, with polynomial bit length). If the consumer insists on a dense global density matrix, output size is exponential and no polynomial claim is made. To physically synthesize arbitrary rational complex one-qubit states in a fixed gate set, compile each factor to a gate approximation and add that gate error to the trace budget; gate synthesis/hardware is outside this abstract algebraic output interface.

The operator result is measurement-robust: for every POVM or quantum channel, the induced output laws/states differ by at most the same trace-distance bound. It does not imply that arbitrary observables can be evaluated in polynomial time or that the target is exactly separable.

6. Scope and counterchecks

This proves a rational positive operator *approximant* for the symmetric-sector Gibbs state at inverse-polynomial accuracy (indeed for every supplied epsilon satisfying N>=40 ln(16/epsilon)). It does not approximate the full exp(-J_z^2/N) operator on all Schur sectors, does not reproduce N117's all-Schur axial Gibbs theorem, and does not give arbitrary alpha,h,delta. The finite Newton residual blocks taking epsilon to zero at fixed N; no exact-separability inference follows. The beta-kernel and finite-support results retain their source provenance; this operator lift is a separate internal proof.

Falsifiers: failure of the roots-of-unity orthogonality for any nonzero excitation-number difference in [-N,N]; a wrong binomial-vs-per-string factor; a normalization error G_N=sum_i mu_i; a failure of the rational amplitude bisection/Lipschitz bound; or a rational phase point not unit length. The exact finite diagnostic is `evidence/check_operator_lift.py`, with receipt `evidence/exact_check.json`. It checks small instances of the Fourier cancellation condition and Dicke matrix entries, rational unit-circle normalization, and finite dyadic-grid variation. Those checks are consistency evidence only; the proof above is uniform. An independent Cycle 4 audit in `../operator_lift_independent/` checked the exact N=3 matrix identity over Q(i) and agreed with the multiplicity factor; this is internal corroboration, not external validation.

7. Polynomial-cost consumer: joint single-qubit POVM sampling

As an explicit consumer of the succinct ensemble, suppose the input is a nonadaptive product POVM with at most s outcomes at each site. Each local effect is a 2-by-2 positive semidefinite Hermitian matrix over Q(i), with total input bit length L_E per effect. There is a bounded-random-bit classical sampler for its full N-site outcome string whose total-variation distance from measuring rho_N^sym is at most 11 epsilon/14.

First construct the rational grid ensemble from Section 4, with M=q(N+1) labels and dyadic weights c_a/2^b, b=ceil(log_2(8M/epsilon)). For a selected label a, the N-qubit state is |phi_a>^{tensor N}. For site r and local effect E_{r,t}, the conditional outcome probability

    q_{a,r,t}=<phi_a|E_{r,t}|phi_a>

is an exact rational in [0,1], and the outcomes factor across sites conditional on a. Sample the label exactly from its dyadic weights using b fair bits. Then, independently at each site, use

    h=ceil(log_2(8Ns/epsilon))

bits to select among the rational cumulative probabilities q_{a,r,t} on the grid {0,1/2^h,...,(2^h-1)/2^h}. Rounding each of at most s CDF intervals changes that site's law in total variation by at most s/2^h. The product-law coupling bound gives at most Ns/2^h<=epsilon/8 total variation conditional on the label, and hence also after mixing the label.

The ideal quantum measurement law of rho_N^sym and the exact rational-grid state differ by at most their trace distance, at most 37 epsilon/56 by Sections 3-4. Therefore the sampler's outcome law differs from the desired law by at most

    37 epsilon/56 + epsilon/8 = 11 epsilon/14 < epsilon.

The sampler consumes exactly b+Nh fair bits. After the N125 mixture and rational amplitude/phase preprocessing, each sample takes O(log M+N+Ns) rational comparisons/arithmetic operations, with integer bit lengths polynomial in the node bit length, L_E, N log(N/epsilon), and h. Input acquisition is explicit: the POVM matrices and rational N125 nodes/weights are supplied or deterministically constructed; no evaluation oracle or tomography is used. Precomputing all conditional probabilities for all labels would cost O(MNs) and is unnecessary for a single or streaming sample. The algorithm outputs one joint outcome string, not the exponentially large distribution table.

This consumer handles product POVMs. It does not evaluate general entangling POVMs efficiently: trace distance controls their statistical error, but evaluating their conditional probabilities on a product vector can still take exponential time for a dense global effect. The result also remains confined to the maximal-spin Gibbs block; the all-Schur extension is an open gate in this packet.
