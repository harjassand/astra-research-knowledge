Disposition
-----------

I find no concrete mathematical gap in the stated EB floor, conditional on
the already banked continuum full-trace limit and all-sector Gibbs tail.
The argument uses only the m=2 block for noncommutation, then a
dimension-independent measure-and-prepare affinity inequality. The bound is
strictly positive for any two distinct positive couplings in the public
compact interval, but it is deliberately tiny. This is an internal audit,
not external validation or a priority determination.

Load-bearing calculations checked
---------------------------------

1. The EB affinity witness has the correct direction. Measurement produces
classical root fidelity at least the input root fidelity. For prepared
states eta_x, joint concavity and homogeneity of
Tr sqrt(X)sqrt(Y) imply output affinity at least the classical root
fidelity. Therefore a measure-and-prepare channel T satisfies
a(T rho,T sigma)>=f(rho,sigma). Powers–Størmer square-root continuity gives
|a(T rho,T sigma)-a(rho,sigma)|<=2 sqrt(delta) when both trace errors are
at most delta, hence delta >=(f-a)^2/4. The commutator conversion is also
consistent: ||[rho,sigma]||_1<=4||[sqrt(rho),sqrt(sigma)]||_1, while the
polar estimate gives ||sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)-sqrt(sigma)sqrt(rho)||_1
<=2 sqrt(2(f-a)); thus delta>=||[rho,sigma]||_1^4/65536. These estimates
are dimension independent and apply to trace-class states.

2. The bounded contact-form normalization is consistent with the LL limit.
For m=2, b(v,v)=2 int |v(t,t)|^2dt and T0=-2J Delta on the square.
The trace inequality b(v,v)<=8||v||||grad v|| implies
C_s=R0^(1/2) B R0^(1/2) satisfies
0<=C_s<=4/sqrt(2Js), with R0=(T0+s)^-1. The form-sum resolvent identity
R_g=R0^(1/2)(I+gC_s)^-1R0^(1/2) is valid without treating bare delta
as a bounded operator. Multiplying the two bounded inverse factors
verifies (3.3); using ||Q-I||<=(a+b)||C_s|| and
||[R0,C_s]||<=2||C_s||/s gives the displayed remainder
4(a+b)||C_s||^2/s^2. No small-g expansion is silently used.

3. The public free-sine witness and constants check. The vectors
u_k=phi_k tensor phi_k have energy e_k=4J pi^2 k^2 and, for k!=l,
b(u_k,u_l)=2. For low band M+1,...,2M and high band 3M+1,...,4M,
the resolvent matrix element is
(2/M) sum_{k,l}(r_k-r_l)r_k r_l, with lower bounds
r_k-r_l>=1/(40J pi^2 M^2), r_k>=1/(20J pi^2 M^2),
r_l>=1/(68J pi^2 M^2). It is therefore at least
1/(27200J^3pi^6M^5). The public M makes the dressing remainder at
most half this leading term, yielding
eta_R=|g1-g2|/(54400J^3pi^6M^5). I checked the constants in the
definition of M: M>=54400g_+/J and tau s>=3 give both required
inequalities.

4. The shifted-log transfer is valid for infinite-dimensional bounded
positive heat operators. With gamma=min(1/(4s),eta_R s/32) and
delta=exp[-tau(gamma^-1-s)], gamma^-1>=4s and tau s>=3 imply
delta<=e^-9, s0=s-tau^-1 log(1+delta)>=s/2, and the scalar map
psi(t)=1/(s-tau^-1 log t) is increasing and concave on [0,1+delta].
Its fixed-width increment is at most psi(delta)=gamma. Thus
psi(T_i+delta I) is within gamma of R_i. The two log-integral
commutator bounds each contribute delta^-1; the inverse commutator uses
four factors bounded by s0^-1. Combining with the shifted resolvent
commutator gives
||[T_1,T_2]||_1 >= eta_R tau^2 s^4 delta^2/32.
The scalar identity shift is handled before integrating, so no divergent
log integral or non-trace-class commutator is being assumed.

5. The Fock normalization and finite-chain transfer have the right
direction. The m=2 normalized block is T_i/Z_i, and the whole
grand-canonical commutator trace norm is at least its m=2 block. The
Gaussian sector bound gives Z_i<=S, so the density commutator is at least
eta_T/S^2. Applying the EB affinity bound yields e_*=eta^4/65536.
For finite-chain EB maps, composing with the public TP complement decoder
and isometric encoder preserves the EB property. The two state-transfer
errors cost at most 2 Delta_L, hence the claimed liminf follows from the
qualitative uniform full-trace limit. No explicit L threshold is claimed.

Finite evidence and boundaries
------------------------------

I reran outputs/research/sol_attractive_critical/eb_checks.py. It reports
