CYCLE 5 — CARDINALITY-FREE CMI GATE FROM THE ACTUAL BROADCASTER
Status: new exact intermediate inequality; common-POVM conversion remains
UNKNOWN. This is not a proof or counterexample to bounded-Holevo
broadcasting -> EB reconstruction.

Question. Can the actual joint two-output channel supply a dimension- and
label-free moment/affinity quantity that tends to zero with the marginal
error, and does that quantity force one common measurement?

1. FIXED-SANDWICH INTERFACE

Let (A,tau) be a finite tracial algebra, sigma a central physical reference,
and

  rho_H = sigma(I+H),  H=H*, tau(H)=0, ||H||_infinity<=theta<1.

Let Phi be the corrected likelihood marginal: UCP, tau-preserving, positive
and selfadjoint on L2(tau). Assume it has an actual symmetric two-output
extension B with both physical marginals Phi, and assume its Schrödinger
channel fixes sigma, Phi_*(sigma)=sigma. This is automatic for the matrix
tracial case sigma=I/d; on a flagged direct sum it must be checked blockwise.
For a finite prior p on H_x, write

  r_p = sum_x p_x ||Phi(H_x)-H_x||_(1,tau),
  Hbar = sum_x p_x H_x.

The target common-POVM quantity remains

  Delta(p)=V(p)-J(p),
  V(p)=sum_x p_x tau(H_x^2),
  J(p)=max_{sum_y w_y |psi_y><psi_y|=sigma}
         sum_y w_y sum_x p_x |<psi_y,H_x psi_y>|^2.

The measurement in J must be one ensemble for the entire prior. No
label-by-label POVM is being used.

2. A NEW DIMENSION- AND CARDINALITY-FREE HOLEVO-LOSS INEQUALITY

Define

  f(H)=D(rho_H||sigma)=tau[(I+H)log(I+H)],
  L_theta=max{-log(1-theta), log(1+theta)}.

For any centered likelihoods H,K in the theta-sandwich,

  |f(H)-f(K)| <= L_theta ||H-K||_(1,tau).                 (1)

Proof: along H_s=(1-s)H+sK the derivative of the traced functional is
tau[(K-H)(I+log(I+H_s))]. Since tau(K-H)=0, the identity term vanishes;
||log(I+H_s)||_infinity<=L_theta. Integrate over s. This trace-functional
derivative is valid without commuting H and K.

Let chi_in(p) be the Holevo information of {p_x,rho_Hx} and chi_A(p) that
of the actual first marginal outputs {p_x,rho_Phi(Hx)}. Donald's identity
relative to sigma gives

  chi_in(p)=sum_x p_x f(H_x)-f(Hbar),
  chi_A(p)  =sum_x p_x f(Phi(H_x))-f(Phi(Hbar)).

Applying (1) to each state and to the prior mean, and using
||Phi(Hbar)-Hbar||_1<=r_p, yields the exact estimate

  0 <= chi_in(p)-chi_A(p) <= 2 L_theta r_p.              (2)

The lower bound is data processing. The upper bound has no Hilbert-space
dimension, family-cardinality, rank, or covariance factor. It is sharper
than using only the worst residual: r_p<=sup_x||Phi(H_x)-H_x||_1.

3. A SHARPER RELATIVE-ENTROPY MOMENT IDENTITY FOR THE EXTENSION

Let omega_x^{AB}=B_*(rho_Hx), omega_sigma^{AB}=B_*(sigma), and
omega_x^A=Phi_*(rho_Hx). The fixed-sandwich tracial assumptions ensure
omega_sigma^A=sigma. Define the two partial-trace data-processing gaps

  delta_x = D(omega_x^{AB}||omega_sigma^{AB})
            -D(omega_x^A||sigma) >= 0,
  delta_bar = D(omega_bar^{AB}||omega_sigma^{AB})
              -D(omega_bar^A||sigma) >= 0.

For Omega_XAB=sum_x p_x |x><x| tensor omega_x^{AB}, Donald's identity on
AB and on A gives the exact formula

  I(X:B|A)_Omega = chi_AB(p)-chi_A(p)
                  = sum_x p_x delta_x-delta_bar.        (3)

The same channel B_* acts on all x; its outputs may be correlated. By data
processing through B_*,

  delta_x <= D(rho_Hx||sigma)-D(omega_x^A||sigma).

The right side is the relative-entropy loss of the marginal Phi_* and is at
most L_theta ||Phi(H_x)-H_x||_(1,tau) by (1). Thus

  0 <= I(X:B|A)_Omega <= sum_x p_x delta_x <= L_theta r_p.   (4)

Symmetrize the joint extension if needed; then the same argument gives

  0 <= I(X:A|B)_Omega <= L_theta r_p.                   (5)

This is sharper than the coarse Holevo-loss estimate (2). It is a
cardinality- and dimension-free relative-entropy moment inequality tied to
the actual joint extension, not merely a statement about its marginal map.
It also implies χ_A(p)<=I(X:AB)_Omega<=χ_in(p), as required by data
processing.

For the unsandwiched bounded-Holevo input, apply the N93 smoothing/Petz
transfer first; (4)-(5) then hold for the reduced flagged experiment with
its reported residual. Any smoothing/flag error stays separately charged.

By the Fawzi-Renner recoverability theorem, (4) also gives a single CPTP
recovery R_{A->AB}, independent of x, whose action on the cq state Omega_XA
reconstructs Omega_XAB with root fidelity at least
exp(-L_theta r_p/2). Fuchs-van de Graaf then gives cq-state trace distance
at most sqrt(1-exp(-L_theta r_p)). This is a genuine dimension-free
affinity/recovery consequence of the joint broadcaster. The theorem provides
a recovered state, not proximity to an exact Markov state or a measurement;
Fawzi-Renner explicitly note examples with small CMI far from every exact
Markov chain.

4. WHY THIS DOES NOT YET GIVE A COMMON POVM

The recovery R is a general quantum channel. Neither (3) nor the
recoverability theorem makes it a measurement or an entanglement-breaking
map. The required target is instead a POVM M on the input, independent of x,
for which the common posterior variance Delta(p) is small (or, after
minimax, one EB decoder has small worst-member trace error).

The missing implication has the form

  Delta(p) <= F_(C,theta)( I(X:B|A)_Omega ),
  F_(C,theta)(u)->0 as u->0,                              (5)

for symmetric actual-broadcast extensions of the admitted families, or a
direct replacement that constructs M. No proof of (5) is supplied here.
Small quantum CMI yields quantum recoverability; converting it to a
measurement requires a dimension-free control of the measured/discord part
of the correlations. A recovery channel alone leaves that gap intact.

The exact constant-channel control shows why the residual premise cannot be
omitted. For A=M_2, sigma=I/2, and the equal prior on

  rho_X=(I+theta X)/2, rho_Z=(I+theta Z)/2,

the constant marginal Phi(D)=Tr(D)I/2 has the symmetric physical broadcaster
rho -> I/2 tensor I/2, so I(X:B|A)=0. But the least-favourable-prior
common-measurement deficit is Delta=theta^2/2: for any pure-state 1-design,
the Bloch coordinates obey n_x^2+n_z^2<=1, and an X-eigenbasis attains the
bound. This is a legal exact broadcaster, but its residual on these scores
is theta, not small. It refutes any CMI-only estimate for the input family;
it does not refute (5) with the actual small-residual condition.

5. STATUS AND NEXT MATHEMATICAL GATE

New proved claim: (2)-(4), under the exact fixed-sandwich and channel
assumptions above. The proof is elementary and independent of source
covering numbers. It supplies a cardinality-free quantity that vanishes
with the actual marginal residual.

Still UNKNOWN: whether small symmetric CMI plus the fact that A and B are
both near the original states forces a measurement with small Delta, with a
modulus depending only on C and theta. Also UNKNOWN is a qualifying sequence
of actual broadcasters with bounded Holevo capacity, residual tending to
zero, and Delta bounded below. The constant-channel example only rejects
dropping residual control. It is not a counterexample to the requested
asymptotic theorem.

The interface can be stated as a concrete new gate: prove (5), or prove an
equivalent dimension-free measured-CMI/relative-entropy-loss bound for a
symmetric broadcast extension. A generic quantum recovery statement is
strictly short of this gate.
