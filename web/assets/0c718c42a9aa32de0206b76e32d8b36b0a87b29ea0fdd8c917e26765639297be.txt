CONTRACTIVE ONE-QUERY SPARSE NC ACQUISITION WITH CERTIFIED FINITE PRECISION
7 October 2026. Internal proof and finite diagnostics; historical priority UNKNOWN.
This is a bounded-degree/height extension of N68, using the affine mechanism in
Lakhani--Mukhopadhyay, ECCC TR26-120. Prony, inertia, and rational recovery are old.

THE EXACT INTERFACE
Supply integers n,s>=1, D>=0, H>=0. Unknown
 f=sum_(j=1)^r c_j w_j in Q<x_1,...,x_n>, r<=s,
has distinct ordered words of length <=D and nonzero reduced rational coefficients
c_j=a_j/b_j with |a_j|<=2^H and 1<=b_j<=2^H. A zero polynomial is allowed.
An active oracle accepts exact rational n-tuples of m-by-m matrices and returns
the FULL matrix response approximated by rational dyadics, with a CERTIFIED
entrywise absolute-error bound chosen by the caller. The oracle's evaluation,
precision production, input transfer, and output transfer are charged.

Construction: m=2s; choose power of two B>n+1. For 0<=a<=b<m, put
 T_i(a,b)=binom(b,a)*i^(b-a)/B^b; put T_i(a,b)=0 for a>b.
Every T_i is nonnegative, has induced column-sum norm exactly 1, and has
O(s log(n+1))-bit entries. The tuple is independent of D,H.

THEOREM
One approximate FULL response at this tuple suffices for deterministic EXACT
reconstruction, using poly(n,s,D,H) bit computation and
 P=O(H+s D log(n+1)+s+log(s+1))
certified fractional precision bits per response entry. Returned integer parts
need at most H+log(s+1)+O(1) bits. No fixed-precision, degree-free, passive-sample,
or succinct-circuit-size capability is asserted. The bit cost of creating the
oracle response is not included for free in the decoder bound.

EXPLICIT SAFE PRECISION
Define
 U=2^(s-1)*B^(D(s-1)), K=2^H*s^2*U^2, m=2s,
 C_ann=32*K*s*m*2^s*B^(Ds), C_coef=8*s*U*m*2^(2H),
 epsilon_0=1/max(C_ann,C_coef).
An entrywise error epsilon<epsilon_0 suffices. For example, ask for at least
 ceil(log2(max(C_ann,C_coef)))+4
fractional bits, with a certified dyadic approximation error <=1.5*2^(-P).
The precise constants are conservative. P is linear in H, rather than sH.

PROOF: AFFINE WORD CODES AND CONTRACTION
For v_m(z)=(1,z,...,z^(m-1)), the binomial theorem gives
 v_m(z)T_i=v_m((z+i)/B).
Consequently, for w=i_1...i_l,
 alpha(w)=B^(-l)*(1+sum_(t=1)^l i_t B^(t-1)), alpha(empty)=1,
 v_m(1)T_w=v_m(alpha(w)).
Nonempty codes are in (0,1). Their numerator has lowest base-B digit i_1+1 in
{2,...,n+1}, strictly less than B; thus it is not divisible by B. The least
integer l with B^l alpha integral is exactly the word length. This proves
injectivity. The lowest digit minus 1 is i_1; successive digits give i_2,...,i_l.
In particular, distinct codes for words of length <=D are separated by >=B^-D.

Column b of T_i has sum ((i+1)/B)^b<=1; column 0 has sum 1. Hence ||T_i||_1=1.
For F=f(T), ||F||_1<=sum_j |c_j|<=s 2^H. Approximate response Y with entrywise
error <=epsilon gives moments t_tilde_k=sum_a Y(a,k) with
 |t_tilde_k-t_k|<=m epsilon,
 t_k=sum_j c_j alpha_j^k, 0<=k<m.

PROOF: EXACT RANK FROM APPROXIMATE MOMENTS
Let V_r=(alpha_j^k)_(0<=k<r,1<=j<=r). Row j of V_r^-1 is the coefficient vector
of the Lagrange polynomial
 prod_(i!=j)(z-alpha_i)/(prod_(i!=j)(alpha_j-alpha_i)).
Since 0<alpha_i<=1 and differences are >=B^-D, every inverse entry is at most
 2^(r-1) B^(D(r-1)) <= U
in absolute value. Thus ||V_r^-1||_2<=r U<=s U.

The s-by-s true Hankel matrix M=(t_(a+b)) has rank r and factors
 M=V_s diag(c_j) V_s^T,
where V_s consists of the first s powers. The leading r-by-r block is invertible.
Indeed det(M_r)=prod_j c_j * prod_(i<j)(alpha_j-alpha_i)^2 !=0, including signed
cancellation. Its inverse has norm <=K because min_j |c_j|>=2^-H.
The nonzero eigenvalues of the full symmetric M also have absolute value >=1/K:
write V_s=Q R with Q an orthonormal column basis; the restriction to its range
is R diag(c_j) R^T, whose inverse norm is bounded by 2^H ||R^-1||_2^2<=K.
Here sigma_min(V_s)>=sigma_min(V_r)>=1/(s U).

For M_tilde=(t_tilde_(a+b)), ||M_tilde-M||_2<=s m epsilon<1/(8K).
Set tau=1/(4K). A symmetric eigenvalue changes by at most the operator norm
of the perturbation (the min--max principle). Therefore zero eigenvalues of M
lie within (-tau,tau) after perturbation, while every nonzero one lies outside.
So the exact integer
 r = positive_inertia(M_tilde-tau I)+negative_inertia(M_tilde+tau I)
recovers the actual sparsity. These inertias are computable with exact rational
symmetric elimination, not an uncharged floating-point spectral oracle.

For completeness, rational symmetric elimination uses a nonzero diagonal pivot
and its Schur complement. If all diagonals vanish but an off-diagonal b!=0
exists, pivot on [[0,b],[b,0]], which has inertia (1,1), and take its Schur
complement. A remaining zero matrix contributes zero eigenvalues. Rational
elimination and determinant bounds give polynomial bit complexity.

PROOF: INTEGER ANNIHILATOR ROUNDING
If r=0 return zero. Otherwise solve the approximate leading-r Hankel system
 M_tilde_r p_tilde = -(t_tilde_r,...,t_tilde_(2r-1))^T.
The true p=(p_0,...,p_(r-1)) belongs to
 p_alpha(z)=z^r+sum_(j<r) p_j z^j=prod_j(z-alpha_j).
Its coefficient-vector 2-norm is <=2^s. The inverse perturbation bound gives
 ||M_tilde_r^-1||_2<=2K,
 ||p_tilde-p||_2<=4K*s*m*2^s*epsilon.
This follows directly by subtracting the two linear systems; their matrix and
right-hand-side errors have norms at most s m epsilon.

Set beta_j=B^D alpha_j. These are distinct positive INTEGERS. The monic polynomial
 p_beta(z)=B^(Dr)*p_alpha(z/B^D)
therefore has integer coefficients. Its j-th coefficient is B^(D(r-j))*p_j.
At the stated epsilon, replacing p_j by p_tilde_j changes each such coefficient
by less than 1/8. Round to the nearest integer to obtain p_beta EXACTLY.

No root-finding oracle is required. A Sturm sequence over Q counts roots in
rational intervals. All roots are promised simple integers in [1,B^D]. Bisect
intervals, retaining only intervals with roots. There are at most r active
intervals at a depth, and O(D log B) depths suffice to isolate integer roots
to width <1/2. Handle a midpoint root explicitly, round the unique integers,
and verify the polynomial values. Polynomial degree, coefficient heights, and
all root/output bits are polynomial in the stated parameters. The diagnostics
use SymPy exact factoring as a convenience rather than implementing this Sturm
alternative.

From beta recover the word: strip trailing base-B zeros; their number is D-l.
The remaining lowest digit minus 1 is i_1, followed by i_2,...,i_l. beta=B^D
is the empty word. This reconstructs all ordered words exactly.

PROOF: RATIONAL COEFFICIENT RECOVERY
Solve the exact-known-node Vandermonde system using the approximate first r
moments. Its coefficient error in infinity norm is <=s U m epsilon. Under
epsilon<1/C_coef this is <1/(8*2^(2H)). Each true reduced coefficient has
denominator <=2^H. Distinct rationals with denominator <=2^H differ by at least
2^(-2H). Continued fractions recover the unique nearest such rational to the
approximate coefficient; this is polynomial bit time. This completes the proof.

WHY THE DEGREE/HEIGHT SUPPLIES ARE SUBSTANTIVE
Without H, the constants 0 and 1/q have arbitrarily close responses. Without D,
the contractive codes approach each other arbitrarily closely as length grows.
For this tuple, there is no finite precision that uniformly identifies all words.
The usual unrestricted coefficient/degree lower bound of matrix dimension 2s
applies to one fixed same-field tuple via a split minimal polynomial. It need
not remain optimal when a competing tuple can depend on finite D,H. No such
finite-height optimality claim is made.

STOCHASTIC ACCESS AND ITS COST
Each T_i has a column-stochastic embedding of dimension m+1:
 S_i=[[T_i,0],[ones^T-ones^T T_i,1]].
It has nonnegative dyadic entries; the top-left block of every S_w is T_w.
Thus the same construction works for an oracle restricted to classical Markov
kernels, with one extra state. For positive coefficients summing to 1, f(S)
is a physical mixture-of-words channel. A certified numerical channel response
is still stronger than ordinary sampling from this channel.

An explicit sampling obstruction for THIS fixed tuple: n=2,s=1,B=4. Compare
length-D words 1^D and 2 1^(D-1), D>=2. Start the 3-state embedding in column 1.
Their response distributions are
 p=(r,lambda,1-r-lambda), q=(r+delta,lambda,1-r-lambda-delta),
 r=(1-4^-D)/3, lambda=delta=4^-D.
Every other starting state is identical for the two words. Since q_0>=5/16 and
q_2>=9/16, KL(p||q)<=chi^2(p,q)<5 delta^2. Even adaptively choosing starting
states, N independent channel uses have KL<=5N delta^2 by the chain rule.
Success probability at least 2/3 on each word requires TV>=1/3; Pinsker then
requires N>=2/(45 delta^2)=(2/45)*16^D. This is not a lower bound against all
possible stochastic query tuples. It shows exactly why polynomial precision
bits at a contractive tuple do not imply polynomial sample acquisition cost.
