NEAR-FULL-RANK CAPACITY PACKET — INDEX AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES
Date: 2026-10-08

Primary target: for the SU(m) Casimir on H_N=Sym^N(C^m), with
p=m-1, n=dim(H_N)=binom(N+p,p),

  Lambda_{N,m}(k)=inf{E(X): ||X||_HS=1, rank X<=n-k}.

The proved cycle-2 general rank-spectral theorem and exact SU(m) spectrum
are in ../RESULT.txt. Near-full rank requires more than that profile: its
lower bound reduces to m k/n when k<=n/2.

SYMMETRIC-POWER NEAR-FULL RESULTS

* `alternative_high_ceiling.txt` gives an exact arbitrary-kernel Green
  compression, a finite-N lower bound in terms of the full spectral profile,
  and matching order for fixed m as k=o(n). It handles nonnormal matrices
  and every right-kernel orientation. For p>=2 it proves
  Lambda=Theta_m((k/n)^((p-1)/p)); for p=1 it gives the inverse-log order.
* `constructive_uniform_upper.txt` gives the explicit occupation-layer
  resistance taper and exact rank rounding. For N>=max(4p,16) and
  1<=k<=floor(32^(-p)n), it proves a finite upper bound with explicit
  constants. `constructive_uniform_upper_verification.txt` records 957
  finite arithmetic checks; those checks are diagnostic, not proof.
* `explicit_polytope_models.txt` gives the exact diagonal occupation graph,
  Green identities, coherent-corner capacities, and k-hole cluster
  asymptotics. For p>=2, a fixed coherent corner hole has energy
  (1+o(1))(p-1)N/n, an upper witness only. For p=1, a path argument proves
  the global diagonal one-hole order.
* `heat_spectral_profile.txt` is for the distinct product carrier
  V_{N/2}^{tensor r} of SU(2)^r. Its r=1 specialization is exactly the
  spin-N/2 representation Sym^N(C^2), giving the sharp
  Lambda=(1+o(1))/log(n/k) for k=o(n). Its r>1 constants and codimensions
  do not transfer to Sym^N(C^m), m>=3.

Thus for Sym^N(C^m), m>=3, the near-full exponent is matched, while the
sharp leading constant and the minimizing kernel orientation remain open.
For m=2, the inverse-log leading constant is one. The flat complement
I-P_coh is not optimal even for its own coherent kernel (exact spin-1
counterexample in `info_gaussian_channel.txt`); that does not rule out a
coherent minimizing kernel.

RELATED LIMITS AND SOURCE AUDITS

* `block_mutual_information.txt` shows that dimension, kernel dimension,
  first gap, and the integrated rank profile alone cannot determine a
  near-full capacity for general invariant operators. It also records the
  SU(2) k=1 proof and product-group diagnostic.
* `affinity_entropy.txt` compares the exact matrix-kernel variational
  problem with noncommutative condenser capacity, scalar graph Green
  functions, and matrix-stratum Sobolev capacity. These are related
  objects, not interchangeable theorems. Novelty/priority is UNKNOWN.
* `primary_prior_green.txt` compares the arbitrary-kernel Green profile
  with compact-group uncertainty, partially entanglement-breaking channels,
  low-Schmidt-rank projector overlap, and bounded-Schmidt-rank subspaces.
  The search is scoped; it does not establish novelty or priority.
* `explicit_radial_models.txt` gives exact one-hole Dirichlet blocks and
  coherent-versus-bulk comparisons for SO(3) spin N; unrestricted matrix
  lower bounds are outside that diagonal analysis.

No KLS-equivalent assumption is used in these near-full results. They are
Casimir-energy/capacity statements only; an operational channel or memory
claim requires a separately proved transfer.
