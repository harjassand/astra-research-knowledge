D-DIMENSIONAL DILUTE-GIBBS STABILITY AND CLASSICAL ARCHIVE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally proved candidate; novelty UNKNOWN.

THEOREM CANDIDATE
-----------------
For fixed d,J,tau>0, B>=0 and finite displacement set Z, take the spinless
fermion Fock space over Λ_L={1,…,L−1}^d. Let

  K=dΓ(4Jd I−2J A_D),  M=Σ_x n_x,
  H_0=K−lambda M/L²,  beta=tau L²,  |lambda|<=B.

Let P exclude configurations with any occupied forbidden pair at a
 displacement in Z. For every finite W_L>=0 with W_L P=0, with no locality,
number-conservation, or norm bound assumption, set

  c0=e^(tau B) Σ_{k∈N^d} e^(−8 tau J |k|²),
  c2=e^(tau B) Σ_{k∈N^d} |k|² e^(−8 tau J |k|²),
  A=2·4^d·pi²·c0·c2·Σ_{z∈Z}|z|²,  a=A/L^(d+2),  S=(L−1)^d,

  G_L=[h2(a)+a+(2+c0)a log S
       +tau·a(2J pi² c2+8Jd L²(2+c0)+B(2+2c0))]/(1−a).

For a<=1/2, the internally proved candidate gives

  D(rho_W||rho_0)<=G_L,
  ||rho_W−rho_0||_1<=sqrt(2G_L)=O(L^(−d/2)),

uniformly in lambda and W. For larger a use the trivial trace bound 2. The
model is genuinely d-dimensional spinless fermions; it is not a local
Jordan–Wigner theorem for hard-core spins in d>1.

The proof uses product-sine wedge bounds and free-state determinantal
correlations to show bad mass Tr(rho_0 Q)<=A/L^(d+2) and particle-weighted
bad mass Tr(rho_0 M Q)<=(2+c0)A/L^(d+2). A coherent allowed-space projection
has relative entropy at most G_L against the free Gibbs state. The direct
reverse-KL step is D(rho_W||rho_0)=−beta Tr(rho_W W)+log(Z0/ZW); min-max gives
ZW>=Z_P for the restricted allowed-space partition, and Gibbs variational
principle gives log(Z0/Z_P)<=G_L. This avoids the older gentle-projection
term and does not use operator monotonicity of exponentials.

COLLECTIVE ARCHIVE
------------------
For n product copies, D(rho_W^⊗n||rho_0^⊗n)<=nG_L and trace distance is at
most sqrt(2nG_L), vanishing for n=o(L^d). Measuring only total particle
number T=Σ_i M_i and preparing the free conditional state gives an
entanglement-breaking reconstruction with error <=2sqrt(2nG_L). The
conditional free state is independent of the unknown lambda. A Chernoff
cutoff t*=O(n+log(1/delta)) leaves O(log(n+2)+log(2+log(1/delta))) archive bits,
independent of L, plus at most 2delta trace error. For known tau, the
conditional Slater mixture is sampled by an elementary-symmetric-polynomial
DP in O(nS t*) arithmetic operations; input preparation, output-state
synthesis, and finite-precision costs are separate and explicit in PROOF.txt.

COMPACT UNKNOWN-TEMPERATURE EXTENSION
-------------------------------------
For public 0<tau_-<=tau<=tau_+<infinity and |lambda|<=B, replacing c0,c2,tau
by their uniform bounds in PROOF.txt gives G*_L=O(L^-d), uniformly over both
unknown parameters and the same W class. For n copies, jointly measure total
number T and bins of total scaled kinetic energy E=Σ_i L²K_i. Conditional on
(T, energy-bin), lambda cancels and the conditional law changes by at most
exp((tau_+-tau_-)h)-1 over an energy bin of width h. Number and energy
Chernoff cutoffs are O(n+log(1/delta)); choosing h=O(epsilon/(tau_+-tau_-))
uses O(log(n+2)+log(2+log(1/delta))+log(1/epsilon)) classical bits, independent of
L, with reconstruction error

  <=2sqrt(2nG*_L)+exp((tau_+-tau_-)h)-1+2delta.

The mode-space decoder has an explicit finite weighted-subset DP. On retained
energy labels it uses U=O((1+(R_g+t_*zeta)/J)^(d/2)) candidate modes per copy,
O(Q' t* Gmax) arithmetic/table entries, Q'=nU and
Gmax=O((R_g+t_*zeta)/g0)=O(R_g t*/epsilon) for the stated grid choice;
precision, randomness, transform measurements, Slater synthesis, and thermal
acquisition are all charged in the full proof. This is an approximate
sufficiency consequence, not a speedup or free physical preparation claim.
The displayed archive error is for ideal exact bins and conditional states.
For quantized bins, replace the shape term by
exp((tau_+-tau_-)(h+2t_*zeta))-1 and add the quantized-weight term
exp(2 tau_ref t_*zeta)-1, sampler trace error 2eta_DP for total variation
eta_DP, and Givens synthesis error eta_gate. Together with the ideal terms,
the complete finite-channel bound is at most 2sqrt(2nG*_L)+2delta plus
these four implementation terms. Certified lower rounding ensures 0<=tilde_e_k<=e_k and
e_k−tilde_e_k<=zeta, so tilde_E>R_g implies E>R_g. Rational grid labels
need no equality oracle for cosine energies. Allocate all four implementation terms within the desired epsilon budget.

BOUNDARIES
-----------
No attractive/indefinite W theorem, arbitrary positive W acting on P, d-D
hard-core-spin locality transfer, growing d or growing displacement set, or
external validation is claimed. The thermal input at beta=tau L² and decoder
state preparation are not free. The model is product copies of one Gibbs
state, not arbitrary correlated inputs.
