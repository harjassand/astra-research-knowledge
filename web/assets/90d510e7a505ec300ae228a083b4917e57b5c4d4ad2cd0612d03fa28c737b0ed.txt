1. NEW PHASE FUNCTION FOR PHYSICAL ACTIVATION OF C

Set
  s = 1 + delta^+/beta^- + 2 delta^+/alpha^- > 1,
  h(A,B) = (B-1)^2/(A+s),
  K = 2 alpha^+ + 3 beta^+,
  J = b^-/[s(s+1)],
  kappa_h = min(beta^-/(s+1), b^-/[2s(s+1)]),
  gamma_h = min(J, alpha^-/[2(s+1)]).
All are positive rational constants. A and B change in separate reactions;
the exact upstream generator identity is
  L h = (B-1)^2[-b/((A+s)(A+s+1))
                 + 1_{A>=1} delta A/((A+s-1)(A+s))]
        + A/(A+s)[-2 beta B^2+(2 alpha+3 beta)B-alpha].        (H)
The zero-root death indicator is essential and retained exactly.

At A=0, this equals -b(B-1)^2/[s(s+1)] <= 0. At A>=1 let
r=A/(A+s). The positive root coefficient is at most r delta^+/s.
Our s gives delta^+/s <= min(beta^-,alpha^-/2). Multiplying this upper
bound by the nonnegative polynomial (B-1)^2, and bounding the middle rates,
gives
  Lh <= r[-beta^- B^2 + K B - alpha^-/2].                   (H1)
Indeed, if z=min(beta^-,alpha^-/2), the intermediate polynomial is
  -(2 beta^- - z)B^2+(2 alpha^+ +3 beta^+-2z)B+z-alpha^-;
its quadratic coefficient is <=-beta^-, linear coefficient <=K and constant
<=-alpha^-/2. Thus three global inequalities follow:
  B=0:     Lh <= -gamma_h;
  B>=1:    Lh <= K B;
  all A,B: Lh <= J - kappa_h B^2 + K B.                    (H2)
For the last inequality, A>=1 uses r>=1/(s+1) and r<=1. At A=0 use
(B-1)^2 >= B^2/2-1. These include every integer boundary state.

2. ACQUIRED UNBOUNDED KILLED-PARTICLE CORRECTOR FOR D

Define
  epsilon = a^-/(4K),
  F = epsilon J + (epsilon K+t^+/2)^2/(4 epsilon kappa_h),
  M = 1 + (F+1)/(u^-/2),
  q_D(A,B,C) = M + 1/(C+1) + epsilon h(A,B),
  gamma_D = min(epsilon gamma_h, a^-/4, 1) > 0.
The function q_D is positive, at least M, and is unbounded as B grows at fixed A.
It satisfies the all-state common killed-generator inequality
  L_t q_D - (u^-/2) C q_D <= -gamma_D.                     (KD)
Only A,B,C reactions change q_D. When C=0,
  Lq_D = epsilon Lh - a B/2.
For B=0 this is <=-epsilon gamma_h. For B>=1 it is <=
(epsilon K-a^-/2)B=-a^- B/4 <=-a^-/4. This explicitly handles both the
all-upstream-zero face and the rapid B birth/death face at arbitrarily large A.

When C>=1, the reciprocal-C contribution is exactly
  -a B/[(C+1)(C+2)] + t B/(C+1).
Discard its negative term and use (H2) to bound
  Lq_D <= epsilon J-epsilon kappa_h B^2
                    +(epsilon K+t^+/2)B <= F.
Since C>=1 and q_D>=M,
  Lq_D-(u^-/2)Cq_D <= F-(u^-/2)M <= -1.
This proves (KD) without assuming an invariant law or a particle/controller
independence property. Actual per-particle death u(t)C is >=u^- C; half its
lower bound is deliberately reserved for cubic count terms below.

For the unit-rate chain, exact constants are

  epsilon=1/20, F=45001/400, M=45601/200, gamma_D=1/400,
  q_D=45601/200+1/(C+1)+(B-1)^2/[20(A+4)].
At A=B=100,C=0 the raw killed drift is

3. EXACT CUBIC IMMIGRATION-DEATH ENVELOPE

If Z has birth coefficient p and death coefficient qZ, both multiplied by
a nonnegative unchanged catalyst U (U=1 for the root), then
  L Z^3 / U <= -3q^- Z^3+3(p^++q^-)Z^2+(3p^+-q^-)Z+p^+.
This formula follows from the actual integer increments (Z+1)^3-Z^3 and
(Z-1)^3-Z^3; the death increment is negative at every Z>=1 and is disabled
at Z=0. Put
  T(p,q)=3(p^++q^-)+max(3p^+-q^-,0)+p^+,
  H(p,q)=1+2T(p,q)/(5q^-),
  K3(p,q)=T(p,q) H(p,q)^2.
For integer Z>=1, the residual after retaining -(q^-/2)Z^3 is at most
  -(5q^-/2)Z^3+T(p,q)Z^2.
It is nonpositive for Z>=H and at most T H^2 below H. At Z=0 the bound
also holds because p^+<=T H^2. Consequently, globally,
  L Z^3 <= U[-(q^-/2)Z^3+K3(p,q)].                        (CUBE)
The rational K3 is acquired by a fixed number of arithmetic operations;
there is no enumeration over a numeric rate-dependent threshold.
Denote the four constants for (b,delta),(alpha,beta),(a,t),(e,u) by
K_A,K_B,K_C,K_D.

4. BOUNDED LOWER-LAYER CORRECTORS, WITH THE RESERVED HALF DEATH

For B set
  c_B=min(1,beta^-/(2 delta^+)),
  g_B(A)=1+c_B/(A+1),
  gamma_B=min(c_B b^-/2,beta^-/4), G_B=1+c_B.
The exact root reciprocal calculation gives
  Lg_B-(beta^-/2)A g_B <=-gamma_B.                         (KB)
At A=0 its left side is -c_B b/2. At A>=1 it is bounded by
c_B delta^+/(A+1)-(beta^-/2)A <=-beta^-/4. Hence, from (CUBE),
  L(g_B B^3) <=-gamma_B B^3+G_B K_B A.                    (BCUBE)

For C use the independently derived three-layer representation, with half
of its death lower bound:
  s_C=1+4 delta^+/alpha^-,
  c_C=min(1,(t^-/2)/(delta^+/s_C+beta^+)),
  q_C(A,B)=1+c_C/[(A+s_C)(B+1)], G_C=1+c_C/s_C,
  gamma_C=min(c_C b^-/[s_C(s_C+1)],
              c_C alpha^-/[4(s_C+1)],t^-/4).
Then
  Lq_C-(t^-/2)B q_C <=-gamma_C.                           (KC)
For completeness, all cases are:
- A=B=0: only root immigration changes q_C, giving
  <=-c_C b^-/[s_C(s_C+1)].
- B=0,A>=1: discard negative root immigration, use the positive root-death
  term and the B-birth term to get
  <=c_C A/(A+s_C)[delta^+/(A+s_C-1)-alpha^-/2]
  <=-c_C alpha^- A/[4(A+s_C)]
  <=-c_C alpha^-/[4(s_C+1)].
- B>=1: positive root and B deaths are at most
  c_C(delta^+/s_C+beta^+)/(B+1) <=(t^-/2)/(B+1);
  the retained killing is at least (t^-/2)B, so their difference is
  <=-t^-/4. Birth and immigration terms discarded here are nonpositive.
There are no cross increments because no event changes both A and B.
Again (CUBE) gives
  L(q_C C^3) <=-gamma_C C^3+G_C K_C B.                    (CCUBE)
The new q_D, although unbounded, also has no dependence on D. Using (KD)
and (CUBE), with the physical D coefficients, therefore gives
  L(q_D D^3) <=-gamma_D D^3+K_D C q_D.                    (DCUBE)

5. ABSORB THE UNBOUNDED CORRECTOR INTO CUBIC UPSTREAM DRIFT

For nonnegative integer B,C,
  C q_D <=(M+1)C+(epsilon/s)C(B-1)^2
         <=(M+1+2epsilon/s)C^3+(2epsilon/s)CB^2
         <=[M+1+8epsilon/(3s)]C^3+[4epsilon/(3s)]B^3.      (ABS)
The second line uses (B-1)^2<=2B^2+2 and C<=C^3. The third uses the
ordinary weighted arithmetic/geometric mean inequality
CB^2 <=(C^3+2B^3)/3. All zero-coordinate cases are included.
Put
  t_B=K_D 4epsilon/(3s),
  t_C=K_D[M+1+8epsilon/(3s)],
  w_C=(1+t_C)/gamma_C,
  w_B=(1+t_B+w_C G_C K_C)/gamma_B,
  w_A=2(1+w_B G_B K_B)/delta^-,
  V=w_A A^3+w_B g_B B^3+w_C q_C C^3+q_D D^3,
  C0=w_A K_A.
All weights are positive rational. Sum the root (CUBE), (BCUBE), (CCUBE)
and (DCUBE) bounds. Use (ABS), A<=A^3 and B<=B^3 to obtain exactly
  LV <=C0-A^3-B^3-C^3-gamma_D D^3.
This proves (MAIN). V is nonnegative and norm-like, with
  V>=min(w_A,w_B,w_C,M)(A^3+B^3+C^3+D^3).
Its degree-five cross term is essential to the new representation. The
right-hand negative drift does NOT uniformly dominate V: no geometric drift
in V, exponential return moment, or exponentiation of V is inferred.

6. NONEXPLOSION, CLASSIFICATION AND ALL-CONTROL RECOVERY

Let N=A+B+C+D. Its positive drift obeys
  LN<=b^++alpha^+ A+a^+ B+e^+ C<=b^++L N
with L=max(alpha^+,a^+,e^+). Stop on a large N-ball and apply Gronwall to
1+N. The probability of reaching radius m by time T tends to zero as m grows.
Within a finite N-ball every allowed rate is bounded uniformly; therefore
infinitely many jumps there in finite time are impossible. This separately
proves nonexplosion for arbitrary predictable controls before using unbounded
Dynkin formulas. No deterministic permanence theorem is imported.

Every lattice state communicates with every other under homogeneous rates:
activate A,B,C in order, adjust D, then C, then B, then A to their desired
counts. Each selected unit birth or death is enabled and has positive finite
intensity. Thus the full N_0^4 lattice is one closed irreducible class. This
is an actual count-lattice proof; N77's separate-component escape cannot be
erased by an unproved deterministic class identification.

Write
  f(x)=A^3+B^3+C^3+gamma_D D^3,
  kappa=min(1,gamma_D),
  R=max(1,ceil((2C0+2)/kappa)), K_R={x:N<R}.
Outside K_R, f>=kappa N>=2C0+2, and hence
  LV<=-f/2-1.
Dynkin stopped also on a finite box and at time T, followed by nonexplosion,
Fatou and monotone convergence, proves for tau=first entrance in K_R
  E_x tau <=V(x),
  E_x integral_0^tau f(X_s) ds <=2V(x).                    (RET)
The inequalities are conditional on every current history and so uniform over
all allowed predictable controls. If x is already in the core, tau=0.

For a homogeneous state-feedback CTMC, the standard Foster theorem already
gives positive recurrence. One can also derive it without assuming finite
stationary V: stopped (MAIN) gives
  E integral_0^T f(X_s)ds <=V(x)+C0 T.
Because f is norm-like, Cesaro laws are tight. A countable-state subsequential
limit is invariant (the Cesaro/P_s total-variation difference is <=2s/T).
Irreducibility/nonexplosion gives uniqueness and positive recurrence. Fatou
gives E_pi f<=C0. This is componentwise for the proved full-lattice class;
it is not uniformity over arbitrary distinct stochastic components.
