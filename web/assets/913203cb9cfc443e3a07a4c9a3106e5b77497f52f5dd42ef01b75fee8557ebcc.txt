1. EXACT INTERFACE AND THEOREM
------------------------------
Use the same BOTH-endpoint spin-half chain and finite Hilbert space as
sol_ground_xxz/STRICT_VACUUM_FOCK.txt. S=L-1, L>=2, J>0, A=L^2 Hcrit,
N=sum n_i, W=sum n_i n_(i+1), B_kappa=A-kappa W-lambda N. Fix tau>0,
kappa>=0 and STRICT lambda<lambda_*(kappa). Define on this SAME finite
chain

 rho_(kappa,L)=exp(-tau B_kappa)/Z_(kappa,L),
 rho_(0,L)=exp[-tau(A-lambda N)]/Z_(0,L).

Every parameter and endpoint convention is public. This is ordinary
full-state trace norm, not a reference-system or diamond-distance claim.
All number sectors and their multiplicities remain included.

There exist finite C_W and L0, depending only on J,tau,kappa,lambda and
the strict margin, such that for all L>=L0:

 Tr(rho_(kappa,L) W)<=C_W/L,                              (1)
 0<=log(Z_(kappa,L)/Z_(0,L))<=tau kappa C_W/L,             (2)
 D(rho_(kappa,L)||rho_(0,L))
   +D(rho_(0,L)||rho_(kappa,L))<=tau kappa C_W/L,          (3)
 (1/2)||rho_(kappa,L)-rho_(0,L)||_1
                              <=sqrt(tau kappa C_W/L)/2. (4)

Natural logarithms are used in D. Both individual relative entropies
are nonnegative and bounded by the right side of (3). Kappa=0 gives
exact equality of the states; no positive lower coupling is needed.
Constants/onset may be chosen uniformly on compact (kappa,lambda) sets
with positive strict margin, at fixed J,tau. No efficient computation of
C_W or L0 is claimed. The asymptotic RATE here is to a FINITE-chain
critical Gibbs state, not a rate to the continuum/Fock limit.

3. CONTACT BOUND FOR THE ENTIRE MIXED THERMAL STATE
--------------------------------------------------
The pinned critical contact inequality states for a normalized pure
vector in the m-sector

 <W><=4m(m-1)/(L-1)
       +4(m-1)sqrt(m/(2J))*sqrt(<A>)/L.                 (10)

It also holds for every normalized mixed state in that sector: decompose
into pure vectors and use concavity of sqrt, or Cauchy--Schwarz. For
m=0,1 both contact terms are zero.

Write the block-diagonal thermal state as sum_m p_m rho_m, and
alpha_m=Tr(rho_m A) for p_m>0. Summing (10) and applying Cauchy--Schwarz
in the probability p_m gives

 Tr(rho W)<=4 E[N(N-1)]/(L-1)
  +[4/(sqrt(2J)L)] sum_m p_m (m-1)sqrt(m alpha_m)
 <=4 M_2/(L-1)+[4/(sqrt(2J)L)]sqrt(M_3 C_A),             (11)

because m(m-1)^2<=m^3 and sum p_m alpha_m=Tr(rho A)<=C_A.
For L>=2, 1/(L-1)<=2/L. Thus (1) holds with the explicit expression

 C_W=8M_2+[4/sqrt(2J)]sqrt(M_3 C_A).                    (12)

The expression is explicit in a and auxiliary partition SUPREMA; their
non-effective acquisition boundary is retained. This is a bound on the
FULL interacting Gibbs state, not a typical sector, a conditional
pure state, or only a bounded-energy eigenspace. No binomial multiplicity
or rare sector is discarded in (7)--(12).

4. EXACT RELATIVE-ENTROPY IDENTITIES AND TRACE COMPARISON
-------------------------------------------------------
Let f=log(Z_kappa/Z_0). Finite thermal states are strictly positive.
Their logarithms are exactly -tau B-log Z, so WITHOUT assuming that
B_kappa,B_0, W or their states commute,

 D(rho_kappa||rho_0)=tau kappa Tr(rho_kappa W)-f,
 D(rho_0||rho_kappa)=f-tau kappa Tr(rho_0 W).             (13)

As B_kappa=B_0-kappa W<=B_0, ordered eigenvalue min-max gives Z_kappa>=Z_0,
hence f>=0. This is a trace inequality; exp is not declared operator
monotone. Nonnegativity of the first entropy in (13) gives
 f<=tau kappa Tr(rho_kappa W)<=tau kappa C_W/L,
proving (2). Adding (13) yields the exact symmetrized identity

 D(rho_kappa||rho_0)+D(rho_0||rho_kappa)
    =tau kappa[Tr(rho_kappa W)-Tr(rho_0 W)]
    <=tau kappa Tr(rho_kappa W)<=tau kappa C_W/L,         (14)

since kappa,W>=0. The left side is nonnegative; this also proves
monotonicity of mean contact between these two Gibbs states, without
requiring a commuting Hamiltonian or a perturbative series.

Quantum Pinsker, in natural-log convention, is D(rho||sigma)>=2T^2
for T=(1/2)||rho-sigma||_1. Applying it to BOTH terms of (14) gives
4T^2<=tau kappa C_W/L, proving (4). Using only one direction gives
a valid weaker constant; the order L^(-1/2) is the material point.
No trace-norm continuity bound proportional to the ambient dimension
is used. The ordinary Pinsker/relative-entropy identities are established
controls, not new mathematical principles or optimality claims.
