# Selection-only feasibility bound for the Bedford IFNγ/IDO1 protocol

## Scope

This is a conditional two-class calculation, not an estimate of what happened in the Bedford et al. MSC experiment. It asks what a fixed-class selection model would require if the relevant responder fraction were known at the start of the ten-day rest. Here `p` is membership in a **latent, fixed response class**, not the instantaneous IDO1-positive fraction at rest start: the paper reports IDO1 expression returns to undetectable levels after rest.

Let the culture contain one unit of cells at rest start. A fraction `p` belongs to an immutable responder class `R`, and `1-p` to immutable nonresponders `N`. Let `G` be the total endpoint cell yield per starting cell after the rest and let `q` be the responder fraction measured after rechallenge. Under no class switching, class-specific mean descendant yields must be

\[
g_R=\frac{qG}{p},\qquad g_N=\frac{(1-q)G}{1-p}.
\]

These follow by equating the final class counts to `qG` and `(1-q)G`. The model is feasible for `0<p<1`, `0<q<1`, and nonnegative yields. It has no net sub-replacement of class `N` exactly when

\[
g_N\ge 1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad p\ge 1-G(1-q).
\]

The threshold is sharp: at equality, `g_N=1`; above it, `g_N>1`; below it, the fixed-class model requires the nonresponder class to have less than one endpoint descendant per rest-start cell on average. That is a net selection/bottleneck requirement, not proof of literal cell death.

## Source-plugged sensitivity, with the missing premise kept explicit

Bedford et al. report `q=0.985` (98.5% IDO1-positive after rechallenge) and `D=2.3` cumulative population doublings for the trained lineage over the ten-day rest. Treating the reported mean as the model input gives `G=2^2.3=4.92458`; therefore selection without sub-replacement of `N` requires

\[
p\ge 1-2^{2.3}(1-0.985)=0.92613.
\]

The paper does **not** report this `p`. Its 70.2% first-time-stimulation value is measured after a single 10-hour challenge in the *untrained* arm. The trained lineage instead receives a 24-hour priming pulse, rests ten days, and is rechallenged for ten hours. Thus `p=0.702` is not a source-supported rest-start fraction.

For sensitivity only, if one substituted `p=0.702`, the equations give `g_R≈6.91` and `g_N≈0.248`; the responder class expands while nonresponders have sub-replacement net yield. This numerical witness demonstrates how much the inference depends on the unmeasured premise; it does not establish such attrition in the source experiment.

## Exact switching alias

For any `p≤q`, a no-selection model with common yield `G` for both classes and conversion of fraction

\[
\alpha=\frac{q-p}{1-p}
\]

of the original `N` class into responders ends at the same `q` and total yield `G`. The source-style endpoint summaries `(q,G)` therefore admit both (i) fixed classes with differential yield and (ii) common yield with state conversion. At `p=0.702`, the latter uses `α≈0.950`; again, this is only an illustration because the required `p` is missing.

More generally, the unknown starting mixture, class-specific yields, transition rates, and transition times outnumber these aggregate observations. Extra endpoint omics do not identify which cells changed state unless they are linked to sufficiently resolved histories or an independently justified selection bound.

## Exact within-barcode counterexample

Coarse clonal barcodes do not by themselves remove the ambiguity. In one barcode clone, take `p_b=0.5`, endpoint `q_b=0.9`, and clone yield `G_b=2`. A fixed-substate selection explanation has `g_R=3.6`, `g_N=0.4`. A switching explanation gives the same clone endpoint and total yield by converting `α_b=0.8` of `N` to `R` while every cell has common yield 2. The barcode is unchanged in both constructions. Therefore observing an increased responder fraction *within a barcode* is not by itself proof of within-cell state conversion; hidden response-heterogeneous sublineages remain possible.

The counterexample is not a claim that the source contains these sublineages. It establishes the resolution requirement: barcode-level composition and clone size need direct birth/death/state histories, or an assay that measures the same cell's state before and after the perturbation.

## What would discriminate in this protocol

The first required observation is the response distribution during the actual 24-hour priming pulse, including at 10 hours (to align with the rechallenge readout) and immediately before washout. This is only an *operational predictor* for the latent `p`; it must be shown to predict response propensity after rest, since IDO1 itself returns to baseline. A matched, nonperturbing live IDO1 transcription reporter must be validated against endogenous RNA/protein and flow gates. Track every initially measured cell through the ten-day rest and the 10-hour rechallenge, retaining cell division, death, detachment, field exit, and tracking failure in the denominator. Neutral high-complexity barcodes or one-founder-per-microwell identity can link endpoint CUT&RUN/CUT&Tag and RNA/ATAC profiles to observed pedigrees, but barcodes alone are not the discriminant.

The direct phenotype test is whether low/negative first-pulse cells that remain tracked and survive produce reporter-positive descendants on rechallenge, with the transition visible in the same observed pedigrees and with measured lineage weights unable to account for the bulk shift by selection alone. A shift in a validated reporter establishes a within-lineage response change; it does not, by itself, establish that H3K27me3 loss caused it. Causal mark control requires a locus-specific perturbation/rescue that preserves matched viability and lineage accounting.

This design has a hard measurement limit: endpoint histone-mark profiling destroys the cell. It can be linked to a lineage or matched sister descendants, but not to the exact earlier molecular state of the same cell. The strongest feasible claim would therefore separate (a) reporter-level within-lineage response change, (b) clone-weight change, and (c) endpoint epigenetic association. Causal chromatin memory remains a separate gate.
