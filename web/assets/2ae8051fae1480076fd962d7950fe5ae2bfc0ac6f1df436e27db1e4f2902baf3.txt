1. SOURCE RATE ORIENTATION AND BIRTH-DEATH GAUGE

Write delta=(q-p)/3, r=p+delta, Q=r+delta, and assume p<=q. In the low
output range p+q<=a, the source parameters from its §1.3, Eq. (18), are

  xi1=N+1-Q, xi2=b+1, xi3=b+1-delta,
  xi4=b-Q, xi5=b-r, xi6=0,
  xi_b=max(b-r,0), xi_a=b+1-delta.

Thus the source basis has size xi_a-xi_b=min(r,b)-delta+1. If its
increasing source index is k=1,...,m, set
  J=b-(xi_b+k-1)=min(r,b)-k+1.
An adjacent source edge k<->k+1 therefore joins J=j+1 to J=j.

Substituting Eq. (18)'s source off-diagonal products gives

  a_(k,k+1)=-(a-Q+J)J(J-delta),
  a_(k+1,k)=(Q+1-J)(r+1-J)(b+1-J).

With lambda_- =(-a+b-3Q-3)/3 and
lambda_+ =2(a+2b+3)/3, the quartic-map off-diagonals are
H_(k,k+1)=-a_(k,k+1)[k+xi_b-lambda_-+2alpha] and
H_(k+1,k)=-a_(k+1,k)[k+xi_b-lambda_++2alpha]. For J=j+1 these
become, respectively,

  H_(j+1,j)=(a-Q+j+1)(j+1)(j+1-delta)
      [(a+2b)/3+Q+1-j+2alpha],
  H_(j,j+1)=(Q-j)(r-j)(b-j)
      [(2a+b)/3+j+2-2alpha].

After division by N^2 these are exactly the displayed A_j and C_j.
This confirms the orientation: A_j is row j+1, column j; C_j is row j,
column j+1. Both are positive on actual interior edges. Positivity follows
from j>=delta, j<=Jmax-1, Jmax=min(r,b), p+q<=a, and

  (2a+b+3)/3-2alpha
      =(2a+b+3)b(b+2)/(3C2)>0.

For a tridiagonal matrix with directed entries K_(j+1,j)=A_j>0 and
K_(j,j+1)=C_j>0, the diagonal similarity D^{-1}KD with
D_(j+1)/D_j=sqrt(A_j/C_j) has symmetric edge sqrt(A_j C_j).
Conjugating by diag((-1)^j) makes the edge negative. Its exact form is

  K_sym=G+diag(P_j),
  G=sum_j (sqrt(A_j)e_(j+1)-sqrt(C_j)e_j)
            (sqrt(A_j)e_(j+1)-sqrt(C_j)e_j)^*,
  P_j=K_(j,j)-A_(j-1)-C_j.

The endpoint conventions are correct: A_(delta-1)=0 by its j-delta
factor, and C_(Jmax)=0 because Jmax=min(r,b). Similarity preserves
eigenvalues; no claim that this is the physical Gram-metric quadratic
form is needed for the spectral estimate.

2. COMPACT POTENTIAL IDENTITY

The raw N^2 P_j from the exact source diagonals and quartic affine map is

  sigma_(p,q)-Y_j-2alpha X_j
  -(a-Q+j)j(j-delta)[(a+2b)/3+2alpha+Q+2-j]
  -(Q-j)(r-j)(b-j)[(2a+b)/3-2alpha+j+2].

The first subtracted term is the incoming A_(j-1); the second is the
outgoing C_j, so this is the correct diagonal of G subtracted from the
source diagonal. With nu=(2a+b+3)/3-2alpha,
t0=(nu-b)/2 and w=a+1-nu, expansion and alpha=C3/C2 give exactly Eq. (3)
of NARROW_WALL_WEIGHTED_COUNT.txt. The constant-term central remainder
is (4/3)alpha(C3-alpha C2), hence vanishes. I reran the checker:

  PASS exact symbolic compact-potential and directed-rate identities
  PASS exact Fraction diagnostics on 6 carriers / 66,378 fiber rows

The symbolic test is exact rational algebra conditional on its encoded
CPV formulas. Its directed-rate equality checks are substitutions of the
same expressions, so the independent orientation check above is the
source-index derivation, not that tautological subcheck. The finite
carrier sweep is corroboration only.

3. AUDIT OF (5)-(13) AND ENDPOINTS

(5)-(6): b/N<=.01 and b>=1 imply N>=100, a>=99b, N/a<=100/99,
b/a<=1/99, and (2a+b+3)/a<=2+4/99. Using 3C2>=a^2 and b+2<=3b
gives nu<=((2+4/99)3(100/99))b^2/N<9b^2/N<=.0009N. Thus
-.5b<=t0<=-.455b, t0^2>=b^2/5, and w>=.9891N>=.98N. These
constants are valid at the inclusive wall endpoint b/N=.01.

(7)-(8): chi=r^2+r delta+delta^2+2r+delta<=N/100 implies
r/N<=.01, Q/N<=.02, delta<=r, and j<=min(r,b). Since nu<=b,
2t0+delta+1<=delta+1. Also
chi-[delta(delta+2)+r(delta+1)]=r^2+r-delta>=0.
Consequently the bracket in the linear coefficient of (3) is at least
.98N(N+2)-N/100>.75N^2. No endpoint exception occurs at delta=0.

(9)-(10): In P0 the only negative terms are r^2 t0 delta and
r delta^2 t0. From |t0|<=b/2 and delta<=r their total is bounded below
by -b delta r^2. The other terms are nonnegative since t0+b>=b/2 and
nu>0. Also t0^2>=b^2/5. Hence
P0/N^2>=.2(br/N)^2-(b delta r^2/N^2). Because
br^2/N^2<=(b/N)(r^2/N)<=10^-4, the negative term is <=10^-4 delta.
For j>=1 the linear coefficient contributes >=.75(delta+1)j, leaving
more than the stated .7(delta+1)j. This proves (10), with z=br/N.
When r=delta=0 (the scalar output), z=0 and no positive-spin estimate
is required.

(11): For j>=1,
  a-Q+j>=.97N,
  (a+2b)/3+2alpha+Q+2-j=N+3-nu+Q-j>=.98N,
so A_(j-1)>=.9506j(j-delta)>.9j(j-delta). This also holds at
j=delta, where both the absent incoming edge and its stated lower bound
are zero. Since C_j has final factor nu+j+1, use Q-j<=r+delta,
r-j<=r and b-j<=b to get
C_j<=b r(r+delta)(nu+j+1)/N^2.
The nu term is at most 18(b/N)z^2<=.18z^2; for the j+1 term use
r(r+delta)<=chi<=N/100 and j+1<=2j to obtain at most .0002j,
hence the looser .0004j in (11). At j=Jmax, C_j=0 exactly.

(12)-(13): The identity
|u-v|^2>=|u|^2/2-|v|^2 is exact for complex u,v. Applied to each edge,
it gives diagonal coefficient P_j+A_(j-1)/2-C_j; (10)-(11) bound it
below by
  .45j(j-delta)+.7(delta+1)j-.0004j+.02z^2
  >=.4j(j+1).
For delta>=1 this holds on every coordinate, including j=delta where
A_(j-1)=0. The diagonal lower-bound sequence increases, so ordered
eigenvalues satisfy the same bound by min-max. For delta=0, deleting
coordinate j=0 gives the tail principal matrix. At j=1, the omitted
0<->1 edge contributes the full A_0|x_1|^2 on that tail, at least the
A_0/2 used above. Cauchy interlacing yields kappa_l>=.4(l-1)l for
l>=2; for l=1 positivity of L_S gives kappa_1>=0. This covers every
nonzero j_l, including m=1, where only the j=0 slot can remain. Since
eta=1-2b/N is in [.98,1) here,
.4j(j+1)>=.2[j+eta j^2]. The stated .2 constant is correct.

4. COUNT CONVERSION AND THE ONLY TEXT OMISSION

The block identity L_w=c/N^2+K^S/(N sqrt(C_S)) gives, at t=T/N^2,
kappa_l<=sqrt(C_S)(T-c)/N<=C(B/N)T. The exact carrier formula gives
sqrt(C_S) comparable to B uniformly for a>=b>=1. The output bound
c<=T implies p+q<=2sqrt(T); on T<=N/100 this is <=a in both regimes
once N>=100 (near-wall a>=.99N; otherwise a>=N/2). The exact low-output
LR interval then gives multiplicity <=min(p,q)+1. In the spectral
counting lemma, for fixed (delta,l) there are O(sqrt(T)) possible
outputs and each Weyl dimension is O(T^(3/2)); their contribution is
O(T^2). The relation j=delta+l-1 has j+1 preimages at fixed j. If
j+eta j^2<=C(B/N)T, then J^2<=C'(B/N)T: use J^2<=CT when B/N>=1/4,
and eta=1-2B/N+2/N>1/2 when B/N<1/4. Hence the near-wall sum is
O(T^2[1+(B/N)T]). For T<3 only the trivial output occurs. In the
interior, L_w>=L_T/N^2, and the exact low-output multiplicity bound,
O(sqrt(T)) output count and Weyl-dimension bound give D_T(T)-1=O(T^3).
Since B/N>=.01 there, O(T^3) is absorbed by O(T^2+(B/N)T^3).
Finally d=(a+1)(b+1)(N+2)/2 is comparable to N^2B, yielding H's
normalization as written.

One missing sentence in the target proof is the reduction p>q to p<=q.
For J(X)=X^dagger and each Hermitian S_A,
J ad(S_A) J=-ad(S_A), so J L_S J=L_S. Hermitian adjoint maps the
SU(3)-isotypic component V_(p,q) to its conjugate V_(q,p), with the same
multiplicity-space spectrum. Therefore the p<=q proof covers p>q,
with at most a factor two in a raw label count. This follows from the
given Hermitian-generator setup and is not a new spectral assumption,
but it should be written explicitly before claiming the full count.
