STRONGEST CLAIM

For rational-complex F of size n and nonnegative rational row activities
lambda_i, acquire an exact determinant-parity norm and all orientation prefix
masses, then an exact classical microstate sampler, in
  O(n(k+1)(d+1)^2 7^w) Gaussian-integer ring operations per contraction,
where an explicitly supplied site ordering has right frontier width w and
maximum future support degree d. The denominator clearing and every ring
operand have polynomial bit length in n and binary input length L.
Recomputing at most 3n contractions gives a complete implemented sampler
with O(n^2(k+1)(d+1)^2 7^w) ring operations per sample. No unknown coefficient,
balancing, conditional-orientation, or success-probability oracle is supplied.
This is polynomial bit time for w=O(log n), including arbitrary phases and
nonbipartite support. It is exponential for unrestricted w.

A full-refresh transition using this sampler has exactly rational acquired
point probabilities, acceptance one, spectral gap one, and an additive
two-coordinate energy comparison with constant one when both coordinates
are refreshed. These mixing claims concern the acquired bounded-width
refresh, with its charged contraction cost. They are not polynomial mixing
claims for an unrestricted local determinant sampler.

An uncapped fair-random-bit program is expected-time exact. The implemented
capped version uses at most R rejection trials per categorical choice; one
complete sample has TV error at most n 2^(-R). Thus a bounded-execution
TV-eta sampler follows using R>=ceil(log2(n/eta)). It avoids conditioning
on the rare event of an original projection and uses no floating arithmetic.

EXACT INTERFACE AND ENLARGED STATE

Fix k with 0<=2k<=n. A microstate is a pair (I,J) of disjoint subsets of sites,
|I|=|J|=k. Its point weight and partition are
  W(I,J)=prod_(i in I) lambda_i * |det F[I,J]|^2,
  C_k=sum_(I,J disjoint,|I|=|J|=k) W(I,J).
If C_k=0 the program reports zero exactly. Otherwise mu(I,J)=W/C_k.
Cauchy--Binet identifies the I marginal with the original selected paired-line
law det Gram({e_i,row_i F : i in I}) times prod lambda_i. J is an acquired
auxiliary orientation register, not an input-dependent uncharged oracle.
For the ambient-hole partition f(U), forbid sites in U and take
k=(n-|U|)/2. All remaining sites are then automatically occupied. Outside
U, row/column orientation is summed. Local allowed-label constraints support
all prefixes, force/exclude row/column sites, and impose a fixed retained set.

The support graph has undirected edge ij when i!=j and F_ij or F_ji is nonzero.
Diagonals never occur because I and J are disjoint. Relabel by the supplied
ordering 0,...,n-1. After processing 0,...,t, define the right frontier
  B_t={j>t : some i<=t has support edge ij}.
Set w=max_t |B_t| and d=max_i #{j>i : support edge ij}. This is a computed
certificate for an acquired ordering, not a free optimal-pathwidth oracle.
A bandwidth-b matrix has w<=b. Some long-edge graphs, including cycles in
the natural order, have w=2 even though the edge span is n-1.

EXACT TWO-DETERMINANT FRONTIER CONSTRUCTION

Label each site a_i in {0,+1,-1}; +1 means I, -1 means J, 0 is unoccupied.
For fixed labels define the skew matrix on occupied sites by, for i<j,
  K_a(i,j)=F_ij if a_i=+1,a_j=-1;
           -F_ji if a_i=-1,a_j=+1;
           0 otherwise.
The grouped row/column form is [[0,F[I,J]],[-F[I,J]^T,0]]. Hence
Pf(K_a)=s(a)*(-1)^(k(k-1)/2) det F[I,J], where s(a) in {+1,-1} is the
ordering sign. Therefore |Pf(K_a)|^2=|det F[I,J]|^2.
The Pfaffian expansion in site order is
  Pf(K_a)=sum_M (-1)^(cr(M)) prod_(ij in M,i<j) K_a(i,j),
where cr(M) counts pairs of arcs i<r<j<s. Expand the squared modulus as
independent perfect matchings M1,M2 on the SAME occupied sites, with common
labels, weights from K and its complex conjugate, and both crossing signs.
These are determinant replicas inside one point-weight calculation. No
same-cut restriction is imposed between two independent Markov microstates.

Scan sites in increasing order. A pending endpoint j>t in replica ell means
an edge from a processed vertex has been opened to j; its other endpoint has
not been processed. The frontier state records two endpoint subsets A,B and
a row-type subset P of A union B. Column type is the complement of P inside
the union. It also records the number q of row labels already processed.
At each frontier site there are exactly seven allowed records:
  no pending endpoint;
  pending in replica1 only, replica2 only, or both, each with row/column type.
Thus at most (k+1)7^w frontier states exist.

At site v, if v is pending in either replica its label is forced by P.
Otherwise choose 0,+1,-1, subject to allowed-label restrictions. Label0
is legal only with no pending endpoints, and opens nothing. An occupied
label closes each pending replica edge at v, or, in a replica without one,
opens a new edge to a future support neighbor j. A future endpoint j may
not already be pending in the same replica. Its required label is opposite
to the current one; if it is pending in the other replica, that type must
agree. A newly assigned future type is stored. Multiply lambda_v once when
v has row label and increment q. Both replicas independently enforce degree
one at every occupied site. End at the empty frontier and q=k.

The sign is acquired without storing an exponential permutation list:
when opening v->j in one replica, multiply (-1)^m, where m is the number
of its existing pending endpoints e with e<j. Every existing open edge has
an earlier opener u<v. If v<e<j it crosses the new edge; if v<j<e it nests
and does not cross. Thus every crossing is charged once, at the later
opener, and every nesting is charged zero times. Closing carries no new
sign. The program implements this as popcount(mask & ((1<<j)-1)).

Each fixed label/pair-of-matchings term occurs exactly once: the vertex scan
uniquely determines its close/open choices, and any legal DP history defines
such a term. The transition weight is precisely its Pfaffian-product weight
including the conjugate and the two crossing signs. Summing final states
therefore gives C_k with allowed labels. This proves the contraction, not
just its agreement on finite fixtures.

NEGATIVE INTERMEDIATE WEIGHTS ARE ESSENTIAL

The transfer coefficients and DP messages may be negative or complex.
They are exact Gaussian-integer algebra, not a positive matching circuit or
an even-windable decomposition. No signed DP edge is interpreted as a sample
probability. Only complete conditional norm sums are sampled, and those
are nonnegative by the determinant-squared identity just proved. Therefore
the four-site even-windability and shared-core sewing obstructions are not
contradicted or bypassed by silently assuming a positive representation.

DENOMINATORS, OPERAND HEIGHTS, AND COSTS

Let D clear the real and imaginary denominators of every F entry, so
G=DF is Gaussian integer. Let A clear all activity denominators and put
alpha_i=A lambda_i in nonnegative integers. The DP computes the integer
  N_k=sum_(I,J) prod_(i in I) alpha_i * |det G[I,J]|^2,
then C_k=N_k/(D^2 A)^k. Allowed-prefix denominators are the same, so every
categorical branch ratio is an exact rational ratio of nonnegative integers.
Use an LCM or product for clearing: its bit length is bounded by the sum of
input denominator bit lengths, hence polynomial. No eigenvalue, square root,
normalization, numerical determinant tolerance, or random zero test is used.

Write H>=1 for a bound on the absolute real/imaginary parts of G entries,
and Amax=max(1,max alpha_i). A partial DP coefficient is a signed sum of at
most 3^n(n+1)^(2n) local double-matching histories. A history has at most
2n edge factors and n activity factors. Its absolute magnitude is at most
(2H)^(2n) Amax^n. Thus every intermediate operand has
O(n(log(n+1)+log H+log Amax)) bits (with a harmless additional constant for
complex components), polynomial in input length. Cancellation cannot cause
unbounded operand height or a floating precision floor, because all arithmetic
is exact. Standard integer arithmetic consequently yields the claimed
polynomial-bit times 7^w cost.

There are at most three label options and (d+1)^2 endpoint choices per state.
One contraction takes O(n(k+1)(d+1)^2 7^w) ring operations and stores
O((k+1)7^w) Gaussian integers, plus input and masks. Mask operations have
polynomial(n) bit cost included in the bit complexity. The actual Python
implementation recomputes whole contractions during self reduction; it does
not claim a precomputed forward/backward optimization. A sample uses at most
3n+1 contractions. Storage stays at the single-contraction bound. Common
input-denominator factors are not repeatedly expanded inside the DP.

SELF-REDUCTION, INITIALIZATION, AND ACCEPTANCE

At a fixed prefix a_0,...,a_(v-1), compute the three exact child masses by
forcing a_v=-1,0,+1. They sum to the current mass. Choose a child by an
integer categorical draw and continue. The conditional masses are exactly
those of mu, so multiplication of the branch probabilities gives W/C_k.
A zero child is never selected. If C_k>0 an actual supported initialization
is acquired without a support theorem: at each site choose the first positive
child. This costs the same order as one sample and yields a witness. There
is no exponentially large precomputed prefix table.

For a uniform integer in [0,N), take b=ceil(log2 N) fair bits and reject
values >=N. Acceptance is at least1/2, and accepted values are uniform.
At most two trials are needed in expectation, with O(b) expected bits.
Every N has polynomial bit length. With R trials per choice and a positive
lexicographic fallback on failure, the implemented run has a fixed polynomial
bit budget times 7^w. Couple to the uncapped ideal algorithm; the chance of
any failure in n choices is at most n2^(-R). This proves TV<=eta for the
stated R. Conditioning on successful capped runs is NOT claimed to preserve
exactness. The ideal computational random primitive is an explicit fair-bit
assumption; Python's seeded diagnostics are not hardware-randomness evidence.

Set P(x,y)=mu(y). All transition probabilities are now acquired rationals
because both point weight and C_k are computed. Every transition is accepted;
detailed balance mu(x)P(x,y)=mu(x)mu(y) is exact. One refresh erases dependence
on the initialized state. On L2(mu), P is projection onto constants; centered
eigenvalues are zero and the spectral gap is one. The lazy version (I+P)/2
has gap1/2. For two coordinates and full-pair refresh Q=P tensor P,
  E_(mu tensor mu,Q)(f1(x)+f2(y))=Var_mu f1+Var_mu f2.
Hence Var_mu f1<=E with constant1. Independent single-coordinate refresh
chosen uniformly instead has gap1/2 and additive constant2. These are the
strongest supported mixing statements, with refresh cost paid in full.
The capped sampler approximates the refresh in TV and is not assigned the
exact stationary-law/spectral-gap claim.
