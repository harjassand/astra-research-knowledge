1. K-DIMENSIONAL NONLINEAR SUFFICIENT STATISTICS

The one-dimensional construction tensorizes. Let T:X -> (R/Z)^k have pushforward mu equal to uniform product measure, with a theta-independent regular conditional law mu_t. Suppose
  p_theta(x)=g_theta(T(x))
(or sup_theta TV(P_theta, g_theta(T)mu)=eta), where each g_theta is a normalized C^2 density on the k-torus. For coordinate l, assume supplied envelopes
  A_(1,l)(t) >= |partial_l g_theta(t)|,
  A_(2,l)(t) >= |partial_l^2 g_theta(t)|
for every theta. Choose positive C^1 monitor q_l(t_l), Q_l=int q_l, q_l,-=min q_l, L_l=||q_l'||_infinity. Use D_l q_l-quantile knots in coordinate l. With delta_l=Q_l/D_l and D_l>=max(3,4 Q_l L_l/q_l,-^2), the one-coordinate operator K_l satisfies
  ||(I-K_l)g_theta||_1/2 <= E_l(D_l),
where
  E_l(D_l)=delta_l^2[(8/3)L_l int A_(1,l)(t)/q_l(t_l)^3 dt
                  +4 int A_(2,l,D_l)(t)/q_l(t_l)^2 dt],
  A_(2,l,D_l)(t)=sup_{dist(s,t_l)<=4delta_l/q_l(t_l)} A_(2,l)(t_1,...,s,...,t_k).
The integrations are over the k-torus. Each K_l is a positive L1 contraction preserving uniform measure. Operators in separate coordinates commute. Telescoping gives
  ||(I-product_l K_l)g_theta||_1/2 <= sum_l E_l(D_l).
The encoder sends the tuple of hat labels; the total register has D_total=product_l D_l values, or sum_l log2 D_l bits. The decoder uses the product conditional reference kernels. The approximate-factorization case adds 2 eta by data processing and the target triangle inequality. For fixed k and fixed envelopes, choosing each D_l=O(epsilon^(-1/2)) yields O((k/2)log2(1/epsilon)) bits, with constants charged to the supplied latent family and monitors. This is not uniform in growing k.

Proof of tensor step: write I-K_1...K_k=sum_l K_1...K_(l-1)(I-K_l); commute coordinate operators and apply their L1 contraction property. The one-coordinate bound applies with other coordinates fixed; integrating them gives E_l. No independence of the unknown parameter or Fisher orthogonality is assumed.

2. WHY THE FIRST-MOMENT DRIFT CANNOT BE REMOVED

Let X,Y be [0,1]-valued with the same reference law and let K be their Markov coupling. If E[Y|X]=X, then
  E[(Y-X)^2]=E[Y^2]+E[X^2]-2E[X E[Y|X]]=0.
Thus Y=X almost surely: a nontrivial reference-preserving Markov codec cannot exactly preserve the ordinary coordinate as a conditional mean. On the circle, the analogous condition E[exp(2 pi i Y)|X]=exp(2 pi i X) also forces equality, because a conditional average of unit-modulus points has modulus one only when the point is constant almost surely. This makes the adjacent-gap drift term in INITIAL structural rather than a proof artifact. Smooth monitor quantiles control it at order D^-2; they do not set it to zero.
