Proof checks
------------
Let d=dim(H), delta=Tr(rho Q), p=1-delta>0, sigma=P rho P/p, and (when
delta>0) omega=Q rho Q/delta. The pinching map gives S(rho)<=S(P rho P+Q rho
Q)=h_2(delta)+pS(sigma)+delta S(omega). Rearranging gives

  S(sigma) >= [S(rho)-h_2(delta)-delta S(omega)]/p,
  S(rho)-S(sigma)
    <= [h_2(delta)+delta S(omega)-delta S(rho)]/p
    <= [h_2(delta)+delta log d]/p,

using S(rho)>=0 and S(omega)<=log d. The direct subtraction
S(rho)-S(sigma)<=h_2(delta)+delta log d is stronger; hence the displayed
PROOF.txt bound is valid (looser) because p<=1 and its numerator is
nonnegative.

Its log dimension is the full finite Hilbert-space dimension, as stated. At
delta=0, sigma=rho and the claim follows directly.

For finite W>=0, rho_W is full rank. If W is supported on the rejected
subspace, W sigma=0 and

  D(sigma||rho_W)=-S(sigma)+beta Tr(sigma H0)+log Z_W.

Since H0+W >= H0, the min-max principle orders their eigenvalues, giving
Z_W<=Z_0. This does not require [H0,W]=0 and does not assert an ordering of
the heat matrices. Subtracting the exact Gibbs identity for rho_0 gives

  D(sigma||rho_W)
    <= S(rho_0)-S(sigma)
       + beta[Tr(sigma H0)-Tr(rho_0 H0)].

The Section 3 upper bound on the K-energy difference is sufficient here; the
field term is safely bounded in absolute value. Inserting delta<=a_L,
p>=1-a_L, h_2(delta)<=h_2(a_L) for a_L<=1/2, and the supplied q_M bound
reproduces G_L term by term. D is in nats, so quantum Pinsker is
||sigma-rho_W||_1<=sqrt(2D). Finally, a purification of rho followed by
successful projection P has overlap sqrt(p) with the original purification;
Fuchs-van de Graaf and contractivity under partial trace give
||sigma-rho_0||_1<=2sqrt(1-p)=2sqrt(delta). Triangle inequality then yields
the stated 2sqrt(a_L)+sqrt(2G_L). No extra conditioning/normalization term is
missing.
