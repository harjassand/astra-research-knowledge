1. Family argument in full

Take a compact quotient M=Gamma\PSL(2,R) and an orthonormal left-invariant
frame E0,E+,E- with brackets
  [E0,E+]=-aE+, [E0,E-]=aE-, [E+,E-]=beta E0,
where a>0 and beta!=0. Such a frame is a basis of sl2(R):
  H=-2E0/a, e=E+, f=-2E-/(a beta)
obeys [H,e]=2e, [H,f]=-2f, [e,f]=H. The frame metric is left-invariant,
so it descends under the left action of Gamma. The group PSL(2,R) is
unimodular; equivalently all three adjoint traces vanish. Its Haar volume is
therefore the Riemannian volume and is invariant under the right-action flow
generated by X=vE0.

The flow is Phi_t(Gamma g)=Gamma g exp(tvE0). For a left-invariant vector
field Y, D Phi_t Y is the left-invariant vector field with value
Ad(exp(-tvE0))Y. The adjoint action is diagonal on E+,E-:
  D Phi_t E+ = exp(avt) E+,
  D Phi_t E- = exp(-avt) E-,
and D Phi_t X=X. This is a uniform Anosov splitting. The smooth invariant
Haar measure has positive exponent av at every point, so the smooth-measure
Pesin formula gives h_mu(Phi_1)=av.

Koszul gives the full covariant-derivative matrix
  A=matrix(nabla X)=[[0,0,0],[0,av,beta*v/2],[0,-beta*v/2,-av]].
The beta entries are skew; dropping them would be an error in the derivative,
though they cancel in its symmetric part:
  Def X=(A+A^T)/2=diag(0,av,-av).
The trace is zero, |Def X|^2=2a^2v^2, and nabla_X X=0. Contracting the
covariant derivative of this invariant tensor gives
  div(Def X)=(-2a^2v,0,0)=-2a^2X.
Thus F=4nu a^2 X and p=0 solve the exact steady constitutive equation.
Work density is 4nu a^2v^2 and equals 4nu h_mu(Phi_1)^2.
The coefficient beta is absent from the equality: it changes the metric
curvature and the skew covariant derivative without changing X's rate or
strain eigenvalues.

For example, a=3/2 and beta=1 produce scalar curvature
  Scal=-2a^2-beta^2/2=-5.
At the same a, beta=2 gives Scal=-13/2. The two metrics on the same
underlying group quotient are not isometric because scalar curvature differs.
Their flow is still the same diagonal one-parameter subgroup up to the
chosen speed v; varying beta alone changes the metric, not the group orbit
formula. This separates metric novelty from dynamical novelty.

2. Constant-curvature geodesic subfamily

On a closed orientable curvature -kappa^2 surface, the standard unit
orthonormal frame has brackets
  [X,H0]=-kappa^2 V0, [X,V0]=-H0, [H0,V0]=X.
With
  E+=(H0+kappa V0)/sqrt(1+kappa^2),
  E-=(H0-kappa V0)/sqrt(1+kappa^2),
the same frame equations hold with a=kappa, v=1 and
beta=-2kappa/(1+kappa^2). This is a direct symbolic change of basis.
The Sasaki volume integrates as 2pi times base area. Gauss-Bonnet gives
Area=4pi(g-1)/kappa^2, hence V=8pi^2(g-1)/kappa^2. The exact total work
is therefore 32pi^2 nu(g-1), invariant under curvature rescaling at fixed
genus and viscosity. Work density is 4nu kappa^2 and is not invariant.

This invariance is a volume-scale cancellation, not a universal device or
algorithmic cost law. Rescaling X at fixed metric still changes H linearly
and work quadratically. Entropy is for the time-one flow under a supplied
clock; the force is a chosen continuum residual, not an actuator schedule.

3. Error-controlled arithmetic and compute budget

The script recomputes all brackets and Koszul coefficients from structure
constants, rather than copying the earlier cycle-3 script. It verifies 15
identities, including Jacobi, trace/unimodularity, torsion-freeness, metric
compatibility, the full nabla-X skew terms, symmetric strain, div stress,
Ricci scalar, exact force/work residual and Sasaki-frame bracket transform.
All parameter examples use Fraction values. The symbolic identity is over
exact rational functions. There is no ODE integrator and no estimated
entropy.

For transcendental display quantities, the code uses positive exact rational
enclosures. Machin's formula plus the alternating arctan remainder gives
pi_low <= pi <= pi_high, width < 10^-30. Since pi>0, squaring preserves the
interval order; multiplying by the exact positive work coefficient gives
outward-rounded 24-decimal W intervals. For exp(x), x>=0, S_N is a lower
bound, and the tail is bounded by t_(N+1)/(1-x/(N+2)) when x/(N+2)<1.
This gives an outward interval for the one-time stretch factor, not an
entropy estimate. Independent measurement error propagation at an equality
point has zero slack; the exact residual proof is essential.

With dimension fixed at three, the symbolic loops have bounded size (at most
81 index tuples for the Jacobi check). For rational input bit length B, the
resulting fixed-degree rational expressions have polynomial bit cost. The
series use 21 terms here and exact rational products. Inputs a,beta,v,nu,
surface genus, constant-curvature model and co-compact lattice are supplied.
No cost is charged for acquiring those objects; no finite-element solve,
instrument calibration, force synthesis, or robustness against model error is
implemented.

4. Prior art and novelty downgrade

The exact examples do not add a new class of Anosov flows. Asaoka's primary
paper states that a closed three-dimensional Anosov flow with a C2
projectively-Anosov splitting is smoothly equivalent to an algebraic Anosov
flow, whose models are the PSL(2,R) and Sol types (Theorem 1.1 and the
preceding discussion). Our family has a smooth invariant splitting and is
explicitly PSL(2,R)-algebraic. Ghys's primary paper already classifies
volume-preserving Anosov flows with smooth weak stable and unstable
distributions. These are direct prior-art contacts for the rigidity part of
the cycle-3 equality analysis. A full novelty assessment of the sharp
entropy/strain inequality still needs specialist comparison, but the
non-Sol examples here do not increase its status.

The primary Navier-Stokes-on-manifolds source validates the chosen
symmetrized-gradient stress convention and integrated work identity. The
Pesin smooth-volume theorem supplies metric entropy from the exact
exponents. Neither source proves a computer-cost theorem for acquiring
entropy or implementing the sustaining force.

VERDICT
The assigned example task is met: exact non-Sol compact examples exist, the
finite algebra/volume/force computations are certified with rational error
controls, and the physical computational boundary is explicit. The result
is a transparent exact benchmark, not a new entropy theorem, an efficient
measurement protocol, external validation, or a field-breaking advance.
