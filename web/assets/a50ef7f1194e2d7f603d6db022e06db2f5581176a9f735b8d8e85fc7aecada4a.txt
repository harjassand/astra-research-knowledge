1. EXACT THEOREM AND SCOPE

Let (M,tau) be a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal tracial
state, H=L2(M,tau), L(m)xi=m xi and R(m)xi=xi m. Let X,Y be finite, with
unit vectors a_x,b_y in H, and finite POVMs A_s^x in L(M), B_t^y in R(M).
Write A_s^x=L(alpha_s^x), B_t^y=R(beta_t^y), with positive M elements
summing to 1. Let nu be ANY joint probability on X times Y, and
                   D=E_nu ||a_x-b_y||_2^2.
The ideal answer table is
 p_xy(s,t)=<a_x,A_s^x B_t^y a_x>.
If a finite trace is not normalized, normalize it and apply the corresponding
scalar L2 isometry to all vectors; D and every answer probability are unchanged.

For every zeta>0, there are finite integers ell,N, ONE unit resource Omega
in L2((M_ell(C) tensor M)^tensor N), and input-local finite POVMs
 Ahat_s^x in the fixed LEFT algebra and Bhat_t^y in its fixed RIGHT algebra,
such that their answer law phat satisfies
              E_nu TV_half(phat_xy,p_xy) <=1000 D^(1/4)+zeta.
Here TV_half(p,q)=(1/2) sum|p-q|. All cross-input pairs commute.
Omega and global parameters may depend on the full finite family, nu and
zeta; Alice's effects depend only on x and Bob's only on y.

This is correlation simulation. It does NOT construct a trace-preserving
output channel into the original fixed M/M' representation. The selected
tensor slot is input-dependent and is read by a local effect; no center-
permuting state-output map is claimed. M can be infinite dimensional and
need not be a factor, hyperfinite, microstate-approximable or separable.
Finite trials/labels do not mean that its standard Hilbert space is finite.
No computational efficiency or game-repetition consequence is asserted.

2. A MODULUS BOUND WITHOUT POLAR UNITARIES OR FIDELITY IMPORTS

For arbitrary a,b in L2(M), form Hermitian affiliated operators in M2(M),
             Ha=[[0,a],[a*,0]], Hb=[[0,b],[b*,0]].
With normalized matrix trace tensor tau, spectral coupling gives, for any
real 1-Lipschitz scalar f,
             ||f(Ha)-f(Hb)||_2^2 <=||Ha-Hb||_2^2.
Indeed the joint spectral measure of LEFT Ha and RIGHT Hb on vector I is
 m(E times F)=tau_2(E_Ha(E) E_Hb(F)). Integrating (f(s)-f(t))^2 and using
the scalar Lipschitz bound proves the claim. Left/right spectral projections
commute on standard L2; this is a genuine positive measure, not a product
assumption about noncommuting projections within M. Truncation gives the
same formula for unbounded L2 operators.

Since |Ha|=diag(|a*|,|a|), taking f(t)=|t| yields
 |||a*|-|b*|||_2^2 + |||a|-|b|||_2^2 <=2||a-b||_2^2.
In particular                       |||a|-|b|||_2^2 <=2||a-b||_2^2.       (1)

Only Alice's bounded polar PARTIAL isometry will be used. Bob's polar
factor is unnecessary: proximity of the right moduli already follows from
(1). Replacing both polar factors by unitaries is neither assumed nor used.

3. COMMON SPECTRAL TRUNCATION FOR ARBITRARY FINITE M

Assume 0<zeta<1 and later choose 0<h<1/2 and 0<epsilon<1/4. For the FINITE
positive families |a_x|,|b_y|, choose common 0<kappa<K, K>=2, so cutting
outside [kappa,K] has L2 error at most epsilon for every family member.
Small-coefficient error is bounded by kappa since tau(1)=1; large tails
tend to zero by L2 integrability and normality. Thus such cutoffs exist,
without a dimension, rank, minimum eigenvalue or operator-norm premise.

Normalize the cut positive elements to unit L2 norm; call them c_x,d_y.
They are bounded, with all positive spectrum in [kappa,2K]. Their distances
to their original moduli are at most 2epsilon. Let u_x be the polar partial
isometry of the normalized truncated vector a'_x=u_x c_x, with
 u_x* u_x=p_x=supp(c_x). Then
 ||a'_x-a_x||_2<=2epsilon,
 delta_xy^2:=||c_x-d_y||_2^2<=4||a_x-b_y||_2^2+32epsilon^2.                 (2)
The normalized vectors a'_x are unit. All truncations and polar choices
use only the corresponding local description and the common cutoffs.

4. FINITE SOFT LABELS AND EXPLICIT SCALAR CONSTANTS

For s=k+t, k integer, t in [0,1), define the unit label
 g(s)=cos(pi t/2)e_k+sin(pi t/2)e_(k+1).
For lambda>0 put g_lambda=g(-log(lambda)/h); put g_0=e_perp.
Take the finite integer interval
 J=[floor(-log(2K)/h)-1, ceil(-log(kappa)/h)+1] intersect Z,
plus the distinct label perp. It contains all labels used by every c_x,d_y.
The label dimension ell=|J|+1 is finite. Define
 T=sum_(k in J) exp(-kh) E_kk tensor 1,   T_perp=0.
Set tau_k=exp(-kh), and write g_k(lambda) for each label component,
including g_perp(lambda)=1_{lambda=0}. All these functions are real.

For lambda,mu>=0 and r_lm=<g_lambda,g_mu> in [0,1], direct scalar bounds are
 ||(T-lambda)g_lambda||<=2h lambda,
 mu(1-r_lm)<=4|lambda-mu|/h,
 lambda mu(1-r_lm^2)<=16(lambda-mu)^2/h^2.                                (3)
The first follows from the e^(+/-h) eigenvalue sandwich. For the other two,
g has Lipschitz constant pi/2<2. If |log(lambda/mu)|<=h, use
max(lambda,mu)(1-e^-x)>=max(lambda,mu)x/2. If x>h, use the bound
max(lambda,mu)(1-e^-h)>=max(lambda,mu)h/2 and r in [0,1]. Zeros obey
the same inequalities separately. Values lambda>1 merely give negative
integer labels; no Schmidt-coefficient upper bound is imported.

5. EXACT ROW OPERATORS IN THE AMPLIFIED FINITE ALGEBRA

Let Mtilde=M_ell(C) tensor M, with finite trace Tr tensor tau. For c=c_x
or d=d_y define the row
 C_c=sum_(k in J union {perp}) E_(perp,k) tensor g_k(c),
 P_c=C_c* C_c.
Then C_c C_c*=E_perp, so P_c is a projection. Put A_c=C_c* c C_c, where
c in this formula is on the output perp corner. Define C_d,P_d,A_d likewise.
All these are bounded elements of Mtilde, even when original a,b were only
L2 vectors. The resource trial is the unit vector T/sqrt(S), where
                     S=||T||_2^2=sum_(k in J) tau_k^2.
Alice filters by LEFT P_c; Bob by RIGHT P_d. Their one-trial success and
joint-success probabilities are a/S,b/S,c0/S, where
 a=||P_c T||_2^2, b=||T P_d||_2^2, c0=||P_c T P_d||_2^2.
By the scalar sandwich,                 e^-2h<=a,b<=e^2h.                 (4)

The synchronization defect is EXACT:
 m=a+b-2c0=||P_c T-T P_d||_2^2.                                         (5)
There is no independence of the two same-trial flags; their joint law
is obtained from the commuting LEFT/RIGHT projections on the one resource.
Using (3),
 ||P_cT-A_c||_2^2<=4h^2,    ||T P_d-A_d||_2^2<=4h^2.
The joint spectral measure of left c and right d on I gives

  <=33 delta_xy^2/h^2.
Here dm_cd(E times F)=tau(E_c(E)E_d(F)); its total mass is 1 and its
marginals are the individual spectral laws. No joint diagonalization in

                     m<=100 F_xy,  F_xy=h^2+delta_xy^2/h^2.              (6)

6. SYNCHRONIZED VECTOR AND THE POLAR ORIENTATION

After local row erasures, the joint-success vector lies in the perp corner
and corresponds to
                 x_xy=C_c T C_d*=sum_k tau_k g_k(c)g_k(d).
It has norm squared c0. Bob's erasure is RIGHT multiplication by C_d*, not
by C_d. In the opposite algebra R(v)R(w)=R(wv); this fixes the orientation.
Its scalar spectral kernel beta(lambda,mu)=<g_lambda,T g_mu> satisfies
 |beta-lambda|^2<=8h^2 lambda^2+32(lambda-mu)^2/h^2,
by (3) and symmetry. Functional calculus with the same positive spectral
coupling measure gives
                       ||x_xy-c||_2^2<=32 F_xy.                         (7)

Since T_perp=0, every summand of x_xy has LEFT support in p_x=supp(c).
Thus u_x x_xy has exactly the same norm as x_xy. If c0>0, the normalized
retained vector is xi_xy=u_x x_xy/sqrt(c0), and the ideal truncated target
is a'_x=u_x c. If F_xy<1/512, (7) gives
                       ||xi_xy-a'_x||_2<=2 sqrt(32 F_xy).
For larger F the trivial half-TV bound 1 suffices. Hence ANY joint POVM
formed from the original commuting A_s^x,B_t^y differs on this retained
vector from its a'_x table in half-TV by at most 24 sqrt(F_xy).             (8)
This uses pure-vector state comparison, independent of answer count.
If c0=0 no synchronized branch occurs, and the large-F trivial case covers it.

7. ACTUAL LOCAL ANSWER EFFECTS; NO UNITARY POLAR COMPLETION NEEDED

Choose one default answer s0(x),t0(y). Define in Mtilde

supported on those projections. The (1-p_x) term is essential to operator
completeness for a nonunitary polar factor. It has ZERO weight in every
actual synchronized branch because p_x x_xy=x_xy. The synchronized answer
law is exactly the original POVM table of xi_xy. In particular Bob's
original measurement is not conjugated by an invented polar unitary.

This is one resource and one complete law for each original question pair.

8. FIRST SUCCESS, FINITE TIMEOUT, AND FINAL CONSTANTS

The trials are independent copies of the same resource. For fixed x,y,

the shared question law remains nu. On a matching first index the retained
copy has the same xi_xy law regardless of its index, since earlier failures
and untouched later copies factor from it. On every other branch charge
half-TV at most 1, without assuming a particular output state.
