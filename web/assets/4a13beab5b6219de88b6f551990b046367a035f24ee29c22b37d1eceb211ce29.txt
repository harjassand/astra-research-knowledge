N16 | finite-trace biosignature identifiability under hidden reservoirs
Frozen record v1 — 2026-10-09

CLAIM STATUS

Candidate, internally derived, and finite-tested. This is an exact boundary for a stated inverse-model class plus a conditional measurement protocol. It is not an empirical confirmation, a claim that any particular planet is inhabited or abiotic, a novelty/priority claim, or a result externally reviewed. No literature result below validates the general theorem. The executable script checks one constructed overlap and one budget separation, not a planet model.

TARGET

Can time-resolved response to known stellar forcing, isotope measurements, and stoichiometric conservation give an exact discriminator between a biosphere-maintained atmospheric disequilibrium and abiotic photochemical/geophysical cycling when reservoirs, transport, detection noise, and reaction rates are uncertain?

Result: not from gas/isotope traces alone when abiotic reservoirs and rates are insufficiently bounded, but the scope needs care. An exact arbitrary-trace reservoir construction is valid only for a phenomenological inverse class that permits unconstrained time-dependent exchange functions. It is not an autonomous chemical-network theorem. A stronger physically forced counterexample is provided for a fixed-shape stellar modulation u(t): oxygenic photosynthesis in its linear-light regime and abiotic water photolysis at fixed water activity/optical depth can both have O2 source rate ηu(t), with finite reactant inventories over a finite observing interval. With equal η, equal O2 loss and overlapping source-water isotope fractionation (including the no-fractionation idealization), they produce the same exact time-series of O2 and its oxygen isotopologues. This overlap does not extend automatically to non-gray flares or to measured H escape, CO, biomass, and complete reservoir inventories. A decisive test becomes attainable only after independently bounding the abiotic reaction inventory, rate/energy supply, reservoir capacities, isotope fractionation, transport and escape. Then the exact test is robust reachability: the measured uncertainty set must lie outside the abiotic reachable-output set. In a linear finite-volume formulation, infeasibility has a Farkas dual certificate interpretable as a weighted atom/redox/energy budget violation.

The result answers the task with a boundary rather than a new empirical biosignature. The strongest supported consequence is that temporal phase, flare correlation, chemical disequilibrium, or isotope ratios are evidence only relative to a specified abiotic model and priors; none is a logically exact life label while the abiotic source/reservoir class is unrestricted. A physically meaningful sufficient protocol is given below, with its current exoplanet limitation made explicit.

1. MODEL AND EXACT FINITE-TRACE CONSTRUCTION

Let x_i(t) be the atmospheric column of observed molecular/isotopologue species i on a finite interval [0,T]. Let ℓ_i(t) be its total known or independently bounded atmospheric loss (escape, chemistry, deposition, and transport divergence, with sign convention that ℓ_i≥0 is loss), and let b_i(t) be independently measured boundary inflow. The source required by the measured trace is

    r_i(t) = d x_i/dt + ℓ_i(t) - b_i(t).

For finite-volume data, avoid noisy numerical differentiation. On bin k=[t_k,t_{k+1}], the integrated source residual is

    R_{i,k} = x_i(t_{k+1}) - x_i(t_k) + ∫_{t_k}^{t_{k+1}} (ℓ_i(t)-b_i(t)) dt.

When loss and transport are uncertain, R_{i,k} is an interval, computed from the full abundance and flux uncertainty bounds. The interval form is the relevant one for data; the differential expression is only shorthand.

If endpoint retrieval errors are independently bounded by σ_{i,k} and σ_{i,k+1}, and the integrated loss, boundary-inflow and transport-divergence errors are bounded by δ_{ℓ,i,k}, δ_{b,i,k} and δ_{T,i,k}, then a valid absolute half-width for the source-residual interval is

    ε_{R,i,k} = σ_{i,k} + σ_{i,k+1} + δ_{ℓ,i,k} + δ_{b,i,k} + δ_{T,i,k}.

For a shared endpoint bound σ this gives 2σ plus the flux/transport terms. Correlated spectral-retrieval errors require a joint covariance/error set rather than summing independent scalar margins. A budget exclusion needs the lower end of the observed source interval to exceed the upper end of the abiotic reachable-source interval; comparing central values is not sufficient.

For one measured isotopologue i, add an unobserved abiotic reservoir R_i containing that same isotopologue. Take positive r_i to be release into the atmosphere and negative r_i to be uptake into the hidden store. Define cumulative exchange C_i(t)=∫_0^t r_i(s)ds and set

    R_i(t) = R_i(0) - C_i(t),
    R_i(0) ≥ max(0, sup_{0≤t≤T} C_i(t)).

Then R_i(t)≥0 throughout the observation. The exchange fluxes are r_i^+=max(r_i,0) from reservoir to atmosphere and r_i^-=max(-r_i,0) from atmosphere to reservoir. The same construction on each isotope-resolved species exactly reproduces the complete observed trace and conserves its atoms and isotope labels at every exchange. The required initial stock is finite for every finite, integrable trace. This only imitates a finite atmospheric maintenance interval: it can draw down a finite stock, so it does not prove the same abiotic turnover can persist indefinitely. If a source-path energy or redox budget is needed, include the matched reduced/oxidized reservoir counterpart and the consumed forcing work; those capacities are likewise finite when the trace and interval are finite. This construction is permitted only in the broad inverse class with no independent upper bounds on reservoir capacity and exchange kinetics. It is not a verified abiotic mechanism for a given world, and it does not prove that every such trace has a finite autonomous mass-action realization.

That distinction matters: a finite autonomous mass-action reaction network has a polynomial (hence analytic) vector field, so its solution is analytic wherever it exists. Arbitrary absolutely continuous traces need not be analytic. The free r_i(t) construction inserts a controller/schedule encoding the observed future trace; it is therefore not a physical autonomous-CRN proof. The main physical counterexample below avoids that defect by using fixed reaction laws driven by the measured external stellar input, with an explicit finite-reserve condition.

The construction survives any finite observation-noise interval: the abiotic twin can reproduce the central retrieved trace exactly, so it lies inside every nonempty error tube around that trace. If the biotic and abiotic forward models predict the same mean trace and the same noise law, no decision rule on those observations can have guaranteed zero error for both labels. With deterministic tolerance ε, a necessary condition for guaranteed classification is separation of the output sets by more than the allowed error: dist(A_T(u),B_T(u))>2ε in a common norm. Overlap makes exact classification impossible, though Bayesian posterior odds may still differ under explicit prior probabilities.

This is an identifiability statement for a prescribed finite input/output experiment. An abiotic rate function fitted to one forcing path need not predict a new forcing path. Randomized multi-input experiments can reject a restricted abiotic kinetic model, but they do not produce a model-independent life criterion: the abiotic class must still have independently justified state, rate and reservoir bounds. A geological state can carry memory; prescribing an unrestricted hidden-state law simply moves the ambiguity into that state.

2. PHYSICALLY GROUNDED COUNTERMODELS TO STATIC, TEMPORAL AND ISOTOPE SHORTCUTS

2.1 Water-origin oxygen is not a unique biological isotope tag.

Oxygenic photosynthesis has net stoichiometry

    CO2 + H2O + light -> CH2O + O2

per mole of fixed carbon, and tracer work established that the evolved O2 oxygen atoms come from water (Ruben et al. 1941). Abiotic water photolysis followed by H escape has net atom balance

    2 H2O + photons -> O2 + 4 H(escape).

Both routes can therefore give O2 carrying the source-water oxygen isotope composition. In the idealized no-fractionation pairing model, if the source water heavy-oxygen atom fraction is ρ, the O2 isotopologue fractions are ((1-ρ)^2, 2ρ(1-ρ), ρ^2) for 16O-16O, 16O-18O and 18O-18O under either route. This is a constructive overlap, not a claim that actual kinetic isotope effects are identical. Distinct fractionation kernels can help only if source-water composition, fractionation ranges, escape and mixing are independently constrained tightly enough that the resulting isotope-output sets are disjoint.

There is an exact input-driven dynamical overlap without a future-trace controller. Let the incident spectrum have fixed shape and a known scalar amplitude u(t)≥0, so F_λ(t)=u(t)F^0_λ. Over an interval T, assume (i) biological oxygenic photosynthesis is in its unsaturated linear-light regime with fixed active biomass and adequate CO2/water, giving gross O2 rate v_B(t)=η_Bu(t); (ii) the abiotic upper-atmosphere water column has fixed activity/optical depth, maintained by a finite liquid reservoir through phase equilibrium, and photolysis plus escape gives v_A(t)=η_Au(t); the vertical transport supply is at least the photolyzed-water flux 2η_Au(t) throughout T; and (iii) both planets have the same known O2 loss λx. These are explicit model conditions, not a fit to an arbitrary trace. For η_A=η_B=η and the same initial O2, both yield

    x(t) = exp(-λt)x(0) + η∫_0^t exp(-λ(t-s))u(s)ds.

The water reserve need only satisfy W≥2η∫_0^T u(s)ds; the cumulative H escape is 4η∫_0^T u(s)ds. These quantities are finite. Under the no-fractionation pairing model above and equal source-water isotope ratios, the oxygen-isotopologue source vectors also match exactly. The biologic route sends the reducing equivalents into organic carbon (with net CH2O production/burial); the abiotic route sends hydrogen to escape. If only atmospheric O2 and O2 oxygen isotopologues are observed, those co-products are hidden and the input-output records overlap exactly under these stated assumptions. Actual isotope fractionation could separate a specified pair of pathways if its bounded output ranges do not overlap. Measuring/bounding the H escape, organic carbon and full water/redox budgets can separate these two specified routes. The construction applies to gray-scaled forcing and does not prove a match for a flare whose spectral shape changes independently with time.

The water route makes the required abiotic costs auditable: producing ξ mol O2 requires at least 2ξ mol H2O photolyzed, 4ξ mol H atoms removed to space if the O2 is left as net oxidizing product, and at least the corresponding photolysis events. If hydrogen stays in a reservoir instead of escaping, the redox inventory must include it; it cannot be discarded from the budget. Oxygen isotopes alone omit this joint hydrogen/escape constraint. On Mars, ExoMars TGO HDO/H2O profiles combined with photolysis calculations show season-dependent formation of atomic H and D and differential HDO/H2O photolysis affecting product isotopes. Thus a D/H or isotope-phase inference also requires a transport/photolysis/escape model, rather than a static fractionation label.

2.2 A known flare response can be abiotic, and CO can be hidden.

The balanced photochemical route

    2 CO2 + 2 photons -> 2 CO + O2

couples O2 to CO. A reduced atmospheric O2/CO model may miss a dissolved or surface reservoir. Harman et al. (2015) modelled CO2 photolysis and found abiotic O2 accumulation conditional on stellar FUV/NUV and slow dissolved CO/O2 recombination or weak O2 sinks; the paper explicitly identifies ocean boundary chemistry and deposition velocities as controlling assumptions. Therefore CO non-detection is useful only after bounding CO dissolution, hydration, oxidation and surface storage. Their source-reported calculation is not a universal false-positive rate.

For the special observable x=O2 with a shared first-order loss k, a biotic source s_B(t) and an abiotic source s_A(t) yield the same output whenever s_A(t)=s_B(t):

    dx/dt = s_B(t) - kx = s_A(t) - kx.

The fixed-law example above supplies one exact overlap without granting an arbitrary rate schedule. More generally, this identity does not assert that an abiotic planet can realize every arbitrary source function. It shows why the output response itself cannot identify the source mechanism unless the physically allowed s_A class is bounded. In their published 2025 time-dependent photochemical model, Miranda-Rosete, Segura & Schwieterman find transient O2/O3 depletion during one flare, temporary changes that are small relative to CO2/H2O/H2 sensitivity, and a maximum modeled change of 47 ppm in the O3 Hartley-band spectrum for their selected scenario; the overall atmospheric chemistry returns on a modeled ≈33-year scale, while potentially detectable spectral changes last around 20 minutes. This is a source-reported simulation, not an observed flare response, and the authors reserve multiple flares and O2-rich atmospheres for future work. The relevant point is that an abiotic atmosphere has a time-dependent, forcing-linked response that depends on hidden atmospheric state and boundary assumptions; synchronization alone is not a unique life label.

2.3 Methane exposes the reservoir/kinetics split.

For the geochemical reaction CO2+4H2 -> CH4+2H2O, a steady atmospheric CH4 inventory may be limited by source flux even when a static methane detection is large. Conversely, a time-varying methane release need not be newly synthesized during the observing interval. Klein, Grozeva & Seewald (2019) examined 160 olivine-bearing samples and reported methane- and hydrogen-bearing fluid inclusions, with a mechanism for storage over geologic time and later release by dissolution or fracturing. McCollom (2016) used 13C-labelled inorganic carbon in 200–320 °C serpentinization experiments and found inorganic-carbon-to-methane formation extremely limited in those experiments, except for greater production in one H2-rich-vapor case. These primary results constrain different stages: a stored abiotic CH4 pool can be released, but a large ongoing synthesis rate cannot be inferred from generic serpentinization. The appropriate use is to bound each source/reservoir separately, not to treat either paper as proving an abiotic explanation for any planetary methane abundance.

3. EXACT BUDGET CERTIFICATE AND SUFFICIENT PROTOCOL

For a selected finite source catalogue, collect all measured and hidden state quantities in q(t), all nonnegative reaction/transfer extents in ξ_k, and define the exact abiotic reachable-output set under the forcing u and independently bounded parameter set Θ_A:

    R_A^T(u; Θ_A) = {H q(0:T): q̇ = S_A v_A(q,u;θ)+b_boundary(t), θ∈Θ_A, q(0)∈Q_0, q(t)∈Q}.

Here v_A is the stated kinetic law, including its state dependence, photolysis yields and rate uncertainty; Q_0 and Q are the allowed initial/final and intermediate reservoir inventories; b_boundary includes measured external material fluxes and escape. This reachable set, not stoichiometric compatibility by itself, is the exact object. The exact finite-data exclusion test is that the measurement error tube does not intersect R_A^T. If it does intersect, that only says the catalogue has a dynamical fit; it does not establish abiotic origin.

For bookkeeping and a sound, computable outer bound, one can write the integrated mass-balance constraints:

    q_{k+1} = q_k + S_A ξ_k + b_k,
    A S_A = 0,
    A q_k ∈ I_k,
    0 ≤ q_k ≤ Q_max,
    0 ≤ ξ_k ≤ K_max(u_k,q_k) Δt,
    E ξ_k ≤ W_k,
    |H q_k - y_k| ≤ ε_k.

Here S_A is the stated abiotic reaction stoichiometric matrix; A tracks C, H, O and isotope atom counts; I_k is the measured/allowed total-inventory interval after boundary fluxes; Q_max bounds accessible atmosphere/ocean/crust/inclusion reservoirs; K_max contains experimentally or geophysically supported rate bounds; E ξ≤W limits photon, chemical-redox, geothermal and escape work; H maps complete state to the measured columns; ε includes retrieval noise and model error. Unknown transport must be a bounded variable in the same feasibility problem. The integrated constraints are generally only necessary: state-dependent rate laws, mixing and phase constraints can make an extent vector unrealizable. Therefore infeasibility of a sound outer relaxation is a valid abiotic rejection certificate, but feasibility of that relaxation is not a kinetic fit. Exact linear kinetics with known rates can instead be propagated by their exact state-transition matrices; uncertain/nonlinear cases need a sound reachability enclosure or global constrained solve. A local optimizer's failure is not a certificate.

Let F_A(y,u) be the set of exact dynamical trajectories satisfying the full kinetic laws and independently justified bounds while fitting the measured error intervals. If F_A is empty, the abiotic-only model catalogue is excluded at the stated confidence/interval level. Calling that a biological attribution additionally requires the source catalogue and all boundary fluxes to be complete enough that an omitted abiotic pathway is not an alternative. If F_A is nonempty, this test does not establish abiotic origin; both biotic and abiotic models may fit.

For an exact linear-reachability formulation or a stated polyhedral outer relaxation, infeasibility is certifiable. Write that linear system as Gz≤g and Mz=d after including measurement intervals and positivity bounds. If it is infeasible, Farkas' lemma supplies λ≥0 and a free vector μ with

    G^T λ + M^T μ = 0,
    g^T λ + d^T μ < 0.

Any feasible z would force 0=(G^Tλ+M^Tμ)^Tz≤g^Tλ+d^Tμ, contradiction. This dual vector is a weighted combination of observations and independent budgets that the abiotic model cannot satisfy. It is a proof certificate only for the chosen reaction catalogue, kinetic enclosure, bounds, discretization and error set. For an outer relaxation, nonempty means unresolved rather than feasible under exact kinetics.

A concrete two-channel example shows what the certificate must track. Let ξ_w be O2 production by water photolysis and escape, and ξ_c by CO2 photolysis, over a finite interval. Ignoring unmeasured sinks only for this bookkeeping identity,

    ΔO2 = ξ_w + ξ_c,
    H_escape(atoms) = 4 ξ_w,
    ΔCO_total = 2 ξ_c.

If total CO means atmosphere plus dissolved/solid CO-equivalent pools, and all boundary fluxes and O2 loss have been corrected, then

    ΔO2 = H_escape/4 + ΔCO_total/2.

This equality is necessary for these two abiotic routes. A violation beyond the propagated observational and budget intervals rejects this two-route explanation; it does not establish life unless other abiotic reactions are likewise bounded. If ΔCO_total is only the atmospheric column, unknown ocean/rock storage invalidates the test.

Given independent caps W on accessible water molecules, H on net escaping H atoms, C on accessible CO2 molecules, S on CO that can be sequestered without co-consuming the measured O2, and P on photolysis events, the two-channel gross abiotic O2 source is bounded by

    ξ_w ≤ min(W/2, H/4),
    ξ_c ≤ min(C/2, S/2),
    ξ_w + ξ_c ≤ P/2,
    O2_abiotic ≤ min(P/2, min(W/2,H/4) + min(C/2,S/2)).

These are atom/photolysis-event constraints. Real applications also require explicit quantum yields, wavelength-dependent absorption, recombination, all O2 sinks, and their uncertainties; using P as absorbed dissociation events must not be confused with raw stellar photons. A measured net O2 source residual that exceeds this upper bound by more than its combined retrieval and model error excludes these routes. Other abiotic pathways require additional columns in the feasible set.

Sufficient physical measurement protocol (for a target where the reservoirs are instrumentally accessible):

1. Obtain synchronized time series of incident stellar spectral photon fluence and planetary O2, O3, CO2, CO, H2O, H2, CH4 and relevant isotopologues; record the forcing cadence and the radiative-transfer retrieval covariance rather than treating retrieved abundances as exact.
2. Independently bound escape, vertical/horizontal transport, ocean dissolution, seasonal phase exchange, surface redox sinks, and accessible inclusion/clathrate/mineral inventories. For H2O photolysis, measure or bound H/D escape and water isotope profiles; for CO2 photolysis, inventory CO-equivalent products in accessible phases.
3. Use laboratory kinetics under recovered temperature, pressure, pH, mineralogy and UV spectrum to set rate bounds, with blank/background-carbon controls. Preserve alternate catalysts and geochemical sources as nuisance pathways rather than assuming one selected mechanism.
4. Fit the complete finite-volume atom/redox/energy balance over the full time series and propagate all retrieval, transport, rate, and reservoir uncertainty. Report a dual budget certificate if the abiotic feasible set is empty. Report only conditional evidence if the result depends on an unmeasured reservoir or unvalidated extrapolation.

This is physically meaningful for an instrumented solar-system site or carefully constrained Earth system. For a remote exoplanet, complete global ocean/crust/mantle inventories and gas flux closure are not presently obtained from spectroscopy; therefore the exact sufficient test is not currently supplied by time-resolved remote sensing alone. A probabilistic ranking may be valuable, but it is not this certificate.

4. COUNTEREXAMPLES, ALTERNATIVES, AND LIMITS

• Static O2/O3/CH4 abundance: an inventory can be a transient, stored source, or steady source/sink balance. Abundance is not source identity.
• A flare-correlated time signal: photochemistry and escape respond directly to forcing; geochemical reservoirs can respond through temperature, solubility, permeability, and transport. A flare response rejects only models whose transfer functions are independently bounded and inconsistent.
• O2 oxygen isotopes matching water: this is expected for oxygenic photosynthesis and is also allowed by abiotic water photolysis. D/H may add an escape constraint, but phase-dependent condensation and isotope-selective photolysis must be modeled.
• O2 plus CO: CO is a valuable companion discriminator only when its full accessible inventory/sinks are bounded. The published O2 false-positive calculations explicitly depend on ocean chemistry and assumed deposition.
• O2 plus CH4: this is a stronger disequilibrium pattern than one gas, but its abiotic exclusion requires upper bounds on water-loss oxidation and a lower/upper bound on methane production and stored-release rates. Existing serpentinization sources show both reservoir plausibility and major rate uncertainty; they do not close a planet-specific flux budget.
• Unknown transport and detection noise: time derivatives amplify retrieval noise, and aliasing can hide forcing phase. Use integrated intervals and covariance sets. Unknown transport enters as an explicit state/flux variable. If its bound is wide enough to absorb the residual, the feasible set remains nonempty.
• Incomplete reaction catalogue: infeasibility means the stated model class failed, not that biology is logically necessary. This is the largest scientific gap between the formal certificate and a life claim.
• Forcing design: controlled randomized multi-band stimulation plus repeated spectra could improve system identification if observations cover the input timescales and have adequate SNR. It still cannot guarantee separation when abiotic response laws and reservoirs are unconstrained.

5. EXECUTABLE EVIDENCE

Run from the campaign root:

    python3 work/natural/n16_biosignature_dynamics/mimic_and_budget.py

This standard-library script integrates the same measured O2 state equation for two fixed source efficiencies driven by a gray-scaled forcing, computes the finite water-photolysis inventory and H escape needed for the abiotic construction, verifies identical water-derived O2 isotopologue fractions in the no-fractionation pairing model, and evaluates a bounded two-channel abiotic upper bound. The exact overlap is input-driven (the star is the measured external input), not an autonomous reservoir releasing an arbitrary programmed future trace. Output:

    finite-trace source integral: 3.000000
    constructed water-path requirements: H2O=6.000000, H_escape_atoms=12.000000, photolysis_events>=6.000000
    max O2 trajectory difference: 0
    O2 isotopologue fractions (16-16, 16-18, 18-18): 0.996004000, 0.003992000, 0.000004000
    bounded two-channel abiotic O2 upper bound: 2.000000
    measurement/model uncertainty: 0.100000
    abiotic catalog excluded: True

Numbers are normalized illustrative units and do not correspond to an observed planet. The final boolean says only that the particular two-route abiotic catalogue, with those stipulated caps, misses the constructed source by >0.1. The code is a derivation check, not a test suite, photochemical solver, or empirical validation.

7. STRONGEST CONSEQUENCE AND OPEN GATES

Strongest consequence supported: (a) the free-reservoir exact-trace argument rules out a universal classifier only for a broad phenomenological inverse class with unconstrained time-dependent exchange; it is not an autonomous-CRN result; (b) a chemically balanced, fixed-law, finite-resource, input-driven water-photolysis countermodel exactly overlaps a biological O2 time series and water-source O2 isotope record under fixed-shape stellar modulation; and (c) the formal source residual and budget-feasibility test specifies what independent constraints would restore attainability. These do not establish indistinguishability for all autonomous abiotic models or all non-gray stellar forcing.

Remaining inference for a planetary life conclusion: (i) establish a target-specific, sufficiently complete abiotic pathway catalogue; (ii) independently bound accessible water, carbon, methane, redox and adsorbed/inclusion reservoirs; (iii) bound photochemical yields, geochemical synthesis and release rates, transport, escape, and isotope fractionation over the observed regime; (iv) supply complete measurement covariance and temporal resolution; and (v) show the robust abiotic feasible set is empty while a biotic model remains feasible. None of these target-specific conditions is established here. The finite campaign result therefore remains an internally derived boundary and protocol, not a positive biosignature detection or a completed empirical capability.
