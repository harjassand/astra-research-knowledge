EXECUTIVE AUDIT

The three Sol constructions survive this interface audit inside their stated
classes. They are distinct contributions, not one general recurrence result:

* c10_s01 gives a global, all-lattice physical-time Foster function and
  positive recurrence for its fixed-rate, two-layer autonomous-root catalytic
  family. The reciprocal g_j(S) correction handles zero-catalyst faces.
* c10_s02 gives an exact recurrent detailed-balanced example defeating the
  specific N76 factorial potential and every globally coercive concave
  potential. It is a certificate-architecture obstruction, not a transient
  or non-recurrent network.
* c10_s03 INITIAL gives a rational rate-box compiler for an admitted weakly
  reversible class with unary drain paths, constructive production words,
  and a source-wise affine/quadratic certificate. Its revision then adds a
  stronger reciprocal-catalytic hinge certificate with uniform predictable-
  control drift and recovery. Both are strict subclasses of general
  bimolecular endotactic networks.

No all-dimensional positive-recurrence theorem, all-network boundary recovery
compiler, or counterexample to recurrence of standard second-order endotactic
systems follows. The known open interface is still constant-rate/classwise
recurrence (or a more precisely selected robust-control analogue) outside
these admissions. Finite exact tests are implementation checks, not proof of
the universal inequalities or priority.

1. c10_s01 — TWO-LAYER CATALYTIC BIRTH/DEATH

Admission: finite disjoint root/child sets; every root has fixed positive
immigration and linear death; every child has at least one root parent; each
root-parent/child pair has both fixed positive catalytic birth and reverse
death rates; children do not act as catalysts; no other reactions. Stochastic
propensities are ordinary falling-factorial mass action (the displayed
channels have order at most two). The claimed state space is the entire
integer nonnegative lattice, not an arbitrary deterministic class. With
fixed rates, root immigration and direct degradation make the lattice
irreducible.

Audit of the load-bearing drift: for child j, S_j is the sum of its parent
counts and g_j(S)=1+c_j/(S+1). Root births and deaths contribute

  -c_j B_j/((S+1)(S+2))
  + 1_{S>=1} c_j sum_i delta_i r_i/(S(S+1)).

The death term is omitted at S=0; there the child bracket equals
-c_j B_j/2. For S>=1, the positive correction is at most
c_j D_j/(S+1), while catalytic child removal is at least beta_j S.
The chosen c_j=min(1,beta_j/D_j) yields a globally negative child
coefficient. The root weights then cancel the bounded child birth terms and
give LV<=C-sum_i r_i-sum_j gamma_j y_j<=C-lambda V. I found no missing
zero-face case or rate sign in these displayed inequalities. This is an
all-state proof in the claimed class; c10_s01's 1,548-state exact run is
additional implementation evidence only.

Interface limits: the rates are fixed constants; no time-varying control,
volume-uniform statement, arbitrary weakly reversible CRN synthesis, or
common deterministic-class theorem is claimed. Positive recurrence and the
stationary conclusions are for this irreducible count chain. The exponential
return-time argument does not establish exponential moments of stationary
counts or a mixing-time bound. The paper's own priority discussion flags
environment-modulated immigration/death prior art; the exact rate-family
construction is not priority-cleared here.

2. c10_s02 — N76 AND CONCAVITY COUNTEREXAMPLE

The network A+C <-> A+B <-> B and C <-> 0, all unit rates, has standard
factorial propensities. The reported class Gamma={a+b>=1} is irreducible and
has the conditioned product-Poisson invariant law; it is positive recurrent.
The other closed component Gamma_0={a=b=0} is the C immigration/death chain.
The deterministic positive ODE class converges to (1,1,1), whereas the
stationary Gamma law has P(A=0)=1/(e+1) at volume 1 and 1/(e^V+1) under the
stated volume scaling. Thus infinite-path strict positivity fails, while
large-volume stationary boundary mass can still be small. This does not
contradict a finite-horizon, buffered N35 theorem.

At x=(a,0,1), a>=2, the exact N76 values are F(x)=log((a-1)!). Enabled
jumps have rates/exp(F)-ratios (a,1), (1,a), (1,1) for A+C->A+B,
C->0, and 0->C respectively. Therefore LF(x)=log a>0, and every strictly
increasing scalar h has L[h(F)](x)>0 because only C->0 changes F upward.
At (0,n,0), both enabled jumps preserve F. The separately proved concave
no-go concerns globally coercive concave W on the indicated communicating
component; it does not rule out non-concave, path, skeleton, or phase-aware
potentials. These claims are duplicated/attributed to c10_s02; our
revisions/interface_checks.py independently checks exact finite jump ratios.

The proof obligations that remain are exactly the stated quantifiers: this
example has a finite invariant measure, so it is not a recurrence
counterexample; the concavity theorem applies only to the specified potential
architecture; and a source-reported finite test does not certify every
integer state. The written infinite-ray arguments, rather than the fixtures,
carry those universal claims.

3. c10_s03 INITIAL — GENERAL LP/PRODUCTION-WORD CERTIFICATE

Admission is explicit: rational positive rate boxes, all source and product
complexes have molecularity <=2, the finite complex graph is weakly
reversible, every species has an acquired unary drain path to zero, and an
acyclic recipe grammar builds every species from zero. A partition of species
plus robust source-wise rational inequalities is additionally required. The
certificate is not a recognizer for all endotactic networks. Its rejected
all-unit detailed-balanced cross fixture is a rejection by this certificate
class, not evidence of instability.

I checked the constraint/generator alignment: each mixed quadratic source
gets a nonpositive worst-case weight increment; a diagonal 2X_i source is
either nonpositive or at most -1 times x_i(x_i-1); a first-order source
without a diagonal sink gets coefficient <=-1; and the completed-square
constant covers an allowed positive unary term paired with a negative
diagonal source. Source-rate-box extrema are attained at endpoints, so exact
vertex checks are appropriate for independent intervals. The intended
recovery attempt first drains a core state to zero, then runs the production
word. Therefore max(core count cap, twice production-word length) bounds the
path population; reading the cover word as starting at the core state would
misread the compiler. With that ordering, the reported rate cap has the
needed quadratic-molecularity envelope. No contradiction was found in this
scoped proof, but its bit bound, path proof, and standard stopped-generator
arguments still need independent formal review.

Cost/probability interface: the LP/basis search may be singly exponential
(the worker reports a conservative 2^O(L^3) bound), while compact rational
rates are input. The selected path probability alpha^H may be astronomically
small; the worker retains it and charges expanded output. It is uniform over
predictable controls only when every channel retains its positive lower
bound. No stationary probability is defined for a general nonstationary
policy; fixed homogeneous choices can use the recurrence conclusion.

4. c10_s03 REVISION — RECIPROCAL CATALYTIC HINGES

Stronger admission: all species have primitive 0<->X_i flows; catalytic
births X_j->X_i+X_j are admitted only with reciprocal single-count removal
X_i+X_j->X_j (self case 2X_i->X_i); optional unimolecular conversions;
weakly reversible complex graph; fixed positive rational rate boxes with
predictable selections. There is no inference that endotacticity supplies
these reciprocal edges or the primitive flows.

The index convention checks out: beta[(i,j)] means produce i using catalyst
j; the cancelling removal is gamma[(j,i)], and h_i>=1+ceil(beta_ij^+/gamma_ji^-)
makes x_i gamma_ji^- x_j dominate beta_ij^+ x_j whenever x_i>=h_i and
x_j>h_j. For self-catalysis, x_i>h_i implies x_i-1>=h_i, so the actual
falling factorial x_i(x_i-1) supplies the cancellation. Below-threshold
catalysts have x_j<=h_j and contribute only the stated constant. Degradation,
immigration, and conversion cases complete the pointwise bound
L_t V<=C-delta Z. The direct recovery path is core->zero by unary deaths,
then zero->(q,...,q) by immigration. Its count cap is max(core_n,dq), a
valid bound because draining cannot increase N and the second segment starts
at zero. Its Q uses (N+1)^2, which dominates every order-0/1/2 falling
factorial. Positive lower bounds are essential to the claimed selected-path
probability; upper bounds alone support the separate non-explosion estimate.

The exponential ratio derivation also aligns: if P and D are the total
positive/negative unit-jump intensities, then L(a^Z)/a^Z
=eta/a[(P-D)+eta P]. The reported bounds P<=C+A Z and P-D<=C-delta Z
give the stated rational exponential Foster inequality. The class therefore
has a uniform controlled drift/recovery result under the stated boxes and
pathwise controls, not a stationary law common to all controllers.

The final report itself marks static positive recurrence and exponential
ergodicity as already covered by Anderson-Kim prior art. Its genuine scope
contribution is the explicit acquired rational hinge and uniform-control
recovery/tail construction, if independently verified and if this exact
algorithmic formulation is novel. No source or corpus review here clears
priority. The recorded 1,099 finite diagnostics support implementation but
cannot replace the pointwise all-state proof, and they do not exercise every
dimension, rate box, controller, or threshold cell.

5. CROSS-CUTTING QUANTIFIER / PRIOR-ART LEDGER

* Xu (arXiv:2409.05340v2), Theorem 4.6: second-order 1-endotactic standard
  stochastic mass action is regular/non-explosive. Its total-count argument
  is not a recurrence or return theorem. The fixed-rate result does not
  imply uniformity for arbitrary controlled rates without checking rate
  bounds; c10_l06's upper-bounded predictable-control proof supplies only
  non-explosion.
* Wiuf-Xu (arXiv:2012.07954v2), Theorem 4.11/Corollary 4.12: one-dimensional
  fixed-rate classwise result under explicit dimension/catalyst/subnetwork
  hypotheses. It cannot be generalized to all dimensions by dropping those
  hypotheses.
* Anderson-Craciun-Kurtz (arXiv:0803.3042): product-Poisson stationary laws
  under complex balance, restricted to actual closed irreducible classes;
  a formal stationary solution alone is not the general recurrence gate.
* Anderson-Kim (arXiv:1710.11263; arXiv:2209.06988), as identified by
  c10_s03: broad static recurrence/exponential-ergodicity prior art for its
  admitted class. This does not cover predictable nonstationary rate
  policies or supply the same acquired uniform recovery constants.
* Anderson-Cappelletti-Fan-Kim (arXiv:2309.06970 / SIADS 2025), as cited by
  c10_s02: path/mixing work already treats a related catalyst/boundary
  difficulty. A general boundary obstruction or the specific concave
  no-go must be compared carefully; no novelty conclusion is made here.

6. OWN CONTINUATION / DECISIVE OPEN GATE

The exact boundary experiment reinforces the core quantifier issue: a real
positive stoichiometric class, its integer span coset, and an actual directed
communicating class are different objects. At total count 2 in
2A->A+B, 2B->A+B, the coset from (2,0) contains three states, but the
enabled reachability set contains only (2,0) and (1,1); the latter is a
closed singleton. At total 1, both states are boundary absorbers. Meanwhile
the deterministic positive class obeys dot(A)=c^2-2cA and converges to c/2.
This checks an exact failed transfer but supplies no transient or explosive
count class; both singleton/finite classes are recurrent.

The remaining useful attack is therefore a properly typed theorem statement:
distinguish (a) fixed-rate positive recurrence on each closed irreducible
integer class, (b) existence of an invariant probability on each class,
(c) a uniform recurrence/recovery estimate over a prescribed rate-box
controller family, and (d) deterministic positive-class attraction or
finite-horizon buffered safety. The Sol certificates establish particular
items inside strict syntax families; the common general gate is to synthesize
a finite, correctly enabled boundary-return mechanism for arbitrary
multidimensional bimolecular endotactic inputs, or exhibit a valid
standard-endotactic counterexample. The cited order-10 explosive example and
Xu's (1,1)-endotactic transient example do not supply that counterexample.
