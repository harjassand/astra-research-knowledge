1. EXACT INTERFACE
Let H be a separable Hilbert space and E a nonempty family of normal density
operators in T(H). Define its Holevo capacity by finite priors and relative
entropy, C=sup sum_i p_i D(rho_i || sum_j p_j rho_j)<infinity, with natural
logs. This definition never subtracts two infinite von Neumann entropies.
Define b and e by the same worst-state half-trace errors as checkpoint03,
with infima over normal full-domain CPTP B:T(H)->T(H tensor H) and normal
entanglement-breaking Lambda:T(H)->T(H). EB means that every ancilla
extension has separable output; it is not a bound in diamond norm.

Write g_C(0)=0, g_C(1)=1 and
 g_C(t)=min(1,400 sqrt((C+1)/ln(1/t))) for 0<t<1.
The candidate conclusion is e<=g_C(b). For every eta>0 a normal EB channel
with FINITELY MANY measurement outcomes achieves error <=g_C(b)+eta.
At b=0 there is also an exact normal countable-outcome dephasing/replacement
channel fixing every state of E. Neither assertion bounds the required
Hilbert cutoff or classical alphabet solely by C and eta.

2. ESTABLISHED CAPACITY CENTER AND TIGHTNESS, RECONSTRUCTED
The normal capacity center and relative compactness of finite-capacity sets
are established prior art: Shirokov, Entropy characteristics of subsets of
states I, Izvestiya Mathematics70(6),2006, Theorem1 and Corollary5.
Primary PDF: https://homepage.mi-ras.ru/~msh/download/EC-1-e.pdf
Below is a short reconstruction using the mixture identity, Pinsker and
lower semicontinuity. These facts are not claimed as new discoveries.

Choose finite ensembles mu_n in E with chi_n approaching C and barycenters
sigma_n. Every chi_n is finite since it is at most the Shannon entropy of
the finite prior. The finite-mixture identity (Donald's identity) gives
 chi((mu_n+mu_m)/2)=(chi_n+chi_m)/2
             +[D(sigma_n||s)+D(sigma_m||s)]/2,
where s=(sigma_n+sigma_m)/2. The left side is <=C. Pinsker in nats gives
 ||sigma_n-sigma_m||_1^2 <= 8[C-(chi_n+chi_m)/2].
Thus sigma_n converges in trace norm to a normal state sigma. In particular
sigma belongs to the closed convex hull of E; no maximizing ensemble or
finite entropy of sigma is assumed.

For fixed rho in E and t in (0,1), mix mu_n with mass t on rho. Its average
is sigma_(n,t)=(1-t)sigma_n+t rho, and the same identity gives
 t D(rho||sigma_(n,t)) <= C-(1-t)chi_n.
Lower semicontinuity, first n->infinity at fixed t and then t->0, yields
 D(rho||sigma)<=C. Hence every promised rho is supported on supp sigma.
This is a common normal reference, not a state chosen after learning rho.

Choose increasing finite-rank spectral projections P_n of sigma whose
union contains supp sigma, and let Q_n=I-P_n. Put q_n=Tr(Q_n sigma)->0.
For p=Tr(Q_n rho), binary-measurement data processing implies
 C>=D((p,1-p)||(q_n,1-q_n))>=p ln(1/q_n)-ln2.
The last inequality uses binary entropy <=ln2 and -ln(1-q_n)>=0.
Thus p<=a_n=min(1,(C+ln2)/ln(1/q_n)) uniformly in rho, with a_n->0.
If q_n=0, support inclusion gives p=0 and a_n=0 directly.

Choose any unit vector e0 in P_n H (fix it once in the first nonzero
projection). Define the finite-dimensional normal CPTP compression
 R_n(X)=P_n X P_n+Tr(Q_n X)|e0><e0|,
with finite codomain T(P_n H), and isometric inclusion J_n back into T(H).
The gentle compression estimate ||rho-P_n rho P_n||_1<=2 sqrt(p) gives
 T(J_n R_n rho,rho)<=sqrt(a_n)+a_n/2=:delta_n ->0 uniformly on E.
This loose estimate suffices. Compression has no postselection and keeps
all probability mass. The bound depends on the ACTUAL reference tail q_n;
finite capacity does not provide a universal rank bound.

3. TRANSPORT OF THE EXACT FINITE-DIMENSIONAL INTERFACE
Let E_n=R_n(E). Relative-entropy data processing gives C(E_n)<=C, including
infinite E and singular finite states. For any actual broadcaster B with
marginal error beta on E, define the legal finite broadcaster
 B_n=(R_n tensor R_n) B J_n.
For every rho in E and each marginal j,
 T((B_n)_j R_n rho,R_n rho)
 <=T(B_j J_n R_n rho,B_j rho)+T(B_j rho,rho)
 <=delta_n+beta.
This uses one B_n for the whole compressed family, with the same input
R_n rho on both sides. Marginal correlations remain unrestricted.
Taking infima yields b(E_n)<=min(1,b(E)+delta_n).

Apply the supplied checkpoint03 theorem in finite dimension, using C as an
upper bound and the monotonicity of g_C. For any epsilon>0 choose a finite
EB channel Lambda_n on P_n H with worst error at most
 g_C(min(1,b+delta_n))+epsilon.
Every finite-dimensional EB channel has a finite measure/prepare
representation. The lift L_n=J_n Lambda_n R_n is a normal full-domain EB
channel on H. Its finite POVM effects are R_n^*(M_y), and its prepared
states are J_n(tau_y). These effects sum to I_H, including the complement
of P_n; no trace escapes. Its error on E is at most
 delta_n+g_C(min(1,b+delta_n))+epsilon.
Continuity of g_C on [0,1] and delta_n->0 prove e<=g_C(b).
They also prove the finite-outcome statement with any prescribed positive
slack. No infinite-dimensional compactness of channels or convergent EB
channel sequence is used or claimed. In particular e is an error infimum;
attainment for b>0 is not established here.

4. ZERO ERROR: AN EXACT NORMAL MAP WITHOUT CHANNEL COMPACTNESS
The b=0 infimum statement above alone does not prove attainment. To close
that separate point, use the audited dimension-free two-state affinity
bridge, another checkpoint03 premise:
if a finite pair admits common marginal error beta, then
 Delta(rho,nu):=||sqrt(rho)sqrt(nu)||_1-Tr(sqrt(rho)sqrt(nu))
 <=2 sqrt(2 eta_L),  eta_L=L beta+sqrt(ln2/(2*2^L)), for every integer L>=1.
Choosing L->infinity sufficiently slowly as beta->0 makes Delta->0.

For each pair rho,nu in E, their embedded compressed states converge in
trace norm to rho,nu. Their broadcasting infimum is <=delta_n when b=0.
Apply the bridge using witnesses approaching that infimum and then choose
L(n) with L(n)delta_n->0 and L(n)->infinity. If delta_n=0 on a subsequence,
take beta witnesses tending to0 and then L->infinity. Thus the compressed
pair's Delta tends to0. Root fidelity and affinity are continuous under
trace-norm convergence, by Powers--Stormer and Schatten Holder, so
 ||sqrt(rho)sqrt(nu)||_1=Tr(sqrt(rho)sqrt(nu)).

For a trace-class operator A, equality ReTr(A)=||A||_1 implies A>=0.
Indeed write A=U|A|. The contraction U has I-ReU>=0, and equality says
Tr[|A|^(1/2)(I-ReU)|A|^(1/2)]=0. Positivity gives (I-ReU)=0 on
supp|A|, hence U=I there (use ||Ux||<=||x|| and Re<x,Ux>=||x||²).
Therefore A=|A|. Applied to A=sqrt(rho)sqrt(nu), this gives
sqrt(rho)sqrt(nu)=sqrt(nu)sqrt(rho), and consequently [rho,nu]=0.

All states of E commute. Their common capacity center sigma is a
trace-norm limit of convex combinations, so commutes with them and has
support containing them. Each positive-eigenvalue eigenspace of the compact
operator sigma is finite-dimensional and invariant for every rho. Inside
each such eigenspace simultaneous diagonalization of the commuting
Hermitian family supplies an orthonormal basis. Combining these countably
many bases yields a common basis {e_k} for supp sigma. Let P=supp sigma
and choose e_1 therein. The map
 Lambda(X)=sum_k <e_k,Xe_k>|e_k><e_k|+Tr[(I-P)X]|e_1><e_1|
is normal CPTP, has a countable measure/prepare representation, and fixes E.
The series converges in trace norm on positive trace-class inputs; linear
extension gives a bounded trace-class channel. Its ancilla extension is a
trace-norm limit of finite sums of positive product operators, hence has
separable output. Thus it is EB in the full ancillary definition.

This also recovers the exact no-broadcasting endpoint. Exact no-broadcasting
and simultaneous diagonalization are established phenomena, not new claims
of historical priority. The new-to-run transfer is the SAME quantitative
modulus for infinite-dimensional normal experiments, with a clean infimum
and normal-channel boundary.

5. COSTS, EARLY FALSIFIERS, AND SIGNIFICANCE
The argument assumes the mathematical family and channel promises, not
an oracle that supplies a computable capacity center. Finding near-optimal
ensembles, sigma's spectral tails, a truncation rank, and finite channel
descriptions is not free. No algorithm for arbitrary uncomputable input,
no rank bound depending only on C, no small classical memory, and no
energy-independent physical compiler follows.

Early failure tests: using entropy differences infinity-infinity, compressing
outputs without input transport, retaining an unnormalized cutoff, taking
a weak channel limit without normality, or equating e=0 with attainment are
all invalid shortcuts. The construction above avoids each by an explicit
relative-entropy definition, full CPTP maps, uniform trace estimates and a
separate commuting-pair proof at zero error.

This extends the scope to continuous-variable normal-state experiments
without an extra energy assumption. It is a consequence of the finite
modulus and established capacity compactness, not an independently justified
10/10 discovery. The finite modulus itself remains internally validated,
not externally or formally certified.
