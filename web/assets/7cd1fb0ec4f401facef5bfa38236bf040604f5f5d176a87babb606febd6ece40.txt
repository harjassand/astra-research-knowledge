GIBBS ROBUSTNESS UNDER ARBITRARY POSITIVE PENALTIES ON A RARE SUBSPACE
8 October 2026. Root abstract derivation; internally proved candidate.
Standard entropy/variational ingredients are prior. No priority claim.

Let K,M commute, 0<=K<=kappa M, M>=0, H0=K-mu M, beta>0, and
rho0=exp(-beta H0)/Z0. Assume Z0 finite, E0K,E0M finite and the entropies
below well defined. This includes finite fermion Fock spaces and finite-site
boson Fock spaces with a stable free grand-canonical Hamiltonian.

Let P be a projection commuting with M, Q=I-P, delta=Tr rho0 Q<1,
p=1-delta, qM=Tr rho0 MQ. Let W>=0 annihilate ran P and let H0+W be a
well-defined self-adjoint form sum whose Gibbs state rhoW has finite partition.
For unbounded operators these domain/form-sum assumptions are real hypotheses;
WP=0 includes the necessary allowed-space domain condition. In the bosonic
collision application ran P is finite dimensional. Minmax gives ZW<=Z0.

For any s>0 with Z_M(s)=Tr exp(-sM)<infinity, define
 G=[h2(delta)+s qM+delta log Z_M(s)
    +beta delta E0K+beta kappa qM
    +beta |mu|(delta E0M+qM)]/(1-delta).
Then
 ||rhoW-rho0||_1 <= min(2,2 sqrt(delta)+sqrt(2G)).               (1)
Every quantity on the right is measured in the FREE reference; no W magnitude
or interacting partition/eigenstate oracle appears. A supplied upper bound on
each term is enough. Positivity and the kernel condition cannot be discarded.

PROOF
Set sigma=P rho0 P/p and omega=Q rho0 Q/delta when delta>0. Projection gives
||sigma-rho0||_1<=2sqrt(delta). Since K commutes with rho0,
Tr rho0 PKP<=E0K+kappa qM: expand PKP, drop the nonnegative cross traces
Tr QK rho0, and use QKQ<=kappa MQ. Normalization therefore gives
Tr sigma K-E0K<=[delta E0K+kappa qM]/p.
Also Tr sigma M-E0M=[delta E0M-qM]/p.

Binary pinching gives
 S(rho0)-S(sigma)<=[h2(delta)+delta S(omega)-delta S(rho0)]/p.
The Gibbs maximum-entropy bound gives
 S(omega)<=s Tr omega M+log Z_M(s).
Drop the nonpositive -delta S(rho0) term. Thus the entropy cost is at most
[h2(delta)+s qM+delta log Z_M(s)]/p. This replaces an exponentially large
Hilbert dimension by a number-moment/reference-partition cost, including in
infinite bosonic Fock space. Truncated spectral arguments or the relative-
entropy variational principle justify it whenever Eomega M<infinity.

Because Wsigma=0,
 D(sigma||rhoW)=-S(sigma)+beta Tr sigma H0+log ZW
 <=S(rho0)-S(sigma)+beta Tr (sigma-rho0)H0<=G.
Pinsker and the projection triangle prove(1). It is not operator monotonicity
of exp; only eigenvalue/variational partition monotonicity is used.

Fermions on S finite modes: Z_M(s)=(1+e^-s)^S. Take s=log(S+1), then
log Z_M(s)<=1 and entropy numerator<=h2(delta)+qM log(S+1)+delta.
Bosons on S finite modes: Z_M(s)=(1-e^-s)^(-S). The SAME choice gives
log Z_M(s)=S log(1+1/S)<=1. Thus the same entropy upper holds for both.

FIXED CLASSICAL REVERSE CHANNEL
If beta,K are public and only mu,W vary, let sigma_m^0 be the canonical
normalized restriction of exp(-beta K) to eigenspace M=m. Whenever these
canonical states are defined, Phi(X)=sum_m Tr(P_m X)sigma_m^0 is one fixed
measure-prepare CPTP map and Phi(rho0(mu))=rho0(mu). Contractivity proves
||Phi(rhoW)-rhoW||_1<=2||rhoW-rho0||_1. With count cutoff m_* and a fixed
replacement state on the tail, the upper becomes 2||rhoW-rho0||_1+2t_*,
where t_*=Pr_rho0(M>m_*). No interacting tail estimate is required. This is
approximate experiment sufficiency; exact sufficiency of interacting rhoW
is not inferred. Parameter changes in beta or K require a different argument.
Decoder preparation/precision/runtime and n-copy telescoping costs are real.

DIMENSIONAL TARGETS SENT FOR INDEPENDENT DERIVATION
On d-dimensional open boxes of side L-1, beta=tau L² and fixed-range
collision exclusions, product-sine free spinless fermions have delta,qM
O(L^(-d-2)) by determinant gradient cancellation. K<=8Jd M implies G=O(L^-d),
and hence wholetrace O(L^-d/2). In bosons the analogous permanent bounds give
delta,qM=O(L^-d), hence G=O(L^(2-d)) and trace O(L^(-(d-2)/2)) for d>=3.
These applications are not proved merely by this abstract lemma: sine,
collision/Wick, stability and domain constants are being checked separately.
Hard-core spins in dimension>1 are NOT free fermions. The d=2 boson hard
projection does not yield a vanishing estimate; a smoother capacity argument
would be a distinct next gate. No conjectured exponent is claimed optimal.

STRONGER PARTITION COMPARISON AND COLLECTIVE CLASSICAL ARCHIVE
Added after independent continuum lead reconstruction, 8 October 2026.

The same G actually bounds D(rhoW||rho0), not only D(sigma||rhoW).
Let H_P be the compression/form restriction of H0 to ran P and
Z_P=Tr_P exp(-beta H_P). Since W vanishes on P, variational eigenvalue
interlacing gives Z_W>=Z_P. The Gibbs variational principle applied to the
legal trial sigma on ran P gives
 log(Z0/Z_P)<=D(sigma||rho0)<=G.
Consequently, whenever the Gibbs energy identity is justified,
 D(rhoW||rho0)=-beta Tr(rhoW W)+log(Z0/Z_W)<=G.            (2)
In finite dimension this is immediate. In finite-mode boson applications
H0 is positive, H0+W has finite half-temperature partition by minmax, hence
its thermal energy is finite and the two positive form expectations of H0,W
are finite. This justifies the identity; alternatively use finite spectral
truncations with the appropriate form convergence. An arbitrary unbounded
form without these domain/energy hypotheses is not silently admitted.
The hard-penalty limit follows by lower semicontinuity of relative entropy.

Thus the sharper single-copy trace bound is sqrt(2G), with no gentle term.
The projection/smoothing is only a variational witness; it is not an acquired
measurement outcome or a heralded decoder. More generally ANY trial sigma
supported in ran P with D(sigma||rho0)<=G gives(2). This observation is what
allows a logarithmic Jastrow witness for 2D bosons instead of a hard projector.

For n independent copies, even with different admissible W_j at a COMMON
chemical parameter mu, additivity gives
 D(tensor_j rhoW_j(mu)||rho0(mu)^(tensor n))<=sum_j G_j.
Pinsker therefore gives trace error <=sqrt(2sum_j G_j), rather than the
weaker sum of single-copy trace errors. Common G gives sqrt(2nG).

Let T=sum_{j=1}^n M_j and K^(n)=sum_j K_j. The public free n-copy family is
 proportional to exp(-beta K^(n)+beta mu T).
Since [K^(n),T]=0, ONE classical outcome T is exactly sufficient for this
entire free family. Prepare the normalized restriction of exp(-beta K^(n))
to that total-number sector. This measure-prepare CPTP map Phi_n is fixed,
independent of the unknown mu and all W_j, and fixes every free n-copy state.
Contractivity yields
 ||Phi_n(tensor_j rhoW_j)-tensor_j rhoW_j||_1
                  <=2sqrt(2sum_j G_j).                 (3)
This is a collective classical archive; it does not store n separate counts.
It is a full density-matrix assertion, not reference preservation or energy
accuracy. Copies with different unknown chemical parameters do not have
this scalar sufficient statistic and are outside the collective claim.

Suppose a public single-copy exponential moment satisfies
 E_rho0 exp(theta M)<=C_theta<infinity, uniformly in the chemical parameter.
Then the free total-number tail satisfies
 Pr(T>R)<=C_theta^n exp(-theta(R+1)).
Keeping outcomes0,...,R and a tail symbol with a fixed replacement state adds
at most twice this tail probability to(3). For chosen tail tolerance delta,
R=max(0,ceil[(n log C_theta+log(1/delta))/theta]) suffices.
The archive uses at most ceil(log2(R+2)) classical bits and NO quantum memory,
which is O(log(n+1)+loglog(1/delta)) at fixed public theta,C_theta.
State preparation, sampling, gate precision, output size and runtime are
still charged decoder tasks; no efficient implementation is implied here.

For the separately checked collision estimates, the admissible many-copy
regimes improve to n=o(L^d) for spinless fermions, n=o(L^(d-2)) for bosons
d>=3, and, CONDITIONAL on the new logarithmic witness, n=o(log L) for bosons
d=2. These are sufficient rates, not optimal distinguishability thresholds.
