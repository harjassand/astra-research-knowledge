Mechanism
---------

minimizing over the legal real-plane noise coefficient, its angular polynomial
is concave. At its two endpoints, the exact determinant is the two-point
minor of

 K_lambda(x,y)=[x sinh(y)+y sinh(x)-4lambda sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2)]
                                      /[2cosh((x-y)/2)], lambda=kappa/2.

Its full Hardy representation is

 D_lambda=(pi/2)M_b^*M_b-lambda M_bM_b^*, b(z)=sqrt(1-z^2).

At lambda=pi/2, this is the positive hyponormal commutator. Thus the old
global scalar proof extends from 2 to pi. The perpendicular imaginary-noise
sector adds the nonnegative term 2(G-lambda h)(1-c^2). Sum the fully general
KMS Lyapunov noises to finish the theorem.
