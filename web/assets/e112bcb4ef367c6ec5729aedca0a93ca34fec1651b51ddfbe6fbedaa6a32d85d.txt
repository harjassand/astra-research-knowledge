STRONGEST CONCRETE LOCAL RESULT
Contractive finite-precision sparse nc acquisition; full proof and exact costs:
 work/cycle1/algebra_contracting_sparse_theorem.txt
For unknown f=sum_(j<=s) c_j w_j over Q, SUPPLIED n,s, maximum word length D,
and numerator/denominator bit-height H, one FULL approximate matrix query at
dimension 2s recovers every ordered word and rational coefficient exactly.
Explicit upper-triangular dyadic tuple, B a power of two >n+1:
 T_i(a,b)=binom(b,a) i^(b-a)/B^b, 0<=a<=b<2s.
Every T_i has induced column-sum norm 1, query-entry bits O(s log(n+1)).
Required certified absolute precision per entry:
 P=O(H+sD log(n+1)+s+log(s+1)).
The deterministic decoder is polynomial BIT time in n,s,D,H. This includes
actual output word lengths; there is no poly(log D) output claim.

Mechanism/proof in six steps:
1. v(z)T_i=v((z+i)/B). Word code
 alpha(w)=B^-l*(1+sum_t i_t B^(t-1))
is injective, in (0,1] and separated by >=B^-D. Column sums of the response
are moments t_k=sum_j c_j alpha_j^k, k=0,...,2s-1.
2. Set U=2^(s-1) B^(D(s-1)), K=2^H s^2 U^2. Lagrange interpolation bounds
Vandermonde inverse norm by sU. The signed Hankel matrix has actual rank r and
ALL nonzero eigenvalues have absolute value >=1/K. This remains true for signed
cancellation, since its restriction is R diag(c_j) R^T and |c_j|>=2^-H.
3. With sufficiently precise noisy moments, exact rational inertia of
 M_tilde +/- I/(4K)
recovers r. Use rational symmetric Schur elimination, including 2-by-2 pivots;
no floating SVD or exact zero test of a noisy matrix is substituted.
4. Solve the approximate leading-r Hankel recurrence. The true annihilator of
 beta_j=B^D alpha_j
is monic with integer coefficients. Proved inverse perturbation bounds make
the scaled approximate coefficients accurate to <1/8; integer rounding recovers
the annihilator exactly.
5. Exact Sturm isolation recovers the promised integer roots with polynomial
degree/height and O(D log B) interval depths. Base-B digits recover each word.
6. Solve the exact-node Vandermonde system with noisy moments. Its coefficient
errors are <1/(8*2^(2H)); continued fractions recover rational coefficients with
denominators <=2^H. Exact rational full-response recovery is unnecessary.

Precision is not free acquisition: dense query input costs O(ns^3 log(n+1)) bits
under an ordinary explicit matrix interface, response transfer costs
O(s^2(P+H+log(s+1))) bits, plus the target oracle's evaluation/precision cost.
The active matrix service and the supplied D,H are substantive. This is not an
arbitrary fixed-noise, passive, finite-field, or general-rational-circuit learner.
Same-field dimension 2s is optimal across unrestricted coefficients/degrees for
one fixed tuple, by splitting a minimal polynomial. No optimality claimed for
competitors whose tuple may depend on bounded D,H.

Important falsification: each T_i embeds in a (2s+1)-state column-stochastic
matrix [[T_i,0],[ones-ones*T_i,1]]. For positive coefficients summing to 1, the
response is a physical mixture-of-word Markov channel. But THIS fixed tuple can
need exponential sampling: with n=2,s=1,B=4, distinguish words 1^D and
2 1^(D-1). Their informative output probabilities differ by delta=4^-D,
per-trial KL<5 delta^2, so 2/3-success binary discrimination requires at least
(2/45)*16^D independent channel uses. This is a specific-query obstruction,
not a lower bound against every active stochastic design. It stops a claim
that polynomial numerical precision implies polynomial statistical access.

FRESH PRIMARY MECHANISM A: NONCOMMUTATIVE LOW-DEGREE CONCENTRATION
Lakhani--Saxena, ECCC TR26-173, 10 September 2026.
 https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/173/
 https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/173/download
Local PDF/text: TR26-173.pdf / TR26-173.txt. Read theorem and base/higher-depth
proofs; page 9 formula visually checked in TR26-173-page9.png.
Its claimed scope is syntactically homogeneous constant-depth nc circuits.
Ordered positions are encoded in a commutative set-multilinear Hadamard model;
independent marker derivatives force low y-degree concentration, and a small
matrix path construction isolates a nonzero word. It offers small randomized
matrix witnesses despite enormous sparsity. It does not reconstruct coefficients
or replace rational-expression inverse-domain checks.

Specific source defect, not a theorem refutation: p.9 declares the preprocessing
 pi:x->x+r y, y->x-r y
invertible. Its determinant is -2r, hence zero in characteristic 2. The nonzero
homogeneous f=xy-yx is sent to zero for EVERY r in characteristic 2. At
x=E12,y=E21 over F2 it evaluates to I2 (verified in the executed script).
Repair: use a generic invertible GL2 substitution; each nonzero input linear
form then has both coefficients nonzero off a finite union of hyperplanes.
The bad polynomial det(M)*product_(forms L)(both transformed coefficients)
is nonzero and has degree O(number of factors), so a sufficiently large field
extension and Schwartz--Zippel sampling fix the local shortcut. The rest of the
all-characteristic proof was not independently formalized or certified.

Interface correction: poly(log s) headline cost excludes explicit construction
and transfer of n matrices and oracle evaluation. Even the matrix-field-slot
transfer is Omega(n d^2) at dimension d. Rational characteristic-zero response
heights can also be large. Matrix dimension is not a complete bit-time bound.

Complete local acquisition obstruction for this mechanism: fix s distinct
equal-length binary words w_j of length ceil(log2 s). The class
 f_c=sum_(j=1)^s c_j w_j
has homogeneous depth-2 circuits of size O(s log s). Along the all-zero response
transcript, an adaptive exact learner's t-th d_t-by-d_t query contributes at
most d_t^2 linear constraints on c. If sum_t d_t^2<s, some nonzero c survives
all constraints and is indistinguishable from the zero polynomial. Therefore
unrestricted-coefficient exact acquisition needs sum_t d_t^2>=s. Small random
PIT witnesses do not give a poly(log s) matrix-response learner. With finite
height packing, query count may compress but at least sH response bits remain.
This is a standard zero-transcript linear-algebra argument, not a new lower bound.

FRESH PRIMARY MECHANISM B: DETERMINISTIC NESTED SPARSE RECOVERY
Omar Graia, arXiv:2608.18496, 19 August 2026, source preprint.
 https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18496v1
Read exact computational model, bounded decoder, Algorithm 1, Lemmas 3.1/3.2,
Theorem 3.3. Its ring/zero-test costs are explicit; no numerical implementation
or external proof certification inferred. Assumes a known upper bound K on the
TOTAL support of C, not an arbitrary supplied mask of K desired entries.

Mechanism independently reconstructed: maintain integer/ring approximation D.
At scale t, already-correct columns of true sparsity <=t/2 leave at most O(K/t)
nonzero residual columns. Inner binary H measures each residual column with
capacity t plus its stored speculative support. Outer binary G treats each
measurement vector as one element of the direct-product ring R^m and locates
all nonzero columns. Decode/add bounded updates. Correct sparse columns stay
correct, and arbitrary updates on denser columns have controlled support. The
scale product is t*(K/t)=K; eventually every column is exact. Evaluating
 H(C-D)G^T=(HA)(BG^T)-HDG^T
uses shared compressed products. This is genuinely a different mechanism from
independently charged complete-block masks.

Small exact extension derived here: if X=Z+E, Z integer K-sparse, and the
ENTRYWISE GLOBAL l1 norm sum_(ij)|E_ij|<1/2, then for EVERY binary H,G,
 round(HXG^T)=HZG^T,
since each sketch error is a subset sum of E bounded by that l1 norm. Thus the
same nested routine recovers latent Z from rounded sketches, with a certified
margin if measurements themselves are approximate. This proves a narrow
quantized near-sparse interface, not general l2 noise robustness.
32 random rational checks passed. Exact counterexample to weakening l1 to
Frobenius-small noise: n=3,E=ones/(4n), ||E||_F=1/4, H=G=ones row; HEG^T=3/4
rounds to 1 although Z=0. The full nested decoder/sketch generator was NOT
implemented, and no new subquadratic bit exponent is claimed.

FRESH PRIMARY MECHANISM C: SUCCINCT RATIONAL-CIRCUIT RIT IN PSPACE
Arvind--Joglekar, arXiv:2609.37319, 29 September 2026.
 https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37319v1
