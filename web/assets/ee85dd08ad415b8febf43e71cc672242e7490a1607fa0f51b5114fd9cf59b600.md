# C9: basis-free quantum variance gate

Date: 2026-10-08. Independent hardproof lead for the exceptional factor-two quantum gate.

## Verdict

The all-channel factor-two claim remains **UNKNOWN**. This lead gives an exact basis-free reformulation in terms of the variance of a common barycenter-constrained ensemble, derives the exact two-output discrepancy identity, proves the reformulated inequality for dual rank at most two, and identifies why the direct measurement argument stops for rank at least three. No legal marginal-feasible counterexample or unrestricted canonical/EB support certificate was found.

The target is: for every unital, trace-preserving, completely positive, Hilbert--Schmidt-self-adjoint channel \(\Phi:M_d\to M_d\) with a supplied symmetric two-output extension \(\mathcal B\), find one canonical EB map \(\Psi\) such that

\[
  \Psi\succeq 2\Phi-I
\]

on the full real Hermitian operator space. Equivalently, \(I-\Psi\preceq2(I-\Phi)\). No positivity of \(\Phi\) as a Hilbert--Schmidt operator is assumed. All maps, the extension, and the comparison are finite-dimensional exact data; no source-oracle-free construction or acquisition is assumed.

## Exact all-dual reduction

Let \(\tau=\operatorname{Tr}/d\), \(\mathcal H_0=\{A=A^*: \tau(A)=0\}\), and write a positive dual on \(\mathcal H_0\) as

\[
 Q=\sum_i w_i |A_i\rangle\!\rangle\langle\!\langle A_i|,
 \qquad w_i\ge0,\quad \tau(A_i^2)=1.
\]

For a POVM \(M=(M_y)\), the canonical map is

\[
 \Psi_M(X)=\sum_{y:\tau(M_y)>0}
       \frac{\tau(M_yX)}{\tau(M_y)}M_y,
\]

and its support value is

\[
 S_d(Q)=\sup_M \operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Psi_M)
 =\sup_M\sum_iw_i\sum_y\frac{\tau(M_yA_i)^2}{\tau(M_y)}.
\]

The full-domain canonical-domination reduction in the C5 proof gives

\[
 \exists\Psi\text{ canonical}:\Psi\succeq2\Phi-I
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 S_d(Q)\ge 2\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q\Phi)-\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q
 \quad\forall Q\succeq0.
 \tag{1}
\]

Each \(Q\) may have its own supporting POVM. Compact-convex separation is what converts all support inequalities into a *single* comparator \(\Psi\). The reduction from unrestricted EB maps uses full-domain identity slack and must not be inferred from an \(\mathcal H_0\)-only inequality. Canonical effects have \(\sum_y M_y=I\), and the induced pure-state ensemble has barycenter \(I/d\).

Put \(p_y=\tau(M_y)\), \(\rho_y=M_y/\operatorname{Tr}M_y\). Then \(\sum_y p_y\rho_y=I/d\). Rank-one refinement weakly increases every squared-score term. Consequently

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q-S_d(Q)
 =\inf_{\mu:\int\rho\,d\mu=I/d}
       \sum_iw_i\int\operatorname{Var}_{\rho}(A_i)\,d\mu(\rho).
 \tag{2}
\]

Thus the exact missing all-positive-dual inequality is

\[
 \boxed{\inf_{\mu:\int\rho\,d\mu=I/d}
       \sum_iw_i\int\operatorname{Var}_{\rho}(A_i)\,d\mu(\rho)
       \le 2\sum_iw_i\langle A_i,(I-\Phi)A_i\rangle_\tau.}
 \tag{V}
\]

The infimum is over **one common ensemble for the whole frame**. Replacing it by separate minima for each \(A_i\), or removing the barycenter condition, is not valid.

## Exact two-output variance identity

Hilbert--Schmidt self-adjointness and complete positivity allow a Hermitian Kraus representation

\[
 \Phi(A)=\sum_jH_jAH_j,\qquad \sum_jH_j^2=I.
\]

Indeed, symmetrize any Kraus representation with its adjoint and split each Kraus operator into its Hermitian and skew-Hermitian parts. Cyclicity of trace gives

\[
 e_\Phi(A):=\langle A,(I-\Phi)A\rangle_\tau
 =\frac12\sum_j\|[H_j,A]\|_{2,\tau}^2\ge0.
 \tag{3}
\]

So \(I-\Phi\succeq0\) on the Hermitian space follows from the stated assumptions; an additional Hilbert--Schmidt-positive-channel assumption is unnecessary for this step.

Let \(\Omega_{RAB}\) be the normalized broadcaster Choi state. Its reference and both output marginals are \(I/d\). The \(RA\) and \(RB\) marginals are the same channel Choi state. With the usual transpose on the reference, for every Hermitian \(A\),

\[
 \mathbb E_\Omega[(A_R-A_A)^2]
 =2\|A\|_{2,\tau}^2-2\langle A,\Phi(A)\rangle_\tau
 =2e_\Phi(A).
 \tag{4}
\]

This preserves the reference/output marginals and gives a basis-free formulation of the right side of (V). The open step is to turn the summed quantum discrepancy in (4) into one barycenter-correct classical ensemble for a noncommuting frame.

## Established cases and exact failed steps

For one observable \(A=\sum_y a_yP_y\), pull its spectral PVM back from one output: \(E_y=\Phi^*(P_y)=\Phi(P_y)\). Let \(p_y=\tau(E_y)=\tau(P_y)>0\) and \(t_y=\tau(E_yA)=\tau(P_y\Phi(A))\). Direct expansion proves

\[
 \sum_y\frac{t_y^2}{p_y}
 -\bigl(2\langle A,\Phi(A)\rangle_\tau-\|A\|_{2,\tau}^2\bigr)
 =\sum_y\frac{(t_y-a_yp_y)^2}{p_y}\ge0.
 \tag{5}
\]

This proves (V) for rank-one \(Q\), without invoking compatibility.

For two observables \(A_1,A_2\), measure their spectral PVMs on separate broadcaster outputs and take the joint pulled-back effects \(M_{yz}=\mathcal B^*(P_y\otimes R_z)\). They form one input POVM with at most \(d^2\) outcomes. Coarse-graining/refinement Cauchy--Schwarz implies that its canonical score for each observable is at least the corresponding one-output score in (5). This proves (V) for all positive duals of rank at most two.

The scalar identity (5) cannot simply be summed over all frame elements because it supplies a different PVM for each observable. Exact qubit control: for Pauli \(X,Z\), \(S_2(|X\rangle\langle X|)=S_2(|Z\rangle\langle Z|)=1\), but for \(Q=|X\rangle\langle X|+|Z\rangle\langle Z|\), every pure state has \(x^2+z^2\le1\), whence \(S_2(Q)=1<2\). This proves nonadditivity of canonical support, not a channel counterexample.

Randomizing a pair of frame labels to choose one observable per broadcaster output also loses the individual diagonal guarantees: if \(\pi_i\) is the probability label \(i\) is selected, the direct estimate is only \(\sum_iw_i\pi_i(2\langle A_i,\Phi(A_i)\rangle_\tau-\|A_i\|_2^2)\) with \(\sum_i\pi_i\le2\). Cross-frame score might improve an actual common POVM, so this defeats only the diagonal-only estimate.

An independent counterattack derived a global but generally non-sharp moment dual. Define

\[
 C_Q=\sum_iw_iA_i^2,\qquad
 \beta(Q)=\inf_{\|\psi\|=1}\sum_iw_i\operatorname{Var}_{\psi}(A_i).
\]

Then \(Z_Q=C_Q-\beta(Q)I\) satisfies \(\operatorname{Tr}(Z_QP)\ge\sum_iw_i(\operatorname{Tr}(PA_i))^2\) for every pure \(P\), so

\[
 S_d(Q)\le\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}Q-\beta(Q).
 \tag{6}
\]

A *legal* channel/extension with \(\operatorname{Tr}_{HS}(Q(I-\Phi))<\beta(Q)/2\) would therefore refute (1). No such witness was found. This is a one-sided counterexample filter, not the proof of (V): its upper bound on support cannot establish the lower bound needed for the theorem.

## Exact controls, failures, and next discriminating step

- Full traceless frame: \(Q=I_{\mathcal H_0}\) has \(\beta=d(d-1)\), \(S_d(Q)=d-1\). The universal symmetric qudit cloner has \(\lambda=(d+2)/(2(d+1))\), and its Dirichlet trace is exactly \(\beta/2\). This is an equality control, not a counterexample.
- Pairwise anticommuting Hermitian involutions: for \(Q=\sum_i|A_i\rangle\langle A_i|\), \(S_d(Q)=1\) and \(\beta=r-1\); a witness in this family needs \(\sum_i\langle A_i,\Phi(A_i)\rangle_\tau>(r+1)/2\). The support-side threshold does not refute the target.
- Direct separate spectral measurements solve ranks one and two. The naive sum fails from support nonadditivity; pair-label randomization keeps only two selected labels.
- Pairwise near-commutation does not give a common measurement. The prior Pauli-frame example is a measurement obstruction, not a legal channel witness.
- An unconstrained pure-state maximizer or star-Hamiltonian eigenvector can drop the POVM barycenter or the extension marginals. The CP-square/Markov proof route covers channels with an extra factorization and has an admissible qubit obstruction; it is not universal.
- The C4 full-domain broadcaster tree gives a logarithmic rank/dimension comparator. Its rank/energy-profile loss remains; it does not imply the factor-two gate.

The next decisive progress must either prove (V) for arbitrary frame rank using the legal extension, while constructing a single barycenter-correct ensemble, or give a legal extension and positive \(Q\) with a globally valid moment-dual envelope strictly below the target support. A fixed-frame test, unconstrained seed, pairwise commutator bound, or unconstrained star spectrum is insufficient.
