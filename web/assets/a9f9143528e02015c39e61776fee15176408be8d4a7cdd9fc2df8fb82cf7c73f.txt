BOUNDED PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISON: O(d)-COVARIANT CHANNELS, BRAUER STATES,
AND THE CANONICAL SPECTRAL COMPARATOR
Date: 2026-10-09

Scope and claim status
----------------------
This note compares the supplied all-dimensional candidate with primary
literature on orthogonally covariant channels, O(d) tensor O(d)-invariant
states (Brauer states), their exact (1,2)-extendibility region, and their
separability region. The supplied candidate remains a premise under review;
this note neither proves it nor makes a priority/novelty claim. No source
result located states the proposed Dirichlet/HS-majorant implication in the
same form.

Parameter bridge (normalized Choi convention)
----------------------------------------------
Let F be the swap, |Omega> = d^(-1/2) sum_i |ii>, P_Omega=|Omega><Omega|,
and J(Phi)=(id tensor Phi)(P_Omega), so Tr J=1. Set

  f = Tr[J(Phi) F],       r = Tr[J(Phi) P_Omega].

For the supplied map, write

  Phi_ab(X) = tau(X) I + a (X-X^T)/2
             + b ((X+X^T)/2 - tau(X) I)
           = u X + v X^T + (1-b) tau(X) I,
  u=(a+b)/2, v=(b-a)/2.

Direct Choi calculation gives

  f = u + d v + (1-b)/d
    = ((1-d)a+(1+d)b)/2 + (1-b)/d,
  r = u + v/d + (1-b)/d^2
    = ((d-1)a+(d+1)b)/(2d) + (1-b)/d^2.

Conversely, for d>=2,

  a = (d r - f)/(d-1),
  b = (d f + d^2 r - 2)/((d-1)(d+2)).

The scalar, antisymmetric, and symmetric-traceless HS eigenvalues of Phi are
1,a,b. Thus the proposed PSD inequality for an O(d)-covariant comparator
with eigenvalues 1,c,e is exactly

  c >= 2|a|-1,       e >= 2|b|-1.

The comparator's Choi coordinates must be those of an EB channel. The
primary-source EB region below is the full rectangle

  0 <= f_Psi <= 1,       0 <= r_Psi <= 1/d.

The proposed real/circular same-state orbit comparator uses only its upper
edge r_Psi=1/d. For a pure-state orbit point

  J_psi = |overline{psi}><overline{psi}| tensor |psi><psi|,

one has f(J_psi)=|psi^T psi|^2 in [0,1] and r(J_psi)=1/d. Real unit vectors
attain (f,r)=(1,1/d); circular vectors psi=(u+iv)/sqrt(2), with real
orthonormal u,v, attain (0,1/d). Their O(d)-twirled product states, and
convex mixtures of the two endpoint orbit states, are separable Choi states
and hence define EB channels. This orbit construction is already present in
the separability literature (explicitly in d=3 in Vollbrecht-Werner; the
all-d region is covered by Chruscinski-Kossakowski).

Three exact imported results
----------------------------

1. Exact O(d) channel normal form and EB test. Ion Nechita and Sang-Jun Park,
   “Random Covariant Quantum Channels,” arXiv:2403.03667v2 (18 Jul 2024),
   Ann. Henri Poincare (2025), DOI 10.1007/s00023-025-01558-y.
   Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2403.03667v2
   Locations: Section 3, Proposition 3.1, Eqs. (11) and parameter table,
   lines 320-338; Proposition 3.3, lines 384-398. Proposition 3.1 says the
   O,O twirl of any channel has form

     p' id + q' top + (1-p'-q') Delta,
     Delta(X)=Tr(X)I/d,

   and gives its coefficients from two Choi invariants. Since an O-covariant
   channel is fixed by this twirl, this is the complete channel family in
   the supplied parameterization (p'=(a+b)/2, q'=(b-a)/2). Their Choi
   convention is unnormalized: lambda_1=d^2 r, lambda_2=d f. Proposition
   3.3 gives, for an O,O-twirled channel, PPT iff EB iff
   0<=lambda_1,lambda_2<=d; in the normalized coordinates above this is
   exactly 0<=r<=1/d and 0<=f<=1. This establishes the EB comparator region,
   not the existence of a comparator dominating a given self-compatible
   channel.

2. Exact all-dimensional Brauer-state separability region and product
   witnesses. Dariusz Chruscinski and Andrzej Kossakowski, “On multipartite
   invariant states II. Orthogonal symmetry,” arXiv:quant-ph/0601110v2
   (01 Feb 2006).
   Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0601110v2
   Locations: Section II.1, Eqs. (17)-(24); Section II.2, Eqs. (25)-(33),
   especially the conclusion at lines 151-177. They use the three Brauer
   projectors and product overlaps

     alpha=|<psi|varphi>|^2, beta=|<psi|overline{varphi}>|^2,
     pi_1=(1-alpha)/2, pi_2=beta/d.

   The exact all-d condition is separable iff PPT iff
   pi_1<=1/2 and pi_2<=1/d. In (f,r) coordinates this is the rectangle
   [0,1] x [0,1/d]. The statement applies to every d in the paper's
   finite-dimensional setting. It characterizes which O-covariant channels
   are EB through Choi; it is not a self-compatibility or spectral-domination
   theorem.

3. Exact (1,2)-extendibility region for the full Brauer family. Adrian
   Solymos, David Jakab, and Zoltan Zimboras, “Extendibility of Brauer
   states,” arXiv:2411.04597v2 (07 Dec 2025), published J. Phys. A: Math.
   Theor. 59 (2026) 325303, DOI 10.1088/1751-8121/ae8088.
   Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04597v2 and
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.04597v2
   Locations: Section 2.1, Definition 1 (printed pp. 2-3); Section 3,
   Eqs. (6)-(9) (printed p. 4); Lemma 1, Eq. (11) and proof (printed
   pp. 5-6); Section 6 (printed pp. 10-11); Appendix F.1 (printed
   pp. 35-38). They define (1,2)-extendibility by the existence of one
   state on A tensor B tensor B' whose AB and AB' reductions equal the
   given bipartite state. Permutation invariance need not be assumed, since
   the extension can be symmetrized. Their Lemma 1 shows that for a Brauer
   state it is enough to impose the diagonal O(d) and output-swap symmetries
   on the extension. The paper explicitly determines the exact (1,2)
   extendible subset for every local dimension d, including the dimension
   dependent low-d cases. In the f=Tr(rho F), r=Tr(rho P_Omega) coordinates,
   the full physical Brauer-state region is

     -1<=f<=1, 0<=r<=1, 2r<=1+f.

   For d>5, Appendix F.1 expresses the exact (1,2) region as the convex hull
   of the five points

     (1/2,(d-1)/(2d)), (-1,0), (1/2,0), (-1/2,0), (1,0)

   and the ellipse

     f(theta)=1/4+(3/4)cos(theta),
     r(theta)=(d+1)/(4d)+(d+5)/(12d)cos(theta)
             +sqrt(2(d+2)(d-1))/(6d) sin(theta),
     0<=theta<2pi.

   The derivation treats d<=5 separately; the article states the exact
   characterization for all d. A self-compatible channel is exactly a
   (1,2)-extendible normalized Choi state: a channel broadcaster gives the
   extension, and the Choi inverse of an extension with reference marginal
   I/d gives the broadcaster. O(d)-invariance forces the one-body marginals
   to I/d. Thus this is an exact characterization of the input region of
   the supplied candidate for all d>=3, stronger than using only necessary
   scalar monogamy caps. It does not state the required EB spectral-majorant
   conclusion.

Context for the orbit witnesses and older shareability results
--------------------------------------------------------------
Vollbrecht and Werner, “Entanglement Measures under Symmetry,”
arXiv:quant-ph/0010095v2 (01 Mar 2001), https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0010095v2,
Section II.4, Eqs. (12)-(14) and lines 182-186, explicitly derive the
O,O-invariant separable square by placing four product-vector expectations
at its corners. The displayed projectors use 1/3 and the figure is labeled
d=3; do not cite this as the all-d separability theorem. Their same-state
real and circular examples give the r=1/d, f=1 and f=0 endpoints in the
normalized Choi convention after identifying the reference conjugation.
Chruscinski-Kossakowski supplies the all-d result above.

Johnson and Viola, “Compatible quantum correlations: on extension problems
for Werner and isotropic states,” arXiv:1305.1342v3,
https://arxiv.org/html/1305.1342v3, Theorems III.6-III.8, Eqs. (25)-(27),
give exact arbitrary-d shareability on the one-parameter Werner and
isotropic subfamilies (including the equal-state 1-n thresholds). These are
useful edge checks, but they do not give the two-parameter Brauer interior;
the 2026 Solymos-Jakab-Zimboras result is the closer theorem for that
interior.

Exact comparison gate; no priority inference
--------------------------------------------
The literature now supplies both sets needed for a direct geometric test:
(i) the exact all-d input set E_d of (1,2)-extendible Brauer Choi points and
(ii) the full EB rectangle, plus the smaller same-state-orbit segment
S_orb={(t,1/d):0<=t<=1} used by the proposed canonical comparator. The
unresolved comparison is the cross-set implication, not either region in
isolation. In the explicit coordinates above it is:

  For every (f,r) in E_d, find t in [0,1] such that

    (1-t)/(d-1) >= 2 |(d r-f)/(d-1)| - 1,
    (d t+d-2)/((d-1)(d+2))
       >= 2 |(d f+d^2 r-2)/((d-1)(d+2))| - 1.

These are exactly the two HS spectral inequalities for the same-state-orbit
EB comparator. If the root candidate uses a specified canonical t(f,r),
that formula must satisfy both inequalities over E_d, not merely at the
Werner/isotropic edges or on a finite sample. The exact Brauer extension
paper gives E_d but does not state this inclusion, the Dirichlet convex-roof
comparison, or the signed tensor-product consequence. Therefore this source
pass identifies a close exact antecedent and a potentially derivable
geometric corollary; it does not establish novelty or priority. The root
all-d proof is reported to be elementary and independent of this region, so
there is no dependency on importing the unvalidated literature theorem.
