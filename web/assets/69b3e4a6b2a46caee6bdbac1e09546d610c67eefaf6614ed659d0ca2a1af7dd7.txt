QHC unbounded-adaptive-height gate: annealed route and square duality repair
Date: 2026-10-09

STATUS
======
The unbounded exact heights created by a high-cluster exploration do not, by
 themselves, block the triangular high-crossing method. The pinned triangular
 proof freezes adaptive exact values, but every energy/moment estimate is
 annealed over the original transcript and stationary joining. It explicitly
 does not claim a bound uniform over each realized exact pin vector. A square
 proof can avoid the bounded-wall SC0--SC3 misuse by following that same order:
 establish sparse-contact and moment inputs for the original actual-Gamma laws,
 then disintegrate and join after the adaptive exploration, preserving those
 original marginals. No post-exploration pin-height bound is needed.

This packet proves two exact finite/measure-theoretic facts and gives a
conditional high-crossing reduction. It does NOT prove the square annealed
reflected-comparison estimate, a square feasible-shift theorem, or the target
uniform QHC probability. Those are the remaining analytic gates. The packet
makes no endpoint or external-validation claim.

The exact high-crossing proof is `geometry.tex`, Lemma g:high-crossing,
lines 312--397. Its sequence is:

1. From a deterministic transverse seed, adaptively explore the component
   of h1-h2 >= K. Query both heights at each exposed vertex. Every nonseed
   queried vertex has difference >= K-2: a successful query is above K, and
   a failed neighbor query differs by at most 2 across one lattice edge.
2. Freeze the exact common queried set and values, then use a stationary
   synchronous joining of the two conditional Gibbs kernels. The source's
   adapted-freezing lemma (coupling.tex, around lines 160--190) proves by
   disintegration that the conditional kernels are a product and every
   stationary joining preserves both original annealed marginals.
3. In a middle testing corridor away from the seed, the truncated tail
       u_K = (1 - (h1-h2)/(K-2))_+
   is zero on the revealed set. Apply the local reflected-comparison estimate
   after rescaling by K-2. The theorem's threshold-uniform specialization
   gives a K-independent energy/Sobolev bound. Its inputs concern the
   original marginal laws; exact adaptive pins need not be bounded.
4. Original macroscopic average moments imply (h1-h2)/(K-2) -> 0 in the
   iterated n then K limit. If a high crossing had probability at least p0,
   conditioning on that event costs at most 1/p0 in the energy bounds.
   Compactness and the varying-set/Frostman trace argument give a limit
   u >= 1 a.e. but zero trace on a positive-diameter crossing continuum,
   contradiction.

The square analogue of step 2 is exact and graph-independent for any finite
hard-Lipschitz polytopes: for an adaptive common sequence of queries whose
next site/stopping rule uses only past answers and independent randomness,
disintegrating over the transcript leaves the product of the two Gibbs
kernels. Replacing that product by any conditional joining with the same
marginals and integrating restores the original marginal laws. The query
values can be arbitrarily large. The K-2 bound uses only the fact that each
field is 1-Lipschitz on square NN edges, so their difference is 2-Lipschitz.

This is an annealed statement, not a quenched bounded-pin estimate. The source
says so explicitly in both the high-crossing proof and c:comparison theorem:
its constants are uniform after averaging over the adaptive transcript, not
uniform over every realized pin vector. If square SC0--SC3 controls only the
original bounded actual-wall instance, it may be used to establish original
marginal inputs there; it cannot be re-applied to each newly revealed exact
fiber.

SQUARE DUALITY REPAIR (PROVED)
==============================
For square site paths the complement of a failed NN crossing is a star
(8-neighbor) crossing, not an NN crossing. The triangular same-adjacency
shortcut therefore cannot be copied verbatim. However, the height constraint
repairs exactly the needed high-crossing implication with a one-unit threshold
loss.

Lemma (star blocker to primal NN blocker). Let h be 1-Lipschitz on square NN
edges. Suppose u and v are diagonally adjacent and h(u),h(v) <= -H. Each of
the two intermediate corners w is NN-adjacent to u, so h(w) <= -H+1. Thus a
star-connected path of vertices with h<=-H converts, by inserting one
intermediate corner for each diagonal step, into an NN-connected path in the
one-cell enlargement with h<=-H+1. If the original star path crosses a
rectangle, the converted NN component still meets the two opposite sides;
therefore it contains an NN crossing of the enlarged rectangle. The reflected
statement holds for h>=H.

Use square site-crossing duality with good set {h>-H}: either it has a primal
NN left-right crossing, or {h<=-H} has a top-bottom star crossing. The lemma
turns the latter into a primal NN high-negative crossing in the one-cell
buffer. Hence, in a free rectangle with that buffer, the square one-field
high-crossing estimate at threshold H-1 bounds failure of an NN crossing of
{h>-H}. Similarly, failure of an NN crossing of {h<H} is bounded by the
high-positive crossing estimate at H-1. The added costs are exactly one unit
of threshold and one lattice cell of free buffer.

This repairs the *path* duality needed to build square NN threshold crossings.
It does not convert a dual/star cycle into a primal cycle. The circuits needed
for dependency swapping must be literal primal NN sign cycles. They must be
built by arranging overlapping square crossing corridors (with overlaps that
force a common lattice vertex) and then extracting a winding-one simple cycle.
A dual separator alone is not a valid dependency barrier.

EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO A QUENCHED HEIGHT-CAP SHORTCUT
======================================================
Let free vertices be V_n={x in Z^2: ||x||_infinity <= n}, with zero pins on
the surrounding ring ||x||_infinity=n+1. Define
  t_n(x)=n+1-||x||_infinity on V_n, and t_n=0 on the pinned ring.
For fixed 0<epsilon<1, set h1=(1-epsilon)t_n and h2=0. Both are strictly
feasible: every NN edge difference in h1 is at most 1-epsilon and every
h2 difference is zero. A full coordinate neighborhood of radius less than
epsilon/2 about each free vector remains feasible, so these configurations
lie in the support of the positive-volume product uniform law, not merely on
its boundary. For each fixed K, the NN component of
  {h1-h2 >= K}
that contains the origin crosses a square of diameter Theta(n); its exact
queried center height is (1-epsilon)(n+1), which diverges with n.

Thus an adaptive exploration can produce exact pins of unbounded magnitude
even from zero bounded exterior data. Any SC0-style deterministic bound
||h_A-h_0||_infinity <= B independent of n fails on such a fiber when h_A
has this center pin and h_0 is zero-pinned there: the left side is at least
(1-epsilon)(n+1). This is a support/counterexample to the *quenched
extension*, not a counterexample to an annealed high-crossing estimate; the
probability of this particular neighborhood may be very small. The distinction
is essential.

CONDITIONAL SQUARE HIGH-CROSSING REDUCTION
==========================================
Assume the following square inputs for the original base laws only, uniformly
in the actual Gamma sign transcript:

(A) Macroscopic anchored average moments for each original marginal.
(B) A square analogue of the full annealed high-crossing package: local
    reflected-comparison for adaptive exact queries, sparse-contact and hole
    bounds under the original marginals, and the varying-set zero-trace step
    for the random revealed component. The conclusion must be a K-uniform
    W^{1,p} bound for the filled tail, L1 closeness to the actual tail, and
    zero trace on the limiting Frostman test supported by queried zero-tail
    vertices. A bare W^{1,p} bound and L1 approximation do not supply this
    trace conclusion. This theorem may not require a deterministic bound on
    newly queried heights.
(C) A square analogue of the feasible-shift proposition for separated smooth
    bumps, with positive image density uniform in R, derived from square
    contact/CAP inputs.
(D) The square path-and-corridor construction that turns the resulting
    threshold paths and shifts into nested strict-sign primal NN cycles. The
    construction must force literal NN intersections; dual/star separation
    alone does not make a primal dependency barrier.

Then (A)--(B), by the source compactness/Frostman argument, exclude an
opposite-order high NN path across any fixed positive fraction of a free
corridor with probability bounded below as K -> infinity after R -> infinity.
The square duality repair above turns that estimate into probability tending
to one for each required NN crossing of {h>-H} or {h<H} at a sufficiently
large fixed H. The source's subsequent shift/corridor construction is a
separate load-bearing step: its triangular version uses triangular site
duality and a prescribed finite crossing network. A square replacement must
prove (C)--(D); this packet does not assert that arbitrary threshold paths
for two replicas automatically coincide or form a sign cycle. If (C)--(D)
are established with uniform costs, they yield the uniform nested primal-cycle
event required by the finite cluster-swap gate.

The reduction has no factor from conditioning on each exact adaptive value.
The actual Gamma transcript itself is retained in full, including every
turn-determined T<0 query. Its wall values are bounded by 2 from the local
opposite-sign edge/turn geometry, so it is part of the original-law input in
(A)--(C); those T constraints are never dropped. The adaptive high-cluster
queries are different: they are integrated out after marginal-preserving
joining. Their count can be O(R^2), but it contributes no probability factor
if (B) is proved uniformly for finite adapted algorithms from the original
marginal hypotheses.

WHAT IS STILL MISSING
=====================
1. Prove (B) on square NN graphs from the admitted square inputs, including
   the local block/contact-slack estimate, hole filling, and the trace on the
   random explored component. A bounded-pin SC0--SC3 statement for each exact
   fiber does not imply (B); the tent family above rules out that shortcut.
   The useful target is an annealed theorem analogous to source c:comparison,
   not a quenched estimate.
2. Prove (C) in the square with the same domain-independent image-density
   control as the triangular shift proposition. CAP alone bounds specified
   near-extremal edge sets; the map still needs slack-component size,
   injectivity, and Jacobian costs.
3. Prove (D) for the actual QHC domains and records: verify macro anchors,
   construct a finite network with literal NN intersections, and keep nested
   strict-sign cycles in the common free annulus. The star-to-NN conversion
   costs a one-cell buffer; the cluster swap needs primal NN cycles of strict
   sign.

No actual square QHC counterexample is found. The exact square path-duality
lemma is proved; the analytic adapted comparison, feasible-shift, and
primal-cycle construction gates remain OPEN. CAP is an external primary input only at its published
zero-pin/degree-four scope; no claim that CAP alone proves (B) or (C) is made.
