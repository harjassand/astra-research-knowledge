QUANTUM CAPABILITY — RESOURCE AND COST LEDGER
Date: 2026-10-08

1. N62 theorem contract

Charged quantity: the exact spectral support of the accepted parallel input
state, supp(rho) subset 1_[0,Q](sum_i H_i). This is a maximum-support
constraint, not Tr(rho H)<=Q and not a total apparatus work bound. The model
H,E and their finite-dimensional exact values are supplied to the optimizer.

Not charged by the theorem: acquisition of H,E or the channel, their bit
precision and conditioning, synthesis of the top tilted vector, coherent
type projection, postselected-filter retry cost and failed-attempt energy,
drive work, phase reference size/restoration, detector implementation,
channel time, and reset. The N62 type construction supplies a polynomial
success lower bound for an ideal local filter; it does not prove an efficient
or minimum-work preparation. A polynomially small herald rate makes
retry-work a separate term.

2. Shared-phase nuisance example

Charged: n=2 parallel qutrit inputs with total spectral ceiling Q=3; type-I
error is exactly zero. Supplied: the group U_theta and the channel/effect
family. No calibration query, phase estimate, side information, or phase
reference is charged. The robust minimax statement is therefore an ideal
uncalibrated-parameter result, not an experiment cost model.

3. Finite-false-alarm channel pair

The global M_0 baseline permits all parallel use counts, arbitrary entangled
input states under Q=1, and arbitrary output tests; its best power at alpha=.01
is 101/200. The M_1/10 witness uses one exact |l> input of energy 1 and a
rank-one coherent output test with false-alarm exactly .01. Both alternatives
and the null are supplied mathematical channels; no learning cost is included.
The state/test coherence, detector readout, gate synthesis, phase reference,
calibration and reset work are uncharged. This is an exact interface
counterexample, not a claim of a device advantage.

4. Mixed-null orthogonal flag

The lemma counts only the same input support budget Q. The test randomizes on
the null-supported sector with probability delta and rejects the orthogonal
click sector. It does not charge flag certification, support leakage,
random-bit generation or output readout. If these effects leak across the
flag, the exact factor (1-delta) need not hold.

5. N32 two-use signal/work comparison (conditional accounting model)

For the supplied qutrit and Q_cap=3, the entangled witness miss is
(3-sqrt(5))/8 and its actual mean signal energy is (25-sqrt(5))/10, about
2.276393. The separable optimum witness miss is 1/8 and its mean signal energy
is 7/3. Under the declared ground-start battery convention, input excitation
work is charged by mean energy; Q_cap remains a support limit and is not
counted as spent energy. A shared reference/detector/final-bit reset charge
c=.01 and an extra dissipative entangling-pulse charge w=.4 yield reliabilities
per total charged work about .874302 (entangled) and .887386 (separable).
The exact crossover is w*=.360389... for this ledger. This demonstrates only
that the signal-only gain does not imply a universal total-work gain; w is an
implementation-specific assumption, not a lower bound on optimal hardware.

6. N40 expected cost versus N62 hard support

N40 constrains expected channel-input cost and uses a smooth-Dicke packet. N62
constrains the full spectral support of the accepted input. The qutrit
expected exponent (5/3)log2, hard exponent log((1+sqrt17)/2), and separable
hard exponent log2 answer different resource questions. Never substitute an
expected-energy tail estimate for hard support.

7. Exact D=4 level-count witness

The three-level operator example charges the N62 hard support only. It
certifies an exponent-ratio order inequality, not a finite-use advantage
witness, a control schedule, or a physical preparation cost. Its purpose is
to show that a universal ratio bound of two fails at k=3.

8. Broader per-leaf accounting

Exact communication and onsite many-body reductions are input-support
results with their own outer coding or volume costs. The phase-covariant
preparation/QEC leaf charges a coherent three-level battery in support
{2,3}; the full syndrome/reset thermodynamics remain open. See leaves 04,
07-10, 12, 16-18 for the scoped ledgers. All cost comparisons are conditional
on their stated interfaces and are not merged into a universal thermodynamic
advantage claim.
