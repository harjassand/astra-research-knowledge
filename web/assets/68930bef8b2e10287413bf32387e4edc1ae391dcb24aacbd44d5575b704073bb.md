# Sources and scope

1. Xiaoyu Chen and Kuikui Liu, *An FPRAS for Counting Common Bases of Two Matroids*, arXiv:2610.06724v1 (posted 5 October 2026): [HTML v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06724v1). Theorem 1.6 and Definitions 1.4–1.5 specify the subset-convolution coefficient, nonnegative multiaffine evaluation oracles, log-concavity, seed sets, coefficient ranges, and arithmetic-call bound. Definition 1.1 gives success probability $3/4$ and its median-trick amplification. Appendix A.1 explains the coefficient restriction and exact support-zero branch. Remark A.4 states a polynomial worst-case bit implementation for rational parameters and polynomial-bit rational oracles. These are source-reported theorem claims; this leaf checks the proposed $Z_k$ interface, not the full theorem proof.
2. The row/column determinant identity, the local selector Hessian, both $2\times2$ counterexamples, and the range bound are derived directly in RESULT.md; no extra external theorem is needed for those calculations.
3. The Gram determinant identity is derived from Cauchy–Binet in RESULT.md. The counterexample concerns only the complete-site-pair selector in that specific two-factor convolution, not every possible Gram lift.
