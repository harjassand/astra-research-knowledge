Key theorem state
-----------------
The rank is unknown. The only observations are a single forward stationary
symbol stream; each look uses fresh length-(2L+1) windows. All prefix words are
algorithmically enumerated. For rank cap R, L>=R-1 gives rank(H_L)=rank(H).
The known rational gap gamma and exponential beta-mixing envelope are needed
for the confidence radius and the skipped-symbol schedule. Minimum history
mass p_min is needed only if the requested output additionally requires every
enumerated history to have been seen; it is not needed for rank-only
certification. The inertia tests are exact rational arithmetic on Hhat^T Hhat,
not floating SVD thresholding.

Known limitations / do not silently upgrade
--------------------------------------------
- The result is a conditional finite-sample rank certificate, not a new
  general spectral learner and not an adaptive feature/basis search.
- The matrix and word-table sizes remain exponential in L.
- The no-horizon parity family is used only to remove the state cap/witness
  horizon; it is not claimed as a fixed-uniform-mixing lower bound. The
  small-theta Markov pair separately proves necessity of a uniform gap.
- The p_min lower bound is tied to explicit history exposure; it is not a
  separate minimax lower bound under fixed gamma.
- No predictive coefficients are emitted, so future prediction, coefficient
  precision, and validation of a realization are outside scope.
- Full text for the primary Carlyle–Paz, Yu, and Berbee sources was not
  completely available in this source pass; see SOURCE_LEDGER.txt.
