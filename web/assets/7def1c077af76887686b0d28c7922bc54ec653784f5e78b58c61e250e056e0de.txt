# c01_l06 — final quantifier and annealing audit

Date: 2026-10-07. Status: conditional annealing bridge audited; determinant-specific local kernel and polynomial energy bound remain unresolved. This work did not establish a determinant sampler, an FPRAS, or a field-breaking result.

## Exact local gate

For each positive annealing tier t, let Ω be the shared support of microscopic full-basis configurations A with weight

  w_t(A) = (product_{ell in A} activity_{ell,t}) |det V_A|²,
  π_t(A) = w_t(A)/Z_t.

For a reversible two-replica kernel K_t with stationary law π_t⊗π_t, the required local statement is

  Var_{π_t}(a) <= L E_t((A,B) -> a(A)+b(B))

for every pair of real functions a,b:Ω→R. The constant L must be polynomial in the charged input size and uniform over every positive field used by the annealing path. The functions may be arbitrary and, after conditioning on earlier product coordinates, may depend on that conditioning. There is no centering, low-degree, boundedness, or determinant-observable restriction. This quantifier is over additive observables with independent coordinate functions; it is not a Poincare inequality for every function on Ω².

In centered H0=L²_0(π), write the additive energy as

  E(a(A)+b(B)) = <a,Aa> + 2<a,Bb> + <b,Cb>.

The block form is positive semidefinite. The all-a,b gate is equivalent to the Schur condition

  A - B C† B* >= L^-1 I,

where C† is the pseudoinverse on range(C). Thus checking b=0 only tests A and misses cancellation through the cross block. A useful sufficient criterion is A(a)>=λ Var(a) and a uniform increment-correlation bound |<a,Bb>|<=ρ sqrt(<a,Aa><b,Cb>) with ρ<1; it gives L<=1/((1−ρ)λ). These are operator diagnostics, not algorithms for large determinant kernels.

## What the local statement does and does not buy

The local inequality transfers to an adjacent-tier pair only with an overlap and kernel-comparison hypothesis. If π_{t−1}(z)/π_t(z)∈[1/2,2] pointwise, the pair laws π_t⊗π_{t−1} and π_t⊗π_t have the same pointwise comparison. For a common proposal Q with Metropolis acceptance, edge capacity is Q(s,s')min(μ(s),μ(s')); hence the cross-tier Dirichlet form dominates half the equal-tier one. For all a,b,

  Var_{π_t}a <= 2L E_{π_t⊗π_{t−1}}(a(x)+b(y)).

Density comparison alone does not transfer an arbitrary tier-dependent proposal; the same proposal or a separate edge-capacity comparison is required.

With q=32L replicas per tier, connect every tier-t slot x to all q tier-(t−1) guide slots y. Add q exact independent refreshes from π_0 and order slots from high to low tier. For a function f on the full product, its martingale increment h_x and guide increment k_xy are arbitrary one-coordinate functions after fixing the preceding slots. The local inequality gives

  ||h_x||² <= 4L E_xy(f) + 8L ||r_xy||²,

where r_xy is the two-slot ANOVA residual. Each residual consists only of interaction components whose last two coordinates are x,y. The ordering assigns each such component to at most one pair, so Σ_xy||r_xy||²<=Var(f). Averaging over q guides and charging base increments to their refreshes yields

  Var_ν(f) <= (1/6)Σ_xy E_xy(f) + (4/3)Σ_base E_i(f).

The lazy uniform mixture of M=Tq²+q updates has gap at least 3/(8M). This is a full Poincare inequality for arbitrary functions on the entire replicated product. The base refresh and ANOVA allocation are essential parts of the proof; they are not consequences of the local gate.

The exact finite witnesses distinguish the levels:

- For θ(x,y)=(1−y,1−x) under uniform {0,1}², the restricted b=0 inequality holds with L=1 for every a, but a(x)=x,b(y)=−y is invariant and has zero energy while Var(a)=1/4. A theorem with only b=0 is insufficient.
- For coordinate swap (x,y)↦(y,x), f(x)+f(y) is invariant for every f. A test restricted to f1=f2=f is vacuous despite nonzero Var(f(X)).
- For resampling uniformly conditional on X xor Y, the all-a,b additive inequality holds with L=1, yet (-1)^(X xor Y) is a nonconstant zero-energy pair invariant. Even the correct additive gate is not a full pair-chain gap.
- At both endpoint laws π_0=(1−2^-m,2^-m) and π_1=(2^-m,1−2^-m), independent pair heatbath has gap 1 and the all-a,b bound with L=1. A direct importance step nevertheless has relative variance (1−2ε)²/[ε(1−ε)]=Θ(2^m). At m=12 it is exactly 16760836/4095 and the maximum likelihood ratio is 4095. Endpoint local gaps do not supply annealing overlap.

All four computations use exact rational arithmetic. Scripts and outputs are in `quantifier_audit.py/json` and `revisions/`.

## Conditional end-to-end charges

If the local gate is proved with polynomial L, all line activities can be varied by rational multiplicative steps c=1+1/(4d). A rank-d basis selects d lines, so one step changes its unnormalized weight by at most c^d and its normalized probability by at most c^(2d)<2. This gives O(d log(activity range)) tiers, with shared positive support. Zero-activity endpoint changes need a separate support/TV argument.

For an encoded rational-complex d×m matrix, take a common denominator D of all entries, R=max(1,max entry magnitude), and positive rational activities λ_j. Any nonzero determinant is a Gaussian integer divided by D^d, so its squared modulus is at least D^(-2d); Hadamard gives |det V_A|²<=d^d R^(2d). Since there are at most 2^m bases,

  log₂(1/min_A π(A))
    <= m + 2d log₂D + d log₂(λ_max/λ_min) + d log₂d + 2d log₂R.

This is polynomial in the input bit length. If H bounds this quantity at every tier, the replica product satisfies Λ=log(1/minν)<=q(T+1)H. From any known state, TV error σ is reached in at most

  ceil((8M/3)(Λ/2 + log(1/(2σ))))

updates. Exact rational determinant point weights and Metropolis ratios are polynomial-time computable without a partition oracle. A candidate kernel must also have proposals and bounded-randomness simulation of polynomial bit cost. If a base refresh is only η-close in TV, couple ideal and implemented trajectories and budget at most (#refresh calls)η; that update is not exactly stationary by itself.

For ratio estimation let R_t=w_t/w_{t−1}; then E_{π_{t−1}}R_t=Z_t/Z_{t−1} and R_t/E R_t=π_t/π_{t−1}. Under the factor-2 density bound, the normalized ratio has second moment at most 2. With n=Θ(T/ε²) independent samples per stage, the product of stage means has relative variance at most (1+1/n)^T−1=O(ε²). A median of O(log(1/δ)) independent products gives total O(T² ε^-2 log(1/δ)) independent samples. Each sample still incurs the warm-start-free mixing, approximate-refresh, finite-bit, and per-step transition costs above. Estimating each stage separately to accuracy ε/T gives a simpler but looser O(T³ ε^-2 log(T/δ)) sample count. Correlated chain states cannot be counted as independent without an additional dependence bound.

The fixed-k dummy-coordinate identity in the shared baseline turns the desired coefficient into a full-basis partition times a known factorial. Conditional on a local kernel for the augmented instance, this avoids a rare marked-event estimator; it does not remove the local-energy gate.

## Exact unresolved gate and source status

The missing determinant-specific result is a reversible two-replica kernel over the actual augmented determinant-basis states whose proposals and Metropolis ratios are computable from rational input without any aggregate coefficient/partition oracle, together with the all-a,b inequality and a polynomial L uniform over the entire positive activity path. The common support and local inequality must include the coordinate-line auxiliaries used by the annealing construction. The prior cycle-5 positive-overlay and common-cut sewing obstructions rule out those specified mechanisms only; they do not refute an arbitrary kernel or the all-a,b inequality.
