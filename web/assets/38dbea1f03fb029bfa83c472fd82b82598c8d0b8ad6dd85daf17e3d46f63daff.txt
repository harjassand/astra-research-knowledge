1. THE EXACT COMPLETED-CROSSCUT SCOPE

The acquired target is the canonical COMPLETE Gamma. A proper simple
crosscut divides a disk into two physical observer components. Each of
these sees ONE Gamma bank and its own original boundary arc. Multiple
primal graph components in that physical component do not change this
description. The conditional mean in each component is harmonic by the
local producer. Its Sobolev conclusion is W^{1,p}_loc in that component.

There is no single ambient W1p extension asserted for the combined mean
on BOTH physical components. Even the ideal means +c and -c on the two
half-disks cut by a diameter have a jump along that diameter and cannot be
one W1p function across it. The physical interpolants at finite n join the
two values in a strip of width O(1/n); their energy can grow like n^{p-1}.
Separate component-specific extensions, if proved, may prescribe different
values on the OTHER component. They are not a joint extension of the actual
two-sided conditional mean.

For a PREFIX one observer component can see two opposite Gamma banks. The
main harmonicity mechanism can be localized to compact free buffers in that
component if its exact sign-kernel opposition hypothesis holds. This does
NOT supply a compatible single ambient mean extension through both banks.
In particular it would be false to replace that conclusion by the complete-
component phrase without charging which accesses the same component sees.

2. EXACT SLIT FALSIFIER OF THE AMBIENT INFERENCE

Let Omega be the unit disk with its nonnegative real radius removed. Write
theta=Arg(z) in (0,2pi) and m(z)=theta/(2pi). This is bounded harmonic and
belongs to W1p(Omega) for every 1<=p<2:
  |grad m|=1/(2pi|z|),
  integral_Omega |grad m|^p = (2pi)^{1-p}/(2-p).
The two banks of the radius have traces 0 and 1. On an annular rectangle
crossing an interior portion of that radius, any ambient W1p function
agreeing with m off the radius would have a jump at y=0 on almost every
vertical line. The ACL representative of a W1p function, p>=1, is absolutely
continuous on almost every such line, which is impossible. This is stronger
than an interior-only counterexample: even the whole-domain W1p norm of m
is finite. The missing property is compatibility of the two bank traces.

The example refutes the inference, not the conditional square law. For the
actual completed-contour limits, the supplied ranges m_+>=c and m_-<=-c
already forbid one ambient extension agreeing with BOTH banks across any
regular positive-length common segment: the same ACL argument applies.
Thus the only meaningful desired ambient mean theorem is explicitly local
and component-specific, or concerns a deficit/difference with matching
wall values. Neither is a consequence of section 5's interior compactness.

3. WHAT THE COMPLETE COMPONENT'S RANGE DOES GIVE

Let f map the unit disk to a completed observer component. A bounded harmonic
m composes to a bounded harmonic function on the disk. Its a.e. radial
boundary values retain every pointwise range of m. Consequently the
supplied finite global ranges and the acquired harmonicity give
  c<=Tr m_+<=1/2,   -1<=Tr m_-<=-c
at harmonic-measure-a.e. prime ends, including their OWN Gamma bank.
The two component-specific harmonic measures and their marks are those of
the physical-walk bridge. No ambient Sobolev extension or source final
theorem is needed for THESE range bounds. This is not a constant height-gap
identity, equality of bank values, or a Frostman trace at every rough point.

4. A GENUINE SAME-AMBIENT MEAN EXTENSION WHEN PINS MATCH

Here is a positive local acquisition with a checkable extra premise, useful
on an ordinary original boundary arc. Consider a window B2 and smaller B1,
and one physical component locally adjacent to a boundary segment Sigma.
Assume all actual incident pins on Sigma have the SAME deterministic value
a, |a|<=1/2. Every other pin/input is at graph path diameter distance >=c n
from B1. The supplied original-domain approximations give an ambient local
zero extension behind Sigma in the fixed triangulation, with exterior nodes
actually present and no hole-dependent interpolation constant. Assume this
precise boundary-chart/area-convergence interface; it holds at a regular
original arc in the already specified one-sided cell approximations. For
an internal Gamma bank it is NOT supplied by bounded random A.

Pair h_A with h_0+a, using the main joining to the zero bath. Put
  z_a=h_A-h_0-a, |z_a|<=2+|a|.
Its mean is EXACTLY m_n-a. At every common pin on Sigma the difference is
zero. Extend z_a by zero behind those true pins in the ambient window.
Free-free edge jumps are <=2, and a free-common-pin edge has the same bound
because both original constraints end at the same numerical value a.
No boundary coordinate is contracted into a transmission vertex.

The initial/path sparsity is exactly CS+FA; the reference is again a
translate of the ZERO law. A local cluster touching Sigma now has zero
input difference. Its projection derivative is a mixture of orthogonal
free-component averages and zero grounded blocks. It is positive,
sub-stochastic and contractive. The signed gap can be treated as two positive
parts, or its squared norm directly; its elementary losses are nonnegative.
Adding the extra initial level 0 gives the same displacement bound as (4).
The finite boundary-facet force calculation, already included in the
reviewed FP pin-incidence attachment, allocates the weighted square of the
adjacent gap to this loss. This is not a new CAP theorem at a random pin.

Stationarity of the uncentered bounded gap square and a cutoff in B1 give
  E sum_good free edges w |grad z_a|^2
   +E sum_good common-pin incidences w |z_a(x)|^2
   +E sum_safe C meeting B1 D_C <=C.
Other-pin clusters must travel distance c n and have an exponentially
small error, paid by the bounded gap and local cluster moments. There is
no artificial zero pin on the window's outer rim. This is the same signed
cutoff proof, with zero grounded blocks replacing free mass conservation;
mass conservation is unnecessary for this Sobolev acquisition.

Fill on fixed initial hulls and retain the full actual exterior zero nodes.
The wall/site weights pay rim paths meeting a retained common pin. Swallowed
pins are not proclaimed zero. Fixed hull and inverse-weight moment thresholds
give an affine ambient fill V_{a,n} with
  sup_n E ||V_{a,n}||_{W1p(B1)}^2 <=C, 1<p<2.
Refining only tests on the SAME joining makes the all-original-edge mean
gradient density tend to zero, including free-common-pin edges and zero
exterior edges. Finite-hole path telescoping and high hole moments therefore
give E||V_{a,n}-z_{a,n}^{ext}||_L1(B1)->0 for the original fixed holes.
This is a FULL ambient error, not just an error on a selected free test set.
All actual paths and interpolation nodes used in this assertion must be
in the stated boundary chart; this is precisely why that chart premise is
retained instead of assuming it for a rough bank.

After expectation and Rellich, the limit is the SAME function
  M_a=(m-a) on the physical component, and 0 behind Sigma,
in W1p(B1). The identification uses the full error and local convergence of
the regular exterior, not just an unrelated Sobolev sequence. In particular
it has zero Sobolev trace on Sigma. It supplies the local actual original-
boundary trace m=a. Taking a ball separated from Gamma and the original
arc-transition marks excludes any other incident label. This is a common-
pin local comparison theorem, not arbitrary rough-wall Dirichlet response.

5. WHAT REMAINS FOR A ROUGH GAMMA BANK

For a Gamma bank the actual pins are numerical A, which are bounded but
not a common constant a. The common-pin acquisition above cannot be invoked
for m-a there. To use the exact screened positive-part fill, keep its SAME
joining, ambient window, component selector, tested set and retained zero
pins. Its mean V_scr is W1p and dominates (c_*-m)_+ on the admitted tested
region; it is not m, and it is not harmonic. The main proof records this
inequality without asserting a global mean extension.

A theorem requiring the actual m to have one ambient W1p extension near
a rough bank therefore still needs either (i) a full signed common-wall
comparison to a reference whose mean is independently identified, with
ALL ambient errors and other-exit mass paid, or (ii) an alternative trace
theorem whose stated input is the dominated deficit V_scr rather than an
ambient extension of m. The screened free-test L1 statement alone supplies
neither. Any bank-wise application must exclude the other bank/accesses
with an explicit geometric screen or charged harmonic exit mass.

The interior PDE acquisition is complete relative to its exact ancestors.
The general same-ambient rough-bank mean-extension gate is explicitly OPEN.
It is unnecessary for the completed-component harmonic-measure RANGE bounds
in section 3, but remains substantive for stronger rough trace assertions.
