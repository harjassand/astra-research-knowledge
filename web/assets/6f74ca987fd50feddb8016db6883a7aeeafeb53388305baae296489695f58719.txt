CYCLE09 NONREVERSIBLE PROFILE: TARGETED PRIOR AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: exact tangent/sectorial coefficient comparison against a few primary
quantum Markov Dirichlet-form theorems. The newly frozen lower-profile
statement was read as authorized. Its proof was not audited. No sibling proof
or upper-phase family was read. No priority conclusion is made.

Candidate interface exposed for this check

For a finite faithful stationary state sigma, a finite-dimensional CPTP QMS
with positive Heisenberg generator L (not assumed KMS-symmetric), finite
1<p<infinity, q=p/(p-1), s=sqrt(sigma), and positive definite f, the exposed
claim is

  E_p^L(f) >= c_nr(p) E_2^L(I_{2,p}(f)),
  c_nr(p) = p sin(pi/p)/[2(p-1)(1+|cos(pi/p)|)].

The same pointwise inequality is asserted after tensoring with every finite
untouched reference at every faithful product stationary weight, for every
positive definite joint input. The source proof/status is not certified here.

Exact tangent-constant match, but not a QMS theorem

Put A_p for the sharp Lp operator norm of the periodic conjugate-function
(Hilbert-transform) operator in Pichorides's theorem. The 1972 source gives
A_p=tan(pi/(2p)) for 1<p<=2 and A_p=cot(pi/(2p)) for p>=2. Direct identities
give

  sin(pi/p)/(1+|cos(pi/p)|) = 1/A_p,
  c_nr(p) = p/[2(p-1) A_p].

This is an exact coefficient-level match to a classical harmonic-analysis
constant, not merely a numerical resemblance. Pichorides's theorem concerns
the scalar conjugate-function operator on ordinary Lp; it contains no
stationary state, quantum Markov generator, weighted power map I_{2,p},
physical Dirichlet form, positivity-preserving Choi constraint, or complete
reference extension. It therefore identifies a likely analytic mechanism
for the tangent factor but does not state the candidate generator inequality.
The direct interface still missing from this source is a transfer from this
scalar Hilbert-transform norm to the full-state quantum power/Dirichlet
comparison for arbitrary stationary CPTP generators.

Closest checked QMS comparisons

Kastoryano–Temme Definition 9 and Conjecture 19 give the low-p weak profile
E_p(f)>=E_2(I_{2,p}f) for 1<=p<=2 as a conjecture for all primitive
Liouvillians. Their proof does establish this coefficient-1 lower branch for
primitive unital (tracial) semigroups, without requiring reversibility,
through convexity of a Kraus-expanded trace functional. This is a stronger
bound in that unital overlap than c_nr(p), but it does not cover arbitrary
nontracial stationary weights. It is not a theorem for all primitive
nonreversible generators.

Beigi–Datta–Rouzé Theorem 14 gives the quantum Stroock–Varopoulos comparison
for positive inputs under strong sigma-reversibility (GNS symmetry). In their
form normalization, E_p^BDR=(p/2)E_p^KT; combining the theorem's exponent-2
case with its power duality yields the KT-normalized strong coefficient 2/p
on this narrower class. Rouzé's thesis Theorem 5.4.2 states the analogous
result under omega-DBC, equivalent there to KMS symmetry plus modular
covariance. These hypotheses exclude the candidate's arbitrary non-KMS
stationary generator. Their stronger coefficient cannot be transferred to
the general class without a theorem changing the symmetry premise.

The sectorial/weak-regularity search found no primary QMS theorem stating
the exact c_nr(p) comparison with arbitrary faithful stationary state and
complete finite references. The tangent match alone is not such a theorem;
standard sectorial or scalar Lp operator bounds do not supply the required
weighted-power generator interface. Exact prior status remains UNKNOWN in
this bounded search, not a priority claim.
