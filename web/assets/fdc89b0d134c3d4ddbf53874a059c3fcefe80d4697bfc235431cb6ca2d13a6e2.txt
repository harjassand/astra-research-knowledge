STRUCTURED COMMON DECODER FROM AN ACTUAL BROADCASTER
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: proved conditional theorem; it does not settle bounded-Holevo alone.

1. Interface

For each n let H_n be finite dimensional, E_n a family of density operators
on H_n, and B_n:C(H_n)->C(H_n tensor H_n) one supplied CPTP channel. Write
B_{n,1}=Tr_2 o B_n and assume

  b_n = sup_{rho in E_n} (1/2)||B_{n,1}(rho)-rho||_1 -> 0.

Only the first marginal is used below; the usual two-marginal error may be
used for b_n and is at least as strong. No product-state approximation or
independence of the two outputs is assumed.

Also supply, for each n:

  (a) a faithful reference state sigma_n on H_n;
  (b) a PVM P_{n,1},...,P_{n,r_n} with [P_{n,j},sigma_n]=0 and
      sup_n r_n <= r_* < infinity.

Put s_{n,j}=Tr(sigma_n P_{n,j})>0,

  tau_{n,j}=sigma_n P_{n,j}/s_{n,j},
  R_n(rho)=sum_j Tr(P_{n,j}rho) tau_{n,j}.

R_n is a CPTP measure-and-prepare channel from the full input algebra to
itself. The condition [P_j,sigma]=0 is a convenient sufficient alignment
condition; the theorem itself only needs specified states tau_j supported on
the mutually orthogonal P_j blocks.

Assume the two uniform entropy-radius conditions

  sup_{rho in E_n} D(rho||sigma_n) <= C,
  eta_n := sup_{rho in E_n} D(rho||R_n(rho)) -> 0,                 (1)

with natural logarithms. The first condition implies Holevo capacity
C(E_n)<=C, since for every finite prior pi,

  chi(pi,E_n)=sum_x pi_x D(rho_x||sigma_n)-D(rho_bar||sigma_n)
             <= sum_x pi_x D(rho_x||sigma_n) <= C.

The second condition is the additional structured promise. It is not inferred
from the first condition or from b_n.

2. The common POVM induced by the broadcaster

Define effects on the original input algebra by

  M_{n,j}=B_n^*(P_{n,j} tensor I).

Complete positivity and unitality of B_n^* give M_{n,j}>=0 and
sum_j M_{n,j}=I. Hence M_n is one POVM, chosen once for all rho in E_n.
Define the full-domain EB channel

  Lambda_n(X)=sum_j Tr(M_{n,j}X) tau_{n,j}.                       (2)

It is trace preserving and entanglement breaking by its explicit
measure-and-prepare form. Operationally it runs the supplied broadcaster,
measures the first receiver with the supplied PVM, discards both quantum
outputs, and prepares the reference-conditioned state tau_j. No unknown
state-dependent measurement or frame is used.

3. Exact trace-norm conversion

Fix rho in E_n. Write p_j=Tr(P_j rho), q_j=Tr(M_j rho). By definition of
the adjoint, q_j=Tr(P_j B_{n,1}(rho)). Measurement contractivity gives

  (1/2)sum_j |q_j-p_j|
     <= (1/2)||B_{n,1}(rho)-rho||_1 <= b_n.                    (3)

The prepared states tau_j have orthogonal supports P_j. Therefore the trace
distance between the two block mixtures is exactly their total variation:

  (1/2)||Lambda_n(rho)-R_n(rho)||_1
     = (1/2)||sum_j(q_j-p_j)tau_j||_1
     = (1/2)sum_j|q_j-p_j| <= b_n.                              (4)

Quantum Pinsker, with natural-log relative entropy and
T(rho,omega)=||rho-omega||_1/2, gives

  T(rho,R_n(rho)) <= sqrt(D(rho||R_n(rho))/2) <= sqrt(eta_n/2).  (5)

Combining (4)-(5), uniformly over the entire family,

  sup_{rho in E_n} T(Lambda_n(rho),rho)
       <= min{1, b_n+sqrt(eta_n/2)}.                            (6)

Thus e_EB(E_n) <= min{1,b_n+sqrt(eta_n/2)} -> 0. The numerical trace-norm
conversion is explicit and dimension-free. It is independent of r_n; the
uniform r_* bound is the additional apparatus/readout-complexity promise.

4. Relation to the supplied reference and Holevo bound

Under [P_j,sigma]=0, the reference has the block form
sigma=sum_j s_j tau_j, and the following relative-entropy Pythagorean
identity holds for every rho:

  D(rho||sigma)=D(rho||R(rho))+D(R(rho)||sigma).                (7)

To check it, write R(rho)=sum_j p_j tau_j and use, on block j,
log sigma=log s_j I_{P_j}+log tau_j and
log R(rho)=log p_j I_{P_j}+log tau_j; off-diagonal blocks of rho do not
contribute to traces against either logarithm. Zero-probability blocks
contribute zero by positivity/support. In particular eta_n<=C, but the
constant upper bound C is not a vanishing bound. The theorem requires the
remainder eta_n itself to vanish.

5. Bounded Holevo and small b do not imply the structured promise

This is a failure of the proposed inference eta_n->0, not a counterexample
to existence of some EB decoder. Let H=C^2, sigma=I/2,
rho_±=(I +/- a X)/2 for fixed 0<a<1, and supply the Z-basis PVM. Then

  R(rho_±)=I/2,
  D(rho_±||sigma)=chi({rho_+,rho_-},uniform)=c(a),
  c(a)=((1+a)ln(1+a)+(1-a)ln(1-a))/2>0.

The family has bounded Holevo capacity (indeed c(a)), yet
eta=c(a) stays positive. At the same time it has an exact broadcaster:

  B(X)=sum_{s=+,-} Tr(Q_s X) Q_s tensor Q_s,
  Q_s=(I+sX)/2,

whose two marginals fix rho_+ and rho_-. Thus b=0 does not make the
remainder to an unrelated supplied PVM small. The actual family is
commuting in the X basis and has a different exact EB decoder. This example
shows that the PVM/reference alignment promise carries real content; it does
not refute the unrestricted implication from bounded Holevo plus small
broadcast error to existence of some EB decoder.

More generally, D(rho||sigma)<=C only gives eta<=C through (7). Neither
Pinsker nor the common-POVM construction turns a fixed C into eta=o(1).
This route therefore cannot close the bounded-Holevo problem without an
additional theorem deriving small conditional relative entropy from
compatibility, or a supplied structure promise that directly certifies it.

6. Scope and costs

Proved: for every finite-dimensional actual broadcaster, every supplied
reference-conditioned orthogonal block family, and every experiment with
uniform conditional divergence eta, (2) is one common full-domain EB map and
(6) holds. The same proof works for any orthogonal block preparations tau_j,
with eta defined relative to rho -> sum_j Tr(P_j rho)tau_j.

The construction needs the supplied PVM and conditional preparations. If
given as matrices/channel data, its formal measurement effects are exactly
B^*(P_j tensor I). The proof counts one use of B and one r_n-outcome
measurement. It makes no claim that B or the PVM can be acquired or
implemented efficiently, and no claim that bounded Holevo capacity or
approximate compatibility alone supplies the PVM or makes eta small.

This is a structured positive result, not an unrestricted bounded-Holevo
classicalization theorem. It uses neither a rank-from-capacity assumption nor
a PBW/Gibbs estimate. External review and novelty/priority are not assessed.
