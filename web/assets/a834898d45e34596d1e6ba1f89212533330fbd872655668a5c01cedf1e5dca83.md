The selected learning contract deliberately supplies the correct prefix basis. Reset blocks are `(U,X,W)`, with fair U, uniform L-bit X, and `W=U XOR s·X XOR Bernoulli(eta)`. The unknown secret is s and `lambda=1−2eta>0`. This is an ordinary binary homogeneous HMM with `4L+4` states and a length `L+2` observation block; at the cut after U its predictive rank is two. The supplied prefix features are `f=(1,(-1)^U)`. The learner acquires arbitrary executable suffix functions `g_j(X,W)∈[-1,1]` and must obtain a true population margin `sigma_2(E[f g^T]) >= kappa`.

The exact derived result is:

- Any fixed M-function suffix bank can cover at most `floor[lambda²(M−kappa²)/kappa²]` secrets. At `kappa=lambda`, the exact maximum is `M−1`; a constant feature plus M−1 parity features attains it.
- If a randomized, arbitrary raw-data learner succeeds with probability at least `1−delta`, its stored output needs `B >= L−log₂K+log₂(1−delta)` bits, where K is the bank's coverage bound. At M=2 and kappa=lambda, K=1: the two-dimensional predictive basis still carries essentially L acquired parameter bits.
- Its expected number T of paid reset blocks obeys `E[T] C(lambda) >= (1−delta)(L−log₂K)−h₂(delta)`, with `C(lambda)=1−h₂((1−lambda)/2)`. This covers adaptive stopping with finite expectation. Physical symbol count is `(L+2)T`.
- A polynomially executable acquired bank with inverse-polynomial kappa permits recovery of s through heavy-Fourier extraction on the acquired functions, followed by paid raw validation. Thus efficient acquisition solves LPN. This reduction is proved; its computational lower-bound interpretation assumes LPN hardness.

The proof uses the common uniform suffix marginal: H has rows mu and lambda beta(s). Parseval bounds total beta energy by `M−||mu||²`, while a covered secret consumes `kappa²/lambda²` energy and the mean row consumes at least kappa². The memory and sample consequences follow by counting and an explicitly derived list entropy inequality. Full proofs and precision/evaluator costs are in `proof.md`.

Two constructive checks prevent overclaiming impossibility. Exhaustive integer-correlation search with a doubling confidence rule acquires the mask and a valid positive margin certificate without supplied lambda, using `O(lambda⁻²[L+ln(1/delta)+ln ln(e/lambda)])` raw blocks, but exponential `O(2^L T L)` work. A consensus-sampling extension of Gaussian elimination is polynomial for supplied rare-noise bounds `eta <= c ln L/L`; fixed nonzero noise still defeats its clean-batch route exponentially. Both are classical techniques. The equally informed retaining baseline performs the same parity search and can export the same basis; no superiority is established.
