STRONGEST CLAIM

A finite direct-sum instrument model gives a single explicit EB channel that preserves every classical center mode exactly, including modes whose Markov relaxation time diverges, while the associated corrected broadcaster marginal can remain non-entanglement-breaking on its quantum fibers.

Let H=C^r tensor C^q, with central projections P_z=|z><z| tensor I_q, and normalized trace tau=Tr/(rq). Supply a symmetric doubly-stochastic matrix P=(P_zw), and define rectangular-sector Kraus operators on H by

  K_wz=sqrt(P_zw) |w><z| tensor I_q.

Their instrument channel is L(A)=sum_wz K_wz A K_wz*, with effects M_wz=K_wz* K_wz=P_zw P_z. Row and column sums of P give sum K* K=sum K K*=I, so L is bistochastic. Symmetry of P makes L Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint. The canonical measurement-and-prepare channel for these effects has Heisenberg map

  Psi(A)=sum_wz tau(M_wz A) M_wz/tau(M_wz)=E_Z(A),
  E_Z(A)=sum_z Tr(A_z)/q P_z.

Thus Psi measures the sector and prepares its maximally mixed fiber. It is EB and fixes every center observable; its physical map fixes all states with a maximally mixed fiber, preserving their sector probabilities.

The actual instrument-append channel is

  B(rho)=sum_wz K_wz rho K_wz* tensor (P_z/q).

It is CPTP; its two marginals are L and Psi. Averaging B with its receiver swap gives common marginal C=(L+Psi)/2. Applying C on both outputs gives a genuine two-output channel with common marginal Phi=C*C=C^2 (here L and Psi are selfadjoint). Hence Phi is UCP, trace preserving, positive selfadjoint as an L2 operator, and selfcompatible. Expansion of the exact sum-of-squares identity gives

  2(I-Phi)-(I-Psi)
    =(I-L*L)+(Psi-Psi^2)+(L-Psi)*(L-Psi)/2 >= 0.

The first term is positive because L is an L2 contraction; the other terms are positive because Psi is an orthogonal projection. Therefore one EB map satisfies I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi), with no lower bound on the Markov spectral gap. This is a specialization of the internally supplied arbitrary-bistochastic-instrument identity in work/cycle4/detailed_balance_broadcast_attack.txt; that identity itself is not claimed as new here. The full-matrix version of that identity already covers these Kraus operators. The contribution here is the explicit sector-transition normal form and spectral calculation showing how its EB comparator preserves slow center modes while the corrected channel retains quantum fiber transmission.

MODE PRESERVATION AND A NON-EB QUANTUM FIBER

On a center eigenfunction f with P f=lambda f, Psi f=f, so

  Phi f=((1+lambda)/2)^2 f.

For the two-sector chain P_delta=[[1-delta,delta],[delta,1-delta]], the center-difference eigenvalue is (1-delta)^2. As delta tends to zero this is an arbitrarily slow nonconstant mode. For every delta>0 the exact fixed-point projection of Phi removes that mode, but Psi retains it with eigenvalue one. For rho_+=P_+/q and rho_-=P_-/q, Psi fixes both states exactly, while Phi moves their sector distributions by total-variation distance delta-delta^2/2. The fixed-point projection maps both to I/(2q), at half-trace distance 1/2 from each. Thus the channel-level EB rounding preserves precisely the slow classical information that the ergodic projection erases.

The corrected marginal need not itself be EB. Encode a q-dimensional state as E(rho)=I_r/r tensor rho and decode with D(X)=Tr_Z X. On this uniform-sector subspace, L acts as identity and Psi depolarizes the fiber, so

  D Phi E(rho)=rho/4+3 I_q/(4q),

the q-dimensional depolarizing channel with parameter 1/4. For q=4 its normalized Choi state has partial-transpose eigenvalue (1-1/4)/4^2-(1/4)/4=-1/64 on the antisymmetric subspace. It is not EB. If Phi were EB, the composition D Phi E would be EB, a contradiction. This gives a concrete finite-algebra family with a diverging classical relaxation time and surviving nonclassical fiber transmission, while a common EB comparator still preserves the slow center modes exactly.

AN INDEPENDENT REPRESENTATION BOUNDARY

The arbitrary-instrument construction cannot be promoted to all positive selfcompatible channels merely by asserting every such channel has a CPTP square-root factorization Phi=C*C. A two-dimensional Pauli channel supplies an exact obstruction. Take Bloch eigenvalues

  (lambda_x,lambda_y,lambda_z)=(9/16,1/2,1/16).

Its normalized Choi eigenvalues are (17/32,1/4,7/32,0); its output marginal is I/2, its purity is 201/512, and its determinant is zero. By Theorem 1 of Chen, Ji, Kribs, Lütkenhaus and Zeng (arXiv:1310.3530), a two-qubit state has a symmetric extension iff Tr(rho_B^2)>=Tr(rho_AB^2)-4 sqrt(det rho_AB). Here 1/2>=201/512, so the Choi state is extendible on the output qubit. The extension is the normalized Choi state of a CPTP two-output channel, proving Phi selfcompatible. Phi is also positive and Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint since all three Bloch eigenvalues are nonnegative.

Suppose Phi=C*C for a unital trace-preserving qubit channel C with Bloch matrix B. Then B^T B=diag(lambda_x,lambda_y,lambda_z), so the singular values of B are 3/4, 1/sqrt(2), 1/4. Any unital qubit CPTP map, after input/output unitary rotations and signed singular-value diagonalization, obeys s_1+s_2<=1+s_3 by positivity of its four Pauli Choi weights. Here that would require 3/4+1/sqrt(2)<=5/4, or 1/sqrt(2)<=1/2, which is false. Thus this positive selfcompatible channel has no CPTP C with Phi=C*C. It is not a counterexample to the desired EB theorem (finite-dimensional experiments can be rounded); it rules out one tempting universal factorization bridge.

COSTS, SCOPE, AND LIMITS

For r sectors and q-dimensional fibers, the instrument has r^2 Kraus matrices of dimension rq, and Psi has r measurement outcomes. The formulas require P and the sector decomposition as supplied data; acquisition of either is not charged or claimed. Direct application is finite and explicit; no optimized compilation or uniform-runtime claim is made. The exact theorem covers these center-moving instruments and their corrected marginals, not every positive selfcompatible channel. The qubit factorization obstruction is finite-dimensional and does not establish failure of any universal EB comparison or vanishing-modulus theorem.
