STRONGEST CONCRETE RESULT
An explicit static mixed-boundary experiment linearizes recovery of the first
Lamé modulus when the shear modulus is spatially constant. On the known unit
ball B in R^3, write a(x)=1/(lambda(x)+2 mu). For arbitrary smooth lambda with
mu>0 and 3 lambda+2 mu>0, the experiment produces exactly

    Q_a(w,h) = integral_S q_w h = integral_B a w h,

for harmonic scalar w,h. The controls depend on mu, geometry, and w, not on
lambda. Thus this is an acquired physical-response interface, not a supplied
matrix oracle. An unknown constant mu is acquired by one separate affine
shear calibration. With the promise a belongs to polynomials of degree <=2,
one further biased-affine pressure experiment w=2+x and ten explicitly listed
sensor projections recover all ten coefficients of a exactly. Its noisy
conditioning is substantial and explicitly charged. A complete construction,
proof, matrix specification, and falsifiers are in materials_pressure_proof.txt.

This is a concrete possible bench protocol for manufactured approximately
constant-shear samples. It does not recover two arbitrary varying moduli, make
soft-tissue models constant-shear, or establish a new practical device.
Novelty of this particular mixed-boundary transform has NOT been established.

TWO THEOREMS / ONE EXACT FALSIFIER
1. Mixed pressure construction. For harmonic w choose known v_w satisfying
   div v_w=0 and mu Delta v_w=-grad w. Let phi solve Delta phi=a w, phi|S=0.
   Then u=v_w+grad phi is an exact elastic equilibrium. On the unit sphere,
   prescribe its tangential displacement as (v_w)_t and prescribe

       traction_n + 4 mu u_n
       = (2 mu e(v_w)n)_n + w + 4 mu (v_w)_n.

   This well-posed mixed problem is positive in its weak energy form. Its
   solution has q_w=u_n-(v_w)_n=partial_n phi. Green's formula gives Q above.
   For a homogeneous harmonic w of degree ell>=1 one explicit v is

       v = ell x_vector w /[mu(2ell+3)(ell+1)]
           -(ell+3) r^2 grad w/[2mu(2ell+3)(ell+1)].

   For w=2+x, v=x_vector*x/(10mu)-r^2 e_x/(5mu). The boundary controls reduce to
   u_t=-(e_x-x n)/(5mu), traction_n+4mu u_n=2+x/5; output q=u_n+x/(10mu).
   Ten harmonic sensors are
   1,x,y,z,xy,xz,yz,x^2-y^2,x^2-z^2,x^3-(3/2)x(y^2+z^2).
   The moment matrix against the ten monomials of P_2 is invertible. Its exact
   determinant after removing pi from every row is 268435456/95761000986328125.
   After L2(S) sensor normalization the coefficient inverse norm is about
   986.44, condition number 2535.50, in this unnormalized monomial parameter
   basis. These values are representation-specific, not intrinsic bounds.

2. Full-DN depth instability. For the same constant-mu class, let
   a_+-a_-=2 beta chi(r)Y_N(omega), where chi is a fixed smooth radial bump
   supported in an annulus r<r0<1, |chi|<=1, and Y_N is a real degree-N
   spherical harmonic normalized to sup norm one. Positive background a0 and
   sufficiently small beta preserve the elasticity assumptions. The coefficient
   separation is order beta. The FULL DN operator difference satisfies

       ||Lambda_+-Lambda_-||_{H^(1/2)->H^(-1/2)}
       <= C_pressure^2 * 2 beta r0^(N+3)(N+1)^(3/2)
          sqrt(1+4r0^2+r0^4)/(1-r0^2)^2.

   C_pressure is the ordinary elliptic energy bound from normalized boundary
   displacement to L2(B) harmonic pressure (lambda+2mu)div u. It depends only
   on geometry and uniform coefficient bounds, not N or their derivatives;
   it was not numerically evaluated. Derivation: exact Alessandrini identity
   becomes integral (a_--a_+)w_+w_-, with both w harmonic. In an orthonormal
   harmonic Bergman basis, all matrix blocks ell+k<N vanish and the remaining
   blocks decay as r0^(ell+k). Summing their squared entries gives the formula.
   With beta=N^(-m), coefficients have bounded C^m norm and this yields a
   logarithmic lower modulus ~[log(1/delta)]^(-m), precluding any fixed Holder
   inverse stability on that class. For adaptive scalar DN probes with unit
   input/output norms and independent Gaussian noise sigma^2, chain-rule KL
   yields a probe lower bound K>=sigma^2/delta_N^2 to attain Bayes error <=1/4.
   This charges total normalized sensing energy/probe count, not merely solve
   arithmetic. It is a lower bound in the stated scalar-noise interface.

3. Falsifier: the ONE biased-affine load cannot recover even all cubic a.
   Put r^2=x^2+y^2+z^2 and

       psi=(40x^2+165x-2y^2-2z^2+164)/40,
       b=3[126x^3+133x^2+126xy^2+126xz^2-98x
             +133y^2+133z^2-79]/10.

   Exactly (2+x)b=Delta[(r^2-1)^2 psi]. Therefore perturbing a by a small
   multiple of b changes phi by (r^2-1)^2 psi, whose boundary value AND
   normal derivative vanish. The entire measured normal-displacement field
   for that load is identical, not just its ten sampled projections. This
   sharply limits the one-load positive result and exposes the polynomial
   degree promise. Four degree-one loads with degree-one sensors have another
   exact blind pair: a_±=1/3 ±(x^3-3xy^2)/10 (mu=1), all ten data equal,
   while ||lambda_+-lambda_-||_infinity=180/91.

FALSIFIABLE CONSEQUENCE / NEXT DECISIVE QUESTION
Manufacture or simulate a unit-ball constant-shear phantom with reciprocal
longitudinal modulus a in P_2. A separate affine shear load followed by the
specified w=2+x mixed load should recover all ten a coefficients through the
fixed moment matrix, with no nonlinear coefficient search. Failure of the PDE
identity in a converged independent FEM model falsifies this construction.
Adding the explicit cubic b should leave the entire q response unchanged;
other loads should distinguish it. This provides an unusually decisive paired
positive/negative test of the control interface.

The next useful task is an independent FEM implementation including finite
boundary actuators, a noise budget, imperfect mu calibration, and comparison
with an equally informed nonlinear inversion under the same two loads. Before
any novelty or broad relevance claim, inspect classical constant-shear elasticity
reconstruction / harmonic Toeplitz / mixed-boundary inverse-source prior art.
For the release-dependent general case, the one load-bearing research question
is stable acquisition of finite physical transport frames from finite boundary
measurements at quantified cost. This cycle supplies no solution to that gate.
