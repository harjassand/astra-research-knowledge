Banked result
-------------
For the fixed intrinsic local filter

  Z_k(A)=sum_{|I|=|J|=k, I∩J=empty}|det A[I,J]|^2,

an internally proved one-query metric reduction maps general-graph perfect-matching counts to the exact half-filled target on polynomial-bit real rational matrices. Denominator-cleared Z is in #P. An FPRAS for Z would imply an FPRAS for general-graph perfect matchings, whose status remains open in the cited source. This is not an inapproximability theorem. The exact reduction, error bound, chart condition estimate, sample-law TV bound, self-reduction, and physical-cost boundary are in RESULT.txt and the full independent derivation in ../../sol_fermionic_filter/RESULT.txt. Exact finite replay passes four graphs and 1,740 selected-pair equalities.

Highest-value next mathematical attacks
---------------------------------------
1. Independently formalize and review the graph-to-chart proof, especially the common-row determinant factor, functional-map nonsingularity, one-query rounding bound, and integer scaling. Recheck the proof after restricting entries to contractive matrices; the known scalar rescaling preserves the target law but can worsen physical success, and does not bound condition number.
2. Seek a polynomial-time approximation or exact sampler for arbitrary rank. The actionable missing ingredient is a positive, polynomial-size representation of the actual pair selector whose evaluation/sampling cost is polynomial and whose conditioning can be bounded; the direct coefficient and paired-Gram Chen–Liu factors both have exact log-concavity counterexamples.
3. Seek an AP reduction from a source with a stronger known approximation barrier to this same fixed filter. The graph-perfect-matching reduction alone reaches an open problem and cannot justify an FPRAS impossibility claim.
4. Improve tractable structure: quantify transfer algorithms for treewidth/bandwidth or bounded interaction blocks, with polynomial bit, exact-zero, sampler, and physical retry costs. Keep N89 monomial/cycle and the rank-d fourfold exterior DP as separate baselines.
5. Keep the general BCS/Pfaffian phase problem separate from nonnegative spin occupation counting. Existing Pfaffian expectation estimators, Gaussian branches, and quartic-loss completion do not currently give a general filtered sampler.

Do not repeat
-------------
The exchange/support barriers do not imply a weighted-counting lower bound. Direct and paired-Gram Chen–Liu LC failures only rule out those lifts. Matchgate tensor failures rule out direct FKT contraction, not all planar algorithms. Exact #P-hardness alone does not exclude an FPRAS. Physical retry energy, numerical chart conditioning, and classical bit complexity are different costs.
