CYCLE09 SINE-PROFILE PRIOR AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: bounded targeted primary-source comparison. I read the explicitly permitted origin baseline for the candidate statement and did not inspect any other Cycle09 proof.

Candidate interface being checked

The exposed candidate states that for every finite-dimensional KMS-symmetric QMS with faithful stationary state, every 1<p<infinity, and positive definite f,

  E_p(f) >= c_p E_2(I_{2,p} f),
  c_p = p sin(pi/p) / (2(p-1)),

with the same constant after tensoring with every finite untouched quantum reference and with sharpness among primitive examples. The candidate further has a signed/polar extension to complex observables. This audit checks prior-source fit only; it does not validate the candidate proof or its sharpness.

Finding

The bounded primary-source search did not identify a theorem with this exact sine coefficient and the full arbitrary-KMS, common-reference hypotheses. The nearest general KMS results establish hypercontractivity, p-log-Sobolev inequalities, or MLSI; those are norm/entropy estimates and do not state the pointwise comparison E_p(f) >= c_p E_2(I_{2,p}f). The best direct p-Dirichlet comparison checked is Stroock–Varopoulos/strong regularity under GNS-strengthened detailed balance. Thus exact prior status is UNKNOWN, not a priority claim.

Kastoryano–Temme (2013) already conjecture the relevant low-p weak profile for every primitive Liouvillian. In Definition 9, Eq. (31), the printed low branch is E_p(f) >= E_2(I_{2,p}f) for 1<=p<=2. Their Conjecture 19 says every primitive Liouvillian is weakly Lp-regular, with strong regularity additionally for reversible generators. This is a conjecture, not a theorem, and its displayed high-p weak coefficient is literally p-1. The arXiv v2 and journal PDF agree on that coefficient; the separate frozen Cycle09 memo records the elementary positive-input falsifier and the KMS-dual reciprocal coefficient 1/(p-1). Do not silently amend the source.

For 1<p<=2, c_p >= 1, so the candidate low branch would imply KT's weak low branch. For p>=2, c_p >= 1/(p-1), the coherent KMS-dual weak threshold. The sine profile is therefore a quantitative strengthening of the conjectured weak branch, while it is a different claim from proving the conjecture by generic semigroup hypercontractivity.

The direct Stroock–Varopoulos prior is narrower. Beigi–Datta–Rouzé, Theorem 14, prove the power/Dirichlet monotonicity for positive inputs under “strongly sigma-reversible” symmetry, which is GNS symmetry. Their form differs from KT's by a factor p/2 and a sign; after applying their Proposition 8 power duality and converting conventions, Theorem 14 gives the strong 2/p profile in KT normalization. Rouzé’s thesis, Theorem 5.4.2, states the result under Theorem 5.2.6(b), namely omega-DBC. In the thesis, Lemma 5.2.5(i) identifies omega-DBC with KMS symmetry plus modular covariance; part (ii) gives the converse under the modular group. Hence this prior covers the GNS-strengthened subclass, not arbitrary KMS maps. The coefficient 2/p is stronger than the candidate sine constant on that subclass, but does not establish the proposed all-KMS sharp profile.

Recent KMS results do not close this interface. Liu–Wan–Wu, Theorem 3.1, prove that every primitive finite-dimensional KMS-symmetric QMS has an MLSI. Wan–Wu, Theorem 7.2, prove weighted Lp-to-Lq hypercontractivity for primitive KMS-symmetric QMS for 1<p<=q<infinity, with a time bound involving log(lambda_min(sigma)^(-1)) and the KMS spectral gap; Theorem 7.3 derives p-log-Sobolev constants. These are genuine all-KMS norm/entropy results, but neither theorem compares the two pointwise Dirichlet forms in the candidate. Wu’s 2026 reverse-hypercontractivity paper concerns p<1; it does not settle the candidate's p>1 comparison.

There is also a sharp stationary-weight noncommutative Lp theorem that is close in vocabulary but not in interface. Xu–Zhang, Theorem 1.1, prove the optimal tangent inequality for Haagerup Lp over a faithful normal semifinite weight, for all complex x,y: the coefficient is p-1 for 1<p<=2, with the reversed inequality for p>=2. This is Banach-space norm geometry (a tangent/uniform-convexity estimate), not a QMS Dirichlet-form comparison and not an inequality for the KMS power maps I_{p,q}. It cannot be substituted for the missing generator comparison.

Domain and reference cautions

KT define their weighted power maps on Hermitian inputs using an absolute value. Their p=1 logarithmic expression is directly meaningful on positive definite inputs; no sign-indefinite p=1 extension is inferred here. The GNS Stroock–Varopoulos theorems are stated for positive inputs. Xu–Zhang's tangent theorem permits general complex Haagerup-Lp vectors, but its different conclusion does not supply the candidate's signed/polar semigroup inequality. No checked prior source states the candidate's arbitrary finite quantum-reference version of this pointwise profile.

Historical status

The exact sine coefficient and full KMS/reference interface remain UNKNOWN in this bounded audit. KT's low-p weak regularity is historically identified as a conjecture. The GNS theorem is stronger only after adding modular covariance. Recent primitive-KMS MLSI/hypercontractivity results use different conclusions and hypotheses. Search non-discovery is not a novelty certificate; no priority conclusion is made.
