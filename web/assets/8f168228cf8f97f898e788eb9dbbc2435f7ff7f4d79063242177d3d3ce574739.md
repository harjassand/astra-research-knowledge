# Classwise stochastic recurrence fails for endotactic networks

## Verdict

The proposed claim is false for finite, time-homogeneous integer stochastic mass-action diagrams with fixed positive rates, even on a closed irreducible class and even for a **strongly endotactic** diagram. A published four-reaction example has a closed irreducible class on which explosion occurs with positive probability from every state. This directly refutes classwise nonexplosion and therefore classwise positive recurrence in the full endotactic class. It is separate from N77: N77 has positive-recurrent components and refutes only uniform tightness across components.

The counterexample is known prior work, not a new discovery. I independently reconstructed its communicating-class boundary and the infinite-product/time-change argument, and added an exact finite-cap risk calculation. The route, proof, source audit, and limitations are below. No novelty or priority claim is made for the mathematical counterexample or the finite arithmetic extension.

## Exact model and class

Use species (A,B), the reaction diagram

\[
0\xrightarrow{r_0}2A\xrightarrow{r_1}4A+B
\xrightarrow{r_2}6A+4B\xrightarrow{r_3}3A,
\]

and arbitrary fixed constants \(\kappa_0,\kappa_1,\kappa_2,\kappa_3>0\). The state is a count vector \(x=(a,b)\in\mathbb N_0^2\). Under the falling-factorial stochastic mass-action convention,

\[
\lambda_0(a,b)=\kappa_0,\quad
\lambda_1(a,b)=\kappa_1(a)_2,\quad
\lambda_2(a,b)=\kappa_2(a)_4(b)_1,\quad
\lambda_3(a,b)=\kappa_3(a)_6(b)_4,
\]

with a propensity zero when its source is unavailable and \((q)_j=q(q-1)\cdots(q-j+1)\). The rates do not depend on time. The third reaction has molecularity ten; this is part of the model contract, not a hidden scaling approximation.

Let

\[
\Gamma=\{(a,b)\in\mathbb N_0^2:a\ge3\}.
\]

This is a **closed irreducible communicating class**, not merely an escaping path in a transient component.

Closure follows reaction by reaction. The first three reactions increase $A$; the final reaction is enabled only when $a\ge6$ and maps $a$ to $a-3\ge3$. Thus no jump leaves \(\Gamma\).

For irreducibility, write the stoichiometric increments of $r_1,r_2,r_3$ as

\[
v_1=(2,1),\qquad v_2=(2,3),\qquad v_3=(-3,-4).
\]

Two enabled finite macros connect the entire class:

- The pump \(r_1,r_2,r_3\) has net increment \((1,0)\). From any \((a,b)\in\Gamma\), its three reactions are enabled in order, so it increases $a$ by one while preserving $b$.
- The decrement macro $r_1$, nine copies of $r_2$, and seven copies of $r_3$ has net increment $(-1,0)$. It is enabled from every $(a,b)$ with $a\ge4$: after $r_1$ and the nine $r_2$'s the state is $(a+20,b+28)$. Before the $j$-th $r_3$, for $j=1,\dots,7$, the counts are $(a+20-3(j-1),b+28-4(j-1))$, whose $A$-coordinate is at least $a+2\ge6$ and whose $B$-coordinate is at least $b+4\ge4$. It ends at $(a-1,b)$.

From $(3,0)$, first $b$ copies of $r_1$ reach $(3+2b,b)$. Apply the decrement macro until the desired $A$-count if $a\le3+2b$; if $a>3+2b$, pump at $B=0$ to $(a-2b,0)$ and then apply $r_1$ $b$ times. Hence every $(a,b)\in\Gamma$ is reachable from $(3,0)$.

Conversely, from any $(a,b)\in\Gamma$, let $s\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ satisfy $b+s\equiv0\pmod4$, and apply $r_1$ $s$ times. Then pump enough times to ensure the final reaction can clear all $t=(b+s)/4$ groups of four $B$'s: it suffices to make $A\ge3t+3$. Apply $r_3$ $t$ times, leaving $(m,0)$ with $m\ge3$, and then use the decrement macro $m-3$ times. This reaches $(3,0)$. Therefore every two states of \(\Gamma\) communicate.

The exact macro vectors and a rational directional sweep for strong endotacticity are checked in [check_endotactic_explosion.py](check_endotactic_explosion.py). The sweep checks all open cells and boundary rays formed by source-order and reaction-sign changes; it is a finite diagnostic for this diagram. The primary paper independently identifies this same network as strongly endotactic.

## Positive-probability explosion proof

At a state $(n,0)$, with $n\ge3$, prescribe the next three reactions to be $r_1,r_2,r_3$. They visit

\[
(n,0)\to(n+2,1)\to(n+4,4)\to(n+1,0),
\]

so each completed cycle raises $A$ by one. The exact probabilities of the prescribed next reaction at the three states are

\[
p_{1,n}=\frac{\kappa_1(n)_2}{\kappa_1(n)_2+\kappa_0},
\]

\[
p_{2,n}=\frac{\kappa_2(n+2)_4}
{\kappa_2(n+2)_4+\kappa_1(n+2)_2+\kappa_0},
\]

\[
p_{3,n}=\frac{24\kappa_3(n+4)_6}
{24\kappa_3(n+4)_6+4\kappa_2(n+4)_4+\kappa_1(n+4)_2+\kappa_0}.
\]

The factor 24 is \((4)_4\); the factor 4 is the current $B$-count multiplying the $4A+B$ source. For each fixed positive rate vector, all three failure probabilities are $O(n^{-2})$: the desired rates grow respectively as $(n^2,n^4,n^6)$, while the largest competing orders are $(1,n^2,n^4)$. Therefore

\[
P_\infty=\prod_{n=3}^\infty p_{1,n}p_{2,n}p_{3,n}>0,
\]

since every factor is positive and the sum of the factor deficits is finite.

Conditional on selecting a specified next channel in a Gillespie race, the waiting time remains exponential with total rate at that state; its mean is at most the reciprocal of the prescribed channel's rate. Thus the expected total time along the prescribed infinite sequence, conditional on all its reaction choices, is at most

\[
\sum_{n=3}^\infty\left[
\frac{1}{\kappa_1(n)_2}
+\frac{1}{\kappa_2(n+2)_4}
+\frac{1}{24\kappa_3(n+4)_6}
\right]<\infty.
\]

By monotone convergence, the conditional sum of holding times is finite almost surely. On an event of probability $P_\infty>0$, infinitely many reactions therefore occur in finite physical time. The minimal CTMC explodes with positive probability from $(3,0)$. Irreducibility gives every state in \(\Gamma\) a finite positive-probability path to $(3,0)$, so explosion has positive probability from every state in this closed class. In particular, the proposed all-endotactic classwise nonexplosion theorem fails. The minimal process on \(\Gamma\) also cannot have an invariant probability on chemical states: positive mass at any state would be lost by some finite time with positive probability, contradicting stationarity of total mass.

## Exact finite calculation and a finite-resource model

For unit rates and unit volume, the prescribed cycle probabilities are the exact rationals

\[
p_{1,n}=\frac{(n)_2}{(n)_2+1},\quad
p_{2,n}=\frac{(n+2)_4}{(n+2)_4+(n+2)_2+1},\quad
p_{3,n}=\frac{24(n+4)_6}{24(n+4)_6+4(n+4)_4+(n+4)_2+1}.
\]

The exact product through the 97 cycle levels $n=3,\dots,99$ (291 channel-choice factors) is 0.354443456804777… . For the remaining infinite tail from $n=100$, let $q_{i,n}=(1-p_{i,n})/p_{i,n}$, the competitor-to-target rate ratio. Direct telescoping gives the exact sum

\[
\sum_{n=100}^\infty\sum_{i=1}^3q_{i,n}
=\frac{27506776741}{1260593928000}<1.
\]

Because $p=(1+q)^{-1}\ge e^{-q}$ and $e^{-q}\ge1-q$ after taking the product as \(e^{-\sum q}\), the full infinite-success probability is at least

\[
P_\infty\ge P_{100}\left(1-\frac{27506776741}{1260593928000}\right)
>0.346709327029.
\]

The last displayed decimal is backed by the exact rational numerator and denominator in [CHECK_OUTPUT.json](CHECK_OUTPUT.json). The checker also verifies the strong-endotactic directional conditions on 28 exact rational directions and checks one standard-volume rational propensity fixture.

An ideal finite-resource variant blocks every reaction whose product would leave
\[
D_M=\{(a,b)\in\mathbb N_0^2:a+b\le M\}.
\]

This is a finite-state CTMC and is nonexplosive; every one of its closed communicating classes is positive recurrent. It changes the generator at the capacity boundary; inside the cap it uses the same propensities. Along a prescribed pump cycle starting at $(n,0)$, the maximum total count is $n+8$. Hence when $M\ge N+7$, the uncapped route to $(N,0)$ is intact. For unit rates and $M\ge107$, choose $N=M-7\ge100$. The conditional expected time to reach that level along the route is at most

\[
S=\sum_{n=3}^\infty\left[\frac1{(n)_2}+\frac1{(n+2)_4}+\frac1{24(n+4)_6}\right]
=\frac{44401}{86400}\approx0.513900463.
\]

Markov's inequality at $T=2S=44401/43200\approx1.027800926$, combined with the rational lower bound on $P_\infty$, gives

\[
\Pr\{\text{hit }(M-7,0)\text{ by }T\}\ge0.1733546635.
\]

This is a rigorous one-sided prediction for the specified hard-cap model, not a claim about an actual chemical system. It is also a usable finite stopping test: for a simulated or engineered capped process at these rates, a hit frequency significantly below the bound falsifies at least one of the prescribed generator, time-homogeneity, or boundary-blocking assumptions. The route count is a lower bound on total threshold hitting; other routes can raise the observed probability.

## Volume scaling and physical boundary

Under the conventional density-dependent propensities

\[
\lambda_r^V(z)=\kappa_r V^{1-|y_r|}z_{\underline{y_r}},
\]

the same communicating class and infinite successful path exist at **each fixed finite** $V>0$. The cycle factors are obtained by replacing the stage rates by

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{state}&\text{enabled rates along the route}\\\hline
(n,0)&\kappa_0V,\;\kappa_1(n)_2/V\\
(n+2,1)&\kappa_0V,\;\kappa_1(n+2)_2/V,\;\kappa_2(n+2)_4/V^4\\
(n+4,4)&\kappa_0V,\;\kappa_1(n+4)_2/V,\;4\kappa_2(n+4)_4/V^4,\;24\kappa_3(n+4)_6/V^9.
\end{array}
\]

At fixed $V$, their high-$n$ orders still force cycle failure probabilities $O(n^{-2})$ and make the conditional-time series finite, with explicit bound

\[
S_V=\frac{V}{2\kappa_1}+\frac{V^4}{72\kappa_2}+\frac{V^9}{86400\kappa_3}.
\]

But the explosion argument is **not uniform in a density limit**. For fixed $n=3$ and fixed rate constants, the first prescribed cycle has probability \(\Theta(V^{-17})\) as $V\to\infty$: its three desired-channel probabilities scale as $(V^{-2},V^{-5},V^{-10})$. The selected runaway route therefore becomes rare from this fixed-count seed as volume grows; this does not bound the total explosion probability, which could use other paths. The levels at which successive desired channels dominate their immediate competitors scale as $n\gg V$, $n\gg V^{3/2}$, and $n\gg V^{5/2}$. At the final stage that means concentration $A/V\gg V^{3/2}$, outside any fixed-concentration regime. Finite-volume explosion and a nonexplosive fixed-time fluid limit are compatible.

The ideal network has two obvious physical debts. Its 10-molecule source (6A+4B) is an elementary event of implausibly high molecularity for ordinary chemistry, and the net cycle creates one (A) without any explicit fuel species. A real implementation would need staged chemistry or external feed; that changes the generator and requires a fresh endotacticity and recurrence analysis. Finite substrate, finite packing capacity, crowding-limited propensities, or explicit fuel depletion are plausible regularizers, but their effects are distinct models. None was measured here.

For a broader sufficient nonexplosion condition, Xu's primary theorem says that if some positive vector $u$ exists such that every reaction with $u\cdot(y'-y)>0$ has propensity bounded by $C\|x\|_1$, then the CTMC is nonexplosive. For stochastic mass action this allows high-order reactions when they do not drive a positive linear mass coordinate upward; it does not by itself imply positive recurrence. A stronger sufficient positive-recurrence subclass is Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen's theorem with added high-order drains: each species has a nonempty set of reactions $aS_i\to\sum_jr'_jS_j$ with $a\ge m-1$, \(\sum_jr'_j\le a-1\), plus the paper's strict source/product molecularity separation; permitted lower-order additions have source order at most $m-2$. Their Foster–Lyapunov proof yields positive recurrence in every closed irreducible component. Fully open binary strongly endotactic networks are one corollary. These are useful known sufficient subclasses, not a necessary-and-sufficient or maximality theorem.

The precise gap in the proposed all-endotactic implication is physical-time control. Endotacticity orders which source complexes are extreme in each direction, but it does not preclude a reachable route whose desired-channel probabilities have a positive infinite product while inverse desired rates are summable. To establish nonexplosion one needs an additional clock-growth control (for example, a linear Lyapunov bound on the generator); positive recurrence needs a classwise proper Lyapunov function with negative drift outside a finite set, or another theorem that supplies finite expected return. Deterministic N33 absorption and finite-horizon N35 safety do not supply either stochastic condition.
