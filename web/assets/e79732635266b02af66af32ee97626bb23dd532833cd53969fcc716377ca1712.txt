Cycle 2 leaf: weighted physical-time tier and cycle audit
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the exact family below; UNKNOWN for a general higher-dimensional WR criterion or novelty.

Result

For every integer m >= 3 there is a three-species, full-stoichiometric-rank, weakly reversible, non-complex-balanced stochastic mass-action network with a common top source tier whose molecule-count increments include negative, zero, and positive values. Its total-molecule-count generator is positive at an infinite sequence of states, so a direct scalar Foster argument using that count fails. Nevertheless, this particular CTMC is nonexplosive and positive recurrent. The reason is an exact projection plus a time-changed immigration-death cycle: the AB projection has a coercive Foster function, and conditional on the AB path the C coordinate is an immigration-death process in operational time. The physical-time invariant law of the C cycle is Poisson(m), while the embedded jump chain has a different, rate-size-biased law.

This is a structured example and proof, not a solution of the general weakly reversible recurrence problem. It is a direct stochastic-generator/time-change proof; no deterministic permanence theorem is used.

Network and exact propensities

Let species be A,B,C and abbreviate M=mB, U=A+(m-1)B, V=mB+C. Use standard stochastic mass action with falling factorials (n)_r=n(n-1)...(n-r+1), zero when n<r. Reactions, rate constants, and propensities are:

  reaction                         k       propensity
  0 -> A                           1       1
  A -> 0                           1       A
  A -> B                           1       A
  B -> mB                          2       2B
  M -> A                           1       (B)_m
  M -> U                           1       (B)_m
  U -> M                           1       A(B)_(m-1)
  M -> V                           m       m(B)_m
  V -> M                           1       C(B)_m

There are six complexes {0,A,B,M,U,V}. The graph is strongly connected: 0->A->B->M, M->A->0, and M is joined in both directions to U and V. The stoichiometric rank is 3, since 0->A, A->B, and M->V span e_A, -e_A+e_B, and e_C. Thus the deficiency is 6-1-3=2. The network is not complex balanced: balance at 0 forces c_A=1, and balance at B forces 2c_B=c_A, hence c_B=1/2. Balance at U would require c_M=c_U, but c_M=c_B^m=2^-m whereas c_U=c_A c_B^(m-1)=2^(-(m-1)). These differ.

Top-tier mass drift

Use w=(1,1,0) to rank source complexes and ell=(1,1,1) to count total molecules. The maximal w-source value is m, attained at M,U,V. The top-tier arrows and their ell-mass increments are

  M -> A       1-m       (negative)
  M -> U       0         (zero)
  U -> M       0         (zero)
  M -> V       +1        (positive)
  V -> M       -1        (negative).

Writing q(B)=(B)_m, their rate-weighted mass drift at state (A,B,C) is

  -(m-1)q + 0*q + 0*A(B)_(m-1) + m q - C q = (1-C)q.

Thus weak reversibility does not make this pointwise tier sum negative: it is positive at C=0, zero at C=1, and negative for C>=2. The same top tier contains zero-mass arrows, and its source reactions have mixed total-molecule signs. At (A,B,C)=(0,n,0), total mass W=A+B+C has

  L W = 1 + 2(m-1)n + (n)_m > 0.

So W itself has no pointwise negative-drift Foster inequality outside a finite set. The rate weighting and the physical-time distribution of C are load-bearing.

Exact projected Foster inequality

Let H(A,B)=A+B. The AB-coordinate process is autonomous: the C birth/death pair changes neither A nor B and its propensities do not affect any AB reaction rate. Its generator gives

  L_AB H = 1 - A + 2(m-1)B - (m-1)(B)_m = -A + g_m(B),
  g_m(b) = 1 + 2(m-1)b - (m-1)(b)_m.

Since g_m(b)->-infinity, choose an integer b_* so g_m(b)<=-1 for all b>=b_*. Let G=max_{0<=b<b_*} g_m(b) and a_*=ceil(G+1). Outside the finite rectangle F={(a,b):a<a_*, b<b_*}, either b>=b_* and L_AB H<=-1, or b<b_* and a>=a_* and L_AB H<=-1. This is a standard irreducible CTMC Foster condition.

For nonexplosion, L_AB H<=1+2(m-1)B<=1+2(m-1)H. All rates on each finite H-level set are finite, and every AB jump has finite size. Stopped Dynkin/Gronwall on tau_N=inf{t:H_t>=N} bounds E[1+H_(t^intersect tau_N)] by exp(c t)(1+H_0), so P(tau_N<=t)->0. Explosion while H stays bounded is impossible because it would require infinitely many jumps in a finite set with bounded total rate. Thus AB is nonexplosive. The AB chain is irreducible: for b>=1, the two-step path B->mB followed by mB->A reduces B by one and increases A by one; repeat to B=0 and drain A by A->0. From the origin, immigrate A and convert the desired number to B. Every requested edge has positive rate. The Foster criterion therefore gives an invariant probability mu_AB and positive recurrence of AB.

Physical-time cycle corrector and full recurrence

At fixed AB state, the C-only generator is q(B)Q, where

  Qf(c)=m[f(c+1)-f(c)] + c[f(c-1)-f(c)].

For the corrector phi(c)=c, Qphi=m-c. The invariant distribution of Q in physical time is rho(c)=exp(-m)m^c/c!, and E_rho[Qphi]=m-E_rho[C]=0. Consequently, for W=H+C,

  L W = L_AB H + q(B)(m-C),
  sum_c rho(c) L W(a,b,c) = L_AB H(a,b).

The conditional physical-time average is at most -1 outside F. This is an exact rate-weighted cycle identity, not an approximation: the C birth/death rates share the factor q(B), and their time change preserves the Poisson(m) law. However, the displayed averaged drift is not a pointwise Foster drift on the full state space and is not used alone as a recurrence theorem.

For the full chain, construct AB as its autonomous CTMC, and set

  tau(t)=integral_0^t (B_s)_m ds,    C_t=Z_(tau(t)),

where Z is an independent immigration-death chain with immigration m and per-capita death 1. This gives exactly the two C propensities m(B)_m and C(B)_m. If AB_0 has law mu_AB and C_0 is independent with law rho, then conditional on the AB path, Z at the deterministic operational time tau(t) still has law rho. Hence mu_AB tensor rho is invariant for the full chain.

The full chain is nonexplosive. On every finite physical interval the nonexplosive AB path has finitely many jumps, so tau(T)<infinity; on a finite operational interval Z has finitely many immigration events and at most C_0 plus that many deaths. It is irreducible: AB can reach any pair while holding C fixed; at B>=m, C can be raised or lowered to any target; then AB can be moved to the target pair while suppressing the finitely many unwanted C jumps, an event of positive probability. Thus the full chain is an irreducible, nonexplosive CTMC with an invariant probability, and is positive recurrent.

Embedded-chain versus CTMC clock

When q=(B)_m>0 is frozen, the C CTMC has total rate q(m+c). Its embedded jump probabilities are

  p(c,c+1)=m/(m+c),    p(c,c-1)=c/(m+c).

If rho is the CTMC stationary occupancy law, the jump-chain invariant law is the rate-size-biased distribution

  rho_tilde(c) = rho(c)(m+c)/(2m)
               = (rho(c)+rho(c-1))/2,

with rho(-1)=0 and mean m+1/2. The CTMC occupancy law has mean m. Holding-time de-biasing recovers it exactly:

  rho(c) proportional to rho_tilde(c) / [q(m+c)].

Therefore one cannot insert the embedded-chain mean m+1/2 into the physical-time generator drift q(m-C); doing so gives a spurious -q/2. The physical-time stationary average is q(m-E_rho C)=0 for the C cycle. Equivalently, the embedded chain has zero average increment per jump, but physical-time occupation requires holding-time weighting. When B<m, q=0 and C is paused; the time-change representation includes these pauses.

Prior-art and scope audit

The author-hosted ConStRAINeD publications page calls the two-dimensional chemical recurrence conjecture proved for every weakly reversible network with two species, says the arbitrary-dimensional conjecture remains open, and lists the full paper as “In preparation”: https://constrained.polito.it/publications/ . The author-hosted Agazzi talk is titled “Solving the chemical recurrence conjecture in 2d,” dated 25 April 2022; its theorem slide is written in generic notation, but the talk's closing slide explicitly lists higher-dimensional proof questions: https://github.com/agazzian/talks/blob/gh-pages/UI_conjecture.html . In this context “dimension two” is species/state dimension d=2, not stoichiometric rank two. Do not claim any two-species or rank-two theorem as new. No full proof manuscript was available in the checked author pages, so the two-dimensional proof status is recorded as author/project-reported, not independently audited here.

An established narrower baseline is the 2020 single-linkage-class weakly reversible result for binary systems with a pure multiple complex for each species; this candidate is one linkage class but is nonbinary for m>=3. Reference: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-applied-probability/article/abs/stochastically-modeled-weakly-reversible-reaction-networks-with-a-single-linkage-class/5E9F7DAE999525F8D4A3392F80B3F0C9 . The standard complex-balanced product-form theorem is also prior art (Anderson, Craciun, Kurtz, 2008 preprint / 2010 journal: https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3042), but this family is not complex balanced. The cycle argument here relies on the exact common modulation q(B), so it is a structured case and not an extension to arbitrary WR networks. It does not transfer deterministic permanence to a jump chain.

Evidence contract, costs, and remaining gate

Supplied: the explicit finite reaction diagram, integer m>=3, positive rate constants, and standard falling-factorial mass-action semantics. Acquired in this derivation: the AB drift proof, autonomous projection, clock representation, invariant probability, irreducibility, and recurrence argument. No data, learned rates, simulations, or deterministic theorem are inputs. The network description has nine arrows and six complexes; the only growing symbolic object is order m. The proof gives no explicit stationary law for AB, quantitative return-time bound, tail constant, or uniform-in-m complexity bound. The Foster cutoff b_* is existential from g_m(b)->-infinity.

Sharp limitations: (i) the total-count function W fails pointwise, as shown at (0,n,0); (ii) the averaged inequality requires the acquired conditional Poisson law and the common time-change factor; (iii) H alone is not coercive in C and cannot by itself certify recurrence of the full chain; the invariant-probability plus irreducibility step is essential. Breaking the common modulation, allowing C to affect AB rates, or adding AB-changing C reactions invalidates this factorization proof (though it would not by itself refute recurrence). The general higher-dimensional WR recurrence gate remains UNKNOWN. Novelty/priority for this special construction is also UNKNOWN.
