QUESTION AND RESULT

Test whether representations near a highest-weight wall can produce a
compatible marginal whose deficit is too small compared with the squared
error of coherent-state EB reconstruction. For the exact SU(m) wall

  H_N=Sym^N(C^m), highest weight (N,0,...,0), N=2s,

the coherent Berezin eigenvalues and an actual full-domain self-broadcaster
are explicit. On every nonconstant harmonic ell, if mu_ell is the coherent
EB eigenvalue and lambda_ell is the marginal eigenvalue of this actual
Petz-splitting broadcaster, then

  q_ell := (1-lambda_ell)/(1-mu_ell)^2 > 1/2.             (1)

More precisely, with c=2(s+m)/(2s+m)<2,

  1-mu_ell <= c(1-lambda_ell),
  q_ell >= 1/[c(1-mu_ell)] >= 1/c > 1/2.                (2)

Thus no squared-ratio collapse occurs on this entire wall family, for any
joint scaling of rank m and highest-weight label 2s. This is actual-channel
evidence, not just a formal scalar sequence: one explicit CPTP map on the
full input algebra has two equal marginals Phi, and one explicit coherent
EB channel gives a bounded-likelihood reconstruction bound. The result
rules out this specified symmetric Petz-splitting route as a counterexample;
it neither characterizes all SU(m)-covariant compatible channels nor
settles the unrestricted bounded-Holevo implication.

1. EXACT WALL REPRESENTATION AND COHERENT BEREZIN SPECTRUM

Fix m>=2 and s>=1. Let

  H_N=Sym^N(C^m),  N=2s,
  D_N=dim(H_N)=binomial(N+m-1,N),  tau_N=Tr/D_N.

The SU(m) Dynkin label is (N,0,...,0), so this is an exact chamber-wall
family, not a regular highest weight approaching a wall only in a limit.
Let z be Haar-uniform on CP^(m-1), and write |z;N>=z tensor ... tensor z
and P_z^N=|z;N><z;N|. Schur's lemma gives

  D_N integral P_z^N dz=I_N.

The canonical coherent measure-and-prepare map

  Psi_N(X)=D_N integral Tr(P_z^N X) P_z^N dz               (3)

is completely positive, trace-preserving, unital, selfadjoint for tau_N,
and entanglement breaking. Its use here is on the full matrix algebra
End(H_N).

The coherent symbol X -> <z;N|X|z;N> identifies End(H_N) with the
bihomogeneous polynomials of bidegree (N,N) on the unit sphere. Fischer
decomposition splits it multiplicity-free into harmonic bidegrees
(ell,ell), ell=0,...,N, whose SU(m) highest weights are
(ell,0,...,0,-ell). For the highest-weight operator

  O_(N,ell)=(a_1^dagger)^ell (a_m)^ell,
  <z;N|O_(N,ell)|z;N>=(N)_ell (bar z_1 z_m)^ell,

where (N)_ell=N(N-1)...(N-ell+1). The Dirichlet moment identity

  integral product_i |z_i|^(2r_i) dz
    =(m-1)! product_i r_i!/(sum_i r_i+m-1)!

evaluates the coherent Berezin eigenvalue on this line. Since each
harmonic occurs once, this is the eigenvalue on the full ell sector:

  Psi_N|_(ell)=mu_(N,ell) I,
  mu_(N,ell)=(N)_ell (N+m-1)!/(N+m+ell-1)!
            = product_(j=0..ell-1) (N-j)/(N+m+j).        (4)

This is an exact finite formula, including mu_(N,0)=1; no asymptotic
Berezin expansion is used.

For the coefficient check, take occupation vectors
|u>=|ell,0,...,0,N-ell> and |v>=|0,...,0,N>. Then
<u|O_(N,ell)|v>=sqrt[ell!(N)_ell]. The integrand for
<u|Psi_N(O_(N,ell))|v> is
D_N (N)_ell sqrt[binomial(N,ell)] |z_1|^(2ell)|z_m|^(2N).
The displayed Dirichlet identity evaluates the integral; division by the
occupation matrix element gives exactly (4).

2. A LEGAL FULL-DOMAIN COMPATIBLE MARGINAL

Let V:H_(2s)->H_s tensor H_s be the canonical split of a symmetric
2s-tensor into two blocks of s tensor factors. In normalized occupation
bases, for n=(n_1,...,n_m), sum_i n_i=2s,

  V|n> = sum_(k_i<=n_i, sum k_i=s)
          sqrt[ product_i binomial(n_i,k_i)/binomial(2s,s) ]
          |k> tensor |n-k>.                              (5)

The multivariate Vandermonde identity proves V*V=I. The embedding is
invariant under exchanging the two blocks, so the two partial traces
coincide on every input X:

  T(X)=Tr_2(V X V*)=Tr_1(V X V*).                         (6)

Schur covariance gives T(I_(2s)/D_(2s))=I_s/D_s. Let T* be the ordinary
trace adjoint and define the Petz recovery

  R(Y)=(D_s/D_(2s)) T*(Y).                                (7)

It is CPTP and maps I_s/D_s to I_(2s)/D_(2s). The channel

  Phi=R T=(D_s/D_(2s))T*T                               (8)

is positive, selfadjoint, trace-preserving and unital. Most importantly,

  B(X)=(R tensor R)(V X V*)                              (9)

is one CPTP map defined on every X in End(H_(2s)), and both of its physical
marginals equal Phi. This certifies actual two-output compatibility; it is
not a statement that a marginal has a hypothetical extension only on a
chosen family.

The exact action on the harmonic sectors is

  lambda_ell=mu_(s,ell)/mu_(2s,ell),   0<=ell<=s,
  lambda_ell=0,                        s<ell<=2s.          (10)

For ell<=s, the coherent-symbol identity
T*O_(s,ell)=[(s)_ell/(2s)_ell]O_(2s,ell), together with the normalized
trace singular-value relation for T, gives (10). Explicitly, the same
Haar calculation gives
||O_(N,ell)||_(2,tau_N)^2=(N)_ell^2 M_ell/mu_(N,ell), where M_ell
is the common positive Haar moment of |bar z_1 z_m|^(2ell). Using the
ordinary-trace adjoint identity
Tr(O_(2s,ell)^*T*O_(s,ell))=Tr((T O_(2s,ell))^*O_(s,ell))
gives

  T O_(2s,ell)=[(2s)_ell/(s)_ell](D_(2s)/D_s)
                [mu_(s,ell)/mu_(2s,ell)] O_(s,ell).

Applying (D_s/D_(2s))T* and the displayed T* identity cancels the
occupation factors and dimensions, leaving mu_(s,ell)/mu_(2s,ell) for
(D_s/D_(2s))T*T. For ell>s, the smaller
operator algebra End(H_s) has no ell-sector, so T annihilates it. Thus the
specified compatible-channel vector is fully known in all directions.

3. ALL-MODE DEFICIT COMPARISON AND NO COLLAPSE

For 0<=j<s set

  a_j=(2s-j)/(2s+m+j),
  r_j=[(s-j)/(s+m+j)]/a_j.

For ell<=s, equations (4),(10) give

  mu_(2s,ell)=product_(j<ell) a_j,
  lambda_ell=product_(j<ell) r_j.

An exact rational subtraction yields

  (1-a_j)/(1-r_j)
    =(2s-j)(s+m+j)/[s(2s+m+j)] <= c,
  c=2(s+m)/(2s+m).                                      (11)

Indeed, subtracting the left side from its j=0 value c gives

  j[m^2+mj+3ms+2js]/[s(m+2s)(m+j+2s)] >=0.

Set A=1-1/c and C=1/c. Equation (11) implies r_j<=A+C a_j. Since

  A+Cuv-(A+Cu)(A+Cv)=AC(1-u)(1-v)>=0

for u,v in [0,1], induction gives the product inequality

  lambda_ell <= A+C mu_(2s,ell),
  1-mu_(2s,ell) <= c(1-lambda_ell).                    (12)

For ell>s, lambda_ell=0 and (12) follows from 1-mu<=1<c; ell=0 is the
identity sector. Therefore the exact nonconstant squared-ratio gate is

  q_ell=(1-lambda_ell)/(1-mu_(2s,ell))^2
      >=1/[c(1-mu_(2s,ell))] >=1/c
      =(2s+m)/[2(s+m)] >1/2.                             (13)

For ell>s, the stronger q_ell=1/(1-mu)^2>=1 holds. Formula (13) is
uniform in m,s,ell and directly rules out q_ell->0 on this family.

The first harmonic makes the boundary behavior transparent:

  mu_(2s,1)=2s/(2s+m),
  lambda_1=(2s+m)/[2(s+m)],
  q_1=(2s+m)^2/[2m(s+m)].                                (14)

Writing kappa=m/s, q_1=(2+kappa)^2/[2kappa(1+kappa)]. It decreases to
1/2 as kappa->infinity, stays strictly above 1/2 for every finite
kappa>0, and diverges as kappa->0. In the rank-dominant wall scaling
m/s->infinity, lambda_1->1/2, so this mode is not near perfectly
broadcastable. In the degree-dominant scaling m/s->0, lambda_1->1 and
mu_(2s,1)->1 together; the coherent reconstruction error vanishes along
with the marginal defect. At intermediate ratios, neither vanishes.

4. OPERATIONAL EXPERIMENT AND EB ERROR

Consider any finite family, or the whole compact family,

  rho_H=(I+H)/D_(2s),
  H=H*, tau_(2s)(H)=0, ||H||_infinity<=theta<1.           (15)

Every member is faithful. Since rho_H<=(1+theta)I/D, its Holevo capacity
obeys C(E)<=ln(1+theta), independent of m,s and family size: each state
has D(rho_H||I/D)<=ln(1+theta), while Holevo information is the average
of these relative entropies minus the nonnegative relative entropy of the
average.

The channel Psi_(2s) in (3) is one fixed full-domain EB decoder. The
quadratic form comparison (12) is exactly

  I-Psi_(2s) <= c(I-Phi)                                 (16)

on the complete operator algebra. Because 0<=Psi<=I, for every H in (15),

  ||(I-Psi)H||_(2,tau)^2
   <= <H,(I-Psi)H>
   <= c <H,(I-Phi)H>
   <= c theta ||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau).                       (17)

Thus, with r_E=sup_H ||(I-Phi)H||_(1,tau),

  sup_H T(Psi(rho_H),rho_H) <= (1/2)sqrt(c theta r_E).    (18)

The factor 1/2 converts normalized trace on H_(2s) to physical half-trace.
The actual broadcaster (9) has both marginals Phi, so for this experiment
its pair-broadcast marginal error is at most r_E/2. Conversely, if b_2 is
defined as the infimum over all legal broadcasters, b_2(E)<=r_E/2 for this
explicit B. Hence this candidate can have bounded capacity and b_2->0,
but the same construction forces e_EB->0 rather than keeping it away from
zero. There is no concealed state-dependent decoder: Psi is fixed for all
H and acts on the full matrix algebra.

For example, take m->infinity, s=m^2, and restrict (15) to the
adjoint/ell=1 harmonic. Then 1-lambda_1=m/[2(s+m)]=1/[2(m+1)],
so the actual marginal residual is at most theta/[4(m+1)]. Also
1-mu_(2s,1)=m/(2s+m)=1/(2m+1), so the coherent EB reconstruction
error is at most theta/[2(2m+1)]. Capacity remains at most ln(1+theta).
This is a growing-dimensional bounded-capacity, asymptotically
broadcastable family, but it is not a counterexample because e_EB->0.

5. GENERAL GRASSMANN WALL: EXACT FIRST MODE, REST UNKNOWN

There is a broader wall family with highest weight s omega_k, where
1<=k<=m-1 and omega_k is the kth fundamental weight. Let H_(s,k) be that
irrep, realized as the Cartan component in (wedge^k C^m)^(tensor s). Its
coherent orbit is Gr(k,m). The Cartan inclusion

  V:H_(2s,k)->H_(s,k) tensor H_(s,k)

is symmetric under output swap. Partial trace T, reference Petz recovery
R=(D_(s,k)/D_(2s,k))T*, and B=(R tensor R)Ad_V therefore give a legal
full-domain common broadcaster exactly as in (6)-(9). The Grassmann
coherent orbit also has a Schur-resolution-of-identity measure/prepare EB
map.

The coherent Berezin eigenvalue on the adjoint (ell=1) module can be
derived without a higher-partition formula. Let X be traceless Hermitian
with Tr(X^2)=1 and J_X=d pi_(s omega_k)(X). For a Haar rank-k projector P,

  E[(Tr XP)^2]=k(m-k)/[m(m^2-1)].                        (19)

For generators orthonormal under Tr on the fundamental, the quadratic
Casimir is C_2(s omega_k)=s k(m-k)(s+m)/m. Invariance gives

  tau(J_X^2)=s k(m-k)(s+m)/[m(m^2-1)].                  (20)

Since the coherent-state symbol of J_X is s Tr(XP), the Rayleigh quotient
of the EB map on the adjoint component is

  mu_(s,k,1)=s^2 E[(Tr XP)^2]/tau(J_X^2)=s/(s+m).        (21)

For the Petz split, output exchange symmetry and the Lie algebra action
give T*J_X^(s)=J_X^(2s)/2. Normalized-trace adjointness then yields

  lambda_(s,k,1)=1/4 ||J_X^(2s)||_(2,tau)^2 /
                         ||J_X^(s)||_(2,tau)^2
                 =(2s+m)/[2(s+m)].                      (22)

So the exact first-mode squared ratio is again

  q_1=(1-lambda_1)/(1-mu_(2s,k,1))^2
     =(2s+m)^2/[2m(s+m)] >1/2,                           (23)

independently of k. No weight-wall counterexample arises from the adjoint
mode, even when k approaches either boundary 1 or m-1, or scales with m.

For k strictly between the edges, I do NOT establish the complete
partition-indexed spectrum or an all-mode deficit comparison here. An
internal earlier diagnostic records the candidate formula

  mu_(s,m,lambda) ?= product_(j=0..r-1)
                     product_(t=0..lambda_(j+1)-1)
                     (s+j-t)/(s+m-j+t),
  r=min(k,m-k), lambda_1<=s,                             (24)

but its full representation-theoretic import and coupled-partition
inequality were not proved. Some individual factor comparisons exceed 2,
so the exact symmetric-power factor proof cannot simply be copied. The
finite scalar search in the prior internal record is diagnostic only.
Accordingly (23) is scalar evidence only for one sector; the existence of
the Grassmann broadcaster is a genuine compatible-channel statement; and
no all-mode EB reconstruction claim for general k is made. A higher-mode
countersequence or a complete coupled-product proof remains UNKNOWN.

6. SCOPE, BASELINE, COSTS, AND DECISIVE NEXT CHECK

What is ruled out: the exact one-row SU(m) representations
Sym^(2s)(C^m), with their canonical particle-split/Petz compatible
marginals, cannot produce a vanishing squared-ratio obstruction. The
calculation is exact for all harmonics and all m,s. Their actual common
broadcaster and common full-algebra EB decoder are specified above.

What is not ruled out: other SU(m)-covariant compatible marginals, other
highest-weight shapes, non-covariant compatible channels, or an N93
counterexample outside this split family. Even an all-mode failure of a
chosen coherent decoder would not prove an EB lower bound; it would still
require a common-measurement lower bound for every EB channel. Capacity and
pair error alone have not been converted to the structural assumptions
used here.
