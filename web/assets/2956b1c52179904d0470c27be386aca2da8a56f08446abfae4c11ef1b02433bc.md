## Verdict

I found no tumor-resistance mechanism in this pass that creates a new prediction against an equally informed branching-process baseline. Two biologically credible mechanisms survive the *diffusion-only* or first-two-moment baseline: stress-dependent ecDNA partitioning and delayed PGCC burst repopulation. But ecDNA partition is ordinary two-daughter reproduction once the full daughter states are observed, while PGCC burst offspring are represented exactly by a multitype/age-structured branching model once event times and the full offspring law are supplied. The mechanisms and their lineage assays also have direct primary-literature predecessors. I therefore reject a novelty claim and any patient-level risk number.

The actionable residue is an **assay-specific identifiability boundary**. Existing work already measures related lineage channels. The unresolved gap is whether a frozen, finite-data risk predictor adds calibration or decision value on held-out models after the same event data are given to a full branching baseline. ## Exact ecDNA daughter-partition construction

This is an internally constructed stress test, not a fitted biological law. A parent has four ecDNA copies before replication and eight partitioned copies at mitosis. Let (X) be the copy count of one randomly labeled daughter; its sister receives (8-X). Compare

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 & X & & \\ \hline
A & 2\ (1/4), & 4\ (1/2), & 6\ (1/4),\\
B & 0\ (1/16), & 4\ (14/16), & 8\ (1/16).
\end{array}
\]

Both daughter-pair laws have

\[
\mathbb E(X,8-X)=(4,4),\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(X,8-X)=\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\-2&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

So their event-level CLE drift and covariance agree exactly. Yet the tail (Y=\mathbf 1\{\max(X,8-X)\ge6\}) has probabilities (1/2) and (1/8). The fourth cumulants of (X) are −4 and 20. If that copy-number tail is the preregistered resistance-relevant state and each such daughter independently establishes with probability (1/2), per-mitosis establishment probability is (3/8) under A and (3/32) under B, a fourfold gap. This is a conditional model prediction only: the copy threshold and daughter establishment probability must be measured in the chosen cell system.

The exact arithmetic is in [cycle3_moment_match_check.py](cycle3_moment_match_check.py); its JSON output confirms the means, covariance, fourth cumulants, and PGCC risk arithmetic. An independent measurement design used the equivalent four-copy/8-total partition and a calibrated event classifier with sensitivity (0.90), false-tail rate (0.01). For its deliberately large (0.375) tail-probability contrast, a one-sided Fisher comparison needs 26 complete mother divisions per arm for about 80.2% power; 44 initiated tracks per arm suffice for at least 26 complete pairs with 95% assurance under an assumed 70% completion yield. These are exact calculations under the stated idealized error/missingness model, not literature-reported classifier performance. If the effect size can approach zero, sensitivity is unknown, or missingness depends on the partition outcome, there is no uniform finite-sample guarantee.

The exact mathematical limitation is useful: when an ordinary mitosis produces exactly two daughters and their full joint typed offspring vector (Z) is measured, the full first and second moments already determine its law because \(\sum_j Z_j=2\):

\[
\Pr(Z=e_j+e_k)=\mathbb E[Z_jZ_k]\ (j\ne k),\qquad
\Pr(Z=2e_j)=\tfrac12\mathbb E[Z_j(Z_j-1)].
\]

Thus the example can only remain aliased if states are coarsened/hidden, daughter pairing or the parent state is missing, or the event clock/selection process is not known. This is a small exact boundary result, not a new cancer theorem.

### Biological and prior-art boundary for ecDNA

Lange et al. already combine random-segregation modeling, paired-daughter FISH/live imaging, copy-number distributions and treatment adaptation ([*Nature Genetics* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01177-x)). Hung et al. measure coordinated, asymmetric co-inheritance of distinct ecDNA species in paired daughters and model it ([*Nature* 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07861-8)). Most directly, Shibata et al. combine clone barcoding, single-cell DNA sequencing, drug-stress tracking, daughter segregation analysis and a beta-binomial model; they report a clone- and treatment-specific active ecDNA reconfiguration pattern under JQ1, with EN2 knockdown partially attenuating it ([*Nature Communications* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76566-5)). That paper directly preempts a generic stress-dependent ecDNA partition mechanism and selection-versus-reconfiguration assay. Its own limitations matter: the effect is cell-line/clone/drug-specific; endpoint and barcode resolution do not yet yield a transferable tumor-level resistant-lineage probability. A 2025 replication-stress study also reports daughter-pair ecDNA partition changes but is a preprint ([Brückner et al.](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.15.648906)).

The proposed residual measurement is a joint, parent-copy-conditioned transition (K(x_1,x_2\mid n,\text{state},\text{stress})) followed by the same daughters' clonogenic fate. The 2026 ecDNA paper already measures closely related segregation and survival endpoints, so this is not a novelty claim. Its distinction is only that endpoint copy distributions do not identify whether a given tail-bearing daughter produced a durable resistant lineage. Spatial tissue/plasma data lose mother-daughter links and can have clone-specific shedding; the whole-tumor extension is `UNKNOWN` without nonzero spatial inclusion and detection bounds.

## PGCC burst branching model and consequence

Polyploid giant cancer cells (PGCCs) are a better setting for a variable offspring count. Use a PGCC state (G), with death rate \(\delta\), burst/neosis rate \(\beta\), and full viable clonogenic offspring PGF (h(s)=\mathbb E[s^K]\). Let each burst daughter subsequently follow a linear birth-death process with rates \(b_R,d_R\), and let (q_R\) be that lineage's extinction probability (\(q_R=1\) if \(b_R\le d_R\), else \(d_R/b_R\)). Competing exponential first events give the exact per-PGCC establishment probability

\[
p_G=\frac{\beta}{\beta+\delta}\,[1-h(q_R)].
\]

For \(N_G\) independently formed PGCCs, risk is \(1-(1-p_G)^{N_G}\); for (N_G\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\Lambda)\), it is \(1-\exp(-\Lambda p_G)\). The assumptions are independent lineages, a memoryless death-versus-burst clock in the modeled phase, instantaneous burst, a measured *clonogenic* offspring count (K), and no unmodeled density feedback or shared microenvironment. An age-structured model is needed if the post-stress waiting-time law is not exponential.

Equal mean burst output does not identify this risk. With \(q_R=1/2\) and \(\beta/(\beta+\delta)=1/2\), deterministic \(K=4\) gives \(p_G=0.46875\), while \(K=0\) with probability \(3/4\) and \(K=16\) with probability \(1/4\) gives \(p_G=0.1249981\). This is an exact equal-mean construction, not an estimate for any tumor. A separate continuous-time branching example in [cycle3_branching_baseline.md](cycle3_branching_baseline.md) matches the CLE coefficients and *all-time first two population moments* yet has eventual persistence probabilities (0.634) and 1; it shows the general moment-closure failure.

The full PGF is the equally informed baseline and computes both probabilities exactly. A second-order diffusion does not. The PGF dependence is standard branching-process theory, not a new mechanism or control principle. Nor is the biology new: Zhang et al. track olaparib-induced PGCC formation and noncanonical daughter generation after washout using labeled cells imaged every 15 minutes at 10x for up to one week; they derive resistant daughter populations from a single PGCC, but do not report a denominator-linked burst hazard or a full clonogenic burst-size law ([*Science Advances* 2023](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adf7195)). Earlier irradiation/neosis lineage work directly documents PGCC death, daughter generation and repopulation ([Zhang et al., *Molecular Oncology* 2021](https://doi.org/10.1002/1878-0261.12913)); a glioblastoma study links neosis to recurrence and perturbation ([Mahaddalkar et al., *Oncogene* 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41388-025-03372-6)). These papers establish selected preclinical mechanism examples, not a transferable (p_G).

The limiting data are joint and lineage-specific: stress-driven PGCC formation count/rate \(\Lambda\), competing \(\beta,\delta\), burst timing, the full viable-clonogenic (K) distribution, and post-burst (b_R,d_R\) (or empirical establishment probability), with censoring, barcode loss, and spatial inclusion. The 2023 ovarian/PARP paper reports the mechanism and repeated 15-minute imaging but no tracked-mother denominator, burst frequency, or burst-size distribution sufficient to fit those inputs. In one illustrative design, a Poisson single-seed null with mean 0.1 and a burst law emitting two viable daughters with probability 0.05 have the same mean, while \(P(Y\ge2)\) is 0.00468 versus 0.05; 59 independent PGCCs are required for about 80% power at one-sided 5% alpha under ideal detection. With zero bursts, an exact 95% upper rate is approximately \(3/E\) over (E) PGCC-days, so 3,000 live PGCC-days are required to upper-bound a rate by 0.001/day. All numbers in this paragraph are prospective design scenarios, not rates reported in the paper.

## What is and is not resolved

- **Resolved model claim:** CLE drift/covariance is not sufficient for variable-offspring PGCC branching risk; the exact PGF is. The displayed examples prove this under explicit model assumptions.
- **Resolved baseline comparison:** full multitype/age-structured branching with the complete event clock and offspring law exactly represents the proposed mechanism. No advantage over that baseline remains.
- **Rejected novelty:** ecDNA stress-dependent reconfiguration, PGCC neosis/burst repopulation, offspring-PGF extinction, and lineage-resolved observation all have direct predecessors.
- **Practical gate:** a preclinical event-resolved assay is plausible, but its resistant-lineage consequence requires linking partition/burst marks to later clonogenic fate. No rate or outcome transfer to patients is justified. A whole-tumor risk-vs-duration calculation must remain `UNKNOWN` without spatial coverage, assay sensitivity, source rates and an outcome link.
- **Narrow untested opportunity:** train and freeze a finite-data PGF risk interval from complete, barcoded event records; test calibration on blinded cell-line/organoid holdouts against a full-information branching model and clone-selection baseline under matched observation cost. This is an experiment/validation gap, not a novelty assertion.
