REGULAR SU(m) RANK-CONSTRAINED CASIMIR PROFILE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally reconstructed proof package; independent finite checks passed.
Scope: fixed m and a fixed regular highest-weight cone. No novelty or
operational-transfer claim is made.

1. THEOREM AND NORMALIZATION

Fix m>=2, put p=|Phi^+|=m(m-1)/2, and let lambda have Dynkin labels
a_1,...,a_(m-1) satisfying

  c N <= a_i <= C N   for every i,

where 0<c<=C<infinity are fixed and N>=1. Let d=dim V_lambda. On
End(V_lambda), use Hermitian generators normalized by
Tr_fund(T_A T_B)=delta_AB/2 and define

  L(X)=sum_A [T_A,[T_A,X]],
  E(X)=<X,LX>=sum_A ||[T_A,X]||_HS^2,
  Lambda_lambda(Q)=inf_{0!=X, rank X<=Q} E(X)/||X||_HS^2.

All ranks Q below are integers. There are positive constants depending only
on m,c,C such that

  Lambda_lambda(Q) ~= (d/Q)^(1/p),       1<=Q<=floor(d/2),       (1)

and, for k=d-Q with 1<=k<=floor(d/2),

  Lambda_lambda(d-k) ~= (k/d)^((p-1)/p),  m>=3,                (2)

  Lambda_lambda(d-k) ~= 1/log(e d/k),     m=2.                 (3)

Here ~= means two-sided bounds with constants uniform in N, lambda, Q,
and k under the fixed cone assumption. Lambda_lambda(d)=0, witnessed by
the identity. The lower bounds in (1)-(3) hold for arbitrary complex,
possibly non-normal X. Positive diagonal filters give the upper bounds.
The exponent is the number p of positive roots, not the rank m-1.

2. PROOF INPUT I: ALL-CARRIER MULTIPLICITY AND SPECTRAL COUNT

The conjugation representation decomposes as

  End(V_lambda)=direct_sum_nu M_(lambda,nu) tensor V_nu.

For every dominant nu, the multiplicity satisfies

  dim M_(lambda,nu) <= dim V_nu[0].                            (4)

An explicit finite-dimensional proof is in multiplicity_injection.txt.
Briefly, multiplicity equals dim Hom(V_lambda tensor V_lambda^*,V_nu),
which by tensor-Hom currying equals dim Hom(V_mu,V_mu tensor V_nu),
mu=lambda^*. Send a highest vector v_mu through an intertwiner and write
its image as sum_beta w_beta in
V_mu[mu-beta] tensor V_nu[beta]. The beta=0 coefficient lies in V_nu[0].
If this coefficient vanishes, choose a nonzero w_beta of least positive
root height. The highest-vector equations imply (e_i tensor 1)w_beta=0
for every simple raising operator. The common kernel in the irreducible
first factor is its highest line, incompatible with first weight mu-beta.
Thus the coefficient map into V_nu[0] is injective. This proof does not
assume regularity or a PRV upper bound.

For the Casimir normalization above, the eigenvalue on V_nu is

  c2(nu)=(nu,nu+2rho)/2,

with long roots of squared length 2. Define D_lambda(T) as the dimension of
the full conjugation-Casimir spectral subspace with eigenvalue at most T,
including all multiplicities. The PBW/Kostant count in spectral_count.txt
proves, uniformly for every carrier lambda and every T>=0,

  D_lambda(T) <= C_m (1+T)^p.                                 (5)

The exponent follows explicitly: with r=m-1 and q=p-r, zero-weight
multiplicity is O_m((1+||nu||)^q), Weyl dimension is
O_m((1+||nu||)^p), and there are O_m((1+T)^(r/2)) dominant weights in a
Casimir ball of radius sqrt(T). The product is O_m((1+T)^p). The exact
all-T estimate, including beyond the finite carrier spectrum, is needed
for the Green lower bound below.

3. LOW-RANK LOWER BOUND

Let Pi_T be the conjugation-invariant projection onto Casimir eigenvalues
in [0,T]. Its dimension is D_lambda(T). Schur's lemma gives

  Tr_2(Pi_T)=(D_lambda(T)/d) I.

For ||X||_HS=1 and rank(X)<=Q, the rank-Q Schmidt estimate yields

  ||Pi_T X||_HS^2 <= Q D_lambda(T)/d.

Layer cake then gives the exact variational lower bound

  E(X) >= integral_0^infinity [1-Q D_lambda(T)/d]_+ dT.        (6)

Using (5) in (6) gives c_m(d/Q)^(1/p) whenever d/Q exceeds a fixed
constant. When d/Q is bounded, use the positive Casimir gap and
|Tr X|^2/d<=Q/d to obtain a positive constant, which completes the
lower bound in (1). This argument makes no log-Sobolev, KLS, or
log-concavity assumption.

4. NEAR-FULL LOWER BOUND FOR ALL MATRICES

For any X of rank at most d-k choose a rank-k projection P onto a subspace
of its right kernel. Write X=aI+Y with Tr Y=0 and P_0=P-(k/d)I. Since
XP=0, cyclicity gives ka=-Tr(P_0Y). On the traceless space L is invertible,
so Cauchy-Schwarz in the L^{-1}/L energy pairing gives the exact bridge

  ||X||_HS^2 <= E(X) [d G(P)/k^2 + gamma_m^(-1)],
  G(P)=<P_0,L^(-1)P_0>,
  gamma_m=(m^2-1)/(2m).                                        (7)

The value gamma_m is a safe lower floor for every nontrivial SU(m)
Casimir; the center restriction in End(V_lambda) can only increase the
actual gap. For the positive Casimir projection Pi_(0,T], the partial-trace
bound and vectorized rank-k P imply

  ||Pi_(0,T]P_0||_HS^2 <= min{k-k^2/d, k^2(D_lambda(T)-1)/d}.

The exact Green layer-cake identity is

  G(P)=integral_0^infinity ||Pi_(0,T]P_0||_HS^2 T^(-2) dT.     (8)

Combining (5), (7), and (8) gives a finite all-carrier bound. Let

  C_D=C_m,
  A_m=C_D(1+gamma_m^(-1))^p,
  s=d/k, delta=s-1.

For p>=2, with S=max{gamma_m,(delta/A_m)^(1/p)},

  Lambda_lambda(d-k) >=
   [gamma_m^(-1) + (p/(p-1)) delta/S]^(-1).                   (9)

For p=1,

  Lambda_lambda(d-k) >=
   [gamma_m^(-1)+A_m(1+log_+(delta/(A_m gamma_m)))]^(-1).     (10)

The max and positive-part logarithm handle the endpoint where the cutoff
falls below the spectral gap. For k<=d/2, s>=2 and delta is comparable
to s. Equations (9)-(10) imply the lower branches in (2)-(3). In
particular, the p=1 logarithm comes from integrating T^(-1) in (8), not
from taking a formal limit of the power formula.

5. MATCHING UPPER FILTERS ON A REGULAR CONE

The constructive proof is detailed in principal_height_upper.txt. Here is
its content and the extension from the written [N,2N] labels to the stated
fixed cone. Put n=min_i a_i and K=max_i a_i/n. Then n>=cN, K<=C/c, and

  (n+1)^p <= d <= (Kn+1)^p,

so d is comparable to N^p with constants depending only on m,c,C. If
n>=40p(m-1), the cap proof in principal_height_upper.txt applies after
replacing its N by n and allowing constants to depend on K. The stable
principal-height PBW shell range is depth <=n; the conductance estimates
are used only through depth n/4. The lower Dynkin bound gives the same
stable-cap equality, while upper shell traces change by at most a factor
depending on K. Hence all ramp and resistance estimates persist with
constants depending only on m,K. If n<40p(m-1), then all labels are
bounded by K*40p(m-1), leaving finitely many carriers for fixed m,c,C;
the finite collection of nonzero rank ratios is absorbed in the same
constants. This proves the cone-uniform statement, not merely the
[N,2N] special case written in the construction note.

For clarity, the stable cap has the exact generating function

  F_m(z)=product_(ell=1)^(m-1) (1-z^ell)^(-(m-ell)),
  V_R=sum_(q=0)^R [z^q]F_m(z) ~=_m (R+1)^p.                   (11)

The Kostant formula shows the carrier shells equal these PBW shells up to
depth n: every nonidentity Weyl correction has depth at least n+1. For a
root of height ell=j-i, the exact trace recurrence is

  c_ij(q+ell)-c_ij(q)=Tr_(W_q)(E_ii-E_jj),
  c_ij(q)=sum_(t>=1,q-t ell>=0) Tr_(W_(q-t ell))(E_ii-E_jj).

For q<=n/4, this gives simple-root conductance

  c n V_(q-1) <= c_1(q) <= C_(m,K) n V_(q-1),                (12)

and a comparable upper bound for every root, with its height factor
included. Thus the linear cap ramp F_R=(1-depth/R)_+ has rank
V_(R-1) ~= R^p, norm squared ~= R^p, and energy <=C_(m,K)nR^(p-1).
Taking R on the scale Q^(1/p), with integer rounding and a fixed-cap
patch at larger Q, proves

  Lambda_lambda(Q) <= C_(m,c,C) n Q^(-1/p)
                    <= C'_(m,c,C)(d/Q)^(1/p),  Q<=d/2.       (13)

For p>=2, the near-full witness is X=I-A, with A equal to one on a
principal-height cap and tapering to zero harmonically over shells
B,...,M. Its resistance is

  rho=sum_(j=B)^M 1/c_1(j) ~=_(m,K) [n B^(p-1)]^(-1).

The taper has nullity at least k, ||X||^2>=d/2, and energy at most
C_(m,K)/rho. Choose B on the order of k^(1/p) for k up to a fixed
positive fraction of d. It follows that

  E(X)/||X||^2 <= C_(m,c,C) n k^((p-1)/p)/d
                 <= C'_(m,c,C)(k/d)^((p-1)/p).               (14)

The last inequality uses d~=n^p. For larger k<=d/2, the positive-part
Cartan filter X_H=(-T_(H0)/L)_+ has rank<=d/2 and bounded energy quotient;
the target in (2) is then bounded below by a fixed positive constant,
so this patches the intermediate nullity range.

For SU(2), the depth shells are one-dimensional and the exact conductance
is c(q)=q(n-q+1). The harmonic resistance is

  rho=sum_(j=k)^M 1/[j(n-j+1)] ~= n^(-1) log(e n/k),

giving E(X)/||X||^2<=C/log(e d/k). The same Cartan patch handles the
remaining fixed-fraction nullities. This proves the upper branch in (3).

6. PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISON AND CLAIM BOUNDARY

The multiplicity cap (4) is already a consequence of the stronger
Fronsdal-Hirai theorem: for connected compact G, End(V_lambda) embeds in
Ind_T^G(1)=L^2(G/T); their stabilizer version improves T to the
coadjoint stabilizer for singular lambda. The type-A zero-weight growth
degree p-(m-1) is also consistent with the chamber-polynomial theorem of
Billey-Guillemin-Rassart, and follows here from Kostant/PBW. Alagic-Russell
give a related compact-group support/Fourier-rank uncertainty principle,
but their constraint is not matrix rank in one carrier. See prior_art.txt
for source details and scope. This package makes no priority or novelty
claim.

The profile does not prove any stochastic-localization, heat-plateau,
log-concavity, KLS, or scale-integrated observation mutual-information
inequality. Those are separate gates; in particular the N75 missing MI
inequality remains open on this evidence. The weak-Gibbs operational
transfer is separately documented under
outputs/research/sol_semiclassical_memory/regular_su_m/RESULT.txt and is
not part of the proof of (1)-(14).
