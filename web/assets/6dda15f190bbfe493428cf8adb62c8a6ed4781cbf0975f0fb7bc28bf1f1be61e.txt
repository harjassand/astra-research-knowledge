EXACT SINGLE-LAG EQUILIBRIUM CLOSURE
Fix lag t>0, finite observed alphabet of size d, and positive observed marginal pi. Let E_t(pi) be pair tables Pr(Y0=a,Yt=b) obtained from stationary finite-state reversible CTMCs, with arbitrary hidden cardinality/rates and deterministic observation map, having observed marginal pi. Requiring irreducible hidden CTMCs does not change the following closure:
 closure E_t(pi) = {P completely positive: P 1=pi}.

Necessity: condition on hidden state at t/2. Detailed balance makes the conditional distributions of Y0 and Yt identical; the Markov property gives conditional independence. Thus P is a sum of nonnegative rank-one symmetric matrices. Its marginal is pi. The finite-dimensional CP cone is closed, preserving necessity under limits.

Sufficiency: write P=sum_{r=1}^R b_r b_r^T with b_r>=0 and b_r nonzero. Put z_r=1^T b_r, lambda_r=z_r^2, p_r=b_r/z_r. Then sum lambda_r=1 and sum lambda_r p_r=pi. Take hidden states (r,a) with p_r(a)>0, stationary law rho(r,a)=lambda_r p_r(a), deterministic output a. Within each r use reset generator
 Q_r(a,b)=K p_r(b) for a!=b.
Each block satisfies detailed balance and exp(t Q_r)=e^(-Kt)I+(1-e^(-Kt))1 p_r^T. Its stationary observed pair table is therefore
               P_K=e^(-Kt)diag(pi)+(1-e^(-Kt))P.
As K→infinity, P_K→P in entrywise l1 norm, and every approximation has entropy production exactly zero.

For irreducibility, add a global reset at rate delta>0 to the entire hidden stationary law rho: Q_delta=Q+delta(1 rho^T-I). This added generator is reversible under rho. It makes all positive-mass states communicate. Coupling with a common within-block path gives probability at most 1-e^(-delta t)<=delta t of any added reset in [0,t], so the output pair law differs by TV at most delta t. Choose delta→0 while K→infinity. The observed marginal remains exactly pi.

This is a complete closure statement at ONE lag only. Separate membership at each lag does not produce a common semigroup. Exact finite-rate realization at a boundary point is not implied. In particular deterministic nonconstant observations of a finite reversible CTMC have strictly positive covariance at finite positive lag for each nonzero centered observable, because exp(tQ) is strictly positive definite on L2(rho); exact independence is generally only approached.

Known algebraic background: normalized CP pair distributions are exactly mixtures of iid pairs, equivalently infinitely exchangeable pair extensions. Stein–Parrilo–Ozdaglar, Exchangeable Equilibria in Symmetric Bimatrix Games (2010), https://web.mit.edu/~nstein/www/documents/BWGT2010.pdf explicitly states this de Finetti consequence. The algebraic CP/CIID fact is NOT new here. The continuous-time reset realization, dissipation certificates and their relation to known stochastic-thermodynamic inference require separate historical assessment.

SMALLER CTMC EXAMPLE WITH A COPOSITIVE SIGNAL AND POSITIVE PAIR COVARIANCE
The following is an exact model specification accompanied so far by floating diagnostics, not a certified interval evaluation.
Use two rings with 5M states each, labels (+,i),(-,i), i mod5M, and five static states s_j. Let M=100, r=14/25, kappa=10^-5. Ring stationary mass is r/(10M) per ring state; static mass is (1-r)/5 each. Output g(±,i)=floor(i/M), g(s_j)=j.
The + ring has clockwise rate M+1 and counterclockwise rate1; the - ring has these reversed. Connect every ring state to its corresponding static state at rate kappa, and the reverse static-to-ring edge at rate r kappa/[2M(1-r)]. These links satisfy detailed balance. The full hidden CTMC is irreducible and stationary with the stated law.
The involution J swaps + and - at the same i and fixes static states. It preserves the stationary law and output, and satisfies JQJ=Q*. Hence the ENTIRE stationary observed path law is time reversible. J is a mathematical symmetry, not a physical odd-variable reversal: the entropy-production interpretation assumes all hidden states physically even.
Without added switches, the active-ring displacement at lag1 is D=Pois(M+1)-Pois(1). A uniform phase inside a block gives exact output-shift probabilities: if D=qM+h, 0<=h<M, the shift is q with probability1-h/M and q+1 with probability h/M. Average the forward/backward directions and add static mass1-r. The resulting pair table is symmetric circulant.
work/root/horn_clock_check.py evaluates the defining convergent series. It reports minimum eigenvalue about0.01058775 and Horn pairing about-0.02982286. The Horn matrix W has diagonal1, adjacent entries-1 along a five-cycle, and remaining entries+1. It is copositive: for nonnegative x, x^T W x=(sum x)^2-4 sum_cycle x_i x_j>=0 by the five-cycle clique-number-two quadratic inequality (also a special case of Motzkin–Straus).
The observed covariance matrix P-pi pi^T is PSD because P has row sums1/5, eigenvalue1/5 in the constant direction, and positive remaining eigenvalues. Thus EVERY scalar state-observable autocovariance at this fixed lag is nonnegative, while the copositive witness detects incompatibility with all equilibrium hidden CTMCs.
The switches perturb the endpoint table by TV at most kappa max(1,r/(1-r))=1.272727...e-5, using the maximum added exit rate. Spectral perturbation is at most twice that amount in operator norm, and a max-entry-one witness changes by at most twice TV. The reported margins are much larger, but rigorous numerical intervals are still required before this specific M=100 numerical certificate is treated as proved. The director's larger clock construction gives a separate analytic-existence proof.
The hidden model's entropy production is r M log(M+1)≈258.45 nats/time, whereas the linear witness lower bound is only about0.119. This is a separation example, not a sharpness or resource-efficiency result.
