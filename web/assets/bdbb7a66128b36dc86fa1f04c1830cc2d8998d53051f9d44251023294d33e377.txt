1. EXACT FINITE IDENTITY
Fix N>=1,0<=k<=N, x=(k-N/2)^2 and A_r=(N/2+r)^2,r>=1. Let a>0, f(x)=e^-ax,
and P_(m-1)(x) be its degree<=m-1 Newton interpolation polynomial at A1..Am.
Its Newton basis is product_(r=1)^l(A_r-x) with coefficients
 (-1)^l f[A1,...,A_(l+1)]>0.
Positivity follows from the divided-difference mean-value theorem because
f^(l)=(-a)^l e^-a x. The basis values factor as
 A_r-x=(r+k)(r+N-k)=:lambda_r>0.

Let independent E_r have exponential rates lambda_r and put T_m=sum_(r=1)^m E_r.
Then EXACTLY
 P_(m-1)(x)=e^-ax P(T_m>a).                                (N1)
To check it, use the Lagrange interpolation formula and A_r-x=lambda_r:
 P_(m-1)(x)=e^-ax sum_(i=1)^m e^-a lambda_i
                    product_(j!=i)lambda_j/(lambda_j-lambda_i).
The rates are distinct and increasing. The displayed sum is the standard
hypoexponential survival expression, verified directly by the Laplace
transform(1/s)[1-product_r lambda_r/(s+lambda_r)] and its simple-pole partial
fractions. This is a complete derivation, not reliance on a numerical table.

Each positive Newton term yields a positive beta coherent mixture. In fact

and

positive-Beta interpretation and all index/normalizer conventions.
