## Candidate and evidence

The exact minimax probe error is `delta/sqrt(E*T)` when `R >= sqrt(E*T)/2 + delta/sqrt(E*T)`. A constant pulse attains it. A constant output-feedback correction settles the exact model into that same radius, with correction energy, amplitude, safety and quantization costs explicit in `proofs/quadratic_sensor_minimax.md` and `FINAL_REPORT.md`.
