Created 2026-10-08; source package references listed in ../.. leaf.

Explicit family:
  mu = Uniform([-1,1]^2)
  h(x)=x/2
  f_t(x1,x2)=(1+t h(x1))(1+t^2 h(x2)), |t|<=a<=1/4.

At t in {-a,0,a}, equal prior, posterior coordinates (pi_-,pi_+) have Jacobian at x=0
  [ -a/6  a^2/18 ]
  [  a/6  a^2/18 ]
with determinant -a^3/54. The prior mixture density is positive at zero. Therefore a small square around zero maps diffeomorphically to a two-dimensional posterior patch, giving posterior-ball mass O_a(h^2).

For any total-dimension-D unassisted hybrid memory, decoder POVM E(dy) has integral I with Tr I=D, while every encoded state obeys sigma_x<=I. Consequently
  Pr[X in C, ||pi(X)-pi(Y)||<=h] <= K_a D h^2.
The simulator's prior joint law (T,Y) is epsilon-TV close to (T,X). Brier Bayes-risk excess is exactly E||pi(X)-pi(Y)||^2 and is at most 2epsilon. The trace-capacity small-ball bound gives the reverse lower bound c_a/D. Thus D>=c_a/epsilon.

For the upper, equispaced positive nodal hats phi_j on [-1,1] encode j with probability phi_j(x). Decode with Q_j(dy)=phi_j(y)mu_1(dy)/m_j. The reference is preserved exactly. Interior hats reproduce affine x; only endpoint half-hats bias the centroid, by O(Delta) on support of measure O(Delta), so ||Kx-x||_1=O(Delta^2). Tensorization exactly covers the polynomial terms 1,x1,x2,x1*x2 in f_t, hence TV error O_a(Delta^2). There are (J+1)^2 labels and Delta=2/J, yielding D=O_a(1/epsilon).

Reproducibility: this is an analytic hand derivation with the full equations above; no script or numerical diagnostic is required. A useful independent check is symbolic recomputation of the posterior Jacobian and a formal measure-theoretic audit of the trace-capacity inequality. Neither check is external validation.

Resource boundary:
  Stored archive: (J+1)^2 labels, 2 ceil(log2(J+1)) fixed bits.
  Encoder: two fresh Bernoulli choices; b random bits per coordinate give O(2^-b) label-law error.
  Input: b-bit coordinate rounding perturbs adjacent-hat probabilities by O(J 2^-b).
  Decoder: exact q_j has a continuous triangular law. Finite float output is atomic and TV=1 from continuous targets. A finite-bit end-to-end TV implementation has not been constructed; exact continuous randomness is an explicit service requirement.

Strong-signal boundary: in N66's linear tilt class, allowing v=+e_i and -e_i forces 1±r_i h_i>=0 everywhere, hence r_i||h_i||_infinity<=1. No unbounded r_i regime exists for fixed nonzero bounded h_i and the full unit-ball parameter set.
