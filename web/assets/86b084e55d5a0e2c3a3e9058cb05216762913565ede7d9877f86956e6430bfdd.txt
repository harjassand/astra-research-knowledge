2. A UNIFORM CONTACT-FORM BOUND WITH EXACT NORMALIZATION

In the normalized symmetric M=2 space write

where Q_D is the projection onto coincident site pairs. Then the M=2
unnormalized heat block in (N.1) is T_L(c)=exp[-(A_L+cV_L)]. The one-

 (1/2)|k|^4<=a_L,k<=(pi^4/32)|k|^4.                   (N.5)

We prove uniformly in L, on the labeled space and hence on its symmetric
subspace,

 V_L <= 25(I+A_L).                                    (N.6)

Extend a square Dirichlet vector oddly in each coordinate to the torus
T_L=(Z/(2L)Z)^2, dividing its amplitudes by 2. Four reflected quadrants
make this an isometry. The two-particle extension divides amplitudes by
4. Its coincident-site squared norm on T_L is exactly one quarter of the
original squared norm on Lambda_L. Boundary axes have zero amplitudes.

Let the normalized torus Fourier basis be
 e_p(x)=(2L)^(-1)exp(i pi p.x/L),
p_j in {-L,...,L-1}; all additions below are modulo 2L. If C_{p,q} are
the extended two-particle Fourier coefficients, the Fourier coefficients

 d_n=(2L)^(-1)sum_p C_{p,n-p}.
Parseval and the preceding factor 1/4 give the exact identity

 <psi,V_L psi>=sum_n |sum_p C_{p,n-p}|^2.              (N.7)

Extend a_L,p to signed Fourier labels by the same cosine formula. For
the principal residues it satisfies the same lower bound in (N.5).
Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz with b_{p,q}=1+a_L,p+a_L,q bounds (N.7) by
sup_n(sum_p 1/b_{p,n-p}) times sum_{p,q}b_{p,q}|C_{p,q}|^2.
The latter sum equals <psi,(I+A_L)psi>. The former is at most

 sum_{p in Z^2}1/(1+|p|^4/2)
 <=1+sum_{r>=1}8r*(2/r^4)

This proves (N.6). No sine-to-exponential transform norm, collision orbit
factor, particle-sector factorial, or lattice-size coefficient is hidden.

6. THE CONTINUUM CONTACT FORM AND A UNIFORM HIGH-MODE TAIL

This section proves actual convergence, rather than using a numerical
continuum analogy. Embed the finite one-particle sine basis by its mode
labels in l^2(N^2). Embed each finite symmetric m-particle space and the
whole finite Fock space by occupation labels, with all other modes empty.
Write
 A_*m=dGamma_m(|k|^4/2),
 A_infty,m=dGamma_m(pi^4 |k|^4/32).
Thus A_L,m>=A_*m and A_L,k->A_infty,k coefficientwise. The operator A_*
on full Fock has compact resolvent: its gap is 2, and a finite energy
cut allows only finitely many modes and finitely many particles.

On symmetric finite sine polynomials on the unit square, let

 V_infty,m[psi]=sum_{a<b} integral
   |psi(x_1,...,x_a=x_b,...,x_m)|^2 dx_a product_{j!=a,b}dx_j. (N.12)

The continuum sine modes are phi_k(x)=2 sin(pi k_1 x_1)sin(pi k_2 x_2).
The labeled symmetric wavefunction has norm 1, with the usual physical
occupation normalization. Define the finite lattice form
 V_L,m=L^2 sum_{a<b}Q_{ab}; it is exactly L^2 W_0 in sector M=m.

The odd-extension argument in Section 2 applies to any fixed pair, with
all other Fourier variables held fixed. It bounds that pair's form by
25(I+A_*m) if weights 1+sum_j |p_j|^4/2 are used. It also proves the
following stronger compact relative bound. Let P_N cut EVERY individual
particle mode to max(k_1,k_2)<=N; Q_N=I-P_N. Then uniformly in L, and also
for the continuum form on polynomials,

 V_L,m[Q_N psi] <=(42 binom(m,2)/N^2)
                      <psi,(I+A_*m)psi>.               (N.13)

Proof of the constant: in the pair-collapse fiber the weight is
b=1+(|p|^4+|q|^4+sum_remaining |p_j|^4)/2. If either collapsed variable
is high, the inverse-weight kernel is at most
2 sum_{max|p|>N}2/|p|^4<=16/N^2. If a remaining variable is high, split
the unrestricted p sum at N. The low part has at most (2N+1)^2<=9N^2
terms, each <=2/N^4, while the high part is <=8/N^2. This gives 26/N^2.
Their union is bounded by 42/N^2. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives (N.13),
then sum over the binom(m,2) pairs. Aliases merely permute q for fixed

The high projection commutes with the odd embedding and all kinetic
weights. No m-dependent claim is made uniform in m at this step.

The same estimates hold in the continuum Fourier series of odd sine

form domain of A_*m. Equivalently its diagonal restriction is a bounded

Each collapsed one-dimensional matrix entry contains four sine factors,

7. COMPLETE GIBBS CONVERGENCE ON THE COMMON FULL FOCK SPACE

First fix m. The forms q_L,m=A_L,m+c V_L,m act on the finite mode spaces.
Their eigenvalues dominate the corresponding ordered eigenvalues of
A_*m by minmax. For vectors of bounded norm and bounded A_*m form norm,
(N.13) and Cauchy-Schwarz for positive V imply uniformly

 |V_L,m[psi]-V_L,m[P_N psi]|=O_m(1/N).                 (N.15)

Here V_L,m[P_N psi]<=25 binom(m,2)<psi,(I+A_*m)psi>, and the cross term
is bounded by twice the square root of this quantity times the bound
in (N.13). The same bound holds for V_infty,m. This spells out the tail
mechanism; no uniform Sobolev trace theorem is imported.

Upper eigenvalue convergence follows by minmax using finite-mode
approximations to the first j limit eigenvectors. Such approximations
converge in the kinetic form norm, hence in q_infty,m norm. On that
finite-dimensional approximating space (N.14) is exact and A_L converges.
For the reverse inequality, take j orthonormal low eigenvectors of q_L,m
along a subsequence of bounded eigenvalues. Their A_*m energies are
bounded, so compactness gives strong convergent subsubsequences, preserving
orthonormality. Fatou gives the kinetic liminf; (N.14)-(N.15) give actual
contact-form convergence. Applying minmax to their span gives the limit
lower inequality. Thus every ordered eigenvalue converges.

Testing each eigenvector equation against finite sine polynomials also
identifies the limiting eigenvectors with those of q_infty,m: the kinetic
cross term converges coefficientwise, and the contact cross term converges
by (N.13)-(N.15) and polarization. This handles degenerate eigenspaces by
their finite-rank aggregate projectors. No choice of basis inside an exact
degeneracy changes the heat operator.

The ordered kinetic lower bounds have summable exponential tails, so
sum_{j>J}exp[-lambda_j(A_*m)] tends to zero independently of L. Finite-
rank eigenprojector convergence plus this tail proves trace-norm
convergence of the embedded unnormalized heat operators in each fixed m.
The preceding subsequence argument shows it for the entire sequence:
every subsequence has a further subsequence with the same unique limit.

To pass to whole Fock, use the same stable theta=1 free fugacity bound
as in the earlier packet. Since the contact forms conserve M and are
nonnegative, block minmax gives, for every M_* and all L,

 sum_{m>M_*}Tr exp[-q_L,m]
 <=exp(-M_*)Tr exp[-A_L+M] <=exp(2-M_*).                (N.16)

The continuum free fugacity partition has the same upper bound, by the

First retain finitely many m sectors, use their heat convergence, then
