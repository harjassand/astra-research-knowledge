COLD AUDIT — CYCLE 5 HIGHER-ORDER CO-CATALYST TOGGLES
=====================================================
Audited: ../RESULT.txt and ../verify_order_d_fixture.py
The theorem source was not edited.

Verdict
-------
I found no algebraic or all-state Foster gap provided that g_+(R) is
understood to take its supremum over b in N_0, including b=0. Under that
interpretation, the two margins, the falling-factorial tail estimate, the
finite M0 certificate, the a*u(c) corrector, and the b-case split close as
claimed. The source writes n in N and does not define whether zero is
included; this must be made explicit. If N means positive integers, the
stated R condition omits b=0 and the claimed LV<=C-cV conclusion can fail.
An exact rational counterexample to that reading is given below.

1. Exact inactive and A-weight margins
--------------------------------------
For u(c)=1/(1+Q(c)), let

  Delta_i=Q(c)-Q(c-e_i),
  Delta_i^+=Q(c+e_i)-Q(c).

Then

  u(c-e_i)-u(c)=Delta_i/[(1+Q-Delta_i)(1+Q)],
  u(c+e_i)-u(c)=-Delta_i^+/[(1+Q)(1+Q+Delta_i^+)].

Thus C deaths contribute exactly H(c), C immigration contributes -J_+(c),
and the A u(c) generator is

  (alpha+Q)u + a[-(delta+E)u + H-J_+]
  <= alpha+1-kappa_A(c)a
  <= alpha+1-kappa_*a.

Here (alpha+Q)u<=alpha+1 and J_+>=0. On a=0 the correction
W*epsilon*u(c) has coefficient

  F(c)=-(alpha+Q)u-J_+ + H=-theta(c),

including A immigration, reverse-toggle activation, and every C
immigration/death denominator change. On a>=1 its only positive correction
jumps occur at a=1 from A death or a forward toggle; their total is at most

  epsilon W u(delta+E) <= epsilon W(delta+eta_max/zeta_min),

because E<= (eta_max/zeta_min)Q. This handles arbitrary c, including the
source-order-d toggle rates eta_i a(c_i)_d.

2. Falling-factorial tail and exact finite certificate
------------------------------------------------------
For n>=d,

  (n)_d-(n-1)_d=d(n-1)_(d-1) <= d n^(d-1).

If M=max_i c_i>=4d, then both Q(c) and Q(c-e_i) are at least
zeta_min(M/2)^d for every i: a coordinate attaining M remains at least
M-1 after the decrement, and each factor in the relevant falling
factorial is >=M/2. Consequently each summand in H obeys

  mu_i c_i Delta_i/[(1+Q-Delta_i)(1+Q)]
  <= mu_max zeta_max d 2^(2d)/(zeta_min^2 M^d).

Summing over i gives a stronger constant than the displayed C_d; the
reported C_d=2^(3d+1) mu_max zeta_max d/zeta_min^2 is conservative and valid.
The extra displayed lower bound M>=4(zeta_max/zeta_min)d2^d is not needed
for this estimate, but is harmless. Also E>=rho Q. If Q>=1 and H<=
min(1/4,lambda/2), then

  theta >= Q/(1+Q)-H >=1/4,
  kappa_A >= (delta+rho Q)/(1+Q)-H >=lambda/2.

The chosen M0 therefore leaves only the finite box 0<=c_i<M0 to check.
For rational rates, all values, the cutoff comparisons, and the box
inequalities are exact rational operations. The search may be large
(M0^r), as the result itself disclaims polynomial complexity.

3. All-state drift split and K boundary
---------------------------------------
Let G_R(b)=[p(R-1)-q(1-R^-1)b]R^b. Its positive part is supported on
b<pR/q. On the b>=N region, G_R(b)<=-q(1-R^-1)bR^b/2. If a>=1, the term
aG_R(b) absorbs the O(R^b) active-correction gain and any fixed positive
multiples of a and R^b; the -kappa_*a and weighted C-death terms control
a and sum c_i. If a=0, -epsilon theta_* R^b controls R^b and the weighted
C drift controls sum c_i. For b<N, R^b is bounded; the condition
sup_{b>=0} (G_R(b))^+<kappa_*/2 combines with the A-u bound to leave a
negative linear multiple of a. The inactive correction is nonpositive,
the active correction is bounded, and the weighted C drift leaves a
negative linear multiple of sum c_i. Subtracting a small multiple of R^b
on this bounded-b region only changes the finite constant. Hence the
claimed linear drift follows, provided the b=0 endpoint is included in
g_+.

The K-indicator generator bound is correct: its positive increment occurs
only on death of the sole catalyst (rate at most max(delta,mu_max));
immigration from the origin gives -K times the immigration rate. Since
d>=2, Q(e_i)=0, and a full C_i-immigration jump at the origin equals
w_i-K, as RESULT correctly notes. Its positive part is at most
(w_i-K)^+ and is a constant-rate contribution.

4. Endpoint ambiguity and exact falsifier if N excludes zero
------------------------------------------------------------
The proof uses the inequality aG_R(b)<=g_+(R)a for every b>=0. If the
printed supremum over n in N excludes 0, it does not control G_R(0)=p(R-1).
The exact script's g0 calculation and claimed fixture value show the likely
intent is N_0; the theorem should write that explicitly.

For a concrete falsifier under the positive-integer reading, take r=1,d=2
and rational rates

  alpha=1, delta=1/2, gamma=1, mu=1/1000,
  eta=zeta=1, p=10, q=100, w=1, R=11/10, epsilon=K=1.

For c=0, theta=1 and kappa_A=1/2. For c=1, theta=5/3 and
kappa_A=1/2. For c>=2, Q=c(c-1), E=Q, and

  H(c)= (1/1000)*2c(c-1)/[(1+(c-1)(c-2))(1+c(c-1))] <=1/500.

Therefore theta(c)=1+J_+(c)-H(c)>0 and
kappa_A(c)=(1/2+Q)/(1+Q)-H(c)>=1/2-1/500>0. Thus theta_*,kappa_*>0.
For every b>=1,

  G_R(b)=[1-(100/11)b](11/10)^b<0,

so a supremum over positive b gives g_+=0<kappa_*/2. But at c=0,b=0,a>=2,
the generator is

  LV=alpha+w gamma-delta a+p(R-1)a=2+(1/2)a,

with no toggle, correction, or K-boundary terms. Meanwhile V=a+O(1).
Thus LV<=C-cV for any c>0 is impossible. Including b=0 gives
g_+(R)>=G_R(0)=1 and rejects this parameter vector, repairing the gap.

5. Exact checker and non-complex-balance fixture
------------------------------------------------
I ran the exact Fraction checker. It passed direct reaction-list versus
closed-form generator equality on 875 states; it found exact box minima
for theta and kappa_A over 0<=c_1,c_2<64; and it checked the stated g_+
and conflicting toggle ratios. These finite checks corroborate the fixture
only; the tail estimate and split above are the all-state arguments.
For the fixture, theta_*>=1, kappa_*>=1/2, and the rate ratios at the
complexes A+2C_1 and A+2C_2 force incompatible A activities 1 and 2.
Every pair is reversible, so the network is weakly reversible and is not
complex balanced (hence not detailed balanced).

Repair requested: define g_+(R)=sup_{b in N_0}(G_R(b))^+ explicitly. With
that endpoint clarified, I found no further gap in the stated proof.
