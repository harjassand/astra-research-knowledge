The primary result is the exact compatible-dilation spectral bound already
recorded there:

  ||[Phi^m(A),Phi^m(C)]||_(2,tau)
    <= 2 sqrt(min{Q_(m-1)(A),Q_(m-1)(C)}),
  Q_k(A)=integral (1-lambda^2)lambda^(2k) d mu_A(lambda).

It controls the entire Hermitian contraction range, assigns zero defect to
the fixed spectral component, and gives the uniform envelope
2 sqrt(d_(m-1))=2/sqrt(e m)+O(m^(-3/2)). The qubit universal-cloner checker
passed its Stinespring identity, marginal, Kadison-defect, spectral and band
diagnostics (largest residuals 1.01e-16 and 2.22e-16; zero bound violations).
These finite checks are transcription diagnostics; the displayed Stinespring
derivation is the proof.

The deeper attack proves two exact supporting facts. First, the fixed-point
space of a positive, trace-selfadjoint selfcompatible UCP map is an abelian
C*-algebra. The L2 spectral projection P at eigenvalue 1 is its
trace-preserving conditional expectation and is EB. With a uniform spectral
gap gamma off the fixed space,

  ||Phi^m-P||_(infinity -> 1,tau) <= (1-gamma)^m.

More generally the input-resolved spectral tail kappa_m in
revisions/cross_phase_and_fixed_point.txt gives the same EB approximation
when it vanishes uniformly. The slow-center instrument example in c05_l05
shows why P alone has no uniform no-gap error; it also supplies an EB
comparator that preserves the slow mode, so it is not a negative result for
EB lifting.

Second, for Hermitian contractions in the spectral subspace
[1-eta,1], the whole subspace is pairwise almost commuting:

  ||[A,C]||_2 <= 2 sqrt(2 eta)+4 eta.

This follows by applying the m=1 input-sensitive estimate to Phi(A),Phi(C)
and bounding the L2 drift back to A,C. The subspace projection is not CP,
and the result does not produce a single EB channel for a growing number of
slow directions. The missing step remains a dimension-free simultaneous,
CP-compatible rounding of the full power range when there is no gap or
bounded anchor/generator description.

An attributed consequence of c05_s03's finite-generator pinching theorem is
that the full map lift does hold when the Hermitian range rank is bounded by
r: substituting this sharper commutator envelope gives
beta_m=O_r(m^(-1/(2*3^(r-1)))) for fixed r (and rank one is already EB).
This retains a fixed-rank premise and is not an unrestricted result.
