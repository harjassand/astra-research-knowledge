# Exact K3 tensor-generator count for independent rank-three RM products

Status: an internal classical Hodge/representation-theoretic theorem derived below. It uses Zarhin's established classification, local quadratic-form classification, and the K3 period theorem. It imports NO new HC or KS manuscript. It counts Hodge tensor generators, not algebraic correspondences, geometric support, or computation. Priority is unverified.

## Interface and theorem

Let E be totally real with 1<=e=[E:Q]<=4. Let S_1,...,S_m be any finite list of projective complex K3 surfaces, V_i=T(S_i)(1), satisfying

    End_Hdg(V_i)=E,  dim_E V_i=3,
    Hg(direct-sum_i V_i)=product_i G_i,
    G_i=Res_(E/Q) SO(V_i,q_i).

Fix common E-identifications under which every V_i has the SAME active embedding sigma0. Write D_i=C_E^+(V_i,q_i), and let m_D be the number of indices whose quaternion algebra has E-Brauer class D.

Let H be any rational Hodge structure in the tensor category generated by the V_i whose representation of product_i G_i is FAITHFUL. Define r_min(H) as the smallest number r of actual projective complex K3 surfaces A_1,...,A_r such that H belongs to the rational tensor category generated by U_j=T(A_j)(1), permitting finite sums, tensor products, duals, direct summands, and compatible Tate twists. The A_j can be chosen by any method; they need not initially lie in the original tensor category.

Then

    r_min(H) = sum_(distinct Brauer classes D) ceil(m_D/2).

This holds for any finite m; only the distinct-embedding LEVEL-TWO application later requires m<=e. Independence, common active embedding, rank three, specified common E-identifications, and e<=4 are actual hypotheses.

The same number counts generators for the whole tensor category of the originals. Indeed the faithful representation H tensor-generates all representations of its Hodge group, so its tensor category contains every V_i. Conversely H was assumed to belong to their tensor category.

## Lower bound: every auxiliary can cover at most two labels, with the same D

Suppose H is generated by U_1,...,U_r, and let K=Hg(direct-sum_j U_j). Because H is faithful for the original product, there is a surjection

    K -> product_i G_i.

Let l=Lie(K)^der. Its coordinate projections onto the individual auxiliary derived Lie algebras are surjective; it embeds as a subdirect semisimple algebra in their product. Decompose l into rational simple ideals. Every target ideal Lie(G_i) is supplied isomorphically by exactly one of those source ideals T_i. Distinct original targets require distinct source ideals: a simple ideal's image is an ideal in the target, so it cannot surject onto two independent target ideals at once.

Every T_i projects nontrivially to at least one simple slot of an auxiliary, because l embeds in the product of all auxiliary derived algebras. Since the full projection onto that auxiliary is onto, the projected image of T_i is a normal ideal; in a simple slot it is the entire slot. Two different T_i commute, so they cannot both project onto the same nonabelian simple slot.

Zarhin says that any single auxiliary K3 has at most two rational simple derived slots. Two slots occur only for RM rank4 with square discriminant, where

    Hg(U_j)^ad = B_j times B_j,
    B_j=Res_(F_j/Q) PGL_1(C_j)

for the SAME quaternion C_j/F_j in both components. Thus one auxiliary can be assigned at most two original labels. If it is assigned two labels i,k, the slot isomorphisms and centroids give field isomorphisms phi_i,phi_k:F_j→E with

    [D_i]=phi_i([C_j]),  [D_k]=phi_k([C_j]).

The relative field automorphism alpha=phi_k phi_i^(-1) satisfies [D_k]=alpha([D_i]). Each D_i is split at the unique real embedding sigma0 and definite everywhere else. Equality of these singleton split-place sets gives sigma0 composed with alpha=sigma0, and injectivity of sigma0 implies alpha=id. Therefore D_i and D_k have the SAME specified E-Brauer class.

Choose one nonzero auxiliary projection for each T_i. This assigns all original labels to r bins of size at most two; every size-two bin is monochromatic in its quaternion Brauer class. Each class D therefore requires at least ceil(m_D/2) bins, proving

    r >= sum_D ceil(m_D/2).

Extraneous auxiliary simple slots, CM auxiliaries, larger endomorphism fields, nonsquare discriminants, and diagonal links in the joint auxiliary group do not evade this count. A nonsquare-discriminant RM rank4 derived algebra is rationally simple, not a pair of rational slots. Diagonal links merge slots and cannot create additional target ideals.

## Upper bound: pair each common-D class using an actual K3

Pair the indices separately within every class D. For a pair i,k, identify their spin groups with SL1(D) and take the E-vector space U_ik=D, acted on by left and right multiplication. Its invariant quadratic form is a(-Nrd_D), with signs of a∈E* chosen by weak approximation to make the active period plane positive and all inactive components negative definite.

The original circles have actual spin weights ±1 at their common active place; the tensor has actual circle weights2,0,0,-2. Its normalized weights are1,0,0,-1, exactly once at each extreme. Both circles are trivial at all inactive places. Thus after untwisting U_ik is K3 type, with rational dimension4e and trace signature(2,4e-2).

The joint independence assumption gives the full left/right spin product as its Hodge group, up to finite center. Its four-dimensional E representation is absolutely irreducible at each embedding, so U_ik is rationally simple with endomorphism field E and no rational(0,0) vectors.

This is the point where e<=4 is used to acquire an actual projective K3. The rational K3 lattice has dimension22 and signature(3,19). The desired complement has dimension22-4e>=6 and signature(1,21-4e). Choose its determinant and local Hasse invariants to make U_ik plus complement isometric to the rational K3 lattice. In this dimension all prescribed finite-place determinant/Hasse pairs exist, and Hilbert reciprocity gives the global compatibility. Hasse–Minkowski supplies the rational embedding. Intersect U_ik with the integral K3 lattice, and use a positive integral vector in its complement and the K3 period theorem. The resulting projective K3 A_ik has T(A_ik)(1) exactly U_ik.

For the tensor-generation statement no new algebraicity theorem is needed. As E-representations and Hodge structures,

    wedge²_E U_ik = V_i direct-sum V_k.

This E-exterior space is the rational diagonal-embedding subspace of wedge²_Q U_ik; equivalently its complexification is direct-sum_sigma wedge² U_ik,sigma. The equality follows from wedge²(A tensor B)=Sym² A direct-sum Sym² B for two spin standards. Therefore BOTH original V_i,V_k belong to the rational tensor category of this ONE actual auxiliary K3. No assertion that this inclusion is an algebraic cycle is made or needed.

Use A_ik for each pair and the original S_i for each unpaired label. This gives exactly sum_D ceil(m_D/2) actual K3 generators containing every V_i and hence H, proving the matching upper bound.

## Higher-degree boundaries

* e=5: the SAME lower bound holds, but the construction has a rank2 rational K3 complement and needs an actual embedding certificate. Without that certificate the automatic upper bound is only r_min(H)<=m, using the originals. No equality formula is asserted.
* e=6 or7: r_min(H)=m for any faithful original-product H. A two-slot auxiliary covering two targets would be rank4 over a field isomorphic to E, hence have transcendental rank4e>21. Therefore each auxiliary covers at most one original target. The originals attain m.
* For simple polarizable K3-type structures without the actual-surface rank/lattice condition, the lower bin-count remains valid, but the actual-projective-K3 upper construction/rank obstruction must retain their geometric hypotheses.

## Level-two mixed-coniveau application

When 2<=m<=e, choose any Galois orbit of ordered pairwise DISTINCT embedding m-tuples. The primitive orbit idempotent of E^(tensor m) gives a rationally simple, faithful H_O with exact normalized level2. Its copy in H^(2m)(product S_i) has Hodge coniveau m-1. The orbit descent and level proof are in MULTI_FACTOR_CARRIER_LOWER_BOUND.md.

The exact generator formula shows the number of distinct actual K3s needed to tensor-generate this cross term. In particular three independent labels need at least two generators even with common D, and two distinct-D labels need two. Thus no single auxiliary K3, no matter how constructed, can provide its one-return exterior carrier. This does not bound the actual geometric coniveau or exclude a new abelian/smooth-projective mixed return.
