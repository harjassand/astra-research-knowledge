CYCLE 3 LEAF — PHYSICAL CLOCK, COMPLEX BALANCE, AND CLASSWISE TIGHTNESS
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE for the stated fixed-rate family and strict small-gain condition; the complex-balance branch invokes cited established theorems. No novelty claim.

SCOPE AND CLAIMS

For the finite multi-catalyst family below, (i) immigration gives the exact uniform lower bound on total reaction intensity q(x)>=alpha_*; (ii) with the supplied strict small-gain weights, a boundary-corrected linear Lyapunov function proves nonexplosion and positive recurrence in physical time, and gives an explicit expected hitting-time bound to a finite set; (iii) strict margins also give an exponential Foster function and exponential-moment bounds for hitting and return to a finite set; (iv) complex balance is an independent, exactly solvable parameter subcase with product-Poisson stationary law and nonexplosion; and (v) the full state lattice for this family is irreducible, so it has no family of separate invariant classes over which uniform tightness could fail. An exact complex-balanced counterexample from the prior N77 record shows that classwise stationary tightness need not be uniform across unbounded stochastic classes in other networks.

The results below do not prove recurrence for every rate vector in this weakly reversible family. LP failure is inconclusive. No claim is made that small gain is necessary, or that the statements are novel.

1. EXACT NETWORK, PROPENSITIES, AND THE TOTAL-RATE LOWER BOUND

Let r,s be finite positive integers. The species are A_1,...,A_r and B_1,...,B_s; each k_ji is a finite integer >=1. Every rate constant alpha_j, delta_j, p_ji, q_ji, u_ji, v_ji is fixed, finite, and strictly positive. At state (a,b) in N_0^r x N_0^s, use standard falling-factorial stochastic mass action:

  0 <-> A_j                                      alpha_j, delta_j a_j
  A_j <-> A_j+B_i                                p_ji a_j, q_ji a_j b_i
  A_j+B_i <-> (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i                    u_ji a_j b_i,
                                                  v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1) b_i.

Here (n)_m=n(n-1)...(n-m+1), set to zero for n<m. The total reaction-event intensity (equivalently, total state-changing rate, summing channels even if two channels happen to give the same jump vector) is

 q(a,b)=alpha_* + sum_j delta_j a_j
          +sum_(j,i)[p_ji a_j+q_ji a_j b_i+u_ji a_j b_i
                         +v_ji(a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i],
 alpha_* := sum_j alpha_j > 0.

Every immigration channel 0->A_j is enabled at every state. Therefore

                         q(a,b) >= alpha_* for all (a,b).                 (1)

The upper bound is not uniform: q is polynomial and unbounded. The holding time at x is Exp(q(x)); hence E[H|x]=1/q(x)<=1/alpha_*, and for 0<t<alpha_*, E[exp(tH)|x]=q(x)/(q(x)-t)<=alpha_*/(alpha_*-t). These are upper bounds on waiting times; q has no finite global upper bound, so they give no positive lower bound on a holding time in high-rate states.

The complex graph is a tree with root 0, branches 0--A_j--(A_j+B_i)--((k_ji+1)A_j+B_i), and every edge is bidirected. The complexes are distinct since k_ji+1>=2 and the species coordinates identify each (j,i). Thus the network is one linkage class and weakly reversible. The vectors from 0->A_j and A_j->A_j+B_i span R^(r+s); there is no linear conservation law. In fact the entire lattice is one irreducible communicating class: from any state, if necessary immigrate one A; use A_j+B_i->A_j to remove each B_i one at a time while retaining an available catalyst; then use A_j->0 to reach the origin. From the origin, make a temporary catalyst if the target has no A, create target B counts by A_j->A_j+B_i, and adjust catalyst counts by immigration/death. Every prescribed finite path has positive probability. All exit rates are finite statewise and there is no absorbing state.

2. EXACT COMPLEX-BALANCE EQUATIONS AND PRODUCT-POISSON BRANCH

Write positive concentrations c_j for A_j and d_i for B_i. The complex-balance equations at (k_ji+1)A_j+B_i, A_j+B_i, A_j, and 0, respectively, are

  u_ji c_j d_i = v_ji c_j^(k_ji+1)d_i;
  p_ji c_j + v_ji c_j^(k_ji+1)d_i = (q_ji+u_ji)c_jd_i;
  alpha_j + sum_i q_ji c_jd_i = delta_j c_j + sum_i p_ji c_j;
  sum_j alpha_j = sum_j delta_j c_j.

All concentrations are positive. The high-complex equations imply u_ji=v_ji c_j^kji. Substitution into the middle-complex equations gives p_ji=q_ji d_i. Substitution into each A_j equation then gives alpha_j=delta_j c_j, and the zero-complex equation is redundant. Consequently the necessary and sufficient conditions for complex balance are

  c_j=alpha_j/delta_j for every j;
  p_ji/q_ji is independent of j for each fixed i (its common value is d_i);
  u_ji/v_ji=(alpha_j/delta_j)^kji for every j,i.                         (2)

Under (2), Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz, Theorem 4.1, gives the stationary distribution

  pi(a,b)= product_j [exp(-c_j)c_j^a_j/a_j!]
           product_i [exp(-d_i)d_i^b_i/b_i!].                            (3)

The whole lattice here is irreducible, so (3) is unique. Anderson–Cappelletti–Koyama–Kurtz, Theorem 3, proves that complex-balanced stochastic mass-action systems are non-explosive for every initial distribution. Thus the invariant probability, nonexplosion, and irreducibility together imply positive recurrence of this CTMC. More generally, their product-form theorem gives a normalized product-form stationary probability on each closed irreducible class; with nonexplosion, each such irreducible class is positive recurrent. Do not infer nonexplosion from a formal invariant equation alone: the cited nonexplosion result uses finite stationary jump flux, which holds for product Poisson times polynomial mass-action rates.

For this family the flux is explicitly finite. Using Poisson factorial moments and (2),

  J := sum_x pi(x)q(x)
    = 2 sum_j alpha_j + 2 sum_(j,i)[p_ji c_j + u_ji c_j d_i] < infinity.  (4)

One can therefore convert exactly between physical-time and event-epoch stationary laws, as in Section 5.

The two sufficient proof routes are distinct. For example, r=s=k_11=1 with alpha=delta=p=q=1 and u=v=2 is complex balanced at c=d=1, but its LP inequalities would require ell>w and w>2ell, which are impossible. Conversely, rates (alpha,delta,p,q,u,v)=(1,2,1,2,1,1), k=1, satisfy the strict LP with ell=w=1 (2>1 and 2>1), but are not complex balanced: immigration/death force c=1/2 whereas the high-complex equation forces c=u/v=1. Thus neither proof mechanism is equivalent to the other.

3. PHYSICAL-TIME FOSTER CERTIFICATE UNDER STRICT SMALL GAIN

Assume supplied weights ell_j,w_i>0 satisfy

  m_j := delta_j ell_j - sum_i p_ji w_i > 0       for each j,
  n_ji := q_ji w_i - k_ji u_ji ell_j > 0         for every (j,i).         (5)

Set H=sum_j alpha_j ell_j, K>max_j ell_j, A=sum_j a_j, B=sum_i b_i,
I_0=1{A=0}, and I_j=1{a=e_j}. Define the proper function

  V(a,b)=sum_j ell_j a_j + sum_i w_i b_i + K I_0.

The exact generator identity is

  LV = H - sum_j m_j a_j - sum_(j,i) n_ji a_j b_i
       -sum_(j,i) k_ji ell_j v_ji (a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i
       -K alpha_* I_0 + K sum_j delta_j I_j.                              (6)

The indicator correction is exact: LI_0=-alpha_* I_0+sum_j delta_j I_j. Immigration turns I_0 off only on the A=0 face; a death turns it on only at a=e_j. All catalytic channels leave I_0 unchanged. Since K>max ell_j,

  LV(0,b)=H-K alpha_* =:-epsilon_0 <0                                 (7)

for every b, including the unbounded catalyst-free boundary. At A=1, say a=e_j, (6) yields

  LV <= H+K delta_max - n B,
  n=min_(j,i) n_ji>0, delta_max=max_j delta_j.                            (8)

At A>=2, writing m=min_j m_j>0,

  LV <= H-mA-nAB <= H-mA-2nB.                                           (9)

The first inequality uses the complete set of all (j,i) pairs: sum_(j,i)n_ji a_jb_i >= nAB. Set

  R_B=ceil((H+K delta_max+1)/n),
  C={A=1,B<R_B} union {A>=2,mA+2nB<=H+1}.

This C is finite. Equations (7)–(9) imply LV<=-epsilon outside C, where epsilon=min(epsilon_0,1)>0. Also LV<=C_+:=H+K delta_max globally, V>=0 is proper, and every reaction jump changes V by a bounded amount (the network and k matrix are finite).

Nonexplosion is proved in physical time, not inferred from an event-chain drift. Let tau_R=inf{t:V(X_t)>=R}. Before tau_R the chain is in a finite V-sublevel set, where all rates are bounded. Stopped Dynkin gives

  E_x V(X_(T wedge tau_R)) <= V(x)+C_+ T,
  P_x(tau_R<=T) <= (V(x)+C_+ T)/R.

As R tends to infinity, escape to infinity by finite time has probability zero. Infinitely many jumps while remaining in a finite set are impossible because its rates are bounded. Hence the minimal CTMC is conservative/non-explosive.

For x outside C, stop at tau_C=inf{t>=0:X_t in C}. Stopped Dynkin and LV<=-epsilon until tau_C give

                        E_x tau_C <= V(x)/epsilon.                       (10)

Together with irreducibility and the standard countable-state CTMC Foster criterion, (6)–(10) prove positive recurrence and a unique invariant probability for every fixed positive rate vector satisfying (5). This is a rate-specific sufficient condition, not a theorem for all weakly reversible rates.

A stronger return-time statement follows from the same strict margins. By differentiation at zero, for all sufficiently small theta>0 the finite collection of quantities

  d_j(theta)=delta_j(1-exp(-theta ell_j))
                    -sum_i p_ji(exp(theta w_i)-1),
  g_ji(theta)=q_ji(1-exp(-theta w_i))
                    -u_ji(exp(theta k_ji ell_j)-1)

is strictly positive: d'_j(0)=m_j and g'_ji(0)=n_ji. This is fully effective from the displayed finite inequalities. For instance, put M=max{ell_j,w_i,k_ji ell_j}; for theta M<=1, define

  D_j=delta_j ell_j^2 + e sum_i p_ji w_i^2,
  E_ji=q_ji w_i^2 + e u_ji(k_ji ell_j)^2.

Any theta<=min{1/M, min_j m_j/D_j, min_ji n_ji/E_ji} gives d_j(theta)>=theta m_j/2 and g_ji(theta)>=theta n_ji/2 by Taylor bounds.

Let W=exp(theta V), A_theta=sum_j alpha_j(exp(theta ell_j)-1),

  h_theta=max_j delta_j[exp(theta(K-ell_j))-exp(-theta ell_j)],
  c_0=sum_j alpha_j[1-exp(-theta(K-ell_j))]>0.

On A=0, LW/W=-c_0 exactly. On A>=1, the ordinary linear increments in (6) exponentiate to

  LW/W <= A_theta -sum_j d_j(theta)a_j -sum_(j,i)g_ji(theta)a_jb_i
       -sum_(j,i) v_ji(a_j)_(k_ji+1)b_i[1-exp(-theta k_ji ell_j)]
       +h_theta 1{A=1}.                                                  (11)

The h_theta term corrects the exceptional death of the sole catalyst; no other reaction toggles I_0. Let rho_theta=min{min_j d_j(theta),min_ji g_ji(theta)}>0. Since A>=1 implies AB>=B, the two negative sums in (11) dominate rho_theta(A+B). Choose an integer R_theta large enough that

  A_theta+h_theta-rho_theta R_theta <= -c_0/2.

For C_theta={A>=1,A+B<R_theta}, (11) and the A=0 identity give

                       LW <= -(c_0/2) W  outside C_theta.                (12)

The set C_theta is finite and W is proper. Localizing the exponential supermartingale exp((c_0/2)(t wedge tau_Ctheta))W(X_(t wedge tau_Ctheta)) and using the already-proved nonexplosion gives

  E_x exp((c_0/2)tau_Ctheta) <= exp(theta V(x)),
  P_x(tau_Ctheta>t) <= exp(theta V(x)-c_0 t/2).                            (13)

There is also a return-after-one-jump bound. Let C_1 be the finite one-step successor set of C_theta, and let sigma_C^+=inf{t>=H_1:X_t in C_theta}, where H_1 is the first holding time. For any 0<c'<min(c_0/2,alpha_*),

  sup_(x in C_theta) E_x exp(c' sigma_C^+)
    <= [alpha_*/(alpha_*-c')] max{1,max_(y in C_1) exp(theta V(y))}.       (14)

Indeed, the first holding-time exponential moment is q(x)/(q(x)-c')<=alpha_*/(alpha_*-c'), and (13) applies after a jump outside C_theta. Thus small gain gives finite, explicit physical-time expected and exponential-moment bounds to a finite Foster set, not merely a jump-count statement.

Limit of (10)–(14): the constants depend on rate scale, all strict margins, weights, K, k_ji, and the chosen finite set. Feasibility alone gives no parameter-uniform clock bound. Multiplying every rate constant by gamma>0 preserves (5) for the same ell,w, multiplies L by gamma, and rescales X_gamma(t) in law as X_1(gamma t); nonzero hitting/return times scale exactly by 1/gamma. Margins can also approach zero while staying strict, which shrinks admissible theta,c and enlarges C_theta. The certificate supplies an x-dependent hitting bound V(x)/epsilon and a return bound to its stated set; by itself it is not a numerical bound to an arbitrary chosen atom or a uniform bound over initial states. An atom bound can be obtained with an additional finite-set path/minorization calculation, whose probability constants are not encoded by mere LP feasibility. LP infeasibility does not imply transience, explosion, or failure of a different Foster function.

4. EMBEDDED CHAIN / PHYSICAL-TIME STATIONARY CONVERSION

For the embedded event chain P(x,y)=q(x,y)/q(x), with q(x,y) the aggregate rate from x to y, q(x)=sum_y q(x,y) is the total channel intensity above. If a non-explosive CTMC has invariant probability pi and finite stationary jump flux

                              J=sum_x pi(x)q(x)<infinity,

then the stationary law seen at event epochs is

                              mu(x)=pi(x)q(x)/J.                           (15)

Global balance verifies mu P=mu. Conversely, if P has invariant probability mu and q(x)>0 finite everywhere, set Z=sum_x mu(x)/q(x). If Z<infinity, then

                              pi(x)=mu(x)/(q(x)Z)                         (16)

is normalized and satisfies CTMC global balance. For this family q>=alpha_*, so 0<Z<=1/alpha_* automatically. The Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim 2020 Theorem 3.1 states the exact sufficient CTMC implication: on a reachable class, if inf q>0 and the embedded chain is positive recurrent, then the CTMC is non-explosive and positive recurrent. The rate lower bound alone is not that theorem’s recurrence premise; it cannot be used to claim recurrence or nonexplosion for an arbitrary embedded chain.

In the complex-balanced branch, (4) gives J<infinity and (15) is available directly. Under (16), Z=1/J; the mean holding time at an event-stationary epoch is 1/J. The event law upweights high-q states by q, while the physical-time law weights them back by 1/q. In the strict-small-gain branch, (12) is a geometric Foster inequality; the standard geometric Foster conclusion gives a finite exponential Lyapunov moment under its invariant law, hence finite moments of the polynomial q and applicability of (15). In either route, the conversions are justified by their stated integrability/nonexplosion assumptions; there is no silent inference from an event-count result to CTMC recurrence.

For comparison, the N76 prior card reports an event-chain upward-increment estimate for its factorial potential, while explicitly not obtaining negative Foster drift or control of the physical clock. It is not a premise of (10)–(14): those are direct rate-weighted CTMC generator bounds.

5. UNBOUNDED STOCHASTIC CLASSES AND UNIFORM TIGHTNESS BOUNDARY

The present family has one irreducible class N_0^(r+s), so its stationary law (whether (3) or the non-CB invariant law from the Foster theorem) is one probability and therefore is tight. The product-Poisson law in the CB case is a single globally tight measure. These facts do not say that classwise stationary laws are uniformly tight for a different network with infinitely many closed classes.

Here is the N77 exact mechanism, rederived from the supplied summary. With all rate constants 1, use species (A,B,C,D) and reactions

  2C -> A+B+C -> 3A+2B -> 2C,       2C <-> 2C+D.

All reactions use falling factorials. At concentrations c=(1,1,1,1), the three cycle fluxes are 1 and the two 2C/D-edge fluxes are 1, so the network is complex balanced. For each n>=0 the set

  Gamma_n={(n,0,2,d),(n+1,1,1,d),(n+3,2,0,d): d>=0}

is closed. The cycle rates at the three phases are respectively 2, n+1, and 2(n+1)(n+2)(n+3). Thus phase masses proportional to

  1/2, 1/(n+1), 1/[2(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)]

have equal directed cycle flux. At phase (n,0,2,d), D has birth rate 2 and death rate 2d, so its Poisson(1) weights satisfy detailed balance; D does not change in the other two phases. Therefore the normalized law pi_n given by these phase weights times Poisson(1) in d is stationary. The directed cycle and D birth/death moves show Gamma_n is irreducible. It is non-explosive (also by complex balance), hence positive recurrent. Since A>=n surely under pi_n, for any finite K subset N_0^4 and every n>max_{x in K} x_A, pi_n(K)=0. Therefore

                         sup_n pi_n(K^c)=1

for every finite K: the family {pi_n} is not uniformly tight, although each pi_n individually is tight. All Gamma_n lie in the same deterministic linear class B+C=2 and each contains a strictly positive state. This shows the correct distinction: componentwise positive recurrence does not yield uniform tightness across unbounded stochastic invariant classes. The current immigration/catalyst family avoids this particular obstruction because it is irreducible.

6. THEOREM SCOPE, COST, FALSIFIERS, AND PRIOR ART

Supplied inputs: finite r,s and k_ji; exact positive fixed rate constants; standard stochastic mass-action convention; for the Foster branch, weights satisfying (5). No rate oracle, simulation, or time-varying parameters. Graph properties, reachability, (1), (2), and the generator identities are derived above. The strict LP is homogeneous and rational feasibility is decidable by exact linear programming when input rates are rational; normalizing sum ell_j+sum w_i=1 and maximizing a common positive margin makes strict feasibility equivalent to optimum>0. Building the LP is O(r+s+rs) rows/variables up to constants. This recognizes only this sufficient Foster certificate. The theta, C, and return constants can be poorly conditioned as margins shrink; no useful uniform complexity or parameter-independent bound is claimed. Product-form CB checking reduces to the finite equalities in (2).

Falsifiers: a statewise transition check violating (6), any purported feasible strict weights for which the finite-set drift conclusion fails, or an admissible LP-feasible rate vector with a rigorous explosion/non-positive-recurrence proof. For the complex-balance algebra, a positive equilibrium violating one of (2), or a rate vector satisfying (2) for which the proposed Poisson law fails global balance. For uniform tightness, a compact containing uniformly positive mass under all displayed pi_n would contradict A>=n.
