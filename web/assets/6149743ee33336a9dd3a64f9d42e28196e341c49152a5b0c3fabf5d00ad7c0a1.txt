INTEGRATING THE ENTIRE DISTURBANCE ORBIT
Root derivation, Cycle06. Exact analytic lemma; not an optimality claim.

1. Abstract entropy-to-disturbance transfer

Let Phi be a finite-dimensional CPTP map fixing faithful sigma, and let
Psi be one fixed CPTP map. Write P_t=exp[t(Phi-I)] in the Schrodinger picture,
D(rho)=D(rho||sigma), J(rho)=Tr[(rho-Phi rho)(logrho-logsigma)],
e(rho)=(1/2)||rho-Psi rho||_1 and b(rho)=(1/2)||rho-Phi rho||_1.
Suppose a constant A>=0 satisfies e(rho)^2<=A J(rho) for every faithful rho.
Then for every density rho,
                         e(rho)^3 <= 6 A D(rho) b(rho).
The same map Psi is used at every time and every input. No minimization
over maps inside the time integral is permitted.

Proof for faithful rho. The Poisson semigroup is CPTP and leaves sigma
fixed. Its exact entropy derivative is dD(P_t rho)/dt=-J(P_t rho).
Integrating the hypothesis and using nonnegativity of relative entropy,
 integral_0^infty e(P_t rho)^2 dt <= A D(rho).
The identity P_t rho-rho=integral_0^t P_s(Phi-I)rho ds and trace contraction
give T(P_t rho,rho)<=t b. Since Psi is CPTP, e is 2-Lipschitz in half-trace
distance. Therefore e(P_t rho)>=(e(rho)-2tb)_+.
For b>0,
 integral_0^infty (e-2tb)_+^2 dt=e^3/(6b).
Combining the two integrals proves the bound. If b=0, the orbit is constant,
so finite entropy integral forces e=0 directly. Singular rho follows by
rho_epsilon=(1-epsilon)rho+epsilon sigma and continuity in finite dimension.
The hypothesis itself forces Psi(sigma)=sigma, since J(sigma)=0.

This integrates the whole cone beneath the orbit, rather than estimating
at one time or minimizing a uniform-time average. An inequality proved at
just the original rho, rather than every orbit state, would not suffice.

2. Ordered weighted comparator consequence

Suppose Phi is additionally KMS symmetric and Psi has weighted implementation
T_Psi with a true trace-energy gate e^2<=c E_Psi. If the physical quadratic
forms obey E_Psi<=C E_Phi and genuine entropy production obeys J>=k E_Phi,
uniformly over the same states, then A=cC/k and
                         e^3 <= (6cC/k) D b.
This is conditional on the genuine entropy inequality, not a replacement
of it by a false bilinear or likelihood-root expression. If references are
included, every premise must hold on that reference class. A qubit theorem
does not automatically extend to an entangled quantum reference.

3. Unconditional tracial reversible improvement

For bistochastic reversible Phi and bistochastic CPTP Psi with the physical
Dirichlet comparison E_Psi<=C E_Phi, the known exact gates are
e^2<=2E_Psi and J>=4E_Phi. Thus
                         e^3 <= 3 C D(rho||I/d) b.
This sharpens the previous internally proved 27C/8 coefficient without
changing any scope; the older bound remains true. The scalar trace gate
follows from Jensen sqrt(Psi rho)>=Psi(sqrt rho), root-fidelity trace
monotonicity, and the half-trace/fidelity inequality. Strong entropy
regularity follows from the paired-modular-Kraus proof in
tensor_frame_sol/cycle06_kms_endpoint/COMPLETE_FOUR_CLASSIFICATION_BASELINE.txt;
modular covariance is automatic at a tracial reference. For the already
proved self-compatible C4 comparator, this gives e^3<=12 D b.
The weighted comparator must be a physical bistochastic channel, not just
an abstract positive superoperator. The prior construction supplies it.

then a stationary self-compatible KMS qubit Phi and its one canonical C4 EB
comparator Psi have c=1+1/sqrt2, C=4, k=2. Consequently
                 e^3 <=12(1+1/sqrt2) D(rho||sigma) b.
The canonical scalar trace gate is independently proved and audited in

Even after that premise closes, this is not a theorem for arbitrary raw
broadcasters, arbitrary quantum references, or optimized observational

best possible constant and external/formal validation are not asserted.
