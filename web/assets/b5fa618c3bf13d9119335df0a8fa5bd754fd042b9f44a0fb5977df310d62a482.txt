2. EXACT IMPORT INTERFACES

DIRECT Theorem 1.1, p1, equation (1.1): For all n,m>=1, A in M_n(C), d>=0 and B_0,...,B_d in M_m(C), put P(z)=sum B_k z^k and P[A]=sum A^k tensor B_k (base factor first). Then
  ||P[A]|| <= 2 max_{z in W(A)} ||P(z)||.
Euclidean/Hilbert tensor operator norm. No normality, diagonalizability, interior assumption on W(A), degree restriction, or relation between n and m. Point/segment numerical ranges included.
Exact short quotation p1: "The constant two cannot be decreased uniformly over n,m, and d."

STRUCTURAL Theorem 1.2, p2: Omega bounded, open, convex, regular real-analytic Jordan boundary; finite A, W(A) subset Omega; interior conformal f:Omega -> unit disk with continuation across boundary. For T=f(A), define
  kappa^2=min{tau: exists H=H*, I<=H<=tau I, T*HT<=H}.
Strict feasibility, attained minimum 1<=kappa^2<=4. Every minimizing H gives S=H^(1/2), A'=SAS^-1, D=Sf(A)S^-1=f(A'), D*D<=I, spectral radius D<1, ||S||||S^-1||=kappa<=2.

STRUCTURAL Theorem 1.3 p2 / Proposition 2.1 pp5-6: Same fixed minimizing S, continuous Lambda:Tcircle -> M_n(C), Lambda(t)>=0, integral Lambda d angular=I, such that for all finite m>=1 and every M_m-valued v holomorphic on a neighborhood of closure Omega,
  v[A'] = integral Lambda(t) tensor v(G(t)) d angular(t),
  ||v[A]|| <= kappa max_{closure Omega} ||v||.
G is exterior conformal coordinate. Density Lambda=j(t)Phi_D(f(G(t))) with j(t)=t G'(t) f'(G(t))/f(G(t))>0 and Phi_D(zeta)=(I-D/zeta)^-1+[(I-D/zeta)^-1]*-I. Same S/Lambda independent of m and v. Exact short quotation p2: "The same S,Λ, and κ work for all m and v."
This theorem supplies an existence claim and a finite SDP interface; it supplies no runtime, bit-precision, conformal-map acquisition or stable numerical implementation bounds.

STRUCTURAL Corollary 6.3 pp16-18: Every bounded A on arbitrary nonzero complex Hilbert H, no separability assumption, K=closure W(A), finite m, all matrix polynomials satisfy ||P[A]||<=2 sup_W(A)||P||=2 max_K||P||. Every finite M_m-valued F holomorphic on an open neighborhood of K satisfies ||F[A]||<=2 max_K||F||; rational matrices with poles outside K included. K complete 2-spectral set. Structural similarity/common continuous density asserted only for finite matrix + strict analytic enclosing domain, not on degenerate W(A) or directly for infinite-dimensional A.

3. DIRECT LOAD-BEARING CHAIN, RECONSTRUCTED

Theorem 2.1 pp3-4: exterior h univalent on |lambda|>r including infinity, simple pole h(lambda)=c lambda+c0+O(lambda^-1), r<1, h maps |lambda|>1 onto complement closure Omega. q=lambda h'. Expansion q/(h-z)=sum b_k(z)lambda^-k gives b0=1, degree k and leading c^-k. Divided difference H=(h(lambda)-h(mu))/(lambda-mu) has no zeros on reciprocal bidisk: injectivity away diagonal, h' nonzero on diagonal, c at either infinity. Hence
  s=q(lambda)/(h(lambda)-h(mu))-lambda/(lambda-mu)
   =lambda partial_lambda H/H=sum_{k,j>=1}s_kj lambda^-k mu^-j.
Its absence of pure/constant powers gives BOTH circle marginals of K=1+s+conjugate(s) equal to 1. Convex supporting-line condition and 2Re[lambda/(lambda-mu)]=1 imply K=2Re[q/(h(lambda)-h(mu))]>=0 off diagonal; continuity extends positivity. Mixed coefficients need not be symmetric.

Proposition 2.2 p5: K is a doubly normalized positive kernel, so Jensen/Cauchy-Schwarz + two marginals makes its L2 operator contractive. For G=sum b_k C_k, its constant, positive and negative Fourier parts are disjoint, and the negative part is K applied to positive part. Therefore
  sum_{k>=0}|C_k|_HS^2 <= ||G o h||_L2,HS^2
    <= |C0|_HS^2+2 sum_{k>=1}|C_k|_HS^2.
This extra coefficient geometry goes beyond the older abstract Crouzeix-Palencia lemma; merely reusing that lemma cannot sharpen its constant (see RS2018 below).

Theorem 3.1 p6: R=q(hI-A)^-1=sum b_k(A)lambda^-k, absolutely uniformly convergent on circle since spectrum A strictly separated inside Omega. Numerical-range supporting line proves R+R*>=0 by congruence with B=hI-A. Constant coefficient R0=I; no diagonalizability needed.

Theorem 3.2 pp6-8: normalize boundary ||F||<=1; gamma=||F[A]||. Choose unit singular pair F[A]x=gamma y, F[A]*y=gamma x. Write x=sum Xe_i tensor e_i, y=sum Ye_i tensor e_i. Set P=X*RX, Q=Y*RY, M=Y*RX, L=X*RY; a^2=sum |M_k|_HS^2>0 (test off-diagonal functional on F). Direct tensor calculation gives y*G[A]x=sum_{k,i,j}(M_k)_ij(C_k)_ij, a bilinear entry pairing, bounded by a times coefficient l2 norm.
Ordered product evaluations preserve NONCOMMUTING coefficients: (FG)[A]=F[A]G[A] and (GF)[A]=G[A]F[A]. Singular relations yield gamma x*G[A]x=y*(FG)[A]x and gamma y*G[A]y=y*(GF)[A]x. Apply the preceding lower coefficient estimate to FG/GF, boundary multiplier bound in its respective order, then upper estimate to G. Weighted dual tests (entrywise conjugate P0, Pk/2; same Q) yield
  |P0|^2+(1/2)sum_{k>=1}|Pk|^2 <= a^2/gamma^2,
  |Q0|^2+(1/2)sum_{k>=1}|Qk|^2 <= a^2/gamma^2.
No coefficient commutativity invoked. p8 visual inspection confirmed the bars in these tests.

Parseval and P0=P0*, Q0=Q0*, L0*=M0 give
  ||P+P*||_L2^2, ||Q+Q*||_L2^2 <=4a^2/gamma^2,
  ||M+L*||_L2^2=4|M0|^2+sum_{k>=1}(|Mk|^2+|Lk|^2)>=a^2.
Compression Delta=[X Y]*(R+R*)[X Y]>=0; J=diag(I,-I), JDelta J>=0. Then nonnegative trace of Delta^(1/2)(JDelta J)Delta^(1/2) gives 2|cross|^2<=|diag1|^2+|diag2|^2 pointwise. Integrate: 2a^2<=8a^2/gamma^2; a>0 implies gamma<=2.

Outer approximation pp9-10 uses support-exponential strictly convex analytic sublevels, valid for any nonempty compact convex K, including point/segment. Uniform continuity shrinks maxima to W(A). Exterior collar pp10-11 uses classical reflection and compactness to obtain global injectivity on a small collar. No obvious flaw found in these inspected reductions; a complete independent audit of all analytic continuation/Fourier steps remains unperformed.
