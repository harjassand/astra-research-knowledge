Falsifier and adversarial check

Claim under pressure: “The same-graph noncommutative lift of any commutative ABP is zero exactly when the commutative ABP output is zero.”

Exact counterexample. Use two paths labeled xy and -yx. In the commutative ring, p=xy-yx=0. In the free algebra, q=xy-yx is a nonzero formal polynomial. For every field F, the d=2 substitution x=E_12, y=E_21 gives q=E_11-E_22, which is invertible (in characteristic 2 it is I_2). Therefore NC PIT correctly reports q nonzero while commutative PIT should report p zero. The mismatch is exact and has no precision or probabilistic component.

The ROABP theorem has a precise falsifier: find a fixed-order instance with p=0 but a nonzero lifted coefficient, or two distinct subsets S,T of layers that map to the same free word or the same commutative monomial after homogenization. The proof in PROOF.txt A rules this out because the word and monomial are both indexed injectively by S.

The rank gadget has a precise falsifier: at x_i=z=0, a=E_12, b=E_21, produce a singular L(A) for some field or ABP. The calculation in PROOF.txt C gives a universally invertible Schur complement and a unitriangular upper-left block, so such a falsifier cannot occur if the gadget is assembled with the stated three branches.

Bounded comparison and costs

- Positive baseline: fixed-order ROABP, explicit graph of size S, deterministic NC-ABP PIT, polynomial time over F_2, same graph size, no blow-up.
- Adversarial input: general two-path ABP for xy-yx, constant-size graph, free lift has two words and commutative output has zero coefficients.
- Rank comparison: for any ABP p, compare commutative rank m versus m+1 with the d=2 blow-up rank 2(m+1). No experiment was run; both are exact symbolic derivations. No claim is made about performance of an implementation.

The next falsifiable extension is to identify a larger commutative circuit class with a polynomial-size canonical ordered-word representation. A proposed class must supply a map p↦q with q=0 iff p=0, not merely ab(q)=p, and must charge graph expansion, coefficient encoding, matrix dimension, response size, and exact arithmetic.
