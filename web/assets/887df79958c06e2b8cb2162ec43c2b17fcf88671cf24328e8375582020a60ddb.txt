INDEPENDENT ADVERSARIAL AUDIT: BOSON COLLISION ROBUSTNESS
8 October 2026. Scope: d>=3 RESULT.txt and Sol's d=2 PROOF.txt.
Status: no gap found in the audited arguments under their stated hypotheses;
this is an internal mathematical review, not formal or external validation.

1. D>=3 COMMUTATOR CORRECTION

For diagonal F and any matrix K,
  [F,[K,F]]_{uv}=-(K)_{uv}(F(u)-F(v))^2, u!=v,
  [F,[K,F]]_{uu}=0.
For a labeled coordinate hop K_uv=-J, the off-diagonal entry is
J(F(u)-F(v))^2. Thus the previous diagonal-entry formula was false;
RESULT.txt now states the zero diagonal and off-diagonal entries explicitly.
For the positive free heat-kernel block rho, PSD gives
0<=rho(v,u)<=sqrt(rho(u,u)rho(v,v))<=(rho(u,u)+rho(v,v))/2.
Inserting this into Dir=(1/2)Tr rho[F,[K,F]] gives the stated
unordered-edge bound with coefficient J/2. This is a trace positivity
argument; [F,[K,F]] need not itself be positive as an operator.
The form identity Tr rho F K F=Tr rho F^2 K+Dir follows from [rho,K]=0.

The local graph count and observable estimate are consistent: a crossing
hop has an allowed endpoint in Q_(R+1), each configuration has at most 2dm
coordinate hops, and the labeled multiplicities are exactly the occupation
factorial multiplicities used in the Wick bound. The energy/entropy and
partition-comparison steps are otherwise unchanged. The finite script
check_form_identity.py was rerun after the correction: all 24 cases
(L=2,3,4; d=2; m=0,1,2,3; R=0,1) pass, covering 10,548 diagonal and
90,384 directed-edge checks. This is only finite algebra/count evidence,
not proof validation.

2. D=2 FILTER/THERMAL AUDIT

I independently reconstructed §§1--9 of
outputs/research/sol_boson_2d/PROOF.txt. No gap was found given its exact
assumptions: finite square Dirichlet box, fixed R and compact lambda in
[-B,0], beta=tau L^2, positive closed form sum, and zero form on the finite
allowed subspace P_R. In particular:

- The sine lower/upper bounds, geometric-mode moments, single fugacity
  tilt, and two-point Wick estimate have the stated constants. The close-
  pair count is O(L^3), while the local pair density is O(L^-4), giving
  delta and q_M of order L^-1 for ell=floor(sqrt L).
- F_m=product f(x_a-x_b) has range exactly P_R once ell>=3R: it vanishes
  on every forbidden configuration and is strictly positive on every
  allowed basis configuration. The discrete shell estimate gives
  C_f<=128/log(ell/R), without assuming discrete harmonicity.
- For the d=2 convention K_m=sum_a h_L^(a), a directed hop has K_XY=-2J.
  The exact identity is
  [F,[F,K_m]]_XY=(F_X-F_Y)^2 K_XY, with zero diagonal, and
  Tr rho F K F=Tr rho F^2K-(1/2)Tr rho[F,[F,K]].
  Therefore the Dir term is J times the sum over directed hops of the
  positive kernel entry r_m(Y,X) times Delta F squared. The product
  difference costs exactly one factor m-1; the exponential number tilt
  absorbs it. The symmetrized labeled diagonal r_m(X,X)=p(n)/w(n), so
  summing labeled representatives cancels w(n) exactly, including repeated
  sites. Wick's pair density and the lattice capacity then give
  beta Dir<=512 C_theta N_theta^2/log(ell/R). No boundary killing term is
  missing: it is diagonal and commutes with F.
- The binary-filter entropy inequality is valid after finite number-sector
  truncation and summation. F,G commute with M, Tr omega M=q_M/delta is
  finite, and the finite-mode number reference at s=log(S+1) bounds the
  conditional entropy by s Tr omega M+log Z_M(s). This handles the full
  boson Fock space without a dimension-dependent continuity inequality.
- For unbounded W, H_W>=H_0 and compact resolvent follows from compactness
  of H_0's form embedding. Half-temperature minmax gives a finite thermal
  energy; summing the nonnegative H_0 and w form values in an H_W eigenbasis
  justifies D(rho_W||rho_0)=log(Z_0/Z_W)-beta E_W w. Minmax against the
  legal P_R trial subspace gives Z_W>=Z_P, and the Gibbs variational bound
  on sigma gives log(Z_0/Z_P)<=D(sigma||rho_0). No operator-monotonicity
  shortcut is used.
- The collective EB channel is fixed by total number for the free common-
  lambda family. For a finite overflow version, compare interacting input
  and free input through the channel by contractivity; the only tail term
  needed is the free total-number tail. It is not necessary to bound the
  interacting tail independently.

The d>=3 and d=2 derivations are internally consistent candidates under
separate stated interfaces. The d=2 estimate is O(1/log L) in relative
entropy and O(1/sqrt(log L)) in trace norm; it is not claimed optimal.

formal verification, or novelty. Priority of the precise full-state
