Cycle 10: primary-prior and consequence audit for the finite-tracial sampler
Date: 2026-10-09
Scope: historical/interface audit only. The target theorem is taken as the supplied premise; this packet does not reproduce its proof and does not inspect the current reviewer proofs.

Target interface
----------------
For every finite von Neumann algebra (M,tau), finite unit L2(M,tau) families (a_x)_x and (b_y)_y, prescribed local left/right POVMs, and arbitrary finite joint distribution nu on pairs z=(x,y), put

    D = E_{z~nu} ||a_x-b_y||_2^2.

The claimed sampler uses one input-independent resource in a finite matrix amplification of M^{tensor N}, and complete all-input-commuting POVMs, to realize the target tables on a_x with mean half-total-variation error at most C D^(1/4)+zeta. Resource size N and finite label sets may depend on the finite family and zeta, but not on the realized input. The distinction from a fixed-H trace-preserving output channel or a matrix density is part of the interface.

Primary-prior finding
---------------------
The finite-dimensional core is already explicit. Dinur, Steurer, and Vidick (DSV), Lemma 17 in arXiv:1310.4113v2 (2015), give quantum correlated sampling: from separate classical descriptions of close bipartite pure states, two noncommunicating parties locally act on an input-independent embezzling state and generate a joint state close to the target. Their stated modulus is weaker, O(max{resource-accuracy^(1/12), state-distance^(1/6)}), and is not the family-averaged D^(1/4) claim.

Zhao Song, “Parallel Repetition for Entangled Games with Gap Exponent Three,” Preprints.org v1, posted 2026-08-12, Lemma 4, gives the closer finite-dimensional prior. For a finite set Z, arbitrary probability nu, and unit vectors psi_z,phi_z in C^d tensor C^d, with D=E||psi_z-phi_z||^2, one finite shared state Omega independent of realized z and local channels indexed separately by psi and phi give E[1-<psi_z|rho_z|psi_z>] <= C_sm(h^2+D/h^2+D)+zeta. Optimizing h gives mean trace-norm error O(D^(1/4))+zeta. The target's mean half-TV table error follows in the matrix case by POVM contractivity of trace distance. Under vec(a)=(a tensor I)|ME>, the unit L2 vectors of (M_d,Tr/d) are precisely the finite-dimensional standard-form pure vectors; left/right POVMs act on the two tensor legs. Thus the target is not a new finite-dimensional correlated-sampling primitive. Song's posting page says v1 was posted 12 August 2026 and “This version is not peer-reviewed”; this audit treats it as a prior claim, not as independent proof validation.

In the primary sources checked for this task, I found no theorem with the full arbitrary-finite-(M,tau) quantifier and the exact internal resource/output interface above. This is a scoped negative search finding, not an exhaustive novelty proof. The source distinction that matters is not merely the word “tracial”: DSV and Song state finite-dimensional Hilbert-space protocols; the target asks for one resource assembled inside finite matrix amplifications of arbitrary M tensor powers and valid complete POVMs for all finite input descriptions, including finite factors without matrix microstates.

Mechanism in the closest prior
------------------------------
Song's soft-label mechanism replaces hard logarithmic spectral bins by raised-cosine labels with support on two adjacent bins. A Schmidt-coefficient filter approximately restores amplitudes; an exact synchronization identity controls unequal keep/timeout events in repeated trials. The scalar label variation is quadratic at scale h, producing the h^2 plus D/h^2 average-infidelity balance. This explains why the family-averaged exponent improves over the older DSV correlated-sampling exponent. In this packet that is a primary-source description, not a second derivation of the sampler. The supplied arbitrary-M result is treated as acquired; no matrix microstate approximation is asserted or needed by this audit.

Adjacent operator-algebra results do not state the same theorem:

* Lin, “Tracially embeddable strategies: Lifting MIP* tricks to MIPco,” arXiv:2304.01940v3 (2025), Theorem 3.2, proves that the l1-closure of tracially embeddable correlations is the commuting-operator correlation set. This is approximation of finite correlation tables, not exact realization in one finite trace algebra and not a common local sampler for a finite family. The paper itself distinguishes the closure statement from whether the tracially embeddable set is closed.
* Vidick's almost-synchronous theorem and the Marrakchi–de la Salle / Lin operator-algebraic versions start from an aggregate game-level diagonal-agreement condition. Their output is a convex mixture or a synchronous strategy. This does not supply the target's arbitrary pair-family distance D and its input-independent state-preparation protocol.
* Culf, “Almost perfect strategies for projection games are approximately tracial,” arXiv:2603.14746v1 (2026), already treats almost-perfect projection-game strategies in both quantum and commuting-operator models, with a tracial strategy and an O((L epsilon)^(1/4)) loss. This closes the obvious “use the sampler to tracialize projection games” novelty route for that class.
* Van Luijk, Stottmeister, Werner, and Wilming, arXiv:2401.07299v4 (2025), study embezzlement in von Neumann algebras. Their catalyst task requires producing a target while perturbing the shared resource arbitrarily little; their abstract says embezzling states can occur only in type III, while finite/semi-finite factors do not permit nontrivial embezzlement. The target sampler does not promise catalyst return or small disturbance, so it neither contradicts nor follows from that classification.

Consequence screen
------------------
1. The only high-upside result that passes an exact-interface screen is the sampler itself: a smooth operational correlated-sampling theorem for arbitrary finite tracial von Neumann algebras, extending the finite-dimensional DSV/Song statement without assuming matrix microstates. Its mathematical significance is a genuine finite-trace local-simulation primitive. The principal falsifier for a novelty claim is a primary theorem already asserting the same all-finite-M, finite-M^{tensor N}, complete-POVM and mean-D^(1/4) interface; none was found in the checked sources. A separate technical falsifier would be a single finite M and finite family for which the internal complete-POVM construction fails despite the stated hypotheses.

2. I found no independent downstream theorem beyond the parallel-repetition application that follows from the sampler alone with compatible premises. The nearest tempting route is to upgrade robustness or rounding for general commuting strategies through finite-tracial models. Lin already gives the closure reduction and a substantial commuting-model synchrony-rounding result; Culf gives the newer almost-perfect projection-game tracialization theorem. A novel strengthening would need an additional explicit premise (for example a quantitative, family-uniform state-description coupling not contained in those hypotheses) and a proof that it yields a stronger endpoint. Early falsifier: a proposed consequence that only changes the correlation by fixed finite-table l1 approximation is already within Lin's closure theorem; a claim of a returned, nearly unchanged catalyst is ruled out as a consequence by the finite/semi-finite embezzlement distinction. No stronger candidate is supported here.

Thus the critical missing lemma for any proposed independent consequence is not another sampling estimate: it is an application-specific theorem that constructs the required paired L2 state descriptions, with a family-uniform D bound from that application's hypotheses, and then shows the sampler's output error is the right metric for the claimed endpoint. Without that coupling, importing the sampler into a robustness, rigidity, or operator-algebra theorem is only an analogy. A fast falsifier is to test the proposed interface on an arbitrary finite family with small mean D but no available conditional-state coupling; if the argument silently assumes a shared pointwise state or a common reference state, it does not follow from the sampler statement.

Charged costs and limits
-----------------------
The finite-dimensional Song result allows shared-resource and label-range size to depend on the finite family, nu, h, and zeta; it is not a dimension-free resource bound. Lin's result is closure, so its approximant can depend on the correlation and tolerance. The embezzlement theorem concerns resource disturbance and cannot be used as a synonym for state preparation. The prior search was targeted, not exhaustive. The arbitrary-M sampler is recorded as an internally acquired target premise only; this packet does not independently certify it.
