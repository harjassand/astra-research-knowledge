# Effective N33 permanence: fixed-family gate restored

I rebuilt the missing fixed-family activity-cutoff argument as an exact finite procedure and implemented it in [fixed_family_activity.py](fixed_family_activity.py). It accepts a supplied rational finite affine family and the actual finite rational comparison diagram, computes E_D(r) exactly at each rational slope, and either returns a dyadic scale cutoff or an exact persistent bad-active-point certificate. It uses rational LP basis enumeration and sparse-polynomial leading signs; no QE or arbitrary-margin oracle is called.

The checker correctly rejects the N50 plateau fixture as an affine-activity family. At x=sqrt(h), the slope-one term is active at exponent p=1/2, exactly distance E_D(1)=1/2 from its slope. The tool outputs this tie-inclusive bad event. This distinction matters: that plateau remains usable by its direct drift certificate, but does not discharge the affine-activity gate.

The all-input compiler remains **unproved**. It still needs a rational finite-family existence theorem specialized to E_D, plus a terminating rational-family search. A prior package summary asserts a joint rationalization argument, but the cited reconstruction and critic audit are missing from the checkout. Preserving the zero-product strata is necessary because E_D jumps on rational comparison hyperplanes; furthermore, exponent perturbations have boundary layers such as h^((beta+1/n)-beta)=e^-1 at h=e^-n, so ordinary compactness or continuity of fixed-parameter active limits does not establish robustness. This attacks an unsupported proof shortcut, not the possible theorem.

Conditional on the missing family theorem, enumeration plus the checker gives a semidecision procedure on promised rational endotactic inputs. Once a family is accepted, the finite margin H, rate cutoff, class-representative scale condition, and final rational absorber are computable exactly. The absorber is uniform over every initial point in the positive class and over measurable rates; a finite entry-time bound is computed per initial point and cannot be uniform over an unbounded class. The fixed-family cost is exponential in dimension through basis enumeration, with potentially large rational exponent LCMs and coefficient heights; no useful complexity bound is established.
