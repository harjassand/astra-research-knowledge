1. Pair tensor and transformed graph
------------------------------------
For q states and one gap repeated twice, the factorial expansion is exactly
1+delta(A,C)delta(B,D). Let T be orthogonal with first row q^(-1/2)1 and
apply it to each port. Equality is preserved by TT^T=I; the all-ones
four-port tensor becomes q^2 times the all-zero pin. For every doubled label,
prewire the two start ports and the two end ports. Under those wires the two
terms give 1 and q^2 1[x=y=0], hence M_q. This prewiring is legitimate for
all statuses: in the constant all-zero term the wire equalities are already
true, and in the equality term they are exactly the tensor constraints.

A singleton tensor contributes q and pins its two ports; the two endpoint
vectors contribute q total. A forced identity term is the constant tensor,
so it pins both wires with factor q^2. A forced swap term supplies the two
wires only. All pins agree. This yields the exact conditional partition
identity W_{q,h}=q^(1+s+2i) Z_{M_q}(G_h;pins=0). The wire graph G_h is not the
degree-two potential equality graph used in the component tail proof.

2. q-color correction and tail
------------------------------
The bond expansion gives W_q=sum_sigma q^kappa=sum_u prod c_{i,d,g}!,
whereas the row denominator is retained in B_q(sigma). Uniform independent
colors on each component have origin totals R_1,...,R_q, giving

  B_q(sigma)=q^kappa (M_{m,q}/m!) g_q(sigma),
  g_q=E[(m!/prod_i R_i!)/M_{m,q}].

The multinomial type bound lower-bounds the ratio by
(m+1)^(-(q-1)) exp[-mD(p||u_q)]. Since
D(p||u_q)<=q sum_i(p_i-1/q)^2 and
Var(R_i)=(q-1)q^(-2)sum_C a_C^2, one gets
E[mD]<=((q-1)/m)sum_C a_C^2<=(q-1)a_max. Jensen gives
g_q(sigma)>=(m+1)^(-(q-1))exp[-(q-1)a_max]. These inequalities have the
right direction; in particular the coefficient q-1 comes from summing the
q component variances.

The potential simple graph has degree at most two. A shared owner edge is
isolated, so any path of length L>=2 has unique edge owners. Under the
q^kappa measure, each selected-bit conditional is at most 1/2. A component
of size at least L+1 requires at least ceil(L/2) selected owner bits along
one of at most 2(m+1) paths. Therefore the same tail bound
2(m+1)2^(-L/2) holds for every q. With L0=ceil(log_2(64(m+1)^2)),
Pr[a_max<=L0]>=3/4, and 2^(-L0)>=1/[128(m+1)^2]. As ln2>1/2, this proves

  E g_q >= (3/4) 128^(-2(q-1)) (m+1)^(-5(q-1)).

The bound is polynomial only for fixed q; no uniform-in-q statement follows.
The m=0 case is handled exactly. No independence between bond bits is used.

3. Ghost-field Ising derivation and rationality
-----------------------------------------------
Replace q^2+1 by r^4, where rational r> (q^2+1)^(1/4), so r^2>q-1.
For a free binary state s=+1 for Fourier zero and s=-1 for any of the
q-1 nonzero states, an interaction factor is r^(1+s+t+st). The color
multiplicity is (q-1)^((1-s)/2). For a free vertex v the total field
coefficient is

  B_v=d_v log r - (1/2)log(q-1),

where d_v counts one per free-free edge endpoint and two per free-pinned
edge or free loop. A nonisolated free vertex has d_v>=1, so B_v>0. Free
isolates contribute q and are removed exactly. Identify all pinned vertices
with a ghost spin. A free-free interaction gives scalar r, one pair coupling
log r, and one field contribution log r at each endpoint. A free-pinned edge
and a free loop each give scalar r^2 plus field 2 log r; pinned-pinned edges
and pinned loops give r^4. Adding one ghost edge of coupling B_v per free
vertex and using global spin flip gives exactly

  Z_{M_{q,r}}=q^I(q-1)^(F/2)
    r^(E_FF+2E_FP+4E_PP+2L_F+4L_P) Z_Ising(H;0)/2.

Every Ising coupling is positive. In high-temperature form, pair activity
for t parallel free-free interactions is (r^(2t)-1)/(r^(2t)+1), and a
ghost-edge activity is (r^(2d_v)-(q-1))/(r^(2d_v)+(q-1)); both are rational.
Although (q-1)^(F/2) alone may be irrational, for each free vertex

  sqrt(q-1) cosh(B_v)=[r^(d_v)+(q-1)r^(-d_v)]/2

is rational, so the complete partition prefactor is rational. This also
shows no irrational Fourier matrix entries are used by the algorithm.

For p=(3/4)128^(-2(q-1))(m+1)^(-5(q-1)), correction estimation takes

O(m) conditional partition calls. Set their relative accuracy and failure

There is no union bound over T: samples are iid from this nearby law, and
bounded concentration is around its expectation. A bad FPRAS call still

W estimate is separately relative epsilon/8 with confidence 1-delta/4.

The number of conditional W calls is O_q(m^(5(q-1)+1) epsilon^-2
log(delta^-1)). Substitution into the source's coarse per-call arithmetic
