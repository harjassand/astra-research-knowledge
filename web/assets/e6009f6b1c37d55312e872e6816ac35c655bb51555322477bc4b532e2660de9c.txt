EXACT COUNTERINSTANCE TO AN UNCERTIFIED PHASE-MATCHING EXTENSION
For n=4q, let F be the symmetric permutation matrix of a perfect matching
whose edges each join two even indices or two odd indices. For q=2, the exact
edges are (0,2),(4,6),(1,3),(5,7); set k=n/2=4. In the full sector every
matching edge contributes exactly one endpoint to I and its partner to J.
There are 2^k legal configurations, each with determinant magnitude one, so
c_k(F)=2^k.

Apply the alternating phases used by c03_s01, gamma_i=s_i on even i and
gamma_i=i s_i on odd i, without a successful matching certificate. Every
matching edge has endpoints of the same phase type, so its skew block entry
is gamma_u-gamma_v = rho(s_u-s_v), with rho in {1,i}. The only full-size
principal Pfaffian has squared magnitude
  X(s)=product_{(u,v) in M}|s_u-s_v|^2.
It equals 4^k iff all k matched sign pairs differ, an event of probability
2^-k, and is zero otherwise. Thus E X=c_k but E[X^2]/c_k^2=2^k. For the
enumerated n=8 instance, the direct exact table has mean 16, second-moment
ratio 16, and zero probability 15/16.

This refutes any extension that uses alternating phases alone as a
polynomial-variance estimator for arbitrary dense/general matching
orientations. It does not refute c03_s01's stated *certified* subclass:
its fixed adjacent-pair acquisition has beta=(0,0,0,0) on this F and
correctly returns not accepted. The example pinpoints the additional costed
gate as matching acquisition/order, rather than a hidden defect in the local
phase identity.

EXACT POSITIVE-PREFIX FALSE-ZERO AND ROLE TESTS
On the same F, force U={0} up, with D=H=empty. This forces the orientation of
edge (0,2), leaving three matching edges free. Direct disjoint-minor
enumeration gives prefix mass 2^(k-1)=8. The prefix norm sample from the
c03_s02/c01_s03 construction is
  Y(s)=product_{(u,v) in M, (u,v)!=(0,2)} |s_u-s_v|^2.
Hence Y=64 with probability 1/8 and zero with probability 7/8; its mean is
8 and its relative second moment is 8. The stated cap is
C_ab=binom(2k-1,k-1)=35, so the exact pointwise inequality Y<=C_ab*c_prefix
holds. This is a concrete positive prefix for which a single zero draw is
common, distinguishing estimator failure/zero from a deterministic support
certificate. It is consistent with the report, which explicitly says that
positive instances can return estimator zero on the confidence-failure event.

Forcing U={0,4,1,5}, one up endpoint from each matched pair, gives the unique
positive occupation: exact prefix mass one and Y(s)=1 for every sign word.
The checker also exhausts 256 sign words for k=3 and separately checks
forced-up, forced-down, forced-empty, contradictory up/empty, and mixed
up/down prefixes. Each empirical mean equals direct exact minor enumeration;
all zero prefixes are identically zero; and every pointwise/moment cap holds.
