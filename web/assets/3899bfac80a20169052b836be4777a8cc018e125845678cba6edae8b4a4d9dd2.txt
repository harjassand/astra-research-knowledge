supports only a superpolynomial-cost global rejection implementation. At
M=.1, B=.2, N=50,000 it gives h_N=68.58, hence a worst-case proposal bound
about 1.2e30. I derived a seed-quartic/martingale-tail bound E[w^2]<=C_2 and a
seed-radius-dependent pathwise envelope g_N(R). In the ideal path law, the
initial heat seed tilted by g_N followed by acceptance w/g_N samples the

unknown target partition function. The envelope normalizer is bounded by a
quartic integral with exponent -cR^4+M R^2/16+O(BR), finite for fixed M,B.

I also replace the small prescribed diffusion radius by r_0^2=theta*x_*,
where x_* is the smaller root of (1-x)^2-Lx=0. The exact ellipticity margin
is (1-theta)(1-theta*x_*^2). Combining theta=.99 with the c07_l08 INITIAL
bound f(u)>=delta*u^4 improves the M=.1 seed exponent at N=50,000 from
kappa_0*N=.053 to 28.19, and the martingale exponent from 1,034 to 12,647.
The original published S12 error remains vacuous at N=50,000 because its

The credible finite-bit route is conditional: tabulate the one-dimensional

weak-error estimate has local defect scale O(N*dt^2), suggesting
K=O(C(M,B,X)N/epsilon), but I have not proved the stopped-boundary trace-norm
remainder or compiled the interval/random-bit path. No finite-bit sampler,
SDE trajectory, or local-qubit output was run. Therefore polynomial total
preparation is NOT established, and no practical advantage is claimed.
