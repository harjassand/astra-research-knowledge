# INITIAL — c08_l08

Status: analytic lower bound; internal derivation, imported positive-entropy disk map, novelty unknown. This is a scoped no-go for uniform finite-trajectory acquisition, not a claim that metric entropy is never estimable.

## Strongest claim

Fix a closed volume-three-manifold and the trajectory-only observation model below. Without a supplied uniform derivative/energy bound, no finite number of noisy trajectories can uniformly distinguish zero volume entropy from a fixed positive volume-entropy rate over all smooth divergence-free vector fields. More quantitatively, for every target rate \(\eta>0\), there is a fixed constant \(C_*>0\) from one local gadget and, for arbitrarily large \(M=n^3\), a family \(X_0=0,X_1,\ldots,X_M\) on the same flat three-torus such that
\[
h_\mu(\Phi^1_{X_0})=0,\qquad h_\mu(\Phi^1_{X_j})=\eta,\qquad
\int |{\rm Def}\,X_j|^2\,d\mu=C_*M\eta^2.
\]
Each field X_j is supported inside its own container U_j; the pairwise disjoint containers have normalized volume 1/(qM), and every field is smooth, divergence-free, and compactly supported. For any adaptive procedure with at most \(m\) trajectory starting points, including full exact path readout and then any common measurement-noise channel, the transcript laws obey
\[
{\rm TV}\!\left(P_{X_0},\,\frac1M\sum_{j=1}^M P_{X_j}\right)\leq \frac mM.
\]
Consequently no estimator can, uniformly over this family, estimate the entropy to error \(\eta/3\) with success probability \(2/3\) when \(m<M/3\). With a supplied normalized strain-energy cap \(B\), this construction gives a minimax lower bound \(m=\Omega(B/\eta^2)\), with a fixed gadget-dependent constant, whenever \(B/\eta^2\) is large enough. Without such a cap, the uniform finite-sample cost is unbounded.

## Observation interface

A procedure may adaptively choose at most \(m\) initial states \(x_i\), and receives a noisy record of each true trajectory \(t\mapsto\Phi_X^t(x_i)\) over any finite time interval it chooses. The noise mechanism is the same conditional on the true path for every candidate field. It may be arbitrarily fine; the lower bound already holds for noiseless full-path access. A pointwise field or derivative oracle over the whole manifold, a global energy/derivative certificate, or a continuum of simultaneous starts is outside this interface.

## Construction and derivation

1. Imported seed. Berger–Turaev prove that an area-preserving smooth disk map can be the identity near the disk boundary and have a positive-area stochastic island, hence positive Lebesgue metric entropy. Their construction is stated in “On Herman’s Positive Entropy Conjecture,” Theorem A and §3.4. I use only this existence result; I do not replay its proof or claim a finite-precision implementation.

2. Suspension to a local steady flow. Let \(f\) be that map on \(D^2\), with area form \(\omega\), and let \(K=\operatorname{supp}(f-\mathrm{id})\Subset D^2\). Form the mapping torus
\[
N_f=(D^2\times[0,1])/\big((x,1)\sim(f(x),0)\big).
\]
The form \(\omega\wedge dt\) descends because \(f\) preserves \(\omega\). Choose a smooth \(\chi:D^2\to[0,1]\) equal to one on a neighborhood of \(K\), zero near \(\partial D^2\), with its transition in the collar where \(f=\mathrm{id}\). Then \(\chi\circ f=\chi\), and \(Y=\chi(x)\partial_t\) is a smooth divergence-free vector field on \(N_f\), zero near its boundary. On the entropy-bearing island, \(Y\)'s time-one map is \(f\) on the disk coordinate and identity on the circle coordinate; its volume entropy is some fixed \(h_0>0\). The transition collar is an integrable variable-speed circle flow and contributes zero entropy. The mapping torus is a solid torus since \(f\) is the identity near the boundary and is isotopic to the identity rel boundary. Embed this solid torus in a Euclidean cube, straighten its positive volume form, and extend \(Y\) by zero. This gives one fixed compactly supported smooth divergence-free gadget on the flat three-torus. Let \(e_0=\int |{\rm Def}\,Y|^2\,d\nu_0\) for normalized volume \(\nu_0\) on its container; \(e_0<\infty\).

3. Hide rescaled copies. Partition the unit flat three-torus into \(M=n^3\) equal cubes. Choose a fixed integer \(q\) large enough that a copy of the gadget, scaled into each cube, fits in a container \(U_j\) of normalized volume \(\alpha=1/(qM)\). The field is zero in a collar of every container, so extension by zero is smooth. First scale the base gadget's speed by \(\eta/h_0\). In each \(U_j\), spatially scale lengths by \(r\asymp M^{-1/3}\) and speed it up by \(qM\):
\[
X_{j,M}(x_j+r z)=r(qM)(\eta/h_0)Y(z).
\]
The constant spatial dilation has Jacobian \(r^3\), so it preserves divergence-freeness. The time-one map inside \(U_j\) is conjugate to the \((qM)\)-th iterate of the base time-one map with speed \(\eta/h_0\). Entropy scales by the integer iterate factor \(qM\), while the invariant volume carried by \(U_j\) is \(\alpha=1/(qM)\). Entropy is zero outside the support. Thus the ambient entropy is
\[
\alpha(qM)(\eta/h_0)h_0=\eta.
\]
For the strain, spatial dilation and speed-up give \({\rm Def}\,X_{j,M}=qM(\eta/h_0)\,{\rm Def}\,Y\) in the rescaled coordinates. The normalized volume contributes \(\alpha\), hence
\[
\int |{\rm Def}\,X_{j,M}|^2d\mu
=\alpha(qM)^2(\eta/h_0)^2 e_0
=qM\frac{e_0}{h_0^2}\eta^2
=C_*M\eta^2,
\quad C_*:=q e_0/h_0^2.
\]
Every member at finite \(M\) is smooth and has finite strain energy, but the required energy grows linearly in the number of candidate locations. The spatial scale is \(\Theta(M^{-1/3})\), the speed is \(\Theta(\eta M^{2/3})\), and the derivative scale is \(\Theta(\eta M)\).

4. Indistinguishable finite transcripts. Compare \(X_0=0\) with \(X_J\), where \(J\) is uniform in \(\{1,\ldots,M\}\). Under \(X_0\), all true paths are stationary. Couple all observations with the same sensor-noise realization. Until the procedure starts a trajectory in \(U_J\), the true path under \(X_J\) is also stationary, so the noisy records and all adaptive choices agree exactly. Along the all-zero-field transcript, each of the \(m\) chosen initial points lies in at most one of the pairwise disjoint \(U_j\). Averaging over \(J\), the probability of ever hitting the active container is at most \(m/M\). The coupling inequality gives the displayed total-variation bound. For \(m<M/3\), Le Cam's bound makes the average error of any zero-versus-\(\eta\) test strictly greater than \(1/3\). An \(\eta/3\)-accurate estimator with probability at least \(2/3\) would induce a test with error at most \(1/3\), contradiction.

If the admissible class is supplied with \(\int |{\rm Def}\,X|^2d\mu\le B\), choose \(M\) on the order of \(B/(C_*\eta^2)\), rounded down to a cube \(n^3\). The same argument forces \(m\) on that order. For the audited viscous model, \(W=2\nu V\int|{\rm Def}\,X|^2d\mu\), so this family has finite sustaining power \(W=\Theta(\nu V M\eta^2)\), and a power cap implies the corresponding lower bound \(m=\Omega(W/(\nu V\eta^2))\), up to the fixed gadget constant. This is consistent with the candidate entropy-power inequality \(H^2\leq \tfrac12\int|{\rm Def}\,X|^2d\mu\); it does not challenge it.

## What this does and does not establish

This is a positive-volume metric-entropy obstruction, not a null-set horseshoe argument: the imported disk map has an area-positive island, and its suspension contributes positive normalized three-dimensional volume entropy. It shows that a trajectory-only protocol cannot infer the global entropy from a finite collection of starting points without paying for spatial coverage or a uniform regularity/energy envelope. Measurement noise does not cause the lower bound; exact trajectory data already fail on the no-hit event.

The obstruction does not imply entropy is unestimable from an asymptotically long typical orbit, does not cover a class with an explicit uniform regularity/energy bound, and does not cover direct global vector-field or derivative queries. It also does not cover dynamical noise that moves trajectories between invariant regions. The lower-bound constants depend on one imported entropy gadget; the construction is existential and does not supply a numerical value for \(h_0/e_0\) or a finite-bit compiler.

A simple separate warning for the energy route: on the flat torus, the shear \(X=\sin(2\pi y)\partial_x\) has nonzero deformation strain but zero metric entropy. Its time-\(t\) map is \((x,y,z)\mapsto(x+t\sin(2\pi y),y,z)\), whose derivative grows at most linearly in \(t\), so all Lyapunov exponents vanish. Thus sampled or even exactly integrated strain cannot by itself certify positive entropy; the power inequality gives a necessary floor, not a converse estimator.
