T24 — LOW-RANK REVERSIBLE LIFT OBSTRUCTION GATE
Frozen record v1 | 9 October 2026
Status: internally proved rank-at-most-two no-separation theorem under the finite-rank OS hypotheses. Exact reversible realization follows without using CP. This is a scoped structural obstruction to T24's counterexample search, not a theorem above rank two and not an external novelty claim.

TARGET
Determine the lowest observable OS/Hankel rank at which a stationary finite-alphabet, reflection-invariant process with positive reflected kernel and self-adjoint shift semigroup can fail to have a finite reversible CTMC realization. In particular, test whether a low-rank nonlattice cone can be the obstruction.

CONVENTIONS
Use the finite-rank OS representation of work/sol/hidden_realization/v1.txt. Thus H is a real Hilbert space generated by future cylinders; Omega=[1], ||Omega||=1; T_t=exp(-tA) is a strongly continuous self-adjoint contraction semigroup with A>=0 and A Omega=0; and the boundary symbol events give mutually orthogonal projections E_a with sum_a E_a=I. For future word a_0 at time zero, a_1 at t_1, ..., a_m at t_m, the observed law is

  P(a_0,t_1,a_1,...,t_m,a_m)
    = <Omega, E_{a_0} exp(-t_1 A) E_{a_1} ...
                  exp(-(t_m-t_{m-1})A) E_{a_m} Omega>.

The process law is determined by these cylinders and stationarity. Symbols of zero stationary probability are omitted: their event is null at every time, so their boundary projection is zero on the cylinder-generated OS space.

THEOREM (RANK AT MOST TWO CANNOT SEPARATE)
If dim(H)<=2, the process has an exact finite stationary reversible CTMC realization with deterministic emissions and at most two states. Therefore no process of OS rank at most two can witness either failure of exact finite reversible realization or failure of membership in its finite-model closure.

PROOF
Rank one: H=span{Omega}; A Omega=0 gives A=0. There is one active symbol because a nonzero boundary projection would otherwise give a second orthogonal nonzero vector E_a Omega. The process is the constant one-symbol process and is realized by the one-state generator Q=[0].

Rank two: If there were only one active output symbol, every future cylinder would be either zero or Omega, contradicting dim(H)=2. There are therefore exactly two active symbols, call them 0 and 1. Their nonzero ranges are orthogonal, sum to H, and hence each has rank one. Put

  p_a = ||E_a Omega||^2 = P(Y_0=a)>0,
  e_a = E_a Omega / sqrt(p_a),   a in {0,1}.

Then (e_0,e_1) is an orthonormal basis and Omega=sqrt(p_0)e_0+sqrt(p_1)e_1, with p_0+p_1=1. Since A is symmetric, positive semidefinite, and A Omega=0, in this basis it has only one possible nonzero eigenvalue lambda>=0:

  A = lambda (I - Omega Omega^T)
    = lambda [[p_1, -sqrt(p_0 p_1)],
               [-sqrt(p_0 p_1), p_0]].                         (1)

Define the isometry J:H -> L^2({0,1},p) by

  (Jv)(a)=<e_a,v>/sqrt(p_a),     p=(p_0,p_1).

Indeed, <Jv,Jw>_p=sum_a <e_a,v><e_a,w>=<v,w>; also J Omega=1 and J E_a = D_a J, where D_a is multiplication by 1_{state=a}. For f:{0,1}->R, direct substitution into (1) gives

  (J(-A)J^{-1} f)(a)
       = lambda (sum_b p_b f(b)-f(a)).                         (2)

Thus Q=J(-A)J^{-1} is the generator

  Q = lambda [[-p_1, p_1],
               [ p_0,-p_0]],

with off-diagonal rates q_{01}=lambda p_1 and q_{10}=lambda p_0. It is a valid CTMC generator, has stationary law p, and is reversible because p_0 q_{01}=p_1 q_{10}=lambda p_0 p_1. This also covers lambda=0 (the static two-state chain).

For every word cylinder, conjugating every factor in the OS formula by J yields

  <1, D_{a_0} exp(t_1 Q) D_{a_1} ...
           exp((t_m-t_{m-1})Q) D_{a_m} 1>_p,

which is exactly the stationary CTMC cylinder probability. Hence all finite-dimensional distributions agree, not merely one-time or pair correlations. Stationarity transfers equality from cylinders whose earliest time is zero to arbitrary finite time sets. This proves the theorem.

EXPLICIT REVERSIBLE FRAME CHECK
The same realization satisfies the frame/edge-flow criterion of work/sol/hidden_realization/v1.txt. Take hidden states i=0,1 and

  x_i=e_i/sqrt(p_i),   pi_i=p_i,   w_01=w_10=lambda p_0 p_1.

Then <Omega,x_i>=1; sum_i pi_i x_i x_i^T=I; E_a x_i=1_{a=i}x_i; and, since A x_i=lambda(x_i-Omega) and Omega=sum_j p_j x_j,

  sum_{j!=i} w_ij (x_j-x_i) = -pi_i A x_i.

Thus the exact Farkas edge-flow condition holds with a two-vertex frame. This is an all-time compatible lift, rather than a collection of separate CP factorizations.

LOW-DIMENSIONAL CONE GEOMETRY (SCOPED)
Let C be the cone generated by nonnegative future-cylinder classes. Each E_a preserves C, and c=sum_a E_a c. Therefore C is the direct sum of its cones C_a=C intersect ran(E_a). Each C_a spans its label block; under the standard pointedness of the classical positive-cylinder cone, a block of dimension one is a ray and a block of dimension two is a wedge with exactly two extreme rays. Thus, if every active label block has dimension at most two, C is simplicial and its order is a lattice order. In particular, for dim(H)<=3 with at least two active symbols, no nonlattice-cone obstruction can occur. The rank-two theorem above is stronger: it constructs the reversible chain directly.

LIMIT OF THIS GEOMETRIC OBSERVATION
Polyhedrality alone has not been shown here to imply a reversible edge-flow lift. The edge-flow criterion still has a monotone-field Farkas dual, and this record does not prove it automatic for every polyhedral cone or every compatible frame. The rank-three case with a 2+1 boundary-block split, and all ranks >=3 in general, remain open in this branch. Thus the proved result excludes rank <=2 separation and excludes a nonpolyhedral-cone mechanism when all symbol blocks have dimension <=2; it does not establish that rank three or four processes always have finite reversible models.

ALTERNATIVE OBSTRUCTION CHECK
The countable mixture of two-state reversible chains in work/theory/t13_reversible_realization/v1.txt is not a T24 answer: its OS/Hankel rank is infinite, and truncations converge in the cylinder topology. No finite-rank counterexample outside the finite-model closure is supplied here. No arbitrary finite CP table is promoted to a process-level counterexample.

CLAIM LEDGER
- OS rank <=2 implies an exact reversible CTMC with <=2 hidden states: internally derived and proved above under the stated OS assumptions.
- Consequently, rank <=2 cannot separate exact finite realization from its closure: follows immediately from the exact realization.
- If each output block has dimension <=2, the natural future cone is simplicial, assuming the classical future cone is pointed: direct geometric consequence; no reversible-lift claim follows.
- Rank 3 or higher exact/closure separation: unresolved here.
- Low-rank finite-hankel-rank process outside the closure: not constructed.
- External validation, novelty, and priority: not assessed.

NEXT DECISIVE GATE
For rank three, settle whether a 2+1 output-block split plus global reflected CP forces a finite edge-flow frame. If it does not, find exact OS data with a genuine stationary process behind them and prove every compatible frame fails the monotone-field inequality. For a nonlattice-cone route, the first possible block dimension is three, hence total OS rank at least four with at least two active labels. A failure at one selected finite truncation is not an all-time obstruction.
