1. BOUNDED HOLEVO CAPACITY AND ONE COMMON CLASSICAL RECONSTRUCTION

Main proof:
entropy_comparator_continuation/BOUNDED_CAPACITY_BROADCASTING_THEOREM.md
Independent end-to-end audit:
entropy_comparator_continuation/END_TO_END_ADVERSARIAL_AUDIT.md

For every fixed finite capacity bound C, there is a dimension- and family-size-
independent modulus f_C(b) tending to zero as b tends to zero. If an experiment
has Holevo CAPACITY at most C and admits approximate broadcasting to two outputs
with worst-case half-trace error b, a SINGLE entanglement-breaking channel
reconstructs every state in that experiment with worst-case error at most f_C(b).
Capacity means a supremum over ALL finite priors. A bound for one chosen prior
is insufficient. The theorem permits growing dimensions and arbitrary family
cardinalities, while each experiment is finite dimensional. It produces a
full-domain finite-outcome physical channel; no uniform outcome count or
computational efficiency is promised.

The proof stitches compatible smoothing, binary extraction, finite flagged
centralization, Petz reference correction, and the weighted-tracial common C4
comparator. Supporting independent interface audits and the exact pinned source
proof extracts are included. The original common-comparator proof and audit
are included as dependencies, not as a new milestone in this addendum.

The end-to-end adversarial audit found no remaining composition gap. This is
positive internal analytic AI review, not formal verification, human peer
review, or established historical priority/significance. Early conditional
language in the chain and interface reports records checking order; the main
stitched theorem, final chain update and end-to-end audit state the final
status. PRIOR_LITERATURE_AND_SIGNIFICANCE.md compares the closest inspected
primary results; no matching capacity-uniform theorem was located, while
priority remains unestablished. A separate quantitative sharpening is still
in progress and is not asserted or included in this snapshot. No diamond-norm or unrestricted-input classicalization is claimed.

3. OPERATIONAL NORM BOUNDARY

Main proof: operational_norm_obstruction/RESULT.md
Weighted comparator interface check:
operational_norm_obstruction/WEIGHTED_COMPARATOR_CROSSCHECK.md

For each fixed positive iterate n, the supremum over growing dimensions of the
diamond distance from these self-compatible channel powers to the entire EB
set is 2, its maximal value. Explicit tensor-power universal-cloner marginals
simultaneously have small, exactly determined induced Hilbert-Schmidt distance
to the EB set. Thus dimension-free HS approximation cannot be upgraded to
uniform diamond approximation by this result.

The report's primary-source caveat is essential: qualitative tensor
amplification against the full separable set was already established by
Beigi and Shor. This is positioned as an explicit useful corollary/boundary,
not a newly discovered foundational theorem. No priority claim is made for
the finite-size certificate or exact HS-distance calculation.
