1. GENERAL FIXED MAP AND EXACT ENTROPY IDENTITY

Fix arbitrary finite-dimensional kappa_BC and ANY basis {|c>} of C.
Let Delta_C be its rank-one pinching on C, let
 kappa'= (id_B tensor Delta_C)kappa,
 p_c=Tr[(I_B tensor |c><c|)kappa],
 beta_B^c=<c|kappa|c>/p_c when p_c>0.
For p_c=0 choose any density beta_B^c once from a fixed convention.
Define the FULL-DOMAIN CPTP EB map
 E_C(Y)=sum_c <c|Y|c> beta_B^c tensor |c><c|.         (1)
The selection depends only on kappa and its chosen C basis. For every
reference A and every extension rho_ABC of kappa, its actual output

      =sum_c X_A^c tensor beta_B^c tensor |c><c|,
 X_A^c=<c|rho_AC|c>.
It is separable across A:(BC), has sigma_A=rho_A and
 sigma_BC=kappa' EXACTLY. For arbitrary kappa, that BC marginal is the
DEPHASED original kappa, not kappa itself. There is no claim otherwise.

Put delta_B=I(A:C|B), delta_C=I(A:B|C), t=delta_B+delta_C and define
the four partial coherence costs
 U=D(rho||rho^C), rho^C=(id_AB tensor Delta_C)rho,
 V=D(rho_AC||(id_A tensor Delta_C)rho_AC),
 Q=D(kappa||kappa'),
 q_C=D(kappa_C||Delta_C kappa_C).
All logs are natural. Pinching gives each coherence as the corresponding
entropy increase, even for singular inputs. The exact identity is
 D(rho||sigma)=delta_C+V+Q-q_C.                     (2)

PROOF OF (2)

The classical-C product conditional form in (1) gives
 -Tr rho log sigma
 =S((id_A tensor Delta_C)rho_AC)+S(kappa')-S(Delta_C kappa_C).
This expression follows by taking each diagonal C block: its logarithm
splits into the conditional A density, conditional B density and block
weight. Those conditional marginals coincide with the corresponding
diagonal blocks of rho. Subtract S(rho) and use
 delta_C=S(rho_AC)+S(kappa)-S(kappa_C)-S(rho).
The three pinching entropy increases give exactly (2). Support is not
a hidden issue: each diagonal conditional joint AB state is supported
on the product of its A and B marginal supports, and positivity ensures
rho's full support is contained in their direct sum. Zero-weight C
blocks of rho vanish.

The other CMI has the exact identity
 U=delta_B-delta_B(rho^C)+Q<=delta_B+Q.               (3)
Here rho_AB and rho_B are unchanged by Delta_C. Insert the entropy
definition of I(A:C|B) to get (3); no invalid monotonicity on a
conditioning system is used. Also relative-entropy data processing under
Tr_B gives V<=U. Combining (2)-(3) yields
 D(rho||sigma)<=t+2Q-q_C<=t+2Q,
 T(rho,sigma)<=min(1,sqrt(t/2+Q)).                  (4)
