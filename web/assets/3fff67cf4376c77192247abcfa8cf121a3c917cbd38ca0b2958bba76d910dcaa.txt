REVISION 03 — ACTIVITY-RANGE PERSISTENCE
7 October 2026.

The activity-one auxiliary obstruction extends continuously to a full interval. For the legal auxiliary pair (e_0,e_1) with activity w>=0, the coefficient update is g_w(U)=f(U)+w f(U union {0,1}) when U avoids {0,1}, and g_w(U)=f(U) otherwise. At the fixed Rayleigh test (0,4), z_1=z_2=z_3=-1, z_5=1, the coefficientwise-square signed polynomial has grouped coefficients

  A(w)=-(55+22w+3w^2),
  B(w)=-9,
  C(w)=-(17+6w+2w^2),
  D(w)=-3.

Therefore
  Delta(w)=B(w)C(w)-A(w)D(w)=9w^2-12w-12.
Its roots are -2/3 and 2, so Delta(w)<0 for every 0<=w<2. The auxiliary family Q_{g_w} remains real stable throughout w>=0 by the supplied coordinate-pair closure theorem; thus a whole interval of valid stable auxiliary extensions has coefficientwise-square instability. At w=1 this specializes to the exact checkpoint in `../check_auxiliary_square.py`. At w>=2 this particular Rayleigh witness gives no conclusion.

This is still a representation boundary, not a no-go for all auxiliary constructions. It excludes using coordinate-pair stability closure followed by coefficientwise power as a generic stability argument on these coefficients.
