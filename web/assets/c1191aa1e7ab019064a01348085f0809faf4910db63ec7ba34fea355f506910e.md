# Rational generalized-polynomial rates: exact denominator lift and tropical order

## Result and status

For a finite family of positive rational generalized-polynomial rates
\(\rho_e=P_e/Q_e\), clearing all denominators by the positive common product
\(D=\prod_e Q_e\) gives a generalized-polynomial vector field. The lifted
source height of reaction channel \(e\) in any log direction \(w\) is exactly
the original rational-rate tropical order plus the same common offset
\(h_D(w)\). Therefore the standard essential-source endotactic test, including
its exact tie rule, is unchanged by denominator clearing.

This is an exact algebraic reconstruction, not a priority claim. It gives a
finite rational decision procedure and a compact witness format, but its
worst-case work is exponential. The fixture below is exact and independently
replayed by `verify_rational_lift_fixture.py`. No wet experiment or novelty
search was performed. This audit is separate from both the one-way N77 network
and the sibling reversible four-pair completion; it changes neither model.

## Interface and assumptions

Fix dimension \(d\) and a finite reaction-channel set \(E=\{1,\ldots,m\}\).
Channel \(e\) has a fixed reaction vector \(\nu_e\in\mathbb R^d\) and rate

\[
 \rho_e(x)=\frac{P_e(x)}{Q_e(x)},\qquad
 P_e(x)=\sum_{\alpha\in A_e}p_{e\alpha}x^\alpha,\quad
 Q_e(x)=\sum_{\beta\in B_e}q_{e\beta}x^\beta,
 \quad x\in\mathbb R^d_{>0}.
\]

Each support \(A_e,B_e\) is finite and nonempty, each exponent belongs to
\(\mathbb Q^d\), and every coefficient \(p_{e\alpha},q_{e\beta}\) is a
positive rational. Duplicate exponents are first aggregated; zero terms are
removed. Thus \(P_e,Q_e\) are strictly positive on the positive orthant and
\(\rho_e\) has no pole there. A vector field may have fixed positive rates
\(\kappa_e\), or measurable time-varying rates satisfying one common box
\(0<\underline\kappa\leq\kappa_e(t)\leq\overline\kappa<\infty\).

The result does not cover signed numerator coefficients, zeros/poles in the
positive orthant, infinitely many terms, or arbitrary black-box rate laws.
Positive coefficients are load-bearing: they prevent cancellation of the
highest monomials on a tie face. Rational exponents are retained exactly; no
change of state coordinates is used.

## Denominator clearing

Set

\[
 D(x)=\prod_{f=1}^m Q_f(x)>0,
 \qquad \widetilde P_e(x)=P_e(x)\prod_{f\ne e}Q_f(x).
\]

For
\(f(x,t)=\sum_e\kappa_e(t)\rho_e(x)\nu_e\), exact multiplication gives

\[
 D(x)f(x,t)=\sum_{e=1}^m\kappa_e(t)\widetilde P_e(x)\nu_e.
 \tag{1}
\]

Before duplicate-source aggregation, the lifted term for channel \(e\) indexed
by \((\alpha_e,(\beta_f)_{f\ne e})\) has source exponent and coefficient

\[
 \gamma=\alpha_e+\sum_{f\ne e}\beta_f,
 \qquad
 c= p_{e\alpha_e}\prod_{f\ne e}q_{f\beta_f}>0.
 \tag{2}
\]

It contributes the generalized exponent arrow \(\gamma\to\gamma+\nu_e\)
with coefficient \(\kappa_e(t)c\). If several tuples give the same \(\gamma\)
for the same channel, their positive coefficients are summed. Arrows from
different channels are retained separately even when their sources coincide.

Because every arrow retains its channel vector, denominator clearing preserves
the stoichiometric subspace \(S=\operatorname{span}\{\nu_e:e\in E\}\) and
all linear conservation laws \(c\cdot\nu_e=0\). It does not in general
preserve weak reversibility, linkage classes, or the molecular-complex
interpretation of the graph: lifted sources are exponent supports and need not
be chemical complexes. A catalytic coordinate with \((\nu_e)_i=0\) remains
unchanged across each lifted arrow, but the lifted source orders still change.

Weak reversibility is a matched baseline only when the rate monomial exponents
are the source complexes of the weakly reversible graph (the mass-action
interface). If arbitrary rational rate laws are attached to the same
stoichiometric arrows, weak reversibility of that bookkeeping graph alone does
not imply (8). For example, annotate the weakly reversible one-species pair
\(\varnothing\rightleftarrows X\) with vectors \(+1,-1\), but choose
\(\rho_+(X)=X^2\) and \(\rho_-(X)=1\). At \(w=1\), both channels are
essential, their orders are 2 and 0, and the top channel points outward. This
is a counterexample to using the stoichiometric arrow cycle as the rational
rate certificate; it is not a counterexample to the standard mass-action
theorem. Its ODE is \(\dot X=X^2-1\), which blows up from any \(X_0>1\) at
time \(\tfrac12\log((X_0+1)/(X_0-1))\). A finite strongly-endotactic or
weakly-reversible graph recognizer is still an informative
stronger/structural baseline after the actual lifted source diagram has been
supplied.

Equation (1) is a positive state-dependent scalar multiple of the vector
field. It preserves pointwise directions and projected signs. It changes speed;
for time-varying \(\kappa_e(t)\), reparameterization also composes the rate
schedule with the new clock. Thus one must not claim equality of solutions at
the same time without a separate bound on \(D\).

## Lifted-source tropical identity

For a finite support \(A\subset\mathbb Q^d\), define its support function

\[
 h_A(w)=\max_{\alpha\in A}\alpha\cdot w.
\]

Write \(h_{P_e}=h_{A_e}\), \(h_{Q_e}=h_{B_e}\), and

\[
 \tau_e(w)=h_{P_e}(w)-h_{Q_e}(w).
 \tag{3}
\]

On the log ray \(x(s)=\exp(sw)\), as \(s\to\infty\),

\[
 P_e(x(s))=e^{s h_{P_e}(w)}(C_{P_e}(w)+o(1)),\quad
 Q_e(x(s))=e^{s h_{Q_e}(w)}(C_{Q_e}(w)+o(1)),
\]

where \(C_{P_e}(w)=\sum_{\alpha\cdot w=h_{P_e}(w)}p_{e\alpha}>0\), and
similarly \(C_{Q_e}(w)>0\). Consequently

\[
 \rho_e(e^{sw})=e^{s\tau_e(w)}
 \left(\frac{C_{P_e}(w)}{C_{Q_e}(w)}+o(1)\right).
 \tag{4}
\]

This formula remains exact on tie directions: every maximizing monomial is
included in the positive leading coefficient. No floating tolerance or
arbitrary single-maximizer choice is permitted for the endotactic decision.

The support of \(D\) is the Minkowski sum of the denominator supports, so

\[
 h_D(w)=\sum_{f=1}^m h_{Q_f}(w).
 \tag{5}
\]

The lifted source support for channel \(e\) is
\(A_e+\sum_{f\ne e}B_f\), hence

\[
 h_{\widetilde P_e}(w)
 =h_{P_e}(w)+\sum_{f\ne e}h_{Q_f}(w)
 =h_D(w)+\tau_e(w).
 \tag{6}
\]

Thus for any two channels \(e,j\),

\[
 h_{\widetilde P_e}(w)-h_{\widetilde P_j}(w)
 =\tau_e(w)-\tau_j(w).
 \tag{7}
\]

In particular, every strict ordering and every exact tie among lifted active
channel source heights matches the corresponding rational-rate orders. On a tie
face, the top lifted coefficient factors as

\[
 C_{\widetilde P_e}(w)=C_{P_e}(w)\prod_{f\ne e}C_{Q_f}(w),\qquad
 C_D(w)=\prod_f C_{Q_f}(w),
\]

so the lifted leading coefficient divided by the common denominator's leading
coefficient is exactly the rational-rate prefactor in (4).

## Exact endotactic equivalence, including neutral channels

For each \(w\), first form the essential channel set

\[
 E(w)=\{e:\nu_e\cdot w\ne0\}.
\]

If \(E(w)=\varnothing\), the test is vacuous. Otherwise let
\(M(w)=\max_{e\in E(w)}\tau_e(w)\). Define the rational-rate graph to pass
the essential-source test when

\[
 \forall w\in\mathbb R^d,\ \forall e\in E(w),\quad
 \tau_e(w)=M(w)\Longrightarrow \nu_e\cdot w<0.
 \tag{8}
\]

Because the additive term \(h_D(w)\) in (6) is common to every channel,
condition (8) holds exactly when the fully lifted exponent-arrow graph is
endotactic under the same convention: discard neutral arrows first, then
compare all remaining source heights, and require **every** top-tied arrow to
point strictly inward. In particular:

- A neutral channel with \(\nu_e\cdot w=0\) is excluded before taking the
  maximum, even if its \(\tau_e(w)\) is largest of all channels.
- If active channels tie at the maximum, every one is checked. A single
  outward top-tied channel refutes endotacticity even when another top-tied
  channel points inward.
- Treating equality as a strict inequality, dropping a tied source, or taking
  the maximum over neutral channels changes the criterion and can misclassify
  the graph.

The primary CRNT definition uses the essential subnetwork (nonorthogonal
reaction vectors) and its extremal source face, with weak reversibility as a
sufficient but not necessary condition; the two-species proof explicitly
supports arbitrary real source exponents. See the primary-source comparison
below. The term here is the source-order form of the standard endotactic test;
it is not the separate Brunner–Craciun definition of a “tropically endotactic”
cone differential inclusion.

## Exact decision procedure and cost

There is a no-expansion exact test for (8) when all input exponents and reaction
vectors are rational. A non-endotactic witness consists of a rational direction
\(w\), one channel \(e\) with \(\nu_e\cdot w>0\), and the condition
\(\tau_j(w)\le\tau_e(w)\) for every \(j\) with \(\nu_j\cdot w\ne0\).
Rational witnesses suffice: after selecting maximizing support terms and a
sign pattern for all \(\nu_j\cdot w\), the constraints are rational linear
inequalities; any feasible relatively open rational polyhedron contains a
rational point.

An exact finite procedure is:

1. For each numerator and denominator support, enumerate a candidate maximizing
   exponent. Add the linear inequalities saying it maximizes that support.
   Ties are represented by non-strict inequalities and overlapping face
   assignments, so all tie strata are covered.
2. Enumerate the \(3^m\) sign patterns for \(\nu_j\cdot w\) (negative, zero,
   positive). For each candidate outward channel \(e\), impose its strict
   positivity and \(\tau_j\le\tau_e\) only for nonneutral channels \(j\).
3. Decide each rational linear feasibility problem exactly. Encode strict
   inequalities by a common margin \(\delta>0\), maximize \(\delta\), and
   bound \(-1\le w_i\le1\); homogeneity lets any witness be rescaled into
   this box. Positive optimum returns a rational counterdirection; no feasible
   cell returns CERTIFIED for (8).

Let \(a_e=|A_e|\), \(b_e=|B_e|\), and \(A=\sum_e a_e\), \(B=\sum_e b_e\).
The direct exhaustive procedure solves at most
\(m\,3^m\prod_e a_eb_e\) rational LPs, each with \(d\) geometric variables
(plus a strict-margin variable) and \(O(A+B+m^2)\) constraints. This is a
finite worst-case bound, not a polynomial-time claim. It avoids materializing
the lifted Cartesian supports. Explicit denominator expansion instead has at
most \(\prod_f b_f\) denominator tuples and

\[
 N_{\rm lift}=\sum_e a_e\prod_{f\ne e}b_f
\]

source tuples before duplicate aggregation. Straight enumeration costs
\(O(N_{\rm lift}\,d)\) rational exponent additions plus coefficient products
and aggregation; exact bit cost includes numerator/denominator growth and
sorting or hashing. A standard endotactic MILP on the materialized lifted
diagram is the strongest direct implementation baseline; as usual, mixed
integer solving has exponential worst-case behavior. The direct LP-cell
procedure is a transparent exact alternative that can save the multiplicative
source expansion, but no general runtime advantage is claimed. All support
acquisition, exact stoichiometry, coefficient/exponent bit lengths, and
solver cost are charged.

## Exact four-channel fixture

Use variables \((X,Y)\) and channels

| \(e\) | \(\kappa_e\) | \(P_e/Q_e\) | \(\nu_e\) |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | \((1+X^2)/(1+X)\) | \((-1,0)\) |
| 2 | 1 | \((1+X)/(1+X^2)\) | \((1,0)\) |
| 3 | 1 | \(X^3/(1+X)\) | \((0,1)\) |
| 4 | 2 | \((1+X^2)/(1+X)\) | \((1,0)\) |

Every coefficient and exponent is rational and positive where required. For
\(w=(1,0)\), the orders are

\[
 (\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3,\tau_4)=(1,-1,2,1),\quad
 E(w)=\{1,2,4\},\quad \max_{e\in E(w)}\tau_e=1.
\]

Channels 1 and 4 are an exact active top tie, with projected changes \(-1\)
and \(+1\); therefore the graph fails (8). Channel 3 has the largest order
among all four, but it is neutral in direction \(w\), so it does not enter the
maximum. A rule that picks only channel 1 among the top tie, or lets neutral
channel 3 mask the active tie, returns the wrong answer.

The common denominator and lifted rates expand exactly to

\[
\begin{aligned}
 D&=(1+X)^3(1+X^2), & h_D(w)&=5,\\
 \widetilde P_1=\widetilde P_4&=(1+X^2)^2(1+X)^2,&h_{\widetilde P_1}=h_{\widetilde P_4}&=6,\\
 \widetilde P_2&=(1+X)^4,&h_{\widetilde P_2}&=4,\\
 \widetilde P_3&=X^3(1+X)^2(1+X^2),&h_{\widetilde P_3}&=7.
\end{aligned}
\]

Equation (6) predicts lifted heights \((6,4,7,6)=(5+1,5-1,5+2,5+1)\),
including the neutral top channel and the active tie. The X-component before
clearing is

\[
 f_X= -\frac{1+X^2}{1+X}+\frac{1+X}{1+X^2}
       +2\frac{1+X^2}{1+X}
     =\frac{1+X^2}{1+X}+\frac{1+X}{1+X^2}\sim X,
\]

whereas

\[
 Df_X=(1+X^2)^2(1+X)^2+(1+X)^4\sim X^6.
\]

The exact common-height shift is five, and the rational rate field has leading
\(X\)-growth exponent one; after lifting the exponent is six. The Y-channel
has \(f_Y=X^3/(1+X)\sim X^2\) and \(Df_Y=X^3(1+X)^2(1+X^2)\sim X^7\),
but its reaction vector is neutral for this \(w\), as required by (8).

At \(X=10\):

\[
 D=134431,\quad f_X=\frac{10322}{1111},\quad Df_X=1248962,\quad
 f_Y=\frac{1000}{11},\quad Df_Y=12221000.
\]

At \(X=100\):

\[
 D=10304040301,\quad f_X=\frac{100030202}{1010101},\quad
 Df_X=1020408090602.
\]

The net X-flux ratio from 10 to 100 is exactly
\(550166111/51615161\approx10.6590\), corresponding to a measured
finite-range log-log slope \(1.02772\). The cleared X-flux ratio is
\(510204045301/624481\approx817004.91\), finite-range slope \(5.91222\).
These finite-range slopes are not asymptotic claims; the exact leading powers
are 1 and 6. The standard-library script verifies all expansions and rational
identities with `Fraction`.

## Biological interface, falsifier, and limits

The input model is a finite list of gross flux channels with fixed stoichiometric
vectors and positive rational generalized-polynomial ratios. Saturating enzyme
laws, Hill-type fits, and rational ecological functional responses can fall in
this class when their full numerator/denominator and chemostat dependencies are
supplied. A fitted approximation or omitted channel is not a certificate. A
real application must acquire the complete channel inventory, supports,
stoichiometric vectors, rate bounds, and measurement ranges; exact rational
supports are particularly strong assumptions on fitted data.

For the fixture, a blinded cell-free assay can normalize substrate concentration
so that \(X=10\) and \(X=100\), hold other substrates/effectors fixed, and
measure channel-specific gross fluxes using isotope-resolved product counts.
After independently calibrating the four rate multipliers to \((1,1,1,2)\),
the preregistered model predictions are

\[
 f_X(10)=10322/1111\approx9.29073,\qquad
 f_X(100)=100030202/1010101\approx99.02990,
\]

with respective direct channel rates at \(X=10\):
\(101/11,11/101,1000/11,202/11\). A replicated 95% confidence interval
excluding either net-flux prediction after propagating independently measured
rate uncertainty falsifies this fixture's kinetic model. The measured
10-to-100 ratio should be \(10.6590\) at the stated parameter values. This is
a deliberately small calibration assay; it is not evidence that a natural
pathway has these laws, and a successful fit cannot establish permanence.

The strongest equal-information graph baseline is to expand \(D\), retain every
lifted arrow and exact tie, and run an established endotactic recognizer. The
published Johnston–Pantea–Donnell MILP determines endotacticity/strong
endotacticity for finite reaction networks; Astra P/N34 adds exact local
certificate scopes and explicit UNKNOWN behavior but does not claim an
all-scale global recognizer. Under the exact two-species power-law interface,
Craciun–Nazarov–Pantea Theorem 19 already gives permanence for endotactic
\(\kappa\)-variable systems; their Section 8.2 explicitly allows arbitrary
real source exponents. The rate-law time change is handled as follows: augment
the lifted system with a clock variable \(t\), solve
\[
 \frac{dx}{ds}=D(x)f(x,t)=\sum_e\kappa_e(t)\widetilde P_e(x)\nu_e,
 \qquad \frac{dt}{ds}=D(x)>0.
\]
Along any such solution, \(s\mapsto(\kappa_e(t(s)))_e\) is an admissible
measurable rate path, and each expanded coefficient \(c\kappa_e(t(s))\) stays
in a fixed positive compact interval. The two-species theorem therefore gives
global permanence of \(x(s)\). Once \(x(s)\) is in its compact positive
absorber, \(D(x(s))\) has a positive minimum, so \(t(s)=\int_0^sD(x(u))du\)
tends to infinity. Inverting \(t(s)\) recovers a global permanent solution of
the original rational-rate system. This proves the two-species rational-rate
permanence consequence under the stated interface; it is a translation of
established theory, not a new permanence theorem. In dimensions three and
higher, this algebraic lift alone proves no permanence result. Astra P/N33 is a
source-derived, internally reconstructed
all-endotactic candidate with arbitrary-dimensional quantifiers, not an
externally verified theorem; the historical arbitrary-dimensional extension
must not be promoted. The separate Brunner–Craciun phrase “tropically
endotactic” refers to a two-dimensional differential-inclusion condition and
should not be silently identified with (8).

  separate tropically-endotactic differential-inclusion condition.

The present contribution is the exact common-height identity (6)–(7), the
associated neutral/tie-safe rational-rate criterion (8), and its explicit
finite LP-cell decision route. These are elementary consequences of positive
support functions and common denominator multiplication. No claim of
historical novelty, efficient general recognition, or biological validation
is made. The next decisive step for an application is a held-out rational-rate
fit on a complete measured channel inventory, followed by an exact recognizer
comparison against the fully expanded graph and a separately matched
strongly-endotactic/weakly-reversible baseline.
