3. What changes and what does not

The prior all-dimensional fixed-sigma KMS power gate lacked the genuine
nonlinear reference entropy premise. Supplying (PI) removes this dependency:
no GNS/modular restriction or local-dimension bound remains in (Q)-(MI).
The one fixed sigma-comparator is uniform across every input/reference;
it is not a separately selected EB map for each tested state or orbit time.

Sigma and Phi remain supplied compatible-stationary-reversible inputs. For
one particular rho, choosing sigma=rho_B is legal ONLY if Phi actually fixes
rho_B and is KMS symmetric there. Varying rho_B and changing sigma or Phi
across states changes the comparator and is not a simultaneous family theorem.
The fixed-sigma general theorem (Q) does allow varying rho_B, with the exact
extra D(rho_B||sigma) budget in (2).

Dimension-independent coefficients do not imply a bounded POVM alphabet or
efficient acquisition of the channel, faithful reference or pure ensemble.
The old C4 construction is existential. Given finite d, one may conservatively
refine the comparator to at most d^2(d^2+3)/2 atoms: write first moments in
the d^2-1-dimensional trace-one Hermitian affine space and weighted Gram
second moments in Sym_(d^2), of dimension d^2(d^2+1)/2, then apply finite
Caratheodory to this compact pure-state moment set. This preserves the SAME
mean sigma and the SAME channel. It is an existence bound, not a finite-
precision/extraction/runtime or information-memory bound; the supplied atom
coordinates may be arbitrary real data. Zero weights may be discarded.
