B. General projection-row versus enforcer-gap theorem

THEOREM B.1. Let 0<=C<=I be a finite Hermitian matrix, P its exact kernel
projection, and let its smallest nonzero eigenvalue be gamma in (0,1).
Suppose every row of C has at most s nonzero entries. Assume a row i and a
set of columns J obey

 sum_(j in J) |P_ij|^2 >=m>0,
 max_(j in J) |P_ij|^2 <=a,

and put D=log[m/(2a)]/log(s+1). If D>1, then

 gamma <= [log(8/m)/(4(D-1))]^2.                        (B.1)

No Markov sign condition, covariance, graph law, locality, circuit structure,
row-oracle assumption or decomposition into constraints is used. All-state
gap and maximum row support are the only enforcer hypotheses.

PROOF. Let T_l denote the degree-l Chebyshev polynomial. For any integer
l>=1 define

 p_l(x)=T_l[(1+gamma-2x)/(1-gamma)]
         /T_l[(1+gamma)/(1-gamma)].

Then p_l(0)=1. For x in [gamma,1] its numerator has absolute value at most
one. For b=(1+gamma)/(1-gamma)>1,

 arcosh(b)=log[(1+sqrt(gamma))/(1-sqrt(gamma))]
           >=2sqrt(gamma),
 T_l(b)=cosh[l arcosh(b)]>=(1/2)exp[2l sqrt(gamma)].

Spectral calculus gives

 ||p_l(C)-P|| <=2 exp[-2l sqrt(gamma)].                  (B.2)

Every row of C^h is supported on length-h walks in its support graph, hence
has at most s^h entries. A polynomial of degree l including its constant
term has row support at most 1+s+...+s^l<=(s+1)^l. This upper bound permits
all coefficient cancellations and arbitrary intermediate workspace vertices.

If A=p_l(C), its selected row can agree with the target row on at most
(s+1)^l columns. Dropping all such columns still leaves target squared mass
at least m-a(s+1)^l. Therefore

 ||A-P||^2 >= sum_(j in J outside supp(A_i,.)) |P_ij|^2
            >=m-a(s+1)^l.                             (B.3)

Take l=ceil[log(8/m)/(4sqrt(gamma))]. Equation (B.2) makes its squared
operator error at most m/2. Equation (B.3) then forces
(s+1)^l>=m/(2a), that is l>=D. Since
l<=log(8/m)/(4sqrt(gamma))+1, rearrangement when D>1 proves (B.1). QED.

If gamma=1 then C=I-P exactly, so P itself has row support at most s+1;
the direct bound m<=a(s+1) applies. There is no singular endpoint loophole.
If D<=1 the displayed bound is vacuous, not a negative conclusion.

The Chebyshev filter is a proof instrument, not an efficient construction of
P. Its coefficients can be large and its row degree grows exponentially in
l. Neither those entries nor P's entries are being supplied as a free oracle.

C. Consequence for ALL enforcers of the full-list history image

Apply B.1 with the exact data in A.1:

 m=q/K^2,  a=1/(K^2 8^T),
 D=log(q 8^T/2)/log(s+1).

Whenever log(q8^T/2)>log(s+1), EVERY positive contraction C with
ker(C)=ran(V) and maximum row support s has

 gamma <= [ log(8K^2/q) log(s+1)
             /{4[log(q8^T/2)-log(s+1)]} ]^2.           (C.1)

When T grows faster than log K+log s, this is
O[(log K log(s+1)/T)^2]. Vanishing is ensured by the stronger condition
T growing faster than log K log(s+1). In particular it tends to zero for the polynomial
repetition regime T>=n^c, K,s<=n^b, with fixed positive b,c. It excludes
a constant all-state gap by ANY positive sparse enforcer of this full-list
uniform-history image. This improves the prior architecture boundary:
there is no reversibility or covariant-connector requirement.

Equivalently, for any fixed positive gap gamma<1, the same proof gives

 log(s+1) >= 4sqrt(gamma) log(q8^T/2)
               /[log(8K^2/q)+4sqrt(gamma)].            (C.1a)

Thus constant gap forces s>=exp[Omega(T/log K)]-1 in the stated regime.
An inexpensive entry oracle would not remove this row-enumeration cost.

It does not say the current path gap Theta(K^-2) is optimal. K can grow
faster than T, and logarithmic factors remain. Nor does it lower-bound the
gap of any alternate isometry or imply that sparse image enforcement itself
is impossible: the banked path construction already has a positive gap.

Classical active workspace extension. Let E be ANY basis-injective isometry

 E|u,z> = exp[i theta(u,z)] |f(u,z)>,

where f is injective into an arbitrarily large computational basis. This
allows arbitrary reversible classical computation of workspace bits from
both clock and data, arbitrary basis permutations and phases, and arbitrary
extra invalid physical basis states. The projector of EV is EPE*. Its row
at f(u,z) is a phase/permutation copy of the original row, so the same m,a
and C.1 hold for every enforcer with kernel ran(EV). No polynomial bound on
the extra ambient dimension is needed for the mathematical obstruction.
If f and its inverse/validity checks are efficient, this is an ordinary
polynomial-circuit active classical workspace interface; inefficiency of f
cannot weaken the row/gap theorem.

The extension does NOT include a workspace computation which coherently
superposes multiple output basis strings per input string, or a general
quantum data-workspace unitary. Such encodings change the projector-row
parameters and remain outside this no-go.

C.2. Arbitrary factorwise seed decoders cannot avoid the obstruction

The exact 8^T count above uses Clifford decoders, but exponential row spread
also follows for ANY seed decoders, with weaker constants. Suppose each
five-wire factor uses arbitrary unitaries U_phi,U_psi and diagonal rank-one
tests |r_phi><r_phi|, |r_psi><r_psi|, so

 U_phi*|r_phi>=exp(i theta_phi)phi,
 U_psi*|r_psi>=exp(i theta_psi)psi.

The unitary W=U_phi U_psi* has a fixed entry in row r_phi, column r_psi,
of squared magnitude |<phi,psi>|^2=1/8. Every OTHER entry in this unit row
has squared magnitude at most 7/8, and the fixed entry does too. Therefore
the tensor-product row r_phi^(tensor T) has maximum squared entry at most
(7/8)^T. Into the q psi-tail clock blocks its total squared projector mass
is still q/K^2. Apply B.1 with

 m=q/K^2,  a=(7/8)^T/K^2,
 D=log[q(8/7)^T/2]/log(s+1).

It gives C.1 with log(q8^T/2) replaced by log[q(8/7)^T/2]. The same
asymptotic vanishing-gap conclusion holds for polynomial K,s and polynomial
T. No Clifford, algebraic-degree, coefficient-height or gate-locality
assumption is needed for this mathematical variant. Decoder phases and
different diagonal rank-one labels do not matter.

More generally, if a decoder acts on an entire base copy and diagonalizes
P_phi tensor I or P_psi tensor I, choose a row in the former's diagonal
support. Its squared mass in the latter's diagonal support is exactly 1/8,
since P_phi P_psi P_phi=(1/8)P_phi. Its complementary squared mass is 7/8;
thus every row entry remains at most 7/8. Tensoring across copies gives the
same bound, allowing arbitrary mixing with other data WITHIN each copy.

This still fixes a uniform-history architecture with factorwise endpoint
decoders. An arbitrary global decoder of the amplified OR projection or a
new coherent active-workspace isometry is not covered. The weaker universal
factorwise bound prevents evasion merely by selecting a different local
diagonalization of the two seed tests.

The equivalent row bound (C.1a) holds here with q8^T/2 replaced by
q(8/7)^T/2, so constant gap again forces exp[Omega(T/log K)] row support.

D. Exact constant-gap positive control: every Clifford image

THEOREM D.1. Let U be a polynomial-size Clifford circuit on n+a qubits,
a>=1, and V_U|xi>=U(|xi> tensor |0^a>). There is an explicit positive
matrix C_U satisfying

 0<=C_U<=I/2,
 ker(C_U)=ran(V_U),
 C_U >=(1/4)(I-V_U V_U*),

with at most q^2+1=O(a^2) nonzero entries per row, polynomial-time exact
construction/row/entry access, and coefficients in Q(i) with polynomial bit
length. No all-state gap is hidden in an unnormalized constraint sum.

PROOF. Define the independent commuting Hermitian Pauli stabilizers
S_j=U(I_n tensor Z_j)U*, j=0,...,a-1. Their common +1 eigenspace is ran V_U.
Let q=2^b be the smallest power of two at least 2a, so 2a<=q<4a. Use the
field F_q and its absolute trace Tr:F_q->F_2. For t,x in F_q define an
a-bit string g(t,x) by

 g_j(t,x)=Tr(x t^j),  j=0,...,a-1.

Use all q^2 pairs, including repetitions, and let
S_g=product_j S_j^(g_j). For a nonzero syndrome z in F_2^a, write the
nonzero field polynomial f_z(t)=sum_j z_j t^j. Orthogonality of the trace
characters gives

 (1/q^2) sum_(t,x) (-1)^(z dot g(t,x))
     =#{t:f_z(t)=0}/q
     <=(a-1)/q<=1/2.                                  (D.1)

For z=0 this average is one. Set

 C_U=(1/2)[I-(1/q^2)sum_(t,x) S_(g(t,x))].

Each (I-S_g)/2 is a positive contraction. In the simultaneous syndrome
basis, C_U is zero at z=0 and has eigenvalue (1-b_z)/2 in [1/4,1/2] for
every nonzero z, because the bias b_z in D.1 is nonnegative. This proves
positivity, exact kernel, norm and the all-state gap.

Each S_g is a Pauli monomial: one nonzero entry per row, with magnitude one
and phase in {1,-1,i,-i}. Enumerate the q^2 contributions to an arbitrary
row, accumulate equal column labels exactly, add the diagonal identity and
remove exact zeros. This gives row support at most q^2+1 and coefficients
with denominator 2q^2. Polynomial-size Clifford conjugation computes each
S_j in a symplectic Pauli representation. Multiplying the a generators and
evaluating the field traces takes polynomial time. The field degree is
b=O(log a); an irreducible binary polynomial can even be found by exhaustive
search over O(q) candidates with polynomial-degree factor tests. Thus all
construction and access costs are polynomial; no randomness, exponential
coefficient precision or dense reflection oracle is invoked. QED.

The a=0 case is V_U=U on the whole space, with C_U=0 and vacuous complement.
The stabilizer terms can act on all n+a qubits. This enforcer is polynomial-
ROW sparse, not claimed constant-local or geometrically local.

E. Failure of the simplest Clifford-image plus diagonal-test route

For any Clifford V_U above and ANY diagonal physical operator B, V_U*BV_U
lies in a single commuting logical Pauli algebra. Indeed diagonal B expands
in physical Z strings. Clifford conjugation sends a Z string to one Pauli
on logical plus ancilla registers. Compression against |0^a> kills it if
its ancilla factor contains X or Y; otherwise it yields a signed logical
Pauli. Any two surviving logical Paulis commute: their original physical
Z strings commute, and their surviving ancilla factors are all Z strings.

This proves a strict obstruction to EXACTLY encoding a Hamiltonian whose
nonzero Pauli coefficients include an anticommuting pair by this particular
Clifford/diagonal route. The augmented seed Hamiltonian has such a pair:
on one five-wire seed block, X on the first seed qubit and Z on all three
seed qubits anticommute. P_phi has expectation -1 for X and zero for ZZZ;
P_psi has expectation zero for X and +1 for ZZZ. Consequently the T-fold
OR seed terms have nonzero coefficients at these single-block Paulis, scaled
by (31/32)^(T-1), and the hard terms act as identity on all seed wires.
Thus the exact full-list compression identity cannot be obtained with a
Clifford image and diagonal B.

The requested alpha/beta CONSTANT COMPARISON is weaker than exact equality.
The preceding Hamiltonian Pauli-support argument does NOT rule it out.
The new full-kernel argument in KERNEL_RIGIDITY.txt does rule it out for the
Clifford/diagonal route whenever the hard-data output has nonzero kernel,
including the explicit complete three-color generated instance there.
A nondiagonal sparse B and a non-Clifford active encoding remain outside
both obstructions. A sparse-stabilizer C by itself therefore closes no
quantum-PCP or unrestricted full-family encoding claim.

F. Read ledger, primary provenance and validation status

One additional attempted escape was ordinary square-root spectral-gap
amplification. Its signed Dirac block on an eigenvalue lambda is
[[0,sqrt(lambda)],[sqrt(lambda),0]], with eigenvalues +/-sqrt(lambda).
This is not a positive image enforcer. Squaring restores positivity but
returns eigenvalues lambda and can retain unwanted ancillary zero modes.
A constant positive ancillary shift leaves a negative eigenvalue in this
2x2 block. The positive block [[lambda,sqrt(lambda)],
[sqrt(lambda),1+delta]], delta>=0, instead has a Rayleigh quotient lambda
on the original first coordinate, so its minimum cannot exceed lambda;
at delta=0 it has an extra zero mode for every lambda. These exact scalar
checks do not preclude a new active isometry, but they invalidate this
direct imported-amplifier upgrade.
