INDEPENDENT REDUCTION
Write iota=Ad_E, kappa=Ad_(E*) for the real first-six-coordinate inclusion.
A=S composed with T10:M10->M6; B=R^dagger:M6->M10.
Phi1=iota A; Phi2=B kappa; kappa iota=id.
The only moving cube maps are Phi1 Phi2 Phi1=iota ABA and
Phi2 Phi1 Phi2=BAB kappa. In full block notation,
Theta^3(X)=c1^2 c2 W1 E ABA(X00) E* W1*
 + c1 c2^2 W0 BAB(E*X11 E) W0*
 + [tr(G0 X00)+tr(G1 X11)+x_star] P_star,
where
G0=F1+c1 Phi1^dagger(F2)+c1 c2 (Phi2 Phi1)^dagger(F1),
G1=F2+c2 Phi2^dagger(F1)+c1 c2 (Phi1 Phi2)^dagger(F2).
Each G_i is positive by CP adjoints. This accounts for absorption at all three
times; the already absorbed output stays at the flag. The flag contributions
are measure/prepare maps. All off-diagonal input blocks were killed at step 1.

Thus Theta^3 is EB iff both ABA and BAB are EB. Sufficiency follows from sums
of EB maps and CP pre/post composition. Necessity follows by compression to
the moving output block and restriction to the relevant input block (and to
the E coordinates for BAB). The flag vanishes under these output compressions.
For non-real isometries a Choi reference compression uses W^T and conjugate W;
all isometries here are real coordinate inclusions, so no hidden conjugation.

STRONGER ONE-CERTIFICATE REDUCTION
The specific pencil has L T4=L=T4 L, not merely the PPT property. Total
transpose and real U,V imply S T10=S=T6 S. Therefore A=S and
B=S^dagger C, where C=Ad_K is a self-adjoint involution. With F=ABA,
BAB=F^dagger C. EB is closed under Hilbert-Schmidt adjoints and unitary
precomposition, so EB of F alone implies EB of BAB. One way to see adjoint
closure without a literature premise: adjoints of rank-one Kraus matrices
are rank one. The usual Choi product-decomposition argument produces such
Kraus matrices for every EB map.
