1. EXACT PHYSICAL CLASS

Let q_v=2S_v>=1 be integers, Q=sum_v q_v. On an arbitrary supplied loopless
graph take physical energy -H, where

 H=sum_(uv)[alpha_uv(S_u^xS_v^x+S_u^yS_v^y)+gamma_uv S_u^zS_v^z]
                    +sum_v[b_v S_v^x+c_v S_v^z+f_v(S_v^z)].

All couplings and all table entries f_(v,r)=f_v(S_v-r), r=0..q_v, are rational.
Require alpha>=|gamma|, b>=0, arbitrary c, and discrete concavity

 2 f_(v,r+1)>=f_(v,r)+f_(v,r+2), r=0..q_v-2.

The explicit table promise is acquired by O(Q) rational comparisons. Spins
cost Q rather than log Q: fixed/unary/poly-size spin input is admitted. This
extends the original spin-half interface to genuine higher spins and onsite
interactions, without claiming every high-spin Hamiltonian.

In particular f_v(m)=-D_v m^2, D_v>=0, is admitted. The PHYSICAL energy then
contains +D_v(S_v^z)^2. For S>=1 this is a nonconstant single-ion anisotropy,
which is absent from the baseline qubit model. For spinhalf it is a known
scalar offset. Constants in f are tracked and never hidden in a relative
eigenvalue lower bound.

2. COMPLETE LOCAL CLASSIFICATION OF WEIGHTED SPIN PROJECTORS

For positive w_0,...,w_q define the rational charge-weighted symmetric operator

 G_w(r,c)=w_|r|/binom(q,|r|) if |r|=|c|, otherwise0.

Its row/complemented-column generating polynomial is

 p_(q,w)(x,y)=sum_(r=0)^q w_r e_r(x)e_(q-r)(y)/binom(q,r).

CLAIM: p_(q,w) is log-concave iff w is ordinarily log-concave:
 w_(r+1)^2>=w_r w_(r+2) for every r. q=1 is the vacuous linear case.

Proof for q>=2. Differentiate a row and b column variables, a+b=q-2. Write
m=q-a=b+2, ell=q-b=a+2. The remaining quadratic Hessian has row off-diagonal
A=w_(a+2)/binom(q,a+2), cross B=w_(a+1)/binom(q,a+1), and column off-diagonal
D=w_a/binom(q,a), with zero diagonals. Row-sum-zero eigenvalues are -A;
column-sum-zero eigenvalues are -D. The other two eigenvalues come from

 [[A(m-1),B sqrt(m ell)],[B sqrt(m ell),D(ell-1)]].

Its trace is positive and its determinant equals

 [m ell/binom(q,a+1)^2]*(w_a w_(a+2)-w_(a+1)^2).

Thus a log-concave sequence makes every nonzero degree-two derivative have
at most one positive eigenvalue. The support is the full uniform rank-q
matroid. Branden--Huh Theorem2.25 / ensuing characterization, plus Theorem2.30,
give Lorentzian / complete log-concavity of the full polynomial. Conversely,
for a multiaffine log-concave polynomial its coordinate derivatives are LC:
partial_i p=lim_(t->infinity) p(x_i=t)/t, and concavity of log passes to this
positive limit. A failed sequence inequality therefore produces a quadratic
derivative with TWO positive eigenvalues, contradicting LC. This proves both
directions and all quantifiers. No finite spectrum test supplies the proof.

For w=1 this is the symmetric projector in INITIAL.txt. This is also the
polarization characterization of a bivariate ultra-log-concave sequence and
uses established Lorentzian theory; no originality is claimed for that theory.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/1902.03719v8, Thm2.25/2.30;
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2020/192-3/p04.

3. EXACT RATIONAL EULER WEIGHTS FOR ANY CONCAVE TABLE

Set M_v=max_r |f_(v,r)|, g_(v,r)=M_v+f_(v,r)>=0 and
 u_(v,r)=1+s g_(v,r). Then u is positive and log-concave for every s>=0:

 u_(r+1)^2-u_r u_(r+2)
 =s[2g_(r+1)-g_r-g_(r+2)]
               +s^2[g_(r+1)^2-g_r g_(r+2)]>=0.

The first term is nonnegative by concavity. The second is nonnegative because
g_(r+1)>=(g_r+g_(r+2))/2>=sqrt(g_r g_(r+2)); the square root is only a proof
device. Consequently u^2 is also log-concave. The gate with charge weights
w_r=u_r^2 has nonnegative RATIONAL coefficients and fits section2 exactly.
Concavity is also the exact tangent condition for every sufficiently small
Euler step: a strictly positive second difference of f makes the displayed
first-order LC difference negative for all small s, whatever scalar shift.

The actual higher-spin compiler puts a projector on every constituent, so
there are no unused qubits. Qpartner is a product of partition/uniform matroid
