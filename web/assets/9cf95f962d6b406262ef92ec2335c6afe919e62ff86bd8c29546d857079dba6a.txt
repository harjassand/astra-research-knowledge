1. LEGAL INTERFACE AND A PARAMETER-INDEPENDENT CPTP CUTOFF

The local reference is the supplied standard two-mode Fock basis. It is
not an acquired unknown plane. Let N=n1+n2 and

  P_K=1_(N<=K), d_K=(K+1)(K+2)/2.

On any two-mode density rho, define the finite encoder and embedding decoder

  E_K(rho)=P_K rho P_K+Tr[(I-P_K)rho]|0,0><0,0|,
  D_K(X)=J_K X J_K^dag.                                   (1.1)

Here J_K identifies the d_K finite number basis with P_K Fock. The encoder
has the projection Kraus P_K plus |0,0><n1,n2| for each discarded number
basis vector. Their A^dag A sum is I. It is therefore CPTP, not postselection,
and the decoder is an isometry. The fallback outcome is ERASED; no classical
flag is required or free. Maximum branch Q=d_K, C_labels=1, D_total=d_K.
The same formulas apply in a finite symmetric carrier with K capped at m.
At K>=m the finite-carrier code is exact full storage.

Writing delta=Tr[(I-P_K)rho], a purification/projection gives

  T(rho,P_K rho P_K)<=sqrt(delta),
  T(rho,D_K E_K(rho))<=sqrt(delta)+delta/2<=2sqrt(delta).   (1.2)

The first term is half trace for the unnormalized projected operator.
Indeed project a purification, use its pure two-dimensional span, and
contract under partial trace; its trace-norm difference is <=2sqrt(delta).
The fallback adds a positive operator of trace delta. All coherences across
the cutoff are included in (1.2); replacing this by delta alone would be
incorrect for displaced or pure inputs. No unknown displacement or frame
enters either channel. Discarded ancillas and the reconstructed output are
not retained memory; all retained quantum sides are the one d_K register.

Abstract number-basis isometries and channels are explicit. Efficient number
measurement, infinite-output gate realization, numerical synthesis precision,
conditioning and runtime are not proved. This is a quantum memory statement,
not a claim that the complete physical preparation costs zero.

2. PURE COMPACT COHERENT UPPER: POISSON TAIL, NOT A GLOBAL ORBIT

The pure experiment is P_alpha=|alpha1,alpha2><alpha1,alpha2| with
alpha in C^2, ||alpha||<=R, fixed R>0. Total number is Poisson with mean
mu=||alpha||^2<=R^2. For L=K+1>=R^2, the exponential Markov bound gives

  Pr(N>=L)<=exp[-L log(L/R^2)+L-R^2]
             <=(e R^2/L)^L.                              (2.1)

One can use the first expression with mu and then monotonicity in mu, or
the second loose expression uniformly at R. At alpha=0 the tail is zero.
Choose the smallest L with L>=max{1,R^2} and

  L log[L/(e R^2)]>=2log(2/epsilon).                       (2.2)

Then (1.2) gives error <=epsilon. For fixed R and epsilon->0, an elementary
inverse of x log x has

  L=Theta_R[log(1/epsilon)/loglog(1/epsilon)].

This proves the upper in (0.1). It has one branch and no measured frame or
parameter label. The logarithm in the denominator comes from the actual
factorial Poisson tail; a general fixed exponential-moment estimate would
lose it.

3. APPROXIMATE PURE-STATE PRESERVATION FOR ARBITRARY HYBRID CHANNELS

The following finite-span lemma supplies the converse. Let x_1,...,x_D be
normalized linearly independent pure vectors, with Gram eigenvalue minimum
g>0, and |<x_s,x_t>|>=b>0 for every pair. Let Phi have Kraus A_a of rank<=Q,
possibly a measurable Kraus integral, and be TP. If

  max_s T(Phi(P_xs),P_xs)<=epsilon<=1/2,

and Q<=D/2, then

  epsilon>=(b g/56)^2.                                   (3.1)

This uses the channel from the TOTAL input to TOTAL output. It makes no
product assumption and allows arbitrary classical label refinements. A
code through a Q-dimensional branch admits such rank-Q composite Kraus
operators, since every decoder-encoder composition factors through that
same register. Discarded environments or regenerated ancillas cannot enlarge
the rank of each composite operator.

Full proof. Write

  A_a x_s=lambda_(a,s)x_s+r_(a,s), <x_s,r_(a,s)>=0.

The pure target projector is a bounded effect, so the total infidelity
f_s=sum_a||r_(a,s)||^2 is <=epsilon; TP gives
sum_a|lambda_(a,s)|^2=1-f_s. Applying TP to the off-diagonal inner product,

  <x_s,x_t>=sum_a<A_a x_s,A_a x_t>,

and using Cauchy-Schwarz on the two cross terms and the remainder term gives

  |1-<lambda_s,lambda_t>|<=3sqrt(epsilon)/b.

Thus ||lambda_s-lambda_t||^2<=6sqrt(epsilon)/b. Fix anchor s=1, put
B_a=(A_a-lambda_(a,1)I)|_V, V=span{x_s}, and use (u+v)^2<=2u^2+2v^2:

  sum_a||B_a x_s||^2
   <=2epsilon+12sqrt(epsilon)/b<=14sqrt(epsilon)/b.

The frame operator sum_s P_xs has smallest nonzero eigenvalue g. Summing
over s therefore proves

  sum_a||B_a||_HS^2<=14D sqrt(epsilon)/(b g).              (3.2)

On the other hand ker(A_a|_V) has dimension >=D-Q. An orthonormal basis
of that kernel gives
||B_a||_HS^2>=(D-Q)|lambda_(a,1)|^2. Hence the left side of (3.2) is
>=(D-Q)(1-epsilon)>=D/4. Combining proves (3.1). All sums can be replaced
by Kraus integrals; the same Cauchy-Schwarz and kernel-rank bounds apply.
No bounded ensemble likelihood, Haar-scalar reference or energy constraint
on the channel output was presumed.

4. EXACT TWO-MODE PHASE GRID AND MATCHING PURE LOCAL LOWER

Fix r=min{R/sqrt(2),1/2}>0. For an integer L>=1 set M=L+1 and use the
D=M^2 vectors

  x_(a,b)=|r exp(2pi i a/M),r exp(2pi i b/M)>, 0<=a,b<M.

These lie in the declared radius-R chart. Their pairwise overlaps have
magnitude >=exp(-4r^2). The single-mode Gram is circulant. Its Fourier
eigenvalue for residue ell=0,...,L is exactly

  M exp(-r^2) sum_(n=ell mod M) r^(2n)/n!,

and is >=M exp(-r^2)r^(2L)/L!, since r^2<=1 and the first L+1 Poisson
weights decrease. The two-mode Gram is a tensor product, so

  g>=D exp(-2r^2)r^(4L)/(L!)^2.                          (4.1)

Consequently every Q<=D/2 code obeys the finite lower

  epsilon>=[D exp(-6r^2)r^(4L)/(56(L!)^2)]^2.             (4.2)

For any branch budget Q choose L=ceil(sqrt(2Q))-1 (and L>=1); then D>=2Q
and L=O(sqrt Q). Taking logarithms of (4.2) gives

  log(1/epsilon)<=C_R[1+sqrt Q log(Q+2)].                 (4.3)

Invert this monotone bound for small epsilon to obtain the lower in (0.1).
Together with Section 2 this closes the PURE compact-chart profile, with
unlimited classical labels included in the lower. In particular every fixed
Q has a strictly positive error floor on any nontrivial radius-R chart.
No sharp leading constant or exact optimizer is asserted.

5. FINITE PURE Sym^m LOCAL CHART AT POLYNOMIAL PRECISION

Supply the normal e3 and the carrier m. The local coherent family is

  v_m(z)=(z1,z2,sqrt m)/sqrt(m+||z||^2), ||z||<=R,

not an acquired frame. In the fixed occupation basis N=n1+n2 is binomial

For the lower choose z=(r e^(2pi i a/M),r e^(2pi i b/M)) on the phase grid.
The finite-carrier Gram is two-dimensional circulant. Its (ell1,ell2) Fourier
eigenvalue is D times the sum of multinomial probabilities with those two

  [m/(m+2r^2)]^m [(m)_fall(ell1+ell2)/m^(ell1+ell2)]
                  r^(2(ell1+ell2))/(ell1!ell2!).

When m>=4L^2, the falling ratio is >=exp(-1): log(1-u)>=-2u for u<=1/2,

  Q_min(m,m^(-alpha))=Theta_(R,alpha)[(log m/loglog m)^2].   (5.2)

More explicitly use (4.3) with the finite constants above whenever the
