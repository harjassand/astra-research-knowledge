## N75 claim and what it leaves missing

**Source-reported.** The card reports that for centered, compactly supported, log-concave μ with Lebesgue density, (P_0=P(\mu)), (\sigma^2=\|\operatorname{Cov}(\mu)\|_{op}), and (\mu_s=\mu*N(0,sI)),
\[
P_0\le P(\mu_s)\le P_0+\frac{\sigma^2s}{P_0-s}\qquad(0<s<P_0).
\]
It sketches eigenfunction and heat-flow identities but says eigenfunction, differentiation, and domain justifications require the missing full note. If a hypothetical KLS-failing sequence has (P_0=1) and (\sigma^2\to0), then the reported inequality gives (P(\mu_s)\to1) for each fixed (s<1). That is a spectral plateau feeding a critical construction, not a contradiction by itself.

**Reconstructed from Family 093.** The exact information bound in the supplied summary is Proposition 7.2, §7.1, equations (7.6)–(7.8), pp. 52–53 of the pinned 65-page PDF. For a sequence (j\to\infty), let (Y_{j,t}) be a stationary diffusion with law \(\eta_j\), and let \(\mathcal R(\eta_j)\) denote its best log-Sobolev constant in the convention
\[
\operatorname{Ent}_{\eta_j}(f^2)\le 2\mathcal R(\eta_j)\int|Df|^2d\eta_j,
\qquad \mathcal R(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1},\quad \lambda_j\to1.
\]
The paper's positive-semidefinite matrix field \(R_j\) and original Sylvester matrices \(\widehat H_{j,i}\) satisfy
\[
\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}R_j^2\to1,\qquad \mathbb E|DR_j|^2\to1,
\]
\[
D_iR_j=\tfrac12(\widehat H_{j,i}R_j+R_j\widehat H_{j,i}),\qquad
\sum_i\mathbb E\|[\widehat H_{j,i},R_j]\|_{HS}^2=o(1).
\]
For **each fixed** (t>0) and (L\ge1), take an independent standard Gaussian (G), and set, for (T>0),
\[
f_L(q)=\frac{q}{1+q/L},\quad C_{j,T}(y)=I+f_L(TR_j(y)^2),\quad Z_{j,T}=C_{j,T}(Y_{j,t})^{1/2}G.
\]
Thus (I\preceq C_{j,T}\preceq(1+L)I). Proposition 7.2 asserts
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\int_0^\infty I(Y_{j,t};Z_{j,T})\,\frac{dT}{T^2}\le C_0=\frac94.
\]
The coefficient is independent of fixed (t,L); the sequence limit is taken at each fixed cap (L). The proof applies LSI to posterior likelihoods (q_z(y)=p_T(z\mid y)/p_T(z)): (2\lambda_j I\le\mathbb E\|D_y\log p_T(Z\mid Y)\|^2) (7.7), followed by the Gaussian score identity
\[
I(Y_{j,t};Z_{j,T})\le\frac{1}{4\lambda_j}\mathbb E\sum_i\operatorname{tr}(C_{j,T}^{-1}D_iC_{j,T}C_{j,T}^{-1}D_iC_{j,T})\tag{7.8}
\]
and a scale integral split into comparable eigenvalue pairs (multiplier at most 9) and noncomparable pairs (at most (4\log(1+L)), multiplied by the vanishing commutator error). This is reconstructed from the source PDF, not independently externally validated.

**Exact covariance-only replacement interface.** Keep the channel and displayed field hypotheses above, but replace only the LSI premise by $P(\eta_j)\le\lambda_j^{-1}$, $\lambda_j\to1$, and seek an absolute bound.
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\int_0^\infty I(Y_{j,t};Z_{j,T})\,\frac{dT}{T^2}\le C_{\rm P}<\infty
\]
For each fixed $t>0,L\ge1$, the same $C_{\rm P}$ must be independent of $t,L$. The exact value $9/4$ is unnecessary: Proposition 7.9 multiplies this upper bound by an absolute constant $C_1$, while Lemma 7.10 gives a scalar lower bound at least $(c/2)\log(t/h_0)$ after the later fixed choice $L=t$. Thus any such bound, with the sequence limit taken before choosing large $t,L$, yields the same contradiction. An upper bound only on compact scales does not suffice.

**Display-only interface refuted; source-provenance version open.** Applying Poincaré to $\sqrt{q_z}$ gives:
\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\eta_j}(\sqrt{q_z})\le\frac{P(\eta_j)}4\int q_z|D\log q_z|^2d\eta_j.
\]
After averaging over $z$, the left side is an affinity/Hellinger divergence of the joint observation from the product law, while the right side is the Fisher-score term appearing in (7.7). This does not bound the required KL mutual information from above. More decisively, [CYCLE2_REPORT.md](CYCLE2_REPORT.md) constructs both an exact Laplace sequence and a smooth, rescaled convolution sequence with $P(\eta_j)=1$, satisfying the displayed field/moment/Sylvester/commutator assumptions while the scale integral grows at least as $(p_0/8)\log(L/(2h_0))$. Thus no cap-independent bound follows from those hypotheses alone. This tail-escape profile cannot have the full source provenance: the near-unit LSI in Proposition 3.9 would bound $\operatorname{Ent}(\operatorname{tr}R_j^2)$, whereas size-biased tail escape forces that entropy to diverge. The one-dimensional candidate also fails the deterministic projection/outlier pair (5.2)-(5.3) under amplitude rescaling. The different question—whether another covariance-only proof can use the actual high-dimensional hierarchy—remains open.

## BKL v1 proof lane and audit verdict

**Reconstructed load-bearing chain in arXiv:2610.05474v1.** Theorem `thm_main` claims dimension-free Poincaré for isotropic log-concave measures. In §§3–7, it proceeds through the following interface:

1. §4, Theorem `thm_cumulants`, aims to prove all-order isotropic cumulant bounds (R_m^\mu\preceq K^{m-1}((m-1)!)^2I). Lemma `lem_third_cumulant` starts from Letwin's quadratic-form inequality. Eldan localization evolves each cumulant into an order-(m+1) martingale term plus a drift involving lower cumulants (`lem_cumulant_dynamics`).
2. §5, `lem_energy_drift` and `lem_integrated_energy`, use the normalized covariance-tensor energy to trade its time integral against the next cumulant order and the lower-order product term. §6 closes this by induction over (m), including the factorial cancellation in (the source's) Eq. `eq_factorial_cancellation`.
3. §7, `prop_suspension`, embeds a normalized sum of independent copies of a test function into one additional coordinate of a larger isotropic log-concave law. Its mixed cumulant entries encode the degree-(d) Taylor tensor of the tilt average. This converts the all-order cumulant estimates into the Song–Zhang criterion (`thm_1032`), which gives the Poincaré bound.

**Portions inspected.** Source-text review of §2 `lem_tensor_recovery`; §3 `thm_1032`, `lem_block_symmetry`, `lem_bochner_step`, and `lem_taylor_doubling`; §4 `lem_third_cumulant` and `lem_cumulant_dynamics`; §5 `lem_energy_drift` and `lem_integrated_energy`; plus the main induction structure in §6 and suspension/main-theorem structure in §7. I checked the displayed cumulant product-rule drift and the energy drift coefficients algebraically, and checked the factorial cancellation and the proposed symmetrizer repair. This is a scoped reconstruction, not a full proof validation.

**One repairable omission in v1.** In the proof of `lem_taylor_doubling` (source lines 1095–1164), (T=\mathcal T_d(\nabla_0h_i)) has (d) Taylor-output slots followed by derivative-output slots. The error approximation (\widetilde T=\mathcal T_d(\mathsf S^{in}_{2d}\nabla_0h_i)) symmetrizes 2d original derivative slots before applying \(\mathcal T_d\), while the tensor-recovery lemma's \(\mathsf S_{2d}\) averages the first 2d output slots (d Taylor and d derivative). Those projections are not identical. The estimate can be repaired without identifying them: since output symmetrization is an orthogonal projection,
\[
|\mathsf S^{out}_{2d}\widetilde T|
\le |\mathsf S^{out}_{2d}T|+|\widetilde T-T|.
\]
Apply recovery to the separately block-symmetric \(\widetilde T\), then use the source's valid bound on \(|\mathsf S^{out}_{2d}T|\) and its approximation-error bound. The error coefficient becomes (1+4^d); its square and the existing (C^dR^{2d}/\lambda_1) error budget are absorbed by enlarging the universal (C). This is a missing comparison, but does not break the stated recursion/constants in the checked estimate. I found no fatal issue here.

**Stochastic endpoint update.** The older subsequence-only note is superseded. A fixed-(n,m) noncircular envelope follows from standard one-dimensional isotropic log-concave moment bounds and the moment-cumulant partition formula: for unit directions, each order-r cumulant coordinate is bounded by a finite J_r, hence R_r^nu<=C_{n,r}I with C_{n,r}=n^(r-1)J_r^2. BKL's affine identity gives E_r(t)<=C_{n,r}q_t, q_t=<A_tu,u>. Compact support diameter D gives q_t<=D^2 pathwise; the order-three noise inequality and the covariance SDE justify E q_t=e^-t, so E E_r(t)<=C_{n,r}e^-t. The normalized first variation yields d<M>/dt<=8E_mE_{m+1}+16(m-1)^2E_m^2; therefore E<M>_infinity<=D^2[8C_{n,m}C_{n,m+1}+16(m-1)^2C_{n,m}^2]. This is enough for the true L2 martingale and full T->infinity endpoint on compact supports, together with the induction's lower-order drift integrability. Detailed independent audit is in `../../kls_proof_audit/FINAL_REPORT.md` lines 165–206 and the expanded derivation is in `LOCALIZATION_GATE_AUDIT.md`. The older b_r-only gate remains a correct post-Theorem 4.1 bracket consequence, but is superseded as an induction-integrability proof because it circularly assumes orders m,m+1. The D^2 dependence leaves noncompact approximation as a separate gate.

**Audit limits.** I did not fully check regularity/form-domain approximation in every step, all of §§6–7 line by line, the paper's complete proof, or any independent formal verification. In particular I did not audit Song–Zhang's later v2 proof at all. The v1 source's conclusion remains an author claim in this audit; the checked repairs do not constitute external validation.

## Versioned prior art and effect on N75

- Before the October 2026 KLS claims, the strongest general bound identified in the primary papers is Letwin, *The KLS constant is (O(\log^{1/4}n))*, [arXiv:2607.24164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24164), Theorem 1.1. Both later KLS papers identify it as their strongest previous bound. This is a literature-reported comparison, not an independent review of Letwin's proof.
- Song–Zhang v1, [arXiv:2610.01447v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01447v1), claims (\psi_n=O(4^{\log^*(n+2)})) and (C_P=O(16^{\log^*(n+2)})), which is still unbounded in dimension and therefore does not resolve KLS.
- BKL v1, [arXiv:2610.05474v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05474v1), claims a complete proof by the cumulant/suspension/Taylor-criterion chain above.
- Song–Zhang v2, [arXiv:2610.01447v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01447v2), is a materially different, much longer source (API comment: 140 pages). Its introduction describes an iterative polynomial-variance/curvature refinement, repeatedly reducing log-star growth until the claimed (O(1)) bound. That changed argument is not audited here. Do not collapse v1 and v2 into a single claim.

These papers materially change the current literature claim state, but neither their abstracts nor BKL v1 fill N75's missing Poincaré-only channel estimate. The latter remains a precise open lemma unless one separately establishes an independently validated KLS proof.
