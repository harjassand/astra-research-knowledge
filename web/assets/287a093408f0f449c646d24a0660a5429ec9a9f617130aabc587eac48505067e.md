# Bridge record: set-valued observability to energy-priced sensing

## Candidate connection

For a fixed control trajectory, define the state-indexed output tube

\[
\Gamma(x_0)=\{(x_0+q(t))^2+v(t):\ |v(t)|\leq\delta,\ 0\leq t\leq T\},
\qquad q(t)=\int_0^t u(s)\,ds.
\]

Its two-state noiseless separation is

\[
\sup_{t\in[0,T]}|(x_0+q(t))^2-(x_1+q(t))^2|
=|x_0-x_1|\sup_t|x_0+x_1+2q(t)|.
\]

Minimizing the last factor over the pair midpoint gives exactly the displacement range `Q=max(q)-min(q)`. Therefore bounded-output tubes overlap for a worst-case pair at radius `delta/Q`. The input-energy inequality `Q <= integral |u| <= sqrt(E*T)` implies a minimax floor `delta/sqrt(E*T)`, and a monotone constant-speed ramp attains it. The proof is in `proofs/quadratic_sensor_minimax.md`.

## Connection status

Deole–Mesbahi (arXiv:2501.09192v3, §3 and Theorem IV.1) already formalize finite-horizon weak observability for set-valued output tubes and optimize a concave degree-of-observability lower bound over trajectories. This candidate appears to be an exact scalar square-law specialization with an explicit quadratic-control-energy price. I did not derive their metric expression and prove it equals `Q`, nor audit older minimax/set-membership control for the same exact optimality claim. Status is therefore `candidate_connection / novelty_unknown`, not a transfer or priority claim.

The 2023 i-IOSS/FIE/MHE and 2025 IQC/MHE theorems instead establish robust estimation/closed-loop stability once incremental or IQC detectability is supplied. They are broader estimator guarantees but do not, in the read result statements, optimize a purchased probe-energy budget. This distinction is a useful research interface; it is not yet an advance.

## Missing decisive gate

An independent prior-art audit must establish whether (a) the open-loop minimax rate, (b) constant-speed optimality under an `L2` control budget, and (c) the inability of extra worst-case-noise samples to improve the bound are already corollaries of set-valued observability/metric regularity or earlier active-sensing/minimax-control work. Only if a precise gap survives should the bridge be extended to adaptive policies, multidimensional states, or nonconstant output uncertainty. Adaptive-policy optimality is not proved here.
