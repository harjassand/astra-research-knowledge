## Sol baselines

**c04_s01 — O(d)-covariant rounding.** The frozen claim is an all-dimension c=2 Dirichlet rounding theorem for positive selfadjoint selfcompatible channels covariant under every real orthogonal conjugation. The proof reduces the map to two non-scalar irreducible components, derives three Choi compatibility inequalities, then mixes two explicit canonical orbit EB channels. I checked the displayed two-sector mechanism against the stated interface; it uses the full O(d) real structure and does not apply after twirling an arbitrary channel and transferring back. This is a meaningful restricted theorem, with external priority and the arithmetic behind some representation bounds still needing independent review. Reported exact-Fraction checks cover d=2..24; the floating spin-1 probe is not proof. Artifacts: work/cycle6/c04_s01/INITIAL.txt, orthogonal_rounding_checks.py/.json.

**c04_s02 — symmetric bosonic splitting.** The frozen claim constructs an actual compatible marginal Φ=R T from the symmetric-half grouping isometry and proves the coherent-state EB comparison with the optimal constant (2(s+d)/(2s+d)<2). The key proof is a multiplicity-one U(d) harmonic decomposition and a factorwise scalar deficit bound, with an explicit product inequality that avoids assuming factor order (a_j\le l_j). It rules out a counterexample only inside this corrected bosonic Petz-splitting family. The reported numerical channel fixtures and 22,320 rational scalar comparisons diagnose transcription; the universal statement rests on the representation derivation. No efficient coherent-state quadrature or acquisition is claimed. Artifacts: work/cycle6/c04_s02/INITIAL.txt, symmetric_split_checks.py/.json.

**c04_s03 — arbitrary reference-preserving EB preparation.** The frozen proof writes the canonical measurement map Ψ=T^†T and an arbitrary EB branch Q=PT, uses (Q^†Q\le\Psi), and derives the factor-two SOS for Φ=C^†C, C=(L+Q)/2. It realizes this by symmetrizing a supplied reference-preserving instrument-append channel and applying the physical Petz correction to both receivers. This is a broad but still structural subclass, not a representation theorem for all compatible maps. Its exact Fraction fixture verifies one block-changing example; no general bare-channel QE search was executed. Artifacts: work/cycle6/c04_s03/INITIAL.txt, weighted_instrument_check.py/.json, povm_compiler_check.py/.json.

## Independent synthesis, proof gaps, and next gate

The reports agree that three scopes must remain separate: (i) exact factor-two rounding for supplied POVM/Luders, instrument-Petz, O(d)-covariant, and related symmetric-splitting classes; (ii) sharp finite lower bounds c>=2, calibrated by universal cloning; (iii) the unrestricted positive selfadjoint selfcompatible channel problem. No peer artifact provides a growing sequence with residual tending to zero and EB error bounded below, and none proves the unrestricted c=2 comparator or weaker modulus.

My additional exact gate is representational: any midpoint (C=(L+Q)/2), with L bistochastic and Q any bistochastic EB map on M_d, has (\|Q\|_{F,0}^2\le d-1) and hence, when C^†C is λ-depolarizing, λ\le(1+1/\sqrt{d+1})^2/4. The universal-cloner marginal exceeds this cap strictly for all d>=2. This broadens the canonical c04_l09 exclusion to the arbitrary-preparation interface c04_s03, but it only rejects that specific midpoint/Petz-square construction. A different factorization or direct EB comparator can still work, as the qutrit example explicitly shows.

The decisive mathematical gate remains an exact small-dimensional channel whose optimal EB Dirichlet ratio is above 2, or a proof of the all-frame inequality for arbitrary compatible maps. Even the former would not refute a weaker dimension-free modulus; that requires a growing near-fixed sequence with nonvanishing collective variance/EB error. The decisive computational gate is also distinct: a finite POVM outcome bound does not acquire the channel, family, reference, or an efficient minimizing measurement.
