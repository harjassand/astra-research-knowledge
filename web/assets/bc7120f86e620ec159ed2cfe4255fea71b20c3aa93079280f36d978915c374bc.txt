P12 ideal heralded law — 2026-10-08

Under a TMSV pair prior P(N=n)=(1-z)z^n, z=lambda^2, balanced R-port idler fanout, independent pure loss eta, and number-resolving heralding of exactly one photon in every idler port:

- N<R cannot herald; q_R=P(H|N=R)=eta^R R!/R^R, equal to R!/R^(2R) at eta=1/R.
- For j>=0, P(H|N=R+j)=q_R binom(R+j,R)(1-eta)^j.
- With a=z(1-eta), J=N-R conditioned on H is exactly NB(R+1,a). Thus P(N>R|H)=1-(1-a)^(R+1) <= (R+1)z(1-eta). This sharpens the requested z/((1-z)q_R) bound.
- For lambda=2^(-R^2), eta=1/R, the exact tail is at most (R+1)2^(-2R^2). The exact success mass is (1-z)z^R q_R(1-a)^(-(R+1)) ~ 2^(-2R^3)q_R.

Crucial interface condition: the claimed Bin(R,1/R) signal law requires independent signal loss with transmissivity 1/R. Then the actual post-herald signal count is within T=1-(1-a)^(R+1) of Bin(R,1/R), and within T+1/R of Poisson(1) in total variation by Le Cam. If the signal is retained losslessly, its count tends to the point mass at R and its TV distance from Poisson(1) tends to 1. So “retain signal” alone does not imply the Poisson law.
