Scope and verdict

This audit checks the explicit expectation constant used in RESULT.txt and
records an optional finite Haar trace diagnostic. The trace diagnostic is not
needed for the main existence result because the exact product-conjugate
quotient-Gram bound applies to every finite representation. The score model
is Haar U(N), for which the Bordenave-
Collins unitary Weingarten argument and Meckes product-Haar concentration
apply. The Bell witness is Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N). The 2026 source's exact
real-orthogonal quotient does not transfer to Phi_N tensor Phi_N with complex
U(N) branches; instead the product-conjugate quotient is derived directly in
evidence/PRODUCT_CONJUGATE_REPAIR.txt. The free-radius proof for C^2<=50 is
reconstructed in evidence/FREE_RADIUS_AUDIT.txt and remains internally
audited rather than externally validated.

1. Explicit upper bound for the expectation constant

For one Haar U(N) generator with an even number q of matrix-entry factors,
Bordenave-Collins 2012 Theorem 11 gives, under N>=4 and 2q^(7/2)<=N^2,

  N^(q/2) |E product U_(x_t,y_t)^(epsilon_t)|
    <= (1 + 3 q^(7/2)/N^2)
       E product (G_(x_t,y_t)^(epsilon_t) + theta),
  theta=q N^(-1/4).

If a coordinate pair occurs a times without conjugation and b times with
conjugation, r=a+b, its shifted-Gaussian factor is exactly

  F_(a,b)(theta) = sum_(j=0)^min(a,b)
      binom(a,j) binom(b,j) j! theta^(r-2j).

This supplies a needed detail omitted from the first draft: multiplicity
three does not vanish after shifting; F_(2,1)=2 theta+theta^3. For r=1,
F=theta. For r=2 or 3, F<=(1+theta)^r. For r>=4, j!<=r^(r/2) and
binom(a,j)binom(b,j)<=binom(r,2j), so
F<=r^(r/2)(1+theta)^r. Multiplying over coordinate pairs gives

  E product (G^epsilon+theta)
      <= theta^(e1) (1+theta)^q q^(m4/2),

where e1 counts singleton coordinate pairs and m4 sums multiplicities at
least four. Since (1+theta)^q<=exp(q theta), no additional numerical factor
is needed in the unitary path estimate.

For a closed colored path of even length m, let q_i count occurrences of
generator i, including inverse letters. Independence multiplies the
Weingarten factors, and

  product_i (1+3 q_i^(7/2)/N^2)
      <= exp(3 sum_i q_i^(7/2)/N^2)
      <= exp(3 m^(7/2)/N^2) <= exp(3/2),

using 2m^(7/2)<=N^2. The Gaussian shifts contribute at most
exp(sum_i q_i^2 N^(-1/4))<=exp(m^2 N^(-1/4)); the Bordenave-Collins graph
bound m4<=4 chi gives q^(m4/2)<=m^(2 chi). Thus the path prefactor in
Corollary 5.5 can be taken as exp(3/2), with exp(m eta), eta=mN^(-1/4).

In Bordenave-Collins 2023 Theorem 5.1, the two geometric ratios are at most
1/2, contributing at most 4. The class count is
(2 d m^(d+1))^4=16d^4m^(4d+4), and exp(m eta)<=e under its displayed ell_0
condition. The other displayed factors in the path-count lemmas have
coefficient one. Hence the explicit trace coefficient can be taken as

  4 * 16 * e * exp(3/2) = 64 exp(5/2)
       = 779.679613485... < 780.

The sum over m=1,...,ell costs no further constant because
sum_m m^(4d+4)<=ell^(4d+5); the ell^11 term gives ell^(4d+16). For d=64,
ell=p, N=p^1104, the stated reduction to c/sqrt(N) is valid since
64^4 p^272<=p^552 iff 64^4<=p^280. Jensen in Theorem 1.1 preserves this
upper-bound constant, and Lemma 9.3 reuses it on each tensor leg. This is an
internal reconstruction, not a constant printed by the authors. The
Corollary 14 q=1 shortcut is not used because that corollary has a separate
hypothesis and unspecified universal constant.

For the chosen even p=31,982,608,962 and N=p^1104, all source gates hold:
p<=N^(1/1104), N>=64^70, and 2q_i^(7/2)<=N^2 for q_i<=p. In Lemma 9.3
with d=64, k=2 and coefficient dimension n=514, the expectation factor is

 R=1028^(3/p) N^(3/p) p^(9/p) (1+780/sqrt(N))^2.

The script assigns half of ln(1+e/2) to the known factor and half to the
constant factor. It checks the known logarithm directly; for the hidden term,
2 ln(1+780/sqrt(N))<=1560/sqrt(N), so it suffices to check
1560/sqrt(N)<=the allocated log budget. The exact logarithmic check passes
at the displayed p,N. This is separate from, and not inferred from, the
concentration tail.

2. Optional product-conjugate Bell word trace diagnostic

Let m=N^2 and let pi_N:F_64 x F_64 -> U(m) be the tensor-leg representation
with independent Haar U(N) generators on the two factors. For the inverse-
closed branch set S of size K=256, write W_s=pi_N(s). The random-unitary
channel E_N(X)=K^-1 sum_s W_s X W_s* has complementary channel

  Phi_N(X)_(s,t)=K^-1 Tr(W_s X W_t*).

For the normalized Bell vector Omega_m, the complementary branch vectors of
E_N tensor bar(E_N) are

  (W_s tensor bar(W_t)) Omega_m
      = vec(W_s W_t*)/sqrt(m)
      = vec(pi_N(s t^-1))/sqrt(m).

Thus they are indexed by the honest relative group element s t^-1. Since S
is inverse-closed, the map (a,b)=(s^-1,t^-1) identifies this fibre map with
the source's a^-1 b. The quotient Gram matrix is Hermitian PSD with diagonal
q, so Schur-Horn gives output entropy <=H(q); the finite source-algebra
extension and its scope are proved in PRODUCT_CONJUGATE_REPAIR.txt.

An output matrix entry is K^-2 times a normalized trace
f(w)=tau_(N^2)(pi_N(w)) for a word of length at most four. The raw trace
word may be identity, in which case it matches the free trace exactly. For a
nontrivial reduced word of length <=4 in independent Haar U(N) generators,
independent phase invariance makes its mean zero unless all generator counts
are balanced. The only nontrivial balanced reduced words at these lengths are
length-four commutators, up to inverse/cyclic variants; Schur averaging gives
E tau_N(U V U* V*)=1/N^2. Tensor-leg traces factor, so
|E f(w)|<=N^-2 for nonidentity words.

Telescoping the at-most-four letters and using normalized trace duality gives
L(f)<=4/sqrt(N) for the product U(N)^128 Hilbert-Schmidt metric. Meckes
Theorem 5.17 applied to plus/minus the real and imaginary parts yields

  P(|f-Ef|>delta/2)
       <=4 exp(-(N-2)N delta^2/3072).

Together with N^-2<=delta/2, this makes every raw trace within delta of its
free value. For the Bell matrix, each entry has error <=delta/K^2. There are
K^4 entries, so ||Delta||_F<=delta and trace distance
T=||Delta||_1/2<=K delta/2. Audenaert continuity at output dimension K^2
allows T<=4.5262491e-5 for entropy error 0.001 nats; thus the corrected
sufficient raw-trace tolerance is delta<=3.5361321e-7. Union over at most
K^4 words gives failure <=1/6 for N>=789,307,382. The older packet used the
valid but unnecessarily strong estimate T<=K^3 delta/2, smaller delta by
K^2 and a Bell threshold 51,728,048,483,335; the evidence script now uses
the correctly normalized entry bound. The score/expectation threshold
N=p^1104 remains overwhelmingly larger.

The N^-2 trace-mean bound is specific to unitary Haar. It is false for
Haar O(N): E tau_N(O^2)=1/N. No orthogonal concentration/expectation claim
is made in this audit.

3. Main finite score gate and limits

At the selected p,N, the score-net failure is <=1/3. The exact product-
conjugate Bell quotient has entropy <=H(q) for every finite representation,
so no second random event or trace-continuity allowance is needed. Conditional
on the free score bound C^2=50, this gives a Phi_N tensor bar(Phi_N)
von Neumann additivity gap at least 0.0011986 nats with probability >=2/3.
A standard flagged channel
Xi_N:M_(2N^2)->M_256 converts this pair witness into a Xi_N tensor Xi_N
witness, adding one input flag qubit per use; see the exact construction in
PRODUCT_CONJUGATE_REPAIR.txt. The flag device is standard and not a novelty
claim.

This is finite probabilistic existence at an explicit integer N, not a
matrix seed, efficient sampler, circuit, or practical channel. The free-radius
source proof is reconstructed in FREE_RADIUS_AUDIT.txt, but has not received
external validation. Novelty and priority remain unknown.
