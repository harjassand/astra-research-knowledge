## Exact independently derived theorem

Let Gamma contain free elements p_1,...,p_m, write lambda for its left regular representation, and put

\[
 T_m=I+\sum_{j=1}^m\lambda(p_j),\qquad Q_m=T_mT_m^*,\qquad e=\delta_1.
\]

The spectral measure of Q_m/m at e has the exact finite-m density

\[
 \rho_m(x)=\frac{m(m+1)}{2\pi((m+1)^2-mx)}
            \sqrt{\frac{4-x}{x}}\;\mathbf1_{(0,4)}(x),
 \qquad \|Q_m\|=4m.
\]

The proof hermitizes T_m as the adjacency operator of the (m+1)-regular tree, derives its root resolvent by the branch recursion F(z)=1/(z-mF(z)), and squares and rescales the resulting Kesten measure. An equivalent singular-value formula already appears in [Basak-Dembo, equation (5.8)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.5100), citing [Haagerup-Larsen, equation (5.7)](https://doi.org/10.1006/jfan.2000.3610). Exact density is therefore prior art, even though the release uses a Catalan-moment limit for its small-cocycle step.

For any vector h, even of infinite support, the simultaneous requirements

\[
 \|h\|_2\le\eta,\qquad \|T_mh-e\|_2\le\eta
\]

force

\[
 m\ge\frac{3}{64\pi^2\eta^4}.
\]

This follows by projecting Q_m to [0,1]. The density gives a projected mass at least sqrt(3)/(2*pi*sqrt(m)), while the two inequalities bound its square root by 2*eta. This lower bound is for the stated prefix-sum interface, not all cocycles or all possible free-factor isomorphisms.

Conversely, given a **rational** 0<eta<=1, take the least power of two m>=2048*eta^-4, set d=eta^4/4096 and b=m(4+d), and choose L by the exact rational stopping test

\[
 (4/(4+d))^{L+1}\le\eta/4.
\]

For a choice not requiring expansion of enormous rational powers to verify this test, put q=ceil(4/d), take the least t with 2^t>=4/eta, and use L+1=q*t. Bernoulli's inequality gives (1+d/4)^q>=2 and proves the test. These scalar parameters are acquired by integer division and bit comparisons.

The explicitly specified rational group-ring vector

\[
 h_L=T_m^*\,b^{-1}\sum_{k=0}^{L}
            \left(\frac{4mI-Q_m}{b}\right)^k e
\]

satisfies ||h_L||_2<eta/4 and ||T_m h_L-e||_2<eta/2. The proof uses the density bound rho_m(x)<=3/(pi*sqrt(x)), two elementary beta integrals, and a positive geometric-series remainder. It requires no empirical spectral approximation or unspecified support cutoff. One can use L=O(eta^-4 log(4/eta)).

For F_3=<a,y,z>, acquire the free p_j as follows. On k-bit strings, y rotates left and z rotates left and flips the final bit. These two permutations give a covering of the two-petal rose with m=2^k vertices. Every vertex lies within k steps of the root. A breadth-first spanning tree yields m+1 free Schreier words of length at most 2k+1 in <y,z>. Choose m of them as b_j, set p_j=(ab_1)...(ab_j), and w=p_m a. Free-basis changes prove that the p_j are free. Setting D_a=h_L and D_y=D_z=0 then gives

\[
 D_w=T_m h_L,\qquad |w|\le 2m(k+1)+1.
\]

This improves the direct conjugate-word implementation's quadratic length in m to O(m log m). That word length is also optimal up to constants within the fixed F_3 positive-a block interface. Freeness of the prefixes forces all b_j distinct, while only 2*3^r-1 reduced <y,z> words have length at most r. Hence at least half of them have length greater than floor(log_3(m/4)), yielding |w|=Omega(m log m). Combining this with the prefix-count lower bound gives the matching eta^-4 log(4/eta) scale in that restricted interface. It works with the other cocycles zero in every F_n, n>=3. A two-generator relative statement does not follow: one unused cyclic generator cannot provide this free family.

The iteration chooses the present tolerance before its witness word, then chooses the future budget after the substituted word-polynomial Lipschitz constant is known. The trace documents abandoned routes and this eventual mechanism. It is an edited account, not a replay of all reasoning or an independent mathematical check. ## Direct finiteness: what was reconstructed and what transfers

The [October 4 torsion-free manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Torsion-Free-Group-Algebra-That-Is-Not-Directly-Finite-October-4-2026/direct-finiteness.pdf) has a short exact algebra interface. Immersed graphs supply outgoing label sets whose intersection parities make components of their simultaneous-step graphs even, except one odd identity component. This proves ab=1 and ac=0 in F_2[G]. Protection of the B root makes the identity coefficient of c exactly one, hence c!=0 and ba!=1. Only B-root protection is required for this algebra argument; the source itself states this simplification. The hard premise is root protection after all graph cycles have been killed. The source obtains it through random bounded-path estimates, a planar separator passage to arbitrary spherical arrangements, and cone surgeries. I reconstructed the finite parity proof and examined the planar/topological interfaces, without certifying that entire package.

The [September 23 characteristic-two construction](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Counterexample-to-Kaplanskys-Direct-Finiteness-Conjecture-in-Characteristic-Two-September-23-2026/paper.pdf) is different. Its local finite-field groups, character orthogonality, rectangular inverse, HNN packing, and finite matrix block retain odd-order torsion. Its [reasoning summary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/reasoning_traces/kaplansky-direct-finiteness-characteristic-two.pdf) discusses the characteristic boundary and leaves suggested geometric strengthening unresolved. A torsion-free heading in the formalization catalogue cannot transfer this older construction to the October 4 companion. The release-forensics worker independently flags that the detailed family-197 comparator scope explicitly includes torsion and omits its nonsofic and determinant spectral-integral conclusions. I did not run the Lean comparator.

From **supplied** a,b in K[G] with ab=1!=ba, all of the following have exact proofs in the second artifact:

- e=1-ba is a nonzero idempotent; b^i e a^j are infinite matrix units. The map K<x,y | xy=1> -> K[G], x->a,y->b, is injective, by a terminating rewrite basis and a coefficient-isolation argument. This is the classical [Toeplitz/Jacobson mechanism](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.00109). It does not imply a Leavitt algebra of type (1,2), or a characteristic-zero defect.
- The finite-memory rule T_b f(g)=sum b_u f(gu) is injective, with left inverse T_a. A delta configuration selected by a nonzero coefficient of ba-1 lies outside its image. For finite K, the converse follows from compactness of K^G and extension of a finite-coordinate linear functional. Thus direct finiteness is equivalent to K-linear surjunctivity at this exact interface.
- For square matrices, rank(I-AB)=rank(I-BA). An exactly unital linear algebra model multiplicative at (a,b) and (b,a) with normalized rank error <=epsilon must give e rank <=2*epsilon. By [Arzhantseva-Paunescu, Theorem 7.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.6780), a rank-metric group embedding over K can be amplified to a faithful K[G] embedding. Consequently the supplied defect group is **not K-linearly sofic**. A characteristic-two defect alone gives no complex linear-sofic or nonhyperlinear conclusion.
- If the protected-root parity construction supplies c=sum_{j=0}^{s-1} h_j^-1 with h_0=1 and h_j!=1 otherwise, a finite permutation model with fixed-point error delta and normalized rank errors epsilon_1 at ab=1 and epsilon_2 at ac=0 must satisfy epsilon_1+epsilon_2 >= (1-(s-1)delta)/s^2. The proof selects disjoint supports in sparse columns of the model of c, then uses Sylvester's inequality. It does not need all h_j distinct. This provides a finite robust implication with the same classical sparse-column mechanism as [Elek-Szabo](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0305440).
- If one such group D is finitely presented, direct finiteness is a Markov property: it is hereditary to subgroups, the trivial group satisfies it, and no positive group contains D. The [Adian-Rabin construction](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.08560), Theorem 1 and Main Lemma on PDF pages 76-78, maps a word w in a fixed torsion-free finitely presented group with undecidable word problem to a presentation that is trivial if w=1 and contains D if w!=1. The torsion-free input makes every nonidentity w infinite-order, as required by that Main Lemma. A failure semidecider would enumerate nonidentity words; together with enumeration of identity words this would decide that word problem. The same proof handles the other hereditary properties listed above.

Presentation-only direct finiteness has the elementary arithmetical upper bound Pi^0_2: for every finite a,b and proof of ab=1, there must exist a proof of ba=1. Failure is Sigma^0_2, without a completeness claim. With an additional correct total word-problem decider, finite defects can instead be enumerated and checked. Asphericity of a finite two-complex alone is not such a decider, a negative-curvature metric, or a linear filling bound.

## Resources, diagnostics, and rejected transfers

The cocycle input is one rational eta of bit length B. The covering, its spanning tree, its free words, m, L, and the rational arithmetic expression are all acquired explicitly; no graph, spectral oracle, or coefficient vector is supplied for free. Elementary integer arithmetic acquires the scalar parameters within a conservative O(B^3) bit-operation bound. The graph and words take O(m log m) retained symbols. A straight-line description of h_L takes O(m log m+log L) arithmetic gates, using repeated doubling for the geometric sum, and an ordinary pointer encoding uses O((m log m+log L)*log(m log m+L+2)+B+log m) retained bits. This description is not the time or space cost for emitting its represented group-ring vector or integrating its operator flow.

A conservative support-emission upper bound is (m+1)*((m+1)^2+1)^L, with maximal word length <=(2L+1)*2m(log_2(m)+1). Coefficient heights have O((L+1)(log m+bitlength(eta))) bits; a common denominator is [m(16384*v^4+u^4)]^(L+1) for eta=u/v. The resulting support upper bound is exp(O(eta^-4 log^2(4/eta))). No matching support lower bound is proved. For eta=1, the chosen m=2048 and safe L=28392 already give an upper support count about 10^188046. This count was not emitted and is not a runtime measurement.

The decisive negative checks and boundaries are:

1. Replacing the freely independent prefixes by cyclic powers changes the m=4 second moment from 45 to 85. Thus an arbitrary prefix family or a cyclic family cannot use the same density.
2. Faithful group representations need not have faithful linear extensions. Over F_4, C_3 embeds as scalar matrices but g-omega*1 is killed. The amplification in the linear-sofic transfer is necessary.
3. Small prefix count and short expression storage do not make support expansion or an infinite operator-algebra construction affordable.
4. The characteristic-two packing/parity does not survive as a characteristic-zero defect; finite von Neumann traces prohibit the latter.
5. The older direct-finiteness construction's torsion, and the companion's mere asphericity, cannot be replaced by the hyperbolicity required by Astra's geometric candidate.
6. The conditional recognition theorem is unrestricted. The Adian-Rabin reduction has not been shown to preserve torsion-freeness, hyperbolicity, an aspherical-complex promise, or a supplied word-problem algorithm.
