ADMITTED GRAMMAR
There are disjoint finite root, middle, third and fourth species sets. Roots
R_i have 0<->R_i with positive rational rate boxes. Each middle B_j has a
nonempty root parent subset P_j and the paired channels R_i<->R_i+B_j for
i in P_j. Every third species C_k has exactly one middle parent p(k) and
B_p(k)<->B_p(k)+C_k. Every fourth species D_l has exactly one third parent
p(l) and C_p(l)<->C_p(l)+D_l. No other reactions are admitted. Roots and the
fourth set are nonempty; branches and shared root catalysts are allowed.
All coefficients are arbitrary predictable full-history controls in their
supplied positive rational boxes. The complete raw-table compiler acquires
the layer partition and consumes all records; no equivalent-realization search
or unprovided grammar-recognition oracle is assumed.

REDUCTION TO THE EXPLICIT SCALAR PROOF
For each middle j define S_j=sum_{i in P_j}R_i. Its root immigration coefficient
is between sum_i b_i^- and sum_i b_i^+. Its death intensity is
sum_i delta_i R_i <=(max_i delta_i^+)S_j. Middle birth/death intensities are
sum_i alpha_ij R_i and B_j sum_i beta_ij R_i. For S_j>0 they equal
alpha_eff S_j and beta_eff S_j B_j, with
  alpha_eff in [min_i alpha_ij^-,max_i alpha_ij^+],
  beta_eff in [min_i beta_ij^-,max_i beta_ij^+].
For S_j=0 they vanish exactly; no effective coefficient is needed there.
Thus every root reciprocal, quadratic-phase, and killed-corrector inequality
in the four-chain proof applies pointwise with A=S_j and these conservative
aggregate bounds. Multiple parents never jump simultaneously. Other species'
reactions leave S_j,B_j,C_k unchanged. Dependence of rate choices on those
species does not change the pointwise bound or introduce an extra generator
derivative.

For each middle acquire g_j=1+c_j/(S_j+1), gamma_j,G_j,K_Bj by section 4 and
the scalar cubic envelope. For each third species acquire bounded q_k,
gamma_k,G_k,K_Ck from its parent middle and section 4. For each fourth species
acquire the NEW unbounded q_l=M_l+1/(C_parent+1)
 +epsilon_l(B_middle-1)^2/(S_middle+s_l), gamma_l,K_Dl from sections 1-3.
Their exact drifts obey
  L(g_j B_j^3)<=-gamma_j B_j^3+G_j K_Bj S_j,
  L(q_k C_k^3)<=-gamma_k C_k^3+G_k K_Ck B_p(k),
  L(q_l D_l^3)<=-gamma_l D_l^3+t_Bl B_middle^3+t_Cl C_parent^3,
where t_Bl=K_Dl 4epsilon_l/(3s_l) and
t_Cl=K_Dl[M_l+1+8epsilon_l/(3s_l)], by section 5's arithmetic/geometric bound.

ACQUIRED BACKWARD WEIGHTS
For third k, middle j and root i, respectively, set
  w_Ck=[1+sum_{l:p(l)=k}t_Cl]/gamma_k,
  w_Bj=[1+sum_{l:middle(l)=j}t_Bl
             +sum_{k:p(k)=j}w_Ck G_k K_Ck]/gamma_j,
  w_Ri=2[1+sum_{j:i in P_j}w_Bj G_j K_Bj]/delta_i^-.
Let K_Ri be the root cubic envelope, C0=sum_i w_Ri K_Ri, and
  V=sum_i w_Ri R_i^3+sum_j w_Bj g_j B_j^3
      +sum_k w_Ck q_k C_k^3+sum_l q_l D_l^3.
Summing the drift inequalities and using each integer count x<=x^3 yields
  LV<=C0-f,
  f=sum_i R_i^3+sum_j B_j^3+sum_k C_k^3+sum_l gamma_l D_l^3.
V is nonnegative and norm-like. No S_j^3 expansion is needed: each parent
cost S_j is distributed as a linear sum over root counts before absorption.

RETURN, RECURRENCE AND TOTAL FIRING COST
Put kappa=min(1,min_l gamma_l), d=number of species,
R=max(1,ceil[(2C0+2)/kappa]), and K={N<R}. Then f>=kappa N, so outside K,
LV<=-f/2-1. Nonexplosion follows separately from LN<=b_total+L N with
finite catalyst birth gains. The same stopped proof as section 6 gives
  E tau_K<=V(x), E integral_0^tau_K f<=2V(x).
All species can be activated in layer order. To move between two lattice
states, adjust fourth counts first, then third, middle and root counts, keeping
their parents positive until those adjustments finish. Hence the full lattice
is actually one irreducible class. Homogeneous state feedback gives positive
recurrence, a unique invariant law, and E_pi f<=C0. General history controls
receive recovery bounds, not fictitious stationary distributions.

Let T0 be the sum of all raw reaction rate upper endpoints (parallel channels
may be aggregated). For N>=1 every propensity is <=N^2 and
  q_total<=T0 N^2<=d T0 sum_x x^3<=(d T0/kappa)f.
The compensation argument gives the acquired all-firing bound
  E[# firings through return to K]<=(2d T0/kappa)V(x).
This includes arbitrary fast cycles among all branches. It does not price
controller evaluation, fuel per firing or hardware energy.

IMPLEMENTATION AND COST
four_layer_forest_certificate.py uses the own scalar module alongside it,
without peer imports or scripts. It admits raw source/target vectors and exact
rational rate intervals, aggregates parallel channels, recognizes the four
layers, and rejects extra/deeper/cyclic/self-catalytic channels. Rational
operation count is polynomial in explicit d and channel count; fixed-depth,
fixed-degree sums/products give polynomial bit work in the ordinary dense
input length. V evaluation is polynomial in that length and binary count length.
Core enumeration is neither required nor done; numeric constants can be enormous.

four_layer_forest_checks.json records 1,009 complete worst-box raw-generator
states, 144 killed-gate states, four successful raw-table reconstructions
(four-species chain and eight-species branched/shared-root fixture, each in
two species orders), and two fifth-layer rejection checks, about 0.184 seconds
reported local script time. They test transcription only. The
all-state result is the reduction and coefficient absorption above. No generic
solver, trajectory simulation, corpus mutation or external validation was run.

PROVENANCE AND REMAINING GATE
The own independent three-layer result supplies lower-layer reciprocals.
The new quadratic phase representation supplies the fourth layer. c10_s03's
separate post-exposure ancestor-closed-gate attachment is complementary and not
a premise; it deliberately does not claim this bare-chain gate. Constant-rate
recurrence through autonomous stochastic environments has substantial published
priority (arXiv:1912.00401). Novelty of this acquired common predictable-control
representation and explicit all-event cost is UNKNOWN.
The fifth-layer or arbitrary-depth attachment is not established by this proof.
The simple reuse q_E=base+1/(D+1)+epsilon q_D(D-1)^2/(C+s) already fails:
at D=1 its added phase value vanishes, while neighboring D jumps contribute
positive multiples of unbounded q_D. At fixed A,C and B->infinity, the killed
drift is positive for every fixed positive epsilon and finite base. A separate
construction must control that faster interaction; one cannot iterate this
four-layer formula by renaming its species.
