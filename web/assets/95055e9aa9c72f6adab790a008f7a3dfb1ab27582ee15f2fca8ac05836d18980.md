## Concrete constructive consequence

Family 332 Remark 5.3 already identifies its witness as an expected coordinatewise median of three independent geometric endpoints. We made that formula into a deterministic finite rational constructor. For rational (A\in\mathbb Q^{n\times n}), positive integer (t), and vectors (x_i\in\mathbb Q^m), compute (G=((t+1)I-tA)^{-1}). For feature (k), sort distinct input values (a_1<\cdots<a_q) and calculate
\[
y_i(k)=a_1+\sum_{r<q}(a_{r+1}-a_r)(3h_{i,k,r}^2-2h_{i,k,r}^3),\quad
h_{i,k,r}=\sum_{j:x_j(k)\ge a_{r+1}}G_{ij}.
\]
This is exact because the median crosses a threshold iff at least two of three samples do. It uses at most (n-1) upper cuts per feature, takes (O(n^3+mn^2+mn\log n)) rational operations, and has polynomial output bit height. It returns vectors in the same finite ambient ℓ₁ space. The prototype differentially matches an exact triple enumerator and passes the finite (3024) inequality on a rational nonreversible stationary fixture for (t=1,2,5). The universal inequality still depends on the released proof; the finite tests do not establish it.

The stochastic endpoint alternative can sample three independent geometric paths per output state, requiring (3t) expected transition steps. That alternative outputs a randomized median sample, not the exact expectation; transition-oracle calls are explicitly charged. The full-matrix exact route is deterministic and bit-polynomial.
