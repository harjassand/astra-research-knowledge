COMMON APPROXIMATE-STATE CONSEQUENCE OF THE SPIN-ONE THEOREM
Coordinator derivation, 2026-10-09; same internal status as the scoped theorem.

Let Phi be a supplied unital self-compatible channel on M3. Select the one
canonical pure-ensemble comparator Psi guaranteed by S3. For ANY state
rho=(I+A)/3 with A in span_R{J1,J2,J3}, ||A||infinity<=theta, if
T(Phi(rho),rho)<=epsilon then the SAME comparator obeys
  T(Psi(rho),rho)<=sqrt(theta epsilon).
The comparator is chosen once for Phi and works for all such rho; it is not
chosen separately for each state. No channel self-adjointness is assumed.

Proof. Write <X,Y>tau=Tr(X*Y)/3. A canonical EB channel is positive
semidefinite as a Hilbert-Schmidt operator and is a unital TP contraction;
therefore (I-Psi)^2 <= I-Psi. The proved quadratic comparison gives
 ||(I-Psi)A||tau,2^2 <= <A,(I-Psi)A>tau
                       <=2<A,(I-Phi)A>tau.
Since Phi(I)=I, <A,(I-Phi)A>tau=Tr[A(rho-Phi(rho))], at most
||A||infinity ||rho-Phi(rho)||1 <=2theta epsilon by Holder's inequality.
Cauchy-Schwarz for normalized trace then gives
 T(Psi(rho),rho)=(1/2)||A-Psi(A)||tau,1
               <=(1/2)||A-Psi(A)||tau,2 <=sqrt(theta epsilon).
The real quadratic form is valid even if Phi is not HS-self-adjoint.

This extends a previously checked elementary conversion from a common
Dirichlet comparison to this new non-Clifford span. No novelty is claimed
for the conversion itself. It does not cover general qutrit density matrices,
which can have five additional quadrupole components. It assumes supplied
channel/state interfaces, and does not provide finite-bit compilation or
experimental acquisition of the <=15-state ensemble.
