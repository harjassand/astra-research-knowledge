1. THE ISOTROPIC STATE-SPACE GENERATOR HAS A GLOBAL POSITIVE FACTORIZATION

 v_F={F,rho}-2Tr(F rho)rho, w_F=-i[F,rho],

HS completeness gives

Write p=Tr rho^2. For coefficient C=I, contractions in the generic drift
formula simplify EXACTLY:

Thus the generator of M_(qQ)(X)={qQ,X}/2 on product kernels has drift

 a_iso(rho)=2q(N-1)(rho^2-p rho),                    (G5)

potential

and covariance q D_I/2. The exact centered KMS identity from the previous
file is
 <Z,D_I Z>=4Tr[rho(Z-Tr(rho Z)I)rho(Z-Tr(rho Z)I)].

Define, at every faithful rho, the Hermitian traceless directions

Parseval gives D_I=4 sum_a u_a tensor u_a, since the dual of u_a is
sqrt(rho) Z sqrt(rho)-Tr(rho Z)rho. This factorization is a positive physical
matrix-state factorization, not a doubled off-diagonal positive-P kernel.
It yields the explicit SDE

All coefficients are smooth in the positive-definite trace-one interior.

2. EXACT LOG-DETERMINANT SDE PREVENTS A FINITE-TIME BOUNDARY HIT

Until any boundary hit, Ito's log determinant formula is
 d log det rho=Tr(rho^-1 d rho)
                    -(1/2)Tr(rho^-1 d rho rho^-1 d rho).
For the directions in (G7),
 Tr(rho^-1 u_a)=-d f_a,
 Tr(rho^-1 u_a rho^-1 u_a)=Tr(F_a^2)+d f_a^2,
 sum_a f_a^2=p-1/d.
Therefore

 d log det rho
 =q[2N-d^2-d(2N-1)p]dt
                  -d sqrt(2q)sum_a f_a dW_a.         (G8)

Its martingale quadratic variation per unit time is exactly
 2q d^2(p-1/d)<=2q d^2.                              (G9)

On the positive trace-one domain 1/d<=p<=1, so both drift and variation in
(G8) are bounded for fixed q,N,d. If the first boundary time were finite,
log det rho would tend to minus infinity there. The bounded drift integral
and a continuous local martingale of bounded quadratic variation
have a finite limit on each finite stopped horizon; hence that
event has probability zero. More formally apply (G8) at successive determinant
cutoffs, the exponential-martingale maximal bound, and let the cutoff tend
to zero. Matrix coordinates cannot explode while rho is positive trace-one
because this set is bounded. Local strong existence/uniqueness thus extends
to every finite time. Repeated eigenvalues cause no problem: the matrix square
root is smooth on the PD cone, independently of eigenvalue multiplicity.

This is a genuine global positive process on normalized local density
matrices. It does not use boundary integration by parts or an assumed
classical path representation of the input quantum state.
