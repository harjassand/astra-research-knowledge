1. SUPPLIED VERSUS ACQUIRED

SUPPLIED: d; an exact rational-complex unnormalized Choi matrix of B; its
CP, TP, common-marginal, unital, and HS-self-adjoint promises; rational
0<delta<=1. Let L bound EACH real/imaginary numerator/denominator bit length,
and let ell be the full binary input length of delta. A requested physical
gate accuracy zeta has its own full input length ell_zeta. Reading these
inputs costs their full serialized length, bounded above by
O(d^6 L+ell+ell_zeta) and with that worst-case order in the dense model.

ACQUIRED: a rational Kraus instrument; fixed rational leaf effects; exact
partial-record effects and probabilities; T pure reference posteriors per
batch; the residual POVM; and exact rational PSD certificates. None is a
free oracle. There are zero physical quantum calls to B in this compiler.
Unbiased classical random bits are charged below. They are independent of
the input quantum state. The final compiled POVM and prepared states can
be used without any further B calls.

UNKNOWN/OUTSIDE SCOPE: acquisition or tomography of an unspecified physical
B; an exact promise from finite noisy data; arbitrary noncomputable real
matrix input; practicality on M4/16GB hardware; infinite-dimensional input.
The polynomial theorem is in d, not log d, and uses a dense d^6-size input.

The accuracy representation matters independently of 1/delta: a rational
near 1/2 can have an enormous numerator/denominator. All bounds retain ell.
After reading it one may round delta down to a dyadic within a factor two,
but the frozen algorithm uses the exact input delta and requires no rounding.

2. AN EXPLICIT RATIONAL ELIMINATION HEIGHT BOUND

For an h-by-h Hermitian rational-complex matrix with each real/imaginary
coefficient of height at most b, use the following deliberately loose bound:

  E(h,b)=8h[(2h^2+1)b+ceil(log2 h)+4].                  (C1)

The global denominator obtained by multiplying its at most 2h^2
denominators has at most 2h^2 b bits. After clearing it, Hadamard bounds
every minor by an integer of height at most
h[(2h^2+1)b+ceil(log2 h)+2]. Pivoted LDL* entries and pivots are ratios of
such minors (with real and imaginary parts). Therefore (C1) bounds their
heights and intermediate fraction-free elimination heights. Exact PSD
testing needs O(h^3) integer operations of O(E(h,b)) bits. With schoolbook
integer arithmetic this is O(h^3 E(h,b)^2) bit operations. Zero pivots are
handled by checking the remaining zero row/column; no minimum positive
pivot or floating-point gap is assumed.

Symmetrization of B increases coefficient height to at most L0=2L+3.
Let n=d^3, e=E(n,L0), and hloc=16e. LDL* supplies at most s<=d^3 branches.
Each coefficient of the rational adjoint branch I_j* has height <=hloc:
it is a sum of a bounded number of products q_j times two Kraus-vector
coefficients. Complex multiplication is accounted for in the factor 16.

Let Qloc be the product of ALL real/imaginary denominators of the dense
adjoint branch maps; there are at most 2s d^6<=2d^9 of them. Retain their
sum B* as a separate dense aggregate map. Put

  h=ceil(log2 Qloc) <=2d^9 hloc.                         (C2)

All these local maps have coefficients whose denominators divide Qloc.
The actual computed Qloc can replace the upper bound. It is needlessly
large but has polynomial bit length. Constructing it, local dense maps,
and the LDL instrument takes at most O(d^9(h+hloc)^2) bit operations;
this generous bound includes multiplication of all denominators.

3. TREE DEPTH, DENOMINATORS, AND EXACT SAMPLING

Put r=2d-1 and choose m by rational comparisons, as the least nonnegative
integer satisfying r(8/9)^m<=delta/8. Computing m by successive integer
multiplication uses O(m) comparisons on O(m+ell+log d) bits. Then

  K=2^m <=2(8r/delta)^alpha,
  alpha=log 2/log(9/8)<5.885, R=dr, s<=d^3.              (C3)

There are K leaves, K-1 internal instruments, and at most
s^(K-1)R^K complete fine records. This last alphabet is NOT enumerated.

Every partial-record root effect M_h can be stored with the SAME denominator

  D=Qloc^(K-1)(2r)^K.                                  (C4)

Proof: assigned leaves use rational effects with denominator 2r, and
unassigned leaves use I with that denominator padded. A depth subtree with
k leaves has denominator Qloc^(k-1)(2r)^k. At its internal node multiply
the two child denominators and one local-map denominator Qloc. This works
for assigned and unassigned Kraus variables, including arbitrary variable
interleavings. It sums entangled correlations; it does not factor a state.

Every event effect obeys 0<=M_h<=I. Thus the absolute real/imaginary parts
of each numerator are <=D. The probability is

  P(h)=c_h/(dD), c_h=Tr(D M_h) a nonnegative integer.

Use the bit bound

  b=2+ceil(log2 d)+(K-1)h+K ceil(log2(2r)).               (C5)

At a positive-probability prefix, child integers c_(h,v) sum EXACTLY to
c_h. Draw an integer uniformly from {0,...,c_h-1} by binary rejection and
locate it in the cumulative integer weights. This needs <=2b random bits
in expectation for each variable. Zero-probability values are not drawn.
No precision proportional to 1/P(h) is needed. For nonzero events the
denominator bound also gives P(h)>=1/(dD), whose logarithm is already
charged by (C5). This is exact sampling, not approximate rare-event conditioning.

Cache the d-by-d effect at each vertex. Each trial value changes its vertex
and at most m ancestors. A dense branch/aggregate pullback costs O(d^6)
integer operations: free nodes use the precomputed aggregate B*, rather
than resumming s branches at every contraction. A variable has at most
max(s,R)<=2d^3 values, and there are 2K-1 variables. Therefore one record
requires O(K(m+1)d^9) integer operations on O(b+log d) bits. Expected total
sampling bit cost across all batches is

  O(T K(m+1)d^9 (b+log d)^2).                            (C6)

Expected random bits are <=8TKb, since expected batches <=2. A completely
uncached implementation has the weaker O(TK^2 d^9) arithmetic bound, which
is still polynomial. The proof algorithm is not predicated on the cache.

For a sampled complete effect, pi=M/Tr M. Since all entries of M share D,
the normalized posterior has denominator dividing c_h, and coefficient
height <=b up to harmless signs. Square-root LDL scalars cancel before
normalization. All samples are explicitly computable rational projectors.

4. COMPRESSION, CERTIFICATION, AND OUTPUT HEIGHT

Take T=ceil(64d^2/delta^2), epsilon=delta/4, a=T+d. For a batch, summing its
sampled projectors and quadratic terms can use the product of their trace
integers as a common denominator. The following loose bounds suffice:

  bA=16[Tb+ell+ceil(log2(2Td))+h+L0],
  u=E(d,bA),
  bout=16d u+4bA,
  bQ=32[a bout+h+L0+ell+ceil(log2(2ad))].               (C7)

bA bounds the residual A=I-S/(1+epsilon), including the exact accuracy
fraction. u bounds its rational LDL factors. Normalizing their rank-one
columns and computing their effect masses involves at most d rational
summands and is bounded by bout. bout also bounds all sampled effects and
prepared pure projectors. A zero residual column is discarded.

bQ bounds the final full quadratic-form matrix in the fixed rational basis
E_jj, E_jk+E_kj, i(E_kj-E_jk). Its dimension is d^2; the basis Gram matrix
has diagonal entries 1 or 2. Full-order certification is therefore an exact
rational PSD test, not a numerical eigenvalue check or a diagonal-mode test.
The extra certificate has O(d^6) integer operations of height <=E(d^2,bQ).

One conservative expected bit-runtime bound for the whole compiler is

  O(d^9(h+hloc)^2
    +T K(m+1)d^9(b+log d)^2
    +a d^4 bQ^2
    +d^3 E(d,bA)^2
    +d^6 E(d^2,bQ)^2).                                 (C8)

Exact input promise checks, if desired, add polynomial CP/TP/marginal and
HS-symmetry tests and are dominated by these loose bounds. The output has
at most a rank-one effects and pure states, requiring O(a d^2 bout) bits.
Serializing the full quadratic PSD certificate's rational LDL factors
and pivots adds O(d^4 E(d^2,bQ)) bits, still polynomial. The replay stores
its full rational quadratic matrix and positive pivots; factors are
recomputed by the exact checker.
Peak storage can be bounded by O(Kd^2 b+Td^2 b+d^9 hloc+d^4 bQ), plus
polynomial elimination workspace. No d^K density matrix is stored.

Substitution of (C1)-(C7) shows expected runtime, random bits, output, and
space polynomial in d, 1/delta, L, ell. The exponents/constants are very
large. The theorem is meaningful computable extraction, not a claim that
its safe worst-case fallback is a useful small-device program. The replay
uses an adaptive shallow candidate ONLY because the exact final certificate
governs acceptance. The proved fallback remains (C3).

For the C8 corollary, Phi is acquired by partial trace from B. The rational
description of Theta=Phi*Phi and its broadcaster (Phi* tensor Phi*) B is
then constructed. Naive dense composition uses at most O(d^10) rational
operations; common-denominator multiplication and finite summation give
polynomial coefficient height in d,L. Apply the same bounds with that
computed height. Access to Phi* as a physical device is not supplied or
assumed: this is classical preprocessing of its explicitly known matrices.

5. STABILITY OF A FIXED POVM WITHOUT MINIMUM OUTCOME PROBABILITY

For PSD M put f(M)=|M><M|/Tr M, with f(0)=0, on Hermitian HS space. Then

  ||f(M)-f(N)||_(HS->HS) <=3||M-N||_1.                  (S1)

If m=Tr M>=n=Tr N>0, expand the difference as

  (|M-N><M|+|N><M-N|)/m +(1/m-1/n)|N><N|.

Use ||M||_2<=m, ||N||_2<=n, ||M-N||_2<=||M-N||_1, and
|m-n|<=||M-N||_1. The three terms are bounded by
||M-N||_1(1+n/m+n/m)<=3||M-N||_1. If n=0, the bound follows from
||f(M)||<=Tr M. Interchange M,N in the other case.

For matched POVM alphabets F_z,G_z (zero padding permitted),

  ||Psi_F-Psi_G||_(HS->HS) <=3 sum_z||F_z-G_z||_1
    <=3d ||measurement_F-measurement_G||_diamond.        (S2)

The second bound follows by applying the measurement difference to a
normalized maximally entangled state: its trace norm is precisely
d^-1 sum_z||F_z-G_z||_1. No outcome normalization is separately conditioned;
the small trace multiplying its pure/mixed posterior cancels that problem.

If each matched local quantum/classical instrument and leaf measurement
is approximated to diamond error eta, the tree hybrid bound gives full
record measurement error <=(2K-1)eta. Equations (S1)-(S2) give canonical
quadratic-form perturbation <=3d(2K-1)eta. For example

  eta<=delta/(48dK)                                     (S3)

adds at most delta/8 to the finite-tree deficit. This concerns a supplied
FIXED Kraus instrument/alphabet. Closeness of two bare B channels does
not itself give closeness of arbitrary selected Kraus decompositions.
LDL pivot choices and the final accepted sample batch can change abruptly.
No Lipschitz dependence of the selected compiler output on B is claimed.
For a fixed returned channel, if an independently supplied error bound is
||Phi-Phi_tilde||_(Hermitian HS->HS)<=kappa, its target certificate transfers
with an added 4kappa I (or to Sym Phi if needed). The corresponding diamond
bound eta gives kappa<=sqrt(d) eta. This does not acquire or justify the
error bound from finite tomography.

6. A PHYSICAL FINITE-GATE IMPLEMENTATION IS AN APPROXIMATION

The returned rational POVM has effects F_i=c_i u_i u_i*, with rational
vectors u_i and positive rational c_i; its prepared pure states are
u_i u_i*/||u_i||^2. Form the a-by-d isometry V with row sqrt(c_i)u_i*.
V*V=I. A useful explicit dilation, avoiding a poorly conditioned arbitrary
basis completion, is the (a+d)-dimensional Hermitian unitary

  U=[[I_a-VV*,V],[V*,0_d]].                             (G1)

It maps a state in the bottom d-dimensional code subspace to V times that
state in the top a-dimensional outcome subspace. Pad to a power-of-two
dimension D<2(a+d)<=4a, act on the encoded input plus initialized ancillas,
then measure the entire outcome basis and DISCARD every original-input
and measurement ancilla register. Prepare the chosen branch state on a
fresh output register. Padding/otherwise ideal-zero outcomes receive a
fixed fresh prepared state, so the approximated instrument remains TP.
This architecture remains exactly EB under finite gate synthesis; it is
not an approximate generic Stinespring implementation of the whole EB map.
Code-space embedding is a basis permutation and is included in the dense
unitary compilation cost. Entries of (G1) require only rational
arithmetic and positive scalar square roots and can be approximated to
p absolute bits in polynomial time in a,d,bout,p. Small probabilities
require neither postselection nor a preparation precision oracle.

Here is a stable dense decomposition with no small-pivot assumption.
At a column of an EXACT unitary after the preceding columns have been
eliminated, the remaining tail x has norm 1. Pick sigma in {1,-1} opposite
the sign of Re x_1; an approximate sign is adequate since a tie permits
either. Put v=x-sigma e_1 and c=v^* x. Then Re c>=1, ||v||<=2, and

  H=I-vv*/c is unitary, Hx=sigma e_1.

Indeed ||v||^2=c+conjugate(c). Choose a two-level rotation list W taking
v/||v|| to e_1, always rotating against its first coordinate. That coordinate
has modulus >=1/2, so every pair normalization is bounded below by 1/2.
Then H=W* diag(-conjugate(c)/c,1,...) W. This constructs O(D^2) two-level
gates for the full unitary. There is no division by a tiny matrix entry.
Approximate sign selection preserves the bounds Re c>=1/2 for sufficiently
small input error. All arithmetic operates on bounded matrices with
denominators bounded below by inverse polynomials. Even a loose product
Lipschitz bound across O(D^3) scalar operations requires only
O(D^3 log(2D)+bout+log(G0/zeta)) working precision bits, hence polynomial
numerical preprocessing. No exact algebraic sign oracle is needed.

Barenco's two-level conversion gives O(D^2 log^3(2D)) continuous one- and
two-qubit gates. Individual pure-state preparation costs
O(d^2 log^3(2d)); computing all a lists is charged. For state preparation,
first pivot on a largest rational-vector coordinate, whose normalized
modulus is >=1/sqrt(d); subsequent pair normalizations are bounded below
by this same value. Rational-vector norm evaluation has height <=poly(bout).

Let G0=O(a^2 log^3(2a)+a d^2 log^3(2d)) denote the total continuous gate
lists, including all conditional prepared states. Approximate each listed
gate to operator error O(zeta/G0) over a FIXED inverse-closed universal
constant-dimensional gate set. Hybrid bounds give an implementation
Lambda with ||Lambda-Psi_delta||_diamond<=zeta. Finite-precision
Solovay-Kitaev at dimension 2 or 4 yields a list and Turing runtime
G0 polylog(G0/zeta), plus polynomial numerical preprocessing and reading
ell_zeta. No growing-dimension Solovay-Kitaev constant is used.

Primary statements checked: Barenco et al., Section 8, gives the generic
continuous-gate O(n^3 4^n) decomposition for n qubits. For actual bit costs
use van Melkebeek--Watson, Theorem 4.3 (2012), which takes polynomially
accurate input matrices at constant gate dimension and proves polylog
Turing time. Dawson--Nielsen's often cited 3.97 gate exponent and 2.71
compressed arithmetic runtime do not by themselves charge numerical
precision; their uncompressed output list already costs the gate count.

The EXACT rational returned Psi_delta is canonical, unital, TP, and
HS-self-adjoint with its exact quadratic certificate. A finite fixed-gate
implementation is EB and diamond-close; it need not be exactly canonical,
unital, or HS-self-adjoint. Do not silently transfer those exact structural
equalities to approximate hardware. Its state trace-distance error increases
by at most zeta/2. Its symmetric Hermitian quadratic form differs by at
most sqrt(d) zeta I, using ||X||_1<=sqrt(d)||X||_2. Exact rational state
preparation is not generally an exact finite Clifford+T circuit.

7. PRESERVED FAILED ROUTES AND UNRESOLVED COMPLEXITY QUESTIONS

(i) Selecting every |lambda|>1/sqrt(2) makes the original finite-depth
convergence nonuniform at the critical spectrum. C4 needs only lambda>3/4;
the resulting margin 2lambda^2>9/8 is supplied by the target coefficient,
not by an extra spectral-gap assumption.

(ii) A direct empirical posterior channel is generally not TP/unital:
its mean S need not equal I. Scaling, exact positive residual completion,
and the full PSD test fix that specific obstruction.

(iii) Decomposing a mixed posterior and randomly preparing a pure component
after a coarse POVM does not make the INPUT effects rank one and canonical.
Retaining all single-Kraus instrument labels and rank-one leaves does.

(iv) Enumerating the raw fine alphabet and then invoking Caratheodory would
have an exponential-in-K cost. A finite support existence theorem is not
a compiler. The conditional sampler plus certified residual construction
avoids this enumeration for delta>0. The raw alphabet is not a complexity
lower bound; no hardness result is claimed.

(v) For delta=0, compactness gives the earlier exact C4 existence theorem,
but the present polynomial compiler and uniform finite-depth bounds do
not give exact effective extraction. A deterministic polynomial compiler,
a practical low-degree implementation, and efficient physical broadcaster
acquisition remain unresolved. Arbitrary exact real input is not made
computable by this rational theorem.

(vi) Shallow warm starts are harmless when followed by the exact structural
and full-order certificates. Their success has no uniform guarantee; the
large-depth fallback establishes expected polynomial termination. The
small replay is diagnostic evidence for the new mechanisms and a legal
nontrivial control, not generic compiler software or external validation.
