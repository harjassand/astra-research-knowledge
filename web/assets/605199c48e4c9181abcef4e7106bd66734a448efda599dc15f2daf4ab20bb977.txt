Unoriented Θ finite-volume completion and shift gate
Date: 2026-10-09

STATUS
======
For the weaker local record Θ, the old need to choose a local completion
compatible with one fixed orientation is removed at the finite-volume map
level: construct the desired circuits from d=h1-h2 in a free annulus, and set
the shift equal to zero on the entire inner record support. The map then preserves
Θ, the full ordered Γ lift, and any inner side/observable tag. It uses one
deterministic prescription on the union of lift cells, so there is no
orientation-branch multiplicity. Arbitrary adaptive exact height queries can
still occur in the high-crossing proof; their values are integrated out under
an annealed original-marginal joining and are never assumed bounded.

I prove a conditional finite-volume lower bound, an exact square threshold
loss, and the shift injectivity criterion. This does NOT establish the needed
square high-crossing/slack estimates or the full QHC/G7 theorem. Unorienting
Θ does not itself force a lower bound: the necessary crossing probability
must hold for the actual weighted lift mixture (and for each retained side
observable if those are part of the target law), or uniformly on every lift.
No actual square QHC counterexample is found.

THE SHARP SQUARE THRESHOLD LOSS
===============================
For each square NN edge e, |∇d(e)|≤2 because d=h1-h2 and each replica is
1-Lipschitz. Square site duality says that failure of a primal NN
left-right crossing of {d>−H} yields a top-bottom star-connected path in
{d≤−H}. On a diagonal step u→v of this star path, insert either intermediate
corner w. Since w is NN-adjacent to u,
  d(w) ≤ d(u)+2 ≤ −H+2.
Thus the star path converts to a primal NN blocker in the one-cell enlargement
at threshold −H+2. The reflected statement converts a star path in {d≥H}
to a primal NN path in {d≥H−2}. The loss 2 is sharp: on a single square,
set d=-H at opposite corners and d=-H+2 at both other corners. This is
realized by two 1-Lipschitz fields (finite rational check below), and there
is no same-threshold NN route between the bad diagonal corners.

Consequently, if a square annealed high-crossing theorem makes NN paths of
d≥K and d≤−K across fixed corridors unlikely as K grows, then for fixed large
H the sets {d>−H} and {d<H} have primal NN crossings of every required fixed
rectangle with probability close to one. A fixed finite crossing network
can make a winding-one primal NN cycle: use longitudinal path rectangles
around the cell ring and overlapping corner squares; require both perpendicular
primal crossings in each corner square. Planar intersection is then a shared
lattice vertex, not merely an overlap of dual supports. The concatenated walk
has winding number one; its decomposition into simple NN cycles includes a
winding-one simple cycle. Use separated rings for the two signs.

CONDITIONAL FINITE-VOLUME SHIFT-COMPLETION THEOREM
==================================================
Work before hookup weighting, under the finite-volume conditional height
laws and an independent walk mark (or a local sign/side tag determined by
the grounded inner record). Let P_{1,θ,o} be the positive-volume conditional
square height law for replica 1 given Θ=θ and retained inner observable O=o.
It may be a disjoint union of full-dimensional sign-lift polytopes
P_{1,γ,o}, γ∈L(θ,o), with actual weights
  w_{γ|θ,o}=Vol(P_{1,γ,o}) / sum_{γ'} Vol(P_{1,γ',o}).
Let P_2 be the second replica's base polytope. Suppose:

(i) In the source G7 geometry, the queried support of Θ_R is contained in
    B_R+O(1), while the two base systems have no original observations,
    walls, or Dirichlet sites in B_{5R/2}; their common graph is unconditioned
    there. Choose narrow cycle bands A_+, A_- around radii 3R/2 and 2R,
    respectively, with every corridor enlargement and shift interpolation
    collar still inside B_{5R/2} minus B_R. Thus the whole Θ support lies in a
    strictly smaller disk than the free annulus, with a fixed-ratio buffer.
    The local ordered
    Γ lift, including every actual turn-determined T query and no unused
    checkerboard/tie flag, and O are determined by the walk and/or field
    coordinates in the grounded inner support. Any additional external strand record is part of
    the fixed base conditioning and lies outside the common free annulus.
(ii) Under the actual conditional product law on P_{1,θ,o}×P_2, the event E_R
     occurs with probability at least q>0, uniformly in R, θ, o, exterior
     records, and the actual lift weights. E_R contains a primal NN cycle
     C_+ in A_+ with d>−H and a nested primal NN cycle C_- in A_- with d<H.
     A sufficient stronger hypothesis is P(E_R|γ,o)≥q for every actual lift
     γ. The weaker weighted statement is enough for a Θ-only bound; an
     observable law retaining O requires this statement in each O stratum.
(iii) For a Θ-only lower bound, the near-contact component and shift
      derivative moment bounds below hold under each base context's actual
      weighted lift mixture (the same weights used in (ii)). A uniform bound
      on every actual sign-lift law is a sufficient stronger hypothesis and
      is required if the conclusion is demanded separately on every ordered
      Γ stratum. These are original-marginal bounds; they are not required
      after conditioning on adaptive exact height queries.

Choose fixed 0<s0<s1<s2<s* and 0<α<1/2. For replica i define
  t_e(h_i)=1-|∇_e h_i|,
  E_s(h_i)={e: t_e(h_i)≤s and e has a nongrounded endpoint}.
Components are in the square NN graph after adding all fixed/grounded
vertices incident to these edges. Let H_x^{(i)} be the size of the s2
component containing x. The exact required slack event is
  Slack_R={ max_{i=1,2} max_{x in the correction graph} H_x^{(i)} ≤ R^α }.
The correction graph includes the support of the shifts, its neighboring
vertices, and the grounded inner record set; fixed--fixed edges are omitted.
Since A_+ and A_- are separated by cR, Slack_R prevents a near-contact
component from joining the two opposite prescriptions. A sufficient CAP/
sparse-box input is sup_x E H_x^k≤C_k for every fixed k, uniformly under the
actual sign-lift laws; it gives
  P(Slack_R^c) ≤ C_k R^(2-αk)=o(1)
by a union bound with k>2/α. This is an original-marginal statement.

Fix deterministic Lipschitz cutoffs χ_+,χ_- that are one on the entire
respective circuit bands, zero on the entire grounded inner record support
(inside B_R+O(1)) and all fixed/sign-wall sites, and zero outside free
interpolation collars. Their supports are separated by cR and
|χ(x)-χ(y)|≤C/R on every edge. For example, the bands can be narrow
neighborhoods of radii 3R/2 and 2R, with collars inside B_{5R/2}.
Put Δ=H+1 and prescribe
  f_1=(Δ/2)(χ_+−χ_-),   f_2=−f_1.
Then f_1−f_2=+Δ on A_+ and −Δ on A_-. The prescription depends only on the
annular geometry, not on Γ orientation, side, walk, or adaptive queries.

For each replica use the source component-averaged correction u_i(f_i,h_i):
for s∈[s0,s1], average f_i on each E_s component unless it touches a
zero-shift grounded vertex, in which case set that component's shift to
zero; integrate over s. The source derivation gives, on Slack_R,
  ||u_i−f_i||_∞ ≤ C R^(α−1),
  ||Du_i||_op ≤ C R^(2α−1),
  ||Du_i||_HS^2 ≤ C R^(-2) sum_x H_x^3.
On edges t_e≤s0 the endpoint shifts agree, while on other edges the
prescription variation is at most C R^(α−1)<s0 for large R; hence h_i+u_i
is feasible. Grounding every inner record site, every actual Γ query site (including T),
and every coordinate supporting O makes the map fix Θ, the local full Γ
sign/port-pair lift, and O exactly. In
particular no sign-lift weight is changed or averaged equally.

Let J_{M,R} be the additional good event
  J_{M,R}={ for i=1,2: |div u_i|≤M and ||Du_i||_HS^2≤M }.
The tangential-vector-field identity and the shift moment bounds give
E(div u_i)^2≤C and E||Du_i||_HS^2≤C. Choose M=M(q,C) so Markov costs at
most q/4; for large R, Slack_R failure costs at most q/4. Then
E'_R=E_R∩Slack_R∩J_{M,R} has probability at least q/2.

The product map T=(I+u_1,I+u_2) is injective on E'_R. For a collision in one
replica, the approximation estimate first gives
  ||h-k||_∞≤2C R^(α−1).
Along the segment h_t=(1-t)h+tk, every s1 contact component at an
intermediate t is then contained in an s2 component of h. The component
matrix estimate yields
  ||u_i(k)-u_i(h)||_2 ≤ C R^(2α−1)||k-h||_2.
Since α<1/2, the coefficient is below one for large R, so the collision
forces h=k. The same argument is block-diagonal for the two-replica map.
On J_{M,R}, the determinant inequality
  log det(I+Du_i) ≥ div u_i−||Du_i||_HS^2
implies det DT≥exp(−4M). The area formula gives the exact conditional
finite-volume completion bound
  P(T(E'_R)) ≥ (q/2) exp(−4M).
Every image pair has the same Θ, Γ, and O labels, and
  d' = d + f_1−f_2 + O(R^(α−1)).
Therefore d'>0 on C_+ and d'<0 on C_- for all sufficiently large R. The
image is contained in the favorable nested primal-cycle event. Its lower
bound is independent of R, the number of lift cells, and adaptive pin
heights. The cost can depend on H, the fixed ring geometry, q, the contact
moment constants, and M.

For the comparison step, apply the exact dependency-cluster swap to each
favorable pair. If the reverse conditional law has the same lower bound p0,
then, writing V_i for the base volumes and V_i(θ,o) for the tagged local
record volumes, the volume-preserving swap gives
  p0 V_1(θ,o)V_2 ≤ V_1 V_2(θ,o),
and the reversed experiment gives the reverse inequality. This is an exact
finite-volume comparison for the tagged Θ/O event. If only Θ is retained, sum
over its disjoint Γ cells using their actual volumes; the same constant holds
when p0 is uniform on the actual mixture. For a full ordered Γ law, require
p0 uniformly on each Γ/O cell and sum those inequalities. No equal-lift
weights or per-query factor enters.

NO LIFT MULTIPLICITY; ACTUAL WEIGHTS
====================================
The shift is a single deterministic map on the union of lift cells. It is
zero at every vertex of the inner record support and at every site on which
the retained local side/observable label depends. Each exact sign-lift cell
is a convex height polytope. If T(h)=T(k), then h=k on that support, so the
exact local Γ/side labels coincide; h,k therefore lie in the same convex
cell, where the segment-based collision proof above gives h=k. Thus the map
is globally one-to-one on the retained union; there is no factor
|L(θ,o)|. Walk coordinates are carried unchanged. The un-oriented event is
simply the disjoint union of the actual
lift cells with their original volume weights; no equal-weight orientation
reference is introduced. If an observable uses field values inside a bump
band, this map does not preserve that observable; its support must be added
to the grounded set or handled by a separate comparison.

If one instead used a different map for each lift and allowed the map to
change the lift, forgetting Γ could make the image multiplicity as large as
the number of lifts. For m branches a valid bound would then lose a factor m
unless disjointness/injectivity is proved. Our map avoids this: it fixes the
whole inner support, uses d-based corridors independent of side, and keeps
each branch in its own cell. Any final comparison of the full sampled record
must still retain actual weights. If q is proved only for the θ-mixture under
one artificial equal-lift law, it is not enough for another exterior law.
A sufficient condition for the joint record is q uniform for each actual
(γ,o) stratum; alternatively prove the q lower bound directly under each
context's actual weighted mixture.

An abstract two-lift falsifier shows why this weight condition is necessary.
Let favorable circuit probability be 1 on lift + and 0 on lift −. In context
A let weights be (1−ε,ε), and in context B let them be (ε,1−ε). Then
P_A(E|θ)=1−ε while P_B(E|θ)=ε, so no context-uniform completion constant
follows from θ-unorientation alone. This is not a counterexample in the
square height model. It falsifies only the inference that forgetting lift
makes the actual weighted mixture favorable. The d-based annular construction
removes side compatibility if its high-crossing/slack hypotheses hold
uniformly on actual lifts; it does not manufacture those hypotheses.

ADAPTIVE UNBOUNDED PINS
=======================
For the high-crossing input, explore the component of d≥K from a transverse
seed, query both heights at each exposed site, and stop at the boundary of
the explored component. Every nonseed queried difference is ≥K−2 because d
is 2-Lipschitz. Conditional on this finite adaptive transcript the two
unqueried Gibbs laws disintegrate as product kernels. Stationary synchronous
joining preserves each original marginal after integration over the record.
Thus all square contact/moment estimates used above must be established for
the original conditional law (union over actual Γ lifts with its real
weights); no fiberwise bound is asked for the unbounded revealed values. The
query count can be O(R^2), but there is no per-query probability factor if
these annealed inputs are uniform for finite adaptive algorithms.

The finite-volume shift is then applied to the original Θ-conditional
polytopes before hookup weighting, not to the exact adaptive fibers. It
grounds K_θ and original walls, not the random adaptive query set. This is the exact point that avoids an invalid bounded-quenched-height
assumption.

REMAINING MATHEMATICAL GATE
===========================
The conditional lower bound reduces square QHC to one explicit probabilistic
input: for every admissible θ, every retained side/observable stratum, and
both base contexts, the actual conditional pair law must give
  P(E_R | Θ=θ, O=o) ≥ q0 > 0
for the two threshold cycles before shifting, with q0 independent of R. In
terms of actual sign-lift weights this is exactly
  q_{φ,θ,o}(R)=Σ_{γ:π(γ)=θ} w_{φ}(γ|θ,o) P(E_R|Γ=γ,O=o,Φ=φ) ≥ q0
for each context φ. A sufficient stronger route is a common lower bound on
each lift term, uniform in γ; no equal-weight orientation average is allowed.
For the shift cost, the corresponding exact Θ-only moment premise uses these
same weights: for the first marginal, for each context and each fixed k
needed,
  sup_{x in correction graph} Σ_γ w_{φ}(γ|θ,o) E_{φ,γ}[H_x^{(1)k}] ≤ C_k,
and for the second marginal sup_x E_{φ}[H_x^{(2)k}]≤C_k, uniformly in
R, θ, o. With N_R=O(R^2) correction vertices, this gives
P(Slack_R^c|φ,θ,o)≤C_k R^{2-αk}, and the source matrix estimate gives, for
each i,
  E_{actual context}||Du_i||_{HS}^2≤C R^{-2}Σ_x E H_x^{(i)3}≤C.
The tangential identity gives the matching divergence-square bound. For the
full ordered Γ law, require q and these bounds uniformly on each Γ/O cell.
Proving these hypotheses needs the square annealed high-crossing theorem
for d with the square zero-trace/Frostman step, plus CAP-derived near-contact
component moments under those same original sign-wall laws. The CAP theorem
alone does not supply the field-difference high-crossing estimate or these
shift moments. No square G7/QHC endpoint follows until this gate is proved.
