# Failure boundaries and restart gate

## Negative and limiting results

- The dimension-varying recognition problem is coNP-complete in the declared sparse positive generalized-rational model. The no-expansion representation removes blowup in the *written formula*, but does not make exact general recognition polynomial unless P=NP. QF-LRA solvers can branch internally; this executable is not a worst-case runtime breakthrough.
- A fixed-dimension arrangement gives an XP bound `H^{O(d)}`, not FPT. No FPT algorithm or lower bound for parameterized dimension was established.
- The 3SAT lower bound does not automatically transfer to canonical Hill or Michaelis–Menten-only syntax. The compiler accepts that subset, but its hardness theorem is for the larger positive sparse `P/Q` class.
- Strong endotacticity uses a different, standard all-reactant maximum that retains neutral source channels. The weak 3SAT theorem is not claimed for strong recognition. In fact the unsatisfiable 3-variable formula with all eight sign clauses has direction `(u1,u2,u3,z)=(0,0,0,-1)`: both guards at each variable are neutral and have source score 2, while there is no inward reaction at the global maximum. Thus the reduction's weak-positive construction need not be strongly endotactic. A child note's optional “essential-top-face strong” convention is not the standard GMS definition and is excluded from the result here.
- `CERTIFIED` means all exact Z3 queries returned `unsat`; no separate proof object is emitted for a proof-checker. `REFUTED` directions are independently replayable with standard-library `Fraction`. `UNKNOWN` is required on resource exhaustion, unavailable solver, or unreplayable model.
- The global permanence consequence for the general endotactic case remains conditional on the N33 source candidate and the positive common-clock/continuation assumptions. The strong subfamily has established permanence prior art after the finite positive lift; that is not a new permanence theorem.
- Positivity is load-bearing: if numerator/denominator coefficients can be signed, top-support cancellations can invalidate the support-only asymptotic. A zero denominator on the positive orthant also leaves the declared input model.

## Restart gates

1. Have an independent reaction-network theorist audit the z-slice proof and the strict-to-rational-witness bit bound, including the exact neutral/tie convention.
2. Perform a scoped primary-literature search for sparse generalized-rational support-order recognition and complexity; keep priority `UNKNOWN` until this is done.
3. Obtain an independently proof-checkable exact UNSAT backend or proof object for `CERTIFIED` outputs, then differential-test it on independently generated fixed-dimension fixtures.
4. If the target is a canonical Hill/MM family, formalize that restricted syntax and either prove a lower bound within it or evaluate whether its structure permits a polynomial/FPT recognizer. Do not carry over the broad-class coNP-hardness without that reduction.
5. Before a general permanence claim, externally review the N33 affine/weighted-drift argument and spell out forward existence and the clock continuation hypotheses. A named biochemical application additionally needs a complete measured gross-flux inventory and validated positive multiplier box.
