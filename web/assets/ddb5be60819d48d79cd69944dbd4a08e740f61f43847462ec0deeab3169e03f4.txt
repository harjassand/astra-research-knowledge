L(X)={V*V,X}/2−V*XV. The semigroup exp(−tL) is unital CP by GKSL form.
Its predual is L_*(rho)={V*V,rho}/2−VrhoV*. Directly L_*sigma=0.
The Hilbert–Schmidt transformed positive generator
H=Gamma^(-1/2)L_*Gamma^(1/2)=Gamma^(1/2)L Gamma^(-1/2),
Gamma(X)=sigma^(1/2)Xsigma^(1/2), has matrix
 [[4,0,0,-2], [0,5/2,-2,0], [0,-2,5/2,0], [-2,0,0,1]]
in matrix-unit order (E00,E01,E10,E11). This is self-adjoint positive, fixes
sqrt(sigma), and has eigenvalues 0,1/2,9/2,5. Hence the QMS is primitive
and KMS symmetric. It is not modular covariant: it mixes E01 with E10.
