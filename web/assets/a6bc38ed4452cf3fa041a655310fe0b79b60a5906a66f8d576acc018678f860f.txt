1. Physical interface

Let sigma=(I+tanh(alpha)Z)/2 on M2, alpha>=0, with arbitrary finite untouched
reference. Tensor X,Y,Z with its identity. Put
  A=cosh(alpha/2), B=sinh(alpha/2), c=cosh(alpha), h=sinh(alpha).
For real a, take N=X+aZ. The legal KMS-positive elementary generator has
  V=(a^2+c)I-hZ+2a(B/A)X,
  H(Q)={V,Q}/2-N Q N.
Its physical jump is K=A X+iB Y+aZ and its physical drift is
  D=sigma^(1/4) V sigma^(-1/4).
The physical positive Schrodinger generator is
  L_*(rho)=(D rho+rho D*)/2-K rho K*.
The Lyapunov identity gives (D+D*)/2=K*K; the residual physical Hamiltonian
must be retained in L_*, although its trace against log rho is zero.

For every faithful joint density rho, define
  J=Tr[L_*(rho)(log rho-alpha Z)],
  E=Tr[Vrho]-Tr[N sqrt(rho) N sqrt(rho)],
  F_kappa=J-kappa E.
The stationary scalar in log sigma and untouched reference log cancel. No
condition on the reference marginal is imposed. A pure Z noise satisfies the
complete constant four by the elementary state-eigenvalue pair inequality.
Thus positivity for N=X+aZ, all a, suffices for arbitrary local Hermitian
qubit noises after phase rotation/scaling, and sums suffice for arbitrary
finite stationary KMS qubit generators by the supplied frozen representation.

2. Diagonalize the entire joint state

Choose a full-state eigenbasis, rho=sum_i lambda_i |i><i|, lambda_i>0.
For each unordered pair choose i,j so p=lambda_i>=q=lambda_j, and set
  w=p+q, beta=(log p-log q)/2>=0,
  t_beta=tanh(beta), m_beta=2 beta tanh(beta),
  n_beta=1-sech(beta), d_kappa(beta)=m_beta-kappa n_beta.
Write x=<i|X|j>, y=<i|Y|j>, z=<i|Z|j> and
  v_ij=(x, i y, a z)^T.
The diagonal elements of the three Hermitian Pauli operators are real.
All formulas are invariant under choices within degenerate eigenspaces;
beta=0 is included by continuity and the displayed elementary formulas.

Define the REAL SYMMETRIC scalar kernel

  M_kappa(alpha,beta)=
  [[ A^2 m_beta-kappa n_beta+B^2 d_kappa(alpha),  -P,  A m_beta-kappa n_beta ],
   [ -P, B^2 m_beta+A^2 d_kappa(alpha),           -Q ],
   [ A m_beta-kappa n_beta,                      -Q,  d_kappa(beta) ]],

where
  P=h beta+(alpha c-kappa h/2)tanh(beta),
  Q=2B beta+[(alpha-kappa B)/A]tanh(beta).

Then the exact identity is

  F_kappa(rho;X+aZ)
    =sum_(i<j) (lambda_i+lambda_j) v_ij* M_kappa(alpha,beta_ij) v_ij
       +d_kappa(alpha) sum_i lambda_i

The notation i<j means one chosen representative of every unordered pair;
the ordering is by decreasing eigenvalue for beta>=0, not a separate physical

In particular, no root/log block commutativity or real off-diagonal joint

matrix implies positivity of the complex quadratic form in (S).

3. Exact derivation

Because log rho commutes with rho, the physical Hamiltonian's entropy trace
vanishes. The entire entropy part is therefore
  Tr[L_*(rho)log rho]
    =sum_(i,j) lambda_j(log lambda_j-log lambda_i)|K_ij|^2.
For U=A X+aZ, K=U+iB Y, the contribution of an unordered pair is
  (p-q)(log p-log q)(|A x+a z|^2+B^2|y|^2)
   +2B(p+q)(log p-log q) Im[(A x+a z)* y].          (1)
This orientation is fixed by K_ij being output i, input j. The reference
potential, directly from the physical generator, is
  -alpha Tr[L_*(rho)Z]
    =2alpha(h-c Tr[rho Z])+(2a alpha/A)Tr[rho X].  (2)
For example, the stationary local state gives both sides zero, including
the physical entropy contribution.

The root energy decomposes as
  E=WY_N(rho)+(c-1)-h Tr[rho Z]+2a(B/A)Tr[rho X],
  WY_N(rho)=sum_(i<j)(sqrt(p)-sqrt(q))^2 |x+a z|^2. (3)
There is no scalar functional calculus of individual blocks in (1)-(3).

The local Pauli product identities XY=iZ and ZY=-iX imply exactly
  sum_(i<j)(p-q) Im[x* y]=-Tr[rho Z],
  sum_(i<j)(p-q) Im[z* y]= Tr[rho X].             (4)
These follow by summing over the full eigenbasis. Diagonal imaginary terms
vanish; exchanging a pair reverses the imaginary sign. Equation (4) holds
for entangled states and all reference dimensions.

The remaining constant in F_kappa is
  C=2alpha h-kappa(c-1)=c d_kappa(alpha).
Use X^2=Y^2=I and allocate this constant in the PRECISE proportions
  B^2/c to the X squared row sums, A^2/c to the Y squared row sums.
Since A^2+B^2=c, this allocation reproduces C exactly:
  C=d_kappa(alpha) Tr[rho(B^2 X^2+A^2 Y^2)].       (5)
Separating diagonal from unordered-pair entries in (5), and using
  p-q=w tanh beta,
  (p-q)(log p-log q)=w m_beta,
  (sqrt p-sqrt q)^2=w n_beta,
converts (1)-(4) into (S), with y replaced by i y so its imaginary cross
products become negative real cross products.

  d_kappa(x)>=d_4(x)>=0,

product of (1,0,1), so this boundary is proved for kappa<=4.
