POSITIVE BOSON GIBBS PENALTIES ON A FINITE GRAPH: A PAIR-FILTER CRITERION
8 October 2026. Internally proved candidate. No acquired-capacity claim.

This abstracts the proof in PROOF.txt. It makes the supplied-versus-acquired
boundary explicit: the pair filter f and the free Gaussian bounds below
must be supplied or constructed. No general graph capacity compiler is
asserted by the theorem. The square logarithmic filter IS constructed in
PROOF.txt and needs no oracle.

Let V be a finite set of S>=1 sites, h>=0 a real symmetric one-particle
matrix with off-diagonal h_uv=-a_uv<=0. Thus a_uv=a_vu>=0 for u!=v.
Let K=dGamma(h), beta>0, and H0(mu)=K-mu M, with unknown mu in a public
bounded interval I. Assume mu_max:=sup I<min spec h; this is the positive
free-gap stability hypothesis. Let rho0(mu) be its full Fock Gibbs state.
Let U=sup_{mu in I}|mu| and choose a public theta with
 0<theta<beta(min spec h-mu_max).
The tilted free state is rho_theta(mu)=exp(theta M)rho0/C_mu.

Suppose the following public uniform free bounds are supplied, or computed
from the finite one-particle spectrum and mode amplitudes:
 E0 M<=N, E0 K<=E, E0 exp(theta M)<=C,
 gamma_0(u,u)<=g0, gamma_theta(u,u)<=gtheta for every u,mu.
These are finite by the gap hypothesis; for example take all occupation
factors at mu_max. This uses the FREE spectrum, not the interacting one.
The free Wick pair density is
 Gamma_s^{(2)}(u,z)=gamma_s(u,u)gamma_s(z,z)+|gamma_s(u,z)|^2
                 <=2 g_s^2,   s=0,theta.

Let f:V x V->[0,1] be symmetric, with f(u,u)=0. Let its zero pairs contain
the admitted forbidden pairs, which must include every (u,u), and let P
be the projection onto configurations
with none of these forbidden pairs. Thus no duplicated site is admitted.
The sector pair product F_m(X)=product_{i<j}f(x_i,x_j) has ran F inside P,
[F,M]=0 and finite rank. Allow ANY nonnegative closed form w vanishing on P,
with a densely defined closed sum H_W=H0+w. Define the two public scores

 A_f=sum_{u,z in V}(1-f(u,z)^2),
 B_f=sum_{u,z in V} sum_{v!=u} a_uv [f(v,z)-f(u,z)]^2.

B_f is a weighted pair-filter Dirichlet energy; it is a finite explicit
sum. A_f charges the volume where the filter differs from 1. Put

 a=g0^2 A_f,
 b=C gtheta^2 A_f/theta,
 d=C gtheta^2 B_f/theta.

If a<=1/2, then uniformly in mu and w,

 D(rhoW(mu)||rho0(mu)) <= G_f,
 G_f=[h2(a)+b log(S+1)+a
          +beta a(E+U N)+beta U b+beta d]/(1-a).         (C.1)

The trace bound is min(2,sqrt(2G_f)). Any exact hard compression onto P
or a larger finite-dimensional allowed subspace containing P has the same
bound. For sequences of graphs, G_f->0 is a complete sufficient criterion
for full Gibbs-state universality under every admitted positive penalty.

Proof of all ingredients not just a capacity heuristic:

The product union bound gives
 delta=Tr rho0(I-F^2)<=E0 sum_{i<j}(1-f(x_i,x_j)^2)
                       =(1/2)sum_{u,z}Gamma_0^{(2)}(u,z)(1-f^2)
                       <=a.
Tilting with M<=exp(theta M)/theta gives
 qM=Tr rho0 M(I-F^2)<=C/theta Etheta sum_{i<j}(1-f^2)<=b.

Embed each m-sector into ell^2(V^m). Its lifted free density is
 r_m=Z0^{-1}exp(beta mu m) P_sym exp(-beta sum_i h_i).
It is positive semidefinite and has nonnegative entries: the heat kernel
is entrywise nonnegative and P_sym is the permutation average. Physical
traces of symmetric operators equal their traces against r_m. The exact
thermal identity is
 Tr rho0 F K F=Tr rho0 F^2 K+Dir,
 Dir=-(1/2)Tr rho0[F,[F,K]].
In this labeled embedding a move x_i=u to v has K matrix entry -a_uv.
The diagonal entries of the commutator vanish, and
 Dir=(1/2)sum_{m,X,i,v} a_{x_i v} r_m(X^{i,v},X)
                                  [F_m(X^{i,v})-F_m(X)]^2.
Use r_m(Y,X)<=(r_m(X,X)+r_m(Y,Y))/2 and reverse moves to get
 Dir<=(1/2)E0 sum_{i,v}a_{x_i v}(Delta F)^2.
Product telescoping and Cauchy-Schwarz give
 (Delta F)^2<=(m-1)sum_{j!=i}[f(v,x_j)-f(x_i,x_j)]^2.
The m-1 factor is bounded by exp(theta m)/theta and absorbed into the
tilt. The exact ordered-pair density identity then gives

 Dir<=C/(2theta) sum_{u,z}Gamma_theta^{(2)}(u,z)
                           sum_{v!=u}a_uv[f(v,z)-f(u,z)]^2
     <=d.

The ordered-pair identity has no m! overhead: an occupation vector has
w(n)=m!/product_u n_u! labeled representatives, each lifted diagonal
weight p(n)/w(n); their sum is exactly the physical occupation weight.
Counting ordered pairs gives n_u(n_z-delta_uz), including coincident sites.

With p=Tr rho0F^2>=1-a and sigma=F rho0 F/p, positivity of rho0 K implies
 Tr sigma K-E0K<=(delta E0K+Dir)/p.
Also Tr sigma M-E0M=(delta E0M-qM)/p. Hence
 beta Tr(sigma-rho0)H0 <=beta[delta(E+U N)+U qM+d]/p.

The isometry with effects F and sqrt(I-F^2), followed by dephasing its
outcome bit, gives S(rho0)-S(sigma)<=h2(delta)+delta S(omega).
The failure state has mean number qM/delta. The number reference with
s=log(S+1) has log Z_M(s)<=1, so
 S(rho0)-S(sigma)<=h2(delta)+qM log(S+1)+delta.
Each sector is finite and the free stable Gaussian has exponential number
moments; this justifies the entropy step on the full Fock space. Combining
the entropy and energy estimates yields D(sigma||rho0)<=G_f.

Finally minmax gives Z0>=ZW>=ZP, and Gibbs variational comparison on P
gives log(Z0/ZP)<=D(sigma||rho0). At beta/2 the interacting partition is
finite by minmax. Therefore its Gibbs total energy, H0 energy, and positive
w form energy are all finite. The exact identity
 D(rhoW||rho0)=log(Z0/ZW)-beta E_{rhoW}w<=G_f
is legitimate even when w is unbounded or mixes particle-number sectors.
This proves (C.1). Hard compression is the same finite-dimensional
variational comparison, without a penalty-limit assumption.

The collective total-number channel and n-copy KL estimate apply whenever
beta,h are public and the n copies share mu, exactly as PROOF.txt Section 8.
No change to the decoder-preparation cost accounting is licensed by the
capacity criterion.

Square specialization: h_uv=-2J for nearest neighbors, g_s=4Ns/L^2,
C=exp(Ntheta), A_f<=S(2ell+1)^2, and B_f<=2J S C_f. The A_f inequality
uses 1-f^2<=1_{|u-z|_infinity<ell}. Thus beta d
<=512 exp(Ntheta)Ntheta^2/log(ell/R), recovering the logarithmic gate.
For lambda<=0, the sign-sensitive chemical estimate in PROOF.txt omits
the beta U b term and yields its slightly smaller displayed G_L.

Sharpness/extension boundary: the criterion is sufficient, not necessary.
A failure of a supplied filter or of these density bounds is not a theorem
of impossibility. On a new graph one must acquire a filter with simultaneously
small A_f and B_f at the free scales; a claimed 'small capacity' without
the loss-volume and moment/entropy costs does not complete that interface.
