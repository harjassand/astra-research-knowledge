ROOT ENTROPY TRUNCATION CONSEQUENCE — CANDIDATE FOR INDEPENDENT AUDIT
Let Phi be any bistochastic self-compatible channel and Psi its one canonical comparator with I-Psi<=C(I-symPhi), C8 in general/C4 when HSselfadjoint. Fix rho, X=d rho, epsilon=||rho-Phi(rho)||1, t>1, f=min(X,tI), g=X-f, q=tau(g), rho_t=(f+qI)/d.
Then fg=tg, 0<=f<=tI, Phi(g)>=0, tau(Phi(g))=tau(g), so tau[f(I-Phi)g]>=0. Consequently
E_Phi(f)=tau[f(I-Phi)f]<=tau[f(I-Phi)X]<=t epsilon/2,
where the last inequality subtracts (t/2)I, using tau[(I-Phi)X]=0. No self-adjointness is needed in this clipping step; the scalar energy equals its symmetrization.
Since ||rho-rho_t||1<=2q and constants have zero comparator energy,
||rho-Psi(rho)||1 <=4q+sqrt(Ct epsilon/2).
For relative entropy D=D(rho||I/d)=tau(X log X), the negative part has absolute trace at most1/e. On X>t, XlogX>=Xlogt, and q<=tau[X 1_{X>t}], so q<=(D+1/e)/logt.
Thus one Psi simultaneously gives
||rho-Psi(rho)||1 <=4(D+1/e)/logt+sqrt(Ct epsilon/2), all t>1.
At epsilon in(0,1), t=epsilon^-1/2 gives 8(D+1/e)/log(1/epsilon)+sqrt(C/2)epsilon^1/4. At epsilon0 take t to infinity. For finite ensembles with average I/d, average D is Holevo chi; averaging and Jensen yield the same bound with average error and chi. Uniform bound follows if each individual D<=M. Arbitrary nonuniform information-radius references are NOT handled by this statement alone.
