## Verdict and exact scope

I found no fatal error in c05_s03 Parts B/E/F. The altered shift
`Lambda=2M+1` keeps the shifted quadratic form's condition number bounded,
which lets the same stopped positive-diffusion method certify an exponential
whole-density distance to

  C_iid,N = conv{ tau^tensor N : tau is a one-qubit density }

through the full subcritical scale `M_N=o(sqrt(N))`, `B_N=o(N^(3/4))`, and
through a sufficiently small universal constant multiple of those scales.
The finite-N boundary example is exact: at
`A_N=-(2+kappa)sqrt(N) I + kappa sqrt(N)(I-e_z e_z^T)` (equivalently
`-2sqrt(N) I-kappa sqrt(N)e_z e_z^T`), `b_N=0`, the target's nearest state in
`C_iid,N` is exactly `I/2^N`, and the distance tends to a positive constant.
Thus the growth exponent 1/2 is sharp for this *identical-site product
mixture* conclusion, although no sharp coefficient separating the valid and
invalid constant-times-sqrt(N) windows is identified.

This is not a counterexample to full separability. The boundary target is
diagonal and is itself a convex mixture of computational-basis product
states. It is far from mixtures of identical local qubit states, not from the
full separable cone. The fixed-M c07_s02 theorem remains a fixed-public-bound
claim; it cannot be extended by simply allowing its constants to depend on a
growing M. The c05_s03 modified-shift proof supplies a different growing-window
argument.

## Part B check: shift, ellipticity, and costs

Use the convention `Q_A=sum_ab A_ab {J_a,J_b}/2`, so `Q_(lambda I)=lambda J^2`.
With `||A||<=M`, choose `Lambda=2M+1`, `C=A+Lambda I`,
`cmin=M+1`, `L=3M+1`. Then

  (M+1) I <= C <= (3M+1) I,     L/cmin <= 3.

For `P=I-mm^T`, the diffusion covariance matrix is
`D=P C P-[m]_x C [m]_x^T`. On `|m|<=r0=1/4`, every vector z satisfies

  z^T D z >= [cmin(1-|m|^2)^2-L|m|^2]|z|^2
            >= (177/256)cmin |z|^2,
  D <= P C P <= L I.

The first inequality uses the smallest singular value of P, at least
`1-|m|^2`, and `||[m]_x||=|m|`; it does not assume C and P commute. Thus the
principal square root is smooth with a uniform positive margin on the fixed
stopping ball. `D` need not be positive near the pure-state boundary, which is
irrelevant because the SDE is stopped inside this ball.

For the drift
`a=(N-1)/(2N^(3/2)) [Cm-(m^TCm)m] + [b-(b.m)m]/(2N^(3/4))`, the identities
`Cm-(m^TCm)m=P C m` and `||P||<=1` give

  |a| <= L/(8 sqrt(N)) + B/(2N^(3/4)).

The displayed condition `L/sqrt(N)+4B/N^(3/4)<=1/4` implies
`|a|<=r0/8`; the constants agree exactly for `r0=1/4`.

The seed coefficient is `t0=2/N-Lambda/N^(3/2)` and
`N>=Lambda^2` implies `t0=(2/N)d0` with `d0=1-Lambda/(2sqrt(N))>=1/2`.
The radial heat-seed tail has universal `kappa0>0` because r0 is fixed, at
the cost of the explicit lower-normalizer penalty `exp(-Lambda/2)` in the
bad-seed estimate. The global semigroup/weight bound is

  h_N = L sqrt(N)/4 + L/(2sqrt(N)) + B N^(1/4)/2.

These are mathematical positive-mixture bounds. They do not certify an
exact-real or finite-bit SDE sampler. In particular the path-weight range
includes `exp(h_N)`, so the subcritical theorem is not a polynomial-time
preparation statement; in the fixed constant window the crude bound can be
exponential in N.

## Part E check: what the certificate proves

The audited certificate (A4) is

  2 C0 N^(3/4) exp(h_N+Lambda/2-kappa0 N)
  + 6 h_N exp(h_N-E_N),
  E_N = 3 r0^2 N^(3/2)/(64 L),

under `N>=max(1,Lambda^2,2/delta)` and the drift condition above. For
`M_N=o(sqrt(N))`, `B_N=o(N^(3/4))`, we have `Lambda^2=o(N)`, `h_N=o(N)`,
`Lambda=o(N)`, and `E_N/N -> infinity` (since `L=3M_N+1=o(sqrt(N))`). Thus
the first exponent is `-kappa0 N+o(N)` and the second is
`-E_N+o(E_N)`. For every such envelope, eventually

  T(rho_N,C_iid,N) <= (2C0+1) exp(-kappa0 N/2).

The constants in this displayed rate can be universal, but the threshold
index is sequence-dependent: an unquantified little-o condition gives no
universal finite N0. The field condition is a sufficient condition here;
this audit found no matching field lower boundary.

For the explicit constant window, take the peer's
`epsilon0=min(1/56,kappa0/16,3r0^2/1024)`. If
`M_N<=epsilon0 sqrt(N)`, `B_N<=epsilon0 N^(3/4)`, and
`N>=epsilon0^(-2)`, then `L<=4epsilon0 sqrt(N)`, `Lambda<=3epsilon0 sqrt(N)`,
the drift condition holds, `h_N<=1.5epsilon0 N+2epsilon0`, and
`E_N>=4N`. The leading coefficient in the first exponent is at most
`-kappa0 N + 1.5epsilon0 N <= -(1-3/32)kappa0 N`; the extra
`Lambda/2=O(sqrt(N))` and `log N` terms are lower order. The second term is
also exponentially smaller. A universal N0 therefore exists for this fixed
window. This verifies a nonempty constant-times-sqrt(N) sufficient regime,
not an optimized epsilon0.

The old c07_s02 fixed-shift constants do not themselves give this conclusion:
their stopping radius scales as M^(-1/2) and their seed exponent as M^(-2).
The c05_s03 shift is the material proof change. Its conclusion is uniform over
all A,b in each stated envelope, while the representation still uses the
known A,b in its diffusion and weights.

## Part F check: exact finite-N nearest-state theorem

Fix `kappa>0`, `A_N=-2sqrt(N) I-kappa sqrt(N)e_z e_z^T`, and `b_N=0`.
Then `||A_N||=(2+kappa)sqrt(N)` and cancellation is exact:

  K_N = 2J^2/N + Q_(A_N)/N^(3/2) = -kappa J_z^2/N.

Let k be the number of up spins, `m=k-N/2`, and

  p_N(k)=2^(-N) binom(N,k),
  g_N(k)=exp[-kappa(k-N/2)^2/N],
  z_N=sum_k p_N(k)g_N(k),
  q_N(k)=p_N(k)g_N(k)/z_N.

These are the magnetization laws of `I/2^N` and `rho_N`. Since `g_N` is
nonincreasing in `|k-N/2|`,
`C_N={k:g_N(k)>=z_N}` is a symmetric central integer interval. On C_N,
`q_N-p_N>=0`, and outside its strict interior the difference is nonpositive;
terms at equality vanish. Therefore

  q_N(C_N)-p_N(C_N) = (1/2) sum_k |q_N(k)-p_N(k)| = D_N.

For a symmetric interval `C={l,...,N-l}`, the probability under
`Bin(N,p)` is maximized at `p=1/2`. For `1<=l<=floor(N/2)`, differentiating
and telescoping gives

  d/dp P_p(C)
    = N binom(N-1,l-1) [p^(l-1)(1-p)^(N-l)
                         - p^(N-l)(1-p)^(l-1)].

The ratio of the first bracket term to the second is
`[(1-p)/p]^(N-2l+1)`. The derivative is nonnegative on `[0,1/2]` and
nonpositive on `[1/2,1]`; l=0 is the full event. Hence every one-qubit
product `tau^tensor N` has `Pr(C_N)<=p_N(C_N)`, because measuring all local
z spins gives `Bin(N,(1+Tr(tau sigma_z))/2)`. Convexity extends the bound to
every `sigma in C_iid,N`. The two-outcome event measurement now gives

  T(rho_N,sigma) >= Tr[E_(C_N)(rho_N-sigma)] >= D_N.

The maximally mixed state belongs to `C_iid,N` and its distance is exactly
`D_N`, proving the finite-N variational identity

  inf_(sigma in C_iid,N) T(rho_N,sigma) = T(rho_N,I/2^N) = D_N.

This proof is independent of a search over Bloch vectors or a discretization
of the mixture measure. It applies to coherent one-qubit tau as well, since
the z-basis event probabilities depend only on tau's z component.

## Positive limit and computational evidence

Under the uniform string law,
`Z_N=2(K-N/2)/sqrt(N)` converges to `Z~N(0,1)`, and
`g_N=exp(-kappa Z_N^2/4)`. Bounded-continuous convergence gives
`z_N -> beta^(-1/2)`, `beta=1+kappa/2`, and

  D_N -> D_inf(kappa)
      = 2[Phi(a sqrt(beta))-Phi(a)] > 0,
  a = sqrt(2 log(beta)/kappa).

The limiting tilted law is `N(0,beta^(-1))`; the two densities cross at
`+/-a`, which yields the formula. At `kappa=2`, the limit is
`0.16606407498...`. My independent script
`revisions/growing_boundary_check.py` checks the central-interval inequality
with exact rational arithmetic for N<=14 and rational p denominators<=17
(10,584 cases), and evaluates the scalar distances for N=16,64,256,1024,4096.
The saved JSON shows convergence to the stated limit. These are diagnostics;
the derivative and CLT arguments above establish the claims.

## Operational boundary and attribution

The lower-bound observable is only the central event of a collective z-spin
measurement, implementable by measuring N qubits and retaining a running
counter (O(N) measurements and O(log N) counter bits). The scalar distance
can be evaluated from N+1 binomial weights, without materializing a 2^N
matrix. This says nothing about acquiring or preparing the target under a
specific hardware model. The exact target is already fully separable:
`rho_N=sum_string q_N(string)|string><string|`, a mixture of possibly
different local z eigenstates. Thus any report must state `C_iid,N` in the
lower bound and must not call it distance from the full separable set.
