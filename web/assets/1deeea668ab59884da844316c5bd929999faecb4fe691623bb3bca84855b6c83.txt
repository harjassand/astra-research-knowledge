C04_S02 STANDARD COLLECTIVE THERMALIZER SCOPE

This finishes the bath normal-form gate stated in Section 7 of
SCHUR_CENTRAL_EXACT_DEPTH.txt. It does not modify the counterexample to an
unspecified collective-bath claim. No peer bath proof was read.

Let N spin-one-half sites have J_z=(1/2)sum_i sigma_z,i and
J_+=sum_i |0><1|_i, J_-=J_+*. Use D[L](rho)=L rho L* -{L*L,rho}/2.
Specify the generator
  L(rho)=-i[omega J_z+F(J^2),rho]
          +gamma_- D[J_-](rho)+gamma_+ D[J_+](rho),
where gamma_-,gamma_+>0, omega is real, and F is real on the spin Casimirs.
Additional nonnegative D[J_z] dephasing can be included. Finite-temperature
thermal detailed balance is the special case gamma_+/gamma_-=exp(-beta omega).

For every j, put theta=log(gamma_+/gamma_-) and
  tau_j=exp(theta J_z^(j))/z_j(theta),
  z_j(theta)=sum_{m=-j}^j exp(theta m).
The ladder transition amplitudes squared are
(j-m)(j+m+1) for m->m+1 and (j+m)(j-m+1) for m->m-1. Their paired upward
and downward rates satisfy balance because
  tau_j(m+1)/tau_j(m)=gamma_+/gamma_-.
Thus L_j(tau_j)=0. Its Hamiltonian and dephasing terms vanish on tau_j.
The density tau_j is faithful for finite positive rates.

The stationary density in V_j is unique. Here is a direct finite-dimensional
argument rather than an unnamed primitive-channel import. For the adjoint
generator acting on observables, the exact dissipative identity is
  L_j*(A*A)-L_j*(A*) A-A* L_j*(A)
      =sum_mu gamma_mu [L_mu,A]*[L_mu,A].
The Hamiltonian part cancels by the Leibniz rule. If L_j*(A)=0, then
L_j*(A*)=0. Taking the trace against the faithful stationary tau_j gives
  0=sum_mu gamma_mu Tr tau_j [L_mu,A]*[L_mu,A].
Consequently [J_-,A]=[J_+,A]=0. Their commutator also gives [J_z,A]=0;
the latter is diagonal with distinct m eigenvalues, so A is diagonal in the
spin basis. Commutation with the nonzero adjacent ladder entries forces all
its diagonal entries equal. Hence ker L_j* consists precisely of scalars.
The generator and its adjoint have the same rank, so ker L_j is one-dimensional
and is spanned by tau_j. This includes the one-dimensional j=0 block.

For a PI density on the full tensor product, Schur's lemma for the site S_N
action gives, at fixed N,
  rho=direct_sum_j X_j tensor I_{M_j},
with no cross-j terms. Collective operators act on V_j and as the identity
on M_j. Therefore the PI stationary states of this specified generator are
exactly
  rho_ss=direct_sum_j p_j tau_j tensor I_{M_j}/f_j,
  p_j>=0, sum_j p_j=1.
Each p_j is conserved sector mass. Equivalently this is
sum_j p_j exp(theta J_z)P_j/[f_j z_j(theta)]. The previously reconstructed
singlet-pair/common-qubit ensemble proves all these states 2-producible.
An independent failure of full separability establishes depth exactly two.
It does not follow merely from p_j>0 for some j<N/2.

At zero-temperature pure downward damping, gamma_->0,gamma_+=0, use
  Tr[J_z L_j(rho)]=-gamma_- Tr[J_+J_- rho].
Stationarity and positivity force support in ker J_-, the one-dimensional
lowest-spin-weight vector. Thus the same PI stationary normal form holds
with tau_j=|-j><-j|; it is a theta->-infinity limit of the 2-producible family.
Pure upward damping is analogous. With both transition rates zero, the
uniqueness argument is absent and collective dephasing alone has the W_N
stationary-state counterexample of depth N.

This scope excludes arbitrary nonthermal collective generators, Hamiltonians
not preserving the stated block thermal density, non-PI multiplicity states,
proper higher-spin representations, and an unspecified thermal-bath access
model. All normalizers and generator conventions are explicit. No dynamics
simulation or physical experiment was executed; this is a finite-dimensional
operator derivation. Standard Schur-Weyl theory is the named representation
input, as in the main artifact.
