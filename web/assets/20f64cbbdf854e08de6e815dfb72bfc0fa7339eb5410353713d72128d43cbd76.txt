1. CLOSURE LEMMA RECONSTRUCTED WITHOUT RATE OR STATE-COUNT BOUNDS

Let P_n be finite stationary reversible CTMC outputs converging to P on every rational finite cylinder. Let F_1,...,F_r be fixed bounded rational future-cylinder representatives whose OS classes form a basis of H(P). Express each target vector used below in this basis once and for all. For a hidden state i of model n, define

  j_n v(i) = sum_k c_k E_i^{(n)}[F_k],
  <x_n(i),v> = j_n v(i),

where v=sum_k c_k[F_k].

Each conditional expectation of an indicator lies in [0,1]; therefore the finitely many chosen basis coordinates give one compact box containing every x_n(i), uniformly over hidden dimensions, stationary masses, and rates. Include the deterministic hidden output label with x_n(i) when forming its stationary feature law mu_n. From any subsequence, compactness yields a further subsequence mu_n=>mu.

For a fixed target future cylinder F, choose coefficients c(F) with [F]=sum_k c_k[F_k]. In the reversible model n, reflection norm is exactly the hidden L2(pi_n) norm of the conditional future expectation. Cylinder convergence gives

  || E_.^(n)[F] - j_n[F] ||_(L2(pi_n))^2
    = || [F]-sum_k c_k[F_k] ||_(OS(P_n))^2 -> 0.             (A)

The right side is a finite linear combination of reflected-cylinder probabilities, each a finite joint cylinder and hence convergent. The same argument applies to the constant, to boundary products 1_{Y_0=a}F, and to shifted cylinders U_tF for each fixed rational t. In particular, under the stationary feature law,

  j_n Omega -> 1 in L2,
  j_n(E_a v)-D_a^(n)j_n(v) -> 0 in L2,
  j_n(T_t v)-exp(tQ_n)j_n(v) -> 0 in L2.                    (B)

The second relation identifies the limit label block. The third uses the hidden Markov property at time t. Neither relation differentiates Q_n.

For each nonnegative rational future cylinder F, its actual conditional expectation E_.^(n)[F] is nonnegative. By (A), the negative part of j_n[F] tends to zero in L2, since pointwise (−j_n[F])_+ <= |j_n[F]-E_.^(n)[F]|. In the feature limit this gives <x,[F]> >=0 almost surely. Take the countable intersection over rational cylinders. The constant and boundary relations in (B) give <Omega,x>=1 and E_a x=x for the included hidden label. Gram convergence for the selected basis gives

  integral x x^T dmu(x)=I_H.

Thus mu belongs to Gamma as defined in Sol v2. This derivation makes the zero-reflection-norm step explicit: null relations of P need not hold pointwise in an approximating model, but their conditional L2 errors vanish because the corresponding finite reflected norms converge to zero. The limiting measure, rather than any approximant's feature points, satisfies all target relations.

Now assume every measure in Gamma is supported on a fixed finite set S={x_1,...,x_m}; write mu= sum_i pi_i delta_(x_i), discarding zero weights. For each fixed rational t>0, take the stationary joint law of (x_n(X_0),x_n(X_t)) under model n. Reversibility makes it symmetric. A subsequential limit kappa_t has both marginals mu and is supported on S×S. Test the last relation in (B) against any bounded continuous function f(x_n(X_0)); the L2 error vanishes by Cauchy–Schwarz. The limiting identity is

  integral f(x)<y,v> dkappa_t(x,y)
       = integral f(x)<x,T_t v> dmu(x).                       (C)

Since S is finite with distinct points, continuous f can isolate each x_i. Therefore

  sum_j kappa_t(i,j) x_j = pi_i T_t x_i,
  kappa_t(i,j)=kappa_t(j,i),  sum_j kappa_t(i,j)=pi_i.       (D)

This is the precise compatible-coupling consequence of cylinder closure. It requires no compactness of the hidden generators.

For any sequence of positive rational t_l down to zero, choose a coupling kappa_(t_l) as in (D). Using the covariance identity and self-adjointness of T_t,

  sum_ij kappa_t(i,j)||x_i-x_j||^2
    =2 dim(H)-2 tr(T_t)
    =2 tr(I-T_t)
    <=2t tr(A).                                                (E)

The first equality follows by expanding the square: the two marginal terms each equal tr(I), while the cross term is sum_i pi_i<x_i,T_t x_i>=tr(T_t). For distinct points in S let d=min_(i!=j)||x_i-x_j||>0. Equation (E) bounds sum_(i!=j) kappa_t(i,j)/t by 2 tr(A)/d^2. A subsequence has finite limits w_ij>=0 with w_ij=w_ji. Subtract pi_i x_i from (D), divide by t, and use (T_t-I)x_i/t -> -A x_i. This yields

  sum_(j!=i) w_ij(x_j-x_i)=-pi_i A x_i,

which is exactly the common reversible edge-flow frame condition. This closes the arbitrary hidden dimension/rate gap. The proof uses support rigidity essentially: without a fixed finite S, the minimum separation d may be zero and (E) does not give a finite edge-flow extraction.

2. EXACT PROCESS AND GLOBAL CP CHECK

The hidden generator is finite rational, so stochastically continuous. Uniform stationarity follows from the exact zero column sums. The base directed internal flows are balanced at every vertex; adding the symmetric reset and marker conductances preserves stationarity. The reset puts every internal off-diagonal rate strictly above zero. Every marker has three positive internal links, as its facet vanishes only at the two adjacent vertices. Hence the full graph is connected; the independent reconstruction reaches all ten states. At least eight internal directed edge pairs are asymmetric after the reset, so detailed balance fails, while every present edge has a reverse. The stationary entropy-production sum is a finite sum over positive rational rates and is strictly positive.

For the observable subspace H0, the five marker indicators are boundary cylinders. Applying Q to each marker indicator has internal component eta*l_k(v_i); the coefficient vectors of the five affine l_k span the three-dimensional internal affine block (exact rank three). Together with the internal indicator, these generate the stated eight-dimensional H0. Conversely Q and all output projections preserve H0, so every future cylinder conditional expectation lies there. The exact identity QH=HL and det(M)>0 establish the dimension and coordinates. Since L is self-adjoint for the M inner product and D_a reduces H0, every observed cylinder product can be reversed by taking its adjoint. This proves time-reversal invariance for all times.

The projected reverse-chain identity used for global CP is correct with the stated block orientation. Relative to H0 plus H0-perp, Q-invariance gives Q=[[L,B],[0,C]]. Since L is self-adjoint on H0, Q*=[[L,0],[B*,C*]], hence Pi Q*=L Pi and Pi commutes with each output projection. Induction on the number of observations gives Pi h_F^(Q*)=h_F^Q. Conditional past and future are independent given X_0, so

  P(F^- cap G^+) = <h_F^(Q*),h_G^Q>_pi
                 = <h_F^Q,h_G^Q>_pi
                 = (1/10)sum_i h_F^Q(i)h_G^Q(i).

The factors are genuine nonnegative conditional future probabilities and are common to every cylinder refinement. This proves process-level global reflection CP; the hidden reverse conditional laws need not equal the forward laws state-by-state.

3. FRAME RIGIDITY AND METRIC-COORDINATE AUDIT

In the nonorthonormal basis phi=(1_internal,x_internal,y_internal,marker indicators), M=H^T H/10 is the Gram matrix. For a frame point x_i in the Hilbert space, write its evaluation row xi_i^T with xi_i[j]=<x_i,phi_j>. Then the covariance identity becomes integral xi xi^T dmu=M. A label-zero point has coordinates (1,v_x,v_y,0,...,0); a marker-k point is the corresponding marker coordinate unit vector.

For label zero, the future event “marker k at time t” has probability evaluation xi^T exp(tL)e_k. This is nonnegative for every rational t>0 because xi is positive on every nonnegative future cylinder. At t=0 it vanishes. Its right derivative is eta*l_k(v), since the internal-to-marker rate is eta*l_k(v). Hence every label-zero factor has l_k(v)>=0 for all five polygon facets and lies in their polygon. This derivative uses the target finite-dimensional semigroup; it does not assume a hidden generator for a factor.

The five v_i are distinct unit-circle points in the listed strict convex order (all five successive turn determinants are positive). Every polygon point has norm <=1, with equality only at its vertices. The second moments give label-zero mass M_00=1/2 and integral_label0 ||v||^2 dmu=M_xx+M_yy=1/2. Equality forces support on the five vertices. The five moments (1,v_x,v_y,v_x^2,v_x v_y) determine their masses uniquely because the exact determinant in the certificate is -15163556658355961856/10160806025773960625. The known CTMC frame supplies mass 1/10 at each internal vertex. Each marker point's mass is 1/10 from its marker-coordinate variance. Thus every Gamma measure has the same ten-point support and weights; duplicated hidden states can only split these masses.

Metric caution. The coordinates used by the certificate are not an orthonormal coordinate system. The certificate's Z_i are coefficient coordinates of vectors z_i in the phi basis, while xi_i are the evaluation coordinates of the frame vectors x_i. If r_i=M^(-1)xi_i are the coefficient coordinates of x_i, then

  <z_i-z_j,x_i-x_j>_H=(Z_i-Z_j)^T M r_{ij}
                       =(Z_i-Z_j)^T(xi_i-xi_j).

Thus the ordinary dot pairing in the verifier is the Hilbert pairing after the Gram matrix cancels; it is not an unlicensed Euclidean identification. By self-adjointness of A=-L on H, the evaluation coordinates of A x_i are the row -xi_i^T L, equivalently the row -(QH)_i. Therefore the verifier's drift pairing Z_i^T[-(QH)_i] is exactly <z_i,A x_i>_H. The full-field extension with marker coordinates z_i dot v_i then has nonnegative pairings: internal pairs use the strict planar monotonicity; internal-marker pairs cancel to zero; marker-marker pairs are zero. The exact weighted drift is half the negative planar pairing. This is a valid Farkas obstruction in the correct metric.

This audit also narrows the earlier cone comment: a simplicial cone gives an abstract vector-lattice order. It does not imply that its rays are orthogonal, that it is self-dual in the OS inner product, or that it is an isometric positive orthant. It therefore does not by itself supply the metric-sensitive reversible frame. The explicit rank-two construction in T24 v1 remains valid because it constructs the isometry and rates directly.
