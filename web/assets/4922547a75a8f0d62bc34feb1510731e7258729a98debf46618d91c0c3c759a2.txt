## Independent audit of the duplicate P2 proof

Write `p2(x)=1+x+x^2/2`. For the shifted easy-plane edge term with
`chi=3 alpha`, its eigenvalues are

    low = alpha-gamma, mid = 3 alpha+gamma, high = 5 alpha-gamma.

The cone `alpha >= |gamma|` gives `0 <= low <= mid <= high`. Since p2 is
increasing on `[0,infinity)`, the Taylor gate has six-vertex weights

    a=p2(t mid), b=(p2(t high)+p2(t low))/2,
    c=(p2(t high)-p2(t low))/2.

Thus `c >= |a-b|`; also `b-c=p2(t low)>0`, so `c <= b < a+b`.
These are exactly the three six-vertex triangle inequalities required by the
complemented edge polynomial. Equivalently, direct expansion gives

    c-(a-b) = 2t(alpha-gamma)(1+4 alpha t),
    c+(a-b) = 2t(alpha+gamma)(1+2 alpha t),
    a+b-c   = 2+4 alpha t+t^2(4 alpha^2+(alpha+gamma)^2),

all nonnegative on the stated cone. The matrix entries are rational and
nonnegative: the shifted edge matrix is entrywise nonnegative, as are its
square and P2 gate.

For a field term use `r=b+|c|`, `q^2=b^2+c^2`,

    U=1+t r+t^2(r^2+q^2)/2,  w=t+t^2 r,
    P2(t A_v) = [[U+w c, w b],[w b,U-w c]].

Its determinant is `U^2-w^2 q^2`. Set `T=tr`, `X=tq`; then `0<=X<=T`
and

    U-wq = 1+T+(T^2+X^2)/2-X(1+T) >= 1,

because this expression decreases in X on `[0,T]` and equals 1 at `X=T`.
The unchanged-row/complemented-column quadratic is
`d(1+xy)+u x+v y`, with `d=wb`, `u=U-wc`, `v=U+wc`; its lift is

    d z1 z2 + (u/2)x(z1+z2) + (v/2)y(z1+z2) + d x y.

For `d>0`, the Hessian's `(x,y)` block has inertia `(1,1)`. Its Schur
complement has eigenvalues `-d` and `(d^2-uv)/d`, hence at most one positive
eigenvalue exactly when `d^2<=uv`. That inequality is the determinant bound
above. For `d=0` the lift factors as `(z1+z2)(ux+vy)/2`. Its coefficients
are rational and nonnegative. This independently checks the field-lift
claim, including boundary cases.

For completeness, the unprojected constant in c06_l08 is sound. Let
`h=beta/(2m)`, `F_i=p2(h A_i)=exp(G_i)`, `S=sum_i G_i`, and
`g=sum_i ||G_i|| <= hD`. The ordered factors of `B=F_1...F_p F_p...F_1`
are a palindrome. Its degree-zero, one, and two terms agree with `exp(2S)`;
the quadratic equality follows because reversal pairs every commutator with
its negative. Each degree-k norm tail is bounded by `(2g)^k/k!`, so

    ||B-exp(2S)|| <= (8/3) g^3 exp(2g) <= (16/3) h^3 D^3

when `hD<=1/4`. Scalar Taylor remainder and
`p2(x) exp(-x) >= 1-x^3/6` imply
`0<=x-log p2(x)<=x^3/5` for `0<=x<=1`; summing gives
`||2S-2h sum_i A_i|| <= (2/5)h^3 D^3`. Both `B` and `exp(2S)` are at
least identity. The resolvent formula

    log X-log Y = integral_0^infinity ((Y+sI)^-1-(X+sI)^-1) ds

gives `||log X-log Y||<=||X-Y||` for `X,Y >= I`, since the integrand norm
is at most `||X-Y||/(1+s)^2`. Hence

    ||m log B-beta K|| <= (43/60) tau^3/m^2,
    tau=beta D,

when `m>=2 tau`; the l08 choice `m^2>=43 tau^3/(15 epsilon)` makes this
at most `epsilon/4`. I found no algebraic error in that derivation. Its
Taylor gate and order are duplicated by my INITIAL; its tighter constant
and explicit cost audit are l08-origin material.

## New composition: P2 gates with the higher-spin projector

Use c06_s01's spin `S_v=q_v/2` realization as the symmetric subspace of
`q_v` qubits, with `Pi=product_v Pi_(q_v)`. Its projector has rational matrix
entries `1/binom(q,r)` within charge-r blocks and zero elsewhere. Keeping row
bits and complementing column bits yields

    p_q(x,y) = sum_(r=0)^q e_r(x)e_(q-r)(y)/binom(q,r).

The all-q Lorentzian check in s01 is directly auditable: after any derivative
of order `q-2` using `a` row and `b` column variables (`a+b=q-2`), the
quadratic Hessian has row-row off-diagonal `A=1/binom(q,a+2)`, cross entry
`B=1/binom(q,a+1)`, and column-column off-diagonal
`D'=1/binom(q,a)`. On the row- and column-sum-zero spaces the eigenvalues
are `-A` and `-D'`. The remaining two-dimensional block has determinant zero
because

    A D'/B^2 = q-a times (a+2) / ((q-a-1)(a+1)),

and positive trace. Thus every nonzero derivative signature has at most one
positive eigenvalue. The support is the full uniform-matroid support, hence
M-convex. Branden--Huh's signature characterization then proves `p_q`
Lorentzian / completely log-concave for every q. This all-q projector
certificate originates in s01; it is not inferred from our finite checks.

Embed the higher-spin Hamiltonian `H` on constituent qubits exactly as s01:
`J_v^a=(1/2)sum_i sigma_(v,i)^a`, pair coefficient `alpha_uv/4` and
`gamma_uv/4` on each constituent pair, and field coefficients `b_v/2`,
`c_v/2` on each constituent. The resulting `Khat=Hhat+C I` is a sum of
positive shifted local terms `A_i`, satisfies `sum_i ||A_i||<=D=2C`, and
commutes with `Pi`; its restriction is `K=H+C I` on the spin space.

For one layer put `h=beta/m`,

    V = product_i p2((h/2) A_i),
    B0 = V V*,
    Bphys = (Pi B0 Pi)|Ran(Pi).

The full factor list in `B0` is
`(A_1/2,...,A_p/2,A_p/2,...,A_1/2)`, each factor being
`I+h B_j+h^2 B_j^2/2`. The constant, first-, and second-degree terms match
`exp(h Khat)`: the second-degree commutators cancel under reversal. With
`x=hD`, coefficientwise scalar domination by `exp(zx)` bounds the degree
at-least-three tail of either the finite product or the exponential by
`exp(x)x^3/6`. Since `||Khat||<=D`,

    ||B0-exp(h Khat)|| <= delta := exp(x)x^3/3.

This is an absolute operator-norm estimate, not a relative trace estimate.
Every P2 gate is at least identity, so `B0=VV* >= I` (the least singular
value of each factor is at least one). Compression gives `Bphys>=I`. Because
`Pi` commutes with `Khat`,

    ||Bphys-exp(h K)|| <= delta,
    exp(h K) >= I.

The same resolvent identity proves `||log Bphys-hK||<=delta`. Consequently

    ||m log Bphys-beta K|| <= exp(tau/m) tau^3/(3m^2).

For `m>=4 tau`, this is at most `exp(1/4) tau^3/(3m^2)`; the simple exact
integer conditions `m>=4 tau` and `m^2>=4 tau^3/epsilon` imply an error
strictly below `epsilon/4`. This is intentionally conservative. They give

    m = O(1+tau+tau^(3/2)/sqrt(epsilon))

layers and the same multiplicative trace sandwich as the spin-half argument.
The compression is critical: the first-order s01 estimate applies a
layerwise compression directly, while the above proof shows the quadratic
local remainder also survives that operation. A one-time final projection
would not be justified by an uncompressed relative trace estimate; s01 gives
an explicit counterexample to that shortcut.

### Exact coefficient count survives the composition

Each P2 edge/field occurrence contributes the same degree-two local
homogeneous signature and, for each field, the same two-dummy LC lift audited
above. Each spin projector contributes `p_q`. Products of these disjoint
local signatures remain homogeneous log-concave. Pair the two incidences of
each physical bit wire with `(x_i+x_j)` and the `2f` field-dummy variables
with the uniform-matroid polynomial `e_f`. If there are `e` edge gates, `f`
field gates, and projector arities `q_j`, then

    W = 2e+f+sum_j q_j physical wires,
    N = 4e+4f+2 sum_j q_j total variables.

The local product has degree `W+f`; so does the wire/dummy partner. The
squarefree full-set coefficient equals `Tr(Bphys^m)`: each physical wire is
selected once, the dummy rank selects f of 2f variables, and summing those
choices restores every field matrix entry. The exact counts and coefficient
oracles are those of s01 with the P2 gate table substituted. No square-root
Dicke coefficients are required.

There are `2m` embedded local Hamiltonian gates per term per trace circuit,
and m projector copies after adjacent `Pi^2` factors are collapsed. With
`Q=sum_v q_v` and `p=sum_edges q_u q_v + sum_v q_v`,

    N <= 8 m p + 2 m Q.

Projector coefficient bit lengths are O(q), P2 entries are rational with
polynomial bit length, and local product-oracle answers use polynomially
many rational multiplications. Chen--Liu's Theorem 5.1 applies conditionally
to this exact count; its Remark A.4 covers rational bit implementation
given polynomial-length value oracles. The displayed operation count is a
large polynomial in `N`, coefficient-range bits, and inverse error. This
composition improves the layer factor, but does not reduce the imported
counter's high exponent or establish useful wall-clock performance.

### Corrected inherited largest-eigenvalue cost

The final paragraph of my immutable INITIAL compared the P2 partition
schedule with an inherited lambda-max objective and gave an unnecessarily
strict epsilon-dependent layer count. For lambda-max, the trace-to-energy
reduction needs only a constant generator/log-count error: its inverse
temperature already scales as `beta=Theta(Q/(epsilon C))`. Therefore
`tau=Theta(Q/epsilon)` and one may take the log-generator error and the
relative count error constant. The corrected layer bounds are

    old Euler: m=O(tau^2)=O(Q^2 epsilon^-2),
    P2:        m=O(tau^(3/2))=O(Q^(3/2) epsilon^-3/2).

For the stricter partition-function target `epsilon`, the earlier schedule
`m=O(tau^(3/2)/sqrt(epsilon))` is valid but not optimal for lambda-max; it
would give `O(Q^(3/2) epsilon^-2)`. This correction affects only the
downstream lambda-max schedule, not the proved partition error guarantee.
With the inherited higher-spin dimension bound `product_v(q_v+1)<=2^Q`
and `C/5<=lambda_max(H)<=C`, the constant-error reduction remains valid.

### Narrow obstruction to widening the coupling cone

The P2 proof preserves the easy-plane cone but does not enlarge it. In fact,
for the fixed six-vertex orientation and any analytic local gate
`I+t A_e+O(t^2)` with the same first derivative, its triangle inequalities
have first-order terms

    c-(a-b) = 2t(alpha-gamma)+O(t^2),
    c+(a-b) = 2t(alpha+gamma)+O(t^2).

If `gamma>alpha` or `gamma<-alpha`, one of these is negative for all
sufficiently small positive t. Thus no analytic higher-order correction
with this same first derivative and local signature orientation can widen
the cone. This does not rule out a larger-block or different-orientation
decomposition; those avenues remain unproved. For fields, a negative X
coefficient similarly makes the first-order off-diagonal matrix entry
negative in this fixed basis. These are representation-specific
obstructions, not hardness claims about the physical Hamiltonians.

## Further attributed consequence: no-balancing at zero longitudinal field

After auditing c06_s03/INITIAL.txt, there is one more composable consequence
for the subfamily `c_v=0`. This is an extension of s03's complement-symmetry
argument, not an original c06_l03 result. For each local gate write its
coefficient as a function on subsets of that gate's row, complemented-column,
and (for fields) dummy variables. At `c_v=0`:

* A P2 edge gate is invariant under simultaneously flipping both row bits
  and both column bits. Its six-vertex entries pair complementary monomials.
* A P2 field gate is `[[U,d],[d,U]]`; the homogeneous field lift has equal
  x- and y-coefficients. Complementation exchanges `d z_1z_2` with `dxy`
  and exchanges the four equal mixed terms.
* The projector coefficient is `1/binom(q,r)` when the row and column
  charges agree. Complementation sends `r` to `q-r`, and
  `binom(q,q-r)=binom(q,r)`.

Thus every factor of the product measure `mu` is complement-invariant. The
wire partner has weight one on exactly one endpoint per pair, so complement
swaps the two endpoints without changing its coefficient. Its dummy factor
`e_f` has weight one on f-subsets of 2f variables, and complement preserves
f-subsets. Therefore the second measure `nu` is also complement-invariant.
Both are homogeneous log-concave of degree N/2; p_q supplies the only new
degree-q local factor. These identities hold for every q, not only the q<=5
finite check.

For clarity, the imported no-balancing implication is short. At z=1 the soft
law is proportional to `mu(S)nu(T)t^|S intersect T|`. The simultaneous
complement involution preserves overlap when both sets have size N/2, so for
each coordinate its zero-copy and two-copy probabilities satisfy p0=p2.
Apply Chen--Liu Lemma 5.5 after removing that coordinate's overlap penalty and
choosing its field in the proof to make the reweighted q0=q2. For the
indicator of zero copies, its variance is q0(1-q0); its Dirichlet form is
at most q1 because it can change only through a one-copy state and that
coordinate has total update rate at most one. The gap-one inequality yields
`q0(1-q0)<=q1`, hence `q1/q0>=1/2` and
`p0*p2<=4t*p1^2`. The proof-only field is not used by the algorithm. Since
p0=p2, `p0+p2<=4 sqrt(t)`. A union bound with
`t=1/(256 N^2)` gives probability at least 3/4 to the hard event
`T` equal to the complement of `S` in `U`, with every actual external field
fixed to one. The only
analytic input is the imported all-field Poincare statement; the symmetry
calculation and implication above are explicit.

Projector factors have higher degree than the fixed-degree-two local tables
in s03's existing transition implementation, so its code and claimed
O(log N) update cannot be copied unchanged. There is nevertheless a simple
exact extension for this specific projector. Fix a supported q-subset in a
projector factor and delete u, leaving q-1 selected coordinates. Let r be
the number of selected row variables after deletion. Each eligible
unselected row variable has reinsertion weight `1/binom(q,r+1)`; each
eligible unselected column variable has weight `1/binom(q,r)`. With z=1
these weights are constant within each of the two groups. Sum the two group
masses exactly, choose a group by a rational coin, then choose a uniform
eligible coordinate from its group using an order-statistic tree. Pins only
remove candidates and do not change weights within a group. Maintaining
the two eligible sets for each projector factor under one-coordinate updates
costs O(log q) operations and O(q) leaves. The field and edge factors retain
s03's degree-two transition. This gives a concrete O(log N) local-transition
implementation route and keeps its no-balancing proof valid, but I have not
implemented or run that projector-aware sampler or its complete count
wrapper. I therefore claim the symmetry/no-balancing lemma for the composed
P2/high-spin coefficient functions, not an executed higher-spin specialized
FPRAS or a newly certified runtime.

If that tree-based transition is implemented as described, the same
majority-path/mixing derivation in s03 gives its displayed arithmetic count
`O(N^6(H+log(N/rho)) rho^-2 log N log(N/delta))`, with
`H=2N+ceil(log2 R)+(N/2)ceil(log2(256N^2))` and `R` recomputed for the P2
and projector coefficient tables. This is a conditional cost transfer from
s03, not a measured runtime or an independent full bit-complexity audit.
