Claim and scope

The O(m^(-1/2)) rate in DIRICHLET_POWER_LIFT.txt cannot in general be
improved to o(m^(-1/2)) for the prescribed post-composition E_m=Psi o Phi^m
from only the hypotheses I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi). An exact unital-qubit family
attains

  sqrt(m) ||Phi^m-Psi o Phi^m||_(infinity->1,tau)
      -> 1/sqrt(2e).

This is sharpness of this certificate-based construction and these abstract
hypotheses. It is NOT a lower bound on the distance of Phi^m from the set of
EB channels: every Phi in the example is itself EB, so the optimal EB
approximation error is zero. It neither refutes a better EB lift nor attacks
the unrestricted compatible-channel question.

Exact qubit construction

Let sigma_u=u_1 X+u_2 Y+u_3 Z for a unit Bloch vector u. Let Delta_u be
dephasing in the eigenbasis of sigma_u; on Bloch vectors its matrix is the
orthogonal projection uu^T. Let D be completely depolarizing. For an angle
alpha with s=sin(alpha), c=cos(alpha), put

  delta=s^2/(1+s^2), lambda=1-delta,
  Phi=lambda Delta_u + delta D,
  Psi=Delta_v,       v=c u+s w,  w perpendicular to u.

Both maps are unital, trace-preserving, EB, selfadjoint, and positive as
L2(tau) operators. Thus Phi is selfcompatible. On the traceless Bloch space,

  I-Phi = I-lambda uu^T,
  I-Psi = I-vv^T.

On the scalar identity direction both defects vanish. In the (u,w) plane,
the generalized comparison matrix is

  (I-Phi)^(-1/2)(I-Psi)(I-Phi)^(-1/2)
   = [s/sqrt(delta), -c]^T [s/sqrt(delta), -c],

whose only nonzero eigenvalue is

  s^2/delta+c^2=(1+s^2)+(1-s^2)=2.

The remaining Bloch direction has comparison eigenvalue 1. Consequently

  I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi),

with best constant exactly 2.

Since Phi has only one nonzero traceless eigenvalue lambda, Phi^m has Bloch
matrix lambda^m uu^T. Therefore

  Phi^m-Psi o Phi^m=(I-Psi)Phi^m,
  ||Phi^m-Psi o Phi^m||_(infinity->1,tau)=lambda^m s.       (S1)

For the norm identity, a Hermitian qubit contraction aI+b.sigma has |b|<=1,
the normalized trace norm of x.sigma is |x|, and
||(I-vv^T)u||_2=s. Equality is attained with a=0 and |b|=1 along u.

An exact rational asymptotic sequence is available. For integer n>=2 choose
the Pythagorean direction

  s_n=2n/(n^2+1),  c_n=(n^2-1)/(n^2+1),
  delta_n=s_n^2/(1+s_n^2), lambda_n=1-delta_n,
  m_n=nearest integer to 1/(2 delta_n).

Then m_n delta_n -> 1/2, s_n sqrt(m_n)->1/sqrt(2), and
lambda_n^(m_n)->e^(-1/2). Equation (S1) gives the asserted limit. In
particular this family forces a uniform lower bound of order m^(-1/2) for
the error of the designated post-composition lift under c=2.

Consequence for the prior revision

The generic bound sqrt(2 g_m) is therefore order-optimal in m for the
specified E_m=Psi o Phi^m construction, up to constants. The example also
pinpoints the source of the square root: the channel's slow eigendirection
is tilted by angle Theta(sqrt(delta)) from the fixed algebra of Psi. If Phi
and Psi commute as L2 operators, simultaneous diagonalization gives

  ||(I-Psi)Phi^m||_(2->2)
    <= 2 max_(0<=lambda<=1) (1-lambda)lambda^m
    = 2 m^m/(m+1)^(m+1) = O(1/m).

The same upper bound applies to the Hermitian infinity-to-one norm by
||.||_1<=||.||_2 and ||H||_2<=||H||_infinity. Thus the noncommuting
geometry is essential to this square-root obstruction.

The phrase “given certificate” is load-bearing. Here Phi itself is EB, and
choosing that different EB comparator makes the approximation exact. No
claim is made that noncommutativity forces a slow optimal EB approximation.
