## Audit findings and relation to N77

**c10_s01.** Its theorem is explicitly restricted to roots with direct
immigration/death, child species catalysed only by those roots, and no other
reactions. The exact drift has a reciprocal root-count correction for a
zero-catalyst face. N77's A/B/C cycle is not an autonomous immigration/death
root layer: it has feedback among species and reaction molecularity up to 5;
D's birth/death requires 2C rather than a root's direct immigration/death.
Thus N77 neither falsifies nor falls inside the c10_s01 theorem. The c10_s01
artifact itself identifies closest prior art in environment-modulated
Poisson mixtures and established Foster/LP methods; its novelty is unresolved.
I found no algebraic contradiction in the displayed boundary drift on the
textual pass, but did not independently formalize every stopping-time step.

**c10_s02.** Its exact network is genuinely bimolecular, with one recurrent
component \(\Gamma\) containing positive states and a separate absorbing
\(\Gamma_0\). The obstruction proves that a named factorial potential and
globally coercive concave potentials cannot be strict Foster functions on that
component; it also proves boundary visits while an explicit light-tailed
stationary law exists. It does not exhibit stationary laws escaping as a
component parameter grows. The c10_l04 family supplies that missing
quantifier pattern, but uses molecularity 5 and so does not contradict any
bimolecular theorem. The c10_s02 text itself reports the close prior-art path
method of Anderson--Cappelletti--Fan--Kim (2023 manuscript / 2025 SIADS); this
substantially limits novelty of the general boundary obstruction. No gap was
found in the exact transition/factorial identities during this read; the
all-state concavity argument remains a textual proof, not an external review.

**c10_s03.** Its admitted certificate requires reaction orders at most 2,
unary paths \(e_i\leadsto0\), a support-production grammar starting from the
zero complex, and source-wise rational Foster inequalities. N77 violates
these gates independently: the product \(3A+2B\) has order 5; there is no
unary drain reaction; and no zero-source reaction starts the support grammar.
It therefore is not a counterexample to the c10_s03 theorem. Conversely,
c10_s03 does not close N77's classwise tightness issue. Its balanced-cross
fixture is expressly a rejection from the admitted certificate class, not a
claim of non-recurrence. I re-read the complete proof narrative and found no
specific algebraic contradiction in the main drift, path, or retry formulas;
the INITIAL itself says detailed independent review of the retry bound,
predictable-rate hazard conditioning, bit bounds, and implementation remains
open. No external proof verification is supplied.

## Prior art, attribution, and unresolved proof/priority gates

Primary-source dependencies of the c10_l04 reconstruction are the established
single-linkage global-attractor result of Anderson (arXiv:1101.0761,
Corollary 28) and the complex-balanced product-form result of Anderson,
Craciun, and Kurtz (arXiv:0803.3042). Exact communicating-class identification
is also established subject matter; see Gupta and Khammash, “Computational
identification of irreducible state-spaces for stochastic reaction networks,”
arXiv:1505.06594. The c10_s01 INITIAL cites prior work on
environment-modulated Poisson mixtures and Foster/LP methods; c10_s02 cites
ACFK's path method; c10_s03 cites LP/Foster and support-exhaustion work. Those
attributions belong to the respective Sol authors. They do not establish
novelty or invalidate the restricted c10_l04 example.

The targeted search did not locate the exact N77 reaction/invariant pair.
Absence from these search results is weak negative evidence only. Historical
priority is UNKNOWN. The source inventory says the full N77 note, executable,
archive, and reported 216 checks are unavailable; only its supplied summary
was available before independent reconstruction. My finite checks do not
replay those reported checks. The remaining load-bearing gates are external
priority search for an exact or equivalent escaping family and an independent
mathematical review of the global-attractor citation and stochastic
class/normalization derivation. No failure of the three Sol results is
established by this audit.
