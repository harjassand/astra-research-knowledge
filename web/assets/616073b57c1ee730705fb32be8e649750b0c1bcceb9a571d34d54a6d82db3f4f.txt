Q10 — ADVERSARIAL EB-INTERFACE AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internal derivation audit; no external proof validation or priority review.

Target. For n copies of ρ_(r,v)=(I+r v·σ)/2, v∈S², r≤1/2 and c0≤r√n≤c1 for fixed 0<c0≤c1<∞, the candidate asserts a worst-case whole-output half-trace lower e_EB≥c(c0,c1)/√(n log n) against every blind entanglement-breaking encoder/decoder. The archive metric is D=Σ_j d_j; no side register or entangled reference is free. This is distinct from the resolved-gap N39 bound (κ≥64), which does not apply at fixed κ=nr²=Θ(1).

Audit verdict. The spin-twirl master inequality survives the full permitted EB interface, including arbitrary outcome alphabets and every input-sector/output-sector transition. The reduction does not assume a same-sector decoder or discard a label the decoder receives for free. The local cutoff gives the claimed order with constants depending on c0,c1, using the exact Schur law and uniform Stirling bounds. This is a worker-level analytic check, not external validation.

1. Arbitrary EB map and sector labels

Every finite-dimensional EB channel has a measure-and-prepare representation Φ(X)=∫Tr(M_y X)ω_y dν(y), with Σ_y M_y=I and each ω_y a density matrix. Continuous alphabets are handled by the integral; the proof has no outcome-count hypothesis. This is the standard EB characterization of Horodecki–Shor–Ruskai, Theorem 2 (source below). The same Φ is used for every v: the encoder is blind to v, and ω_y cannot depend on v. Knowing r can only help the encoder; the lower is pointwise in r and applies whether or not it is supplied.

Schur decomposition, with L=n mod 2,…,n and m_L=binom(n,(n−L)/2)−binom(n,(n−L)/2−1), is
ρ_(r,v)^⊗n ≅ ⊕_L P_L τ_(L,v)⊗I_(m_L)/m_L,
τ_(L,v)=q^(L/2−J_v)/Z_L(q), q=(1−r)/(1+r), Z_s(q)=Σ_(a=0)^s q^a.
The law P_L is independent of v. It is not an extra decoder register. In the POVM, only the diagonal compression M_(y,L)=Π_L M_y Π_L contributes because the input is block diagonal; arbitrary off-diagonal POVM blocks are allowed. In the prepared state, only s_(y,K)=Tr(Π_K ω_y) contributes to the block-diagonal witness; arbitrary output coherences are allowed.

Define the genuine joint sector law
W_(LK)=P_L ∫ [Tr(M_(y,L)(I_(L+1)⊗I_(m_L)/m_L))/(L+1)] s_(y,K) dν(y)
= P_L ∫ [Tr M_(y,L)/((L+1)m_L)]s_(y,K)dν(y).
It is nonnegative, has input marginal P_L, and has output marginal Q_K=∫Tr(Π_K Φ(ρ_(r,v)^⊗n))dμ(v). Thus W couples every L to every K; no L=K restriction occurs. Measuring output sectors and data processing give TV(P,Q)≤∫T(Φ(ρ_v^⊗n),ρ_v^⊗n)dμ(v)≤e. A decoder that records L must encode it in y; W does not hand L to the decoder.

2. Twirl and master inequality

For all L,K, direct SU(2) twirling gives
||∫τ_(L,v)⊗q^(K/2−J_v)dμ(v)||∞ = Z_(L+K)(q)/((L+K+1)Z_L(q)).
On total spin j the scalar is q^((L+K)/2−j) Z_(2j)(q)/((2j+1)Z_L(q)); it is largest at j=(L+K)/2. This is a direct state⊗effect calculation, with no Choi transpose.

Put f(s)=Z_s(q)/(s+1), t_L=(L+1)/Z_L(q), b_L=t_L²f(2L), T_L=Z_L(q²)/Z_L(q), g_L=T_L−t_Lf(2L). For S={L≤J}, C=max_(L∈S)t_L, η=P(L>J), use the valid output effect
F_v=⊕_(K∈S)(t_K/C)q^(K/2−J_v)⊗I_(m_K), 0≤F_v≤I.
Its target score is C^−1Σ_(L∈S)P_Lt_LT_L. The twirl bounds the EB score on each L,K∈S by W_(LK) C^−1t_Lt_Kf(L+K). Since f is a moment integral, f(L+K)≤√(f(2L)f(2K)); hence this is at most W_(LK)(b_L+b_K)/(2C). The input-marginal term is C^−1Σ_SP_Lb_L. Since 0≤b_K/C≤1 on S, replacing the output marginal Q by P costs at most TV(Q,P)≤e, with the preceding factor 1/2. Input mass L>J can contribute at most η; output K>J is excluded by F_v. Consequently the EB score is ≤C^−1Σ_SP_Lb_L+e/2+η. Whole-state trace error bounds the target/decoded score gap by e for each v. Subtraction yields
(3/2)e ≥ C^−1Σ_(L∈S)P_L t_L g_L−η.
This calculation includes sector-changing prepared outputs. It also shows why the hypothesis is whole-state error: F_v depends on v, and is a legal witness only because T(Φ(ρ_v^⊗n),ρ_v^⊗n)≤e for every v.

3. Local-scale cutoff and rate check

Use J=ceil[nr+2√(n log(1/r))]. Let θ=r√n∈[c0,c1]. With a=1−q≤2r,
t_L=(L+1)a/(1−q^(L+1))≤1+a(L+1),
so C≤1+2r(J+1)=O_(c1)(√log(1/r)).
The exact sector law is
P_L(r)=m_L[(1−r²)/4]^((n−L)/2)[((1+r)/2)^(L+1)−((1−r)/2)^(L+1)]/r,
with P_L(0)=m_L(L+1)2^−n. Uniform Stirling expansion on compact x=L/√n and θ gives parity-lattice mass
P_L(r)=(2/√n) f_θ(x)+o(n^−1/2),
f_θ(x)=√(2/π)x² exp[−(x²+θ²)/2] sinh(θx)/(θx).
For A=2/c0 and B=A+1, the integral of f_θ over [A,B] has a strictly positive minimum over θ∈[c0,c1]. For large n this interval is retained (B<J/√n), has probability at least p0(c0,c1)>0, and rL≥2 there. The exact inequality g_L≥r/2−1/[2(2L+1)] and t_L≥1 imply t_Lg_L≥r/4 on that interval. Thus Σ_SP_Lt_Lg_L≥p0 r/4.

For the upper tail, the same exact law can be written
P_L(r)=P_L(0)(1−r²)^((n+1)/2) sinh((L+1)atanh r)/(r(L+1)).
Stirling’s binomial bound and sinh x≤e^x/2 for x≥1 give, for J<L≤n/2,
P_L(r)≤C_(c0,c1)n^−1/2(1+y/c0)e^(−y²/2), y=(L−nr)/√n.
The remaining L>n/2 tail is exponentially small in n. Since y≥2√log(1/r)+O(n^−1/2), summing the parity lattice gives η=O_(c0,c1)(r²). Hence η=o(r/√log(1/r)), and the master inequality yields e≥c'(c0,c1)r/√log(1/r)=c''(c0,c1)/√(n log n) for sufficiently large n. Constants and the threshold n0 depend on c0,c1. This is an asymptotic analytic check; no finite-n numerical threshold or optimized constant is claimed.

4. Memory and label costs

The original output Hilbert space has dimension 2^n. Its spin direct sum has floor(n/2)+1 sectors and reduced spin dimension Σ_(L parity)(L+1)=(floor(n/2)+1)(ceil(n/2)+1)=Θ(n²); multiplicities m_L are regenerated fixed states in the statistical-experiment representation. This Θ(n²) is not an archive bound or a free stored flag. If a physical archive retains L, it pays for that input-dependent symbol: at least ceil(log₂(floor(n/2)+1)) bits for the sector index, and it is included in D. The lower itself needs no finite outcome or D assumption and remains true with unlimited classical outcomes. For a classical algebra all d_j=1, D is its number of symbols; this EB result says no amount of classical capacity beats the floor. If any d_j>1, the composite need not be EB; the lower does not determine Q=max d_j or a minimum hybrid D. In finite input/output dimension h=2^n, Carathéodory on the normalized separable Choi state gives a finite measure-and-prepare realization with at most h^4=2^(4n) outcomes (at most 4n label bits); this is a representation upper bound, not a limit used in the converse. Any actual input-dependent archive label is charged. Fixed randomness only mixes EB maps and does not change the lower. Entangled references and parameter-correlated side registers are outside the stated interface.

5. Attempted finite counterchannel; single-POVM boundary

For n=1, take the covariant POVM M(du)=(I+u·σ)dμ(u) and prepare |u⟩⟨u|=(I+u·σ)/2. On ρ_(r,v), the outcome density is 1+r v·u and the output is (I+(r/3)v·σ)/2, so the half-trace error is r/3. This is a concrete EB sanity channel, not a counterexample to the n→∞ local lower; it is outside the asymptotic cutoff argument and gives no o(r/√log(1/r)) sequence.

If the task asks only for one fixed finite-outcome later POVM E={E_z} (known before encoding), a classical encoder measures E and stores z; a decoder returns z. This exactly reproduces that POVM’s outcome law, with D=|Z| symbols (ceil(log₂|Z|) bits). Therefore the full-state EB floor cannot be claimed for that one-query task. A query selected after storage from a family is a different interface; this construction does not cover it.
