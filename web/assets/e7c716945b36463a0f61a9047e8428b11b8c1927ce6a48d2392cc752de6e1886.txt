STRONGEST CLAIM AT THIS CHECKPOINT

There is a projective-bundle representation of the normal cocycle that turns
the power deficit into a sum containing an exact, weighted misalignment cost.
For the supplied smooth, nowhere-zero, divergence-free three-dimensional
interface, and using the imported Pesin entropy formula for smooth invariant
volume, the identity is

  Delta = Var_mu(s) + 4 H A + 4 A^2
                 + (3/4) E_mu[q^2] + E_mu[|b|^2],
  A = integral_{P(N)} s(x) sin^2(angle(l,E_+(x))) d nu_+(x,l) >= 0.

Here Delta = W/(4 nu_visc V) - H^2, H=h_mu(phi_1)>0, N=X^perp,
q=X(log |X|), b=P(Def X)e, and s is the positive eigenvalue of the
traceless part of P(Def X)P on N. The probability nu_+ is a projective
invariant lift of normalized volume attaining the top normal Lyapunov
exponent; E_+ is the measurable +s principal strain line (arbitrary when
s=0). Consequently A <= Delta/(4H), E_mu[(s-H)^2] <= Delta, and, with
delta=Delta/H^2, the projective lift sees E[sin^2 angle(l,E_+)] <= 9 delta/2.
For every fixed time T, if K_T=sup_x cond(Q_T(x)) is the condition number of
the normal cocycle, the strain line's mean-square invariance defect obeys

  integral sin^2(angle(Q_T E_+(x), E_+(phi_T x))) dmu(x)
       <= 9 (1+K_T^2) Delta/H^2.

The bound charges K_T; K_T <= exp(2 T ||s||_infinity), and the power deficit
does not bound ||s||_infinity. This is a measurable projective statement,
not global geometric closeness for arbitrary near-minimizers.

A second exact reduction gives actual geometric stability on a positive-volume
subclass. For hyperbolic torus mapping tori with metric
g=dt^2+dx^T G_t dx, det G_t=1, G_{t+1}=A^T G_t A, and X=partial_t, the
strain path is a curve in SL(2,R)/SO(2) with speed 2s. The mapping-torus
entropy H=log rho(A) is the translation length divided by two. If
Delta=integral_0^1 s(t)^2 dt-H^2 and epsilon is the path-length excess over
the isometry's translation length 2H, then

  Delta >= H epsilon + epsilon^2/4,
  epsilon <= 2(sqrt(H^2+Delta)-H),
  sup_t dist_P(G_t, Axis(A)) <= sqrt(Delta) + r_0,

where r_0=dist_P(G_0,Axis(A)) satisfies
  r_0 <= arcosh(sinh(H+epsilon/2)/sinh(H)).
When G_0 lies on the axis, r_0=0 and the uniform bound is sqrt(Delta).
Distance in P controls multiplicative distortion of the transverse metric.
This closes a quantitative rigidity gate for the stated mapping-torus ansatz,
whose entropy is carried by normalized volume on every torus fiber. It does
not cover general incompressible flows or establish a computation-per-work

DERIVATION: PROJECTIVE NORMAL COCYCLE

Let (M,g) be a smooth closed Riemannian three-manifold, X smooth and
nowhere-zero with div X=0, V=Vol(M), mu=dVol/V, phi_t its autonomous flow,
and S=Def X. Put r=|X|, e=X/r, P=I-e tensor e, and N=e-perp. The audited
moving-normal identity is

  P nabla_X w = P nabla_w X

for w=P Dphi_t v. It follows by writing v=w+a e in
nabla_X v=nabla_v X, differentiating P v, and cancelling the a terms using
nabla_X e=P nabla_e X. Therefore the normal quotient cocycle
Q_t=P_(phi_t x) Dphi_t|_(N_x) has logarithmic radial derivative

  d/dt log |Q_t v| = <B v,v>/|v|^2,
  B=(P S P)|_N.

Write q=<Se,e>=X(log r), b=PSe, and B=B_0-(q/2)I_N. Since div X=0,
tr B=-q. The symmetric traceless two-dimensional B_0 has eigenvalues
plus/minus s. In an orthonormal frame (e, normal), this gives

  |S|^2 = 2s^2 + (3/2)q^2 + 2|b|^2.

The induced flow on the projective bundle P(N) is denoted phihat_t, and

  ghat(x,[v])=<Bv,v>/|v|^2.

There is a phihat_t-invariant probability nu_+ with base marginal mu such
that integral ghat dnu_+ = integral chi dmu, where chi(x)>=0 is the top
normal Lyapunov exponent. To construct it, use the measurable top Oseledets
line where chi>0; on the invariant chi=0 set, all projective directions have
zero exponent and time averages of any lift give an invariant lift. Conversely
any invariant lift has growth at most chi, so the top lift maximizes. This
is the projective variational formula

  integral chi dmu = max_{nu invariant, pi_*nu=mu} integral ghat dnu.

The normal exponents are chi,-chi because the normal determinant is
r(x)/r(phi_t x), whose exponential rate is zero. The X-line exponent is
zero. Pesin's formula applies to the C-infinity map phi_1 and its smooth
invariant volume, so H=integral chi dmu. This is an imported theorem, not a
new proof of Pesin theory. Also integral q dmu=integral X(log r)dmu=0.

Let E_+(x) be the +s eigenline of B_0 when s>0 and let theta be its
unoriented angle from [v]. Then

  ghat = s cos(2 theta) - q/2,
  integral ghat dnu_+ = integral s dmu - 2 A.

Thus integral s-H=2A. The stationary strain-viscosity power identity is
W=2 nu_visc integral |S|^2 dVol. Combining this identity with the block
decomposition gives

  Delta = E[s^2]-H^2+(3/4)E[q^2]+E[|b|^2]
         = Var(s)+(E[s]-H)(E[s]+H)+(3/4)E[q^2]+E[|b|^2]
         = Var(s)+4HA+4A^2+(3/4)E[q^2]+E[|b|^2].

For the robust line-invariance estimate, Delta>=4HA and
E[(s-H)^2]=Var(s)+4A^2<=Delta. Hence
mu{s<H/2}<=4 Delta/H^2, while
integral_{s>=H/2} sin^2(theta)dnu_+ <= Delta/(2H^2). Their sum gives
m:=integral sin^2(theta)dnu_+ <=9 Delta/(2H^2). On RP^1 the sine of the
angle is a metric, and Q_T acts with Lipschitz constant at most its linear
condition number. Invariance of nu_+ and the squared triangle inequality
then give the stated factor 2(1+K_T^2)m. Gronwall on the largest and smallest
normal growth rates gives K_T<=exp(2T||s||_infinity). No uniform bound on
this cost follows from Delta alone.

If one uses only the earlier Ruelle inequality rather than Pesin's formula,
set R=integral chi dmu-H>=0. The projective identity remains
E[s]-H=2A+R, and the exact power identity is
Delta=Var(s)+(2A+R)(2H+2A+R)+(3/4)E[q^2]+E[|b|^2]. The robust upper bounds
above still follow, with the slightly weaker estimates obtained from
E[(s-H)^2]<=Delta+Delta^2/(4H^2); the smooth-volume Pesin input removes R.

DERIVATION: MAPPING-TORUS REDUCTION AND STABILITY

Let A in SL(2,Z) have positive hyperbolic eigenvalues lambda,lambda^-1,
lambda>1, and form M_A=(T^2 x R)/((x,t)~(Ax,t-1)). Let G_t be a smooth
determinant-one positive matrix satisfying G_(t+1)=A^T G_t A. Then
g=dt^2+dx^T G_t dx descends, X=partial_t is unit and divergence-free,
and phi_1 acts as A on each fiber. Haar-volume metric entropy is H=log
lambda (the standard toral-automorphism formula; the cycle-1 report also
contains a direct cylinder-area derivation).

Let P={G=G^T>0:det G=1}, with
  ||dot G||_G^2=(1/2)tr[(G^-1 dot G)^2].
The map G -> A^T G A is an isometry. The curve t -> G_t has speed 2s(t),
because the eigenvalues of (1/2)G^-1 dot G are plus/minus s. The coordinate
map z=x+iy -> y^-1 [[1,x],[x,x^2+y^2]] identifies P with the curvature -1
hyperbolic plane and this metric with (dx^2+dy^2)/y^2.

For any G, let sigma_G(A) be the operator norm of A in the G-inner product.
The eigenvalues of G^-1/2 A^T G A G^-1/2 are sigma_G(A)^2 and
sigma_G(A)^-2; hence
  dist_P(G,A^T G A)=2 log sigma_G(A)>=2 log rho(A)=2H.
The minimum translation length ell=2H is attained exactly on the invariant
axis, where the eigenlines of A are G-orthogonal. If L=2 integral s and
epsilon=L-ell, Jensen gives
  Delta=integral s^2-H^2 >= (integral s)^2-H^2
       = H epsilon + epsilon^2/4.

For any t, set d0=dist_P(G0,G1)=ell+delta0, 0<=delta0<=epsilon. The two
subpath lengths through G_t sum to L=ell+epsilon, so their Euclidean
comparison triangle has base d0 and triangle excess at most epsilon-delta0.
Its altitude squared is at most
  d0(epsilon-delta0)/2 + (epsilon-delta0)^2/4
  = ell epsilon/2 + epsilon^2/4 - ell delta0/2 - delta0^2/4
  <= H epsilon + epsilon^2/4 <= Delta.
CAT(0) comparison therefore puts every G_t within sqrt(Delta) of the
geodesic segment [G0,G1]. The hyperbolic translation formula in Fermi
coordinates around the A-axis gives
  sinh(d0/2)=cosh(r0) sinh(H),
where r0=dist_P(G0,Axis(A)). Since d0<=ell+epsilon,
  r0<=arcosh(sinh(H+epsilon/2)/sinh(H)).
The geodesic segment stays within r0 of the axis by convexity of distance to
that axis. This proves the uniform tube bound. If G0 is on the axis, the
segment is the axis itself and the bound is sqrt(Delta). Also
integral(s-H)^2=Delta-H epsilon<=Delta. If d_P(G,G')<=rho, the relative
fiber-metric eigenvalues are e^{+/-rho}, so the tube estimate is a
multiplicative metric-closeness certificate.

The exact Sol case is G_t=A^{2t} in an orthonormal eigenbasis for the
positive symmetric matrix A=[[2,1],[1,1]], with H=log((3+sqrt(5))/2).
It has s=H, Delta=0, and its full torus volume realizes entropy. This
mapping-torus reduction does not imply that general near-minimizers are
close to homogeneous geometry.
