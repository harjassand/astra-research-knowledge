## Attacks on the load-bearing steps

1. **Affine activity with ties and limiting faces.** Candidate lines 166–399 construct a finite affine minimum for an arbitrary pointwise-positive tolerance, including discontinuous tolerances. I checked the induction's comparison to the type-zero label, the raised-coordinate cutoff, the competitor on an unraised coordinate, closure of the active-label limit set, and the finite interval gluing. The latter depends on the intervals being centered at their scale parameter: after deleting contained intervals, their endpoints are ordered, so cuts can be placed in every overlap. The global offsets preserve within-family activity; the (h^{q_j}) shifts exclude labels whose limiting minimum exponent lies outside the corresponding local interval. No issue found. This is a nontrivial argument and has not been formalized here.

2. **Endotactic sign and weighted drift.** Candidate lines 401–522 use (D) containing coordinate vectors, all nonzero reaction vectors, and all nonzero source differences. At an active slope (r\notin S^\perp), applying the essential-source definition in direction (-r) selects a reaction at the minimum (r\cdot y) among nonneutral reactions, and every such edge has (r\cdot\nu>0). The selected contribution is at least (2\kappa Hx^w). Each edge with negative (r\cdot\nu) is essential and has strictly higher (r\cdot y); the sign gap gives (x^{y_e}\le h^H x^w). Retaining the factors (r\cdot\nu_e) and charging their sum by (L=\sum_e\|\nu_e\|_1) gives

   \[
   r\cdot f_k(x)\ge (2\kappa H-KLh^H)x^w\ge\kappa Hh^M>0.
   \]

   This estimate is uniform over the entire supplied independent rate box. A raw comparison of unweighted reaction fluxes would not suffice; the candidate retains the projection factors.

3. **Plateau, ties, and measurable time dependence.** Candidate lines 524–682 make the plateau an intersection of finitely many affine halfspaces because one zero-slope label at the class representative supplies a global upper bound on the concave minimum. The plateau lies strictly inside the comparison box by the active zero label and the activity lemma. Below the plateau, no active slope can be neutral on the stoichiometric subspace. The finite-minimum chain rule uses all active labels at ties and is applied almost everywhere, which is the correct Carathéodory interface for merely measurable rates. Compact positive invariance then supplies global continuation.

4. **Nonnested class absorption.** Candidate lines 683–743 start any positive initial point in an initial scale plateau, then use (h_{j+1}=\min(\sqrt{h_j},h_P)). Since the inner comparison box at scale (h_j) lies inside the next, larger box, finite entry at the next scale follows without assuming plateau nesting. Repeated square roots reach the fixed class scale in finitely many steps. The waiting-time bound is finite for each initial point and independent of the rate path, but no useful uniform numerical bound is acquired.

## Strict-collar bridge to N35

Fix the candidate N33 class plateau (Q=K_P\subset c+S\cap\mathbb R^d_{>0}). It is a compact finite-halfspace polytope with nonempty relative interior. Let (F_i\) be a proper relative facet and write its inward gap as (g_i(x)=a_i\cdot x+b_i\ge0). At least one defining inequality supports (F_i); otherwise it is redundant and does not define that facet. Its slope is not in (S^\perp), because a class-constant inequality cannot support a proper relative facet. At every (x\in F_i), that defining label is active and N33's weighted-drift lemma gives

\[
\inf_{x\in F_i,\ k\in[\kappa,K]^R} a_i\cdot f_k(x)\ge\delta_h:=\kappa Hh^M>0.
\]

The facet and rate box are compact and (f_k(x)) is jointly continuous. If no positive collar retained margin (\delta_h/2), a sequence with (g_i(x_n)\downarrow0) and rates (k_n\) would have a convergent subsequence ending in (F_i\times[\kappa,K]^R), contradicting the face bound. Thus an (\ell_i>0) exists with (a_i\cdot f_k(x)\ge\gamma_i:=\delta_h/2\) throughout (Q\cap\{0\le g_i\le\ell_i\}). There are finitely many facets, so the N35 hypotheses hold once a finite-volume model and actual certificate are supplied.

This is an existence bridge, not a constructive consequence of the N33 manuscript. It cannot be used to claim N35 safety from an arbitrary initial molecular count: N35 requires a supplied start inside a *buffered, lattice-compatible* (Q). N33's deterministic entry theorem does not establish stochastic entry into (Q), classwise recurrence, or infinite-time survival.

## Acquisition route and explicit volume dependence

For an already acquired rational (Q) and integer complexes, certificate acquisition can be separated into auditable tasks:

1. compute relative facets and exact rational coordinate upper bounds (linear programs over (Q));
2. propose rational collar widths, and verify strict drift over each rational collar and the supplied rate set, using exact polynomial bounds/subdivision or real-algebraic quantifier elimination;
3. calculate the factorial-error (D_i), projected quadratic bound (B_i), jump size (J_i), total activity (\Lambda), (V_0), (\theta_i), and (c_*\) with rational arithmetic;
4. verify a buffered initial lattice count and conservation/coset compatibility.

For rational polynomial data, strict positivity on a compact rational collar permits a terminating subdivision witness in principle; this does not give a complexity bound. P/N34 labels exhaustion `UNKNOWN`, not a network counterexample. R/N50 reports a conditional all-scale rational search, but the general CAD engine is unimplemented and no general cost bound is supplied. More seriously, N33 provides no practical bound on the number/height of affine labels, its scale (h_P), (H\), its collar widths, or the acquired waiting-time bound. A named network therefore needs its own certificate acquisition and interval ledger; the general permanence proof alone is not an executable compiler.

Given acquired collar constants and buffered gaps (g_i(X^V_0)\ge d_i>0), N35 gives

\[
 p_V(T)=\sum_i e^{-\theta_i Vd_i}+2mV\Lambda T e^{-c_*V}.
\]

For a target risk (\varepsilon\) with (A_V:=\sum_i e^{-\theta_i Vd_i}<\varepsilon\), every

\[
T\le {\varepsilon-A_V\over 2mV\Lambda}\,e^{c_*V}
\]

is certified. At (T=e^{(c_*-\delta)V}\), (\delta>0\), the generator contribution is (2m\Lambda V e^{-\delta V}); the exponential horizon must stay strictly below exponent (c_*). A thinning simulation pays up to (V\Lambda T\) candidate events per replicate. Thus the long-horizon probability statement is not automatically a feasible simulation plan.

The internal facet-local refinement reduces the factorial correction and projected quadratic constants using a separate certified coordinate envelope on each facet collar, while retaining the global (\Lambda). On the stipulated two-species test in `proofs/facet_local_stochastic_safety.md`, it lowers (V_0) from 8 to 3 and raises (c_*) from (29/39600\) to (29/20460\); at (V=20{,}000) and (T=e^{c_*V/4}\), the local bound is (8.224\times10^{-4}), whereas the global-envelope expression is about 734. This is an exact-rational certificate calculation followed by rounded exponentials, not measured biology. Two hundred independent replicates with at least three exits have null binomial tail about (6.472\times10^{-4}) under the local upper bound. The conservative thinning envelope is still about (2.11\times10^8) candidate events per replicate.

## Status, failure conditions, and next gate

- **Internal verdict:** no counterexample found to the N33 proof mechanism in this audit; candidate theorem remains externally unverified.
- **External prior-art boundary:** the primary literature explicitly proves the two-species time-varying endotactic case and states the any-species extension as a conjecture. A correct N33 proof would be a major theorem, so independent proof review is the decisive gate.
- **Finite-volume result:** N35 is a supplied-certificate theorem. It proves only finite-horizon exit control; it does not yield recurrence, a stationary law, or stochastic permanence. The source's N35 proof and a localized re-derivation are separately retained.
- **Concrete failure tests:** a claimed facet certificate is invalid if a single feasible ((x,k)) has (a_i\cdot f_k(x)<\gamma_i); the N35 constants are invalid if any collar coordinate/factorial/quadratic/total-activity upper bound is missed; an empirical exit test is not a theorem falsifier unless volume, initial counts, propensities, rate/control envelope, observation error, and independent-run assumptions all match.
- **Next gate:** have an independent CRNT analyst formally audit the affine lemma and weighted essential-source step; then acquire a small real closed or explicitly budgeted network with matched stoichiometry and flux-rate intervals, generate exact collars, and compare against same-information independent-box and finite-state CTMDP controls. No measured metabolic parameter set or physical validation was obtained here.
