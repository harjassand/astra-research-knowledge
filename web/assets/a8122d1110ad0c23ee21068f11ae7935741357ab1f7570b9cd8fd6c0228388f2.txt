EXACT RANK-ONE CASIMIR PROFILE FOR REGULAR CLASSICAL CARRIERS
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; direct variational derivation.

CLAIM

Let G be compact, connected, and simple; let V_Lambda be an irreducible
highest-weight representation with the invariant generator metric used
in the classical_types packet. Set C2(Lambda)=||Lambda||^2+2<Lambda,rho>
and

  Lambda(1)=min_{rank A<=1, ||A||_HS=1} sum_a ||[T_a,A]||_HS^2.

Then exactly

  Lambda(1)=2(C2(Lambda)-||Lambda||^2)=4<Lambda,rho>.

This is a rank-one profile point for every simple type; it does not give
the full-Q profile.

PROOF

Every HS-unit rank-one matrix is A=|u><v| for unit vectors u,v. For a
Hermitian generator T_a,

  ||[T_a,A]||_HS^2
     =<u,T_a^2u>+<v,T_a^2v>
       -2<u,T_au><v,T_av>.

The two expectations are real. Summing over a and using
sum_a T_a^2=C2(Lambda)I gives

  E(A)=2 C2(Lambda)-2 <mu(u),mu(v)>,
  mu(psi)_a=<psi,T_a psi>.

For every unit psi, ||mu(psi)||<=||Lambda||. Indeed, coadjoint
conjugation can move mu(psi) into a Cartan; there its coordinates are
the expectation of the Cartan generators, hence a convex combination
of weights of V_Lambda. The weight polytope is conv(W Lambda), whose
points have norm at most ||Lambda|| by convexity of norm and Weyl
invariance. Thus
<mu(u),mu(v)><=||Lambda||^2. A highest-weight unit vector has moment
mu=Lambda, so u=v=psi_highest attains equality. This proves the exact
minimum, with no normality restriction missing because every rank-one
matrix has the displayed form.

APPLICATION TO THE CLASSICAL-TYPES THEOREM

For the supplied regular family Lambda=N lambda, lambda in compact K
inside the open chamber,

  Lambda(1)=4N<lambda,rho>.

The positive continuous function <lambda,rho> has a positive minimum
and finite maximum on K. Hence Lambda(1)=Theta_K(N), and the audited
all-Q transfer yields the branch-Q=1 full-state Gibbs risk

  e*_1=Theta_(K,H,h0,h1)(N^-1).

This is an endpoint law for all fixed regular B_l,C_l,D_l cones, not a
rank profile for Q growing with d. The same proof applies to the other
simple types whenever the same Casimir transfer theorem has been
established. The rank-one upper witness is explicit: F is the
highest-weight projector. An efficient implementation still requires
representation/circuit acquisition, which is not costed in the current
packet.

CONSISTENCY CONTROLS

For the standard SU(2) spin j=n/2 convention, C2=j(j+1), ||Lambda||^2=j^2,
so Lambda(1)=2j=n, matching the symmetric-power profile. In the B1 metric
the spin-n/2 energy is unchanged; in C1 it is four times the standard
SU(2) energy, giving Lambda_C1(1)=4n. For regular SU(3),
4<Lambda,rho>=2(a+b), matching the separately recorded exact formula.
These are convention checks, not independent validation of the cited
source profiles.

FALSIFIERS / GATES

The equality would fail if the Casimir and weight inner product use
different metric normalizations, if the moment-vector norm bound is
omitted, or if the target risk charged a different resource class. The
proof assumes the standard weight-polytope theorem for a finite-dimensional
highest-weight module and the supplied Casimir formula. It does not
establish novelty, efficient preparation of the highest-weight state,
or any Q>1 profile.
