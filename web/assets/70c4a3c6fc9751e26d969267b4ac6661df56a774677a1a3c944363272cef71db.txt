REACTION DYNAMICS PROGRAMME — 2026-10-08

Outcome

No externally established field-breaking advance was produced. The programme did produce two exact, useful obstruction results and one independently audited deterministic proof candidate. The most important scientific boundary is that deterministic permanence does not transfer automatically to stochastic recurrence, stochastic tightness across communicating classes, or PDE permanence with unequal diffusion.

The claims must be separated by provenance:

1. OA149 already proves permanence for finite weakly reversible mass-action networks with fixed positive autonomous rates: each positive stoichiometric class has a compact convex forward-invariant eventual set, with entry time depending on the initial point. Its arbitrary-tolerance affine lemma and initial-point trapping mechanism are inherited inputs. The exact supplied source pages are `work/astra_prior/web/pages/d-c1db2d7f9be9f655.html` (Theorem and attribution), `d-d88e4c68667984dc.html` (affine lemma), `d-cb306228f935032e.html` (trapping), and `d-c39cd728321e5cfb.html` (class absorber).

2. Astra P/N33 claims a broader extension: all finite ordinary-endotactic actual power-law diagrams, including real or negative source exponents on the positive orthant, arbitrary measurable time-varying rates in a common compact positive interval, and one common compact convex invariant absorber per positive stoichiometric class. The key claimed extension is the weighted essential-source drift and its use with one finite affine family across rate paths. This is an internally reconstructed candidate with unresolved external mathematical validation and priority. It is not a theorem newly established by this programme. An independent Sol reconstruction found the affine lemma, its compactness uniformization, and cut-gluing sound; that removes an internal audit premise, not the external validation gate.

3. The strongest independently checked stochastic construction is a three-species, three-reaction complex-balanced network with infinitely many finite closed classes inside one positive deterministic class. Let reactions, all with rate constant 1, be
   (0,1,3) -> (0,2,2) -> (1,3,1) -> (0,1,3).
   The vector c=(1,2,2) is complex-balanced. For every n>=1,
   Gamma_n = {(n,1,3), (n,2,2), (n+1,3,1)}
   is a closed irreducible 3-cycle under standard falling-factorial stochastic mass action. Its jump rates are 6, 4, and 6(n+1). The product-Poisson weights on its three states are proportional to (1, 3/2, 1/(n+1)); each directed stationary flux is exactly 6 after the same common factor is removed. Thus the normalized conditional law is stationary and positive recurrent, while A>=n surely. Since all Gamma_n lie in the single linear stoichiometric class B+C=4, these stationary laws are not uniformly tight across stochastic components in that deterministic class. The associated complex-balanced ODE converges to c by the established global-attractor theorem. This refutes deterministic common attraction implying uniform tightness across components, not componentwise recurrence. Some additional boundary singleton classes can occur in the same linear class; the claim only uses the displayed closed family.

4. A separate exact four-reaction weakly reversible deficiency-zero network disproves the direct N76 Foster repair. For 2A <-> A+B, 0 <-> A with rates (1,3,1,1), the N76 factorial potential F(x)=min_available log((x-y)!) has at x_n=(2n,n) the exact generator
   LF(x_n) = 2n(2n-1) log((n+1)/(2n-2)) + 6n^2 log((2n-1)/n) + log(2n-1) - 2n log(2n-2)
   = 2 log(2)n^2 + O(n log n),
   while F(x_n)=3n log n+O(n). Hence no global inequality LF<=aF+b holds. Every positive exponential tilt exp(theta F), theta>0, also has positive generator ratio diverging along this ray. This is a failure of this Lyapunov route, not a counterexample to weakly reversible recurrence: the chain is nonexplosive by a linear total-count comparison, and complex balanced, hence positive recurrent by product form.

5. N35's acquired strict-inward polytope is a finite-horizon controlled safety certificate, not infinite-time survival or recurrence. Integrating its exit estimate gives a mean-exit lower bound. A conditional return-time bound yields a stationary occupation bound, but without the return assumption this does not imply a stationary tail estimate. The exact 2A/2B example exits its protected interval infinitely often almost surely despite exponentially long finite-horizon safety; its exact stationary tail has exponent 2(log 2-H(1/4)) approximately 0.261624, whereas the supplied certificate exponent is 0.015. The N35 guarantee is therefore useful for a specified horizon but cannot be promoted to all-time stochastic permanence.

6. N54's common-scalar-diffusion Neumann PDE barrier is conditional on N33's deterministic affine-family theorem and the precise equal-diffusion/interface assumptions. Reconstruction supports finite-time entry into a uniform positive concentration range for positive profiles whose mean lies in the stoichiometric class. Entry into the pointwise mean class in finite time is false even for A<->B because heat modes decay asymptotically. With unequal diffusion, supplied facet-crossing examples refute automatic product-polytope safety, but do not prove asymptotic nonpermanence. General unequal-diffusion permanence remains open here.

7. N50 is a conditional effective-certificate route: for rational inputs, rational Puiseux all-scale affine families lead to finite real-closed-field obligations and a terminating witness enumeration if the joint-parameter affine reconstruction and N33 proof hold. General CAD is unimplemented, there is no complexity bound, and this is not a practical general recognizer or a two-sided decision procedure.

   An exact N50 witness was independently reconstructed for the unbounded one-species class of 0<->A with rates in [1,4]. Take q=4, z_P=1/4, eta=1/4, c=1, a=1/2, p=0 and F_z(x)=min{0, x-2z^2, (1/2)z^-2-x}; then Q_z=[2z^2,(1/2)z^-2]. For 0<z<=1/4, Q_z lies strictly inside [z^2,z^-2], contains [z,z^-1], and contains c. At its lower active facet, k_birth-k_death*x >= 1-8z^2 >= 1/2; at the upper active facet, k_death*x-k_birth >= (1/2)z^-2-4 >=4. Thus this finite rational object verifies every scale for this unbounded class. It is an exact example, not a general acquired-certificate algorithm.

   There is also an oracle-model limitation: no procedure with only point-evaluation access to an arbitrary positive tolerance E can always terminate with a valid affine family for the general arbitrary-E lemma. An adversary preserves a nonempty interval J of possible t while answering each queried slope r with a positive E(r) smaller than half the distance from r to J. If the procedure halts, choose t in J avoiding its finite output slopes, and lower E at those unqueried slopes so that every output tolerance ball misses t. At X_h(t), some output label is active on a subsequence, contradicting the required approximation. This rules out a black-box synthesis algorithm for arbitrary E; it does not refute N50 because the network tolerance has an explicit finite rational hyperplane structure and N50's rational-family proof uses that structure. The arbitrary-tolerance lemma's oracle lower bound is therefore not a no-go for the network-specific certificate route.

Stochastic theorem baseline and unresolved target

For finite standard stochastic mass-action networks that are complex balanced at c>0, the independent-Poisson product law is invariant. The total stationary jump flux is the finite sum of reaction fluxes sum_r k_r c^{y_r}. The finite-stationary-flux CTMC theorem gives nonexplosion from every state, and restriction to each irreducible closed class gives positive recurrence. This is established prior art, not a new contribution. General weakly reversible positive recurrence beyond complex-balanced and named subclasses remains unresolved in the audited sources. N76 supplies an event-chain upper-tail bound but no negative Foster drift or physical-time holding-time control; the direct factorial-potential repair above fails exactly. N77's cross-class escape is compatible with every individual class being positive recurrent.

The central next mathematical bottleneck is a rate-weighted cycle/tier argument for arbitrary higher molecularity weakly reversible stochastic networks that simultaneously controls event-index drift and physical holding times, or a genuine weakly reversible counterexample to nonexplosion/positive recurrence. Neither deterministic ODE permanence, finite-horizon N35 safety, nor an unweighted factorial event potential closes it. For PDE, the next decisive theorem would require a structural compatibility condition for unequal diffusion that controls reaction facets beyond the local counterexamples.
