GENERAL IRREDUCIBLE GIBBS-ORBIT SELF-SCORE CONVERSE
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-PROVED-CANDIDATE; analytic proof below.

This lemma does not require a Lie group, an adjoint multiplicity formula,
a unital channel, a posterior moment, or recovery of a purification.

Let G be a compact group with Haar probability, U_g a finite-dimensional
irreducible unitary representation on H of dimension d, and B_0 a supplied
Hermitian operator with ||B_0||_op<=b. Define

  B_g=U_g B_0 U_g^dagger,
  tau_(h,g)=exp(h B_g)/Z_h,       W_g=d tau_(h,g),
  0<h0<=h<=h1<infinity,
  E_B(A)=int_G ||[B_g,A]||_HS^2 dg,
  Lambda_B(Q)=min_(rank A<=Q,||A||HS=1) E_B(A).

Any blind encode/decode scheme that passes through a quantum space of
dimension <=Q in each arbitrary classical branch, with no entanglement
assistance or quantum side channel and output H, obeys

  int_G (1/2)||Phi(tau_(h,g))-tau_(h,g)||_1 dg
    >=(h0^2/2)exp(-6h1 b) Lambda_B(Q).                    (1)

The same lower holds for worst-case g error. There is no assumed optimal
rank-energy upper in this lemma.

Proof. Legal composite Kraus operators A_i have rank<=Q and
sum A_i^dagger A_i=I, hence sum ||A_i||HS^2=d. Let M=exp(2h1 b).
The normalizer zeta=Z_h/d lies in [exp(-hb),exp(hb)], so 0<=W_g<=MI.
The effect W_g/M gives an averaged score bounded above by half-trace
error. Irreducibility makes

  int W_g^2 dg=[Tr W_e^2/d]I.

Expanding ||[W_g,A_i]||^2, its two square terms become equal after
averaging and summing, each equal to Tr W_e^2. Only trace preservation
is used; sum A_i A_i^dagger need not equal I. Thus the exact identity is

  int Tr[W_g(tau_(h,g)-Phi(tau_(h,g)))]dg
       =1/(2d)sum_i int||[W_g,A_i]||^2 dg.                (2)

In a B_g eigenbasis the divided difference of exp(hx)/zeta is between
h exp(-2hb) and h exp(2hb). Therefore

  h^2 exp(-4hb) E_B(A)
       <=int||[W_g,A]||^2dg
       <=h^2 exp(4hb) E_B(A).                            (3)

Combine the effect discrepancy (2)/M with the lower in (3), and use
weights ||A_i||^2/d summing to one. Every normalized Kraus operator has
energy at least Lambda_B(Q), giving (1). Continuous classical outcomes
replace sums with integrals of these nonnegative forms; closure yields
the same inequality.

E_B is positive and invariant under conjugation. It may have a larger
kernel than the scalar matrices for an arbitrary compact-group orbit:
for example a commuting covariant family can be recovered classically.
Thus this lemma does NOT infer a Casimir from irreducibility alone.

For a simple compact Lie group whose Lie algebra adjoint is irreducible,
if B_0=d pi(H)/N with fixed H and scalar-normalized orthonormal generators,
Haar covariance of its coefficient vector makes
E_B=(||H_coeff||^2/dim Lie algebra)E_Cas/N^2 exactly. For multiple simple
factors the result is the corresponding positive weighted Dirichlet
form; coercivity requires a nonzero H component on each desired factor.
For SU(3), gamma=2Tr_fund(H^2)/8 and the regular-carrier bound b=B0 in
RESULT.txt makes all constants independent of N,Q.

The lower uses full output density error, allows unlimited classical
labels, and does not say that its h^2 constant is sharp as h tends to 0.
Small exact irreducible finite-Weyl-orbit checks, including nonunital
reset channels, are recorded in verify_regular_su3.py / CHECKS.json.
