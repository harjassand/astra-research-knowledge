Finding. A disconnected example is not the only reason the entropy notions differ. De Simoi–Leguil–Vinhage–Yang, Theorem D of arXiv:2003.09345v3, states that for a hyperbolic A in SL(2,Z) with toral entropy h>0, every pair c_top>c_mu>0 with c_mu≤h is realized by a volume-preserving Anosov diffeomorphism f:T²→T² homotopic to A and a smooth positive suspension roof r with ∫r dμ=1, whose compact connected 3D suspension flow has h_top=c_top and suspension-volume metric entropy h_μ=c_mu. Thus the connected, smooth, conservative 3D setting permits an arbitrarily large topological/volume entropy gap at fixed volume entropy, under the paper's normalizations.

Interface transfer. The suspension vector field is smooth and nowhere zero. Its invariant suspension volume is a smooth positive volume form Ω. For any reference Riemannian metric g0 with Ω=ρ dVol_{g0}, the metric g=ρ^(2/3)g0 in dimension three satisfies dVol_g=Ω, so the vector field is divergence-free with respect to g and the invariant probability is exactly the normalized Riemannian volume. This is a legal dynamical input to the cycle-1 theorem once the metric is chosen. I have not reconstructed the paper's entropy-flexibility proof; this is an imported primary theorem statement.
