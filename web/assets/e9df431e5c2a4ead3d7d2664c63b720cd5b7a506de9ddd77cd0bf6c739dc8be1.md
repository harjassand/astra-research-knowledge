# Distinct-embedding constituents and the number of K3 carriers

Status: classical representation/Hodge-theoretic obstruction, with no new HC/KS premise. It bounds tensor generation by auxiliary K3s and the generic exterior-return support argument. It is not a lower bound on geometric coniveau.

## Rational level-two constituents with several independent labels

Let E be totally real of degree e, let 2<=m<=e, and let V_i=T(S_i)(1), 1<=i<=m, be rank-three E-modules of projective complex K3s. Fix common E-identifications with the same active embedding sigma0. Assume their joint Hodge group is the full independent product G=product_i G_i, G_i=Res_(E/Q) SO(V_i,q_i). No equality of quaternion classes is required. For actual K3s, e<=7.

Let Sigma be the embedding set of E. Choose any Galois orbit O of ordered m-tuples of PAIRWISE DISTINCT embeddings in Sigma^m. Such tuples exist because m<=e. Let H_O be the image in tensor_i V_i of the primitive idempotent of the finite etale algebra E^(tensor m) corresponding to O.

Then H_O is rationally simple, Hg(H_O)=G faithfully, and H_O has normalized Hodge level exactly two. The corresponding substructure of H^(2m)(product_i S_i) has Hodge coniveau exactly m-1.

Proof of descent and simplicity. Over Qbar,

    E^(tensor m) tensor Qbar = product_(Sigma^m) Qbar,
    (H_O)_Qbar = direct-sum_(sigma_1,...,sigma_m in O)
                      tensor_i V_i,sigma_i.

The indicator of O is Galois-invariant and descends to a primitive rational idempotent. Every displayed module is irreducible, the different tuples give inequivalent modules of the original split product, and each occurs with multiplicity one. Any rational subrepresentation selects a Galois-stable subset of O, hence all or none. Thus H_O is rationally simple; no rational selection of an individual complex constituent is asserted.

Faithfulness. Each coordinate projection of O is all of Sigma, by transitivity. If a product of standard SO3 matrices has tensor product equal to the identity, every matrix is scalar. Orthogonality and determinant one in odd dimension force that scalar to be one. Every original simple factor occurs in some tuple, so the entire product action on H_O is faithful.

Level. In each tuple at most one sigma_i equals sigma0. Every other tensor factor has normalized type(0,0). Thus the Hodge weights are contained in {-1,0,1}. Some tuple has sigma_i=sigma0 because the i-th coordinate projection is onto, so weights1 and-1 actually occur. The level is exactly two. Untwisting by m gives the extreme types(m+1,m-1),(m-1,m+1), and hence exact Hodge coniveau m-1.

## Every single K3 has at most two rational simple derived factors

For U=T(S)(1), Zarhin's classification gives either an RM orthogonal group or a CM unitary group. The derived rational Lie algebra is simple except for RM rank four with square discriminant; in that exceptional case it has precisely two rational simple ideals. RM rank-four nonsquare discriminant is rationally simple because Galois acts transitively on the A1 ideals indexed by embeddings of its discriminant field. Centers contribute no semisimple quotient.

Since Hg(H_O) has m rational simple ideals, **m>=3 implies H_O is absent from the entire tensor category of every single K3 U**. This holds even if all original quaternion classes agree. In particular Hom_Hdg(wedge^k U,H_O)=0 for every k, and no finite direct sum of individual one-K3 exterior carriers can surject onto H_O.

For m=3, this is exactly the formerly unproved three-label boundary: three original factors over any shared E with e>=3 have a simple level-two cross term needing codimension-two support in H6, yet no possible single-K3 replacement can carry it.

## Lower bound for r auxiliary K3s

If H_O belongs to the rational tensor category generated by U_1,...,U_r, then

    r >= ceil(m/2).

Let K=Hg(direct-sum_j U_j). Its projections onto the individual Hg(U_j) are surjective, and its derived Lie algebra embeds as a subdirect semisimple algebra in the product of their derived Lie algebras. Those individual algebras together have at most2r rational simple slots.

To justify the subdirect count, decompose Lie(K)^der into rational simple ideals. Each source ideal has nonzero image in some target slot; its image there is an ideal because the entire projection is onto. In a simple target slot that image must be the entire slot. Two different source ideals commute, so they cannot both map onto the same nonabelian simple slot. Assigning each source ideal one nonzero target slot is therefore injective. There are at most2r source ideals. A quotient onto the m-ideal Lie(G) requires at least m, proving m<=2r.

This also handles diagonal identifications among auxiliary groups: they reduce the number of independent source ideals, never increase it. For m=2 and distinct original quaternion classes, ONE_K3_CARRIER_OBSTRUCTION.md gives the stronger r>=2 bound.

For e=6 or7, the bound strengthens to r>=m. An auxiliary K3 whose two rational simple ideals both supply original target ideals would have to be RM rank4 over a field isomorphic to E, by the same centroid/classification argument. Its transcendental rank would then be4e>21, impossible for a projective K3. Thus each auxiliary can supply at most one target ideal. Taking the original m K3s achieves r=m, so this stronger tensor-generator bound is exact.

## Consequence for the imported exterior-return mechanism

In the generic support construction, a product of c exterior returns with total exterior degree s, followed by ordinary-HC transport to H^(2m)(X), has resulting cycle codimension

    dim Z + m - c,

and only the generic support guarantee N^(m-c). To reach the level-two constituent's required N^(m-1) by this count, one needs c<=1. For m>=3, any nonzero carrier assembled from c exterior powers of arbitrary K3s needs c>=ceil(m/2)>=2. For m=2 with distinct quaternion classes it also needs c>=2. Thus this generic one-return support mechanism cannot reach the required codimension in either case.

This does not prohibit additional geometric support, a special composition whose support is smaller, an abelian H2 source, a new mixed return, or GHC itself. The lower bound is on tensor generation by K3 transcendental modules; the support deficit refers only to the advertised generic return accounting.
