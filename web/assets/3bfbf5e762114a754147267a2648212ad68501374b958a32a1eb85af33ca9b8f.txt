SPECTRAL GEOMETRY — CYCLE 2 NEAR-FULL-RANK LEAF: SO(3) WEIGHT PATH
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: exact diagonal-sector formulas; unrestricted-matrix lower bound UNKNOWN.

SCOPE AND CLAIM BOUNDARY

This note studies the diagonal subspace of End(V_N), where V_N is the
spin-N SO(3) representation (degree-N spherical harmonics on S^2), under
the conjugation Casimir. A diagonal rank defect is exactly a set of zero
magnetic-basis coordinates, so the constrained problem becomes a weighted
path Dirichlet eigenvalue problem. The formulas below are exact in this
diagonal sector. The coherent-hole and central-bulk one-hole eigenvalues
are compared exactly/asymptotically. They do not give a lower bound for
arbitrary rank-deficient matrices: a general matrix need not have any zero
diagonal entries in this basis.

1. EXACT WEIGHT-PATH DIRICHLET OPERATOR

Let N≥1, d=2N+1, and use the magnetic basis |N,m>, -N≤m≤N. Relabel the
diagonal entries by s=N-m, so s=0,...,2N. For

  X=diag(x_0,...,x_{2N}),

the J_3 commutator vanishes. The ladder coefficient on the edge s↔s+1 is

  c_s=(s+1)(2N-s),        0≤s≤2N-1,
  c_{-1}=c_{2N}=0.

The J_1 and J_2 commutators together give exactly one copy of each edge
term, hence

  E(X)=sum_{s=0}^{2N-1} c_s |x_{s+1}-x_s|^2.             (1.1)

The associated path Laplacian A_N is

  (A_N x)_s=c_{s-1}(x_s-x_{s-1})+c_s(x_s-x_{s+1}),
  E(X)=x^*A_Nx,       ||X||_HS^2=sum_s |x_s|^2.

Its spectrum is exactly {ell(ell+1): ell=0,...,2N}. Indeed, the diagonal
subspace is the weight-zero subspace for the adjoint J_3 action, and each
spin-ell summand in End(V_N)=sum_{ell=0}^{2N}V_ell contributes one
weight-zero vector. This also fixes the conductance normalization in (1.1).

Let H⊂{0,...,2N} be a set of k forced-zero coordinates, 1≤k≤2N. The
diagonal Dirichlet eigenvalue is exactly

  lambda_D(H)=min_{x≠0, x_h=0 (h∈H)} x^*A_Nx / sum_s |x_s|^2
             =lambda_min(A_N[H^c,H^c]).                    (1.2)

Removing H splits the path into its nonempty consecutive intervals I=[a,b].
The principal matrix on I has diagonal entries c_{s-1}+c_s and adjacent
off-diagonal entries -c_s. Therefore

  lambda_D(H)=min_{I=[a,b]} lambda_min(B_[a,b]),

where B_[a,b] is that tridiagonal block. Equivalently, its eigenvalues are
the roots of the determinant recurrence

  p_{a-1}(z)=1,
  p_a(z)=c_{a-1}+c_a-z,
  p_j(z)=(c_{j-1}+c_j-z)p_{j-1}(z)-c_{j-1}^2p_{j-2}(z),
       a<j≤b,

and lambda_min(B_[a,b]) is the smallest root p_b(z)=0. This is an exact
finite-N computation for any prescribed hole pattern. The ground vector
of each block is strictly positive. If rank is required to be exactly
d-k rather than at most d-k, the minimum over blocks can be an infimum
when its eigenvector vanishes on other blocks; arbitrarily small nonzero
entries on those blocks approach the same value.

2. ONE COHERENT HOLE: EXACT SECULAR EQUATION AND CAPACITY SCALE

A magnetic endpoint s=0 (m=N) or s=2N (m=-N) is a spin-coherent basis
state. By reflection it suffices to force x_0=0. Let lambda_coh(N) be the
smallest eigenvalue of A_N[1:2N,1:2N]. Interlacing gives

  0 < lambda_coh(N) < 2.

Let e_0 be the s=0 coordinate vector, and let w_ell be its spectral weight
in the ell sector of A_N. The extremal Clebsch–Gordan coefficient gives

  w_ell=(2ell+1) ( (2N)! )^2 /
                  ((2N-ell)!(2N+ell+1)!),   0≤ell≤2N,
  w_0=1/d.

The principal-minor secular equation is

  sum_{ell=0}^{2N} w_ell/(ell(ell+1)-lambda)=0,
       0<lambda<2.                                         (2.1)

The zero-mode-removed Green value

  S_N=sum_{ell=1}^{2N} w_ell/[ell(ell+1)]
      =<e_0,A_N^+e_0>
      =[H_{2N}-2N/(2N+1)]/(2N+1),                          (2.2)

where H_q=sum_{r=1}^q 1/r. To check (2.2) directly, solve
A_N u=e_0-d^{-1}1 with 1^*u=0. The edge flux is
c_s(u_s-u_{s+1})=1-(s+1)/d, hence
u_s=u_0-H_s/d, and averaging u gives the displayed u_0=S_N.

Write

  a_N=w_0/S_N=1/[H_{2N}-2N/(2N+1)].

At the root of (2.1), w_0/lambda=sum_{ell≥1}w_ell/(ell(ell+1)-lambda).
Since ell(ell+1)≥2, the right side lies between S_N and
S_N/(1-lambda/2). Consequently

  a_N/(1+a_N/2) ≤ lambda_coh(N) ≤ a_N,
  lambda_coh(N)=(1+o(1))/log(2N+1).                        (2.3)

Thus the normalized spectral capacity, defined here as

  Cap_spec(H)=d lambda_D(H)

for counting measure on the diagonal coordinates, satisfies

  Cap_spec({coherent endpoint}) ~ (2N+1)/log(2N+1).

The underlying two-terminal path resistance from s=0 to the bulk midpoint
s=N is also explicit:

  R(0,N)=sum_{s=0}^{N-1}1/c_s=H_{2N}/(2N+1),
  R(0,N)^(-1)~2N/log N.                                   (2.4)

This capacity scale is why a coherent endpoint hole is much cheaper than
a bulk hole in the diagonal Dirichlet problem.

3. ONE CENTRAL BULK HOLE: EXACT EIGENVALUE

Take the central magnetic coordinate m=0, equivalently s=N, and force
x_N=0. The path splits into [0,N-1] and [N+1,2N], which are reflections
of one another. On the left block, x_s=N-s is strictly positive and is
the restriction of the full-path eigenvector m↦m, whose Casimir eigenvalue
is 2. It obeys the Dirichlet value x_N=0, so it is a block eigenvector
with eigenvalue 2. Positivity of this eigenvector makes it the block's
lowest eigenvalue. Reflection gives the same result on the other block.
Therefore

  lambda_D({s=N})=2   exactly for every N≥1.                 (3.1)

The normalized spectral capacity is 2(2N+1)=Theta(N), compared with
Theta(N/log N) for a coherent endpoint hole. The coherent hole therefore
has a factor Theta(log N) smaller diagonal Dirichlet eigenvalue.

For scale comparison, the conductance from the central hole to a
macroscopic shell a distance Theta(N) away is Theta(N): the path has
Theta(N) edges, each with conductance Theta(N^2) in any fixed central
fraction. The coherent endpoint-to-midpoint path resistance (2.4) is
Theta(log N/N), giving capacity Theta(N/log N). A resistance to a distant
endpoint alone is not a universal proxy for a Dirichlet eigenvalue; the
mass profile of the minimizing vector matters, as the exact bulk value
(3.1) illustrates.

4. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE

For diagonal rank d-k matrices, the set H of zero coordinates has size k,
and (1.2) is the exact constrained Rayleigh eigenvalue for that fixed H.
Minimizing over hole locations gives the diagonal-sector infimum
min_{|H|=k} lambda_D(H). In particular, for k=1, a rank-(d-1) diagonal
matrix with a coherent zero can attain lambda_coh(N)=Theta(1/log N),
whereas forcing the central bulk coordinate to zero gives eigenvalue 2.

These are not lower bounds for arbitrary matrices of rank d-k. A
rank-deficient matrix need not be diagonal and its kernel need not remove
any magnetic-basis coordinate. The coherent-hole construction is an
admissible unrestricted rank-(d-1) witness, so it gives an upper bound
lambda_min over all matrices of rank at most d-1. No matching lower bound
for the unrestricted matrix problem, and no conclusion for general
off-diagonal rank defects, is proved here.
