2. PROJECTOR SUPPORT, LOCALITY AND THE SMALL-INTEGER TRANSITION

For q constituents, keep row variables x_1,...,x_q and complement the column
variables y_1,...,y_q. The symmetric projector polynomial is
  p_q = sum_r e_r(x)e_(q-r)(y) / binom(q,r).
Its support is EVERY q-subset of the 2q coordinates. Its coefficient depends
only on the selected row count r; hence it has an implicit O(q)-coordinate
description, positive local support seeds under consistent pins, and coefficient
ratio <=binom(q,floor(q/2))<=2^q. Computing a global coefficient uses an explicit
binomial evaluation plus polynomially many rational multiplications; this is
charged in acquisition/oracle cost rather than hidden in an expanded table.

The all-q LC argument is attributed to s01 and audited by l03: after q-2
derivatives selecting a rows and b columns (a+b=q-2), the Hessian has negative
row/column sum-zero eigenvalues. The remaining two-dimensional quotient has
determinant zero and positive trace, by the exact consecutive-binomial ratio.
Uniform-matroid support is M-convex. Branden--Huh's homogeneous signature
characterization establishes the required Lorentzian/complete-LC property.

For an Eq19 mu update, deleting u leaves q-1 selected projector coordinates.
Let r be the remaining row count, so 0<=r<q. A row reinsertion has coefficient
  1/binom(q,r+1),
while a column reinsertion has coefficient
  1/binom(q,r).
All other local factors cancel in the conditional ratio, since degrees of
the disjoint factors are fixed. The common positive scale cancels too:
  [row coefficient] : [column coefficient] = (r+1) : (q-r).
Thus the exact row and column class masses are
  (r+1) sum_(eligible rows v) z_v,
  (q-r) sum_(eligible cols v) z_v.
The class coin uses integers of O(log q) bits; it does not compute a binomial
or a q-bit projector coefficient at each production step. Choose within a
class by an exact weighted tree. At unit fields with no pins the two class
masses are equal, since their eligible counts are q-r and r+1 respectively.
Pins can change the counts, but not the two class coefficients.

The trees store only unselected eligible coordinates. The deleted u is a
separate reinsertion option until all coins resolve, so no temporary mutation
survives a tape abort. After a successful exchange exactly two leaves and the
row count change. Field changes update a single eligible leaf. Production
updates use O(log q)<=O(log N) rational operations and O(q) leaves. Tree-sum
operand height includes all rational field denominators; arbitrary fields
are charged, never treated as fixed-precision machine arithmetic.

The nu factor consists of disjoint rank-one wire pairs and a dummy uniform
quota. Its existing exact local/tree transition remains valid. Choose one
of the N present copies uniformly, attempt deletion/reinsertion with probability
1/2 if the other set contains u and t/2 otherwise. Reinsertion contains no
additional t_v factor. Consequently the full lazy kernel is precisely
  P=I-(t/(2N))L_q,
with gap >=t/(2N), conditional on the imported gap-one theorem. This general
gap is NOT inferred from the finite tests.

3. POSITIVE TAYLOR GATES AND THE PHYSICAL PROJECTED CIRCUIT

Use p_r(x)=sum_(k=0)^r x^k/k!, r>=2, with rational step s. Edge eigenvalues
are low=alpha-gamma, mid=3alpha+gamma, high=5alpha-gamma, ordered and
nonnegative inside alpha>=|gamma|. For any polynomial with nonnegative
coefficients and constant>=1, f(low)<=f(mid)<=f(high). Therefore the
six-vertex table a=f(mid), b=(f(high)+f(low))/2, c=(f(high)-f(low))/2 satisfies
all triangle inequalities. A field generator is PSD and entrywise nonnegative;
f(A)>=I and is entrywise nonnegative. Its determinant is at least one, so
the existing rational two-dummy homogeneous lift remains LC. This is the
positive-functional-calculus argument already established in our revision001;
the phase2 compiler now accepts Taylor degrees2,3,4 (admission up to12).

For spins S_v=q_v/2, use J_v^a=(1/2)sum constituent Pauli operators. An edge
alpha J_u.J_v with admitted XX/YY/ZZ anisotropy embeds into q_u*q_v local
terms with coefficients alpha/4,gamma/4. Fields embed with b/2,c/2. The
shifted sum Khat is PSD, commutes with the site-symmetric projector Pi, and
restricts to Kphys=Hphys+C I. Charge numerical Q=sum q_v and embedded term
count p explicitly. Constituents/terms are admitted BEFORE their expansion.

With h=beta/m and s=h/2, put V=prod_i p_r(s A_i), B0=V V*, and
  Bphys=(Pi B0 Pi)|RanPi.
Every factor is >=I, so V has least singular value>=1 and B0>=I; compression
gives Bphys>=I. Because Pi commutes with Khat, an absolute one-layer operator
estimate ||B0-exp(hKhat)||<=d compresses to the same estimate on RanPi.
For X,Y>=I the integral resolvent identity gives
  ||log X-log Y||<=||X-Y||.
Hence ||m log Bphys-beta Kphys||<=m d. No operator monotonicity of exp and
no commutation of individual gates with Pi is assumed. Individual local
products generally DO NOT commute with Pi.

The trace circuit is (B0 Pi)^m: adjacent idempotent projector factors are
collapsed. Its trace equals Tr_phys(Bphys^m). It has
  N=8mp+2mQ
coordinates, mu and nu degree N/2, explicit homogeneous LC factors, supported
seeds and rational range bound. Projectors are inserted EVERY layer, not just
at the final boundary. The circuit compiler assigns O(N) wires and factors
and retains implicit projector factors; there is no 2^q local expansion.

IMPORTANT READOUT REPAIR: the first physical trace cut of (B0 Pi)^m does not
generally have constituent diagonal equal to the embedded Bphys^m diagonal.
The exact small comparator exhibits unequal diagonal values. Thus individual
constituent bits returned by the trace sampler are auxiliary. The physical
site Sz output is nevertheless correct: its charge projector E commutes
with Pi, and cyclic trace/idempotence give
  Tr(E (B0 Pi)^m)=Tr(E (Pi B0 Pi)^m).
Reading each site's constituent Hamming weight therefore samples its physical
Sz law. General constituent observables cannot use this readout without an
additional projector/observable argument. Our docstring and report restrict
the claimed physical output to site Sz charges.

4. ACQUIRING CUBIC AND QUARTIC DEFECTS WITHOUT DENSE MATRICES

Expand the positive palindromic product as a polynomial in s:
  B_r(s)=sum_k s^k C_k,
and write S=2sum_i A_i. For all r>=2, C0=I,C1=S,C2=S^2/2. Acquire
  R_k=C_k-S^k/k!, k=3,...,K,
by exact rational complex Pauli dictionary arithmetic; K is admitted as3 or4.
Use Hermitian Pauli basis P(x,z)=i^popcount(x&z) X^x Z^z. All acquired R_k
are Hermitian; their coefficients are real. The l1 bound
  L_k=sum_P |coefficient_P(R_k)|
satisfies ||R_k||<=L_k because each Pauli has norm1. No 2^Q matrix is built.
Dictionary multiplications, exact Pauli-pair products and peak keys are recorded.
For fixed K, support/cost is polynomial in embedded p and Q; a conservative
bound is O(p^(K+1)) rational operations with O(p^K) words, plus operand-bit
arithmetic. Admission defaults to12 terms/4096 keys and is not free acquisition.

Let D=2C, tau=beta D, delta=tau/m. The scalar coefficients of the product
are dominated by exp(delta), since every positive truncated Taylor series is
coefficientwise bounded by its exponential. The exponential comparison has
the same degree bound. For delta<K+2 the elementary tail ratio yields
  sum_(k>=K+1) delta^k/k!
      <= delta^(K+1) / [(K+1)! (1-delta/(K+2))].
Therefore the acquired all-size operator certificate is
  kappa <= sum_(k=3)^K beta^k L_k/[2^k m^(k-1)]
           +2 tau^(K+1)/[(K+1)! m^K (1-tau/((K+2)m))].
It survives the physical compression by the absolute-error argument above.
All arithmetic is rational. A monotone integer binary search chooses the
smallest admitted m with this displayed bound <=epsilon/4; there is no guessed
floating tolerance or dense eigensolver oracle.

Increasing r from2 to3 removes the LOCAL cubic Taylor defect. It leaves the
generic splitting cubic. Exact three-qubit checks give L3=18995/324 for r2
and L3=1453/81>0 for both r3 and r4. Thus this refinement does not establish
global order>2. It improves acquired constants/remainders and can choose a
shorter legal circuit for particular instances. No field-breaking claim follows.

5. COMPLETE CAPPED DIRECT Sz SAMPLER

At c_v=0 every positive edge/field Taylor gate and p_q are complement
invariant. The nu half-size quotas are also invariant. Our frozen symmetry
argument plus the imported all-field/vector-penalty gap gives, at z=1 and
t=1/(256N^2), hard probability >=3/4. More generally the rational complement
ratio Rflip acquired from local tables is charged NUMERICALLY, and
t=1/(256N^2 Rflip) retains the same bound. Projectors contribute ratio1.
This optional bounded-bias interface does not make arbitrary longitudinal
bias efficient in its binary encoding.

Let R be the product coefficient range and
  H=2N+ceil(log2 R)+(N/2)ceil(log2(1/t)).
The original exact mixing planner uses a sufficient
  J=ceil[(2N/t)(H+ceil(log2(1/theta))+1)]
steps per soft draw. With rtries=ceil(log2(4/eta)), theta=eta/(4rtries),
independent fresh soft chains are accepted on S disjoint T. At the hard event
the soft overlap weight is one and its normalized law is the required positive
trace configuration. Failure after rtries has probability <=4^-rtries.
Coupling the approximate soft draws costs <=rtries*theta; fair-coin truncation
is budgeted separately; fixed fallbacks give total TV <=eta. Generator error
kappa adds at most tanh(kappa/2) to Gibbs measurement TV.

Each step has <=2ceil(log2 N)+3 nontrivial Bernoulli choices, covering copy,
attempt, projector class, reinsertion-u and tree decisions. If A bounds the
total choices, bits per choice B=ceil(log2(4A/eta)) bounds total abort by
A*2^-B<=eta/4. All loops and bit tapes have deterministic caps. Planning
and resource admission occur before random work. Seeded PRNG execution is a
replay fixture, not certification of independent fair entropy.

Arithmetic direct-sampler work is O(N^3 Rflip (H+log(rtries/eta)) logN
log(1/eta)), plus explicitly charged compiler/acquisition and operand costs.
It avoids count self-reduction and epsilon^-2 empirical count batches; it does
not avoid the very conservative N/t mixing factor. General partition counting
still requires the separate majority-path wrapper, unexecuted at useful size.
