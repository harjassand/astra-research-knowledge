1. INTERFACE AND THEOREM

Take an open spin-1/2 chain on sites {1,...,L-1}, J>0, with

  N=sum_i n_i,  W=sum_{i=1}^{L-2} n_i n_{i+1},
  K_L=-J sum_i (X_i X_{i+1}+Y_i Y_{i+1})+4J N,
  H_L(V)=K_L+V W.

The grand-canonical Gibbs state is

  rho_{L,V}(tau,lambda) = Z_{L,V}^{-1}
       exp{-tau L^2[H_L(V)-lambda N/L^2]},
  beta=tau L^2.

Fix compact intervals tau in [tau_-,tau_+] with tau_->0,
lambda in [-B,B], and V in a compact interval K contained in (-4J,infinity).
There is one common, parameter-independent, exact isometric embedding of
the finite hard-core Fock spaces into the direct sum of symmetric continuum
sector spaces such that, uniformly in (tau,lambda,V) on those intervals,

  I_L rho_{L,V} I_L^* -> rho_TG(tau,lambda) in trace norm,

where rho_TG is the grand-canonical Tonks-Girardeau state on (0,1). Its
q-particle sector is unitarily equivalent to q free Dirichlet fermions:

  Z_TG = product_{k>=1}(1+exp(tau(lambda-2J pi^2 k^2))).

Moreover, uniformly on the same parameter sets,

  D(rho_{L,V} || rho_{L,0}) -> 0.

More generally, the trace-norm conclusion still holds for a sequence of
couplings V_L>-4J whenever

  g_L := L(4J+V_L) -> infinity,

provided the sequence stays in a fixed bounded interval and tau,lambda stay
in the compact sets above. This includes fixed subcritical attraction
-4J<V<0 and approaches to -4J slower than 1/L. The proof does not establish
relative-entropy convergence for every such shrinking-gap sequence; see §5.

The positive finite-g critical window V_L=-4J+g/L is the distinct
Lieb-Liniger limit already banked in the attractive-critical packet:
finite g>0 gives a finite contact form, not the Tonks form. Thus the
condition g_L->infinity is the exact boundary-form criterion in this
proof, not a claim that the lattice thermodynamics is uniform down to
g_L=0.

2. EXACT CONFIGURATION FORM

In the q-particle sector let X={x_1<...<x_q} and let G_{L,q} join two
configurations when one particle makes a permitted nearest-neighbor hop.
Put b(X)=1_{1 in X}+1_{L-1 in X} and
w(X)=|{i:x_{i+1}=x_i+1}|. Direct expansion of the matrix elements gives

  <psi,K_L psi>
    =2J sum_{unordered X~Y}|psi(X)-psi(Y)|^2
       +2J sum_X b(X)|psi(X)|^2+4J sum_X w(X)|psi(X)|^2.

Indeed deg_G(X)=2q-b(X)-2w(X); each edge contributes diagonal 2J at
each endpoint and off-diagonal -2J. Hence

  Hcrit:=K_L-4J W >=0,
  <psi,Hcrit psi>=2J sum_{X~Y}|psi(X)-psi(Y)|^2
                      +2J sum_X b(X)|psi(X)|^2,
  H_L(V)=Hcrit+delta W,  delta=4J+V.

This identity fixes the sign and the boundary normalization. It is the
mechanism: every delta>0 penalizes a collision layer, while delta=0 leaves
the reflecting (bosonic) collision boundary.

3. FIXED-SECTOR FORM LIMIT, INCLUDING DIVERGING g_L

Here are the common embeddings and the load-bearing estimates. Fix q. In
an ordered configuration put y_i=x_i-i+1, so
1<=y_1<=...<=y_q<=n=L-q. If y has tie multiplicities r_v, its labeled
orbit has w(y)=q!/prod_v r_v! elements. Define the exact polar isometry

  U_q psi(z)=psi(sort z)/sqrt(w(sort z)),
       z in {1,...,n}^q.

Let h=1/(n+1). On the centered product cell of side h about hz put the
constant h^(-q/2)U_q psi(z), and put zero outside these cells. This map
I_{L,q} is an exact isometry into the symmetric subspace of
L^2((0,1)^q); outer half-strips are zero. It does not depend on V.

For a lower bound use K_q psi(z)=psi(sort z)/sqrt(q!) and set
u_z=h^(-q/2)K_q psi(z), zero at ghost nodes with a coordinate 0 or n+1.
Let I_h u be tensor multilinear interpolation and

  E_h(u)=h^(q-2) sum_{grid edges {z,z+e_i}} |u(z+e_i)-u(z)|^2.

Counting labeled preimages of each legal exclusion hop gives the exact
form comparison

  2J(Lh)^2 E_h(u) <= L^2 <psi,Hcrit psi>.              (3.1)

For fixed q, Lh->1. Convexity of the interpolation weights and a local
coordinate-path estimate give

  integral |grad I_hu|^2 <= E_h(u),
  ||I_hu-U_hu||_2^2 <= q 3^q h^2 E_h(u),               (3.2)

where U_hu is the nearest-node step function. The exact isometric cell
map and K_q satisfy

  ||I_{L,q}psi-U_hu||_2^2 <= <psi,W psi>.              (3.3)

The reason is that their squared discrepancy at a tied tuple is at most
its probability mass, and they agree away from ties.

For each pair i<j let D_{h,ij}u be the collapsed-node step function at
z_i=z_j. Its exact norm is

  ||D_{h,ij}u||_2^2=h^(q-1)sum_{z_i=z_j}|u_z|^2.

The same local-path estimate on the collapsed grid gives

  ||D_{h,ij}u-Tr_{ij}(I_hu)||_2^2
       <= q 3^q h E_h(u).                              (3.4)

The ordinary H^1 trace on a fixed diagonal is compact into its L^2
surface measure; e.g. on the cube its squared norm is bounded by
4||F||||grad F||. Thus bounded E_h gives precompact bulk embeddings and
convergent diagonal traces.

The exact tie-multiplicity inequality is also needed. Write

  W(y)=sum_v(r_v-1), P(y)=sum_v binom(r_v,2),
  w(y)=q!/prod_v r_v!.

Since prod r_v! >= r_v for every v,

  q! W(y) >= 2 w(y) P(y).

Grouping labeled pair-diagonal nodes over each orbit consequently gives

  g_L L <psi,W psi>
       >= 2g_L(Lh) sum_{i<j}||D_{h,ij}u||_2^2.        (3.5)

This includes triple and higher ties; no multiplicity-one assumption is
made.

Now take any L_a->infinity and g_{L_a}->infinity, with
V_{L_a}=-4J+g_{L_a}/L_a. If
<psi_a,L_a^2 H_L(V_L)psi_a> is bounded, (3.1)-(3.2) give an H^1_0
bounded interpolant and a strongly convergent subsequence. Equation (3.5)
forces every diagonal D_{h,ij} norm to zero. Equations (3.3)-(3.4) show
the exact embedded vectors have the same limit F and Tr_{ij}F=0. Weak
lower semicontinuity in (3.1) yields

  2J integral |grad F|^2
      <= liminf <psi_a,L_a^2 H_L(V_L)psi_a>.           (3.6)

The limiting form domain is

  D_q={F in H^1_0((0,1)^q): F symmetric,
                   Tr_{ij}F=0 for all i<j},
  a_q(F)=2J integral |grad F|^2.

For recovery, first take a symmetric smooth function supported a positive
distance from all outer and collision faces. Sample it at the gap nodes
y_i; contact energy is exactly zero for all sufficiently fine meshes,
and the graph sum is the usual finite-difference Riemann sum. Such
functions are dense in D_q in H^1: restrict to one ordered chamber,
approximate in H^1_0 by C_c^infinity, then symmetrize over chambers.
The trace theorem and a diagonal approximation give recovery for all of
D_q. The construction is independent of g_L, so the form convergence is
uniform over every family with inf g_L->infinity.

The variational resolvent minimizers, (3.6), recovery and collective
compactness yield norm-resolvent convergence on the common continuum
Hilbert space (finite resolvents are extended by zero outside the exact
embedded range). A contradiction subsequence proves the same uniformly
over parameter families with inf g_L->infinity. Functional calculus gives
operator-norm heat convergence. To upgrade to trace norm, order the
sector eigenvalues a_{L,1}<=a_{L,2}<=... . For every K,

  sum_{j>K} exp(-tau a_{L,j})
    <= exp(-tau a_{L,K+1}/2) Tr exp(-tau A_L/2).

The half-temperature trace on the right is bounded uniformly in L by
(4.2) on a fixed V compact, or by (4.4) for the moving-g_L regime.
Norm-resolvent/min-max convergence gives a_{L,K+1}->a_{K+1}, and
a_{K+1}->infinity as K->infinity. Thus heat tails vanish after first
choosing K and then L. The first K spectral clusters converge in norm,
which gives trace-norm convergence in the fixed sector.

Finally multiply a chamber function by the sign of the coordinate
permutation. Since its collision trace is zero, this sign extension is an
H^1 isometry from D_q onto antisymmetric H^1_0((0,1)^q). The limiting
operator is therefore the free-fermion q-sector Hamiltonian, with
eigenvalues

  2J pi^2 sum_{j=1}^q k_j^2,   1<=k_1<...<k_q.

This is the Tonks-Girardeau map at the form level. Girardeau's mapping is
the established continuum identification; it is not used in place of
the discrete contact-layer proof.

4. WHOLE-FOCK TAIL AND TRACE-NORM CONCLUSION

For a fixed compact V interval K subset (-4J,infinity), let
delta_*=min_{V in K}(4J+V)>0 and c=min(1,delta_*/(4J)). Since
Hcrit=K_L-4JW>=0,

  H_L(V)>=c K_L,   V in K.                            (4.1)

For V<=0 this follows from
H_L(V)=c_V K_L+(1-c_V)Hcrit with c_V=(4J+V)/(4J); for V>=0 it follows
from H_L(V)=K_L+VW>=K_L. The one-particle energies of K_L are

  epsilon_{k,L}=4J(1-cos(pi k/L)), 1<=k<L,
  L^2 epsilon_{k,L}>=8J k^2.

The last inequality follows from sin(x/2)>=x/pi on [0,pi]. Thus, for
tau in [tau_-,tau_+] and |lambda|<=B, the full grand trace is bounded by

  Z_{L,V} <= product_{k>=1}
       (1+exp(tau_+ B-8J c tau_- k^2)) < infinity,    (4.2)

uniformly in L,V. For t>1 its q-tail is at most

  t^(-Q) product_{k>=1}
       (1+t exp(tau_+B-8Jc tau_- k^2)),                (4.3)

for q>Q. The vacuum gives Z>=1. This proves uniform Fock tightness and
uniform sector heat tails. Combine the fixed-q trace-norm convergence
with (4.3), then normalize; this proves the theorem uniformly on K.

The trace theorem extends to moving couplings with g_L=L(4J+V_L)->infty,
even if delta_L->0. The fixed-q proof only needs g_L->infinity. To control
the Fock tail in this wider regime, use the explicit finite-chain lemma
already proved at outputs/research/sol_attractive_critical/proof.txt,
Theorem (1.2)-(1.3): for every fixed g_*>0 and all g>=g_*, it gives

  Z_{L,q}(lambda,g) <= A^q exp(-tau c_* q^2),
  c_*=min(g_*/96,J/72),
  A=e C exp(tau(B+c_*)),
  C=1+2 sum_{k>=1}exp(-4J tau k^2),                  (4.4)

with no upper bound on g. Since g_L->infinity, eventually g_L>=g_*.
Equation (4.4) gives an L-independent Gaussian q-tail. This imported lemma
is a named proof dependency, not an inference from fixed-q convergence.

5. RELATIVE ENTROPY AND THE THRESHOLD BOUNDARY

Let F_L(V)=log Z_{L,V}(tau,lambda). The trace theorem implies
F_L(V)->F_infinity=log Z_TG uniformly on every compact subinterval of
(-4J,infinity); the limit is independent of V. F_L is convex and
differentiable, with

  F_L'(V)=-tau L^2 Tr(rho_{L,V} W).

For any target compact K subset (-4J,infinity), choose a larger compact
interval K' subset (-4J,infinity) containing K and 0 in its interior,
with a fixed positive distance from K union {0} to the endpoints. Convex
secant bounds on F_L at fixed step a>0 then give

  sup_{V in K union {0}} |F_L'(V)| ->0.

The exact Gibbs identity is

  D(rho_{L,V}||rho_{L,0})
       = V F_L'(V)+F_L(0)-F_L(V).                     (5.1)

Uniform convergence of F_L and F_L' proves uniform relative-entropy
convergence to zero. Pinsker gives
||rho_{L,V}-rho_{L,0}||_1 <= sqrt(2D). Both states also converge to the
same rho_TG in the common embedding.

The buffer away from -4J is essential to this proof of the KL statement.
In fact, KL convergence fails for some sequences with g_L->infinity and
V_L->-4J, despite trace-norm convergence to rho_TG. Here is an exact
diagonal counterexample. Set lambda=0 and

  f_L(g)=log Tr exp[-tau L^2 Hcrit-tau g L W],
  f_infinity(g)=log Z_LL(g),  f_TG=log Z_TG.

For each finite g>0, the already proved fixed-g continuum limit gives
f_L(g)->f_infinity(g). The repulsive Lieb-Liniger contact form is strictly
positive on the two-particle ground state, so f_infinity is strictly
decreasing; equivalently, the positive-contact interaction strictly raises
the two-particle eigenvalues and hence strictly lowers that sector's heat
trace (thermal_eb.txt §5). To see strictness directly, the q=2 ground
state at finite g is nonnegative and has positive overlap with the free
ground state. If its diagonal trace were zero, its weak eigen-equation
would reduce to the free one, forcing it to be the free ground state,
which has nonzero diagonal trace. Thus its contact is positive, and the
variational principle strictly raises its energy when g increases.
Monotone form convergence as g->infinity, plus
the all-sector tail bound (4.4), gives f_infinity(g)->f_TG. Choose any
g_j->infinity with f_infinity(g_j)-f_TG<=1/j and put

  d_j=f_infinity(g_j)-f_infinity(g_j+1)>0.

By fixed-g partition convergence and free sine-mode convergence, choose
increasing thresholds L_j so that for every L>=L_j,

  f_L(g_j)-f_L(g_j+1)>=d_j/2,
  |f_L(g_j)-f_infinity(g_j)|<=1/j,
  |f_L(4JL)-f_TG|<=1/j.

Also require L_j d_j>=j, 4J L_j>=2g_j, and g_j/L_j<=1/j; all are
possible since d_j>0 and thresholds can be increased. For
L_j<=L<L_{j+1}, set g_L=g_j and V_L=-4J+g_j/L. Then g_L->infinity,
g_L/L->0, and our trace theorem gives

  I_L rho_{L,V_L} I_L^* -> rho_TG in trace norm.

Convexity of f_L and the forward secant give
-f_L'(g_j)>=f_L(g_j)-f_L(g_j+1)>=d_j/2. Since
f_L'(g)=-tau L Tr(rho_{L,g}W), the exact Gibbs identity is

  D(rho_{L,V_L}||rho_{L,0})
    = V_L L f_L'(g_j)+f_L(4JL)-f_L(g_j).

Here rho_{L,0} denotes physical coupling V=0, equivalently the parameter
g=4JL in f_L; it does not denote the resonant parameter g=0. Also
V_L L=-4JL+g_j and 4JL-g_j>=2JL. Therefore throughout the jth
block,

  D(rho_{L,V_L}||rho_{L,0}) >= J L d_j-3/j >= Jj-3/j -> infinity.

Thus trace-norm closeness to the free state does not control this
relative entropy in the moving threshold window. The counterexample is
existential: without quantitative finite-g convergence thresholds it is
not a finite-bit schedule. It falsifies a uniform KL extension, not the
fixed-gap KL theorem. The sharper condition on g_L needed to recover KL
convergence is not identified here.

The finite positive window g_L->g in (0,infinity) is instead the
finite-coupling Lieb-Liniger limit from
outputs/research/sol_attractive_critical/continuum_limit.txt, with an
existing noncommutativity/entanglement-breaking obstruction in
outputs/research/sol_attractive_critical/eb_obstruction.txt. Hence an
unknown V in a fixed compact K away from -4J can be ignored asymptotically
for the state itself and for relative entropy; an unknown V allowed to
approach the resonance at rate 1/L cannot be replaced by the free Gibbs
state in general.

6. COSTS, FALSIFIERS, AND SCOPE

This is a convergence theorem, not a finite-L sampler or finite-bit
certificate. The continuum target can be sampled in mode space by
independent Bernoulli occupations

  p_k=(1+exp(tau(2J pi^2 k^2-lambda)))^(-1).

Its occupation tail after mode K is bounded by
sum_{k>K} exp(tau_+B-2J pi^2 tau_- k^2); thus K=O(sqrt(log(1/epsilon)))
at fixed J,tau_-,B suffices for an epsilon cutoff. This charges O(K)
Bernoulli draws and evaluations, but no spatial-register synthesis,
finite-L error tolerance, or finite-chain Gibbs-preparation claim is
made. Finite L energies use the exact sine values above. For rational
input parameters, precision must be chosen from the desired mode-logit
error; this proof supplies no convergence modulus or condition estimate
uniform as the gap to -4J closes. The constants in (4.2) deteriorate as
c->0; the moving-g_L trace extension uses (4.4) with fixed g_*, not an
unknown exact g_L.

The positive physical energy has a uniform thermal bound: writing
A_L=L^2 H_L(V)>=0 and using x e^(-tau x)<=2(e tau)^(-1)e^(-tau x/2),

  Tr(rho_{L,V} A_L)
    <=2/(e tau) Z_{L,V}(tau/2,2lambda),

since Z_{L,V}(tau,lambda)>=1. The right side is uniformly bounded by
(4.2) with tau/2 and chemical parameter 2lambda. Hence
Tr(rho H_L(V))=O(L^-2), uniformly on the fixed compact parameter set.
This bound controls expected energy only; the proof gives no energy-norm
state approximation.

For an epsilon-accurate mode sampler, choose K so the displayed Gaussian
tail is at most epsilon/4. If each retained logit
theta_k=tau(lambda-2J pi^2 k^2) is evaluated with absolute error at most
epsilon/K, the logistic map's derivative at most 1/4 makes the sum of
Bernoulli total-variation errors at most epsilon/4. For rational J,tau,
lambda and fixed parameter bounds, O(log(1/epsilon)+3 log K) fractional
bits suffice up to constants fixed by those bounds, in addition to the
input bit lengths (including a certified pi^2 approximation); exact
integer arithmetic/exp evaluation is not claimed. The continuum
occupation state itself is diagonal, so this is a
classical mode-sampling cost, not a circuit cost to synthesize a chain
state.

Finite evidence: evidence/form_identity_check.py uses exact rational/integer
matrices and passes all 54 particle sectors for L=2,...,10 (largest sector
dimension 126), checking Hcrit=K_L-4JW and H_L(V)=Hcrit+(4J+V)W for
V=-3,0,7/3 at J=1. This checks the finite algebra only; it does not test
the interpolation, Mosco, resolvent, or thermal-tail arguments.

Falsifiers: an exact finite-chain configuration violating the form
identity; a sequence with g_L->infinity whose embedded fixed-q resolvent
does not converge to the Dirichlet-collision resolvent; or a failure of
the cited all-sector bound (4.4) for some g>=g_*. Finite checks cannot
replace these all-size proofs.

and the XXZ-to-Lieb-Liniger scaling is established. No novelty or priority
claim is made. The specific full-Fock trace and uniform relative-entropy
convergence on fixed-gap compacts are internally proved candidates here and
