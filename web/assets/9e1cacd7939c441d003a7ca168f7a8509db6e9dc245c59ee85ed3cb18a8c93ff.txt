Independent analytic receipt: SU(m) symmetric mode identity
===========================================================

Audited artifact: `outputs/research/sol_classicality/SU_M_MODE_IDENTITY_PROOF.txt`.
Scope requested: canonical overlap trace, multiplicity-one coupling,
zero-row/column phase fixing, source factorial sum, Sears substitution, and
the k+l>N boundary.  This audit does not re-audit the separate scalar-gap
argument.

Verdict
-------

The canonical overlap, normalization, source phase calibration, and
Sears-to-4F3 coefficient algebra are correct.  The boundary-continuation
sentence is slightly underspecified: the noninteger perturbation must
preserve the balanced-series condition of DLMF 16.4.14.  If “introduce a
noninteger nu near N in the exact finite identity” means replace every
occurrence of N by nu, then it already does so (in particular
e=-nu and f=nu+m), and the continuation is valid.  If it means only
e=-N -> -nu while holding f=N+m fixed, it is invalid because the series
becomes unbalanced.  The explicit deformation and gamma limit below settle
the intended global-replacement reading.  No counterexample to the
identity was found; this is a precision/statement issue, not a gap in the
mathematical identity once N is varied throughout.

1. Canonical overlap and channel normalization
-----------------------------------------------

For tau-orthonormal Hermitian operator bases, Tr(B_ka B_lb)=d delta_kl
delta_ab.  Thus

  w_k(i,j,h,t)=(d sqrt(r_k))^(-1) sum_a (B_ka)_ij (B_ka)_ht

has norm one: the squared norm is
`(d^2 r_k)^(-1) sum_ab |Tr(B_ka^dag B_kb)|^2=1`.  Different k are
orthogonal.  Since H tensor barH contains each self-dual W_k once, the
invariant subspace of its two-pair product has dimension N+1 and these
vectors form an orthonormal basis.  The crossed-pair vectors w'_l have the
same property.

In their overlap, take the complex conjugate of the two first-pair entries
and use Hermiticity.  The summand becomes

  (B_ka)_ji (B_ka)_th (B_lb)_it (B_lb)_hj,

which sums over i,j,h,t to Tr(B_ka B_lb B_ka B_lb).  Consequently the
reported formula

  U_kl = [d^2 sqrt(r_k r_l)]^(-1)
          sum_ab Tr(B_ka B_lb B_ka B_lb)

has the stated order and no transpose or d-factor error.  Covariance and
multiplicity one make D_k scalar on W_l.  Expanding
D_k(B_lb)=r_k^(-1) sum_a B_ka B_lb B_ka, taking its Hilbert-Schmidt inner
product with B_lb, and summing over b gives

  sum_ab Tr(B_ka B_lb B_ka B_lb)=d r_k r_l eta_k(l),

so eta_k(l)=d U_kl/sqrt(r_k r_l).  This also agrees with the normalized
Choi convention Q_k/r_k followed by the standard reshuffling factor d.

2. Phase calibration from both zero axes
-----------------------------------------

With B_0=I and r_0=1, the overlap formula gives U_0l=U_k0=sqrt(r_l)/d
(respectively sqrt(r_k)/d), both nonzero and positive.  In the primary
kind-I source sum, setting k=0 leaves z=l and gives

  R_raw(0,l)=(-1)^l N!(m-1)!/(N+m-1)!=(-1)^l/d.

The source vertex convention is epsilon=(-1)^(N+k+l), hence
S_0l=S_k0=(-1)^N sqrt(r_l)/d (respectively sqrt(r_k)/d).  Multiplicity
one implies U=D S E for diagonal unit phases D,E.  The zero row yields
D_0 E_l=(-1)^N for every l; the zero column yields D_k E_0=(-1)^N for
every k.  These imply D_k E_l=(-1)^N for all k,l, and therefore
U=(-1)^N S.  Both nonzero axes are needed for this conclusion.  The
resulting coefficient is eta_k(l)=(-1)^(k+l) d R_raw(k,l).  No phase
ambiguity remains.

The source's kind-I setup is precisely the four alternating factors
H,barH,H,barH and its two pairings; its symmetric-power degree is the N
used here and its SU(m) rank is m.  The source gives the factorial sum and
epsilon=(-1)^(i+j+r) in its §2 kind-I definition.  At q=1 its quantum
factorials and quantum dimensions reduce to the ordinary factorials and
dimensions used in the proof.  This checks the imported source boundary;
the channel interpretation comes from the direct overlap above.

3. Sears substitution and factorial matching for k+l<=N
-------------------------------------------------------

NIST DLMF 16.4.14 is

  4F3[-n,a,b,c;d,e,f;1]
   = (e-a)_n(f-a)_n/[(e)_n(f)_n]
     4F3[-n,a,d-b,d-c;d,a-e-n+1,a-f-n+1;1],

when `a+b+c-n+1=d+e+f`.  With

  n=k, a=-l, b=l+m-1, c=k+m-1,
  d=m-1, e=-N, f=N+m,

both sides are balanced at 2m-1.  The prefactor is

  P=(-N+l)_k (N+m+l)_k/[(-N)_k(N+m)_k],

and the transformed series is exactly the one in equation (8) of the
audited proof.  For k+l<=N its factorial expansion at r, 0<=r<=min(k,l),
has coefficient

  (N+m-1)!(N-k)!(N-l)! k!^2 l!^2 (-1)^r
  (N+m+k+l-r-1)!(m-2)!
  /[N!(N+m+k-1)!(N+m+l-1)!(k-r)!^2(l-r)!^2
      (N-k-l+r)!(m+r-2)!r!].

This is exactly (-1)^(k+l)d times the source summand after putting
z=k+l-r: the source denominator terms become `(l-r)!^2`, `(k-r)!^2`,
`(N-k-l+r)!`, `r!`, and `(m+r-2)!`; and its sign satisfies
(-1)^(k+l)(-1)^z=(-1)^r.  The prefactor also matches exactly.

4. Corrected balanced perturbation for k+l>N
---------------------------------------------

For the boundary case, the precise balanced perturbation is to replace

  e=-N by e=-nu, and simultaneously f=N+m by f=nu+m.

Then `d+e+f=2m-1` remains equal to `a+b+c-k+1`, and for noninteger nu
near N the terminating identity is valid.  The left series is finite and
regular at nu=N because k<=N: its lower factor (-nu)_r has no zero for
0<=r<=k at nu=N.  Write the r-th term of the product of the Sears
prefactor with the transformed series as

  T_r(nu)=P(nu) (-k)_r^2(-l)_r^2 /
     [(m-1)_r (nu-k-l+1)_r (1-nu-m-k-l)_r r!],

where

  P(nu)=(-nu+l)_k(nu+m+l)_k/[(-nu)_k(nu+m)_k].

Use gamma functions and the reflection identity to obtain

  lim_(nu->N) P(nu) Gamma(nu-k-l+1)
    = (N-l)!(N-k)!(N+m-1)!(N+m+k+l-1)!
      /[N!(N+m+l-1)!(N+m+k-1)!].

For clarity, the cancellation uses

  Gamma(l-nu+k) Gamma(nu-k-l+1)/Gamma(l-nu)
      =(-1)^k Gamma(nu-l+1),

and

  lim_(nu->N) Gamma(-nu)/Gamma(-nu+k)
      =(-1)^k (N-k)!/N!.

After also using
`(1-nu-m-k-l)_r=(-1)^r Gamma(nu+m+k+l)/Gamma(nu+m+k+l-r)`,
the limit of T_r is the factorial coefficient in §3, with
`1/(N-k-l+r)!` interpreted as
`1/Gamma(N-k-l+r+1)`.  This reciprocal gamma is zero exactly when
r<k+l-N and is finite otherwise.  Thus the surviving range is

  max(0,k+l-N) <= r <= min(k,l).

The substitution z=k+l-r maps it bijectively onto
`max(k,l)<=z<=N`, which is precisely the source sum range
`max(k,l)<=z<=min(N,k+l)` when k+l>N.  Therefore the corrected limit
proves the identity at this boundary as well.

5. Recommended textual repair and status
-----------------------------------------

Recommended precision edit: replace the current sentence “introduce a
noninteger nu near N in the exact finite identity” with “replace N by nu
throughout the balanced Sears identity, so e=-nu and f=nu+m,” and include
the gamma-limit in §4 above or cite it as the reason the finite terms have
removable singularities.  With the natural global-replacement reading the
proof is already analytically valid; this edit makes the balance condition
and boundary limit explicit for independent readers.
