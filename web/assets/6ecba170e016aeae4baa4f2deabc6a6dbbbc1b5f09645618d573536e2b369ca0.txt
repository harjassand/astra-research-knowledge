Current result
--------------

  ∂t f + v∂x f − κx∂v f = ∂v(vf)+∂v²f,  0<κ<1/4,

has invariant Gaussian Fκ proportional to exp(−(κx²+v²)/2), exact mean-zero L² gap

  ακ=(1−√(1−4κ))/2 ~ κ,

and sharp relative-entropy exponent 2ακ. A covariance-preserving Gaussian shift in the slow drift eigenvector gives H_t=e^(−2ακt)H_0, disproving every positive decay exponent uniform as κ↓0. The position Poincaré gap is κ. Arnold–Erb (arXiv:1409.5425v2, 2014-10-24), Theorems 4.9 and 6.1, gives the same parameterwise entropy exponent and sharpness.
