2. EB LOWER BOUND WITH ARBITRARY OUTPUT FLAG MASSES

Let Lambda:B'->B' be ANY full-domain TP EB channel, with no premise that
it preserves flags, block weights, or coherences. Put
 sigma'=(id_A' tensor Lambda)(rho').
Its A' marginal equals rho'_A' by local trace preservation. In particular
 Tr[(Pi_A tensor I)sigma']=w.                                  (F4)
The A-active compression is therefore exactly
 (Pi_A tensor I)sigma'(Pi_A tensor I)=w sigma_active,
 sigma_active=(id_A tensor Lambda|_{M(B)})(rho),                (F5)
where sigma_active is normalized and separable on active A tensor the
ENTIRE B' output. The restriction Lambda|_{M(B)} is CP, TP and EB into
B'; no B-active conditioning, renormalization, or flag-preserving
assumption has been made.

Let E be the original direct-sum antisymmetric projector effect P on
active A tensor active B, extended by zero if either party is vacuum.
Then Tr(E rho')=w. For a unit product vector a in active A, b in B',
decompose b into vacuum and active block components b_j. The original
projector compression identity yields
 <a,b|E|a,b>=sum_j ||Q_{a,n_j}b_j||^2/(n_j+1)
             <=(1/2)sum_j ||b_j||^2<=1/2.                      (F6)
Thus Tr(E sigma_active)<=1/2. Equations (F4)-(F6) imply
 Tr(E sigma')<=w/2,
 T(rho',sigma')>=Tr[E(rho'-sigma')]>=w/2.                       (F7)
Taking the infimum over all local EB maps proves
 e_loc(rho')>=w/2.                                            (F8)

The unchanged A-active mass is load-bearing. An unrestricted separable
state of total trace one only gives Tr(E sigma)<=1/2, which alone would
be useless when w<1/2. Conditioning on A, whose flag a local B map
cannot change, gives the additional factor w. Even coherent B vacuum
outputs and arbitrary B active/vacuum mass transfers obey this proof.

A full-domain EB upper control can preserve the vacuum and replace the
active input by any fixed active B state. It leaves the vacuum component
unchanged and gives active trace distance at most one. Consequently
 w/2<=e_loc(rho')<=w.                                         (F9)

3. MUTUAL INFORMATION AND THE BOUNDED-I EXPONENT OBSTRUCTION

Both local flags and the joint vacuum/active components are orthogonal.
For h(w)=-w ln w-(1-w)ln(1-w), their exact entropies give
 I(A':B')_rho'=h(w)+w I(A:B)_rho.                              (F10)
Set w=1/L. From the original identity
I(A:B)_rho=(L-1)ln2/2+delta_L, 0<delta_L<4/L, obtain
 I(A':B')_rho'=h(1/L)+(L-1)ln2/(2L)+delta_L/L
                ->ln2/2.                                    (F11)
For every L>=2 it is at most the finite constant
 M=1+(3/2)ln2,
because h(1/L)<=ln2, (L-1)ln2/(2L)<ln2/2 and delta_L/L<4/L^2<=1.

The simultaneous bounds are
 b_loc(rho')<=1/L^2,
 1/(2L)<=e_loc(rho')<=1/L,
 I(A':B')_rho'<=M.                                            (F12)
Therefore for every alpha>1 and fixed finite K>=0,
 e_loc(rho')^2>=1/(4L^2),
 K(I(A':B')_rho'+1)b_loc(rho')^alpha
      <=K(M+1)L^(-2alpha).                                  (F13)
The ratio of the former lower bound to the latter upper bound tends to
infinity. Thus the claimed exponent-alpha inequality fails already in
a uniformly bounded-information family for every alpha>1.

This strengthens the original unflagged exponent obstruction. It does
not show that no continuity modulus can hold at bounded information:
the displayed EB upper bound tends to zero. Nor does this proof decide
the exponent-one inequality or identify the actual broadcasting infimum.
The asymptotic mutual information converges to a constant; an exactly
fixed mutual-information family is not required for the stated result
and no such additional construction is claimed here.
