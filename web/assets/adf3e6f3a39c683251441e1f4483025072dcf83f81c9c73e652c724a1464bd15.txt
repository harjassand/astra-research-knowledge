1. Local noise normalization
-----------------------------
Permute the tensor factors to qubit tensor reference and diagonalize sigma_B.
Write

  sigma_B=(I+tanh(alpha)Z)/2, alpha>=0,
  c=cosh(alpha), h=sinh(alpha), A=cosh(alpha/2),
  k=tanh(alpha/2), X=sigma_x, Z=sigma_z,

and tensor every local operator with the reference identity. Identity additions
to the local Hermitian noise cancel. If its off-diagonal entry is nonzero, a
local diagonal unitary commuting with sigma_B and an overall scaling normalize
the noise to N=X+aZ with a real. This phase normalization acts on rho as well;
it does NOT make the operator-valued off-diagonal block of rho real or Hermitian.
Pure Z noise already has the complete strong constant 4. Thus it suffices to
handle N=X+aZ for arbitrary complex Hermitian joint rho.

2. Exact physical generator decomposition
-----------------------------------------
Let rho>0, q=sqrt(rho), ell=log(rho). In the local eigenbasis, write their
two-by-two reference-operator blocks as rho_ij,q_ij,ell_ij. No commuting-block
assumption is imposed. Let G_0,G_1,G_2 be the following trace-annihilating linear
components in the positive-generator convention (the individual cross
component G_1 need not be a positive generator):

  G_0(rho)=
    [[ e^(-alpha)rho_00-e^(alpha)rho_11, c rho_01-rho_10 ],
     [ c rho_10-rho_01, e^(alpha)rho_11-e^(-alpha)rho_00 ]],

  G_1(rho)=(1/A)
    [[ -(rho_01+rho_10), e^(alpha)rho_11-e^(-alpha)rho_00 ],
     [ e^(alpha)rho_11-e^(-alpha)rho_00, rho_01+rho_10 ]],

  G_2(rho)=rho-Zrho Z
          =[[0,2rho_01],[2rho_10,0]].                  (1)

Then L_(X+aZ),*(rho)=G_0(rho)+aG_1(rho)+a^2G_2(rho).
To verify (1), the original-picture jump and drift are

  K=[[a,e^(alpha/2)],[e^(-alpha/2),-a]],
  V=(a^2+c)I-hZ+2akX,
  D=s_B^(1/2)V s_B^(-1/2),

The cross coefficient in this multiplication simplifies using
(1-k)e^(alpha/2)=(1+k)e^(-alpha/2)=1/A. The full generator is legal by the
Hermitian-noise Lyapunov representation, including its original-picture

  j_i=Tr[G_i(rho)(ell-alpha Z)], i=0,1,2,

All full traces include the reference. The log rho in (2) is the genuine joint

3. Exact root-energy coefficients
---------------------------------
From E_N=Tr[V rho]-Tr[NqNq], direct expansion gives

  e_0=Tr[(cI-hZ)rho]-Tr[XqXq],
  e_1=2k Tr[Xrho]-2Tr[XqZq],
  e_2=1-Tr[ZqZq]=(1/2)||[Z,q]||_HS^2,
  E_(X+aZ)=e_0+a e_1+a^2 e_2.                        (3)

The two cross traces in the expansion agree by cyclicity, and Tr[XqZq] is
real. Thus all coefficients in (2)-(3) are real without a block-reality premise.

Set f_i=j_i-2e_i. The complete nonlinear one-noise target is exactly

  f_0+a f_1+a^2 f_2>=0 for every real a.              (4)

The coefficient f_2 is nonnegative by the complete strong endpoint for the
Hermitian unital Z-noise:

  j_2>=4e_2, so f_2>=2e_2>=0.                        (5)

This follows from diagonalizing rho and applying
(x-y)(log x-log y)>=4(sqrt(x)-sqrt(y))^2 to its commutator entries; it does not
use stationarity or a reference-dimension bound.

If f_2=0, then (5) implies [Z,q]=0. In that case rho and ell are locally block
diagonal, hence f_1=0 by (1)-(3). Also rho commutes with sigma_B tensor I_R,
so the earlier all-dimensional commuting-state pair control gives f_0>=0.
This handles the degenerate coefficient case exactly.

If f_2>0, completion of the real square makes the remaining target equivalent

  4f_0 f_2-f_1^2>=0.                                (6)
