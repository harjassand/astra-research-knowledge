LEAD RESULT ALREADY PROVED INTERNALLY

For stationary HMMs with known A_ij>=rho/d and E_i(a)>=e, a rational finite cover plus separated blocks gives fresh finite-H conditional-KL oracle risk

  oracle + tau + 2 M_H sqrt(log(4N_D/delta)/(2B)) + 2 zeta,
