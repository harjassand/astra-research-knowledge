Representation closure and primary import:

The complex symplectic tensor-square commutant is spanned by I,F,P_singlet: the r=2 case of the Brauer first fundamental theorem. It is valid in characteristic zero. For d>=4 these three maps are independent, and compact complete reducibility makes the three projector ranges inequivalent irreducibles. Passing from compact USp(d) to its complexification does not change this commutant (differentiate to its Lie algebra and complexify). Thus the conjugation representation has exactly scalar plus two nontrivial sectors. At d=2 the last projector is zero.
