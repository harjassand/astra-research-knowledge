2. Quantum dynamics: the most developed general theorem

For every finite quantum Markov generator with a faithful stationary state,
the internally completed positive-input, reference-stable Dirichlet estimate
has sharp coefficient

  c_p = p sin(pi/p) / [2(p-1)(1+|cos(pi/p)|)], p>1.

For KMS-symmetric dynamics, the larger sharp coefficient is

  c_p^KMS = p sin(pi/p) / [2(p-1)].

The precise weighted powers and energy conventions are part of the proof
packet; they cannot be interchanged with unweighted or differently signed
forms. The corresponding actual forward entropy/root-energy constants are
pi/2 for general stationary dynamics and pi for KMS dynamics. Every finite
untouched quantum reference and joint positive input is included. A separate
signed/polar extension exists in the KMS case; the non-KMS result is not
silently extended to signed inputs.

The mechanism keeps the complete physical generator, including Hamiltonian
and stationary-log terms, then reduces its exact modular-frequency form to
a Hardy-space kernel. Independent finite physical constructions approach
the same constants. Within the internally checked conventions, the explicit
primitive counterfamilies contradict both substantive clauses of the checked
Kastoryano–Temme 2013 regularity conjecture. This is a candidate conjecture
resolution with complete internal proof, not an externally certified one.
An unrelated printed high-p typo is not the basis of the counterexamples.

The earlier work also established a common entanglement-breaking comparator
for every finite bistochastic self-compatible channel, with universal factor
4 in the self-adjoint case and factor 8 in the general symmetric-part form.
Structured Clifford, spin, covariant and tensor-product classes admit factor
2 results. A separate rational-input compiler produces an approximate factor
4 comparator with finite outcome and expected-polynomial construction bounds
under its supplied-data contract. None of those structured factor-2 results
proves the optimal factor 2 for every arbitrary channel.

3. Approximate broadcasting and one common classical reconstruction

For any actual two-output local broadcaster and one faithful input marginal
sigma, the internally completed theorem selects ONE canonical
measure-and-prepare channel Psi that works simultaneously for every joint
state with that marginal, including arbitrary finite reference systems:

  e_Psi(rho)^3 <= [240(2+sqrt(2))/pi] I(R:B)_rho sqrt(b_raw(rho)).

Errors here are half trace distances and information is in natural logs.
The original broadcaster need not be symmetric, stationary or KMS. The proof
repairs stationarity and compares energies along the actual dynamics, avoiding
a previously unresolved general Petz trace-error transfer.

A later moving-reference recovery construction removes the common-marginal
restriction. With optimized JOINT observational radius R, best actual
two-output error b, and best error e of one local entanglement-breaking map,
the strongest completed internal consequence is, for b>0,

  e^2 <= min{1, R^2, 169 [R log(euler/b)]^(6/7) b^(2/7)}.

There is an exact common fixer at b=0 or R=0. The same selected channel works
over the entire family, with varying local margins and arbitrarily large
finite references. Its proof has a longer dependency chain than the
standalone smoothing result below. The logarithmic correction and exponent
are not known optimal for actual state families.

The output of a measure-and-prepare map need not be diagonal in one common
orthogonal basis. We found an exact regular-simplex family with observational
radius log d, broadcasting/EB error O(1/d), but common orthogonal-output error
at least (1-1/d)/2. This rules out a broad proposed polynomial-radius
orthogonal-basis upgrade. The fixed-common-marginal bounded-information basis
problem remains unresolved. A separate archived dyadic-copy construction
shows why a radius-free broadcasting-to-EB modulus is impossible.

We also separated reconstruction from memory: bounded reachable information
and exact broadcasting do not by themselves bound HMM state count or Shannon
memory. A supplied-instrument HMM can inherit length-n statistical error
at most n epsilon from a common EB approximation, but this is not a theorem
about efficient acquisition or small memory.

4. Standalone quantum smoothing and a sharp coherence separation

For states rho,sigma, let R be their one-effect observational divergence and
let D_epsilon minimize Umegaki relative entropy to sigma over normalized
states within half trace distance epsilon of rho. The internally proved and
independently reconstructed theorem gives

  D_epsilon <= 5 R log(euler/epsilon), 0<epsilon<=1/2,
  D_epsilon = 0 when R<=epsilon.

The logarithmic order is sharp at each fixed positive radius. The proof uses
a noncommuting normalized filter that contracts every testing-tail curve,
followed by the primary Umegaki integral formula. It does not infer the
claim from a stronger max-relative-entropy bound. This is one of the cleanest
standalone results because it avoids the long stationary-QMS dependency chain.
The type-I trace-class extension has a lower review level: Luna derivation
and coordinator review, not a separate fresh Sol reconstruction.

In a different testing-tail regime, both tails bounded by (1+a)^(-s),
0<s<1, we obtained sharp worst-case smoothing orders

  quantum:   Theta_s(epsilon^[-2(1-s)/s]),
  commuting: Theta_s(epsilon^[-(1-s)/s]).

Faithful qubits already realize the separation. Thus coherence can change
the exponent even when both testing tails are controlled. The construction
does not maintain a uniform eigenvalue floor; its thermodynamic consequence
is asymptotic under explicit resource assumptions, not a one-shot work claim.

5. Finite graph height fields: completed results and exact obstructions

For a uniform real 1-Lipschitz field on a finite bounded-degree graph with
zero pins meeting every component, we internally proved the full signed-load
quadratic-form inequality

  Var(q.h) >= c_Delta q^T L_D^(-1) q, c_Delta>0.

The constant is independent of graph size, pin count and spectral gap.
The standalone argument imports the primary zero-pin controlled-gradient
theorem, not the generated corpus's final Gaussian theorem. Two constructions
and fresh adversarial review support it. Exact forests and K_(2,k) controls
check normalization and show a degree-free constant cannot be expected.

For heterogeneous pins in [-B,B], we then proved an interior all-edge-set
near-saturation estimate. A buffer of order B separates tested edges from
the pins. A pin-preserving fixed-level reflection, followed by deletion and
dilution, bounds the probability that every edge in a specified set is nearly
saturated by rho(epsilon) to the number of edges, with rho tending to zero.
A corrected covariance consequence covers loads deep beyond a grounded pin
layer. It does not yield an unrestricted all-load covariance order.

The proposed alternative assumption of a strict pairwise pin slope is
insufficient: degree-three tree examples have a uniform strict slope margin
but a free-edge variance tending to zero while the corresponding Dirichlet
Green form stays bounded below. Pin amplitudes grow with depth, so this does
not contradict the bounded-amplitude interior theorem.

Read the graph-covariance-lower-bound, bounded-pin-interior-cap, and
strict-slope-tree-counterexamples START_HERE files under outputs/.

6. Square-lattice Gaussian universality: complete internal candidate

uniform real hard-Lipschitz field with zero exterior data on the specified
smooth-domain approximations. It concludes

  Phi_n => A^(-1/2) Dirichlet GFF, in H^(-s) for every fixed s>0,

with one positive finite deterministic stiffness A independent of domain,
admitted approximation sequence and test functions. The unscaled height
convention, fixed affine interpolation and square vertex density are explicit;
there is no additional factor of two. Small actual Gibbs tilts have the
corresponding shifted Gaussian limit.

This was built through finite heat-bath covariance identities, sparse-contact
estimates, stationary bulk limits, reflected comparison, recurrence and

The entire physical proof chain remains an internal reconstruction. It is
not established merely by analogy with the triangular-lattice manuscript,
and neither a Gaussian field limit nor improved topology supplies SLE4.

  dim_H Sing_support(V) <= m-1.

8. Commuting-operator games: the most consequential live quantum gate

The target is a uniform, unanchored parallel-repetition theorem for arbitrary
finite two-player commuting-operator games. The intended intermediate bound
controls the mean remaining-coordinate win probability conditioned on wins
in C by

  omega_co(G) + B [(log(1/p)+|C|log(|A||B|))/(n-|C|)]^(1/4),
  p=Pr[all coordinates in C win]>0.

This removes dependence on a question-support spectral gap. It is not yet
an established full theorem at this cutoff.

The critical new component is a finite-tracial CORRELATION sampler: for
finite families of unit L2 descriptions with mean squared discrepancy D,
one preagreed enlarged resource and complete local POVMs approximate the
desired outcome tables with mean half-TV at most C D^(1/4)+zeta. Two Sol
workers have now frozen independent constructions (constants 2048 and 1000).
They handle partial polar isometries, zero support, finite truncation and
all-input commutation. The fresh exposed comparison is beginning.

The distinction between correlation sampling and a fixed-Hilbert-space
trace-preserving output channel is essential: a central-sector example
refutes the latter proposed interface. The first-success construction
operates legally on enlarged correlation resources and charges mismatch.

A Luna worker also falsified a proposed global history-decoupling lemma with
a noisy-parity example. Independent replacements now use local history
kernels, compare only the relevant question marginals, and keep the sampler's
description discrepancy measured under the diagonal posterior law. Sampling
mismatch and law changes are paid outside the fourth root. This avoids a
spurious degradation to exponent 1/8.

The current missing assembly is precise: implement the resolvent descriptors
and transported fine-answer POVMs in one finite-tracial setting, retain
exact raw mass identities, then compose the history and correlation samplers.
An originator is preparing a full proof; independent workers are checking
finite-feature completion, private-auxiliary compression and positive-p
finite-table limits. Lin's density theorem permits a final continuous
finite-table transfer to commuting correlations; it is not matrix density.

A separate Luna derivation shows that the displayed intermediate bound would
give threshold tails of the form

  Pr[W >= ceil((v+delta)n)]
    <= exp[-c_B delta^5 n/(delta+log(|A||B|))].

That implication is conditional. It would give a meaningful unanchored
commuting repetition result, but it does not imply quantum PCP, a local
Hamiltonian gap theorem or a new MIPco complexity-class equality. Historical
novelty still requires broader checking even if the proof closes.
