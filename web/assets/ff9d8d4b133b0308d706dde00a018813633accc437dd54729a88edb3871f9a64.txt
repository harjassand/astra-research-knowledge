Stochastic source checks and theorem interfaces
-----------------------------------------------
- Anderson, Cappelletti, Fan, and Kim, “A New Path Method for Exponential
  Ergodicity of Markov Processes on Z^d, with Applications to Stochastic
  Reaction Networks,” SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst. 24(2) (2025), Corollary 3.5,
  <https://arxiv.org/html/2309.06970v2>. Covers the relevant layered
  complex-balanced subfamily under its exact rate restrictions; not arbitrary
  positive rate vectors or the all-rate weakly reversible class.
- Anderson, Cappelletti, and Kim, “A weak reversibility criterion for the
  ergodicity of stochastically modeled reaction networks,”
  <https://arxiv.org/html/1904.08967v2>, Theorems 4.1 and 6.1. Scope audit is
  in `leaves/sparse_theorem_coverage/REPORT.txt`; their sufficient tier
  hypotheses fail on a reachable catalyst-free high-substrate ray, which is
  not itself a recurrence counterexample.
- Anderson and Kim, “Some network conditions for positive recurrence of
  stochastically modeled reaction networks,” Theorem 3.1,
  <https://arxiv.org/html/1710.11263v3>. Used only after the internal proof
  establishes the physical-time drift/nonexplosion interface.
- Anderson and Kurtz, “Product-form stationary distributions for deficiency
  zero chemical reaction networks,” <https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3042>.
  Complex-balance/product-Poisson use and scope are audited in
  `leaves/sparse_prior_art/REPORT.txt` and the exact support-switch witness in
  `leaves/sparse_geometric_drift/REPORT.txt`.
- Anderson, Cappelletti, Koyama, and Kurtz, “Non-explosivity of stochastically
  modeled reaction networks that are complex balanced,”
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.09356>. Used for the broader complex-balanced
  potential-template witness; it does not establish the sparse all-rate
  theorem.
- Meyn and Tweedie, “Stability of Markovian Processes III: Foster-Lyapunov
  Criteria for Continuous-Time Processes,” Adv. Appl. Prob. 25(3) (1993),
  Theorem 6.1/CD3, <https://doi.org/10.2307/1427522>. Fixed-process theorem;
  the compact-box uniformity is separately proved from common drift and
  common finite-path minorization. Source reconstruction:
  `leaves/geometric_ergodicity_prior/REPORT.txt`.
