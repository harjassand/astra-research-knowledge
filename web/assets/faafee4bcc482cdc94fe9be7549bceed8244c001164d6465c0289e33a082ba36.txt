T1. EXACT THEOREM AND PROOF

Let Phi be unital, TP and HS-self-adjoint on M_d, and Psi an EB CPTP map.
Suppose, for every Hermitian A,
  Tr[A(I-Psi)(A)] <= C Tr[A(I-Phi)(A)].
Then a canonical POVM EB channel Gamma has the same inequality and same C.
In fact Gamma can be chosen to HS-dominate the symmetric quadratic form
of Psi on every Hermitian A.

1. The full-domain inequality forces Psi to be unital.
For any traceless Hermitian X and every real t, insert A=tI+X.
TP implies Tr[(I-Psi)(X)]=0 and Tr[(I-Psi)(I)]=0. Unitality
and HS-self-adjointness of Phi eliminate both identity cross terms on the
right. The only left cross term is
  t Tr[X(I-Psi(I))].
Since t is arbitrary, it vanishes for every traceless X. Since
Tr[I-Psi(I)]=0, this gives Psi(I)=I.

2. Use an EB measure-and-prepare decomposition
  Psi(A)=sum_y Tr(F_y A) sigma_y,
where F_y >=0, sum_y F_y=I, sigma_y>=0, Tr sigma_y=1.
Remove effects of zero trace; put f_y=Tr F_y>0 and N_y=f_y sigma_y.
The preceding unitality gives sum_y N_y=I, while Tr N_y=f_y.
Thus both (F_y)_y and (N_y)_y are POVMs with matching traces.

3. Let Gamma_F(A)=sum_y Tr(F_y A)F_y/f_y and define Gamma_N similarly.
Then Gamma=(Gamma_F+Gamma_N)/2 is itself canonical: use the disjoint
POVM effects F_y/2 and N_y/2. It is EB, TP, unital and HS-self-adjoint.
For every Hermitian A its quadratic domination is the exact identity
  Tr[A Gamma(A)]-Tr[A Psi(A)]
     = (1/2) sum_y [Tr((F_y-N_y)A)]^2/f_y >=0.
Hence I-Gamma <= I-Psi as Hermitian quadratic forms, which proves T1.

Interface warning: if the input inequality is imposed only on traceless
Hermitians, the identity cross-term argument is unavailable. For example,
Psi(A)=Tr(A)sigma, sigma != I/d, and Phi(A)=Tr(A)I/d obey the same
inequality with C=1 on the traceless space, although Psi is not unital.
That example does not refute a separate traceless canonicalization theorem;
it precisely refutes silently deducing unitality from a traceless contract.
If Psi is independently assumed unital, the square-completion proof works
on any chosen test space.

T2. EXACT WEIGHTED-FRAME DUAL

For Q=sum_i |A_i><A_i| on the real traceless Hermitian space, put
  T=sum_i Tr A_i^2,  S_Phi=sum_i Tr[A_i Phi(A_i)].
Refining each POVM effect into rank-one effects increases every canonical
score, by weighted Cauchy-Schwarz. Consequently the canonical support is
  S_can(Q)=d sup_{E rho=I/d} E sum_i (Tr A_i rho)^2,
where pure states suffice. Compact-convex separation shows that the
universal constant-C comparator exists exactly if, for every legal Phi
and every Q>=0,
  S_can(Q) >= T-C(T-S_Phi).
Thus C=2 is exactly S_can(Q)>=2S_Phi-T. This is a convex-roof variance
rounding problem for a bistochastic 2-extendible Choi state, with the
additional HS-self-adjointness constraint. Ordinary additive de Finetti
trace-distance bounds do not imply this multiplicative energy comparison.

Rank <=2 dual frames are handled by a single broadcaster layer and the
source's marginal-score/refinement argument, giving C=2. A failure of
C=2 therefore needs at least three real frame directions. A failure of
C=2 alone would not refute all universal finite C.
