1. EXPLICIT RATIONAL PROJECTOR AND ITS LOG-CONCAVITY

Realize spin S=q/2 as Sym^q(C^2). In the q-qubit computational basis, its
orthogonal projector Pi_q has entries

 (Pi_q)_(r,c) = 1/binom(q,|r|) if |r|=|c|, and 0 otherwise.

Indeed, its charge-r block is the all-one matrix divided by binom(q,r),
the rank-one projector onto the normalized Dicke vector. These blocks are
orthogonal and exhaust the q+1-dimensional symmetric subspace. All entries
are nonnegative rationals. No normalized Dicke square roots are input data.

Keep row bits and complement column bits, exactly as in the baseline transfer.
With q row variables x and q complemented-column variables y, the local
projector polynomial is

 p_q(x,y)=sum_(r=0)^q e_r(x)e_(q-r)(y)/binom(q,r).

It is homogeneous, multiaffine, degree q, with EVERY q-subset of its 2q
variables in support. Its positive coefficient range is at most 2^q.
The q=1 case is linear. Here is an exact signature proof for every q>=2.

Take a nonzero derivative of order q-2, using a row and b column variables,
a+b=q-2. Let m=q-a=b+2, ell=q-b=a+2 be the remaining row/column counts and

 A=1/binom(q,a+2), B=1/binom(q,a+1), D=1/binom(q,a).

The resulting quadratic Hessian has row-row off-diagonals A, column-column
off-diagonals D, cross entries B, and zero diagonals. On row-sum-zero vectors
it has eigenvalue -A; on column-sum-zero vectors it has eigenvalue -D.
The remaining two-dimensional block is

 [[A(m-1), B sqrt(m ell)], [B sqrt(m ell), D(ell-1)]].

Its determinant is ZERO, since

 AD/B^2 = binom(q,a+1)^2/[binom(q,a+2)binom(q,a)]
        = m ell/[(m-1)(ell-1)].

Its trace is positive. Thus every such derivative has exactly one positive
eigenvalue, one zero and all other eigenvalues negative. Repeated-variable
derivatives vanish by multiaffinity. Full uniform-matroid support is M-convex.
The established Branden--Huh characterization (Theorem2.25, explanatory
paragraph following it; Theorem2.30) therefore makes p_q Lorentzian and
completely log-concave. This is a proof for every q, not an extrapolation
from the executable q<=12 arithmetic checks.

Equivalently p_q is the separate x/y polarization of (X+Y)^q; this observation
explains why this projector has a legal gate polynomial. The displayed
signature calculation is the certificate actually used here.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/1902.03719v8 and
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2020/192-3/p04 (published established theorem).

6. TWO IMPORTANT FAILED SHORTCUTS

6a. One projection only cannot inherit the unweighted relative trace proof.
For K=diag(-M,M), Pi=|0><0|, L=K+kappa X, ||L-K||=kappa. With
R=sqrt(M^2+kappa^2),

 Tr Pi e^L =cosh R-(M/R)sinh R,
 Tr Pi e^K =e^-M.

For fixed kappa>0 and M->infinity their ratio grows asymptotically like
kappa^2 e^(2M)/(4M^2), although K commutes with Pi. Small off-diagonal leakage
can reach an exponentially heavier excluded sector. Thus a naive final
projection does NOT follow from the baseline Hermitian generator norm bound.
The layerwise compression in section3 solves this exact problem.

6b. The projector is a legal inserted gate but I+sPi_4 is NOT a legal
infinitesimal Hamiltonian gate. In row variables x0..x3 / column y0..y3,
q_I=product_i(x_i+y_i), q= q_I+s p_4. After differentiating x0,x1, let
p=x2+x3+y2+y3 and w=y0+y1 denote the coordinate vectors with these entries
one. The Hessian quadratic restriction on span{p,w} is

 [[8+13s/3,5s/3],[5s/3,s/3]],
 determinant=(4s/3)(2-s)>0 for 0<s<2.

Both diagonal entries are positive, so this derivative has at least TWO
positive eigenvalues. A multiaffine LC polynomial's derivatives are LC by
the standard positive-coordinate limit. The quadratic signature criterion
therefore refutes LC of q_I+s p_4 in this range. This excludes a claimed
arbitrary high-order symmetric-projector Hamiltonian extension through the
same infinitesimal gates; it does not prove computational hardness.
