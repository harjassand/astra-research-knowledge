EXACT CLAIM AND INTERFACE

On M_3, define P_+=(I+F)/2, where F swaps the two qutrit outputs, and

  C(A) = (1/2) P_+ (A tensor I) P_+.

This is a CPTP map M_3 -> M_9. Its Kraus operators are

  K_a = (1/sqrt(2)) P_+ (I tensor |a>),  a=0,1,2,

and direct multiplication gives sum_a K_a^* K_a=I. The output is invariant
under swap. Both marginals equal

  Phi(A) = (5 A + Tr(A) I)/8.

Thus Phi is UCP, Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint and positive, and self-compatible
by an actual full-domain channel. On the traceless operator subspace Phi has
eigenvalue 5/8.

For omega=exp(2*pi*i/3), take the computational basis and the three bases

  |a,k> = (1/sqrt(3)) sum_(j=0)^2 omega^(a j^2 + k j) |j>,
  a,k in {0,1,2}.

Their twelve rank-one projectors P_y form a complete qutrit MUB projective
2-design. The POVM effects are M_y=P_y/4, and prepare P_y after outcome y.
This gives the full-domain EB channel

  Psi(A) = (1/4) sum_y Tr(P_y A) P_y = (A + Tr(A) I)/4.

On traceless operators Psi has eigenvalue 1/4. Therefore, on all of M_3,

  I - Psi = 2 (I - Phi).

This is equality of superoperators, hence an exact Loewner comparison. It
shows a concrete EB rounding and that this example saturates c=2.

DUAL LOWER BOUND: NO EB ROUNDER CAN USE c<2 HERE

Let Lambda be any EB CPTP map satisfying I-Lambda <= c(I-Phi). Twirl Lambda
over conjugation by U(3). The twirl is still EB and CPTP, and averaging the
operator inequality preserves it because Phi is unitarily covariant. By
irreducibility of the traceless conjugation representation, the twirled map
is depolarizing:

  D_q(A)=q A+(1-q)Tr(A)I/3.

Its normalized Choi state is

  J_q=q |Omega><Omega|+(1-q) I_9/9,
  |Omega>=(1/sqrt(3)) sum_j |j,j>.

For a product state, overlap with |Omega> is at most 1/3 by Cauchy-Schwarz;
convexity gives <Omega|J_q|Omega> <=1/3 for every separable Choi state.
Hence q+(1-q)/9 <=1/3, so q<=1/4. On traceless matrices the assumed
comparison requires 1-q <= c(1-5/8)=3c/8. Since 1-q>=3/4, c>=2.
The displayed MUB channel attains equality. This dual uses a valid
separability witness, rather than a PPT relaxation.
