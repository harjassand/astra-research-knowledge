STRONGEST POST-EXPOSURE RESULT

In the standard adaptive black-box channel-query model (each call exposes only the channel output, not its environment), there exist exp(Ω(d²)) exact self-compatible channels on M_d which are already EB and each have a d-outcome rank-one measure-and-prepare representation, but any learner that must output a standalone description approximating the unknown channel requires Ω(d²/log d) channel queries at constant diamond error. The same lower bound holds at accuracy below 1/(8d) in the normalized infinity-to-one norm used in the campaign. This is a representation-acquisition theorem, not an obstruction when the unknown EB oracle itself may be retained and invoked.

The family is the hidden-basis dephasing channels Δ_U(X)=Σ_i Tr(P_i^U X)P_i^U, with P_i^U=U|i><i|U*. Each admits an exact broadcaster B_U(ρ)=Σ_i Tr(P_i^Uρ)P_i^U⊗P_i^U. For a relative Haar basis W, if all columns have a coordinate of squared mass at least 7/8, unitarity forces those coordinates to be a permutation; conditioning sequentially on the first floor(d/2) columns bounds the probability of this event by d!·8^(-floor(d/2)^2)=exp(-Ω(d²)). Random coding therefore gives exp(c d²) bases pairwise separated by >1/4 in unhalved diamond norm and >1/(4d) in normalized infinity-to-one norm. Each query can add at most 2 log₂d bits of information even with adaptive control and arbitrary ancilla, while Fano requires Ω(d²) bits to identify the codeword. Full derivation and explicit access-model caveat are in [BLACK_BOX_BASIS_ACQUISITION_LOWER.txt](revisions/BLACK_BOX_BASIS_ACQUISITION_LOWER.txt).

The executable [basis_packing_bound.py](revisions/basis_packing_bound.py) checks the finite logarithmic union-bound arithmetic for d=8,16,32,64,128. It does not verify Haar concentration or the symbolic information argument. The proof is internal and has not received external expert or priority review.

TOTAL-COST BOUNDARY

There are three distinct resource ledgers: (1) finite support size, with a dimension-polynomial existential cap and dimension-growing lower bounds; (2) precision after a POVM is supplied, where the c04_s03 perturbation proof removes rare-outcome inverse-probability blowup but still charges effect entries and input representation; and (3) acquisition from a bare channel or state oracle, where no general polynomial-time optimizer is known and the hidden-basis example gives an Ω(d²/log d) query lower bound for standalone reconstruction. A physical oracle can be a strictly better retained representation than its acquired classical POVM, so any claimed practical advantage must state which is supplied and charge comparable alternatives.

The general fixed-faithful-reference, arbitrary-selfcompatible-channel claim remains OPEN here. This work neither proves nor refutes universal I−Ψ≤c(I−Φ), does not settle broadcasting ⇒ q(E)→0, and does not make N46's qualitative compactness modulus constructive. The decisive remaining gate is an explicit access model for Φ, σ, and E together with a costed construction (or a general lower bound) for a POVM that meets the channel's required comparator. The black-box packing only addresses the standalone-description branch; it is bypassed by retaining the exact EB device.
