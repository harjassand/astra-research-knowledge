EXACT CONSTANT-SHEAR MIXED-BOUNDARY TOMOGRAPHY AND COST OBSTRUCTIONS
Companion to materials_inverse.txt. Internally derived proof, not external
validation or a claim of priority. All lengths/stresses nondimensionalized.

A. INTERFACE
Let B={x in R^3:|x|<1}, S=boundary B, n=x on S. Let mu>0 be constant,
lambda in C-infinity(cl B), and 3lambda+2mu>0. Define
  a=1/(lambda+2mu), so 0<a<3/(4mu).
The elastic equation is
  div[lambda(div u)I+2mu e(u)]=0,
  e(u)=(Du+Du^T)/2.
The input is two tangential displacement components and a scalar normal Robin
condition t_n+4mu u_n=g over all of S. The measured output is u_n over S,
or its specified weighted integrals. Dense spatial controls and sensing are
part of the interface. The unknown does not supply interior displacements,
transport frames, a complete DN matrix, or a FEM solver.

The spring coefficient 4mu is calibrated from a separate experiment if mu is
unknown. The model promise that mu is constant must be independently justified.

B. THE PHYSICAL CONSTRUCTION
Take any harmonic polynomial w. Choose known polynomial v with
  div v=0,    mu Delta v=-grad w.
For each homogeneous harmonic component w_ell, ell>=1, set
  v_ell=ell*x_vector*w_ell/[mu(2ell+3)(ell+1)]
        -(ell+3)*r^2*grad w_ell/[2mu(2ell+3)(ell+1)].
Set v_0=0 and sum. Euler's identity gives
  div(x_vector w_ell)=(ell+3)w_ell,
  div(r^2 grad w_ell)=2ell w_ell,
  Delta(x_vector w_ell)=2grad w_ell,
  Delta(r^2 grad w_ell)=(4ell+2)grad w_ell.
Substitution proves the two required properties. No lambda is used.

Solve the scalar Dirichlet Poisson problem
  Delta phi=a w in B,  phi=0 on S,
and put u=v+grad phi. Let d=div u=a w. Then
  L u = mu Delta u+grad[(lambda+mu)div u]
       = mu Delta v+mu grad(a w)+grad[(1-mu a)w]
       = -grad w+grad w=0.
This is exact for arbitrary smooth a in the stated interval.

Let q=partial_n phi. Since phi=0 on S, grad phi=q n there. For any tangent
vector tau, differentiation of grad phi along the sphere gives
  Hess phi(tau,n)=partial_tau q,
  tr_tangent Hess phi=2q,
  Hess phi(n,n)-Delta phi=-2q.
Consequently
  stress(grad phi)n
  = [w-4mu q]n + 2mu grad_S q.
Therefore
  u_t=v_t,
  t_n+4mu u_n=(2mu e(v)n)_n+w+4mu v_n.
These controls only use w, mu, and geometry. They are the claimed mixed
boundary experiment, and output q=u_n-v_n.

Uniqueness: for a difference with zero controls, u_t=0 and t_n=-4mu u_n.
Testing the equilibrium against u gives
  integral_B [lambda(div u)^2+2mu|e(u)|^2]
    +4mu integral_S |u_n|^2=0.
Positive elastic energy forces e(u)=0, hence u is rigid, and the trace conditions
force it to be zero. The coercive weak form on the space of H1 vector fields
with zero tangential trace gives existence by Lax--Milgram, or the constructed
solution itself supplies existence for these particular controls. Thus no
uncontrolled second solution is selected by the experiment.

For harmonic h, Green's second identity gives
  integral_B a w h = integral_B h Delta phi = integral_S h q.
This is the exact linear observable Q_a(w,h). The nonlinear reciprocal lambda
is applied only after a is recovered.

C. ACQUIRING CONSTANT mu
Impose full boundary displacement f=A x with A=diag(1,-1,0). The interior
solution u=A x is divergence free and harmonic for every admissible lambda,
and traction is 2mu A n. Hence
  integral_S traction dot f = (16pi/3)mu,
  mu=(3/(16pi))*integral_S traction dot f.
This is one additional known shear pattern and one integrated scalar statistic.
Calibration error and control error are separate from the exact noiseless claim.

D. ONE PRESSURE PATTERN FOR QUADRATIC a
Take w=2+x, with x the first coordinate. Its v is
  v=x_vector*x/(10mu)-r^2 e_x/(5mu).
On S, v_n=-x/(10mu), v_t=-(e_x-xn)/(5mu), and
  (2mu e(v)n)_n+w+4mu v_n=2+x/5.
Thus the experiment is
  u_t=-(e_x-xn)/(5mu),  t_n+4mu u_n=2+x/5,
with q=u_n+x/(10mu).

Promise a belongs to P_2, dimension 10. In the script's order the monomials are
  [1,z,z^2,y,yz,y^2,x,xz,xy,x^2].
The ten harmonic sensors are
  [1,x,y,z,xy,xz,yz,x^2-y^2,x^2-z^2,
   x^3-(3/2)x(y^2+z^2)].
Define the exact rational matrix
  M_ij=integral_B (2+x) p_j h_i,
where p_j is the monomial list. All moments of odd monomials vanish and
  integral_B x^(2a)y^(2b)z^(2c)
   =4pi (2a-1)!!(2b-1)!!(2c-1)!! / [2(a+b+c)+3]!!.
The acquired data m_i=integral_S q h_i satisfy m=M c. Exact rational arithmetic
gives det(M/pi)=268435456/95761000986328125 !=0. Hence c=M^(-1)m.
This proves the stated uniform reconstruction on the promised class, not only
reconstruction of the script's single example.

An independent structural injectivity proof for the full q output is useful.
For any polynomial F of degree d, the unique solution Delta phi=F, phi|S=0 is
polynomial of the form phi=(r^2-1)p, p in P_d. Indeed Delta[(r^2-1)p] defines an
endomorphism of P_d whose kernel is zero by Dirichlet uniqueness; dimension
then makes it onto. If F=(c+x)a with a in P_2 and c!=0, phi has degree <=5.
If also q=0, phi is divisible by (r^2-1)^2, so
  phi=(r^2-1)^2[A+B x+C y+D z].
Its Laplacian is
  F=(20r^2-12)(A+B x+C y+D z)+8(r^2-1)(B x+C y+D z).
Divisibility by c+x forces F to vanish on the plane x=-c. Comparing y,z
coefficients yields C=D=0. Comparing the y^2+z^2 term yields A=(7/5)c B;
the remaining constant gives (16/5)c B=0, hence A=B=0. Thus a=0.
The full one-load response is injective on P_2. The exact finite matrix above
identifies ten sufficient projections of that response.

Sensor/basis conditioning: divide every h_i by its L2(S) norm. The corresponding
matrix has smallest numerical singular value 0.0010137473 and inverse 2-norm
986.4391 in the monomial parameter basis. For sensor noise vector eta,
  ||c_hat-c||_2 <=986.4392 ||eta||_2.
Since every monomial has absolute value <=1 in B,
  ||a_hat-a||_infinity <=sqrt(10)*986.4392 ||eta||_2.
If a_hat and a are at least a_min>0,
  ||lambda_hat-lambda||_infinity
    <= a_min^(-2)||a_hat-a||_infinity +2|mu_hat-mu|,
plus the response/control error caused by imperfect calibration. This is not an
error bound that permits waiving the latter control error. The numerical matrix
values are evidence in the stated basis, not exact interval-certified constants.

E. SHARP ONE-LOAD CUBIC FAILURE
The P_2 assumption cannot be replaced by all cubic polynomials. Define
  psi=(40x^2+165x-2y^2-2z^2+164)/40,
  b=3[126x^3+133x^2+126xy^2+126xz^2-98x
        +133y^2+133z^2-79]/10.
Direct differentiation gives
  (2+x)b=Delta[(r^2-1)^2 psi].
Take a_+=a0+epsilon b, a_-=a0-epsilon b with a0>0 and epsilon sufficiently
small to keep 0<a_±<3/(4mu). Their Poisson potentials differ by
  2epsilon(r^2-1)^2 psi,
which has both boundary value and normal derivative zero. Thus their entire
measured normal response q, and consequently boundary displacements and the
known imposed normal Robin condition, agree for w=2+x. This is an exact
nonlinear lambda counterexample for this measurement interface.
Tangential tractions also agree: they equal the known v traction plus
2mu grad_S q. Consequently even recording the entire traction vector for this
particular experiment does not distinguish these two materials. Other loads
can distinguish them, consistent with full-DN uniqueness.

F. BALANCED FINITE-BANDWIDTH TOMOGRAPHY
If all harmonic w and h of degrees <=L are used, Q determines every moment of
a against a polynomial of degree <=2L. Products of these harmonic polynomials
span P_(2L). Here is the classical harmonic-algebra mechanism behind that claim.
In degree 2m, the product (x+iy)^m(x-iy)^m=(x^2+y^2)^m belongs to the allowed
product span. On S its zonal profile is (1-z^2)^m. Every even harmonic degree
2j, 0<=j<=m, has a nonzero Legendre projection, since
  integral_-1^1 (1-t^2)^m P_(2j)(t)dt
  =(-1)^j [(2j)!/(4^j(j!)^2)] B(1/2,m+1)
     * (m+1-j)_j/(m+3/2)_j !=0.
The rotation orbit spans all those irreducible harmonic components, namely
P_(2m)^hom=H_(2m) + r^2 H_(2m-2)+...+r^(2m)H_0. In odd degree 2m+1, use
(x+iy)^m[z(x-iy)^m], both factors harmonic. Its profile is t(1-t^2)^m and
  integral_-1^1 t(1-t^2)^m P_(2j+1)(t)dt
  =(-1)^j [(2j+1)!/(4^j(j!)^2)] B(3/2,m+1)
     * (m+1-j)_j/(m+5/2)_j !=0.
Its orbit spans the odd harmonic decomposition. For any degree <=2L, the
factor degrees floor(d/2),ceil(d/2) are <=L. Summing homogeneous degrees proves
span. The representation facts here are standard spherical harmonic algebra,
not a new OpenAI lemma or a new novelty claim.

If a is promised in P_(2L), the moment Gram matrix is positive definite, so
reconstruction is a finite linear solve. Number of loads is (L+1)^2; symmetric
data at most (L+1)^2[(L+1)^2+1]/2; number of unknown coefficients binomial(2L+3,3).
No optimal load count or uniform condition number is asserted for general L.

There is exact blindness outside that promise. A harmonic polynomial h_(2L+1)
is L2(B)-orthogonal to every polynomial of degree <=2L, so a0±epsilon h_(2L+1)
produce equal data. For L=1, mu=1, a0=1/3, epsilon=1/10, choose
  h_3=x^3-3xy^2,  |h_3|<=1 on B.
Then the ten balanced degree-one data agree and
  ||lambda_+-lambda_-||_infinity
   =1/(1/3-1/10)-1/(1/3+1/10)=180/91.
This is different from the cubic nullspace of the biased affine one-load
protocol. The latter remains invisible even when all output harmonics are read.

G. EXPONENTIAL DEPTH OBSTRUCTION FOR FULL STATIC DN DATA
Let lambda_±=1/a_±-2mu with a_±=a0±beta chi(r)Y_N(omega). Choose fixed smooth
chi supported in r1<r<r0<1, sup norm 1, and Y_N a real degree-N spherical
harmonic of sup norm 1. Then a_± and lambda_± are smooth and agree near all
of S. Small fixed beta preserves positive energy and uniform L-infinity bounds.
Their L-infinity coefficient separation is order beta.

For every elastic solution at constant mu, let w=(lambda+2mu)div u. Taking
divergence of L u=0 gives Delta w=0. Standard weak elliptic energy estimates,
using bounded coefficients and positive energy (no derivative bounds), yield
  ||w||_L2(B) <= C_pressure ||f||_H^(1/2)(S).
The exact Alessandrini identity simplifies to
  <(Lambda_+-Lambda_-)f,g>
   =integral_B (lambda_+-lambda_-) (div u_+^f)(div u_-^g)
   =integral_B (a_--a_+) w_+^f w_-^g.
Thus the operator norm is bounded by C_pressure^2 times the norm of harmonic
Bergman multiplication by a_+-a_-.

Let Y_ell,m be an L2(S)-orthonormal spherical harmonic basis and
  e_ell,m=sqrt(2ell+3) r^ell Y_ell,m,
an orthonormal basis of harmonic L2(B). For b=beta chi Y_N,
  <b e_ell,m,e_k,j>=0 if ell+k<N
by orthogonality of degree-N harmonics to lower-degree polynomials. For s=ell+k,
  |<b e_ell,m,e_k,j>|
  <= beta sqrt((2ell+3)(2k+3))*r0^(s+3)/(s+3)
  <= beta r0^(s+3).
For each s, the number of such entries is
  sum_ell=0^s (2ell+1)(2(s-ell)+1)
   =(s+1)[2(s+1)^2+1]/3 <=(s+1)^3.
Consequently
  ||T_b||^2 <=||T_b||_HS^2
   <=beta^2 r0^6 sum_s>=N (s+1)^3 r0^(2s)
   <=beta^2 r0^(2N+6)(N+1)^3
      (1+4r0^2+r0^4)/(1-r0^2)^4.
Taking square roots and replacing b by a_+-a_-=2b proves the bound in the
main report. This proof uses no release uniqueness result.

For a fixed finite regularity order m, choose
  Y_N=Re[(omega_x+i omega_y)^N],  beta_N=c N^(-m).
All derivatives of order <=m on the fixed annulus grow at most O_m(N^m), so
a_± and lambda_± have uniform C^m bounds for sufficiently small fixed c.
The coefficient separation is order N^(-m), while the DN difference is at most
C N^(3/2-m)r0^N. Thus any uniform modulus of inverse continuity for that class
must be at least order [log(1/delta)]^(-m) along this sequence. No uniform
Holder or Lipschitz guarantee follows from full smooth uniqueness.

For noisy scalar probes, each returns <Lambda f,g>+Z with independent
Z~N(0,sigma^2), ||f||_H^(1/2),||g||_H^(1/2)<=1. The next probe may depend on
all previous results. If the two operator means differ by at most delta_N for
every admissible pair, the conditional Gaussian KL contribution is <=
delta_N^2/(2sigma^2) per probe. Chain-rule KL is <=K delta_N^2/(2sigma^2).
To have equal-prior testing error <=1/4, total variation must be >=1/2.
Pinsker then requires KL>=1/2, hence K>=sigma^2/delta_N^2. This is a lower
bound in the declared sensing-energy/noise model; it does not describe every
hardware noise model or waive actuator amplitude.

H. A ROUTINE QUANTITATIVE EXTENSION OF THE RELEASE'S FINITE-TYPE STEP
The inspected source finishes coefficient recovery using
  eta_ij=sum_a B_ij^a eta_a+delta_ij q.
For an approximate relation add residual F_ij. Put v_a=eta_a. Fix i!=s.
Commuting third derivatives gives
  partial_s q=sum_b C_s^b v_b+d_s q+R_s,
where C,d are exactly the source's finite-type coefficients, and
  R_s=partial_i F_si-partial_s F_ii
      +sum_a [B_si^a F_ai-B_ii^a F_as].
Also
  partial_s v_a=sum_b B_as^b v_b+delta_as q+F_as.
Along a path of length D, ordinary Gronwall therefore bounds (v,q) by
  exp(CD) [initial norm + D*C'(||F||_C1)],
with C,C' controlled by ||B||_C1. One further integration controls eta.
This independently proves stability of this final coefficient stage when its
interior residual and exterior initial values are already supplied. It does
not acquire F or B from finite noisy boundary measurements. Making this small
lemma quantitative leaves the expensive boundary-to-transport step unresolved.
