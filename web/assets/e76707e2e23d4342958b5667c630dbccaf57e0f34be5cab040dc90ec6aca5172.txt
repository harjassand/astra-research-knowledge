Scientific outcome

The dimension-free implication remains UNKNOWN:

    C(E_n) := sup_prior chi(E_n) <= C < infinity,
    b_2(E_n) -> 0
        implies e_EB(E_n) -> 0 ?

Here E_n is any finite-dimensional quantum experiment, T(ρ,σ)=||ρ−σ||_1/2,
b_2 is the infimum over one CPTP map A→A⊗A of the worst trace error in
both individual marginals over every state in E_n, and e_EB is the infimum
of the worst trace error over one full-domain entanglement-breaking
measure-and-prepare channel. No common-reference or dimension bound is part
of the target. This work found neither a proof nor a counterexample satisfying
all of these premises. The strongest banked adjacent results are exact
factor-two EB Dirichlet comparators for full-O(d)-covariant channels and
spin-2 SU(2)-covariant channels, the arbitrary-spin constant-capture cone,
logarithmic score-rank, rank-two, and full-qubit noncovariant cases, and an
all-mode coherent squared EB comparison for every exactly SU(2)-covariant
bistochastic marginal with an actual common two-output UCP extension. Its
universal comparison constant is finite but non-effective; sharp factor two
and the unrestricted bounded-Holevo implication remain open. These results
accompany a precise dual
separation formulation of the unrestricted Dirichlet gate and independent
falsification of several tempting transfer routes.

The surviving proof gate is a dimension-free common-measurement/trace lift.
After the faithful-reference and flagged log-bin reductions recorded in N93,
a small two-output broadcasting defect controls each state's positive
self-compatible likelihood-channel Dirichlet energy. Uniform EB reconstruction
is equivalent by a compact-convex minimax characterization to controlling a
least-favourable prior's joint pure-state posterior variance with ONE common
POVM. The missing statement is a uniform lift from the individual
Dirichlet-energy bounds to that common-POVM variance (or an equivalent EB
Dirichlet comparison). N92 proves such a comparison for supplied instrument
families and several explicit channels, not for every compatible channel.

A. A bounded-Holevo experiment can have exponentially large trace covers

Fix d divisible by four and 0<a<1. For every balanced sign vector
s∈{−1,+1}^d with sum_j s_j=0, define the faithful diagonal state

    ρ_s = diag((1+a s_1)/d, ..., (1+a s_d)/d).

All states have the same spectrum. Put

    c(a) = 1/2 [(1+a) ln(1+a) + (1−a) ln(1−a)].

Each entropy is S(ρ_s)=ln d−c(a). Every prior has average state \barρ with
S(\barρ)≤ln d, so χ≤c(a); the uniform prior over all balanced vectors has
\barρ=I/d and reaches equality. Thus the Holevo capacity is exactly c(a),
independent of d (in nats).

For two sign vectors s,t, their half trace distance is

    T(ρ_s,ρ_t) = (a/d) Hamming(s,t).

The balanced vectors number binom(d,d/2)≥2^d/(d+1). A greedy code that
deletes all remaining vectors at Hamming distance below d/4 at each selection
produces a subfamily of size at least

    binom(d,d/2) / sum_{j≤d/4} binom(d,j)
      ≥ exp((ln 2−h(1/4))d−2 ln(d+1)),

where h(u)=−u ln u−(1−u)ln(1−u). Since d is divisible by four, the displayed
integer endpoints are exact.
Its distinct members have trace distance at least a/4. Hence for covering
radius a/10, the trace covering number is exponential in d although C(E)=c(a)
is fixed. (A finite greedy enumeration for d=8,10,12,14,16 is included with
the runnable check.)

This is not a counterexample to the target. The computational-basis dephasing
channel fixes every ρ_s and is EB, so e_EB=0. The channel

    B(ω) = sum_j <j|ω|j> |jj><jj|

has both marginals equal to the dephasing of ω; it broadcasts every ρ_s
exactly, so b_2=0. The example proves that bounded Holevo capacity cannot
replace N46's fixed-radius covering hypothesis by a dimension-free cover
bound. It also makes clear why a successful theorem must detect noncommuting
complexity rather than raw metric entropy.

Proof status: the capacity, distance, commutativity, EB, and broadcasting
identities are exact. The packing lower bound is the standard greedy-code
count proved above. Novelty/priority is not claimed. Finite enumeration is
only a diagnostic, not the proof.

B. Pair broadcasting amplifies to logarithmically many receivers

Let B:A→A⊗A be any CPTP broadcaster and let Λ_0,Λ_1 be its two marginal
channels. Suppose sup_{ρ∈E} T(Λ_i(ρ),ρ)≤ε for i=0,1. Define B^[0]=id and
recursively

    B^[h+1] = (B^[h] ⊗ B^[h]) ◦ B.

For each leaf of the depth-h binary tree, its marginal channel is a composition
Λ_{i_h}⋯Λ_{i_1}. For every ρ∈E, trace-distance contraction gives

    T(Λ_{i_h}⋯Λ_{i_1}(ρ),ρ) ≤ h ε.

Indeed, if T(Γ(ρ),ρ)≤r then
T(Λ_iΓ(ρ),ρ)≤T(Γ(ρ),ρ)+T(Λ_i(ρ),ρ)≤r+ε.
Discarding leaves yields any K≤2^h outputs. Randomly permuting the retained
outputs, if equal marginal errors are desired, cannot increase their maximum
error by convexity. Therefore

    b_K(E) ≤ ceil(log_2 K) b_2(E),

where b_K is the optimal worst marginal error for K receivers. An
ε-approximate minimizer gives the same inequality with an arbitrarily small
additive slack before taking the infimum. The construction calls B exactly
2^h−1 times and creates K output registers of dimension d each; circuit size,
ancillas, memory, and channel-description acquisition are additional costs.

This is an operational consequence, not an EB theorem. It matches the
log_2(K)/L phase on the N61 copy ladder to first order; N67 supplies sharper
lower bounds and fixed-K constants for that family. No claim of independent
clones or product output is made. Primary-source priority is unassessed; the
proof here is self-contained.

For arbitrary K, one can balance the tree to minimize average leaf depth,
then randomly permute its K outputs. If 2^r≤K≤2^(r+1), the minimum mean
depth is

    phi(K) = r + 2 − 2^(r+1)/K.

Indeed the optimal full binary tree has 2^(r+1)−K leaves at depth r and
2K−2^(r+1) at depth r+1. Trace-distance convexity after a uniform output
permutation gives the sharper bound b_K≤min{1,phi(K)b_2}. The channel still
uses K−1 invocations of B and log_2(K!) random-seed bits for exact
symmetrization. N67's N61 ladder has L b_K→phi(K) for each fixed K, so this
receiver factor is sharp there to first order.

C. Capacity-sensitive finite-label reconstruction

For a finite family E={ρ_1,...,ρ_m}, m≥2, with C(E)≤C, receiver
amplification plus an adaptive Holevo-information argument gives

    e_EB(E) ≤ inf_{K≥1} [phi(K)b_2(E) + sqrt(m C/(2K))].

The proof is as follows. Fix any prior p on the m labels, and pass the input
through a K-receiver broadcaster with per-marginal error at most ε_K. Measure
the receivers sequentially. Given past outcomes h, write p_x(h) for the
posterior, σ_x^h for the conditional state of receiver j, and
τ_h=Σ_x p_x(h)σ_x^h. Set d_x(h)=T(σ_x^h,τ_h). Choose x_h maximizing
p_x(h)d_x(h)^2, then measure receiver j with the Helstrom binary test for
σ_{x_h}^h against τ_h. If r_x^h and r^h are the outcome laws under σ_x^h
and τ_h, classical Pinsker in nats yields

    g_h := Σ_x p_x(h)D(r_x^h||r^h)
       ≥ 2 p_{x_h}(h)d_{x_h}(h)^2
       ≥ (2/m)(Σ_x p_x(h)d_x(h))^2.

Average over histories to obtain g_j=I(X:Y_j|Y_1,...,Y_{j−1}). The chain
rule, Holevo data processing, and the capacity bound give Σ_{j=1}^K g_j≤C,
so one j has g_j≤C/K. Jensen then bounds the prior-average posterior
distance at that stage by sqrt(mC/(2K)). Measuring the prefix and preparing
τ_h is a prior-dependent EB channel whose average error is at most this term
plus ε_K. For every prior the same upper bound holds, though the channel may
depend on the prior. Finite-dimensional minimax over the compact convex EB
channels supplies one channel with worst-label error bounded by the same
quantity. Inserting ε_K≤phi(K)b_2 proves the displayed inequality. This is
an existence proof, not an efficient acquisition algorithm.

For a trace-distance a-net of E of size N_E(a), apply the finite-label
theorem to the net and use trace contraction to extend its decoder. This gives

    e_EB(E) ≤ 2a + phi(K)b_2(E)
                   + sqrt(N_E(a) C(E)/(2K)).

Therefore if C(E_n)≤C_0 and there are a_n→0 with
b_2(E_n)ln N_{E_n}(a_n)→0, then e_EB(E_n)→0. One can choose K_n of order
N_{E_n}(a_n)(C_0+1)/b_2(E_n)^2; since phi(K)≤log_2K+1 and
b_2 ln(1/b_2)→0, all three terms vanish. This leaves the general target
open because bounded Holevo capacity does not control N_E(a), as the
balanced-sign family in Section A demonstrates.

D. Fixed-d benchmark and exact source-derived boundaries

For a fixed input dimension d, vanishing b_2 does imply vanishing e_EB, with
no Holevo premise: take a subsequence of near-optimal broadcasters and a
convergent subsequence in the compact set of CPTP maps A→A⊗A. Every pair of
cluster states from the experiment is then exactly broadcast by the limiting
map, hence commutes by the exact no-broadcasting theorem. The full cluster set
is pairwise commuting and has one common eigenbasis. Dephasing in that basis
is EB and approximates the sequence uniformly, since the state sets are
compact in fixed dimension. This is a qualitative compactness proof; it gives
no modulus uniform in d. The primary literature audit found the exact
no-broadcasting result of Barnum, Caves, Fuchs, Jozsa, and Schumacher
(arXiv:quant-ph/9511010, submitted 8 Nov 1995; PRL 76 (1996), 2818–2821).

N46 gives the dimension-free route when each fixed-radius trace net has size
at most N_n(a):

    e_EB(E_n) ≤ 2a + inf_{L≥0}
       {L b_2(E_n) + sqrt(N_n(a) ln N_n(a)/(2·2^L))}.

Thus a_n→0 and b_2(E_n) ln N_n(a_n)→0 suffice. The balanced-sign family
shows that C(E_n)≤C does not imply such a net bound, even though this family
itself is already exactly classical.

N61 is not a qualifying bounded-capacity counterexample: its Haar Holevo
capacity grows at least (2^(L−1)/L) ln L under the cited dimension choice,
while b_2→0 and e_EB→1. N69 proves an EB reconstruction bound for the scoped
bounded-capacity pure uniform ladder, but leaves the general mixed-family
target open. N93's faithful smoothing, flagged centralization, Petz correction,
common-measurement minimax, and collective Clifford witnesses remain
source-derived candidates / limitations as recorded in its card; none closes
the global target.

E. Stronger operational routes that fail without extra structure

Approximate Markov recovery from an arbitrary supplied broadcaster is invalid.
A full-domain exact broadcaster can preserve each marginal while placing
one bit of conditional mutual information between receivers; the family can
still have an exact EB simulator. The broadcaster's output correlation is
therefore not the missing invariant. Likewise, N93's bounded-capacity
Clifford/Pauli family has affinity gap tending to zero and EB error bounded
away from zero, but its pair-broadcast error has a positive lower bound. It
refutes pair-gap and accessible-information shortcuts, not the target.

For self-compatible channels, N92's explicit Kraus/instrument SOS gives
Dirichlet domination and an EB decoder when the instrument and reference data
are supplied. The unrestricted positive tracial self-adjoint self-compatible
channel case still lacks a common-POVM comparison. A fixed spectral gap could
control projection onto the fixed-point algebra, but the target permits
arbitrarily slow classical modes, so a gap assumption changes the problem.

The audited N93 reduction is stronger than a bare smoothing heuristic. Given
a supplied capacity bound C_0, it constructs a faithful reference sandwich
after compatible smoothing and affine dilution, then a flagged centralizer
with at most d+1 reference-log-bin shifts and no family-label multiplier in
the decoder error. The fixed-sandwich flag distortion is
O((b ln(1/b))^(1/12)); starting from bounded capacity and fixing the smoothing
parameter gives O_{C_0,eta,theta}(b^(1/96)ln(1/b)^(1/12)), with an exponentially
large constant in C_0/eta. Petz correction makes the flagged marginal
positive, tracial-selfadjoint, self-compatible, and small in full trace norm.
These are inherited reduction steps, not the EB conclusion: the flagged
family can still have an uncontrolled trace cover, and the corrected map
still needs a dimension-free common-POVM / EB lift.

F. Independent all-dimension O(d)-covariant reconstruction and prior comparison

Sol's independent bottleneck attack supplies an all-dimension reconstruction
for a multiplicity-free covariance class. For every d≥2, let Φ:M_d→M_d be a
unital, completely positive, normalized-trace-preserving map, covariant
under the full real orthogonal group X↦UXU^T, and actually self-compatible
through a UCP joint map
J:M_d⊗M_d→M_d with J(X⊗I)=J(I⊗X)=Φ(X). Then a single canonical
O(d)-orbit rank-one measure-and-prepare channel Ψ satisfies

    I−Ψ ≤ 2(I−Φ)

on the whole matrix algebra, with the constant 2 sharp for every d.

Full O(d) covariance makes the channel selfadjoint on normalized-trace L2
with real sector eigenvalues λ_s,λ_a; Kadison–Schwarz gives |λ_l|≤1. The
proof works for negative as well as nonnegative eigenvalues, so positivity
of Φ as an L2 operator is not required. A compatible EB depolarizing
negative-spectrum control is Φ_−(X)=(d Tr(X)I−X)/(d²−1), with
λ_s=λ_a=−1/(d²−1).

The previous internal artifact c04_s01/FINAL.txt (7 October 2026) proves the
same factor-two conclusion for the larger signed-permutation-covariant class
under its stated nonnegative-L2-spectrum assumption, and supplies a
four-orbit EB selector. It therefore subsumes the nonnegative-spectrum part
of this O(d) channel-class result. A source-comparison leaf checked that
c04's active-set inequalities appear algebraically to extend to signed
eigenvalues, but c04 was not formally reissued under that broader statement.
The present proof explicitly handles signed O(d)-sector eigenvalues and may
simplify the selector to one pure orbit, with exact all-d O(d) star maxima.
Novelty and priority remain UNKNOWN; neither internal artifact has external
proof validation.

The traceless operator space splits into the irreducible, inequivalent
orthogonal sectors S=Sym_0 and A=Λ², of dimensions
n_s=(d−1)(d+2)/2 and n_a=d(d−1)/2. Covariance makes Φ scalar with
eigenvalues λ_s,λ_a. For normalized maximally-entangled pair projectors P
and swaps F, completeness of τ-orthonormal Hermitian bases gives

    G_s=Σ_{H∈S}H^T⊗H=(d²/2)P+(d/2)F−I,
    G_a=Σ_{H∈A}H^T⊗H=(d²/2)P−(d/2)F.

On RAB the corresponding star operators are
W_s=(d²/2)(P_RA+P_RB)+(d/2)(F_RA+F_RB)−2I and
W_a=(d²/2)(P_RA+P_RB)−(d/2)(F_RA+F_RB). The full star gives
(d+2)λ_s+dλ_a≤d+2. The exact all-d contraction-span calculation gives
λ_max(W_a)=d²/2 and
λ_max(W_s)=L_+(d)=[d²+2d−8+d√(d²+4d+20)]/4. Therefore

    λ_a≤d/[2(d−1)],
    λ_s≤L_+(d)/[(d−1)(d+2)]≤(d+4)/[2(d+2)].

For a unit vector ψ, write z=|ψ^Tψ|². The orbit channel
Ψ_z(X)=d∫_{O(d)}Tr(P_U X)P_U dU, P_U=U|ψ⟩⟨ψ|U^T,
is EB and has sector eigenvalues
μ_a=(1−z)/(d−1), μ_s=[d(1+z)−2]/[(d−1)(d+2)]. Put
u_l=(2λ_l−1)_+ and choose z=1−(d−1)u_a. The antisymmetric star bound
ensures z∈[0,1]. The symmetric individual bound gives u_s≤2/(d+2), and
the full-star constraint gives (d+2)u_s+d u_a≤2 when both are active;
the one-active cases follow from the individual bounds. Hence
μ_a=u_a and μ_s=(2−d u_a)/(d+2)≥u_s, proving the operator comparison.
An explicit seed realizing every z is
ψ_z=√((1+√z)/2)e_1+i√((1−√z)/2)e_2.

The sharp control is the universal symmetric 1→2 d-level cloner, whose
marginal depolarizing eigenvalue is (d+2)/[2(d+1)]. Its canonical Haar EB
comparator has eigenvalue 1/(d+1), giving exact equality in the factor-two
comparison. Twirling any competing EB bistochastic comparator over U(d)
and applying the separable Choi overlap bound forces c≥2. This is an
equally-informed control and related depolarizing factor-two results are
already present in N92; no novelty claim is made for the cloner or the
compatibility region. A primary comparison located exact Brauer-state
(1,2)-extendibility regions in Solymos–Jakab–Zimborás (arXiv:2411.04597),
which must be treated as prior art for the compatibility geometry.

This result does not close the global target. Full O(d) covariance is a
substantive promise; twirling a general broadcaster changes its behavior on
a non-invariant experiment. N93's generic flagged centralizer is only
covariant under the reference stabilizer, not under O(d). A separate
conditional corollary is available when the experiment and tracial
reference are O(d)-invariant and the corrected marginal meets the factor
theorem's exact hypotheses; the smoothing-flags audit gives its charged
error propagation. A generic covariance residual is not controlled by
bounded Holevo capacity and b_2 alone.

The spin-1 proof received an independent algebra audit; the spin-3/2 matrix
certificate was independently rebuilt exactly; two independent audits of
the positive-spectrum O(d) contraction-span proof passed with the exact
matrix checker; and an independent audit verified the signed-eigenvalue
extension.
These are internal checks; none validates the global target or replaces
external proof review.

G. Finite-factor consequences and sharper falsification boundaries

For a supplied finite tensor product of factors with positive
selfadjoint contractions Φ_i and EB comparators Ψ_i satisfying
I−Ψ_i≤2(I−Φ_i), one has Φ_i≤(I+Ψ_i)/2. Tensor order and the scalar
inequality

    ∏_{i=1}^m (1+x_i)/2 ≤ (1+∏_{i=1}^m x_i)/2,  0≤x_i≤1,

give I−⊗_iΨ_i≤2(I−⊗_iΦ_i). To prove the scalar inequality, expand the
product and pair each monomial x_S with its complement x_{S^c}; each pair
satisfies x_S+x_{S^c}≤1+∏_i x_i. The product EB comparator therefore
controls the entire tensor-product algebra, including entangled observables
and inputs. Convex mixtures, simultaneous unitary conjugates and
block-preserving weighted direct sums preserve the same constant. The scalar
inequality 1−∏x_i≤Σ_i(1−x_i) gives the operator order
I−⊗_iΦ_i≤Σ_i(I−Φ_i⊗I_{others}). For a tracial perturbation H with
||H||∞≤θ, define local Dirichlet energies
q_i=⟨H,(I−Φ_i⊗I_{others})H⟩ and local trace losses
r_i=||(I−Φ_i⊗I_{others})H||_{1,τ}. Then

    T(Ψ(ρ_H),ρ_H) ≤ (1/2)√(2 Σ_i q_i)
                    ≤ (1/2)√(2θ Σ_i r_i).

This is a closure theorem for supplied factors, not a factorization theorem
for arbitrary compatible channels. See
work/programmes/classicality/leaves/followup_tensor_closure.txt for the
full scope and block-coherence conditions.

Several independent routes sharpen, but do not close, the unrestricted
gate. First, the finite-label bound admits the sharper parameter
Γ(E)=max_π (Σ_x√π_x)^2 χ(π,E):

    e_EB(E) ≤ inf_K [φ(K)b_2(E)+√(Γ(E)/(2K))].

This improves the mC term by posterior effective support, but Holevo
capacity alone does not bound Γ(E) dimension-free. Signed Pauli labels
show that the m scaling in the original extraction step is necessary in
order; they do not furnish a sequence with b_2→0.

Second, finite-Heisenberg covariance gives an exact non-SU(2) control: the
qutrit universal cloner marginal has a Haar EB comparator with
I−Ψ=2(I−Φ), and c=2 is sharp against all EB bistochastic comparators.
More generally, if arbitrary multiplicities are allowed, a universal
Weyl-covariant c=2 theorem would imply the unrestricted c=2 lift by
embedding any channel Γ as Depol_d⊗Γ and compressing the EB comparator.
Thus multiplicity-bearing covariance does not itself reduce the missing
operator-algebra problem.

Third, the exact spin-2 SU(2) orbit is a two-dimensional moment triangle.
An explicit CP-plus-all-star-inequalities point lies outside the downward
pure-orbit region, but no common compatible extension was constructed; it
is a relaxation gap, not a counterexample. The actual spin-2 universal
cloner is rounded at c=2 by an exact orbit seed and by an exact mixture of
three simple orbit seeds. Higher-spin star maxima can be sharp separately
without being simultaneously attainable by one channel, so combining
rankwise constraints as if independent is invalid.

Finally, two tempting flagged data-hiding constructions have explicit EB
simulators with vanishing error. A fixed finite seed alphabet is learned
from its high-copy sectors; an exponentially large Pauli alphabet scaled
to keep Holevo capacity bounded admits a constant-reference EB decoder of
error O(log L/L). A conditional weighted-flag estimate also closes if a
uniform relative-entropy radius certificate is supplied, but bounded
Holevo capacity has not been shown to supply that certificate. Each route
is documented under work/programmes/classicality/leaves/followup_*.txt.

Costs, inputs, and controls

The balanced-sign example supplies the diagonal basis and exact parameter a;
no basis-learning or channel-acquisition claim is made. Its finite code
selection uses enumeration and has exponential family size. The amplification
lemma assumes an explicit broadcaster B; it does not charge the cost of
finding or representing an optimal B. The global target charges only
existence of a full-domain channel in its current mathematical formulation;
any algorithmic version must additionally charge the Holevo center, faithful
smoothing, flagged reference eigenvalues, POVM construction, precision, and
runtime. The strongest controls are exact commuting families (e_EB=0), the
N61 unbounded-capacity pure ladder, N69's bounded-capacity pure ladder,
and N93's Clifford family with nonvanishing b_2.

H. Exact dual geometry of the unrestricted c=2 Dirichlet gate

There is a concise equivalent inequality whose proof would close the missing
tracial lift. This is a reformulation, not a result about its truth.

Let H_0={A=A*:tau(A)=0} with real inner product <A,B>=tau(AB),
where tau=Tr/d. Let Q be any positive semidefinite operator on H_0 and write
Q=Σ_i |A_i><A_i| with A_i in H_0. Thus Tr_H0 Q=Σ_i tau(A_i^2). Define

  h_1(Q)=sup_{(p_y,P_y)} Σ_y p_y Σ_i [Tr(P_y A_i)]^2,

where P_y are rank-one projections, p_y≥0, Σp_y=1, and
Σ_y p_y P_y=I/d. It suffices to take finite ensembles; compactness and
Caratheodory give a finite representative. This is the support function of
the compact convex set of canonical pure-state 1-design frame operators

  Ψ(X)=d Σ_y p_y Tr(P_y X)P_y.

Each such Ψ is UCP, trace-preserving, EB and positive selfadjoint on L2(tau),
and

M. Sharp arbitrary-d star inequality on the constant-pure-capture cone

Sol has supplied a separate analytic theorem in
outputs/research/sol_classicality/CONSTANT_CAPTURE_PROOF.txt. Let
H_1,...,H_N be traceless Hermitian d-by-d operators satisfying the exact
identity

  sum_i <psi|H_i|psi>^2 = c for every complex unit vector psi.

For K=sum_i H_i tensor H_i and the Choi star

  W=sum_i H_i^T_R tensor (H_i^A tensor I_B+I_A tensor H_i^B),

the theorem proves

  sum_i H_i^2=(d+1)c I,     W <= (d+2)c I.

The proof polarizes the pure quartic to K Pi_sym=c Pi_sym. With
E=|sum_r rr><sum_r rr| and the positive Gram operator
F=sum_i |vec(H_i^T)><vec(H_i^T)|, the entry identity gives, on each pair,
W_RA+F_RA=c(I+E_RA). Summing over the two outputs yields
W=2cI+c(E_RA+E_RB)-(F_RA+F_RB). The range of E_RA+E_RB splits into a
symmetric d-dimensional subspace with eigenvalue d+1 and an antisymmetric
d-dimensional subspace with eigenvalue d-1. Tracelessness and the polarized
identity make the Gram sum equal c on the first subspace; positivity makes
the remainder nonpositive there and on its symmetric complement. This gives
the stated maximum cd for cE_sum-F_sum. The bound is sharp: the normalized
projector onto the first subspace is an actual swap-symmetric normalized
Choi state with all one-system marginals I/d, namely the universal cloner
control.

For an actual compatible marginal Phi, this implies the exact frame
Dirichlet comparison

  2 sum_i tau[H_i(I-Phi)(H_i)] >= V-c,
  V=sum_i tau(H_i^2)=(d+1)c.

For spin j and positive multipole weights w_l, it applies whenever
sum_l w_l beta_l(k) is constant over all symmetric pair-spin sectors
k congruent to 2j modulo 2, where
beta_l(k)=d(2l+1)(-1)^(2j+k){j j k; j j l}.
This is an arbitrary-spin analytic result on the exact constant-capture

Q. A logarithmic score-rank EB comparison from receiver amplification

The noncovariant broadcaster argument in
outputs/research/sol_general_rounding/RESULT.txt §C.2 yields a further
universal partial theorem. Let Φ be bistochastic, selfadjoint on normalized-
trace L2, and actually selfcompatible through a supplied two-output UCP map

subspace S of traceless Hermitian operators, with r=dim S≥2, one canonical
pure-design EB channel Ψ satisfies

  P_S(I−Ψ)P_S ≤ 2 ceil(log2 r) P_S(I−Φ)P_S.             (Q.1)

To prove this, set m=ceil(log2 r) and recursively build a binary tree
broadcaster with 2^m leaves from m levels of J. Every leaf marginal is Φ^m;
discarding unused leaves leaves an r-output broadcaster with that same
marginal. For every PSD witness Q supported on S, rank(Q)≤r. Measure each
factor in a different output and pull the product PVM back through the
supplied extension. The exact meter identity gives

  Tr(Q)−h_1(Q) ≤ 2 E_(Φ^m)(Q).

1−λ^m=(1−λ)Σ_(q=0)^(m−1)λ^q≤m(1−λ), so
E_(Φ^m)(Q)≤mE_Φ(Q), including signed spectrum. Thus every PSD Q on S
satisfies the dual inequality for factor 2m. Compact-convex separation gives
one common EB moment operator on all of S, proving (Q.1). For r=1 an
eigenbasis design fixes its sole generator, and r=0 is vacuous.

On the full traceless space, r=d²−1 and this gives the explicit
dimension-dependent factor 2 ceil(log2(d²−1))=O(log d). For a tracial
likelihood family ρ_H=(I+H)/d in the same S, with ||H||∞≤θ and
r_Φ=sup_H||(I−Φ)H||_(1,τ), it implies

  sup_H T(Ψ(ρ_H),ρ_H) ≤ (1/2)sqrt(2 ceil(log2 r) θ r_Φ).

The common design is independent of family labels, so this is a genuine
uniform EB reconstruction theorem whenever the score span is supplied and

the high-rank tail. The full-domain factor remains logarithmic in d.
