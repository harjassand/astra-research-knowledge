ROOT DERIVATION: bounded-score variational interface for filtered Slaters
Status: internally derived elementary extension; not claimed new, not ground-state optimization guarantee.

Let unnormalized amplitudes be a_theta(S)=a_0(S) exp[-(1/2) sum_l theta_l F_l(S)], with fixed exact evaluable complex a_0 phases and bounded real diagonal features |F_l|<=K_l. Let p_theta=|a_theta|^2/Z and normalized psi_theta. Assume the relevant conditional counting/preparation interface has already been justified (e.g. source Chen-Liu + acquired Slater and laminar repulsive filter); availability is not automatic for arbitrary F.
For a supplied Hermitian H with ||H||<=M and efficient sparse row enumeration, define E_L(S)=(H a_theta)(S)/a_theta(S) on positive p support. Then E_p Re E_L=<H> and E_p |E_L|^2 = ||P_support H psi||^2 <= M^2. The latter is <= rather than equality if H has off-support leakage. A tiny filter norm does not itself inflate this second moment.

Since d_l psi=-(F_l-<F_l>)psi/2, gradient d_l<H>=-Re <psi|(F_l-<F_l>)H|psi> = -Cov_p(F_l,Re E_L). A sampled product (F_l-E F_l)Re E_L has second moment <=4K_l^2 M^2. Estimate component means/products independently with median-of-means; finite error budgets require only polynomial K_l,M,1/epsilon and log failure. Exact independent p samples are not presumed: request sampler TV small enough after clipping observable at threshold polynomial in the second-moment bound, as in N63. No inverse Z arises. Paired ratio evaluations require polynomial-bit precision, charged separately; irrational filters need certified relative approximation.

The pure-state quantum Fisher matrix is Cov_p(F_l,F_k); Fubini-Study real metric is one quarter of it. Estimating its entries has bounded moments. Inverting it may require inverse-smallest-nonzero-eigenvalue cost; this is NOT covered by bounded estimation. Fixed support is necessary for differentiability; theta-dependent hard constraints must be treated as separate domains. Restricting theta to a cone preserving the admitted weight class is necessary for the sampler. Energy as function of theta can be nonconvex; this does not establish polynomial global optimization, good ground overlap, a spectral gap, or general resolution of the fermion sign problem.

Potential consequence worth implementation: certified energy/gradient evaluation without local-Markov-chain mixing assumptions for an explicitly admitted hierarchical or weak-attraction variational family. Existing variational Monte Carlo identities and bounded-ratio estimators are prior art. Increment, if any, is the new guaranteed sampling interface, not the differentiation identity.
