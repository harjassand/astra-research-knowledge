THEOREM CANDIDATE
For every integer d>=2 let Phi:M_d->M_d be completely positive, trace
preserving, unital and self-adjoint for the real Hermitian Hilbert-Schmidt
inner product <A,B>_tau=Tr(AB)/d. Suppose a completely positive trace-
preserving B:M_d->M_d tensor M_d has both marginals Phi. Then there is ONE
finite pure-ensemble canonical entanglement-breaking channel Psi with

  Psi >= (2Phi^2-I)_+                                      (T1)

in real Hermitian HS quadratic-form order. In particular

  I-Psi <= 2(I-Phi^2) <= 4(I-Phi).                        (T2)

The plus denotes positive spectral part of an HS operator, not a physical
CP map. Psi itself is CPTP, unital, HS-self-adjoint, and HS-positive. The
same Psi works for EVERY Hermitian A. There is no HS-positive-spectrum,
spectral gap, covariance, invariant-subspace, or measurement-per-query
premise. The exact existence construction uses a compact limit of trees;
it supplies no uniform finite-depth algorithm or hardware compiler.

CONVENTIONS AND CENTERING
Phi and B act in the Schrodinger picture on density matrices and extend
linearly to all matrices. Their adjoints are defined by the ordinary
trace pairing. Since Phi is HS-self-adjoint, Phi*=Phi. The two marginal
conditions are equivalent to
  B*(A tensor I)=Phi(A),  B*(I tensor A)=Phi(A).
Replace B by (B+Ad_swap composed with B)/2 if necessary. This preserves
the marginal channel and CP/TP and makes B output-symmetric. B need not
be unital as a map into the larger algebra.

Write H_0 for traceless Hermitian matrices. TP and unitality make it
Phi-invariant and make identity/traceless cross terms zero. Bistochastic
Schwarz implies ||Phi(A)||_2,tau<=||A||_2,tau. Hence Phi has a real
tau-orthonormal Hermitian eigenbasis {I,E_1,...,E_n}, n=d^2-1, with
  Phi(E_a)=lambda_a E_a,  lambda_a in [-1,1].
Let J be the index set |lambda_a|>1/sqrt(2), q=|J|. Only these modes
need unbiased amplification. Their eigenvalues are nonzero. Relabel them
E_1,...,E_q and put Lambda=diag(lambda_1,...,lambda_q). All E_a are
traceless, so every estimator below has mean zero at reference I/d.

If q=0, choose the rank-one dephasing channel in any orthonormal input
basis. It is pure canonical, fixes I, and is HS-positive. On H_0 the
right side of (T1) is zero, so (T1) holds. Thus assume q>=1.

1. ONE LEAF JOINT MEASUREMENT WITH UNBIASED VECTOR ESTIMATORS
Fix p_a>0, sum_a p_a=1; p_a=1/q is sufficient. On a single d-dimensional
receiver choose a with probability p_a, retain that coin, and perform the
spectral PVM E_a=sum_y e_ay P_ay. This ONE randomized POVM has effects
  F_ay=p_a P_ay.
For its outcome z=(a,y), define a real q-vector x_0(z) by
  (x_0(z))_b=delta_ab e_ay/p_a.
Consequently, as operator-valued first moments,
  sum_z (x_0(z))_a F_z=E_a.                            (1)
At reference I/d its mean is zero and covariance is
  N_0=E[x_0 x_0^T]=diag(1/p_a).                       (2)
All off-diagonal entries in (2) are ZERO, because exactly one coordinate
is nonzero on each outcome, even when the E_a do not commute. The
diagonal entry is sum_y tau(p_a P_ay)e_ay^2/p_a^2
=tau(E_a^2)/p_a=1/p_a. Degenerate spectral eigenspaces cause no problem.

2. BROADCAST TREE AND EXACT UNBIASED PULLBACK
Let T_0=id. For m>=1 define the full binary broadcast tree channel
  T_m=(T_(m-1) tensor T_(m-1)) composed with B.
It has 2^m receiver systems. Perform the same one-leaf POVM F independently
on every receiver, retaining every leaf coin and spectral outcome. This
is one product POVM on the tree output and pulls back through T_m to ONE
input POVM M^(m)_z, with no measurement selected after the input query.

For a complete leaf record z, define the vector estimator
  x_m(z)=Lambda^(-m) 2^(-m) sum_leaves x_0(z_leaf).       (3)
Equivalently, on the two top subtrees,
  x_m=Lambda^(-1)(x_(m-1)^B+x_(m-1)^C)/2.             (4)
For every a its Heisenberg first moment is exactly
  sum_z (x_m(z))_a M^(m)_z=E_a.                       (5)
One proof: each leaf has marginal Phi^m, so its first-moment pullback is
Phi^m(E_a)=lambda_a^m E_a, canceled in (3). Equivalently, induction in
(4) uses B*(E_a tensor I+I tensor E_a)=2lambda_a E_a.
Signs of negative lambda_a are retained; no positivity of Phi as an HS
operator is used. Equation (5) is a full operator identity on M_d.

3. SIBLING COVARIANCE: NO INDEPENDENCE ASSUMPTION
Let rho_BC=B(I/d). Its two marginals are I/d. Symmetry of B gives a real
symmetric matrix
  C_ab=Tr[rho_BC(E_a tensor E_b)].                     (6)
For every real u and A=sum_a u_a E_a,
  0<=Tr[rho_BC(A tensor I-I tensor A)^2]
    =2tau(A^2)-2Tr[rho_BC(A tensor A)].
Therefore
  C<=I_q                                               (7)
in real matrix order. This uses only positivity of rho_BC and its tracial
marginals. C need not be positive; no independent-clone assertion is made.

Let N_m=E_(I/d)[x_m x_m^T] for the full tree outcome at input reference.
Each top child marginal is I/d, so each child's subtree estimator has
covariance N_(m-1). For the cross moment, the product measurement on two
subtrees and their exact first-moment identities give
  E[x_(m-1),a^B x_(m-1),b^C]
   =Tr[rho_BC(E_a tensor E_b)]=C_ab.                   (8)
This remains true for entangled sibling outputs: the first moment of a
product-outcome function pulls back as the tensor product of the two
subtree first moments. Equation (8) does NOT factor a state or probability.

Combining (4), (8), and C=C^T proves the exact recursion
  N_m=(1/2)Lambda^(-1)(N_(m-1)+C)Lambda^(-1).          (9)
All means are zero by (5), so these second moments are centered covariances.

Define the order-preserving congruence map
  R(X)=(1/2)Lambda^(-1)X Lambda^(-1).
It preserves real PSD order even for signed Lambda. From (7), (9), and
(2), inductively
  N_m <= R^m(N_0)+sum_(k=1)^m R^k(I_q)=D_m,           (10)
where D_m is diagonal with
  (D_m)_aa=(2lambda_a^2)^(-m)/p_a
       +[1-(2lambda_a^2)^(-m)]/(2lambda_a^2-1).       (11)
Since every a in J satisfies 2lambda_a^2>1,
  D_m -> D_infinity=diag[1/(2lambda_a^2-1)].          (12)
The +/-1 modes are included normally: their factors (2lambda_a^2)^(-m)
=2^(-m) decay, and their limiting diagonal entry is 1. There is no division
by 1-|lambda_a|. Boundary |lambda_a|=1/sqrt(2) is excluded because its
desired positive-part lower bound is zero.

4. CANONICAL POVM COVARIANCE AND FULL-DOMAIN SCHUR BOUND
For the pulled-back input POVM M_z=M^(m)_z, let
  r_z=tau(M_z),  rho_z=M_z/Tr M_z,
omitting zero effects. Its posterior ensemble has mean I/d. Its canonical
channel is
  Psi_m(X)=d sum_z r_z rho_z Tr(rho_z X).
It is CPTP, unital, EB, HS-self-adjoint and HS-positive. Let
  a_z,b=Tr(rho_z E_b), b=1,...,n,
in the FULL traceless HS eigenbasis, not just the amplified q modes.
The matrix of its quadratic form on H_0 is
  S_m=E_r[a_z a_z^T].                                  (13)
Let J: R^q -> R^n be the coordinate embedding of the selected modes.
The exact unbiased operator pullback (5) implies
  E_r[a_z x_m(z)^T]=J,                                (14)
because the b,a entry is tau[E_b sum_z (x_m(z))_a M_z]
=tau(E_b E_a). This includes EVERY fast/slow cross term: on nonselected
modes the right side is zero, not an unproved decorrelation assumption.

The classical second-moment block matrix is PSD:
  [[S_m,J],[J^T,N_m]]>=0.                             (15)
N_m is positive definite. Indeed a vector in its kernel would have zero
estimator almost surely, making its cross moment Jv zero; J is injective.
The Schur complement of (15) and inverse monotonicity in (10) now give
  S_m>=J N_m^(-1)J^T>=J D_m^(-1)J^T.                  (16)
This is a FULL-DOMAIN lower bound with zero on the complementary modes.
It is not merely a list of diagonal or individual-direction scores.

For an explicitly pure canonical representation, spectrally refine each
M_z into rank-one effects M_zj, retaining the parent outcome z and its
estimator x_m(z) on every refinement. This is a single input POVM. Its
first moment (5), probabilities' sum, and N_m remain identical. Its
canonical covariance dominates (13) by scalar Jensen for every direction,
or (14)-(16) apply directly to the refined pure posterior states. Thus
replace Psi_m by this pure canonical refinement without weakening (16).
No physical apparatus implementing this refinement is claimed for free.

5. COMPACT LIMIT GIVES ONE FINITE PURE CANONICAL CHANNEL
The normalized Choi states of pure canonical channels are
  Z=sum_y p_y pi_y^T tensor pi_y,
pi_y pure, sum_y p_y pi_y=I/d. The set of pure states is compact, and the
convex hull of pi^T tensor pi is compact in finite-dimensional Hermitian
space (Caratheodory). The barycenter condition is its closed marginal
constraint. Thus this pure-canonical set is compact. Every point has a
finite pure-ensemble representation; d^4 atoms is a safe bound using the
trace-one affine dimension of Hermitian d^2-by-d^2 Choi matrices.

Take a convergent subsequence of the pure canonical Psi_m. By (12), (16),
and closedness of HS PSD order, its limit Psi satisfies
  Psi|H_0 >= J diag(2lambda_a^2-1)J^T
           = (2Phi^2-I)_+|H_0.                         (17)
Both Psi and the spectral operator fix I, and identity/traceless cross
terms vanish. Hence (17) proves (T1) on the FULL real Hermitian HS space.
This is one fixed channel; no frame or query enters the limiting choice.

Finally positive spectral part dominates its underlying self-adjoint
operator, so Psi>=2Phi^2-I. Also
  (2Phi^2-I)-(4Phi-3I)=2(I-Phi)^2>=0.
Therefore I-Psi<=2(I-Phi^2)<=4(I-Phi), proving (T2).

6. WHY THE LOGARITHMIC LEAF CHARGE DISAPPEARS
The pinned LA001 tree assigns ONE particular leaf to each frame direction
and uses its attenuated first moment Phi^m(A). Bernoulli turns the deficit
into m times its one-step Dirichlet energy. Prefix weighting replaces m
by entropy but still charges a private leaf path for each test.

Here every leaf measures the same randomized vector POVM. After averaging
2^m leaves, the bottom-level variance diag(1/p_a) is suppressed by
(2lambda_a^2)^(-m), while sibling correlation contributes only the
geometric series in (11). It is the covariance, not an individual leaf's
attenuated correlation, that is charged. Sibling dependence is retained
exactly in C and bounded by square positivity. The full Schur estimate
then yields one common-ensemble comparison without an additive deficit
floor. There is no multiplication or rounding of the preservation score.

7. SUPPLIED VERSUS ACQUIRED INTERFACE AND COSTS
Phi and a compatible B are supplied exactly as finite-dimensional maps;
no broadcaster acquisition is proved. Symmetrization adds a retained
random swap coin. The mathematical proof diagonalizes the d^2-dimensional
HS operator and computes the selected Hermitian spectral PVMs exactly.
All coordinates, estimator weights, channel operations, and comparisons
are exact. No finite-precision stability theorem is supplied.

A depth m tree uses 2^m-1 calls of the supplied B and 2^m receivers. Each
leaf has at most qd outcomes including its random coin. The raw joint
POVM can have (qd)^(2^m) outcomes, multiplied by at most d under input
rank-one refinement. Estimator amplitudes may grow as |lambda_a|^(-m).
The exact comparator is selected by compactness; a d^4-atom representation
exists afterward, but no efficient selection/design/circuit algorithm is
provided. Its small abstract support does not erase tree/storage costs.

Convergence slows when any selected |lambda_a| approaches 1/sqrt(2).
For p_a=1/q one has D_m^(-1)>=diag(2lambda_a^2-1)-q kappa^m I with
kappa=max_a[1/(2lambda_a^2)]<1. This yields an additive finite-depth
approximation, not the exact multiplicative target at gap-zero modes.
The exact existence statement uses the compact limit, including +/-1.
