1. EXACT HARMONIC CARRIER AND CONTRACTION GAP

Let a>=b>=1,N=a+b and let

 W=Sym^a(C^3) tensor Sym^b((C^3)*),
 C=sum_(j=1)^3 A_j B_j : W -> W_(a-1,b-1),
 P=1_ker C, H=ker C.

Here A_j,B_j are normalized bosonic annihilation operators. The
Hermitian fundamental generators obey Tr(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2; their
represented action on W is

 T_A=pi_a(t_A) tensor I-I tensor pi_b(t_A)^T.              (1.1)

The contraction is an SU3 intertwiner. Its kernel contains the
highest vector e1^a tensor (e3*)^b of weight(a,b). The commutator

 [C,C^dagger]=N_A+N_B+3

on each degree proves that C is surjective: on W_(a-1,b-1),
CC^dagger=C^dagger C+(N+1)I>0. Thus

 dim H=binom(a+2,2)binom(b+2,2)
       -binom(a+1,2)binom(b+1,2)
      =(a+1)(b+1)(N+2)/2,

the Weyl dimension of V_(a,b). It follows that H is that irreducible
carrier, with the invariant inherited inner product, and [P,T_A]=0.

For completeness the exact contraction spectrum is

 spec(C^dagger C)={r(N+2-r):0<=r<=min(a,b)}.               (1.2)

Indeed the iterated orthogonal Fischer decomposition consists of
(C^dagger)^r ker C in degrees(a-r,b-r). Commuting C through r raises
using the displayed commutator gives eigenvalue r(N+2-r). Surjectivity
and induction exhaust W; these eigenvalues increase strictly on the
range because r<=N/2. The smallest positive eigenvalue is N+1. Hence

             I-P <= C^dagger C/(N+1).                    (1.3)

An explicit finite description of P is the polynomial
prod_(r=1)^min(a,b)[I-C^dagger C/(r(N+2-r))]. No claim of a cheap
physical circuit or fixed precision cost follows from this formula.

2. SUPPORT NEAR THE HARMONIC HIGHEST VECTOR

For 0<=R<=a let U_R in Sym^a consist of occupations with
s=a-n1=n2+n3<=R. Let p_b project onto the normalized coherent vector
(e3*)^b and let S_R=1_(U_R) tensor p_b. Its rank is

                   q_R=(R+1)(R+2)/2.                   (2.1)

Only B_3 acts nontrivially on this dual coherent vector. On this
support the exact compressed contraction square is

 S_R C^dagger C S_R=b n3 S_R<=bR S_R.

Combining with(1.3) gives

 S_R P S_R >=(1-epsilon)S_R,
              epsilon=bR/(N+1).                        (2.2)

This is an operator inequality on the actual support, not merely
an average overlap. If epsilon<1, P restricted to U_R tensor p_b is
injective, so compressing a strictly positive support filter preserves
its rank. The argument does not treat the two degrees as independent
physical sectors after compression.

3. THE POSITIVE TAPER AND EXACT AMBIENT ENERGY

Define on Sym^a the real positive taper

 f_R(n1,n2,n3)=(R+1-s)_+, s=a-n1,

One derivation is to sum shell size s+1 and conductance

The coherent dual projector has E_b(p_b)=2b. The two ambient

 E_W(A_R)/S_A=(4a-3R)/(R+2)+2b.                         (3.3)

Both terms are charged. In particular, no unrepresented spin-fiber

                  [(4a-3R)/(R+2)+2b].                  (4.3)
