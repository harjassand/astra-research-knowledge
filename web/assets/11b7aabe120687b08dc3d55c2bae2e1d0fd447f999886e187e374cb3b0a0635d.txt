RANK-CONSTRAINED CASIMIR PROFILE FOR FIXED SIMPLE ROOT SYSTEMS
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally reconstructed and independently audited by existing
research leaves; no external expert review or novelty assessment.

1. THEOREM

Let G be compact, connected, and simple, with fixed irreducible root system
Phi, rank r, p=|Phi+| positive roots, Weyl vector rho, and coroot Weyl
vector rho^vee. Normalize the invariant form so long roots have squared
length 2 and use the half-Casimir eigenvalue

  c2(nu)=1/2 (nu,nu+2rho).

Let V_lambda be a regular highest-weight representation with Dynkin labels

  cN <= a_i <= C N,

for fixed 0<c<=C<infinity, and put d=dim V_lambda. Then

  d ~=_(Phi,c,C) N^p.                                          (1)

For E(X)=<X,LX>, where L is the conjugation-Casimir with eigenvalue c2(nu)
on each V_nu summand of End(V_lambda), define

  Lambda_lambda(Q)=inf_{0!=X, rank X<=Q} E(X)/||X||_HS^2.

There are positive constants depending only on Phi,c,C such that

  Lambda_lambda(Q) ~=_(Phi,c,C) (d/Q)^(1/p),
                       1<=Q<=floor(d/2),                     (2)

and, for 1<=k<=floor(d/2),

  Lambda_lambda(d-k) ~=_(Phi,c,C) (k/d)^((p-1)/p),  p>=2,     (3)

  Lambda_lambda(d-k) ~=_(c,C) 1/log(e d/k),        p=1.        (4)

For an irreducible root system p=1 is exactly type A1. The lower bounds
cover every complex matrix, including nonnormal matrices. Upper witnesses
are positive principal-height caps and harmonic filters. Lambda_lambda(d)=0,
witnessed by the scalar identity. The theorem is for each fixed root system;
no bound uniform in rank is asserted.

The lower half of (2)-(4), as well as the all-T spectral count below, holds
for every irreducible carrier, without regularity. Regularity is used to
construct the matching upper filters and to convert shell scale n to
d^(1/p).

2. UNIFORM ALL-T SPECTRAL COUNT

Write the conjugation decomposition as

  End(V_lambda)=direct_sum_nu M_(lambda,nu) tensor V_nu,

and let D_lambda(T) count the full dimension of all isotypic summands with
c2(nu)<=T, including every multiplicity. The general compact-group
highest-vector coefficient injection gives

  dim M_(lambda,nu)<=dim V_nu[0].                               (5)

It is valid for every carrier and dominant nu. In the highest-vector proof,
the beta=0 coefficient of an intertwiner lies in V_nu[0]; a nonzero
least-positive-root-height coefficient with zero beta=0 term would be
annihilated by every simple raising operator in its first irreducible
factor, which is impossible away from its highest line. The source audit
also records the stronger Fronsdal-Hirai inclusion
End(V_lambda) -> L^2(G/T), and its stabilizer form for singular lambda.

Kostant's zero-weight formula plus PBW partition counting gives

  dim V_nu[0] <= C_Phi(1+||nu||)^(p-r).

Indeed, there are p positive roots and r simple roots; choose the p-r
nonsimple occupation coefficients in a positive-root partition and the
simple coefficients are uniquely determined. Ordinary root height is
ht(beta)=<rho^vee,beta>, also for nonsimply-laced types. Weyl's dimension
formula gives dim V_nu<=C_Phi(1+||nu||)^p, and there are at most
C_Phi(1+sqrt(T))^r dominant labels in a Casimir ball. Since
c2(nu)>=||nu||^2/2, multiplying these estimates proves the all-T bound

  D_lambda(T)<=C_Phi(1+T)^p,  T>=0.                            (6)

The exponent is exact at the counting level:
(p-r)+p+r=2p powers of sqrt(T). The proof sums over the whole dominant
weight lattice, so it remains valid beyond the finite support plateau.
Nonsimply-laced normalization is handled in
spectral_count.txt: ordinary height uses rho^vee; Weyl dimension uses
coroot height (rho,alpha^vee); the two must not be interchanged.

3. LOWER PROFILE FOR ALL MATRICES

For the conjugation-invariant Casimir projector Pi_T, Schur's lemma gives

  Tr_2(Pi_T)=(D_lambda(T)/d)I.

The vectorization of a unit-HS matrix of rank at most Q has Schmidt rank
at most Q. Thus its Casimir mass below T is at most QD_lambda(T)/d, and
layer cake gives

  E(X)>= integral_0^infinity [1-QD_lambda(T)/d]_+ dT.         (7)

Substitution of (6) proves the lower branch in (2) when d/Q is large. For
bounded d/Q, the positive Casimir gap and
|Tr X|^2/d<=Q/d leave a fixed fraction of the norm outside the scalar
kernel. This completes the low-rank lower bound.

For the near-full range choose a rank-k projection P onto a subspace of
the right kernel of any X with rank(X)<=d-k. Write X=aI+Y with Tr Y=0
and P0=P-(k/d)I. The exact bridge is

  ||X||_HS^2 <= E(X)[d G(P)/k^2 + gamma_Phi^(-1)],
  G(P)=<P0,L^(-1)P0>,

where a safe gap is gamma_Phi=min_i(omega_i,rho)>0. This uses no positivity
or normality of X. For the positive spectral projection Pi_(0,T],

  ||Pi_(0,T]P0||_HS^2
       <= min{k-k^2/d, k^2(D_lambda(T)-1)/d},

and the exact Green identity is

  G(P)=integral_0^infinity ||Pi_(0,T]P0||_HS^2 T^(-2)dT.    (8)

Splitting (8) at the gap-safe balance scale
T~(d/k)^(1/p) yields (3); for p=1 the low-frequency integral is
logarithmic and yields (4). The full finite cutoff formulas and all endpoint
cases are in lower_profile.txt. This Green step is the reason the near-full
lower bound is stronger than the elementary integrated projector profile.

4. MATCHING UPPER FILTERS AND SHORT-ROOT NORMALIZATION

Put n=min_i a_i and K=max_i a_i/n<=C/c. Weyl's formula gives
d~=_(Phi,K)n^p. The principal height is H0=rho^vee, and a weight mu has
depth q=<lambda-mu,rho^vee>. For all q<=n the Kostant carrier shells
equal PBW shells with generating function

  product_(alpha>0)(1-z^ht(alpha))^(-1).

Their cumulative volume V_R satisfies V_R~=_(Phi)(R+1)^p. The exact
root-string trace recurrence is

  c_alpha(q+ht(alpha))-c_alpha(q)=Tr_(W_q)H_alpha,
  c_alpha(q)=sum_(t>=1,q-t ht(alpha)>=0)Tr_(W_(q-t ht(alpha)))H_alpha.

For q<=epsilon_Phi n, the coroot trace is between positive constants
times n g_q: <lambda,alpha^vee> is at least n, whereas the weight
displacement beta obeys |<beta,alpha^vee>|<=B_Phi q. The weighted simple
conductance is therefore comparable to nV_(q-1), and every root crossing
is controlled by the active simple increments. The exact root-energy
factor under the half-Casimir normalization is

  eta_alpha=(alpha,alpha)/2.

It is 1 on long roots and retains the short-root ratios (1/2 for
B/C/F4; 1/3 for G2). The recurrence uses coroot pairings but has no
root-length multiplier. The detailed invariant-form derivation and B2/G2
diagnostics are in root_length_audit.txt; the checker passes.

The linear ramp F_R=(1-depth/R)_+ has rank and norm squared of order R^p,
energy O_(Phi,K)(nR^(p-1)), and quotient O(n/R). Integer rounding plus a
fixed stable cap covers every Q<=d/2. For p>=2, a profile equal to one
through depth R and tapering harmonically to zero at depth M has nullity
at least k, norm squared at least d/2, and energy controlled by the
resistance

  rho=sum_(j=R+1)^M 1/c_1(j) >= c_(Phi,K)/(n(R+1)^(p-1)).

Choosing R on the k^(1/p) scale gives quotient
O_(Phi,K)((k/d)^((p-1)/p)). For A1, the exact resistance is
Theta(n^(-1)log(e d/k)), giving the inverse-log upper bound. A
Weyl-symmetric positive-part Cartan filter has rank at most d/2 and
bounded quotient, patching the remaining fixed-fraction nullities.

The global Cartan trace check uses the same invariant metric: if B is the
form inducing the long-root-length-2 weight metric, then

  Tr_(V_lambda)(T_H^2)=2 d c2(lambda) B(H,H)/dim(g).

The factor two is equivalent to writing the generator metric as 2B. Since
lambda(rho^vee)~n and c2(lambda)~n^2, the positive-part Cartan filter has
HS norm squared comparable to d. Its Lipschitz root increments and the
positive Casimir trace bound give energy O(d). The full all-Q cutoff proof
and the two endpoint clarifications are in upper_profile_working.txt;
the independent audit in upper_profile.txt found no substantive gap.

5. EVIDENCE, SOURCES, AND LIMITS

Existing leaves independently produced the multiplicity/source audit,
all-T spectral count, nonsimply-laced root-length audit, and full upper
profile audit. The exact B2/G2 checker and all source files are under this
directory. These internal checks are not external mathematical review.
The primary source comparison is in multiplicity.txt and
spectral_count.txt; Fronsdal-Hirai already proves the stronger multiplicity
inclusion, and Kostant is the exact weight-multiplicity source. No novelty
or priority claim is made.

This theorem does not assert an operational E/F/G score transfer, a
tensor-power acquisition result, or a result uniform in rank. It also does
not prove KLS, log-concavity, stochastic localization, or the N75
scale-integrated observation mutual-information inequality. That N75 MI
gate remains separate and open on this evidence.
