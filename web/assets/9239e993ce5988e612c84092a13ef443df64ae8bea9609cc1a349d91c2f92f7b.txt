SCALAR-WEIGHTED GAUSSIAN STABILITY MECHANISM v1
Coordinator derivation 2026-10-09. Candidate internal proof; requires worker/source audit. Not a foundational discovery claim.

Model: independent samples (a_i,Y_i), a_i~N(0,I_d); Y_i may depend on a_i. Define X_i=Y_i a_i 1_{||Y_i a_i||<=tau}, deterministic tau; Q=E XX^T. Assume lambda_min(Q)>=lambda>0, ||Q||<=Lambda. For L>0 let tail covariance T_L=E[XX^T 1_{|Y|>L}], ||T_L||<=eta. Let k=ceil(epsilon*m). All epsilon,gamma and numerical parameters explicitly charged.

1. Gaussian subset bound. For a fixed subset I of k rows, Gaussian matrix A_I has ||A_I||<=sqrt(k)+sqrt(d)+t with failure exp(-t^2/2). Union bound over binom(m,k) subsets with t=sqrt(2[log binom(m,k)+log(1/delta)]) gives simultaneously
  sup_{|I|<=k} ||sum_{i in I} a_i a_i^T/m||
  <= C[d/m+(k/m) log(em/k)+log(1/delta)/m].
For fractional deleted weights v_i in [0,1], sum v_i<=epsilon*m, convexity/extreme points or ordering directional quadratic forms gives the same upper bound, with k=ceil(epsilon*m). This is uniform after seeing all data.

2. Low-amplitude deleted mass is <= L^2 times the preceding Gaussian subset covariance, pointwise, even when Y depends arbitrarily on a.

3. Tail mass Z_i=X_i X_i^T 1_{|Y_i|>L} has 0<=Z_i<=tau^2 I, E Z_i=T_L, and E Z_i^2 <=tau^2 T_L. Matrix Bernstein implies
  ||m^{-1} sum Z_i - T_L|| <= sqrt(2 tau^2 eta log(2d/delta)/m) + 2 tau^2 log(2d/delta)/(3m)
(up to checking the usual centered-matrix constants; centered bound <=tau^2 also holds for PSD bounded summands). Thus ALL deleted tail mass is at most eta plus that radius, independent of selected deletion set.

4. Whole covariance concentration similarly gives
  ||Qhat-Q|| <= sqrt(2 tau^2 Lambda log(2d/delta)/m)+2 tau^2 log(2d/delta)/(3m).

5. For w_i in [0,1], alpha=m^{-1}sum(1-w_i)<=epsilon, D=m^{-1}sum(1-w_i)X_iX_i^T, weighted covariance Q_w=(Qhat-D)/(1-alpha). Hence
 ||Q_w-Q|| <= [||Qhat-Q||+||D||+epsilon Lambda]/(1-epsilon).
If this bound <=gamma lambda, then (1-gamma)Q <= Q_w <=(1+gamma)Q in Loewner order for every admissible adaptive fractional deletion.

6. Therefore at fixed dimension-free lambda,Lambda,L,eta,epsilon,gamma and tau^2=O(d), choosing eta small, L such that tail covariance <=eta, epsilon log(e/epsilon) small enough, and m=C d log(d/delta) yields fixed-parameter stability. The constants depend on all displayed parameters. This is NOT a claim that original source contamination tolerance is preserved without extra logarithmic/polynomial losses.
