1. EXACT INCIDENT-EDGE GOOD TEST

Fix the forcing geometry/enlargement m first, so that the free coordinate
set F_z of each local cut body is contained in
  P_z=(L z+[-mL,(m+1)L]^2) intersect Z^2.
The body may include its free shell/collar coordinates. True zero pins
are not free body coordinates. Define
  E_inc(P_z)={original NN square edges with at least one endpoint in P_z}.
The local failure event is now
  E_z={there exists e in E_inc(P_z) with |grad h(e)|>1-delta}.
The long-pair failure L_z is unchanged: ordered endpoints in P_z with
d1 distance at least k0, using deterministic Manhattan paths inside P_z.
Set B_z=E_z union L_z. This convention is imposed for BOTH initial
replicas and their admitted small-tilt laws.

In particular every original edge incident to F_z has initial slack at
least delta on a good patch, including a true zero pin just outside the
cut shell. The body retains that free-pin inequality. A cross-shell
free-free edge may be cut, but it also belongs to the test, supplying the
initial gap used in the exterior-quietness argument. Edges with two true
zero endpoints have gradient zero and never supply a failure witness.
There is no artificial assertion that every exterior neighbor is free.

All witnesses are still original edges of the finite zero-pinned hull
used for CAP. In particular we do not omit an edge by contracting its
pin, then apply a graph-degree bound to a different graph. Degree four
and the original CAP law are used before grounding the force body.

2. COUNTS, COLORS AND PARAMETER ORDER

For L>=1,
  |P_z|=((2m+1)L+1)^2 <= (2m+2)^2 L^2,
  |E_inc(P_z)| <= 4 |P_z| <=4(2m+2)^2 L^2.
The number of ordered endpoint pairs is at most (2m+2)^4 L^4.
The previous safe constant C_m=(2m+4)^4 bounds both coefficients for
every integer m>=1. Thus the local-edge CAP base remains
  C_m L^2(C4 delta)^(1/C4),
and the long-pair base remains C_m L^4 lambda^k0.

Every endpoint of an edge in E_inc(P_z) lies in the one-site dilation
P_z^+=P_z+[-1,1]^2. Color block indices by their coordinates modulo
Q=2m+4, giving J=Q^2 classes. Two distinct same-color patches have a
coordinate gap at least 3L before dilation; after dilation it is at least
3L-2>0 for the chosen even L>=2. Thus the dilated vertex sets are
disjoint in that coordinate, and all incident-edge sets and canonical
long-path edge sets within one color are pairwise distinct.

No new threshold or circular parameter choice is necessary. Fix m and
C4 first, then the previous a0,delta0,lambda with lambda<=1/8. Given
rho in (0,1), let eta=(rho^J/2)^2. Choose k0 large, with k0>=4/delta0;
let L be the least even integer at least 8(k0+2)/delta0, so L grows at
most linearly in k0. Make C_m L^4 lambda^k0<=eta. Only AFTER L is fixed
choose 0<delta<=min(delta0,1/8) with
  C_m L^2(C4 delta)^(1/C4)<=eta.
For each mixed type assignment, at least half its boxes have one type.
Discard the minority event and pay 2^k assignments: the one-color bound
is (2 sqrt(eta))^k=(rho^J)^k. Select a largest color class to obtain
  P0(A subset Bad)<=rho^|A|
for every finite A, including zero-pin collars. Under a tilted law with
L2 density <=sqrt(M), run the zero-law argument at
rho0=(rho/sqrt(M))^2. Holder gives the same target rho for every nonempty
A; the empty case is equality one. Subsequent dynamic s0 is chosen at
most delta AFTER L, then T is shortened; m,L are not enlarged again.

Thus all retained body inequalities, including every cross-shell true
pin, have the strict gap needed for the uniform interior ball and local
forcing. The polynomial edge/pair counts, color exponent, mixed-type
cost and the original k0,L,delta order are preserved exactly.

3. PERSISTENT LIMITING-BODY FACETS AND PATHS

Fix one finite free/pin pattern and normalized parameter family from
frozen Section 2. It is compact, with a common strict feasible origin,
interior ball, bounded diameter and finitely many constraint normals.
Merge proportional/duplicate normals using the minimum right-hand side
before naming geometric facets. If several true pins produce the same
facet, only its aggregate impulse is charged.

At a limiting parameter body K(theta0), delete redundant inequalities.
For a nominated original central free-free/free-ground edge, select its
shortest genuine-facet coordinate path, with cost at most its original
constraint cost <=2. The collar exclusion has a STRICT margin Lambda-2>0.
No auxiliary collar constraint is on this selected path. If the path
passes through ground, its legs end in genuine pin facets and its pin
difference is exactly zero. All cycles have positive cost and are removed.

For each selected facet choose a relative-interior point y* strict for
every other DISTINCT normal constraint and for the artificial collars.
These strict margins are positive. For a nearby parameter theta, move
this point by
  y(theta)=y*+[b_a(theta)-b_a(theta0)] a/||a||^2
to lie on a.y=b_a(theta). Continuity of the right-hand sides and the
strict margins keep every other distinct inequality and every collar
strict in a sufficiently small neighborhood. The selected geometric
facet therefore persists there. Where duplicate labels compete, the
minimum right-hand side is continuous; the geometric normal/aggregate
facet persists even if its minimizing edge label changes.

Use the SAME coordinate path from theta0 in that neighborhood. Its cost
may exceed the nearby nominated edge's constraint cost slightly. No step
requires it to remain a shortest implication path at theta. By shrinking
the neighborhood its total cost is still <Lambda, using Lambda-2>0.
Its coordinate telescoping identity for the initial z jump is exact and
parameter independent, so one selected facet still has jump at least
the nominated jump divided by the fixed path length. Genuine pin legs
and exclusion of the artificial collar remain valid. It is unnecessary
and incorrect to force a disappearing facet chosen at a nearby body.

The feasible segment to each strict selected point, followed by its
outward normal driver, gives the facet impulse at theta0. Fixed-body
driver continuity and the separately proved graph parameter maximum
principle preserve the positive impulse and collar margins nearby.
Compactness yields a FINITE cover by such limiting-body neighborhoods
and fixed positive Brownian tubes. Minimize their probabilities and
impulse margins only after constructing that finite cover. For a tilt,
compensate its constant local drift as in frozen Section 2. This is the
uniform persistent-facet cover used by the proof, not an unproved
changing-domain theorem or a cover built from disappearing faces.

4. OPTIONAL SOURCE-ERROR BOOKKEEPING (SAME PROOF)

The frozen displacement estimate is
  sup_C |z_t-z0| <= C(H+1)sqrt(D_C)
                     +C A T(H+1)^(3/2)n^-2.
On the force event for an initial facet jump b, the displacement or
persistent-contact alternatives give the explicit safe bound
  D_C >= c b^2/(H+1)^2-C A^2 T^2(H+1)n^-4.
This is immediate for jumps below the displayed source-error scale;
otherwise either displacement occurs or a positive impulse sees the
retained jump, exactly as in the source-free argument. Since H>=1,
dividing by H in the box allocation bounds the additive error by
C A^2 T^2 n^-4 per box, without a random positive H multiplier. The
positive term has denominator H(H+1)^2<=4H^3. Conditional Cauchy gives
the previous denominator 1+E(H_B^3|I). Consequently weights
  w_B=c/[1+E(H_B^8|I)]+suffice, after fixed facet-path and duplicate-edge constants. Summing
the O(n^2) boxes gives the existing O(A^2 T^2 n^-2) remainder in the
full boundary energy estimate. T is fixed there, so this is the same
O(A^2 n^-2) convention used in frozen (2.2), not a new higher moment
assumption or an extra scientific branch.
