CYCLE 3 — FERMIONIC REVERSE INTERFACE / PFaffian QUADRATURE
Date: 8 October 2026

Current scoped result:
- For the explicit rational real full-sector block-pair family, Z_{n/2} is
  product_i(a_i^2+b_i^2).
- The normalized Pfaffian-square sign integrand factors as
  product_i(1-gamma_i sigma_{u_i}sigma_{v_i}).
- Requiring one input-independent exact sign quadrature to work for every
  pairing and every rational block weight forces all nontrivial even Walsh
  moments to vanish.
- Fourier inversion on {+/-1}^n / global sign forces uniform mass on all
  2^(n-1) quotient classes. The bound is tight.
- Any fixed atom support that relatively approximates every rational
  block-pair input with nonnegative coefficients and epsilon<1 must cover
  every perfect pairing's all-opposite event. Pairing coverage forces at
  least ceil(binomial(2k,k)/2^k)=2^Omega(n) atoms, even if weights depend on A.
- Exact finite replay passes at n=6: 15 pairings, 31 nontrivial even
  characters, and quotient Walsh rank 32.
- These results rule out polynomial-support fixed-atom Pfaffian quadrature,
  including the stated relative-approximation version. They are not an FPRAS
  lower bound or a general Z_k hardness result.

exact for all rational block-pair inputs at an even n, or a nonuniform
probability on the global-sign quotient with all nontrivial character
moments zero, falsifies the theorem.
