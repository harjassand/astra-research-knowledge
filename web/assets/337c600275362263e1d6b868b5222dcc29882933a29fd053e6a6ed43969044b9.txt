RESULT

The acquisition/precision subproblem has a clean conditional resolution:
for any explicitly proposed finite-support microscopic MH kernel on
rational-complex full-basis line configurations, pointwise acceptance
ratios and finite-neighborhood proposal ratios are exactly computable in
polynomial bit length without the global partition function. Strict
worst-case runtime can be obtained by either (a) bounded dyadic approximation
with a proved trajectory TV budget, or (b) truncated exact randbelow with
explicit abort probability charged to the algorithm's failure budget.

The coordinate-dominated refresh also admits a sharper acquired bound for the
actual parity representation [I,F^T]: its contaminant upper bound is a
computable elementary-symmetric sum of row norms. This gives an exponentially
small numeric activity whose binary description has only
O(r log r + r*ell + log(1/eta)) bits in the intended explicit input regime.
The adjacent annealing normalizer ratio can be bounded and estimated without
an oracle along a uniform activity ramp.

These results do not provide the microscopic two-replica energy inequality,
a polynomial mixing bound, or a general parity FPRAS. Existing examples show
why arithmetic tractability is insufficient: a natural symmetric move can
have acceptance exactly 2^(-4L), and some bounded-size fixed-sector moves are
disconnected. The needed kernel must avoid or quantitatively overcome those
bottlenecks.

1. EXACT ACCEPTANCE AND POLYNOMIAL BIT HEIGHT

For even r, a line system has M explicit paired-vector lines
v_l=(v_l^(1),v_l^(2)) in Q(i)^r, with positive rational activities theta_l.
A full-basis state A selects r/2 lines and has

  w(A)=(product_(l in A) theta_l)|det W_A|^2,
  pi(A)=w(A)/Z,       Z=sum_A w(A).

For any rational proposal q(A,B)>0, the MH decision is

  alpha(A,B)=min(1, w(B)q(B,A)/(w(A)q(A,B))).

The normalization Z cancels. A fixed symmetric proposal makes q(B,A)/q(A,B)=1.
An explicitly enumerable polynomial-size neighborhood also permits exact
proposal normalization and reverse-degree computation. A proposal that
conditions on all completions of a hole pattern instead requires its
aggregate coefficient f(U); this is not made computable by pointwise
determinants or a support oracle.

Let ell be full input bit length. Clearing all rational-complex matrix
denominators gives a common D with O(ell) bits. Hadamard bounds every
r-dimensional determinant numerator by 2^(O(r(ell+log r))); its denominator
is D^r. The squared determinant and a product of at most r/2 rational line
activities therefore have polynomial bit length. Exact fraction-free
elimination, multiplication, reduction/comparison, and the MH ratio require
polynomial bit operations. A proposed singular basis is rejected after an
exact zero determinant test. No square roots or floating-point thresholds
are needed.

All full-basis weights share a denominator dividing D^(2r) times the product
of all line-activity denominators. There are at most binom(M,r/2) terms.
Thus an exact partition value also has polynomial output bit length when M is
polynomially bounded, even though direct summation can take exponentially
many operations. The same common-denominator argument gives polynomial bit
height for each f(U) sum of rational squared minors. The unresolved issue is
computing/acquiring these positive sums, not writing their exact values.

2. EVERY-EXECUTION ACCEPTANCE IMPLEMENTATIONS

For alpha=a/b reduced with 0<a<b, an exact fair-bit Bernoulli coin cannot
have a finite worst-case bit bound unless a/b is dyadic: every bounded-depth
fair-bit output event has dyadic probability. Ordinary rejection sampling
has constant expected trials but an unbounded tail.

Two bounded alternatives were derived.

- Dyadic coupling: with k fair bits U in {0,...,2^k-1}, accept iff
  U<floor(2^k a/b). The implemented probability differs from alpha by less
  than 2^-k. A T-transition trajectory is within T*2^-k TV of the ideal
  rational-MH trajectory under stepwise coupling. Choosing
  k=ceil(log2(T/eta)) costs k bits per transition and makes this extra error
  at most eta. The ideal MH kernel retains detailed balance; the finite
  trajectory is what is approximated.
- Exact-on-success abort: let B=ceil(log2 b). Repeatedly draw B bits X from
  [0,2^B), accept/reject exactly if X<b, and cap the number of trials at m.
  Since b/2^B>1/2, abort probability is below 2^-m. On non-abort paths the
  decision agrees exactly with ideal rejection sampling. Over T adaptive
  calls, choose m=ceil(log2(T/eta)) and charge total abort probability below
  eta as algorithmic failure. Worst-case bit use is O(T*B*log(T/eta)); B is
  polynomial from the determinant/activity height bounds. Do not describe
  the abort-conditioned kernel as exactly stationary.

The same capped randbelow sampler gives a uniform coordinate matching
exactly on success. Recursive partner counts r-1,r-3,...,1 are deterministic;
with m=ceil(log2(r/(2 eta))) trials per choice, total abort probability is
at most eta. Conditional on no abort the whole matching is uniform because
each choice's success probability depends only on its deterministic count.
Worst-case random-bit use is
O(r log r log(r/eta)). The lower-bit modulo sampler in
acceptance_cost_audit.py instead always returns and has a separately bounded
TV bias.

3. COORDINATE-DOMINATED REFRESH: REFINED ACTUAL-INTERFACE BOUND

In V=[I,F^T], an original line is (e_i,f_i), one unit column and one row
vector; a coordinate line (e_a,e_b) has two unit columns. Add all
M0=binom(r,2) coordinate lines at activity 1 and original line i at activity
epsilon*lambda_i. Let

  h_i=||f_i||_2^2=sum_j |F_ij|^2,   a_i=lambda_i h_i,
  S_F=sum_(s=1)^(r/2) binom(M0,r/2-s)e_s(a_1,...,a_N).

For a selected set I of s original lines, Hadamard gives
|det W_A|^2<=product_(i in I)h_i. There are at most
binom(M0,r/2-s) coordinate-line choices. Summing these bounds yields

  C <= sum_(s=1)^(r/2) epsilon^s binom(M0,r/2-s)e_s(a)
    <= epsilon*S_F,                 (epsilon<=1),

where C is all non-coordinate base weight. The coordinate-only partition is
Z0=(r-1)!!, with uniform perfect-matching law. The base-law TV distance from
that matching law is the contaminant mass, at most C/Z0. Choose dyadic
epsilon=2^-K with 2^-K S_F<=eta Z0. S_F is exactly computable by the
elementary-symmetric recurrence and a binomial convolution in O(Nr+r^2)
rational arithmetic. Integer comparisons find K; no approximate logarithm is
needed. For M0=Theta(r^2), N=O(r), and ell-bit explicit rational input,

  K=O(r log r+r*ell+log(1/eta)+log Lambda),

where Lambda can bound nonuniform final activities (or is absorbed into the
exact a_i). Hence epsilon can be numerically exponentially small while its
description and all uses in weights remain polynomial-bit. A loop count
proportional to 1/epsilon would still be exponential and is not justified by
this refresh bound.

The earlier global bound epsilon*2^M*r^r*R^(2r) is also valid for arbitrary
paired vectors; it gives a polynomial-bit but looser K=O(M+r log r+r*ell).
The refined O(r log r+r*ell) form relies on the special (e_i,f_i) structure.

For a uniform scalar ramp theta_i(t)=t*lambda_i with fixed coordinate
activities, a state with s original lines changes weight by c^s when
t'=ct, 0<=s<=r/2. Therefore

  Z(t')/Z(t)=E_(pi_t)[c^s] in [1,c^(r/2)],
  c^(-r/2)<=pi_(t')(A)/pi_t(A)<=c^(r/2).

At c=1+1/r, this density ratio lies in [e^(-1/2),e^(1/2)]. From
t=2^-K to one takes at most 2rK stages; rational schedule entries have
polynomial bit length. The unknown partition ratio is bounded by the known
pointwise ratio and can be estimated as an expectation from pi_t samples. A
Hoeffding telescoping estimate uses O(J^3 epsilon^(-2)log(J/delta)) draws
for J stages, conditional on obtaining those samples within a polynomial
mixing bound. Coordinate activities that change independently and zero
endpoints require their own pointwise-ratio/support analysis.

4. EXACT BOTTLENECK AND FAILURE BOUNDARIES

For the inherited four-site matrix

  F=[[0,delta,1,0],[delta,0,0,1],[1,0,0,1],[0,1,1,0]], delta=2^(-L),

the paired determinants for (I,J)=({1,2},{3,4}) and its complementary
minor are 1,1. For the reassignment ({1,3},{2,4}) they are delta,delta.
The two-replica squared-weight ratio is delta^4=2^(-4L). Conditional on
proposing this reassignment each attempt, its symmetric MH acceptance is
2^(-4L); if it is the sole bridge, the expected crossing wait is 2^(4L).
The ratio takes O(L) bits to compute and simulate.
This is not a universal mixing lower bound, but refutes any claimed
polynomial lower bound on all such local acceptances from input precision
alone. The directed-cycle example separately disconnects fixed-size local
pair-label moves for exchange size below q. These cases demand a new route,
not a higher-precision determinant computation.
