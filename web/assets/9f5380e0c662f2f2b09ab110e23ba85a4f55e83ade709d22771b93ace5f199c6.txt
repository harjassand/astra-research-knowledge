FROZEN ROOT DERIVATION V4 — NONLINEAR MIDPOINT BOUND
Internal candidate; independently checking constants and priority remains necessary.

For probability vectors p,q with total variation v, the Jeffreys divergence J=D(p||q)+D(q||p) satisfies
                         J >= 4 v atanh(v).
Proof: apply data processing to A={p>q}, whose binary probabilities differ by v. If q(A)=a, p(A)=a+v, binary Jeffreys is v log[(a+v)(1-a)/(a(1-a-v))]. Its minimum for a in [0,1-v] occurs at a=(1-v)/2 (differentiate, or use the symmetric product denominator), giving 4v atanh(v). Endpoint v=1 has infinite divergence.

The function g(z)=4 sqrt(z) atanh(sqrt(z))=4 sum_{k>=1} z^k/(2k-1), 0<=z<1, is convex and increasing. Apply Jensen to conditional hidden-midpoint pairs, whose average Jeffreys is <=T sigma, as in v2.

For a centered bounded observable of range R with negative autocovariance C(T)=-c, the midpoint identity requires E TV_i^2>=4c/R^2. Therefore
        sigma T >= g(4c/R^2).
The argument already forces 4c/R^2<=1. Equality at1 cannot occur for finite entropy production. The linear bound 16c/R^2 is the first series term, not a claimed sharp physical EP law.

For symmetric copositive W, gap delta=[-<W,P>]_+, M=max|W_uv|, the same argument gives
        sigma T >= g(delta/M).
This nonlinear strengthening requires the same physical parity and stationarity assumptions as v2; no additional observations or independence between hidden trajectories are assumed.

EXACT BALANCED-BINARY MIDPOINT RELAXATION
For a binary pair law with marginal1/2 and covariance -c, 0<c<1/4, the convex-roof midpoint cost K(P) defined in v2 equals
                 K(P)=g(4c)=8 sqrt(c) atanh(2sqrt(c)).
The lower bound was just proved for every latent factorization by midpoint algebra, regardless of whether it comes from a CTMC. For the upper bound take two latent atoms with equal weights, with the factors Bernoulli(1/2+sqrt(c)), Bernoulli(1/2-sqrt(c)), swapped in the second atom. Their mixture has entries P11=P00=1/4-c, P10=P01=1/4+c and exactly the displayed Jeffreys cost.

This is an exact variational factorization formula, NOT the exact least entropy production among compatible stationary CTMCs. In the dynamical problem, data-processing from entire conditional paths to endpoint observations and compatibility with one generator may impose an additional positive gap. Determining that gap is an active stronger target supplied to Sol.
