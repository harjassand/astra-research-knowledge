NEGATIVE SCALED COUPLING: A FULL-FOCK THERMAL DIVERGENCE WITNESS
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: internally derived theorem/counterexample; external proof review and
priority remain UNKNOWN. This result concerns thermal number tightness only;
it makes no sampler-impossibility claim.

1. Exact interface and statement
--------------------------------
There are S=L-1 open-chain spin-1/2 sites, with n_i=(1-Z_i)/2,

  N=sum_{i=1}^S n_i,     W=sum_{i=1}^{S-1} n_i n_{i+1},
  Hcrit=J sum_{i=1}^{S-1}(I-X_iX_{i+1}-Y_iY_{i+1}-Z_iZ_{i+1})
             +J(I-Z_1)+J(I-Z_S),

where J>0. For S=1 the two endpoint terms both act on that site; only
large L matters below. The supplied open-chain Hamiltonian is

  H_L=Hcrit+(g_L/L)W-(lambda/L^2)N,
  beta_L=tau L^2,   tau>0,   g_L -> g<0,   |lambda|<=B.

The full partition function is Z_L=Tr exp(-beta_L H_L), over all 2^S
occupation states. In the occupation basis, if X~X' means a permitted
nearest-neighbor particle hop, then exactly

  <f,Hcrit f>=2J sum_{unordered {X,X'}:X~X'}|f(X)-f(X')|^2
                  +2J sum_X(n_1(X)+n_S(X))|f(X)|^2 >=0.       (1)

Equivalently, Hcrit has diagonal entries 4J N(X)-4J W(X) and hopping
matrix entries -2J. Formula (1) includes both open-end fields; they are not
discarded in the trial calculation.

The theorem proved here is

  lim_{L->infinity} L^(-2) log Z_L = tau |g|.                  (2)

In particular the grand-canonical Gibbs particle number is not tight: for
every fixed K, P_L(N<=K)->0. More quantitatively, every fixed-m canonical
partition contribution is O_m(1), while Z_L grows as exp(tau|g|L^2+o(L^2));
thus the probability of N<=K is at most exp(-tau|g|L^2+o(L^2)).

2. Exact coherent boundary-ramp construction
---------------------------------------------
Let w be an integer with 2w+1<=S. Define the angle profile, for i=1,...,S,

  theta_i = pi*min{1,(i-1)/w,(S-i)/w}.

Thus theta_1=theta_S=0, theta_i=pi on the macroscopic interior, and there
are two linear ramps of w bonds each. Use the normalized product state

  |Psi_{L,w}> = tensor_{i=1}^S
       [cos(theta_i/2)|0>_i + sin(theta_i/2)|1>_i].

Its one-site occupation is p_i=sin^2(theta_i/2). The endpoint occupations
are exactly zero and the plateau occupations are exactly one. The state is
pure and has zero von Neumann entropy; no entropy term is needed in the
Gibbs variational lower bound.

For a product state with Bloch vector (sin theta_i,0,cos theta_i), the exact
expectations of each bond and endpoint term are

  <J(I-sigma_i.sigma_{i+1})>=J[1-cos(theta_{i+1}-theta_i)],
  <J(I-Z_1)>=J[1-cos(theta_1)],
  <J(I-Z_S)>=J[1-cos(theta_S)].

Every ramp has w increments of magnitude pi/w, and both endpoint fields
vanish. Therefore the complete critical energy, including hopping,
diagonal terms, and endpoints, is exactly

  <Hcrit>=2Jw[1-cos(pi/w)].                               (3)

The number and contact observables are also exact. The ramp-site sum uses
sum_{r=0}^{w-1} sin^2(pi r/(2w))=(w-1)/2, so

  <N>=S-2w+2 sum_{r=0}^{w-1}sin^2(pi r/(2w))=L-w-2.       (4)

For W, the full-occupation plateau contributes L-2w-2 bonds. Each ramp
contributes

  R_w=sum_{r=0}^{w-1}sin^2(pi r/(2w))sin^2(pi(r+1)/(2w)).

For w>=2, the finite trigonometric sum is
R_w=w/4+(w/8)cos(pi/w), hence

  <W>=L-2w-2+2R_w
      =L-(3/2)w-2+(w/4)cos(pi/w).                         (5)

In particular <W>/L->1 whenever w=o(L). The unsimplified sum above is an
exact formula as well and fixes the ramp/plateau bond counting.

3. Gibbs variational lower bound and a matching upper bound
-----------------------------------------------------------
The Gibbs variational principle gives log Z_L >= -beta_L<Psi|H_L|Psi>.
Using (3)-(5), this is the explicit finite-L bound

  log Z_L >= tau[-2J L^2 w(1-cos(pi/w)) - g_L L<W> + lambda<N>].   (6)

Choose, for example, w=floor(sqrt(L)); for sufficiently large L it obeys
2w+1<=L-1. Then w->infinity, w/L->0,

  2Jw(1-cos(pi/w)) -> 0,  <W>/L -> 1,  <N>/L^2 -> 0.

Since g_L->g<0, (6) yields

  liminf L^(-2)log Z_L >= tau|g|.                            (7)

The bound is uniform over |lambda|<=B: the lambda term divided by L^2 has
magnitude at most B/L. More explicitly, writing gamma=|g|, for all large
L one has -g_L>=gamma/2 and <W>/L>=1/2. The positive interaction term in
(6) is then at least gamma L^2/4 before multiplication by tau, whereas the
critical-ramp loss is O(JL^2/w) and the field loss is O(BL). Thus
log Z_L>=c L^2 for some c>0 depending on tau,J,g and all sufficiently large
L.

For the reverse leading bound, (1), W<=S-1=L-2, N<=L-1, and |lambda|<=B
imply

  H_L >= g_L (L-2)/L - B(L-1)/L^2,
  log Z_L <= (L-1)log 2 - tau g_L L(L-2) + tau B(L-1).    (8)

Divide (8) by L^2 and combine with (7) to obtain (2). This identifies the
leading free-energy scale, not just a divergent subsequence.

4. Fixed-number sectors: O(1) bound and attractive LL limit
----------------------------------------------------------
For each fixed m, the canonical sector partition remains bounded uniformly
in L when g_L is bounded. Here is a finite-lattice proof; it is not inferred
from the grand partition or from a free-fermion spectrum.

Write an m-particle configuration as x_1<...<x_m, and use gap coordinates
y_i=x_i-i+1, so 1<=y_1<=...<=y_m<=n with n=L-m. If the value v occurs r_v
times, let w(y)=m!/prod_v r_v!. Define the symmetric-cube contraction

  (K_m psi)(z_1,...,z_m)=psi(sort(z_1,...,z_m))/sqrt(m!),
  z in {1,...,n}^m.

Then ||K_m psi||^2=sum_y [w(y)/m!]|psi(y)|^2<=||psi||^2. Let Hfree be
the sum of m independent Dirichlet path Laplacians on this labeled cube,
each one-particle form being

  2J[sum_{v=1}^{n-1}|u(v+1)-u(v)|^2+|u(1)|^2+|u(n)|^2].

Counting labeled preimage edges of each legal exclusion hop, including the
two ghost-boundary edges, gives the exact form comparison

  K_m^* Hfree K_m <= Hcrit,m.                              (9)

More explicitly, for a chamber hop moving a coordinate from v to v+1,
with source multiplicities r_v and r_{v+1}, the number of labeled cube
preimage edges is w(y)r_v=m!/[(r_v-1)!r_{v+1}!prod_{u not in {v,v+1}}r_u!]
<=m!. Each K amplitude contributes the factor 1/m!, so its total edge
energy is no larger than the corresponding exclusion edge energy. At a
ghost face the preimage count is w(y)r_1 or w(y)r_n, also at most m!; these
are controlled by the corresponding endpoint-killing terms in (1).

Only its kinetic-form consequence is used: with
Q=L^2<Hcrit> and Qgrid=sum over labeled-cube edges (including ghost edges)
of |Delta K_m psi|^2, (9) gives the scaled inequality
2J L^2 Qgrid<=Q. It does NOT imply a free
fermion trace bound through K_m, which is nonisometric.

For a weakly ordered y define
W(y)=sum_v(r_v-1), P(y)=sum_v binom(r_v,2). Then P(y)>=W(y), and an exact
orbit count gives

  sum_{i<j}sum_{z_i=z_j}|K_m psi(z)|^2
      =sum_y [P(y)/prod_v r_v!] |psi(y)|^2.

Since prod_v r_v!<=m!, this implies

  <W> <= m! sum_{i<j}sum_{z_i=z_j}|K_m psi(z)|^2.         (10)

The required discrete trace estimate is elementary. Fix a pair i<j, a
diagonal node z_i=z_j=s, and all other coordinates. Choose a direction in
coordinate i with at least R available steps; this is possible when
n>=2R+1. For a_r=(K_m psi)(...,s+direction*r,...,z_j=s,...), telescoping
and Cauchy-Schwarz give

  |a_0|^2 <= 2/(R+1)sum_{r=0}^R|a_r|^2
                 +2R sum_{r=0}^{R-1}|a_{r+1}-a_r|^2.

For fixed pair the sampled nodes and edges are injective as the diagonal
node and remaining coordinates vary. Sum this inequality, then use
||K_m psi||<=1 and Qgrid<=Q/(2J L^2). With chi=binom(m,2),

  L<W> <= m! chi[2L/(R+1) + R Q/(J L)].                 (11)

Let G bound |g_L| eventually, choose

  eta=min{1/4,J/(2G m! chi)},  R=floor(eta L).

For fixed m and sufficiently large L, n>=2R+1, R>=eta L/2, and (11)
implies

  Q+g_L L<W> >= Q/2-C_m,
  C_m=4G m! chi/eta.                                      (12)

Thus the scaled attractive sector operator is bounded below by half the
critical operator minus a fixed scalar. Min-max and Hcrit>=Hneu, where
Hneu is the reflecting symmetric-exclusion graph Laplacian, give

  Tr_m exp[-tau L^2(Hcrit+(g_L/L)W)]
      <= exp(tau C_m) Tr_m exp[-(tau/2)L^2 Hneu].         (13)

For clarity, the last heat trace is uniformly bounded at fixed m. The
reflecting exclusion process has jump rate 2J. Its diagonal return kernel
satisfies

  P_t(X,X) <= product_{i in X}sum_{j in X}p_t(i,j),

by the occupation-product supersolution for the symmetric exclusion
generator. One direct verification is to set
u_i(t)=E_X[n_i(t)]=sum_{j in X}p_t(i,j),
v_t(Y)=E_X[product_{i in Y}n_i(t)], and F_t(Y)=product_{i in Y}u_i(t).
The exclusion generator gives partial_t v=mathcal L_m v, while direct product-rule
calculation gives

  (partial_t-mathcal L_m)F_t(Y)
    =2J sum_{edges {i,j} subset Y}(u_i-u_j)^2
                       product_{l in Y\{i,j}}u_l >=0.

Since v_0=F_0 and the finite Markov semigroup preserves positivity,
v_t<=F_t. At Y=X, all m initial sites are occupied iff the full
configuration is X, giving the displayed return-kernel bound. On a
reflecting path of S sites, at t=(tau/2)L^2 the one-particle kernel obeys
p_t(i,j)<=C_{1/2}/S, where

  C_{1/2}=1+2sum_{k>=1}exp(-4J tau k^2).

Summing over m-sets yields

  Tr_m exp[-(tau/2)L^2 Hneu] <= (e C_{1/2})^m.             (14)

Equations (13)-(14), including the fugacity e^(tau lambda m), give

  Z_{L,m}(lambda,g_L)
      <= exp[tau(C_m+B m)](e C_{1/2})^m = O_m(1).          (15)

For m=0 the sector weight is 1; for m=1 W=0 and the same exclusion heat
bound applies directly. The constants in (15) may grow rapidly with m and
are not a uniform number-tail bound. This is exactly compatible with (2).

The fixed-m attractive Lieb-Liniger limit has symmetric Dirichlet form

  q_{m,g}(F)=2J sum_i integral |partial_i F|^2
      +2g sum_{i<j} integral_{x_i=x_j}|F|^2,
  F in H^1_0((0,1)^m) intersect L^2_sym((0,1)^m).          (16)

For g<0, the diagonal trace inequality
||F|_{x_i=x_j}||_2^2<=4||F||||grad F|| gives, with chi=binom(m,2),

  q_{m,g}(F) >= J||grad F||^2
                  -(16 g^2 chi^2/J)||F||^2.             (17)

Consequently the fixed-m attractive LL operator is lower bounded, has
compact resolvent, and has finite heat trace for every tau>0. Its grand
fugacity factor exp(tau lambda m) is finite for fixed m. If one uses the
fixed-m heat-trace convergence in the attractive LL scaling, (15) is the
finite-chain uniform bound and the sector trace converges to this finite
continuum value. No convergence uniform in m is asserted or needed for the
cutoff conclusion.

5. Exact finite checks, complexity, and scope
---------------------------------------------
`checks.py` uses SymPy exact algebraic/trigonometric arithmetic to compare,
for L=6,w=2; L=8,w=3; and L=10,w=4, the complete occupation-basis matrix
expectation (all 2^(L-1) amplitudes, including all hopping terms) against
the independent spin-product formula. It also checks exact normalization,
<N>, and <W>. All three cases pass; the exact values are recorded in
`checks.json`. The L=6 witness has <Hcrit>=4J, <N>=2, <W>=1. These are
finite algebraic checks only, not evidence for (2) beyond the explicit
proof.

The full physical Hilbert dimension is 2^(L-1), or L-1 occupation qubits;
the fixed-m block dimension is binom(L-1,m). Preparing the exact product
trial requires L-1 single-qubit real rotations, O(L) angle descriptions,
and O(L) arithmetic evaluations of the observables in (3)-(5). A
conservative synthesis to total state error delta uses O(log(L/delta))
angle bits per rotation (telescoping the L local errors). Since
||H_L||=O(JL+|g_L|+B/L) and beta=O(L^2), delta=O(L^-3) suffices to keep the
variational exponent error O(1); a smaller accuracy can be selected for a
prescribed exponent tolerance. This is a trial-state witness, not a
finite-L Gibbs sampler or partition-function algorithm. Generic storage of
the full partition still ranges over 2^(L-1) states; no sampler lower bound
is inferred.

Claims are restricted to the open chain, bounded lambda, fixed J,tau,
g_L->g<0, and beta=tau L^2. The proof does not apply to the repulsive side,
the exactly resonant g=0 endpoint, periodic chains, number-conserving
cutoff algorithms, or arbitrary Slater/BCS inputs. Internal proof review,
external review, and historical priority are separate and remain open.
