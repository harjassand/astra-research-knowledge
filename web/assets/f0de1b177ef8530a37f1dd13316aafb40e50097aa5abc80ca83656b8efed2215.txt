INDEPENDENT CYCLE06 SCALAR THEOREM — FROZEN BLIND DERIVATION

Status: internally derived exact theorem. No Cycle06 sibling proof read.
No operator Jensen, entropy production, modular covariance, or tensor
amplification premise is used. All spaces in this theorem are finite
dimensional. Faithfulness is required only of the physical reference sigma.

THEOREM
Let sigma>0 be a density matrix on C^d. Let
  Psi(X)=sum_i p_i pi_i Tr(sigma^(-1/2) pi_i sigma^(-1/2) X),
where pi_i=|v_i><v_i|, ||v_i||=1, p_i>0, sum_i p_i pi_i=sigma.
For every density matrix rho (not necessarily faithful), put q=sqrt(rho)
and
  T(X)=sigma^(-1/4) Psi(sigma^(1/4) X sigma^(1/4)) sigma^(-1/4),
  E=Tr(q(q-T(q))),  e=(1/2)||rho-Psi(rho)||_1.
Then
  e^2 <= (1+1/sqrt(2)) E < 2 E  whenever E>0,
and e=0 when E=0. The non-strict bound e^2<=2E holds uniformly.
There is no dimension, smallest-reference-eigenvalue, or atom-count factor.
The sharper constant above is an upper bound; sharpness is not asserted.

1. EXACT OVERCOMPLETE-FRAME REDUCTION
First suppose the ensemble is finite, with N atoms. Define U:C^N->C^d
by its columns
  u_i=sqrt(p_i) sigma^(-1/2) v_i.
Then UU^*=I_d. Thus V=U^*:C^d->C^N is an isometry and P=VV^* is the
rank-d support projection. Embed physical matrices by X -> V X V^*.
Set
  A=V q V^*, B=V sqrt(sigma) V^*.
These are positive, Tr A^2=Tr B^2=1, and B is faithful ON P. B may be
singular on C^N when the ensemble is overcomplete. Positive powers of B
are the ordinary supported powers (zero on P^perp); no inverse of B is
needed in the argument. In particular
  p_i=(B^2)_{ii}>0, a_i=A e_i, b_i=B e_i,
  Q_i=||a_i||^2=(A^2)_{ii}, ||b_i||^2=p_i.
Using B e_i=sqrt(p_i) V v_i, direct substitution gives
  V Psi(rho) V^*=B diag(Q_i/p_i) B.
Using supported B^(1/2)=V sigma^(1/4) V^*, direct substitution also gives
  V T(q) V^*=mathcal T(A),
where on all N-by-N matrices
  mathcal T(X)=sum_i Z_i Tr(Z_i X)/p_i,
  Z_i=B^(1/2)|e_i><e_i|B^(1/2).
All trace norms and Hilbert-Schmidt norms of physical embedded matrices
are preserved by V. Nothing here assumes U is square or unitary.

2. POSITIVE CONTRACTION AND THE TWO EXACT ENERGY PIECES
The map mathcal T is a positive semidefinite, Hilbert-Schmidt selfadjoint
operator: it is a sum of positive rank-one Gram operators |Z_i><Z_i|/p_i.
Its nonzero spectrum is the spectrum of the N-by-N Gram matrix
  G_ij=Tr(Z_i Z_j)/sqrt(p_i p_j)=|B_ij|^2/sqrt(p_i p_j).
Writing P_ij=|B_ij|^2/p_i, the identity sum_j |B_ij|^2=p_i shows that P
is row stochastic. G=diag(sqrt(p)) P diag(1/sqrt(p)), so its spectral
radius is at most 1. G is also positive semidefinite as a Gram matrix.
Consequently 0<=mathcal T<=I in Hilbert-Schmidt order, including its
zero eigenspace outside span{Z_i}. This is an exact finite spectral
argument, not a numerical spectrum.

Put
  z_i=(B^(1/2) A B^(1/2))_ii,
  f_i=z_i/p_i, F=diag(f_i), A'=mathcal T(A)=B^(1/2) F B^(1/2),
  S=<A,mathcal T(A)>=sum_i p_i f_i^2=||BF||_2^2,
  E=1-S,
  D=<A,mathcal T(I-mathcal T)A>.
Here f_i>=0 because A>=0; the estimates below need only that f_i is real.
Expansion of the trace, using F diagonal, gives the EXACT identities
  D=S-||A'||_2^2
   =sum_i p_i f_i^2-sum_ij |B_ij|^2 f_i f_j
   =(1/2)sum_ij |B_ij|^2(f_i-f_j)^2
   =(1/2)||[B,F]||_2^2,
  ||A-A'||_2^2=1-2S+||A'||_2^2=E-D.
Positive contraction implies 0<=D<=E. This includes E=0 and all support
degeneracies of the overcomplete representation.

3. THE COMMUTATOR ESTIMATE (NO OPERATOR JENSEN)
Split BF into its Hermitian and anti-Hermitian parts:
  H=(BF+FB)/2, K=(BF-FB)/2, R=A'-H.
Then R is Hermitian, K is anti-Hermitian, and
  ||K||_2^2=D/2.
Diagonalize B with eigenvalues beta_j>=0, and write F in that eigenbasis.
The (j,k) coefficient of R is
  (sqrt(beta_j beta_k)-(beta_j+beta_k)/2) F_jk
  =-(1/2)(sqrt(beta_j)-sqrt(beta_k))^2 F_jk.
Since
  (sqrt(beta_j)-sqrt(beta_k))^2 <= |beta_j-beta_k|,
including zero eigenvalues, summing coefficient squares gives
  ||R||_2^2 <= (1/4)||[B,F]||_2^2=D/2.
Hermitian and anti-Hermitian matrices are orthogonal for the REAL
Hilbert-Schmidt inner product; cross terms in squared norms vanish.
Therefore, with delta=A-A',
  ||A-BF||_2^2=||delta+R||_2^2+||K||_2^2
   <= (sqrt(E-D)+sqrt(D/2))^2+D/2
   =E+sqrt(2D(E-D))
   <=(1+1/sqrt(2))E,
because D(E-D)<=E^2/4. This also gives ||A-BF||_2=0 when E=0.

4. THE SCALAR TRACE-DISTURBANCE ESTIMATE BY COLUMNS
The two physical states, embedded by V, have decompositions
  A^2=sum_i |a_i><a_i|,
  B diag(Q_i/p_i) B=sum_i (Q_i/p_i)|b_i><b_i|.
For Q_i=0 both terms vanish. For Q_i>0, the equal-trace rank-one
trace-distance formula gives
  (1/2)|| |a_i><a_i|-(Q_i/p_i)|b_i><b_i| ||_1
   =sqrt(Q_i) sqrt(Q_i-|<a_i,b_i>|^2/p_i).
The inner quantity is nonnegative by Cauchy-Schwarz. Triangle inequality
and weighted Cauchy-Schwarz, with sum_i Q_i=Tr A^2=1, now give
  e^2 <= sum_i (Q_i-|<a_i,b_i>|^2/p_i)
       =min_{C diagonal, complex entries} ||A-BC||_2^2
       <=||A-BF||_2^2.
Combining with section 3 proves the theorem. This is where scalar pure
columns are used. It is NOT automatically valid for block columns in an
ancilla/reference amplification.

5. ENSEMBLES WITH A MEASURE, ZERO WEIGHTS, AND OTHER SUPPORTS
Zero-weight atoms may simply be deleted. For a general pure probability
ensemble in fixed finite d, the map can be represented by a finite pure
ensemble preserving the same sigma and the same Psi. Indeed, writing
  J=Psi circ [X -> sigma^(1/2) X sigma^(1/2)]
as a real operator on Hermitian matrices gives
  J=sum_i p_i |pi_i><pi_i|.
The pure atom set is compact in the real symmetric-operator space on
n=d^2 dimensions, and every atom has superoperator trace 1. Hence its
closed convex hull is its finite convex hull. Caratheodory gives at most
n(n+1)/2=d^2(d^2+1)/2 atoms. The barycenter is preserved automatically
because J(I)=sigma. Thus the finite-frame proof covers arbitrary pure
ensemble representations of a canonical channel in finite d. The atom
count is a representation statement, not an algorithmic cost assertion.
No extension to an infinite-dimensional nonfaithful global reference is
asserted here. Nonfaithful rho causes no problem at any step.

6. SCOPE AND NEXT INTERFACES
This closes the requested SCALAR Q_rho trace-disturbance gate. It does
not establish a bound after id_R tensor Psi, since that map is neither a
global pure canonical EB channel nor covered by section 4 automatically.
It does not establish entropy production >=2E (or >=4E) for arbitrary
KMS-reversible maps. No general nontracial power theorem is claimed.
The separate still-needed entropy interface would be an exact, uniform
inequality between genuine physical relative-entropy production and
the Q_rho KMS Dirichlet form, with stated constant and complete scope.
The raw-broadcaster-to-KMS-reference interface is a further distinct
obligation for an unrestricted broadcasting/observational-radius theorem.
