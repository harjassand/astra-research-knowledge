SU(d) GLOBAL-CASIMIR POSITIVITY: INDEPENDENT AUDIT AND SUBALGEBRA GATE

ATTRIBUTION AND SCOPE

The assigned question was whether the global-Casimir positivity mechanism
extends to the full fundamental SU(d) algebra, and whether it survives
replacement by a proper generator subalgebra. I read c04_s02's
QUDIT_POSITIVITY_LOCALIZATION.txt and c05_s01's
03_su_q_casimir_stopped_filter.txt as authorized peer science; I did not read
peer scripts. I reconstructed the full-basis identity, the heat integral,
and the proper-spin-1 counterexample below. The exact SU(d) diffusion and
Weyl-heat identities are attributed starting machinery in those peer reports
and in standard compact-group heat-kernel theory; this audit makes no
priority claim. c04_s01's FULL_RANK_STOPPING result is separate attributed
work. The new exact arithmetic diagnostic is my own.

RESULT

The global-Casimir route is valid for the COMPLETE fundamental su(d) basis.
Two independent exact representations establish it:

  (i) a positive Weyl/Gaussian integral for exp(t sum_a F_a^2), valid in
      every finite SU(d) representation;
  (ii) a globally PSD principal tensor on the open full-rank qutrit/qudit
       density manifold, yielding an exact Ito/Feynman--Kac iid-product
       mixture for exp(kappa Q_I+J_B), including arbitrary traceless
       one-body B.

Full-algebra completeness is load-bearing. On a spin-1 qutrit, the same
principal-tensor construction using only the embedded three-generator su(2)
subalgebra has an exact negative quadratic direction at a full-rank state,
even though its Casimir is positive and scalar on one site. Thus the global
PSD SDE does not transfer to arbitrary proper subalgebras on the full
density-matrix manifold. This counterexample does not refute the special
spin-s thermal-orbit method, which uses a smaller invariant family rather
than all qutrit density directions.

1. EXACT POSITIVE FULL-SU(d) HEAT IDENTITY

Use Hermitian traceless generators T_a with Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2, set
F_a=sum_i T_a^(i), and C2=sum_a F_a^2. Let X be traceless Hermitian, with
eigenvalues x_1,...,x_d. Define the positive function

  j_+(X)=product_(i<j) sinh((x_i-x_j)/2)/((x_i-x_j)/2),

where each factor is 1 at zero, and the probability measure

  mu_t(dX)=Z_t^(-1) exp[-Tr(X^2)/(2t)] j_+(X) dX,   t>0.

This is finite and positive: j_+ grows at most exponentially in ||X||,
while the Gaussian is quadratic. For every irreducible SU(d) representation
with highest weight lambda, conjugation invariance makes
int pi_lambda(e^X) mu_t(dX) a scalar. Taking the trace and applying Weyl
integration, the Cartan integrand is, up to constants independent of lambda,

  exp[-|x|^2/(2t)] Delta(x) A_(lambda+rho)(x),

because the squared Weyl Jacobian Delta(x)^2 is multiplied by
j_+(x)=A_rho(x)/Delta(x), and the Weyl character is
A_(lambda+rho)(x)/A_rho(x). Expand the alternant A_(lambda+rho). For each
term, completing the square shifts the Gaussian by t w(lambda+rho). The
discriminant Delta is harmonic: its Laplacian would be an alternating
polynomial of degree two less than the least possible degree, hence zero.
Gaussian averaging therefore evaluates Delta at the shifted mean. Summing
the Weyl terms and dividing by the trivial-representation integral gives

  int pi_lambda(e^X) mu_t(dX)
    = exp{(t/2)(|lambda+rho|^2-|rho|^2)} I
    = exp(t C2(lambda)) I.

Here the norm is the standard root-length-squared-2 weight norm; with
Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2, the Casimir eigenvalue is exactly the displayed
half-difference. Colliding eigenvalues follow by continuity. This proves
the operator identity on reducible representations as well, including
(C^d)^tensor N:

  exp(t C2) = int (exp X)^tensor N mu_t(dX).

Each factor exp(X)/Tr(exp X) is a positive local density. Reweight mu_t by
(Tr exp X)^N and normalize to obtain an exact positive mixture of identical
local products for exp(t C2)/Tr exp(t C2). For a traceless Hermitian
one-body B, [C2,J_B]=0. Any Hermitian B is covered by writing
B=B_0+(Tr B/d)I: the scalar part contributes only N Tr(B)/d times the
identity to the collective exponent and disappears after normalization.
Hence

  exp(t C2+J_B)=exp(J_B/2) exp(t C2) exp(J_B/2),

and exp(J_B/2)=(exp(B/2))^tensor N sends every integrand to the product of
the positive local matrices exp(B/2) exp(X) exp(B/2). Therefore the exact
isotropic-plus-field density is also a positive iid-product mixture for
every finite d,N,t>=0. The t=0 case is the single product seed filtered by
exp(B/2).

This derivation uses only Weyl integration/character identities and a
positive, explicit law. Hall's compact-group Segal--Bargmann framework is a
primary background reference (JFA 122 (1994), DOI
10.1006/jfan.1994.1064); the scalar identity above is derived here rather
than imported as a black box. c05_s01 independently gives the same
normalization and identity in its Section 1.

2. FULL-ALGEBRA PRINCIPAL FORM AND EXACT STOCHASTIC REPRESENTATION

For a full-rank density rho, k_N(rho)=rho^tensor N, a traceless Hermitian T,
x_T=Tr(rho T), define tangent fields

  v_T={T,rho}-2 x_T rho,       r_T=-i[T,rho].

Normalized filtering and local rotation give

  {F_T,k_N}=(2N x_T+v_T.grad)k_N,
  [F_T,k_N]=i(r_T.grad)k_N,
  {F_T^2,k_N}/2 = ((2N x_T+v_T.grad)^2-(r_T.grad)^2)k_N/4.

The last equality follows from commuting constant left/right F_T actions;
the commutator contribution has the minus sign shown. For the complete
Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2 basis, the second-order principal tensor is

  D_I=sum_a (v_a tensor v_a-r_a tensor r_a).

For any Hermitian tangent Y, put y=Tr(rho Y). Basis completeness gives

  <Y,D_I Y>
   =1/2(Tr({rho,Y}-2y rho)^2-Tr(-i[rho,Y])^2)
   =2 Tr[rho(Y-yI)rho(Y-yI)]
   =2 ||sqrt(rho)(Y-yI)sqrt(rho)||_2^2 >= 0.

Equivalently,

  D_I=sum_a w_a tensor w_a,
  w_a=2(sqrt(rho) T_a sqrt(rho)-x_a rho).

This is a global factorization on the entire open state space. If rho>=e I,
then D_I>=2e^2 I on the traceless tangent space; at the boundary the tensor
may degenerate, so the margin is not uniform up to rank loss.

For H=kappa Q_I+J_B, kappa>=0, the exact full-kernel generator has
covariance (kappa/2)D_I and yields the explicit SDE

  d rho = [kappa(N-1)(rho^2-Tr(rho^2)rho)
           +{B,rho}/2-Tr(rho B)rho]dt
          +sqrt(2 kappa) sum_a
              (sqrt(rho)T_a sqrt(rho)-x_a rho) dW_a.

The trace and Hermiticity are preserved. The log-determinant ell=log det rho
has the exact drift and quadratic variation

  L ell=-kappa d[(2N-1)||x||^2+c_d]-d Tr(rho B),
  d< M^ell >_t=2 kappa d^2 ||x||^2 dt,
  c_d=(d^2-1)/(2d),     ||x||^2=(Tr(rho^2)-1/d)/2.

Both are bounded on the whole positive trace-one state space: purity is at
most one and |Tr(rho B)|<=||B||. Consequently a
trajectory starting at I/d cannot hit det rho=0 in finite time: on
successive cutoffs rho>=epsilon I, a boundary hit would force ell to
minus infinity, while ell is a bounded-drift continuous martingale with
uniformly bounded quadratic variation. The martingale maximal inequality
makes the probability of such a hit tend to zero as epsilon tends to zero.

Let U(rho)=Tr(H rho^tensor N), w_t=exp(int_0^t U(rho_s)ds). For fixed
finite d,N,H, |U|<=||H||. Ito applied to k_N and the exact differential
identity gives

  d/dt E[w_t rho_t^tensor N]
      = {H,E[w_t rho_t^tensor N]}/2.

No stopped-boundary residual remains after taking epsilon to zero, by the
no-hit result and bounded weights. Starting at I/d yields

  E[w_1 rho_1^tensor N]=exp(H)/d^N.

Reweighting the endpoint law by w_1 gives an exact normalized positive iid
mixture. This proves the global isotropic route independently of the
heat-character integral. In particular it needs no assumption that the
Gibbs mixture is concentrated near I/d.

3. EXACT FAILURE FOR A PROPER HIGHER-SPIN SUBALGEBRA

Take the spin-1 representation on C^3 with
S_x=(E12+E21+E23+E32)/sqrt(2),
S_y=-i(E12-E21+E23-E32)/sqrt(2), and S_z=diag(1,0,-1).
Put T_a=S_a/2, so Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2 and
sum_a T_a^2=I_3/2. These three generators form the proper spin-1 su(2)
subalgebra of su(3); the five quadrupole directions of su(3) are absent.

Choose the full-rank state and traceless tangent

  rho=diag(4/5,1/10,1/10),
  Y=(E12+E21)/5-9(E23+E32)/10.

For D_sub=sum_(a=x,y,z)(v_a tensor v_a-r_a tensor r_a), direct traces give
Tr(Y v_x)=Tr(Y v_y)=Tr(Y v_z)=Tr(Y r_x)=Tr(Y r_z)=0 and
Tr(Y r_y)=7/(50 sqrt(2)). Thus

  <Y,D_sub Y>=-49/5000 < 0.

Both rho and Y are exact rational/algebraic inputs, and the negative value
is checked by my exact SymPy diagnostic. Therefore positive Casimir
coefficients alone do not give a positive diffusion on the full qutrit
state simplex for a proper higher-spin su(2) subalgebra. The full su(3)
identity above passes an independent exact rational fixture in the same
diagnostic. This is a failure of the proposed unrestricted full-density
principal-symbol transfer; it is not a counterexample to the known
spin-s restricted thermal-orbit construction, whose coordinates omit the
quadrupole tangent directions.

4. CRITICAL-CENTER GATE, ANISOTROPY, AND COSTS

The exact global Casimir heat identity is not a concentration theorem. For
fundamental SU(d>=3), at susceptibility criticality the quadratic term in
the heat-mixture free energy cancels and the cubic term Tr(X^3)/(6d) in
log Tr(exp X) remains; its sign varies across directions. In the
Tr(T_aT_b)=delta_ab/2 convention used above the critical heat time is t=d/N;
this equals alpha=d/2 in c04_s01's Tr(T_aT_b)=delta_ab convention. The
attributed ordered-state variational witness makes the obstruction
quantitative: at that latter alpha, the local spectrum
((d-1)/d,1/[d(d-1)],...,1/[d(d-1)])
has positive free-energy gain

  Delta_d=(d-2)^2/(2(d-1))-(d-2)log(d-1)/d > 0.

Thus the qubit method's assumption of exponentially small mass outside a
small ball around I/d cannot be transferred. This does not contradict the
positive global heat identity or the exact isotropic SDE above. It only
blocks using central-seed concentration as a gate for the local stopped
diffusion.

For a full su(d) basis with C=alpha I+A N^(-gamma), the elementary bound
|<Y,D_A Y>|<=5||A|| ||Y||_2^2 in the Tr(T_aT_b)=delta_ab convention and
D_I>=epsilon^2 I on rho>=epsilon I give a compact positive margin by taking
epsilon proportional to N^(-gamma/2), for every fixed gamma>0. Logdet has
bounded drift and quadratic variation O(1/N) in the associated mean-field
scaling, so the chance of reaching this cutoff is exp[-cN(log N)^2].
This is the route c04_s01/c04_s02 developed for weak anisotropy; its
stopped-Duhamel norm, normalization and finite-bit details remain attributed
to those reports, not promoted here to an independent end-to-end proof.
For an O(1) arbitrary anisotropy, the global PSD result is false and the
weak-perturbation cutoff argument does not apply without a new reachable-
region margin or a different representation.

Interface/cost: inputs are known local generators, d,N, Casimir strength,
and any supplied one-body/aniso parameters. The exact heat formula is a
continuous law in d^2-1 real coordinates; the SDE has d^2-1 real Brownian
coordinates. Both specify a mathematical mixture and require N local-state
emissions. No finite-bit sampler, discretization error bound, local
preparation gate count, polynomial runtime, worst-case importance-weight
control, or hardware run is established here. For kappa fixed, the crude
global sandwich norm can scale as O(kappa N^2+||B||N); for kappa=beta/N it
scales as O(beta N+||B||N). These are mathematical representation results,
not an efficiency claim.
