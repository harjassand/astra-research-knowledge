N01 — Cosmic inference, version 1
2026-10-09

Status: internally derived identities and an explicit stable-causal counterexample; no observational claim, novelty claim, or external validation.

Target
======
Find a mechanism using H(a), linear matter growth, and lensing that cancels galaxy bias and primordial-amplitude/shape freedom, then determine whether it distinguishes modified gravity (MG) from general dark energy (DE), including when DE must arise from a local stable causal field theory. Preserve gauge, matter, scale, and tensor-sector assumptions.

1. Observable closure already in the literature
===============================================
Use the convention of Amendola et al. (2019):

  ds^2 = -(1+2 Psi) dt^2 + a^2(1+2 Phi) d x^2,
  eta = -Phi/Psi,

and primes denote d/dN, N=ln(a). In the linear scalar subhorizon/quasi-static description their observable amplitudes are

  A(k,N) = b(k,N) G(k,N) delta_m0(k),
  R(k,N) = f(k,N) G(k,N) delta_m0(k),
  L(k,N) = Omega_m0 Y(k,N)[1+eta(k,N)] G(k,N) delta_m0(k),
  E(N) = H(N)/H0.

Here A and R are the two coefficients from the linear Kaiser redshift-space galaxy power, and L is the lensing amplitude after geometry is divided out. Their ratios

  P2 = L/R = Omega_m0 Y(1+eta)/f,
  P3 = R_N/R

cancel b and the initial spectrum shape/amplitude. The pressureless matter growth and lensing equations are

  -khat^2 Psi = delta_m,NN + (2+E_N/E) delta_m,N,
  -khat^2(Psi-Phi) = 3(1+z)^3 L/(2 E^2),
  khat = k/(aH).

With R=delta_m,N (the slowly-varying-potential/quasi-static continuity reduction), division gives

  eta_obs = 3 P2 (1+z)^3 / [2 E^2 (P3+2+E_N/E)] - 1 = eta.

This is not a new discovery. Amendola, Bettoni, Pinho & Casas, arXiv:1902.06978v2, Sec. XI, equations (122)-(137), state the same cancellation and quote the assumptions: FLRW with small perturbations, universal gravity, standard-model matter behaving as in lab tests, and cold pressureless dark matter. Their text also says the result depends on derivatives of E and f sigma8 and is therefore observationally noisy. The estimator is a useful way to cancel bias, sigma8, Omega_m0 and growth initial conditions; it estimates the metric slip, not the ontology of the source of the metric.

Derivation and sign check: in their convention Phi=-eta Psi, so (Psi-Phi)/Psi=1+eta. The ratio of the two displayed Laplacians is therefore 1+eta. Substituting L/R=P2 and R_N/R=P3 gives the expression above. It is undefined when the denominator (equivalently Psi or the relevant velocity response) vanishes. It is not independent of the assumptions that make R observable as f G delta_m0.

2. Exact Euler representation candidate (not claimed novel)
==========================================================
The QSA growth equation is not needed to reconstruct the dynamical potential if the velocity divergence itself can be observed. In standard Newtonian gauge, with metric

  ds^2 = a^2[-(1+2 Psi)d tau^2 + (1-2 Phi)d x^2],

let theta_m = i k_i v_m^i be the conformal-time velocity divergence of a minimally coupled pressureless matter component, and define U=-theta_m/(aH). The exact linear Euler equation is

  theta_m,tau + Hcal theta_m = k^2 Psi,    Hcal=aH.

Since d/dtau = aH d/dN and theta_m=-aH U,

  -khat^2 Psi = U_N + (2+E_N/E) U.                 (E1)

No growth/Poisson law, Omega_m0, primordial amplitude, or QSA appears in (E1). The exact linear dust continuity equation in this convention is delta_m,N = U+3 Phi_N, which explains why identifying U with the density-growth derivative requires dropping the metric-derivative term. The Bardeen potentials are gauge invariant; U must be the corresponding Newtonian-gauge (gauge-invariant) velocity variable, not an arbitrary coordinate velocity.

Let W(k,N)=-khat^2(Psi-Phi) be the local Weyl-potential Laplacian reconstructed from lensing, with the source convention above. Then, whenever the denominator is nonzero,

  eta_EL(k,N) = W/[U_N+(2+E_N/E)U] - 1 = eta.   (E2)

This follows only from Euler plus the lensing potential; the unknown initial amplitude cancels if W and U refer to the same linear mode/transfer seed. E2 removes the pressureless continuity/QSA step in the known eta_obs estimator. It does not solve the full observational inverse problem: lensing gives a line-of-sight projection, RSD on horizon scales includes relativistic number-count, selection and magnification terms, direct peculiar-velocity reconstruction is difficult, time derivatives are noisy, and multiple independent primordial modes require estimating a transfer/covariance matrix rather than dividing one deterministic transfer function. The known review notes that direct velocity plus its time derivative gives no new linear amplitude beyond R,E,L; equation (E2) is a re-expression in terms of the two potentials and a directly observed velocity response, not a priority-checked novelty claim.

3. Exact scalar-metric dark degeneracy
======================================
At the level of a given metric and minimally coupled matter, observations of distances, lensing, redshifts, and geodesic motion see the metric and matter trajectories. For any solution of a metric theory with conserved ordinary matter, define in Einstein notation

  T_eff,mu nu = M_Pl^2 G_mu nu - T_m,mu nu.

The Bianchi identity and matter conservation imply nabla_mu T_eff^{mu nu}=0 on that solution. Einstein gravity plus this effective source has the same metric and matter trajectories by construction. This identity alone does not prove that T_eff comes from a healthy local microscopic field; it only identifies the inference obstruction. A physically realizable local counterexample is needed before using the broad DE/MG ambiguity as the end of the analysis.

For the limited source-free perfect-fluid class in GR, linear scalar perturbations have no anisotropic stress and hence eta=1, up to known standard relativistic species and conventions. Thus a robust eta != 1 can reject GR plus a perfect-fluid DE component under those assumptions. It cannot reject general DE. Kunz & Sapone, arXiv:astro-ph/0612452, Sec. IV and equations (12)-(13), explicitly choose dark-energy perturbations and anisotropic stress to reproduce the DGP metric perturbations; they conclude all cosmological observables in that setup coincide. This is an exact prior counterexample to treating a scalar-potential slip measurement as an ontology label.

4. Local, minimally coupled, stable, causal counterexample: elastic-solid DE
============================================================================
A stronger counterexample is available than an unrestricted T_eff relabelling. Consider Einstein gravity plus an isotropic solid made of three scalar material-coordinate fields. At leading derivative order its local, minimally coupled action is

  S = integral d^4x sqrt(-g) [M_Pl^2 R/2 + F(X,Y,Z)],
  B^{IJ}=g^{mu nu} partial_mu phi^I partial_nu phi^J,
  X=[B], Y=[B^2]/[B]^2, Z=[B^3]/[B]^3,
  <phi^I>=x^I.

Endlich, Nicolis & Wang, arXiv:1210.0569, Sec. 2, equations (2.3)-(2.17), derive this local solid EFT, its phonon quadratic action and longitudinal/transverse speeds. Their Sec. 3, equations (3.1)-(3.5), gives its metric stress tensor and FRW background. Battye & Moss, arXiv:astro-ph/0703744, equations (34)-(35),(45),(57d), give the same elastic-medium stress, displacement dynamics, and stable-wave conditions; their Sec. IV explicitly treats elastic media as a DE component and notes that its nonzero rigidity sources anisotropic stress.

An explicit smooth-on-the-background choice is

  F(X,Y,Z) = -A X^(1/2) + C X^(1/2)(Y-1/3),
  A>0, C=3A/16.

On the isotropic background Y=1/3 and Z=1/9, X=3/a^2. Thus the shear term and its first background variation vanish. From rho=-F and p=F-(2/a^2)F_X, with F_X=-A/(2 sqrt(X)),

  rho=A sqrt(X) proportional to a^(-1),
  p/rho = -1 + 2(1/2)/3 = -2/3.

The background accelerates when this component dominates. The phonon speed formula from Endlich et al. (2.16) is

  c_T^2 = 1 + (2/3)(F_Y+F_Z)/(F_X X),
  c_L^2 = 1 + (2/3)F_XX X^2/(F_X X) + (8/9)(F_Y+F_Z)/(F_X X).

Here F_XX X^2/(F_X X)=n-1=-1/2 and (F_Y+F_Z)/(F_X X)=C/(-A/2)=-3/8, so

  c_T^2=3/4,   c_L^2=1/3.

The phonon kinetic coefficient is -F_X X/3=A sqrt(X)/6>0. Both scalar and transverse gradient speeds are positive and subluminal. In the equivalent isotropic elastic-fluid variables, w=-2/3 and c_T^2=mu/(rho+p)=3/4 give c_L^2=w+4c_T^2/3=1/3, consistent with Battye–Moss (45). These inequalities leave a strict margin from zero and one. This is a local first-derivative matter action coupled minimally to Einstein gravity, not a formal conserved-tensor rewrite.

Because F_Y+F_Z is nonzero, the linear stress contains a traceless spatial part proportional to the solid shear strain (the displayed stress formula in Endlich et al. (3.5), or Battye–Moss (34c)). The scalar traceless Einstein equation therefore gives a nonzero Bardeen-potential difference for generic scalar displacement. In the notation used above, eta generally differs from one. The model is consequently a physically local, linearly stable and causal GR+DE counterexample to the claim that eta != 1 alone establishes MG. This construction establishes nonzero slip, not exact equality to every MG transfer function; an action-level full-response match to a selected MG target remains open.

A useful restricted inequality does follow for this constant-w elastic-solid subclass:

  c_L^2 = w + (4/3)c_T^2,
  0 <= c_T^2 <= 1, 0 <= c_L^2 <= 1.

For w<0 this requires c_T^2 >= -3w/4 and c_T^2 <= min(1,3(1-w)/4). It restricts the solid but does not force eta=1. Since c_L^2 can approach zero from above at the lower bound, causal stability alone does not furnish a universal lower sound-horizon cutoff. A high-k slip plateau is therefore not, by itself, a model-independent rejection of all stable solids without an additional strong-coupling/UV or lower-sound-speed condition.

The DE-solid action has no curvature-dependent tensor kinetic term, so the tensor principal cone and Planck mass are the GR ones. Its anisotropic stress can still source tensor perturbations at order H^2; it is safer to say that high-frequency standard-siren propagation approaches the GR luminosity-distance law up to O(H^2/omega^2), not that the equality is exact at every frequency. The multi-messenger result GW170817/GRB170817A (LIGO/Virgo et al., arXiv:1710.05834, abstract) constrains the speed difference to roughly -3e-15 < (c_GW-c)/c < 7e-16 under its emission-delay assumptions. That constraint does not remove a scalar-slip response from minimally coupled elastic matter. Conversely, standard-siren amplitude differences probe tensor friction/running Planck mass in specified MG classes (e.g. Lagos et al., arXiv:1901.03321; Belgacem et al., arXiv:1906.01593); a null tensor-sector result does not exclude all MG with GR-like tensor propagation.

5. Identifiability result and what is not achieved
=================================================
The observable closure measures the scalar metric slip (or, with E2, the ratio of lensing and dynamical potentials) without bias, primordial amplitude, or Omega_m0. A nonzero value decisively rejects only a specified zero-slip GR matter class. The explicit F(X,Y,Z) example proves that imposing locality, minimal coupling, linear stability and subluminal sound speeds still does not make that inference broad enough to separate all DE from all MG. Adding the tensor propagation speed bound does not fix this, because the counterexample preserves the GR tensor principal cone; amplitude/phase tests can add information about tensor-sector couplings but do not label every scalar-sector theory.

No decisive universal positivity inequality linking an arbitrary reconstructed eta(k,a) to an impossible stable local DE response has been established here. The solid counterexample only realizes a family, not an arbitrary response kernel. A stronger classification requires a declared DE field class and its admissible constitutive/action functions. Plausible next step: take one concrete stable MG target with a fully specified H(a), mu(k,a), eta(k,a), and tensor propagation law; solve the inverse problem for an F(X,Y,Z) solid (or a minimal extension) across the full observed (k,a) domain; then test its kinetic matrix, characteristic speeds, strong-coupling cutoff, tensor response, and initial modes. If that inverse problem fails for a target despite allowing the full stable causal solid EFT, the failure could yield a restricted, experimentally useful inequality. Until then, no model-independent classification is justified.
