EXACT RESULT

For every n>=1, consider M_(2^n) with normalized trace and the finite likelihood family

  H_(i,X)=I tensor ... tensor X_i tensor ... tensor I,
  H_(i,Z)=I tensor ... tensor Z_i tensor ... tensor I,
  i=1,...,n,

with the uniform prior on the 2n labels. Its collective variance value is exactly

  q_p = 1/2,

while its normalized prior-pair average squared commutator is

  sum_(a,b) p_a p_b ||[H_a,H_b]||_(2,tau)^2 = 2/n.

Thus the prior-pair average by itself cannot be lifted to any dimension-free estimate q_p <= f(C_p) with f(t)->0 as t->0. Any use of this statistic alone as the intermediate control in the all-frame lift is insufficient. The peer bound (3) remains valid; this example does not refute q_p<=c E, does not have E->0, and does not rule out a proof using both the energy and stronger structure.

This is a stress test of that proof route, not a counterexample to broadcasting rounding. Indeed, the tensor product of n qubit universal 1-to-2 cloners is a valid self-compatible channel with Phi(H_(i,X/Z))=(2/3)H_(i,X/Z). Its mean Dirichlet energy for this frame is 1/3, so q_p/(mean energy)=3/2; the candidate factor 2 remains satisfied. The small commutator average arises from bounded-degree sparsity of the noncommutation graph, not from small channel energy.

PROOF OF q_p=1/2

For a pure n-qubit state psi, write x_i=<psi|H_(i,X)|psi> and z_i=<psi|H_(i,Z)|psi>. The one-qubit reduced state at site i has a Bloch vector of norm at most 1, so x_i^2+z_i^2<=1. Therefore the prior-averaged variance at psi obeys

  f_p(psi) = 1 - (1/(2n)) sum_i (x_i^2+z_i^2) >= 1/2.

The dual coherent-state witness Y=I/2 is feasible in the LP from INITIAL.txt and has tau(Y)=1/2, proving q_p>=1/2 for every canonical rank-one POVM frame.

For equality, independently at each site choose one of the four states |+X>,|-X>,|+Z>,|-Z> uniformly, and take their tensor products with weight 4^(-n). This is a finite frame measure because the one-site average projector is I/2 and hence the n-site average is I/2^n. It has 4^n product-state outcomes (an exact existence witness, not an efficient-size compiler). For each target H_(i,X) or H_(i,Z), the squared expectation is 1 with probability 1/2 and 0 otherwise. Its average capture is 1/2, so the frame has prior-averaged variance exactly 1/2. This is a primal witness matching Y.

COMMUTATOR COUNT

Distinct sites commute; same-label pairs commute; for each i the two ordered pairs (X_i,Z_i),(Z_i,X_i) have squared normalized HS commutator norm 4 because [X,Z]=2iY. There are exactly 2n nonzero ordered pairs among (2n)^2 pairs, each weighted 1/(2n)^2. Hence C_p=(2n*4)/(4n^2)=2/n.

WHY THE SEPARABLE CHOI RESTRICTION IS LOAD-BEARING

Already at n=1 with H_X=X, H_Z=Z and equal prior, the symmetric maximally entangled state |Phi+>=(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2) has both marginals I/2 and satisfies (X tensor I)|Phi+>=(I tensor X)|Phi+> and likewise for Z. Thus Tr(|Phi+><Phi+| K_p)=0, although q_p=1/2. Replacing conv{P tensor P : average P=I/2} by all symmetric bipartite states with the same marginals collapses this example's primal value to zero. The relaxation loses the crucial identical-coherent-product/separability condition; compatibility or symmetric Choi extendibility alone cannot be substituted into the variance primal.
