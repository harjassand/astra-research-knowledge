Independent coordinator audit of Sol6 intrinsic decoder, 2026-10-09

Target record: work/sol/local_chart_decoder/v1.txt, frozen source-dependent
single-shot decoder for the GGJ family. This audit does not rebuild the
GGJ qLTC construction or assert novelty or external verification.

I read the complete Sol proof and reconstructed the following interfaces.
The source passages used were GGJ Lemmas6.1.3,6.2.1 and their proofs,
plus the actual-array definitions/identities recorded in Section7. T22
independently rendered and checked source Theorem7.2.1, Lemmas7.3.1--2,
and Theorem13.2.1, including the dual-tuple expansion and lambda L<1/2.

1. The noncocycle term is legitimate. For a locally cominimal d-cochain
x and an ell-face v, every local correction extends by zero to a global
correction supported above any vertex of v. Thus the local filling
lower bound applies to x itself. A nonzero local coboundary coordinate
at u either has global syndrome s(u) nonzero or receives a nonzero
external facet. The source uniqueness lemma still injects selected
external facets into witnesses at fixed v. This yields
 r_ell H_ell <= O_ell + (d-ell+1)H_(ell-1) + S_ell.
The substitution coefficient is (d-ell)!/product_(h=ell)^d r_h.
For each nonzero s(u), there are at most
 binom(d+1,ell)2^(d+1-ell) * binom(t-ell,d-ell)n_+^(d-ell)
pairs (v,f) with v<=u,f. This is the stated crude M_d after division
by the product of r_h. No hypothesis delta x=0 was silently restored.

2. Terminal syndrome local minimality suffices operationally. The greedy
algorithm reduces the observed syndrome's integer weight at every flip.
At termination b is locally cominimal and delta b=delta m. Its small
initial support bounds its final support, so the noncocycle inequality
gives |b|<=A D|m|. The flip count also bounds the raw residual by
(1+ell D)|e|+ell|m|. Source cocycle soundness gives a small u sharing
the residual's syndrome; the separate raw-weight promise makes residual
plus u smaller than cohomological distance. Hence it is a stabilizer
boundary. This last step is necessary: soundness alone only gives
distance to the entire kernel. Sol explicitly supplies it.

3. The vertical splittings do not need naturality. At each actual anchor
f, choose S right-inverse on ker T and L=S P for a projection P. The map
is defined on all inputs, sends zero to zero, and cannot create a new
anchor. Equivalently any linear extension L of S has TL a projection:
T^2=0 puts its image in ker T, and TL is identity there. On ideal inputs
vertical exactness gives the required equalities. On noisy inputs one
compares with the ideal sequence by linearity, gaining factors D for
horizontal copying. No noisy input is falsely assumed to lie in ker T.

4. I checked every index in the homology chase. At the top set
a_L=x_L+Delta q with q in K. In the next diagonal,
b_(L-1)=z_(L-1)+H a_L+q is horizontally closed and satisfies
T b_(L-1)=a_(L-1)+x_(L-1).
For each lower r>0, choose v_(r-1) with Delta v_(r-1)=b_r and set
b_(r-1)=z_(r-1)+H a_r+T v_(r-1).
Then Delta b_(r-1)=x_r+a_r+T b_r=0, and
T b_(r-1)=a_(r-1)+x_(r-1), using T^2=0.
All identities occur in actual stalk arrays. At degree zero, horizontal
closedness means the opinions on each upper face agree; J reads that
common vector. The final identity is exactly B_(k+1)h=f_ideal+e.
This is stronger than a syndrome equality and removes the chart-image
preservation gate in initial T14 v1.

5. Arbitrary noisy descent is covered. The operational y_r need not be
closed. The proof instead compares with closed ideal a_r using the same
linear H on all inputs. Its difference recurrence is
 E_r <= c_m D^r M + H E_(r+1),
 E_L <= c_m D^L(1+beta_K D)M.
Unwinding gives exactly G c_m[sum_(r=0)^(L-1)(HD)^r +
(HD)^L(1+beta_K D)]M. Therefore a missing H Delta error is not discarded;
all noisy arrays are handled by the direct difference calculation.

6. Complexity and conversion. At fixed local parameters, every local
table and flip candidate set has constant size, possibly enormous.
A flip changes boundedly many syndrome blocks. Only vertices incident
to those blocks can change flippability, so a queue gives O(N) updates
for at most |y|=O(N) strict decreases. The finite-field dual-to-binary
conversion must use the trace-dual basis; the Sol record states this.
The bit support factor is bounded by the fixed maximum block dimension.
These observations justify asymptotic sequential O(N) decoding on the
supplied code incidence model, not constant parallel depth or practical
resource bounds.

Audit conclusion. I found no gap in the displayed decoder theorem on
the exact source hypotheses. T22 separately reports source coefficient
and dual-expansion checks. This establishes an internally reconstructed
conditional algorithm theorem, with explicit positive data/noise
promises. Source qLTC validity, historical priority, independent external
referee verification, and any practical or constant-depth capability
remain separate issues. The raw good-qLDPC single-shot target is already
known; the potential addition is applicability to the specified good
qLTC family and the actual-stalk reduction with noisy-input guarantees.
