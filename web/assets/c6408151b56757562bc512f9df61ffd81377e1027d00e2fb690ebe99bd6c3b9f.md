# Cell-state control, regeneration, and aging

## Main finding

No biological cell-state controller or aging intervention is validated by this research run. The strongest defensible output is a model-level decision boundary: population mean trajectories can fail to determine founder-clone safety when birth and death are hidden behind the same net growth, even when the trajectory is known continuously and under every candidate intervention.

In an exact branching witness, two models give identical expected state-count curves for both candidate treatments but reverse which treatment maximizes founder-level target success without harmful fate. A terminal, founder-resolved clone census can distinguish them in the stated model; a finite-sample rule then selects an action only when its target lower bound and harm upper bound clear prespecified thresholds. The mathematics is standard branching-process theory and finite-sample concentration, so no novelty or biological-validity claim is made. The exact Cycle 2 statement, proof, counts, and code are in [CYCLE2_REPORT.md](CYCLE2_REPORT.md), [CLAIMS_CYCLE2.json](CLAIMS_CYCLE2.json), and [branching_safety_controller.py](branching_safety_controller.py).

This branching result is separate from the parent’s two-state CTMC challenge. A constant two-state CTMC with an accurately measured nonconstant full state trajectory can identify its switching rates and growth difference. Endpoint-only observations, arbitrary time-varying rates, and branching birth/death hazards are different interfaces; the result here does not claim otherwise.

## Lead result: mean curves do not determine clone-level action ranking

The model has a single starting state S. A cell divides S→2S at rate b, dies at rate d, switches to a persistent target mark R at rate a, or switches to a persistent harm mark U at rate q. R and U are nonbranching and persistent through terminal time T. Under action u=(a,q), the expected counts satisfy

\[
\dot m_S=(b-d-a-q)m_S,\qquad \dot m_R=a m_S,\qquad \dot m_U=q m_S.
\]

Thus exact expected-count curves depend on b,d only through b−d. At T=1, two worlds with (b,d)=(1,0.5) and (10.5,10) have identical expected state-count trajectories for each of two actions: A=(a,q)=(0.1,0), B=(0.2,0.3). Their founder outcome is J=P(R_T>0,U_T=0). The exact branching PGF gives:

| Action | Expected (S,R,U) at T in either world | J in (1,0.5) world | J in (10.5,10) world |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | (1.491825, 0.122956, 0) | 0.118170 | 0.087765 |
| B | (1, 0.2, 0.3) | 0.157318 | 0.054211 |

B is better in the low-turnover world; A is better in the high-turnover world. A policy receiving only the common expected-count curves has minimax regret 0.018068 even with optimal randomization. This does not defeat analyses that use replicate count distributions, clone pedigrees, covariance, or direct lineage outcomes.

A single complete founder-resolved terminal clone census supplies a sufficient moment set in this restricted model: the unconditional means of S,R,U plus Var(S) identify a,q and b,d, including the zero-net-growth limit. At T=1/action B, Var(S)=2 in the low-turnover world and 21 in the high-turnover world. This is a population-moment identifiability result, not a finite-sample estimator guarantee or a universal assay-minimality theorem.

For m randomized actions and n independent founder clones per action, the direct decision algorithm records Y=1{R_T>0,U_T=0} and Z=1{U_T>0}. The simultaneous Hoeffding radius is h=√(log(4m/α)/(2n)). It certifies an action only when the upper harm bound is below δ, then maximizes the target lower bound; if no action clears the risk gate, it returns `UNKNOWN / no certified action`. For two actions, 95% confidence and half-width 0.05 require 1,016 independent founders per arm. Distinguishing the explicit ranking reversal at the smaller gap requires 2,661 founders per arm for the displayed sign-test bound. Counts are founders, not descendants; full clone recovery, reporter calibration, independent donor/animal replication, and imaging/sequencing costs are additional.

The independent audit by the lineage-graph lane verified the PGF, four action values, moment inversion, minimax calculation, and sample bounds. A closed-form/RK4 comparison across all four action/world outcomes has maximum absolute error below 6.2×10⁻¹⁵. These checks establish arithmetic/model consistency only; the independent audit details are preserved in [INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md](INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md).

## First-cycle workstreams

- **Pulse controllability and partial reprogramming.** The three-state CTMC S→R at rate a, S→U at c, R→U at b has an exact fixed-dose target-state peak at T*=log((a+c)/b)/(a+c−b), with limit 1/b when a+c=b. A risk cap truncates that interval. Common rate rescaling preserves the transition graph but rescales optimal time, so topology alone cannot specify a pulse duration. The formula is standard and biological rates remain unmeasured. See [cell_controllability/FINAL_REPORT.md](cell_controllability/FINAL_REPORT.md).
- **Endpoint sensing.** Two two-state constant-rate CTMCs can have the same terminal state probability and different path occupancy, making endpoint-only control decisions ambiguous for a path-dependent objective. A finite-grid intermediate snapshot resolves the supplied two-model example; this is not a general active-sensing novelty claim. The cycle-2 follow-up also finds the classical Markov-embedding issue for unrestricted K≥3 one-lag generators. See [cell_observation_design/FINAL_REPORT.md](cell_observation_design/FINAL_REPORT.md) and its [Cycle 2 report](cell_observation_design/CYCLE2_REPORT.md).
- **Lineage offspring dependence.** Equal daughter marginals and expected progenitor counts do not determine clone survival or unsafe-family probability when sisters are dependent. Exact Galton–Watson bounds show the size of the missing joint-offspring information; CLiNC and other prior work already model joint daughter distributions. See [cell_lineage_graph/FINAL_REPORT.md](cell_lineage_graph/FINAL_REPORT.md) and [cell_lineage_graph/CYCLE2.md](cell_lineage_graph/CYCLE2.md).
- **Metastable transitions.** Action derivatives and barrier approximations yield conditional dose-timing formulas in an acquired diffusion model. A separate three-state example shows that endpoint target fraction need not equal founder reach probability when state growth differs; its finite-sample selector is synthetic and makes no biological recommendation. See [cell_metastability/FINAL_REPORT.md](cell_metastability/FINAL_REPORT.md) and [cell_metastability/CYCLE2_REPORT.md](cell_metastability/CYCLE2_REPORT.md).
- **Aging and epigenetic memory.** A two-mode washout model is observable only under non-collinear assay loadings and distinct recovery rates. Finite follow-up cannot prove exact irreversibility without a lower bound on slow recovery. The September 2026 preprint’s corrected donor-level analysis does not establish DamAge equivalence or resistance; small donor counts and wide intervals leave substantial uncertainty. See [cell_aging_memory/FINAL_REPORT.md](cell_aging_memory/FINAL_REPORT.md), [model audit](cell_aging_memory/CYCLE2_MODEL_AUDIT.md), and [identifiability proof](cell_aging_memory/CYCLE2_IDENTIFIABILITY.md).
- **Causal transport across cell models.** A target perturbation effect is not identified from source randomization and target observational similarity alone. Transport needs explicit conditional response invariance, overlap, and a consistent intervention version; the positive standardization formula is established prior art. See [cell_causal_transport/FINAL_REPORT.md](cell_causal_transport/FINAL_REPORT.md) and [Cycle 2 report](cell_causal_transport/cycle2/CYCLE2_REPORT.md).

## Strongest primary-literature baselines

The key mechanistic baseline is Gunnarsson, Foo & Leder, “Statistical inference of the rates of cell proliferation and phenotypic switching in cancer,” *Journal of Theoretical Biology* 568 (2023), [DOI 10.1016/j.jtbi.2023.111497](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2023.111497), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.08096). Their multitype branching mean matrix depends on birth and death through net rates, while replicated count/covariance data can identify additional branching parameters. This rules out presenting mean/net-rate confounding or covariance-based turnover identification as new.

For lineage trajectories, Ventre et al., “Trajectory inference for a branching SDE model of cell differentiation via lineage tracing,” *Journal of Mathematical Biology* 92 (2026), [DOI 10.1007/s00285-026-02375-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-026-02375-5), prove generation-number reweighting under no death and complete observed leaves, and explicitly analyze death/subsampling bias. Hormoz et al.’s KCA uses lineage trees and endpoint single-cell measurements ([Cell Systems 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2016.10.015)); Transcompp fits time-series compositions and optional relative proliferation ([Bioinformatics 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa021)). These are stronger comparators when those richer data are available.

The aging lane independently checked the current September 2026 Tanigawa–Iwaki v2 preprint against the supplied v1 material and recorded that the current code archive could not be downloaded due to a 403/timeout. Its statistical outputs are preprint-reported and not independently re-run here. Other biological claims throughout remain source-derived unless explicitly marked as model calculations.

## Evidence status and next gate

The pulse, endpoint-alias, offspring-law, aging-observability, causal-transport, and metastability formulas are conditional model statements. Numerical tables are finite synthetic calculations. Current primary papers establish substantial prior art in branching-process inference, lineage reconstruction, time-series transition estimation, partial-reprogramming endpoints, and perturbation transport. There is no reanalyzed real dataset, wet-lab validation, external proof verification beyond the internal independent audit described above, or clinical claim.

The decisive next gate for the lead is a specified biological context where each founder can be randomized, its complete descendants recovered, and persistent target/harm labels measured at a fixed endpoint. Compare direct founder-outcome bounds against the full Gunnarsson likelihood and applicable Ventre/KCA lineage methods with equal independent founders, reporter error, censoring, imaging/sequencing cost, and biological replicate structure. If clone recovery or event permanence cannot be established, the controller must return unknown rather than use bulk expected fractions as a substitute.
