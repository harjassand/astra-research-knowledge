# Boundaries, failed routes, and restart gate

## Preserved negative results and limits

1. **Arbitrary positive rational channels remain hard.** The separate C8
   report gives an internally proved candidate coNP-completeness result for
   unrestricted sparse positive `P_e/Q_e` channel data. Its corrected z>0
   neutral-clause proof and exact SAT guard scores are recorded in
   `work/agents/c8_implicit_rational_geometry/CORRECTION_RECORD.md`. The lower
   bound is not imported into canonical Michaelis–Menten syntax without a
   restricted reduction.
2. **One common denominator is not enough for permanence or endotacticity.**
   Schnell–Mendoza alternative-substrate kinetics has one nonsingular shared
   enzyme-occupancy denominator but only forward substrate-to-product
   channels. The exact compiler constructs a counterdirection, and the
   reduced model has explicit exponential substrate depletion. The
   reversible-pair condition is load-bearing.
3. **Weakly reversible is not automatically strongly endotactic.** The
   two-module positive-constant fixture `weak_not_strong_distinct_linkage_spans.json`
   passes weak endotacticity but fails standard strong endotacticity at
   `w=(1,0,-1)`. A neutral global-top source blocks the strong condition.
   Therefore the generic multi-rank strong answer is `UNKNOWN`; the code does
   not use only the essential-source set for the strong maximum.
4. **The strong gate is sufficient only.** Rank-one lifted stoichiometry is
   exactly recognized. Higher-rank modules might also be strongly endotactic
   through a single merged linkage class or other geometry; this compiler
   does not decide those cases.
5. **Permanence is status-separated.** General-rank permanence from weak
   endotacticity is conditional on the N33 source theorem and its classwise
   assumptions. Strong-gated permanence is a transfer from published GMS
   strong-endotactic permanence. No independent formal/external validation
   of the new transfer package was done.
6. **QSSA is a scientific approximation.** The Schnell–Mendoza compiler
   recognizes the stated reduced equations exactly. It does not bound the
   difference from the full enzyme-complex ODE; the paper's QSSA condition
   must be checked from the relevant enzyme, substrate, and Michaelis scales.
   The synthetic fixture's initial Eq. 38 ratios are 1/3, 1/6, and 2/9
   (maximum 1/3), so it gives no evidence that the required
   much-less-than-one regime holds. No paper parameter fit or biological
   dataset was acquired.
7. **Representation and costs are charged.** The reversible compiler reads
   all endpoint and denominator-factor terms. It avoids the cross-module
   product expansion but does not compress an input denominator that is
   supplied as an already exponentially expanded list. It has no data
   acquisition, coefficient-estimation, or solver cost hidden behind the
   polynomial structural check.
8. **Novelty remains unknown.** Prior art includes explicit finite-diagram
   MILP recognition and published strong-endotactic permanence. The targeted
   source pass does not establish historical priority for implicit
   reversible rational modules.

## Cycle 10 implementation repairs

The hostile audit exposed two implementation-contract defects; both now have
exact regressions. The coefficient box uses a simple directed-graph
convention that sums colliding same-direction reactions and sets
K equal to the sum of modulewise upper bounds M_r; the two-identical-unit-
module fixture checks the merged coefficient 2. The standalone compiler
disables Python's default decimal digit-conversion cap for exact rational
input/output; it exactly processes a 5,001-digit rate and a generated
15,000-factor fixture with a 2^15000 tuple count. No floating-point
conversion or truncation is used.

The parser still requires a nonempty module list and distinct endpoints.
Self-loops are INVALID_INPUT, so rank zero is outside the schema and the
strong gate is exactly rank one versus higher-rank UNKNOWN. The validation record retains the exact regressions, including rejection of
JSON float inputs, and the reduced-QSSA ratio caveat. See work/agents/cycle9_canonical_kinetics/CYCLE10_REPAIR_RECORD.md
for the correction record; the original hostile audit remains at
work/agents/c10_reversible_module_hostile/REPORT.md.

## Restart gate

Do not broaden the classifier to arbitrary per-channel denominators by
assuming they are shared or cancel. To continue, first acquire a real
reversible modular SBML model with its declared kinetic law and parameter
positivity domain; parse the endpoint orders and binding-denominator factors;
then test whether forward/reverse numerator orders really match the
stoichiometric endpoints and share one denominator per module. If that
matches, replay the compiler against an independently generated rate-law
expansion on a small module and check the emitted coefficient interval.

The next theoretical gate is to recognize standard strong endotacticity for
some genuinely higher-rank implicit lifts without materializing
`2 sum_r product_{s != r} T_s` edges. A candidate method must keep neutral
sources in the global maximum and must first pass the explicit weak-not-strong
fixture. Otherwise keep returning `UNKNOWN` on the strong question.

For general-rank permanence, obtain independent review of the N33
affine-activity and weighted-essential-drift proof before upgrading the
conditional gate. For the QSSA application, acquire a source-supported
operating window and verify the source inequality
`max_i E0/(Ki*(1+sum_j S'_j)) << 1` on a measured/declared operating window
before relating reduced-model decay to enzyme experiments. The current
synthetic fixture's maximum ratio is 1/3 and does not pass as regime evidence.
