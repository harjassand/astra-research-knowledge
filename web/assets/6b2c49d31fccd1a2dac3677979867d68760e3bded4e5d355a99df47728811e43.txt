VERDICT AND THEOREM SCOPE

The B_l, C_l, D_l polynomial-frame construction and its all-Q operational
application survive independent, disjoint audits. Exact metric factors,
odd-power gradients, the D_l Pfaffian orientation and zero-coordinate
case, centered classical Gram bounds, PBW central-Gram constants, deep
adjoint multiplicity, and the finite-N reset patch all check. The theorem
is scoped to a fixed classical compact simple type, a supplied compact
regular highest-weight set K, a fixed nonzero supplied H, a fixed positive
interval [h0,h1], and same-carrier full-state half-trace recovery with
unlimited classical labels and maximum quantum branch dimension Q, with
no entanglement assistance or uncharged quantum side information.

With the packet's metric, the checked conclusion is

  e*_Q = Theta(Lambda(Q)/N^2), 1<=Q<=d,

uniformly in N and lambda in K, where Lambda minimizes the full complex
matrix Casimir commutator energy over rank<=Q. This is a comparison to
Lambda, not an all-Q evaluation of Lambda. At Q=d both sides are zero.
No profile for B_l,C_l,D_l with rank l>=2 is proved here.

PROOF AUDIT

1. Metric and classical gradients. The defining Cartan generators have
norm one for <X,Y>=Tr_fund(XY)/2, so the stated coordinates z_i are
orthonormal. This packet uses Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=2 delta_AB, unlike the
SU(m) packet. Keeping that change together with the Casimir, coordinates,
and energy removes the apparent factor mismatch. For B/C, odd powers
X^(2j-1) remain in the compact Hermitian Lie algebra and are exactly the
metric gradients of Tr(X^(2j))/(4j); the B/C evaluation determinant is

  det M=(product_i z_i) product_(i<k)(z_k^2-z_i^2).

The B/C regular chamber excludes precisely the zero factors. For D_l,
odd powers alone fail at a regular zero coordinate. The gradient of
Pf(-iX) is SO(2l), hence Spin(2l), equivariant; Pf changes sign under
determinant-minus-one O(2l), which is outside the acting group. In the
specified block orientation Pf(-iX)=product_i z_i and its gradient has
Cartan column w_i=product_(k!=i)z_k. The complete D determinant is

  det M_D=(-1)^(l-1) product_(i<k)(z_k^2-z_i^2).

The identity first follows off the coordinate hyperplanes and extends
polynomially through z_l=0. Such a point is regular in D_l when the
squares remain distinct. Repeated polynomial degrees do not cause a
collision because the determinant stays nonzero. Compactness of K away
from root hyperplanes gives the displayed positive determinant and trace
bounds, hence lambda_min(H)>=det(H)/(Tr H)^(l-1). The epsilon powers and
rank-one exponent-zero case check.

2. Quantization and Gram. With PBW symmetrization on T/N, each polynomial
family is an exact Hermitian adjoint tensor. For a degree-e word, ordered
telescoping against the highest-vector means leaves one centered factor
on the right acting on psi; the other e-1 factors are bounded by B.
Consequently the supplied coefficient norm gives exactly

  d_s=L_s e_s B^(e_s-1) sqrt(beta_0),
  C_s=L_s^2 e_s^2 B^(2e_s-2),

for the highest-vector error d_s/sqrt(N) and the arbitrary-matrix
commutator-energy bound C_s E_T(F)/N^2. The central contraction is scalar
by Schur; its normalized trace gives q G_(N,st) exactly, where q=dim g
and G is normalized by carrier dimension. Expanding the highest-vector
inner products gives

  |qG_(N,st)-H_st| <= (u_s d_t+u_t d_s)/sqrt(N)+d_s d_t/N.

Thus N_Gram yields the stated positive quantum Gram and trace upper bound.
No SU partial-trace/Parseval factor is used or needed for these direct PBW
families. The constants are finite under the supplied fixed metric,
coefficient lists, degrees, and K, but the source does not instantiate
their numerical values for a concrete K.

3. Complete adjoint block and cutoff. For every adjoint weight mu,
|mu(alpha_i^vee)|<=2 in the stated B/C/D coroot coordinates. The explicit
N_dom therefore makes N lambda+mu dominant for each lambda in K; the
shift by rho is strictly dominant. In the Weyl-character product with
the adjoint, only its zero weight contributes the original carrier, with
multiplicity rank(g)=l. Positive quantum Gram makes the l polynomial
families independent, so they fill all l adjoint copies for
N>=max(N_Gram,N_dom). Both D spinor chiralities are covered.

For smaller N, bounded K and finite integer highest weights leave finitely
many carriers. Under this metric the full simply-connected weight
lattices have shortest squared length sigma_G=min(1,l/4) for B/D and 1
for C. Since C2(nu)=||nu||^2+2<nu,rho> for dominant nonzero nu,
C2(nu)>=sigma_G. The commutator energy therefore has gap sigma_G off
the scalar kernel. The rank trace inequality gives Lambda(Q)>=sigma_G/d
for Q<d. With d bounded by the Weyl dimension product, the reset error
<=1 is covered by C_small=N_cut^2 d_max/sigma_G; Q=d is handled by the
identity. No small-N frame-conditioning claim is needed.

4. Operational transfer. The frame constants substitute into the
abstract transfer as g0=kappa/(2q), g1=(A+l kappa/2)/q,
C0=sum_s C_s, gamma=|x|^2/q, B0=|x|B, and
rho=|x|sqrt(g1 C0)/(qg0). The arbitrary-code converse uses TP-only
self-score and divided differences; it does not require unital or
sector-preserving channels. The upper uses the complete adjoint frame,
the qd G theta=v coefficient solve, and the ordered double-commutator
series to control the whole Gibbs density in half trace norm. The
positive rank-energy minimizer and exact Choi quadrature exist, but
finding them is not an efficient algorithm. The upper's cost for
optimizer acquisition, node/weight generation, bit precision and circuit
implementation remains unbounded. The abstract transfer should repeat
the no-extra-quantum-resource restriction if used without the inherited
classical_types/regular_su_m interface; the application itself states it.

RANK-PROFILE CONSEQUENCE: B1 AND C1 ONLY

The only profile consequence derived in this audit is rank one. B1=Spin(3)
and C1=Sp(1) representations of Dynkin label n have spin j=n/2 and d=n+1.
Under the B1 metric, its defining three-dimensional spin-one generators
J_i have (1/2)Tr_3(J_iJ_k)=delta_ik and act on spin j as J_i. Thus
Lambda_B1(Q)=Lambda_A1(Q) for the SU(2) symmetric-power profile. Under
the C1 metric, the defining generators are Pauli matrices sigma_i, with
(1/2)Tr_2(sigma_i sigma_k)=delta_ik; sigma_i=2J_i in the standard SU(2)
normalization. Thus Lambda_C1(Q)=4 Lambda_A1(Q). Both identities include
all complex matrices and preserve rank.

The separately banked SU(2) symmetric-power profile
(sol_semiclassical_memory/near_full.txt, §§1-8) therefore gives, for n>=8,
d=n+1,

  Q<=d/2:       Lambda_B1(Q)=Theta(n/Q),
                Lambda_C1(Q)=4 Theta(n/Q),
  Q=d-k>d/2:    Lambda_B1(d-k)=Theta(1/log(e d/k)),
                Lambda_C1(d-k)=4 Theta(1/log(e d/k)),
  Q=d:          Lambda=0.

Since n=Theta(N) on a fixed rank-one cone, the transferred Gibbs error is

  Q<=d/2:       e*_Q=Theta(1/(NQ)),
  Q=d-k>d/2:    e*_(d-k)=Theta(1/[N^2 log(e d/k)]),
  Q=d:          e*_d=0.

Finite n<8 can be absorbed into fixed-cone constants using the all-N
transfer and the finite set of remaining cases. This is an inherited
SU(2) profile consequence, not a new high-rank B/C/D rank-profile proof.

There is also a direct all-simple-type rank-one identity, independent of
the SU(2) identification. For any irreducible highest weight Lambda,

  Lambda(1)=2(C2(Lambda)-||Lambda||^2)=4<Lambda,rho>.

For A=|u><v|, its commutator energy is
2C2-2<mu(u),mu(v)>, where mu(psi)_a=<psi,T_a psi>. Conjugate mu(psi)
into a Cartan; there it is an expectation of weights, hence lies in the
weight polytope conv(W Lambda) and has norm at most ||Lambda||. The
highest-weight projector attains this norm in both slots, proving the
identity. On a compact regular cone Lambda=N lambda, it gives
Lambda(1)=Theta(N), and hence e*_1=Theta(1/N) for all B/C/D ranks under
the audited transfer. The full derivation is recorded in
rank_one_all_types.txt. This sharpens the endpoint consequence beyond
B1/C1, but does not give any Q>1 profile.
