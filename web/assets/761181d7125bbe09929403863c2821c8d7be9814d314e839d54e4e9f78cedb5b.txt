Recorded 2026-10-07. Status: scoped prior-art/interface audit; no theorem or

STRONGEST CLAIM

For the target obtained from broadcasting, the whole image of the operator
norm unit ball under a power of the corrected channel has a dimension-free
pairwise commutator bound. I checked the strongest relevant primary sources
located in this pass. They provide (i) dimension-independent stability for
each pair, and bounds for a fixed finite number of generators with explicit
or implicit dependence on that number; (ii) ultraproduct liftings for fixed
countably generated abelian algebras under diffuseness assumptions; or
(iii) state/extension approximation in weaker or differently constrained
norms. None supplies one trace-preserving entanglement-breaking (EB) map
approximating the entire growing CP image in normalized infinity-to-one
norm, with a modulus independent of matrix dimension.

This is a failure to find a theorem matching the interface, not a proof that
no such theorem exists. The useful additional obstruction is exact: a
diagonal pinching channel has an abelian, already-EB image of its full
operator-norm unit ball whose trace metric covering numbers grow
exponentially. Thus the uniformly trace-total-bounded state-family route in
the N46/R package cannot be applied to full CP images, even in this simplest
case. This example does not refute EB rounding or broadcasting.

TARGET INTERFACE AND DERIVATION

Let (A,tau) be finite-dimensional with faithful normalized trace, and let
Phi:A->A be UCP, tau-preserving, positive and selfadjoint on L2(tau), and
selfcompatible. Let J be a UCP joint channel with
J(X tensor I)=J(I tensor X)=Phi(X). For Hermitian contractions A,C and m>=1,
put A_m=Phi^m(A), C_m=Phi^m(C).

Since Phi is a positive selfadjoint L2 contraction, its spectrum lies in
[0,1]. Spectral calculus gives

  ||Phi(A_m)-A_m||_2^2
    <= max_(0<=lambda<=1) (1-lambda)^2 lambda^(2m) ||A||_2^2
    <= d_m^2,
  tau(Phi(A_m^2)-Phi(A_m)^2)
    = ||A_m||_2^2-||Phi(A_m)||_2^2
    <= max_(0<=lambda<=1) (1-lambda^2)lambda^(2m)=d_m,

where d_m=max (1-lambda)lambda^m=m^m/(m+1)^(m+1)<=1/(m+1).
In a Stinespring representation of J, the commuting receiver copies
A_m tensor I and I tensor C_m have multiplicative defects bounded by the
square roots of these Kadison defects. This yields

  ||[Phi(A_m),Phi(C_m)]||_2 <= 2 sqrt(d_m).

Replacing Phi(A_m),Phi(C_m) by A_m,C_m costs at most 4d_m, since left and
right multiplication by contractions is contractive in tracial L2. Hence

  sup_(A,C=A*,C*, ||A||,||C||<=1)
     ||[Phi^m(A),Phi^m(C)]||_(2,tau)
   <= 2 sqrt(d_m)+4d_m
   <= 2/sqrt(m+1)+4/(m+1).                         (1)

The estimate is uniform over every input contraction, all dimensions, and
all trace weights. It is the exact full-image hypothesis worth attacking.
The desired next step is a single EB map E_m such that

  sup_(A=A*, ||A||<=1) ||E_m(A)-Phi^m(A)||_(1,tau) -> 0,               (2)

uniformly over the channel class. (1) alone does not furnish that map.

PRIMARY-SOURCE AUDIT

1. Bansil–Kachkovskiy, arXiv:2608.31000v1, Theorems 1.1, 1.3 and
Corollary 1.4, https://arxiv.org/html/2608.31000 .
Theorem 1.1 gives, for each pair of selfadjoint contractions X,Y, commuting
X',Y' with

  ||X-X'||_(2,d)^2+||Y-Y'||_(2,d)^2
      <= 24 ||[X,Y]||_(2,d)^(2/3).

Thus (1) gives an O(m^(-1/6)) normalized-2 error for each selected pair.
The correction depends on the pair: the paper explicitly notes that its
two-matrix theorem cannot immediately be iterated. Theorem 1.3 handles a
fixed N-tuple around a common X with a bound

  24 sqrt(N) (sum_j ||[X,Y_j]||_(2,d)^2)^(1/3),

and Corollary 1.4 handles a fixed N-tuple with C(N) and exponent
2/3^(N-1). These statements are uniform in matrix dimension for fixed N;
they are not uniform in a family whose number of generators grows with d.
They also approximate tuples, not a linear UCP map, and impose no CP,
trace-preservation, or EB-factorization constraint on the corrected tuple.
If a basis is used, its size grows as d^2, and basiswise errors do not by
themselves control the operator-norm unit ball.

2. Hadwin–Shulman, A Note on Approximate Liftings, arXiv:0804.1387,
https://arxiv.org/html/0804.1387 . Theorem 4.2 gives a dimension-free
modulus for a fixed number n of almost commuting normal elements in a
tracial C*-algebra; n is fixed in the theorem. Theorem 4.4 lifts a
trace-preserving *-homomorphism from a countably generated commutative
von Neumann algebra into an ultraproduct of diffuse finite von Neumann
algebras. The CP-range problem has a potentially nonseparable range in the
matrix tracial ultraproduct, finite matrix coordinates are not diffuse,
and the target is a uniform EB approximation of a channel rather than a
lift of a *-homomorphism. This source does not bridge those gaps.

3. Raynaud, On ultrapowers of non-commutative L_p spaces, J. Operator
Theory 48 (2002), 41–68, https://jot.theta.ro/jot/archive/2002-048-001/2002-048-001-003.html .
The primary-source statement identifies ultraproducts of Haagerup L_p
spaces with the L_p space of the associated von Neumann ultraproduct. It
is an L_p identification, not a lifting/rounding theorem for CP maps.
The N46/R finite-mixture argument additionally assumes uniform
trace-total-boundedness of the state experiments; its modulus is
qualitative. The full CP image does not meet that compactness hypothesis
uniformly, as shown below.

4. Brandao–Harrow, Quantum de Finetti Theorems under Local Measurements,
arXiv:1210.6367, Theorem 1 and Conjecture 5,
https://arxiv.org/html/1210.6367 . The proved theorem has a k-extendible
state and a local-measurement norm with a specific max/expectation order.
Their Conjecture 5 changes that order. Neither statement is an induced
trace-norm approximation of a channel on every operator contraction.

5. Kossmann–Zeiss, Fixed points in de Finetti hierarchies, arXiv:2607.23689v1,
Theorem 5.3, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23689 . It assumes an actual
one-sided permutation-invariant n-extension exactly fixed by the specified
local channels, and approximates a bipartite state by a constrained
separable state with a bound carrying fixed-point block parameters. This
is an extension theorem, not a map-lifting theorem or a dimension-free
EB approximation on a full CP image.

6. Ioana, Almost commuting matrices and stability for product groups,
arXiv:2108.09589v1, Theorem A,
https://arxiv.org/html/2108.09589 . It supplies a negative stability
example for general non-normal contractions with small normalized
Hilbert–Schmidt commutators. It is not a CP image and is not a
selfcompatible channel, so it is a warning against generic tuple
arguments, not a counterexample to (2).

EXACT COMPACTNESS OBSTRUCTION FOR THE N46 ROUTE

For d divisible by 8, let Delta_d:M_d->M_d be diagonal pinching. It is UCP,
trace preserving, has commuting range, and is EB. For every balanced sign
vector s in {−1,+1}^d (sum_i s_i=0), define

  H_s=diag(s),   rho_s=(I+theta H_s)/d,   0<theta<1.

Then tau(H_s)=0, ||H_s||=1, rho_s is a state, and
Delta_d(rho_s)=rho_s. By greedy packing of the balanced sign vectors, one
can select a family C_d of size at least

  binom(d,d/2) / sum_(j<d/4) binom(d,j)
    >= 2^d / ((d+1) 2^(H_2(1/4)d)),

with pairwise Hamming distance at least d/4. Here
H_2(1/4)=-(1/4)log_2(1/4)-(3/4)log_2(3/4)<1, so the lower bound grows
exponentially. For s,t in C_d,

  (1/2)||rho_s-rho_t||_1
       = theta * dist_H(s,t)/d >= theta/4.

Consequently any trace-distance cover at radius theta/9 needs at least
|C_d| points. Thus the state set in the full image of Delta_d's
operator-norm unit ball is not uniformly trace totally bounded. Because
Delta_d itself is EB, this demonstrates a route limitation only; it is
not a negative result for (2).

BOUNDARIES AND NEXT GATE

There is no counterexample to the broadcasting conjecture, no theorem
proving (2), and no novelty or priority claim. The primary-source pass was
targeted, not exhaustive. The N46 compact-experiment bridge cannot simply
be applied to an entire CP image: covering numbers can grow even when the
map is already EB. Finite-tuple almost-commuting results cannot be
promoted to a channel without dimension-independent control of tuple size,
linear/CP consistency, and the full operator-norm ball.

Next decisive gate: find or prove a CP-map-level lifting theorem that
takes the uniform full-image commutator bound (1) to a single EB map in
the normalized infinity-to-one norm, retaining all dimension, range-size,
and precision dependence; alternatively construct a sequence satisfying
the actual positive, trace-selfadjoint, selfcompatible channel interface
for which (2) fails. General nonstable tuples and pairwise approximants
alone do neither.
