# Qualification and current-correction retrieval views

The optional local neural lane previously ranked statement/construction and
source-passage views. It could omit the very contract or current correction
that makes a result useful. It now adds a separate bounded view of actual
requirements, returned capability, assumptions, costs, units and obstruction,
and at most one query-selected current notice. The candidate generators,
192-record cap, family expansion, pinned CPU model and independent lexical
safety route are unchanged. No question-specific terms, aliases or IDs were
added. The complete operation packet remains authoritative for scope; these
are relevance excerpts, never an applicability classifier or a proof.

A notice hit exposes its exact current notice identity. The existing source
command accepts `--notice`, whose citation index belongs to that notice rather
than the card's evidence array. It retains ordinary source hashes, ranges,
current corrections and byte limits. The optional neural MCP lane preserves
the matched notice through independent-lane fusion; fetching the full capsule
exposes its actual citation routes. Portable CLI, web and MCP routes continue
without this runtime or any private database.

## Exposure and comparison

implementation or proposed retrieval runs. The owner had already read the
scientific corpus, curator reviews and the exposed I06 failure. These are
source-informed, supervised use checks outside implementation fixtures, not
blind scientific problems or untouched held-out evaluation. QUESTION_FREEZE.json

promoted unchanged: SCORING_EQUIVALENCE.json checks16,088 exact comparisons
(2,011 current records times eight queries). That is implementation
correspondence, not additional scientific evidence.

## Exposed regressions and unsupported scope

I06 is an exposed diagnostic: the old neural top five omitted N33 after corpus
expansion. The new current-notice view returns N33 first and directs the reader
to the actual historical b91 review. Its positive audit does not automatically
accept the available bbe manuscript. The exact older subject remains unlocated
in the bounded search. This success is not held-out evidence.

G06 remains a direct neural miss: generic hidden-parameter information can be
worthless for a signed task. Its target is present in the candidate set and is
not token-truncated, yet the model ranks it poorly. The optional-neural MCP menu also returns N768; its pre-existing
common-consumer navigation supplies a path to U1069 and its exact
primal/dual example, with the source-qualified mapping and no provider-to-
provider equivalence. No query alias, score override or ID-specific fix was
added. The next substantive retrieval improvement is better selection of
output-specific mathematical premises, measured against this failure and
fresh problems rather than inferred from a larger index.

ADDITIONAL_PROBES.json was frozen after the pilot and before its two runs, so
it has a different exposure boundary. Q09 returns conditional design and
acquisition-boundary candidates; no acquired room-temperature device/total-
power advantage is supplied. Q10 returns the actual Gaussian-record compiler,
but unavailable joint-record/precision inputs remain NOT ESTABLISHED and
potential conflicts are exposed. Neither search claims an answer, discharges
those assumptions, or infers corpus-wide impossibility from a missing match.

Coverage here is eight main problems, two additional scope probes and two
explicitly exposed regressions. The unchanged corpus contains2,011 current
records and9,975 referenced/selected public artifact identities, of which9,832
have searchable scientific text/ranges under their declared scopes. Aliases,
repeated citations and overlapping field labels are not distinct discoveries.
The count of all potentially reusable mechanisms is UNKNOWN; this is not a
census of all Astra research or a Google-superiority claim. Protected or
unadmitted work is not silently promoted by retrieval.
