## Demonstration and guide

Run `python3 technology/fixtures/contraction/demo.py --check`, then inspect the
adjacent record. For the exact supplied recurrence, the candidate and equally
informed control both take four updates. There is no claimed advantage. Only the
scoped simulation gate is complete; independent benchmark, prototype and system
gates are absent/open. The forward Foundry handoff requests matched total-cost
validation. The reverse theoretical handoff asks how to acquire the supplied
contraction certificate, or prove a scoped obstruction. No outside message was
sent and no measurement was invented.

For a new opportunity, fill `templates/LEAD.json`, retain exact public sources and
their current status, and identify the next obligation. Add engineering/test
sections only when needed. Pro branches may pursue or decline a scoped handoff
without altering current research. Primitive Genesis remains independently
creative. Sol Ultra can dynamically allocate invention work through the `ultra`
route. Foundry executes only authorized bounded checks, records failures and
advances completed gates only with the corresponding evidence. Future agents
should follow `README.md` and `WORKFLOW.md`; process observations use the optional
workflow trace, while causal comparisons still require `evaluation/PROTOCOL.md`.

and recorded attestations. It does not check governing-equation validity,

manufacturability, economic forecasts or deployment safety. Those require the

included. No AI-generated claim is promoted. Numerical TRL assignment, new
controlled prompting interventions and confidential data integrations remain
future explicit work; no broad migration is required to use the public v1 files.
