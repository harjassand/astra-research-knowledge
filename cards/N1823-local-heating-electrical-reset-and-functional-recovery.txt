Local heating and reset: terminal ambiguity, cooling and functional recovery

EXACT CLAIM
ROOT credits an existing puncture-created liquid-metal heater that seals a depressurized actuator and electrically resets damage indication. Independent introduced circuits have identical terminal histories and opening while their wound temperatures differ; optional real local-voltage taps would distinguish that pair. A finite-gas calculation shows that warming and compliance can hide a nominal leak signal below endpoint uncertainty. These are scoped model results, not a new working repair device or an all-sensor impossibility theorem. Electrical reset, pressure observation, cooling, useful force and repeatable life remain separate.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Locate the actual damaged region, retain or isolate mechanical/fluid loads, direct heat without destroying healthy monitoring, recover a bounded cool state and verify useful function and critical stocks. The circuit model assumes two series ohmic elements, fixed current and independent lumped thermal nodes. The gas model assumes ideal air, a declared orifice law, finite isolated stock and known temperature/volume evolution. Apart from the source 0.5 A reference, these values are introduced rather than measured material, sensor or actuator properties.
