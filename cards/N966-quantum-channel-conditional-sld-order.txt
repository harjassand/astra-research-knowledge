EXACT CLAIM
For arbitrary finite-dimensional quantum channels P,Q with the same input, the uniform conditional Umegaki relative-entropy order with constant c is equivalent to the complete equal-reference SLD metric order with the same c. Taking Q to be identity identifies the conditional Umegaki contraction coefficient with the complete equal-reference SLD coefficient.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Finite-dimensional channels, arbitrary finite references, faithful states and faithful baselines; output supports are restricted to effective support and finite boundary cases use common faithful regularization.

BOUNDARIES
No equality with complete equal-reference Umegaki for arbitrary quantum channels follows. The N675 source contributes only its selected Section 2 saturated-reference filter; hardness and compiler claims remain unreviewed.
