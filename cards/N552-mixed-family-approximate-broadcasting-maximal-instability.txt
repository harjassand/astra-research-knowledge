Claim: Let T(ρ,σ)=½‖ρ−σ‖₁. For every finite recipient count m≥2 and ε,η>0, the source constructs finite d,L, a finite family F={ρ_{ψ_x}} on H=⊕_{r=0}^L Sym^{m^r}(C^d), and one fixed positive finite prior (w_x), such that a full-domain permutation-symmetric CPTP map B:H→H^{⊗m} has every one-recipient marginal error at most 1/L≤ε, while every common entanglement-breaking map E:H→H and every family of commuting replacement states diagonal in one common basis have worst-case error at least 1−η. The same lower bounds hold as averages under the fixed prior. For the corresponding classical–quantum state ω_XH=Σ_x w_x|x⟩⟨x|⊗ρ_{ψ_x}, each XH_j marginal can be broadcast within 1/L, yet its trace distance from every state classical on H is at least 1−η, even when the comparator may change its X marginal. Consequently the suprema of EB and commuting reconstruction error are one for every positive broadcasting tolerance at fixed finite m. The proof gives formulas a=(1/L)Σ_{r=1}^L(k_r+1)/(k_r+d), β=min{1,√(Σ_{r=1}^L D_{k_r}/D_{2k_r})}, with k_r=m^r and D_s=binom(d+s−1,s); choose L large and d sufficiently large (or d=m²m^{2L}) so a,β→0. One common finite weighted projective 2m^L-design supports all comparators and bases simultaneously.
Interface: All maps act on finite-dimensional full matrix algebras and are CPTP; T is half trace norm. The input family consists of rank-L mixed direct-sum states ρ_ψ=0_{H_0}⊕(1/L)⊕_{r=1}^L p_ψ^{⊗m^r}, p_ψ=|ψ⟩⟨ψ|, with a fixed finite design prior. The construction's input already contains a classically uncertain number of genuine unknown-state copies, up to K=m^L. The EB comparator is one common physical measure-and-prepare channel for all family states, with arbitrary finite outcomes. The commuting comparator must use one common basis. The cq comparator can choose a different X marginal but must be classical on H. The source's marginal error is exactly 1/L for its constructed channel; the optimal broadcast error is only bounded above by 1/L. Outputs need not be close to the product family and are highly correlated.
Source-reported proof spine: Let H_r=Sym^{k_r}(C^d), k_r=m^r. The explicit isometry partitions each symmetric k_r-copy block into m groups of k_{r−1}; the full-domain channel maps each sector down one level and sends H_0 to a fixed product output. Only the top and bottom blocks differ in each marginal, giving exact half-trace error 1/L. A fixed decoder extracts one qudit; composing it with any EB map and applying the pure-state estimation bound (k+1)/(k+d) yields the prior-average EB lower bound. For commuting families, the support projections Q_ψ=Lρ_ψ obey a second-moment bound summed over an arbitrary common orthonormal basis; the finite exact 2K design preserves the needed moments. A measurement of X and Q_{ψ_x}, followed by data processing and Cauchy–Schwarz, handles arbitrary changes to the comparator's X marginal. Explicit parameter choices make the bounds approach one while the marginal error tends to zero.
Costs: K=m^L genuine copies occur in the highest sector, dim H=Σ_{r=0}^L D_{k_r}, and the Carathéodory finite prior may require up to D_{2K}² points. The explicit algebraic cubature also has (ceil((2K+1)/2)(2K+1))^{d−1} points; the paper does not establish an efficient construction at large parameters. No cost is supplied for preparing the input from one unknown qudit; such preparation is not part of the contract. The channel is an explicit mathematical CPTP map on a very large space, not a demonstrated resource-bounded physical implementation. Global cloning error approaches one; for L≥2 the source also gives ‖Φ−Φ²‖_diamond=2. Small marginal trace distance therefore does not imply global product fidelity or near-idempotence.
