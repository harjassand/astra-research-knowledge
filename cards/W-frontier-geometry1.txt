## Primary technical result

On a single smooth complete metric on `R×S²`, constructed from locally finite sharp smooth dips in a sphere-radius profile,

\[
\lambda_4(-4\Delta+R)\ge2,\qquad
\lambda_a(-a\Delta+R)=-\infty\quad\text{for every }0<a<4.
\]

Any finite `k` diagonal log warps necessarily yield a positive supersolution at coefficient `4k/(k+1)`. Thus their scalar curvature cannot have any finite global lower bound. Every compact exhaustion member nevertheless admits arbitrarily close to scalar lower bound2 by splitting the logarithmic drift into sufficiently many equal warps. Stabilization rank and exhaustion infimum do not interchange.

The corrected fixed-rank interface is `sup_warps inf scalar=λ_(4k/(k+1))`. A useful sufficient repair is explicit: if `R≥−C` and `λ_4≥λ>0`, any integer `k>C/λ` admits positive finite stabilization. The characterization uses standard positive-solution theory; no novelty is claimed for it alone.
