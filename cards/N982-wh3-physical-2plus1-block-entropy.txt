N982-wh3-physical-2plus1-block-entropy | Every physical 2+1 block-diagonal qutrit state satisfies the W3 entropy target
status=source_derived_unreviewed; internal acceptance is bound to an exact content version and review scope.

EXACT CLAIM
Let rho = B direct-sum C be a normalized state for a physical C^3 = C^2 direct-sum C decomposition, where B is positive on C^2 tensor R and C is positive on R. For every finite reference R, 2 S((W3 tensor id)rho) - S(rho) - S(rho_R) >= ln 3, with equality exactly at rho = I3/3 tensor tau. The B block may be entangled.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
An actual physical 2+1 qutrit decomposition with no cross-sector coherence; arbitrary finite reference and positive B,C blocks with total trace one.

BOUNDARIES
The result does not extend to arbitrary coherence between the two- and one-dimensional sectors and does not settle the unrestricted W3 entropy inequality. No separability premise or reference-dimension bound is used.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
An exact entropy decomposition into three nonnegative information terms; no physical measurement or implementation is reported.

NEXT OBLIGATION
A global proof must account for cross-sector coherence rather than discard it by pinching.
