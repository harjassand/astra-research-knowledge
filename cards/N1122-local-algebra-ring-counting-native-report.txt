Acquired local-algebra counters with eight-state moments and a same-residue hardness boundary

EXACT CLAIM
From explicit cubic Boolean ANF, acquire the chain algebra A=F_(2^d)[t]/(t^e), module coordinates, affine center and ordinary phase. For the acquired repeated-pair residue with one common nonzero phase scalar, binary pair types, d>=3,3 not dividing d,r>=2,b=2r+1, any depth e and quotient-quadratic height3h<=e+1, exact signed/zero counting has bit time O(Ln^2+n^12)+softO(d^31) and space O(L+n^6)+softO(d^20), n=bde, using the published fixed-variable point-count algorithm. The separate rank-three branch has a broader ordinary ternary cubic promise. The generic point-count engine is unimplemented in this distribution; executable reference terminals explicitly scan a bounded field.

BOUNDARIES
No unrestricted Boolean cubic, SAT or generic circuit counter follows. The hard fixed F8 depth-two family permits higher nilpotent coefficients and shows why the degree qualification cannot simply be dropped. A chain ring with e>1 is not a field: erasing its nilradical can discard the phase carrying the hardness reduction. Even e=1 does not turn the repeated-pair cubic into every other field-decoder promise. Keep quotient height, full ordinary coefficients, signed moment weights, radial phase and0^0=1. The generic Lauder-Wan backend is a mathematical dependency, not an implemented/executed engine. Reference field scans default to24 bits; archived examples and callback replays do not demonstrate polynomial scaling or practical advantage.
