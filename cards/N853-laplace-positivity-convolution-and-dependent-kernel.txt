N853-laplace-positivity-convolution-and-dependent-kernel | Laplace-positivity criterion with an adaptive dependent kernel and strict product boundary
Status: source_derived_unreviewed. Mission status: NOT_ACHIEVED.

Let E be a real Banach space, C subsetneq E a closed convex cone with nonempty interior, e in int(C), and T a compactly supported continuous linear E-valued distribution on R^m with T(1)=e. With L_T(g)=T_y(exp(g·y)), the source-reported theorem makes strict interior positivity L_T(g) in int(C) for every real g equivalent to existence of either (i) a scalar probability measure with every exponential moment whose convolution with T is C-positive, or (ii) a strictly positive smooth density p in the all-exponential-moment, all-derivative-integrability class for which T*p is pointwise C-valued. Pointedness is not required. In the matrix PSD cone this is L_T(g) positive definite at every g. Semidefinite transform positivity with zeros is insufficient.

There is no quantitative lower margin assumption on L_T. Under strict positivity, an adaptive construction selects a smooth radial strictly log-concave p=Z^(-1)exp(-V), with every exponential moment, so the normalized output M=T*p is pointwise interior-positive and its value tracks p(x)L_T(grad V(x)) within any specified relative cone error 0<epsilon<1. The construction uses the margin on each gradient ball; it does not give a finite-cost kernel compiler.

The producer proof and root reconstruction preserve a smooth compact signed scalar example with L_T(g)>0 everywhere but valleys below exp(-exp(r_n^2)); any positive regularization forces cumulant growth at least exp(r_n^2)-O(r_n). It therefore excludes every fixed two-tail bound C exp(-c|x|log|x|), for every c>0, while the adaptive all-exponential-moment construction still applies. This tail-class separation is carried by the producer proof and root reconstruction, not the independent consequence report, and is an analytic construction rather than a numerically resolved valley.

For Qp=p+partial_x^2 partial_y^2 p, the Laplace symbol is 1+g_x^2 g_y^2>0. The explicit radial strictly log-concave p proportional to exp(-5 rho log rho), rho=sqrt(1+(x^2+y^2)/25), satisfies Qp >= 0.1832635445963654... p > 0 everywhere and integral Qp=1. Every strictly positive C^2 product density with all exponential moments fails to make Qp nonnegative, and every nondegenerate Gaussian, including correlated/translated Gaussians, fails. Dependence changes positivity while both one-dimensional marginals and covariance stay fixed; E_Q[X^2Y^2]=E_p[X^2Y^2]+4.

Positive convolution multipliers are established mathematics.
