Explicit serial CP/two-copositive entanglement-breaking cutoff

STATUS | accepted_internally_verified at the exact stated scope
EXACT CLAIM | For d>=2, every serial composition of2(d-1) varying complex-linear CP maps on M_d whose output-transposed maps are two-positive is entanglement breaking for every finite reference and positive joint input. Trace preservation and unitality are unnecessary. The bound is sufficient, not claimed optimal.
INTERFACE | Every counted factor must satisfy both CP and two-copositivity on the same M_d. Serial composition has no noiseless quantum bypass. Zero output is separable; normalize nonzero output only afterwards.
PROOF SPINE | Two-reference positivity and a singular-support Schur decomposition give a two-EB pair. Equal-diagonal positive blocks are separable. CP supplies arbitrary-reference positivity for Schmidt-number reduction, dropping the number by at least one per pair. After d-1 pairs it is one.
COST |2(d-1) serial factors suffice; no efficient EB decomposition, implementation or runtime guarantee is acquired.
BOUNDARIES | PSI qubit block construction and prior Christandl–Mueller-Hermes–Wolf iteration are credited. N574 October9 already claims finite exact existence and the same Werner-form non-PPT example. The direct proof supplies an explicit exponent independently of the broader cone argument. N577 approximation remains useful below this cutoff and for quantitative questions. The PSI full-PPT Lean result does not formalize this weaker adaptation. No tensor-power, arbitrary-network, optimality, priority, device or practical advantage claim follows.
