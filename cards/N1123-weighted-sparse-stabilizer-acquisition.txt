N1123-weighted-sparse-stabilizer-acquisition

Acquire sparse stabilizers while charging X updates and Z phases differently

EXACT CLAIM

A hidden independent family with rank n-delta_star and total joint weight W_star guarantees delta<=delta_star+floor(W_star/(w+1)). Acquisition/identities are unconditional; asymptotic counting remains conditional on the local-phase theorem.
