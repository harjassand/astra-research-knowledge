N1241-finite-memory-terminal-calibration

Count all clock and monitor states before using rare terminal evidence

EXACT CLAIM
A finite autonomous homogeneous controller with fixed support and K total states has terminal conditional TV at most tanh(Delta/4), hence at most tanh[K/4 log((1+alpha)/(1-alpha))] under the stated row likelihood perturbation. Forest normalization cancels rare-event probability and waiting time. Independent reset/row-access and a positive transition floor support a separately paid finite calibration procedure.

INTERFACE
Finite homogeneous absorbing chain/controller, common fixed transition support and all physical control states counted
Positive terminal conditioning probability and declared per-row multiplicative likelihood bounds
For calibration, independent row reset access plus positive floor beta; finite row sampling is paid

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
O(K^2/(beta epsilon^2) log(M/delta)) samples PER ROW under supplied independent access, plus all rows/resets
Rare example evidence 2^-40 and wait 2^41-2 are analytic calculations, not acquired measurements; 47 million seeded PRNG bits are diagnostic

BOUNDARIES
