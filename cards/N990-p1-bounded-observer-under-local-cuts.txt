N990-p1-bounded-observer-under-local-cuts | Private noise contracts a finite-dimensional observer under acquired local cuts

STATUS | Source-derived theorem and implementation-audit report; scoped review pending. The claim concerns the altered protocol with physical local cuts and private onsite noise. It is not a simulation of the uncut experiment or an established practical advantage.

EXACT CLAIM
For a finite-dimensional lattice with a supplied block partition of maximum size B maintained by visible local measure-and-prepare cuts, apply independent private full-rank onsite replacement noise after each sweep. Under the stated finite-operation and model conditions, a positive block-state observer maintains a uniform trace-distance allowance for every supported current record and for future physical experiments started from the encoded state. The state uses at most (N/B)d^(2B) independent real coordinates, with sufficient precision growing logarithmically in N and inverse tolerance when d, B and noise strength are fixed. Dependence on B remains exponential.

PHYSICAL CONTRACT
The cut is part of the physical protocol: it separates the measured site while retaining the conditionally updated remainder of its former block as one component. Remote entanglement is not split by assuming spatial adjacency. Private noise labels are not observer inputs. The initial state, local operations, maximum block size, finite work between sweeps and noise lower bound must satisfy the source contract.

SOURCE-REPORTED FINITE EXAMPLE
The selected report describes an N=4, B=2, b=34 two-qubit control with 512 sweeps, 2048 weak marks and 510 visible endpoint cuts, with a uniform current-state allowance 31/67108864. Its independent audit text reports 48 exact noise/rounding cases. No producer code was selected or replayed in this intake. The 10,000-site, 4.8 MB coordinate payload is a theoretical scaling calculation, not an execution; it excludes partition, model, controller, cache and working-array costs.

LIMITS AND COMPARATORS
A fixed current-state error does not give a fixed error for the whole infinite record; that requires a separate summable error schedule with growing precision. The theorem is exponential in B and the cuts/noise change the experiment. The positive finite-dimensional cone floor does not transfer to the unbounded bosonic thermal space using mean energy alone. Conventional density filters and tensor methods receive the same information and are direct comparators; exclusive advantage is not established. This result is distinct from N959's classical local-preparation record and does not validate that claim.
