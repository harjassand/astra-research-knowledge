N983-wh3-pinching-coherence-obstruction | Physical pinching can increase the W3 entropy objective, even with uniform marginals
status=source_derived_unreviewed; internal acceptance is bound to an exact content version and review scope.

EXACT CLAIM
The physical 2+1 pinching map is not a monotone reduction for the W3 entropy objective. A faithful coherence perturbation gives a local BKM quotient above 1/2; one exact interval result is 0.5659667... at epsilon = 1/100, and a stronger example keeps both input marginals exactly I3/3 with quotient 0.7182625... at the same epsilon. This refutes the pinching proof step, not the W3 entropy target.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
The source's faithful baselines and physical cross-sector tangents have vanishing reference partial trace; for sufficiently small nonzero perturbation the states remain faithful and retain the stated marginals.

BOUNDARIES
The coherence gain can consume the block theorem's safety margin, but the source does not show that it exceeds the margin or violates the target. This is a counterexample to a half-contraction/pinching mechanism only.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Exact local BKM metric formulas plus interval enclosures at epsilon = 1/100; no physical experiment is involved.

NEXT OBLIGATION
Any global argument using block pinching must account for both the finite block slack and the coherence gain.
