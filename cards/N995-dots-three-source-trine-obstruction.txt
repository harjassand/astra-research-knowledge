N995-dots-three-source-trine-obstruction | A qubit trine obstructs a universal three-source balanced entropy transfer
status=source_derived_unreviewed; exact scoped internal acceptance, where present, is bound by knowledge/reviews/A993-dots-binary-entropy-source-read.json.

EXACT CLAIM
The scalar balanced third finite difference s(a+b+c)-s(a+b)-s(a+c)-s(b+c)+s(a)+s(b)+s(c), for s(t)=-t log t, is nonnegative for all a,b,c>=0. For the three real qubit trine projectors P_0,P_1,P_2, the corresponding balanced matrix trace-entropy expression is (3/2)log(3/4)<0, since the sum has spectrum (3/2,3/2), each pair has spectrum (3/2,1/2), and each individual projector has spectrum (1,0). The scalar-to-matrix transfer therefore fails in this explicit three-source case, and negativity persists under sufficiently small positive epsilon-identity perturbations.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Three positive semidefinite operators on C^2; entropy S(Y)=-Tr Y log Y with natural logarithms; compare the same balanced coefficients as the scalar third finite difference.

BOUNDARIES
This is an obstruction to one universal balanced three-source entropy lift. It is not a W3 counterexample, does not contradict the older arbitrary-classical-channel/qubit-reference global mutual-information theorem, and does not establish a t log t fixed-prior matrix-SDPI separation.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Exact 2x2 spectral evaluation and a continuity argument for faithful perturbations; no channel construction, physical measurement, or computational advantage is asserted.

NEXT OBLIGATION
A multi-source result needs additional hypotheses or a different mechanism. The general coherent WH3 target and a compatible fixed-prior SDPI separation remain open.
