Claim: Given T explicit trace-preserving instruments on C^d with Gaussian-rational Kraus matrices of at most B-bit numerators/denominators, a polynomial-bit algorithm decides whether every length-T history product has rank at most one without listing histories. For a repeated fixed instrument, eventual all-history collapse occurs iff it has occurred by D=binom(d,2) steps. Separately, the exact terminal channel after forgetting outcomes has a representation sum_{j<=d^4} c_j B_j X B_j*, where c_j are positive rationals and every B_j is an actual length-T branch product. If all products have rank at most one, this yields an exact measure-prepare form.
Interface: The instrument and Kraus matrices are supplied. Complexity is polynomial in dense Hilbert dimension d and input bit length, not in qubit count log2(d). Compression preserves only the terminal forgotten-outcome channel; it need not preserve histories, their probabilities, or intermediate intervention responses.
Proof spine: Lift each Kraus operator to the exterior square; the span of all branch images is propagated by V_t=sum_i (Lambda^2 K_i^(t))V_(t-1), and is zero iff every branch product has rank<=1. For a fixed instrument the descending subspace chain stabilizes within D steps. For compression, vectorize branch products into Hermitian Choi rank-one matrices in a d^4-dimensional real space and use exact rational conic elimination with positive weights.
Costs: The source gives polynomial bit complexity in dense dimension d, instrument length and input bit size, with O(sum_t r_t D^3) field operations for the span test and at most d^4 retained products.
Failure: This is not representation-independent entanglement-breaking recognition: the source gives an invertible-Pauli Kraus representation of a depolarizing entanglement-breaking channel that fails the test. d^4 is polynomial in d but exponential in qubits. No generic simulation improvement follows. Its exact combined novelty is unestablished.
