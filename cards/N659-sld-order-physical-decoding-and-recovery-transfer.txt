Interface: Finite channels with common input; ordinary measured-chi-square dominance for every input state pair. Arbitrary-prior k-letter ensembles, k>=2; each fixed encoding of a d-dimensional logical system, d>=2. Task-dependent physical decoders and average criteria.

Claim (source-reported, independent of N402/N403). Write M >=_SLD N when measured Pearson divergences satisfy chi_m^2(M rho || M sigma) >= chi_m^2(N rho || N sigma) for every input state pair, with extended support values. For k>=2 define
  h_k(p) = 1/k + (p-1/k)^2/(1-1/k) = p^2 + (1-p)^2/(k-1).
For any k-letter input ensemble, including arbitrary priors, the manuscript derives p_guess(M) >= h_k(p_guess(N)). For any fixed encoding E of a d-dimensional logical system (d>=2), it derives F*(M E) >= h_{d^2}(F*(N E)), where F* optimizes CPTP decoding and entanglement fidelity is overlap with the normalized maximally entangled logical state. Thus epsilon_M <= 2 epsilon_N - k epsilon_N^2/(k-1), using k letters or k=d^2 respectively. These are optimal average message error and maximally mixed logical entanglement infidelity, not worst-case input guarantees.

Mechanism and support contract. Let S_theta(X)=(theta X+X theta)/2 and q_theta(X)=<X,S_theta^{-1}X>_HS, with inverse taken on the effective output support. The integral S_theta^{-1}(C)=2 integral_0^infinity exp(-t theta) C exp(-t theta) dt gives a CP inverse there. For theta=sum_x p_x M(rho_x), G_x=S_theta^{-1}(p_x M(rho_x)) is a physical POVM on supp(theta), completed on its orthogonal complement. For logical channel L, theta=L(I/d), R_L=(1/d)L* o S_theta^{-1} is CPTP on the effective support and extends by a replacer, discarding cross blocks. The ambient Moore-Penrose superoperator alone need not be CP. Centering at 1/k or 1/d^2 and metric Cauchy-Schwarz give the bounds. Ordinary order suffices for each fixed task; the source's parallel-block extension assumes complete order (N660).

Sharp obstruction. For 0<a<1, let M reveal a classical binary input with probability a^2 and otherwise erase; let N be the BSC of bias a. The source gives an explicit complete signed-lift certificate and uniform-prior successes p_M=(1+a^2)/2=h_2((1+a)/2), p_N=(1+a)/2. Their error ratio 1+a tends to 2. Binary equality rules out a universal linear error factor below two, while p_M<p_N rules out a universal physical simulator and exact guessing dominance. This does not establish general-k or quantum sharpness.

Prior-art boundary (source-reported). The Bures metric-adjoint decoder and near-optimal recovery/discrimination have established antecedents; the source claims only their cross-channel order consequence. It does not claim a better decoder. Its internal reviews and six new replays are reported evidence, not repeated here.
