N968-qubit-depolarizing-mutual-information | Qubit depolarization has exact mutual-information contraction distinct from complete contraction

EXACT CLAIM
For qubit depolarization Dλ(ρ)=λρ+(1−λ)I₂Trρ/2 on its full CP interval −1/3≤λ≤1, the ordinary arbitrary-finite-reference quantum mutual-information contraction coefficient is 2λ²/(1+λ). The complete and conditional Umegaki coefficient is 3λ²/(1+2λ); these differ for interior nontrivial λ. For d>2 the retained sources establish bounds and a strict gap, not an exact MI formula.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Qubit depolarizing channel on the full completely-positive interval; MI supremum over arbitrary finite reference states; coefficient definitions are those in the cited proofs.

BOUNDARIES
Ordinary MI, complete equal-reference Umegaki, and conditional relative entropy remain different quantities. Higher-dimensional exact MI is not claimed.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
