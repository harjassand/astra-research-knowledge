N985-wh3-rank-five-schmidt-aligned-product-direction | A rank-five Schmidt-aligned product-direction class obeys the W3 entropy bound for every Schmidt spectrum
status=source_derived_unreviewed; internal acceptance is bound to an exact content version and review scope.

EXACT CLAIM
For every normalized qutrit Schmidt vector psi = sum_i sqrt(p_i)|ii> and every off-diagonal Schmidt-basis product vector eta = |jk>, j != k, every state rho supported on span{psi,eta} satisfies S(rho) - 2 S((W3 tensor id)rho) + S(rho_R) <= -ln 3. The exact homogeneous resolvent certificate covers all nonnegative Schmidt weights. It closes this aligned rank-five support mechanism only.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
The two-dimensional support is generated by one Schmidt vector and one off-diagonal product basis vector in the same Schmidt coordinates; any phase of the logical coherence is removed by a diagonal local unitary.

BOUNDARIES
It does not cover arbitrary superposed product vectors u tensor w or every rank-five kernel space. No nonunitary filtering is used as an entropy symmetry. The global W3/qutrit target remains open.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
A finite exact symbolic certificate records the cleared-denominator identity and a square term plus nonnegative remainder. The checker was not replayed during intake.

NEXT OBLIGATION
General full-Schmidt-plus-arbitrary-product directions and unrestricted higher-rank boundary states remain open.
