N406-depolarizing-complete-coefficient | Depolarizing complete and conditional coefficients are exact; ordinary MI differs
status=source_derived_unreviewed; the version-bound A406 overlay accepts the exact complete/conditional coefficient at the cited finite-dimensional source scope. External priority and physical advantage remain unestablished.

EXACT CLAIM
For d>=2 and D_lambda(X)=lambda X+(1-lambda)Tr(X)I/d on its complete-positivity interval -1/(d^2-1)<=lambda<=1, the complete equal-reference and conditional Umegaki contraction coefficients are both (d^2-1)lambda^2/[1+(d^2-2)lambda]. For the qubit this is 3lambda^2/(1+2lambda). The ordinary quantum mutual-information coefficient is a separate quantity; see N968.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Finite-dimensional depolarizing channel, arbitrary finite untouched quantum references, faithful states and the stated full CP parameter interval. The coefficient suprema use positive denominators and common faithful regularization at finite boundary pairs.
