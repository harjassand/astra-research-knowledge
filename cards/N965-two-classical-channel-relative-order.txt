N965-two-classical-channel-relative-order | Two classical channels have equivalent complete, conditional and SLD relative-order comparisons
status=source_derived_unreviewed; source internal audits are accepted only at exact version and scope through a knowledge review overlay. External correctness/priority, physical evidence and practical advantage remain separately scoped.

EXACT CLAIM
For two finite stochastic channels K and L with a shared input alphabet and any finite c≥0, uniform complete equal-reference Umegaki comparison is equivalent to the conditional Umegaki comparison and to the complete SLD metric comparison, with arbitrary finite quantum references.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Two finite stochastic channels on the same input alphabet; arbitrary finite quantum reference; uniform quantification over faithful state pairs or faithful cq baseline/tangent pairs as stated.

BOUNDARIES
A comparison between two classical channels only. It is not a theorem about a single classical channel versus identity, nor does it imply equality for arbitrary quantum channels.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Mathematical comparison; no physical channel acquisition or operational advantage is established.
