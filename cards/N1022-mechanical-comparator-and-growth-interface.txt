EXACT CLAIM
Two linear elastic branches with top/bottom stiffnesses a,b and c,d, positive shared extension Delta and midpoint probe k>=0 give g=Delta*(c*b-a*d)/[(a+b)*(c+d)+k*(a+b+c+d)]; sign(g) compares d with c*b/a independently of positive load magnitude. Zero drive gives no signal. Multiple finite probes require joint calibration. In a separate instantaneous response model, a cubically closing nutrient slit gives e_dot=-A*e^3 and asymptotic exact completion; a positive nutrient threshold can stop it earlier. Delayed precursor stock can keep adding stiffness after feed stops. Reference drift, nonlinear elasticity and aliased incomplete states prevent this scalar response from universally certifying strength or choosing the missing construction action.

INTERFACE AND ASSUMPTIONS
Quasistatic elastic equilibrium with positive Hookean a,b,c,d, nonnegative sensing stiffness k, common fixed boundary geometry and positive tensile excitation. Multiple sensing arms use the actual coupled equilibrium, not independently assumed thresholds. Flow and precursor results require their separately declared ODEs, fixed conversion and actual useful incorporation. Observation counterexamples preserve prescribed displacement, full scaled reference/probe or the stated equal-prior two-action toy.

BOUNDARIES
The bridge is a known elastic-comparison principle, not a new elasticity theorem or acquired repair device. Sign independence does not give load-independent signal amplitude, noise margin, zero-load sensing, absolute strength or fatigue certification. Cubic-slit asymptotics assume instantaneous response; delayed precursor dynamics are a separate model. Synthetic calibrated thresholds, 1.004–1.005 endpoints and repeated-repair fractions are producer reports, not independently replayed or measured biology. Uniform region independence and finite noise/assessment windows cannot be turned into a joint material guarantee. Ionic continuity does not acquire glucose/oxygen flux. Guided fungal growth, cellulose composites, sensors and fluidic hysterons are source-cited components, not newly primary-audited papers or an integrated interface.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Charge sensing stiffness/backaction, required tensile drive, displacement resolution/noise and stable reference calibration. Nutrient flow must remain productive until a declared tolerance; account for delayed stock, conversion efficiency, finite feedstock and oxygen. Hysteresis trades sensitivity to small new damage against noisy reopening; filtering delays response. Externally supplied calibrated sensors/valves and nonliving repair agents remain equally informed comparators; no priced advantage is supplied.
