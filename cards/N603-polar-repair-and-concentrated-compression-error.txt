Claim: Use the source convention ||X||_{2,n}^2=Tr(X*X)/n even on a corner of C^n. For an orthogonal projection P, Q=I-P, U in U(n), A=QUQ restricted to Q C^n, and T=QU*PUQ restricted to Q C^n, the report gives inf_{V in U(Q C^n)} ||A-V||_{2,n}^2 = Tr((I_Q-sqrt(I_Q-T))^2)/n, between Tr(T^2)/(4n) and Tr(T^2)/n. With B=PUQ, T=B*B, so the cost is controlled by Tr((B*B)^2)/n, not rank alone. It also gives, for every integer q>=2, n=q^4 and a swap of two q^2-dimensional spaces for which an error of normalized size epsilon=q^-2 is killed by deleting an epsilon fraction, while the compressed unitary needs repair distance q^-1=sqrt(epsilon).
Interface: P is an orthogonal projection of rank k, Q=I-P, and the compressed operator acts on Q C^n. Every normalized Hilbert-Schmidt norm in this result uses denominator n, not n-k. The example is a finite-dimensional matrix construction.
Proof spine: A*A=I_Q-T. If s_j are singular values of A and t_j are eigenvalues of T, the closest-unitary Frobenius distance is sum_j(1-s_j)^2 with s_j=sqrt(1-t_j). The scalar bound t/2<=1-sqrt(1-t)<=t gives the two trace bounds. In the example, QUQ has q^2 zero singular values and the rest equal to one.
