N981-wh3-werner-gibbs-separatrix | A faithful Werner stationary point separates two W3 Gibbs-search basins at a = 2
status=source_derived_unreviewed; internal acceptance is bound to an exact content version and review scope.

EXACT CLAIM
On the exact normalized Werner family, the W3 update at a = 2 is x_next = sqrt((x^3 + 2)/3). It has a product fixed point x = 1 and a faithful correlated fixed point x = 1 + sqrt(3). The latter is a full stationary point with objective sqrt(3)/6, below the 1/3 target; it is not a local maximum. The exact separatrix explains why different search starts can enter different basins, but proves no global upper bound.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Finite-dimensional W3 update restricted to the normalized Werner invariant family for its exact scalar dynamics; full stationarity at the correlated point follows from the stated self-consistency.

BOUNDARIES
The Werner curve does not classify the full state-space dynamics. The stationary point is not a local maximum and does not contradict the proposed bound. The unrestricted W3/qutrit target remains open.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Exact one-variable algebra only; no algorithmic novelty or practical search advantage is claimed.

NEXT OBLIGATION
A global proof must control states outside the Werner invariant family and all phase-bearing directions.
