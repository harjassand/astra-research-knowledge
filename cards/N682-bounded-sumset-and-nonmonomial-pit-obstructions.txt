BOUNDED-EXPONENT SUMSET OBSTRUCTION
For A subset {0,...,B}^n, let E_l(A) be the union of vector sums of k elements of A for 1<=k<=l. For any positive integer weight vector beta, scalarization A^beta={<beta,a>:a in A} satisfies

    | union_(k=1)^l k A^beta | <= |E_l(A)| <= (l B + 1)^n.

The report notes equality can be achieved in the second comparison by radix weights beta_j=(l B+1)^(j-1); large weights cannot create exponent vectors outside the box.

VERSION-SPECIFIC LITERATURE COMPARISON
The report applies this bound to Theorem 2 of Narayanan, ECCC Report 2026/138, using the parameters it says were printed in the version inspected on 2026-10-11: 1<=r<=n, m=n binom(n+r-1,r), superpolynomial s=n^(omega(1)), iteration parameter l=s/r, exponent entries bounded by 2^(n^O(1)), and a required scalar sumset size 4^s. Its deduction is that the stated exponent box gives at most 2^(o(s)) scalar sums, so the expander required by that simultaneous parameter regime cannot exist.
