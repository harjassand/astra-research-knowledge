EXACT CLAIM
For faithful positive A,X on the same finite-dimensional space, with no commutation assumption, the positive measure mu=h'' of h(t)=Tr(A-tX)_+ represents the positive and negative traces of every real pencil alpha A+beta X by the scalar densities alpha r+beta against mu. Consequently, for nonnegative mixture coefficients alpha_j,beta_j and real c_j satisfying both sum_j c_j alpha_j=0 and sum_j c_j beta_j=0, the balanced entropy combination of the mixtures equals its scalar integral against mu. Every scalar inequality of this balanced form, with optional linear trace terms, holds for all such operator pairs iff it holds for all nonnegative scalar pairs. Singular finite matrices follow by the stated epsilon-identity regularization and entropy continuity.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Two fixed positive operators on the same finite-dimensional space; nonnegative mixtures; both coefficient-balance equations; singular inputs only by the stated continuity extension.

BOUNDARIES
Does not cover three or more independent sources, arbitrary products or log-Euclidean means, partial transposes, general matrix-valued functional calculus inequalities, or unbalanced entropy combinations. The testing measure is abstract and does not represent one physical measurement.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
An exact finite-dimensional identity in nats with natural logarithms. Matrix dimension and numerical precision are not assigned an algorithmic or experimental cost by the source.

NEXT OBLIGATION
Any use with three or more sources needs a separate proof; the retained trine obstruction rules out a blanket three-source scalar-to-matrix lift. General WH3 remains open.
