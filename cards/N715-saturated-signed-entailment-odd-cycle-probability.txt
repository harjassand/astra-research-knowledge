Global saturation is the essential interface: labels are 0, o and (u,v,s), u<v, s in F2, with N=2+2 binom(n,2). E_0 is all edge sets, E_o the odd-cycle event, and E_(u,v,s) is the union of that event with existence of a simple u-v path of sign s. An odd cycle anywhere, including in a remote component, makes every label feasible. In a balanced edge set a feasible pair must be connected with its unique path sign.
