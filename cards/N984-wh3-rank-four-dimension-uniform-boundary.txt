N984-wh3-rank-four-dimension-uniform-boundary | Rank-four boundary violations are excluded uniformly over finite reference dimensions
status=source_derived_unreviewed; internal acceptance is bound to an exact content version and review scope.

EXACT CLAIM
For arbitrary finite reference spaces R_n, faithful normalized X_n on C^3 tensor R_n, and states xi_n with rank xi_n <= 4, if ||X_n - xi_n||_1 tends to zero then limsup Tr exp[2(W3 tensor id)(log X_n) - I tensor log (X_n)_R] <= 1/3. Equivalently, each fixed positive gap above 1/3 has a dimension-uniform positive trace-distance from all rank-at-most-four states. The source gives existence, not an explicit or effective modulus.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
The Gibbs-dual boundary argument with active reference dimension at most 12, combined with the exact rank-four Kraus-span trichotomy and the source's prior dimension-uniform rank-three boundary proof.

BOUNDARIES
This excludes only neighborhoods of rank-at-most-four states. It does not prove the unrestricted faithful inequality, provide a computable delta(epsilon), or rule out rank-five or higher boundary mechanisms.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Dimension-free compactness and entropy-continuity argument; the modulus is nonconstructive. No hardware or computational advantage is established.

NEXT OBLIGATION
Rank-five and higher boundary mechanisms, including arbitrary coherence and product directions, remain unresolved.
