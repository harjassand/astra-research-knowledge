N972-complex-povm-ppt-separable-order | Arbitrary complex POVM pairs have collapsed comparison orders on PPT and separable classes
status=source_derived_unreviewed; source internal audits are accepted only at exact version and scope through a knowledge review overlay. External correctness/priority, physical evidence and practical advantage remain separately scoped.

EXACT CLAIM
For two arbitrary finite complex POVM channels P,Q and either the PPT or separable input-state class across signal and reference, the uniform complete equal-reference Umegaki, conditional Umegaki, and complete SLD comparisons are equivalent over all finite references.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Two finite POVM channels on a common finite signal; input states restricted to PPT or separable across A:R; every finite reference; uniform comparison constant c≥0.

BOUNDARIES
Relative comparison of two POVM channels only. This does not replace a channel by quantum identity and does not resolve the separable Werner–Holevo MI-ratio question.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Mathematical information-order statement; no physical implementation or advantage is implied.
