Interface: Explicit independent two-state CTMC coordinates with rational parameters, a finite explicit list of target configurations and an explicit product initial law. Additive CDF queries; finite-bin transcript and finite-support sampling guarantees have separate error metrics.
Failure: The continuous first-hit CDF result is an additive approximation, not an exact continuous-time sampler or total-variation guarantee for continuous hit times. Finite-support sampling has an absolute Kolmogorov guarantee. Marked-event transcript results require supplied bins and policy restrictions. The finite interval receipt is one diagnostic; no priority or independent proof certification.

For n independent two-state CTMCs with rational parameters, a list B of m distinct explicit configurations and a product initial law, the source reports a deterministic finite-bit algorithm approximating Pr(H_B<=t) to additive epsilon in time polynomial in input length, m and log(1/epsilon), without enumerating 2^n states. A separate finite-support distribution approximates the full CDF uniformly in absolute Kolmogorov distance, with support size and bit cost polynomial in 1/epsilon; it can be sampled with expected polynomial cost. At fixed rational t>0, a relative approximation for lower-tail CDF values is also claimed. These guarantees do not imply relative accuracy for arbitrarily small upper survival tails, total variation for continuous hit times or exact continuous-time sampling.

The marked-event construction additionally approximates hit-label sub-CDFs and distributions over explicitly supplied time bins. For K-event feedback observing only past labels and those bins, it reports a transcript total-variation guarantee with charged approximation error. It excludes finer continuous-time observations, hidden trajectories and arbitrary nonproduct posteriors.

The packet reports a positive-quadrature boundary-resolvent implementation and one 32-component, two-target Laplace-transform enclosure with width 2^-48; this is not a full exact continuous-time sampler or a full CDF implementation.
