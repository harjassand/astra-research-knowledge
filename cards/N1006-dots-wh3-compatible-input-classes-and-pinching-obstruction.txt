N1006-dots-wh3-compatible-input-classes-and-pinching-obstruction | WH3 entropy receivers cover specific spectral and support classes while forbidding free pinching
STATUS | accepted_internally_verified at the completed scoped theorem/audit versions; their union does not establish the unrestricted target.

EXACT CLAIM
For N=W3 tensor id, W3(X)=(Tr(X)*I-X^T)/2 and E(X)=I3/3 tensor Tr_A(X), the deficit Delta=D(X||EX)-2D(NX||EX) is nonnegative for every normalized projector, every actual affine-projector input aI+bP with a,b>=0, every flat reference marginal, every input commuting with its own reference marginal, and every matched maximally-correlated support input. The last class has the stronger homogeneous bound Delta(X)>=3*lambda_min(X_block)*log(4/3). Each is a proved compatible receiver; arbitrary spectra/support/marginal coherence are not automatically covered.

INTERFACE AND ASSUMPTIONS
The actual W3 channel and conditional expectation. Spectral classes refer to the actual product support; modular commutation must be with the input's own marginal. The correlated-support theorem requires a matched orthonormal physical/reference basis and its audited anchored-source envelope.

BOUNDARIES
Two positive spectral levels plus an additional nonproduct kernel may have three levels and are outside aI+bP. Purification duality is a special exact identity, not monotonicity under discarding a reference. The exact marginal-pinching example locally lowers Delta when coherence is added, defeating free pinching but not producing a WH3 counterexample. General three-or-more-level nonaligned inputs remain open. No physical decoder, acquisition, capacity advantage or full target claim.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Finite-dimensional exact source inequalities, supported logarithm limits and exact audit imports. The complement index and unmatched shifted interval are paid in the proof; there is no claimed efficient testing procedure, measured device or total runtime bound.

NEXT OBLIGATION
Retain the actual margin and coherent cost outside the proved classes. A layer-cake decomposition, convex mixture, purification or reference discard needs its own signed entropy argument. Do not silently turn the union of proper classes into all states.
