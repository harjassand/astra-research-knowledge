SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
The classified inputs are finite-dimensional channels supplied by valid rational-complex Choi matrices, with positivity and trace preservation exact. Individual references, code blocks, and blocklengths are finite but have no uniform bound. Q and P denote the usual unassisted regularized quantum and private capacities, respectively; the source allows finite ensembles of mixed states entangled across channel uses. It makes no catalyst, preshared-entanglement, feedback, or postselection assumption.

SOURCE-REPORTED MAIN CLAIM
Assuming the source's uniformly gapped channel-order reduction and complete-order equivalence, exact-zero quantum-capacity and exact-zero private-capacity sets for finite rational channels are each coRE-complete under computable many-one reductions. Their positive-capacity complements are each RE-complete. The claimed reduction maps a machine M and input x to a rational channel T(M,x)=C(M,x) tensor E_{2,1/2}, with

    M(x) does not halt  =>  Q(T(M,x)) = P(T(M,x)) = 0,
    M(x) halts          =>  Q(T(M,x)) > 0 and P(T(M,x)) > 0.

This is an exact zero-versus-positive claim. The source does not claim capacity approximation hardness, a fixed-gap promise result, an efficient reduction, a computable bound on a positive witness blocklength, or a practical code construction.

SOURCE-REPORTED KERNEL INTERFACE
The assumed wrapper data has finite sector spaces H0,H1; finitely many positive rational effects A_i and B_i summing to the identities on their respective sectors; and rational rectangular cross-numerators O_i^s for s in {E,B}. For fixed positive rational gamma, finite rational B, and a common quadratic seminorm V, the companion source supplies the following uniform alternatives. In its nonhalting case, for every finite reference, positive definite baselines R0,R1, and complex cross-coordinate Z, the forms obey F_E-F_B >= gamma V and F_E+F_B <= B V. In its halting case, some finite faithful R0,R1 and some Z satisfy F_E-F_B < 0. The source says these gap parameters do not depend on a halting-witness dimension. N684 reports a physical completion only for this supplied, uniformly gapped family.

REPORTED NEW BRIDGE
The source reports a genuine rational Stinespring completion for the uniformly gapped inverse-Jordan kernel family supplied by the companion N675 channel-order construction. In the positive-source case it claims complete environmental SLD dominance for coherent inputs, arbitrary Hermitian tangents, and every finite reference, without a reference-size or state-conditioning bound; in the negative-source case it transfers a strict finite witness. The proposed construction combines coherent hub-channel control, exact purification duality for the physical leaf scores, sector-copy padding, and rational Gram/Stinespring factors. It is specific to the uniformly gapped source family, not a completion theorem for arbitrary ungapped POVM pairs.

The source also reports a zero-rate compilation of each fixed finite-dimensional half-erasure helper into repeated uses of the fixed qubit half-erasure channel. Random isometric encodings, an input-independent erasure-mask threshold, receiver decoders, and explicit failure flags are claimed to converge to the logical helper in diamond norm as blocklength grows. This removes a finite-helper-dimension dependence for positivity, but supplies no computable or efficient code-length bound. The proof applies continuity after private-to-quantum activation; it does not identify the physical erased subsystem with an ideal helper complement.

The final chain additionally uses the N675 complete-SLD/complete-relative-entropy source equivalence and the N676 fixed-qubit half-erasure capacity criterion. N675 and N676 remain source-derived and unreviewed. Their inclusion here is a reported proof dependency, not validation of either source.
