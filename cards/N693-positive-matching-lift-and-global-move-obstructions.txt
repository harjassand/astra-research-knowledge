N693-positive-matching-lift-and-global-move-obstructions | A positive bipartition lift exposes precise global-move obstructions for weighted matching sampling

For an explicitly represented graph G=(V,E), |V|=2n, with nonnegative rational edge weights, let G_S retain exactly the edges crossing S and V\S, and let Z_S be its weighted perfect-matching partition function. The report derives the exact positive identity sum_{S subset V} Z_S = 2^n Z_G. The joint law Pi(M,S)=w(M)/(2^n Z_G) 1{M subset E(G_S)} has matching marginal w(M)/Z_G. Its exact conditionals are: choose one endpoint of each matching edge for S given M, and use the weighted perfect-matching law on G_S for M given S. Existing bipartite machinery gives polynomial-time approximate implementations of the latter conditional; the report’s exact ideal-chain spectral-gap calculation is separate from those approximation costs. This is an exact representation, not an efficient general-graph counting algorithm.

The source further derives E[Y^2]/Z_G^2 = E_{M,N~pi_G} 2^{c(M,N)}, Cov(X)=1/4(I-R), while every graph has a negative-semidefinite log-Hessian at the all-ones point; a six-cycle derivative supplies a counterexample to global log-concavity of the lift polynomial. The proposed alternating conditional chain has exact gap 2^(1-n) on C_{2n}; a gadget family obstructs simple alternating-walk repairs while allowing an exact rational small-gadget elimination. The report also proves, under its stated input promise, that a polynomial-time sampler within TV 1/8 of uniform alternating simple cycles would imply NP=RP.

Scope boundary: these are obstructions to specified estimator/move primitives, not hardness of approximate perfect-matching counting. No general weighted perfect-matching FPRAS, polynomial-mixing sampler, or polynomial-cost general compressed elimination was obtained.
