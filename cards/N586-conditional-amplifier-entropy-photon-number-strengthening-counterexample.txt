Claim: Let g(x)=(x+1)ln(x+1)-x ln x and N(X|R)=g^{-1}(S(X|R)) when the conditional entropy is nonnegative. Prepare R,A by a two-mode squeeze of parameter 1/4 applied to tau_{1/2} tensor tau_{1/2}; take an independent vacuum B and apply a one-mode quantum-limited amplifier of gain G=6/5 to A,B. The source reports outward interval bounds N(A|R)>0.3895 and N(C|R)<0.6607, whereas the proposed conditional extension would require N(C|R)>=(6/5)N(A|R)+1/5>0.6674. Both conditional entropies are positive. Thus this finite-energy product-with-vacuum example refutes that reference-conditioned strengthening. The A and B input marginals remain independent and the unconditioned candidate inequality is saturated by the thermal marginals.
Interface: One mode in each amplifier port; ancillary input B is vacuum and independent of the reference-correlated A. The reference R is correlated with A by a two-mode squeezed thermal state. Vacuum covariance is 1/2 and entropies use natural logarithms. Only the proposed replacement S(X)->S(X|R) followed by g^{-1} is refuted; the independent-input unconditional theorem has no shared-reference premise.

The source encloses S(A|R) and S(C|R) with outward intervals, then uses monotonicity of g to establish inverse bounds separated by more than 0.0067.
