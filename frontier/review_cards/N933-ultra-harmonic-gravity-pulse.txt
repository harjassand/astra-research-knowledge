## Source-reported pulse construction

The serialized return gives

\[
H_m=\sum_{i=1}^2\left(\frac{p_i^2}{2m}+\frac12m\omega^2x_i^2\right)+Kx_1x_2,
\qquad H_{\rm int}=-f(t)(Z_1x_1+Z_2x_2),
\]

with \(K=2Gm^2/r^3\), \(\kappa=K/m\), and the stability condition \(|\kappa|<\omega^2\). Writing \(L_0=\partial_t^2+\omega^2\), the report selects an endpoint-flat trajectory \(u\) with \(u,u',u'',u'''=0\) at both endpoints and sets

\[
f(t)=m\bigl[L_0^2-\kappa^2\bigr]u(t).
\]

It reports conditional oscillator responses proportional to \((L_0u,-\kappa u)\) and \((-\kappa u,L_0u)\), and the phase

\[
\theta_{ZZ}=-\frac K\hbar\int_0^T\bigl[(L_0u)^2-\kappa^2u^2\bigr]dt.
\]

The claimed factorization is \(U_{\rm probes}\otimes U_m(T)\): it disentangles the probes from the mechanics, but does not return an arbitrary mechanical input state to itself. Literal return requires the stated stationary-initial-state premise. A \(\sin^6(\omega t/2)\) pulse is reported to join force and slew smoothly to zero.

## Noise result and physical benchmark

For continuous independent stationary high-temperature Ohmic baths, the source reports

\[
|\theta_{\rm sym}|\leq\frac{\epsilon}{4}\sqrt{\Gamma_1\Gamma_2},
\qquad \epsilon=\frac{\hbar|K|}{m\gamma k_BT}.
\]

This is a scoped noise comparison, not a separability theorem for all inputs or bath models. The worked benchmark is hypothetical: mass 5.6 micrograms, separation 200 micrometres, force about \(9.27\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm N\), and a 14-second ideal \(\pi/4\) loop. Even granting a quality factor \(Q=2.6\times10^7\) at an unmeasured low trap frequency, the source estimates \(\epsilon\approx3\times10^{-9}\), leaving no credible gate regime under that Ohmic model.

The source notes that a rare-collision alternative may avoid a Gaussian-diffusion approximation, but supplies no acquired platform with the required isolation/force model. Higher-order Newtonian thermal terms also remain unresolved. Compatible spin force, trap frequency, force duration, isolation and measured noise are unacquired.
