EXACT CLAIM
The earlier symmetric four-arm two-hit shared-window model retains its N^-1/2 tail. A mechanically useful preanchor changes the required operation to one additional old-side graft. With k=d and X=N*a*eta*theta*S, independent thinning under T~Exp(d) gives one-hit no-link failure(1-exp(-X))/X and fixed-m failure asymptotic m/X. This is a changed interface, not a refutation of the two-hit law. Correlated loss of every preanchor in a region imposes a ceiling that abundance cannot remove. A single usable terminus per chain also makes useful sites per mass proportional to a*e*theta*S/M when other kinetic/retention factors are held fixed. Neither a physical weld nor a cost advantage is acquired.

INTERFACE AND ASSUMPTIONS
Actual old/new contact and a mechanically effective preanchor; fixed positive graft/shutdown rates; Poisson candidate count and independent candidate outcomes conditional on the common window. The product a*eta*theta*S must represent justified association, donor retention, old-side targeting and later individual service survival, distinct from shared regional delamination. Single-end mass accounting requires one usable terminus per chain and comparable end kinetics.
