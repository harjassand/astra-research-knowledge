For every h∈C_E and protected family F, the report proves there is g∈C_E preserving every protected marginal, satisfying I_g(A;B|C)=0 for every partition separating F, and with g(E)≥h(E). The proof maximizes total rank on the compact fiber of protected marginals; any positive separating conditional-mutual-information defect can be removed by an internal copy while increasing total rank by exactly that defect.
