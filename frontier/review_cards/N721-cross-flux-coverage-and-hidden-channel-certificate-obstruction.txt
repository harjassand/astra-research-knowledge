In a finite undirected network with supplied c_e>0 and terminals A,B, an admissible potential u_A=0,u_B=1 orients an exactly conserved unit flow j_e>=0 along strictly positive drops Delta_e. No incoming flow at A or outgoing flow at B is allowed; zero-drop edges require zero flow. Discrete integration by parts gives sum j_e Delta_e=1. Draw a path by normalized outgoing flows, then a uniform potential level: its crossing edge has q_e=j_e Delta_e. Acquiring/checking the graph, outgoing tables, conservation, monotonicity and paths is charged; explicit input generally costs O(|E|). This reconstructs classical principles, not a new variational theorem.

Only if every positive Dirichlet-energy edge has q_e>0 do a_e=c_e Delta_e/j_e and b_e=j_e/(c_e Delta_e) satisfy E_q a=E(u), E_q b=R(j), ab=1, giving 1/E_q b<=cap(A,B)<=E_q a. With two parallel edges c=(1,M), u=(0,1) and j=(1,0), q is normalized but sampled E_q a=1 misses cap=1+M. Feasibility and normalization do not certify the upper bound. In the continuum both Dirichlet and resistance energy measures require absolute continuity under q=j dot grad u, plus feasible traces/fluxes and tractable surface/streamline sampling. Globally certified bounds a<=A_*, b<=B_* yield Hoeffding enclosures; useful relative width still requires a small primal-dual gap and domination bounds relative to the means. Capacity is distinct from a transition rate (basin normalization needed) and from a pointwise committor probability.

Berman-Konsowa supplies the relevant stronger existing path-flow baseline cap>=E_path[(sum j_e/c_e)^(-1)], at least the Thomson endpoint by Jensen. The source compares series and parallel examples and reports no advantage. Zero-variance harmonic fields do not supply cheap field acquisition or certify an approximate sampler's coverage. The remaining opening is a specified structured model with acquired exact feasible flows, global overlap and tail bounds, not an unspecific rarity-independent promise.

Any adaptive randomized random-stopping algorithm returning [L,U], L>0, U/L<=1+epsilon, with success >=1-alpha on every instance (0<alpha<1/2), needs E_0 Q>=M(1-2alpha).
