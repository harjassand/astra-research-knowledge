EXACT CLAIM
For N>=2 fixed positive reciprocal thermal-tree lumps, source-only initial energy and R=C_total/C_source>1, CV²>=1/[1+(N-1)tanh(log(R)/(2(N-1)))], sharply attained by positive ladders. Arbitrarily many lumps still require R>=exp(2(epsilon^-2-1)) for CV<=epsilon. A different autonomous single-reservoir memoryless curve g_min<=G(T)<=g_max with K=g_max/g_min>1 has optimum CV²=v, where 2(atanh(v)-v)=-log(1-1/K)-1/K; its low-to-high conductance threshold attains the bound in the measurable class.

INTERFACE AND ASSUMPTIONS
Linear fixed reciprocal tree, one initially excited source, fixed-temperature bath and positive capacities/conductances for the first theorem. The second requires one constant-capacity reservoir, local memoryless G(T), finite contrast, no subsequent input and all leakage counted.

BOUNDARIES
Tree theorem does not cover loops, narrow-window concentration, nonlinear/scheduled switches or multiple excited nodes. RMS waveform width differs from timing jitter. Thermal ladder optimization maps to established OU optimal design. The continuum realization requires actual geometric regime: fixed capacity/material/delay do not independently make the slenderness parameter small. Physical switch examples do not supply the ideal local G(T); two-temperature closure/ON regimes cannot be blended. QP trajectory did not reproduce identically, although saved feasible objectives were checked. No acquired measurement, fabricated device, historical priority, efficient algorithm or practical advantage is inferred from these mathematical/model results. Mission NOT_ACHIEVED.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Tree precision charges capacity ratio; timescale scaling is not precision. Switch CV scales K^-1/3 but needs actual conductance contrast, transition width, hysteresis/storage and threshold calibration. Fixed C,g_min,g_max do not allow independent mean-delay choice. The specified constant-power loading protocol needs P>g_max*theta and costs charging/reset.

NEXT OBLIGATION
Acquire the whole compatible reservoir–switch–bath response, geometry, loading/reset and matched ordinary comparator before using the ideal frontier as a physical timing claim.
