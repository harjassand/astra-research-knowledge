N661-complete-sld-petz-order-and-sufficient-lift-certificate | Complete SLD order matches all modular Petz orders with a finite sufficient lift certificate
status=source_derived_unreviewed; Source-derived, unreviewed manuscript claims. Intake preserves and routes the supplied TeX only; proofs and reported diagnostics were not independently replayed. External correctness and historical priority UNKNOWN. No foundational breakthrough is established.
Symbols and resource contracts are local to this card.

Interface: Arbitrary finite-dimensional channels with common input; complete order quantifies every finite untouched reference and all state pairs with extended modular-Petz support values, allowing infinity. Finite certificate uses supplied unnormalized Choi matrices.
Failure: Pairwise dominance does not identify linear contraction coefficients. A common signed lift is only sufficient absent extra span assumptions; no finite-reference bound or general polynomial membership algorithm for the unsmoothed order follows. Matrix size may be exponential in qubit count.

Claim (source-reported, independent of N402/N403). For finite channels the following are equivalent: (i) complete measured-chi-square/SLD dominance; (ii) complete modular Petz dominance for f_1(x)=(x-1)^2/(x+1); (iii) simultaneous complete dominance for every operator-convex modular Petz divergence D_f. Complete means every finite untouched quantum reference. Affine tangents at one are immaterial. Standard extended Petz support values permit infinity.

Proof interface. For faithful rho,sigma, X=rho-sigma and s>0, append an untouched qubit and set Omega_s=diag(rho,s sigma)/(1+s), H=[[0,X],[X,0]]. The source derives q_{Omega_s}(H)=4(1+s)<X,(L_rho+s R_sigma)^{-1}X>=4(1+s)D_{f_s}(rho||sigma), f_s(x)=(x-1)^2/(x+s), where L and R are left/right multiplication. Positive operator-convex representation, endpoint limits and integration give the forward order; a local second-order expansion for f_1 gives the reverse. The PSD-boundary argument uses the spectral perspective, omits 0/0 and applies positive integration/Tonelli. This is an equivalence of pairwise orders, not equality of their linear contraction coefficients or a new operational simulator.

Finite sufficient certificate (source-reported formulation of an established criterion). For M:A->C and N:A->B, a state-independent HP unital observable lift J:L(B)->L(C) obeying M*J=N* and complete square domination certifies complete SLD order. In row-major Hilbert-Schmidt coordinates with unnormalized Choi matrices, D_N=C_N tensor I_B, D_M=C_M tensor I_C and T=I_A tensor [J]. The CP outer-defect inequality D_N-T* D_M T>=0 is equivalent to the affine PSD block [[D_N,T*D_M],[D_M T,D_M]]>=0, including singular D_M. Add the linear HP, unitality and reconstruction constraints. The main block order is d_A(d_B^2+d_C^2). A Stinespring formulation is also reported.

Unresolved distinction. Necessity of a common signed lift for unrestricted complete unsmoothed SLD order is open; the source reports a known full-output-span structural case. The certificate's finite reference bound cannot be transferred to the weaker general order. No general polynomial algorithm for arbitrary complete entropy-order membership, polynomial-in-qubit-count guarantee, or rigorous generic numerical decision without conditioning/exact certificates is established. The source says its CVXPY builder was not solved and its qutrit Stinespring reconstruction is diagnostic. No companion builder, replay scripts or certificates were supplied with this attachment.

Relation to existing capital. N656's equal-coefficient extension still depends on N402/N403; this all-f channel-order equivalence neither proves that formula nor closes their review gates. The source assesses the equivalence as a short extension of existing coherent-block/integral machinery, with priority unestablished.
