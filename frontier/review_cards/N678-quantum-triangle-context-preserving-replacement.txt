This is a weighted joint-law guarantee; it does not assert a uniform bound on each conditional row. The estimate depends on preserving the surrounding source and reference context.

QUADRATIC SCORE AND IMPORTED HARDNESS

    T_G(P) = K sum_{x,y,a,b} pi(x,y) V(a,b,x,y) P(x,a,y,b),
    lambda = K^2(32+m+n),
and
    H_G(P) = T_G(P) - lambda[
        delta_X + delta_Y
        + sum_x (u_x - 1/m)^2
        + sum_y (v_y - 1/n)^2
