N1124-annihilator-acquired-perturbation-code

Manufacture a hidden perturbation code by applying an annihilator to the raw matrix

EXACT CLAIM
If raw symmetric B=D+S has unknown rank-r D=UKU^T, row-weight(S)<=Delta, monic degree-q h with h(S)=0, full-rank orbit W=[U,SU,...,S^(q-1)U] and distance(im W)>2Delta, then im h(B)=im W. Exact D,S are acquired in2^(qr)poly(n+q) time and polynomial space. Acquisition is unconditional; local-phase counting consequences retain their dependency.

INTERFACE
