EXACT CLAIM
An all-to-all irreversible matching race among n desired handle pairs gives Pcorrect=product_{j=0}^{n-1}(1+j*beta/k)^(-1), while a uniform bound D*beta on each remaining site's wrong-partner hazard gives Pcorrect>=(1+D*beta/k)^(-n). Independent multivalent rebinding can make perfect-sink clearance exponential in arm count. Ideal whole-prefix reset has E[R]=1+L*(1-p)/p rounds but wastes (1-p)*L*(L-1)/2 successful downstream bonds. Independently rooted b-edge packets reduce within-packet waste, excluding fusion. Equal total catalyst exposure alone gives no repair advantage in the common-rate-scaling model.

INTERFACE AND ASSUMPTIONS
Actual registered reactive handles, constant intended/wrong hazards or their explicitly bounded spatial alternative; for reset, independent identical per-edge success, no wrong bonds/damage, complete unrooted-island clearance and retained rooted product in the original register. Multivalent clearance assumes independent arms and an absorbing wash sink. These are model premises, not demonstrated CuAAC reset capabilities.
